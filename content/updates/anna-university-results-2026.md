## Anna University Results 2026 — Overview

**Anna University, Chennai** conducts semester examinations for all UG (B.E./B.Tech) and PG (M.E./M.Tech/MBA/MCA) courses across its affiliated colleges in Tamil Nadu. The **November/December 2025 semester exam results** are expected to be declared soon on the official Controller of Examinations portal.

Students who appeared for the odd semester (1st, 3rd, 5th, 7th semester) exams held in November–December 2025 can check their results on **coe1.annauniv.edu** or **coe2.annauniv.edu** once published.

## How to Check Anna University Results 2026

Follow these steps to view your Anna University semester exam results online:

### Step 1: Visit the Official Result Portal

Go to the Anna University COE portal at [coe1.annauniv.edu](https://coe1.annauniv.edu/) or [coe2.annauniv.edu](https://coe2.annauniv.edu/).

### Step 2: Find the Results Link

On the homepage, look for the **"UG/PG Results"** or **"Exam Results November December 2025"** link.

### Step 3: Enter Your Register Number

Enter your **Register Number** in the required field. Some portals may also ask for your date of birth.

### Step 4: View and Download Your Result

Your semester result with subject-wise grades and GPA will be displayed. **Take a screenshot or print** the result for your records. The official mark statement will be issued by your college later.

> **Tip:** If the main portal (coe1) is slow due to heavy traffic, try the mirror site at [coe2.annauniv.edu](https://coe2.annauniv.edu/) or [aucoe.annauniv.edu](https://aucoe.annauniv.edu/).

## Important Details

- **Grading System:** Anna University follows a **CGPA-based grading system**. Each subject is graded from O (Outstanding) to U (Re-appear). The GPA for each semester and cumulative CGPA are displayed in the results.

- **Revaluation:** Students unsatisfied with their results can apply for **revaluation** or **photocopy of answer sheets** through the COE portal within the announced deadline.

- **Arrear Results:** Results for arrear (supplementary) examinations are published separately. Students can check arrear results on the same portal.

- **Result SMS/Notification:** Anna University does not officially send results via SMS. Always check results only from the official COE portals.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### When will Anna University Nov/Dec 2025 results be declared?

The Anna University November/December 2025 semester results are expected to be declared soon. The exact date will be announced on the official COE portal. Bookmark this page for updates.

### How can I check my Anna University result?

Visit [coe1.annauniv.edu](https://coe1.annauniv.edu/), click on the results link, enter your register number, and view your result.

### What if the Anna University result website is not loading?

During result declaration, the portal experiences heavy traffic. Try the mirror site [coe2.annauniv.edu](https://coe2.annauniv.edu/) or check after some time.

### How do I apply for revaluation of Anna University results?

After results are published, Anna University announces a revaluation window. You can apply through the COE portal by paying the prescribed fee within the deadline.

### Is CGPA mentioned in Anna University results?

Yes. Anna University results display subject-wise grades, semester GPA, and cumulative CGPA.

### Where can I get my original mark statement?

The official consolidated mark statement is issued through your affiliated college. The online result is for immediate reference only.
