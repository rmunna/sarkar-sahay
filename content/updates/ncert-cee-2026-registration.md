### Eligibility Criteria (Overview)

The eligibility criteria for NCERT CEE vary based on the program chosen (e.g., B.Sc. B.Ed., B.A. B.Ed., M.Sc. Ed., B.Ed., M.Ed.). Key general points include:

*   **Educational Qualification:**
    *   For Integrated B.Sc. B.Ed./B.A. B.Ed.: Passed 10+2 (or equivalent) with a minimum percentage of marks (usually 50% for General/OBC/EWS, 45% for SC/ST/PwD) in relevant subjects.
    *   For B.Ed.: Bachelor's degree (or Master's degree) from a recognized university with a minimum percentage of marks (usually 50% for General/OBC/EWS, 45% for SC/ST/PwD).
    *   For M.Ed.: B.Ed./B.A. B.Ed./B.Sc. B.Ed./B.El. Ed./D.El. Ed. with an undergraduate degree from a recognized university with a minimum percentage of marks.
*   **Age Limit:** Generally, there is no upper age limit for most NCERT CEE programs, but candidates should refer to the official notification for any program-specific age restrictions.

It is imperative to thoroughly read the official **NCERT CEE 2026 Information Bulletin** for detailed and exact eligibility requirements for your desired program before applying.

### NCERT CEE 2026 Exam Pattern and Syllabus

The NCERT CEE is typically a Computer-Based Test (CBT) consisting of multiple-choice questions. The exam pattern and syllabus vary slightly depending on the course applied for (e.g., Group A for integrated courses, Group B for B.Ed., Group C for M.Ed.).

**General Structure (Tentative):**
*   **Mode:** Online (Computer Based Test)
*   **Type of Questions:** Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)
*   **Sections:** Typically cover subjects like Language Proficiency, General Awareness, and Teaching Aptitude/Reasoning Ability.
*   **Marking Scheme:** Usually +2 for each correct answer and negative marking (-0.5) for incorrect answers.

Refer to the official NCERT CEE 2026 Information Bulletin for the precise exam pattern, number of questions per section, total marks, and detailed syllabus for your specific program.
