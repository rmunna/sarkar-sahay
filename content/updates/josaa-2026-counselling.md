## JoSAA 2026 Counselling — Registration Open from June 2

The Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA) has opened registration for **JoSAA 2026 counselling from June 2, 2026 (5 PM)**. JoSAA 2026 covers seat allotment across 23 IITs, IISc Bangalore, NITs, IIITs, and other Government Funded Technical Institutes (GFTIs) — a total of **67,323 seats**.

Candidates who have qualified **JEE Main 2026** or **JEE Advanced 2026** are eligible to participate.

## Who Can Apply for JoSAA 2026

- Candidates who qualified **JEE Main 2026** (for NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs)
- Candidates who qualified **JEE Advanced 2026** (for IITs and IISc Bangalore)

## Institutes Covered Under JoSAA 2026

- **23 IITs** (Indian Institutes of Technology)
- **IISc Bangalore** (Indian Institute of Science)
- **NITs** (National Institutes of Technology)
- **IIITs** (Indian Institutes of Information Technology)
- **GFTIs** (Government Funded Technical Institutes)
- **Total seats: 67,323**

## How to Register for JoSAA 2026

1. Go to the official website **[josaa.nic.in](https://josaa.nic.in)**
2. Click on **"Candidate Registration"**
3. Log in using your **JEE Main / JEE Advanced roll number and password**
4. Fill in personal and academic details
5. Proceed to **Choice Filling** — add and order your preferred institutes and branches
6. **Lock your choices** before June 10, 2026 (choices cannot be changed after locking)
7. Check the **Mock Allotment** on June 8 and June 10 to get an idea of your likely seat
8. Wait for **Round 1 allotment** on June 13, 2026

## JoSAA 2026 Choice Locking — Key Points

- Choices can be added, edited, and reordered until June 10, 2026
- After choice locking, no further changes are permitted
- Mock allotments on June 8 and June 10 do not affect actual allotment — they are for guidance only
- Order your choices carefully: your most preferred institute-branch combination should be ranked first

## Documents Required for JoSAA 2026 Reporting

- JEE Main / JEE Advanced admit card and scorecard
- Class 10 mark sheet (for date of birth proof)
- Class 12 mark sheet and passing certificate
- Category certificate (OBC-NCL / SC / ST / EWS), if applicable
- PwD certificate, if applicable
- Passport-size photographs
- Aadhaar card

## FAQs

**Q: When does JoSAA 2026 registration start?**
A: JoSAA 2026 registration opened on June 2, 2026 at 5 PM at josaa.nic.in.

**Q: What is the last date to register for JoSAA 2026?**
A: The registration and choice filling window closes on June 11, 2026.

**Q: When is Round 1 allotment for JoSAA 2026?**
A: Round 1 seat allotment is on June 13, 2026. Online reporting for Round 1 begins from June 14, 2026.

**Q: How many rounds are there in JoSAA 2026?**
A: JoSAA 2026 has a total of 6 rounds.

**Q: How many seats are available under JoSAA 2026?**
A: A total of 67,323 seats are available across IITs, IISc, NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs.

**Q: Can JEE Main qualified candidates apply for IITs through JoSAA?**
A: No. IIT seats are only for JEE Advanced 2026 qualified candidates. JEE Main qualification gives access to NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs.

**Q: What happens after choice locking in JoSAA 2026?**
A: After locking, choices are frozen and used for seat allotment. Round 1 allotment results are declared on June 13, 2026.

**Q: Is there a mock allotment in JoSAA 2026?**
A: Yes. Two mock allotments are scheduled — June 8 and June 10, 2026 — to help candidates gauge their likely seat before locking choices.
