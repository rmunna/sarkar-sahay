# UP DELED Result 2026 – Check BTC Semester Results Online

The **UP DELED (Diploma in Elementary Education) Result 2026** has been declared by the Exam Regulatory Authority, Uttar Pradesh. Candidates who appeared for the UP DELED semester examinations can now check their results online at **updeled.gov.in** or **btcexam.in**.

The result is available for all four semesters — 1st, 2nd, 3rd, and 4th semester. Read on to learn how to check your result, understand pass marks, and apply for re-evaluation if needed.

## How to Check UP DELED Result 2026

Follow these steps to check your UP DELED / BTC result online:

1. Visit the official website **[updeled.gov.in](https://updeled.gov.in)** or **[btcexam.in](https://btcexam.in)**.
2. On the homepage, click on the **"Result"** or **"Exam Result 2026"** link.
3. Select your **semester** (1st / 2nd / 3rd / 4th).
4. Enter your **roll number** in the required field.
5. Click on **"Submit"** or **"Show Result"**.
6. Your result will appear on the screen with subject-wise marks.
7. **Download or print** the result for future reference.

> **Tip:** Keep your admit card handy as it contains your roll number and other details needed to access the result.

## Semester-Wise Pass Marks

To pass the UP DELED examination, candidates must secure the minimum marks in each semester:

- **Theory Papers:** Minimum **36% marks** in each subject.
- **Practical / Internal Assessment:** Minimum **40% marks**.
- **Overall Aggregate:** Minimum **36%** across all subjects combined.

Candidates who fail to meet the minimum pass marks in any subject can appear for the **supplementary examination** conducted later in the year.

## What to Do After Checking Your Result

- **If you passed:** Download the marksheet and keep it safe. Wait for the official marksheet to be issued by your institution.
- **If you failed in one or more subjects:** You can appear for the supplementary exam. Check the official website for supplementary exam dates.
- **If you want re-evaluation:** Follow the re-evaluation process below.

## Re-Evaluation and Re-Checking Process

If you are not satisfied with your result, you can apply for re-evaluation or re-checking:

1. Visit **updeled.gov.in** and look for the **"Re-evaluation / Re-checking"** link.
2. Fill in the application form with your roll number and subject details.
3. Pay the re-evaluation fee online (typically ₹500–₹1000 per subject).
4. Submit the application within the **prescribed deadline** (usually 15–30 days from result declaration).
5. The revised result will be published on the website after re-evaluation.

> **Note:** Re-checking involves verifying whether all answers were evaluated and marks were totalled correctly. Re-evaluation involves a fresh evaluation of your answer sheet.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where can I check my UP DELED Result 2026?
You can check your result at **updeled.gov.in** or **btcexam.in** by entering your roll number.

### Is UP DELED the same as UP BTC?
Yes. The UP BTC course was renamed to **UP DELED (Diploma in Elementary Education)** in 2017. The result is issued under the DELED name.

### What is the passing percentage for UP DELED?
You need a minimum of **36% marks** in theory subjects and **40% in practicals** to pass.

### Can I apply for re-evaluation if I failed?
Yes, you can apply for re-evaluation or re-checking through the official website within the deadline. A fee is applicable per subject.

### When will the UP DELED supplementary exam be held?
The supplementary exam dates are usually announced 1–2 months after the main result. Check **updeled.gov.in** for official notifications.

### How do I download my UP DELED marksheet?
After checking your result online, use the download or print option on the result page. The official marksheet will be issued by your training institution separately.
