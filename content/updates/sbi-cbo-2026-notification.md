## What is SBI CBO 2026?

The State Bank of India (SBI) has released the Circle Based Officer (CBO) Recruitment 2026, inviting applications for 2,273 posts across 16 circles. This recruitment is for experienced banking professionals holding officer-grade positions in Scheduled Commercial Banks or Regional Rural Banks. The notification was published under Advertisement No. CRPD/CBO/2025-26/18.

## Eligibility Criteria

- **Age Limit:** 21 to 30 years (as on 31.12.2025). Relaxation: SC/ST — 5 years, OBC (NCL) — 3 years, PwBD — 10 years
- **Educational Qualification:** Graduation in any discipline from a recognized university. Professional degrees (Engineering, CA, CMA, etc.) are also accepted
- **Experience:** Minimum 2 years as an Officer in any Scheduled Commercial Bank or Regional Rural Bank (as per RBI schedule)
- **Local Language:** Proficiency in the opted circle's local language (read, write, understand)

## Selection Process

The selection involves four phases:

### Phase 1: Online Examination
- **Objective Test** (120 marks, 2 hours): English Language (30), Banking Knowledge (40), General/Economy Awareness (30), Computer Aptitude (20)
- **Descriptive Test** (50 marks, 30 minutes): Letter Writing and Essay in English
- No negative marking in the objective section

### Phase 2: Screening
- Eligibility and job-profile matching by a Screening Committee
- Approximately 3× candidates shortlisted (circle and category-wise) for the interview

### Phase 3: Interview
- 50 marks with minimum qualifying marks required

### Phase 4: Local Language Test
- Exempted if the local language was studied in Class 10th or 12th

### Final Merit
- Weightage: 75% Online Test + 25% Interview (circle-wise and category-wise)

## Salary & Benefits

- **Starting Basic Pay:** ₹48,480 per month (JMGS-I) + 2 advance increments
- **Pay Scale:** ₹48,480 – ₹85,920 per month
- **Allowances:** DA, HRA/Lease, CCA, PF/NPS, LFC, Medical, and other perks as per SBI rules

## Preparation Tips

1. Focus on Banking Knowledge — this carries the highest weightage (40 marks). Study banking regulations, RBI guidelines, and financial products
2. Practice descriptive writing regularly — letter and essay writing in English can make or break your score
3. Stay updated on current affairs related to economy and banking for the GA section
4. Review your own bank's policies and procedures as practical banking knowledge is tested
5. Take mock tests to manage time effectively across sections

## FAQs

### Q: Who is eligible for SBI CBO 2026?
Graduates with minimum 2 years of experience as an Officer in any Scheduled Commercial Bank or Regional Rural Bank can apply.

### Q: What is the last date to apply for SBI CBO 2026?
The extended last date to apply online is 25 February 2026.

### Q: How many vacancies are there in SBI CBO 2026?
A total of 2,273 Circle Based Officer posts are available across 16 circles.

### Q: Can I apply for multiple circles?
No, candidates can apply for only one circle. Posting will be in the opted circle with no inter-circle transfer up to SMGS-IV or 12 years, whichever is later.

### Q: What is the salary for SBI CBO?
The starting basic pay is ₹48,480 per month with a pay scale ranging up to ₹85,920, plus allowances.

### Q: Is there negative marking in the SBI CBO exam?
No, there is no negative marking in the objective test section.
