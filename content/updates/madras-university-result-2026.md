## How to Check Madras University Result 2026

Follow these steps to check your UNOM exam result:

1. Visit the official results portal at [results.unom.ac.in](https://results.unom.ac.in).
2. On the homepage, select your **degree programme** (UG or PG).
3. Choose the **examination month/year** and **semester**.
4. Enter your **Register Number** as printed on your hall ticket.
5. Click **Submit** or **Get Result**.
6. Your result will be displayed on screen with subject-wise marks.
7. Download or print the result page for your records.

## Important Details

### Courses Covered
- **UG Programmes:** BA, BSc, BCom, BCA, BBA, B.Lit, BSW
- **PG Programmes:** MA, MSc, MCom, MCA, MBA, M.Phil
- **Professional Courses:** BL, ML, BEd, MEd

### Grading System
The University of Madras follows a Choice Based Credit System (CBCS) with letter grades. Students need a minimum of 40% (or Grade D) to pass in each subject.

### Revaluation and Retotalling
Students not satisfied with their results can apply for **revaluation** or **retotalling** through the university portal within the prescribed deadline. The fee for revaluation is typically ₹500–₹750 per subject.

## What to Do After Checking Results

- **Passed all subjects:** Proceed to next semester registration or collect your degree certificate (for final semester students).
- **Failed in any subject:** Apply for supplementary examinations when notified by the university.
- **Discrepancy in marks:** Apply for revaluation or retotalling within the deadline.
