MPSC State Service (Rajyaseva) Group A officers like Deputy Collector and DSP start at **₹56,100 basic pay (Pay Level 10)**. With DA, HRA, and Maharashtra government allowances, the **in-hand salary is approximately ₹75,000–₹90,000/month** in urban postings. Group B posts like Tahsildar start at a lower pay level.

## Benefits Beyond Salary

- **Government quarters:** Bungalow/flat in district headquarters
- **Vehicle:** Official vehicle for field posts (Collector office level and above)
- **Medical:** Maharashtra Government Medical Scheme for employee and family
- **LTC:** Leave Travel Concession
- **NPS pension:** 10% employee + 14% state government contribution
- **Children's education allowance:** ₹2,250/month per child (max 2)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is the in-hand salary for MPSC PSI (Police Sub-Inspector)?**
PSI starts at ₹38,600 basic (Pay Level 6). With DA, HRA, and police-specific allowances, in-hand salary is approximately ₹52,000–₹60,000/month in urban Maharashtra postings.

**Q: Does MPSC officer salary follow Maharashtra 7th Pay Commission or central?**
Maharashtra has its own 7th Pay Commission implementation with state-specific pay matrix and DA revision schedule. The basic pay structure is broadly similar to central government but with state DA rates and allowances.

**Q: Is MPSC Deputy Collector salary on par with IAS?**
At entry level, both MPSC Group A and IAS start at Level 10 (₹56,100 basic). IAS officers have a higher career ceiling (going up to Cabinet Secretary level), central deputation options, and more prestigious postings. MPSC officers top out at Additional Chief Secretary / Principal Secretary level in Maharashtra state service.
