## NEET UG 2026 Cut Off: Everything You Need to Know

Welcome to our dedicated page for NEET UG 2026 Cut Off information. With the NEET UG 2026 results officially declared by the National Testing Agency (NTA) on **June 13, 2026**, candidates are now eagerly searching for the cut-off scores required for admission to MBBS, BDS, and other medical courses across India. This article provides a comprehensive guide on understanding, checking, and utilizing the NEET UG 2026 cut-offs for your medical admission journey.

## Understanding NEET UG 2026 Cut Offs

The NEET UG cut-off essentially refers to the minimum qualifying marks or percentile required for candidates to be eligible for counselling and admission. It's crucial to understand that there are primarily two types of cut-offs:

### 1. NEET UG Qualifying Cut Off
This is the minimum percentile/score that a candidate must achieve in the NEET UG 2026 examination to be considered eligible for participation in the subsequent counselling process. These cut-offs are declared by the NTA along with the results. Candidates scoring below this threshold are not eligible for any medical or dental admission through NEET UG 2026. The qualifying cut-off varies for different categories:

*   **General (UR)/EWS:** 50th percentile
*   **OBC/SC/ST:** 40th percentile
*   **UR-PwD:** 45th percentile
*   **OBC-PwD/SC-PwD/ST-PwD:** 40th percentile

### 2. NEET UG Admission/Counselling Cut Off
Also known as the college-wise or seat allotment cut-off, this refers to the last rank or score at which admission was granted to a particular medical or dental college for a specific course and category. These cut-offs are dynamic and are released after each round of counselling by the Medical Counselling Committee (MCC) for All India Quota (AIQ) seats and by respective State Counselling Authorities for State Quota seats. They are significantly higher than the qualifying cut-off and depend on various factors.

## How to Check NEET UG 2026 Cut Offs

To ensure you access accurate and official information, follow these steps:

### For NEET UG 2026 Qualifying Cut Off:
1.  **Visit the Official NTA NEET Website:** Go to [https://neet.ntaonline.in](https://neet.ntaonline.in) or [https://nta.ac.in](https://nta.ac.in).
2.  **Check Result Notification/Information Bulletin:** The qualifying cut-off percentile and corresponding scores are usually published within the official NEET UG 2026 Result notification or the Information Bulletin on the NTA website.

### For NEET UG 2026 Counselling Cut Offs (Admission Cut Offs):
1.  **For All India Quota (AIQ) Seats (15%):**
    *   Visit the official Medical Counselling Committee (MCC) website: [https://mcc.nic.in](https://mcc.nic.in).
    *   Navigate to the 'UG Medical Counselling' section.
    *   After each round of counselling, MCC publishes the 'Opening and Closing Ranks/Scores' for various colleges and categories. This data represents the admission cut-offs.
2.  **For State Quota Seats (85%):**
    *   Visit the official website of your respective State Medical Counselling Authority. Each state has its own designated body (e.g., DME, DMER, etc.) responsible for 85% state quota counselling.
    *   These authorities will publish their own merit lists and subsequent round-wise cut-offs after each round of state counselling.

**Note:** Counselling for Deemed Universities, Central Universities, ESIC, AFMS (except for AFMC initial screening) is also conducted by MCC.

## Factors Determining NEET UG 2026 Cut Offs

Several factors influence the NEET UG 2026 cut-off scores, particularly for admission to colleges:

*   **Number of Candidates:** A higher number of candidates appearing for the exam can potentially increase the competition and thus the cut-off.
*   **Difficulty Level of the Exam:** A tougher exam generally leads to lower scores, potentially reducing the cut-off, and vice-versa.
*   **Total Number of Seats:** An increase in the total number of MBBS/BDS seats can slightly lower the cut-off, while fewer seats can raise it.
*   **Category of the Candidate:** Reservation policies ensure different cut-offs for General, OBC, SC, ST, EWS, and PwD categories.
*   **Previous Year's Cut-off Trends:** While not definitive, past year's cut-offs provide a reference point for predicting current year trends.

## What to Do After Checking Cut Offs

Based on your NEET UG 2026 score and the declared cut-offs, here's what you should do next:

*   **If you meet the Qualifying Cut Off:** You are eligible to participate in the counselling process. Begin preparing for counselling registration, choice filling, and document verification. Keep an eye on the official MCC ([https://mcc.nic.in](https://mcc.nic.in)) and your State Counselling Authority websites for counselling schedules.
*   **If your score is close to or above the Admission Cut Offs (based on previous trends):** You have a good chance of securing a seat. Focus on strategic choice filling during counselling.
*   **If your score is below the Qualifying Cut Off:** Unfortunately, you are not eligible for medical or dental admissions through NEET UG 2026. You may consider other career options or prepare for the next NEET UG examination.

## Important Points for NEET UG 2026 Counselling

*   **Registration:** Both AIQ (MCC) and State Quota counselling require separate registrations.
*   **Choice Filling & Locking:** Carefully research colleges and courses before filling your choices. The order of preference is critical.
*   **Document Verification:** Be ready with all original documents, including NEET UG 2026 admit card, result, mark sheets, category certificates, identity proof, etc.
*   **Security Deposit:** Some counselling processes require a refundable security deposit.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: When were NEET UG 2026 results declared?
**A1:** The NEET UG 2026 results were declared on **June 13, 2026**, by the National Testing Agency (NTA) on their official website [https://neet.ntaonline.in](https://neet.ntaonline.in).

### Q2: What is the NEET UG 2026 qualifying percentile for the General category?
**A2:** For the General (UR) category, the qualifying percentile for NEET UG 2026 is the 50th percentile. The corresponding minimum score will be released by NTA along with the result notification.

### Q3: Where can I find the official cut-off list for AIQ seats?
**A3:** The official admission cut-off list (opening and closing ranks/scores) for All India Quota (AIQ) seats will be published by the Medical Counselling Committee (MCC) on their official website, [https://mcc.nic.in](https://mcc.nic.in), after each round of counselling.

### Q4: What is the difference between NEET UG cut-off and rank?
**A4:** NEET UG cut-off refers to a score or percentile that determines eligibility or the last score admitted to a college. NEET UG rank, on the other hand, is your position in the national merit list, indicating your performance relative to all other candidates. Both are crucial for admission.

### Q5: Will the NEET UG 2026 cut-off for state quota be different from AIQ?
**A5:** Yes, absolutely. The NEET UG 2026 admission cut-offs for the 85% State Quota seats are usually different from the 15% All India Quota (AIQ) seats, as they are influenced by state-specific merit, reservation policies, and candidate preferences within that state.

### Q6: What documents should I keep ready for NEET UG 2026 counselling?
**A6:** You should keep the following documents ready: NEET UG 2026 Admit Card, NEET UG 2026 Scorecard/Result, Class 10th Mark Sheet & Certificate, Class 12th Mark Sheet & Certificate, Provisional Allotment Letter (from MCC/State Authority), Identity Proof (Aadhar/PAN/Driving License/Passport), Domicile Certificate (for State Quota), Category Certificate (if applicable), PwD Certificate (if applicable), Passport size photographs, and relevant migration/transfer certificates.
