BSF Head Constable Ministerial (HCM) 2026 applications are submitted online at [rectt.bsf.gov.in](https://rectt.bsf.gov.in). The **last date to apply and pay the fee** is specified in the official notification. Do not wait until the final day — server load increases on the deadline, leading to timeouts and submission failures.

## Application Timeline

| Event | Date |
|-------|------|
| Notification released | As per official announcement |
| Online Application Start | As per notification |
| Last Date to Apply | Check rectt.bsf.gov.in |
| Last Date for Fee Payment | Same as application last date (or 1 day after) |
| Admit Card | 2–3 weeks before exam |
| Written Exam | As per schedule |

*All dates are announced in the official BSF HCM 2026 notification at [rectt.bsf.gov.in](https://rectt.bsf.gov.in)*

## How to Apply for BSF Head Constable 2026

1. **Register:** Go to [rectt.bsf.gov.in](https://rectt.bsf.gov.in) → new user registration → enter email and mobile
2. **Login** with registration credentials
3. **Fill Application Form:**
   - Personal details (name, DOB, category, address)
   - Educational qualification details
   - Preferred exam centre
   - Employment status (employed/unemployed)
4. **Upload Documents:**
   - Passport-size photo (JPG, max 20KB)
   - Signature (JPG, max 10KB)
   - Caste/category certificate (if applicable)
5. **Pay Application Fee:**
   - General / OBC: ₹100
   - SC / ST / Ex-Servicemen / Female: Nil
   - Payment via SBI net banking / credit/debit card / UPI
6. **Submit and Print** acknowledgment slip — note your application/registration number

## Documents Needed at Application Stage

You'll need scanned copies ready:
- Class 12th mark sheet (to verify qualification)
- Date of birth proof (10th certificate)
- Category certificate (OBC Non-Creamy Layer / SC / ST — issued by competent authority)
- Aadhaar card (for identity verification during application)

Original documents are verified only at the document verification stage after all exam stages.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Wrong category selection:** OBC candidates must have a Non-Creamy Layer certificate; central OBC certificate must be used (state OBC doesn't apply for central posts)
- **Photo specifications:** Ensure the photo shows your face clearly against a white background; a blurry or selfie photo may get rejected
- **Late submission:** Do not submit on the last day — apply at least 3 days before deadline

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I edit my BSF HCM application after submission?**
BSF typically allows a correction window for basic details (name, DOB) shortly after the application period ends. Major corrections (category change, qualification) are usually not permitted. Check the notification for the correction window dates.

**Q: Is there an offline application option for BSF HCM 2026?**
No. Applications are online only at [rectt.bsf.gov.in](https://rectt.bsf.gov.in). Physical application forms are not accepted.

**Q: I missed the BSF HCM 2026 last date — can I still apply?**
No. The portal closes at the deadline and late submissions are not accepted under any circumstances. Wait for the next recruitment cycle.
