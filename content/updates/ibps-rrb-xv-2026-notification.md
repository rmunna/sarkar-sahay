## What is IBPS RRB XV?

The IBPS RRB XV examination is a highly anticipated recruitment drive conducted by the Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) to fill various Group "A" - Officers (Scale I, II & III) and Group "B" - Office Assistant (Multipurpose) posts in Regional Rural Banks (RRBs) across India. This annual examination provides a significant opportunity for aspiring candidates to secure a stable and rewarding career in the rural banking sector, which plays a crucial role in India's financial inclusion and economic development.

Regional Rural Banks are government-sponsored commercial banks that operate at the regional level in various states of India. They are designed to cater to the banking and financial needs of the rural population. The IBPS RRB XV notification signifies the commencement of the selection process for thousands of vacancies across these banks, offering diverse roles from customer service and clerical duties (Office Assistant) to managerial and specialized positions (Officer Scales).

Successful candidates will be allocated to one of the participating RRBs based on their merit, preference, and the availability of vacancies in different states. This recruitment is vital for strengthening the workforce of RRBs, ensuring efficient banking services, and contributing to the socio-economic upliftment of rural areas. It's an excellent opportunity for graduates seeking a government job with good growth prospects and a chance to contribute to the nation's rural development agenda.

## Selection Process

The selection process for IBPS RRB XV is rigorous and designed to assess candidates' aptitude, knowledge, and suitability for banking roles. It typically involves multiple stages, as confirmed by the official source:

1.  **Preliminary Examination:**
    *   This is the first stage for Office Assistant (Multipurpose) and Officer Scale I.
    *   It is an online objective test.
    *   Candidates must clear the sectional as well as overall cut-off marks to qualify for the Main Examination.
    *   Marks obtained in the Preliminary Exam are only for qualification and are not counted in the final merit list.

2.  **Main Examination:**
    *   Candidates who qualify the Preliminary Exam (for Office Assistant and Officer Scale I) or directly apply (for Officer Scale II & III) proceed to the Main Examination.
    *   This is also an online objective test, but with a higher level of difficulty and more sections compared to the Preliminary Exam.
    *   For Office Assistant, the Main Exam is the final written stage.
    *   For Officer Scale I, the Main Exam is followed by an Interview.
    *   For Officer Scale II & III, there is a single online examination followed by an Interview.
    *   Marks obtained in the Main Examination are considered for the final merit list.

3.  **Interview (for Officer Posts Only):**
    *   Candidates who qualify the Main Examination for Officer Scale I, II, and III will be called for an Interview.
    *   The Interview is conducted by the Nodal Regional Rural Bank in each state/UT, with the assistance of IBPS.
    *   The Interview assesses candidates' communication skills, personality, general awareness, and banking aptitude.
    *   The final merit list for Officer posts is prepared based on the combined scores of the Main Examination and Interview.

4.  **Document Verification:**
    *   Candidates provisionally selected after the Main Exam (for Office Assistant) or Main Exam + Interview (for Officer posts) will undergo document verification.
    *   All original documents related to age, educational qualification, caste, disability, etc., will be scrutinized. Any discrepancy will lead to disqualification.

5.  **Medical Examination:**
    *   A medical fitness test may be conducted as per the norms of the respective Regional Rural Bank. This is a standard procedure to ensure candidates are medically fit for the job.

6.  **Final Merit List & Provisional Allotment:**
    *   A final merit list is prepared based on the combined scores (Main Exam for Office Assistant; Main Exam + Interview for Officer posts).
    *   Candidates are then provisionally allotted to one of the participating Regional Rural Banks based on their merit, preferences, and available vacancies.

4.  **Click on "Apply Online":** Once you have read the notification, click on the "Click here to Apply Online for CRP RRBs-XV" link.
5.  **New Registration:** If you are a new user, click on "Click here for New Registration." Provide your basic details like name, mobile number, and email ID to generate a provisional registration number and password. Note these down carefully.
6.  **Fill the Application Form:** Log in using your registration number and password. Fill in all the required details in the online application form, including personal information, educational qualifications, and preferred posts/states. Ensure all information is accurate.
7.  **Upload Documents:** Upload scanned copies of your photograph, signature, left thumb impression, and a handwritten declaration as per the specifications mentioned in the notification (size, format, dimensions).

9.  **Pay Application Fee:** Proceed to the payment gateway and pay the application fee online using your preferred method (Debit Card, Credit Card, Net Banking, etc.).
