## What is SSC GD Constable 2026?

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has released the GD Constable 2026 notification for recruitment of **25,487 Constable (General Duty)** posts across Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs), Secretariat Security Force (SSF), and Rifleman (GD) in Assam Rifles. The application window has closed, and the Computer-Based Test (CBT) is scheduled to begin from **23 February 2026 onwards**.

This is one of the largest central government recruitments for 10th-pass candidates, offering a stable career in uniformed services with a pay scale of Rs. 21,700 – Rs. 69,100 (Level 3, 7th CPC).

## Selection Process

The SSC GD Constable selection involves 5 stages:

1. **Computer-Based Test (CBT)** — 160 marks (main merit stage)
2. **Physical Efficiency Test (PET)** — Qualifying only (running test)
3. **Physical Standard Test (PST)** — Height, chest, weight measurement
4. **Document Verification** — Original certificates checked
5. **Medical Examination** — Final fitness check

Approximately 8 times the total vacancies are called for PET/PST based on CBT performance.

Each question carries 2 marks. There is negative marking of 0.50 marks for every wrong answer.

## Syllabus Overview

- **General Intelligence & Reasoning:** Analogies, classification, coding-decoding, series, puzzles, blood relations, directions
- **General Knowledge:** Current affairs, history, geography, polity, economics, general science
- **Elementary Mathematics:** Number system, percentages, ratio, profit-loss, time-speed-distance, basic geometry
- **English / Hindi:** Reading comprehension, fill in the blanks, error spotting, synonyms-antonyms, basic grammar

## Preparation Tips

1. **Focus on Mathematics and Reasoning** — these are scoring sections with less ambiguity
2. **Read daily current affairs** — GK section covers recent events from the last 6–8 months
3. **Practice previous year papers** — SSC repeats question patterns; solve at least 10 previous papers
4. **Time management** — 80 questions in 60 minutes means 45 seconds per question; don't spend too long on one question
5. **Prepare for PET early** — start running practice now, as the physical test eliminates many candidates

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: What is the SSC GD Constable 2026 exam date?

The CBT exam is scheduled to begin from **February 23, 2026 onwards**. Exact slot dates will be shared via admit card.

### Q: How many vacancies are there in SSC GD 2026?

There are **25,487 vacancies** — 23,467 for male candidates and 2,020 for female candidates across BSF, CISF, CRPF, SSB, ITBP, Assam Rifles and SSF.

### Q: What is the educational qualification for SSC GD 2026?

Candidates must have passed **Matriculation (10th Class)** from a recognised Board or University by January 1, 2026.

### Q: Is there negative marking in SSC GD Constable exam?

Yes, **0.50 marks are deducted** for every wrong answer. Each correct answer carries 2 marks.

SSC GD Constable gets **Level 3 pay (Rs. 21,700 – Rs. 69,100)** under the 7th CPC, plus allowances like DA, HRA, and ration allowance.

No, the application deadline was **December 31, 2025**. The application window is now closed.
