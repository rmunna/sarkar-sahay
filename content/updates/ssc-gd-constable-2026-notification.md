## What is SSC GD Constable Recruitment?

SSC GD (General Duty) Constable recruitment is conducted by the Staff Selection Commission (SSC) for Constable posts in Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs) — BSF, CISF, CRPF, SSB, ITBP — as well as Assam Rifles, Secretariat Security Force (SSF), and NCB. This is one of India's largest government job recruitments.

For 2026, SSC has announced **25,487 vacancies** for GD Constable posts. The application window has closed, and the **Computer Based Test (CBT) begins from February 23, 2026**. Candidates who applied should download their admit cards from ssc.gov.in.

- **Age:** 18–23 years as on January 1, 2026 (relaxation for reserved categories as per government rules — 3 years for OBC, 5 years for SC/ST)

## Selection Process

1. **Computer Based Test (CBT)** — Online exam with MCQs
2. **Physical Efficiency Test (PET) & Physical Standard Test (PST)** — Running, height, chest measurement
3. **Medical Examination** — Detailed medical fitness check
4. **Document Verification** — Original documents verification

## Syllabus Overview

- **General Intelligence & Reasoning:** Analogies, Similarities, Differences, Spatial Visualization, Coding-Decoding, Number Series
- **General Knowledge:** Current Affairs, Indian History, Geography, Indian Polity, Economics, General Science
- **Elementary Mathematics:** Number System, Percentage, Ratio & Proportion, Profit & Loss, Time & Distance, Algebra, Geometry
- **English/Hindi:** Reading Comprehension, Fill in the Blanks, Error Spotting, Synonyms/Antonyms, Idioms & Phrases

## Preparation Tips

1. **Focus on speed** — 80 questions in 60 minutes means less than 1 minute per question. Practice with a timer
2. **GK & Current Affairs** — Read a daily newspaper or current affairs app. Focus on last 6 months events
3. **Mathematics basics** — Master shortcut methods for percentage, ratio, and time-speed-distance problems
4. **Start physical training now** — If you clear CBT, PET/PST comes next. Begin running practice immediately
5. **Solve SSC previous papers** — SSC repeats question patterns. Practice GD papers from 2022, 2023, 2024
