## How to Check TN 10th & 12th Supplementary Results 2026

Checking your Tamil Nadu Class 10th or 12th Supplementary Examination result is a straightforward process. Follow these steps to access your scores:

1.  **Visit the Official Website:** Open your web browser and go to either of the official TNDGE result portals: `https://dge.tn.gov.in` or `https://tnresults.nic.in`.
2.  **Locate the Result Link:** On the homepage, look for the dedicated link for "SSLC Supplementary Exam Result 2026" or "HSE(+2) Supplementary Exam Result 2026". The exact link name may vary slightly but will be clearly visible.
3.  **Click on the Link:** Click on the relevant result link for your class (10th or 12th).
4.  **Enter Credentials:** You will be redirected to a new page where you need to enter your exam Roll Number and Date of Birth (DD/MM/YYYY) in the specified fields.
5.  **Submit Details:** After entering the required information, click on the "Get Marks" or "Submit" button.
6.  **View and Download Result:** Your provisional mark sheet will be displayed on the screen. Carefully check all the details. You can then download and print a copy of your result for future reference.

## Details Mentioned on Your Provisional Mark Sheet

Once you access your result, ensure all the information displayed is accurate. Your provisional mark sheet will typically include the following details:

*   Student's Name
*   Roll Number
*   Registration Number
*   Date of Birth
*   Father's Name
*   Mother's Name
*   School Name and Code
*   Subject-wise Marks Obtained (Theory and Practical, if applicable)
*   Total Marks Obtained
*   Grades (if applicable)
*   Overall Result (Pass/Fail/Compartment)
*   Remarks (if any)
*   Examination Name (e.g., SSLC Supplementary Exam 2026)

In case of any discrepancy in your personal details or marks, contact your school authorities immediately.

## What to Do After Checking Your Results

After successfully checking and downloading your provisional mark sheet, here are the next steps you should consider:

1.  **Save/Print Your Provisional Mark Sheet:** This document is crucial for college admissions, job applications, or other immediate needs until the original mark sheet is issued.
2.  **Collect Original Mark Sheet:** Your original mark sheet and other certificates will be distributed by your respective schools a few days or weeks after the online declaration of results. Keep an eye on announcements from your school or the TNDGE website for the exact dates.
3.  **Re-evaluation/Re-totalling (if needed):** If you are not satisfied with your marks, TNDGE usually provides an option for re-evaluation or re-totalling of answer scripts. Details and application procedures for this will be announced shortly on the official websites.
4.  **Plan Your Next Steps:** Based on your results, start planning for your higher education, vocational training, or career path. Consult with teachers, counsellors, or parents for guidance.

**A2:** To check your results, you will need your exam Roll Number and your Date of Birth (in DD/MM/YYYY format).
