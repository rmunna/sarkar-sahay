## What is KPSC FDA SDA Recruitment 2026?

The Karnataka Public Service Commission (KPSC), through the Karnataka Examination Authority (KEA), released the **FDA SDA Recruitment 2026 notification on 8 October 2025**, announcing **178 vacancies** for First Division Assistant (FDA) and Second Division Assistant (SDA) posts across various Karnataka government departments.

**FDA (First Division Assistant)** is a upper-division clerical post requiring a Bachelor's degree. **SDA (Second Division Assistant)** is a lower-division clerical post open to 12th/PUC pass candidates. Both posts carry job security, pension benefits, and a clear promotion path within Karnataka government service.

The application window closed on **14 November 2025**. The written examination was conducted as an OMR-based offline test. Results have been declared at [cetonline.karnataka.gov.in](https://cetonline.karnataka.gov.in) — candidates who appeared can check their status there.

> **Looking for the next KPSC FDA SDA notification?** KPSC typically issues a fresh recruitment cycle every 2–3 years. The previous major recruitment (2022) had 1,112 vacancies. Bookmark [kpsc.kar.nic.in](https://kpsc.kar.nic.in) and check back regularly.

## Selection Process

The KPSC FDA SDA 2026 selection involves three stages:

1. **Written Examination (OMR-based)**
   - FDA: 3 papers (General Kannada 150 marks + General English 150 marks + General Knowledge 100 marks = 400 marks total)
   - SDA: 2 papers (General Kannada & English 150 marks + General Knowledge & Arithmetic 100 marks = 250 marks total)
   - Offline OMR-based objective test; no negative marking
   - Conducted in Kannada and English medium

2. **Document Verification**
   - Shortlisted candidates are called to verify original certificates (degree/PUC, DOB, caste, domicile, Kannada language certificate)

3. **Medical Examination**
   - Final step before appointment; candidates must meet the medical fitness standards specified by Karnataka government
