## MHT CET 2026 Result — Expected in Mid-June 2026

The Maharashtra State Common Entrance Test Cell (CET Cell) is expected to declare the **MHT CET 2026 result in mid-June 2026**. MHT CET 2026 was held in two phases — the PCM (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics) group from April 22–30, 2026, and the PCB (Physics, Chemistry, Biology) group from May 2–4, 2026.

> **Note:** The result date has not been officially announced by CET Cell as of June 2, 2026. This page will be updated immediately when the result is declared. Check **mahacet.org** for official updates.

The result will be available as a percentile-based score card. MHT CET does not use a merit rank but assigns percentiles based on performance within each session.

## How to Check MHT CET 2026 Result

When results are declared, follow these steps:

1. Go to the official website — **mahacet.org** or **cetcell.mahacet.org**
2. Click on the **"MHT CET 2026 Result"** link on the homepage
3. Enter your **Application Number** and **Date of Birth**
4. Click Submit — your score card will appear on screen
5. Download and save your score card as PDF

## MHT CET 2026 Score Card — Details Mentioned

Your score card will contain:
- Candidate name and application number
- Subject-wise marks (out of 100 each — Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry or Biology)
- **Percentile score** (separate for PCM and PCB groups)
- Category and sub-category

## MHT CET 2026 Qualifying Marks

MHT CET does not have a fixed passing percentage. Candidates need to meet a **minimum percentile** set during the CAP (Centralised Admission Process) counselling, which varies by category and college:

| Category | General Cut (Typical) |
|----------|----------------------|
| General / Open | ~90+ percentile for top colleges |
| OBC | ~80–85 percentile |
| SC / ST / VJ / NT | ~50–70 percentile |

*Actual cutoffs are determined during CAP rounds and vary by college and course. Do not treat the above as official cutoffs for 2026.*

## After Result — MHT CET 2026 Counselling (CAP)

Post result, CET Cell conducts the **Centralised Admission Process (CAP)** for admission to:
- Engineering colleges in Maharashtra (via PCM percentile)
- Pharmacy and Agriculture courses (via PCB percentile)

CAP typically involves:
1. Registration and document upload
2. Preference filling (choice filling)
3. Multiple allotment rounds (CAP Round 1, 2, 3)
4. Option Form confirmation and institute reporting

CAP dates will be announced after the result. Check **mahacet.org** for the official CAP schedule.
