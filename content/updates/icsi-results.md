### How to Check Your ICSI CS June 2026 Results 

Follow these simple steps to access and download your ICSI CS Executive or Professional Programme June 2026 examination results:

1.  **Visit the Official ICSI Website**: Open your web browser and go to `https://www.icsi.edu`. 
2.  **Navigate to Results Section**: Look for the 'Results' or 'Student' section on the homepage. There will typically be a prominent link for 'Results of CS Executive & Professional Programme Examinations, June 2026'. 
3.  **Select Your Programme**: Click on the link corresponding to your programme – 'CS Executive Programme' or 'CS Professional Programme'. 
4.  **Enter Credentials**: You will be redirected to a login page. Enter your **17-digit Registration Number** and your **Roll Number**. Ensure these details are entered accurately as per your admit card. 
5.  **View and Download Result**: After entering the details, click on 'Submit' or 'Get Result'. Your detailed mark sheet will be displayed on the screen. 
6.  **Print/Save**: It is highly recommended to download the e-mark sheet and take a printout for your records and future reference. This digital copy serves as an immediate proof of your performance.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Q1: When were the ICSI CS June 2026 examinations conducted?**
A1: The examinations for the CS Executive and Professional Programmes for the June 2026 session were typically conducted in the first two weeks of June 2026. Exact dates can be found on the ICSI examination calendar.

**Q2: How can I get my physical mark sheet?**
A2: ICSI usually dispatches the physical mark sheets to the candidates’ registered postal addresses within a few weeks of the online result declaration. There is no need to apply separately for it.

**Q3: What should I do if I forget my 17-digit Registration Number or Roll Number?**
A3: You can usually retrieve your Registration Number through the student portal on the ICSI website (`https://www.icsi.edu/student`) by entering other personal details. Your Roll Number is mentioned on your admit card.

**Q4: Can I apply for re-evaluation of my answer sheets?**
A4: ICSI does not offer a re-evaluation facility. However, candidates can apply for 'Verification of Marks' to ensure correct totalling and that all answers have been evaluated. They can also apply for 'Inspection' or 'Certified Copies' of their answer books.

**Q5: What are the passing criteria for ICSI CS exams?**
A5: To pass a module, a candidate must secure a minimum of 40% marks in each individual paper and an aggregate of 50% marks in all papers of that module/group. Exemption in individual papers is also provided if certain conditions are met.

**Q6: When is the registration for the December 2026 session starting?**
A6: The registration window for the December 2026 examination session is expected to open soon after the declaration of June 2026 results. Candidates are advised to regularly check the official ICSI website (`https://www.icsi.edu`) for exact notification and deadlines.
