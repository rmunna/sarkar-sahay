## TS EAMCET 2026 Result — Declared on May 17

JNTUH (Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University Hyderabad), on behalf of TSCHE, declared the **TS EAMCET 2026 (TG EAPCET) result on May 17, 2026**. Candidates can download their rank cards from the official website **eapcet.tgche.ac.in**.

## Qualifying Statistics — TS EAMCET 2026

| Stream | Appeared | Qualified | Pass Percentage |
|--------|----------|-----------|-----------------|
| Engineering (MPC) | 1,97,241 | 1,44,704 | 73.36% |
| Agriculture & Pharmacy (BiPC) | 84,954 | 73,294 | 86.27% |

## How to Download TS EAMCET 2026 Rank Card

1. Visit the official website **[eapcet.tgche.ac.in](https://eapcet.tgche.ac.in)**
2. Click on **"TS EAMCET 2026 Result / Rank Card"**
3. Enter your **Registration Number**
4. Enter your **Hall Ticket Number**
5. Enter your **Date of Birth**
6. Click **Submit**
7. Your rank card will appear — download and save it

## What the TS EAMCET 2026 Rank Card Contains

- Name of the candidate
- Hall ticket number
- Marks scored in the exam
- Normalized score
- Category
- IPE (Intermediate Public Exam) marks
- EAMCET weightage
- Final rank allotted

## What Happens Next — TS EAMCET 2026 Counselling

Qualified candidates will be called for web-based counselling conducted by the **Telangana State Council of Higher Education (TSCHE)**. Counselling will cover seat allotment for:

- B.Tech / B.E. Engineering colleges in Telangana (Engineering stream)
- B.Sc Agriculture / B.Pharmacy / Pharm.D courses (Agriculture & Pharmacy stream)

Counselling dates will be announced on **[tsche.ac.in](https://tsche.ac.in)**. Candidates are advised to keep the following documents ready: rank card, hall ticket, Class 10 and 12 mark sheets, category certificate (if applicable), income certificate, and Aadhaar card.
