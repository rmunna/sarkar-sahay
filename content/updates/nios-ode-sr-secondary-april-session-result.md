## NIOS ODE Sr Secondary April 2026 Session Result — Overview

The National Institute of Open Schooling (NIOS) has officially declared the results for the NIOS ODE (On-Demand Examination) Senior Secondary April 2026 Session. This result pertains to students who appeared for their Class 12 examinations conducted by NIOS. The results were declared on **August 5, 2026**. Students who appeared for these examinations can now check their individual results online through the official NIOS results portal. The official notification does not specify the total number of candidates who appeared or the overall pass percentage for this particular session. However, individual mark sheets are available for viewing and download.

## How to Check Your Result Online

To check your NIOS ODE Senior Secondary April 2026 Session result, please follow the step-by-step guide below. Ensure you have your Roll Number handy before proceeding.

1.  **Visit the Official NIOS Results Website:** Open your web browser and navigate to the official NIOS results portal: [results.nios.ac.in](https://results.nios.ac.in).
2.  **Locate the Result Link:** On the homepage, look for a section or link related to "Results" or "Public Examination Result."
3.  **Select the Examination:** Find and click on the specific link for "NIOS ODE Senior Secondary April 2026 Session Result" or a similar designation for the Senior Secondary On-Demand Examination.
4.  **Enter Your Roll Number:** A new page will open where you will be prompted to enter your NIOS Roll Number. Carefully type in your unique Roll Number as it appears on your admit card.
5.  **Submit Your Details:** After entering your Roll Number, click on the "Submit" or "View Result" button.
6.  **View and Download Your Result:** Your NIOS Senior Secondary April 2026 Session result will be displayed on the screen. You can view your subject-wise marks, overall status, and other relevant details. It is highly recommended to download and print a copy of your provisional mark sheet for future reference.
