The Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI) has officially declared the results for the **CA Intermediate and CA Foundation examinations** held in January 2026. Candidates who appeared for the exams can now check their results and download scorecards from the official ICAI website at **icai.nic.in**.

The January 2026 session exams were conducted on **January 6, 8, 10, 12, 15, and 17, 2026** across multiple centres in India.

## CA Foundation January 2026 Topper

**Logapriya PP** has secured **All India Rank 1 (AIR 1)** in the CA Foundation January 2026 examination. Congratulations to Logapriya and all successful candidates!

## How to Check CA Inter & Foundation Result January 2026

Follow these steps to check your result online:

**Step 1:** Visit the official ICAI result portal at [icai.nic.in/caresult/](https://icai.nic.in/caresult/).

**Step 2:** Select your examination — either **CA Intermediate** or **CA Foundation** — for the January 2026 session.

**Step 3:** Enter your **Registration Number** or **PIN Number** as displayed on your admit card.

**Step 4:** Enter the **captcha code** shown on the screen.

**Step 5:** Click on **Submit**. Your result will be displayed on the screen.

**Step 6:** Download or print your **scorecard** for future reference.

## How to Download Your Scorecard

1. Go to [icai.nic.in/caresult/](https://icai.nic.in/caresult/).
2. Log in using your registration number.
3. Once your result is displayed, click on **Download Scorecard**.
4. Save the PDF and take a printout for your records.

The scorecard contains your subject-wise marks, total score, and pass/fail status. Keep it safe as it may be required during articleship registration and future exam registrations.

## What to Do After Passing

### For CA Foundation Candidates
- Register for the **CA Intermediate course** through the ICAI portal.
- Complete the required **Integrated Course on Information Technology and Soft Skills (ICITSS)** training.
- Begin preparation for the CA Intermediate examination.

### For CA Inter Candidates
- Register for **3 years of articleship** training under a practising Chartered Accountant.
- Complete the **Advanced Integrated Course on Information Technology and Soft Skills (AICITSS)** training.
- Begin preparation for the **CA Final examination** while completing articleship.

### For Candidates Who Did Not Pass
- Don't lose hope. You can appear in the **next attempt** (May 2026 session).
- Apply for **certified copies** of answer sheets if you want to review your answers.
- The deadline for certified copy applications is usually within 30 days of result declaration — check the ICAI website for exact dates.
