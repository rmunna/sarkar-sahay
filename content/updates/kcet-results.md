## KCET 2026 Results Declared: Check Your Scores and Rank Card Now!

The Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) has officially declared the results for the Karnataka Common Entrance Test (KCET) 2026 today, June 7, 2026. Candidates who appeared for the entrance examination can now access their scores and rank cards on the KEA's official websites. This highly anticipated announcement paves the way for admissions to various undergraduate courses, including Engineering, Pharmacy, Architecture, Farm Science, and AYUSH (Ayurveda, Yoga and Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, and Homoeopathy) courses across institutions in Karnataka for the academic year 2026-27.

The KCET examination is a crucial gateway for aspiring students in Karnataka. With the results now out, candidates should meticulously check their scores, ranks, and begin preparing for the subsequent counselling rounds, which will determine their college and course allocation.

## How to Check and Download Your KCET 2026 Result and Rank Card

Follow these simple steps to access your KCET 2026 results and download your provisional rank card:

1.  **Visit the Official KEA Website:** Go to either `https://kea.kar.nic.in` or `https://cet.kar.nic.in`. Look for the "KCET 2026 Results" or "KCET 2026 Rank Card" link prominently displayed on the homepage.
2.  **Click on the Result Link:** Click on the activated link for KCET 2026 Results. You might be redirected to a dedicated result portal, such as `https://karresults.nic.in`.
3.  **Enter Your Credentials:** You will be prompted to enter your KCET 2026 Registration Number. Some portals may also ask for the first four characters of your name or your date of birth as an additional verification step.
4.  **Submit Details:** After entering the required information, click on the "Submit" or "View Result" button.
5.  **View and Download Your Result:** Your KCET 2026 result and provisional rank card will be displayed on the screen. Carefully review all the details.
6.  **Print for Future Reference:** It is highly recommended to download and take a printout of your result and rank card. This document will be essential for the upcoming counselling process and document verification.

## Details Mentioned on Your KCET 2026 Scorecard

Your KCET 2026 rank card will contain important information, including:

*   Candidate's Name
*   KCET 2026 Roll Number/Application Number
*   Parent's Name
*   Subject-wise Marks (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics/Biology)
*   Total Marks Obtained in KCET 2026
*   Overall KCET Rank (Engineering, Pharmacy, Agriculture, etc., as applicable)
*   Qualifying Status
*   Other relevant personal details

Ensure all the details printed on your scorecard are accurate. In case of any discrepancy, contact the KEA examination authority immediately.

## What's Next? KCET 2026 Counselling Process

After the declaration of KCET 2026 results, the next crucial step is the counselling process. KEA conducts a centralized online counselling process for seat allotment based on KCET ranks. Here’s a general overview of the steps involved:

1.  **Document Verification:** Candidates will be required to get their original documents verified at designated centres. An official schedule for document verification will be released by KEA.
2.  **Option Entry:** Based on their verified documents and eligibility, candidates can fill in their preferred choices of colleges and courses online.
3.  **Mock Allotment:** KEA conducts a mock allotment round to give candidates an idea of the seats they might get based on their choices and ranks.
4.  **Real Allotment Rounds:** Following the mock allotment, actual seat allotment rounds will take place. Candidates will be allotted seats based on their merit, preferences, and seat availability.
5.  **Reporting to Colleges:** Candidates who are allotted seats must download their admission orders and report to the respective colleges within the stipulated timeframe to confirm their admission.

**Important Note:** The detailed schedule for KCET 2026 counselling, including dates for document verification, option entry, and seat allotment, will be released shortly by KEA on their official website (`kea.kar.nic.in`). Candidates are advised to regularly visit the official website for the latest updates.
