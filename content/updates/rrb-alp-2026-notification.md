## Selection Process

The RRB ALP selection process consists of four stages:

1. **CBT 1 (Computer Based Test 1)** — Screening test with questions on Mathematics, General Intelligence & Reasoning, General Science, and General Awareness
2. **CBT 2 (Computer Based Test 2)** — Part A (General subjects) and Part B (Trade-specific questions)
3. **CBAT (Computer Based Aptitude Test)** — Tests psychological aptitude for loco pilot duties
4. **Document Verification & Medical Examination** — Final stage with fitness standards check

## Exam Pattern

### CBT 1
- Total Questions: 75
- Total Marks: 75
- Duration: 60 minutes
- Sections: Mathematics (20), General Intelligence & Reasoning (25), General Science (20), General Awareness (10)
- Negative Marking: 1/3 mark deducted for wrong answers

### CBT 2
- Part A: 100 questions (100 marks) — 90 minutes
- Part B: 75 questions (75 marks) — 60 minutes (trade-specific)

## Syllabus (Brief Overview)

- **Mathematics:** Number Systems, BODMAS, Decimals, Fractions, LCM & HCF, Ratio & Proportion, Percentages, Mensuration, Time & Work, Time & Distance, Simple & Compound Interest, Profit & Loss, Algebra, Geometry, Trigonometry, Statistics
- **General Intelligence & Reasoning:** Analogies, Alphabetical & Number Series, Coding & Decoding, Mathematical Operations, Relationships, Syllogism, Venn Diagrams, Data Interpretation, Conclusions
- **General Science:** Physics, Chemistry, and Life Sciences (up to 10th standard NCERT level)
- **General Awareness:** Current Affairs, Indian History, Economy, Polity, General Knowledge

## Preparation Tips

1. **Start with NCERT books** for General Science — focus on Physics and Chemistry concepts from Class 9-10
2. **Practice Mathematics daily** — speed and accuracy matter in CBT; use previous year papers
3. **Focus on Reasoning shortcuts** — learn pattern recognition techniques for series and coding questions
4. **Read current affairs monthly** — follow a reliable monthly GK magazine for the last 6 months
5. **Take mock tests regularly** — simulate exam conditions with timed tests to build speed
