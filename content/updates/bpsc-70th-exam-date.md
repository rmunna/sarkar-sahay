**Detailed Timeline Expectation:**

## Admit Card / Hall Ticket Date

The **Admit Card**, also known as the Hall Ticket, is a mandatory document for appearing in any stage of the BPSC examination. Without a valid admit card, candidates will not be permitted to enter the examination hall.

**Release Date:**
The admit card for each stage (Prelims, Mains, Interview) is typically released **7 to 10 days prior to the respective examination date**. As the exam dates for BPSC 70th 2026 are not yet announced, the admit card release dates are also **not yet announced — check bpsc.bihar.gov.in**.

**How to Download:**
Candidates must download their admit cards from the official BPSC website. No physical admit cards are sent by post. Follow these steps:

1.  Visit the official BPSC website: **bpsc.bihar.gov.in**.
2.  Look for the "Apply Online" or "Admit Card" section on the homepage.
3.  Click on the link for "70th BPSC CCE Prelims/Mains/Interview Admit Card" (as applicable).
4.  You will be redirected to a login page. Enter your **Registration Number** and **Password** (or Date of Birth, as specified).
5.  After successful login, your admit card will be displayed on the screen.
6.  Verify all the details mentioned on the admit card, including your name, photograph, signature, roll number, exam date, time, and venue.
7.  Download the admit card and take **at least two printouts**. It is advisable to keep a digital copy as well.

**Important Points Regarding Admit Card:**

*   Ensure your photograph and signature on the admit card are clear and visible.
*   If there are any discrepancies in your admit card, contact the BPSC helpline immediately for rectification before the exam date.
*   Carry one printout to the exam center and keep another as a backup.

## Exam Centers and City Allocation

BPSC conducts its examinations across various cities and districts within Bihar. The allocation of exam centers is a critical aspect for candidates.

**How Cities Are Allotted:**
During the online application process, candidates are usually asked to provide their preference for exam centers. However, the **final allocation of exam centers rests solely with the BPSC**. The Commission endeavors to allot centers based on the preferences indicated by candidates, the number of applicants from a particular region, and the availability of suitable examination venues. Typically, centers are allocated in district headquarters across Bihar. The specific exam center (venue name and address) will be clearly mentioned on your admit card.

**Can You Change Center?**
**Generally, no, candidates cannot request a change in their allotted exam center once the admit card has been issued.** BPSC has a stringent policy regarding center changes to maintain the integrity and logistical efficiency of the examination process. Requests for changes are rarely entertained and only under extremely exceptional circumstances (e.g., a genuine medical emergency or an administrative error on the part of BPSC), which would require direct communication with the Commission and their discretion. Aspirants should therefore be prepared to appear at the center assigned to them. It is advisable to check the exact location of your exam center a few days before the exam to plan your travel accordingly.

Punctuality and adherence to the exam day timeline are paramount for a smooth examination experience. The following timeline is typical for the BPSC Preliminary Examination. Mains examination will have different timings for multiple papers spread across days.

| Activity               | Time (Approximate for Prelims) |

| **Exam Start Time**    | 12:00 PM                       |
| **Exam End Time**      | 02:00 PM                       |

**Detailed Explanation:**

*   **Reporting Time:** Candidates are advised to reach the examination center at least **1.5 to 2 hours before the scheduled start time** of the exam. This allows ample time for security checks, frisking, verification of admit cards and identity proofs, and locating your designated seat.
*   **Gate Closing Time:** The gates of the examination center are strictly closed **30 minutes before the exam commences**. No candidate will be allowed entry after the gate closing time, irrespective of the reason. It is crucial to adhere to this deadline to avoid last-minute stress and disqualification.
*   **Exam Start Time:** The question paper and OMR sheet will be distributed, and the invigilator will announce the start of the examination.
*   **Exam End Time:** The invigilator will announce the end of the examination. Candidates must stop writing immediately and hand over their OMR sheet and question booklet (if required) to the invigilator.

For the Mains examination, there are usually two papers conducted on a single day, with a lunch break in between, or papers are spread across different days. Specific timings for Mains will be provided in the Mains admit card and instructions.

## Exam Pattern on the Day

Understanding the exam pattern is crucial for effective time management and strategy on the examination day. This section primarily focuses on the Preliminary Examination pattern, which is the first hurdle.

The BPSC Preliminary Examination consists of a single paper: **General Studies**.

*   **Number of Sections:** The Prelims paper is generally considered one comprehensive section covering various subjects.
*   **Question Type:** The questions are **Objective Type, Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)**. Each question will have five options (A, B, C, D, E), out of which only one is the correct answer.
*   **Total Questions:** There are typically **150 questions** in the Prelims paper.
*   **Total Marks:** The paper is for **150 marks**, with each question carrying 1 mark.
*   **Duration:** The duration of the Prelims exam is **2 hours**.
*   **Negative Marking:** BPSC has introduced negative marking in the Prelims. Typically, **1/3rd or 1/4th of the marks allotted to the question** is deducted for each incorrect answer. It is imperative for candidates to be aware of the exact negative marking scheme mentioned in the official notification and on the question paper itself. Unattempted questions do not incur negative marks.
*   **Medium:** The question paper is usually bilingual (Hindi and English).

The Main Examination, on the other hand, is descriptive in nature, requiring candidates to write detailed answers. It comprises General Hindi (qualifying), General Studies Papers I & II, and an Optional Subject paper. The interview is a personality test.

## What to Carry

To ensure a hassle-free experience on exam day, candidates must carry only the permitted items.

**Mandatory Items:**

*   **Valid Admit Card:** Two printouts of your downloaded admit card. Ensure all details are clear.
*   **Original Photo Identity Proof:** A valid, original photo ID such as Aadhar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Passport, Driving License, or any other government-issued ID. The name on the ID must match the name on the admit card.
*   **Two Recent Passport-Size Photographs:** These should ideally be the same as the one uploaded during the application process. These might be required for attendance sheets or in case of any verification.
*   **Blue/Black Ballpoint Pen:** For marking answers on the OMR sheet (Prelims) or writing descriptive answers (Mains). Gel pens or fountain pens are generally not allowed for OMR sheets.

**Permitted (Check Specific Instructions):**

*   **Transparent Water Bottle:** A clear, transparent water bottle without any labels is often allowed.
*   **Small Hand Sanitizer:** A small, transparent bottle of hand sanitizer (50ml or 100ml) might be permitted, especially in the context of health advisories.

**Items Strictly NOT Allowed:**

*   Any electronic gadgets: Mobile phones, smartwatches, calculators (unless specifically allowed for certain optional papers in Mains, which is rare), Bluetooth devices, earphones, etc.
*   Study material: Books, notes, loose papers, chits.
*   Bags, wallets, purses.
*   Jewelry or metallic items (except for religious symbols if they do not obstruct frisking).
*   Any food items (unless for medical reasons with prior permission).
*   Any kind of communication device.

Candidates found with prohibited items will be disqualified and may face further disciplinary action.

## FAQs

**Q1: What if the BPSC 70th exam date clashes with another competitive exam I have applied for?**
A: BPSC generally does not reschedule its examinations for individual candidate clashes with other exams. The Commission announces its exam calendar well in advance. Candidates are usually expected to prioritize and choose which exam they wish to appear for. It is advisable to check the official BPSC notification for any specific guidelines on this matter, though such provisions are rare.

**Q2: How early should I report to the examination center on the exam day?**
A: You should report to the examination center at least **1.5 to 2 hours before the scheduled start time** of the examination. This allows sufficient time for security checks, verification procedures, and locating your seat without any rush. Remember, gates close strictly 30 minutes before the exam starts.

**Q3: Can I carry a transparent water bottle to the exam hall?**
A: Yes, typically, a transparent water bottle without any labels is allowed inside the examination hall. However, it is always best to check the specific instructions provided on your admit card or the official BPSC website for the particular examination, as rules can sometimes vary.

**Q4: Is a calculator allowed for the BPSC 70th Preliminary or Main Examination?**
A: **No, calculators are generally NOT allowed** for the BPSC Preliminary Examination or most papers of the Main Examination. For certain optional subjects in the Mains, a non-programmable calculator might be permitted, but this is rare and would be explicitly mentioned in the official notification or admit card instructions for that specific paper. Assume calculators are prohibited unless stated otherwise.

**Q5: What should I do if I lose my admit card before the exam?**
A: If you lose your admit card, do not panic. Since admit cards are downloaded online, you can simply **re-download and print another copy** from the official BPSC website (bpsc.bihar.gov.in) using your login credentials. It is always recommended to keep multiple printouts and a digital copy of your admit card for such contingencies. If you face any technical issues close to the exam date, immediately contact the BPSC helpline for assistance.
