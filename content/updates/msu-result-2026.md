## How to Check MSU Result 2026 Online

Follow these steps to view your Maharaja Sayajirao University exam result:

### Step 1: Visit the Official Exam Portal

Go to the MSU examination portal at [msuexam.net](https://msuexam.net/) or the main website at [msubaroda.ac.in](https://www.msubaroda.ac.in/).

### Step 2: Navigate to the Results Section

Look for the **"Examination Results"** or **"Results"** link on the homepage. Click on it to open the result page.

### Step 3: Select Your Exam and Faculty

Choose your **faculty** (Arts, Commerce, Science, etc.), **course name**, and **semester** from the dropdown menus.

### Step 4: Enter Your Details

Enter your **seat number** or **enrollment number** as printed on your hall ticket.

### Step 5: Submit and View Result

Click the **"Submit"** or **"View Result"** button. Your result will be displayed on screen with subject-wise marks and overall status.

### Step 6: Download and Print

Take a **screenshot** or use the **print/download** option to save your result for future reference. Keep this copy until you receive the official marksheet from the university.

## Important Details

- **Revaluation/Re-checking:** Students not satisfied with their marks can apply for revaluation within the deadline announced by the examination department. Check the exam portal for the revaluation application form and fee details.
- **Supplementary Exams:** Students with ATKT (Allowed to Keep Terms) or failed subjects can appear for supplementary examinations. Dates will be notified separately on the MSU website.
- **Official Marksheet:** The downloaded result is for reference only. Collect your original marksheet from your respective faculty office.
- **Discrepancy in Result:** If you find any error in your result (name, subject, marks), contact the MSU Examination Section immediately with supporting documents.
