## How to Check ICAI CA Result on icai.nic.in

1. Visit the official result portal at [https://icai.nic.in](https://icai.nic.in).
2. Click on the **"CA Final Result January 2026"** link on the homepage.
3. Enter your **Registration Number** or **Roll Number**.
4. Enter your **PIN** (as received during registration).
5. Click **Submit** to view your result.
6. Your scorecard with subject-wise marks and pass/fail status will appear.
7. Download or print the scorecard for your records.

## How to Check ICAI CA Result on icaiexam.icai.org

1. Go to [https://icaiexam.icai.org](https://icaiexam.icai.org).
2. Select **"Result"** from the menu.
3. Choose the exam level — **CA Final / CA Inter / CA Foundation**.
4. Enter your **Registration Number** and **Roll Number**.
5. Complete the captcha verification.
6. Click **Submit** to view your result and scorecard.
7. Download the scorecard PDF for future reference.

## Important Details

- **Passing Criteria:** Candidates must score at least **40% in each paper** and **50% aggregate** across all papers in a group to pass.
- **Merit List:** The All India merit list (top 50 ranks) is published along with the results.
- **Marks Statement:** Individual marks statements can be downloaded from the ICAI portal within a few days of result declaration.
- **CA Inter & Foundation Results:** ICAI is expected to declare CA Intermediate and CA Foundation results for the January 2026 session around **6–7 March 2026**.

## What's Next After Passing CA Final?

- Apply for **ICAI Membership** through the ICAI portal.
- Complete the **GMCS (General Management and Communication Skills)** course if not already done.
- Obtain your **Certificate of Practice (CoP)** to start practising as a Chartered Accountant.
