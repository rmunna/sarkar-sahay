## How to Check ICAI CA Result January 2026

### Method 1: Via icai.nic.in

1. Visit **[icai.nic.in/icaiexam](https://icai.nic.in/icaiexam)**
2. Click on the result link for your level — Final, Inter, or Foundation
3. Enter your **Roll Number** or **Registration Number**
4. Enter PIN/Captcha as displayed
5. Click **Submit**
6. Your result with subject-wise marks will appear on screen
7. Download and save the PDF for your records

### Method 2: Via caresults.icai.org

1. Go to **[caresults.icai.org](https://caresults.icai.org)**
2. Select the appropriate examination (Final / Inter / Foundation)
3. Enter your Roll Number and Registration Number
4. Click **Check Result**
5. Download or print your result

### Method 3: Via Email/SMS

ICAI also sends results via email and SMS to candidates who registered for this service on the ICAI website before the result date.

## Revaluation & Verification Process

Not satisfied with your marks? ICAI offers:

### Verification of Marks
- Apply within **30 days** of result declaration
- Fee: ₹100 per paper
- Only clerical errors (totalling mistakes) are checked
- Apply online at [icai.org](https://icai.org)

### Certified Copies
- Request certified copies of your answer sheets
- Fee: ₹500 per paper
- Useful for understanding where you lost marks

### RTI for Answer Sheets
- File an RTI application to ICAI under the Right to Information Act
- Fee: ₹10 (via Indian Postal Order)
- Address: The ICAI, ICAI Bhawan, Indraprastha Marg, New Delhi – 110002
- Response within 30 days

> **Note:** ICAI does not offer a formal revaluation/re-checking process. Verification only covers totalling errors.

## What After the Result?

- **Passed CA Final:** Apply for ICAI membership → Get your Certificate of Practice (CoP)
- **Passed CA Inter:** Register for CA Final course + complete articleship
- **Passed CA Foundation:** Register for CA Intermediate course
- **Not cleared:** Appear in the next attempt (May/June 2026). Use certified copies to identify weak areas.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When will ICAI CA January 2026 result be declared?
The result is expected in **mid-March 2026**. ICAI typically announces results 45–60 days after the last exam date.

### Can I check my CA result by roll number only?
Yes, you can check at **icai.nic.in/icaiexam** using your roll number. Some portals also require your registration number or PIN.

### What is the passing criteria for CA exams?
You need a minimum of **40% in each paper** and **50% aggregate** across all papers in a group to pass.

### How can I get my CA marksheet online?
Log in to the ICAI student portal at **icai.org** after results are declared. Digital marksheets are available for download.

### Is there a revaluation option for CA exams?
ICAI does not offer revaluation. You can apply for **verification of marks** (checks totalling errors only) or request **certified copies** of answer sheets.
