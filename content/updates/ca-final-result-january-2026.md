## How to Check CA Final Result January 2026

Follow these steps to check your CA result:

1. Visit [icai.nic.in](https://icai.nic.in) or [icaiexam.icai.org](https://icaiexam.icai.org).
2. Click on **"CA Final Result January 2026"** or **"CA Intermediate Result January 2026"**.
3. Enter your **roll number** or **registration number**.
4. Enter the **PIN/captcha** displayed on screen.
5. Click **"Submit"** to view your result.
6. Your result will show **Pass/Fail** status with subject-wise marks.
7. Download and take a **printout** for your records.

> **Tip:** ICAI also sends results via SMS and email to registered mobile numbers and email IDs.

## CA Final January 2026 — Pass Percentage and Merit List

ICAI announces the **merit list** (top 50 rank holders) alongside the results. The pass percentage and toppers list will be updated on the official website.

**Typical CA Final pass rates** range between 10-15% for the final level and 15-25% for the intermediate level. Check the official notification for January 2026 specific figures.

## How to Download CA Marksheet

1. Visit [icaiexam.icai.org](https://icaiexam.icai.org).
2. Log in with your **registration number and password**.
3. Go to the **"Marksheet"** section.
4. Download your **digital marksheet** in PDF format.
5. Physical marksheets are dispatched by ICAI to your registered address.

## Details on Your CA Result

Your result scorecard shows:

- **Roll number and registration number**
- **Candidate name**
- **Group-wise result** (Group I, Group II, or both)
- **Subject-wise marks** out of 100
- **Total marks and percentage**
- **Pass/Fail/Exempt status** for each subject

## What's Next After Passing?

- **CA Final passed:** Apply for ICAI membership and complete the member registration process.
- **CA Inter passed:** Register for CA Final and begin articleship if not already started.
- **Failed candidates:** Can apply for the next attempt (May 2026). Subject-wise exemption rules apply.

## FAQs

### What is the minimum passing marks for CA Final?
Candidates need a minimum of **40 marks in each subject** and an **aggregate of 50%** in the group to pass.

### Can I apply for re-verification of marks?
Yes, ICAI allows re-verification and re-evaluation of answer sheets. Apply within **30 days** of the result declaration through the ICAI portal.

### When is the next CA exam after January 2026?
The next CA examination is scheduled for **May 2026**. Registration details will be available on icai.org.

### How do I check my result via SMS?
ICAI sends results via SMS to your registered mobile number. You can also register for result alerts on the ICAI website.

### Is the digital marksheet valid for all purposes?
Yes, the ICAI digital marksheet with QR verification is valid. Physical marksheets are also sent by post to your registered address.
