IBPS PO 2026 admit card (also called the call letter) is released on **ibps.in** — typically **10–14 days before the Prelims exam date**. A separate admit card is issued for Prelims, Mains, and Interview. The admit card is mandatory for entry — no candidate is allowed without it. There is no physical dispatch; it must be downloaded and printed.

## How to Download IBPS PO Admit Card 2026

1. Go to **[ibps.in](https://ibps.in)** → Click **"Click here to Download Call Letter"** under CRP PO/MT 2026
2. Select **"Common Recruitment Process for POs/MTs (CRP PO/MT XVI)"** or the relevant cycle
3. Enter your **Registration Number / Roll Number** and **Date of Birth** (or Password)
4. Enter the captcha and click Submit
5. Your IBPS PO admit card appears on screen — verify all details
6. Click **Download / Print** and save the PDF
7. Print on A4 paper — carry it with a valid photo ID

## What to Carry to the Exam Hall

IBPS is strict — carry both:
- **Printed IBPS PO admit card** (both sides if double-sided)
- **Original photo ID** — Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, Passport, or Driving Licence
- **Passport-size photograph** (same as uploaded, in case required at the centre)

Photocopies of ID and printouts of digital Aadhaar (DigiLocker) are accepted.

## Separate Admit Cards for Each Stage

| Stage | Admit Card Released | What to Expect |
|-------|---------------------|----------------|
| Prelims | ~10–14 days before Prelims | Registration No. + exam centre details |
| Mains | ~10–14 days before Mains | Separate download link; only shortlisted candidates |
| Interview | ~7 days before interview | Call letter sent via email + ibps.in |

Download each admit card fresh from ibps.in — the Prelims call letter does NOT grant entry to Mains.

## Errors on the Admit Card

If you notice a mistake (name spelling, photo, DOB):
- **Minor discrepancy:** Carry original ID; the centre invigilator usually accepts it with a note
- **Major error (wrong name, photo not matching):** Contact IBPS immediately at ibps.in → "Contact Us" before the exam date. IBPS may issue a corrected call letter with sufficient notice

Do NOT travel with a mismatched admit card without checking — some strict centres reject entry.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: When is IBPS PO 2026 Prelims admit card released?**
IBPS releases the Prelims call letter approximately 10–14 days before the exam date. For IBPS PO 2026, the exact date will be announced at ibps.in. Monitor the official site from August–September 2026 onwards.

**Q: Can I download the IBPS PO admit card without registration number?**
No. You need your Registration Number (received at the time of application) and Date of Birth. If you forgot the registration number, retrieve it from your application confirmation email.

**Q: Is IBPS PO admit card available on mobile?**
Yes — ibps.in is mobile-friendly and you can download the PDF on mobile. However, print a physical copy for the exam hall. Some exam centres do not accept mobile/digital versions.
