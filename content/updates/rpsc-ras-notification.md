The RPSC RAS (Rajasthan Administrative Service) and RTS (Rajasthan Taxation Service) Combined Competitive Exam 2026 notification is released by the **Rajasthan Public Service Commission** at [rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in](https://rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in). ## Posts Recruited Through RPSC RAS Exam

The exam recruits for multiple Rajasthan state services simultaneously:
- **RAS** — Rajasthan Administrative Service (Deputy Collector, SDM, BDO)
- **RTS** — Rajasthan Taxation Service (Commercial Tax Officer)
- **RPS** — Rajasthan Police Service (DSP)
- Rajasthan Accounts Service, Rajasthan Cooperative Service, and 15+ other allied services

Post allotment depends on rank and candidate preferences.

## Eligibility at a Glance

- **Age:** 21–40 years (General); up to 45 years for OBC/SC/ST of Rajasthan
- **Education:** Any Bachelor's degree from a recognized university
- **Rajasthan domicile:** Required for reserved category benefits

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How many vacancies does RPSC RAS 2026 have?**
The vacancy count varies each cycle. Previous RPSC RAS exams (2021, 2023) had 988 and 905 vacancies respectively across all services. For 2026 exact vacancy, check the official notification PDF at rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in.

**Q: How often does RPSC conduct the RAS exam?**
RPSC typically conducts the RAS/RTS Combined Competitive Exam every 1–2 years. The gap between notification and final result can be 2–3 years due to the multi-stage process.

**Q: Is RPSC RAS harder than UPSC IAS?**
Both are demanding state/central civil services exams. RPSC RAS has a Rajasthan-specific focus (geography, history, culture, polity) and is conducted in Hindi medium as an option. Competition is intense within Rajasthan but the candidate pool is smaller than UPSC.
