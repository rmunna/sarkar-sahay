## Karnataka PGCET Result 2026 Declared: Check Your Scores Now!

The Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) has officially announced the results for the Karnataka Post Graduate Common Entrance Test (PGCET) 2026 today, August 19, 2026. Candidates who appeared for the PGCET 2026 examination for admission to MBA, MCA, and M.Tech programs across various colleges in Karnataka can now check their scores and ranks.

This eagerly awaited result marks a crucial step for thousands of aspirants seeking postgraduate admissions in the state. KEA has activated the result download link on its official website. Candidates are advised to keep their registration number and date of birth ready to access their scorecards.

### How to Check/Download Your Karnataka PGCET Result 2026 Scorecard

Checking your Karnataka PGCET 2026 result is a straightforward process. Follow the steps below to download your scorecard:

1.  **Visit the Official KEA Website:** Open your web browser and navigate to the official Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) portal: `https://cetonline.karnataka.gov.in/kea/`.
2.  **Locate the PGCET 2026 Result Link:** On the homepage, look for a prominent link titled "PGCET 2026 Result" or "PGCET 2026 Scorecard Download Link". This link is usually found under the "Latest Announcements" or "Admissions" section. You may also find a direct link on `https://karresults.nic.in/`.
3.  **Click on the Result Link:** Click on the activated result link.
4.  **Enter Your Credentials:** You will be redirected to a login page. Here, you will need to enter your Karnataka PGCET 2026 Registration Number and Date of Birth (DD/MM/YYYY) as entered during your application.
5.  **Submit Details:** After entering the required information, click on the "Submit" or "View Result" button.
6.  **View and Download Your Scorecard:** Your Karnataka PGCET 2026 scorecard will be displayed on the screen. Carefully review all the details.
7.  **Print for Future Reference:** It is highly recommended to download the scorecard and take a printout for your future reference, especially for the upcoming counselling and admission process.

### Details Mentioned on Your Karnataka PGCET 2026 Scorecard

Your downloaded PGCET 2026 scorecard will contain several important pieces of information. Candidates must verify all the details to ensure accuracy. The scorecard typically includes:

*   Candidate's Name
*   PGCET 2026 Registration Number
*   Candidate's Roll Number
*   Date of Birth
*   Category (General, SC, ST, OBC, etc.)
*   Marks Obtained in Paper 1 (if applicable)
*   Marks Obtained in Paper 2 (if applicable)
*   Total Marks Scored
*   PGCET 2026 Rank (Overall and Category-wise, if applicable)
*   Eligibility Status for Counselling

In case of any discrepancy in the details printed on the scorecard, candidates should immediately contact the KEA examination authority for rectification.

### What's Next After Karnataka PGCET 2026 Results? (Counselling Process)

The declaration of PGCET 2026 results is the first major step towards securing your postgraduate admission. The next crucial stage is the counselling process, which will be conducted by KEA.

The counselling for Karnataka PGCET 2026 will involve:

1.  **Release of Counselling Schedule:** KEA will soon release a detailed schedule for counselling, including document verification, option entry, seat allotment, and reporting to colleges. This schedule will be published on the official KEA website.
2.  **Document Verification:** Eligible candidates will be required to appear for document verification at designated centres. It is essential to carry all original documents, along with self-attested photocopies, as specified by KEA.
3.  **Option Entry:** Based on their PGCET rank and eligibility, candidates will be able to select their preferred colleges and courses during the option entry window.
4.  **Seat Allotment:** KEA will conduct seat allotment rounds based on the candidates' ranks, preferences, and availability of seats.
5.  **Reporting to Colleges:** After seat allotment, candidates must report to their allotted colleges within the stipulated timeframe, pay the admission fees, and complete the admission formalities.

Candidates are strongly advised to regularly visit the official KEA website for the latest updates regarding the counselling schedule and detailed instructions. Do not rely on unofficial sources for critical information.
