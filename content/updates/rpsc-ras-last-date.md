The RPSC RAS 2026 application last date is announced in the official notification at **rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in**. Applications are submitted through **sso.rajasthan.gov.in** and RPSC typically keeps the window open for **30–45 days** from the notification date. The last date for form submission and fee payment is usually the same. Check the official notification PDF for the exact date — do not rely on third-party job portals.

## Application Timeline (Typical RPSC RAS Pattern)

| Stage | Gap from Notification |
|-------|----------------------|
| Notification released | Day 1 |
| SSO application opens | Day 1–3 |
| Last date to submit form | 30–45 days |
| Last date for fee payment | Same day or 1 day after |
| Correction window | 7 days after close |
| Prelims date | 3–6 months after notification |

## How to Apply Before the Last Date

1. Visit [sso.rajasthan.gov.in](https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in) and log in (or create an SSO ID)
2. Search for "RPSC" → find RAS/RTS Combined Competitive Exam 2026
3. Fill Part 1 (personal details, category) and Part 2 (qualifications, optional subject)
4. Upload photo and signature
5. Pay fee online via Rajasthan's payment gateway
6. Submit and save the acknowledgment receipt

**Submit at least 3–5 days early** — the SSO portal experiences heavy load near the deadline.

## What If You Miss the Deadline?

RPSC does not accept late applications under any condition — no late fee window, no court order extensions. If you miss RPSC RAS 2026:
- Watch rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in for the next notification (typically 1–2 years later)
- Consider other RPSC exams (Junior Accountant, Sub-Inspector, Lecturer) with overlapping preparation
- Build RPSC-specific Rajasthan GK and Hindi preparation during the gap

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I submit the RPSC RAS 2026 form after the last date?**
No. RPSC strictly closes applications on the last date. The SSO portal disables the form immediately after midnight on the deadline. No exceptions.

**Q: Is the fee payment deadline same as form submission?**
Usually yes — both close on the same date. In some RPSC cycles, a 1-day extension for fee payment is granted. Check the official notification for clarity.

**Q: Does RPSC extend the RAS 2026 last date?**
RPSC has occasionally extended deadlines (e.g., due to technical issues on SSO portal). However, do not count on an extension. Apply well before the announced last date.
