## How to Check Your SSC LDC (CHSL) Tier 1 Result 2026

Checking your SSC LDC (CHSL) Tier 1 Result 2026 is a straightforward process. Follow these step-by-step instructions to access your result and qualifying status:

1.  **Visit the Official SSC Website**: Open your web browser and go to the official website of the Staff Selection Commission: [https://ssc.nic.in](https://ssc.nic.in).
2.  **Navigate to the 'Results' Section**: On the homepage, look for the 'Results' tab located in the top navigation bar or on the left-hand side panel. Click on it.
3.  **Select CHSL Tab**: A new page will open displaying results for various examinations. Click on the 'CHSL' tab.
4.  **Find the Relevant Result Link**: Look for the link titled “Combined Higher Secondary (10+2) Level Examination, 2026 – Declaration of Result of Tier-I for appearing in Tier-II” or similar. It will typically be dated September 28, 2026.
5.  **Download the Result PDF**: Click on the 'Click Here' link under the 'Result' column. This will open a PDF document containing the roll numbers of all candidates who have qualified for Tier-II.
6.  **Search Your Roll Number**: Once the PDF is open, use the search function (Ctrl+F on Windows/Linux or Command+F on Mac) and enter your Roll Number to quickly find if your name/roll number is on the list.
7.  **Check Cut-off Marks**: Alongside the result PDF, SSC usually releases a separate PDF containing the category-wise cut-off marks for Tier-I. Make sure to download and review this as well to understand the qualifying criteria.

**Note**: While the result PDF lists qualified candidates, individual scorecards with marks obtained will be released by SSC a few days after the result declaration. Keep an eye on the official website for updates.

## What's Next After Qualifying SSC CHSL Tier 1?

Congratulations to all candidates who have successfully qualified for the SSC CHSL Tier 1 Exam 2026! Your journey towards securing a government job as an LDC or other CHSL posts continues. Here's what you need to focus on next:

1.  **Prepare for Tier-II (Descriptive Paper)**: The next stage is Tier-II, which is a descriptive paper (Essay and Letter/Application Writing) carrying 100 marks. This paper assesses writing skills and is usually conducted offline. Start practicing writing essays and letters on current affairs and relevant topics.
2.  **Stay Updated for Tier-II Admit Card**: The admit cards for Tier-II will be released separately a few weeks before the examination. Keep checking the SSC regional websites for updates.
3.  **Understand Skill Test/Typing Test**: For certain posts like LDC/JSA and PA/SA, a Skill Test/Typing Test (Tier-III) will be conducted for candidates who qualify Tier-II. Familiarize yourself with the requirements (typing speed, error limits, etc.) for these tests.
4.  **Document Verification**: Candidates who qualify all stages will be called for Document Verification (DV). Ensure all your educational certificates, caste certificates (if applicable), identity proof, etc., are in order.
5.  **Final Merit List**: The final merit list will be prepared based on the combined performance in Tier-I and Tier-II, subject to qualifying the Skill Test/Typing Test and Document Verification.
