NVS (Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti) Recruitment 2026 result is declared at **navodaya.gov.in** and **cbseitms.nic.in**. The result is available as an individual scorecard (accessed using registration number + DOB) as well as a merit list PDF. NVS declares results for each post category (TGT, PGT, PRT, Female Staff Nurse, and non-teaching staff) separately.

## How to Check NVS Recruitment 2026 Result

1. Visit **[navodaya.gov.in](https://navodaya.gov.in)** → "Recruitment" section
2. Find the result link for your post category (TGT/PGT/PRT/Non-Teaching)
3. Click the result link — it may redirect to cbseitms.nic.in (the exam portal)
4. On cbseitms.nic.in: enter **Registration Number** and **Date of Birth**
5. Your individual scorecard appears — check subject-wise and total marks
6. Download and save the scorecard PDF

**Alternative:** CBSE ITMS portal (cbseitms.nic.in) directly manages NVS exam results. Bookmark both sites.

## NVS Result — What's in the Scorecard

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Candidate Name & Roll Number | As per application |
| Subject / Post | TGT English, PGT Maths, etc. |
| Paper I Score | Reasoning & General Awareness (subject to category) |
| Paper II Score | Subject-specific (for TGT/PGT) |
| Total Score | Combined |
| Qualifying Status | Qualified / Not Qualified |
| Cutoff | Applicable category cut-off |

## NVS Selection Process After Result

Result → Document Verification → Medical Examination → Final Appointment

Candidates who qualify the written test are called for document verification. Original certificates are checked for all eligibility criteria (education, age, category). Medical examination follows to confirm fitness for the post.

## NVS Merit List

In addition to individual scorecards, NVS publishes a **merit list PDF** containing all qualified candidates' roll numbers and scores, arranged by rank within each post category and region (NVS divides India into regional offices). Merit lists are published post-result on navodaya.gov.in.

## If You Don't Qualify

- Wait list / reserve list is also published for some posts
- NVS conducts fresh recruitment each year — watch navodaya.gov.in for the next notification
- Address weak areas: NVS TGT/PGT tests subject-specific depth (Paper II) + general reasoning (Paper I)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Where is NVS Recruitment 2026 result published?**
The result is published at navodaya.gov.in (official NVS website) and cbseitms.nic.in (CBSE exam portal that manages NVS exams). Always use these two sources — do not trust unofficial score-check sites.

**Q: How long after the NVS exam is the result declared?**
NVS typically declares results within 4–8 weeks of the written exam. The exact timeline varies by recruitment cycle. Check navodaya.gov.in for result date announcements.

**Q: Does NVS publish a cut-off with the result?**
Yes. The cut-off marks for each post and category (General, OBC, SC, ST, PwD) are published alongside or shortly after the result declaration.
