CISF Head Constable Ministerial (HCM) answer key 2026 is released on [cisfrectt.cisf.gov.in](https://cisfrectt.cisf.gov.in) after the CBT (Computer Based Test). A **provisional key** is released first; candidates can raise objections within the specified window. A **final answer key** is published after objection review and is used for computing the merit list.

## How to Download CISF HCM Answer Key 2026

1. Visit [cisfrectt.cisf.gov.in](https://cisfrectt.cisf.gov.in)
2. Look for the **Answer Key** notification for HCM 2026
3. Download the PDF for your exam set (if set-wise keys are released)
4. Compare with your responses noted during the exam

## How to Calculate Your Score

CISF Head Constable CBT scoring:
- **Correct answer:** +1 mark
- **Wrong answer:** 0 marks (no negative marking)
- **Unattempted:** 0 marks

**Score = Number of correct answers**  
Maximum score: 100 marks (100 questions × 1 mark each)

## How to Raise Objections Against Answer Key

If you believe an answer in the provisional key is incorrect:

1. Login to the CISF recruitment portal
2. Navigate to the **Answer Key Objection** section for HCM 2026
3. Select the question number and your objection
4. Upload supporting reference: NCERT textbook page, government circular, or any credible published source
5. Submit within the specified deadline

**Objection fee** (if applicable): varies by notification — check official site.

CISF reviews all valid objections. If the committee accepts an objection, the question may be:
- Corrected (new correct answer)
- Dropped (deleted from scoring, marks awarded to all)

## Answer Key vs Final Score

The provisional answer key score is **not your final score**. The final result is computed using the **Final Answer Key** published after objection resolution. Do not assume selection based on provisional key calculation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: When is the CISF HCM answer key released after the exam?**
Typically within 7–14 days after the exam. The exact date is announced on cisfrectt.cisf.gov.in. Monitor the site regularly post-exam.

**Q: Is the CISF HCM answer key set-wise or combined?**
The CBT is typically computer-based with randomised questions per candidate, so the answer key may be released question-ID wise (not set-wise). Download and verify based on your question numbers.

**Q: Can I challenge all questions in the CISF answer key?**
You can raise objections for any number of questions. However, only objections with valid published supporting references are entertained. Objections based solely on memory or coaching institute keys are not acceptable.
