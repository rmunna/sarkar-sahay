GPSC Class 1 & 2 2026 requires a **Bachelor's degree (any discipline)** from a recognised university and an age of **20–35 years for General category**. Reserved category candidates (SEBC, OBC, SC, ST) get age relaxation. Gujarat domicile is required for most posts.

## Educational Qualification

A **Bachelor's degree in any discipline** from a UGC-recognised university is required. The degree stream doesn't matter — B.A., B.Sc., B.Com., B.Tech., BBA, BCA all qualify.

Final-year students can apply provisionally but must submit their degree certificate before the document verification stage.

## Age Limit

| Category | Minimum Age | Maximum Age |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| General (UR) | 20 | 35 |
| SEBC (Socially & Educationally Backward Class) | 20 | 40 (+5 years) |
| OBC (Other Backward Class) | 20 | 38 (+3 years) |
| SC / ST | 20 | 40 (+5 years) |
| PwD (General) | 20 | 45 (+10 years) |
| Ex-Servicemen | 20 | Relaxation as per Gujarat state rules |
| EWS | 20 | 35 (same as General) |

*Age calculated as on date specified in the official notification. Verify in the 2026 notification for Advt. No. 05/2026-27.*

## Gujarat Domicile Requirement

Most GPSC Class 1 & 2 posts require **Gujarat domicile** or knowledge of Gujarati language. The Mains examination includes a qualifying paper in Gujarati (300 marks, 30% qualifying threshold). Candidates who do not have Gujarati language knowledge will find the Mains qualification paper a challenge.

Non-Gujarat domicile candidates can appear in Prelims, but the Gujarati qualifying paper makes selection difficult for non-Gujarati speakers.

## Nationality

Must be an **Indian citizen**. Candidates of Nepali / Bhutanese origin are eligible as per central rules applicable to state services.

## Number of Attempts

GPSC Class 1 & 2 does **not specify a fixed number of attempts**. Candidates can appear as many times as they are within the age limit.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is Gujarat domicile mandatory for GPSC Class 1 & 2 2026?**
Most posts under the GPSC Class 1 & 2 Combined Exam require Gujarat domicile. A few central deputation posts may be open to all. Check the post-wise eligibility table in the official notification (Advt. No. 05/2026-27).

**Q: Can candidates outside Gujarat apply for GPSC 2026?**
Yes, you can apply. However, the Mains has a qualifying Gujarati language paper (300 marks, must score 30% = 90 marks minimum). Candidates without Gujarati language background need to prepare the Gujarati paper separately to clear Mains.

**Q: How many vacancies are there in GPSC Class 1 & 2 2026?**
Advt. No. 05/2026-27 has **213 posts** across Gujarat Administrative Service Class 1 and Class 2 posts including GFSS (Gujarat Forest Service), GCES (Gujarat Civil Engineering Service), and others.
