## What is JIPMAT-2026?

The Joint Integrated Programme in Management Admission Test (JIPMAT)-2026 is a national-level entrance examination conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA). This examination serves as the gateway for aspiring students seeking admission to the prestigious 5-year Integrated Programme in Management (IPM) offered by premier Indian Institutes of Management (IIMs). Currently, IIM Jammu and IIM Bodh Gaya admit students to their IPM programs through JIPMAT scores.

The Integrated Programme in Management (IPM) is a unique course designed for students who have completed their 12th standard and wish to pursue a career in management. It offers a comprehensive curriculum that integrates undergraduate and postgraduate studies, culminating in a BBA + MBA degree. JIPMAT-2026 is significant for candidates as it provides a direct pathway to a world-class management education from an IIM, right after their schooling, without the need for a separate MBA entrance exam later.

The NTA, a premier autonomous organization under the Ministry of Education, Government of India, is responsible for conducting efficient, transparent, and international standard tests to assess the competency of candidates for admission to premier higher education institutions. Its role in conducting JIPMAT ensures a fair and standardized evaluation process for all applicants.

## Vacancy Details

It is important for candidates to understand that JIPMAT is an admission test for an academic program, not a recruitment examination for government jobs. Therefore, there are no "vacancies" in the traditional sense. Instead, JIPMAT facilitates admission to a specific number of "seats" available in the 5-year Integrated Programme in Management (IPM) at participating IIMs (currently IIM Jammu and IIM Bodh Gaya).

The exact number of seats for the IPM program is determined by the respective Indian Institutes of Management (IIMs) and is typically specified in their individual admission policies or information brochures for the academic year 2026-27. These seat numbers are subject to change based on the IIMs' discretion and infrastructure.

As per Government of India norms, reservation policies apply to admissions in IIMs. This means that a certain percentage of seats will be reserved for candidates belonging to Scheduled Castes (SC), Scheduled Tribes (ST), Other Backward Classes (OBC - Non-Creamy Layer), Economically Weaker Sections (EWS), and Persons with Disabilities (PwD) categories. Candidates are advised to refer to the official admission brochures of IIM Jammu and IIM Bodh Gaya for detailed seat matrix and reservation specifics.

## Selection Process

The selection process for admission to the Integrated Programme in Management (IPM) through JIPMAT-2026 involves multiple stages, primarily managed by NTA for the entrance test and subsequently by the participating IIMs for final admission.

1.  **JIPMAT Written Examination:** Candidates must appear for the Joint Integrated Programme in Management Admission Test (JIPMAT) conducted by NTA. This is a Computer Based Test (CBT) designed to assess quantitative aptitude, logical reasoning, and verbal ability.
2.  **Shortlisting by IIMs:** Based on the JIPMAT-2026 scores, the participating IIMs (IIM Jammu and IIM Bodh Gaya) will shortlist candidates for the next stage of the admission process. The minimum cut-off scores for shortlisting are determined by each IIM independently.
3.  **Personal Interview (PI) and/or Written Ability Test (WAT):** Shortlisted candidates may be required to appear for a Personal Interview (PI) and/or Written Ability Test (WAT) conducted by the respective IIMs. This stage assesses communication skills, general awareness, and personality traits. Some IIMs might waive this stage and rely solely on JIPMAT scores and academic profile.
4.  **Final Merit List:** Each IIM will prepare a final merit list based on a composite score, which typically includes the JIPMAT score, performance in PI/WAT (if conducted), and academic profile (Class 10th and 12th marks).
5.  **Document Verification and Medical Examination:** Candidates offered admission will undergo document verification to confirm their eligibility and may be required to clear a medical examination as per the IIM's admission guidelines.
