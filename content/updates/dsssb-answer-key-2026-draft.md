## DSSSB Draft Answer Keys Released for 2026 Exams

The Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB) has released draft answer keys for online Computer Based Test (CBT) examinations scheduled in **February, March, April, and May 2026**. Candidates who appeared in these exams can now view the draft answer keys and raise objections through the official DSSSB portal.

Additionally, DSSSB published separate answer keys on **20 February 2026** for exam codes **54/25, 53/25, 18/25, and 31/25**.

## How to Check DSSSB Draft Answer Key 2026

1. Visit the official DSSSB website at [dsssb.delhi.gov.in](https://dsssb.delhi.gov.in)
2. Look for the **"Answer Key"** or **"Draft Answer Key"** section on the homepage
3. Click on the relevant answer key link for your exam code
4. Log in using your **Registration Number** and **Date of Birth** (if required)
5. Select your exam date and shift to view the answer key
6. Download or print the answer key PDF for your records
7. If you find any discrepancy, submit an objection within the given window

## How to Raise Objections

Candidates who disagree with any answer in the draft answer key can submit objections online through the DSSSB portal. Keep the following in mind:

- Objections must be submitted **within the specified deadline** (check the official notice)
- Provide proper justification and reference material for each objection
- A fee may be applicable per objection — refunded if the objection is accepted
- Only objections submitted through the official portal will be considered

## What Happens After Draft Answer Keys?

After the objection window closes, DSSSB will review all objections received from candidates. Based on expert evaluation:

- Accepted objections will be incorporated into the **final answer key**
- The final answer key will be used for result preparation
- Results will be declared after finalizing the answer keys

## FAQs

**Q: Where can I download DSSSB draft answer key 2026?**

**Q: Is there a fee to raise objections against the DSSSB answer key?**
A: DSSSB may charge a nominal fee per objection. The fee is refunded if your objection is found valid. Check the official notice for exact details.

**Q: When will the DSSSB final answer key be released?**
A: The final answer key will be released after reviewing all objections. The exact date will be notified on the official website.

**Q: Which exams are covered in the Feb-May 2026 draft answer keys?**
A: The draft answer keys cover all online CBT examinations scheduled by DSSSB during February, March, April, and May 2026, along with specific answer keys for exams 54/25, 53/25, 18/25, and 31/25.
