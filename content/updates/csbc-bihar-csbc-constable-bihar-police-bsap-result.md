## What Happens Next — After the Result

Candidates who have successfully qualified the written examination for CSBC Constable (Bihar Police & BSAP) 2025 should now prepare for the next crucial stages of the selection process. As per the official selection process, the next steps include:

1.  **Physical Efficiency Test (PET) / Physical Standard Test (PST):** Qualified candidates will be called for the PET/PST. This stage assesses candidates' physical fitness and measurements (height, chest, weight) according to the prescribed standards.
