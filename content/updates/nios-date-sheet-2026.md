# NIOS Date Sheet 2026: Theory Exam Schedule for Class 10 and 12

The **National Institute of Open Schooling (NIOS)** is expected to release the **date sheet for April/May 2026 theory examinations** soon. Students enrolled in NIOS Secondary (Class 10) and Senior Secondary (Class 12) courses should stay alert for the official announcement.

This page covers everything you need to know — expected dates, how to download the date sheet, and preparation tips.

## What Is the NIOS Date Sheet?

The NIOS date sheet is the official exam timetable published by the National Institute of Open Schooling. It includes:

- **Subject-wise exam dates** for Class 10 and Class 12
- **Exam timing** (usually 2:30 PM to 5:30 PM for theory papers)
- **Practical exam schedule** (released separately or alongside)

NIOS conducts public examinations twice a year — in **April/May** and **October/November**.

## How to Download NIOS Date Sheet 2026

Once released, follow these steps to download the date sheet:

### Step 1: Visit the Official Website

Go to [www.nios.ac.in](https://www.nios.ac.in).

### Step 2: Navigate to Examinations

Click on **"Examinations"** in the top menu, then select **"Date Sheet"** from the dropdown.

### Step 3: Select Your Course

Choose either:
- **Secondary (Class 10)** — for 10th date sheet
- **Senior Secondary (Class 12)** — for 12th date sheet

### Step 4: Download the PDF

The date sheet will open as a PDF. Download and save it for reference. Take a printout if needed.

### Step 5: Cross-Check with Hall Ticket

When your hall ticket is released, verify that the exam dates and subject codes match the date sheet.

## How to Download NIOS Hall Ticket 2026

Your hall ticket (admit card) is equally important. Here's how to get it:

1. Visit [sdmis.nios.ac.in](https://sdmis.nios.ac.in)
2. Click on **"Hall Ticket"** under the Student Login section
3. Enter your **Enrollment Number** and **Date of Birth**
4. Download and print the hall ticket

Keep the hall ticket safe — you cannot enter the exam centre without it.

## Important Preparation Tips

With exams approaching, use this time wisely:

1. **Download previous year question papers** from the NIOS website — they follow a predictable pattern
2. **Focus on Tutor Marked Assignments (TMAs)** — ensure they are submitted, as they carry marks
3. **Use NIOS study material** available free on the website under "Learning Material"
4. **Practice time management** — theory papers are 3 hours long
5. **Check your practical schedule** separately if your subjects include practicals
6. **Carry valid ID proof** along with your hall ticket to the exam centre

## Who Can Appear for NIOS April 2026 Exams?

You are eligible if:

- You are enrolled in NIOS Secondary or Senior Secondary course

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. When will NIOS release the date sheet for 2026?

The NIOS date sheet for April/May 2026 exams is expected to be released in **March 2026**. Keep checking [nios.ac.in](https://www.nios.ac.in) for the official announcement.

### 2. How can I download my NIOS hall ticket?

Visit [sdmis.nios.ac.in](https://sdmis.nios.ac.in), log in with your enrollment number and date of birth, and download your hall ticket from the Student Login section.

### 3. What time do NIOS theory exams start?

NIOS theory exams are usually held from **2:30 PM to 5:30 PM**. The exact timing will be mentioned on your hall ticket and date sheet.

### 4. Are NIOS practical exams held before or after theory exams?

Practical exams are typically conducted **before the theory exams**, usually in March–April. Your study centre will notify you of the exact schedule.

### 5. Can I appear for exams if I haven't submitted my TMA?

No. **Tutor Marked Assignments (TMAs) are mandatory.** If you haven't submitted them, contact your study centre immediately as TMA marks are part of your final score.
