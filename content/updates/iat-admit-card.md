## IISER IAT 2026 Admit Card Released: Your Gateway to Science Education! 

Good news for all aspirants! The Indian Institutes of Science Education and Research (IISERs) have officially released the Admit Card for the IISER Aptitude Test (IAT) 2026 today, May 29, 2026. If you've applied for admission to the prestigious BS-MS Dual Degree or BS degree programs at IISERs, your admit card is now available for download on the official admission portal. 

The admit card is a mandatory document to appear for the IISER IAT 2026 examination, which is typically conducted in June. Without a valid admit card, you will not be permitted to enter the examination hall. Therefore, it is crucial to download, print, and verify all details on your admit card immediately. 

This article provides a comprehensive guide on how to download your IISER IAT 2026 admit card, what details to check, and crucial instructions for the examination day. 

## How to Download Your IISER IAT 2026 Admit Card 

Downloading your IISER IAT 2026 Admit Card is a straightforward process. Follow these step-by-step instructions to get your admit card:

1.  **Visit the Official Website:** Go to the official IISER Admission portal: `https://www.iiseradmission.in/` 
2.  **Locate Admit Card Link:** On the homepage, look for the 'IAT 2026 Admit Card Download' or 'Applicant Login' link. This link is usually prominent in the 'News & Updates' section or directly on the main page. 
3.  **Login to Your Account:** Click on the link. You will be redirected to the candidate login page. Enter your login credentials, which typically include your User ID (Application Number) and Password, created during the application process. 
4.  **Access Admit Card:** After successful login, your dashboard will display an option to view or download your Admit Card for IAT 2026. Click on this option. 
5.  **Review and Download:** Your IISER IAT 2026 Admit Card will be displayed on the screen. Carefully check all the details. 
6.  **Print Multiple Copies:** Download the PDF file and print at least two copies of the admit card on good quality A4 paper. It's always advisable to keep an extra copy for emergencies. 

*Note: If you face any issues during the download process or forget your password, look for 'Forgot Password' or 'Helpdesk' options on the login page.* 

## Details to Verify on Your IISER IAT 2026 Admit Card 

Once you have downloaded your admit card, it is imperative to cross-check every detail to avoid any discrepancies on the exam day. Here are the key details you must verify:

*   **Candidate's Name:** Ensure your name is spelled correctly as per your application. 
*   **Photograph and Signature:** Check if your photograph and signature are clearly visible and match your uploaded images. 
*   **Roll Number/Application Number:** Verify your unique roll number or application number. 
*   **Date of Birth:** Confirm your date of birth is accurate. 
*   **Category:** Ensure your applied category (General, SC, ST, OBC-NCL, EWS, PwD) is correctly mentioned. 
*   **Examination Date and Time:** Note the exact date and reporting time for your IAT 2026 exam. 
*   **Examination Centre Details:** Verify the name and complete address of your allotted examination centre. 
*   **Important Instructions:** Read all the instructions provided on the admit card carefully. 

*If you find any error or discrepancy in your admit card, immediately contact the IISER Admissions office through their official helpdesk numbers or email, which are usually provided on the website.* 

## What to Do After Downloading Your Admit Card 

Downloading your admit card is just the first step. Here's what you should do next to prepare for the IISER IAT 2026:

1.  **Read Instructions Carefully:** Go through all the instructions printed on the admit card regarding exam day decorum, prohibited items, and required documents. 
2.  **Gather Required Documents:** On the day of the exam, you must carry: 
    *   A printed copy of your IISER IAT 2026 Admit Card. 
    *   A valid original photo identity proof (e.g., Aadhaar Card, Passport, Driving License, PAN Card, Voter ID). 
    *   (Optional) A passport-sized photograph similar to the one uploaded in your application, in case of verification needs. 
3.  **Locate Your Exam Centre:** Plan your travel to the examination centre in advance. Visit the centre a day or two before the exam, if possible, to familiarize yourself with the route and avoid last-minute rush. 
4.  **Familiarize with Exam Pattern:** Revisit the IISER IAT exam pattern and syllabus. The test typically comprises questions from Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Biology. 
5.  **Stay Calm and Prepared:** Focus on your revision. Maintain a positive attitude and ensure you get adequate rest before the exam.
