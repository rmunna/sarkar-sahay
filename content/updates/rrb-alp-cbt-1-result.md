## RRB ALP CBT-1 2025 Result — Overview

Candidates who appeared for the Computer Based Test Stage 1 (CBT-1) can now check their individual results online. The RRB ALP CBT-1 exam was conducted on February 13, 2026, and the results were announced on June 12, 2026. This CBT-1 serves as a screening test, and candidates who qualify will be eligible to appear for the CBT-2 stage of the selection process. All candidates are advised to check their results promptly to understand their qualification status for the next stage.

Checking your RRB ALP CBT-1 2025 result is a straightforward process. Follow the steps below to access your scorecard and determine your qualification status:

2.  **Locate the Results Section:** On the homepage, look for a section typically labeled "Results," "CBT-1 Results," or "Latest Updates." This section is usually prominently displayed.
3.  **Select the Relevant Exam Link:** Within the results section, find and click on the link specifically for "RRB ALP CBT-1 2025 Result" or a similar designation. There might be separate links for different RRB regions; ensure you select the correct one for your applied region.
4.  **Enter Your Credentials:** You will be redirected to a login page where you need to enter your required credentials. This typically includes your:
    *   Roll Number
    *   Registration Number
    *   Date of Birth (DOB)
    *   Security Captcha (if displayed)
    Ensure all details are entered accurately as per your admit card.
5.  **Submit and View Result:** After entering your details, click on the "Submit," "Login," or "Get Result" button. Your RRB ALP CBT-1 2025 result and scorecard will be displayed on the screen.
6.  **Download and Print:** It is highly recommended to download your result/scorecard and take a printout for future reference. This document will be essential for the subsequent stages of the recruitment process.
