## BSEAP AP SSC 10th Class Hall Ticket 2026 Released

The **Board of Secondary Education Andhra Pradesh (BSEAP)** has officially released the **AP SSC 10th Class Hall Tickets 2026** on March 2, 2026. Students appearing for the AP SSC board exams can now download their admit cards from the official website **bse.ap.gov.in**. The AP 10th class exams are scheduled from **March 16 to April 1, 2026**.

The hall ticket is a mandatory document for appearing in the AP SSC 2026 examination. Without a valid admit card, students will not be permitted to enter the examination hall.

## How to Download AP SSC Hall Ticket 2026

Follow these steps to download your AP 10th class admit card:

1. **Visit the official website** — Go to [bse.ap.gov.in](https://bse.ap.gov.in)
2. **Click on "Hall Tickets"** — Look for the AP SSC Hall Ticket 2026 link on the homepage
3. **Enter your details** — Provide your registration number or application number along with date of birth
4. **Click Submit** — Your hall ticket will appear on screen
5. **Download and print** — Save the PDF and take a printout for the exam day

### Alternative Method — WhatsApp (Mana Mithra)

Students can also download their hall tickets via the **Mana Mithra WhatsApp service** by sending a message to **9552300009**. Select "Educational Services" and enter your application number or child ID along with date of birth to receive the hall ticket.

## Important Details on the Hall Ticket

The AP SSC 2026 admit card contains the following information:

- **Student name and photograph**
- **Roll number and registration number**
- **Exam centre name and address**
- **Subject-wise exam date and time**
- **Exam day instructions and guidelines**

Students must carefully verify all details on the hall ticket. If there are any discrepancies in name, photo, or other details, contact the school headmaster or the concerned District Educational Officer (DEO) immediately for corrections.

## AP SSC 2026 Exam Guidelines

- Reach the exam centre **30 minutes before** the scheduled time
- Carry a **printed copy of the hall ticket** along with a **valid school ID card**
- No electronic devices, mobile phones, or calculators are allowed
- The exam duration is **3 hours 15 minutes** for each paper
- Read the question paper carefully before answering
