## CBSE Class 12th Board Exam Result 2026 Declared: Check Your Scores Now!

**New Delhi, [2026-07-22]** – The Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) has officially declared the Class 12th Board Examination Results for the academic year 2025-26 today, July 22, 2026. This highly anticipated announcement brings an end to the wait for lakhs of students across India and abroad who appeared for the examinations held earlier this year.

Students can now access their provisional mark sheets and results online through the official CBSE results portal and other government-designated platforms. To check your results, ensure you have your Roll Number, School Number, and Admit Card ID handy.

This page provides you with all the essential information, direct links, and a step-by-step guide to check your CBSE Class 12th Result 2026. We also cover what details you'll find on your result, what to do next, and answer frequently asked questions.

## Overview of CBSE Class 12th Result 2026

The CBSE Class 12th examinations for the academic session 2025-26 were conducted between February and April 2026. These exams are a crucial milestone for students, determining their eligibility for higher education courses and career paths. The results declared today include marks for all streams – Science, Commerce, and Arts.

The board has worked diligently to ensure the timely release of results, despite the vast number of candidates. The online declaration allows for immediate access and reduces the dependency on physical mark sheets initially. The results released online are provisional and should be treated as such until the original mark sheets and passing certificates are issued by the respective schools.

## How to Check CBSE Class 12th Result 2026 Online

Students can check their results through various official channels. Here's a detailed guide:

### Method 1: Via Official CBSE Results Website (cbseresults.nic.in)

1.  **Visit the Official Website:** Open your web browser and go to `https://cbseresults.nic.in`.
2.  **Locate Result Link:** On the homepage, look for a link that says "Senior School Certificate Examination (Class XII) Results 2026" or similar.
3.  **Enter Credentials:** Click on the link. You will be redirected to a login page where you need to enter the following details:
    *   Your Roll Number
    *   Your School Number
    *   Your Admit Card ID
4.  **Submit:** After entering the details, click on the "Submit" button.
5.  **View Result:** Your CBSE Class 12th Result 2026 will be displayed on the screen. It will include your subject-wise marks, grades, and overall percentage.
6.  **Download and Print:** It is highly recommended to download the result page and take a printout for future reference. This provisional mark sheet will be useful for immediate admission procedures.

### Method 2: Via National Results Portal (results.nic.in)

1.  **Go to National Portal:** Visit `https://results.nic.in`.
2.  **Find CBSE Link:** Look for the link related to "CBSE Class 12 Results 2026".
3.  **Enter Details:** Follow similar steps as above, entering your Roll Number, School Number, and Admit Card ID.
4.  **Access Result:** Your result will be displayed.

### Method 3: Via DigiLocker App/Website

CBSE also facilitates students to access their mark sheets and certificates through DigiLocker. This is an official platform for issuing digital documents.

1.  **Download/Access DigiLocker:** If you haven't already, download the DigiLocker app on your smartphone or visit `https://digilocker.gov.in`.
2.  **Login/Sign Up:** Log in using your registered mobile number or Aadhaar number. If you are a new user, sign up first.
3.  **Link Aadhaar (if not done):** Ensure your Aadhaar is linked to your DigiLocker account.
4.  **Find CBSE Section:** Navigate to the "Education" section and select "Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE)".
5.  **Access Documents:** You will find links to your Class 12 Marksheet and Passing Certificate. Click on them to view and download your official digital documents.

## What to Do After Checking Your Results?

Congratulations to all students who have successfully cleared the examination! For those who faced challenges, remember that this is just one step in your academic journey.

1.  **Secure Provisional Mark Sheet:** Download and print multiple copies of your provisional result for immediate use in college admissions.
2.  **Collect Original Documents:** Your original mark sheets and passing certificates will be distributed by your respective schools a few weeks after the online declaration. Stay in touch with your school for collection dates.
3.  **Higher Education Planning:** Begin or finalize your applications for undergraduate courses in colleges and universities. Many institutions require the Class 12th mark sheet for admission.
4.  **Re-evaluation/Verification of Marks:** If you are unsatisfied with your marks, CBSE usually provides options for verification of marks, obtaining photocopies of answer sheets, and re-evaluation. The dates and procedures for these processes will be announced by CBSE on its official website `https://www.cbse.gov.in` shortly.
5.  **Compartment Examination:** Students who have not qualified in one or two subjects may be eligible for compartment examinations. Details regarding the application and schedule for compartment exams will be released by CBSE soon.
