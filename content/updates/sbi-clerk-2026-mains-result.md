## What is SBI Clerk (Junior Associate) Exam?

The SBI Clerk exam, officially called the Junior Associate (Customer Support & Sales) recruitment, is conducted by the State Bank of India to fill clerical positions across its branches nationwide. It is one of the most sought-after banking exams in India with lakhs of applicants every year.

## SBI Clerk Mains Result 2025-26

The State Bank of India declared the SBI Clerk Mains Result 2025-26 on **February 27, 2026**. The result has been released in PDF format containing roll numbers of candidates who have been provisionally shortlisted for the next stage.

Candidates can download the result PDF from the official SBI careers portal at sbi.co.in/web/careers.

## Vacancy Details

A total of **6,589 vacancies** have been notified for Junior Associate (Customer Support & Sales) posts across SBI branches pan-India.

## What Happens After Mains Result?

Candidates who have qualified the SBI Clerk Mains Exam will now proceed to the **Language Proficiency Test (LPT)**. Key points:

- The LPT is the final stage of the selection process
- It is applicable for candidates who have not studied the opted local language at the specified level during their academic career
- Candidates who have studied the local language are exempted from LPT

## Scorecard and Answer Key

For the first time, SBI will release the **Mains Answer Key, Response Sheet, and Individual Scorecard** on **March 10, 2026**. This is a significant step towards transparency in the SBI Clerk recruitment process.

## How to Check SBI Clerk Mains Result 2026

1. Visit the official SBI website at www.sbi.co.in/web/careers
2. Click on "Current Openings"
3. Find "Recruitment of Junior Associates (Customer Support & Sales)" — Advertisement No. CRPD/CR/2025-26/06
4. Click on the Mains Result PDF link
5. Search for your roll number using Ctrl+F
6. Download and save the PDF for future reference

## Selection Process

The SBI Clerk recruitment follows a three-stage process:

1. **Preliminary Exam** — Screening test (100 marks, 1 hour)
2. **Mains Exam** — Merit-based selection (200 marks, 2 hours 40 minutes)
3. **Language Proficiency Test (LPT)** — Final qualifying stage

## SBI Clerk Mains Exam Pattern

| Section | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| General/Financial Awareness | 50 | 50 | 35 min |
| General English | 40 | 40 | 35 min |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 50 | 50 | 45 min |
| Reasoning Ability & Computer Aptitude | 50 | 60 | 45 min |
| **Total** | **190** | **200** | **2 hr 40 min** |

## Preparation of Final Merit List

The final merit list for SBI Clerk 2025-26 will be prepared based on:

- Marks obtained in the Mains examination
- Category-wise cut-off scores
- Qualifying the LPT (where applicable)
- Document verification
