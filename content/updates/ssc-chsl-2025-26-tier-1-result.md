## What is SSC CHSL?

The Staff Selection Commission Combined Higher Secondary Level (CHSL) examination is conducted annually to recruit candidates for Lower Division Clerk (LDC), Junior Secretariat Assistant (JSA), Postal Assistant, Sorting Assistant, and Data Entry Operator (DEO) posts in various central government ministries and departments. The selection process involves Tier 1 (CBT) and Tier 2 (descriptive/skill test) stages.

## Important Dates

| Event | Date |
|-------|------|
| Notification Released | June 2025 |
| Tier 1 Exam | 12–30 November 2025 |
| Tier 1 Result Declared | 27 February 2026 |
| Tier 2 Exam Date | To be announced |

## SSC CHSL Tier 1 Result 2025-26 Highlights

The Staff Selection Commission declared the SSC CHSL Tier 1 Result on 27th February 2026 on its official website ssc.gov.in. The result has been published in PDF format containing roll numbers of shortlisted candidates.

- **Total Vacancies:** 3,131
- **Total Candidates Shortlisted for Tier 2:** 39,901
- **Result Format:** PDF with qualified roll numbers
- **Posts:** LDC, JSA, Postal Assistant, Sorting Assistant, DEO

## How to Check SSC CHSL Tier 1 Result

1. Visit the official SSC website at [ssc.gov.in](https://ssc.gov.in)
2. Click on the "Result" tab on the homepage
3. Look for "SSC CHSL Tier 1 Result 2025-26" link
4. Download the PDF file
5. Search for your roll number using Ctrl+F
6. If your roll number is listed, you are shortlisted for Tier 2

## What's Next — Tier 2 Exam

Candidates who have qualified Tier 1 will now appear for the Tier 2 examination. Key details:

- **Tier 2 Exam Date:** To be announced by SSC
- **Tier 2 Format:** Descriptive paper (essay + letter writing) and skill test for DEO posts
- **Duration:** Typically 60 minutes for descriptive paper
- **Language:** English or Hindi

Candidates should regularly check ssc.gov.in for Tier 2 exam date announcements.

## Selection Process

1. **Tier 1 (CBT):** 100 questions, 200 marks, 60 minutes — ✅ Completed
2. **Tier 2:** Descriptive paper + skill test — Upcoming
3. **Document Verification:** After final result
4. **Final Posting:** Based on merit and preference

## Preparation Tips for Tier 2

1. Practice essay and letter writing daily in both English and Hindi
2. Focus on current affairs topics for essay questions
3. Maintain neat handwriting if appearing in pen-and-paper mode
4. For DEO candidates, practice typing speed to meet the required threshold
5. Review previous year Tier 2 papers for pattern understanding
