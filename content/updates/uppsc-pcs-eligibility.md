To apply for UPPSC PCS (Provincial Civil Services) 2026, candidates need a **Bachelor's degree from a recognized university**, be aged **21–40 years** (General category), and be an Indian citizen. UP domicile is required only for candidates seeking reservation benefits (OBC/SC/ST of Uttar Pradesh). The exam is conducted by the Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission at [uppsc.up.nic.in](https://uppsc.up.nic.in).

## Age Limit for UPPSC PCS 2026

| Category | Minimum Age | Maximum Age |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| General (UR) | 21 years | 40 years |
| OBC (UP Domicile) | 21 years | 45 years (+5 years) |
| SC / ST (UP Domicile) | 21 years | 45 years (+5 years) |
| PwD (General) | 21 years | 55 years (+15 years) |
| PwD (OBC/SC/ST, UP) | 21 years | 55 years (+15 years) |
| Ex-Servicemen | As per UP government rules | Age relaxation applicable |
| State Govt employees (UP) | 21 years | 40–45 years (check notification) |

*Age is calculated as on the date specified in the official notification. Refer to [uppsc.up.nic.in](https://uppsc.up.nic.in) for the exact cut-off date.*

## Educational Qualification

**Minimum qualification:** A **Bachelor's degree** (graduation) in any discipline from a University established by law in India, or an equivalent qualification recognized by the Government of India.

There is no restriction on the stream — candidates with B.A., B.Sc., B.Com., B.Tech., MBBS, LLB, or any other graduation degree are eligible.

Some posts under UPPSC PCS may have **additional subject-specific requirements**. For example:
- Posts requiring law background (e.g. Sub-Registrar, Assistant Prosecution Officer) may need an LLB
- Posts in medical/technical cadres carry additional professional qualifications

Always check the specific post-wise eligibility table in the official UPPSC PCS 2026 notification.

## Domicile and Nationality

- **Nationality:** Must be an Indian citizen (or eligible under Article 5–8 of the Constitution as specified by UP government rules)
- **Domicile:** UP domicile is **not mandatory for General category** candidates. However, to avail reservation benefits (OBC/SC/ST category benefits) of Uttar Pradesh, candidates must hold a valid **UP domicile certificate** issued by a competent authority
- Female candidates of UP domicile get additional age relaxation and reservation as per UP government rules
- Candidates of other states can apply in the General (UR) category but will not be eligible for UP state reservations

## Physical Standards

UPPSC PCS is primarily an administrative/civil services exam — there are no general physical fitness requirements for most posts. However, certain posts (Police, Revenue enforcement, Forest) may carry specific physical standards. Check the post-wise requirements in the notification.

---

## Number of Attempts

UPPSC does not impose a strict attempt limit for PCS like UPSC does for IAS. However, eligibility is bound by the **upper age limit** — once you cross the maximum age, you can no longer appear regardless of prior attempts. Candidates should factor this in when planning their preparation timeline.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can candidates from other states apply for UPPSC PCS 2026?**
Yes. There is no domicile bar for applying in the General (UR) category. Candidates from any state can apply but will not be eligible for reserved category benefits (OBC/SC/ST relaxation in age, fee, and seats) which are meant for UP domicile holders.

**Q: What is the age limit for OBC candidates in UPPSC PCS 2026?**
OBC candidates with UP domicile get a relaxation of 5 years, making the upper age limit 45 years. This relaxation is only available to candidates holding a valid OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) certificate issued by a Uttar Pradesh authority.

**Q: Is graduation mandatory for UPPSC PCS, or can final-year students apply?**
UPPSC generally allows final-year students to apply provisionally, but the degree must be completed before the document verification/interview stage. Confirm this in the specific 2026 notification since the rule can vary.

**Q: What certificates are needed for the UPPSC PCS application?**
At the application stage, only basic information is required. For interview/document verification you'll need: graduation marksheets and certificate, DOB proof, caste/category certificate (UP-issued for reserved candidates), domicile certificate (if claiming UP reservation), and a valid ID.
