## Re-evaluation / Re-checking / Compartment Exam

For competitive examinations like the RRB TECHNICIAN GRADE I SIGNAL - CEN 02/2025 CBT, the provision for re-evaluation or re-checking of answer sheets is generally **not available**. The Computer Based Test (CBT) system is designed for objective assessment, and marks are calculated automatically, minimizing errors. Therefore, candidates typically cannot apply for a re-check of their scores.

Similarly, the concept of a "compartment exam" or "supplementary exam" does not apply to competitive recruitment examinations conducted by the RRB. These types of exams are usually associated with academic board examinations to allow students to pass failed subjects. In the context of RRB recruitment, if a candidate does not qualify in the CBT, they are not eligible for the subsequent stages of the current recruitment cycle. Their only option is to apply for future recruitment notifications when they are released.

Candidates are advised to rely on the officially declared results. Any updates regarding the result process, if any, will be communicated solely through the official RRB website [www.rrbcdg.gov.in](https://www.rrbcdg.gov.in).
