## How to Download Answer Key

1.  **Visit the Official HPSC Website:** Navigate to the official website of the Haryana Public Service Commission: `https://hpsc.gov.in`.
2.  **Locate the "Answer Key" Section:** On the homepage, look for sections like "Announcements," "Results," or specifically "Answer Keys." This section is usually prominently displayed or can be found under the "Candidate's Corner" or "Latest Updates."
3.  **Find HPSC HCS 2026 Answer Key Link:** Within the answer key section, search for the link titled "Answer Key for HPSC HCS (Ex. Br.) & Other Allied Services Preliminary Examination 2026" or similar.
4.  **Select Your Paper Set/Series:** The answer keys are typically released separately for different paper sets (e.g., Set A, Set B, Set C, Set D). Click on the link that corresponds to the paper set you received during the examination.
5.  **Download the PDF:** The answer key will usually open as a PDF document. You can then download and save it to your device for future reference and score calculation.

The HPSC HCS preliminary examination typically uses multiple sets of question papers to ensure fairness and prevent malpractices. These sets are usually designated as **Set A, Set B, Set C, and Set D**. While the questions across these sets are identical, their order is shuffled.

*   **Shift-wise Exams:** In some cases, if an exam is conducted in multiple shifts, separate answer keys might be released for each shift. However, for HPSC HCS Prelims, it is typically a single-shift examination with multiple paper sets. Always refer to the official notification and your question booklet for definitive information regarding your paper set.

## How to Calculate Expected Score

Calculating your expected score using the provisional answer key is a critical step to gauge your performance and assess your chances of qualifying for the Mains examination. The HPSC HCS Prelims typically involves negative marking.

**Typical Marking Scheme (HPSC HCS Prelims):**
*   **Correct Answer:** +2 marks
*   **Incorrect Answer:** -0.50 marks (1/4th of the marks allotted for the question)
*   **Unattempted Question:** 0 marks

**Formula for Expected Score:**

Expected Score = (Number of Correct Answers × Marks per Correct Answer) - (Number of Incorrect Answers × Penalty for Incorrect Answer)

**Example Calculation:**

Let's assume a candidate attempted 80 questions out of 100 in the HPSC HCS Prelims.
*   Number of Correct Answers: 60
*   Number of Incorrect Answers: 20 (80 attempted - 60 correct)
*   Number of Unattempted Questions: 20 (100 total - 80 attempted)

Using the typical marking scheme:
*   Marks for Correct Answers: 60 × 2 = 120 marks
*   Penalty for Incorrect Answers: 20 × 0.50 = 10 marks

**Expected Score = 120 - 10 = 110 marks**

This calculated score is provisional and subject to change if any objections are accepted and the final answer key differs.

## How to Raise Objections

The HPSC provides a specific window for candidates to challenge the provisional answer key if they believe there are errors. This process is typically conducted online.

1.  **Login to the HPSC Portal:** Go to the official HPSC website (`hpsc.gov.in`) and look for the link to "Online Objection Portal" or "Challenge Answer Key" for the HPSC HCS 2026 examination. You will likely need to log in using your registration number/roll number and date of birth/password.
2.  **Select the Relevant Paper:** Once logged in, select the specific paper (e.g., General Studies Paper I) and your question paper set (A, B, C, or D) for which you wish to raise an objection.
3.  **Identify the Question to Challenge:** A list of questions with their provisional answers will be displayed. Identify the question number(s) you wish to challenge.
4.  **Provide Justification and Evidence:** For each challenged question, you will need to:
    *   Select the question number.
    *   State your proposed correct answer.
    *   Provide a detailed justification for your objection.
    *   **Upload supporting evidence:** This is crucial. You must upload clear, verifiable proof from standard textbooks, official government reports, or authoritative academic sources (e.g., scanned pages, screenshots with citations). Personal opinions or coaching institute notes are generally not accepted as valid evidence.
5.  **Pay the Objection Fee:** A non-refundable fee is usually charged per question challenged. Proceed to the payment gateway to complete the transaction.
6.  **Review and Submit:** Before final submission, review all your objections, justifications, and uploaded evidence. Once satisfied, submit your challenges. Keep a record of your submitted objections and payment receipt.

## Objection Fee and Refund Policy

HPSC typically charges a fee for each question challenged to deter frivolous objections and cover administrative costs.

*   **Objection Fee:** The fee per question challenged is usually **₹1000 (approximate)**. This amount is subject to change and will be specified in the official notification for raising objections. Candidates must pay this fee online through the designated payment gateway.
*   **Refund Policy:** The objection fee is generally **refunded only if the candidate's objection is found to be valid and accepted by the Commission**. If the objection is rejected, the fee is typically non-refundable. The refund process, if applicable, is usually initiated automatically by the HPSC to the original payment source after the final answer key is released.

## Provisional vs Final Answer Key

Understanding the distinction between the provisional and final answer keys is important for all aspirants.

*   **Provisional Answer Key:** This is the first version of the answer key released by HPSC after the examination. Its primary purpose is to allow candidates to estimate their scores and identify any discrepancies. It is "provisional" because it is subject to change based on the objections raised by candidates.
*   **Final Answer Key:** After reviewing all the valid objections received during the stipulated period, the HPSC's expert committee evaluates them. If any objections are found to be correct, the provisional answer key is revised. The revised key, incorporating all accepted changes, is then released as the **Final Answer Key**. This key is considered definitive and is used for the final evaluation of OMR sheets and the preparation of the preliminary examination results.

**What typically changes:**
*   **Correction of Errors:** Obvious errors in the provisional key (e.g., incorrect option marked as correct).
*   **Deletion of Ambiguous Questions:** Questions found to be ambiguous, having multiple correct answers, or being factually incorrect might be deleted. If a question is deleted, its marks are usually awarded to all candidates, or the marking scheme is adjusted proportionally for the remaining questions.
*   **Change in Correct Option:** The designated correct option for a particular question might change from the provisional to the final key.

The final answer key is usually released along with or just before the preliminary examination results.

## FAQs

Here are answers to some frequently asked questions regarding the HPSC HCS answer key:

**Q1: Can I challenge all questions in the answer key?**
A1: You can challenge any question in the provisional answer key if you genuinely believe the provided answer is incorrect. However, you must provide strong, verifiable evidence for each challenge and pay the applicable fee per question.

**Q2: How long does it take for objections to be reviewed and the final key released?**
A2: The review process typically takes a few weeks to a couple of months, depending on the number of objections received and the complexity of the issues. The final answer key is usually released along with or just prior to the preliminary examination results.

**Q3: What kind of evidence is needed to support an objection?**
A3: You need to provide clear, verifiable evidence from standard, authoritative textbooks, official government publications, or recognized academic journals. Screenshots or scanned pages with proper citations (book name, author, page number, edition) are generally required. Personal notes or coaching institute materials are usually not accepted.

**Q4: Is the objection fee refunded if my challenge is accepted?**
A4: Yes, if your objection is found to be valid and accepted by the HPSC's expert committee, the fee paid for that specific question is typically refunded to your original payment source. If the objection is rejected, the fee is non-refundable.

**Q5: Where is the official answer key published?**
A5: The official provisional and final answer keys are exclusively published on the Haryana Public Service Commission's official website, `https://hpsc.gov.in`. Candidates should always refer to the official website for the most accurate and up-to-date information.
