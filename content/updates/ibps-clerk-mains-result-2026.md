## How to Check IBPS Clerk Mains Result 2025-26

Follow these steps to download your result:

1. Visit the official IBPS website at [www.ibps.in](https://www.ibps.in).
2. On the homepage, click on **"CRP-CSA"** and then select **"Common Recruitment Process for Customer Service Associate XV"**.
3. Click on the link for **"Result Status of Online Main Examination"**.
4. Enter your **Registration Number / Roll Number** and **Password / Date of Birth**.
5. Enter the **captcha code** displayed on the screen.
6. Click **Submit** to view your result.
7. Download and **save a PDF copy** of your result for future reference.

## IBPS Clerk Mains Cut-Off 2025-26

The IBPS Clerk Mains cut-off for 2025-26 has been released **state-wise and category-wise** along with the result. Cut-off marks vary depending on the state, number of vacancies, and candidate category (General, OBC, SC, ST, EWS, PwBD). Candidates should check their individual scorecards against the applicable cut-off for their state and category.

## What Your Result Contains

Your IBPS Clerk Mains Result 2025-26 will include:

- **Candidate Name** and Registration/Roll Number
- **Category** (General, OBC, SC, ST, EWS, PwBD)
- **Section-wise Marks** obtained in the Mains exam
- **Total Marks** and **Qualifying Status**
- **Provisional Allotment Status** — whether you are allotted to a participating bank
- **Cut-off Marks** applicable to your state and category

## What Happens Next?

The IBPS Clerk Mains is the **final written stage** — there is no interview for this post. However, the result is **provisional** and subject to:

- **Document Verification (DV)** at the allotted bank
- **Local Language Proficiency Test (LPT)** where applicable
- Confirmation of eligibility criteria as per IBPS norms

Candidates who are provisionally allotted must report to their assigned bank with original documents for verification. Keep all exam-related documents and your scorecard safely.
