## WBJEE 2026 Result — Expected in June 2026

The West Bengal Joint Entrance Examinations Board (WBJEEB) is expected to declare the **WBJEE 2026 result in June 2026**. The examination was held on **April 27, 2026** (Sunday) in two shifts — the forenoon shift for Mathematics and the afternoon shift for Physics and Chemistry.

> **Note:** As of June 2, 2026, WBJEEB has not officially announced the result date. This page will be updated as soon as results are declared. Check **wbjeeb.nic.in** for official updates.

WBJEE is used for admission to engineering and technology undergraduate programmes at government, government-aided, and self-financed universities and colleges in West Bengal.

## How to Download WBJEE 2026 Rank Card

When the result is declared:

1. Visit the official website — **wbjeeb.nic.in**
2. Click on **"WBJEE 2026 Rank Card"** link
3. Enter your **Application Number** and **Date of Birth**
4. Click Submit — your rank card will be displayed
5. Download and save the rank card (PDF) for counselling

---

## WBJEE 2026 Rank Card — Details Mentioned

Your rank card will include:
- Candidate name and application number
- Subject-wise marks obtained
- **WBJEE 2026 Overall Rank**
- Category rank (GEN / OBC-A / OBC-B / SC / ST)
- Qualifying status

## WBJEE 2026 Qualifying Criteria

Candidates must score **minimum marks** in each subject group to be ranked. WBJEEB typically announces the minimum qualifying marks along with the result. Candidates who do not meet the qualifying threshold are not assigned a rank.

*Exact qualifying marks for 2026 will be announced with the result.*

## WBJEE 2026 Counselling (After Result)

After rank declaration, WBJEEB or the respective participating institutions conduct counselling for admissions to:
- Government engineering colleges in West Bengal (MAKAUT affiliated)
- Jadavpur University (separate counselling via WBJEEB)
- Government-aided and self-financed engineering colleges
- Some pharmacy and architecture seats

WBJEE rank is also used by **IIEST Shibpur** for admission (in addition to JEE Advanced).

*Counselling schedule will be announced post result on wbjeeb.nic.in.*
