## How to Check and Download Your CUET UG 2026 Scorecard

Follow these simple, step-by-step instructions to check and download your CUET UG 2026 result and scorecard as soon as it is declared:

1.  **Visit the Official Website**: Open your web browser and navigate to the official CUET UG portal: `https://cuet.samarth.ac.in/`.
2.  **Locate Result Link**: On the homepage, look for a prominent link titled "CUET UG 2026 Result" or "CUET UG 2026 Scorecard". This link will become active once the results are officially announced.
3.  **Login Credentials**: Click on the result link. You will be redirected to a login page where you need to enter the following details:
    *   Your CUET UG 2026 Application Number.
    *   Your Date of Birth (DD/MM/YYYY) or Password.
4.  **Enter Security Pin**: A security pin (captcha) will be displayed on the screen. Carefully enter the characters as shown in the image.
5.  **Submit**: After entering all the required information, click on the "Submit" or "Login" button.
6.  **View Scorecard**: Your CUET UG 2026 Scorecard will be displayed on your screen.
7.  **Download and Print**: Review all the details on your scorecard. It is highly recommended to download the PDF version of the scorecard and take multiple printouts for future reference, especially during the counselling and admission process.

*Note: In case of heavy traffic, the website might be slow. Please be patient and try again after some time.* Also, ensure you are checking the results only on the official NTA or CUET Samarth portals to avoid misinformation.

## Details Mentioned on the CUET UG 2026 Scorecard

Your CUET UG 2026 Scorecard is a crucial document and will contain the following important information:

*   **Candidate's Name**: Your full name as provided in the application form.
*   **Roll Number**: Your unique examination roll number.
*   **Application Number**: Your CUET UG 2026 application form number.
*   **Father's Name**: Your father's name.
*   **Mother's Name**: Your mother's name.
*   **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth.
*   **Gender**: Your gender.
*   **Category**: Your social category (e.g., General, SC, ST, OBC-NCL, EWS).
*   **Photograph and Signature**: Your scanned photograph and signature uploaded during registration.
*   **Subject-wise NTA Score**: The NTA Score obtained by you in each subject attempted.
*   **Subject-wise Percentile Score**: The percentile score for each subject, indicating your relative performance.
*   **Total NTA Score**: The aggregate NTA score, if applicable for specific university requirements.
*   **Qualifying Status**: Information regarding your eligibility for further admission processes.

It is imperative to carefully check all these details for any discrepancies. In case of any errors, candidates should immediately contact the NTA helpdesk.

## What to Do After CUET UG 2026 Results

The declaration of CUET UG 2026 results marks the beginning of the admission phase. Here's what you need to do next:

1.  **Retain Your Scorecard**: Keep several copies of your downloaded scorecard safe. It is an essential document for all future admission processes.
2.  **Check University-Specific Counselling**: NTA does not conduct a centralized counselling process for CUET UG. Instead, individual participating universities (Central, State, Deemed, and Private) will release their own admission notifications and conduct their counselling based on the CUET UG NTA scores.
3.  **Visit University Websites**: Regularly check the official websites of your target universities for their specific admission policies, merit lists, cutoff scores, registration deadlines for counselling, and detailed instructions.
4.  **Register for Counselling**: You will need to register separately on the admission portals of the universities you wish to apply to, using your CUET UG scores.
5.  **Document Verification**: Prepare all necessary documents for verification during the counselling process. This typically includes mark sheets of 10th and 12th standards, CUET UG 2026 scorecard, admit card, caste certificate (if applicable), income certificate (if applicable), transfer certificate, migration certificate, passport-size photographs, etc.
6.  **Understand Cutoffs**: Each university and course will have specific cutoff scores. Analyse these cutoffs based on your NTA score to make informed decisions about your preferred institutions and courses.

### Q2: How can I check my CUET UG 2026 result?
A2: You can check your result by visiting the official CUET UG website at `https://cuet.samarth.ac.in/`. You will need to log in using your CUET UG 2026 Application Number and Date of Birth (or Password).

### Q3: What is an NTA Score in CUET UG?
A3: The NTA Score is a normalized score used in multi-shift entrance examinations like CUET UG. It is a percentile equivalent, representing the percentage of candidates who scored equal to or below that particular score. It helps ensure fairness across different exam shifts.

### Q4: What should I do if there's a discrepancy in my CUET UG 2026 scorecard?
A4: In case of any discrepancy (e.g., incorrect personal details, score calculation error), you should immediately contact the NTA Helpdesk through their official contact channels available on `https://nta.ac.in/`.

### Q5: Will NTA conduct centralized counselling for CUET UG admissions?
A5: No, the NTA is only responsible for conducting the exam and declaring results. The admission and counselling process will be conducted individually by each participating university based on the CUET UG 2026 scores.

### Q6: Can I apply for re-evaluation or re-checking of my CUET UG 2026 result?
A6: NTA typically does not entertain requests for re-evaluation or re-checking of CUET UG results. The NTA scores are considered final. However, you should refer to the official information bulletin for the most accurate policy regarding this.
