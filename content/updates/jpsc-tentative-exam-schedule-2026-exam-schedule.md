## JPSC Tentative Exam Schedule 2026

The Jharkhand Public Service Commission (JPSC) has released a tentative examination schedule for 2026. This is an early announcement that gives candidates advance notice to plan their preparation — detailed notifications with exact dates, vacancies, and application windows will be released separately for each examination.

> **Note:** This is a tentative schedule. All confirmed dates and notifications will be published at [jpsc.gov.in](https://jpsc.gov.in). Check the official website regularly for updates.

## Examinations Expected in 2026

Based on the tentative schedule and JPSC's typical recruitment cycle, the following examinations are expected:

- **JPSC Civil Services (Combined) Exam** — Jharkhand's equivalent of the state civil services exam
- **JPSC Combined Graduate Level** — For various Group B & C posts
- **JPSC Lecturer/Teaching posts** — For state government colleges
- **JPSC Combined Civil Services (Prelims + Mains)** — Three-stage selection

## Selection Process

The JPSC Civil Services Examination follows a three-stage process:

1. **Preliminary Exam** — Objective MCQ screening test (marks not counted in final merit)
2. **Mains Exam** — Descriptive papers; marks count towards final rank
3. **Interview / Personality Test** — Final stage for shortlisted candidates

## FAQs

**When will the detailed JPSC 2026 notification be released?**
Detailed notifications for each examination will be released separately on [jpsc.gov.in](https://jpsc.gov.in). JPSC typically releases notifications 2–3 months before the application window opens.

**What is the exam pattern for JPSC Civil Services Preliminary?**
The Prelims consists of two papers — General Studies I and General Studies II (CSAT) — each of 200 marks. Both are objective MCQ-based.

**Is Jharkhand domicile mandatory for JPSC?**
For most posts, Jharkhand domicile or local language proficiency (Hindi/Urdu/Santhali) is required. Some reserved-category fee relaxations are only for Jharkhand domicile candidates.

**How many vacancies are expected in JPSC 2026?**
Vacancy counts are not yet announced in the tentative schedule. Previous JPSC Combined Civil Services cycles recruited 200–600 candidates. Exact numbers will be in the official notification.
