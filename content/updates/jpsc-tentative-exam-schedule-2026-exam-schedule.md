**Typical Selection Process (as per JPSC rules):**
The selection process for major JPSC examinations, such as the Civil Services Exam, generally involves three distinct stages:

1.  **Preliminary Examination (Prelims):** A screening test consisting of objective-type multiple-choice questions. Marks obtained in Prelims are usually for shortlisting candidates for the Mains exam and are not counted in the final merit list.
2.  **Main Examination (Mains):** A descriptive-type examination designed to assess the candidate's academic talent, intellectual capabilities, and understanding of various subjects. Marks from the Mains exam are crucial for the final merit list.
3.  **Interview/Personality Test:** Candidates who qualify the Mains exam are called for an interview. This stage assesses the candidate's general awareness, personality, communication skills, and suitability for public service.
