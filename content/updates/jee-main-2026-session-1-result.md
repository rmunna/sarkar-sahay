## What is JEE Main 2026?

JEE Main is the national-level entrance examination conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) for admission to BTech, BE, BArch, and BPlanning programmes at NITs, IIITs, and other centrally funded institutions. It also serves as the qualifying exam for JEE Advanced, the gateway to IITs.

## JEE Main 2026 Session 1 Result

The NTA declared the JEE Main 2026 Session 1 (January session) result on **February 16, 2026**, at jeemain.nta.nic.in. The result was originally expected on February 12 but was postponed by a few days.

Over **13 lakh candidates** appeared for the January session examination.

## How to Check JEE Main 2026 Session 1 Result

1. Visit the official website: [jeemain.nta.nic.in](https://jeemain.nta.nic.in)
2. Click on "Session 1 Result / Scorecard" link
3. Log in with your Application Number and Date of Birth
4. Your scorecard will display percentile scores in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics
5. Download and save the scorecard PDF for future reference

## What the Scorecard Contains

- **Subject-wise NTA Percentile** — Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics
- **Overall Aggregate NTA Percentile**
- Note: Cut-off marks and All India Rank (AIR) will **not** be included in Session 1 results. These will be announced after Session 2 is completed.

## About NTA Percentile Score

The NTA uses a **normalisation process** to ensure fairness across different shifts and papers. Raw marks are converted into a standardised NTA percentile score ranging from 0 to 100. A percentile of 90 means you scored better than 90% of all candidates.
