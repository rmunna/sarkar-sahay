## SSC Delhi Police Constable (Executive) Result 2025 — Overview

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has officially declared the result for the Delhi Police Constable (Executive) Examination 2025. This highly anticipated result was announced on **June 19, 2026**, for candidates who appeared in the Computer Based Examination (CBE) held on **December 18, 2025**. This examination aims to fill a total of **7177 vacancies** for Constable (Executive) posts in Delhi Police. Candidates who appeared for the examination can now check their individual results and status directly on the official website of the Staff Selection Commission, ssc.gov.in. The declaration of results marks a crucial step in the recruitment process, leading candidates towards the subsequent stages of selection.

## How to Check Your Result Online

Checking your SSC Delhi Police Constable (Executive) Result 2025 is a straightforward process. Follow the steps below to access your result and download it for future reference:

1.  **Visit the Official SSC Website:** Open your web browser and navigate to the official Staff Selection Commission website: [ssc.gov.in](https://ssc.gov.in).
2.  **Locate the 'Results' Section:** On the homepage, you will typically find a prominent section or tab labeled "Results." Click on this tab to proceed.
3.  **Select the Relevant Exam:** Within the results section, look for a link related to "Constable (Executive) in Delhi Police Examination 2025" or similar. The results are often categorized by examination name and year.
4.  **Click on the Result Link:** Once you find the correct examination, click on the result link. This might lead you to a PDF document containing the roll numbers of qualified candidates, or a login portal to check individual scores.
5.  **Enter Your Credentials (if required):** If prompted, enter your Roll Number, Registration Number, and/or Date of Birth as per your admit card. Ensure all details are entered accurately.
6.  **Submit and View Your Result:** After entering the required information, click on the "Submit" or "Login" button. Your result will be displayed on the screen.
7.  **Download and Print:** It is highly recommended to download your result/marksheet and take a printout for your records. This will be essential for the subsequent stages of the selection process.

## What Happens Next — After the Result

The declaration of the Computer Based Examination (CBE) result is a significant milestone. For candidates who have successfully qualified, the journey continues to the next crucial stages of the selection process. As per the official notification, the selection process for Delhi Police Constable (Executive) involves:

1.  **Computer Based Examination (CBE)** - *Result Declared*
2.  **Physical Endurance & Measurement Test (PE&MT)**

**For Qualified Candidates:**
Candidates who have cleared the Computer Based Examination will now be shortlisted to appear for the **Physical Endurance & Measurement Test (PE&MT)**. The schedule and admit cards for the PE&MT will be released by the Staff Selection Commission on its official website in due course. It is imperative for qualified candidates to regularly check [ssc.gov.in](https://ssc.gov.in) for updates regarding the PE&MT dates, venue, and specific requirements. Start preparing physically to meet the standards set for the PE&MT. Following successful completion of PE&MT, candidates will undergo **Document Verification (DV)**.

**For Candidates Who Did Not Qualify:**
Candidates who did not qualify in this examination should not be disheartened. Competitive exams are challenging, and success often comes with persistence. It is advisable to analyze your performance, identify areas for improvement, and begin preparing for future opportunities. The Staff Selection Commission conducts various examinations throughout the year, offering multiple chances to secure a government job.
