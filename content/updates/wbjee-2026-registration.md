The West Bengal Joint Entrance Examinations Board (WBJEEB) has opened WBJEE 2026 registration from **March 10, 2026**. The WBJEE 2026 exam is scheduled for **May 24, 2026**.

## How to Register for WBJEE 2026

1. Visit the official WBJEE registration website at [wbjeeb.nic.in](https://wbjeeb.nic.in/wbjee/)
2. Click on the **"New Registration"** link
3. Enter personal details — name, father's name, mother's name, date of birth, gender, and contact number
4. Fill in address details — district, state, PIN code, email ID, and mobile number
5. Create a **password** and set a security question with answer
6. Preview all details and click **"Submit"**
7. Note down your **application number** (sent to registered email and mobile)
8. Log in with your application number and password
9. Upload required **photograph and signature**
10. Pay the **application fee** online via Net Banking / Debit Card / Credit Card / UPI / QR Code
11. Download and save the **confirmation page** for future reference

## Eligibility Criteria

- **Nationality:** Indian citizen
- **Age Limit:** Must have been born on or after January 1, 2002 (General). Age relaxation applicable for reserved categories as per West Bengal government rules
- **Educational Qualification:** Passed or appearing in 10+2 (or equivalent) from a recognized board
- **Minimum Marks:** 45% aggregate in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (40% for SC/ST/OBC/PwD candidates)
- **Subjects Required:** Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as compulsory subjects in 10+2
