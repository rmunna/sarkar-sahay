HPSC HCS (Haryana Civil Services) Group A officers start at **₹56,100 basic pay** (Pay Level 10). With Dearness Allowance, HRA, and other allowances, the **in-hand salary ranges from ₹70,000 to ₹85,000/month** depending on posting location. Allied Services posts like DSP and Tehsildar have separate pay scales.

## HCS Post-wise Pay Scale

| Post | Pay Level | Starting Basic | Pay Range |

| HCS (Judicial Branch) | Level 10 | ₹56,100 | ₹56,100–₹1,77,500 |
| Deputy Superintendent of Police (DSP) | Level 10 | ₹56,100 | ₹56,100–₹1,77,500 |

*Pay structure follows Haryana 7th Pay Commission. Verify exact pay levels in the official 2026 notification.*

## Salary Growth & Career Progression

HCS officers follow a defined promotion track within Haryana IAS:

- **HCS → IAS (on empanelment):** Exceptional HCS officers are empanelled to IAS after 8–10 years of service and senior Selection Committee clearance
- **District-level posting (SDM → ADC → DC):** Each carries additional charge allowances
- **Annual increment:** 3% of basic pay every July

Senior HCS officers at the level of Additional Deputy Commissioner (ADC) or above can reach **₹1,44,200+ basic pay** (Level 14).

## Perks Beyond Salary

- **Government accommodation:** Bungalow-type official residences in district headquarters (subject to availability)
- **Vehicle & driver:** Available at SDM level and above
- **Medical:** CGHS-equivalent state health scheme for officer and family
- **LTC:** Leave Travel Concession for travel to home town twice in 4 years
- **Pension:** NPS (National Pension System) — 10% employee + 14% employer contribution
- **Children's education allowance:** ₹2,250/month per child (max 2 children)

## HCS vs IAS Salary Comparison

| Aspect | HCS Officer | IAS Officer |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Starting Basic | ₹56,100 | ₹56,100 |
| In-Hand (Year 1) | ~₹70,000–₹85,000 | ~₹70,000–₹85,000 |
| Career ceiling | DGP / ACS level | Cabinet Secretary |
| Central deputation | Limited | Yes (IAS is eligible) |

At entry level, the salary is identical. IAS officers have a broader career ceiling and central posting options.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is the salary of an HCS officer after 5 years of service?**
After 5 years, basic pay rises to approximately ₹65,000–₹70,000 with annual increments. With DA, in-hand salary at the 5-year mark is typically ₹95,000–₹1,05,000/month.

**Q: Is HCS Group A salary on par with IPS or IFS?**
Yes. All Group A services (HCS, HPS/DSP, HFS) start at Pay Level 10 (₹56,100). The differentiation comes at senior levels — IPS officers are deputed to central forces and IFS to forest service, each with distinct perks. At the same pay level, HCS, HPS, and HFS officers receive the same basic pay.

**Q: Does HPSC HCS salary include field posting allowances?**
Field postings (rural/tribal areas) attract additional hardship allowances as per Haryana government rules. Urban postings in Chandigarh or Gurugram get higher HRA. Check state government circulars for the current allowance schedule.
