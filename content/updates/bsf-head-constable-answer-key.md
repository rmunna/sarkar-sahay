BSF Head Constable Ministerial (HCM) answer key 2026 is published on [rectt.bsf.gov.in](https://rectt.bsf.gov.in) after the Computer Based Test. A **provisional key** is released first, followed by a **final answer key** after objection review. The final key is used to compute the merit list. No negative marking — wrong answers score 0.

## How to Download BSF HCM Answer Key 2026

1. Visit [rectt.bsf.gov.in](https://rectt.bsf.gov.in)
2. Navigate to the **Answer Key** section for HCM 2026
3. Download the PDF (may be question-ID wise for CBT)
4. Cross-check with your answers noted during the exam

## Scoring Rule (No Negative Marking)

| Response | Marks |
|----------|-------|
| Correct answer | +1 |
| Wrong answer | 0 |
| Unattempted | 0 |

**Your estimated score = total correct answers out of 100**

## How to Raise Objections

If you believe a provisional answer key answer is wrong:

1. Login to [rectt.bsf.gov.in](https://rectt.bsf.gov.in)
2. Go to **"Representation / Objection"** section for HCM answer key
3. Select the question number and your objection
4. Attach a supporting reference: printed page from NCERT/standard textbook, government circular, or published source
5. Submit before the objection deadline (typically 7–10 days after provisional key release)

BSF reviews all objections. Accepted objections result in:
- **Answer correction** (new correct answer applied), OR
- **Question deletion** (marks awarded to all candidates for that question)

## Tips for Using the Answer Key

- **Note answers during CBT:** Since CBT doesn't give back the question paper, note answers mentally for key questions you are unsure about — comparing with the key helps objection filing
- **Don't rely on coaching keys:** Coaching institutes sometimes publish wrong answer keys. Always wait for the official key from rectt.bsf.gov.in
- **Objection deadline is strict:** Submit within the window — even valid objections are ignored after the deadline closes

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: When does BSF release the answer key after the CBT?**
Typically within 7–15 days after the exam date. BSF announces the release on rectt.bsf.gov.in. Check the site regularly post-exam.

**Q: Is the BSF HCM answer key set-wise or question-ID wise?**
For CBT exams, BSF typically releases a question-ID wise key since questions are randomised per candidate. Your CBT performance report (available post-exam) shows your question IDs.

**Q: If I score 70+ on the provisional key, is my selection confirmed?**
No. The provisional key may change after objections. Also, selection depends on the total merit list, cutoff marks by category, and clearing all subsequent stages (PST/PET, typing, medical).
