## CUET PG 2026 Admit Card — Download from cuet.nta.nic.in

The **National Testing Agency (NTA)** has released the **CUET PG 2026 Admit Card** for candidates appearing in the Common University Entrance Test for Postgraduate programmes. Candidates can download their hall tickets from the official website **cuet.nta.nic.in**.

The CUET PG 2026 examination is scheduled to begin from **March 13, 2026**. The admit card is mandatory for entry into the examination centre.

## How to Download CUET PG 2026 Admit Card

Follow these steps to download your CUET PG hall ticket:

1. Visit the official NTA website at [cuet.nta.nic.in](https://cuet.nta.nic.in).
2. Click on **"CUET PG 2026 Admit Card"** link on the homepage.
3. Enter your **application number** and **date of birth**.
4. Enter the **security pin/captcha** shown on screen.
5. Click **"Download Admit Card"**.
6. Verify all details — name, photograph, exam centre, date, and shift.
7. Take a **colour printout** on A4 size paper.

> **Tip:** Download the admit card well in advance. Do not wait until the last day as the website may experience heavy traffic.

## CUET PG 2026 Exam City Slip

NTA releases the **exam city slip** before the admit card. The city slip indicates the city where your exam centre is allotted. If you haven't checked your city slip yet:

1. Visit [cuet.nta.nic.in](https://cuet.nta.nic.in).
2. Log in with your application number and date of birth.
3. Download the **city intimation slip**.

The exact exam centre details are available only on the admit card.

## FAQs

### What if my photo or details are wrong on the admit card?
Contact NTA immediately through the official helpline or raise a grievance on cuet.nta.nic.in. Do not skip the exam — carry the admit card along with your original ID proof.

### Can I change my exam centre after the admit card is issued?
No, exam centre changes are not allowed after the admit card is released. The centre allotted by NTA is final.

### What is the reporting time for CUET PG 2026?
Candidates must report to the exam centre at least **60 minutes before** the scheduled exam time. Gates close 30 minutes before the exam starts.

### When will CUET PG 2026 results be declared?
CUET PG results are typically declared within 4-6 weeks after the exam. Check cuet.nta.nic.in for the exact date.

### Is a colour printout of the admit card mandatory?
Yes, NTA requires a **clear colour printout** of the admit card on A4 paper. Black and white printouts may not be accepted.
