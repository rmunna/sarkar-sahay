## AP SSC Hall Ticket 2026 — Overview

The **Board of Secondary Education, Andhra Pradesh (BSEAP)** has released the **AP SSC Hall Tickets 2026** for the upcoming Class 10 board examinations. Students appearing for the AP SSC (10th Class) Annual Examination 2026 can now download their hall tickets from the official website **bse.ap.gov.in**.

The AP SSC examinations are scheduled from **March 16, 2026 to April 1, 2026**. The hall ticket is a mandatory document — no student will be allowed to enter the examination hall without it.

## How to Download AP SSC Hall Ticket 2026

Regular students will receive their hall tickets through their respective schools. Private candidates can download the AP SSC admit card online. Follow these steps:

### Step 1: Visit the Official Website

Go to the official BSEAP portal at [bse.ap.gov.in](https://bse.ap.gov.in/).

### Step 2: Find the Hall Ticket Link

On the homepage, look for the **"SSC Hall Tickets 2026"** link or navigate to the hall ticket download section.

### Step 3: Enter Your Details

Enter the required credentials such as your **registration number** or **roll number** and other details as asked.

### Step 4: Download and Print

Once the hall ticket appears on screen, verify all the details carefully. Click **Download** and take a **clear printout** on A4 paper. Keep multiple copies for safety.

> **Note:** Regular school-going students will typically receive hall tickets through their schools. The online download option is primarily available for private candidates.

### Alternative: Download via Mana Mitra on WhatsApp

BSEAP also offers hall ticket download through the **'Mana Mitra'** service on WhatsApp. Students can use this service to get their admit cards digitally.

## AP SSC 2026 Exam Schedule

The AP SSC examinations will be conducted from **March 16 to April 1, 2026**. The exams are held in two sessions:

- **Morning Session:** 9:30 AM to 12:45 PM
- Papers include First Language, Second Language, English, Mathematics, General Science, and Social Studies

> The detailed subject-wise timetable is available on the official BSEAP website. Students are advised to check [bse.ap.gov.in](https://bse.ap.gov.in/) for the complete date sheet.

The following information is printed on the AP SSC admit card:

## Important Instructions for Students

1. **Verify all details** — Check your name, photo, date of birth, and roll number immediately after receiving the hall ticket. Report any errors to your school or the BSEAP office without delay.

2. **Carry the hall ticket to every exam** — Entry to the examination hall is strictly based on the admit card. Without it, you will not be allowed to sit for the exam.

3. **Reach the exam centre early** — Arrive at least **30 minutes before** the scheduled time. Late entry may not be permitted.

4. **Carry a valid photo ID** — Along with the hall ticket, carry a school ID card or any government-issued photo identity proof.

5. **No electronic devices** — Mobile phones, smartwatches, calculators, and any electronic gadgets are **strictly prohibited** inside the exam hall.

6. **Follow exam hall rules** — Read all instructions printed on the hall ticket and the question paper carefully before starting the exam.

7. **Preserve the hall ticket** — Keep the admit card safe even after exams are over. It will be required during the results and for further admission processes.

## What to Do If There Is an Error on the Hall Ticket?

If you find any discrepancy in the details printed on your hall ticket — such as incorrect name, wrong date of birth, or photo mismatch — take the following steps:

- **Regular students:** Inform your school principal immediately. The school will raise a correction request with BSEAP.
- **Private candidates:** Contact the nearest BSEAP district office or visit [bse.ap.gov.in](https://bse.ap.gov.in/) for the grievance redressal process.

Do not attempt to make manual corrections on the hall ticket — only officially corrected admit cards are valid.
