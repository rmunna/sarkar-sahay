## How to Download AP SSC Hall Ticket 2026

Follow these steps to download your AP 10th class admit card:

1. Visit the official BSEAP website at [bse.ap.gov.in](https://bse.ap.gov.in).
2. Click on **"AP SSC Hall Tickets 2026"** link on the homepage.
3. Select your **district** from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter your **school registration number** or **SSC roll number**.
5. Click **"Download"** to view your hall ticket.
6. Verify all details — name, photo, exam centre, and subject codes.
7. Take a **printout** and keep it safe for all exam days.

> **Tip:** If the website is slow due to heavy traffic, try during early morning or late evening hours.

## AP SSC 2026 Exam Schedule

The AP 10th class exams are scheduled from **March 16 to April 1, 2026**. Exams are conducted in **morning session (9:30 AM to 12:45 PM)**.

Students should reach the exam centre at least **30 minutes before** the scheduled time.

## Documents to Carry on Exam Day

- **Printed AP SSC Hall Ticket 2026** (mandatory)
- **School ID card** or any government photo ID
- **Stationery** — blue/black pens, pencils, geometry box
- **No electronic devices** — mobile phones, calculators, and smartwatches are strictly prohibited

## What to Do If There Are Errors on Your Hall Ticket

If you find any discrepancy in your name, photo, date of birth, or exam centre:

1. Report immediately to your **school principal**.
2. The school can raise a correction request through the official BSEAP portal.
3. Get the corrected hall ticket before the first exam date.

## FAQs

### Can I download the AP SSC hall ticket from my mobile phone?
Yes, you can download it from any device with a browser by visiting bse.ap.gov.in. However, you must take a printout to carry to the exam hall.

### What if I lose my hall ticket?
You can re-download it from the BSEAP website anytime before the exams. You can also contact your school for a duplicate copy.

### Is the hall ticket mandatory for all exam days?
Yes, you must carry the printed hall ticket to every exam. Without it, you will not be allowed to enter the examination hall.

### When will AP SSC 2026 results be declared?
AP SSC results are typically announced in May-June. Check bse.ap.gov.in for the exact result date after exams conclude.

### Can private candidates also download hall tickets from bse.ap.gov.in?
Yes, private candidates can download their hall tickets from the same portal using their registration details.
