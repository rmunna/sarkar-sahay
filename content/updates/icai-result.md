## How to Check Your ICAI CA May 2026 Result Online

Follow these steps to access and download your ICAI CA Final, Intermediate, or Foundation May 2026 Statement of Marks:

1.  **Visit the Official ICAI Result Portals:** Open your web browser and navigate to any of the following official ICAI result websites:
    *   `https://icai.nic.in/`
    *   `https://icaiexam.icai.org/`
    *   `https://www.icai.org/` (and look for result links)
2.  **Locate the Result Link:** On the homepage, look for specific links related to 'CA Final Examination May 2026 Result', 'CA Intermediate Examination May 2026 Result', or 'CA Foundation Examination May 2026 Result'. Click on the relevant link.
3.  **Enter Your Credentials:** You will be redirected to a login page. Here, you will need to enter your:
    *   Roll Number
    *   PIN / Registration Number
    *   A Captcha code (for verification)
4.  **Submit and View Result:** After entering the required details, click on the 'Submit' or 'Check Result' button. Your ICAI CA May 2026 result will be displayed on the screen.
5.  **Download and Print:** It is highly recommended to download your Statement of Marks and take a printout for future reference. This will serve as a provisional mark sheet.

## What to Do After Checking ICAI CA May 2026 Results

### For Passed Candidates:
*   **Congratulations!** You have cleared a significant stage of the CA course.
*   **For Foundation Passed:** You are now eligible to register for the CA Intermediate course.
*   **For Intermediate Passed:** You are eligible to register for the CA Final course and commence your mandatory practical training (articleship).
*   **For Final Passed:** You are now a qualified Chartered Accountant! Apply for ICAI membership to officially use the 'CA' prefix.
*   **Official Mark Sheet & Pass Certificate:** ICAI dispatches physical mark sheets and pass certificates (for final qualified candidates) to registered addresses shortly after the online result declaration.

*   **Future Attempts:** Analyze your performance, identify weak areas, and strategize for your next attempt. ICAI conducts these examinations twice a year.
*   **Seek Guidance:** Talk to mentors, seniors, or coaching institutes for guidance on improving your preparation.
