# JEE Main 2026 Paper 2 Result Out — Download BArch & BPlanning Scorecard

The National Testing Agency (NTA) has declared the **JEE Main 2026 Session 1 Paper 2 result** on **February 24, 2026**. Candidates who appeared for the B.Arch and B.Planning exam (conducted on January 29, 2026) can now download their scorecards from the official portal.

## How to Download JEE Main Paper 2 Scorecard

1. Visit [jeemain.nta.nic.in](https://jeemain.nta.nic.in)
2. Click on **"Results for JEE(Main)-2026 [Session-I] [Paper-2 (B.Arch/B.Planning)]"** under Latest News
3. Log in with your **Application Number** and **Password / Date of Birth**
4. Your scorecard with percentile score will be displayed
5. Download and save for future reference

## What's on the Scorecard?

- **NTA Percentile Score** for each subject and overall
- Marks obtained in Mathematics, Aptitude Test, and Drawing Test (B.Arch) or Planning-based questions (B.Planning)
- Category-wise qualifying status

## Key Details

| Detail | Info |

| Result Portal | [jeemain.nta.nic.in](https://jeemain.nta.nic.in) |

## What Next?

- **Session 2 registration** opens tomorrow (February 25) — you can attempt again to improve your percentile
- **Best of two sessions** is considered for final ranking
- Top percentile scorers in Paper 2 can apply for **B.Arch admissions** at NITs, IIITs, and other centrally funded institutions
- **JEE Advanced** is only for Paper 1 (B.Tech) — Paper 2 scores are used separately for architecture admissions
