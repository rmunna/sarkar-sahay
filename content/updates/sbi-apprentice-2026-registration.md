## Key Details

Understanding the specifics of the SBI Apprentice 2026 recruitment is vital for all aspiring candidates. Here are the confirmed details extracted directly from the official notification:

### Total Vacancies

SBI has announced a total of **7150 Apprentice vacancies** for the 2026 recruitment cycle. This large number presents a substantial opportunity for eligible candidates across India.

### Application Fee

The application fee varies based on the candidate's category. Please note that the fee is non-refundable.

*   **General/EWS/OBC:** ₹300 (Rupees Three Hundred Only)
*   **SC/ST/PwD:** NIL (Exempted from fee payment)

Candidates must pay the application fee online using Debit Cards (RuPay/Visa/MasterCard/Maestro), Credit Cards, Internet Banking, IMPS, Cash Cards/Mobile Wallets.

### Age Limit

As of the date specified in the official notification, candidates must meet the following age criteria:

*   **Minimum Age:** 20 years
*   **Maximum Age:** 28 years

Candidates born between 01.06.1998 and 01.06.2006 (both dates inclusive) are eligible to apply. Age relaxation for reserved categories (SC/ST/OBC/PwD) will be applicable as per Government of India guidelines.

### Education Qualification

To be eligible for the SBI Apprentice 2026 program, candidates must possess the following educational qualification:

*   **Graduation from a recognized University/ Institute.**

Candidates must have completed their graduation on or before the last date of application (2026-06-08). Those in their final year/semester of graduation are not eligible to apply.

### Salary / Pay Scale

Apprentices selected under this program will receive a fixed monthly stipend:

*   **Stipend:** ₹15,000 per month
*   **Duration:** For a period of one year.

This stipend is provided to support the apprentices during their training period and is not considered a regular salary.

### Selection Process

The selection for SBI Apprentice 2026 will be based on a multi-stage process designed to assess candidates' aptitude and suitability for the role. The confirmed stages are:

1.  **Online Written Test:** This will be the primary screening stage, evaluating candidates on various subjects.
2.  **Test of Local Language:** Candidates will be required to qualify a test of the specified local language of the state/UT for which they have applied. This test is mandatory and qualifying in nature.
3.  **Medical Examination:** Shortlisted candidates will undergo a medical examination as per SBI's standard medical fitness criteria.

**www.sbi.co.in**

## What to Do Next: Step-by-Step Application Guide

Candidates interested in applying for the SBI Apprentice 2026 must follow these steps carefully to ensure a successful online registration:

**Step 1: Visit the Official Website**
Go to the official SBI career portal: www.sbi.co.in. Navigate to the "Careers" section and find the link for "Recruitment of Apprentices" or "SBI Apprentice 2026 Notification."

**Step 2: New Registration**
*   Click on the "NEW REGISTRATION" link.
*   Enter your basic details such as Name, Contact No., and Email ID.
*   A provisional registration number and password will be generated and sent to your registered email ID and mobile number. Keep these safe for future logins.

**Step 3: Fill in Application Details**
*   Log in using your provisional registration number and password.
*   Fill in all required personal, educational, and communication details accurately.
*   Ensure there are no spelling mistakes or factual errors.

**Step 4: Upload Documents**
*   Upload your scanned photograph and signature as per the specifications mentioned in the official notification (usually specific dimensions and file size).
*   Ensure the photograph is recent and clear, and the signature is on white paper with black ink.
*   You may also need to upload a scanned copy of your Left Thumb Impression and a Hand Written Declaration. Refer to the official notification for precise requirements.

**Step 5: Preview and Verify**
*   Before final submission, carefully review all the information entered in the application form.
*   Make any necessary corrections at this stage. Once submitted, details cannot be changed.

**Step 6: Pay Application Fee**
*   Proceed to the payment gateway.
*   Select your preferred payment method (Debit Card, Credit Card, Internet Banking, etc.).
*   Complete the payment process.
*   Upon successful payment, an e-receipt will be generated. Save and print this receipt for your records.

**Step 7: Final Submission and Print**
*   After successful payment, click on the "FINAL SUBMIT" button.
*   Take a printout of your complete application form for future reference. This printout is not required to be sent to SBI.

**Important Reminders:**
*   Ensure you have a valid email ID and mobile number that remain active throughout the recruitment process.
*   Keep all necessary documents (educational certificates, identity proof, photograph, signature) ready before starting the application.
*   Apply well in advance of the last date to avoid any last-minute technical glitches.
