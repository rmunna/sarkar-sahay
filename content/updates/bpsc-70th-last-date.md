## Step-by-Step: How to Apply Before the Deadline

The application process for the BPSC 70th CCE is entirely online. Follow these steps carefully to ensure a successful submission:

1.  **New Registration:**
    *   Visit the official BPSC website: bpsc.bihar.gov.in.
    *   Look for the "Apply Online" link for the "70th Combined Competitive Examination".
    *   Click on "New Registration".
    *   Fill in basic details such as your name, father's name, mother's name, date of birth, gender, mobile number, and email ID. Ensure all details match your matriculation certificate.
    *   Verify your mobile number and email ID through an OTP (One-Time Password).
    *   Upon successful registration, you will receive a Registration ID and Password. Keep these safe.

2.  **Login and Fill Application Form:**
    *   Log in using your Registration ID and Password.
    *   The application form will appear. Carefully fill in all personal, educational, and communication details.
    *   Provide details of your educational qualifications (Matriculation, Intermediate, Graduation, etc.), including board/university, year of passing, marks/percentage, and division.
    *   Select your preferred posts, if applicable, based on the notification's guidelines.
    *   Declare your reservation category (SC, ST, EBC, BC, EWS, PwD, etc.) if you belong to one. Ensure you meet the eligibility criteria and possess the required certificates issued by the competent authority of Bihar.

3.  **Upload Documents:**
    *   Scan and upload your recent passport-sized photograph and signature (both in Hindi and English) as per the specifications mentioned in the official notification.
    *   Upload scanned copies of all necessary certificates, including:
        *   Matriculation Certificate (for Date of Birth proof)
        *   Graduation Degree and Marksheet
        *   Caste Certificate (for SC/ST/EBC/BC candidates from Bihar)
        *   Non-Creamy Layer Certificate (for BC/EBC candidates from Bihar, valid for the current financial year)
        *   EWS Certificate (for Economically Weaker Section candidates from Bihar, valid for the current financial year)
        *   Domicile Certificate (for Bihar residents claiming reservation benefits)
        *   Disability Certificate (for PwD candidates)
        *   Any other relevant certificates (e.g., Ex-serviceman, Freedom Fighter Dependent).
    *   Ensure all uploaded documents are clear, legible, and meet the specified file size and format requirements.

4.  **Pay Application Fee:**
    *   After filling the form and uploading documents, proceed to the fee payment section.
    *   Select your preferred online payment mode (Net Banking, Debit Card, Credit Card).
    *   Enter the required payment details and complete the transaction.
    *   A successful payment will generate a transaction ID. Note it down.
    *   It is crucial to ensure the payment is successful. A failed payment means your application is incomplete.

5.  **Review and Submit:**
    *   Before final submission, thoroughly review all the information entered in the application form.
    *   Check for any spelling errors, factual inaccuracies, or incorrect category selections.
    *   Once satisfied, click on the "Submit" button. Please note that after final submission, you might not be able to make further changes without using the edit window (if provided).

6.  **Take Printout:**
    *   After successful submission and fee payment, a confirmation page will be displayed.
    *   Download and take a printout of the submitted application form for your records. This printout often contains your registration number, transaction ID, and other crucial details needed for future reference, including admit card download.

## Common Mistakes That Cause Rejection

Many applications get rejected due to avoidable errors. Be vigilant to prevent these common mistakes:

*   **Incorrect/Unclear Photograph or Signature:** Blurry, old, or improperly sized images are a frequent cause of rejection. Ensure your photo is recent, clear, and meets all specifications.
*   **Mismatch in Name/Date of Birth:** Discrepancies between the application form and your matriculation certificate (or other official documents) for your name, father's name, or date of birth can lead to disqualification at later stages.
*   **Incorrect Category Selection:** Selecting the wrong reservation category, especially if you are from another state (who are treated as General in Bihar), or failing to provide a valid, current Non-Creamy Layer/EWS certificate for Bihar domicile candidates, will result in rejection of reservation claims.
*   **Application Fee Not Paid Successfully:** An incomplete payment transaction means your application is not submitted. Always verify that the payment was successful and you received a confirmation.
*   **Incomplete Application Form:** Leaving mandatory fields blank or not uploading all required documents will render your application incomplete and liable for rejection.
*   **Uploading Invalid/Expired Certificates:** Using an outdated Non-Creamy Layer or EWS certificate (not valid for the current financial year) is a common error. Ensure all reservation-related certificates are current and issued by the competent authority of Bihar.
*   **Multiple Applications:** Submitting more than one application form for the same examination is strictly prohibited and usually leads to the rejection of all your applications.

## What If You Miss the Deadline

Missing the application deadline for the BPSC 70th CCE is a serious matter, as the Commission is generally very strict about its timelines.

*   **Is an extension likely?** Extensions are rare and typically granted only in exceptional circumstances, such as widespread technical glitches on the BPSC server that prevent a large number of candidates from applying. Minor individual issues or personal delays are not usually considered grounds for an extension.
*   **Historical Precedent:** Historically, BPSC has maintained a firm stance on application deadlines. Aspirants should not rely on the possibility of an extension. The best practice is to apply well in advance of the closing date.
*   **Consequence:** If you miss the final deadline, your opportunity to apply for the BPSC 70th CCE 2026 will be lost, and you will have to wait for the next recruitment cycle.

Therefore, it is imperative to mark the application start and end dates prominently and complete your application process without procrastination.

## FAQs

Here are answers to some frequently asked questions regarding the BPSC application process:

**Q1: Can I edit the form after submission?**
A1: BPSC usually provides a specific "Edit Window" for a few days after the final submission deadline. During this period, candidates can log in and make corrections to certain fields, sometimes for an additional fee. However, critical details like name, date of birth, and registration ID are often uneditable. Always check the official notification for specific details on the edit window.

**Q2: What if my payment fails or gets stuck?**
A2: If your payment fails, try again. If the amount is debited but the application status isn't updated, wait for 24-48 hours. Sometimes, it takes time for the payment gateway to reconcile. If the issue persists, contact your bank and the BPSC helpline (details usually provided in the notification) with your transaction ID. Do not attempt multiple payments if the first one is pending.

**Q3: Is a late fee accepted for BPSC applications?**
A3: No, BPSC generally does not accept applications with a late fee. All applications must be submitted and the fee paid within the stipulated deadline. There is no provision for late submissions.

**Q4: Which browser works best for the BPSC online application?**
A4: It is generally recommended to use popular and updated web browsers like Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, or Microsoft Edge for the BPSC online application portal. Ensure your browser is updated to its latest version and clear your browser cache and cookies if you encounter any issues. Avoid using mobile browsers for critical steps.

**Q5: Can I apply offline for the BPSC 70th CCE?**
A5: No, the application process for the BPSC 70th CCE is strictly online. There is no provision for offline application submission. All steps, from registration to fee payment and final submission, must be completed through the official BPSC website.
