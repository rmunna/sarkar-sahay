## CSBC Constable 2025 Result — Overview

The Central Selection Board of Constable (CSBC), Bihar, has officially declared the much-awaited result for the CSBC Constable 2025 recruitment examination. Candidates who appeared for the written examination can now check their results online on the official CSBC Bihar website. This recruitment drive aims to fill a significant 19,838 vacancies for Constable posts across Bihar, making it a crucial opportunity for many aspirants.

The written examination for CSBC Constable 2025 was conducted on 2025-07-16, and the results were officially released on 2026-05-27. This result marks the first major step in the multi-stage selection process. Candidates who have successfully qualified in the written exam will proceed to the next stages, which include the Physical Efficiency Test (PET), Document Verification, and Medical Examination. While the total number of candidates who appeared for the exam and the overall pass percentage have not been officially released yet, individual results are available for all candidates to check online.

## How to Check Your Result Online

Checking your CSBC Constable 2025 result is a straightforward process. Follow the steps below to access your scorecard and determine your qualification status:

1.  **Visit the Official Website:** Open your web browser and navigate to the official website of the Central Selection Board of Constable, Bihar: [csbc.bihar.gov.in](https://csbc.bihar.gov.in).
2.  **Locate the Results Section:** On the homepage, look for a dedicated section or tab labeled "Results" or "परिणाम". This is usually prominently displayed.
3.  **Find the Relevant Result Link:** Within the results section, search for the link specifically pertaining to "CSBC Constable 2025 Result" or "Bihar Police Constable Recruitment Exam 2025 Result". Click on this link.
4.  **Enter Your Credentials:** You will be redirected to a new page where you need to enter your examination credentials. This typically includes your Roll Number and/or Registration Number. In some cases, your Date of Birth might also be required for verification. Ensure you enter these details accurately as per your admit card.
5.  **Submit and View:** After entering the required information, click on the "Submit" or "View Result" button.
6.  **Download Your Result:** Your CSBC Constable 2025 result will be displayed on the screen. It is highly recommended to download the result in PDF format and take a printout for future reference and record-keeping.

## What Happens Next — After the Result

For candidates who have successfully qualified in the CSBC Constable 2025 written examination, the journey continues with the next crucial stages of the selection process. As per the official notification, the selection process involves:

1.  **Written Exam:** (Completed, results declared)
2.  **Physical Efficiency Test (PET):** Qualified candidates will be called for the PET. This stage assesses candidates' physical fitness and endurance through various tasks like running, long jump, high jump, etc. The dates and detailed schedule for the PET will be announced by CSBC Bihar on their official website, [csbc.bihar.gov.in](https://csbc.bihar.gov.in). Candidates are advised to start preparing physically for this rigorous stage.
3.  **Document Verification (DV):** Candidates who clear the PET will proceed to Document Verification. Here, all original documents submitted during the application process, along with other necessary certificates (educational qualifications, caste certificate, domicile, etc.), will be thoroughly checked against the information provided. Any discrepancy can lead to disqualification.
4.  **Medical Examination:** The final stage involves a comprehensive medical examination to ensure candidates meet the prescribed physical and medical standards for the Constable post.

Candidates who did not qualify in the written examination should not be disheartened. Competitive exams are challenging, and success often comes with persistence. It is advisable to analyze your performance, identify areas for improvement, and prepare for future opportunities. There is generally no provision for re-evaluation or compartment exams for competitive recruitment tests like the CSBC Constable exam.
