## How to Check Your Result Online

Checking your NIOS Secondary April/May 2026 result online is a straightforward process. Follow these numbered steps carefully to access your marksheet:

1.  **Visit the Official NIOS Results Website:** Open your web browser and navigate to the official NIOS results portal: [results.nios.ac.in](https://results.nios.ac.in). Ensure you are on the correct domain to avoid fraudulent websites.
2.  **Locate the Result Link:** On the homepage, look for the section dedicated to results. You will find a prominent link titled "Secondary Examination Result April/May 2026" or similar. Click on this link.
3.  **Enter Your Roll Number:** A new page will open, prompting you to enter your examination roll number. Carefully type in your 12-digit NIOS Secondary Examination Roll Number as it appears on your admit card. Double-check for any typing errors.
4.  **Submit Your Details:** After entering your roll number, click on the "Submit" or "View Result" button.
5.  **View Your Result:** Your NIOS Secondary April/May 2026 result will be displayed on the screen. It will show your subject-wise marks, total marks, and pass/fail status.
6.  **Download and Print:** It is highly recommended to download a copy of your provisional marksheet. You can also take a printout for your immediate reference and record-keeping. This provisional result can be useful for initial applications, but remember that the official physical marksheet will be required for all formal purposes.

## What Happens Next — After the Result

The declaration of NIOS Secondary results marks a significant turning point for students. The next steps depend largely on whether a student has passed the examination or not.

**For Students Who Have Passed:**

*   **Collect Official Marksheet:** Passed candidates will need to collect their official marksheet, migration certificate, and provisional certificate from their respective Accredited Institutions (AIs) or Study Centres. The dates for the distribution of these documents will be announced by NIOS separately.
*   **DigiLocker Access:** NIOS usually uploads digital copies of marksheets and certificates to DigiLocker. Students can access and download these digital documents, which are legally valid as per the IT Act, 2000.
*   **Further Education:** Successful candidates can now apply for admission to Senior Secondary (Class 12th) courses, vocational training programs, or other higher education opportunities. It is advisable to keep all original documents safe and make multiple attested copies for application purposes.
*   **Career Planning:** For those looking to enter the workforce, the NIOS Secondary certificate is a foundational qualification.

**For Students Who Have Not Passed:**

*   **Compartment/Supplementary Examination:** Students who have not passed in one or two subjects typically have the option to appear for a compartment or supplementary examination to clear those subjects without having to repeat the entire year. The application process and dates for these exams will be announced by NIOS on their official website.
*   **Re-evaluation / Re-checking:** If a student believes there has been an error in the marking of their answer script, they can apply for re-checking or re-evaluation. This process involves a review of the marks awarded. Details regarding the application window, fees, and procedure will be released by NIOS.
*   **Improvement Examination:** Students who have passed but wish to improve their marks in certain subjects may also be eligible to appear for an improvement examination.
*   **Seek Guidance:** It is advisable for students who have not passed to consult with their study centre coordinators or NIOS counsellors to understand the best course of action for their academic future.

## Re-evaluation / Re-checking / Compartment Exam

NIOS provides specific provisions for students who wish to review their results or clear subjects they could not pass.

**Re-checking of Answer Scripts:**
NIOS typically offers a facility for re-checking of answer scripts. This process involves:
*   Re-totalling of marks.
*   Ensuring all answers have been evaluated.
*   Checking for any unmarked answers.
*   Verifying that marks have been correctly transferred to the marksheet.
*   It does **not** involve re-evaluation of the content of the answers.

**Photocopy of Evaluated Answer Script:**
Students can also apply for a photocopy of their evaluated answer script. This allows them to personally review their answers and the marks awarded, which can be helpful in deciding whether to apply for re-checking or to understand areas for improvement.

**Compartment/Supplementary Examination:**
For students who have failed in one or two subjects, NIOS conducts supplementary examinations (often referred to as Public Examinations in October/November). This provides another opportunity to pass the failed subjects without waiting for the next full examination cycle.

**Important Note on Dates and Fees:**
The specific application window, detailed procedure, and applicable fees for re-checking, obtaining photocopies of answer scripts, and applying for compartment/supplementary examinations are **not announced in the official result notice**. NIOS will release separate notifications with these crucial details on their official website, [results.nios.ac.in](https://results.nios.ac.in), shortly after the result declaration. Candidates are strongly advised to regularly check the official website for these updates and adhere to the deadlines.
