TNPSC Group 2 posts are classified under Tamil Nadu government's pay structure. Most Group 2 posts fall under **Pay Band 2 (₹9,300–₹34,800 pre-revised) or Grade Pay ₹4,200–₹4,800**, now revised to the Tamil Nadu 7th Pay Commission-equivalent scale. The starting basic for most Group 2 posts is **₹36,400/month**, with in-hand salary approximately **₹48,000–₹58,000/month** in Chennai, including allowances.

## Salary Growth & Promotion Path

Group 2 posts offer a structured promotion track within Tamil Nadu government departments:

- **Sub-Registrar → District Registrar → Inspector General of Registration** (within TN Registration Department)
- **Revenue Officer → RDO (Revenue Divisional Officer) → Collector** (requires departmental promotion exams)
- **Municipal Commissioner III → II → I → Joint Commissioner** (within Municipal Administration)

Each promotion brings increment to the next pay level with 3% annual basic pay increment.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is the Sub-Registrar salary in Tamil Nadu in 2026?**
A Sub-Registrar in Tamil Nadu starts at approximately ₹36,400 basic pay. With DA, HRA, and allowances in Chennai, the gross salary is approximately ₹58,000–₹62,000/month. In-hand (after NPS, professional tax) is around ₹52,000–₹56,000.

**Q: Does TNPSC Group 2 salary follow 7th Pay Commission?**
Tamil Nadu implemented its own pay revision (effective 2017) aligned with the 7th Pay Commission principles but with state-specific adjustments. DA revision follows a TN-specific timeline. Confirm the current pay matrix from the official TNPSC notification or Tamil Nadu government G.O.

**Q: What are the perks beyond salary in TNPSC Group 2 posts?**
Group 2 state government employees get: DA (revised periodically), HRA, medical benefits under Tamil Nadu Government Health Scheme (TNHS), LTC, children's education allowance, NPS-based pension, and government quarters (subject to availability in the posting district).
