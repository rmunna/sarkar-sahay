## How to Check Result

Students can follow the step-by-step guide below to check their CBSE Class 12 Board Examination 2026 results:

1.  **Visit the Official Website:** Open your web browser and navigate to the official CBSE results portal: [cbseresults.nic.in](https://cbseresults.nic.in/).
2.  **Locate the Result Link:** On the homepage, look for the link specifically for "CBSE Class 12 Board Examination 2026 Result" or similar. Click on this link.
3.  **Enter Required Credentials:** You will be redirected to a new page where you need to enter your examination details. Typically, you will be required to enter:
    *   Your Roll Number
    *   Your School Number
    *   Your Admit Card ID
    Ensure that all details are entered accurately as per your admit card.
4.  **Submit Details:** After entering the required information, click on the "Submit" or "Get Result" button.
5.  **View and Download Result:** Your CBSE Class 12 Board Examination 2026 result will be displayed on the screen. Carefully review all the details. It is advisable to download the provisional mark sheet and take a printout for future reference. This printout serves as a temporary document until the original mark sheet and passing certificate are issued by the board.
