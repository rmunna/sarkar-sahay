## CBSE Class 12 Board Examination 2026 Result — Overview

The Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) declared the Class 12 Board Examination 2026 result on **25 May 2026**. Every year, over 14 lakh students appear for the CBSE Class 12 boards — one of India's largest annual examinations. This result directly determines college admission across all major universities and central university admission portals (CUET).

Students can check their individual result, subject-wise marks, and grade on the official result portal [cbseresults.nic.in](https://cbseresults.nic.in). The provisional online result is valid for initial college applications; the original marksheet and passing certificate are distributed by schools separately.

The CBSE Class 12 exams for 2026 were conducted from February to March 2026 across thousands of exam centres in India and abroad.

## How to Check Your CBSE Class 12 Result 2026 Online

Follow these steps carefully to view and download your result:

1. **Go to the official result portal**: Visit [cbseresults.nic.in](https://cbseresults.nic.in) — this is the primary CBSE result website maintained by NIC (National Informatics Centre).
2. **Find the correct result link**: On the homepage, click on **"Senior School Certificate Examination (Class XII) — 2026"**.
3. **Enter your credentials**: You will need all three of the following from your admit card:
   - **Roll Number** (printed on your admit card)
   - **School Number** (your school's CBSE code)
   - **Admit Card ID** (a unique number on your admit card)
4. **Click Submit / Get Result**: After entering the details correctly, click the submit button.
5. **View your result**: Your subject-wise marks and qualifying status will appear on screen.
6. **Download and save**: Click the download or print option to save the provisional marksheet as a PDF. Keep 2–3 printed copies — colleges will ask for one during admission.

> **Tip:** The result portal sees extremely high traffic on the declaration day. If cbseresults.nic.in is slow, try the CBSE DigiLocker link or alternate portals like results.gov.in (if activated by CBSE).

## What Is Shown on the Marksheet

The online provisional marksheet for CBSE Class 12 2026 shows:

- **Candidate details**: Name, roll number, mother's name, father's name, school name
- **Subject-wise marks**: Marks obtained vs. maximum marks for each subject
- **Total marks** and **overall percentage**
- **Grades**: A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, C2, D, E (for each subject)
- **Qualifying status**: Pass / Compartment / Essential Repeat / Absent
- **Special remarks**: Improvement, Fail, etc. (if applicable)

**What the grades mean:**  
A1 = 91–100 | A2 = 81–90 | B1 = 71–80 | B2 = 61–70 | C1 = 51–60 | C2 = 41–50 | D = 33–40 | E (Fail) = Below 33

**Passing criteria**: Students must score at least 33% in each subject AND 33% in aggregate to pass. Those failing in one subject get a Compartment chance; those failing in two or more are considered failed.

## Get Your Digital Marksheet on DigiLocker

CBSE issues the official digital academic documents on DigiLocker — these are equivalent to the original marksheet for most college admissions and are accepted by CUET, DU, and most state universities.

1. Visit [digilocker.gov.in](https://digilocker.gov.in) or open the DigiLocker app
2. Register or log in using your mobile number (linked to Aadhaar)
3. In **Issued Documents**, search for **CBSE** under Education
4. Your Class 12 marksheet, passing certificate, and migration certificate will appear here once published by CBSE (usually within 1–2 weeks of result declaration)

## Compartment Examination — What to Do If You Didn't Pass

If you received a **Compartment** result (failed in one subject), you are eligible for the CBSE Compartment Examination 2026:

- **Who can appear**: Students with compartment in one subject
- **Exam timing**: Typically held in **July 2026** — exact dates announced on [cbse.gov.in](https://cbse.gov.in) after the main result
- **Registration**: Done through your school; CBSE notifies schools about the process
- **If you pass the compartment exam**: Your final result is updated to "Pass" and you are eligible for admission

Students with two or more subjects failed must re-appear as a private candidate in the next regular examination cycle.

## Re-checking and Re-evaluation of Answer Sheets

CBSE offers the following post-result options if you are unsatisfied with your marks:

| Option | What It Covers | Approximate Fee |
|--------|---------------|-----------------|
| **Verification of Marks** | Checking if marks were totalled correctly | ₹500 per subject |
| **Photocopy of Answer Sheet** | Getting a scanned copy of your evaluated answer sheet | ₹700 per subject |
| **Re-evaluation** | Full re-checking of specific question answers | ₹100 per question |

- **How to apply**: Online through the CBSE student portal — link is activated after the result.
- **Deadline**: Usually **15–20 days after result declaration** — exact date announced on [cbse.gov.in](https://cbse.gov.in).
- **Important**: You can only challenge specific questions after reviewing your photocopy. Apply for photocopy first.

## What to Do After the Result — Next Steps

**If you passed:**
- Apply for college admissions on the CUET (UG) 2026 portal — most central universities use CUET scores alongside Class 12 marks
- Download your DigiLocker documents for admission forms
- Your school will distribute the original marksheet and passing certificate within 4–6 weeks
- Apply for a Migration Certificate from CBSE if you plan to join a university in a different state

**If you got Compartment:**
- Register for the CBSE Compartment Exam 2026 through your school immediately
- Continue applying to colleges where admission is provisional or where results are declared later

## FAQs

**Q1: When was CBSE Class 12 Result 2026 declared?**  
A1: The CBSE Class 12 Board Examination 2026 result was declared on **25 May 2026**. You can check it at [cbseresults.nic.in](https://cbseresults.nic.in).

**Q2: The website is not opening / very slow — what do I do?**  
A2: High traffic is normal on result day. Try again after 30–60 minutes, or try alternate result portals like results.gov.in or the DigiLocker app. You can also ask your school to check, as schools have a separate CBSE portal access.

**Q3: Is the online result printout valid for college admission?**  
A3: Yes, the provisional online marksheet is accepted for initial registration at most colleges and universities. However, you will need the original marksheet (distributed by your school) for final admission confirmation and document verification.

**Q4: I got a compartment in one subject — can I still apply to colleges?**  
A4: Yes, many colleges offer provisional admission pending your compartment exam result. Appear for the compartment exam (typically July 2026) to clear it. Check each college's individual policy.

**Q5: What are the passing marks in CBSE Class 12?**  
A5: You must score at least **33% in each subject individually** AND **33% in aggregate** to pass. Theory and practical marks are counted together for each subject.

**Q6: How to get my marksheet on DigiLocker?**  
A6: Log in to [digilocker.gov.in](https://digilocker.gov.in) and search for CBSE under Issued Documents. Your Class 12 marksheet will appear there, usually 1–2 weeks after the result. DigiLocker marksheets are officially valid for admissions.

**Q7: How do I apply for re-evaluation of my answer sheet?**  
A7: First apply for a photocopy of your answer sheet through the CBSE student portal (application window opens ~15 days after result). After reviewing the photocopy, you can challenge specific questions for re-evaluation. Fees apply per question and per subject. Check [cbse.gov.in](https://cbse.gov.in) for the exact deadline.
