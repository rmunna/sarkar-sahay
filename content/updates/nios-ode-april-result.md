## Re-evaluation / Re-checking / Compartment Exam

For students who are not satisfied with their NIOS ODE April 2026 results or have not cleared certain subjects, NIOS typically provides mechanisms for re-evaluation, re-checking, and appearing for compartment examinations.

**Re-checking of Answer Scripts:**
Re-checking usually involves a re-totalling of marks and ensuring that all answers have been evaluated. It does not involve a re-evaluation of the content of the answers. If there's any discrepancy in marks awarded or calculation, it will be rectified. The application window, prescribed fee, and detailed procedure for applying for re-checking will be announced separately by NIOS on its official website. Students should look for a dedicated notification regarding this process.

**Re-evaluation of Answer Scripts:**
Re-evaluation involves a fresh assessment of the answer script by another examiner. This option is generally available for theory papers. If you are confident that your performance was significantly better than the marks awarded, you might consider this option. Similar to re-checking, the specific dates, application form, and fee for re-evaluation are **not yet announced in the official result notification**. Candidates are strongly advised to monitor results.nios.ac.in for the official announcement.

**Compartment / Supplementary Examinations:**
NIOS provides opportunities for students to clear subjects they have failed in. These are often conducted as part of the subsequent examination cycles or specific supplementary exams. For the NIOS ODE April 2026 batch, students who wish to appear for compartment exams or improve their marks can typically register for the next available On-Demand Examination cycle. The registration process, eligibility criteria, and examination dates for these subsequent opportunities will be published on the official NIOS website. As of the result declaration, the specific dates for compartment/supplementary exams are **not announced in the official notice**.

It is crucial for students to refer only to the official NIOS website for accurate and up-to-date information regarding these processes. Do not rely on unofficial sources for application deadlines or fees.
