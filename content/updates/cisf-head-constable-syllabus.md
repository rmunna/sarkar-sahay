CISF Head Constable Ministerial (HCM) written exam is an **objective MCQ test with 100 questions, 100 marks, and 90 minutes duration**. The 5 subjects are General Awareness, Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning, English, and Computer Knowledge. **No negative marking** — wrong answers score 0.

## Exam Pattern

| Subject | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| General Awareness / GK | 20 | 20 | — |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 20 | 20 | — |
| Reasoning Ability | 20 | 20 | — |
| English Language | 20 | 20 | — |
| Computer Knowledge | 20 | 20 | — |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** | **90 min** |

- **Type:** MCQ (4 options each)
- **Negative marking:** None
- **Language:** Bilingual (Hindi + English) for GK, Reasoning, Maths; English only for English paper

## Detailed Syllabus

### 1. General Awareness (20 marks)

- **Current Affairs:** Last 6 months — national, state, international events
- **History:** Ancient, Medieval, Modern Indian History; Indian Freedom Movement
- **Geography:** Physical geography of India, rivers, mountains, climate
- **Indian Polity:** Constitution, Parliament, Fundamental Rights, Election process
- **Economy:** GDP, inflation, budget concepts, government schemes
- **Science & Technology:** Basic physics, chemistry, biology; ISRO, defence tech
- **Sports:** Recent national/international winners
- **Awards:** Padma awards, Bharat Ratna, Nobel Prize, Olympic medals

### 2. Quantitative Aptitude (20 marks)

- Number System: LCM, HCF, fractions, decimals
- Percentage, Profit & Loss, Discount
- Simple Interest, Compound Interest
- Ratio & Proportion, Average
- Time & Work, Time & Distance, Pipes & Cisterns
- Mensuration: Area, perimeter, volume (basic 2D and 3D shapes)
- Data Interpretation: Tables, bar charts, pie charts

### 3. Reasoning Ability (20 marks)

- Series completion (number, letter)
- Coding-Decoding
- Blood Relations
- Direction & Distance
- Analogy and Classification
- Syllogism (logical statements)
- Non-verbal: Mirror image, embedded figures, paper folding
- Puzzles and Seating Arrangement (basic)

### 4. English Language (20 marks)

- Reading Comprehension (one passage, 4–5 questions)
- Fill in the blanks (grammar rules)
- Sentence Correction / Error Spotting
- Synonyms & Antonyms
- One-word substitution
- Idioms and phrases
- Spelling correction

### 5. Computer Knowledge (20 marks)

- **Computer Fundamentals:** Hardware, software, input/output devices
- **Operating System:** Windows basics, file management
- **MS Office:** MS Word (formatting, editing), MS Excel (basic formulas), MS PowerPoint (slides)
- **Internet Basics:** Browser, email, search engines, downloading
- **Networking:** LAN, WAN, Wi-Fi basics
- **Security:** Virus, antivirus, firewall basics
- **Shortcuts:** Common keyboard shortcuts (Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V, etc.)

## Preparation Tips

1. **GK newspaper reading:** Read a daily newspaper or follow current affairs apps for 30 minutes daily — the GK section rewards regular reading
2. **Maths practice:** Focus on percentage, ratio, and time & work — these are the most frequently tested topics
3. **Computer Knowledge is scoring:** This section is often neglected but easy to prepare; cover MS Office operations thoroughly
4. **English — grammar rules:** Subject-verb agreement, tense errors, and preposition errors are the most common question types
5. **Previous papers:** CISF HCM previous year papers (2023, 2022, 2021) are available on toppersexam.com — solve at least 3 full sets
