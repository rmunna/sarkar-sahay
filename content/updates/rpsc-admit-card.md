## Overview of RPSC Admit Card Release

The Rajasthan Public Service Commission (RPSC) is the premier commission of the Government of Rajasthan for organizing recruitment examinations and procedures for various state-level government posts. At any given time, RPSC manages multiple recruitment cycles, from administrative services to teaching roles and specialized technical positions.

The release of admit cards is a critical phase, allowing candidates to know their examination date, time, and allocated center. Given the current trend, it is highly probable that admit cards for one or more major RPSC recruitment examinations or interview stages have been activated today, May 31, 2026.

Candidates must remember that without a valid admit card and a government-issued photo ID, entry to the examination center will be denied. It is essential to download, print, and carefully review all details on your admit card as soon as possible.

## How to Download Your RPSC Admit Card 2026

Follow these step-by-step instructions to download your RPSC admit card for the 2026 recruitment examinations:

1.  **Visit the Official RPSC Website:** Open your web browser and go to the official website of the Rajasthan Public Service Commission: [https://rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in/](https://rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in/).
2.  **Navigate to the Admit Card Section:** On the homepage, look for the 'News & Events' section or a prominent link titled 'Admit Card' or 'Applicant Dashboard'. You may also find a direct link under 'Candidate Information'. A common direct portal for applicants is [https://rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in/applicant-dashboard](https://rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in/applicant-dashboard).
3.  **Find Your Specific Exam Link:** Within the Admit Card section, you will see a list of recent admit card releases. Identify the link corresponding to the specific recruitment exam you have applied for (e.g., Senior Teacher, School Lecturer, Assistant Professor, etc.).
4.  **Login with Your Credentials:** Click on the relevant admit card link. You will be redirected to a login page. Enter your application number/registration number, date of birth, and any other required details (like a captcha code) as prompted.
5.  **View and Download:** After successful login, your admit card will be displayed on the screen. Carefully check all the details.
6.  **Print Your Admit Card:** Download the admit card as a PDF file and take at least two clear printouts. It is advisable to print in color if your photograph or signature clarity is crucial.

**Note:** If you face any difficulty in downloading, ensure your internet connection is stable and you are using the correct login credentials. In case of persistent issues, refer to the RPSC helpline numbers or contact details provided on the official website.

## What to Do After Downloading Your Admit Card

Once you have downloaded and printed your admit card, follow these essential steps:

1.  **Verify All Details:** Immediately check every detail mentioned on the admit card for accuracy. Pay special attention to your name, photograph, signature, date of birth, exam date, time, and center details. If you find any discrepancies, contact RPSC authorities immediately.
2.  **Read Instructions Carefully:** Go through all the instructions provided on the admit card regarding allowed items, prohibited items, reporting time, documents to carry, and COVID-19 safety protocols.
3.  **Arrange for Photo ID:** Along with your admit card, you **must** carry a valid, original government-issued photo identification proof (e.g., Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Passport, Driving License, PAN Card) to the examination center.
4.  **Locate Your Exam Center:** If unfamiliar with the exam center, try to visit it a day or two before the exam to understand the route and travel time. This will help you avoid last-minute stress and delays.
5.  **Keep Multiple Copies:** Keep at least two printouts of your admit card, one for the exam day and another as a backup.

## FAQs (Frequently Asked Questions)

**Q1. When will the RPSC admit card for my specific exam be released?**
A1. Admit cards for various RPSC recruitment exams are released periodically. As of May 31, 2026, many admit cards are active. You must regularly check the 'News & Events' or 'Admit Card' section on the official RPSC website ([rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in](https://rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in/)) for updates related to your particular examination.

**Q2. What should I do if there is an error in my admit card details?**
A2. If you find any discrepancy or error in your admit card, such as an incorrect name, photo, or exam center, contact the RPSC helpline or support desk immediately. The contact details are usually available on the RPSC website. Do not wait until the last moment.

**Q3. Can I change my examination center after the admit card is released?**
A3. Generally, RPSC does not allow changes to the examination center once the admit cards are released. Centers are allotted based on various logistical factors. In exceptional circumstances, RPSC might issue specific guidelines, so always refer to the official notifications.

**Q4. What documents do I need to carry to the examination hall along with the admit card?**
A4. You must carry a printed copy of your RPSC admit card and an original, valid government-issued photo identification proof (e.g., Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Passport, Driving License, PAN Card). Sometimes, a photocopy of the ID might also be required, so it's best to carry one as a backup.

**Q5. I forgot my application number. How can I download my admit card?**
A5. Most RPSC login portals provide an option like "Forgot Application Number" or "Retrieve Registration ID". You can usually retrieve it by entering your name, father's name, date of birth, and registered mobile number/email ID. If not, contact the RPSC helpdesk.

**Q6. Is it mandatory to print the admit card in color?**
A6. While it's not strictly mandatory unless specified by RPSC, it's highly recommended to print your admit card in color, especially if your photograph or signature appears faint or unclear in a black and white print. Clarity of details is crucial for verification at the exam center.
