## Selection Process

The Central Selection Board of Constable (CSBC), Bihar, has outlined a multi-stage selection process for the CSBC Constable 01/2025 recruitment. Candidates must successfully clear each stage to be considered for final selection. The process is designed to assess candidates' written knowledge, physical fitness, and other essential attributes required for the Constable post.

The confirmed stages of the selection process are as follows:

1.  **Written Examination:** This will be the first stage, where candidates' knowledge will be assessed through a written test. The syllabus, exam pattern, and marking scheme will be detailed in the full notification.
2.  **Physical Efficiency Test (PET):** Candidates who qualify the written examination will be called for a Physical Efficiency Test. This stage typically involves various physical tasks such as running, high jump, long jump, and shot put, designed to evaluate the physical fitness and endurance of the candidates.
3.  **Other Prescribed Procedures:** This may include document verification, medical examination, or other specific assessments as deemed necessary by CSBC Bihar, which will be detailed in the comprehensive notification.
4.  **Tie-breaking based on Date of Birth:** In cases where two or more candidates secure equal marks and have cleared all preceding stages, the candidate who is older in age (based on their Date of Birth) will be given preference.
5.  **Tie-breaking based on Educational Qualification:** If the tie persists even after considering the Date of Birth, candidates with a higher educational qualification will be preferred.
6.  **Tie-breaking based on Alphabetical Order of Name:** Should a tie still remain after applying the above two criteria, the candidate whose name appears earlier in alphabetical order (as per their 10th board certificate) will be given preference.

Candidates are strongly encouraged to prepare thoroughly for each stage and stay updated with the official announcements on [csbc.bihar.gov.in](https://csbc.bihar.gov.in) for detailed information on each stage of the selection process.
