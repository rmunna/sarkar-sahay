## ⚠️ Registration Closes Tonight — Apply Before 11:59 PM

The last date to apply for **RRB Group D 2026 (CEN 09/2025)** is **March 2, 2026 at 11:59 PM**. If you haven't submitted your application yet, do it now — the portal at [rrbapply.gov.in](https://www.rrbapply.gov.in) will stop accepting new applications after midnight.

This recruitment is for **22,195 Level-1 posts** across Indian Railways — one of the biggest railway recruitment drives this year.

> **Fee payment deadline is March 4, 2026** — but you MUST submit the application form by tonight.

## Step-by-Step: How to Apply Before the Deadline

Follow these steps carefully. Keep your documents ready before you start.

1. **Go to [www.rrbapply.gov.in](https://www.rrbapply.gov.in)** and click on CEN 09/2025
2. **New users:** Click "Register" — enter your name, email, mobile number, date of birth, and Aadhaar number
3. **Already registered?** Log in with your registration ID and password
4. **Fill Personal Details** — father's name, address, category, PwBD status, ex-serviceman status
5. **Fill Educational Details** — 10th pass / ITI / NAC certificate details with board name and percentage
6. **Select Preferences** — choose your preferred Railway Zone and post preferences (you can pick multiple)
7. **Upload Documents:**
   - Passport-size photograph (20–50 KB, JPG)
   - Signature (10–40 KB, JPG)
   - 10th certificate / ITI certificate as applicable
8. **Pay Application Fee** via UPI, Debit/Credit Card, or Net Banking
9. **Review everything** — check name spelling, DOB, category, and qualification carefully
10. **Click Submit** — take a screenshot and download the confirmation PDF

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

🚫 **Wrong photo size** — The portal rejects photos outside 20–50 KB. Resize before uploading.

🚫 **Mismatched name** — Your name must match your 10th certificate EXACTLY. No nicknames.

🚫 **Wrong category** — Selecting General instead of OBC/SC/ST means you lose age relaxation and fee refund. Double-check.

🚫 **Incomplete preferences** — Select multiple Railway Zones to increase your chances. Don't leave it at just one.

🚫 **Not saving Registration ID** — Write down your Registration ID and password immediately after registration. You'll need it for admit card download later.

🚫 **Waiting until the last minute** — Server traffic is highest in the final hours. If the site is slow, keep trying. Don't give up.

## FAQs

### Can I apply after March 2, 2026?
No. The application portal closes at 11:59 PM on March 2, 2026. No late applications will be accepted. However, fee payment can be completed until March 4.

### What if I submitted the form but haven't paid the fee?
You have until **March 4, 2026** to complete fee payment. Your application is not valid without fee payment.

### Can I edit my application after submitting?
Yes, an application modification window is available from **March 5–14, 2026**. You can correct personal details during this period.

### What qualification do I need?
Minimum 10th Pass (Matriculation) from a recognized board. ITI or National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC) is also accepted.

### When will the exam be held?
The CBT exam date has not been announced yet. Check [rrbcdg.gov.in](https://www.rrbcdg.gov.in) for updates.
