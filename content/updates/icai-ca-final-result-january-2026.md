## How to Check ICAI CA Final Result January 2026

Follow these steps to check your result online:

1. Visit the official result portal at [icai.nic.in/caresult](https://icai.nic.in/caresult/)
2. Click on the **"CA Final Result – January 2026"** link
3. Enter your **Roll Number** and **Registration Number**
4. Fill in the **Captcha** code displayed on screen
5. Click **Submit** to view your result
6. Your scorecard with subject-wise marks will appear on screen
7. **Download and save** the scorecard as PDF for future reference

You can also check results at [icai.org](https://icai.org) and [icaiexam.icai.org](https://icaiexam.icai.org).

## How to Download Marksheet

The official marksheet/scorecard can be downloaded from the ICAI portal:

1. Log in to the **ICAI Self-Service Portal (SSP)** using your Student Registration Number
2. Navigate to **Examination → Results/Marksheet**
3. Select **CA Final — January 2026 Session**
4. Download the detailed marksheet with subject-wise break-up
5. Take a printout for your records — the physical mark sheet will be dispatched by ICAI later

## Merit List

ICAI releases the **All India Merit List** along with the result for candidates who score exceptionally. The merit list includes:

- **Top 50 rank holders** for individual groups (Group I and Group II)
- Merit list can be checked at [icai.nic.in](https://icai.nic.in/caresult/) by entering your six-digit Roll Number

## Verification of Marks

Not satisfied with your score? ICAI offers **verification of marks** (not re-evaluation):

- Apply within **30 days** from the date of result declaration
- Login to the **ICAI e-Services Portal** or SSP Dashboard
- Select **"Apply for Verification of Marks"** under the Final exam section
- Upload a **scanned handwritten application** in the exam medium (English/Hindi)
- Verification covers totaling errors, unvalued questions, and clerical mistakes

## What to Do After Passing CA Final

Congratulations if you've cleared! Here are your next steps:

1. **Complete Articleship** — If not already completed, finish your 3-year practical training (articleship)
2. **Apply for ICAI Membership** — Submit Form 2 along with required documents and fee to your regional ICAI office
3. **Obtain Certificate of Practice (CoP)** — If you wish to practice independently, apply for a CoP along with membership
4. **GMCS (General Management and Communication Skills)** — Complete the mandatory GMCS course if pending
5. **Enroll with Firms or Start Practice** — You can join a CA firm, start independent practice, or pursue careers in finance, audit, taxation, and consulting
