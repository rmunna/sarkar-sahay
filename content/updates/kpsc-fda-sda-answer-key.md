The KPSC FDA SDA 2026 answer key is released by the Karnataka Examination Authority (KEA) at **cetonline.karnataka.gov.in** after the written examination. The answer key shows the correct responses to all MCQ questions across each paper, allowing candidates to estimate their score, challenge incorrect answers, and gauge cut-off eligibility before the official result is declared.

## Where to Find the Official Answer Key

The answer key is published exclusively through official KEA/KPSC channels:

- **KEA portal:** [cetonline.karnataka.gov.in](https://cetonline.karnataka.gov.in) — primary source; look for "FDA SDA Answer Key 2026" under the relevant notification
- **KPSC website:** [kpsc.kar.nic.in](https://kpsc.kar.nic.in) — official notices and corrigenda are posted here
- **Do not rely on coaching site answer keys** — third-party sites may have errors or use unofficial sources. Always verify against the official provisional key.

## How to Download the KPSC FDA SDA Answer Key

1. Go to [cetonline.karnataka.gov.in](https://cetonline.karnataka.gov.in)
2. Find the **FDA SDA 2026** recruitment notification on the homepage
3. Click on the **"Answer Key"** link next to the relevant paper
4. The answer key opens as a PDF — download and save it
5. Cross-check each question using your question booklet or memory notes

The answer key is usually released **7–14 days after the exam date**. If you don't see it yet, check back in a few days or watch the official KPSC social media handles.

## How to Raise an Objection

If you believe a published answer is incorrect, KPSC allows candidates to challenge it during the **objection window** (typically 3–7 days from the provisional key release):

1. Visit [cetonline.karnataka.gov.in](https://cetonline.karnataka.gov.in) and log in with your application number
2. Navigate to the **"Challenge Answer Key"** or **"Objection"** section under your FDA/SDA application
3. Select the question number, state the error, and provide supporting evidence (textbook reference, official source)
4. Pay the objection fee if applicable (usually ₹50–₹100 per question; refunded if objection is accepted)
5. Submit before the deadline — late objections are not considered

KPSC reviews all valid objections and releases a **final answer key** before the result declaration. The final key is used for scoring.

## How to Estimate Your Score

FDA and SDA are OMR-based with no negative marking, so score estimation is straightforward:

| Post | Formula |
|------|---------|
| **FDA** | Correct answers × 1 mark (across Papers 1, 2, 3; max 400) |
| **SDA** | Correct answers × 1 mark (across Papers 1, 2; max 250) |

Note: Mark weightage per question may vary by paper — verify in the official question paper or key. For the 2026 cycle, the result has been declared at [cetonline.karnataka.gov.in](https://cetonline.karnataka.gov.in). If you missed this cycle, use this process as a reference for the next KPSC FDA SDA recruitment.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: When is the KPSC FDA SDA 2026 answer key released?**
The answer key is typically released 7–14 days after the exam at cetonline.karnataka.gov.in. Check the official KEA portal regularly. For the 2026 cycle (exam conducted post-Nov 2025), verify the current status on the official site.

**Q: Can I challenge the KPSC FDA SDA answer key?**
Yes. KPSC allows objections during a specified window (usually 3–7 days). Log in to cetonline.karnataka.gov.in, go to the objection section under your application, and submit your challenge with supporting evidence before the deadline.

**Q: How many marks per question in KPSC FDA SDA?**
Each correct answer carries 1 mark. There is no negative marking for wrong answers. FDA has 400 total marks across 3 papers; SDA has 250 total marks across 2 papers.

**Q: Which website has the official KPSC FDA SDA answer key?**
The official answer key is only at cetonline.karnataka.gov.in (KEA portal) and kpsc.kar.nic.in. Third-party coaching sites may publish unofficial keys — always cross-check with the official provisional key.
