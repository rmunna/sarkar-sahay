## CBSE Class 10 Board Result 2026 Declared! Check Your Scores Here

**New Delhi:** The Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) has officially declared the Class 10 Board Examination results for 2026 today, June 16, 2026. This announcement brings an end to the anticipation for lakhs of students who appeared for the board exams across the country. Students can now access their provisional mark sheets and check their subject-wise scores, grades, and overall qualifying status through the official CBSE result portals and DigiLocker.

This is a critical moment for students as these results will pave the way for their admission into Class 11, determining their choice of streams and future academic pursuits. It is advised to check the results promptly and follow the necessary steps for post-result procedures.

## How to Check CBSE Class 10 Result 2026

Students can check their CBSE Class 10 Board Result 2026 through multiple official platforms. Ensure you have your Roll Number, School Number, and Admit Card ID ready before attempting to check your results.

### Method 1: Via Official CBSE Results Website (cbseresults.nic.in)

This is the primary and most commonly used method to check your Class 10 results. Follow these simple steps:

1.  **Visit the Official Website**: Open your web browser and go to the official CBSE result portal: `https://cbseresults.nic.in/`.
2.  **Locate Result Link**: On the homepage, look for the link that says "CBSE Class 10 Board Examination Result 2026" or similar. Click on it.
3.  **Enter Credentials**: You will be redirected to a login page. Here, you will need to enter the following details as per your admit card:
    *   Your Roll Number
    *   Your School Number
    *   Your Admit Card ID
4.  **Submit Details**: After entering all the required information accurately, click on the "Submit" or "Get Result" button.
5.  **View and Download Result**: Your CBSE Class 10 result 2026 will be displayed on the screen. Carefully review all the details. It is highly recommended to download and print a copy of your provisional mark sheet for future reference and immediate use for Class 11 admissions.

### Method 2: Via DigiLocker

DigiLocker is a secure cloud-based platform for the issuance and verification of documents and certificates. CBSE partners with DigiLocker to provide digital academic documents, including mark sheets and passing certificates. This is an official and convenient way to access your results and certificates.

1.  **Visit DigiLocker Website/App**: Go to `https://www.digilocker.gov.in/` or download the DigiLocker mobile application from your app store.
2.  **Login/Sign Up**: If you already have a DigiLocker account, log in using your Aadhar number or registered mobile number. If not, sign up by providing your Aadhar number and other required details.
3.  **Link CBSE Account (if needed)**: Once logged in, you might need to link your CBSE account. Look for the "Education" section and select "Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE)".
4.  **Access Documents**: Navigate to the "Issued Documents" section or look for "CBSE Class X Mark Sheet 2026" and "CBSE Class X Passing Certificate 2026".
5.  **Download Documents**: You can view and download your digital mark sheet and certificate, which are legally valid.

Ensure that all personal details on your mark sheet are accurate. In case of any discrepancies, immediately contact your school authorities.

## What to Do After Checking Your CBSE Class 10 Result 2026

Once you have viewed and downloaded your result, several important steps follow:

1.  **Print Provisional Mark Sheet**: Keep a printed copy of your online mark sheet. This is a provisional document and will be required for immediate admissions and record-keeping.
2.  **Class 11 Admissions**: Begin the admission process for Class 11 in your desired stream (Science, Commerce, Arts/Humanities) at your preferred school. Schools typically start their admission procedures shortly after board results are declared.
3.  **Collect Original Mark Sheet and Certificates**: The original mark sheets and passing certificates will be issued by CBSE and distributed through your respective schools a few weeks after the online declaration. You will need to visit your school to collect these official documents.
4.  **Re-evaluation/Verification of Marks**: If you are not satisfied with your marks in one or more subjects, CBSE provides an opportunity for re-evaluation or verification of marks. The application process, fees, and deadlines for this will be announced by CBSE on its official website (`cbse.gov.in`) soon. Students should keep an eye on official notifications for these dates.
5.  **Compartment Exams**: Students who have failed in one or two subjects may be eligible to appear for compartment examinations to improve their results without having to repeat the entire academic year. Details regarding the compartment exam application, schedule, and fees will also be released by CBSE in due course.
