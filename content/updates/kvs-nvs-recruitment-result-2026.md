# KVS NVS Recruitment Result 2026 – TGT, PGT, Principal & Other Posts

Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan (KVS) and Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (NVS) have released the recruitment results for 2026. Candidates who appeared for the written examination for TGT, PGT, Principal, and other teaching posts can now check their results and download score cards from the official websites.

## Result Overview

| Detail | KVS | NVS |
|---|---|---|
| **Organization** | Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan | Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti |
| **Posts** | TGT, PGT, Principal, Vice Principal | TGT, PGT, Principal, Staff Nurse, Catering Assistant |
| **Result Status** | Declared | Declared |
| **Official Website** | kvsangathan.nic.in | navodaya.gov.in |
| **Next Stage** | Document Verification & Interview | Document Verification & Interview |

## Posts Covered

### KVS Posts
- **PGT (Post Graduate Teacher)** – English, Hindi, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, History, Geography, Economics, Commerce, Computer Science
- **TGT (Trained Graduate Teacher)** – English, Hindi, Mathematics, Science, Social Studies, Sanskrit, Physical Education, Art Education, Work Experience
- **Principal & Vice Principal**

### NVS Posts
- **PGT** – All subjects
- **TGT** – All subjects including Music and Art
- **Principal**
- **Staff Nurse, Catering Assistant** and other non-teaching posts

## How to Check KVS Result 2026

1. Visit the official KVS website at [kvsangathan.nic.in](https://kvsangathan.nic.in)
2. Click on the **"Recruitment"** section on the homepage
3. Find and click **"KVS Recruitment Result 2026"**
4. Enter your **Registration Number** and **Date of Birth**
5. Click **Submit** to view your result
6. Your result status (Qualified / Not Qualified) will appear on screen
7. Download or print the result for your records

## How to Check NVS Result 2026

1. Go to the official NVS website at [navodaya.gov.in](https://navodaya.gov.in)
2. Navigate to the **"Recruitment"** tab
3. Click on **"NVS Recruitment Result 2026"**
4. Enter your **Application Number** and **Password/Date of Birth**
5. Click **View Result**
6. Check your result status and category-wise merit position
7. Save or print the score card

## How to Download Score Card

- After viewing your result, look for the **"Download Score Card"** button
- The score card contains your subject-wise marks, total score, and qualifying status
- Save the PDF and take a printout — you will need it during document verification
- If the download link is not active yet, check back after 2-3 days

## Cut-Off Marks

Cut-off marks vary by post, category, and subject. Generally:

- **General category** cut-offs are highest
- **OBC, SC, ST, EWS** categories have relaxed cut-offs as per government norms
- Official cut-off lists are published alongside results on the respective websites

Check the official result notification PDF for detailed category-wise and subject-wise cut-off marks.

## Next Steps After Result

### 1. Document Verification
Shortlisted candidates will be called for document verification. Keep these documents ready:
- Original mark sheets and certificates (10th, 12th, Graduation, Post Graduation)
- B.Ed / CTET / relevant teaching qualification certificate
- Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS) if applicable
- Identity proof (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport)
- Passport-size photographs
- Experience certificate (if any)

### 2. Interview
- For PGT, TGT, and Principal posts, a personal interview or demo class may be conducted
- Interview dates and venue details will be communicated via email and the official website
- Carry all original documents to the interview

### 3. Final Merit List
- The final selection is based on combined scores from the written exam and interview
- Final merit lists will be published on the official websites

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: When was the KVS/NVS result 2026 declared?**
A: Results have been declared in March 2026. Check the official websites for exact date and time.

**Q: I forgot my registration number. How do I check my result?**
A: Use the "Forgot Registration Number" option on the login page. You can recover it using your registered email or mobile number.

**Q: Is there a negative marking in KVS/NVS exams?**
A: Yes, generally 0.25 marks are deducted for each wrong answer in objective-type questions.

**Q: When will document verification start?**
A: Document verification schedule will be announced separately after the result. Keep checking the official website and your registered email.

**Q: Can I request re-evaluation of my score?**
A: KVS and NVS typically do not allow re-evaluation. However, you may raise grievances through the official portal if you believe there is a discrepancy.
