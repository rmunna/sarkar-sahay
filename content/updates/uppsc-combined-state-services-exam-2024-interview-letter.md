## What is the UPPSC Combined State/Upper Subordinate Services Exam?

The UPPSC Combined State/Upper Subordinate Services Examination is one of the most prestigious competitive exams conducted by the Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission. It is the gateway to various Group A and Group B posts in the Uttar Pradesh state government, including positions such as Sub Divisional Magistrate (SDM), Deputy Superintendent of Police (DSP), Block Development Officer (BDO), District Commandant (Home Guards), and many more.

The selection process typically consists of three stages:

1. **Preliminary Examination** — Objective-type screening test
2. **Main Examination** — Descriptive written examination
3. **Interview/Personality Test** — The final stage of selection

Candidates who have cleared both the Preliminary and Main examinations are now being called for the Interview/Personality Test round, and the interview letters for this stage have been released.

## How to Download UPPSC Interview Letter 2024 — Step-by-Step Guide

Follow these steps to download your interview call letter:

### Step 1: Visit the Official Website
Open your web browser and go to the official UPPSC website at **[https://uppsc.up.nic.in/](https://uppsc.up.nic.in/)**.

### Step 2: Locate the Interview Letter Link
On the homepage, look for the notification or link related to **"Combined State/Upper Subordinate Services Exam 2024 — Interview Letter"**. This is usually displayed under the "Latest Notifications" or "Important Links" section.

### Step 3: Click on the Download Link
Click on the relevant link to open the interview letter download page.

### Step 4: Enter Your Credentials
You will be asked to enter your details such as:
- Registration Number / Roll Number
- Date of Birth
- Any other required credentials

### Step 5: Submit and Download
After entering the correct details, click on the **Submit** button. Your interview letter will be displayed on the screen. Click on **Download** or **Print** to save a copy.

### Step 6: Take a Printout
Print at least **two copies** of the interview letter. Keep one copy safe at home and carry the other to the interview venue.

## Important Information for Candidates

- **Carry the interview letter** along with a valid photo ID proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, etc.) to the interview venue.
- **Check all details** on the interview letter carefully — your name, roll number, photograph, interview date, time, and venue. If there are any discrepancies, contact UPPSC immediately.
- **Reach the venue on time** — arrive at least 30 minutes before the scheduled interview time.
- **Carry original documents** including educational certificates, caste certificate (if applicable), experience certificates, and any other documents mentioned in the interview letter.
- **Dress formally** for the personality test/interview round.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Where can I download the UPPSC Interview Letter for Combined State Services Exam 2024?
You can download it from the official UPPSC website at [uppsc.up.nic.in](https://uppsc.up.nic.in/).

### Q2: What is the last date to download the interview letter?
The interview letter will be available on the portal until **20th March 2026**. Download it before this date.

### Q3: I am unable to download my interview letter. What should I do?
Ensure you are entering the correct registration number and date of birth. If the problem persists, contact the UPPSC helpdesk or visit the UPPSC office in Prayagraj.

### Q4: Can I appear for the interview without the printed interview letter?
No. The printed interview letter is mandatory for entry to the interview venue. Make sure you carry a printed copy.

### Q5: What if there is an error in my interview letter?
If you notice any discrepancy in your name, photograph, or other details, contact UPPSC immediately through their official contact channels before the interview date.

### Q6: What is the interview venue?
The interview venue details are mentioned on your individual interview letter. Download it to check your specific date, time, and location.
