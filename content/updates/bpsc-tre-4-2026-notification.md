## What is BPSC TRE 4.0?

BPSC TRE 4.0 (Teacher Recruitment Examination 4.0) is the fourth edition of Bihar's teacher recruitment drive conducted by the Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC). This recruitment aims to fill 46,595 teacher vacancies across Primary, Middle, Secondary, and Senior Secondary levels in government schools throughout Bihar.

## Vacancy Details

Total vacancies: approximately 46,595 posts across four levels:

- **Primary Teacher** — For classes 1 to 5
- **Middle School Teacher** — For classes 6 to 8
- **Secondary Teacher** — For classes 9 to 10
- **Senior Secondary Teacher** — For classes 11 to 12

Category-wise and subject-wise breakdowns will be available in the detailed official notification.

## Eligibility Criteria

- **Age Limit:** As per BPSC norms (typically 21–37 years for General category with relaxations for reserved categories)
- **Educational Qualification:** Graduation/Post-graduation in relevant subject with B.Ed./D.El.Ed. as applicable for the level
- **TET Qualification:** Valid CTET or Bihar STET score as per the post level
- **Nationality:** Indian citizen, domicile of Bihar preferred

Exact eligibility criteria for each level will be specified in the detailed notification.

## How to Apply Online

1. Visit the official BPSC website at onlinebpsc.bihar.gov.in
2. Complete One-Time Registration (OTR) — this is mandatory before applying
3. During OTR, enter personal details, verify mobile number and email via OTP
4. Create username and password for future logins
5. Register on DigiLocker and upload 10th, 12th, Graduation, and B.Ed./D.El.Ed. certificates
6. Once the application form is live, log in with OTR credentials and fill the form
7. Upload required documents and pay the application fee
8. Submit the form and take a printout for records

## Selection Process

2. Document Verification

## Exam Pattern

- **Mode:** Offline (OMR-based)
- **Type:** Objective Multiple Choice
- **Duration:** Typically 2–2.5 hours
- **Subjects:** Vary by level — Language, Mathematics, Environmental Studies (Primary), Subject-specific papers (Secondary/Senior Secondary)

Detailed exam pattern will be confirmed in the official notification.

- **Middle Level:** Child Development & Pedagogy, Language, Mathematics/Science/Social Science
- **Secondary Level:** Subject-specific papers with pedagogy
- **Senior Secondary Level:** Subject-specific papers with pedagogy

## FAQs

### Q: What is OTR registration for BPSC TRE 4.0?
OTR (One-Time Registration) is a mandatory pre-registration step. You must complete OTR to get login credentials needed to fill the actual application form when it becomes available.

### Q: How many vacancies are available in BPSC TRE 4.0?
Approximately 46,595 teacher vacancies across Primary, Middle, Secondary, and Senior Secondary levels in Bihar government schools.

### Q: Is CTET/Bihar STET mandatory for BPSC TRE 4.0?
