KVS (Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan) pays its teachers as per the **7th Pay Commission** pay matrix. PGT (Post Graduate Teacher) starts at ₹47,600 basic (Pay Level 8), TGT (Trained Graduate Teacher) at ₹44,900 (Level 7), and PRT (Primary Teacher) at ₹35,400 (Level 6). With DA, HRA, and other allowances, in-hand salary ranges from **₹55,000–₹85,000/month** depending on post and posting city.

## KVS In-Hand Salary Calculation (2026)

KVS is a Central Government organisation — salary follows the same structure as other Central Govt employees. Key components:

- **DA (Dearness Allowance):** Revised twice a year (Jan & Jul). As of 2026, DA is approximately 53% of basic pay.
- **HRA (House Rent Allowance):** 24% of basic (X-class cities: Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, Bangalore, Hyderabad), 16% (Y-class), 8% (Z-class). Most KVS schools are in cantonment areas or residential colonies — HRA tier depends on city classification.
- **TA (Transport Allowance):** ₹3,600/month for Level 6–8 in X-class cities; lower for other tiers.
- **NPS Deduction:** 10% of basic + DA (employee contribution to National Pension System).

## Additional Benefits & Perquisites

Beyond the monthly salary, KVS teachers receive:

- **Free/subsidised education** for up to 2 children in Kendriya Vidyalayas
- **Medical benefits** under Central Government Health Scheme (CGHS) for self and family
- **Leave Travel Concession (LTC):** Once in 2 years to home town; once in 4 years anywhere in India
- **Children's Education Allowance:** ₹2,250/month per child (max 2 children) for schooling
- **Pension:** National Pension System (NPS) for all new joiners
- **Accommodation:** Government quarters available at select locations (subject to availability)
- **Annual increment:** 3% of basic pay every July

## KVS Salary vs State Government Teacher Salary

KVS teachers are often paid higher than many state government teachers, especially at entry level, because KVS follows Central Government pay scales. State teacher pay varies widely — Karnataka, Kerala, and Maharashtra pay comparably; Bihar and UP pay scales are significantly lower than KVS.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is the KVS TGT in-hand salary per month?**
A KVS TGT posted in a major city (Delhi, Mumbai) takes home approximately ₹74,000–₹78,000/month after deductions, using current DA rates. The figure varies by city class (HRA differs) and any loans/advances outstanding.

**Q: Does KVS PGT get more than TGT?**
Yes. PGT starts at ₹47,600 basic (Level 8) vs TGT's ₹44,900 (Level 7) — a difference of ₹2,700 basic. With DA, the gap widens over service. PGT also has faster promotion prospects to Principal.

**Q: What is the KVS PRT salary in 7th Pay Commission?**
PRT is placed at Pay Level 6 with a starting basic of ₹35,400. In-hand in a major city works out to approximately ₹58,000–₹62,000/month depending on allowances and deductions.

**Q: Is KVS salary tax-free?**
No. KVS salary is taxable as per the Income Tax Act. However, HRA exemption, Section 80C deductions (NPS, LIC), and medical reimbursements help reduce tax liability. Use Form 16 issued by KVS for filing ITR.
