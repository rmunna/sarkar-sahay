SSC CGL recruits for Group B and Group C posts across central government departments. The salary depends on the post and Pay Level — **Inspector/ASO posts** (the most sought-after) carry Pay Level 7 (basic ₹44,900), while **Tax Assistant** posts start at Level 4 (₹25,500). In-hand salary in a metro city ranges from **₹40,000–₹80,000/month** depending on the post and posting location.

## In-Hand Salary Calculation (7th Pay Commission, 2026)

Gross salary = Basic + DA + HRA + TA + other allowances. Deductions: NPS (10% of basic+DA), CGHS, professional tax, any loans.

### Inspector / ASO — Level 7 (Delhi posting)

| Component | Amount |
|-----------|--------|
| Basic Pay | ₹44,900 |
| DA @ 53% | ₹23,797 |
| HRA @ 24% (X-class city) | ₹10,776 |
| Transport Allowance | ₹3,600 |
| **Gross Salary** | **~₹83,073** |
| Less: NPS (10% of basic+DA) | –₹6,870 |
| Less: CGHS | –₹350 |
| **Approximate In-Hand** | **~₹75,853** |

### Tax Assistant — Level 4 (Delhi posting)

| Component | Amount |
|-----------|--------|

*DA changes every January and July. The 53% rate reflects 2026 estimates. Posting city matters: HRA drops to 16% in Y-class cities, 8% in Z-class. Check the exact DA rate at [ssc.gov.in](https://ssc.gov.in) or your department's pay office.*

## Which SSC CGL Post Has the Best Salary?

**Best in-hand pay:** Inspector posts (Income Tax, Central Excise, GST) and ASO (CSS, MEA) — all at Level 7 with ~₹75,000–₹80,000 in-hand in Delhi.

**Best overall package (salary + perks):** ASO (CSS) or Inspector (Income Tax) because of the central posting (usually Delhi), CGHS coverage, and stable increment path. Income Tax Inspector also has field posting in most cities across India.

**Best for transfers and mobility:** Sub-Inspector CBI is highly regarded for job profile, though Level 6 means slightly lower pay than inspectors.

## Additional Benefits

- **CGHS (Central Government Health Scheme):** Medical coverage for employee and family at government hospitals and empanelled private hospitals
- **LTC (Leave Travel Concession):** Twice in 4 years — once to home town, once anywhere in India (air travel for X-class cities)
- **NPS:** Pension under National Pension System (10% employee + 14% government contribution)
- **Risk Allowance:** For posts like Inspector (Central Excise), hazardous duty allowance may apply
- **Annual increment:** 3% of basic pay every July

## SSC CGL Salary Growth Over Time

Starting from Level 7 (₹44,900), a CGL recruit can expect:
- **After 5 years:** Basic ~₹52,000 (with increments + one MACP)
- **After 10 years:** Basic ~₹60,000 (MACP promotion to Level 8 typically at 10 years)
- **Promotion path:** Inspector → Superintendent → Asst. Commissioner (IRS cadre) for departmental promotion via LDCE

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is the SSC CGL Inspector salary in hand per month?**
An SSC CGL Inspector (Income Tax / Central Excise) posted in Delhi takes home approximately ₹74,000–₹78,000/month after NPS and CGHS deductions, at current DA rates. Posting in a smaller city reduces HRA and slightly changes TA, bringing in-hand to ~₹65,000–₹70,000.

**Q: Which is better — SSC CGL Tax Assistant or Inspector?**
Inspector is significantly better: higher pay (Level 7 vs Level 4), better job profile, faster promotion, and more allowances (risk allowance, field allowance for some posts). Tax Assistant at Level 4 is more of an entry-level clerical role. Most candidates prefer Inspector posts; the merit list fills Inspector vacancies first.

**Q: Does SSC CGL salary change with posting city?**
Yes — HRA varies: 24% of basic in X-class cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore etc.), 16% in Y-class, 8% in Z-class. For an Inspector at Level 7, this difference is ₹10,776 (X) vs ₹7,184 (Y) vs ₹3,592 (Z) per month — a meaningful gap.
