The Atal Bihari Vajpayee Medical University (ABVMU), Uttar Pradesh, has officially announced the results for the Common Nursing Entrance Test (CNET) 2026 today, June 13, 2026. Candidates who appeared for the B.Sc. Nursing entrance examination can now access their scorecards and check their performance. This article provides all essential details regarding the CNET 2026 results, how to download your scorecard, and the crucial next steps for admission into various nursing colleges affiliated with ABVMU.

## CNET 2026 Result Overview

The ABVMU CNET 2026 examination was conducted on June 9, 2026, across various centers in Uttar Pradesh. This entrance test is a gateway for aspirants seeking admission to the B.Sc. Nursing program (4-year course) in all Government and Private Nursing Colleges affiliated with ABVMU. The result declaration marks a critical phase for thousands of candidates vying for limited seats. The scorecard will display individual marks, rank, and qualifying status.

## How to Check and Download Your CNET 2026 Result Scorecard

Follow these simple steps to check and download your ABVMU CNET 2026 result:

1.  **Visit the Official Website:** Go to the official website of Atal Bihari Vajpayee Medical University, Uttar Pradesh: `https://abvmuup.edu.in`.
2.  **Navigate to CNET 2026 Section:** Look for the 'CNET 2026' or 'B.Sc. Nursing Entrance Exam 2026' section on the homepage. You might find a direct link for 'CNET 2026 Result' or 'Scorecard Download'. Alternatively, you can directly try accessing `https://abvmuup.edu.in/cnet2026/result` if available.
3.  **Click on Result Link:** Click on the link that states 'CNET 2026 Result' or 'Download Scorecard'.
4.  **Enter Credentials:** You will be redirected to a login page. Enter your Roll Number and Date of Birth (DD/MM/YYYY) as mentioned on your admit card.
5.  **Submit and View:** After entering the required details, click on the 'Submit' or 'Login' button. Your CNET 2026 scorecard will be displayed on the screen.
6.  **Download and Print:** Carefully review all the details on your scorecard. Download the PDF version and take a printout for future reference. It is advisable to keep multiple copies.

## CNET 2026 Counselling Process

The CNET 2026 counselling will be conducted online. Key stages include:

*   **Online Registration:** Candidates will need to register for counselling on the official portal by paying a counselling fee.
*   **Choice Filling:** Based on their rank, candidates can fill in their preferred colleges and courses.
*   **Seat Allotment:** Seats will be allotted based on merit, choices filled, and availability of seats.
*   **Document Verification:** Allotted candidates must report to the designated center for physical document verification.
*   **Admission Confirmation:** After successful verification, candidates need to pay the admission fee to confirm their seat.
