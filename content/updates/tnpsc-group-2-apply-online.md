TNPSC Group 2 2026 applications are submitted online at **tnpscexams.net** — the Tamil Nadu Public Service Commission's official exam portal. The notification is published at [tnpsc.gov.in](https://www.tnpsc.gov.in). Group 2 recruits for posts like Sub-Registrar, Revenue Officer, Municipal Commissioner (Grade III), and other non-interview posts (Group 2A) and interview posts (Group 2). Applications typically open for **30–45 days** after the notification.

## How to Apply for TNPSC Group 2 2026

1. **Register on tnpscexams.net** (if new): Go to [tnpscexams.net](https://tnpscexams.net) → "New User Registration" → enter name, email, mobile
2. **Login** with your registered credentials
3. **Click "Apply Online"** → find "TNPSC Group 2 / 2A 2026" from the list
4. **Fill the application form:**
   - Personal details (name, DOB, category, address)
   - Educational qualifications
   - Medium of exam (Tamil/English)
   - Exam centre preference (district)
5. **Upload documents:** Passport-size photo (JPG, 10–50KB) and signature (JPG, 5–20KB)
6. **Pay application fee** via online banking, UPI, or challan
7. **Submit and print** acknowledgment — keep your application reference number

## TNPSC Group 2 vs Group 2A — Key Difference

| Aspect | Group 2 (Interview posts) | Group 2A (Non-Interview posts) |
|--------|--------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Selection | Written exam + Interview | Written exam only |
| Posts | Sub-Registrar, Mun. Commissioner III, etc. | Junior Co-operative Auditor, etc. |
| Higher competition? | Yes (interview adds subjectivity) | No interview = merit-only |

Both are notified together and share the same written exam. At application stage you choose which posts to apply for.

## Documents at Application Stage

Only scanned photo and signature required at the time of online application. Original certificates verified only after written test at document verification / interview stage.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can candidates outside Tamil Nadu apply for TNPSC Group 2?**
Yes. Any Indian citizen meeting age and qualification criteria can apply. However, TN domicile candidates get fee waivers and Tamil language paper appears in Tamil medium. All candidates must have knowledge of Tamil (a key eligibility requirement for most TNPSC posts).

**Q: Is Tamil compulsory for TNPSC Group 2 2026?**
Yes. Tamil language (Paper I — General Tamil) is mandatory for most TNPSC Group 2 posts. Candidates must have studied Tamil at SSLC (10th) level or have Tamil proficiency. Some posts have exemptions — check the notification.

**Q: What is the minimum qualification for TNPSC Group 2?**
A Bachelor's degree (any stream) from a recognised university is required for most Group 2 posts. Group 2A posts may require specific degrees — check the post-wise eligibility table in the notification.
