## Scoring Rule Reminder

**No negative marking in GPSC Prelims:**
- Correct: +2 marks
- Wrong / Skipped: 0 marks

This means you should **attempt all 100 questions**. Even guessing gives you an expected score of +0.5 per question across 4 options.

## Mains Qualifying Papers Cutoff

In addition to Prelims, the Mains exam has two **mandatory qualifying papers** (marks not counted in merit):

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: When will the official GPSC 2026 Prelims cutoff be released?**
The official cutoff is released simultaneously with the Prelims result, expected 4–8 weeks after 7 June 2026. Monitor gpsc.gujarat.gov.in for the announcement.

**Q: Does GPSC release sectional cutoffs (topic-wise) in Prelims?**
No. GPSC releases only the overall cut-off mark for each category. There are no sectional or subject-wise cutoffs in the Prelims stage.

**Q: I scored 108 as a General category candidate — do I qualify?**
108 is below the typical General category expected cutoff of 110–125. Your qualification depends on the official cutoff released with the result. Given past trends, 108 has a low probability — but since the paper's difficulty and exact vacancy distribution for 2026 aren't final yet, wait for the official cutoff announcement.
