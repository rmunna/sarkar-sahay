## NEET UG 2026 Result Declared: Your Path to Medical & Dental Colleges! 

The National Testing Agency (NTA) has officially declared the **NEET UG 2026 results today, July 20, 2026**. This is a crucial moment for lakhs of medical aspirants across India who appeared for the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (Undergraduate) on May 03, 2026. Your wait for the highly anticipated "night result" is now over! 

The NEET UG examination is the gateway to undergraduate medical (MBBS), dental (BDS), AYUSH (BAMS, BUMS, BHMS, BSMS), and some nursing courses in various government and private institutions nationwide. Candidates can now access and download their individual scorecards from the official NTA NEET website. 

This article provides comprehensive information on how to check your NEET UG 2026 result, what details your scorecard contains, and the crucial next steps, including the counselling process. 

## How to Check and Download Your NEET UG 2026 Result/Scorecard 

Follow these simple steps to access your NEET UG 2026 scorecard:

1.  **Visit the Official Website:** Go to the official NTA NEET UG portal: [https://neet.nta.nic.in/](https://neet.nta.nic.in/). You can also try [https://nta.ac.in/](https://nta.ac.in/) or [https://results.nic.in/](https://results.nic.in/) if the main site is busy.
2.  **Locate Result Link:** On the homepage, look for the link that says "NEET UG 2026 Result" or "NEET UG 2026 Scorecard Download."
3.  **Enter Credentials:** Click on the link, and you will be redirected to a login page. Here, you will need to enter:
    *   Your NEET UG 2026 **Application Number**
    *   Your **Date of Birth** (as per your application form)
    *   The **Security Pin** (captcha) displayed on the screen.
4.  **Submit and View:** After entering the details, click on the "Submit" or "Login" button. Your NEET UG 2026 scorecard will be displayed on the screen.
5.  **Download and Print:** Carefully review all the details on your scorecard. Download it as a PDF and take multiple printouts for future reference and the counselling process. It is highly recommended to keep several copies safe.

## What to Do After NEET UG 2026 Result Declaration 

Congratulations to all qualified candidates! Here's what you need to focus on next:

### 1. Understand Qualifying Marks and Percentile

The NTA releases category-wise qualifying criteria. Only candidates who score equal to or above the prescribed percentile will be eligible to participate in the counselling process. The minimum qualifying percentile for NEET UG is generally:

*   **General/EWS:** 50th percentile
*   **SC/ST/OBC:** 40th percentile
*   **PwBD (General):** 45th percentile

### 2. Prepare for Counselling (MCC & State Quota)

The next major step is the counselling process. This is conducted by two main bodies:

*   **Medical Counselling Committee (MCC):** Conducts counselling for 15% All India Quota (AIQ) seats in Government Medical/Dental Colleges, all deemed/central university seats, AFMC, ESIC, and AIIMS/JIPMER seats. Registration for MCC counselling will open shortly at [https://mcc.nic.in/](https://mcc.nic.in/).
*   **State Counselling Authorities:** Conduct counselling for the remaining 85% State Quota seats in Government Medical/Dental Colleges and all seats in private medical/dental colleges within their respective states. Visit the official health department websites of your state for details (e.g., dme.tn.gov.in for Tamil Nadu, upneet.gov.in for Uttar Pradesh, etc.).

Keep an eye on the official websites for the detailed counselling schedule, information bulletin, and registration dates, which are usually announced within a few weeks of result declaration.

### 3. Gather Essential Documents for Counselling

Having your documents ready is crucial. Keep both original and multiple photocopies of the following:

*   NEET UG 2026 Admit Card
*   NEET UG 2026 Scorecard/Rank Letter
*   Class 10th Certificate and Marksheet (for Date of Birth proof)
*   Class 12th Certificate and Marksheet
*   Provisional Allotment Letter (downloaded from MCC/State Counselling portal)
*   Identity Proof (Aadhar Card, Passport, Voter ID, Driving License)
*   Passport-size Photographs (usually 6-8 copies)
*   Caste Certificate (if applicable - OBC-NCL, SC, ST) as per central government format
*   PwBD Certificate (if applicable)
*   Domicile Certificate (for State Quota counselling)

### 4. Stay Updated

Regularly check the official websites of NTA, MCC, and your respective state counselling authorities for the latest updates on counselling schedules, seat matrix, and admission guidelines.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: When was the NEET UG 2026 result declared?
**A1:** The NEET UG 2026 result was declared on July 20, 2026, by the National Testing Agency (NTA).

### Q2: How can I check my NEET UG 2026 result?
**A2:** You can check your result by visiting the official NTA NEET website [https://neet.nta.nic.in/](https://neet.nta.nic.in/) and logging in with your Application Number, Date of Birth, and Security Pin.

### Q3: What details are required to check the NEET UG 2026 result?
**A3:** You need your NEET UG 2026 Application Number, your Date of Birth, and the security pin (captcha) displayed on the login page.

### Q4: What is a good score in NEET UG 2026?
**A4:** A 'good' score in NEET UG depends on several factors, including the difficulty of the exam, the number of candidates, and the cutoff for desired colleges/categories. Generally, scores above 600 are considered competitive for government MBBS seats, but it varies each year. Refer to previous year's cutoffs for a general idea.

### Q5: What happens after the NEET UG 2026 result is declared?
**A5:** After the result, qualified candidates will participate in the counselling process conducted by the Medical Counselling Committee (MCC) for All India Quota seats and by respective State Counselling Authorities for State Quota seats. This involves registration, choice filling, seat allotment, and document verification.

### Q6: Where can I find information about NEET UG counselling?
**A6:** For All India Quota (15%) and central university seats, visit the MCC website: [https://mcc.nic.in/](https://mcc.nic.in/). For 85% State Quota seats, refer to the official medical education or health department websites of your respective state.
