## How to Check Result

To check the UP Board Class 10 & 12 Examination 2026 results, students should follow the simple steps outlined below. It is recommended to have a stable internet connection and your examination roll number ready before proceeding.

1.  **Visit the Official Website:** Open your web browser and navigate to the official website of the Uttar Pradesh Madhyamik Shiksha Parishad: `upmsp.edu.in`.
2.  **Locate Result Link:** On the homepage, look for the dedicated link for "UP Board Class 10 Result 2026" or "UP Board Class 12 Result 2026." These links are usually prominently displayed under the 'Important Information' or 'Results' section.
3.  **Select Your Class:** Click on the appropriate link for your examination (High School/Class 10 or Intermediate/Class 12).
4.  **Enter Roll Number:** A new page will open where you will be prompted to enter your examination roll number in the designated field. Ensure that the roll number is entered correctly as per your admit card.
5.  **Submit Details:** After entering your roll number, click on the "Submit" or "View Result" button.
6.  **View and Download Result:** Your UP Board Class 10 or Class 12 Result 2026 will be displayed on the screen. Carefully review all the details. It is advisable to download the result and take a printout for future reference. This printout will serve as a provisional mark sheet until the original documents are issued by the board.
