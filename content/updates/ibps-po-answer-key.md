IBPS releases the PO answer key on **ibps.in** separately for Prelims and Mains, usually within **7–10 days of the exam**. The provisional key is open for objections for 3 days. After reviewing objections, IBPS releases a final key used for scoring. Candidates can use the provisional key to estimate their score and determine cut-off eligibility before the result.

## How to Download IBPS PO Answer Key 2026

1. Visit **[ibps.in](https://ibps.in)** → find "CRP PO/MT 2026" section
2. Click the **"Answer Key"** link (appears under the relevant exam cycle)
3. Log in with Registration Number and Date of Birth
4. View and download your response sheet + provisional answer key side by side
5. Cross-check each question; note any you want to challenge

IBPS shows your marked response alongside the official key — so you can directly calculate your tentative score.

## How to Raise an Objection

1. Login to ibps.in with registration credentials
2. Click **"Challenge Answer Key"** under CRP PO/MT 2026
3. Select the question number, your suggested correct answer, and provide justification
4. Pay ₹100 per challenge (refunded if objection is accepted)
5. Submit before the 3-day objection window closes

IBPS reviews all valid objections. Accepted corrections appear in the **final answer key**, which is used for result computation.

**Negative marking: 0.25 marks per wrong answer** — deduct accordingly when estimating.

**Sectional cut-offs exist:** You must clear each section's minimum cut-off AND the overall cut-off to proceed to Mains. The answer key lets you check section-wise as well as overall.

## IBPS PO Mains Answer Key

IBPS also releases a Mains answer key for the objective sections. Note: the Descriptive Paper (Letter + Essay writing, 25 marks) is evaluated manually and does not have an answer key. The objective sections follow the same download and objection process.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: When is IBPS PO 2026 answer key released?**
Typically within 7–10 days after the exam at ibps.in. The Prelims key usually comes first, followed by the Mains key after the second phase. Watch the official site for the exact release date.

**Q: How many days do I have to challenge the IBPS PO answer key?**
IBPS provides a 3-day objection window from the date the provisional key is published. Late objections are not accepted.

**Q: Does IBPS PO have negative marking?**
Yes — 0.25 marks are deducted for each wrong answer in both Prelims and Mains objective sections. Unanswered questions carry no penalty. Factor this in when estimating your score.
