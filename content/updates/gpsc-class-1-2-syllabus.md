The Gujarat Public Service Commission (GPSC) Class 1 & 2 examination is a highly competitive process designed to recruit eligible candidates for various administrative and executive posts within the Gujarat state government. This multi-stage examination typically comprises a Preliminary Test, a Main Written Examination, and a Personality Test (Interview), assessing candidates' comprehensive knowledge, analytical abilities, and suitability for public service. The selection process is rigorous, aiming to identify individuals capable of contributing effectively to the state's governance and development.

## GPSC Class 1 2 2026 Syllabus — Overview

The GPSC Class 1 & 2 examination for 2026 is conducted by the Gujarat Public Service Commission (GPSC) to select candidates for various Class 1 and Class 2 posts in the Gujarat state administration. The selection process typically involves three stages: a Preliminary Examination, a Main Written Examination, and a Personality Test (Interview). The total marks for the Mains and Interview stages combined are 1000, which determine the final merit list.
