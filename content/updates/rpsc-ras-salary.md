An RAS (Rajasthan Administrative Service) officer starts at **Pay Level 10** with a basic pay of ₹56,100/month as per Rajasthan's 7th Pay Commission-equivalent pay matrix. With DA, HRA, and other allowances, in-hand salary in Jaipur works out to approximately **₹90,000–₹1,00,000/month** at entry level. The salary rises significantly on promotion from Deputy Collector to SDO to Collector.

## RAS & Allied Services — Pay Scale at a Glance

| Service | Level | Basic Pay | Pay Range |
|---------|-------|-----------|-----------|
| RAS (Rajasthan Administrative Service) | Level 10 | ₹56,100 | ₹56,100–₹1,77,500 |
| RTS (Rajasthan Taxation Service) | Level 7 | ₹44,900 | ₹44,900–₹1,42,400 |
| RPS (Rajasthan Police Service) | Level 10 | ₹56,100 | ₹56,100–₹1,77,500 |
| Rajasthan Accounts Service | Level 8 | ₹47,600 | ₹47,600–₹1,51,100 |
| Other Allied Services | Level 6–8 | ₹35,400–₹47,600 | Varies by post |

*Rajasthan follows the 7th Pay Commission pay matrix. Confirm the exact pay level for each post from the official RPSC notification.*

## Additional Benefits

- **Government accommodation:** Priority allotment at district/divisional level postings
- **Orderly / Staff:** Posted with support staff at senior designations
- **Official vehicle:** Provided at SDO level and above
- **Medical facilities:** State government medical facilities + reimbursement
- **Pension:** NPS for post-2004 recruits; defined benefit pension if applicable under Rajasthan rules
- **Children's education allowance:** As per Rajasthan government rules
- **Leave Travel Concession:** Once in 2 years

## RAS vs IAS — Salary Comparison

RAS officers serve within Rajasthan state cadre. IAS officers have All-India Service (AIS) pay scale which starts at Level 10 (same basic) but they receive higher Special Allowance (₹6,600 + DA thereon) and eventually draw the coveted Apex Scale (₹2,25,000/month) and Cabinet Secretary scale. RAS officers who clear the DPC/UPSC combined list can be promoted to IAS after 8+ years of service.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is the starting salary of an RAS officer?**
An RAS officer at entry level draws ₹56,100 basic pay (Level 10). With DA (~53%), HRA (16% in Jaipur), transport allowance and other perks, gross salary comes to approximately ₹1,00,000–₹1,05,000/month before deductions. In-hand is approximately ₹90,000–₹96,000.

**Q: Does the RAS exam recruit only for RAS posts or other services too?**
The RPSC RAS exam is a combined competitive exam — it recruits for RAS and about 20+ allied services including RTS (Rajasthan Taxation Service), RPS (Rajasthan Police Service), Rajasthan Accounts Service, and others. Post allotment depends on rank and preference.

**Q: Is RAS salary better than a central government Group A job?**
RAS and Central Govt Group A (like IRS, IPS through UPSC) start at the same basic (Level 10, ₹56,100). Central Govt jobs may have slightly better HRA in metro cities. However, RAS offers strong local status, government accommodation, and a clear path to IAS promotion.
