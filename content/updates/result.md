The National Testing Agency (NTA) has officially declared the much-awaited results for the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (Undergraduate) 2026, commonly known as NEET UG 2026, today, June 18, 2026. Candidates who appeared for the examination on May 03, 2026, can now access their individual scorecards and All India Ranks (AIR) through the official NTA NEET UG website.

NEET UG is the sole entrance examination for admission to undergraduate medical (MBBS), dental (BDS), AYUSH (BAMS, BUMS, BHMS, BSMS) courses, and B.Sc. Nursing courses across various government and private institutions in India. This result marks a crucial step for lakhs of aspiring medical and dental professionals towards their career goals. It is imperative for all candidates to download their scorecards, as they will be essential for the upcoming counselling and admission processes.

## How to Download/Check Your NEET UG 2026 Result

Follow these simple steps to access your NEET UG 2026 scorecard:

1.  **Visit the Official Website:** Go to the primary NEET UG result portal: `https://neet.ntaonline.in/` (or check `https://www.nta.ac.in/` for direct result links and public notices).
2.  **Locate the Result Link:** On the homepage, look for a link that says "NEET UG 2026 Result" or "NEET UG 2026 Scorecard Download". This link will typically be prominently displayed.
3.  **Enter Your Credentials:** You will be redirected to a login page. Here, you need to enter the following details:
    *   Your NEET UG 2026 Application Number
    *   Your Date of Birth (as per your application form)
    *   The Security Pin (captcha) displayed on the screen
4.  **Submit and View:** After entering the details, click on the "Submit" or "Login" button. Your NEET UG 2026 scorecard will be displayed on the screen.
5.  **Download and Print:** Carefully review all the details on your scorecard. It is highly recommended to download the PDF version of your scorecard and take multiple printouts for future reference and the counselling process.

## What to Do After Checking Your NEET UG 2026 Result

Once you have downloaded and verified your NEET UG 2026 scorecard, consider the following next steps:

1.  **Understand Your Rank:** Your All India Rank (AIR) and Category Rank are crucial for determining your admission chances. Research previous year's cutoffs for your desired colleges and courses.
2.  **Prepare for Counselling:** The Medical Counselling Committee (MCC) will conduct counselling for 15% All India Quota (AIQ) seats and seats in Deemed/Central Universities, ESIC, AFMS, etc. State counselling authorities will conduct counselling for the remaining 85% State Quota seats. Stay updated on their official websites (`mcc.nic.in` for AIQ and respective state medical education websites for state quota).
3.  **Gather Documents:** Start preparing all necessary documents in advance. This typically includes:
    *   NEET UG 2026 Admit Card
    *   NEET UG 2026 Scorecard
    *   Class 10th and 12th Mark Sheets and Certificates
    *   Proof of Identity (Aadhaar Card, Passport, etc.)
    *   Category Certificate (if applicable)
    *   Domicile Certificate (for state quota)
    *   Passport-size Photographs
    *   Provisional Allotment Letter (during counselling)
4.  **Stay Informed:** Regularly check the official NTA, MCC, and your state counselling authority websites for updates on counselling schedules, seat matrix, and admission notifications.
5.  **Research Colleges:** Based on your score and rank, research potential colleges and their admission criteria. Understand the fee structures and other requirements.

## NEET UG 2026 Qualifying Cutoff Scores

The NTA releases qualifying cut-off percentiles along with the result. Candidates must score at or above these percentiles to be eligible for counselling. The corresponding scores typically vary slightly each year based on the difficulty of the exam and the number of candidates. The expected qualifying percentiles for NEET UG 2026 are generally:

*   **Unreserved (UR)/EWS:** 50th Percentile
*   **OBC/SC/ST:** 40th Percentile
*   **UR/EWS-PwD:** 45th Percentile
*   **OBC/SC/ST-PwD:** 40th Percentile

The actual cutoff scores will be mentioned on your scorecard and in the official information bulletin released by NTA. Only candidates meeting these minimum percentile requirements will be considered qualified for admission to medical and dental courses.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: When was the NEET UG 2026 result declared?
**A1:** The NEET UG 2026 result has been declared today, June 18, 2026.

### Q2: What credentials are required to check the NEET UG 2026 result?
**A2:** You need your NEET UG 2026 Application Number, Date of Birth, and the security pin (captcha) displayed on the login page.

### Q3: How is the All India Rank (AIR) for NEET UG calculated?
**A3:** The All India Rank (AIR) is determined based on the total marks obtained by candidates in the NEET UG examination, in order of merit. In case of a tie, NTA's tie-breaking criteria are applied (Biology marks, Chemistry marks, correct attempts, fewer incorrect attempts, age).

### Q4: What is the minimum qualifying percentile for NEET UG 2026?
**A4:** For the unreserved category, it is generally the 50th percentile. For OBC/SC/ST categories, it is the 40th percentile. Specific score cut-offs vary annually and are provided on the scorecard.

### Q5: What are the next steps after checking the NEET UG 2026 result?
**A5:** After checking your result, you should prepare for the counselling process conducted by MCC (for All India Quota) and respective state counselling authorities (for State Quota). Start gathering all required documents and stay updated on official counselling websites.

### Q6: Can I request a re-evaluation of my NEET UG 2026 OMR sheet or answer sheet?
**A6:** No, NTA does not entertain requests for re-evaluation or re-checking of NEET UG OMR sheets or answer sheets. The result declared by NTA is considered final.
