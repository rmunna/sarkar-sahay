## NEET UG 2026 Result Declared: Your Gateway to Medical & Dental Admissions

The National Testing Agency (NTA) has officially declared the results for the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (Undergraduate) – NEET UG 2026 today, **July 26, 2026**. This highly anticipated result determines admission to MBBS, BDS, and various AYUSH courses across India. Candidates who appeared for the examination on May 4, 2026, can now download their scorecards and check their All India Ranks (AIRs) from the official NTA NEET website.

The declaration of the NEET UG 2026 result marks a crucial step for aspiring medical and dental students. The scorecard contains vital information, including marks obtained, percentile score, and qualifying status, which will be essential for the upcoming counselling process. ## How to Download Your NEET UG 2026 Scorecard

Follow these simple, step-by-step instructions to download your official NEET UG 2026 scorecard:

1.  **Visit the Official NTA NEET Website:** Open your web browser and navigate to the dedicated NEET UG portal: `https://neet.nta.nic.in`.
2.  **Locate Result Link:** On the homepage, look for a prominent link stating "NEET UG 2026 Result" or "Download Scorecard – NEET UG 2026".
3.  **Enter Credentials:** Click on the link. You will be redirected to a login page where you need to enter the following details:
    *   **Application Number:** Your unique NEET UG 2026 application number.
    *   **Date of Birth:** Your date of birth in DD/MM/YYYY format.
    *   **Security Pin:** A security pin (captcha) displayed on the screen.
4.  **Submit and View Result:** After entering the details, click on the "Submit" or "Login" button. Your NEET UG 2026 scorecard will be displayed on the screen.
5.  **Download and Print:** Carefully review all the details on your scorecard. It is highly recommended to download the PDF version of the scorecard and take multiple printouts for future reference during the counselling and admission process.

*Always ensure you are accessing the result from the official NTA NEET website to avoid any misinformation or fraudulent links.*

## What's Next After NEET UG 2026 Results? (Counselling Process)

Qualified candidates will now proceed to the counselling phase for admission to various medical and dental colleges. The counselling process is broadly divided into two main categories:

1.  **MCC Counselling (All India Quota - AIQ and Deemed/Central Universities):** The Medical Counselling Committee (MCC) conducts counselling for 15% All India Quota (AIQ) seats in government medical/dental colleges, all seats in Central Universities (like DU, AMU, BHU), Deemed Universities, AFMC, and ESIC colleges. Registration and choice filling for MCC counselling will be announced on their official website: `https://mcc.nic.in`.
2.  **State Quota Counselling (85% Seats):** The remaining 85% of seats in government medical/dental colleges fall under the State Quota. Each state's Directorate of Medical Education (DME) or equivalent authority will conduct its own counselling process. Candidates must apply separately to the respective state counselling authorities where they hold domicile.

**Key Steps in Counselling:**
*   **Registration:** Online registration on MCC or state counselling portals.
*   **Choice Filling & Locking:** Selecting preferred colleges and courses.
*   **Seat Allotment:** Based on rank, choices, and availability.
*   **Reporting to Allotted College:** Document verification and fee payment.

Candidates are advised to stay updated with official announcements from both MCC and their respective state counselling authorities.
