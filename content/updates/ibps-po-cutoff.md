IBPS PO cut-off is declared in two stages — Prelims cut-off (to qualify for Mains) and Mains cut-off (for interview shortlisting). The official cut-off is published at **ibps.in** along with the result. Cut-offs vary by **state** (since vacancies are state-wise) and **category** (General, OBC, SC, ST, EWS). Based on previous years, General category Prelims cut-off typically falls between **55–65 out of 100**.

Mains has **sectional cut-offs** for each paper and an overall cut-off. Failing any section's minimum — even with a high overall — disqualifies the candidate.

## State-Wise Variation

IBPS PO vacancies and recruitment are state-specific (candidates apply to banks in their preferred states). Cut-offs are highest in high-competition states (Maharashtra, UP, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka) and lower in low-applicant states (northeastern states, Jammu & Kashmir). If you apply to multiple states, check the state-specific cut-off after result declaration.

## How to Check Your Cut-Off After Result

1. Go to [ibps.in](https://ibps.in) → CRP PO/MT 2026 Result section
2. Login with Registration Number + DOB
3. Your scorecard shows section-wise and overall scores
4. The official cut-off document (PDF) is published alongside the result

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is a safe score in IBPS PO Prelims to qualify for Mains?**
Based on previous trends, scoring 70+ out of 100 in Prelims (General category) is considered safe for most states. Sectional accuracy matters equally — ensure you meet each section's minimum.

**Q: Are IBPS PO cut-offs the same for all banks?**
IBPS PO CRP is a common exam but vacancies are bank-specific and state-specific. Cut-offs are computed after combining the merit list with available vacancies, so they differ by state. After clearing overall cut-off, final bank allotment depends on preference and merit.

**Q: Where is the IBPS PO cut-off 2026 published?**
The official cut-off marks are published on ibps.in with the result. IBPS does not publish provisional cut-offs before the result — any cut-off you see on coaching sites before results are estimates, not official figures.
