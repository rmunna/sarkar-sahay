## How to Check ICAI CA May 2026 Results

Once the results are officially declared by the ICAI, candidates can check their scores online by following these simple steps:

1.  **Visit the Official Result Portals:** Navigate to either of the official ICAI result websites: `https://icai.nic.in/` or `https://icaiexam.icai.org/`.
2.  **Locate the Result Link:** On the homepage, look for the link pertaining to the "CA Foundation/Intermediate/Final May 2026 Results". This link will become active immediately after the results are published.
3.  **Enter Credentials:** You will be redirected to a login page. Here, you will typically need to enter the following details:
    *   Your **Roll Number**
    *   Your **PIN (Personal Identification Number)** or **Registration Number**
    *   A **Captcha Code** (for verification purposes)
4.  **Submit and View:** After entering the required information, click on the "Submit" or "Check Result" button. Your provisional scorecard will be displayed on the screen.
5.  **Download and Print:** It is highly recommended to download your result and take a printout for future reference. This provisional mark sheet will serve as proof of your performance until the official statement of marks is issued.

Candidates can also register their email addresses on the ICAI website to receive their results directly in their inbox as soon as they are declared. This facility is usually opened a few days before the result declaration.
