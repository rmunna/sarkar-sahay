Category-wise cutoffs are lower. The final merit list (2,035 posts) is expected June 2026 after interview completion in February 2026.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: When will BPSC officially release the 70th Prelims cutoff?**
The official cutoff was released with the Prelims result. For the 70th, Prelims result has been declared and the cutoff is available on bpsc.bihar.gov.in. The Mains cutoff will be released with the final merit list (expected June 2026).

**Q: Does BPSC release separate cutoffs for each post in the final merit list?**
No. BPSC releases a combined category-wise cutoff. Post allocation happens after selection — candidates are assigned posts based on merit rank and post preference. A single merit list covers all 2,035 posts.

**Q: How many candidates qualify from General category in BPSC Prelims?**
Typically 10–12× the number of Mains vacancies are shortlisted. With 2,035 posts and General category getting ~50% reservation, approximately 10,000–12,000 General category candidates were shortlisted for Mains from the 70th.
