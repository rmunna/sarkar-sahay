## How to Register for NEET UG 2026 — Step by Step

1. **Visit the official website** — Go to [neet.nta.nic.in](https://neet.nta.nic.in)
2. **Click on "New Registration"** — Read the instructions and agree to the terms
3. **Fill in personal details** — Name (as on Aadhaar), date of birth, category, mobile number, email
4. **Upload documents** — Passport-size photo, signature, left thumb impression, Class 10 certificate
5. **Select exam city preferences** — Choose 4 city preferences in order of priority
6. **Pay the application fee** — Via UPI, debit card, credit card, or net banking
7. **Download confirmation page** — Save and print your application form for future reference

## 5 Common Mistakes to Avoid in NEET 2026 Application

1. **Spelling errors in name** — Your name must exactly match your Aadhaar and Class 10 certificate. Any mismatch can cause rejection.
2. **Wrong category selection** — Selecting the wrong category (General instead of OBC-NCL, etc.) affects counselling and fee. Double-check before submitting.
3. **Blurry or incorrect photo/signature** — NTA strictly rejects unclear uploads. Follow the exact size and format specifications.
4. **Not saving the confirmation page** — Many students pay the fee but forget to download the final confirmation. Without it, your application is incomplete.
5. **Waiting until the last day** — Server traffic spikes on the last date. Website crashes and payment failures are common. Apply at least 2-3 days before the deadline.

## What Happens After Registration?

- **Correction window** — NTA usually opens a correction window 1-2 weeks after the last date. Only limited fields can be edited.
- **Admit card** — Will be released 15-20 days before the exam on [neet.nta.nic.in](https://neet.nta.nic.in)
- **Exam day** — Carry your admit card + valid photo ID (Aadhaar, passport, or PAN card)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I apply for NEET UG 2026 after March 8?
No. March 8, 2026 is the final date. NTA has not indicated any extension. Apply before the deadline.

### What is the age limit for NEET UG 2026?
Candidates must be at least 17 years old as of December 31, 2026. There is no upper age limit as per the latest Supreme Court ruling.

### How many attempts are allowed for NEET UG?
There is no limit on the number of attempts for NEET UG.

### Can I change my exam city after submitting the form?
Only during the correction window, if NTA allows it. Choose your city preferences carefully.

### Is Aadhaar mandatory for NEET registration?
Aadhaar is required for Indian nationals. Foreign nationals can use a passport.
