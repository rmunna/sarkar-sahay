## What is UPSC Civil Services Examination?

The UPSC Civil Services Examination (CSE) is India's most prestigious competitive exam conducted by the Union Public Service Commission. It recruits officers for the Indian Administrative Service (IAS), Indian Police Service (IPS), Indian Foreign Service (IFS), and other Group A and Group B central services. The 2026 notification has been released for approximately 933 vacancies.

## Vacancy Details

A total of approximately **933 vacancies** are available across Group A and Group B services:

- **All India Services:** IAS, IPS, IFS

## Eligibility Criteria

- **Age Limit:** 21 to 32 years as on August 1, 2026
  - SC/ST: 5 years relaxation (up to 37 years)
  - OBC (NCL): 3 years relaxation (up to 35 years)
  - PwBD: Up to 10 years relaxation
- **Educational Qualification:** Graduate degree from a recognized university in any discipline
- **Nationality:** Indian citizen (with exceptions for certain services as per notification)
- **Number of Attempts:** General/EWS — 6, OBC — 9, SC/ST — Unlimited (up to age limit)

6. Pay the application fee (if applicable)

## Selection Process

The UPSC CSE selection has three stages:

1. **Preliminary Examination** (Objective) — Screening test
2. **Main Examination** (Written) — Descriptive papers
3. **Interview / Personality Test** — Final assessment

Prelims marks are only used for qualification to Mains. Final ranking is based on Mains + Interview marks.

## Exam Pattern

### Preliminary Examination

| Paper | Subject | Marks | Duration |
|-------|---------|-------|----------|
| Paper I | General Studies | 200 | 2 Hours |
| Paper II | CSAT (Aptitude) | 200 | 2 Hours |

- Paper II is qualifying (minimum 33% required)
- Only Paper I marks count for Mains cutoff
- Negative marking: 1/3rd deduction per wrong answer

### Main Examination

| Paper | Subject | Marks | Nature |
|-------|---------|-------|--------|
| Paper A | Compulsory Indian Language | 300 | Qualifying |
| Paper B | English | 300 | Qualifying |
| Paper I | Essay | 250 | Merit |
| Papers II–V | General Studies I–IV | 250 each | Merit |
| Papers VI–VII | Optional Subject (2 papers) | 250 each | Merit |

- **Total Written Merit Marks:** 1,750
- **Interview Marks:** 275
- **Grand Total:** 2,025

## Syllabus (Brief Overview)

### Prelims — General Studies (Paper I)
- Current events of national and international importance
- History of India and Indian National Movement
- Indian and World Geography
- Indian Polity and Governance
- Economic and Social Development
- Environment, Ecology, Biodiversity, and Climate Change
- General Science

### Prelims — CSAT (Paper II)
- Comprehension, Interpersonal Skills, Logical Reasoning
- Analytical Ability, Decision Making, Problem Solving
- General Mental Ability, Basic Numeracy, Data Interpretation

## Preparation Tips

1. **Start with NCERT textbooks** (Class 6–12) for building a strong foundation in History, Geography, Polity, and Economy
2. **Read newspapers daily** — The Hindu or Indian Express — and make notes on current affairs
3. **Practice previous year papers** to understand the UPSC pattern and difficulty level
4. **Choose your optional subject wisely** — pick a subject you enjoy and that has good scoring potential
5. **Take mock tests regularly** to improve time management and accuracy in Prelims
