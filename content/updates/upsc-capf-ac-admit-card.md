## UPSC CAPF (AC) 2026 Admit Card – Overview

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) released the Admit Card for the Central Armed Police Forces (Assistant Commandants) Examination 2026 on **July 18, 2026**. Candidates who successfully applied for the CAPF (AC) 2026 examination can now download their e-Admit Cards from the official UPSC website. The examination is scheduled to be conducted on **August 04, 2026**.

The admit card is a crucial document that serves as your entry ticket to the examination hall. It contains vital information such as your roll number, examination centre details, and important instructions. Without a valid admit card, candidates will not be permitted to appear for the CAPF (AC) 2026 Written Examination. It is also essential for identity verification purposes throughout the selection process.

## How to Download the UPSC CAPF (AC) 2026 Admit Card

Downloading your CAPF (AC) 2026 Admit Card is a straightforward process. Follow the steps below to access and print your e-Admit Card from the official UPSC website:

1.  **Visit the Official UPSC Website:** Open your web browser and navigate to the Union Public Service Commission's official website: [upsc.gov.in](https://www.upsc.gov.in).
2.  **Locate the Admit Card Section:** On the homepage, look for the "Admit Cards" or "e-Admit Cards" section. This is usually found in the "What's New" section or under a dedicated menu item.
3.  **Find the CAPF (AC) 2026 Link:** Click on the link specifically for "e-Admit Card – Central Armed Police Forces (ACs) Examination 2026".
4.  **Enter Your Credentials:** You will be redirected to a login page. Here, you will typically have two options to log in:
    *   **By Registration ID:** Enter your Registration ID and Date of Birth.
    *   **By Roll Number:** Enter your Roll Number (if already provided) and Date of Birth.
    *   Ensure you enter the captcha code (if present) correctly.
5.  **Submit and View Admit Card:** After entering the required details, click on the "Submit" or "Login" button. Your CAPF (AC) 2026 Admit Card will be displayed on the screen.
6.  **Download and Print:** Carefully review all the details on your admit card. Download the PDF file and take at least two to three printouts. It is advisable to keep a digital copy saved on your device as well.

## Details Printed on Your CAPF (AC) 2026 Admit Card

Your UPSC CAPF (AC) 2026 Admit Card will contain several important pieces of information. It is crucial to verify all these details immediately after downloading. The typical details include:

*   **Candidate's Name:** Your full name as provided in the application form.
*   **Father's Name:** Your father's name.
*   **Mother's Name:** Your mother's name.
*   **Roll Number:** Your unique identification number for the examination.
*   **Registration ID:** The ID generated during your application process.
*   **Photograph:** Your passport-sized photograph uploaded during the application.
*   **Signature:** Your scanned signature uploaded during the application.
*   **Examination Centre:** The complete address of your assigned examination centre.
*   **Date and Time of Examination:** The specific date and shift (Paper I and Paper II) for your CAPF (AC) 2026 Written Examination.
*   **Reporting Time:** The time by which you must report to the examination centre.
*   **Gate Closing Time:** The deadline after which no candidate will be allowed entry.
*   **Important Instructions:** A list of guidelines and rules to be followed on the examination day.
*   **Space for Invigilator's Signature:** A designated area for the invigilator to sign.

## Important Exam Day Instructions for CAPF (AC) 2026

To ensure a smooth examination experience, candidates must adhere to the following instructions on the day of the CAPF (AC) 2026 examination:

*   **Carry Your Admit Card:** Bring a clear printout of your e-Admit Card to the examination centre. Without it, entry will be denied.
*   **Photo Identity Proof:** Carry one original, valid photo identity proof (e.g., Aadhaar Card, Passport, Driving License, Voter ID Card, PAN Card) along with a photocopy. The name on your ID proof must match the name on your admit card.
*   **Passport Size Photographs:** It is advisable to carry two recent passport-size photographs, identical to the one uploaded in your application form, in case of any discrepancy in the photograph on the admit card.
*   **Writing Instruments:** Bring a black ballpoint pen for marking answers on the OMR sheet.
*   **Reporting Time:** Reach the examination centre well before the specified reporting time mentioned on your admit card to complete pre-examination formalities.
*   **Prohibited Items:** Do not carry any electronic gadgets such as mobile phones, smartwatches, calculators, Bluetooth devices, or any other communication devices. Bags, study material, food items, and any other unauthorized items are also strictly prohibited inside the examination hall.
*   **Maintain Discipline:** Follow all instructions given by the invigilators and maintain decorum inside the examination hall.
*   **COVID-19 Guidelines:** Adhere to any specific COVID-19 related guidelines issued by UPSC or the examination centre authorities, such as wearing masks, maintaining social distancing, and carrying hand sanitiser.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. When was the UPSC CAPF (AC) 2026 Admit Card released?
The UPSC CAPF (AC) 2026 Admit Card was released on **July 18, 2026**.

### Q2. How can I download my CAPF (AC) 2026 Admit Card?
You can download your admit card from the official UPSC website, [upsc.gov.in](https://www.upsc.gov.in), by navigating to the "Admit Cards" section and logging in with your Registration ID and Date of Birth or Roll Number and Date of Birth.

### Q3. What if I forgot my Registration Number for the CAPF (AC) 2026 exam?
If you forgot your Registration Number, the UPSC website usually provides an option to retrieve it. Look for a "Forgot Registration ID" link on the admit card download page. You might need to enter details like your name, father's name, and date of birth to retrieve it.

### Q4. Can I use a mobile printout of the admit card for entry to the exam hall?
No, a mobile printout or a digital copy on your phone is generally not accepted. You must carry a clear, physical printout of your e-Admit Card to the examination centre.

### Q5. What should I do if my photo or other details are wrong on the CAPF (AC) 2026 Admit Card?
If you find any discrepancies in your photograph, signature, name, or any other details on your admit card, you should immediately contact the UPSC Facilitation Counter. The contact details are usually provided in the official notification or on the admit card itself. It is advisable to carry proof of correct information (e.g., original application form, ID proof) to the examination centre as well.
