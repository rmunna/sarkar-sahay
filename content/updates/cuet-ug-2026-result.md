## CUET UG 2026 Result — Expected First Week of July 2026

The National Testing Agency (NTA) is expected to declare **CUET UG 2026 result in the first week of July 2026**. The Common University Entrance Test (CUET UG) 2026 was conducted from **May 13 to June 1, 2026** across multiple shifts.

> **Note:** As of June 2, 2026, NTA has not announced an official result date. This page will be updated as soon as results are declared. Keep checking **exams.nta.ac.in/CUET-UG** for official announcements.

CUET UG is used for undergraduate admissions to **250+ central, state, and private universities** including Delhi University, JNU, BHU, AMU, Jamia Millia Islamia, and many others.

## How to Check CUET UG 2026 Result

When results are declared:

1. Visit **exams.nta.ac.in/CUET-UG** or **cuetug.ntaonline.in**
2. Click on **"CUET UG 2026 Score Card / Result"** link
3. Enter your **Application Number** and **Date of Birth**
4. Submit and view your result
5. Download and print the score card for future reference

---

## CUET UG 2026 Score Card — What It Contains

- Candidate name and application number
- Subject-wise raw scores and NTA scores
- **NTA Score (Percentile)** for each subject attempted
- Qualifying status

NTA uses **normalization** across different shifts using a percentile-based method. Raw scores are not directly comparable across shifts.

---

## CUET UG 2026 — Subject Groups & Marks

CUET UG 2026 consists of three sections:

| Section | Details | Duration |
|---------|----------|----------|
| Section IA/IB | Language papers (26 languages) | 45 min each |
| Section II | Domain-specific subjects (27 subjects) | 45 min each |
| Section III | General Test | 60 min |

Each subject has **50 questions** (attempt any 40), with **+5 for correct** and **-1 for wrong** answers.

## Participating Universities — CUET UG 2026

CUET UG 2026 results are accepted by:
- **All 45 Central Universities** (including DU, BHU, JNU, AMU, Hyderabad University)
- **State universities** across multiple states
- **Deemed and private universities** that opted in

Each university sets its own cut-off and merit list based on the NTA score. Check your target university's official website for admission schedules after the result.
