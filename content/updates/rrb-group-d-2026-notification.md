## What is RRB Group D Recruitment?

RRB Group D recruitment (CEN 09/2025) is a large-scale recruitment drive by the Railway Recruitment Boards under the Ministry of Railways for Level-1 posts in the 7th CPC Pay Matrix. A total of **22,195 vacancies** are available across 16 Railway Zones for posts like Track Maintainer, Pointsman, and various Assistant positions. The application window is currently open and closes on March 2, 2026.

## Eligibility Criteria

- **Age Limit:** 18 to 33 years as on January 1, 2026
  - OBC (NCL): 3 years relaxation (up to 36 years)
  - SC/ST: 5 years relaxation (up to 38 years)
  - PwBD: Up to 10 years additional relaxation
  - Ex-Servicemen: 3 years after deducting military service
- **Educational Qualification:** 10th Pass (Matriculation) OR ITI from recognized institute OR National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC) from NCVT
- **Nationality:** Indian citizen

**Note:** Candidates awaiting final results are NOT eligible. Diploma/Degree in Engineering is NOT accepted in lieu of ITI.

## How to Apply Online

1. Visit the official RRB recruitment portal at **www.rrbapply.gov.in**
2. Register with a valid email ID, mobile number, and Aadhaar
3. Fill in personal, educational, and preference details
4. Select preferred Railway Zone and post preferences
5. Upload photograph, signature, and required documents
6. Pay the application fee online
7. Review and submit before **March 2, 2026 (11:59 PM)**

## Selection Process

1. **Computer Based Test (CBT)** — Single stage objective exam
2. **Physical Efficiency Test (PET)** — Qualifying in nature
3. **Document Verification (DV)**
4. **Medical Examination** — As per prescribed medical standards

## Exam Pattern

### Computer Based Test (CBT)

| Section | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| General Awareness & Current Affairs | 20 | 20 |
| Mathematics | 25 | 25 |
| General Intelligence & Reasoning | 30 | 30 |
| General Science | 25 | 25 |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** |

- Duration: 90 minutes (120 minutes for PwBD candidates)
- Negative marking: 1/3rd mark deducted per wrong answer
- Questions available in 15 languages

### Physical Efficiency Test (PET)

| Test | Male | Female |
|------|------|--------|
| Lifting & Carrying | 35 kg for 100 metres in 2 minutes | 20 kg for 100 metres in 2 minutes |
| Running | 1000 metres in 4 min 15 sec | 1000 metres in 5 min 40 sec |

## Syllabus (Brief Overview)

- **Mathematics:** Number System, BODMAS, Decimals, Fractions, LCM/HCF, Ratio & Proportion, Percentage, Time & Work, Profit & Loss, SI/CI, Geometry, Mensuration
- **General Intelligence & Reasoning:** Analogies, Classification, Series, Coding-Decoding, Syllogism, Venn Diagrams, Puzzles, Data Interpretation
- **General Science:** Physics, Chemistry, and Life Sciences (up to 10th standard NCERT level)
- **General Awareness:** Current Affairs, Indian History, Culture, Geography, Polity, Economy, Sports, Important Schemes

## Preparation Tips

1. **Focus on NCERT books (Class 9–10)** for Science and Mathematics — they cover most of the syllabus
2. **Practice mental math daily** — speed and accuracy are crucial for the 90-minute time limit
3. **Read monthly current affairs compilations** for General Awareness section
4. **Take full-length mock tests** weekly to build exam stamina and time management
5. **Don't neglect PET preparation** — start a running routine early to meet the fitness standards

## FAQs

### Q: What is the last date to apply for RRB Group D 2026?
The last date to submit the online application is **March 2, 2026 at 11:59 PM**. Fee payment deadline is March 4, 2026.

### Q: How many vacancies are there in RRB Group D 2026?
A total of **22,195 Level-1 posts** across 16 Railway Zones.

### Q: What is the minimum qualification for RRB Group D?
10th Pass (Matriculation) from a recognized board, or ITI, or National Apprenticeship Certificate from NCVT.

### Q: What is the salary for RRB Group D posts?

### Q: When is the RRB Group D 2026 exam?
The CBT exam date has not been announced yet. It will be notified on official RRB websites.

### Q: Is there negative marking in RRB Group D exam?
Yes, 1/3rd of the marks allotted to a question are deducted for each wrong answer.
