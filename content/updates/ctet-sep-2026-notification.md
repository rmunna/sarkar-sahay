## What is CTET Sep 2026?

The Central Teacher Eligibility Test (CTET) is a national-level examination conducted by the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) on behalf of the Central Government. Its primary purpose is to determine a candidate's eligibility for appointment as a teacher for Classes I to VIII in schools across India. This particular notification pertains to the CTET September 2026 examination cycle.

Qualifying the CTET is a mandatory requirement for individuals aspiring to teach in Central Government schools such as Kendriya Vidyalayas (KVS), Navodaya Vidyalayas (NVS), and schools under the administrative control of Union Territories (UTs). Many state governments and private schools also consider CTET qualification for their teacher recruitment processes. The exam is structured into two papers: Paper I for candidates intending to teach Classes I to V, and Paper II for those aspiring to teach Classes VI to VIII. Candidates can choose to appear for either one or both papers based on their educational qualifications and teaching aspirations.

The CTET qualification is significant as it grants a lifetime validity, meaning once a candidate qualifies, they remain eligible to apply for teaching positions without needing to re-appear for the test. This examination ensures a benchmark of quality and standards for teachers, contributing to the overall improvement of education in the country.

## Vacancy Details

It is important for candidates to understand that the Central Teacher Eligibility Test (CTET) is an **eligibility examination** and not a direct recruitment drive for specific teaching vacancies. Therefore, the official notification for CTET Sep 2026 does **not specify any number of vacancies**.

Candidates who successfully qualify the CTET become eligible to apply for various teaching posts (Primary Teacher, Trained Graduate Teacher, etc.) that are subsequently announced by different central government schools (like KVS, NVS, Eklavya Model Residential Schools), state government schools, and private educational institutions. The actual number of teaching vacancies will be specified in the individual recruitment notifications released by these respective organizations.

## Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for the CTET Sep 2026 examination, candidates must meet the following criteria:

### Nationality

*   Candidates must be citizens of India.

### Age Limit

*   There is **no upper age limit** for applying to the CTET examination. Any person who meets the educational qualifications as prescribed by the National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE) can apply.

### Educational Qualification

The minimum educational qualifications for appearing in the CTET Sep 2026 examination are as notified by the National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE) for teaching Classes I to VIII. Candidates must possess the following qualifications for Paper I (for Classes I-V) or Paper II (for Classes VI-VIII):

**For Paper I (Primary Stage - Classes I-V):**

*   Senior Secondary (or its equivalent) with at least 50% marks and passed or appearing in the final year of a 2-year Diploma in Elementary Education (D.El.Ed.).
    **OR**
*   Senior Secondary (or its equivalent) with at least 45% marks and passed or appearing in the final year of a 2-year Diploma in Elementary Education (D.El.Ed.), in accordance with the NCTE (Recognition Norms and Procedure), Regulations, 2002.
    **OR**
*   Senior Secondary (or its equivalent) with at least 50% marks and passed or appearing in the final year of a 4-year Bachelor of Elementary Education (B.El.Ed.).
    **OR**
*   Graduation with at least 50% marks and passed or appearing in the final year of a Bachelor of Education (B.Ed.). (Note: Candidates with B.Ed. qualification for primary teaching must undergo a 6-month Bridge Course in Elementary Education recognized by NCTE, within two years of appointment as a primary teacher).
    **OR**
*   Graduation with at least 50% marks and passed or appearing in the final year of a 2-year Diploma in Elementary Education (D.El.Ed.).

**For Paper II (Elementary Stage - Classes VI-VIII):**

*   Graduation and passed or appearing in the final year of a 2-year Diploma in Elementary Education (D.El.Ed.).
    **OR**
*   Graduation with at least 50% marks and passed or appearing in the final year of a 1-year Bachelor in Education (B.Ed.).
    **OR**
*   Graduation with at least 45% marks and passed or appearing in the final year of a 1-year Bachelor in Education (B.Ed.), in accordance with the NCTE (Recognition Norms and Procedure) Regulations issued from time to time in this regard.
    **OR**
*   Senior Secondary (or its equivalent) with at least 50% marks and passed or appearing in the final year of a 4-year Bachelor in Elementary Education (B.El.Ed.).
    **OR**
*   Senior Secondary (or its equivalent) with at least 50% marks and passed or appearing in the final year of a 4-year B.A./B.Sc.Ed. or B.A.Ed./B.Sc.Ed.
    **OR**
*   Graduation with at least 50% marks and passed or appearing in 1-year B.Ed. (Special Education).

Candidates are strongly advised to refer to the detailed Information Bulletin for CTET Sep 2026 on the official website ctet.nic.in for the precise and complete eligibility criteria before applying.

## Selection Process

The selection process for CTET Sep 2026 is straightforward and consists solely of a **Written Examination**. CTET is an eligibility test, not a recruitment examination, so there are no further stages like interviews, document verification (beyond the initial application stage), or medical examinations conducted by CTET itself.

1.  **Written Examination:**
    *   The examination comprises two papers: Paper I (for Classes I-V) and Paper II (for Classes VI-VIII).
    *   Both papers consist of Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs).
    *   Each paper is of 150 marks, with 150 questions, and candidates are allotted 2.5 hours (150 minutes) for each paper.
    *   There is **no negative marking** for incorrect answers.
    *   **Qualifying Marks:** A candidate scoring 60% or more in the CTET examination will be considered CTET qualified. For reserved categories (SC/ST/OBC/PwD), there is a relaxation of 5% in the qualifying marks, meaning they need to score 55% or more.

**Exam Structure:**

*   **Paper I (for Classes I-V):**
    *   Child Development and Pedagogy (30 MCQs)
    *   Language I (30 MCQs)
    *   Language II (30 MCQs)
    *   Mathematics (30 MCQs)
    *   Environmental Studies (30 MCQs)
*   **Paper II (for Classes VI-VIII):**
    *   Child Development and Pedagogy (30 MCQs)
    *   Language I (30 MCQs)
    *   Language II (30 MCQs)
    *   Mathematics and Science (60 MCQs) **OR** Social Studies/Social Science (60 MCQs) (Candidates choose one based on their graduation subject)

Upon successful qualification, candidates receive a CTET eligibility certificate, which is valid for a lifetime. This certificate makes them eligible to apply for teaching positions in various schools.

## Salary and Pay Scale

As CTET is an eligibility test and not a direct recruitment examination, there is no specific salary or pay scale associated with qualifying the CTET itself. The salary and pay scale will depend entirely on the recruiting organization (e.g., KVS, NVS, State Government schools, private schools) and the specific teaching post (e.g., Primary Teacher, Trained Graduate Teacher, Post Graduate Teacher) for which a candidate is eventually selected.

Do not treat CTET qualification as a salary offer. Pay level, allowances, appointment terms, and service conditions are decided only in the separate recruitment notification issued by the employing body. Check that recruitment notice before applying for a teaching post.
