TNPSC Group 2 / 2A written exam has **two papers: General Tamil (100 marks) and General Studies (200 marks)**. The exam is conducted offline (OMR-based) with objective multiple-choice questions and no negative marking. Group 2A (non-interview posts) total marks = 300; Group 2 (interview posts) adds a 40-mark interview. Download the official syllabus PDF at [tnpsc.gov.in](https://www.tnpsc.gov.in).

## Paper I — General Tamil Syllabus (100 Marks)

General Tamil tests language proficiency and is compulsory for all candidates.

**Grammar (இலக்கணம்) — approx. 30–40 marks**
- Ezhuthu (Letters): Types of letters, vowels, consonants, compound letters
- Sol (Words): Parts of speech — noun, verb, adjective, adverb
- Tholkappiyam basics; Nannool grammar rules
- Sandhi (letter joining), Pugai (compound words)
- Case endings (Vேற்றுமை), Kural sentences

**Literature (இலக்கியம்) — approx. 30–40 marks**
- Sangam Literature: Ettuthokai, Pattuppattu — key works and authors
- Thirukkural — selected couplets (kurals) and their meanings
- Epic Literature: Silappatikaram, Manimekalai, Kambaramayanam
- Bhakti Literature: Thevaram, Divya Prabandham
- Modern Tamil Literature: Bharati, Bharatidasan, Subramania Siva

**Comprehension & Vocabulary — approx. 20–30 marks**
- Reading comprehension (unseen Tamil passage)
- Synonyms (ஒரு பொருள் தரும் சொற்கள்), Antonyms
- Proverbs and idioms (பழமொழி, தொடர்)
- Translation (Tamil to English / English to Tamil)

## Paper II — General Studies Syllabus (200 Marks)

### Unit 1: General Science (25–30 marks)
- Physics: Units, laws of motion, optics, electricity, magnetism
- Chemistry: Elements, compounds, chemical reactions, acids/bases
- Biology: Cell biology, human anatomy, genetics, ecology, nutrition
- Science in everyday life: medicines, fertilisers, food preservation

### Unit 2: Current Events (25–30 marks)
- National events (last 12 months): politics, economy, infrastructure
- Tamil Nadu state events: government schemes, new districts, appointments
- International events: summits, treaties, sports, awards
- Science and technology: ISRO missions, AI/tech developments

### Unit 3: Geography (25–30 marks)
- World geography: continents, oceans, climate zones, natural resources
- India: physical features, rivers, monsoon, soils, agriculture
- Tamil Nadu geography: districts, rivers (Cauvery, Vaigai, Palar), dams, forests
- Environmental geography: pollution, conservation, climate change

### Unit 4: History & Culture (25–30 marks)
- Ancient Tamil history: Sangam Age, Cholas, Pandyas, Cheras
- Medieval India: Delhi Sultanate, Mughals, Vijayanagara
- Modern India: British rule, freedom struggle, key movements
- Tamil Nadu history: Nayaks, Marathas, British in Madras
- Cultural heritage: temples, arts, music, dance (Bharatanatyam, Carnatic)

### Unit 5: Indian Polity (20–25 marks)
- Constitution: Preamble, Fundamental Rights, DPSP, Duties
- Parliament: Lok Sabha, Rajya Sabha, legislative process
- State government: Governor, CM, legislature
- Tamil Nadu political history: major parties, important events
- Local self-government: Panchayati Raj, urban local bodies

### Unit 6: Indian Economy (20–25 marks)
- Planning: Five-Year Plans, NITI Aayog
- Agriculture, industry, services sector overview
- Tamil Nadu economy: industries (auto, textiles, IT), SEZs
- Government schemes: central and Tamil Nadu state schemes
- Poverty, employment, demographic dividend

### Unit 7: Aptitude & Mental Ability (25–30 marks)
- Number series, coding-decoding
- Blood relations, directions
- Ratio, proportion, percentage, average
- Simple problems: profit & loss, time & work, speed & distance
- Data interpretation: tables, bar charts, pie charts

## Preparation Tips

1. **Tamil paper is the differentiator** — most candidates lose marks here. Study Thirukkural (first 10 chapters), Sangam literature authors, and grammar rules thoroughly.
2. **Tamil Nadu focus in GS** — TNPSC tests TN-specific geography, history, economy, and current events heavily. National/international GK is secondary.
3. **No negative marking = attempt everything** — never leave a question blank.
4. **Previous year papers:** TNPSC repeats question patterns extensively. Solve at least 5 years of Group 2 papers.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How many marks is TNPSC Group 2 written exam?**
The written exam is 300 marks (Paper I: General Tamil 100 + Paper II: General Studies 200). Group 2 posts add a 40-mark interview; Group 2A posts have no interview and selection is purely on written exam merit.

**Q: Is English medium available for TNPSC Group 2?**
Yes. Paper II (General Studies) is available in both Tamil and English. Paper I is General Tamil only (tested in Tamil). Candidates can choose their medium at the application stage.

**Q: Is there negative marking in TNPSC Group 2?**
No negative marking. Each correct answer gets 1 mark (for 1-mark questions) or as specified. Attempt all questions.
