## What is CUET UG 2026?

The Common University Entrance Test (CUET UG) is a national-level entrance exam conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) for admissions to undergraduate programmes in central and participating universities across India. CUET UG 2026 is scheduled to be held from May 11 to May 31, 2026.

## Important Dates

| Event | Date |
|-------|------|
| Registration Reopened | February 23, 2026 |
| Last Date to Apply (Reopened Window) | February 26, 2026 (11:50 PM) |
| Exam Dates | May 11–31, 2026 |
| Admit Card | To be announced |

## Registration Reopened — Key Details

The NTA has reopened the CUET UG 2026 application portal following requests from aspirants for a deadline extension. Key points:

- **Application window:** February 23 to February 26, 2026 (up to 11:50 PM)
- **Fee payment deadline:** February 26, 2026
- **No correction facility** is available during this reopened window
- Only new registrations and pending fee payments are accepted
- This is the **final opportunity** to apply for CUET UG 2026

## Eligibility Criteria

- **Educational Qualification:** Must have passed or appearing in Class 12 (or equivalent) from a recognized board
- **Age Limit:** No upper age limit for CUET UG
- **Nationality:** Indian nationals, NRIs, OCI, PIO candidates can apply

## How to Apply Online

1. Visit the official website at cuet.nta.nic.in
2. Click on "Registration" or "Apply Online" link
3. Register with a valid email ID and mobile number
4. Fill in personal, academic, and exam details
5. Upload photograph and signature as per specifications
6. Select test paper(s) and preferred exam cities
7. Pay the application fee online
8. Download and save the confirmation page

## Exam Pattern

- **Mode:** Computer Based Test (CBT)
- **Sections:**
  - Section IA: Languages (13 options)
  - Section IB: Languages (20 options)
  - Section II: Domain-specific subjects (27 options)
  - Section III: General Test
- **Duration:** 45 minutes per section
- **Questions:** 40 MCQs per section (attempt any 35)
- **Marking:** +5 for correct, -1 for incorrect

## Preparation Tips

1. Focus on NCERT textbooks for domain subjects — they form the core syllabus
2. Practice previous year CUET papers to understand the pattern
3. Manage time wisely — 45 minutes per section is tight
4. For General Test, prepare current affairs, quantitative reasoning, and logical reasoning
5. Take mock tests regularly on the NTA practice platform
