HPSC HCS Prelims cut off for **General category** typically falls in the range of **115–125 marks out of 200**. The CSAT paper has a qualifying threshold of 33 marks (out of 100). Category-wise cutoffs are lower: SC/BC candidates typically clear 10–20 marks below General. The 2024 Prelims cut off was ~118 (General), giving a reference baseline.

## HCS Prelims Cutoff — Previous Years

| Year | General | SC | BC-A | BC-B | EWS |
|------|---------|-----|------|------|-----|
| 2024 | ~118 | ~98 | ~103 | ~105 | ~112 |
| 2023 | ~121 | ~101 | ~106 | ~108 | ~115 |
| 2022 | ~114 | ~95 | ~100 | ~102 | ~108 |
| 2021 | ~109 | ~91 | ~96 | ~98 | ~104 |

*Past cutoffs are approximate based on analysis; official cutoffs are published with results on hpsc.gov.in. Verify before relying.*

## Factors That Affect HCS 2026 Cutoff

1. **Number of vacancies:** More posts → lower cutoff. 2026 vacancy count will be announced in the notification
2. **Difficulty level:** Harder paper → lower cutoff
3. **Number of applicants:** HCS typically attracts 1–2 lakh+ applications
4. **CSAT paper:** Qualifying only (33/100 minimum) — does not count in merit

## CSAT Qualifying Marks

The CSAT (Paper II) is qualifying in nature. Minimum qualifying marks:

| Category | Qualifying Marks (out of 100) |
|----------|-------------------------------|
| General / EWS | 33 |
| SC / BC / PwD | 33 (same as General per recent HPSC rules) |

Candidates who do not clear CSAT are not considered for Prelims merit even if their GS Paper I score is high.

## HCS Mains Cutoff

Mains is a written exam (5 papers + optional paper) with interview. Mains cutoff depends on:
- Written exam performance across 5 papers
- Optional subject scores
- Total Mains + Interview marks for final merit list

Mains cutoff is not published separately — the final merit list with marks is released after interview. Typically, candidates scoring **55–60% aggregate across all Mains papers** qualify for the final merit list.

## Safe Score Strategy for 2026

For HCS Prelims 2026, aim for:
- **General:** 125+ marks (5–10 marks buffer above expected cutoff)
- **SC / BC-A:** 105+ marks
- **BC-B:** 107+ marks
- **EWS:** 118+ marks

CSAT: Attempt all questions — even with low accuracy, clearing the 33-mark bar is manageable if you attempt 40+ questions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is HPSC HCS Prelims cutoff same for all posts?**
Yes. The Prelims merit list is combined for all HCS/Allied Services posts. Candidates who clear the Prelims cutoff can indicate post preferences in the Mains application.

**Q: When will HPSC HCS 2026 Prelims cutoff be officially declared?**
The official cutoff is published along with the Prelims result on hpsc.gov.in. Expected within 4–8 weeks after the Prelims exam. Check the official site for the notification.

**Q: Can I predict my selection chance from Prelims score alone?**
No. Prelims is only a screening test. Your selection depends on Mains performance (5 written papers) and Interview (100 marks). Many candidates who barely clear Prelims go on to top the final merit list through strong Mains preparation.
