BPSC 70th CCE requires a **Bachelor's degree in any stream** and an age of **20–37 years for General category**. OBC and EBC candidates get 3 years relaxation; SC/ST get 5 years. There is **no limit on number of attempts** — candidates can appear until they exceed the age limit. Bihar domicile is required for reserved category benefits.

## Educational Qualification

**Minimum:** Bachelor's degree (any stream) from a UGC-recognised university.

Any stream qualifies — B.A., B.Sc., B.Com., B.Tech., MBBS, B.Ed., etc. The degree must be from a university recognised by the Association of Indian Universities (AIU). Distance/correspondence degrees from recognised universities also qualify.

Final-year graduates may apply provisionally subject to producing their degree at document verification.

## Age Limit

| Category | Minimum Age | Maximum Age |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| General (UR) | 20 | 37 |
| OBC (Bihar domicile) | 20 | 40 (+3 years) |
| EBC (Bihar domicile) | 20 | 40 (+3 years) |
| BC Women (Bihar) | 20 | 40 (+3 years) |
| SC / ST (Bihar domicile) | 20 | 42 (+5 years) |
| PwD (General) | 20 | 47 (+10 years) |
| Women (General — Bihar domicile) | 20 | 40 (+3 years) |

*Age is calculated as on the cutoff date specified in the official notification. The 71st BPSC notification (released 5 May 2026) has its own cutoff date — verify for whichever cycle you are applying.*

## Bihar Domicile & Reserved Categories

Reserved category benefits (age relaxation, reservation in vacancies) are available **only to Bihar domicile candidates**. Candidates from other states can appear as General category.

For OBC/EBC/SC/ST benefits, candidates need:
- Bihar domicile certificate
- Valid caste certificate issued by Bihar government authority
- Non-Creamy Layer certificate (for OBC/EBC — verify validity date)

## Nationality

Indian citizen. Subjects of Nepal or Bhutan, Tibetan refugees, and persons of Indian origin from specified countries are eligible as per central rules adopted by Bihar.

## Number of Attempts

**Unlimited** — No attempt limit for BPSC CCE. Candidates can appear as many times as they are within the age limit. This is different from UPSC (which has attempt limits).

## Disqualification

- More than one spouse living
- Criminal conviction (moral turpitude)
- Dismissed from government service
- Involvement in anti-national activities

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can a 38-year-old General category candidate apply for BPSC 71st?**
The 70th has a General category upper age of 37. If you are 38, check the 71st BPSC notification (released 5 May 2026) — the age cutoff date is different and you may qualify based on that date.

**Q: Is there any subject restriction for BPSC 70th?**
No. Any Bachelor's degree qualifies. The exam tests General Studies — not your graduation subject.

**Q: Can a non-Bihar candidate get OBC benefits in BPSC?**
No. Reserved category benefits (OBC, EBC, SC, ST) are available only to Bihar domicile holders with Bihar-issued caste/domicile certificates. Non-Bihar candidates appear as General.
