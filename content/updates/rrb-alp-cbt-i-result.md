## How to Check Your Result Online

Checking your RRB ALP CBT-I 2025 result is a straightforward process. Follow these step-by-step instructions to access your scorecard and qualifying status:

1.  **Visit the Official RRB Website:** Open your web browser and navigate to the official website of the Railway Recruitment Board: [www.rrbcdg.gov.in](https://www.rrbcdg.gov.in).
2.  **Locate the Results Section:** On the homepage, look for the "Results" or "Latest Updates" section. This is usually prominently displayed.
3.  **Find the Relevant Result Link:** Search for the link specifically titled "RRB ALP CBT-I 2025 Result" or "Scorecard for ALP CBT-I (CEN No. XX/2025)".
4.  **Select Your RRB Zone (If Applicable):** If the results are published zone-wise, click on the link corresponding to the RRB zone you applied from.
5.  **Enter Your Credentials:** You will be redirected to a login page. Here, you will need to enter your:
    *   **Roll Number / Registration Number**
    *   **Date of Birth** (in the specified format, e.g., DD-MM-YYYY)
6.  **Submit Details:** After entering the required information, click on the "Submit," "Login," or "Get Result" button.
7.  **View and Download Your Result:** Your RRB ALP CBT-I 2025 scorecard and qualifying status will be displayed on the screen. Carefully review all the details.
8.  **Print for Future Reference:** It is highly recommended to download and print a copy of your result/scorecard for your records and for future stages of the recruitment process.
