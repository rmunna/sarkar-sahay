## UP CNET 2026 Result Declared: Check Your Scorecard and Rank

The Atal Bihari Vajpayee Medical University (ABVMU), Lucknow, has officially declared the results for the Uttar Pradesh Common Nursing Entrance Test (UP CNET) 2026 today, **June 13, 2026**. Candidates who appeared for the entrance examination can now check their individual scores and ranks on the official ABVMU website. This highly anticipated result paves the way for admissions into various B.Sc. Nursing, Post Basic B.Sc. Nursing, and M.Sc. Nursing courses offered by government and private nursing colleges affiliated with ABVMU across Uttar Pradesh.

The UP CNET 2026 examination was conducted successfully on **June 02, 2026**, for which a large number of aspirants competed for seats in esteemed nursing institutions. With the declaration of results, the counselling process is expected to commence shortly. Candidates are advised to download their scorecards and keep them safe for the subsequent admission procedures.

## How to Check Your UP CNET 2026 Result

Follow these simple steps to download your UP CNET 2026 scorecard:

1.  **Visit the Official Website:** Go to the official website of Atal Bihari Vajpayee Medical University (ABVMU) at `https://abvmuup.edu.in/`.
2.  **Locate Result Link:** On the homepage, look for the "UP CNET 2026 Result" or "CNET 2026 Scorecard" link. This will usually be prominently displayed under the "Latest Updates" or "Admissions" section.
3.  **Click on the Link:** Click on the activated result link. This will redirect you to the result portal.
4.  **Enter Credentials:** You will be prompted to enter your application number/roll number and date of birth (DOB) or other required login credentials.
5.  **Submit Details:** After entering the correct information, click on the "Submit" or "Get Result" button.
6.  **View and Download Scorecard:** Your UP CNET 2026 scorecard will be displayed on the screen. Verify all the details mentioned.
7.  **Print for Future Reference:** Download the scorecard and take multiple printouts for future reference during the counselling and admission process.

*   Date of Birth
*   Category (General, OBC, SC, ST, EWS, etc.)

*   Qualifying Status

Carefully check all these details for any discrepancies. In case of any error, contact the ABVMU examination authority immediately.

## What's Next: UP CNET 2026 Counselling Process

With the declaration of results, the next significant step for qualified candidates is the counselling process. The ABVMU will release a separate notification detailing the UP CNET 2026 counselling schedule, procedure, participating colleges, and seat matrix.

Key points regarding counselling:
*   **Merit List:** A state-wise merit list will be prepared based on the marks obtained in UP CNET 2026.
*   **Online Registration:** Candidates will need to register online for counselling, fill their choices of colleges and courses.
*   **Document Verification:** Shortlisted candidates will be called for document verification at designated centres. Essential documents include the UP CNET 2026 scorecard, admit card, mark sheets of qualifying examinations (10th, 12th), caste certificate (if applicable), domicile certificate, identity proof, and passport-size photographs.
*   **Seat Allotment:** Seats will be allotted based on merit, choices filled by candidates, and availability of seats in various institutions.
*   **Admission:** After seat allotment, candidates must report to the allotted college within the stipulated time frame to complete the admission formalities and pay the required fees.

Candidates are strongly advised to regularly visit the official ABVMU website for the latest updates on the counselling schedule and detailed guidelines.
