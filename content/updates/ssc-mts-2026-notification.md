## What is SSC MTS 2026?

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) conducts the Multi Tasking (Non-Technical) Staff examination to recruit candidates for Group C non-gazetted, non-ministerial posts in various central government offices. SSC MTS is one of the most accessible government job exams as it requires only a 10th pass qualification, attracting millions of applicants annually.

## Eligibility Criteria

- **Nationality:** Indian citizen
- **Age Limit:** 18–25 years for MTS, 18–27 years for Havaldar (relaxation as per government norms: 5 years SC/ST, 3 years OBC)
- **Educational Qualification:** Matriculation (10th class pass) from a recognized board
- **Physical Standards:** Required for Havaldar posts only (height, chest, weight criteria)

## How to Apply Online

1. Visit [ssc.gov.in](https://ssc.gov.in/)
2. Complete one-time registration if not already done
3. Log in and click "Apply" for MTS 2026 examination
4. Fill in all required details — personal, educational, preferences
5. Upload photograph and signature as per specifications
6. Pay the application fee online
7. Submit the form and save the confirmation printout

## Selection Process

1. **Computer Based Test (CBT):** Single-tier objective exam — used for merit preparation.
2. **Physical Efficiency Test (PET) / Physical Standard Test (PST):** Only for Havaldar posts.
3. **Document Verification:** Final stage for all shortlisted candidates.

*No interview, no typing test for MTS posts.*

## Exam Pattern

- **Total:** 90 questions, 270 marks
- **Negative Marking:** 1 mark deducted for each wrong answer
- Questions available in English and Hindi (except English section)

## Syllabus (Brief Overview)

**Numerical Ability:** Number System, Fundamental Operations, Percentages, Ratio & Proportion, Average, SI/CI, Profit & Loss, Time & Distance, Time & Work.

**Reasoning:** Analogies, Similarities & Differences, Spatial Visualization, Spatial Orientation, Visual Memory, Problem Solving, Analysis, Judgement, Non-verbal Series, Coding-Decoding.

**General Awareness:** Current Affairs, India & Neighbouring Countries (History, Culture, Geography, Economic Scene, General Polity, Scientific Research), Sports, Awards.

**English:** Basic comprehension, vocabulary, grammar, sentence structure, synonyms, antonyms.

## Preparation Tips

1. **Focus on basics** — The syllabus is 10th level, but competition is fierce. Master fundamentals.
2. **Speed matters** — 45 minutes per session means fast answering is critical.
3. **Read Hindi/English newspapers** — General Awareness is high-weightage (75 marks).
4. **Practice reasoning puzzles daily** — This section is the key differentiator.
5. **Attempt previous year SSC MTS papers** — Pattern and difficulty level remain consistent.

## FAQs

### Q: What is the minimum qualification for SSC MTS?
**A:** You need to have passed the 10th class (Matriculation) from a recognized board. No higher qualification is required.

### Q: What is the salary of SSC MTS?
**A:** SSC MTS posts are in Pay Level 1 (₹18,000–₹56,900). The in-hand salary ranges from ₹20,000–₹28,000+ depending on posting city (X, Y, or Z category).

### Q: Is there any physical test for MTS posts?
**A:** No, the Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST) are only for Havaldar posts. Regular MTS posts do not require physical tests.

### Q: What is the job profile of an MTS?
**A:** MTS duties include physical maintenance of records, general cleanliness & upkeep of the section, carrying of files & papers, photocopying, dispatching of dak, and other non-ministerial tasks assigned.

### Q: Can 12th pass or graduates apply for SSC MTS?
**A:** Yes, candidates with higher qualifications can also apply. The minimum requirement is 10th pass.

### Q: Is SSC MTS a permanent government job?
**A:** Yes, SSC MTS is a permanent central government job with all benefits including pension (under NPS), medical facilities, and LTC.
