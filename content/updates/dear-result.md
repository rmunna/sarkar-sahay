### How to Check Your CUET UG 2026 Result (NTA Scorecard)

Once declared, your CUET UG 2026 scorecard will be available for download on the official CUET NTA website. Follow these simple steps to access your result:

1.  **Visit the Official CUET UG Website:** Go to `https://cuet.samarth.ac.in` or `https://nta.ac.in`.
2.  **Locate the Result Link:** Look for a prominent link on the homepage that reads "CUET UG 2026 Result" or "CUET UG 2026 Scorecard Download."
3.  **Enter Your Credentials:** You will be prompted to enter your:
    *   **Application Number**
    *   **Date of Birth** (as per your application form)
    *   **Security Pin** (captcha code as displayed on the screen)
4.  **Submit and View:** After entering the details, click on the "Submit" or "Login" button. Your CUET UG 2026 Scorecard will be displayed on the screen.
5.  **Download and Print:** It is highly recommended to download your scorecard and take multiple printouts for future reference during the admission process.

### Understanding NTA Score and Percentile

The CUET UG results are declared based on the 'NTA Score,' which is a normalized score across different shifts/sessions in which the exam was conducted. The NTA uses a percentile-based normalization process to ensure fairness, as question paper difficulty may vary across different shifts.

*   **Percentile Score:** Indicates the percentage of candidates who have scored equal to or below that particular percentile in that specific session/shift of the examination. The topper of each session will get 100 percentile.
*   **NTA Score:** This is the normalized score used for merit list preparation and admissions by participating universities. It is calculated using the percentile score. Universities will use these NTA scores to prepare their respective merit lists.

### What to Do After CUET UG 2026 Results Are Declared

Receiving your CUET UG result is just the first step. Here's what you need to do next:

1.  **Verify Scorecard Details:** Carefully check all personal details and scores on your scorecard. In case of any discrepancy, immediately contact the NTA Helpdesk.
2.  **Research University-Specific Admission Criteria:** Each participating university will release its own admission notification, merit list, and counselling schedule based on CUET UG 2026 scores. Visit the official websites of your preferred universities to understand their specific admission criteria, program-wise cutoffs, and application process.
3.  **Prepare for Counselling/Registration:** Universities will conduct their own counselling processes. This usually involves online registration, choice filling of courses and colleges, document verification, and seat allocation.
4.  **Keep Documents Ready:** Ensure you have all necessary documents ready for the admission process. This typically includes:
    *   CUET UG 2026 Scorecard
    *   CUET UG 2026 Admit Card
    *   Class 10th Mark Sheet and Certificate
    *   Class 12th Mark Sheet and Certificate
    *   Transfer Certificate (TC)
    *   Migration Certificate
    *   Category Certificate (if applicable - SC/ST/OBC-NCL/EWS)
    *   PwBD Certificate (if applicable)
    *   Passport-size photographs
    *   ID Proof (Aadhar Card, PAN Card, etc.)

### Important Note on Admission

The NTA only conducts the CUET UG examination and provides the NTA Scores. The admission process, including merit list preparation, counselling, and final seat allocation, is entirely handled by the individual participating universities. You must apply separately to the universities of your choice after the CUET UG results are out.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Q1: When will CUET UG 2026 results be declared?**
A1: The CUET UG 2026 results are expected to be declared in mid-July 2026. Candidates are advised to regularly check the official NTA websites for the exact announcement date.

**Q2: How can I check my CUET UG NTA Scorecard?**
A2: You can check your scorecard by visiting the official CUET UG website (`cuet.samarth.ac.in`), clicking on the result link, and entering your Application Number and Date of Birth.

**Q3: What is the difference between NTA Score and Percentile Score?**
A3: The Percentile Score indicates the percentage of candidates who scored equal to or less than you in a particular session. The NTA Score is a normalized score derived from the percentile, used for creating merit lists across all shifts to ensure fairness.

**Q4: What documents are needed for university admissions after CUET?**
A4: Typically, you will need your CUET UG Scorecard, Admit Card, Class 10th & 12th Mark Sheets/Certificates, Transfer & Migration Certificates, Category/PwBD Certificates (if applicable), ID proof, and passport-size photographs.

**Q5: Can I request a re-evaluation or re-checking of my CUET UG result?**
A5: The NTA does not entertain requests for re-evaluation or re-checking of CUET UG results. The scores are finalized after a robust normalization process.

**Q6: What if I forget my CUET UG 2026 Application Number?**
A6: If you forget your Application Number, you can usually retrieve it from the login portal on `cuet.samarth.ac.in` by using the "Forgot Application Number" option, which typically requires your name, father's name, mother's name, and date of birth to verify your identity. You might also find it on your Admit Card or confirmation page.
