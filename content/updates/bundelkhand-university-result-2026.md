## Bundelkhand University Result 2026 — Overview

**Bundelkhand University (BU), Jhansi** has released the odd semester examination results for 2025–26 session for various UG and PG courses on its official website. ## How to Check Bundelkhand University Result 2026

Follow these steps to view and download your BU Jhansi semester result:

### Step 1: Visit the Official Result Portal

Go to the Bundelkhand University examination portal at [exam.bujhansi.ac.in](https://exam.bujhansi.ac.in/).

### Step 2: Navigate to the Results Section

On the homepage, scroll down to the **"Examination"** section and click on **"Results"**.

### Step 3: Select Your Course

Click on **"View Results"** and select your course (BA, BSc, BCom, MA, MCom, etc.) from the list.

### Step 4: Enter Your Roll Number

Enter your **Roll Number**, select your course, and click on **"View Result"**.

### Step 5: Download Your Marksheet

Your semester result will be displayed on screen. **Download the PDF** and save it for future reference. Verify all details carefully.
