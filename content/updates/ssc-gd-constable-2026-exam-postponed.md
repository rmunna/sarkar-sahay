## SSC GD Constable 2026 Exam Postponed

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has officially announced the postponement of the SSC GD Constable 2026 Computer-Based Test (CBT). The exam, which was originally scheduled to begin from **February 23, 2026**, has been deferred until further notice.

The postponement notice was issued on **February 17, 2026** through the official SSC website.

## Why Was the Exam Postponed?

According to the official notice, the SSC GD Constable 2026 CBT for Constable (GD) in Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs), SSF, and Rifleman (GD) in Assam Rifles has been postponed due to administrative reasons, including an overlap with the ongoing SSC MTS examination.

## What Should Candidates Do?

1. **Keep checking the official SSC website** at ssc.gov.in for revised exam dates

3. **Do not panic** — postponements are common and the exam will be conducted

5. **Admit cards** will be available 2-3 days before the exam
