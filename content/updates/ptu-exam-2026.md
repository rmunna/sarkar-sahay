## PTU Exam 2026 — Eligibility Criteria

### For B.Tech / B.E.

- Passed 10+2 with **Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics**
- Minimum **45% aggregate** (40% for reserved categories)
- Valid JEE Main 2026 score (for centralized counseling)

### For MBA

- Graduation in any discipline with minimum **50% marks**
- Valid CAT / MAT / CMAT score

### For B.Pharm

- Passed 10+2 with **Physics, Chemistry, and Biology/Mathematics**
- Minimum **45% aggregate**

### For M.Tech

- B.Tech / B.E. in relevant discipline with minimum **50% marks**
- Valid GATE score preferred

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What is PTU Exam 2026?

PTU Exam 2026 is the entrance examination conducted by IK Gujral Punjab Technical University (IKGPTU), Jalandhar, for admission to B.Tech, MBA, B.Pharm, M.Tech, and other professional courses.

### When will the PTU Exam 2026 be held?

The PTU Exam 2026 is expected to be held in **June 2026**. The exact dates will be announced on [ptu.ac.in](https://ptu.ac.in/).

### How can I download the PTU admit card 2026?

Visit the [PTU Examination Portal](https://ptu.ac.in/examination-portal/), click on the admit card link, enter your registration number and date of birth, and download.

### What is the PTU Exam 2026 application fee?

The application fee varies by category and course. Check the official notification on [ptu.ac.in](https://ptu.ac.in/) for the exact fee structure.

### Is JEE Main score required for PTU B.Tech admission?

Yes, for centralized counseling to B.Tech programs, a valid JEE Main score is typically required. Some seats may also be filled through PTU's own entrance process.

### What documents are needed for PTU counseling?

You will need your admit card, scorecard, Class 10 and 12 marksheets, category certificate (if applicable), photo ID proof, passport-size photographs, and domicile certificate.
