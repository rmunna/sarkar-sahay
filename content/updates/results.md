## JEE Advanced 2026 Result Declared: Your Gateway to IITs

Big news for aspiring engineers! The Joint Admission Board (JAB) has officially declared the JEE Advanced 2026 results today, **June 19, 2026**. This announcement marks a crucial milestone for lakhs of students who appeared for the prestigious examination, the gateway to admissions into the Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs) and other premier engineering institutes.

Candidates can now access their individual scorecards, check their All India Ranks (AIRs), and category-wise ranks by logging into the official JEE Advanced website. The result declaration also includes the final answer keys for both Paper 1 and Paper 2, along with the minimum marks required for inclusion in the rank list (cutoffs).

This result will determine eligibility for the upcoming JoSAA (Joint Seat Allocation Authority) counselling process, through which seats will be allocated to IITs, NITs, IIITs, and other Government Funded Technical Institutes (GFTIs) based on JEE Advanced and JEE Main ranks, respectively.

### How to Check Your JEE Advanced 2026 Result

Checking your JEE Advanced 2026 result is a straightforward process. Follow these step-by-step instructions to download your scorecard:

1.  **Visit the Official Website:** Open your web browser and go to the official JEE Advanced 2026 website: `jeeadv.ac.in`.
2.  **Locate Result Link:** On the homepage, look for the 'JEE Advanced 2026 Result' or 'Candidate Portal' link. This is usually prominently displayed.
3.  **Enter Credentials:** Click on the result link. You will be redirected to a login page where you need to enter your credentials:
    *   **JEE Advanced Registration Number:** This is your unique registration ID.
    *   **Date of Birth:** Enter your date of birth in DD/MM/YYYY format.
    *   **Mobile Number:** The registered mobile number you used during application.
4.  **Submit and View:** After entering the required details, click on the 'Submit' or 'Login' button. Your JEE Advanced 2026 scorecard will be displayed on the screen.
5.  **Download and Print:** Review all the details on your scorecard carefully. It is highly recommended to download the scorecard and take multiple printouts for future reference, especially for the JoSAA counselling process.

Keep your admit card handy as it contains your registration number and other crucial details required for accessing the result.

### What's Next? JoSAA Counselling and Admissions

Congratulations to all qualified candidates! Your next crucial step is to participate in the **JoSAA (Joint Seat Allocation Authority) 2026 counselling process**. JoSAA manages the joint seat allocation for admissions to 118 institutes, including 23 IITs, 31 NITs, 26 IIITs, and 38 Other-GFTIs.

Here’s a general overview of the next steps:

1.  **JoSAA Registration:** Visit the official JoSAA website (`josaa.nic.in`) and register using your JEE Main Application Number and Password. This is typically the first step, often commencing within days of the JEE Advanced result.
2.  **Choice Filling:** Based on your JEE Advanced rank and eligibility, you will be able to fill in your preferred choices of institutes and courses. Research extensively and make informed decisions, considering your rank, category, and interests.
3.  **Choice Locking:** After filling your choices, you must lock them by the specified deadline. Once locked, choices cannot be changed.
4.  **Mock Allotment:** JoSAA usually conducts mock allotment rounds to give candidates an idea of the seats they might get based on their filled choices. This helps in refining choices.
5.  **Seat Allotment:** Based on the choices locked, ranks, and availability of seats, JoSAA will conduct several rounds of seat allotment.
6.  **Acceptance and Reporting:** If you are allotted a seat, you must accept it by paying the seat acceptance fee and uploading the required documents online. Subsequently, you will need to report online or at a designated reporting centre for document verification.

Ensure you keep track of all official JoSAA announcements and deadlines. Detailed information on JoSAA 2026 counselling will be available on their official website shortly.

### Understanding JEE Advanced Cutoff and Ranks

The JEE Advanced cutoff refers to the minimum aggregate marks and minimum marks in each subject that candidates must secure to be included in the rank list. These cutoffs vary each year based on factors such as the difficulty level of the exam, the total number of candidates appearing, and the availability of seats.

Separate cutoffs are usually declared for different categories (General, EWS, OBC-NCL, SC, ST, PwD). Only candidates who meet these minimum cutoffs are assigned an All India Rank (AIR) and become eligible to participate in JoSAA counselling for IIT admissions.

### Important Points for Candidates

*   **Original Documents:** Keep all your academic and personal documents ready for the JoSAA verification process. This includes your 10th and 12th mark sheets, JEE Advanced admit card, category certificate (if applicable), and identity proof.
*   **JoSAA Brochure:** Thoroughly read the official JoSAA 2026 Information Brochure for detailed guidelines on counselling, seat allocation rules, and important dates.
*   **Eligibility Criteria:** Even if you qualify JEE Advanced, ensure you meet the specific eligibility criteria (e.g., 75% marks in Class 12th or top 20 percentile in respective board exams) for admission to IITs and other institutes.
*   **Beware of Scams:** Rely only on official websites (`jeeadv.ac.in`, `josaa.nic.in`) for information. Do not fall for fraudulent calls or websites promising admissions.
