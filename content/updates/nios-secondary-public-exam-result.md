## NIOS Secondary Public Exam 2026 Result — Overview

The National Institute of Open Schooling (NIOS) has officially declared the results for the NIOS Secondary Public Exam 2026. This crucial examination, conducted by NIOS, provides an opportunity for learners to complete their secondary education through an open and distance learning mode. The results for the examinations held in April 2026 were announced on **June 13, 2026**.

Candidates who appeared for the NIOS Secondary Public Exam 2026 can now check their individual results online through the official NIOS results portal. The online result provides detailed subject-wise marks, overall status, and other essential information. While the total number of candidates appeared and the overall pass percentage for this cycle have not been officially announced yet, students are encouraged to promptly access their results to plan their next academic or career steps. This guide provides comprehensive instructions on how to check your result, understand the details on your marksheet, and what to do next.

## How to Check Your Result Online

Checking your NIOS Secondary Public Exam 2026 result online is a straightforward process. Follow these numbered steps carefully to access your marksheet:

1.  **Visit the Official NIOS Results Website:** Open your web browser and navigate to the official NIOS results portal: [results.nios.ac.in](https://results.nios.ac.in). Ensure you are on the correct and secure website to protect your personal information.
2.  **Locate the Result Link:** On the homepage, look for a section dedicated to "Public Examination Results" or "Secondary Examination Results." You will typically find a prominent link for "NIOS Secondary Public Exam 2026 Result" or similar wording.
3.  **Select Your Examination:** Click on the relevant link for the "Secondary Public Exam 2026 Result." There might be separate links for different examination cycles, so ensure you select the correct one.
4.  **Enter Your Roll Number:** You will be redirected to a new page where you need to enter your NIOS Roll Number. This is a unique identification number provided on your admit card. Double-check the roll number for any typing errors before proceeding.
5.  **Submit Your Details:** After entering your roll number, click on the "Submit" or "View Result" button.
6.  **View and Download Your Result:** Your NIOS Secondary Public Exam 2026 result will be displayed on the screen. Carefully review all the details. It is highly recommended to download the result in PDF format and take a printout for your records. This provisional marksheet will be useful until the official physical marksheet is issued.

## Re-evaluation / Re-checking / Compartment Exam

NIOS provides specific provisions for candidates who are not satisfied with their results or need to clear pending subjects.

**Re-checking of Answer Scripts:**
Candidates who feel there might be a discrepancy in the totaling of marks or that an answer has gone unchecked can apply for re-checking of their answer scripts. This process primarily involves:
*   Re-totaling of marks.
*   Ensuring all answers have been evaluated.
*   Verification of marks entered in the marksheet.
*   Re-checking does **not** involve re-evaluation of answers from scratch.
The official notice regarding the application window, prescribed fee, and detailed procedure for re-checking for the NIOS Secondary Public Exam 2026 result has **not yet been announced**. Candidates are advised to visit the official NIOS website ([results.nios.ac.in](https://results.nios.ac.in)) regularly for updates on this process.

**Obtaining Photocopy of Answer Scripts:**
NIOS also provides an option for candidates to obtain a photocopy of their evaluated answer scripts. This allows candidates to review their answers and the marks awarded. The application window, fee, and procedure for obtaining photocopies will be announced by NIOS separately.

**Compartment/Supplementary Examination:**
For candidates who have not passed in one or two subjects, NIOS conducts supplementary or compartment examinations. These exams allow students to clear their failed subjects without waiting for a full academic year. The application forms, examination dates, and fees for the NIOS Secondary Public Exam 2026 compartment/supplementary examinations have **not yet been announced**. Candidates should monitor the official NIOS website ([results.nios.ac.in](https://results.nios.ac.in)) for detailed notifications regarding these examinations.
