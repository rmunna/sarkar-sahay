## CUET UG 2026 Result Declared! Check Your Scorecard Now

**New Delhi:** The National Testing Agency (NTA) has officially declared the Common University Entrance Test (CUET) Undergraduate (UG) 2026 results today, **July 8, 2026**. Candidates who appeared for the highly anticipated entrance examination can now check and download their scorecards from the official CUET UG portal.

The CUET UG examination is a pivotal national-level entrance test for admissions into various undergraduate programs offered by Central, State, Deemed, and Private Universities across India. The declaration of results marks a crucial phase for lakhs of students aspiring to secure admission in their desired higher education institutions.

Students are advised to keep their Application Number and Date of Birth handy to access their results swiftly. This page provides a comprehensive guide on how to check your scorecard, understand the details mentioned, and what crucial steps to take next for university admissions.

## How to Check and Download Your CUET UG 2026 Scorecard

Follow these step-by-step instructions to access your CUET UG 2026 result and download your scorecard:

1.  **Visit the Official CUET UG Website:** Navigate to the dedicated CUET (UG) 2026 official portal: `https://cuet.samarth.ac.in/`.
2.  **Locate the Result Link:** On the homepage, look for the link prominently displayed as "CUET (UG) 2026 - Result/Scorecard Download" or similar.
3.  **Login to Candidate Portal:** Click on the link. You will be redirected to a login page. Enter your:
    *   **Application Number** (as per your admit card)
    *   **Date of Birth** (in DD/MM/YYYY format)
    *   **Security Pin** (captcha code displayed on the screen)
4.  **Submit Details:** After entering the required information, click on the "Login" or "Submit" button.
5.  **View and Download Scorecard:** Your CUET UG 2026 scorecard will be displayed on the screen. Carefully review all the details.
6.  **Print and Save:** Download the PDF version of your scorecard and take multiple printouts for future reference and the upcoming admission process.

It is highly recommended to download and save your scorecard immediately, as the website might experience high traffic.

## What After CUET UG 2026 Results? (Next Steps)

The declaration of CUET UG results is the first step towards university admissions. Here's what candidates should do next:

1.  **Understand NTA Score:** The NTA Score is a normalized score used for preparing the merit list. It is not the raw score. Universities will use this NTA score for admissions.
2.  **Check University-Specific Cutoffs:** Each participating university and college will release its own admission criteria, including cut-off marks or merit lists, based on the CUET UG 2026 NTA scores. Candidates must visit the official websites of their preferred universities/colleges to check these details.
3.  **Register for Counselling:** Universities will conduct their individual counselling processes. Candidates will need to register separately for the counselling rounds of the universities they wish to apply to. This usually involves filling an application form, selecting courses, and paying a counselling fee.
4.  **Document Verification:** Shortlisted candidates will be called for document verification. Ensure you have all original documents ready, including:
    *   CUET UG 2026 Scorecard
    *   CUET UG 2026 Admit Card
    *   Class 10th Marksheet and Certificate
    *   Class 12th Marksheet and Certificate
    *   Category Certificate (if applicable)
    *   Transfer Certificate (TC)
    *   Migration Certificate
    *   Character Certificate
    *   Passport-size Photographs
5.  **Provisional Admission & Fee Payment:** Upon successful document verification, candidates will be offered provisional admission. They will then need to pay the admission fee to confirm their seat.

Stay updated by regularly visiting the official websites of NTA and the universities you are interested in.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: When was the CUET UG 2026 result declared?
**A1:** The CUET UG 2026 result was declared by the National Testing Agency (NTA) on **July 8, 2026**.

### Q2: How can I download my CUET UG 2026 scorecard?
**A2:** You can download your scorecard from the official website `https://cuet.samarth.ac.in/` by logging in with your Application Number, Date of Birth, and Security Pin.

### Q3: What is the NTA Score?
**A3:** The NTA Score is the percentile score calculated for each candidate across different shifts and subjects. It indicates the percentage of candidates who have scored equal to or below that particular score. It is used for fair comparison and admission purposes, as exams are conducted over multiple days and shifts with varying difficulty levels.

### Q4: What is the normalisation process in CUET UG?
**A4:** The normalisation process (Equipercentile Method) is used to ensure fairness in results, as the CUET UG exam is conducted in multiple shifts and sessions. It converts the raw scores of candidates into percentile scores (NTA Scores) to account for variations in difficulty levels across different question papers.

### Q5: What should I do if there's an error in my CUET UG 2026 scorecard?
**A5:** In case of any discrepancy or error in your scorecard (e.g., incorrect personal details, subject errors), you should immediately contact the NTA Helpdesk at their official contact numbers or email provided on the NTA website. Keep all supporting documents ready for verification.

### Q6: What are the next steps after CUET UG 2026 results are declared?
**A6:** After the results, candidates should check the individual websites of their preferred universities/colleges for their specific admission policies, merit lists, and counselling schedules. You will need to register separately for the counselling process of each university you wish to apply to.
