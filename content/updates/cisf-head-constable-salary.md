CISF Head Constable Ministerial (HCM) starts at **₹25,500 basic pay (Pay Level 4)** under the 7th Pay Commission, with Grade Pay ₹2,400. With DA, HRA, MSP (Military Service Pay equivalent for CAPFs) and other allowances, the **in-hand salary is approximately ₹35,000–₹42,000/month**. As a Central Armed Police Force (CAPF) post, CISF employees get additional CAPF allowances not available to regular civil servants.

## Pay Scale — CISF Head Constable

| Component | Amount |
|-----------|--------|
| Basic Pay (Pay Level 4) | ₹25,500 |
| Grade Pay (pre-7th CPC equivalent) | ₹2,400 |
| Pay Scale Range | ₹25,500–₹81,100 |

## In-Hand Salary Calculation

| Allowance | Amount (Approx.) |
|-----------|------------------|
| Basic Pay | ₹25,500 |
| Dearness Allowance (DA) @ 55% | ₹14,025 |
| HRA @ 8–16% (posting dependent) | ₹2,040–₹4,080 |
| Transport Allowance | ₹1,800 |
| CAPF Ration Allowance | ₹3,000 |
| Other allowances | ~₹1,500 |
| **Gross Salary** | **~₹47,865–₹49,905** |
| Less: NPS (10%) | –₹3,953 |
| Less: CGHS | –₹250 |
| **Approximate In-Hand** | **~₹43,000–₹45,000** |

*DA is revised twice yearly by the central government. HRA depends on city class (X = 16%, Y = 8%, Z = 4%). Above is for a Y-category city posting.*

## Additional CISF/CAPF Allowances

As a CAPF (Central Armed Police Force) employee, CISF HCM gets:
- **Risk & Hardship Allowance:** Varies by deployment area; higher for border/sensitive postings
- **Uniform Allowance:** Annual kit and uniform maintenance allowance
- **Free Accommodation:** Government quarters or HRA if quarters not available
- **Free Medical:** CGHS coverage for employee and family
- **Leave Travel Concession (LTC):** Travel fare reimbursement
- **Children's Education Allowance:** ₹2,250/month per child (max 2)

## Promotion Path & Salary Growth

| Rank | Pay Level | Starting Basic |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| Constable | Level 3 | ₹21,700 |
| **Head Constable (HCM)** | **Level 4** | **₹25,500** |
| Assistant Sub-Inspector (ASI) | Level 5 | ₹29,200 |
| Sub-Inspector (SI) | Level 6 | ₹35,400 |
| Inspector | Level 7 | ₹44,900 |

Promotion from Head Constable to ASI happens via Departmental Competitive Examination (DCE) after 5 years of service. Direct appointments from HCM to SI/Inspector are rare but possible via Limited Departmental Competitive Examination (LDCE).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is the CISF Head Constable salary after 5 years?**
With annual 3% increments on basic pay, after 5 years the basic pay rises to approximately ₹29,500–₹30,500. In-hand salary would be approximately ₹48,000–₹52,000/month.

**Q: Does CISF Head Constable get pension?**
CISF HCM employees recruited after 2004 are under the **National Pension System (NPS)** — not the old defined-benefit pension. NPS contribution is 10% (employee) + 14% (government) of basic pay. Employees recruited before Jan 2004 get the old pension scheme.

**Q: Is CISF Head Constable salary higher than state police constable?**
Yes. CISF is a central government post with 7th Pay Commission salary structure, DA at central rates (higher than most states), CGHS medical coverage, and CAPF-specific allowances. State police constable salaries vary by state but are typically lower at the constable/head constable level.
