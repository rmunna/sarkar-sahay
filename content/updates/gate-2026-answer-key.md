## What is GATE 2026?

The Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering (GATE) 2026 is a national-level examination conducted by IIT Guwahati. GATE scores are used for admission to postgraduate programmes (M.Tech, M.E., PhD) at IITs, NITs, and other institutions, as well as recruitment in Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs).

## GATE 2026 Answer Key — Key Details

IIT Guwahati released the GATE 2026 provisional answer key and candidate response sheet on **February 22, 2026**, through the GOAPS portal. The exam was held on **February 7, 8, 14, and 15, 2026** across multiple centres nationwide.

## How to Download GATE 2026 Answer Key

1. Visit the official website [gate2026.iitg.ac.in](https://gate2026.iitg.ac.in)
2. Click on the "GOAPS Portal" link
3. Log in using your Enrollment ID and Password
4. Select your respective subject paper
5. Download the provisional answer key PDF
6. Download your response sheet to compare answers

## How to Raise Objections

Candidates who believe any answer in the provisional key is incorrect can submit objections through the GOAPS portal within the prescribed timeline. A fee may be applicable per objection. The authorities will review all challenges and publish the final answer key accordingly.

## What Happens After the Answer Key?

After the objection window closes, IIT Guwahati will:
1. Review all objections submitted by candidates
2. Release the final answer key
3. Declare the GATE 2026 result on **March 19, 2026**

## Uses of GATE Score

- **M.Tech / M.E. Admissions** at IITs, NITs, IIITs, and other CFTI institutions
- **PhD Admissions** at top research institutions
- **PSU Recruitment** — organisations like BHEL, IOCL, NTPC, GAIL, ONGC use GATE scores for hiring
- **GATE Scholarship** — qualified candidates in M.Tech programmes receive MHRD scholarship

## Preparation Tips for Future GATE Aspirants

1. Start with NCERT basics and standard textbooks for your branch
2. Focus on previous year question papers — patterns often repeat
3. Practice numerical answer type (NAT) questions regularly
4. Take mock tests on the GOAPS practice portal
5. Revise formulas and key concepts in the last month before the exam
