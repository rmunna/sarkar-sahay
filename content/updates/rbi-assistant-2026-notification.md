## What is RBI Assistant?

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) conducts the RBI Assistant examination every year to recruit Assistants for its regional offices across India. It is one of the most sought-after banking jobs, offering a prestigious career with India's central bank. The selection process includes Prelims, Mains, and a Language Proficiency Test (LPT).

## Eligibility Criteria

- **Age Limit:** 20 to 28 years as on notification date. Age relaxation applicable for reserved categories as per government norms.
- **Educational Qualification:** Bachelor's Degree in any discipline from a recognized university with a minimum of 50% marks (pass marks for SC/ST/PwBD as per RBI norms).
- **Nationality:** Indian citizen

## Selection Process

The selection process for RBI Assistant 2026 consists of three stages:

1. **Preliminary Examination** — Online objective test (60 minutes)
2. **Main Examination** — Online objective test (135 minutes)
3. **Language Proficiency Test (LPT)** — Tests proficiency in the local language of the state

Final selection is based on performance in the Mains exam and LPT, subject to eligibility verification.

## Preparation Tips

1. **Start with Prelims** — Focus on speed and accuracy in English, Reasoning, and Quantitative Aptitude
2. **Practice mock tests** — Attempt full-length mocks regularly to improve time management
3. **General Awareness** — Read daily newspapers and follow current affairs, especially banking and economy news
4. **Computer Knowledge** — Cover basics of hardware, software, networking, and MS Office
5. **Previous year papers** — Solve past RBI Assistant papers to understand the pattern and difficulty level
