## NSSNET 2026 Registration Last Date Extended to March 22

The **National Testing Agency (NTA)** has extended the last date for submission of online application forms for the **Navayug School Sarojini Nagar Entrance Test (NSSNET) 2026**. As per the official public notice dated March 18, 2026, the new deadline to apply is **March 22, 2026 (5:00 PM)**.

The original last date was March 18, 2026. NTA has clearly stated that **no further extension will be granted** beyond the revised deadline.

> **Apply now:** [exams.nta.nic.in/nssnet](https://exams.nta.nic.in/nssnet/)

## How to Apply for NSSNET 2026 Online

1. Visit the official NSSNET portal at [exams.nta.nic.in/nssnet](https://exams.nta.nic.in/nssnet/).
2. Click on **"Registration for NSSNET – 2026"**.
3. Register using a valid email ID and mobile number.
4. Fill in personal details, educational qualification, and class preference (VI or VII).
5. Upload required documents — photograph, signature, and other supporting documents as specified.
6. Review the application form carefully.
7. Submit the form — **no application fee is required**.
8. Download and print the confirmation page for your records.

## Eligibility Criteria

### Class VI Admission
- **Age:** Between 10 and 12 years as on March 31, 2026
- **Date of Birth:** Born between April 1, 2014 and March 31, 2016
- **Minimum Marks:** 65% overall (78 marks) and 50% in each subject in NSSNET 2026

### Class VII Admission
- **Age:** Between 11 and 13 years as on March 31, 2026
- **Date of Birth:** Born between April 1, 2013 and March 31, 2015
- **Minimum Marks:** 65% overall (78 marks) and 50% in each subject in NSSNET 2026

## Exam Pattern

- **Mode:** Offline (OMR sheet-based)
- **Duration:** 2 hours 30 minutes
- **Question Type:** Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)
- **Language:** Bilingual (English and Hindi)
- **Exam Centres:** Within New Delhi Municipal Council (NDMC) area
- **Selection:** Based on merit list prepared from NSSNET 2026 marks
