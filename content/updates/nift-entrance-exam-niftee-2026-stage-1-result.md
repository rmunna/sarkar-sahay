# NIFTEE 2026 Stage 1 Result Out — Check NIFT Entrance Exam Score at nta.ac.in

The National Testing Agency (NTA) has declared the result for the **National Institute of Fashion Technology Entrance Examination (NIFTEE) 2026 — Stage 1**. Candidates who appeared for the NIFT entrance exam can now check their results and download scorecards from the official NTA website.

## How to Check NIFTEE 2026 Stage 1 Result

Follow these steps to check your result:

1. Visit the official NTA website at [nta.ac.in](https://nta.ac.in)
2. Click on the **NIFTEE 2026** link or navigate to the NIFTEE section
3. Look for "Declaration of Result for NIFTEE 2026 Stage 1" notice
4. Click the result link to open the login page
5. Enter your **Application Number** and **Date of Birth**
6. Click **Submit** to view your result
7. Download and save your scorecard for future reference

You can also view the official result notice here:
[NIFTEE 2026 Stage 1 Result Notice (PDF)](https://nta.ac.in/Download/Notice/Notice_20260320140707.pdf)

## Important Details

- Stage 1 is the **written exam** component of NIFT admissions
- Candidates who qualify Stage 1 will be eligible for **Stage 2** (Situation Test / Group Discussion / Personal Interview, depending on the programme)
- **Cut-off marks** and category-wise qualifying scores will be available in the result notice
- Keep your scorecard safe — it will be needed during the Stage 2 process

## NIFT Admission Process After Stage 1

1. **Stage 1 Result** — Written exam scores (declared now)
2. **Stage 2** — Situation Test (for B.Des) / GD+PI (for other programmes)
3. **Final Merit List** — Combined Stage 1 + Stage 2 scores
4. **Counselling & Seat Allotment** — Based on final merit and preferences

## FAQs

### What is the NIFTEE 2026 Stage 1 cut-off?
Category-wise cut-off marks are available in the official result notice. Check the PDF linked above for detailed cut-off information.

### When will NIFTEE 2026 Stage 2 be conducted?
NTA will announce Stage 2 dates separately. Qualified candidates should regularly check nta.ac.in and the NIFT official website for updates.

### I qualified Stage 1. What should I prepare for Stage 2?
Stage 2 varies by programme — B.Des candidates typically have a Situation Test, while M.Des/M.FTech/MFM candidates may have GD and PI. Check the NIFT prospectus for programme-specific details.

### How can I get my NIFTEE 2026 scorecard?
Log in to the NTA NIFTEE portal with your application number and date of birth. The scorecard will be available for download.
