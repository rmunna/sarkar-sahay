3.  **Select the Relevant Examination:** You will be redirected to the results page displaying various examination results. 4.  **Enter Your Credentials:** You will likely be prompted to enter your Roll Number and/or Registration Number, along with your Date of Birth or a password, as specified on the result page. Ensure you enter these details accurately as per your admit card.
5.  **Submit and View Result:** After entering the required information, click on the 'Submit' or 'Login' button. Your result will be displayed on the screen.
6.  **Download and Print:** It is highly recommended to download your result/scorecard and take a printout for future reference and record-keeping. This provisional result copy will be crucial for the subsequent stages of the selection process.

## Re-evaluation / Re-checking / Compartment Exam

For the Staff Selection Commission's Computer Based Examinations (CBEs), which are typically objective-type (Multiple Choice Questions), there is generally **no provision for re-evaluation or re-checking of answer sheets**. The evaluation process is highly standardized and automated, minimizing errors. Therefore, candidates cannot apply for a re-evaluation of their marks for the Delhi Police Constable (Exe) 2025 CBT.

Furthermore, the concept of a 'compartment exam' or 'supplementary exam' does not apply to competitive recruitment examinations like the Delhi Police Constable (Exe). These are typically associated with academic board examinations. If a candidate does not qualify, they would need to re-apply and appear for the examination in a future recruitment cycle, should one be announced.
