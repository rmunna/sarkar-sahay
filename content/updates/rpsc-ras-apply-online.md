RPSC RAS 2026 applications are submitted online through the **SSO (Single Sign-On) portal at sso.rajasthan.gov.in** — not directly on the RPSC website. The notification and application link appear on [rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in](https://rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in), which redirects to SSO for form submission. Candidates who do not have an SSO ID must register first before filling the RAS application.

## Step-by-Step: How to Apply for RPSC RAS 2026

1. **Create SSO ID** (if you don't have one): Go to [sso.rajasthan.gov.in](https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in) → Register → Use Bhamashah/Aadhaar/Facebook/Google or Rajasthan Govt employee ID
2. **Login to SSO portal** with your SSO ID and password
3. **Find RPSC RAS 2026** in the "Citizen Apps" or search "RPSC" on the dashboard
4. **Click "Apply"** next to the RAS/RTS Combined Competitive Exam 2026
5. **Fill Part 1 — Personal Details:** Name, DOB, category, address, domicile status
6. **Fill Part 2 — Educational Qualification:** Graduation details, optional subject choice
7. **Upload documents:** Passport-size photo (JPG, under 100KB), signature (JPG, under 50KB)
8. **Pay application fee** online (credit/debit card, net banking, UPI via Rajasthan payment gateway)
9. **Submit and download** confirmation/acknowledgment receipt — save the application number

## Documents Needed at Application Stage

- Scanned passport-size photograph (white/light background)
- Scanned signature
- SSO ID / Bhamashah card / Aadhaar number (for SSO registration)

Full document verification (original certificates) happens only at the interview stage.

## Key Tips

- **Do not use third-party links** to fill the form — only sso.rajasthan.gov.in is official
- RPSC conducts Prelims → Mains → Interview; optional subject is chosen at the application stage (Mains only)
- Rajasthan domicile is required for reserved category benefits and some post preferences
- Check the RPSC notification for the optional subject list — strategy matters here

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Where exactly do I apply for RPSC RAS 2026?**
Applications are submitted on the SSO portal (sso.rajasthan.gov.in), not directly on rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in. The RPSC website carries the notification and links to SSO.

**Q: Do I need a Rajasthan domicile to apply for RPSC RAS?**
Candidates from any state can apply in the General (UR) category. Rajasthan domicile is required only to claim OBC/SC/ST category benefits of Rajasthan state.

**Q: Can I edit my RPSC RAS 2026 application after submission?**
RPSC typically offers a correction window of 5–7 days after the application period. Minor details (category, photo, exam centre) can be changed; core details (name, DOB, optional subject) usually cannot. Check the official notification for correction window dates.
