### Selection Process

The selection process for India Post GDS is distinct from many other government exams as it does not involve a written examination. The selection is based on a merit list.

*   **Shortlisting Based on Merit:** Candidates will be shortlisted purely based on the marks obtained in their 10th standard (Matriculation) examination. The system will generate a merit list based on the percentage of marks secured by the applicants.
*   **No Written Examination:** There will be no written test or interview for the GDS posts. The entire selection hinges on academic performance in the 10th standard.
*   **Document Verification:** Shortlisted candidates will undergo a rigorous document verification process. It is crucial for candidates to possess all original documents, including educational certificates, caste certificates (if applicable), PwD certificates (if applicable), and other identity proofs, as specified in the detailed notification. Any discrepancy or failure to produce required documents during verification will lead to disqualification.
