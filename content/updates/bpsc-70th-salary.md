BPSC 70th CCE recruits for posts like **SDM, DSP, BDO** with starting pay at **Pay Level 10 (₹56,100 basic)**. With Bihar state DA, HRA, and allowances, **in-hand salary ranges from ₹48,000 to ₹72,000/month** depending on the post and posting location.

*Bihar implements state-specific 7th Pay Commission with its own DA revision schedule.*

## Additional Benefits

- **Government accommodation:** Official residence at posting place (bungalow-type for SDM/DSP)
- **Government vehicle:** Staff car/jeep for field operations
- **Orderly/Peon:** Official support staff assigned
- **Medical:** Bihar State Health Scheme
- **NPS pension:** 10% + 14% employer contribution
- **LTC:** Leave travel concession

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is the SDM in-hand salary in Bihar in 2026?**
An SDM in Bihar starts at ₹56,100 basic. With DA at ~50% (Bihar rate), HRA, and allowances, in-hand salary is approximately ₹65,000–₹72,000/month in Patna and urban postings. Mofussil postings are slightly lower due to lower HRA.

**Q: Is BPSC officer salary on par with central IAS?**
Both BPSC and IAS start at Pay Level 10 (₹56,100 basic) at entry. However, Bihar DA rate is lower than central DA, so BPSC officers receive slightly lower in-hand salary than central IAS officers at the same basic pay level.

**Q: Does the BDO get a vehicle and accommodation?**
Yes. Block Development Officers typically get a government jeep for rural/block-level travel and official residence in the block headquarters. This makes the effective compensation significantly higher than the monetary in-hand salary.
