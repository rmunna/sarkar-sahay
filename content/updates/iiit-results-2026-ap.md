## AP IIIT Results 2026 Declared: Your Gateway to RGUKT Admissions

The wait is over for lakhs of aspirants! The Rajiv Gandhi University of Knowledge Technologies (RGUKT) has officially declared the results for the Common Entrance Test (CET) 2026, commonly known as AP IIIT CET 2026. Candidates who appeared for the examination for admission into the six-year integrated B.Tech programs at RGUKT's four campuses – Nuzvid, R.K. Valley (Idupulapaya), Ongole, and Srikakulam – can now check their scores and rank cards.

This declaration marks a crucial step for students aiming to pursue their higher education in the prestigious IIITs of Andhra Pradesh. The results are now available on the official RGUKT website, `www.rgukt.in`. It is highly recommended that candidates download and print their result cards for future reference during the counselling and admission process.

## How to Check Your RGUKT CET 2026 Results

Checking your AP IIIT 2026 result is a straightforward process. Follow these step-by-step instructions to access your score and rank card:

1.  **Visit the Official Website:** Open your web browser and go to the official RGUKT portal: `https://www.rgukt.in`. You may also directly visit the results portal at `https://results.rgukt.in` if available.
2.  **Locate the Results Link:** On the homepage, look for a prominent link titled "RGUKT CET 2026 Results" or "AP IIIT Admissions 2026 Result". This link is usually highlighted under the 'Latest Updates' or 'Admissions' section.
3.  **Click on the Link:** Click on the respective results link to proceed.
4.  **Enter Credentials:** You will be redirected to a new page where you need to enter your login credentials. Typically, you will be required to provide your:
    *   **RGUKT CET 2026 Hall Ticket Number / Roll Number**
    *   **Date of Birth (DOB)**
5.  **Submit Information:** After entering the required details, click on the "Submit" or "Get Result" button.
6.  **View and Download Result:** Your RGUKT CET 2026 Score Card and Rank Card will be displayed on the screen. Carefully review all the details.
7.  **Print for Records:** Download the result card and take at least two printouts for your records. This printed copy will be essential during the counselling and document verification stages.

In case of any discrepancy in the details, candidates are advised to immediately contact the RGUKT CET convenor's office for rectification.

## What's Next After Checking Your AP IIIT 2026 Results?

Once you have successfully checked and downloaded your RGUKT CET 2026 result, the next crucial phase is the counselling process. Admission to the RGUKT IIITs is purely based on merit, considering the ranks obtained in the CET and other specified criteria.

### 1. Merit List Publication

RGUKT will release detailed merit lists, often category-wise, based on the performance in the CET. These lists will indicate the candidates eligible for the counselling rounds. Keep an eye on the official website for these publications.

### 2. Counselling Schedule and Notification

Qualified candidates will be notified about the counselling schedule, dates, venues, and specific instructions. This information will be released exclusively on the `www.rgukt.in` website. It is imperative to check the website regularly to avoid missing any deadlines.

### 3. Document Verification

During the counselling process, candidates will be required to report to the designated venue with a set of original documents for verification. Prepare the following documents in advance:

*   RGUKT CET 2026 Rank Card
*   RGUKT CET 2026 Hall Ticket
*   SSC (10th Class) Marks Sheet / Provisional Certificate
*   Study Certificates from Class IV to X
*   Caste Certificate (for SC/ST/BC candidates) issued by the competent authority
*   Income Certificate (issued after 01.01.2026) for tuition fee reimbursement claims
*   EWS Certificate (if applicable)
*   Residence Certificate (for candidates who studied in private schools or out of state)
*   Physical Fitness Certificate from a Civil Assistant Surgeon
*   Aadhaar Card
*   Passport-size photographs

### 4. Choice Filling and Seat Allotment

Based on your rank and eligibility, you will be guided through the choice-filling process, where you can select your preferred campus. Seat allotment will follow, taking into account ranks, category, and availability of seats.
