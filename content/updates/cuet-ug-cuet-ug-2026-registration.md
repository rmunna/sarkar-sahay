## Key Details

Understanding the specifics of the CUET (UG) 2026 registration process is crucial for all stakeholders. Here are the essential details as per the official notification:

### Application Fee

The application fee structure for CUET (UG) 2026 varied based on the candidate's category and the number of subjects chosen. It is important to note that these fees were non-refundable.

*   **General/EWS Category:**
    *   Up to 03 Subjects: ₹1000
    *   Each Additional Subject: ₹400
*   **OBC Category:**
    *   Up to 03 Subjects: ₹900
    *   Each Additional Subject: ₹375
*   **SC/ST Category:**
    *   Up to 03 Subjects: ₹800
    *   Each Additional Subject: ₹350
*   **Persons with Disabilities (PwD) Category:**
    *   Up to 03 Subjects: ₹800
    *   Each Additional Subject: ₹350

Candidates were required to pay the application fee online through various payment gateways as specified during the application process.

### Education Qualification

To be eligible for CUET (UG) 2026, candidates needed to meet the following educational criteria:

*   **Passed Class 12/equivalent examination:** Candidates who had successfully passed their Class 12 or an equivalent examination from a recognized board were eligible.
*   **Appearing in 2026:** Candidates who were appearing for their Class 12 or equivalent examination in the year 2026 were also eligible to apply. Such candidates would typically need to meet the minimum passing criteria of their respective boards by the time of admission to the universities.

It is important to note that specific university programs might have additional subject-specific eligibility criteria, which candidates were advised to check directly with the respective universities.

### Selection Process

The selection process for admission through CUET (UG) 2026 was based entirely on the candidate's performance in the Computer Based Test (CBT).

*   **Computer Based Test (CBT):** The examination was conducted in an online mode, where candidates answered questions on a computer system. The test comprised multiple-choice questions (MCQs) across various sections, including Language, Domain-Specific Subjects, and General Test, depending on the candidate's chosen program and university requirements.

Universities would then use the CUET (UG) scores to prepare their merit lists and conduct their respective admission counseling processes.

### Vacancies

The official notification for CUET (UG) 2026 registration did **not specify** the number of vacancies. Admissions are offered by individual participating universities and institutions based on their available seats for various undergraduate programs. Candidates were advised to refer to the admission brochures and websites of their desired universities for details on program-wise seat matrix.
