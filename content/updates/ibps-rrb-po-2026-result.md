## What is IBPS RRB PO?

The IBPS RRB PO (Officer Scale I) examination is conducted by the Institute of Banking Personnel Selection for recruitment of Probationary Officers in Regional Rural Banks across India. The selection process involves Prelims, Mains, and Interview stages, with the final merit based on an 80:20 weightage ratio of Mains and Interview marks.

## Result Details

IBPS has declared the final results for three categories of officer posts under CRP RRB XIV:

- **Officer Scale I** (Probationary Officer) — Final result out
- **Officer Scale II** (Specialist Officer & GBO) — Final result out
- **Officer Scale III** — Final result out

The final merit list has been prepared based on:
- Mains Examination Marks (80% weightage)
- Interview Marks (20% weightage)

## How to Check IBPS RRB PO Final Result 2026

1. Visit the official IBPS website at ibps.in
2. Click on the "CRP RRBs XIV" section on the homepage
3. Look for the link titled "Combined Result for Online Main Exam and Interview"
4. Log in using your Registration Number or Roll Number
5. Enter your Password or Date of Birth
6. Your final result status will be displayed on screen
7. Download and save the result for future reference

## Cut-Off Marks

The combined cut-off marks (out of 100) for Mains and Interview stages have been published along with the result on the official website. Candidates can check category-wise cut-off marks by logging in to the portal.

## Scorecard Download

Individual scorecards showing Mains and Interview marks are available for download on the IBPS portal. Candidates should download and save their scorecards for records.

## What Happens After Selection?

After the final result, selected candidates will undergo:

1. **Provisional Allotment** — Bank-wise allotment based on merit and preference
2. **Document Verification** — Original documents will be verified
3. **Joining** — Selected candidates will receive joining letters from their allotted Regional Rural Banks

## Documents Required After Selection

- Valid photo ID proof (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport)
- Educational qualification certificates and mark sheets
- Caste/Category certificate (if applicable)
- Experience certificate (if applicable)
- Passport-size photographs
- IBPS call letter and scorecard
