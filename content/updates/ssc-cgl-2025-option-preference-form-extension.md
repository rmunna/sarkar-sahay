The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has issued an important notice regarding the **extension of the window for the Option-cum-Preference Form** for the **Combined Graduate Level (CGL) Examination 2025**. Candidates who qualified in the CGL 2025 examination now have additional time to submit their post preferences.

| **Conducting Body** | Staff Selection Commission (SSC) |
| **Notice Type** | Extension of Option-cum-Preference Form |
| **Notice Date** | March 12, 2026 |
| **Official Website** | [ssc.gov.in](https://ssc.gov.in) |

## What Is the Option-cum-Preference Form?

The Option-cum-Preference Form allows qualified CGL candidates to select and rank their preferred posts and ministries/departments for final allocation. This is a critical step — your post allocation depends on:

- Your **rank** in the merit list
- Your **preferences** submitted in this form
- **Vacancy availability** in each post/department

> **Important:** If you don't submit the form within the deadline, SSC may allocate posts based on default preference order, which may not match your choice.

## How to Fill the SSC CGL 2025 Option-cum-Preference Form

1. Visit [ssc.gov.in](https://ssc.gov.in)
2. Log in to your candidate dashboard using **Registration Number** and **Password**
3. Navigate to the **Option-cum-Preference Form** section
4. Review the list of available posts (AAO, Auditor, Tax Assistant, UDC, etc.)
5. **Rank the posts** in order of your preference (1 = most preferred)
6. Select preferred **ministries/departments** and **posting locations** where applicable
7. Review all selections carefully — changes may not be allowed after submission
8. **Submit the form** before the extended deadline

Official notice: [SSC CGL 2025 Notice (PDF)](https://ssc.gov.in/api/attachment/uploads/masterData/NoticeBoards/Notice_12032026.pdf)

## Tips for Filling Preferences

- **Research each post** — understand pay scale, promotion prospects, and job profile before ranking
- **Consider posting location** — some posts have fixed locations, others involve transfers
- AAO (Assistant Audit/Accounts Officer) is Group B; most others are Group C
- **Submit early** — don't wait until the last day; server load may cause issues

## FAQs

### Why was the SSC CGL 2025 option form deadline extended?
SSC extended the window to give candidates more time to submit their post preferences. The exact reason is mentioned in the official notice.

### Can I modify my preferences after submitting?
Typically no. Once submitted, preferences are locked. Make sure to review carefully before final submission.

### What happens if I don't fill the preference form?
SSC may allocate posts based on a default order, which may not align with your preferred choices. It is strongly recommended to fill the form.

### Where can I find the official notice?
Visit [ssc.gov.in](https://ssc.gov.in) or download the notice directly from the [SSC Notice Board](https://ssc.gov.in/api/attachment/uploads/masterData/NoticeBoards/Notice_12032026.pdf).
