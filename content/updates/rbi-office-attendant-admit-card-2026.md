# RBI Office Attendant Admit Card 2026 – Download Your Call Letter

The **Reserve Bank of India (RBI)** has released the admit card for the **Office Attendant 2026 recruitment exam**. Candidates who successfully registered for the RBI Office Attendant examination can now download their call letter from the official website at **opportunities.rbi.org.in**.

The RBI Office Attendant exam is expected to be conducted in **March–April 2026** across multiple centres nationwide. Downloading and printing the admit card well in advance is essential to avoid last-minute issues.

## How to Download RBI Office Attendant Admit Card 2026

Follow these steps to download your call letter:

1. Visit the official RBI recruitment portal at [opportunities.rbi.org.in](https://opportunities.rbi.org.in).
2. Click on **"Current Vacancies"** or look for the **Office Attendant 2026** link.
3. Select **"Download Call Letter"** for the Office Attendant examination.
4. Enter your **Registration Number / Roll Number**.
5. Enter your **Date of Birth** (DD/MM/YYYY format).
6. Enter the **captcha code** displayed on screen.
7. Click **"Submit"** to view your admit card.
8. Download the PDF and take **two colour printouts**.

> **Tip:** If you have forgotten your registration number, use the "Forgot Registration Number" option on the login page. You will need your registered email or mobile number for recovery.

## Important Details on the Admit Card

Your RBI Office Attendant call letter will contain the following information:

- **Candidate name and photograph**
- **Roll number and registration number**
- **Exam date, time, and reporting time**
- **Exam centre name and address**
- **Category and post applied for**
- **Instructions for the examination day**

Verify all details carefully. If there is any discrepancy in your name, photograph, or category, contact the RBI helpdesk immediately using the contact details provided on the admit card.

## Syllabus Overview

- **General Awareness:** Current affairs, banking awareness, Indian economy, national events, sports, awards.
- **English Language:** Reading comprehension, fill in the blanks, error spotting, sentence rearrangement.
- **Numerical Ability:** Simplification, number series, percentages, ratio, profit & loss, time and work.
- **Reasoning Ability:** Puzzles, seating arrangement, coding-decoding, blood relations, syllogisms, inequalities.

## What to Carry to the Exam Centre

Candidates must bring the following on exam day:

- **Printed admit card** (colour printout, two copies recommended)
- **Valid photo ID proof** (original) – Aadhaar card, PAN card, passport, voter ID, or driving licence
- **One passport-size photograph** (same as uploaded during registration)
- **A transparent water bottle** and personal hand sanitiser
- **A ball-point pen** (for signing attendance sheet)

**Do not carry:** Mobile phones, electronic gadgets, calculators, watches, wallets, bags, or any written material. These items are strictly prohibited inside the exam hall.
