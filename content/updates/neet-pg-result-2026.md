## NEET PG 2026 Result Declared: Download Scorecard and Check Merit List

**New Delhi, September 24, 2026:** The National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS) has officially declared the much-awaited result for the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test – Postgraduate (NEET PG) 2026 examination. All candidates who appeared for the exam can now check their individual scorecards and the NEET PG 2026 Merit List on the official NBEMS website. This is a crucial step for aspiring medical postgraduates seeking admission to various MD/MS/PG Diploma courses across India.

The NEET PG examination is the single qualifying entrance examination for admission to postgraduate medical courses. The declaration of results marks the beginning of the counselling process, which will be conducted by the Medical Counselling Committee (MCC) for All India Quota seats and by respective State Counselling Authorities for State Quota seats.

Candidates are advised to carefully review their results and keep their scorecards safe, as it will be an essential document for the upcoming counselling rounds.

## NEET PG 2026 Result Overview

The NEET PG 2026 examination was conducted by NBEMS as a computer-based test (CBT) across various centers nationwide. This year, the exam saw a significant number of medical graduates vying for limited postgraduate seats in both government and private medical colleges, as well as deemed universities. The result includes individual scores, the NEET PG rank, and eligibility status for counselling.

NBEMS has released a merit list comprising the names and ranks of all qualified candidates. Only candidates who meet the prescribed cut-off percentile are eligible to participate in the counselling process. The NEET PG score is valid for the current academic session only.

## How to Check and Download NEET PG 2026 Result/Scorecard

Checking your NEET PG 2026 result and downloading your scorecard is a straightforward process. Follow these steps carefully:

1.  **Visit the Official NBEMS Website:** Go to the official website of the National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences at `https://natboard.edu.in` or `https://nbe.edu.in`.
2.  **Navigate to NEET PG Section:** On the homepage, look for the 'NEET PG' section or a direct link for 'NEET PG 2026 Result' or 'Scorecard'.
3.  **Click on Result Link:** Click on the link that typically reads "Result of NEET-PG 2026" or "NEET PG 2026 Scorecard".
4.  **Login with Credentials:** You will be redirected to a login page. Enter your NEET PG 2026 Roll Number and your Date of Birth (DD/MM/YYYY) or other required credentials as prompted.
5.  **View and Download Scorecard:** Once logged in, your individual NEET PG 2026 scorecard will be displayed on the screen. Review all details carefully.
6.  **Print for Future Reference:** Download the scorecard in PDF format and take a printout. It is highly recommended to keep multiple copies for future counselling and admission procedures.

## What After NEET PG 2026 Result? Counselling Process

Upon the declaration of NEET PG 2026 results, the next critical step for all qualified candidates is the counselling process. This is where seats for MD/MS/PG Diploma courses are allocated based on merit and choices filled by candidates. The counselling is divided into two main categories:

*   **All India Quota (AIQ) Counselling (50% of seats):** This counselling is conducted by the Medical Counselling Committee (MCC) under the Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS), Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Government of India. It includes 50% of the total seats in Government Medical Colleges across India (excluding Jammu & Kashmir), all seats in Deemed Universities, Central Universities, Armed Forces Medical Services (AFMS) institutions, and some AIIMS/JIPMER/PGIMER seats. Registration for MCC counselling typically involves online registration, choice filling, choice locking, and seat allotment rounds.
    *   **Official MCC Website:** `https://mcc.nic.in`

*   **State Quota Counselling (50% of seats):** The remaining 50% of seats in Government Medical Colleges, along with all seats in private medical colleges within a state, are filled through counselling conducted by the respective State Counselling Authorities. Candidates must register separately with the specific state authorities they wish to apply to. Eligibility for State Quota often includes domicile requirements.

Candidates are strongly advised to keep all necessary documents ready for verification during counselling. These generally include the NEET PG 2026 scorecard, admit card, MBBS mark sheets, internship completion certificate, MBBS degree certificate, permanent/provisional registration certificate, identity proof, caste certificate (if applicable), and PwD certificate (if applicable).

## NEET PG 2026 Cut-off and Merit List

The final cut-off ranks for various specialties and colleges will be determined after the completion of all counselling rounds.

## FAQs (Frequently Asked Questions)

**Q1: When was NEET PG 2026 exam conducted?**
A1: The NEET PG 2026 examination was conducted on 2026-03-07 as a computer-based test (CBT) across various centers in India.

**Q2: How can I check my NEET PG 2026 result?**
A2: You can check your NEET PG 2026 result and download your scorecard from the official NBEMS website, `https://natboard.edu.in`. You will need your Roll Number and Date of Birth to log in.

**Q3: What details are mentioned on the NEET PG 2026 scorecard?**

A6: NBEMS does not entertain requests for re-evaluation or re-checking of NEET PG results. The results are final and binding.
