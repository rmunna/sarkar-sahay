Candidates who have applied for the KPSC FDA (First Division Assistant) and SDA (Second Division Assistant) posts can download their KPSC Admit Card 2026 from the official website, kpsc.kar.nic.in. The admit card, also known as the hall ticket, is a mandatory document for appearing in the examination. It is generally released 7-10 days before the official exam date, so candidates should regularly check the official portal for updates.

## How to Download KPSC FDA SDA 2026 Admit Card
The process for downloading the KPSC FDA SDA 2026 Admit Card is straightforward. Candidates should follow these steps to ensure a smooth download:
1.  **Visit the Official Website:** Navigate to the Karnataka Public Service Commission's official website, which is kpsc.kar.nic.in. This is the primary source for all official notifications and downloads related to the KPSC FDA SDA 2026 examination.
2.  **Locate the Admit Card Link:** On the homepage, look for a section dedicated to "Admit Card," "Hall Ticket," or "Latest Notifications." A direct link for "KPSC FDA SDA Admit Card 2026" or "KPSC Hall Ticket 2026" will be made available once the admit cards are released.
3.  **Click on the Download Link:** Once you find the relevant link, click on it. This will typically redirect you to a login page or a page where you need to enter your credentials.
4.  **Enter Login Credentials:** You will likely need to enter your application number, registration ID, date of birth, or other required details as specified on the page. Ensure that you enter the information accurately to avoid any issues.
5.  **View and Verify Admit Card:** After submitting your details, your KPSC FDA SDA 2026 Admit Card will be displayed on the screen. Carefully review all the information printed on it, including your name, photograph, signature, exam date, time, and venue.
6.  **Download and Print:** If all details are correct, proceed to download the admit card. It is highly recommended to take multiple printouts of the admit card. Keep one copy safe for future reference and carry another to the examination hall. The printout should be clear and legible.
7.  **Save a Digital Copy:** It is also advisable to save a digital copy (PDF) of your admit card on your device for backup purposes.
