## What to Do Next: Step-by-Step Application Guide

Candidates interested in applying for the SBI Engagement of Apprentices 2026-27 must follow the detailed steps below to complete their online registration successfully. Ensure you have all necessary documents and information ready before starting.

**Before You Begin:**

1.  **Scan Documents:**
    *   **Photograph:** Recent passport-size colour photograph (4.5cm × 3.5cm), file size 20KB – 50KB, in .jpeg format.
    *   **Signature:** Black ink on white paper, file size 10KB – 20KB, in .jpeg format. Ensure signature is not in capital letters.
    *   **Left Thumb Impression:** Black or blue ink on white paper, file size 20KB – 50KB, in .jpeg format.
    *   **Handwritten Declaration:** On white paper with black ink, file size 50KB – 100KB, in .jpeg format. The text for the declaration will be provided in the official notification.
2.  **Keep Details Ready:** Ensure you have your educational certificates, age proof, category certificate (if applicable), and a valid personal email ID and mobile number for communication.
3.  **Payment Method:** Have details of your Debit Card/Credit Card/Net Banking ready for online fee payment.

**Application Steps:**

1.  **Visit the Official Website:** Go to the SBI official website: [bank.sbi](https://bank.sbi).
2.  **Navigate to Careers:** On the homepage, look for the "Careers" or "Recruitment" section. This is usually found under "About Us" or in the footer/header menu.
3.  **Find the Notification:** Under the "Latest Announcements" or "Current Openings" section, locate the link for "Engagement of Apprentices 2026-27" and click on it.
4.  **New Registration:** Click on the "APPLY ONLINE" link. If you are a new user, select "Click here for New Registration."
5.  **Basic Information:**
    *   Enter your Name, Contact details, and Email ID.
    *   A Provisional Registration Number and Password will be generated and sent to your registered email ID and mobile number. Note these down carefully.
6.  **Upload Photo & Signature:**
    *   Log in using your Provisional Registration Number and Password.
    *   Upload your scanned photograph and signature as per the specified dimensions and file sizes.
    *   Click "NEXT."
7.  **Fill in Details:**
    *   Carefully fill in all required personal, educational, and communication details.
    *   Select the state/UT for which you wish to apply.
    *   Provide details of your graduation.
    *   Ensure all information is accurate and matches your official documents.
8.  **Preview and Verify:**
    *   Before final submission, a preview of your application form will be displayed.
    *   Thoroughly check all the entered details. Make any necessary corrections at this stage. No changes will be allowed after final submission.
9.  **Upload Left Thumb Impression & Handwritten Declaration:**
    *   Upload your scanned left thumb impression and handwritten declaration.
    *   Ensure they meet the specified file size and format requirements.
10. **Payment of Fees:**
    *   Proceed to the payment gateway.
    *   Select your preferred payment method (Debit Card/Credit Card/Net Banking).
    *   Pay the applicable application fee (₹300 for General/EWS/OBC; NIL for SC/ST/PwD).
    *   Upon successful payment, an e-receipt will be generated.
11. **Final Submission and Print:**
    *   After successful payment, click on "Submit."
    *   Take a printout of your e-receipt and the complete application form for your records. This is crucial for future reference.

**Important Reminders:**

*   Apply well in advance of the last date (2026-06-08) to avoid any last-minute technical glitches.
*   Ensure your email ID and mobile number remain active throughout the recruitment process, as all communications will be sent to them.
*   Keep copies of your application form and fee receipt safe.
