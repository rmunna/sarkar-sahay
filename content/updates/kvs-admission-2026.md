# KVS Admission 2026-27 — Online Registration, Eligibility, Important Dates

Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan (KVS) is set to begin the online admission process for the academic session **2026-27** in **March 2026**. Parents looking to enrol their children in Kendriya Vidyalaya schools — one of India's most trusted government school networks — can apply through the official KVS online admission portal. Admission to **Class 1** is conducted entirely online, and seats are allotted through a **computerized lottery system** with no entrance test or interview.

KVS operates over 1,200 schools across India under the Ministry of Education, primarily serving children of transferable central government employees and defence personnel. However, seats are also available to the general public through a structured priority category system.

## How to Apply for KVS Admission 2026-27 Online

Follow these steps to complete your child's KVS Class 1 registration:

1. **Visit the Official Portal** — Go to [kvsonlineadmission.kvs.gov.in](https://kvsonlineadmission.kvs.gov.in).
2. **New Registration** — Click on "New Registration" and provide the child's and parents' details. You will receive a unique Registration Number.
3. **Fill the Application Form** — Log in using your credentials. Select up to **3 preferred Kendriya Vidyalayas**.
4. **Upload Documents** — Upload scanned copies of all required documents.
5. **Review & Submit** — Preview the form for accuracy, then submit.
6. **Print Confirmation** — Save and print the submitted application and confirmation receipt for future reference.

## Documents Required

Keep scanned copies of the following documents ready before starting the application:

- **Birth Certificate** (proof of date of birth)
- **Residence/Address Proof** (Aadhaar card, utility bill, etc.)
- **Recent Passport-size Photograph** of the child
- **Category Certificate** (SC/ST/OBC/EWS, if applicable)
- **Service Certificate** of parent (for priority category — central/state government, defence, etc.)
- **Transfer Certificate** (if applicable)
- **Disability Certificate** (for CwSN category, if applicable)

## Selection Process — Lottery System

KVS does **not** conduct any written test or interview for Class 1 admission. The selection is based on:

1. **Priority Categories** — Admissions follow a priority order based on parent's service category (central government employees, armed forces, state government, PSU/autonomous bodies, general public).
2. **Computerized Lottery** — Within each priority category, a transparent computerized draw of lots determines selection.
3. **Provisional List** — Selected candidates are displayed on the school notice board and the KVS portal.
4. **Waiting List** — A waiting list is also published for vacancies that may arise.

The lottery is conducted by a committee at each Kendriya Vidyalaya in the presence of officials and observers to ensure fairness.

## FAQs

**Q1. Is there any entrance exam for KVS Class 1 admission?**
No. Admission to Class 1 in Kendriya Vidyalayas is based entirely on a computerized lottery system. No written test or interview is conducted.

**Q2. How many schools can I select in the KVS admission form?**
Parents can choose up to **3 preferred Kendriya Vidyalayas** while filling the online application form.

**Q3. What is the age limit for KVS Class 1 admission 2026-27?**
The child must be at least **6 years old** and less than **8 years old** as on 31 March 2026. Children with special needs (CwSN) get up to 2 years of age relaxation.

**Q4. Is there any application fee for KVS admission?**
No. The KVS online registration process for Class 1 is **completely free**. There is no application fee.

**Q5. When will the KVS admission lottery result be declared?**
The provisional selection list for Class 1 is expected to be declared in **late March 2026**. Check the official KVS portal for exact dates.
