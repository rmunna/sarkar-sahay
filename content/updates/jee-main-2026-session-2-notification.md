## What is JEE Main 2026 Session 2?

JEE Main (Joint Entrance Examination Main) is a national-level entrance exam conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA). It is the gateway for admission to B.E./B.Tech programs at NITs, IIITs, and other Centrally Funded Technical Institutions (CFTIs). The top 2,50,000 qualifiers also become eligible for JEE Advanced, which leads to IIT admission.

JEE Main 2026 is held in two sessions. Session 1 was conducted in January 2026 (January 21–30), and results are already out. **Session 2 registration is now open and closes on February 25, 2026.** Candidates can appear in one or both sessions — the best score is considered for ranking.

## Eligibility Criteria

- **Age:** No age limit for JEE Main 2026
- **Education:** Passed or appearing in Class 12 (or equivalent) with Physics, Mathematics, and one of Chemistry/Biology/Biotechnology/Technical Vocational Subject
- **Attempts:** Candidates can appear for 3 consecutive years from the year of passing Class 12
- **Nationality:** Indian nationals, OCI, PIO, and foreign nationals can apply

## How to Apply Online

1. Visit the official website at [jeemain.nta.nic.in](https://jeemain.nta.nic.in/)
2. Click on "New Registration" and fill in basic details
3. Note your registration number and set a password
4. Log in and complete the application form with academic details
5. Upload your photograph, signature, and required documents
6. Select exam centre preferences
7. Pay the application fee online
8. Submit the form and download the confirmation page

**Already registered for Session 1?** You can log in with your existing credentials and apply for Session 2 directly.

## Selection Process

1. **JEE Main CBT** — Computer-based test in online mode
2. **JEE Main Score/Rank** — Used for admission to NITs, IIITs, CFTIs through JoSAA counselling
3. **JEE Advanced Eligibility** — Top 2,50,000 qualifiers can appear for JEE Advanced (IIT admission)

## Syllabus Overview

JEE Main syllabus covers Class 11 and 12 topics from NCERT:

- **Physics:** Mechanics, Thermodynamics, Electrodynamics, Optics, Modern Physics
- **Chemistry:** Physical Chemistry, Organic Chemistry, Inorganic Chemistry
- **Mathematics:** Algebra, Calculus, Coordinate Geometry, Trigonometry, Statistics & Probability

## Preparation Tips

1. **Focus on NCERT first** — JEE Main questions are heavily based on NCERT concepts
2. **Solve previous year papers** — Practice Session 1 questions and past years from NTA's official website
3. **Use your Session 1 analysis** — If you appeared in Session 1, identify weak areas from your response sheet
4. **Mock tests weekly** — Take at least 2 full-length mock tests per week in April exam conditions
5. **Revise formulas daily** — Keep a formula sheet and revise for 30 minutes every day

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Can I appear for JEE Main Session 2 without appearing in Session 1?

Yes. You can appear for Session 1 only, Session 2 only, or both sessions. If you appear in both, the better score will be considered for ranking.

### Q: What is the last date to register for JEE Main 2026 Session 2?

The last date to register and pay the fee is February 25, 2026 (11:50 PM). No late applications are accepted.

### Q: When will JEE Main Session 2 exam be held?

JEE Main 2026 Session 2 exam will be conducted from April 2 to April 9, 2026 in CBT mode.

### Q: Is there any age limit for JEE Main 2026?

No. There is no age limit to appear for JEE Main. However, for JEE Advanced, there are separate eligibility criteria.

### Q: How many times can I attempt JEE Main?

You can attempt JEE Main for 3 consecutive years from the year of passing Class 12.
