## How to Check Maharashtra HSC Result 2026

Students can check their Maharashtra HSC Examination March 2026 results by following these simple steps:

1.  **Visit the Official Website:** Open your web browser and go to the official Maharashtra results portal: [mahresult.nic.in](https://mahresult.nic.in).
2.  **Locate Result Link:** On the homepage, look for the link that says "HSC Examination March 2026 Result" or similar. Click on this link.
3.  **Enter Credentials:** You will be redirected to a new page where you need to enter your examination details. Typically, this includes your Roll Number and your Mother's First Name. Ensure you enter these details exactly as they appear on your admit card.
4.  **Submit and View Result:** After entering the required information, click on the "View Result" or "Submit" button.
5.  **Download/Print Provisional Mark Sheet:** Your Maharashtra HSC March 2026 result will be displayed on the screen. It is advisable to download the provisional mark sheet and take a printout for future reference. This printout can be used for temporary purposes until the original mark sheet is issued by the board.

## Details Available on Maharashtra HSC Result 2026

The online provisional mark sheet for the Maharashtra HSC Examination March 2026 will typically display the following important details:

*   Student's Name
*   Roll Number / Seat Number
*   Mother's Name
*   Father's Name
*   School/College Name
*   Subject-wise Marks (Theory and Practical)
*   Total Marks Obtained
*   Maximum Marks
*   Grade
*   Percentage
*   Qualifying Status (Pass/Fail/ATKT)

Students should carefully verify all the details mentioned on their provisional mark sheet. In case of any discrepancy, they should contact their respective schools or the Maharashtra Board authorities immediately.
