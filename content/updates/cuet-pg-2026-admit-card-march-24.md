## CUET PG 2026 Admit Card Released — What You Need to Know

The **National Testing Agency (NTA)** has officially released the **CUET PG 2026 admit cards** for candidates appearing in exams scheduled on **March 24, 25, and 27, 2026**. Candidates can download their hall tickets from the official CUET PG portal at [cuet.nta.nic.in](https://cuet.nta.nic.in/).

The admit card is a mandatory document for entry to the examination centre. Candidates must download and print it immediately to avoid any last-minute issues.

## How to Download CUET PG 2026 Admit Card

Follow these steps to download your CUET PG 2026 hall ticket:

### Step 1: Visit the Official Portal

Go to the CUET PG official website at [cuet.nta.nic.in](https://cuet.nta.nic.in/).

### Step 2: Click on Admit Card Link

On the homepage, look for the **"Download Admit Card"** link or navigate to the admit card section.

### Step 3: Log In with Your Credentials

Enter your **Application Number** and **Date of Birth** (or password) to log in.

### Step 4: View Your Admit Card

Your CUET PG 2026 admit card will appear on the screen with your exam details, centre information, and photograph.

### Step 5: Download and Print

Click the **Download** button to save the admit card as a PDF. Take **at least two printouts** on A4 paper.

### Step 6: Verify All Details

Check your name, photograph, exam date, shift timing, and exam centre address carefully. If there are any discrepancies, contact NTA immediately.

## Details on the CUET PG 2026 Admit Card

Your admit card will contain the following information:

- **Candidate's name and photograph**
- **Application number and roll number**
- **Exam date and shift timing**
- **Exam centre name and address**
- **Subject/paper details**
- **Important instructions for exam day**

## Documents to Carry on Exam Day

Along with the printed admit card, candidates must carry:

- **Valid photo ID proof** (Aadhaar card, PAN card, Passport, Driving Licence, or Voter ID)
- **Passport-size photograph** (same as uploaded during registration)
- **PwD certificate** (if applicable)

## Important Instructions

- **Reach the exam centre at least 60 minutes** before the reporting time mentioned on the admit card.
- **Electronic devices** including mobile phones, smartwatches, and calculators are **not allowed** inside the exam hall.
- Wear **simple clothing** without large buttons, brooches, or accessories to avoid delays during frisking.
- The admit card must be **preserved until the admission process is complete** at the university.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What should I do if my CUET PG 2026 admit card has incorrect details?

Contact NTA immediately through their official helpline or email at cuet.pg@nta.ac.in. Do not skip the exam — carry the admit card along with supporting documents showing the correct information.

### Can I download the admit card from my mobile phone?

Yes, you can download the admit card from a mobile phone or any device with internet access. However, you must take a **clear printout on A4 paper** to carry to the exam centre.

### What if I forget my application number?

Visit [cuet.nta.nic.in](https://cuet.nta.nic.in/) and use the **"Forgot Application Number"** option. You can retrieve it using your registered email ID or mobile number.

### Is the admit card available for all exam dates?

NTA has released admit cards for exams on **March 24, 25, and 27, 2026**. Admit cards for other dates (if any) will be released separately as per the NTA schedule.

### Where can I find the official notice about the admit card release?

The official notice is available on the NTA website. You can [download the notice PDF here](https://nta.ac.in/Download/Notice/Notice_20260318205101.pdf).
