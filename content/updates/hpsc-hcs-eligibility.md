HPSC HCS (Haryana Civil Services) 2026 requires candidates to be **21–42 years old** with a **Bachelor's degree** from a recognised university. Age relaxation applies for reserved categories as per Haryana government rules. There is no domicile restriction for most HCS posts, but candidates must know Hindi/Sanskrit up to Matriculation level.

## Educational Qualification

A **Bachelor's degree in any stream** from a UGC-recognised university is the minimum qualification for all HCS and Allied Services posts. Final-year students may apply provisionally but must produce their degree certificate before joining.

For specific Allied Service posts (e.g., Tehsildar, BDO, DSP), the same graduate qualification applies — no stream restriction.

## Age Limit

| Category | Minimum Age | Maximum Age |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| General (UR) | 21 | 42 |
| SC / BC (Haryana domicile) | 21 | 47 (+5 years) |
| PwD (General) | 21 | 52 (+10 years) |
| Ex-Servicemen | 21 | Relaxation as per state rules |
| ESM (Haryana) | 21 | 47 (+5 years over max) |

*Age calculated as on the date specified in the official notification. Verify exact cutoff date in the 2026 notification.*

## Physical Standards (DSP Post Only)

The Deputy Superintendent of Police (DSP) post under HCS Allied Services has additional physical requirements:

| Standard | Male | Female |
|----------|------|--------|
| Height | 168 cm | 155 cm |
| Chest | 83–87 cm (unexpanded–expanded) | N/A |
| Vision | 6/6 (one eye), 6/9 (other) | Same |

Candidates with corrected vision are eligible for most posts. DSP has stricter norms; check the notification for exact standards.

## Language Requirement

Candidates must have **passed Hindi or Sanskrit** up to Matriculation level (10th standard) from a recognised board. This is mandatory for all HCS posts. Candidates from other states who haven't studied Hindi/Sanskrit at 10th should note this eligibility criterion carefully.

## Nationality

Must be an **Indian citizen**. Subjects of Nepal or Bhutan are eligible as per central government rules applicable to state services.

## Disqualification Criteria

A candidate is disqualified if they:
- Have more than one spouse living (exceptions via state government exemption)
- Have been convicted of an offence involving moral turpitude
- Have been dismissed from Government service
- Have a criminal record — any pending case must be declared in the application

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is there a Haryana domicile requirement for HCS 2026?**
No strict domicile requirement for appearing in HCS. However, the Hindi/Sanskrit at Matriculation language criterion effectively filters many non-Haryana candidates. Some reserved category benefits (SC/BC age relaxation, fee waiver) are available only to Haryana domicile candidates.

**Q: Can engineering or MBA graduates apply for HCS 2026?**
Yes. HCS requires any Bachelor's degree — B.Tech., MBA, B.Sc., B.A., B.Com. all qualify. The exam tests general studies and CSAT, not technical subjects.

**Q: What is the upper age limit for HCS 2026 for OBC candidates?**
Haryana uses BC (Backward Classes) instead of central OBC. BC candidates from Haryana get 5 years relaxation: upper age limit is 47 years. Candidates with central OBC certificates (non-Haryana) do not automatically get this benefit.
