## KCET 2026 Result — Expected in June 2026

The Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) is expected to declare the **KCET 2026 result in June 2026**. The Karnataka Common Entrance Test (KCET) 2026 was held on **April 16–17, 2026** across examination centres in Karnataka.

> **Note:** As of June 2, 2026, KEA has not officially declared the KCET 2026 result. This page will be updated immediately when results are announced. Check **kea.kar.nic.in** for official updates.

KCET is the gateway to undergraduate engineering, pharmacy, farm science, and Yoga & Naturopathy courses at Karnataka state colleges.

## How to Check KCET 2026 Result

When the result is declared:

1. Visit the official KEA website — **kea.kar.nic.in**
2. Click the **"KCET 2026 Result"** link on the homepage
3. Enter your **CET Number** (application number) and **Date of Birth**
4. Submit and view your score card
5. Download the score card for use in counselling

---

## KCET 2026 Score Card — Details Mentioned

Your score card will contain:
- Candidate name and CET number
- Marks obtained in each subject (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics or Biology)
- **KCET 2026 Rank** (overall and category-wise)
- Category (GM / OBC / SC / ST / others)

Candidates must score **minimum qualifying marks** to be eligible for counselling. Typically:

| Category | Minimum Qualifying % |

## After KCET 2026 Result — Counselling

KEA conducts multiple rounds of centralised counselling for:
- B.E. / B.Tech at government and private aided engineering colleges
- B.Pharm at pharmacy colleges
- B.Sc (Agriculture) and related streams at farm science institutions

Candidates ranked in KCET 2026 also need to appear for the **Horanadu / Gadinadu Kannadiga verification** and other eligibility processes during counselling.

*Complete counselling schedule will be published by KEA on kea.kar.nic.in after the result.*
