## RRB NTPC Graduate Level 2026 Exam Date Announced

The Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) have released the exam schedule for the RRB NTPC Graduate Level 2026 CBT 1 examination. As per the official notice released on **February 13, 2026**, the Computer-Based Test (CBT 1) will be conducted from **March 16 to March 27, 2026**.

## What is RRB NTPC?

The RRB NTPC (Non-Technical Popular Categories) recruitment is conducted by the Railway Recruitment Boards for various graduate-level posts in Indian Railways, including Commercial Apprentice, Station Master, Traffic Assistant, Goods Guard, and other non-technical positions.

## Exam Pattern — CBT 1

| Detail | Information |
|--------|-------------|
| Mode | Computer-Based Test (CBT) |
| Duration | 90 minutes |
| Total Questions | 100 |
| Total Marks | 100 |
| Negative Marking | 1/3 mark per wrong answer |

### Sections

- General Awareness — 40 questions
- Mathematics — 30 questions
- General Intelligence & Reasoning — 30 questions

## How to Download Admit Card

1. Visit the official RRB website for your region (e.g., rrbcdg.gov.in)
2. Click on "Admit Card / E-Call Letter" link
3. Enter your Registration Number and Date of Birth
4. Download and print the admit card
5. Verify all details including exam centre, date, and shift

## Eligibility Criteria

- **Educational Qualification:** Graduation from a recognized university
- **Age Limit:** 18 to 33 years (relaxation as per government rules for reserved categories)
- **Nationality:** Indian citizen

## Selection Process

1. CBT 1 (Computer-Based Test — Stage 1)
2. CBT 2 (Computer-Based Test — Stage 2)
3. Typing Skill Test / Computer-Based Aptitude Test (where applicable)
4. Document Verification
5. Medical Examination

## Preparation Tips

1. **Focus on General Awareness** — it carries the highest weightage (40 questions)
2. **Practice speed mathematics** — 30 questions in limited time requires quick calculation
3. **Take RRB-specific mock tests** to get familiar with the exam interface
4. **Revise current affairs** from the last 6-8 months thoroughly
5. **Manage time effectively** — allocate roughly 50-55 seconds per question
