## How to Check Your IBPS PO Prelims 2026 Result/Scorecard

Checking your IBPS PO Prelims result is a straightforward process. Follow these steps carefully to access your qualifying status and scorecard:

1.  **Visit the Official IBPS Website:** Open your web browser and go to `https://www.ibps.in`. Make sure you are on the legitimate official website to avoid phishing attempts.
2.  **Navigate to CRP PO/MT Section:** On the homepage, look for the 'CRP PO/MT' section or a direct link for 'Click Here to View Your Online Preliminary Examination Result for CRP-PO/MT-XVI'. This link is usually prominently displayed under the 'Latest Updates' or 'Results' section.
3.  **Select the Correct Recruitment:** Click on the link specifically for 'Common Recruitment Process for Probationary Officers/Management Trainees XVI'.
4.  **Enter Your Credentials:** You will be redirected to a login page. Here, you will need to enter the following details:
    *   **Registration Number / Roll Number:** This was generated during your application process and printed on your admit card.
    *   **Password / Date of Birth (DOB):** Use the password you set during registration or your Date of Birth in DD-MM-YYYY format.
5.  **Enter Captcha Code:** Complete the security captcha code as displayed on the screen.
6.  **Click 'Login':** After entering all details correctly, click on the 'Login' or 'Submit' button.
7.  **View Your Result:** Your IBPS PO Prelims 2026 result status will be displayed on the screen. You can see if you have qualified for the Mains examination. Your scorecard, showing sectional and overall marks, will also be available for download.
8.  **Download and Print:** It is highly recommended to download your scorecard and take a printout for your records. This document will be essential for future reference and potentially during the Mains exam and interview stages.

## Details Mentioned on Your Scorecard

Your IBPS PO Prelims 2026 scorecard contains several important pieces of information. Familiarize yourself with these details:

*   **Candidate's Name:** Your full name as provided during registration.
*   **Roll Number/Registration Number:** Unique identifiers for your application.
*   **Date of Examination:** The date on which you appeared for the Prelims exam.
*   **Category:** Your declared category (General, SC, ST, OBC, EWS, PwBD).
*   **Maximum Scores:** The maximum marks for each section and the total maximum marks.
*   **Obtained Scores:** The marks you secured in each of the three sections (English Language, Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning Ability) and your overall total score.
*   **Cut-off Scores:** The category-wise and sectional cut-off marks for the Prelims exam.
*   **Qualifying Status:** Whether you have qualified for the Main Examination (e.g., "Qualified for Mains" or "Not Qualified").

Carefully verify all the details mentioned on your scorecard. In case of any discrepancy, contact IBPS authorities immediately.

## Understanding the Cut-off Scores

The cut-off scores play a crucial role in the IBPS PO selection process. IBPS applies both sectional cut-offs and an overall cut-off for the Preliminary examination. To qualify for the Mains exam, a candidate must:

1.  Score equal to or above the minimum qualifying marks (sectional cut-off) in each of the three sections.
2.  Score equal to or above the overall minimum qualifying marks (overall cut-off) as decided by IBPS.

The cut-offs are determined based on several factors, including the number of vacancies, the difficulty level of the exam, and the total number of candidates who appeared. The released scorecard will clearly indicate the official cut-offs for each category.

## What's Next? For Qualified Candidates: Prepare for Mains!

Congratulations on clearing the Prelims! This is a significant achievement, but the journey doesn't end here. The next and most crucial stage is the IBPS PO Mains Examination. The Mains exam is more comprehensive and challenging, covering a broader range of subjects including Reasoning & Computer Aptitude, General/Economy/Banking Awareness, English Language, and Data Analysis & Interpretation, along with a Descriptive Paper.

Here’s what you should do next:

*   **Intensify Mains Preparation:** Immediately shift your focus to the Mains syllabus. Dedicate more time to practicing advanced questions, mock tests, and previous year's Mains papers.
*   **Focus on Descriptive Paper:** Don't neglect the Descriptive Paper (Letter Writing & Essay). Practice writing regularly to improve your speed, structure, and content.
*   **Stay Updated:** Keep an eye on the official IBPS website for the release of Mains admit cards and the exact exam schedule. We will also update citizennest.com with the latest information.
*   **Document Preparation:** Start gathering all necessary documents (educational certificates, caste certificates if applicable, identity proof) as they will be required during the interview stage and provisional allotment.
