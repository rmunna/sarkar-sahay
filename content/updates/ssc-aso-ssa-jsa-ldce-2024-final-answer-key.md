The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has released the **final answer key and marks** for three Limited Departmental Competitive Examinations (LDCE):

- **ASO (Assistant Section Officer) Grade LDCE 2022-2024** (DoPT)
- **SSA (Senior Secretariat Assistant) Grade LDCE 2024** (DoPT)
- **JSA (Junior Secretariat Assistant) Grade LDCE 2024** (DoPT)

Candidates who appeared in these examinations can now check their final answer keys and download their marks from the official SSC website.

## How to Check Your Final Answer Key and Marks

1. Visit the official SSC website at [ssc.gov.in](https://ssc.gov.in)
2. Navigate to the **"Answer Keys"** or **"Results"** section
3. Look for the relevant LDCE examination notice
4. Log in using your **Registration Number** and **Password**
5. View the final answer key and download your marks statement
6. Save or print the document for your records

You can also access the official notice directly: [SSC Notice (PDF)](https://ssc.gov.in/api/attachment/uploads/masterData/NoticeBoards/writeup_12032026.pdf)

## Important Information

- The **final answer key** is released after considering objections raised by candidates against the tentative answer key
- Marks are calculated based on the **final answer key** — this is the definitive version
- No further objections will be entertained against the final answer key
- Candidates should check their marks carefully and keep a copy for future reference

## FAQs

### Where can I check SSC ASO/SSA/JSA LDCE 2024 final answer key?
Visit [ssc.gov.in](https://ssc.gov.in) and navigate to the Answer Keys section, or check the notice board for the relevant PDF.

### Can I raise objections against the final answer key?
No. The final answer key is released after processing all objections. No further challenges are accepted.

### How are marks calculated?
Marks are calculated based on the final answer key. There is typically a negative marking of 0.25 marks for each wrong answer.

### When will the final result be declared?
The final result (merit list/cut-off) will be declared separately by SSC. Keep checking ssc.gov.in for updates.
