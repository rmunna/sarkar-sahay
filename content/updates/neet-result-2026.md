### How to Check Your NEET UG 2026 Result and Download Scorecard

Once declared, the NEET UG 2026 results will be accessible on the official NTA NEET website. Follow these step-by-step instructions to check and download your scorecard:

1.  **Visit the Official NEET Website:** Go to the official NTA NEET UG portal: `https://neet.nta.nic.in`.
2.  **Look for the Result Link:** On the homepage, locate the link that reads "NEET UG 2026 Result" or "NEET UG 2026 Scorecard Download." This link will become active immediately upon result declaration.
3.  **Enter Your Credentials:** Click on the result link. You will be redirected to a login page where you need to enter your:
    *   Application Number
    *   Date of Birth
    *   Security Pin (displayed on the screen)
4.  **Submit and View Result:** After entering the required details, click on the "Submit" or "Login" button. Your NEET UG 2026 scorecard will be displayed on the screen.
5.  **Download and Print:** Carefully review all the details on your scorecard. It is highly recommended to download the scorecard and take multiple printouts for future reference and the counseling process.

**Note:** Always ensure you are on the official NTA website to avoid any phishing attempts. Verify the URL (`neet.nta.nic.in` or `nta.ac.in`) before entering your personal information.

### What to Do After NEET UG 2026 Results

Once you have downloaded your NEET UG 2026 scorecard, the next critical phase is the counseling and admission process. Here's what you should do:

1.  **Check Qualifying Cutoff:** The NTA will also release the NEET UG 2026 qualifying cutoffs along with or shortly after the results. Candidates must meet these minimum percentile requirements to be eligible for counseling.
2.  **Understand All India Rank (AIR) and State Ranks:** Your AIR will determine your eligibility for the 15% All India Quota (AIQ) seats. For the remaining 85% state quota seats, individual states will release their own merit lists based on NEET UG scores and state-specific eligibility criteria.
3.  **Stay Updated on Counseling Schedules:** The Medical Counselling Committee (MCC) will conduct counseling for 15% AIQ seats, deemed universities, central universities, ESIC, and AFMC. State counseling authorities will conduct counseling for the 85% state quota seats. Keep a close watch on the respective official websites for counseling notifications, schedule, and procedures.
4.  **Prepare Documents:** Gather all necessary documents for counseling, including your NEET UG 2026 scorecard, admit card, mark sheets, ID proof, caste certificate (if applicable), domicile certificate, etc.
5.  **Research Colleges and Courses:** Based on your scores and expected rank, start researching colleges and courses to make informed choices during the counseling registration and choice filling process.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Q1: When is the NEET UG 2026 result expected?**
A1: The NEET UG 2026 result is highly anticipated and is expected to be declared very soon by the National Testing Agency (NTA). Candidates should regularly check the official website `neet.nta.nic.in` for the latest announcements.

**Q2: What credentials are required to check the NEET UG 2026 result?**
A2: To check your result, you will need your NEET UG 2026 Application Number, Date of Birth, and the Security Pin displayed on the login screen.

**Q3: Can I check my NEET UG 2026 result without my Application Number?**
A3: No, the Application Number is mandatory to access your result and scorecard. Ensure you keep your NEET UG 2026 admit card handy as it contains this crucial detail.

**Q4: What if there is an error in my NEET UG 2026 scorecard?**
A4: If you find any discrepancies in your scorecard (e.g., name, category, marks), immediately contact the NTA Helpdesk through the official channels provided on their website. Do not delay in reporting errors.

**Q5: What is the difference between All India Rank (AIR) and Category Rank?**
A5: All India Rank (AIR) is your overall rank among all candidates who appeared for NEET UG. Category Rank is your rank within your specific reservation category (e.g., SC, ST, OBC, EWS).

**Q6: What happens after the NEET UG 2026 results are declared?**
A6: After the results, the counseling process for admission to medical and dental colleges will begin. This involves registration for counseling, choice filling, seat allotment, and document verification. Stay updated on MCC and respective state counseling websites for detailed schedules.
