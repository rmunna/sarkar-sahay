# Sainik School Result 2026 – AISSEE Result, Cut-Off & Merit List

The **AISSEE 2026 (All India Sainik School Entrance Exam) result** has been released by the National Testing Agency (NTA). Students who appeared for the entrance exam for Class 6 and Class 9 admission can now check their results and download scorecards from the official website.

## How to Check AISSEE 2026 Result

The result is available on the official NTA AISSEE portal. Follow these steps:

### Step-by-Step Process

1. Visit the official website — [aissee.nta.nic.in](https://aissee.nta.nic.in)
2. Click on **"AISSEE 2026 Result"** link on the homepage
3. Enter your **Application Number** and **Date of Birth**
4. Enter the security captcha and click **Submit**
5. Your result with marks and qualifying status will be displayed
6. Click **Download Scorecard** to save a PDF copy

> **Tip:** Keep your AISSEE 2026 admit card handy — you'll need the application number printed on it.

### Alternative Portal

If the NTA website is slow due to heavy traffic, you can also check at [sainikschool.ncog.gov.in](https://sainikschool.ncog.gov.in).

## AISSEE 2026 Result Details

The scorecard contains the following information:

- **Candidate name and roll number**
- **Marks obtained** in the entrance exam
- **Qualifying status** (Qualified / Not Qualified)
- **Category-wise rank** (if applicable)
- **Overall percentile score**

## Merit List & School Allotment

After the result is declared:

- **School-wise merit lists** are prepared based on marks, category, and preference
- Selected candidates receive an **allotment letter** via email and on the portal
- A **waiting list** is also published for candidates who may get admission if seats remain vacant

## Admission Process After Result

If your child has qualified in AISSEE 2026, follow these steps for admission:

1. **Download the allotment letter** from the portal
2. **Report to the allotted Sainik School** on the specified date
3. Carry all **original documents** — birth certificate, Aadhaar, category certificate, mark sheets
4. **Medical fitness test** will be conducted at the school
5. **Pay the admission fee** as mentioned in the allotment letter
6. Complete the **document verification** process

### Documents Required

- AISSEE 2026 scorecard and allotment letter
- Birth certificate (original + photocopy)
- Aadhaar card of student and parent
- Category/EWS certificate (if applicable)
- Passport-size photographs (4–6 copies)
- Transfer certificate from previous school

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Where can I check AISSEE 2026 result?**
Visit [aissee.nta.nic.in](https://aissee.nta.nic.in) and enter your application number and date of birth.

**Q: What if I lost my AISSEE admit card?**
You can recover your application number using the "Forgot Application Number" option on the NTA portal using your registered email or mobile number.

**Q: Is there a re-evaluation process for AISSEE?**
NTA does not offer re-evaluation. However, you can apply for re-checking of marks through the official portal within the specified window.

**Q: Can I change my Sainik School preference after result?**
School preference changes are generally not allowed after allotment. However, check the official notice for any special provisions.

**Q: What happens if I'm on the waiting list?**
Waiting list candidates are called for admission as and when seats become vacant. Keep checking the portal and your registered email for updates.
