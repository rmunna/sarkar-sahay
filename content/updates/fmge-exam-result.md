## FMGE June 2026 Result Declared: Check Your Qualifying Status Now

The National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS) has officially declared the result for the Foreign Medical Graduate Examination (FMGE) June 2026 session. As of November 9, 2026, candidates who appeared for the examination can now check their qualifying status and individual scorecards. This highly anticipated result is crucial for Indian citizens and Overseas Citizens of India (OCIs) who hold primary medical qualifications awarded by medical institutions outside India and wish to practice medicine in India.

FMGE is a mandatory screening test conducted by NBEMS as per the Screening Test Regulations, 2002, to determine the eligibility of foreign medical graduates to register with the National Medical Commission (NMC) or any State Medical Council (SMC).

## How to Check FMGE June 2026 Result

Follow these simple steps to access your FMGE June 2026 result:

1.  **Visit the Official NBEMS Website:** Open your web browser and go to the official website of the National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences: [https://natboard.edu.in/](https://natboard.edu.in/) or [https://nbems.edu.in/](https://nbems.edu.in/). Both URLs typically lead to the NBEMS portal.
2.  **Navigate to the Results Section:** On the homepage, look for the 'Results' tab or 'Public Notice' section. You may also find a dedicated 'FMGE' section under 'Examinations'.
3.  **Locate the Result Link:** Click on the link titled "Result of Foreign Medical Graduate Examination (FMGE) June 2026 Session" or similar wording. The result is typically released as a PDF document initially, containing the Roll Numbers of qualified candidates, marks obtained, and qualifying status.
4.  **Download the Result PDF:** Once the PDF opens, download it to your device. You can then use the 'Ctrl+F' (for Windows) or 'Cmd+F' (for Mac) function to search for your Roll Number.
5.  **Access Individual Scorecard (If Available):** NBEMS usually releases individual scorecards a few days after the initial result PDF. If prompted, you may need to log in to your applicant portal using your User ID and Password to view and download your detailed scorecard.
6.  **Print/Save for Future Reference:** It is highly recommended to download and print a copy of your result and scorecard for your records and for future admission/registration processes.

## Important Details on Your FMGE Scorecard

Your FMGE June 2026 scorecard will contain vital information, including:

*   **Candidate's Name and Roll Number:** Your unique identification for the examination.
*   **Application ID:** Your specific application number.
*   **Date of Birth:** As provided during registration.
*   **Marks Obtained:** Your scores in various sections (if applicable) and total marks out of 300.
*   **Qualifying Status:** Clearly stating 'PASS' or 'FAIL'.
*   **Remarks (if any):** Any specific notes regarding your eligibility or disqualification.

Candidates must ensure all details on their scorecard are accurate. In case of any discrepancy, contact NBEMS immediately through their official communication channels.

## FMGE June 2026 Cutoff Marks

To qualify for the FMGE, candidates must secure a minimum of 150 marks out of a total of 300 marks. This is the sole qualifying criterion; there are no separate sectional cutoffs. The exam does not have negative marking, so every correct answer contributes positively to your score.

## What to Do After Checking Your FMGE June 2026 Result

### For Qualified Candidates:

Congratulations on passing the FMGE! Your next steps are crucial for your medical career in India:

1.  **FMGE Pass Certificate:** NBEMS will issue FMGE Pass Certificates to qualified candidates. The schedule for in-person collection of these certificates from the NBEMS office in New Delhi will be announced shortly on the official website. Ensure you carry all required documents, including original identity proof, primary medical qualification certificates, and any other documents specified by NBEMS.
2.  **Internship and Provisional Registration:** After obtaining the FMGE Pass Certificate, you must apply to the National Medical Commission (NMC) or the respective State Medical Council (SMC) for provisional registration to undertake a compulsory rotating medical internship (CRMI) of one year. The internship must be done in an NMC/MCI recognized institution in India.
3.  **Permanent Registration:** Upon successful completion of the internship, you can apply for permanent registration with the NMC/SMC, which will allow you to practice medicine independently in India.

### For Non-Qualified Candidates:

If you did not clear the FMGE June 2026 session, do not be disheartened. This examination is known for its challenging nature, and many aspirants require multiple attempts. Here are your options:

1.  **Analyze Your Performance:** Review your scorecard to understand your strengths and weaknesses. Identify subjects or topics where you need to improve your conceptual understanding and practice.
2.  **Re-attempt the Examination:** The FMGE is conducted twice a year. You can apply for the upcoming FMGE December 2026 session. Keep a close eye on the NBEMS website for the detailed notification and application schedule, which is expected to be released in the coming weeks.
3.  **Seek Guidance:** Consider joining coaching programs, online platforms, or study groups to enhance your preparation and develop effective exam strategies for the next attempt.
