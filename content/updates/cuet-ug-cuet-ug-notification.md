## What is CUET (UG) 2026?

The Common University Entrance Test (CUET) (UG) 2026 is a national-level entrance examination conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) for admission to various undergraduate programs offered by Central Universities, State Universities, Deemed Universities, and Private Universities across India. This examination serves as a single-window opportunity for students seeking admission to a wide array of courses in these participating institutions.

The CUET (UG) aims to provide a common platform and equal opportunities to candidates nationwide, streamlining the admission process and reducing the burden of appearing in multiple entrance examinations. It assesses candidates' understanding of subjects at the Class 12 level, enabling universities to select eligible students based on a standardized test. For aspiring undergraduate students in India, the CUET (UG) 2026 is a crucial gateway to higher education, offering access to diverse academic programs and institutions across the country.

## Application Fee

The application fee for CUET (UG) 2026, as per the official notification, varies based on the candidate's category and the number of subjects chosen. The fee structure is as follows:

## Selection Process

The selection process for admission to undergraduate programs through CUET (UG) 2026 involves the following stages:

1.  **Common University Entrance Test (CUET) (UG) 2026:** Candidates must appear for and qualify in the CUET (UG) 2026 examination. The test will assess candidates' knowledge in various subjects based on the Class 12 syllabus.
2.  **Merit List Prepared by Participating Universities/Institutes/Organizations:** Based on the scores obtained in the CUET (UG) 2026, individual participating universities, institutes, or organizations will prepare their respective merit lists for admission to their undergraduate programs. The admission criteria, including minimum CUET scores, subject combinations, and any additional university-specific requirements, will be determined by each university. Candidates will then apply to their desired universities using their CUET (UG) scores, and admissions will be finalized based on the university's merit list and counseling process.

## How to Apply Online

Candidates interested in applying for CUET (UG) 2026 must follow the steps below to complete their online application:

1.  **Visit the Official Website:** Go to the official CUET (UG) website: cuet.nta.nic.in.
2.  **Locate the Application Link:** On the homepage, look for the "CUET (UG) 2026 Registration" or "Apply Online" link. Click on it to proceed.
3.  **New Registration:** If you are a first-time applicant, click on "New Registration" or "Register Here." Provide the required basic details such as your name, parent's name, date of birth, email ID, and mobile number to create your login credentials (Application Number and Password).
4.  **Login:** After successful registration, log in to the applicant portal using the generated Application Number and Password.
5.  **Fill the Application Form:** Carefully fill out the online application form with all the necessary personal, academic, contact, and other relevant details. Ensure all information is accurate as per your official documents.
6.  **Select Subjects:** Choose the subjects you wish to appear for in the CUET (UG) 2026 examination, keeping in mind the requirements of the universities and programs you are interested in.
7.  **Upload Documents:** Upload scanned copies of your recent passport-sized photograph and signature in the specified format and size. Ensure the images are clear and meet the prescribed specifications.
8.  **Pay Application Fee:** Proceed to pay the application fee online using the available payment gateways (Debit Card, Credit Card, Net Banking, UPI). The fee amount will depend on your category and the number of subjects chosen.
9.  **Review and Submit:** Before final submission, carefully review all the details entered in the application form. Make any necessary corrections. Once satisfied, click on the "Submit" button.
10. **Print Confirmation Page:** After successful submission and fee payment, a confirmation page will be generated. Download and print this confirmation page for your records and future reference.
