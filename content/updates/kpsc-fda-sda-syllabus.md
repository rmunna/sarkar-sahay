The KPSC FDA SDA exam syllabus differs by post: **FDA (First Division Assistant)** has 3 papers totalling 400 marks, while **SDA (Second Division Assistant)** has 2 papers totalling 250 marks. Both are OMR-based offline tests with no negative marking, conducted in Kannada and English medium.

## Exam Pattern at a Glance

### FDA Exam Pattern (400 Marks)

| Paper | Subject | Marks | Duration |
|-------|---------|-------|----------|
| Paper 1 | General Kannada | 150 | 3 hours |
| Paper 2 | General English | 150 | 3 hours |
| Paper 3 | General Knowledge | 100 | 2 hours |
| **Total** | | **400** | |

### SDA Exam Pattern (250 Marks)

| Paper | Subject | Marks | Duration |
|-------|---------|-------|----------|
| Paper 1 | General Kannada & English | 150 | 3 hours |
| Paper 2 | General Knowledge & Arithmetic | 100 | 2 hours |
| **Total** | | **250** | |

**Key rules:** Multiple-choice questions (MCQ), OMR answer sheet, no negative marking, bilingual question papers (Kannada + English).

## General Kannada Syllabus (150 Marks — FDA Paper 1; part of SDA Paper 1)

Kannada is the highest-weighted section and the one most candidates underestimate. Preparation should focus on grammar rules first, then literature.

**Grammar (ವ್ಯಾಕರಣ) — approx. 40–50 marks**
- Sandhi (ಸಂಧಿ): Svara sandhi, Vyanjana sandhi, Visarga sandhi — rules with examples
- Samaasa (ಸಮಾಸ): Tatpurusha, Dvandva, Bahuvrihi, Karmadharaya, Avyayibhava
- Vibhakti (ವಿಭಕ್ತಿ): All 8 cases with Kannada suffixes
- Kriya (ಕ್ರಿಯಾ): Verb forms, tenses, causative, passive voice in Kannada
- Alankara (ಅಲಂಕಾರ): Upama, Rupaka, Utpreksha, Anuprasa, Yamaka

**Kannada Literature — approx. 30–40 marks**
- Ancient Kannada (ಹಳಗನ್ನಡ): Pampa, Ponna, Ranna (Three Gems), Janna, Raghavanka
- Medieval Kannada (ನಡುಗನ್ನಡ): Vachana literature — Basavanna, Akkamahadevi, Allama Prabhu
- Modern Kannada (ಹೊಸಗನ್ನಡ): Kuvempu, D.R. Bendre, Masti, Shivaram Karanth
- Dalit and women's writing in Kannada

**Comprehension & Vocabulary — approx. 30–35 marks**
- Reading comprehension (unseen passage)
- Synonyms (ಸಮಾನಾರ್ಥ ಪದ), Antonyms (ವಿರುದ್ಧಾರ್ಥ ಪದ), one-word substitution
- Proverbs (ಗಾದೆ ಮಾತು), translation (short sentences)

**Essay / Letter Writing — approx. 25–30 marks** *(FDA only)*
- Essays on Karnataka topics: rivers, dams, personalities, government schemes
- Formal and informal letter writing in Kannada

## General English Syllabus (150 Marks — FDA Paper 2)

*SDA candidates have a combined Kannada & English paper; the English portion follows the same topics at proportional weight.*

**Grammar — approx. 50–60 marks**
- Tenses: Simple, Continuous, Perfect (all three times) — usage and error correction
- Articles (a/an/the) — rules and exceptions
- Prepositions: in, on, at, by, for, since, until
- Voice: Active ↔ Passive conversion (all tenses)
- Narration: Direct ↔ Indirect speech (all sentence types)
- Conjunctions, Modals (can, could, may, might, should, would, must)
- Conditional sentences (Types 0, 1, 2, 3)

**Vocabulary — approx. 30–40 marks**
- Synonyms, Antonyms
- One-word substitution
- Idioms and phrases
- Spelling correction

**Reading & Writing — approx. 30–40 marks**
- Comprehension passage (unseen)
- Sentence rearrangement
- Letter / paragraph writing *(FDA)*

## General Knowledge Syllabus (100 Marks — FDA Paper 3 / SDA Paper 2)

**History — approx. 20–25 marks**
- Indian history: Ancient, Medieval, Freedom Movement
- Karnataka history: Vijayanagara Empire, Hoysalas, Chalukyas, Wodeyars
- Karnataka Unification Movement (Ekikarana), important personalities

**Geography — approx. 15–20 marks**
- Indian geography: rivers, mountains, climate zones
- Karnataka geography: districts, rivers (Cauvery, Krishna, Tungabhadra), dams (KRS, Narayana Pura), national parks

**Indian Polity & Constitution — approx. 15–20 marks**
- Fundamental Rights, Directive Principles, Duties
- Parliament, State Legislature, Judiciary
- Karnataka Panchayat Raj / local self-government

**Economy — approx. 10–15 marks**
- Indian economy basics; Union and Karnataka state budgets
- Five-Year Plans / NITI Aayog initiatives relevant to Karnataka
- Agriculture, major industries in Karnataka

**Science & Technology — approx. 10–15 marks**
- Basic general science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
- Space, defence, health — national milestones
- IT and startup ecosystem in Karnataka (KIADB, electronics city)

**Current Affairs — approx. 15–20 marks**
- National and Karnataka-specific events (last 12 months)
- Important awards, appointments, sports results
- Government schemes: central + Karnataka state schemes

## Arithmetic Syllabus (SDA Paper 2 — combined with GK, 100 marks total)

*Arithmetic is tested only in SDA. FDA does not have an Arithmetic paper.*

- Number system, HCF & LCM
- Percentage; Ratio & Proportion
- Average, Profit & Loss
- Simple Interest & Compound Interest
- Time & Work; Pipes & Cisterns
- Time, Speed & Distance
- Mensuration (area, perimeter, volume of basic shapes)
- Data Interpretation (basic tables and charts)

## Preparation Strategy

1. **Kannada first:** It has the highest marks and is often the differentiator. Use old KPSC question papers for grammar pattern practice.
2. **Current Affairs — Karnataka-focus:** KPSC questions strongly favour Karnataka-specific GK over national GK. Read Udayavani, Vijayavani, or The Hindu Karnataka edition.
3. **No negative marking = attempt all:** Unlike central government exams, KPSC FDA/SDA has no penalty for wrong answers. Attempt every question.
4. **Past papers are the best syllabus proxy:** KPSC repeats question patterns. Solve at least 5 previous year papers for each paper.

For the official syllabus PDF, check [kpsc.kar.nic.in](https://kpsc.kar.nic.in) under the relevant notification download.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How many papers are there in the KPSC FDA exam?**
FDA has 3 papers: General Kannada (150 marks), General English (150 marks), and General Knowledge (100 marks) — 400 marks total. All are OMR-based objective tests.

**Q: Is there negative marking in KPSC SDA/FDA?**
No. There is no negative marking. Attempt all questions.

**Q: What is the syllabus for KPSC SDA?**
SDA has 2 papers: Paper 1 — General Kannada & English (150 marks); Paper 2 — General Knowledge & Arithmetic (100 marks). Total 250 marks. The Kannada portion has higher weight than English in Paper 1.

**Q: Is Kannada compulsory for KPSC FDA SDA?**
Yes. Knowledge of Kannada is mandatory for both FDA and SDA posts. The exam is conducted in Kannada and English medium, and Kannada is a full paper (150 marks) for FDA.
