CISF Head Constable Ministerial (HCM) Computer Based Test (CBT) date is announced on [cisfrectt.cisf.gov.in](https://cisfrectt.cisf.gov.in). The admit card is released **2–3 weeks before the exam**. The written test is followed by Physical Standard Test (PST), Physical Efficiency Test (PET), Medical Examination, and Document Verification.

## CISF HCM 2026 — Exam Schedule

| Stage | Date |
|-------|------|
| Official Notification | cisfrectt.cisf.gov.in |
| Application Window | As per notification |
| Admit Card Release | 2–3 weeks before Written Exam |
| Written Exam (CBT) | As per official schedule |
| PST / PET | After Written Exam result |
| Medical Examination | After PST/PET |
| Final Result | After all stages |

*Check [cisfrectt.cisf.gov.in](https://cisfrectt.cisf.gov.in) for the 2026 exam date announcement.*

## How to Download Admit Card

1. Visit [cisfrectt.cisf.gov.in](https://cisfrectt.cisf.gov.in)
2. Click on the **Admit Card** link for Head Constable Ministerial 2026
3. Login with your **Registration Number and Date of Birth**
4. Download and print the admit card
5. Carry it to the exam centre along with a government-issued photo ID

## Exam Centres

CISF conducts the CBT at centres across major cities in all states. Candidates select their preferred exam city during the application. Allotted centre is shown on the admit card — centre change requests are typically not entertained after admit card generation.

## What to Carry to the Exam

- Printed admit card (A4 size)
- Original photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, Passport, or Driving Licence)
- Blue/black ballpoint pen (for any rough work; CBT is computer-based)
- **Do not carry:** Mobile phone, calculator, Bluetooth devices, smart watches

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How many days before the CISF HCM exam is the admit card released?**
Typically 10–21 days before the exam date. Once the admit card link is active on cisfrectt.cisf.gov.in, download it immediately — the link may be disabled a few days before the exam.

**Q: What happens if I lose or misplace my CISF HCM admit card?**
Log in to the official CISF recruitment portal (cisfrectt.cisf.gov.in) and re-download using your registration number and date of birth. Always keep a backup PDF copy.

**Q: Is the CISF Head Constable exam conducted in multiple shifts?**
Yes. Due to the large number of applicants, CBT may be held in multiple shifts over multiple days. Your specific date and shift are mentioned on your admit card.
