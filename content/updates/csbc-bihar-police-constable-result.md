## What Happens Next — After the Result

Candidates who have successfully qualified the written examination for the CSBC Bihar Police Constable 2025 recruitment should now focus on preparing for the next crucial stages of the selection process. As per the official selection process, the stages following the written exam are:

1.  **Physical Efficiency Test (PET) / Physical Standard Test (PST):** Qualified candidates will be called for the PET/PST. This stage assesses candidates' physical fitness and endurance through various tasks such as running, high jump, long jump, and shot put. Physical standards like height, chest (for males), and weight will also be measured. The dates for the PET/PST, along with the admit cards and detailed instructions, will be announced separately on the official CSBC website. Candidates are strongly advised to start preparing physically without delay.
2.  **Document Verification (DV):** Candidates who clear the PET/PST will proceed to the Document Verification stage. Here, all original documents submitted during the application process, such as educational certificates, caste certificates, domicile certificates, identity proof, etc., will be thoroughly checked against the information provided in the application form. Any discrepancy can lead to disqualification.
3.  **Medical Examination:** The final stage involves a comprehensive medical examination to ensure that candidates meet the prescribed medical standards for the Bihar Police Constable post. This includes checks for vision, hearing, general physical fitness, and any pre-existing medical conditions.
