## Eligibility Summary

- **Age:** 18–25 years (General). Relaxation: OBC +3 years, SC/ST +5 years
- **Education:** 12th pass (Intermediate) from a recognised board
- **Computer Literacy:** Basic computer knowledge required for Ministerial posts
- **Physical Standards:** Height and chest requirements apply (see official notification)

## Application Process

1. Visit [cisfrectt.cisf.gov.in](https://cisfrectt.cisf.gov.in)
2. Register with email and mobile number
3. Fill the online application form with personal, educational, and category details
4. Upload photo and signature as per specifications
5. Pay application fee via online banking/UPI
6. Submit and note application reference number

**Application Fee:**

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is CISF Head Constable Ministerial (HCM) post?**
HCM is an administrative/clerical post within CISF units. Unlike Constable GD (field duty), HCM handles office work — record keeping, correspondence, dispatch, and administrative functions at CISF establishments across India.

**Q: Is CISF Head Constable a central government job?**
Yes. CISF is a Central Armed Police Force (CAPF) under the Ministry of Home Affairs. Head Constable is a central government post with 7th Pay Commission benefits, allowances, and government accommodation.

**Q: Can female candidates apply for CISF Head Constable 2026?**
Yes. Female candidates can apply for HCM posts. Physical standards differ for female candidates. Application fee is waived for women candidates.
