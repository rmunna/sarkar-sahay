## How to Download SSC Phase 13 Final Answer Key

Follow these steps to download the final answer keys:

1. Visit the official SSC website at [ssc.gov.in](https://ssc.gov.in)
2. Go to the **Notice Board** or **Latest News** section on the homepage
3. Look for the notice titled "Phase-XIII/2025/Selection Posts Examination - Uploading of Final Answer Keys along with Question Paper cum Response Sheets"
4. Click on the PDF link to open the official notice
5. Download the final answer keys for your respective post and paper
6. Cross-check your answers with the final answer key
7. Save or print the document for your records

## Important Details

- **Final answer keys** are the definitive version — these are used for result preparation
- Unlike tentative/provisional answer keys, **no objections can be raised** against final answer keys
- Candidates should compare their responses with the final answer keys to estimate their scores
- The result based on these final answer keys will be declared separately by SSC

## What's Next?

After the release of final answer keys, SSC will proceed with:
- **Score calculation** based on final answer keys
- **Result declaration** with category-wise cut-off marks
- **Document verification** for shortlisted candidates

Keep checking the official SSC website for result updates.

## FAQs

### Can I raise objections against the final answer key?
No. Objections can only be raised against tentative/provisional answer keys. Final answer keys are conclusive and used for result preparation.

### When will the SSC Selection Posts Phase 13 result be declared?
SSC has not announced a specific date for the result. It is expected within a few weeks after the final answer key release. Check ssc.gov.in regularly.

### How is the score calculated?
SSC uses the final answer keys to calculate marks. Correct answers receive positive marks, and there is negative marking (as per exam rules) for incorrect answers.

### Where can I download my response sheet?
Response sheets are available along with the final answer keys on the SSC website. Log in with your registration details to access your individual response sheet.
