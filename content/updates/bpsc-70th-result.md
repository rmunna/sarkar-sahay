BPSC 70th CCE (Combined Competitive Examination) final result 2026 is **expected in June 3rd week** — interviews concluded February 2026 and the result is overdue. The exam fills **2,035 posts** including SDM (Sub-Divisional Magistrate), DSP (Deputy Superintendent of Police), BDO (Block Development Officer), and other gazetted posts in Bihar. Check [bpsc.bihar.gov.in](https://bpsc.bihar.gov.in) for the official announcement.

## How to Check BPSC 70th Final Result

1. Visit [bpsc.bihar.gov.in](https://bpsc.bihar.gov.in)
2. Click **"What's New"** or **"Results"** on the homepage
3. Find **"BPSC 70th CCE Final Result 2026"**
4. Download the PDF merit list
5. Search your roll number using Ctrl+F

Individual marks and scorecard will be available via the candidate portal login after the merit list release.

## What Happens After Result

1. **Merit list published** → Roll numbers of selected candidates
2. **Document verification** → Original certificates at BPSC office or designated venue
3. **Police verification** → Background check for all selected candidates
4. **Posting order** → Bihar government issues posting orders to departments
5. **Joining** → Candidates report to their posting district/department

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Why is BPSC 70th result delayed?**
The 70th CCE interviews concluded in February 2026. Administrative processes including final verification, processing of reserved category lists, and result compilation have caused the delay. The result is expected June 3rd week 2026 based on BPSC's tentative schedule.

**Q: How many total candidates appeared in BPSC 70th?**
Approximately 4–5 lakh candidates appeared in the Prelims. Around 20,000–25,000 were shortlisted for Mains. Final interview candidates numbered approximately 10,000–12,000 for 2,035 posts.

**Q: If I'm selected in BPSC 70th, can I also appear in BPSC 71st?**
Yes. Selection in one BPSC cycle does not bar you from appearing in a new cycle. Many candidates continue appearing even after selection to get a preferred post or department.
