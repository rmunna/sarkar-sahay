## What Happened

The Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission (UPPSC) has released **Interview Letters** for candidates who qualified for the interview round of the **Combined State/Upper Subordinate Services Examination 2024**. Candidates can download their interview letters from the official UPPSC website until **20 March 2026**.

## How to Download UPPSC Interview Letter

Follow these steps to download your interview letter:

1. Visit the official UPPSC website at [uppsc.up.nic.in](https://uppsc.up.nic.in/)
2. Look for the **"Combined State / Upper Subordinate Services Exam-2024"** interview letter link on the homepage or notifications section
3. Click on the interview letter download link
4. Enter your **Registration Number** and **Date of Birth** (or other required credentials)
5. Click **Submit** to view your interview letter
6. Download and **take a printout** of the interview letter for the interview day

## Important Instructions for Candidates

- **Carry the printed interview letter** to the interview venue along with original identity proof
- Bring **all original documents** including educational certificates, caste certificate (if applicable), and experience certificates
- Verify your **interview date, time, and venue** mentioned on the letter
- Report at the venue **well before the scheduled time**
- The interview letter is available for download only until **20 March 2026** — download it immediately

## Documents to Carry for Interview

- Printed Interview Letter
- Photo ID proof (Aadhaar Card / Voter ID / Passport)
- All original educational certificates and mark sheets
- Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS) if applicable
- Domicile certificate
- Recent passport-size photographs

## FAQs

### How can I download my UPPSC Interview Letter?

Visit [uppsc.up.nic.in](https://uppsc.up.nic.in/), find the Combined State/Upper Subordinate Services Exam 2024 interview letter link, enter your registration number and date of birth, and download the letter.

### What documents should I bring to the UPPSC interview?

Carry your printed interview letter, photo ID proof, all original educational certificates, category certificate (if applicable), domicile certificate, and recent passport-size photographs.
