## KCET 2026 Result Declared: Your Gateway to Engineering & Medical Courses!

The Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) has officially announced the results for the Karnataka Common Entrance Test (KCET) 2026 today, June 7, 2026. This eagerly awaited declaration marks a crucial step for lakhs of students aspiring for admission to various undergraduate professional courses, including Engineering, Pharmacy, Agriculture, and Veterinary Sciences, across colleges in Karnataka.

Candidates who appeared for the KCET 2026 examination can now access their individual results, including subject-wise marks and overall rank, directly from the official KEA website. It is highly recommended to check your results immediately to prepare for the upcoming counselling process.

## How to Check Your KCET 2026 Result

Checking your KCET 2026 result is a straightforward process. Follow these step-by-step instructions to download your scorecard:

1.  **Visit the Official KEA Website:** Open your web browser and navigate to either `https://cetonline.karnataka.gov.in/kea/` or `https://kea.kar.nic.in/`.
2.  **Locate the Result Link:** On the homepage, look for the 'KCET 2026 Result' or 'CET 2026 Results' link. This link is usually prominently displayed under the 'Latest Announcements' or 'Results' section.
3.  **Click on the Link:** Click on the designated KCET 2026 Result link.
4.  **Enter Your Credentials:** You will be redirected to a login page. Here, you will need to enter your:
    *   **KCET Registration Number/Application Number**
    *   **First Four Letters of Candidate's Name** (This is a common security measure used by KEA).
5.  **Submit Details:** After entering the required information, click on the 'Submit' or 'View Result' button.
6.  **View and Download Your Scorecard:** Your KCET 2026 result and rank card will be displayed on the screen. Carefully review all the details. It is advisable to download and take a printout of your scorecard for future reference and for the upcoming counselling rounds.

Ensure you have a stable internet connection and your correct credentials ready before attempting to check your result.

## Details Available on Your KCET 2026 Scorecard

Once you access your KCET 2026 result, your scorecard will contain several important pieces of information. It is crucial to verify all these details for accuracy. The scorecard typically includes:

*   **Candidate's Name**
*   **KCET Roll Number/Application Number**
*   **Candidate's Photograph and Signature**
*   **Subject-wise Marks:** Marks obtained in Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics/Biology (as per your chosen subjects)
*   **Total Marks Obtained in KCET 2026**
*   **KCET 2026 Rank:** Your overall rank, which is crucial for the counselling process.
*   **Qualifying Status:** Whether you have qualified for counselling.
*   **Category:** Your declared reservation category.

In case of any discrepancy in your scorecard, immediately contact the Karnataka Examinations Authority for clarification and necessary corrections.

## What to Do After KCET 2026 Result Declaration

The declaration of KCET 2026 results is just the first step. The next crucial phase is the counselling process, conducted by KEA, for seat allotment. Here’s a general overview of the steps you should prepare for:

### 1. Counselling Registration and Document Verification

Qualified candidates will need to register for the KCET 2026 counselling process online. Following registration, a mandatory document verification process will be conducted at designated centres. Candidates must carry all original documents along with photocopies as specified by KEA. Essential documents typically include:

*   KCET 2026 Application Form printout
*   KCET 2026 Admit Card
*   KCET 2026 Scorecard/Rank Card
*   Class 10th and 12th Mark Sheets and Passing Certificates
*   Study Certificate and Rural Study Certificate (if applicable)
*   Caste/Income Certificate (if applicable)
*   Kannada Medium Certificate (if applicable)
*   Two passport-size photographs

### 2. Option Entry (Choice Filling)

After successful document verification, candidates will be eligible to participate in the option entry process. During this phase, you can choose your preferred colleges and courses in order of priority. It is essential to research colleges, cutoffs from previous years, and course availability before filling your options. You can enter as many choices as you wish.

### 3. Seat Allotment

Based on your KCET 2026 rank, the options you have filled, and the availability of seats, KEA will conduct multiple rounds of seat allotment. You will be able to view your allotted college and course online.

### 4. Reporting to College

If you are satisfied with the allotted seat, you must download the admission order from the KEA portal, pay the prescribed fees, and report to the allotted college within the specified timeline to confirm your admission.
