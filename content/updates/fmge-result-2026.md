## What After FMGE Result? Next Steps

Passing the FMGE is a significant milestone for foreign medical graduates. Here’s what generally follows:

1.  **Provisional Registration with NMC/SMC:** Candidates who successfully clear the FMGE will need to apply for provisional registration with the National Medical Commission (NMC) or the respective State Medical Councils (SMC). This is a mandatory step to undertake an internship in India. The detailed procedure and required documents will be notified by NBEMS/NMC/SMC.
2.  **Internship:** After provisional registration, candidates must complete a compulsory rotatory medical internship of 12 months in a medical college or institution approved by the NMC.
3.  **Permanent Registration:** Upon successful completion of the internship, candidates can apply for permanent registration with the NMC/SMC, which allows them to practice medicine independently in India.

Keep all your academic documents (medical degree, transcripts, FMGE scorecard, etc.) ready for the registration process. NBEMS will usually issue a detailed public notice regarding the schedule and procedure for collecting Pass Certificates post-result declaration.

## About FMGE Exam

The Foreign Medical Graduate Examination (FMGE) is a mandatory licensure examination conducted by the National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS) in India. It serves as a screening test for Indian citizens/OCIs holding foreign medical qualifications to be eligible to practice medicine in India. The exam is held twice a year – typically in June and December. It consists of 300 multiple-choice questions (MCQs) covering various subjects of the MBBS curriculum. A candidate must score a minimum of 150 marks out of 300 to pass the examination.
