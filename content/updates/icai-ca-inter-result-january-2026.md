# ICAI CA Inter Result January 2026 Out — Check Score at icai.nic.in

The **Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI)** has declared the **CA Intermediate (Inter) January 2026 examination result**. Candidates who appeared for the CA Inter Group 1, Group 2, or both groups can now check their results on the official ICAI website at **icai.nic.in**.

The CA Inter examination was conducted in January 2026 across multiple centres in India. The result is now available for download along with the subject-wise marksheet.

## How to Check CA Inter Result January 2026

Follow these steps to check your CA Intermediate result online:

### Step 1: Visit the Official Website

Go to **[https://icai.nic.in](https://icai.nic.in)** in your browser.

### Step 2: Select the Examination

Click on the link for **"CA Intermediate Result — January 2026"** on the homepage.

### Step 3: Enter Your Details

Enter your **Roll Number** and **Registration Number** (or PIN) in the required fields.

### Step 4: Submit and View Result

Click the **Submit** button. Your result with subject-wise marks will appear on screen.

### Step 5: Download and Save

Download or take a screenshot of your result for future reference. The official marksheet will be available for download separately.

## Important Details About CA Inter Result

### Passing Criteria

To pass the CA Intermediate examination, candidates must score:

- **Minimum 40%** marks in each individual subject
- **Minimum 50%** marks in aggregate for the group (Group 1 or Group 2)

### Result Verification

Candidates can verify their result through multiple channels:

- **Website:** [icai.nic.in](https://icai.nic.in) or [icaiexam.icai.org](https://icaiexam.icai.org)
- **Email:** ICAI sends result to registered email addresses
- **SMS:** Result notification via registered mobile number

### After the Result

- **Passed both groups:** Eligible to register for CA Final and complete articleship requirements
- **Passed one group:** Can appear for the remaining group in the next attempt
- **Did not pass:** Can re-register for the next examination session

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How can I check my CA Inter January 2026 result?

Visit [icai.nic.in](https://icai.nic.in), click on the CA Intermediate result link, enter your roll number and registration number, and click Submit to view your result.

### What is the passing percentage for CA Inter?

Candidates need a minimum of 40% in each subject and 50% aggregate marks in the group to pass the CA Intermediate examination.

### Can I get my CA Inter marksheet online?

Yes, ICAI provides digital marksheets that can be downloaded from [icaiexam.icai.org](https://icaiexam.icai.org) after the result is declared.

### What should I do if the result website is not loading?

The website may experience heavy traffic immediately after result declaration. Wait a few minutes and try again, or check your result via the email sent to your registered email address.

### When is the next CA Inter exam after January 2026?

ICAI typically conducts the CA Intermediate examination twice a year, in January and July. Check [www.icai.org](https://www.icai.org) for the next exam schedule.
