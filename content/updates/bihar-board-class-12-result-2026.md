## How to Check Bihar Board Class 12 Result 2026 Online

Follow these steps to check your BSEB Intermediate result:

### Step 1: Visit the Official Result Portal

Go to [results.biharboardonline.com](https://results.biharboardonline.com) or [biharboardonline.bihar.gov.in](https://biharboardonline.bihar.gov.in).

### Step 2: Click on the Result Link

Look for **"Bihar Board Class 12 Intermediate Result 2026"** link on the homepage and click it.

### Step 3: Enter Your Roll Number

Enter your **Roll Number** and **Roll Code** as mentioned on your BSEB admit card.

### Step 4: Submit and View Result

Click the **Submit** or **Search** button to view your result on screen.

### Step 5: Check Your Marks

Your result will display — **subject-wise marks, total marks, percentage and pass/fail status**.

### Step 6: Download and Save

Click **Download** or **Print** to save a copy of your result for future reference.

### Step 7: Keep the Marksheet Safe

This downloaded result is provisional. Collect the **original marksheet** from your school when BSEB distributes it.

## Important Details

- **Passing marks:** Minimum 33% in each subject and overall aggregate to pass.
- **Compartment exam:** Students who fail in one or two subjects are eligible for the compartment (supplementary) exam.
- **Scrutiny/Challenge:** Students not satisfied with their marks can apply for **scrutiny** or **challenge of answer sheets** through the BSEB portal within the notified window.
- **Original marksheet:** Will be distributed to schools by BSEB. Collect from your school/college.
