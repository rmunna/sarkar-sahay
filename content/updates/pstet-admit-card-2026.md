## How to Download PSTET Admit Card 2026 — Step-by-Step Guide

Follow these steps to download your PSTET 2026 hall ticket:

**Step 1:** Visit the official PSTET website at [pstet2025.org](https://pstet2025.org/).

**Step 2:** On the homepage, look for the **"Download Admit Card"** link or button. Click on it.

**Step 3:** Enter your **Registration Number** in the designated field.

**Step 4:** Enter your **Password** that you set during the registration process.

**Step 5:** Click the **"Login"** or **"Submit"** button.

**Step 6:** Your PSTET 2026 Admit Card will appear on the screen. Verify all the details carefully.

**Step 7:** Click on **"Download"** or **"Print"** to save the admit card as a PDF.

**Step 8:** Take **at least two printouts** of the admit card and keep them safely.

> **Tip:** If you have forgotten your password, use the "Forgot Password" option on the login page to reset it using your registered email or mobile number.

## What to Carry on Exam Day

Make sure you carry the following items to the examination centre:

1. **Printed PSTET Admit Card 2026** — coloured or black-and-white printout
2. **Government-issued Photo ID** — Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Driving Licence, Passport, or PAN Card
3. **Passport-size photographs** — carry 2 extra copies (same as uploaded during registration)
4. **Black/Blue ballpoint pen** — for filling in the OMR sheet

- Smartwatches or Bluetooth devices
- Calculators
- Bags, books, or notes
- Any communication device

## About PSTET

The Punjab State Teacher Eligibility Test (PSTET) is conducted by the Punjab State Education Board (PSEB) for candidates aspiring to become teachers in Punjab government schools. Passing PSTET is mandatory for recruitment as a teacher for Classes I to VIII in the state.

- **Paper I** — For candidates who wish to teach Classes I to V (Primary Level)
- **Paper II** — For candidates who wish to teach Classes VI to VIII (Upper Primary Level)

Candidates can appear for both papers if eligible.
