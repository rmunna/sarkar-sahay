## SSC GD Constable Selection Process at a Glance

The SSC GD Constable examination is a multi-stage process designed to select eligible candidates for various Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs), NIA, SSF, and Rifleman (GD) in Assam Rifles. The stages are:

1.  **Computer Based Examination (CBE):** An online objective-type examination covering General Intelligence and Reasoning, General Knowledge and General Awareness, Elementary Mathematics, and English/Hindi.
2.  **Physical Efficiency Test (PET) & Physical Standard Test (PST):** Candidates who qualify the CBE are called for PET (running) and PST (measurement of height and chest).
3.  **Detailed Medical Examination (DME):** Candidates who qualify PET/PST undergo a thorough medical examination to ensure they meet the prescribed medical standards.
4.  **Review Medical Examination (RME):** Candidates declared unfit in DME can appeal for an RME.
5.  **Final Merit List:** Based on performance in the CBE (and subject to qualifying all subsequent stages), a final merit list is prepared for selection to various forces.
