While the Preliminary Examination date for the 2026 cycle is likely to have been announced or already conducted by today's date (June 17, 2026), the dates for the subsequent stages are usually declared progressively.

## Admit Card / Hall Ticket Date

The **Admit Card**, also known as the **Hall Ticket**, is a mandatory document for appearing in any stage of the GPSC Class 1 2 examination. It is typically released **7 to 10 days** prior to the scheduled examination date for each stage. For the Preliminary Examination held in May 2026, the admit cards would have been released in late April 2026. For the Main Examination and Interview, the admit cards will be released approximately 7-10 days before their respective, yet-to-be-announced dates.

**How to Download:**
1.  Visit the official GPSC website: **gpsc.gujarat.gov.in**.
2.  Navigate to the "Download Call Letter" or "Print Admit Card" section.
3.  Select the specific examination, i.e., "GPSC Class 1 2 2026".
4.  Enter your **Confirmation Number** and **Date of Birth** (DD-MM-YYYY format) as provided during the application process.
5.  Click on "Print Call Letter" or "Download".
6.  Verify all details on the admit card, including your name, photograph, signature, roll number, exam date, time, and venue.
7.  Download and print **at least two copies** of the admit card. It is advisable to keep a digital copy as well.

Candidates must ensure that the photograph and signature on the admit card are clear and legible. Any discrepancy should be immediately reported to the GPSC helpline well before the exam date. Without a valid admit card, entry into the examination hall will be strictly prohibited.

## Exam Centers and City Allocation

GPSC endeavors to allocate examination centers based on the preferences indicated by candidates during the online application process. Aspirants are usually given the option to select a few preferred cities or districts for their examination center. However, the **final decision regarding the allocation of exam centers rests solely with the GPSC**. The Commission reserves the right to allot any center to a candidate, irrespective of their stated preferences, based on logistical considerations, availability of venues, and the number of applicants.

Information regarding the allotted exam center, including the specific venue name and address, will be clearly mentioned on the candidate's admit card. Once an exam center is allotted, **requests for changes in the examination center are generally not entertained** under any circumstances. Therefore, candidates are advised to carefully review their admit card upon download and plan their travel and accommodation well in advance, especially if the allotted center is in a different city. It is crucial to familiarize yourself with the route to the exam center a day or two before the examination to avoid any last-minute stress or delays.

## Exam Pattern on the Day

The GPSC Class 1 2 examination follows a structured pattern designed to assess various aspects of a candidate's knowledge and aptitude.

**Preliminary Examination (Objective Type - MCQ):**
This stage comprises two papers, both conducted offline using OMR sheets.
*   **Paper 1: General Studies 1** (200 Marks, 3 Hours): Covers topics such as History, Cultural Heritage, Indian Constitution, Sports, and Current Affairs.
*   **Paper 2: General Studies 2** (200 Marks, 3 Hours): Encompasses Indian Economy, Geography, Science & Technology, Reasoning, and Aptitude.
Both papers consist of **Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)**. There is a system of **negative marking**, where **0.33 marks** are deducted for each incorrect answer. Candidates must be careful while marking their responses on the OMR sheet.

**Main Examination (Descriptive Type):**
The Main Examination consists of multiple descriptive papers, typically six, each of 150 marks and 3 hours duration. These papers are designed to test a candidate's writing skills, analytical ability, and in-depth understanding of various subjects. The papers usually include:
*   Gujarati Language
*   English Language
*   General Studies 1
*   General Studies 2
*   General Studies 3
*   General Studies 4
The Mains examination is crucial as its marks, along with the interview marks, determine the final merit list.

**Interview (Personality Test):**
Candidates who qualify the Main Examination are called for an interview, which carries 100 marks. This stage assesses the candidate's personality, communication skills, mental alertness, critical thinking, and general awareness.

## What to Carry

To ensure a hassle-free experience on the exam day, candidates must carry only the essential items specified by GPSC.

*   **Valid Admit Card / Hall Ticket:** A printed copy of your GPSC Class 1 2 2026 admit card. Ensure all details are clear.
*   **Original Photo Identity Proof:** A valid government-issued photo ID such as Aadhar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Driving License, or Passport. The name on the ID must match the name on the admit card.
*   **Passport Size Photograph:** One or two recent passport-size photographs (matching the one uploaded in the application form). These might be required for attendance sheets or in case of any discrepancy.
*   **Blue/Black Ballpoint Pen:** For offline (OMR-based) Preliminary Examination. Only ballpoint pens are allowed for marking answers on the OMR sheet. For descriptive Mains examination, a blue/black ballpoint pen is also required.
*   **Transparent Water Bottle:** (Optional) A transparent water bottle is usually permitted inside the examination hall.
*   **Face Mask and Hand Sanitizer:** (If prevailing health advisories require it) Always check the latest instructions from GPSC regarding health and safety protocols.

**Items strictly prohibited:** Electronic gadgets (mobile phones, smartwatches, calculators, Bluetooth devices), study material, rough papers, eatables (except for medical reasons with prior permission), and any metallic items or jewelry (beyond essential items).

## FAQs

**Q1: What if the GPSC Class 1 2 exam date clashes with another important examination?**
**A1:** GPSC generally does not make provisions for individual exam date clashes. The Commission announces its schedule well in advance, and candidates are expected to manage their commitments accordingly. It is highly unlikely that GPSC will reschedule the exam for an individual candidate's conflict.

**Q2: How early should I report to the examination center?**
**A2:** You should report to the examination center at least **60 to 90 minutes** before the scheduled gate closing time. This allows sufficient time for security checks, verification of documents, finding your seat, and settling down without any last-minute rush.

**Q3: Can I carry a water bottle inside the examination hall?**
**A3:** Yes, typically, a **transparent water bottle** is allowed inside the examination hall. However, it must be completely transparent, without any labels or markings. It is always advisable to check the specific instructions on your admit card for any changes in this policy.

**Q4: Is a calculator allowed for the GPSC Class 1 2 examination?**
**A4:** For the Preliminary Examination, **calculators are strictly NOT allowed**. For the Main Examination, non-programmable calculators might be permitted for specific papers (e.g., those involving numerical problems), but this will be explicitly mentioned in the official notification or admit card instructions for that stage. Assume calculators are not allowed unless stated otherwise.

**Q5: What if I lose my admit card before the exam?**
**A5:** If you lose your admit card, you should immediately **re-download it** from the official GPSC website (gpsc.gujarat.gov.in) using your confirmation number and date of birth. It is always recommended to print multiple copies and keep a digital backup. If you face any technical difficulties in re-downloading, contact the GPSC helpline well in advance of the exam date.
