## How to Apply Online for CTET 2026

The application process for CTET December 2026 will be entirely online. Follow these steps to apply once the registration window opens:

1.  **Visit the Official Website:** Go to https://ctet.nic.in.
2.  **Click on 'Apply for CTET Dec 2026':** Look for the dedicated link on the homepage (it will be activated once registration starts).
3.  **New Registration:** Register by providing your basic details such as name, email ID, and mobile number. A registration number and password will be generated.
4.  **Fill Application Form:** Log in with your credentials and fill out the detailed application form. This includes personal information, educational qualifications, preferred exam city, and choice of Paper I/Paper II or both.
5.  **Upload Documents:** Upload scanned images of your photograph and signature as per the specifications mentioned in the information bulletin.
6.  **Pay Application Fee:** Pay the required examination fee online through Net Banking, Credit Card, or Debit Card.
7.  **Review and Submit:** Carefully review all the entered information before final submission. No changes will be allowed after final submission (except during the correction window).
8.  **Print Confirmation Page:** Download and print the confirmation page for your records.

## Eligibility Criteria (Key Points)

To be eligible for CTET, candidates must meet specific educational qualifications as prescribed by the National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE). Here's a general overview; always refer to the detailed official notification for precise requirements:

*   **For Paper I (Classes I-V - Primary Stage):** Candidates should typically have passed 12th grade with at least 50% marks and a 2-year Diploma in Elementary Education (D.El.Ed) OR Graduation with B.Ed. There are various combinations specified; check the official bulletin.
*   **For Paper II (Classes VI-VIII - Elementary Stage):** Candidates generally require a Graduation degree with at least 50% marks and a 2-year Diploma in Elementary Education (D.El.Ed) OR B.Ed. Similar to Paper I, other specific qualifications are also accepted.
*   **Age Limit:** There is no age limit for appearing in CTET.

Candidates appearing in the final year of a D.El.Ed/B.Ed or equivalent teacher training course are also eligible to apply.

## CTET Exam Pattern & Syllabus

CTET consists of two papers:

*   **Paper I:** For candidates intending to be teachers for Classes I to V.
*   **Paper II:** For candidates intending to be teachers for Classes VI to VIII.

Each paper is of 150 multiple-choice questions (MCQs), carrying one mark each, with no negative marking. The duration for each paper is 2.5 hours.

The syllabus covers Child Development and Pedagogy, Language I, Language II, Mathematics, Environmental Studies (for Paper I), and Mathematics & Science or Social Studies (for Paper II). For a detailed breakdown of the syllabus and exam structure, refer to the Information Bulletin on https://ctet.nic.in.
