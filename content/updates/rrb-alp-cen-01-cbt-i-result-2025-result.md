## RRB ALP CEN 01/2025 CBT-I Result 2026 — Overview

The Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) has officially declared the results for the Computer Based Test Stage-I (CBT-I) of the Assistant Loco Pilot (ALP) recruitment under Centralized Employment Notice (CEN) 01/2025. This result is a crucial milestone for lakhs of candidates who appeared for the examination, as it determines their eligibility for the next stage of the selection process, which is CBT-II.

The RRB ALP CEN 01/2025 recruitment drive aims to fill various Assistant Loco Pilot positions across different Railway Zones in India. The CBT-I exam, held on **February 13, 2026**, served as the initial screening stage. Candidates who have successfully qualified in CBT-I will now proceed to CBT-II. The official notice has not specified the total number of candidates who appeared or the overall pass percentage at this stage. ## How to Check Your RRB ALP CBT-I Result Online

Checking your RRB ALP CBT-I Result is a straightforward process. Follow the steps below to access your scorecard and determine your qualification status for CBT-II:

1.  **Visit the Official RRB Website:** Navigate to the official website of the Railway Recruitment Board at [www.rrbcdg.gov.in](https://www.rrbcdg.gov.in). It is crucial to use the official domain to avoid fraudulent websites.
2.  **Locate the Result Link:** On the homepage, look for the "Results" section or a direct link related to "CEN 01/2025 ALP CBT-I Result" or "Scorecard for ALP CBT-I". This link is usually prominently displayed under the "Latest Updates" or "News" section.
3.  **Select Your Regional RRB:** If the main website redirects to regional RRB portals, select the specific RRB zone you applied from (e.g., RRB Ahmedabad, RRB Mumbai, RRB Chennai, etc.).
4.  **Click on the Result Link:** Click on the relevant link for the RRB ALP CEN 01/2025 CBT-I Result.
5.  **Enter Your Credentials:** You will be redirected to a login page. Here, you will need to enter your:
    *   **Roll Number / Registration Number:** As provided in your application form and admit card.
    *   **Date of Birth:** In the specified format (DD-MM-YYYY).
6.  **Submit Details:** After entering the required information, click on the "Submit" or "Login" button.
7.  **View and Download Your Result:** Your RRB ALP CBT-I scorecard and qualifying status will be displayed on the screen. Carefully review all the details.
8.  **Print for Future Reference:** It is highly recommended to download and print a copy of your result/scorecard for your records and for future stages of the recruitment process.

## Re-evaluation / Re-checking for RRB ALP CBT-I Result

For competitive examinations like the RRB ALP CBT-I, which are objective (Multiple Choice Questions - MCQ) and conducted in a Computer Based Test (CBT) format, there is generally **no provision for re-evaluation or re-checking of answer sheets**.

The scoring process for such exams is highly standardized and automated, minimizing the scope for manual errors. Furthermore, the RRB often employs a normalization process to ensure fairness across different exam shifts, which accounts for variations in question paper difficulty. Once the results are declared based on the final answer keys and normalization, they are considered final.

Candidates are advised to accept the officially declared results. Any grievances regarding answer keys are typically addressed during a specific window provided after the provisional answer key release, allowing candidates to raise objections before the final result compilation. After the final result declaration, no further requests for re-evaluation are usually entertained. Therefore, candidates should rely on the scores and qualifying status as published by the RRB on its official website.
