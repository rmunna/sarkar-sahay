## What is IBPS CRP RRB XV 2026?

The IBPS CRP RRB XV 2026 refers to the 15th Common Recruitment Process for Regional Rural Banks (RRBs) conducted by the Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS). This recruitment drive aims to select eligible candidates for various Group 'A' - Officers (Scale-I, II & III) and Group 'B' - Office Assistant (Multipurpose) posts in Regional Rural Banks across India. IBPS is an autonomous body that conducts recruitment examinations for various public sector banks and financial institutions. The CRP RRB examination is a crucial opportunity for aspirants seeking a career in the rural banking sector, offering a chance to contribute to financial inclusion and rural development.

## Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must meet the eligibility criteria specified by IBPS to apply for the CRP RRB XV 2026. The criteria typically include age limit, educational qualification, and nationality.

*   **Nationality/Citizenship:** A candidate must be either:
    *   a Citizen of India or
    *   a subject of Nepal or
    *   a subject of Bhutan or
    *   a Tibetan Refugee who came over to India before 1st January 1962 with the intention of permanently settling in India or
    *   a person of Indian origin who has migrated from Pakistan, Burma, Sri Lanka, East African countries of Kenya, Uganda, the United Republic of Tanzania (formerly Tanganyika and Zanzibar), Zambia, Malawi, Zaire, Ethiopia and Vietnam with the intention of permanently settling in India.
