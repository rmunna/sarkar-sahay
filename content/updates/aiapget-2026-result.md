## AIAPGET 2026 Result Declared: Your Gateway to AYUSH PG Admissions

The National Testing Agency (NTA) has officially declared the results for the All India Ayush Post Graduate Entrance Test (AIAPGET) 2026 today, September 18, 2026. Candidates who appeared for the examination can now access and download their individual scorecards from the official NTA AIAPGET website. This result is crucial for admission to Postgraduate AYUSH courses (Ayurveda, Yoga & Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, and Homoeopathy) for the academic session 2026-27.

The AIAPGET is a single entrance examination conducted annually for admission to approximately 2000 AYUSH PG seats across various government, government-aided, deemed universities, and private institutes throughout India. The declaration of results marks a significant step towards the counselling process, which will determine admissions based on the scores obtained.

### How to Check and Download Your AIAPGET 2026 Result/Scorecard

Follow these simple steps to access your AIAPGET 2026 result and download your scorecard:

1.  **Visit the Official Website**: Go to the official NTA AIAPGET website: `https://aiapget.nta.ac.in` or `https://ntaresults.nic.in`.
2.  **Locate Result Link**: On the homepage, look for a prominent link that says "AIAPGET 2026 Result" or "AIAPGET 2026 Scorecard".
3.  **Enter Credentials**: Click on the link. You will be redirected to a login page. Here, you will need to enter your:
    *   Application Number
    *   Date of Birth
    *   Security Pin (Captcha code displayed on the screen)
4.  **Submit and View**: After entering the details, click on the "Submit" or "Login" button. Your AIAPGET 2026 scorecard will be displayed on the screen.
5.  **Download and Print**: Carefully review all the details on your scorecard. It is highly recommended to download the PDF version and take a printout for future reference and the upcoming counselling process.

Candidates are advised to keep their application number and password/date of birth handy before attempting to check their results.

### Details Mentioned on Your AIAPGET 2026 Scorecard

The AIAPGET 2026 scorecard is a critical document that contains vital information about your performance in the examination. It typically includes the following details:

*   Candidate's Name
*   Father's Name
*   Mother's Name
*   Roll Number
*   Application Number
*   Date of Birth
*   Category (General, SC, ST, OBC, EWS, PwD)
*   Sub-Category
*   Subject (Ayurveda, Unani, Siddha, Homoeopathy)
*   Marks obtained (overall and subject-wise, if applicable)
*   Overall Rank
*   Category Rank
*   NTA Score (Percentile Score)
*   Qualifying Status

Ensure all details are correct. In case of any discrepancy, contact the NTA helpdesk immediately.

### What to Do Next After AIAPGET 2026 Results

With the AIAPGET 2026 results now declared, the next crucial phase is the counselling process. Here's what you should expect and prepare for:

1.  **Counselling Notification**: The AYUSH Admissions Central Counseling Committee (AACCC) will release a detailed notification regarding the AIAPGET 2026 counselling schedule. Keep a close watch on the official AACCC website: `https://aaccc.gov.in`.
2.  **Merit List**: Based on the AIAPGET scores, NTA will also release an All India Merit List. The counselling process will utilize this merit list.
3.  **Cutoff Scores**: Various institutes and categories will have their respective cutoff scores. Candidates meeting or exceeding these cutoffs will be eligible for counselling and seat allocation.
4.  **Registration for Counselling**: Eligible candidates will need to register online for the AIAPGET 2026 counselling process, which usually involves choice filling and locking.
5.  **Document Verification**: Prepare all necessary documents in original and photocopies, as they will be required during the document verification stage of counselling. This includes your AIAPGET scorecard, admit card, academic certificates, category certificate (if applicable), etc.

It is imperative to refer only to official sources for counselling updates and instructions to avoid misinformation.
