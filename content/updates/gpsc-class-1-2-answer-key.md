GPSC has released the **provisional answer key for Class 1 & 2 Prelims (Advt. No. 05/2026-27)** held on **7 June 2026**. Download the official PDF from [gpsc.gujarat.gov.in/StageDocument?name=answerkey](https://gpsc.gujarat.gov.in/StageDocument?name=answerkey). The answer key is released paper-wise. **No negative marking** — wrong or unattempted answers score 0.

## How to Download GPSC Class 1 & 2 Answer Key 2026

1. Go to [gpsc.gujarat.gov.in](https://gpsc.gujarat.gov.in)
2. Click **"Answer Key"** or navigate to **Stage Documents → Answer Key**
3. Find the entry for **Advt. No. 05/2026-27** (Class 1 & 2 Combined Competitive Exam)
4. Download the PDF for your paper set (A/B/C/D)
5. Cross-check your responses against the official key

Alternatively, the key is hosted on [gpsc-ojas.gujarat.gov.in](https://gpsc-ojas.gujarat.gov.in) under the answer key section.

## How to Calculate Your Score

GPSC Prelims uses a **simple scoring system — no negative marking**:
- Correct answer: **+2 marks** (per question)
- Wrong answer: **0 marks**
- Unattempted: **0 marks**

Total marks per paper: Paper 1 (General Studies) — 200 marks (100 questions × 2 marks each)

**Estimated score calculation:**
```
Score = Correct answers × 2
```

## How to Raise Objections Against the Answer Key

If you believe an answer in the provisional key is incorrect:

1. Log in to [gpsc-ojas.gujarat.gov.in](https://gpsc-ojas.gujarat.gov.in) with your registration credentials
2. Go to "Representation/Objection" section
3. Select the question number and paper set
4. Provide the correct answer with supporting reference (textbook, official source)
5. Pay the prescribed objection fee (if applicable) and submit before the deadline

GPSC reviews all objections and publishes a **Final Answer Key** after verification. The Final Answer Key is used for computing the merit list.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is the GPSC Prelims answer key set-wise?**
Yes. GPSC releases the answer key separately for each question paper set (A, B, C, D). Download the set that matches your question booklet.

**Q: How long is the objection window for GPSC answer key 2026?**
Typically 7–10 days from the provisional key release date. The exact window is announced on gpsc.gujarat.gov.in. Missing the deadline means your objection will not be entertained.

**Q: When will the GPSC Class 1 & 2 Prelims Result 2026 be declared?**
Results are typically declared 4–8 weeks after the exam. The shortlisted candidates for Mains are announced along with the result PDF.
