The Karnataka Public Service Commission (KPSC) is a pivotal body for recruitment to various Group A, B, C, and D posts in the state government. The FDA (First Division Assistant) and SDA (Second Division Assistant) examinations are highly sought-after opportunities for aspirants looking to join the Karnataka state administration. This comprehensive guide will walk you through the online application process for the KPSC FDA SDA 2026 recruitment, ensuring you navigate each step smoothly and accurately.

## Before You Start — Documents Needed

Before commencing your online application, gather and prepare all necessary documents. Having these ready will streamline the process and prevent last-minute rushes or errors. Ensure all scanned documents meet the specified format and size requirements.

*   **Scanned Photograph:**
    *   **Format:** JPEG/JPG
    *   **File Size:** 20 KB to 50 KB (approximate)
    *   **Dimensions:** 3.5 cm (width) x 4.5 cm (height) (approximate)
    *   **Specifications:** A recent passport-size colour photograph taken against a white or light-coloured background. The face should occupy 70-80% of the photo, with a clear view of both ears. No caps, hats, or dark glasses are allowed. The photo must be clear, without red-eye, and not blurry.
*   **Scanned Signature:**
    *   **Format:** JPEG/JPG
    *   **File Size:** 10 KB to 20 KB (approximate)
    *   **Dimensions:** 3.5 cm (width) x 1.5 cm (height) (approximate)
    *   **Specifications:** Sign on a white paper with a black ink pen. The signature must be clear and legible. Ensure only the signature area is scanned, not the entire page.
*   **Educational Qualification Certificates:**
    *   SSLC/10th Mark Sheet (for Date of Birth verification)
    *   PUC/12th Mark Sheet
    *   Degree Certificate and Mark Sheets (for FDA)
    *   Relevant Diploma/Degree Certificates (for SDA, if applicable)
    *   Any other higher qualification certificates as per the notification.
*   **Identity Proof:**
    *   Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Passport, Driving License, or any other government-issued ID proof.
*   **Caste Certificate (if applicable):**
    *   For SC/ST/Category-1/2A/2B/3A/3B candidates, issued by the competent authority in Karnataka. Ensure it is recent and valid.
*   **Income Certificate (if applicable):**
    *   For claiming fee exemption or other benefits based on income.
*   **EWS Certificate (if applicable):**
    *   Economically Weaker Sections certificate issued by the competent authority.
*   **PwBD Certificate (if applicable):**
    *   Persons with Benchmark Disabilities certificate issued by the competent medical board.
*   **Ex-Serviceman Certificate (if applicable):**
    *   Discharge certificate and other relevant documents.
*   **Kannada Medium/Rural/Project Displaced Certificates (if applicable):**
    *   As per KPSC norms for claiming reservation benefits.

## Step 1 — New Registration

The first step involves creating a new user account on the KPSC online application portal. This is a one-time process for new applicants.

1.  **Visit the Official Website:** Open your web browser and navigate to the official KPSC website: **https://kpsc.kar.nic.in**.
2.  **Locate "Apply Online":** On the homepage, look for the "Apply Online" or "Online Application" section. Within this, you will find a link for the KPSC FDA SDA 2026 recruitment. Click on it.
3.  **Initiate New Registration:** Find and click on the "New Registration" or "Click Here for New Registration" link.
4.  **Fill Basic Details:** You will be directed to a registration form. Carefully enter the following details:
    *   **Applicant's Full Name:** As per your SSLC/10th certificate.
    *   **Father's Name:**
    *   **Mother's Name:**
    *   **Date of Birth (DOB):** As per your SSLC/10th certificate.
    *   **Gender:**
    *   **Mobile Number:** Ensure this is active and accessible as OTPs and important communications will be sent here.
    *   **Email ID:** A valid and active email address is crucial for receiving updates.
    *   **Aadhaar Number (optional but recommended):**
5.  **Generate OTP and Verify:** After entering the details, an OTP (One Time Password) will be sent to your registered mobile number and/or email ID. Enter the OTP in the designated field to verify your contact details.
6.  **Set Password:** You will then be prompted to set a password for your account. Choose a strong password that includes a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters.
7.  **Receive Registration Number/User ID:** Upon successful registration, a unique **Registration Number** or **User ID** will be generated and displayed on the screen. It will also be sent to your registered mobile number and email ID. **Note down this Registration Number and your chosen password carefully**, as they are essential for all future logins.

## Step 2 — Fill the Application Form

Once registered, you can proceed to fill out the detailed application form.

1.  **Login to the Portal:** Go back to the KPSC online application portal and click on "Login" or "Registered Candidate Login." Enter your **Registration Number/User ID** and the **password** you set during registration.
2.  **Access Application Form:** After logging in, you will see a link to "Fill Application Form" for KPSC FDA SDA 2026. Click on it.
3.  **Personal Information:**
    *   **Nationality:** Indian
    *   **Marital Status:**
    *   **Permanent Address:**
    *   **Correspondence Address:**
    *   **Religion:**
    *   **Mother Tongue:**
    *   **Identification Mark (if any):**
    *   **Are you a Government Employee?** (Yes/No, if yes, provide details)
    *   **Are you an Ex-Serviceman?** (Yes/No, if yes, provide details)
4.  **Educational Qualifications:**
    *   Enter details for your **SSLC/10th, PUC/12th, Degree, and any other relevant qualifications**.
    *   Provide the **Board/University Name, Year of Passing, Percentage/CGPA, and Roll Number/Register Number** for each qualification.
    *   Ensure the details match your certificates precisely.
5.  **Category Details:**
    *   Select your **Reservation Category** (General, SC, ST, Category-1, 2A, 2B, 3A, 3B, EWS).
    *   Indicate if you belong to **PwBD, Ex-Serviceman, Rural Candidate, Kannada Medium Candidate, Project Displaced Person**, etc., as applicable.
    *   Provide the **certificate number, issuing authority, and date of issue** for each claim.
6.  **Post Preference:**
    *   If the notification allows for preference between FDA and SDA, or specific departments, select your choices in the order of preference. Read the official notification carefully for eligibility criteria for each post.
7.  **Exam Center Preference:**
    *   Choose your preferred **examination centers** from the available list. KPSC usually provides a list of districts in Karnataka. Select centers that are convenient for you.

## Step 3 — Upload Photo and Signature

This is a crucial step where you upload the scanned images prepared earlier.

1.  **Navigate to Upload Section:** After filling the form, you will be directed to the photo and signature upload section, or there will be a specific link for it.
2.  **Upload Photograph:**
    *   Click on "Choose File" or "Browse" next to the "Photograph" field.
    *   Select your scanned passport-size photograph file (JPEG/JPG, 20-50 KB, 3.5x4.5 cm).
    *   Click "Upload."
3.  **Upload Signature:**
    *   Click on "Choose File" or "Browse" next to the "Signature" field.
    *   Select your scanned signature file (JPEG/JPG, 10-20 KB, 3.5x1.5 cm).
    *   Click "Upload."
4.  **Verify Uploads:** After uploading, the images will be displayed. **Carefully check if the photo and signature are clear, correctly oriented, and meet all specifications.** Incorrect uploads can lead to rejection of the application.
    *   **Tips for Resizing:** If your images do not meet the size/dimension requirements, you can use online image editors or basic photo editing software (like Paint on Windows) to resize and crop them. Ensure the aspect ratio is maintained.

## Step 5 — Submit and Save Confirmation

The final step is to submit your application and save the confirmation.

1.  **Review Application:** Before final submission, a preview of your entire application form will be displayed. **Thoroughly review all the details** you have entered, including personal information, educational qualifications, category claims, photo, and signature. Ensure there are no errors or discrepancies.
2.  **Declaration:** Read the declaration statement carefully and tick the checkbox to confirm that all information provided is true and correct to your knowledge.
3.  **Final Submit:** Click on the "Final Submit" or "Submit Application" button. Once submitted, you generally cannot make any further changes to the application form (unless a specific correction window is announced).
4.  **Download/Print Confirmation Page:** After successful submission, a confirmation page or application receipt will be generated. This page will contain your **Application Number/Registration Number**, payment details, and all the information you submitted.
    *   **Download and save this page** as a PDF file on your computer.
    *   **Print multiple copies** for your records. This confirmation page is crucial for future reference, including admit card download and document verification.
5.  **Note Application Number:** Make sure to note down your **Application Number** separately, as it is your primary identifier for this recruitment process.

## Common Errors and Fixes

Applying online can sometimes present challenges. Here are some common errors and how to address them:

*   **Wrong Photo/Signature Format or Size:**
    *   **Error:** "File format not supported" or "File size exceeds limit."
    *   **Fix:** Use an online image converter/resizer or basic photo editing software (like Paint) to convert to JPEG/JPG and adjust the size and dimensions as per the specifications (e.g., photo 20-50KB, signature 10-20KB).
*   **Payment Failure/Transaction Issues:**
    *   **Error:** "Payment failed," "Transaction declined," or amount debited but no confirmation.
    *   **Fix:** Wait for at least 24-48 hours. Often, the amount is automatically refunded, or the payment status gets updated. If not, contact your bank and KPSC helpdesk with your transaction ID. Do not attempt multiple payments immediately.
*   **Browser Compatibility Issues:**
    *   **Error:** Form not loading correctly, buttons not working.
    *   **Fix:** Use recommended browsers like Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, or Microsoft Edge (latest versions). Clear browser cache and cookies, or try using an incognito/private window.
*   **Server Down/Slow Internet:**
    *   **Error:** "Server error," "Page not found," or extremely slow loading.
    *   **Fix:** This often happens during peak hours or close to the deadline. Try applying during off-peak hours (late night or early morning). Ensure you have a stable internet connection.
*   **Incorrect Data Entry:**
    *   **Error:** Realizing a mistake after submission.
    *   **Fix:** Generally, once submitted, data cannot be edited. KPSC *might* open a correction window, but it's not guaranteed. Always review thoroughly before final submission.

## FAQs

**Q1: Can I edit my application form after final submission?**
A1: Generally, no. Once you click "Final Submit," the application is locked. KPSC may, at its discretion, open a correction window for a limited period, but this is not always guaranteed. It is crucial to review all details meticulously before final submission.

**Q2: What if I pay the application fee twice?**
A2: If you accidentally pay the fee twice, one of the transactions is usually refunded automatically by the payment gateway or bank within 5-7 working days. If not, contact the KPSC helpdesk with both transaction IDs and proof of payment.

**Q3: Is applying through a mobile phone accepted?**
A3: While some portals are mobile-friendly, it is **highly recommended to use a desktop or laptop computer** for filling out the application form. Mobile browsers may not display the full interface correctly, leading to errors in data entry or document uploads.

**Q4: Which web browsers are best for applying online?**
A4: For the best experience, use the latest versions of **Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, or Microsoft Edge**. Ensure your browser's JavaScript is enabled and pop-ups are not blocked.

**Q5: How do I get a fee refund if my application is rejected?**
A5: Application fees for government exams are generally non-refundable, even if your application is rejected due to ineligibility or errors on your part. A refund is typically only issued if the recruitment process itself is cancelled by KPSC or if there's an error on their part.
