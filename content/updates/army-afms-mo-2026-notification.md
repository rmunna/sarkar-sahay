## What is AFMS Medical Officer SSC Recruitment 2026?

The Indian Army has released the **Armed Forces Medical Services (AFMS) Medical Officer (MO) Short Service Commission (SSC) Recruitment 2026** notification. A total of **100 Medical Officer posts** are available for eligible male and female MBBS graduates. The notification was published on **February 14, 2026**, and online applications will be accepted from **February 21 to March 4, 2026** through the official portal join.afms.gov.in.

This is a prestigious opportunity for doctors who want to serve in the Indian Army's medical wing while gaining clinical experience in diverse settings.

## Vacancy Details

**Total Vacancies: 100 Medical Officer (SSC) Posts**

| Category | Posts |
|----------|-------|
| Male | 75 |
| Female | 25 |

Posts are open to both male and female candidates holding an MBBS qualification.

## Eligibility Criteria

- **Education:** MBBS degree from a recognised university. Must have appeared in the NEET PG examination.
- **Age:** 18–30 years as on December 31, 2026. Age relaxation as per government rules.
- **Nationality:** Indian citizen

## Application Fee

| Category | Fee |
|----------|-----|
| All Categories (General, OBC, EWS, SC, ST, Female, PH, PwD) | Rs. 200 |

Fee must be paid through the online process only. No offline fee payment is accepted.

## How to Apply Online

Applications open on **February 21, 2026**. Follow these steps:

1. Visit [join.afms.gov.in](https://join.afms.gov.in)
2. Click on the AFMS MO SSC 2026 recruitment link
3. Register and create your login credentials
4. Fill in the application form with personal and educational details
5. Upload required documents and photograph
6. Pay Rs. 200 application fee online
7. Submit the application and download the confirmation page

**Important:** Only online applications are accepted. Offline or paper applications will not be processed. Apply well before the deadline to avoid last-minute technical issues.

## Selection Process

The selection is based on the following stages:

1. **Shortlisting of Applications** — Based on qualifications and NEET PG score
2. **Interview** — Shortlisted candidates called for interview in March 2026
3. **Document Verification** — Original certificates and mark sheets verified
4. **Medical Examination** — Final medical fitness assessment as per Army standards

There is **no written exam** — selection is based on application shortlisting and interview performance.

## Who Should Apply?
