CISF Head Constable Ministerial (HCM) admit card 2026 is released online at [cisfrectt.cisf.gov.in](https://cisfrectt.cisf.gov.in) approximately **10–21 days before the written exam**. The admit card is the mandatory entry document for the exam centre — candidates without a valid admit card are not allowed inside.

## How to Download CISF Head Constable Admit Card 2026

1. Go to [cisfrectt.cisf.gov.in](https://cisfrectt.cisf.gov.in)
2. Click on the **"Admit Card"** link for HCM 2026
3. Enter your **Registration Number** and **Date of Birth** (DD-MM-YYYY)
4. Verify details shown on screen
5. Click **Download** and save the PDF
6. Print on A4 paper — both sides if double-sided

## What's Printed on the Admit Card

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Candidate Name | As per application |
| Roll Number | Assigned by CISF for the exam |
| Registration Number | Your application number |
| Date of Birth | As per records |
| Exam Date & Shift | Your assigned date and time slot |
| Exam Centre Name | Full address of your exam venue |
| Reporting Time | Usually 1 hour before exam |
| Photo | Uploaded during application |
| Signature | Uploaded during application |
| Category | General / OBC / SC / ST / EWS |

## Documents to Carry at Exam Centre

Mandatory:
- **Admit card** (printed, A4 size)
- **Original government photo ID** — any one of: Aadhaar, PAN card, Voter ID, Passport, Driving Licence

Prohibited items (leave at home):
- Mobile phones, smart watches, Bluetooth devices
- Calculator, stationery (pens provided at CBT centres)
- Bags, wallets with metal (lockers provided, but avoid carrying unnecessary items)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: My admit card shows wrong DOB or name — what do I do?**
Contact CISF recruitment cell immediately via the email/helpline on cisfrectt.cisf.gov.in. Do not attend the exam with a discrepant admit card — you may be barred from appearing. Carry proof documents that show the correct information.

**Q: I didn't receive SMS/email about admit card release — how do I know when it's out?**
CISF does not always send individual notifications. Monitor the official site [cisfrectt.cisf.gov.in](https://cisfrectt.cisf.gov.in) and check the Admit Card section every few days once the exam date is announced.

**Q: Can I take a mobile photo of the admit card instead of printing?**
No. Exam centres require a printed admit card. Digital copy on phone is not accepted. Print in advance — don't leave it to the last minute.
