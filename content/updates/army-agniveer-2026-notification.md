## What is Army Agniveer Scheme?

The Agnipath Scheme is the Indian Armed Forces' recruitment pathway for soldiers, introduced by the Government of India. Under this scheme, young candidates are recruited as Agniveers for a four-year tenure in the Indian Army. The Army Agniveer 2026 recruitment covers posts including General Duty (GD), Tradesman, Clerk/Store Keeper, Technical, and other categories.

## Eligibility Criteria

- **Age Limit:** 17½ to 21 years (candidates born between July 1, 2005 and July 1, 2009 are eligible). Upper age limit relaxable up to 30 years for widows of defence personnel who died in harness.
- **Educational Qualification:**
  - **Agniveer GD:** Class 10th pass with 45% marks aggregate in five subjects and minimum 33% in each subject
  - **Agniveer Technical:** 12th pass with Physics, Chemistry, Maths, and English with 50% aggregate and 40% in each subject
  - **Agniveer Clerk/SKT:** 12th pass with 60% aggregate and minimum 50% in each subject
  - **Agniveer Tradesman:** 8th/10th pass (depending on trade)
- **Nationality:** Indian citizen

## Selection Process

The recruitment is conducted in two phases:

1. **Phase I — Computer Based Examination (CEE):** Online written test conducted at designated centres (tentatively June 1–15, 2026)
2. **Phase II — Recruitment Rally:** Shortlisted candidates based on CEE scores are called for physical fitness tests, medical examination, and document verification

## FAQs

### Q: What is the last date to apply for Army Agniveer 2026?
A: The last date to apply online is April 1, 2026.

### Q: Is there any application fee for Agniveer recruitment?
A: No, the application is free of charge.

### Q: What is the age limit for Agniveer 2026?
A: Candidates must be between 17½ and 21 years of age (born between July 1, 2005 and July 1, 2009).

### Q: Can female candidates apply for Agniveer?
A: Yes, women can apply for the Women Military Police category under the Agniveer scheme.

### Q: What happens after 4 years of Agniveer service?
A: After completing the 4-year tenure, 25% of Agniveers are selected for regular service. The remaining receive Seva Nidhi (approximately ₹11.71 lakh) and can pursue other career opportunities.

### Q: What is the salary of an Agniveer?
A: The monthly salary ranges from ₹30,000 in the first year to ₹40,000 in the fourth year.
