BSF Head Constable Ministerial (HCM) written exam is a **Computer Based Test (CBT) with 100 questions, 100 marks, and 2 hours duration**. The exam covers 5 subjects: General Knowledge, English Language, Numerical Ability, Reasoning, and Computer Fundamentals. No negative marking for wrong answers.

## Exam Pattern

| Subject | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| General Knowledge / Awareness | 25 | 25 |
| English Language | 25 | 25 |
| Numerical Ability (Maths) | 25 | 25 |
| Reasoning Ability | 25 | 25 |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** |

**Note:** BSF HCM also tests Computer Knowledge as part of the selection process — either integrated into the written test or as a separate typing/skill test depending on the cycle. Check the official notification for the exact pattern.

- **Duration:** 120 minutes (2 hours)
- **Type:** MCQ (4 options)
- **Negative marking:** None

## Detailed Syllabus

### 1. General Knowledge / Awareness (25 marks)

- Current Affairs: Last 6 months (national, state, international)
- History of India: Ancient, Medieval, Modern; Freedom Struggle
- Indian Geography: Rivers, mountains, climate, states & capitals
- Indian Polity: Constitution, Parliament, Fundamental Rights
- Economy: Basic economic terms, government schemes, GDP, inflation
- Science: Basic physics, chemistry, biology — class 9–10 level
- Defence & Security: CAPF structure, BSF history, national security
- Sports, Awards, Books & Authors: Recent winners and recipients

### 2. English Language (25 marks)

- Reading Comprehension (one passage, 4–5 questions)
- Fill in the blanks (grammar-based)
- Sentence Correction / Error Spotting
- Synonyms & Antonyms
- One-word substitution
- Idioms and phrases
- Active / Passive voice
- Spelling mistakes

### 3. Numerical Ability (25 marks)

- Number System: LCM, HCF, fractions
- Percentage, Profit & Loss, Discount
- Simple & Compound Interest
- Ratio & Proportion
- Average, Age problems
- Time & Work, Pipes & Cisterns
- Speed, Distance & Time
- Mensuration: Area, perimeter, volume
- Data Interpretation: Tables, charts (basic)

### 4. Reasoning Ability (25 marks)

- Number and Letter Series
- Coding-Decoding
- Blood Relations
- Directions & Distance
- Analogy
- Odd One Out (Classification)
- Syllogism
- Calendar and Clock problems
- Non-verbal: Mirror image, paper folding

### 5. Computer Knowledge (if tested)

- Computer Fundamentals: Hardware, software, input/output
- Operating Systems: Windows basics, file and folder management
- MS Office: Word, Excel (basic formulas), PowerPoint
- Internet: Browser, email, downloading
- Keyboard shortcuts and basic operations

## Skill Test (Typing)

For HCM posts, a **typing speed test** may also be required after the written exam:
- **English typing:** 35 words per minute (WPM)
- **Hindi typing:** 30 words per minute (WPM)

Candidates who fail the typing test are not selected for HCM posts even if they clear the written exam. Practice typing daily.

## Preparation Tips

1. **GK current affairs** — read newspaper or a monthly magazine; BSF GK section covers recent defence news
2. **English grammar** — focus on error spotting (tense, preposition, subject-verb agreement)
3. **Typing practice** — 30–35 WPM is essential; use free online typing tutors
4. **Maths** — percentage, time & work, and ratio cover ~40% of the Maths section
5. **BSF previous papers** — available on toppersexam.com and prepp.in
