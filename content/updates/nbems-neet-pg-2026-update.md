## What is NEET PG 2026?

**NEET PG 2026** (National Eligibility cum Entrance Test for Post Graduate) is the national-level entrance examination conducted by the **National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS)** for admission to MD, MS, and PG Diploma courses across India. The exam is tentatively scheduled for **August 30, 2026**, in a single-shift, computer-based format.

NBEMS has released a tentative exam calendar for 2026, and NEET PG 2026 is one of the most awaited medical entrance exams this year. Candidates who have completed or are completing their MBBS internship are eligible to appear.

## Eligibility Criteria

- **Qualification:** MBBS degree from a recognised institution under NMC (National Medical Commission)
- **Internship:** Must have completed or be completing internship by the cutoff date specified by NBEMS
- **Citizenship:** Indian citizens, OCI cardholders, and NRI candidates
- **Attempts:** No limit on number of attempts
- **Age:** No upper age limit

## How to Apply for NEET PG 2026

Follow these steps once registration opens on the NBEMS portal:

1. **Visit the official website** — Go to [natboard.edu.in](https://natboard.edu.in) or [nbe.edu.in](https://nbe.edu.in)
2. **Register** — Create an account using your email and mobile number
3. **Fill the application form** — Enter personal, academic, and internship details
4. **Upload documents** — Photograph, signature, MBBS degree/provisional certificate, and internship completion certificate
5. **Pay the application fee** — Pay online via debit card, credit card, net banking, or UPI
6. **Download confirmation** — Save and print the application confirmation page

## Exam Pattern

- **Total Questions:** 200 multiple-choice questions (MCQs)
- **Duration:** 3 hours 30 minutes
- **Marking:** +4 for each correct answer, −1 for each incorrect answer
- **Subjects:** All 19 subjects of MBBS curriculum including Medicine, Surgery, Obstetrics & Gynaecology, Paediatrics, Ophthalmology, ENT, Orthopaedics, Dermatology, Psychiatry, Anaesthesia, Radiology, and more
- **Language:** English only

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. When is NEET PG 2026 exam date?

NEET PG 2026 is tentatively scheduled for **August 30, 2026**, as per the NBEMS exam calendar. The confirmed date will be announced in the official information bulletin.

### 2. When will NEET PG 2026 registration start?

Registration is expected to open in **April–May 2026** on the official NBEMS website natboard.edu.in. Keep checking the website for the exact date.

### 3. What is the NEET PG 2026 exam pattern?

The exam consists of **200 MCQs** to be completed in **3 hours 30 minutes**. Each correct answer carries +4 marks and each wrong answer carries −1 mark. The total marks are 800.

### 4. Is there a limit on NEET PG attempts?

No, there is **no limit** on the number of attempts for NEET PG as per current NBEMS rules.

### 5. What is the qualifying cutoff for NEET PG?

The qualifying percentile varies each year. For NEET PG 2025, the general category cutoff was the 50th percentile. SC/ST/OBC/PwBD categories get relaxed cutoffs as per NBEMS norms.
