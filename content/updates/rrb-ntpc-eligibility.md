RRB NTPC 2026 has two eligibility tracks: **Graduate Level posts** require a Bachelor's degree with age 18–33 years, and **Undergraduate Level posts** require 12th pass (or equivalent) with age 18–30 years. Reserved category candidates get standard age relaxation. There is no domicile requirement — candidates from any state can apply to any RRB zone.

## Educational Qualification

| Level | Posts | Required Qualification |
|-------|-------|------------------------|
| **Graduate Level** | Station Master, Goods Guard, Junior Account Assistant cum Typist, Senior Commercial cum Ticket Clerk, Senior Time Keeper | Any Bachelor's Degree from a recognized university |
| **Undergraduate Level** | Junior Clerk cum Typist, Accounts Clerk cum Typist, Junior Time Keeper, Trains Clerk, Commercial cum Ticket Clerk | 12th pass (any stream) from a recognised board |

**Additional requirements for specific posts:**
- **Station Master / Traffic Assistant:** Must pass a Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBAT) — tests speed, accuracy, vigilance
- **Typist posts:** Typing speed in English (30 WPM) or Hindi (25 WPM) tested via skill test
- **Account Assistant cum Typist:** Commerce background preferred (not mandatory)

## Nationality

Must be an Indian citizen, or a subject of Nepal/Bhutan, or a Tibetan refugee (settled in India before 1 January 1962), or a person of Indian origin migrated from specific countries — all as per Railway Recruitment Board's standard eligibility rules.

## No Zone Restriction

Unlike state government exams, RRB NTPC has no domicile requirement. Candidates can apply to **any Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) zone** across India. However, each candidate can apply to only **one RRB zone** per recruitment cycle — choose your preferred zone at the time of application.

## Physical Fitness

RRB conducts a **medical examination** before final appointment. Standards vary by post:

- **Station Master, Traffic Assistant:** Eyesight and colour vision tested strictly (must distinguish railway signals)
- **Goods Guard:** Physical fitness for field operations
- **Commercial/Ticket Clerk:** Standard fitness; no major restrictions

Candidates with corrected vision (spectacles) are eligible for most posts; some posts require uncorrected vision within prescribed limits.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can final-year students apply for RRB NTPC 2026?**
Yes. Candidates in the final year of their degree can apply provisionally. However, the degree certificate must be produced at the document verification stage before joining. If you cannot produce it, candidacy is cancelled.

**Q: Is there any subject restriction for graduate posts in RRB NTPC?**
No. Any Bachelor's degree (B.A., B.Sc., B.Com., B.Tech., BCA, etc.) from a UGC-recognised university qualifies for Graduate Level NTPC posts. There is no stream restriction unless a specific post specifies otherwise.

**Q: Can I apply to multiple RRBs in RRB NTPC 2026?**
No. Each candidate is allowed to apply to only one RRB zone. Candidates who apply to multiple zones are disqualified. Choose the zone carefully based on your preferred posting location — RRB assigns postings within its region.
