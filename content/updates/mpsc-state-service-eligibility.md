MPSC (Maharashtra Public Service Commission) State Service Exam 2026 requires a **Bachelor's degree** from a recognised university and age **19–38 years for General and OBC categories**. SC/ST candidates get a higher age limit of 43 years. Maharashtra domicile is required for most posts. No limit on attempts — candidates can appear until they exceed the age limit.

## Educational Qualification

A **Bachelor's degree in any stream** from a UGC-recognised university is required. The degree stream is not restricted — B.A., B.Sc., B.Com., B.Tech., BCA, MBA all qualify.

Final-year graduates may apply provisionally but must produce the degree certificate before document verification.

## Age Limit

| Category | Minimum Age | Maximum Age |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| General (UR) | 19 | 38 |
| OBC / SEBC (Maharashtra) | 19 | 38 (same as General — MPSC gives no extra relaxation to OBC) |
| SC / ST (Maharashtra) | 19 | 43 (+5 years) |
| VJ-A, NT-B, NT-C, NT-D, SBC | 19 | 43 (+5 years) |
| PwD (General) | 19 | 48 (+10 years) |
| Ex-Servicemen | 19 | No upper limit (subject to conditions) |

*Note: MPSC does NOT give age relaxation to OBC/SEBC candidates unlike most other state PSCs. Age is calculated as on the cutoff date in the notification.*

## Maharashtra Domicile Requirement

Most MPSC posts require Maharashtra domicile (15+ years of residence or born in Maharashtra to Maharashtra domicile parents). Check the official notification for post-wise domicile requirements.

Non-Maharashtra candidates can appear in the exam but may not be eligible for all posts.

## Marathi Language Requirement

Candidates must have passed **Marathi language** at 10th Standard (SSC/equivalent) level. This is a mandatory eligibility criterion for MPSC posts. Candidates who haven't studied Marathi at school must clear a Marathi language qualifying paper.

## Nationality

Indian citizen. Subjects of Nepal or Bhutan are also eligible under standard central government rules applicable to state services.

## Number of Attempts

MPSC State Service Exam has **no attempt limit**. Candidates can appear as many times as they are within the age limit.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is Maharashtra domicile mandatory for MPSC Rajyaseva 2026?**
For most Group A gazetted posts (Deputy Collector, DSP, etc.), Maharashtra domicile is required. Check the post-wise eligibility in the official notification for the specific cycle you are applying to.

**Q: Does MPSC give OBC age relaxation like UPSC?**
No. MPSC State Service Exam does NOT give age relaxation to OBC/SEBC candidates. The upper age limit for OBC/SEBC is the same as General category (38 years). Only SC/ST, PwD, and Ex-Servicemen get age relaxation.

**Q: Can candidates outside Maharashtra apply for MPSC 2026?**
Yes — anyone can apply if they meet the age and qualification criteria. However, Marathi language knowledge (at 10th level) is required. Posts that have Maharashtra domicile as a requirement will bar non-domicile candidates at document verification.
