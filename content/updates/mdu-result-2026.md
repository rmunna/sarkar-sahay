## How to Check MDU Result 2026 on mdu.ac.in

Follow these steps to check your result on the main MDU website:

1. Visit the official MDU website at [mdu.ac.in](https://mdu.ac.in).
2. Click on the **"Results"** tab on the homepage menu.
3. Select your **programme** (UG/PG) and **examination session**.
4. Enter your **Roll Number** or **Registration Number**.
5. Click **"Submit"** to view your result.
6. Your result with subject-wise marks will appear on screen.
7. **Download or print** the result for your records.

## How to Check MDU Result 2026 on result.mdurtk.in

MDU also hosts results on a dedicated result portal:

1. Go to [result.mdurtk.in](https://result.mdurtk.in).
2. Select your **course/programme** from the list.
3. Choose the **semester/year** of examination.
4. Enter your **Roll Number**.
5. Click **"View Result"**.
6. Save or print the displayed result page.

## Important Details

- **Mark Sheet:** The online result is provisional. Original mark sheets will be distributed through respective colleges.
- **Reappear:** Students who did not clear any subject can apply for reappear/supplementary exams as per the university schedule.
- **Grace Marks:** MDU applies grace marks policy as per university regulations. Check your result carefully for any grace marks applied.

## Revaluation / Rechecking Process

If you are not satisfied with your marks, MDU allows you to apply for revaluation or rechecking:

1. **Apply online** through the MDU official portal within **15–20 days** of result declaration (check the exact deadline on mdu.ac.in).
2. Pay the prescribed **revaluation fee** (typically ₹500–₹1,000 per subject, may vary).
3. Submit the application with your **Roll Number, subject code**, and **exam details**.
4. Rechecking involves verifying totalling errors; revaluation involves fresh evaluation of answer sheets.
5. Revaluation results are usually declared within **30–45 days** of the application deadline.

> Check the official MDU website for the exact revaluation schedule and fee for your examination session.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How can I check my MDU result 2026?
Visit [mdu.ac.in](https://mdu.ac.in) or [result.mdurtk.in](https://result.mdurtk.in), select your course, enter your roll number, and click submit to view your result.

### Is the online MDU result considered official?
The online result is provisional. The official mark sheet will be issued by the university through your college.
