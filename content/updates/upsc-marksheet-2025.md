# UPSC Civil Services 2025 Marksheet Released — How to Download at upsc.gov.in

The **Union Public Service Commission (UPSC)** has officially released the marksheets for the **Civil Services Examination (CSE) 2025**. Candidates who appeared for the examination — whether qualified or not — can now download their detailed scorecards from the official portal at **upsconline.gov.in**.

The marksheet release follows the declaration of the UPSC CSE 2025 final results and provides a complete breakdown of marks obtained across all stages of the examination.

## How to Download UPSC CSE 2025 Marksheet

Follow these steps to download your marksheet:

1. **Visit the official website** — Go to [upsconline.gov.in](https://upsconline.gov.in)
2. **Navigate to the Examinations section** — On the homepage, look for the "Examinations" or "Marksheet" section
3. **Select the relevant link** — Click on "Civil Services Examination 2025 Marksheet"
4. **Enter your details** — Provide your Roll Number and other required credentials
5. **View and download** — Your marksheet will appear on screen in PDF format. Click "Download" or use Ctrl+P to save it

> **Tip:** If the website is slow due to high traffic, try accessing it during off-peak hours (early morning or late night).

## What's Included in the UPSC Marksheet

The UPSC CSE 2025 marksheet provides a comprehensive breakdown of your performance:

- **Prelims marks** — Scores in General Studies Paper I and Paper II (CSAT)
- **Mains written examination marks** — Subject-wise marks for all 9 papers including Essay, General Studies I–IV, and Optional papers
- **Personality Test (Interview) marks** — Marks obtained out of 275 in the interview round
- **Total aggregate marks** — Combined score across Mains and Interview (out of 2025)

### Important Points About Scoring

- **Prelims** is qualifying in nature — marks are used only for shortlisting candidates for Mains
- **Final merit** is determined by Mains + Interview marks only
- The marksheet also indicates the **cut-off marks** for each category (General, OBC, SC, ST, EWS) at each stage

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Can non-qualified candidates also download the marksheet?

**Yes.** UPSC releases marksheets for all candidates who appeared in the examination, regardless of whether they qualified or not. You can download your marksheet from upsconline.gov.in.

### 2. What details do I need to download my marksheet?

You will need your **Roll Number** and possibly your date of birth or registration ID to access your marksheet on the UPSC portal.

### 3. How long will the marksheet be available for download?

UPSC typically keeps marksheets available on the portal for **a limited period** (usually a few months). It is advisable to download and save your marksheet as soon as possible.

### 4. Is the downloaded marksheet valid as an official document?

The marksheet downloaded from the official UPSC portal is an authentic document. However, for official purposes, candidates may need to contact UPSC for a physical copy or certified version.

### 5. The website is not loading. What should I do?

Due to heavy traffic after marksheet release, the UPSC portal may experience slowdowns. Try accessing the website during **off-peak hours**, use a stable internet connection, or clear your browser cache and try again.
