## What Happens After the Final Result?

After the UPSC CSE final result, recommended candidates will be allocated services (IAS, IPS, IFS, etc.) based on their rank, preference, and availability of vacancies. The process includes:

- **Service Allocation:** Based on rank and cadre preferences submitted during the Mains application
- **Training:** Selected candidates will join the Lal Bahadur Shastri National Academy of Administration (LBSNAA) in Mussoorie for the foundation course
- **Reserve List:** UPSC may release a reserve list later if additional vacancies arise
