## Samastha Kerala Public Exam Result 2026 Declared

The **Samastha Kerala Islam Matha Vidyabhyasa Board (SKIMVB)** has officially declared the **Samastha Public Exam Result 2026** for Classes 5, 7, 10, and 12 on **March 2, 2026**. The announcement was made by **Raisul Ulama E. Sulaiman Musliyar**, President of the Samastha Kerala Jamiyyathul Ulama. Students who appeared for the SKIMVB Pothu Pareeksha 2026 can now check their results online at **result.samastha.info**.

The public examinations were conducted on **January 31 and February 1, 2026**, across madrasa centres in Kerala.

## How to Check Samastha Result 2026 Online

Follow these steps to check your SKIMVB public exam result:

1. Visit the official result website at **[result.samastha.info](https://result.samastha.info)**
2. On the homepage, select your **exam class** (5th, 7th, 10th, or 12th)
3. Enter your **Register Number** as printed on your hall ticket
4. Enter your **Date of Birth** if required
5. Click the **"View Result"** or **"Check Result"** button
6. Your result with marks/grades will be displayed on screen
7. **Download or print** the scorecard for your records

> **Tip:** If the website is slow due to heavy traffic, try again after some time or use a different browser.
