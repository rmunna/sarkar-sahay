4.  **Click on the Result Link:** Find and click on the link specifically for "RRB ALP CEN 01/2025 CBT-I Result."
5.  **Enter Your Credentials:** You will be redirected to a login page. Here, you will need to enter your Roll Number and/or Registration Number, along with your Date of Birth (DD/MM/YYYY) as per your application form.

7.  **Download and Print:** It is highly recommended to download your result/scorecard and take a printout for future reference and for the next stages of the recruitment process.
