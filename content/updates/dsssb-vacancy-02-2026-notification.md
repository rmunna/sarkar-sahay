## How to Apply Online

Follow these steps to submit your application for DSSSB Advertisement No. 02/2026:

1. Visit the official DSSSB website at [dsssb.delhi.gov.in](https://dsssb.delhi.gov.in).
2. Click on **"Online Application"** or look for the Advertisement No. 02/2026 link.
3. If you are a new user, **register** by providing your basic details, email, and mobile number.
4. **Log in** with your credentials and select the post code you wish to apply for.
5. Fill in all required details — personal information, educational qualifications, and experience.
6. **Upload** your photograph, signature, and any required documents in the specified format.
7. **Pay the application fee** online (if applicable) and submit the form.
8. **Download** and print the confirmation page for your records.
