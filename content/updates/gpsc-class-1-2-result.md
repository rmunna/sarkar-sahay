GPSC Class 1 & 2 Prelims (Advt. No. 05/2026-27) was held on **7 June 2026**. The **Prelims result is expected within 4–6 weeks** of the exam — check [gpsc.gujarat.gov.in](https://gpsc.gujarat.gov.in) for the official announcement. The result PDF contains roll numbers of candidates shortlisted for Mains. Separately, the previous cycle (Advt. 23/2024-25) Mains result is expected mid-2026.

## How to Check GPSC Class 1 & 2 Result 2026

1. Visit [gpsc.gujarat.gov.in](https://gpsc.gujarat.gov.in)
2. Go to **"Results"** section on the homepage
3. Find the result notification for **Advt. No. 05/2026-27 Prelims**
4. Download the PDF — it contains the roll number list of shortlisted candidates
5. Search your roll number using Ctrl+F

Alternatively, log in to [gpsc-ojas.gujarat.gov.in](https://gpsc-ojas.gujarat.gov.in) for individual result status.

## What the Result Contains

GPSC Prelims result typically shows:
- Roll numbers of candidates qualified for Mains (no names in the PDF)
- Category-wise shortlisted count
- Cut-off marks (provisional or confirmed)
- Date of Mains examination (if announced simultaneously)

Individual marks are **not shown in the initial result PDF**. Marks can be obtained separately after the Mains stage or via RTI.

## Qualifying for Mains — How Shortlisting Works

GPSC shortlists candidates for Mains based on Prelims score:
- **Merit-based shortlisting:** Top candidates from each category are shortlisted (typically 10–15× the number of vacancies)
- **No negative marking** in Prelims — every attempt helps
- Separate category-wise lists: General, SEBC, OBC, SC, ST, EWS, PwD

Additionally, two **qualifying language papers** must be passed:
- Gujarati Language (300 marks, 30% qualifying threshold)
- English Language (300 marks, 30% qualifying threshold)

These qualifying papers are part of Mains, not Prelims.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How long does GPSC take to declare Prelims result?**
Typically 4–8 weeks after the exam. The timeline can extend if answer key objections result in significant re-evaluation. Follow gpsc.gujarat.gov.in for official updates.

**Q: Will GPSC send SMS/email when the result is out?**
GPSC does not routinely send SMS notifications for results. Check the official website directly or follow reliable state exam news portals.

**Q: Can I get my Prelims marks before Mains shortlisting?**
No. Individual Prelims marks are not released before the Mains result stage. The Prelims result only shows qualified roll numbers and the cut-off.
