BSF Head Constable Ministerial (HCM) CBT (Computer Based Written Examination) is scheduled as announced via official notice on [rectt.bsf.gov.in](https://rectt.bsf.gov.in). Per a BSF Headquarters notice dated **5 June 2026**, the written exam for HCM and ASI Steno posts in CAPFs is being scheduled. Admit cards are released **2–3 weeks before the exam date**.

## BSF HCM 2026 — Exam Timeline

| Stage | Date |
|-------|------|
| Official Notification | rectt.bsf.gov.in |
| Application period | As per notification |
| Application last date | As per notification |
| Admit Card release | 2–3 weeks before exam |
| Written Exam (CBT) | As per official schedule |
| PST / PET | After CBT result |
| Typing / Skill Test | After PST/PET |
| Medical Exam | After Skill Test |
| Document Verification | Final stage |
| Final Result | After all stages |

*Check [rectt.bsf.gov.in](https://rectt.bsf.gov.in) for the official exam date confirmation.*

## How to Get Admit Card

1. Go to [rectt.bsf.gov.in](https://rectt.bsf.gov.in)
2. Navigate to **Admit Card** section for HCM 2026
3. Login with your **Registration Number and Date of Birth**
4. Download and print the admit card
5. Carry with original photo ID to exam centre

## Selection Process Stages

| Stage | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Written Exam (CBT) | 100 questions, 100 marks, 2 hours |
| PST (Physical Standard Test) | Height, chest measurement |
| PET (Physical Efficiency Test) | Running, long jump, high jump |
| Typing / Skill Test | 35 WPM English or 30 WPM Hindi |
| Medical Exam | At BSF/CAPF hospital |
| Document Verification | Original certificates |

## What to Carry to the Exam

- Printed admit card (A4 size)
- Original government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar/Voter ID/PAN/Passport)
- Passport-size photographs (2–3 copies)
- Do NOT carry: mobile phones, calculators, smart watches

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Where can I check the BSF HCM exam date 2026?**
The official source is [rectt.bsf.gov.in](https://rectt.bsf.gov.in). Once the exam schedule is announced, the date is displayed prominently on the homepage. BSF also sends SMS to registered mobile numbers.

**Q: How many days before the BSF HCM exam is the admit card out?**
Typically 10–21 days. Download as soon as the link goes live — don't wait until exam day.

**Q: Is the BSF HCM exam online or offline in 2026?**
The exam is conducted as a Computer Based Test (CBT) — candidates appear at designated computer centres. It is not a pen-and-paper exam.
