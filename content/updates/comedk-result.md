## COMEDK UGET 2026 Results Officially Declared: Download Your Scorecard! 

The Consortium of Medical, Engineering and Dental Colleges of Karnataka (COMEDK) has officially declared the results for the Under Graduate Entrance Test (UGET) 2026. Candidates who appeared for the entrance examination on May 10, 2026, can now check and download their individual rank cards and scorecards from the official COMEDK website. 

This declaration marks a crucial step for aspirants seeking admission to engineering courses in participating private engineering colleges across Karnataka. The COMEDK UGET 2026 scorecard will be instrumental in the upcoming counseling process, which will determine seat allocation based on merit and preferences. 

It is highly recommended that all candidates download and securely save their rank cards, as it is a mandatory document for the counseling and admission process. 

## How to Check and Download Your COMEDK UGET 2026 Result 

Follow these simple steps to access your COMEDK UGET 2026 scorecard and rank details: 

1.  **Visit the Official Website:** Open your web browser and go to the official COMEDK website: `https://www.comedk.org/` 
2.  **Locate Result Link:** On the homepage, look for a prominent link stating "COMEDK UGET 2026 Result Declared" or "Download Rank Card". This link is typically found under the 'Latest News' or 'Applicant Login' section. 
3.  **Login to Applicant Portal:** Click on the result link. You will be redirected to the applicant login page. 
4.  **Enter Credentials:** Carefully enter your Application Sequence Number/User ID and your Password in the designated fields. Ensure there are no typos. 
5.  **View Result:** After successful login, your COMEDK UGET 2026 scorecard and rank card will be displayed on the screen. 
6.  **Download and Print:** Review all the details on your scorecard. It is advisable to download the PDF version of your rank card and take a printout for future reference and for the counseling process. 

**Important:** Do not share your login credentials with anyone. Always download your result from the official COMEDK portal only.

## What's Next? COMEDK UGET 2026 Counseling Process 

With the results declared, the next crucial step for all qualified candidates is the COMEDK UGET 2026 online counseling process. This multi-stage process will involve: 

1.  **Online Registration for Counseling:** Candidates must register for counseling on the official website by paying the counseling fee. 
2.  **Document Verification:** Uploading and online verification of necessary documents (10th, 12th mark sheets, admit card, rank card, ID proof, category certificates, etc.). 
3.  **Choice Filling:** Candidates will be required to fill and lock their preferred colleges and courses based on their rank and eligibility. 
4.  **Seat Allotment:** Based on the COMEDK UGET 2026 rank, choices filled, and availability of seats, provisional seat allotment will be done. 
5.  **Reporting to Allotted College:** Candidates who are satisfied with their allotment must confirm their seat and report to the allotted college for final admission formalities. 

**Official counseling dates and detailed procedures will be released shortly by COMEDK.** Candidates are strongly advised to regularly check the official website `https://www.comedk.org/` for the latest updates regarding counseling schedules, guidelines, and document requirements. Do not rely on unofficial sources for critical information.
