## What is RRB Technician Recruitment?

The Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) conducts the Technician recruitment under CEN 02/2025 to fill Technician Grade I (Signal) and Technician Grade III posts across various railway zones in India. The selection involves a Computer-Based Test (CBT) followed by document verification and medical examination.

## Important Dates

| Event | Date |
|-------|------|
| Notification Released | July 2025 |
| Last Date to Apply | 7 August 2025 |
| City Intimation Slip Released | 25 February 2026 |
| Admit Card (E-Call Letter) | 2 March 2026 |
| CBT Exam Dates | 6, 9, 10, 13 March 2026 |

## Vacancy Details

- **Total Vacancies:** 6,238
- **Posts:** Technician Grade I (Signal), Technician Grade III
- **CEN Number:** CEN 02/2025
- **Exam Mode:** Computer-Based Test (CBT)

## How to Download City Intimation Slip

1. Visit the official RRB website or [rrb.digialm.com](https://rrb.digialm.com)
2. Click on "City Intimation Slip" link for CEN 02/2025
3. Enter your registration number and date of birth
4. Download and save the slip

The city intimation slip contains your exam city, exam date, and shift timing. Note that this is NOT the admit card.

## How to Download RRB Technician Admit Card 2026

The admit card (E-Call Letter) will be released on **2 March 2026**, four days before the first exam date. To download:

1. Visit [rrb.digialm.com](https://rrb.digialm.com) or your regional RRB website
2. Log in with registration number and date of birth
3. Download the admit card PDF
4. Take a printout for the exam day

### Details on the Admit Card

- Candidate name and roll number
- Exam date and shift timing
- Exam centre address with directions
- Photograph and signature
- Important exam day instructions

## Exam Pattern

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Mode | Computer-Based Test (CBT) |
| Duration | 90 minutes |
| Total Questions | 100 |
| Total Marks | 100 |
| Sections | Mathematics, General Intelligence & Reasoning, General Science, General Awareness |
| Negative Marking | 1/3 mark deducted per wrong answer |
| Language | English, Hindi, and regional languages |

## Selection Process

1. **Computer-Based Test (CBT)** — 6, 9, 10, 13 March 2026
2. **Document Verification (DV)** — After CBT result
3. **Medical Examination** — As per railway medical standards

## Exam Day Instructions

- Carry the printed admit card and original photo ID used during registration
- Reach the exam centre at least 60 minutes before the shift timing
- Electronic devices including mobile phones are NOT allowed
- Carry a transparent water bottle and necessary stationery

## Preparation Tips

1. Focus on Mathematics and General Science — they carry the highest weightage
2. Practice previous year RRB Technician papers for question pattern
3. Revise basic electrical, mechanical, and electronics concepts for technical questions
4. Manage time — aim for 50-60 seconds per question
5. Avoid negative marking — do not guess randomly
