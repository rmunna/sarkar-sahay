RRB NTPC 2026 is conducted in two Computer Based Tests (CBT): **CBT 1 (100 questions, 90 min)** is a screening test for all posts; **CBT 2 (120 questions, 90 min)** is held separately for Graduate Level and Undergraduate Level posts with different difficulty and syllabus emphasis. Both stages test Mathematics, General Intelligence & Reasoning (GI&RA), and General Awareness (GA). There is negative marking of 1/3 mark per wrong answer.

## RRB NTPC Exam Pattern

### CBT 1 (Preliminary — All Posts)

| Section | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| Mathematics | 30 | 30 | |
| General Intelligence & Reasoning | 30 | 30 | 90 minutes |
| General Awareness | 40 | 40 | |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** | |

- Negative marking: **1/3 mark per wrong answer**
- CBT 1 is qualifying — merit is based on CBT 2 score
- Different cut-offs for Graduate Level and Undergraduate Level posts

### CBT 2 (Main Exam — Graduate Level Posts)

| Section | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| Mathematics | 35 | 35 | |
| General Intelligence & Reasoning | 35 | 35 | 90 minutes |
| General Awareness | 50 | 50 | |
| **Total** | **120** | **120** | |

### CBT 2 (Main Exam — Undergraduate Level Posts)

Same structure as Graduate Level CBT 2 (120 questions, 120 marks, 90 min) but with different difficulty — Undergraduate Level has easier Maths and GK emphasis on 10th/12th level topics.

## Mathematics Syllabus

**Both CBT 1 & CBT 2 — same topics, higher difficulty in CBT 2**

- Number System: HCF, LCM, Divisibility rules, fractions, decimals
- Arithmetic: Percentage, Ratio & Proportion, Average
- Profit & Loss, Discount, Simple Interest, Compound Interest
- Time & Work, Pipes & Cisterns, Time, Speed & Distance
- Algebra: Basic algebraic identities, linear equations
- Geometry: Lines, angles, triangles, circles, quadrilaterals
- Mensuration: Area and perimeter of 2D shapes; volume of 3D shapes
- Trigonometry: Basic identities, heights and distances
- Statistics: Mean, median, mode, bar charts, pie charts, data interpretation

---

## General Intelligence & Reasoning Syllabus

- Analogies: Word analogies, number analogies, letter analogies
- Alphabetical and Number Series
- Coding-Decoding
- Mathematical Operations: BODMAS-based reasoning
- Blood Relations
- Syllogism
- Jumbling (re-arranging)
- Venn Diagrams
- Data Sufficiency
- Similarities and Differences
- Directions and Distances
- Statement and Conclusions
- Decision Making

## General Awareness Syllabus

This section is the most important differentiator — higher questions (40 in CBT 1, 50 in CBT 2).

**Current Affairs (30–40% weight)**
- National events: government schemes, budget highlights, infrastructure projects
- International events: summits, awards, sports results
- Science and technology: ISRO launches, defence, health
- Railway-specific news: new trains, stations, Railway Budget

**History (10–15%)**
- Ancient India: Indus Valley, Vedic period, Mauryas, Guptas
- Medieval India: Delhi Sultanate, Mughal Empire
- Modern India: British rule, 1857 revolt, freedom movement, key leaders

**Geography (10–15%)**
- India: Rivers, mountains, states, agricultural zones, natural resources
- World: Major countries, capitals, rivers, climate

**Science & Technology (10–15%)**
- Physics: Laws of motion, electricity, optics, sound
- Chemistry: Periodic table basics, common compounds
- Biology: Human body systems, diseases, ecology
- Computers: Basic input/output, internet, software

**Polity & Economy (10–15%)**
- Indian Constitution: Fundamental Rights, Directive Principles
- Parliament, President, Elections
- RBI, monetary policy basics, banking terms
- Railway-related economy: zones, production units

## Preparation Strategy

1. **GA is the highest-scoring section** — daily current affairs (15 min) for 3 months covers most questions. Focus on Railway-specific news.
2. **Maths requires speed** — 30 questions in 90 min alongside other sections means practice speed. Focus on short-cut methods for percentage, time-work, and SI/CI.
3. **Negative marking changes strategy** — don't guess randomly. Skip questions you're 50-50 on; focus on certainties.
4. **CBT 1 is just qualifying** — don't over-invest. Clear the cut-off, then focus on CBT 2 preparation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is RRB NTPC CBT 1 syllabus the same as CBT 2?**
Yes — same three subjects (Mathematics, GI&RA, General Awareness). CBT 2 has more questions (120 vs 100) and higher difficulty, especially in Maths. The GK portion in CBT 2 has more depth.

**Q: Does RRB NTPC have negative marking?**
Yes — 1/3 mark (0.33) is deducted for each wrong answer in both CBT 1 and CBT 2. Do not mark answers you are unsure about.

**Q: What is the RRB NTPC skill test after CBT 2?**
For posts like Station Master, Junior Account Assistant, and Senior Commercial cum Ticket Clerk, a Typing Test or Computer-Based Aptitude Test (CBAT) is conducted after CBT 2. Typing speed: 30 WPM in English or 25 WPM in Hindi. CBAT tests basic computer operation skills.
