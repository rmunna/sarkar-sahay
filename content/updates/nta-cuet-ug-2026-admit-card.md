## CUET (UG) 2026 Admit Card — Overview

The National Testing Agency (NTA) has officially released the Admit Cards for the Common University Entrance Test (CUET) (UG) 2026 today, May 27, 2026. This crucial document is now available for download for all registered candidates appearing for the examination scheduled on May 31, 2026. The CUET (UG) is a national-level entrance examination for admission to various undergraduate programs offered by Central Universities and other participating institutions across India.

The Admit Card is a mandatory document for entry into the examination hall. Candidates will not be permitted to take the test without a valid Admit Card and a government-issued photo identification. It serves as proof of your registration for the exam and contains vital information regarding your candidature, exam centre, and important instructions. Candidates are strongly advised to download their Admit Cards immediately and thoroughly check all the details printed on it.

## How to Download the Admit Card

Follow these step-by-step instructions to download your CUET (UG) 2026 Admit Card from the official NTA website:

1.  **Visit the Official NTA Website:** Open your web browser and navigate to the official National Testing Agency (NTA) website: [nta.ac.in](https://nta.ac.in).
2.  **Locate the Admit Card Link:** On the homepage, look for the "Latest @ NTA" section or a prominent link specifically for "CUET (UG) 2026 Admit Card Download". This link is usually highlighted or placed under the "Public Notices" section.
3.  **Click on the Download Link:** Click on the relevant link for the CUET (UG) 2026 Admit Card. You will be redirected to a login page.
4.  **Enter Login Credentials:** On the login page, you will typically need to enter your Application Number and Date of Birth (DD/MM/YYYY) or Password, as used during your application process. Ensure you enter these details accurately.
5.  **Submit and View Admit Card:** After entering your credentials, click on the "Submit" or "Login" button. Your CUET (UG) 2026 Admit Card will be displayed on the screen.
6.  **Download and Print:** Carefully review all the details on the Admit Card. Once satisfied, click on the "Download" button to save the Admit Card as a PDF file. It is highly recommended to take at least two to three clear printouts of the Admit Card on A4 size paper for examination day and future reference.

## Details Printed on the Admit Card

The CUET (UG) 2026 Admit Card will contain several crucial pieces of information. Candidates must verify all these details immediately after downloading to ensure accuracy.

*   **Candidate's Name:** Your full name as provided during registration.
*   **Father's Name:** Your father's name.
*   **Application Number:** The unique identification number assigned to your application.
*   **Roll Number:** Your specific roll number for the examination.
*   **Candidate's Photograph:** The photograph uploaded during the application process.
*   **Candidate's Signature:** The scanned signature uploaded during the application process.
*   **Date of Birth:** Your date of birth.
*   **Category:** Your declared category (e.g., General, OBC, SC, ST, EWS, PwD).
*   **Gender:** Your declared gender.
*   **Exam Date and Shift:** The specific date and time slot (shift) for your examination.
*   **Reporting Time:** The time by which you must report to the examination centre.
*   **Gate Closing Time:** The last time candidates will be allowed entry into the examination centre.
*   **Test Centre Name and Address:** The complete address of your allotted examination centre.
*   **Subject(s) Opted:** The list of subjects you have chosen for the CUET (UG) 2026 examination.
*   **Important Instructions for Candidates:** A set of guidelines and rules to be followed on the exam day.
*   **List of Documents to Bring:** A clear mention of all necessary documents to carry to the exam hall.

In case of any discrepancy in the details printed on the Admit Card, candidates should immediately contact the NTA Help Desk for rectification.

## Exam Day Instructions

Adhering to the exam day instructions is vital for a smooth examination experience. Candidates must be well-prepared and follow all guidelines strictly.

*   **Mandatory Documents:**
    *   A printed copy of your CUET (UG) 2026 Admit Card (preferably in colour).
    *   One passport-size photograph (same as uploaded on the application form) to be affixed on the attendance sheet at the centre.
    *   A valid original photo identification proof (e.g., Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Driving License, Voter ID, Passport). Photocopies or scanned copies will not be accepted.
    *   PwD certificate (if applicable) issued by the competent authority.
*   **Reporting Time:** Candidates must report to the examination centre at the reporting time mentioned on their Admit Card. This is typically 30-60 minutes before the actual exam start time to allow for necessary formalities like frisking, biometric registration, and document verification.
*   **Gate Closing Time:** The entry gates to the examination centre will close strictly at the time specified on the Admit Card. No candidate will be allowed entry after the gate closing time under any circumstances.
*   **Prohibited Items:**
    *   Any electronic devices such as mobile phones, smartwatches, calculators, electronic pens, pagers, etc.
    *   Any study material, notes, books, or loose papers.
    *   Wallets, handbags, goggles, belts, caps, or any metallic items.
    *   Eatables (packaged or open) and beverages (except a transparent water bottle).
    *   Any kind of communication device.
*   **Rough Work:** All rough work must be done only on the rough sheets provided at the examination centre. No personal paper or rough sheets are allowed.
*   **COVID-19 Guidelines:** Candidates must adhere to all prevailing COVID-19 safety protocols, including wearing a mask, maintaining social distancing, and using hand sanitiser. Specific instructions will be provided on the Admit Card.

A2: You can download your Admit Card from the official NTA website, [nta.ac.in](https://nta.ac.in). You will need to click on the CUET (UG) 2026 Admit Card link and log in using your Application Number and Date of Birth (or Password).

### Q3: What if I forgot my Application Number or Password to download the Admit Card?
A3: If you forgot your Application Number, look for the "Forgot Application Number" option on the login page. ### Q5: What should I do if there is an error in my photograph, signature, or other details on the Admit Card?
A5: If you find any discrepancy in your photograph, signature, name, date of birth, or any other crucial detail on your CUET (UG) 2026 Admit Card, you must immediately contact the NTA Help Desk. Do not wait until the exam day. Keep a record of your communication with NTA. The contact details for the NTA Help Desk are usually available on the official website [nta.ac.in](https://nta.ac.in) or in the information bulletin.
