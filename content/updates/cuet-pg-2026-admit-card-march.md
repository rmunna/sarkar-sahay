The National Testing Agency (NTA) has released the admit cards for the **Common University Entrance Test for Postgraduate (CUET PG) 2026** examination. Candidates scheduled to appear on **March 16, 17, 18, and 19, 2026** can now download their hall tickets from the official NTA website.

## How to Download CUET PG 2026 Admit Card

Follow these steps to download your hall ticket:

1. Visit the official CUET PG website at [cuet.nta.nic.in](https://cuet.nta.nic.in/)
2. Click on the **"Download Admit Card"** link on the homepage
3. Log in using your **Application Number** and **Date of Birth** (or password)
4. Your admit card will appear on screen — verify all details carefully
5. Click **"Download"** to save the PDF
6. Take **two printouts** on A4 paper — one for the exam centre and one for your records

> **Tip:** If the CUET PG website is slow, try downloading from the main NTA website at [nta.ac.in](https://nta.ac.in).

## Important Instructions for Exam Day

- **Carry your admit card** — entry without it will not be allowed
- Bring a **valid photo ID** (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, Voter ID, or Driving License)
- Reach the exam centre **at least 60 minutes** before the scheduled time
- **No electronic devices** (phones, smartwatches, calculators) are allowed inside
- Carry a **passport-size photograph** and a **transparent water bottle**
- Read all instructions printed on the admit card carefully

## What to Do If You Find Errors

If your name, photo, signature, or any other detail on the admit card is incorrect:

1. Contact NTA immediately through their official helpline
2. Visit [nta.ac.in](https://nta.ac.in) for the correction window (if available)
3. Carry supporting documents to the exam centre as proof

## FAQs

### When was the CUET PG 2026 admit card released?
NTA released the CUET PG 2026 admit cards on **March 12, 2026** for exams scheduled on March 16-19.

### Where can I download my CUET PG 2026 hall ticket?
Visit [cuet.nta.nic.in](https://cuet.nta.nic.in/) and log in with your application number and date of birth.

### What should I carry to the CUET PG 2026 exam centre?
Carry a printed admit card, a valid photo ID (Aadhaar/PAN/Passport/Voter ID), a passport-size photo, and a transparent water bottle.

### What if I can't download my admit card?
Try using a different browser, clear your cache, or try during off-peak hours. You can also download from [nta.ac.in](https://nta.ac.in). If issues persist, contact NTA's helpline.

### Can I take the exam without an admit card?
No. The admit card is mandatory for entry into the examination centre. No candidate will be allowed without it.
