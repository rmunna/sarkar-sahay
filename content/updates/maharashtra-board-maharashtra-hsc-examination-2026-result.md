## Maharashtra HSC Examination 2026 Result — Overview

The Maharashtra State Board of Secondary and Higher Secondary Education (MSBSHSE) has officially declared the results for the Maharashtra HSC Examination 2026. Students who appeared for the Higher Secondary Certificate (HSC) Class 12 examinations can now access their results. The results were announced on May 27, 2026, making them available for all candidates across the state. This guide provides comprehensive information on how to check your result, understand the details displayed, and important dates related to the examination outcome. The MSBSHSE conducts the HSC examinations annually, and the declaration of results is a crucial step for students planning their higher education. Further statistics regarding the overall pass percentage and district-wise performance will be released by the board.

## How to Check Result

To check your Maharashtra HSC Examination 2026 result, follow these simple steps:

1.  **Visit the Official Website:** Open your web browser and go to the official Maharashtra Board result portal: [mahresult.nic.in](https://mahresult.nic.in).
2.  **Locate Result Link:** On the homepage, look for the link that says "Maharashtra HSC Examination Result 2026" or "HSC Examination Result March 2026".
3.  **Click on the Link:** Click on the relevant result link to proceed.
4.  **Enter Credentials:** You will be redirected to a new page where you need to enter your examination details. Typically, this includes your Roll Number and your Mother's First Name (as per your admit card).
5.  **Submit Details:** After entering the required information, click on the "View Result" or "Submit" button.
6.  **View and Download Result:** Your Maharashtra HSC Examination 2026 result will be displayed on the screen. Carefully check all the details. It is advisable to download and take a printout of your provisional mark sheet for future reference and record-keeping.

## What Details Are Shown

The provisional mark sheet displayed online for the Maharashtra HSC Examination 2026 will contain several important details. Students should carefully verify all the information. The typical details shown include:

*   Student's Name
*   Roll Number
*   Seat Number
*   Mother's Name
*   Father's Name (or Guardian's Name)
*   School/College Name
*   Subject-wise Marks (Theory and Practical)
*   Total Marks Obtained
*   Maximum Marks
*   Grade/Percentage (if applicable)
*   Qualifying Status (Pass/Fail/ATKT)
*   Remarks (if any)

This online statement of marks is provisional and should be used for immediate reference. The original mark sheet and passing certificate will be issued by the respective schools/junior colleges at a later date.
