## What Happens Next — After the Result

Candidates who have successfully qualified the Written Examination for CSBC Bihar Constable (Advt. 01/2025) will now proceed to the next crucial stages of the selection process. As per the official selection process, the sequence of events after the written exam result is:

1.  **Physical Efficiency Test (PET):** Qualified candidates will be called for the Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST). This stage assesses candidates' physical fitness and endurance through various tasks like running, shot put, and high jump, along with measuring physical standards (height, chest, weight). The dates and admit cards for PET will be announced separately on the official website.
2.  **Document Verification (DV):** Candidates who clear the PET/PST will then undergo Document Verification. This involves verifying all original documents submitted during the application process, such as educational certificates, caste certificates, domicile certificates, identity proof, etc. Any discrepancy found during this stage can lead to disqualification.
3.  **Medical Examination:** The final stage for provisionally selected candidates will be a Medical Examination to ensure they meet the prescribed medical standards for the post of Constable.

Candidates who did not qualify the written examination are encouraged not to be disheartened. This is an opportunity to analyze areas for improvement, prepare more rigorously, and appear for future recruitment cycles. There is generally no provision for re-evaluation or compartment exams for competitive examinations of this nature.
