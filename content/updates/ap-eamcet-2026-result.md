## AP EAMCET 2026 Result — Declared on June 1

JNTU Kakinada, on behalf of APSCHE, has declared the **AP EAMCET 2026 (EAPCET) result on June 1, 2026**. Candidates can download their rank cards from the official website **cets.apsche.ap.gov.in**.

## How to Download AP EAMCET 2026 Rank Card

1. Go to **[cets.apsche.ap.gov.in](https://cets.apsche.ap.gov.in)**
2. Click on **"AP EAMCET 2026 Result / Rank Card"**
3. Enter your **Registration Number / Hall Ticket Number**
4. Enter your **Date of Birth**
5. Enter the **Captcha** code shown
6. Click **Submit**
7. Your rank card appears — download and print it

## What the Rank Card Contains

- Subject-wise marks (Mathematics / Physics / Chemistry / Biology)
- Total marks obtained
- EAMCET rank (overall)
- Category rank
- Qualifying status

## AP EAMCET 2026 Qualifying Marks

| Category | Minimum Qualifying Marks |
|----------|--------------------------|
| General (OC) | 40 out of 160 (25%) |
| OBC / BC | 40 out of 160 (25%) |
| SC | No minimum qualifying mark |
| ST | No minimum qualifying mark |

> SC/ST candidates are ranked and given admission under reserved seats even without a minimum cutoff mark.

## What Happens Next — AP EAMCET 2026 Counselling

Qualified candidates will be called for web-based counselling (online seat allotment) for:
- B.Tech / B.E. Engineering colleges in Andhra Pradesh
- B.Sc Agriculture / Pharmacy / other courses (EAMCET Agriculture stream)

Counselling dates will be announced on **sche.ap.gov.in/EAPCET**. Documents needed: rank card, hall ticket, Class 10 & 12 mark sheets, category certificate (if applicable), Aadhaar card.

## FAQs

**Q: When was AP EAMCET 2026 result declared?**
A: June 1, 2026, by JNTU Kakinada on behalf of APSCHE.

**Q: Where do I download the AP EAMCET 2026 rank card?**
A: Visit cets.apsche.ap.gov.in and log in with your registration number and date of birth.

**Q: What is the qualifying mark for AP EAMCET 2026?**
A: General and OBC candidates need a minimum of 40 out of 160 marks (25%). SC/ST candidates have no minimum qualifying mark.

**Q: I qualified AP EAMCET — what is the next step?**
A: Wait for the APSCHE to announce AP EAMCET 2026 counselling dates on sche.ap.gov.in/EAPCET.

**Q: What documents are needed for AP EAMCET counselling?**
A: EAMCET rank card, hall ticket, Class 10 and 12 certificates and mark sheets, transfer certificate, category certificate (for reserved categories), and Aadhaar card.

**Q: Can I get admission without qualifying AP EAMCET?**
A: No. Qualifying AP EAMCET 2026 is mandatory for admission to engineering and agriculture courses in Andhra Pradesh government and private colleges under AP EAMCET quota.

**Q: Is there a separate result for Engineering and Agriculture streams?**
A: Yes — AP EAMCET has separate ranks for the Engineering (PCM) and Agriculture/Pharmacy (PCB) streams. Your rank card shows the stream-specific rank.
