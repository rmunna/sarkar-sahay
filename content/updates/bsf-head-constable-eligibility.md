BSF Head Constable Ministerial (HCM) 2026 requires candidates to be **18–25 years old** with **12th pass (Intermediate)** qualification. Reserved category candidates get standard age relaxation. A typing speed of **35 WPM in English or 30 WPM in Hindi** is also required for the ministerial (clerical) post.

## Educational Qualification

**Minimum:** 12th pass (Intermediate) or equivalent from a recognised board.

For the HCM (Ministerial) post specifically:
- Computer proficiency — working knowledge of computers is required
- **Typing Speed:** English — 35 WPM minimum; Hindi — 30 WPM minimum
- Candidates who cannot clear the typing test at the skill test stage are not selected

Graduate qualification is preferred but not mandatory. 10th pass candidates are not eligible for HCM — this is different from Constable GD which may accept 10th pass.

## Age Limit

| Category | Minimum Age | Maximum Age |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| General (UR) | 18 | 25 |
| OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) | 18 | 28 (+3 years) |
| SC / ST | 18 | 30 (+5 years) |
| Ex-Servicemen | 18 | 28 (+3 years over UR max, subject to rules) |
| PwD (General) | 18 | 35 (+10 years) |
| PwD (OBC) | 18 | 38 (+13 years) |
| PwD (SC/ST) | 18 | 40 (+15 years) |

*Age calculated as on the cutoff date specified in the official notification. The 2026 notification will specify the exact date.*

## Nationality

Must be an **Indian citizen**. Subjects of Nepal or Bhutan, Tibetan refugees (settled before 1 Jan 1962), and persons of Indian origin from specified countries are also eligible — as per standard BSF/CAPF rules.

## Physical Standards

Physical standards are tested after the written exam:

| Standard | Male | Female |
|----------|------|--------|
| Height | 170 cm (General/OBC) | 157 cm |
| Height (SC/ST, hill regions, NE) | 165 cm | 155 cm |
| Chest (Male only) | 80 cm unexpanded, 85 cm expanded | N/A |
| Weight | Proportionate to height/age | Proportionate |

Medical standards: Candidates must pass a medical examination at a BSF/CAPF medical facility. Vision, hearing, and general fitness are tested.

## Physical Efficiency Test (PET)

| Test | Male | Female |
|------|------|--------|
| Running | 5 km in 24 minutes | 1.6 km in 8.5 minutes |
| Long Jump | 11 feet | N/A |
| High Jump | 3.5 feet | N/A |

PET is qualifying in nature — no marks are awarded. Failure means disqualification from the recruitment.

## Disqualification

Candidates are disqualified if they:
- Have been convicted of any criminal offence
- Were dismissed from any government service
- Have more than one spouse living (unless exempted by government)
- Are engaged in part-time/other government employment without NOC

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can a 26-year-old General category candidate apply for BSF HCM 2026?**
No. The upper age limit for General category is 25 years. If you turned 26 before the application cutoff date, you are ineligible for this recruitment cycle.

**Q: Is the typing test mandatory for all BSF HCM candidates?**
Yes. Typing skill test is a mandatory qualifying stage for Head Constable Ministerial posts. The speed requirement is 35 WPM (English) or 30 WPM (Hindi). Failing this stage means no selection regardless of written exam score.

**Q: Is the BSF HCM age limit the same for all states?**
Yes. BSF is a central government organization — age limits are uniform across all states. Only category-based relaxation applies.
