## How to Check MPSC State Service Result 2026

1. Go to [mpsc.gov.in](https://mpsc.gov.in)
2. Click on **Results** or **Advertisements** section
3. Find the MPSC Rajyaseva State Service 2026 result notification
4. Download the PDF merit list
5. Search your roll number (use Ctrl+F in the PDF)

Alternatively, login to the MPSC candidate portal for individual result and scorecard access.

## Prelims Result — What Was Included

The Prelims result (27 Feb 2026) included:
- Roll numbers of candidates shortlisted for Mains
- Category-wise cutoff marks
- Shortlisting ratio (typically 10–12× vacancies)

Prelims individual marks are released separately on the MPSC portal after the Mains stage.

## Mains Result — What to Expect

The Mains result will include:
- Mark sheets for each paper
- Total aggregate score
- Roll numbers qualified for interview
- Category-wise cutoff for Mains to Interview stage

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: When will MPSC Rajyaseva Mains result 2026 be declared?**
The Mains exam concluded May 2, 2026. Results are typically declared 2–4 months after the Mains exam. Mid-2026 (August–September) is the expected window. Follow mpsc.gov.in for the official announcement.

**Q: How can I check my individual MPSC Mains marks?**
After the Mains result is declared, individual marksheets become available on the MPSC candidate portal (login with registration number). The PDF merit list only shows roll numbers — individual marks require portal login.

**Q: What happens after MPSC Mains result 2026?**
Candidates who qualify the Mains cutoff are called for Interview. Interview is 150 marks for Group A posts and 75 marks for Group B. Final merit list is prepared based on Mains + Interview combined score.
