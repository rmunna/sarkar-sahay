## NORCET 11 Result 2026 Declared by AIIMS New Delhi

**AIIMS, New Delhi has officially released the NORCET 11 Result for 2026 today, September 18, 2026.** This highly anticipated result is for the Nursing Officer Recruitment Common Eligibility Test (NORCET 11), a crucial examination for aspiring Nursing Officers across various AIIMS and other central government hospitals.

Candidates who appeared for NORCET 11 can now access their individual scorecards and check the provisional merit list directly on the official AIIMS Examinations website, `aiimsexams.ac.in`. The declaration of the result marks a significant step towards the recruitment process, which will be followed by document verification and subsequent seat allocation. This update page provides all the essential information you need to check your result, understand the next steps, and clarify common queries.

The NORCET examination is a standardized test conducted by AIIMS, New Delhi, to recruit eligible candidates for the post of Nursing Officer. NORCET 11 2026, like its predecessors, attracts thousands of applicants nationwide, making the result declaration a high-stakes event for many.

## How to Check and Download Your NORCET 11 Result/Scorecard 2026

Follow these simple, step-by-step instructions to access your NORCET 11 Result and download your scorecard from the official AIIMS Examinations website:

1.  **Visit the Official Website:** Open your web browser and navigate to the official AIIMS Examinations portal: `https://www.aiimsexams.ac.in`.
2.  **Navigate to Recruitment Section:** On the homepage, look for the 'Recruitment' or 'Results' tab. Click on it. You might find a direct link on the homepage itself, prominently displayed as 'Result of NORCET 11' or 'NORCET 11 Scorecard'.
3.  **Find NORCET 11 Link:** Within the recruitment section, locate and click on the specific link for 'Nursing Officer Recruitment Common Eligibility Test (NORCET)'. Then, identify the link pertaining to 'NORCET 11 Result 2026' or 'NORCET 11 Scorecard'.
4.  **Login with Credentials:** You will be redirected to a login page. Here, you will need to enter your:
    *   Registration ID
    *   Roll Number
    *   Date of Birth
    *   (Sometimes) a Captcha code for verification.
5.  **View and Download Result:** After successfully logging in, your NORCET 11 scorecard and/or a link to the provisional merit list will be displayed on the screen. Review your details carefully.
6.  **Print/Save Your Scorecard:** It is highly recommended to download the PDF of your scorecard and take a printout for your future reference and for the upcoming document verification process.

*Note: Ensure you have a stable internet connection and access to your registration details before attempting to check your result.*

## What's Next After NORCET 11 Result? Document Verification & Allotment

The declaration of NORCET 11 results is the first major step. Qualified candidates will now proceed to the next stages of the recruitment process. Here’s what you can expect:

1.  **Provisional Merit List:** Along with individual scorecards, AIIMS will release a provisional merit list based on the marks obtained in the CBT. This list will categorize candidates based on their overall rank and category rank.
2.  **Document Verification (DV):** Shortlisted candidates will be called for document verification. This is a critical stage where all the information provided during the application process is cross-checked with original documents. Ensure you have all the necessary certificates ready, including:
    *   NORCET 11 Admit Card
    *   NORCET 11 Scorecard
    *   Date of Birth Certificate (Matriculation Certificate)
    *   Education Qualification Certificates (B.Sc Nursing/GNM mark sheets and degree/diploma certificates)
    *   Experience Certificate (if applicable, for GNM candidates)
    *   Category Certificate (OBC/SC/ST/EWS) issued by a competent authority, if applicable.
    *   PwBD Certificate (if applicable).
    *   Identity Proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, etc.)
    *   No Objection Certificate (for government employees).
    *   Passport-size photographs.
    *   Any other document specified in the original NORCET 11 notification.
3.  **Choice Filling/Counseling:** After successful document verification, candidates will typically be given an opportunity to fill their preferences for the various participating AIIMS and central government hospitals. The seat allocation will be done based on merit, preference, and availability of vacancies in respective categories.
4.  **Final Selection List:** A final selection list will be published after the completion of document verification and counseling/choice filling process. Selected candidates will then receive offer letters for appointment.

Candidates are advised to regularly check the official AIIMS Examinations website (`aiimsexams.ac.in`) for all updates regarding document verification schedules, counseling notices, and final selection lists. Detailed instructions will be provided in subsequent notifications.
