## SSC CGL 2026 Tier 1 Result Declared: Your Gateway to Government Jobs

**New Delhi, [2026-08-10]** – The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has today declared the much-awaited results for the Combined Graduate Level (CGL) Examination 2026, Tier 1. This announcement marks a crucial step for lakhs of aspirants who appeared for one of India's most competitive government job exams. Candidates can now check if they have qualified for the next stage, Tier 2, by downloading the official merit list from the SSC website. 

The SSC CGL Tier 1 2026 examination was conducted between May 10, 2026, and May 25, 2026, across various centers nationwide. The result, released as per the Commission's schedule, includes the list of candidates provisionally shortlisted for SSC CGL Tier 2 2026. This page provides a comprehensive guide on how to check your result, understand the cut-off, and prepare for the subsequent stages of the selection process.

### How to Check and Download Your SSC CGL 2026 Tier 1 Result

Checking your SSC CGL Tier 1 result is a straightforward process. Follow these step-by-step instructions to access your status and the official merit list:

1.  **Visit the Official SSC Website:** Go to the Staff Selection Commission's official portal: [https://ssc.gov.in](https://ssc.gov.in).
2.  **Navigate to the 'Results' Section:** On the homepage, locate and click on the 'Results' tab usually found in the top navigation bar or a prominent section.
3.  **Select CGL Tab:** Within the results section, you will find different examination categories. Click on the 'CGL' tab.
4.  **Find the Relevant Result Link:** Look for the link titled "Combined Graduate Level Examination (Tier-I) 2026 - List of Candidates Shortlisted for appearing in Tier-II" or similar. There might be separate lists for different posts/cut-off categories (e.g., List-1 for A.A.O., List-2 for J.S.O., List-3 for all other posts).
5.  **Download the Result PDF:** Click on the 'Click Here' or 'Result' link next to the relevant notice. This will download a PDF file containing the roll numbers and names of the qualified candidates.
6.  **Search Your Roll Number/Name:** Open the downloaded PDF. Use the search function (Ctrl+F on Windows, Command+F on Mac) to quickly find your roll number or name in the list.

**Note:** Individual marks and final answer keys will be released by the Commission shortly after the result declaration. Keep checking the official website for updates on scorecards.

### Key Information Available in the Result PDF

The SSC CGL Tier 1 2026 result PDF typically contains the following crucial details:

*   **Roll Numbers of Qualified Candidates:** The primary identifier for checking your status.
*   **Names of Qualified Candidates:** For easy verification.
*   **Category-wise Cut-Off Marks:** The minimum marks required to qualify for Tier 2 in each category (UR, OBC, SC, ST, EWS, ESM, OH, HH, VH, PwD-Other).
*   **Number of Candidates Shortlisted:** Total count of aspirants who have cleared Tier 1 for various posts.
*   **Disclaimer:** Important notes regarding the provisional nature of selection and further stages.

### Understanding the SSC CGL Tier 1 Cut-Off Marks 2026

The cut-off marks are the minimum scores candidates must achieve to qualify for the next stage of the examination. The SSC CGL Tier 1 2026 cut-offs are declared category-wise (e.g., General/Unreserved, OBC, SC, ST, EWS, ESM, PwD). Factors influencing the cut-off include the total number of vacancies, the difficulty level of the exam, and the total number of candidates who appeared. Candidates whose scores are equal to or above the declared cut-off for their respective category will find their roll numbers in the merit list.

### What's Next? Preparing for SSC CGL Tier 2 2026

Congratulations to all candidates who have successfully cleared SSC CGL Tier 1 2026! Your journey is far from over. The next crucial stage is the SSC CGL Tier 2 examination. This phase is designed to further assess candidates' comprehensive abilities and is typically more rigorous than Tier 1.

**SSC CGL Tier 2 2026 Exam Pattern (Expected):**
Tier 2 usually consists of different papers, generally including:
*   **Paper-I (Compulsory for all posts):** Mathematical Abilities and Reasoning & General Intelligence.
*   **Paper-II (Compulsory for all posts):** English Language and Comprehension and General Awareness.
*   **Paper-III (For JSO posts only):** Statistics.
*   **Paper-IV (For Assistant Audit Officer/Assistant Accounts Officer posts only):** General Studies (Finance & Economics).

Candidates are advised to immediately start their preparation for Tier 2, focusing on the specific papers relevant to their desired posts. The dates for the SSC CGL Tier 2 2026 exam will be announced by the Commission in due course. Keep an eye on the official SSC website for the latest updates and exam schedule.

### Document Verification and Final Selection

After clearing Tier 2, candidates will generally proceed to Tier 3 (Skill Test/Data Entry Speed Test, wherever applicable) and finally Document Verification (DV). The final selection is made based on the cumulative performance in Tier 2 and Tier 3 (if applicable), followed by the successful verification of all required documents.
