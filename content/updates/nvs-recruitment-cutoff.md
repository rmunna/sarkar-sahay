NVS Recruitment 2026 cut-off marks are published at **navodaya.gov.in** after the result declaration. Cut-offs differ by post (TGT/PGT/PRT), subject (English, Maths, Science etc.), and category (General, OBC, SC, ST, PwD). Based on previous NVS recruitment cycles, General category TGT cut-off typically falls between **110–130 out of 150**, and PGT around **90–115 out of 150**.

## NVS Exam Structure and Marks

Understanding the paper helps interpret cut-offs:

| Paper | Content | Marks | Post |
|-------|---------|-------|------|
| Paper I | Reasoning (20) + GK/GA (20) + Hindi (10) + English (10) | 60 | TGT & PGT |
| Paper II | Subject-specific | 90 | TGT |
| Paper II | Subject-specific | 90 | PGT |
| **Total** | | **150** | |

No negative marking — so cut-offs reflect raw score out of 150.

## What Affects Cut-Off Each Year

1. **Number of vacancies** — more vacancies = lower cut-off
2. **Paper difficulty** — tougher Paper II = lower subject cut-off
3. **Number of applicants** — NVS attracts large pools for popular subjects (Maths, Science)
4. **Regional distribution** — NVS has regional offices; cut-offs may vary by region

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is a safe score in NVS TGT to get selected?**
Based on previous cycles, scoring 120+ out of 150 (General category) for most TGT subjects gives a reasonable chance. Some subjects like Hindi and English have higher competition; others like Physical Education or Work Experience may have lower cut-offs. Aim for maximum marks in your subject paper (Paper II) — that's where most marks come from.

**Q: Where is the official NVS cut-off 2026 published?**
The official cut-off is published at navodaya.gov.in along with the result and merit list. CBSE ITMS (cbseitms.nic.in) may also carry the scorecard with individual marks.

**Q: Does NVS have a separate cut-off for PRT (Primary Teacher)?**
Yes. PRT has a different exam pattern and separate cut-off. PRT typically has a lower total marks structure compared to TGT/PGT. Check the NVS notification for the PRT-specific exam pattern and cut-off trend.
