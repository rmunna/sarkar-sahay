## JEE Main 2026 Session 2 Result — Overview

JEE Main 2026 Session 2 was conducted by NTA in April 2026. The result, which will include NTA scores, percentile, and All India Rank (AIR), is expected to be declared at **jeemain.nta.nic.in** in June–July 2026.

Along with the result, NTA will announce the **JEE Advanced 2026 cut-off** — the minimum percentile required to appear for JEE Advanced and compete for IIT seats. Only the top 2.5 lakh candidates (approximately) from both sessions combined qualify for JEE Advanced.

This page will be updated with the result link, cut-off, and counselling dates as soon as NTA announces them.

## Best of Two Sessions — How Final Rank is Calculated

NTA takes the **better NTA score** from Session 1 and Session 2 for each candidate and uses that for the final merit list. So:
- If you scored higher in Session 1 → Session 1 score counts
- If you scored higher in Session 2 → Session 2 score counts
- The final AIR is based on this best-of-two score

## What Happens After JEE Main 2026 Result

### Path 1 — JEE Advanced 2026 (for IITs)
Candidates who meet the cut-off percentile must separately register for JEE Advanced 2026 at [jeeadv.ac.in](https://jeeadv.ac.in). JEE Advanced registration typically opens 1–2 weeks after JEE Main result.

### Path 2 — JoSAA Counselling (for NITs, IIITs, GFTIs)
All qualified candidates can participate in **JoSAA (Joint Seat Allocation Authority)** counselling for:
- 31 NITs
- 26 IIITs
- 33 Government Funded Technical Institutes (GFTIs)

JoSAA counselling begins typically 1–2 weeks after JEE Advanced.

### Path 3 — State Engineering Counselling
Many state engineering colleges and CSAB (Central Seat Allocation Board) use JEE Main scores. Check your state's counselling authority.

## FAQs

**Q1. When will JEE Main 2026 Session 2 result be declared?**
A1. NTA typically declares JEE Main Session 2 results within 15–20 days of the last exam date. The exact date has not been announced — check jeemain.nta.nic.in.

**Q2. How is the JEE Main 2026 NTA score calculated?**
A2. NTA uses equi-percentile normalisation to adjust for different difficulty levels across shifts and sessions. The NTA score is NOT the raw marks — it's a percentile-based score on a scale of 100.

**Q3. What percentile is needed to qualify for JEE Advanced 2026?**
A3. Approximately 88–92 percentile for General category. The exact cut-off is announced with the result. Top ~2.5 lakh candidates from JEE Main qualify.

**Q4. Can I appear for JEE Main 2026 Session 2 if I missed Session 1?**
A4. Yes. Session 1 and Session 2 are independent. If you only appeared in Session 2, your Session 2 score is your final score.

**Q5. Is JEE Main 2026 score valid for 2027 admissions?**
A5. No. JEE Main scores are valid only for the current year's admission cycle (2026–27 academic year).

**Q6. How many attempts are allowed in JEE Main?**
A6. Candidates can appear for JEE Main for 3 consecutive years after passing Class 12. Each year has 2 sessions, giving up to 6 attempts total.
