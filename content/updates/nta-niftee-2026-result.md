## How to Check Your Result Online

Checking your NIFTEE 2026 result online is a straightforward process designed for ease of access. Follow the detailed steps below to view and download your scorecard:

1.  **Visit the Official NTA Website:** Open your web browser and navigate to the official website of the National Testing Agency: [www.nta.ac.in](https://www.nta.ac.in).
2.  **Locate the Results Section:** On the homepage, look for a prominent link or section dedicated to "Latest @ NTA" or "Results." This section is usually updated with recent announcements.
3.  **Find NIFTEE 2026 Result Link:** Within the results section, search for the specific link titled "NIFTEE 2026 Result" or "NIFTEE Scorecard 2026." Click on this link to proceed.
4.  **Enter Your Credentials:** You will be redirected to a login page. Here, you will need to enter your unique identification details. Typically, this includes your:
    *   Application Number / Roll Number
    *   Date of Birth (DD/MM/YYYY format)
    *   Security Pin (a captcha code displayed on the screen to verify you are not a robot)
5.  **Submit and View Result:** After entering all the required information accurately, click on the "Submit" or "Login" button. Your NIFTEE 2026 scorecard will be displayed on the screen.
6.  **Download and Print:** Carefully review all the details on your scorecard. It is highly recommended to download the result in PDF format and take multiple printouts for future reference and admission formalities. Keep these copies safe.

In case of any difficulty accessing the result, ensure your internet connection is stable and that you are entering the correct credentials as per your admit card.

For competitive entrance examinations conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA), including NIFTEE, the provision for re-evaluation or re-checking of answer sheets is generally **not available**.
