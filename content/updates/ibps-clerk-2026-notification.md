## What is IBPS Clerk 2026?

The Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) conducts the Clerical Recruitment Examination (CRP Clerks) annually to recruit clerks for 11 participating public sector banks across India. IBPS Clerk is one of the most popular banking exams, ideal for graduates seeking a stable government banking career.

## Eligibility Criteria

- **Nationality:** Indian citizen
- **Age Limit:** 20–28 years (relaxation: 5 years SC/ST, 3 years OBC, 10 years PwD)
- **Educational Qualification:** Bachelor's degree from a recognized university in any discipline
- **Computer Literacy:** Candidate should have a Certificate/Diploma/Degree in computer operations/language OR studied computer as a subject in school/college
- **Language Proficiency:** Proficiency in the official language of the state/UT applying for

## How to Apply Online

1. Visit [ibps.in](https://www.ibps.in/) and find the Clerk CRP notification
2. Click "New Registration" and enter basic details
3. Log in and fill the complete application form
4. Choose your preferred states/banks for posting
5. Upload photo and signature
6. Pay the application fee
7. Submit and take a printout

## Selection Process

1. **Preliminary Examination:** Online objective test for shortlisting.
2. **Main Examination:** Online objective test for final merit.
3. **Provisional Allotment:** Based on Mains score, reservation, and preference.

*No interview for Clerk posts.*

## Syllabus (Brief Overview)

**Reasoning:** Puzzles, Seating Arrangement, Syllogism, Inequality, Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations, Direction, Alphanumeric Series, Computer Aptitude (Mains).

**Quantitative Aptitude:** Number Series, Simplification, Approximation, Data Interpretation, Quadratic Equations, Arithmetic (Percentage, Ratio, Profit/Loss, Time & Work).

**English:** Reading Comprehension, Cloze Test, Error Spotting, Phrase Replacement, Para Jumbles.

**General/Financial Awareness:** Banking Terms, RBI Policies, Financial Institutions, Government Schemes, Current Affairs (last 5–6 months), Static GK.

## Preparation Tips

1. **Focus on state-level language skills** — You need proficiency in the local language of the state you're applying for.
2. **Master Prelims first** — Prelims cut-offs are often high; scoring well here is essential.
3. **Banking and financial awareness** — This is unique to Mains; study banking operations, RBI guidelines, and financial news.
4. **Speed and accuracy** — Each section is timed individually; practice with timers.
5. **Previous year papers** — IBPS Clerk patterns are predictable; solve at least 5 years of papers.

## FAQs

### Q: What is the salary of an IBPS Clerk?
**A:** IBPS Clerk starts at a basic pay of ₹19,900. In-hand salary is approximately ₹26,000–₹35,000 depending on the posting city and allowances.

### Q: Is there an interview for IBPS Clerk?
**A:** No, there is no interview for IBPS Clerk. Selection is purely based on Prelims (qualifying) and Mains (merit).

### Q: Can I apply for multiple states in IBPS Clerk?
**A:** No, you can apply for only one state. You must know the local/official language of that state.

### Q: What is the local language requirement?
**A:** You should be proficient in the official language of the state/UT you apply for. This may be tested during document verification or posting.

### Q: Is there any bond or service agreement?
**A:** Some banks may require a minimum service period. Details vary by bank and are communicated during allotment.

### Q: Can I appear for both IBPS PO and IBPS Clerk?
**A:** Yes, you can appear for both exams. They are separate recruitments with independent processes.
