## What is SBI SCO Recruitment 2026?

State Bank of India (SBI) has released the Specialist Cadre Officers (SCO) Recruitment 2026 notification (Advertisement No: CRPD/SCO/2025-26/27) for 116 posts of Assistant Vice President and Deputy Manager. The recruitment is for IS Audit and Chartered Accountancy profiles in internal audit, cyber security, and related fields.

| Interview Date | To be announced |

## Eligibility Criteria

### Educational Qualification (as on 31 January 2026)

- **AVP & DM (IS Audit):** BE/BTech/BSc in Computer Science/IT/Electronics (min 50%) OR BCA/MCA OR MTech/MSc in relevant fields (min 50%). Preferred certifications: CISA, CISSP, CISM, DISA, ISO 27001 LA, CEH, GIAC
- **DM (CA — Internal Audit):** Chartered Accountant (CA) with active ICAI membership. Preferred: CISA, DISA

### Experience (post-qualification, as on 31 January 2026)

- **AVP (IS Audit):** Minimum 6 years in BFSI/IT/InfoSec Consultancy, with 3 years in IS Audit/Cyber Security Audit
- **DM (IS Audit):** Minimum 3 years in BFSI/IT/InfoSec Consultancy, with 2 years in IS Audit/Cyber Security Audit
- **DM (CA — Internal Audit):** Minimum 1 year in Statutory/Internal/Concurrent Audit of Banks or with CA firm in bank audits

### Age Limit (as on 31 January 2026)

| Post | Minimum Age | Maximum Age |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| AVP (IS Audit) | 27 years | 45 years |
| DM (IS Audit) | 25 years | 35 years |
| DM (CA — Internal Audit) | 21 years | 35 years |

Age relaxation: SC/ST — 5 years, OBC (NCL) — 3 years, PwBD — 10 years (additional for reserved categories).

## How to Apply Online

1. Visit [SBI Careers](https://sbi.co.in/web/careers/current-openings)
2. Click on the SCO recruitment advertisement link
3. Register with valid email ID and mobile number
4. Fill in personal, educational, and experience details
5. Upload required documents — photograph, signature, resume, ID proof, DOB proof, educational certificates, experience certificates, Form-16/salary slip
6. Pay the application fee online
7. Submit and print the confirmation page

## Selection Process

- **Shortlisting** based on application and eligibility criteria
- **Interview** (100 marks) — qualifying marks decided by the Bank
- **CTC Negotiation** for AVP (Contractual) post after interview
- **Merit list** based on interview scores (age as tie-breaker)
- **Document verification and medical examination**

No written exam is required. Call letters will be sent via email only.

## Pay Scale / CTC

| Deputy Manager (Regular) — MMGS-II | ₹64,820 – ₹93,960 (plus DA, HRA, PF, NPS, LFC, Medical, etc.) |

Probation period: 1 year for regular posts.

## Job Location

Mumbai, Hyderabad, Circle Audit Offices, or Mobile Duty as assigned by the Bank.

## Preparation Tips

1. **Brush up on IS Audit concepts** — focus on information security frameworks, SOC operations, and risk assessment methodologies
2. **Review banking technology** — understand core banking systems, digital banking, and cyber security challenges in BFSI
3. **Prepare for behavioural interview questions** — SBI interviews assess domain knowledge and situational judgement
4. **Get certified if possible** — CISA, DISA, or ISO 27001 certifications significantly strengthen your profile
5. **For CA candidates** — revise bank audit procedures, RBI guidelines, and statutory compliance frameworks

## FAQs

### Q: What is the last date to apply for SBI SCO 2026?
A: The last date to submit the online application is 15 March 2026.

A: There are 116 total vacancies — 12 for AVP (IS Audit), 43 for DM (IS Audit), and 61 for DM (CA — Internal Audit).
