## What is NEET UG 2026?

NEET UG (National Eligibility cum Entrance Test - Undergraduate) is India's single largest medical entrance exam, conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA). It is mandatory for admission to MBBS, BDS, BAMS, BHMS, BVSc, and other undergraduate medical courses in government and private colleges across India.

NEET UG 2026 covers seats in 542 medical colleges, 313 dental colleges, 914 AYUSH colleges, and 47 BVSc & AH colleges. **Registration is currently open and closes on March 8, 2026.** The exam is scheduled for **May 3, 2026** in pen-and-paper (OMR) mode.

## Eligibility Criteria

- **Age:** Minimum 17 years as on December 31, 2026. No upper age limit.
- **Education:** Passed or appearing in Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Biotechnology, and English as core subjects
- **Minimum Marks in Class 12:** 50% aggregate in PCB (General), 40% (SC/ST/OBC), 45% (PwD)
- **Nationality:** Indian nationals, NRIs, OCIs, PIOs, and foreign nationals

## How to Apply Online

1. Visit the official website at [neet.nta.nic.in](https://neet.nta.nic.in/)
2. Click on "New Registration" and fill in your basic details (name, father's name, contact number, email)
3. Note your registration number and set a secure password
4. Verify via OTP sent to your registered mobile number
5. Log in and fill personal, educational, and residential details
6. Upload photograph, signature, and required documents in specified format
7. Select 4 exam centre preferences
8. Pay the application fee online
9. Submit the form and print the confirmation page for your records

## Selection Process

1. **NEET UG Exam** — Pen-and-paper OMR-based test
2. **NEET Score & All India Rank** — Based on exam performance
3. **Counselling** — Through MCC (Medical Counselling Committee) for All India Quota seats and State Counselling Authorities for state quota seats

## Syllabus Overview

NEET UG syllabus is based on Class 11 and 12 NCERT:

- **Physics:** Mechanics, Thermodynamics, Waves, Optics, Electrostatics, Current Electricity, Magnetism, Modern Physics
- **Chemistry:** Physical Chemistry (Mole Concept, Thermodynamics, Equilibrium), Organic Chemistry, Inorganic Chemistry
- **Biology:** Cell Biology, Genetics, Ecology, Human Physiology, Plant Physiology, Biotechnology

## Preparation Tips

1. **NCERT is your bible** — Over 90% of NEET questions come directly from NCERT textbooks, especially Biology
2. **Biology carries the most weight** — 360 out of 720 marks. Master NCERT diagrams and definitions
3. **Practice OMR-based tests** — NEET is pen-and-paper, not online. Practice filling OMR sheets under timed conditions
4. **Previous year papers** — Solve at least 10 years of NEET previous papers to understand the pattern
5. **Start revision now** — With the exam on May 3, focus on revision and mock tests rather than new topics

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: What is the last date to apply for NEET UG 2026?

The last date to submit the application form and pay the fee is March 8, 2026 (9:00 PM). A correction window is available from March 10–12, 2026.

### Q: When is the NEET UG 2026 exam?

NEET UG 2026 is scheduled for May 3, 2026 (Sunday) in a single shift across India.

### Q: Is NEET UG conducted online or offline?

NEET UG is conducted in pen-and-paper (OMR) mode. It is NOT a computer-based test.

### Q: What is the minimum age to appear for NEET UG 2026?

Candidates must be at least 17 years old as on December 31, 2026. There is no upper age limit.

### Q: Can I apply for NEET UG 2026 if I am appearing in Class 12 this year?

Yes. Candidates appearing in Class 12 in 2026 are eligible to apply, provided they meet the minimum marks criteria when results are declared.

### Q: How many marks are needed to qualify NEET UG?

The qualifying percentile is 50th percentile for General category, 45th for PwD, and 40th for SC/ST/OBC. Exact cutoff marks vary each year.
