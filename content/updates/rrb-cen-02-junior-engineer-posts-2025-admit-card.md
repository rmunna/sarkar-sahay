## RRB CEN 02/2025 Junior Engineer Admit Card — Overview

The Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) has released the admit cards for the CEN 02/2025 Junior Engineer (JE) recruitment examination. Candidates who successfully applied for the RRB CEN 02/2025 Junior Engineer posts can now download their admit cards from the official website of the Railway Recruitment Board.

The admit card is a mandatory document for appearing in the examination. It contains crucial information such as the candidate's name, roll number, examination date, time, and venue details. The examination for the Junior Engineer posts under CEN 02/2025 is scheduled for 2026-06-05. Candidates are advised to download their admit cards well in advance of the exam date to avoid any last-minute issues.

The admit card serves as proof of eligibility to enter the examination hall and must be presented along with a valid photo identity proof. Without a valid admit card and photo ID, candidates will not be permitted to take the examination.

## How to Download RRB CEN 02/2025 Junior Engineer Admit Card

Candidates can follow the simple steps given below to download their admit card for the RRB CEN 02/2025 Junior Engineer examination:

1.  **Visit the Official Website:** Go to the official website of the Railway Recruitment Board at [www.rrbcdg.gov.in](https://www.rrbcdg.gov.in).
2.  **Locate Admit Card Link:** On the homepage, look for a link related to "CEN 02/2025 Junior Engineer Admit Card Download" or "e-Call Letter for JE Posts." This link is typically found in the "Latest Updates" or "Notifications" section.
3.  **Enter Credentials:** Click on the admit card link. You will be redirected to a login page where you need to enter your Registration Number and Date of Birth (or Password) as required.
4.  **Submit and View:** After entering the details, click on the "Login" or "Submit" button. Your RRB CEN 02/2025 Junior Engineer Admit Card will be displayed on the screen.
5.  **Download and Print:** Carefully check all the details on the admit card. Download the admit card and take a clear printout. It is advisable to print multiple copies for safety.
