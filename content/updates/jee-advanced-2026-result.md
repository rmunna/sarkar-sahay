## JEE Advanced 2026 Result — Declared on June 1

IIT Roorkee has officially declared the JEE Advanced 2026 result on **June 1, 2026**. Candidates who appeared in both Paper 1 and Paper 2 on May 17, 2026 can now download their scorecards from **jeeadv.ac.in**.

## Key Highlights

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| Result date | June 1, 2026 |
| Exam date | May 17, 2026 |
| Conducting body | IIT Roorkee |
| Total candidates appeared | 1,79,694 |
| Total qualified | 56,880 |
| Female candidates qualified | 10,107 |
| AIR 1 (CRL) | Shubham Kumar — IIT Delhi Zone |
| AIR 1 marks | 330 / 360 |
| Official website | jeeadv.ac.in |

## How to Check JEE Advanced 2026 Result

1. Visit **[jeeadv.ac.in](https://jeeadv.ac.in)**
2. Click the **"Result 2026"** link on the homepage
3. Enter your **JEE Advanced Registration Number**
4. Enter your **Date of Birth**
5. Enter your **Registered Mobile Number**
6. Click **Submit** — your scorecard appears
7. Download and save the PDF

> **No password needed** — only registration number, date of birth, and mobile number are required to log in.

## What the Scorecard Shows

- Paper 1 and Paper 2 marks — subject-wise (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics)
- Total aggregate marks (out of 360)
- Common Rank List (CRL) rank
- Category rank (OBC-NCL / EWS / SC / ST / PwD)
- Qualifying status

## JEE Advanced 2026 Cutoff — Category Wise

Candidates must score the minimum percentage in **each subject individually** AND in the aggregate:

| Category | Minimum % per subject | Minimum % aggregate |
|----------|-----------------------|---------------------|
| General (CRL) | 25.56% | 25.56% |
| GEN-EWS | 22.78% | 22.78% |
| OBC-NCL | 22.78% | 22.78% |
| SC | 12.78% | 12.78% |
| ST | 12.78% | 12.78% |
| PwD | 12.78% | 12.78% |

> The cutoff is the **minimum to appear in the rank list**, not a guarantee of IIT admission. Your actual branch and institute depend on your rank and JoSAA choices.

## What Happens Next — JoSAA 2026 Counselling

Qualified candidates will participate in **JoSAA counselling** for admission to:
- 23 IITs
- IISc Bangalore
- NITs, IIITs, GFTIs (via JEE Main rank)

JoSAA 2026 registration dates are yet to be announced. Check **josaa.nic.in** for updates.
