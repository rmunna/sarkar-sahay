## COMEDK UGET 2026 Results Declared: Check Your Rank Card Now!

The Consortium of Medical, Engineering and Dental Colleges of Karnataka (COMEDK) has officially declared the results for the COMEDK Undergraduate Entrance Test (UGET) 2026 today, May 29, 2026. Candidates who appeared for the entrance examination on May 19, 2026, can now access their individual scorecards and rank cards directly from the official COMEDK website. This highly anticipated result marks a crucial step for aspiring engineering and architecture students seeking admission to various private unaided engineering and architecture colleges in Karnataka.

The COMEDK UGET is conducted annually to facilitate admissions to over 150 engineering colleges and some architecture colleges in Karnataka. The examination assesses candidates' proficiency in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (for engineering) and an aptitude test (for architecture). With the declaration of results, candidates can now determine their eligibility and participate in the subsequent counselling process based on their ranks.

### How to Download Your COMEDK UGET 2026 Score Card and Rank Card

Follow these simple steps to check and download your COMEDK UGET 2026 result and rank card:

1.  **Visit the Official Website:** Go to [comedk.org](https://www.comedk.org/).
2.  **Locate the Result Link:** On the homepage, look for the "COMEDK UGET 2026 Result" or "Applicant Login" link. This is usually prominently displayed under the latest announcements section.
3.  **Click on the Link:** Click on the designated result link. You will be redirected to the applicant login portal at [cdn.comedk.org](https://cdn.comedk.org/).
4.  **Enter Your Credentials:** Enter your Application Sequence Number/User ID and Password as provided during registration.
5.  **View Your Result:** After logging in, your COMEDK UGET 2026 Score Card and Rank Card will be displayed on the screen.
6.  **Download and Print:** Download the rank card and take multiple printouts for future reference and for the counselling process.

It is crucial to keep your rank card safe, as it is a mandatory document for the counselling and seat allotment process.

### Details Mentioned on Your COMEDK UGET 2026 Rank Card

Your COMEDK UGET 2026 Rank Card will contain essential information, including:

*   Candidate's Name
*   Application Sequence Number
*   Candidate's Date of Birth
*   Category
*   COMEDK UGET 2026 Score (Subject-wise and Total)
*   COMEDK UGET 2026 Rank
*   Exam Session Details
*   Important instructions for counselling

Verify all these details carefully. In case of any discrepancy, contact the COMEDK helpline immediately.

### What's Next? COMEDK UGET 2026 Counselling Process

The declaration of results is the first major step. The next critical stage for qualified candidates is the COMEDK UGET 2026 counselling process. The counselling will be conducted online and will involve several stages:

1.  **Online Registration for Counselling:** Candidates who have qualified and wish to participate in the counselling must register online on the COMEDK portal. This typically involves paying a counselling fee.
2.  **Document Upload and Verification:** Candidates will be required to upload scanned copies of their original documents (such as mark sheets, identity proof, caste certificate if applicable, etc.) for online verification.
3.  **Choice Filling:** Based on their COMEDK UGET rank, candidates will be able to fill in their preferred choices of colleges and courses. It is advisable to research colleges and programs thoroughly before making choices.
4.  **Mock Allotment:** COMEDK usually conducts a mock allotment round to give candidates an idea of the seats they might get based on their filled choices and ranks. This helps candidates refine their final choices.
5.  **Seat Allotment:** Actual seat allotment will be done in multiple rounds based on merit (rank), preferences filled, and availability of seats.
6.  **Reporting to Allotted College:** Candidates who are allotted a seat and wish to accept it must download their allotment letter and report to the respective college within the stipulated time frame with all original documents and pay the admission fee.

Detailed schedules for COMEDK UGET 2026 counselling, including registration dates, document verification timelines, and choice filling dates, will be released shortly on the official website. Candidates are strongly advised to regularly check [comedk.org](https://www.comedk.org/) for the latest updates.

### Important Note for Architecture Aspirants (COMEDK UGET 2026 & NATA)

For admissions to B.Arch courses through COMEDK, candidates must have qualified in NATA (National Aptitude Test in Architecture) conducted by the Council of Architecture (COA) or a JEE Main Paper 2 score. The COMEDK UGET 2026 exam itself is for Engineering admissions. B.Arch admissions are based on NATA/JEE Main Paper 2 scores and academic performance. Candidates seeking B.Arch admissions through COMEDK should ensure they meet these additional criteria.
