## SSC CGL 2025 Tier-II Answer Key — Overview

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has officially released the Provisional Answer Key for the Combined Graduate Level (CGL) Examination 2025 Tier-II. This is a crucial document for all candidates who appeared for the Tier-II examination, as it allows them to cross-verify their responses with the officially correct answers.

The answer key is typically released in a provisional format first, allowing candidates to review it and, if necessary, raise objections against any discrepancies they find. SSC usually releases the answer keys along with the candidate's individual response sheets. Candidates can log in using their credentials to access their specific response sheet and the corresponding master answer key for their examination shift and paper. This process helps maintain transparency and allows candidates to estimate their probable scores even before the official results are declared.

The provisional answer key serves as an essential tool for candidates to:
*   **Estimate Scores:** By comparing their marked answers with the official key, candidates can calculate their raw scores.
*   **Identify Mistakes:** It helps in understanding where errors were made, which can be valuable for future exam preparation.
*   **Raise Objections:** If a candidate believes an answer provided in the key is incorrect, they have a window to challenge it with proper justification.

Following the objection period, SSC reviews all challenges and, if valid, makes necessary corrections before releasing the Final Answer Key. The final answer key is then used for the preparation of the official results.

## How to Calculate Your Score

Calculating your probable score for the SSC CGL 2025 Tier-II examination is straightforward once you have downloaded your response sheet and the provisional answer key. The Staff Selection Commission generally follows a specific marking scheme for its examinations.

For SSC CGL Tier-II, the typical marking scheme is as follows:
*   **Correct Answer:** For every correct answer, **+3 marks** will be awarded.
*   **Incorrect Answer:** For every incorrect answer, **-1 mark** will be deducted (negative marking).
*   **Unattempted Questions:** No marks will be awarded or deducted for questions that are not attempted.

**Steps to Calculate Your Score:**

1.  **Count Correct Answers:** Go through your response sheet and the provisional answer key. Mark each question where your answer matches the official key as correct. Count the total number of correct answers.
2.  **Count Incorrect Answers:** Similarly, mark each question where your answer does not match the official key as incorrect. Count the total number of incorrect answers.
3.  **Apply the Formula:** Use the following formula to calculate your raw score:

    **Raw Score = (Number of Correct Answers × 3) - (Number of Incorrect Answers × 1)**

    *Example:* If you attempted 100 questions, got 80 correct, and 20 incorrect:
    Raw Score = (80 × 3) - (20 × 1)
    Raw Score = 240 - 20
    Raw Score = 220

It is important to note that this calculated score is a raw score. The final scores may be subject to normalization by SSC, especially if the examination was conducted in multiple shifts with varying difficulty levels. Always refer to the official notification for any specific changes in the marking scheme for the current examination cycle.

## How to Raise an Objection / Challenge

The Staff Selection Commission provides a window for candidates to challenge the Provisional Answer Key if they find any discrepancies or believe an answer provided by SSC is incorrect. This process ensures fairness and accuracy in the evaluation.

Here’s how you can raise an objection:

1.  **Access the Objection Portal:** After downloading your answer key and response sheet, the link to raise objections will be available on the same portal where you logged in to download the answer key.
2.  **Identify the Question:** Clearly identify the question number(s) you wish to challenge.
3.  **Provide Justification:** For each objection, you must provide a clear and concise justification. This typically involves citing standard textbooks, official government reports, or other credible sources that support your claim for the correct answer. Screenshots or links to supporting documents may be required.
4.  **Pay the Objection Fee:** A non-refundable fee is usually charged per question challenged. As per previous trends, this fee is typically ₹100 per question. You will need to pay this fee online through the designated payment gateway. Objections without the required fee will not be considered.
5.  **Submit Objection:** Review your objection(s) and supporting documents carefully before final submission. Once submitted, you will usually receive a confirmation.

**Important Points to Remember:**
*   **Deadline:** Objections can only be submitted within a specified timeframe. Challenges received after the deadline will not be entertained. The exact dates for raising objections will be announced along with the release of the provisional answer key.
*   **Review by Experts:** All submitted objections are reviewed by subject matter experts appointed by SSC.
*   **Final Answer Key:** Based on the review, if an objection is found to be valid, the answer key will be revised, and a Final Answer Key will be released. The decision of the Commission in this regard will be final.
