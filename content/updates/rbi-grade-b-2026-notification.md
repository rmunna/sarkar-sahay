## What is RBI Grade B 2026?

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) recruits Grade B Officers (Direct Recruit — DR) through a competitive examination. RBI Grade B is one of the most prestigious exams in the banking and finance sector, offering a career as an officer in India's central bank. Officers work across departments like monetary policy, financial regulation, banking supervision, and more.

## Eligibility Criteria

- **Nationality:** Indian citizen
- **Age Limit:** 21–30 years (relaxation as per government norms: 5 years SC/ST, 3 years OBC, 10 years PwD)
- **Educational Qualification:**
  - **General DR:** Minimum 60% in Bachelor's degree (55% for SC/ST/PwD) OR Postgraduate degree/professional qualification (CA, CS, ICWA, MBA, etc.)
  - **DEPR:** Master's degree in Economics with minimum 55%
  - **DSIM:** Master's degree in Statistics/Mathematical Statistics/Applied Statistics with minimum 55%

## Selection Process

1. **Phase-I (Online Objective):** Screening test — shortlisting for Phase-II.
2. **Phase-II (Online Objective + Descriptive):** Paper-I (Objective — Economic & Social Issues), Paper-II (Descriptive — English Writing), Paper-III (Objective — Finance & Management).
3. **Interview:** 75 marks.

Final merit = Phase-II + Interview scores.

## Syllabus (Brief Overview)

**Phase-I:** Similar to banking exams — GA, Quant, English, Reasoning. Higher focus on economic affairs and RBI functions.

**Phase-II Paper-I (Economic & Social Issues):** Growth & Development, Economic Reforms, Globalization, Social Structure, Issues of Employment, Poverty, Inflation, Fiscal Policy, Monetary Policy.

**Phase-II Paper-II (English):** Essay Writing, Précis Writing, Comprehension, Business/Office Communication.

**Phase-II Paper-III (Finance & Management):** Financial System, Financial Markets, Public Finance, Corporate Governance, Management Principles, HR, Strategic Management, Marketing.

## Preparation Tips

1. **Study economics deeply** — Phase-II is heavily economics and finance-focused. Read Economic Survey, RBI Annual Report.
2. **Read RBI publications** — RBI Bulletin, Financial Stability Report, Monetary Policy statements. These are gold mines.
3. **Practice descriptive English** — Phase-II Paper-II (English) requires essay and précis writing skills.
4. **Build conceptual clarity in finance** — Focus on banking regulation, NPA management, fintech, digital payments.
5. **Follow current economic events** — Budget, trade policy, inflation data, GDP growth — all are frequently tested.

## FAQs

### Q: What is the salary of an RBI Grade B Officer?
**A:** RBI Grade B Officers start at approximately ₹55,200 basic pay. The total CTC including allowances, perks (housing, medical, pension contributions) is estimated at ₹18–22 LPA, making it one of the highest-paying government jobs.

### Q: How is RBI Grade B different from IBPS PO?
**A:** RBI Grade B is an officer position in India's central bank (RBI), while IBPS PO recruits for commercial public sector banks. RBI Grade B is higher in seniority, has a significantly higher salary, and focuses on policy/regulation rather than retail banking.

### Q: Is work experience required for RBI Grade B?
**A:** No, work experience is not mandatory. Fresh graduates who meet the educational qualification can apply.

### Q: How many attempts are allowed?
**A:** There is no limit on attempts as long as you are within the age limit.

### Q: What is the posting location for RBI Grade B officers?
**A:** RBI has offices across India. Officers can be posted at RBI's central office (Mumbai), regional offices, or training establishments. Transfers are possible.

### Q: Can CA/MBA holders apply directly?
**A:** Yes, professionals with qualifications like CA, CS, ICWA, MBA from recognized institutions are eligible. Having these qualifications can also help in Phase-II preparation.
