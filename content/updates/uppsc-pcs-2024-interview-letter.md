## UPPSC PCS 2024 Interview Letter Released

The Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission (UPPSC) has released interview letters for the **Combined State/Upper Subordinate Services Examination 2024** (commonly known as UPPSC PCS 2024). Candidates who cleared the written examination and are shortlisted for the interview/personality test can now download their interview admit card from the official UPPSC website.

The interview letter is available for download at **uppsc.up.nic.in** and will remain accessible until **20 March 2026**.

## How to Download UPPSC PCS 2024 Interview Letter

Follow these steps to download your interview admit card:

1. Visit the official UPPSC website at [https://uppsc.up.nic.in](https://uppsc.up.nic.in).
2. On the homepage, look for the **"Interview Letter"** or **"Admit Card"** section.
3. Click on the link for **Combined State/Upper Subordinate Services Exam 2024 Interview Letter**.
4. Enter your **Registration Number** and **Date of Birth** (or other required credentials).
5. Click **Submit** to view your interview letter.
6. Verify all details on the admit card — name, photo, interview date, and venue.
7. **Download and print** at least two copies of the interview letter for your records.

## Important Details for Interview

### Documents to Carry

Candidates appearing for the UPPSC PCS 2024 interview must carry the following documents:

- **Interview letter / admit card** (printed copy)
- **Photo ID proof** (Aadhaar, PAN card, Voter ID, or Passport)
- **Original certificates** — educational qualifications, category certificate (if applicable), domicile certificate
- **Passport-size photographs** (as specified in the interview letter)
- **Prelims and Mains admit cards** (if required)

### General Guidelines

- Arrive at the interview venue well before the reporting time mentioned on the admit card.
- Ensure all details on the interview letter are correct. If there are any discrepancies, contact UPPSC immediately.
- Carry original documents along with self-attested photocopies.
- Mobile phones and electronic devices may not be permitted inside the interview venue.

## About UPPSC PCS Exam

The UPPSC PCS exam is conducted annually by the Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission for recruitment to various Group A and Group B services in the state government. The selection process consists of three stages:

1. **Preliminary Examination** (Objective)
2. **Main Examination** (Descriptive)

## FAQs

### How can I download my UPPSC PCS 2024 interview letter?
Visit [uppsc.up.nic.in](https://uppsc.up.nic.in), navigate to the interview letter section, enter your registration number and date of birth, and download the admit card.

### Until when is the interview letter available for download?
The interview letter is available for download until **20 March 2026**. Download it as soon as possible to avoid last-minute issues.

### What should I do if there is an error on my interview letter?
Contact UPPSC immediately through their official helpline or visit the UPPSC office in Prayagraj with supporting documents to get the error corrected before your interview date.

### Is the interview letter mandatory for appearing in the interview?
Yes, the interview letter / admit card is a mandatory document. You will not be allowed entry to the interview venue without it.

### What is the interview process for UPPSC PCS 2024?
The interview is a personality test where a panel evaluates the candidate's suitability for civil services. It typically assesses general awareness, communication skills, decision-making ability, and overall personality.
