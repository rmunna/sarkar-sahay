### How to Check Your Raj BCI 2026 Result

Follow these simple steps to download your scorecard and check your result for the Rajasthan Basic Computer Instructor 2026 examination:

1.  **Visit the Official Website:** Open your web browser and navigate to the official website of the Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB) at [https://rsmssb.rajasthan.gov.in/](https://rsmssb.rajasthan.gov.in/).
2.  **Locate the 'Results' Section:** On the homepage, look for the 'Results' or 'Latest News' section. This is usually prominently displayed.
3.  **Find the Result Link:** Search for the specific link titled "Basic Computer Instructor 2026 Result" or "BCI 2026 Result". The link might also include the notice number.
4.  **Click and Proceed:** Click on the result link. You might be redirected to a new page or a PDF document will open directly.
5.  **Enter Credentials (if prompted):** If a login page appears, you will need to enter your Application Number/Roll Number and Date of Birth (DD/MM/YYYY) as requested. Some results are released as a consolidated PDF list where you search your roll number.
6.  **View Your Result:** After entering the required details, submit them. Your Raj BCI 2026 scorecard or your name/roll number in the merit list will be displayed on the screen.
7.  **Download and Print:** It is highly recommended to download your result/scorecard and take a printout for future reference and for the upcoming document verification process.

Alternatively, you may also be able to access your result via the Rajasthan Single Sign-On (SSO) portal at [https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in/](https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in/) by logging in with your credentials and navigating to the recruitment section.

### Understanding the Cut-Off Marks

The RSSB will release category-wise cut-off marks for the Basic Computer Instructor 2026 examination. These cut-off marks represent the minimum scores required for candidates to qualify for the next stage of the selection process. Factors influencing the cut-off include:

*   Total number of vacancies
*   Number of candidates who appeared for the exam
*   Difficulty level of the examination
*   Reservation policies

Candidates whose scores meet or exceed these cut-off marks will be provisionally shortlisted for Document Verification (DV).

### What's Next? Document Verification (DV) & Counseling

Successfully qualified candidates in the written examination will be called for the Document Verification (DV) stage. This is a crucial step to confirm the authenticity of the information provided during the application process. The RSSB will release a detailed schedule and list of required documents for DV. Typically, candidates will need to present:

*   Original educational mark sheets and certificates (10th, 12th, Graduation, Computer Degree/Diploma)
*   Caste Certificate (if applicable)
*   Domicile Certificate
*   Photo ID proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Driving License, etc.)
*   Recent passport-size photographs
*   Application form printout
*   Scorecard printout

After successful document verification, a final merit list will be prepared, and candidates will be allotted positions based on their performance, preference, and availability of vacancies.
