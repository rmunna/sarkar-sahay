## National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (UG) 2026 Result — Overview

The National Testing Agency (NTA) declared the results for the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (UG) 2026 on May 26, 2026. This highly anticipated examination serves as the gateway for aspiring students seeking admission to undergraduate medical courses (MBBS), dental courses (BDS), and various AYUSH courses (BAMS, BHMS, BUMS, BSMS) across India.

Candidates who appeared for the NEET (UG) 2026 examination can now access their individual scorecards through the official NTA website. The result provides crucial information including subject-wise scores, total marks, percentile scores, and All India Ranks (AIR), which are essential for the subsequent counselling and admission processes. The total number of candidates who appeared for the examination will be announced by NTA in due course.

## How to Check NEET (UG) 2026 Result

To check your National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (UG) 2026 result, please follow these steps carefully:

1.  **Visit the Official Website:** Go to the official website of the National Testing Agency (NTA) at [nta.ac.in](https://nta.ac.in).
2.  **Locate Result Link:** On the homepage, look for the link related to "NEET (UG) 2026 Result" or "NEET (UG) Scorecard 2026". This link is typically prominently displayed under the latest announcements or candidate login section.
3.  **Enter Credentials:** Click on the result link. You will be redirected to a login page where you need to enter your required credentials. These typically include your NEET (UG) 2026 Roll Number, Date of Birth, and a Security Pin (as displayed on the screen).
4.  **Submit Details:** After entering all the required information, click on the "Submit" or "Login" button.
5.  **View Scorecard:** Your NEET (UG) 2026 scorecard will be displayed on the screen.
6.  **Download and Print:** Carefully review all the details on your scorecard. It is highly recommended to download the scorecard and take a printout for your future reference and for the upcoming counselling process.

## What Details Are Shown on the NEET (UG) 2026 Scorecard

The NEET (UG) 2026 scorecard is a comprehensive document that provides detailed information about a candidate's performance in the examination. The following details are typically displayed on the scorecard:

*   Candidate's Name
*   Roll Number
*   Application Number
*   Date of Birth
*   Category (General, OBC, SC, ST, EWS, PwD)
*   Father's Name
*   Mother's Name
*   Subject-wise Scores (Physics, Chemistry, Biology - Botany & Zoology)
*   Total Marks Obtained
*   NEET (UG) All India Rank (AIR)
*   Category Rank (if applicable)
*   NEET (UG) Percentile Score
*   Qualifying Status (Qualified/Not Qualified)
*   Cut-off scores for different categories (often provided alongside the result or on the scorecard itself)

Candidates must ensure that all personal details mentioned on the scorecard are accurate.
