The Haryana Public Service Commission (HPSC) conducts the prestigious Haryana Civil Services (HCS) examination to recruit eligible candidates for various Group A and B posts in the Haryana state administration. This comprehensive guide will walk you through the entire online application process for the HPSC HCS 2026 examination, ensuring you complete your application accurately and efficiently. Follow these steps carefully to avoid common pitfalls and submit a successful application.

## Before You Start — Documents Needed

Before you begin the online application, ensure you have all necessary documents and information ready. This will streamline the process and prevent errors.

*   **Scanned Photograph:**
    *   Recent passport-size colour photograph.
    *   File Format: JPG/JPEG.
    *   File Size: 20 KB to 50 KB (approximate).
    *   Dimensions: 3.5 cm (width) x 4.5 cm (height) (approximate).
    *   The photograph should be clear, with a light background (preferably white). Ensure your face is clearly visible, without caps or dark glasses.
*   **Scanned Signature:**
    *   Signature made with a black ink pen on a white paper.
    *   File Format: JPG/JPEG.
    *   File Size: 10 KB to 20 KB (approximate).
    *   Dimensions: 3.5 cm (width) x 1.5 cm (height) (approximate).
    *   The signature must be clear and legible.
*   **Educational Qualification Certificates:**
    *   Matriculation (10th) Certificate for Date of Birth proof.
    *   Graduation Degree and Mark Sheets (all semesters/years).
    *   Post-Graduation Degree and Mark Sheets (if applicable).
*   **Identity Proof:**
    *   Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Passport, Driving License, or any other government-issued ID.
*   **Category/Reservation Certificates (if applicable):**
    *   **SC/ST/BCA/BCB Certificate:** Issued by the competent authority of Haryana Government, as per DOPT guidelines.
    *   **EWS Certificate:** Economically Weaker Sections certificate issued by the competent authority for the financial year 2025-26 (as per DOPT norms for central government jobs, adapted for state).
    *   **PwBD Certificate:** Persons with Benchmark Disabilities certificate issued by the competent medical authority.
    *   **Ex-Servicemen Certificate:** Discharge book/certificate.
    *   **Haryana Domicile Certificate:** For claiming reservation benefits.
*   **Other Information:**
    *   Valid Email ID (must be active throughout the recruitment process).
    *   Active Mobile Number (for OTPs and communication).
    *   Details of previous employment (if any).
    *   Net banking details, Credit Card, Debit Card, or UPI details for fee payment.

## Step 1 — New Registration

The first step involves creating a new user account on the HPSC online application portal.

1.  **Visit the Official Website:** Go to the Haryana Public Service Commission's official website: **https://hpsc.gov.in**.
2.  **Locate the Application Link:** On the homepage, look for the "Advertisements" or "Apply Online" section. Find the specific advertisement for "HPSC HCS (Executive Branch) & Other Allied Services Examination 2026".
3.  **Click on "New Registration" / "New Candidate":** Once you find the advertisement, there will be a link to "Apply Online" or "New Registration". Click on it.
4.  **Fill Basic Details:** You will be redirected to a registration page. Accurately enter the following details:
    *   Candidate's Full Name (as per Matriculation Certificate).
    *   Father's Name.
    *   Mother's Name.
    *   Date of Birth (as per Matriculation Certificate).
    *   Gender.
    *   Valid Email ID.
    *   Active Mobile Number.
    *   Security Code/Captcha.
5.  **Verify and Submit:** Review all entered details carefully. Once confirmed, click on the "Register" or "Submit" button.
6.  **Receive Registration Credentials:** Upon successful registration, you will receive a **Registration Number** and a **Password** (or an OTP to set a password) on your registered email ID and mobile number. **Note down these credentials carefully** as they are essential for all future logins and correspondence.

## Step 2 — Fill the Application Form

After successful registration, you can proceed to fill out the detailed application form.

1.  **Login to the Portal:** Go back to the HPSC online application portal and click on "Login" or "Already Registered Candidate". Enter your **Registration Number** and **Password** (or Date of Birth, as per portal instructions) to log in.
2.  **Access the Application Form:** Once logged in, you will see a link to "Fill Application Form" or "Complete Application".
3.  **Personal Information:** Fill in all required personal details:
    *   Nationality.
    *   Marital Status.
    *   Permanent Address.
    *   Correspondence Address.
    *   Identification Mark (e.g., a mole on the face).
    *   Aadhaar Number (optional, but recommended).
    *   Any other specific personal details requested.
4.  **Educational Qualification Details:** This is a crucial section. Enter details of your academic qualifications starting from Matriculation (10th) onwards.
    *   Board/University Name.
    *   Year of Passing.
    *   Marks Obtained/Total Marks or CGPA/Percentage.
    *   Subject/Stream.
    *   Ensure all details match your certificates.
5.  **Category and Reservation Details:**
    *   Select your category (General, SC, BCA, BCB, EWS, PwBD, ESM).
    *   If you belong to a reserved category, ensure you have the valid certificate as per Haryana Government norms.
    *   Indicate if you are a Domicile of Haryana to avail reservation benefits.
    *   Specify if you are a government employee, ex-serviceman, or PwBD candidate.
6.  **Post Preference:** For HCS (Executive Branch) & Other Allied Services, you might be asked to provide preferences for various posts (e.g., HCS (EB), DSP, ETO, DFSC, AETO, BDPO, etc.). Rank them carefully as per your interest and eligibility.
7.  **Exam Center Preference:** Select your preferred examination centers from the available list. HPSC usually provides a list of cities within Haryana for preliminary and main examinations. Choose centers that are convenient for you.
8.  **Review and Save:** After filling all sections, carefully review all the information entered. Make sure there are no spelling mistakes or factual errors. Click on "Save and Next" to proceed. You may be allowed to save the form multiple times before final submission.

## Step 3 — Upload Photo and Signature

This step involves uploading the scanned copies of your photograph and signature as per the specified guidelines.

1.  **Navigate to Upload Section:** After filling the application form, you will be directed to the photo and signature upload section.
2.  **Upload Photograph:**
    *   Click on "Upload Photo".
    *   Browse and select your scanned passport-size photograph file (JPG/JPEG format, 20-50 KB, 3.5x4.5 cm).
    *   Ensure the image is clear and meets all specifications.
3.  **Upload Signature:**
    *   Click on "Upload Signature".
    *   Browse and select your scanned signature file (JPG/JPEG format, 10-20 KB, 3.5x1.5 cm).
    *   Ensure the signature is legible and meets all specifications.
4.  **Preview and Confirm:** After uploading, a preview of your photo and signature will be displayed. Verify that they are correctly uploaded and clearly visible. If not, re-upload them.
5.  **Tips for Resizing/Scanning:**
    *   Use a good quality scanner or a mobile scanner app (like Adobe Scan) to get clear images.
    *   If the file size or dimensions are incorrect, use online image resizing tools or photo editing software (like Paint, GIMP, or online photo editors) to adjust them to the specified requirements. Ensure the aspect ratio is maintained to avoid distortion.

## Step 4 — Pay Application Fee

The application fee is a mandatory part of the submission process. Your application will not be considered complete without successful fee payment.

1.  **Proceed to Fee Payment:** After uploading documents, you will be directed to the fee payment gateway.
2.  **Verify Fee Amount:** The system will display the applicable fee based on your selected category.
    *   **Category | Fee (Approximate)**
    *   General Male / All Other State Candidates (Male) | ₹1000
    *   General Female / All Other State Candidates (Female) | ₹250
    *   SC/BCA/BCB/ESM (Haryana only) Male | ₹250
    *   SC/BCA/BCB/ESM (Haryana only) Female | ₹250
    *   EWS (Haryana only) Male | ₹250
    *   EWS (Haryana only) Female | ₹250
    *   PwBD (Haryana only) | ₹0
3.  **Select Payment Method:** You can pay the fee using various online modes:
    *   Debit Card (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay).
    *   Credit Card (Visa, MasterCard).
    *   Net Banking (of various banks).
    *   UPI (Unified Payments Interface).
4.  **Complete Transaction:** Follow the on-screen instructions for your chosen payment method. Enter the required details (card number, expiry date, CVV, OTP for cards; UPI ID for UPI; Net Banking credentials).
5.  **Confirmation:** Upon successful payment, you will receive a transaction ID and a confirmation message. **Do not close the browser window until you receive this confirmation.** It is advisable to take a screenshot or note down the transaction ID.

## Step 5 — Submit and Save Confirmation

This is the final step to complete your HPSC HCS 2026 online application.

1.  **Final Review:** Before clicking the final submit button, take one last thorough review of your entire application form. Check all details, especially personal information, educational qualifications, category, and post preferences. Ensure there are no discrepancies.
2.  **Click Final Submit:** Once you are absolutely sure that all information is correct and complete, click on the "Final Submit" or "Submit Application" button. Be aware that usually, no changes are allowed after final submission.
3.  **Download/Print Confirmation Page:** After final submission, a confirmation page or application receipt will be generated. This page will contain your **Registration Number**, application details, payment status, and a unique application ID. **It is crucial to download and print multiple copies of this confirmation page for your records.** This document serves as proof of your application.
4.  **Note Your Registration Number:** Always keep your Registration Number and Password safe. You will need them for downloading admit cards, checking results, and all future correspondence with HPSC.

## Common Errors and Fixes

Applying online can sometimes lead to technical glitches or human errors. Here are some common issues and how to address them:

*   **Wrong Photo/Signature Format or Size:**
    *   **Error:** "Image not uploaded", "File size too large/small", "Invalid format".
    *   **Fix:** Ensure images are in JPG/JPEG format. Use online tools or image editors to resize images to the exact specifications (e.g., 20-50KB for photo, 10-20KB for signature) and dimensions. Re-upload after correction.
*   **Payment Failure:**
    *   **Error:** "Transaction failed", "Payment not confirmed", "Amount debited but application not updated".
    *   **Fix:**
        *   **Check internet connection:** Ensure stable internet.
        *   **Try again:** Sometimes, a temporary network issue causes failure. Wait for some time (e.g., 30 minutes) and try again.
        *   **Check bank balance:** Ensure sufficient funds.
        *   **Use alternative payment method:** If one method fails, try another (e.g., Net Banking instead of Debit Card).
        *   **Amount debited but not confirmed:** Wait for 24-48 hours. The amount might be reversed automatically, or the payment status might update. If not, contact HPSC helpline and your bank with transaction details. **Do not attempt multiple payments immediately if the first one shows as pending.**
*   **Browser Issues:**
    *   **Error:** Page not loading correctly, buttons not working, form fields not responding.
    *   **Fix:** Use recommended browsers like Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, or Microsoft Edge (latest versions). Clear browser cache and cookies. Try using an incognito/private window. Avoid using mobile browsers for complex forms.
*   **Server Down/Slow:**
    *   **Error:** "Service unavailable", "Page not found", extremely slow loading.
    *   **Fix:** This often happens during peak application times (especially closer to the deadline). Try applying during off-peak hours (late night or early morning). Be patient and refresh the page.
*   **Incorrect Data Entry:**
    *   **Error:** Realizing a mistake after submission.
    *   **Fix:** Most government exam applications do not allow editing after final submission. Always **double-check every detail** before clicking "Final Submit". If a correction window is provided by HPSC, utilize it. Otherwise, contact HPSC support immediately, though changes are rarely entertained.

## FAQs

**Q1: Can I edit my application form after final submission?**
A1: Generally, no. HPSC usually does not allow candidates to edit their application forms after the final submission. It is crucial to review all details meticulously before clicking the "Final Submit" button. In rare cases, HPSC might open a short correction window, which will be announced on their official website.

**Q2: What if I pay the application fee twice or if the amount is debited but the application status is not updated?**
A2: If the fee is debited twice, one of the transactions is usually reversed automatically by the bank within 3-5 working days. If the amount is debited but the application status remains "Payment Pending," wait for 24-48 hours. If the issue persists, contact the HPSC helpline with your transaction details (transaction ID, bank statement) and also contact your bank. Do not attempt to pay again immediately.

**Q3: Is it acceptable to fill the application form using a mobile phone or tablet?**
A3: While some parts of the website might be mobile-responsive, it is strongly recommended to use a desktop or laptop computer with a stable internet connection for filling out the application form. Mobile browsers can sometimes cause display issues, submission errors, or difficulties in uploading documents, leading to an incomplete or incorrect application.

**Q4: Which web browsers are best suited for filling the HPSC HCS online application?**
A4: For optimal performance and compatibility, it is recommended to use the latest versions of Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, or Microsoft Edge. Ensure your browser's JavaScript is enabled and that you clear your cache and cookies if you encounter any issues.

**Q5: How do I get a fee refund if my application is rejected or if I decide not to appear for the exam?**
A5: Application fees for competitive examinations like HPSC HCS are generally non-refundable, irrespective of whether the application is rejected, the candidate does not appear for the exam, or withdraws their candidature. Refunds are typically only processed in specific scenarios like double payment or if the examination itself is cancelled by the commission and they announce a refund policy.
