BPSC CCE (70th and 71st) syllabus covers **Prelims** (one GS paper, 150 questions) and **Mains** (5 papers — GS I, GS II, GS III, GS IV, and Essay). The 70th Mains added an Essay paper for the first time. Bihar-specific topics carry significant weight in all GS papers.

## BPSC Prelims Syllabus (Paper: General Studies)

**150 questions | 150 marks | 2 hours | Negative marking: −0.25 per wrong**

### Topics Covered

**1. General Science (15–20 questions)**
- Physics: Laws of motion, optics, electricity
- Chemistry: Acids, bases, elements, reactions
- Biology: Human body, genetics, diseases, nutrition
- Science in everyday life; environment and ecology basics

**2. Current Affairs — National & International (20–25 questions)**
- Last 12 months of national events (politics, economy, infrastructure)
- Bihar-specific current events (new projects, appointments, schemes)
- International events: summits, treaties, sports, Nobel/Padma awards

**3. History of India & Bihar (20–25 questions)**
- Ancient India: Harappan civilisation, Mauryas, Guptas — with focus on Bihar (Nalanda, Pataliputra)
- Medieval India: Delhi Sultanate, Mughals, regional kingdoms
- Modern India: British rule, 1857 revolt, freedom struggle, independence
- Bihar's role in freedom struggle (JP Narayan, Dr. Rajendra Prasad)

**4. Bihar Geography (10–15 questions)**
- Rivers of Bihar: Ganga, Sone, Gandak, Kosi, Bagmati
- Districts, divisions, and borders
- Soil types, flood-prone areas, agriculture (rice, wheat, maize, litchi)
- Industrial areas and natural resources

**5. Indian Geography (10–15 questions)**
- Physical features: mountains, plains, plateaus, rivers
- Climate and monsoon patterns
- Agriculture, minerals, industries

**6. Indian Polity & Governance (15–20 questions)**
- Constitution: Preamble, Fundamental Rights, Directive Principles
- Parliament, President, Prime Minister, Governor
- Bihar state government structure
- PRIs (Panchayati Raj) and local bodies
- Important constitutional amendments

**7. Indian Economy & Bihar Economy (10–15 questions)**
- National income, GDP, inflation, fiscal policy
- Bihar's economy: GSDP, poverty levels, MGNREGA, agriculture share
- Government schemes: PM Awas Yojana, Mukhyamantri Gram Parivahan Yojana (Bihar-specific)
- Banking system, RBI, monetary policy basics

**8. Indian & Bihar Social Sectors (10–15 questions)**
- Population, census data (2011; latest available)
- Education, health indicators for Bihar
- Gender, caste demographics
- Important social welfare schemes

## BPSC Mains Syllabus

**5 Papers | Written exam (subjective)**

### Paper 1 — General Hindi (Qualifying)
- 100 marks
- Qualifying only (33 marks minimum); marks not counted in merit
- Essay, Grammar, Translation (English → Hindi)

### Paper 2 — General Studies I (300 marks)
- History of India (with focus on socio-cultural and economic history of Bihar)
- History of Bihar and the freedom struggle
- Post-independence Bihar

### Paper 3 — General Studies II (300 marks)
- Indian Polity, Constitution, Governance
- Economy: Indian & Bihar economy
- Current affairs (national and Bihar focus)

### Paper 4 — General Studies III (300 marks)
- Science & Technology (applied science, technology policy)
- Environment and Ecology (climate change, conservation)
- Sports: major achievements and policy
- Disaster management: Bihar flood context

### Paper 5 — General Studies IV (300 marks)
- Ethics, Integrity, and Aptitude
- Emotional intelligence, values in public service
- Case studies (Bihar administrative scenarios)

### Paper 6 — Essay (NEW from 70th onwards) (300 marks)
- 3 essays (choose 2 to write)
- Topics cover social, economic, ethical, and national issues
- Bihar-relevant topics frequently appear

**Total Mains Marks: 1,500 (excluding qualifying Hindi)**

## Key Preparation Tips

1. **Bihar focus is essential** — 30–40% of questions in every GS paper relate to Bihar's history, geography, economy, polity. Read Bihar Ek Parichay and state-specific materials
2. **Current affairs for Bihar** — Prabhat Khabar, Hindustan (Hindi) newspapers carry Bihar news that BPSC tests
3. **Essay writing** — New from 70th; practice structured essay writing with introduction, body (3 points), and conclusion
4. **NCERT foundation** — Class 9–12 NCERT books for History, Geography, Polity, Economics
5. **Previous papers** — BPSC repeats themes; solve 68th, 69th, 70th papers for pattern recognition
