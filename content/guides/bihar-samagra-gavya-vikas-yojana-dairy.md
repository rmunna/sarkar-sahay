# Bihar Samagra Gavya Vikas Yojana 2026 — Dairy Farm Subsidy (75% SC/ST, 50% General)

**Bihar Samagra Gavya Vikas Yojana** is a state scheme that gives major subsidies to Bihar residents for setting up dairy farms. SC/ST/EBC applicants get **75% subsidy**, while general category applicants get **50% subsidy** on the total project cost.

**Quick answer:** Apply online at [dairy.bihar.gov.in](https://dairy.bihar.gov.in). Choose 2–4 cow unit or 15–20 cow unit. Submit with Aadhaar, land document (LPC), bank account details, and caste certificate (if SC/ST). Helpline: 1800 345 6681 (Sudha COMFED toll-free).

---

## Subsidy Rates

| Category | Subsidy |
|---|---|
| SC / ST | **75% of project cost** |
| EBC (Extremely Backward Class) | **75% of project cost** |
| General category | **50% of project cost** |

The subsidy is provided as a grant — you don't repay it. The remaining cost is funded by the applicant (own funds or bank loan).

---

## Unit Options

Bihar Samagra Gavya Vikas Yojana offers two unit sizes:

### Small Unit: 2–4 Cows

- Suitable for individual farmers or families starting small
- Lower initial investment
- Milk sold via Sudha COMFED network or local market
- Eligible animals: local improved breeds, crossbred cattle, graded Murrah buffalo

### Large Unit: 15–20 Cows

- Higher subsidy amount in absolute terms
- Requires more land, infrastructure, and animal management capacity
- Comes under structured dairy enterprise support
- COMFED may facilitate bulk milk procurement

> The government subsidy is calculated on the **approved project cost** (land, sheds, animals, equipment, initial feed), not on market rates.

---

## Eligibility Criteria

- Permanent resident of Bihar
- Age: typically 18–60 years
- Must own or have access to sufficient **land for the dairy unit** (LPC/land document required)
- **No existing default** on government loans
- Should not already be a beneficiary of the same scheme in the same financial year

Preference given to:
- SC/ST applicants (also highest subsidy tier)
- Women applicants
- BPL families
- Applicants in backward districts

---

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (identity proof)
- **LPC (Land Possession Certificate)** — proof of land ownership/access (also called Jameen ka Vivran)
- **Bank account details** — passbook / cancelled cheque (Aadhaar-linked)
- **Caste certificate** — mandatory for SC/ST/EBC to avail 75% subsidy (if not applying for reserve category, skip this)
- **Passport-size photo**
- **Project proposal** — estimated cost of animals, shed, equipment, feed (format available on dairy.bihar.gov.in)

## How to Apply — Step by Step

### Step 1: Visit the Official Portal

Go to **[dairy.bihar.gov.in](https://dairy.bihar.gov.in)**

Click on **"Samagra Gavya Vikas Yojana"** or the scheme application section.

### Step 2: Register / Login

- Register with your **Aadhaar number** and mobile OTP
- If already registered, log in with your credentials

### Step 3: Fill the Application Form

Enter:
- Personal details (name, address, district, block)
- **Land details** (area in decimal/acres, land document type)
- **Unit size** — 2–4 cow unit OR 15–20 cow unit
- **Category** (SC/ST/EBC for 75% subsidy, General for 50%)
- Bank account details (account number, IFSC)
- Project cost estimate

### Step 4: Upload Documents

Upload scanned copies (JPG/PDF) of:
- Aadhaar card
- LPC (land document)
- Caste certificate (if reserve category)
- Bank passbook

### Step 5: Submit and Note Application Number

Submit the form and note your **application reference number** for tracking.

## Selection Process

Applications are reviewed at the district level by the **District Dairy Officer** (Jila Gavya Vikas Adhikari). Selection is based on:

1. Verification of documents (Aadhaar, LPC, caste certificate)
2. Priority to SC/ST, women, BPL applicants
3. Availability of funds under the scheme for that financial year

After selection:
- Physical verification of land site
- Sanction letter issued
- Animal purchase facilitated through COMFED/government-empanelled vendors
- Subsidy disbursed directly to selected vendor; remaining amount paid by applicant

---

## Where to Sell the Milk

Bihar Samagra Gavya Vikas Yojana beneficiaries are typically linked to **Sudha COMFED** (Bihar State Milk Cooperative Federation):
- COMFED collects milk at your doorstep or at the nearest collection point
- Milk price fixed by COMFED rates (revised periodically)
- Helpline: **1800 345 6681** (toll-free)
- Alternatively, sell directly to local markets, dhaba owners, or sweet shops

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I apply for a bank loan for the remaining 25% or 50% cost?**
Yes — many applicants take a Kisan Credit Card (KCC) or NABARD-linked dairy loan for the remaining amount. Sudha COMFED sometimes facilitates loan linkages for registered beneficiaries.

**Is the subsidy given upfront or after purchase?**
Typically the subsidy is routed to the animal supplier/vendor directly on your behalf, or released to your bank account after physical verification of the dairy setup. The process varies — check with your district office.

**What breeds of cattle qualify?**
Improved local breeds (Sahiwal, Red Sindhi), crossbred cattle (HF/Jersey cross), and Graded Murrah buffaloes are typically approved. Exotic breeds may also qualify — check the approved list on dairy.bihar.gov.in.

**Can women apply for this scheme?**
Yes — women applicants are given priority, and the scheme actively encourages women dairy entrepreneurs.

**What if my application is rejected?**
Check the reason given. Common reasons: incomplete documents, land ownership issue, category mismatch. Rectify and reapply in the next application window.

**How long does the subsidy take to disburse?**
After selection, physical verification, and document approval: typically 1–3 months. Timelines vary by district and fund availability.
