## What is Delhi Rozgar Bazaar?

**Delhi Rozgar Bazaar** is the Delhi government's **official online job portal** — a free platform that connects job seekers in Delhi with employers looking for workers. Launched under the Delhi Employment Exchange system, it allows:

- Job seekers to register and post their CV free of charge
- Employers to post vacancies and search for candidates
- Direct matching of skills and job requirements

| Feature | Details |

## Who Can Use Delhi Rozgar Bazaar?

**Job seekers:**
- Any person looking for employment in Delhi
- Fresh graduates, experienced workers, women, persons with disabilities
- No minimum or maximum age restriction

**Employers:**
- Any business registered in Delhi
- Can post vacancies for free or at minimal cost

## How to Register as a Job Seeker

### Step 1 — Go to the Portal

Visit **[jobs.delhi.gov.in](https://jobs.delhi.gov.in)**

### Step 2 — Register

1. Click **"Job Seeker Registration"**
2. Enter mobile number and OTP
3. Fill in personal details — name, address, Aadhaar (optional)
4. Select your **skills, education level**, and work experience
5. Upload your **resume/CV** (PDF)

### Step 3 — Search and Apply

- Browse jobs by category, location, or salary range
- Click **"Apply"** on relevant job listings
- Employer receives your profile and contacts you

### Step 4 — Attend Rozgar Melas

The Delhi government organizes **Rozgar Melas (Job Fairs)** at different locations — large employers come with immediate offer letters. Get notified via the portal about upcoming melas.

## Tips for Getting Hired Through Delhi Rozgar Bazaar

1. **Fill your profile completely** — incomplete profiles get fewer responses
2. **Upload a clear photograph** — employers prefer profiles with photos
3. **Select specific skills** — be precise (e.g., "MS Excel" not just "Computer")
4. **Check notifications daily** — jobs fill fast in high-demand categories
5. **Apply broadly** — apply to multiple listings; shortlisting is competitive

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. Is Delhi Rozgar Bazaar only for Delhi residents?**

The portal is open to anyone looking for work in Delhi. You don't need to be a Delhi resident — if you are willing to work in Delhi, you can register.

**2. Are the jobs on Rozgar Bazaar government jobs?**

Most listings are **private sector jobs**. Government (DSSSB) vacancies are on a separate portal. Rozgar Bazaar is for private employment in Delhi.

**3. I am a school dropout (Class 8). Can I register?**

Yes. There is no minimum education requirement. You can register and apply for suitable positions.

**4. I haven't received any call from employers even after a month. What should I do?**

Update your profile, add skills, and ensure your mobile number is correct. Also consider attending upcoming Rozgar Melas where face-to-face interaction is faster. Check the portal regularly for new listings.

**5. Is there a helpline for Rozgar Bazaar?**

Contact the Employment Exchange Helpline: **011-23890399** or reach out through the portal's contact section.
