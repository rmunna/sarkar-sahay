# IRCTC Account Locked After Multiple Attempts — How to Unlock

Getting locked out of your IRCTC account is frustrating, especially when you need to book a train ticket urgently. IRCTC automatically locks accounts after repeated failed login attempts as a security measure. This guide explains exactly why it happens, how long the lock lasts, and step-by-step methods to unlock your account.

## Why Does IRCTC Lock Your Account?

IRCTC locks accounts to protect them from unauthorized access. Here are the common reasons:

1. **Too many failed login attempts** — If you enter the wrong password 5 times in a row, IRCTC temporarily locks your account. This is the most common reason.
2. **Suspicious activity detected** — Unusual login patterns (e.g., login attempts from multiple locations or devices simultaneously) can trigger a security lock.
3. **Long period of inactivity** — Accounts inactive for an extended period (typically 12+ months) may be deactivated or locked for security reasons.
4. **Multiple wrong OTP entries** — Entering incorrect OTPs repeatedly during login or transactions can also lock your account.
5. **Browser/app issues** — Sometimes cached data or outdated apps send incorrect credentials automatically, triggering the lock.

## How Long Does the IRCTC Account Lock Last?

| Lock Type | Duration | Action Needed |
|-----------|----------|---------------|
| Temporary lock (failed attempts) | 30 minutes | Wait and retry |
| Extended lock (repeated lockouts) | Up to 24 hours | Wait or reset password |
| Permanent block (suspicious activity) | Indefinite | Contact IRCTC customer care |
| Deactivated account (inactivity) | Indefinite | Contact IRCTC customer care |

**Note:** Most locks are temporary (30 minutes). If you simply entered the wrong password a few times, waiting 30 minutes usually resolves it.

## Step-by-Step: How to Unlock Your IRCTC Account

### Method 1: Wait 30 Minutes and Retry

This works for temporary locks caused by failed login attempts.

1. **Stop trying to log in immediately** — Each additional failed attempt may extend the lock duration
2. **Wait at least 30 minutes** — Do not attempt login during this time
3. **Clear your browser cache and cookies** — Or try a different browser/device
4. **Try logging in again** with your correct username and password
5. **Enter the captcha carefully** — Incorrect captcha also counts as a failed attempt

### Method 2: Reset Your Password (Forgot Password Flow)

If you're unsure of your password or the wait doesn't help:

1. Go to the IRCTC website: [https://www.irctc.co.in](https://www.irctc.co.in)
2. Click **"Login"** on the top-right corner
3. Click **"Forgot Password?"** below the login form
4. Enter your **registered Username**
5. Enter the **captcha** displayed on screen
6. Click **"Submit"**
7. Choose your verification method:
   - **OTP on registered mobile number**
   - **OTP on registered email**
8. Enter the **OTP** received on your mobile or email
9. Set a **new password** following IRCTC's password rules:
   - Minimum 8 characters, maximum 15 characters
   - Must contain at least 1 uppercase letter, 1 lowercase letter, 1 number, and 1 special character
   - Example format: `Train@2026`
10. Confirm the new password and click **"Submit"**
11. Log in with your new password

**Important:** If you don't receive the OTP, check our guide on [IRCTC OTP not received — how to fix](/guide/irctc-otp-not-received-fix).

### Method 3: Contact IRCTC Customer Care

If the above methods don't work, or if your account is permanently blocked:

1. **Call IRCTC Customer Care:**
   - **Phone:** 14646 (toll-free from any Indian number)
   - **Alternate:** 011-23340000
   - **Timings:** Monday to Sunday, 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM
2. **Email IRCTC:**
   - **Email:** care@irctc.co.in
   - **Subject line:** "Account Locked — Request to Unlock" followed by your username
   - Include: your registered username, registered email ID, registered mobile number, and a brief description of the issue
3. **Use the IRCTC Helpdesk Portal:**
   - Visit [https://www.irctc.co.in](https://www.irctc.co.in) and look for the "Contact Us" or "Help" section
   - Submit a grievance with your account details

**Expected response time:** IRCTC typically responds within 24–72 hours for email queries. Phone support is immediate during working hours.

## How to Reset Your IRCTC Password Properly

To avoid getting locked out again, follow these tips when resetting your password:

1. **Use a strong, memorable password** — Combine a word you remember with numbers and symbols (e.g., `Mumbai@2026`)
2. **Write it down safely** — Store it in a password manager or a secure note
3. **Don't reuse passwords** — Use a unique password for IRCTC
4. **Update your registered mobile and email** — Make sure they're current so you can receive OTPs
5. **Test your new password immediately** — Log in right after resetting to confirm it works

## How to Avoid Getting Locked Again

Follow these practices to prevent future account lockouts:

1. **Save your password in a password manager** — Apps like Google Password Manager or browser auto-fill can prevent wrong entries
2. **Don't share your credentials** — Third-party ticket booking agents may misuse or incorrectly enter your password
3. **Use the official IRCTC app or website only** — Avoid third-party websites that ask for your IRCTC login
4. **Clear saved passwords if they're outdated** — Old saved passwords in your browser will cause repeated failed attempts
5. **Check Caps Lock before typing** — A common cause of "wrong password" errors
6. **Keep your app updated** — Outdated IRCTC Rail Connect app versions may cause login failures
7. **Don't attempt rapid logins** — If login fails once, wait a moment and try carefully rather than clicking repeatedly

## FAQs

### How many wrong attempts lock an IRCTC account?
IRCTC locks your account after 5 consecutive failed login attempts. This is a security feature to prevent unauthorized access.

### Can I unlock my IRCTC account immediately without waiting?
Yes — use the "Forgot Password" option to reset your password. This bypasses the 30-minute waiting period and unlocks your account immediately after successful password reset.

### My IRCTC account is permanently blocked. What should I do?
Contact IRCTC customer care at 14646 or email care@irctc.co.in with your username, registered email, and mobile number. They will verify your identity and unblock your account.

### Will I lose my booked tickets if my account is locked?
No. Your existing bookings remain safe even if your account is locked. You can still check PNR status using the [IRCTC PNR status guide](/guide/irctc-train-ticket-pnr-status). Once you unlock your account, all bookings will be accessible.

### I forgot both my username and password. How do I recover?
Go to the IRCTC login page and click "Forgot Username?" first. Enter your registered email or mobile number to retrieve your username. Then use "Forgot Password?" to reset your password.

### Does IRCTC lock accounts for using VPN?
IRCTC may flag unusual login locations. While using a VPN is not officially prohibited, it can trigger security measures. Use your regular internet connection for IRCTC logins.

### How do I update my registered mobile number or email on IRCTC?
Log in to your IRCTC account, go to "My Profile," and update your mobile number or email. You'll need OTP verification on the old number/email to make changes.
