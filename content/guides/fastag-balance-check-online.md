# FASTag Balance Check Online: All Banks & Apps

FASTag has made toll payments cashless and automatic, but keeping track of your balance is essential to avoid embarrassing stops at toll plazas. Whether your FASTag was issued by a bank, Paytm, or any other issuer, this guide covers every way to check your FASTag balance — from apps to SMS to USSD codes.

## Understanding Your FASTag Account

Your FASTag is linked to a **prepaid wallet** or **savings account** depending on the issuer:

- **Prepaid wallet FASTags** (Paytm, Amazon, PhonePe) — require manual recharge
- **Bank-issued FASTags** (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, etc.) — may be linked to your savings account or a prepaid wallet

Every toll deduction reduces your balance. When the balance falls below the **minimum threshold** (usually ₹100–150), the tag may not work at toll plazas.

---

## Method 1: NHAI My FASTag App

The official app by IHMCL (Indian Highways Management Company Limited) works for **all FASTag issuers**:

1. Download **My FASTag** from [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nhai.myfastag) or Apple App Store
2. Register/log in with your **vehicle number** and **registered mobile number**
3. Your **current balance** is displayed on the home screen
4. View **transaction history** — every toll deduction with date, plaza name, and amount
5. **Recharge** directly from the app via UPI/debit card/netbanking

> **Tip:** My FASTag app works for FASTags issued by any bank or wallet — it pulls data from the central NETC system.

## Method 2: Bank Mobile App

If your FASTag was issued by a bank, check balance through the bank's app:

### SBI FASTag
- Open **YONO SBI** app → FASTag section → View Balance

### HDFC Bank FASTag
- Open **HDFC Bank** app → Cards → FASTag → Check Balance

### ICICI Bank FASTag
- Open **iMobile Pay** → FASTag → View Balance & Transactions

### Axis Bank FASTag
- Open **Axis Mobile** → FASTag → Balance & History

### Kotak Mahindra Bank FASTag
- Open **Kotak 811** app → FASTag → Check Balance

### Other Banks
- Most bank apps have a dedicated **FASTag** section under Cards or Services
- Log in and navigate to FASTag to see balance and recent toll deductions

## Method 3: Paytm FASTag

If you purchased FASTag via Paytm:

1. Open **Paytm** app
2. Tap **FASTag** (under "My Payments" or search)
3. Your FASTag balance is displayed
4. View **toll transaction history**
5. **Recharge** instantly via Paytm wallet, UPI, or debit card

---

## Method 4: PhonePe FASTag

For PhonePe-issued FASTags:

1. Open **PhonePe** app
2. Go to **My Money** → **FASTag**
3. View current balance
4. Check **transaction history** with toll plaza details
5. Recharge via UPI

---

## Method 5: Netbanking

Check via your bank's internet banking portal:

1. Log in to your bank's **netbanking** portal
2. Navigate to **FASTag** or **Prepaid Cards** section
3. View balance, transaction history, and statements
4. Download statement for your records

---

## Method 6: SMS Balance Check

Some FASTag issuers support SMS-based balance check:

- **NHAI/IHMCL:** SMS `FASTAG <Vehicle Number>` to the number provided by your issuer
- **Bank-specific:** Check with your FASTag issuer for their SMS format

> **Note:** SMS-based balance check availability varies by issuer. Check your FASTag issuer's website for the exact SMS format and number.

---

## Method 7: USSD (Without Internet)

Some banks support USSD-based FASTag balance check:

- Dial the USSD code provided by your bank (e.g., `*99#` for some banks)
- Navigate to FASTag/Prepaid services
- Check balance without internet

> **Note:** USSD support for FASTag is limited. Contact your issuer to confirm availability.

## How to Check Recharge History & Toll Deductions

To see a detailed breakdown of every toll charge:

1. **My FASTag app** — Tap "Transaction History" for complete records
2. **Bank app** — Navigate to FASTag → Statement/History
3. **Email alerts** — Most issuers send email/SMS for every toll deduction
4. **Monthly statement** — Available via netbanking or issuer portal

Each transaction shows:
- Date and time
- Toll plaza name
- Amount deducted
- Remaining balance

## How to Set Up Low Balance Alerts

Never get caught with an empty FASTag:

1. **My FASTag app** — Enable push notifications
2. **Bank app** — Set FASTag balance alert threshold
3. **SMS alerts** — Most banks send automatic SMS when balance drops below ₹100–200
4. **Auto-recharge** — Set up auto-recharge via bank standing instruction or Paytm auto-pay

---

## Important Tips

1. **Maintain a minimum balance of ₹200–500** to avoid failed toll transactions
2. **Enable auto-recharge** if your issuer supports it — never worry about low balance
3. **Check balance before long highway trips** — multiple tolls can drain your balance quickly
4. **Save toll deduction SMSs** — useful for expense tracking and disputes
5. **If balance shows incorrect**, contact your FASTag issuer — toll reversals take 1–3 days

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the minimum FASTag balance required at toll plazas?

Most toll plazas require a minimum balance of **₹100–150** for the tag to work. However, it's recommended to maintain at least **₹200–500** for uninterrupted travel.

### Can I check FASTag balance without the app?

Yes. You can check via SMS, USSD, netbanking, or by calling your FASTag issuer's customer care helpline.

### Why does my FASTag balance not match my recharges?

Toll deductions happen automatically. Check your **transaction history** for recent toll charges. If you suspect an error, contact your issuer for a detailed statement.

### How do I recharge my FASTag?

You can recharge via the **My FASTag app**, your **bank app**, **Paytm/PhonePe**, **netbanking**, or **UPI**. Most methods reflect the balance within minutes.

### What happens if my FASTag balance is zero at a toll plaza?

You'll be directed to the cash lane and charged **double the toll amount** as a penalty. The FASTag will also be temporarily blacklisted until recharged.

### Can I transfer my FASTag balance to another FASTag?

No. FASTag balances are non-transferable. If you're closing a FASTag, request a refund of the remaining balance from your issuer.

### How often should I check my FASTag balance?

Check before every long trip. If you commute through tolls daily, check weekly or set up **auto-recharge** and **low balance alerts**.
