# Name Change After Marriage: Complete Guide to Update All Documents

After marriage, many people — especially women — choose to change their surname across all official documents. This process involves updating multiple government IDs and records. This guide provides a complete checklist and step-by-step process for changing your name in Aadhaar, PAN, passport, bank accounts, voter ID, driving licence, and property documents.

## Documents You'll Need (Master Checklist)

Keep these ready before starting the update process:

- ✅ Marriage certificate (registered under state marriage act)
- ✅ Husband's Aadhaar card (copy)
- ✅ Current Aadhaar card
- ✅ Current PAN card
- ✅ Passport-size photographs (recent)
- ✅ Joint photograph (for passport)
- ✅ Gazette notification (optional, but helpful for passport and property)

**Tip:** Get your marriage certificate first — it's the primary proof for all name changes.

---

## Recommended Update Order

1. **Marriage Certificate** → 2. **Aadhaar** → 3. **PAN Card** → 4. **Bank Accounts** → 5. **Passport** → 6. **Voter ID** → 7. **Driving Licence** → 8. **Property Documents**

Update Aadhaar first because the updated Aadhaar serves as proof for other document changes.

## 1. Name Change in Aadhaar Card

### Online (myAadhaar Portal)

1. Visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/)
2. Log in with Aadhaar number and OTP
3. Select **Update Aadhaar Online**
4. Edit your name field
5. Upload marriage certificate as proof
6. Pay ₹50 and submit
7. Note the URN for tracking

### Offline (Aadhaar Centre)

1. Visit the nearest [Aadhaar enrolment centre](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
2. Fill the Update/Correction Form with new name
3. Submit marriage certificate (original + copy)
4. Provide biometric verification
5. Pay ₹50 and collect acknowledgement slip

- **Fee:** ₹50
- **Processing time:** 3-10 working days

---

## 2. Name Change in PAN Card

### Online Process

1. Visit [NSDL PAN portal](https://www.onlineservices.nsdl.com/paam/endUserRegisterContact.html)
2. Select **Changes or Correction in existing PAN Data**
3. Fill the form with your new name (as per marriage certificate)
4. Upload: Marriage certificate, updated Aadhaar, photograph
5. Pay ₹107 (Indian address) or ₹1,017 (foreign address)
6. Submit and note the acknowledgement number

You can also apply through [UTIITSL portal](https://www.pan.utiitsl.com/).

- **Fee:** ₹107
- **Processing time:** 15-20 working days
- **Track status:** [tin.tin.nsdl.com/pantan/StatusTrack.html](https://tin.tin.nsdl.com/pantan/StatusTrack.html)

---

## 3. Name Change in Passport

### Process

1. Visit [passportindia.gov.in](https://www.passportindia.gov.in/)
2. Register/log in
3. Apply for **Re-issue of Passport** (select reason: Change in existing personal particulars)
4. Fill the form with new name details
5. Upload marriage certificate, old passport copy, updated Aadhaar
6. Pay the fee and book an appointment at PSK (Passport Seva Kendra)
7. Visit PSK with original documents for verification

### Fee

| Passport Type | Normal (45 days) | Tatkal (7-14 days) |
|---|---|---|
| 36 pages | ₹1,500 | ₹3,500 |
| 60 pages | ₹2,000 | ₹4,000 |

### Documents for PSK Visit

- Old passport (original)
- Marriage certificate (original + copy)
- Updated Aadhaar card
- Joint photograph with spouse
- Husband's passport copy (if available)

## 4. Name Change in Bank Accounts

### General Process (All Banks)

1. Visit your home branch
2. Fill the **Name Change Request Form** (or Account Modification Form)
3. Submit: Marriage certificate, updated Aadhaar, updated PAN
4. Bank will update records in 2-5 working days
5. New cheque book and debit card will be issued with the new name

### Online (Select Banks)

Some banks like SBI, HDFC, and ICICI allow name change requests through net banking under profile update — but most require a branch visit for name changes.

### Don't Forget

- Update name on **debit card** and **credit card**
- Update name in **net banking** profile
- Update **joint account** details if applicable
- Update **FD, RD, and locker** records

## 5. Name Change in Voter ID (EPIC)

### Online Process

1. Visit [nvsp.in](https://www.nvsp.in/) (National Voter Service Portal)
2. Log in or register
3. Select **Form 8 — Correction of Entries in Electoral Roll**
4. Fill in your new name details
5. Upload marriage certificate and updated Aadhaar
6. Submit the application
7. BLO (Booth Level Officer) may visit for verification

### Offline Process

1. Visit the ERO (Electoral Registration Officer) office
2. Fill **Form 8**
3. Submit marriage certificate and ID proof
4. Collect the acknowledgement receipt

- **Fee:** Free
- **Processing time:** 15-30 days

---

## 6. Name Change in Driving Licence

### Online Process (Parivahan)

1. Visit [parivahan.gov.in](https://parivahan.gov.in/parivahan/)
2. Select your state → **Driving Licence Related Services**
3. Choose **Change of Name in DL**
4. Fill the application form with new name
5. Upload marriage certificate, updated Aadhaar
6. Pay the fee (varies by state, typically ₹200-500)
7. Book an appointment at RTO if required

### Offline Process

1. Visit your RTO with Form 1 (application) and Form CMV (medical, if expired)
2. Submit marriage certificate, old DL, updated Aadhaar
3. Pay the applicable fee
4. New DL issued in 7-30 days

- **Fee:** ₹200-500 (varies by state)
- **Processing time:** 7-30 days

---

## 7. Name Change in Property Documents

For property registered in your maiden name:

1. **Execute a supplementary deed** or **endorsement** at the Sub-Registrar's office
2. Submit: Marriage certificate, gazette notification (recommended), old property documents
3. Pay stamp duty and registration fee (varies by state)
4. Update the **khata/mutation records** at the municipal corporation

### For Property Tax Records

- Visit the local municipal corporation
- Submit name change application with marriage certificate
- Update reflects in property tax bills

**Tip:** A gazette notification of name change strengthens the legal record for property documents.

## Important Tips

1. **Get the marriage certificate first** — It's the foundation document for all name changes
2. **Update Aadhaar first** — Updated Aadhaar serves as proof for other changes
3. **Update PAN before filing taxes** — Mismatched names cause ITR processing issues
4. **Keep old documents safe** — Don't discard old IDs; keep copies for reference
5. **Gazette notification is optional but useful** — Especially for passport and property changes

## FAQs

### Is it mandatory to change name after marriage?

No, it is not legally mandatory to change your name after marriage in India. It is entirely a personal choice.

### How long does the entire process take?

If you start with the marriage certificate and update documents one by one, the entire process takes approximately 1-3 months.

### Can I change my name back after divorce?

Yes. You can revert to your maiden name after divorce using the divorce decree as proof, following the same process for each document.

### Do I need a gazette notification for name change?

A gazette notification is not mandatory for most documents if you have a marriage certificate. However, it is recommended for passport and property document changes.

### Can I change only my surname and keep my first name?

Yes. You can change only your surname (last name) across all documents. Specify clearly in each form which part of the name is being changed.

### Is there a time limit to change name after marriage?

No. There is no legal time limit. You can update your name anytime after marriage with a valid marriage certificate.

### Can the husband change his surname to the wife's name?

Yes. Indian law allows either spouse to adopt the other's surname. The same process applies with the marriage certificate as proof.
