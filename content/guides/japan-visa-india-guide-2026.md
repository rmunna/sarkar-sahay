# Japan Visa from India 2026 — Complete Application Guide

Japan is one of the most popular tourist destinations for Indians — and the visa process is straightforward once you know the steps. No biometrics required, processing in 5–7 days, and one of the fastest consular decisions in Asia.

---

## Japan Visa Types

| Type | Purpose | Max Stay |
|------|---------|---------|
| **Single Entry Tourist** | Tourism, sightseeing | 15 or 30 days per entry |
| **Double Entry Tourist** | Same as above | 15 or 30 days per visit |
| **Multiple Entry (5-year)** | Repeat visitors | 90 days per stay (multiple trips) |
| **Business Visa** | Business meetings, conferences | 15–90 days |
| **Transit Visa** | Layover in Japan | 72 hours |

**First-time applicants**: Usually get single-entry 15–30 day visa. Clean travel history helps get multiple-entry.

**Good news for frequent travelers**: Japan now offers 5-year multiple-entry visas for eligible applicants (previous Japan visit + clean immigration record + financial stability).

## Where to Apply — VFS Global

Japan has outsourced visa applications to VFS Global. Consulate cities:

| Consulate | Jurisdiction (States) |
|-----------|----------------------|
| New Delhi | Delhi, UP, Bihar, Rajasthan, Jammu, Himachal, Haryana, Punjab, NE states |
| Mumbai | Maharashtra, Goa, Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh |
| Bangalore | Karnataka, Kerala, Andhra, Telangana, Tamil Nadu |
| Chennai | Tamil Nadu, Andhra, Kerala, Puducherry, Andaman |
| Kolkata | West Bengal, Odisha, Jharkhand, Assam, NE states |

**Apply at the consulate covering your state of residence** — you must apply based on where you live, not where you work or want to fly from.

Book appointment at [vfsglobal.com](https://www.vfsglobal.com/en/individuals/index.html) → Japan → India.

## Documents Required

### All Applicants

| Document | Details |
|----------|---------|
| **Passport** | Valid for 6+ months beyond return date; at least 2 blank pages |
| **Visa application form** | Download from Japan consulate or VFS website; fill completely |
| **Photo** | 45×45mm, white background, taken within 6 months |
| **Cover letter** | Introduce yourself, state purpose of visit, travel dates, planned activities |
| **Flight itinerary** | Round-trip booking (refundable/dummy ticket acceptable) |
| **Hotel bookings** | All accommodations for Japan stay |
| **Travel itinerary** | Day-by-day plan (city-by-city, day-by-day is sufficient; not overly detailed) |

### Financial Documents

| Document | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| **Bank statements (last 3–6 months)** | Show ₹1 lakh minimum balance or comfortable funds for trip |
| **Salary slips (last 3 months)** | Current income proof |
| **ITR or Form 16 (last 1–2 years)** | Annual income proof |

**No strict minimum balance requirement** is published by Japan consulate, but ₹1,000–1,500 per day of travel is a common benchmark.

### Employment / Status Documents

| Type | Documents |
|------|----------|
| **Salaried** | Employment letter on letterhead (confirming employment, salary, leave approved) |
| **Business owner** | Business registration, GST certificate, income proof |
| **Student** | College enrollment letter, parent/guardian's financial documents, permission to travel |
| **Retired** | Pension certificate, bank statements |

## How to Fill Japan Visa Application Form

Key fields to be careful about:
- **Purpose of visit**: Write "Sightseeing/Tourism"
- **Planned stay**: Be specific (e.g., "October 10–22, 2026 — 12 nights")
- **Funds**: State how you'll finance: "Personal savings / salary income"
- **Accommodation**: Write hotel names and addresses (as per your bookings)
- **Previous Japan visits**: If any, list accurately
- **Travel history**: List key countries visited in last 5 years; Japan is interested in this

## Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1: Check Which Consulate Is Yours

Based on your state of residence (not where you work or fly from).

### Step 2: Download and Fill Application Form

From the Japan consulate website or VFS. Fill in English. Print, sign.

### Step 3: Book VFS Appointment

Go to vfsglobal.com → Japan. Choose your consulate city. Appointments are usually available within 1–3 weeks.

### Step 4: Compile Documents

Originals + self-attested copies of all documents. No notarization required for Japan visa.

### Step 5: Attend VFS Appointment

VFS staff will check your documents and submit to the consulate. No biometric collection for Japan — just documents.

**No interview**: Japan tourist visa does not require a personal interview.

### Step 6: Wait 5–7 Working Days

Consulate processes and returns passport to VFS. You can collect in-person or use VFS courier service (extra fee).

### Step 7: Verify Visa Stamp

When passport returned: Verify visa dates, number of entries, and duration match what you applied for.

## Preparing Your Itinerary — What Japan Consulate Looks For

Japan consulates appreciate organized applications. A good itinerary:

**Sample (10-day trip)**:
- Day 1–3: Tokyo (Shinjuku, Shibuya, Akihabara, Asakusa)
- Day 4: Day trip to Nikko or Hakone
- Day 5–6: Kyoto (Fushimi Inari, Arashiyama, Kinkakuji)
- Day 7: Nara (day trip)
- Day 8–9: Osaka (Dotonbori, Osaka Castle)
- Day 10: Departure from Osaka/Tokyo

City names and activities are enough — you don't need to minute-by-minute schedule.

## IC Card / JR Pass — Practical Note for India Travelers

Not relevant to visa, but useful to know:
- **JR Pass**: Buy from authorised Indian agents before departing India; significantly cheaper than buying in Japan. The pass covers most Shinkansen (bullet train) and JR lines
- **IC Card (Suica/Pasmo)**: Reloadable transport card for local trains, buses, convenience stores

| Reason | Fix |
|--------|-----|

| Insufficient financial proof | Show 3–6 months bank statements with adequate balance |
| Vague purpose of visit | Write clear day-by-day itinerary |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I want to visit both Japan and South Korea. Do I need separate visas?

Yes — Japan and South Korea are separate countries with separate visas. However, South Korea offers free visa-on-arrival for certain Indian passport holders (check current policy at South Korean consulate). Japan requires advance visa application from India.

### Can I apply for a Japan multiple-entry visa as a first-time applicant?

Generally no — first-time applicants typically receive single-entry visas. After a successful Japan visit, subsequent applications can include multiple-entry request.

### Is hotel booking mandatory or can I show an Airbnb booking?

Any confirmed accommodation booking is acceptable — hotels, Airbnb, guesthouses, or a host invitation letter if staying with a resident of Japan.

### Is travel insurance required for Japan visa?

Not mandatory (unlike Schengen), but strongly recommended — Japanese medical costs are very high without insurance. Buy travel insurance with medical coverage before departing.

### Can I extend my Japan visa after arrival?

Tourist visa extensions are rare and not guaranteed. Japan immigration can grant limited extensions in emergencies (illness, etc.) — contact the nearest immigration office in Japan. Plan to leave on or before your authorized stay date.
