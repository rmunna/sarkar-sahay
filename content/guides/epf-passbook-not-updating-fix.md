# EPF Passbook Not Updating — How to Fix

Your EPF passbook should reflect contributions within **3–7 days** after your employer deposits them. If your passbook is stuck showing an old balance, this guide covers every possible reason and how to fix it.

---

## Why Is Your EPF Passbook Not Updating?

| Reason | How Common | Fix Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Employer has not deposited contributions | Very common | Medium |
| UAN not activated | Common (new employees) | Easy |
| KYC not approved | Common | Easy–Medium |
| EPFO server/portal issues | Occasional | Wait it out |
| Employer filed ECR but payment pending | Common | Medium |
| Member ID not linked to UAN | Rare | Medium |

---

## Fix 1: Check if Your Employer Has Actually Deposited

The most common reason — your employer has not yet remitted the EPF contribution for the month.

### How to verify

1. Go to [EPFO Unified Portal](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in)
2. Login with your **UAN** and password
3. Click **View** → **Passbook**
4. Check the last entry date — if the latest month is missing, your employer hasn't deposited yet

### What to do

- Ask your **HR/accounts department** for the payment challan or ECR receipt
- Employers must deposit by the **15th of the following month** (e.g., June salary PF by July 15)
- If your employer consistently delays, file a complaint at [EPFO Grievance Portal](https://epfigms.gov.in)

> **Tip:** Under Section 14B of the EPF Act, employers who delay deposits are liable to pay damages.

---

## Fix 2: Activate Your UAN

You cannot view your passbook if your UAN is not activated on the member portal.

### Steps to activate

1. Go to [EPFO Member Portal](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in)
2. Click **Activate UAN** (below the login form)
3. Enter your **UAN**, **Aadhaar** or **PAN**, name, date of birth, and mobile number
4. Verify with the OTP sent to your registered mobile
5. Set a password and login

Your passbook should be accessible within **24 hours** after activation.

> Having trouble? See our detailed guide: [EPFO UAN Activation Failed — How to Fix](/guides/epfo-uan-activation-failed-fix)

## Fix 3: Get Your KYC Approved

If your KYC (Aadhaar, PAN, bank account) is pending or rejected, passbook access may be restricted.

### Check KYC status

1. Login to [EPFO Member Portal](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in)
2. Go to **Manage** → **KYC**
3. Check the status column — it should show **Verified** (green tick)

### If KYC is pending

- **Aadhaar:** Ensure your name and date of birth on EPFO match your Aadhaar exactly. Even a single character mismatch causes rejection.
- **PAN:** Name must match your PAN card records.
- **Bank account:** IFSC code and account number must be correct and account must be active.

### If KYC is rejected

1. Correct the mismatch (update Aadhaar at your nearest enrolment centre if needed)
2. Re-submit KYC on the portal
3. Ask your employer to approve it from their end
4. Wait **2–5 working days** for verification

---

## Fix 4: Fix EPFO Portal / Server Errors

The EPFO passbook portal frequently shows errors during peak hours.

### Common errors and solutions

| Error | Solution |
|---|---|
| "Service unavailable" | Try after 10 PM or early morning (before 9 AM) |
| "Passbook not available" | Your employer may not have filed the first ECR yet |
| Page not loading / timeout | Clear browser cache, try a different browser |
| "Invalid UAN/password" | Reset password using the **Forgot Password** option |
| Blank passbook page | Disable ad-blocker and try in incognito mode |

### Best times to access

The [EPFO Passbook Portal](https://passbook.epfindia.gov.in) works best during **off-peak hours**: before 9 AM or after 9 PM IST.

---

## Fix 5: Link All Member IDs to Your UAN

If you changed jobs and your previous Member ID is not linked to your current UAN, those contributions won't show in your passbook.

### How to check and link

1. Login to [EPFO Member Portal](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in)
2. Go to **View** → **Service History**
3. Check if all your previous Member IDs are listed
4. If any are missing, request an **EPF transfer** to consolidate

> Related: [How to Transfer EPF Online After Job Change](/guides/epf-transfer-online-job-change)

## Alternative Ways to Check EPF Balance

If the passbook portal is not working, use these methods:

### 1. UMANG App

1. Download **UMANG** from Play Store / App Store
2. Open → Search **EPFO**
3. Select **View Passbook**
4. Enter UAN and OTP
5. View your latest balance and transaction history

### 2. SMS (No Internet Needed)

Send an SMS to **7738299899** from your UAN-registered mobile number:

```
EPFOHO UAN ENG
```

Replace `ENG` with your preferred language code:
- `HIN` — Hindi
- `TAM` — Tamil
- `TEL` — Telugu
- `MAL` — Malayalam
- `KAN` — Kannada
- `BEN` — Bengali

### 3. Missed Call

Give a **missed call to 011-22901406** from your registered mobile number. You'll receive an SMS with your latest PF balance.

> **Note:** SMS and missed call services only work if your UAN is linked to your Aadhaar and bank account (KYC verified).

### 4. EPFO Website Direct

Visit [https://passbook.epfindia.gov.in](https://passbook.epfindia.gov.in) → Enter UAN and password → Download passbook as PDF.

## When Does the Passbook Typically Update?

| Event | Expected Update Time |
|---|---|
| Employer deposits contribution | 3–7 working days |
| New UAN activation | 24–48 hours |
| KYC approval | 2–5 working days |
| EPF transfer credited | 15–30 working days |
| Interest credit (annual) | End of financial year (usually by June–July) |

> **Annual interest** is credited once a year, typically between April and July. Don't worry if you don't see monthly interest entries.

---

## Still Not Updated? File a Grievance

If your passbook hasn't updated for more than **2 months** despite employer deposits:

1. Go to [EPFiGMS Grievance Portal](https://epfigms.gov.in)
2. Register with your UAN
3. Select grievance category → **Passbook related**
4. Describe the issue with specific months and amounts
5. Attach employer's payment challan if available
6. Submit — you'll get a **PGR number** for tracking

Grievances are typically resolved within **15–30 days**.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why does my passbook show ₹0 balance?
Your employer may not have filed the first ECR (Electronic Challan cum Return) yet, or your Member ID is not linked to your UAN. Contact your employer's HR department.

### Can I check my passbook without activating UAN?
No. UAN activation is mandatory to access the online passbook. Use the SMS or missed call method as a workaround if activation is taking time.

### My employer closed down. How do I update my passbook?
Visit your regional EPFO office with your UAN, last salary slip, and ID proof. You can also file a grievance on [EPFiGMS](https://epfigms.gov.in).

### Passbook shows deposits but balance is wrong — what to do?
Interest may not have been credited yet (it's annual). If the issue persists after July, raise a grievance citing the discrepancy.
