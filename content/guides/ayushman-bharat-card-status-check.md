# How to Check Ayushman Bharat Card Status Online

Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB-PMJAY) provides health insurance cover of ₹5 lakh per family per year to over 12 crore poor and vulnerable families. If you have applied for an Ayushman Bharat card, here is how to check your application status and download your e-card.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent platform and is not affiliated with the Government of India. Always verify details on official government portals.

---

## What is the Ayushman Bharat Card?

The Ayushman Bharat card (also called PMJAY card or Ayushman card) is your proof of enrollment under the PM Jan Arogya Yojana. It entitles eligible families to:

- **₹5 lakh health cover** per family per year
- **Cashless treatment** at empanelled hospitals across India
- Coverage for **1,500+ medical procedures** including surgeries, diagnostics, and day care
- **No age limit** and **no family size restriction**

---

## How to Check Ayushman Bharat Eligibility

Before checking card status, verify if you are eligible:

### Method 1: Online via PMJAY Portal

1. Visit [mera.pmjay.gov.in](https://mera.pmjay.gov.in)
2. Enter your **mobile number** and CAPTCHA
3. You will receive an **OTP** on your mobile
4. Enter the OTP to log in
5. Search using your **name, Aadhaar number, ration card number, or mobile number**
6. If your name appears, you are eligible for the Ayushman card

### Method 2: Call the Helpline

- Dial **14555** (Ayushman Bharat toll-free helpline)
- Provide your Aadhaar number or ration card details
- The operator will confirm your eligibility status

### Method 3: Visit a Common Service Centre (CSC)

- Visit your nearest CSC or Ayushman Mitra at an empanelled hospital
- Carry your Aadhaar card and ration card
- They will check your eligibility on the spot

## How to Check Ayushman Card Application Status

### Step-by-Step Process

1. Visit the beneficiary portal at [beneficiary.nha.gov.in](https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Am I Eligible"** or **"Check Status"**
3. Enter your **Aadhaar number** or **PMJAY ID**
4. Verify with **OTP** sent to your registered mobile
5. Your card application status will be displayed:
   - **Pending** — Application is under review
   - **Approved** — Card has been approved; you can download it
   - **Rejected** — Application did not meet criteria

### Check via Ayushman App

1. Download the **Ayushman Bharat (PMJAY)** app from Google Play Store
2. Register with your mobile number
3. Log in and go to **"My Cards"** section
4. Your card status and details will be displayed
5. You can also download the e-card directly from the app

## How to Download Ayushman Bharat E-Card

1. Visit [beneficiary.nha.gov.in](https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in)
2. Log in with your Aadhaar-linked mobile number and OTP
3. Click on **"Download Card"**
4. Verify with **Aadhaar biometric or OTP authentication**
5. Your Ayushman Bharat e-card will be downloaded as a PDF

You can also download your e-card from:
- **Common Service Centres (CSCs)**
- **Ayushman Mitra** at empanelled hospitals
- The **PMJAY mobile app**

## Documents Required for Ayushman Card

- **Aadhaar Card** (mandatory for all family members)
- **Ration Card** or SECC-2011 data reference
- **Mobile Number** linked to Aadhaar
- **Family ID or PMJAY ID** (if already assigned)
- **Passport-size Photo** (for physical card)

---

## How to Apply for Ayushman Card (If Eligible but Not Enrolled)

1. Visit the nearest **CSC centre** or **empanelled hospital**
2. Carry your Aadhaar card and ration card
3. The Ayushman Mitra will verify your eligibility via the PMJAY portal
4. Complete **Aadhaar eKYC** (biometric or OTP verification)
5. Your Ayushman card will be generated on the spot or within a few days
6. You will receive an SMS confirmation on your registered mobile

---

## Processing Time

- **Eligibility Verification:** Instant (online)
- **Card Generation at CSC:** Same day to 7 working days
- **E-card Download:** Instant once approved
- **Physical Card:** 15-30 days (if applicable in your state)

---

## Important Tips

1. **Aadhaar is mandatory** — Without Aadhaar, the Ayushman card cannot be generated
2. **Keep mobile number updated** — OTP verification is required for card download and hospital admission
3. **Check hospital empanelment** before visiting — Not all hospitals accept Ayushman cards. Check the list at [pmjay.gov.in](https://pmjay.gov.in)
4. **No premium or fees** — The Ayushman card is completely free. Do not pay anyone for card creation
5. **Portability across India** — Your Ayushman card works at empanelled hospitals in any state, not just your home state

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: How can I check if my Ayushman card is active?
Visit [mera.pmjay.gov.in](https://mera.pmjay.gov.in) and search with your Aadhaar or ration card number. If your details appear with "Active" status, your card is valid for cashless treatment.

### Q2: Can I check Ayushman card status without Aadhaar?
You can check eligibility using your ration card number or SECC data on the PMJAY portal. However, Aadhaar is mandatory for card generation and download.

### Q3: My name is in the eligible list but I don't have a card. What should I do?
Visit the nearest CSC or Ayushman Mitra at an empanelled hospital with your Aadhaar card. They will create your Ayushman card on the spot after eKYC verification.

### Q4: Is there a fee to get the Ayushman Bharat card?
No. The Ayushman Bharat card is completely free. If anyone demands money, report them to the Ayushman helpline at **14555**.

### Q5: Can all family members get separate Ayushman cards?
Yes, each eligible family member gets an individual Ayushman card, but the ₹5 lakh cover is shared by the entire family per year.

### Q6: What is the difference between ABHA card and Ayushman card?
ABHA (Ayushman Bharat Health Account) is a 14-digit health ID for digital health records. The Ayushman Bharat PMJAY card is specifically for cashless treatment under the ₹5 lakh health insurance scheme. They are different. See our [ABHA Health ID guide](/guide/abha-health-id) for details.
