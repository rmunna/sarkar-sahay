## What is the Amma Vodi Scheme?

Jagananna Amma Vodi is a flagship education welfare scheme of the Andhra Pradesh government that provides **₹15,000 per year** to mothers or recognized guardians of school-going children. The scheme incentivizes parents to keep their children in school and reduce dropout rates.

"Amma Vodi" translates to "Mother's Lap" — symbolizing the role of mothers in nurturing their children's education. The scheme covers children studying in Classes 1 to 12 in government, aided, and recognized private schools.

## Who is Eligible?

### Eligibility Criteria
- **Mother or recognized guardian** of children studying in Classes 1-12
- Children must be enrolled in **government, government-aided, or recognized private schools** in Andhra Pradesh
- Family must hold a **white ration card** (BPL equivalent in AP)
- The child must have **75% minimum attendance** in the previous academic year
- Must be a **resident of Andhra Pradesh**

### Who is NOT Eligible
- Families holding a non-white ration card (economically stronger sections)
- Families where any member is an income tax payer
- Families where any member is a government employee drawing ₹10,000+ salary
- Children studying in unrecognized schools
- Children with less than 75% attendance

### Special Provisions
- **Orphans:** The scheme recognizes institutional guardians
- **Single mothers:** Given priority
- **Differently-abled children:** Eligible with relaxed attendance criteria

## Benefits

| Component | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Annual financial assistance | ₹15,000 per mother |
| Payment mode | Direct Bank Transfer |
| Payment timing | January each year (academic year linked) |
| Per child or per mother | Per mother (regardless of number of children) |

**Note:** The amount is ₹15,000 per mother, not per child. Whether a mother has one child or five children in school, she receives ₹15,000.

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** of the mother/guardian
- **Aadhaar card** of the child/children
- **White ration card** (AP BPL card)
- **Bank account details** of the mother (linked with Aadhaar)
- **School enrollment certificate** or student ID
- **Attendance certificate** from the school (75% minimum)
- **Study certificate** from the school head

## How to Apply

### Automatic Enrollment
- Amma Vodi uses **automatic identification** through government databases
- If you hold a white ration card and your child is in a recognized school, you should be automatically identified
- The school headmaster verifies student enrollment and attendance
- The Education Department cross-references with ration card data

### If Not Automatically Included
1. Visit your child's **school** and speak to the headmaster
2. Request inclusion in the Amma Vodi beneficiary list
3. Provide your white ration card and Aadhaar details
4. The headmaster uploads your details to the Amma Vodi portal
5. District Education Office verifies and approves

### Online Status Check
1. Visit [jaganannaammavodi.ap.gov.in](https://jaganannaammavodi.ap.gov.in)
2. Enter your Aadhaar number or child's enrollment details
3. Check beneficiary status and payment history

## Verification Process

1. **School-level verification:** Headmaster confirms enrollment and attendance
2. **Ration card verification:** Cross-checked with Civil Supplies Department
3. **Aadhaar verification:** Identity confirmed through UIDAI
4. **Income/employment check:** Government employee and IT payer databases checked
5. **Volunteer verification:** Ward/village volunteers conduct physical verification
6. **Final approval:** District Education Officer approves the beneficiary list

## Important Tips

- **White ration card is mandatory** — if you have a different color card, get it converted at your local Meeseva centre
- **Ensure 75% attendance** — this is strictly monitored; encourage your child to attend school regularly
- **Aadhaar-bank linking** — the mother's bank account must be linked with Aadhaar for DBT
- **Speak to the school headmaster** — if you're eligible but not receiving benefits, the headmaster is your first point of contact
- **One payment per mother** — the amount is per mother, not per child; having multiple children in school doesn't increase the benefit

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does the amount increase for multiple children?
No, the benefit is ₹15,000 per mother regardless of how many children she has in school. The objective is to incentivize the mother.

### Can fathers apply for Amma Vodi?
The scheme is primarily for mothers. Fathers can apply only in specific cases: if the mother is deceased, or in cases of single fatherhood with legal documentation.

### Is Amma Vodi available for private school students?
Yes, but the private school must be **recognized by the AP government**. Unrecognized or unapproved schools are not covered.

### What if my child fails a class?
The child must continue to be enrolled in school with 75% attendance. Failing a class does not disqualify the mother from receiving benefits as long as the child continues studying.

### When is the Amma Vodi payment made?
Payments are typically made in **January** each year, coinciding with Sankranti. The exact date is announced by the government.

### Can I receive both Amma Vodi and Vidya Deevena?
Yes, Amma Vodi is for school-going children (Classes 1-12), while Vidya Deevena covers college/higher education fees. If you have children in both school and college, you can benefit from both schemes.
