# NSP Scholarship Portal OTP Not Working 2026? Fix All Login Errors

The National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in) is used by millions of students to apply for central and state government scholarships. OTP failures are the most common complaint, especially at peak application periods (August–October). Here's how to fix every error.

## Why NSP OTP Fails

| Reason | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| OTP going to old/wrong mobile | Mobile registered at Aadhaar is different from your current number |
| SMS delayed during peak hours | Wait 5 minutes, resend |
| DND on your mobile number | Government OTPs should bypass DND, but try disabling DND |
| Account locked from too many attempts | Wait 30 minutes before retrying |
| Portal sending OTP to email (not mobile) | Check your registered email too |

## Fix 1: OTP Not Received — Check Registered Mobile

NSP uses the mobile number linked to your **Aadhaar** (not the mobile you're typing in during registration). If your Aadhaar has an old number, OTP goes there.

1. Check which mobile is linked to your Aadhaar at [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/) → "Check Aadhaar Status" → "Mobile Number Status"
2. If it's an old number: **update mobile in Aadhaar** at nearest Aadhaar Seva Kendra (takes 30 days) OR check if you still have that SIM
3. **Workaround**: if you have the old SIM (even in another phone), use it to receive the OTP for NSP registration, then update your mobile after

## Fix 2: "Resend OTP" Not Working or Still No OTP

1. Wait **at least 2 minutes** before clicking Resend OTP — the first OTP may still be in transit
2. Maximum **3 resend attempts** — after that, wait 30 minutes before starting over
3. Try at **off-peak hours**: 8 AM–9 AM or after 9 PM (NSP gets extremely heavy traffic during application seasons)
4. Switch from WiFi to **mobile data** — some WiFi networks block OTP gateway numbers
5. Check **spam/DND inbox**: some carriers route government SMS to a secondary folder

## Fix 3: NSP Registration Page Not Loading or Blank Screen

1. **Use Chrome Incognito** (Ctrl+Shift+N) — previous session data often breaks the registration form
2. **Disable browser extensions**: Grammarly, ad blockers, and student browser extensions conflict with NSP JavaScript
3. **Use a laptop/desktop**, not mobile browser — NSP forms work poorly on mobile browsers
4. **Try at a different time**: NSP portal frequently crashes between 10 AM and 6 PM during application season
5. **Clear cookies completely**: Chrome → Settings → Privacy → Cookies → Clear all

## Fix 4: "User Already Registered" Error

This means your Aadhaar/mobile is already linked to an existing NSP account:

1. Click **"Forgot Password"** on the login page
2. Enter your Aadhaar number or registered mobile
3. Reset password via OTP and login to your existing account
4. If you don't remember your registration details, contact NSP helpline with Aadhaar details

## Fix 5: Password Reset Not Working

1. Go to [scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in/) → **"Applicant Corner"** → **"Fresh Registration"** or **Login → "Forgot Password"**
2. Enter the **Application ID / Registration ID** (from your acknowledgement SMS or email)
3. OTP sent to registered mobile for reset
4. If mobile is inaccessible, call NSP helpline for manual reset

## Fix 6: Institute/Renewal Login Issues

If you're renewing a scholarship (not fresh registration) and facing login issues:

1. Your **Application ID from last year** is your login username
2. Password may need reset — use "Forgot Password" with the registered mobile
3. **Renewal window**: opens September–October each year. Before that window, renewal login shows "session not available"
4. Check your institute's status — your application may be held if the institute hasn't renewed their NSP registration

## Frequently Asked Questions

### NSP portal is showing "under maintenance." When will it open?

NSP maintenance is usually scheduled between 11 PM – 6 AM. During scholarship application seasons, the portal may also show temporary maintenance during peak hours. Wait 2–3 hours and try again. NSP announces maintenance on its homepage.

### I submitted my application but it's showing "Defective." What does this mean?

"Defective" means a document is missing or unclear, or there's a data mismatch. Login → Track Application → see the defect reason. Correct the specific document and re-upload. You have a limited window (check your state's deadline) to fix defects.

### My NSP scholarship amount isn't credited. What to do?

Payment failures usually mean: bank account closed, Aadhaar not seeded in bank (NPCI mapper), or institute verification pending. Check your payment status at [pfms.nic.in](https://pfms.nic.in/) → Know Your Payment using Aadhaar. If payment shows as made but not received, contact NSP helpline with the PFMS transaction ID.

### Can I change my bank account after submitting NSP application?

Yes, during the application year if your bank account changes, login to NSP and update bank details before the institute verifies your application. After institute verification, bank changes may require helpdesk support.
