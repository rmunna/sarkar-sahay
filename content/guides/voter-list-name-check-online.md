# Voter List Name Check Online: How to Search Electoral Roll

Before any election, it is important to check whether your name is in the voter list (electoral roll). If your name is missing, you will not be able to vote. The Election Commission of India provides multiple ways to search your name in the voter list — online at [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in/), via SMS, and through the Voter Helpline App.

## Why Should You Check the Voter List?

- **Confirm your registration** before elections
- **Verify your details** (name, address, polling booth) are correct
- **Find your polling booth** and part number for election day
- **Detect errors** early so you can apply for corrections using Form 8

---

## Method 1: Search Online at Voters.eci.gov.in

This is the most common and easiest method.

### Search by Name (Details-Based Search)

1. Visit [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"Search in Electoral Roll"** or **"Know Your Electoral Status"**
3. Select **"Search by Details"**
4. Enter the following:
   - **Name** (in English or regional language)
   - **Father's / Husband's name**
   - **Age** or **Date of Birth**
   - **Gender**
   - **State**
   - **District**
   - **Assembly Constituency**
5. Click **Search**
6. Your voter details will appear if your name is in the electoral roll

### Search by EPIC Number

If you have your **EPIC number** (Voter ID card number printed on your voter ID):

1. Visit [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"Search in Electoral Roll"**
3. Select **"Search by EPIC No."**
4. Enter your **EPIC number** (e.g., ABC1234567)
5. Click **Search**
6. Your complete voter details including polling booth and part number will be displayed

**Tip:** EPIC number search is faster and more accurate than name-based search.

---

## Method 2: Check via SMS

You can check your voter details by sending an SMS from your registered mobile number.

**SMS format:**
```
EPIC <Your EPIC Number>
```

**Send to:** 1950 or the state-specific number

**Example:**
```
EPIC ABC1234567
```

You will receive an SMS with your voter details and polling booth information.

**Note:** SMS service availability may vary by state and telecom operator.

---

## Method 3: Voter Helpline App

The **Voter Helpline App** is the official Election Commission mobile app for voter services.

### Steps to Check Name:

1. Download the **Voter Helpline** app from [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.eci.citizen) or Apple App Store
2. Open the app and select **"Search in Electoral Roll"**
3. Choose search method:
   - **By EPIC Number** — enter your voter ID number
   - **By Details** — enter name, father's name, age, state, constituency
4. Tap **Search**
5. View your voter details, polling booth, and part number

### Additional App Features:
- Download e-EPIC (digital voter ID)
- Apply for new registration or corrections
- Find your BLO and polling booth
- File complaints
- Get election day reminders

---

## Method 4: Call Helpline 1950

Call the toll-free voter helpline **1950** to check your voter details over the phone. Provide your EPIC number or name and address details to the helpline executive.

**Available:** Monday to Saturday, 10 AM to 5 PM

---

## What Information You Get from the Search

When you successfully find your name in the voter list, you will see:

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Name | Your name as registered |
| Father's/Husband's Name | Relation name on record |
| Age/DOB | Age or date of birth |
| Gender | Male/Female/Other |
| EPIC Number | Your voter ID card number |
| Part Number | Section number in the electoral roll |
| Assembly Constituency | Your AC name and number |
| Parliamentary Constituency | Your PC name and number |
| Polling Station | Your designated polling booth name and address |

---

## What to Do if Your Name is Missing

If your name is not found in the voter list:

1. **Double-check your search** — try both EPIC number and name-based search
2. **Check spelling variations** — try different spellings of your name
3. **Apply for new registration** — submit [Form 6 for voter registration](/guide/voter-id-family-member-add) at voters.eci.gov.in
4. **Contact your BLO** — use the "Know Your BLO" feature to find your booth-level officer
5. **Call helpline 1950** for assistance

---

## Important Tips

1. **Check your name well before elections** — don't wait for election day to discover your name is missing
2. **EPIC number search is most reliable** — use it whenever possible instead of name-based search
3. **Verify all details** — check that name spelling, address, and photograph are correct
4. **Note your polling booth** — save the polling station name and address for election day
5. **Apply for corrections early** if any detail is wrong — use Form 8 at voters.eci.gov.in

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. How do I check my name in the voter list online?

Visit [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in/), click "Search in Electoral Roll," and search by your EPIC number or by entering your name, father's name, age, state, and constituency.

### Q2. What is the EPIC number and where can I find it?

EPIC stands for Electors Photo Identity Card. The EPIC number is a unique alphanumeric code (e.g., ABC1234567) printed on your voter ID card. It is also available on your e-EPIC downloaded from the Voter Helpline App.

### Q3. Can I check the voter list by mobile number?

No, you cannot search the voter list by mobile number. You need to use either your EPIC number or your personal details (name, father's name, age, constituency) to search.

### Q4. My name is in the voter list but details are wrong. How to correct?

Apply for correction using **Form 8** (Application for Correction of Entries in Electoral Roll) at [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in/). You can correct name, address, age, photograph, and other details.

### Q5. How often is the voter list updated?

The electoral roll is revised annually during the **Summary Revision** period (usually October-January). Continuous updating also happens through supplement publications during the year.

### Q6. Can I search the voter list for another person?

Yes. You can search for any person's name in the voter list if you know their details (name, constituency) or EPIC number. This is public information available on the electoral roll.

### Q7. What if voters.eci.gov.in is not working during my search?

If the portal is down, use the Voter Helpline App, send an SMS, or call helpline 1950. See our [portal troubleshooting guide](/guide/voter-id-online-not-working-fix) for detailed fixes.
