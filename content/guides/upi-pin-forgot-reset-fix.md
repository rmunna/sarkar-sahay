# Forgot UPI PIN? How to Reset UPI PIN in GPay, PhonePe, Paytm

Forgetting your UPI PIN is one of the most common digital payment problems in India. With over 30 crore active UPI users, thousands of people get locked out of payments every day. The good news: resetting takes under 5 minutes if you have your debit card handy.

## What You Need to Reset UPI PIN

Before starting, keep these ready:

- ✅ **Registered mobile number** (the SIM must be in your phone)
- ✅ **Debit card** linked to your bank account (card number + expiry date)
- ✅ The **UPI app** (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc.) installed on your phone

> ⚠️ **No debit card?** You cannot reset UPI PIN without one. Visit your bank branch or apply online — new debit cards arrive in 7-10 business days.

## Reset UPI PIN in Google Pay (GPay)

1. Open **Google Pay** app
2. Tap your **profile icon** (top-right)
3. Go to **Bank accounts** → Select your bank
4. Tap **"Forgot UPI PIN"**
5. Enter your **debit card number** (last 6 digits) and **expiry date**
6. An **OTP** will be sent to your registered mobile number
7. Enter OTP + set your **new 4-digit or 6-digit UPI PIN**
8. Done ✅ — Try a ₹1 test transaction

**Common GPay error:** *"Something went wrong. Try again later."*
- Fix: Clear GPay cache (Settings → Apps → Google Pay → Clear Cache) and retry.

## Reset UPI PIN in PhonePe

1. Open **PhonePe** app
2. Tap **"My Money"** (bottom menu) → **Bank Accounts**
3. Select your bank account
4. Tap **"Forgot UPI PIN"** or **"Reset UPI PIN"**
5. Enter **last 6 digits of debit card** + **expiry date (MM/YY)**
6. OTP arrives on registered mobile → Enter it
7. Set **new UPI PIN** (4 or 6 digits depending on your bank)
8. PIN reset successful ✅

**Common PhonePe error:** *"Unable to reset PIN. Bank server down."*
- Fix: Wait 30 minutes and retry. Bank servers often have intermittent issues.

## Reset UPI PIN in Paytm

1. Open **Paytm** app
2. Go to **"Passbook"** → **"UPI"** → Select linked bank
3. Tap the **three dots (⋮)** → **"Forgot/Reset UPI PIN"**
4. Enter **debit card details** (last 6 digits + expiry)
5. Verify with **OTP**
6. Create **new UPI PIN**
7. Confirmed ✅

## Reset UPI PIN in BHIM App

1. Open **BHIM** app
2. Go to **Bank Account** section
3. Tap **"Reset PIN"**
4. Enter debit card last 6 digits + expiry date
5. OTP verification → Set new PIN
6. Done ✅

## Reset UPI PIN in Other Banking Apps

The process is nearly identical across all UPI apps:

| App | Path to Reset PIN |
|---|---|
| Amazon Pay | Amazon → Amazon Pay → UPI → Bank → Forgot PIN |
| WhatsApp Pay | Settings → Payments → Bank → Forgot PIN |
| CRED | CRED → Bank Account → Reset UPI PIN |
| SBI YONO | YONO → UPI → Manage → Reset PIN |
| iMobile Pay (ICICI) | iMobile → UPI → Settings → Reset PIN |

## UPI PIN Reset Not Working? Troubleshoot These Issues

### Error: "Card details do not match"

Your debit card must be linked to the **same bank account** that's connected to UPI. Ensure:
- You're entering the **last 6 digits** (not first 6)
- Expiry date format is correct (**MM/YY**)
- Card is **not expired**
- Card is a **debit card**, not credit card

### Error: "OTP not received"

- Check your **registered mobile** has network signal
- Ensure **DND is disabled** (dial 1909 to turn off)
- SMS inbox isn't full
- Wait 2-3 minutes before requesting resend

### Error: "UPI ID temporarily blocked"

After multiple wrong PIN attempts, UPI gets blocked:
- **Wait 24 hours** — block lifts automatically
- If still blocked after 24 hours, contact your bank
- Some banks require a branch visit to unblock

### Error: "Bank server is not reachable"

- Try during **bank working hours** (10 AM – 4 PM)
- Avoid **month-end dates** (heavy load)
- Check if bank has announced maintenance on their website/social media

## UPI PIN Security Tips

- **Never share** your UPI PIN with anyone — no bank employee will ask for it
- **Don't use** obvious PINs like 1234, 0000, or your birth year
- UPI PIN is needed only for **sending money**, never for receiving
- **Scam alert:** If someone asks you to enter PIN to "receive" money, it's fraud
