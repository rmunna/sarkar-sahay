# RBI Floating Rate Savings Bond (FRSB) India 2026 — Complete Guide

The RBI Floating Rate Savings Bond 2035 is a government-guaranteed savings instrument currently yielding 8.05% per annum — one of the highest risk-free rates available to Indian retail investors.

---

## Key Features at a Glance

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Interest rate** | 8.05% p.a. (as of Jan 2026; reset every 6 months) |
| **Rate benchmark** | NSC rate + 0.35% (NSC currently 7.7% → Bond = 8.05%) |
| **Interest payment** | Semi-annual (every 6 months — Jan 1 and Jul 1) |
| **Tenure** | 7 years from issue date |
| **Premature exit** | Not allowed for below 60 years |
| **Senior citizen exit** | After 4 years (65+), 5 years (60–64) |
| **Minimum investment** | ₹1,000 |
| **Maximum investment** | No limit |
| **Who can invest** | Resident individuals, HUF |
| **NRIs eligible?** | No |
| **Transferable?** | No — non-negotiable, non-transferable |
| **Tax** | Interest taxable at slab rate; no TDS (self-declare in ITR) |
| **Capital gains** | None — redemption at face value |
| **Where to buy** | SBI, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, PNB, IDBI, and other authorized banks; also RBI Retail Direct |

## Current Interest Rate — How It's Calculated

The FRSB rate is reset every January 1 and July 1:

**Rate = NSC Rate + 0.35%**

- NSC current rate: 7.7%
- FRSB rate: 7.7% + 0.35% = **8.05%**

If the government changes NSC rate, FRSB rate changes automatically 6 months later. Historical NSC rate has stayed 7.7% for several quarters — making FRSB a stable 8.05% for now.

**Interest example on ₹5 lakh investment**:
- Semi-annual interest: ₹5,00,000 × 8.05% / 2 = **₹20,125 every 6 months**
- Annual interest: **₹40,250**

## RBI FRSB vs Bank FD vs NSC — Comparison

| Instrument | Rate | Lock-in | Tax on Interest | Risk |
|-----------|------|---------|----------------|------|
| **RBI FRSB** | 8.05% | 7 years | Taxable (slab) | Zero (government) |
| SBI 5-year FD | 6.5–7.0% | 5 years | Taxable (TDS deducted) | Minimal (insured ₹5L) |
| NSC (5 years) | 7.7% | 5 years | Taxable (deemed reinvested) | Zero (post office/govt) |
| SCSS (5 years) | 8.2% | 5 years | Taxable | Zero (government) |
| PPF | 7.1% | 15 years | Tax-free | Zero (government) |

**Key advantage of FRSB over FD**: 8.05% vs 6.5–7% at banks. Both are taxable. FRSB beats FD by ~1–1.5% for the same risk level (actually lower risk — direct government guarantee).

**Key advantage of SCSS over FRSB**: SCSS rate is 8.2% vs FRSB 8.05%, SCSS has shorter 5-year tenure and is available only to 60+ citizens. FRSB is available to all ages.

## How to Buy RBI FRSB

### Method 1: Through Authorized Banks (Easiest)

Most major banks are authorized to sell FRSB:
- **SBI** → Visit branch or SBI Net Banking → Investments → RBI Bonds
- **HDFC Bank** → Net banking → Transact → RBI Savings Bonds
- **ICICI Bank** → Net banking → Investments → RBI Bond
- **PNB, Axis, Kotak, BOB** — most authorized banks offer this

**Walk-in process**:
1. Visit bank branch with PAN + Aadhaar
2. Fill RBI Bond application form
3. Submit cheque/demand draft (or debit from your account)
4. Bond is issued in **electronic/dematerialized form**
5. You receive Bond Ledger Account (BLA) statement

**Online (Net Banking)**:
1. Login to your bank's net banking
2. Look for "Investments" or "Government Securities" section
3. Select RBI Floating Rate Savings Bond
4. Choose investment amount (minimum ₹1,000, multiples of ₹1,000)
5. Debit authorized, bond appears in your account within 2–3 days

### Method 2: RBI Retail Direct (Direct Platform)

1. Register at [rbiretaildirect.org.in](https://rbiretaildirect.org.in)
2. Complete KYC (PAN + Aadhaar + bank account)
3. One-time registration (free)
4. Purchase FRSB directly from RBI's portal
5. Bond held in your RBI Retail Direct account

**RBI Retail Direct advantage**: Also gives access to G-Sec (government securities), T-bills, SDL (State Development Loans) — a full fixed-income platform.

## Interest Payment and TDS

- Interest paid **semi-annually**: January 1 and July 1 each year
- **No TDS** (as of current rules — interest paid gross)
- **You must declare interest income** in your ITR under "Income from Other Sources"
- Tax at your slab rate (10%, 15%, 20%, 25%, 30%)

**Record keeping**: Bank sends interest credit to your savings account. Track this for ITR.

## Premature Exit Rules

| Age | Premature Exit Allowed After |
|-----|---------------------------|
| Below 60 | NOT allowed (must hold 7 years) |
| 60–64 years | After 5 years from issue |
| 65–70 years | After 4 years from issue |
| 70+ years | After 3 years from issue (check for updates — varies) |

**Penalty on premature exit**: 50% of last coupon (interest) forfeited.

**For individuals below 60**: This is a strict 7-year lock-in. Do not invest money you might need before 7 years.

## Who Should Invest in RBI FRSB?

**Best suited for**:
- Investors in 0–10% tax bracket who want government safety + 8% return
- Senior citizens who've maxed SCSS (₹30L limit) and want more government-backed fixed income
- Conservative investors who want higher than FD rates with government guarantee
- Retirees with surplus funds they don't need for 7 years

**Less suited for**:
- Investors in 30% tax bracket (post-tax return = 8.05% × 0.69 = 5.55% — comparable to PPF/NPS)
- Young investors needing liquidity
- NRIs (ineligible)

## FRSB vs PPF — After-Tax Comparison

For someone in 30% tax bracket:
- FRSB: 8.05% pre-tax → 5.55% post-tax
- PPF: 7.1% tax-free → 7.1% post-tax
- PPF wins for 30% bracket investors despite lower nominal rate

For someone in 0% bracket:
- FRSB: 8.05% pre-tax = 8.05% post-tax
- PPF: 7.1% tax-free
- FRSB wins for zero-tax-bracket investors (senior citizens with income below ₹3L, for example)

## What Happens on Maturity?

At maturity (7 years from issue date):
- Principal returned to your linked bank account
- No capital gains tax (redeemed at face value = cost price = no gain)
- Final semi-annual interest credited

No action needed — bank auto-processes maturity redemption.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is RBI Floating Rate Bond safe?

Yes — it's backed by the Government of India. The highest possible safety in India. No credit risk whatsoever.

### Can I buy RBI bonds online without visiting a bank?

Yes — via your bank's net banking (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis etc.) or directly via RBI Retail Direct portal. No branch visit needed for most major banks.

### What is the RBI bond interest rate now (June 2026)?

8.05% per annum (semi-annual payment). Reset date: January 1, 2026. Next reset: July 1, 2026 based on NSC rate at that time.

### Is there any TDS on RBI bond interest?

No TDS is deducted on FRSB interest (as per current rules). But the interest is fully taxable — you must declare it in ITR and pay self-assessment tax if needed.

### Can I hold RBI bond jointly with family member?

Yes — you can have a joint holder. On death of primary holder, joint holder receives the bond.

### RBI bond vs NSC — which is better?

RBI FRSB (8.05%) beats NSC (7.7%) in rate. Both are government-backed. NSC has 5-year tenure vs FRSB's 7 years. NSC interest is "deemed reinvested" (counted as income each year for tax even though you don't physically receive it), while FRSB pays interest semi-annually (actual cash). For cash flow: FRSB. For shorter commitment: NSC.
