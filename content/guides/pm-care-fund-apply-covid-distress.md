## What is PM CARES Fund?

**PM CARES Fund** (Prime Minister's Citizen Assistance and Relief in Emergency Situations Fund) is a public charitable trust set up in March 2020 to provide emergency relief during COVID-19 and other disaster situations. It is distinct from the PM National Relief Fund (PMNRF).

PM CARES has provided three major types of direct citizen benefits:

---

## PM CARES — Three Main Benefit Programmes

### 1. PM CARES for Children (COVID Orphans)

Children who lost **both parents** or the **sole breadwinner** to COVID-19 (from March 2020 onwards):

| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Monthly support | ₹4,000/month until 18 years (from PM CARES corpus interest) |
| Education | Free school education in Kendriya Vidyalaya / government school |
| Higher education | PM CARES scholarship — tuition fees paid directly to college |
| Health insurance | ₹5L/year under PM-JAY until age 18 |
| Lump sum at 23 | ₹10 lakh on turning 23 years |

**Who applies:** District Magistrate / Collector in each district identifies and enrolls eligible children. Parents/guardians can also approach DM office.

---

### 2. PM CARES Insurance for Health Workers

Health workers (ASHA, ANM, doctors, nurses, sanitation workers) who died due to COVID-19 while on duty:

| Coverage | ₹50 lakh insurance (for govt health workers) |
|----------|---------------------------------------------|
| Who is covered | Government healthcare staff, contractual health workers |
| Claim filed by | Family / nominee |
| Where to claim | State Health Department / Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Package |

---

### 3. PM CARES Oxygen & Equipment Support

PM CARES funded procurement of:
- 1.5 lakh oxygen concentrators for district hospitals
- Ventilators for government hospitals
- Vaccine purchase support

These are institutional benefits — directly available at government hospitals. Citizens access them by going to government hospitals.

---

## PM CARES Scholarship for COVID Orphans — How to Apply

The most actionable benefit for families:

### Step 1 — Identify Eligibility

Child must have lost parent(s) to COVID-19. Date of death should be on record.

### Step 2 — Approach District Magistrate Office

Go to your **District Collectorate** and meet the DM or the Child Welfare Committee (CWC). They will:
- Verify the COVID death certificate of parent
- Register the child in the PM CARES Children scheme
- Initiate monthly support and school admission

### Step 3 — Higher Education Scholarship

When the child reaches Class 11/12 or college age:
- Contact **State Child Protection Society** or the DM office
- Scholarship is processed through **National Scholarships Portal** (scholarships.gov.in)
- Course fees are paid directly to the college

---

## PM CARES Fund — What It Doesn't Cover

PM CARES is NOT a general COVID relief fund for individuals. It does NOT provide:
- Cash grants to COVID-affected families (use state Chief Minister Relief Funds)
- Compensation for job loss
- Treatment cost for COVID patients (use PM-JAY / Ayushman Bharat)
- Support to migrant workers (use BOCW welfare boards)

For general hardship: approach your **State CM Relief Fund** or **Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Yojana** (food scheme).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. My father was a daily wage worker who died of COVID. Can I apply to PM CARES?**

PM CARES for Children specifically covers loss of the sole earning parent to COVID. Approach your District Collector's office — they will assess eligibility and enroll your child in the scheme if eligible.

**2. How do I donate to PM CARES Fund?**

Donate at [pmcares.gov.in](https://pmcares.gov.in) via net banking, UPI, or credit/debit card. Donations qualify for 80G tax deduction.

**3. I am a private hospital nurse who treated COVID patients. Am I covered by PM CARES insurance?**

The ₹50L insurance under Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Package covered government and contractual health workers. Private hospital workers were generally not covered — check with your state health department.

**4. Can I apply for PM CARES Children scheme now in 2025?**

Yes, if the parent's COVID death occurred from March 2020 onwards and the child has not yet turned 23. Approach the DM office in your district.

**5. Where can I check the status of PM CARES funds used?**

PM CARES releases quarterly reports on pmcares.gov.in with details of fund utilization by category.
