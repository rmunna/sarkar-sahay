# Ration Card Not Showing Name After Addition — How to Fix

You applied to add a family member's name to your ration card, the application was accepted — but the new name still doesn't appear on the card or the FPS dealer refuses to give ration for that member. This is a common problem across states, and it usually has a fixable cause. This guide covers every reason your name addition may not be reflecting and how to resolve it.

## Common Symptoms

- Name addition application shows **"Approved"** but name is **missing on the ration card**
- New member's name **not visible on the state portal** or NFSA website
- FPS (Fair Price Shop) dealer says **"name not in list"** and refuses to give ration
- Application status stuck at **"Pending at DSO"** or **"Under Verification"** for months
- eKYC status shows **"Pending"** or **"Not Completed"** for the newly added member
- Aadhaar seeding **not done** for the new member, causing biometric failure at ePoS machine
- Name added online but **physical card not updated**
- Added member shows on portal but **not receiving ration entitlement**

---

## Why Your Name Addition Is Not Showing

### 1. eKYC Not Completed for the New Member

After name addition is approved, **eKYC (electronic Know Your Customer)** must be completed for the new member. Until eKYC is done, the name will not appear in the ePoS system used by FPS dealers.

**How to check:**
- Visit your state's ration card portal (links below) and check eKYC status against the member's Aadhaar number
- On the NFSA portal ([nfsa.gov.in](https://nfsa.gov.in/)), search your ration card and check if the new member shows "eKYC: Pending"

**How to fix:**
- Visit your nearest **CSC (Common Service Centre)** or **block/tehsil food office** with the new member's Aadhaar card
- Complete **biometric eKYC** — this links Aadhaar to the ration card entry
- eKYC typically reflects within **24–72 hours** after completion

### 2. Aadhaar Seeding Not Done

Aadhaar seeding means linking the new member's Aadhaar number to the ration card in the government database. Without this, the ePoS machine at the FPS shop **cannot authenticate** the member.

**How to check:**
- On NFSA portal → "Know Your Ration Card Details" → check if the Aadhaar number appears next to the new member's name
- If it shows blank or "Not Seeded," the linking is incomplete

**How to fix:**
- Visit the **tehsil/block supply office** or **CSC centre** with:
  - Ration card (original + photocopy)
  - New member's Aadhaar card (original + photocopy)
- Request **Aadhaar seeding** for the new member
- Alternatively, some states allow Aadhaar seeding online through their food department portal

### 3. Application Still Pending or Under Verification

Your name addition application may not actually be fully approved yet. It could be stuck at:
- **Gram Panchayat/Ward level** verification
- **Block/Tehsil supply officer** approval
- **District Supply Officer (DSO)** final approval

**How to check:**
- Check application status on your state portal (see state-wise section below)
- Look for status like "Pending at Block," "Sent to DSO," or "Under Verification"

**How to fix:**
- Visit the **block or tehsil food & supply office** and inquire about your application
- If stuck for more than 30 days, **file a complaint** with the District Supply Officer (see complaint section below)

### 4. FPS Dealer Not Updated or Refusing

Sometimes the name has been added in the system but the FPS dealer:
- Has an **outdated beneficiary list** and hasn't downloaded the latest one
- Is **deliberately refusing** to provide ration to the new member
- Claims **"server not showing the name"** without checking properly

**How to fix:**
- Ask the dealer to **refresh/download the latest beneficiary list** on the ePoS machine
- Show your **online ration card details** from the state portal as proof
- If the dealer still refuses, **file a complaint** at the block supply office or via the state grievance portal
- You can also call the **national food helpline: 1967** (toll-free)

### 5. Name Added Online but Physical Card Not Updated

In many states, the online database is updated first but the **physical ration card** is not reprinted. This doesn't affect your ration entitlement (which works via ePoS/biometric), but it can cause confusion.

**How to fix:**
- Your ration entitlement works via the **digital record**, not the physical card
- For a reprinted card, visit the **tehsil/block food office** and request a new printout
- Some states issue updated cards during periodic **card renewal drives**

### 6. Data Entry Error or Mismatch

The name may have been added but with a **spelling mistake**, **wrong Aadhaar number**, or other data entry error that prevents it from showing correctly.

**How to check:**
- Compare the name and Aadhaar on the state portal with the actual Aadhaar card
- Look for spelling variations (e.g., "Mohammad" vs "Mohammed")

**How to fix:**
- Apply for a **name correction** through the state portal or food office
- See our guide: [Ration Card Name Not Updated: How to Fix](/guides/ration-card-name-not-updated-fix)

## Check Status on Your State Portal

### Uttar Pradesh (UP FCS)

1. Go to [fcs.up.gov.in](https://fcs.up.gov.in/)
2. Click **"खाद्यान्न वितरण"** or **"राशन कार्ड"** section
3. Enter your **ration card number** or **Aadhaar number**
4. Check if the new member's name appears in the member list
5. Check eKYC and Aadhaar seeding status next to each member

### Maharashtra (RCMS)

1. Go to [rcms.mahafood.gov.in](https://rcms.mahafood.gov.in/)
2. Click **"Ration Card"** → **"Search Ration Card"**
3. Enter your ration card number or head of family details
4. View the member list — check if the new name appears
5. For application tracking: **"Application Status"** → enter your application ID

### Andhra Pradesh (EPDS AP)

1. Go to [epds.ap.gov.in](https://epds.ap.gov.in/)
2. Click **"Ration Card Details"**
3. Enter your ration card number
4. Check member list for the newly added name
5. Verify Aadhaar seeding status for each member

### Tamil Nadu (TNPDS)

1. Go to [tnpds.gov.in](https://www.tnpds.gov.in/)
2. Click **"Search Ration Card"**
3. Enter your **ration card number** or **smart card number**
4. View the family member list
5. Check if the new member shows with proper Aadhaar linking

> **Tip:** For other states, visit [nfsa.gov.in](https://nfsa.gov.in/) → **"Know Your Ration Card Details"** → select your state to check member details.

## How to Complain to the District Supply Officer (DSO)

If your name addition has been pending for more than **30 days** or the FPS dealer is refusing to provide ration, you have the right to escalate.

### Option 1: Written Complaint to DSO Office

1. Write a complaint letter (in Hindi/English/regional language) addressed to the **District Supply Officer**
2. Include:
   - Your ration card number
   - Name of the member added
   - Date of application
   - Application/reference number
   - Nature of problem (not reflecting / dealer refusing / eKYC pending)
3. Submit it at the **District Food & Supply Office** and get an **acknowledgement receipt**
4. The DSO is required to respond within **30 days** under the NFSA grievance redressal mechanism

### Option 2: Online Grievance Portal

- **National:** File on [pgportal.gov.in](https://pgportal.gov.in/) under Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution
- **UP:** [jansunwai.up.nic.in](https://jansunwai.up.nic.in/)
- **Maharashtra:** [grievances.maharashtra.gov.in](https://grievances.maharashtra.gov.in/)
- **AP:** [spandana.ap.gov.in](https://spandana.ap.gov.in/)
- **TN:** [tnpds.gov.in](https://www.tnpds.gov.in/) → Grievance section
- **Bihar:** [grievance.bihar.gov.in](https://grievance.bihar.gov.in/)

### Option 3: Helpline Numbers

| Channel | Number/Link |
|---|---|
| National Food Helpline | **1967** (toll-free) |
| UP Food Helpline | **1800-1800-150** |
| Maharashtra Food Helpline | **1800-22-4950** |
| AP Food Helpline | **1967** |
| TN Food Helpline | **1967** / **044-2345 0962** |

### Option 4: Contact via CM Helpline / District Collector

If the DSO office doesn't resolve your issue within 30 days:
- Escalate to the **District Collector/Magistrate** office
- Use the **CM Helpline** in your state (e.g., UP: **1076**, AP: **1902**)
- File an RTI application asking for the status and reason for delay

## Documents to Keep Ready

When visiting any food office for name addition issues, carry:
- **Ration card** (original + photocopy)
- **Aadhaar card** of the new member (original + photocopy)
- **Application receipt/reference number** from the name addition request
- **Birth certificate** or **marriage certificate** (if the addition is for a newborn or spouse)
- **Address proof** if required by your state

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does it take for a new name to appear after addition?
Typically **7 to 30 days** after approval, depending on the state. eKYC and Aadhaar seeding must also be completed for the name to appear at the FPS level.

### Can I get ration for the new member before the name appears?
No. Until the name reflects in the ePoS system with completed Aadhaar seeding and eKYC, the FPS dealer's machine will not authenticate the new member.

### My name addition was approved but the FPS dealer says it's not showing. What do I do?
First check the state portal to confirm the name is listed. If it is, ask the dealer to refresh the beneficiary list. If they still refuse, complain to the block supply office or call **1967**.

### Is eKYC mandatory for name addition?
Yes. Under NFSA guidelines, **Aadhaar-based eKYC** is mandatory for all ration card members. Without eKYC, ration cannot be distributed through the ePoS system.

### Can I add a name and complete eKYC at the same time?
In some states, yes — the CSC centre or food office can process both together. Ask at your local centre.
