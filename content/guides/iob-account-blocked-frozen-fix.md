# Indian Overseas Bank (IOB) Account Blocked or Frozen? How to Unblock (2026)

Indian Overseas Bank (IOB), headquartered in Chennai, is a major PSB with over 3,400 branches. If your IOB account is blocked or frozen, this guide tells you exactly how to unblock it.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is independent. Contact IOB directly at 1800-425-4445 for account-specific assistance.

---

## Common Reasons for IOB Account Block

| Reason | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| KYC not updated (re-KYC pending) | Complete KYC via app or branch |
| PAN not linked | Link PAN online or at branch |
| Dormant account (no transactions 24 months) | Reactivation at branch |
| Government/court order | Requires legal resolution |
| Suspicious transaction pattern | Call customer care + branch visit |

---

## How to Find the Reason

1. **Check SMS** — IOB sends restriction alert via SMS
2. **Call 1800-425-4445** — ask for exact reason
3. **IOB Mobile App** — check for KYC update notifications

---

## Unblock IOB Account Online

### Via IOB Mobile App

1. Download/open **IOB Mobile** app
2. Login → Navigate to **Services → Account**
3. If KYC update is prompted: upload Aadhaar + PAN photos
4. For other issues: **Service Request → Account Activation/Unblock**

### Via IOB Net Banking

1. Login at **[iobnet.co.in](https://www.iobnet.co.in)**
2. Go to **My Profile → KYC Update** (for KYC issues)
3. Or submit a service request

---

## Call Customer Care

**IOB Toll-Free:** **1800-425-4445**
**Alternate:** 044-2521-5166

**Script:** "My IOB account ending XXXX has debit restrictions. Can you tell me the reason and what I need to submit?"

---

## Visit Branch for Unblock

1. Visit any IOB branch (home branch preferred for sensitive issues)
2. Carry: Aadhaar, PAN, passbook, 2 passport photos
3. Fill **Account Reactivation / KYC Update** form
4. Submit documents + get acknowledgment
5. Processing: **1–2 working days**

---

## IOB KYC Update

**Online (via IOB Mobile App):**
- IOB Mobile → Services → Re-KYC Update
- Upload Aadhaar + PAN (clear photos, self-attested)
- Timeline: 1–2 working days

**At Branch:**
- Fill Re-KYC form + submit documents
- Same-day processing at branch

---

## IOB Dormant Account Reactivation

1. Visit home branch
2. Fill dormant account reactivation form
3. Submit fresh KYC documents
4. Do one transaction (cash deposit ₹100+)
5. Active in 1 working day

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does IOB account unblocking take?**
KYC update: 1–2 working days. Dormant reactivation: 1 working day. Suspicious transaction hold: 3–7 working days after investigation.

**Can I update IOB KYC without branch visit?**
Yes — IOB Mobile app supports digital KYC upload. However, for dormant accounts or regulatory holds, a branch visit is usually needed.

**My IOB ATM card is not working but account is active — why?**
The card may be separately blocked or expired. Call 1800-425-4445 for card-specific issues.

**IOB net banking login is blocked — how to reset?**
Call customer care or visit branch for net banking credential reset. This is separate from account block.
