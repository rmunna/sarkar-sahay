## What is Kerala Karunya Health Insurance Scheme?

**Karunya Arogya Suraksha Padhathi (KASP)** — also known as the Kerala Karunya Health Insurance Scheme — provides **financial assistance for hospitalization and medical treatment** to Kerala residents below the poverty line. It is Kerala's implementation of **PM-JAY (Ayushman Bharat)** with state top-ups.

| Coverage | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| PM-JAY central component | ₹5,00,000 per family per year |
| Kerala state top-up (KASP) | Additional ₹5,00,000 for identified critical conditions |
| Total combined | Up to ₹5 lakh (standard) |

## Who is Eligible?

**PM-JAY / KASP eligibility:**
- Families listed in **SECC 2011** socioeconomic survey as deprived, OR
- BPL families with **annual income ≤ ₹3 lakh**
- Kerala state has expanded PM-JAY beyond SECC to include additional categories

> Check your eligibility: Go to [beneficiary.nha.gov.in](https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in) and enter your Aadhaar/name to see if you are in the PM-JAY database.

---

## Critical Illness Top-up Under KASP

KASP additionally covers critical conditions like:
- Cancer (chemotherapy, surgery, radiotherapy)
- Organ transplants
- Rare diseases requiring specialized treatment

For these, the Kerala state government may authorize additional funding beyond the ₹5 lakh PM-JAY limit.

## How to Use KASP / PM-JAY in Kerala

### Step 1 — Check Eligibility and Get Ayushman Card

1. Visit [beneficiary.nha.gov.in](https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in) or the **Ayushman Bharat App**
2. Check if your family is listed
3. Generate your **Ayushman Card (e-card)**

OR visit your nearest **Akshaya Centre** (CSC) for assisted card generation.

### Step 2 — Go to Empanelled Hospital

Find KASP-empanelled hospitals at [arogyakeralam.gov.in](https://arogyakeralam.gov.in).

### Step 3 — Present Card at Arogya Mitra

At the hospital, go to the PM-JAY / KASP Help Desk. Present:
- Ayushman Card (or Aadhaar for verification)
- Ration card (if applicable)

### Step 4 — Cashless Treatment

Eligible procedures are done cashless. Hospital claims from NHA/KASP.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. I am from Kerala and SECC listed. My treatment is in Chennai. Does KASP cover it?**

Yes. PM-JAY (and KASP) is portable. Any KASP/PM-JAY empanelled hospital in any Indian state accepts your Ayushman card.

**2. I am not in SECC but very poor. Can I enroll?**

Kerala has expanded PM-JAY beyond SECC to include additional beneficiaries through state expansion. Contact your nearest **Kerala Health Mission** district office or Akshaya Centre for alternative enrollment routes.

**3. My mother needs a kidney transplant. Is ₹5 lakh enough?**

Kidney transplants can cost ₹5–15 lakh. Under KASP, the Kerala state may provide additional top-up for organ transplants. Approach the hospital's KASP help desk and the Kerala Health Mission for pre-authorization of enhanced limits.

**4. I have an Ayushman card but my name is wrong. How to correct it?**

Visit your nearest **Akshaya Centre** or **District KASP office** with your Aadhaar (which has the correct name) for name correction in the beneficiary database.
