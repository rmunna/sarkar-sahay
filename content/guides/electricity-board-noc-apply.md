## What is an Electricity Board NOC?

A No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the electricity board (DISCOM) is an official document stating that there are no pending dues or disputes on the electricity connection of a property. It certifies that the electricity board has no objection to a specific action — such as property sale, demolition, or new construction.

This NOC is required in several important situations and is issued by the local electricity distribution company (DISCOM).

## When is Electricity Board NOC Required?

- **Property sale or transfer** — buyer or registrar may ask for electricity NOC
- **Building plan approval** — municipal corporations require it for new construction or renovation
- **Demolition of building** — before demolishing a structure with active connection
- **Mortgage or loan** — banks may require it for property-backed loans
- **Connection surrender** — when you want to permanently disconnect
- **Electricity line clearance** — if construction is near high-tension power lines
- **Solar panel installation** — net metering approval often needs NOC
- **Change of land use** — residential to commercial conversion

## Types of Electricity NOC

### 1. No Dues Certificate (NDC)

Certifies that all electricity bills are paid and there are no pending dues on the consumer account. Most commonly required for property transactions.

### 2. Line Clearance NOC

Required when construction or activity is planned near electricity lines (especially high-tension lines). Ensures safe distance is maintained as per Indian Electricity Rules.

### 3. Building Plan NOC

Required by municipal authorities before approving building plans, ensuring the proposed construction doesn't interfere with existing electrical infrastructure.

### 4. Disconnection/Surrender NOC

Issued when you want to permanently surrender an electricity connection, confirming all dues are cleared and the meter is removed.

## Documents Required

1. **Application form** — from DISCOM office or website
2. **Consumer number** — latest electricity bill with consumer details
3. **Identity proof** — Aadhaar card, PAN card, voter ID
4. **Property ownership proof** — sale deed, registry, or allotment letter
5. **All paid bill receipts** — last 6-12 months
6. **Purpose statement** — reason for requesting NOC
7. **Building plan** (for line clearance NOC) — site map showing proposed construction
8. **Photographs** — passport-size photos of applicant

## How to Apply Online

### Step 1: Visit DISCOM Portal

Go to your state DISCOM's official website. Navigate to "Online Services" or "Consumer Services."

### Step 2: Login to Your Account

Use your consumer number and registered mobile to login. New users may need to register first.

### Step 3: Select NOC Service

Look for "Apply for NOC" or "No Dues Certificate" in the service menu.

### Step 4: Fill Application

- Enter consumer/account number
- Select type of NOC required
- Fill in purpose and property details
- Enter contact details

### Step 5: Upload Documents

Upload scanned copies of required documents.

### Step 6: Pay Fee

Pay the processing fee online (₹50-200 depending on DISCOM and NOC type).

### Step 7: Download NOC

Once approved (usually 3-7 working days), download the NOC from the portal. Some DISCOMs issue it instantly if there are no pending dues.

## Offline Application Process

1. Visit your DISCOM subdivision or division office
2. Collect the NOC application form from the counter
3. Fill in details and attach photocopies of documents
4. Submit with the processing fee
5. The office will verify your account for pending dues
6. If clear, NOC is issued in 3-7 working days
7. Collect from the office or receive by post

## Timeline

- **No Dues Certificate:** 1-7 working days (instant if no dues)
- **Line Clearance NOC:** 7-15 working days (site inspection needed)
- **Building Plan NOC:** 7-15 working days
- **Disconnection NOC:** 7-15 working days (after meter removal)

## Important Points

- **Clear all dues first** — NOC will not be issued if there are unpaid bills, penalties, or arrears
- **Meter tampering cases** — if there's any case of meter tampering or electricity theft under investigation, NOC will be withheld
- **Validity** — most NOCs are valid for 3-6 months from the date of issue
- **Multiple connections** — if the property has multiple meters, NOC is needed for each
- **HT connections** — high-tension connections have separate and more detailed NOC process

## What to Do If NOC is Denied

- **Check for pending dues** — pay all outstanding amounts including late fees
- **Resolve disputes** — if there's a billing dispute, file a complaint with the consumer grievance cell
- **Approach ombudsman** — if DISCOM is unreasonably withholding NOC, approach the electricity ombudsman of your state
- **Legal recourse** — consumer court or state electricity regulatory commission

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is electricity NOC mandatory for property registration?

It depends on the state. Some states and sub-registrar offices mandate an electricity NOC for property registration, while others don't. Check with your local sub-registrar office.

### How long is the NOC valid?

Most electricity NOCs are valid for 3-6 months from the date of issue. After that, you need to apply again.

### Can I get NOC if there are pending dues?

No, you must clear all pending dues, including late payment surcharges, before the NOC can be issued. Pay all bills and then apply.

### What if I never had an electricity connection on the property?

If the property never had an electricity connection, you can get a "No Connection Certificate" from the DISCOM stating that no connection exists on the property.

### Can I apply for NOC for someone else's property?

You need an authorization letter or power of attorney from the property owner/connection holder to apply on their behalf.

### Is line clearance NOC different from no dues NOC?

Yes, line clearance NOC is specifically about ensuring safe distance from power lines for construction. No dues NOC is about confirming payment of all bills. These are separate documents.
