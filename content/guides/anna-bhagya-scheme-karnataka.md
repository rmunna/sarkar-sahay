## What is Anna Bhagya Scheme Karnataka?

Anna Bhagya is Karnataka's free rice distribution scheme for Below Poverty Line (BPL) and Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) ration card holders. Under this scheme, eligible families receive 10 kg of rice per person per month completely free of cost, in addition to the 5 kg rice they already receive under the National Food Security Act (NFSA).

Combined with NFSA rice, BPL families in Karnataka receive up to 10 kg of rice per person per month from the Public Distribution System (PDS) either free or at highly subsidised rates.

**Current status (as of 2026):** The scheme has seen changes in implementation. From time to time, cash transfers have been used as an alternative when state procurement of additional rice was not possible. Check with your Fair Price Shop (Ration Shop) for the latest status in your area.

## Who Gets What — Rice Distribution Table

| Card Type | NFSA Rice | Anna Bhagya Additional | Total |
|-----------|-----------|----------------------|-------|
| AAY (Antyodaya) | 35 kg/family | up to 10 kg/person | Highest entitlement |
| BPL (Priority Household) | 5 kg/person | up to 5 kg/person | up to 10 kg/person |

Rice is distributed at: **₹0 (free)** for Anna Bhagya allocation.

## Who is Eligible?

- Residents of **Karnataka** holding a valid **BPL (Below Poverty Line)** or **AAY (Antyodaya Anna Yojana)** ration card
- Card must be registered under the **National Food Security Act (NFSA)** through the Karnataka food department
- The ration card must be active and linked to the correct Fair Price Shop

**Not eligible:**
- APL (Above Poverty Line) ration card holders
- Families with annual income above the state poverty threshold
- Government employees and income tax payers

## Documents Required (for ration card verification/new enrollment)

1. Aadhaar card for all family members
2. BPL or AAY ration card
3. Mobile number linked to Aadhaar (for OTP-based authentication at ration shop)
4. Residence proof in Karnataka

## How to Get Anna Bhagya Rice — Step by Step

**If you already have a BPL/AAY ration card:**
1. Visit your designated **Fair Price Shop (FPS / Ration Shop)** in your area
2. Carry your **ration card** and **Aadhaar card**
3. Your details are verified via **ePoS (electronic Point of Sale)** machine using **biometric (fingerprint)** or **OTP**
4. Rice is weighed and given to you based on your family members on the ration card
5. Rice is distributed on specific dates every month — check with your shop owner for the schedule

**If you don't have a BPL ration card and want to apply:**
1. Go to **ahara.kar.nic.in** (Karnataka Food Department portal)
2. Click **"Apply for Ration Card"** or visit your nearest **Nadakacheri / Seva Sindhu** center
3. Fill the application with all family members' details and Aadhaar numbers
4. Submit with income certificate, Aadhaar, and address proof
5. A field verification is done by the food inspector
6. If approved, a ration card is issued and you are added to the Fair Price Shop roster

## How to Check If Your Name is in Anna Bhagya List

1. Visit **ahara.kar.nic.in**
2. Click **"Beneficiary / Ration Card Details"**
3. Enter your ration card number or Aadhaar number
4. Your details, including the FPS name and monthly entitlement, will be displayed

Alternatively:
- Visit your **Gram Panchayat** or **ward office** for the beneficiary list
- Call the Karnataka food helpline: **1967** (toll-free)

## How to Complain About Not Getting Rice

If rice was not given or quantity was short:
1. Talk to the FPS (ration shop) owner first
2. If unresolved, file a complaint at **ahara.kar.nic.in** → "Grievances"
3. Or call **Food Helpline: 1967** (toll-free, Karnataka)
4. Or submit a complaint at your Taluk Supply Office (TSO)
5. You can also visit the District Food and Civil Supplies office

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many kg of rice does each family member get?
Under Anna Bhagya combined with NFSA: Priority Household (BPL) card holders get up to 5 kg per person per month from NFSA plus additional Anna Bhagya rice (amounts may vary by current policy). AAY families get 35 kg per family. Check current rates at your FPS.

### Can I transfer rice entitlement to another person in my family?
No — rice must be collected by a member of the household. If the primary member is unavailable, another family member listed on the ration card can collect with their Aadhaar card.

### What if my Aadhaar is not linked to my ration card?
Aadhaar linking is required for biometric verification at the FPS. Visit ahara.kar.nic.in or your Nadakacheri to link Aadhaar to your ration card. Until it's linked, you may be unable to collect rice at some shops.

### I just moved to Karnataka. Can I get rice?
You must first get a Karnataka ration card. Surrender your previous state's ration card and apply for a new one at ahara.kar.nic.in or a Seva Sindhu center.

### Is cash given instead of rice sometimes?
Yes — at times the state has provided direct cash transfers (typically ₹170 per kg × entitlement) when rice stock from central government was insufficient. This may vary by district and time period.

### Can I change my Fair Price Shop?
Yes — you can apply to change your FPS at ahara.kar.nic.in or the Taluk Supply Office if you have moved or have issues with your current shop.
