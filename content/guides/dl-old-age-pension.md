## What is Delhi Old Age Pension Scheme?

The **Delhi Old Age Pension Scheme** provides monthly financial assistance to senior citizens residing in Delhi. Under this scheme, elderly persons aged **60 years and above** receive **₹2,000 to ₹2,500 per month** depending on their age group. It is administered by the **Department of Social Welfare, Government of NCT of Delhi**.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Age:** Must be **60 years or older**
2. **Residency:** Must have lived in Delhi for at least **5 years**
3. **Income:** Annual family income should not exceed **₹1,00,000**
4. **Other Pension:** Should not be receiving any other pension (government/PSU)
5. **Aadhaar:** Required for DBT

## Pension Amount

| Age Group | Monthly Pension |
|-----------|----------------|
| 60-69 years | ₹2,000 |
| 70+ years | ₹2,500 |

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar Card** (linked to bank account)
- **Age Proof** (Birth Certificate / Aadhaar / Voter ID)
- **Proof of Delhi Residence** for 5+ years (Voter ID / Ration Card / Electricity Bill)
- **Income Certificate** (self-declaration or from SDM)
- **Bank Passbook** (Aadhaar-linked account)
- **Passport-size photographs** (2 copies)

## How to Apply

### Online (e-District Delhi)

1. **Visit** [edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in](https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in)
2. **Register** / Login (use mobile number + OTP)
3. **Select** "Old Age Pension" under Social Welfare schemes
4. **Fill** the form with personal, age, income, and bank details
5. **Upload** documents
6. **Submit** and save the application ID
7. **Track** status online

### Offline

1. **Visit** the **District Social Welfare Office** or **SDM Office**
2. **Collect** the pension application form
3. **Fill** and attach documents
4. **Submit** and get acknowledgement receipt
5. **Field verification** by social welfare inspector

## Processing Time

- **Verification:** 30-45 days
- **Approval:** After field verification
- **First Payment:** 1-2 months after approval
- **Ongoing:** Monthly via DBT

## Important Tips

1. **5-Year Residency:** You must prove 5 years of Delhi residence — Voter ID is the strongest proof
2. **Aadhaar-Bank Link:** Mandatory for DBT
3. **Life Certificate:** Submit Jeevan Pramaan annually
4. **No Duplicate Pension:** Cannot combine with widow/disability pension
5. **e-District Portal:** Track everything online at the Delhi e-District portal

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. What is the pension amount?
₹2,000/month for ages 60-69 and **₹2,500/month** for ages 70+.

### Q2. What is the income limit?
Annual family income should not exceed **₹1,00,000**.

### Q3. How many years of Delhi residence is needed?
At least **5 years** of continuous residence in Delhi.

### Q4. Can I apply online?
Yes, at [edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in](https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in).

### Q5. Is Aadhaar mandatory?
Yes, for DBT disbursement.

### Q6. Can I get Old Age Pension and Widow Pension both?
No, only one pension at a time.

### Q7. How is pension paid?
Monthly, directly to your Aadhaar-linked bank account via DBT.
