# Bali / Indonesia Visa from India 2026 — Complete Guide

Bali is one of the most popular international destinations for Indians. The visa process is simple — Indians get Visa on Arrival at Indonesian airports, or can apply for e-VOA online before departure to skip queues.

---

## Do Indians Need a Visa for Bali/Indonesia?

Yes — Indonesia is not visa-free for Indian passport holders. But the process is very easy:

| Option | How | Cost | Stay |
|--------|-----|------|------|
| **Visa on Arrival (VoA)** | At airport on arrival | USD 35 (₹2,900) | 30 days (extendable once) |
| **e-VOA (before travel)** | Online at molina.imigrasi.go.id | USD 35 + USD 3 admin fee | 30 days (extendable) |
| **Tourist Visa B211A** | At Indonesian consulate | USD 35–50 | 60 days |

**Most travelers use VoA or e-VOA** — simple, affordable, no prior consulate visit needed.

---

## Visa on Arrival (VoA) — On Arrival at Airport

Available at major Indonesian entry points:
- Ngurah Rai International Airport, Bali (DPS)
- Soekarno-Hatta International Airport, Jakarta (CGK)
- Juanda Airport, Surabaya
- I Gusti Ngurah Rai, Bali
- Several sea/land entry points

### What You Need at VoA Counter

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| **Passport** | Valid for 6+ months beyond your Indonesia stay |
| **Return/onward ticket** | Confirmed departure ticket out of Indonesia |
| **Hotel booking** | At least first accommodation |
| **USD 35 cash (or card)** | VoA fee — USD accepted; some counters now accept credit cards, but bring USD cash as backup |
| **Arrival card** | Fill on the plane or at airport (given to you before landing) |

**Process**: Land → go to VoA counter before immigration → pay → get stamp → go to immigration. Queue time: 20–45 minutes at Bali airport peak hours.

---

## e-VOA (Online Visa on Arrival) — Apply Before Travel

Apply at [molina.imigrasi.go.id](https://molina.imigrasi.go.id) 1–7 days before travel:

1. Register and log in
2. Select "Visa on Arrival" → Tourist
3. Fill details: passport number, arrival info, hotel
4. Upload passport scan (bio-data page)
5. Pay USD 35 + USD 3 admin fee online (Visa/Mastercard)
6. Receive e-VOA PDF via email in a few hours to 24 hours
7. At airport: go to **e-VOA lane** (separate, faster queue) with your e-VOA QR code

**Advantage**: Faster immigration lane, no counting cash at the VoA counter.

## Extending Your VoA in Bali

VoA is initially 30 days. If you want to stay longer:
- Apply for **30-day extension** at any Indonesian immigration office (Kantor Imigrasi) before your 30 days expire
- Fee: IDR 500,000 (₹2,500 approx)
- Maximum total stay: 60 days (30 + 30 extension)

Popular immigration office in Bali: Jalan Raya Puputan, Denpasar (online appointment recommended via imigrasi.go.id).

## Entry Requirements — What Indonesia Checks

### Customs Regulations (Strict)

Indonesia is strict about customs declarations:
- **Alcohol**: Declare if bringing more than 1L; beyond that, duty applies
- **Cigarettes**: Up to 200 cigarettes duty-free
- **Foreign cash**: Declare if above USD 100,000 equivalent
- **Food items**: Some food products restricted (fresh fruit, meat, milk products)
- **Drugs/narcotics**: Zero tolerance — penalties include death penalty for trafficking

### Funds Requirement

Indonesia does not formally publish a minimum funds requirement, but immigration officers occasionally check:
- USD 1,500–2,000 per person equivalent is commonly cited
- Have access to funds visible in your banking app

## Bali — Practical Travel Information

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Currency | Indonesian Rupiah (IDR). ATMs widely available; notify bank before travel |
| SIM card | Get Telkomsel or XL at airport — ₹500–800 for 10 days unlimited data |
| Transport | Grab (ride-hailing) is reliable in Kuta/Seminyak/Ubud areas; rent a scooter (requires international driving permit) |
| Language | Indonesian (Bahasa); English widely spoken in tourist areas |
| Best time | April–October (dry season). November–March is monsoon season — still beautiful but rainier |

**Bali temples**: Wear temple scarf (sarong) to enter Hindu temples — usually provided at the entrance for IDR 10,000 (₹50) donation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can Indians enter Bali without any visa?

No — but the process is very simple. VoA at the airport takes 20–45 minutes and costs USD 35. e-VOA online before travel is even easier.

### Is travel insurance required for Indonesia?

Not required for the VoA, but strongly recommended. Indonesian hospitals are decent in tourist areas, but private hospitals for serious emergencies cost significantly.

### I'm flying via Singapore or Kuala Lumpur to Bali. Do I need a transit visa there?

For Singapore transit: No visa required for Indian passport holders for short connections (no overnight stay). For Malaysia transit: KLIA Transit Pass available for free. Check current requirements.

### Bali in monsoon season — is it worth going?

Monsoon (November–March) means daily rain (usually afternoon showers, not all-day). Accommodation is cheaper by 20–40%. Hindu festival season (January–February) is actually excellent with full temple ceremonies happening. Many travelers prefer the quieter, greener Bali.
