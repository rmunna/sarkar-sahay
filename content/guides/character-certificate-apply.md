## What is a Character Certificate?

A Character Certificate (also called Good Conduct Certificate or Moral Character Certificate) is an official document certifying that an individual has **good moral character and no criminal background**. It is issued by the **local police station** or district administration based on police verification.

Character certificates are commonly required for:
- **Government job applications** and competitive exams
- **Educational admissions** (college, university, professional courses)
- **Passport application** (as supporting document)
- **Visa applications**
- **Professional licences** (arms licence, etc.)
- **Army, Navy, Air Force** recruitment
- **State government schemes** and benefits

This is different from a [Police Clearance Certificate (PCC)](/guide/police-clearance-certificate), which is issued by the Passport Office for international purposes.

## Types of Character Certificates

### 1. Police Character Certificate
- Issued by the **local police station** or SP office
- Based on police records verification
- Required for government jobs, arms licences, security clearances
- Most commonly requested type

### 2. Educational Character Certificate
- Issued by your **last attended educational institution**
- School, college, or university provides it
- Required for further admissions and some job applications

### 3. Employer Character Certificate
- Issued by your **previous or current employer**
- Confirms good conduct during employment
- Used for new job applications

## Documents Required

### For Police Character Certificate
- **Application form** (available at police station or online portal)
- **Identity proof** — Aadhaar card, PAN card, voter ID — see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
- **Address proof** — Aadhaar, utility bill, rent agreement
- **Passport-size photographs** (2-4 copies)
- **Purpose certificate** — Letter from the requesting organization/employer
- **Affidavit** (in some states) — Self-declaration of no criminal record

### For Educational Character Certificate
- **Application to the institution** (written request)
- **Student ID** or admission details
- **Last mark sheet** or leaving certificate
- **Fee receipt** (if applicable)

## How to Apply Online

Many states offer online character certificate applications through their e-district portals:

### General Process (State E-District Portals)
1. Visit your state's e-district or service portal:
   - **UP:** edistrict.up.gov.in
   - **Bihar:** serviceonline.bihar.gov.in
   - **MP:** mpedistrict.gov.in
   - **Rajasthan:** emitra.rajasthan.gov.in
   - **Maharashtra:** aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in
2. Register with your mobile number and Aadhaar
3. Select **"Character Certificate"** from the services list
4. Fill the application form with personal details
5. Upload required documents (photo, ID proof, address proof)
6. Pay the fee online (₹10 – ₹100)
7. Submit and note the application number
8. Track status online

### Through CSC (Common Service Centre)
If online access is difficult:
1. Visit your nearest **CSC (Jan Seva Kendra)**
2. The operator fills the application for you
3. Provide documents and photographs
4. Pay the fee plus service charge
5. Collect the certificate when ready

## How to Apply Offline

### At Police Station
1. Visit your **local police station** (based on your residential address)
2. Meet the **Station House Officer (SHO)** or designated officer
3. Submit a written application stating the purpose
4. Provide identity proof, address proof, and photographs
5. The police may conduct a **field verification** at your address
6. Collect the certificate after verification (7-15 days)

### Through District Administration
1. Visit the **District Magistrate's office** or SDM office
2. Fill the prescribed application form
3. Submit documents and affidavit
4. Certificate issued after police verification report

## Validity

- Character certificates are typically valid for **6 months** from the date of issue
- Some organizations may accept certificates up to **1 year** old
- For government jobs, the certificate should ideally be **not more than 6 months old** at the time of document verification

## Character Certificate Format

```
OFFICE OF THE STATION HOUSE OFFICER
[Police Station Name], [District]

CHARACTER CERTIFICATE

This is to certify that Shri/Smt. [Name], S/o, D/o, W/o [Parent/Spouse Name],
resident of [Full Address], has been verified and no criminal case is found
registered against him/her in the records of this police station.

As per the verification report, the above-named person bears a good
moral character.

This certificate is issued on his/her request for the purpose of [Purpose].

Date: [Date]
Place: [Place]

[Signature]
Station House Officer
[Police Station Name]
[Official Seal]
```

## Important Tips

1. **Apply early** — Start the process at least 3-4 weeks before you need the certificate
2. **Check state-specific portals** — Many states now offer online applications, saving time and visits
3. **Be honest** — If you have any criminal record, disclose it; providing false information can lead to rejection and legal trouble
4. **Keep multiple copies** — Get 2-3 copies as different organizations may require originals
5. **Address consistency** — Your address on the application should match your ID proof; discrepancies delay verification

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is a character certificate the same as a police clearance certificate?
No, a character certificate is issued by the local police station for domestic purposes (jobs, admissions). A [Police Clearance Certificate (PCC)](/guide/police-clearance-certificate) is issued by the Passport Office for international purposes (visa, immigration).

### Can I get a character certificate from any police station?
The certificate must be issued by the police station that has **jurisdiction over your residential address**. You cannot get it from a police station in a different area.

### What if the police station delays issuing my character certificate?
File a complaint with the Superintendent of Police (SP) or the District Magistrate. You can also apply through the e-district portal where processing is time-bound.

### Is a character certificate required for private jobs?
Most private companies do their own background verification and don't require a police character certificate. However, some sectors (banking, security) may require it.

### Can a character certificate be denied?
Yes, if there's a pending criminal case or FIR against you, the character certificate may be denied or issued with remarks. Minor compounded offences may not affect it.

### How many times can I apply for a character certificate?
There's no limit. You can apply as many times as needed, but each certificate has its own validity period and you'll need to go through the verification process each time.
