## What is Mukhyamantri Ladli Bahna Yojana?

**Mukhyamantri Ladli Bahna Yojana** is the flagship scheme of the Madhya Pradesh government that gives **₹1,250 per month** (targeted to reach ₹3,000/month eventually) directly to the bank accounts of married women aged 23 to 60 years. The scheme was launched in March 2023 and is currently benefiting over **1.29 crore women** in Madhya Pradesh.

The monthly amount is transferred on the **10th of every month** via DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer) to the woman's own bank account. No middlemen, no paperwork at offices — the money comes directly.

## Who is Eligible?

**Must meet ALL of the following:**
- **Married woman** residing in Madhya Pradesh (divorced, widowed, and abandoned women are also eligible)
- Age between **23 and 60 years** as of January 1 of the current year
- A domicile of Madhya Pradesh
- Family's annual income must be **less than ₹2.5 lakh**
- Family should NOT own more than **5 acres of agricultural land**
- Beneficiary or her family should **not be a current/former income taxpayer**

**Not eligible if:**
- Husband or wife is a government employee (permanent)
- Family member is a current/former elected representative (MP/MLA/Zila Panchayat/Mayor)
- Family income exceeds ₹2.5 lakh/year or owns 5+ acres of land

## Key Benefits

- **₹1,250/month** direct to your bank account (10th of every month)
- Government has announced it will increase to **₹3,000/month** in phases
- No repayment — it is a grant, not a loan
- **Linked schemes:** Eligible women automatically get gas cylinder at ₹450 (Ladli Bahna Gas Yojana), free health coverage under Ayushman Bharat

## How to Apply — Step by Step

> **Important:** New applications open periodically. Check the portal for the current application window.

### During Application Window (Camp-Based Process)

1. Visit your nearest **Gram Panchayat / Ward Office / Camp** set up by the government (the scheme uses a camp model — officials come to your village/ward)
2. Carry **all documents** listed below
3. The camp operator takes your photo (eKYC)
4. Form is filled on the spot by the operator with your Aadhaar OTP verification
5. You receive an **application acknowledgement slip** with your registration number
6. No need to go anywhere else — the government verifies and adds you to the list

### Online Application (When Portal is Open)

1. Go to **[cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in](https://cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in)**
2. Click **"Application Form"**
3. Enter your Aadhaar number → verify with OTP
4. Fill family details, bank account number
5. Submit and note your application number

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card** (mandatory — linked to mobile for OTP)
2. **Samagra ID** (MP's family ID — get it from samagra.gov.in if not yet created)
3. **Bank account** (must be in the woman's own name — Aadhaar-linked for DBT)
4. **Mobile number** linked to Aadhaar

> **No income certificate or caste certificate is required.** The government verifies income through Samagra data and land records.

## How to Check Status

**Application Status:**
1. Go to [cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in](https://cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in)
2. Click **"Application and Payment Status"**
3. Enter your registration number or Aadhaar number → check

**Payment Status:**
1. Check your bank passbook or bank account statement
2. The credit shows as "DBT" or "Ladli Bahna" on the 10th of each month
3. Check via PFMS: [pfms.nic.in](https://pfms.nic.in) → "Know Your Payment"

**Beneficiary List:**
1. Go to cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in → "Beneficiary List"
2. Select your district, block, gram panchayat/ward
3. Search for your name

## Payment Dates

Ladli Bahna Yojana payment is released on the **10th of every month**. It is transferred directly to your linked bank account. If the 10th falls on a bank holiday, it may be credited a day earlier.

## Common Problems and Solutions

### Bank account not in own name / payment not received
The bank account must be in **the woman's own name**, not husband's or parents'. Open a new savings account in your name at any bank and update it on the portal.

### Aadhaar not linked to bank account
Go to your bank branch with Aadhaar card and request Aadhaar-bank linking. Once done, payments start in the next cycle.

### Samagra ID not available
Visit your nearest gram panchayat/ward office or create it at [samagra.gov.in](https://samagra.gov.in). Samagra ID is an MP-specific family identity number.

### Name missing from beneficiary list
Visit your local camp or Gram Panchayat office. File an objection at cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in → "Objection/Claim" during the objection window.

### Application rejected
Common reasons: husband is a govt employee, income/land exceeds limit, or age outside 23-60. Check the rejection reason on the portal and correct/appeal if the rejection was incorrect.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can unmarried women apply?
No — the scheme is for married women. However, **divorced, widowed, and abandoned women** are explicitly included.

### Can a woman receive both Ladli Bahna and other state schemes?
Yes — Ladli Bahna beneficiaries automatically get access to Ladli Bahna Gas Yojana (₹450 cooking gas), and their Ayushman health card is active.

### How do I update my bank account number?
Visit the nearest Jan Seva Kendra or camp site. Take your Aadhaar and new bank passbook. The operator can update the bank account in the system.

### My husband is a government employee but retired. Am I eligible?
Retired government employees' families are eligible as long as the annual family income is below ₹2.5 lakh and they do not hold a political post.

### Is the scheme also for urban women?
Yes — both rural and urban women (town/city residents) in Madhya Pradesh are eligible. Urban applications are processed through Ward offices.

### What if applications are currently closed?
The scheme was done in phases. If applications are closed, watch for new registration rounds at cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in or visit your local Gram Panchayat for the next camp.
