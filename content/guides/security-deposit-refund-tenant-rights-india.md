# Security Deposit Not Refunded India 2026 — How to Recover It

Security deposit disputes are the most common landlord-tenant conflict in India. Landlords often delay, deduct arbitrarily, or refuse entirely. But your rights are clear — and recovery is possible without expensive litigation.

---

## What Can a Landlord Legally Deduct from Security Deposit?

**Valid deductions** (landlord must prove with evidence):
- Actual damage you caused beyond normal use (broken fixtures, damaged walls beyond normal marks, stained flooring beyond normal wear)
- Unpaid rent at the time of vacating
- Utility bills in your name that weren't cleared
- Cleaning charges if the property was left in unusable condition (with photos/invoices)

**Invalid deductions** (landlord cannot claim these):
- Normal wear and tear (paint fading, slight scuffs on walls, normal floor wear — these are landlord's maintenance cost)
- Repair costs for items that were already old/broken before you moved in
- "Renovation" costs where they're upgrading the property using your deposit
- Vague charges like "miscellaneous damage" without itemized bills
- Deductions not mentioned in the agreement

**Key principle**: The landlord must **itemize every deduction with evidence** (photos, invoices). A claim of "general damage" without specifics is not legally valid.

---

## How Long Does a Landlord Have to Return the Deposit?

There is no central law mandating a specific timeline for deposit return. Check your rent agreement — most specify 30–60 days after vacating.

**If your agreement doesn't specify**: Courts and consumer forums generally hold that **30 days** is a reasonable period. If deposit is not returned within 30 days without valid deduction reasons, it's a violation.

## Before You Escalate — Document Everything

**At the time of vacating**:
1. Do a **joint inspection** with the landlord — both parties walk through the property together
2. Take **photos and video** of every room, every fixture (timestamp them)
3. Get a written acknowledgment that you've returned the keys and vacated
4. Keep all communication (WhatsApp, email) — screenshots are evidence

**If landlord refuses joint inspection**: Take photos yourself and send them to the landlord via WhatsApp with a text like "Photos of the property at time of handover on [date]." This creates documented proof.

## Step-by-Step Recovery Process

### Step 1: WhatsApp/Email Reminder

Send a formal message (not just verbal): "Requesting return of security deposit of ₹[amount] as per our agreement dated [date], since I vacated on [date]. Please confirm expected return date."

Give 7–10 days for response.

### Step 2: Send Legal Notice

If no satisfactory response, send a **legal notice via registered post** (or through an advocate):
- State the deposit amount, date of vacating, demand for full return within 15 days
- Reference the rent agreement
- State you'll approach consumer forum / Rent Court if not resolved

**Legal notice cost**: ₹1,000–₹5,000 through an advocate. Many disputes resolve at this stage — landlords don't want formal legal proceedings.

**You can send a legal notice yourself** (no advocate required): Write a formal letter, send via registered post with acknowledgment. Keep the receipt.

### Step 3: File at Consumer Forum (Fastest Route for Up to ₹50 Lakh)

**For amounts up to ₹50 lakh**: District Consumer Commission is the fastest option.

**Grounds**: The rent agreement is a "service" contract — landlord failing to return deposit after providing accommodation services is a "deficiency in service" under Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

**How to file**:
1. Go to [edaakhil.nic.in](https://edaakhil.nic.in) → "New Complaint"
2. Register, fill complainant (you) and opposite party (landlord) details
3. Attach: rent agreement, receipt of deposit payment, vacating photos, all communications
4. Describe the deficiency: "Landlord withheld security deposit without valid reason"
5. Relief sought: Refund of ₹[amount] + 12% interest + compensation for harassment + litigation costs
6. Filing fee: ₹100–₹2,000 depending on amount (see the fee schedule on the portal)
7. Forum typically orders response within 30 days, hearing within 90 days

**Success rate**: Consumer forums are tenant-friendly in clear deposit-return cases. If you have documentation of vacating and deposit payment, outcomes are often favorable.

### Step 4: Rent Court / Civil Court

**For cases under state Rent Control Acts** (Mumbai, Delhi, Kolkata have strict rent control laws):
- File complaint in designated Rent Court
- Slower than consumer forum (6–24 months) but stronger jurisdiction

**For large deposits (>₹50 lakh)**: File civil suit in civil court for recovery of money + interest.

## Illegal Eviction and Landlord Harassment

**Landlord cannot**:
- Change locks while you're living there (even if rent is due)
- Cut electricity/water to force you out
- Enter without notice (most agreements require 24-hour notice)
- Throw out your belongings
- Threaten you physically

**These are criminal acts**:
- Changing locks = wrongful restraint/force → FIR under IPC Section 441 (criminal trespass)
- Cutting utilities = coercion → IPC Section 503 (criminal intimidation)

**If landlord locks you out**: Call police immediately, file complaint. Police must restore your access as a lawful tenant.

## Security Deposit Amount — What's Legal?

**No national law caps security deposit**. But:
- Maharashtra: Maximum 3 months advance rent (under Maharashtra Rent Control Act for non-exempt properties)
- Delhi: No cap but 1–3 months is standard
- Bengaluru/Karnataka: 2–10 months is common (no legal cap)
- Chennai/Tamil Nadu: Typically 10 months advance (different structure — advance rent, not separate deposit)

**Tamil Nadu/Chennai specific**: "Advance" is functionally the security deposit but the structure differs — no separate deposit, advance rent held instead. Same legal principles for recovery apply.

---

## When Agreement Has "Forfeiture" Clause

Some agreements say "security deposit forfeited if tenant leaves before [X] months."

**Enforceability**: Courts look at reasonableness. A clause forfeiting entire 10-month advance if tenant leaves after 8 months of a 12-month term has been held unreasonable by consumer forums.

**Valid lock-in clauses**: If you leave before completing 11 months, losing 1 month as penalty is typically upheld. Losing all 3–10 months for leaving 1 month early is typically held unreasonable.

---

## Claiming Interest on Delayed Deposit

Consumer forums routinely award 12% per annum interest on withheld deposits from the date of vacating. On a ₹1 lakh deposit held for 6 months: ₹6,000 interest.

You can also claim compensation for "mental agony and harassment" — consumer forums award ₹5,000–₹50,000 depending on severity.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My landlord says they'll return deposit once they get a new tenant. Is this valid?

No. Getting a new tenant is the landlord's responsibility and unrelated to your deposit return. Your deposit must be returned based on the agreement terms — not contingent on future tenancy.

### I don't have a written receipt for the deposit payment. Can I still claim?

Yes — bank transfer records (NEFT/UPI payment to landlord), WhatsApp acknowledgment, or any written communication about the deposit amount is evidence. If you paid cash, any reference to the amount in messages or the agreement helps establish the claim.

### Landlord claims property damage exceeds my deposit. What now?

Ask for an itemized written claim with photos and invoices within 7 days. Review each item. Normal wear and tear is not your liability. For items you did damage, you owe the depreciated value (not replacement cost for a 5-year-old item). Dispute inflated claims via consumer forum.

### I'm still living in the property — this guide doesn't apply yet, right?

Correct — this guide is for deposit recovery after vacating. If you're still a tenant and facing harassment, consult a lawyer about your Rent Control Act rights and the legal eviction process your landlord must follow.
