# GST Invoice Mismatch? Fix ITC Reconciliation Errors

One of the most common GST compliance issues is mismatch between Input Tax Credit (ITC) claimed in GSTR-3B and what appears in GSTR-2A/2B. Such mismatches can lead to ITC reversals, notices, and penalties. This guide explains how to identify, fix, and prevent ITC reconciliation errors.

## Understanding GSTR-2A, GSTR-2B, and GSTR-3B

| Return | What It Contains | Generated By |
|--------|-----------------|--------------|
| GSTR-2A | Dynamic view of all inward supplies based on suppliers' GSTR-1 filings | Auto-generated from suppliers' GSTR-1 |
| GSTR-2B | Static monthly ITC statement (fixed for each period) | Auto-generated on 14th of following month |
| GSTR-3B | Self-declared return where you claim ITC | Filed by you |

**Key point:** GSTR-2B is the primary document for ITC matching. It is a static statement — once generated, it does not change for that period. GSTR-2A is dynamic and keeps updating as suppliers file/amend their returns.

## Common ITC Mismatch Scenarios and Fixes

### 1. ITC Claimed in GSTR-3B But Not in GSTR-2A/2B

**Why this happens:**
- Your supplier has not filed their GSTR-1
- Supplier filed GSTR-1 with incorrect invoice details (wrong GSTIN, invoice number, or amount)
- Supplier filed GSTR-1 late (after your GSTR-2B was generated)

**How to fix:**
1. **Identify mismatched invoices:** Download GSTR-2B from the portal and compare with your purchase register
2. **Contact your supplier** — request them to:
   - File their pending GSTR-1 if not filed
   - Amend incorrect invoices in their next GSTR-1 using amendment tables
3. **Keep documentary proof** — maintain valid tax invoices, payment proof, and goods receipt notes
4. **Claim ITC with Rule 36(4) limits** — you can claim ITC beyond GSTR-2B but only up to 5% of eligible ITC available in GSTR-2B
5. If the supplier does not respond, consider adjusting future business or withholding payments until compliance

### 2. Excess ITC in GSTR-2A/2B But Not Claimed

**Why this happens:**
- You missed claiming ITC for valid invoices
- You were conservative in claiming to avoid mismatches

**How to fix:**
1. This is not a compliance issue — you can claim the missed ITC in subsequent periods
2. ITC can be claimed up to November 30 of the following financial year or the date of filing the annual return (GSTR-9), whichever is earlier
3. Review GSTR-2B monthly to ensure you claim all eligible ITC

### 3. Duplicate Invoices in GSTR-2A/2B

**Why this happens:**
- Supplier uploaded the same invoice twice in GSTR-1
- Supplier filed an amendment that created a duplicate entry

**How to fix:**
1. Do not claim ITC twice — only claim once per unique invoice
2. Inform the supplier to issue a credit note for the duplicate entry
3. The credit note will automatically reduce the ITC in your GSTR-2A/2B

### 4. ITC Reversal Due to Mismatch

**When ITC reversal is required:**
- ITC claimed exceeds what is available in GSTR-2B beyond Rule 36(4) limits
- Supplier has not filed returns for consecutive periods
- Payment not made to supplier within 180 days of invoice date (Section 16(2))

**How to reverse:**
1. In GSTR-3B, go to Table 4(B) — ITC Reversed
2. Enter the amount of ITC being reversed under the appropriate head (IGST, CGST, SGST)
3. You can reclaim the reversed ITC in a later period when the condition is fulfilled (e.g., supplier files their return)
4. Maintain a reconciliation sheet tracking all reversals and reclaims

### 5. Rule 36(4) — ITC Restriction on Unmatched Invoices

**What Rule 36(4) says:**
- ITC on invoices/debit notes that are **not reflected in GSTR-2B** is restricted to **5% of the eligible ITC** available in GSTR-2B for that period

**Example:**
- Eligible ITC available in GSTR-2B: ₹10,00,000
- Maximum additional ITC you can claim (not in GSTR-2B): ₹50,000 (5% of ₹10,00,000)
- Total maximum ITC claimable: ₹10,50,000

**How to comply:**
1. Download GSTR-2B on the 14th of each month
2. Calculate 5% of matched ITC
3. If your total ITC claim exceeds matched + 5%, reduce the excess
4. Track the restricted ITC — claim it in future periods when invoices appear in GSTR-2B

## How to Reconcile ITC: Step-by-Step Process

1. **Download GSTR-2B** from the GST portal for the relevant period
2. **Export your purchase register** from your accounting software
3. **Match invoice-by-invoice** — compare GSTIN, invoice number, date, taxable value, and tax amount
4. **Categorize mismatches:**
   - Missing in GSTR-2B → Contact supplier
   - Wrong amount → Contact supplier to amend
   - Extra in GSTR-2B → Check if you missed recording a purchase
   - Duplicate → Ignore the duplicate
5. **Calculate Rule 36(4) limit** and adjust ITC claim accordingly
6. **File GSTR-3B** with the reconciled ITC figure
7. **Document everything** — keep the reconciliation sheet for audit purposes

## Vendor Follow-Up Process

When invoices are missing from your GSTR-2B:

1. **Send a formal communication** to the supplier listing the missing invoices with details (invoice number, date, amount, tax)
2. **Set a deadline** — typically 7-10 days for the supplier to file/amend GSTR-1
3. **Follow up** via phone and email if no response
4. **Escalate** — if the supplier consistently fails to comply:
   - Consider withholding future payments until compliance
   - Evaluate whether to continue the business relationship
   - You may report non-compliant suppliers to the jurisdictional GST officer
5. **Protect yourself** — maintain all purchase invoices, payment records, and communication proof

## Amendment Process for Incorrect Invoices

If your supplier filed incorrect details in GSTR-1:

1. Supplier must use the **Amendment tables** in their GSTR-1:
   - Table 9A: Amendment of B2B invoices
   - Table 9B: Amendment of credit/debit notes
   - Table 9C: Amendment of export invoices
2. Amendments can be made in the GSTR-1 of any subsequent period
3. The corrected invoice will reflect in your GSTR-2A/2B after the supplier files the amended GSTR-1
4. Deadline for amendments: November 30 of the following financial year or date of annual return filing

---

## Important Tips

1. Reconcile ITC **every month** before filing GSTR-3B — do not wait for year-end
2. Use the GSTR-2B (static statement) as the primary document for ITC matching, not GSTR-2A
3. Maintain a vendor compliance tracker to identify habitually non-compliant suppliers
4. Keep all purchase invoices, payment proofs, and delivery records for at least 6 years
5. File your [GST returns on time](/guide/gst-return-filing-error-fix) to avoid cascading compliance issues

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. What is the difference between GSTR-2A and GSTR-2B?
GSTR-2A is dynamic — it updates whenever your supplier files or amends their GSTR-1. GSTR-2B is static — it is generated once on the 14th of each month and does not change. GSTR-2B is used for ITC matching under Rule 36(4).

### Q2. Can I claim ITC if the invoice is not in GSTR-2B?
Yes, but only up to 5% of the eligible ITC available in GSTR-2B for that period (Rule 36(4)). You must have a valid tax invoice and proof of receipt of goods/services.

### Q3. What happens if I claim excess ITC beyond Rule 36(4) limit?
You will need to reverse the excess ITC with interest at 18% per annum. If detected during audit or assessment, penalties may also apply.

### Q4. My supplier has shut down and will not file GSTR-1. Can I still claim ITC?
If the supplier does not file GSTR-1, the invoices will not appear in your GSTR-2B. You can claim only within Rule 36(4) limits. If the supplier's registration is cancelled, ITC on invoices issued after cancellation date is not available.

### Q5. How often should I reconcile ITC?
Monthly reconciliation is strongly recommended — ideally before filing GSTR-3B. Annual reconciliation should also be done before filing GSTR-9 (annual return).

### Q6. Can the GST department reject my ITC claim if it does not match GSTR-2A?
Yes. During assessment or audit, the department may disallow ITC that is not reflected in GSTR-2A/2B and exceeds Rule 36(4) limits. You will need to prove the genuineness of the transaction with supporting documents.

### Q7. Is there a tool on the GST portal for ITC reconciliation?
Yes. The GST portal provides a "Match/Mismatch" report under the Returns section. You can also use the GSTR-2B download to compare with your records. Many accounting software tools also offer automated reconciliation features.
