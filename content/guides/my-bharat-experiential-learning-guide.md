# MY Bharat Experiential Learning Guide

**Quick answer:** MY Bharat Experiential Learning Programmes are official youth opportunities where eligible participants can learn through practical activities offered by government, institutions, non-profits or other organisations. Apply only through the official MY Bharat portal or an official organiser link.

For registration, profile setup and older NYPS / NYPS 2.0 search terms, read the [MY Bharat Registration Guide](/guide/my-bharat-registration-guide). For certificates after completion, read the [MY Bharat Certificate Download Guide](/guide/my-bharat-certificate-download).

## What Experiential Learning Means

Experiential learning is learning by doing. On MY Bharat, it can include practical activities, events, field exposure, volunteering, institutional tasks or project-based opportunities.

The exact work, duration, eligibility and certificate rules depend on each opportunity.

## How To Find Opportunities

1. Log in at [mybharat.gov.in](https://mybharat.gov.in/).
2. Open **Experiential Learning**, **Explore Opportunities** or **Events**.
3. Filter by location, sector and organisation.
4. Read eligibility, dates, workload and location.
5. Check whether the opportunity is online, offline or hybrid.
6. Apply only if you can complete the required activity.

## Eligibility Checklist

Before applying, check:

- Age requirement
- Education or skill requirement
- Location or travel requirement
- Date and time
- Language needed
- Whether certificate is offered
- Whether any documents are required
- Whether the organiser is official/verified on the portal

Do not assume every opportunity is suitable for every student.

## How To Choose a Good Opportunity

Choose opportunities that give:

- Clear activity description
- Real organiser details
- Defined dates and expected effort
- Relevant skills for your study or career
- Completion/certificate rules
- Safe location and contact process

Avoid opportunities that ask for fees, bank OTP, UPI PIN or unrelated personal documents.

## After Applying

Keep a record of:

- Opportunity name
- Organiser name
- Application date
- Confirmation message
- Attendance or completion proof
- Certificate status

For certificate issues, read [MY Bharat Certificate Download Guide](/guide/my-bharat-certificate-download).

## MY Bharat for Resume Building

When adding an opportunity to your resume:

- Write the official activity name.
- Mention the organiser.
- Explain what you did, not only that you attended.
- Add measurable output if possible.
- Link certificate only if officially issued.

MY Bharat participation is strongest when it shows actual work, not just registration.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is MY Bharat experiential learning?
It is a practical youth opportunity route on MY Bharat where participants learn through real activities, events, volunteering or projects.

### Is every opportunity online?
No. Some opportunities may be offline or location-specific. Check the listing before applying.

### Will I get a certificate?
Only if the specific opportunity supports certificates and your participation/completion is verified.

### Can school students apply?
Eligibility depends on the opportunity. Check age and education requirements on the listing.

### Is MY Bharat a job portal?
No. It is a youth opportunity and experiential learning platform. Some experience may help your resume, but it does not guarantee a job.
