# Ration Card Portability — Use Your Ration Card Anywhere in India

If you have moved to a new city or state for work, you no longer need to worry about losing your ration benefits. Under the **One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC)** scheme, you can collect your subsidised foodgrains from any Fair Price Shop (FPS) in the country. This guide explains how ration card portability works and how you can use it.

## What Is Ration Card Portability?

Ration card portability means you can use your existing ration card to buy subsidised wheat, rice, and other essentials from **any Fair Price Shop in India** — not just the one assigned to you in your home state.

This is possible because of the **One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC)** scheme launched by the Government of India. The scheme connects all state PDS (Public Distribution System) databases into one central system.

> 📘 Want to know more about the scheme itself? Read our detailed guide on [One Nation One Ration Card](/guide/one-nation-one-ration-card).

## Why Was Portability Introduced?

Before ONORC, ration cards were tied to a specific Fair Price Shop in your home district. If you moved to another state for work — like millions of migrant workers do — you could not collect rations at your new location. You had two bad choices:

1. **Go without rations** at your new location
2. **Surrender your old card** and apply for a new one in the new state (a long and painful process)

The ONORC scheme solves this problem. Now your entitlement follows you, not your address.

## How Does Ration Card Portability Work?

The system relies on three things working together:

### 1. Aadhaar-Linked Ration Card

Your ration card and all family members listed on it must be **linked to Aadhaar numbers**. This is the most important requirement. Without Aadhaar linking, portability will not work.

### 2. Central Database (IMPDS)

The **Integrated Management of Public Distribution System (IMPDS)** is a central portal that connects all state ration card databases. When you visit a Fair Price Shop in any state, the ePoS machine checks your details against this national database.

### 3. ePoS Machines at Fair Price Shops

Every Fair Price Shop under ONORC is equipped with an **electronic Point of Sale (ePoS)** machine. This device:

- Verifies your identity using **Aadhaar-based biometric authentication** (fingerprint or iris scan)
- Checks your entitlement from the central database
- Records the transaction so your home state knows how much you have already collected

### How the Transaction Happens

1. You visit any ePoS-enabled Fair Price Shop
2. You provide your **ration card number** or **Aadhaar number**
3. The ePoS machine does biometric verification (fingerprint)
4. The system checks how much quota you have left for the month
5. You collect your foodgrains
6. The transaction is recorded centrally

It is that simple. No paperwork, no transfer request, no new card needed.

## Who Can Use Ration Card Portability?

You can use portability if:

- You hold a valid ration card under the **National Food Security Act (NFSA)**
- Your Aadhaar is linked to your ration card
- You belong to any category — [AAY, BPL, or APH](/guide/ration-card-types-aph-bpl-aay)
- The Fair Price Shop you visit has a working ePoS machine

### Eligible States and Union Territories

As of 2026, **all 36 states and Union Territories** are integrated into the ONORC system. This includes major migration destinations like:

- Maharashtra
- Delhi
- Tamil Nadu
- Karnataka
- Gujarat
- Kerala
- Andhra Pradesh
- Telangana
- Punjab
- Haryana

You can use portability across any of these states.

## Step-by-Step: How to Use Your Ration Card in Another State

### Step 1: Make Sure Aadhaar Is Linked

Check that your Aadhaar number is linked to your ration card. You can verify this by:

- Visiting your state's PDS portal (e.g., epds.bihar.gov.in, nfsa.up.gov.in)
- Checking on the **Mera Ration** mobile app
- Asking at your local Fair Price Shop

If Aadhaar is not linked, visit your nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) or PDS office to get it done.

### Step 2: Download the Mera Ration App (Optional but Helpful)

The **Mera Ration** app (available on Android) helps you:

- Find nearby Fair Price Shops in any state
- Check your monthly entitlement
- View transaction history
- Verify Aadhaar seeding status

Download it from the Google Play Store.

### Step 3: Visit Any ePoS-Enabled Fair Price Shop

Go to any FPS that has an ePoS machine. You can find one using the Mera Ration app or by asking locally.

### Step 4: Provide Your Details

Tell the shopkeeper your **ration card number** or **Aadhaar number**. You can use either one.

### Step 5: Complete Biometric Verification

Place your finger on the ePoS machine for fingerprint authentication. If fingerprint fails, iris scan may be available at some shops.

### Step 6: Collect Your Rations

Once verified, the machine will show your entitlement for the month. Collect your foodgrains and take the receipt.

### Step 7: Keep the Receipt

Always collect and keep the transaction receipt. It shows what you received and how much quota remains for the month.

## What Entitlements Can You Get?

Under portability, you get the **same entitlement** as in your home state:

| Category | Entitlement |
|----------|------------|
| **AAY (Antyodaya Anna Yojana)** | 35 kg per family per month |
| **PHH (Priority Household)** | 5 kg per person per month |

The prices remain the same — **₹2/kg for wheat, ₹3/kg for rice, ₹1/kg for coarse grains** under NFSA.

> 📘 Not sure which category you fall under? Check our guide on [Ration Card Types — APH, BPL, and AAY](/guide/ration-card-types-aph-bpl-aay).

## Intra-State vs Inter-State Portability

| Type | What It Means |
|------|--------------|
| **Intra-State** | Use your ration card at any FPS within your own state |
| **Inter-State** | Use your ration card at any FPS in a different state |

Both work the same way through the ePoS system.

## Troubleshooting Common Problems

### "Aadhaar not seeded" Error

Your Aadhaar is not linked to your ration card. Visit your home state's PDS office or a CSC to complete Aadhaar seeding.

### Biometric Authentication Fails

- Make sure your fingers are clean and dry
- Try a different finger
- Ask the shopkeeper to try iris scan if available
- If it keeps failing, visit a CSC to update your biometrics in Aadhaar

### "No entitlement found" Message

- Check if someone in your family already collected the full quota this month
- Verify your ration card is active and not cancelled
- Check on the Mera Ration app or IMPDS portal

### Shopkeeper Refuses to Serve

Some shopkeepers may not be aware of portability rules. You can:

- Show them the ONORC guidelines
- Call the **national food helpline: 1967** or **14445**
- File a complaint on the PDS portal of the state you are in

### ePoS Machine Not Working

If the machine is offline or broken, you cannot complete the transaction. Try another nearby FPS or visit on a different day.

## Important Things to Remember

- **Partial collection is allowed** — Some family members can collect in one state, others in another state
- **Monthly quota is shared** — If you collect 3 kg in Delhi, only the remaining quota is available at your home state FPS
- **No extra documents needed** — Just your Aadhaar number or ration card number
- **Free of cost** — There is no charge for using portability
- **Works for NFSA beneficiaries only** — State-specific schemes may not be portable

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use my ration card in any state without transferring it?

Yes. Under ONORC, you can use your ration card at any Fair Price Shop in India without any transfer. Just make sure your Aadhaar is linked.

### Do I need to inform my home state before using portability?

No. You do not need to inform anyone. The system automatically tracks your transactions across states.

### Can different family members collect rations in different states?

Yes. If your family has 4 members, some can collect in one state and others in another. The total monthly quota is shared across all transactions.

### What if my Aadhaar is not linked to my ration card?

You will not be able to use portability. Visit your nearest PDS office or Common Service Centre to link Aadhaar to your ration card as soon as possible.

### Is the Mera Ration app mandatory?

No. The app is optional but very helpful. You can use portability without the app — just visit any ePoS-enabled Fair Price Shop with your Aadhaar or ration card number.

### Can I get ration for the full month from another state?

Yes. You can collect your entire monthly entitlement from any state. There is no restriction on how much you collect from which state, as long as it is within your total monthly quota.

### What happens if the ePoS machine is not working?

You will need to try another Fair Price Shop or come back later. Manual distribution is not allowed under the portability system because biometric verification is mandatory.
