# Post Office Complaint Online: How to File & Track Grievance

Facing issues with delayed parcels, lost letters, money order not received, or rude behaviour at a post office? You can file a complaint online through official government portals. India Post has multiple grievance redressal channels including the **CPGRAMS portal (pgportal.gov.in)** and the **Department of Posts (DOP) complaint system**. This guide covers all methods to register and track your postal complaint.

## Types of Post Office Complaints

You can file complaints for these common issues:

- **Delayed or lost letters/parcels** — domestic or international
- **Money order not delivered** — regular or [electronic money order (eMO)](/guide/emo-electronic-money-order)
- **Damaged articles** — parcels received in broken condition
- **Savings account issues** — [passbook not updated](/guide/post-office-passbook-update), wrong balance, interest not credited
- **Insurance complaints** — [PLI](/guide/postal-life-insurance-guide) or RPLI claim delays
- **Staff behaviour** — rude or unhelpful post office employees
- **Speed Post tracking issues** — status not updating
- **Account transfer delays** — [post office account transfer](/guide/post-office-account-transfer) pending

## Method 1: File Complaint on CPGRAMS (pgportal.gov.in)

CPGRAMS (Centralized Public Grievance Redress and Monitoring System) is the central government's official grievance portal for all ministries including Department of Posts.

### Steps to File Complaint on CPGRAMS

1. Visit [pgportal.gov.in](https://pgportal.gov.in/)
2. Click **"Lodge Your Grievance"**
3. Register with your **name, email, and mobile number** (or login if already registered)
4. Verify your mobile number with OTP
5. Select **Ministry/Department** → Choose **"Communications"** → **"Department of Posts"**
6. Select the **grievance category** (e.g., Mail Operations, Savings, Insurance)
7. Enter your **complaint details** — be specific with dates, article numbers, tracking IDs
8. Upload supporting documents (receipt, tracking screenshot, etc.) — max 4 MB per file
9. Enter the **post office name and PIN code** related to your complaint
10. Submit the grievance
11. Note down the **Grievance Registration Number** for tracking

### Track CPGRAMS Complaint Status

1. Visit [pgportal.gov.in](https://pgportal.gov.in/)
2. Click **"View Status"**
3. Enter your **Registration Number**
4. Enter registered **email or mobile number**
5. View current status and any response from the department

## Method 2: DOP Online Complaint Portal

The Department of Posts also has its own complaint mechanism on the India Post website.

### Steps to File on India Post Portal

1. Visit [India Post Contact Us page](https://www.indiapost.gov.in/VAS/Pages/ContactUs.aspx)
2. Select your **complaint type** (Speed Post, Registered Post, Parcel, etc.)
3. Enter **article/consignment number** if applicable
4. Fill in your contact details
5. Describe the complaint in detail
6. Submit and save the reference number

## Method 3: Email Complaint

You can also email your complaint directly:

| Circle/Level | Email |
|---|---|
| National level | pgcomplaints-dop@gov.in |
| Your Postal Circle | Check on indiapost.gov.in under "Circle Offices" |

Include these details in your email:
- Your name, address, and contact number
- Article/tracking number
- Date of posting
- Post office name and PIN code
- Clear description of the issue

## Method 4: Toll-Free Helpline

- **India Post Helpline:** 1800-11-2011 (toll-free)
- **Darpan Helpline:** 1800-425-0000
- Available Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 6 PM

## Method 5: Offline Complaint

1. Visit your **Head Post Office** or **Divisional Superintendent's office**
2. Write a complaint letter addressed to the **Postmaster General**
3. Mention all details — tracking number, date, amount (if money order), etc.
4. Get an acknowledgement with date stamp
5. Keep a photocopy for your records

## Escalation Process

If your complaint is not resolved within the expected timeline, you can escalate:

| Level | Authority | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| Level 1 | Sub Post Office / Head Post Office | 7 days |
| Level 2 | Divisional Superintendent | 15 days |
| Level 3 | Chief Postmaster General (Circle) | 30 days |
| Level 4 | CPGRAMS / Secretary, DOP | 45-60 days |
| Level 5 | Postal Ombudsman | After 60 days |

### Filing Appeal with Postal Ombudsman

If you are not satisfied after exhausting all levels:

1. Write to the **Postal Ombudsman** of your region
2. Include all previous complaint reference numbers
3. Attach copies of all correspondence
4. The Ombudsman will investigate and pass an order within 30 days

## Important Tips

1. **Always note the tracking number** — for Speed Post, Registered Post, and parcels, the tracking number is essential for filing complaints
2. **File within 6 months** — complaints about lost articles should be filed within 6 months of posting
3. **Keep receipts safe** — the postal receipt is your proof of posting and is needed for compensation claims
4. **Be specific** — mention exact dates, amounts, and article numbers in your complaint
5. **Follow up regularly** — check CPGRAMS status every 7 days and send reminders if needed

## Compensation for Lost/Damaged Articles

India Post provides compensation for lost or damaged articles:

| Article Type | Maximum Compensation |
|---|---|
| Registered Letter | ₹50 |
| Speed Post | Based on declared value |
| Insured Parcel | Up to insured value |
| Money Order | Full amount |
| VP Article | Based on declared value |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I file a complaint against a post office online?
Visit pgportal.gov.in and select Department of Posts under Ministry of Communications. Fill in your complaint details with supporting documents and submit. You will get a registration number to track your complaint.

### What is the helpline number for India Post complaints?
The toll-free helpline number for India Post is **1800-11-2011**. It is available Monday to Saturday from 9 AM to 6 PM.

### How long does it take to resolve a post office complaint?
Simple complaints like delayed delivery are resolved in 7-15 days. Complex issues like lost articles or insurance claims may take 30-60 days.

### Can I file a complaint for a lost Speed Post?
Yes, you can file a complaint on pgportal.gov.in or call 1800-11-2011. You will need the Speed Post tracking number (13-digit consignment number) from your receipt.

### What is CPGRAMS and how does it work?
CPGRAMS (Centralized Public Grievance Redress and Monitoring System) is the Government of India's central portal for filing complaints against any government department. Complaints are forwarded to the concerned department and tracked until resolution.

### How do I escalate an unresolved post office complaint?
If your complaint is not resolved at the post office level within 15 days, escalate to the Divisional Superintendent. If still unresolved after 30 days, approach the Chief Postmaster General. After 60 days, file with the Postal Ombudsman.

### Can I get compensation for a lost parcel from India Post?
Yes, India Post provides compensation for lost insured parcels up to the insured value. For uninsured parcels, compensation is limited. File a complaint with the tracking number and posting receipt.
