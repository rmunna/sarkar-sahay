# AP EAMCET 2026 (APEAPCET) — Andhra Pradesh Engineering & Medical CET

**AP EAMCET 2026** — officially called **APEAPCET (Andhra Pradesh Engineering, Agriculture and Pharmacy Common Entrance Test)** — is conducted by **JNTU Kakinada** on behalf of the **APSCHE** for engineering (PCM) and agriculture/pharmacy (PCB) admissions in Andhra Pradesh colleges.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [cets.apsche.ap.gov.in](https://cets.apsche.ap.gov.in). AP domicile (10 years residence OR 7 years school study) required for state quota. Two streams: Engineering and Agriculture/Pharmacy.

## Eligibility Criteria

| Criteria | Engineering (PCM) | Agriculture / Pharmacy (PCB) |
|----------|--------------------|------------------------------|
| **Qualification** | Class 12 with PCM | Class 12 with PCB |
| **Minimum marks** | 45% in PCM (40% SC/ST/OBC) | 45% in PCB |
| **AP domicile** | 10 years residence OR 7 years study in AP | Same |

### AP Local Area Quota

AP has a unique **local area quota** — seats reserved for students from within the same district or region:

- **AU Region**: Andhra University region (Coastal AP)
- **SVU Region**: Sri Venkateswara University region (Rayalaseema)
- **ANU Region**: Acharya Nagarjuna University region

## Frequently Asked Questions

### APEAPCET vs TS EAMCET — different exams?

Yes. **AP EAMCET (APEAPCET)** is for **Andhra Pradesh colleges**. **TS EAMCET** is for **Telangana colleges**. After the bifurcation of AP and Telangana in 2014, they hold separate exams. Students from both states can apply for both exams.

### What is the local area quota in APEAPCET?

AP reserves seats for **local area students** — if you studied your last 4–7 years in a particular university region (AU/SVU/ANU), you get priority for colleges in that region's seats. This is separate from state quota and is a unique AP feature.
