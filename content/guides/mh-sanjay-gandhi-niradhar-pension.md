## What is Sanjay Gandhi Niradhar Anudan Yojana?

Sanjay Gandhi Niradhar Anudan Yojana is a social security pension scheme by the Government of Maharashtra for **destitute and vulnerable persons** who have no means of livelihood and no family support. It is administered by the Department of Social Justice and Special Assistance.

The scheme provides a **monthly pension** to help meet basic needs of the most vulnerable sections of society. The pension amount is approximately **₹600-₹1,000 per month** depending on the category and government revisions.

## Who is Eligible?

The scheme covers the following categories of destitute persons:

- **Widows** (any age, destitute)
- **Orphans** under 25 years of age
- **Disabled persons** with 40% or more disability
- **Destitute persons** suffering from serious illnesses (AIDS, cancer, leprosy, paralysis, sickle cell anaemia)
- **Transgender persons** without support
- **Destitute women** — deserted wives, women victims of atrocities
- **Acid attack victims**

### Common Conditions
- Must be a **resident of Maharashtra**
- Annual income must not exceed **₹21,000** from all sources
- Must not be receiving any other government pension
- Age: As applicable per category (no minimum age for orphans, disabled)

## Documents Required

- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
- Income certificate from Tehsildar (annual income below ₹21,000)
- Domicile certificate of Maharashtra
- Bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked, nationalized bank)
- Passport-size photographs (2)
- **Category-specific documents:**
  - Widows: Husband's death certificate
  - Orphans: Parents' death certificate or proof of abandonment
  - Disabled: Disability certificate (40%+) from civil surgeon
  - Serious illness: Medical certificate from government hospital
  - Transgender: Self-declaration or identity document
  - Deserted women: Legal separation documents or affidavit
- Self-declaration of not receiving other pensions
- Ration card (if available)

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Online Application

1. **Visit** [Aaple Sarkar portal](https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in)
2. **Register/Login** using mobile number
3. **Navigate** to Social Justice → Sanjay Gandhi Niradhar Anudan Yojana
4. **Select** the applicable category (widow, orphan, disabled, etc.)
5. **Fill** the application form with all required details
6. **Upload** scanned documents
7. **Submit** and note the application ID
8. **Track** status on the portal

### Offline Application

1. **Visit** the Tehsil office or District Social Welfare Office
2. **Collect** the application form for Sanjay Gandhi Niradhar Yojana
3. **Fill** the form, attach all document copies
4. **Submit** to the Talathi or Social Welfare Officer
5. **Collect** acknowledgement receipt
6. **Verification** by Talathi and approval at Tehsil/District level

## Pension Amount

| Category | Approximate Monthly Pension |
|----------|---------------------------|
| Individual beneficiary | ₹600 – ₹1,000 |
| Family with 2+ eligible members | ₹900 – ₹1,500 |

> Amounts are subject to revision. Check the official portal for the latest rates.

## Processing Time

- Document verification: **30-60 days**
- Approval: **2-3 months**
- Pension credited via DBT monthly after approval

## Fees

**No fees.** The application and all related services are completely free.

## Important Tips

1. **Submit life certificate annually** — Use [Jeevan Pramaan](https://jeevanpramaan.gov.in) for digital submission
2. **Keep bank account active** — Pension is transferred via DBT; inactive accounts cause delays
3. **Report changes** — Inform the welfare office if your income or family status changes
4. **Apply at CSC** — If unable to apply online, visit a Common Service Centre for assisted application
5. **Don't fall for middlemen** — No agent or tout is needed; apply directly at government offices

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Can a widow apply for both Sanjay Gandhi Niradhar and Shravan Bal pension?
No. You can receive only **one** state social security pension at a time. Choose the one that gives a higher benefit. See our [Shravan Bal Pension guide](/guide/mh-shravan-bal-pension).

### Q2. Is there an age limit for widows?
No. Destitute widows of **any age** can apply, provided they meet income and domicile criteria.

### Q3. Can orphans above 18 apply?
Yes. Orphans up to **25 years of age** can apply under this scheme.

### Q4. What disability percentage is required?
A minimum of **40% disability** as certified by the District Civil Surgeon is required.

### Q5. Can I apply if I have a job?
If your annual income exceeds ₹21,000, you are not eligible. The scheme is for destitute persons with no significant income.

### Q6. How do I check my application status?
Log in to [Aaple Sarkar portal](https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in) and check under "My Applications" with your application ID.
