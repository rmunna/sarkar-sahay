# Banglarbhumi Not Working 2026? Fix Portal Errors Step by Step

The Banglarbhumi portal (banglarbhumi.gov.in) is West Bengal's official land records portal. It frequently faces downtime, SSL certificate errors and loading issues — especially during peak hours. This guide walks you through every fix.

## Common Banglarbhumi Errors

| Error | What It Means |
|-------|--------------|
| **"Your connection is not private"** | SSL certificate expired on the server |
| **"This site can't be reached"** | Portal server is down |
| **"ERR_CONNECTION_TIMED_OUT"** | Overloaded server, try later |
| **OTP not received** | Mobile not registered or network issue |
| **Login page not loading** | Browser cache conflict |
| **Map/GIS layer not showing** | JavaScript disabled or unsupported browser |

## Fix 1: SSL Certificate Error ("Your connection is not private")

This is the most common Banglarbhumi error. The portal's SSL certificate sometimes expires and Chrome shows a warning.

**Quick fix in Chrome:**
1. On the error page, click **"Advanced"** (bottom left)
2. Click **"Proceed to banglarbhumi.gov.in (unsafe)"**
3. You can now access the portal normally

**Quick fix in Firefox:**
1. Click **"Advanced"**
2. Click **"Accept the Risk and Continue"**

> **Note:** This bypass is safe for a government portal — the risk is minimal since no sensitive financial data is being entered. You're only checking land records.

## Fix 2: Portal Is Down — Use bhulekh.wb.gov.in as Alternative

When the main portal is completely unreachable, use these alternatives:

| Alternative | URL | What It Has |
|-------------|-----|-------------|
| **Bhulekh WB** | [bhulekh.wb.gov.in](https://bhulekh.wb.gov.in/) | Plot info, khatian, mouza search |
| **WB Registration** | [wbregistration.gov.in](https://wbregistration.gov.in/) | Registration + mutation status |
| **eDistrict WB** | [edistrict.wb.gov.in](https://edistrict.wb.gov.in/) | Certified copy of records |
| **Banglar Bhumi App** | Play Store/App Store | Mobile access to land records |

## Fix 3: Best Time to Access Banglarbhumi

The portal is heavily loaded between 10 AM and 6 PM on weekdays:

- **Best window**: 6 AM – 9 AM (before peak)
- **Second best**: 9 PM – 11 PM
- **Avoid**: 11 AM – 3 PM (maximum load)
- **Weekend**: Saturday morning is usually faster

## Fix 4: Browser and Cache Issues

1. **Clear browser cache**: Chrome → Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data → Cached images and files
2. **Use Incognito mode**: Opens without cached data (Ctrl+Shift+N in Chrome)
3. **Disable ad blockers**: Some browser extensions block the portal's scripts
4. **Use Chrome or Firefox**: Edge and Safari sometimes have compatibility issues with banglarbhumi.gov.in

## Fix 5: OTP Not Received for Login

If the OTP for your Banglarbhumi account isn't arriving:

1. **Check registered mobile**: OTP goes to the mobile you registered at account creation
2. **Wait 2 minutes**: OTP can be delayed
3. **Check SMS spam folder**: Some carrier networks filter government SMS
4. **Try a different time**: Peak hours cause OTP gateway delays
5. **Resend OTP**: Maximum 3 resend attempts before a 30-minute lockout

## How to Check Land Records Without Login

Most basic searches on Banglarbhumi do not require an account:

1. Go to **[banglarbhumi.gov.in](https://banglarbhumi.gov.in/)** → **"Know Your Property"**
2. Select **District** → **Block** → **Mouza**
3. Enter **Khatian Number** or **Plot/Dag Number**
4. View land ownership details and area

Alternatively on **bhulekh.wb.gov.in**:
1. Select **Search by Khatian** or **Search by Plot**
2. Enter your location details
3. Get land owner name, area, and record type (RS/LR)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is bhulekh.wb.gov.in the same as banglarbhumi.gov.in?

Both are West Bengal government land record portals but serve slightly different purposes. Banglarbhumi.gov.in is the primary portal with maps and mutation tracking. Bhulekh.wb.gov.in focuses on khatian/plot record searches. When banglarbhumi is down, bhulekh is often still accessible.

### Can I check mutation status if banglarbhumi is not working?

Yes. Go to **[wbregistration.gov.in](https://wbregistration.gov.in/)** → Mutation Case Status and enter your case number. Mutation data is separately maintained there.

### My GIS map is not loading on banglarbhumi. How to fix?

The map feature requires JavaScript and WebGL enabled in your browser. If the map doesn't load: update Chrome, disable VPN, and ensure you're not using a corporate network that blocks WebGL content. Sometimes the map server is separately down even when the main portal is working.

### How do I download a certified copy of land records?

Certified copies (Certified Copy of RoR) must be obtained through **edistrict.wb.gov.in** by paying the prescribed fee (₹10–₹30 depending on pages). The regular banglarbhumi search shows records for viewing only, not certified copies.
