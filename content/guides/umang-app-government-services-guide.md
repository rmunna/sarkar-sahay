## What is UMANG App?

UMANG (Unified Mobile Application for New-age Governance) is a Government of India initiative that brings **1,600+ government services** from central and state departments into a single mobile app. Developed by MeitY and NeGD (National e-Governance Division), UMANG lets you access services like PF balance check, passport application tracking, gas booking, Aadhaar services, PM-KISAN status, and much more — all from one platform.

**Key highlights:**
- **1,600+ services** from 200+ government departments
- Available in **13 Indian languages**
- Works on **Android, iOS, and web** ([umang.gov.in](https://umang.gov.in))
- **Free** to use
- Supports **Aadhaar-based authentication**

## How to Register on UMANG

### Step 1: Download the App

- **Android:** Search "UMANG" on Google Play Store or visit the official link
- **iOS:** Download from Apple App Store
- **Web:** Go to [https://umang.gov.in](https://umang.gov.in)

### Step 2: Enter Mobile Number

Open the app and enter your **10-digit mobile number**.

### Step 3: Verify OTP

Enter the **OTP** sent to your mobile number.

### Step 4: Set MPIN

Create a **4-digit MPIN** for secure login. This will be your quick-access PIN.

### Step 5: Link Aadhaar (Optional but Recommended)

Link your Aadhaar for seamless access to services that require identity verification (EPFO, PAN, passport, etc.).

Your UMANG account is ready.

## Top Services on UMANG

### 1. EPFO — PF Balance & Passbook

Check your EPF (Employee Provident Fund) balance and download passbook:

1. Open UMANG → Search **"EPFO"**
2. Select **"Employee Centric Services"**
3. Choose **"View Passbook"**
4. Enter your **UAN (Universal Account Number)**
5. Verify with OTP sent to UAN-registered mobile
6. View and download your EPF passbook with all contribution details

### 2. Passport — Application Status

Track your passport application:

1. Open UMANG → Search **"Passport Seva"**
2. Select **"Track Application Status"**
3. Enter your **Application Reference Number** (ARN) or **File Number**
4. View current status — from application receipt to dispatch

### 3. Gas Booking (LPG Cylinder)

Book an LPG gas cylinder refill:

1. Open UMANG → Search **"LPG"** or your gas company (HP Gas, Bharat Gas, Indane)
2. Select **"Book Refill"**
3. Enter your **LPG Consumer Number** or **registered mobile number**
4. Verify with OTP
5. Confirm booking — delivery will be scheduled

### 4. PM-KISAN — Payment Status

Check your PM-KISAN instalment status:

1. Open UMANG → Search **"PM-KISAN"**
2. Select **"Beneficiary Status"** or **"Payment Status"**
3. Enter your **Aadhaar number**, **mobile number**, or **account number**
4. View instalment-wise payment history

See our [PM-KISAN status check guide](/guide/pm-kisan-status-check) for detailed instructions.

### 5. Aadhaar Services

Access various Aadhaar services without visiting UIDAI website:

- **Download e-Aadhaar** — Get your Aadhaar PDF
- **Check Aadhaar Update Status** — Track update requests
- **Verify Aadhaar** — Confirm if an Aadhaar number is valid
- **Lock/Unlock Biometrics** — Secure your biometric data

See our [Aadhaar card guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) for more details.

### 6. Income Tax — PAN & Filing

- **Know Your PAN** — Retrieve PAN details using Aadhaar
- **Link PAN-Aadhaar** — Link your PAN and Aadhaar
- **Check Refund Status** — Track income tax refund

## Common Errors and Fixes

### "OTP Not Received"

- **Cause:** Network issue or mobile number not registered
- **Fix:** Ensure you have mobile network. Wait 2–3 minutes and click "Resend OTP." For service-specific OTPs (like EPFO), ensure your number is registered with that service

### "Service Temporarily Unavailable"

- **Cause:** The backend department's server is down
- **Fix:** This is not an UMANG issue — the connected department (EPFO, Passport, etc.) is temporarily offline. Try again after a few hours

### "UAN Not Found" (EPFO)

- **Cause:** Incorrect UAN or your UAN is not activated
- **Fix:** Verify your UAN on the EPFO portal ([https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in)). Activate it if not already done

### "MPIN Forgot / Account Locked"

- **Cause:** Forgot your 4-digit MPIN or too many failed attempts
- **Fix:** Use **"Forgot MPIN"** option on the login screen. Verify with OTP to reset your MPIN

### "Aadhaar Linking Failed"

- **Cause:** Aadhaar details mismatch or UIDAI server issue
- **Fix:** Ensure the mobile number linked to your Aadhaar is the same as your UMANG-registered number. Try again later if it's a server issue

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Is UMANG app safe to use?

Yes, UMANG is developed by the Government of India (MeitY/NeGD). It uses Aadhaar-based authentication and encrypted connections. Download only from official app stores.

### Q2: Can I check my PF balance on UMANG?

Yes, search for "EPFO" in UMANG, select "View Passbook," and enter your UAN. You can view your complete EPF contribution history.

### Q3: Is UMANG app free?

Yes, the UMANG app is completely free. There are no charges for downloading or using any service. However, some services (like gas booking) may have their own charges.

### Q4: Can I book a gas cylinder through UMANG?

Yes, UMANG supports LPG refill booking for HP Gas, Bharat Gas, and Indane. Enter your consumer number and confirm the booking.

### Q5: Does UMANG work without internet?

No, UMANG requires an active internet connection as it fetches data from government servers in real-time.

### Q6: Can I use UMANG on a computer?

Yes, visit [https://umang.gov.in](https://umang.gov.in) to access UMANG services via web browser on your computer.

### Q7: How is UMANG different from DigiLocker?

UMANG is a **service access platform** (check status, book services, make payments), while [DigiLocker](/guide/digilocker-document-download-guide) is a **document storage platform** (store and share digital documents). Both are complementary.
