## What is MP Mukhyamantri Tirth Darshan Yojana?

**Mukhyamantri Tirth Darshan Yojana** is Madhya Pradesh government's scheme to provide **free pilgrimage by special train** to senior citizens aged 60 and above. The entire cost — train travel, food, accommodation at pilgrimage site, and escort facility — is borne by the state government.

One of the most popular state welfare schemes in MP, it has facilitated pilgrimage for lakhs of senior citizens.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Madhya Pradesh resident** (minimum 5 years continuous residency)
- **Age 60 years or above** — Aadhaar-verified DOB
- **Not an income tax payer**
- **Not a government pensioner** (in some programme versions — check current year)
- Can apply **once per lifetime** under this scheme
- One attendant allowed if the senior citizen is physically challenged or unwell

---

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card**
2. **Age proof** (Aadhaar, birth certificate, voter ID with DOB)
3. **Residence proof** (ration card, voter ID with MP address)
4. **Income certificate** (from Patwari/Tehsildar if required)
5. **Passport-size photograph**

## How to Apply

### Step 1 — Online Application

Go to **[tirthdarshan.mp.gov.in](https://tirthdarshan.mp.gov.in)**:
- Register with Aadhaar / Samgra ID
- Choose destination and preferred batch (based on availability)
- Upload required documents
- Submit application

### Step 2 — Offline Application

If not comfortable with online application:
- Visit the **Tehsil / Nagar Panchayat / Nagar Palika** office
- Submit filled application form with documents to the local SDM/Tehsildar
- They upload to the portal on your behalf

### Step 3 — Selection and Allotment

Applications are reviewed by district administration. Seats per district per batch are limited. Selected applicants are notified by SMS/call.

### Step 4 — Departure

Report at the designated railway station on the date given. Government escort guides the group from boarding to return.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. My mother is 68 years old and wants to visit Varanasi. Can she apply?**

Yes. She is eligible. Apply online at tirthdarshan.mp.gov.in or at the nearest Tehsil office. She should mention Varanasi/Gaya as the preferred destination.

**2. Can my father's attendant also travel free?**

If the senior citizen is physically challenged or medically needs an attendant, one attendant is permitted free. Provide medical certificate from a government doctor.

**3. I am a government pensioner. Can I apply?**

Earlier versions of the scheme excluded government pensioners. Check the current year's eligibility at tirthdarshan.mp.gov.in — some scheme iterations have included pensioners too.

**4. How many times can I apply?**

The scheme allows **once per lifetime** per senior citizen. If you have already availed the benefit, you cannot apply again.

**5. What if I am not selected in the first batch?**

Your application remains active. You will be considered for the next available batch to the same destination. Multiple batches run through the year.
