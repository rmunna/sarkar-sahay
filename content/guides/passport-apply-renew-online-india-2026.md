# Apply & Renew Indian Passport Online 2026 — Complete Guide

Indian passports can now be applied entirely online at **passportindia.gov.in** with appointment at the nearest Passport Seva Kendra (PSK) or Post Office Passport Seva Kendra (POPSK). Here's everything you need — step by step.

---

## Types of Passport Applications

| Type | For Whom | Validity | Fee |
|------|---------|---------|-----|
| **Fresh Passport** (36 pages) | First-time applicant | 10 years (adults), 5 years (minors below 18) | ₹1,500 |
| **Fresh Passport** (60 pages) | Frequent travellers | Same | ₹2,000 |
| **Renewal** (re-issue) | Passport expiring / expired / lost | 10 years | ₹1,500 |
| **Tatkal Fresh** | Urgent need | 10 years | ₹3,500 + ₹1,500 = ₹5,000 |
| **Tatkal Renewal** | Urgent need | 10 years | ₹3,500 + ₹1,500 = ₹5,000 |
| **Minor Passport** (below 18) | Children | 5 years | ₹1,000 |

---

## Documents Required — Fresh Passport

**Identity Proof (any one):**
- Aadhaar card
- PAN card
- Voter ID
- Driving licence

**Address Proof (any one — current address):**
- Aadhaar card (current address must match)
- Voter ID
- Utility bill (less than 3 months old)
- Bank passbook/statement (less than 3 months)
- Driving licence

**Date of Birth Proof (any one):**
- Birth certificate (most preferred)
- School leaving certificate / SSLC with DOB
- PAN card
- Voter ID
- Aadhaar (if DOB mentioned)

**Additional (if applicable):**
- Marriage certificate (for name change after marriage)
- Deed poll / gazette notification (for name change)
- Parents' passports (for minor passports)

---

## Documents for Passport Renewal

- Old passport (original — all pages) **mandatory**
- Current address proof if address changed from old passport
- Court affidavit if name changed

## Step-by-Step: Apply Online

### Step 1: Register on Passport Seva Portal

1. Go to **[passportindia.gov.in](https://www.passportindia.gov.in)**
2. **"New User? Register Here"**
3. Create account: Name, email, login ID, password
4. Email verification

### Step 2: Fill Passport Application Form

1. Login → **"Apply for Fresh Passport / Re-issue of Passport"**
2. Select: 36-page or 60-page
3. Fill form: Personal details, family details, emergency contact, previous passports (if any)
4. Declaration → Submit form
5. **Note the Application Reference Number (ARN)**

### Step 3: Pay Fees and Book Appointment

1. Pay fees online: Net banking / UPI / Debit card
2. **Book appointment** at nearest PSK/POPSK:
   - Choose nearest city
   - Select available date + time slot
3. Print/save **Appointment Confirmation**

### Step 4: Visit Passport Seva Kendra (PSK)

**Documents to carry on appointment day:**
- Appointment confirmation printout
- Original + self-attested photocopies of all documents
- Application reference number (ARN)

**At PSK:**
1. Document verification at counter A
2. Data verification at counter B
3. Photo + biometrics (fingerprint) at counter C
4. Supervisor verification (if needed) at counter D
5. **Grant letter received** (final status by officer)

### Step 5: Police Verification

- Police may visit your address for verification (within 7–14 days for normal; before appointment for Tatkal)
- Keep your address proof ready
- Ensure someone is home when police visit

### Step 6: Passport Delivered

- Passport printed at printing centre → Dispatched via Speed Post
- **Normal passport**: 15–30 working days after PSK appointment
- **Tatkal**: 1–3 working days after PSK appointment

## Tatkal Passport — How It Works

Tatkal is an urgent category with faster processing:

**Extra charges:** ₹2,000 more than normal (total: ₹3,500 for 36-page)

**Key difference:** Police verification done AFTER dispatch (post-police verification) for Tatkal — you get the passport faster. If police verification reveals issues later, passport can be revoked.

**When to choose Tatkal:**
- Urgent travel (medical emergency, visa deadline, sudden work requirement)
- Exam abroad / visa interview in 1–2 weeks

## Track Passport Status

1. passportindia.gov.in → **"Track Application Status"**
2. Enter application number + date of birth
3. Status updates at each stage: Granted, Printed, Dispatched, Delivered

## Passport Validity and Renewal Timeline

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Passport expiring in 6+ months | Wait, no renewal needed (most countries accept 6-month validity) |
| Passport expiring in < 6 months | **Renew immediately** (most countries need 6-month validity remaining) |
| Passport expired | Renewal process — same as fresh but with old passport |
| Passport lost/stolen | File police FIR first → Apply for fresh passport online → Submit FIR copy |
| Pages full | Apply for renewal + new passport |

## PSK vs POPSK — Which to Choose?

| Criteria | PSK (Main) | POPSK (Post Office) |
|----------|-----------|-------------------|
| Locations | 77 PSKs across India | 400+ POPSKs (smaller cities) |
| Speed | Faster processing | Sometimes slower |
| Rush | Higher rush in big cities | Less crowded |
| Appointment availability | Book online — 7–21 days wait | Often faster appointments |

**Recommendation:** For smaller cities without PSK nearby, POPSK is excellent.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Passport application rejected — what to do?**
If rejected at PSK (documents incomplete/mismatch): Get a refund reference, fix the issue, apply fresh. Rejection reasons are mentioned in the letter given at PSK. Common reasons: Address mismatch between Aadhaar and form, DOB mismatch, document not self-attested.

**Can I track when police verification will happen?**
After PSK appointment: Check status at passportindia.gov.in → "Track Application Status" → Shows "Police Verification Sent/Received." Police don't give prior notice — they may visit anytime between 9 AM – 6 PM on working days.

**I submitted Aadhaar as address proof but my current address is different — is this a problem?**
Yes — if Aadhaar shows old address: Use a utility bill or bank statement showing the new address. Alternatively: Update Aadhaar address first (myAadhaar portal), then apply for passport.

**Minor passport — do both parents need to be present at PSK?**
If both parents are alive: Both should ideally be present (one parent + minor is sufficient for most PSKs but specific requirements vary). If single parent: Bring court order / death certificate of other parent.

**Passport fees — are they refundable if appointment is cancelled?**
Yes — paid fees are valid for 1 year. If you miss your appointment, you can reschedule using the same payment reference. Fees are refundable only if your passport application is rejected due to a government error (rare).
