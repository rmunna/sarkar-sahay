# PAN Card 2.0 India 2026 — Everything You Need to Know

PAN 2.0 (Permanent Account Number 2.0) is the government's upgraded PAN system launched in late 2024 as part of the Income Tax Department's digital overhaul. Here's what changed, whether you need to do anything, and how to get the new card.

---

## What Is PAN 2.0?

PAN 2.0 is the upgraded version of India's Permanent Account Number system. The changes:

| Feature | Old PAN | PAN 2.0 |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Card format | Plain plastic/paper card | Plastic card with QR code |
| QR code | No | Yes — encrypted PAN data for instant verification |
| Data system | Multiple legacy systems (NSDL, UTITSL, IT Dept) | Unified single database |
| e-PAN | PDF (basic) | Upgraded digital format |
| Issuance speed | 7–15 days (physical) | 3–5 days (physical), instant e-PAN |
| PAN number | Same 10-digit format (no change) | Same — your old number continues |

**The most important fact**: **Your old PAN card is still completely valid.** You don't have to replace it. PAN 2.0 is an optional upgrade, not a mandatory change.

---

## Is Old PAN Card Still Valid?

**Yes — completely.** Income Tax Department has officially stated:

> "Existing PAN cards issued before PAN 2.0 launch remain valid. There is NO need for PAN card holders to replace their existing PAN."

Businesses and banks cannot reject your old PAN card for KYC or transactions. If anyone claims your old PAN is invalid after PAN 2.0 launch — that is incorrect.

---

## Who Benefits From Upgrading to PAN 2.0?

**Upgrade is useful if you**:
- Need a new physical PAN card anyway (lost, damaged, name correction)
- Want the QR-enabled card for faster digital KYC at banks/financial institutions
- Are applying for PAN for the first time

**Don't bother upgrading if**:
- Your old card is fine and you just need it for ITR / KYC
- You use e-PAN (digital PDF) — the old format is still accepted everywhere

---

## PAN 2.0 — Free for Existing Holders

For existing PAN cardholders who want to upgrade:
- **e-PAN (digital)**: **Free** — download updated e-PAN with QR code
- **Physical PAN card (PVC)**: ₹50 delivery charges (domestic) / ₹959 (international)

---

## How to Apply for PAN 2.0 / Get Updated e-PAN — Step by Step

### Via NSDL Portal (onlineservices.nsdl.com)

1. Visit [onlineservices.nsdl.com](https://onlineservices.nsdl.com) → PAN → "Request for New PAN Card Or/And Changes or Correction in PAN Data"
2. Select "Changes or Correction in Existing PAN Data"
3. Enter existing PAN number, Aadhaar number, date of birth
4. Verify with OTP on Aadhaar-linked mobile
5. Review existing details — if only getting PAN 2.0 (no corrections), confirm without changes
6. If you want physical card: pay ₹50 via net banking/UPI
7. If only e-PAN: no payment — download updated e-PAN with QR
8. New PAN card issued in 3–7 days (physical) or instantly (e-PAN)

### Via UTIITSL Portal (www.utiitsl.com)

1. Go to [utiitsl.com](https://www.utiitsl.com) → PAN Services → "PAN Card for Existing PAN Holders"
2. Enter PAN, Aadhaar, date of birth
3. OTP verification
4. Select physical card or e-PAN
5. Pay and submit

### New PAN Card Application (First-Time)

If you don't have a PAN and applying fresh — the new application automatically issues PAN 2.0 format:
1. Same NSDL/UTIITSL portals → "New PAN" option
2. Fill personal details, upload documents (photo, ID proof, address proof)
3. Pay: ₹107 (Indian address) + ₹993 (foreign address)
4. e-PAN in 48 hours; physical in 7–10 days

## How to Download Updated e-PAN 2.0 (Free)

1. Visit [incometax.gov.in](https://incometax.gov.in) → Quick Links → "Instant e-PAN"
2. Enter Aadhaar number → OTP verification
3. Download updated e-PAN PDF with QR code (free, instant)

The e-PAN generated through Income Tax portal is free and valid for all KYC purposes. No need to pay NSDL/UTIITSL if you just need digital PAN.

---

## What the QR Code in PAN 2.0 Contains

The QR code on PAN 2.0 cards contains encrypted information:
- Name as per Income Tax records
- Father's name
- Date of birth
- PAN number
- Photograph

When scanned at a bank or KYC point, this allows instant verification without manual data entry. Reduces fraud (fake PAN cards harder to make with encrypted QR).

---

## Aadhaar-PAN Linking Status for PAN 2.0

Your Aadhaar must be linked to PAN to use PAN 2.0 features. Check linking status:
1. [incometax.gov.in](https://incometax.gov.in) → Quick Links → "Link Aadhaar Status"
2. Enter PAN and Aadhaar number
3. If not linked — link first (mandatory; Rs. 1000 late fee applicable)

**Unlinked PAN**: Your PAN may be "inoperative" for financial transactions if not linked to Aadhaar by the deadline. Link immediately at incometax.gov.in.

## Common Questions About PAN 2.0

### Do I need to update my PAN in bank records when I get PAN 2.0?

No — your PAN number doesn't change. The card is just a new format. Banks already have your PAN number — no re-submission needed.

### Can I still use my old PAN card for income tax filing?

Yes — the 10-digit PAN is what matters for ITR, Form 16, TDS, etc. The physical card format is irrelevant to these processes.

### I have a PAN card but no QR code. Am I missing out?

Only for in-person digital verification use cases (some banks scan QR for faster KYC). For all tax and financial purposes, old PAN works identically to PAN 2.0.

### Is PAN 2.0 the same as PAN-Aadhaar linking?

No — they're separate:
- **PAN-Aadhaar linking**: Mandated process linking your PAN to your Aadhaar in the Income Tax database (done at incometax.gov.in — ₹1,000 fee if not done earlier)
- **PAN 2.0**: Just a card format upgrade with QR code

Both are separate actions. You can link Aadhaar-PAN without getting a PAN 2.0 card.

### My name spelling differs slightly between PAN and Aadhaar. What to do?

Fix the name mismatch before using PAN 2.0 QR features — the QR pulls data from IT department records. If IT records and Aadhaar have different spellings, QR verification will show the IT department version (which banks may flag). Correct PAN name at NSDL/UTIITSL or Aadhaar name at UIDAI to make them match.
