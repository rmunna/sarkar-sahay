# Sikkim Widow Pension – Complete Guide 2026

## What is the Sikkim Widow Pension?

The **Sikkim Widow Pension** provides monthly financial support to widowed women from economically weaker sections. It operates under the **Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme (IGNWPS)** with additional top-up from the Sikkim state government to help widows meet their daily living expenses.

## Pension Amount

| Category | Approximate Monthly Pension |
|----------|---------------------------|
| Widows aged 40-79 | ₹1,000-1,500 |
| Widows aged 80+ | ₹1,500-2,000 |

> Exact amounts include central (₹300) and state top-up. Verify with the District Social Welfare Office.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Status:** Must be a widow (husband deceased)
- **Age:** 40-79 years (under IGNWPS); state scheme may cover from 18+
- **Residency:** Permanent resident of Sikkim with SSC/COI
- **Income:** BPL category or below prescribed income limit
- **Not remarried**
- **Not receiving** any other government pension

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar Card
- Sikkim Subject Certificate / COI
- Husband's death certificate
- Income certificate / BPL card
- Bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked)
- Age proof
- Passport-size photographs (2)
- Self-declaration of not being remarried

## How to Apply – Step-by-Step

1. Visit the **Block Administrative Centre (BAC)** or **District Social Welfare Office**
2. Collect the **Widow Pension application form**
3. Fill in all personal and bank details
4. Attach self-attested copies of all documents
5. Get the form attested by the **Gram Panchayat / Ward Councillor**
6. Submit at the BAC or District office
7. After verification and approval, pension is credited monthly via DBT

## Processing Time

- **Verification to approval:** 1-3 months
- **First payment:** Within 1-2 months of approval

## Important Tips

1. **Obtain the death certificate first** — this is mandatory and can be obtained from the Municipal/Panchayat office
2. **Annual life verification** is required to continue pension
3. **Pension stops upon remarriage** — inform authorities of any status change
4. **Keep bank account active** — dormant accounts may cause payment failures
5. **Apply as soon as possible** after becoming eligible

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Can a widow receive both old age pension and widow pension?
**A:** Generally, **only one pension** is provided under NSAP. You can choose whichever gives a higher benefit.

### Q2: What if I don't have my husband's death certificate?
**A:** You must obtain it. Visit the **Municipal Office or Gram Panchayat** where the death was registered. See our guide on [death certificate](/guide/death-certificate-apply-online) for help.

### Q3: Is there an age limit?
**A:** Under IGNWPS, the age range is **40-79 years**. Widows aged 80+ may shift to the old age pension scheme for a higher amount.

### Q4: How is the pension paid?
**A:** Through **Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)** to the beneficiary's Aadhaar-linked bank account.

### Q5: Can I apply online?
**A:** Check [nsap.nic.in](https://nsap.nic.in) for online IGNWPS application. Otherwise, apply offline at the BAC.
