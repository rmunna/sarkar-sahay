## What is the National Food Security Act?

The National Food Security Act (NFSA), 2013 is a landmark law that provides a **legal right to food** for approximately 81.35 crore people (about two-thirds of India's population). It ensures that eligible households receive subsidized food grains through the Public Distribution System (PDS).

The Act converts the existing food security programs from **welfare schemes** to **legal entitlements**, meaning if you're eligible, receiving food is your right, not a favour.

## Your Rights Under NFSA

### Right to Subsidized Food Grains
- **Priority Households (PHH):** 5 kg of food grains per person per month at:
  - Rice: ₹3/kg
  - Wheat: ₹2/kg
  - Coarse grains: ₹1/kg
- **Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY):** 35 kg per household per month at the same prices

### Right to Maternity Benefit
- Pregnant and lactating women are entitled to a **meal** (through Anganwadi) and a **maternity benefit of ₹6,000** in installments (first two live births)
- This right exists independent of other maternity schemes

### Right to Children's Meals
- Children aged **6 months to 6 years:** Entitled to appropriate meals through Anganwadi centres
- Children aged **6 to 14 years:** Entitled to mid-day meals in government schools
- See our [PM POSHAN guide](/guide/mid-day-meal-scheme-pm-poshan) for details

### Right to Food Security Allowance
- If entitled food grains are **not supplied**, beneficiaries are entitled to a **food security allowance** (cash equivalent)
- This is a compensation for non-supply, not a replacement for food

### Right to Grievance Redressal
- The Act mandates a **grievance redressal mechanism** at district and state levels
- Every district must have a **District Grievance Redressal Officer (DGRO)**
- Every state must have a **State Food Commission**

## Who is Covered?

### Coverage
- **75% of rural population** and **50% of urban population**
- States identify eligible households based on socio-economic criteria
- Total coverage: approximately **81.35 crore persons**

### Two Categories

| Category | Coverage | Entitlement |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| Priority Household (PHH) | Most of the covered population | 5 kg per person per month |
| Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) | Poorest of the poor | 35 kg per household per month |

See our [Antyodaya Anna Yojana guide](/guide/antyodaya-anna-yojana) and [ration card types guide](/guide/ration-card-types-priorities) for detailed eligibility.

## How to Check If You're Covered

### Check Your NFSA Status
1. Visit [nfsa.gov.in](https://nfsa.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Know Your Entitlement"** or **"Check Ration Card Status"**
3. Enter your ration card number or Aadhaar number
4. View your entitlement details — category, quantity, and linked FPS

### Through State Portals
- Each state has its own PDS portal where you can check beneficiary status
- Enter your ration card or Aadhaar details
- See your entitled quantity and collection history

## How to Claim Your Entitlements

### Step 1: Get a Ration Card
- If you don't have one, apply at your local Tehsil/Food & Civil Supplies office
- You need: Aadhaar card, address proof, income proof, photographs
- Both online and offline application options available in most states

### Step 2: Know Your Fair Price Shop (FPS)
- Your ration card is linked to a specific FPS
- Visit the FPS on your designated day (check with the shop)

### Step 3: Collect Your Rations
- Carry your ration card and Aadhaar card
- Authenticate through **biometric verification** (fingerprint/iris) at the ePoS machine
- Collect your entitled quantity of rice, wheat, or coarse grains
- Verify quantity and quality before signing

### Step 4: Use One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC)
- If you've migrated to another state, you can collect rations from **any FPS in India**
- Use your existing ration card with Aadhaar-based biometric authentication
- Entitlement is portable across states

## Grievance Redressal

### If You Don't Receive Your Entitlement

**Step 1: Complain to the FPS Dealer**
- If short supply, poor quality, or refusal to serve, first complain to the FPS dealer

**Step 2: Contact Block/Taluk Supply Officer**
- If FPS doesn't resolve, escalate to the Block/Taluk Food Supply Officer

**Step 3: File Complaint with DGRO**
- Every district has a **District Grievance Redressal Officer**
- File a written complaint or use the online grievance portal at [nfsa.gov.in](https://nfsa.gov.in)
- DGRO must resolve within 30 days

**Step 4: Approach the State Food Commission**
- If DGRO doesn't resolve, escalate to the State Food Commission
- The Commission has powers to investigate, inspect, and order corrective action

**Step 5: Legal Action**
- As NFSA provides a legal right, you can approach the court for enforcement
- Legal aid is available for persons unable to afford legal representation

### Toll-Free Helplines
- National helpline: **1967** (food helpline)
- State-specific helplines available on NFSA portal

## Penalties Under NFSA

| Violation | Consequence |
|-----------|-------------|
| FPS dealer diverts food grains | Imprisonment up to 7 years + fine |
| FPS dealer charges more than NFSA price | Fine + license cancellation |
| Government fails to supply entitled grains | Must pay food security allowance to beneficiary |

## Important Tips

- **Know your exact entitlement** — check on nfsa.gov.in using your ration card number
- **Biometric must work** — keep your Aadhaar fingerprints updated; if they fail, request OTP-based authentication
- **Report black market sale** — if you see FPS dealers selling PDS grains in open market, report to the DGRO
- **Collect every month** — uncollected rations may be diverted; regular collection ensures accountability
- **Your right is justiciable** — if denied food, you have a legal right to approach courts

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is NFSA a scheme or a law?
NFSA is a **law** (Act of Parliament), not a scheme. This means food security is your **legal right**, and the government is legally bound to provide it.

### What if I'm eligible but don't have a ration card?
Apply for a ration card immediately at your Tehsil office. Meanwhile, you can use the Aadhaar-based system through ONORC to claim entitlements in some states.

### Can the government reduce NFSA entitlements?
The entitlements (quantity and price) are enshrined in law. Changing them requires amending the Act in Parliament. The government can enhance entitlements through additional schemes like PMGKAY.

### What is the role of the State Food Commission?
The State Food Commission monitors NFSA implementation, investigates complaints, recommends corrective measures, and publishes annual reports on food security.

### Can migrant workers get rations in their work state?
Yes, through **One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC)**, migrant workers can draw rations from any FPS in India using Aadhaar authentication.
