# KPSC FDA/SDA Exam 2025 — Qualification, Salary, Exam Pattern & Preparation Guide

The Karnataka Public Service Commission (KPSC) conducts the recruitment exam for First Division Assistant (FDA) and Second Division Assistant (SDA) posts in various government departments of Karnataka. These are among the most popular clerical/administrative government jobs in the state.

FDA and SDA posts offer a stable government career with opportunities for promotion to higher administrative positions. In 2025, the Karnataka Examination Authority (KEA) released a notification for 178 FDA and SDA vacancies under the RPC (Roster Promotion Category) quota.

## FDA Qualification in Karnataka — Detailed Eligibility

This is the most important section for aspirants searching for FDA qualification requirements.

### Educational Qualification

**FDA (First Division Assistant):**
- Must hold a **Bachelor's degree** (BA, B.Com, B.Sc, BBA, BCA, or any graduation) from a university recognized by UGC
- **Computer knowledge** is mandatory — candidates must have basic computer literacy (MS Office, typing)
- Knowledge of **Kannada language** is compulsory (reading, writing, and speaking)
- Typing speed as per Karnataka Secretariat Rules (KSR) may be tested

**SDA (Second Division Assistant):**
- Must have passed **PUC (12th/Pre-University)** or equivalent from a recognized board
- **Computer knowledge** is mandatory
- Knowledge of **Kannada language** is compulsory

### Age Limit

| Category | Minimum Age | Maximum Age |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| General (UR) | 18 years | 35 years |
| OBC (Category 2A/2B/3A/3B) | 18 years | 38 years |
| SC/ST/Category-1 | 18 years | 40 years |
| Ex-Servicemen | As per Karnataka government rules | Additional relaxation applicable |

### Nationality & Domicile
- Must be an Indian citizen
- Knowledge of Kannada is mandatory for all candidates
- Domicile restrictions may vary by notification — check the specific recruitment advertisement

## FDA vs SDA — Detailed Comparison

Understanding the difference between FDA and SDA is crucial for choosing the right exam to target.

| Parameter | FDA (First Division Assistant) | SDA (Second Division Assistant) |
|-----------|-------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| **Full Form** | First Division Assistant | Second Division Assistant |
| **Qualification** | Bachelor's Degree (Graduation) | PUC (12th Pass) |
| **Pay Scale** | ₹44,425 – ₹83,700 | ₹34,100 – ₹67,600 |
| **In-Hand Salary (Approx.)** | ₹48,000 – ₹58,000 | ₹36,000 – ₹45,000 |
| **Job Level** | Upper Division Clerk | Lower Division Clerk |
| **Exam Papers** | 3 Papers (400 marks) | 2 Papers (250 marks) |

| **Promotion Path** | FDA → Superintendent → Asst. Secretary | SDA → FDA → Superintendent |
| **Minimum Age** | 18 years | 18 years |
| **Work Responsibility** | File management, drafting, correspondence | Data entry, record keeping, clerical work |

## Syllabus Overview

### General Kannada
- Kannada grammar (ವ್ಯಾಕರಣ), comprehension, literature, vocabulary, translation
- Sandhi, Samaasa, Vibhakti, Alankara
- Kannada prose and poetry from standard texts

### General English
- Grammar (Tenses, Articles, Prepositions, Voice, Narration)
- Vocabulary (Synonyms, Antonyms, One-word substitution)
- Comprehension passages, sentence correction, essay writing

### General Knowledge
- History of India & Karnataka (Vijayanagara Empire, Hoysalas, Chalukyas, Unification Movement)
- Indian Geography & Karnataka Geography
- Indian Polity & Constitution
- Economy (Indian & Karnataka budget basics)
- Science & Technology
- Current Affairs (National & Karnataka-specific)
- Karnataka — Culture, Literature, Awards, Rivers, Districts

### Arithmetic (SDA Only)
- Number system, Percentage, Ratio & Proportion
- Average, Profit & Loss, Simple & Compound Interest
- Time & Work, Time & Distance, Mensuration

## Preparation Strategy for KPSC FDA SDA

### 6-Month Study Plan

**Months 1–2: Foundation Building**
- Complete Kannada grammar basics (Sandhi, Samaasa, Vibhakti)
- English grammar fundamentals (Tenses, Voice, Narration)
- Start reading newspapers daily (Prajavani/Vijaya Karnataka for Kannada, The Hindu for English)

**Months 3–4: Subject Deep Dive**
- Karnataka history and geography in detail
- Indian Polity and Constitution
- Arithmetic practice (for SDA aspirants)
- Solve topic-wise MCQs

**Months 5–6: Revision & Mock Tests**
- Solve previous year question papers (at least 5 years)
- Take weekly full-length mock tests
- Focus on weak areas identified through test analysis
- Current affairs revision (last 6 months)

### Recommended Books

| Subject | Book / Resource |
|---------|-----------------|
| General Kannada | Spardha Vijetha Kannada Guide |
| General English | Wren & Martin (Grammar), SP Bakshi Objective English |
| General Knowledge | Lucent GK (Kannada/English), Karnataka Parichaya |
| Karnataka GK | Karnataka Samanya Jnana by Spardha Vijetha |

### Key Preparation Tips

1. **Kannada language mastery:** This carries the highest marks — invest maximum time here
2. **Karnataka-specific GK:** Study Vijayanagara empire, Hoysalas, Chalukyas, unification movement, districts, rivers
3. **English grammar:** Focus on rules-based questions — they are scoring
4. **Previous papers:** KPSC follows a predictable pattern — solve at least 5 years of papers
5. **Current affairs:** Follow Karnataka-specific news — state budget, schemes, appointments
6. **Time management:** Practice completing papers within the allotted time
7. **No negative marking:** Attempt all questions — don't leave any blank

## Promotion Path & Career Growth

The career progression for FDA and SDA posts is well-defined:

```
SDA → FDA → Superintendent → Assistant Secretary → Deputy Secretary
```

- SDA can be promoted to FDA through **departmental exams**
- FDA can rise to **Superintendent** and further to **Assistant Secretary**
- Additional career growth possible through **KAS (Karnataka Administrative Service)** exam

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the FDA qualification in Karnataka?
FDA (First Division Assistant) requires a **Bachelor's degree (graduation)** from any UGC-recognized university, along with mandatory computer knowledge and Kannada language proficiency. Any stream — BA, B.Com, B.Sc, BBA, BCA — is accepted.

### What is the difference between FDA and SDA?
FDA is a higher post requiring graduation with a pay scale of ₹44,425–₹83,700, while SDA is a lower post requiring only PUC (12th) with a pay scale of ₹34,100–₹67,600. FDA has more responsibilities and a faster promotion track.

### What is the KPSC FDA salary?
The FDA pay scale is ₹44,425–₹83,700. With DA, HRA, and other allowances, the in-hand salary ranges from **₹48,000 to ₹58,000 per month** depending on posting location (urban vs rural).

### Is Kannada mandatory for FDA/SDA exam?
Yes, knowledge of Kannada is **mandatory** for both FDA and SDA posts. Paper 1 is entirely in Kannada, and candidates must be able to read, write, and speak Kannada.

### Is there an interview for FDA/SDA?
The selection process primarily involves a **written examination**. Some notifications may include a skill test (typing/computer test) for final selection.

### Can other state candidates apply for KPSC FDA/SDA?
Knowledge of Kannada is mandatory for all applicants. While there is no strict domicile requirement in all notifications, candidates from other states must demonstrate Kannada proficiency. Check the specific notification for domicile restrictions.

### How often is FDA/SDA recruitment conducted?
KPSC conducts FDA/SDA recruitment typically **every 2–3 years** based on vacancy availability. The 2025 notification had 178 vacancies, while the 2022 cycle had 1,112 vacancies.

### What is the SDA salary in Karnataka?
SDA pay scale is ₹34,100–₹67,600. The approximate in-hand salary is **₹36,000–₹45,000 per month** including allowances, depending on the posting location.

### Is there negative marking in KPSC FDA/SDA exam?
**No**, there is no negative marking. Candidates should attempt all questions to maximize their score.

### What is the age limit for KPSC FDA/SDA?
General category: 18–35 years. OBC: up to 38 years. SC/ST/Cat-1: up to 40 years. Age relaxation for ex-servicemen and PwD candidates as per government rules.

### Can I apply for both FDA and SDA simultaneously?
Yes, if separate notifications are released for both posts, eligible candidates can apply for both. However, if exams are held on the same date, you'll need to choose one.

### What is the promotion path for SDA?
SDA can be promoted to FDA through departmental exams, then further to Superintendent, Assistant Secretary, and Deputy Secretary levels over the course of their career.

### Which books are best for KPSC FDA/SDA preparation?
Spardha Vijetha guides for Kannada and Karnataka GK, Wren & Martin for English grammar, Lucent GK, and RS Aggarwal for Arithmetic (SDA) are widely recommended by toppers.
