# Karnataka FDA/SDA Exam Guide — Eligibility, Pattern & Preparation Tips

The Karnataka Public Service Commission (KPSC) conducts the recruitment exam for First Division Assistant (FDA) and Second Division Assistant (SDA) posts in various government departments of Karnataka. These are among the most popular clerical/administrative government jobs in the state.

FDA and SDA posts offer a stable government career with opportunities for promotion to higher administrative positions.

## Eligibility Criteria

### Age Limit

| Category | Minimum Age | Maximum Age |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| General | 18 years | 35 years |
| OBC (Category 2A/2B/3A/3B) | 18 years | 38 years |
| SC/ST | 18 years | 40 years |

### Educational Qualification

**FDA (First Division Assistant):**
- Bachelor's degree from a recognized university
- Computer knowledge (KSR/equivalent typing speed)

**SDA (Second Division Assistant):**
- PUC (12th pass) or equivalent
- Computer knowledge

## Exam Pattern

### FDA Exam

| Paper | Subject | Marks | Duration |
|-------|---------|-------|----------|
| Paper 1 | General Kannada | 150 | 3 hours |
| Paper 2 | General English | 150 | 3 hours |
| Paper 3 | General Knowledge | 100 | 2 hours |

### SDA Exam

| Paper | Subject | Marks | Duration |
|-------|---------|-------|----------|
| Paper 1 | General Kannada & English | 150 | 3 hours |
| Paper 2 | General Knowledge & Arithmetic | 100 | 2 hours |

- **Type:** OMR-based (Objective)
- **Negative marking:** None

## Syllabus Overview

### General Kannada
- Kannada grammar, comprehension, literature, vocabulary, translation

### General English
- Grammar, vocabulary, comprehension, sentence correction, essay

### General Knowledge
- History (India & Karnataka), Geography, Polity, Economy, Science, Current Affairs, Karnataka-specific GK

### Arithmetic (SDA)
- Number system, Percentage, Ratio, Average, Profit & Loss, SI/CI, Time & Work

## How to Apply

1. Visit KPSC at [kpsc.kar.nic.in](https://kpsc.kar.nic.in)
2. Find FDA/SDA recruitment notification
3. Register online and fill application
4. Upload photo and signature
5. Pay application fee
6. Submit and take printout

## Application Fee

| Category | Fee |
|----------|-----|
| General | ₹600 |
| OBC (2A/2B/3A/3B) | ₹300 |
| SC/ST/Cat-1 | Free |

## Salary & Benefits

- **FDA:** ₹27,650 – ₹52,650 per month
- **SDA:** ₹21,400 – ₹42,000 per month
- DA, HRA, and other allowances as per Karnataka Government
- Pension (NPS), medical benefits

## Preparation Tips

1. **Kannada language:** Strong Kannada gives a major edge — highest marks
2. **Karnataka GK:** Study Karnataka history, Vijayanagara empire, Hoysalas, unification movement
3. **English grammar:** Focus on basic grammar rules and comprehension
4. **Previous papers:** KPSC papers follow a pattern — solve at least 5 years
5. **Current affairs:** Follow Karnataka-specific and national news

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between FDA and SDA?
FDA is a higher post (Graduate level, First Division Clerk) while SDA is lower (12th level, Second Division Clerk).

### Is Kannada mandatory?
Yes, knowledge of Kannada is mandatory for both FDA and SDA posts.

### Is there an interview?
No, selection is based on written exam merit only.

### Can other state candidates apply?
Knowledge of Kannada is mandatory, but domicile restrictions vary by notification.

### What is the promotion path?
SDA → FDA → Superintendent → Assistant Secretary (through departmental exams).

### How often is FDA/SDA recruitment conducted?
Typically every 2-3 years based on vacancies.
