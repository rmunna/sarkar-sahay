# Assam Domicile Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **permanent resident certificate** (স্থায়ী বাসিন্দা প্রমাণপত্র) in Assam — also called domicile certificate — is issued by the Revenue Department (Circle Officer) and is required for NEET Assam state quota (85%) admissions, APSC government job applications, Assam state scholarships, and scheme eligibility. Applications go through the **Assam e-District portal**.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [edistrict.assam.gov.in](https://edistrict.assam.gov.in) or **Seva Setu camp / CSC**. Issued by Circle Officer within **15–30 days**.

| **Eligibility** | Born in Assam OR permanent resident |

| **Processing time** | 15–30 days |
| **Fee** | ₹10–₹30 |
| **Validity** | Generally permanent once issued |
| **Portal** | edistrict.assam.gov.in |

## Why You Need Assam Domicile Certificate

- **NEET Assam state quota (85%)** — domicile mandatory for SLUHS (Srimanta Sankaradeva University of Health Sciences) NEET counselling
- **APSC (Assam PSC)** — Assam government jobs (Grade-II/III posts)
- **SLRC (State Level Recruitment Commission)** — state recruitment (Grade-III/IV)
- **Assam Post-Matric Scholarship** — SC/ST/OBC/Minority students
- **Orunodoi Scheme** — Assam government cash transfer (requires Assam residency)
- **CM Samagra Gramya Unnayan Yojana** — scheme eligibility

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory — with Assam address)
- **Ration card** (Assam ration card)
- **Voter ID** (Assam voter enrollment — NRC Assam also relevant)
- **Birth certificate** — showing birth in Assam
- **School Transfer Certificate (TC)** — from Assam schools
- **Land Records / Jamabandi** (for rural applicants)
- **Tea garden wage certificate** — for tea garden worker families (unique Assam provision — wage slip from tea garden management as residence proof)
- Utility bills at Assam address
- Self-declaration of Assam residency
- Passport-size photograph

## How to Apply Online — Assam e-District Portal

1. Go to [edistrict.assam.gov.in](https://edistrict.assam.gov.in)
2. Register / log in with mobile number (OTP)
3. Navigate to **"Revenue Department"** → **"Permanent Resident Certificate"** or **"Domicile Certificate"**
4. Fill form:
   - Applicant name, father's name, date of birth
   - District, Sub-Division, Revenue Circle, Village/Ward
   - Duration of residence in Assam
   - Purpose of certificate
5. Upload documents (Aadhaar, ration card, voter ID, birth certificate, school TC)
6. Pay fee: **₹10–₹30** (online)
7. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via Seva Setu Camp / Arunodoy Sewa Kendra

1. Visit **Seva Setu** camp (periodic government camp at village/circle level — free service) or **Arunodoy Sewa Kendra (CSC)**
2. Carry Aadhaar, ration card, voter ID, school TC
3. Operator fills and submits on Assam e-District
4. Seva Setu camps are **free** — no service charge
5. Collect acknowledgement

---

## Status Check & Download

1. Go to [edistrict.assam.gov.in](https://edistrict.assam.gov.in) → **"Track Application"**
2. Enter application reference number
3. Once approved: download digitally signed PDF

## NEET Assam State Quota 2026

For **NEET 2026 Assam state quota** (SLUHS medical/dental counselling):
1. Assam permanent resident certificate — proof of Assam residency
2. OR studied in Assam schools for prescribed period (check SLUHS NEET notification)
3. Certificate must be Circle Officer/Deputy Commissioner issued
4. Apply well before NEET results (15–30 day processing)
5. SLUHS verifies during document round

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Tea garden worker families — special provision?

Yes. For Assam's large **tea garden worker population**, the tea garden management's wage certificate or residence proof from the tea garden estate is accepted as residency proof — since many tea garden workers don't have traditional land/property documents. Seva Setu camps in Assam specifically target this population.

### NRC Assam — does it affect domicile certificate?

The NRC (National Register of Citizens) updates are separate from the Revenue Department's domicile/permanent resident certificate process. Having your name in the NRC supports your Assam residency claim, but the domicile certificate is issued independently by the Revenue Department based on documents like voter ID, ration card, and school TC.

### APSC — is Assam domicile mandatory?

**Yes, for most Assam state government posts** (APSC Grade-I/II), Assam permanent resident certificate is required. Check the specific APSC advertisement.
