# Delhi Doorstep Delivery of Services – Complete Guide

## What is Doorstep Delivery of Services?

Delhi's Doorstep Delivery of Services is a revolutionary scheme that brings **government services directly to your home**. Instead of visiting government offices, standing in queues, and dealing with bureaucracy, Delhi residents can request a government-trained facilitator (Mobile Sahayak) to come to their doorstep and help them complete the application process.

The service covers **100+ government services** across multiple departments — from ration cards to driving licenses to caste certificates — all for a nominal fee of **₹50**.

## Who Can Use This Service?

- **Residency:** Must be a resident of Delhi
- **Age:** Any person above 18 (or parents/guardians for minors' services)
- **No Income Limit:** Available to all Delhi residents regardless of income
- **Coverage:** All areas of NCT of Delhi

## How It Works

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Call 1076:** Dial the Doorstep Delivery helpline **1076** from any phone
2. **Select Service:** Tell the operator which government service you need
3. **Provide Details:** Share your name, address, and contact number
4. **Schedule Visit:** A Mobile Sahayak (government facilitator) will be assigned and a visit will be scheduled within 24-48 hours
5. **Prepare Documents:** Keep all required documents ready (the operator will tell you what's needed)
6. **Facilitator Visit:** The Mobile Sahayak visits your home in uniform with an official ID
7. **Document Collection:** The facilitator helps fill forms, collects documents, and takes your biometrics/photos if needed
8. **Pay ₹50:** Pay the service charge of ₹50 (cash or digital)
9. **Get Receipt:** Receive a receipt with tracking number
10. **Service Delivery:** The processed document/certificate is delivered to your home or available for download

### Online Booking

1. **Visit Portal:** Go to [https://doorstepservice.delhi.gov.in](https://doorstepservice.delhi.gov.in)
2. **Register/Login:** Create account with mobile number
3. **Browse Services:** Select the service you need from the catalog
4. **Book Appointment:** Choose a convenient date and time slot
5. **Confirm:** Get SMS confirmation with facilitator details

## Processing Time

- **Facilitator Visit:** Within 24–48 hours of calling 1076
- **Document Submission:** Same day as facilitator visit
- **Service Processing:** Depends on the specific service:
  - Certificates (income, caste, domicile): 7–15 days
  - Ration card: 15–30 days
  - Driving license: 7–15 days
  - Pensions: 30–60 days

## Important Tips

1. **Call 1076 Early:** Call during working hours (9 AM – 6 PM) for faster scheduling. The service operates Monday to Saturday.
2. **Keep Documents Ready:** When you call 1076, the operator will list required documents. Have them ready before the facilitator arrives to avoid a repeat visit.
3. **Verify the Facilitator:** The Mobile Sahayak will carry a government ID card and wear a uniform. You can verify their identity by calling 1076.
4. **Track Your Application:** Use the receipt number to track your application status online at [https://doorstepservice.delhi.gov.in](https://doorstepservice.delhi.gov.in).
5. **Feedback Matters:** After the service, you'll receive a feedback call. Honest feedback helps improve the service quality.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Is this service available on Sundays and holidays?
**A:** The service primarily operates Monday to Saturday during working hours (9 AM – 6 PM). Sunday and holiday services may be limited.

### Q2: Can I trust the Mobile Sahayak with my original documents?
**A:** Mobile Sahayaks are government-trained and verified facilitators. They provide a proper receipt for any documents collected. However, avoid handing over documents without receiving a receipt.

### Q3: What if I need a service that's not in the list?
**A:** Call 1076 and ask — new services are added regularly. If it's not available, they'll guide you to the appropriate government office.

### Q4: Can someone else in my family request the service on my behalf?
**A:** Yes, any family member can call 1076 and book the service. However, the actual applicant may need to be present during the facilitator's visit for biometric verification.

### Q5: Is the ₹50 fee refundable if my application is rejected?
**A:** The ₹50 is a facilitation fee and is non-refundable regardless of the application outcome. However, the facilitator ensures your application is complete before submission.

### Q6: How is this different from e-District services?
**A:** E-District requires you to apply online yourself and may still require office visits. Doorstep Delivery brings a trained facilitator to your home who handles everything — especially useful for elderly, disabled, or tech-unfamiliar citizens.

### Q7: Is this available for commercial/business registrations?
**A:** Some business-related services like shop registration are available. Call 1076 for the complete updated list.
