# West Bengal Income Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **income certificate** (আয়ের সার্টিফিকেট) in West Bengal is issued by the Revenue Department and is required for the Swami Vivekananda Scholarship, NSP scholarship, Kanyashree, Aikyashree, and various state government scheme eligibility checks. Applications are processed through **e-District West Bengal** at [edistrict.wb.gov.in](https://edistrict.wb.gov.in).

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [edistrict.wb.gov.in](https://edistrict.wb.gov.in) or visit your BDO (Block Development Office) / SDO office. Issued by Revenue Officer within **21–30 days**.

---

## Why You Need WB Income Certificate

- **Swami Vivekananda Merit-cum-Means Scholarship** — mandatory income proof
- **Aikyashree Scholarship** (minority students) — income below ₹2 lakh
- **NSP National Scholarship** — all scholarship categories
- **Kanyashree K3** (higher education) — income criterion
- **BPL/RKSY ration card** eligibility in WB
- **OBC-A / OBC-B Non-Creamy Layer** certificate — needs income proof
- **Government college fee waiver** (SC/ST/OBC)

---

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory)
- **Voter ID** (West Bengal address mandatory for state schemes)
- **Ration card** (RKSY/PHH — shows family composition)
- Self-declaration of income (affidavit or online form)
- For salaried: **salary certificate** from employer
- For farmers: **land records** (from BANGLARBHUMI portal — Khatian/RS Dag)
- Passport-size photograph

---

## How to Apply Online — e-District WB

1. Go to [edistrict.wb.gov.in](https://edistrict.wb.gov.in)
2. Click **"Apply for Service"** → Register / log in with mobile number
3. Navigate to **"Revenue"** → **"Income Certificate"**
4. Fill the form:
   - Applicant name, address (district, block/municipality, gram panchayat)
   - Annual family income (declare all sources)
   - Purpose of certificate
5. Upload documents (Aadhaar, voter ID, income proof)
6. Pay fee: **₹10–30** (online payment)
7. Submit — note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via BDO / SDO Office (Offline)

1. Visit **Block Development Office (BDO)** (rural) or **SDO office** (urban / sub-division)
2. Collect income certificate application form
3. Submit with all documents (Aadhaar, voter ID, ration card, income declaration)
4. Receipt is issued; verification starts
5. Collect certificate from BDO/SDO office after approval

---

## Apply via Duare Sarkar Camp

During **Duare Sarkar** ("Government at Your Doorstep") camps, income certificates are issued directly. Watch for camp announcements from your Gram Panchayat or Municipality — they occur periodically across WB.

---

## Status Check

1. Go to [edistrict.wb.gov.in](https://edistrict.wb.gov.in)
2. Click **"Track Application"** → enter reference number
3. Or log in → **"My Applications"** → view status
4. Status: Submitted → Under Verification → BDO Approval → Issued

---

## Download WB Income Certificate

Once approved:
1. Log in to edistrict.wb.gov.in
2. Go to **"My Applications"** → click approved certificate
3. Download digitally signed PDF
4. Verify at e-District WB → "Verify Certificate" using certificate number
