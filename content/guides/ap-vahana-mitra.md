# Vahana Mitra – ₹10,000/Year for Auto & Taxi Drivers in Andhra Pradesh

Vahana Mitra is a financial assistance scheme by the Government of Andhra Pradesh that provides ₹10,000 per year to auto-rickshaw drivers, taxi drivers, and other small transport vehicle owners. The scheme helps drivers meet vehicle maintenance, insurance, and fuel costs.

## What is Vahana Mitra?

The AP government introduced Vahana Mitra to support the livelihood of auto-rickshaw and taxi drivers who form a significant part of the urban and semi-urban transport workforce. The annual financial assistance of ₹10,000 is credited directly to the driver's bank account via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Occupation:** Must be an auto-rickshaw driver, taxi driver, or maxi-cab driver
2. **Valid driving license:** Must hold a valid driving license for the vehicle type
3. **Vehicle ownership/operation:** Must own or operate an auto-rickshaw, taxi, or similar small transport vehicle
4. **Age:** 18 years or above
5. **Residency:** Must be a resident of Andhra Pradesh
6. **Income:** Should belong to economically weaker sections (white ration card holders preferred)
7. **Exclusions:**
   - Government employees
   - Income tax payers
   - Owners of heavy transport vehicles (trucks, buses)

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar card (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- Valid driving license
- Vehicle registration certificate (RC) in the applicant's name
- Bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked)
- Ration card (white card preferred)
- Passport-size photographs
- Mobile number

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Through Village/Ward Secretariat

1. **Visit Ward/Village Secretariat:** Go to your nearest Ward Secretariat (urban) or Village Secretariat (rural)
2. **Meet the Transport Assistant:** Approach the relevant assistant at the Secretariat
3. **Submit application:** Fill in the application form with personal, vehicle, and bank details
4. **Provide documents:** Submit copies of driving license, RC, Aadhaar, bank passbook, and ration card
5. **Verification:** The Secretariat staff verifies your documents and vehicle details
6. **Approval:** Applications are approved at the mandal/district level
7. **DBT credit:** ₹10,000 is credited to your bank account annually

### Verification Process

- Transport department officials may verify that the applicant is actively operating the vehicle
- Physical verification at the Secretariat level
- Cross-checking with RTO records

## Benefit Details

| Detail | Information |

| Amount | ₹10,000 per year |
| Mode | Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) |
| Frequency | Annual |
| Eligible vehicles | Auto-rickshaw, taxi, maxi-cab |
| Payment to | Driver's Aadhaar-linked bank account |

## Processing Time

- **Application to verification:** 15–30 days
- **Disbursement:** Annual, usually disbursed on a date announced by the government

## Important Tips

1. **Keep license and RC updated:** Expired driving license or vehicle registration will make you ineligible
2. **Aadhaar-bank linking is mandatory:** Without this, DBT cannot be processed
3. **Apply through Secretariat only:** Do not pay any agent — the application is free
4. **One benefit per person:** Even if you own multiple vehicles, you get ₹10,000 once per year
5. **Renew annually:** You may need to re-verify your eligibility each year

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: How much money will I get under Vahana Mitra?
₹10,000 per year, credited directly to your bank account.

### Q2: Can I apply if I drive someone else's auto?
The scheme primarily targets vehicle owner-drivers. If you are a hired driver without vehicle ownership, eligibility may vary — check with your local Secretariat.

### Q3: Is this scheme for car taxi drivers too?
Yes, taxi drivers and maxi-cab drivers are also eligible, not just auto-rickshaw drivers.

### Q4: When is the amount disbursed?
The disbursement date varies each year. The government announces the date — typically once a year.

### Q5: Do I need a white ration card?
While white ration card holders are prioritized, check current eligibility criteria at your Secretariat as the rules may have been updated.

### Q6: Can women auto drivers apply?
Yes, women who drive auto-rickshaws or taxis are equally eligible.

### Q7: What if my application is rejected?
Visit your Ward/Village Secretariat to understand the rejection reason. Correct any document issues and reapply.
