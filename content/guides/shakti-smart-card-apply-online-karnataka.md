# Shakti Smart Card Apply Online — Karnataka Free Bus Scheme

The **Shakti Smart Card** is a dedicated travel card issued under Karnataka's [Shakti free bus scheme](/guide/shakti-free-bus-karnataka) that allows women to travel free on all non-AC state buses (KSRTC, BMTC, NWKRTC, NEKRTC) without needing to show Aadhaar at every trip. This guide focuses specifically on **how to apply for the Shakti Smart Card online**.

> **Current Status:** While the Shakti scheme currently works with Aadhaar-based ticketing, the Karnataka government has announced plans for dedicated Shakti Smart Cards with integrated metro access. The smart card application is being rolled out in phases across the state.

---

## What Is the Shakti Smart Card?

The Shakti Smart Card is a **reusable tap-and-go card** that replaces the current process of showing Aadhaar and getting a printed ticket for each journey. Benefits include:

- **Faster boarding** — just tap the card at the validator
- **No need to carry Aadhaar** physically on every trip
- **Digital record** of all journeys
- **Future metro integration** — planned Namma Metro compatibility

## Who Is Eligible?

| Criteria | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Gender** | Women and transgender persons |
| **Residency** | Karnataka resident (Aadhaar address in Karnataka) |
| **Age** | No age restriction |
| **Income** | No income limit — universal for all women |
| **Existing Requirement** | Must have Aadhaar with Karnataka address |

> Men are **not eligible** for the Shakti Smart Card. For other Karnataka schemes, see our [Gruha Lakshmi guide](/guide/gruha-lakshmi-karnataka).

## Documents Required

Keep these ready before starting your application:

1. **Aadhaar Card** — with Karnataka address (mandatory)
2. **Recent passport-size photograph** — white background, clear face
3. **Mobile number** linked to Aadhaar — for OTP verification
4. **Ration Card** (optional) — for additional address verification
5. **Existing bus pass** (if any) — for migration to smart card

## How to Apply for Shakti Smart Card Online

### Method 1: Through Seva Sindhu Portal

1. Visit [sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in/)
2. Click **"New User? Register Here"** if you don't have an account
3. Register using your mobile number and create a password
4. Log in and search for **"Shakti Smart Card"** in the services section
5. Click on the application form
6. Enter your **Aadhaar number** and verify with OTP
7. Fill in personal details — name, age, address, mobile number
8. Upload your **passport-size photograph** (JPEG/PNG, max 200KB)
9. Upload **Aadhaar card copy** (PDF, max 500KB)
10. Review all details and submit
11. Note down your **Application Reference Number** for tracking

### Method 2: Through BMTC/KSRTC Counters (Offline)

If you prefer offline application or face issues online:

1. Visit any **BMTC bus pass counter** (Bengaluru) or **KSRTC depot** (other districts)
2. Carry original Aadhaar card and one photocopy
3. Carry one passport-size photograph
4. Fill the application form available at the counter
5. Submit documents and collect acknowledgment receipt
6. Smart card will be dispatched or available for collection in 7-15 days

### Method 3: Through Bapuji Seva Kendras

1. Visit your nearest **Bapuji Seva Kendra** (Karnataka Citizen Service Centre)
2. Carry Aadhaar card (original + copy)
3. The operator will fill the form and submit on your behalf
4. Fee: ₹0 (free application, though Seva Kendra may charge nominal processing fee)

## How to Track Shakti Smart Card Application Status

1. Visit [sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in/)
2. Click **"Track Application"** or **"Check Status"**
3. Enter your **Application Reference Number** or **Aadhaar number**
4. View current status — Submitted / Under Processing / Approved / Dispatched

You can also call the **KSRTC helpline: 1800-425-5955** (toll-free) or **BMTC helpline: 1800-425-1663** for status updates.

## How to Use the Shakti Smart Card

Once you receive the card:

1. **Tap the card** on the Electronic Ticketing Machine (ETM) when boarding the bus
2. The conductor will validate it — the machine shows your name and photo
3. **No ticket needed** — the card acts as your ticket
4. Works on all **non-AC buses** of KSRTC, BMTC, NWKRTC, and NEKRTC
5. **Does not work** on Airavat (AC/Volvo), Vayu Vajra, or private buses

## Common Issues and Fixes

### Application not visible on Seva Sindhu
The Shakti Smart Card service may be listed under a different name. Search for "Shakti" or "Free bus" or "Women bus pass". If not found, the online portal for your district may not have launched yet — try the offline method.

### Aadhaar OTP not received
Ensure your mobile number is linked to Aadhaar. See our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) for mobile update steps.

### Photo upload failing
Resize your photo to under 200KB. Use JPEG format. Photo should have a white background and show your face clearly.

### Smart card not received after 15 days
Visit the BMTC/KSRTC depot where you applied, or call the helpline with your reference number.

## Important Tips

1. **You can still travel free without the Smart Card** — show your Aadhaar to the conductor for a free ticket
2. **Don't share your Smart Card** — it's linked to your Aadhaar and photo; misuse can lead to blacklisting
3. **Report lost cards immediately** — call the helpline to block and request a replacement
4. **Keep your Aadhaar updated** — if you change address to another state, the card may be deactivated
5. **The Smart Card doesn't cover metro yet** — Namma Metro integration is planned for a future phase

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the Shakti Smart Card mandatory for free bus travel?
No. You can still travel free by showing your Aadhaar card to the conductor. The Smart Card just makes the process faster and more convenient.

### Can I apply for the Smart Card for my daughter/mother?
Each woman must apply individually using her own Aadhaar. You can help fill the form, but the Aadhaar and OTP must be the applicant's own.

### Does the Smart Card work on Volvo/AC buses?
No. The Shakti scheme and Smart Card are valid only on **non-AC/ordinary** services. AC, Airavat, and Vayu Vajra buses require regular tickets.

### Can I use the Smart Card outside Karnataka?
No. It works only on Karnataka state transport buses within the state.

### What if my Aadhaar has a different state address?
You must have a **Karnataka address on your Aadhaar** to be eligible. Update your Aadhaar address first if you've moved to Karnataka.

### Is there a validity period for the Smart Card?
The card is valid as long as the Shakti scheme is active and your Aadhaar is linked to a Karnataka address. No renewal needed.

### Can transgender persons apply?
Yes. Transgender persons are eligible for the Shakti scheme and can apply for the Smart Card using the same process.
