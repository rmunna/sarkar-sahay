# Metro Card Recharge Online: Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore

Metro smart cards save you from buying tokens every trip and offer discounted fares. Most metro systems in India now support online recharging via UPI apps, official portals, and QR-based tickets. This guide covers how to recharge your metro card for Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, and other cities.

## Delhi Metro (DMRC) Smart Card Recharge

### Online Recharge Methods

**1. Paytm App**
1. Open Paytm → Search "Delhi Metro"
2. Enter your **smart card number** (printed on the card)
3. Enter recharge amount (₹50–₹2,000)
4. Pay via UPI, wallet, or card
5. **Tap the card at any metro station** AFC gate or customer care counter to activate the online recharge

**2. PhonePe App**
1. Open PhonePe → Metro Recharge
2. Select "Delhi Metro"
3. Enter card number and amount
4. Complete payment
5. Activate at station

**3. DMRC Official App (Delhi Metro Rail)**
1. Download the "Delhi Metro Rail" app
2. Select "Smart Card Recharge"
3. Enter card number and amount
4. Pay via UPI or net banking
5. Activate at station reader

**4. Amazon Pay / Google Pay**
- Available through the respective apps
- Same process: enter card number → pay → activate at station

### At Station Recharge
- Use **Add Value Machines (AVM)** at metro stations
- Accept cash and cards
- Balance updated instantly — no activation needed

### QR Ticket (Token-Free Travel)
- Buy a **QR ticket** via the DMRC app or Paytm
- Select source and destination stations
- Pay online and receive a QR code
- Scan the QR code at AFC gates to enter and exit
- **No physical token or card needed**

## Mumbai Metro Card Recharge

### Online Recharge
1. **Chalo App** — The official app for Mumbai Metro
   - Download Chalo app → Select Mumbai Metro
   - Add your card → Enter recharge amount
   - Pay via UPI/card → Activate at station
2. **Paytm** — Search "Mumbai Metro" → Enter card number → Pay
3. **PhonePe** — Metro Recharge → Mumbai Metro → Pay

### At Station
- Customer Service Centre at any Mumbai Metro station
- Accepts cash and cards
- Top-up machines available at major stations

### QR Tickets
- Available via the Chalo app for Mumbai Metro lines
- Buy single journey or return journey QR tickets

## Bangalore Namma Metro (BMRCL) Recharge

### Online Recharge
1. **WhatsApp Recharge** — BMRCL offers recharge via WhatsApp
   - Save the BMRCL WhatsApp number
   - Send "Hi" and follow the prompts
   - Enter card number and amount → Pay via UPI
   - Activate at any metro station

2. **Paytm**
   - Search "Namma Metro" or "Bangalore Metro"
   - Enter smart card number
   - Pay → Activate at station

3. **PhonePe**
   - Metro section → Namma Metro → Pay

4. **BMRCL Official Website**
   - Visit [english.bmrc.co.in](https://english.bmrc.co.in/)
   - Online recharge section
   - Enter card details and pay

### At Station
- Ticket counters at all Namma Metro stations
- Top-up at **Ticket Vending Machines (TVMs)**
- Cash and UPI accepted

### QR Tickets
- Available via the Namma Metro app and WhatsApp
- Buy QR-based single journey tickets

## National Common Mobility Card (NCMC)

The **NCMC** (One Nation One Card) is a RuPay-based card that works across multiple metro systems, buses, and toll plazas. Key features:

- Issued by banks (SBI, PNB, Bank of Baroda, etc.)
- Works on Delhi Metro, Mumbai Metro, and expanding to other cities
- Can also be used for retail purchases (stored value)
- Recharge via any bank app or at metro stations

Check if your city's metro supports NCMC at [npci.org.in](https://www.npci.org.in/).

## Important Tips

1. **Online recharge requires activation** — you must tap your card at a station reader or AFC gate before the recharged amount becomes usable
2. **Keep minimum balance** of ₹50–₹100 to avoid being stuck at the exit gate
3. **QR tickets are valid for limited time** — usually 30 minutes to 1 hour from purchase; plan accordingly
4. **Register your card** on the official app to track balance, history, and get refund if lost
5. **NCMC cards** are the future — consider getting one if available in your city for cross-city travel

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Can I recharge my metro card using UPI?
Yes, all major metro systems support UPI-based recharge via Paytm, PhonePe, Google Pay, and official metro apps.

### Q2. Why is my online recharge not showing on the card?
Online recharges need to be **activated at a metro station**. Tap your card at an Add Value Machine (AVM) or AFC gate to sync the balance.

### Q3. Can I use a Delhi Metro card in Mumbai or Bangalore?
Regular smart cards are city-specific and cannot be used across cities. However, NCMC (National Common Mobility Card) works across participating metro systems.

### Q4. What if I lose my metro smart card?
If your card is registered, visit the customer care counter at any metro station to block the card and transfer the balance to a new card. Unregistered cards cannot be recovered.

### Q5. Is there a metro card for students or senior citizens?
Yes, most metro systems offer concessional cards for students, senior citizens, and differently-abled persons. Visit the metro station counter with valid ID proof to apply.

### Q6. Can I get a refund on my metro smart card balance?
Yes, you can surrender your card at a customer care centre and get a refund of the balance and deposit (if applicable). Processing takes 7–15 days.
