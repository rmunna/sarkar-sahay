# PhonePe Cashback Not Received? How to Fix Rewards Issues

PhonePe frequently runs cashback offers, scratch cards, and reward campaigns. If you completed a qualifying transaction but didn't receive your expected cashback or reward, this guide will help you check eligibility, understand processing times, and raise a complaint if needed.

## Why You Might Not Have Received Cashback

Before raising a complaint, understand that most "missing cashback" cases are due to not meeting the offer's terms and conditions. Here are the common reasons:

### 1. Eligibility Check Failed

Every PhonePe offer has specific eligibility criteria. Common reasons for disqualification:

- **New user only offers** — Available only to users making their first transaction on PhonePe. If you've used PhonePe before, you don't qualify.
- **Specific payment method** — Some offers work only with UPI, not wallet or credit card (or vice versa).
- **Minimum transaction amount** — The offer may require a minimum spend (e.g., ₹200 or more).
- **Specific merchant/biller** — Cashback may apply only to specific merchants, recharges, or bill payments.
- **Bank-specific offer** — Some cashbacks apply only when paying from a specific bank account.
- **Limited redemptions** — "First 10,000 users" type offers may have already exhausted the quota.

**How to check:** Go to PhonePe > **Offers** section > find the offer > read the **Terms & Conditions** carefully.

### 2. Terms and Conditions Not Met

Common T&C clauses that cause cashback denial:

| Condition | Example |
|---|---|
| Minimum amount | "Pay ₹500 or more" |
| First transaction only | "Valid for first-time users only" |
| Specific category | "Only on electricity bill payments" |
| Payment method | "Pay via UPI only" |
| Promo code required | "Enter code SAVE50 before payment" |
| Offer period | "Valid from 1st–15th March 2026 only" |
| Per user limit | "Cashback once per user per month" |
| Maximum cashback | "Up to ₹100 cashback" (you may get less) |

### 3. Cashback Processing Time

Cashback is **not always instant**. Different offers have different timelines:

- **Scratch cards** — Usually available within 24 hours of the qualifying transaction.
- **Direct cashback** — Credited within **24–72 hours** after the transaction.
- **Campaign rewards** — May take up to **15–30 days** after the campaign ends.
- **Promotional cashback** — Some offers credit cashback only after the campaign period ends and verification is complete.

**Check offer T&C** for the specific crediting timeline mentioned.

### 4. Cashback Credited but Not Visible

Sometimes cashback is credited but you may not realize it:

- **PhonePe wallet** — Some cashbacks go to your PhonePe wallet, not your bank account. Check **My Money > PhonePe Wallet**.
- **Scratch card** — You may have received a scratch card instead of direct cashback. Check **My Rewards > Scratch Cards**.
- **Gift voucher** — Some rewards are credited as vouchers. Check **My Rewards > Vouchers**.

## How to Check Your Cashback and Rewards

1. Open PhonePe app.
2. Tap on **My Rewards** (gift icon on home screen).
3. Check these sections:
   - **Scratch Cards** — Unclaimed scratch cards.
   - **Rewards** — Cashback history.
   - **Vouchers** — Gift vouchers received.
4. Also check **PhonePe Wallet balance** under My Money.
5. Check **Transaction History** for any cashback credit entries.

## How to Raise a Ticket for Missing Cashback

If you've verified eligibility and the processing time has passed:

1. Open PhonePe > tap **Help (?)** icon.
2. Select **"Cashback & Rewards"** category.
3. Choose **"Cashback not received"** or similar option.
4. Select the **qualifying transaction** from the list.
5. Describe the issue briefly (e.g., "Completed ₹500 recharge on 5th March, cashback of ₹50 not received as per offer XYZ").
6. Submit the ticket.
7. PhonePe support will review and respond within **3–5 working days**.

**Tip:** Take a screenshot of the offer's T&C page before making the transaction. Attach it to your complaint as proof.

## Common Offer Conditions to Watch Out For

1. **"Up to ₹X cashback"** — This means the cashback amount varies. You may get ₹1 or ₹5 via scratch card, not the maximum amount.
2. **"Assured cashback"** — Guaranteed but the amount may be very small (as low as ₹1).
3. **Promo codes** — Must be applied **before** making the payment. Cannot be applied retroactively.
4. **Offer stacking** — Usually only one offer applies per transaction. You can't combine multiple offers.
5. **Transaction must be successful** — Failed or refunded transactions don't qualify for cashback.

## Important Tips

1. Always read the full **Terms & Conditions** of any offer before making a transaction.
2. Take a screenshot of the offer details and T&C as proof.
3. Wait for the full processing period (mentioned in T&C) before raising a complaint.
4. Check all sections — wallet, scratch cards, vouchers — before assuming cashback wasn't received.
5. PhonePe reserves the right to modify or withdraw offers without notice.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does PhonePe cashback take to credit?

Most cashbacks are credited within 24–72 hours. Some promotional campaigns may take 15–30 days after the campaign ends. Check the specific offer's T&C for the exact timeline.

### Where is my PhonePe cashback credited?

Cashback can be credited to your **PhonePe wallet**, as a **scratch card**, or as a **gift voucher** depending on the offer. Check My Rewards and Wallet balance.

### I applied a promo code but didn't get cashback. Why?

Verify that you entered the code correctly before payment, met the minimum amount, used the required payment method, and the offer was still active. Also check if the offer was limited to a certain number of users.

### Can I get cashback on a failed transaction?

No. Cashback applies only to **successful transactions**. If your transaction failed and was refunded, you will not receive any cashback.

### PhonePe scratch card shows ₹0 or "Better luck next time." Is this normal?

Yes. Scratch cards are chance-based. PhonePe doesn't guarantee a reward on every scratch card. "Better luck next time" means you didn't win anything on that particular card.

### How do I contact PhonePe support for cashback issues?

Open PhonePe > Help (?) > Cashback & Rewards > select the issue. You can also email support@phonepe.com with your registered mobile number and transaction details.
