# How to Change Address in [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) Online and Offline

If you have shifted to a new address, you must update your address in Aadhaar. UIDAI allows you to change your address both online (through the myAadhaar portal) and offline (by visiting an Aadhaar enrolment centre). This guide covers both methods with complete steps.

## What is Aadhaar Address Update?

Aadhaar address update is the process of changing the residential address recorded in your Aadhaar database. After the update, your new address will reflect in your e-Aadhaar and any future Aadhaar prints. You can update your address:

- **Online** — via the myAadhaar Self Service Update Portal (SSUP) at [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/)
- **Offline** — by visiting any Aadhaar enrolment or update centre

**Important:** Your mobile number must be registered with Aadhaar to use the online method. If your mobile is not linked, you must visit an enrolment centre.

---

## Online Process — Change Address via myAadhaar Portal

### Prerequisites

- Aadhaar number
- Registered mobile number linked to your Aadhaar (for OTP verification)
- Scanned copy of a valid address proof document (JPEG/PNG, max 2 MB)

### Step-by-Step Process

1. Go to [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/).
2. Click on **"Login"** and enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number.
3. Enter the OTP received on your registered mobile number.
4. After login, select **"Address Update"** from the services menu.
5. Enter your **new address** in the required fields (House No., Street, Locality, District, State, PIN Code).
6. Enter the address in English. The portal will automatically transliterate it into your selected regional language. You can edit the transliteration if needed.
7. Upload a **scanned colour copy** of your address proof document.
8. Review all details carefully and click **"Submit"**.
9. A **Service Request Number (SRN)** will be generated. Note it down and download the invoice.
10. You will also receive the SRN via SMS on your registered mobile number.

### Supported Regional Languages for Online Update

Bengali, Gujarati, Hindi, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Odia, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, and Urdu.

## Offline Process — Change Address at Aadhaar Enrolment Centre

### Prerequisites

- Aadhaar number or Enrolment ID (EID)
- Original address proof document for verification
- Photocopy of the address proof document to submit

### Step-by-Step Process

1. Locate the nearest Aadhaar enrolment or update centre using the [UIDAI Centre Locator](https://bhuvan-app3.nrsc.gov.in/aadhaar/).
2. Visit the centre and collect an **Aadhaar Update/Correction Form**.
3. Fill in the form with your Aadhaar number and new address details.
4. Attach a **photocopy** of your address proof document.
5. Carry the **original document** for verification by the operator.
6. Submit the form. The operator will process your request and capture your biometrics if needed.
7. You will receive an **Update Request Number (URN)** on the acknowledgement slip.
8. A fee of **₹75** (inclusive of GST) is charged for any update at the enrolment centre.

## Documents Accepted as Address Proof (Proof of Address)

UIDAI accepts the following documents as valid proof of address for Aadhaar address update:

1. Passport
2. Bank Statement or Passbook
3. Post Office Account Statement or Passbook
4. Ration Card
5. Voter ID (EPIC)
6. Driving Licence
7. Government Photo ID Card or Service Photo ID Card issued by PSU
8. Electricity Bill (not older than 3 months)
9. Water Bill (not older than 3 months)
10. Telephone Landline Bill (not older than 3 months)
11. Property Tax Receipt (not older than 1 year)
12. Credit Card Statement (not older than 3 months)
13. Insurance Policy
14. Signed Letter with Photo from a Recognised Educational Institution on its letterhead
15. NREGS Job Card
16. Arms Licence
17. Pensioner Card
18. Freedom Fighter Card
19. Kissan Passbook
20. CGHS / ECHS Card
21. Certificate of Address issued by MP, MLA, Group A Gazetted Officer, or Tehsildar
22. Address Card with Photo issued by Department of Posts
23. Caste and Domicile Certificate with Photo issued by State Government
24. Income Certificate with Photo issued by State Government or UT Administration
25. Disability ID Card / Handicapped Medical Certificate issued by the respective State / UT Government
26. Gas Connection Bill (not older than 3 months)
27. Certificate from Superintendent / Warden of a recognised Shelter Home
28. Marriage Certificate issued by Government
29. Registered Rent / Lease Agreement
30. Certificate from a Gazetted Officer / Tehsildar on official letterhead with photo
31. Vehicle Registration Certificate
32. Registered Sale / Purchase Agreement

> **Note:** The document must be in the name of the Aadhaar holder. The address in the document should match the new address being updated. For online updates, upload a clear colour scan of the original document.

## Address Validation Letter (For Those Without Address Proof)

If you do not have any address proof document in your name at your current address, you can still update your Aadhaar address using the **Address Validation Letter** process.

### How It Works

1. Log in to [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/).
2. Select **"Address Update"** and then choose the option **"Address Validation Letter"**.
3. Enter your new address where you currently reside.
4. Submit the request. A **Secret Code** will be sent via **Indian Post** to your new address in a sealed letter.
5. The letter typically arrives within **30 days**.
6. Once you receive the letter, log in to myAadhaar again.
7. Enter the **Secret Code** from the letter to verify your new address.
8. Your address will be updated after successful verification.

### Key Points

- No address proof document is required for this method.
- The secret code is valid for a **limited period** (usually 30 days from the date of generation).
- This service is available only through the online portal.
- Your mobile number must be registered with Aadhaar.

## How to Track Aadhaar Address [update status](/guide/aadhaar-update-status-check)

### For Online Updates (SRN-based)

1. Go to [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/).
2. Log in with your Aadhaar number and OTP.
3. Click on **"Check Aadhaar Update Status"**.
4. Enter your **SRN (Service Request Number)**.
5. The current status of your request will be displayed.

### For Offline Updates (URN-based)

1. Go to [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/).
2. Log in and navigate to **"Check Aadhaar Update Status"**.
3. Enter your **URN (Update Request Number)** from the acknowledgement slip.
4. Check your update status.

You can also call the **UIDAI toll-free number 1947** to check your update status.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Can I change my Aadhaar address online without a registered mobile number?

No. A registered mobile number is mandatory for online address updates, as OTP verification is required. If your mobile is not linked to Aadhaar, visit an enrolment centre for the update.

### 2. How many times can I update my address in Aadhaar?

There is no fixed limit on the number of times you can update your address. However, each request must be supported by a valid address proof document.

### 3. What if my online address update request gets rejected?

Your request may be rejected if:
- The document is invalid or not from the accepted list
- The uploaded image is blurry or not a colour scan
- The address in the document does not match the new address entered
- The document is not in the Aadhaar holder's name

You can re-submit the request with a corrected document.

### 4. Can I update my address and other details (name, date of birth) together online?

No. The online portal currently supports only **address update and document update**. For name, date of birth, or gender changes, you must visit an Aadhaar enrolment centre.

### 5. Is biometric capture required for address update at the enrolment centre?

The operator may capture your biometrics (fingerprint and iris) during the update process at the enrolment centre. This is part of standard verification.

### 6. Can I update my address if I am an NRI?

Yes. NRIs with an Indian Aadhaar can update their address. If updating to an Indian address, the same online/offline process applies. A valid Indian address proof document is required.

### 7. What happens after my address is updated?

Once updated, your new address reflects in the Aadhaar database. You can download your updated e-Aadhaar from the myAadhaar portal. If you need a physical PVC Aadhaar card with the new address, you can order one from myAadhaar for ₹50.
