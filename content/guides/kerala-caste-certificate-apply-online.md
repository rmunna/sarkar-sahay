# Kerala Caste Certificate Apply Online 2026

In Kerala, the official term for caste certificate is **"Community Certificate"** (സമുദായ സർട്ടിഫിക്കറ്റ്). It is issued by the Revenue Department (Village Officer / Tahsildar) and is required for KEAM/NEET counselling reserved category seats, Kerala government scholarships, government job reservations, and central scheme eligibility. Applications go through the **Kerala e-District portal**.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [edistrict.kerala.gov.in](https://edistrict.kerala.gov.in) or nearest **Akshaya Centre**. Issued by Village Officer within **7–15 days**.

## Caste / Community Categories in Kerala

| Category | Reservation % | Notes |
|----------|---------------|-------|
| **SC** | 8% | Scheduled Castes — Kerala state list |
| **ST** | 2% | Scheduled Tribes (Adivasi communities) |
| **OBC (OEZ)** | 9% | Other Backward Classes (OEZ — Other Eligible Communities) |
| **OBC (SEBC/EZ)** | Varies | Ezhava, Dheevara and allied communities (EZ) |
| **Latin Catholics / Anglo-Indians** | Included in OBC | |
| **OBC (Muslim OBC)** | Included in OBC | Specified Muslim communities |

> Kerala uses a unique **OBC classification system** — communities are listed as OBC-H (Hindu OBC), OBC-M (Muslim OBC), OBC-C (Christian OBC), and so on. The relevant community list is maintained by the Kerala government. Your community must be in the official list to claim reservation.

## Why You Need Kerala Community Certificate

- **KEAM engineering / pharmacy admissions** — reserved category proof mandatory
- **NEET state quota counselling (Kerala)** — community certificate required
- **Kerala government scholarships** — SC/ST Post-Matric, OBC scholarships
- **Government job reservation** — Kerala PSC exams
- **Central OBC NCL certification** — for central government jobs
- **DC/DUS merit scheme eligibility** for SC/ST students

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory)
- **Ration card** (shows family community — Kerala ration card mentions community)
- **Father's community certificate** or **SSLC book** showing caste/community
- Self-declaration of community
- **Voter ID** (address proof)
- Passport-size photograph

> **Key document:** In Kerala, the **SSLC (class 10) certificate/records** showing your community, or your **father's community certificate**, is the strongest proof. Village Officer verifies community from local records.

## How to Apply Online — Kerala e-District Portal

1. Go to [edistrict.kerala.gov.in](https://edistrict.kerala.gov.in)
2. Register / log in with mobile number (OTP)
3. Click **"Revenue"** → **"Community Certificate"**
4. Fill form:
   - Applicant name, address (district, taluk, village)
   - Community declaration (select your OBC/SC/ST category)
5. Upload documents (Aadhaar, ration card, father's community proof)
6. Pay fee: **₹15–30** (online payment)
7. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via Akshaya Centre

**Akshaya Centres** (government-run digital service centres) across Kerala assist:

1. Visit nearest **Akshaya Centre**
2. Carry Aadhaar, ration card, father's community proof
3. Operator fills and submits on e-District portal
4. Service charge: approximately ₹30–50
5. Collect acknowledgement receipt

---

## Apply at Village Office (Offline)

1. Visit your **Village Office** (Desam office)
2. Meet the **Village Officer**
3. Submit community certificate application with documents
4. Village Officer verifies community from village records and household data
5. Tahsildar approves and certificate is issued
6. Collect from Village Office or Taluk office

---

## Status Check

1. Go to [edistrict.kerala.gov.in](https://edistrict.kerala.gov.in)
2. Click **"Application Status"** → enter reference number
3. Or log in → **"My Applications"** → view status

## Download Kerala Community Certificate

Once approved:
1. Log in to e-District Kerala → **"My Certificates"**
2. Click community certificate → Download digitally signed PDF
3. Valid for all official purposes including KEAM, Kerala PSC, NSP

## OBC NCL (Non-Creamy Layer) — Kerala

For **central government OBC reservation** (UPSC, railway, SSC, central colleges):
- Apply for **OBC NCL Certificate** at e-District Kerala (separate from community cert)
- Income < ₹8 lakh/year (non-agricultural income)
- Issued by Tahsildar
- Valid for **3 years** for central purposes (check latest DOPT guidelines)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My community is "Ezhava" — which category in Kerala?

**Ezhava** (and allied communities — Thiyya, Billava, etc.) falls under the **OBC (EZ/Ezhava, Dheevara and Allied Communities)** category in Kerala. This gives state-level OBC reservation. For central OBC, check if Ezhava is in the central OBC list for Kerala — it is listed in some central OBC notifications.

### Community certificate for KEAM — what validity?

For **KEAM and Kerala college admissions**, the community certificate issued **within the academic year** is required. Old certificates are typically not accepted for fresh admissions.

### Can my community certificate be used for both Kerala PSC and UPSC?

The **Kerala e-District community certificate** is valid for Kerala PSC. For **UPSC and central jobs**, you need a separate **OBC NCL certificate** (or SC/ST certificate for those categories) — which can also be obtained from Kerala e-District but is a different document type.
