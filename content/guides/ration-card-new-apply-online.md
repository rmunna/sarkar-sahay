# How to Apply for New Ration Card Online: State-Wise Step-by-Step Guide

A ration card is one of the most essential documents for Indian households, providing access to subsidized food grains under the National Food Security Act (NFSA) and serving as an important ID and address proof. This guide covers the complete process to apply for a new ration card online across all major Indian states.

## Types of Ration Cards

Under the NFSA and state food security schemes, ration cards are categorized as:

| Category | Colour (Common) | Eligibility | Benefits |
|----------|-----------------|-------------|----------|
| AAY (Antyodaya Anna Yojana) | Yellow | Poorest of the poor | 35 kg food grains per family at ₹2/kg wheat, ₹3/kg rice |
| PHH (Priority Household) | Pink/Saffron | Below poverty line families | 5 kg per person per month at subsidized rates |
| NPHH (Non-Priority) | White/Blue | Above poverty line | Limited benefits, used as ID proof |

**Note:** Card colours and categories may vary by state.

## Eligibility for New Ration Card

- Must be a **resident of the state** where you're applying
- Must have a **family** — ration cards are issued per household
- Must **not already have** a ration card in any state (checked via Aadhaar)
- **Newly married couples** who have separated from parents' ration card
- **New residents** in a state (after surrendering previous state's card)
- Family members **not included** in any existing ration card

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar card of all family members (mandatory — linked to ration card)
- Address proof — electricity bill, rent agreement, voter ID
- Income proof — [income certificate](/guides/income-certificate-apply-online), salary slip, or self-declaration
- Family details — marriage certificate, birth certificates of children
- Passport-size photographs of the head of family
- Surrender certificate (if you had a ration card in another state)
- Bank account details (for DBT/cash transfer of subsidies)
- Gas connection details (LPG number, if applicable)

## How to Apply for New Ration Card Online (State-Wise)

### Uttar Pradesh

1. Visit [fcs.up.gov.in](https://fcs.up.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"नया राशन कार्ड आवेदन"** (New Ration Card Application)
3. Login with registered mobile number
4. Select ration card type (PHH, NPHH, AAY)
5. Enter head of family details and all family members' details
6. Enter Aadhaar numbers of all members
7. Upload documents — photos, address proof
8. Submit and note application number
9. Track status on the same portal

### Bihar

1. Visit [epds.bihar.gov.in](https://epds.bihar.gov.in/)
2. Go to **"Apply for New Ration Card"**
3. Register with mobile number
4. Fill in family details with Aadhaar numbers
5. Upload documents
6. Submit — no fee
7. Field verification by supply inspector within 15 days

### Maharashtra

1. Visit [rcms.mahafood.gov.in](https://rcms.mahafood.gov.in/)
2. Login/register
3. Select **"New Ration Card Application"**
4. Fill in Form (includes family details, income, address)
5. Upload Aadhaar, photos, and supporting documents
6. Pay fee (if applicable)
7. Submit and track using application ID

### Delhi

1. Visit [nfs.delhi.gov.in](https://nfs.delhi.gov.in/)
2. Select **"Apply for New Ration Card"**
3. Login with mobile number
4. Fill application form with all family members' details
5. Upload Aadhaar copies and photographs
6. Submit — processing takes 15–30 days
7. Card delivered to registered address

### Rajasthan

1. Visit [food.rajasthan.gov.in](https://food.rajasthan.gov.in/) or [emitra.rajasthan.gov.in](https://emitra.rajasthan.gov.in/)
2. Select ration card services
3. Apply online or visit nearest e-Mitra kiosk
4. Submit family details and Aadhaar numbers
5. Track application status online

### Tamil Nadu

1. Visit [tnpds.gov.in](https://tnpds.gov.in/) or [www.tnrd.gov.in](https://www.tnrd.gov.in/)
2. Select **"Apply for New Smart Card"**
3. Fill in family details with Aadhaar seeding
4. Upload documents
5. Submit at PDS office or online

## Offline Application Process

1. Visit the nearest **Tehsildar office**, **Block office**, or **Food & Civil Supplies office**
2. Collect the ration card application form
3. Fill in details of all family members
4. Attach self-attested copies of all documents
5. Submit the form with photographs
6. Receive acknowledgement receipt
7. Supply inspector conducts home verification
8. Card issued within 15–30 days of verification

## One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC)

Under the ONORC scheme:

- You can use your ration card at **any Fair Price Shop (FPS) across India**
- No need to apply for a new card when migrating to another state
- Aadhaar-based biometric authentication at FPS
- Check portability status at [impds.nic.in](https://impds.nic.in/)

This is especially useful for migrant workers who move between states for employment.

## How to Add/Remove Members from Existing Ration Card

If you need to add a newborn, spouse, or remove a family member:

1. Visit the state food department portal
2. Select **"Ration Card Modification"** or **"Add/Delete Member"**
3. Enter ration card number
4. Add new member details with Aadhaar or remove existing member
5. Upload supporting documents (birth certificate for newborn, death certificate for removal)
6. Submit and track status

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Can I have ration cards in two different states?

No. Under the Aadhaar-linked PDS system, duplicate ration cards across states are automatically detected and flagged. You must surrender your old card before applying in a new state.

### 2. Is Aadhaar mandatory for ration card?

Yes. Aadhaar seeding (linking Aadhaar to ration card) is mandatory for all family members under NFSA. Without Aadhaar, you may not receive subsidized food grains.

### 3. What is the fee for a new ration card?

Most states issue ration cards free of charge. Some charge a nominal fee of ₹5–25 for the card.

### 4. Can a single person get a ration card?

Yes. Single individuals living independently can apply for a ration card. The card will have only one member listed.

### 5. How do I check if my ration card application is approved?

Visit your state's PDS portal and enter your application number. You'll also receive an SMS update when the card is ready.

### 6. Can I apply for ration card online without visiting any office?

In most states, you can submit the application online, but field verification by a supply inspector is mandatory. So at least one home visit will occur before the card is issued.
