## What is Aadhaar Card?

Aadhaar is a 12-digit unique identity number issued by UIDAI (Unique Identification Authority of India) to every resident of India. It serves as proof of identity and address anywhere in India.

## Who is Eligible?

- Any resident of India (including children and infants)
- No age restriction
- No citizenship requirement — residency is sufficient
- NRIs with Indian passport can also apply

## Documents Required

You need **one document from each category**:

### Proof of Identity (any one)
- Passport
- PAN Card
- Voter ID
- Driving License
- Government Photo ID

### Proof of Address (any one)
- Passport
- Bank Statement (last 3 months)
- Voter ID
- Utility Bill (electricity, water, gas — not older than 3 months)
- Ration Card

### Proof of Date of Birth (any one)
- Birth Certificate
- Passport
- Class 10 Marksheet
- PAN Card

> **For children under 5:** Only birth certificate is needed. Aadhaar of one parent is mandatory.

## Step-by-Step Process (Online Appointment)

### Step 1: Visit the UIDAI Appointment Portal
Go to [appointments.uidai.gov.in](https://appointments.uidai.gov.in)

### Step 2: Select "Book an Appointment"
Choose your city and preferred Aadhaar enrollment center.

### Step 3: Fill in Your Details
Enter your name, mobile number, email, and preferred date/time.

### Step 4: Choose Appointment Slot
Select an available slot. You'll receive a confirmation SMS.

### Step 5: Visit the Enrollment Center
On the appointment date, carry:
- Appointment confirmation
- Original documents + photocopies
- Mobile phone (for OTP verification)

### Step 6: Biometric Enrollment
At the center:
1. Submit your documents
2. Photograph will be taken
3. Fingerprints of all 10 fingers scanned
4. Iris scan of both eyes

### Step 7: Collect Acknowledgment Slip
You'll receive an **Enrollment ID (EID)**. Keep this safe — you'll need it to check status.

### Step 8: Track Your Aadhaar Status
Visit [uidai.gov.in](https://uidai.gov.in) → "Check Aadhaar Status" → Enter EID.

## Processing Time

- **Standard:** 15-30 days after enrollment
- **e-Aadhaar:** Available for download within 3-5 days on uidai.gov.in

## Important Tips

1. **Keep your mobile number updated** — it's linked to your Aadhaar for OTP
2. **Download e-Aadhaar** as a backup from the UIDAI website
3. **Don't share your Aadhaar number** publicly — use masked Aadhaar when possible
4. **Update address** whenever you move — you can do it online at ssup.uidai.gov.in

## FAQs

**Q: Can I apply for Aadhaar online without visiting a center?**
A: No, biometric enrollment (fingerprints, iris, photo) requires physical presence at an enrollment center.

**Q: Is Aadhaar mandatory?**
A: Aadhaar is not legally mandatory for all services, but it's practically required for bank accounts, mobile SIM, tax filing, and most government schemes.

**Q: What if I lose my Aadhaar card?**
A: Download e-Aadhaar from uidai.gov.in using your registered mobile number. You can also order a reprint via mAadhaar app.

**Q: Can NRIs get Aadhaar?**
A: Yes, NRIs with a valid Indian passport can apply at any enrollment center during their visit to India.

**Q: How to update details on Aadhaar?**
A: Visit ssup.uidai.gov.in for online updates (name, address, mobile, email) or visit an enrollment center for biometric updates.
