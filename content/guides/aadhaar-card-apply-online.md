## What is Aadhaar Card?

Aadhaar is a 12-digit unique identity number issued by UIDAI (Unique Identification Authority of India) to every resident of India. It serves as proof of identity and address anywhere in India. The Aadhaar system is the world's largest biometric ID program, covering over 130 crore residents.

Aadhaar is used for:
- **Bank account opening** and KYC verification
- **Mobile SIM** purchase
- **Income tax filing** and PAN linking
- **Government scheme benefits** (LPG subsidy, PM Kisan, scholarships)
- **Passport application** and other government services
- **Digital signature** and e-verification

## Who is Eligible?

- Any resident of India (including children and infants)
- No age restriction — even newborns can get Aadhaar (Baal Aadhaar)
- No citizenship requirement — residency is sufficient
- NRIs with Indian passport can also apply
- Foreign nationals who have resided in India for 182+ days in the preceding 12 months

> **Baal Aadhaar (Blue Aadhaar):** For children under 5 years. It is issued without biometrics (no fingerprint or iris scan). When the child turns 5, biometrics must be updated (free of cost). Biometrics are updated again at age 15.

## Documents Required

You need **one document from each category**:

### Proof of Identity (any one)
- Passport
- [PAN card](/guide/pan-card-apply-online)
- Voter ID
- Driving License
- Government Photo ID
- NREGA Job Card
- Arms License
- Photo ID issued by a recognized educational institution

### Proof of Address (any one)
- Passport
- Bank Statement (last 3 months)
- Voter ID
- Utility Bill (electricity, water, gas — not older than 3 months)
- Ration Card
- Property tax receipt
- Rental agreement (registered)
- Post office passbook with address

### Proof of Date of Birth (any one)
- Birth Certificate
- Passport
- Class 10 Marksheet
- PAN Card
- Government hospital birth certificate
- Driving License with DOB

> **For children under 5:** Only birth certificate is needed. Aadhaar of one parent is mandatory. The child's Aadhaar is linked to the parent's Aadhaar.

> **No documents at all?** If you have no documents, you can use an "Introducer" — a person who already has Aadhaar and is registered with UIDAI as an introducer at the enrollment center. Head of Family (HoF) based enrollment is also available.

## Step-by-Step Process (Online Appointment + Offline Enrollment)

Aadhaar enrollment requires a physical visit to an enrollment center for biometric capture. However, you can **book an appointment online** to avoid long queues.

### Step 1: Visit the UIDAI Appointment Portal
Go to [appointments.uidai.gov.in](https://appointments.uidai.gov.in)

### Step 2: Select "Book an Appointment"
Choose your city and preferred Aadhaar enrollment center. You can also search by PIN code.

### Step 3: Fill in Your Details
Enter your name, mobile number, email, and preferred date/time.

### Step 4: Choose Appointment Slot
Select an available slot. You'll receive a confirmation SMS and email with appointment details.

### Step 5: Visit the Enrollment Center
On the appointment date, carry:
- Appointment confirmation (printed or on phone)
- Original documents + photocopies
- Mobile phone (for OTP verification)

### Step 6: Fill the Enrollment Form
At the center, fill the enrollment form with your personal details. The operator will assist you. Verify all details carefully — especially your name spelling, date of birth, and address.

### Step 7: Biometric Enrollment
At the center:
1. Submit your documents for verification
2. Photograph will be taken
3. Fingerprints of all 10 fingers scanned
4. Iris scan of both eyes

> **Tip:** Ensure your fingers are clean and dry. Wet, oily, or injured fingers can cause scan failures. If fingerprints fail, iris scan is used as the primary biometric.

### Step 8: Review and Confirm
Before final submission, review all your details on the operator's screen. Check name, DOB, gender, address, and mobile number carefully. Any error now will require a paid update later.

### Step 9: Collect Acknowledgment Slip
You'll receive an **Enrollment ID (EID)** — a 14-digit number along with date and time. Keep this safe — you'll need it to check status and download e-Aadhaar.

### Step 10: Track Your Aadhaar Status
Visit [uidai.gov.in](https://uidai.gov.in) → "Check Aadhaar Status" → Enter your EID number.

## Offline Process (Walk-in at Enrollment Center)

If you prefer not to book online, you can directly visit any Aadhaar enrollment center:

1. **Find a center:** Use the UIDAI locator at [bhuvan-app3.nrsc.gov.in/aadhaar](https://bhuvan-app3.nrsc.gov.in/aadhaar/) or search on Google Maps for "Aadhaar enrollment center near me"
2. **Walk in** during working hours (usually 9:30 AM to 5:30 PM, Monday to Saturday)
3. **Carry documents** — originals and photocopies of POI, POA, and DOB proof
4. **Wait for your turn** — walk-in applicants are served after appointment holders
5. Follow steps 6-9 from the online process above

> **CSC Centers:** Common Service Centers (CSC) across India also offer Aadhaar enrollment services. Find one at [locator.csccloud.in](https://locator.csccloud.in).

## Fees

| Service | Fee |
|---------|-----|
| New Aadhaar Enrollment | **Free** |
| Aadhaar Update (demographic — name, address, DOB, gender, mobile, email) | ₹75 |
| Aadhaar Update (biometric — photo, fingerprint, iris) | ₹125 |
| PVC Aadhaar Card (plastic card) | ₹50 |
| e-Aadhaar Download | **Free** |
| Aadhaar reprint (order online) | ₹50 |

> **Important:** New Aadhaar enrollment is always free. If any center charges you for new enrollment, report it to UIDAI helpline **1947**.

## Processing Time

- **Standard processing:** 15-30 days after enrollment
- **e-Aadhaar download:** Available within 3-5 days on [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in)
- **Physical Aadhaar letter:** Delivered by India Post within 30-60 days
- **PVC card (if ordered):** 15-20 days via Speed Post

## How to Download e-Aadhaar

1. Visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in)
2. Click **"Download Aadhaar"**
3. Enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number or 14-digit EID
4. Verify via OTP on your registered mobile
5. Download the PDF — it is password protected (first 4 letters of name in CAPS + birth year, e.g., RAJA1990)

## Common Problems and Solutions

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Biometric (fingerprint) failure | Ask operator to try iris scan instead. Ensure fingers are clean and dry. |
| Mobile OTP not received | Check if mobile number is correct. Request OTP again after 60 seconds. Check if your phone has network. |
| Name spelling mistake on Aadhaar | Apply for demographic update online at myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in (₹75 fee) |
| Aadhaar status shows "rejected" | Re-apply at enrollment center. Common reasons: poor biometric quality, duplicate Aadhaar, or document issues. |
| Aadhaar letter not received by post | Download e-Aadhaar online. The e-Aadhaar is equally valid. |
| Lost Aadhaar card | Download e-Aadhaar or order reprint from myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in |
| Duplicate Aadhaar issue | Contact UIDAI helpline 1947. Only one Aadhaar per person is allowed. |

## Important Tips

1. **Keep your mobile number updated** — it's linked to your Aadhaar for OTP verification across all services
2. **Download e-Aadhaar** as a backup from the UIDAI website — it is legally valid and accepted everywhere
3. **Don't share your Aadhaar number** publicly — use masked Aadhaar (shows only last 4 digits) when possible
4. **Update address** whenever you move — you can do it online at myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in
5. **Lock your Aadhaar biometrics** when not in use — go to myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in → Lock/Unlock Biometrics
6. **Use mAadhaar app** — Download from Play Store/App Store for quick access to your Aadhaar on mobile
7. **Use Virtual ID (VID)** instead of Aadhaar number for KYC verification to protect your privacy
8. **Visit early morning** — Enrollment centers get crowded after 11 AM
9. **Check for free update camps** — UIDAI periodically runs free update camps, especially for biometric updates

## FAQs

**Q: Can I apply for Aadhaar online without visiting a center?**
A: No, biometric enrollment (fingerprints, iris, photo) requires physical presence at an enrollment center. You can only book an appointment online.

**Q: Is Aadhaar mandatory?**
A: Aadhaar is not legally mandatory for all services, but it's practically required for bank accounts, mobile SIM, tax filing, and most government schemes.

**Q: What if I lose my Aadhaar card?**
A: Download e-Aadhaar from myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in using your registered mobile number. You can also order a PVC reprint for ₹50.

**Q: Can NRIs get Aadhaar?**
A: Yes, NRIs with a valid Indian passport can apply at any enrollment center during their visit to India. They need to be present in person.

**Q: How to update details on Aadhaar?**
A: Visit myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in for online updates (name, address, DOB, gender, email) or visit an enrollment center for biometric updates.

**Q: What is Baal Aadhaar?**
A: Baal Aadhaar is a blue-colored Aadhaar issued to children under 5. It does not contain biometrics. When the child turns 5 and 15, mandatory biometric updates are required (free of cost).

**Q: What is the UIDAI helpline number?**
A: Call **1947** (toll-free) for any Aadhaar-related queries, complaints, or grievances.

**Q: Can I have two Aadhaar cards?**
A: No. Only one Aadhaar is issued per person. If a duplicate is detected, UIDAI will deactivate one.

**Q: Is e-Aadhaar valid as an official document?**
A: Yes, e-Aadhaar downloaded from the UIDAI website is equally valid as the physical Aadhaar card and is accepted everywhere.
