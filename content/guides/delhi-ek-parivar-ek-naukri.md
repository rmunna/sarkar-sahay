# Delhi Ek Parivar Ek Naukri Yojana – Complete Guide

## What is Ek Parivar Ek Naukri Yojana?

Ek Parivar Ek Naukri Yojana (One Family One Job Scheme) is an employment initiative by the Government of NCT of Delhi aimed at providing **employment to at least one member from each family** where no family member is currently employed in a government or organized sector job. The scheme focuses on skill development, job placement, and self-employment support for economically weaker families in Delhi.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Residency:** Must be a resident of Delhi (with valid address proof)
- **Age:** Applicant must be between 18–45 years
- **Employment Status:** No family member should be employed in government service or organized sector
- **Education:** Minimum 8th pass (varies based on the job category)
- **Income:** Priority given to families with annual income below ₹3,00,000
- **Family Definition:** Family as per ration card or Aadhaar-linked address

## Documents Required

### Identity & Residence
- Aadhaar card
- Voter ID card (Delhi)
- Residence proof (electricity bill, rent agreement)

### Employment & Education
- Educational certificates (minimum 8th class)
- Skill certificates (if any — ITI, diploma, etc.)
- Self-declaration of unemployment

### Family
- Ration card (showing family members)
- Income certificate or self-declaration

### Financial
- Bank account passbook (Aadhaar-linked)

### Other
- Passport-size photographs (3-4)
- Registration at Employment Exchange (if applicable)

## Types of Employment Offered

| Category | Type | Examples |
|----------|------|---------|
| Government Jobs | Contractual/outsourced | Peons, drivers, data entry operators, security guards |
| Private Sector | Placement through job fairs | Retail, hospitality, logistics, BPO |
| Self-Employment | Entrepreneurship support | Auto/taxi permits, shop allotment, vendor licenses |
| Skill Training | Training + placement | ITI courses, short-term skill programs |

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Online Process

1. **Visit Portal:** Go to [https://employment.delhi.gov.in](https://employment.delhi.gov.in) or the Delhi government's e-District portal
2. **Register:** Create an account with Aadhaar and mobile number
3. **Fill Application:** Enter personal details, educational qualifications, skills, and job preferences
4. **Upload Documents:** Upload scanned copies of required documents
5. **Select Preferences:** Choose preferred job category (government, private, self-employment, training)
6. **Submit:** Review and submit the application
7. **Track Status:** Use the application number to check status

### Offline Process

1. **Visit Employment Exchange:** Go to your nearest Delhi Employment Exchange office
2. **Register:** Get registered as a job seeker (if not already registered)
3. **Collect Form:** Get the Ek Parivar Ek Naukri application form
4. **Fill & Submit:** Complete the form and attach all documents
5. **Get Receipt:** Collect the acknowledgment slip

### Through Job Fairs

1. **Check Announcements:** Delhi government regularly organizes Rozgar Melas (Job Fairs)
2. **Attend with Documents:** Bring all original documents and copies
3. **Interview On-Spot:** Some employers conduct interviews at the job fair
4. **Placement:** If selected, receive offer letter on the spot or within days

## Processing Time

- **Application Processing:** 15–30 days
- **Skill Training Assignment:** 1–2 months
- **Job Placement:** 2–6 months (depends on availability and candidate profile)
- **Self-Employment Support:** 1–3 months for license/permit processing

## Benefits

- **Job Placement:** Direct employment in government or private sector
- **Skill Training:** Free training programs at government ITIs and skill centers
- **Self-Employment Loans:** Subsidized loans through Delhi Financial Corporation
- **Stipend During Training:** Monthly stipend during the training period
- **Job Fairs:** Priority invitation to Rozgar Melas organized by the government

## Important Tips

1. **Employment Exchange Registration:** Register at the local Employment Exchange first — this is often a prerequisite for the scheme.
2. **Skill Up:** Candidates with ITI certification, computer skills, or driving licenses have much better placement chances.
3. **Attend Rozgar Melas:** Delhi government organizes regular job fairs — attend these even before your application is processed.
4. **Update Profile:** Keep your employment portal profile updated with any new skills or qualifications.
5. **Explore Self-Employment:** If job placement takes time, explore self-employment options like auto permits or vendor licenses — the government provides active support.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Is this scheme only for unemployed youth?
**A:** The primary target is families where no member has organized sector employment. The applicant should be currently unemployed or underemployed.

### Q2: What if I already have a temporary/daily wage job?
**A:** You can still apply. The scheme aims to provide stable, organized employment. Daily wage and temporary workers are eligible.

### Q3: Do I get a government job guaranteed?
**A:** The scheme does not guarantee a permanent government job. It provides a mix of contractual government positions, private sector placements, skill training, and self-employment support.

### Q4: Can multiple family members apply?
**A:** The scheme targets one member per family. However, additional family members can register at the Employment Exchange for general job assistance.

### Q5: Is there an age relaxation for SC/ST/OBC candidates?
**A:** Age relaxation may be applicable as per Delhi government norms — typically 5 years for SC/ST candidates.

### Q6: What kind of self-employment support is available?
**A:** The scheme can help with auto/taxi permits, street vendor licenses, shop allotments in government markets, and subsidized loans through Delhi Financial Corporation.

### Q7: How is this different from regular Employment Exchange registration?
**A:** Regular Employment Exchange registration puts you in a general pool. This scheme provides proactive placement support, skill training, and self-employment assistance with a focus on one job per family.
