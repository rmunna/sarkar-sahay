# Canara Bank KYC Update Online 2026 — Without Branch Visit

Canara Bank (merged with Syndicate Bank in 2020) requires periodic KYC updates per RBI mandate. Canara Bank customers can now update KYC online via the Canara ai1 app or internet banking — without visiting a branch.

---

## Why Canara Bank Is Asking for KYC Update

| Reason | Canara Bank Action |
|--------|------------------|
| RBI periodic re-KYC | Debit restrictions after deadline |
| Post-Syndicate Bank merger re-KYC | KYC re-verification for migrated accounts |
| Address / name change | Records update required |
| Dormancy → reactivation | KYC mandatory |

---

## Method 1: Canara ai1 App (Recommended)

1. Open **Canara ai1** app → Login with MPIN
2. Go to **"Profile"** → **"Update KYC"** or **"Services"** → **"KYC Services"**
3. Upload:
   - Aadhaar (front + back photos taken in-app)
   - PAN card
   - Live selfie (in-app camera)
4. Accept self-declaration
5. Submit → Note reference number
6. **Processing: 24–72 hours**

### Aadhaar OTP e-KYC (Fastest):
1. Canara ai1 → KYC → **"Aadhaar-based e-KYC"**
2. Enter Aadhaar number
3. OTP on Aadhaar-registered mobile
4. UIDAI verifies digitally → **Instant KYC update**

---

## Method 2: Canara Bank Net Banking

1. Login at Canara Bank internet banking
2. **"Customer Services"** → **"KYC Update"**
3. Upload: Aadhaar (front + back, self-attested) + PAN
4. Submit
5. **Processing: 3–5 working days**

---

## Method 3: Canara Bank Video KYC

For customers with good camera/internet:
1. Canara ai1 → KYC → **"Video KYC"** (if available)
2. Canara executive joins within minutes (working hours 9 AM–6 PM)
3. Show original Aadhaar + PAN live
4. Answer basic verification questions
5. KYC confirmed on call → SMS within 2 hours

---

## Method 4: Canara Bank Branch

1. Visit any Canara Bank branch
2. Carry: Aadhaar (original + copy) + PAN + photo
3. Fill **"Re-KYC Form"** at service counter
4. Same-day processing

**Canara Bank Customer Care:** **1800-425-0018** (toll-free)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Canara Bank KYC update — what is the deadline?**
Canara Bank gives 30–90 days notice. After deadline, debits are blocked. Incoming credits (salary) continue. Complete KYC immediately upon receiving notice.

**Canara ai1 app not showing KYC update option — why?**
Some older or specialized accounts may not have the KYC option in Canara ai1. Try net banking. If not there either: Branch visit required with Aadhaar + PAN.

**My Canara Bank name is spelled differently from Aadhaar — KYC rejected?**
Yes — name mismatch causes rejection. Solution: (1) Update Aadhaar name to match bank records, or (2) Visit branch to correct name in bank records. Either way, name must match before online KYC succeeds.

**KYC update done but Canara account still showing restricted for 2 days?**
Allow up to 72 hours (3 working days) for the KYC update to propagate and lift restrictions. Call 1800-425-0018 if restriction persists after 3 days with your reference number.

**Canara Bank KYC for NRI account (NRE/NRO)?**
NRI KYC requires: Passport copy (attested) + Overseas address proof. Contact Canara NRI desk at your branch or email nriservices@canarabank.com. Standard Canara ai1 may not support NRI KYC update.
