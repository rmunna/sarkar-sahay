## Can NRIs Get Aadhaar?

Yes. Since the **Aadhaar and Other Laws (Amendment) Act, 2019**, NRIs (Non-Resident Indians) holding an **Indian passport** can apply for Aadhaar **without the 182-day waiting period** that was previously required.

Earlier, only residents who had lived in India for 182 days or more in the preceding 12 months were eligible. This restriction has been **removed for NRIs** with a valid Indian passport.

### Who Is Eligible?

| Category | Eligible? | Requirement |
|----------|-----------|-------------|
| NRI with Indian passport | ✅ Yes | Valid Indian passport |
| OCI (Overseas Citizen of India) | ❌ No | Not eligible (foreign nationals) |
| PIO (Person of Indian Origin) | ❌ No | Not eligible (foreign nationals) |
| Indian citizen returned permanently | ✅ Yes | Any valid ID proof |
| NRI without Indian passport | ❌ No | Must have valid Indian passport |

## Documents Required for NRI Aadhaar

### Mandatory Documents:

1. **Valid Indian Passport** — This is the primary and mandatory document for NRI Aadhaar enrolment
2. **Proof of Address in India** — Any one of:
   - Indian address in passport
   - Utility bill (electricity, water, gas) of Indian residence
   - Bank statement with Indian address
   - Property tax receipt
   - Rent agreement

### Additional Documents (Helpful):

- Indian mobile number (for OTP verification)
- Email address
- Photograph (taken at the centre)

**Note:** Biometric data (fingerprints and iris scan) will be captured at the enrolment centre.

## How to Apply for Aadhaar as an NRI

### Step 1: Book an Appointment

1. Visit the [UIDAI Appointment Portal](https://appointments.uidai.gov.in/)
2. Select your **state and city** in India where you'll visit
3. Choose **"New Aadhaar Enrolment"**
4. Select a convenient date and time slot
5. Note the appointment confirmation number

**Tip:** Book your appointment before travelling to India to ensure availability.

### Step 2: Visit the Aadhaar Enrolment Centre

On your appointment date:

1. Reach the centre with your **original Indian passport** and address proof
2. Fill the **Aadhaar Enrolment Form** (available at the centre)
3. The operator will:
   - Verify your passport and documents
   - Enter your demographic details (name, DOB, address, gender)
   - Capture your **photograph**
   - Capture **biometrics** (10 fingerprints + iris scan)
4. Verify all details on screen before confirming
5. Collect the **Aadhaar Enrolment Acknowledgement Slip**

### Step 3: Track Your Aadhaar Status

1. Visit [myAadhaar portal](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/)
2. Click **"Check Enrolment & Update Status"**
3. Enter your **14-digit Enrolment ID (EID)** from the acknowledgement slip
4. Enter the date and time of enrolment

Aadhaar generation typically takes **15–90 days** after enrolment.

### Step 4: Download e-Aadhaar

Once generated:
1. Visit [myAadhaar portal](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/)
2. Click **"Download Aadhaar"**
3. Enter your Aadhaar number or EID
4. Verify via OTP sent to your registered mobile
5. Download the password-protected PDF

## How to Update Aadhaar as an NRI

If you already have Aadhaar and need to update details:

### Online Update (from anywhere):

1. Visit [myAadhaar portal](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/)
2. Log in with your Aadhaar number and OTP
3. Select **"Update Aadhaar"**
4. Update demographic details: name, address, DOB, gender, mobile, email
5. Upload supporting documents
6. Pay ₹50 (for demographic updates)
7. Track update status using the Update Request Number (URN)

**Note:** Address update online requires an Indian address proof document.

### Offline Update (at Aadhaar Centre):

For biometric updates or if online update isn't possible:
1. Book appointment on [appointments.uidai.gov.in](https://appointments.uidai.gov.in/)
2. Visit the centre with passport and documents
3. Request the specific update needed
4. Biometrics may be re-captured
5. Pay ₹50 and collect acknowledgement

See our [Aadhaar address change guide](/guide/aadhaar-address-change-online) for detailed address update steps.

Even while abroad, NRIs can use several Aadhaar services online:

## Important Tips

1. **Get an Indian SIM before applying** — You'll need an Indian mobile number for OTP-based Aadhaar services
2. **Book appointment in advance** — Aadhaar centres in metros can be very busy; book before your India trip
3. **Carry original passport** — Photocopies are not accepted for NRI enrolment
4. **Keep EID slip safe** — You'll need the Enrolment ID to track and download your Aadhaar
5. **Update mobile number** — Ensure your Indian mobile number stays active for future Aadhaar services

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Is the 183-day residency requirement still applicable for NRIs?

No. The 2019 amendment removed the 182-day residency requirement for NRIs. You can apply on your first day in India with a valid Indian passport.

### Q2. Can I apply for Aadhaar at an Indian embassy abroad?

No. Aadhaar enrolment is only available at **Aadhaar Enrolment Centres within India**. You must visit India to apply.

### Q3. Can OCI cardholders get Aadhaar?

No. OCI holders are not Indian citizens and are not eligible for Aadhaar. Only Indian passport holders can apply.

### Q4. How long does it take to get Aadhaar after enrolment?

Typically **15 to 90 days**. You can check status online using your Enrolment ID. e-Aadhaar is available for download once generated.

### Q5. Can I use my foreign address for Aadhaar?

No. Aadhaar requires an **Indian address**. You must provide an address in India (can be a family member's address where you stay during visits).

### Q6. Do I need Aadhaar for my NRE/NRO bank account?

While Aadhaar is commonly used for KYC, NRIs can use their **passport** as the primary KYC document for NRE/NRO accounts. However, having Aadhaar can simplify many banking processes.

### Q7. Can I link my NRI Aadhaar to PAN?

Yes. PAN-Aadhaar linking is mandatory for all PAN holders, including NRIs. You can do this online via the [Income Tax e-filing portal](https://www.incometax.gov.in/).
