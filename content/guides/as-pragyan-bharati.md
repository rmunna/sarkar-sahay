# Pragyan Bharati Scheme Assam – Complete Guide 2026

## What is the Pragyan Bharati Scheme?

The **Pragyan Bharati Scheme** is an education initiative by the Government of Assam that provides free education support to students from economically weaker sections. Under this scheme, the state government provides financial assistance for admission fees, tuition fees, and other educational expenses for students enrolled in government and provincialised educational institutions across Assam.

The scheme covers students from **Class 1 to post-graduation level**, making education accessible and affordable for families that cannot bear the cost of higher education.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Residency:** Must be a permanent resident of Assam
- **Enrollment:** Must be enrolled in a government, provincialised, or government-aided educational institution in Assam
- **Income:** Annual family income should not exceed ₹2 lakh (for higher education benefits)
- **Academic:** Must have passed the previous qualifying examination
- **Category:** Students from all categories (General, SC, ST, OBC, Minority) are eligible

### Coverage by Education Level

| Level | Benefit |
|-------|---------|
| Class 1-10 | Free admission, tuition, and textbooks in government schools |
| Class 11-12 | Waiver of admission and tuition fees |
| Graduation | Financial assistance for fees in provincial colleges |
| Post-Graduation | Financial assistance for eligible PG courses |

## Documents Required

### Mandatory Documents
- Aadhaar Card of the student
- Previous class marksheet / pass certificate
- Admission receipt from the institution
- Bank account details (student's or parent's, Aadhaar-linked)
- Income certificate (issued by Circle Officer)
- Passport-size photographs (2)

### Supporting Documents
- Caste certificate (if applicable)
- Domicile / residential certificate
- BPL card (if available)
- Institution bonafide certificate

## How to Apply – Step-by-Step

### Through Educational Institution (Primary Method)

1. **Take admission** in a government / provincialised institution in Assam
2. **Inform the institution** that you wish to avail the Pragyan Bharati benefit
3. **Submit required documents** to the institution's office
4. The institution verifies your eligibility and forwards the application to the **Directorate of Education**
5. Upon approval, **fees are waived** or reimbursed directly

### Online Application (When Available)

1. Visit the Assam Education Department portal at [education.assam.gov.in](https://education.assam.gov.in)
2. Look for the Pragyan Bharati section or scholarship portal
3. Register with your mobile number and email
4. Fill in personal, educational, and family income details
5. Upload scanned documents
6. Submit and save the application reference number

## Processing Time

- **Verification:** 2-4 weeks by the institution
- **Approval:** 1-2 months by the directorate
- **Fee waiver/reimbursement:** Typically within the same academic session

## Important Tips

1. **Apply early** — submit documents immediately after admission to avoid delays
2. **Maintain attendance** — irregular students may lose benefits
3. **Keep all original documents** safe for verification
4. **Check with your institution** for institution-specific requirements
5. **Renewal is required** each academic year — resubmit documents annually

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Does Pragyan Bharati cover private colleges?
**A:** No, the scheme primarily covers **government, provincialised, and government-aided institutions** in Assam. Private institutions are generally not covered.

### Q2: Can students from other states studying in Assam avail this?
**A:** No. The scheme is exclusively for **permanent residents of Assam** studying in Assam.

### Q3: Is there any entrance exam for this scheme?
**A:** No entrance exam is required. Eligibility is based on admission to an eligible institution and meeting income criteria.

### Q4: What if I fail in a year?
**A:** Benefits may be discontinued if you fail. You must pass the previous qualifying exam to continue receiving benefits.

### Q5: Can I avail Pragyan Bharati along with other scholarships?
**A:** This depends on the specific scholarship rules. Some central scholarships may allow concurrent benefits, while others may not. Check with your institution.

### Q6: How do I check my application status?
**A:** Contact the **office of your educational institution** or the **District Education Office** for status updates.
