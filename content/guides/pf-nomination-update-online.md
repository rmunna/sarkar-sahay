# EPF Nomination Update Online: How to Add or Change Nominee in EPFO

Updating your EPF (Employee Provident Fund) nomination is essential to ensure that your provident fund, pension, and insurance benefits reach the right family members in case of an emergency. EPFO has made the e-Nomination process completely online, allowing you to add or change nominees from the comfort of your home.

## What is EPF Nomination (e-Nomination)?

EPF nomination (called **e-Nomination** in the online process) is the declaration of family members who will receive your:

- **EPF balance** — Employees' Provident Fund accumulation
- **EPS benefits** — Employees' Pension Scheme benefits
- **EDLI benefits** — Employees' Deposit Linked Insurance (up to ₹7 lakh life insurance)

Without a valid nomination, your family may face delays and legal complications in claiming these benefits after your demise.

## Why Update Your EPF Nomination?

- **Marriage** — add spouse as nominee
- **Birth of child** — add children
- **Death of existing nominee** — update records
- **Divorce** — remove former spouse
- **Initial setup** — many employees skip nomination at joining; it's mandatory now
- **EPFO mandate** — EPFO has made e-Nomination mandatory and periodically reminds members to complete it

## How to Update EPF Nomination Online (Step-by-Step)

### Prerequisites

- Active UAN (Universal Account Number)
- UAN linked to Aadhaar (mandatory for e-Nomination)
- Active mobile number linked to UAN
- Aadhaar-linked bank account

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Visit** the EPFO Member Portal: [unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in/memberinterface/)
2. **Login** with your UAN and password
3. Go to **"Online Services"** → **"E-Nomination"**
4. Click on **"Provide Details"** under the Family Declaration section

#### Step 4a: Add Family Details

5. Click **"Add Family Details"**
6. Enter details for each family member:
   - Name
   - Relationship (spouse, son, daughter, father, mother)
   - Date of birth
   - Gender
   - Aadhaar number (optional but recommended)
   - Address
   - Whether the member is financially dependent on you
   - Bank account details of the nominee
7. Upload a **photograph** of each nominee (if required)
8. Click **"Save"** after adding each family member
9. Add all family members you want to nominate

#### Step 4b: Set Nomination Shares

10. Click on **"Nomination Details"**
11. Select the family members you want to nominate for EPF/EPS/EDLI
12. Set the **share percentage** for each nominee (must total 100%)
13. If you have minor children as nominees, designate a **guardian**
14. Click **"Save EPF Nomination"**

#### Step 4c: Aadhaar e-Sign

15. Click **"E-Sign"** to digitally sign the nomination
16. Enter your Aadhaar number
17. An OTP is sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile number
18. Enter the OTP to complete the e-Signature
19. **Done!** Your e-Nomination is now registered

## Checking Your Current Nomination Status

1. Login to the EPFO Member Portal
2. Go to **"Online Services"** → **"E-Nomination"**
3. The page shows your current family declaration and nomination details
4. Status shows as **"E-Nomination Registered"** if completed
5. If it shows **"Pending"** or no nomination exists, complete it immediately

## Changing or Updating an Existing Nomination

If you've already filed an e-Nomination and need to change it:

1. Login to the EPFO Member Portal
2. Go to **"Online Services"** → **"E-Nomination"**
3. Click on **"Update"** next to the family member details
4. Modify the details — add new members, remove old ones, change share percentages
5. Re-sign using Aadhaar e-Sign (OTP)
6. The new nomination **replaces** the previous one

## Important Rules for EPF Nomination

| Rule | Details |
|------|---------|
| Who can be nominated | Only family members — spouse, children, parents |
| Non-family nominees | Allowed only if you have no family members |
| Share allocation | Total must equal 100% for EPF and EDLI |
| Minor nominees | Must designate a guardian |
| Multiple nominees | You can add multiple nominees with different share percentages |
| Aadhaar mandatory | Aadhaar must be linked to UAN for e-Sign |

## EPF Nomination for Unmarried Members

If you are unmarried:
- Nominate your parents or siblings
- You can nominate **non-family members** only if you have no surviving family
- After marriage, **update the nomination** to include your spouse — previous nomination of non-family members becomes automatically invalid

## What Happens Without Nomination?

If no nomination is registered:
- EPF accumulation is distributed as per **succession laws** (Hindu Succession Act, Indian Succession Act, etc.)
- Family members need to obtain a **legal heir certificate** or **succession certificate** from the court
- The claim process becomes lengthy (6 months to 2+ years)
- EDLI insurance benefit may also be delayed

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Is e-Nomination mandatory for EPF members?

Yes. EPFO has made e-Nomination mandatory for all members. Without it, claim settlement for your family may be delayed.

### 2. Can I nominate someone who is not a family member?

Only if you have **no family members** (no spouse, children, or parents). In such cases, you can nominate anyone. Once you acquire a family (marriage, children), the non-family nomination becomes void.

### 3. How many nominees can I add?

There is no fixed limit, but nominees must be family members. You can add your spouse, all children, and parents. The EPF share must total 100%.

### 4. Can I update nomination without Aadhaar?

No. Aadhaar is mandatory for e-Nomination as the e-Sign process requires Aadhaar OTP verification. Ensure your UAN is linked to Aadhaar first.

### 5. Will my employer see my nomination details?

No. The e-Nomination is filed directly by the member on the EPFO portal. The employer does not have access to view or approve your nomination details.

### 6. What is the difference between family declaration and nomination?

Family declaration lists all your family members. Nomination specifies **which family members** receive your EPF/EPS/EDLI benefits and in what proportion.
