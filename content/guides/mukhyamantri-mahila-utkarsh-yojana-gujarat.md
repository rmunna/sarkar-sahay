## What is Mukhyamantri Mahila Utkarsh Yojana (MMUY)?

**Mukhyamantri Mahila Utkarsh Yojana (MMUY)** is a Gujarat government scheme that provides **interest-free loans up to ₹1 lakh** to women's Self Help Groups (SHGs / Sakhi Mandals) for self-employment and micro-enterprise activities. The scheme aims to promote women's economic empowerment by eliminating the interest burden on small business loans.

Under MMUY, the Gujarat government pays the interest subsidy (over and above 7% rate of interest) directly to the banks, making the loan **effectively interest-free** for the women borrowers. The scheme covers both **urban and rural women** across the state.

The scheme has two main components:
1. **Individual loans** for women entrepreneurs (through SHG linkage)
2. **Group loans** for Sakhi Mandals / Self Help Groups

## Who is Eligible?

**For individual women (through SHG):**
- Must be a **resident of Gujarat**
- Must be a member of a registered **Sakhi Mandal / SHG**
- Age: 18 years and above
- Must have a bank account linked with Aadhaar

**For Sakhi Mandal (SHG):**
- Must be a registered SHG under Gujarat Livelihood Promotion Company (GLPC) or NULM/NRLM
- Must have been active for at least **6 months**
- Must have regular savings and meeting records
- Must have a group bank account
- Group should have **10 members** (urban) or **10-15 members** (rural)

## Loan Details

| Component | Loan Amount | Interest Rate | Repayment Period |
|-----------|-------------|---------------|------------------|
| SHG Group Loan | Up to ₹1,00,000 per group | 0% (interest-free) | 3 years |
| Individual through SHG | Up to ₹1,00,000 per woman | 0% (interest-free) | 3 years |

**How interest-free works:**
- Banks charge normal interest rate (7-8%)
- Gujarat government reimburses the interest amount directly to the bank
- Borrower only repays the **principal amount** in EMIs

## Documents Required

**For the Sakhi Mandal:**
- SHG registration certificate
- Group resolution for loan application
- Minutes of last 6 months of meetings
- Group savings passbook
- List of all group members with Aadhaar details

**For individual members:**
- Aadhaar Card (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- PAN Card (if available)
- Bank account passbook (Aadhaar-linked)
- Passport-size photographs
- Address proof / Domicile of Gujarat
- Business plan / Purpose of loan

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Online Application

1. **Visit MMUY portal:** Go to [mmuy.gujarat.gov.in](https://mmuy.gujarat.gov.in)
2. **Register Sakhi Mandal:** If your SHG is not already registered, register it through the portal
3. **Login:** Use your SHG registration number and password to log in
4. **Apply for loan:** Click on "Apply for Loan" and fill in group/member details
5. **Upload documents:** Upload scanned copies of SHG registration, member Aadhaar cards, and business plan
6. **Select bank branch:** Choose the bank branch where the group has its account
7. **Submit application:** Review and submit the application
8. **Bank verification:** The bank will verify your application and SHG credentials
9. **Loan disbursement:** After approval, the loan amount is credited to the SHG bank account

### Through Anganwadi / GLPC / Sakhi

1. Contact your nearest **Anganwadi Centre** or **GLPC Sakhi** (community resource person)
2. The Sakhi will help your SHG prepare and submit the application
3. This is the recommended route for SHGs in rural areas

## Processing Time

| Stage | Timeline |
|-------|----------|
| Application submission to verification | 15-30 days |
| Bank processing | 15-30 days |
| Loan disbursement | Within 60 days of application |

## Fees

There is **no application fee** or processing charge for this scheme. The loan is interest-free, so the total repayable amount equals the principal borrowed.

## Repayment

- Repayment is in **monthly installments** over 3 years (36 months)
- For a ₹1 lakh loan: approximately **₹2,778 per month**
- Repayment is from the SHG group account
- No penalty for early repayment
- If the group defaults, the interest subsidy is stopped and normal bank interest applies

## Important Tips

1. **Form or join a Sakhi Mandal first** — Individual women cannot apply directly. You must be part of a registered SHG.
2. **Maintain regular meetings and savings** — Banks check SHG records before approving the loan. At least 6 months of regular activity is required.
3. **Use the loan productively** — The loan is meant for self-employment activities like tailoring, food processing, handicrafts, dairy farming, etc.
4. **Repay on time** — Timely repayment makes the group eligible for higher loan amounts in the future.
5. **Combine with skill training** — Gujarat government offers free skill development programs for SHG members. Attend these to improve your business skills.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. What is the maximum loan amount under MMUY?

The maximum loan amount is **₹1,00,000** per Sakhi Mandal group or per individual member (through the SHG).

### Q2. Do I have to pay any interest?

No, the loan is **completely interest-free** for the borrower. The Gujarat government pays the interest subsidy to the bank on your behalf.

### Q3. Can an individual woman apply without being part of an SHG?

No, you must be a **member of a registered Sakhi Mandal / SHG** to apply under MMUY. Consider forming a new group with 10 women in your area.

### Q4. What can the loan be used for?

The loan can be used for income-generating activities such as: tailoring, beauty parlour, food processing, dairy, poultry, retail shop, handicrafts, or any other micro-enterprise.

### Q5. What happens if we don't repay the loan?

If the SHG defaults on repayment, the **interest subsidy will be stopped** and normal bank interest will be charged. The group will also become ineligible for future government scheme benefits.

### Q6. Can urban women also apply?

Yes, the scheme covers **both rural and urban women** in Gujarat. Urban SHGs registered under NULM and rural SHGs under NRLM are both eligible.

### Q7. How do I form a new Sakhi Mandal?

Contact your nearest Anganwadi worker, GLPC district office, or Block Development Officer. They will guide you through the SHG registration process, which typically takes 1-2 months.
