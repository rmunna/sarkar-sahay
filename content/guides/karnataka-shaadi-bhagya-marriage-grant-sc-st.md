## What is Karnataka Shaadi Bhagya?

**Shaadi Bhagya** is Karnataka government's marriage grant scheme that provides financial assistance at the time of marriage for girls from SC/ST and OBC communities. The scheme gives:

- **₹75,000** for brides from SC/ST communities
- **₹50,000** for brides from OBC/EBC communities

The grant is given in **two installments** (50% before marriage, 50% after).

---

## Key Benefits

| Community | Grant Amount |
|-----------|-------------|
| SC/ST brides | ₹75,000 |
| OBC / Category 1 / 2A / 2B / 3A / 3B brides | ₹50,000 |
| Minority communities (separate scheme) | Check Minority Welfare Dept |

---

## Who is Eligible?

**The bride is eligible if:**
- She belongs to **SC/ST** or **OBC (2A/2B/3A/3B/Category 1)** communities in Karnataka
- She is a **permanent resident of Karnataka** (minimum 15 years)
- She is **18 years or older** on marriage date
- It is her **first marriage**
- Annual family income does not exceed **₹2 lakh** (SC/ST) / **₹1.5 lakh** (OBC) — check current limit at sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in

---

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar Card** — bride's
2. **Caste certificate** — SC/ST/OBC from tahsildar
3. **Age proof** — Aadhaar, SSLC certificate
4. **Income certificate** — from tahsildar/revenue officer
5. **Marriage invitation card** (for pre-wedding application)
6. **Marriage certificate** (for post-wedding application)
7. **Bank passbook** — bride's Aadhaar-linked account
8. **Residence proof** — ration card or voter ID
9. **Photograph** — bride's

## How to Apply — Step by Step

### Step 1 — Apply on Seva Sindhu

Go to **[sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in)**:
1. Search for **"Shaadi Bhagya"**
2. Register / login with Aadhaar
3. Fill the application form
4. Upload all documents
5. Submit

### Step 2 — Apply at Nadakacheri / Atalji Janasnehi Kendra

Alternatively, visit any **Nadakacheri** (local revenue office) or **Atalji Janasnehi Kendra** for assisted application.

### Step 3 — Verification

Revenue officer verifies caste, income, age, and residency.

### Step 4 — First Installment (Pre-Wedding)

After approval: **50% of the grant** is released before the wedding.

### Step 5 — Second Installment (Post-Wedding)

After submitting the **marriage certificate** post-wedding: remaining **50%** is released.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. My marriage is next week. Is there time to apply?**

Applications must ideally be submitted at least 30 days before marriage for the pre-wedding installment. If your marriage is very soon, apply immediately and also apply post-wedding for the second installment.

**2. I belong to 2B OBC community. How much do I get?**

OBC communities (all sub-categories 1, 2A, 2B, 3A, 3B) are eligible for ₹50,000. The amount is the same for all OBC sub-categories.

**3. Can both the bride and groom be from OBC and apply separately?**

The scheme is for the **bride's family** — the grant is for the bride (paid to her account). The groom's community doesn't matter for this application.

**4. The scheme says income limit ₹1.5 lakh but our income is ₹2 lakh. Can we still apply?**

Income limits are updated periodically. Confirm the current limit at sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in. If you marginally exceed the limit, the application may still be processed at the officer's discretion — try applying.

**5. We applied online but the status shows "pending for long." What to do?**

Visit the nearest **Nadakacheri** or **Deputy Director, Social Welfare** office with your application number. Pending applications are often delayed due to high volume — follow up personally.
