# Land Mutation Online Apply — Process After Property Purchase

Land mutation (also called "dakhil kharij" or "name transfer") is the process of updating land revenue records to reflect the new owner's name after a property purchase, inheritance, or gift. Without mutation, the previous owner's name continues in government records, causing problems with property tax, loans, and future sales. This guide explains how to apply for mutation online across Indian states.

## What is Land Mutation?

Land mutation is the **transfer of title in revenue records** (Record of Rights / Khata / Patta) from the previous owner to the new owner. It is done at:
- **Municipal corporation/council** — for urban properties
- **Tehsildar/Revenue office** — for agricultural/rural land

> **Important:** Mutation does not create ownership. It only updates revenue records to reflect ownership established by the registered sale deed.

## When is Mutation Required?

- After **property purchase** (registered sale deed)
- After **inheritance** (death of property owner)
- After **gift deed** registration
- After **partition** of joint family property
- After receiving property through **court decree/order**

## Documents Required for Land Mutation

| Document | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Registered Sale Deed / Gift Deed / Will | Proof of ownership transfer |
| Death Certificate | For mutation due to inheritance |
| Succession Certificate / Legal Heir Certificate | If multiple heirs claim property |
| Previous owner's Khata / Patta / RoR | Existing land record |
| Encumbrance Certificate | Proving no pending mortgages |
| Property Tax Receipts | Latest tax paid by previous owner |
| Aadhaar Card | Identity proof of new owner |
| Photograph | Passport-size photo of applicant |
| Application Form | Available online or at tehsil office |
| NOC from Co-owners | If property has multiple owners |

### Step 2: Register/Login on Portal
1. Create an account with your mobile number and Aadhaar
2. Log in to the portal

### Step 3: Select Mutation Application
1. Navigate to **"Apply for Mutation"** or **"Dakhil Kharij"** or equivalent
2. Select the type of mutation:
   - Sale/Purchase
   - Inheritance/Succession
   - Gift
   - Partition
   - Court Order

### Step 4: Enter Property Details
1. Select **District → Tehsil → Village/Ward**
2. Enter **Khasra/Survey Number** or **Khata Number**
3. The current owner's details will be auto-fetched from records
4. Enter the **new owner's details** (name, father's name, address, Aadhaar)

### Step 5: Upload Documents
Upload scanned copies of:
- Registered sale deed / gift deed / death certificate
- Previous land records (RoR / Khata)
- Aadhaar card
- Encumbrance Certificate
- Application form (if required separately)

### Step 6: Pay Mutation Fee
- Fees vary by state: typically ₹25–500
- Pay online via net banking, UPI, or debit card
- Download the payment receipt

### Step 7: Submit Application
1. Review all details
2. Submit the application
3. Note down the **application/reference number**
4. You'll receive SMS confirmation

### Step 8: Field Verification
- The Tehsildar/Revenue Inspector may conduct a field inspection
- They verify the property boundaries and ownership documents
- Objections from other parties (if any) are heard during this stage
- A public notice may be issued inviting objections (typically 15-30 days)

### Step 9: Mutation Order
- After verification and no objections, the Tehsildar passes a mutation order
- Updated land records (RoR/Khata) will reflect the new owner's name
- Download the updated records from the portal

## Offline Process (If Online Not Available)

1. Visit the **Tehsil office / Revenue office / Municipal office**
2. Collect the **mutation application form**
3. Fill the form and attach required documents
4. Submit at the designated counter
5. Pay the mutation fee
6. Collect the receipt with reference number
7. Attend the hearing date if scheduled
8. Collect the mutation order after approval

## How to Check Mutation Status Online

1. Visit your state's land records portal
2. Go to **"Check Mutation Status"** or **"Application Status"**
3. Enter your reference/application number
4. The current status will be displayed (pending/approved/rejected)

## Common Issues and Fixes

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Mutation pending for months | Follow up with Tehsildar office. File RTI if needed. |
| Objection raised by third party | Attend the hearing with all original documents and lawyer if needed |
| Wrong survey/khasra number | Verify from existing land records before applying |
| Mutation rejected | Check reason, correct documents, and reapply |
| Online portal not working | Try during off-peak hours or apply offline at tehsil office |

## Important Tips

1. **Apply within 3-6 months** of property purchase — delays complicate the process
2. **Mutation is different from registration** — registration transfers ownership; mutation updates revenue records
3. **Keep all original documents** safe — they're needed for verification
4. **Check for encumbrances** before buying — get an EC from the Sub-Registrar office
5. **Update property tax** in your name after mutation is completed

## FAQs

### 1. Is mutation mandatory after buying property?
While not legally mandatory, it is practically essential. Without mutation, you'll face problems with property tax, bank loans, and selling the property in the future.

### 2. How long does land mutation take?
Typically 15-90 days depending on the state and whether objections are raised. Some states like Telangana (Dharani portal) process mutations within 15 days.

### 3. What is the difference between registration and mutation?
Registration (at Sub-Registrar) legally transfers ownership. Mutation (at Revenue/Municipal office) updates land revenue records to reflect the new owner. Both are needed.

### 4. Can mutation be done without a registered sale deed?
No, for sale/purchase mutations, a registered sale deed is mandatory. For inheritance, death certificate and legal heir certificate are required.

### 5. What is the cost of land mutation?
Mutation fees are minimal — typically ₹25-500 depending on the state. Some states like Telangana offer free online mutation.

### 6. Can I check my mutation status online?
Yes, most states offer online tracking. Visit your state's land records portal and enter your application reference number.

### 7. What if someone objects to my mutation?
The Tehsildar will conduct a hearing where both parties present their case. If your documents are genuine and complete, the mutation will be approved. You may need a lawyer for contested cases.
