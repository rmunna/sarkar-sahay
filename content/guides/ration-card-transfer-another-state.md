# Ration Card Transfer to Another State — ONORC Process

If you are moving to another state for work, family, or any other reason, you need to ensure you can still access your ration entitlements. Thanks to the [One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC)](/guide/one-nation-one-ration-card-onorc) scheme, you no longer need to formally transfer your ration card in most cases — you can simply collect rations from any Fair Price Shop (FPS) in India. However, if you are permanently relocating, a formal transfer or new card application may be better. This guide covers both options.

## Option 1: Use ONORC (No Transfer Needed)

The easiest option for migrant workers and families moving temporarily is to use the ONORC scheme. Here's how it works:

### How ONORC Works

- Your existing [ration card](/guide/ration-card-apply-online) from your home state remains valid
- You can buy rations from **any Fair Price Shop in any state**
- Authentication is done using your **Aadhaar-linked biometric** (fingerprint or iris)
- The ePoS (electronic Point of Sale) machine at the FPS connects to a central database and verifies your entitlement
- Whatever you collect in the new state is deducted from your monthly quota

### Requirements for ONORC

1. Your ration card must be **linked to Aadhaar** — [apply for Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) if needed
2. All family members should have Aadhaar seeded in the ration card
3. Your ration card must be active under NFSA
4. The FPS must have a working ePoS machine

### Steps to Use ONORC

1. Locate a nearby Fair Price Shop in your new city/state using the **Mera Ration** app
2. Visit the FPS on distribution day
3. Provide your ration card number and Aadhaar number
4. Complete biometric verification on the ePoS machine
5. Collect your entitled rations
6. Get a receipt

**Note:** Family members split across states can collect their individual share separately. For example, if 3 members are in one state and 2 in another, each group can collect rations locally.

## Option 2: Formal Ration Card Transfer (Permanent Move)

If you are permanently relocating to another state, you may want to surrender your old card and apply for a new one in the destination state.

### Step 1: Surrender Old Ration Card

1. Visit the Food & Civil Supplies office in your **home state** (the state where your current ration card was issued)
2. Submit a written application requesting cancellation/surrender of your ration card
3. Mention the reason: "permanently relocating to [new state]"
4. Provide your new address details
5. Obtain a **surrender certificate** or **No Objection Certificate (NOC)**

If you cannot visit in person, some states allow:
- Online surrender through the state PDS portal
- Application through a family member with authorisation letter

### Step 2: Apply for New Ration Card in New State

1. Visit the Food & Civil Supplies office in your **new state**
2. Apply for a fresh [ration card](/guide/ration-card-apply-online) with required documents:
   - Surrender certificate/NOC from old state
   - Aadhaar cards of all family members
   - New address proof (rent agreement, utility bill)
   - Income certificate from new location
   - Passport-size photographs
3. A field verification will be conducted
4. New ration card issued within 15–30 working days

### Step 3: Link Aadhaar

Ensure your new ration card is linked with [Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) for all family members to enable smooth biometric authentication at FPS.

## Which Option Should You Choose?

| Scenario | Recommended Option |
|----------|-------------------|
| Temporary migration for work | ONORC (no transfer needed) |
| Seasonal migration | ONORC |
| Permanent relocation | Formal transfer (surrender + new card) |
| Student moving for education | ONORC |
| Marriage — moving to spouse's state | Formal transfer or [add name to spouse's card](/guide/ration-card-add-remove-name) |

## Common Issues and Solutions

### Aadhaar Not Linked

If your Aadhaar is not linked to your ration card, ONORC won't work. Visit your home state's Food & Civil Supplies office or use the state portal to link Aadhaar.

### ePoS Machine Not Working at FPS

If biometric authentication fails or the machine is down, try again later or visit another nearby FPS. Report non-functional machines on the state helpline.

### FPS Dealer Refusing ONORC

Some dealers may be unaware of or uncooperative with ONORC. Show them the government notification and file a complaint with the District Supply Officer if needed.

### Old State Not Issuing NOC

If your old state is delaying the surrender certificate, file a complaint on the state PDS portal or call the national helpline at **1967**.

Download the **Mera Ration** mobile app (available on Google Play) to:

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. Can I use my ration card in another state without transferring it?

Yes, through the [ONORC scheme](/guide/one-nation-one-ration-card-onorc), you can use your existing ration card to collect rations from any FPS in India using Aadhaar-based authentication.

### 2. Do I need to surrender my old ration card if I use ONORC?

No. ONORC allows you to keep your existing card and use it anywhere. Surrender is only needed if you want a permanent new card in the destination state.

### 3. Can different family members collect rations in different states?

Yes. Under ONORC, individual family members can collect their share of rations from different states. The system tracks per-person allocation.

### 4. How long does it take to get a new ration card in another state?

After submitting the application with all documents including the surrender certificate, a new ration card is typically issued within 15 to 30 working days.

### 5. Is ONORC available in all states?

Yes, ONORC is now operational in all 36 states and Union Territories of India.

### 6. What if I lose my ration card during the move?

Apply for a duplicate ration card in your home state or directly apply for a new card in your destination state with a police FIR and an affidavit about the lost card.

### 7. Can I transfer my AAY ration card to another state?

Yes, the process is the same. However, the new state will assess whether you qualify for AAY category based on their own criteria. You may be issued a PHH card instead if you don't meet the new state's AAY threshold.
