# NSP Fresh Scholarship Application Error? Fix It

Applying for a scholarship on the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) for the first time? Errors during registration and form filling are very common. This guide covers every major error in the fresh application process and how to fix it.

## NSP Fresh Application Process Overview

1. **Register** on [scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in/) with mobile number and email
2. **Verify Aadhaar** through OTP or biometric
3. **Fill the application form** — personal, academic, and bank details
4. **Upload documents** — income certificate, fee receipt, category certificate, etc.
5. **Final submit** → Institute verification → District verification → Sanction

## Common Fresh Application Errors and Fixes

### 1. Registration OTP Not Received

**Problem:** You register on NSP but the OTP does not arrive on your mobile number or email.

**Causes:**
- Network issues or DND (Do Not Disturb) activated on your mobile
- Incorrect mobile number entered
- NSP server overloaded near deadlines
- Email OTP landing in spam folder

**Fix:**
1. Wait at least 2–3 minutes; OTPs can be delayed during peak hours
2. Check if DND is active — call your telecom provider or dial the DND deactivation number
3. Check your email spam/junk folder for the email OTP
4. Click **"Resend OTP"** (available after 60 seconds)
5. If OTP still doesn't arrive, try registering during off-peak hours (early morning 6–8 AM)
6. Try using a different mobile number if permitted
7. Clear browser cache and try in an incognito window

### 2. Aadhaar Verification Failed

**Problem:** Aadhaar OTP verification fails, or the system says "Aadhaar details do not match."

**Causes:**
- Name mismatch between Aadhaar and application form
- Date of birth mismatch
- Aadhaar mobile number is different from the one registered on NSP
- UIDAI server is down

**Fix:**
1. Ensure your name on NSP matches your [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) **exactly** (including spelling, initials, and middle name)
2. Date of birth must match Aadhaar records precisely
3. The OTP for Aadhaar verification goes to the mobile number registered with UIDAI — not your NSP mobile number
4. If your Aadhaar mobile is outdated, update it at the nearest Aadhaar enrolment centre first
5. Try verification during non-peak hours when UIDAI servers are less loaded
6. If biometric option is available, visit a CSC (Common Service Centre) for biometric-based verification

### 3. Income Certificate Upload Error

**Problem:** The income certificate upload fails or is rejected during verification.

**Common Issues:**
- File size exceeds the limit (usually 200 KB max)
- Wrong format (NSP typically accepts PDF and JPG only)
- Certificate is not from an authorized authority
- Certificate has expired (older than 1 year)

**Fix:**
1. Compress the file to under 200 KB — use an online PDF compressor
2. Convert to PDF or JPG format if your file is in another format
3. Ensure the income certificate is issued by a **Tehsildar, SDM, or District Magistrate**
4. The certificate should be from the **current financial year** (not older than 12 months)
5. For EWS certificates, ensure it follows the format prescribed by the Government of India
6. If scanning, use 150 DPI in black and white to keep file size small

> See our [document upload guide](/guide/nsp-scholarship-documents-upload-failed-fix) for detailed file preparation tips.

### 4. Category Selection Error

**Problem:** You are unable to select your category (SC/ST/OBC/Minority/General) or the wrong category is pre-filled.

**Causes:**
- Category is auto-filled from Aadhaar data and may not match your actual category
- Some schemes are category-specific and your category may not be eligible

**Fix:**
1. If category is pre-filled incorrectly, check if there's an edit option in the form
2. For OBC applicants: ensure you have a valid **Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) certificate** — some scholarships require it
3. For Minority scholarships: select the correct minority community (Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain, Parsi)
4. If you cannot change the category after Aadhaar verification, contact NSP helpline at 0120-6619540
5. Select the scholarship scheme that matches your category — applying to the wrong scheme will result in rejection

### 5. Fee Receipt Upload Issues

**Problem:** The fee receipt upload fails or the fee details section shows errors.

**Fix:**
1. Upload a clear scan of your **original fee receipt** from the institute (not a photocopy)
2. The receipt must show: student name, course, academic year, amount paid, institute name
3. File size: under 200 KB, format: PDF or JPG
4. If your institute has not issued a receipt yet, ask for a **provisional fee receipt** or admission slip
5. Enter the fee amount exactly as shown on the receipt — mismatches cause verification failure
6. For fee-waiver students, upload the fee waiver letter from the institute

## Step-by-Step: Complete Fresh Application

1. Go to [scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in/) → Click **"New Registration"**
2. Select **"Academic Year"** and agree to terms
3. Enter your state of domicile, scholarship category, scheme name
4. Enter name, date of birth, mobile number, and email
5. Enter your [Aadhaar number](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) and verify via OTP
6. Create a password (must include uppercase, lowercase, number, special character)
7. Log in with your Application ID and password
8. Fill **Personal Details**: address, category, religion, income
9. Fill **Academic Details**: 10th/12th marks, current course, institute (with AISHE code)
10. Fill **Bank Details**: account number, IFSC code (must be in student's name)
11. Upload documents: photo, income certificate, fee receipt, category certificate
12. **Save as Draft** → Review all fields → **Final Submit**

## Documents Required for Fresh Application

- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
- 10th and 12th mark sheets
- Current year fee receipt
- Income certificate (from Tehsildar/SDM)
- Caste/category certificate (for SC/ST/OBC)
- Non-Creamy Layer certificate (for OBC, if applicable)
- Bank passbook front page (student's own account)
- Passport-size photograph (JPG, under 50 KB)
- Domicile/residence certificate

## Important Tips

1. **Register early** — don't wait for the deadline; server crashes are common in the last week
2. **Use the same name spelling** across all documents, Aadhaar, and the NSP form
3. **Keep Aadhaar mobile number active** — you'll need it for OTP verification
4. **Prepare all documents in digital format** before starting the application
5. **Note down your Application ID and password** — there is no easy recovery process

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. I forgot my NSP Application ID. How do I recover it?
Click "Forgot Application ID" on the login page. Enter your registered mobile number or Aadhaar number to retrieve it.

### Q2. Can I apply for multiple scholarships on NSP simultaneously?
No. NSP allows only **one application per academic year**. Choose the most beneficial scheme for your situation.

### Q3. My mobile number has changed since registration. Can I update it?
Contact the NSP helpline at 0120-6619540 with your Application ID and identity proof to request a mobile number change.

### Q4. The application form shows "Aadhaar already registered." What should I do?
Your Aadhaar was used in a previous application. If it was you, recover your old credentials. If someone else used your Aadhaar, report to NSP helpline immediately.

### Q5. Can I edit my application after final submission?
No. After final submit, the application is locked for verification. Only if the institute returns it as defective can you make corrections.

### Q6. I'm from a different state than my institute. Which state scholarship do I select?
Select the scholarship based on your **state of domicile** (permanent residence), not the state where your institute is located.
