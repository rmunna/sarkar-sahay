# Income Tax Refund Status India 2026 — Check, Timeline, and Delay Fixes

Filed your ITR and claimed a refund? Here's how to track it, understand the timeline, and fix common delays.

## Typical Refund Timeline

| Filing Stage | Expected Time |
|-------------|--------------|
| ITR filed → E-verification | Do immediately (within 30 days) |
| E-verified → CPC processing | 7–21 days |
| CPC processed → Refund issued | 15–45 days (after processing) |
| Refund issued → Bank credit | 5–7 working days |
| **Total from e-verify to bank credit** | **3–8 weeks** (normal cases) |

**If you filed early (June–July)**: Typically faster — CPC processes early filers first.
**If filed late (November–December)**: May take 45–90 days due to processing backlog.

## How to Check Refund Status

### Method 1: Income Tax Portal (Recommended)

1. Go to **incometax.gov.in**
2. Login with PAN/Aadhaar
3. Dashboard → **"Pending Actions"** or go to **e-File → Income Tax Returns → View Filed Returns**
4. Click on your filed return for the relevant AY
5. The return status shows: "Successfully e-Verified", "Under Processing", "Processed", "Refund Issued", "Refund Failed"

When status shows "Refund Issued": You'll see the refund amount and the date of issue. Bank credit typically takes 5–7 more days.

### Method 2: NSDL Refund Tracking

1. Go to tin.tin.nsdl.com/oltas/refundstatuslogin.html
2. Enter PAN and Assessment Year (e.g., 2026-27 for FY 2025-26)
3. View refund status and remarks

NSDL shows specific remarks like "Refund credited", "Refund returned (bank account invalid)", "ECS failed", etc.

### Method 3: via 26AS / AIS

After refund is credited: The refund amount appears in your Form 26AS (Part D: Details of Paid Refund). Check AIS for confirmation.

## Common Refund Status Messages — What They Mean

| Status | Meaning | Action |
|--------|---------|--------|
| **Return Successfully e-Verified** | Verification done, processing not yet started | Wait |
| **Under Processing** | CPC Bengaluru is processing | Wait (usually 7–21 days) |
| **Intimation u/s 143(1) Sent** | Processing done; intimation issued (may have demand/refund details) | Check email; verify amount |
| **Refund Issued** | Money sent to bank | Wait 5–7 days for bank credit |
| **Refund Failure** | Bank rejected the credit | Fix bank account; request reissue |
| **Refund Adjusted against Demand** | Previous tax demand offset against your refund | Check demand via portal |
| **Refund Returned — No Credit (RNC)** | Sent but returned by bank | Validate bank account and request reissue |

## Common Reasons for Refund Delay or Failure

### 1. Bank Account Not Pre-Validated
The most common issue. Your bank account must be "pre-validated" on the IT portal before a refund can be credited.

**Fix**:
1. Go to incometax.gov.in → Login → Profile → My Bank Account
2. Add your bank account
3. Click "Pre-validate"
4. Verify via net banking OTP or bank statement
5. Also mark it as "ECS Enabled" for refunds

### 2. Name Mismatch (PAN vs Bank Account)
Your name on PAN card must match your name in the bank account. Even a small difference (e.g., "Rahul Kumar" on PAN vs "R. Kumar" in bank) causes refund failure.

**Fix**: Either correct your bank account name (visit bank branch) or correct your PAN card name (NSDL/UTIITSL). Then re-validate the bank account.

### 3. Return Under Processing for Long Time
If your return has been "Under Processing" for more than 60–90 days without moving forward, there may be a manual processing requirement.

**Fix**: Wait. If more than 90 days: File a grievance via the IT portal (e-Nivaran / Grievance section). Mention your PAN, acknowledgement number, and that processing is pending.

### 4. Section 143(1) Demand Raised (Refund Adjusted)
CPC may raise a demand (if they find discrepancy in your return) and adjust part or all of your refund against this demand.

**Fix**: Check the Section 143(1) intimation (sent to your registered email). If the demand is correct, pay the balance. If incorrect, file a rectification request via incometax.gov.in.

### 5. Return Not E-Verified
If you filed but didn't e-verify within 30 days, the return is treated as invalid. No refund is processed.

**Fix**: Check if you can e-verify late (CBDT sometimes allows late verification). Or file a fresh return and e-verify immediately.

## How to Request Refund Re-Issue

If your refund failed (bank account issue, name mismatch):

1. Fix the underlying issue (pre-validate bank account, correct name mismatch)
2. Go to incometax.gov.in → Login
3. Go to **e-File → Income Tax Returns → View Filed Returns**
4. Click on the relevant return
5. Look for **"Refund Re-issue Request"** option (appears after a failed refund)
6. Submit the request
7. Refund is typically reissued within 30 days

## Grievance for Long-Pending Refunds

If refund hasn't come within 3 months of e-verification:

1. Go to incometax.gov.in → **Grievances → Submit Grievance**
2. Select: "Income Tax" → "Return Processing & Refunds"
3. Describe: Your refund amount, AY, date of filing, e-verification date
4. A grievance number is generated — typically resolved in 30 days

## Refund Under Old Outstanding Demand

If you have an old demand from previous years that you dispute, the IT Department may "hold" your current refund until the demand is settled.

**Fix**: Respond to the demand (agree/disagree via IT portal) under **"e-Proceedings"**. If you disputed correctly, the held refund may be released. If the demand is valid, pay it; the excess refund (after adjustment) is then released.

## Refund Amount Different From What You Expected

Check Section 143(1) intimation — this shows the IT Department's computation of your refund. Differences arise from:
- TDS claims not matching 26AS
- Deductions disallowed
- Income declared differently
- Mathematical adjustments

If you disagree: File a **Rectification Request** via incometax.gov.in (within the time limit). This is different from a revision — it's correcting a "mistake apparent from the record."

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Filed on July 25th, e-verified same day. It's now October 10th. Status still "Under Processing." What do I do?

After 90 days with no movement: File a grievance via the IT portal. Mention your PAN, Ack No., date of filing, and that the return has been under processing for 75+ days with no update. Most grievances are resolved within 30–45 days.

### My refund shows "Issued" on the portal but hasn't reached my bank in 15 days. What's wrong?

Possible issues:
1. Bank account not pre-validated or ECS not enabled → Refund "returned" by bank
2. Name mismatch → Bank rejected
3. Account closed or changed

Check NSDL refund tracker for specific reason code. If refund was returned, submit "Refund Re-issue Request" on the IT portal with a valid pre-validated bank account.

### My refund is for ₹30,000 but I received only ₹18,000. Where's the rest?

The IT Department may have adjusted ₹12,000 against an outstanding demand from a previous year. Check your 143(1) intimation and look for any outstanding demand in your portal dashboard. If you dispute the demand, raise a response via e-Proceedings.
