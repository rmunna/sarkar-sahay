# Maharashtra Talathi Exam Guide — Eligibility, Pattern & Preparation Tips

The Maharashtra Revenue Department conducts the Talathi Bharti (recruitment) exam for the post of Talathi (Village Revenue Officer) across all districts of Maharashtra. The Talathi is a crucial village-level officer responsible for land records, revenue collection, and grassroots governance.

Maharashtra Talathi is one of the most popular government exams in the state with massive participation from graduates across Maharashtra.

## Eligibility Criteria

### Age Limit

| Category | Minimum Age | Maximum Age |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| General (Open) | 18 years | 38 years |
| OBC | 18 years | 41 years |
| SC/ST | 18 years | 43 years |

### Educational Qualification

- **Minimum:** Graduate (Bachelor's degree) from a recognized university
- Must have passed Marathi and English at graduation level
- Computer knowledge (MS-CIT or equivalent)

## Exam Pattern

| Subject | Marks |
|---------|-------|
| Marathi | 25 |
| English | 25 |
| General Knowledge | 25 |
| Mathematics & Reasoning | 25 |
| **Total** | **100** |

- **Duration:** 1 hour (60 minutes)
- **Type:** Online (Computer-based)
- **Negative marking:** Varies by notification

## Syllabus Overview

### Marathi
- Grammar, Comprehension, Vocabulary, Muhavare, Lokoktiyan

### English
- Grammar, Comprehension, Vocabulary, Sentence correction

### General Knowledge
- Maharashtra History & Culture, Indian History, Geography, Polity, Economy, Current Affairs

### Mathematics & Reasoning
- Number system, Percentage, Ratio, Average, Profit & Loss, Logical reasoning, Series, Coding-decoding

## How to Apply

1. Visit the official Talathi Bharti website (district-wise notification)
2. Register and fill the application form online
3. Upload photo, signature, and required certificates
4. Pay application fee
5. Submit and download confirmation

## Application Fee

| Category | Fee |
|----------|-----|
| General (Open) | ₹500 |
| OBC / EWS | ₹375 |
| SC / ST | ₹250 |

## Salary & Benefits

- **Pay Scale:** ₹25,500 – ₹81,100
- DA, HRA, and allowances as per Maharashtra Government
- Pension (NPS), medical benefits
- Promotion to Circle Inspector (Mandal Adhikari)

## Preparation Tips

1. **Marathi language:** Strong Marathi is essential — practice grammar and vocabulary
2. **Maharashtra GK:** Focus on Maharashtra history, Maratha Empire, and state schemes
3. **Speed is key:** 100 questions in 60 minutes — very fast pace
4. **MS-CIT:** Complete the computer certificate if not already done
5. **Previous papers:** Solve past Talathi papers for each district

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is graduation mandatory for Maharashtra Talathi?
Yes, a Bachelor's degree is the minimum requirement.

### Is MS-CIT certificate mandatory?
Yes, MS-CIT or equivalent computer certificate is required.

### What is the role of a Talathi?
Maintains land records (7/12 extracts), revenue collection, assists in surveys, and provides citizen services.

### Is there an interview?
No, selection is based on the written exam merit only.

### Are vacancies district-wise?
Yes, Talathi recruitment is usually conducted district-wise.

### Is Marathi mandatory?
Yes, knowledge of Marathi is essential — it is tested in the exam.
