## What is MahaDBT Scholarship Portal?

**MahaDBT (Maharashtra Direct Benefit Transfer)** is the unified portal at [mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in](https://mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in) where students can apply for **all Maharashtra government scholarships** under one roof. Instead of visiting multiple department offices, students apply once and the system routes the application to the relevant department.

Over **30 different scholarship schemes** are available on MahaDBT, covering:
- SC (Scheduled Caste) students
- ST (Scheduled Tribe) students
- OBC / VJNT / SBC students
- EWS (Economically Weaker Section)
- Minority students (Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, Parsi, Jain)
- Differently-abled students

Scholarships range from pre-matric (Class 1–10) to post-matric (Class 11 to PhD and professional courses).

## Eligibility (Common Conditions)

- Maharashtra domicile (permanent residence in Maharashtra)
- Category proof (caste certificate from Maharashtra)
- Annual family income within the scheme's limit
- Enrolled in a recognized educational institution (in Maharashtra or approved institution outside Maharashtra)
- Not receiving another scholarship simultaneously

**Income limits vary by scheme:**
- SC/ST post-matric: up to ₹2.5 lakh/year
- OBC post-matric: up to ₹1 lakh/year
- EWS: up to ₹8 lakh/year (central definition)
- Minority: up to ₹2 lakh/year (state schemes) or ₹2.5 lakh/year

## How to Apply on MahaDBT — Step by Step

### New Registration

1. Go to **[mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in](https://mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in)**
2. Click **"New Registration"**
3. Enter your Aadhaar number → verify with OTP
4. Fill basic details: name, date of birth, mobile, email
5. Create password → your account is created
6. Log in and complete your **profile**:
   - Personal details
   - Bank account (your own — Aadhaar-linked)
   - Category and caste details
   - Upload caste/income certificate

### Apply for Scholarship

1. Log in to MahaDBT portal
2. Go to **"Schemes"** — all available schemes for your profile are shown
3. Click **"Apply"** on the relevant scholarship
4. Fill scheme-specific details (institution, course, year, income proof)
5. Upload required documents
6. Submit application → note the Application ID

### Renewal (For Continuing Students)

1. Log in → go to **"My Applications"** → **"Renewal"**
2. Update current year details (course, marks, fees)
3. Upload fresh documents if expired
4. Submit for institutional verification

## Documents Required

1. Aadhaar card (mandatory — must match name in application)
2. Caste / Category certificate (Maharashtra issued — SC/ST/OBC/VJNT/SBC/NT)
3. Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) certificate — for OBC/VJNT/SBC (renewed every year)
4. Income certificate (Maharashtra — from Tehsildar/SDM/Tahsildar)
5. Domicile certificate (Maharashtra residency proof)
6. Previous year marksheet / passing certificate
7. Bonafide certificate / fee receipt from current institution
8. Bank passbook (account in student's name, Aadhaar-linked)
9. Photograph

## How to Check MahaDBT Application Status

1. Log in to **mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in**
2. Click **"My Applications"** → select the scheme
3. Application stages:
   - **Submitted** — waiting for institutional verification
   - **Institute Verified** — forwarded to district/department
   - **Sanctioned** — approved, payment processing
   - **Payment Released** — amount credited to bank account

**Check payment via PFMS:**
→ Go to [pfms.nic.in](https://pfms.nic.in) → "Know Your Payment" → enter bank account number

## Common Problems and Fixes

### OBC Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) certificate missing
NCL certificate is needed every year for OBC/VJNT/SBC students — it cannot be more than 1 year old. Get a fresh NCL certificate from your Tehsildar before applying.

### Aadhaar name mismatch with caste certificate
The name on Aadhaar must match caste/income certificates exactly. If different, get the documents corrected at the issuing authority before applying.

### Institution not registered on MahaDBT
Your college must be registered on the portal. Ask the college accounts or scholarship department to register. Without registration, student applications cannot be forwarded.

### Payment not received despite sanctioned status
Verify that your bank account is in your name and Aadhaar is linked to it. Check PFMS for payment status. Raise a grievance if not credited within 30 days of sanctioning.

### Application rejected — income above limit
Ensure your income certificate is from Maharashtra and shows the correct annual income. If income was calculated incorrectly, get a revised income certificate.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I apply for more than one scholarship on MahaDBT?
No — you can only receive one scholarship at a time. The portal allows you to see eligible schemes, but once sanctioned under one, you cannot receive another simultaneously.

### My college is in another state (e.g., Pune student studying in Delhi). Can I apply?
Post-matric scholarships cover studies outside Maharashtra only for courses not available in Maharashtra. You will need a certificate from Maharashtra's university/government confirming the course is not available in the state.

### I missed the last date. Can I apply now?
Late applications are generally not accepted. Watch for any grace period announcements on the MahaDBT portal or Maharashtra government websites.

### Is the scholarship taxable?
No — scholarship amounts received under government scholarship schemes are exempt from income tax under Section 10(16) of the Income Tax Act.

### I am an SC student — is there an income limit?
For SC post-matric scholarship, the income limit is ₹2.5 lakh per year. For some central government SC scholarships, there may be no income limit. Check the specific scheme.

### How do I get the Swadhaar Gruh Yojana benefit?
Swadhaar is for SC/NavBauddha students who cannot get admission to government hostels. Apply on MahaDBT after confirming that no government hostel seat is available for you.
