## What is a No Objection Certificate (NOC)?

A No Objection Certificate (NOC) is an official document issued by an organization, agency, or individual stating that they have **no objection** to a specific action, transaction, or decision. NOCs are widely used in India for various administrative, legal, and personal purposes.

NOCs serve as a clearance or consent document. They confirm that the issuing authority has no pending claims, disputes, or objections regarding the subject matter.

## Common Types of NOC in India

### 1. Vehicle NOC (Inter-State Transfer)
Required when transferring vehicle registration from one state to another:
- Issued by the **RTO** of the state where the vehicle is currently registered
- Valid for **6 months** from the date of issue
- Needed to re-register the vehicle in the new state
- See our [vehicle registration guide](/guide/vehicle-registration-transfer) for the complete process

### 2. Bank Loan NOC
Issued by the bank after full repayment of a loan:
- **Home loan NOC** — Needed to release property from mortgage
- **Vehicle loan NOC** — Required to remove hypothecation from RC
- **Personal/education loan NOC** — Confirms loan closure

### 3. Employer NOC
Issued by an employer permitting an employee to:
- Apply for higher education
- Take on additional work or consultancy
- Apply for government jobs or competitive exams
- Travel abroad for personal purposes

### 4. Society/Landlord NOC
- From housing society for property sale or renovation
- From landlord for passport application or address change
- For running a business from residential premises

### 5. Police NOC
- Required for certain business licences (restaurant, bar, hotel)
- Needed for large public events or gatherings
- Character verification for specific purposes

### 6. Fire NOC
- Mandatory for commercial establishments, factories, malls
- Issued by the **Fire Department** after safety inspection
- Required for building completion certificate

### 7. Environmental NOC
- Required for industrial projects and construction
- Issued by **State Pollution Control Board** or **MoEFCC**
- Mandatory before starting certain categories of projects

### 8. Educational NOC
- From one institution to join another (school/college transfer)
- Required for migration certificate
- From university to appear in external examinations

## How to Apply for Vehicle NOC Online

### Through Parivahan Portal
1. Visit [parivahan.gov.in](https://parivahan.gov.in)
2. Go to **Online Services** → **Vehicle Related Services**
3. Select your state and RTO
4. Choose **"NOC/Cancellation of NOC"**
5. Enter vehicle registration number
6. Fill in the application with reason (inter-state transfer)
7. Upload documents:
   - RC (Registration Certificate)
   - Insurance copy
   - PUC certificate
   - Address proof of new state
   - Identity proof
8. Pay the fee (₹100-500 depending on state)
9. Book an appointment at RTO if inspection is required
10. Download NOC after approval

### Offline at RTO
1. Visit the RTO where the vehicle is registered
2. Fill **Form 28** (Application for NOC)
3. Submit with:
   - Original RC
   - Valid insurance
   - PUC certificate
   - Tax clearance certificate
   - Address proof
4. Vehicle inspection may be conducted
5. Pay the prescribed fee
6. Collect NOC within 7-15 days

## How to Get Bank Loan NOC

### After Home Loan Closure
1. Submit final EMI payment
2. Request loan closure letter from the bank
3. Bank issues NOC along with:
   - Original property documents
   - Mortgage release letter
   - Lien removal letter
4. Register the mortgage release at the Sub-Registrar's office
5. Bank should issue NOC within **15 days** of loan closure

### After Vehicle Loan Closure
1. Complete all EMI payments
2. Request NOC from the bank/NBFC
3. Receive:
   - Loan closure NOC
   - Form 35 (hypothecation removal)
   - Original RC (if held by bank)
4. Visit RTO with Form 35 and NOC to update RC
5. Remove hypothecation endorsement from RC

## NOC Letter Format (General Template)

```
[Organization Letterhead]

NO OBJECTION CERTIFICATE

Date: [Date]
Reference No: [Ref No]

To Whom It May Concern,

This is to certify that [Organization Name] has no objection to
[Mr./Ms. Name], [Employee ID/Designation], [specific action/purpose].

[He/She] has been associated with us since [Date] and has no pending
dues or obligations with us as of [Date].

This NOC is issued upon request for the purpose of [specific purpose].

Authorized Signatory
[Name and Designation]
[Organization Name]
[Official Seal/Stamp]
```

## Important Tips

1. **Banks must issue loan NOC within 15 days** of closure — If delayed, send a written complaint and escalate to the Banking Ombudsman
2. **Vehicle NOC is valid for 6 months** — Re-register the vehicle in the new state within this period
3. **Keep multiple copies** of all NOCs — They're needed for various linked processes
4. **Fire NOC must be renewed** annually for commercial establishments
5. **Society NOC may have conditions** — Read carefully before accepting, especially for property transactions

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a bank charge for issuing a loan NOC?
No, banks cannot charge any fee for issuing a loan closure NOC. It must be provided free of cost within a reasonable time (15 days). If the bank delays, complain to the Banking Ombudsman.

### How long does a Vehicle NOC take?
Online NOC through Parivahan may take 3-7 working days. Offline at RTO can take 7-15 days. Some states provide instant online NOC.

### Is NOC mandatory for selling a property?
A society NOC is typically required for flat sales (confirming no pending dues). Bank NOC is needed if there's an existing home loan. The buyer usually requires these before completing the purchase.

### What if my employer refuses to give NOC?
An employer cannot legally prevent you from pursuing other opportunities. If NOC is unreasonably withheld, you can try applying without it and explain the situation, or seek legal recourse.

### Do I need NOC from all family members to sell property?
If the property is jointly owned, all co-owners must consent. For ancestral property, consent of all legal heirs may be needed. Consult a lawyer for property-specific situations.

### Can I cancel a Vehicle NOC?
Yes, if you decide not to transfer the vehicle, you can apply for **NOC cancellation** at the same RTO within the 6-month validity period.
