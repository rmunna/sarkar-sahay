## Why You Must Renew Vehicle Insurance On Time

Under the **Motor Vehicles Act, 1988 (Section 146)**, every vehicle on Indian roads must carry valid insurance. Driving with an expired policy is illegal and comes with serious consequences:

- **Fine of ₹2,000** and/or 3 months imprisonment for first offence
- **Fine of ₹4,000** and/or 3 months imprisonment for repeat offence
- **No claim settlement** — if you meet with an accident while uninsured, you bear the entire cost
- **RC renewal blocked** — you cannot renew your Registration Certificate without valid insurance
- **Legal liability** — you are personally liable for damages caused to third parties

> **Tip:** Set a reminder 15–30 days before your policy expiry date. Most insurers send renewal reminders via SMS and email.

## Types of Vehicle Insurance

Before renewing, understand which type of policy you have (or need):

### 1. Third-Party Insurance (Mandatory)

Covers damage or injury you cause to **other people, vehicles, or property**. It does NOT cover your own vehicle's damage.

- Required by law under the Motor Vehicles Act
- Premiums are fixed by **IRDAI** — same across all insurers
- Cheapest option but offers limited protection

### 2. Comprehensive Insurance (Recommended)

Covers **third-party liability + own vehicle damage** from accidents, theft, fire, natural disasters, and more.

- Includes personal accident cover for owner-driver
- Add-ons available: zero depreciation, engine protect, roadside assistance, NCB protect
- Premium varies by insurer, vehicle age, city, and add-ons chosen

### 3. Standalone Own Damage (OD) Policy

Since 2019, IRDAI allows purchasing **own damage cover separately** from third-party insurance. Useful if your third-party policy has a different expiry date.

## How to Renew Vehicle Insurance Online — Step by Step

Renewing your bike or car insurance online takes just 10–15 minutes. Here are the two main methods:

### Method 1: Directly on the Insurer's Website or App

1. **Visit your insurer's website** (e.g., ICICI Lombard, HDFC Ergo, Bajaj Allianz, New India Assurance)
2. **Click on "Renew Policy"** — usually on the homepage
3. **Enter your policy number** or vehicle registration number
4. **Review the policy details** — IDV (Insured Declared Value), coverage, add-ons
5. **Choose add-ons** if needed (zero depreciation, engine protect, NCB protect)
6. **Pay online** via UPI, net banking, credit/debit card, or wallet
7. **Download your renewed policy** — the new policy document is emailed instantly

### Method 2: Through Insurance Aggregators

Platforms like **PolicyBazaar, Coverfox, InsuranceDekho, and Acko** let you compare quotes from multiple insurers side-by-side.

1. **Enter your vehicle registration number**
2. **Compare premiums, coverage, and add-ons** across insurers
3. **Select the best plan** and proceed to payment
4. **Policy issued instantly** via email

> **Pro tip:** Aggregators often offer exclusive discounts and cashback that direct insurer websites may not.

### Method 3: Via Insurer's Mobile App

Most insurers (Bajaj Allianz, ICICI Lombard, HDFC Ergo) have mobile apps. Just log in, go to "My Policies," and hit renew. Quick and convenient.

## What is NCB (No Claim Bonus) and How to Protect It

**NCB** is a discount on your premium for every claim-free year. It accumulates as follows:

| Claim-Free Years | NCB Discount |
|-----------------|-------------|
| 1 year | 20% |
| 2 years | 25% |
| 3 years | 35% |
| 4 years | 45% |
| 5+ years | 50% |

### Key NCB Rules

- **NCB belongs to the owner**, not the vehicle — you can transfer it when buying a new vehicle
- **NCB resets to 0%** if you make a claim during the policy year
- **NCB is lost** if the policy lapses beyond 90 days
- **NCB can be transferred** to a new insurer when switching companies

### How to Protect NCB

Purchase the **NCB Protect add-on** (available with comprehensive policies). This lets you make a certain number of claims (usually 1–2 per year) without losing your accumulated NCB discount.

> **Example:** With 50% NCB on a ₹15,000 base premium, you save ₹7,500 annually. Protecting it with a ₹500–800 add-on is a smart move.

## What Happens If Your Vehicle Insurance Lapses

If your policy expires and you don't renew within the **grace period**, it is considered lapsed.

### Grace Period

- Most insurers allow renewal **up to 90 days** after expiry without a vehicle inspection
- After 90 days, your policy is treated as a **fresh policy** — inspection is required
- **There is no legal grace period** — driving without valid insurance is illegal from day one after expiry

### Consequences of a Lapsed Policy

- **Loss of NCB** — your accumulated no-claim bonus resets to zero
- **Vehicle inspection required** — the insurer may need a physical or video inspection
- **Higher premium** — you lose continuity benefits and NCB discounts
- **No coverage during the gap** — any accident or damage during the lapsed period is entirely your liability
- **Break-in insurance process** — you'll need to apply for break-in insurance, which takes longer

### How to Revive a Lapsed Policy

1. Contact your previous insurer or use an aggregator
2. Get a **vehicle inspection** done (many insurers now accept video/photo inspection via app)
3. Pay the revised premium (without NCB discount)
4. New policy is issued after inspection approval

## Third-Party vs Comprehensive Insurance — Which to Choose

| Feature | Third-Party Only | Comprehensive |
|---------|-----------------|--------------|
| Legal requirement | ✅ Mandatory | ✅ Covers mandatory + extra |
| Third-party damage cover | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes |
| Own vehicle damage | ❌ No | ✅ Yes |
| Theft protection | ❌ No | ✅ Yes |
| Natural disaster cover | ❌ No | ✅ Yes |
| Personal accident cover | ✅ Yes (owner-driver) | ✅ Yes |
| Add-ons available | ❌ No | ✅ Yes |
| Premium | Low (IRDAI-fixed) | Higher (varies) |
| Best for | Old vehicles, budget buyers | New/expensive vehicles |

**Recommendation:** For vehicles less than 5 years old, comprehensive insurance is strongly recommended. For older vehicles where repair costs may exceed the vehicle's value, third-party may suffice.

## Tips to Lower Your Vehicle Insurance Premium

1. **Maintain your NCB** — avoid small claims to preserve your no-claim discount (up to 50% off)
2. **Compare quotes** on aggregators like PolicyBazaar before renewing
3. **Increase your voluntary deductible** — opting for a higher deductible (amount you pay from pocket during a claim) reduces premium
4. **Install anti-theft devices** — ARAI-approved devices can get you a discount
5. **Choose the right IDV** — don't over-insure; set the Insured Declared Value close to market value
6. **Skip unnecessary add-ons** — evaluate if you really need all add-ons, especially for older vehicles
7. **Renew on time** — avoid policy lapse to retain NCB and continuity benefits
8. **Opt for long-term policies** — 2–3 year policies often come with a discount vs annual renewal
9. **Use your insurer's app** — some insurers offer app-exclusive discounts

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I renew my vehicle insurance after the expiry date?

Yes, but there are time limits. Most insurers allow renewal within **90 days of expiry** without inspection. After 90 days, you'll need a vehicle inspection and will lose your NCB. Driving without insurance at any point after expiry is illegal.

### Can I switch my insurer during renewal?

Yes, you can switch to any insurer during renewal. Your NCB is transferable — just provide an NCB certificate or declaration from your previous insurer. Compare quotes on aggregators to find the best deal.

### Is third-party insurance enough for my vehicle?

Legally, yes — third-party insurance meets the minimum legal requirement. However, it doesn't cover damage to your own vehicle. For newer or expensive vehicles, **comprehensive insurance** is strongly recommended for full protection.

### How do I check my vehicle insurance status online?

Visit the **IIB (Insurance Information Bureau) portal** at [iib.gov.in](https://iib.gov.in) or the **Vahan portal** at [vahan.parivahan.gov.in](https://vahan.parivahan.gov.in) and enter your vehicle registration number to check insurance validity.

### What is IDV and how does it affect my premium?

**IDV (Insured Declared Value)** is the current market value of your vehicle as determined by the insurer. It is the maximum amount the insurer will pay in case of total loss or theft. A higher IDV means higher premium but better payout; a lower IDV means lower premium but less coverage.

### Can I renew my insurance from a different city?

Yes. Vehicle insurance is **not city-specific** in terms of renewal. You can renew online from anywhere in India. However, your registration zone may affect the premium slightly.

### What happens if I make a claim — does my premium increase?

Yes. If you make a claim, you **lose your NCB** for that year, which effectively increases your next year's premium. The base premium itself doesn't change, but the NCB discount (up to 50%) is removed.
