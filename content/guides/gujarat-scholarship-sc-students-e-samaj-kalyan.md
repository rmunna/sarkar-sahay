## What is Gujarat SC Post-Matric Scholarship?

**Gujarat Post-Matric Scholarship for Scheduled Caste (SC) Students** provides **full tuition fee reimbursement and monthly maintenance stipend** to SC students studying after Class 10. It is implemented through the e-Samaj Kalyan portal (esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in) — Gujarat's unified social welfare schemes platform.

---

## Key Benefits

| Benefit | Amount |
|---------|--------|
| Tuition fee | 100% reimbursement |
| Examination fee | Fully covered |
| Maintenance allowance | ₹250–1,200/month (varies by course and hostel) |
| Study tour | Covered for professional courses |

### Maintenance Allowance Rates

| Course | Hostel | Day Scholar |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Class 11 & 12 / ITI / Diploma | ₹380/month | ₹230/month |
| Graduation (General) | ₹570/month | ₹300/month |
| Graduation (Professional — Engg, Medical) | ₹1,200/month | ₹550/month |
| Post-Graduation | ₹820/month | ₹530/month |

---

## Who is Eligible?

- **Gujarat resident SC (Scheduled Caste) student**
- Studying in Class 11 onwards in a recognized Gujarat institution
- Family annual income: **₹2.5 lakh or below**
- First-time applicants (fresh scholarship) or renewal (continuing students)
- Not already receiving full scholarship from another source

---

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card**
2. **SC caste certificate** from competent authority (Mamlatdar / Collector)
3. **Income certificate** (from Mamlatdar — annual income ≤₹2.5 lakh)
4. **8A** (revenue record showing caste / land details — if applicable)
5. **Admission receipt** and fee receipt from institution
6. **Previous year mark sheet**
7. **Bank passbook** — student's Aadhaar-linked account
8. **Photograph**

## How to Apply — e-Samaj Kalyan Portal

### Step 1 — Register on e-Samaj Kalyan

Go to **[esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in)**:
- Click "New Registration"
- Enter Aadhaar, mobile, email
- Set password and complete registration

### Step 2 — Fill Scholarship Application

Login → "Post Matric Scholarship for SC":
- Select academic year and course
- Enter institution details (name, course, fees)
- Fill personal and family income details
- Upload all documents

### Step 3 — Institution Verification

Your college/ITI verifies enrollment and fee structure on the portal (institutions have their own login).

### Step 4 — District Social Welfare Office Approval

District SC Welfare Officer verifies caste and income certificates.

### Step 5 — Disbursement

- Tuition fee: Paid directly to institution (or reimbursed to student)
- Maintenance allowance: Credited monthly to student's bank account

---

## Application Deadline

Applications typically open in **June–August** each year for the upcoming academic session. **Do not wait until October** — apply immediately after admission. Late applications may be rejected.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. I am an SC student in a private polytechnic in Ahmedabad. Can I get this scholarship?**

Yes. Private recognized polytechnic / diploma institutions are covered. Tuition fees are reimbursed up to the government-approved rate. Apply at esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in.

**2. I live in a hostel. How does that affect the scholarship?**

Hostel students receive a higher maintenance allowance than day scholars (as shown in the table). You will need to provide a hostel admission certificate/fee receipt as proof.

**3. My family income is ₹3 lakh. Am I eligible?**

The income limit for the central post-matric SC scholarship is ₹2.5 lakh. At ₹3 lakh, you exceed the limit. Check if Gujarat's state-funded scholarship has a higher income limit (sometimes ₹4–6 lakh for state component). Consult the District SC Welfare Office.

**4. I applied last year. Do I need to reapply every year?**

Yes. You must renew your scholarship application each year on esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in. Renewal requires fresh caste certificate (or existing one if valid), current year fee receipt, and previous year mark sheet.

**5. When do I get the tuition fee money?**

Tuition fee is typically paid directly to the institution. The college submits its fee claim, and the government releases payment within 2–4 months. If there's a delay, contact the District SC Welfare Office with your scholarship reference number.
