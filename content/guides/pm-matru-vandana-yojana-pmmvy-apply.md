## What is PM Matru Vandana Yojana?

**Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY)** is a maternity benefit programme launched by the Ministry of Women and Child Development. It provides **₹5,000 in cash** directly to a pregnant woman's bank account for the **first child** in three installments to encourage proper nutrition and health checkups during pregnancy.

From 2022, the scheme was expanded to also provide **₹6,000** for the **second child** (if it is a girl).

Key features:
- **₹5,000** for the first live birth (in 3 installments)
- **₹6,000** for the second child if it is a **girl baby**
- No income limit for the first child
- Payment via DBT directly to mother's Aadhaar-linked bank account
- Applies to all working and non-working women

## Who is Eligible?

**For ₹5,000 (First Child):**
- Pregnant women carrying their **first child**
- Age 19 years or above at the time of pregnancy
- Not employed in any public sector institution (central/state government salaried employees who get maternity benefit are excluded)
- Must register early in pregnancy (preferably within first trimester)

**For ₹6,000 (Second Child — Girl):**
- Applicable if the second live birth is a **girl child**
- Amount paid as a lump sum after childbirth

## Installment Structure — First Child

| Installment | When | Amount | Condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st | Early pregnancy (1st trimester) | ₹1,000 | Registration at Anganwadi + MCP card |
| 2nd | 6 months into pregnancy | ₹2,000 | At least 1 ANC (antenatal checkup) |
| 3rd | After child birth | ₹2,000 | Child birth registration + 1st vaccination |
| **Total** | | **₹5,000** | |

## How to Apply — Step by Step

### Method 1: Via Anganwadi Centre (Recommended)

1. Visit your **nearest Anganwadi Centre (AWC)** as early as possible after confirming pregnancy (ideally in the 1st trimester)
2. Carry your **Aadhaar card** and **MCP (Mother and Child Protection) card**
3. The Anganwadi Worker (AWW) will register you in the **PMMVY portal**
4. You'll receive an **application ID**
5. Each subsequent installment is triggered when you complete the required checkup/condition and report back to the Anganwadi

### Method 2: Online at pmmvy.wcd.gov.in

1. Go to **[pmmvy.wcd.gov.in](https://pmmvy.wcd.gov.in)**
2. Click **"Beneficiary Registration"**
3. Enter your **Aadhaar number** → verify via OTP
4. Fill in pregnancy details, bank account details
5. Upload required documents
6. Submit → note application ID
7. For subsequent installments, submit the relevant forms after completing conditions

### Method 3: Via Health Centre / ASHA Worker

ASHA workers and ANM nurses at sub-health centres also assist with PMMVY registration. They can submit applications on your behalf.

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card** of the mother (mandatory — Aadhaar-linked bank account for DBT)
2. **MCP card** (Mother and Child Protection card — given at the first ANC visit)
3. **Bank passbook** (savings account in the mother's name, Aadhaar-seeded)
4. **Spouse's Aadhaar** (optional, for filling application)
5. **Birth certificate of child** (for 3rd installment)
6. **Vaccination record** (for 3rd installment — BCG/OPV/DPT first dose)

## PMMVY + JSY = Combined Benefit

Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY) and **Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY)** are two different maternity schemes:

| Scheme | For | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| PMMVY | All pregnant women (1st child) | ₹5,000 |
| JSY | BPL women, institutional delivery | ₹700–₹1,400 (depending on state/rural/urban) |

Both can be availed simultaneously — the total benefit from both can be up to **₹6,400** for institutional delivery in a rural area.

## Check Application Status

1. Go to **[pmmvy.wcd.gov.in](https://pmmvy.wcd.gov.in)**
2. Click **"Beneficiary Login"** or **"Application Status"**
3. Enter your **Aadhaar number** or **application ID**
4. View installment status and payment history

OR ask your Anganwadi Worker — they have access to the backend system and can check your status.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What if I am a government employee?
Central and state government employees who get **paid maternity leave** are not eligible for PMMVY, as they already receive maternity benefits from their employer.

### I am pregnant for the second time but the first child died. Am I eligible?
Yes — if the first child did not survive and this is your first **live birth**, you are eligible for ₹5,000.

### Can I apply for PMMVY if I gave birth at home (not hospital)?
Yes — PMMVY is not restricted to institutional delivery. However, the 3rd installment requires the child's vaccination record, which is easier to maintain if birth registration is done.

### My Aadhaar is not linked to my bank account. What to do?
Visit your bank branch with Aadhaar and request Aadhaar-bank seeding. Also update Aadhaar with NPCI mapper. Without this, DBT payment will fail.

### When is the last date to apply?
Apply within **730 days (2 years)** of the delivery date for the installments. Earlier is better — first installment must be applied for during pregnancy.

### What happens to the benefit for a second girl child?
The ₹6,000 for the second girl child is paid as a **lump sum** after the child's birth is registered and immunization has begun.
