# Universal Pension Scheme Jharkhand: Monthly Pension for Senior Citizens and Others

The Jharkhand Universal Pension Scheme provides a monthly pension of **₹1,000** to eligible senior citizens, widows, and persons with disabilities in the state. The scheme aims to provide social security to vulnerable sections of society who have no regular source of income. Jharkhand was among the first states to universalise pension for all eligible elderly residents.

## What is Universal Pension Jharkhand?

The Jharkhand government merged and expanded multiple pension schemes into a more inclusive Universal Pension framework. This covers:

| Category | Pension Amount | Age/Criteria |
|----------|---------------|-------------|
| Old Age Pension | ₹1,000/month | 60 years and above |
| Widow Pension | ₹1,000/month | Widows aged 18+ |
| Disability Pension | ₹1,000/month | 40%+ disability |

The pension is transferred directly to the beneficiary's bank account via DBT every month.

## Who is Eligible?

### Old Age Pension
- Must be **60 years or above**
- Must be a **permanent resident of Jharkhand**
- Must not be receiving pension from **any other government source** (central/state retirement pension)

### Widow Pension
- Must be a **widow** residing in Jharkhand
- Age: **18 years and above**
- Must not have remarried
- Must not be receiving pension from another government scheme

### Disability Pension
- Must have **40% or more disability** (certified by a government medical board)
- Must be a **permanent resident of Jharkhand**
- No age restriction

### General Criteria for All
- Must have an **Aadhaar card**
- Must have a **bank account** linked with Aadhaar
- Should belong to **economically weaker sections** (no strict income limit for elderly pension in universalised scheme)

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card**
- **Age proof** — voter ID, birth certificate, school certificate (for old age pension)
- **Bank account passbook** with IFSC code
- **Passport-size photograph**
- **Death certificate of husband** (for widow pension)
- **Disability certificate** from government hospital (for disability pension)
- **Residential certificate** of Jharkhand
- **BPL/ration card** (if available)

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Online Application

1. Visit the official pension portal at **[nsap.jharkhand.gov.in](https://nsap.jharkhand.gov.in/)** or the e-Pension Jharkhand portal
2. Click on **"Apply for Pension"** or **"New Application"**
3. Select the **pension type** (Old Age / Widow / Disability)
4. Enter **Aadhaar number** and verify via OTP or biometric
5. Fill in personal details — name, DOB, address, category
6. Upload supporting documents — age proof, photo, bank details
7. For widow pension: upload **husband's death certificate**
8. For disability pension: upload **disability certificate**
9. Submit the application and note the **Application ID**

### Offline Application

1. Visit the **Block Development Office (BDO)** or **District Social Welfare Office**
2. Obtain the pension application form
3. Fill in details and attach documents
4. Submit to the Block/District office
5. Collect acknowledgement receipt
6. Application is verified and forwarded for approval

### Check Application Status

Visit the pension portal and enter your Application ID or Aadhaar number to check status and payment history.

## Fees

There are **no fees** for applying. The process is completely free.

## Processing Time

- Application verification: **1-3 months**
- Approval: **1-2 months**
- First pension credit: Within **1 month** of approval
- Monthly pension thereafter: Credited by the 15th of each month (approximately)

## Important Tips

1. **Ensure Aadhaar biometric is active** — biometric authentication may be required
2. **Update bank details** if you change your account — inform the BDO office immediately
3. **Annual life certificate** may be required — check with local authorities
4. **Report non-receipt** of pension to the Block office promptly
5. **Apply through the official portal only** — avoid middlemen who charge fees

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Is there an income limit for old age pension?
Under the universalised scheme, the income criteria have been significantly relaxed. Most elderly residents aged 60+ who are not government retirees are eligible.

### Q2: Can both husband and wife receive old age pension?
Yes, if both are aged 60+ and meet the eligibility criteria, both can receive pension independently.

### Q3: What if I'm already receiving IGNOAPS (central pension)?
If you receive the central government's Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension, you may receive a **top-up** from the state scheme to bring the total to ₹1,000/month. Check with local authorities.

### Q4: How is the pension credited?
Through **DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer)** to the Aadhaar-linked bank account every month.

### Q5: What happens when the pensioner passes away?
The family must inform the Block office. Pension stops from the next month. Continued withdrawal after death is a punishable offence.

### Q6: Can I apply for both widow and old age pension?
No, you can receive **only one type** of pension under this scheme.

### Q7: Is the ₹1,000 amount fixed or does it increase?
The amount is reviewed periodically by the state government. It was increased from earlier amounts to ₹1,000. Future revisions depend on government policy.
