# UK Standard Visitor Visa from India 2026 — Complete Guide

The UK is NOT part of the Schengen Area, so it requires a separate visa. The UK Standard Visitor Visa (tourist/business visit) allows stays up to 6 months. Here's everything you need to apply from India.

---

## UK Visa Types — Which One Do You Need?

| Visa | Purpose | Max Stay |
|------|---------|---------|
| **Standard Visitor** | Tourism, family visit, business meetings, conferences, short study (<6 months) | 6 months per visit |
| **Student Visa** | Full-time study at a registered UK institution | Duration of course |
| **Skilled Worker Visa** | Employment (requires employer sponsorship) | Up to 5 years |
| **Graduate Visa** | Post-study work (after UK degree) | 2 years |

**For most India-to-UK travel**: Standard Visitor Visa is what you need.

## UK Visa Fee (2026)

| Visa Type | Fee |
|-----------|-----|
| Standard Visitor (up to 6 months) | £115 (₹12,000–12,500 approx) |
| Standard Visitor (2 years, multiple entry) | £400 |
| Standard Visitor (5 years, multiple entry) | £670 |
| Standard Visitor (10 years, multiple entry) | £837 |
| VFS service charge | ₹2,000–2,500 |

**Note**: Unlike Schengen, the UK charges different fees for different validity periods. For a first trip, pay for the 6-month single entry (£115).

## Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS) — Important

The UK requires most non-EEA applicants to pay the **Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS)** upfront:
- Rate: **£470 per year** for adults
- For a 6-month visitor visa: £235 (half year)
- For a 2-year multiple-entry: £940

**Why this matters**: IHS is paid during the online application before biometrics. It's significant — add it to your cost calculation.

**Exception**: Students under 18, people from certain countries (check uk.gov), and some specific categories are exempt. Standard Visitor applicants from India generally pay IHS.

## Documents Required

### Core Documents (All Applicants)

| Document | Details |
|----------|---------|
| **Valid passport** | Valid for entire trip + some buffer; old passports with travel stamps |
| **Online application** | Completed at gov.uk; application reference number needed for appointment |
| **Photo** | UK visa standard: 35×45mm, white background, recent |
| **Biometrics** | Enrolled at VFS Global appointment |

### Financial Documents (Critical)

UK Entry Clearance Officers look for:
1. **Bank statements (last 6 months)**: Show salary credits, consistent balance
2. **Balance**: No hard minimum, but **£1,000–1,500 per week of stay** is a common benchmark. 10-day trip: ₹1.3L–2L equivalent ideally visible
3. **Bank letter**: Stating account since date, current balance, type of account (VFS can arrange this from your bank)
4. **Payslips (last 3 months)**: For salaried applicants
5. **ITR (last 2 years)**: Income proof

### Employment/Business Documents

| Type | Documents |
|------|----------|
| **Salaried** | Employment letter on letterhead (confirming role, salary, leave approved, employer confirms you'll return), Form 16, payslips |
| **Business owner** | Business registration (GST, company incorporation), ITR, CA certificate of income |
| **Student** | Enrollment letter, previous bank statements of parent/guardian |

### Purpose of Visit Documents

| Purpose | Documents |
|---------|----------|
| **Tourism** | Itinerary (day-by-day), hotel bookings (refundable), flight itinerary |
| **Family visit** | Sponsor's proof of status in UK (passport/visa), sponsor's bank statements, invitation letter |
| **Business meeting** | Invitation letter from UK company on letterhead, meeting details |
| **Medical** | Hospital appointment letter from UK, doctor letter from India explaining why UK treatment |

### Proof of Ties to India (Essential)

UK visa refusals frequently cite "not satisfied you'll return." Show:
- Property ownership (sale deed, Khata)
- Dependent family in India (spouse staying, children, parents)
- Permanent employment in India (employer letter stating this is temporary travel)
- Business commitments in India
- Financial investments in India

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Step 1: Apply Online at gov.uk

1. Go to [gov.uk/standard-visitor-visa](https://www.gov.uk/standard-visitor-visa)
2. Click "Apply from outside the UK"
3. Create a UKVI (UK Visas and Immigration) account
4. Fill the application form: personal details, travel history, purpose of visit, finances
5. Upload photo (during online process or at VFS)
6. Pay visa fee (£115) + IHS online
7. Get application reference number

### Step 2: Book Biometrics Appointment at VFS

After paying:
1. Go to [vfsglobal.com](https://www.vfsglobal.com) → UK → India
2. Book appointment at VFS centre in your city (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Ahmedabad, etc.)
3. Available slots typically within 1–3 weeks

### Step 3: Attend VFS Appointment

Bring to VFS:
- Application reference number (or IHS receipt)
- Passport (original)
- Printed photographs (VFS-spec if not uploaded online)
- All supporting documents (originals + copies)

VFS collects: biometrics (fingerprints + photo), documents, fee confirmation.

**Document upload**: UK now allows online document upload for many applicants — check if your application qualifies for online upload (skip in-person document submission).

### Step 4: Wait for Decision

Standard processing: **15 working days** (3 weeks). Priority service: 5 working days (+£500). Super priority: Next business day (+£1,000) — overkill for most applicants.

**Track**: Check UKVCAS portal or VFS tracking for status.

### Step 5: Receive Decision

If approved: Passport returned with a **vignette sticker** (entry clearance) valid for travel. On entry to UK, you get a stamp showing allowed duration.

**BRP (Biometric Residence Permit)**: Long-stay applicants (6+ months) get a BRP — collect at the named Post Office in the UK within 10 days of arrival. Visitor visas (up to 6 months) typically don't require BRP.

## UK vs Schengen Visa — Key Differences

| Aspect | UK Visa | Schengen Visa |
|--------|--------|--------------|
| Countries covered | UK only | 27 European countries |
| Can use for Europe | No | No (UK excluded) |
| Fee | £115 + IHS £235 = ~₹14,000 | €90 = ~₹8,200 |
| Processing | 15 working days | 15 working days |
| Max stay | 6 months per visit | 90 days in any 180 days |
| Insurance required | Not mandatory (but recommended) | Mandatory €30K |

**If you're visiting both UK and Europe**: You need BOTH a UK visa AND a Schengen visa (two separate applications, two fees).

## Common Reasons for UK Visa Rejection

| Rejection Reason | Prevention |
|-----------------|-----------|
| Insufficient financial evidence | Show 6 months of consistent statements, not just recent deposits |
| No genuine tourism intent | Detailed itinerary, confirmed accommodation |
| Unclear ties to India | Employment letter, property, family staying in India |
| Inconsistent information | Application details must match documents exactly |
| Previous UK/Schengen refusal not disclosed | Must declare all previous visa refusals |
| Overstay on previous visa (any country) | Consult immigration lawyer; hard to overcome |

## UK Entry Rules — What You Can and Cannot Do on Visitor Visa

**Allowed**:
- Tourism, sightseeing, visiting family/friends
- Attending business meetings, conferences, seminars
- Studying a short course (<6 months, not public funding)
- Medical treatment

**NOT allowed** on visitor visa:
- Paid work in the UK
- Claiming public funds
- Enrolling in a long course of study
- Setting up a business or selling goods/services directly

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I have a Schengen visa. Can I enter the UK with it?

No — the UK is not in the Schengen Area. Schengen visas do not grant UK entry. You need a separate UK Standard Visitor Visa.

### Is proof of return flight booking required?

Not strictly mandatory, but strongly recommended. Entry Clearance Officers want to see you have concrete plans to leave. Have a return flight itinerary (refundable booking is fine).

### Can I work remotely for my Indian employer while in the UK on visitor visa?

Remote work for an Indian employer (where you're employed in India, not providing services to a UK client) is generally considered acceptable under visitor visa — you're not working in the UK, just happening to be there. However, working for a UK employer or UK clients is not allowed.

### First trip to UK vs second. Is it easier to get visa again?

Having a clean track record (entered UK on previous visa, stayed as allowed, left on time) helps significantly. Second application with clean history is typically smoother.

### I was rejected by the UK before. Do I need to disclose it?

Yes — absolutely. The application form asks about previous UK visa refusals. Non-disclosure is a serious matter that can lead to future bans. Disclose and explain the circumstances.
