## What is PM Kisan Beneficiary Status?

PM Kisan Samman Nidhi provides **₹6,000 per year** in three installments of ₹2,000 to eligible farmer families. The beneficiary status check lets you verify whether you are registered, track your installment payments, and identify any issues blocking your payment.

This guide shows you how to check your status online and understand what each status means.

## How to Check Beneficiary Status Online

### Method 1: PM Kisan Portal

1. Visit [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Beneficiary Status"** in the menu (or go directly to [pmkisan.gov.in/BeneficiaryStatus.aspx](https://pmkisan.gov.in/BeneficiaryStatus.aspx))
3. Enter any one of the following:
   - **Aadhaar number**
   - **Registered mobile number**
   - **Bank account number**
4. Click **"Get Data"**
5. Your status and payment history will be displayed

### Method 2: PM Kisan Mobile App

1. Download the **PM Kisan** app from Google Play Store
2. Open the app and tap **"Beneficiary Status"**
3. Enter your Aadhaar number or mobile number
4. View your registration status and payment details

### Method 3: Through CSC or Agriculture Office

1. Visit your nearest **Common Service Centre (CSC)** or **District Agriculture Office**
2. Provide your Aadhaar number or registration details
3. The operator will check your status and help resolve any issues

## Understanding Your Status

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Registered** | Your application is in the system and verified |
| **Pending for approval at State/District level** | Land records verification is in progress by state authorities |
| **Rejected** | Application rejected — reason will be shown (ineligibility, duplicate, incorrect details) |
| **FTO is generated** | Fund Transfer Order created — payment is being processed |
| **Rft Signed by State** | State has approved the payment — money will be credited soon |
| **Payment Success** | ₹2,000 has been transferred to your bank account |
| **Payment Failure** | Transfer failed — usually due to bank account or Aadhaar linking issues |

## How to Check Installment History

On the beneficiary status page, you can see all past installments:

- **Installment number** (1st through current)
- **Amount** credited (₹2,000 per installment)
- **Date** of credit
- **Status** (Success/Failure/Pending)

If any installment shows **"Payment Failure"**, note the reason and take corrective action (see fixes below).

## Common Issues & Quick Fixes

### Status: "Pending for approval"
- Your land records are being verified by state authorities
- Contact your **local Patwari/Lekhpal** to expedite verification
- Ensure your **land mutation** is complete if you recently acquired land

### Status: "Rejected"
- Check the **rejection reason** on the status page
- Common reasons: government employee, income tax payer, institutional landholder
- If rejected incorrectly, contact your **District Agriculture Officer** with supporting documents

### Status: "Payment Failure"
- **Aadhaar not linked to bank account** — visit your bank and complete Aadhaar seeding
- **Bank account closed or inactive** — reactivate your account or update bank details
- **Name mismatch** — ensure Aadhaar and bank account names match exactly
- Update details via **"Updation of Self Registered Farmer"** on the portal

### No Record Found
- Your registration may not have been submitted successfully
- Re-register at [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in) under **"New Farmer Registration"**
- Or visit your local agriculture office for assisted registration

## Important Tips

1. **Check your status before each installment** to catch issues early
2. **Save your beneficiary status page** as a screenshot for reference
3. **Keep Aadhaar, bank, and land records consistent** — mismatches cause payment failures
4. **Complete eKYC** — it's mandatory and payments are blocked without it
5. **Use the grievance portal** on pmkisan.gov.in if your issue isn't resolved through normal channels

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How often should I check my PM Kisan status?

Check at least **once before each installment period** (April, August, December) to ensure there are no blocks on your payment.

### I can see "Payment Success" but money is not in my account. What do I do?

Payment processing takes **2-5 working days** after showing success. If it doesn't arrive within a week, contact your bank with the **transaction reference number** shown on the status page.

### Can I check someone else's PM Kisan status?

Yes, you can check any beneficiary's status if you have their **Aadhaar number, mobile number, or bank account number**. No authentication is required for status checks.

### How many installments have been released so far?

As of 2026, over **18 installments** have been released since the scheme started in February 2019. Check the PM Kisan portal homepage for the latest installment number.

### My status shows registered but I never received any payment. Why?

Possible reasons: **eKYC pending**, **land records not verified**, **Aadhaar-bank linking incomplete**, or **bank account issues**. Check each of these and fix the one causing the block.

### Can I update my bank account details on the portal?

Yes, use the **"Updation of Self Registered Farmer"** option on [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in). Enter your Aadhaar number, then update your bank account number and IFSC code.
