# NRI Renting Out Indian Property 2026 — Complete Tax and Management Guide

If you're an NRI with property in India, renting it out generates rental income — but with specific tax obligations for both you and your tenant. Here's everything about TDS, tax filing, repatriation, and managing property from abroad.

---

## TDS on Rent Paid to NRI — Tenant's Obligation

When a **tenant pays rent to an NRI landlord**, the tenant must deduct TDS:

| Rent Amount | TDS Rate | Section |
|------------|---------|---------|
| Any amount (no threshold) | **31.2%** (30% + 4% cess) | Section 195 |

**Key difference from resident landlord**: For resident Indian landlords, TDS on rent is 2–10% (Section 194-I). For NRI landlords, it jumps to 31.2%.

**Tenant's obligation**:
- Deduct 31.2% from rent before paying NRI landlord
- Pay TDS to IT department by 7th of following month
- File TDS return (Form 27Q) quarterly
- Issue TDS certificate (Form 16A) to NRI

**Example**: NRI charges ₹50,000/month rent
- Tenant deducts: ₹15,600 (31.2%)
- Tenant pays NRI: ₹34,400
- Tenant remits ₹15,600 to IT department

---

## Reducing TDS via Lower Deduction Certificate

If 31.2% TDS is too high relative to your actual tax liability, you can get a **Lower Deduction Certificate** (Section 197):

1. Apply at incometax.gov.in → Services → Lower Deduction Certificate (Form 13)
2. Show your actual income, expected tax, and request lower TDS rate
3. IT officer may approve (e.g., 10% or 15% TDS instead of 31.2%)
4. Provide approved certificate to tenant → they deduct TDS at the lower approved rate

**Also useful**: If your total income from India is below taxable threshold — get Nil deduction certificate.

## How the Rental Income Flows — NRO Account

All rental income from India must be credited to your **NRO (Non-Resident Ordinary) Account**:

| Flow | Details |
|------|---------|
| Tenant pays net rent → NRO account | ₹34,400 in the example above |
| TDS certificate received | From tenant (Form 16A) |
| Rental income declared in ITR | File ITR-2 as NRI |
| Tax paid on rental income | Slab rate (after TDS credit) |
| Excess TDS refund | Credited to NRO account |

**NRO Interest**: NRO account interest itself is taxed at 30% TDS — separate from rental income.

## NRI Tax Filing for Rental Income

**Which ITR**: ITR-2 (for NRIs with rental income and/or capital gains)

**Tax on rental income**:
- Taxable as "Income from House Property"
- Standard deduction: 30% of rental income (regardless of actual maintenance cost)
- If home loan on property: Interest deduction up to actual interest paid (no limit for let-out property)
- Net income taxed at slab rate for NRI

**NRI Tax Rate (New Regime for NRI)**:
- NRIs cannot use the ₹12L zero-tax rebate (Section 87A)
- Tax starts from first rupee at applicable slab

**Example**:
- Annual rent: ₹6L
- Standard deduction (30%): ₹1.8L
- Home loan interest: ₹2L (if any)
- Taxable rental income: ₹6L - ₹1.8L - ₹2L = ₹2.2L
- Tax at 5% slab: ₹11,000
- TDS already paid: ₹1,87,200 (31.2% of ₹6L)
- Refund due: ₹1,87,200 - ₹11,000 = ₹1,76,200

File ITR to claim this refund — it's credited to your NRO account.

## Repatriating Rental Income Abroad

Money in NRO account can be repatriated (sent abroad) subject to:
- **Limit**: USD 1 million per financial year (April–March)
- **Process**: Contact your bank; submit Form 15CA (self-declaration) + Form 15CB (CA certificate for amounts > ₹7.5L per transaction)

**What you can repatriate**:
- Rental income after Indian taxes
- FD interest from NRO (after TDS)
- Sale proceeds from property (after taxes + documentary proof)

**What you need**:
- Form 15CA: Filled online at incometax.gov.in
- Form 15CB: Signed by a Chartered Accountant, certifying taxes have been paid
- Bank forwards both forms to complete the wire transfer

## FEMA Compliance — What NRIs Must Know

**FEMA (Foreign Exchange Management Act)** governs NRI property transactions:

| Transaction | FEMA Requirement |
|-------------|----------------|
| Receiving rent | Must go to NRO account |
| Repatriation | USD 1M limit/year, Forms 15CA+15CB |
| Selling property | Proceeds go to NRO first |
| Buying property in India | Permitted (FEMA allows NRIs to buy residential property) |

**You cannot**: Credit rental income directly to foreign account or NRE account (only income earned abroad can go to NRE).

## Managing Property from Abroad — Practical Options

### Option 1: Trusted Family Member as Caretaker

Most common arrangement:
- Family member (parent, sibling) collects rent, handles minor repairs
- Power of Attorney (POA) given to family member for maintenance decisions
- Wire rent to NRO account after deduction

**Limitation**: Family members can't always handle tenant disputes, maintenance coordination, or legal issues professionally.

### Option 2: Professional Property Management Company

Many cities have dedicated NRI property management services:
- Monthly fee: 5–10% of rental income (or fixed monthly fee ₹1,500–5,000)
- Services: Tenant sourcing, rent collection, maintenance coordination, TDS handling, utility payments
- Platforms: NoBroker, Square Yards Property Management, local agencies in Bangalore/Mumbai/Hyderabad

**For NRIs with high-value property or living far from family**: Professional management is worth the fee.

### Option 3: Leave Property Managed by Tenant (Long Lease)

Some NRIs enter 3–5 year lease agreements with:
- Higher security deposit (6–12 months rent)
- Tenant responsible for minor maintenance
- Professional lease documentation reducing disputes

**Limitation**: Less flexibility to revise rent or recover property if needed before lease end.

## Challenges NRI Landlords Face

| Challenge | Solution |
|-----------|---------|
| Tenant refuses to vacate | Rent Control Court / Civil Court for eviction (can take 1–3 years) — draft strong leave-and-license agreement |
| Rent not paid | Clause in agreement for eviction; send legal notice via advocate |
| Property encroachment | Local police + civil court injunction; needs physical presence or strong POA holder |
| TDS compliance by tenant | Educate tenant at lease signing; include TDS clause in agreement |
| Municipal tax payments | Set up online payment with tenant/family member |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My tenant says he doesn't know how to deduct TDS. What do I do?

Explain that TDS deduction is mandatory for NRI landlords and the tenant is legally responsible. Provide them with your PAN card, NRO account details, and information on filing Form 27Q. If they're a company/business, their accountant will know how to do it.

### I'm NRI but my PAN is not updated. Can I still receive rent?

Rent can be credited to NRO even without updated PAN, but for TDS reconciliation and ITR filing, your PAN must be linked to your current status. If your PAN shows you as resident, update via AO or Aadhaar linking.

### Can I just not declare rental income and hope for the best?

Not advisable. The Income Tax Department has access to Form 27Q filed by your tenant showing payments made to you. Non-declaration invites notices, penalties (50–200% of tax), and interest. File ITR and claim refund of excess TDS — you likely owe no or minimal tax.

### Is it better to keep the property in India or sell?

That's a financial and personal decision beyond the scope of this guide. Consider: rental yield (rent/property value × 100 — typically 2–3% in India), capital appreciation potential, hassle of NRI management, your plans to return to India, and tax efficiency of sale (capital gains) vs holding.
