## What is PM Matsya Sampada Yojana?

**Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY)** is the Government of India's flagship fisheries scheme to transform India's fisheries sector — boosting fish production, fishermen's income, and creating 55 lakh employment opportunities.

Key features:
- **40–60% subsidy** on fisheries activities (60% for SC/ST/women)
- Covers: aquaculture ponds, fish farming equipment, fish feed mills, cold storage, boats, fishing nets
- Budget: **₹20,050 crore** (2020–2025)
- Implemented by state fisheries departments
- Both freshwater and marine fisheries covered

## Subsidy Structure

| Beneficiary Category | Subsidy (Central + State) |
|---|---|
| General category | **40%** of project cost |
| SC / ST / Women | **60%** of project cost |
| Farmer Cooperatives / SHGs | **60%** |

The remaining cost is borne by the beneficiary — either from savings or via bank loan (MUDRA, NABARD, or commercial bank loan).

## Activities Covered Under PMMSY

| Activity | What's Funded |
|---|---|
| **Fish pond construction** | New freshwater aquaculture ponds |
| **Cage culture** | Net cages in rivers/reservoirs for fish |
| **Shrimp/prawn farming** | Brackish water aquaculture |
| **Recirculating Aquaculture Systems (RAS)** | Indoor high-density fish farming |
| **Fishing boats** | Motorised boats for marine fishermen |
| **Fishing nets** | Gill nets, seine nets, cast nets |
| **Cold storage / ice plants** | Post-harvest infrastructure |
| **Fish feed mill** | Feed manufacturing for aquaculture |
| **Ornamental fish** | Aquarium fish production |
| **Seaweed cultivation** | Coastal seaweed farming |
| **Fish retail outlets (kiosks)** | Fish market infrastructure |

## Who is Eligible?

- **Fishermen** — both inland (freshwater) and marine fishers
- **Fish farmers** — individuals with land for aquaculture ponds
- **SC/ST/women fishers** (get higher subsidy)
- **Self Help Groups (SHGs)** of fisherfolk
- **Cooperatives** of fishermen
- **Entrepreneurs** setting up fish processing units
- **FPOs (Farmer Producer Organizations)** in fisheries sector

**Landholding or lease document** for the land where fish pond/activity is planned is needed.

## How to Apply — Step by Step

PMMSY is implemented by state fisheries departments:

1. Visit your **State Fisheries Department** website or office:
   - AP: [apfisheries.gov.in](https://apfisheries.gov.in)
   - WB: [wbfisheries.gov.in](https://wbfisheries.gov.in)
   - Odisha: [fisheries.odisha.gov.in](https://fisheries.odisha.gov.in)
   - UP: [upfisheries.gov.in](https://upfisheries.gov.in)
   - Bihar: [fisheries.bihar.gov.in](https://fisheries.bihar.gov.in)

2. Find the **PMMSY scheme** section
3. Fill the **application form** — activity type, land details, project cost estimate
4. Attach supporting documents
5. Submit to **District Fisheries Officer (DFO)**
6. DFO inspects and recommends to state fisheries department
7. If approved: subsidy released after completion / in stages

**OR apply via the central PMMSY portal:**
[pmmsy.dof.gov.in](https://pmmsy.dof.gov.in) — check your state's online application option.

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card**
2. **Land records** (patta/khasra/khatauni) or **lease agreement** for land to be used
3. **Caste certificate** (SC/ST for higher subsidy)
4. **Bank account details** (for subsidy credit)
5. **Project proposal** — description of proposed fish farming activity
6. **Fishing license / fisherman card** (for marine fishers)
7. Photographs of the land/existing facility

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I don't own land but want to do fish farming on leased land. Am I eligible?
Yes — leased land with a registered lease agreement of at least 7 years (in most states) is acceptable for PMMSY pond construction. Check your state fisheries department for the minimum lease period required.

### Can I get both PMMSY and MUDRA loan?
Yes — PMMSY is a subsidy (reduces project cost), while MUDRA is a loan (covers remaining cost). You can use MUDRA/NABARD loan for the non-subsidised portion of the project.

### My application was approved but subsidy not released. What to do?
Visit your **District Fisheries Officer (DFO)** office with your approval letter. Delays are common — follow up persistently. Also check if the pond/activity has been inspected and certified as complete by the DFO.

### Is PMMSY available for ornamental fish (aquarium fish)?
Yes — ornamental fish culture is specifically listed as an eligible activity under PMMSY.
