# CIBIL Score — Check Free, Understand & Improve (2026 Guide)

Your **CIBIL score** is a 3-digit number (300–900) that determines whether you get a loan, credit card, or home loan — and at what interest rate. Banks check it in seconds before approving any credit.

A score of **750+** gets you the best rates. Below **650**, most banks will reject you.

This guide shows you how to check your score for free and exactly how to improve it.

---

## How to Check CIBIL Score for Free (2 Minutes)

### Method 1: TransUnion CIBIL Official Website

1. Go to **[freecibil.com](https://www.freecibil.com)** (official free check — 1 per year free)
2. Click **"Get Free CIBIL Score"**
3. Enter PAN card number, date of birth, mobile
4. Verify OTP
5. View your score + credit report

*Free once per year on the official site. For more frequent checks, use the methods below.*

### Method 2: Paisabazaar (Free, Unlimited)

1. Go to **[paisabazaar.com/free-credit-score](https://www.paisabazaar.com/free-credit-score/)**
2. Enter mobile number, PAN, date of birth
3. Get instant free credit score (soft inquiry — does NOT affect score)

### Method 3: BankBazaar (Free)

1. Go to **[bankbazaar.com/cibil-score.html](https://www.bankbazaar.com/cibil-score.html)**
2. Enter PAN + basic details
3. Get credit score instantly (also soft inquiry)

### Method 4: Through Your Bank App

Most major banks now show your credit score free inside their mobile app:
- **HDFC Bank app** → Account → Credit Score
- **SBI YONO** → Financial Health → Credit Score
- **Kotak Mobile Banking** → Tools → Credit Score
- **Axis Bank app** → Products → Free Credit Score

## Understanding Your CIBIL Score

| Score Range | Rating | What It Means |
|-------------|--------|---------------|
| **750–900** | Excellent | Best loan rates, instant approvals |
| **700–749** | Good | Most loans approved, slightly higher rates |
| **650–699** | Fair | Loans possible but at higher rates |
| **600–649** | Poor | Difficult to get loans; NBFC possible at high rates |
| **300–599** | Very Poor | Most banks reject; secured credit card as starting point |
| **-1 or NH** | No History | No credit history (first time) |

## 7 Ways to Improve CIBIL Score Fast

### 1. Pay ALL Bills on Time (Impact: High, Timeline: 3–6 months)
Set up auto-pay for every credit card and EMI. Even one missed payment drops score by 50–100 points. Payment history is 35% of the score.

### 2. Reduce Credit Card Utilisation to Under 30% (Impact: High, Timeline: 1–2 months)
If your credit limit is ₹1 lakh, keep outstanding balance under ₹30,000. If you regularly use more, request a credit limit increase from the bank (without using the extra limit).

### 3. Don't Close Old Credit Cards (Impact: Medium, Timeline: Immediate)
Closing an old credit card reduces your total credit limit + removes credit history. Keep old cards active with minimal spend (even ₹100/month on OTT subscription).

### 4. Stop Applying for Multiple Loans/Cards (Impact: Medium, Timeline: 3–6 months)
Each loan/card application = hard inquiry on CIBIL = -5 to -15 points. If your score is low, don't apply for 6 months while improving it.

### 5. Get a Secured Credit Card (If No Credit History or Very Low Score) (Impact: High, Timeline: 6–12 months)
Secured credit cards are issued against an FD (Fixed Deposit). Even with a bad score, you'll get one. Use it for small purchases and pay full balance monthly. ICICI, SBI, Kotak offer secured cards.

### 6. Dispute Errors in CIBIL Report (Impact: High if errors exist, Timeline: 30–45 days)
1. Check full CIBIL report (not just score) at freecibil.com
2. Look for loans/credit cards you never took, wrong payment status, settled accounts marked as active
3. File dispute at freecibil.com → Dispute Resolution
4. CIBIL has 30 days to investigate and correct

### 7. Maintain Credit Mix (Impact: Low, Timeline: Long-term)
Having both a home/car loan (secured) + credit card (unsecured) is better than only credit cards. If you only have credit cards, taking a small personal loan and repaying diligently can help.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is checking your own CIBIL score free?**
Yes — TransUnion CIBIL allows 1 free check per year at freecibil.com. Unlimited free checks available via Paisabazaar, BankBazaar, Groww, Paytm (soft inquiry — does not affect score).

**Does checking CIBIL score reduce it?**
No — checking your own score (soft inquiry) does NOT reduce it. Only hard inquiries (when a bank checks your score after a loan/card application) reduce it.

**What is the difference between CIBIL and credit score?**
CIBIL (TransUnion CIBIL) is the most widely used credit bureau in India. Credit score refers to the same concept — a 3-digit rating of creditworthiness. Other bureaus in India: Experian, Equifax, CRIF HighMark.

**Can I get a loan with a CIBIL score of 650?**
Possible but hard. Some NBFCs (Bajaj Finance, IIFL, Tata Capital) approve loans for 650 scores at higher interest rates (14%–20%+). Work on improving score first if not urgent.

**My CIBIL score shows -1 — what does that mean?**
-1 or NH (No History) means you have no credit history. You've never taken a loan or credit card. Solution: Get a secured credit card against FD, use it for 6 months, and you'll get a credit score.
