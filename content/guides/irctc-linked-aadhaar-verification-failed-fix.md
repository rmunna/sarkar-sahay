# IRCTC Aadhaar Verification Failed? Fix Linking Errors

IRCTC requires Aadhaar verification for certain services — booking more than 6 tickets per month, Tatkal bookings from some quotas, and identity verification. If your Aadhaar linking or verification is failing, this guide covers every common error and its fix.

## Why IRCTC Asks for Aadhaar Verification

- **Book more than 6 tickets per month** — Aadhaar-verified users get a higher booking limit (up to 12 tickets/month)
- **Tatkal booking** — some quotas require Aadhaar-verified identity
- **Agent/corporate bookings** — mandatory for authorized agents
- **Identity verification** — reduces fraud and duplicate accounts

## Common Aadhaar Verification Errors on IRCTC

- **"Aadhaar verification failed"** — authentication did not complete
- **"Name mismatch — details do not match"** — name on IRCTC differs from Aadhaar
- **"Aadhaar OTP not received"** — OTP from UIDAI not arriving
- **"Aadhaar already linked to another account"** — duplicate linking
- **"Authentication failed — please try again"** — UIDAI server timeout
- **"Invalid Aadhaar number"** — wrong number entered
- Page hangs after clicking "Verify"

## Fix 1: Name Mismatch Between IRCTC and Aadhaar

This is the most common reason for verification failure. IRCTC matches your name exactly with UIDAI records.

1. **Check your Aadhaar name** — download your e-Aadhaar from [uidai.gov.in](https://uidai.gov.in/) and verify the exact spelling
2. **Update your IRCTC profile** to match your Aadhaar name exactly:
   - Log in to IRCTC → My Profile → Update Profile
   - Change First Name, Last Name to match Aadhaar **exactly** (including spelling, spaces, initials)
   - Example: If Aadhaar says "RAJKUMAR S" and IRCTC has "Raj Kumar Singh" — update IRCTC to "RAJKUMAR S"
3. **If your Aadhaar name is wrong**, update it at UIDAI:
   - Visit [ssup.uidai.gov.in](https://ssup.uidai.gov.in/) for online name correction
   - Or visit your nearest Aadhaar Enrolment Centre
   - See our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) for detailed name update steps
4. After updating either IRCTC or Aadhaar, **wait 24-48 hours** and retry verification

> **Tip:** UIDAI matching is case-insensitive but exact on spelling and initials. "Suresh Kumar" ≠ "S Kumar" ≠ "Suresh K".

## Fix 2: Aadhaar OTP Not Coming

The OTP for Aadhaar verification is sent by UIDAI (not IRCTC) to your **Aadhaar-registered mobile number**.

1. Make sure your **mobile number is linked to Aadhaar** — this is the number you registered at the Aadhaar centre, NOT your IRCTC mobile
2. To check/update your Aadhaar mobile:
   - Visit the nearest **Aadhaar Enrolment Centre** (this cannot be done online)
   - Carry your Aadhaar card and any ID proof
3. If your mobile is correct but OTP isn't coming:
   - **Wait 2-3 minutes** — UIDAI OTPs can be delayed
   - Check if **DND is active** on your number — send "STOP DND" to 1909
   - Make sure you have **network signal** on the Aadhaar-linked number
   - Click **"Resend OTP"** (you get limited attempts)
4. UIDAI servers may be down — try again after 30 minutes or during off-peak hours
5. If your number is not linked to Aadhaar at all, you **must visit an Aadhaar centre** to link it first

## Fix 3: Aadhaar Already Linked to Another Account

If IRCTC says your Aadhaar is already linked to another account:

1. **You may have an older IRCTC account** — your Aadhaar might be linked there
2. Try the **"Forgot Username"** option to find your other account
3. If you want to move Aadhaar to your current account:
   - First, **unlink Aadhaar from the old account** (log in to the old account → My Profile → remove Aadhaar)
   - Then link it to your new account
4. If you **can't access the old account**:
   - Email **care@irctc.co.in** explaining the issue
   - Provide: Your current IRCTC username, Aadhaar number (masked), and a request to delink from the old account
   - Call **139** for assistance
5. IRCTC allows only **one Aadhaar per account** and **one account per Aadhaar**

## Fix 4: UIDAI Authentication Server Error

Sometimes the error is on UIDAI's side, not IRCTC:

1. **Try again after 30 minutes** — UIDAI servers may be temporarily overloaded
2. Avoid trying during **peak hours** (10 AM – 2 PM on weekdays)
3. Try during **early morning (6-8 AM) or late night** for best results
4. Check UIDAI server status — if other Aadhaar services are also down, it's a server issue
5. Clear browser cache and try in an **incognito window**
6. Use the **IRCTC website** instead of the app (or vice versa) — sometimes one works when the other doesn't

## Fix 5: Invalid Aadhaar Number

1. **Double-check your 12-digit Aadhaar number** — no spaces or extra digits
2. Make sure you're entering Aadhaar (12 digits), not EID/Enrollment number (14 digits)
3. Download your **e-Aadhaar** from [uidai.gov.in](https://uidai.gov.in/) to verify the correct number
4. If your Aadhaar was **deactivated** (rare), contact UIDAI at 1947

## Step-by-Step: How to Link Aadhaar to IRCTC

1. Log in to [irctc.co.in](https://www.irctc.co.in/)
2. Go to **My Profile** (click on your username → My Profile)
3. Scroll down to the **Aadhaar KYC** section
4. Enter your **12-digit Aadhaar number**
5. Click **"Send OTP"** — an OTP will be sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile
6. Enter the OTP and click **"Verify"**
7. If successful, your profile will show **"Aadhaar Verified"**

## Important Tips

- Your IRCTC name must **exactly match** your Aadhaar name — this is the #1 cause of failure
- Aadhaar OTP goes to your **Aadhaar-registered mobile**, not your IRCTC mobile (they may be different)
- Each Aadhaar can be linked to only **one IRCTC account**
- After verification, you can book up to **12 tickets per month** instead of 6
- If Aadhaar verification keeps failing, you can still book tickets — just with the standard 6-ticket monthly limit

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Is Aadhaar verification mandatory for IRCTC?
No. Aadhaar verification is optional for regular users. It's required only if you want to book more than 6 tickets per month or use certain special quotas.

### Q2: My Aadhaar name and IRCTC name are slightly different. What should I do?
Update your IRCTC profile name to exactly match your Aadhaar name. Even small differences like initials (S. Kumar vs Suresh Kumar) will cause verification to fail.

### Q3: Can I link the same Aadhaar to two IRCTC accounts?
No. One Aadhaar can be linked to only one IRCTC account. You need to unlink it from the old account before linking to a new one.

### Q4: The Aadhaar OTP goes to my old mobile number. How do I change it?
Aadhaar mobile number can only be updated by visiting an Aadhaar Enrolment Centre in person. It cannot be changed online. Carry your Aadhaar card and an ID proof.

### Q5: How long does Aadhaar verification take on IRCTC?
If everything matches, verification is instant (takes under a minute). If there are name mismatches or server issues, you may need to fix those first and retry.

### Q6: I verified Aadhaar but still can't book more than 6 tickets. Why?
After verification, it may take **24-48 hours** for the enhanced limit to activate. If it still doesn't work after 48 hours, contact care@irctc.co.in.

### Q7: Can I remove Aadhaar from my IRCTC account?
Yes. Go to My Profile on IRCTC and look for the option to remove Aadhaar KYC. Your booking limit will revert to 6 tickets per month.
