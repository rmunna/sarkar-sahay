## What is UP Ration Card?

A ration card in Uttar Pradesh is an official document issued by the Department of Food & Civil Supplies that entitles eligible families to buy subsidised food grains (wheat, rice, sugar) from Fair Price Shops (FPS) under the National Food Security Act (NFSA), 2013.

UP ration cards are categorised into:
- **Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY)** — For the poorest families; 35 kg food grains per month
- **Priority Household (PHH)** — BPL families; 5 kg per person per month
- **APL (Above Poverty Line)** — General category; limited entitlements

The ration card also serves as an important identity and address proof document for various government services.

## Who is Eligible?

- Any family residing in Uttar Pradesh
- Must not already hold a ration card in any other state
- **AAY card**: Families with no regular income, widows, disabled persons, elderly without support, primitive tribal households
- **PHH card**: Families meeting BPL criteria based on SECC data
- Annual family income below the prescribed limit set by the state government
- All family members' Aadhaar must be linked

## Documents Required

### For New Ration Card Application
- **Aadhaar card** of all family members
- **Address proof** — Electricity bill, water bill, rent agreement, or voter ID
- **Income certificate** — From tehsil or self-declaration
- **Passport-size photographs** of head of family
- **Bank account details** (for DBT linkage)
- **Family details** — Name, age, gender, relationship of all members
- **Mobile number** linked to Aadhaar

### For Adding/Removing Members
- Aadhaar card of the member to be added/removed
- Marriage certificate (for adding spouse)
- Death certificate (for removing deceased member)
- Birth certificate (for adding newborn)

## How to Apply for UP Ration Card Online (Step-by-Step)

### Step 1: Visit the Official Portal

Go to [fcs.up.gov.in](https://fcs.up.gov.in) — the official Food & Civil Supplies Department portal.

### Step 2: Click on Online Application

On the homepage, click **"खाद्य एवं रसद विभाग"** and look for the ration card application link, or go to the **"ऑनलाइन आवेदन करें"** section.

### Step 3: Register/Login

- New users: Register with your mobile number and create an account
- Existing users: Login with your credentials

### Step 4: Fill the Application Form

Enter the following details:
1. **District, tehsil, town/village** selection
2. Head of family details — name, father's name, Aadhaar number, mobile
3. **All family members' details** — name, age, gender, Aadhaar, relationship
4. Address details
5. Income details and category selection (AAY/PHH)

### Step 5: Upload Documents

Upload scanned copies of:
- Aadhaar card (front & back)
- Passport photo
- Address proof
- Income certificate

### Step 6: Submit Application

Review all details carefully and submit. Note down your **application reference number** for tracking.

### Step 7: Verification

- The Supply Inspector (आपूर्ति निरीक्षक) will verify your application
- A physical verification of your residence may be conducted
- After verification, the card is approved and issued

## How to Check UP Ration Card Status Online

### Method 1: Via FCS Portal

1. Visit [fcs.up.gov.in](https://fcs.up.gov.in)
2. Click on **"राशन कार्ड की पात्रता सूची"** (Ration Card Eligibility List)
3. Select your **district → block/town → FPS (fair price shop)**
4. View the list of ration card holders
5. Search for your name in the list

### Method 2: Check NFSA List

1. Visit [nfsa.up.gov.in](https://nfsa.up.gov.in)
2. Select your **district → block → gram panchayat/town**
3. View the NFSA beneficiary list
4. Check if your family is included with ration card number

### Method 3: By Ration Card Number

1. Visit [fcs.up.gov.in](https://fcs.up.gov.in)
2. Go to **"राशन कार्ड खोजें"** (Search Ration Card)
3. Enter your ration card number
4. View card details including members and entitlements

## Ration Entitlements Under NFSA in UP

| Category | Rice | Wheat | Sugar |
|----------|------|-------|-------|
| AAY | 35 kg/family/month | At ₹3/kg | 1 kg at subsidised rate |
| PHH | 5 kg/person/month | At ₹2/kg (wheat), ₹3/kg (rice) | As per availability |

## Processing Time

- Online application: **15–30 days** after submission
- Verification by Supply Inspector: **7–15 days**
- Total time from application to card issuance: **30–45 days** (approximate)

## How to Download UP Ration Card

1. Visit [fcs.up.gov.in](https://fcs.up.gov.in)
2. Navigate to **"राशन कार्ड डाउनलोड करें"** section
3. Enter your ration card number or search by name
4. Download and print your e-ration card

## Important Tips

1. **Link Aadhaar** — All family members' Aadhaar must be seeded with the ration card for biometric authentication at FPS.
2. **Update mobile number** — Keep your registered mobile number updated for OTP verification during ration collection.
3. **Check entitlements monthly** — Verify your monthly entitlement on the portal before visiting the FPS.
4. **Report issues** — If you face problems at the Fair Price Shop (short weighing, denial), complain on the portal or call the helpline **1967**.
5. **One nation one ration card** — UP is part of the ONORC scheme. You can collect rations from any FPS in India using Aadhaar-based authentication.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How can I apply for a new ration card in UP?
Visit [fcs.up.gov.in](https://fcs.up.gov.in) and apply online by registering with your mobile number, filling the application form with family details, uploading documents, and submitting. You can also apply offline at the block/tehsil food supply office.

### How do I check if my name is in the UP ration card list?
Visit [nfsa.up.gov.in](https://nfsa.up.gov.in), select your district, block, and gram panchayat/town. The beneficiary list will show all eligible ration card holders in your area.

### What documents are needed for UP ration card?
You need Aadhaar cards of all family members, address proof, income certificate, passport photo of the family head, and bank account details.

### Can I get ration without a ration card in UP?
Under emergency provisions, the government may distribute free rations. However, for regular monthly entitlements, a valid ration card under NFSA is required.

### How to add a new member to my UP ration card?
Login to [fcs.up.gov.in](https://fcs.up.gov.in), go to the modification section, select "Add Member," enter the new member's details and Aadhaar number, and submit. The change will be verified by the Supply Inspector.

### What is the helpline for UP ration card complaints?
You can call the toll-free helpline **1967** or **1800-1800-150** for ration card-related complaints in Uttar Pradesh.

### Can I use my UP ration card in another state?
Yes, under the One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC) scheme, you can collect your entitled ration from any Fair Price Shop across India using Aadhaar-based biometric authentication.
