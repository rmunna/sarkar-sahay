# Vidyasiri Scholarship Karnataka – For SC/ST/OBC Students

## What is Vidyasiri Scholarship?

Vidyasiri is a post-matric scholarship scheme by the Government of Karnataka for students belonging to **Scheduled Castes (SC), Scheduled Tribes (ST), and Other Backward Classes (OBC)**. The scheme provides financial assistance to cover tuition fees, maintenance allowance, and hostel charges for students pursuing higher education (post-10th standard) in recognized institutions.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Category:** Must belong to **SC, ST, or OBC** (Category 1, 2A, 2B, 3A, 3B)
2. **Education:** Must be studying in **post-matric courses** (Class 11, 12, degree, PG, professional courses)
3. **Residency:** Must be a **domicile of Karnataka**
4. **Income:** Annual family income must be within prescribed limits:
   - SC/ST: Up to **₹2.5 lakh per annum**
   - OBC: Up to **₹1 lakh per annum** (varies by sub-category)
5. **Attendance:** Must maintain **minimum 75% attendance**
6. **Institution:** Must be enrolled in a **recognized government or private institution** in Karnataka

## Scholarship Amount

| Course Level | Maintenance Allowance (Day Scholar) | Maintenance Allowance (Hosteller) |
|-------------|-------------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Class 11-12 | ₹1,200/year | ₹3,000/year |
| Graduation | ₹1,500/year | ₹3,500/year |
| Post-Graduation | ₹2,000/year | ₹4,000/year |
| Professional Courses | ₹2,500/year | ₹5,000/year |

*Note: Actual amounts may vary. Tuition fees and exam fees are covered separately based on actuals.*

## Documents Required

- **Caste Certificate** (SC/ST/OBC)
- **Income Certificate** (issued by Tahsildar)
- **Previous Year Marks Card** (10th or qualifying exam)
- **Aadhaar Card**
- **Bank Account Passbook** (student's own account, linked to Aadhaar)
- **College Admission Letter / Bonafide Certificate**
- **Ration Card** (for address proof)
- **Passport Size Photo**
- **Domicile Certificate** (if required)

## How to Apply for Vidyasiri Scholarship

### Online Application

1. **Visit** the SSP (State Scholarship Portal) at [ssp.postmatric.karnataka.gov.in](https://ssp.postmatric.karnataka.gov.in)
2. **Register** using your Aadhaar number and mobile number
3. **Login** with your credentials
4. **Select** the Vidyasiri / Post-Matric Scholarship scheme
5. **Fill** personal details — name, address, caste, income
6. **Enter** educational details — course, college, year of study
7. **Upload** documents — caste certificate, income certificate, marks card, photo
8. **Enter** bank account details
9. **Submit** the application
10. **Print** the application and submit a copy to your college

### Through College

- Many colleges facilitate the application process
- Submit your documents to the college's scholarship cell
- The college verifies and forwards applications to the department

## Verification & Disbursement

1. College verifies the application and attendance
2. District-level Social Welfare department verifies caste and income certificates
3. After verification, scholarship amount is credited to student's bank account via DBT

## Processing Time

- **Application Window:** Usually July–October (check annual notification)
- **Verification:** 1-2 months after submission
- **Disbursement:** 3-6 months after verification

## Important Tips

1. **Apply Within Deadline:** The scholarship has an annual application window. Missing it means waiting another year.
2. **Keep Attendance Above 75%:** Low attendance can disqualify you from receiving the scholarship.
3. **Bank Account Must Be in Student's Name:** Joint accounts or parent's accounts are not accepted.
4. **Renewal Required:** You must reapply every academic year — it's not auto-renewed.
5. **Income Certificate Validity:** Get a fresh income certificate each year from the Tahsildar's office.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Can I apply if I'm studying in a private college?
**Yes**, provided the college is recognized by the university and the government.

### Q2: Is Vidyasiri only for Karnataka students studying in Karnataka?
**Yes**, generally the student must be studying in a recognized institution within Karnataka.

### Q3: Can I get Vidyasiri if I already receive another scholarship?
**No.** You cannot receive two government scholarships simultaneously. You must choose one.

### Q4: What if my income certificate is pending?
Apply with the previous year's income certificate and update it once the new one is issued.

### Q5: I missed the deadline. Can I still apply?
Usually **no**. The portal closes after the deadline. Wait for the next academic year's notification.

### Q6: Do engineering and medical students also get this scholarship?
**Yes.** Professional course students are eligible and may receive higher maintenance allowance and full tuition fee coverage.
