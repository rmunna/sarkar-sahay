## What is mParivahan?

mParivahan is the official mobile app by the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH), Government of India. It lets you carry a virtual Driving Licence (DL) and Registration Certificate (RC) on your phone, search vehicle information, and access transport-related services digitally.

The app connects to the national Vahan and Sarathi databases and is available on both Android and iOS. Under the Motor Vehicles (Amendment) Act 2019, the digital DL and RC displayed through mParivahan or DigiLocker are legally valid and must be accepted by traffic police during checks.

Despite being widely used, the app often runs into issues — crashes, login failures, DL/RC not loading, and "no data available" errors. This guide covers all common problems and how to fix them.

---

## 1. mParivahan App Crashes on Launch or Login Failure

**Symptoms:** App closes immediately after opening, login OTP not received, or stuck on loading screen.

### How to Fix

1. **Update the app** — Go to Google Play Store or Apple App Store and check for updates. Outdated versions often crash.
2. **Clear app cache and data:**
   - Android: Settings → Apps → mParivahan → Storage → Clear Cache → Clear Data
   - iOS: Uninstall and reinstall the app
3. **Check your internet connection** — The app requires a stable connection to fetch data from government servers.
4. **Try after some time** — Government servers (Vahan/Sarathi) face heavy traffic, especially during peak hours (10 AM – 2 PM). Try early morning or late evening.
5. **Check phone storage** — Ensure at least 200 MB free space on your device.
6. **Restart your phone** — A simple restart can fix many app glitches.

> **OTP not received?** Make sure your mobile number is linked to your DL/RC records at the RTO. If you changed your number, update it at your RTO office first.

## 2. Driving Licence (DL) Not Showing in mParivahan

**Symptoms:** You've logged in but your DL doesn't appear, or it says "No DL found."

### Why This Happens

- Your DL data hasn't been digitized by the issuing RTO
- The mobile number registered with RTO doesn't match your mParivahan login number
- Your state's Sarathi database hasn't synced with the national database
- DL was issued very recently and hasn't appeared in the system yet

### How to Fix

1. **Verify your DL number format** — Enter the exact DL number as printed on your physical licence (e.g., KA01 20190012345).
2. **Check on Parivahan website first** — Visit [https://parivahan.gov.in/parivahan/](https://parivahan.gov.in/parivahan/) → "Know Your DL" → Enter DL number and date of birth. If it doesn't show here, the problem is with your RTO's records, not the app.
3. **Link via DigiLocker** — Open [DigiLocker](https://digilocker.gov.in), search for "Driving Licence" under issued documents, and pull your DL. Once fetched in DigiLocker, it often syncs with mParivahan.
4. **Contact your RTO** — If your DL doesn't appear anywhere online, visit your issuing RTO with your physical DL and request digitization or data correction.
5. **Use the correct mobile number** — Log into mParivahan with the same mobile number that is registered with your RTO.

> **Tip:** Some older DLs (pre-2010) from certain states are not yet digitized. You may need to get a duplicate/renewed DL to get it in the digital system. See our [Driving Licence guide](/guide/driving-licence-online-apply) for the renewal process.

## 3. RC (Registration Certificate) Not Found

**Symptoms:** Searching for your vehicle's RC shows "No data found" or "Vehicle not found."

### How to Fix

1. **Enter the correct registration number** — Use the format exactly as on your RC (e.g., MH12AB1234). No spaces, no dashes.
2. **Check on Vahan website** — Go to [https://vahan.parivahan.gov.in/vahan4dashboard/](https://vahan.parivahan.gov.in/vahan4dashboard/) and search your vehicle number. If it doesn't show here, the data is not in the national Vahan database.
3. **New vehicle?** — If your vehicle was registered very recently (within 1-2 weeks), the data may not have synced yet. Wait a few days and try again.
4. **State not migrated** — A few RTOs still haven't fully migrated to the Vahan 4.0 system. Contact your RTO to check.
5. **Second-hand vehicle** — If ownership was recently transferred, the new RC may take time to reflect. Check with the RTO that the transfer is complete in the system.

## 4. "No Data Available" Error for Vehicle Info

**Symptoms:** You can search but the result says "No data available" or shows partial/blank information.

### Common Causes and Fixes

| Cause | Fix |
|-------|-----|
| Government server downtime | Try again after a few hours |
| Incorrect vehicle number format | Double-check number — no spaces or special characters |
| Vehicle data not digitized | Contact your RTO for data entry |
| App cache corrupted | Clear cache and data, then retry |
| Internet connectivity issue | Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data |

> **Note:** The mParivahan app fetches data in real-time from Vahan/Sarathi servers. If those servers are down or under maintenance, you'll see this error regardless of your vehicle details being correct.

## 5. QR Code Scan Not Working

**Symptoms:** Scanning the QR code on a DL or RC through mParivahan shows an error or no result.

### How to Fix

1. **Ensure good lighting** — The camera needs clear visibility of the QR code.
2. **Hold steady** — Keep your phone stable and at the right distance (15–25 cm from the QR code).
3. **Check camera permissions** — Go to Settings → Apps → mParivahan → Permissions → Enable Camera.
4. **QR code damaged?** — If the physical DL/RC QR code is scratched or faded, the scan will fail. Apply for a duplicate DL/RC from your RTO.
5. **Old format DL/RC** — QR codes are only on smart card format DLs and RCs issued after 2019 (in most states). Older booklet-type DLs don't have scannable QR codes.

## 6. Virtual DL/RC Not Loading

**Symptoms:** The virtual DL or RC page shows a blank screen, keeps loading, or times out.

### How to Fix

1. **Check server status** — Government servers have scheduled maintenance, usually on weekends or late nights. Try during business hours on weekdays.
2. **Update the app** — Virtual DL/RC rendering depends on the latest app version.
3. **Re-login** — Log out of mParivahan completely, clear cache, and log in again.
4. **Screenshot as backup** — Once your virtual DL/RC loads successfully, take a screenshot and save it. You can show the screenshot during police checks as a temporary measure.
5. **Use DigiLocker as alternative** — If mParivahan consistently fails, download your DL/RC from [DigiLocker](https://digilocker.gov.in). It's equally valid.

## 7. mParivahan vs DigiLocker — Which DL/RC is Valid for Police?

This is one of the most common doubts. Here's the clear answer:

**Both are legally valid.** Under the Motor Vehicles (Amendment) Act 2019 and IT Act 2000, digital documents from both mParivahan and DigiLocker are accepted as valid proof during police verification and traffic checks.

| Feature | mParivahan | DigiLocker |
|---------|-----------|------------|
| Issued by | MoRTH | MeitY |
| Shows DL | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes |
| Shows RC | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes |
| Legal validity | ✅ Valid | ✅ Valid |
| Works offline | ❌ Needs internet | ✅ Downloaded copy works offline |
| QR code scan | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes |
| Vehicle info search | ✅ Yes | ❌ No |

> **Pro Tip:** Keep both apps installed. Use DigiLocker as your primary (works offline with downloaded documents) and mParivahan for vehicle info search. See our [DigiLocker guide](/guide/digilocker-account-setup) for setup instructions.

## 8. mParivahan App vs Parivahan Website — Key Differences

| Feature | mParivahan App | Parivahan Website |
|---------|---------------|-------------------|
| DL/RC virtual copy | ✅ Yes | ❌ No (view only) |
| Vehicle info search | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes |
| DL application/renewal | ❌ No | ✅ Yes |
| RC transfer | ❌ No | ✅ Yes |
| Challan payment | ✅ Yes (some states) | ✅ Yes |
| Fancy number booking | ❌ No | ✅ Yes |

**Key takeaway:** mParivahan is for carrying and viewing your documents. For any new applications, renewals, or transfers, use the [Parivahan website](https://parivahan.gov.in).

## General Troubleshooting Checklist

If nothing else works, go through this checklist:

- [ ] App updated to latest version
- [ ] Phone OS updated (Android 8+ or iOS 13+ recommended)
- [ ] Stable internet connection (try both Wi-Fi and mobile data)
- [ ] App cache and data cleared
- [ ] Correct mobile number linked to DL/RC at RTO
- [ ] Tried at a non-peak hour (before 10 AM or after 6 PM)
- [ ] Checked data on Parivahan website first
- [ ] Phone restarted after clearing cache

If the issue persists after all these steps, you can:
- **Call the Parivahan helpline:** 0120-2459169
- **Email:** helpdesk-vahan@gov.in
- **Visit your local RTO** with physical documents for data correction

## FAQs

### Is the mParivahan virtual DL valid for police checks?
Yes. Under the Motor Vehicles (Amendment) Act 2019, the virtual DL shown on mParivahan or DigiLocker is legally valid. Police and transport authorities must accept it.

### Why is my DL not showing in mParivahan even though I have a valid licence?
Your DL data may not be digitized in the Sarathi database, or your mobile number may not match. Check on the Parivahan website first. If it doesn't show there either, contact your RTO.

### Can I use mParivahan without internet?
No. mParivahan fetches data in real-time from government servers and requires an active internet connection. For offline access, use DigiLocker — once you download your DL/RC, it's available offline.

### What should I do if mParivahan shows wrong vehicle owner details?
This means the data in the Vahan database is incorrect. Visit your RTO with original documents and request a data correction. The app only displays what's in the database.

### Is mParivahan available for iOS (iPhone)?
Yes. mParivahan is available on both Google Play Store (Android) and Apple App Store (iOS). Search for "mParivahan" by NIC eGov.

### Can I pay traffic challans through mParivahan?
Yes, in some states. The app shows pending e-challans linked to your vehicle number and allows online payment. However, not all states have enabled this feature yet.

### My RC shows "blacklisted" on mParivahan. What does this mean?
This usually means your vehicle has unpaid challans, failed to clear fitness tests, or is involved in a legal case. Contact your RTO immediately to resolve the issue.
