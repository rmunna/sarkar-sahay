# mParivahan App Not Working? Fix Login & DL Errors

The **mParivahan app** by the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH) lets you carry your Driving Licence (DL) and Registration Certificate (RC) digitally on your phone. However, users frequently face issues like app crashes, login OTP failures, DL not loading, and server errors.

This guide covers all common mParivahan app problems and how to fix them.

## Common mParivahan App Problems

Here are the most reported issues:

- App crashes on opening or after login
- OTP not received during login
- DL details not loading or showing errors
- RC fetch failed or vehicle details not found
- Server error or "Something went wrong" message
- App stuck on loading screen

## Fix 1: mParivahan App Crashing or Not Opening

If the mParivahan app crashes immediately or freezes:

1. **Force close the app** — Go to Settings → Apps → mParivahan → Force Stop
2. **Clear app cache** — Settings → Apps → mParivahan → Storage → Clear Cache
3. **Clear app data** — Settings → Apps → mParivahan → Storage → Clear Data (you will need to log in again)
4. **Check for updates** — Open Google Play Store or Apple App Store, search "mParivahan", and update if available
5. **Reinstall the app** — Uninstall mParivahan completely, restart your phone, and install again from the official store
6. **Check phone storage** — Ensure you have at least 200 MB free storage

> **Tip:** mParivahan requires Android 6.0+ or iOS 12+. If your phone is older, the app may not work properly.

## Fix 2: mParivahan Login OTP Not Received

If you're not receiving the OTP during login:

1. **Check your mobile number** — Ensure the number you entered is the one linked to your Aadhaar/DL
2. **Wait 2-3 minutes** — OTP delivery can be delayed during peak hours
3. **Check SMS inbox and spam** — The OTP may be filtered by your phone's spam filter
4. **Ensure network signal** — Move to an area with better mobile network coverage
5. **Try again after 30 minutes** — The Parivahan server may be overloaded
6. **Check DND status** — Call your telecom provider and ensure DND (Do Not Disturb) is not blocking transactional SMS
7. **Try a different network** — Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data

If OTP still doesn't arrive after multiple attempts, try logging in during **early morning hours (6-8 AM)** when server load is lower.

## Fix 3: DL (Driving Licence) Not Loading

If your Driving Licence details are not showing in mParivahan:

1. **Verify your DL number** — Double-check the DL number format (e.g., KA01 20190001234)
2. **Check DL status on Parivahan portal** — Visit [parivahan.gov.in](https://parivahan.gov.in) → "Know Your DL" and verify your DL exists in the system
3. **RTO data sync issue** — If your DL was recently issued or renewed, it may take **7-15 days** for data to sync with the central Parivahan database
4. **Clear cache and retry** — Clear the app cache and try fetching DL details again
5. **Use Sarathi portal** — Check your DL status at [sarathi.parivahan.gov.in](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in)

> **Note:** If your DL was issued before 2010, your RTO may not have digitized the records. Contact your local RTO office for data entry.

## Fix 4: RC Fetch Failed or Vehicle Not Found

If you see "RC fetch failed" or your vehicle details are not loading:

1. **Enter the correct registration number** — Use the format without spaces (e.g., KA01AB1234)
2. **Try with spaces** — Some states use formats like "KA 01 AB 1234"
3. **Check on Vahan portal** — Visit [vahan.parivahan.gov.in](https://vahan.parivahan.gov.in) → "Know Your Vehicle Details"
4. **Recently registered vehicle** — New vehicles may take **3-7 days** to appear in the system after registration
5. **State server issue** — Some state RTOs have intermittent connectivity issues with the central database. Try again later
6. **Contact RTO** — If your vehicle is registered but not showing, visit your RTO with original RC for data correction

## Fix 5: Server Error or "Something Went Wrong"

The mParivahan app depends on the Parivahan/Vahan backend servers. Server errors are common:

1. **Check server status** — If you see "Server Error" or "Something Went Wrong", the Parivahan servers are likely down
2. **Peak hours** — Avoid using the app between **10 AM - 2 PM** on weekdays when traffic is highest
3. **Try after some time** — Most server issues resolve within 1-2 hours
4. **Switch network** — Try switching between Wi-Fi and mobile data
5. **Use the website instead** — If the app is not working, use the Parivahan website directly at [parivahan.gov.in](https://parivahan.gov.in)
6. **Check for maintenance** — The Parivahan portal sometimes undergoes scheduled maintenance on weekends

## Fix 6: Update the mParivahan App

Running an outdated version causes many issues:

1. Open **Google Play Store** (Android) or **App Store** (iOS)
2. Search for **"mParivahan"**
3. If an **Update** button is visible, tap it
4. Wait for the update to complete
5. Open the app and log in again

> **Important:** Only download mParivahan from official app stores. The official developer is **National Informatics Centre (NIC)**.

## Alternative: Use DigiLocker for DL and RC

If mParivahan is consistently not working, you can use **DigiLocker** as an alternative to carry your DL and RC digitally:

1. Download the **DigiLocker app** from Play Store or App Store
2. Sign in with your Aadhaar number
3. Go to **Issued Documents** → Search for "Ministry of Road Transport"
4. Pull your **Driving Licence** and **Vehicle Registration Certificate**
5. These documents are legally valid as per the IT Act, 2000

See our [DigiLocker guide](/guide/digilocker-advanced-features) for detailed setup instructions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the mParivahan virtual DL valid during traffic checks?

Yes, the virtual DL shown on mParivahan is legally valid. Traffic police and RTOs accept it as per MoRTH guidelines. However, keep a screenshot as backup in case of app issues.

### Why is mParivahan showing "Invalid DL Number"?

This usually happens when the DL number format is incorrect or the DL hasn't been digitized by your RTO. Verify your DL on [sarathi.parivahan.gov.in](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in) first.

### Can I use mParivahan without Aadhaar?

Yes, you can search for DL and RC details without Aadhaar. However, to add documents to "My Account" and get the virtual DL, you need Aadhaar-linked mobile number for OTP verification.

### mParivahan shows old address on my DL. How to update?

The app displays data from the Parivahan database. To update your address, apply for a DL address change at [sarathi.parivahan.gov.in](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in) or visit your RTO office.

### Is mParivahan available for iOS?

Yes, mParivahan is available on both Android (Google Play Store) and iOS (Apple App Store). Search for "mParivahan" by NIC eGov.

### What to do if mParivahan shows wrong vehicle details?

If your RC shows incorrect details (wrong name, address, or insurance), you need to get the data corrected at your RTO office. The app only displays data from the central Vahan database. See our [RC details correction guide](/guide/mparivahan-rc-details-wrong-fix) for more help.
