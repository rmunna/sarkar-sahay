## What is KVS Teacher Recruitment?

Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan (KVS) recruits teachers for over 1,250 Kendriya Vidyalayas (Central Schools) across India and abroad. KVS hires for three categories of teaching positions — PGT (Post Graduate Teacher), TGT (Trained Graduate Teacher), and PRT (Primary Teacher). These are Central Government positions with excellent pay, benefits, and job security.

KVS also recruits for non-teaching positions like Vice Principal, Finance Officer, and other administrative roles.

## Who is Eligible?

### PGT (Post Graduate Teacher) — Classes 11-12
- **Qualification:** Master's degree in the relevant subject with at least **50% marks**
- **B.Ed** from a recognized university
- **Age:** 40 years maximum (relaxation for SC/ST/OBC/PwD as per government rules)
- **Subjects:** English, Hindi, Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology, History, Geography, Economics, Commerce, Computer Science

### TGT (Trained Graduate Teacher) — Classes 6-10
- **Qualification:** Bachelor's degree with the relevant subject and at least **50% marks** in aggregate
- **B.Ed** from a recognized institution
- **CTET Paper II** qualified (desirable)
- **Age:** 35 years maximum
- **Subjects:** English, Hindi, Mathematics, Science, Social Studies, Sanskrit

### PRT (Primary Teacher) — Classes 1-5
- **Qualification:** Senior Secondary (10+2) with at least **50% marks** AND 2-year Diploma in Elementary Education (D.El.Ed) OR 4-year B.El.Ed OR 2-year B.Ed
- **CTET Paper I** qualified
- **Age:** 30 years maximum

### PRT (Music)
- Bachelor's degree in Music or equivalent diploma from a recognized institution
- **Age:** 30 years maximum

## KVS Exam Pattern

### Part I — Common for All Posts

| Section | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| General Hindi | 10 | 10 |
| General English | 10 | 10 |
| General Knowledge & Current Affairs | 10 | 10 |
| Reasoning Ability | 10 | 10 |
| Computer Literacy | 10 | 10 |
| **Total Part I** | **50** | **50** |

### Part II — Subject-Specific

| Post | Questions | Marks |
|------|-----------|-------|
| PGT | 80 | 80 |
| TGT | 80 | 80 |
| PRT | 80 | 80 |

### Part III — Perspectives on Education

| Section | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| Education & Pedagogy | 20 | 20 |

- **Total Marks:** 150
- **Duration:** 2.5 hours (150 minutes)
- **Mode:** Computer-based test (CBT)
- **Negative Marking:** No negative marking

## Selection Process

### Stage 1: Computer-Based Test (CBT)
- Online exam at centres across India
- Shortlisting based on CBT score

### Stage 2: Demo Teaching / Skill Test
- Shortlisted candidates appear for a demonstration class
- Evaluated on teaching methodology, content knowledge, and communication
- PRT Music candidates have a practical skill test

### Stage 3: Interview
- Personal interview assessing subject knowledge, teaching aptitude, and personality
- Weightage varies by post

### Final Merit
| Component | PGT/TGT | PRT |
|-----------|---------|-----|
| CBT | 85% | 85% |
| Demo Teaching | 10% | 10% |
| Interview | 5% | 5% |

## Preparation Strategy

- **Part I** — Practice general knowledge, reasoning, and computer basics; this is common across all posts
- **Part II** — Revise your subject from NCERT books (Class 6-12) and graduation-level textbooks
- **Part III** — Study NCF 2005, NEP 2020, child development, and pedagogy concepts
- **Previous papers** — Solve KVS previous year papers for pattern familiarity
- **Demo teaching** — Practice explaining topics clearly in 10-15 minutes

## Important Tips

- **CTET qualification is essential** — clear CTET before or alongside KVS preparation
- **NCERT is the foundation** — KVS follows CBSE curriculum; NCERT books are the primary source
- **Stay updated on education policy** — NEP 2020, NCF, and RTE Act questions are common
- **Demo teaching is crucial** — practice with a timer; clarity and engagement matter more than depth
- **Apply for multiple KV regions** — this increases chances of posting in a preferred location

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is CTET mandatory for KVS recruitment?
CTET is mandatory for PRT and desirable for TGT. PGT positions do not require CTET, but having it strengthens your application.

### Can private school teachers apply for KVS?
Yes, anyone meeting the educational qualification and age criteria can apply, regardless of current employment.

### How often does KVS conduct recruitment?
KVS recruitment is not annual — it depends on vacancies. Major recruitments happen every 2-3 years. Keep checking the official website.

### Is there an interview in KVS recruitment?
Yes, shortlisted candidates (based on CBT) appear for demo teaching and interview. The CBT score carries 85% weightage.

### Can I get a transfer to a different city after joining KVS?
Yes, KVS allows inter-station transfers based on vacancy and seniority. Teachers posted in remote areas get priority for transfers.

### What is the difference between KVS and NVS recruitment?
KVS is for Kendriya Vidyalayas (urban/semi-urban schools for Central Government employees' children), while NVS is for Navodaya Vidyalayas (rural residential schools for talented students). See our [Navodaya Vidyalaya teacher guide](/guide/navodaya-vidyalaya-teacher) for details.
