# MY Bharat Certificate Download Guide

**Quick answer:** MY Bharat certificates are activity-based. You can download or access a certificate only if the event, volunteering activity or experiential learning opportunity supports certificates and your participation/completion is recorded on the official portal.

Certificate not showing does not always mean an error. Sometimes attendance, organiser approval or completion marking is still pending.

For account setup, profile details and NYPS 2.0 / MY Bharat confusion, start with the [MY Bharat Registration Guide](/guide/my-bharat-registration-guide). For finding activities, use the [MY Bharat Experiential Learning Guide](/guide/my-bharat-experiential-learning-guide).

## Before Looking for Certificate

Check:

- You registered for the activity on the official MY Bharat portal.
- You attended or completed the activity.
- The organiser marked participation/completion.
- The activity actually offers a certificate.
- You are logged in with the same mobile/email account used for registration.

## How To Check Certificate

1. Log in at [mybharat.gov.in](https://mybharat.gov.in/).
2. Open your dashboard or profile.
3. Check events, participation history, learning opportunities or certificate section.
4. Select the completed activity.
5. Download or view certificate if the portal shows it.

Portal labels can change, so look for similar sections such as Profile, My Activities, Certificates or Participation.

## Certificate Not Showing - Fix

| Problem | What To Check |
|---|---|
| Activity not listed | You may have used a different mobile/email account |
| Certificate button missing | The activity may not issue certificates |
| Attendance pending | Organiser may not have verified participation |
| Event recently completed | Wait for organiser processing |
| Wrong name on certificate | Correct profile details and contact organiser/support if already issued |
| Download fails | Try another browser or clear cache |

## What To Ask the Organiser

Use a short message:

> I registered for the MY Bharat activity using this mobile/email. I completed the activity, but the certificate is not visible in my MY Bharat profile. Please confirm whether attendance/completion has been marked and whether the activity issues certificates.

Do not send Aadhaar, OTP or passwords in support messages.

## Can Certificates Be Used in Resume?

Yes, if they are officially issued for your completed activity. Add:

- Activity name
- Organisation/department
- Date
- Certificate or participation ID if visible
- Skills or work done

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does every MY Bharat event give a certificate?
No. Certificate availability depends on the specific event or opportunity.

### Why is my certificate pending?
The organiser may still need to mark attendance, verify completion or close the activity.

### Can I download MY Bharat certificate without login?
Normally you should log in with the same account used for registration or participation.

### What if my name is wrong on the certificate?
Check your profile details first. If the certificate is already generated, contact the organiser or portal support through official channels.

### Is a paid MY Bharat certificate valid?
Avoid paid certificate sellers. Use only certificates generated through the official portal or official organiser route.
