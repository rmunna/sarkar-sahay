# Karnataka Government Schemes and Services – Complete Guide

Karnataka runs one of India's most comprehensive welfare programmes under the "Pancha Guarantee" umbrella and beyond. From free bus travel for women under Shakti to rice at ₹1/kg under Anna Bhagya, the state offers a wide range of schemes for women, farmers, students, and senior citizens. This guide covers every major Karnataka government scheme with eligibility, benefits, and application details.

## Key Government Portals in Karnataka

- **Seva Sindhu Portal** – [sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in/) – One-stop portal for 700+ government services, certificates, and scheme applications. See our [complete Seva Sindhu portal guide](/guide/seva-sindhu-karnataka-portal-guide) for step-by-step usage instructions.
- **Guarantee Schemes Portal** – [sevasindhugs.karnataka.gov.in](https://sevasindhugs.karnataka.gov.in/) – Dedicated portal for Pancha Guarantee scheme applications and status tracking
- **Karnataka One** – [karnataka.gov.in/karnatakaone](https://www.karnataka.gov.in/) – Citizen services portal
- **Bhoomi Land Records** – [landrecords.karnataka.gov.in](https://landrecords.karnataka.gov.in/) – RTC, mutation, land records. See: [Bhoomi Land Records Karnataka](/guide/bhoomi-land-records-karnataka)
- **e-Janma** – Birth and death certificate registration portal

## Pancha Guarantee Schemes

The Karnataka government's five guarantee schemes form the backbone of the state's welfare programme:

### 1. Gruha Lakshmi – Women's Financial Assistance

Monthly financial assistance of ₹2,000 to women heads of households. One of Karnataka's most impactful schemes for women's empowerment.

**Eligibility:**
- Woman must be the head of a BPL/APL household
- Must have a ration card in her name
- Family income limits apply

Read the complete guide: [Gruha Lakshmi Karnataka](/guide/gruha-lakshmi-karnataka) | [Karnataka Gruha Lakshmi Scheme](/guide/karnataka-gruha-lakshmi-scheme)

### 2. Anna Bhagya – Subsidized Rice Scheme

Free rice (10 kg per person) to BPL families, replacing the earlier ₹1/kg pricing with completely free distribution for priority households.

**Key features:**
- 10 kg free rice per person per month for BPL/Antyodaya families
- Distributed through Fair Price Shops
- Ration card is mandatory

Read the complete guide: [Karnataka Anna Bhagya Rice Scheme](/guide/karnataka-anna-bhagya-rice-scheme)

### 3. Shakti – Free Bus Travel for Women

All women in Karnataka can travel free on KSRTC, BMTC, NWKRTC, and NEKRTC buses (non-AC, ordinary services). A transformative scheme for women's mobility.

Read the complete guide: [Shakti Free Bus Karnataka](/guide/shakti-free-bus-karnataka)

### 4. Gruha Jyothi – Free Electricity

Free electricity up to 200 units per month for domestic consumers. Families with a single electricity connection are eligible.

### 5. Yuva Nidhi – Unemployment Allowance

Monthly allowance of ₹3,000 (graduates) and ₹1,500 (diploma holders) for unemployed youth for up to two years while they search for employment.

## Women and Child Welfare Schemes

### Bhagyalakshmi Scheme

Financial assistance for girl children born in BPL families. A bond of ₹19,300 is deposited at birth which matures to ₹1,00,034 when the girl turns 18, provided she completes education milestones.

Read the complete guide: [Karnataka Bhagyalakshmi Scheme](/guide/karnataka-bhagyalakshmi-scheme)

### Stree Samarthya – Women's Livelihood

Skill development and livelihood support for women through SHGs and micro-enterprises.

## Health Schemes

### Ayushman Bharat – Arogya Karnataka

Karnataka's health insurance scheme integrated with Ayushman Bharat provides cashless treatment up to ₹5 lakh per family per year at empanelled hospitals.

**Coverage:**
- 1,900+ medical procedures covered
- Cashless treatment at government and private hospitals
- BPL and APL families eligible (with different tiers)

See: [Ayushman Bharat vs State Health Schemes](/guide/ayushman-bharat-vs-state-health-schemes)

### Yashaswini Health Insurance

Cooperative society-based health insurance for farmers and rural workers in Karnataka, providing cashless surgical treatment.

## Housing Schemes

### Basava Vasathi Yojana

Housing scheme for SC/ST families providing financial assistance for constructing pucca houses. Beneficiaries receive up to ₹1.20 lakh.

### Ambedkar Housing Scheme

Housing for urban slum dwellers and economically weaker sections in Karnataka's cities and towns.

## Agriculture Schemes

### Raitha Siri – Farmer Welfare

Comprehensive package for farmers including crop insurance, input subsidies, and market support.

### Krishi Bhagya Yojana

Farm pond, polyhouse, shade net, and drip/sprinkler irrigation support for farmers in drought-prone areas.

### PM-KISAN in Karnataka

₹6,000 per year to eligible farmer families. Registration through local agriculture offices or PM-KISAN portal.

## How to Apply for Karnataka Government Schemes

### Online Application

1. **Visit Seva Sindhu** – [sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in/)
2. **Register/Login** – Use Aadhaar or mobile number
3. **Select the scheme** – Browse by department or search
4. **Fill application** – Enter personal and eligibility details
5. **Upload documents** – Aadhaar, ration card, income certificate, bank passbook
6. **Submit** – Track status using the application number

### Offline Application

1. Visit the nearest **Karnataka One centre** or **Gram Panchayat office**
2. Carry original documents and photocopies
3. Fill the application form and submit
4. Collect acknowledgment receipt for tracking

### Common Documents Required

- Aadhaar card
- Ration card (BPL/APL)
- Income certificate
- Caste certificate (for category-based schemes)
- Bank passbook with IFSC
- Passport-size photographs

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I check my Karnataka scheme application status?**
Log in to Seva Sindhu portal with your credentials and check status under "My Applications."

**Can I apply for Pancha Guarantee schemes online?**
Yes, most Pancha Guarantee schemes accept online applications through Seva Sindhu. Gruha Lakshmi and Shakti also have dedicated portals.

**Is ration card mandatory for Karnataka welfare schemes?**
For food security and many welfare schemes, yes. Apply for a ration card first if you don't have one.
