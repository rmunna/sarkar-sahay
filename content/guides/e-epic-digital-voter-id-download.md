# e-EPIC — How to Download Your Digital Voter ID Card Online

**e-EPIC** (Electronic Electoral Photo Identity Card) is the official **digital PDF version of your Voter ID card**, introduced by the Election Commission of India (ECI) in January 2021. It carries the same legal validity as the physical plastic EPIC card and can be shown on your phone as photo ID at polling booths and for other ID-proof purposes.

> **Quick download:** Go to [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in) → Login → Download e-EPIC. You need your mobile number registered with your voter ID for OTP verification.

---

## What Is e-EPIC?

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Full form** | Electronic Electoral Photo Identity Card |
| **Format** | Password-protected PDF |
| **Introduced** | January 2021 by ECI |
| **Legal validity** | Same as physical EPIC card |
| **Contains** | Photo, name, EPIC number, address, serial number, QR code |
| **Password** | First 4 letters of your name (capitals) + birth year (e.g., RAJA1990) |
| **Usable as ID** | Yes — at polling booth, for other government/private ID requirements |

---

## Who Can Download e-EPIC?

**You can download e-EPIC if:**
- Your mobile number is **registered with your voter ID** (linked in the NVSP/ECI database)
- You are enrolled as a voter in any constituency in India

**Two categories of eligibility:**

| Category | Condition | OTP Method |
|----------|-----------|-----------|
| New voter (enrolled after Aug 1, 2021) | Direct OTP login with registered mobile | Mobile OTP only |
| Existing voter (enrolled before Aug 1, 2021) | Mobile OTP if linked; else Aadhaar OTP | Mobile OTP or Aadhaar OTP |

> If your mobile number is **not** linked to your voter registration, you need to link it first before downloading. See the section below on how to link your mobile.

## How to Download e-EPIC — Step by Step

### Method 1 — Via voters.eci.gov.in (Recommended)

1. Go to **[voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in)**
2. Click **"Login"** on the top right
3. Enter your **registered mobile number** → click **"Send OTP"**
4. Enter the OTP received → click **"Verify"**
5. You are now on your voter dashboard
6. Click **"Download e-EPIC"**
7. Enter your **EPIC number** (voter ID number) or it may auto-fill
8. Click **"Download"** — the PDF saves to your device
9. **Open the PDF** — when prompted for password, enter: first 4 letters of your name in CAPITALS + birth year
   - Example: Name = Rajesh Kumar, DOB = 1988 → Password = **RAJE1988**

### Method 2 — Via NVSP Portal

1. Go to **[nvsp.in](https://nvsp.in)** (National Voter Service Portal)
2. Log in with your voter credentials or mobile OTP
3. Go to "e-EPIC Download" section
4. Enter your EPIC number and state
5. Verify with OTP → Download

### Method 3 — Via Voter Helpline App

1. Download the **"Voter Helpline"** app (Android/iOS)
2. Register with your mobile number
3. Log in → Go to e-EPIC section
4. Enter EPIC number → Download

## e-EPIC Password Format

The downloaded PDF is password-protected:

**Password = First 4 letters of your name (CAPS) + 4-digit birth year**

| Your Name | Birth Year | Password |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| Priya Sharma | 1995 | PRIY1995 |
| Mohan Lal | 1980 | MOHA1980 |
| A. Kumar | 1975 | A.KU1975 |
| Ram | 1970 | RAM01970 |

> If your name has fewer than 4 letters, pad with zeros or the next character as shown. If the password doesn't work, try variations — some accounts use the first 4 letters without spaces or special characters.

## What If My Mobile Number Is Not Linked?

If your mobile number is not registered in the ECI database, you cannot directly download e-EPIC.

**Options to link your mobile:**

**Option 1 — Online at voters.eci.gov.in**
1. Go to voters.eci.gov.in → Select "Correction in Voter ID"
2. Fill Form 8 (correction form)
3. Add your mobile number
4. Submit — it gets updated within a few weeks

**Option 2 — Through your BLO (Booth Level Officer)**
1. Find your BLO: voters.eci.gov.in → "Know Your BLO"
2. Visit the BLO with your Aadhaar and existing Voter ID
3. Request mobile number update
4. BLO updates the record within a few days

**Option 3 — At Voter Facilitation Centre**
Visit your nearest Voter Facilitation Centre (usually at ERO / Tehsil office) with documents.

---

## Using Aadhaar OTP Instead of Mobile OTP

If your mobile isn't linked but your **Aadhaar is linked** to your voter registration:
1. On the login screen, choose "Login with Aadhaar OTP"
2. Enter your Aadhaar number
3. OTP goes to the mobile linked to your Aadhaar (not necessarily your voter registration)
4. Proceed to download e-EPIC

## Can I Store e-EPIC in DigiLocker?

e-EPIC is not yet automatically pushed to DigiLocker by ECI. However, you can:
- **Upload manually** to DigiLocker (as a self-uploaded document)
- Use the downloaded PDF directly from your phone

## Is e-EPIC Legally Valid?

**Yes.** The Election Commission has officially stated that e-EPIC is valid for:
- Voting at polling booths (you can show it on your phone)
- Government ID proof where Voter ID is accepted
- KYC documents at banks, mobile operators, etc.

The PDF has a QR code that can be scanned to verify authenticity.

## Common Problems and Fixes

### "Mobile number not found" error

Your mobile is not linked to your voter registration. Use the Form 8 correction process or visit your BLO to link it.

### OTP not received

- Check if the mobile number shown on screen is correct
- Ensure your phone has network coverage
- Wait 2 minutes and retry
- Try at off-peak hours (early morning) — ECI portal can be slow

### PDF won't open / wrong password

- Password is CASE SENSITIVE — must be CAPITALS
- Try: First 4 letters in CAPS + 4-digit birth year
- If your name has a prefix (Mr., Dr.) ignore it
- If name is written differently (e.g., "S Kumar" vs "Satish Kumar"), try both versions

### e-EPIC shows old/wrong address

The e-EPIC reflects your current voter registration data. If address is wrong, update it via Form 8 at voters.eci.gov.in and re-download after update.

### Download button not appearing

- Complete full login (OTP verification)
- Check if you are using a supported browser (Chrome/Firefox — avoid Internet Explorer)
- Clear browser cache and try again
- ECI portal occasionally goes down during peak times — try non-peak hours

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is e-EPIC card?

e-EPIC (Electronic Electoral Photo Identity Card) is the digital PDF version of your physical Voter ID card issued by the Election Commission of India. It is downloadable from voters.eci.gov.in and is legally valid as a photo identity proof.

### Can I use e-EPIC to vote?

Yes — you can show your e-EPIC on your mobile phone at the polling booth instead of the physical card. ECI explicitly permits this. However, carry a backup (physical card or another ID) in case the polling officer has difficulty with digital verification.

### Is e-EPIC the same as Voter ID?

Yes. e-EPIC is the digital equivalent of the physical EPIC (Voter ID) card. Both have the same EPIC number, photo, and details. One is plastic; the other is a PDF on your phone.

### Can I download e-EPIC for my family members?

You need separate accounts for each family member. Each person's e-EPIC can only be downloaded using the mobile number registered against their individual voter ID. You can help a family member by logging into their registered number.

### How to check my EPIC number?

Your EPIC number is the alphanumeric code printed on your physical Voter ID card (usually starts with state code letters, e.g., DEL/1234567). You can also find it by searching your name at [electoralsearch.eci.gov.in](https://electoralsearch.eci.gov.in).

### ECI helpline

**1950** — National Voter Service Helpline (call or SMS). Available in multiple languages.
