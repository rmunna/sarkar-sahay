## What is Laadli Laxmi Yojana (Goa)?

The **Laadli Laxmi Yojana** is a girl child welfare scheme by the **Government of Goa** under the **Directorate of Women and Child Development**. It provides financial benefits to families on the birth of a girl child, with payments at key milestones — birth, school admission, and age 18 — to encourage girls' education and well-being.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Girl child** born in Goa to Goan residents
2. **Residency:** Parents must be residents of Goa for at least **15 years**
3. **Income:** Annual family income should not exceed **₹3,00,000**
4. **Registration:** Birth must be registered in Goa
5. **Family Limit:** Maximum **two girl children** per family
6. **Family Planning:** Parents must have adopted family planning norms (after second child)

## Benefits

| Milestone | Amount |
|-----------|--------|
| At birth | ₹5,000 (FD) |
| Admission to Class 1 | ₹5,000 |
| Admission to Class 6 | ₹5,000 |
| Admission to Class 9 | ₹5,000 |
| Admission to Class 11/12 | ₹5,000 |
| On turning 18 (if unmarried) | Matured FD amount |

> Exact amounts may vary — check [dwcd.goa.gov.in](https://dwcd.goa.gov.in) for the latest notification.

## Documents Required

- **Birth Certificate** of the girl child
- **Aadhaar Card** of parents and child (when available)
- **Goa Residence Certificate** (15 years) of parents
- **Income Certificate** (family income ≤ ₹3,00,000)
- **Bank Account** (in girl child's or mother's name)
- **Passport-size photographs**
- **School enrollment proof** (for education milestone claims)
- **Family planning certificate** (if applicable)

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Online Application

1. **Visit** [goaonline.gov.in](https://goaonline.gov.in)
2. **Register** / Login
3. **Navigate** to Women & Child Development → Laadli Laxmi Yojana
4. **Fill** application with girl child's birth details, parents' info, income
5. **Upload** Birth Certificate, residence proof, income certificate
6. **Submit** and note application number
7. **Track** status on Goa Online

### Offline Application

1. **Visit** the **DWCD Office** or **Mamlatdar Office**
2. **Collect** the Laadli Laxmi form
3. **Fill** and attach documents
4. **Submit** to the designated officer
5. **Get** acknowledgement

### Claiming Milestone Benefits

1. When the girl reaches each education milestone, **submit** the school enrollment proof
2. **Apply** for the next installment through the DWCD office or Goa Online
3. **Benefit is released** after verification

## Processing Time

- **Application:** 30-60 days for initial approval
- **FD Creation:** After approval, FD is created in girl's name
- **Milestone Payments:** On submission of education proof

## Important Tips

1. **Register Early:** Apply as soon as the girl child is born
2. **Keep All Documents:** Birth certificate, school enrollment proofs are needed at each milestone
3. **15-Year Residency:** Parents must have 15 years of Goa residence
4. **Education is Key:** Benefits are linked to education milestones — keep the girl enrolled in school
5. **Two Girls Only:** Maximum benefit for two girl children per family

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. How much total benefit does a girl child get?
Approximately **₹25,000+** across milestones, plus FD maturity value at age 18.

### Q2. Can I apply for both daughters?
Yes, benefits are available for up to **two girl children** per family.

### Q3. What is the income limit?
Annual family income should not exceed **₹3,00,000**.

### Q4. Is the scheme only for Goan residents?
Yes, parents must be residents of Goa for at least **15 years**.

### Q5. When can the FD be withdrawn?
When the girl turns **18 years** and is unmarried.

### Q6. Do I need to apply separately for each milestone?
Yes, submit education proof at each milestone to claim the next installment.

### Q7. How do I apply?
Apply online at [goaonline.gov.in](https://goaonline.gov.in) or visit the DWCD office.
