## What is Ladli Scheme (Haryana)?

The **Ladli Scheme** is a social welfare initiative by the **Government of Haryana**, launched to improve the declining child sex ratio in the state. Under this scheme, the government provides **₹5,000 per year** for five years into a **Kisan Vikas Patra (KVP)** in the name of the second girl child born in a family.

The total benefit of **₹25,000** (₹5,000 × 5 years) matures with interest and can be withdrawn when the girl turns **18 years old**. The scheme is managed by the **Department of Women and Child Development, Haryana**.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Birth Date:** The second girl child must be born on or after **20 August 2005**
2. **Residency:** Parents must be permanent residents of Haryana
3. **Family:** The scheme applies specifically to the **second girl child** in the family (both children should be girls, or the second child should be a girl)
4. **Income:** No strict income limit, but the scheme is targeted at all economic sections
5. **Registration:** Birth must be registered within the prescribed time

## Benefits

| Year | Deposit Amount (KVP) |
|------|---------------------|
| Year 1 (birth year) | ₹5,000 |
| Year 2 | ₹5,000 |
| Year 3 | ₹5,000 |
| Year 4 | ₹5,000 |
| Year 5 | ₹5,000 |
| **Total Deposited** | **₹25,000** |
| **Maturity (at age 18)** | ₹25,000 + accumulated interest |

## Documents Required

### For Parents
- Aadhaar Card of mother and father
- Parivar Pehchan Patra (Haryana Family ID)
- Proof of Haryana residence (Domicile Certificate)

### For Girl Child
- Birth Certificate of the second girl child
- Birth Certificate of the first girl child (to prove she is the second daughter)

### Bank & Other
- Mother's bank account passbook
- Passport-size photographs of parent and child
- Caste Certificate (if applicable)

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Online Application

1. **Visit** [saralharyana.gov.in](https://saralharyana.gov.in) or the WCD Haryana portal
2. **Login** or register a new account
3. **Search** for "Ladli Scheme" under Women & Child Development
4. **Enter** Parivar Pehchan Patra number
5. **Fill** details: both children's birth information, mother's bank account
6. **Upload** Birth Certificates, Aadhaar, and other documents
7. **Submit** and note the application ID
8. **Track** status via Saral Haryana

### Offline Application

1. **Visit** the nearest **Anganwadi Centre** or **Women & Child Development Office** at the district level
2. **Collect** the Ladli Scheme application form
3. **Fill** the form with accurate details
4. **Attach** all required documents
5. **Submit** to the Anganwadi worker or WCD office
6. **Get** the acknowledgement receipt

## Processing Time

- **Verification:** 30-60 days
- **KVP Purchase:** After approval, the government purchases KVP in the girl child's name
- **Annual Deposits:** Subsequent deposits are made annually for 5 years
- **Maturity:** Withdrawal possible after the girl turns 18

## Important Tips

1. **Apply Early:** Register as soon as the second girl child is born for timely benefit
2. **Keep Birth Certificates Safe:** Both children's birth certificates are essential
3. **Parivar Pehchan Patra:** Mandatory for all Haryana government schemes
4. **Don't Withdraw Early:** The KVP matures when the girl turns 18 — early withdrawal may not be possible
5. **Combine with Education:** Encourage the girl child's education alongside this financial benefit

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. Who is eligible for the Ladli Scheme?
Families with a second girl child born on or after 20 August 2005 in Haryana.

### Q2. How much money does the girl child receive?
₹5,000/year is deposited for 5 years (total ₹25,000), which matures with interest at age 18.

### Q3. Can the first girl child also get this benefit?
No, the Ladli Scheme specifically covers the **second girl child**. For the first girl child, other schemes like Lado Lakshmi may apply.

### Q4. When can the money be withdrawn?
The maturity amount can be withdrawn when the girl child turns **18 years old**.

### Q5. Is there any income limit?
There is no strict income ceiling — the scheme is available to all Haryana residents.

### Q6. Where do I apply?
Apply online at [saralharyana.gov.in](https://saralharyana.gov.in) or at the nearest Anganwadi Centre / WCD Office.

### Q7. What if I have twin girls as the first pregnancy?
Both twins may be considered eligible. Check with the local WCD office for specific cases.
