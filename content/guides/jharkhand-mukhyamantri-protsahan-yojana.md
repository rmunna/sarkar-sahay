## What is Mukhyamantri Protsahan Yojana?

The Mukhyamantri Protsahan Yojana is an unemployment allowance scheme by the Government of Jharkhand that provides financial assistance to educated but unemployed youth of the state. The scheme aims to support graduates and postgraduates who are actively seeking employment by providing a yearly allowance while they search for jobs.

Under this scheme, eligible unemployed youth receive ₹5,000 per year as a one-time annual allowance, credited directly to their bank account. The scheme is managed by the Department of Labour, Employment, Training & Skill Development, Government of Jharkhand.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Permanent resident of Jharkhand**
- **Age:** Must be between 18 and 35 years
- **Education:** Must hold a graduation degree or above from a recognized university
- **Employment status:** Must be currently unemployed and registered at the District Employment Exchange
- **Income limit:** Annual family income should not exceed ₹3,00,000 (₹3 lakh)
- **Not receiving** any other government scholarship, stipend, or unemployment benefit
- **Must have an active bank account** linked to Aadhaar
- **Must have a valid voter ID** or domicile certificate of Jharkhand

## Documents Required

### Mandatory Documents
- **Aadhaar Card** (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- **Graduation degree/marksheet** from a recognized university
- **Domicile certificate** or permanent residence certificate of Jharkhand
- **Employment Exchange registration card** (valid and up to date)
- **Income certificate** (annual family income below ₹3 lakh) — issued by Circle Officer/SDO
- **Bank account passbook** or cancelled cheque (Aadhaar-linked account)
- **Voter ID card** (as additional identity/residence proof)
- **Passport-size photographs**

### Additional Documents
- **Caste certificate** (if applicable, for SC/ST/OBC candidates)
- **Disability certificate** (if applicable)
- **Affidavit** declaring unemployment status (self-declaration)

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Online Process

1. **Visit** the official portal at [rojgar.jharkhand.gov.in](https://rojgar.jharkhand.gov.in)
2. **Click on** "Mukhyamantri Protsahan Yojana" or "New Registration"
3. **Register** using your mobile number and create a login ID
4. **Fill the application form** — personal details, educational qualifications, employment exchange number, income details
5. **Upload required documents** — Aadhaar, degree certificate, domicile, income certificate, bank passbook, photo
6. **Verify your details** and submit the application
7. **Note your application ID/reference number** for tracking
8. **Application verified** by the District Employment Officer
9. **Allowance credited** to your bank account after approval (via DBT)

### Offline Process

1. **Visit** the District Employment Exchange office in your district
2. **Ensure your employment registration** is active and up to date
3. **Collect the Protsahan Yojana application form** from the office
4. **Fill the form** with all personal, educational, and bank details
5. **Attach self-attested copies** of all required documents
6. **Submit the form** at the employment exchange office
7. **Collect the acknowledgement receipt**
8. **Track status** by visiting the office or checking online

## Processing Time

- **Application verification:** 30–60 days
- **Allowance disbursement:** Within 1–3 months after approval, annually
- **Employment Exchange registration:** Same day to 7 days

## Allowance Details

| Benefit | Amount |
|---|---|
| Annual unemployment allowance | ₹5,000 per year |
| Mode of payment | Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to bank account |
| Duration | Available each year as long as eligibility criteria are met |

## Important Tips

1. **Register at the Employment Exchange first** — this is a prerequisite; without active registration, your application will be rejected
2. **Renew your employment registration** — it must be renewed annually; an expired registration means loss of eligibility
3. **Link Aadhaar to bank account** — the allowance is sent via DBT; ensure Aadhaar-bank linking is done (see our [Aadhaar linking guide](/guide/aadhaar-linking-guide))
4. **Apply every year** — the allowance is annual; you need to apply or confirm eligibility each year
5. **Keep looking for jobs** — this is a temporary support scheme; use the employment exchange services and job portals to find employment

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. How much allowance do I get under Mukhyamantri Protsahan Yojana?
Eligible unemployed graduates receive ₹5,000 per year as an unemployment allowance, directly credited to their bank account.

### Q2. Is the allowance monthly or annual?
The allowance is annual. You receive ₹5,000 once per year, not monthly.

### Q3. Do I need to be registered at the Employment Exchange?
Yes, active registration at the District Employment Exchange is mandatory. You must register before applying and keep the registration renewed annually.

### Q4. Can diploma holders apply for this scheme?
The scheme is primarily for graduates (degree holders) and above. Diploma holders may not be eligible unless they hold a graduation-equivalent degree. Check the latest official notification for clarification.

### Q5. What is the income limit for this scheme?
The annual family income must not exceed ₹3,00,000 (₹3 lakh). An income certificate from the Circle Officer/SDO is required as proof.

### Q6. Can I apply if I'm doing part-time work?
The scheme is for unemployed youth. If you have any form of regular employment (including government/private jobs), you are not eligible. Occasional part-time work may be assessed on a case-by-case basis.

### Q7. How do I check my application status?
Visit [rojgar.jharkhand.gov.in](https://rojgar.jharkhand.gov.in) and use the "Track Application" feature with your application reference number.
