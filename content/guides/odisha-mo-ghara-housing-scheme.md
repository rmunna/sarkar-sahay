# Odisha Mo Ghara Housing Scheme 2026 — 0% Interest Loan for Rural Homes

**Mo Ghara** (meaning "My Home" in Odia) is the Odisha government's rural housing scheme that provides a **subsidised loan of up to ₹3 lakh at 0% interest** to BPL families to construct or upgrade a pucca house. Unlike PMAY-G which is a grant, Mo Ghara is a low-interest loan — it fills the gap for families who need more than the PMAY-G grant amount.

## Key Benefits

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Loan Amount** | Up to ₹3 lakh |
| **Interest Rate** | 0% (interest subsidy from state government) |
| **Repayment Period** | 10 years (120 monthly installments) |
| **Target Beneficiaries** | BPL families in rural Odisha |
| **Banks Involved** | Bank of India, UCO Bank, and other scheduled banks |
| **Goal** | Pucca house for families who own no permanent home |

## Eligibility Criteria

- **Odisha resident** with domicile proof
- **BPL (Below Poverty Line)** status — income below ₹3 lakh per year
- **No pucca house** (must currently live in kutcha/semi-pucca house)
- **Landowner** — must have land to build on (own or family land)
- **Not already covered** under PMAY-G for full construction amount
- Age: 18–60 years (loan applicant)
- **Priority**: SC/ST families, single women, persons with disability

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar card
- BPL ration card or income certificate
- Land ownership documents (patta)
- Bank account passbook (Aadhaar-linked)
- Photograph of current house showing kutcha/semi-pucca condition
- Caste certificate (for SC/ST)
- Disability certificate (if applicable)

## How to Apply for Mo Ghara

### Online at moghara.odisha.gov.in

1. Visit **[moghara.odisha.gov.in](https://moghara.odisha.gov.in/)**
2. Click **"Apply Online"**
3. Login / register with Aadhaar-linked mobile OTP
4. Fill application form with personal details, house condition, land details
5. Upload all documents (PDF/JPG under 2 MB)
6. Submit and note your **Application Reference Number**
7. Track status on same portal

### Through Bank Branch

1. Visit your nearest **Bank of India** or **UCO Bank** branch
2. Ask for the **Mo Ghara Scheme application form**
3. Fill the form and submit with all documents
4. The bank will verify eligibility and forward to District Rural Housing Agency (DRHA)

### Through Block Development Office (BDO)

1. Visit your **Block Development Office**
2. BDO can help identify eligible families and submit applications via the portal
3. Gram Panchayat also facilitates applications in some blocks

## How the Loan is Disbursed

1. **Application approved** by DRHA after verification
2. **Loan sanction letter** issued from bank
3. **First tranche released** after foundation construction begins
4. **Second tranche** after construction reaches lintel level
5. **Third tranche** after roof completion and final inspection
6. **Repayment starts** 6 months after final disbursal

## Check Mo Ghara Application Status

1. Visit **[moghara.odisha.gov.in](https://moghara.odisha.gov.in/)** or **[rhodisha.gov.in](https://rhodisha.gov.in/)**
2. Click **"Track Application"** or **"Beneficiary Status"**
3. Enter your Application Reference Number or Aadhaar
4. View: approval status, loan sanction, disbursement dates

## Repayment

- **Monthly EMI**: approximately ₹2,500/month for ₹3 lakh loan over 10 years
- **No interest**: state government pays the interest subsidy directly to the bank
- **Auto-debit**: set up from your bank account for convenience
- **Early repayment**: allowed without penalty

## Mo Ghara vs PMAY-G — Key Difference

| Feature | Mo Ghara | PMAY-G |
|---------|---------|--------|
| **Type** | Loan (0% interest) | Grant (free money) |
| **Amount** | Up to ₹3 lakh | ₹1.2–₹1.8 lakh |
| **Repayment** | Yes (10 years, 0%) | No |
| **Coverage** | BPL families with land | Poorest families (SECC data) |
| **Who applies** | Via bank or portal | Via BDO/Gram Panchayat |

Many families use **both** — PMAY-G grant + Mo Ghara loan for a more complete construction.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I get both PMAY-G and Mo Ghara?

Yes, you can use PMAY-G grant (₹1.2–₹1.8 lakh) AND take a Mo Ghara loan for additional construction funds. Many Odisha families do this to build a better quality house. Inform your BDO about both applications.

### Is Mo Ghara only for new construction?

Primarily yes — Mo Ghara targets families building new pucca houses on their land. Significant renovation/extension of existing structures may also qualify in some cases; verify at your BDO.

### What if I can't repay the EMI for a few months?

Contact your bank branch immediately if you're facing repayment difficulty. Banks can offer a temporary moratorium for genuine cases (natural disaster, illness). Missing EMIs without notice will affect your credit score.

### My Mo Ghara application is rejected. What to do?

Common rejection reasons: income above limit, already has pucca house, no land ownership documents, SECC data mismatch. Visit DRHA office with rejection notice and documents to understand the specific reason. You can file an appeal at the District Collector's office.
