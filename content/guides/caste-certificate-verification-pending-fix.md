## Why Is Your Caste Certificate Verification Pending?

A caste certificate is essential for availing **reservation benefits** in education, government jobs, and schemes for SC/ST/OBC categories. After applying, many applicants face the issue of their certificate being **stuck in "verification pending"** status for weeks or even months.

This guide explains **why verification gets delayed** and **how to track and speed up** the process.

## Common Reasons for Pending Verification

### 1. Field Verification Not Yet Conducted

After you submit the application, a **revenue inspector or patwari** is assigned to verify your caste and residence details. If they haven't visited your area yet, the status remains pending.

**Typical timeline:** Field verification should happen within **7-15 days** of application. Delays occur due to officer workload.

### 2. Document Verification Issues

The verifying officer may have found issues with your documents:
- Father's/grandfather's caste certificate is old or unclear
- Address on documents doesn't match the application
- School records don't mention the caste consistently

### 3. Officer Workload / Staff Shortage

Revenue offices often have a **high volume of applications** and limited staff. Your application may be in a queue waiting for processing.

### 4. Incomplete Application

If your application is missing documents or has errors, it sits in pending status until you provide the missing information. Some portals don't notify you proactively.

### 5. Inter-District Verification Required

If your family is originally from a **different district**, the verification may need to be done in that district — adding significant time to the process.

## How to Track Caste Certificate Status

### Online Tracking

1. Visit your **state's e-District portal** (see table below)
2. Log in with your credentials
3. Go to **"Track Application"** or **"Application Status"**
4. Enter your **application number** or **reference ID**
5. View the current status and officer remarks

## How to Follow Up and Speed Up Verification

### Step 1: Check Online Status First
- Note the exact status and which officer/level it's pending at
- Check if there are any **remarks or queries** from the verifying officer
- If there's a query, respond to it immediately with required documents

### Step 2: Visit the Tehsil Office

1. Visit the **Tehsildar/Naib Tehsildar office** during working hours
2. Carry your **application receipt** and **application number**
3. Ask the clerk to check which stage your application is at
4. If it's pending with the revenue inspector, ask for the inspector's name and contact
5. Request the **Tehsildar** to expedite if it's been more than 15 days

### Step 3: Contact the Revenue Inspector

1. Get the name and phone number of the **assigned revenue inspector/patwari**
2. Call or visit them and ask about the verification status
3. Offer to be available at your address for the field verification
4. Politely request them to prioritise your case

### Step 4: File a Grievance (If Excessively Delayed)

If your application has been pending for **more than 30 days**:

1. **File online grievance** on your state's grievance portal (IGRS/CM Helpline)
2. **Write to the District Magistrate (DM)** — submit a written complaint at the DM office
3. **RTI application** — file an RTI asking for the reason for delay (fee: ₹10)
4. **Call the state helpline** — many states have dedicated helpline numbers

## What to Do If Verification Is Rejected

If the verification results in **rejection**:

1. **Check the rejection reason** on the portal or at the tehsil office
2. Common reasons:
   - Documents insufficient or unclear
   - Caste not verifiable from records
   - Address/residence verification failed
   - Family caste records not found
3. **Fix the issue** and **reapply** with correct documents
4. If the rejection is wrong, **appeal to the SDM or DM**
5. See our [caste certificate application guide](/guide/caste-certificate-online-apply) for document requirements

## Important Tips

1. **Apply well in advance** — don't wait until the last moment before a deadline
2. **Keep all original documents ready** for field verification
3. **Be available at your address** during the verification period (first 2-3 weeks after application)
4. **Follow up every week** if status hasn't changed
5. **Escalate to SDM/DM** if tehsil-level follow up doesn't work
6. **Keep your application receipt safe** — you need it for all tracking and follow-up

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. How long does caste certificate verification take?

Typically 7-21 working days depending on the state. If it's been more than 30 days, follow up at the tehsil office.

### Q2. Can I use a pending caste certificate for college admission?

No. You need the issued certificate. Some institutions accept the application receipt temporarily but require the actual certificate within a deadline.

### Q3. My verification is pending for 2 months. What should I do?

Visit the tehsil office, file a grievance on the state portal, and if needed, write to the District Magistrate. Also file an RTI for the delay reason.

### Q4. Will the revenue inspector visit my home?

Yes, field verification typically involves a visit to your residence to confirm your identity, address, and community status. Be available during daytime hours.

### Q5. Can I track my caste certificate status by SMS?

Some states offer SMS tracking. Check your state's e-District portal for SMS tracking instructions. You can also call the tehsil office with your application number.

### Q6. My application shows "query raised." What does it mean?

The verifying officer needs additional information or documents. Log in to the portal, check the query, and respond with the requested information as soon as possible.

### Q7. Is there a way to get expedited processing?

Some states offer tatkal (express) processing for an additional fee. Otherwise, following up at the tehsil office and filing grievances can help speed up the process.
