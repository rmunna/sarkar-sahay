## What is UP Startup Policy?

**Uttar Pradesh Startup Policy** (also called Udyam Unnati) is the state government's comprehensive startup ecosystem initiative that provides **financial incentives, seed funding, tax reimbursements, and infrastructure support** to startups registered and operating in UP.

The policy targets technology, agritech, healthtech, edtech, and manufacturing startups, with special incentives for women and SC/ST entrepreneurs.

---

## Key Benefits for Startups

| Benefit | Amount / Details |
|---------|-----------------|
| Seed fund | ₹5 lakh to ₹25 lakh (equity-free grant) |
| GST reimbursement | 100% GST reimbursed for 3 years |
| Patent fee reimbursement | Up to ₹2 lakh for Indian patent; ₹5 lakh for international |
| Stamp duty waiver | 100% stamp duty exemption on land/lease |
| Electricity subsidy | 100% electricity duty exemption for 5 years |
| Incubation support | Free desk space at government incubators |
| Mentorship | Connects to experienced mentors via UP Startup Conclave |

---

## Who is Eligible?

**To qualify under UP Startup Policy:**
- Entity is a **DPIIT-recognized startup** (registered at dpiit.gov.in) **OR** has applied for recognition
- The startup is **incorporated and operational in UP** (GST registration in UP required)
- Business is in operation for **less than 10 years** from date of incorporation
- Annual turnover does not exceed **₹100 crore**

**Enhanced benefits for:**
- Women-led startups: Additional 25% on seed fund
- SC/ST founder startups: Additional 25% on seed fund
- Agritech / rural-focused startups: Priority in incubation access

## Seed Fund — Amounts and Criteria

| Stage | Amount |
|-------|--------|
| Idea stage (pre-revenue) | ₹5 lakh (grant) |
| Early stage (prototype ready) | ₹10 lakh (grant) |
| Growth stage (some revenue) | ₹25 lakh (convertible note/grant) |

Selection is through an application + pitch to UP Startup Review Committee.

---

## How to Apply

### Step 1 — DPIIT Recognition

If not yet recognized, apply at **[dpiit.gov.in/startups](https://dpiit.gov.in)** to get DPIIT Startup recognition certificate.

### Step 2 — Register on UP Startup Portal

Go to **[startup.up.gov.in](https://startup.up.gov.in)**:
- Create company account
- Upload: incorporation certificate, DPIIT certificate, GST registration (UP), PAN, pitch deck

### Step 3 — Apply for Specific Benefits

After registration:
- Apply for seed fund (submit business plan)
- Apply for GST reimbursement (after GST filings)
- Apply for patent support (after filing patent)

### Step 4 — Review and Disbursement

The UP Startup Policy Committee reviews applications. Seed fund is disbursed in tranches upon milestone completion.

Free or subsidized desk space for UP-registered startups.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. I am a student at AKTU and have an idea. Can I apply for the UP seed fund?**

Yes, if you have incorporated a company (even a Private Limited or LLP) and applied for DPIIT recognition. Student startups are welcome. You can also access AKTU's own incubator facilities.

**2. My startup is in Delhi but I plan to expand to UP. Can I get UP benefits?**

You need a GST registration and operational presence in UP. If you register a branch or relocate your entity to UP, you become eligible for UP Startup Policy benefits.

**3. Is the ₹5-25 lakh seed fund a loan or grant?**

At idea and early stage, it is an **equity-free grant** (you don't repay). At growth stage, part may be a convertible note. Check the UP startup portal for current year's terms.

**4. What sectors are NOT eligible?**

Liquor, tobacco, gambling, real estate (pure), and traditional retail businesses (without technology innovation) are typically excluded. Manufacturing with technology/innovation qualifies.

**5. How long does the GST reimbursement take?**

GST reimbursement is processed quarterly. File your GST returns → apply on the portal → reimbursement credited to bank in 60–90 days.
