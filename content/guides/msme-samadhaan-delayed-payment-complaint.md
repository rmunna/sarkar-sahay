# MSME Samadhaan — File Complaint for Delayed Payment by Large Buyers

If you're an MSME (Micro, Small, Medium Enterprise) and a large buyer hasn't paid you within the stipulated period, you're entitled to **compound interest** at 3× the RBI bank rate. You can file a complaint at the **MSME Samadhaan portal** and force the buyer to either pay or face legal proceedings.

> **Key law**: Section 15–16 of the MSMED Act, 2006 mandates payment to MSMEs within 45 days (or agreed credit period if less than 45 days). Interest for late payment: compounded at 3× RBI bank rate (~14.5% p.a. as of 2025).

## The 45-Day Payment Rule — What It Means

| Scenario | Payment Deadline |
|----------|----------------|
| No written agreement | 15 days from date of supply |
| Written agreement with credit period ≤ 45 days | As per agreement (max 45 days) |
| Written agreement with credit period > 45 days | **Still capped at 45 days** — agreement is void for anything beyond |

**Important**: The 45-day rule applies regardless of what the purchase order or contract says. Buyers cannot contractually extend beyond 45 days for MSME suppliers.

**Budget 2024 — Income Tax Disallowance**: If a company pays an MSME supplier after the 45-day period, that payment is **not deductible as business expense** in that financial year — it gets deducted only in the year of actual payment. This has created strong financial incentive for large companies to pay on time.

## Who Can Use MSME Samadhaan?

You can use Samadhaan portal if:
- You are registered as **Udyam** (MSME registration)
- Your buyer (the one who hasn't paid) is **NOT an MSME** (i.e., they're a large enterprise or government entity)
- The unpaid invoice is for goods supplied or services rendered

**Note**: Samadhaan is specifically for MSME vs large-buyer disputes. MSME vs MSME payment disputes follow regular civil court route.

## How to File Complaint at samadhaan.msme.gov.in

### Step 1: Ensure You Have Udyam Registration

1. You must have an active **Udyam Registration Certificate** at [udyamregistration.gov.in](https://udyamregistration.gov.in/)
2. Confirm your MSME category (Micro/Small/Medium)

### Step 2: Login to Samadhaan Portal

1. Visit **[samadhaan.msme.gov.in](https://samadhaan.msme.gov.in/)**
2. Click **"Entrepreneur Login"**
3. Login using your **Udyam Registration Number** + mobile OTP

### Step 3: File the Application

1. Click **"File Application"**
2. Enter buyer/respondent details:
   - Company name
   - PAN/GSTIN of the buyer
   - Address
3. Enter transaction details:
   - Invoice numbers and dates
   - Invoice amounts
   - Date of supply/service completion
   - Date of payment (if partial)
   - Outstanding amount
4. Upload:
   - Purchase order copies
   - Invoice copies
   - Delivery challan / proof of supply
   - Emails/communications showing non-payment
5. Specify the relief sought (full outstanding + interest)
6. Submit — receive **Application Reference Number**

### Step 4: What Happens Next

1. Application is forwarded to the **MSME Facilitation Council (MSEFC)** of the state where the buyer is located
2. Buyer (respondent) is notified and given a chance to respond
3. If buyer agrees: Settlement through conciliation (mediated settlement)
4. If no conciliation within 45 days: MSEFC passes an **Award** (like a court decree)
5. Award can be executed as a decree of civil court if buyer still doesn't pay

**Typical timeline**: 3–6 months for conciliation; 12–18 months for formal award.

## Interest Calculation on Delayed Payment

| Item | Rate |
|------|------|
| **Compound interest rate** | 3× RBI Bank Rate |
| **RBI Bank Rate (2025)** | ~6.5% → 3× = **~19.5% compound p.a.** |
| **TDS on interest** | Interest paid as per MSMED Act is exempt from TDS |

**Example**:
- Invoice: ₹5,00,000
- Due date: September 15, 2024
- Actual payment date: March 15, 2025 (6 months late)
- Interest ≈ ₹5,00,000 × 19.5% × (6/12) = **₹48,750**

The buyer owes ₹5,48,750 (principal + interest).

## MSME-1 Return — Buyer's Mandatory Disclosure

Companies with **≥ 250 employees** must file the **MSME-1 half-yearly return** with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) disclosing:
- All outstanding amounts owed to MSME suppliers
- If overdue beyond 45 days: Reason for delay

**Filing deadline**: April 30 (for October–March period) and October 31 (for April–September period).

If companies fail to file MSME-1: Fine up to ₹25,000 per director per day of default.

This creates a strong compliance incentive for public companies and large private companies.

## What If the Buyer Is a Government Department?

If a government department or PSU hasn't paid:
1. **Samadhaan portal works** — Government entities are also covered under MSMED Act
2. Additionally, file complaint at **CPSEs MSME Grievance Portal** (for Central PSU buyers)
3. For state government: State MSME department grievance redressal
4. Last resort: Writ petition in High Court for unreasonable payment delays

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My buyer says they have "cash flow issues" and can't pay in 45 days. Is that valid?

No — "cash flow issues" is not a legal defense under the MSMED Act. The 45-day rule is absolute. You can file the Samadhaan complaint regardless of the buyer's reasons.

### I don't have Udyam registration. Can I still use Samadhaan?

No — Samadhaan requires Udyam registration. Get registered at [udyamregistration.gov.in](https://udyamregistration.gov.in/) first (it's free and only requires Aadhaar + PAN). Then file the Samadhaan complaint.

### The buyer has paid some amount but not the interest. Do I get the interest?

Yes — you're entitled to compound interest on the delayed period even if the principal is now paid. Include the interest claim in your Samadhaan application.

### I supplied goods 2 years ago and wasn't paid. Is there a time limit to complain?

The Limitation Act applies — you have 3 years from the date the debt became due to file the complaint. For a 2022 invoice, the window is still open in 2025.
