# Axis Bank KYC Update Online 2026 — Without Branch Visit

Axis Bank conducts periodic KYC verification (usually every 5–8 years). If you receive a KYC update notice, you can complete it entirely online through the Axis Mobile app or Axis net banking — without visiting a branch.

---

## Why Axis Bank Is Asking for KYC Update

| Reason | Axis Bank Action |
|--------|-----------------|
| RBI periodic re-KYC mandate | Debit transactions blocked after deadline |
| Address change in records | Update required within 30 days of change |
| High-value account activity | Enhanced due diligence required |
| Account inactive (>2 years) | KYC mandatory before reactivation |

---

## Method 1: Axis Mobile App (Recommended)

### Video KYC (Instant Verification)

1. Open **Axis Mobile** app → Login
2. Go to **"Profile"** → **"KYC Update"** or look for **"Complete Your KYC"** banner
3. Select **"Video KYC"**
4. Grant camera + microphone permissions
5. Keep ready: Original Aadhaar + PAN under good lighting
6. Axis executive joins within minutes
7. During call:
   - Show Aadhaar (front → back) to camera
   - Show PAN card
   - Answer: occupation, income, current address
8. **KYC completed on call — SMS within 1 hour**

### Document Upload Method

1. Axis Mobile → **"My Profile"** → **"Update KYC"**
2. Select documents:
   - Aadhaar (front + back photos)
   - PAN card photo
   - Live selfie (taken in-app)
3. Submit → Get reference number
4. **Processing: 24–48 hours**

---

## Method 2: Axis Bank Net Banking

1. Login at **[axisbank.com](https://www.axisbank.com)** → NetBanking
2. **"My Accounts"** → **"Profile"** → **"KYC Documents"**
3. Upload:
   - Aadhaar (front + back — self-attested with signature + date)
   - PAN card
   - Photo (recent passport-size)
4. Submit → Reference number
5. **Processing: 2–4 working days**

---

## Method 3: Axis Aadhaar OTP KYC

For customers with mobile number linked to Aadhaar:

1. Axis Mobile app → KYC Update → **"Aadhaar-based KYC"**
2. Enter Aadhaar number
3. OTP sent to Aadhaar-registered mobile
4. Enter OTP → UIDAI verifies Aadhaar digitally
5. **No document upload needed** — Aadhaar data fetched electronically
6. **Fastest method — KYC updated in minutes**

---

## Method 4: Axis Bank Branch

1. Visit any Axis Bank branch (home branch not required)
2. Ask for **"KYC Updation Form"**
3. Submit self-attested copies:
   - Aadhaar (front + back)
   - PAN
   - Passport-size photo
4. Show originals for verification
5. **Same-day processing**

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Axis Bank KYC — how long does Video KYC take?**
The video call itself is 3–5 minutes. KYC is approved on the call. SMS confirmation comes within 30–60 minutes. Avoid lunch hours (1–2 PM) for faster executive availability.

**My Axis KYC document was rejected — why?**
Reasons: Name on document doesn't match bank records exactly (check spelling), Aadhaar photo is too dark/blurry, document is expired. Retry with a fresh clear photo in good natural light.

**Can I update Axis Bank KYC for my demat + bank account together?**
Axis Bank savings account KYC covers linked accounts. For Axis Securities (demat/trading) KYC: Login to Axis Direct portal separately → "My Profile" → KYC Update.

**Axis Bank asked for "enhanced KYC" for my account — what's this?**
Enhanced KYC is required for high-value accounts (typically >₹50 lakh balance or high transaction volume). You'll need: 3 months bank statement + ITR or salary slip + Aadhaar + PAN. This must be done at branch for first-time enhanced KYC.

**ICICI rejected my KYC status shows "submitted" but account still restricted?**
Allow 2–3 working days for processing. If account is still restricted after 3 days: Call Axis customer care **1860-419-5555** or **1800-419-5577** with your KYC reference number.
