# Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana 2026 — ₹1,500/Month for Maharashtra Women

**Quick answer:** Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana gives ₹1,500/month (₹18,000/year) to women aged 21–65 in Maharashtra with family income below ₹2.5 lakh. Apply at [ladakibahin.maharashtra.gov.in](https://ladakibahin.maharashtra.gov.in) with Aadhaar and ration card. Payments go directly to Aadhaar-linked bank account.

## What is Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana?

Majhi Ladki Bahin (My Dear Sister) Yojana is a welfare scheme launched by the Maharashtra government to provide **₹1,500 per month (₹18,000 per year)** to eligible women in the state. The scheme aims to empower women economically, improve their quality of life, and promote financial independence.

It is Maharashtra's response to similar women-focused welfare schemes in other states and has been designed to cover a wide section of women across the state.

## Who is Eligible?

### Eligibility Criteria
- **Women aged 21 to 65 years**
- Must be a **resident of Maharashtra** (domicile)
- Must hold a **valid ration card** issued in Maharashtra
- Family annual income should not exceed **₹2,50,000**
- Must have an Aadhaar-linked **bank account in her own name**

### Who is NOT Eligible
- Women who are income tax payers
- Government employees (state/central) or their spouses
- Women receiving more than ₹1,500 per month from other government schemes
- Families owning a four-wheeler (tractor excluded)
- Women who are elected representatives (MP/MLA/MLC/local body head)

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** of the applicant
- **Ration card** (yellow or orange — BPL/Antyodaya or priority household)
- **Bank account passbook** (in the applicant's name, linked with Aadhaar)
- **Domicile certificate** or residence proof in Maharashtra
- **Income certificate** (annual income below ₹2,50,000)
- **Voter ID** (optional, for address verification)
- **Passport-size photograph**
- **Mobile number** linked with Aadhaar

## How to Apply

### Online Application
1. Visit [ladakibahin.maharashtra.gov.in](https://ladakibahin.maharashtra.gov.in)
2. Click on **"New Registration"**
3. Enter your Aadhaar number and verify through OTP
4. Fill in personal details — name, date of birth, address, marital status
5. Enter ration card number and bank account details
6. Upload photograph and documents
7. Submit and save the application reference number

### Offline Application
1. Visit your nearest **Aaple Sarkar Seva Kendra** or **Gram Panchayat / Ward office**
2. Collect the Ladki Bahin application form
3. Fill in all details with supporting documents
4. Submit at the designated counter
5. Collect acknowledgment receipt

### Through Nari Shakti Doot (Women Empowerment Volunteers)
- Government-appointed volunteers assist with door-to-door applications
- They help fill forms and upload documents
- Available in rural and urban areas

## Application Status Check

1. Visit the official portal
2. Click on **"Check Status"**
3. Enter application number or Aadhaar number
4. View current status and payment history

## Verification Process

1. Aadhaar and ration card verified electronically
2. Income certificate cross-checked with government databases
3. Field verification by local authorities (if needed)
4. Taluka/Ward level approval
5. District-level final approval
6. Payment initiated from the next cycle

## Important Tips

- **Ration card color matters** — yellow and orange card holders get priority; ensure your ration card category is correct
- **Aadhaar-bank linking** — verify through your bank that Aadhaar seeding is active
- **Apply through official channels only** — beware of fake websites and agents charging fees
- **Income certificate** — get a fresh one from the Tehsildar if yours is old
- **Keep your mobile active** — all OTPs and payment notifications go to your Aadhaar-linked mobile

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can unmarried women apply for Ladki Bahin?
Yes, all women aged 21-65 meeting the income and residency criteria are eligible regardless of marital status.

### Is there a separate scheme for widows in Maharashtra?
Maharashtra has the Sanjay Gandhi Niradhar Anudan Yojana for widows, destitute, and disabled persons. Widows can apply for Ladki Bahin as well if eligible.

### Can I receive both Ladki Bahin and Shravan Bal pension?
If you're receiving less than ₹1,500 from Shravan Bal or other pensions, you may receive the difference under Ladki Bahin. If already receiving ₹1,500+, you cannot avail both.

### What if I don't have a ration card?
A ration card is a key eligibility document. Apply for one at your local Tahsildar office or through the Maharashtra PDS portal before applying for Ladki Bahin.

### Will the amount increase in the future?
The government may revise the amount based on budget and policy decisions. The scheme started at ₹1,500 and may increase in future announcements.

### How is this different from Ladli Behna of MP?
Ladli Behna (MP) provides ₹1,250/month while Ladki Bahin (Maharashtra) provides ₹1,500/month. Both target married and eligible women but have different eligibility criteria based on their respective states. See our [MP Ladli Behna guide](/guide/madhya-pradesh-ladli-behna) for comparison.
