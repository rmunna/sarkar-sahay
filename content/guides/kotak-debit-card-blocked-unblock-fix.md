# Kotak Bank Debit Card Blocked / Not Working — Fix Guide 2026

Kotak Mahindra Bank (including Kotak 811 virtual debit card) issues can be resolved via the Kotak Mobile Banking app. Most fixes take under 3 minutes.

---

## Kotak 811 Virtual Card vs Physical Card

| Feature | Kotak 811 Virtual Card | Kotak Physical Debit Card |
|---------|----------------------|--------------------------|
| Issued | Immediately on account opening | After full KYC + 7 days delivery |
| Use | Online transactions / UPI | ATM + POS + Online |
| International | Limited | Enabled via app |
| ATM | ❌ Not applicable | ✅ Yes |
| Blocked | Unblock via app | Unblock via app |

---

## Fix 1: Reset Kotak ATM PIN

**Blocked after wrong PIN attempts:**

1. Open **Kotak Mobile Banking** app → Login
2. **"Cards"** → Select physical debit card
3. **"Reset PIN"** / **"Generate PIN"**
4. Enter card last 6 digits + expiry
5. OTP on Kotak-registered mobile
6. Set new 4-digit ATM PIN
7. **Works immediately**

---

## Fix 2: Unblock Temporarily Blocked Kotak Card

1. Kotak Mobile → **"Cards"** → Select blocked card
2. **"Unblock Card"** → Confirm
3. Card active immediately

---

## Fix 3: Enable Kotak International Transactions

1. Kotak Mobile → **"Cards"** → **"Card Controls"**
2. **"International Transactions"** → Toggle ON
3. Works immediately

---

## Fix 4: Enable Kotak Online/e-Commerce Transactions

1. Kotak Mobile → **"Cards"** → **"Card Controls"**
2. **"Online Transactions"** → Enable

---

## Fix 5: Kotak 811 Virtual Card — Fix Online Payment Issues

If Kotak 811 virtual card is failing for online payments:
1. Kotak Mobile → **"Cards"** → Virtual card details
2. Note: Card number (16 digits), expiry, CVV
3. Ensure "Online Transactions" is enabled
4. Try payment again with the 16-digit virtual card number
5. If card blocked: **Complete Video KYC first** (811 minimum KYC expiry blocks all card functions)

---

## Fix 6: Request New Kotak Physical Debit Card

1. Kotak Mobile → **"Cards"** → **"Request Debit Card"**
2. Select card type: Platinum / Elite
3. Delivery address confirmed
4. **New card: 5–7 working days**

**Kotak Customer Care: 1860-266-0811**

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Kotak 811 debit card declined everywhere — why?**
Most likely cause: Kotak 811 minimum KYC has expired (12 months from account opening without Video KYC). This blocks ALL card functions. Fix: Complete Video KYC via Kotak app. Once KYC done: Card works again.

**Kotak physical card vs 811 virtual — which is better for daily use?**
Physical card: For ATM + POS + online. 811 virtual: Online only. Get physical card issued after completing Video KYC. Both can be active simultaneously.

**Kotak debit card for Netflix / streaming — works?**
Yes — if "Online Transactions" is enabled in Card Controls. Kotak Visa debit card works on most streaming platforms. Enter card details + OTP for subscription payment.

**Kotak 811 — I never applied for physical card. How do I get one?**
Complete Video KYC → Your account upgrades to full KYC → Apply for physical card via Kotak app → Delivered in 7–10 days.

**Kotak ATM card works at Kotak ATM but not at SBI ATM — why?**
Could be: SBI ATM network issue, or "Other Bank ATM" transactions disabled. Check Kotak Mobile → Card Controls → "ATM" toggle. Also try another SBI/HDFC ATM — it may be that specific machine's issue.
