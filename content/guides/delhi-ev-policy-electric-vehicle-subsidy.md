## What is the Delhi EV Policy?

The **Delhi Electric Vehicle Policy** provides **purchase subsidies and other incentives** to residents who buy electric vehicles (EVs) in Delhi. The policy is among the most generous state EV policies in India, offering:

- **₹5,000** subsidy on electric 2-wheelers
- **₹30,000** subsidy on electric 4-wheelers (private cars)
- **₹30,000** subsidy on electric autos and e-rickshaws
- Road tax and registration fee waiver
- Additional incentive for scrapping old petrol vehicles

## Who is Eligible?

**You ARE eligible if:**
- You are purchasing a **new electric vehicle** from a dealer in Delhi
- You are a **Delhi resident** (Aadhaar with Delhi address, or voter ID)
- The vehicle is **registered in Delhi**
- You apply within the deadline after purchase (typically 6 months)

## How to Claim the Subsidy

### Step 1 — Buy an EV from Delhi Dealer

Purchase your EV from any authorized dealer in Delhi. The vehicle must be:
- A new EV (not second-hand)
- Registered in Delhi (at the RTO)
- On the approved vehicle list

### Step 2 — Register on EV Portal

1. Go to **[ev.delhi.gov.in](https://ev.delhi.gov.in)**
2. Register with your mobile number and Aadhaar
3. Login

### Step 3 — Submit Claim

1. Click **"Apply for Incentive"**
2. Select vehicle category
3. Enter:
   - Vehicle registration number (Delhi RC)
   - Purchase invoice details
   - Bank account number (for subsidy transfer)
4. Upload:
   - Invoice copy
   - Aadhaar
   - Vehicle RC (registration certificate)

### Step 4 — Subsidy Credited

After verification (usually 2–4 weeks), subsidy is credited directly to your bank account via DBT.

## Road Tax and Registration Waiver

One of Delhi's biggest EV benefits — you **pay zero road tax and zero registration fee** when buying an EV in Delhi. This alone saves:
- 2-wheelers: ₹2,000–₹5,000
- 4-wheelers: ₹50,000–₹2,00,000 (depending on vehicle cost)

The waiver is applied automatically at the RTO when you register your EV.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. I bought an electric scooter in UP and registered it there. Can I claim Delhi subsidy?**

No. The vehicle must be **registered in Delhi** to claim Delhi EV subsidy.

**2. The portal shows the 4-wheeler subsidy is exhausted. What do I do?**

Delhi's 4-wheeler subsidy has a cap (first 1,000 buyers per year). If exhausted, wait for the next cycle announcement or check for alternative benefits (road tax waiver still applies).

**3. Can I claim subsidy on an electric e-rickshaw I bought for commercial use?**

Yes. E-rickshaws used commercially (passenger transport) are specifically included in the Delhi EV policy with ₹30,000 subsidy.

**4. I am an auto driver. Does the Delhi EV policy help me switch to an electric auto?**

Yes. Delhi EV policy has specific provisions for converting petrol autos to electric autos — subsidy + interest subsidy on the conversion loan. Contact the Transport Department for auto conversion details.

**5. Is the road tax waiver automatic or do I apply for it?**

The road tax waiver is **automatic** — when your electric vehicle is registered at the Delhi RTO, no road tax is charged. You don't need to apply separately for this.
