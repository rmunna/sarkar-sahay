# Gujarat Domicile Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **domicile certificate** in Gujarat is issued by the Revenue Department (Mamlatdar) and is required for GUJCET/NEET Gujarat state quota (85%) admissions, GPSC government job applications, Gujarat government scholarships, and state scheme eligibility. Applications go through **Digital Gujarat** — Gujarat's integrated e-services platform.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [digitalgujarat.gov.in](https://digitalgujarat.gov.in) or **Antyodaya Seva Kendra**. Issued by Mamlatdar within **15–30 days**.

---

## Gujarat Domicile Certificate — Key Facts

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Minimum residence** | Typically 10–15 years (varies by purpose) |
| **Issuing authority** | Mamlatdar (Taluka Development Officer) |
| **Processing time** | 15–30 days |
| **Fee** | ₹20–30 (Digital Gujarat) |
| **Validity** | Permanent once issued |
| **Portal** | digitalgujarat.gov.in |

---

## Why You Need Gujarat Domicile Certificate

- **GUJCET/NEET Gujarat state quota (85%)** — domicile mandatory for ACPC counselling
- **ACPC (Admission Committee for Professional Courses)** — state quota engineering/pharmacy
- **GPSC (Gujarat PSC)** — Gujarat government jobs (most posts require Gujarat domicile)
- **GSSSB (Gujarat Secondary Service Selection Board)** — state recruitment
- **Gujarat state scholarship** — e-Samaj Kalyan scholarships for SC/ST/OBC residents
- **Mukhyamantri schemes** — Atmanirbhar Gujarat schemes

---

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory — with Gujarat address)
- **Ration card** (Gujarat ration card showing address)
- **Voter ID** (Gujarat voter enrollment)
- **School/college certificates** — TC from Gujarat schools
- **Utility bills** — electricity/water bills at Gujarat address
- Self-declaration of residency period in Gujarat
- **Voter ID of parent** (if claiming through parental Gujarat residency)
- Passport-size photograph

## How to Apply Online — Digital Gujarat Portal

1. Go to [digitalgujarat.gov.in](https://digitalgujarat.gov.in)
2. Register / log in with Aadhaar-linked mobile number
3. Click **"Revenue Department"** → **"Domicile Certificate"**
4. Fill form:
   - Applicant name, address (district, taluka, village)
   - Duration of residence in Gujarat
   - Purpose of certificate
5. Upload documents (Aadhaar, ration card, voter ID, utility bills)
6. Pay fee: **₹20–30** (online)
7. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via Antyodaya Seva Kendra / Seva Setu

1. Visit nearest **Antyodaya Seva Kendra** (jan seva kendra) or **Seva Setu camp**
2. Carry Aadhaar, ration card, voter ID, school TCs
3. Operator submits application on Digital Gujarat
4. Service charge: approximately ₹30–60
5. Collect acknowledgement

---

## Status Check & Download

1. Log in to [digitalgujarat.gov.in](https://digitalgujarat.gov.in)
2. Click **"My Applications"** → view domicile certificate status
3. Once approved: download digitally signed PDF
4. Valid for ACPC counselling, GPSC, e-Samaj Kalyan scholarship portal

## GUJCET/NEET Gujarat State Quota 2026

For **NEET 2026 Gujarat state quota** (ACPC medical college counselling):
1. Gujarat domicile certificate — residence in Gujarat required
2. OR studied in Gujarat schools for minimum required period
3. ACPC verifies domicile during counselling document verification
4. Certificate should be from Mamlatdar — Digital Gujarat digital certificate is accepted
5. Apply 4–6 weeks before NEET result / counselling begins

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many years of residence for Gujarat domicile?

Gujarat typically requires **10–15 years** of continuous residence for domicile certificate. The exact requirement may vary by SDO district order. If you've lived in Gujarat from childhood, school TCs + utility bills cover the period easily.

### GPSC — is Gujarat domicile mandatory?

**Yes, for most Gujarat state government posts**, GPSC/GSSSB jobs require Gujarat domicile. Verify in the specific recruitment advertisement — some centrally funded posts may not require domicile.
