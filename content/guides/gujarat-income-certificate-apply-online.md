# Gujarat Income Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **income certificate** in Gujarat is issued by the Revenue Department and is required for scholarship applications, Economically Weaker Section (EWS) eligibility, OBC non-creamy layer certificates, and government scheme benefits. Applications are processed through **Digital Gujarat** — the state's e-governance portal — or at **Antyoday Seva Kendra** (Gujarat's CSC).

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [digitalgujarat.gov.in](https://digitalgujarat.gov.in) or visit nearest Antyoday Seva Kendra / Taluka Mamlatdar office. Processed by Mamlatdar within **15–30 days**.

---

## Why You Need Gujarat Income Certificate

- **SC/ST/OBC/SEBC scholarships** — mandatory income proof
- **EWS (Economically Weaker Section)** certificate — annual income < ₹8 lakh
- **OBC Non-Creamy Layer (NCL)** certificate — income certificate required first
- **BPL ration card** application
- **Government scheme eligibility** — PM Awas Yojana, Kisan credit
- **Fee waivers** in government colleges and hospitals

---

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory for all members)
- **Ration card** (shows family composition)
- Self-declaration of annual income (affidavit or online form)
- For salaried employees: **salary slip** / employer certificate
- For farmers: **7/12 extract** (Satbara) + **8A** from AnyRoR (anyror.gujarat.gov.in)
- Voter ID (additional ID proof)
- Passport-size photograph

---

## How to Apply Online — Digital Gujarat Portal

1. Go to [digitalgujarat.gov.in](https://digitalgujarat.gov.in)
2. Click **"Services"** → Search **"Income Certificate"** or **"Revenue"**
3. Click **"Apply for Income Certificate"**
4. Log in / register with Aadhaar-linked mobile number
5. Fill the application:
   - Name, address (district, taluka, village)
   - Annual family income (declare all sources)
   - Purpose of certificate
6. Upload documents (Aadhaar, ration card, income proof)
7. Pay fee: **₹20–50** (online payment)
8. Submit — note the **Application Reference Number**

---

## How to Apply at Antyoday Seva Kendra / Mamlatdar Office

1. Visit nearest **Antyoday Seva Kendra** (Gujarat's CSC / Jan Seva Kendra)
2. Or visit your **Taluka Mamlatdar office** directly
3. Provide Aadhaar, income documents, and family details
4. Operator fills and submits form
5. Service charge: approximately ₹30–60
6. Collect acknowledgement receipt

---

## Status Check

1. Go to [digitalgujarat.gov.in](https://digitalgujarat.gov.in)
2. Click **"Track Application"** → Enter reference number
3. Or log in → **"My Applications"** → view income certificate status
4. Status flow: Submitted → Mamlatdar Office → Field Verification → Approved

---

## Download Gujarat Income Certificate

Once approved by Mamlatdar:
1. Log in to Digital Gujarat portal
2. Go to **"My Applications"** → Click approved income certificate
3. Download digitally signed PDF
4. Valid for all official purposes
