# PAN-Aadhaar Link Failed: Common Errors and How to Fix

Linking PAN with Aadhaar is mandatory under Section 139AA of the Income Tax Act. If your PAN is not linked with Aadhaar, it becomes **inoperative** — meaning you cannot file ITR, receive refunds, or use PAN for financial transactions. Many taxpayers face errors during the linking process. This guide covers every common error and its fix.

## Common Error Messages

When attempting to link PAN with Aadhaar on the Income Tax e-filing portal, you may see:

- **"Your PAN is already linked with some other Aadhaar"** — PAN mapped to a different Aadhaar number
- **"Name as per PAN does not match with Aadhaar"** — name mismatch between PAN and Aadhaar
- **"Date of birth in PAN and Aadhaar do not match"** — DOB discrepancy
- **"PAN not found / Invalid PAN"** — incorrect PAN entered or PAN deactivated
- **"Aadhaar number is invalid"** — wrong Aadhaar number entered
- **"Your request could not be processed at this time"** — server error
- **"PAN is already linked with given Aadhaar"** — already linked (no action needed)
- **"Payment of fee of Rs.1000 is required"** — late linking fee not paid
- Challan payment successful but linking still shows failed
- OTP not received on mobile during linking

## Step-by-Step Fixes for Each Error

### Fix 1: Name Mismatch Between PAN and Aadhaar

This is the most common error. Your name must match **exactly** in both documents.

**How to check:**
1. Check your name on [PAN verification](https://eportal.incometax.gov.in/iec/foservices/#/pre-login/verifyYourPAN) 
2. Check your name on [e-Aadhaar download](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/)
3. Compare spelling, initials, and order (first name / last name)

**How to fix:**
- **If PAN name is wrong:** Apply for [PAN correction at NSDL](https://www.tin-nsdl.com/) or UTIITSL — costs ₹107 (₹1,007 for addresses outside India)
- **If Aadhaar name is wrong:** Apply for [Aadhaar name correction online](/guide/aadhaar-name-correction-online)
- **Minor variations** (like initials expanded): The system allows some fuzzy matching — try linking anyway after paying the fee

### Fix 2: Date of Birth Mismatch

If your DOB differs between PAN and Aadhaar:

1. Determine which document has the **correct** DOB
2. **Correct PAN DOB:** Apply for PAN correction at [tin-nsdl.com](https://www.tin-nsdl.com/) with DOB proof
3. **Correct Aadhaar DOB:** Visit an Aadhaar enrolment centre with DOB proof (birth certificate, passport, or matriculation certificate)

**Note:** Aadhaar allows DOB correction only once if your DOB was initially recorded as "verified."

### Fix 3: PAN Already Linked with Another Aadhaar

This means your PAN number is associated with a different Aadhaar in UIDAI/Income Tax records. This can happen due to:

- Data entry error during a previous linking attempt
- Someone else's Aadhaar accidentally linked to your PAN

**How to fix:**
1. Raise a grievance on the [Income Tax e-filing portal](https://eportal.incometax.gov.in/)
2. Call the Income Tax helpline at **1800-103-0025**
3. Visit your nearest Income Tax office with PAN card, Aadhaar card, and an application letter
4. UIDAI may also need to verify — contact UIDAI helpline at **1947**

### Fix 4: Payment of ₹1,000 Fee Required

Since the original deadline has passed, a late fee of ₹1,000 is required for linking:

1. Go to [e-Pay Tax](https://eportal.incometax.gov.in/iec/foservices/#/pre-login/e-pay-tax-prelogin) on the Income Tax portal
2. Select **AY 2025-26** (current assessment year)
3. Under **Type of Payment**, select **Other Receipts (500)**
4. Choose the ₹1,000 fee under **Fee for delay in linking PAN with Aadhaar (234H)**
5. Pay via net banking, debit card, or UPI
6. **Wait 4–5 working days** after payment before attempting to link

**Common payment issues:**
- If payment is made but linking fails, the challan may need time to reflect — wait 5 working days
- Check payment status under **e-Pay Tax** → **Payment History**

### Fix 5: OTP Not Received

During linking, an OTP is sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile:

- Ensure your mobile number is registered with Aadhaar — check via [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/)
- If mobile is not linked, [update your Aadhaar mobile number](/guide/aadhaar-update-mobile-email) first
- Check if your phone has network/signal issues
- Wait 2–3 minutes and click **Resend OTP**
- Try during off-peak hours

### Fix 6: Server Error / Request Could Not Be Processed

- Clear browser cache and cookies
- Try a different browser (Chrome recommended)
- Attempt during off-peak hours (early morning or late evening)
- Try on a different day — the portal has scheduled maintenance windows
- Use **incognito/private browsing** mode

## How to Check PAN-Aadhaar Link Status

1. Visit [eportal.incometax.gov.in/iec/foservices/#/pre-login/bl-link-aadhaar](https://eportal.incometax.gov.in/iec/foservices/#/pre-login/bl-link-aadhaar)
2. Enter your PAN and Aadhaar number
3. Click **View Link Aadhaar Status**

Alternatively, send an **SMS** from your registered mobile:
```
UIDPAN <12-digit Aadhaar> <10-digit PAN>
```
Send to **567678** or **56161**.

## What Happens if PAN Becomes Inoperative?

If your PAN is not linked with Aadhaar, it becomes inoperative. This means:

- You **cannot file income tax returns**
- Pending [income tax refunds](/guide/income-tax-refund-not-received-fix) will not be processed
- TDS will be deducted at a **higher rate** (20% instead of normal rates)
- You cannot open new bank accounts or demat accounts using PAN
- Existing investments and transactions may face issues

**To reactivate:** Link your PAN with Aadhaar after paying the ₹1,000 fee. PAN becomes operative again within **30 days** of successful linking.

## When to Contact Helpline

Reach out if:
- Your linking has been stuck in "processing" for over 7 days after fee payment
- PAN is linked to someone else's Aadhaar and the portal won't let you change it
- You've corrected name/DOB but linking still fails
- Your PAN has been marked inoperative despite successful linking

**Income Tax Helpdesk:**
- **Toll-free:** 1800-103-0025
- **Paid:** 1800-103-4215
- **Email:** e-filing helpdesk via the portal
- **e-Nivaran (grievance):** [eportal.incometax.gov.in](https://eportal.incometax.gov.in/) → Grievance

**UIDAI Helpline:** 1947

## Prevention Tips

1. **Keep name and DOB consistent** across PAN and Aadhaar — correct discrepancies early
2. **Save payment challans** — always download and save the fee payment receipt
3. **Register mobile with Aadhaar** — needed for OTP during linking
4. **Check link status periodically** — don't assume linking was successful
5. **Link early** — avoid peak deadline periods when servers are overloaded
6. **Keep both documents updated** — see [Aadhaar-PAN linking guide](/guide/aadhaar-pan-link) for detailed steps

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is there a deadline for PAN-Aadhaar linking?

The original deadline has passed. You can still link by paying a late fee of ₹1,000. It is strongly recommended to link immediately to avoid PAN becoming inoperative.

### My PAN is already inoperative. Can I still link it with Aadhaar?

Yes. Pay the ₹1,000 fee and complete the linking process. Your PAN will become operative again within 30 days of successful linking.

### I paid ₹1,000 but linking still shows error. What should I do?

Wait 4–5 working days for the payment to reflect in the system. If it still fails after that, check your payment status under e-Pay Tax → Payment History on the Income Tax portal and contact the helpline.

### Can I link PAN and Aadhaar if my name has minor spelling differences?

The system allows some fuzzy matching for minor variations. However, if the difference is significant (e.g., completely different spelling), you need to correct the name in either PAN or Aadhaar before linking.

### What if I have two PAN cards?

Having two PANs is illegal under the Income Tax Act. Surrender the duplicate PAN by applying for PAN correction at NSDL/UTIITSL and mention the PAN to be cancelled. Link the valid PAN with your Aadhaar.

### Can NRIs skip PAN-Aadhaar linking?

Aadhaar-PAN linking is mandatory for all PAN holders who are eligible for Aadhaar. NRIs who do not have Aadhaar are currently exempt, but should check the latest CBDT notifications.
