# ITR e-Verification Failed — How to Fix

Filing your Income Tax Return is only half the job. You must **e-verify** it within **30 days** of filing — otherwise your ITR is treated as **not filed**. If your e-verification is failing via Aadhaar OTP, net banking, bank EVC, or DSC, this guide covers every error and fix.

---

## Why Is e-Verification Mandatory?

After you file your ITR on the [Income Tax Portal](https://eportal.incometax.gov.in/), it remains in **"Filed but not verified"** status until you e-verify. Without verification:
- Your return is considered **invalid**
- You won't get any **tax refund**
- You cannot carry forward **losses**

### Verification Deadline
You must e-verify within **30 days** of filing. If you miss this, you can still verify later, but your ITR filing date will be considered as the date of e-verification (with potential late filing consequences).

---

## e-Verification Methods Available

| Method | Requirements | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **Aadhaar OTP** | Aadhaar linked to PAN, mobile linked to Aadhaar | Most common, fastest |
| **Net Banking EVC** | Net banking with a bank that supports income tax e-filing | If Aadhaar OTP fails |
| **Bank Account EVC** | Pre-validated bank account on IT portal | Good alternative |
| **Demat Account EVC** | Pre-validated demat account on IT portal | For investors |
| **Bank ATM (Offline EVC)** | ATM card of supported bank | If all online methods fail |
| **Digital Signature Certificate (DSC)** | Registered DSC on IT portal | For companies/professionals |
| **Physical ITR-V** | Send signed ITR-V to CPC Bangalore by post | Last resort |

## Fix 1: Aadhaar OTP Not Received or Failed

This is the most common e-verification method and the most common failure.

### Problem: OTP Not Received
**Causes:**
- Mobile number not linked to Aadhaar
- UIDAI OTP server overloaded (especially near deadline)
- Telecom network issues

**Fix:**
1. **Verify your mobile number is linked to Aadhaar** — Check on [myAadhaar](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/) → Login → Profile
2. If mobile number is wrong, update it at an Aadhaar Enrolment Centre first (see our [Aadhaar mobile update guide](/guide/change-mobile-number-in-aadhaar-card))
3. **Wait and retry** — If near filing deadline, UIDAI servers get overloaded. Try early morning (6-8 AM) or late night
4. Check if your phone has **DND (Do Not Disturb) enabled** — OTP may be blocked. Disable DND or add UIDAI to whitelist
5. Try on a different device/browser — sometimes browser issues prevent the OTP request from going through

### Problem: OTP Expired
OTP is valid for only **10 minutes**. If you take too long to enter it:
1. Request a new OTP immediately
2. Enter it within 3-4 minutes to be safe
3. Keep the income tax portal page open — don't navigate away

### Problem: "Aadhaar Not Linked to PAN"
You must link PAN to Aadhaar before using this method.
1. Go to [Income Tax Portal → Link Aadhaar](https://eportal.incometax.gov.in/iec/foservices/#/pre-login/bl-link-aadhaar)
2. Enter PAN and Aadhaar details
3. Pay ₹1,000 late linking fee if applicable
4. Wait for linking confirmation (can take 4-5 days)
5. Then retry e-verification

See our [PAN Aadhaar link guide](/guide/pan-aadhaar-link-failed-fix) for detailed steps.

## Fix 2: Net Banking EVC Failed

### Problem: Bank Not Showing on Income Tax Portal
Not all banks support e-verification via net banking. Currently supported banks include SBI, ICICI, HDFC, Axis, Kotak, and others.

**Fix:**
1. Check the list of supported banks on the income tax portal during e-verification
2. If your bank isn't listed, use another method (Aadhaar OTP or Bank Account EVC)

### Problem: Session Timeout After Bank Login
**Fix:**
1. Clear browser cookies and cache
2. Use **Chrome or Edge** (avoid Safari for income tax portal)
3. Disable any ad blockers or pop-up blockers
4. Complete the net banking verification quickly — the session expires in 10-15 minutes
5. Keep only one tab open for the income tax portal

### Problem: "EVC Generation Failed" After Net Banking
**Fix:**
1. Ensure your PAN is registered with your net banking profile
2. Some banks require you to register for income tax e-filing through their net banking portal first
3. Log into your net banking → Look for "Tax" or "Income Tax" section → Register/link your PAN
4. Then retry on the income tax portal

## Fix 3: Bank Account EVC Failed

### Problem: Bank Account Not Pre-Validated
To use Bank Account EVC, your bank account must be **pre-validated** on the income tax portal.

**How to Pre-Validate:**
1. Login to [Income Tax Portal](https://eportal.incometax.gov.in/)
2. Go to **Profile → My Bank Account**
3. Click **Add Bank Account**
4. Enter bank details (account number, IFSC, account type)
5. Validate using EVC via the bank — bank will send a validation request
6. Once validated (shown as "Validated" with a green tick), you can use it for e-verification

### Problem: EVC Not Received on Mobile/Email
**Fix:**
1. Check that your **email and mobile** are correct on the income tax portal profile
2. EVC is sent to the mobile/email registered on the IT portal, not necessarily your bank's number
3. Check spam/junk folder for email EVC
4. Retry after 30 minutes if not received

## Fix 4: DSC Verification Failed

### Problem: DSC Not Recognized
**Fix:**
1. Ensure your DSC is **registered** on the income tax portal → Profile → Register DSC
2. Install the **emsigner** utility (required for DSC on the IT portal)
3. Use **Internet Explorer or Chrome** with the emsigner plugin enabled
4. Ensure DSC is not expired — check validity date on the certificate
5. The PAN on the DSC must match your filing PAN

## Fix 5: e-Verification Deadline Missed

If 30 days have passed and you haven't e-verified:

### Option 1: Late e-Verification
You can still e-verify after 30 days. The ITR will be processed, but the **filing date** will be considered as the date of verification (not the original filing date).

1. Login to income tax portal
2. Go to **e-File → Income Tax Returns → e-Verify Return**
3. Select the relevant assessment year
4. Complete e-verification using any available method

### Option 2: Send Physical ITR-V
If all electronic methods fail:
1. Login to IT portal → **e-File → Income Tax Returns → View Filed Returns**
2. Download **ITR-V** (Acknowledgment)
3. **Print it**, sign in **blue ink**
4. Send via **ordinary post or Speed Post** to:
   > **CPC, Income Tax Department, Bengaluru — 560500**
5. CPC processes it within **30-60 days** after receiving
6. Do NOT send by courier or registered post — only ordinary/Speed Post accepted

## Troubleshooting Common Portal Errors

| Error | Fix |
|---|---|
| "Service temporarily unavailable" | Try during off-peak hours (early morning/late night) |
| "Invalid OTP" | Request a fresh OTP; don't copy-paste with spaces |
| "PAN not linked to Aadhaar" | Link PAN-Aadhaar first, wait 4-5 days, then retry |
| "No return found for e-verification" | Check you're selecting the correct Assessment Year |
| "Already verified" | Your return is already verified — check the filing status |
| Page not loading | Clear cache, try incognito mode, use Chrome/Edge |

## Important Tips

1. **e-Verify immediately after filing** — Don't wait; do it right after submitting your ITR to avoid deadline issues
2. **Keep multiple verification methods ready** — Pre-validate your bank account and ensure Aadhaar-PAN is linked before filing season
3. **Try early morning for Aadhaar OTP** — UIDAI servers are least loaded between 6-8 AM
4. **Use incognito/private browsing** — This avoids cached session conflicts on the income tax portal
5. **Keep ITR-V as backup** — Download and save the ITR-V immediately after filing, in case you need to send it physically

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What happens if I don't e-verify my ITR?
Your ITR is treated as **not filed**. You won't get refunds, can't carry forward losses, and may face penalty for non-filing.

### How many days do I have to e-verify?
**30 days** from the date of filing. After that, you can still verify but with consequences (filing date changes to verification date).

### Can I e-verify ITR from previous years?
Yes. Login to the income tax portal, go to **e-Verify Return**, and select the relevant Assessment Year.

### Which e-verification method is the fastest?
**Aadhaar OTP** — it takes less than 2 minutes when it works. Bank Account EVC is a good backup.

### I filed ITR but the portal shows "Not Verified" — is my ITR valid?
No. Until e-verified, your ITR is **not valid**. Complete e-verification as soon as possible.

### Can I e-verify after the due date of filing?
Yes. e-Verification can be done anytime, even after the filing due date. But do it within 30 days of filing to preserve your original filing date.

### What is the CPC address for sending physical ITR-V?
**Centralized Processing Centre, Income Tax Department, Bengaluru — 560500, Karnataka, India.** Send by ordinary post or Speed Post only.
