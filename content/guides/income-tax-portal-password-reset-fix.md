# Income Tax Portal Password Reset Not Working? How to Fix Login Issues

Struggling to log into the Income Tax e-filing portal? **Password reset failures, OTP not received, and account locked errors** are among the most common complaints about the incometax.gov.in portal. Whether you need to file ITR, check refund status, or download Form 16, you need access.

This guide covers every login issue and how to fix it step by step.

## Common Error Messages

- "Invalid credentials. Please try again"
- "Password reset failed. Please try after some time"
- "OTP not received or expired"
- "Account has been locked due to multiple failed attempts"
- "Your session has expired"
- "Technical error. Please try later"
- "PAN not registered on e-filing portal"

## Method 1: Standard Password Reset (Forgot Password)

### Step-by-Step Process
1. Go to [incometax.gov.in](https://www.incometax.gov.in)
2. Click **"Login"** → Enter your **PAN** as User ID
3. Click **"Forgot Password?"**
4. Enter your **PAN number** and captcha
5. Select reset method:
   - **OTP on registered mobile/email** (easiest)
   - **Aadhaar OTP** (if Aadhaar is linked to PAN)
   - **Digital Signature Certificate (DSC)**
   - **Net Banking**
6. Enter the OTP received
7. Set a **new password** following the rules:
   - Minimum 8 characters, maximum 14 characters
   - At least 1 uppercase letter, 1 lowercase letter
   - At least 1 number and 1 special character
   - Cannot be the same as last 3 passwords
8. Confirm and login with new password

## Method 2: Reset via Aadhaar OTP

Use this when your registered mobile/email is no longer active:

1. On the Forgot Password page, select **"Reset via Aadhaar OTP"**
2. Your Aadhaar must be **linked to your PAN** (check at incometax.gov.in)
3. OTP is sent to the **mobile number registered with Aadhaar** (not e-filing portal)
4. Enter the Aadhaar OTP and set new password
5. This method works even if your e-filing registered email/phone has changed

## Method 3: Reset via Net Banking

1. Select **"Reset via Net Banking"** on the Forgot Password page
2. Choose your bank from the list
3. You'll be redirected to your **bank's net banking login**
4. Log in to net banking
5. After authentication, you're redirected back to set a new password
6. Works with: SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, PNB, BOB, and other major banks

## Fixing Common Issues

### OTP Not Received
1. **Wait 5 minutes** — OTP delivery can be delayed during peak hours
2. Check **SMS spam/blocked** list on your phone
3. Check **email spam folder** for email OTP
4. Ensure your phone has **network signal**
5. Click **"Resend OTP"** (wait for the timer to expire first)
6. If mobile OTP fails, try **email OTP** or vice versa
7. Switch to **Aadhaar OTP method** as fallback

### Account Locked Error
Your account locks after **5 failed login attempts**:
1. **Wait 30 minutes** — the lock automatically releases
2. After 30 minutes, use **Forgot Password** to reset
3. Do NOT keep trying — each failed attempt extends the lock
4. If locked for extended period, call **1800-103-0025**

### "PAN Not Registered" Error
If you see this error, you haven't registered on the new portal:
1. Go to [incometax.gov.in](https://www.incometax.gov.in)
2. Click **"Register"**
3. Select **"Individual"** as taxpayer type
4. Enter your **PAN number**
5. Validate with OTP on your PAN-linked mobile
6. Complete registration and set password

### Portal Showing Technical Error
1. **Clear browser cache and cookies**: Settings → Privacy → Clear Browsing Data
2. Use **Google Chrome** or **Microsoft Edge** (avoid Safari/Firefox for this portal)
3. Try in **Incognito/Private browsing** mode
4. **Disable VPN** and ad blockers
5. Disable any browser extensions
6. Try during **off-peak hours**: before 10 AM or after 8 PM IST
7. Check if portal is under maintenance at [incometax.gov.in](https://www.incometax.gov.in)

### Password Rules Error During Reset
If your new password keeps getting rejected:
- Must be **8-14 characters** long
- Must include: `A-Z`, `a-z`, `0-9`, and special character (`@#$%^&*!`)
- **Cannot** contain your PAN number or name
- **Cannot** be the same as your last 3 passwords
- Example of valid password: `Tax@2026Pass`

### Session Expired Error
1. Complete the reset process **within 15 minutes**
2. Don't open multiple tabs of the portal
3. Don't click the back button — use portal navigation
4. If session expires, start the Forgot Password process again from scratch

## How to Update Registered Mobile/Email

If your registered contact details are outdated:

### Option 1: Update via Profile (If You Can Login)
1. Login to [incometax.gov.in](https://www.incometax.gov.in)
2. Go to **Profile Settings** → **Personal Details**
3. Update mobile number and/or email
4. Verify with OTP sent to new number/email

### Option 2: Update Without Login
1. Use **Aadhaar OTP** or **Net Banking** to reset password first
2. After logging in, immediately update your contact details in Profile
3. Verify the new contact details

### Option 3: Contact Helpdesk
1. Call **1800-103-0025** (toll-free, 8 AM to 8 PM)
2. Email **ask@incometax.gov.in**
3. Provide your PAN, name, and date of birth for verification
4. Request manual update of registered contact details

## Browser and Device Tips

For the best experience on the Income Tax portal:

- **Recommended browsers**: Google Chrome (latest), Microsoft Edge
- **Enable JavaScript** and cookies
- **Disable pop-up blockers** for incometax.gov.in
- **Clear cache** before each login attempt if facing issues
- Use a **desktop/laptop** for complex operations (mobile browser can be buggy)
- Bookmark the correct URL: **www.incometax.gov.in** (not old URLs)
