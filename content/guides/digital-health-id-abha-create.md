# ABHA Health ID: How to Create Your Digital Health Card Online

ABHA (Ayushman Bharat Health Account) is your unique 14-digit digital health ID issued under the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM). It links all your medical records — prescriptions, lab reports, diagnoses — into one digital profile that you control. Creating an ABHA is free and takes under 5 minutes.

## What is ABHA Health ID?

ABHA is a standardized digital health identity for every Indian citizen. Previously called "Health ID" under the National Digital Health Mission (NDHM), it was rebranded to ABHA. Key features:

- **14-digit unique number** (e.g., 91-1234-5678-9012)
- **ABHA address** — a username like yourname@abdm (used to share records)
- **Linked health records** from hospitals, labs, and pharmacies participating in ABDM
- **You control access** — only you decide who sees your health records
- **Free to create** — no charges at any step

---

## Who Can Create ABHA?

- Any **Indian resident** with a valid Aadhaar card, OR
- Any person with a valid **Driving Licence** issued in India
- **No age restriction** — ABHA can be created for minors by parents/guardians
- Available for citizens across **all states and union territories**

---

## Documents Required

You need **one** of the following:

- **Aadhaar Card** (recommended — instant verification via OTP)
- **Driving Licence** (DL number for verification)

Additionally:
- **Mobile number** (linked to Aadhaar for OTP verification, or any active number for DL-based creation)
- **Email address** (optional but recommended)

---

## How to Create ABHA Health ID Online

### Method 1: Using Aadhaar (Recommended)

1. Visit the official ABHA portal at [abha.abdm.gov.in](https://abha.abdm.gov.in/)
2. Click **"Create ABHA Number"** or **"Register"**
3. Select **"Using Aadhaar"** as the verification method
4. Enter your **12-digit Aadhaar number**
5. Check the consent box and click **"Submit"**
6. Enter the **OTP** sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile number
7. Verify your details — name, date of birth, gender, and address will auto-fill from Aadhaar
8. Create your **ABHA address** (e.g., yourname@abdm) — this is your health ID username
9. Set a **password** for your ABHA account
10. Your **14-digit ABHA number** is generated instantly
11. Download your **ABHA card** (PDF) from the dashboard

### Method 2: Using Driving Licence

1. Visit [abha.abdm.gov.in](https://abha.abdm.gov.in/)
2. Click **"Create ABHA Number"**
3. Select **"Using Driving Licence"**
4. Enter your **Driving Licence number**
5. Enter your **name, date of birth, and gender** exactly as on the DL
6. Verify via **OTP** sent to your mobile number
7. Complete profile details and create your ABHA address
8. Your ABHA number is generated after verification

### Using the ABHA Mobile App

1. Download the **ABHA app** from Google Play Store or Apple App Store
2. Open the app and tap **"Create ABHA"**
3. Follow the same Aadhaar/DL verification steps as above
4. Your ABHA card is stored in the app for easy access

---

## How to Download Your ABHA Card

1. Log in to [abha.abdm.gov.in](https://abha.abdm.gov.in/) with your ABHA number or address
2. Go to **"My Profile"** or **"Dashboard"**
3. Click **"Download ABHA Card"**
4. The card downloads as a **PDF** with your photo, ABHA number, and QR code

---

## How to Link Health Records

1. Log in to your ABHA account on the portal or app
2. Go to **"Link Health Records"**
3. Search for the hospital or health facility
4. The facility sends a link request; approve it
5. Your records from that facility appear in your ABHA timeline

---

## Important Tips

1. **Use Aadhaar for fastest creation** — Aadhaar-based verification is instant; DL-based may take longer for manual checks
2. **Keep your ABHA address memorable** — You'll share this with hospitals, like an email address for health
3. **Link records proactively** — Ask ABDM-registered hospitals to link your visit records to your ABHA
4. **Update mobile number** — If your Aadhaar mobile changes, update it at [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/) first. See our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) for details
5. **Privacy is in your hands** — You can revoke any facility's access to your records anytime from your ABHA dashboard

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is ABHA Health ID mandatory?
No. Creating an ABHA is **completely voluntary**. However, many government and private hospitals are now integrating with ABDM, making it useful for managing your medical records digitally.

### Is there any fee to create ABHA?
No. ABHA creation is **100% free**. Do not pay anyone claiming to create your ABHA for a fee.

### What is the difference between ABHA number and ABHA address?
Your **ABHA number** is a 14-digit unique identifier (like Aadhaar for health). Your **ABHA address** (e.g., yourname@abdm) is a user-friendly username you use to share and receive health records.

### Can I create ABHA without Aadhaar?
Yes. You can create ABHA using your **Driving Licence**. However, the Aadhaar method is faster and recommended.

### Can I have multiple ABHA accounts?
No. Each person can have only **one ABHA number**. If you accidentally create a duplicate, contact the ABDM helpline to merge or delete the extra account.

### How do I update my details in ABHA?
Log in to [abha.abdm.gov.in](https://abha.abdm.gov.in/), go to **My Profile**, and edit your details. Changes linked to Aadhaar (name, DOB) require Aadhaar update first.

### What is the ABHA helpline number?
You can reach ABDM support at the **toll-free number 1800-114-477** or email at ndhm@nha.gov.in.
