# Marriage Assistance for BPL Brides – Puducherry

The Government of Puducherry provides financial assistance to brides from BPL (Below Poverty Line) families for their marriage expenses. The scheme aims to reduce the financial burden of weddings on economically weaker families and support girls from underprivileged backgrounds.

## What is the Marriage Assistance Scheme?

This scheme provides a one-time financial grant to BPL brides at the time of their marriage. The amount includes cash assistance and in some cases, a gold coin or its equivalent value. The benefit is provided through the Department of Social Welfare.

### Assistance Amount

| Component | Amount |
|-----------|--------|
| Cash assistance | ₹25,000 – ₹50,000* |
| Gold coin / equivalent | 4 grams (or cash equivalent)* |

*Exact amounts vary as per current government orders. Verify latest amounts on the official portal.*

## Who is Eligible?

1. **BPL family:** Bride must belong to a BPL family (with valid BPL certificate/ration card)
2. **Age:** Bride must be 18 years or above at the time of marriage
3. **Residency:** Bride's family must be residents of Puducherry UT for at least 5 years
4. **Income:** Annual family income below the prescribed BPL limit
5. **First marriage:** Generally applicable for the bride's first marriage
6. **Education:** In some categories, the bride should have completed at least Class 10 (varies by scheme variant)

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar card of bride (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- Age proof of bride (birth certificate / SSC certificate)
- BPL certificate / ration card
- Income certificate from Revenue Department
- Residence proof (5+ years in Puducherry)
- Marriage invitation card
- Educational certificates (if applicable)
- Bank passbook of bride (Aadhaar-linked)
- Passport-size photographs of bride
- Community certificate (for SC/ST, if claiming additional benefits)

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### How to Apply

1. **Collect application form:** Obtain the marriage assistance form from the Social Welfare Department office or Commune Panchayat
2. **Fill the form:** Enter bride's personal details, family income, BPL status, and marriage details
3. **Attach documents:** Self-attested copies of all required documents
4. **Submit before marriage:** Submit the application at least 15–30 days before the marriage date
5. **Verification:** Social welfare inspector verifies the family's BPL status, income, and residence
6. **Approval:** Application approved by the competent authority
7. **Disbursement:** Cash assistance and/or gold coin provided before or at the time of marriage

### Online Application (When Available)

1. Visit [edistrict.py.gov.in](https://edistrict.py.gov.in/)
2. Select Marriage Assistance under Social Welfare
3. Fill online application with all details
4. Upload scanned documents
5. Submit and track using reference number

## Processing Time

- **Application to approval:** 15–30 days
- **Disbursement:** Before or at the time of marriage (if applied well in advance)

## Important Tips

1. **Apply well in advance:** Submit the application at least 1 month before the wedding date
2. **Marriage must be registered:** Ensure the marriage is legally registered after the ceremony
3. **One-time benefit:** This is a one-time payment per bride
4. **Keep originals ready:** Original documents may be required during verification
5. **SC/ST brides may get additional benefits:** Check for extra assistance available for SC/ST brides

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: How much money will the bride receive?
The amount varies from ₹25,000 to ₹50,000 plus a gold coin (or equivalent), depending on current government orders.

### Q2: Can the application be submitted after the marriage?
Ideally, apply before the marriage. Post-marriage applications may be accepted within a limited period — check with the Social Welfare office.

### Q3: Is this scheme only for BPL families?
Yes, the scheme is primarily for brides from BPL families. Some variants may cover SC/ST families regardless of BPL status.

### Q4: Can inter-state marriages avail this benefit?
The bride must be from Puducherry. Inter-state marriages are generally eligible if the bride meets all other criteria.

### Q5: Is a gold coin given or its cash value?
This varies based on government policy. Some beneficiaries receive a gold coin, others receive the cash equivalent.

### Q6: Can the assistance be used for any purpose?
While intended for marriage expenses, the cash is given to the bride and can be used as needed.

### Q7: What if the bride's father has died?
The bride can apply through her mother or guardian. Submit the father's death certificate along with the application.
