# SBI Clerk 2026 (Junior Associate) — Complete Guide

The State Bank of India Clerk examination, officially called **Junior Associate (Customer Support & Sales)**, is India's largest banking clerical recruitment. With 8,000–11,000 vacancies per year, it is the most accessible entry into SBI for candidates who have completed graduation (or are in final year).

SBI Clerk is a stable, respected government bank job with good pay, excellent perks, and a clear promotion path to Scale I Officer.

## Eligibility Criteria

| Criterion | Requirement |
|-----------|------------|
| **Education** | Graduate in any discipline from a recognised university |
| **Final Year Students** | Eligible to apply — must produce degree before joining |
| **Age** | 20–28 years |
| **Age Relaxation** | SC/ST: +5 yrs | OBC (Non-Creamy Layer): +3 yrs | PwBD: +10 yrs |

> **Note on Education:** Unlike SSC CHSL, SBI Clerk requires a **graduation degree**, not just 12th pass. Final year students may apply but must complete graduation before joining.

## Detailed Syllabus

### English Language
**Prelims:** Reading Comprehension (1 passage), Error Spotting, Sentence Correction, Fill in the Blanks, Para Jumbles, Cloze Test

**Mains:** Reading Comprehension (2–3 passages), Error Detection, Paragraph Completion, Sentence Rearrangement, Vocabulary (synonyms/antonyms in context), Idioms & Phrases, Double Fillers

### Numerical Ability / Quantitative Aptitude
**Prelims:** Simplification/Approximation, Number Series, Quadratic Equations, Data Interpretation (simple bar/pie), Percentage, Ratio, Profit/Loss, SI/CI, Time-Work, Time-Distance, Averages

**Mains (Advanced):** Caselet DI, Mixed DI, Data Sufficiency, Probability, Permutation-Combination, Mensuration, Number System advanced

### Reasoning Ability
**Prelims:** Seating Arrangement (linear, circular), Puzzles (simple), Syllogism, Inequality, Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations, Direction Sense, Alphanumeric Series

**Mains (Advanced):** Complex Puzzles (3+ variables), Input-Output, Logical Reasoning, Critical Reasoning, Data Sufficiency, Order/Ranking

### General/Financial Awareness (Mains only)
- Current Affairs (last 6 months) — banking, economy, government, sports
- Banking Fundamentals: RBI, CRR, SLR, Repo Rate, NPA, PMJDY, PM schemes
- Financial Inclusion: Jan Dhan, PMMY, PMSBY, PMJJBY
- Government Schemes: PM Awas, PM Kisan, Make in India
- Static GK: Bank headquarters, MD/CEOs, financial institutions
- Budget 2026 highlights (Union Budget)
- Stock markets, SEBI, IRDAI, PFRDA

### Computer Aptitude (Mains)
MS Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint), Internet & Email basics, Input/Output Devices, Operating Systems, Computer Networks, Security basics, Number Systems (binary/decimal basics), Keyboard Shortcuts

## Preparation Strategy

### 3-Month Plan

| Month | Daily Schedule |
|-------|---------------|
| **Month 1** | Prelims foundation: Quant (2 hr) + Reasoning puzzles (1.5 hr) + English grammar (1 hr) |
| **Month 2** | Prelims mocks: 1 full mock daily + analysis. Start banking GK (30 min/day) |
| **Month 3** | Mains mocks 3×/week + DI caselets + financial GK revision + weak area grinding |

### Key Tips

1. **SBI Clerk Prelims is speed-based** — 100 questions in 60 minutes = 36 seconds per question. Prioritise speed over thoroughness
2. **Reasoning is the differentiator** — most candidates score similarly in Quant and English. High reasoning score = rank boost
3. **Financial GK = easy marks in Mains** — 50 marks section, many questions are static/predictable. Daily 30 min on this
4. **Computer section** — very easy, don't ignore it. It's free marks in Mains
5. **State language test** — prepare if you're not fluent in the state you're applying for

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can a 12th pass student apply for SBI Clerk?**
No — SBI Clerk requires a graduation degree. However, final year students can apply provisionally. For 12th pass candidates, look at SSC CHSL or India Post GDS.

**Is there an interview for SBI Clerk 2026?**
No — SBI abolished interviews for Clerk posts. Final selection is based purely on Mains score + language test (qualifying).

**Can I apply for both SBI Clerk and SBI PO in the same year?**
Yes — both are separate exams with different notification timelines. Applying for both is a smart strategy.

**How many vacancies are released in SBI Clerk?**
Usually 8,000–11,000. The 2023–24 notification had ~8,700 vacancies. 2026 numbers will be in the official notification.

**What is the maximum age for SBI Clerk?**
28 years for general category. With relaxation: OBC 31 years, SC/ST 33 years.

**Does SBI have a rural posting quota for Clerk?**
Yes — some vacancies are reserved for rural/semi-urban postings. Your preference for the state determines likely posting area, but SBI can post you anywhere in the circle.
