# PM Awas Yojana Apply Online: Eligibility, Subsidy & Process

**Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY)** is the Government of India's flagship housing scheme that provides financial assistance to economically weaker sections for building or buying their first pucca house. This guide covers how to apply online, eligibility criteria, subsidy amounts, and the complete application process for both **PMAY Urban** and **PMAY Gramin**.

## What is PM Awas Yojana?

PMAY was launched in 2015 with the vision of **"Housing for All."** It has two components:

- **PMAY Urban (PMAY-U):** For urban areas — provides interest subsidy on home loans through Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme (CLSS)
- **PMAY Gramin (PMAY-G):** For rural areas — provides direct financial assistance for house construction

## PMAY Urban: Eligibility Criteria

| Category | Annual Household Income | Loan Subsidy | Max Loan for Subsidy | House Size |
|----------|----------------------|--------------|---------------------|------------|
| **EWS** | Up to ₹3 lakh | 6.5% | ₹6 lakh | Up to 30 sq.m |
| **LIG** | ₹3–6 lakh | 6.5% | ₹6 lakh | Up to 60 sq.m |
| **MIG-I** | ₹6–12 lakh | 4% | ₹9 lakh | Up to 160 sq.m |
| **MIG-II** | ₹12–18 lakh | 3% | ₹12 lakh | Up to 200 sq.m |

### Key Eligibility Conditions
- Beneficiary family must **not own a pucca house** anywhere in India
- The house must be in the **woman member's name** (or joint ownership)
- Family should **not have availed** any central housing scheme benefit earlier
- Aadhaar number is **mandatory**

## PMAY Gramin: Eligibility Criteria

- Beneficiaries are selected from **SECC-2011 (Socio-Economic Caste Census)** data
- Verified and approved by **Gram Sabha**
- Priority to: houseless families, families living in kutcha/dilapidated houses, SC/ST, minorities, freed bonded labourers

### PMAY Gramin Assistance Amount

| Area | Assistance Amount |
|------|------------------|
| **Plains** | ₹1,20,000 |
| **Hilly/Difficult/IAP areas** | ₹1,30,000 |
| **MGNREGA labour** | 90-95 person-days additional |
| **Toilet (SBM)** | ₹12,000 additional |

## How to Apply for PMAY Urban Online

### Step 1: Visit the Official Portal
1. Go to [pmaymis.gov.in](https://pmaymis.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Citizen Assessment"** menu
3. Select **"For Slum Dwellers"** or **"Benefits Under Other 3 Components"**

### Step 2: Enter Aadhaar Details
1. Enter your **12-digit Aadhaar number**
2. Enter your **name as per Aadhaar**
3. Click **"Check"** — the system verifies your Aadhaar

### Step 3: Fill the Application Form
Fill in all required details:
- **Personal Details:** Name, father's name, date of birth, gender, religion, caste category
- **Contact Details:** Mobile number, email, current address
- **Income Details:** Annual household income, income category (EWS/LIG/MIG-I/MIG-II)
- **Bank Details:** Bank name, branch, account number, IFSC code
- **Property Details:** State, city, ward, type of house required
- **Family Details:** Number of family members, Aadhaar details of family head

### Step 4: Upload Documents
Upload scanned copies of:
- Aadhaar card
- Income certificate or salary slip
- Bank account passbook first page
- Affidavit (no pucca house declaration)
- Caste certificate (if applicable)

### Step 5: Submit and Save
1. Review all details carefully
2. Click **"Submit"**
3. Note down your **Application ID** — this is essential for tracking
4. Take a **printout** of the application form

## How to Apply for PMAY Gramin

PMAY Gramin beneficiaries are **not selected through online applications**. The process is:

1. **SECC-2011 data** identifies eligible households
2. **Gram Sabha** verifies and approves the list
3. Approved list is uploaded to [pmayg.nic.in](https://pmayg.nic.in)
4. Beneficiaries are notified by **Block/Panchayat office**
5. Contact your **Gram Pradhan** or **Block Development Officer** to check if you're on the list

### Check PMAY Gramin List
1. Visit [pmayg.nic.in](https://pmayg.nic.in)
2. Click on **"Stakeholders"** → **"IAY/PMAYG Beneficiary"**
3. Search by **registration number** or **name**
4. Or visit [rhreporting.nic.in](https://rhreporting.nic.in) for state-wise beneficiary lists

## Documents Required for PMAY

| Document | PMAY Urban | PMAY Gramin |
|----------|-----------|-------------|
| **Aadhaar Card** | Mandatory | Mandatory |
| **Income Proof** | Required | Not needed (SECC data) |
| **Bank Passbook** | Required | Required |
| **Caste Certificate** | If SC/ST/OBC | If applicable |
| **No Pucca House Affidavit** | Required | Not needed |
| **Land/Property Papers** | If available | Required (for construction) |
| **Passport Photos** | 2 copies | 2 copies |
| **PAN Card** | For MIG categories | Not needed |

## PMAY Subsidy Calculator

To estimate your subsidy:

### EWS/LIG Example
- Home loan: ₹6,00,000
- Interest subsidy: 6.5% for 20 years
- **Net Present Value of subsidy: ~₹2,67,000**
- Effective EMI reduction: ~₹1,100/month

### MIG-I Example
- Home loan: ₹9,00,000 (subsidy applicable on this amount)
- Interest subsidy: 4% for 20 years
- **Net Present Value of subsidy: ~₹2,35,000**

### MIG-II Example
- Home loan: ₹12,00,000 (subsidy applicable on this amount)
- Interest subsidy: 3% for 20 years
- **Net Present Value of subsidy: ~₹2,30,000**

**Note:** The subsidy is credited to your loan account upfront, reducing your outstanding principal and EMI.

## How to Track PMAY Application Status

### PMAY Urban Status
1. Visit [pmaymis.gov.in](https://pmaymis.gov.in)
2. Click **"Track Your Assessment Status"**
3. Search by **Aadhaar Number** or **Assessment ID**
4. View your current application status

### PMAY Gramin Status
1. Visit [pmayg.nic.in](https://pmayg.nic.in)
2. Go to **"Stakeholders"** → **"IAY/PMAYG Beneficiary"**
3. Enter your **registration number**
4. Check installment release status

## Participating Banks for PMAY Subsidy

The interest subsidy under CLSS is available through:
- **All Scheduled Commercial Banks** (SBI, PNB, BOB, etc.)
- **Housing Finance Companies** (HDFC, LIC Housing, etc.)
- **Regional Rural Banks**
- **Cooperative Banks**
- **National Housing Bank** (central nodal agency)

Apply for a home loan at any of these banks and mention PMAY-CLSS to avail the subsidy.

## Important Tips

- **Apply early** — scheme has limited time period and funds
- **Ensure Aadhaar is updated** with correct name and address before applying
- **Women ownership** is mandatory — property must be in woman's name or joint
- **Don't pay agents** — the application is free on pmaymis.gov.in
- **Link bank account to Aadhaar** for direct subsidy transfer
- Check the **official PMAY eligibility calculator** on pmaymis.gov.in

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Who is eligible for PM Awas Yojana?
Families with no pucca house anywhere in India, belonging to EWS (income up to ₹3 lakh), LIG (₹3-6 lakh), MIG-I (₹6-12 lakh) or MIG-II (₹12-18 lakh) categories are eligible for PMAY Urban. For PMAY Gramin, BPL families identified through SECC data are eligible.

### How much subsidy do I get under PMAY?
EWS/LIG get ₹2.67 lakh interest subsidy (6.5% on ₹6 lakh loan for 20 years). MIG-I gets ₹2.35 lakh (4% on ₹9 lakh). MIG-II gets ₹2.30 lakh (3% on ₹12 lakh). PMAY Gramin provides ₹1.20 lakh (plains) or ₹1.30 lakh (hilly areas) as direct assistance.

### Can I apply for PMAY online myself?
Yes, for PMAY Urban you can apply at pmaymis.gov.in using your Aadhaar number. For PMAY Gramin, beneficiaries are selected from SECC-2011 data by Gram Sabha and you cannot apply directly online.

### What documents are required for PMAY application?
Aadhaar card (mandatory), income proof, bank account details, property/land documents, caste certificate (SC/ST/OBC), affidavit declaring no pucca house, and passport-size photographs.

### How do I check my PMAY application status?
Visit pmaymis.gov.in, click "Track Your Assessment Status", and search using your Aadhaar number or application ID to check your current status.

### Can I get PMAY benefit if I already own a plot?
Yes, if you own a plot but don't have a pucca house, you can apply under the "Enhancement" component of PMAY Urban for construction assistance on your existing plot.
