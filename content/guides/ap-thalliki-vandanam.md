# Thalliki Vandanam (Amma Vodi Restructured) – Andhra Pradesh Complete Guide

Thalliki Vandanam is the restructured version of the Jagananna Amma Vodi scheme in Andhra Pradesh. Under this scheme, the government provides ₹15,000 per year to mothers (or guardians) who send their children to school, encouraging school enrollment and reducing dropout rates. The benefit is transferred directly to the mother's bank account.

## What is Thalliki Vandanam?

Originally launched as Jagananna Amma Vodi, the scheme has been continued and restructured as Thalliki Vandanam by the AP government. It recognizes mothers as key stakeholders in children's education and incentivizes them financially to ensure children attend school regularly.

**Key Benefit:** ₹15,000 per year per family (for mothers/guardians sending children to school from Class 1 to Class 12 and Intermediate)

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Mother/Guardian:** The mother (or authorized guardian) of a child studying in a government, aided, or recognized private school in AP
2. **Classes covered:** Children studying in Class 1 to Class 12 / Intermediate
3. **Income limit:** Annual family income should not exceed ₹10,00,000 (varies as per government orders)
4. **Residency:** Must be a resident of Andhra Pradesh
5. **Exclusions:**
   - Government employees and their spouses
   - Income tax payers
   - Families owning more than 3 acres of wet land or 10 acres of dry land
   - Families owning four-wheelers (except auto-rickshaws and taxis)

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar card of mother/guardian (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- Aadhaar card of the child
- Bank passbook of mother (Aadhaar-linked)
- Ration card / White ration card
- Child's school admission/bonafide certificate
- Income certificate (if required)
- Passport-size photograph of mother

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Through School (Primary Method)

1. **School enrollment:** Ensure your child is enrolled and attending a recognized school in AP
2. **Data collection at school:** Schools collect mother/guardian details, Aadhaar numbers, and bank account information
3. **Verification:** School headmaster verifies the data and uploads to the government portal
4. **Village/Ward Secretariat verification:** The Ward/Village Secretary cross-verifies the family's eligibility
5. **Approval:** District education authorities approve eligible beneficiaries
6. **DBT credit:** ₹15,000 is transferred directly to the mother's bank account (usually in January)

### If Not Automatically Included

1. **Visit your child's school** and enquire with the headmaster
2. **Visit Village/Ward Secretariat** and submit your details
3. **Provide documents** — Aadhaar, bank passbook, ration card, school certificate
4. **Request inclusion** in the beneficiary list
5. **Track status** on the official Amma Vodi portal or through the school

## Disbursement Details

| Detail | Information |
|--------|------------|
| Amount | ₹15,000 per year per family |
| Mode | Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to mother's bank account |
| Frequency | Annual (usually disbursed in January) |
| Coverage | Class 1 to Intermediate |

## Processing Time

- **Data collection:** August–November each academic year
- **Verification:** November–December
- **Disbursement:** January (on or around January 26)

## Important Tips

1. **Aadhaar-bank link is essential:** Ensure the mother's Aadhaar is linked to her bank account for seamless DBT
2. **One payment per family:** The ₹15,000 is per family, not per child — even if multiple children attend school
3. **Regular attendance matters:** Children must attend school regularly; irregular attendance may affect eligibility
4. **No application fee:** The scheme is completely free — do not pay any middleman
5. **Check school records:** Ensure the school has correctly entered your details in the portal

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Is the ₹15,000 given per child or per family?
The ₹15,000 is given per family per year, regardless of how many children are studying.

### Q2: Can fathers apply for this scheme?
The scheme prioritizes mothers. Fathers or male guardians can apply only if the mother is deceased or absent, with appropriate documentation.

### Q3: Are private school students eligible?
Yes, children studying in recognized private schools are also eligible, provided the family meets income and asset criteria.

### Q4: When is the money credited?
The amount is typically credited in January each year, often around Republic Day.

### Q5: What if my child changes schools mid-year?
Inform both the old and new school. The new school should update your details in the portal.

### Q6: Will government employees' families get this benefit?
No, families of government employees, income tax payers, and those exceeding the asset limits are excluded.

### Q7: How can I check if I'm in the beneficiary list?
Check with your child's school headmaster or visit the official Amma Vodi portal to search your details.
