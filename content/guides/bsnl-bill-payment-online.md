# BSNL Bill Payment Online: Landline & Broadband

Need to pay your BSNL landline or broadband bill? You can pay online through the official BSNL portal, UPI apps, or set up auto-pay for hassle-free payments. This guide covers all payment methods, bill download, and what to do if something goes wrong.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent platform and is not affiliated with BSNL or the Government of India. Information is for guidance purposes only.

## How to Pay BSNL Bill Online — Step by Step

### Method 1: Via portal.bsnl.in (Official Portal)

1. Visit [portal.bsnl.in](https://portal.bsnl.in/)
2. Click **Quick Bill Payment** (no login required)
3. Select your **service type**: Landline / Broadband / Mobile
4. Enter your **telephone/account number**
5. Select your **circle/state**
6. Click **View Bill** — your outstanding amount will appear
7. Click **Pay Now**
8. Choose payment method: **Credit Card / Debit Card / Net Banking / UPI**
9. Complete the payment
10. Save the **payment receipt** (screenshot or PDF)

### Method 2: Via BSNL Selfcare Portal

1. Visit [selfcare.bsnl.co.in](https://selfcare.bsnl.co.in/)
2. **Register/Login** with your BSNL number
3. Go to **Bill Payment** section
4. View current bill and past bills
5. Click **Pay** and complete via your preferred method
6. You can also view usage details and plan information here

### Method 3: Via UPI Apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm)

1. Open **Google Pay / PhonePe / Paytm**
2. Go to **Bill Payments** → **Landline** or **Broadband**
3. Select **BSNL** as the provider
4. Enter your **telephone/account number** and **circle**
5. Fetch bill → Confirm amount → Pay via UPI
6. Payment reflects within a few minutes to 24 hours

### Method 4: Via Bank Apps and Net Banking

Most bank apps (SBI YONO, HDFC, ICICI, etc.) support BSNL bill payment:

1. Open your **bank app** → **Bill Payments / Recharge**
2. Search for **BSNL**
3. Select service type (Landline/Broadband)
4. Enter account number → Fetch and pay bill

### Method 5: Offline Payment

| Method | Where |
|--------|-------|
| BSNL Office | Visit the nearest BSNL customer service centre with your bill |
| CSC Centre | Common Service Centres accept BSNL payments |
| Authorized collection centres | Some shops/agents collect BSNL payments |

## How to Download BSNL Bill

1. Visit [selfcare.bsnl.co.in](https://selfcare.bsnl.co.in/) → Login
2. Go to **View Bill** or **Bill History**
3. Select the month/billing period
4. Click **Download PDF**
5. You can also view itemized call details here

**Alternative:** Visit [portal.bsnl.in](https://portal.bsnl.in/) → Quick Bill Payment → Enter your number → View and download bill.

## How to Set Up BSNL Auto-Pay

Avoid late payments by setting up automatic bill payment:

### Via BSNL Portal
1. Login to [selfcare.bsnl.co.in](https://selfcare.bsnl.co.in/)
2. Go to **Auto-Pay / Standing Instruction**
3. Add your bank account or card details
4. Set auto-debit for bill payment
5. Bills will be automatically paid on the due date

### Via UPI Apps
1. In Google Pay / PhonePe → Bill Payments → BSNL
2. After paying, you'll see an option to **Set Up Autopay**
3. Enable it → Approve the UPI mandate
4. Your bill will be auto-paid each month

## BSNL Bill Payment Due Date and Overdue Charges

| Detail | Information |
|--------|-------------|
| Bill generation | Monthly (around 1st week) |
| Payment due date | Printed on your bill (usually 15-20 days from generation) |
| Grace period | 15 days after due date |
| Late payment surcharge | 2% per month on outstanding amount |
| Disconnection | After 30 days overdue (service suspended) |
| Permanent disconnection | After 90 days (number may be released) |

> **Important:** Pay before the due date to avoid surcharges. If your service is suspended, pay the full outstanding amount including surcharge at a BSNL office or online to restore service.

## Troubleshooting Payment Issues

### Payment Done but Bill Still Shows Unpaid
- Wait 24-48 hours for online payments to reflect
- Check your bank statement to confirm deduction
- If still not updated, call **198** with your payment receipt/transaction ID

### Payment Failed, Money Deducted
- Wait 48 hours for automatic reversal
- Contact your bank with the transaction reference
- Also report to BSNL at **198** with transaction details
- Refund typically takes 5-7 working days

### Cannot View Bill Online
- Ensure you're entering the correct telephone number (with STD code if required)
- Select the correct circle/state
- Try both portal.bsnl.in and selfcare.bsnl.co.in
- If bill not available online, visit the BSNL office

## How to File a BSNL Billing Complaint

1. **Call 198** → Select "Billing Issue" → Describe the problem
2. **Selfcare portal** → Complaints → Billing complaint
3. **Email** your circle's designated email (find on bsnl.co.in)
4. **PGPORTAL** → [pgportal.gov.in](https://pgportal.gov.in/) for unresolved complaints
5. **Consumer Forum** → For persistent billing disputes, approach the consumer forum

## Important Tips

1. **Save payment receipts** — Always screenshot or save PDF of payment confirmation. You may need proof if the bill shows unpaid.
2. **Pay 2-3 days before due date** — Online payments can take 1-2 days to reflect in BSNL's system. Don't wait until the last day.
3. **Check for bill errors** — Review itemized bills for incorrect charges. Report discrepancies immediately at 198.
4. **Use auto-pay** — Set up UPI autopay or standing instruction to never miss a payment.
5. **Keep your account number handy** — It's on the top of your BSNL bill. Save it in your phone for quick online payments.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How can I pay my BSNL landline bill online?
Visit portal.bsnl.in → Quick Bill Payment → Enter your landline number with STD code → View bill → Pay via card, net banking, or UPI. You can also use Google Pay or PhonePe.

### Where can I download my BSNL bill?
Login to selfcare.bsnl.co.in → View Bill → Download PDF. You can access current and past months' bills. The portal.bsnl.in also lets you view bills without login.

### What happens if I don't pay my BSNL bill on time?
A 2% monthly surcharge is added to overdue amounts. After 30 days, your service is suspended (outgoing calls blocked). After 90 days, permanent disconnection may happen and your number could be released.

### How do I set up auto-pay for BSNL bills?
Use UPI apps (Google Pay, PhonePe) — after paying your BSNL bill, enable the autopay option. You can also set up standing instructions through the BSNL selfcare portal.

### My BSNL bill payment is not reflecting. What should I do?
Wait 24-48 hours for the payment to reflect in BSNL's system. If still not updated, call 198 with your transaction ID and payment receipt. Keep your bank statement ready as proof.

### Can I pay BSNL broadband and landline bills together?
If both services are on the same account number, they appear on one bill. If separate, you'll need to pay each bill individually using the respective account numbers.

### How do I check my BSNL bill amount without logging in?
Visit portal.bsnl.in → Quick Bill Payment → Enter your telephone number and circle → Click View Bill. No login or registration required.
