## What is BSNL Bill Payment?

BSNL (Bharat Sanchar Nigam Limited) is India's government-owned telecommunications company providing landline, broadband, mobile, and FTTH services. BSNL customers can pay their monthly bills online through the official selfcare portal, UPI apps, net banking, or authorized payment platforms.

Whether you have a BSNL landline connection, broadband (DSL/Bharat Fiber), or postpaid mobile — you can view, download, and pay your bills from home without visiting a BSNL office.

## How to Pay BSNL Bill Online — Step by Step

### Method 1: BSNL Selfcare Portal

1. Visit the BSNL Selfcare Portal at [selfcare.bsnl.co.in](https://selfcare.bsnl.co.in)
2. Click on **"Quick Bill Payment"** (no login required for payment)
3. Select your **service type** — Landline, Broadband, or Mobile
4. Enter your **telephone number** or **account number**
5. The portal will show your **outstanding bill amount**
6. Choose payment method — **Credit Card, Debit Card, Net Banking, or UPI**
7. Complete the payment through the payment gateway
8. Save or print the **payment receipt** for your records

### Method 2: BSNL Portal (portal.bsnl.in)

1. Go to [portal.bsnl.in](https://portal.bsnl.in)
2. Register or log in with your BSNL number
3. Navigate to **"My Bills"** section
4. Click on **"Pay Bill"**
5. Select payment method and complete transaction

### Method 3: UPI Apps (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm)

1. Open your UPI app (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, etc.)
2. Go to **"Bill Payments"** or **"Recharge & Bills"**
3. Select **"Landline/Broadband"** → **BSNL**
4. Enter your BSNL telephone number or customer ID
5. The bill amount will be fetched automatically
6. Pay using UPI balance, linked bank account, or wallet

### Method 4: Net Banking

1. Log in to your bank's internet banking portal
2. Go to **"Bill Payment"** section
3. Search for **"BSNL"** as biller
4. Add your BSNL account number
5. Confirm the amount and pay

### Method 5: Bharat BillPay (BBPS)

1. Visit any BBPS-enabled platform or bank
2. Select **BSNL** under telecom billers
3. Enter your account details
4. Pay and receive instant confirmation

## How to View and Download BSNL Bill

1. Visit [selfcare.bsnl.co.in](https://selfcare.bsnl.co.in)
2. Click **"View/Print Bill"**
3. Enter your telephone number
4. Select the billing month
5. Click **"View Bill"** — the bill will display as a PDF
6. Click **Download** or **Print** to save a copy

## How to Get a Duplicate BSNL Bill

If you've lost your physical BSNL bill:

1. **Online:** Download from the selfcare portal (see steps above) — bills for the last 6 months are available
2. **BSNL Office:** Visit your nearest BSNL customer service center with ID proof and request a duplicate bill
3. **Call 1500** (BSNL helpline) and request a duplicate bill by post or email

## How to Set Up BSNL Auto-Pay

To avoid missing due dates, set up automatic bill payment:

### Via BSNL Portal:
1. Log in to [portal.bsnl.in](https://portal.bsnl.in)
2. Go to **"Payment Settings"**
3. Select **"Auto Pay / Standing Instruction"**
4. Link your bank account or card
5. Set the auto-debit limit and confirm

### Via Bank/UPI App:
1. In your UPI app or net banking, go to **"Bill Payments"**
2. Add BSNL as a biller
3. Enable **"Autopay"** or **"Standing Instruction"**
4. Set maximum debit limit
5. Bills will be paid automatically each month

## BSNL Bill Due Dates and Late Fees

- BSNL bills are generated monthly (billing cycle varies by circle)
- **Due date** is typically **15-20 days** from the bill date
- **Late payment surcharge:** ₹25-50 or as per circle norms
- If unpaid for 2+ months, your connection may be **temporarily disconnected**
- You can get reconnection by clearing all dues (reconnection charges may apply)

## Important Tips

1. **Save your Customer ID** — it's printed on every BSNL bill and needed for online payments
2. **Check bill details carefully** — verify call charges and data usage before paying
3. **Keep payment receipts** — download and save transaction confirmations
4. **Use autopay** to avoid late payment surcharges
5. **Dial 1500** (toll-free from BSNL) for any billing disputes or queries

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: How do I find my BSNL account number?
Your BSNL account number (also called Customer ID or telephone number) is printed on your monthly bill. You can also call 1500 from your BSNL number to get your account details.

### Q2: Can I pay BSNL bill without registration?
Yes. The BSNL Selfcare Portal offers a **Quick Bill Payment** option where you can pay by entering just your telephone number — no registration needed.

### Q3: What if my BSNL bill payment fails but money is deducted?
If payment fails but the amount is debited, it is typically refunded within **3-7 working days**. If not, contact BSNL helpline at 1500 or raise a complaint on [portal.bsnl.in](https://portal.bsnl.in).

### Q4: Can I pay someone else's BSNL bill?
Yes. You can pay any BSNL bill by entering the telephone number. There's no restriction on who makes the payment.

### Q5: How do I check my BSNL broadband data usage?
Log in to [selfcare.bsnl.co.in](https://selfcare.bsnl.co.in) and go to **"Usage Details"** to check your broadband data consumption for the current and previous months.

### Q6: Is there a BSNL mobile app for bill payment?
Yes. BSNL offers the **"My BSNL" app** (available on Google Play Store and Apple App Store) where you can view bills, make payments, and manage your account.

### Q7: What is the BSNL helpline number for billing queries?
Call **1500** (toll-free from BSNL landline/mobile) or **1800-345-1500** (toll-free from any phone) for billing assistance.
