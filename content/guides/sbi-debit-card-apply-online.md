## What is SBI Debit Card?

An SBI Debit Card (ATM Card) is issued to State Bank of India savings and current account holders for cash withdrawals at ATMs, online shopping, and POS transactions. SBI offers multiple card variants with different features, daily limits, and fees.

## Types of SBI Debit Cards

| Card Type | Annual Fee | Daily ATM Limit | Daily POS Limit | Insurance Cover |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SBI Classic Debit Card | ₹125 + GST | ₹20,000 | ₹50,000 | ₹2 lakh |
| SBI Silver Debit Card | ₹125 + GST | ₹40,000 | ₹75,000 | ₹2 lakh |
| SBI Gold Debit Card | ₹175 + GST | ₹1,00,000 | ₹2,00,000 | ₹4 lakh |
| SBI Platinum Debit Card | ₹250 + GST | ₹1,00,000 | ₹2,00,000 | ₹5 lakh |
| SBI Global Debit Card (Visa) | ₹350 + GST | ₹40,000 | ₹2,00,000 | ₹5 lakh (international) |

> **Note:** Fees and limits are subject to change. Check [sbi.co.in](https://sbi.co.in/web/personal-banking/cards/debit-card) for the latest details.

## Who is Eligible?

- SBI savings account or current account holders
- Account must be active and KYC-compliant
- Registered mobile number linked to the account
- Minimum age: 18 years (minors can get cards through guardian accounts)

## How to Apply for SBI Debit Card Online

### Method 1: Via YONO SBI App

1. Download and log into the **YONO SBI** app
2. Go to **Cards** section from the dashboard
3. Tap **Apply for Debit Card**
4. Select your account number
5. Choose the card type (Classic / Gold / Platinum / Global)
6. Verify your delivery address (branch address or registered address)
7. Confirm the application
8. The card will be dispatched to your registered address or branch within 7-10 working days

### Method 2: Via SBI Net Banking (OnlineSBI)

1. Visit [www.onlinesbi.sbi](https://www.onlinesbi.sbi/) and log in
2. Go to **e-Services** → **ATM Card Services**
3. Click **Request ATM / Debit Card**
4. Select the account and card variant
5. Choose delivery option: **Branch pickup** or **Registered address**
6. Submit the request
7. Note down the reference number for tracking

### Method 3: Visit SBI Branch (Offline)

1. Visit your SBI home branch with a filled debit card application form
2. Carry a valid photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport)
3. Submit the form at the counter
4. Card will be issued within 7-15 working days

## How to Activate SBI Debit Card

Once you receive the card, it must be activated before use:

### ATM Activation (Recommended)

1. Visit any SBI ATM
2. Insert the new debit card
3. Enter the 4-digit PIN sent via SMS (or the PIN in the sealed envelope)
4. Select **PIN Change** → Enter old PIN → Set new PIN
5. Perform a balance inquiry to confirm activation

### Green PIN via SMS

1. Send SMS: **PIN <last 4 digits of card> <last 4 digits of account>** to **567676**
2. You will receive a Green PIN via SMS
3. Use this PIN at any SBI ATM and change it immediately

### YONO SBI Activation

1. Open YONO SBI → **Cards** → **Activate Debit Card**
2. Enter card details and OTP
3. Set your ATM PIN digitally

## Documents Required

- **Passport-size photograph** (for branch application)
- **Photo ID proof:** Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, or Passport
- **Address proof:** Aadhaar, utility bill, or bank passbook
- **Account details:** Account number and CIF number

## Fees and Charges

| Item | Charges |
|---|---|
| Card issuance (new) | Free with new account; otherwise ₹100-350 depending on type |
| Annual maintenance | ₹125 to ₹350 + GST (varies by card type) |
| ATM cash withdrawal (SBI ATMs) | Free (up to 8-10 transactions/month in metro; unlimited in non-metro) |
| ATM withdrawal (other bank ATMs) | First 3-5 free/month, then ₹20 + GST per transaction |
| Card replacement (lost/damaged) | ₹200 + GST |
| International ATM withdrawal | ₹200 + GST per transaction (for Global/International cards) |

## Processing Time

- **Online request (YONO/Net Banking):** 7-10 working days for dispatch
- **Branch request:** 7-15 working days
- **Card delivery:** Via Speed Post to registered address

## Important Tips

1. **Always change the default PIN** at an SBI ATM immediately after receiving the card
2. **Enable/disable international usage** via YONO SBI or net banking for security
3. **Set transaction limits** in YONO to prevent unauthorized large transactions
4. **Report lost cards immediately** by calling SBI helpline 1800-11-2211 or blocking via YONO
5. **Link your new debit card to UPI** — you'll need it for UPI PIN reset. See our [SBI UPI guide](/guide/sbi-upi-not-working-fix)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. How long does it take to get an SBI debit card?

SBI debit cards are typically delivered within 7-10 working days for online requests and up to 15 working days for branch applications.

### Q2. Can I apply for SBI debit card without visiting the branch?

Yes. You can apply online via YONO SBI app or SBI Net Banking (OnlineSBI). The card will be delivered to your registered address.

### Q3. What is the difference between SBI Classic and Gold debit card?

SBI Gold has higher ATM withdrawal limits (₹1 lakh/day vs ₹20,000), higher POS limits, and better insurance coverage, but costs more in annual fees (₹175 vs ₹125).

### Q4. Can I choose which type of debit card I want?

Yes. When applying through YONO or net banking, you can select your preferred card variant. At branch, mention the desired card type on the application form.

### Q5. How do I block my SBI debit card if lost?

Call **1800-11-2211** (toll-free) or **1800-425-3800**, block via YONO SBI app under Cards section, or send SMS **BLOCK <last 4 digits>** to **567676**.

### Q6. Is SBI debit card free with a new savings account?

Yes. A basic debit card (usually Classic/Silver) is provided free with new SBI savings account opening.

### Q7. Can I upgrade my SBI debit card to Platinum?

Yes. Apply for an upgrade via YONO SBI or net banking. The old card will be deactivated and a new Platinum card will be issued.
