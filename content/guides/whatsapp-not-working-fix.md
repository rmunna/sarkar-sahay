## WhatsApp Not Working? Here's How to Fix It

WhatsApp is India's most-used messaging app with over 50 crore users. When it stops working — messages not sending, calls failing, or media not downloading — it can be frustrating. This guide covers every common WhatsApp problem and how to fix it quickly.

## Common Reasons WhatsApp Stops Working

- **No internet connection** or weak network
- **WhatsApp server outage** (affects everyone)
- **Outdated app version**
- **Phone storage full**
- **Corrupted cache data**
- **Date and time incorrect** on your phone
- **VPN or firewall blocking** WhatsApp
- **Phone OS too old** (WhatsApp drops support for older Android/iOS versions)

## Step-by-Step Fixes

### Fix 1: Check Your Internet Connection

1. Open your phone's browser and try loading any website (e.g., google.com)
2. If it doesn't load, your internet is the problem — not WhatsApp
3. **Mobile data:** Toggle airplane mode on for 10 seconds, then off
4. **Wi-Fi:** Restart your router or switch to mobile data
5. Try switching between Wi-Fi and mobile data to isolate the issue

### Fix 2: Check if WhatsApp Server Is Down

1. Visit [downdetector.in](https://downdetector.in/status/whatsapp/) on your browser
2. If many users are reporting issues, it's a **server outage**
3. In this case, **wait** — WhatsApp will fix it from their end (usually within 30 minutes to a few hours)
4. You can also check WhatsApp's official Twitter/X account [@WhatsApp](https://twitter.com/WhatsApp) for updates

### Fix 3: Update WhatsApp

1. Open **Google Play Store** (Android) or **App Store** (iPhone)
2. Search for "WhatsApp"
3. If you see an **Update** button, tap it
4. Restart WhatsApp after updating

> **Tip:** Enable auto-updates so you always have the latest version. Old versions can cause connection issues.

### Fix 4: Clear WhatsApp Cache (Android)

1. Go to **Settings → Apps → WhatsApp**
2. Tap **Storage & Cache**
3. Tap **Clear Cache** (NOT "Clear Data" — that will delete your chats)
4. Restart WhatsApp

> **iPhone users:** iOS doesn't allow clearing cache directly. You'll need to delete and reinstall WhatsApp (backup your chats first via Settings → Chats → Chat Backup).

### Fix 5: Fix Date and Time Settings

WhatsApp requires correct date and time to connect to its servers.

1. Go to **Settings → Date & Time**
2. Enable **Automatic date & time** (or "Set automatically")
3. Restart WhatsApp

### Fix 6: Messages Not Sending (Single Tick Issue)

If your messages show only **one grey tick** (not delivered):

1. Check your internet connection (Fix 1)
2. The **recipient** may have no internet — wait and try later
3. Check if you've been **blocked** by the contact (you won't see their "last seen" or profile photo)
4. Try sending a message to a different contact to isolate the issue

**Tick meanings:**
- ✓ (single grey) — Sent from your phone
- ✓✓ (double grey) — Delivered to recipient's phone
- ✓✓ (double blue) — Read by recipient

### Fix 7: Calls Not Connecting

1. Ensure you have a **stable internet connection** (Wi-Fi recommended for video calls)
2. Grant WhatsApp **microphone and camera permissions**: Settings → Apps → WhatsApp → Permissions
3. Disable any **VPN** or proxy app that may be blocking WhatsApp's calling servers
4. Check if your **carrier blocks VoIP calls** — some networks restrict WhatsApp calls (switch to Wi-Fi)
5. Restart your phone and try again

### Fix 8: Media Not Downloading (Photos/Videos)

1. Check available **phone storage**: Settings → Storage. Free up space if less than 500 MB available
2. Check WhatsApp media settings: **WhatsApp → Settings → Storage and Data**
3. Ensure **media auto-download** is enabled for your connection type
4. If on mobile data, try switching to Wi-Fi (large files may need faster connection)
5. Clear cache (Fix 4) and retry

### Fix 9: Reinstall WhatsApp

If nothing else works:

1. **Backup your chats:** WhatsApp → Settings → Chats → Chat Backup → Back Up
2. Uninstall WhatsApp
3. Download and install WhatsApp from Play Store / App Store
4. Verify your phone number
5. Restore your chat backup when prompted

### Fix 10: Check Phone Compatibility

WhatsApp requires:
- **Android:** Version 5.0 (Lollipop) or newer
- **iPhone:** iOS 16 or newer (as of 2026)
- **KaiOS:** Version 2.5.1 or newer

If your phone OS is too old, WhatsApp may stop working. Check for OS updates in Settings → System → Software Update.

## WhatsApp Web / Desktop Not Working?

1. Ensure your **phone has active internet** (WhatsApp Web needs your phone online)
2. Open WhatsApp on your phone → **Linked Devices** → check if the device is connected
3. Log out from WhatsApp Web and **re-link** by scanning the QR code
4. Clear your browser cache or try a different browser
5. Disable browser extensions that might block WhatsApp Web

## Important Tips

- **Always update WhatsApp** to the latest version — most bugs are fixed in updates
- **Restart your phone** before trying complex fixes — this solves many issues
- **Don't use unofficial WhatsApp** versions (GB WhatsApp, WhatsApp Plus) — they can get your account banned
- **Check storage regularly** — WhatsApp media can fill up your phone quickly
- **Keep your phone OS updated** — WhatsApp drops support for old OS versions periodically

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is WhatsApp not connecting even with internet?
Your date/time settings may be wrong, your app may be outdated, or a VPN may be blocking the connection. Follow fixes 3, 5, and 7 above.

### How do I know if WhatsApp is down for everyone?
Check [downdetector.in/status/whatsapp](https://downdetector.in/status/whatsapp/) — if there's a spike in reports, it's a server issue.

### Will I lose my chats if I reinstall WhatsApp?
Not if you backup first. Go to WhatsApp → Settings → Chats → Chat Backup before uninstalling.

### Why are my WhatsApp calls not working on mobile data?
Some telecom operators in India throttle or block VoIP calls on certain plans. Try switching to Wi-Fi or contact your operator (Jio, Airtel, Vi).

### Can WhatsApp stop working on my old phone?
Yes. WhatsApp periodically drops support for older phone models and OS versions. Check the official [WhatsApp supported devices page](https://faq.whatsapp.com/general/download-and-installation/).

### Why are photos and videos not downloading in WhatsApp?
Most likely your phone storage is full or your internet is slow. Free up space and switch to Wi-Fi for large files.

### My WhatsApp shows "Connecting..." all the time. What do I do?
This means WhatsApp can't reach its servers. Check internet, fix date/time, update the app, and restart your phone.
