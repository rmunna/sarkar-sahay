## What is Meri Fasal Mera Byora?

**Meri Fasal Mera Byora (MFMB)** — meaning "My Crop, My Details" — is the Government of Haryana's **mandatory crop registration portal** at [fasal.haryana.gov.in](https://fasal.haryana.gov.in). Every Haryana farmer must register their crop, land, and sowing details on this portal before selling crops at government mandis or claiming any agricultural scheme benefit.

**Why it matters:** Without MFMB registration, Haryana farmers **cannot** sell wheat, paddy, or other crops at MSP rates through HAFED/government procurement. It is also required for:
- Crop damage compensation (from floods, drought, hail)
- PM Fasal Bima Yojana (crop insurance)
- Agricultural input subsidies
- All state agriculture department schemes

## What Information to Register

When registering on MFMB, you enter:
- **Farm details:** Khasra/khewat number, area under cultivation
- **Crop details:** Which crop (wheat/paddy/mustard etc.) was sown, approximate date of sowing
- **Land ownership:** Own or tenant farming
- **Bank account:** For MSP payment or compensation credit

## Who Must Register?

- **All Haryana farmers** planning to sell crops at:
  - Government procurement centres (HAFED, HSAMB mandis)
  - Regulated mandis for MSP procurement
- **Tenant farmers** (those who farm leased land)
- Farmers claiming crop damage compensation
- Farmers taking crop insurance (PMFBY)

## How to Register on Meri Fasal Mera Byora

### Online (Farmer Self-Registration)
1. Go to **[fasal.haryana.gov.in](https://fasal.haryana.gov.in)**
2. Click **"किसान पंजीकरण"** (Farmer Registration)
3. Enter mobile number → OTP verification
4. Enter **Aadhaar number**
5. Fill crop details:
   - Select crop (wheat/paddy/etc.)
   - Enter khasra/khata number of each plot
   - Area in acres under the crop
   - Sowing date
6. Bank account details (for payment)
7. Submit → download registration certificate

### Via CSC / Jan Seva Kendra
Farmers can also register at any **Common Service Centre (CSC)** near them. Bring all documents.

### Via Arya Kendras (Agriculture offices)
Visit the nearest **Arya Kendra** (Agriculture Knowledge Centre) in your block.

## Important Seasonal Deadlines

| Season | Crop | Registration Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Rabi | Wheat, Mustard | Before March (before harvesting starts) |
| Kharif | Paddy, Maize | Before October-November |

**Deadlines change each year** — check [fasal.haryana.gov.in](https://fasal.haryana.gov.in) for current year's deadlines. **Late registration = no MSP procurement.**

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card**
2. **Land records** (Jamabandi / Khasra-Khatauni) from [jamabandi.nic.in](https://jamabandi.nic.in)
3. **Bank passbook** (Aadhaar-linked)
4. **Mobile number** (registered with Aadhaar)
5. For **tenant farmers:** Lease/rent agreement with the landowner

## Benefits Linked to MFMB Registration

Once registered:
1. **MSP Procurement:** Can sell wheat/paddy at government procurement centre at MSP rates
2. **Crop Damage Compensation:** If crops are damaged (hail/flood), compensation is processed through MFMB data
3. **PM Fasal Bima:** Insurance claim processed using MFMB crop area data
4. **Input subsidy:** Applications for fertilizer/seed subsidy link to MFMB

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I forgot to register before the deadline. What should I do?
Unfortunately, late registrations after the government procurement deadline may not qualify for MSP procurement in that season. However, you can register for the next season. Some years, deadline extensions are given — watch for announcements on [agriharyana.gov.in](https://agriharyana.gov.in).

### I grow wheat on 3 different plots. Do I register all of them?
Yes — enter each khasra number separately with the area under wheat on each plot. All plots should be registered to claim full MSP procurement entitlement.

### I am a tenant farmer and the land is in the owner's name. How do I register?
Tenant farmers can register using the landowner's khasra details + a signed lease/rent agreement. Both tenant and owner cannot register for the same plot for the same season.

### I registered on MFMB. Where do I sell my crop for MSP?
After registration, go to the assigned **Haryana mandi/procurement centre** on the allotted date/slot. Take printout of your MFMB registration. HAFED/HSAMB agents will verify and weigh your produce.

### How is payment made for MSP sales?
Directly to your **registered Aadhaar-linked bank account** via RTGS/NEFT — typically within 72 hours of the crop being weighed and accepted at the mandi.
