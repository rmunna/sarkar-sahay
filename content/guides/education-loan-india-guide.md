# Education Loan in India – Complete Guide for Students

An education loan helps students finance higher education in India or abroad. Most banks and financial institutions offer education loans covering tuition fees, hostel charges, books, and related expenses. This guide covers everything — from eligibility and interest rates to repayment and tax benefits.

---

## What is an Education Loan?

An education loan is a financial product designed to cover the cost of higher education. Key highlights:

- **Purpose:** Tuition fees, hostel, books, equipment, travel (for abroad)
- **Loan range:** ₹50,000 to ₹1.5 crore+ (varies by lender)
- **Moratorium period:** Course duration + 6–12 months after completion
- **Repayment tenure:** 5–15 years after moratorium
- **Tax benefit:** Interest deduction under Section 80E (no upper limit)
- **Government schemes:** Vidya Lakshmi Portal, PM Vidyalaxmi, interest subsidy for economically weaker sections

---

## Who is Eligible?

### Student
- Indian citizen
- Secured admission in a recognized institution (India or abroad)
- Age: Typically 16–35 years
- Courses: Graduation, post-graduation, professional courses (engineering, medicine, MBA, etc.), diploma, PhD

### Co-borrower (Mandatory)
- Parent, guardian, or spouse is required as co-applicant
- Co-borrower's income determines repayment capacity

### Collateral Requirements
| Loan Amount | Collateral |
|-------------|-----------|
| Up to ₹4 lakh | No collateral, no guarantor |
| ₹4 lakh – ₹7.5 lakh | No collateral, third-party guarantor needed |
| Above ₹7.5 lakh | Collateral required (property, FD, etc.) |

## Documents Required

### Student Documents
- **Admission letter** from the institution
- **Mark sheets** (10th, 12th, graduation — as applicable)
- **Entrance exam scorecard** (if applicable)
- **Identity proof:** Aadhaar, PAN, Passport
- **Address proof:** Aadhaar, utility bill
- **Passport-size photographs** (2–4)
- **Cost of study statement** from institution
- **Passport & visa** (for abroad studies)

### Co-borrower Documents
- Identity and address proof
- Income proof (salary slips, ITR for 2 years, Form 16)
- Bank statements (6 months)
- Property documents (if collateral required)

## How to Apply – Step-by-Step

### Option 1: Vidya Lakshmi Portal (Recommended)

1. Visit [vidyalakshmi.co.in](https://www.vidyalakshmi.co.in/)
2. Register with your email and mobile number
3. Fill the Common Education Loan Application Form (CELAF)
4. Select up to **3 banks** to apply simultaneously
5. Upload all required documents
6. Track application status on the portal
7. Banks will process and contact you for further steps

### Option 2: Directly at Bank Branch

1. Visit the nearest branch of your preferred bank
2. Collect and fill the education loan application form
3. Submit along with all documents
4. Bank verifies documents and admission details
5. Collateral valuation (if applicable)
6. Sanction letter issued (7–15 working days)
7. Loan amount disbursed directly to the institution

## Repayment Details

- **Moratorium:** Course duration + 6 to 12 months grace period
- **During moratorium:** No EMI required (interest accrues — you can pay interest to reduce total cost)
- **Repayment tenure:** 5 to 15 years after moratorium ends
- **EMI starts:** After moratorium period
- **Prepayment:** Most banks allow prepayment without penalty

### EMI Example
For a ₹10 lakh loan at 9% interest over 7 years:
- Monthly EMI: ~₹16,089
- Total interest: ~₹3,51,470

## Tax Benefit – Section 80E

- **Who can claim:** The person paying the interest (student or parent)
- **Deduction:** Entire interest amount (no upper limit)
- **Duration:** Up to 8 years from the year of first repayment
- **No benefit on principal** — only interest is deductible
- **Loan must be** from a bank or approved financial institution (not from relatives)

See our [Section 80C guide](/guide/section-80c-deductions-guide) for other tax-saving options.

## Government Interest Subsidy Schemes

### Central Sector Interest Subsidy Scheme (CSIS)
- For students from economically weaker sections (family income < ₹4.5 lakh/year)
- Government pays full interest during moratorium period
- Only for courses in recognized institutions in India

### PM Vidyalaxmi Scheme (2024–25 onwards)
- Unified digital platform for education loans
- Collateral-free loans up to ₹10 lakh
- Interest subvention for students from families with annual income up to ₹8 lakh

## Important Tips

1. **Apply early** — start the loan process 2–3 months before the course starts
2. **Use Vidya Lakshmi Portal** to compare and apply to multiple banks at once
3. **Pay interest during moratorium** if possible — this significantly reduces total cost
4. **Choose PSU banks** for lowest interest rates; NBFCs for faster processing
5. **Keep all fee receipts** for claiming Section 80E tax benefits

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I get an education loan without collateral?
Yes, loans up to ₹7.5 lakh are available without collateral from most banks. Some NBFCs offer collateral-free loans up to ₹40 lakh based on the institution's ranking and placement record.

### What is the Vidya Lakshmi Portal?
It is a government portal (vidyalakshmi.co.in) that provides a single window for students to apply for education loans from multiple banks using one application form.

### Is education loan available for part-time or distance courses?
Most banks do not fund part-time or distance learning courses. However, some banks like SBI offer loans for select distance MBA programs from IIMs. Check with your specific bank.

### Can I get a loan for foreign university education?
Yes, all major banks offer education loans for studies abroad. The loan amount, collateral requirements, and interest rates may differ. A valid admission letter and visa are required.

### What happens if I can't repay the education loan?
Contact your bank immediately and discuss restructuring options. Banks may extend tenure, offer reduced EMI, or provide a moratorium extension. Defaulting will hurt your CIBIL score and may lead to legal action.

### Can I transfer my education loan to another bank?
Yes, education loan balance transfer is possible if another bank offers a lower interest rate. You'll need to pay any applicable processing fees.
