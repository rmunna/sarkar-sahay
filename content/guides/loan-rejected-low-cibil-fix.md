# Loan Rejected Due to Low CIBIL Score? What to Do Next

Getting your loan application rejected is frustrating, especially when you need the money urgently. A low CIBIL score is one of the most common reasons for loan rejection in India. But rejection is not the end — there are practical steps you can take to get approved.

This guide covers minimum CIBIL scores required for different loans, alternative options, and how to improve your chances of approval.

---

## Why Was Your Loan Rejected?

While a low CIBIL score is a major reason, banks may reject loans for multiple factors:

- **CIBIL score below the minimum threshold** (most common)
- Too many recent loan applications (hard inquiries)
- High existing debt-to-income ratio
- Unstable employment or insufficient income
- Loan settlements or defaults in credit history
- Incomplete or incorrect documentation

**Important:** Banks are not required to give specific reasons for rejection. However, you can check your CIBIL report to understand potential issues.

## Minimum CIBIL Score Required for Different Loans

| Loan Type | Banks (Min Score) | NBFCs (Min Score) | Best Score for Approval |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Home Loan** | 700–750 | 650+ | 750+ |
| **Personal Loan** | 720–750 | 650+ | 750+ |
| **Car Loan** | 700–750 | 650+ | 750+ |
| **Education Loan** | 700+ | 650+ | 700+ |
| **Credit Card** | 700–750 | 650+ | 750+ |
| **Gold Loan** | No minimum* | No minimum* | Any |
| **Loan Against FD** | No minimum* | No minimum* | Any |

*Gold loans and loans against FD are fully secured — CIBIL score is less important.

## What to Do After Loan Rejection: Step-by-Step

### Step 1: Don't Apply Immediately to Another Lender

This is the most common mistake. Every new application creates a **hard inquiry** on your CIBIL report, further lowering your score. Wait at least **3–6 months** before your next application.

### Step 2: Check Your CIBIL Report

Download your [free CIBIL report](/guide/cibil-score-check-free-online) and review:
- What is your exact score?
- Are there any errors? ([Dispute errors immediately](/guide/cibil-report-error-dispute-fix))
- Any "Settled" accounts that should be "Closed"?
- High credit card utilization?
- Multiple hard inquiries?

### Step 3: Fix What You Can

Based on your report, take these actions:
- **Errors:** Raise disputes on cibil.com
- **Settled accounts:** Pay remaining amount and get them marked "Closed"
- **High utilization:** Pay down credit card balances
- **Late payments:** Start paying all EMIs on time from now

See our detailed guide: [How to Improve Your CIBIL Score](/guide/cibil-score-low-improve-fix)

### Step 4: Explore Alternative Options (While You Improve Score)

## Alternative Loan Options for Low CIBIL Score

### Option 1: Secured Loans (Score Doesn't Matter Much)

| Loan Type | How It Works | CIBIL Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| **Gold Loan** | Pledge gold jewelry as collateral | Minimal or none |
| **Loan Against FD** | Get 80–90% of FD value as loan | Minimal or none |
| **Loan Against Property** | Mortgage your property | Lower threshold (~650) |
| **Loan Against Insurance** | Borrow against LIC policy | Minimal |
| **Loan Against Mutual Funds** | Pledge mutual fund units | Minimal |

These are your best bet when CIBIL score is low because the collateral reduces risk for the lender.

### Option 2: NBFC and Fintech Lenders

NBFCs (Non-Banking Financial Companies) often have more relaxed CIBIL requirements:

- **Bajaj Finserv** — Personal loans with flexible criteria
- **Tata Capital** — Considers overall profile beyond just score
- **Muthoot Finance** — Gold loans with no score requirement
- **Home Credit** — Consumer loans with lower score requirements

**Note:** NBFC loans typically come with **higher interest rates** (14–24% vs 10–14% for banks). Compare total cost before accepting.

### Option 3: Add a Co-Applicant or Guarantor

If your CIBIL score is low, adding a co-applicant with a good score can help:
- **Co-applicant** — Usually spouse, parent, or sibling with score 750+
- **Both incomes are considered**, improving debt-to-income ratio
- The co-applicant is equally liable for repayment
- **Home loans** most commonly allow co-applicants

### Option 4: Apply with Your Existing Bank

Your primary salary or savings account bank has access to your transaction history. They may be more willing to lend even with a lower CIBIL score because they can see your actual financial behavior.

### Option 5: Reduce Loan Amount

If your original loan amount was high, applying for a smaller amount increases approval chances. Your debt-to-income ratio improves, and the risk for the lender is lower.

## After Getting a Loan with Low CIBIL: Build Your Score

If you get approved through any alternative route:

1. **Pay every EMI on time** — this is the fastest way to rebuild credit
2. **Don't take more loans** until your score improves
3. Get a **secured credit card** and use it responsibly
4. **Monitor your CIBIL score** every few months
5. Target **750+ score** before applying for your next loan at competitive rates

---

## Important Tips

1. **Never apply to multiple banks after rejection** — each inquiry drops your score further
2. **Wait 3–6 months** before reapplying while you fix issues
3. **Secured loans are your best friend** when CIBIL is low
4. **Your salary bank** is more likely to approve you — try there first
5. **Avoid "guaranteed loan" scams** — no legitimate lender guarantees approval regardless of score

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. Can I get a personal loan with a CIBIL score of 600?

Most banks will reject at 600. Some NBFCs like Home Credit or Bajaj Finserv may approve at higher interest rates. Your better option is a secured loan (gold loan, loan against FD) while you [improve your score](/guide/cibil-score-low-improve-fix).

### Q2. What is the minimum CIBIL score for a home loan?

Most banks require 700–750 for home loans. Some NBFCs may approve at 650+. Adding a co-applicant with a good score significantly improves chances. SBI, for example, generally needs 700+.

### Q3. Does loan rejection affect my CIBIL score?

The rejection itself doesn't affect your score. But the **hard inquiry** made during the application process does reduce your score slightly (5–10 points). Multiple rejections mean multiple inquiries, which compounds the damage.

### Q4. How long should I wait before reapplying after rejection?

Wait at least **3–6 months**. Use this time to improve your score — pay down debts, fix errors in your report, and reduce credit utilization.

### Q5. Can I get a loan with no CIBIL score (score -1)?

Some banks and NBFCs approve loans for people with no credit history, especially if you have a good income and bank account relationship. Secured loans and your salary bank are the best options.

### Q6. Is a gold loan a good option when CIBIL is low?

Yes. Gold loans don't require a high CIBIL score since they're fully secured. Interest rates are reasonable (7–15%). Major providers include Muthoot Finance, Manappuram, SBI, and HDFC Bank.

### Q7. Will adding a co-applicant guarantee loan approval?

Not guaranteed, but it significantly improves chances. The co-applicant should have a CIBIL score of 750+ and stable income. The loan will appear on both your and the co-applicant's credit reports.
