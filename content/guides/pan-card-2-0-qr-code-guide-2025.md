# PAN Card 2.0 — What It Is, Free Upgrade, and Whether Your Old PAN Is Still Valid

The Union Cabinet approved **PAN Card 2.0** in November 2024, with rollout beginning in early 2025. PAN 2.0 adds a **QR code** to the PAN card that contains your verified PAN details, making instant digital verification possible without typing.

> **Quick clarification**: Your existing PAN card is **still fully valid**. No deadline to upgrade. PAN 2.0 is optional and free — you're not getting a new PAN number.

## What Is PAN 2.0?

PAN 2.0 is an upgraded version of the physical PAN card:

| Feature | Old PAN | PAN 2.0 |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **QR code** | ❌ No | ✅ Yes — dynamically updated |
| **PAN number** | Same | **Same number, no change** |
| **Online verification** | Manual lookup | Instant scan via QR |
| **Physical card** | Same look | Updated with QR |
| **Valid for KYC** | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes |
| **Aadhaar link** | Not embedded | QR contains Aadhaar link status |

Your PAN number does not change. PAN 2.0 is the same PAN with a QR code added.

## Is the Old PAN Card Still Valid?

**Yes — 100% valid.** The government clarified:
- No deadline to upgrade
- Old PAN cards continue to work for all KYC, ITR filing, bank account opening, investments
- No penalty for not upgrading
- Businesses and institutions cannot refuse old PAN cards

You upgrade only if you want the QR-enabled card for convenience.

## How to Get PAN 2.0 (Free)

### Option 1: Free e-PAN (Digital PAN 2.0)

You can download a **free digital e-PAN** with QR code at no cost:

1. Visit **[incometax.gov.in](https://incometax.gov.in/)** → "Instant e-PAN"
2. Enter your Aadhaar number
3. Complete OTP verification
4. Download e-PAN as PDF — this is your PAN 2.0 in digital form

Alternatively via NSDL:
1. Visit [onlineservices.tin.egov-nsdl.com](https://www.onlineservices.nsdl.com/paam/endUserRegisterContact.html)
2. Go to "Download e-PAN"
3. Enter PAN + date of birth + captcha
4. Receive e-PAN on registered email / download directly

### Option 2: Physical PAN 2.0 Card (₹50 fee)

To get a **physical card** with QR code sent by post:

**Via NSDL:**
1. Visit [onlineservices.tin.egov-nsdl.com](https://www.onlineservices.nsdl.com/paam/endUserRegisterContact.html)
2. Select "Reprint/Duplicate PAN Card"
3. Enter existing PAN details
4. Pay ₹50 (for Indian address) or ₹959 (for overseas address)
5. New PAN 2.0 card dispatched by India Post within 2-3 weeks

**Via UTIITSL:**
1. Visit [utiitsl.com](https://www.utiitsl.com/)
2. Click "PAN Card Services" → "Reprint PAN Card"
3. Enter details and pay ₹50
4. Card delivered in 10-15 working days

> Note: By paying ₹50 for a reprint, you get the new PAN 2.0 physical card at the same address on your NSDL/UTIITSL records.

## When Will PAN 2.0 Cards Start Coming?

NSDL and UTIITSL began issuing new PAN cards with QR codes in 2025. Existing cardholders do not automatically receive a new card — you must request one (free e-PAN or ₹50 physical).

New PAN applications (first-time) automatically get PAN 2.0 cards.

## How the QR Code Works

The QR code on PAN 2.0:
- Contains: Name, Father's name, Date of Birth, PAN number, Aadhaar link status
- Can be scanned by banks, NBFCs, and KYC-compliant apps for instant verification
- Linked to the **Income Tax database** — real-time verification possible
- Cannot be forged (cryptographically signed)

**For businesses**: PAN 2.0 QR verification reduces fake PAN submissions and speeds up KYC.

## What If Your PAN Details Are Wrong?

If your PAN has any errors (name mismatch, wrong DOB), **correct them before applying for PAN 2.0** — the QR code will encode whatever is in the IT department database.

To correct PAN details:
1. Visit NSDL or UTIITSL
2. Select "PAN Correction"
3. Upload supporting documents (Aadhaar, birth certificate)
4. Pay ₹107 (physical card with correction)
5. Corrected PAN 2.0 card issued

## Link PAN to Aadhaar Before Upgrading

The PAN 2.0 QR code shows Aadhaar-PAN link status. If your PAN is not linked to Aadhaar, the QR will reflect "not linked" — which may cause friction with KYC processes.

To link PAN with Aadhaar:
1. Visit [incometax.gov.in](https://incometax.gov.in/) → "Link Aadhaar"
2. Pay ₹1,000 penalty (mandatory for all PAN holders post June 30, 2023)
3. Linking completes within 3-7 days

**Inoperative PAN**: PAN cards not linked to Aadhaar are inoperative — cannot be used for financial transactions. Fix this before requesting PAN 2.0.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Will my ITR filing or Zerodha account be affected if I don't upgrade?

No. Old PAN is fully valid for ITR filing, stock trading, mutual fund investments, and all financial activities. PAN 2.0 is only for added verification convenience.

### I already have a PAN. Will I automatically receive a PAN 2.0 card?

No. You won't automatically get a new card. You need to request it (free e-PAN or ₹50 physical).

### Is there a deadline to get PAN 2.0?

No deadline. The government has not set any deadline to upgrade to PAN 2.0.

### My PAN 2.0 card didn't arrive after 3 weeks. What to do?

Track via NSDL (TIN status tracking) using your 15-digit acknowledgement number. If delayed, contact:
- NSDL PAN helpline: 020-27218080
- Email: tininfo@nsdl.co.in

### Can I use PAN 2.0 e-PAN as address proof?

PAN card (including e-PAN) is proof of identity, not address proof. For address proof, use Aadhaar, Voter ID, utility bills, or Passport.
