# CTET Certificate on DigiLocker: How to Download

The Central Teacher Eligibility Test (CTET) certificate is issued by CBSE and is available for download on DigiLocker. Since 2023, CTET certificates have **lifetime validity** (previously 7 years). This guide explains how to find and download your CTET certificate and mark sheet from DigiLocker.

## What is CTET?

CTET is conducted by the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) to certify eligibility for teaching positions in central government schools (KVS, NVS, etc.) and schools under the Union Territory of Delhi. It has two papers:

- **Paper I** — For classes 1 to 5 (Primary level)
- **Paper II** — For classes 6 to 8 (Upper Primary level)

A candidate must score **60% or above** (90 out of 150) to qualify CTET. Qualified candidates receive a CTET certificate.

---

## CTET Certificate Validity — 7 Years to Lifetime

### Previous Rule (Before 2023)
CTET certificates were valid for **7 years** from the date of issue. Teachers had to re-qualify after expiry.

### Current Rule (2023 Onwards)
As per NCTE notification dated February 2023, CTET qualifying certificates now have **lifetime validity**. This applies to:

- All CTET certificates issued from the December 2021 cycle onwards
- **Retrospectively** to all previously issued CTET certificates (even those that had "expired")

**This means:** If you qualified CTET in 2014 and your certificate showed validity until 2021, it is now valid for life. You do not need to re-appear.

## How to Download CTET Certificate from DigiLocker

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Visit DigiLocker** at [digilocker.gov.in](https://digilocker.gov.in/) or open the DigiLocker mobile app
2. **Sign in** with your registered mobile number or Aadhaar — see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) if needed
3. **Go to "Issued Documents"** section in your dashboard
4. **If the certificate is not auto-fetched**, go to **"Get Documents"** or **"Search Documents"**
5. **Search for issuer:** Type **"CBSE"** or **"Central Board of Secondary Education"**
6. **Select document type:** Choose **"CTET Qualifying Certificate"** or **"CTET Mark Sheet"**
7. **Enter your details:**
   - CTET Roll Number
   - Date of Birth
   - Year of CTET exam
8. **Click "Get Document"**
9. **Your CTET certificate will appear** in your DigiLocker issued documents
10. **Download as PDF** by clicking the download icon

### Alternative: Search by Document Type

1. In DigiLocker, click **"Search"**
2. Type **"CTET"** in the search bar
3. Select **"CTET Certificate"** or **"CTET Marksheet"** from results
4. Select **"CBSE"** as the issuing authority
5. Enter your details and fetch the document

## CTET Mark Sheet vs Certificate — What's the Difference?

| Document | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| **CTET Mark Sheet** | Shows subject-wise marks obtained in Paper I and/or Paper II. Available for all candidates who appeared (pass or fail). |
| **CTET Qualifying Certificate** | Issued only to candidates who scored 60% or above. This is the eligibility certificate required for teaching jobs. |

**For job applications, you need the Qualifying Certificate, not just the mark sheet.**

Both documents are available on DigiLocker under the CBSE issuer.

## What If Your CTET Certificate Is Not Available on DigiLocker?

If you cannot find your CTET certificate on DigiLocker:

1. **Ensure your DigiLocker is linked to Aadhaar** — the details must match your CTET registration
2. **Check the issuer name** — search for "CBSE" not "CTET"
3. **Verify your roll number and DOB** — these must match exactly with your CTET registration
4. **For older CTET certificates (before 2019):** These may not be available on DigiLocker. Contact CBSE directly:
   - Email: ctet@cbse.gov.in
   - Visit: [ctet.nic.in](https://ctet.nic.in/)
5. **Request CBSE to upload:** Write to CBSE requesting digital issuance of your certificate

---

## How to Download CTET Certificate from CBSE Website

If DigiLocker doesn't work, try the direct CBSE route:

1. Visit [ctet.nic.in](https://ctet.nic.in/)
2. Look for **"Download Certificate"** or **"Download Mark Sheet"** link
3. Enter your Registration Number/Roll Number and Date of Birth
4. Download the PDF certificate

**Note:** Availability of download links depends on the CTET cycle. Recent cycles are usually available.

## Important Tips

1. **DigiLocker certificates are legally valid** — they are equivalent to original certificates as per IT Act 2000
2. **Keep both mark sheet and qualifying certificate** downloaded for job applications
3. **Old certificates are now lifetime valid** — you do not need to re-appear for CTET
4. **Ensure your Aadhaar details match** your CTET registration for smooth DigiLocker access
5. **For KVS/NVS recruitment**, the CTET qualifying certificate is mandatory — apply through the respective portals

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Is the CTET certificate on DigiLocker valid for government jobs?

Yes. DigiLocker documents are legally valid as per the IT Act 2000. They are accepted by KVS, NVS, DSSSB, and all government recruitment agencies.

### Q2: I qualified CTET in 2015. Is my certificate still valid?

Yes. As per the 2023 NCTE notification, all CTET certificates now have lifetime validity, including those issued before 2023.

### Q3: Can I download someone else's CTET certificate from DigiLocker?

No. DigiLocker is linked to your Aadhaar and mobile number. You can only access your own documents.

### Q4: What if my name is different on Aadhaar and CTET certificate?

You may face issues fetching the document on DigiLocker. Get your name corrected on either Aadhaar or contact CBSE for correction on the CTET certificate first.

### Q5: Is there a fee to download the CTET certificate from DigiLocker?

No. Downloading documents from DigiLocker is completely free.

### Q6: I passed both Paper I and Paper II. Will I get two certificates?

Yes. You will receive separate qualifying certificates for Paper I and Paper II. Both will be available on DigiLocker.
