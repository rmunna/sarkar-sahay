# Punjab Mukh Mantri Sehat Yojana (MMSY) — ₹10 Lakh Free Health Insurance

The **Mukh Mantri Sehat Yojana (MMSY)** is a universal health insurance scheme launched by the Punjab government on **23 January 2026**. Chief Minister Bhagwant Mann and AAP national convener Arvind Kejriwal inaugurated the scheme in Mohali.

Under MMSY, **every family in Punjab** gets **₹10 lakh per year** of free cashless medical treatment at over **823 empanelled hospitals** — both government and private. The scheme replaces the earlier Sarbat Sehat Bima Yojana / AB PM-JAY MMSBY and doubles the coverage from ₹5 lakh to ₹10 lakh. Punjab is the first state to offer ₹10 lakh universal health insurance with no income limit.

> **Also see:** [All Punjab Government Schemes & Services](/guide/punjab-government-schemes-services) for a complete list of benefits available to Punjab residents.

## Who is Eligible?

MMSY has **universal coverage** — there is **no income limit**. All bonafide residents of Punjab are eligible.

- **All Punjab families** — approximately 65 lakh families covering 3 crore people
- **Government employees** — including pensioners, contractual staff, outsourcing and consultancy-based workers in Punjab government departments, boards, corporations, trusts and societies
- **Children under 18** — covered through parents' or guardians' Voter ID
- **No income ceiling** — rich or poor, every family qualifies

### Basic Requirements

- Must be a bonafide resident of Punjab
- Must have an **Aadhaar card**
- Must have a **Punjab Voter ID**

---

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar Card** — of family members (mandatory for verification and health card generation)
- **Voter ID (Punjab)** — proof of Punjab residency
- **Family details** — names and Aadhaar numbers of all family members
- **Mobile number** — for OTP verification during registration

> For children below 18, parents' Voter ID and Aadhaar are sufficient.

---

## What is Covered?

MMSY covers **2,300+ medical treatment packages** across secondary and tertiary healthcare:

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Cardiology** | Heart surgeries, angioplasty, bypass |
| **Oncology** | Cancer treatment — chemotherapy, radiation, surgery |
| **Neurology** | Brain and spine surgeries |
| **Nephrology** | Kidney dialysis and transplants |
| **Orthopaedics** | Knee and hip replacements |
| **Maternity** | Delivery, newborn care |
| **Emergency & Trauma** | Accident care, ICU |
| **General Medicine** | Hospitalisation, diagnostics |

**Also covered:**
- Pre-hospitalisation expenses (consultations, tests before admission)
- Post-hospitalisation expenses (follow-up care, medicines after discharge)

## How to Apply & Get Your Health Card

### Online / Sewa Kendra Process

1. **Visit a Sewa Kendra** (Common Service Centre) near your home — or apply online through the State Health Agency portal
2. **Carry Aadhaar card and Punjab Voter ID** for all adult family members
3. **Verify identity** — biometric/OTP verification using Aadhaar
4. **Submit family details** — names and Aadhaar numbers of all members
5. **Health card is issued** — a single **Mukh Mantri Sehat Yojana health card** for the entire family
6. **Card links to Aadhaar** — this card is your pass for cashless treatment at any empanelled hospital

### Door-to-Door Enrolment

The Punjab government has deployed **trained youth club members** who visit homes to help residents register and get their health cards made. If you haven't received your card yet, contact your local Sewa Kendra or wait for the door-to-door team.

---

## How to Use the Health Card (Cashless Treatment)

1. Visit any of the **823 empanelled hospitals** (government or private) in Punjab or Chandigarh
2. Show your **MMSY health card** at the hospital reception
3. Hospital verifies your card and provides **cashless treatment** — you pay nothing
4. Hospital submits the claim to the implementing agency (State Health Agency / insurance company)
5. In medical emergencies, treatment outside Punjab is also possible using the health card

---

## Empanelled Hospitals

- **823+ hospitals** across Punjab are empanelled
- Includes government hospitals, district hospitals, sub-divisional hospitals, community health centres, and medical colleges
- Large number of **private multi-specialty and teaching hospitals** included
- Hospitals in **Chandigarh** are also empanelled
- Out-of-state treatment available in cases of medical necessity

To check the full list of empanelled hospitals, visit the official State Health Agency portal: [sha.punjab.gov.in](https://sha.punjab.gov.in)

## Fees

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Health card | **Free** |
| Registration/Enrolment | **Free** |
| Treatment at empanelled hospitals | **Free (cashless)** |
| Premium | **Paid by Punjab government** |

The beneficiary pays **nothing** — the entire scheme is government-funded. The Punjab government has allocated a budget of **₹1,200 crore** for MMSY. Insurance coverage of ₹1 lakh per family is provided through **United India Insurance Company**, while the remaining ₹9 lakh is funded directly by the state government through the State Health Agency.

## Processing Time

- **Health card issuance** — typically same-day at Sewa Kendras, subject to Aadhaar verification
- **Treatment** — cashless and immediate at empanelled hospitals upon card verification

## Important Tips

1. **Keep your health card safe** — it is the single document needed for cashless treatment. One card covers the entire family.
2. **Verify hospital empanelment** before visiting — check the list at [sha.punjab.gov.in](https://sha.punjab.gov.in) or call the helpline.
3. **No income proof needed** — do not pay any middleman or agent claiming to "process" your application. Registration is free.
4. **Children are automatically covered** under parents' card — no separate registration needed for kids under 18.
5. **Emergency treatment outside Punjab** is possible — carry your health card when travelling.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. Who is eligible for Mukh Mantri Sehat Yojana?
**All bonafide residents of Punjab** are eligible — there is no income limit. You need an Aadhaar card and Punjab Voter ID to enrol.

### Q2. How much health coverage does MMSY provide?
MMSY provides **₹10 lakh per family per year** of free cashless medical treatment. This is double the ₹5 lakh limit under the previous scheme.

### Q3. How do I get my MMSY health card?
Visit your nearest **Sewa Kendra** with Aadhaar and Voter ID. You can also wait for the government's door-to-door enrolment drive. Registration is completely free.

### Q4. Which hospitals accept the MMSY health card?
Over **823 government and private hospitals** across Punjab and Chandigarh are empanelled. Check the full list at [sha.punjab.gov.in](https://sha.punjab.gov.in).

### Q5. What treatments are covered under MMSY?
The scheme covers **2,300+ treatment packages** including heart surgery, cancer treatment, kidney dialysis, knee/hip replacement, brain surgery, maternity care, ICU, trauma care, and more.

### Q6. Do I need to pay anything at the hospital?
**No.** Treatment is completely cashless. Show your health card, get treated, and the hospital claims reimbursement from the government.

### Q7. Are government employees eligible?
**Yes.** Government employees, pensioners, and contractual/outsourced staff are all covered under MMSY.

### Q8. Does MMSY replace the earlier Sarbat Sehat Bima Yojana?
Yes. MMSY replaces the Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY Mukh Mantri Sehat Bima Yojana (AB PM-JAY MMSBY) with enhanced coverage of ₹10 lakh and universal eligibility.

### Q9. Can I get treatment outside Punjab?
Yes, in cases of **medical necessity**, beneficiaries can seek treatment outside the state using their MMSY health card.

### Q10. What is the official website for MMSY?
The official portal is the State Health Agency, Punjab: [https://sha.punjab.gov.in](https://sha.punjab.gov.in)
