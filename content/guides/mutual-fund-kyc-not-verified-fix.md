# Mutual Fund KYC Not Verified — How to Fix

You try to invest in a mutual fund and the platform says "KYC not verified" or "KYC incomplete." This is one of the most common blockers for mutual fund investors in India. Your KYC may be registered with a KRA (KYC Registration Agency) but not yet validated — or there could be a data mismatch. This guide covers every scenario and how to fix it.

## Understanding KYC Status Types

Before troubleshooting, know what each KYC status means:

| Status | Meaning | Can You Invest? |
|--------|---------|-----------------|
| **KYC Registered** | Documents submitted but not fully verified | ❌ No (most platforms reject) |
| **KYC Verified** | Old status — identity verified but may lack Aadhaar validation | ⚠️ Limited (some AMCs allow) |
| **KYC Validated** | Aadhaar-based validation done, all details confirmed | ✅ Yes |
| **KYC On Hold** | Discrepancy found, action needed | ❌ No |
| **KYC Rejected** | Application rejected due to errors | ❌ No |

> **Important:** Since SEBI's April 2024 circular, "KYC Validated" (via Aadhaar authentication) is required for new mutual fund investments. Older "KYC Verified" or "KYC Registered" status may not be sufficient.

## Step 1 — Check Your Current KYC Status

Check your KYC status on any of the 4 SEBI-registered KRA portals using your PAN number:

| KRA | Portal URL |
|-----|-----------|
| **CAMS KRA** | [camskra.com](https://www.camskra.com/) |
| **CVL KRA** | [cvlkra.com](https://www.cvlkra.com/) |
| **KFintech (Karvy) KRA** | [karvykra.com](https://www.karvykra.com/) |
| **NDML KRA** | [kra.ndml.in](https://kra.ndml.in/) |

### How to check:
1. Visit any KRA portal above
2. Go to **KYC Status Check** or **KYC Inquiry**
3. Enter your **PAN number**
4. Complete the captcha
5. Your KYC status will be displayed

> **Tip:** Your PAN may be registered with only one KRA. If one portal shows "PAN not found," try another. You can also check on [CAMS Online KYC Enquiry](https://www.camsonline.com/Investors/Statements/KYC-Enquiry) or [KFintech KYC Check](https://mfs.kfintech.com/investor/general/validatekyc).

## Problem 1 — KYC Registered but Not Verified/Validated

This is the most common issue. Your KYC was done years ago (possibly paper-based) and shows as "Registered" or "Verified" but not "Validated."

### How to fix:
1. Visit your **AMC's website or app** (e.g., Groww, Zerodha Coin, Kuvera, MF Central)
2. Look for **"KYC Update"** or **"KYC Modification"** option
3. Complete **Aadhaar-based OTP verification** (eKYC)
4. Your mobile number and email will be re-validated via Aadhaar
5. Status changes to **"KYC Validated"** within 24–72 hours

### Alternative — Update via KRA portal directly:
1. Visit [CAMS KRA](https://www.camskra.com/) → **Modify KYC**
2. Enter PAN → Authenticate with Aadhaar OTP
3. Verify mobile number and email linked to Aadhaar
4. Submit — KYC status updates to Validated

> **Note:** The KRA your PAN is registered with matters. If your PAN is with CVL KRA, update on CVL KRA's portal or through an AMC that routes to CVL.

## Problem 2 — Aadhaar-Based eKYC Rejected

Your Aadhaar OTP verification failed or eKYC was rejected during mutual fund KYC.

### Common causes and fixes:

| Cause | Fix |
|-------|-----|
| Mobile number not linked to Aadhaar | Visit nearest Aadhaar centre → link your current mobile number |
| Aadhaar details don't match PAN (name/DOB) | Update Aadhaar or PAN to match (see Problem 4 below) |
| UIDAI server down | Try again after a few hours |
| Biometric lock enabled on Aadhaar | Unlock biometrics on [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/) |
| OTP not received | Check if your mobile is active; try after 30 minutes |

### If eKYC keeps failing:
- Try **Aadhaar XML** or **DigiLocker** method instead of OTP
- Some platforms (like CAMS KRA) support Aadhaar XML upload as an alternative
- As a last resort, do **physical KYC** at an AMC branch or KRA Point of Service (PoS)

## Problem 3 — CKYC Number Issues

CKYC (Central KYC) is a centralized KYC record maintained by CERSAI. Some platforms ask for your CKYC number.

### "CKYC number not found":
- Your KYC may exist with a KRA but not yet migrated to CKYC
- Complete Aadhaar-based KYC validation on any AMC platform — a CKYC record is auto-created
- Check CKYC status at [ckycindia.in](https://www.ckycindia.in/) using PAN

### "CKYC details mismatch":
- Name, DOB, or address in CKYC doesn't match your mutual fund application
- Update CKYC through your bank or AMC by submitting corrected documents
- Banks can update CKYC records directly via CERSAI portal

## Problem 4 — Name or DOB Mismatch

A name or date of birth mismatch between PAN, Aadhaar, and KRA records is a frequent KYC blocker.

### How to identify:
- KYC status shows **"On Hold"** with reason: name/DOB mismatch
- eKYC fails with "details do not match" error
- Check exact name and DOB on:
  - **PAN card** — [incometax.gov.in](https://www.incometax.gov.in/) → Verify PAN
  - **Aadhaar** — [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/) → Profile

### How to fix:
1. **If Aadhaar is wrong:** Update name/DOB on Aadhaar at [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/) or visit an Aadhaar centre
2. **If PAN is wrong:** Correct PAN details at [onlineservices.nsdl.com/paam/endUserRegisterContact.html](https://onlineservices.nsdl.com/paam/endUserRegisterContact.html) (NSDL) or [utiitsl.com](https://www.utiitsl.com/) (UTIITSL)
3. **Once both match:** Re-do Aadhaar eKYC on your AMC platform or KRA portal
4. KYC status should update to Validated within 1–3 working days

> **Critical:** Name must match **exactly** — even minor differences like "RAJESH KUMAR" vs "RAJESH KUMAR SHARMA" will cause rejection.

## Problem 5 — IPV (In-Person Verification) Pending

Some older KYC records or new KYC applications require IPV — a video or in-person identity check.

### How to complete IPV:
1. **Via AMC app** — Most apps (Groww, Kuvera, Paytm Money, ET Money) offer **video IPV** during KYC
   - You'll be asked to show your PAN card on camera
   - Record a short video or take a live selfie
   - Ensure good lighting and clear PAN visibility
2. **Via AMC branch** — Visit any mutual fund AMC branch with original PAN and Aadhaar
3. **Via KRA Point of Service (PoS)** — Find nearest PoS on the KRA portal

### IPV keeps failing?
- Ensure your camera permissions are enabled for the browser/app
- Use a well-lit room with a plain background
- Hold PAN card steady — avoid blur
- Try a different browser (Chrome works best)
- Some apps have a 60-second timeout — keep PAN ready before starting

## How to Complete KYC via MFCentral

[MFCentral](https://www.mfcentral.com/) is a unified platform by CAMS and KFintech where you can manage KYC for all your mutual fund investments.

### Steps:
1. Visit [mfcentral.com](https://www.mfcentral.com/) → Register/Login
2. Go to **Services** → **KYC Validation**
3. Enter PAN → Authenticate via Aadhaar OTP
4. Verify your mobile number and email
5. Complete video IPV if prompted
6. KYC status updates across all AMCs automatically

### Benefits of MFCentral:
- Single platform for KYC across all mutual funds
- View consolidated portfolio
- Initiate redemptions and switches
- Download CAS (Consolidated Account Statement)

## How to Complete KYC via AMC Apps

Most mutual fund platforms offer end-to-end KYC within their apps:

| Platform | KYC Path |
|----------|----------|
| **Groww** | Profile → KYC Status → Complete KYC |
| **Zerodha Coin** | Console → Account → KYC Details |
| **Kuvera** | Profile → KYC → Update KYC |
| **Paytm Money** | Profile → KYC Verification |
| **ET Money** | Profile → KYC → Verify Now |
| **SBI MF** | [sbimf.com](https://www.sbimf.com/) → Investor Services → KYC |

### What you'll need:
- PAN card (original + clear photo/scan)
- Aadhaar number (mobile linked to Aadhaar required)
- Cancelled cheque or bank statement (for bank verification)
- Smartphone with camera (for video IPV)

## Troubleshooting Quick Reference

| Issue | Quick Fix |
|-------|-----------|
| KYC Registered, not Validated | Complete Aadhaar eKYC on AMC app or KRA portal |
| eKYC OTP not received | Link mobile to Aadhaar first |
| Name mismatch | Correct PAN or Aadhaar to match exactly |
| DOB mismatch | Update the incorrect document (PAN/Aadhaar) |
| CKYC not found | Complete Aadhaar validation — CKYC auto-creates |
| IPV pending | Use video KYC on AMC app or visit branch |
| KYC on hold | Check KRA portal for specific reason, fix accordingly |
| Status not updating | Wait 3 working days; contact AMC if still stuck |

## How Long Does KYC Validation Take?

| Method | Time |
|--------|------|

| Aadhaar XML/DigiLocker | 2–5 working days |
| Physical KYC at branch | 5–7 working days |
| KYC modification/correction | 3–5 working days |
