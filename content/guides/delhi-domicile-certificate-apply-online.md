# Delhi Domicile Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **domicile certificate** in Delhi (National Capital Territory) is issued by the Revenue Department (Sub-Divisional Magistrate) and is required for NEET Delhi state quota admissions, DSSSB (Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board) government jobs, Delhi government scholarship eligibility, and DU/IP University state quota seats. Applications go through the **Delhi e-District** portal.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in](https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in). Must be a Delhi resident for **3 years**. Issued by SDM within **7–21 days**. Fee: ₹10.

- **DSSSB government jobs** — Delhi domicile for Delhi-specific posts
- **DU / IP University state quota** — state quota seats for Delhi domicile holders
- **Delhi government scholarship** — requires Delhi domicile proof

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** — mandatory (with Delhi address)
- **Ration card** — Delhi ration card shows permanent address
- **Voter ID** — Delhi voter enrollment shows residency
- **School/college certificates** — if studied in Delhi (supporting 3-year residency)
- **Electricity or water bill** — showing Delhi address for 3 years
- **Rent agreement** — if renting (showing duration at Delhi address)
- Self-declaration of 3-year Delhi residency
- Passport-size photograph

> **3-year proof:** You need documents covering 3 continuous years at a Delhi address. The combination of ration card + voter ID + utility bills is the strongest proof bundle.

## How to Apply Online — Delhi e-District

1. Go to [edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in](https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in)
2. Click **"New User? Register"** → register with mobile number
3. Log in → click **"Revenue Department"** → **"Domicile Certificate"**
4. Fill form:
   - Name, address (district, sub-division, ward/village)
   - Duration of residence in Delhi
   - Purpose of certificate
5. Upload documents (Aadhaar, ration card, voter ID, utility bills)
6. Pay fee: **₹10** (online payment)
7. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

## Delhi Domicile for NEET 2026

For **NEET 2026 Delhi state quota** (DGHS Delhi counselling — 85% seats):
1. Delhi domicile certificate — must be a Delhi resident for 3 years
2. Must have studied at a **Delhi school** (Class 8–12 or as per DGHS notification)
3. Upload at DGHS Delhi counselling portal during document verification
4. Apply early — processing takes 7–21 days and NEET results come quickly
5. Aadhaar alone is not enough — formal SDM-issued certificate required

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I've lived in Delhi for 3 years on rent — am I eligible?

**Yes.** Rent agreement + utility bills covering 3 years of Delhi address is sufficient. You don't need to own property in Delhi. Consistent rent receipts + Aadhaar with Delhi address + voter enrollment in Delhi strengthen the application.

### My Aadhaar shows Delhi address — do I still need a domicile certificate?

Yes — for formal purposes (NEET state quota, DSSSB), you need the **SDM-issued domicile certificate**, not just Aadhaar. Aadhaar is used as supporting document, not the domicile certificate itself.

### Is Delhi domicile valid for central government jobs?

**No.** Delhi domicile is for Delhi-specific state benefits. For central government jobs (UPSC, SSC, railways), there is no domicile requirement — they are open to all Indian citizens.

### My family is from UP but I've been in Delhi for 5 years — which domicile?

If you've been a **continuous resident of Delhi for 3+ years** with verifiable documents (ration card, voter ID, utility bills in Delhi), you are eligible for Delhi domicile. However, if your family still has UP address and you return regularly, this may complicate the "continuous residence" argument.
