# EPF Withdrawal Online 2026 — Complete Guide (Full, Partial & Advance)

Your **EPF (Employee Provident Fund)** is one of your most valuable financial assets. You can withdraw it fully after leaving a job (with conditions) or take a partial advance for specific needs. Since 2015, the process is fully online through the EPFO portal — no employer signature needed for most withdrawals.

---

## Types of EPF Withdrawal

| Type | When | Form | Timeline |
|------|------|------|---------|
| **Full withdrawal** | After retirement (58+) or 2 months of unemployment | Form 19 | 5–10 working days |
| **Full withdrawal (age 54)** | Can withdraw 90% before retirement at 57 | Form 19 | 5–10 working days |
| **Partial advance — Medical** | Any time (for self/family) | Form 31 | 5–7 days |
| **Partial advance — House purchase/construction** | After 5 years | Form 31 | 5–10 days |
| **Partial advance — Marriage** | After 7 years | Form 31 | 5–7 days |
| **Partial advance — Education** | After 7 years | Form 31 | 5–7 days |
| **EPS Pension withdrawal** | After leaving service (< 10 years) | Form 10C | 5–10 days |

---

## Prerequisites for Online EPF Withdrawal

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| **UAN (Universal Account Number)** | Activated + KYC verified |
| **Aadhaar linked and OTP verified** | Mandatory for online withdrawal |
| **Bank account linked** | IFSC + account number seeded with EPFO |
| **PAN linked** | Required (TDS applies without PAN) |
| **Not in employment** | For full withdrawal: must not be employed for 2+ months |

## Full EPF Withdrawal — Step by Step (After Leaving Job)

**Eligible after:** Not employed for **2 months** after leaving previous employer

1. Go to **[unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in)**
2. Login with UAN + password
3. Go to: **"Online Services"** → **"Claim (Form 31, 19, 10C & 10D)"**
4. Verify bank account (last 4 digits shown — confirm)
5. Click **"Proceed for Online Claim"**
6. Select claim type: **"PF Full Settlement"** (Form 19)
7. Check Reason for leaving, last date of employment
8. Enter your complete address
9. Click **"Get Aadhar OTP"** → OTP on Aadhaar-linked mobile
10. Enter OTP → Submit claim
11. **Claim reference number** generated — note it

**Disbursement:** Amount credited in **5–10 working days** to linked bank account.

## Partial EPF Advance — Step by Step

### For Medical Emergency (No Minimum Service)

1. EPFO portal → Online Services → Claim (Form 31)
2. Select: **"PF Advance (Form 31)"**
3. Purpose: **Medical**
4. Eligible amount: Up to 6 months basic salary + DA OR total employee share with interest, whichever is lower
5. Aadhaar OTP → Submit
6. **Disbursement: 5–7 working days**

### For House Purchase (After 5 Years Service)

1. Form 31 → Purpose: **Housing**
2. Amount: Up to 90% of EPF balance
3. Condition: Property must be in your name (or spouse)
4. Upload: Registered sale agreement / property documents

### For Marriage (After 7 Years)

1. Form 31 → Purpose: **Marriage (Self/Son/Daughter/Brother/Sister)**
2. Amount: Up to 50% of employee's contribution
3. Available: **3 times** in entire service period

### For Education (After 7 Years)

1. Form 31 → Purpose: **Education**
2. Amount: Up to 50% of employee's contribution
3. Available 3 times in service period

## EPF Withdrawal — TDS Rules

| Condition | TDS |
|-----------|-----|
| Withdrawal before 5 years service + PAN submitted | **10% TDS** |
| Withdrawal before 5 years service + no PAN | **34.608% TDS** |
| Withdrawal after 5 years service | **No TDS** |
| Withdrawal due to ill-health/employer closure | **No TDS** |

**Form 15G/15H:** Submit to EPFO before withdrawal if:
- Your income is below taxable limit
- You want to avoid TDS even on early withdrawal

File online: EPFO portal → Online Services → Form 15G/15H

## EPS (Pension) — How to Withdraw

EPS (Employee Pension Scheme) is separate from EPF (Provident Fund):

| Service Period | What You Get |
|---------------|-------------|
| Less than 6 months | Cannot withdraw EPS |
| 6 months – 10 years | Withdraw EPS as lump sum (Form 10C) |
| 10 years + | Monthly pension from age 58 (Form 10D) |

**EPS pension amount formula (simplified):**
Monthly Pension = (Pensionable Salary × Pensionable Service) / 70

## Claim Rejected — Common Reasons & Fix

| Rejection Reason | Fix |
|-----------------|-----|
| Bank account not linked/verified | Link bank in EPFO portal → Employer approves → Try claim again |
| Aadhaar not seeded/OTP failed | Link Aadhaar in EPFO portal → Aadhaar-based OTP |
| Date of exit not updated | Ask previous employer to update exit date in EPFO |
| Claim amount mismatch | Recalculate and refile |
| PAN not linked | Link PAN on EPFO portal |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I withdraw EPF while still employed?**
You can take advances (for house, medical, marriage, education) while employed. Full settlement is only after leaving the job and waiting 2 months.

**Is EPF withdrawal taxable?**
Full PF withdrawal before 5 years of continuous service: Taxable as salary income. After 5 years: Tax-free. So ideally, don't withdraw before 5 years.

**How do I check EPF claim status?**
EPFO portal → Track Claim Status → Enter UAN → See status. Or UMANG app → EPFO → Track Claim.

**My previous employer is defunct — how to get PF?**
File a claim directly on EPFO portal. For defunct employers, EPFO processes claims based on available records. If not resolved: Contact EPFO regional office directly.

**Can I merge multiple PF accounts from different jobs?**
Yes — UAN is supposed to consolidate all PF accounts. If not auto-merged: EPFO portal → Online Services → One Member One EPF Account (Transfer Request). Submit for merging.
