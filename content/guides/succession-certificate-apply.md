# Succession Certificate: How to Apply & Documents Needed

When a person dies without leaving a will (intestate), the legal heirs need a **Succession Certificate** to claim the deceased's debts and securities — such as bank deposits, shares, mutual funds, and insurance. This guide explains the complete process, documents, and fees.

## What is a Succession Certificate?

A Succession Certificate is a legal document issued by a **Civil Court** (District Court) under the **Indian Succession Act, 1925** (Sections 370–390). It authorises the holder to:

- Withdraw money from the deceased's bank accounts
- Transfer shares, mutual funds, and securities
- Collect debts owed to the deceased
- Claim insurance payouts and provident fund

**Important:** A succession certificate deals only with **debts and securities** — not immovable property. For property transfer, you may need a [Legal Heir Certificate](/guide/legal-heir-certificate-apply) or probate.

---

## When is a Succession Certificate Needed?

- Deceased person died **without a will** (intestate)
- Banks or financial institutions require it to release funds above a threshold (typically ₹1–2 lakh)
- To transfer shares, debentures, or mutual fund holdings
- Insurance company requires it for claim settlement
- When multiple legal heirs need to establish rights to the estate

## Succession Certificate vs Legal Heir Certificate

| Feature | Succession Certificate | Legal Heir Certificate |
|---------|----------------------|----------------------|
| Issued by | Civil Court (District Judge) | Tahsildar / SDM / Revenue authority |
| Purpose | Claim debts & securities of deceased | Establish relationship with deceased |
| Legal basis | Indian Succession Act, 1925 | State revenue rules |
| Scope | Debts and securities only | General purpose — pension, insurance, job |
| Fee | Court fee (% of estate value) | ₹50 – ₹100 |
| Timeline | 3–12 months | 15–45 days |
| Legal weight | High — court order | Medium — administrative certificate |

## Documents Required

### Mandatory Documents
- **Death certificate** of the deceased
- **Proof of relationship** — birth certificate, marriage certificate, family tree, ration card
- **ID proof of applicant** — [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online), PAN card, voter ID
- **Address proof** of applicant
- **Details of assets** — bank passbook, share certificates, FD receipts, MF statements
- **Affidavit** stating the applicant is the legal heir and no will exists
- **NOC from other legal heirs** (if not all are applying jointly)

### Supporting Documents
- Family pension certificate (if applicable)
- Ration card showing family members
- Indemnity bond (as required by court)

## Step-by-Step Process to Apply

### Step 1: Prepare the Petition
Draft a petition (through a lawyer) to the District Court having jurisdiction where the deceased:
- Last resided, OR
- Had property at the time of death

The petition must include:
- Details of the deceased (name, date of death, last address)
- Relationship of the petitioner to the deceased
- Details of all legal heirs
- List of debts and securities to be claimed
- Statement that no will exists

### Step 2: File the Petition in Court
- File the petition along with supporting documents at the District Court
- Pay the court fee (ad valorem — based on estate value)
- The court assigns a case number

### Step 3: Court Issues Public Notice
- The court publishes a notice in a **local newspaper** and the **Official Gazette**
- The notice invites objections from any person within **45 days**
- The petitioner bears the cost of publication

### Step 4: Waiting Period (45 Days)
- If no objection is received within 45 days, the court proceeds
- If objections are raised, the court conducts a hearing

### Step 5: Court Hearing
- The judge verifies documents and hears the petitioner
- May require witnesses to confirm the relationship and facts
- If satisfied, the judge grants the Succession Certificate

### Step 6: Obtain the Certificate
- The court issues the Succession Certificate specifying the debts/securities covered
- The certificate is stamped and signed by the court
- Present this to banks, mutual fund companies, or other institutions

## Fees

| Component | Amount |
|-----------|--------|
| Court fee (ad valorem) | 2% – 5% of estate value (varies by state) |
| Stamp duty on certificate | Varies by state (typically 2–3% of asset value) |
| Lawyer fee | ₹5,000 – ₹25,000 (varies by complexity) |
| Newspaper publication | ₹1,000 – ₹3,000 |
| Miscellaneous (affidavit, notary) | ₹500 – ₹1,000 |

**Note:** Court fees vary significantly by state. In some states, the maximum court fee is capped. Check with the local District Court.

---

## Processing Time

- **Typical timeline:** 3–6 months (if no objections)
- **With objections:** 6–12 months or longer
- **Minimum mandatory waiting:** 45 days (public notice period)

## E-Filing Option

Some District Courts now accept e-filing through [efiling.ecourts.gov.in](https://efiling.ecourts.gov.in/). You can:
- File the petition online
- Upload supporting documents
- Track case status at [districts.ecourts.gov.in](https://districts.ecourts.gov.in/)

Physical appearance is still required for hearings.

## Important Tips

1. **Hire a lawyer** — the court process requires properly drafted petitions and legal knowledge
2. **Get NOC from all heirs** — this speeds up the process and avoids objections
3. **Keep asset details ready** — the court needs a complete list of debts and securities
4. **Don't delay** — banks may freeze accounts; apply as soon as possible after obtaining the death certificate
5. **Check if you really need it** — for small amounts, banks may release funds with just a legal heir certificate and indemnity bond

## FAQs

### Who can apply for a succession certificate?
Any legal heir of the deceased — spouse, children, parents, or siblings. If there are multiple heirs, one can apply with NOC from others, or all can apply jointly.

### Can I get a succession certificate if there is a will?
If a valid will exists, you generally need **probate** (in cities like Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai) instead of a succession certificate. The will's executor handles the estate.

### How long does it take to get a succession certificate?
Typically 3–6 months. The mandatory 45-day public notice period is the minimum. Court backlogs may extend the timeline.

### Is a succession certificate valid across India?
Yes, a succession certificate issued by any District Court in India is valid across the country for the debts and securities mentioned in it.

### Can a succession certificate be challenged?
Yes, any interested party can file objections during the 45-day notice period. Even after issuance, it can be revoked by the court if obtained through fraud or misrepresentation.

### Do I need a succession certificate for property transfer?
No, a succession certificate covers only debts and securities. For immovable property, you need mutation of records based on a [legal heir certificate](/guide/legal-heir-certificate-apply) or court order.

### What is the court fee for a succession certificate?
Court fees are ad valorem (based on estate value) and vary by state — typically 2–5% of the total value of debts and securities claimed.
