The **Uttar Pradesh Free Ration Yojana** provides free food grains to eligible families under the **National Food Security Act (NFSA), 2013** and state-level schemes. With over 15 crore beneficiaries across the state, UP's Public Distribution System (PDS) is one of the largest in India, distributing free rice, wheat, sugar and other essential commodities through a network of Fair Price Shops.

## What is UP Free Ration Yojana?

Under NFSA, eligible households in Uttar Pradesh receive subsidised and free food grains monthly through their ration cards. The Government of India extended the **Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana (PMGKAY)** provisions, providing **5 kg free food grains per person per month** to all NFSA beneficiaries. Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) families receive **35 kg per household per month**.

## Types of Ration Cards in UP

| Card Type | Colour | Entitlement |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) | Yellow | 35 kg food grains per household/month at free/subsidised rates |
| Priority Household (PHH) | Green | 5 kg per person per month free food grains |
| Above Poverty Line (APL) | Blue/White | Subsidised grains based on availability |

## Who is Eligible?

- **Permanent resident** of Uttar Pradesh
- **BPL families** (Below Poverty Line) and economically weaker households
- **AAY eligibility:** Landless labourers, marginal farmers, artisans, widows, disabled persons, households headed by women, destitute families
- **PHH eligibility:** Families not covered under AAY but identified through SECC data
- **Annual family income** should not exceed the prescribed state limit
- Must possess a valid **ration card** issued by the UP Food & Civil Supplies Department

## Documents Required

### For New Ration Card Application
- Aadhaar card of family head and all family members
- Income certificate
- Address proof (electricity bill, water bill, rent agreement)
- Passport-size photographs of family head
- Bank account details
- Mobile number (linked to Aadhaar)
- Self-declaration form
- Existing ration card (for modifications/additions)

## Step-by-Step Process

### Check Beneficiary List Online

1. **Visit** the UP Food & Civil Supplies portal at [fcs.up.gov.in](https://fcs.up.gov.in/)
2. **Click** on "NFSA Beneficiary List" or "राशन कार्ड की पात्रता सूची"
3. **Select** your district, block/urban area, and Fair Price Shop
4. **View** the list of beneficiaries — check if your name and ration card number appear
5. **Download** or print the list for your records

### Apply for New Ration Card

1. **Visit** the UP FCS portal at [fcs.up.gov.in](https://fcs.up.gov.in/)
2. **Click** on "Online Application" or visit your nearest CSC centre
3. **Fill** the application form with family details, Aadhaar numbers and income details
4. **Upload** required documents (Aadhaar, photographs, address proof)
5. **Submit** the application and note the reference number
6. **Verification** is conducted by the supply inspector at your residence
7. **Ration card issued** after successful verification within 15–30 days
8. **Collect** your ration card from the designated office or it's sent digitally

### How to Get Monthly Ration

1. **Visit** your designated Fair Price Shop on the scheduled distribution day
2. **Carry** your ration card and Aadhaar card
3. **Authenticate** via biometric (fingerprint) at the ePoS machine
4. **Collect** your entitled food grains (rice, wheat, sugar)
5. **Check** the quantity and quality before leaving the shop

## Fees

- **Ration card application:** Free (no charges)
- **Food grains:** Free for NFSA/PMGKAY beneficiaries; ₹2/kg wheat, ₹3/kg rice for PHH under NFSA rates (currently free under PMGKAY)

## Processing Time

- **New ration card:** 15–30 days after application
- **Name addition/deletion:** 7–15 days
- **Monthly ration distribution:** 1st–25th of each month

## Important Tips

1. **Link Aadhaar to your ration card** — biometric authentication is mandatory for collecting ration
2. **Use the One Nation One Ration Card** facility if you migrate to another state — your ration can be collected at any FPS across India
3. **Report** any irregularity at the Fair Price Shop by calling the UP helpline **1967** or **1800-1800-150**
4. **Check your entitlement** regularly on the FCS portal to ensure your ration card is active
5. **eKYC must be completed** for all family members to avoid ration card suspension

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much free ration do I get per month in UP?

Under PMGKAY/NFSA, PHH cardholders get **5 kg free food grains per person per month**. AAY households receive **35 kg per family per month**. This includes rice and/or wheat.

### How can I check if my name is on the ration card list?

Visit [fcs.up.gov.in](https://fcs.up.gov.in/) and check the NFSA beneficiary list by selecting your district and FPS. You can also check via the Mera Ration mobile app.

### Can I get ration in another district of UP?

Yes. Under the **One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC)** scheme, you can collect your entitled food grains from any Fair Price Shop in India using your Aadhaar-linked ration card.

### What should I do if the FPS dealer gives less ration?

Complain on the UP Food helpline **1967**, the FCS portal grievance section, or visit the District Supply Officer. You can also register a complaint on the **IGRS UP** portal.

### How can I add a new family member to my ration card?

Apply online through the FCS portal or visit a CSC/Lok Seva Kendra with the new member's Aadhaar card, birth certificate and a photograph. The addition is processed within 7–15 days.

### Is Aadhaar mandatory for getting ration?

Yes. Aadhaar-based biometric authentication is mandatory for collecting ration from Fair Price Shops in UP. Ensure all family members have their Aadhaar linked to the ration card.
