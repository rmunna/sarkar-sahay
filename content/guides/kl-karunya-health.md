# Karunya Health Scheme (KASP) Kerala – ₹5 Lakh Free Health Cover

## What is Karunya Health Scheme?

The Karunya Arogya Suraksha Padhathi (KASP) is Kerala's state health insurance scheme, integrated with the central **Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY** scheme. It provides **free treatment up to ₹5 lakh per family per year** at empanelled government and private hospitals. The scheme covers **over 1,600 medical procedures** including surgeries, diagnostics, and hospitalization.

## Who is Eligible?

1. Must be a **resident of Kerala**
2. Must hold a valid **ration card** (priority and AAY categories)
3. Families identified under the **Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC)** database
4. **All family members** on the ration card are covered
5. **No age limit** — covers all ages

### Additional Coverage

- Kerala has extended KASP coverage beyond PM-JAY beneficiaries to cover a **larger population** including some APL families
- Check eligibility at [kasp.kerala.gov.in](https://kasp.kerala.gov.in)

## Coverage Details

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Annual Cover | ₹5,00,000 per family |
| Procedures | 1,600+ medical/surgical procedures |
| Hospitals | Government + empanelled private hospitals |
| Pre-existing Diseases | Covered from day one |
| Follow-up | Post-hospitalization expenses covered for 15 days |

### What is Covered?

- Heart surgeries (bypass, angioplasty, valve replacement)
- Cancer treatment (surgery, chemotherapy, radiation)
- Kidney diseases (transplant, dialysis)
- Neurosurgery and brain surgery
- Joint and knee replacement
- Eye surgeries
- Burns and trauma care
- Neonatal and paediatric surgeries
- And 1,600+ other procedures

## Documents Required

- **Ration Card** (Kerala)
- **Aadhaar Card** of the patient
- **KASP/AB-PMJAY Card** (if already issued)
- **Hospital Referral** (for private hospital treatment)
- **Previous Medical Records** (if any)

## How to Avail KASP Treatment

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Check Eligibility:** Visit [kasp.kerala.gov.in](https://kasp.kerala.gov.in) and enter your ration card number
2. **Visit Hospital:** Go to any KASP-empanelled hospital (government or private)
3. **KASP Help Desk:** Visit the KASP help desk at the hospital with ration card and Aadhaar
4. **Biometric Verification:** Fingerprint authentication to confirm identity
5. **Doctor Consultation:** Doctor examines and recommends treatment
6. **Pre-Authorization:** Hospital sends request to KASP for approval
7. **Treatment:** Once approved, treatment is completely free
8. **Discharge:** Post-discharge medicines and follow-up covered for 15 days

### Finding Empanelled Hospitals

1. Visit [kasp.kerala.gov.in](https://kasp.kerala.gov.in) or [sha.kerala.gov.in](https://sha.kerala.gov.in)
2. Search "Hospital List" by district
3. Or call the KASP helpline for nearest empanelled hospital

## How to Get the KASP Card

- Visit your local **Akshaya Centre** or **KASP help desk** at a government hospital
- Carry your ration card and Aadhaar
- Biometric verification will be done
- E-card is generated instantly

## Processing Time

- **Emergency Treatment:** Immediate (no pre-authorization delay)
- **Planned Treatment:** Pre-authorization takes 1-3 days
- **Card Generation:** Instant at Akshaya centres

## Important Tips

1. **Carry Ration Card:** Always keep your ration card accessible — it's the primary eligibility proof.
2. **Emergencies First:** For emergencies, hospitals must treat immediately. Pre-authorization happens later.
3. **Verify Hospital:** Not all private hospitals are empanelled. Check the list before visiting.
4. **No Cash Needed:** You should not pay anything at empanelled hospitals for covered procedures.
5. **Helpline:** Call the KASP helpline **104** for any queries or complaints.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Is KASP the same as Ayushman Bharat?
KASP is Kerala's implementation of Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY with additional state coverage. It covers more families than PM-JAY alone.

### Q2: Can I use KASP at any hospital?
Only at **KASP-empanelled** hospitals. Check the list at [kasp.kerala.gov.in](https://kasp.kerala.gov.in).

### Q3: What if my ₹5 lakh limit is used up?
You can seek treatment at **government hospitals** where treatment continues to be free regardless of the KASP limit.

### Q4: Are medicines covered after discharge?
**Yes.** Post-discharge medicines for **15 days** and follow-up consultations are covered.

### Q5: Do I need a referral from a government hospital?
For government hospitals, no referral is needed. For private empanelled hospitals, a referral from a government doctor may be required for planned procedures.

### Q6: Can I use KASP outside Kerala?
Under the PM-JAY portability feature, you may be able to use it at **empanelled hospitals in other states**. Check with the hospital.
