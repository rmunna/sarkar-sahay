# IRCTC Account Login Problems: How to Fix

IRCTC (Indian Railway Catering and Tourism Corporation) is the only platform for booking train tickets online in India. Login issues can prevent you from booking tickets, checking PNR status, or managing your bookings. This guide covers every common IRCTC login problem and its solution.

## Common Error Messages and Symptoms

- **"Invalid username or password"** — credentials not accepted
- **"Account is locked"** — too many failed login attempts
- **"OTP not received"** — verification OTP does not arrive on mobile or email
- **"Captcha is incorrect"** — captcha validation fails repeatedly
- **"User ID does not exist"** — account not found
- **"Your session has expired"** — logged out during booking
- **"Service unavailable — please try later"** — server overload
- Login page loads but clicking "Login" does nothing
- App shows spinning loader indefinitely after login
- Password reset email not received
- New registration fails with "Mobile/Email already registered"

## Why IRCTC Login May Not Work

### 1. Forgotten or Incorrect Password

IRCTC passwords have specific requirements (8-15 characters, uppercase, lowercase, number, special character). Misremembering even one character causes failure.

### 2. Account Locked

After 5 consecutive failed login attempts, IRCTC locks the account for security. You must wait or reset to unlock.

### 3. Server Overload

During Tatkal booking hours (10 AM for AC classes, 11 AM for non-AC) and festival seasons, IRCTC servers are heavily loaded.

### 4. Browser Issues

IRCTC has known issues with certain browsers, cookie settings, and cached data.

### 5. Captcha Problems

IRCTC's captcha system sometimes generates unclear or unreadable images, especially on mobile browsers.

## Step-by-Step Troubleshooting

### Step 1: Reset Your Password

1. Visit [irctc.co.in](https://www.irctc.co.in/)
2. Click **"Forgot Password"** or **"Forgot Account"**
3. Enter your **User ID**
4. Select reset method: **OTP on registered mobile** or **email**
5. Enter the OTP and set a new password
6. Password requirements: 8-15 characters, must include uppercase, lowercase, number, and special character

### Step 2: Unlock Locked Account

If your account is locked after multiple failed attempts:
1. Wait **30 minutes** — the lock expires automatically
2. Or reset your password using "Forgot Password" to unlock immediately
3. After unlocking, log in carefully with the new password

### Step 3: Fix OTP Issues

If OTP is not received for login or password reset:
1. Check DND settings — SMS `STOP` to 1909 to disable
2. Wait 2-3 minutes before requesting another OTP
3. Check if OTP was sent to your **email** instead — look in the spam/junk folder
4. Ensure your registered mobile number is active and has signal
5. Try the **email OTP** option if mobile OTP consistently fails

### Step 4: Fix Captcha Errors

1. Refresh the captcha by clicking the **reload icon** next to it
2. Look carefully — captcha is case-sensitive
3. Zoom in on the captcha image if characters are unclear
4. Use a **desktop browser** — captcha is easier to read on larger screens
5. Clear browser cache and try again
6. Some IRCTC versions show audio captcha — use that if visual captcha is unreadable

### Step 5: Clear Browser Data

1. Clear cookies and cache for irctc.co.in specifically
2. In Chrome: **Settings → Privacy → Clear Browsing Data**
3. Select **Cookies** and **Cached images/files**
4. Try in **Incognito mode**
5. Disable any browser extensions (especially ad blockers)

### Step 6: Recover Forgotten User ID

If you do not remember your User ID:
1. Click **"Forgot Account"** on the login page
2. Select **"Forgot User ID"**
3. Enter your registered **email** or **mobile number**
4. Verify with OTP
5. Your User ID will be displayed or sent to your registered email

### Step 7: Fix Registration Issues

If trying to register but getting "already registered":
1. Your mobile number or email is already linked to an existing account
2. Try recovering the existing account using "Forgot Account"
3. If you want a new account, you need a different mobile number or email
4. IRCTC allows only one account per mobile number and one per email

## Alternative Methods and Workarounds

### Use the IRCTC Rail Connect App

1. Download **IRCTC Rail Connect** from Google Play or App Store
2. The app often has better performance than the website during peak times
3. Login process is the same — User ID and password
4. The app supports biometric login (fingerprint/face) once set up

### Book Through Third-Party Apps

Licensed apps that connect to IRCTC for ticket booking:
- **Paytm Travel**
- **MakeMyTrip**
- **Ixigo Trains**
- **ConfirmTkt**

You can book IRCTC tickets through these apps using your IRCTC credentials. They may have better server stability during peak hours.

### Book at Railway Counter

If online booking fails:
1. Visit any **computerized railway reservation counter**
2. Carry a valid ID proof
3. You can book reserved tickets up to the same advance period as online
4. Tatkal tickets can also be booked at the counter starting at the same time

### Use IRCTC iMudra Wallet

If payment failures are causing the issue:
1. Pre-load your IRCTC iMudra wallet
2. Use the wallet balance for instant payment during booking
3. This avoids payment gateway delays during peak Tatkal hours

## Prevention Tips

1. **Save your User ID securely** — write down or save your IRCTC User ID in a password manager
2. **Use a strong but memorable password** — avoid changing it too frequently
3. **Set up the app with biometric login** — fingerprint/face login on the IRCTC app avoids password issues
4. **Keep contact details updated** — ensure your registered mobile and email are current for OTP delivery
5. **Pre-load iMudra wallet** — for frequent travellers, this speeds up booking and avoids payment failures
6. **Bookmark the correct URL** — always use [irctc.co.in](https://www.irctc.co.in/) to avoid phishing sites

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I unlock my IRCTC account?

IRCTC accounts are automatically locked after 5 consecutive failed login attempts. The lock expires automatically after 30 minutes. Alternatively, use the "Forgot Password" option to reset your password, which also unlocks the account immediately.

### Can I have two IRCTC accounts?

IRCTC allows only one account per mobile number and one account per email address. If you need a new account, you must use a different mobile number and email. It is recommended to recover your existing account instead of creating a new one.

### Why does IRCTC captcha keep failing?

IRCTC captcha is case-sensitive and the images can be difficult to read. Refresh the captcha for a clearer image, zoom in on your screen, and ensure you are matching the exact case. Using a desktop browser with a larger screen makes captcha easier to read. Clear your browser cache if captcha images are not loading properly.

### What should I do if IRCTC is slow during Tatkal booking?

Log in 10-15 minutes before Tatkal opens (10 AM for AC, 11 AM for non-AC). Keep the passenger details and payment method ready. Use the IRCTC Rail Connect app or a third-party app like Paytm or Ixigo for better speed. Pre-load your iMudra wallet to skip payment gateway delays.

### Is it safe to log into IRCTC from third-party apps?

Yes, if the app is an authorized IRCTC agent. Apps like Paytm, MakeMyTrip, and Ixigo are officially authorized to sell IRCTC tickets. Always verify the app is genuine by checking the IRCTC authorized agents list. Never share your IRCTC credentials with unknown websites.
