# PM Kisan eKYC Aadhaar OTP Not Coming? How to Fix Verification Failed Error

Millions of farmers across India face the **"OTP not received"** and **"eKYC verification failed"** error while trying to complete PM Kisan eKYC. Without completing eKYC, your **₹2000 instalment gets stuck**. This guide shows you exactly how to fix the issue and complete eKYC successfully.

## Common Error Messages

- "OTP not received. Please try again"
- "eKYC verification failed"
- "Aadhaar authentication failed"
- "Aadhaar number not found"
- "Technical error. Service temporarily unavailable"
- "OTP expired. Please request new OTP"
- "Biometric authentication failed"

## Why PM Kisan eKYC OTP Fails

### 1. Wrong Mobile Number
The OTP goes to the **mobile number registered with Aadhaar** — NOT the number registered with PM Kisan. If these are different, you won't receive the OTP on your expected phone.

### 2. Aadhaar Mobile Number Inactive
If you've changed your SIM or your Aadhaar-linked number is no longer active, OTP cannot be delivered.

### 3. UIDAI Server Overload
During PM Kisan instalment periods, millions try eKYC simultaneously, causing UIDAI servers to slow down or fail.

### 4. Aadhaar Number Mismatch
The Aadhaar number in your PM Kisan registration may be incorrect or have a typo.

### 5. Network Issues
Poor mobile network in rural areas can prevent OTP delivery.

## How to Fix and Complete eKYC

### Method 1: Fix OTP Issues and Retry Online

#### Step 1: Verify Which Mobile Gets OTP
- OTP is sent to your **Aadhaar-registered mobile** number
- To check which number is linked to Aadhaar: visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in) → "Verify Mobile/Email"
- If the number is different from your current phone, you need to **update Aadhaar mobile** at an enrolment centre

#### Step 2: Try eKYC During Off-Peak Hours
1. Go to [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in)
2. Click **"eKYC"** option on homepage
3. Enter your **Aadhaar number**
4. Click **"Get OTP"**
5. **Best times to try**: Early morning (6-8 AM) or late night (9-11 PM)
6. Avoid trying during **10 AM - 4 PM** — heaviest server load

#### Step 3: Wait and Retry Properly
- After clicking "Get OTP", **wait at least 3-5 minutes**
- Don't click "Resend OTP" immediately — it may block your request
- Check all **SMS inboxes** — OTP sometimes comes from different sender IDs
- If OTP doesn't come in 5 minutes, click **"Resend OTP"**
- After 3 failed attempts, **wait 24 hours** before trying again

#### Step 4: Ensure Good Connectivity
- Use **Wi-Fi** instead of mobile data if possible
- If on mobile data, move to an area with **strong network signal**
- Try using a **different browser** (Chrome recommended)
- Clear browser cache before trying

### Method 2: Complete eKYC at CSC (Common Service Centre)

This is the **most reliable method**, especially in rural areas:

1. Find your nearest CSC at [locator.csccloud.in](https://locator.csccloud.in)
2. Visit with:
   - **Aadhaar card** (original)
   - **PM Kisan registration details** (Aadhaar number linked to PM Kisan)
3. The CSC operator will use **biometric (fingerprint) authentication**
4. No OTP needed — your fingerprint verifies your identity
5. eKYC completion is **instant** at CSC
6. **Fee**: ₹0 (free) — do not pay more than nominal charges

### Method 3: eKYC via PM Kisan App

1. Download **PM Kisan Mobile App** from Google Play
2. Open the app and select **"eKYC"**
3. Enter your Aadhaar number
4. Select **OTP method** and try
5. If OTP fails on app too, select **"Face Authentication"** (if available)
6. The app sometimes works better than the website during peak hours

## Fixing Specific Errors

### "Aadhaar Number Not Found"
Your Aadhaar in PM Kisan records is wrong:
1. Visit your **local agriculture department office** or **Lekhpal/Patwari**
2. Request correction of Aadhaar number in PM Kisan records
3. Carry your **Aadhaar card** and **PM Kisan registration slip**
4. After correction, wait **48 hours** and retry eKYC

### "Aadhaar Authentication Failed"
1. Check if your **Aadhaar is locked** — unlock at [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in)
2. Ensure your **biometrics are not locked** (if using fingerprint at CSC)
3. If your Aadhaar details (name/DOB) have errors, get them corrected first

### "Technical Error / Service Unavailable"
1. This is a **server-side issue** — not your fault
2. Try again after **2-3 hours**
3. Try on a **different day**
4. Early morning attempts have the highest success rate
5. Check [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in) — sometimes the portal displays maintenance notices

### "OTP Expired"
- OTP is valid for **10 minutes** only
- Enter OTP **immediately** after receiving
- Don't navigate away from the page while waiting for OTP
- If expired, click **"Resend OTP"** and enter quickly

## How to Update Mobile Number in Aadhaar (For OTP Issues)

If your Aadhaar mobile is inactive or changed:

1. Visit nearest **Aadhaar Enrolment Centre** ([appointments.uidai.gov.in](https://appointments.uidai.gov.in/))
2. Carry **Aadhaar card** (original)
3. Request **mobile number update**
4. Biometric verification (fingerprint) will be done
5. **No fee** for mobile number update
6. New number is active within **3-7 working days**
7. After mobile update, retry PM Kisan eKYC

## How to Check eKYC Status

1. Go to [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in)
2. Click **"Beneficiary Status"**
3. Enter your **Aadhaar number** or **mobile number**
4. Check if eKYC status shows **"Completed"** or **"Pending"**
5. If completed, your next instalment will be processed

## Important Deadlines

- eKYC must be completed **before each instalment release**
- Missing eKYC deadline means your **instalment is held** (not lost — released after eKYC)
- Check [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in) for current instalment deadlines

## Summary

PM Kisan eKYC OTP failure is usually because the OTP goes to your Aadhaar-registered mobile, not your current phone. The fastest fix is visiting a **CSC centre** for biometric (fingerprint) eKYC — it's free and doesn't need OTP. If you want to fix OTP, update your Aadhaar mobile number at an enrolment centre. Try the online method during early morning or late night for best results.
