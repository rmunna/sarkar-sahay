# PM Ujjwala Yojana 2.0: Free LPG Connection, Eligibility, and How to Apply

Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY) is a flagship scheme launched in May 2016 to provide **free LPG (cooking gas) connections** to women from Below Poverty Line (BPL) and economically weaker households. The scheme aims to replace unclean cooking fuels like firewood, coal, and cow-dung with clean LPG fuel, improving health and quality of life.

## What is PM Ujjwala Yojana?

Under PMUY, the government provides a **free LPG connection** with financial assistance of ₹1,600 per connection to eligible households. The scheme was initially targeted at providing 5 crore connections and was later expanded under **Ujjwala 2.0** in August 2021 to cover 1 crore additional beneficiaries, including migrant workers.

As of 2024, over **10 crore LPG connections** have been provided under the scheme.

---

## Who is Eligible?

- **Women only** — the LPG connection is issued in the name of an adult woman of the household
- **Age:** 18 years or above
- **BPL household** — belonging to a Below Poverty Line family (SECC-2011 data)
- **No existing LPG connection** — the household should not already have an LPG connection
- **Indian citizen**

### Additional Categories (Under Ujjwala 2.0)
- SC/ST households
- Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) beneficiaries
- Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) beneficiaries
- Forest dwellers
- Most Backward Classes (MBC)
- Tea garden and ex-tea garden tribes
- People in islands and river islands
- **Migrant workers' families** (new under Ujjwala 2.0 — self-declaration of address is accepted)

---

## What You Get Under PMUY

| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Financial assistance | ₹1,600 for LPG connection |
| Free first refill | First LPG cylinder provided free |
| Free hot plate/stove | Provided along with connection |
| Subsidized refills | LPG subsidy on subsequent refills (as per government policy) |
| EMI option | Cost of stove and first refill can be recovered in EMIs from future subsidy |

## Documents Required

### Mandatory
- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) of the applicant (woman)
- BPL card / [Ration card](/guide/ration-card-apply-online) / SECC list inclusion
- Bank account details (Aadhaar-linked bank account in woman's name)
- Passport-size photograph

### For Ujjwala 2.0 (Migrant Workers)
- Aadhaar card
- Self-declaration of address (if proof of address is not available)
- Bank account details

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Online Application

1. **Visit PMUY website** — Go to [pmuy.gov.in](https://www.pmuy.gov.in/)
2. **Click Apply** — Select "Apply for New Ujjwala 2.0 Connection"
3. **Choose distributor** — Select your preferred LPG distributor (Indian Oil, Bharat Petroleum, or Hindustan Petroleum)
4. **Fill form** — Enter personal details, Aadhaar number, address, bank account details
5. **Upload documents** — Upload Aadhaar, photograph, and other required documents
6. **Submit** — Review and submit the application
7. **Track status** — Use the reference number to track your application

### Offline Application

1. **Visit nearest LPG distributor** — Go to your nearest Indian Oil, BPCL, or HPCL distributor
2. **Collect form** — Ask for the Ujjwala Yojana application form (Form KYC)
3. **Fill form** — Complete all details carefully
4. **Attach documents** — Attach copies of Aadhaar, BPL/ration card, bank passbook, and photograph
5. **Submit** — Submit the form at the distributor's office
6. **Verification** — The distributor will verify your details and eligibility
7. **Connection delivery** — After approval, the LPG connection, cylinder, regulator, and stove are delivered to your home

---

## Processing Time

- **Application to verification:** 7-15 days
- **Connection delivery:** 15-30 days after approval
- **Total time:** 3-6 weeks

---

## Important Tips

1. **Apply in woman's name** — The LPG connection must be in the name of an adult woman of the household
2. **Link Aadhaar to bank** — Ensure your bank account is linked to Aadhaar for receiving LPG subsidy
3. **Choose nearby distributor** — Select a distributor close to your home for convenient refills
4. **Check SECC list** — Verify if your household is listed in the SECC-2011 database. If not, check if you qualify under other categories (SC/ST, AAY, PMAY beneficiary)
5. **Beware of fraud** — PMUY registration is free. Do not pay any agent or middleman for getting the connection

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. Is PM Ujjwala Yojana completely free?
The connection (₹1,600 subsidy), first refill cylinder, and stove are provided free or on EMI basis (adjusted from future subsidies). Subsequent refills are at subsidized rates.

### Q2. Can men apply for Ujjwala Yojana?
No, the connection is issued only in the name of an adult woman (18+ years) of the household.

### Q3. I already have an LPG connection. Can I apply?
No, if your household already has an LPG connection from any company, you are not eligible for PMUY.

### Q4. I am a migrant worker. Can I apply?
Yes, under Ujjwala 2.0, migrant workers can apply with just an Aadhaar card and a self-declaration of address. No address proof document is needed.

### Q5. How do I get my LPG subsidy?
LPG subsidy is credited directly to your Aadhaar-linked bank account (DBTL — Direct Benefit Transfer for LPG). Ensure your bank account is linked to Aadhaar.

### Q6. Can I choose my LPG company?
Yes, you can choose between Indian Oil (Indane), Bharat Petroleum (Bharatgas), or Hindustan Petroleum (HP Gas) based on distributor availability in your area.

### Q7. What if my application is rejected?
If rejected, check the reason (usually — existing connection, name not in SECC/BPL list, or incomplete documents). Correct the issue and reapply at the distributor.
