# How to Become Aadhaar Centre Operator via CSC (2026)

Aadhaar enrollment and update services are among the most in-demand services at Common Service Centres (CSCs). To operate an Aadhaar centre, you need to become a certified Aadhaar Enrollment Operator by clearing the NSEIT/UIDAI certification exam. This guide explains the complete process to become an Aadhaar operator through CSC in 2026.

## What is an Aadhaar Enrollment Operator?

An Aadhaar Enrollment Operator is a UIDAI-certified person who can:
- Enroll new residents for Aadhaar
- Process Aadhaar updates (demographic and biometric)
- Operate Aadhaar enrollment machines at CSCs and authorized centres

Operators work under an Aadhaar Enrollment Agency (EA) or through CSC's Digital Seva network.

## Eligibility Criteria

- **Age:** Minimum 18 years
- **Education:** Minimum 12th pass (senior secondary)
- **CSC VLE Status:** Must be a registered CSC VLE (or working under one)
- **Basic Computer Skills:** Familiarity with computer operations required
- **Clean Record:** No criminal background
- **Infrastructure:** Enrollment station with biometric devices, iris scanner, camera, and printer

## Step-by-Step Process to Become Aadhaar Operator via CSC

### Step 1: Ensure You're a Registered CSC VLE

You must have an active CSC VLE registration. If not, apply first — see our [CSC registration guide](/guide/csc-registration-apply-online).

### Step 2: Register for NSEIT Certification Exam

NSEIT (formerly NSDL e-Governance Infrastructure) conducts the Aadhaar Operator/Supervisor certification exam on behalf of UIDAI:

1. Visit the NSEIT testing portal or access through Digital Seva portal
2. Register for the **"UIDAI Aadhaar Operator/Supervisor Certification Test"**
3. Fill in personal details — name, Aadhaar number, mobile, email
4. Select your preferred exam centre and date
5. Pay the examination fee

### Step 3: Pay Examination Fee

| Exam Type | Fee (Approx.) |
|-----------|---------------|
| Aadhaar Operator Certification | ₹500 + GST |
| Aadhaar Supervisor Certification | ₹500 + GST |
| Re-examination (if failed) | ₹500 + GST |

Payment can be made via net banking, debit/credit card, or UPI.

### Step 4: Prepare for the Exam

The NSEIT certification exam covers:

- **Aadhaar Act and Regulations** — legal framework, resident rights, data protection
- **Enrollment Process** — step-by-step enrollment procedure, exceptions
- **Biometric Capture** — fingerprint, iris, and photograph quality standards
- **Update Process** — demographic and biometric update procedures
- **Technical Operations** — enrollment client software, troubleshooting
- **Quality Standards** — UIDAI quality benchmarks, audit procedures

**Study Material:**
- Download the official UIDAI study material from [https://uidai.gov.in](https://uidai.gov.in)
- Study the Aadhaar Enrollment & Update Process document
- Review UIDAI circulars and procedural guidelines

### Step 5: Take the Certification Exam

- **Format:** Online multiple-choice questions (MCQ)
- **Questions:** 75 questions
- **Duration:** 90 minutes
- **Passing Score:** Minimum 50% (approximately 38 out of 75)
- **Language:** Available in English and Hindi
- **Exam Centre:** NSEIT authorized centres across India

### Step 6: Receive Certification

After passing the exam:
1. Download your **Aadhaar Operator Certificate** from the NSEIT portal
2. The certificate is valid for **3 years** from the date of issue
3. You'll receive an Operator ID from UIDAI

### Step 7: Activate Aadhaar Services on CSC Portal

1. Log in to [https://digitalseva.csc.gov.in](https://digitalseva.csc.gov.in)
2. Navigate to **Aadhaar Services** section
3. Upload your NSEIT certification
4. Submit operator activation request
5. After verification (typically 7–15 days), Aadhaar enrollment services will be activated

### Step 8: Set Up Enrollment Station

Ensure your centre has the required equipment:

| Equipment | Specification |
|-----------|--------------|
| Computer/Laptop | Windows OS, 4GB+ RAM |
| Fingerprint Scanner | UIDAI-certified (e.g., Cogent, Morpho) |
| Iris Scanner | UIDAI-certified dual iris scanner |
| Camera | Web camera for photograph capture |
| Printer | For acknowledgment slips |
| GPS Device | For location capture |
| Internet | Broadband with minimum 2 Mbps |

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar card (operator's own)
- 12th pass certificate/marksheet
- NSEIT certification (after passing)
- CSC VLE ID proof
- Passport-size photograph
- PAN card
- Bank account details

## Fees Charged to Residents

UIDAI has fixed the rates that can be charged to residents:

| Service | Maximum Fee |
|---------|-----------|
| New Aadhaar Enrollment | Free |
| Mandatory Biometric Update (children at 5 & 15 years) | Free |
| Demographic Update (name, address, DOB, etc.) | ₹50 |
| Biometric Update (fingerprint/iris) | ₹100 |

> **Note:** Charging more than the prescribed fees is a violation of UIDAI regulations and can lead to de-activation.

## Earning Potential

As an Aadhaar operator through CSC, you can earn:
- Commission per enrollment/update transaction (as per CSC commission structure)
- Additional revenue from other CSC services offered at your centre
- Active Aadhaar centres in high-demand areas can process 20–50 transactions daily

## Important Tips

1. **Renew certification before expiry** — you must re-certify every 3 years
2. **Follow UIDAI guidelines strictly** — any violation can lead to blacklisting
3. **Never share operator credentials** — each operator must use their own login
4. **Maintain enrollment quality** — UIDAI audits biometric quality regularly
5. **Stay updated with UIDAI circulars** — processes change frequently

## FAQs

### 1. What is the NSEIT exam for Aadhaar operator?
NSEIT conducts the UIDAI-authorized certification exam for Aadhaar operators and supervisors. You must pass this exam to legally operate an Aadhaar enrollment centre.

### 2. What is the passing score for the Aadhaar operator exam?
You need to score at least 50% (approximately 38 out of 75 questions) to pass the certification exam.

### 3. How much does the Aadhaar operator certification cost?
The exam fee is approximately ₹500 plus GST. There's no separate registration fee for the certification.

### 4. Can I become an Aadhaar operator without being a CSC VLE?
You can get NSEIT certified independently, but to operate through CSC, you need an active VLE registration. You can also work as an operator under an existing VLE.

### 5. How long is the Aadhaar operator certificate valid?
The certification is valid for 3 years. You must pass the re-certification exam before expiry to continue operating.

### 6. What happens if I fail the NSEIT exam?
You can re-appear for the exam by paying the fee again (₹500 + GST). There's no limit on the number of attempts.

### 7. How much can an Aadhaar operator earn per month?
Earnings depend on location and volume. Active operators processing 20–40 transactions daily can earn ₹15,000–30,000 per month from Aadhaar services alone, plus earnings from other CSC services.
