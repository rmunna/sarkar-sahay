# PAN Card Application Status Not Updating — How to Fix

Applied for a new PAN card or requested corrections, but the tracking status isn't updating? Whether you applied through **NSDL (now Protean)** or **UTIITSL**, your application may appear stuck at "Under Process" or the tracking page may show no results at all.

This guide covers every reason your PAN status may not update and how to fix it step by step.

---

## Why PAN Card Status Is Not Updating

| Reason | Explanation |
|---|---|
| Application just submitted | Status takes **24–72 hours** to appear on tracking portals after submission |
| Documents under verification | Physical documents sent by post haven't reached the processing centre yet |
| Stuck at "Under Process" | Documents received but verification is pending — can take 15–20 working days |
| Incorrect acknowledgement number | Wrong number entered on the tracking page |
| Portal maintenance | NSDL/UTIITSL portals go offline for scheduled maintenance |
| Documents rejected | Application returned due to incomplete or mismatched documents |

---

## How to Check PAN Card Application Status Online

### If You Applied Through NSDL (Protean)

1. Visit [NSDL PAN Status Track](https://tin.tin-nsdl.com/pantan/StatusTrack.html)
2. Select **Application Type** → "PAN - New / Change Request"
3. Enter your **15-digit acknowledgement number** (starts with a letter)
4. Enter the captcha and click **Submit**

### If You Applied Through UTIITSL

1. Visit [UTIITSL PAN Status](https://www.trackpan.utiitsl.com/PANONLINE/)
2. Select **PAN Card Status** → choose your application type
3. Enter your **coupon number** (printed on acknowledgement receipt)
4. Click **Submit** to view status

## How to Track PAN Card Status via SMS

You can check your NSDL PAN application status by SMS:

**Send SMS to 57575:**
```
NSDLPAN <15-digit acknowledgement number>
```

**Example:**
```
NSDLPAN AAABR1234567890
```

You will receive the current status on your registered mobile number.

> **Note:** SMS tracking works only for NSDL applications, not UTIITSL.

## Fix: PAN Status Showing "No Record Found"

If the tracking portal shows no record:

1. **Wait 48–72 hours** — Status is not available immediately after submission
2. **Double-check your acknowledgement/coupon number** — Even one wrong digit will show "no record"
3. **Verify the portal** — NSDL applications cannot be tracked on UTIITSL portal and vice versa
4. **Check email** — The acknowledgement number is sent to your registered email after successful submission
5. **Payment verification** — If payment failed, the application was not submitted. Check your bank statement

---

## Fix: PAN Application Stuck at "Under Process"

"Under Process" means the processing centre has received your application but verification is still ongoing.

### Normal Processing Timeline

| Application Type | Expected Time |
|---|---|
| Online (e-KYC / Aadhaar-based) | 7–10 working days |
| Physical documents (by post) | 15–25 working days |
| Corrections / reprint | 10–15 working days |

### What to Do If It's Been Too Long

1. **Wait at least 20 working days** before raising a concern
2. **Check for email/SMS from NSDL/UTIITSL** — They may have requested additional documents
3. **Call the helpline** (details below) and quote your acknowledgement number
4. **Raise a grievance online** on the NSDL or UTIITSL portal

---

## Fix: Lost Coupon Number or Acknowledgement Number

If you've lost your acknowledgement number:

### For NSDL Applications
1. **Check your email** — Search for "NSDL" or "Protean" in your inbox (including spam folder)
2. **Check SMS** — The number is sent to your registered mobile
3. **Contact NSDL helpline** at **020-27218080** — Provide your name, date of birth, and application date

### For UTIITSL Applications
1. **Check email** for the coupon number confirmation
2. **Visit the nearest UTIITSL PAN centre** with your ID proof — they can look up your application
3. **Call UTIITSL helpline** at **033-40802999**

> **Tip:** If you applied at a PAN centre (offline), the printed acknowledgement slip has your coupon/acknowledgement number.

---

## Fix: PAN Card Dispatched But Not Received

If status shows **"PAN card dispatched"** but you haven't received it:

1. **Check the dispatch date** on the tracking portal — delivery takes **5–15 days** via Indian Post / courier
2. **Track using Speed Post number** — If dispatched via Speed Post, track on [India Post](https://www.indiapost.gov.in/_layouts/15/dop.portal.tracking/trackconsignment.aspx)
3. **Verify your address** — Ensure the address on the application is correct and complete with PIN code
4. **Wait 30 days from dispatch** before applying for reprint

### If PAN Card Is Not Received After 30 Days

You can apply for a **PAN card reprint** (not a new application):

- **NSDL:** Apply at [Protean PAN Reprint](https://www.onlineservices.nsdl.com/paam/endUserRegisterContact.html) — Fee: ₹50 (domestic)
- **UTIITSL:** Apply at [UTIITSL PAN Portal](https://www.pan.utiitsl.com/) — Fee: ₹50 (domestic)

> For a detailed guide, see: [PAN Card Not Received by Post — How to Fix](/guide/pan-card-not-received-by-post-fix)

## Reprint vs New PAN Application — Which Do You Need?

| Scenario | What to Apply For |
|---|---|
| PAN allotted but card not received | **Reprint** (₹50) |
| PAN allotted but details are wrong | **Correction/Change** (₹50) |
| Never had a PAN before | **New PAN application** (₹107) |
| PAN card lost or damaged | **Reprint** (₹50) |

> **Important:** Do not apply for a new PAN if you already have one. Having two PANs is illegal under the Income Tax Act and attracts a penalty of **₹10,000** under Section 272B.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does it take for PAN status to update after applying?
Online applications (e-KYC based) update within **48 hours**. Physical applications take **5–7 days** after documents reach the processing centre.

### Can I track PAN status by name and date of birth?
No. Both NSDL and UTIITSL require your **acknowledgement number** or **coupon number** to track status. You cannot search by name alone.

### My PAN status shows "Documents returned" — what does this mean?
Your documents were rejected — usually due to mismatch in name/DOB, unclear copies, or missing documents. You need to **re-submit** the documents or apply again.

### I applied for PAN via Aadhaar e-KYC but status is not updating?
Aadhaar-based PAN applications are usually processed within **48 hours**. If status doesn't update after 3 days, check if your Aadhaar mobile number was active for OTP verification. Contact the NSDL helpline.

### Can I check someone else's PAN status?
Yes, if you have their acknowledgement number. The tracking portal does not require login — just the acknowledgement/coupon number.
