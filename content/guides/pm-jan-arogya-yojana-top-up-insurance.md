## PM-JAY and State Top-Up Health Insurance

PM Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY) under **Ayushman Bharat** provides **₹5 lakh per family per year** health insurance for BPL and socially vulnerable families. Many states have expanded this coverage with their own **top-up schemes** — increasing total coverage to ₹7 lakh, ₹10 lakh, or even ₹25 lakh.

This guide explains:
1. How PM-JAY works and who is eligible
2. State top-up schemes that go beyond ₹5 lakh
3. How to use your Ayushman card at empanelled hospitals

---

## PM-JAY Core Benefits

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Annual coverage | ₹5,00,000 per family per year |
| Cashless treatment | Yes — no payment needed at empanelled hospitals |
| Pre-existing conditions | Covered from Day 1 |
| Family size limit | None — entire family is covered under one card |
| Portability | Use at any empanelled hospital in any state |
| Covered procedures | 1,900+ medical packages including surgery |

## Who is Eligible for PM-JAY?

Eligibility is based on **SECC 2011 (Socio-Economic Caste Census)** data. You are eligible if:

- Your family is listed in SECC 2011 as a deprived rural or urban household
- You belong to one of 8 occupational categories (rickshaw pullers, domestic workers, construction workers, etc.)
- You are covered under a state government's integrated scheme (many states have expanded beyond SECC)

**Check your eligibility immediately:** Visit [beneficiary.nha.gov.in](https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in) or call **14555** (toll-free).

## How to Get Your Ayushman Card

### Option 1 — Ayushman App / Portal

1. Download **Ayushman App** from Play Store / App Store
2. Enter your mobile number (registered with Aadhaar)
3. Enter OTP
4. If eligible, download your **Ayushman Card (e-card)**

### Option 2 — Common Service Centre (CSC)

1. Visit nearest CSC/Jan Seva Kendra
2. Provide Aadhaar number and mobile number
3. CSC operator verifies eligibility and prints the Ayushman card (free of cost)

### Option 3 — At the Hospital

At any PM-JAY empanelled hospital, the **PMJAY Help Desk** can verify your eligibility and issue an e-card on the spot.

---

## How to Use PM-JAY at a Hospital

1. Visit any **PM-JAY empanelled hospital** (look for the green Ayushman Bharat board)
2. Go to the **PM-JAY Help Desk / Arogya Mitra**
3. Show your **Aadhaar + Ayushman card** (e-card on phone is fine)
4. The Arogya Mitra verifies your eligibility in real-time via the PMJAY portal
5. For eligible procedures, you are **admitted cashless** — no money required
6. Hospital sends a **pre-authorization request** → NHA approves → treatment proceeds → hospital bills NHA directly

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. I am eligible for PM-JAY but my name is not showing on the portal. What do I do?**

Call the PM-JAY helpline: **14555** (toll-free). You can also visit your state health agency office or the CSC with your Aadhaar to verify and link your record.

**2. My card shows ₹5 lakh. Is there a state top-up I can use?**

Check with your state health department. In Rajasthan (Chiranjeevi), all families can register for ₹25 lakh coverage. In Chhattisgarh, state-linked coverage goes up to ₹20 lakh for serious conditions. These are handled separately from PM-JAY.

**3. Can I use my PM-JAY card in another state?**

Yes. PM-JAY is **fully portable** across India. You can get cashless treatment at any empanelled hospital in any state using your PM-JAY card.

**4. Which diseases are NOT covered under PM-JAY?**

PM-JAY does not cover: outpatient (OPD) expenses, cosmetic surgery, fertility treatments, organ transplant costs for the donor, and some rare conditions. Check the full package list at [pmjay.gov.in](https://pmjay.gov.in).

**5. I already had surgery this year. Can I use the ₹5 lakh again?**

The ₹5 lakh limit is per family per year. If your total family's claims in the year exceed ₹5 lakh, additional expenses are out of pocket unless your state has a top-up scheme.
