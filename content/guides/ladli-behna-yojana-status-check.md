# Ladli Behna Yojana Status Check — Payment Status, e-KYC & Certificate Download

The Mukhyamantri Ladli Behna Yojana is Madhya Pradesh's flagship women empowerment scheme, providing **₹1,500 per month** directly to eligible women's bank accounts via DBT. Whether you want to check your payment status, verify your application, complete e-KYC, or download your certificate — this guide covers everything step by step.

> **Quick link:** Check your status at [cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in](https://cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in/)

## Scheme Overview at a Glance

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| **Scheme Name** | Mukhyamantri Ladli Behna Yojana |
| **State** | Madhya Pradesh |
| **Monthly Amount** | ₹1,500 (increased from ₹1,250) |
| **Beneficiaries** | Women aged 21–60 years |
| **Payment Mode** | Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to bank account |
| **Official Portal** | [cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in](https://cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in/) |
| **Required ID** | Samagra ID + Aadhaar |

## How to Check Ladli Behna Yojana Payment Status

Follow these steps to check whether your monthly installment has been credited:

### Step 1: Visit the Official Portal

Go to [cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in](https://cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in/) on your mobile or computer.

### Step 2: Click on "आवेदन एवं भुगतान की स्थिति"

On the home page, find and click the **"Application & Payment Status"** (आवेदन एवं भुगतान की स्थिति) option in the menu.

### Step 3: Enter Your Details

On the new page, enter either your:
- **Ladli Behna Application Number** (आवेदन क्रमांक), OR
- **Samagra Member ID** (सदस्य समग्र आईडी)

Then enter the **captcha code** and click **"OTP भेजें"** (Send OTP).

### Step 4: Verify OTP

An OTP will be sent to your registered mobile number. Enter it in the box and click **"खोजें"** (Search).

### Step 5: View Payment Status

Your dashboard will open showing:
- **Applicant name** and Samagra ID
- **Bank account number**
- **Payment date** for each installment
- **Amount** (₹1,500)
- **Payment status** — Success, Pending, or Failed

Click on the **"भुगतान स्थिति"** (Payment Status) tab to see a detailed history of all installments received.

## How to Check Application Status

If you've recently applied and want to track your application:

1. Visit [cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in](https://cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in/)
2. Click **"आवेदन एवं भुगतान की स्थिति"**
3. Enter your **Application Number or Samagra ID** + captcha
4. Complete OTP verification
5. Your application dashboard will show the current status — **Approved, Pending, or Rejected** — along with any remarks

If your application shows **"Pending"**, it may still be under verification at the district or block level. If it shows **"Rejected"**, check the reason mentioned and re-apply after correcting the issue.

## How to Complete e-KYC for Ladli Behna Yojana

e-KYC (electronic Know Your Customer) is **mandatory** to continue receiving payments. Without completing e-KYC, your installments may be paused.

### Method 1: Through Samagra Portal (Online)

1. Visit [samagra.gov.in](https://samagra.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"e-KYC करें"** option
3. Enter your **Samagra Member ID**
4. Choose **Aadhaar-based OTP verification**
5. Enter your **Aadhaar number** linked to the same mobile number
6. An OTP will be sent to your Aadhaar-registered mobile — enter it
7. On successful verification, your e-KYC status will show as **"Verified"**

### Method 2: Biometric e-KYC at CSC/Camp

If your mobile number is not linked with Aadhaar or OTP verification fails:

1. Visit your nearest **CSC (Common Service Centre)**, Gram Panchayat, or Ward Office
2. Carry your **Aadhaar card** and **Samagra ID**
3. The operator will perform **biometric authentication** (fingerprint)
4. You'll receive a confirmation SMS once e-KYC is complete

### Important e-KYC Tips

- Ensure your **Aadhaar is linked to an active mobile number** — this is essential for OTP-based e-KYC
- If your Aadhaar details (name, date of birth) don't match your Samagra profile, **update Samagra first**
- e-KYC must be completed **for each family member separately** on the Samagra portal
- Check your e-KYC status anytime on the Samagra portal by entering your member ID

For detailed Aadhaar linking steps, see our [Aadhaar Linking Guide](/guide/aadhaar-linking-guide).

## How to Download Ladli Behna Yojana Certificate

After your application is approved, you can download your Ladli Behna Yojana certificate:

1. Go to [cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in](https://cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"आवेदन एवं भुगतान की स्थिति"**
3. Log in using your **Application Number/Samagra ID** and OTP
4. On your dashboard, look for the **"प्रमाण पत्र डाउनलोड करें"** (Download Certificate) option
5. Click to download — the certificate will be in PDF format
6. Save or print it for your records

> **Note:** The certificate download option is only available for **approved and active beneficiaries**. If your application is still pending or rejected, this option won't appear.

## Latest Installment Details

The Ladli Behna Yojana installment amount has been progressively increased:

| Phase | Monthly Amount |
|---|---|
| Initial launch (March 2023) | ₹1,000 |
| Increased (mid-2024) | ₹1,250 |
| Current (2025 onwards) | ₹1,500 |

- Installments are credited on the **10th of every month** (may vary by a few days)
- Payment is made directly to the **Aadhaar-linked bank account** via DBT
- The government has announced plans to eventually increase the amount to **₹3,000/month**

## How to Update Aadhaar or Bank Details

If your bank account or Aadhaar details have changed, update them promptly to avoid payment failures.

### Update Bank Account Details

1. Visit [cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in](https://cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in/)
2. Log in via **"आवेदन एवं भुगतान की स्थिति"** with OTP
3. On the dashboard, click **"बैंक खाता अपडेट करें"** (Update Bank Account)
4. Enter your **new bank account number**, **IFSC code**, and **bank name**
5. Submit — the update will be verified and reflected in the next installment cycle

### Update Aadhaar Details

If your name or other details on Aadhaar don't match:

1. First update your Aadhaar at [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/) or visit an Aadhaar Enrolment Centre
2. Once Aadhaar is updated, redo your **e-KYC on the Samagra portal**
3. The updated details will automatically sync with the Ladli Behna database

### Ensure NPCI/Aadhaar-Bank Mapping

For DBT payments to work, your Aadhaar must be mapped to your bank account via NPCI:

1. Visit your bank branch with Aadhaar card
2. Request **Aadhaar seeding** (linking Aadhaar to your bank account)
3. Ask the bank to enable **NPCI mapping** so that DBT transfers reach your account
4. You can check mapping status at [npci.org.in](https://npci.org.in/)

## How to Check Your Name in the Beneficiary List

1. Visit [cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in](https://cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"अंतिम सूची"** (Final List) from the menu
3. Enter your **registered mobile number** and captcha, then get OTP
4. Choose **"क्षेत्र वार"** (Area Wise) to see your area's full list, or **"व्यक्ति विशेष वार"** (Individual) to search by Samagra ID
5. For area-wise search: select your **District → Local Body → Gram Panchayat/Zone → Village/Ward**
6. Click **"अंतिम सूची देखें"** to view the list

## Common Problems and Solutions

### Payment Not Received

- **Check e-KYC status** — incomplete e-KYC is the #1 reason for payment hold
- **Verify Aadhaar-bank linking** — ensure NPCI mapping is active
- **Check for incorrect bank details** — wrong account number or IFSC causes failure
- **Contact helpline** if payment shows "Failed" on the portal

### e-KYC Pending or Failed

- Ensure your **Aadhaar mobile number is active** — OTP won't work otherwise
- Try **biometric verification** at a CSC centre if OTP fails
- **Name mismatch** between Aadhaar and Samagra is a common issue — update whichever is incorrect
- After updating Aadhaar, wait **2-3 days** before retrying e-KYC

### Application Rejected

Common rejection reasons include:
- **Age not in 21-60 range**
- **Family income exceeds ₹2.5 lakh/year**
- **Already receiving benefits from another similar scheme**
- **Samagra ID not active or details mismatch**
- **Applicant or spouse is a government employee or income tax payer**

If rejected, correct the issue and **re-apply through the nearest camp or Gram Panchayat**.

### OTP Not Received

- Check if your **registered mobile number** is active and has network
- Wait a few minutes and try again — the server may be overloaded
- If you've changed your number, **update it on the Samagra portal** first

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What is the current monthly amount under Ladli Behna Yojana?

The current amount is **₹1,500 per month**, credited directly to the beneficiary's bank account via DBT.

### How can I check my Ladli Behna payment status online?

Visit [cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in](https://cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in/), click "आवेदन एवं भुगतान की स्थिति", enter your Samagra ID or Application Number, verify via OTP, and view your payment history on the dashboard.

### Is e-KYC mandatory for Ladli Behna Yojana?

Yes, **e-KYC is mandatory**. Without it, your monthly payments will be paused. Complete it via the Samagra portal (OTP method) or at a CSC centre (biometric method).

### What documents are needed for Ladli Behna Yojana?

You need: **Samagra ID**, **Aadhaar card**, **bank passbook** (with Aadhaar-linked account), **passport-size photo**, and a **filled application form**.

### When is the Ladli Behna installment credited every month?

Installments are generally credited around the **10th of each month**, though the exact date may vary slightly.

### What should I do if my payment shows "Failed"?

Check your e-KYC status, verify Aadhaar-bank linking and NPCI mapping, and ensure your bank account is active. If everything looks correct, contact the helpline at **0755-2700800**.

### Can I apply for Ladli Behna Yojana online?

Applications are primarily accepted through **offline camps** at Gram Panchayats, Ward Offices, and designated camp sites. The form is filled and uploaded by authorized operators.

### Who is eligible for Ladli Behna Yojana?

Women aged **21 to 60 years** who are married, widowed, divorced, or deserted, with a family annual income below **₹2.5 lakh**, and who are not government employees or income tax payers.

### How do I download my Ladli Behna certificate?

Log in to the portal using your Samagra ID/Application Number, navigate to your dashboard, and click on **"प्रमाण पत्र डाउनलोड करें"** to download the PDF certificate.

### Can I change my bank account linked to Ladli Behna Yojana?

Yes, log in to the portal, go to your dashboard, and use the **bank account update** option. The new account must be in the beneficiary's name and linked with Aadhaar.

### What if my Aadhaar name doesn't match my Samagra ID?

You must update either your Aadhaar or Samagra profile so both match. Name mismatch will cause e-KYC failure and payment issues.

### Is Ladli Behna Yojana available in states other than MP?

The Ladli Behna Yojana is a **Madhya Pradesh state scheme**. However, Maharashtra has a similar scheme called "Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana."

### Will the monthly amount increase in the future?

The MP government has indicated plans to **progressively increase the amount to ₹3,000/month**, though no confirmed timeline has been announced yet.
