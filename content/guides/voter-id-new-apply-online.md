## What is Voter ID Card (EPIC)?

The Voter ID card, officially known as the **Electors Photo Identity Card (EPIC)**, is issued by the **Election Commission of India (ECI)**. It serves as proof of identity and confirms your registration in the electoral roll, allowing you to vote in elections.

Every Indian citizen aged **18 years or above** (as on the qualifying date — 1st January, 1st April, 1st July, or 1st October of the year) is eligible to register as a voter.

## Who is Eligible to Apply?

- **Indian citizen** residing in India
- **Age 18 years or above** as on the qualifying date
- Must be a **resident of the constituency** where you are applying
- Should **not be registered** as a voter in any other constituency
- Not disqualified under any law (e.g., unsound mind, convicted under certain offences)

### Qualifying Dates for Age Eligibility

| Qualifying Date | You Must Be Born On or Before |
|---|---|
| 1st January | 1st January (18 years prior) |
| 1st April | 1st April (18 years prior) |
| 1st July | 1st July (18 years prior) |
| 1st October | 1st October (18 years prior) |

## Documents Required

### Mandatory Documents

- **Age proof** (any one):
  - Birth certificate
  - Class 10th or 12th marksheet
  - Aadhaar card
  - Passport
  - PAN card
  - Driving licence

- **Address proof** (any one):
  - Aadhaar card
  - Ration card
  - Passport
  - Utility bill (electricity, water, gas — not older than 3 months)
  - Bank passbook with address
  - Rent agreement with landlord's ID

- **Passport-size photograph** (recent)

### Photo Requirements

- **Size:** Passport size (3.5 cm × 4.5 cm recommended)
- **Format:** JPEG or JPG for online upload
- **File size:** Between 10 KB and 50 KB
- **Background:** White or light-coloured
- **Face:** Clearly visible, front-facing, no sunglasses
- **Recency:** Taken within the last 6 months

## How to Apply for New Voter ID Online (Form 6)

### Step-by-Step Process on voters.eci.gov.in

1. Visit the official portal at [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Register as a New Voter"** or **"New Voter Registration"**
3. **Login/Register:** If you are a new user, click **"Register"** and create an account using your mobile number and email ID. Verify via OTP
4. After logging in, select **"Form 6 — New Voter Registration"**
5. Fill in your **personal details:**
   - Full name (as per documents)
   - Date of birth
   - Gender
   - Father's/Mother's/Husband's name
   - Relationship type
6. Enter your **current residential address** with:
   - State and district
   - Assembly constituency (auto-populated based on address)
   - House number, street, locality, pin code
7. Upload the required documents:
   - **Photograph** (JPEG, 10-50 KB)
   - **Age proof** document
   - **Address proof** document
8. Fill in the **declaration** section
9. **Preview** your application — check all details carefully
10. Click **"Submit"** to file your application
11. Note down the **Reference ID** for tracking

### Offline Application Process

1. Download **Form 6** from [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in) or collect from the local Electoral Registration Office (ERO)
2. Fill in the form with a **black or blue pen**
3. Attach **2 passport-size photographs**
4. Attach **self-attested copies** of age and address proof documents
5. Submit the form to the **ERO/Assistant ERO** of your constituency
6. You can also hand it over to the **Booth Level Officer (BLO)** during door-to-door verification drives

## BLO Verification Process

After you submit Form 6, the **Booth Level Officer (BLO)** will:

1. Visit your residential address for **field verification**
2. Verify your identity and address in person
3. Check if you actually reside at the mentioned address
4. Submit a verification report to the ERO

> **Tip:** Keep your original documents ready during BLO verification. If you are not at home during the BLO visit, they may revisit or contact you on the mobile number provided.

## Processing Time

- **Online application:** Typically **15–30 days** after BLO verification
- **During special revision drives:** May take **7–15 days**
- **During election period:** Registrations may be paused after the cut-off date

## Important Tips

1. **Check existing registration** first — visit [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in) and search by name or EPIC number to avoid duplicate entries
2. **Use your name exactly as on documents** — mismatches cause rejection
3. **Ensure mobile number is correct** — OTP verification and updates are sent to this number
4. **Apply well before elections** — registration may be suspended during election notification periods
5. **Download the Voter Helpline App** — available on Android and iOS for easy registration and tracking

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Form 6 used for?
Form 6 is the official application form for **new voter registration** in India. It is used by first-time voters or by those who want to register in a new constituency where they have shifted.

### Can I apply for Voter ID before turning 18?
You can fill the form if you will turn 18 on or before the next qualifying date (1st January, 1st April, 1st July, or 1st October). Your name will be added to the electoral roll on the qualifying date.

### What happens after I submit Form 6 online?
After submission, the ERO assigns a BLO who visits your address for verification. Once verified, your name is included in the electoral roll and the EPIC card is issued.

### Can NRIs apply for Voter ID?
Yes, NRIs can register as overseas electors using **Form 6A** on the same portal. They need to provide their Indian passport details.

### How do I check my Voter ID application status?
You can track your application on [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in) using the reference ID. See our detailed guide on [checking Voter ID status online](/guide/voter-id-status-check-online).

### What if my Form 6 application is rejected?
Common reasons include incomplete documents, incorrect details, or failed BLO verification. You can re-apply after correcting the issues. Check our guide on [Voter ID application rejection fixes](/guide/voter-id-application-rejected-fix).

### Can I apply for Voter ID in a constituency where I am renting?
Yes, you can apply with a rent agreement and landlord's identity proof as address proof. The BLO will verify your residence during the field visit.
