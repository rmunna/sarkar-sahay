# Sikkim Old Age Pension – Complete Guide 2026

## What is the Sikkim Old Age Pension?

The **Sikkim Old Age Pension** provides monthly financial assistance to senior citizens from economically weaker sections. It operates under the **Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS)** with an additional state top-up from the Sikkim government, which is known for providing one of the higher pension amounts among Indian states.

## Pension Amount

| Age Group | Approximate Monthly Pension |
|-----------|---------------------------|
| 60-79 years | ₹1,000-1,500 |
| 80 years and above | ₹1,500-2,000 |

> **Note:** Sikkim provides a generous state top-up over the central NSAP amount. Exact figures may vary — confirm with the District Social Welfare Office.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Age:** 60 years or above
- **Residency:** Permanent resident of Sikkim (with SSC/COI or domicile proof)
- **Income:** BPL category or annual income below the prescribed limit
- **Not receiving** any other government pension or retirement benefits
- Must not be employed in any government service

## Documents Required

- Sikkim Subject Certificate (SSC) / Certificate of Identification (COI)
- Aadhaar Card
- Age proof (birth certificate / Class 10 certificate / voter ID)
- BPL card or income certificate
- Bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked)
- Passport-size photographs (2)
- Self-declaration form

## How to Apply – Step-by-Step

### Offline Application

1. Visit the **Block Administrative Centre (BAC)** or **District Collector's Office**
2. Collect the **Old Age Pension application form**
3. Fill in all personal, age, income, and bank details
4. Attach self-attested copies of documents
5. Get the form verified by the **Gram Panchayat / Ward Councillor**
6. Submit at the BAC or District Social Welfare Office
7. After verification and approval, pension is credited via DBT

### Online

- Check [nsap.nic.in](https://nsap.nic.in) for IGNOAPS application
- State-specific online portals may be available through [sikkim.gov.in](https://sikkim.gov.in)

## Processing Time

- **Verification:** 2-4 weeks
- **Approval:** 1-3 months
- **First credit:** Within 1-2 months of approval

## Important Tips

1. **Carry original documents** for verification at the BAC
2. **Annual life verification** is mandatory to continue receiving pension
3. **Update bank details** immediately if you change your account
4. **Apply through Gram Panchayat** for faster local verification
5. **Senior citizens above 80** automatically qualify for a higher pension rate

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: What is the pension amount for Sikkim senior citizens?
**A:** Approximately **₹1,000-1,500 per month** for ages 60-79, and **₹1,500-2,000** for those 80 and above, including central and state components.

### Q2: Is SSC/COI mandatory to apply?
**A:** Yes, you need a **Sikkim Subject Certificate or Certificate of Identification** as residency proof.

### Q3: Can I receive this pension along with OFOJ benefits?
**A:** These are separate schemes. A senior citizen pension does not affect another family member's OFOJ eligibility. See our [OFOJ guide](/guide/sk-one-family-one-job).

### Q4: How is the pension paid?
**A:** Through **Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)** to the beneficiary's Aadhaar-linked bank account.

### Q5: What if I don't have a bank account?
**A:** You must open a bank account. Visit the nearest bank or post office with your Aadhaar card and SSC/COI.
