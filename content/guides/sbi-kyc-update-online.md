# SBI KYC Update Online: How to Update PAN & Aadhaar

KYC (Know Your Customer) is mandatory for all bank accounts in India. If your SBI KYC is pending or outdated, your account may face restrictions or even get frozen. This guide explains how to update KYC online through YONO, net banking, and at the branch.

## Why is KYC Update Important?

As per RBI guidelines, banks must periodically verify customer identity. If KYC is not updated:

- Your account may be **partially frozen** (only credits allowed, no debits)
- After further delay, the account can be **fully frozen** (no transactions at all)
- Online banking, UPI, and debit card may stop working
- You won't be able to open new FDs or apply for loans

> **Related:** If your account is already frozen, see our [SBI Account Frozen Fix guide](/guide/sbi-account-frozen-fix) for step-by-step reactivation.

## When is KYC Update Required?

- **Periodic re-KYC:** Every 2 years for high-risk customers, every 8 years for low-risk customers, every 10 years for medium-risk customers (as per RBI norms)
- **Change of address, name, or phone number**
- **PAN or Aadhaar not linked** to the account
- **Bank sends SMS/email** asking you to update KYC
- **CKYC migration** — SBI is migrating all customers to Central KYC (CKYC) registry

## Documents Required for KYC Update

### For Identity Proof (any one)
- Aadhaar card
- PAN card
- Voter ID
- Passport
- Driving License

### For Address Proof (any one)
- Aadhaar card (most commonly accepted)
- Utility bill (electricity, gas, water — not older than 3 months)
- Passport
- Rental agreement with utility bill

### Must-Have
- **PAN card** — mandatory for all accounts
- **Aadhaar card** — mandatory for all savings accounts (as per PMLA rules)

## How to Update KYC Online

### Method 1: Through YONO App

1. Open the **SBI YONO** app on your phone
2. Log in with your credentials
3. Go to **"Service Requests"** or search for **"KYC"**
4. Select **"KYC Updation"** or **"Update PAN/Aadhaar"**
5. Enter your **Aadhaar number** — an OTP will be sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile
6. Enter the OTP to verify
7. For **PAN update**, enter your PAN number — it will be verified with the Income Tax database
8. Submit the request
9. Your KYC will be updated within **1-3 working days**

### Method 2: Through SBI Net Banking

1. Log in to [SBI Online Banking](https://www.onlinesbi.sbi/)
2. Navigate to **"My Accounts"** → **"Profile"** → **"KYC Updation"**
3. Select the type of update — Aadhaar linking, PAN update, or address change
4. For Aadhaar: Enter Aadhaar number → verify with OTP
5. For PAN: Enter PAN → instant verification
6. Confirm and submit
7. Status updates via SMS and email

### Method 3: At SBI Branch (For Re-KYC or Complex Cases)

1. Visit your **SBI home branch** with original documents
2. Carry **Aadhaar and PAN** (originals + photocopies)
3. Fill in the **KYC updation form** (available at the branch or download from sbi.co.in)
4. Submit the form with self-attested copies of documents
5. The branch officer will verify originals and return them
6. KYC is typically updated within **2-7 working days**
7. Collect acknowledgement receipt for your records

## What is CKYC?

**CKYC (Central KYC)** is a centralized KYC registry maintained by CERSAI (Central Registry of Securitisation Asset Reconstruction and Security Interest of India).

- Once your KYC is registered in CKYC, you get a **14-digit KIN (KYC Identification Number)**
- This KIN can be used across banks, mutual funds, and insurance companies — no need to submit KYC documents again
- SBI is gradually migrating all customer KYC records to the CKYC registry
- You can check your CKYC status at [ckycindia.in](https://www.ckycindia.in/)

## Account Freeze Risk: What to Do

If your account is already frozen due to pending KYC:

1. **Visit your home branch immediately** with Aadhaar and PAN originals
2. Fill the KYC update form and submit documents
3. The branch will process re-KYC and unfreeze your account
4. Online methods (YONO/net banking) may **not work** if the account is fully frozen — you must visit the branch
5. Account is typically reactivated within **1-3 working days** after KYC submission

## Important Tips

1. **Don't ignore KYC SMS alerts** from SBI — update before the deadline to avoid account freeze
2. **Keep Aadhaar mobile number active** — OTP-based verification requires it
3. **YONO is the fastest** way to update PAN and Aadhaar KYC if your account is still active
4. **Carry original documents** when visiting the branch — photocopies alone are not accepted for verification
5. **Check CKYC status** at ckycindia.in to confirm your KYC is centrally registered

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Can I update KYC online if my account is frozen?
If the account is partially frozen (credits only), you may still use YONO or net banking. If fully frozen, you must visit the branch in person.

### Q2: Is there a fee for KYC update at SBI?
No. KYC update is completely free of charge at SBI — whether done online or at the branch.

### Q3: How often do I need to update KYC?
As per RBI norms, re-KYC is required every 2-10 years depending on your risk category. Your bank will notify you when it's due.

### Q4: What if my Aadhaar address is different from my bank address?
You can update your address in either Aadhaar or your bank records. For the bank, submit a new address proof at the branch.

### Q5: Can I update KYC at any SBI branch?
For simple updates like PAN/Aadhaar linking, any branch may help. For full re-KYC, it's best to visit your **home branch** (where the account was opened).

### Q6: What is the RBI deadline for KYC update?
RBI does not set a single nationwide deadline. Your bank will notify you of your individual KYC renewal date. Act within the timeline mentioned in the SMS/email.

### Q7: Will my account be automatically unfrozen after KYC update?
Yes. Once the branch processes your KYC documents, the account is unfrozen — usually within 1-3 working days.
