# Meebhoomi AP: Check Land Record & Adangal Online

**Meebhoomi** is the official land records portal of the Andhra Pradesh government. Through [meebhoomi.ap.gov.in](https://meebhoomi.ap.gov.in/), you can check Adangal (village account), 1B (ownership record), digital passbook, search by survey number, and even search using Aadhaar number. This guide covers the complete process.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent informational platform and is not affiliated with any government department. Always verify details on official government portals.

## Key Terms in AP Land Records

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Adangal** | Village account showing land use, crop, owner, tax, and extent details |
| **1B** | Ownership and tenancy record linking landowners to their survey numbers |
| **Passbook** | Digital land ownership passbook with owner photo and Aadhaar details |
| **Survey Number** | Unique identification for each land parcel |
| **Subdivision** | Sub-plot number within a survey number |
| **FMB** | Field Measurement Book sketch showing plot boundaries |
| **Pahani** | Combined Adangal and 1B record |

## How to Check Adangal Online

### Step-by-Step Process

1. Visit [meebhoomi.ap.gov.in](https://meebhoomi.ap.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"Adangal"** from the menu
3. Select your **District (జిల్లా)**
4. Select **Mandal (మండలం)**
5. Select **Village (గ్రామం)**
6. Choose search method:
   - **Survey Number** — Enter your survey number
   - **Account Number** — Enter Khata number
   - **Aadhaar Number** — Search using linked Aadhaar
   - **Owner Name** — Search by name
7. Enter the relevant details
8. Enter the **captcha code**
9. Click **"Search"**
10. The Adangal record will display with ownership, extent, crop, and tax information

## How to Check 1B Record Online

The 1B record shows ownership and tenancy details:

1. Visit [meebhoomi.ap.gov.in](https://meebhoomi.ap.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"1B"** from the menu
3. Select District → Mandal → Village
4. Enter survey number or account number
5. Complete the captcha
6. Click **"Search"**
7. The 1B record shows: owner name, father's name, extent, classification, and tenancy status

## How to Check Digital Passbook

The AP government issues digital land passbooks to all landowners:

1. Visit [meebhoomi.ap.gov.in](https://meebhoomi.ap.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"Passbook"** from the menu
3. Search by:
   - **Aadhaar Number** — Most convenient method
   - **Survey Number** — Select location and enter survey number
   - **Passbook Number** — If you have the passbook ID
4. Enter details and captcha
5. Click **"Search"**
6. The digital passbook shows: owner photo, Aadhaar number, land details, survey numbers, and ownership status

> **Note:** Passbooks are linked to Aadhaar. If your land is not showing, visit your Mandal Revenue Office (MRO) to get it linked.

## How to Search by Aadhaar Number

Meebhoomi offers Aadhaar-based search, which is unique among Indian land record portals:

1. Go to [meebhoomi.ap.gov.in](https://meebhoomi.ap.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"Adangal"** or **"1B"** or **"Passbook"**
3. Select **"Aadhaar Number"** as the search method
4. Enter your **12-digit Aadhaar number**
5. Complete the captcha
6. All land records linked to your Aadhaar will be displayed

> **Tip:** This is the fastest way to find all your land holdings across different villages in AP.

## How to Search by Survey Number

1. Visit Meebhoomi portal
2. Select the record type (Adangal/1B/Passbook)
3. Select District → Mandal → Village
4. Choose **"Survey Number"** search
5. Enter the survey number (e.g., 123) and subdivision if applicable (e.g., 123/2)
6. Click **"Search"**

## Important Tips

1. **Link Aadhaar to land records** — AP mandates Aadhaar linking for all land records. Visit MRO if not linked.
2. **Check before property purchase** — Verify Adangal, 1B, and encumbrance certificate before buying land in AP.
3. **Passbook is your digital proof** — The digital passbook with photo is accepted as ownership proof in AP.
4. **Report discrepancies** — If your land details are wrong in Meebhoomi, apply for correction at the MRO office.
5. **Use Bhunaksha for maps** — Visit [bhunaksha.ap.gov.in](https://bhunaksha.ap.gov.in/) for detailed plot maps and survey sketches.

## Common Issues and Solutions

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Aadhaar search shows no records | Visit MRO to link your Aadhaar to land records |
| Wrong owner name in records | Apply for correction at MRO with sale deed and identity documents |
| Survey number not found | Check if the village was recently reorganised — mandal boundaries may have changed |
| Passbook not generated | Visit MRO with Aadhaar and sale deed for passbook generation |

## FAQs

### What is Meebhoomi?

Meebhoomi (meebhoomi.ap.gov.in) is the official online land records portal of the Andhra Pradesh government. It provides access to Adangal, 1B, digital passbook, and other land records for all districts in AP.

### How can I check my land record in Andhra Pradesh online?

Visit meebhoomi.ap.gov.in, click on Adangal or 1B, select your District, Mandal, and Village, then search by survey number, Aadhaar number, or owner name. Your land record will display immediately.

### What is the difference between Adangal and 1B in AP?

Adangal is the village account showing land use, crop, tax, and ownership details for each survey number. 1B is the ownership register that lists all landowners with their survey numbers and extent of holding.

### Can I search AP land records using Aadhaar number?

Yes, Meebhoomi allows search by Aadhaar number. Click on Adangal, 1B, or Passbook, select "Aadhaar Number" as search type, and enter your 12-digit Aadhaar. All linked land records will be displayed.

### What is the digital passbook in Meebhoomi?

The digital passbook is an Aadhaar-linked land ownership document with the owner's photo, land details, and survey numbers. It serves as a digital proof of land ownership in Andhra Pradesh.

### How do I get my land record corrected in AP?

Visit your Mandal Revenue Office (MRO) with supporting documents like sale deed, Aadhaar card, and any previous records. Submit a correction application. The MRO will verify and update the record.

### Is Meebhoomi data updated in real-time?

Meebhoomi records are updated periodically by the Revenue Department. After property transactions, there may be a delay of a few weeks before the records are updated online. For urgent updates, contact your MRO.
