# Manipur Government Schemes and Services – Complete Guide

Manipur, the "Jewel of India" nestled in the north-eastern hills, is governed by the BJP-led government under Chief Minister N. Biren Singh. The state government runs numerous welfare schemes targeting health, education, livelihood, women's empowerment, tribal development, and digital governance. This comprehensive guide covers every major Manipur government scheme and service to help citizens access the benefits they deserve.

## Health Schemes

### Chief Minister-gi Hakshelgi Tengbang (CMHT)

CMHT is Manipur's flagship health assurance scheme providing **cashless treatment** to poor and disabled families. It works alongside the central **Ayushman Bharat – PMJAY** scheme.

- **Coverage:** Up to ₹2 lakh per family per year (CMHT) and up to ₹5 lakh under PMJAY for SECC-2011 families
- **Benefits:** Cashless hospitalisation at government hospitals, health centres, and empanelled private hospitals in Manipur and Guwahati
- **Eligibility:** Families listed in SECC-2011 database, AAY cardholders, and persons with disabilities
- **How to enrol:** Visit the nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) or CMHT enrolment camp with Aadhaar card and ration card
- **Portal:** [cmhtmanipur.gov.in](https://cmhtmanipur.gov.in)

> 💡 Also see our guide on [Ayushman Bharat – PMJAY](/guide/ayushman-bharat-pmjay-health-card) for the central scheme component.

## Education Schemes

### Lairik Leishemlon / Lairik Tamhanlasi (Let Them Learn)

This scheme targets **school dropouts** and children from economically weaker families to ensure uninterrupted education.

- **Objective:** Reduce dropout rates by providing financial assistance for uniforms, books, and school fees
- **Target:** Students from BPL families in government and government-aided schools
- **Coverage:** Elementary and secondary school levels across all districts

### School Fagat-Hansi Mission

Launched under the **Go to Village** initiative, this mission focuses on improving school infrastructure, teacher attendance, and learning outcomes in remote areas.

### Manipur University and Higher Education

- **Manipur University** (a central university at Canchipur) offers UG, PG, and research programmes
- State scholarships are available for SC/ST/OBC students through the **Directorate of University & Higher Education**
- **Post-Matric Scholarship** for SC/ST students: Apply through the [National Scholarship Portal](https://scholarships.gov.in)

## Startup and Entrepreneurship

### StartUp Manipur

The **StartUp Manipur** initiative aims to build an entrepreneurial ecosystem for youth by providing incubation, mentoring, and funding support.

- **Benefits:** Seed funding, incubation support, mentoring by industry experts, fast-track statutory clearances
- **Eligibility:** Youth entrepreneurs and innovators from Manipur with viable business ideas
- **Focus sectors:** Agri-tech, handloom & textiles, food processing, IT, tourism, bamboo-based industries
- **Portal:** [startupmanipur.in](https://startupmanipur.in)
- **How to apply:** Register on the StartUp Manipur portal with a business plan

### Ima Market and Women Entrepreneur Schemes

The iconic **Ima Keithel** (Mothers' Market) in Imphal is one of Asia's largest all-women markets. The state government supports women entrepreneurs through:

- **Ima Market modernisation** – Infrastructure upgrades and stall allotments for women vendors
- **Women entrepreneurship development programmes** under MSRLM and National Urban Livelihoods Mission (NULM)
- **SHG-based micro-enterprise support** – Training and credit linkage for women's self-help groups
- Financial assistance through **Manipur Women Development Corporation**

## Rural Development and Livelihood

### Manipur State Rural Livelihood Mission (MSRLM)

MSRLM implements the **National Rural Livelihoods Mission (NRLM/Aajeevika)** in Manipur under the Department of Rural Development & Panchayati Raj.

- **Objective:** Reduce poverty by building sustainable livelihoods for rural poor
- **Key programmes:** Self-Help Group (SHG) formation, skill training, Start-up Village Entrepreneurship Programme (SVEP), Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Grameen Kaushalya Yojana (DDU-GKY)
- **Coverage:** All rural blocks and districts in phased manner
- **Portal:** [manipursrlm.gov.in](https://manipursrlm.gov.in)

### Go to Hills Mission

The **Go to Hills** mission brings government services, grievance redressal, and development programmes directly to hill district populations. Key features:

- Mobile government camps in remote hill areas
- Distribution of benefits under various welfare schemes
- Direct interaction between government officials and tribal communities
- Focus districts: Churachandpur, Chandel, Ukhrul, Tamenglong, Senapati, Kangpokpi, Kamjong, Noney, Pherzawl, Tengnoupal, Jiribam

### Go to Village Mission

A parallel initiative to **Go to Hills**, the Go to Village mission focuses on delivering government services to villages across the valley and hill areas:

- Last-mile delivery of welfare scheme benefits
- Door-to-door survey and beneficiary identification
- School Fagat-Hansi (school improvement drives)
- Health camps and awareness programmes

## Handloom, Handicraft and Textile Schemes

Manipur is renowned for its exquisite handloom and handicraft traditions. The **Directorate of Handlooms & Textiles** implements several schemes:

- **Mega Handloom Clusters** – Bishnupur, Moirang, Kamjong, Ukhrul, and other cluster-based development programmes
- **Crafts Handloom Village Scheme** – Infrastructure and training support for weaver communities
- **National Handloom Development Programme (NHDP)** – Central scheme implemented in Manipur for individual weavers and SHGs
- **Manipur Handloom & Handicraft Development Corporation** – Marketing support, exhibitions, and buyer-seller meets
- **GI-tagged products:** Manipur's Shaphee Lanphee (traditional shawl) and Moirang Phee carry Geographical Indication tags

**Portal:** [dhtmanipur.mn.gov.in](https://dhtmanipur.mn.gov.in)

> 💡 Weavers can also apply for benefits under [MUDRA Loan Scheme](/guide/mudra-loan-yojana) for working capital needs.

## Tribal Welfare

Manipur has a significant tribal population across its hill districts. Key tribal welfare schemes include:

- **Scheduled Tribe scholarship schemes** – Pre-Matric and Post-Matric scholarships for ST students
- **Tribal Affairs Department programmes** – Hostels, residential schools, and vocational training for tribal youth
- **Forest Rights Act (FRA) implementation** – Recognition of community and individual forest rights
- **Special Central Assistance (SCA) to Tribal Sub-Scheme** – Infrastructure and livelihood support in tribal areas
- **Manipur Tribal Development Corporation** – Economic development programmes for tribal communities
- **Eklavya Model Residential Schools (EMRS)** – Quality education for tribal students in hill districts

## e-District Manipur and Digital Services

The **e-District Manipur** portal enables citizens to apply for certificates, permits, and government services online.

### Services Available on e-District

- **Certificates:** Income, caste (SC/ST/OBC), domicile, residence, birth, death certificates
- **Revenue services:** Land records, mutation, revenue court cases
- **Permits:** Inner Line Permit (ILP) for visitors
- **Other services:** Electoral services, ration card, social welfare applications

**Portal:** [eservicesmanipur.gov.in](https://eservicesmanipur.gov.in)

### How to Use e-District Manipur

1. Visit [eservicesmanipur.gov.in](https://eservicesmanipur.gov.in)
2. Register with mobile number and Aadhaar
3. Select the required service/certificate
4. Fill the application form and upload documents
5. Pay applicable fees online
6. Track application status using the reference number

> 💡 For general guidance, see our [e-District Portal Guide](/guide/e-district-portal-online-certificates).

## How to Apply for Manipur Government Schemes

Most Manipur government schemes follow a common application process:

1. **Check eligibility** – Visit the respective scheme portal or CM Dashboard at [cmdashboard.mn.gov.in](https://cmdashboard.mn.gov.in)
2. **Gather documents** – Aadhaar card, ration card, income certificate, bank passbook, passport-size photos, caste certificate (if applicable)
3. **Apply online** – Use e-Services Manipur ([eservicesmanipur.gov.in](https://eservicesmanipur.gov.in)) or the specific scheme portal
4. **Apply offline** – Visit the nearest Common Service Centre (CSC), district office, or block development office
5. **Track status** – Use the application reference number on the respective portal
6. **Go to Village/Hills camps** – Attend government outreach camps for on-the-spot enrolment

> 💡 Need help with documents? Check our guides on [Aadhaar Card](/guide/aadhaar-card-complete-guide) and [Income Certificate](/guide/income-certificate-online-apply).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the CMHT scheme in Manipur?
Chief Minister-gi Hakshelgi Tengbang (CMHT) is Manipur's health assurance scheme providing cashless treatment up to ₹2 lakh per family per year at empanelled hospitals. It works alongside Ayushman Bharat–PMJAY, which provides coverage up to ₹5 lakh for eligible families.

### How do I apply for StartUp Manipur?
Register on the [StartUp Manipur portal](https://startupmanipur.in) with your business idea and required documents. The portal provides information on incubation centres, mentoring, and seed funding available for eligible entrepreneurs.

### What is the Go to Hills mission?
Go to Hills is a flagship initiative of the Manipur government that brings welfare services, grievance redressal, and development programmes directly to remote hill areas, ensuring tribal and rural communities can access government benefits without travelling to district headquarters.

### How can I get an income certificate or caste certificate in Manipur?
Apply through the e-District portal at [eservicesmanipur.gov.in](https://eservicesmanipur.gov.in). Register, submit the online form with supporting documents, and track your application status using the reference number provided.

### What is Lairik Leishemlon / Lairik Tamhanlasi?
It is an education scheme launched by the Manipur government to prevent school dropouts by providing financial assistance for uniforms, books, and fees to students from economically weaker families.

### Who is eligible for MSRLM benefits?
Rural poor households across all districts of Manipur are eligible. MSRLM focuses on women from BPL families, organising them into Self-Help Groups (SHGs) for livelihood support, skill training, and credit linkage.

### How do I check MPSC exam notifications?
Visit the official MPSC website at [mpscmanipur.gov.in](https://mpscmanipur.gov.in) for the latest recruitment notifications, exam schedules, syllabus downloads, and results.
