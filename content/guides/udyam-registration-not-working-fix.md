# Udyam Registration Not Working? Fix Portal Errors & Common Issues

Trying to register on the [Udyam portal](https://udyamregistration.gov.in/) but getting errors? You're not alone — the MSME registration portal frequently faces technical glitches, especially during peak hours. This guide covers every common error and how to fix it.

## Error 1: Udyam Portal Server Down or Not Loading

The Udyam registration website often becomes slow or unresponsive due to heavy traffic.

### How to Fix

1. **Check server status** — Visit [champions.gov.in](https://champions.gov.in/) to check if MSME services are operational
2. **Try off-peak hours** — Access the portal early morning (6–8 AM) or late evening (after 9 PM) when traffic is low
3. **Clear browser cache** — Press `Ctrl + Shift + Delete` and clear cached data
4. **Use a different browser** — Try Google Chrome or Mozilla Firefox (avoid Internet Explorer)
5. **Disable VPN** — VPN connections can interfere with government portal access
6. **Try mobile data** — Some ISPs have routing issues; switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data

> **Tip:** If the portal shows "502 Bad Gateway" or "Service Unavailable," it's a server-side issue. Wait 30–60 minutes and try again.

## Error 2: PAN Verification Failed

The portal may reject your PAN during registration with errors like "PAN not verified" or "PAN details mismatch."

### Common Causes & Fixes

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Name mismatch between PAN and Aadhaar | Ensure your name on PAN card matches exactly with what you entered. Update PAN or Aadhaar if needed |
| PAN not linked to Aadhaar | Link PAN to Aadhaar on [incometax.gov.in](https://www.incometax.gov.in/) first |
| PAN marked as "Inoperative" | Pay ₹1,000 penalty on Income Tax portal to reactivate PAN |
| Entering wrong PAN type | Use individual PAN for proprietorship, company PAN for Pvt Ltd/LLP |
| CBDT verification server down | Wait and retry after a few hours |

### Step-by-Step Fix for PAN Mismatch

1. Visit [incometax.gov.in](https://www.incometax.gov.in/)
2. Check your PAN details under Profile
3. If name doesn't match Aadhaar, apply for PAN correction via [NSDL](https://www.onlineservices.nsdl.com/paam/endUserRegisterContact.html) or [UTIITSL](https://www.pan.utiitsl.com/)
4. After correction (takes 15–20 days), retry Udyam registration

## Error 3: Aadhaar OTP Not Received

OTP issues are the most common problem during Udyam registration.

### Quick Fixes

1. **Check mobile number** — The OTP goes to the mobile number linked to your [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online). Verify on [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/)
2. **Wait 2–3 minutes** — OTPs can be delayed during peak hours
3. **Check SMS inbox** — Search for messages from "UIDAI" or 6-digit codes
4. **Don't click "Resend OTP" too quickly** — Wait at least 60 seconds before resending
5. **UIDAI server issue** — If UIDAI is down, all Aadhaar-based OTPs will fail. Check back later

For detailed OTP troubleshooting, see our [Udyam Aadhaar OTP fix guide](/guide/udyam-registration-aadhaar-otp-fix).

## Error 4: "Already Registered" Error

If you see "This Aadhaar/PAN is already registered under Udyam," it means a registration already exists.

### How to Fix

1. **Forgot your Udyam number?** — Visit [udyamregistration.gov.in](https://udyamregistration.gov.in/) and click "Forgot Udyam Registration Number"
2. Enter your Aadhaar number or PAN and verify via OTP
3. Your existing Udyam Registration Number (URN) will be sent to your registered mobile/email
4. **Need to update details?** — Use the "Update Udyam Registration" option instead of new registration. See our [Udyam update guide](/guide/udyam-registration-update-details-fix)
5. **Duplicate by mistake?** — Contact the MSME DI (District Industries Centre) in your area or raise a grievance on [champions.gov.in](https://champions.gov.in/)

## Error 5: Browser and Technical Issues

### Recommended Browser Settings

- **Browser:** Google Chrome (latest version) or Mozilla Firefox
- **JavaScript:** Must be enabled
- **Pop-ups:** Allow pop-ups for udyamregistration.gov.in
- **Cookies:** Enable third-party cookies
- **Cache:** Clear before attempting registration

### Other Technical Fixes

1. **"Session Expired" error** — Don't keep the page idle for more than 15 minutes. Complete the form in one go
2. **Form not submitting** — Check all mandatory fields (marked with *) are filled correctly
3. **Captcha not loading** — Refresh the page. If captcha images don't appear, try a different browser
4. **Page stuck on loading** — Disable browser extensions, especially ad blockers
5. **Mobile browser issues** — Use desktop mode or a laptop/computer for best results

## When to Contact MSME Helpline

If none of the above fixes work, contact the official helpline:

- **MSME Champions Portal:** [champions.gov.in](https://champions.gov.in/) — raise a grievance ticket
- **Toll-Free Number:** 1800-11-1955 (MSME DI helpline)
- **Email:** champion@gov.in
- **District Industries Centre (DIC):** Visit your nearest DIC office with documents for in-person help

## Important Tips

1. Always use the official portal [udyamregistration.gov.in](https://udyamregistration.gov.in/) — beware of fake websites charging fees (Udyam registration is **100% free**)
2. Keep your PAN linked to Aadhaar before starting registration
3. Have your previous year's ITR, investment details, and bank account information ready before you begin
4. Take screenshots of errors for reference when contacting the helpline
5. Udyam registration is valid for lifetime — no renewal needed

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Udyam registration free?
Yes, Udyam registration is completely free on the official portal [udyamregistration.gov.in](https://udyamregistration.gov.in/). Any website asking for payment is fraudulent.

### Can I register on Udyam using someone else's Aadhaar?
No. For proprietorship firms, the owner's Aadhaar is mandatory. For partnership/company, the authorized signatory's Aadhaar and organization PAN are required.

### What is the difference between Udyam and Udyog Aadhaar?
Udyog Aadhaar (UAM) was the old MSME registration system. It has been replaced by Udyam Registration since July 1, 2020. All UAMs have been migrated to Udyam automatically.

### How long does Udyam registration take?
If the portal is working smoothly, registration can be completed in 10–15 minutes. The Udyam Registration Number is generated instantly after successful submission.

### Can I have multiple Udyam registrations?
No. One enterprise gets one Udyam Registration Number. If you have multiple businesses, each legally separate entity can have its own registration.

### My Udyam registration is stuck at "Processing." What should I do?
Wait 24–48 hours. If still stuck, raise a grievance on [champions.gov.in](https://champions.gov.in/) with your reference number.
