# Indian Bank Account Blocked or Frozen? How to Unblock (2026)

Indian Bank (formerly Allahabad Bank merged entity) is one of India's major public sector banks with over 6,000 branches. If your Indian Bank account is blocked, frozen, or debit-restricted, here's exactly how to fix it — online and offline.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is independent and not affiliated with Indian Bank. For account-specific help, contact Indian Bank directly at 1800-425-1400.

---

## Why Is Your Indian Bank Account Blocked?

| Reason | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| KYC not updated | Complete re-KYC via app or branch |
| Dormant (no transactions 24 months) | Reactivation at branch |
| Suspected fraudulent activity | Call customer care + branch visit |
| Court/government order | Legal resolution required |
| PAN not linked | Link PAN via net banking or branch |
| Minimum balance non-maintenance | Pay fee at branch (rare for salary accounts) |

---

## Step 1: Find Out Why Your Account Is Blocked

1. **Check SMS alerts** — Indian Bank sends SMS for account restrictions
2. **Call 1800-425-1400** — ask for specific reason account is restricted
3. **Visit branch** — branch can see exact flag on account

---

## Step 2: Unblock Indian Bank Account Online

### Via IndOASIS Mobile Banking App

1. Open **IndOASIS** (Indian Bank's official app)
2. Go to **Services → Account Services**
3. If you see a KYC update prompt: **Update KYC** → Upload Aadhaar + PAN
4. For other restrictions: **Raise Service Request** → Account Unblock Request
5. Track request via the app's notification section

**Note:** IndOASIS allows KYC submission digitally. For debit freeze due to regulatory reasons, branch visit is typically needed.

### Via Indian Bank Net Banking

1. Login at **[indianbank.net.in](https://www.indianbank.net.in)**
2. Navigate: **Services → Customer Request → Account Related**
3. Submit KYC documents if prompted
4. For other blocks: **Submit Complaint** with account details

---

## Step 3: Call Customer Care

**Indian Bank 24x7 Toll-Free:** **1800-425-1400**
**Alternate:** 1800-425-00-000

**When calling:**
- Say: "My account XXXX is restricted. I want to know the reason and how to resolve it."
- Note the complaint/reference number provided

---

## Step 4: Visit Home Branch

For regulatory freezes, court-ordered freezes, or complex cases:

1. Visit your **home branch** (where account was opened)
2. Carry: Aadhaar (original + copy), PAN (original + copy), passbook, 2 passport photos
3. Ask for: **"Account Unfreeze / Activation Request"** form
4. Submit and get written acknowledgment

**Processing time:** 1–3 working days for KYC-related freeze. Court-ordered freeze requires legal order.

---

## KYC Update for Indian Bank Account

**Via IndOASIS App:**
1. IndOASIS → Services → KYC Update
2. Upload Aadhaar (front + back) + PAN card photo
3. Self-attest digitally + submit

**Via Branch:**
1. Fill Re-KYC form
2. Submit Aadhaar + PAN + passport photo (self-attested)
3. Completed: 1–2 working days

---

## Dormant Indian Bank Account — Reactivation

1. Visit home branch
2. Fill **"Dormant Account Reactivation"** form
3. Submit fresh KYC documents
4. Do one cash deposit (₹100 minimum)
5. Account active in 1 working day

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does it take to unblock an Indian Bank account?**
KYC-related freeze: 1–3 working days after document submission. Dormant reactivation: same day to 1 working day. Regulatory freeze: varies by case.

**Can I update KYC for Indian Bank without visiting the branch?**
Yes — IndOASIS app allows digital KYC submission with Aadhaar + PAN. However, for complex cases the branch may still be required.

**My Indian Bank net banking is blocked — what do I do?**
Call 1800-425-1400 or visit branch to reset credentials. Net banking block is often separate from account block.

**My salary was credited but I can't withdraw — why?**
This is a debit freeze. Your account accepts credits (salary, ECS) but outward transactions are blocked. Likely reason: KYC pending or regulatory hold. Call customer care with your account details for exact reason.
