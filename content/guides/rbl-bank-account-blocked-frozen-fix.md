# RBL Bank Account Blocked or Frozen? How to Unblock (2026)

RBL Bank (formerly Ratnakar Bank), a private sector bank with a strong presence in credit cards and retail banking, serves customers across India. If your RBL Bank account is blocked or frozen, here's how to resolve it.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is independent. Contact RBL Bank at 1800-121-9050 for account-specific help.

---

## Why Is Your RBL Bank Account Blocked?

| Reason | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| KYC not updated | Re-KYC via app or branch |
| PAN not linked | Link PAN via net banking or branch |
| Dormant account | Reactivation at branch |
| Suspicious transactions | Call customer care + branch visit |
| Government / court order | Legal resolution required |

---

## Identify the Reason

1. **SMS alerts** — RBL Bank sends restriction messages via SMS
2. **Call 1800-121-9050** — ask for exact restriction reason
3. **RBL MoBank app** — may show KYC or action notification

---

## Unblock RBL Bank Account Online

### Via RBL MoBank App

1. Open **RBL MoBank** app
2. Login → **Services → Account Services**
3. If KYC update visible: Upload Aadhaar + PAN
4. Raise Service Request for other blocks

### Via RBL Net Banking

1. Login at **[rblbank.com](https://www.rblbank.com)** → Net Banking
2. Profile → KYC Documents → Upload / Update
3. Or submit service request

---

## Call RBL Customer Care

**Toll-Free:** **1800-121-9050**
**Alternate:** 022-6115-6300

When calling: "My RBL Bank account ending XXXX has debit restrictions. Please confirm the reason and how to unblock."

---

## Visit RBL Bank Branch

1. Visit nearest RBL Bank branch
2. Carry: Aadhaar, PAN, passbook, 2 passport photos
3. Fill **Account Reactivation / KYC Update** form
4. Submit documents + get written acknowledgment
5. Processing: **1–2 working days**

---

## RBL Bank KYC Update

**Via MoBank App:**
1. MoBank → Profile → KYC Update
2. Upload Aadhaar + PAN (clear, self-attested)
3. Timeline: 1–2 working days

**At Branch:**
- Fill KYC form + submit documents
- Same-day processing at counter

---

## Dormant Account Reactivation

1. Visit home branch
2. Fill reactivation form + submit fresh KYC documents
3. Do one transaction (cash deposit)
4. Account active: 1 working day

---

## RBL Credit Card Account Blocked (Separate Issue)

If your **RBL credit card** is blocked (separate from savings account):
1. Call dedicated credit card helpline: **1800-121-9050** → Select Credit Card
2. Or email: **creditcard@rblbank.com**
3. Credit card blocks are processed separately from savings account blocks

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long to unblock RBL Bank account?**
KYC freeze: 1–2 working days. Dormant: 1 working day. Fraud/suspicious hold: 3–7 working days.

**Is RBL Bank safe? Is my money protected?**
Yes — RBL Bank is RBI-regulated and DICGC-insured (deposits up to ₹5 lakh per depositor protected).

**My RBL credit card works but savings account is blocked — are they linked?**
They are with the same bank but can have separate blocks. A savings account block does not necessarily block the credit card and vice versa. Call customer care to confirm.
