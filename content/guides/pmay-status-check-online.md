## What is PMAY Status Check?

After applying for Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY), you can track your application status online through the official government portals. Whether you've applied under PMAY-Urban or PMAY-Gramin, the government provides multiple ways to check where your application stands — by application ID, Aadhaar number, or name search.

If you haven't applied yet, check our [complete PMAY application guide](/guide/pmay-online-apply) first.

## How to Check PMAY-Urban Application Status

### Method 1: Using Application Reference Number

**Step 1:** Visit the official PMAY-Urban portal at [pmaymis.gov.in](https://pmaymis.gov.in).

**Step 2:** Click on **"Citizen Assessment"** in the top menu bar.

**Step 3:** Select **"Track Your Assessment Status"** from the dropdown.

**Step 4:** Enter your **Application Reference Number** (received during application submission).

**Step 5:** Click **"Submit"**. Your current application status will be displayed — including approval stage, subsidy status, and any pending actions.

### Method 2: Using Aadhaar Number

**Step 1:** Go to [pmaymis.gov.in](https://pmaymis.gov.in).

**Step 2:** Click **"Citizen Assessment"** → **"Track Your Assessment Status"**.

**Step 3:** Select the option to search by **Aadhaar Number**.

**Step 4:** Enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number and click **"Submit"**.

**Step 5:** If your Aadhaar is linked to an application, the status will be displayed.

### Method 3: Using Name, Father's Name, and Mobile Number

**Step 1:** On the same tracking page, select **"By Name, Father's Name & Mobile Number"**.

**Step 2:** Enter your full name (as in application), father's name, and registered mobile number.

**Step 3:** Click **"Submit"** to view your application details.

## How to Check PMAY-Gramin Status

### Method 1: Check on PMAY-G Portal

**Step 1:** Visit [pmayg.nic.in](https://pmayg.nic.in).

**Step 2:** Click on **"Stakeholders"** in the top menu.

**Step 3:** Select **"IAY/PMAYG Beneficiary"** from the dropdown.

**Step 4:** Enter your **Registration Number** and click **"Submit"**.

**Step 5:** Your beneficiary details, sanction status, and installment release information will be displayed.

### Method 2: Using Awaas+ App

**Step 1:** Download the **AwaasApp** from Google Play Store.

**Step 2:** Open the app and select your state, district, block, and village.

**Step 3:** Search for your name in the beneficiary list.

**Step 4:** View detailed status including construction progress and installment details.

### Method 3: Check on AwaasSoft/Rhreporting Portal

**Step 1:** Visit [rhreporting.nic.in](https://rhreporting.nic.in).

**Step 2:** Navigate to beneficiary details section.

**Step 3:** Select your state, district, block, and panchayat.

**Step 4:** Find your name in the list to view status.

## Understanding PMAY Application Status

Your application can show one of these statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Under Verification** | Application submitted, being verified by ULB/Block office |
| **Approved** | Application approved, pending subsidy disbursement |
| **Subsidy Released** | Subsidy amount credited to your loan/bank account |
| **Rejected** | Application rejected — [see how to fix](/guide/pmay-application-rejected-fix) |
| **Incomplete** | Missing documents or information — resubmit required details |
| **Sanctioned** | (PMAY-G) House construction sanctioned, installments to follow |

## How to Check PMAY Subsidy Credit Status

If your application is approved and you want to track when the subsidy hits your bank:

1. **Check with your bank** — ask the home loan branch about CLSS subsidy credit status
2. **NHB/HUDCO tracking** — National Housing Bank processes CLSS subsidies; contact them at their helpline
3. **Check bank statement** — subsidy appears as a lump-sum credit to your loan account
4. **Contact CNA (Central Nodal Agency)** — your bank's CNA processes the claim

If your subsidy is delayed, read our [PMAY subsidy not received troubleshooting guide](/guide/pmay-subsidy-not-received-fix).

## What to Do If Status Shows "Not Found"

If your application is not appearing in the system:

1. **Verify application ID** — check if you have the correct reference number
2. **Wait 48–72 hours** — newly submitted applications may take time to reflect
3. **Check Aadhaar details** — ensure your Aadhaar name matches the application name exactly
4. **Contact ULB office** — visit your local Urban Local Body or municipal office for PMAY-U
5. **Contact Gram Panchayat** — for PMAY-G, visit your panchayat office
6. **Call PMAY helpline** — dial **1800-11-3377** (toll-free) or **1800-11-3388**

## Processing Timeline

| Stage | Expected Duration |
|-------|------------------|
| Application to verification | 1–4 weeks |
| Verification to approval | 1–3 months |
| Approval to subsidy release | 2–6 months |
| PMAY-G sanction to first installment | 1–2 months |
| Total (application to subsidy) | 3–8 months |

*Timelines vary significantly by state and local body efficiency.*

## Important Tips

1. **Save your Application Reference Number** — it's the fastest way to track status
2. **Keep Aadhaar updated** — mismatched details cause delays; [update Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) if needed
3. **Check regularly** — status updates may not trigger SMS/email notifications
4. **Don't pay agents** — status checking is free on official portals; avoid middlemen
5. **Keep documents handy** — you may be called for physical verification at any stage

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. How long does it take for PMAY status to update after applying?

Your application typically appears in the system within 48–72 hours of submission. Verification status updates depend on the local body and may take 2–4 weeks.

### 2. Can I check PMAY status without an application reference number?

Yes. You can search using your Aadhaar number, or by name, father's name, and mobile number on the PMAY-U portal. For PMAY-G, search by registration number or through the beneficiary list.

### 3. My PMAY status shows "Rejected." What should I do?

Check the rejection reason displayed on the portal. Common reasons include income mismatch, existing house ownership, or incomplete documents. See our [PMAY rejection fix guide](/guide/pmay-application-rejected-fix) for step-by-step solutions.

### 4. How do I check if PMAY subsidy has been credited to my loan account?

Check your home loan account statement with the bank. The subsidy appears as a lump-sum credit that reduces your outstanding principal. You can also ask your bank's PMAY cell for status.

### 5. Is there a mobile app to check PMAY status?

Yes. For PMAY-Gramin, use the **AwaasApp** (official app by Ministry of Rural Development). For PMAY-Urban, use the official PMAY-U app available on Play Store.

### 6. I applied through CSC but my status is not showing. What should I do?

Visit the same CSC with your receipt and ask them to verify the submission. If the CSC operator did not complete the submission, you may need to reapply. Always collect the application reference number at the time of submission.
