# Singapore Visa from India 2026 — Complete Guide

Singapore is one of the easiest visa processes for Indians — fast online application, 3–5 day processing, and in some cases no visa needed at all if you hold a valid US/UK/Schengen/Australia visa. Here's everything you need to know.

---

## Do You Need a Visa? — Key Exceptions First

**Visa NOT required for Singapore if you hold:**
- Valid US visa (B1/B2 or any non-immigrant US visa) — can enter Singapore visa-free for 30 days
- Valid UK visa — visa-free for 30 days
- Valid Schengen visa — visa-free for 30 days
- Valid Australian visa — visa-free for 30 days
- Valid Canadian visa — visa-free for 30 days

**Condition**: The above visa must be valid (not expired) and you must enter Singapore as a genuine tourist.

**Indian passport holders without the above visas**: You need a Singapore tourist visa.

---

## Singapore Visa Types

| Type | Purpose | Stay |
|------|---------|------|
| **Tourist/Social Visit** | Tourism, family visit, short business | 30 days (extendable) |
| **Multiple Journey Visa** | Frequent travelers | 30 days per entry, 5-year validity |
| **Transit Visa** | Layover | 96 hours (usually not needed for Indians on transit) |
| **Long-Term Visit Pass** | Family member of Singapore PR/Citizen | 1–2 years |

**Most applicants need**: Tourist/Social Visit Visa (also called Social Visit Visa or SVP).

## Singapore Visa Fee

| Type | Fee |
|------|-----|
| Tourist visa (single/multiple journey) | SGD 30 (₹1,800–1,900 approx) |
| IVAC (Indian Visa Application Centre) service charge | ₹800–1,200 |

**Singapore visa is among the cheapest for Indian applicants.** Total cost: ~₹2,500–3,100.

## How to Apply — Three Routes

### Route 1: Online via Accredited Agents (Recommended)

Singapore visa for Indian nationals must be submitted through an **accredited agent** or the **Indian Visa Application Centre (IVAC)**. You cannot apply directly to ICA as an individual.

**Accredited agents**: Travel agencies registered with Singapore's High Commission. Examples: Thomas Cook, SOTC, Cox & Kings, MakeMyTrip (visa services section), local travel agents.

**Process**: Submit documents to agent → agent submits to IVAC → IVAC forwards to ICA → decision in 3–5 days.

### Route 2: IVAC (Indian Visa Application Centre)

**IVAC centres**: Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Ahmedabad.

Walk in or book appointment at IVAC → submit documents → pay fee → wait for decision.

### Route 3: Self-Application via SG Government Portal (limited cases)

Some categories can apply at [eservices.ica.gov.sg](https://eservices.ica.gov.sg/esvclandingpage/save) directly — but this is mainly for those with Singapore sponsors. Tourists typically go through accredited agents or IVAC.

## Documents Required

### Mandatory Documents

| Document | Details |
|----------|---------|
| **Passport** | Valid for 6+ months after return; at least 2 blank pages |
| **Visa application form** | Filled (agent or IVAC provides) |
| **Photo** | 35×45mm, white background, recent |
| **Return flight itinerary** | Round-trip booking (refundable or firm) |
| **Hotel bookings** | All accommodation in Singapore |

### Financial Documents

| Document | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| **Bank statements (last 3 months)** | Show sufficient funds; SGD 1,000 minimum visible is typical benchmark |
| **Payslips (last 3 months)** | Income confirmation |
| **ITR or Form 16** | Annual income proof |

### Employment/Status Documents

| Type | Documents |
|------|----------|
| **Salaried** | Employment letter (confirming role, salary, leave approved, will return to job) |
| **Business owner** | Business registration, GSTIN, income proof |
| **Student** | Enrollment letter from college; parents' financial documents |

## Processing Time and Collection

- Standard: **3–5 working days** from submission
- Urgent processing: Not officially offered; agent may have faster routes
- Collection: Passport returned to IVAC/agent; collect or request courier

**No biometrics required** for Singapore tourist visa.

## If You Have a US/UK/Schengen Visa — Singapore Visa on Arrival (STVP)

If you hold one of the qualifying visas:
1. At Singapore Changi Airport, proceed to immigration
2. You'll receive a **Social Visit Pass** stamp in your passport — typically 30 days
3. No application, no fee, no prior approval needed

**Conditions**:
- Qualifying visa must be **valid** (not expired) and **issued for at least a year**
- You must be entering as a genuine tourist, not for work

This is the easiest way for Indians who travel internationally — often no separate Singapore visa needed.

---

## Singapore Entry Rules — What You Should Know

**MOM (Ministry of Manpower) regulations**:
- Cannot work in Singapore on a tourist/social visit pass
- Cannot conduct any business activity in Singapore (though attending a conference is generally okay)
- Must have sufficient funds and return ticket

**Extension**: Can apply for 30-day extension at ICA Building in Singapore before your pass expires. Cost: SGD 40. Not guaranteed.

## What to Expect on Arrival

- Singapore immigration is strict but efficient
- Common questions: purpose of visit, where staying, how much money you have
- Bring hotel booking and return ticket printouts — they may ask
- Be truthful and concise in immigration responses

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I have a valid US tourist visa. Do I need to apply separately for Singapore?

If your US B1/B2 visa is valid (not expired), you can enter Singapore visa-free for up to 30 days as a tourist. No separate application or fee needed.

### Can I apply for Singapore visa from any IVAC city?

Yes — you can apply at any IVAC city regardless of your state of residence. Choose the most convenient IVAC to you.

### Is travel insurance required for Singapore visa?

Not officially mandatory, but highly recommended — Singapore's medical costs are very high without insurance.

### My previous Singapore visa application was rejected. Can I reapply?

Yes — there's no mandatory wait. Address the reason for rejection (usually insufficient financial proof or incomplete documentation) and reapply.

### Can I stay more than 30 days in Singapore?

On a tourist visa, initial entry is 30 days. You can request a 30-day extension at ICA Building (Lavender area) in Singapore. For longer stays, you need a different visa type (LTVP, etc.).
