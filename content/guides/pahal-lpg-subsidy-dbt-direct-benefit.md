## What is PAHAL (DBTL)?

**PAHAL (Pratyaksh Hastaantarit Laabh)** — also called **DBTL (Direct Benefit Transfer for LPG)** — is the Government of India scheme where the **LPG cooking gas subsidy is transferred directly to your Aadhaar-linked bank account** instead of being given as a discount on the cylinder price.

How it works:
1. You pay the **market price** (non-subsidised price) for the LPG cylinder when you book/deliver
2. Within a few days, the **subsidy amount is credited to your bank account** via DBT
3. The net cost to you is: **Market price minus subsidy** = subsidised price

## Current LPG Subsidy Status (2024)

> **Important:** The government reduced LPG subsidies for domestic consumers significantly from 2023–2024. In many periods, the subsidy transferred is **₹0 or very small** for general consumers. However, the PM Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY) beneficiaries continue to receive **higher subsidies** (₹200–300 per cylinder in 2024).

Check the current subsidy at [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in) or your oil company's portal.

## How to Activate PAHAL / Link Aadhaar to LPG

If you are not receiving any subsidy in your bank account, first check if your Aadhaar is properly linked to both your LPG connection and your bank account:

### Step 1: Link Aadhaar to Your LPG Connection

**Online:**
1. Go to your LPG company's portal:
   - **Indane (IOCL):** [iocl.com](https://www.iocl.com) or [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in)
   - **Bharat Gas (BPCL):** [ebharatgas.com](https://www.ebharatgas.com)
   - **HP Gas (HPCL):** [hindustanpetroleum.com](https://www.hindustanpetroleum.com)
2. Log in with your **Consumer Number**
3. Go to **"PAHAL / DBTL"** or **"Link Aadhaar"**
4. Enter your Aadhaar number
5. Verify with OTP

**At LPG Distributor:**
- Visit your nearest LPG distributor
- Fill the PAHAL enrollment form
- Submit your Aadhaar copy
- They will link your Aadhaar to your LPG connection

### Step 2: Link Aadhaar to Your Bank Account

Your bank account must be **Aadhaar-linked** (NPCI mapper seeded) to receive DBT:
1. Visit your bank branch
2. Submit Aadhaar copy with account details
3. Or update via net banking → Aadhaar seeding section

### Step 3: Verify Both Links Are Active

Check at [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in):
1. Enter your Consumer Number
2. Check "PAHAL Status" — should show "Active" for Aadhaar link
3. Check bank account linked for DBT

## Check LPG Subsidy Status Online

1. Go to **[mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in)**
2. Enter your **LPG Consumer Number** and registered mobile
3. View subsidy history — amount transferred per booking
4. Or check your **bank account statement** — subsidy appears as "LPG SUBSIDY" or your oil company name

## Why is Subsidy ₹0 or Not Coming?

Common reasons:
1. **Aadhaar not linked** to LPG or bank account
2. **Subsidy suspended** — if annual income of family member is above ₹10 lakh (under "GiveItUp" campaign)
3. **Government subsidy rate is ₹0** for the period (market price below "subsidised" rate)
4. **New connection** — first 2–3 deliveries may not have subsidy while PAHAL activation completes
5. **Bank account mismatch** — wrong account number linked

## GiveItUp — Voluntary Subsidy Surrender

If any earning member of your household earns above ₹10 lakh/year or pays income tax, they should voluntarily **surrender LPG subsidy** ("GiveItUp"). You can check if your subsidy is suspended due to this at [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in).

If your subsidy was mistakenly suspended, you can request reinstatement via the portal.

## PM Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY) — Higher Subsidy for BPL Women

If you received your LPG connection under **PM Ujjwala Yojana** (free connection for BPL women), you may be eligible for **additional subsidy** of ₹200–300 per cylinder. Check at [pmuy.gov.in](https://pmuy.gov.in) or your distributor.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many days after delivery does the subsidy arrive?
Typically **3–7 working days** after the cylinder is delivered. Check your bank account or [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in) transaction history.

### I booked a cylinder 2 weeks ago but no subsidy. What to do?
1. Check [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in) for PAHAL status
2. Ensure Aadhaar is linked to both LPG and bank
3. Contact your LPG distributor
4. Call your oil company helpline:
   - Indane (IOCL): 1800-233-3555
   - Bharat Gas (BPCL): 1800-22-4344
   - HP Gas (HPCL): 1800-233-3555

### My family has 2 LPG connections (different names). Do both get subsidy?
Only **one subsidised connection per family address** is allowed. The second connection at the same address may not receive subsidy. Surrender the extra connection if you don't use both.

### Can I transfer subsidy to another bank account?
Yes — you can change the linked bank account at [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in) or your distributor. Allow 2–3 cycles for the change to activate.
