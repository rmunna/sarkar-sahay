## What is UP Free Laptop Yojana?

**UP Free Laptop Yojana** (also called **UP Digi Shakti Yojana** or **UP Free Tablet Scheme**) is the Uttar Pradesh government's scheme to provide **free laptops and tablets** to meritorious students to bridge the digital divide. The scheme provides laptops to students who have passed their board exams with good marks or are enrolled in higher education.

The UP government announced distribution of **1 crore tablets + smartphones** to students under the **UP Digi Shakti scheme** — one of the largest digital device distribution drives in India.

## Latest Scheme Details (2024)

| Programme | Beneficiaries |
|---|---|
| **UP Digi Shakti (Tablet/Smartphone)** | Class 10 pass, Class 12 pass, undergraduate/postgraduate students |
| **Free Laptop (Meritorious)** | High-scoring board students (70%+ in Class 10/12) |

> **Note:** The scheme has multiple phases and announcements. Always verify the latest status at [digishaktiup.in](https://digishaktiup.in) or [upcmo.up.nic.in](https://upcmo.up.nic.in) — as distribution is announced in batches.

## Who is Eligible?

**UP Digi Shakti Tablet/Smartphone:**
- Students who have passed **Class 10 or 12** from a UP Board school
- Students enrolled in **undergraduate or postgraduate** courses in UP government colleges and universities
- Must be a **Uttar Pradesh resident**
- Students studying in government colleges, ITIs, polytechnics, and skill development institutes

**Free Laptop (Merit-based):**
- Students scoring **70% or above** in Class 10 or 12 UP Board exams
- Also includes students passing B.Tech, MBBS, BBA, BCA from state institutions

## How to Apply / Register

### Step 1: Check if Your Institution is Registered

1. Go to **[digishaktiup.in](https://digishaktiup.in)**
2. Your **college/university registers students** — individual students don't typically apply directly
3. Check if your institution has uploaded your data on the Digi Shakti portal

### Step 2: Student Registration

1. Go to **[digishaktiup.in](https://digishaktiup.in)**
2. Click **"Student Registration"** (if available)
3. Enter your name, date of birth, roll number, institution details
4. Your eligibility is checked against the marks data

### Step 3: Distribution

- Tablets/laptops are distributed at **college/district camps**
- The government announces **distribution schedules** district-wise
- Students receive SMS/notification when their district's camp is scheduled
- Bring your **admit card / marksheet** and **Aadhaar card** to collect the device

## Check Application/Distribution Status

1. Go to **[digishaktiup.in](https://digishaktiup.in)**
2. Enter your **roll number and date of birth**
3. View if you are in the beneficiary list and when distribution is scheduled

OR go to **[upcmo.up.nic.in](https://upcmo.up.nic.in)** for UP government scheme updates.

## Documents Required at Distribution Camp

1. **Aadhaar card**
2. **Class 10/12 marksheet** (or college enrollment proof)
3. **College ID card**
4. Passport-size photograph

## What Device Do You Get?

The UP Digi Shakti scheme provides:
- **Tablet** with 4G SIM capability
- Some batches: **Smartphone** instead of tablet
- Free **internet data** for 6 months–1 year (via government BSNL SIM in some batches)

The specific model and brand varies by the batch and procurement round.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I passed Class 12 in 2022. Am I eligible for the 2024 distribution?
Eligibility is based on the **year of passing** and scheme phase. Check at [digishaktiup.in](https://digishaktiup.in) with your roll number. Earlier batches may have already covered 2022 passouts.

### I study at a private college. Will I get a free laptop?
UP Digi Shakti is primarily for **government colleges and universities** and recognised state institutions. Students at private colleges may not be covered. Check the official notification for the current scheme.

### I scored 65% in Class 12. Am I eligible?
For the merit-based laptop (70%+), you may not qualify. However, the tablet scheme under Digi Shakti has broader coverage — check your eligibility at [digishaktiup.in](https://digishaktiup.in).

### How will I know when distribution is in my district?
Subscribe to SMS alerts via the Digi Shakti portal. Also follow local news and your college's notices — distribution dates are announced with advance notice.

### Can I sell the free laptop/tablet?
The devices distributed under government schemes are meant for educational use. Selling government-provided devices is discouraged and may be against scheme terms. Additionally, devices may be registered with government tracking.

### I received the tablet but the screen is broken after 1 month. What do I do?
Contact the **helpdesk at digishaktiup.in** or the district education office. There may be a warranty process for device defects.
