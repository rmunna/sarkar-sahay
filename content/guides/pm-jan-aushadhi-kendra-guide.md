# PM Jan Aushadhi Kendra: Find Cheap Medicine Stores Near You

Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Pariyojana (PMBJP) is a Government of India initiative that provides quality generic medicines at affordable prices — **50 to 90% cheaper** than branded equivalents. These medicines are sold through dedicated stores called **Jan Aushadhi Kendras** across the country.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent platform and is not affiliated with the Government of India or PMBJP. For official details, visit [janaushadhi.gov.in](https://janaushadhi.gov.in/).

## What is PM Jan Aushadhi Kendra?

- A government-backed scheme to make **quality generic medicines** affordable for all
- Medicines sold at **50-90% less** than branded equivalents
- Managed by the **Bureau of Pharma PSUs of India (BPPI)** under the Department of Pharmaceuticals
- Over **12,000+ Jan Aushadhi Kendras** operational across India
- Product range includes **1,900+ medicines** and **290+ surgical/medical devices**
- All medicines meet the same quality standards as branded medicines (WHO-GMP certified)

## How to Find Jan Aushadhi Kendra Near You

### Method 1: Online Store Locator

1. Visit [janaushadhi.gov.in](https://janaushadhi.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"Store Locator"** or **"Find a Store"**
3. Select your **State**, **District**, and **City/Town**
4. The portal will show a list of all Jan Aushadhi Kendras in your area with address and contact details

### Method 2: Jan Aushadhi Sugam App

1. Download the **"Jan Aushadhi Sugam"** app from [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store) or Apple App Store
2. Open the app and allow **location access**
3. The app shows **nearby Jan Aushadhi Kendras** on a map
4. You can also search for specific medicines and check their availability and price

### Method 3: Call the Helpline

- Call the toll-free helpline: **1800 267 2700**
- Ask for the nearest Jan Aushadhi Kendra in your area

## What Medicines Are Available?

Jan Aushadhi Kendras stock medicines across all major therapeutic categories:

- **Cardiovascular** — blood pressure, cholesterol, heart medicines
- **Anti-diabetic** — metformin, glimepiride, insulin (select stores)
- **Antibiotics** — amoxicillin, azithromycin, ciprofloxacin
- **Pain relief** — paracetamol, ibuprofen, diclofenac
- **Gastro-intestinal** — pantoprazole, ranitidine, ORS
- **Women's health** — iron-folic acid, sanitary napkins (Suvidha brand at ₹1/pad)
- **Surgical items** — masks, gloves, syringes, bandages
- **Nutritional supplements** — calcium, multivitamins, protein powder

**Note:** You need a **valid prescription** for prescription medicines, just like any other pharmacy.

## How to Open a Jan Aushadhi Kendra

If you're interested in opening a Jan Aushadhi Kendra as a business, here are the details:

### Eligibility

| Criteria | Details |
|----------|---------|
| Who can apply | Individual pharmacist, doctor, NGO, trust, society, cooperative, hospital, government institution |
| Pharmacist requirement | Must employ a registered pharmacist (B.Pharm / D.Pharm) |
| Space required | Minimum 120 sq ft shop area |
| Investment | ₹1.5-2 lakh (approximate initial stock + setup) |

### How to Apply

1. Visit [pmbjp.org](https://www.pmbjp.org/) or [janaushadhi.gov.in](https://janaushadhi.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"Apply for Kendra"** or **"Open a Store"**
3. Register with your details and upload documents:
   - Aadhaar card
   - PAN card
   - Drug license (or application for one)
   - Pharmacist registration certificate
   - Shop/premises proof
4. Submit the application online
5. BPPI will review and approve based on area feasibility
6. Once approved, you receive a **Kendra ID** and can order medicines from BPPI warehouse

### Government Incentives for Kendra Owners

| Incentive | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Trade margin | Up to 20% on MRP for Kendra owner |
| Furniture allowance | Up to ₹2 lakh reimbursement (for SC/ST/women/divyang/NE states) |
| Normal area incentive | Up to ₹50,000 one-time assistance |
| Special category incentive | Up to ₹2 lakh for SC/ST, women entrepreneurs, divyang, and North-East states |

## Important Tips

1. **Download the Jan Aushadhi Sugam app** to search for nearby stores and check medicine prices before visiting
2. **Carry your doctor's prescription** — prescription medicines require a valid prescription, just like branded pharmacies
3. **Generic medicines are equally effective** — they contain the same active ingredients and meet WHO-GMP quality standards
4. **Ask your doctor for generic names** — request prescriptions with generic drug names instead of brand names
5. **Check medicine expiry dates** at the time of purchase, as with any pharmacy

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are Jan Aushadhi medicines safe and effective?
Yes, all medicines sold at Jan Aushadhi Kendras are generic equivalents of branded medicines. They are manufactured by WHO-GMP certified companies and undergo quality testing by NABL-accredited labs.

### How much cheaper are Jan Aushadhi medicines?
Jan Aushadhi medicines are 50 to 90% cheaper than their branded equivalents. The savings depend on the specific medicine.

### Do I need a prescription to buy from Jan Aushadhi Kendra?
Over-the-counter (OTC) medicines like paracetamol can be bought without prescription. Prescription medicines (antibiotics, cardiac drugs, etc.) require a valid doctor's prescription.

### How many Jan Aushadhi Kendras are there in India?
As of 2025, there are over 12,000 Jan Aushadhi Kendras operational across all states and union territories of India.

### Can I order Jan Aushadhi medicines online?
Currently, the scheme primarily works through physical stores. Some Kendras may offer local delivery. Check the Sugam app or contact your nearest Kendra for home delivery options.

### How much can I earn by opening a Jan Aushadhi Kendra?
Kendra owners get a trade margin of up to 20% on MRP. Earnings depend on location, footfall, and medicine sales. An average Kendra can earn ₹15,000-50,000 per month.

### Where can I get the complete medicine list?
The complete product list with prices is available on the [PMBJP website](https://www.pmbjp.org/) and the Jan Aushadhi Sugam mobile app.
