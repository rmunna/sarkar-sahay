# Maximize Credit Card Rewards India 2026 — Strategy Guide

Most credit card users earn rewards inefficiently — splitting spend across many cards, letting points expire, or holding cards that don't match their spending pattern. Here's how to extract maximum value.

---

## The Core Principle: Concentration Over Diversification

Spreading ₹1L monthly spend across 4 cards = weak rewards on each.  
**Concentrating** ₹1L on 1–2 high-reward cards = significant accumulated value.

**Target**: 3–5% effective return on your credit card spend.

## Reward Points — Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Impact | Fix |
|---------|--------|-----|
| **Not checking expiry** | Points expire in 2–3 years | Set calendar reminder; check monthly card statement |
| **Redeeming for cash/statement credit** | 50–70% value loss vs travel | Redeem for flight/hotel vouchers |
| **Paying annual fee unnecessarily** | ₹500–12,500 wasted | Ensure spend threshold met; cancel if not |
| **Using credit card for cash advance** | 3–4% charge + high interest, NO reward points | Never use for cash advance |
| **Paying minimum due** | 30–42% annualized interest on balance | Always pay full outstanding by due date |
| **Missing payment due date** | 2–3% of bill as penalty + CIBIL hit | Set auto-pay for minimum (safety); pay full manually |

## Tax on Credit Card Rewards

Credit card rewards (cashback, reward points redeemed as gift vouchers) are **generally not taxable** in India under current rules — they're treated as a discount/rebate, not income.

However:
- If you receive large cash rewards through a credit card referral bonus (₹5,000+), it may be treated as "income from other sources" technically — gray area.
- Business credit card rewards may need to be shown as business income if used for tax purposes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I pay credit card bills from a rewards credit card?

No — most banks don't give reward points on credit card bill payment (it would be circular). Pay your bills via bank debit/net banking, not via another credit card.

### Does closing an old credit card hurt CIBIL?

Closing an old card can reduce your credit history length and increase your credit utilization ratio (both reduce score). Keep old zero-fee cards open with minimal activity. Close high-fee cards only if you're not using them.

### Can I transfer reward points from one card to another?

Generally no — reward points are locked to the issuing card. Exception: some bank ecosystems allow pooling (HDFC SmartBuy allows using Infinia + Regalia points together for flight bookings). Miles programs (like Axis EDGE Miles or SBI Air India card) can transfer to airline miles accounts.

### Which card for international spends abroad?

Standard credit cards charge 2–3.5% foreign transaction fee. Choose zero-forex cards:
- **Niyo Global** (zero forex, prepaid)
- **HDFC Regalia** (waived forex for LRS compliance)
- **SBI Elite** (lower forex markup)

Or use Wise card for fixed-rate international transactions.
