# PM Mudra Loan Yojana 2025 – Complete Guide

## What is PM Mudra Yojana?

Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY) is a flagship scheme launched by the Government of India in April 2015 to provide collateral-free loans up to ₹10 lakh to micro and small enterprises. The scheme is managed by Micro Units Development & Refinance Agency Ltd (MUDRA), a subsidiary of SIDBI.

The goal is to fund the unfunded — supporting small business owners, street vendors, artisans, and entrepreneurs who lack access to formal banking credit. Since its launch, PMMY has disbursed over ₹27 lakh crore to more than 47 crore loan accounts.

## Mudra Loan Categories

PMMY offers three categories of loans based on the stage of business:

| Category | Loan Amount | Target |
|----------|-------------|--------|
| **Shishu** | Up to ₹50,000 | New/startup businesses |
| **Kishore** | ₹50,001 to ₹5,00,000 | Businesses seeking expansion |
| **Tarun** | ₹5,00,001 to ₹10,00,000 | Well-established businesses needing growth capital |

## Who is Eligible?

You can apply for a Mudra loan if you meet the following criteria:

- **Indian citizen** with a valid identity proof
- **Non-corporate, non-farm** small/micro enterprise
- Business engaged in **manufacturing, trading, services, or allied activities**
- Existing businesses or new startups
- **No collateral required** — loans are unsecured
- Individuals, proprietorship firms, partnerships, LLPs, private limited companies, and self-help groups can apply
- **No minimum income requirement**

### Who Cannot Apply?
- Large enterprises with turnover above ₹5 crore
- Businesses already classified as NPA with any bank
- Agricultural activities directly (allied agri-activities like food processing are eligible)

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Online Application

1. **Visit** the Udyami Mitra portal at [udyamimitra.in](https://www.udyamimitra.in)
2. **Register** with your mobile number and email ID
3. **Select** "MUDRA Loans" from the loan products
4. **Fill** the application form with personal and business details
5. **Upload** required documents (scanned copies)
6. **Choose** your preferred bank/lender from the list
7. **Submit** the application — you'll receive a reference number
8. **Track** your application status on the portal using the reference number
9. Bank will **verify** documents and may schedule a visit
10. Upon approval, **loan amount is disbursed** to your bank account or as Mudra Card

You can also apply via **PSB Loans in 59 Minutes** portal at [psbloansin59minutes.com](https://www.psbloansin59minutes.com) for faster processing.

### Offline Application

1. **Visit** your nearest bank branch (any public sector bank, private bank, RRB, MFI, or NBFC)
2. **Collect** the Mudra loan application form
3. **Fill** the form with accurate details
4. **Attach** all required documents
5. **Submit** to the branch manager
6. Bank will **process** and verify within 7-14 working days
7. Upon approval, funds are disbursed or a **Mudra Card** (RuPay debit card) is issued

## Fees and Charges

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Processing fee | 0% to 0.50% of loan amount (varies by bank) |
| Interest rate | 7.30% to 12% per annum (varies by bank and category) |
| Collateral | Not required |
| Prepayment penalty | Nil |
| Mudra Card fee | Free (RuPay debit card for working capital) |

> **Note:** Interest rates are not fixed by the government. Each lending institution sets its own rates. Compare rates across banks before applying.

## Processing Time

- **Online applications:** 7-14 working days
- **Offline applications:** 14-30 working days
- **PSB Loans in 59 Minutes:** In-principle approval within 59 minutes; disbursement in 7-8 working days

## Mudra Card – Working Capital on the Go

Approved borrowers receive a **Mudra Card** — a RuPay debit card linked to a working capital loan account. You can:

- Withdraw funds as needed from ATMs
- Make purchases using the card
- Pay interest only on the amount withdrawn
- Revolve credit within the sanctioned limit

## Important Tips

1. **Prepare a solid business plan** — Kishore and Tarun loans require a detailed project report. Banks assess viability before approving.
2. **Maintain a good credit score** — While not mandatory, a CIBIL score above 650 improves your chances significantly.
3. **Compare across lenders** — Interest rates vary from 7.30% to 12%. Check multiple banks and NBFCs before finalizing.
4. **Use the Mudra Card wisely** — It's a revolving credit facility. Only withdraw what you need to minimize interest outgo.
5. **Keep all documents ready** — Incomplete applications are the #1 reason for delays and rejections.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Can I get a Mudra loan without any collateral?
**Yes.** All Mudra loans (Shishu, Kishore, and Tarun) are collateral-free. No security deposit or guarantee is required from the borrower.

### Q2: What is the interest rate on Mudra loans?
Interest rates are **not fixed** by the government. They typically range from **7.30% to 12% per annum** depending on the bank, loan category, and your credit profile. Shishu loans generally have lower rates.

### Q3: Can I apply for a Mudra loan if I already have another loan?
**Yes**, you can apply for a Mudra loan even if you have an existing loan, provided you are not a defaulter (NPA) with any bank. Your repayment capacity will be assessed.

### Q4: How is a Mudra loan different from a regular business loan?
Mudra loans are specifically designed for micro/small businesses with **no collateral requirement**, **government backing**, and **lower interest rates**. Regular business loans may require collateral and charge higher rates.

### Q5: Can women entrepreneurs get special benefits under PMMY?
**Yes.** Women borrowers often get **preferential interest rates** (0.25% to 0.50% lower) from many banks. Over 70% of Mudra loan accounts belong to women entrepreneurs.

### Q6: What happens if I cannot repay my Mudra loan?
Like any bank loan, non-repayment affects your **credit score** and the bank can initiate recovery proceedings. Contact your bank early if you face difficulties — restructuring options may be available.

### Q7: Can I use a Mudra loan to start a new business?
**Yes.** The Shishu category (up to ₹50,000) is specifically designed for new business startups. You'll need a basic business plan showing how you intend to use the funds.
