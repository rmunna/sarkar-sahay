# Kalaignar Magalir Urimai Thogai Payment Not Received? Fix Guide

The **Kalaignar Magalir Urimai Thogai** scheme gives ₹1,000/month to women who are the heads of their household in Tamil Nadu. If your payment hasn't arrived, here's how to track it and fix the issue.

> **Quick fix**: Check your bank account — payments come from "PFMS" or "Tamil Nadu Gov" around the 18th of each month. If not visible, verify your bank account is the same one registered with your family card.

## Check Payment Status Online

### cms.tn.gov.in — Official Portal

1. Visit **[cms.tn.gov.in](https://cms.tn.gov.in/)**
2. Click on **"Magalir Urimai Thogai"** → **"Beneficiary Status"** or **"Payment Status"**
3. Enter your **Family Card Number** (பகுதி அட்டை எண்) or **Aadhaar number**
4. Enter the captcha and submit
5. View: Registration status, Payment approved/rejected, Bank account details, Payment date

### Via PFMS — Track DBT Payment

1. Visit **[pfms.nic.in](https://pfms.nic.in/)** → **"Know Your Payment"**
2. Enter your bank account number
3. See all DBT payments credited (Tamil Nadu government payments appear as "TN-GOV")

## Common Reasons Payment Is Delayed or Missing

| Reason | How to Fix |
|--------|-----------|
| **Bank account not linked to Aadhaar (NPCI mapper)** | Visit bank branch; request "Aadhaar seeding to NPCI mapper" |
| **Bank account inactive/dormant** | Visit bank; transact to reactivate; ensure minimum balance |
| **Wrong bank account on record** | Visit your nearest Collector office or Taluk office to update |
| **eKYC/verification pending** | Visit Aadhaar Seva Kendra or district ration office |
| **Name mismatch (Aadhaar vs family card)** | Correct via CM cell or district revenue office |
| **Already a household head under another scheme** | May be disqualified if another member is registered as head |
| **Payment held for government audit** | Wait for resolution; no action needed from you |

## Fix 1: Aadhaar Not Linked to Bank Account

The most common reason — DBT payments go to NPCI-mapped bank account. If your Aadhaar isn't correctly seeded:

1. Visit your **bank branch** with Aadhaar card + bank passbook
2. Request: **"Aadhaar seeding / Aadhaar bank link for DBT"**
3. Bank updates NPCI mapper within 24–48 hours
4. Payment of pending months typically credited in next payment cycle

## Fix 2: Bank Account Changed But Record Not Updated

If you closed your old bank account or changed banks after enrollment:

1. Visit your nearest **Taluk office or Revenue office** with:
   - Old and new bank passbook
   - Family Card
   - Aadhaar card
2. Request bank account update for Magalir Urimai Thogai
3. Or visit the nearest **CM Cell** (Chief Minister's Cell) at district headquarters

Some districts also allow bank update at the **e-Sevai centre** (Tamil Nadu government service centres).

## Fix 3: Account Inactive or Insufficient KYC

If your bank account is dormant (no transactions for 2+ years), banks freeze it:

1. Visit bank branch and request **account reactivation**
2. Make a small transaction (deposit ₹100 or withdraw)
3. Complete **KYC update** if bank requests it (Aadhaar + PAN/mobile)
4. After reactivation, the next payment cycle will credit your account

## Fix 4: Name Mismatch Between Aadhaar and Family Card

If your name in Aadhaar is spelled differently from your Family Card (e.g., "Malathi" vs "Malasi"):

1. Visit the **Taluk Revenue Office** or **Block Development Office**
2. Request name correction or linkage with supporting documents
3. Or file through the CM Cell grievance portal

## Fix 5: "Not Eligible" Despite Meeting Criteria

Check eligibility exclusions — you may be disqualified if:
- A male member in the family is already registered as the "head of household" with government
- You receive income from government salary or pension above certain threshold
- Another female member of the same household is already registered under the scheme

Visit nearest **block office or taluk office** to understand the specific reason.

## Payment Schedule

- Payments are typically made on or around the **15th–18th of each month**
- Delays of 5–10 days are common and not unusual
- **Public holidays** or audit months may cause delays of 15–20 days

If payment hasn't arrived by the **25th of the month**, take action.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I applied 3 months ago but haven't received any payment. What's wrong?

New applications take 2–3 months to process. If you applied more than 3 months ago, check your status at cms.tn.gov.in. If the status shows "Approved" but payment hasn't come, contact the helpline with your approval reference number.

### Can I get payments for months I missed due to bank issues?

Generally yes — missed payments due to bank/Aadhaar issues are credited when the issue is resolved, either as a lump sum or over the next 2–3 months. Confirm with your taluk office.

### My family card has my mother's name as head of household. Can I change it to mine?

Yes, but only if she is no longer the head (deceased or has moved households). Contact taluk office with death certificate or relevant documents. Only one person per household can be registered.

### Is there a deadline to apply for Magalir Urimai Thogai?

The scheme is ongoing — applications are accepted throughout the year. New beneficiaries are added to the system quarterly. There is no annual application deadline.

### The scheme gave ₹1,000 initially. Has it increased?

As of 2026, the amount remains ₹1,000/month. Government has announced plans to increase it — verify at cms.tn.gov.in for current amount.
