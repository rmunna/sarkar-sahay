## What is Deen Dayal Lado Lakshmi Yojana?

The **Deen Dayal Lado Lakshmi Yojana** is a flagship scheme by the **Government of Haryana** aimed at empowering the girl child and promoting their education and well-being. Under this scheme, families meeting the eligibility criteria receive financial assistance for girl children born in the state.

The scheme provides a savings bond or financial benefit that matures at different stages of the girl's life — covering education, health, and marriage-related expenses. It was introduced to improve the child sex ratio in Haryana and ensure that every girl child gets equal opportunities.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Residency:** Parents must be permanent residents of Haryana
2. **Income:** Annual family income should be within the prescribed BPL/low-income category
3. **Birth Registration:** The girl child must be born in Haryana and registered at the local authority
4. **Family Size:** Benefits typically apply to the first two girl children in a family
5. **Hospital Delivery:** Institutional delivery (hospital/government health centre) is preferred

## Benefits

- Financial assistance through savings bond or installments at key milestones
- Payments linked to school enrollment, completion of secondary education, and higher education
- Support for marriage expenses upon reaching legal marriageable age
- Encourages families to educate and nurture girl children

## Documents Required

### For Parents
- Aadhaar Card of both parents
- Domicile Certificate / Parivar Pehchan Patra (Family ID)
- Income Certificate (from Tehsildar or via Saral Haryana)
- BPL Card (if applicable)

### For Girl Child
- Birth Certificate of the girl child
- Aadhaar Card (if available)

### Bank & Other
- Bank account details (mother's or girl child's joint account preferred)
- Passport-size photographs
- Caste Certificate (if applicable for additional benefits)

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Online Application (Saral Haryana Portal)

1. **Visit** [saralharyana.gov.in](https://saralharyana.gov.in)
2. **Register** or log in with your credentials
3. **Search** for "Lado Lakshmi Yojana" or navigate to Women & Child Development schemes
4. **Fill** the application form with family details, girl child's birth information, and bank account
5. **Upload** scanned documents (Birth Certificate, Aadhaar, Income Certificate)
6. **Submit** the form and save the application ID
7. **Track** status online using the application ID

### Offline Application

1. **Visit** the nearest **Anganwadi Centre** or **Women & Child Development Office**
2. **Collect** the application form
3. **Fill** the form and attach all required documents
4. **Submit** to the concerned officer
5. **Receive** acknowledgement receipt

## Processing Time

- **Application Review:** 30-60 days
- **Verification:** Field verification by Anganwadi worker or block officer
- **Approval & Disbursement:** Benefits begin after successful verification

## Important Tips

1. **Parivar Pehchan Patra:** Ensure your family is registered on the Haryana Family ID system — it is mandatory for most Haryana schemes
2. **Institutional Delivery:** Hospital birth improves eligibility chances
3. **Keep Documents Updated:** Ensure Aadhaar and bank details are current
4. **Track Application:** Use Saral Haryana portal to track your application status
5. **Education Milestones:** Benefits are released at education milestones, so ensure the girl child is enrolled in school

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. What is the main benefit of Lado Lakshmi Yojana?
Financial assistance for families with girl children, disbursed at key life milestones including education and marriage.

### Q2. How many girl children are covered?
Typically the first two girl children in a family are eligible.

### Q3. Is there an income limit?
Yes, the scheme targets BPL and low-income families. Income criteria are verified through Parivar Pehchan Patra.

### Q4. Can I apply online?
Yes, applications can be submitted through [saralharyana.gov.in](https://saralharyana.gov.in) or at the local Anganwadi centre.

### Q5. Is Parivar Pehchan Patra mandatory?
Yes, the Haryana Family ID (Parivar Pehchan Patra) is required for almost all state government schemes.

### Q6. When do I receive the money?
Benefits are released in installments linked to the girl child's education milestones and age.

### Q7. Can I avail this along with Ladli Scheme?
Check with the WCD office — some benefits may be combinable while others may not.
