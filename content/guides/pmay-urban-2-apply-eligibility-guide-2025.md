# PMAY Urban 2.0 (2025–2030) — New Components, Higher Subsidy, How to Apply

**PMAY Urban 2.0** (Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana — Urban) is the second phase of India's flagship urban housing scheme, launched in **August 2024** with a budget of **₹2.30 lakh crore** to build **1 crore urban houses** by 2030.

> **Key difference from PMAY-U 1.0 (2015–2022)**: Higher subsidy (₹2.5L vs ₹1.5L for BLC), new Affordable Rental Housing component for migrant workers, and streamlined application process via Urban Local Bodies (ULBs).

## 4 Components of PMAY Urban 2.0

### 1. Beneficiary Led Construction (BLC) — For EWS Families Who Own Land

**Who**: Economically Weaker Section (EWS) families with annual income up to ₹3 lakh who already own land/plot.

**What**: ₹2.5 lakh central assistance to build a new house or upgrade an existing kutcha/semi-pucca house on their own land.

**How**: Apply at your ULB (Municipal Corporation/Nagar Panchayat) with land documents.

### 2. Affordable Housing in Partnership (AHP) — For EWS Without Land

**Who**: EWS families with annual income up to ₹3 lakh who do not own land.

**What**: State government or ULB builds houses; central government gives ₹2.5 lakh per unit. Families pay the remaining cost (subsidized rates).

**How**: Apply at your city's Urban Local Body or housing board.

### 3. Affordable Rental Housing (ARH) — New Component for Migrant Workers

**Who**: Migrant workers, urban poor, students, and low-income urban residents without permanent housing.

**What**: Government converts underutilized public housing into affordable rentals at below-market rates. Private sector also incentivized to build ARH.

**How**: Applications through the city's Rental Housing Authority or via your employer (for organized sector workers).

### 4. PM Awas Yojana Urban Interest Subsidy (PMAYU-IS) — For MIG Home Loans

**Who**: Low Income Group (LIG: income ₹3–6L/year) and Middle Income Group (MIG-I: income ₹6–9L; MIG-II: income ₹9–18L).

**What**: Interest subsidy on home loans:
- **LIG**: 4% subsidy on loans up to ₹8 lakh
- **MIG-I**: 4% subsidy on loans up to ₹12 lakh
- **MIG-II**: 3% subsidy on loans up to ₹20 lakh

**How**: Apply through your bank when taking a home loan. Bank submits to PMAY portal.

## Eligibility Summary

| Category | Income Limit | House Size Limit | Component |
|----------|-------------|------------------|-----------|
| **EWS** | Up to ₹3L/year | Up to 30 sqm carpet area | BLC, AHP |
| **LIG** | ₹3L–₹6L/year | Up to 60 sqm carpet area | AHP, Interest Subsidy |
| **MIG-I** | ₹6L–₹9L/year | Up to 120 sqm carpet area | Interest Subsidy |
| **MIG-II** | ₹9L–₹18L/year | Up to 200 sqm carpet area | Interest Subsidy |

**Universal eligibility conditions**:
- Must not own a pucca house (you or your spouse) anywhere in India
- Not previously availed PMAY benefit (central housing scheme benefit)
- Valid Aadhaar number mandatory
- Female name as co-owner is preferred (for BLC and AHP)

## How to Apply for PMAY Urban 2.0

### Step 1: Check Eligibility

- Verify your household income falls within the applicable slab
- Confirm you or your spouse doesn't own a pucca house
- Check if your city/ULB is participating (most cities are; check at pmay-urban.gov.in)

### Step 2: Visit Your ULB Office or Apply Online

**Online**:
1. Visit **[pmay-urban.gov.in](https://pmay-urban.gov.in/)**
2. Click "Citizen" → "Apply for PMAY-U 2.0"
3. Login with Aadhaar-linked mobile OTP
4. Fill the application with household and land details

**Offline**:
1. Visit your **Municipal Corporation / Nagar Panchayat (ULB) office**
2. Ask for the PMAY-U 2.0 application form
3. Submit with documents; the ULB verifies and uploads to the national portal

### Step 3: Documents Required

- Aadhaar card (all adult family members)
- Income proof: Salary slip, Form 16, or self-declaration (for EWS/LIG)
- Land documents (for BLC component only)
- Bank account passbook (Aadhaar-linked)
- Passport-size photos
- Photograph of current house (kutcha/semi-pucca condition)
- Caste certificate (for SC/ST priority)

## Difference: PMAY-U 1.0 vs PMAY-U 2.0

| Feature | PMAY-U 1.0 (2015–2022) | PMAY-U 2.0 (2024–2030) |
|---------|------------------------|------------------------|
| **BLC Subsidy** | ₹1.5 lakh | ₹2.5 lakh |
| **Portal** | pmaymis.gov.in | pmay-urban.gov.in (new) |
| **ARH Component** | Not available | New — for migrant workers |
| **Interest Subsidy** | CLSS — ₹2.67L max | New structure (4% for LIG) |
| **Application** | City-wise camps | Continuous online + ULB |
| **Target** | 1.12 crore units | 1 crore new units |

> **If you applied under PMAY-U 1.0 and your application is still pending**: Your application migrates to PMAY-U 2.0 for processing. Contact your ULB for status.

## Common Questions

### Is the old PMAY-U portal (pmaymis.gov.in) still active?

The old portal is being phased out for new applications. **New applications** go through pmay-urban.gov.in. Old pending applications from PMAY-U 1.0 are still tracked at pmaymis.gov.in.

### My PMAY-U 1.0 application was approved but house not given. What happens?

Contact your ULB — your case transfers to PMAY-U 2.0 for completion. You should not need to re-apply; the ULB should process you under the new phase.

### Can I get both BLC subsidy (₹2.5L) and home loan interest subsidy?

Generally no — EWS/LIG beneficiaries usually get either BLC or interest subsidy, not both. Those with own land typically take BLC; those buying from a developer or public housing authority take interest subsidy.

### What if my city is not showing available houses?

Availability depends on state government and ULB targets. Contact your local ULB or municipal commissioner's office. In many cities, applications are registered on a waiting list even if immediate allocation isn't available.

### Is there a deadline to apply for PMAY-U 2.0?

The scheme runs 2024–2030. No announced hard deadline for EWS applications yet. However, **interest subsidy** claims for home loans must be made at time of loan disbursement — the subsidy window may close if scheme targets are met early. Apply as early as possible.
