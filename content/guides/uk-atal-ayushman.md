## What is Atal Ayushman Uttarakhand Yojana?

The **Atal Ayushman Uttarakhand Yojana (AAUY)** is a state health insurance scheme that provides **₹5 lakh per family per year** for cashless hospitalization at empanelled hospitals. Launched alongside Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY, this scheme extends health coverage to **all families in Uttarakhand** — not just those on SECC lists.

The scheme is managed by the **State Health Authority (SHA), Uttarakhand** and covers nearly the entire population of the state.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Residency:** Must be a resident of Uttarakhand with a valid ration card or family register entry
2. **Coverage:** Almost **all families** in Uttarakhand are eligible (universal coverage)
3. **Exclusions:** Government employees covered under CGHS/ECHS and income tax payees may be excluded
4. **Smart Card:** Must have an Atal Ayushman Golden Card

## Coverage

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Coverage | ₹5,00,000 per family per year |
| Procedures | 1,500+ medical/surgical packages |
| Pre-existing Diseases | Covered from day one |
| Hospitals | Government + empanelled private hospitals |
| Cashless | Yes — no out-of-pocket payment |

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar Card** of family members
- **Ration Card** (Uttarakhand)
- **Any government-issued ID** for verification
- **Mobile number** linked to Aadhaar

## How to Get Your Golden Card

### At Common Service Centre (CSC) / Atal Seva Kendra

1. **Visit** the nearest **CSC** or **Atal Seva Kendra**
2. **Carry** Aadhaar Card and Ration Card
3. **Biometric verification** of family members
4. **Golden Card** is generated on the spot
5. **Nominal fee** of ₹30 for card generation

### At Empanelled Hospital

1. **Visit** any AAUY-empanelled hospital
2. **Go to** the Ayushman/AAUY help desk
3. **Show** Aadhaar and Ration Card
4. **Verification** and card generation can be done at the hospital

### Check Eligibility Online

1. **Visit** [ayushmanuttarakhand.org](https://ayushmanuttarakhand.org)
2. **Enter** your ration card number or Aadhaar
3. **Check** if your family is listed as eligible
4. **Find** empanelled hospitals near you

## How to Use for Treatment

1. **Visit** any AAUY-empanelled hospital
2. **Show** your Golden Card and Aadhaar at the Ayushman help desk
3. **Biometric authentication** confirms identity
4. **Get admitted** for cashless treatment
5. **No payment** for covered procedures

## Important Tips

1. **Get Card in Advance:** Don't wait until you're sick — get your Golden Card made early
2. **Carry Aadhaar Always:** Needed for biometric verification at hospitals
3. **Universal Coverage:** Almost all Uttarakhand residents are eligible — check the portal
4. **Helpline:** Call **14555** for Ayushman-related queries
5. **Both Govt & Private:** You can use the card at both government and empanelled private hospitals

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. What is the coverage amount?
**₹5 lakh per family per year** for cashless hospitalization.

### Q2. Is everyone in Uttarakhand eligible?
Almost all families are eligible. Government employees under CGHS/ECHS and income tax payees may be excluded.

### Q3. How do I get my Golden Card?
Visit the nearest CSC or empanelled hospital with Aadhaar and Ration Card. Card generation costs ₹30.

### Q4. Can I use it outside Uttarakhand?
Yes, at empanelled hospitals across India that accept Ayushman/AAUY cards.

### Q5. Are pre-existing diseases covered?
Yes, from day one of enrollment.

### Q6. What if a hospital refuses treatment?
Call the Ayushman helpline **14555** or contact the State Health Authority.

### Q7. Is this the same as Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY?
AAUY is the state extension of PM-JAY with broader coverage for Uttarakhand residents.
