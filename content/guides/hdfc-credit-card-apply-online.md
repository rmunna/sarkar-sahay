# HDFC Credit Card Apply Online: Best Cards & Eligibility

HDFC Bank is India's largest private sector bank and offers over 20 credit card variants for different needs — travel, shopping, cashback, and premium lifestyle. You can apply for an HDFC credit card entirely online through the official HDFC Bank website or mobile app. Pre-approved customers can get instant approval.

## Who Is Eligible for an HDFC Credit Card?

### Salaried Individuals
- Age: 21–60 years
- Minimum monthly income: ₹25,000 (varies by card)
- Good CIBIL score: 750+ recommended
- Stable employment for at least 1 year

### Self-Employed Individuals
- Age: 21–65 years
- Minimum annual income: ₹6 lakh (varies by card)
- ITR filed for last 2 years
- Good credit history

### For Premium Cards (Regalia/Infinia)
- Regalia: Minimum income ₹12 lakh per annum or existing HDFC relationship
- Infinia: By invitation only or existing high-value HDFC Bank relationship (typically ₹10 lakh+ monthly balance)

## Documents Required

- **Identity Proof:** Aadhaar card, PAN card, passport, voter ID
- **Address Proof:** Aadhaar, utility bill (recent), rent agreement, passport
- **Income Proof (Salaried):** Latest 3 months' salary slips, 6 months' bank statement, Form 16
- **Income Proof (Self-Employed):** Last 2 years' ITR, 6 months' bank statement, business proof
- **Photograph:** Passport-size photograph

## How to Apply for HDFC Credit Card Online

### Method 1: HDFC Bank Website

1. Visit [HDFC Bank Credit Cards page](https://www.hdfcbank.com/personal/pay/cards/credit-cards)
2. Browse available cards and click **"Apply Now"** on your preferred card
3. Enter your mobile number and verify with OTP
4. Fill in personal details — name, date of birth, PAN, address
5. Enter employment details and income information
6. Upload documents (identity, address, and income proof)
7. Review the application and submit
8. Note your **Application Reference Number** for tracking

### Method 2: HDFC Mobile Banking App

1. Open the **HDFC Bank Mobile Banking** app
2. Go to **Cards** → **Credit Cards**
3. Check for **pre-approved offers** (shown if you're an existing customer)
4. If pre-approved, you can get instant approval with minimal documentation
5. If not pre-approved, apply through the standard flow within the app
6. Complete KYC and submit

### Method 3: Pre-Approved Offers (Fastest)

If you're an existing HDFC Bank customer:
1. Log in to NetBanking → **Offers** → **Credit Card Offers**
2. Or check the HDFC Mobile App → **Pre-Approved Offers**
3. Pre-approved cards can be issued within minutes with e-KYC
4. No income documents needed for pre-approved applications

## Important Tips

1. **Check pre-approved offers first** — If you're an existing HDFC customer, pre-approved cards have instant approval and no documentation hassle
2. **Maintain a CIBIL score of 750+** — This significantly increases approval chances and may qualify you for premium cards

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. How long does HDFC credit card approval take?
Pre-approved cards can be approved instantly. New applications typically take 7–21 working days for approval and card dispatch.

### Q2. Can I apply for HDFC credit card without salary slip?
Yes, if you have a pre-approved offer or if you're self-employed with ITR. Some cards accept bank statements as income proof instead of salary slips.

### Q3. What is the minimum CIBIL score for HDFC credit card?
HDFC Bank generally prefers a CIBIL score of 750 and above. For premium cards like Regalia and Infinia, a higher score (800+) is preferred.

### Q4. Can I get HDFC credit card against fixed deposit?
Yes, HDFC Bank offers secured credit cards against FD for applicants with low credit scores or no credit history. The credit limit is typically 80%–90% of the FD value.

### Q5. How to upgrade my HDFC credit card to Regalia?
Log in to NetBanking or the mobile app, go to Cards section, and check for upgrade offers. You can also call customer care at 1800-202-6161 and request an upgrade if your spending pattern qualifies.

### Q6. Is HDFC credit card lifetime free?
Some entry-level cards like HDFC Freedom may be offered as lifetime free. Most other cards waive the annual fee if you meet the minimum spend threshold. Check the specific card's terms.

### Q7. How to close HDFC credit card?
Clear all outstanding dues, redeem reward points, then call HDFC customer care or visit a branch with a written request. Ensure you get a closure confirmation letter.
