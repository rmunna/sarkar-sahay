# NTA Application Correction Not Working? Fix Portal Errors

Unable to access the NTA application correction window? NTA provides a limited correction window after registration closes, allowing candidates to fix errors in their application forms. However, the correction portal often faces technical issues due to heavy traffic. This guide covers all common problems and solutions.

## What is NTA Correction Window?

The NTA correction window is a limited period (usually 2-4 days) after the application deadline during which candidates can edit certain fields in their submitted application forms. NTA announces the correction window dates separately for each exam via official notifications on [nta.ac.in](https://nta.ac.in/).

**Important:** The correction window is a one-time opportunity. Changes made during this period are final.

---

## What Can Be Corrected?

NTA allows corrections to specific fields only. The allowed fields may vary by exam, but generally include:

### ✅ Fields You CAN Correct
- Personal details — Name, Father's Name, Mother's Name
- Date of birth
- Gender
- Category (General/OBC/SC/ST/EWS)
- PwD status
- Exam medium/language
- Exam city preferences
- Photograph and signature
- Educational qualification details
- Address and correspondence details

### ❌ Fields You CANNOT Correct
- Mobile number (used for OTP verification)
- Email address (primary communication channel)
- Exam choice (e.g., cannot switch from JEE Main to NEET)

*Note: The exact list of editable fields is published by NTA in the correction window notification. Always check the official notice.*

## Common Correction Window Errors and Fixes

### 1. Correction Window Not Opening or Portal Error

**Problem:** The correction link does not appear after login, or the portal shows "Correction facility not available."

**Fix:**
1. **Check the dates** — Correction windows have very specific dates and times (usually 11:00 PM closing time). Verify on [nta.ac.in](https://nta.ac.in/) that the window is currently open
2. **Check your exam** — Each exam has a separate correction window. Ensure you are on the correct exam portal
3. **Clear browser cache** — Press Ctrl + Shift + Delete, clear all data, and reload
4. **Try a different browser** — Use Chrome, Firefox, or Edge
5. **Try during off-peak hours** — Access between 11 PM and 7 AM for better server response
6. **Check your application status** — If your application is "Incomplete" or fee is "Unpaid", the correction facility may not be available

### 2. Server Issues During Correction Window

**Problem:** Portal is extremely slow, times out, or shows "502 Bad Gateway" during the correction period.

**Fix:**
1. **Do not wait for the last day** — Server load peaks on the final day of the correction window
2. **Try early morning** — 4 AM to 7 AM typically has the lowest traffic
3. **Use a stable internet connection** — Wired connections are more reliable than Wi-Fi
4. **Keep your changes ready** — Prepare all corrections in advance so you can make changes quickly once logged in
5. **Save after each change** — Click "Save" after correcting each field to avoid losing changes if the session expires

### 3. Fee Payment for Corrections

**Problem:** The portal asks for additional fee payment after making corrections.

**Details:**
- **Category change** (e.g., General to OBC, or OBC to General) — May require fee difference payment or refund
- **Photo/Signature re-upload** — Usually free
- **Personal details correction** — Usually free
- **Exam city preference change** — Usually free

**Important notes:**
- NTA charges a correction fee only for specific changes (varies by exam). Check the correction window notification
- If additional fee is required, the same payment methods are available (UPI, debit card, net banking)
- If payment fails during correction, the correction will not be saved. Retry after ensuring the previous transaction was not debited

### 4. Correction Window Deadline

**Problem:** You missed the correction window deadline, or are unsure about the timing.

**Key facts:**
- NTA correction windows are **strictly time-bound** — even a few minutes late means no access
- The window typically closes at **11:50 PM** on the last date (not midnight)
- NTA does **not extend** correction windows except in extraordinary circumstances
- After the correction window closes, no changes are possible through the portal

**What to do if you missed it:**
1. **Contact NTA helpdesk immediately** — Call 011-40759000 and explain the situation
2. **Send an email** — Write to the exam-specific email with your application number, the correction needed, and supporting documents
3. **Note:** NTA may or may not accommodate late corrections; there is no guarantee. Critical errors (wrong category, PwD status) are sometimes handled on a case-by-case basis

### 5. Changes Not Saving or Reverting

**Problem:** You make corrections but they do not save, or the old information reappears after saving.

**Fix:**
1. **Check internet connection** — A dropped connection during save can cause data loss
2. **Click "Save" explicitly** — Do not just navigate away; click the Save/Submit button on each page
3. **Download the updated confirmation** — After all corrections, download the revised confirmation page to verify changes were saved
4. **Take screenshots** — Screenshot each corrected field before and after saving as proof
5. **Try a different device** — If the portal is not responding on your current device, try another laptop or desktop

## How to Use NTA Correction Window — Step-by-Step

1. Visit [exams.nta.ac.in](https://exams.nta.ac.in/) during the announced correction window dates
2. Click on the relevant exam link (e.g., "JEE Main 2026 Correction Window")
3. Log in with your **Application Number** and **Password**
4. The editable fields will be highlighted or unlocked
5. Make the required corrections carefully
6. Re-upload photograph/signature if needed (follow the same specifications as registration)
7. Pay additional fee if prompted
8. Click **"Submit Correction"**
9. Download the **revised confirmation page** and verify all changes

---

## Important Tips

1. **Make corrections on Day 1** — Do not wait for the last day; server issues are worst at the deadline
2. **Double-check before submitting** — Corrections are final; you will not get another chance to edit
3. **Keep the revised confirmation** — Download and print the updated confirmation page as proof
4. **Verify photo specifications** — If re-uploading, follow NTA specifications exactly (JPG, 10-200 KB, white background)
5. **Check the notification** — Each exam's correction window may allow different fields; read the official notice carefully

## FAQs

### Q1. How many times can I make corrections during the correction window?
You can typically make corrections only once during the correction window. Some exams may allow multiple edits within the window period, but it is safer to make all changes in one session.

### Q2. Can I change my exam city preference during correction?
Yes, exam city preference is usually editable during the correction window. You can change up to 4 city choices (the exact number depends on the exam).

### Q3. I changed my category but the fee difference is not being refunded. What should I do?
If you changed from a higher fee category to a lower one, the refund may take 7-15 working days. If not received, contact NTA helpdesk with your application number and bank details.

### Q4. The correction window is open but I cannot see the edit option for my field. Why?
NTA restricts editing to specific fields only. If a field is not editable, it means NTA has not allowed corrections for that particular field. Check the official notification for the list of editable fields.

### Q5. I made a wrong correction by mistake. Can I undo it?
If the correction window is still open, log in and try to correct it again. If you already submitted and the window has closed, contact NTA helpdesk immediately to request a reversal.

### Q6. Will my admit card reflect the corrections I made?
Yes. If your corrections were saved successfully, the admit card will be generated with the updated information. Download the admit card after it is released and verify the corrected details.

### Q7. Is there a separate correction window for the fee payment issue?
No. Fee payment issues must be resolved during the original application period or through the correction window. If you missed the fee payment, contact NTA helpdesk to check if late payment is possible.
