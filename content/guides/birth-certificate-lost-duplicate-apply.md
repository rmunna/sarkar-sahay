# Birth Certificate Lost? How to Get a Duplicate Copy in India

If your original birth certificate is lost, damaged, or destroyed, you can apply for a **duplicate copy** from the municipal corporation, nagar palika, or gram panchayat where the birth was originally registered. This guide covers the complete process — online and offline — along with documents, fees, and timeline.

## Who Can Apply for a Duplicate Birth Certificate?

- The person whose birth certificate is lost (if adult)
- Parents or legal guardians
- Legal heirs (with proof)
- Authorised representative (with power of attorney)

## Documents Required

1. **Application form** — Available at the municipal office or online portal
2. **Affidavit on stamp paper** — Declaring loss of original certificate (₹10-50 stamp paper, notarised)
3. **FIR / Police complaint copy** (optional but recommended) — If the certificate was stolen
4. **Aadhaar card** of the applicant
5. **Identity proof** — Voter ID, passport, or driving licence
6. **Address proof** — Utility bill, ration card, or Aadhaar
7. **Any old photocopy** of the original birth certificate (if available)
8. **Hospital discharge summary / birth record** (if available) — Helps in verification

## Online Process (via CRS Portal or State Portal)

Many states now allow duplicate birth certificate applications through the **CRS (Civil Registration System)** portal or state e-district portals.

1. **Visit** [crsorgi.gov.in](https://crsorgi.gov.in/) or your state's e-district portal
2. **Register / Login** — Create an account with mobile number and email
3. **Select service** — Choose "Birth Certificate" → "Duplicate / Copy"
4. **Fill application form** — Enter name, date of birth, place of birth, registration number (if known)
5. **Upload documents** — Scanned affidavit, ID proof, address proof
6. **Pay fee online** — ₹10-50 (varies by state)
7. **Submit application** — Note down the application/reference number
8. **Track status** — Check status online using reference number
9. **Download / Collect** — Some states provide digital download; others require physical collection

**State-specific portals:**
- **Delhi:** [edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in](https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in/)
- **Telangana:** [meeseva.gov.in](https://www.meeseva.gov.in/)
- **Maharashtra:** [aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in](https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in/)
- **Karnataka:** [nadakacheri.karnataka.gov.in](https://nadakacheri.karnataka.gov.in/)

## Offline Process

1. **Visit the municipal corporation / nagar palika / gram panchayat** where the birth was registered
2. **Collect application form** — Ask for a duplicate birth certificate application form
3. **Attach documents** — Affidavit, ID proof, address proof, and old copy (if any)
4. **Submit application** with required fee
5. **Verification** — The office verifies records from the birth register
6. **Collect duplicate certificate** — Within 7-15 working days

## Processing Time

| Mode | Timeline |
|------|----------|
| Online (e-district) | 7-15 working days |
| Offline (municipal office) | 7-21 working days |
| Older records (before 2000) | 15-30 working days |

> **Tip:** If the birth was registered before computerisation, verification takes longer as manual register search is required.

## What if Birth Was Never Registered?

If the birth was never registered, you need to apply for **late registration** first:
- Within 21 days to 1 year: Apply at the registrar's office with a late fee
- After 1 year: Requires an order from the First Class Magistrate or Executive Magistrate

After late registration, you can obtain the birth certificate normally.

## Important Tips

1. **Keep the affidavit ready** — It is mandatory for duplicate certificate applications in most states
2. **Know the registration number** — If you have any old copy or record with the registration number, it speeds up the process significantly
3. **Hospital records help** — If municipal records are unclear, a hospital birth record can support your application
4. **Digital certificates** — Many states now issue digitally signed certificates via DigiLocker; check if yours is available at [digilocker.gov.in](https://www.digilocker.gov.in/)
5. **Apply in the correct jurisdiction** — You must apply where the birth was originally registered, not your current residence

## FAQs

### Q1. Is an FIR mandatory for getting a duplicate birth certificate?
No, an FIR is not mandatory in most states. A notarised affidavit declaring the loss is sufficient. However, an FIR is recommended if the document was stolen.

### Q2. Can I apply for a duplicate from a different city?
Generally, you must apply at the registrar's office where the birth was registered. Some states allow online applications from anywhere.

### Q3. What if I don't know my birth registration number?
You can still apply. The office will search records using your name, date of birth, and parents' names. It may take longer.

### Q4. Is a duplicate birth certificate legally valid?
Yes, a duplicate birth certificate issued by the registrar is equally valid as the original for all legal and official purposes.

### Q5. Can I get a duplicate through DigiLocker?
If your birth certificate was digitally issued and linked, you can access it via DigiLocker. For older certificates, you need to apply for a physical duplicate.

### Q6. How many duplicate copies can I get?
There is no limit. You can apply for multiple duplicate copies by paying the fee for each.
