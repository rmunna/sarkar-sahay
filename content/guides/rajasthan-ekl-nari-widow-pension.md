## What is Rajasthan Ekl Nari Samman Pension Yojana?

**Mukhyamantri Ekl Nari Samman Pension Yojana** is Rajasthan's pension scheme for **single women** — widows, divorced, separated, and abandoned women — who have no income source. It provides monthly financial assistance based on age:

| Age | Monthly Pension |
|-----|----------------|
| 18–54 years | ₹500/month |
| 55–59 years | ₹750/month |
| 60–74 years | ₹1,000/month |
| 75+ years | ₹1,500/month |

## Who is Eligible?

**You ARE eligible if:**
- You are a **single woman (widow, divorced, or abandoned/deserted)** residing in Rajasthan
- You are a **permanent resident of Rajasthan** (minimum 15 years)
- You have **no regular income source** and are not paying income tax
- You are **18 years or older**

> "Ekl Nari" means "single/alone woman" — it specifically targets women living without a husband's support due to death, divorce, or abandonment.

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar Card** — mandatory
2. **Jan Aadhaar card** — Rajasthan family ID
3. **Proof of single status:**
   - Widows: Husband's death certificate
   - Divorced: Divorce decree/certificate
   - Abandoned/deserted: Self-declaration + community/panchayat certificate
4. **Age proof** (Aadhaar with DoB, school certificate)
5. **Domicile certificate** — Rajasthan resident for 15+ years
6. **Bank passbook** — Aadhaar-linked savings account
7. **Income declaration** — self-declaration of no income
8. **Photograph**

## How to Apply — Step by Step

### Step 1 — Ensure Jan Aadhaar is Made

Get your **Jan Aadhaar card** (Rajasthan family ID) if you don't have one — visit any E-Mitra kiosk.

### Step 2 — Apply Online

1. Go to **[rajssp.raj.nic.in](https://rajssp.raj.nic.in)**
2. Login with Jan Aadhaar
3. Select **"Ekl Nari Samman Pension Yojana"**
4. Fill the application and upload documents

### Step 3 — Apply at E-Mitra

Visit the nearest **E-Mitra centre** for assisted application. Bring all original documents.

### Step 4 — Verification

- Patwari/Village Officer verifies residency and single status
- SDO (Sub-Divisional Officer) approves the pension

### Step 5 — Pension Starts

Monthly pension is credited to your bank account after approval.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. My husband left me 5 years ago. I have no divorce certificate. Can I still apply?**

Yes. Abandoned/deserted women can apply with a **self-declaration** + supporting community or panchayat certificate. The SDO has discretion to accept these cases — visit the SDO office for guidance.

**2. I am a widow with two adult children. Do I still get pension?**

Yes. Having adult children does not disqualify you. The scheme is for women without their own income — having children does not affect eligibility.

**3. I am 25, widowed, with a young child. Am I eligible?**

Yes. Minimum age is 18 — you qualify. You receive ₹500/month at age 18–54.

**4. Can I get both Ekl Nari Pension and IGNWPS (central widow pension)?**

No. You cannot receive both a state and central pension for the same condition. The higher of the two benefits typically applies — in Rajasthan, Ekl Nari may be integrated with IGNWPS. Confirm with the SDO.

**5. The pension was approved but stopped after 3 months. Why?**

Common reasons: Aadhaar-bank link issue, re-verification pending, or data mismatch in Jan Aadhaar. Visit the E-Mitra centre or SDO office to diagnose the issue.
