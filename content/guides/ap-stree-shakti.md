# Stree Shakti – Free Bus Travel for Women in Andhra Pradesh

Stree Shakti is a scheme by the Government of Andhra Pradesh that provides free bus travel for women in APSRTC (Andhra Pradesh State Road Transport Corporation) buses. The scheme empowers women by providing free mobility for work, education, healthcare, and personal purposes across the state.

## What is Stree Shakti?

Under the Stree Shakti scheme, women can travel free of cost in APSRTC ordinary (Palle Velugu and Express) buses across Andhra Pradesh. The scheme covers regular RTC services and is aimed at improving women's access to opportunities by removing transportation costs as a barrier.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **All women:** Women of all ages can avail free bus travel
2. **Residency:** Open to all women traveling within Andhra Pradesh on APSRTC buses
3. **Bus types covered:** Palle Velugu, Express, and other ordinary APSRTC services
4. **Not covered:** Premium services like Garuda, Garuda Plus, Vennela (sleeper), and AC buses are NOT included
5. **No income restriction:** Available to all women regardless of economic status

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** or any government-issued photo ID (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- No separate registration or pass is needed — simply board the bus

## How to Avail Free Bus Travel

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Go to the bus stop:** Visit the nearest APSRTC bus stop or bus station
2. **Board an eligible bus:** Board any APSRTC Palle Velugu or Express bus
3. **Carry ID proof:** Keep your Aadhaar card or government photo ID handy
4. **Inform conductor:** Tell the conductor you are traveling under Stree Shakti
5. **Get a zero-fare ticket:** The conductor will issue a free ticket (zero-fare)
6. **Travel:** Travel to your destination free of charge
7. **Keep the ticket:** Retain the ticket until you reach your destination for any checks

### Important Notes

- No prior registration or application is required
- Women can travel any number of times — there is no limit on trips
- The scheme applies only within Andhra Pradesh on APSRTC buses
- Inter-state buses and private buses are not covered

## Important Tips

1. **Carry photo ID always:** Conductors may ask for ID verification — keep your Aadhaar or voter ID ready
2. **Check bus type:** Free travel is only on ordinary and express buses, not premium/AC services
3. **No limit on travel:** You can use this benefit as many times as needed
4. **Arrive early at bus stops:** Free travel has increased demand — buses may fill up faster
5. **Respect other passengers:** The scheme has made buses busier — be patient during peak hours

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Do I need to apply or register for Stree Shakti?
No. Simply board an eligible APSRTC bus with your photo ID and travel for free.

### Q2: Can I travel in AC or Garuda buses for free?
No. Stree Shakti covers only ordinary and express APSRTC buses. Premium and AC services require normal tickets.

### Q3: Is there a limit on how many times I can travel?
No, there is no limit. Women can use the free travel benefit unlimited times.

### Q4: Do girls and elderly women also qualify?
Yes, women of all ages — from children to senior citizens — can avail free bus travel.

### Q5: Can I travel from AP to another state for free?
No, the scheme covers only intra-state travel within Andhra Pradesh on APSRTC buses.

### Q6: What if the conductor refuses to give a free ticket?
You can complain to APSRTC through their helpline or at the bus depot. Conductors are required to issue free tickets to women.

### Q7: Is the scheme available on city buses too?
Yes, APSRTC city ordinary services within AP are also covered under Stree Shakti.
