# MP Domicile Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **Mool Niwasi Praman Patra** (मूल निवासी प्रमाण पत्र) in Madhya Pradesh — also called domicile certificate — is issued by the Revenue Department (Tehsildar / SDM) and is required for NEET MP state quota (85%) admissions, MPPSC government job applications, MP state scholarships, and scheme eligibility. Applications go through **MP e-District portal**. **Samagra ID is mandatory**.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [mpedistrict.gov.in](https://mpedistrict.gov.in). **Samagra ID required** (MP Family ID). Issued by Tehsildar within **15–30 days**.

## Samagra ID — First Step (Mandatory)

**Samagra ID is mandatory for all MP government services.** Without it, you cannot apply for a domicile certificate online.

- **Samagra ID**: MP's family database ID (8-digit family ID + 9-digit individual ID)
- Apply at [samagra.gov.in](https://samagra.gov.in) or nearest Lok Sewa Kendra
- Free to register — required documents: Aadhaar + family details + address in MP
- Samagra auto-fills income, caste, and family information — speeds up all certificate applications

## Why You Need MP Domicile Certificate

- **NEET MP state quota (85%)** — domicile mandatory for DMET (Directorate of Medical Education & Training) counselling
- **MPPSC (MP PSC)** — MP government jobs (most state posts require MP domicile)
- **MPESB / VYAPAM** — state-level competitive exams
- **MP Post-Matric Scholarship** — SC/ST/OBC scholarships for MP residents
- **Ladli Laxmi / CM Kanya Vivah** — state scheme eligibility
- **Mukhyamantri Jan Kalyan (Sambal) Yojana** — MP scheme benefits

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory — with MP address)
- **Samagra ID** (8-digit family + 9-digit individual) — **mandatory**
- **Ration card** (MP ration card)
- **Voter ID** (MP voter enrollment)
- **Birth certificate** — showing birth in Madhya Pradesh
- **School Transfer Certificate (TC)** — from MP schools
- **Khasra-Khatauni** — land records (for rural/agricultural families)
- Utility bills at MP address
- Self-declaration of MP residency
- Passport-size photograph

## How to Apply Online — MP e-District Portal

1. Go to [mpedistrict.gov.in](https://mpedistrict.gov.in)
2. Register / log in with Samagra ID + mobile number (OTP)
3. Navigate to **"Revenue Department"** → **"Mool Niwasi Praman Patra"**
4. System auto-fills details from Samagra database
5. Verify and fill remaining fields:
   - Duration of residence in Madhya Pradesh
   - Purpose of certificate
6. Upload documents (Aadhaar, voter ID, birth certificate, school TC, Khasra-Khatauni)
7. Pay fee: **₹20–₹40** (online)
8. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via Lok Sewa Kendra / MP Online Kiosk

1. Visit nearest **Lok Sewa Kendra** or **MP Online kiosk** (at every tehsil/ward level)
2. Carry Samagra ID, Aadhaar, ration card, voter ID, school TC
3. Operator fills and submits on MP e-District
4. Service charge: approximately ₹30–60
5. Collect acknowledgement receipt

---

## Status Check & Download

1. Go to [mpedistrict.gov.in](https://mpedistrict.gov.in) → **"Application Status"**
2. Enter Samagra ID or application reference number
3. Once approved: download digitally signed PDF

## NEET MP State Quota 2026

For **NEET 2026 MP state quota** (DMET Bhopal / MP government medical college counselling):
1. MP Mool Niwasi Praman Patra — proof of MP residency
2. OR born in MP (birth certificate) OR studied in MP schools for prescribed period
3. Check DMET MP NEET counselling notification for exact eligibility rules
4. Certificate must be Tehsildar/SDM issued — MP e-District digital certificate accepted
5. Apply well before NEET results (15–30 day processing)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I don't have Samagra ID — can I apply offline?

You can apply offline at the Tehsildar office without Samagra ID. However, **online application requires Samagra ID**. Get your Samagra ID first at any Lok Sewa Kendra — it's free and takes 1–3 days. It benefits all future MP services.

### MPPSC — is MP domicile mandatory for all posts?

**Yes, for most MP state government posts** (MPPSC/VYAPAM), Mool Niwasi Praman Patra is required. Some centrally funded posts in MP may not require state domicile — check the specific advertisement.

### Born in MP but currently studying outside — do I qualify?

**Birth in Madhya Pradesh** is a valid domicile route. Submit birth certificate showing MP birth + one supporting address document (parent's ration card or voter ID in MP). You don't need to currently reside in MP if you were born there.
