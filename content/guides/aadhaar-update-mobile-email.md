## Why Update Mobile Number or Email in Aadhaar?

Your mobile number linked to Aadhaar is essential for:
- **OTP-based authentication** for government services
- **Downloading e-Aadhaar** PDF online (see our [e-Aadhaar download guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-download-online))
- **Banking and financial services** using Aadhaar eKYC
- **Digital Locker** and other DigiGov services
- **Government scheme benefits** (DBT, subsidies, scholarships)

If your mobile number has changed or was never registered, updating it should be a priority.

## Methods to Update Mobile Number in Aadhaar

### Method 1: Visit an Aadhaar Enrolment Centre (Recommended)

This is the **most reliable method** and works even if your old number is no longer active.

1. **Locate the nearest centre** — visit [appointments.uidai.gov.in](https://appointments.uidai.gov.in) or search "Aadhaar centre near me"
2. **Book an appointment** online (optional but recommended to avoid long queues)
3. **Visit the centre** with your original Aadhaar card and one valid ID proof
4. Fill the **Aadhaar Update/Correction Form** — tick the "Mobile" checkbox
5. Enter your **new mobile number** on the form
6. The operator will verify your **biometrics** (fingerprint/iris scan)
7. You will receive an **acknowledgement slip** with a Update Request Number (URN)
8. An OTP will be sent to your **new mobile number** for verification
9. Update will be processed within **7-10 working days**

### Method 2: Online via myAadhaar Portal (Limited)

> **Note:** As of 2024, UIDAI allows limited online updates. Mobile number update may require a visit to the enrolment centre. Check the portal for current availability.

1. Visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in)
2. Log in using your **Aadhaar number** and OTP on your **currently registered** mobile
3. Select **"Update Aadhaar"**
4. Choose **"Mobile Number"** or **"Email"** update
5. Enter the new mobile number or email
6. Verify via OTP sent to the new number/email
7. Submit the request

### Method 3: Using mAadhaar App

1. Download **mAadhaar** from Play Store / App Store
2. Register with your **current registered mobile number**
3. Go to **"Update Aadhaar"** section
4. Follow the prompts to submit a mobile/email update request
5. Biometric verification may be required at a centre

## How to Update Email Address in Aadhaar

The process for email update is similar:

**Online:**
1. Visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in)
2. Login with Aadhaar number + OTP
3. Select **"Update Aadhaar"** → choose **"Email"**
4. Enter your new email address
5. Verify via OTP/link sent to the new email
6. Submit

**Offline (at Aadhaar centre):**
1. Visit the nearest Aadhaar enrolment centre
2. Fill the update form and tick **"Email"**
3. Provide biometric verification
4. Email update is processed within 7-10 days

## Documents Required

For **mobile number update at a centre**, you need:

- **Original Aadhaar card** (or e-Aadhaar printout)
- **Valid ID proof** (any one):
  - Passport
  - [PAN card](/guide/pan-card-apply-online)
  - Voter ID
  - Driving License
- **New mobile phone** (to receive OTP at the centre)

> **No documents needed** for mobile/email update if your biometrics match in the system.

## How to Check Update Status

1. Visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Check Aadhaar Update Status"**
3. Enter your **URN (Update Request Number)** from the acknowledgement slip
4. Enter the captcha and click **"Check Status"**

## Important Tips

1. **Always carry your phone** with the new SIM when visiting the Aadhaar centre — OTP verification happens on the spot
2. **Keep your URN safe** — you need it to track your update status
3. **Only one mobile number** can be linked to one Aadhaar at a time
4. **Update before your old SIM expires** — if your old number is deactivated, you must visit a centre (online update won't work)
5. **Aadhaar-linked services** (bank, gas, etc.) will automatically use the new number after update is processed

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Can I update my mobile number in Aadhaar without the old number?
Yes, you can update your mobile number by visiting an Aadhaar enrolment centre. Biometric verification (fingerprint/iris) is used instead of OTP on the old number.

### 2. How many times can I change my mobile number in Aadhaar?
There is no official limit on the number of times you can update your mobile number. However, frequent changes may trigger additional verification.

### 3. Is mobile number mandatory in Aadhaar?
While not legally mandatory, it is practically essential for OTP-based services like [downloading e-Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-card-download-online), banking eKYC, and receiving government scheme benefits.

### 4. Can I link the same mobile number to multiple Aadhaar cards?
Yes, the same mobile number can be linked to multiple Aadhaar numbers (e.g., for family members). However, each Aadhaar can have only one mobile number.

### 5. Will updating my mobile number change my Aadhaar number?
No, your 12-digit Aadhaar number remains the same. Only the mobile number in the records is updated.

### 6. What if my biometrics don't match at the centre?
If fingerprints fail, the operator will try iris scan. If both fail, you may need to visit a different centre or contact UIDAI helpline at **1947**.

### 7. Can NRIs update their mobile number in Aadhaar?
Yes, NRIs can update by visiting an Aadhaar centre when in India. An Indian mobile number is required for registration.
