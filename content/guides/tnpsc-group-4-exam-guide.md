# TNPSC Group 4 Exam Guide — Eligibility, Pattern & Preparation Tips

The Tamil Nadu Public Service Commission (TNPSC) conducts the Group 4 (Combined Civil Services Examination - IV) for recruitment to various clerical and office assistant posts in Tamil Nadu government departments.

TNPSC Group 4 is one of the most popular entry-level government exams in Tamil Nadu, offering positions like Junior Assistant, Typist, Village Administrative Officer (VAO), and Steno-Typist.

## Eligibility Criteria

### Age Limit

| Category | Minimum Age | Maximum Age |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| General (Others) | 18 years | 30 years |
| BC/MBC | 18 years | 32 years |
| SC/ST | 18 years | 35 years |

### Educational Qualification

- **Minimum:** SSLC (10th pass) for most posts
- Higher qualifications for specific posts (typing knowledge for Typist posts)
- Must be able to read and write in Tamil

## Exam Pattern

| Subject | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| Tamil (Language) | 100 | 150 |
| General Studies | 75 | 150 |
| Aptitude & Mental Ability | 25 | — |
| **Total** | **200** | **300** |

- **Duration:** 3 hours
- **No negative marking**
- **Type:** OMR-based (offline)

## Syllabus Overview

### Tamil (Language)
- Tamil grammar (Ilakkanam), Literature, Comprehension, Vocabulary, Translation

### General Studies
- Indian History & Culture, Geography, Polity, Economy, Science, Tamil Nadu History & Culture, Current Affairs

### Aptitude & Mental Ability
- Number series, Logical reasoning, Puzzles, Coding-decoding, Blood relations

## How to Apply

1. Visit TNPSC at [tnpsc.gov.in](https://www.tnpsc.gov.in)
2. Register with One-Time Registration (OTR) if not done
3. Apply for Group 4 notification
4. Fill in details and upload documents
5. No application fee for most categories
6. Submit and download hall ticket when available

## Application Fee

| Category | Fee |
|----------|-----|
| All Categories | Free (No exam fee for TNPSC exams) |

*TNPSC exams are generally free of cost.*

## Salary & Benefits

- **Junior Assistant:** ₹19,500 – ₹62,000
- **VAO:** ₹19,500 – ₹62,000
- **Typist:** ₹19,500 – ₹62,000
- DA, HRA, medical benefits as per TN Government
- Pension (NPS), group insurance

## Preparation Tips

1. **Tamil is key:** Strong Tamil knowledge gives a major advantage — 150 marks
2. **TN History & Culture:** Focus on Sangam literature, Chola dynasty, freedom movement in TN
3. **6-10th NCERT/Samacheer:** Study Tamil Nadu state board textbooks
4. **Current affairs:** TN-specific government schemes and policies
5. **Previous papers:** TNPSC repeats patterns — solve at least 10 years' papers

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is TNPSC Group 4 exam free?
Yes, TNPSC does not charge any exam fee.

### What is the minimum qualification?
10th pass (SSLC) is sufficient for most Group 4 posts.

### Is there negative marking?
No, there is no negative marking.

### Is Tamil mandatory?
Yes, candidates must be able to read and write Tamil. The Tamil language paper carries 150 marks.

### How often is Group 4 conducted?
Generally every 1-2 years based on vacancies.

### What posts are available under Group 4?
Junior Assistant, Typist, Village Administrative Officer (VAO), Steno-Typist, and similar posts.
