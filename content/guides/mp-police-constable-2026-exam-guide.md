# MP Police Constable 2026 — Complete Madhya Pradesh Police Recruitment Guide

Madhya Pradesh Police Constable recruitment is conducted by the **Madhya Pradesh Employee Selection Board (MPESB)**. With **7,000–10,000 vacancies** expected, it is one of the major state police recruitments.

**12th pass** candidates with good physical fitness can apply for MP Police Constable.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is independent and not affiliated with MP Police. Verify all details at [peb.mp.gov.in](https://peb.mp.gov.in).

## Eligibility

| Criterion | Requirement |
|-----------|------------|
| **Education** | Class 12 from a recognised board |
| **Age (Male)** | 18–25 years (General); extended for reserved categories |
| **Age (Female)** | 18–28 years |
| **Height (Male — General/OBC/SC)** | 167 cm |
| **Height (Male — ST)** | 160 cm |
| **Height (Female)** | 152 cm |
| **Chest (Male)** | 81–86 cm |

## Salary

| Component | Amount |
|-----------|--------|
| **Basic Pay** | ₹19,500/month (Level 5) |
| **DA + HRA + Allowances** | ~₹7,000–₹9,000 |
| **Gross In-Hand** | ₹22,000–₹28,000/month |

**Benefits:** Government accommodation, pension (NPS), medical.

## Syllabus

### GK & Science
Indian History, Indian & MP Geography, Indian Polity, Economy, Science (Class 10), Current Affairs, MP-specific GK (schemes, geography, history, culture)

### Reasoning
Analogies, Series, Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations, Directions, Venn Diagrams, Non-verbal Reasoning

### Mental Ability & Psychology
Attitude assessment, Decision making, Situational judgement, Basic mental ability questions

## MP-Specific GK — Key Areas
- **MP History:** Madhya Pradesh statehood (1956), major kingdoms (Holkar, Scindia, Gondwana)
- **MP Geography:** Vindhya Range, Satpura Range, rivers (Narmada, Chambal, Betwa), national parks (Kanha, Bandhavgarh, Pench)
- **MP Economy:** Agriculture (soybean, wheat), diamond mining (Panna), tourism
- **MP Schemes:** Mukhyamantri Jan Kalyan Sambal, Ladli Behna Yojana, CM Seekho Kamao

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the minimum height for MP Police Constable?**
167 cm for male General/OBC/SC candidates; 160 cm for male ST candidates; 152 cm for all female candidates.

**Can candidates from other states apply?**
Yes — but MP domicile candidates get lower application fees and reserved category benefits. Non-MP candidates are treated as General category for reservations.

**Is there negative marking in MP Police written exam?**
Typically no negative marking in MPESB police exams. Confirm with official notification.

**Escalation for non-shortlisting issues:** Contact MPESB at [peb.mp.gov.in](https://peb.mp.gov.in) or call MP Police helpline.
