## What is Haryana Mahila Samridhi Yojana?

**Haryana Mahila Samridhi Yojana** is a scheme by the **Haryana Scheduled Castes and Backward Classes Corporation (HSCBC)** that provides **low-interest loans to SC women** for starting self-employment ventures. The scheme gives:

- Loans up to **₹60,000**
- Interest rate of **5% per year**
- Repayment over **3.5 years (42 months)**

The scheme is specifically for Scheduled Caste women in Haryana who want to start or expand a small business.

## Eligible Business Activities

The loan can be used for a wide range of self-employment activities:
- Grocery/general store
- Beauty parlour / tailoring
- Food stall / small canteen
- Handicrafts and artisan work
- Dairy — buying cattle/buffalo
- Vegetable/fruit vending
- Mobile/electronics repair shop
- Any other small trade or service business

---

## Who is Eligible?

**You ARE eligible if:**
- You are a **Scheduled Caste (SC) woman**
- You are a **permanent resident of Haryana**
- Your age is **18 to 55 years**
- Your **family's annual income does not exceed ₹3 lakh**
- You have **no default** on any previous loan
- You have not previously availed of this scheme (first-time applicant)

---

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar Card** — identity proof
2. **Caste certificate** — SC certificate from competent authority
3. **Domicile certificate** — Haryana residency
4. **Income certificate** — annual family income ≤ ₹3 lakh
5. **Parivar Pehchaan Patra (PPP)** — Haryana family ID
6. **Bank passbook** — savings account in her name
7. **Business plan** — brief description of the business to be started
8. **Photograph**

## How to Apply — Step by Step

### Step 1 — Apply on Saral Haryana

1. Go to **[saralharyana.gov.in](https://saralharyana.gov.in)**
2. Register / Login with Aadhaar / PPP
3. Search for **"Mahila Samridhi Yojana"**
4. Fill the application form with business details
5. Upload documents and submit

### Step 2 — Apply at HSCBC District Office

Alternatively, visit the **District HSCBC Office** (Haryana Scheduled Castes and Backward Classes Corporation) in your district.

### Step 3 — Verification

- HSCBC verifies your SC certificate, income, and residence
- A brief interview/counselling session on business plan

### Step 4 — Loan Approval and Disbursement

Approved loan is credited to your bank account via DBT.

### Step 5 — Repay in EMIs

Repay in equal monthly instalments (EMI) over 42 months. Default-free repayment makes you eligible for future loans from HSCBC.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. My mother-in-law is 58 years old, SC, and wants to start a tailoring business. Is she eligible?**

No. The maximum age is 55 years. At 58, she is not eligible for Mahila Samridhi Yojana.

**2. Can I take this loan to buy a sewing machine?**

Yes. A sewing machine for a tailoring business is an eligible use of the loan amount.

**3. I have a OBC certificate, not SC. Is there a similar scheme?**

HSCBC has separate loan schemes for **Backward Classes (OBC)** women as well. Contact the HSCBC district office or visit saralharyana.gov.in for OBC-specific schemes.

**4. What happens if I can't repay?**

Missing EMIs leads to penalty interest. Continued default can lead to legal action under the corporation's recovery process. If you face financial difficulties, contact HSCBC before defaulting — they may allow rescheduling.

**5. I have already availed this loan 3 years ago and fully repaid. Can I apply again?**

Yes, if you have fully repaid the previous loan without default. Contact HSCBC district office to confirm eligibility for a repeat loan.
