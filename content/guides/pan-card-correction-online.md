## Why Correct Your PAN Card Details?

If your PAN card has a **wrong name, incorrect date of birth, or spelling mistakes**, it can cause serious issues — failed Aadhaar-PAN linking, bank account problems, ITR filing errors, and KYC rejection. Getting the correction done is essential.

You can correct PAN details online through **NSDL (now Protean)** or **UTIITSL** portals. This guide covers both methods step by step.

## What Can Be Corrected?

| Detail | Correctable Online? |
|--------|-------------------|
| Name (spelling/full name) | ✅ Yes |
| Date of Birth | ✅ Yes |
| Father's Name | ✅ Yes |
| Gender | ✅ Yes |
| Address | ✅ Yes |
| Photo/Signature | ✅ Yes |
| PAN Number itself | ❌ No (PAN number never changes) |

## Documents Required for Correction

### For Name Correction
- **Any two** of the following (name should match the correct name):
  - Aadhaar card
  - Passport
  - Voter ID
  - Driving Licence
  - Class 10 marksheet/certificate
  - Marriage certificate (for name change after marriage)
  - Gazette notification (for legal name change)

### For Date of Birth Correction
- **Any one** of the following:
  - Birth certificate
  - Passport
  - Class 10 marksheet/certificate
  - Aadhaar card (with correct DOB)

### For Address Correction
- Aadhaar card, Passport, Voter ID, Utility bill, Bank statement

## Method 1: Correction via NSDL (Protean) Portal

### Step 1: Visit NSDL Portal
1. Go to [NSDL PAN Portal](https://www.onlineservices.nsdl.com/paam/endUserRegisterContact.html)
2. Select **"Changes or Correction in existing PAN Data"**
3. Select **Category** — Individual, HUF, Company, etc.

### Step 2: Fill the Form
1. Enter your **existing PAN number**
2. Enter your **name, DOB, and contact details**
3. **Tick the checkbox** next to the field you want to correct (e.g., tick "Name" if correcting name)
4. Enter the **correct details** in the respective fields
5. Fill in communication address and contact information

### Step 3: Submit Documents
Choose one of two modes:

**Option A: Physical Documents (by post)**
- After online submission, print the acknowledgement
- Attach **self-attested copies** of supporting documents
- Send by post to NSDL's processing centre (address on the acknowledgement)
- Must reach within **15 days** of online application

**Option B: e-KYC / e-Sign (fully online)**
- Authenticate using **Aadhaar OTP**
- Documents are verified digitally
- No need to send physical documents
- **Recommended for faster processing**

### Step 4: Pay Fees
| Delivery | Fee |
|----------|-----|
| Indian address | ₹101 (+ GST) ≈ ₹120 |
| Foreign address | ₹1,011 (+ GST) ≈ ₹1,193 |

Pay via debit card, credit card, net banking, or UPI.

### Step 5: Track Application
1. Note your **15-digit acknowledgement number**
2. Track at [NSDL PAN Status](https://www.onlineservices.nsdl.com/paam/endUserRegisterContact.html) → "Track PAN Status"
3. Processing time: **15-20 working days**

## Method 2: Correction via UTIITSL Portal

### Step 1: Visit UTIITSL Portal
1. Go to [UTIITSL PAN Portal](https://www.pan.utiitsl.com)
2. Click **"PAN Card Correction/Change Request"**

### Step 2: Fill the Application
1. Enter your **PAN number**
2. Select fields to correct
3. Enter the correct information
4. Upload supporting documents (JPEG/PDF, max 2 MB each)

### Step 3: Pay and Submit
- Fee: ₹107 (+ GST) for Indian address
- Payment via debit card, credit card, or net banking
- Download the acknowledgement

### Step 4: Send Documents (If Physical Mode)
- Print acknowledgement form
- Attach self-attested document copies
- Post to UTIITSL address mentioned on the form
- Must reach within **15 days**

## Correction via Income Tax Portal (Aadhaar-Based)

For **minor corrections** that match Aadhaar data:

1. Visit [Income Tax Portal](https://www.incometax.gov.in)
2. Log in with your PAN and password
3. Go to **"Profile Settings"** → **"PAN Details"**
4. If your name/DOB on PAN doesn't match Aadhaar, there's an option to **update PAN details as per Aadhaar**
5. Authenticate with Aadhaar OTP
6. Details are updated automatically
7. **Note:** This only works if Aadhaar has the correct details

## Common Issues and Fixes

### PAN-Aadhaar Link Failing Due to Name Mismatch
1. First check which document has the correct name
2. If Aadhaar is correct → correct PAN using NSDL/UTIITSL
3. If PAN is correct → update Aadhaar name at [UIDAI](https://ssup.uidai.gov.in)
4. After correction, retry PAN-Aadhaar linking — see our [PAN-Aadhaar link guide](/guide/pan-card-aadhaar-link-failed-fix)

### Correction Request Rejected
- **Reason:** Supporting documents don't match the requested correction
- **Fix:** Reapply with stronger documents (e.g., passport + Class 10 certificate)
- Ensure the name/DOB on documents **exactly matches** what you're requesting

### Multiple PANs
- Having more than one PAN is illegal
- Surrender the extra PAN during the correction process
- Mention the PAN to be retained and the one to be cancelled

## Important Tips

1. **Use Aadhaar OTP (e-KYC) mode** for fastest processing — no physical documents needed
2. **Ensure your Aadhaar has correct details** before using the Income Tax Portal method
3. **Keep the acknowledgement number safe** — you'll need it to track status
4. **The PAN number itself never changes** — only the details on the card are corrected
5. **After correction, download e-PAN** from the Income Tax portal for immediate use while the physical card is in transit

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. How much does PAN card correction cost?

₹101-120 (including GST) for Indian address via NSDL. UTIITSL charges ₹107 + GST. Both portals have similar pricing.

### Q2. Can I correct PAN card details online without visiting any office?

Yes, using the e-KYC/Aadhaar OTP method on NSDL or UTIITSL portal. The entire process is online — no office visit or physical document submission needed.

### Q3. How long does PAN correction take?

7-15 working days with e-KYC, 15-20 days with physical documents. You'll receive the corrected PAN card by Speed Post.

### Q4. My PAN card has my old name (before marriage). How do I update?

Apply for name correction on NSDL/UTIITSL portal. Upload your marriage certificate as supporting proof along with Aadhaar or passport showing the new name.

### Q5. Will my PAN number change after correction?

No. Your PAN number (10-character alphanumeric) remains the same. Only the details printed on the card are corrected.

### Q6. Can I correct multiple fields at once?

Yes. You can correct name, DOB, father's name, address, and photo/signature all in a single application.

### Q7. I don't have my PAN card but know the number. Can I still apply for correction?

Yes. You only need the PAN number to apply online. The corrected card will be sent to your address. You can also download e-PAN from the Income Tax portal.
