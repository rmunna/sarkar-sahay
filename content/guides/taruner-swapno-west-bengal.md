**Taruner Swapno** (তরুণের স্বপ্ন — "Youth's Dream") is a West Bengal government scheme that provides **₹10,000 as financial assistance** to Class 11 students studying in government-run schools and madrasahs for purchasing a **tablet, smartphone or personal computer**. The scheme aims to bridge the digital divide and support students in accessing online educational resources.

## What is Taruner Swapno?

Launched by the West Bengal School Education Department, Taruner Swapno recognises that digital devices are essential for modern education. The scheme provides a one-time financial assistance of ₹10,000 directly to the bank accounts of eligible Class 11 students through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT), enabling them to purchase a digital device for their studies.

## Who is Eligible?

- Must be enrolled in **Class 11** in a **government-run or government-aided school or madrasah** in West Bengal
- Must be a **student of a West Bengal government school** (private school students are not covered)
- Must have a **bank account** in their own name
- Must have a valid **Aadhaar card** linked to the bank account
- All Class 11 students in eligible institutions qualify — **no income limit** or merit cutoff
- Students in both **Arts, Science and Commerce** streams are eligible

## Benefits

| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Financial assistance | ₹10,000 (one-time) |
| Purpose | Purchase of tablet, smartphone or PC |
| Transfer mode | Direct Benefit Transfer to student's bank account |
| Eligibility | All Class 11 students in government schools/madrasahs |

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar card of the student
- Bank account passbook (in student's name)
- School/madrasah identity card or enrolment proof
- Account mandate form (Annexure-F) provided by the school
- Passport-size photograph
- Student's mobile number

## Step-by-Step Process

### How to Get the Benefit

1. **Enrol** in Class 11 at a government-run or government-aided school/madrasah in West Bengal
2. **Open a bank account** in your name (if you don't have one) — preferably in a nationalised bank
3. **Collect** the prescribed Account Mandate Form (Annexure-F) from your school
4. **Fill** the form with your bank account details, IFSC code and Aadhaar number
5. **Attach** a copy of your bank passbook (first page showing name, account number, IFSC)
6. **Submit** the form to your school/madrasah within the deadline
7. **School verifies** your enrolment and submits the data to the Block/District office
8. **Data is uploaded** to the state portal for DBT processing
9. **₹10,000 is credited** directly to your bank account
10. **Use the amount** to purchase a tablet, smartphone or personal computer for your studies

### Verification at School Level

- The **Headmaster/Teacher-in-Charge** verifies each student's enrolment and bank details
- Verified data is compiled and forwarded to the Sub-Inspector of Schools or Block Education Office
- The Block office consolidates and uploads to the state education portal

## Fees

There is **no fee** to avail this scheme. It is a completely free benefit for eligible students.

## Processing Time

- **Form submission:** During the academic session (as per school notification)
- **Verification:** 15–30 days at school and block level
- **DBT credit:** Within 1–2 months of final verification
- **Total timeline:** 2–4 months from form submission

## Important Tips

1. **Open your bank account early** — don't wait until the deadline. A savings account in any nationalised bank works
2. **Ensure Aadhaar is linked** to your bank account — DBT will fail without Aadhaar seeding
3. **Fill the mandate form carefully** — any error in account number or IFSC code will delay payment
4. **Keep your bank passbook updated** — you may need to show proof of credit to the school
5. **This is a one-time benefit** for Class 11 only — it is not renewable in Class 12

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Taruner Swapno available for Class 12 students?

No. The scheme is currently available only for **Class 11 students** in government schools and madrasahs. It is a one-time benefit.

### Can private school students apply?

No. Only students of **government-run, government-aided or government-sponsored schools and madrasahs** in West Bengal are eligible.

### Do I need to submit bills for the device purchased?

Currently, the scheme provides the amount via DBT and **does not mandate submission of purchase bills**. However, the intended purpose is to buy a digital device for educational use.

### What if I don't have a bank account?

Open a savings account in any nationalised bank. You can open a **zero-balance account** under Jan Dhan Yojana. Carry your Aadhaar card, school ID and a photograph to the bank.

### Can the money be used for anything other than a device?

The scheme is intended for purchasing a tablet, smartphone or PC. While the DBT is unconditional, students are encouraged to use the funds for their educational benefit.

### How will I know when the money is credited?

You will receive an **SMS notification** from your bank when the DBT is credited. You can also check your bank passbook or online banking.
