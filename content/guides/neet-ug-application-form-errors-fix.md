# NEET UG Application Form Errors — How to Fix Common Problems

Filling the NEET UG application form on the NTA website can be stressful — and small mistakes or technical glitches can block your submission. This guide covers every common error candidates face and the exact steps to fix them.

> **Related:** [NEET UG Exam Guide — Complete Details](/guides/neet-ug-exam-guide)

---

## 1. Photo or Signature Upload Rejected

This is the most common error. NTA has strict specifications for the photo and signature.

### Required Specifications

| Item | Format | Size | Dimensions | Background |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Photograph** | JPG/JPEG | 10 KB – 200 KB | 3.5 cm × 4.5 cm | White background |
| **Signature** | JPG/JPEG | 4 KB – 30 KB | 3.5 cm × 1.5 cm | White background |
| **Left thumb impression** | JPG/JPEG | 10 KB – 50 KB | 3.5 cm × 1.5 cm | White background |
| **Class 10 marksheet** | JPG/JPEG | 50 KB – 300 KB | — | — |

### How to Fix

1. **Resize your image** — Use free tools like [iLoveIMG](https://www.iloveimg.com/resize-image) or MS Paint to match exact dimensions.
2. **Compress file size** — Use [TinyJPG](https://tinyjpg.com/) to bring the file within the size limit.
3. **Use white background** — NTA rejects photos with coloured or cluttered backgrounds. Use a plain white wall or edit the background.
4. **Photo must be recent** — Taken within the last 3 months, with your name and date printed below the photo.
5. **Clear signature** — Sign on a clean white paper with a black pen. Crop tightly around the signature.

> ⚠️ **Do not use selfies or filtered photos.** The photo must match your face during the exam — mismatch can lead to disqualification.

## 2. Wrong Category or State Selected

Selecting the wrong category (SC/ST/OBC-NCL/General/EWS) or wrong state of eligibility affects your seat allotment and fee.

### Can You Change It?

- **Before final submission:** You can go back and edit any field.
- **After submission, during correction window:** NTA opens a correction window (usually 3–5 days after the application deadline). You can change category, state, and most fields.
- **After correction window closes:** Changes are **not possible**. You must contact NTA directly.

### What to Do

1. Wait for the **correction window** announcement on [neet.nta.nic.in](https://neet.nta.nic.in).
2. Log in with your application number and password.
3. Edit the incorrect fields and re-upload documents if needed.
4. **Pay the fee difference** if your category change requires it (e.g., reserved to general).

> 💡 **Tip:** Double-check your category certificate's issuing authority — OBC-NCL certificates must be in the central list and issued within the current financial year for central counselling.

## 3. Payment Failed but Amount Deducted

Your payment was debited from your bank but the NTA portal shows "payment pending" or "not submitted."

### Why This Happens

- Internet disconnection during payment
- Bank server timeout
- Multiple payment attempts creating duplicate transactions

### How to Fix

1. **Wait 24–48 hours.** Most failed transactions are auto-refunded by the bank within 3–7 working days.
2. **Check application status.** Log in to [neet.nta.nic.in](https://neet.nta.nic.in) → Check if status shows "Confirmed." If yes, your payment went through.
3. **Do NOT pay again immediately.** This may cause a double deduction.
4. **If status still shows pending after 48 hours:**
   - Take a screenshot of the deducted amount from your bank statement.
   - Email NTA at **neet@nta.ac.in** with your application number, transaction ID, and bank statement screenshot.
   - Call the NTA helpline (see section below).
5. **If refund doesn't arrive in 7 days**, raise a dispute with your bank.

## 4. Correction Window Missed

If you missed the NTA correction window and have errors in your form:

### Options Available

- **Minor errors** (spelling, photo): NTA sometimes allows corrections at the exam centre. Carry original documents.
- **Critical errors** (wrong category, eligibility state): Write to NTA immediately at **neet@nta.ac.in** explaining the error. Attach supporting documents.
- **During counselling**: MCC (Medical Counselling Committee) may allow document verification corrections at the time of reporting to college. However, this is not guaranteed.

### Prevention

- Set a **calendar reminder** when the application opens — correction window dates are announced on the NTA website.
- Review your form **at least twice** before final submission.

## 5. Application Form Not Submitted (Incomplete)

You filled the form but forgot to click "Final Submit" or the page timed out.

### How to Know

- Log in to [neet.nta.nic.in](https://neet.nta.nic.in) and check your application status.
- If it says **"Incomplete"** or **"Draft"**, your form was not submitted.

### How to Fix

- **Before the deadline:** Log in, complete all steps (personal details → photo upload → payment → final submit), and submit.
- **After the deadline:** There is no way to submit a draft application after the deadline. You will have to apply in the next cycle.

### Common Reasons for Incomplete Forms

| Reason | Solution |
|---|---|
| Payment not completed | Complete payment before deadline |
| Photo/signature not uploaded | Upload before final submit |
| Session timed out | Use a stable internet connection, don't use mobile browser |
| Clicked "Save" instead of "Submit" | Go back and click "Final Submit" |

## 6. OTP Not Received

NTA sends OTP to your registered mobile number and email for registration and login.

### Troubleshooting Steps

1. **Wait 2–3 minutes** — OTPs can be delayed during peak hours (especially near deadlines).
2. **Check spam/junk folder** for email OTP.
3. **Ensure correct mobile number** — If you entered the wrong number during registration, you cannot change it without contacting NTA.
4. **Try "Resend OTP"** — Click the resend button after the timer expires. You usually get 3 attempts.
5. **Network issues** — Switch from Wi-Fi to mobile data or vice versa. DND (Do Not Disturb) settings on your phone can block NTA's SMS.
6. **Clear browser cache** and try in **incognito/private mode**.
7. **Try a different browser** — Chrome or Firefox on desktop works best with the NTA portal.

> If OTP still doesn't arrive after multiple attempts, contact NTA helpline immediately.

## 8. Common Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| Using a very old photograph | Photo mismatch → barred from exam |
| Not printing name and date below photo | NTA rejects the photo |
| Entering parent's mobile instead of own | OTP and updates go to wrong number |
| Selecting wrong exam centre city | You may have to travel far; correction window is limited |
| Not saving application number | You cannot log in or track your application |
| Paying through shared/third-party accounts | Refund goes to the payer, not you |
| Applying from mobile browser | NTA portal works best on desktop Chrome/Firefox |
| Waiting until last day to apply | Server crashes, no time to fix errors |

- [ ] Category and sub-category are correct (verify certificate)

- [ ] Mobile number and email are your own and active

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I change my NEET application form after final submission?**
A: Only during the NTA correction window. After that, changes are not possible through the portal.

**Q: My payment was deducted twice. What should I do?**
A: The duplicate amount will be auto-refunded in 7–10 working days. If not, email neet@nta.ac.in with both transaction IDs.

**Q: I uploaded the wrong photo. Can I change it?**
A: Yes, during the correction window. If the window has closed, carry your correct photo and ID to the exam centre.

**Q: The NTA website is not loading. What should I do?**
A: Try during off-peak hours (early morning or late night). Clear cache, use a different browser, or try a different internet connection.

**Q: I forgot my NEET application number. How to recover it?**
A: Use the "Forgot Application Number" option on the login page. You'll need your registered email or phone number.
