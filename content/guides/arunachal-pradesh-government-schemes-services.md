# Arunachal Pradesh Government Schemes and Services – Complete Guide

Arunachal Pradesh, the "Land of the Dawn-Lit Mountains," is India's northeastern frontier state governed by the BJP under Chief Minister Pema Khandu. Despite its challenging terrain and sparse population, the state government has rolled out an impressive range of welfare schemes covering healthcare, education, women empowerment, tribal welfare, horticulture, tourism, and digital governance.

This comprehensive hub covers all major Arunachal Pradesh government schemes and services — helping citizens, students, and families find the right benefits and apply with confidence.

## Health Schemes

### Chief Minister's Arogya Arunachal Yojana (CMAAY)

CMAAY is the flagship health insurance scheme of Arunachal Pradesh, providing cashless treatment coverage to all indigenous residents and eligible citizens of the state. It works in convergence with Ayushman Bharat – Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB-PMJAY).

**Key Details:**
- **Coverage:** Up to **₹5 lakh** per family per year for secondary and tertiary hospitalisation
- **Eligibility:** All Arunachal Pradesh Scheduled Tribe (APST) families and other eligible residents
- **Benefits:** Cashless treatment at empanelled hospitals across India
- **Card:** CMAAY Golden Card issued at Common Service Centres
- **Portal:** [cmaay.com](https://cmaay.com)

The scheme covers over 1,500 medical packages including surgeries, cancer treatment, cardiac procedures, and more. Both government and private empanelled hospitals are included.

## Women and Child Welfare Schemes

### Dulari Kanya Yojana

Launched to reduce infant mortality and promote institutional births of girl children, the Dulari Kanya scheme provides financial incentives to families upon the birth of a girl child in a government hospital.

**Key Details:**
- **Fixed Deposit:** ₹30,000 deposited at birth (enhanced from ₹20,000), maturing at age 18
- **Education Bonus:** ₹20,000 provided upon admission to Class XI
- **Direct Assistance:** ₹20,000 given directly to families for immediate expenses
- **Eligibility:** Institutional birth of a girl child in Arunachal Pradesh
- **Condition:** Girl must remain unmarried until age 18 to claim the maturity amount

The scheme encourages hospital deliveries, promotes girl child education, and discourages child marriage.

### Indira Gandhi Sarkari Yojana

This welfare initiative provides financial assistance to women from economically weaker sections for livelihood support and self-employment. Benefits are delivered through Direct Benefit Transfer to ensure transparency.

## Education and Scholarship Schemes

### Chief Minister's Vidya Scheme

The CM Vidya scheme aims to improve access to quality education for students in Arunachal Pradesh, particularly those from tribal and remote areas. The scheme provides:

- **Scholarships** for meritorious students pursuing higher education
- **Book grants and tuition fee reimbursement** for students from low-income families
- **Hostel and boarding support** for students studying outside the state
- **Focus** on STEM education and professional courses

### Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Swavalamban Yojana (DDUSY)

DDUSY promotes self-employment and entrepreneurship among the youth of Arunachal Pradesh. The scheme provides:

- **Subsidised loans** for starting small businesses and micro-enterprises
- **Skill development training** linked to market demand
- **Special focus** on tribal youth, women entrepreneurs, and rural applicants
- **Sectors covered:** Handicrafts, weaving, food processing, tourism services, and more

## Employment and Recruitment

### APPSC Examinations

The Arunachal Pradesh Public Service Commission (APPSC) conducts competitive examinations for recruitment into state government services. Key exams include:

- **APPSC Combined Competitive Examination (CCE)** — for state civil services (ACS, APS, allied services)
- **Assistant Engineer Recruitment** — for hydropower, PWD, and PHE departments
- **Departmental Examinations** — for serving government employees
- **Portal:** [appsc.gov.in](https://appsc.gov.in)

The state cabinet has approved reforms to streamline APPSC processes, ensuring fair and transparent recruitment with improved digital systems.

### Hydropower Employment Opportunities

Arunachal Pradesh holds over 50,000 MW of hydropower potential — the highest in India. The state government actively promotes employment in the hydropower sector through:

- **Direct recruitment** via APPSC for Assistant Engineers in hydropower development
- **Skill training programmes** for local youth in dam construction and maintenance
- **Policy incentives** for hydropower companies to hire local residents
- **Cabinet-approved hydropower development incentives** to boost investment and create jobs

## Agriculture and Horticulture Schemes

### Organic Farming and Horticulture Missions

Arunachal Pradesh is a leading producer of kiwi fruit, oranges, and other subtropical/temperate fruits. The state government runs several horticulture and organic farming schemes:

- **State Organic Farming Mission:** Supports farmers in transitioning to organic cultivation with subsidies on inputs, certification costs, and marketing support
- **Kiwi Development Programme:** Arunachal is India's largest kiwi producer; the scheme provides planting material, training, and market linkages to kiwi farmers
- **Orange Rejuvenation Scheme:** Revives declining orange orchards in Khasi Mandarin and other varieties through replanting, pest management, and improved cultivation practices
- **Integrated Horticulture Development Mission:** Covers floriculture, spices (large cardamom), and medicinal plants with input subsidies and cold storage support

Farmers can access scheme benefits through the District Horticulture Offices and Krishi Vigyan Kendras across the state.

## Rural Development

### Arunachal Pradesh Rural Mission

The state rural mission focuses on improving livelihoods, infrastructure, and connectivity in remote villages. Key components include:

- **Rural road connectivity** under PMGSY and state schemes to connect far-flung villages
- **Self-Help Group (SHG) formation and support** for women in rural areas
- **Livelihood promotion** through skill training in weaving, bamboo craft, and food processing
- **Rural housing** in convergence with Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (Gramin)
- **Drinking water supply** under Jal Jeevan Mission with state supplementation

## Tribal Welfare

Arunachal Pradesh is home to 26 major tribes and over 100 sub-tribes, with nearly 69% of the population classified as Scheduled Tribes. The state government provides comprehensive tribal welfare through:

- **Scheduled Tribe scholarships** — pre-matric and post-matric scholarships for APST students
- **Tribal hostel facilities** — government hostels in district headquarters and major cities
- **Cultural preservation grants** — funding for tribal festivals, museums, and documentation of indigenous languages
- **Land rights protection** — policies safeguarding tribal land ownership and community forests
- **Special Development Packages** for Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Groups (PVTGs)

Benefits are administered through the Department of Indigenous Affairs and the Directorate of Social Welfare.

## Tourism and Homestay Schemes

### Homestay and Tourism Promotion

Arunachal Pradesh's stunning landscapes, biodiversity, and tribal culture make it a growing tourism destination. The state government promotes tourism through:

- **Homestay Scheme:** Financial assistance of up to ₹50,000 for eligible households to set up registered homestays, promoting community-based tourism
- **Adventure Tourism Support:** Grants for setting up trekking, rafting, and camping infrastructure
- **Cultural Tourism Circuits:** Development of tribal village tourism corridors with heritage walks and festival tourism calendars
- **Eco-Tourism Projects:** Support for nature camps, birdwatching lodges, and wildlife tourism near national parks (Namdapha, Mouling, Pakke)
- **Skill training** for local youth in hospitality, guiding, and tourism management

The Department of Tourism issues Inner Line Permits (ILPs) required for non-APST visitors to enter the state.

## How to Apply for Arunachal Pradesh Government Schemes

Most state schemes follow a common application process:

1. **Check eligibility:** Visit the specific scheme portal or [myScheme.gov.in](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/search/state/Arunachal%20Pradesh) to check if you qualify
2. **Gather documents:** Typically required — Aadhaar card, APST certificate, income certificate, bank passbook, passport-size photos, and scheme-specific documents
3. **Apply online or offline:**
   - **Online:** Use e-District portal for certificates; CMAAY portal for health cards; APPSC portal for jobs
   - **Offline:** Visit the nearest Deputy Commissioner's office, Block Development Office, or Common Service Centre (CSC)
4. **Track application:** Use the reference number on the respective portal to track status
5. **Receive benefits:** DBT-linked schemes credit amounts directly to your bank account; health schemes provide cashless treatment at empanelled hospitals

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is CMAAY and who is eligible?

CMAAY (Chief Minister's Arogya Arunachal Yojana) is the state health insurance scheme providing up to ₹5 lakh cashless treatment per family per year. All APST families and eligible non-APST residents of Arunachal Pradesh can avail benefits at empanelled hospitals across India.

### How much does the Dulari Kanya scheme provide?

The enhanced Dulari Kanya scheme provides ₹30,000 as a fixed deposit at institutional birth of a girl child (maturing at age 18), ₹20,000 upon Class XI admission, and ₹20,000 as direct family assistance — totalling up to ₹70,000 in benefits.

### How do I get an ST certificate in Arunachal Pradesh?

Apply through the e-District Arunachal portal at edistrict.arunachal.gov.in. You'll need supporting documents including proof of tribal identity, residential proof, and an affidavit. The certificate is issued by the Deputy Commissioner's office.

### What exams does APPSC conduct?

APPSC conducts the Combined Competitive Examination (CCE) for state civil services, departmental exams, and direct recruitment for various posts including Assistant Engineers. Check appsc.gov.in for current notifications and exam schedules.

### Do I need an Inner Line Permit to visit Arunachal Pradesh?

Yes, non-APST Indian citizens require an Inner Line Permit (ILP) to enter Arunachal Pradesh. ILPs can be obtained online through the Arunachal Pradesh government portal or at ILP counters in Guwahati, Delhi, and Kolkata.

### How can I start a homestay under the government tourism scheme?

Eligible households can apply through the Department of Tourism, Arunachal Pradesh. You'll need to have suitable accommodation in a tourist-friendly location, register your homestay, and meet basic amenity standards. Financial assistance of up to ₹50,000 is provided for approved homestays.
