# RC Transfer Online: How to Transfer Vehicle Ownership in India

When you buy or sell a used vehicle in India, transferring the Registration Certificate (RC) to the new owner's name is mandatory under the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988. The transfer must be completed within 14 days of the sale. This guide covers the complete process for RC transfer — both online via the Parivahan portal and offline at the RTO.

## What is RC Transfer?

RC Transfer is the legal process of changing vehicle ownership from the seller to the buyer in RTO records. It involves submitting Form 29 (notice of transfer) and Form 30 (report of transfer) along with supporting documents and fees. Without RC transfer, the previous owner remains legally liable for any violations or accidents involving the vehicle.

## Who Needs RC Transfer?

- Any person buying a used/second-hand vehicle (car, bike, scooter, truck, etc.)
- Both buyer and seller must participate in the transfer process
- Transfer within the same state or from one state to another (requires NOC)

## Documents Required

### From the Seller

- Original Registration Certificate (RC)
- Form 29 (Notice of Transfer of Ownership) — signed by seller
- Form 30 (Report of Transfer of Ownership) — signed by seller
- Valid [PUC Certificate](/guide/vehicle-pollution-certificate-puc-apply)
- [Insurance](/guide/vehicle-insurance-expired-renew) transfer or new policy in buyer's name
- PAN Card or Form 60
- Address proof ([Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online), passport, etc.)
- NOC from financer (if vehicle was under loan)
- Road tax receipt

### From the Buyer

- Address proof ([Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online), voter ID, passport)
- Identity proof (PAN card, Aadhaar)
- Passport-size photographs (2-3)
- [Insurance policy](/guide/vehicle-insurance-expired-renew) in buyer's name
- Valid [PUC certificate](/guide/vehicle-pollution-certificate-puc-apply)

### For Inter-State Transfer (Additional)

- NOC (No Objection Certificate) from the registering authority of the original state
- Road tax payment receipt of the new state

## Step-by-Step Online Process (Parivahan Portal)

### Step 1: Visit the Parivahan Portal

Go to [parivahan.gov.in](https://parivahan.gov.in/) and click on **"Online Services"** → **"Vehicle Related Services"**.

### Step 2: Select Your State and RTO

Choose the state and RTO office where the vehicle is currently registered.

### Step 3: Select "Transfer of Ownership"

From the list of services, select **"Transfer of Ownership"**.

### Step 4: Enter Vehicle Details

Enter the vehicle registration number and other required details (chassis number, engine number).

### Step 5: Fill Form 29 and Form 30

- **Form 29** — Notice of Transfer: Both buyer and seller must sign this form declaring the transfer
- **Form 30** — Report of Transfer: Contains details of the new owner

### Step 6: Upload Documents

Upload scanned copies of all required documents (RC, address proof, identity proof, insurance, PUC).

### Step 7: Pay the Fee Online

Pay the transfer fee and applicable taxes via net banking, debit card, or UPI.

### Step 8: Book an Appointment (if required)

Some RTOs require a physical inspection. Book an appointment for vehicle inspection at the RTO.

### Step 9: Visit RTO for Verification

Carry original documents and the vehicle for inspection (if applicable). The RTO officer will verify details and complete the transfer.

### Step 10: Receive Updated RC

After approval, the new RC in the buyer's name will be dispatched or can be collected from the RTO. This typically takes 7-15 working days.

## Offline Process at RTO

1. Obtain **Form 29** and **Form 30** from the RTO or download from the Parivahan website
2. Both buyer and seller must fill and sign the forms
3. Get the vehicle inspected at the RTO (if required)
4. Submit all documents along with the forms at the RTO counter
5. Pay the prescribed fee at the RTO cash counter
6. Collect the acknowledgment receipt
7. The new RC will be issued within 7-15 working days

| Transfer fee (four-wheeler) | ₹500 – ₹1,000 |

| Smart card fee | ₹200 – ₹300 |

> **Note:** Fees vary by state. Check your state RTO website for exact charges.

## Processing Time

- **Within same RTO:** 7-15 working days
- **Within same state (different RTO):** 15-30 working days
- **Inter-state transfer:** 30-60 working days (includes NOC process)

## NOC for Inter-State Transfer

If you are transferring a vehicle from one state to another:

1. Apply for NOC at the original state's RTO
2. The NOC is valid for 6 months from the date of issue
3. Submit the NOC along with transfer application at the new state's RTO
4. Pay applicable road tax in the new state
5. The old registration will be cancelled, and a new registration number will be issued

## Important Tips

1. **Complete within 14 days** — The Motor Vehicles Act requires the transfer intimation within 14 days of sale. Late transfers attract penalties.
2. **Get insurance transferred first** — Ensure the vehicle insurance is transferred to the buyer's name or a new policy is purchased before RC transfer.
3. **Verify hypothecation status** — If the vehicle had a loan, ensure the hypothecation is removed (Form 35) before initiating transfer.
4. **Check for pending challans** — Verify there are no pending [traffic challans](/guide/vehicle-challan-check-pay-online) on the vehicle before purchase.
5. **Keep photocopies** — Always keep photocopies of all submitted documents and the acknowledgment receipt.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. Can I transfer RC online without visiting the RTO?

For most states, you can initiate the process online but may need to visit the RTO for document verification and vehicle inspection. Some states like Delhi and Karnataka are moving towards fully online transfers.

### 2. What is the penalty for not transferring RC within 14 days?

A penalty of up to ₹500 – ₹5,000 can be imposed. Additionally, the previous owner remains legally liable for the vehicle.

### 3. Can I drive a vehicle with RC in the previous owner's name?

Technically, the vehicle can be driven with valid insurance and the original RC during the transfer process. However, it is advisable to complete the transfer at the earliest.

### 4. What if the seller is not available for RC transfer?

The seller can sign Form 29 and Form 30 in advance and provide attested copies of their documents. In some cases, a notarized authorization letter may be accepted.

### 5. Is vehicle inspection mandatory for RC transfer?

Vehicle inspection is mandatory in most states to verify the chassis number, engine number, and overall condition. Some states may waive it for newer vehicles.

### 6. How do I check the RC transfer status online?

Visit [parivahan.gov.in](https://parivahan.gov.in/), go to "Know Your Vehicle Details" and enter the registration number to check the current ownership status.

### 7. Can a vehicle with expired RC be transferred?

The RC must be renewed before transfer. If the RC is expired, apply for [RC renewal](/guide/vehicle-rc-renewal-online) first, then proceed with the transfer.
