# CBSE Board Exam Results — How to Check Class 10 and 12 Results Online

The Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) announces Class 10 and Class 12 board exam results every year on its official portals. This guide covers all the methods to check your CBSE results online, download your marksheet, and what to do after results are declared.

## Official Websites for CBSE Results

CBSE publishes results on these official portals:

| Portal | URL |
|--------|-----|
| CBSE Results (primary) | [results.cbse.nic.in](https://results.cbse.nic.in) |
| CBSE Results (redirect) | [cbseresults.nic.in](https://cbseresults.nic.in) |
| DigiLocker | [digilocker.gov.in](https://www.digilocker.gov.in) |
| UMANG App | [web.umang.gov.in](https://web.umang.gov.in) |

> **Tip:** On result day, websites experience heavy traffic. Try early morning or late night, or use DigiLocker/UMANG as alternatives.

---

## Method 1: Check CBSE Result on results.cbse.nic.in

This is the primary and most commonly used method.

1. Go to [results.cbse.nic.in](https://results.cbse.nic.in).
2. Click on **CBSE Class 10 Result** or **CBSE Class 12 Result** link.
3. Enter your **Roll Number**, **School Number**, and **Date of Birth**.
4. Enter the security code (captcha) shown on screen.
5. Click **Submit**.
6. Your result will be displayed on screen with subject-wise marks.
7. Click **Print** or take a screenshot for your records.

> **Note:** This is a provisional result. The original marksheet is issued by your school later.

---

## Method 2: Check CBSE Result via DigiLocker

DigiLocker provides digitally signed marksheets and certificates directly from CBSE. These are legally valid documents.

1. Go to [digilocker.gov.in](https://www.digilocker.gov.in) or open the DigiLocker app.
2. Log in with your **Aadhaar-linked mobile number**.
3. If you don't have an account, sign up using your Aadhaar number.
4. Once logged in, go to the **Issued Documents** section.
5. Search for **CBSE** under the issuing organisation.
6. Your marksheet and pass certificate will appear automatically if your Aadhaar is linked with CBSE records.
7. Download the digitally signed PDF.

> **Important:** CBSE pushes documents to DigiLocker automatically. If your Aadhaar was provided during registration, your marksheet will appear within a few days of result declaration. Check our [DigiLocker Guide](/guide/digilocker-guide) for detailed setup instructions.

---

## Method 3: Check CBSE Result on UMANG App

UMANG (Unified Mobile Application for New-age Governance) also provides CBSE results.

1. Download the **UMANG app** from Google Play Store or Apple App Store.
2. Open the app and register with your mobile number.
3. Search for **CBSE** in the services section.
4. Select **CBSE Results**.
5. Choose **Class 10** or **Class 12**.
6. Enter your **Roll Number** and other required details.
7. View and download your result.

---

## Method 4: Check CBSE Result via SMS

CBSE sometimes provides an SMS-based result service on result day.

- **Class 10:** SMS `CBSE10 <RollNumber> <SchoolNumber> <CentreNumber>` to the number announced by CBSE.
- **Class 12:** SMS `CBSE12 <RollNumber> <SchoolNumber> <CentreNumber>` to the number announced by CBSE.

> **Note:** The SMS number changes each year. Check CBSE's official website or news for the current year's SMS number before result day.

## Understanding Your CBSE Marksheet

Your CBSE marksheet contains the following details:

- **Subject-wise marks** — Theory and practical marks shown separately
- **Total marks** — Sum of all subjects
- **Grade** — Each subject is graded on a scale (for Class 10, grades are given based on positional grading)
- **Result status** — PASS, COMPARTMENT, or ESSENTIAL REPEAT
- **Roll Number, Name, Date of Birth** — Personal identification details

### Grading System (Class 10)

| Grade | Marks Range |
|-------|-------------|
| A1 | 91–100 |
| A2 | 81–90 |
| B1 | 71–80 |
| B2 | 61–70 |
| C1 | 51–60 |
| C2 | 41–50 |
| D | 33–40 |
| E (Failed) | Below 33 |

For Class 12, marks are shown as obtained marks out of total marks.

## What to Do After Results

### If You Passed

1. Collect your **original marksheet** and **pass certificate** from your school.
2. Download digitally signed copies from [DigiLocker](https://www.digilocker.gov.in).
3. If you need a **Migration Certificate** (for changing boards or moving to another state), apply through your school. The school forwards the request to the CBSE regional office.
4. Start your admission process for the next level of education.
5. Check if you are eligible for scholarships — see our [National Scholarship Portal Guide](/guide/national-scholarship-portal-apply).

### If You Got Compartment

A **compartment** means you have failed in one or two subjects but can appear for a supplementary exam.

- **Class 10:** Compartment is given if you fail in **1 or 2 subjects** (out of 5 main subjects).
- **Class 12:** Compartment is given if you fail in **1 subject** only.
- CBSE conducts the **compartment exam** usually within 2–3 months of the main result.
- You need to apply through your school within the deadline announced by CBSE.
- If you pass the compartment exam, your result is treated as a regular pass.

### If You Want to Improve Marks

- Students who have **passed** Class 12 can appear for **improvement exams** in up to 5 subjects in the next year's board exam.
- Apply through a CBSE-affiliated school.
- The **better of the two scores** (original or improvement) is considered in the final marksheet.
- Improvement facility is **not available for Class 10**.

## Re-evaluation and Verification of Marks

If you feel your answer sheet was not evaluated correctly, CBSE offers two options:

### 1. Verification of Marks

- This is a **re-check** — CBSE verifies that all answers have been evaluated and marks are correctly totalled.
- Apply online on the [CBSE website](https://www.cbse.gov.in) within **the deadline** (usually within 1 week of results).
- Fee: ₹500 per subject (subject to change).

### 2. Obtaining Photocopy of Answer Sheet

- You can request a **photocopy of your evaluated answer sheet**.
- Apply online within the deadline.
- Fee: ₹700 per subject (subject to change).
- After receiving the photocopy, if you find errors, you can apply for **re-evaluation**.

### 3. Re-evaluation

- Available only after you have obtained the photocopy and found discrepancies.
- Apply within the stipulated time after receiving the photocopy.
- Fee: ₹100 per question (subject to change).
- CBSE will re-evaluate the specific answers you have challenged.

> **Important:** Fees and deadlines are updated each year. Always check the latest circular on [cbse.gov.in](https://www.cbse.gov.in).

## How to Get Your Physical Marksheet and Certificates

1. **Original marksheet** and **pass certificate** are sent by CBSE to your school.
2. Collect them from your school, usually 1–2 months after results.
3. **Migration Certificate** — Apply through your school if you plan to study in a different board or state. Your school submits the application to the CBSE Regional Office.
4. **Duplicate marksheet** — If you lose your original, apply on the [CBSE website](https://www.cbse.gov.in) under the Duplicate Documents section. Fee applies.

For digitally signed documents, use [DigiLocker](/guide/digilocker-guide) — these are accepted by most universities and employers.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: When does CBSE declare Class 10 and 12 results?

CBSE usually declares results between **May and July** each year. Class 12 results are typically announced before Class 10. The exact date is announced on [cbse.gov.in](https://www.cbse.gov.in).

### Q2: What is the minimum passing marks in CBSE board exams?

For both Class 10 and Class 12, the minimum passing marks are **33% in each subject** (33 out of 100). For subjects with practical exams, you must pass in both theory and practical separately.

### Q3: Can I check my CBSE result using my name instead of roll number?

No. CBSE requires your **roll number** to check results. If you have lost your roll number, contact your school to retrieve it.

### Q4: Is the DigiLocker marksheet valid for college admission?

Yes. DigiLocker documents are digitally signed by CBSE and are **legally valid** under the IT Act. Most universities and institutions accept them.

### Q5: What if the CBSE result website is not loading?

Try these alternatives:
- Use [DigiLocker](https://www.digilocker.gov.in) or the UMANG app.
- Try during off-peak hours (early morning or late night).
- Clear your browser cache and try again.

### Q6: How long does CBSE take to process a re-evaluation request?

CBSE typically processes verification and re-evaluation requests within **2–4 weeks** after the application deadline. Results are communicated through the CBSE website and to your school.

### Q7: Can I apply for both verification and photocopy of answer sheet?

Yes. You can apply for **verification of marks** and also request a **photocopy of your answer sheet**. If you find errors in the photocopy, you can then apply for re-evaluation.
