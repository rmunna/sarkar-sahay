# UMANG EPF Service Not Working? Fix PF Balance Check Errors

The UMANG app provides quick access to **EPFO (Employees' Provident Fund Organisation)** services including PF balance check, passbook download, and claim tracking. If these services are not working for you, follow this troubleshooting guide.

## Common UMANG EPF Service Errors

1. **UAN not linked** or not recognized on UMANG
2. **EPF passbook not loading** or showing blank
3. **Claim status error** or "No records found"
4. **Server timeout** when checking PF balance
5. **Wrong PF balance** or outdated information

---

## Fix 1: UAN Not Linked on UMANG

If UMANG shows "UAN not found" or fails to link your UAN:

1. **Verify your UAN** — Log in to the [EPFO Member Portal](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in/memberinterface/) to confirm your UAN is active
2. **Check UAN activation** — Your UAN must be activated with your mobile number. If not, activate it on the EPFO portal first
3. **Ensure mobile number match** — The mobile number on UMANG must match the one registered with your UAN
4. **Link UAN on UMANG:**
   - Open UMANG → Search for **"EPFO"**
   - Select **"Employee Centric Services"**
   - Enter your **UAN** and verify with OTP
5. **Check KYC status** — Incomplete KYC (Aadhaar, PAN, bank account) on the EPFO portal can cause linking failures
6. **Contact employer** — If UAN is not activated, ask your HR department to activate it through the employer portal

> **Tip:** You can check your UAN status by sending an SMS: "EPFOHO UAN" to 7738299899 from your registered mobile number.

---

## Fix 2: EPF Passbook Not Loading

If your EPF passbook shows blank or fails to load on UMANG:

1. **Check if passbook is updated** — EPFO updates passbooks periodically. Your employer's latest contribution may take 3-7 days to reflect
2. **Try the direct portal** — Visit [passbook.epfindia.gov.in](https://passbook.epfindia.gov.in/) to check if the passbook loads there
3. **Clear UMANG app cache:**
   - Settings → Apps → UMANG → Storage → Clear Cache
4. **Check your internet speed** — Passbook download requires a stable connection. Switch to Wi-Fi if on mobile data
5. **Try during off-peak hours** — EPFO servers are heavily loaded between 10 AM and 4 PM
6. **Verify UAN-Aadhaar linking** — Passbook access requires UAN to be linked with Aadhaar

---

## Fix 3: Claim Status Error or "No Records Found"

If you've submitted a PF withdrawal or transfer claim and the status is not showing:

1. **Wait for processing** — PF claims take 10-20 working days to process. Check after this period
2. **Verify claim submission** — Log in to the [EPFO Member Portal](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in/memberinterface/) and check under "Track Claim Status"
3. **Check claim reference number** — Ensure you're entering the correct claim reference number
4. **Verify KYC approval** — Claims require approved KYC (Aadhaar + bank account). Check KYC status on the EPFO portal
5. **Contact EPFO directly:**
   - **Toll-free:** 1800-118-005
   - **Email:** grievance at epfindia.gov.in
6. **File a grievance** — If the claim is stuck, file a grievance at [epfigms.gov.in](https://epfigms.gov.in/)

---

## Fix 4: Server Timeout When Checking PF Balance

If UMANG shows timeout errors for EPF services:

1. **Try alternative methods to check PF balance:**
   - **SMS:** Send "EPFOHO UAN ENG" to 7738299899
   - **Missed call:** Give a missed call to 011-22901406 from your registered number
   - **Web portal:** [passbook.epfindia.gov.in](https://passbook.epfindia.gov.in/)
2. **Retry during off-peak hours** — Early morning (7-9 AM) or evening (7-10 PM)
3. **Update the UMANG app** — Outdated versions may have timeout issues
4. **Switch networks** — Try Wi-Fi if on mobile data, or vice versa
5. **Use the UMANG web version** — Visit [web.umang.gov.in](https://web.umang.gov.in/)

## How to Check PF Balance on UMANG (Step-by-Step)

1. Open the **UMANG** app and log in with your MPIN
2. Search for **"EPFO"** in the search bar
3. Select **"Employee Centric Services"**
4. Tap **"View Passbook"**
5. Enter your **UAN (Universal Account Number)**
6. Verify with **OTP** sent to your UAN-registered mobile
7. Your **EPF passbook** with balance details will be displayed

> See our [EPF withdrawal guide](/guide/epf-pf-withdrawal-online) for details on claiming your PF balance.

## Important Tips

1. Always keep your UAN KYC updated — link Aadhaar, PAN, and bank account on the EPFO portal
2. Your UMANG mobile number and UAN-registered mobile number should ideally be the same for seamless OTP verification
3. EPF balance updates depend on employer contributions — contact HR if contributions are not reflected
4. Download your passbook as PDF from the EPFO portal for official records

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Why is my PF balance showing zero on UMANG?
Your employer may not have deposited the latest contributions, or the passbook may not be updated. Check with your employer and also verify on the [EPFO portal](https://passbook.epfindia.gov.in/) directly.

### Q2: Can I withdraw PF through UMANG?
No, PF withdrawal claims must be submitted through the [EPFO Member Portal](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in/memberinterface/). UMANG only allows you to view balance, passbook, and track claim status.

### Q3: My UAN is not activated. How do I activate it?
Visit the [EPFO Member Portal](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in/memberinterface/), click "Activate UAN," enter your UAN, Aadhaar, and mobile number, and verify with OTP.

### Q4: How long does it take for employer contributions to reflect in the passbook?
Employer contributions typically reflect within 3-7 days after the employer uploads the ECR (Electronic Challan cum Return). Monthly updates may take until the 15th of the following month.

### Q5: Can I check PF balance without the UMANG app?
Yes. You can check via SMS (send "EPFOHO UAN ENG" to 7738299899), missed call (011-22901406), or the [EPFO passbook portal](https://passbook.epfindia.gov.in/).

### Q6: What is the UMANG helpline number for EPF issues?
For UMANG-specific issues, call 1800-115-565. For EPFO-specific issues, call 1800-118-005.

### Q7: Why does UMANG ask for Aadhaar OTP for EPF services?
EPFO uses Aadhaar-based authentication for security. The OTP is sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile number to verify your identity before showing sensitive financial data.
