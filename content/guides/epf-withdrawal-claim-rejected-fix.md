# EPF Withdrawal Claim Rejected — Fix PF Settlement Errors

Getting your EPF (Employees' Provident Fund) withdrawal claim rejected is frustrating, especially when you need the money urgently. This guide covers every common reason for EPF claim rejection and how to fix each one.

## How to Check EPF Claim Status

Before troubleshooting, check your claim status to understand why it was rejected.

### Steps:

1. Visit the **EPFO Member Portal**: [https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in/memberinterface/](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in/memberinterface/)
2. Log in with your **UAN (Universal Account Number)** and password
3. Go to **"Online Services"** → **"Track Claim Status"**
4. You'll see your claim with one of these statuses:
   - **Under Process** — claim is being reviewed
   - **Settled** — money has been sent to your bank
   - **Rejected** — claim was rejected (reason will be shown)
5. Note the **rejection reason** — this is key to fixing the issue

## Top Reasons for EPF Claim Rejection and Fixes

### Reason 1: KYC Mismatch (Name/DOB Not Matching)

**The most common reason.** Your name, date of birth, or other details in EPFO records don't match your Aadhaar or bank account.

**How to Fix:**

1. Log in to the **EPFO Member Portal**
2. Go to **"Manage"** → **"KYC"**
3. Check if your **Aadhaar, PAN, and Bank Account** are verified (green tick)
4. If name doesn't match:
   - Check for spelling differences (e.g., "Raj Kumar" vs "Rajkumar")
   - Request your **employer** to correct the name in EPFO records via the employer portal
   - Or update your Aadhaar name to match — see our [Aadhaar name change guide](/guide/aadhaar-name-correction-online)
5. If DOB doesn't match:
   - Request employer to submit a **Joint Declaration (Form 5)** with correct DOB
   - Attach supporting documents (Aadhaar, PAN, 10th marksheet)
6. After correction, wait **2-3 days** for KYC to re-verify, then resubmit claim

---

### Reason 2: Bank Account Details Incorrect

Your bank IFSC code, account number, or account holder name doesn't match.

**How to Fix:**

1. Go to **"Manage"** → **"KYC"** on the Member Portal
2. Check the **bank account** linked to your UAN
3. Verify:
   - Account number is correct (no extra/missing digits)
   - IFSC code matches your branch (check on bank's website)
   - Account holder name matches your EPFO name exactly
4. If incorrect, **delete the old bank KYC** and **add the correct bank details**
5. Your employer must **approve the new bank KYC** via their portal
6. Wait for **digital verification** (usually 1-3 days)
7. Resubmit the withdrawal claim

> **Tip:** Use the same bank account that was linked to your salary account for faster verification.

---

### Reason 3: Employer Has Not Approved the Claim

For many claim types, your employer (or former employer) must approve the request.

**How to Fix:**

1. Contact your **HR department** or **employer's EPF administrator**
2. Ask them to log in to the **EPFO Employer Portal** and approve your pending claim
3. If former employer is unresponsive:
   - Send a written email requesting approval (keep as evidence)
   - Wait 10 days — if no response, EPFO may auto-approve certain claims
   - File a grievance on **EPFO iGMS**: [https://epfigms.gov.in/](https://epfigms.gov.in/)
   - Mention the employer's non-cooperation in the grievance
4. For **Form 19 (full withdrawal)**, employer approval is mandatory unless you file through the EPFO field office directly

### Reason 4: Form 19 / Form 31 Errors

Using the wrong form or filling it incorrectly causes rejection.

**Which form to use:**

| Form | Purpose | When to Use |
|---|---|---|
| Form 19 | Final PF Settlement (full withdrawal) | After leaving job and completing 2 months of unemployment |
| Form 10C | Pension Withdrawal | Withdraw EPS pension contribution (under 10 years of service) |
| Form 31 | Partial/Advance Withdrawal | Medical, housing, education, marriage (while still employed or between jobs) |

**Common Form Errors:**

| Error | Fix |
|---|---|
| Wrong form selected | Cancel and resubmit with correct form |
| Reason for withdrawal not matching form type | Form 31: select correct reason (medical/housing/education) |
| Service period insufficient for Form 31 | Some advances require 5-7 years of service — check eligibility |
| Form 19 submitted before 2 months gap | Wait for 2 months after leaving job, then resubmit |
| Missing documents for Form 31 | Attach required proofs (medical bills, property papers, etc.) |

---

### Reason 5: UAN Not Activated or Not Linked to Aadhaar

EPFO requires an active UAN linked to Aadhaar for online claims.

**How to Fix:**

1. Check UAN activation: go to [https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in/memberinterface/](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in/memberinterface/)
2. If not activated:
   - Click **"Activate UAN"**
   - Enter UAN, Aadhaar/PAN, mobile number, and captcha
   - Verify with OTP
3. Link Aadhaar:
   - Go to **"Manage"** → **"KYC"**
   - Add Aadhaar number
   - Employer must approve the Aadhaar KYC
   - UIDAI verification may take 2-3 days
4. Once UAN is active and Aadhaar is linked, resubmit claim

---

### Reason 6: Insufficient Service Period

Some withdrawal types require a minimum service period.

| Withdrawal Type | Minimum Service Required |
|---|---|
| Full Withdrawal (Form 19) | 2 months gap after leaving job |
| Housing Advance (Form 31) | 5 years of service |
| Medical Advance (Form 31) | No minimum |
| Education/Marriage (Form 31) | 7 years of service |
| Pre-retirement (at age 54) | Age 54+ |

**Fix:** If you don't meet the service requirement, you cannot withdraw for that purpose. Check if you qualify under a different reason.

---

### Reason 7: Multiple Member IDs / UAN Issues

If you have multiple PF accounts or UANs, claims may get rejected.

**How to Fix:**

1. Log in to the Member Portal
2. Go to **"Our Services"** → **"One Member – One EPF Account"**
3. **Transfer** old PF accounts to your current UAN using **Form 13** (online)
4. Once all accounts are merged, resubmit the claim
5. If transfer request is stuck, file a grievance on EPFO iGMS

## How to Resubmit EPF Claim After Fixing Issues

1. Fix the rejection reason (KYC, bank details, employer approval, etc.)
2. Wait **2-3 days** for changes to reflect in the system
3. Log in to the **EPFO Member Portal**
4. Go to **"Online Services"** → **"Claim (Form 31, 19, 10C)"**
5. Select the correct form and fill in details
6. Verify with **Aadhaar OTP**
7. Submit the claim
8. Track status under **"Track Claim Status"**
9. If approved, money is credited to your bank within **7-15 working days**

## How to File EPFO Grievance

If your claim is repeatedly rejected or stuck:

1. Visit **EPFO iGMS**: [https://epfigms.gov.in/](https://epfigms.gov.in/)
2. Click **"Register Grievance"**
3. Select **"Member"** as the grievance category
4. Enter your **UAN, PF number, and mobile number**
5. Describe the issue in detail — include:
   - Claim reference number
   - Rejection reason
   - Steps you've already taken to fix it
6. Submit and note the **grievance registration number**
7. Expected response: **7-15 working days**
8. You can also call the **EPFO helpline**: **1800-118-005** (toll-free)

## Important Tips

1. **Ensure all KYC (Aadhaar, PAN, Bank) is verified** before submitting any claim — check for green ticks
2. **Match your name exactly** across Aadhaar, PAN, bank account, and EPFO records
3. **Get employer approval quickly** — follow up with HR within 3 days of submitting a claim
4. **Don't submit Form 19 before 2 months** — it will be auto-rejected
5. **Use EPFO iGMS** for unresolved issues — it creates a trackable record

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: How long does EPF withdrawal take after claim is approved?
Once approved, the amount is typically credited to your bank account within 7-15 working days. SBI and nationalized bank transfers are usually faster.

### Q2: Can I withdraw EPF while still employed?
Yes, using **Form 31** for specific reasons like medical emergency, housing, education, or marriage. Each reason has eligibility criteria and service period requirements.

### Q3: What if my employer has shut down and can't approve my claim?
File a grievance on EPFO iGMS mentioning the employer's closure. Visit your regional EPFO office with your PF passbook, last salary slip, and ID proof. The EPFO office can process claims without employer approval in such cases.

### Q4: How do I check my EPF balance?
Check via **EPFO Passbook**: [https://passbook.epfindia.gov.in/](https://passbook.epfindia.gov.in/). Log in with your UAN and password. You can also send an SMS with "EPFOHO UAN" to 7738299899.

### Q5: Can I withdraw both PF and pension amounts?
Yes. Use **Form 19** for PF withdrawal and **Form 10C** for pension withdrawal. If you have more than 10 years of service, pension can only be withdrawn as a scheme certificate (Form 10C is not applicable).

### Q6: My EPF claim was rejected due to "digital signature not verified." What does this mean?
This usually means the employer's digital signature on the claim is not valid. Contact your employer's HR to resubmit the approval with a valid digital signature.

### Q7: Can I cancel a submitted EPF claim and resubmit?
If the claim is still "Under Process," you may not be able to cancel it online. Wait for it to be processed or rejected, then resubmit with corrections. Contact EPFO helpline for urgent cancellation.
