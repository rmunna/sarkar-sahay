# CM's Breakfast Scheme Tamil Nadu – Free Breakfast for School Children

## What is the CM's Breakfast Scheme?

The Chief Minister's Breakfast Scheme provides **free nutritious breakfast** to all students studying in **Classes 1 to 5 in government schools** across Tamil Nadu. The scheme aims to improve school attendance, reduce malnutrition, and ensure that children do not study on an empty stomach. It is an extension of Tamil Nadu's historic mid-day meal programme.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Students** studying in **Classes 1 to 5**
2. Must be enrolled in a **Tamil Nadu government school** or government-aided school
3. **No income criteria** — all students in eligible schools automatically benefit
4. **No application required** — breakfast is served at school

## Menu Details

The breakfast menu is designed by nutritionists and includes:

| Day | Sample Menu |
|-----|-------------|
| Monday | Upma with vegetables |
| Tuesday | Pongal with sambar |
| Wednesday | Semiya Upma |
| Thursday | Rava Kichadi |
| Friday | Lemon Rice/Variety Rice |

*Note: The actual menu may vary by school and is updated periodically to ensure variety and nutrition.*

Each meal provides approximately **300-400 calories** with adequate protein and micronutrients.

## How the Scheme Works

1. **Schools prepare breakfast** in the school kitchen every morning
2. **Breakfast is served** before the first class begins (typically 8:00-8:30 AM)
3. **All Class 1-5 students** in the school are served
4. **No fee or registration** required from parents
5. Quality is monitored by school management and government inspectors

## Key Features

- **Completely free** — no cost to parents
- **Nutritious and balanced** meals designed by experts
- **Served fresh** every school day
- **Covers all government schools** across Tamil Nadu
- Complements the **Mid-Day Meal** (lunch) scheme

## Important Tips for Parents

1. **Send Children on Time:** Breakfast is served before classes start. Send your child early to benefit.
2. **Check Quality:** If the food quality is poor, report to the school headmaster or the local BEO (Block Education Officer).
3. **Allergies:** Inform the school if your child has any food allergies so that arrangements can be made.
4. **Encourage Attendance:** The breakfast + mid-day meal ensures your child gets two nutritious meals at school.
5. **Participate in Monitoring:** Parents can join school management committees to monitor food quality.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Do I need to apply for this scheme?
**No.** All students in Classes 1-5 in government schools automatically receive breakfast. No application or registration is needed.

### Q2: Is breakfast available on holidays and Saturdays?
**No.** Breakfast is served only on **working school days**.

### Q3: Are private school students eligible?
**No.** Only students in government and government-aided schools are covered.

### Q4: What if my child is in Class 6 or above?
Students in Class 6 and above are not covered under the breakfast scheme but continue to receive the **mid-day meal (lunch)**.

### Q5: Who monitors the food quality?
The school headmaster, teachers, parent-teacher committee, and government food inspectors all participate in quality monitoring.

### Q6: Is this scheme available in all districts?
**Yes.** The CM's Breakfast Scheme covers government schools across all 38 districts of Tamil Nadu.
