# Two Wheeler Loan — How to Apply for Bike Loan Online

Buying a bike or scooter is one of the most common financial goals in India. Whether you want a commuter bike for daily travel or a premium motorcycle, a **two wheeler loan** makes it affordable by letting you pay in easy EMIs. This guide covers everything — interest rates, eligibility, documents, EMI calculation, and how to apply online.

## What Is a Two Wheeler Loan?

A two wheeler loan (also called a bike loan) is a type of personal vehicle loan offered by banks and NBFCs to help you purchase a new or used motorcycle, scooter, or moped. Key features:

- **Loan amount:** ₹20,000 to ₹10 lakh (varies by lender)
- **Tenure:** 6 months to 5 years (up to 60 months)
- **Down payment:** Usually 10–30% of on-road price
- **Funding:** Up to 85–100% of ex-showroom price (some lenders cover on-road price)
- **Collateral:** The vehicle itself acts as security (hypothecation)

## Eligibility Criteria

Most banks and NBFCs have similar eligibility requirements:

| Criteria | Details |
|---|---|
| **Age** | 21–65 years at loan maturity |
| **Income (Salaried)** | ₹10,000–₹15,000/month minimum |
| **Income (Self-employed)** | ₹1,50,000/year minimum |
| **Employment** | Minimum 1 year work experience |
| **CIBIL Score** | 650+ (750+ for best rates) |
| **Residence** | Indian citizen or resident |

> 💡 A higher [CIBIL score](/guide/cibil-score-check-improve) significantly improves your chances of approval and helps you get lower interest rates.

## How to Apply for Two Wheeler Loan Online

### Step 1: Compare Lenders
Compare interest rates, processing fees, and loan terms from multiple banks. Use an [EMI calculator](/guide/emi-calculator-how-emi-works) to estimate your monthly payments.

### Step 2: Check Eligibility
Visit the lender's website and use the eligibility calculator. Ensure your CIBIL score is above 650.

### Step 3: Fill Online Application
- Go to the lender's official website or app
- Navigate to "Two Wheeler Loan" or "Bike Loan"
- Fill in personal details, employment info, and vehicle details
- Upload required documents

### Step 4: Get Approval
The lender verifies your documents and credit score. Most online applications get instant or same-day approval.

### Step 5: Loan Disbursement
Once approved, the loan amount is disbursed directly to the dealer. You pay the down payment, and the bike is yours!

### Popular Online Application Links

## EMI Calculation Example

The EMI formula is:

**EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n / [(1+r)^n – 1]**

Where: P = Principal, r = monthly interest rate, n = number of months.

### Example Calculation

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Bike price (on-road) | ₹1,20,000 |
| Down payment (20%) | ₹24,000 |
| Loan amount | ₹96,000 |
| Interest rate | 11% p.a. |
| Tenure | 3 years (36 months) |
| **Monthly EMI** | **₹3,143** |
| Total interest paid | ₹17,148 |
| Total amount paid | ₹1,13,148 |

| Charge | Typical Range |
|---|---|

| **Prepayment/Foreclosure** | 2%–5% of outstanding (some banks: nil after 6 months) |
| **Late payment penalty** | 1%–2% per month on overdue EMI |
| **Bounce charges** | ₹300–₹750 per instance |
| **Documentation charges** | ₹200–₹500 (some lenders waive this) |
| **Insurance** | Mandatory comprehensive insurance for Year 1 |

## Tips to Get a Lower Interest Rate

1. **Maintain a high CIBIL score** — 750+ gets you the best rates. [Check your score here](/guide/cibil-score-check-improve).
2. **Compare multiple lenders** — Don't settle for the first offer. Compare at least 3–4 lenders.
3. **Opt for shorter tenure** — Lower tenure means less total interest, even if EMI is slightly higher.
4. **Pay a higher down payment** — A 30–40% down payment reduces loan amount and interest burden.
5. **Apply through your salary bank** — Existing customers often get preferential rates.
6. **Negotiate processing fees** — Many lenders waive or reduce processing fees during festivals.
7. **Look for festive offers** — Navratri, Diwali, and year-end sales often come with reduced interest rates.

## New Bike Loan vs Used Bike Loan

| Feature | New Bike Loan | Used Bike Loan |
|---|---|---|
| **Interest rate** | 7.60%–15% p.a. | 14%–24% p.a. |
| **Loan tenure** | Up to 5 years | Up to 3 years |
| **Loan-to-value** | Up to 100% of ex-showroom | 50%–70% of current value |
| **Down payment** | 10%–20% | 30%–50% |
| **Processing** | Faster, fully online | May require physical inspection |
| **Availability** | All banks & NBFCs | Select NBFCs (Bajaj, Hero FinCorp) |

> For used bike loans, the vehicle's age is typically capped at 5–7 years. The total of vehicle age + tenure should not exceed 8–10 years.

## Important Tips Before Taking a Bike Loan

- **Read the fine print** — Check for hidden charges like documentation fees, insurance bundling, and mandatory add-ons
- **Don't stretch beyond your budget** — Your total EMI (all loans) should not exceed 40% of monthly income
- **Get insurance sorted** — Comprehensive insurance is mandatory for the first year and highly recommended for subsequent years
- **Keep documents ready** — Having all documents ready speeds up approval
- **Plan for total cost** — Factor in registration, insurance, accessories, and servicing costs beyond the bike price
- **Consider [car loan options](/guide/car-loan-apply-online-compare)** if your budget allows — four-wheelers may offer better value for families

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What is the minimum CIBIL score for a two wheeler loan?
Most banks require a minimum CIBIL score of 650. However, a score of 750 or above helps you get better interest rates and faster approval. Some NBFCs may approve loans for scores as low as 600 with higher interest rates.

### Can I get a bike loan with zero down payment?
Some lenders like Bajaj Finance and Hero FinCorp offer up to 100% financing on select models, effectively requiring zero down payment. However, paying at least 10–20% upfront is recommended to reduce your EMI burden.

### How long does bike loan approval take?
Online applications typically get approved within 24–48 hours. If you apply through your existing bank with pre-approved offers, approval can be instant. Offline applications may take 3–5 working days.

### Can I prepay or foreclose my two wheeler loan?
Yes, most lenders allow prepayment after 6–12 months. Some banks charge a foreclosure fee of 2–5% of the outstanding amount, while others (especially for floating rate loans) allow free prepayment as per RBI guidelines.

### Is bike loan interest tax deductible?
No, two wheeler loan interest is **not** eligible for tax deduction under the Income Tax Act for personal use. However, if the vehicle is used for business purposes and registered in the business name, the interest and depreciation may be claimed as business expenses.

### What happens if I miss an EMI payment?
Missing an EMI attracts a late payment fee (1–2% of overdue amount) and negatively impacts your [CIBIL score](/guide/cibil-score-check-improve). Consecutive missed payments can lead to loan default and vehicle repossession.

### Can I transfer my bike loan to another bank?
Yes, bike loan balance transfer is possible if another lender offers a lower interest rate. You'll need to pay the foreclosure fee to the current lender and processing fee to the new one. This is worthwhile if the rate difference is 2%+ and significant tenure remains.
