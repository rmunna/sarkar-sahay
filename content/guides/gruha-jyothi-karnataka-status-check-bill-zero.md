# Gruha Jyothi Karnataka 2026 — Free 200 Units, Status Check & Bill Zero Fix

**Gruha Jyothi** is the Karnataka government's scheme giving **200 units of free electricity per month** to all domestic households in Karnataka. If you consume up to 200 units, your bill is ₹0. Above 200 units, you pay only for the excess.

> **Quick answer:** Check your Gruha Jyothi status at [sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in). If your bill is still not zero despite consuming under 200 units, the most common reason is that Aadhaar is not linked to your electricity consumer number — fix at your nearest BESCOM/GESCOM office.

---

## How Gruha Jyothi Works

| Usage This Month | Bill Amount |
|---|---|
| Up to 200 units | **₹0 (completely free)** |
| 201 units | You pay only for 1 unit (above 200) |
| 300 units | You pay for 100 units at normal rate |
| 500 units | You pay for 300 units at normal rate |

The scheme covers all BESCOM, GESCOM, HESCOM, MESCOM, and CHESCOM consumers across Karnataka.

## How to Check Gruha Jyothi Application Status

**Method 1: Via Seva Sindhu Portal**

**Step 1:** Go to [sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in)

**Step 2:** Click **"Track Application Status"**

**Step 3:** Enter your application reference number or **Aadhaar number**

**Step 4:** Status shows: Applied / Verified / Active / Rejected

**Method 2: Via BESCOM Consumer Portal (for BESCOM area)**

**Step 1:** Go to [bescom.karnataka.gov.in](https://bescom.karnataka.gov.in)

**Step 2:** Click **"Pay Bill"** → enter your consumer number

**Step 3:** If Gruha Jyothi is active, bill for ≤200 units shows ₹0

**Method 3: Check Your Electricity Bill**

Look for **"Gruha Jyothi Rebate"** or **"Subsidy Applied: ₹XXX"** on your monthly bill. If this line appears, the scheme is active.

## Gruha Jyothi Bill Not Zero — Fix by Reason

### Reason 1: Aadhaar Not Linked to Consumer Number (Most Common)

Your electricity consumer number must be linked to your Aadhaar for the subsidy to apply automatically.

**Fix:**
- Visit your nearest **BESCOM/GESCOM/HESCOM** subdivision office
- Carry: Aadhaar card + latest electricity bill (showing consumer number)
- Ask for "Aadhaar seeding with consumer number" — done in 1–2 working days
- Next month's bill will be zero

### Reason 2: Application Not Submitted / Pending Verification

**Fix:**
- Go to [sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in) → Gruha Jyothi
- Check if your application is still "pending" — re-submit if needed
- Or apply fresh using your Aadhaar + consumer number

### Reason 3: More Than One Electricity Connection in Family

Gruha Jyothi is for **one connection per household**. If there are multiple connections at the same Aadhaar, only one gets the benefit.

**Fix:** Identify which connection to keep under Gruha Jyothi and update at the ESCOM office.

### Reason 4: Consumer Number Type Not "Domestic" (LT-2)

Only **LT-2 (Domestic)** tariff consumers are eligible. Commercial (LT-5) or industrial connections are NOT eligible.

**Fix:** Check your bill — the tariff category is printed on it. If it's commercial, contact ESCOM for re-categorization if it's genuinely a domestic connection.

### Reason 5: Name Mismatch — Aadhaar vs ESCOM Records

**Fix:**
- Visit the ESCOM office
- Get the consumer number transferred to the correct Aadhaar name
- Carry: Aadhaar + property document / ration card showing ownership

## How to Apply for Gruha Jyothi (New Registration)

**Step 1:** Visit [sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in) → Gruha Jyothi

**Step 2:** Click **"Apply Now"**

**Step 3:** Enter your **Aadhaar number** and OTP

**Step 4:** Enter your **electricity consumer number** (printed on your bill)

**Step 5:** Verify your details and submit

**Step 6:** Note the application reference number — status in 7–15 working days

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does Gruha Jyothi work for rented homes?**
Yes — but the Aadhaar linked to the consumer number must match the registered consumer or the tenant's Aadhaar must be added by the owner. Practically: get the ESCOM office to link your Aadhaar to the consumer number.

**I consumed 195 units but still got a ₹50 bill. Why?**
Check your bill for fixed charges (meter rent, etc.) — these may be charged separately. The 200-unit exemption covers energy charges; some ESCOMs still charge meter rent. Contact your ESCOM for clarification.

**My Gruha Jyothi was active last month but not this month. Why?**
Usually because your Aadhaar link was reset during a system update. Visit the ESCOM office to re-link.
