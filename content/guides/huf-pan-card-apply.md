# HUF PAN Card Apply: How to Get PAN for Hindu Undivided Family

A Hindu Undivided Family (HUF) is treated as a separate entity for income tax purposes in India. To file tax returns and conduct financial transactions, every HUF needs its own PAN (Permanent Account Number). This guide explains how to apply for a PAN card for HUF step by step.

## What is HUF PAN Card?

A PAN card issued to a Hindu Undivided Family is a 10-digit alphanumeric number allotted by the Income Tax Department. It is different from the individual PAN of the Karta (head of HUF). The HUF PAN is mandatory for:

- Filing income tax returns for the HUF
- Opening a bank account in the HUF name
- Making investments and financial transactions
- Purchasing or selling property under HUF

## Who is Eligible?

- Any Hindu Undivided Family as recognized under Hindu law
- The Karta (eldest male/female member) applies on behalf of the HUF
- Jain, Sikh, and Buddhist families can also form an HUF
- The HUF must have a valid HUF deed

## Documents Required

### Proof of Identity of HUF
- HUF deed (mandatory)
- Affidavit signed by Karta declaring HUF details

### Proof of Identity of Karta
- Aadhaar card of Karta
- Passport of Karta
- Voter ID of Karta

### Proof of Address of HUF
- Aadhaar card of Karta (as address proof)
- Electricity bill / water bill / telephone bill (in name of Karta or HUF)
- Bank account statement of HUF or Karta
- Passport of Karta

### Additional Documents
- Copy of PAN cards of all coparceners (members)
- Passport-size photographs of Karta

## Step-by-Step Process

### Online Process (via NSDL/Protean)

1. Visit the NSDL PAN application portal at [onlineservices.nsdl.com](https://www.onlineservices.nsdl.com/paam/endUserRegisterContact.html)
2. Select **"Form 49A"** (for Indian citizens/entities)
3. Under "Category of Applicant," select **"Hindu Undivided Family (HUF)"**
4. Fill in the HUF name, Karta details, and address
5. Enter the date of HUF deed as the "Date of Birth/Incorporation"
6. Upload supporting documents (HUF deed, Karta ID proof, address proof)
7. Upload the Karta's photograph and signature
8. Pay the fee of **₹107** (including GST) online via net banking, debit card, credit card, or demand draft
9. Submit the form and note the **acknowledgement number**
10. If documents are sent physically, print the acknowledgement, attach documents, and courier to NSDL within 15 days

### Online Process (via UTIITSL)

1. Visit [pan.utiitsl.com](https://www.pan.utiitsl.com/)
2. Click on **"PAN Card for Indian Citizen / NRI"**
3. Select Form 49A and category as HUF
4. Fill in all required details
5. Upload documents and pay ₹107 fee
6. Submit and note the token/coupon number

### Offline Process

1. Download **Form 49A** from the NSDL or UTIITSL website
2. Fill in the form in **BLOCK LETTERS** with black ink
3. Attach self-attested copies of all required documents
4. Paste two passport-size photographs of the Karta
5. Submit the form at the nearest PAN application centre (NSDL or UTIITSL)
6. Pay ₹107 fee at the counter
7. Collect the acknowledgement receipt

## Processing Time

- **Online with e-KYC:** 7–10 working days
- **Online with physical documents:** 15–20 working days
- **Offline application:** 15–20 working days
- **e-PAN (digital copy):** Available on email within 7 days of processing

## Important Tips

1. The HUF deed is the most critical document — get it drafted on stamp paper and notarized before applying
2. The HUF name on PAN will be the name mentioned in the HUF deed
3. Keep the acknowledgement number safe to track your application status on the NSDL/UTIITSL portal
4. Open a bank account in HUF name after receiving PAN for tax-saving investments
5. If the Karta changes due to death or other reasons, apply for PAN correction with updated HUF deed

## How to Track HUF PAN Application Status

1. Visit [tin.tin.nsdl.com/pantan/StatusTrack.html](https://tin.tin.nsdl.com/pantan/StatusTrack.html)
2. Select "PAN" and enter your 15-digit acknowledgement number
3. Click "Submit" to view current status

## FAQs

### Can an HUF have its own PAN card?
Yes. Under Indian income tax law, HUF is a separate taxable entity and must have its own PAN for filing returns and financial transactions.

### What form is used for HUF PAN application?
Form 49A is used for HUF PAN application, the same form used by Indian individuals. Select "HUF" under category of applicant.

### What is the fee for HUF PAN card?
The fee is ₹107 (including GST) for Indian address and ₹1,017 for foreign address communication.

### Is HUF deed mandatory for PAN application?
Yes. The HUF deed is the primary document that establishes the existence of the HUF and lists its members. Without it, the PAN application will be rejected.

### Can a woman be the Karta of an HUF?
Yes. After a Supreme Court ruling, the eldest female member can also be the Karta of an HUF and can apply for PAN on behalf of the HUF.

### How long does it take to get HUF PAN?
Typically 7–20 working days depending on whether you apply online with e-KYC or submit physical documents.

### Can I apply for HUF PAN and individual PAN simultaneously?
Yes. HUF PAN and individual PAN are separate. The Karta will have their own individual PAN and the HUF will have a different PAN number.
