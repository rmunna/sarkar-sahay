# PM Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY) 2025 – Complete Guide

## What is PM Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana?

Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY) is a government-backed life insurance scheme launched on 9th May 2015. It provides a life cover of **₹2 lakh** in case of death (due to any reason) at an annual premium of just **₹436** — making it one of the most affordable term life insurance plans in the world.

Unlike [PM Suraksha Bima Yojana](/guide/pm-suraksha-bima-yojana) which covers only accidental death, PMJJBY covers **death due to any cause** — illness, accident, natural causes, or any other reason during the policy period.

## Coverage Details

| Event | Cover Amount |
|-------|-------------|
| **Death due to any cause** | ₹2,00,000 |

> Coverage period: 1st June to 31st May each year. Premium is auto-debited annually.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Indian citizens** aged **18 to 50 years** (coverage continues till age 55)
- Must have a **savings bank account** with any participating bank
- Must have **Aadhaar linked** to the bank account
- Must give **consent for auto-debit** of premium
- One PMJJBY policy per person
- **No medical examination required** for enrollment

### Age Limits
- **Enrollment age:** 18 to 50 years
- **Coverage continues:** Till the age of 55 (even if enrolled at 50)
- **Auto-termination:** At age 55 or on closing the bank account

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar card linked to bank account (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- Savings bank account with any participating bank
- Consent-cum-declaration form (available at bank)
- Nominee details (name, relationship, Aadhaar)
- Self-declaration of good health

## Step-by-Step Enrollment Process

### Through Net Banking / Mobile Banking

1. **Login** to your bank's net banking portal or mobile app
2. **Navigate** to "Insurance" or "Social Security Schemes"
3. **Select** "PMJJBY" or "PM Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana"
4. **Review** your details — name, date of birth, Aadhaar
5. **Enter/update** nominee details
6. **Declare** good health status
7. **Give consent** for annual auto-debit of ₹436
8. **Confirm** enrollment — SMS confirmation received
9. **Download** the insurance certificate

### Through Bank Branch

1. **Visit** your bank branch
2. **Collect** the PMJJBY enrollment form
3. **Fill** the form with personal and nominee details
4. **Sign** the self-declaration of good health
5. **Submit** with Aadhaar copy and auto-debit mandate
6. **Enrollment processed** within 7-10 working days

### Through CSC / BC Agent

1. **Visit** nearest Common Service Centre
2. **Provide** bank account and Aadhaar details
3. **Fill** the form, sign the health declaration
4. **Pay** ₹436 premium

## Premium and Fees

| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| **Annual premium** | ₹436 (auto-debited from bank account) |
| Enrollment fee | **Free** |
| Claim processing fee | **Free** |
| GST | Included in ₹436 |

> Premium is auto-debited between 25th May and 1st June each year. Maintain sufficient balance.

## How to File a Claim

1. **Nominee** must visit the bank branch of the deceased
2. **Collect** the PMJJBY claim form
3. **Submit** the following documents:
   - Claim form (duly filled and signed by nominee)
   - Death certificate (original or attested copy)
   - Nominee's Aadhaar card (copy)
   - Nominee's bank account details (for claim credit)
   - Cancelled cheque of nominee's account
4. Bank verifies and forwards to the **insurance company**
5. Insurance company processes the claim
6. **₹2 lakh credited** to nominee's bank account within **30-60 days**

### Claim Rejection Scenarios
- Death occurred outside the coverage period (June 1 to May 31)
- Premium was not successfully debited
- Subscriber was above 55 years at time of death
- Fraud or misrepresentation in enrollment

## Processing Time

- **Enrollment:** Immediate (net banking) or 7-10 days (branch)
- **Claim settlement:** 30-60 days after complete document submission
- **Auto-renewal:** Automatic every June 1st

## Important Tips

1. **Combine with PMSBY** — Enroll in both PMJJBY (₹436) and [PMSBY](/guide/pm-suraksha-bima-yojana) (₹12) for total coverage of **₹4 lakh** at just **₹448/year**.
2. **Maintain bank balance** — Keep at least ₹436 in your account before June 1st. Failed auto-debit means lapsed coverage with no grace period.
3. **Update nominee regularly** — Ensure your nominee details are current. Disputes between family members can delay claim settlement.
4. **Self-declaration of good health** — While no medical exam is needed, you must declare that you are in good health at the time of enrollment. Fraudulent declarations can lead to claim rejection.
5. **Enroll all eligible family members** — At ₹436/person/year, ensure your spouse and adult children (18+) are also covered.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: What is the premium for PMJJBY?
**₹436 per year**, auto-debited from your savings bank account. This includes applicable taxes and covers you for one year (June 1 to May 31).

### Q2: Does PMJJBY cover accidental death as well?
**Yes.** PMJJBY covers death due to **any cause** — natural, accidental, illness, or any other reason. For additional accidental disability cover, also enroll in [PMSBY](/guide/pm-suraksha-bima-yojana).

### Q3: Is there a medical check-up required?
**No medical examination** is required. You just need to sign a self-declaration of good health at the time of enrollment.

### Q4: Can I enroll after the age of 50?
**No.** The maximum enrollment age is 50 years. However, if you enrolled before 50, coverage continues until age 55.

### Q5: What if my premium auto-debit fails?
Your coverage lapses immediately. To re-enroll, you must pay the premium and submit a fresh self-declaration of good health. There is no grace period.

### Q6: Can I have PMJJBY from multiple banks?
**No.** Only one PMJJBY policy per person is allowed, linked to one bank account. Multiple enrollments will be identified and only one will be valid.

### Q7: Is the claim amount taxable?
The claim amount of ₹2 lakh received by the nominee is **exempt from income tax** under Section 10(10D) of the Income Tax Act.
