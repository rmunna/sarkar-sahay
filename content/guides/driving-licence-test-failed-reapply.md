## What Happens When You Fail the Driving Licence Test?

If you fail the driving licence (DL) test — either the **learner's licence (LL) test** or the **permanent DL driving test** — don't panic. You can **reattempt the test** after a mandatory waiting period. The rules are straightforward and the process is fully online through the Sarathi/Parivahan portal.

This guide covers the **waiting period, rebooking process, fees**, and **tips to pass** on your next attempt.

## Types of DL Tests

### 1. Learner's Licence Test (Computer-Based)
- Multiple-choice questions on traffic rules, road signs, and vehicle regulations
- Usually **15-20 questions**, must score **60% or above** to pass
- Conducted at the RTO on a computer terminal

### 2. Permanent DL Driving Test (Practical)
- Conducted at the RTO test track or on road
- Tests: Figure of 8, H-track, slope/gradient, parking, traffic signals
- Evaluated by an RTO inspector

## Waiting Period After Failure

| Test Type | Waiting Period | Notes |
|-----------|---------------|-------|
| Learner's Licence (LL) Test | **7 days** | Can reattempt after 7 days from failure date |
| Permanent DL Driving Test | **7 days** | Some states allow rebooking within 7-30 days |
| Maximum Attempts | **No limit** | You can attempt as many times as needed |

**Important:** Your learner's licence is valid for **6 months** from the date of issue. If you fail the DL driving test multiple times and your LL expires, you'll need to **get a new LL first** before reattempting the driving test.

## How to Rebook Your DL Test — Step by Step

### For Learner's Licence Test (Reattempt)

1. Visit [Sarathi portal](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in)
2. Select your **state**
3. Click **"Apply Online"** → **"New Learner's Licence"**
4. Enter your **application number** from the previous attempt
5. Select **"LL Test Reattempt"** or reapply for LL
6. Choose a new **test slot date** (after the 7-day waiting period)
7. Pay the **reattempt fee** (varies by state, typically ₹50-200)
8. Get your new slot confirmation
9. Visit the RTO on the scheduled date with your documents

### For Permanent DL Driving Test (Reattempt)

1. Visit [Sarathi portal](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in)
2. Select your **state**
3. Click **"Apply Online"** → **"New Driving Licence"**
4. Enter your **LL number** or application number
5. Select **"DL Test Slot Booking"**
6. Choose an available date (after 7-day waiting period)
7. Pay the **retest fee** (typically ₹50-300 depending on state)
8. Note your slot date and time
9. Visit the RTO on the scheduled date with your LL and vehicle

## Documents Required for Reattempt

- **Learner's Licence** (original) — for DL driving test reattempt
- **Application receipt** from previous attempt
- **Aadhaar card** or address proof
- **Passport-size photographs** (2-3)
- **Medical certificate** (Form 1A) — if not already submitted
- **Vehicle** for driving test — with valid insurance and registration

## Tips to Pass the Learner's Licence Test

1. **Study the Motor Vehicles Act** — questions are based on traffic rules and signs
2. **Use practice apps** — many apps simulate the LL test with actual questions
3. **Learn road signs** — know the difference between mandatory, cautionary, and informatory signs
4. **Focus on right-of-way rules** — common question topic
5. **Read questions carefully** — many mistakes happen due to rushing
6. **Arrive early** — being relaxed helps you focus

### Common LL Test Topics
- Traffic signal meanings (red, yellow, green, flashing)
- Speed limits in different zones
- Overtaking rules
- Parking rules
- Alcohol limits and penalties
- Road sign identification
- One-way and no-entry rules

## Tips to Pass the Driving Test

### Figure of 8 (Most Common Failure Point)
- Practice this **extensively** before the test
- Keep a slow, steady speed — don't rush
- Use clutch control (for manual vehicles)
- Look ahead, not at the ground
- Don't touch the boundary markers

### H-Track
- Take wide turns
- Use mirrors (or turn your head — examiners check this)
- Keep the vehicle in first gear

### Slope/Gradient Test
- Practice hill start with handbrake
- Don't roll back more than a foot
- Smooth clutch release is key

### General Tips
1. **Practice at the RTO test track** if your state allows it (some RTOs have practice sessions)
2. **Bring a well-maintained vehicle** — engine stalling fails you immediately in some RTOs
3. **Wear your seatbelt** and adjust mirrors before starting
4. **Follow the instructor's commands** exactly
5. **Don't argue** with the examiner if they point out mistakes
6. **Be confident but careful** — examiners look for safe driving, not speed
7. **Take driving lessons** from a registered driving school if you're not confident

## What If Your LL Expires Before Passing?

If your Learner's Licence expires (6-month validity):

1. You must **apply for a new LL** through Sarathi
2. Pass the **LL test again**
3. Wait the mandatory **30 days** after getting LL (before applying for DL)
4. Then book and take the DL driving test

To avoid this, plan your DL test well before LL expiry.

## Important Tips

1. **Book your reattempt early** — popular RTO slots fill up fast
2. **Keep your LL validity in mind** — don't let it expire before getting your DL
3. **Practice on the actual test track** if possible
4. **Some RTOs offer automated tests** — these are more standardised and often easier
5. **Check your state's specific rules** — some states have slight variations in waiting period

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. How many times can I reattempt the DL test?

There's no limit on attempts. You can reattempt as many times as needed within your LL validity period (6 months).

### Q2. Do I need to pay again for each reattempt?

Yes, a reattempt fee (₹50-300) is charged for each attempt. The exact amount depends on your state.

### Q3. Can I take the DL test at a different RTO?

Generally, you should take the test at the RTO where you applied. Some states allow transfer to another RTO within the state — check with your local RTO.

### Q4. Is the waiting period 7 days or 30 days?

The waiting period for a **reattempt after failure** is typically **7 days**. The 30-day period is the mandatory gap between **getting your LL and applying for DL** (first attempt).

### Q5. What if I fail only one part of the test?

In most RTOs, you need to pass the entire driving test. If you fail one component (e.g., Figure of 8), you'll need to retake the full test.

### Q6. Can I change my vehicle type between attempts?

You should test in the same vehicle category as your LL. If your LL is for LMV (Light Motor Vehicle), test with a car. For MCWG (motorcycle), use a two-wheeler.

### Q7. How do I check my test result online?

Visit [Sarathi portal](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in) → "Application Status" → Enter your application number. The result is usually updated within 1-2 days of the test.

### Q8. My LL is about to expire and I failed the test. What should I do?

Book the earliest available slot for reattempt. If you can't get a slot before LL expiry, you'll need to reapply for a new LL, pass the LL test again, wait 30 days, and then attempt the DL test.
