# Mobile Tower Near House Complaint: How to File in India

Mobile towers (cell towers) are essential for telecom connectivity, but many residents worry about electromagnetic field (EMF) radiation from towers installed near homes, schools, or hospitals. The Department of Telecommunications (DoT) and TRAI have set strict guidelines for mobile tower installation and radiation limits. If a tower near your house violates these norms, you can file a complaint.

## TRAI and DoT Guidelines for Mobile Towers

The Indian government regulates mobile tower installations through several rules:

- **EMF radiation limits**: India follows radiation norms that are **10 times stricter** than ICNIRP (International Commission on Non-Ionizing Radiation Protection) guidelines. The limit is **1/10th** of the international standard.
- **Self-certification**: Telecom operators must submit a self-certification to DoT that their towers comply with EMF radiation norms.
- **Tarang Sanchar portal**: DoT launched [Tarang Sanchar](https://tarangsanchar.gov.in/) to allow citizens to check EMF compliance of towers near them and request radiation testing.
- **Distance norms**: While there is no mandatory minimum distance rule from DoT, many municipal corporations and state governments have local rules requiring towers to be a certain distance from residential areas, schools, and hospitals.

## Who Can File a Complaint?

Any citizen who believes a mobile tower near their house:

- Exceeds EMF radiation limits
- Was installed without proper permissions
- Violates local municipal or state government norms
- Is causing health concerns in the neighbourhood

## Step-by-Step Process to File a Complaint

### Method 1: Through Tarang Sanchar Portal (Online)

1. Visit [tarangsanchar.gov.in](https://tarangsanchar.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"Request EMF Measurement"** or **"Check Tower Compliance"**
3. Enter the location details of the tower (address, landmark, pin code)
4. You can view all towers in your area on the map
5. Submit an **EMF testing request** — the TERM (Telecom Enforcement, Resource & Monitoring) cell will schedule a physical audit
6. You will receive a reference number for tracking
7. The TERM cell will visit the site, measure radiation levels, and share the report

### Method 2: Complaint to TERM Cell (Offline)

1. Identify the TERM cell office for your state/zone from the [DoT website](https://www.dot.gov.in/)
2. Write a formal complaint letter addressed to the **Deputy Director General (DDG), TERM Cell** of your zone
3. Include:
   - Your full name and address
   - Exact location and address of the mobile tower
   - Name of the telecom operator (if known)
   - Nature of complaint (radiation concern, illegal installation, etc.)
   - Supporting photographs
4. Send by post or email to the TERM cell office
5. The TERM cell is required to conduct an audit within **30 days**

### Method 3: Complaint to Municipal Corporation

1. Visit your local municipal corporation or nagar palika office
2. File a complaint that the tower was installed **without valid building permission** or **violates local zoning rules**
3. Attach:
   - Photographs of the tower
   - Location details
   - Any health complaints from residents (if applicable)
4. The municipal authority can issue a notice to the telecom operator and even order tower removal if norms are violated

### Method 4: Centralized Public Grievance Portal

1. Visit [pgportal.gov.in](https://pgportal.gov.in/)
2. Register and log in
3. File a grievance under **Department of Telecommunications**
4. Describe your complaint with tower location and issue details
5. Upload supporting documents and photos
6. You will receive a grievance ID for tracking
7. The department must respond within **30 days**

## Documents Required

- **Photograph** of the mobile tower with location visible
- **Written complaint** describing the issue
- **Identity proof** (Aadhaar card, voter ID) — see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
- **Address proof** showing you live near the tower
- **Medical reports** (if claiming health issues due to radiation)
- **Signatures of neighbourhood residents** (strengthens complaint)
- **RTI reply or municipal records** (if available, showing tower permission status)

## EMF Radiation Limits in India

| Frequency Range | Indian EMF Limit (W/m²) |
|---|---|
| 900 MHz (2G/3G) | 0.45 |
| 1800 MHz (2G/4G) | 0.90 |
| 2100 MHz (3G/4G) | 1.00 |
| 2300 MHz (4G/5G) | 1.00 |

These limits are **1/10th** of ICNIRP standards. If a tower exceeds these limits, the telecom operator faces a penalty of ₹10 lakh per tower and must bring emissions into compliance within a fixed timeline.

## Penalties for Non-Compliance

- **First violation**: ₹10 lakh penalty on the telecom operator
- **Continued violation**: Tower shutdown can be ordered by DoT
- **Illegal installation**: Municipal corporation can order demolition and impose fines

## Processing Time

- **Tarang Sanchar EMF test**: 15-30 days for audit
- **TERM cell complaint**: 30 days for investigation
- **Municipal corporation**: Varies by city (15-60 days)
- **PG Portal grievance**: 30 days for initial response

## Important Tips

1. **Use Tarang Sanchar first** — it is the fastest way to get an official EMF radiation test done for any tower
2. **File with multiple authorities** — submit complaints to both TERM cell and municipal corporation for faster action
3. **Collect neighbourhood support** — a collective complaint signed by multiple residents carries more weight
4. **Keep copies** of all complaints and acknowledgement receipts
5. **Check local rules** — many states and cities have specific rules about tower distance from schools and hospitals

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is mobile tower radiation harmful to health?

According to WHO and Indian government studies, mobile towers operating within prescribed EMF limits do not pose proven health risks. However, India maintains stricter limits (1/10th of international norms) as a precautionary measure.

### Can I get a mobile tower removed from near my house?

Yes, if the tower violates EMF radiation limits, lacks proper permissions, or breaks local municipal rules, authorities can order its removal. File complaints through Tarang Sanchar and your municipal corporation.

### How do I check radiation levels of a tower near me?

Visit [tarangsanchar.gov.in](https://tarangsanchar.gov.in/), enter your location, and request an EMF measurement. The TERM cell will conduct a physical audit and share the report.

### What is the TERM cell?

TERM (Telecom Enforcement, Resource & Monitoring) cell is the enforcement wing of DoT. It monitors telecom operators for compliance with rules including EMF radiation limits, tower permissions, and service quality.

### Is there a minimum distance rule for mobile towers from houses?

DoT does not mandate a specific minimum distance nationally, but many municipal corporations and state governments have local rules (often 50-100 metres from schools and hospitals). Check your local municipal norms.

### Can I file an RTI to check if a tower has permission?

Yes. File an RTI application with your municipal corporation or DoT asking for the tower's installation permission documents and EMF compliance certificates.

### What happens after I file a complaint on Tarang Sanchar?

The TERM cell schedules a site visit, measures EMF radiation using calibrated equipment, and generates a report. If radiation exceeds limits, the operator is penalised and must reduce emissions.
