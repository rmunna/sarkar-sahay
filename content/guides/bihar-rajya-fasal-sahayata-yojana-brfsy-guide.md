# Bihar Rajya Fasal Sahayata Yojana (BRFSY) 2026 — Free Crop Insurance for Bihar Farmers

**Important**: Bihar does **NOT** participate in PMFBY (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana). If you're searching for crop insurance in Bihar, you need **BRFSY — Bihar Rajya Fasal Sahayata Yojana** (Bihar State Crop Assistance Scheme). Bihar has been running this state-funded scheme since 2018.

> **Key advantage**: BRFSY is **completely free** — farmers pay **zero premium**. The state government bears the entire cost.

## What is Bihar Rajya Fasal Sahayata Yojana?

BRFSY is Bihar's own crop protection scheme launched in 2018 when Bihar chose to exit PMFBY. It compensates farmers for crop losses due to flood, drought, pest attack, or other natural calamities through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to their bank accounts.

## Key Benefits

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Premium** | ₹0 — completely free for farmers |
| **Compensation (up to 20% loss)** | ₹7,500 per hectare |
| **Compensation (above 20% loss)** | ₹10,000 per hectare |
| **Maximum Area** | 2 hectares per farmer |
| **Maximum Payout** | ₹20,000 per farmer |
| **Payment Mode** | DBT to Aadhaar-linked bank account |
| **Crops Covered** | As notified each season (wheat, paddy, maize, mustard, etc.) |

## Eligibility

- **Landowner or tenant farmer** in Bihar
- Must cultivate the notified crop in the notified area
- Must have enrolled **before the season deadline** (enrollment is mandatory each season)
- Should have an **Aadhaar-linked bank account**
- No outstanding PACS loan dues (cooperative society loans)
- Must be enrolled on **DBT Agriculture Bihar portal**

## How to Apply for BRFSY

### Online Application at dbtagriculture.bihar.gov.in

1. Visit **[dbtagriculture.bihar.gov.in](https://dbtagriculture.bihar.gov.in/)**
2. Click on **"Bihar Rajya Fasal Sahayata Yojana"** on the homepage
3. Click **"Registration/Apply"**
4. Login with your **registered mobile number / Aadhaar**
5. If new: first **register as a farmer** on DBT Agriculture Bihar portal
6. Select **Kharif** or **Rabi** season
7. Fill in your land details (Khata Number, Khesra/Survey Number)
8. Select the **crop you are cultivating**
9. Upload required documents
10. Submit and note your **Application ID**

### Documents Required

- Aadhaar card (mandatory)
- Bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked account)
- Land documents: Jamabandi / Land Record (from bhulekh.bihar.gov.in or your LRC)
- Mobile number registered with Aadhaar
- For tenant farmers: agreement or declaration from landowner

### Application Deadlines (Approximate)

| Season | Crop | Enrollment Deadline |
|--------|------|---------------------|
| **Kharif** | Paddy, Maize, etc. | By end of July/August |
| **Rabi** | Wheat, Mustard, etc. | By end of December/January |

> Deadlines change each year — always check the official portal for current year's last date.

## How Claims Are Processed (No Need to "File" a Claim)

Unlike PMFBY, BRFSY does **not require individual loss notification**. The process is:

1. **State does area-wise crop cutting experiments (CCE)** to measure average yield
2. If yield falls below threshold → **all enrolled farmers in that area get compensation**
3. Payment is calculated automatically based on the shortfall
4. **DBT credit** to your bank account

> You do NOT need to individually report crop damage within 72 hours like PMFBY. The area-based approach means if your region suffered loss, you'll get compensation automatically (if enrolled).

## Check BRFSY Application/Payment Status

## Common Issues and Fixes

### "Not enrolled before deadline — can I still apply?"

No. BRFSY enrollment must happen **before** crop sowing or within the announced window. Late enrollment is not accepted. You must wait for the next season.

### "Applied but payment not received"

1. Verify area-level crop loss was officially recorded by the state
2. If the yield assessment showed less than threshold loss for your area, no payment is made even if individual farmers lost crops
3. Check your **NPCI Aadhaar mapper** — ensure your Aadhaar is seeded in the current bank
4. Contact your local **Block Agriculture Officer (BAO)** with your registration number

### "Name mismatch between Aadhaar and land records"

Common problem — your name in Bihar land records (Jamabandi) may differ from Aadhaar. Fix:
- Update Aadhaar name to match land records (or vice versa) at nearest Aadhaar centre
- Or get a certificate from your Block Development Office confirming both names refer to the same person

### "PACS Loan Due — ineligible message"

If your PACS (Primary Agricultural Credit Society) loan is overdue, you may be flagged as ineligible. Clear dues at your nearest PACS before applying.

## BRFSY vs PMFBY — Key Differences for Bihar Farmers

| Feature | BRFSY (Bihar) | PMFBY (Other States) |
|---------|--------------|----------------------|
| **Who manages** | Bihar state government | Central + state joint |
| **Premium** | Zero | 2% (Kharif), 1.5% (Rabi) |
| **Coverage** | Area-based yield | Individual or area-based |
| **Loss notification** | Not required individually | 72-hour notification required |
| **Maximum per farmer** | ₹20,000 (2 ha) | Proportional to sum insured |
| **Payment speed** | After annual assessment | Within 2 months of loss event |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Bihar farmers get confused between PMFBY and BRFSY. Which should I apply for?

Apply for **BRFSY only**. Bihar opted out of PMFBY in 2018. There is no PMFBY available for Bihar farmers. If someone tells you to apply for PMFBY in Bihar, that information is incorrect.

### I am a tenant farmer. Can I apply?

Yes, tenant farmers are eligible. You need a declaration or written agreement from the landowner. Some blocks also accept a registered tenant farming document.

### Can I get both BRFSY and Krishi Input Anudan (KIA) for the same crop?

Yes, these are two separate schemes. KIA provides input cost compensation for flood/drought damage (for crop re-sowing). BRFSY compensates for yield loss. You can receive both for the same season if eligible for each.

### What crops are covered under BRFSY?

The state notifies the crops each season. Commonly covered: Paddy (Kharif), Maize (Kharif), Wheat (Rabi), Mustard (Rabi). Vegetable and horticulture crops may have separate schemes (HORTICULTURE).
