# e-Shram Card Download Not Working? Fix PDF Errors

Having trouble downloading your **e-Shram card**? You're not alone — thousands of workers face download issues on the e-Shram portal daily. Whether your PDF won't generate, UAN is not found, or the server is down, this guide covers every fix.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent information platform and is not affiliated with any government body. Always verify information on [eshram.gov.in](https://eshram.gov.in).

## Common e-Shram Download Errors

Before jumping to fixes, identify your specific error:

| Error | Likely Cause |
|-------|-------------|
| "UAN Not Found" | Incorrect UAN or incomplete registration |
| PDF not generating | Server overload or browser issue |
| Blank/corrupted PDF | Cache or ad-blocker interference |
| "Service Temporarily Unavailable" | Server maintenance or high traffic |
| OTP not received | Aadhaar-linked mobile issue |

## Fix 1: e-Shram Server Down or Slow

The e-Shram portal experiences heavy traffic, especially around scheme enrollment deadlines.

**What to do:**
1. Check if the site loads at all — visit [eshram.gov.in](https://eshram.gov.in)
2. If the site is slow or shows error pages, **wait 30-60 minutes**
3. Try during **off-peak hours**: before 9 AM or after 9 PM IST
4. Clear your browser cache and cookies before retrying
5. If down for more than 24 hours, call helpline **14434**

## Fix 2: UAN Not Found Error

If the portal says your UAN is not found:

1. **Double-check your UAN** — it's a 12-digit number starting with a state code
2. **Try with Aadhaar number instead** — go to [register.eshram.gov.in](https://register.eshram.gov.in), click "Already Registered", and use Aadhaar + OTP
3. **Registration may be incomplete** — if you never received a UAN SMS, your registration may not have been completed. Re-register
4. **Check SMS history** — search for messages from "ESHRAM" or "NDUW" in your phone

## Fix 3: PDF Not Generating

If you can log in but the PDF download fails:

1. **Use Google Chrome or Firefox** — avoid Samsung Internet or older browsers
2. **Disable ad-blockers and popup blockers** temporarily
3. **Allow popups** from eshram.gov.in (the PDF may open in a new tab)
4. **Check your internet connection** — PDF generation requires a stable connection
5. **Try incognito/private mode** to rule out extension conflicts

### On Mobile:
1. Use Chrome browser (not the in-app browser)
2. Ensure sufficient storage space
3. Check your Downloads folder — the file may have downloaded but the notification was missed

## Fix 4: Browser-Specific Issues

### Chrome
- Go to Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data → Clear cache and cookies for eshram.gov.in
- Disable extensions one by one to find conflicts

### Firefox
- Clear cache: Settings → Privacy → Cookies and Site Data → Clear Data
- Try with Enhanced Tracking Protection turned off for eshram.gov.in

### Mobile Browsers
- Update your browser to the latest version
- If download fails, try "Request Desktop Site" option

## Fix 5: Alternative Download Methods

If the main portal isn't working, try these alternatives:

### Method 1: UMANG App
1. Download **UMANG app** from Google Play Store or Apple App Store
2. Search for "e-Shram" in the app
3. Log in with your Aadhaar-linked mobile number
4. Navigate to your e-Shram card and download

### Method 2: CSC Center
1. Visit your nearest **Common Service Centre (CSC)**
2. Carry your Aadhaar card and mobile phone
3. The CSC operator can download and print your e-Shram card
4. Fee: ₹20-30 (varies by state)

### Method 3: DigiLocker
1. Log in to [digilocker.gov.in](https://www.digilocker.gov.in)
2. Search for "e-Shram" under issued documents
3. If your card is available, download from DigiLocker

## How to Download e-Shram Card (Step-by-Step)

If you haven't tried the standard process yet:

1. Visit [register.eshram.gov.in](https://register.eshram.gov.in)
2. Click **"Already Registered"** on the homepage
3. Enter your **Aadhaar number** or **UAN**
4. Enter the **captcha** code
5. Click **"Send OTP"** — OTP comes to Aadhaar-linked mobile
6. Enter the OTP and click **"Verify"**
7. Your dashboard opens — click **"Download UAN Card"**
8. The PDF downloads to your device

## Important Tips

- **Save your UAN** — note it down separately; you'll need it for scheme benefits
- **Keep a printed copy** — useful for scheme enrollment at government offices
- **Update your mobile number** if you've changed it — see our [e-Shram update guide](/guide/eshram-card-update-mobile-name-fix)
- **Don't share your UAN** with unauthorized agents or websites
- **Check registration status** before trying to download — incomplete registrations won't generate a card
