# Link Aadhaar with Bank Account: Complete Process for All Banks

Linking your [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) with your bank account is mandatory for receiving government subsidies, LPG benefits, scholarships, and other Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) payments. The process is called **Aadhaar seeding** and can be done online, via ATM, or by visiting your bank branch.

## Why Link Aadhaar with Bank Account?

- **Receive DBT payments** — LPG subsidy, PM-KISAN, scholarships, MGNREGA wages, pension
- **Avoid subsidy delays** — Aadhaar-linked accounts get priority for government transfers
- **KYC compliance** — RBI mandates Aadhaar-based e-KYC for bank accounts
- **Financial inclusion** — Required for Jan Dhan Yojana and other government scheme benefits

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar card (original or e-Aadhaar)
- Bank account passbook or account number
- Registered mobile number linked to Aadhaar (for OTP verification)
- Bank debit card (for ATM-based linking)

## Method 1: Link Aadhaar via Net Banking (Online)

This is the fastest method. Steps vary slightly by bank but the general process is:

1. Log in to your bank's net banking portal
2. Go to **"Update Aadhaar"** or **"Aadhaar Seeding"** section (usually under Profile/Settings)
3. Enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number
4. Verify using OTP sent to your Aadhaar-registered mobile number
5. Submit — linking is confirmed within 24-48 hours

## Method 2: Link Aadhaar via Mobile Banking App

1. Open your bank's mobile banking app
2. Navigate to **Service Requests** or **Profile Settings**
3. Select **"Link Aadhaar"** or **"Update Aadhaar"**
4. Enter your Aadhaar number and verify with OTP
5. Confirmation message is sent via SMS

## Method 3: Link Aadhaar via ATM

1. Insert your debit card at your bank's ATM
2. Select **"Services"** or **"Aadhaar Registration"**
3. Enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number
4. Confirm the details
5. Collect the acknowledgment slip

> **Note:** ATM-based linking is available only at select bank ATMs (SBI, Bank of Baroda, PNB).

## Method 4: Link Aadhaar via Branch Visit (Offline)

1. Visit your nearest bank branch with Aadhaar card and passbook
2. Fill the **Aadhaar Seeding Form** (available at the branch)
3. Submit the form with a self-attested copy of Aadhaar
4. Branch staff will update your account within 5-7 working days
5. You will receive SMS confirmation once linking is complete

## Bank-Specific Steps

### SBI (State Bank of India)

- **Online:** Log in to [onlinesbi.sbi](https://onlinesbi.sbi/) → My Accounts & Profile → Update Aadhaar with SBI → Enter Aadhaar → OTP verification
- **YONO App:** Login → Select account → Service Requests → Link Aadhaar
- **ATM:** Insert card → Services → Aadhaar Registration → Enter Aadhaar
- **SMS:** Send `UID <space> Aadhaar Number <space> Account Number` to 567676

### HDFC Bank

- **Online:** Log in to HDFC NetBanking → Update Aadhaar → Enter details → OTP verification
- **Mobile App:** Profile → Link Aadhaar → Enter Aadhaar number → Submit
- **Branch:** Fill Aadhaar update form at any HDFC branch

### ICICI Bank

- **Online:** Log in to ICICI NetBanking → Service Requests → Aadhaar Linking → Enter Aadhaar → OTP
- **iMobile App:** Account Services → Link Aadhaar → Enter details → Verify
- **Branch:** Visit with Aadhaar and account details

## How to Check Aadhaar-Bank Linking Status

1. Visit [resident.uidai.gov.in](https://resident.uidai.gov.in/aadhaar-seeding-status)
2. Enter your Aadhaar number or Virtual ID
3. Enter the security code
4. Click **"Send OTP"** and verify
5. The portal will show whether your Aadhaar is linked to your bank account

Alternatively, check through your bank's net banking portal or call your bank's customer care.

## Important Tips

1. Ensure your **mobile number is registered with Aadhaar** — OTP verification requires it
2. The **name in Aadhaar must match** the name in your bank account. If there is a mismatch, update either document first
3. Link Aadhaar with your **primary account** to receive DBT benefits
4. If you have multiple bank accounts, you can link Aadhaar with all of them, but **DBT will be credited to one account** (the one mapped via NPCI)
5. Keep your [Aadhaar updated](/guide/aadhaar-address-change-online) with correct details before linking

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it mandatory to link Aadhaar with bank account?

Yes, linking Aadhaar with bank account is mandatory for receiving Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) payments from the government. RBI also requires Aadhaar-based KYC for bank accounts under Prevention of Money Laundering rules.

### Can I link Aadhaar with multiple bank accounts?

Yes, you can link your Aadhaar with multiple bank accounts across different banks. However, for receiving DBT benefits, only one account will be mapped through NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India).

### What if my name in Aadhaar and bank account are different?

If there is a name mismatch, the linking may fail. You need to first update your name in either your [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) or your bank account to ensure both match.

### How do I change the bank account linked for DBT?

Visit the new bank branch and request Aadhaar seeding. The latest bank to complete seeding will become the active account for DBT. You can also do this via NPCI mapper through your bank.

### What is Aadhaar seeding?

Aadhaar seeding is the process of linking your 12-digit Aadhaar number with your bank account. Once seeded, the bank maps your account through NPCI's Aadhaar Payment Bridge (APB) system for receiving government payments.

### Can NRIs link Aadhaar with their bank account?

Yes, NRIs who have an Aadhaar card can link it with their NRE/NRO bank accounts in India through net banking or by visiting the branch.

### I linked Aadhaar but not receiving DBT. What should I do?

Ensure your Aadhaar is correctly seeded by checking status on the UIDAI portal. If linked but still not receiving benefits, contact your bank to verify NPCI mapper status. The bank account receiving DBT must be the one mapped in NPCI.
