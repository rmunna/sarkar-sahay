# DigiLocker Document Not Found? How to Fix Missing Documents Issue

Searching for your Aadhaar, PAN, driving licence, or marksheet on DigiLocker but seeing **"Document Not Found"**? This is one of the most common issues on DigiLocker, and it usually has a simple fix. This guide explains why documents go missing and how to find, fetch, and add them.

## Common Error Messages

- "Document not found"
- "No documents available from this issuer"
- "Unable to fetch document"
- "Document not available for your Aadhaar/PAN"
- "Issuer service temporarily unavailable"
- "Record not found with given details"

## Why Documents Are Missing from DigiLocker

### 1. Document Not Fetched from Issuer
DigiLocker doesn't automatically show all your documents. You need to **manually fetch** each document from the respective issuing authority (called "issuers" or "partners").

### 2. Issuing Authority Hasn't Digitized Your Record
Older documents, records from smaller institutions, or departments that haven't fully integrated with DigiLocker won't appear.

### 3. Details Mismatch
If your name, DOB, or ID number in DigiLocker (linked to Aadhaar) doesn't match the issuer's records, the document won't be fetched.

### 4. Issuer Service is Down
Some issuer APIs experience downtime. The document exists but can't be fetched temporarily.

### 5. Wrong Issuer Selected
Many users search the wrong department. For example, searching for driving licence under the wrong state RTO.

## How to Fix: Fetch Documents Step by Step

### Step 1: Login to DigiLocker
1. Go to [digilocker.gov.in](https://www.digilocker.gov.in) or open the **DigiLocker app**
2. Login with your **mobile number** and **Aadhaar OTP** or **PIN**
3. You'll see two sections:
   - **Issued Documents** — documents fetched from government issuers
   - **Uploaded Documents** — documents you've manually uploaded

### Step 2: Fetch Documents from Issuers
1. Click **"Get from DigiLocker Partner"** or **"Search Documents"**
2. Select the **issuer** (document source):

| Document | Issuer to Select |
|----------|-----------------|
| **Aadhaar** | UIDAI |
| **PAN Card** | Income Tax Department |
| **Driving Licence** | Your State Transport Department |
| **Vehicle RC** | Your State Transport Department |
| **Class 10 Marksheet** | CBSE / ICSE / State Board |
| **Class 12 Marksheet** | CBSE / ICSE / State Board |
| **Voter ID** | Election Commission of India |
| **Insurance Policy** | Your insurance company |

3. Enter the required details (document number, DOB, etc.)
4. Click **"Get Document"**
5. If successful, the document appears in your **Issued Documents** section

### Step 3: If Document Still Not Found

#### For Aadhaar Card
- Aadhaar is usually **auto-fetched** when you create a DigiLocker account
- If missing: Go to Issuers → UIDAI → Enter Aadhaar Number → Fetch
- If it fails, your Aadhaar name may not match your DigiLocker profile name

#### For PAN Card
1. Select issuer: **Income Tax Department**
2. Enter your **PAN number** and **date of birth**
3. If "not found": your PAN may not be linked to Aadhaar. Link PAN-Aadhaar first at [incometax.gov.in](https://www.incometax.gov.in)

#### For Driving Licence
1. Select issuer: **Transport Department of [Your State]**
2. Enter your **DL number** and **date of birth**
3. **Important**: Select the correct state — DL from Maharashtra won't appear under Karnataka RTO
4. If your state RTO isn't listed, they haven't integrated with DigiLocker yet
5. **Workaround**: Upload a scanned copy manually

#### For Class 10/12 Marksheet
1. Select issuer: **CBSE** / **Your State Board**
2. Enter your **roll number** and **year of passing**
3. Older records (before 2004 for CBSE) may not be digitized
4. Contact your **school or board office** to request digitization

#### For Vehicle Registration (RC)
1. Select: **Transport Department of [Your State]**
2. Enter **vehicle registration number**
3. Must match the **owner name** linked to your Aadhaar

### Step 4: Upload Documents Manually
If the document can't be fetched from an issuer:

1. Go to **"Uploaded Documents"** section
2. Click **"Upload"**
3. Select document type from dropdown
4. Upload a **clear scan or photo** (PDF, JPEG, PNG)
5. File size limit: **10 MB**
6. The uploaded document is stored securely but marked as "Self-uploaded" (not issued)

**Note:** Self-uploaded documents may not be accepted everywhere. Issuer-fetched documents carry more weight as they're verified.

## Fixing Specific Issues

### "Unable to Fetch Document — Service Unavailable"
- The issuer's server is down — **try after a few hours**
- Check during **off-peak hours** (early morning or late evening)
- Try the **DigiLocker app** instead of website, or vice versa

### "Record Not Found with Given Details"
- **Double-check** the document number you entered
- Ensure **date of birth** matches what's on the document
- Try with **name variations** if the form allows
- Your record may simply not be in the issuer's digital database

### "Document Fetched But Shows Wrong Details"
- The issuer's records have errors — contact the **issuing authority** to correct
- For Aadhaar: update details at [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in)
- For PAN: correct at [onlineservices.nsdl.com](https://www.onlineservices.nsdl.com/paam/endUserRegisterContact.html)

### Documents Disappearing from DigiLocker
- Documents don't disappear — you may be logged into a **different DigiLocker account**
- Ensure you're using the **same mobile number** as your original registration
- If you have multiple Aadhaar-linked numbers, check which one is linked to DigiLocker

## List of Major DigiLocker Partner Issuers

## Tips for Best DigiLocker Experience

1. **Fetch documents proactively** — don't wait until you need them urgently
2. **Download PDFs** of important documents for offline access
3. Keep your **Aadhaar details updated** — DigiLocker uses Aadhaar for verification
4. **Use the mobile app** for quick access on the go
5. Set up **PIN/biometric lock** on the app for security
6. **Upload scans** of documents that can't be fetched as backup

## Summary

DigiLocker "Document Not Found" usually means you haven't fetched the document from the issuer yet. Go to the issuer section, select the correct department, and enter your document details to fetch it. If the issuer hasn't digitized your record or isn't a DigiLocker partner, upload a scanned copy manually. For fetch failures, try during off-peak hours or use the mobile app. Always download PDFs of fetched documents for offline backup.
