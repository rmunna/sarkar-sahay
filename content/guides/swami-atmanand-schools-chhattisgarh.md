# Swami Atmanand Schools Chhattisgarh – Admission, Eligibility and Details

Swami Atmanand Government Excellent English and Hindi Medium Schools are a flagship education initiative of the Chhattisgarh government. These schools provide free, high-quality education with modern facilities to students from all backgrounds. The scheme has transformed government schooling in the state by offering English and Hindi medium instruction with well-equipped classrooms, computer labs, libraries, and trained teachers.

## What are Swami Atmanand Schools?

Launched by the Government of Chhattisgarh, Swami Atmanand Schools aim to provide quality education equivalent to private schools — completely free of cost. The initiative converts existing government schools into centres of excellence with upgraded infrastructure and teaching standards.

**Key features:**
- **Free education** from Class 1 to Class 12
- **English and Hindi medium** options available
- **CBSE and SCERT curriculum** (varies by school)
- **Modern infrastructure** — smart classrooms, science labs, computer labs, libraries
- **Qualified teachers** with regular training
- **No tuition or admission fees** — completely government-funded
- **Spread across all districts** of Chhattisgarh

As of 2024, over 700 schools across Chhattisgarh have been upgraded under this initiative, covering both urban and rural areas.

---

## Who is Eligible for Admission?

- **Residency:** The student (or parent/guardian) should be a resident of Chhattisgarh
- **Age:** As per class-wise age criteria set by the state education department
- **Classes available:** Class 1 to Class 12 (availability depends on the school)
- **No income restriction:** Students from all economic backgrounds can apply
- **Transfer students:** Students from private or other government schools can also seek admission based on seat availability

### Priority in Admission

When applications exceed available seats, a **lottery system (computerised draw)** is used to select students fairly. Priority may be given to:
- Students from the same block or district
- Students from economically weaker families
- Students already studying in government schools

## Documents Required for Admission

- **Birth certificate** of the student
- **[Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)** of the student (if available)
- **Aadhaar card of parent/guardian**
- **Transfer certificate (TC)** from previous school (for Class 2 and above)
- **Report card / marksheet** of previous class
- **Caste certificate** (if applicable, for SC/ST/OBC)
- **Domicile / residence certificate** of Chhattisgarh
- **Passport-size photographs** (2-3 copies)
- **Income certificate** (if applicable)
- **[Ration card](/guide/ration-card-apply-online)** (optional, for address proof)

## How to Apply for Admission – Step-by-Step

### Online Registration (via cgschool.in)

1. Visit the official portal at [cgschool.in](https://cgschool.in/)
2. Navigate to **Swami Atmanand School Admission** section
3. Click on **"Student Registration"** or **"New Admission"**
4. Fill in the student's details — name, date of birth, class, parent information
5. Select the **preferred school(s)** from the list (you may choose up to 3 schools)
6. Upload required documents (photograph, birth certificate, etc.)
7. Review all information and **submit the application**
8. Note down the **application number** and take a printout of the confirmation

### After Submission

1. Applications are verified by the school authorities
2. If seats are limited, a **computerised lottery** is conducted
3. The **selection list** is published on the school notice board and on cgschool.in
4. Selected students must complete **document verification** at the school within the given deadline
5. Admission is confirmed after verification — no fees are charged

### Offline Application

Some schools may also accept applications in person. Visit the nearest Swami Atmanand School with the required documents during the admission window.

## Admission Timeline

- **Registration window:** Typically opens in **March–April** each year
- **Lottery / selection list:** Published within 2–3 weeks after the registration deadline
- **Document verification:** 1 week after selection list
- **Classes begin:** As per the state academic calendar (usually June/July)

> **Tip:** Check [cgschool.in](https://cgschool.in/) regularly from February onwards for the latest admission notification.

## Important Tips

1. **Apply early** — Don't wait for the last date. Registration portals may face heavy traffic near deadlines
2. **Choose multiple schools** — Select 2-3 preferred schools to improve your chances
3. **Keep documents ready** — Have all documents scanned and ready before starting the online form
4. **Check lottery results** — Results are published on cgschool.in. Check regularly after the registration deadline
5. **No donation required** — Admission is completely free and merit/lottery-based. Do not pay anyone for a seat

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Are Swami Atmanand Schools completely free?

Yes, there are no admission, tuition, or registration fees. In most schools, textbooks and uniforms are also provided free of charge.

### What curriculum do these schools follow?

Most Swami Atmanand Schools follow the SCERT Chhattisgarh curriculum. Some schools also follow CBSE curriculum. Check with the specific school for details.

### Can students from private schools take admission?

Yes, students from private or other schools can apply for admission in Swami Atmanand Schools based on seat availability. A transfer certificate from the previous school is required.

### How are students selected if there are more applicants than seats?

A computerised lottery system is used for fair selection when applications exceed available seats.

### Is there a reservation policy for SC/ST/OBC students?

The state government's reservation policy applies. Seats are reserved for SC, ST, and OBC categories as per Chhattisgarh government norms.

### Can I apply for admission in the middle of the academic year?

Generally, admissions are conducted at the start of the academic year. Mid-session admission depends on seat availability and school-level approval.

### How many Swami Atmanand Schools are there in Chhattisgarh?

As of 2024, over 700 schools have been established or upgraded under this initiative across all districts of Chhattisgarh.
