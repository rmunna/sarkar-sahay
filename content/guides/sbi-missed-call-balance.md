# SBI Missed Call Balance Check: Number & Process

You don't need internet or a smartphone to check your SBI account balance. Just give a missed call from your registered mobile number and get an instant SMS with your balance. Here's everything you need to know.

## SBI Missed Call Numbers

| Service | Number | How It Works |
|---|---|---|
| **Balance Enquiry** | **09223766666** | Give a missed call → receive SMS with account balance |
| **Mini Statement** | **09223866666** | Give a missed call → receive SMS with last 5 transactions |

> Both services are **completely free** — no charges from SBI. Standard call/SMS rates from your telecom operator may apply (though the call auto-disconnects, so no airtime is used).

## How to Check SBI Balance via Missed Call

1. **Ensure your mobile number is registered** with your SBI account
2. Open your phone dialer
3. Dial **09223766666** from your registered mobile number
4. The call will **automatically disconnect** after 1-2 rings
5. Within a few seconds, you'll receive an **SMS** from SBI with:
   - Your account number (last 4 digits)
   - Available balance
   - Date and time of the balance

**Example SMS:**
> *Your a/c XXXX1234 has a Bal of Rs.25,430.50 as on 06-MAR-2026*

## How to Get Mini Statement via Missed Call

1. Dial **09223866666** from your registered mobile number
2. The call auto-disconnects
3. You receive an SMS with the **last 5 transactions** — showing date, amount, and type (credit/debit)

## How to Register Your Mobile Number

Your mobile number must be registered with SBI to use this service. If it's not registered:

### Method 1: At SBI Branch
1. Visit your nearest SBI branch
2. Fill a **mobile number registration/change form**
3. Submit with your **Aadhaar or ID proof**
4. Mobile number is updated within **1-2 working days**

### Method 2: Via SBI ATM
1. Insert your **SBI debit card** at any SBI ATM
2. Enter your **PIN**
3. Select **"Registration"** → **"Mobile Number Registration"**
4. Enter your **new mobile number**
5. Confirm — registration is usually instant or within 24 hours

### Method 3: Via Net Banking
1. Log in to [SBI Online Banking](https://www.onlinesbi.sbi/)
2. Go to **"My Profile"** → **"Contact Details"**
3. Update your mobile number
4. Verify with OTP

## Important Tips

1. **Only your registered mobile number works** — calls from other numbers will not get a response
2. **Works with any phone** — no smartphone or internet needed, even a basic feature phone works
3. **If you have multiple accounts**, the SMS shows the balance of your primary account linked to that mobile number
4. **Service works 24/7** — check balance anytime, including holidays
5. **If you don't receive SMS**, check if your mobile number is correctly registered with SBI

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Is the missed call balance service really free?
Yes. SBI does not charge for this service. The call auto-disconnects so no airtime is used.

### Q2: Can I check balance for multiple SBI accounts?
The missed call service returns the balance of the **primary account** linked to your registered mobile. For other accounts, use net banking or YONO.

### Q3: Why am I not getting SMS after missed call?
Your mobile number may not be registered with SBI, or it may be linked to a different account. Visit your branch or use ATM to register/update your mobile number.

### Q4: Does this work for current accounts and NRI accounts?
Yes, the missed call service works for savings, current, and NRI accounts as long as the mobile number is registered.

### Q5: Can I use this service from outside India?
It may work if you're roaming with your Indian SIM. However, international calling/roaming charges may apply from your telecom operator.

### Q6: What is the SMS method to check balance?
Send **"BAL"** to **09223766666** from your registered mobile number. You'll receive your balance via SMS.

### Q7: Is there a limit on how many times I can check balance?
There is no official limit. However, excessive use may be flagged. For regular monitoring, 2-3 checks per day is perfectly fine.
