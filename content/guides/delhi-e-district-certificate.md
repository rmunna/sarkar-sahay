## What is Delhi eDistrict?

Delhi eDistrict is the official online portal of the Revenue Department, Government of NCT of Delhi, for applying and obtaining various certificates and services. Through **edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in**, residents of Delhi can apply for income certificates, caste certificates, domicile certificates, SC/ST certificates, and many other documents without visiting government offices.

### Certificates Available on Delhi eDistrict

- **Income Certificate** — Proof of annual family income
- **Caste Certificate** — OBC/SC/ST caste verification
- **Domicile Certificate** — Proof of residence in Delhi
- **SC/ST Certificate** — Scheduled Caste / Scheduled Tribe certificate
- **Surviving Member Certificate**
- **Solvency Certificate**
- **Lal Dora Certificate**
- **Marriage Registration**

## Who is Eligible?

### Income Certificate
- Any resident of Delhi
- Based on family income from all sources

### Caste Certificate (OBC)
- Residents of Delhi belonging to OBC categories listed in the Central/State OBC list

### Domicile Certificate
- Persons residing in Delhi for at least **3 years continuously**

### SC/ST Certificate
- Residents belonging to Scheduled Caste or Scheduled Tribe communities as per the Constitution

## Documents Required

### For Income Certificate
- **Aadhaar Card** — Mandatory identity proof
- **Voter ID / Ration Card** — Address proof in Delhi
- **Self-declaration of income** — On stamp paper or plain paper
- **Salary slip / ITR** — If employed or filing tax returns
- **Employer certificate** — If employed in private sector
- **Passport-size photograph**

### For Caste Certificate (OBC/SC/ST)
- **Aadhaar Card**
- **Address proof** — Voter ID, ration card, or utility bill
- **Father's/family member's caste certificate** — If available
- **School certificate** — Showing caste (TC/LC)
- **Affidavit** — Self-declaration of caste on stamp paper
- **Passport-size photograph**

### For Domicile Certificate
- **Aadhaar Card**
- **Address proof for 3+ years** — Voter ID, ration card, electricity bill, rent agreement
- **School/college certificate** — Showing Delhi address (if applicable)
- **Employer certificate** — If employed in Delhi
- **Passport-size photograph**

## How to Apply Online — Step-by-Step

### Step 1: Register on Delhi eDistrict Portal

1. Visit **[edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in](https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in)**
2. Click **"New User"** or **"Register"**
3. Enter your **Aadhaar number** and verify via OTP
4. Create your **username and password**
5. Complete the registration

### Step 2: Login and Select Certificate

1. Login with your credentials
2. Go to **"Apply for Services"** or **"Certificate Services"**
3. Select the certificate you want (Income / Caste / Domicile / SC-ST)
4. Click **"Apply"**

### Step 3: Fill the Application Form

1. Enter your **personal details** — Name, father's name, address, date of birth
2. Enter **certificate-specific details**:
   - For Income: Annual income amount and source
   - For Caste: Caste name and sub-caste
   - For Domicile: Duration of stay in Delhi
3. Upload the **required documents** (scanned copies in PDF/JPG)
4. Upload a **passport-size photograph**

### Step 4: Pay the Fee

1. Review your application
2. Pay the fee online via **net banking, debit card, or UPI**
3. Download the **payment receipt**

### Step 5: Track and Download

1. After submission, note your **Application ID**
2. Go to **"Track Application"** to check status
3. The application goes for **field verification** by the concerned SDM/Tehsildar
4. Once approved, download the certificate from the portal

### Offline Method: Apply via CSC or SDM Office

1. Visit the nearest **Common Service Centre (CSC)** or **SDM office**
2. Carry original documents and photocopies
3. Fill the application form (available at the counter)
4. Pay the fee and collect the acknowledgment receipt
5. Certificate will be ready after verification

> Fees may vary. Check the latest fee on the official portal before applying.

## Processing Time

| Certificate | Timeline |
|---|---|
| Income Certificate | 7–21 working days |
| Caste Certificate (OBC) | 15–30 working days |
| SC/ST Certificate | 15–30 working days |
| Domicile Certificate | 7–21 working days |

> Processing times depend on field verification. Follow up with the SDM office if there's a delay beyond the expected timeline.

## Important Tips

1. **Keep documents ready before starting** — All documents must be scanned in clear quality (PDF or JPG, under 1 MB each).
2. **Use correct income figures** — Mismatch between declared income and documents can cause rejection. Be accurate and consistent.
3. **Aadhaar must be linked to Delhi address** — If your Aadhaar shows a different state address, update it first. See our [Aadhaar address change guide](/guide/aadhaar-address-change-online).
4. **Track your application regularly** — Login and check status. If field verification is pending, ensure someone is available at the address for the verifier's visit.
5. **CSC is an alternative** — If you face issues with online application, visit any Common Service Centre for assisted filing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Can I apply for Delhi eDistrict certificates from outside Delhi?
You can submit the application online from anywhere, but field verification will be done at your Delhi address. You must have a valid Delhi address to receive the certificate.

### Q2. How long is the income certificate valid?
The income certificate issued through Delhi eDistrict is typically valid for **1 year** from the date of issue. You need to apply afresh each year.

### Q3. Can I apply for a caste certificate if my father's certificate is from another state?
You may face challenges. Delhi eDistrict typically requires proof of caste from the Delhi records or a caste certificate of a family member already issued in Delhi. Consult the SDM office for specific guidance.

### Q4. Is domicile certificate the same as residence certificate?
They are similar but not identical. A domicile certificate proves that Delhi is your permanent home (3+ years of continuous stay). A residence certificate may simply confirm current address. For most government purposes, a domicile certificate is required.

### Q5. What if my Delhi eDistrict application is rejected?
You will receive a reason for rejection on the portal. Fix the issue (e.g., missing document, incorrect information) and re-apply. You can also visit the SDM office for clarification.

### Q6. Can I get an urgent or tatkal certificate?
Delhi eDistrict does not have a separate tatkal category for most certificates. Processing depends on verification timelines. Visit the SDM office directly for urgent requirements.

### Q7. Is the eDistrict certificate digitally signed and valid?
Yes, certificates issued through Delhi eDistrict are digitally signed and legally valid. They can be verified online using the certificate number on the portal.
