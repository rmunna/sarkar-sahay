# Assam Youth Employment Scheme 2026 — Complete Guide

The new Assam government (2026) has launched an expanded youth welfare agenda — combining self-employment grants, skill training with stipend, apprenticeship support, and entrepreneurship development for youth aged 18–35. This guide covers all major Assam youth schemes for 2026.

---

## CM Youth Self-Reliance Scheme 2026 (New)

The new Assam government's flagship youth initiative — providing self-employment grants and skill training support to unemployed youth from Assam.

**Expected Key Features:**
- One-time self-employment grant of ₹50,000–₹2,00,000 for approved ventures
- Skill training stipend of ₹3,000–₹5,000/month during training
- Priority sectors: fishery, dairy, piggery, poultry, handloom, small manufacturing, IT
- Priority to youth from rural areas and tribal communities
- Application through [sewasetu.assam.gov.in](https://sewasetu.assam.gov.in/)

> Official notification at [assam.gov.in](https://assam.gov.in/) — check for updated amounts and eligibility.

---

## SVAYEM — Swami Vivekananda Assam Youth Empowerment Mission (Continuing)

SVAYEM is one of Assam's most successful youth welfare programmes — providing non-refundable grants to unemployed youth for self-employment ventures. The new government is expected to continue and expand this scheme.

**Key Details (as last notified):**
- **Grant:** ₹50,000 one-time, non-refundable
- **Eligibility:** Unemployed youth aged 18–40, Assam resident, family income below ₹2 lakh/year
- **Eligible ventures:** Piggery, fishery, dairy, poultry, handloom, small business, services
- **Application:** Through District Commissioner's office or Common Service Centre

### How to Apply for SVAYEM:
1. Visit your District Commissioner's office with Aadhaar, bank passbook, income certificate, and a project proposal
2. Submit application in the prescribed SVAYEM form
3. Application reviewed by a district committee
4. Grant disbursed to your Aadhaar-linked bank account upon approval

## Skill Assam — Skill Development with Stipend

The **Skill Assam** initiative (under Skill Development Department, Assam) provides short-term vocational training with a monthly stipend to youth registered with the employment exchange.

**Key Details:**
- Free training in trades: construction, healthcare, IT, hospitality, retail, beauty
- Training duration: 2–6 months at registered training centres
- Monthly stipend: ₹1,500–₹3,500 during training
- Placement support after successful course completion
- Apply at [skill.assam.gov.in](https://skill.assam.gov.in/) or nearest Industrial Training Institute (ITI)

---

## Assam Skill Development Mission (ASDM)

ASDM is the nodal agency for skill training in Assam — coordinating all central and state skill programmes including Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVYY) implementation.

**Courses Available:**
- IT-ITeS (basic computer, coding, data entry)
- Healthcare (nursing assistant, phlebotomy, medical transcription)
- Agri and allied (organic farming, floriculture, pisciculture)
- Logistics and supply chain
- Construction (masonry, plumbing, electrical)
- Textile and handloom (weaving, embroidery)

**Apply:** At any empanelled ASDM training centre or at [asdm.assam.gov.in](https://asdm.assam.gov.in/)

## National Apprenticeship Training Scheme (NATS) in Assam

The central apprenticeship scheme operates actively in Assam's industrial belt — Guwahati, Dibrugarh, Jorhat, Tezpur, and Silchar.

**Key Details:**
- Monthly stipend: ₹5,000–₹9,000 during apprenticeship (1 year typically)
- Certificate from Board of Apprenticeship Training (BOAT)
- Major employers: Oil India Limited, ONGC (Assam fields), IOCL Guwahati Refinery, Assam Petro-Chemicals, NRL (Numaligarh Refinery)
- Register at [mhrdnats.gov.in](https://mhrdnats.gov.in/)

---

## Chief Minister's Special Scholarship (Continuing)

For meritorious Assam students admitted to top national institutions — IITs, IIMs, AIIMS, NLUs, NIT, and other centrally ranked institutions.

**Key Details:**
- Full tuition fee reimbursement
- Monthly maintenance allowance ₹5,000–₹10,000 during course
- Family income below ₹6 lakh/year
- **Apply:** Through Directorate of Scholarships, Assam at [directorateofscholarships.assam.gov.in](https://directorateofscholarships.assam.gov.in/)

## How to Apply — General Steps

### For SVAYEM and CM Youth Scheme:
1. Visit District Commissioner's office or Common Service Centre in your district
2. Collect SVAYEM / CM Youth Scheme application form
3. Fill form with business/project proposal details
4. Attach: Aadhaar, bank passbook, income certificate, domicile certificate, and project details
5. Submit and collect acknowledgement
6. Track at [rtps.assam.gov.in](https://rtps.assam.gov.in/)

### For Skill Training (Skill Assam / ASDM):
1. Register at nearest Employment Exchange
2. Visit [skill.assam.gov.in](https://skill.assam.gov.in/) or nearest ITI to find available courses
3. Apply for the course of your choice
4. Begin training and receive monthly stipend in your bank account

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the maximum grant under the Assam CM Youth Self-Reliance Scheme 2026?
As per the new government's announced programme, grants range from ₹50,000 to ₹2,00,000 depending on the sector and project size. Exact amounts are specified in the official notification at [assam.gov.in](https://assam.gov.in/).

### Is SVAYEM still available in 2026 under the new government?
Yes. SVAYEM has strong implementation history and the new government is expected to continue it. Official confirmation and any changes will be notified at [assam.gov.in](https://assam.gov.in/).

### Do I need to repay the SVAYEM grant?
No. SVAYEM is a non-refundable grant — it does not need to be repaid as long as you use it for the approved self-employment purpose.

### What kind of business can I start with SVAYEM?
Approved ventures include piggery, fishery (pond fish culture), poultry, dairy farming, handloom weaving, small retail, food processing, beauty salon, and other service-sector micro-businesses. Submit a brief project proposal with your application.

### Can a woman apply for the SVAYEM / CM Youth Self-Employment scheme?
Yes. Women entrepreneurs are specifically encouraged and may receive priority consideration, particularly in schemes targeting rural micro-enterprise development.

### What is the monthly stipend for Skill Assam training?
Monthly training stipend ranges from ₹1,500 to ₹3,500 depending on the trade and training centre. Check the specific course listing at [skill.assam.gov.in](https://skill.assam.gov.in/).

### Where can I get more information and apply?
- Visit [sewasetu.assam.gov.in](https://sewasetu.assam.gov.in/) for scheme application
- Call helpline **181** for guidance
- Visit your **District Employment Exchange** for employment-linked scheme registration
- Visit nearest **CSC / Common Service Centre** for assisted application
