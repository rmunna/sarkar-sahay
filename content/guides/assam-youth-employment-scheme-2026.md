# Assam Youth Scheme 2026 — Jibon Prerana ₹2,500/Month & 2 Lakh Jobs

Under **CM Himanta Biswa Sarma's second term**, Assam has a multi-layered youth package — from a live monthly stipend for fresh graduates to a ₹5 lakh startup fund, a ₹50,000 self-employment grant, and a commitment to 2 lakh government jobs over 5 years.

**The standout scheme is CM Jibon Prerana — already live and paying ₹2,500/month to unemployed fresh graduates since February 2026.**

---

## 1. CM Jibon Prerana Scheme — ₹2,500/Month (Paying; Applications Closed)

Launched 3 November 2025 by the Skill, Employment & Entrepreneurship Department. DBT payments running since February 2026:

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| **Monthly Amount** | **₹2,500/month** |
| **Duration** | 12 months |
| **Launched** | 3 November 2025 |
| **DBT Started** | February 2026 |
| **Application status** | **CLOSED — last date was 30 November 2025** |
| **Payment status** | Live — DBT flowing for approved applicants |
| **Eligible batch** | 2025 pass-outs from Assam government/public institutions only |

**Who was eligible?**
- 2025 batch fresh graduates (Class 12 or degree) from **government or public institutions in Assam**
- **Private college and open university graduates were NOT eligible**
- Currently unemployed (no regular salaried job)
- Assam permanent resident

**Who was excluded:**
- Children of government employees (except Class IV)
- Children of MLAs, ministers, or government contractors
- Graduates from private institutions or open universities
- Applicants whose parents earn over ₹10 lakh/year

> **Application window closed 30 November 2025.** If you applied on time and were approved, your ₹2,500/month DBT continues for the 12-month period. A new round for 2026 batch graduates has not yet been announced under the second term.

## 2. CM Atmanirbhar Asom Abhijan 2.0 — Up to ₹5 Lakh

The second-term expansion of Assam's flagship youth entrepreneurship scheme:

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| **Grant Amount** | **Up to ₹5 lakh** per startup |
| **Target** | 10 lakh youth beneficiaries over 5 years |
| **Priority Sectors** | Agriculture, fishery, dairy, handloom, food processing, IT, services |
| **Priority Groups** | Rural youth, tribal communities, women entrepreneurs |
| **Application** | sewasetu.assam.gov.in / District Commissioner's office |

**How it works:**
- Submit a business project proposal
- District-level committee reviews and approves
- Grant disbursed in tranches to Aadhaar-linked bank account
- Mentorship support for 12 months post-grant

## 3. SVAYEM — ₹50,000 Self-Employment Grant (Continuing)

Swami Vivekananda Assam Youth Empowerment Mission — one of Assam's most successful youth schemes:

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| **Grant** | **₹50,000 one-time, non-refundable** |
| **Age** | 18 to 40 years |
| **Income** | Family income below ₹2 lakh/year |
| **Eligible ventures** | Piggery, fishery (pond aquaculture), dairy, poultry, handloom weaving, small retail, food processing, beauty/barber, service businesses |
| **Application** | District Commissioner's office |

**How to apply for SVAYEM:**
1. Visit your District Commissioner's office with Aadhaar, bank passbook, income certificate, and a brief project proposal
2. Fill SVAYEM application form (available at DC office)
3. Application reviewed by a district committee within 30 days
4. Grant disbursed to Aadhaar-linked bank account on approval
5. Track at [rtps.assam.gov.in](https://rtps.assam.gov.in/)

## How to Register at Assam Employment Exchange

Registration at the District Employment Exchange is required for Jibon Prerana and many other employment-linked schemes:

1. Visit your nearest **District Employment Exchange** in your district headquarters
2. Carry: Aadhaar card, all mark sheets (Class 10 onwards), passport photo, and domicile/residence proof
3. Register and receive Employment Exchange Registration Number
4. Renew registration annually — can be done online at [employment.assam.gov.in](https://employment.assam.gov.in/)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is CM Jibon Prerana open to apply right now?
**No** — the application window closed on **30 November 2025**. The scheme was open only for 2025 batch pass-outs from government/public institutions in Assam. If you applied on time, payments continue. A new round for 2026 batch graduates has not been announced yet. Watch [assam.gov.in](https://assam.gov.in/) for future notifications.

### What qualifications did CM Jibon Prerana require?
It targeted **2025 batch fresh graduates** from Assam **government or public institutions only**. Private college and open university graduates were excluded. Applicants had to be unemployed Assam residents. Children of government employees (except Class IV), MLAs, ministers, or parents earning >₹10 lakh/year were not eligible.

### How is CM Atmanirbhar 2.0 different from SVAYEM?
SVAYEM gives a fixed ₹50,000 non-refundable grant for any micro-enterprise. CM Atmanirbhar 2.0 goes up to **₹5 lakh** for larger startups, targeting more significant business proposals. Both require a project proposal and district-level committee approval.

### When will the 2 lakh government jobs be filled?
The BJP government's target is **5 years** (2026–2031), with the first recruitment examination calendar due from the Chief Secretary's task force by **August 2026** (3 months from Day 1 on 13 May 2026).

### Do I need an Employment Exchange card for Jibon Prerana?
Yes — Employment Exchange registration is required as part of verifying your unemployed status. Register at your nearest District Employment Exchange before applying.

### Can a girl student apply for Jibon Prerana?
Yes — CM Jibon Prerana is open to all eligible fresh graduates regardless of gender. Women applicants are specifically encouraged. Separately, the Pragyan Bharati Scooty Scheme is exclusively for girl students who scored 60%+ in Class 12.

### Where do I apply for SVAYEM?
Visit your **District Commissioner's office** with your project proposal, Aadhaar, income certificate, bank passbook, and domicile certificate. The SVAYEM form is available at DC offices and selected CSC centres.
