# PMAY-G Payment Not Received 2026? Fix Installment Delays

PM Awas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G) releases house construction money in **3 installments**. Payments are frequently delayed or blocked due to Aadhaar-bank linking issues, missing geo-tagged photos, or FTO rejections. This guide covers every fix.

## PMAY-G Installment Schedule

| Installment | Amount | Condition |
|------------|--------|-----------|
| **1st Installment** | ₹40,000 (plain) / ₹60,000 (hilly/NE) | After approval |
| **2nd Installment** | ₹80,000 / ₹1,20,000 | After lintel level construction + geo-tagged photo uploaded |
| **3rd Installment** | ₹40,000 / ₹60,000 | After roof completion + final photo uploaded |

Total: ₹1.2 lakh in plains, ₹1.8 lakh in hilly/NE areas. Additional ₹12,000 for toilet from SBM-G.

## Fix 1: Check Your Payment Status at pmayg.nic.in

Before troubleshooting, check what the system shows:

1. Visit **[pmayg.nic.in](https://pmayg.nic.in/)** → **"Stakeholders"** → **"IAY/PMAYG Beneficiary"**
2. Enter your **Registration Number** (from your sanction letter)
3. View: sanction status, payment dates, installment amounts and FTO status

Alternatively at [awaassoft.nic.in](https://awaassoft.nic.in/):
1. Go to **"Report"** → **"Physical and Financial Progress"**
2. Select State → District → Block → Panchayat to see beneficiary data

## Fix 2: First Installment Not Released

If your application is approved but 1st installment hasn't come:

1. **FTO not generated**: the BDO/Block hasn't raised the Fund Transfer Order. Visit BDO office and follow up
2. **Aadhaar not linked to bank**: your bank account must be Aadhaar-seeded. Link at your bank branch
3. **Wrong account number on record**: visit BDO with bank passbook to update
4. **Bank account inactive**: ensure the account has had at least one transaction in the last 6 months

## Fix 3: Second Installment Not Released

2nd installment is held until construction reaches lintel level AND geo-tagged photos are uploaded:

1. **Check if photos are uploaded**: visit your Gram Panchayat or BDO — they must upload construction photos via the AwaasSoft app with geo-tagging
2. **Construction not verified**: a field team must inspect and certify construction at lintel level
3. **If photos are uploaded but payment is still pending**: check FTO status at pmayg.nic.in; the FTO may be rejected (see Fix 5)

## Fix 4: Third Installment Not Released

3rd and final installment requires:
- ✅ Roof completion verified and photographed
- ✅ Toilet constructed (SBM-G sanction)
- ✅ Final inspection by BDO/block team
- ✅ SECC data confirmation of occupancy

If all done and payment is still pending, visit BDO with construction completion certificate and photos.

## Fix 5: FTO Rejected — Most Common Cause of Delay

If your PMAY-G FTO (Fund Transfer Order) is rejected by the bank:

| Rejection Reason | Fix |
|-----------------|-----|
| Account closed/merged | Open new account; update at BDO |
| Aadhaar not seeded in bank | Link Aadhaar at bank branch |
| Name mismatch (Aadhaar vs bank) | Get name corrected at bank or update Aadhaar |
| Wrong IFSC code | Give updated IFSC to BDO (bank mergers change IFSC) |
| Account not operative | Make one deposit/withdrawal to activate |

After correction at BDO, FTO regeneration takes 7–15 working days.

## Fix 6: Check Payment via PFMS

Your payment may have been sent but credited to a different Aadhaar-linked account:

1. Visit **[pfms.nic.in](https://pfms.nic.in/)** → **"Know Your Payment"**
2. Enter your **Aadhaar number** or **bank account + IFSC**
3. Check if any PMAYG payment has been sent and to which account
4. If sent to wrong account, contact BDO immediately with PFMS report

## Fix 7: Raise a Grievance

If payment is stuck despite everything being correct:

1. **Online grievance**: [grievance.pmay.gov.in](https://grievance.pmay.gov.in/) → Submit grievance with Registration Number
2. **PMAY-G Helpline**: **1800-11-6446** (toll-free)
3. **District Rural Development Agency (DRDA)**: visit your district DRDA office with Aadhaar + sanction letter
4. **CPGRAMS**: [pgportal.gov.in](https://pgportal.gov.in/) → Ministry of Rural Development → PMAY-G

## Important Tips

1. **Never pay anyone** to release your PMAY-G installment — this is a scam. BDO and Gram Panchayat cannot charge you
2. **Keep all construction photos** yourself with date stamps — useful for disputes
3. **Check your account regularly**: payments come as "PMAYG" or "AWAASGO" credit
4. **Do not change bank accounts** mid-construction without updating BDO immediately

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My first installment came but 2nd hasn't in 8 months. What's wrong?

Most likely: construction photos (geo-tagged) haven't been uploaded by your Gram Panchayat. Visit the GP and ask the Ward Member or Secretary to upload the lintel-level photos via the AwaasSoft mobile app. Once uploaded, the 2nd installment typically releases within 30–45 days.

### PMAY-G payment went to wrong account. How to recover?

File a complaint at BDO immediately. If the money went to an inactive or unknown account via Aadhaar Payment Bridge, the bank will reverse it (usually within 30–60 days if reported quickly). File a grievance at grievance.pmay.gov.in with your PFMS transaction details.

### My name is not in the PMAY-G list even though I was selected. Why?

PMAY-G beneficiaries are selected from the Awaiting List based on SECC 2011 data. If you were verbally told you'd get it but your name isn't in the system at pmayg.nic.in, you were not formally sanctioned. Visit BDO and check the priority list.

### Can I surrender my PMAY-G allocation and let someone else take it?

No individual transfer is possible. If you don't want to proceed, the allocation is cancelled and given to the next person on the waitlist — it cannot be transferred to a specific person.
