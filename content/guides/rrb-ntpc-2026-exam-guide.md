## Exam Pattern

### 1st Stage CBT (Computer Based Test)

| Section | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---------|-----------|-------|---------|
| General Awareness | 40 | 40 | |
| Mathematics | 30 | 30 | 90 min |
| General Intelligence & Reasoning | 30 | 30 | |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** | **90 min** |

- **Negative Marking:** 1/3 mark deducted per wrong answer
- Qualifying nature — shortlists for 2nd stage CBT

### 2nd Stage CBT — For Graduate Level Posts

| Section | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---------|-----------|-------|---------|
| General Awareness | 50 | 50 | |
| Mathematics | 35 | 35 | 90 min |
| General Intelligence & Reasoning | 35 | 35 | |
| **Total** | **120** | **120** | **90 min** |

### 2nd Stage CBT — For 12th Level Posts

Same as above (100 questions, 90 minutes)

### Computer-Based Aptitude Test (CBAT) — For Station Master and Traffic Assistant

- Spatial reasoning, memory, attention, perception tests
- Qualifying — no marks added to merit

### Typing Skill Test — For Clerk Posts

- English: 30 wpm OR Hindi: 25 wpm
- No marks — only qualifying

## Detailed Syllabus

### General Awareness
Current Events (National + International), Indian History (ancient, medieval, modern), Indian Geography, Indian Polity & Constitution, Indian Economy, Science & Technology, Sports, Culture, Railway-specific GK.

### Mathematics
Number systems, BODMAS, decimals, fractions, LCM/HCF, percentage, ratio, SI/CI, profit & loss, time & work, time-distance-speed, mensuration, algebra basics, geometry, data interpretation.

### General Intelligence & Reasoning
Analogies, completion of number/alphabetical series, coding/decoding, similarities & differences, venn diagrams, syllogism, statement conclusions, directions, blood relations, calendar, clock, mathematical operations.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How many RRB NTPC vacancies are expected in 2026?**
Based on pattern: 35,000–50,000 vacancies across all RRBs. The 2024 cycle had 11,558 vacancies; demand has been rising. 2026 notification will have exact figures.

**Can I apply for multiple RRBs?**
No — you can apply to only ONE RRB (Railway Recruitment Board) per cycle. Choose based on your examination city preference.

**Is Station Master the best post in NTPC?**
Station Master (Level 6, ₹35,400 basic) has the highest salary in NTPC. However, it requires passing the Computer-Based Aptitude Test (CBAT) and involves shift duties and operational responsibilities.

**Which is better — RRB NTPC or Railway Group D?**
NTPC has higher salary (Graduate Level), better posting, and career growth. Group D requires physical labour and is at Level 1 pay. If eligible, always aim for NTPC.

**Does RRB NTPC have typing test for all posts?**
No — typing test only for Clerk/Typist category posts (Junior Clerk cum Typist, Senior Clerk cum Typist, etc.). Station Master, Goods Guard, and other non-clerical posts don't require typing test.
