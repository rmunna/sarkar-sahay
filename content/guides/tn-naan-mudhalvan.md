# Naan Mudhalvan Scheme Tamil Nadu – Free Skill Training for Youth

## What is Naan Mudhalvan?

Naan Mudhalvan (I Am the First) is a Tamil Nadu government initiative providing **free skill training, industry-relevant certifications, and career guidance** to college students and youth. The scheme partners with leading companies and edtech platforms to offer courses in technology, communication, and employability skills — all at **no cost to the student**.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Students** currently enrolled in any recognized **college, polytechnic, or university** in Tamil Nadu
2. **Age:** No strict age limit — open to all enrolled students
3. **Course:** Open to students of **all streams** (arts, science, engineering, etc.)
4. **No income criteria** — available to all students regardless of family income

## What Training is Available?

| Category | Examples |
|----------|---------|
| Technology | Python, Java, AI/ML, Data Science, Cloud Computing, Cybersecurity |
| Communication | Spoken English, Business Communication, Presentation Skills |
| Employability | Resume Building, Interview Skills, Aptitude Training |
| Industry Certifications | Google, Microsoft, AWS, Infosys certifications |
| Domain-Specific | Finance, Marketing, Design, Healthcare |

## How to Register

### Online Registration

1. **Visit** [naanmudhalvan.tn.gov.in](https://naanmudhalvan.tn.gov.in)
2. **Click** "Student Registration"
3. **Enter** your college name, course, and year of study
4. **Register** with Aadhaar and mobile number
5. **Create** your student profile
6. **Browse** available courses and certifications
7. **Enroll** in courses of your choice
8. **Complete** the courses online at your own pace
9. **Get certified** — certificates are issued by partner companies

### Through College

1. Many colleges have **Naan Mudhalvan coordinators**
2. Contact your college placement cell or HOD
3. Register through college-facilitated sessions
4. Attend training sessions organized on campus

## Key Features

- **Completely Free** — no registration fee, no course fee
- **Industry-Recognized Certifications** from top companies
- **Online and Offline** training modes
- **Self-Paced Learning** — complete courses at your convenience
- **Career Guidance** and placement support
- **Assessment-Based** certificates with real value

## Documents Required

- **College ID Card**
- **Aadhaar Card**
- **Mobile Number and Email ID**
- No other documents needed for registration

## Important Tips

1. **Start Early:** Register in your first year of college to maximize the courses you can complete.
2. **Choose Industry Certifications:** Prioritize courses that offer Google, Microsoft, or AWS certifications — these add real value to your resume.
3. **Complete Assessments:** Just enrolling isn't enough. Complete all modules and pass assessments to earn the certificate.
4. **Update LinkedIn:** Add your Naan Mudhalvan certifications to your LinkedIn profile for better visibility.
5. **Attend Live Sessions:** If your college organizes offline sessions, attend them for hands-on learning.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Is Naan Mudhalvan only for engineering students?
**No.** Students from all streams — arts, science, commerce, engineering, polytechnic — can register and benefit.

### Q2: Do I get paid for completing courses?
**No.** This is a free training scheme, not a stipend scheme. The benefit is free certifications and skills.

### Q3: Are the certificates recognized by companies?
**Yes.** Many certificates are issued by Google, Microsoft, Infosys, etc. — these are recognized industry-wide.

### Q4: Can I enroll in multiple courses?
**Yes.** You can enroll in as many courses as you like and complete them at your own pace.

### Q5: Is this only for Tamil Nadu students?
**Yes.** You must be enrolled in a recognized institution in Tamil Nadu.

### Q6: What happens after I complete the training?
You receive a certificate. Some courses also connect you with job opportunities and placement drives.
