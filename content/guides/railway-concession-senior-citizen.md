## What is Railway Concession for Senior Citizens?

Indian Railways offers concessional fares to senior citizens travelling across India. Women aged 58 years and above, and men aged 60 years and above, are eligible for discounted train tickets. This concession applies across most classes and can be availed during both online and counter booking.

> **Note:** The Government of India had suspended senior citizen concessions during COVID-19. As of the latest updates, the concession for senior citizens on Indian Railways has **not been restored** for general passengers. However, it remains available for specific categories. Please check the latest notification on the official Indian Railways website before booking.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Women:** 58 years and above
- **Men:** 60 years and above
- Age is calculated as on the date of journey
- Valid for Indian citizens only
- Applicable on all trains except a few premium/special trains (like Rajdhani, Shatabdi under Flexi-Fare — concession may vary)

## Discount Percentage (When Available)

| Category | Discount |
|----------|----------|
| Male Senior Citizen (60+) | 40% on basic fare |
| Female Senior Citizen (58+) | 50% on basic fare |

**Note:** The concession applies only to the **basic fare**, not on reservation charges, superfast surcharge, or GST.

## Classes Eligible for Concession

Senior citizen concession is applicable in the following classes:

- Second Sitting (2S)
- Sleeper Class (SL)
- AC Chair Car (CC)
- AC 3 Tier (3A)
- AC 2 Tier (2A)
- AC First Class (1A)
- First Class (FC)

**Not applicable** on Garib Rath, Duronto (in some cases), and trains under dynamic/flexi-fare pricing.

## Documents Required

- **Age Proof (any one):**
  - Aadhaar Card
  - Voter ID
  - Passport
  - PAN Card
  - Pension document with date of birth
- **Valid Photo ID** to carry during the journey (Aadhaar, Voter ID, or Passport)

## How to Book: Step-by-Step Process

### Online Booking (via IRCTC)

1. Visit [irctc.co.in](https://www.irctc.co.in) and log in to your account
2. Enter the journey details — from station, to station, date of travel, and class
3. Select the train and click **Book Now**
4. While entering passenger details, enter the correct **date of birth**
5. If eligible, the system will automatically show a checkbox: **"Opt for Senior Citizen Concession"**
6. Tick the checkbox to avail the discount
7. The discounted fare will be reflected in the fare summary
8. Complete payment and download the e-ticket
9. Carry a valid photo ID during the journey

### Offline Booking (Railway Counter)

1. Visit the nearest railway reservation counter
2. Fill out the reservation form with correct date of birth
3. Submit the form along with a photocopy of age proof
4. The booking clerk will apply the senior citizen concession
5. Pay the discounted fare and collect the ticket
6. Carry the original age proof during travel

## Fees

| Component | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Concession | Automatically applied (no extra fee) |
| Booking charges (online) | ₹20 for SL/2S, ₹40 for AC classes |
| Cancellation | Standard IRCTC cancellation rules apply |

## Processing Time

- **Online:** Instant booking with concession applied automatically
- **Counter:** Immediate at the reservation window

## Important Tips

1. **Always enter the correct date of birth** in your IRCTC profile — the concession depends on it
2. The concession is on **basic fare only**, not on total ticket cost
3. Senior citizens get **lower berth preference** — select this option while booking
4. If the concession checkbox does not appear, verify your date of birth in your IRCTC profile
5. Keep checking the official Indian Railways website for restoration of senior citizen concession for general passengers

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Is senior citizen railway concession currently available in 2026?

The Government suspended senior citizen concessions during COVID-19. As of March 2026, the concession has not been fully restored for general passengers. Check [indianrailways.gov.in](https://www.indianrailways.gov.in) for the latest status.

### Q2: What is the age limit for senior citizen concession in railways?

Women aged 58 years and above, and men aged 60 years and above are eligible.

### Q3: Can I avail senior citizen concession on Tatkal tickets?

No, senior citizen concession is **not available** on Tatkal quota bookings.

### Q4: Is the concession available on Rajdhani and Shatabdi trains?

Yes, the concession is available but may be limited under flexi-fare pricing. The discount applies to base fare only.

### Q5: Do I need to carry age proof during the journey?

Yes, you must carry a valid photo ID with date of birth during the journey for verification by TTE (Ticket Checking Staff).

### Q6: Can I book a senior citizen concession ticket for someone else?

Yes, you can book on behalf of a senior citizen by entering their correct date of birth and selecting the concession option.

### Q7: Is lower berth guaranteed for senior citizens?

Lower berth is given on a **priority basis** to senior citizens, but it is not guaranteed. You can select the preference during booking.
