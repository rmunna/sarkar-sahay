# Assam Ration Card Apply Online 2026

The Assam ration card is issued by the **Food, Civil Supplies & Consumer Affairs Department, Government of Assam**. Assam manages its PDS through the **EPDS Assam** (Electronic Public Distribution System) portal. Assam has a large rural population and significant tribal communities in hill districts — ration card coverage is extensive under NFSA.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [epds.assam.gov.in](https://epds.assam.gov.in) or your nearest Arunodoi Seva Kendra / CSC. PHH families get 5 kg grain/person/month free under PM GKAY.

---

## Ration Card Types in Assam

| Type | Category | Entitlement |
|------|----------|-------------|
| **PHH (Priority Household)** | NFSA BPL families | 5 kg grain/person/month free (PM GKAY) |
| **AAY (Antyodaya Anna Yojana)** | Poorest households | 35 kg/family/month free |
| **NPHH (Non-Priority Household)** | Non-subsidised / APL | Identity document; no grain subsidy |
| **State BPL** | State-covered BPL beyond central quota | As per state order |

---

## Eligibility

**PHH/BPL ration card:**
- Annual household income below ₹1.20 lakh (rural) or ₹1.50 lakh (urban)
- Not a regular government employee
- No income taxpayer in the family
- No motorised 4-wheeler vehicle
- No agricultural land above 2.5 acres irrigated / 5 acres unirrigated (general); tribal norms may differ
- Aadhaar card mandatory for all members

**AAY:**
- Landless agricultural labourers with no stable income
- Old-age pensioners living alone
- Widow / disabled persons with no family support
- Migrant workers with no fixed residence
- Primitive Tribal Groups (PTGs)

---

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar cards (all family members — mandatory)
- Residence proof (electricity bill, panchayat certificate, voter ID with address)
- Passport-size photographs
- Income certificate from Circle Officer (CO) or SDO
- Caste / tribe certificate (if SC/ST — especially for hill districts and plains tribal areas)
- Bank account passbook (for DBT)
- Voter ID (additional identity proof)

## How to Apply

**Method 1: EPDS Assam Online Portal**

1. Go to [epds.assam.gov.in](https://epds.assam.gov.in)
2. Click **"Citizen Services"** → **"Apply for New Ration Card"**
3. Log in / register with Aadhaar-linked mobile number
4. Fill in household head details and add all members
5. Select district, block, gram panchayat / ward
6. Upload documents (income certificate, address proof, photographs)
7. Submit and note acknowledgement number

**Method 2: Arunodoi Seva Kendra / CSC**

1. Visit nearest **Arunodoi Seva Kendra** (Assam's CSC network) or **Jan Seva Kendra**
2. Operator fills the online application with your documents
3. Service fee: approximately ₹30–50
4. Collect printed acknowledgement

**Method 3: Circle Office / Block Office**

1. Visit your **Circle Office** or **Block Development Office**
2. Collect and fill the ration card application form
3. Submit with all documents to Block Supply Inspector
4. Inspector verifies and forwards to DSO for approval

## Status Check

1. Go to [epds.assam.gov.in](https://epds.assam.gov.in)
2. Click **"Application Status"**
3. Enter acknowledgement number or Aadhaar number
4. View status — submitted / under verification / approved / rejected

## Processing Time

| Stage | Duration |
|-------|----------|
| Circle Officer / field inspection | 10–20 days |
| DSO (District Supply Officer) approval | 15–30 days |
| Total | 30–45 days |

> In remote hill districts (Karbi Anglong, Dima Hasao, etc.), verification may take longer. Follow up with your Circle Office if no update after 45 days.

| Card Type | Quantity | Price |
|-----------|----------|-------|
| PHH per person | 5 kg (rice + wheat) | Free (PM GKAY) |
| AAY per family | 35 kg | Free |

> Assam's PDS allocation is predominantly **rice** — as per local preference, some districts receive more rice than wheat.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can tea garden workers in Assam get ration cards?

Yes — tea garden workers are among the targeted groups for AAY/PHH cards. They should apply at the nearest Circle Office or CSC with employer letter, Aadhaar, and residence proof. Some tea gardens have in-house FPS operations.

### I belong to a tribal community in Assam. Are there special provisions?

Yes — Scheduled Tribe and Bodo families, especially in BTAD (Bodoland Territorial Area Districts) and hill districts, are given priority. Tribe certificate is required. Primitive Tribal Groups (PTGs) are automatically eligible for AAY.

### My family lives across two districts. Can we have separate ration cards?

Each separately residing household unit can have its own ration card. If family members live at different addresses (different towns/districts), they may apply as separate households.

### Does ONORC work in Assam?

Yes — Assam is part of **One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC)**. Migrant workers from other states can use Aadhaar biometric authentication at Assam FPS shops. Assam residents working in other states can also do the same.
