## What is Police Verification Certificate?

A Police Verification Certificate (PVC) is an official document issued by the police authorities confirming that a person has **no criminal record** or pending criminal cases. It is commonly required for passport applications, employment, tenant verification, and visa processing.

In India, police verification is done at different levels:

- **Police Clearance Certificate (PCC)** — For Indian citizens going abroad (issued through Passport Seva Kendra)
- **Character Certificate** — For employment and general purposes (issued by local police station)
- **Tenant Verification** — For landlords to verify tenants (done online in most states)
- **Passport Police Verification** — Mandatory part of the passport application process

## When Do You Need Police Verification?

| Purpose | Type Required |
|---------|--------------|
| Passport application | Automatic (part of passport process) |
| Visa for foreign country | Police Clearance Certificate (PCC) |
| Government job | Character Certificate from police |
| Private employment | Verification through employer |
| Renting a property | Tenant Verification |
| Gun license | Police Verification Report |
| Adoption | Character Certificate |

## How to Get Police Clearance Certificate (PCC)

PCC is the most commonly requested form of police verification, especially for people moving abroad.

### Step 1: Apply Online at Passport Seva

Go to [passportindia.gov.in](https://passportindia.gov.in) and log in to your account.

### Step 2: Select "Police Clearance Certificate"

From the services menu, choose **"Apply for Police Clearance Certificate"**.

### Step 3: Fill the Application Form

- Personal details — name, date of birth, address
- Passport number (you must already have a valid passport)
- Purpose of PCC — employment, immigration, education, etc.
- Country for which PCC is required

### Step 4: Pay the Fee

- PCC fee: **₹500** (approximately)
- Payment via online banking, credit/debit card, or SBI challan

### Step 5: Book Appointment at PSK

- Select your nearest **Passport Seva Kendra (PSK)** or Regional Passport Office (RPO)
- Book an appointment slot

### Step 6: Visit PSK

- Carry original passport and appointment confirmation
- Biometrics and photo will be captured
- Document verification at the counter

### Step 7: Receive PCC

- If your address matches passport records and you have no criminal record, PCC may be issued **same day**
- If police verification is required, it takes **1-3 weeks**
- PCC is sent by post or available for collection at PSK

## How to Get Character Certificate from Police Station

For employment and other domestic purposes, you need a character certificate from your local police station.

### Online Process (Available in Most States)

1. Visit your **state police portal** (e.g., UP Police, Maharashtra Police, Delhi Police)
2. Look for **"Character Certificate"** or **"Police Verification"** section
3. Fill the online application with personal details and address
4. Upload documents — Aadhaar card, address proof, passport-size photo
5. Pay the fee (₹50-200 depending on state)
6. Police will conduct a **field verification** at your address
7. Certificate is issued within **1-2 weeks**

### Offline Process

1. Visit your **local police station** (under whose jurisdiction your address falls)
2. Submit an application addressed to the SHO/Station In-charge
3. Attach photocopies of Aadhaar, address proof, and photographs
4. Police will verify at your residence
5. Collect the certificate after verification (7-15 days)

## Tenant Police Verification

Tenant verification is mandatory in most Indian cities and is now mostly done online.

### Online Process

1. Visit your state's **tenant verification portal**
   - **Delhi:** [dfroms.delhi.gov.in](https://dfroms.delhi.gov.in)
   - **Maharashtra:** [citizen.mahapolice.gov.in](https://citizen.mahapolice.gov.in)
   - **Karnataka:** [ksp.karnataka.gov.in](https://ksp.karnataka.gov.in)
   - **UP:** [uppolice.gov.in](https://uppolice.gov.in)
2. Register as **landlord or tenant**
3. Fill in tenant details — name, Aadhaar number, permanent address
4. Upload tenant's ID proof and photograph
5. Submit the form — local police may visit for physical verification
6. Verification completed within **1-2 weeks**

## Documents Required

### For PCC
- Valid Indian passport (original)
- Appointment letter from Passport Seva
- Current address proof (if different from passport)

### For Character Certificate
- Aadhaar card
- Address proof (ration card, electricity bill, etc.)
- Passport-size photographs (2-3)
- Application form (varies by state)

### For Tenant Verification
- Tenant's Aadhaar card copy
- Tenant's photograph
- Rental agreement copy
- Landlord's ID proof

## Tips for Faster Processing

- **Keep documents ready** — Incomplete documents cause delays
- **Ensure address matches** — PCC is fastest when your current address matches passport address
- **Apply online** where possible — Online applications are tracked and processed faster
- **Follow up** — If verification is delayed beyond 3 weeks, contact the local police station or file a grievance on the passport portal
- **Be available** for field verification — The constable may visit your address without prior notice

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long is a Police Clearance Certificate valid?
PCC is generally valid for **6 months** from the date of issue. Some countries may accept it for up to 1 year. Check the specific requirements of the country or organization requesting it.

### Can I get PCC without a passport?
No. PCC is issued only to Indian passport holders. If you don't have a passport, apply for one first through Passport Seva.

### Is police verification mandatory for passport?
Yes, police verification is part of the passport issuance process. However, under the **Tatkal scheme**, passport is issued before police verification is completed.

### What if I have a criminal case pending?
If you have a pending criminal case, the PCC will **not** be issued until the case is resolved. The character certificate will also mention the pending case.

### Can NRIs apply for PCC?
Yes, NRIs can apply for PCC through the nearest Indian Embassy or Consulate, or through Passport Seva during their visit to India.
