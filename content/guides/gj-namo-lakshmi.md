## What is Namo Lakshmi Yojana?

Namo Lakshmi Yojana is a scholarship scheme launched by the Government of Gujarat in the 2024-25 budget to encourage girls' education in secondary and higher secondary schools. The scheme provides a total of **₹50,000** over 4 years (Class 9 to Class 12) to girl students studying in government and grant-in-aid schools.

### Year-wise Benefits

| Class | Annual Scholarship |
|-------|-------------------|
| Class 9 | ₹10,000 |
| Class 10 | ₹10,000 |
| Class 11 | ₹15,000 |
| Class 12 | ₹15,000 |
| **Total** | **₹50,000** |

The scholarship is credited directly to the student's bank account in instalments.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Girl students** studying in Class 9 to 12
- Must be studying in a **government or grant-in-aid school** in Gujarat
- Must be a **resident of Gujarat**
- **No income limit** specified — all girls in government/aided schools are eligible
- Must maintain **minimum attendance** as per school norms
- Must be enrolled through the school's student portal

## Documents Required

- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) of the student
- School enrollment/bonafide certificate
- Bank account in the student's name (or joint with parent)
- Bank passbook copy
- Passport-size photograph
- Previous year marksheet (for Class 10-12 applicants)

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Through School

1. **Schools initiate** — In most cases, the school applies on behalf of eligible students through the education department portal
2. **Students provide documents** — Submit Aadhaar, bank passbook copy, and photo to the school
3. **School uploads data** — The school uploads student details to the Digital Gujarat or education portal
4. **Verification** — Department verifies student enrollment and attendance
5. **Scholarship credited** — Amount transferred directly to the student's bank account

### Online (if self-application available)

1. **Visit** [Digital Gujarat portal](https://digitalgujarat.gov.in)
2. **Register** with student details
3. **Select** Namo Lakshmi Yojana
4. **Fill** the application form
5. **Upload** documents
6. **Submit** and track status

## Processing Time

- School data compilation: **1-2 months** after academic year begins
- Verification: **1-2 months**
- Scholarship disbursement: Typically within **3-6 months** of the academic year start

## Fees

**No fees.** The scholarship application is completely free.

## Important Tips

1. **Open bank account early** — Ensure the girl student has a bank account before the academic year starts
2. **Link Aadhaar to bank** — DBT requires Aadhaar-linked bank account
3. **Maintain attendance** — Regular attendance is important for scholarship continuation
4. **Inform school** — Make sure the school has included you in the scheme's student list
5. **Continue studies** — Scholarship is only for enrolled, active students; dropping out disqualifies

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Is Namo Lakshmi Yojana available for private school students?
No. The scheme is only for girls studying in **government and grant-in-aid schools** in Gujarat.

### Q2. Is there any income limit?
No specific income limit has been announced. All eligible girls in government/aided schools can benefit.

### Q3. Can boys apply?
No. This scheme is exclusively for **girl students**. Boys may check the [Namo Saraswati Yojana](/guide/gj-namo-saraswati) for science stream scholarships.

### Q4. Do I need to apply every year?
Yes, typically the school updates the student list each academic year. Ensure your details are submitted at the start of each year.

### Q5. What if I transfer to another school?
Inform the new school to include you in the scheme. The scholarship continues as long as you study in a government/aided school in Gujarat.

### Q6. Can I get Namo Lakshmi along with other scholarships?
Check with the education department. Some scholarships may be combinable while others may not.
