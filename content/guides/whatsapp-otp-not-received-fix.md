## WhatsApp OTP Not Received? Here's How to Fix It

When you install or reinstall WhatsApp, it sends a **6-digit verification code via SMS** to confirm your phone number. If this OTP (One-Time Password) is not arriving, you won't be able to use WhatsApp. This is one of the most common issues faced by Indian users, especially on Jio, Airtel, Vi, and BSNL networks.

## Why Is WhatsApp OTP Not Coming?

- **DND (Do Not Disturb)** is active on your number — blocks promotional/transactional SMS
- **SIM card not inserted** properly or SIM is damaged
- **Wrong country code** entered (India is +91)
- **Network congestion** — SMS delivery delayed during peak hours
- **Phone storage full** — SMS cannot be received
- **SMS inbox full** — old messages blocking new ones
- **Wait timer active** — you requested too many OTPs and hit a cooldown
- **Dual SIM issue** — OTP sent to SIM 1 but SIM 2 is active for SMS

## Step-by-Step Fixes

### Fix 1: Wait a Few Minutes

- SMS delivery can take **1-5 minutes** depending on network load
- Don't tap "Resend" immediately — wait for the full timer to expire
- If you tap resend too quickly, you may trigger a **longer wait period** (up to 12 hours)

### Fix 2: Check Country Code

1. On the WhatsApp verification screen, ensure country is set to **India**
2. Country code should be **+91**
3. Enter your **10-digit mobile number** WITHOUT leading zero
4. Example: If your number is 98765 43210, enter exactly that after +91

### Fix 3: Deactivate DND (Do Not Disturb)

DND blocks many types of SMS including OTPs for some users. To deactivate:

**Via SMS:**
- Send **STOP** to **1909** from your registered number

**Via call:**
- Call **1909** and follow the IVR instructions to deactivate DND

**Via operator app:**
- **Jio:** MyJio app → Menu → DND settings
- **Airtel:** Airtel Thanks app → More → DND Preferences
- **Vi:** Vi app → Menu → DND
- **BSNL:** Send "STOP" to 1909

> **Note:** After deactivating DND, wait **10-15 minutes** before requesting a new OTP. DND deactivation can take up to 24 hours with some operators, but usually works within minutes.

### Fix 4: Use the "Call Me" Option

If SMS doesn't work, WhatsApp offers a **phone call verification**:

1. Wait for the SMS timer to expire on the verification screen
2. Tap **"Call Me"** button that appears
3. You'll receive an **automated phone call** reading out the 6-digit code
4. Enter the code in WhatsApp

> **This is the fastest fix** if SMS is consistently not arriving. The call usually comes within 30 seconds.

### Fix 5: Check Your SIM Card

1. **Remove and reinsert** your SIM card
2. Make sure the SIM is in the **correct slot** (if dual SIM, use the slot that receives SMS)
3. Try inserting the SIM in a **different phone** to check if it works
4. If the SIM is **damaged or expired**, visit your operator's store for a replacement

### Fix 6: Check Phone Storage

If your phone storage is full, new SMS messages may not arrive:

1. Go to **Settings → Storage**
2. Free up space by deleting unused apps, old photos, or WhatsApp media
3. Delete old SMS messages from your inbox
4. Restart your phone and request the OTP again

### Fix 7: Dual SIM Configuration

If you have a dual SIM phone:

1. Make sure the SIM number you're **registering with WhatsApp** is the one set as **default for SMS**
2. Go to **Settings → SIM Cards → Default SMS SIM** and set it to the correct SIM
3. Or temporarily **remove the other SIM** during verification

### Fix 8: Wait Timer / Cooldown

If you've requested too many OTPs:
- WhatsApp imposes a **wait timer** that increases each time: 1 min → 5 min → 10 min → 1 hour → 12 hours
- **Do not keep requesting** — each failed attempt increases the wait
- Wait for the full timer to expire, then try **once more**
- If you hit a 12-hour wait, come back after 12 hours and try the **"Call Me"** option

### Fix 9: Network Issues

1. Toggle **Airplane Mode** on for 10 seconds, then off
2. Restart your phone
3. Try moving to an area with **better network coverage**
4. If on Wi-Fi calling, temporarily disable it and try on regular network
5. Contact your operator if SMS is not working for any sender

## Important Tips

- **"Call Me" is your best friend** — if SMS fails twice, use the call option immediately
- **Don't switch SIMs rapidly** — WhatsApp may flag your number if you verify from different numbers frequently
- **Keep your SIM active** — TRAI requires telecom operators to deactivate SIMs not used for 90 days
- **Save your WhatsApp backup** before changing phones or numbers
- **Two-step verification PIN** is different from the OTP — that's a PIN you set yourself for extra security

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long should I wait for the WhatsApp OTP?
Wait at least 5 minutes before requesting again. The OTP can be delayed due to network congestion.

### What is the "Call Me" option in WhatsApp verification?
After the SMS timer expires, WhatsApp shows a "Call Me" button. Tapping it triggers an automated call that reads out your 6-digit verification code.

### Will deactivating DND help me receive the WhatsApp OTP?
Yes, in many cases. DND can block transactional SMS on some operator configurations. Send STOP to 1909 to deactivate it.

### I'm stuck on a 12-hour wait. What do I do?
Unfortunately, you must wait. This happens when too many OTP requests are made. After 12 hours, try once using the "Call Me" option.

### Can I use WhatsApp without a SIM card?
No, WhatsApp requires a valid phone number for verification. You need an active SIM that can receive SMS or calls.

### WhatsApp says "You've guessed too many times." What does this mean?
You've entered the wrong verification code too many times. Wait for the timer to reset (usually 12 hours) before trying again.

### My OTP goes to a different phone. Why?
If you have call forwarding or SMS forwarding enabled, the OTP may go to another number. Disable forwarding in your phone settings.
