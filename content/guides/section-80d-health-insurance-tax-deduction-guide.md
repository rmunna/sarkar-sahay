# Section 80D Health Insurance Tax Deduction India 2026

**Section 80D** of the Income Tax Act allows you to deduct health insurance premiums from your taxable income. This is one of the most commonly claimed deductions — and has limits that many people don't fully use.

> **Important**: Section 80D deduction is only available under the **old tax regime**. Under the new default tax regime, Section 80D cannot be claimed.

## Section 80D Deduction Limits at a Glance

| Premium Paid For | Deduction Limit |
|-----------------|----------------|
| Self + Spouse + Children (all below 60) | ₹25,000 |
| Self + Spouse + Children (self/spouse 60+) | ₹50,000 |
| Parents (both/one below 60) | ₹25,000 |
| Parents (one or both 60+) | **₹50,000** |
| **Maximum if parents are 60+** | **₹1,00,000** (₹50K self + ₹50K parents) |

### Preventive Health Check-Up

Within the above limits, you can also claim **preventive health check-up** expenses:
- Up to **₹5,000** (per family)
- This ₹5,000 is included within — not in addition to — the ₹25,000/₹50,000 limit

**Example**: Premium ₹20,000 + health check ₹5,000 = claim ₹25,000 total (not ₹30,000).

## Detailed Deduction Scenarios

### Scenario 1: All family members below 60

| Coverage | Limit |
|----------|-------|
| Self + spouse + children | ₹25,000 |
| Parents (both below 60) | ₹25,000 |
| **Total 80D max** | **₹50,000** |

### Scenario 2: Self/spouse is 60+ (senior citizen)

| Coverage | Limit |
|----------|-------|
| Self + spouse (senior citizen) | ₹50,000 |
| Parents (below 60) | ₹25,000 |
| **Total 80D max** | **₹75,000** |

### Scenario 3: Parents are 60+ (most common scenario)

| Coverage | Limit |
|----------|-------|
| Self + spouse + children (below 60) | ₹25,000 |
| Parents (senior citizens 60+) | ₹50,000 |
| **Total 80D max** | **₹75,000** |

### Scenario 4: Both self and parents are 60+

| Coverage | Limit |
|----------|-------|
| Self (60+) | ₹50,000 |
| Parents (60+) | ₹50,000 |
| **Total 80D max** | **₹1,00,000** |

## What Counts for Section 80D

### Eligible Payments

| Payment Type | Eligible? |
|-------------|----------|
| Health insurance premium (individual plan) | ✅ Yes |
| Family floater health insurance premium | ✅ Yes |
| Top-up health insurance premium | ✅ Yes |
| Critical illness insurance premium | ✅ Yes |
| Preventive health check-up fees | ✅ Yes (up to ₹5,000 within limit) |
| Group health insurance (employer policy) | ✅ If you pay out of pocket (not pre-tax) |

### Not Eligible for 80D

| Payment Type | Eligible? |
|-------------|----------|
| Life insurance premium | ❌ No — claim under 80C |
| Premium paid in **cash** (except preventive check-up) | ❌ No — must be digital/cheque/card |
| Premium paid for siblings, grandparents, in-laws | ❌ No — only self, spouse, children, parents |
| OPD expenses, hospital bills, medicines | ❌ No — not premium |
| CGHS/ESIC contribution | ❌ Not typically, check CG rules |

> **Important**: All health insurance premiums (except the ₹5,000 preventive check-up) must be paid by **non-cash means** (bank transfer, cheque, credit/debit card, net banking, UPI). Cash payment is not eligible for 80D.

## When Parents' Premium Qualifies

You can claim 80D for parents' health insurance if:
- You are paying the premium (even if parents have their own income)
- Parents don't need to be dependent on you
- You pay via your bank account / card (not cash, not parents' account)

**Key nuance**: If your parents pay their own premium themselves, you cannot claim 80D for it — even if they're your parents. The person paying must claim the deduction.

## How to Claim Section 80D While Filing ITR

1. Collect premium receipts from your health insurance provider (usually emailed)
2. Note the premium amount and whose name the policy is in
3. In ITR: Go to "Deductions" → Section 80D
4. Enter:
   - Premium for self/family: up to ₹25,000
   - Premium for parents: up to ₹25,000 or ₹50,000 (depending on age)
   - Preventive check-up: within the limit
5. ITR system caps at the applicable limit automatically

> Your employer's Form 16 may already show 80D in the TDS calculation if you declared it to your employer. Cross-check Form 16 before claiming in ITR to avoid double-claiming.

## 80D and the New Tax Regime

| Tax Regime | Section 80D Available? |
|-----------|----------------------|
| **Old regime** | ✅ Yes — full deduction |
| **New regime (default from FY 2023-24)** | ❌ No |

If you're filing under the new tax regime (which is now default), you **cannot claim Section 80D**. The new regime offers lower slab rates in exchange for removing most deductions.

To use Section 80D, you must explicitly opt for the **old tax regime** when filing ITR.

## Is It Worth Switching to Old Regime Just for 80D?

It depends on your total deductions. The new regime is better for most people with few deductions. But if you have:
- Section 80C: ₹1,50,000
- Section 80D: ₹50,000–₹1,00,000
- HRA exemption
- Home loan interest (Section 24)

...the old regime may give lower total tax. Use a tax calculator to compare.

## Senior Citizens Without Health Insurance

Senior citizens (60+) who have **no health insurance** can still claim up to **₹50,000** for medical expenses paid (as long as they're not covered by any health insurance). This provision was introduced specifically for senior citizens who may not get health insurance after a certain age.

> Note: This only applies to senior citizens (60+). For non-senior citizens, you must have actual insurance to claim 80D.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My company provides group health insurance. Can I also claim 80D for a personal policy?

Yes — if your company's group insurance is completely employer-funded (you don't pay the premium), you can still claim 80D for a separate personal policy you buy. Only deduct what you actually pay out of pocket.

### My spouse and I both pay our own insurance premiums. Who claims the deduction?

Each of you can claim the deduction for what you individually pay. You can claim for your portion; your spouse claims for theirs. However, within the ₹25,000 family limit, the deduction can't be claimed twice for the same policy.

### I have a family floater covering me, spouse, child, and parents. How is 80D split?

Split the premium into two parts:
- Premium attributable to self + spouse + child → claim under self limit (₹25,000)
- Premium attributable to parents → claim under parents limit (₹25,000 or ₹50,000)

In practice, divide the premium proportionally or ask the insurer for a split certificate.

### What's the 80D benefit for preventive check-ups at a government hospital?

The ₹5,000 preventive health check-up deduction applies to private clinics and also diagnostic centers. Cash payment is allowed for health check-ups (unlike insurance premiums). Keep receipts.
