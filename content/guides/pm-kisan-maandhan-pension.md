# PM Kisan Maandhan Yojana — Pension Scheme for Farmers

**PM Kisan Maandhan Yojana (PMKMY)** is a government pension scheme for **small and marginal farmers** in India. After age 60, you get **₹3,000 every month** as pension. The government pays **50% of the contribution** — you only pay a small amount every month.

This scheme was launched on **12 September 2019** by the Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare.

---

## What is PM Kisan Maandhan Yojana?

PM Kisan Maandhan is a **voluntary and contributory pension scheme**. Here is how it works:

- You pay a **small monthly amount** (₹55 to ₹200 depending on your age)
- The **government matches your contribution** — pays the same amount
- Both amounts go into a **pension fund** managed by LIC
- After you turn **60 years old**, you get **₹3,000 per month** pension
- Pension comes directly to your **bank account**

> **Simple example:** If you join at age 18, you pay only ₹55/month. The government also puts ₹55/month. After age 60, you get ₹3,000/month for life.

---

## Who Can Apply? (Eligibility)

You can apply if you meet **all** these conditions:

| Condition | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Age** | 18 to 40 years |
| **Land** | Small/marginal farmer with up to **2 hectares** (about 5 acres) cultivable land |
| **Aadhaar** | Must have Aadhaar card |
| **Bank Account** | Must have savings bank account or Jan Dhan account |

### Who Cannot Apply?

You **cannot** apply if you are already covered under:

- **NPS** (National Pension System)
- **ESIC** (Employees' State Insurance Corporation)
- **EPFO** (Employees' Provident Fund Organisation)
- Any other social security scheme of the government

Also, **higher income tax payers** are not eligible.

## Documents Required

Keep these documents ready before applying:

1. **Aadhaar Card** — mandatory for identity and linking
2. **Bank Passbook / Account Details** — IFSC code and account number
3. **Mobile Number** — linked to Aadhaar (for OTP verification)
4. **Land Records** — to prove you are a small/marginal farmer (up to 2 hectares)

---

## How to Apply for PM Kisan Maandhan Yojana

There are **two ways** to apply:

### Option 1: Apply Online at pmkmy.gov.in (Self-Registration)

1. Go to **[pmkmy.gov.in](https://pmkmy.gov.in)**
2. Click on **"Self Enrollment"** (using mobile number)
3. Enter your **mobile number** → receive OTP → verify
4. Enter your **name, Aadhaar number, date of birth**
5. Enter **bank account details** (account number + IFSC)
6. Upload **land record details** if asked
7. Choose your **monthly contribution** (auto-calculated based on age)
8. Set up **auto-debit** from your bank account
9. Submit the form → **Kisan Pension Card** is generated
10. Take a **printout** of the pension card for your records

### Option 2: Apply Through CSC (Common Service Centre)

If you are not comfortable with online process, go to your **nearest CSC centre**:

1. Visit any **CSC (Common Service Centre)** or **PM Kisan Seva Kendra**
2. Carry your **Aadhaar card**, **bank passbook**, and **land records**
3. The CSC operator (VLE) will fill your form
4. Give your **Aadhaar-based e-KYC** (fingerprint or OTP)
5. The operator will set up your pension account
6. You will get a **Kisan Pension Card** — keep it safe!

> **Tip:** To find the nearest CSC, visit [csc.gov.in](https://csc.gov.in) or call **14155**.

## How to Check PM Kisan Maandhan Status

To check your enrollment or pension status:

1. Visit **[maandhan.in](https://maandhan.in)**
2. Click on **"Login"** using your mobile number
3. Enter OTP and log in
4. You can see your **enrollment status**, **contribution history**, and **pension details**

You can also visit your nearest CSC centre and ask them to check for you.

If you are also a PM Kisan beneficiary, check your [PM Kisan status here](/guide/pm-kisan-status-check-beneficiary-list).

## Exit and Withdrawal Rules

### If You Want to Leave Before Age 60

- **Before 10 years of joining:** You get back only your contribution (the amount you paid) with savings bank interest rate
- **After 10 years but before age 60:** You get back your contribution with accumulated interest (as earned by the pension fund) or savings bank interest, whichever is higher
- **If you don't pay regularly:** Your account doesn't close immediately. You can pay the pending amount with interest and continue. Otherwise, the account will be closed and you get your share back.

### If Subscriber Dies Before Age 60

- The **spouse (husband or wife)** can choose to continue the scheme by paying the remaining contributions
- If the spouse does not want to continue, the subscriber's share (contribution + interest) is returned

---

## Family Pension (For Spouse)

This is an important benefit:

- After the subscriber (pension holder) passes away **after age 60**, the spouse gets **50% of the pension amount** — that is **₹1,500 per month** as family pension
- This family pension is **only for the spouse** — not for children or other family members
- If both subscriber and spouse pass away, the entire money goes back to the pension fund

## PM Kisan Maandhan vs Atal Pension Yojana (APY)

Many people ask — should I choose PM Kisan Maandhan or [Atal Pension Yojana](/guide/atal-pension-yojana-apy)? Here is the comparison:

| Feature | PM Kisan Maandhan | Atal Pension Yojana (APY) |
|---------|-------------------|--------------------------|
| **Who can join** | Only small/marginal farmers | Any Indian citizen (18-40) |
| **Pension amount** | Fixed ₹3,000/month | ₹1,000 to ₹5,000/month (you choose) |
| **Government contribution** | 50% matching | Available for some (who joined before 2016) |
| **Maximum land** | Up to 2 hectares | No land condition |
| **Monthly payment** | ₹55 to ₹200 | ₹42 to ₹1,454 (varies by plan) |
| **Pension starts** | At age 60 | At age 60 |
| **Family pension** | 50% to spouse | 100% to spouse |
| **Can join both?** | Yes, you can join both schemes |  |

> **Our suggestion:** If you are a farmer with small land, PM Kisan Maandhan is better because the government pays half. You can also join [APY](/guide/atal-pension-yojana-apy) separately for extra pension.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I join PM Kisan Maandhan if I already get PM Kisan Samman Nidhi?

**Yes!** In fact, you can use your [PM Kisan](/guide/pm-kisan-samman-nidhi) installment to pay the monthly contribution. Both schemes are separate — you can get benefits of both.

### What if I miss a monthly payment?

Your account will not close immediately. You can pay the pending amount along with interest and continue. But try not to miss payments regularly.

### Is there any tax benefit?

The pension amount you receive is **taxable as per your income tax slab**. However, most small farmers fall below the taxable limit, so they won't need to pay tax on this pension.

### Can women farmers apply?

**Yes, absolutely.** Women farmers who own land (up to 2 hectares) or are co-owners can apply. There is no gender restriction.

### What is the helpline number?

Call **1800-267-6888** (toll-free) for any questions about PM Kisan Maandhan Yojana.
