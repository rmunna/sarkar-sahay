## What is PM Kaushal Vikas Yojana?

**Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY)** is the Government of India's flagship skill development scheme under the **Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship**. It provides **free, short-term skill training** in 300+ job roles across 35+ sectors, along with a **monetary reward** upon successful certification.

PMKVY 4.0 (the latest version) emphasises on-the-job training, industry linkage, and placement support.

Key features:
- **Free training** — no fees charged to the trainee
- **₹8,000** cash reward (on average, varies by course) for completing training + passing assessment
- **Government certificate** recognized by industry
- Placement assistance after training
- Available to school/college dropouts and graduates alike

## Who is Eligible?

- **Indian citizen** aged **18 to 35 years** (some courses up to 45 years)
- School dropout or 10th / 12th pass (minimum qualification varies by course)
- Should not have any formal vocational certification in the same skill area
- Unemployed or looking to upgrade skills

There is **no income limit** — the scheme is open to all eligible youth.

## Courses Available (PMKVY 4.0)

PMKVY covers 300+ job roles across sectors including:

| Sector | Sample Courses |
|---|---|
| IT / ITES | Data entry operator, CRM domestic, hardware technician |
| Healthcare | General duty assistant, medical records executive |
| Construction | Mason, electrician, bar bender |
| Retail | Retail sales associate, store manager |
| Automotive | Service technician, auto body repair |
| Beauty & Wellness | Beauty therapist, spa therapist |
| Agriculture | Organic farming, soil testing technician |
| Electronics | Field technician (AC/refrigerator/washing machine) |
| Apparel | Sewing machine operator, tailor |
| Logistics | Warehouse packer, delivery executive |

Course duration: typically **150–400 hours** (3 weeks to 3 months).

## Monetary Reward (Cash Award)

Upon completing training and **passing the assessment**:
- Average reward: **₹8,000** (varies by course — typically ₹7,000–₹10,000)
- Paid directly to the trainee's Aadhaar-linked bank account via DBT
- Reward is higher for longer/more skilled courses

## How to Apply — Step by Step

### Method 1: Online at pmkvyofficial.org

1. Go to **[pmkvyofficial.org](https://pmkvyofficial.org)**
2. Click **"Candidate Registration"**
3. Enter personal details — name, Aadhaar, mobile, address, education
4. Select your preferred **sector** and **job role** (course)
5. Select a **Training Centre** near you
6. Submit → receive registration confirmation

### Method 2: Visit a Training Centre Directly

1. Find PMKVY-approved training centres near you via:
   - [skillindia.gov.in](https://skillindia.gov.in) → "Find Training Centre"
   - Or at your nearest **Skill Hub / Jan Shikshan Sansthan / ITI**
2. Walk in and register for a course that has open seats
3. Training is free — do not pay any fees

> **Caution:** If a training centre asks for fees, it is **NOT authorized**. Report to Skill India helpline.

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card** (mandatory for enrollment)
2. **Educational certificates** (10th marksheet or equivalent)
3. **Bank account in your name** (for receiving the cash reward — Aadhaar-linked)
4. Passport-size photograph

## PMKVY Certification

After completing training and clearing the assessment:
- You receive a **Government of India / NSDC / Sector Skill Council certificate**
- Certificate is issued by the relevant **Sector Skill Council (SSC)** — for example, NASSCOM SSC for IT courses, ASDC for automotive courses
- Certificates are recognized by employers in India and some foreign countries

## Placement After PMKVY

PMKVY 4.0 has a strong placement focus:
- Training centres are required to provide **placement assistance**
- **Rozgar Melas** are organized by Skill India to connect trained youth with employers
- Many companies (Flipkart, Maruti, Taj Hotels, etc.) are official hiring partners

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is PMKVY training really free? No hidden charges?
Yes — officially completely free. If a centre charges any fees, they are not following PMKVY norms. You can report to 1800-123-9626.

### Will I get a job guaranteed after PMKVY?
PMKVY does not guarantee a job. It provides **placement assistance**. The certificate and skill improve your employability. Many sectors like field technician, data entry, retail see good employment outcomes.

### What is the validity of PMKVY certificate?
There is no expiry for the base PMKVY certificate. However, some sectors require periodic re-certification (like healthcare). The certificate is valid for employment purposes indefinitely.

### Can I do PMKVY if I already have a job?
Yes — the scheme allows employed individuals to upgrade skills. However, priority is given to unemployed youth.

### Is there a PMKVY online course?
PMKVY 4.0 includes **e-Skill India** online learning modules. Short courses can be done online via [eskillindia.org](https://eskillindia.org) — these are free but may not carry the full assessment reward.

### Can I do PMKVY after Class 12?
Yes — Class 12 pass, dropout, and graduates can all apply. Choose a course appropriate to your education level.
