# TCS on Foreign Remittance LRS India 2026 — Complete Guide

If you're sending money abroad — for travel, education, investment, or gifts — a portion is collected as Tax Collected at Source (TCS) by your bank. This is not an additional tax; it's an advance deduction that you claim back via ITR. But the rates have changed significantly since Budget 2023.

## What Is LRS? What Is TCS?

**LRS (Liberalised Remittance Scheme)**: RBI's scheme allowing Indian residents to remit up to **USD 2,50,000 per financial year** abroad for permitted purposes (education, travel, medical, investment, gifts, maintenance of family abroad, etc.).

**TCS (Tax Collected at Source)**: Your bank/AD (Authorised Dealer) deducts a percentage from the remittance and deposits it with the government. You get credit for this in Form 26AS and claim it against your tax liability in ITR.

TCS is NOT a final tax — it's a prepayment toward your eventual income tax liability.

## TCS Rates — After Budget 2023 (Effective October 1, 2023)

| Purpose | TCS Rate | Threshold |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| **Education (via loan from financial institution)** | 0.5% | Above ₹7L |
| **Education (own funds, not via loan)** | 5% | Above ₹7L |
| **Medical treatment abroad** | 5% | Above ₹7L |
| **Overseas tour packages** | **20%** | From ₹1 (no threshold) |
| **Any other remittance (investment, gifts, maintenance)** | **20%** | Above ₹7L |

**The Budget 2025 update**: TCS threshold for most purposes raised to ₹10L (from ₹7L). Check the current year's applicable threshold — Budget 2025 memo specified ₹10L threshold for LRS remittances under general category. Verify with your bank.

## Overseas Tour Package — 20% TCS From ₹1

This is the most impactful change. If you book an overseas tour package (flight + hotel + other services bundled):
- **20% TCS** applies from the very first rupee (no ₹7L threshold)
- The travel company/booking agent collects this TCS

**Example**: International family trip package ₹5,00,000
- TCS collected: 20% × ₹5,00,000 = ₹1,00,000
- You pay: ₹6,00,000 total (₹5L trip + ₹1L TCS upfront)
- TCS of ₹1L appears in your Form 26AS
- Claim in ITR: If your total tax liability is ₹60,000, you get ₹40,000 refund

**What counts as a "tour package"**: Any booking that combines 2+ components (flight + hotel, or hotel + sightseeing + transport). A standalone flight booking does NOT qualify as a tour package and falls under "other remittance" with ₹7L/₹10L threshold.

## Forex Card / International Debit Card

If you load money onto a forex card or use your international debit card for overseas spending:
- Below ₹7L in the FY: **No TCS**
- Above ₹7L in the FY: TCS at 20% on the amount exceeding ₹7L

Your bank tracks cumulative LRS outflow in the financial year. When you cross the threshold, TCS kicks in.

## Education Remittance — Favorable Rates

For students studying abroad:

| Condition | TCS Rate |
|-----------|---------|
| Education via education loan from recognized Indian financial institution | **0.5%** (above ₹7L) |
| Education own funds (no loan) | **5%** (above ₹7L) |

**Example**: ₹20L sent abroad for university fees (own funds):
- First ₹7L: 0% TCS
- Remaining ₹13L: 5% TCS = ₹65,000
- Total TCS: ₹65,000

If financed via education loan:
- First ₹7L: 0% TCS
- Remaining ₹13L: 0.5% TCS = ₹6,500
- Total TCS: ₹6,500

**Always use an education loan** if possible — saves ₹58,500 in TCS (which is upfront cash flow even if eventually refunded).

## How TCS Is Collected

Your **Authorised Dealer (AD)** — the bank processing the remittance — collects TCS:
1. You initiate the remittance through your bank
2. Bank calculates TCS based on purpose, amount, and cumulative FY remittances
3. You pay the principal amount + TCS to the bank
4. Bank remits the principal abroad and deposits TCS with the government
5. TCS appears in Form 26AS under Part B (Tax Collected at Source)

## TCS Credit in ITR — Getting Your Money Back

TCS is not lost money — it's credited against your tax liability:
1. File your ITR by July 31 of the AY
2. Under "Tax Payments" → "TDS/TCS Credits" — your TCS appears here (from 26AS)
3. This reduces your total tax payable
4. If TCS > total tax liability → **Refund** of the difference

**Typical scenario for salaried person**:
- Annual tax: ₹1,00,000 (after TDS from employer)
- TCS on foreign trip: ₹40,000
- Net refund: ₹40,000 (since employer already covered the ₹1,00,000 and TCS is extra)

Wait — actually: If employer deducted ₹1,00,000 TDS and your total tax is also ₹1,00,000, there's zero balance. The TCS of ₹40,000 then becomes a full ₹40,000 refund.

## Budget 2025 Update — TCS Threshold Raised

Budget 2025 (presented February 1, 2025) raised the TCS threshold:
- **₹10 lakh** (from ₹7 lakh) for most LRS remittances
- Travel packages: Still 20% from ₹1 (threshold unchanged)
- Effective date: Confirm with your bank for exact implementation date

## Common Scenarios

### Booking International Flight Directly (Not Package)
- Direct flight booking (e.g., on airline website or booking platform without hotel bundled)
- This is "forex outflow" not "tour package" 
- TCS applies only above ₹7L (or ₹10L) cumulative in the year
- Most regular travelers won't cross this threshold with flights alone

### Sending Money to Child Studying Abroad (Monthly Living Expenses)
- If monthly wire transfer (maintenance): 20% TCS above ₹7L threshold
- If you send ₹15L for the year: TCS on ₹8L excess = ₹1,60,000 upfront (reclaimed in ITR)
- If from education loan disbursed to you: 0.5% rate applies

### International Credit Card Spending
Purchases on an Indian international credit card for overseas spending:
- Credit card company reports the total spending
- TCS applies at 20% if cumulative in the year crosses ₹7L
- Bank/card issuer deducts TCS when the bill comes

## Aggregation Across Remittances

The threshold (₹7L or ₹10L) applies to the **aggregate of all LRS remittances** in a financial year, not per transaction. Your bank tracks cumulative LRS outflow under your PAN.

If you send ₹4L in August and ₹5L in January (total ₹9L), and the threshold is ₹7L:
- First ₹7L: No TCS
- Remaining ₹2L: 20% TCS = ₹40,000

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I'm going to Dubai for a week. My tour package is ₹1.5L. Will I pay TCS?

Yes — tour packages attract 20% TCS from the first rupee. Your ₹1.5L package will have ₹30,000 TCS added. You pay ₹1,80,000 total. The ₹30,000 is credited in Form 26AS and reduces your ITR tax liability (refunded if your total tax is less).

### I booked flight and hotel separately (not as a package). TCS?

If booked separately from different vendors: Not a "tour package." Your flight booking in INR via forex card is LRS outflow — TCS applies only above ₹7L/₹10L cumulative threshold in the year. For a single trip below ₹7L in total foreign spending, no TCS.

### I'm a salaried employee in the 20% tax bracket. I paid ₹80,000 TCS on a big international trip. When do I get it back?

When you file your ITR. The refund is processed by the IT Department typically within 30–90 days of filing. The sooner you file (e.g., filing in July rather than December), the sooner you get the refund. Ensure the TCS appears in your 26AS before claiming.
