# AIS / Form 26AS TDS Mismatch — How to Fix and Claim Your Full TDS Credit

If the TDS amount in your **Form 26AS** or **AIS (Annual Information Statement)** doesn't match your **Form 16**, your bank TDS certificate, or what you declared in your ITR, you need to fix this — or you may lose TDS credits you're entitled to, triggering a tax demand.

> **Most common mismatch**: Employer deposits TDS late (after the TDS return quarter closes), so it appears in 26AS in a different quarter than your salary period.

## Why TDS Mismatches Happen

| Cause | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Employer late TDS deposit** | Your employer deducted TDS from salary but didn't deposit to government on time — appears in 26AS later |
| **Wrong PAN linked** | Employer or bank filed TDS return with wrong PAN — credit goes to someone else |
| **Bank TDS on FD interest** | Bank shows full FD interest in AIS but deducted only 10% TDS; the AIS figure looks inflated |
| **TDS challan not linked to your PAN** | TDS was paid but not mapped to your PAN in the TDS return |
| **Previous employer's TDS missing** | Changed jobs mid-year; one employer's TDS not visible |
| **AIS shows higher amount than Form 16** | AIS includes ALL transactions (some may be non-taxable) |

## Step 1: Identify the Type of Mismatch

### Case A: Your 26AS Shows LESS TDS Than Your Form 16

You should have TDS of ₹80,000 per Form 16, but Form 26AS only shows ₹60,000.

**Most likely cause**: Employer deposited TDS late OR filed TDS return with error.

**Solution**: Contact your employer's accounts/payroll department and ask them to:
- Check Form 24Q (employer's quarterly TDS return) to see if they correctly mapped TDS to your PAN
- If incorrectly filed: File a TDS correction return (rectified Form 24Q)
- After correction: 26AS updates within 7-14 days

> You can claim TDS credit even if 26AS doesn't show it — but the IT department may raise a mismatch demand. Get the employer to fix it before filing ITR if possible.

### Case B: AIS Shows HIGHER Income Than You Declared

AIS shows ₹15 lakh income from "salary + interest" but you declared only ₹12 lakh.

**Most likely cause**: AIS includes income not fully taxable (HRA exemption, FD interest shown gross before TDS, SFT transactions).

**What to do**:
1. Open AIS at incometax.gov.in → "Annual Information Statement"
2. Check each transaction listed
3. Transactions that are wrong or non-taxable: Click **"Dispute"** in the AIS portal

**AIS Dispute Process**:
1. Login → AIS → click on the mismatched transaction
2. Select "Feedback" → "Information is incorrect" OR "Information relates to another person"
3. Add reason (e.g., "This FD interest is already included in salary figure" or "This is loan repayment, not income")
4. AIS feedback updates the view but the source (bank/employer) also needs to correct at their end

### Case C: TDS in 26AS But Not in AIS

26AS and AIS are different statements. AIS also shows 26AS TDS data but may display it under a different section. Cross-check both.

### Case D: 26AS and AIS Match But Not Your Records

If 26AS/AIS both show ₹50,000 TDS but your Form 16 shows ₹80,000, the error is on the employer's side — they haven't deposited or filed correctly.

## Step 2: How to Dispute AIS Transactions Online

1. Login to **[incometax.gov.in](https://incometax.gov.in/)** → "Services" → **"Annual Information Statement (AIS)"**
2. Select the Assessment Year
3. Click on the transaction you want to dispute
4. In the "Feedback" section, choose:
   - **"Information is correct"** (accept the transaction)
   - **"Information is not fully correct"** (partially correct, explain)
   - **"Information relates to other PAN/year"** (wrong PAN or year)
   - **"Information is duplicate/included in other information"** (already captured elsewhere)
   - **"Information is denied"** (transaction never happened)
5. Submit feedback — AIS updates your view immediately

**Note**: Disputing in AIS doesn't change what the source (employer/bank) reported. For the IT department's records to change, the source must file a correction. Your dispute is visible to the AO during processing.

## Step 3: File ITR With Correct Numbers

**Important rule**: When filing ITR, declare your **actual income** (per your books/Form 16), not the inflated AIS figure.

In the TDS credit section:
- Enter TDS from your **Form 16 / actual TDS certificates**
- If TDS is in 26AS: It auto-populates in ITR pre-fill
- If TDS NOT in 26AS (employer error): You can still manually enter the TDS — but mark it as per "TDS certificate"

**Risk**: If you claim TDS not reflected in 26AS, the IT department may raise a demand after processing (because their system doesn't see the matching credit). This is resolvable via rectification but causes extra paperwork.

## Step 4: What to Do If IT Department Raises a Demand Due to Mismatch

After filing ITR, if you receive a 143(1) demand due to TDS mismatch:

1. **Check if employer has corrected**: Go to TRACES (tracess.gov.in) → Taxpayer Login → "View 26AS" for the updated position
2. **File Rectification**: Login to incometax.gov.in → e-File → Income Tax Returns → Rectification → "Tax Credit Mismatch"
3. Provide the TDS certificate (Form 16 / 16A) as support
4. If employer has now deposited and updated TDS return, the credit will appear and your demand is resolved

## Checking 26AS vs AIS

| Statement | Where to Find | What It Shows |
|-----------|---------------|--------------|
| **Form 26AS** | incometax.gov.in → Services → View 26AS | TDS by quarter, self-assessment tax, advance tax |
| **AIS** | incometax.gov.in → Services → Annual Information Statement | Full financial transactions (SFT data, bank interest, mutual funds, property, dividends, salary, etc.) |

AIS is more comprehensive but may include transactions that are non-taxable or already captured elsewhere.

## Getting Employer to Fix TDS Return

If employer TDS is not reflecting in 26AS:
1. Email your employer's payroll/accounts team explaining the issue
2. Ask them to check if they filed **Form 24Q** (quarterly TDS return) with correct PAN and amounts
3. If correction needed: They file a **Revised Form 24Q** via TRACES (traces.gov.in) as a deductor
4. After revised return is processed (7-14 days): Your 26AS updates

**If employer is unresponsive**: File a grievance at incometax.gov.in mentioning employer TAN and asking for facilitation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My employer deducted TDS but it's not in 26AS. Should I still claim credit in ITR?

Yes — you are entitled to the TDS credit your employer deducted. Claim it in ITR using your Form 16. If 26AS doesn't show it, you may get a demand later, but you can respond with Form 16 as proof of TDS deduction. Pursue employer correction simultaneously.

### AIS shows ₹2 lakh FD interest but bank deducted only ₹20,000 TDS (10%). The TDS in 26AS is right. Why does AIS show full ₹2 lakh?

AIS shows the full interest (pre-TDS), while 26AS shows the TDS credit. Both are correct — AIS is informational; you declare ₹2 lakh as income and claim ₹20,000 TDS credit in ITR.

### I filed ITR and got a 143(1) demand for ₹8,000. It's exactly the TDS mismatch amount. What do I do?

File a **Rectification Request** under "Tax Credit Mismatch" with a scanned copy of your Form 16A (TDS certificate) showing the correct TDS. If the employer has corrected their TDS return, the system will reconcile and the demand should get resolved.
