# Rajasthan Police Constable 2026 — Complete Recruitment Guide

Rajasthan Police Constable recruitment is one of the most popular state police jobs in Rajasthan. With **10,000–15,000 vacancies** in major recruitment cycles, it attracts lakhs of applicants from across the state.

**12th pass (Intermediate) is the qualifying education** for the constable post.

Analogies, Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations, Direction Sense, Seating Arrangement, Number Series, Odd One Out, Syllogism, Venn Diagrams, Non-verbal Reasoning

Last 6 months — national + Rajasthan-specific events

Number System, Percentage, Ratio, Profit/Loss, SI/CI, Time-Work, Time-Distance, Mensuration, Statistics

### Hindi
Grammar, Comprehension, Vocabulary (Rajasthani influence in Hindi also asked)

## Rajasthan-Specific GK — Key Topics

This is often the **differentiating section** in Rajasthan Police exam:
- **Rajasthan History:** Mewar, Marwar, Jaipur kingdoms; major battles (Haldighati, Tarain)
- **Rajasthan Geography:** Thar Desert, Aravalli hills, rivers (Chambal, Banas, Luni)
- **Rajasthan Culture:** Folk music (Ghoomar, Kalbeliya), festivals (Pushkar, Nagaur Fair), handicrafts
- **Rajasthan Economy:** Agriculture (Bajra, Jowar, Mustard), tourism
- **Rajasthan Schemes:** CM schemes (Mukhyamantri Chiranjeevi, Jan Aadhaar, Indira Gandhi Urban Employment)
- **Government:** State CM, Governor, Rajasthan Police headquarters (Jaipur)

## Preparation Tips

1. **Rajasthan GK is the biggest differentiator** — invest 30–40 min/day in Rajasthan History and Culture from Day 1. Most UP/Bihar/Delhi pattern books miss Rajasthan-specific content
2. **Physical test is strict** — 5 km in 25 minutes (male) requires 5 min/km pace. Start running from Day 1
3. **Hindi section** — focus on Rajasthani-influenced Hindi; state-level question papers differ from SSC/UPSC Hindi
4. **Previous year papers** — available at [police.rajasthan.gov.in](https://police.rajasthan.gov.in); solve all old papers
5. **Reasoning** — scoring area, completely scoreable with practice (30 marks)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the age limit for Rajasthan Police Constable 2026?**
18–23 years for General category males. Female candidates and reserved categories get age relaxation up to 28 years. Check notification for exact age limits.

**Can candidates from other states apply for Rajasthan Police Constable?**
Yes — but reserved category benefits (lower fees, relaxed cutoffs) apply only to Rajasthan domicile/caste certificate holders. Other state OBC/SC/ST candidates are treated as General category.

**Is there a bond period after joining Rajasthan Police?**
Yes — typically 3–5 years mandatory service. Leaving before the bond period attracts penalty charges.

**How many vacancies are expected in Rajasthan Police Constable 2026?**
Based on historical patterns, 10,000–15,000 vacancies per cycle. The 2023–24 cycle had ~9,000 vacancies. The 2026 notification will confirm exact numbers.

**Is there any age relaxation for ex-servicemen in Rajasthan Police?**
Yes — ex-servicemen get significant age relaxation. Refer to the official notification for exact relaxation quantum.
