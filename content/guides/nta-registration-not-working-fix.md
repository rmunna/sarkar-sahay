# NTA Registration Not Working? Fix Exam Portal Errors

Facing errors while registering on the NTA exam portal? You are not alone. The National Testing Agency (NTA) portal at [exams.nta.ac.in](https://exams.nta.ac.in/) handles registrations for JEE Main, NEET UG, CUET, UGC NET, and other national exams. During peak registration periods, the portal often experiences technical issues. This guide covers the most common NTA registration errors and how to fix them.

## What is NTA Exam Portal?

The NTA (National Testing Agency) is an autonomous body established by the Ministry of Education, Government of India to conduct entrance examinations for higher educational institutions. The official portal [exams.nta.ac.in](https://exams.nta.ac.in/) is used for candidate registration, application form submission, admit card download, and result checking for exams like:

- **JEE Main** — Engineering entrance
- **NEET UG** — Medical entrance
- **CUET UG/PG** — Central university admissions
- **UGC NET** — Lectureship and JRF eligibility
- **CMAT, GPAT** — Management and pharmacy entrance

## Common NTA Registration Errors and Fixes

### 1. NTA Server Down or Website Not Loading

**Problem:** The portal shows "502 Bad Gateway", "Service Unavailable", or the page simply does not load.

**Why it happens:** During the first and last few days of registration, millions of candidates access the portal simultaneously, causing server overload.

**Fix:**
1. **Try during off-peak hours** — Access the portal between 11 PM and 7 AM or early morning for best results
2. **Clear your browser cache** — Press Ctrl + Shift + Delete, clear cache and cookies, then retry
3. **Use a different browser** — Try Chrome, Firefox, or Edge; avoid using mobile browsers if possible
4. **Check NTA social media** — Follow [@DG_NTA on X](https://x.com/DG_NTA) for official server status updates
5. **Use a wired connection** — Wi-Fi drops can cause timeouts; a stable connection helps
6. **Do not use VPN** — VPN can slow down or block access to government portals

### 2. Photo Upload Failed

**Problem:** The portal rejects your photograph or signature image with errors like "Invalid file format", "File size too large", or the upload button does not respond.

**Why it happens:** NTA has strict image specifications that must be followed exactly.

**NTA Photo Specifications:**
| Requirement | Photograph | Signature |
|---|---|---|
| Format | JPG/JPEG | JPG/JPEG |
| Size | 10 KB to 200 KB | 4 KB to 30 KB |
| Dimensions | 3.5 cm × 4.5 cm (min 132×170 px) | 3.5 cm × 1.5 cm (min 132×56 px) |
| Background | White | White paper |

**Fix:**
1. **Resize your image** — Use [imageresizer.com](https://imageresizer.com/) or Microsoft Paint to adjust dimensions and file size
2. **Convert to JPEG** — If your photo is PNG or HEIC, convert it to JPG format
3. **Use a recent passport-size photo** — The photo must be taken within the last 3 months
4. **Write your name and date on the photo** — Some exams require this; check the notification
5. **Disable browser extensions** — Ad blockers can interfere with file upload scripts
6. **Try incognito mode** — Open Chrome incognito (Ctrl + Shift + N) and try uploading again

### 3. Fee Payment Error

**Problem:** Payment fails, money is deducted but confirmation not received, or the payment page does not load.

**Fix:**
1. **Wait 24-48 hours** — If money was deducted but status shows "unpaid", wait for the bank to process; most failed transactions are auto-refunded within 7 working days
2. **Check payment status** — Log in to [exams.nta.ac.in](https://exams.nta.ac.in/) → Application Status → check fee payment status
3. **Do NOT pay again immediately** — Double payment creates complications; verify first
4. **Try a different payment method** — If UPI fails, try debit card or net banking, or vice versa
5. **Use SBI/Canara/ICICI net banking** — These banks have better integration with NTA payment gateway
6. **Contact your bank** — If amount was debited, get a transaction reference number and contact NTA helpdesk

### 4. OTP Not Coming

**Problem:** OTP is not received on registered mobile number or email during registration or login.

**Fix:**
1. **Wait at least 2-3 minutes** — OTPs can be delayed during peak hours
2. **Check your spam/junk folder** — Email OTPs sometimes land in spam
3. **Ensure correct mobile number** — Verify the number shown on screen matches yours
4. **Check DND status** — If your number has DND (Do Not Disturb) activated, OTPs may be blocked. Send "STOP DND" to 1909 to deactivate
5. **Try resend OTP** — Click "Resend OTP" after the timer expires (usually 30-60 seconds)
6. **Check network signal** — Low signal areas may delay SMS delivery; try moving to a location with better coverage
7. **Contact your telecom provider** — Some networks block bulk SMS; ask them to whitelist NTA messages

### 5. Form Auto-Logout or Session Expired

**Problem:** The form logs you out automatically while filling in details, or shows "Session Expired" error.

**Why it happens:** NTA portal sessions typically expire after 15-20 minutes of inactivity. Heavy server load can also cause premature session timeouts.

**Fix:**
1. **Keep all details ready before starting** — Prepare all information (personal details, educational qualifications, address) in a notepad before logging in
2. **Fill the form quickly** — Do not leave the form idle; complete each page and click "Save & Next" promptly
3. **Do not open multiple tabs** — Opening the portal in multiple browser tabs causes session conflicts
4. **Disable auto-fill extensions** — Some extensions interfere with form submission
5. **Save after each section** — Click "Save & Next" after completing each section rather than filling everything at once
6. **Take screenshots** — Before submitting, screenshot each page for your records

## How to Register on NTA Portal — Step-by-Step

1. Visit [exams.nta.ac.in](https://exams.nta.ac.in/) and click on the exam you want to register for
2. Click **"New Registration"** and enter your name, date of birth, mobile number, and email
3. Receive OTP on mobile and email — enter both to verify
4. Note down the **Application Number** and **Password** generated
5. Log in with your credentials and fill the **Application Form** — personal details, educational qualification, exam preferences
6. Upload **photograph and signature** as per specifications
7. Pay the **application fee** via UPI, debit card, credit card, or net banking
8. Download and print the **confirmation page** for your records

## FAQs

### Q1. NTA website is not opening. What should I do?
NTA servers face heavy traffic during registration deadlines. Try accessing during off-peak hours (late night or early morning). Clear browser cache, try a different browser, and ensure you are using the correct URL: [exams.nta.ac.in](https://exams.nta.ac.in/).

### Q2. I paid the fee but the status shows unpaid. Will I lose my money?
No. If the amount was debited from your account but not reflected on the portal, wait 24-48 hours. Most failed transactions are auto-refunded within 7 working days. If not resolved, contact NTA helpdesk with your transaction reference number.

### Q3. Can I edit my application after submitting?
Yes, but only during the correction window announced by NTA. You can correct details like personal information, exam city preferences, and photo/signature. Some changes may require additional fee payment.

### Q4. My photo keeps getting rejected. What should I do?
Ensure your photo is in JPG format, between 10-200 KB, with a white background. The photo must be recent (within 3 months). Use an online image resizer to adjust dimensions to at least 132×170 pixels.

### Q5. I did not receive OTP for registration. How do I proceed?
Wait 2-3 minutes and try "Resend OTP". Check if DND is active on your mobile — deactivate by sending "STOP DND" to 1909. If using email OTP, check the spam folder. If the issue persists, try during off-peak hours.

### Q6. Can I register using my mobile phone?
While NTA does not restrict mobile registration, it is strongly recommended to use a desktop or laptop. The portal's interface is optimized for larger screens, and photo uploads work more reliably on desktops.

### Q7. I forgot my NTA registration password. How to reset it?
Visit [exams.nta.ac.in](https://exams.nta.ac.in/), click "Forgot Password", and enter your Application Number, date of birth, and registered email/mobile. You will receive a reset link or OTP to create a new password.
