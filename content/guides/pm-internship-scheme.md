# PM Internship Scheme 2025: ₹5,000 Stipend, Eligibility, and How to Apply

The PM Internship Scheme is a Central Government initiative announced in the Union Budget 2024-25 to provide **internship opportunities to 1 crore youth** over 5 years with **India's top 500 companies**. Selected candidates receive a **monthly stipend of ₹5,000** and a **one-time grant of ₹6,000**. The scheme is managed by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA).

## What is PM Internship Scheme?

The PM Internship Scheme aims to skill India's youth by offering them real-world work experience in top companies across various sectors. The internship duration is **12 months**, and companies from diverse industries — IT, manufacturing, finance, healthcare, energy, and more — participate in the scheme. For the pilot phase (2024-25), **1.25 lakh internship positions** were offered.

---

## Who is Eligible?

- **Age:** 21 to 24 years
- **Education:** Passed 10th, 12th, ITI, diploma, or graduate (not currently enrolled in full-time education)
- **Indian citizen**
- **Family income:** Annual household income should not exceed ₹8 lakh
- **Not employed** in full-time job at the time of application
- Must have an [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)

### Who Cannot Apply?
- IIT, IIM, IISER, or NLU graduates/students
- Candidates already employed in organized sector
- Candidates from families where any member is a government employee
- Candidates with family income above ₹8 lakh per annum

---

## Stipend and Benefits

| Benefit | Amount |
|---------|--------|
| Monthly stipend | ₹5,000 (₹4,500 from government + ₹500 from company CSR) |
| One-time grant | ₹6,000 (for incidentals, paid upfront) |
| Insurance | Coverage under PM Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana and PM Suraksha Bima Yojana |
| Duration | 12 months |
| Certificate | Internship completion certificate from the company |

## How to Apply Online

1. **Visit the portal** — Go to [pminternship.mca.gov.in](https://pminternship.mca.gov.in/)
2. **Register** — Click on "Candidate Registration" and create account with Aadhaar, mobile number, and email
3. **Verify** — Complete OTP verification
4. **Login** — Use credentials to login
5. **Complete profile** — Fill personal details, educational qualifications, skills, and preferences
6. **Upload documents** — Upload educational certificates, Aadhaar, photograph
7. **Browse internships** — Search available internship opportunities by location, sector, and company
8. **Apply** — Select up to 5 internship preferences and apply
9. **Company shortlisting** — Companies review applications and shortlist candidates
10. **Selection** — Selected candidates receive offer notification on the portal and via SMS/email
11. **Accept and join** — Accept the offer and join the company on the specified date

---

## Selection Process

1. **Application screening** — based on eligibility criteria
2. **AI-based matching** — candidates matched to companies based on skills, location, and preferences
3. **Company shortlisting** — companies select from matched candidates
4. **Final selection** — confirmed via the portal
5. **Priority to** — SC/ST/OBC/PwD candidates, women, and candidates from underrepresented districts

---

## Processing Time

- **Registration to application:** Immediate
- **Company shortlisting:** 2-4 weeks after application window closes
- **Selection notification:** 4-6 weeks
- **Joining:** As per company schedule

---

## Important Tips

1. **Complete your profile fully** — Companies shortlist based on your profile. Fill all sections including skills, interests, and location preferences
2. **Apply to multiple internships** — You can select up to 5 preferences. Apply to diverse options to increase your chances
3. **Check regularly** — Login to the portal regularly to check for new internship postings and selection updates
4. **Prepare for the role** — Once selected, research the company and prepare for the internship to make the best of the opportunity
5. **Keep Aadhaar-bank linked** — Stipend is paid via DBT to your Aadhaar-linked bank account

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. How much stipend do PM interns receive?
₹5,000 per month (₹4,500 from government + ₹500 from company) plus a one-time ₹6,000 grant.

### Q2. Is the PM Internship Scheme only for graduates?
No, candidates who have passed 10th, 12th, ITI, or diploma are also eligible. You do not need to be a graduate.

### Q3. Can final-year college students apply?
No, you should not be currently enrolled in full-time education at the time of application.

### Q4. Which companies participate in the scheme?
India's top 500 companies by CSR expenditure participate, including companies from IT, manufacturing, banking, energy, healthcare, FMCG, and other sectors.

### Q5. Is there a guarantee of job after the internship?
No, the scheme provides a 12-month internship experience. There is no guarantee of a permanent job, but companies may offer employment based on performance.

### Q6. Can I apply if I'm from a rural area?
Yes, the scheme is open to all eligible Indian youth. Priority is given to candidates from underrepresented districts and rural areas.

### Q7. Is there an application fee?
No, registration and application on the PM Internship portal is completely free.
