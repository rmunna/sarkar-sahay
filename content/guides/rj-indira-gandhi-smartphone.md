## What is Indira Gandhi Free Smartphone Yojana?

Indira Gandhi Free Smartphone Yojana (IGSY) is a scheme by the Government of Rajasthan that provides **free smartphones with internet connectivity** to women heads of families (Chiranjeevi families) and girl students. The scheme aims to bridge the digital divide and empower women through digital access.

### Benefits

- **Free smartphone** (touchscreen, 4G enabled)
- **3 years of free internet data** and calling
- **Digital literacy** — access to government services, banking, education
- Phone distributed through organized **camps** across Rajasthan

## Who is Eligible?

### Phase-wise Priority Groups
- **Women heads of Chiranjeevi families** (registered under Chiranjeevi health scheme)
- **Girl students** studying in government schools/colleges (Class 9 to college/ITI/polytechnic)
- **Widows** and **single women** receiving pension
- **MGNREGA workers** (100 days completion)
- **Urban Employment Guarantee** beneficiaries

### Common Conditions
- Must be a **resident of Rajasthan**
- Must have **Jan Aadhaar** registration
- Must be the **female head** of the family (or eligible girl student)
- Only **one smartphone per family** (for Chiranjeevi families)

## Documents Required

- **Jan Aadhaar card** (mandatory)
- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
- Chiranjeevi registration proof (for family head category)
- School/college ID and enrollment certificate (for girl students)
- Pension PPO number (for widows/single women)
- MGNREGA job card (for MGNREGA workers)

> **Important:** You will receive an **SMS invitation** if you are on the eligible list. Carry all documents to the camp.

## How to Get the Free Smartphone

### Camp-Based Distribution

1. **Check eligibility** on [igsy.rajasthan.gov.in](https://igsy.rajasthan.gov.in) using Jan Aadhaar number
2. **Receive SMS** — Eligible beneficiaries get an SMS with camp date and location
3. **Visit the camp** on the scheduled date with all documents
4. **Biometric verification** — Aadhaar-based fingerprint/iris scan at the camp
5. **Receive smartphone** — Phone and SIM with data plan activated on the spot
6. **Basic training** provided at the camp on how to use the phone

### Checking Eligibility Online

1. Visit [igsy.rajasthan.gov.in](https://igsy.rajasthan.gov.in)
2. Enter Jan Aadhaar number or Aadhaar number
3. Check if your name appears in the eligible list
4. If eligible, note the camp schedule for your district

## Processing Time

- Eligibility check: **Instant** (online)
- SMS invitation: Based on district-wise camp schedule
- Smartphone distribution: **At the camp** (same day)

## Fees

**Completely free.** No charges for the smartphone, SIM, or data plan. Do not pay anyone claiming to process the application.

## Important Tips

1. **Check eligibility first** — Visit the portal to confirm before going to any camp
2. **Wait for SMS** — Don't go to camps without receiving an official SMS invitation
3. **Beware of fraud** — No payment is required; don't pay middlemen or agents
4. **Carry originals** — Bring original Jan Aadhaar and Aadhaar for verification
5. **Register for Chiranjeevi** — If not registered, first register under [Chiranjeevi scheme](/guide/rj-chiranjeevi-health) to become eligible

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Can men apply for the free smartphone?
No. The scheme is specifically for **women family heads and girl students**.

### Q2. Is Jan Aadhaar mandatory?
Yes. **Jan Aadhaar** is the primary identity document for this scheme.

### Q3. What if I didn't receive an SMS?
Check your eligibility on the portal. If eligible but no SMS, contact the district collector office or call the helpline.

### Q4. Can I choose the smartphone brand?
No. The government procures smartphones centrally, and a **standard model** is distributed to all.

### Q5. What happens after 3 years of free data?
After the free data period ends, you will need to **recharge the SIM yourself** to continue services.

### Q6. Can girl students from private schools apply?
The scheme primarily targets girl students in **government institutions**. Check the latest notification for any expansion to private schools.
