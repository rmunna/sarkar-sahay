# IRCTC Website Down or Slow? Quick Fix for Server Errors

IRCTC's website is one of the busiest in India, handling over 20 lakh bookings daily. It frequently slows down, throws 503 errors, or stops loading entirely — especially during Tatkal booking hours. Here's how to fix these issues and book your tickets.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent platform and is not affiliated with Indian Railways or IRCTC.

## Common IRCTC Website Errors

| Error | What It Means |
|-------|--------------|
| 503 Service Unavailable | Server overloaded — too many users |
| 502 Bad Gateway | Server communication failure |
| Connection Timed Out | Server not responding |
| Blank white page | Page failed to load completely |
| "Service temporarily unavailable" | Scheduled or unscheduled maintenance |
| Captcha not loading | CDN or browser cache issue |

## Quick Fixes When IRCTC Website Is Down

### Fix 1: Hard Refresh the Page

- **Windows:** Press `Ctrl + Shift + R` or `Ctrl + F5`
- **Mac:** Press `Cmd + Shift + R`
- **Mobile:** Pull down to refresh or clear browser tab and reopen

This forces your browser to reload the page from the server instead of cached version.

### Fix 2: Clear Browser Cache

Old cached files can conflict with updated IRCTC pages.

**Chrome:**
1. Press `Ctrl + Shift + Delete`
2. Select **"All time"** from the dropdown
3. Check **Cached images and files** and **Cookies**
4. Click **Clear data**
5. Go to [irctc.co.in](https://www.irctc.co.in) again

### Fix 3: Try a Different Browser

If IRCTC doesn't load in Chrome, try:
- **Firefox** — often works when Chrome doesn't
- **Microsoft Edge** — Chromium-based but different caching
- **Brave Browser** — disable Shields for irctc.co.in
- **Incognito/Private mode** — opens without cache or extensions

### Fix 4: Switch Your Internet Connection

1. If on WiFi, switch to **mobile data**
2. If on mobile data, switch to a **different SIM** or WiFi
3. Some ISPs have routing issues with IRCTC servers
4. Try a **different DNS** — change to Google DNS (8.8.8.8) or Cloudflare (1.1.1.1)

### Fix 5: Disable Browser Extensions

Ad blockers and privacy extensions can break IRCTC:
1. Open Chrome → click three dots → **Extensions**
2. Disable **all extensions** temporarily
3. Reload irctc.co.in
4. If it works, enable extensions one by one to find the culprit

## How to Book During IRCTC Server Overload

When IRCTC is down during Tatkal hours, try these strategies:

1. **Pre-fill everything:** Login 5 minutes early, add passengers to your master list, keep payment ready
2. **Use multiple devices:** Open IRCTC on phone browser + laptop simultaneously
3. **Use IRCTC app as backup:** Sometimes the app works when the website doesn't (and vice versa)
4. **Try the direct booking URL:** Go straight to `https://www.irctc.co.in/nget/train-search` instead of the homepage
5. **Keep refreshing:** During server overload, persistent refreshing (every 5-10 seconds) eventually gets through
6. **Use UPI for payment:** UPI transactions process faster than net banking during peak load

## Is IRCTC Down Right Now?

To check if IRCTC is actually down:

1. **Try the website:** Visit [irctc.co.in](https://www.irctc.co.in)
2. **Check Twitter:** Search "#IRCTCDown" — other users will be reporting
3. **Try alternative URL:** Use `https://www.irctc.co.in/nget/train-search`
4. **Use Down Detector:** Search "IRCTC down detector" on Google
5. **Check IRCTC Twitter:** [@IRCTCofficial](https://twitter.com/IRCTCofficial) posts maintenance notices

## When IRCTC Is Under Maintenance

IRCTC performs scheduled maintenance regularly:

- **Daily:** Brief maintenance window between 11:30 PM - 12:30 AM
- **Monthly:** Occasional extended maintenance (announced on the website)
- **Unscheduled:** Server issues during festivals, holidays, and special trains announcement

During maintenance, you'll see a message: "Service temporarily unavailable. Please try after some time."

## Alternative Ways to Book When Website Is Down

If you urgently need to book and IRCTC won't load:

1. **IRCTC Rail Connect App** — try the mobile app
2. **Authorized agents** — IRCTC authorized travel agents can book for you
3. **Railway counter** — Visit your nearest PRS (Passenger Reservation System) counter
4. **139 helpline** — Call 139 for enquiry and limited booking services
5. **Third-party platforms** — Paytm, MakeMyTrip, and Cleartrip connect to IRCTC

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why does IRCTC website show 503 error?
The 503 error means IRCTC servers are overloaded with too many simultaneous users. This is common during Tatkal booking hours (10-11 AM). Wait a few minutes and try again, or use the mobile app.

### How long does IRCTC maintenance last?
Daily maintenance usually lasts 30-60 minutes (11:30 PM to 12:30 AM). Special maintenance can last 2-4 hours and is announced in advance on the IRCTC website and social media.

### Why is IRCTC website so slow?
IRCTC handles millions of users daily on limited server capacity. The website is slowest during Tatkal hours and festival seasons. Use off-peak hours for a smoother experience.

### Can I book IRCTC tickets if the website is down?
Yes. Use the IRCTC Rail Connect app, visit a railway counter, call 139, or book through authorized agents like Paytm or MakeMyTrip.

### Why does IRCTC website not load on my phone?
Try clearing your browser cache, switching from WiFi to mobile data, using a different browser, or disabling ad blockers. Also ensure your browser is updated.

### Is IRCTC website safe to use?
Yes, irctc.co.in is the official website of Indian Railway Catering and Tourism Corporation. Always ensure the URL shows "https://" and avoid fake IRCTC websites.

### What time does IRCTC website work fastest?
IRCTC works fastest between 6-8 AM and after 10 PM when user traffic is lowest. Avoid 10-11:30 AM (Tatkal hours) for the best experience.

### Why does IRCTC logout automatically?
IRCTC logs you out after 5 minutes of inactivity for security. Keep moving your mouse or clicking on the page to stay logged in, especially during Tatkal booking.
