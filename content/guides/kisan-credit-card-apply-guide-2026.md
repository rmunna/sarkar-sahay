# Kisan Credit Card 2026 — Complete Apply & Benefits Guide

**Kisan Credit Card (KCC)** is a subsidized credit scheme by the Government of India for farmers. It provides short-term credit for crops and farm-related expenses at a highly subsidized interest rate — as low as **4% per annum** (with timely repayment interest subvention).

---

## KCC — Key Benefits

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Interest rate** | 7% per annum (base); with subvention: effective **4%** if repaid on time |
| **Loan limit** | Based on crop + ancillary needs (typically ₹50,000–₹3 lakh for small farmers) |
| **Repayment** | Revolving credit — repay after harvest, redraw before next season |
| **Validity** | 5 years (renewed) |
| **Collateral** | Not required for loans up to ₹1.6 lakh |
| **Insurance** | Crop insurance under PMFBY + accidental death cover |

---

## Who Is Eligible for KCC?

| Eligibility | Details |
|------------|---------|
| **Farmers** | Individual farmers (own land or tenant) |
| **Tenant farmers** | Who cultivate land of others on lease/share |
| **Self-help groups** | Farmer SHGs/JLGs can apply jointly |
| **Allied activities** | Animal husbandry, fisheries farmers |
| **Age** | 18–75 years (above 60 needs co-applicant) |

**Not eligible:** Non-farmers, those with existing agricultural loan default, those without land records or cultivation evidence.

---

## KCC Loan Limit — How Is It Calculated?

**Basic formula:**
1. **Scale of Finance** (per acre, per crop — set by District Level Technical Committee annually)
2. KCC Limit = Scale of Finance × Area under cultivation
3. Add 10% for crop insurance + 20% for post-harvest expenses
4. Add repair/maintenance needs + allied activities

**Example:**
- Scale of Finance: ₹30,000/acre for paddy
- Land: 3 acres
- Basic limit: ₹30,000 × 3 = ₹90,000
- Add 30% overhead = ₹1,17,000
- **KCC limit: ~₹1.2 lakh**

---

## KCC Interest Subvention — How to Get 4% Rate

| Repayment | Effective Rate |
|-----------|--------------|
| Repaid within 1 year (prompt repayment) | **4% per annum** (7% - 3% subvention) |
| Repaid after 1 year (delayed) | **7% per annum** (subvention lost) |
| Defaulter | Penal interest applied |

**Key:** Always repay within the due date (after harvest) to get the 4% rate. Delayed repayment even by 1 day loses subvention.

## How to Apply for KCC

### Online (PM Kisan linked):

1. Go to **[pmkisan.gov.in](https://www.pmkisan.gov.in)**
2. Click **"Farmer Corner"** → **"KCC Form"**
3. Download KCC application form
4. Fill + submit at your nearest bank branch with documents

### At Bank Branch:

Most nationalized banks and RRBs (Regional Rural Banks) issue KCC:

1. Visit nearest SBI, PNB, Canara Bank, BOB, or Regional Rural Bank branch
2. Request **"Kisan Credit Card Application Form"**
3. Submit form with documents
4. Bank official may visit farm to assess land/crop
5. **Processing: 14 days** (RBI mandate — KCC must be issued within 2 weeks of application)

## SBI Kisan Credit Card — Specific Process

SBI is India's largest KCC issuer:

1. Visit nearest **SBI Agricultural Branch** (or any SBI branch)
2. Submit KCC application + documents
3. SBI field officer visits within 3–5 days
4. **SBI YONO Krishi app**: Check KCC application status online
5. KCC card + passbook issued within 14 working days

**SBI KCC hotline:** Toll-free 1800-1234 (SBI Agri services)

---

## KCC + PM Kisan Linkage

Government mandates KCC linkage for PM Kisan beneficiaries:

1. PM Kisan beneficiaries get priority for KCC issuance
2. ₹4 installment (₹2,000 each) from PM Kisan credited to linked bank
3. Bank uses PM Kisan credit history for KCC assessment
4. **Special drive:** Banks hold KCC camps at Gram Panchayat level periodically — apply there for fastest processing

## KCC for Animal Husbandry & Fisheries

KCC extended to non-crop farming:

| Activity | KCC Limit |
|----------|----------|
| Dairy/poultry | Based on animal units |
| Fisheries | Based on water area + equipment |
| Allied farm activities | As per assessment |

Interest subvention (4% effective) applies to these as well.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**I'm a tenant farmer with no land documents — can I get KCC?**
Yes — tenant farmers are eligible. Documents needed: Lease agreement / NOC from landowner + crop sowing declaration. Some banks also accept Patta/Chitta (regional land documents) + village officer certification.

**KCC application rejected by bank — what to do?**
If bank rejects without valid reason: (1) Write formal appeal to Branch Manager, (2) Approach Lead District Manager (LDM) — every district has one, (3) Contact District Collector's office → they have authority over KCC implementation, (4) Call NABARD helpline: 1800-200-7400.

**Can I use KCC for buying a tractor or irrigation equipment?**
KCC is primarily for short-term crop credit + maintenance. Long-term investment (tractor, irrigation) needs separate agricultural term loan (Kisan Vikas Patra, farm mechanization loan). Some banks bundle a small term component with KCC — ask your branch.

**KCC interest rate vs gold loan for farming — which is better?**
KCC at 4% (subsidized) is far better than gold loan (8–12%). Always exhaust KCC limit first. Gold loan is backup for emergencies when KCC limit is insufficient or for non-farming expenses.

**How do I check my KCC balance?**
KCC is typically a savings account with KCC facility. Check balance via: Bank passbook, bank ATM, or YONO/mobile banking. Limit utilization + available credit is shown in the bank statement.
