## What is Gold Hallmarking?

Gold hallmarking is a **purity certification system** managed by the **Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS)**. When gold jewellery is hallmarked, it means the item has been tested at a BIS-recognised Assaying and Hallmarking Centre (AHC) and certified to contain the exact purity of gold as claimed by the jeweller.

In simple terms, a hallmark on your gold jewellery is a **government guarantee of gold purity**. It protects consumers from being cheated with impure or adulterated gold.

The Government of India made **hallmarking mandatory from 23 June 2021** under the Bureau of Indian Standards (Hallmarking of Gold Jewellery and Gold Artefacts) Order, 2020. From **1 April 2023**, only HUID-based hallmarking is accepted — the old hallmarking system without HUID is no longer valid for new jewellery.

## Why Gold Hallmarking is Important

- **Purity guarantee** — Ensures you get exactly the karat (22K, 18K, or 14K) you pay for
- **Consumer protection** — If hallmarked gold is found impure, you get **2x compensation** for the purity shortfall plus testing charges
- **Prevents fraud** — Eliminates the risk of buying adulterated or under-karat gold
- **Resale value** — Hallmarked jewellery fetches better resale prices as purity is certified
- **Traceability** — Every hallmarked item has a unique HUID that links it to the jeweller and AHC
- **Lifetime validity** — The hallmark remains valid for the entire lifetime of the jewellery

## Hallmark Symbols Explained

Since the HUID-based hallmarking system (mandatory from April 2023), every hallmarked gold item carries **three marks**:

### 1. BIS Logo
The official **BIS triangle logo** confirms the item has been certified by the Bureau of Indian Standards.

### 2. Purity Grade (Fineness Number)
This indicates the gold purity in parts per thousand:

| Karat | Fineness | Gold Purity |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| 24K   | 999      | 99.9%       |
| 23K   | 958      | 95.8%       |
| 22K   | 916      | 91.6%       |
| 21K   | 875      | 87.5%       |
| 20K   | 833      | 83.3%       |
| 18K   | 750      | 75.0%       |
| 14K   | 585      | 58.5%       |

> **Most common:** 22K (916) is the standard for Indian gold jewellery. 18K (750) is used for diamond-studded and designer pieces. 14K (585) is common in lightweight daily-wear jewellery.

### 3. HUID (Hallmark Unique Identification Number)
A **6-character alphanumeric code** uniquely assigned to each piece of jewellery. This is the most important mark — it links the jewellery to the jeweller, AHC, and purity details in the BIS database.

> **Note:** The old hallmarking system (before 2023) had 4 marks — BIS logo, purity, AHC mark, and jeweller's identification mark. The new system replaced the last two with a single HUID.

## What is HUID?

**HUID (Hallmark Unique Identification Number)** is a 6-character alphanumeric code stamped on every hallmarked gold article. It was introduced to make hallmarking **tamper-proof and digitally traceable**.

Each HUID is unique and stored in the **BIS database**. Using this code, anyone can verify:

- **Gold purity** (karat and fineness)
- **Jeweller's registration number**
- **Assaying and Hallmarking Centre** that tested the item
- **Date of hallmarking**

HUID-based hallmarking became mandatory for all gold jewellery sold in India from **1 April 2023**.

## How to Verify Gold Hallmark Online

You can verify the authenticity of any hallmarked gold jewellery using the HUID in two ways:

### Method 1: Using BIS Care App

The **BIS Care app** is the official mobile application by the Bureau of Indian Standards.

**Steps to verify:**

1. **Download** the BIS Care app from [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.verifybis) or Apple App Store
2. **Open** the app and tap on **"Verify HUID"**
3. **Enter the 6-character HUID** printed on your jewellery
4. **Tap Search** — the app will display complete details including purity, jeweller info, and AHC details
5. **Cross-check** the displayed purity with what's stamped on the jewellery

> **Tip:** You can also use the app to scan the HUID if your jewellery has a QR code.

### Method 2: Using BIS Website

1. Visit the official BIS website: [www.bis.gov.in](https://www.bis.gov.in)
2. Navigate to **Hallmarking** section
3. Use the **"Verify HUID"** option
4. Enter the 6-character HUID code
5. View the complete hallmark details

### What If Verification Fails?

If the HUID does not show any results or shows different purity than claimed:
- The jewellery may be **fake or tampered**
- **Do not buy** the item or **file a complaint** with BIS immediately
- Get the jewellery tested at a BIS-recognised AHC independently

## How to Read Hallmark on Gold Jewellery

When examining gold jewellery, look for these marks (usually stamped on the inner surface):

1. **BIS Logo** — A small triangular mark with "BIS" inside
2. **Fineness number** — e.g., 916 (for 22K), 750 (for 18K), or 585 (for 14K)
3. **HUID code** — A 6-character alphanumeric code (e.g., A1B2C3)

**Where to look:**
- **Rings** — Inner band
- **Chains/Necklaces** — On the clasp or near the hook
- **Bangles** — Inner surface
- **Earrings** — On the post or back

> **Use a magnifying glass** — Hallmark impressions are very small and may be difficult to read with the naked eye.

## Mandatory Hallmarking Rules in India

Key rules you should know:

- **Mandatory since 23 June 2021** — All gold jewellery sold by registered jewellers must be hallmarked
- **HUID mandatory from 1 April 2023** — Only HUID-based hallmarking is valid for new jewellery
- **Applicable karats** — 14K, 18K, 20K, 21K, 22K, 23K, and 24K gold
- **Jeweller registration** — All jewellers must register with BIS to sell hallmarked gold
- **Exemptions** — Jewellery for export, items below 2 grams, kundan/polki/jadau work, and watches are exempt
- **Penalty for violations** — Selling non-hallmarked gold attracts penalties including fine up to ₹1 lakh and imprisonment up to 1 year under the BIS Act, 2016
- **Old jewellery** — Consumers can freely sell their old un-hallmarked jewellery to jewellers
- **Phased implementation** — The mandate was rolled out in phases across districts

## How to File a Complaint for Fake Hallmark

If you suspect your hallmarked gold jewellery is impure or carries a fake hallmark:

### Online Complaint

1. **BIS Care App** — Open the app → Go to **Complaints** section → Fill the form with HUID, purchase details, and your complaint
2. **BIS Website** — Visit [www.bis.gov.in](https://www.bis.gov.in) → Navigate to **Complaint Registration** portal
3. **Email** — Send complaint to **complaints@bis.gov.in** with full details and purchase receipt

### Offline Complaint

- Visit or write to the **Public Grievance Officer** at the nearest BIS Regional or Branch Office

### Compensation

Under **Rule 49 of BIS Rules, 2018**, if hallmarked gold is found to have lower purity than declared, you are entitled to:

- **2x the value difference** based on the shortage in purity for the weight of the article
- **Testing charges** reimbursement

## Important Tips for Buying Gold Jewellery

1. **Always ask for hallmarked jewellery** — Don't buy gold without the BIS hallmark
2. **Verify HUID immediately** — Use the BIS Care app at the shop itself before paying
3. **Check the bill** — Ensure the HUID number is mentioned on the purchase invoice
4. **Buy from BIS-registered jewellers** — Check jeweller registration on the BIS website
5. **Keep your invoice safe** — Required for complaints and resale
6. **Know your karat** — 22K (916) is standard for jewellery; 24K (999) is for coins and bars
7. **Compare making charges** — Hallmarking standardises purity, so compare making charges and rates across shops
8. **Test old jewellery** — Get un-hallmarked old jewellery tested at any BIS-recognised AHC (charges apply)
9. **Don't rely on colour** — Gold colour can be deceptive; only hallmark and HUID verify purity
10. **Check for all 3 marks** — BIS logo, fineness number, and HUID must all be present

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is hallmarking mandatory for all gold jewellery in India?
Yes, since 23 June 2021, hallmarking is mandatory for gold jewellery sold by registered jewellers in notified districts. From 1 April 2023, only HUID-based hallmarking is accepted for new jewellery.

### What is HUID and where do I find it on my jewellery?
HUID (Hallmark Unique Identification Number) is a 6-character alphanumeric code stamped on hallmarked gold jewellery. It is usually found on the inner surface — inside rings, on clasps of chains, or inner side of bangles.

### How can I verify if my gold jewellery is genuinely hallmarked?
Download the **BIS Care app** and use the "Verify HUID" feature. Enter the 6-character HUID code from your jewellery. The app will show purity details, jeweller information, and AHC details. If no results appear, the hallmark may be fake.

### Is the hallmark valid forever?
Yes, the BIS hallmark is valid for the **entire lifetime** of the jewellery. You do not need to get it re-hallmarked.

### Can I sell old un-hallmarked gold jewellery?
Yes, consumers can freely sell their old un-hallmarked or hallmarked gold jewellery to jewellers. The mandatory hallmarking rule applies only to the **sale of new jewellery by jewellers**.

### What compensation do I get if hallmarked gold is found impure?
Under BIS Rules 2018, you get **2 times the value difference** calculated on the basis of purity shortage for the weight of the article sold, plus testing charges.

### Which types of gold jewellery are exempt from mandatory hallmarking?
Jewellery meant for export, items weighing less than 2 grams, kundan, polki and jadau jewellery, watches, and fountain pens are exempt from mandatory hallmarking.

### How much does hallmark testing cost?
The hallmarking charges are typically **₹45 per article** (may vary slightly by AHC). The cost is usually included in the jeweller's making charges.
