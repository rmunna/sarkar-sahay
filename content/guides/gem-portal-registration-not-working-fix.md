# GeM Portal Registration Not Working — How to Fix

The Government e-Marketplace (GeM) is India's official public procurement portal where government buyers and sellers transact. Registration errors are extremely common — PAN verification failures, GST mismatches, Aadhaar OTP issues, and category selection problems stop thousands of users daily. This guide covers every registration error and its fix.

## Common Registration Error Messages

You may encounter these errors during GeM registration:

- **"PAN verification failed"** — PAN details don't match Income Tax records
- **"GSTIN details could not be verified"** — GST number mismatch or inactive GSTIN
- **"OTP could not be sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile"** — Aadhaar mobile not updated
- **"Organization type mismatch"** — wrong entity type selected during signup
- **"Category selection error"** — product/service category not available or incorrectly chosen
- **"Email already registered"** — duplicate account exists with same email
- **"Registration request pending approval"** — stuck in verification queue

## Fix 1: PAN Verification Failed

PAN verification is the first step in GeM seller registration. If it fails:

1. **Check PAN status on Income Tax portal** — Visit [incometax.gov.in](https://www.incometax.gov.in/) and verify your PAN is active
2. **Match name exactly** — The name on GeM must match your PAN card letter-by-letter (including middle name, initials)
3. **Date of birth must match** — For individuals, DOB must match PAN records exactly
4. **Company PAN vs Personal PAN** — Sellers registering as a firm must use the firm's PAN (not personal PAN)
5. **PAN-Aadhaar linking** — Ensure your PAN is linked to Aadhaar; unlinked PANs may cause verification failures. See our [PAN-Aadhaar linking guide](/guide/pan-aadhaar-link-online) for help

If PAN is correct but still failing, wait 2-3 hours and retry — the NSDL/UTIITSL verification API may be temporarily down.

## Fix 2: GST Mismatch During Registration

GeM validates your GSTIN against the GST portal. Common causes of mismatch:

1. **GSTIN must be active** — Log into [gst.gov.in](https://www.gst.gov.in/) and check your GSTIN status is "Active"
2. **Business name must match** — The trade name or legal name on GST must match what you entered on GeM
3. **PAN embedded in GSTIN** — Characters 3-12 of your GSTIN are your PAN; ensure both match
4. **State code alignment** — Your GSTIN's first two digits indicate the state; this must match your registered address
5. **Multiple GSTINs** — If you have GSTINs in multiple states, register with the primary one first
6. **Composition scheme sellers** — Composition dealers can register but face limitations on certain categories

**Pro tip:** Copy your exact legal name from the GST portal and paste it into GeM registration to avoid spacing/spelling mismatches.

## Fix 3: Aadhaar OTP Not Received

GeM uses Aadhaar-based OTP verification for identity confirmation:

1. **Check Aadhaar-linked mobile** — The OTP goes to the mobile number registered with UIDAI, not the one you enter on GeM
2. **Update mobile in Aadhaar** — If your Aadhaar mobile is outdated, visit the nearest Aadhaar centre to update it. See our [Aadhaar mobile update guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
3. **Wait and retry** — UIDAI OTP servers can be slow; wait 2-3 minutes before requesting again
4. **Don't request multiple OTPs** — Rapid OTP requests may temporarily block your number; wait 10 minutes
5. **Check SMS blockers** — DND services or spam filters may block UIDAI messages; check your filtered/spam folder
6. **Try during off-peak hours** — UIDAI servers are less loaded between 7-9 AM and after 8 PM

## Fix 4: Organization Type Selection Error

Selecting the wrong organization type causes registration rejection:

| Organization Type | Who Should Select |
|---|---|
| Proprietorship | Single-owner businesses with GST |
| Partnership | Partnership firms registered under Partnership Act |
| LLP | Limited Liability Partnerships registered with MCA |
| Private Limited | Companies registered under Companies Act |
| Public Limited | Listed or unlisted public companies |
| Society/Trust | Registered societies and trusts |
| Government | Central/State government departments |
| PSU | Public Sector Undertakings |

**How to fix if wrong type selected:**
- You cannot change organization type after submission
- Create a new registration with the correct type using a different email
- Contact GeM support at 1800-419-3436 (toll-free) to deactivate the incorrect account

## Fix 5: Category Selection Problems

Sellers must select the correct product/service categories during registration:

1. **Search by keyword** — Don't browse the full category tree; use the search box to find your product
2. **Multiple categories allowed** — You can add more categories after registration from your seller dashboard
3. **Service categories** — If you provide services (not products), select from the "Services" tab, not "Products"
4. **Category not found** — Some niche categories require brand/OEM approval first; check the GeM category list at [gem.gov.in](https://gem.gov.in/)
5. **Minimum one category required** — You must select at least one category to complete registration

## Fix 6: Email Already Registered

If you see "Email already registered":

1. **Check if you have an existing GeM account** — Try logging in or use "Forgot Password"
2. **Different email for different roles** — Buyer and seller accounts need separate email addresses
3. **Organization already registered** — Someone else from your organization may have already registered; check with your admin
4. **Use a fresh email** — Register with a new email address if the old account is inaccessible

## Step-by-Step: Fresh GeM Seller Registration

1. Visit [gem.gov.in](https://gem.gov.in/) and click **"Sign Up"** → **"Seller"**
2. Enter your **PAN number** and click Verify
3. Enter **GSTIN** and click Verify — business details auto-populate
4. Verify your identity via **Aadhaar OTP**
5. Enter **contact details** — email, mobile, address
6. Select **organization type** from the dropdown
7. Choose at least one **product/service category**
8. Upload required documents — PAN card copy, GST certificate, cancelled cheque
9. Set your **password** and submit
10. Wait for **verification** — typically 1-3 working days

## Step-by-Step: Fresh GeM Buyer Registration

1. Visit [gem.gov.in](https://gem.gov.in/) and click **"Sign Up"** → **"Buyer"**
2. Select your **organization type** (Central/State Government, PSU, etc.)
3. Enter **official email ID** (must be government domain like @gov.in or @nic.in)
4. Complete **Aadhaar verification** via OTP
5. Enter **designation and department** details
6. Submit for **admin approval** from your department's GeM nodal officer
7. Approval typically takes **3-5 working days**

## Documents Required for Registration

### For Sellers:
- PAN card (individual or company)
- GST registration certificate
- Cancelled cheque or bank statement (for payment setup)
- Aadhaar card (for OTP verification)
- Company incorporation certificate (for LLP/Pvt Ltd/Public Ltd)
- MSME/Udyam registration (optional, for benefits)

### For Buyers:
- Government ID card
- Aadhaar card
- Official email ID (@gov.in/@nic.in)
- Authorization letter from department head

## Important Tips

1. **Use Chrome or Firefox** — GeM portal works best on updated Chrome or Firefox browsers; avoid Internet Explorer
2. **Disable popup blockers** — Some verification steps open new windows
3. **Keep documents ready in PDF** — Maximum file size is usually 5 MB per document
4. **Register during business hours** — API verifications (PAN, GST, Aadhaar) are more reliable 9 AM - 6 PM on weekdays
5. **Save application ID** — Note your registration reference number for tracking

## FAQs

### Can I register on GeM as both buyer and seller?
Yes, but you need separate accounts with different email addresses. The same email cannot be used for both buyer and seller registration.

### How long does GeM registration approval take?
Seller registration typically takes 1-3 working days. Buyer registration requires department admin approval and may take 3-5 working days.

### My GSTIN is new — can I register on GeM immediately?
Yes, but ensure your GSTIN shows as "Active" on the GST portal. Newly issued GSTINs may take 24-48 hours to reflect in GeM's verification system.

### I'm a startup — do I need GST registration for GeM?
Yes, GST registration is mandatory for all sellers on GeM. There is no exemption for startups, though Startup India-registered entities get additional benefits on the platform.

### Can I change my organization type after registration?
No, you cannot change the organization type once submitted. You'll need to create a new account with the correct details and contact GeM support to deactivate the old one.

### What if my PAN is under correction at NSDL?
Wait until the PAN correction is processed and reflected in Income Tax records before attempting GeM registration. This usually takes 7-15 working days.

### Is Aadhaar mandatory for GeM registration?
Aadhaar-based verification is required for identity confirmation during registration. You cannot skip this step.
