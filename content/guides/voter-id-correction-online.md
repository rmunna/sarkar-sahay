## What is Voter ID Correction?

If your **Voter ID card (EPIC)** has incorrect details — such as a wrong name, date of birth, photo, address, or gender — you can correct them online using **Form 8** on the official Election Commission portal. The correction process is free and can be completed entirely online.

> **Note:** CitizenNest is an independent informational website and is not affiliated with the Government of India or the Election Commission of India.

## What Can Be Corrected?

You can correct the following details on your Voter ID using Form 8:

- **Name** (spelling error or complete name change)
- **Date of birth**
- **Gender**
- **Photograph** (blurred, old, or incorrect photo)
- **Father's/Mother's/Husband's name**
- **Address** (within the same constituency)
- **EPIC number-related errors**

> **Important:** If you are **shifting to a new constituency**, you need to file **Form 6** (new registration), not Form 8. See our guide on [Voter ID transfer to new address](/guide/voter-id-transfer-state-assembly).

## Documents Required for Each Correction

### Name Correction
- Aadhaar card, passport, or PAN card showing the correct name
- Class 10th or 12th marksheet
- Gazette notification (for legal name change)

### Date of Birth Correction
- Birth certificate
- Class 10th marksheet
- Passport
- Aadhaar card

### Photo Correction
- Recent passport-size photograph (JPEG, 10–50 KB)
- White or light background, front-facing, clear face

### Address Correction (Same Constituency)
- Aadhaar card with updated address
- Utility bill (electricity/water/gas — not older than 3 months)
- Bank passbook with address
- Rent agreement with landlord's ID

### Gender Correction
- Aadhaar card or passport showing correct gender
- Medical certificate (if applicable)

## How to Correct Voter ID Online (Form 8)

### Step-by-Step Process on voters.eci.gov.in

1. Visit [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in)
2. **Login** with your registered mobile number and password. If not registered, create an account first
3. Click on **"Correction of Entries in Electoral Roll"** or select **"Form 8"**
4. Enter your **EPIC number** (Voter ID number) to fetch your existing details
5. Select the **field(s) you want to correct:**
   - Name
   - Date of birth
   - Gender
   - Photo
   - Address
   - Relative's name
6. Enter the **correct details** in the relevant fields
7. Upload **supporting documents** as proof for the correction:
   - Document file: PDF or JPEG, max 2 MB
   - Photo: JPEG, 10–50 KB
8. Fill in the **declaration** section
9. **Preview** your application — verify all changes carefully
10. Click **"Submit"**
11. Note down the **Reference ID** for tracking

### Offline Correction Process

1. Download **Form 8** from [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in) or collect from the local ERO office
2. Fill in the form with correct details in **black or blue pen**
3. Attach **self-attested copies** of supporting documents
4. Attach a **passport-size photograph** (if correcting photo)
5. Submit to the **Electoral Registration Officer (ERO)** or hand it to the **BLO** during a verification drive

## Approval Timeline

| Correction Type | Typical Timeline |
|---|---|
| Name correction | 15–30 days |
| DOB correction | 15–30 days |
| Photo update | 15–30 days |

> **Note:** BLO verification may be required for address and name corrections. The BLO will visit your address to verify details.

## Important Tips

1. **Correct one detail at a time** if possible — multiple corrections in one form sometimes cause delays
2. **Upload clear, readable documents** — blurry scans are a common reason for rejection
3. **Match your name exactly** with the supporting document you're uploading
4. **Track your application** regularly using the reference ID on [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in)
5. **Keep originals ready** for BLO verification if one is scheduled

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I change my name on Voter ID after marriage?
Yes, you can correct your name using Form 8. Upload your marriage certificate or updated Aadhaar/passport as proof of the new name.

### Can I change the photo on my Voter ID online?
Yes, select the photo correction option in Form 8 and upload a new passport-size photograph in JPEG format (10–50 KB, white background).

### How do I know if my correction was approved?
Track your application on [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in) using the reference ID. You will also receive SMS updates on your registered mobile number. See our [status check guide](/guide/voter-id-status-check-online).

### What if my correction request is rejected?
Rejections usually happen due to unclear documents or mismatched details. Review the rejection reason on the portal, fix the issue, and re-apply with correct documents.

### Can I correct multiple fields at once?
Yes, Form 8 allows you to correct multiple fields in a single application. However, submitting fewer changes per form may result in faster processing.

### Do I need to visit any office for correction?
For online applications, a BLO may visit your address for verification (especially for address or name changes). For most corrections, no office visit is needed from your side.

### How many times can I correct my Voter ID?
There is no formal limit, but frequent corrections may attract additional verification. Ensure your details are correct before submitting.
