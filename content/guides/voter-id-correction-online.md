# How to Correct Voter ID Card Online — Name, Address, Photo Change (NVSP)

Made a spelling mistake while registering? Address changed after marriage? Photo looking outdated? Don't worry — the Election Commission of India (ECI) allows you to correct your Voter ID (EPIC) details online through the **National Voters' Service Portal (NVSP)** at [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in).

This guide covers everything: correcting name, address, photo, date of birth, gender, and father's/husband's name using **Form 8**, shifting your voter ID to a new address using **Form 8A**, and downloading the **e-EPIC (digital Voter ID)**.

---

## When Do You Need Voter ID Correction?

You should apply for a correction if any of the following details on your Voter ID card are wrong or outdated:

- **Name** — spelling mistake or name change after marriage
- **Father's / Mother's / Husband's Name** — incorrect or misspelt
- **Date of Birth** — wrong year, month, or date
- **Gender** — incorrectly recorded
- **Address** — old address or wrong details (within the same constituency)
- **Photo** — blurry, outdated, or incorrect photo
- **EPIC Number Issues** — any mismatch in your electoral records

> **Important:** If you have moved to a **different constituency** (different Assembly or Parliamentary area), you need **Form 8A** (shifting), not Form 8.

---

## How to Correct Voter ID Details Online (Form 8 — Step-by-Step)

**Form 8** is used for correction of entries in the electoral roll. Here's how to do it online:

### Step 1: Visit the Voters' Service Portal

1. Go to [https://voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Login/Register"**
3. Register using your **mobile number** and verify with OTP
4. If already registered, simply log in

### Step 2: Start the Correction Application

1. After logging in, go to **"Forms"** or **"Correction in Existing Electoral Roll Entry"**
2. Select **"Form 8 — Correction of Entries in Electoral Roll"**
3. Enter your **EPIC number** (Voter ID number) to pull up your existing details

### Step 3: Select What You Want to Correct

You can correct one or more of these fields in a single application:

- Name (English and regional language)
- Relative's name (father / mother / husband)
- Date of birth
- Gender
- Photo
- Address (within the same constituency)

### Step 4: Fill in the Correct Details

1. Enter the **correct details** in the form fields
2. For **photo change** — upload a recent passport-size photo (JPEG, max 200 KB)
3. For **name or DOB correction** — upload supporting documents as proof

### Step 5: Upload Supporting Documents

1. Upload scanned copies of proof documents (see Documents Required section below)
2. Accepted formats: **JPEG or PDF**, typically max **2 MB** each
3. Make sure documents are clear and readable

### Step 6: Declaration and Submit

1. Fill in the declaration section
2. Enter the **place** and **date**
3. Click **"Submit"**
4. Note down your **Reference ID** for tracking

## How to Shift Voter ID to a New Address (Form 8A)

If you have **moved to a new address in a different constituency**, use **Form 8A** to shift your voter registration.

### Steps to File Form 8A Online

1. Visit [https://voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in) and log in
2. Select **"Form 8A — Shifting of Residence"** under Forms
3. Enter your **existing EPIC number**
4. Fill in your **new address details** — state, district, Assembly constituency, and full address
5. Upload **address proof** for the new location (e.g., Aadhaar, utility bill, rent agreement)
6. Submit the form and note down your **Reference ID**

> **Note:** After successful verification by the BLO (Booth Level Officer), your voter ID will be shifted to the new constituency. Your old entry will be deleted automatically.

### What Happens After Filing Form 8A?

- A **BLO** (Booth Level Officer) may visit your new address for physical verification
- Once verified, your name is added to the new constituency's electoral roll
- Your old registration is removed to avoid duplicate entries
- You will receive a **new EPIC card** with the updated constituency details

## How to Download e-EPIC (Digital Voter ID)

The **e-EPIC** is the digital version of your Voter ID card. It's an official document you can store on your phone.

### Steps to Download e-EPIC

1. Visit [https://voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in)
2. Log in with your registered mobile number
3. Go to **"Download e-EPIC"** section
4. Enter your **EPIC number**
5. Verify with **OTP** sent to your registered mobile
6. Download the e-EPIC as a **PDF file**

### Through the Voter Helpline App

1. Download the **Voter Helpline** app from [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.eci.citizen) or Apple App Store
2. Register / log in with your mobile number
3. Go to **"e-EPIC Download"**
4. Enter your EPIC number and verify via OTP
5. Download and save the PDF

> **Tip:** The e-EPIC has a QR code and is a valid identity proof. Keep it on your phone for quick access.

## Documents Required for Voter ID Correction

| Correction Type | Accepted Documents |
|---|---|
| **Name correction** | Aadhaar card, PAN card, Passport, Class 10 marksheet |
| **Father's/Husband's name** | Aadhaar, Passport, Birth certificate, Ration card |
| **Date of birth** | Birth certificate, Class 10 marksheet, Passport, Aadhaar |
| **Address change** (same constituency) | Aadhaar, Utility bill (electricity/water/gas), Bank passbook, Rent agreement |
| **Address shift** (different constituency) | Aadhaar with new address, Utility bill, Rent agreement, Passport |
| **Photo change** | Recent passport-size photograph (JPEG, max 200 KB) |
| **Gender correction** | Aadhaar card, Passport, Medical certificate (if applicable) |

> **General rule:** Any government-issued document showing the correct detail is accepted. Always upload clear, readable scans.

## Tracking Your Application Status

After submitting your correction, you can track its status:

### On the NVSP Portal

1. Go to [https://voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in)
2. Log in to your account
3. Navigate to **"Track Application Status"**
4. Enter your **Reference ID** (received at the time of submission)
5. View the current status — pending, under verification, approved, or rejected

### On the Voter Helpline App

1. Open the **Voter Helpline** app
2. Go to **"Track Application"**
3. Enter your Reference ID
4. Check status updates

### By Contacting the BLO

- You can also contact your local **Booth Level Officer (BLO)** for updates
- Find BLO details on the NVSP portal under **"Know Your BLO"**
- Call the **Voter Helpline: 1950** (toll-free) for assistance

## Common Rejection Reasons

Applications sometimes get rejected. Here are the most common reasons:

1. **Blurry or unclear documents** — uploaded scans are not readable
2. **Mismatch in details** — the name on the document doesn't match your application
3. **Wrong form used** — used Form 8 instead of 8A (or vice versa)
4. **Incomplete form** — mandatory fields left blank
5. **Invalid photo** — photo doesn't meet size or format requirements
6. **Duplicate application** — multiple pending applications for the same correction
7. **BLO verification failed** — BLO couldn't verify you at the given address
8. **No supporting document** — correction requested without uploading proof

> **What to do if rejected?** Check the rejection reason on the portal, fix the issue, and **resubmit a fresh application**. There is no limit on reapplications.

## FAQs

### 1. Is there any fee for correcting Voter ID details?

No. Voter ID correction through the NVSP portal is **completely free**. There is no charge for Form 8 or Form 8A.

### 2. How long does it take for the correction to reflect?

Typically **15 to 30 days** after submission, depending on BLO verification and the ERO's (Electoral Registration Officer) approval. During election season, it may take longer.

### 3. Can I correct multiple details in one application?

Yes. You can correct **name, photo, address, date of birth**, and other fields in a **single Form 8 application**. No need to file separately for each correction.

### 4. What if my mobile number is not linked to my Voter ID?

You can register on the NVSP portal with any mobile number. However, for **e-EPIC download**, the mobile number must be linked to your EPIC record. Visit your nearest BLO or ERO office to link your number.

### 5. Can I apply for Voter ID correction offline?

Yes. Download **Form 8** from the ECI website, fill it out, attach supporting documents and passport-size photos, and submit it to your local **ERO office** or through your **BLO**.

### 6. What is the difference between Form 8 and Form 8A?

- **Form 8** — For correcting details (name, DOB, photo, address within same constituency)
- **Form 8A** — For shifting voter registration to a **new constituency** when you move to a different area

### 7. Can NRIs correct their Voter ID details?

Yes. NRIs registered as overseas electors can apply for corrections through the NVSP portal. They need to upload their **Indian Passport** as proof.
