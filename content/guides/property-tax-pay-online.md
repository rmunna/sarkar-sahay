# How to Pay Property Tax Online in India: State-Wise Guide

Property tax is a mandatory annual payment that every property owner in India must make to their local municipal corporation or urban local body. With digital India initiatives, almost every major city now allows you to **pay property tax online** in just a few minutes.

This guide covers the step-by-step process for paying property tax online across major Indian states and cities.

## What Is Property Tax?

Property tax is a levy charged by municipal corporations on all residential, commercial, and industrial properties within their jurisdiction. The revenue is used for maintaining civic infrastructure — roads, drainage, street lighting, and sanitation.

Every property owner must pay this tax annually or semi-annually, depending on the municipality.

## Documents You Need Before Paying Online

Before you start the online payment process, keep these details handy:

- **Property ID / Assessment Number** (printed on previous tax receipts)
- **Owner's name and address**
- **Khata number** (in Karnataka)
- **Zone/Ward number** (if applicable)
- **Payment method** — net banking, UPI, debit/credit card

## General Steps to Pay Property Tax Online

While each city has its own portal, the general process is similar:

1. **Visit the official municipal corporation website** for your city
2. **Navigate to the Property Tax section** — usually on the homepage
3. **Enter your Property ID** or search by owner name/address
4. **Verify property details** and check the tax amount due
5. **Select the payment year** (current year or arrears)
6. **Choose a payment method** — UPI, net banking, card, etc.
7. **Complete the payment** and save/download the receipt

## State-Wise Property Tax Payment Portals

### Karnataka (BBMP — Bengaluru)

- **Portal:** [bbmptax.karnataka.gov.in](https://bbmptax.karnataka.gov.in)
- Search using SAS Application Number or Khata Number
- Early payment discount of 5% available before April 30
- Supports UPI, net banking, and card payments

### Maharashtra (BMC — Mumbai)

- **Portal:** [ptaxportal.mcgm.gov.in](https://ptaxportal.mcgm.gov.in)
- Enter your Account Number from the property tax bill
- Pay via net banking, debit/credit card, or UPI
- Penalty of 2% per month on late payments

### Delhi (MCD)

- **Portal:** [ptax.mcdonline.nic.in](https://ptax.mcdonline.nic.in)
- Unified MCD portal covers all zones
- Enter UPIC (Unique Property Identification Code)
- 15% rebate for timely payment before June 30

### Telangana (GHMC — Hyderabad)

- **Portal:** [onlineghy.ghmc.gov.in](https://onlineghy.ghmc.gov.in)
- Search using PTIN (Property Tax Identification Number)
- 5% early bird discount available
- Supports all digital payment methods

### Tamil Nadu (Chennai Corporation)

- **Portal:** [chennaicorporation.gov.in](https://chennaicorporation.gov.in)
- Enter Zone, Division, and Bill Number
- Semi-annual payment option available
- Pay via net banking or card

### Uttar Pradesh (Lucknow, Noida, etc.)

- **Portal:** [nagarvikas.up.gov.in](https://nagarvikas.up.gov.in)
- Available for all municipal corporations in UP
- Enter House Tax Number to view dues
- UPI and net banking accepted

### Rajasthan (Jaipur, Jodhpur, etc.)

- **Portal:** [urban.rajasthan.gov.in](https://urban.rajasthan.gov.in)
- Available for all ULBs in Rajasthan
- Enter Property ID to check and pay dues
- Supports multiple payment gateways

### Gujarat (Ahmedabad, Surat, etc.)

- **Portal:** [ahmedabadcity.gov.in](https://ahmedabadcity.gov.in) (Ahmedabad)
- Enter Tax Number or search by name
- 10% early payment rebate in some cities

## Early Payment Discounts

Many municipalities encourage timely payment with rebates:

| City | Discount | Deadline |
|------|----------|----------|
| Bengaluru (BBMP) | 5% | April 30 |
| Delhi (MCD) | 15% | June 30 |
| Hyderabad (GHMC) | 5% | April 30 |
| Mumbai (BMC) | Varies | Check portal |
| Chennai | No rebate | — |

## What to Do If Payment Fails

If your online payment doesn't go through or the amount is debited but not reflected:

1. Check your bank statement for the debit
2. Wait 24-48 hours for the transaction to settle
3. Check the portal again with your Property ID
4. If still not reflected, raise a grievance on the portal
5. Keep the transaction reference number safe

For detailed troubleshooting, read our guide on [Property Tax Payment Not Reflecting](/guide/property-tax-payment-not-reflecting-fix).

## Tips for Hassle-Free Payment

- **Save your Property ID** — you'll need it every year
- **Pay early** to avail discounts and avoid server congestion near deadlines
- **Download and save receipts** immediately after payment
- **Check for arrears** before paying the current year's tax
- **Set a calendar reminder** for the annual due date
