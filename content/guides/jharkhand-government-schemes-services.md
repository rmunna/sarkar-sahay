# Jharkhand Government Schemes and Services – Complete Guide

Jharkhand, rich in minerals and tribal heritage, runs a comprehensive welfare system focused on tribal empowerment, women's financial independence, education, healthcare, and rural development. Flagship schemes like Maiyan Samman Yojana, Guruji Credit Card for students, and Mukhyamantri Protsahan Yojana for graduates have expanded the state's social safety net significantly. This guide covers every major Jharkhand government scheme with eligibility, benefits, and application details.

## Key Government Portals in Jharkhand

- **Jharkhand State Portal** – [jharkhand.gov.in](https://www.jharkhand.gov.in/) – Official state government website
- **JharSewa** – [jharsewa.jharkhand.gov.in](https://jharsewa.jharkhand.gov.in/) – Online citizen services and scheme applications
- **Jharbhoomi** – [jharbhoomi.jharkhand.gov.in](https://jharbhoomi.jharkhand.gov.in/) – Land records, khata, plot details
- **e-Kalyan** – [ekalyan.cgg.gov.in](https://ekalyan.cgg.gov.in/) – Scholarship portal for SC, ST, OBC students
- **CM Dashboard** – Real-time monitoring of government schemes

## Women Empowerment Schemes

### Maiyan Samman Yojana

Jharkhand's flagship women's empowerment scheme providing **₹2,500 per month** (₹30,000 per year) to women aged 18-50 for financial independence and dignity. The amount was increased from ₹1,000 to ₹2,500 per month from January 2025.

**Eligibility:**
- Women aged 18-50 years
- Resident of Jharkhand
- Not a government employee or income tax payer
- Aadhaar-linked bank account required

**Official Portal:** [mmmsy.jharkhand.gov.in](https://mmmsy.jharkhand.gov.in/)

Read the complete guide: [Jharkhand Maiyan Samman Yojana](/guide/jharkhand-maiyan-samman)

### Mukhyamantri Kanyadan Yojana

Financial assistance of **₹30,000** for the marriage of daughters from BPL families, with additional amounts for SC/ST beneficiaries.

### Phulo Jhano Ashirwad Yojana

Scheme to liberate women from illegal liquor brewing by providing alternative livelihood support — loans, training, and SHG linkage.

### Savitribai Phule Kishori Samridhi Yojana

Conditional cash transfer for school-going girls:
- ₹2,500 each in Classes 8 and 9
- ₹5,000 in Class 10
- ₹20,000 on turning 18 (for higher education or marriage)

**Official Portal:** [savitribaipksy.jharkhand.gov.in](https://savitribaipksy.jharkhand.gov.in/)

## Education Schemes

### Guruji Credit Card – Student Education Loan

Interest-free education loan of up to **₹10 lakh** for students from Jharkhand pursuing higher education. The state government bears the interest.

**Eligibility:**
- Domicile of Jharkhand
- Admission in recognized institution (within or outside Jharkhand)
- Family income below ₹4 lakh per annum
- For diploma, degree, and professional courses

Read the complete guide: [Jharkhand Guruji Credit Card for Students](/guide/jharkhand-guruji-credit-card-students)

### Mukhyamantri Protsahan Yojana – Graduate Unemployment Allowance

Financial assistance of **₹5,000** as a one-time grant for unemployed graduates from Jharkhand to support their job search.

Read the complete guide: [Jharkhand Mukhyamantri Protsahan Yojana](/guide/jharkhand-mukhyamantri-protsahan-yojana)

### e-Kalyan Scholarships

Comprehensive scholarship portal for SC, ST, and OBC students:

- **Pre-Matric Scholarship** – For Classes 1-10
- **Post-Matric Scholarship** – For Classes 11 and above
- **Overseas Scholarship** – For pursuing education abroad

See also: [Post-Matric and Pre-Matric Scholarships](/guide/scholarship-post-matric-pre-matric-apply) | [Education Loan Guide](/guide/education-loan-apply-india)

### Eklavya Model Residential Schools

## Health Schemes

### Mukhyamantri Abua Swasthya Suraksha Yojana

Jharkhand's flagship health insurance scheme (replacing the earlier Mukhyamantri Swasthya Bima Yojana) providing **free health coverage up to ₹15 lakh per family** at empanelled hospitals. It complements Ayushman Bharat and extends coverage to families not covered under the central scheme.

**Coverage:**
- Up to ₹15 lakh per family for serious illnesses
- Cashless treatment at government and private empanelled hospitals
- All ration card holders and food security beneficiaries eligible
- Apply via [bis.jharkhand.gov.in](https://bis.jharkhand.gov.in/)

See: [Ayushman Bharat vs State Health Schemes](/guide/ayushman-bharat-vs-state-health-schemes)

### 108 Ambulance Service

Free emergency ambulance available across Jharkhand. Dial 108 for medical emergencies.

### Didi Kitchen (Mukhyamantri Dal Bhat Yojana)

Subsidized meals at **₹5 per plate** through community kitchens run by women SHGs near hospitals and bus stands.

## Agriculture Schemes

### Mukhyamantri Krishi Ashirwad Yojana

Financial assistance to small and marginal farmers (with up to 5 acres of land) for crop cultivation:

- ₹5,000 per acre per year (Kharif season)
- Direct bank transfer
- Minimum ₹5,000 even for farmers with less than 1 acre

### Birsa Munda Krishi Kranti Yojana

Comprehensive agricultural development for tribal farmers including seeds, tools, irrigation, and training.

### Farm Mechanization

Subsidized farm equipment and machinery for Jharkhand's farmers through the agriculture department.

## Social Security and Pension

### Jharkhand Social Security Pension

- **Old Age Pension** – ₹1,000 per month for persons aged 60+ from BPL families
- **Widow Pension** – ₹1,000 per month
- **Disability Pension** – ₹1,000 per month for persons with 40%+ disability

## Housing Schemes

### Abua Awas Yojana

Jharkhand's state housing scheme providing **3-room pucca houses** to houseless families, primarily targeting rural and tribal areas.

**Eligibility:**
- Houseless families with no pucca structure
- Priority to SC, ST, widows, and disabled persons
- Must be a resident of Jharkhand

### PMAY (Urban and Rural) in Jharkhand

Central housing scheme complementing state housing programmes with subsidies for house construction.

## Tribal Welfare Schemes

### Birsa Munda Schemes

Multiple welfare schemes named after the tribal icon:
- **Birsa Munda Scholarship** – For tribal students
- **Birsa Munda Krishi Kranti** – Agriculture support for tribal farmers
- **Birsa Munda Awas Yojana** – Housing for tribal families

### Forest Rights Act Implementation

Jharkhand actively implements FRA for recognizing forest rights of tribal communities, including individual and community forest resource rights.

### Van Dhan Vikas Kendras

Tribal livelihood centres for processing and marketing minor forest produce — lac, tasar silk, medicinal herbs, etc.

## How to Apply for Jharkhand Government Schemes

### Online Application

1. **Visit JharSewa** – [jharsewa.jharkhand.gov.in](https://jharsewa.jharkhand.gov.in/) or scheme-specific portal
2. **Register/Login** – Using mobile number or Aadhaar
3. **Select the scheme** – Browse by department or search
4. **Fill application** – Enter personal and eligibility details
5. **Upload documents** – Aadhaar, ration card, income certificate, bank passbook
6. **Submit and track** – Note the application reference number

### Offline Application

1. Visit the nearest **Block/Panchayat office** or **CSC (Pragya Kendra)**
2. Carry original documents and photocopies
3. Fill the application form
4. Submit and collect acknowledgment receipt
5. Track through JharSewa portal

### Common Documents Required

- Aadhaar card
- Ration card
- Income certificate
- Caste/Tribe certificate (for ST/SC/OBC schemes)
- Bank passbook with IFSC
- Passport-size photographs
- Domicile certificate
- Disability certificate (for disability pension)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I check Maiyan Samman Yojana payment status?**
Visit the scheme portal or check your Aadhaar-linked bank account for the monthly credit.

**Can I apply for multiple Jharkhand schemes at once?**
Yes, you can apply for multiple schemes through JharSewa portal, provided you meet the eligibility criteria for each.

**How do I get my Jharbhoomi land record?**
Visit [jharbhoomi.jharkhand.gov.in](https://jharbhoomi.jharkhand.gov.in/), select your district, block, and halka to view khata and plot details.
