## What is the iKhedut Portal?

**iKhedut** (meaning "I am a farmer") is Gujarat government's **single online window** that brings together **75+ agricultural and allied schemes** for farmers across multiple departments. Instead of visiting different offices for different schemes, farmers can apply for all agricultural benefits at one portal.

## Who Can Use iKhedut?

Any **farmer in Gujarat** who:
- Has agricultural land (own or leased) in Gujarat
- Has an Aadhaar card
- Has a **8A land record** (7/12 equivalent in Gujarat) showing cultivator status

## How to Apply on iKhedut — Step by Step

### Step 1 — Register on Portal

Go to **[ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in)**:
1. Click **"Farmer Registration"**
2. Enter your Aadhaar number
3. OTP verification
4. Fill basic farmer details (land survey number, village, taluka)
5. Complete registration

### Step 2 — Browse Available Schemes

After registration, browse the scheme list:
- Filter by category (crop, animal husbandry, irrigation, etc.)
- Check eligibility criteria for each scheme
- Note the application deadline (many schemes have annual windows)

### Step 3 — Apply for Scheme

Click on the scheme and apply:
- Fill required details
- Upload supporting documents (land records, Aadhaar, bank details, photos)
- Submit application

### Step 4 — Application Number

Note your Application Number — you'll need this to track status.

### Step 5 — Field Verification

For equipment/infrastructure schemes, the Agriculture Officer verifies your application and field.

### Step 6 — Subsidy Disbursement

After verification and approval, subsidy is credited directly to your Aadhaar-linked bank account via DBT.

## Key Individual Schemes on iKhedut

**Drip/Sprinkler Irrigation (PMKSY-PDMC):**
- 45–55% subsidy for small/marginal farmers
- Up to 65% for SC/ST farmers
- Apply during October–March window each year

**Tractor Subsidy (RKVY):**
- 25–50% subsidy on tractor purchase
- Limited seats per district — early application recommended

**Mini Dal Mill / Oil Expeller:**
- 50% subsidy for SC/ST women farmers
- For adding value to crops

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. Do I have to visit any office after applying on iKhedut?**

For most schemes, yes — a field verification visit by Agriculture Officer is required after online application. You don't need to go to an office, but the officer will visit your farm.

**2. I applied last year and the application is still pending. What to do?**

Log in to iKhedut → My Applications → Check status. If "pending at department," contact your **Taluka Agriculture Officer** with your application number for follow-up.

**3. Can I apply for multiple schemes at the same time?**

Yes. You can apply for multiple schemes simultaneously on iKhedut. There is no restriction on applying for multiple schemes for different activities.

**4. I don't have internet. Can someone else apply for me?**

Visit any **Common Service Centre (CSC / Jan Seva Kendra)** in your taluka. The VLE can apply on your behalf on the iKhedut portal.

**5. I have 10 acres of land. Are there any exclusions?**

Most schemes have a per-farmer area cap (e.g., drip irrigation: maximum 5 hectares). Large landholders may be ineligible for some specific components but can apply for others.
