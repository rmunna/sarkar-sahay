# Old Age Pension Apply Online: State-Wise Process & Eligibility

Old age pension schemes in India provide monthly financial assistance to senior citizens from economically weaker sections. The central government runs the **Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS)** under the National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP), while most states add their own top-up amount. This guide covers eligibility, documents, pension amounts, and state-wise application process.

## What is IGNOAPS (Old Age Pension Scheme)?

The **Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme** is a central government pension for BPL (Below Poverty Line) senior citizens. It was launched under the **National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP)** by the Ministry of Rural Development.

**Central government contribution:**
- **Age 60-79 years:** ₹200 per month
- **Age 80+ years:** ₹500 per month

Most states add a top-up amount ranging from ₹200 to ₹2,500, making the total pension significantly higher depending on your state.

## Who is Eligible?

To apply for old age pension under IGNOAPS or state pension schemes, you must meet these criteria:

1. **Age:** Must be 60 years or above (some states have separate schemes for 58+)
2. **Income/BPL:** Must belong to a BPL family or have annual income below the state-defined limit
3. **Residency:** Must be a permanent resident of the state where you are applying
4. **Not receiving other pension:** Should not be receiving any other government pension (some states allow exceptions)
5. **Bank account:** Must have a bank account linked with [Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) for DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer)

## Documents Required

### Mandatory Documents
- **Age proof:** [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online), birth certificate, voter ID, or school certificate
- **Identity proof:** Aadhaar card, voter ID, or [PAN card](/guide/pan-card-apply-online)
- **Address proof:** Aadhaar card, ration card, voter ID, or electricity bill
- **Income certificate** or **BPL card/list** (issued by Tehsildar/Block office)
- **Bank passbook** (first page with account number, IFSC, and name)
- **Passport-size photographs** (2-4 copies)

### Additional Documents (State-Specific)
- **Ration card** (required in UP, Bihar, MP)
- **Samagra ID** (required in Madhya Pradesh)
- **Caste certificate** (for SC/ST/OBC quota in some states)
- **Self-declaration form** (that you are not receiving any other pension)

## How to Apply for Old Age Pension Online

### Step 1: Visit Your State Portal

Go to your state's social security or pension portal. Major state portals:

- **Uttar Pradesh:** [sspy-up.gov.in](https://sspy-up.gov.in/)
- **Bihar:** [elabharthi.bih.nic.in](https://elabharthi.bih.nic.in/)
- **Madhya Pradesh:** [pension.socialsecurity.mp.gov.in](https://pension.socialsecurity.mp.gov.in/)
- **Rajasthan:** [rajssp.rajasthan.gov.in](https://rajssp.rajasthan.gov.in/)
- **Delhi:** [edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in](https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in/)
- **Maharashtra:** [aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in](https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in/)
- **Tamil Nadu:** [tnpds.gov.in](https://www.tnpds.gov.in/)
- **Karnataka:** [sw.kar.nic.in](https://sw.kar.nic.in/)

### Step 2: Register / Login

1. Click on "Old Age Pension" or "Vridha Pension" section
2. Register with your mobile number if you are a new user
3. Verify OTP and create your login credentials
4. Login with your username and password

### Step 3: Fill the Application Form

1. Enter personal details: name, father's/husband's name, date of birth, gender
2. Enter address details: district, block, village/ward
3. Enter bank account details: account number, IFSC code, bank name
4. Enter Aadhaar number (mandatory for DBT)
5. Select pension scheme: "Old Age Pension" / "IGNOAPS"

### Step 4: Upload Documents

1. Upload scanned copies of required documents (usually JPEG/PDF, under 200KB)
2. Upload passport-size photograph
3. Upload bank passbook first page

### Step 5: Submit and Track

1. Review all details carefully
2. Submit the application
3. Note down your **application number / reference ID**
4. Track status on the same portal using your reference ID

## How to Apply Offline

If online application is not available in your area:

1. Visit your nearest **Block Development Office (BDO)** or **Tehsil office** or **District Social Welfare Office**
2. Collect the old age pension application form (free of cost)
3. Fill the form and attach all required documents
4. Submit at the same office
5. Collect the acknowledgement receipt
6. The Block/District office will verify your details and forward to the sanctioning authority

**Processing time:** 1-3 months (varies by state)

## Important Tips

1. **Link Aadhaar to bank account** — Pension is transferred via DBT, so Aadhaar-linked bank account is mandatory
2. **Keep BPL certificate updated** — Without valid BPL/income certificate, your application may be rejected
3. **Apply through CSC if needed** — If you are not comfortable with online applications, visit your nearest Common Service Centre (CSC)
4. **Annual verification** — Many states require annual life verification (Jeevan Pramaan). Complete it on time to avoid pension stoppage
5. **Check for state-specific schemes** — Some states like Delhi, Haryana offer much higher pensions than IGNOAPS alone

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: What is the minimum age to apply for old age pension?
**A:** Under IGNOAPS, the minimum age is 60 years. Some states like Rajasthan allow applications from 55 years (women) and 58 years (men) under their own state scheme.

### Q2: How much pension will I get under old age pension scheme?
**A:** The central IGNOAPS provides ₹200/month (60-79 years) and ₹500/month (80+ years). States add their own top-up, so total pension ranges from ₹400 to ₹2,500 depending on your state.

### Q3: Can I apply if I don't have a BPL card?
**A:** In most states, BPL status is mandatory for IGNOAPS. However, some states have their own pension schemes with income-based eligibility (e.g., Delhi allows income up to ₹1 lakh/year). Check your state's specific criteria.

### Q4: How long does it take to get the pension after applying?
**A:** Typically 1-3 months after application, depending on verification process and state. You can track your application status on the state portal.

### Q5: Can I apply for old age pension online from any state?
**A:** You must apply in the state where you are a permanent resident. Each state has its own portal and application process.

### Q6: What is Jeevan Pramaan and why is it required?
**A:** Jeevan Pramaan is a digital life certificate for pensioners. It is required annually to prove that the pensioner is alive. You can generate it using the Jeevan Pramaan app or at CSC centres using Aadhaar biometric authentication.

### Q7: My pension has stopped. What should I do?
**A:** Pension may stop due to missed life verification, bank account issues, or Aadhaar mismatch. Visit your block office or district social welfare office with your documents to resolve the issue.
