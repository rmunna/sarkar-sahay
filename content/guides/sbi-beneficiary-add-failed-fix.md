# SBI Beneficiary Add Failed? Fix NEFT/IMPS Transfer

Getting an error while adding a beneficiary in SBI net banking or YONO for NEFT/IMPS/RTGS transfers? This is a common issue with several possible causes — cooling period restrictions, OTP problems, incorrect IFSC codes, or beneficiary limits. This guide covers all the reasons and how to fix them.

---

## Why Does SBI Beneficiary Add Fail?

Here are the most common reasons:

| Reason | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Cooling Period** | New beneficiaries have a mandatory waiting period before transfers |
| **OTP Not Received** | OTP delay or failure due to network/mobile issues |
| **Wrong IFSC Code** | Incorrect or outdated IFSC code entered |
| **Beneficiary Limit Reached** | Maximum number of beneficiaries already added |
| **Server/Technical Error** | SBI system downtime or maintenance |
| **Account Number Mismatch** | Incorrect account number or name mismatch |
| **Profile Not Complete** | Net banking profile incomplete or not activated for transactions |

---

## Fix 1: Understand the Cooling Period

SBI enforces a **mandatory cooling period** after you add a new beneficiary. During this time, you **cannot transfer funds** to the new beneficiary.

### Cooling Period Details:

| Transfer Type | Cooling Period | Transfer Limit (During First 24 hrs After Cooling) |
|--------------|---------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| NEFT | **4 hours** | Up to ₹2 lakh |
| RTGS | **4 hours** | ₹2 lakh and above |
| IMPS | **4 hours** | Up to ₹2 lakh |

**What to do:**
1. Add the beneficiary and **wait at least 4 hours**
2. Do not delete and re-add — this resets the cooling period
3. After the cooling period, try the transfer again
4. For higher limits, wait **24 hours** after activation

**Tip:** If you need to make an urgent transfer, use **SBI Quick Transfer** (available in net banking) which allows transfers up to ₹10,000 without adding a beneficiary.

## Fix 2: OTP Not Received

If you're not receiving the OTP while adding a beneficiary:

### Steps to Fix:

1. **Check your registered mobile number** — OTP is sent only to the number registered with SBI
2. **Wait 2-3 minutes** — there may be a network delay
3. **Click "Resend OTP"** if available
4. **Check if DND is active** — contact your telecom provider to whitelist banking SMS
5. **Ensure mobile network** — OTP won't arrive if your phone is in flight mode or has no signal
6. **Try during off-peak hours** — SBI servers may be slow during salary days (1st of month) or end of quarter

**Still not working?** Try these alternatives:
- Use **high-security OTP** via the SBI Secure OTP app (download from Play Store/App Store)
- Contact SBI at **1800-11-2211** for OTP delivery issues
- Visit your branch to check if your [mobile number is correctly registered](/guide/sbi-mobile-number-change)

## Fix 3: Incorrect IFSC Code

An invalid or outdated IFSC code is a very common reason for beneficiary add failure.

### How to Fix:

1. **Verify the IFSC code** from the beneficiary's cheque book or passbook
2. **Search on RBI website** — [https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BankSearch.aspx](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BankSearch.aspx) (official IFSC lookup)
3. **Note:** After bank mergers (e.g., erstwhile associate banks merged with SBI), old IFSC codes may no longer work
4. **Use the new IFSC** — if the beneficiary's bank branch has been merged, the IFSC code may have changed
5. **Double-check for typos** — IFSC is always **11 characters** (4 letters + 0 + 6 characters)

**Example:** After SBI merger, branches of former State Bank of Travancore, Mysore, etc., received new SBI IFSC codes starting with **SBIN0**.

## Fix 4: Beneficiary Limit Reached

SBI has a maximum limit on the number of beneficiaries you can add.

### Limits:

- **Net Banking:** Maximum **25 beneficiaries** (across NEFT/RTGS/IMPS)
- **YONO App:** Maximum **25 beneficiaries**

### How to Fix:

1. Log in to **SBI Net Banking** or **YONO**
2. Go to **"Fund Transfer"** → **"Manage Beneficiary"**
3. **Delete inactive beneficiaries** that you no longer use
4. Try adding the new beneficiary again

---

## Fix 5: Beneficiary Activation Issues

Sometimes the beneficiary appears as "added" but transfers still fail.

### Check Activation Status:

1. Go to **"Manage Beneficiary"** in net banking or YONO
2. Check if the beneficiary status shows **"Active"** or **"Pending"**
3. If **"Pending"** — the cooling period is still in effect; wait 4 hours
4. If **"Active"** but transfer fails — verify account number and IFSC again

### Common Activation Issues:

- **Beneficiary deleted and re-added:** Cooling period resets to 4 hours
- **Net banking session expired** during addition: The beneficiary may not have been saved — try again
- **Browser issues:** Clear cache and cookies, or try a different browser

---

## Fix 6: Technical/Server Errors

If you get error messages like "Service temporarily unavailable" or "Transaction failed":

### Steps:

1. **Try again later** — SBI net banking has scheduled maintenance (usually late night)
2. **Clear browser cache** and cookies
3. **Use a different browser** (Chrome, Firefox) or try the YONO app
4. **Avoid peak hours** — salary days (1st, 7th of month), quarter-end, and year-end are busiest
5. **Check SBI server status** — if multiple services are down, it's likely a server issue

## Step-by-Step: How to Add Beneficiary Correctly in SBI Net Banking

1. Log in to [https://www.onlinesbi.sbi/](https://www.onlinesbi.sbi/)
2. Go to **"Fund Transfer"** → **"Add Beneficiary"**
3. Select transfer type: **"Inter Bank Beneficiary"** (for other banks) or **"Intra Bank Beneficiary"** (for SBI-to-SBI)
4. Enter beneficiary **name** (as per bank records), **account number**, and **IFSC code**
5. Verify the **branch name** auto-populated from IFSC
6. Enter the **OTP** sent to your registered mobile
7. Click **"Submit"**
8. Wait for **4 hours** (cooling period) before making a transfer

## Important Tips

1. **Never delete and re-add** a beneficiary — this resets the 4-hour cooling period
2. **Double-check IFSC codes** using the RBI IFSC search tool before adding
3. **Keep your mobile number updated** with SBI to receive OTPs — see our [mobile number change guide](/guide/sbi-mobile-number-change)
4. **Use SBI Secure OTP app** for faster and more reliable OTP delivery
5. **For urgent small transfers** (up to ₹10,000), use Quick Transfer to skip beneficiary addition

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: How long is the SBI beneficiary cooling period?

The SBI beneficiary cooling period is **4 hours** for NEFT, RTGS, and IMPS. After adding a beneficiary, you must wait 4 hours before you can make the first transfer.

### Q2: Can I transfer money immediately after adding a beneficiary in SBI?

No, you must wait for the **4-hour cooling period** to end. For immediate small transfers (up to ₹10,000), use the **Quick Transfer** option in SBI net banking which doesn't require adding a beneficiary.

### Q3: Why does SBI show "beneficiary not activated" error?

This means the cooling period has not ended yet. Wait for **4 hours** after adding the beneficiary. If it still shows inactive after 4 hours, delete the beneficiary and re-add it (note: this resets the cooling period).

### Q4: What is the maximum number of beneficiaries in SBI net banking?

You can add a maximum of **25 beneficiaries** in SBI net banking and YONO app. Delete unused beneficiaries to free up slots.

### Q5: SBI beneficiary OTP not coming — what should I do?

Check if your mobile number is registered, wait 2-3 minutes for delay, click resend, check DND settings, and ensure network availability. If none work, install the **SBI Secure OTP** app or contact SBI helpline 1800-11-2211.

### Q6: Can I add a beneficiary in SBI YONO app?

Yes. Open YONO → Fund Transfer → Add Beneficiary. The process and cooling period are the same as net banking. Ensure your YONO app is updated to the latest version.
