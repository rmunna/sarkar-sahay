# Link Aadhaar with Ration Card: State-Wise Process

Linking your [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) with your ration card is essential for receiving subsidised food grains under the **National Food Security Act (NFSA)**. Aadhaar seeding with ration cards enables the **One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC)** scheme, allowing beneficiaries to draw rations from any Fair Price Shop (FPS) across India.

## Why Link Aadhaar with Ration Card?

- **One Nation One Ration Card** — Draw rations from any FPS in any state across India
- **Eliminate duplicate/bogus cards** — Aadhaar-based authentication prevents fraud
- **Biometric authentication at FPS** — Ensures genuine beneficiaries receive food grains
- **Portability** — Migrant workers can access rations in their working state
- **Transparent distribution** — Real-time tracking of grain distribution through ePoS machines

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar card of all family members listed on the ration card
- Ration card (original or ration card number)
- Registered mobile number
- Family member details (name, relationship as on ration card)

## What is Aadhaar Seeding?

Aadhaar seeding is the process of linking each family member's Aadhaar number with their name in the ration card database. Once seeded, food grain distribution at Fair Price Shops uses **biometric authentication (fingerprint/iris)** through ePoS (electronic Point of Sale) machines.

## Method 1: Link Online via State PDS Portal

Each state has its own PDS (Public Distribution System) portal for Aadhaar-ration card linking:

1. Visit your state's PDS/food supply portal (see state-wise links below)
2. Log in or enter your ration card number
3. Select the family member to link
4. Enter the 12-digit Aadhaar number
5. Verify via OTP sent to Aadhaar-registered mobile
6. Submit — seeding is updated in 7-15 days

## Method 2: Link via Common Service Centre (CSC)

1. Visit your nearest **CSC (Common Service Centre)** or **e-Seva centre**
2. Carry original Aadhaar cards of all family members and ration card
3. The CSC operator will enter Aadhaar details for each member
4. Biometric verification (fingerprint) is done for authentication
5. Acknowledgment receipt is issued
6. Seeding is completed within 7-15 days

## Method 3: Link at Fair Price Shop (FPS)

1. Visit your designated Fair Price Shop
2. Carry Aadhaar cards of all family members and ration card
3. The FPS dealer uses the ePoS machine for biometric verification
4. Aadhaar details are captured and sent for seeding
5. Next ration distribution will use biometric authentication

## Method 4: Link at Tehsil/Block/District Office

1. Visit the **Food & Civil Supplies office** (Tehsil or Block level)
2. Submit a written application with Aadhaar copies of all family members
3. Attach self-attested photocopies of Aadhaar and ration card
4. The office processes seeding within 15-30 days

## One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC)

The **ONORC scheme** allows NFSA beneficiaries to purchase subsidised food grains from **any Fair Price Shop in any state**. This is especially beneficial for migrant workers. Requirements:

- Aadhaar must be seeded with ration card
- Biometric authentication at the FPS using ePoS machine
- Family members can split entitlements — some draw at home state, others at working state
- Available in all 36 states and UTs

Check portability status at [impds.nic.in](https://impds.nic.in/).

## How to Check Aadhaar-Ration Card Linking Status

1. Visit your state's PDS portal
2. Enter your ration card number
3. Check the **"Aadhaar Seeding Status"** column against each family member
4. Status will show **"Seeded"** or **"Not Seeded"**

Alternatively, visit [impds.nic.in](https://impds.nic.in/) for integrated PDS data.

## Processing Time

- **Online (State Portal):** 7-15 days
- **CSC Centre:** 7-15 days
- **Fair Price Shop:** Next distribution cycle
- **District Office:** 15-30 days

## Important Tips

1. **All family members** on the ration card must have their Aadhaar seeded — not just the head of family
2. If a family member doesn't have Aadhaar, [apply for Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) first; ration will continue with partial seeding
3. Ensure **name spelling matches** between Aadhaar and ration card — mismatches cause seeding failure
4. If biometric authentication fails at FPS, request **exception handling** or OTP-based authentication
5. Migrant workers should ensure seeding is done before moving to another state for ONORC benefits

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Aadhaar mandatory for getting ration?

While Aadhaar seeding is strongly encouraged and required for ONORC portability, the Supreme Court has directed that **no genuine beneficiary should be denied ration** for lack of Aadhaar. Alternative identification can be used in exceptional cases.

### What if my family member doesn't have Aadhaar?

Ration will continue for other seeded members. The unseeded member's share may be distributed based on state policy. It is advisable to [apply for Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) as soon as possible.

### Can I draw ration from any state with ONORC?

Yes, once your Aadhaar is seeded with your ration card, you can draw your entitled food grains from any ePoS-enabled Fair Price Shop in any state under the One Nation One Ration Card scheme.

### What if biometric authentication fails at the Fair Price Shop?

If fingerprint fails, try iris scan. If both fail, request **exception handling** from the FPS dealer — states have provisions for OTP-based or manual authentication in failure cases. Report persistent issues to the District Supply Officer.

### How do I add a new family member to my ration card with Aadhaar?

Apply for ration card modification through your state's PDS portal or food supply office. Submit the new member's Aadhaar along with the addition request. The member will be added and Aadhaar-seeded simultaneously.

### Can I link Aadhaar with ration card if names don't match?

No, if names don't match, seeding will fail. First update either your [Aadhaar name](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) or ration card name to ensure they match, then proceed with seeding.
