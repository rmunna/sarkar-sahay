# Scholarship Payment Not Received — How to Check & Fix

You applied for a scholarship months ago, your application shows "sanctioned" or "approved," but the money hasn't arrived in your bank account. This is one of the most common complaints from students across India. This guide explains how to track your payment, find out what's stuck, and fix it.

## Common Problems Students Face

- **"Application approved but payment pending"** — scholarship sanctioned on NSP but money not credited
- **"Payment failed — bank account mismatch"** — name on bank account doesn't match NSP records
- **"DBT transaction failed"** — Direct Benefit Transfer returned by the bank
- **"PFMS shows no record"** — payment not initiated despite approval
- **"State scholarship delayed"** — state-level scholarship has different timelines than central schemes
- **"Wrong bank account linked"** — old or inactive bank account on the scholarship portal

---

## Why Scholarship Payments Get Delayed

### 1. Bank Account Mismatch

The most common reason. Your payment will fail if:

- Name on your bank account doesn't exactly match the name on your scholarship application
- Your bank account is not linked to your Aadhaar
- The bank account is a joint account (some schemes require individual accounts)
- IFSC code entered was wrong
- Bank account has been closed or is dormant

### 2. Aadhaar Not Seeded with Bank Account

For DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer), your Aadhaar must be linked to your bank account. If it's not, the payment system cannot route the funds.

### 3. Institute/College Has Not Verified

On NSP, after the student applies, the institute must verify the application. If your college hasn't completed verification, the application stays in pending state and payment is never initiated.

### 4. State Government Fund Release Delay

For state-level scholarships, the state government must release funds to the disbursing authority. Budget delays, election code of conduct, or administrative changes can cause months of delay.

### 5. Duplicate Application

If you applied for the same scholarship twice or applied on both NSP and a state portal, the system may flag your application as duplicate and hold the payment.

### 6. PFMS Verification Pending

The Public Financial Management System (PFMS) validates bank account details before releasing funds. If your bank details fail PFMS verification, the payment is held.

## Step-by-Step: How to Check & Fix

### Step 1 — Check Payment Status on NSP

1. Go to [scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in/) or [nsp.gov.in](https://nsp.gov.in/)
2. Log in with your Application ID and password
3. Go to **"Check Your Payment Status"** or **"Application Status"**
4. Note the current status:
   - **Submitted** → institute verification pending
   - **Institute Verified** → forwarded to department
   - **Sanctioned** → approved, payment should be processed
   - **Payment Processed** → money sent to your bank
   - **Payment Failed** → bank returned the transaction

### Step 2 — Check on PFMS

1. Visit [pfms.nic.in](https://pfms.nic.in/)
2. Click **"Know Your Payments"**
3. Enter your bank account number and bank name
4. Enter the captcha and search
5. This shows all DBT payments made to your account — check if the scholarship amount appears here

### Step 3 — Fix Bank Account Mismatch

If payment failed due to bank account issues:

1. Log in to [scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in/)
2. Check if there's an option to **update bank details** (available during renewal window)
3. Ensure the name on your bank account exactly matches your scholarship application
4. Visit your bank branch and:
   - Ensure your account is active (not dormant)
   - Link your Aadhaar to the bank account (Aadhaar seeding)
   - Get the correct IFSC code
5. If you cannot update on the portal, contact your institute's scholarship nodal officer to request a correction

### Step 4 — Contact Your Institute

If the status shows "Submitted" but not "Institute Verified":

1. Meet your college's scholarship coordinator or nodal officer
2. Ask them to verify your application on the NSP institute login
3. Provide any additional documents they may need
4. Follow up regularly — many institutes delay verification due to workload

### Step 5 — File a Grievance

If your payment has been stuck for more than 3 months after sanctioning:

1. Visit [scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in/) → **Grievance** section
2. Submit a grievance with your Application ID and details of the issue
3. You can also email the NSP helpdesk at **helpdesk@nsp.gov.in**
4. For state scholarships, contact the respective state scholarship department

## When to Contact the Helpline

Contact the helpline if:

- Payment status shows "sanctioned" but no money received for 3+ months
- Payment failed multiple times and you've already fixed your bank details
- Your institute refuses to verify your application
- You suspect your scholarship was sanctioned but diverted (corruption)
- The portal doesn't allow you to update incorrect bank details

**Helpline contacts:**
- **NSP Helpline:** 0120-6619540
- **NSP Email:** helpdesk@nsp.gov.in
- **PFMS Helpline:** 1800-118-111 (toll-free)
- **Portal:** [scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in/) → Grievance
- For state scholarships, check the respective state education department website

## FAQs

### How long does it take to receive scholarship money after approval on NSP?

After the application is sanctioned, payment is typically processed within 1-3 months. However, delays of 3-6 months are common due to fund release cycles and bank verification. Check PFMS regularly for updates.

### My NSP application shows "sanctioned" but I haven't received money. What should I do?

First check on [pfms.nic.in](https://pfms.nic.in/) → "Know Your Payments" to see if the payment was attempted. If it shows a failed transaction, fix your bank details. If there's no record on PFMS, the payment hasn't been initiated yet — file a grievance on the NSP portal.

### Can I change my bank account on NSP after applying?

Bank account changes are typically allowed only during the renewal window. If you need an urgent change, contact your institute's nodal officer or the NSP helpdesk at helpdesk@nsp.gov.in with your application ID and the reason for the change.

### Why does PFMS show "no record found" for my scholarship?

This means the payment has not been processed yet through PFMS. Your application may still be at the verification stage, or funds may not have been released by the sponsoring department. Check your NSP application status for the current stage.

### My scholarship was approved last year but I never received the money. Can I still get it?

Yes, sanctioned scholarships are typically valid for payment. File a grievance on the NSP portal with your Application ID. The department may reprocess the payment. Keep following up regularly through the helpline.

### Do I need to link Aadhaar to my bank account for scholarship payment?

Yes. Most government scholarships are disbursed through DBT, which requires Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts. Visit your bank branch with your Aadhaar card to complete the linking if not done already.

### I applied on both NSP and my state scholarship portal. Is that a problem?

Yes, this can cause your application to be flagged as a duplicate, and both payments may be held. If you've done this, contact the NSP helpdesk to clarify which application should be processed and request cancellation of the duplicate.
