## What is the PMAY Beneficiary List?

The PMAY Beneficiary List is the official list of approved beneficiaries under Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana. If your name appears in this list, it means your application has been approved and you are entitled to receive housing benefits under the scheme.

Both PMAY-Urban and PMAY-Gramin maintain separate beneficiary lists that are updated regularly. Checking your name in the list is the first step to confirming your eligibility and benefit status.

Haven't applied yet? See our [PMAY application guide](/guide/pmay-online-apply) first.

## How to Check PMAY-Urban Beneficiary List

### Method 1: Search by Application ID

**Step 1:** Visit [pmaymis.gov.in](https://pmaymis.gov.in).

**Step 2:** Click **"Search Beneficiary"** on the homepage.

**Step 3:** Enter your **Application Reference Number** and click **"Search"**.

**Step 4:** If your name is in the approved list, your details will be displayed including approval status and subsidy information.

### Method 2: Search by Aadhaar Number

**Step 1:** Go to [pmaymis.gov.in](https://pmaymis.gov.in).

**Step 2:** Navigate to **"Search Beneficiary"**.

**Step 3:** Select **"Search by Aadhaar"** and enter your 12-digit [Aadhaar number](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online).

**Step 4:** Click **"Show"** to see if you're listed as a beneficiary.

### Method 3: Search by Name

**Step 1:** On the PMAY-U portal, go to **"Search Beneficiary"**.

**Step 2:** Select **"Search by Name"**.

**Step 3:** Enter the first three letters of your name.

**Step 4:** Browse through the results to find your entry. Note: this method may show many results — use application ID or Aadhaar for faster results.

## How to Check PMAY-Gramin Beneficiary List

### Method 1: On PMAY-G Official Portal

**Step 1:** Visit [pmayg.nic.in](https://pmayg.nic.in).

**Step 2:** Click on **"Stakeholders"** in the menu bar.

**Step 3:** Select **"IAY/PMAYG Beneficiary"**.

**Step 4:** Enter your **Registration Number** and click **"Submit"**.

**Step 5:** Your beneficiary details including sanction status and installment information will be shown.

### Method 2: Using AwaasSoft Reporting Portal

**Step 1:** Visit [rhreporting.nic.in](https://rhreporting.nic.in).

**Step 2:** Go to **"Social Audit Reports"** or **"Beneficiary Details"**.

**Step 3:** Select your **State → District → Block → Panchayat**.

**Step 4:** The complete beneficiary list for your panchayat will be displayed. Search for your name.

### Method 3: Using AwaasApp (Mobile)

**Step 1:** Download **AwaasApp** from Google Play Store (official app by Ministry of Rural Development).

**Step 2:** Select your **State → District → Block → Village/Panchayat**.

**Step 3:** Browse the beneficiary list or search by name.

**Step 4:** View details including construction progress, installments, and geo-tagged photos.

### Method 4: At Gram Panchayat Office

Visit your local Gram Panchayat office. The PMAY-G beneficiary list is also displayed on the panchayat notice board. Ask the Panchayat Secretary for the latest approved list.

## Understanding the Beneficiary List Details

When you find your name in the PMAY list, you'll see these details:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Beneficiary Name** | Name as registered in the application |
| **Registration Number** | Unique ID for tracking |
| **Category** | EWS/LIG/MIG-I/MIG-II (Urban) or SECC category (Gramin) |
| **Sanction Status** | Whether the house/subsidy has been sanctioned |
| **Subsidy/Amount** | Subsidy amount (Urban) or assistance amount (Gramin) |
| **Installments** | (Gramin) Installments released and pending |
| **Construction Status** | (Gramin) Foundation/walls/roof completion status |

## What If Your Name Is Not in the List?

### For PMAY-Urban:

1. **Application may still be under processing** — [check application status](/guide/pmay-status-check-online) separately
2. **Application may have been rejected** — check rejection status and [fix the issue](/guide/pmay-application-rejected-fix)
3. **Data entry errors** — if your name is misspelled, try searching with Aadhaar instead
4. **Not yet approved** — approval depends on state/ULB processing; wait and check periodically

### For PMAY-Gramin:

1. **Not in SECC 2011 database** — if your household wasn't captured in SECC survey, you won't be in the initial list
2. **Gram Sabha hasn't verified yet** — attend Gram Sabha meetings to ensure your case is considered
3. **Waiting list** — you may be on the waiting list; check with the BDO office
4. **Appeal process** — if wrongly excluded, file an appeal with the Gram Panchayat or Block office

## How to Get Added to the PMAY-Gramin List

If you believe you're eligible but not in the SECC list:

1. **Attend Gram Sabha** — raise your case during the meeting
2. **Apply to Gram Panchayat** — submit a written application with supporting documents
3. **Approach Block Development Officer** — request inclusion in the Awaas+ survey
4. **Await Awaas+ survey** — the government conducts periodic surveys to identify left-out eligible households
5. **Provide documents** — submit [Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online), income proof, and photographs as evidence of housing need

## State-Wise Beneficiary List Access

Most states publish PMAY beneficiary lists through the central portals. Some states also maintain separate portals:

- Access all states through [rhreporting.nic.in](https://rhreporting.nic.in) for PMAY-G
- Access urban lists through [pmaymis.gov.in](https://pmaymis.gov.in) for PMAY-U
- Lists are updated as new beneficiaries are approved each quarter

## Important Tips

1. **Check with correct spelling** — ensure name matches exactly as in your [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
2. **Use registration/application number** — faster and more accurate than name search
3. **Check periodically** — new beneficiaries are added in batches; check monthly
4. **Don't trust unofficial lists** — only use .gov.in and .nic.in portals to verify
5. **Keep your registered mobile active** — SMS notifications may be sent for status updates

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. How often is the PMAY beneficiary list updated?

PMAY-Urban lists are updated as applications are processed and approved by ULBs. PMAY-Gramin lists are updated after Gram Sabha verification and block-level approval, typically in batches.

### 2. My name was in the list earlier but now it's missing. What happened?

This could indicate a data error, system update, or cancellation due to non-compliance (e.g., not starting construction within the deadline for PMAY-G). Contact your ULB (Urban) or Block office (Gramin) immediately.

### 3. Can I check the beneficiary list for another person?

Yes. The PMAY-G beneficiary list at the panchayat level is public information. For PMAY-U, you need the person's Aadhaar or application number to search.

### 4. I'm in the beneficiary list but haven't received any money. What should I do?

Being in the list means you're approved. If money/subsidy hasn't come, check the [subsidy tracking guide](/guide/pmay-subsidy-not-received-fix) for next steps. For PMAY-G, ensure construction milestones are reported to receive installments.

### 5. Is there a separate beneficiary list for each state?

The data is maintained centrally but organized by state, district, block/ULB, and panchayat/ward. You can filter by location on the official portals to see area-specific lists.

### 6. Can I download the full PMAY beneficiary list PDF?

PMAY-G panchayat-level lists can be viewed and printed from rhreporting.nic.in. PMAY-U lists are searchable online but not available as downloadable PDFs for privacy reasons.
