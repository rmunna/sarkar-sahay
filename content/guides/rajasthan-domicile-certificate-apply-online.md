# Rajasthan Domicile Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **Mool Niwas Praman Patra** (मूल निवास प्रमाण पत्र) in Rajasthan — commonly called the domicile certificate — is issued by the Revenue Department (SDM / Tehsildar) and is required for NEET Rajasthan state quota (85%) admissions, RPSC government jobs (which require Rajasthan domicile for most posts), state scholarships, and scheme eligibility. Rajasthan requires **10 years of continuous residence**. Applications go through **E-Mitra** / SSO portal.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [emitra.rajasthan.gov.in](https://emitra.rajasthan.gov.in) or SSO portal. Must have **10 years continuous residence** in Rajasthan. Issued by SDM within **15–30 days**.

## Why You Need Rajasthan Domicile Certificate

- **NEET Rajasthan state quota (85%)** — domicile mandatory for RUHS/SMS counselling
- **RPSC (Rajasthan PSC)** — Rajasthan government jobs (most posts require domicile)
- **RSSSB (Rajasthan Staff Selection Board)** — state government posts
- **Rajasthan state scholarship** — state scholarship for Rajasthan residents
- **Mukhyamantri schemes** — Anuprati Coaching, Chiranjeevi, other Rajasthan schemes
- **REET (Rajasthan Eligibility Exam for Teachers)** — school teacher posts

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory — with Rajasthan address)
- **Jan Aadhaar** (Rajasthan family ID — important for all Rajasthan services)
- **Ration card** (Rajasthan ration card at local address)
- **Voter ID** (Rajasthan voter enrollment)
- **School/college certificates** (TC from Rajasthan institutions showing 10-year period)
- **Utility bills** — electricity/water bills covering 10-year period at Rajasthan address
- **Property documents** — if claiming through property ownership
- Self-declaration of 10-year continuous residency
- Passport-size photograph

## How to Apply Online — E-Mitra / SSO Portal

1. Go to [sso.rajasthan.gov.in](https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in)
2. Log in / register with Aadhaar / Jan Aadhaar / Gmail
3. Search **"Mool Niwas"** or **"Domicile Certificate"**
4. Select **"Revenue Department — Mool Niwas Praman Patra"**
5. Fill form:
   - Applicant name, address (district, tehsil, gram panchayat)
   - Duration of residence in Rajasthan (10+ years)
   - Purpose of certificate
6. Upload: Jan Aadhaar + voter ID + utility bills + school TC
7. Pay fee: **₹25–50** (online via SSO)
8. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via E-Mitra Kiosk

1. Visit nearest **E-Mitra kiosk** (at every panchayat and ward)
2. Carry Jan Aadhaar, Aadhaar, voter ID, utility bills
3. Operator fills and submits application
4. Service charge: approximately ₹30–60
5. Collect acknowledgement receipt

## Status Check

1. Log in at [sso.rajasthan.gov.in](https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in) → **"My Applications"**
2. Or SMS **RJSTATUS <Application Number>** to **56070**
3. Once approved: download digitally signed PDF

For **NEET 2026 Rajasthan state quota** (RUHS Medical College, SMS and other government medical colleges):
1. Rajasthan Mool Niwas Praman Patra — 10-year residence required
2. OR studied in Rajasthan schools for minimum required period (check RUHS notification)
3. Certificate must be SDM/Tehsildar issued — E-Mitra digital certificate accepted
4. Apply well in advance (15–30 days processing)
5. Upload during RUHS NEET counselling document verification

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Jan Aadhaar — what if I don't have one?

**Jan Aadhaar** is Rajasthan's family ID. If you don't have one, register at any E-Mitra kiosk. Bring Aadhaar and family details. Jan Aadhaar is free and issued immediately at kiosk. It is mandatory for accessing all Rajasthan e-services.

### RPSC — is Rajasthan domicile mandatory for all posts?

**Yes, for most Rajasthan state government posts** (RPSC/RSSSB recruitment), Rajasthan domicile is mandatory. A few centrally sponsored posts may not require it — check the specific RPSC advertisement for domicile requirement.

### I've lived in Rajasthan for 8 years — can I apply?

No. The requirement is **10 continuous years**. Wait until you complete 10 years and apply with documents covering the full period. Some SDM offices may accept 10 years from birth if you were born in Rajasthan.
