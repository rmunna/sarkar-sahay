# Delhi Free Bus Ride for Women – Complete Guide

## What is the Delhi Free Bus Ride Scheme?

The Delhi Free Bus Ride Scheme, also known as the **Pink Pass Scheme**, allows all women to travel **completely free** on Delhi Transport Corporation (DTC) and cluster buses. Launched in October 2019, this initiative aims to improve women's mobility, safety, and economic participation in the national capital.

Women simply need to collect a **pink ticket** from the bus conductor — no registration, no pass card, no paperwork.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Gender:** All women (including transgender women)
- **Age:** No age restriction
- **Residency:** No residency requirement — any woman boarding a DTC/cluster bus in Delhi
- **Documents:** No documents needed while traveling

This is one of the simplest government schemes — **no application, no eligibility check, no income limit**.

## How It Works

### Step-by-Step: Getting Your Free Ride

1. **Board the Bus:** Get on any DTC bus or Delhi cluster (orange) bus
2. **Request Pink Ticket:** Tell the conductor you want a "pink ticket" (महिला टिकट)
3. **Receive Ticket:** The conductor issues a **pink-colored zero-value ticket**
4. **Travel:** Ride to your destination — the ticket is valid for the full journey
5. **Keep Ticket:** Hold on to the pink ticket until you get off (for inspection purposes)

That's it. No card, no app, no registration.

## Key Facts

- **Cost to Women:** Absolutely free (₹0)
- **Funding:** The Delhi government reimburses DTC/cluster operators for women's rides
- **Daily Beneficiaries:** Approximately 15–20 lakh women use this scheme daily
- **Budget Allocation:** ₹1,600+ crore annually
- **No Physical Pass Needed:** Unlike many other free travel schemes, Delhi's scheme requires no card or pass

## Important Tips

1. **Always Take the Pink Ticket:** Even though it's free, always collect the pink ticket from the conductor. Inspectors may check, and traveling without a ticket (even free) can cause inconvenience.
2. **Available on All Routes:** The scheme works on every DTC and cluster bus route across Delhi, including AC buses.
3. **No Time Restriction:** Free rides are available during all operating hours — morning, evening, and night services.
4. **Combine with Metro:** While Delhi Metro isn't free, you can take a free bus to the nearest metro station to save on travel costs.
5. **Report Issues:** If a conductor refuses to issue a pink ticket or misbehaves, note the bus number and complaint via the DTC website or call 011-23370557.

## Safety Features on Delhi Buses

The free bus scheme is part of Delhi's broader women's safety initiative:
- **CCTV cameras** installed in all DTC and cluster buses
- **Panic buttons** available on newer buses
- **GPS tracking** of all buses
- **Reserved seats** for women in every bus
- **Women helpline:** 181 (Women in Distress)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Do I need any ID or document to get a free ride?
**A:** No. No ID, no document, no registration. Just board the bus and ask for a pink ticket.

### Q2: Is this scheme available for men?
**A:** No. The free ride scheme is exclusively for women and transgender individuals. Men pay regular fares.

### Q3: Can I use this on metro trains?
**A:** No. The scheme covers only DTC and cluster buses, not Delhi Metro, private buses, or inter-state buses.

### Q4: Do school girls and senior women also get free rides?
**A:** Yes. There is no age restriction — all females from children to senior citizens can avail free bus rides.

### Q5: What if the conductor refuses to give a pink ticket?
**A:** This is a violation. Note the bus number, route, and time. File a complaint with DTC at [https://dtc.delhi.gov.in](https://dtc.delhi.gov.in) or call their helpline.

### Q6: Is the pink ticket valid for multiple journeys?
**A:** No. Each pink ticket is valid for one journey (boarding to alighting). You need a new pink ticket for each bus ride.

### Q7: Do women from other states/cities get free rides too?
**A:** Yes. There is no residency requirement. Any woman boarding a DTC/cluster bus in Delhi gets a free ride — tourists, visitors, and residents alike.
