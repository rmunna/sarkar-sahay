## How to Apply Online (Step by Step)

1. Check your **CIBIL score** for free at [cibil.com](https://www.cibil.com) or paytm/BankBazaar (score 700+ is safe)
2. Choose a card from this guide based on your income and spend pattern
3. Go to the bank's website or use an aggregator (BankBazaar, PaisaBazaar)
4. Click **"Apply Now"** and fill basic details (name, PAN, mobile, income)
5. Upload documents: PAN, Aadhaar, salary slip (or ITR for self-employed)
6. Submit — get approval decision in 1–7 working days
7. Card delivered within 7–10 working days after approval

## Credit Card Do's and Don'ts

**Always Do:**
✅ Pay the **full statement balance** every month by due date  
✅ Maintain credit utilisation **below 30%** of limit  
✅ Set auto-pay for minimum due (as safety net, not routine)  
✅ Check your credit score quarterly

**Never Do:**
❌ Pay only the **minimum due** — remaining balance attracts 36–42% annual interest  
❌ Use credit card for cash advances — 3–4% fee + high interest  
❌ Max out your credit limit — damages credit score  
❌ Apply for too many cards simultaneously — hard inquiries hurt credit score

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is a good credit score to get a credit card in India?**
700+ CIBIL score is generally safe. 750+ gives access to premium cards. For entry-level cards (Flipkart Axis, SBI SimplyCLICK), 680+ is often sufficient. Check your free score at [freecibil.com](https://www.freecibil.com).

**Can I get a credit card without a salary slip?**
Yes — self-employed individuals can apply with ITR (last 2 years). Some banks accept Form 16 + bank statement as income proof.

**Is it safe to use a credit card online?**
Yes, if you shop on trusted sites (Amazon, Flipkart, official bank apps). Enable transaction alerts and 2FA. Never share card OTP with anyone.

**What happens if I don't pay my credit card bill?**
Missing the due date triggers late payment fees (₹100–₹1,300) and interest (36–42% annual) on the outstanding amount. After 90 days, it becomes a "bad debt" and severely damages your CIBIL score.

**Can a student get a credit card in India?**
Rarely directly — most cards require regular income. Students can get an add-on card under a parent's account, or a secured credit card (against FD) from ICICI, SBI, etc.

**How many credit cards should I have?**
1–3 cards is ideal. One primary cashback card + one travel card if you travel. More cards = harder to track due dates + higher temptation to overspend.
