# Chhattisgarh Government Schemes and Services – Complete Guide

Chhattisgarh, carved out of Madhya Pradesh in 2000, is a mineral-rich state with a large tribal and rural population. The state government runs a wide range of welfare programmes targeting farmers, women, students, labourers, and urban poor. Following the change in government in late 2023 (from Congress-led to BJP-led under CM Vishnu Deo Sai), some schemes launched by the previous government have been continued, modified, or replaced. This guide covers all major active Chhattisgarh government schemes with eligibility, benefits, and application details.

## Women Welfare Schemes

### Mahtari Vandana Yojana

The flagship scheme of the BJP-led Chhattisgarh government providing **₹1,000 per month** (₹12,000/year) to married women via DBT. Launched in early 2024, it covers married, widowed, and divorced women aged 21–60 who are Chhattisgarh residents.

**Key benefits:**
- ₹1,000 monthly directly to bank account
- No income ceiling — broadly targeted
- Covers widows and separated women

**Eligibility:** Chhattisgarh domicile, married women 21–60, Aadhaar linked to bank, not an income tax payer or government employee.

👉 **Detailed guide:** [Mahtari Vandana Yojana – Eligibility & Application](/guide/chhattisgarh-mahtari-vandana-yojana) | [Mahtari Vandan Overview](/guide/chhattisgarh-mahtari-vandan)

### Chhattisgarh Dhan Lakshmi Yojana

A conditional cash transfer scheme for **girl children** from BPL families. Financial assistance is provided in instalments linked to milestones — birth registration, immunization, school enrolment, and remaining unmarried until age 18. On turning 18, the beneficiary receives a maturity amount through insurance.

**Key benefits:**
- Instalment-based payments from birth to age 18
- Insurance cover of ₹1,00,000 on maturity at age 18
- Encourages girl child education and discourages child marriage

**Eligibility:** Girl child born in a BPL family, Chhattisgarh resident, birth registered within 14 days.

## Agriculture and Farmer Schemes

### Rajiv Gandhi Kisan Nyay Yojana

Launched by the previous Congress government, this scheme provided **input subsidy** to farmers for paddy, maize, sugarcane, and other crops. After the BJP government took over in December 2023, this scheme was **discontinued**. No new payments have been made under the scheme, and its official portal (rgkny.cg.nic.in) is no longer operational.

**Original benefits (for reference):**
- ₹9,000 per acre for paddy farmers
- ₹10,000 per acre for sugarcane
- Input subsidy for other notified crops
- Direct transfer to farmer bank accounts

**Current status:** ❌ **Discontinued.** The BJP government has replaced this with other agricultural support measures. Farmers should check the agriculture department website or call the CM Helpline (181) for current farmer welfare schemes.

### Godhan Nyay Yojana

A unique scheme that **purchases cow dung from cattle rearers** at ₹2 per kg to produce vermicompost, supporting both livestock owners and organic farming. Launched in 2020, it gained national attention. The BJP government has continued the scheme.

**Key benefits:**
- ₹2 per kg for cow dung procurement
- Promotes organic farming through vermicompost
- Additional income for cattle owners and landless labourers
- Gauthans (cattle sheds) serve as procurement centres

👉 **Detailed guide:** [Godhan Nyay Yojana – Complete Guide](/guide/chhattisgarh-godhan-nyay-yojana)

### Rajiv Gandhi Gramin Bhoomihin Krishi Mazdoor Nyay Yojana

This scheme provided **₹6,000 per year** to landless agricultural labourers in rural areas. Launched by the Congress government in 2022, its continuation under the new BJP government is uncertain.

**Original benefits:**
- ₹6,000 annual assistance to landless farm workers
- Direct bank transfer
- Targeted at rural landless agricultural labourers registered in villages

**Current status:** ❓ **Uncertain.** The official portal (rggbkmny.cg.nic.in) appears inactive. Beneficiaries should verify scheme status at their nearest Jan Seva Kendra, district collectorate, or by calling the CM Helpline (181).

## Education Schemes

### Swami Atmanand English Medium Schools (SAGEMS)

A transformative initiative converting government schools into **free English-medium schools** with quality infrastructure, smart classrooms, and trained teachers. Started by the Congress government, the BJP government has continued and expanded the scheme — though some have been renamed or restructured.

**Key features:**
- Free English-medium education from Class 1 to 12
- Smart classrooms, science labs, and computer labs
- No tuition or admission fees
- Over 700 schools operational across the state
- Admission through school-level process

### Saraswati Cycle Yojana

Free bicycles for **girl students of Class 9** studying in government schools to reduce dropout rates and promote girls' education.

**Key benefits:**
- Free bicycle to girl students entering Class 9
- Available in government and aided schools
- No income criterion — all eligible girl students covered

**Eligibility:** Girl student enrolled in Class 9 in a government or government-aided school in Chhattisgarh.

### Other Education Schemes

- **Chhattisgarh Scholarship Portal** – Pre-matric and post-matric scholarships for SC/ST/OBC students
- **Mukhyamantri Gyan Protsahan Yojana** – Merit-based cash awards for Class 10 and 12 toppers from SC/ST categories
- **Free Textbooks and Uniforms** – Distributed to all government school students

## Health Schemes

### Mukhyamantri Slum Swasthya Yojana

A healthcare outreach programme targeting **urban slum populations** with free health check-ups, treatment, and medicine through mobile health units and dedicated clinics.

**Key features:**
- Free health screening in urban slums
- Mobile health units visiting slum areas
- Free medicines and diagnostic services
- Focus on maternal health, child health, and communicable diseases

### Dr. Khubchand Baghel Swasthya Sahayata Yojana

Chhattisgarh's state health insurance scheme providing **cashless treatment up to ₹5 lakh per family per year** at empanelled hospitals. Works alongside Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY.

**Key benefits:**
- Cashless treatment up to ₹5 lakh annually
- Covers hospitalisation, surgery, and critical illnesses
- Available at government and empanelled private hospitals
- Ration card used for beneficiary identification

## How to Apply for Chhattisgarh Government Schemes

1. **Identify the scheme** – Use the CHOICE portal or this guide to find schemes you're eligible for
2. **Gather documents** – Aadhaar card, ration card, bank passbook, domicile certificate, caste certificate (as applicable)
3. **Apply online** – Visit the relevant portal (e-District, Mahtari Vandan portal, etc.) and submit your application
4. **Apply offline** – Visit your nearest Jan Seva Kendra, Gram Panchayat office, or district collectorate
5. **Track status** – Use the application reference number to track status online on the respective portal
6. **Helpline** – Call 181 (CM Helpline) for any assistance

### Documents Commonly Required

- Aadhaar Card
- Ration Card (BPL/APL)
- Bank Passbook (with IFSC)
- Domicile Certificate
- Caste Certificate (for SC/ST/OBC schemes)
- Income Certificate
- Passport-size photographs
- Mobile number linked to Aadhaar

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which Chhattisgarh schemes are still active after the 2023 government change?

Key schemes like **Mahtari Vandana Yojana** (new), **Godhan Nyay Yojana**, **Swami Atmanand Schools**, and **Saraswati Cycle Yojana** are active. Some Congress-era schemes like Rajiv Gandhi Kisan Nyay Yojana have been modified or replaced. Always verify on official portals.

### How can I apply for Mahtari Vandana Yojana?

Apply through the official portal [mahtarivandan.cgstate.gov.in](https://mahtarivandan.cgstate.gov.in) or visit your nearest Anganwadi centre or Jan Seva Kendra. See our [detailed guide](/guide/chhattisgarh-mahtari-vandana-yojana).

### Where can I get my caste, income, or domicile certificate in Chhattisgarh?

Apply online through the **e-District portal** at [edistrict.cgstate.gov.in](https://edistrict.cgstate.gov.in) or visit your nearest Jan Seva Kendra or Tehsil office.

### Is Godhan Nyay Yojana still running?

Yes, the Godhan Nyay Yojana continues to operate. Cow dung is procured at ₹2/kg through Gauthans. Read our [complete guide](/guide/chhattisgarh-godhan-nyay-yojana).

### How do I check my scheme application status?

Visit the respective portal (e-District, Mahtari Vandan, etc.) and enter your application or reference number. Alternatively, call the CM Helpline at **181**.

### Can I apply for multiple Chhattisgarh schemes simultaneously?

Yes, you can apply for multiple schemes if you meet the eligibility criteria for each. There is no restriction on availing benefits from different departments simultaneously.

### What is the CHOICE portal used for?

The CHOICE portal ([choice.cg.nic.in](https://choice.cg.nic.in)) helps citizens discover government schemes they are eligible for. Enter your basic profile details and the portal suggests applicable schemes with application links.
