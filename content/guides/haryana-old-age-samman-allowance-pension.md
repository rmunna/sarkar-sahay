## What is the Haryana Old Age Samman Allowance?

The **Old Age Samman Allowance** is Haryana government's monthly pension for senior citizens aged **60 years and above**. As of 2024–25, the monthly pension amount is **₹3,000 per month**. The scheme is notable for having **no income limit** — any resident of Haryana aged 60+ who is not receiving any other government pension is eligible.

## Who is Eligible?

**You ARE eligible if:**
- You are **60 years of age or older**
- You are a **permanent resident of Haryana** (minimum 15 years)
- You are **not receiving any other central/state government pension** (e.g., IGNOAPS, government job pension)
- You do not hold an elected post (MLA, MP, etc.)

**You are NOT eligible if:**
- You are receiving a government servant pension
- You are receiving any other old age / social security pension from Central or Haryana government

> **Important:** Unlike most other states, Haryana Old Age Samman Allowance has **no income/BPL limit** — it is universal for all 60+ Haryana residents who don't already have a pension.

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar Card** — mandatory for identity and DBT
2. **Parivar Pehchaan Patra (PPP)** — Haryana family ID (mandatory for all Haryana scheme applications)
3. **Age proof** — Aadhaar (if DoB included), school certificate, or voter ID with age
4. **Residence proof** — ration card, voter ID, or any document showing 15+ years in Haryana
5. **Bank passbook** — Aadhaar-linked savings account
6. **Declaration** — self-declaration that no other pension is being received
7. **Photograph**

## How to Apply — Step by Step

### Step 1 — Get Your Parivar Pehchaan Patra (PPP)

The Haryana family ID (**PPP — Parivar Pehchaan Patra**) is mandatory. If you don't have one:
- Visit the nearest **CSC / Atal Sewa Kendra**
- Or visit [meraparivar.haryana.gov.in](https://meraparivar.haryana.gov.in)

### Step 2 — Apply Online on Saral Portal

1. Go to **[saralharyana.gov.in](https://saralharyana.gov.in)**
2. Register / Login
3. Search for **"Old Age Samman Allowance"**
4. Fill in your PPP ID, Aadhaar, bank account details
5. Upload documents and submit

### Step 3 — Offline Application (Alternative)

Visit your nearest:
- **Atal Sewa Kendra**
- **District Social Welfare Officer (DSWO) office**
- **Block Development & Panchayat Officer (BDPO)**

Collect the form, fill it, and submit with documents.

### Step 4 — Verification

Field verification is done by the Patwari/BDO to confirm age and residency. This typically takes 4–8 weeks.

### Step 5 — Approval and Pension Start

After verification:
- Your name is added to the beneficiary list
- ₹3,000/month starts being deposited to your bank account

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. My father is 65 and a Haryana resident but lives with us in Delhi. Is he eligible?**

No. You must be a **permanent resident of Haryana** currently living in Haryana. Residing outside the state disqualifies you.

**2. My mother receives ₹200/month from IGNOAPS. Can she also get Haryana pension?**

She cannot get both central (IGNOAPS) and state (Old Age Samman) pension simultaneously — typically only one is given. Check with the DSWO office to confirm which is higher and switchable.

**3. How is PPP (Parivar Pehchaan Patra) made?**

Visit any CSC (Common Service Centre) or Atal Sewa Kendra with Aadhaar of all family members. The VLE creates the PPP for your family — it is free.

**4. My pension was stopped without notice. What do I do?**

Visit the DSWO office with your PPP ID and bank passbook. Common reasons: Aadhaar-bank link broken, incorrect data in PPP. Get the issue corrected and pension will resume.

**5. The payment shows "sent" on the portal but hasn't reached my bank account. What do I do?**

Check if your bank account Aadhaar seeding is active. Visit your bank branch with Aadhaar to verify. Also check with the District Treasury office through the DSWO.
