# APAAR ID vs ABC ID vs DigiLocker - Difference for Students

**Quick answer:** APAAR ID is the student's academic identity. ABC, or Academic Bank of Credits, is the platform that manages academic credits and uses APAAR ID. DigiLocker is the digital document wallet where APAAR card and academic documents may appear. Many people say "ABC ID", but the current official ABC portal describes APAAR ID as the learner identity used in ABC.

## Simple Difference

| Term | Meaning | Used For |
|---|---|---|
| APAAR ID | Automated Permanent Academic Account Registry ID | Student academic identity |
| ABC | Academic Bank of Credits | Credit storage, accumulation and transfer |
| DigiLocker | Digital document wallet | Login, issued documents, APAAR card access |
| NAD | National Academic Depository | Academic record/document depository used by institutions |

## Why Students Get Confused

Many college forms, admission portals and exam notices use older or mixed wording:

- ABC ID
- APAAR ID
- ABC/APAAR ID
- Academic Bank ID
- DigiLocker ABC ID

The safer approach is:

1. Log in to the official ABC/DigiLocker route.
2. Get the APAAR number/card.
3. Enter that number where the form asks for APAAR/ABC learner ID, unless the institution gives a different instruction.

## What Official Portals Say

The official APAAR portal describes APAAR as a unique student ID under One Nation, One Student ID.

The official ABC portal says APAAR ID is integrated within the Academic Bank of Credits system and consolidates educational records in one place. The ABC FAQ also explains that APAAR provides each learner with a unique lifelong ID and ABC uses APAAR to track/manage academic credits.

## Is APAAR ID 12 Digits?

The official APAAR FAQ describes APAAR ID as a unique 12-digit code. If a form asks for a 12-digit APAAR/ABC ID, use the APAAR number shown in your official ABC/DigiLocker record.

## Is DigiLocker the Same as APAAR?

No. DigiLocker is not APAAR. DigiLocker is the login/document wallet. APAAR is the academic identity. APAAR may be stored or shown in DigiLocker as an issued document/card.

## Is ABC the Same as APAAR?

No. ABC is the credit-management platform. APAAR is the learner identity used in that platform. Think of it like this:

- APAAR = your academic identity number
- ABC = your academic credit bank/platform
- DigiLocker = where your digital card/docs can be accessed

## What Should I Enter in Admission Forms?

If the form asks for:

| Form Field | What to Enter |
|---|---|
| APAAR ID | APAAR number/card from DigiLocker/ABC |
| ABC ID | Usually the APAAR/ABC learner ID from ABC portal; confirm institution wording |
| DigiLocker ID | DigiLocker username/mobile/login detail only if specifically asked |
| PEN ID | School education identifier; ask school |

Do not enter Aadhaar number unless the form specifically asks for Aadhaar and is an official institution/government form.

## Safe Rule for Forms

If a form asks for APAAR/ABC details and you are unsure:

1. Open the official ABC portal through DigiLocker.
2. Check the learner ID/card shown there.
3. Compare it with the APAAR card in DigiLocker Issued Documents.
4. Ask your institution whether the field expects APAAR ID, ABC learner ID, or PEN.
5. Keep a screenshot/PDF of the official card for your records, but do not share it publicly.

This avoids the common mistake of entering Aadhaar, DigiLocker mobile number, school admission number or PEN in the wrong field.

## Common Wrong Entries

| Wrong Entry | Why It May Fail |
|---|---|
| Aadhaar number | APAAR/ABC field is usually for learner ID, not Aadhaar |
| DigiLocker mobile number | This is login/contact detail, not APAAR ID |
| School admission number | This is local school data, not the national academic identity |
| PEN ID | PEN is school-record identifier, different from APAAR |
| Roll number only | College roll number is used for matching, not always the final learner ID |

## FAQs

### Is ABC ID now called APAAR ID?
The official ABC ecosystem now describes APAAR ID as the learner identity. Many users still call it ABC ID because they create it from the ABC portal.

### Can I use APAAR ID for college admission?
If the college/admission form asks for APAAR/ABC ID, use the official APAAR/ABC learner ID shown in your DigiLocker/ABC account.

### Where do I download APAAR card?
Check DigiLocker Issued Documents after APAAR generation.

### What is PEN ID?
PEN is used in school student records and is required for school APAAR generation. Ask your school to check it.

### Should I write ABC ID or APAAR ID on forms?
Use the label requested by the form. If the form uses old wording like ABC ID but links to ABC/DigiLocker, it may be asking for the same learner identity. Confirm with your institution when the form is unclear.
