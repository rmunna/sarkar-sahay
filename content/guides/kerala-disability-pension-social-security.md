## What is Kerala Disability Social Security Pension?

Kerala's **Disability Social Security Pension** is a monthly pension of **₹1,600** for persons with **40% or more disability** who have no other income source. It is part of Kerala's comprehensive Social Security Mission and is one of the better-funded state disability pensions in India.

---

## Key Benefits

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Monthly pension | ₹1,600/month |
| Minimum disability | 40% (certified by government medical authority) |
| Income limit | No other income source |
| Age limit | None — any age above 18 |
| Payment | DBT to Aadhaar-linked bank account |
| Department | Social Security Mission, Kerala |

---

## Who is Eligible?

**You ARE eligible if:**
- You are a **permanent resident of Kerala** (minimum 5 years)
- You have a **disability of 40% or more** — certified by a government medical authority (CMO/Civil Surgeon)
- You have **no regular income source** — no job, no other pension
- You are **not an income tax payer**
- You are **18 years or older**

> Kerala's disability pension covers all types of disability — physical, visual, hearing, intellectual, psychiatric, and multiple disabilities.

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar Card** — identity
2. **Disability certificate** — 40%+ disability from District Medical Board / CMO
3. **UDID card** (Unique Disability ID) — if available ([swavlambancard.gov.in](https://swavlambancard.gov.in))
4. **Residence certificate** — 5+ years in Kerala (from Village Officer)
5. **Income certificate** — self-declaration / income certificate from Village Officer
6. **Bank passbook** — Aadhaar-linked account in beneficiary's name
7. **Photograph**

## How to Apply — Step by Step

### Step 1 — Get Disability Certificate

Visit your nearest **District Hospital / Government Hospital** and request a disability assessment. The District Medical Board:
- Examines the disability
- Issues a certificate specifying the type and percentage

### Step 2 — Apply on Seva Sadhya Portal

1. Go to **[sevasadhya.kerala.gov.in](https://sevasadhya.kerala.gov.in)**
2. Register with your Aadhaar
3. Select **"Disability Pension"**
4. Fill the application and upload documents
5. Submit and note Application ID

### Step 3 — Apply at Village / Block Office (Alternative)

Visit your **Village Office (VO)** or **Block Panchayat Office**. The Village Officer can help with the online application.

### Step 4 — Verification

The Village Officer verifies:
- Disability certificate (40%+ disability)
- Residency (5+ years in Kerala)
- No other income

### Step 5 — District Social Justice Office Approval

After field verification, the **District Social Justice Officer** approves. Pension starts from the next month after approval.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. My child has 60% disability. Can she get the pension at age 12?**

Minimum age for pension application is 18 years. However, **Kerala has a separate scholarship and benefit scheme for disabled children** through the Social Security Mission. Contact the District Social Justice Office for children's specific benefits.

**2. I have a hearing impairment but my certificate shows 35%. Am I eligible?**

No. The minimum threshold is **40% disability**. If you believe your disability assessment undervalued your impairment, request a review from the District Medical Board.

**3. I already get ₹500/month from IGNDPS (Central scheme). Will I get ₹1,600 from Kerala on top?**

Kerala typically integrates IGNDPS with the state pension to give a total of ₹1,600/month. You won't get both separately — the higher amount is what you receive.

**4. Can I apply online or only in person?**

Both online ([sevasadhya.kerala.gov.in](https://sevasadhya.kerala.gov.in)) and offline (Village Office) applications are accepted.

**5. My pension was stopped without notice. Why?**

Common reasons: Aadhaar-bank link broken, periodic renewal required, or re-verification. Visit the nearest Village Office or Akshaya Centre to check and resolve.
