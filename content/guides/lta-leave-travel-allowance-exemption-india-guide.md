# LTA Leave Travel Allowance India 2026 — Complete Guide

LTA (Leave Travel Allowance) is a tax-exempt salary component in India — but only if you actually travel and submit the right documentation to your employer. Understanding the rules helps you maximize this benefit.

## What Is LTA?

LTA is an allowance your employer gives you for travel within India during leave. The **actual travel cost** (transport only) is tax-exempt under Section 10(5), subject to the limits below.

**Tax-free**: The actual fare paid for air/train/bus travel for yourself and eligible family members.

**Taxable**: Any LTA received that you don't use for actual travel, or amounts exceeding the economy class fare.

## LTA Rules — Key Conditions

### 1. Travel Must Be Within India Only
LTA covers travel **within India** only. International travel does not qualify. If you vacation in Thailand but claim LTA — not eligible.

### 2. Eligible Family Members
- Spouse
- Children (up to 2 children born after October 1, 1998 — this limit applies only to children, not to spouse or parents)
- Dependent parents
- Dependent brothers and sisters

**Note**: If you have more than 2 children born after October 1, 1998, LTA for the additional children is taxable. Children born before October 1, 1998 don't count toward the "2 children" limit.

### 3. Transport Costs Only
LTA covers only **transport fare** — not hotel, food, sightseeing, tours, shopping. Only the actual journey cost:
- Air: Economy class fare (cheapest available)
- Train: 1st class AC fare
- Bus: Deluxe class fare (where train not available)

If you book business class, only economy class fare equivalent is tax-exempt.

### 4. Block Year System — Twice in 4 Years

LTA can be claimed for **2 journeys in every 4-year block**. The blocks are:

| Block | Years |
|-------|-------|
| 2014–2017 | 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017 |
| 2018–2021 | 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021 |
| **2022–2025** | **2022, 2023, 2024, 2025** |
| 2026–2029 | 2026, 2027, 2028, 2029 |

You can claim LTA exemption for **2 trips** in the current block (2022–2025). These 2 trips can be taken in any 2 of the 4 years within the block.

### 5. Must Actually Take Leave and Travel
You must take leave from work and actually travel. The company's leave policy and LTA policy must align. Most companies require submission of travel proofs (tickets, boarding passes, hotel bills for reference) to release LTA.

## LTA Carry Forward Rule

If you claimed only **1 trip** in a block instead of 2, the **unused trip can be carried forward** to the next block — but only for 1 year.

**Example**: In block 2018–2021, you claimed only 1 LTA trip. The second trip from 2018–2021 can be carried forward to **2022** (first year of the new block). So in 2022, you could potentially claim 2 trips (1 carry-forward + 1 from the new block), up to the maximum of 3 in 2022.

## LTA in Your Salary Structure

Many employers include LTA in the CTC. Typical amounts: ₹20,000–₹60,000/year. 

**How it appears**: Some companies credit LTA to your account monthly (or twice yearly) and you submit bills to claim exemption. Others give LTA only when you submit travel bills.

If LTA is credited but you don't submit bills: The entire LTA becomes taxable as salary income.

## How to Claim LTA Exemption

### From Your Employer (Before April)
- Submit to HR/payroll before the company's declaration deadline (usually January–February)
- Provide: flight/train tickets or boarding passes, e-tickets showing fare paid
- Employer adjusts your TDS based on the claimed LTA exemption

### In ITR (If Employer Doesn't Accept)
- LTA exemption can also be claimed directly in ITR under Schedule S (Salary)
- Calculate exempt amount: actual travel cost × eligible family members × 2 trips in block
- Declare only the taxable portion of LTA as salary

## New Regime — LTA Not Available

Section 10(5) LTA exemption is available **only under the old tax regime**. If you opt for the new regime, your entire LTA is taxable as salary.

This is one factor to consider when choosing between old and new regime — if you have substantial LTA and actually travel, old regime may give better net income.

## Cash Voucher LTA Scheme (COVID-Era Provision)

During COVID (2020–21), the government allowed a one-time "LTA Cash Voucher Scheme" — employees could claim LTA tax exemption without actually traveling by spending the amount on goods/services with 12%+ GST. This was a temporary provision and is no longer applicable.

## Practical LTA Examples

### Example 1: Air Travel
- LTA received: ₹40,000
- Actual air tickets (economy class for self + spouse + child): ₹35,000
- Tax-exempt LTA: ₹35,000 (actual travel cost)
- Taxable LTA: ₹5,000 (₹40,000 − ₹35,000)

### Example 2: No Travel Taken
- LTA received: ₹40,000
- No travel taken in the year
- Tax-exempt LTA: ₹0
- Taxable LTA: ₹40,000 (full amount taxable)

### Example 3: Carried Forward
- LTA received: ₹40,000
- Claimed 1 trip in 2018–2021 block (only 1 of 2 allowed)
- Carry forward 1 trip to 2022
- In 2022: Can claim 2 trips (1 carry-forward + 1 from new block)

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Consequence |
|---------|------------|
| Claiming international travel as LTA | Not eligible; fully taxable |
| Claiming hotel/food costs | Not eligible; only transport fare |
| Not submitting bills to employer | LTA amount goes as taxable salary |
| Claiming LTA for 3+ children born after Oct 1998 | Third+ child travel cost taxable |
| Claiming business class when economy available | Only economy equivalent is exempt |
| Using LTA in new tax regime | LTA is fully taxable in new regime |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My employer gave me ₹50,000 LTA. I traveled by road (own car) to Shimla. Can I claim LTA?

LTA covers fare for journey by "recognized modes of transport" — public bus, train, air. **Own car travel is NOT eligible**. However, if there's no direct rail/air/bus connection to the destination, the actual fare for the available mode of public transport is allowed. Driving your own car doesn't qualify.

### I'm in the current 2022–2025 block. I haven't claimed LTA at all. I want to claim 2 trips in 2025. Is that possible?

Yes — you can use both your LTA allowances in the final year of the block. Both trips must be actual travel, both in 2025 (FY 2025-26). Submit bills for both. Note that the employer's LTA disbursement policy may require separate applications for each trip.

### Can I claim LTA for my parents' travel to visit me (not for me traveling to visit them)?

LTA is for the **employee's** travel with eligible family. If you travel with your parents, their fare is covered. If your parents travel independently to visit you while you stay home — that's not covered under LTA.
