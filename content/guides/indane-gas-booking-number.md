# Indane Gas Booking Number — How to Book LPG Cylinder Online and Offline

Indane Gas is the LPG brand of Indian Oil Corporation Limited (IOCL), India's largest oil marketing company. If you are an Indane Gas customer, you can book your LPG cylinder refill using multiple methods — missed call, WhatsApp, SMS, IVRS, the Indian Oil website, or the Indane mobile app. This guide covers all Indane gas booking numbers and step-by-step online booking instructions.

## What is Indane Gas?

Indane is the domestic LPG (Liquefied Petroleum Gas) brand marketed by **Indian Oil Corporation Limited (IOCL)**. With over 12 crore active customers and a network of 12,000+ distributors across India, Indane is the country's largest LPG distribution network. Indane provides:

- **Domestic LPG cylinders** — 14.2 kg and 5 kg options
- **Online and offline refill booking** through multiple channels
- **Subsidised and non-subsidised connections** under government schemes like Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY)

## How to Book Indane Gas Cylinder Online

You can book your Indane Gas refill online through the Indian Oil website or the Indane mobile app.

### Method 1: Book Through Indian Oil Website

1. Visit the Indian Oil customer portal at [cx.indianoil.in](https://cx.indianoil.in/)
2. Click on **"LPG"** and then select **"Request for Refill"**
3. Enter your **17-digit LPG Consumer ID** (printed on your subscription voucher or previous delivery receipt)
4. Verify with your **registered mobile number** via OTP
5. Confirm your refill request
6. You will receive a **booking confirmation SMS** with estimated delivery date

### Method 2: Book Through Indane App

1. Download the **Indian Oil One** app from [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.IndianOil.CustomerOne) or Apple App Store
2. Register using your **LPG Consumer ID** and **registered mobile number**
3. Log in and tap **"Book Refill"**
4. Confirm the request and choose your **payment method** (online or cash on delivery)
5. Track your delivery status within the app

## Indane Gas New Connection — How to Apply

If you don't have an Indane Gas connection yet, you can apply for a new one online or offline.

### Apply Online

1. Visit the Indian Oil new connection portal at [cx.indianoil.in](https://cx.indianoil.in/webcenter/portal/LPG/pages_lpgservicenewconnection)
2. Click **"New Connection"** and fill in your personal details
3. Upload required documents:
   - **Proof of Identity** — Aadhaar card, Voter ID, Passport, or Driving Licence
   - **Proof of Address** — Aadhaar card, Electricity bill, Ration card, or Passport
   - **Passport-size photograph**
4. Complete **e-KYC verification** using Aadhaar OTP
5. Make the online payment (security deposit for cylinder and regulator)
6. Choose your nearest distributor for delivery

### Apply Offline

1. Visit your nearest **Indane Gas distributor**
2. Fill the **Subscription Voucher (SV) form**
3. Submit the required documents (ID proof, address proof, photograph)
4. Pay the security deposit
5. The distributor will process your application and deliver the connection

**Estimated Cost:** A new Indane domestic connection costs approximately ₹1,600–₹2,200 (includes security deposit for cylinder, regulator, and installation charges).

## Important Tips for Indane Gas Customers

- **Keep your mobile number updated** — All booking and delivery notifications are sent to your registered mobile number. Visit your distributor to update it if needed.
- **Link your Aadhaar** — Aadhaar linking is mandatory to receive LPG subsidy directly in your bank account via DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer).
- **Book early during peak demand** — During winter months and festive seasons, delivery times may be longer. Book your refill as soon as the cylinder runs low.
- **Use the Indian Oil One app** — The app lets you book refills, track delivery, check subsidy status, and update your details in one place.
- **Check subsidy status** — Visit [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in/) to verify if your LPG subsidy is being credited to your bank account.
- **Report delivery issues** — If your cylinder is not delivered within 7 days of booking, contact customer care at **1800-2333-555**.
