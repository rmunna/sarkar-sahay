## What is MP Vriddha Pension Yojana?

**Vriddha Pension Yojana** (Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme integrated with state top-up) is the Madhya Pradesh government's old age pension scheme providing **₹600 per month** to BPL elderly citizens aged 60 and above.

The ₹600 comprises:
- **Central share (IGNOAPS):** ₹200 (60–79 years) or ₹500 (80+ years)
- **State share (MP government):** ₹400 (60–79 years) or ₹100 (80+ years)
- **Total:** ₹600/month for both age groups

## Pension Amount

| Age | Central (IGNOAPS) | State (MP) | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| 60–79 years | ₹200/month | ₹400/month | **₹600/month** |
| 80+ years | ₹500/month | ₹100/month | **₹600/month** |

**Note:** The total comes to ₹600 in both age groups due to the blended state + central share. Some states and years may see revisions — verify current amounts at the portal.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Age:** 60 years or above
- **Residence:** Permanent Madhya Pradesh resident
- **BPL (Below Poverty Line)** as per state BPL list
- Not receiving any government employee pension (state, central, or Army)
- Not covered under EPFO/NPS retirement pension

## How to Apply

1. Go to **[socialjustice.mp.gov.in](https://socialjustice.mp.gov.in)**
2. Click **"वृद्धावस्था पेंशन योजना"** (Vriddha Pension)
3. Click **"ऑनलाइन आवेदन करें"**
4. Enter Aadhaar + OTP for authentication
5. Fill in:
   - Personal details (name, DOB, address, Samagra ID if available)
   - BPL/income details
   - Bank account details (Aadhaar-linked)
6. Upload documents
7. Submit → District Social Justice office verifies and approves

**Samagra ID:** If you have a Samagra ID (MP family registration), use it — it speeds up verification significantly.

**Offline application:** Visit your nearest **Gram Panchayat** or the district's **Social Justice office (Samaj Nyay Vibhag)**.

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card** (with DOB showing 60+)
2. **BPL ration card** (or other BPL proof)
3. **Bank passbook** (account in applicant's name, Aadhaar-linked)
4. **MP domicile** — Aadhaar with MP address, or voter ID
5. **Samagra ID** (if available — makes verification faster)
6. Passport-size photograph
7. **Age proof** if Aadhaar has incorrect DOB — school certificate, birth certificate

## Samagra Portal (MP's Family Database)

The **Samagra portal** ([samagra.gov.in](https://samagra.gov.in)) is MP's unique family registry. Every MP family and individual has a Samagra ID. Most MP government schemes use this for verification. If your family is not registered on Samagra:
- Apply for Samagra registration at your Gram Panchayat or CSC
- Once registered, pension and other scheme applications become much faster

## Checking Pension Status

1. Go to [socialjustice.mp.gov.in](https://socialjustice.mp.gov.in)
2. Click "पेंशन हितग्राही की स्थिति जानें"
3. Enter Aadhaar, Samagra ID, or application number
4. View payment status and history

OR check your bank account — pension credit shows as DBT.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My father is 65 and BPL but has no Samagra ID. Can he still apply?
Yes — Samagra ID speeds up the process but is not mandatory. Apply with Aadhaar, BPL ration card, and bank passbook. The district office will verify.

### My mother is 80 years old. Will she get more than ₹600?
The total is ₹600 for both age groups (the central share increases for 80+ but the state share decreases proportionally). Some years or revisions may change this — check the current rate on [socialjustice.mp.gov.in](https://socialjustice.mp.gov.in).

### My father receives NPS (National Pension System) from his employer. Is he still eligible?
If he is receiving a monthly NPS pension, he may not be eligible (government pension disqualification). If he received a lump-sum NPS withdrawal but no monthly pension, he may still qualify. Consult the district Social Justice office.

### Can my father apply if he doesn't know how to use a computer?
Yes — apply offline at the Gram Panchayat or CSC (Jan Seva Kendra). Alternatively, you can apply on his behalf online if you have his Aadhaar and bank details.

### The pension is not coming for the last 2 months. What do I do?
Check status on the portal. Common reasons: Aadhaar-bank seeding failure, account inactive, or verification pending. Visit the bank to re-activate and re-link Aadhaar, then inform the district Social Justice office.
