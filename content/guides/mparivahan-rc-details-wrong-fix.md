# mParivahan RC Details Wrong? Fix Vehicle Info Errors

The mParivahan app displays your **vehicle Registration Certificate (RC)** details from the central Vahan database. If the app shows wrong owner name, incorrect address, outdated insurance, or old hypothecation details, the problem is in the RTO database — not the app itself.

This guide explains how to identify and fix wrong RC details on mParivahan.

## Common RC Errors on mParivahan

| Error Type | Likely Cause |
|---|---|
| Wrong owner name | Data entry error at RTO or name not updated after transfer |
| Wrong address | Address change not processed |
| Insurance not updated | Insurer didn't update Vahan database |
| Hypothecation still showing | Bank/financier didn't file HP termination |
| Wrong vehicle colour/model | Data entry mistake at registration |
| Fitness/tax expired | Status not updated after renewal |

## Fix 1: Wrong Name or Address on RC

If your RC shows an incorrect name or old address:

### Online Method (Some States)

1. Visit [vahan.parivahan.gov.in](https://vahan.parivahan.gov.in)
2. Select your **State** and **RTO**
3. Go to **"Online Services"** → **"Change of Address"** or **"Correction of RC"**
4. Enter your **Registration Number**
5. Upload required documents:
   - Aadhaar card (for address)
   - Affidavit (for name correction)
   - Original RC copy
6. Pay the applicable fee (₹200-₹500 depending on state)
7. Submit the application

### Offline Method (Visit RTO)

1. Visit your **registering RTO** with:
   - Original RC book
   - Aadhaar card
   - Address proof (for address change)
   - Affidavit on stamp paper (for name correction)
   - Insurance policy copy
   - PUC certificate
2. Fill **Form 33** (for transfer/correction)
3. Submit at the RTO counter
4. Pay the fee at the cash counter
5. Updated RC will be issued in **7-15 working days**
6. mParivahan will automatically reflect the updated data once the RTO updates Vahan

## Fix 2: Insurance Not Updated on mParivahan

If your RC shows old or expired insurance even though you've renewed it:

**Why this happens:** Insurance companies are required to update policy details in the Vahan database, but many fail to do so promptly.

**How to fix:**

1. **Contact your insurance company** — Call customer care and ask them to update your policy in the Vahan/Parivahan portal
2. **Provide your details:**
   - Vehicle registration number
   - Policy number
   - Policy start and end date
3. **Timeline** — Insurance companies typically update within **3-7 days** after you request
4. **If insurer doesn't update** — File a complaint on the [IRDAI grievance portal](https://igms.irda.gov.in)
5. **Visit RTO as last resort** — Carry your insurance policy to the RTO and request manual update

> **Tip:** When buying or renewing insurance, ask the insurer to confirm that they will update the Vahan database. Some insurers do it instantly through their integration.

## Fix 3: Hypothecation Still Showing After Loan Closure

If you've paid off your vehicle loan but mParivahan still shows "Hypothecated to [Bank Name]":

### Step-by-Step HP Termination

1. **Get NOC from your bank/financier** — After loan closure, request a No Objection Certificate (NOC) and Form 35
2. **Bank should file HP cancellation** — The financier is supposed to file HP termination with the RTO. Ask them to do it
3. **If bank doesn't file**, visit the RTO yourself with:
   - Bank NOC (original)
   - Form 35 signed by the bank
   - Original RC book
   - Loan closure letter
   - Your Aadhaar card
   - Insurance copy
4. **Fill Form 35** at the RTO
5. **Pay the fee** — Approximately ₹200-₹500
6. **RTO will update the Vahan database** and remove the hypothecation entry
7. **Timeline** — 7-15 working days for the update to reflect on mParivahan

### Online HP Termination (Available in Some States)

1. Visit [vahan.parivahan.gov.in](https://vahan.parivahan.gov.in)
2. Select your State → RTO
3. Go to **"HP Termination"** under Online Services
4. Enter your registration number
5. Upload NOC and Form 35
6. Pay the fee online
7. Track status on the same portal

## Fix 4: Wrong Vehicle Details (Model, Colour, etc.)

If the vehicle model, colour, engine number, or chassis number is wrong:

1. This is a **data entry error** made during initial registration
2. **Visit your RTO** with:
   - Original RC book
   - Vehicle invoice showing correct details
   - Insurance policy with correct details
   - Aadhaar card
3. File an application for **"Correction in RC"**
4. The RTO may inspect the vehicle to verify details
5. Corrected RC will be issued in **7-15 days**
6. Fee: ₹200-₹300 approximately

## Fix 5: Verify Your RC Details Online

Before visiting the RTO, verify what the central database shows:

1. Visit [vahan.parivahan.gov.in](https://vahan.parivahan.gov.in)
2. Click **"Know Your Vehicle Details"**
3. Enter your **Registration Number** and complete the captcha
4. The displayed details are exactly what mParivahan shows
5. Note down the specific errors to report at the RTO

> This step helps you confirm the problem is in the database and not just an app glitch.

## How to Contact Your RTO

1. **Find your RTO** — Visit [parivahan.gov.in](https://parivahan.gov.in) → RTO Directory
2. **Visit in person** — Carry all original documents
3. **Timing** — RTOs typically operate **10 AM - 5 PM**, Monday to Saturday (except 2nd Saturday and holidays)
4. **Online grievance** — Some states have online grievance portals on their transport department websites

## Important Tips

1. **Always check Vahan portal first** — Confirm what the database shows before visiting the RTO
2. **Keep photocopies** — Carry photocopies of all documents along with originals
3. **Get acknowledgment receipt** — Always collect an application receipt from the RTO for tracking
4. **Follow up after 15 days** — If the correction hasn't been made, visit the RTO with your receipt
5. **Insurance update is insurer's responsibility** — Don't let them push it back to you

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Will correcting RC at the RTO automatically update mParivahan?

Yes, once the RTO updates your RC details in the Vahan database, mParivahan will automatically show the corrected information. This typically takes 3-7 days after the RTO makes the change.

### How much does RC correction cost at the RTO?

RC correction fees vary by state, typically ranging from **₹200 to ₹500**. Additional charges may apply for smart card RC reissuance.

### Can I correct RC details online without visiting the RTO?

Some states allow online applications for address change and HP termination through the Vahan portal. However, name corrections and vehicle detail corrections usually require an RTO visit.

### My insurance company refuses to update the Vahan database. What can I do?

File a complaint with **IRDAI** (Insurance Regulatory Authority) through their grievance portal at [igms.irda.gov.in](https://igms.irda.gov.in). Insurance companies are mandated to update the Vahan database.

### How long does hypothecation removal take?

From the date of filing at the RTO, HP termination typically takes **7-15 working days** to reflect in the Vahan database and mParivahan app.

### Can wrong RC details cause problems during traffic checks?

Yes, mismatched details between your physical RC and the Vahan database can cause confusion during checks. Get corrections done promptly to avoid issues.
