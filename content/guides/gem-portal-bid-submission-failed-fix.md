# GeM Bid Submission Failed — How to Fix Technical Errors

Submitting bids on the Government e-Marketplace (GeM) is critical for sellers competing for government contracts. But technical errors — document upload failures, session timeouts, EMD payment issues, and format errors — frequently block bid submissions, especially close to deadlines. This guide covers every common bid error and its fix.

## Common Bid Submission Errors

- **"Document upload failed"** — file size/format rejected during bid attachment
- **"Session expired, please login again"** — automatic logout during bid preparation
- **"Invalid bid amount format"** — currency/decimal formatting issues
- **"EMD payment not confirmed"** — Earnest Money Deposit transaction pending
- **"Bid submission time has expired"** — missed the deadline by seconds
- **"Technical error, please try again"** — server-side failure during submission
- **"Incomplete bid — mandatory fields missing"** — required sections not filled

## Fix 1: Document Upload Failed

Document upload is the most common failure point during bidding:

1. **Check file size** — Maximum upload size is typically 5-10 MB per document; compress large PDFs
2. **Use correct format** — GeM accepts PDF, JPG, and PNG; avoid Word documents or ZIP files
3. **File name rules** — Keep file names short, use only letters/numbers/hyphens; avoid special characters (#, &, %)
4. **One file at a time** — Upload documents individually rather than trying multiple simultaneous uploads
5. **Clear browser cache** — Cached data can interfere; clear cache or use incognito mode
6. **Check internet speed** — Slow connections cause upload timeouts; use a stable broadband connection
7. **Compress PDFs** — Use free tools like [smallpdf.com](https://smallpdf.com) or Adobe Acrobat to reduce file size below 5 MB

**Pro tip:** Prepare all documents in the correct format and size before the bid opens. Don't wait until the last day.

## Fix 2: Session Expired During Bid

GeM sessions automatically expire after a period of inactivity:

1. **Session timeout** — GeM sessions typically expire after 15-30 minutes of inactivity
2. **Save progress frequently** — Click "Save as Draft" regularly while preparing your bid
3. **Don't open multiple tabs** — Multiple GeM tabs can conflict and invalidate your session
4. **Avoid browser back button** — Use GeM's internal navigation; the back button can break the session
5. **Login fresh before final submission** — If you've been working for a long time, log out and log back in before clicking Submit
6. **Disable browser extensions** — Ad blockers and privacy extensions can interfere with session cookies

**Recovery steps:**
- If session expires mid-bid, log back in immediately
- Check "My Bids" → "Draft Bids" — your progress may be saved
- If draft is lost, start fresh but work quickly from pre-prepared documents

## Fix 3: Bid Amount Format Error

The bid amount field is strict about formatting:

1. **No commas in numbers** — Enter `150000` not `1,50,000`
2. **Use period for decimals** — Enter `150000.50` not `150000,50`
3. **Maximum two decimal places** — Don't enter more than two digits after the decimal point
4. **No currency symbols** — Don't type ₹ or Rs before the amount
5. **No negative values** — All amounts must be positive
6. **Check unit price vs total** — Ensure you're entering the per-unit price if the form asks for unit rate
7. **GST inclusive/exclusive** — Read carefully whether the bid amount should include or exclude GST

**Common mistake:** Entering the total contract value when GeM asks for per-unit rate. Check the quantity and unit mentioned in the bid document.

## Fix 4: EMD Payment Not Confirmed

Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) is mandatory for many GeM bids:

1. **Pay EMD before bid submission** — EMD must be confirmed before you can submit the bid
2. **Payment modes** — GeM accepts NEFT/RTGS, net banking, and sometimes online payment through the portal
3. **NEFT/RTGS delay** — Bank transfers can take 2-4 hours to reflect; don't wait until the last day
4. **Transaction reference** — After payment, enter the exact transaction reference number on GeM
5. **EMD exemption** — MSME-registered sellers and Startup India-certified sellers may be exempt from EMD; check your eligibility
6. **Check Udyam registration** — If claiming MSME exemption, ensure your Udyam certificate is updated and linked to your GeM profile
7. **Bank account mismatch** — The payment must come from the bank account registered on your GeM seller profile

**If payment is made but not reflecting:**
- Wait 4-6 hours for NEFT/RTGS
- Contact your bank for the UTR number
- Raise a ticket on GeM with payment proof (bank statement, UTR)

## Fix 5: Last-Minute Submission Errors

Submitting bids in the final minutes is risky:

1. **Server load** — GeM servers experience heavy traffic near bid deadlines; response times slow down
2. **Submit at least 2 hours before deadline** — Don't wait for the last moment
3. **Keep bid ready in draft** — Prepare everything and save as draft well in advance
4. **Screenshot everything** — Take screenshots of each step during submission as proof
5. **Note the deadline timezone** — GeM uses IST (Indian Standard Time); confirm the exact closing time
6. **Auto-extension** — Some bids have auto-extension if bids come in the last few minutes; check the bid terms
7. **No grace period** — Once the deadline passes, the system blocks submission permanently

**If you missed the deadline due to technical error:**
- Take screenshots of the error with timestamp
- Raise a grievance on GeM immediately
- Contact the buying organization and explain with proof
- GeM may consider re-opening in genuine technical failure cases (rare)

## Fix 6: General Technical Errors

For generic "Technical error" messages:

1. **Use Google Chrome (latest version)** — GeM is optimized for Chrome
2. **Enable JavaScript and cookies** — Both must be enabled for GeM to work
3. **Disable VPN** — VPN connections can cause authentication issues
4. **Clear cache and cookies** — Go to Chrome Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data
5. **Try incognito mode** — Eliminates extension and cache interference
6. **Check GeM server status** — If the portal is down for maintenance, wait and retry
7. **Stable internet** — Use wired broadband over mobile data for large uploads

## Bid Submission Checklist

Before hitting Submit, verify:

- [ ] All mandatory fields filled (technical and financial sections)
- [ ] Documents uploaded successfully (green checkmarks visible)
- [ ] Bid amount entered correctly (check unit vs total, GST inclusion)
- [ ] EMD payment confirmed or exemption claimed
- [ ] Terms and conditions accepted
- [ ] Digital signature applied (if required for the bid)
- [ ] Preview bid once before final submission
- [ ] Save/download bid confirmation after submission

## Important Tips

1. **Prepare offline first** — Draft your bid responses in a Word document, then copy-paste into GeM
2. **Multiple browser test** — If Chrome fails, try Firefox as a backup
3. **Keep GeM helpline handy** — Call 1800-419-3436 immediately if facing errors near deadline
4. **Check bid corrigendum** — Sometimes bid requirements change via corrigendum; check for updates before submitting
5. **Digital Signature Certificate (DSC)** — If required, ensure your DSC is valid and the browser plugin is installed

## How to Track Bid Status

1. Log into [gem.gov.in](https://gem.gov.in/)
2. Go to **Dashboard** → **My Bids**
3. Check bid status: Draft, Submitted, Under Evaluation, Awarded, Rejected
4. Download bid confirmation PDF for your records

## FAQs

### Can I edit a bid after submission on GeM?
No, once submitted you cannot edit the bid. You can withdraw and resubmit a new bid before the deadline, but the original bid cannot be modified.

### What happens if GeM portal is down during bid closing time?
GeM may extend the bid deadline if there's a verified server outage. Check the bid page for any deadline extension notifications.

### Is EMD refundable if I don't win the bid?
Yes, EMD is refunded to unsuccessful bidders after the bid evaluation is complete. Refund typically takes 7-15 working days.

### Can I submit a bid from my mobile phone?
While GeM is accessible on mobile browsers, bid submission is best done on a desktop/laptop for reliability. Document uploads and digital signatures work better on desktop.

### What is the minimum bid amount on GeM?
There is no universal minimum. The bid amount depends on the specific tender requirements. You must meet the buyer's specifications and offer a competitive price.

### How do I know if my bid was successfully submitted?
After successful submission, you'll see a confirmation page with a bid reference number. You'll also receive a confirmation email. Check "My Bids" in your dashboard to verify.

### Can I participate in multiple bids simultaneously?
Yes, sellers can bid on multiple tenders at the same time. Each bid is independent and requires separate document uploads and EMD (if applicable).
