# WB Swami Vivekananda Merit-cum-Means Scholarship 2025 – Complete Guide

## What is the Swami Vivekananda Scholarship?

The Swami Vivekananda Merit-cum-Means (SVMCM) Scholarship is a merit-based financial assistance scheme by the Government of West Bengal. It provides monthly stipends to meritorious students from economically weaker families pursuing higher education in West Bengal.

The scholarship covers students from **Class 11 onwards** through postgraduate and professional courses. It is one of the most popular scholarship schemes in West Bengal, benefiting lakhs of students every year.

## Who is Eligible?

### Basic Eligibility
- **Domicile:** Must be a resident of West Bengal
- **Institution:** Must be studying in a **recognized institution** within West Bengal
- **Family Income:** Annual family income must not exceed **₹2.5 lakh**
- **Attendance:** Must maintain a minimum of **75% attendance**

### Course-wise Eligibility & Marks

| Course Level | Minimum Marks Required |
|-------------|----------------------|
| Class 11–12 (HS) | 75% in Madhyamik (Class 10) |
| Undergraduate (General) | 53% in HS (Class 12) |
| Undergraduate (Technical/Professional) | Admission through recognized entrance exam |
| Postgraduate | 53% in graduation |
| Diploma/Polytechnic | 75% in Madhyamik |

### Who is NOT Eligible?

- Students with family income above ₹2.5 lakh
- Students receiving another scholarship of equal or higher value
- Students studying outside West Bengal
- Students with attendance below 75%

## Scholarship Amount

| Course Level | Monthly Stipend |
|-------------|----------------|
| Class 11–12 (HS) | ₹1,000/month |
| Undergraduate (General) | ₹1,000/month |
| Undergraduate (Technical/Professional) | ₹1,500/month |
| Postgraduate | ₹2,000/month |
| Diploma/Polytechnic | ₹1,500/month |
| M.Phil/Doctoral | ₹2,000–₹5,000/month |

**Book Grant:** An additional book grant of ₹500 is provided once per year for some categories.

## Documents Required

### Mandatory Documents
- Aadhaar card of the student
- Previous exam marksheet (Madhyamik/HS/Graduation as applicable)
- Admission receipt or enrollment proof in current course
- Family income certificate (issued by competent authority, not exceeding ₹2.5 lakh)
- Bank account passbook (in student's name, linked to Aadhaar)
- Passport-size photograph
- Institution verification certificate

### Additional Documents
- Caste certificate (for SC/ST/OBC candidates)
- Disability certificate (if applicable)
- Domicile certificate (if Aadhaar address is outside WB)

## How to Apply Online – Step-by-Step

### Step 1: Visit the Official Portal
Go to [https://svmcm.wbhed.gov.in](https://svmcm.wbhed.gov.in).

### Step 2: Register as a New Student
- Click on "Student Registration" or "New Registration"
- Enter your personal details, Aadhaar number, mobile number, and email
- Create a username and password
- Verify your mobile number via OTP

### Step 3: Log In and Fill the Application
- Log in with your new credentials
- Fill in the complete application form:
  - Personal details (name, DOB, gender, category)
  - Educational details (previous exam, marks, current enrollment)
  - Family income details
  - Bank account information

### Step 4: Upload Documents
Upload scanned copies of:
- Passport-size photograph (JPG, max 50KB)
- Previous marksheet
- Income certificate
- Aadhaar card
- Bank passbook first page

### Step 5: Submit and Print
- Review all details carefully
- Submit the application
- **Print the application form** for your records and institutional verification

### Step 6: Institutional Verification
Take the printed application to your **college/school office**. The institution head (principal/HOD) must **verify and approve** your application on the SVMCM portal.

### Step 7: Track Your Application
After institutional verification, track your application status at [svmcm.wbhed.gov.in](https://svmcm.wbhed.gov.in) using your login credentials.

### Step 8: Receive Scholarship
Once approved, the monthly stipend is credited directly to your **bank account via DBT**.

## Processing Time

- **Application Window:** Usually July–October (check the portal for exact dates)
- **Institutional Verification:** 2–4 weeks
- **Approval:** 1–3 months after verification
- **Disbursement:** Scholarship is paid for the full academic year, usually in 1–2 installments

## Important Tips

1. **Apply within the deadline** — the portal has strict opening and closing dates. Don't wait until the last day.
2. **Ensure Aadhaar-bank linking** — your stipend comes via DBT. See our [Aadhaar linking guide](/guide/aadhaar-linking-guide).
3. **Maintain 75% attendance** — if attendance drops, the scholarship can be discontinued.
4. **Get institutional verification done quickly** — your application is incomplete until the institution approves it.
5. **Renew every year** — the scholarship must be renewed each academic year by submitting updated marks and enrollment proof.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: What is the family income limit for the SVMCM scholarship?
**A:** The annual family income must not exceed **₹2.5 lakh** from all sources.

### Q2: Can I apply if I'm studying in a private college?
**A:** Yes, as long as the private college is **recognized by the state government** or relevant university in West Bengal.

### Q3: How do I renew my SVMCM scholarship?
**A:** Log in to the [SVMCM portal](https://svmcm.wbhed.gov.in) during the renewal window, update your marks and enrollment details for the new academic year, and submit for institutional verification again.

### Q4: Can I get this scholarship along with other scholarships?
**A:** Generally, you **cannot hold two scholarships** simultaneously. However, if the other scholarship is of a lower value, you may be allowed to retain the SVMCM one. Check the latest guidelines.

### Q5: What happens if my attendance falls below 75%?
**A:** Your scholarship may be **suspended or discontinued**. Maintain regular attendance to keep receiving the stipend.

### Q6: I missed the application deadline. Can I apply late?
**A:** The portal usually does not accept applications after the deadline. Watch for any extended deadlines announced on the website.

### Q7: Is there a separate application for SC/ST students?
**A:** No. SC/ST students apply on the **same portal**. However, they may have relaxed criteria for marks. Upload your caste certificate as an additional document.
