# GeM Portal Payment Not Received — How to Track & Fix

Getting paid on time after delivering goods or services through GeM is a common pain point for sellers. Payment delays can occur due to PFMS integration issues, invoice acceptance delays, bank account mismatches, and processing cycle gaps. This guide helps you track your payment and resolve delays.

## Common Payment Issues

- **"Invoice not accepted by buyer"** — buyer hasn't confirmed receipt/quality
- **"PFMS payment processing"** — stuck in Public Financial Management System
- **"Bank account verification failed"** — account details mismatch on PFMS
- **"Payment rejected by treasury"** — government treasury returned the transaction
- Payment shows "Processed" on GeM but not credited to bank account
- Consignment accepted but payment not initiated for weeks

## Understanding the GeM Payment Cycle

The standard payment flow on GeM:

1. **Seller delivers goods/services** → Generates invoice on GeM
2. **Buyer inspects and accepts** → Consignee Receipt Acceptance (CRAC)
3. **Invoice approved** → Buyer's finance section approves the invoice
4. **PFMS processing** → Payment routed through Public Financial Management System
5. **Bank credit** → Amount credited to seller's registered bank account

**Standard timeline:** 10-30 working days from CRAC acceptance to bank credit, depending on the buying organization.

## Fix 1: Invoice Not Accepted by Buyer

The most common delay — buyer hasn't accepted the consignment:

1. **Check CRAC status** — Log into GeM → Dashboard → Orders → Check if "Consignment Received and Accepted" is marked
2. **Contact the consignee** — The person who received the goods must log into GeM and mark acceptance
3. **10-day deemed acceptance** — If the buyer doesn't act within 10 days of delivery, the system may auto-accept (check bid terms)
4. **Quality inspection pending** — Some orders require quality inspection before acceptance; follow up with the buyer
5. **Partial acceptance** — Buyer may accept partial quantity; check if there's a quantity dispute
6. **Raise a ticket** — If the buyer is unresponsive after 10+ days, raise a ticket on GeM support

**Pro tip:** After delivery, immediately call the consignee and request them to complete CRAC on GeM. Don't wait passively.

## Fix 2: PFMS Integration Issues

PFMS (Public Financial Management System) handles the actual payment:

1. **PFMS registration** — Your bank account must be verified on PFMS; check at [pfms.nic.in](https://pfms.nic.in/)
2. **Bank account mismatch** — The bank account on GeM must exactly match PFMS records (name, IFSC, account number)
3. **IFSC code changed** — If your bank branch merged or IFSC changed, update on both GeM and inform the buyer
4. **PFMS validation pending** — New accounts take 3-5 working days to get validated on PFMS
5. **PFMS server issues** — PFMS occasionally has downtime; payment may be queued and will process when servers recover
6. **Check PFMS status** — Visit [pfms.nic.in](https://pfms.nic.in/) → "Know Your Payment" to check if payment was initiated

**If PFMS shows payment processed but not credited:**
- Check with your bank — the NEFT/RTGS may be pending on their side
- Verify account number — even one wrong digit will cause a bounce
- Contact GeM helpline with UTR number from PFMS

## Fix 3: Payment Cycle Delays

Government payment cycles have inherent timelines:

1. **Budget availability** — Payments depend on the buying department's budget allocation; year-end (March) is especially slow
2. **Financial year transition** — Payments for March deliveries may spill into April-May due to account closing
3. **Treasury processing** — State government payments route through state treasuries which have their own timelines
4. **Central vs State** — Central government payments via PFMS are generally faster (10-15 days); state payments vary
5. **Batch processing** — Some departments batch-process payments weekly or fortnightly, not daily
6. **Multiple approvals** — Large-value orders may require multi-level approval before payment release

## Fix 4: Raise a Payment Ticket on GeM

If payment is overdue beyond 30 days from CRAC acceptance:

1. Log into [gem.gov.in](https://gem.gov.in/) with your seller account
2. Go to **Help** → **Raise a Ticket**
3. Select category: **"Payment Related"**
4. Enter details:
   - Order number / Contract ID
   - Invoice number and date
   - CRAC acceptance date
   - Expected payment amount
   - Bank account details on GeM
5. Attach supporting documents — delivery challan, invoice copy, CRAC screenshot
6. Submit and note the **ticket number** for follow-up
7. Follow up after **5 working days** if no response

## Fix 5: Seller Dashboard Payment Tracking

Track your payment status from the seller dashboard:

1. **Login** → Go to **Dashboard** → **My Orders**
2. Click on the specific **order number**
3. Check the **payment tab** — it shows the current payment stage:
   - Invoice Generated
   - Invoice Accepted
   - Payment Initiated
   - Payment Processed
   - Payment Credited
4. **Download payment advice** — Once processed, download the payment advice for your records
5. **Check "My Payments"** — Separate section showing all payment transactions with dates

## When to Escalate

Escalate beyond regular tickets if:

- Payment pending for **45+ days** after CRAC acceptance
- Multiple tickets raised with no resolution
- Buyer organization is unresponsive

**Escalation options:**
- **GeM Helpline:** 1800-419-3436 (mention ticket number)
- **Email:** helpdesk-gem@gov.in
- **Buying organization's finance officer** — contact directly with order details
- **GeM Grievance Cell** — available on the GeM portal for serious delays
- **CPGRAMS** — For Central government buyers, file on [pgportal.gov.in](https://pgportal.gov.in/)

## Important Tips

1. **Verify bank details regularly** — Check that your GeM profile bank account matches PFMS; update immediately if you change banks
2. **Don't delay invoicing** — Generate the invoice on GeM immediately after delivery confirmation
3. **Follow up proactively** — Call the buyer's accounts section 7 days after CRAC acceptance
4. **Keep delivery proof** — Maintain signed delivery challans, photos, and POD receipts
5. **MSME advantage** — MSME sellers are entitled to payment within 45 days under MSMED Act; cite this if delayed

## FAQs

### What is the standard payment timeline on GeM?
Payment should be credited within 10-30 working days from consignment acceptance (CRAC). Central government payments are typically faster than state government payments.

### Can I charge interest on delayed payments?
MSME sellers registered under MSMED Act can claim interest on payments delayed beyond 45 days. The interest rate is three times the bank rate notified by RBI.

### Why does my payment show "Processed" but I haven't received money?
"Processed" means GeM/PFMS has initiated the transfer. Bank credit takes 1-3 additional working days for NEFT/RTGS. If not received after 3 days, check with your bank and verify your account details.

### What if the buyer rejects my invoice?
If the invoice is rejected, check the rejection reason on GeM. Common reasons include quantity mismatch, wrong amount, or missing documentation. Correct the issue and resubmit the invoice.

### Do I need a separate PFMS registration?
No, GeM automatically creates your vendor profile on PFMS using your bank details. However, you should verify your details are correctly reflected on PFMS.

### What happens to payment if the financial year ends?
If payment is pending at the financial year-end (March 31), it may be re-validated in the new financial year. This can cause additional delays of 2-4 weeks in April.

### Can I receive payment in a different bank account?
Payment goes only to the bank account registered on your GeM seller profile. To change it, update your bank details on GeM and wait for re-verification (3-5 working days).
