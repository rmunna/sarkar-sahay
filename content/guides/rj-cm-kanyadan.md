## What is Mukhyamantri Kanyadan Yojana?

Mukhyamantri Kanyadan Yojana is a marriage assistance scheme by the Government of Rajasthan that provides **financial aid** to families from economically weaker sections for the marriage of their daughters. The scheme helps reduce the financial burden of marriage on BPL, SC/ST, minority, and widow families.

### Benefits

| Category | Assistance Amount |
|----------|------------------|
| SC/ST/Minority BPL families | ₹31,000 – ₹51,000 |
| BPL general category | ₹31,000 |
| Widows' daughters | ₹31,000 – ₹51,000 |
| Women players (sports) | ₹31,000 – ₹51,000 |

The assistance is given in two parts:
- **₹21,000** as a fixed deposit/bank transfer to the bride
- **₹10,000-₹30,000** for marriage expenses (varies by category)

> Exact amounts may vary based on latest government notifications.

## Who is Eligible?

- The **bride must be 18 years or above** at the time of marriage
- The **groom must be 21 years or above**
- Family must be **BPL** or belong to SC/ST/minority communities
- Annual family income must not exceed **₹50,000** (BPL threshold)
- Must be a **resident of Rajasthan**
- The benefit is for **first two daughters** of a family
- Marriage must be registered
- Applicable for **widow's daughter**, **orphan girl**, or girl from specified categories

## Documents Required

- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) of bride and groom
- Jan Aadhaar card
- Age proof of bride (18+) and groom (21+)
- BPL ration card / income certificate
- Caste certificate (for SC/ST/minority applicants)
- Marriage registration certificate or invitation card
- Bank passbook of the bride
- Passport-size photographs of bride and groom
- Father's/mother's death certificate (for orphans/widow's daughter)

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Application Timing

- Apply **at least 1 month before** the marriage date, OR
- Apply **within 6 months after** the marriage

### Online Application

1. **Visit** [SJE Rajasthan portal](https://sje.rajasthan.gov.in) or apply through E-Mitra
2. **Login** with SSO ID
3. **Select** Mukhyamantri Kanyadan Yojana
4. **Fill** the form with bride, groom, family, income, and marriage details
5. **Upload** all required documents
6. **Submit** and note application number
7. **Track** on the portal

### Through E-Mitra

1. **Visit** nearest E-Mitra kiosk
2. **Carry** all original documents
3. **Operator fills** and submits the application
4. **Collect** receipt

### Offline Application

1. **Visit** District Social Justice Office
2. **Collect** application form
3. **Fill** and attach documents
4. **Submit** and collect acknowledgement

## Processing Time

- Verification: **1-2 months**
- Disbursement: **2-3 months** after approval
- Amount via DBT to bride's bank account

## Fees

**No fees** for application.

## Important Tips

1. **Apply before marriage** — Applying before the marriage date gives more time for processing
2. **Register the marriage** — Marriage registration is important for the application
3. **Minimum age compliance** — Bride must be 18+ and groom 21+; underage marriages are illegal and not supported
4. **Only two daughters** — Benefits available for first two daughters per family
5. **Combine with other schemes** — Check if eligible for state or central marriage-related benefits

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Can I apply after the marriage?
Yes, you can apply **within 6 months** after the marriage date.

### Q2. Is this scheme only for BPL families?
Primarily for BPL families and SC/ST/minority communities. The income threshold is ₹50,000 per year.

### Q3. Can the groom's family apply?
No. The application must be submitted by the **bride's family** or the bride herself.

### Q4. What if I have three daughters?
The scheme covers the **first two daughters** only.

### Q5. Is the money given to the bride or the family?
The financial assistance is credited to the **bride's bank account**.

### Q6. Can inter-caste marriages apply?
Yes. Inter-caste marriages are eligible, and additional benefits may be available under separate inter-caste marriage schemes.
