# Telangana Scheme Renaming 2024 — Congress vs BRS Scheme Names

When the **Congress party** (under CM A. Revanth Reddy) came to power in Telangana in December 2023 (defeating K. Chandrashekhar Rao's BRS), many flagship welfare schemes were renamed, restructured, or modified. If you were receiving benefits under BRS-era scheme names and are confused about continuity, this guide maps old names to new names.

## Key Telangana Scheme Renamings (2024 Onwards)

| Old Name (BRS/KCR Era) | New Name / Status (Congress 2024) | Benefit |
|------------------------|----------------------------------|---------|
| **KCR Kit (Pregnancy Kit)** | **Bangaru Thalli** | Pregnant women delivery kit + cash |
| **Aasara Pension** | Modified / **Indiramma Pension** (proposed) | ₹3,500/month — increased under Congress |
| **Rythu Bandhu** | **Rytu Bharosa** | Farmer input subsidy (amount modified) |
| **Rythu Bima** | Continuing as Rythu Bima | Farmer life insurance |
| **KCR Ration Shops** | Continuing (rebrand) | Food distribution |
| **Kalyanalakshmi / Shaadi Mubarak** | **Kalyana Lakshmi + Shaadi Mubarak** | Marriage assistance (amounts modified) |
| **Dalit Bandhu** | Under review / Modified | SC entrepreneur grant scheme |
| **TS iPass / Business** | Continuing under TG-iPass | Industrial approvals |
| **Telangana Housing (KCR era)** | Modified under 2BHK program | Free housing |
| **Mid-Day Meal (Jagananna style)** | Continuing under new name | School nutrition |
| **Basthi Dawakhana** | Modified / continuing | Free health clinics |

> **Note**: Unlike Andhra Pradesh (where TDP renamed almost all YSRCP schemes), Telangana Congress kept several BRS schemes with minor modifications and new branding — changes are more selective than in AP.

## Pension: The Biggest Change

**Aasara Pension** was the BRS flagship welfare scheme:
- **Old amount (BRS)**: ₹2,016/month (Old Age, Widow, Disabled)
- **Congress promise**: Increase to ₹4,016/month (₹2,000 increase)
- **Current status (2026)**: Pension continues; partial increase implemented; final amount depends on state budget
- **Portal**: tgsocialwelfare.cgg.gov.in

If your pension stopped or reduced after December 2023, visit your **MeeSeva centre** or **Gram Panchayat** to verify your current enrollment.

## Rythu Bandhu → Rytu Bharosa

- **Old**: Rythu Bandhu — ₹10,000/acre/year to farmers (2 installments of ₹5,000)
- **New**: Rytu Bharosa — Congress modified the scheme
  - **Amount**: ₹7,500/acre/year (reduced from ₹10,000 due to fiscal constraints)
  - **Who gets it**: Small and marginal farmers prioritized
  - **Check status**: [rythubharosa.telangana.gov.in](https://rythubharosa.telangana.gov.in/)

Many farmers are asking why the amount decreased — this is a policy change, not a technical error.

## KCR Kit → Bangaru Thalli

- **Old**: KCR Kit — healthcare items + nutrition for pregnant women at delivery
- **New**: Bangaru Thalli — same concept, rebranded under the new government
- **Cash benefit**: ₹13,000 in installments for SC/ST/BC pregnant women
- **Who to contact**: Your nearest government hospital (PHC) or CDPO office

## Kalyanalakshmi / Shaadi Mubarak — Both Continuing

These two marriage assistance schemes continue under Congress:
- **Kalyanalakshmi**: For SC/ST brides — ₹1,01,116 (updated amounts)
- **Shaadi Mubarak**: For BC/Minority brides — amounts revised
- **Portal**: [tskalyanamasthu.gov.in](https://tskalyanamasthu.gov.in/)
- Applications through MeeSeva

## Dalit Bandhu — Status

The KCR-era Dalit Bandhu (₹10 lakh grant for SC families to start businesses) was one of India's largest SC welfare schemes:
- **Current status**: Under Congress review; phased implementation continues for previously approved beneficiaries
- **New applications**: Check at your district SC welfare office or tsdalitbandhu.telangana.gov.in

## How to Continue Receiving Benefits Under New Names

1. **No re-application needed** for most existing beneficiaries — the government migrated beneficiary data
2. **If payment stopped**: Visit your nearest MeeSeva or Gram Panchayat with Aadhaar + bank passbook
3. **New applications**: Apply under the new scheme names at MeeSeva centres
4. **Bank account/Aadhaar link issues**: Same fix as always — visit bank with Aadhaar for NPCI mapper seeding

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My Aasara pension stopped after the government changed. How do I restart?

Pension continued for most existing beneficiaries after December 2023. If yours stopped, likely reasons: bank account issue, Aadhaar seeding problem, or a verification drive. Visit MeeSeva with Aadhaar + bank passbook. The staff will flag your case for revival.

### Did Congress stop Rythu Bandhu for rich farmers?

Congress modified Rythu Bandhu → Rytu Bharosa to focus on smaller landholdings and reduced the per-acre amount from ₹10,000 to ₹7,500. Large commercial farms may have been deprioritized. Check eligibility at [rythubharosa.telangana.gov.in](https://rythubharosa.telangana.gov.in/).

### How is Telangana's scheme renaming different from Andhra Pradesh?

Andhra Pradesh (TDP) did near-complete renaming of all YSRCP schemes (YSR names → NTR/Chandra Babu names). Telangana Congress kept several BRS schemes with same names or minor name changes, as they were popular and politically sensitive (e.g., Kalyanalakshmi, Shaadi Mubarak retained). The changes are selective rather than wholesale.

### I was on the waiting list for Dalit Bandhu. Will I still get it?

Previously approved Dalit Bandhu beneficiaries continue to be processed. New waiting list applicants should inquire at the district SC welfare office — the program continues in a phased manner but the original timelines under KCR are not being followed.
