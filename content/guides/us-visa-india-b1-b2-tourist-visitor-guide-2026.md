# US B1/B2 Visa from India 2026 — Complete Guide

The US B1/B2 visa is the most commonly sought US visa from India — for tourism, family visits, business meetings, or medical treatment. The process involves multiple steps across several weeks. Here's the complete guide.

---

## US Visa Types — Which One Do You Need?

| Visa Type | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| **B1** | Business: meetings, conferences, contract negotiations (NOT employment) |
| **B2** | Tourism: sightseeing, family visits, medical treatment, weddings |
| **B1/B2** | Combined (most commonly issued) — used for both tourism and business trips |
| F-1 | Student visa (full-time academic study) |
| H-1B | Work visa (requires employer sponsorship) |
| J-1 | Exchange visitor, research |

**For most personal travel from India**: Apply for B1/B2 combined.

## Are You Eligible for Dropbox? (Interview Waiver)

Dropbox allows submission of documents via courier without attending an in-person interview if:
- You've previously been issued a US B/F/J/H/L/O visa AND
- Your previous visa was issued when you were 14 years or older AND
- Your most recent visa expired less than **48 months ago** (previously 48 months, check current rules) AND
- Your new application is in the **same or related category**

If eligible: Much faster (2–8 weeks) and avoids the long consulate queue.

**How to check eligibility**: ustraveldocs.com → Interview Waiver Eligibility

## Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1: Fill DS-160 (Online Application Form)

1. Go to [ceac.state.gov/genniv](https://ceac.state.gov/genniv)
2. Select location: India (choose any consulate — you'll visit the one you select)
3. Fill all personal details, travel history, employment, family
4. Upload photo: 2×2 inch, white background, recent (within 6 months)
5. Submit DS-160 — note the **Barcode/Application ID number** (you'll need this for appointment)

**DS-160 tips**:
- Don't leave fields blank — write N/A if not applicable
- Travel history: List all countries visited in last 5 years accurately
- US travel history: List all prior US trips (dates, purpose, duration)
- Employer/income: Be accurate — inconsistency is a red flag at interview

### Step 2: Pay MRV Fee

**Fee (2026)**: USD 185 ≈ ₹15,500 (at current exchange rate; check exact INR amount at ustraveldocs.com)

Payment options: Demand Draft payable to "US Embassy New Delhi" / Online via ustraveldocs.com

**MRV fee is non-refundable** — not returned even if visa is denied.

### Step 3: Create Account at ustraveldocs.com

1. Go to [ustraveldocs.com/in](https://www.ustraveldocs.com/in)
2. Register with your email
3. Enter DS-160 barcode number, passport details
4. Enter fee payment receipt number

### Step 4: Schedule OFC and Consulate Appointments

**Two appointments needed**:
1. **OFC (Offsite Facilitation Center)**: Biometric data collection — fingerprints, photo. OFC appointment should be BEFORE or on the same day as consulate appointment.
2. **Consulate appointment**: Actual visa interview.

At ustraveldocs.com, schedule both in the same session. OFC locations are more widespread than consulates (multiple cities). Consulates: Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata.

**Appointment availability**: May show zero slots for months. Keep checking — cancellations appear and disappear quickly. Use the "check appointment availability" option.

### Step 5: Attend OFC (Biometrics)

Bring to OFC:
- Appointment confirmation printout
- Passport (original)
- DS-160 confirmation page printout
- Photo

**Process at OFC**: 10–15 minutes. Fingerprints scanned, photo taken. No interview here.

### Step 6: Attend Consulate Interview

**Documents to bring** (originals + copies):
- Valid passport (+ any previous passports with US visas)
- DS-160 confirmation page
- Visa fee (MRV) receipt
- OFC appointment confirmation (if they gave you one)
- Photo (same spec as DS-160 photo)
- Supporting documents (see below)

**Interview duration**: 2–5 minutes for most applicants. Quick Q&A with a consular officer.

## Documents Required — Supporting Evidence

### Always Required

| Document | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| Valid passport | Valid for 6+ months beyond intended US stay |
| DS-160 confirmation | Print with barcode |
| MRV fee receipt | Payment proof |
| US visa appointment confirmation | Print from ustraveldocs.com |

### Financial Documents

| Document | What It Shows |
|----------|--------------|
| Bank statements (last 6 months) | Sufficient funds for travel; stability |
| ITR / Form 16 (last 2 years) | Income proof; returns to India for livelihood |
| Salary slips (last 3 months) | Current income |
| Fixed deposits / investments | Financial stability |

**Key**: Show you have money for the trip AND strong financial ties to return.

### Professional/Employment Documents

| Document | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| Leave approval from employer | Employer allows time off; you have a job to return to |
| Employment letter on company letterhead | Confirms employment, position, salary |
| Business registration / CA certificate (self-employed) | Shows established business in India |

### Proof of Ties to India (Critical)

Consular officers want assurance you'll return to India after your US visit:
- Property ownership documents
- Family: spouse's documents, children's birth certificates, school certificates
- Dependent's presence in India while you travel

### Itinerary and Purpose

- Flight booking (tentative — not final; don't book non-refundable before visa)
- Hotel bookings or invitation letter from US host (for family/friend visits)
- Purpose of trip: For tourist visits — must-see list, planned cities; for medical: doctor referral

## Common Reasons for US Visa Rejection

| Rejection Code | Meaning | What To Do |
|----------------|---------|-----------|
| Section 214(b) | Failed to show strong ties to India; suspected immigrant intent | Strengthen ties evidence; wait and re-apply |
| 221(g) | Administrative processing (requires more documents or security check) | Submit requested documents; may take weeks/months |
| Incomplete application | Missing documents | Re-apply with complete documentation |
| Travel history issues | Overstay or violation on previous visa | Consult immigration lawyer |

**214(b) is the most common rejection**: "Your application has been rejected under Section 214(b)" means the officer was not convinced you'll return to India. Rebuild your case — more financial stability, property, family, or career advancement — and re-apply after 6–12 months.

## Emergency/Urgent US Visa — Expedite Request

If you have genuine urgency (medical emergency, funeral, time-sensitive business):
1. Schedule a regular appointment first (even far away)
2. Apply for **Expedite Appointment** at ustraveldocs.com → "Request an Expedited Appointment"
3. Explain urgency with supporting evidence (doctor letter for medical emergency, death certificate for funeral, meeting invitation for urgent business)
4. Expedite requests are at consular officer's discretion — not guaranteed

## Visa Stamp Duration — What to Expect

B1/B2 visa stamp validity varies by consular officer's discretion. Common outcomes for Indian applicants:
- **10-year multiple-entry visa**: Most common outcome for first-time applicants with strong profile
- **1-year multiple-entry**: Common for first-time applicants or those with less established profiles
- **Entry for 6 months**: Standard period of stay per entry (regardless of visa validity stamp)

**Visa validity ≠ stay duration**: A 10-year visa allows multiple entries, each for up to 6 months. Don't overstay — overstay is a serious immigration violation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is dropbox/interview waiver still available for Indians in 2026?

Yes — check eligibility at ustraveldocs.com. Dropbox is significantly faster (2–8 weeks vs 12–24+ months for regular interview). If eligible, always use Dropbox.

### My DS-160 has an error after submission. Can I fix it?

Fill a new DS-160 and use the new barcode number. You can link the new DS-160 to an existing appointment at ustraveldocs.com. The old DS-160 is abandoned.

### Can I apply at a consulate not in my city?

Yes — Indian citizens can apply at any of the 5 consulates (Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata) regardless of their home city. Choose the one with earliest availability.

### I got rejected last time. How soon can I reapply?

No mandated waiting period, but you must show changed circumstances or new information. Applying immediately with the same profile usually leads to same rejection. Wait 6–12 months and address the original rejection reason.
