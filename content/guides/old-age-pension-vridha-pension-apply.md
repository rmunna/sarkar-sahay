# How to Apply for Old Age Pension / Vridha Pension Online — State-wise Guide

Growing old should bring peace, not financial worry. The Indian government — both at the **Central** and **State** level — provides monthly pension to senior citizens so they can live with dignity. This guide walks you through everything: which scheme applies to you, how much pension you will get, and exactly how to apply online.

> **In a hurry?** Jump to your state: [UP](#uttar-pradesh) | [Bihar](#bihar) | [Rajasthan](#rajasthan) | [MP](#madhya-pradesh) | [Maharashtra](#maharashtra) | [Tamil Nadu](#tamil-nadu) | [Karnataka](#karnataka) | [Delhi](#delhi)

## What Is Old Age Pension (Vridha Pension)?

Old Age Pension — also called **Vridha Pension** or **Vriddhawastha Pension** — is a monthly financial assistance given by the government to senior citizens, especially those from economically weaker sections. The aim is simple: **no elderly person in India should go without basic support.**

There are two layers:

1. **Central Government Scheme** — Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS), funded by the Centre
2. **State Government Schemes** — Each state adds its own top-up amount and may have separate pension schemes with higher amounts

Most states combine both into a single monthly payment deposited directly into your bank account via **Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)**.

---

## Central Scheme: Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS)

The **IGNOAPS** is part of the National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP) run by the Ministry of Rural Development.

### Pension Amount

| Age Group | Central Contribution |
|-----------|---------------------|
| **60 to 79 years** | ₹300/month (states typically top up to ₹500+) |
| **80 years and above** | ₹500/month (states typically top up to ₹1,000+) |

> **Note:** The amounts above are the Central share only. Most states add their own contribution, so the actual pension you receive is higher. See the state-wise table below.

### Eligibility for IGNOAPS

- **Age:** 60 years or above
- **Income:** Must belong to a **BPL (Below Poverty Line)** household
- **Residence:** Must be a permanent resident of the state where you apply
- **Not receiving** any other government pension (like EPF pension or government employee pension)

### Documents Required

Keep these documents ready before applying:

| Document | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| **Aadhaar Card** | Identity & DBT verification |
| **Age Proof** | Birth certificate, voter ID, Aadhaar, or school certificate showing DOB |
| **BPL Card / Ration Card** | To prove BPL status |
| **Income Certificate** | From Tehsildar / SDM office (some states require annual income below ₹46,000–₹2,00,000 depending on state) |
| **Bank Passbook** (front page copy) | For pension transfer via DBT |
| **Passport-size Photographs** | 2–3 recent photos |
| **Residence / Domicile Certificate** | Proof of state residency |
| **Mobile Number** (linked to Aadhaar) | For OTP verification and updates |

## How to Apply for Old Age Pension Online — State-wise Steps

### Uttar Pradesh (UP) — Vridha Pension Yojana {#uttar-pradesh}

**Portal:** [sspy-up.gov.in](https://sspy-up.gov.in)

**Eligibility:** Age 60+, annual income below ₹46,080 (rural) or below poverty line, resident of UP.

**Steps to Apply Online:**

1. Visit [sspy-up.gov.in](https://sspy-up.gov.in) and click on **"वृद्धावस्था पेंशन"** (Old Age Pension)
2. Click **"ऑनलाइन आवेदन करें"** (Apply Online)
3. Fill in personal details: name, date of birth, gender, address, Aadhaar number
4. Upload documents: photo, age proof, income certificate
5. Enter bank account details (must be linked to Aadhaar)
6. Submit and note down your **Application Number**
7. Your application will be verified at the **District Social Welfare Office**

**Check Status:** Visit the portal → Click "आवेदन की स्थिति" → Enter your registration number.

**Helpline:** 18004190001 (Toll-free)

---

### Bihar — Mukhyamantri Vridhjan Pension Yojana (MVPY) {#bihar}

**Portal:** [serviceonline.bihar.gov.in](https://serviceonline.bihar.gov.in)

**Eligibility:** Age 60+, resident of Bihar. All senior citizens eligible (not limited to BPL for state scheme). Those already receiving government pension are not eligible.

**Steps to Apply Online:**

1. Visit [serviceonline.bihar.gov.in](https://serviceonline.bihar.gov.in)
2. Go to **"समाज कल्याण विभाग"** → **"मुख्यमंत्री वृद्धजन पेंशन योजना"**
3. Enter Aadhaar number for verification
4. Fill in personal and bank details
5. Upload required documents
6. Submit — you will receive an **acknowledgement receipt**

**Check Status:** Use your application reference number on the same portal under "Application Status."

**Helpline:** 1800-345-6262 (Toll-free)

---

### Rajasthan — Social Security Pension {#rajasthan}

**Portal:** [rajssp.rajasthan.gov.in](https://rajssp.rajasthan.gov.in)

**Eligibility:** Age 55+ (women) / 58+ (men) for state scheme; 60+ for IGNOAPS. Income limit: ₹48,000/year or less. BPL families get priority.

**Steps to Apply Online:**

1. Visit [rajssp.rajasthan.gov.in](https://rajssp.rajasthan.gov.in)
2. You can also apply through **eMitra centres** or **SSO Portal** ([sso.rajasthan.gov.in](https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in))
3. Login/Register on SSO with Jan Aadhaar or Aadhaar
4. Search for "Old Age Pension" and fill in the application form
5. Upload documents: Aadhaar, Jan Aadhaar, bank passbook, photo
6. Submit and save your application ID

**Check Status:** Visit rajssp.rajasthan.gov.in → "Pensioner Status" → Enter PPO number or application ID.

**Helpline:** 0141-5111007, 5111010

---

### Madhya Pradesh — Social Security Pension {#madhya-pradesh}

**Portal:** [socialsecurity.mp.gov.in](https://socialsecurity.mp.gov.in)

**Eligibility:** Age 60+, BPL family or destitute, resident of MP.

**Steps to Apply Online:**

1. Visit [socialsecurity.mp.gov.in](https://socialsecurity.mp.gov.in)
2. Click **"पेंशन हेतु ऑनलाइन आवेदन"**
3. Enter your **Samagra ID** (mandatory for MP residents — get it from [samagra.gov.in](https://samagra.gov.in))
4. Fill personal details, upload documents
5. Application goes to the local body (Gram Panchayat / Nagar Palika) for verification
6. After approval, pension is credited to your bank account

**Check Status:** Visit the portal → "पेंशन की स्वीकृति की स्थिति" → Enter your Samagra ID.

**Helpline:** 0755-2556916

---

### Maharashtra — Shravan Bal Yojana {#maharashtra}

**Portal:** [aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in](https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in)

**Eligibility:** Age 65+ (for state scheme, 60+ for IGNOAPS), annual family income below ₹21,000 (rural) / ₹28,000 (urban), resident of Maharashtra.

**Steps to Apply Online:**

1. Visit [aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in](https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in)
2. Register / Login with mobile number
3. Go to **Social Justice Department** → **Old Age Pension**
4. Fill the form with personal details, age proof, income certificate
5. Upload scanned documents and passport photo
6. Submit — you will get a tracking ID

**Check Status:** Login to AapleSarkar → "Track Application" → Enter application ID.

**Helpline:** 022-22025251

---

### Tamil Nadu — Old Age Pension {#tamil-nadu}

**Portal:** [tnsocialwelfare.tn.gov.in](https://www.tnsocialwelfare.tn.gov.in)

**Eligibility:** Age 60+, destitute or with no regular income, resident of Tamil Nadu for at least 5 years.

**Steps to Apply Online:**

1. Visit [tnsocialwelfare.tn.gov.in](https://www.tnsocialwelfare.tn.gov.in) or apply through **TN e-Sevai centres**
2. Click on **"Pension Schemes"** → **"Old Age Pension"**
3. Fill the application with Aadhaar number, personal details
4. Upload: photo, age proof, income certificate, ration card
5. Application is verified by the **Revenue Department** at Taluk level
6. Approved pension is disbursed via bank transfer or post office

**Check Status:** Visit the portal or contact your nearest **Revenue Divisional Office.**

**Helpline:** 044-25671444

---

### Karnataka — Sandhya Suraksha Yojana {#karnataka}

**Portal:** [sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in)

**Eligibility:** Age 65+, BPL, annual income below ₹17,000 (rural) / ₹22,000 (urban), resident of Karnataka.

**Steps to Apply Online:**

1. Visit [sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in)
2. Register / Login
3. Search for **"Old Age Pension"** or **"Sandhya Suraksha"**
4. Fill the form — provide Aadhaar, BPL card details, bank account
5. Upload documents and submit
6. Application is forwarded to the **Taluk Social Welfare Officer** for approval

**Check Status:** Login to Seva Sindhu → "Track Application."

**Helpline:** 080-22230282

---

### Delhi — Old Age Pension Scheme {#delhi}

**Portal:** [edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in](https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in)

**Eligibility:** Age 60+, resident of Delhi for at least 5 years, annual income below ₹1,00,000.

**Steps to Apply Online:**

1. Visit [edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in](https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in)
2. Register / Login (using Aadhaar or mobile OTP)
3. Go to **"Social Welfare Department"** → **"Old Age Pension Scheme"**
4. Fill the application form with personal details
5. Upload: Aadhaar, age proof, residence proof, income certificate, bank passbook, photo
6. Submit — receive acknowledgement number
7. Verification by SDM office; pension starts after approval

**Check Status:** Login to e-District portal → "Track Application" → Enter application number.

**Pension Amount:**
- Age 60–69: **₹2,000/month**
- Age 70 and above: **₹2,500/month**

**Helpline:** 011-23935730, 1076

## How to Check Old Age Pension Status Online

No matter which state you are in, follow these general steps:

1. **Visit your state's pension portal** (see links above)
2. Look for **"Application Status"** or **"आवेदन की स्थिति"**
3. Enter your **Application Number / Registration Number / Aadhaar Number**
4. Your current status will show: Pending, Under Verification, Approved, or Rejected
5. If rejected, the reason will usually be mentioned — you can re-apply after correcting the issue

### Track Pension Payment

Once approved, you can check if your monthly pension has been credited:

- Check your **bank statement** or **passbook** for DBT credit
- Visit the NSAP portal at [nsap.nic.in](https://nsap.nic.in) for central scheme payment tracking
- Many states also send **SMS alerts** to your registered mobile number
- Submit your [Jeevan Pramaan (Life Certificate)](/guide/jeevan-pramaan-life-certificate-online) annually to ensure pension continues without interruption

## Important Tips for Applicants

### ✅ Do's

- **Link your Aadhaar to your bank account** — this is essential for DBT pension payment
- **Submit Jeevan Pramaan** (Digital Life Certificate) every year — [learn how here](/guide/jeevan-pramaan-life-certificate-online)
- **Keep your mobile number updated** with the bank and Aadhaar
- **Apply early** — verification can take 1–3 months in most states
- **Save your application number** — you'll need it to track status

### ❌ Don'ts

- Don't pay any middleman or agent — applying is **free**
- Don't provide false age or income details — it can lead to rejection and blacklisting
- Don't miss the annual Jeevan Pramaan submission — your pension may be paused

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q: What is the minimum age to apply for old age pension?
**A:** For the central IGNOAPS scheme, the minimum age is **60 years**. Some state schemes allow application from age 55 (women) or 58 (men) — like Rajasthan.

### Q: Can I apply if I am not BPL?
**A:** For the central IGNOAPS scheme, BPL status is required. However, many state schemes (like Delhi, Bihar) have **income-based criteria** instead — so even if you don't have a BPL card, you may be eligible if your income is below the state's threshold.

### Q: How long does it take to get the pension after applying?
**A:** Typically **1 to 3 months** after application, depending on verification by local authorities. Some states are faster with Aadhaar-based instant verification.

### Q: Can I apply offline?
**A:** Yes. You can visit your nearest **Block Development Office, Tehsil Office, District Social Welfare Office**, or **Common Service Centre (CSC)** to apply in person. The CSC operator will fill the online form on your behalf.

### Q: What if my pension stops suddenly?
**A:** This usually happens because of **missed Jeevan Pramaan (Life Certificate)** submission. Submit it immediately via [Jeevan Pramaan portal](/guide/jeevan-pramaan-life-certificate-online) or at your bank. Your pension will resume.

### Q: Is the pension taxable?
**A:** Old age pension under social welfare schemes is generally **not taxable** as it is a welfare benefit, not income from employment.
