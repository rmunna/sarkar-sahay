## What is MA Amrutam & MA Vatsalya Yojana?

**MA Amrutam Yojana (MAA)** and **MA Vatsalya Yojana** are health insurance schemes by the Government of Gujarat that provide **cashless medical treatment** at empanelled hospitals for families below the poverty line and lower-middle-class families.

- **MA Amrutam:** For BPL families (annual income up to ₹1.20 lakh in rural / ₹1.50 lakh in urban)
- **MA Vatsalya:** For families with annual income up to ₹4 lakh (middle class coverage)

Both schemes now operate in convergence with **Ayushman Bharat – PMJAY**, providing coverage up to **₹5 lakh per family per year**.

### Coverage

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Coverage | Up to ₹5 lakh per family per year |
| Procedures | 1,800+ medical and surgical procedures |
| Pre-hospitalization | 15 days |
| Post-hospitalization | 15 days |
| Family members covered | Up to 5 (floater basis) |

## Who is Eligible?

### MA Amrutam (BPL)
- BPL families with annual income ≤ ₹1.20 lakh (rural) / ₹1.50 lakh (urban)
- Must be a Gujarat resident
- Must hold BPL ration card

### MA Vatsalya (Middle Class)
- Families with annual income up to **₹4 lakh**
- Families pay a nominal annual premium of **₹300-₹400** (subsidized)
- Must be a Gujarat resident

### Now Merged with AB-PMJAY
- Families in the SECC list are automatically covered
- No premium for AB-PMJAY eligible families

## Documents Required

- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) of all family members
- BPL ration card / income certificate
- MA Card (if already issued)
- Bank account details
- Passport-size photographs
- For MA Vatsalya: Premium payment receipt

## How to Get MA Card / Avail Benefits

### Getting the MA Card

1. **Visit** the nearest MA card distribution centre, Taluka Health Office, or empanelled hospital
2. **Carry** ration card, Aadhaar, and income certificate
3. **Biometric registration** — fingerprints and photo captured
4. **MA Card issued** on the spot or within a few days
5. **Card covers** up to 5 family members

### Availing Treatment

1. **Visit** any empanelled hospital (government or private)
2. **Show MA Card** and Aadhaar at the reception / Arogya Mitra desk
3. **Biometric verification** of identity
4. **Pre-authorization** — hospital sends request to insurance/TPA
5. **Cashless treatment** — no payment required for covered procedures
6. **Post-discharge** — follow-up covered for 15 days

### Finding Empanelled Hospitals

- Visit [magujarat.com](https://www.magujarat.com) and search hospital directory
- Call helpline: **1800-233-1022** (toll-free) or **104**

## Processing Time

- MA Card issuance: **Same day to 7 days**
- Pre-authorization at hospital: **6-24 hours**
- Treatment: As per medical requirement

## Fees

| Category | Premium |

| MA Amrutam (BPL) | Free |

| MA Vatsalya (₹1.5L-₹4L income) | ₹300-₹400/year |

## Important Tips

1. **Get MA Card made** — Even if you're AB-PMJAY eligible, having the MA Card makes hospital verification faster
2. **Carry Aadhaar always** — Biometric verification requires Aadhaar
3. **Use only empanelled hospitals** — Non-empanelled hospitals are not covered
4. **No middlemen** — Approach the Arogya Mitra directly; don't pay agents
5. **Report overcharging** — Call **1800-233-1022** if any hospital demands money for a covered procedure

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. What is the difference between MA Amrutam and MA Vatsalya?
MA Amrutam is **free** for BPL families. MA Vatsalya covers middle-class families (income ₹1.5-4 lakh) with a **small annual premium** of ₹300-400.

### Q2. Can I use the MA Card outside Gujarat?
Under AB-PMJAY convergence, you can avail cashless treatment at **any empanelled hospital across India**.

### Q3. Are pre-existing diseases covered?
Yes. There is **no waiting period** for pre-existing conditions.

### Q4. What if I don't have an MA Card?
If you're in the SECC/BPL list, you can still avail treatment using **Aadhaar-based verification** at empanelled hospitals.

### Q5. How many family members are covered?
Up to **5 family members** per card on a floater basis (total coverage shared among members).

### Q6. Is dialysis covered?
Yes. Regular dialysis is covered under the scheme at empanelled centres.
