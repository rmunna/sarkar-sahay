## What is Bihar Mukhyamantri Cycle Yojana?

**Mukhyamantri Cycle Yojana** is the Bihar government scheme that provides a **free bicycle** (or **₹3,000 cash** for purchase) to **girl students enrolling in Class 9** in government schools. The scheme aims to increase girls' attendance in secondary school by solving the transportation problem — girls in rural areas often drop out because schools are far from home.

Key features:
- **Free bicycle** (or ₹3,000 cash to buy one) for girls joining Class 9
- Only for **government school students**
- Automatically eligible — **no separate application needed** (school handles it)
- Bihar state funded scheme covering all 38 districts

> **Note:** A similar scheme also covers **boys** in some districts / for Class 9 boys in government schools. Check with your school.

## Who is Eligible?

- Must be a **girl student**
- Must be **newly enrolled in Class 9** in a **government / government-aided school in Bihar**
- Must be a **resident of Bihar**
- Attendance must be regular (school certifies attendance)

No income limit — all girl students in Class 9 at government schools are eligible.

## How to Apply (or Get the Benefit)

**No separate application is needed by the student.** Here's how it works:

1. You **enroll in Class 9** at a government school in Bihar
2. Your **school collects the list** of eligible girls (new Class 9 students)
3. School submits the list to the **District Education Office**
4. Government either:
   - Distributes **bicycles** at the school (through empanelled vendors), OR
   - Transfers **₹3,000 directly to the student's bank account** (DBT) for the student to buy locally
5. Student receives bicycle or ₹3,000 — typically **within 3 months** of the academic year start

## Documents Required (School Submits on Your Behalf)

- Aadhaar card
- Class 8 marksheet / pass certificate (to prove passing Class 8)
- Bank account details (for ₹3,000 DBT)
- School enrollment form (Class 9 admission)

If you don't have a bank account, open a **Jan Dhan account** at any bank with your Aadhaar.

## How to Check Status

1. Ask your **school's head teacher** or Class 9 form teacher about the cycle yojana status for your batch
2. Check at [educationbihar.gov.in](https://educationbihar.gov.in) under scholarship/welfare schemes
3. Or visit the **District Education Office** in your district

## Bihar Girls' Education Incentive Schemes — Full Picture

Bihar has multiple schemes to keep girls in school:

| Class | Scheme | Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Class 1–2 | Uniform scheme | Free school uniform |
| Class 9 | **Cycle Yojana** | **Free bicycle / ₹3,000** |
| Class 10 pass | Mukhyamantri Balika Protsahan | ₹10,000 incentive |
| Class 12 pass (1st div) | Mukhyamantri Balika Protsahan | ₹25,000 incentive |
| Graduation pass | Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana | ₹50,000 |

All Bihar girl education schemes are managed through the **Bihar government education portal** ([educationbihar.gov.in](https://educationbihar.gov.in)) and the **Medhasoft portal** for scholarship payments.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I study in a private school. Am I eligible?
No — Mukhyamantri Cycle Yojana is only for students in **government and government-aided schools**.

### I passed Class 9 last year and didn't get a cycle. Can I apply now?
The cycle is given at the time of **Class 9 enrollment**. If you missed it, contact your school immediately. Schools can submit missed cases in some states.

### The school said cycle will come but it has been 8 months. What to do?
Contact the **District Education Officer (DEO)** of your district. Ask for the status of cycle distribution for your school's Class 9 batch.

### Can I get ₹3,000 cash instead of physical bicycle?
Yes — the state often gives ₹3,000 DBT to the student's bank account so they can buy a cycle locally. The mode (physical bicycle or cash) depends on how your district is implementing it for the year. Ask your school.

### I am a boy. Am I eligible?
Mukhyamantri Cycle Yojana primarily targets **girls**. However, some years the scheme is extended to boys in government schools as well. Check with your school and district education office.
