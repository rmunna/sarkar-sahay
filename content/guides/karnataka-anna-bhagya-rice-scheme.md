## What is Karnataka Anna Bhagya Scheme?

The Anna Bhagya Scheme is a flagship food security programme of the Government of Karnataka that provides **free rice to Below Poverty Line (BPL) families**. Launched in 2013, it supplements the central government's National Food Security Act (NFSA) by providing additional rice at zero cost.

Under this scheme, each eligible family member receives **10 kg of rice per month free of cost** through the state's Public Distribution System (PDS) network. This is over and above the subsidised grains available under NFSA. The scheme aims to ensure that no family in Karnataka goes hungry.

## Who is Eligible?

To benefit from the Anna Bhagya scheme, you must meet the following criteria:

- **BPL ration card holder** — Must hold a valid BPL (Below Poverty Line) or Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) ration card issued by Karnataka
- **Resident of Karnataka** — Must be a permanent resident of the state
- **NFSA beneficiary** — Your family must be covered under the National Food Security Act
- **Annual income** — Family income should be below the BPL threshold as defined by the state government
- **Aadhaar linked** — Ration card must be linked to Aadhaar for biometric authentication at Fair Price Shops

### Who is NOT Eligible?

- Families holding APL (Above Poverty Line) ration cards
- Government employees and their families
- Income tax payees
- Families owning more than 2 hectares of irrigated land

## Documents Required

### Mandatory Documents
- BPL or AAY ration card (Karnataka-issued)
- Aadhaar card of all family members
- Aadhaar-linked mobile number (for OTP verification)

### Supporting Documents (if applying for new ration card)
- Proof of residence in Karnataka (electricity bill, rent agreement)
- Income certificate from Tahsildar
- Passport-size photographs
- Caste certificate (if applicable for priority category)

## How to Get Benefits — Step-by-Step

### Collecting Rice from Fair Price Shop (Monthly Process)

1. **Visit your assigned Fair Price Shop (FPS)** — Check your ration card for the shop name and number
2. **Carry your ration card and Aadhaar** — Both are mandatory for biometric verification
3. **Biometric authentication** — Place your finger on the ePoS (Electronic Point of Sale) machine for Aadhaar-based verification
4. **Collect your rice** — You will receive 10 kg rice per eligible member listed on your card
5. **Verify the quantity** — Ensure the shopkeeper weighs the rice correctly in front of you
6. **Get receipt** — Collect the printed receipt from the ePoS machine as proof of collection

### If You Don't Have a Ration Card (New Application)

**Online Process:**
1. Visit the AHARA portal at [ahara.kar.nic.in](https://ahara.kar.nic.in)
2. Click on "New Ration Card Application"
3. Fill in family details, income, and address
4. Upload required documents (Aadhaar, income certificate, photo)
5. Submit the application and note the reference number
6. Your application will be verified by the local Food Inspector
7. After verification, the ration card will be issued within 30 days

**Offline Process:**
1. Visit the nearest **Taluk Office** or **Food and Civil Supplies Department office**
2. Collect the ration card application form
3. Fill in all details and attach photocopies of documents
4. Submit the form to the designated officer
5. An inspector will visit your residence for verification
6. Ration card will be delivered within 30–45 days after approval

## Benefits Under the Scheme

| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Rice per person | 10 kg per month (free) |
| NFSA rice (additional) | 5 kg per person at ₹3/kg |
| Eligible card types | BPL and AAY |
| Distribution channel | Fair Price Shops via ePoS |

## Processing Time

- **Monthly rice collection** — Available from the 1st to the last day of each month
- **New ration card** — 30 to 45 days from application
- **Aadhaar seeding** — 7 to 15 days for linking Aadhaar to ration card

## Important Tips

1. **Always verify weight** — Ensure the FPS shopkeeper weighs rice in front of you. Report discrepancies to the Food Inspector.
2. **Keep Aadhaar updated** — If biometric authentication fails, visit the nearest Aadhaar centre to update your biometrics. See our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) for details.
3. **Collect within the month** — Uncollected rice for a month cannot be carried forward.
4. **Report corruption** — Call the Karnataka Food Helpline at **1967** or **1800-425-9339** to report issues.
5. **Check portability** — Under One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC), you can collect grains from any ePoS-enabled FPS across India. See our [ration card guide](/guide/ration-card-apply-online) for more.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: How much rice does each person get under Anna Bhagya?
Each eligible BPL/AAY ration card holder gets **10 kg of rice per month completely free**. This is in addition to the 5 kg per person at ₹3/kg under NFSA.

### Q2: Can I get Anna Bhagya benefits if I moved to Karnataka recently?
You need a Karnataka-issued BPL ration card. If you have moved from another state, you must first surrender your old ration card and apply for a new one in Karnataka. The process takes 30–45 days.

### Q3: What if biometric authentication fails at the Fair Price Shop?
If your fingerprint doesn't work, try iris-based authentication. If that also fails, the FPS dealer can use an exception handling process. Visit your nearest Aadhaar centre to update biometrics if the problem persists.

### Q4: Is Anna Bhagya different from the central ration scheme?
Yes. The central government provides 5 kg rice at ₹3/kg under NFSA. Anna Bhagya is a **state government top-up** that provides an additional 10 kg rice free. Both benefits go to BPL families.

### Q5: How do I check if my ration card is eligible for Anna Bhagya?
Visit [ahara.kar.nic.in](https://ahara.kar.nic.in) and search using your ration card number. You can also check at your local FPS or Taluk office.

### Q6: Can APL card holders get Anna Bhagya benefits?
No. The scheme is exclusively for BPL and AAY ration card holders. APL families can benefit from other PDS schemes at subsidised rates.

### Q7: What should I do if the FPS says "no stock"?
Report the issue immediately to the **Food Inspector** of your taluk or call the helpline at **1967**. Stock allocation is tracked digitally, and every FPS is mandated to maintain adequate supply.
