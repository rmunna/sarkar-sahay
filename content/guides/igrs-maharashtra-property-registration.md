# Maharashtra IGRS — Property Registration Online, Stamp Duty & e-Search (2026)

Buying property in Maharashtra? Every sale deed, gift deed, or lease agreement must be registered with the **Inspector General of Registration and Stamps (IGRS)** department through the official portal — **igrmaharashtra.gov.in**. This guide walks you through the entire process: stamp duty, online e-Filing, slot booking, Sub-Registrar office visit, and property search.

See our [property registration guide](/guide/property-registration-online) for general information.

---

## What Is IGRS Maharashtra?

The **Inspector General of Registration and Controller of Stamps (IGR)** is the Maharashtra state authority responsible for:

- Registration of property documents (sale deeds, agreements, leases, gift deeds)
- Collection of stamp duty and registration fees
- Maintenance of property records (Index-II)
- Publication of Annual Statement of Rates (Ready Reckoner / ASR)
- e-Search facility for public property record access

The department operates through **Sub-Registrar Offices (SROs)** across all districts, with online services available at [igrmaharashtra.gov.in](https://igrmaharashtra.gov.in).

## Stamp Duty Rates in Maharashtra (2026)

Stamp duty in Maharashtra varies by **location, property type, and gender** of the buyer.

### Stamp Duty for Property Sale (Conveyance Deed)

| Area | Male Buyer | Female Buyer |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| Mumbai (BMC area) | 6% | 5% |
| Mumbai Suburban | 6% | 5% |
| Navi Mumbai, Thane, Pune (Municipal Corp. areas) | 7% | 6% |
| Pimpri-Chinchwad | 7% | 6% |
| NMMC / TMC / PCMC areas | 7% | 6% |
| Other Municipal Corporation areas | 7% | 6% |
| Rural / Gram Panchayat areas | 7% | 6% |

> **Note:** The 1% concession for female buyers applies when the property is purchased **solely in a woman's name** or as the **first-named buyer** in joint ownership. The above rates include the base stamp duty + Local Body Tax (LBT) surcharge + metro cess where applicable.

### Stamp Duty Breakdown (Example: Pune Municipal Corporation)

| Component | Rate |
|-----------|------|
| Base stamp duty | 5% |
| Municipal surcharge (LBT) | 1% |
| Metro cess | 1% |
| **Total** | **7% (6% for women)** |

### Registration Charges

| Property Value | Registration Fee |
|---------------|-----------------|
| Up to ₹30 lakh | 1% of property value |
| Above ₹30 lakh | ₹30,000 (capped) |

> Registration fee is **capped at ₹30,000** regardless of property value. This is the same for all buyers.

### Stamp Duty for Other Document Types

| Document Type | Stamp Duty |
|--------------|-----------|
| Gift Deed (family member) | 3% |
| Gift Deed (non-family) | Same as conveyance |
| Lease Agreement (≤ 5 years) | 0.25% of total rent |
| Leave & Licence (11 months) | 0.25% of total licence fee |
| Power of Attorney (property) | 5% |
| Partition Deed | 4% |

## Documents Required for Property Registration

Prepare these documents before starting the online process:

### Mandatory Documents

1. **Original sale deed / agreement** (drafted by your advocate)
2. **PAN card** of all parties (buyer and seller)
3. **Aadhaar card** of all parties
4. **Passport-size photographs** (2 each for buyer, seller, and witnesses)
5. **Property card / 7/12 extract** (for land)
6. **Previous sale deed** or chain of title documents
7. **Stamp duty payment receipt** (e-Stamp or franking)
8. **Society NOC** (for resale flats in cooperative housing societies)
9. **Encumbrance certificate** (recommended)
10. **Two witnesses** with valid photo ID

### Additional Documents (If Applicable)

- Builder-Buyer Agreement (for new flats)
- RERA registration certificate
- Commencement Certificate / Occupancy Certificate
- Bank loan sanction letter (if loan-funded purchase)
- Power of Attorney (if signing through PoA holder)

## Step-by-Step: Online Property Registration Process

Maharashtra uses the **iSarita 2.0** system. Here's the complete flow:

### Step 1: Pay Stamp Duty

You can pay stamp duty through:

- **e-Stamp (e-SBTR):** Purchase from authorised SHCIL centres or online via `pay2igr.igrmaharashtra.gov.in`
- **Franking:** Pay at authorised bank branches (HDFC, SBI, etc.)
- **Physical stamp paper:** Available at licensed vendors (less common now)

> **Tip:** e-Stamp is the fastest and most secure method. You get a unique certificate number that can be verified online.

### Step 2: Public Data Entry (PDE) — e-Filing

1. Visit **[pdeigr.maharashtra.gov.in](https://pdeigr.maharashtra.gov.in)**
2. Register / login with your credentials
3. Select your **district and Sub-Registrar Office (SRO)**
4. Choose the **document type** (e.g., Sale Deed, Agreement to Sale)
5. Enter details:
   - Property description (survey number, CTS number, flat number, area)
   - Party details (buyer, seller — name, PAN, Aadhaar, address)
   - Consideration amount (sale price)
   - Stamp duty and registration fee details
6. Upload the **scanned document** (PDF of the drafted deed)
7. Submit and note the **PDE number / token number**

### Step 3: Book Appointment Slot (eStepin)

1. After PDE submission, use the **eStepin** facility
2. Select your SRO and choose an available **date and time slot**
3. Confirm the appointment — you'll receive an SMS/email with the token
4. Print the appointment slip

> **Important:** Walk-ins are generally not accepted. Slot booking is mandatory in most urban SROs.

### Step 4: Visit the Sub-Registrar Office (SRO)

On your appointment date:

1. **All parties must be present** — buyer, seller, and 2 witnesses
2. Carry **original documents** plus one photocopy set
3. Carry valid **photo ID** (Aadhaar preferred) for biometric verification
4. The SRO staff will verify your PDE data and documents
5. **Biometric capture** — thumb impression and photograph of all parties
6. **Document scanning** — the original deed is scanned into the system
7. Pay **registration fee** (if not already paid online)
8. Receive the **registration number** and acknowledgment

### Step 5: Collect Registered Document

- The registered document is usually available for collection within **15–30 minutes** at computerised SROs
- You'll receive the original deed with the registration endorsement stamped on it
- A digital copy is stored in the IGRS database and accessible via e-Search

## e-Search: How to Search Property Records Online

The **e-Search** portal lets you check property transaction history, previous owners, and encumbrance status.

### How to Use e-Search

1. Visit **[esearchigr.maharashtra.gov.in](https://esearchigr.maharashtra.gov.in)**
2. Select search type:
   - **By document details** (registration year, document number)
   - **By party name** (buyer or seller name)
   - **By property details** (survey/CTS number, village)
3. Select **district and SRO**
4. Enter search criteria and submit
5. Results show: document type, registration date, parties, property description

### e-Search Fees

| View Type | Fee |
|-----------|-----|
| Index-II view (summary) | ₹15 per record |
| Certified copy request | ₹50+ depending on pages |

> **Tip:** e-Search records are available from **2000 onwards** for most SROs. For older records, you'll need to visit the SRO in person.

## Ready Reckoner Rates (Annual Statement of Rates)

The **Ready Reckoner (ASR)** is the government-determined minimum property value used for stamp duty calculation. Even if your actual transaction price is lower, stamp duty is calculated on the **Ready Reckoner value or the transaction value — whichever is higher**.

### How to Check Ready Reckoner Rates

1. Visit **[igrmaharashtra.gov.in/eASR](https://igrmaharashtra.gov.in/eASR)**
2. Select: **Division → District → Taluka → Village/Zone**
3. Choose property type (residential, commercial, industrial, land)
4. View the rate per square metre

### Key Facts About ASR

- Published **annually** (usually effective from January each year)
- Rates vary by **zone, road, and property type** within the same city
- Mumbai typically has the highest rates in the state
- ASR rates generally increase 3–10% year-on-year

## How to Check Encumbrance (Property Search)

Checking encumbrance ensures the property has no pending loans, disputes, or legal claims.

### Methods to Check

1. **e-Search portal** — Search by property details to see all registered transactions
2. **Encumbrance Certificate (EC)** — Apply at the SRO with property details
3. **Bank verification** — If taking a home loan, the bank's legal team conducts independent encumbrance checks

### What to Look For

- Any existing **mortgage or charge** on the property
- Pending **litigation or court orders**
- Multiple sale deeds for the **same property** (fraud indicator)
- **Unpaid property tax** liens
- **Society dues** or maintenance arrears

## Common Issues and Solutions

### 1. PDE Rejection or Errors
**Problem:** Data mismatch between PDE form and actual documents.
**Solution:** Double-check all names (as per PAN/Aadhaar), property details, and amounts before submission. You can edit PDE data before the SRO visit.

### 2. Slot Not Available
**Problem:** No appointment slots available at your preferred SRO.
**Solution:** Try early morning (slots open around 10 AM for future dates). Consider nearby SROs in the same district — any SRO within the district can register your property.

### 3. Biometric Failure
**Problem:** Fingerprint not matching during biometric verification.
**Solution:** Carry an alternate biometric method. If fingerprints are worn (elderly/labourers), the SRO may accept iris scan or manual override with additional ID proof.

### 4. Stamp Duty Shortfall
**Problem:** SRO says stamp duty paid is less than required (usually due to Ready Reckoner value being higher).
**Solution:** Pay the deficit amount at the SRO. You can pay the additional stamp duty via demand draft or online.

### 5. Property Not Found in e-Search
**Problem:** Old transactions don't appear.
**Solution:** Records before 2000 may not be digitised. Visit the SRO in person and request a manual search or certified copy.

### 6. Name Mismatch Between Documents
**Problem:** Name on sale deed differs from PAN/Aadhaar.
**Solution:** Carry a gazette notification for name change, or an affidavit of name correction. Match the name exactly as per PAN card in the deed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can NRIs register property in Maharashtra?**
Yes. NRIs can register through a **Power of Attorney (PoA)** holder. The PoA must be notarised and apostilled from the country of residence. The PoA holder attends the SRO on behalf of the NRI.

**Q: Is property registration mandatory for all agreements?**
Yes. Under the Registration Act, 1908, any document transferring immovable property worth more than ₹100 must be registered. Unregistered documents are not admissible as evidence in court.

**Q: How long is a Leave & Licence agreement valid without registration?**
Leave & Licence agreements up to 60 months can be registered. However, agreements exceeding 12 months should always be registered for legal protection.

**Q: Can I register property online without visiting the SRO?**
No. Physical presence at the SRO is mandatory for biometric verification of all parties. The e-Filing (PDE) and slot booking are done online, but the final registration requires an in-person visit.

**Q: What happens if I don't pay stamp duty or pay less?**
The document can be **impounded** by the registrar. A penalty of up to **2% per month** (maximum 400% of the deficit amount) can be levied under the Maharashtra Stamp Act.
