## Who is a Minor for Passport Purposes?

For passport purposes, a **minor** is any person below **18 years of age**. Minors are issued passports with a validity of **5 years** or until they turn 18 (whichever is earlier), unlike adult passports which are valid for 10 years.

Minors cannot apply for passports on their own — the application must be submitted by a **parent or legal guardian**.

## Types of Minor Passports

### Based on Age

| Age Group | Passport Type | Validity |
|-----------|-------------|----------|
| Below 15 years | Minor passport (no ECR stamp) | 5 years or until 18 |
| 15-17 years | Minor passport (36 or 60 pages) | 5 years or until 18 |
| Newborn to 1 year | Minor passport | 5 years or until 18 |

### Key Differences from Adult Passport
- **Shorter validity** — 5 years or until age 18
- **No signature required** for children below 10 years (thumb impression used)
- **Both parents' consent** generally required
- **Address proof** from parents is acceptable
- **Police verification** may or may not be required depending on documents

## Documents Required

### Essential Documents

**For the Child:**
- **Birth certificate** issued by Municipal Corporation / Gram Panchayat
- **Aadhaar card** of the child (if available)
- **School ID card** or bonafide certificate (if school-going)
- **Photographs** (as per passport photo specifications)

**For Parents/Guardian:**
- **Passport of both parents** (or at least one parent)
- **Aadhaar card** of the applying parent
- **Marriage certificate** of parents
- **Address proof** — Aadhaar, voter ID, utility bill, or bank statement

### Additional Documents for Special Cases

**Single Parent:**
- Court order granting custody (if divorced/separated)
- Death certificate of the deceased parent (if widowed)
- **Annexure C** (if applying on the authority of one parent)

**Adopted Child:**
- Adoption deed registered under applicable law
- Court order of adoption

**Born Out of Wedlock:**
- Annexure G (declaration by the applying parent)
- Only the applying parent's details appear on the passport

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Step 1: Register Online
1. Visit [passportindia.gov.in](https://passportindia.gov.in)
2. Click on **"New User? Register Now"** (or login if already registered)
3. Create an account using your (parent's) email ID

### Step 2: Fill the Application Form
1. Log in and click **"Apply for Fresh Passport"**
2. Select **"Minor"** as the applicant type
3. Fill in the child's details — name, date of birth, gender
4. Enter parent details — both parents' names, passport numbers
5. Enter the applying parent's address (used as the child's address)
6. Select the **Passport Seva Kendra (PSK)** for appointment
7. Choose the type of application:
   - **Normal:** 36-page booklet
   - **Jumbo:** 60-page booklet (for frequent travelers)

### Step 3: Pay the Fee
- Pay online through net banking, credit/debit card, or SBI branch challan
- Fee depends on normal or tatkal processing

### Step 4: Book Appointment
- Select an available date and time at your chosen PSK
- Download and print the appointment confirmation

### Step 5: Visit the PSK
- Arrive at the PSK with the **child**, **both parents** (or the applying parent), and all original documents
- **The child must be physically present** at the PSK
- Process at PSK:
  1. **Token counter** — appointment verification
  2. **Counter A** — document submission and scanning
  3. **Counter B** — verification by Passport Officer
  4. **Counter C** — approval (if granted same day)
  5. **Biometrics** — photograph captured at PSK; fingerprints for children above 5-10 years (varies)

### Step 6: Police Verification (If Required)
- Not all minor passport applications require police verification
- If submitted with birth certificate + parents' passports + non-ECR category, police verification may be waived
- If required, a police constable visits your registered address

### Step 7: Receive Passport
- If approved, passport dispatched via **Speed Post**
- Track on [passportindia.gov.in](https://passportindia.gov.in) using your file number

| Type | Timeline |

## Important Situations

### Divorced Parents
- The applying parent must have **legal custody** (court order)
- File **Annexure C** (passport can be issued with one parent's details only)
- The other parent's consent is not needed if custody order is provided
- Passport will show only the custodial parent's name

### One Parent is an NRI
- The NRI parent's passport copy is required
- If the NRI parent cannot be present at PSK, an authorization letter (notarized) is needed
- The child's application can be submitted by the parent present in India

### Newborn Baby Passport
- Apply as soon as you have the **birth certificate**
- No minimum age — passports can be issued to newborns
- Required for international travel with the baby
- Baby must be physically present at the PSK

## Important Tips

- **Both parents should visit the PSK** — while not always mandatory, it avoids complications; if one parent can't come, carry an authorization letter
- **Birth certificate is non-negotiable** — ensure it's issued by the correct authority (municipal/panchayat)
- **Child's presence is mandatory** — the child must be at the PSK, regardless of age (even infants)
- **Address proof** — the child's address is taken as the parent's address; ensure it matches across documents
- **Renew before expiry** — minor passports have shorter validity; track the expiry date and renew 6 months before it expires

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a newborn baby get a passport?
Yes, there is no minimum age requirement. A newborn can get a passport once the birth certificate is issued. The passport is valid for 5 years or until the child turns 18.

### Do both parents need to sign the application?
Both parents' details are required on the form. If one parent is unavailable, the applying parent must file Annexure C (giving consent on behalf of both parents or explaining the situation).

### My child's school name differs from the birth certificate name. What to do?
Use the name as on the birth certificate for the passport application. If there's a genuine discrepancy, get the birth certificate or school records corrected first.

### Can a grandparent apply for a minor's passport?
Only parents or legal guardians can apply. If grandparents have legal guardianship (court order), they can apply as legal guardians.

### When does a minor passport need to be renewed?
Minor passports expire after 5 years or when the child turns 18 (whichever is earlier). After turning 18, the child must apply for a fresh adult passport (re-issue is not possible for minor-to-adult conversion).
