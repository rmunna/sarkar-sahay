# Caste Certificate Apply Online 2026 — All States

A **caste certificate** (जाति प्रमाण पत्र / Community Certificate) is an official document issued by the state government certifying that you belong to a specific Scheduled Caste (SC), Scheduled Tribe (ST), or Other Backward Class (OBC) community. It is required for reservations in education, government jobs, scholarships, and welfare schemes.

> **Quick summary:** Find your state below — each guide has the official portal link, exact documents needed, step-by-step process, and processing time.

## What Is a Caste Certificate Used For?

- **Government job reservation** — UPSC, SSC, state PSC, railways (SC/ST/OBC quota)
- **OBC Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) certificate** — income + caste for central OBC reservation
- **Education reservations** — engineering, medical, law college (SC/ST/OBC seats)
- **NEET state quota counselling** — community certificate required in every state
- **Post-matric scholarships** — NSP, state SC/ST/OBC scholarships
- **Welfare scheme eligibility** — SC/ST/OBC-specific government schemes
- **Age relaxation** — SC/ST/OBC candidates get relaxation in government jobs

## Common Documents Required (All States)

- **Aadhaar card** — mandatory identity proof
- **Ration card** — shows family caste/community
- **Father's school leaving certificate (TC)** — caste listed in school records
- **Self-declaration of caste** — online affidavit or stamp paper
- **Voter ID** — additional address proof
- **Passport-size photograph**

> **Key document across all states:** Your **father's SSLC/school TC or old ration card showing caste** is the primary caste proof. Revenue officials verify community from these ancestral documents.

## General Process (Common Flow Across States)

1. **Go to your state's portal** (see table above)
2. **Register** with mobile number / Aadhaar
3. **Select** "Revenue Department" → "Caste Certificate"
4. **Fill form** — name, address, community declaration, father's details
5. **Upload documents** — Aadhaar, father's TC, ration card, photo
6. **Pay fee** — typically ₹15–60 depending on state
7. **Submit** — note Application Reference Number
8. **Track** — Revenue Inspector verifies at field level
9. **Download** — digitally signed PDF once approved

## OBC vs Central OBC NCL — Important Distinction

| Certificate | Used For | Income Required |
|------------|----------|----------------|
| **State OBC/BC/SEBC Certificate** | State government jobs, state college admissions, state scholarships | No (just community proof) |
| **Central OBC NCL Certificate** | Central government jobs (UPSC/SSC), IIT/NIT/central colleges | Yes — income < ₹8 lakh/year |
| **SC/ST Certificate** | Both state and central purposes (same certificate) | No income limit |

> If you are applying for **UPSC, SSC CGL, or IIT/NIT via JEE**, you need the **OBC NCL certificate** (not just the state OBC certificate). Apply for OBC NCL at the same state portal — it is a separate service.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is state caste certificate valid for central government jobs?

**SC/ST certificates**: Yes — valid for both state and central purposes. **OBC certificates**: No — for central government jobs (UPSC, SSC, railways), you specifically need an **OBC Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) certificate** that confirms income < ₹8 lakh/year. Apply for OBC NCL separately at your state portal.

### Caste certificate validity — how long is it valid?

**SC/ST caste certificates**: Typically **permanent** — issued once and valid for life (for state job reservations). **OBC NCL certificate**: Valid for **1 year** (income-based, must be renewed annually). **For admissions** (NEET, KEAM, EAMCET): the counselling authority often requires a certificate **issued in the current academic year** — check counselling guidelines.

### Can I get a caste certificate if I'm a convert to Christianity / Islam?

**Scheduled Caste converts**: SC status is restricted to **Hindus, Sikhs, and Buddhists** (per Presidential Order 1950). SC converts to Christianity or Islam lose SC status. However, some states have separate BC/OBC categories for "Scheduled Caste converts" (e.g., BC-C in Telangana, BCM in Tamil Nadu). For ST: religion does not affect ST status.

### My community is in the state OBC list but not in the central OBC list?

This is common. The state OBC list and central OBC list are different. For state benefits: use state OBC certificate. For central benefits (UPSC, SSC, IIT): check the central OBC list at [ncbc.nic.in](https://ncbc.nic.in). If your community is not in the central list, you cannot claim central OBC reservation even with a state OBC certificate.

### How to check if my community is in OBC/SC/ST list?

- **Central SC/ST lists**: [tribal.nic.in](https://tribal.nic.in) and MOMA website
- **Central OBC list**: [ncbc.nic.in](https://ncbc.nic.in) → OBC list by state
- **State OBC/BC list**: Check your state government official website or ask at Tehsil office
