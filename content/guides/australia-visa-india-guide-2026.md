# Australia Tourist Visa (Subclass 600) from India 2026 — Complete Guide

Australia is a major destination for Indian tourists, students, and business travelers. Unlike some countries, Australia doesn't use VFS Global for Indian applicants — everything is done online at the Australian government portal. Here's the complete guide.

---

## Australian Visa Overview for Indians

| Visa Subclass | Purpose | Max Stay per Visit |
|---------------|---------|------------------|
| **600 (Visitor)** | Tourism, family visit, business visits | 3 months (up to 12 months in some approvals) |
| **500 (Student)** | Full-time study at CRICOS-registered institution | Duration of course |
| **500 (Student Guardian)** | Accompanying minor student | Duration of child's course |
| **189/190 (Skilled Migration)** | Permanent Residency via points-based system | Permanent |

**For most Indian tourists**: Subclass 600 Visitor Visa.

**No ETA for Indians**: Australia's ETA (Electronic Travel Authority, subclass 601) is available only for passport holders of certain countries — Indian passport holders cannot get ETA. You must apply for the full Visitor Visa (600).

---

## Australia Visitor Visa Fee (2026)

| Visa Type | Fee |
|-----------|-----|
| Subclass 600 (Visitor) | AUD 190 (₹10,000–11,000 approx) |

**Fee is non-refundable** — even if visa is refused.

There is no VFS Global fee for Australian visa — you apply directly online. However, some applicants use migration agents (additional fee of ₹5,000–20,000).

---

## How to Apply — Fully Online

All Australian visa applications from India are submitted online:

1. Go to [immi.homeaffairs.gov.au](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au)
2. Click "Apply for a visa" → Select "Visitor" → Subclass 600
3. Create an ImmiAccount (free registration)
4. Complete the form online — personal details, travel history, purpose, finances
5. Upload all documents
6. Pay AUD 190 via credit/debit card
7. Submit and note reference number

**No appointment needed** — everything is uploaded online.

## Documents Required

### Core Documents

| Document | Details |
|----------|---------|
| **Passport** | Valid for entire stay + 6 months buffer; upload biographical page (photo page) |
| **Photo** | Passport-size, recent, white background |
| **Travel itinerary** | Day-by-day plan of cities and activities |
| **Flight bookings** | Round-trip itinerary (refundable booking is fine) |
| **Hotel/accommodation bookings** | All nights in Australia |

### Financial Documents (Highly Scrutinized)

Australia immigration takes a thorough look at finances:

| Document | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| **Bank statements (6 months)** | All accounts — savings, current, FD. Show AUD 5,000+ (₹2.75L+) available |
| **Salary slips (last 3 months)** | Current income proof |
| **ITR (last 2 years)** | Annual income — shows sustainable earnings |
| **FD/investment statements** | Shows financial stability beyond salary |
| **Property documents** | Home ownership shows ties to India |

**AUD 5,000 benchmark**: Unofficial but commonly discussed. For longer stays (3 months), AUD 8,000–10,000 is safer to show.

### Employment / Purpose Documents

| Type | Documents |
|------|----------|
| **Salaried** | Employment letter on company letterhead (role, salary, leave approved, confirms employment continues), employer details including ABN is NOT needed |
| **Business owner** | Business registration, ITR, CA income certificate |
| **Student in India** | Enrollment letter, previous academic records, parents' financial docs |
| **Retired** | Pension proof, superannuation or savings proof |

### Ties to India (Critical)

Australian immigration applies a "genuine visitor test" — you must prove you intend to return to India:
- Employment (employer confirms ongoing employment)
- Business/assets in India
- Dependent family members in India
- Property ownership in India
- Financial commitments in India (EMIs, investments)

---

## The Genuine Visitor Test — How Australia Assesses Your Application

Australia explicitly evaluates whether you are a genuine temporary entrant:
- **Do you have strong reasons to return to India?**
- **Are your claimed purpose and documents consistent?**
- **Is your financial situation credible for the stated purpose?**

**Strong application signals**:
- Consistent salary reflected in bank statements and ITR
- Specific, realistic itinerary (not vague "visiting Australia")
- Family or property in India
- Previous international travel to countries you've returned from

**Weak application signals**:
- Large cash deposits in account just before applying
- No international travel history
- No clear employment or income source
- Vague itinerary

## Visa Grant vs Visa Validity

When granted, your subclass 600 typically shows:
- **Grant date**: When visa was issued
- **Travel window**: Period within which you must first enter Australia (often 12 months from grant)
- **Stay duration**: How long you can stay per visit (typically 3 months)
- **Multiple entry**: Usually granted as multiple-entry within the travel window

**Example**: Visa granted June 2026 → Travel window until June 2027 → Can visit multiple times → Stay up to 3 months per visit.

## Australia vs Other Countries — Why Applications Sometimes Fail

Australia has one of the more thorough visa assessment processes:
- **Higher rejection rates** than Japan, Singapore, or Schengen for some applicant profiles
- **No interview** — entirely based on documents
- Refusal does not have mandatory waiting period, but you must address the reason

### Most Common Rejection Reasons for Indians

| Reason | Prevention |
|--------|-----------|
| Insufficient financial evidence | 6 months of statements, not just 3; show savings trend |
| No credible employment | Employer letter must be detailed and on proper letterhead |
| Weak ties to India | Property, family, ongoing business; make India ties explicit in cover letter |
| Inconsistent information | Name spelling, DOB must match exactly across all documents |
| Previous visa violation (any country) | Declare and explain; hiding is far worse |

## After Visa Grant — Practical Tips

- **Health insurance**: Australia has excellent healthcare but it's expensive without insurance. Travel insurance with medical coverage is strongly recommended (not mandatory for tourist visa)
- **Customs**: Australia is extremely strict about biosecurity — declare all food, plant material, animal products
- **COVID rules**: Check current health requirements at immi.homeaffairs.gov.au before travel

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I work in Australia on a tourist visa?

No — subclass 600 visitor visa does not permit work. Working on a tourist visa is a serious immigration violation and can lead to cancellation and future bans. For work: you need subclass 482 (Temporary Skill Shortage) or similar employer-sponsored visa.

### Is an Australian visa sufficient for New Zealand travel?

Australian visa holders (specifically those with valid Australian permanent visa) can enter New Zealand visa-free. However, a regular tourist subclass 600 does not qualify for NZ visa exemption. Indians need a separate New Zealand visa.

### Is a cover letter required?

Not strictly mandatory but highly recommended. A well-written cover letter explaining: who you are, why you're visiting Australia, your itinerary, your ties to India, and how you'll finance the trip — significantly helps your application.

### Australia refused my visa. Can I reapply immediately?

You can reapply immediately but must address the specific refusal reason mentioned in the refusal letter. Australian immigration provides reasons for refusal — read carefully and strengthen those specific areas before reapplying.

### Does India-Australia free trade agreement affect visa requirements?

The ECTA (Economic Cooperation and Trade Agreement) between India and Australia doesn't directly simplify tourist visas. It mainly affects trade and some skilled migration pathways.
