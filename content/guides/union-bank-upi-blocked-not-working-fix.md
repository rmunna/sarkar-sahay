# Union Bank UPI Blocked or Not Working? Fix Guide (2026)

Union Bank of India UPI works through the **Vyom app** and third-party apps like PhonePe, Google Pay, BHIM. If your Union Bank UPI is failing or blocked, here's how to fix it.

---

## Why Is Union Bank UPI Failing?

| Issue | Fix |
|-------|-----|
| Wrong UPI PIN 5 times | Reset UPI PIN |
| Daily limit (₹1 lakh) hit | Wait till midnight |
| Bank account frozen/blocked | Fix account block first |
| Vyom app UPI not registered | Register in Vyom app |
| Bank server down | Try after 30 minutes |

---

## Reset Union Bank UPI PIN

### Via Union Bank Vyom App
1. Open **Vyom** app → **UPI** → **Manage UPI PIN**
2. Select bank account → **"Change UPI PIN"** or **"Forgot UPI PIN"**
3. Enter: Debit card last 6 digits + expiry
4. OTP on registered mobile → New 6-digit UPI PIN set

### Via PhonePe / Google Pay
1. App → Profile → Bank Accounts → Select Union Bank
2. **"Forgot UPI PIN"** → Debit card details → OTP → New PIN

---

## Register Union Bank UPI on Vyom App

If UPI was never set up:

1. Vyom → **UPI Services** → **Register/Activate UPI**
2. Select your Union Bank account
3. Mobile number verified (must match bank-registered number)
4. Set UPI PIN with debit card
5. Done — use Union Bank UPI from any UPI app

---

## Union Bank UPI via Third-Party Apps

Union Bank accounts work on all UPI apps. If UPI is working on one app but not another:

- App-specific issue: Reinstall the problem app
- Check: Bank account properly linked in that app
- Reset UPI PIN within the failing app

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Union Bank UPI fails on PhonePe but works on Vyom — why?**
PhonePe may have an old/expired bank mandate. Remove Union Bank from PhonePe → Re-add → Reset UPI PIN. Each app independently registers the UPI account.

**Union Bank daily UPI limit — how much?**
₹1,00,000 per day per bank account. Resets at midnight.

**My Union Bank account is fine but UPI says "account blocked" — why?**
UPI may have a separate flag. Call 1800-22-2244 with account details — they can check UPI-specific blocks.
