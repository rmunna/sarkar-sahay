# How to Download e-EPIC (Digital Voter ID Card) — Step-by-Step Guide

The Election Commission of India (ECI) launched the **e-EPIC (Electronic Electors Photo Identity Card)** to give voters a digital version of their voter ID card. You can download it as a PDF with a secure QR code — no need to wait for the physical card.

This guide covers how to download e-EPIC from the official portal and Voter Helpline app, common errors, and whether e-EPIC is valid as ID proof.

---

## What is e-EPIC?

e-EPIC is the **digital version of your EPIC (Voter ID Card)** issued by the Election Commission of India. Key features:

- **PDF format** with a secure QR code containing your details
- Contains your photo, name, EPIC number, date of birth, and constituency details
- Can be stored on your phone and shown digitally
- Protected by a QR code that can be verified by officials
- Available for download from the official ECI portal and Voter Helpline app

> **Note:** e-EPIC is not a separate document — it is the digital copy of your existing voter ID card.

---

## Who Can Download e-EPIC?

You can download e-EPIC if you meet **all** these conditions:

1. **Your name is on the electoral roll** — You must be a registered voter
2. **Your photo is on record** with the Election Commission — If your voter ID was issued without a photo, e-EPIC won't be available
3. **Your mobile number is linked** to your voter ID — OTP verification is required for download

If your mobile number is not linked, you'll need to update it first. See our [guide on changing mobile number in voter ID](/guide/voter-id-mobile-number-change-fix).

## How to Download e-EPIC from voters.eci.gov.in (Online Portal)

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Go to** [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in/) in any browser
2. **Click on "Login/Register"** — Register if you're a first-time user
3. **Register using your mobile number** linked to your voter ID
4. **Verify OTP** sent to your registered mobile number
5. **Complete your profile** by entering your EPIC number or Form reference number
6. **After login**, go to **"Download e-EPIC"** from the dashboard menu
7. **Select your EPIC number** if multiple entries exist
8. **Verify via OTP** again (security verification)
9. **Click "Download e-EPIC"** — The PDF will be downloaded to your device

> **Tip:** The downloaded PDF file name usually contains your EPIC number. Save it safely on your phone or cloud storage.

## How to Download e-EPIC from Voter Helpline App

The Voter Helpline app is available on both Android and iOS.

### Step 1 — Download the App

- **Android:** [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=in.eci.citizen) — Search "Voter Helpline"
- **iOS:** [Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/voter-helpline/id1456535004) — Search "Voter Helpline"

### Step 2 — Register and Login

1. Open the app and tap **"Register"**
2. Enter your **mobile number** (must be linked to your voter ID)
3. Verify the **OTP** sent to your mobile
4. Complete your profile with your **EPIC number** or personal details

### Step 3 — Download e-EPIC

1. After login, tap on **"e-EPIC Download"** from the home screen
2. Verify your identity with **OTP**
3. Tap **"Download"** — The PDF will be saved to your phone
4. You can also view it directly within the app

## e-EPIC vs Physical EPIC Card — What's the Difference?

| Feature | e-EPIC (Digital) | Physical EPIC Card |
|---|---|---|
| **Format** | PDF with QR code | Plastic/laminated card |
| **Availability** | Instant download | Delivered by BLO (takes weeks) |
| **Contains photo** | Yes | Yes |
| **Contains QR code** | Yes (digitally verifiable) | Newer cards have it |
| **Accepted as ID proof** | Yes, by most agencies | Yes, universally accepted |
| **Can be stored on phone** | Yes | No |
| **Replacement if lost** | Download again anytime | Must apply for duplicate |

## Is e-EPIC Valid as ID Proof?

**Yes.** The Election Commission of India has confirmed that e-EPIC is a valid identity document. It is accepted by:

- **Banks** — For KYC and account opening (most banks accept it)
- **Government offices** — For identity verification
- **Airports** — For domestic air travel
- **Exam centres** — For competitive exam identity verification
- **Telecom companies** — For SIM card purchases

However, some private organizations or older government offices may still ask for the physical card. If you face rejection, you can request a physical EPIC card.

## How to Get a Physical EPIC Card

If e-EPIC is not sufficient and you need a physical voter ID card:

1. **Contact your BLO (Booth Level Officer)** — They distribute physical EPIC cards in your area
2. **Visit your District Election Office** with your EPIC number and ID proof
3. **Apply through the Voter Helpline app** — Use "Track Application Status" to check if a card has been dispatched
4. **Apply for duplicate** if your original card is lost — Fill Form 002 on [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in/)

## Common Errors and Troubleshooting

### "e-EPIC Not Available"

This error appears when:

- **Your photo is not on record** — Older voter IDs were issued without photos. Visit your nearest ERO (Electoral Registration Officer) office with a passport-size photo to get it updated
- **Your EPIC was recently issued** — Wait 2-3 days after registration for the e-EPIC to become available
- **Data mismatch** — Your details on the portal don't match electoral roll records

### "Photo Not Found"

- Your voter ID record doesn't have a photo uploaded
- **Solution:** Visit your BLO or ERO office with a recent passport-size photo and request a photo update
- After the photo is updated (takes 1-2 weeks), try downloading e-EPIC again

### OTP Not Received

- Check if your **mobile number is correct** and linked to your voter ID
- Wait at least **2-3 minutes** before requesting a new OTP
- Check your **SMS inbox and spam folder**
- If your mobile number has changed, update it first — See our [mobile number change guide](/guide/voter-id-mobile-number-change-fix)

### Download Fails or PDF Won't Open

- **Clear browser cache** and try again
- Use **Google Chrome or Mozilla Firefox** for best compatibility
- Check your **internet connection**
- Try the **Voter Helpline app** as an alternative

> **Facing persistent issues?** See our detailed [voter ID download not working fix guide](/guide/voter-id-download-not-working-fix).

## Important Tips

1. **Keep your mobile number updated** with your voter ID — It's required for OTP verification and e-EPIC download
2. **Save the e-EPIC PDF** to cloud storage (Google Drive, iCloud) so you always have a backup
3. **Check your EPIC number** on your physical card or search on the [National Voter Service Portal](https://voters.eci.gov.in/)
4. **e-EPIC can be re-downloaded** anytime — If you lose the PDF, just log in again and download
5. **Update your photo** if it's missing — Without a photo on record, e-EPIC cannot be generated

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. Is e-EPIC the same as my voter ID card?

Yes, e-EPIC is the digital version of your physical voter ID (EPIC) card. It contains the same details — your name, photo, EPIC number, date of birth, and constituency information — in PDF format with a QR code.

### Q2. Can I download e-EPIC without a mobile number linked to my voter ID?

No. OTP verification via your registered mobile number is mandatory to download e-EPIC. If your mobile number is not linked, update it first through the [Voter Helpline app](https://voters.eci.gov.in/) or by submitting Form 6B to your ERO.

### Q3. Is e-EPIC accepted for domestic air travel?

Yes. e-EPIC with a QR code is accepted as a valid photo ID for domestic flights within India by most airlines and airport authorities.

### Q4. Can I use e-EPIC for bank KYC?

Yes. Most banks accept e-EPIC as a valid identity document for KYC purposes. However, some bank branches may still prefer the physical card — carry both if possible.

### Q5. How long does it take to get e-EPIC after voter registration?

If your voter registration is approved and your photo is on record, e-EPIC usually becomes available within **2-3 working days** after your name appears on the electoral roll.

### Q6. What if my e-EPIC shows incorrect details?

If your name, photo, or other details are incorrect in the e-EPIC, you need to apply for correction through Form 8 on [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in/) or the Voter Helpline app. The e-EPIC will reflect the corrected details after approval.

### Q7. Can I download someone else's e-EPIC?

No. e-EPIC can only be downloaded by the voter themselves after OTP verification on their registered mobile number. Each voter must download their own e-EPIC.
