# PNB Net Banking Not Working? How to Fix Login & OTP Errors

Punjab National Bank (PNB) offers internet banking for fund transfers, bill payments, and account management. However, many users face login failures, OTP errors, locked profiles, and PNB ONE app crashes. This guide covers every common PNB net banking problem and how to fix it.

## Common PNB Net Banking Problems & Fixes

### 1. PNB Net Banking User ID Registration Issues

If you haven't registered or forgot your user ID:

**How to Register for PNB Net Banking (New User):**

1. Visit [PNB Net Banking portal](https://netbanking.netpnb.com/)
2. Click **"New User? Register Here"**
3. Enter your **Account Number** and **Registered Mobile Number**
4. Enter the **OTP** received on your mobile
5. Set your **User ID**, **Login Password**, and **Transaction Password**
6. Complete registration and log in

**Forgot User ID?**

1. Visit the PNB Net Banking login page
2. Click **"Forgot User ID"**
3. Enter your account number and registered mobile number
4. Your User ID will be sent via SMS to your registered mobile

**Common Registration Errors:**

| Error | Fix |
|-------|-----|
| "Account not found" | Verify account number; ensure account is active |
| "Mobile number not registered" | Visit your PNB branch to update mobile number |
| "User already registered" | Use "Forgot User ID" to retrieve existing ID |

---

### 2. PNB Net Banking Password Reset

**Forgot Login Password:**

1. Go to [PNB Net Banking](https://netbanking.netpnb.com/)
2. Click **"Forgot Login Password"**
3. Enter your **User ID** and **Account Number**
4. Verify via **OTP** sent to registered mobile
5. Set a new login password

**Forgot Transaction Password:**

1. Log in to PNB Net Banking
2. Go to **My Profile → Change Transaction Password**
3. If locked out, use the "Forgot Transaction Password" link on the login page
4. Follow OTP verification and set a new transaction password

**Password Rules:**
- Minimum 8 characters, maximum 20
- Must include uppercase, lowercase, number, and special character
- Cannot reuse last 3 passwords

### 3. PNB OTP Not Received

OTP failure is the most common PNB net banking issue. Try these fixes:

1. **Check mobile number:** Ensure your registered mobile number is correct — visit branch if needed
2. **Wait 2-3 minutes:** OTP can be delayed during peak hours
3. **Check SMS inbox:** Look in spam/blocked messages folder
4. **Network issues:** Ensure you have mobile network signal (OTP won't come on Wi-Fi-only mode if SIM has no signal)
5. **Request resend:** Click "Resend OTP" on the portal (wait at least 60 seconds)
6. **DND/block list:** Ensure bank SMS (sender ID: PNB/PNBSMS) is not blocked
7. **SIM issue:** If you recently changed SIM or ported number, visit the branch to re-register

**Still not working?** Call PNB Customer Care: **1800-180-2222** (toll-free) or **0120-2490000**

### 4. PNB Net Banking Profile Locked

Your profile gets locked after **3 consecutive wrong password attempts**.

**How to Unlock:**

**Option 1 — Online (Self-service):**
1. Wait **24 hours** — the profile auto-unlocks in most cases
2. Or click **"Forgot Login Password"** to reset and unlock immediately

**Option 2 — Branch Visit:**
1. Visit your home branch with your **passbook and ID proof**
2. Request net banking profile unlock
3. You may need to re-register

**Option 3 — Customer Care:**
1. Call **1800-180-2222**
2. Verify your identity (account number, DOB, registered mobile)
3. Request profile unlock

### 5. PNB ONE App Not Working

PNB ONE is PNB's unified mobile banking app. Common issues:

**App Not Opening / Crashing:**
- Update to latest version from [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.Version1) or Apple App Store
- Clear app cache: Settings → Apps → PNB ONE → Clear Cache
- Ensure minimum 200MB free storage
- Restart your phone

**Login Failed on PNB ONE:**
- Ensure you're using the correct **MPIN** or **login credentials**
- If MPIN is forgotten: Use **"Forgot MPIN"** option in the app
- Re-register if you changed your device

**Device Binding Issues:**
- PNB ONE is linked to one device at a time
- To change device: Install app on new phone → Select **"Existing User"** → Verify with debit card + OTP
- If binding fails, visit branch for de-registration

**Server Down:**
- PNB systems undergo maintenance usually between **11:30 PM – 12:30 AM**
- Check PNB's official Twitter handle [@paboraofficial](https://twitter.com/paboraofficial) for outage updates
- Try again after 30 minutes

## Important Tips

1. **Always use the official URL** — `https://netbanking.netpnb.com/` — never click links from emails or SMS
2. **Keep your registered mobile active** — all OTPs and alerts go to this number
3. **Change passwords every 90 days** to avoid forced resets
4. **Don't use public Wi-Fi** for banking transactions
5. **Enable SMS/email alerts** for all transactions in net banking settings

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. How do I activate PNB net banking for the first time?
Visit [netbanking.netpnb.com](https://netbanking.netpnb.com/), click "New User Register Here," enter your account number and registered mobile number, verify OTP, and set your credentials. You need an active PNB savings/current account with a registered mobile number.

### Q2. Why is PNB net banking showing "Invalid Credentials"?
This usually means wrong User ID or password. Use "Forgot User ID" or "Forgot Password" to recover. If your profile is locked after 3 wrong attempts, wait 24 hours or call customer care.

### Q3. PNB OTP is not coming on my mobile. What should I do?
Check if your registered mobile has network signal, wait 2-3 minutes, and try resending. If the issue persists, check if bank SMS is blocked by DND settings. Contact PNB at 1800-180-2222 if nothing works.

### Q4. Can I use PNB net banking on mobile browser?
Yes, you can access PNB net banking via mobile browser at the same URL. However, PNB ONE app is recommended for better experience and MPIN-based quick login.

### Q5. How do I reset my PNB ONE MPIN?
Open PNB ONE app → tap "Forgot MPIN" → verify with debit card details and OTP → set new MPIN. If this fails, uninstall and reinstall the app, then register as an existing user.

### Q6. Is PNB net banking down right now?
PNB systems have scheduled maintenance between 11:30 PM and 12:30 AM daily. If you face issues outside this window, it could be a temporary server issue — try again after 30 minutes.

### Q7. Can I access PNB net banking from outside India?
Yes, PNB net banking works internationally. However, OTP delivery to Indian mobile numbers may be delayed abroad. Consider enabling international roaming or using the PNB ONE app with MPIN login.
