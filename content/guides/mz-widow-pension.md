# Mizoram Widow Pension – Complete Guide 2026

## What is the Mizoram Widow Pension?

The **Mizoram Widow Pension** provides monthly financial support to widowed women from economically weaker sections under the **IGNWPS** with state top-up from the Mizoram government.

## Pension Amount

| Category | Approximate Monthly Pension |
|----------|---------------------------|
| Widows aged 40-79 | ₹400-800 |
| Widows aged 80+ | ₹700-1,200 |

## Who is Eligible?

- **Status:** Widow, not remarried
- **Age:** 40 years and above (IGNWPS)
- **Residency:** Permanent resident of Mizoram
- **Income:** BPL or below prescribed limit
- **Not receiving** any other government pension

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar Card
- Husband's death certificate
- Age proof
- Income certificate / BPL card
- Bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked)
- Residential proof
- Village Council certificate (rural)
- Passport-size photographs (2)
- Self-declaration of not being remarried

## How to Apply – Step-by-Step

1. Visit the **Village Council office** (rural) or **Municipal office** (urban)
2. Collect the **Widow Pension application form**
3. Fill in all details and attach documents
4. Get verified by the **Village Council President / Ward Councillor**
5. Submit at the **District Social Welfare Office**
6. After verification and approval, pension is credited via DBT

## Processing Time

- **Verification to first payment:** 2-4 months

## Important Tips

1. **Death certificate is mandatory** — obtain from the registering authority
2. **Village Council endorsement** helps expedite the process
3. **Pension stops on remarriage**
4. **Annual life verification** is required
5. **Keep bank account active**

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Can I receive both widow and old age pension?
**A:** Only **one NSAP pension** at a time. See our [Mizoram Old Age Pension guide](/guide/mz-old-age-pension).

### Q2: What if I can't get a death certificate?
**A:** Contact the **Municipal/Village Council office** for late registration of death. An FIR copy or hospital records may help.

### Q3: Is there an online application?
**A:** Check [nsap.nic.in](https://nsap.nic.in). Primary method is offline.

### Q4: What age is required?
**A:** **40 years and above** under IGNWPS.

### Q5: How is the pension paid?
**A:** Through **DBT** to the Aadhaar-linked bank account.
