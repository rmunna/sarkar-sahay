## What is Mukhyamantri Seekho Kamao Yojana?

Mukhyamantri Seekho Kamao Yojana (MMSKY) is a flagship skill development scheme launched by the **Government of Madhya Pradesh** to provide on-the-job training and a monthly stipend to unemployed youth. The scheme connects young people aged 18–29 with registered companies across various sectors, allowing them to earn while they learn.

The state government pays **75% of the stipend** directly to the trainee's bank account via DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer), while the **training company pays the remaining 25%**.

> **Hindi:** मुख्यमंत्री सीखो कमाओ योजना — मध्य प्रदेश सरकार द्वारा युवाओं को प्रशिक्षण के साथ स्टाइपेंड प्रदान करने वाली योजना।

## Key Highlights

- **Monthly stipend:** ₹8,000 to ₹10,000 (based on qualification)
- **Training duration:** Up to 1 year
- **Age group:** 18 to 29 years
- **Who pays:** 75% state government + 25% company
- **Registration portal:** [mmsky.mp.gov.in](https://mmsky.mp.gov.in)

## Eligibility Criteria

To apply for Seekho Kamao Yojana, you must meet **all** of the following conditions:

| Criteria | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Domicile** | Must be a resident of Madhya Pradesh |
| **Age** | 18 to 29 years |
| **Education** | Minimum 12th pass (Higher Secondary) |
| **Employment** | Currently unemployed |
| **Bank Account** | Active bank account linked with [Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) |
| **Samagra ID** | Valid Samagra ID is mandatory |

### Additional Notes on Eligibility

- Candidates with ITI, Diploma, Graduate, or Post-Graduate qualifications are also eligible (and receive higher stipends)
- No income limit for the applicant's family has been specified
- Candidates already receiving benefits from other state/central employment schemes may not be eligible

## Stipend Amount by Qualification

The monthly stipend varies based on your educational qualification:

| Qualification | Monthly Stipend | State Share (75%) | Company Share (25%) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **12th Pass** | ₹8,000 | ₹6,000 | ₹2,000 |
| **ITI Certificate** | ₹8,500 | ₹6,375 | ₹2,125 |
| **Diploma Holder** | ₹9,000 | ₹6,750 | ₹2,250 |
| **Graduate or above** | ₹10,000 | ₹7,500 | ₹2,500 |

> The state government's share (75%) is transferred directly to the trainee's bank account via DBT. The company pays the remaining 25% separately.

## How to Register and Apply

### Step 1: Visit the Official Portal

Go to [mmsky.mp.gov.in](https://mmsky.mp.gov.in) and click on **"अभ्यर्थी पंजीयन" (Candidate Registration)**.

### Step 2: Enter Samagra ID

Enter your **Samagra ID**. Your details will be auto-fetched from the Samagra database. Verify your name, date of birth, and other information.

### Step 3: Complete Registration

- Enter your mobile number and email address
- You will receive an OTP — verify it
- Set a password for your account
- Upload required documents

### Step 4: Fill Application Form

Log in to your account and complete the application form:

- Select your **educational qualification**
- Choose your **preferred training sector and course**
- Select the **district** where you want to train
- Review and submit

### Step 5: Choose a Company

After submitting your application, browse available training positions from registered companies. You can apply to multiple establishments.

### Step 6: Company Approval & Training Contract

Once a company selects you:

- A **training contract** is generated on the portal
- Both you and the company sign the contract digitally
- Training begins as per the scheduled date

## Documents Required

Keep these documents ready before applying:

- **Samagra ID** (mandatory)
- **Aadhaar Card** (linked to bank account)
- **Educational certificates** (12th marksheet, degree, diploma, ITI certificate — as applicable)
- **Bank passbook** (with account number and IFSC)
- **Passport-size photograph**
- **Domicile certificate** or proof of MP residence

## Training Sectors

The scheme covers **700+ courses** across diverse sectors, including:

- **Information Technology (IT)** — Software development, data entry, web design
- **Manufacturing** — CNC operation, quality control, assembly
- **Banking & Financial Services** — Accounting, insurance, financial planning
- **Hotel Management & Tourism** — Hospitality, food production, front office
- **Media & Arts** — Graphic design, animation, journalism
- **Engineering** — Civil, mechanical, electrical trades
- **Healthcare** — Lab technician, pharmacy assistant, nursing support
- **Retail & Marketing** — Sales, visual merchandising, e-commerce
- **Automobile** — Vehicle servicing, EV technology
- **Telecom & Electronics** — Mobile repair, networking, IoT

> The full list of eligible courses and sectors is available on the [MMSKY portal](https://mmsky.mp.gov.in).

## Training Duration and Structure

- **Maximum duration:** 1 year (12 months)
- **Minimum duration:** Varies by course (typically 6–12 months)
- Training is conducted **at the company's premises** (on-the-job)
- Attendance is tracked through the MMSKY portal
- Stipend is released monthly based on attendance records

## Placement Support After Training

One of the key benefits of Seekho Kamao Yojana is the focus on employment after training:

- Companies are **encouraged to absorb trainees** as regular employees after successful completion
- The portal maintains a **placement tracking system**
- Trainees who complete their training receive a **certificate** from the State Council for Vocational Training
- The certificate and on-the-job experience improve employability across sectors

> While placement is not guaranteed, many registered establishments hire trainees who perform well during the training period.

## For Companies / Establishments

Businesses can also register on the MMSKY portal to participate:

- Must be registered under **GST** or other applicable registrations
- Need a valid **EPFO (PF)** registration
- Can onboard multiple trainees across different courses
- Only required to pay **25% of the stipend** — the rest is borne by the state

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is there any application fee?
No. Registration and application are completely **free of cost**.

### Can I apply if I already have a job?
No. The scheme is for **unemployed youth** only.

### What if I drop out of training midway?
Your stipend will stop from the date of discontinuation. You may re-apply for a different training position later.

### Can I choose which company to train with?
Yes. After registration, you can browse and apply to multiple companies. The final selection depends on the company.

### Is Samagra ID mandatory?
Yes. Without a valid **Samagra ID**, you cannot register on the MMSKY portal.

### Do I need to be from a specific caste or category?
No. The scheme is open to **all categories** — General, OBC, SC, ST — as long as eligibility criteria are met.

### Will I get a certificate after training?
Yes. A **training completion certificate** is issued through the portal, recognized by the State Council for Vocational Training.
