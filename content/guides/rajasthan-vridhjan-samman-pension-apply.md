## What is Rajasthan Vridhjan Samman Pension?

**Mukhyamantri Vridhjan Samman Pension Yojana** is Rajasthan's state government old age pension scheme that gives monthly financial assistance to eligible senior citizens who have no income source. The benefit depends on age:

| Age | Monthly Pension |
|-----|----------------|
| Women aged 55–74 | ₹750/month |
| Women aged 75+ | ₹1,000/month |
| Men aged 58–74 | ₹750/month |
| Men aged 75+ | ₹1,000/month |

Women become eligible 3 years earlier than men (55 years vs 58 years).

## Who is Eligible?

**You ARE eligible if:**
- You are a **permanent resident of Rajasthan** (minimum 15 years)
- You are a woman aged **55+** or a man aged **58+**
- You have **no regular source of income** — no government pension, no taxable income
- Your family is **not income tax paying**
- You are **not already receiving** any other pension (IGNOAPS, IGNDPS, IGNWPS)

> Widowed women below 55 years apply under the **Ekl Nari Pension Yojana** (separate scheme).

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar Card** — identity and age proof
2. **Jan Aadhaar card** — Rajasthan family ID (mandatory)
3. **Age proof** (Aadhaar with DoB, school certificate, voter ID)
4. **Domicile certificate** — Rajasthan resident for 15+ years
5. **Income self-declaration** — declaring no regular income
6. **Bank passbook** — Aadhaar-linked savings account
7. **Photograph**

## How to Apply — Step by Step

### Step 1 — Get Jan Aadhaar Card

If you don't have a Jan Aadhaar card (Rajasthan family ID):
- Visit nearest **CSC / E-Mitra centre**
- Register your family — it is free
- Get your Jan Aadhaar ID

### Step 2 — Apply Online (Rajssp Portal)

1. Go to **[rajssp.raj.nic.in](https://rajssp.raj.nic.in)**
2. Register / login with Jan Aadhaar
3. Apply for **"Vridhjan Samman Pension"**
4. Fill in personal details and upload documents

### Step 3 — Apply at E-Mitra Centre (Offline)

Visit your nearest **E-Mitra kiosk** (CSC). The operator fills the online form on your behalf with your documents. Nominal service fee may be charged.

### Step 4 — Sub-Divisional Officer Verification

After online application:
- Application is routed to the Sub-Divisional Officer (SDO) for approval
- Field verification by Patwari/Village Officer confirms eligibility

### Step 5 — Pension Starts

After approval, pension is credited monthly to your Aadhaar-linked bank account.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. My mother is 55 and my father is 56. Can both apply?**

Your mother (woman, 55+) is eligible — yes. Your father (man, 58 required) is not yet eligible at 56. He can apply when he turns 58.

**2. My father has a small pension of ₹500 from a private employer. Is he eligible?**

Vridhjan Samman Pension is for those with **no regular income source**. A private employer pension (even small) may make him ineligible — consult with the local SDO or E-Mitra operator for clarification.

**3. I applied 3 months ago but the status says "pending at SDO." What do I do?**

Visit the SDO office directly with your application number. Delays at SDO level are common. You can also contact the **District Social Welfare Officer**.

**4. My grandmother receives ₹750/month but she is now 76 years old. Should she get ₹1,000?**

Yes. At age 75+, the pension increases to ₹1,000/month. This upgrade should happen automatically, but if not, visit the E-Mitra centre to update age records.

**5. Can a couple where both are above 58 both receive the pension?**

Yes. Each individual applies separately. Both husband and wife above the age threshold can receive their own pension.
