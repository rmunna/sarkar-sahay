# UP Divyang Pension Yojana: ₹1,000 Monthly Pension for Disabled Persons

UP Divyang Pension Yojana (Uttar Pradesh Disability Pension Scheme) provides a **monthly pension of ₹1,000** to persons with 40% or more disability from economically weaker sections. The scheme is managed by the Social Welfare Department, Government of Uttar Pradesh, through the SSPY portal.

## What is UP Divyang Pension?

The Uttar Pradesh government provides financial assistance to persons with disabilities (Divyangjan) who are unable to earn a livelihood due to their disability. The pension amount includes the Central Government's share under [IGNDPS](/guide/nsap-disability-pension) (for 80%+ disability) and the state contribution. Even persons with **40% disability** are eligible for the UP state pension.

---

## Who is Eligible?

- **Disability:** 40% or more disability as certified by a government medical board
- **Age:** 18 years or above
- **Resident:** Permanent resident of Uttar Pradesh
- **Income:** BPL or family annual income below ₹2 lakh
- **Not receiving** any other government disability pension
- **Indian citizen**

---

## Pension Amount

| Category | Monthly Amount |
|----------|---------------|
| All eligible Divyang persons | ₹1,000 per month |

*Paid quarterly (₹3,000 every 3 months) via DBT to bank account.*

## Documents Required

- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
- Disability certificate (40%+ disability from government hospital/CMO)
- UDID card (Unique Disability ID) — if available
- Age proof
- Income certificate (from Tehsildar)
- BPL card / [Ration card](/guide/ration-card-apply-online)
- Bank account passbook (Aadhaar-linked, UP bank branch)
- Passport-size photograph
- Domicile certificate / residence proof of UP
- Mobile number

---

## How to Apply Online

1. **Visit SSPY portal** — Go to [sspy-up.gov.in](https://sspy-up.gov.in/)
2. **Select "दिव्यांग पेंशन"** — Click on Divyang/Disability Pension
3. **Click "ऑनलाइन आवेदन करें"** — Apply Online
4. **Fill form** — Enter personal details, disability type and percentage, date of birth, address
5. **Upload documents** — Upload photograph, disability certificate, income certificate
6. **Enter bank details** — Bank account number and IFSC code
7. **Submit** — Review and submit
8. **Save registration number** — Note for tracking
9. **Verification** — Block/District level verification
10. **Approval** — Pension approved and payments begin

### Offline Application

1. Visit **Block Development Office (BDO)** or **District Social Welfare Office**
2. Collect Divyang Pension form
3. Fill and attach all documents
4. Submit at BDO office
5. After verification, pension starts

---

## How to Get Disability Certificate

If you don't have a disability certificate:

1. Visit the **District Hospital** or **Community Health Centre**
2. Meet the **Chief Medical Officer (CMO)** or medical board
3. Carry medical records and previous treatment documents
4. The board assesses your disability and issues a certificate with **disability percentage**
5. You need **40% or more** for UP Divyang Pension (compared to 80% for Central IGNDPS)
6. Also apply for **UDID card** at [swavlambancard.gov.in](https://www.swavlambancard.gov.in/)

---

## Processing Time

- **Disability certificate:** 15-30 days
- **Application to verification:** 30-60 days
- **Approval and first payment:** 2-4 months

---

## Important Tips

1. **40% is enough** — Unlike the Central IGNDPS (which requires 80%), UP Divyang Pension requires only 40% disability
2. **Get disability certificate first** — This is the most important document. Visit a government hospital
3. **Apply for UDID card** — Get your Unique Disability ID at [swavlambancard.gov.in](https://www.swavlambancard.gov.in/)
4. **Fresh income certificate** — Income certificate from Tehsildar should be recent
5. **Check pension list** — Monitor the SSPY portal for your name in the approved beneficiary list

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. What disability percentage is needed for UP Divyang Pension?
40% or more disability as certified by a government medical board.

### Q2. How much pension do disabled persons get in UP?
₹1,000 per month, paid quarterly (₹3,000 every 3 months).

### Q3. Can children with disabilities apply?
The minimum age is 18 years. For children below 18, check other state assistance programmes for disabled children.

### Q4. Can I get both UP Divyang Pension and Central IGNDPS?
The UP pension includes the Central share for those with 80%+ disability. You receive one combined payment.

### Q5. What types of disabilities are covered?
All disabilities recognized under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 — locomotor, visual, hearing, intellectual, mental illness, multiple disabilities, and others.

### Q6. Is the disability certificate valid permanently?
For permanent disabilities, yes. For temporary or progressive disabilities, it may need renewal as specified by the medical board.

### Q7. How do I check my pension status?
Visit [sspy-up.gov.in](https://sspy-up.gov.in/), click on Divyang Pension, and check application status using your registration number.
