# Paytm Refund Not Received? Track & Fix Refund

Waiting for a Paytm refund that hasn't arrived? Whether it's a cancelled order, failed transaction, or merchant refund, this guide explains how to track your refund, understand timelines, and escalate if needed.

## Refund Timelines — How Long Does a Paytm Refund Take?

| Payment Method | Refund Destination | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| Paytm Wallet | Paytm Wallet | Instant to 24 hours |
| UPI | Bank account | 3-5 working days |
| Debit card | Bank account | 5-7 working days |
| Credit card | Credit card account | 7-10 working days |
| Net banking | Bank account | 5-7 working days |
| Paytm Postpaid | Postpaid balance | 1-3 working days |

**Working days** exclude weekends and bank holidays. Actual times depend on your bank's processing speed.

---

## Where Does the Refund Go? (Source vs Wallet)

### Refund to Source
Most refunds are processed **back to the original payment source**:
- Paid via UPI → refund goes to your bank account
- Paid via credit card → refund goes to your credit card
- Paid via wallet → refund goes to Paytm wallet

### Refund to Wallet
Some merchants or Paytm offers may refund to your **Paytm wallet** regardless of the original payment method. Check the refund details in your transaction history.

### How to Check Where Your Refund Went
1. Open Paytm → **Passbook**.
2. Find the original transaction.
3. Tap on it → look for **"Refund"** details.
4. It will show the refund destination (wallet/bank/card) and status.

## How to Check Paytm Refund Status

### Method 1: Through Paytm App
1. Open Paytm → tap **Passbook**.
2. Find the transaction for which you expect a refund.
3. Tap on the transaction → check refund status:
   - **Refund Initiated** — processing, wait for the timeline to complete.
   - **Refund Completed** — money has been sent. Check your bank/card/wallet.
   - **No refund shown** — refund may not have been initiated yet.

### Method 2: Check Bank Statement
1. Log in to your bank's app or net banking.
2. Check recent transactions for a credit from "Paytm" or "One97".
3. For credit cards, check the **unbilled transactions** section — refunds may appear there first.

### Method 3: UPI Transaction Check
1. If paid via UPI, open your bank's UPI app (or the BHIM app).
2. Go to transaction history and check if the refund credit appears.

## Refund Not Received After Expected Timeline? Here's What to Do

### Step 1: Wait for the Full Timeline
Don't raise a complaint too early. Allow the full processing time based on payment method (see table above).

### Step 2: Raise a Ticket with Paytm
1. Open Paytm → **Help & Support** (24/7 icon or from the menu).
2. Select **"Payments"** → **"Refund Related"**.
3. Select the specific transaction.
4. Tap **"Raise a Ticket"** or **"Chat with Us"**.
5. Provide:
   - Transaction ID
   - Date and amount
   - Screenshot of the failed/cancelled transaction
6. You'll receive a ticket number — save it.

### Step 3: Call Paytm Support
- **Customer Care:** 0120-4456-456
- Available 24/7 for payment-related issues
- Keep your transaction ID and ticket number ready

### Step 4: Escalate Within Paytm
If your ticket isn't resolved within 7 days:
1. Reply to the support email with your ticket number.
2. Mention that the issue is unresolved and request escalation.
3. You can also reach out via Paytm's social media handles (Twitter/X: @Paytmcare).

### Step 5: Escalate to External Authorities
If Paytm doesn't resolve within 30 days:

**For UPI transactions:**
- File a complaint on [NPCI's UPI dispute portal](https://www.npci.org.in/what-we-do/upi/product-overview) or through your bank.

**For all payment methods:**
- File a complaint at [National Consumer Helpline](https://consumerhelpline.gov.in/) — call **1800-11-4000** (toll-free).
- File online at consumerhelpline.gov.in.
- For amounts above ₹10 lakh, approach the **District Consumer Forum**.

**For banking ombudsman:**
- If the issue involves your bank, file a complaint with RBI's [Banking Ombudsman](https://cms.rbi.org.in/).

## Common Refund Scenarios

### Cancelled Order but No Refund
1. Check with the merchant/seller if they've initiated the refund.
2. Some merchants take 2-3 days to process cancellation before initiating refund.
3. Refund timeline starts from when the **merchant initiates** it, not when you cancel.

### Failed Transaction — Money Debited
1. This is an auto-refund scenario — Paytm/bank should refund automatically.
2. Wait 3-5 working days for UPI, 5-7 for cards.
3. If not received, raise a ticket with both Paytm and your bank.

### Partial Refund Received
1. Check if the order had multiple items — some may be refunded separately.
2. Coupon/discount amounts may not be refunded.
3. Convenience fees or platform charges may be non-refundable.
4. Contact the merchant for partial refund clarification.

## Important Tips

- Always note the **Transaction ID** for every payment — you'll need it for refund tracking.
- Check both Paytm Passbook and your bank statement — sometimes refunds appear in bank before Paytm updates.
- For [failed UPI payments](/guide/paytm-payment-failed-fix), money is usually returned automatically within 48 hours.
- Credit card refunds take the longest — check your next billing statement if the refund doesn't appear immediately.
- Keep screenshots of all transactions and support conversations.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Paytm shows refund completed but I haven't received money. What to do?
Check your bank statement carefully — the credit may appear with a different name (like "One97" or "PAYTM"). For credit cards, check unbilled transactions. If still not found after 48 hours, contact your bank with the transaction reference number.

### Q2: Can I get a Paytm refund to my bank instead of wallet?
Refunds typically go back to the original payment source. If you paid via wallet, the refund goes to wallet. You can then transfer wallet balance to bank (Full KYC required) — see our [wallet to bank transfer guide](/guide/paytm-wallet-to-bank-transfer-failed-fix).

### Q3: How do I track my Paytm refund?
Open Paytm → Passbook → find the transaction → tap to see refund status. You'll see "Refund Initiated", "Processing", or "Completed".

### Q4: Paytm refund is taking more than 7 days. Is this normal?
For credit card refunds, up to 10 working days is normal. For UPI/debit card, it should arrive within 5-7 working days. If exceeded, raise a support ticket.

### Q5: Can I cancel a Paytm refund?
No, once a refund is initiated, it cannot be cancelled. It will be processed to the destination.

### Q6: Who do I contact if Paytm doesn't resolve my refund complaint?
Escalate to the National Consumer Helpline (1800-11-4000), NPCI for UPI disputes, or RBI Banking Ombudsman for bank-related issues.
