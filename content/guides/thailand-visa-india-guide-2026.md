# Thailand Visa from India 2026 — Complete Guide

Thailand is one of the most visited destinations by Indians. The good news: no prior visa application needed for most trips — Indians get Visa on Arrival at major airports. Here's everything you need to know.

---

## Do Indians Need a Visa for Thailand?

**Yes**, but there's an easy Visa on Arrival option that works for most tourists.

| Option | How to Get | Cost | Stay |
|--------|-----------|------|------|
| **Visa on Arrival (VOA)** | At Bangkok/Phuket/Chiang Mai airport | THB 2,000 (₹4,800) | 30 days |
| **e-Visa (before travel)** | Online at thaievisa.go.th | THB 2,000 (₹4,800) | 30 days |
| **Tourist Visa (TR)** | At Thai consulate in India | THB 2,000 (₹4,800) | 60 days |
| **Thailand ELITE (long-stay)** | Membership program | THB 600K–1.2M | 5–20 years |

**For most tourists**: Visa on Arrival OR e-Visa is the easiest option.

---

## Visa on Arrival (VOA) — What You Need at the Airport

VOA is available at:
- Suvarnabhumi Airport (Bangkok, BKK)
- Don Mueang Airport (Bangkok, DMK)
- Phuket International Airport
- Chiang Mai International Airport
- Krabi International Airport (and several other airports)

### Documents at VOA Counter

| Document | Details |
|----------|---------|
| **Passport** | Valid for 6+ months from entry date |
| **VOA form** | Fill on the plane or at airport (forms available at the counter) |
| **Photo** | 4×6cm or passport size (some airports accept digital; carry physical) |
| **Onward/return ticket** | Proof you're leaving Thailand |
| **Hotel booking** | Confirmed first-night accommodation (at minimum) |
| **Cash** | THB 20,000 (₹48,000) equivalent per person — immigration may ask |
| **VOA fee** | THB 2,000 cash (₹4,800 approx) — Thai Baht preferred at counter; some accept USD |

**Queue time**: Suvarnabhumi VOA queue can be 45–90 minutes during peak hours (arrivals from India at night). Arrive with patience or use e-Visa (see below).

## Thailand e-Visa — Apply Before Leaving India

To skip the airport VOA queue, apply for the e-Visa at [thaievisa.go.th](https://www.thaievisa.go.th) 3–5 days before departure:

1. Register at thaievisa.go.th
2. Select "Tourist Visa" → TR 30 days
3. Upload: passport scan, photo, flight itinerary, hotel booking
4. Pay THB 2,000 online (credit/debit card)
5. Receive e-Visa via email in 3–5 working days
6. Show e-Visa QR code at immigration (no separate counter needed)

**Advantage**: Same cost as VOA but no queue at airport. Fast immigration lane for pre-approved visitors.

## Thailand Tourist Visa (60-Day) from Thai Consulate

For longer stays or multiple entries, apply at Thai consulate in India:

**Consulates**: New Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata

**Documents**:
- Visa application form
- Passport (6+ months validity)
- 2 photos
- Bank statement (3 months, showing funds)
- Hotel booking / itinerary
- Return flight
- Employment letter (for salaried)

**Fee**: THB 2,000 (₹4,800)  
**Processing**: 3–5 working days  
**Stay**: 60 days per visit, single or double entry  
**Extension**: Can extend 30 days at Thai immigration office inside Thailand

## Thailand Entry Requirements — Key Rules

### THB 20,000 Cash Requirement

Thailand immigration requires tourists to have **THB 20,000 (₹48,000) per person** or **THB 40,000 per family** in cash or accessible funds. In practice:
- Rarely physically checked but can be asked
- If you're backpacking with minimal funds and look it, you may be questioned
- Having adequate bank funds visible in your account app is usually sufficient
- Carry some Thai Baht cash for the first day

### Return Ticket

You **must** have a confirmed return/onward ticket. One-way entry to Thailand can result in denial at immigration. Book a refundable return flight before departure.

### Dress Code at Temples

Not an entry rule, but Thai temples require covered shoulders and knees. Carry a sarong or scarf for temple visits.

## Extending Your Thailand Stay

If you entered on VOA (30 days) and want to extend:
- Visit nearest Thai Immigration office before your visa expires
- Extension: 30 additional days, cost THB 1,900 (₹4,500)
- One extension typically permitted per VOA entry

For more time: Exit Thailand (even to Malaysia or Cambodia for one day) and re-enter on a fresh VOA.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I enter Thailand on Visa on Arrival multiple times in a year?

Yes — each VoA gives you 30 days. You can re-enter on a fresh VoA after exiting (border run). However, Thai immigration has become stricter about multiple VoA entries — more than 3–4 per year may raise questions about your actual purpose (tourism vs long-stay without proper visa).

### Is travel insurance required for Thailand?

Not mandatory for the visa, but highly recommended — Thai hospitals are good but private hospitals are expensive without insurance.

### I was denied entry in Thailand before. Can I try again?

Denial at immigration is serious. If denied for suspicion of working illegally or immigration irregularity, you may be flagged in Thai systems. A clean record is essential for future entries.

### Bangkok or Phuket for first trip from India?

Both are excellent. Bangkok for culture, shopping, temples, food (3–4 nights). Phuket/Krabi for beaches (3–4 nights). A 7-day trip typically covers both.
