# PNB Dormant Account Reactivation 2026 — Complete Guide

Punjab National Bank (PNB) classifies accounts dormant after **24 consecutive months** without a customer-initiated transaction. This is per RBI mandate applicable to all Indian banks. Here's how to reactivate your PNB account.

---

## PNB Dormant Account — What Changes

| Feature | Active | Dormant |
|---------|--------|---------|
| ATM withdrawals | ✅ | ❌ |
| NEFT/IMPS debits | ✅ | ❌ |
| UPI payments | ✅ | ❌ |
| Incoming credits | ✅ | ✅ Continues |
| Net banking (debit) | ✅ | ❌ |
| Balance enquiry | ✅ | ✅ |

---

## Method 1: PNB ONE App (Online Reactivation)

1. Open **PNB ONE** app → Login
2. **"Services"** → **"Account Services"** → **"Reactivate Dormant Account"** (if available)
3. Complete KYC update (Aadhaar OTP or document upload)
4. Submit reactivation request
5. **Processing: 24–72 hours**

*Note: PNB ONE dormant reactivation is available for select account types. Branch visit needed if option is not visible.*

---

## Method 2: PNB Net Banking

1. Login at **[netpnb.com](https://www.netpnb.com)**
2. **"Other Services"** → **"Account Services"** → **"Dormant Account Activation"**
3. Complete KYC online if prompted
4. Submit
5. **Processing: 2–5 working days**

---

## Method 3: PNB Branch (Standard Method)

1. Visit any PNB branch (home branch recommended for faster processing)
2. Carry:
   - Aadhaar card (original + self-attested copy)
   - PAN card (original + copy)
   - Passbook or account number
3. Request: **"Dormant Account Reactivation"**
4. Fill form + update KYC if needed
5. Make fresh transaction: Deposit ₹100–₹500 at counter
6. **Same-day processing**

**PNB Customer Care:** **1800-180-2222** (toll-free)

---

## PNB Legacy Account Reactivation (Merged Banks)

PNB merged with **Oriental Bank of Commerce (OBC)** and **United Bank of India** in 2020. If your account was with OBC or UBI:
- Account is now a PNB account with a new account number
- Dormancy rules are the same as PNB
- Visit any PNB branch with old account passbook + Aadhaar + PAN
- Branch will verify merger records and process reactivation

---

## PNB Dormant Account — Special Situations

**If account has been dormant for 7–9 years:**
- Balance likely still at PNB (not yet DEAF-transferred at 10 years)
- Visit home branch immediately
- Process is same: Aadhaar + PAN + reactivation form

**After 10 years:**
- Balance transferred to RBI DEAF Fund
- Visit PNB branch → DEAF claim → Takes 5–7 working days for DEAF recovery

## Frequently Asked Questions

**PNB dormant account — can my family member reactivate on my behalf?**
No — account holder must be present in person for dormant account reactivation. A nominee or family member cannot reactivate on your behalf (only in case of death claim). Power of Attorney can be used for incapacitated customers.

**PNB net banking password expired and account dormant — which to fix first?**
If net banking is expired, fix the account reactivation at branch first (more important). Net banking login can be reset separately via branch or PNB ONE app.

**PNB sent me a message about "unclaimed deposit" — is that different from dormant?**
"Unclaimed deposit" is the next stage after dormancy — typically after 10 years when DEAF transfer happens. Dormant accounts (2–10 years) are different. Visit branch immediately if you received a DEAF/unclaimed notice.

**Is there a minimum balance required to reactivate PNB dormant account?**
No minimum balance required for reactivation itself. However, after reactivation, you need to maintain minimum balance as per your account type (savings: usually ₹500–₹1,000 for non-metro, ₹2,000–₹5,000 for metro branches).

**PNB dormant reactivation — how long does branch processing take?**
At branch: 2–4 hours on the same day. The branch updates the account in the core banking system. You can test your ATM card/UPI by end of business day.
