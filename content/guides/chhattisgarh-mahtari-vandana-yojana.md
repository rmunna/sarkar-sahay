## What is Mahtari Vandana Yojana?

Mahtari Vandana Yojana is a flagship women's welfare scheme launched by the Government of Chhattisgarh that provides a monthly financial assistance of ₹1,000 to married women of the state. The scheme aims to promote women's economic empowerment, improve their health and nutrition status, and ensure financial independence.

Under this scheme, ₹12,000 per year (₹1,000 per month) is transferred directly to the bank accounts of eligible married women through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT). The scheme covers married women including widows and divorced women.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Married women** who are permanent residents of Chhattisgarh
- **Widows and divorced/separated women** are also eligible
- **Age:** Must be 21 years or above and up to 60 years
- **Income limit:** No strict income ceiling — scheme is broadly targeted at all married women, though priority is given to economically weaker sections
- **Must have a valid Aadhaar card** linked to bank account
- **Must not be an income tax payer** (the woman herself should not be filing income tax returns)
- **Must not hold a government job** (the woman should not be a government employee or pensioner)

## Documents Required

### Mandatory Documents
- **Aadhaar Card** (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- **Marriage certificate** or marriage proof (ration card with husband's name, voter ID, etc.)
- **Bank account passbook** — account must be in the woman's name and linked to Aadhaar
- **Residence proof** — voter ID, ration card, or electricity bill showing Chhattisgarh address
- **Passport-size photograph**
- **Mobile number** linked to Aadhaar

### Additional Documents (if applicable)
- **Death certificate of husband** (for widows)
- **Divorce decree/separation proof** (for divorced/separated women)
- **Age proof** — birth certificate or Aadhaar (for age verification)

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Online Process

1. **Visit** the official portal at [mahtarivandan.cgstate.gov.in](https://mahtarivandan.cgstate.gov.in)
2. **Click on "Apply Online"** or "New Registration"
3. **Enter your Aadhaar number** and verify via OTP
4. **Fill the application form** — name, husband's name, date of birth, address, marital status
5. **Enter bank account details** — account number, IFSC code (must be Aadhaar-linked)
6. **Upload required documents** — marriage certificate, photo, residence proof
7. **Review and submit** the application
8. **Note the application reference number** for tracking
9. **Application verified** by the local Anganwadi worker/supervisor and block-level officials
10. **Monthly allowance starts** after approval — ₹1,000 credited on the 10th of each month

### Offline Process

1. **Visit** your nearest Anganwadi Centre, Gram Panchayat office, or CSC
2. **Collect the Mahtari Vandana application form**
3. **Fill the form** with personal details, marital status, and bank information
4. **Attach photocopies** of Aadhaar, marriage certificate, bank passbook, and photo
5. **Submit the form** at the Anganwadi Centre or Gram Panchayat
6. **Get acknowledgement receipt** with reference number
7. **Verification** by Anganwadi worker and block officials at the local level
8. **Monthly payment starts** after successful verification

### Camp-Based Registration

The government also organizes **special registration camps** at the ward/village level. Check with your local Anganwadi worker or Gram Panchayat for camp dates in your area.

## Important Tips

1. **Link your Aadhaar to your bank account** — this is mandatory for receiving the monthly allowance via DBT (see our [Aadhaar linking guide](/guide/aadhaar-linking-guide))
2. **Open a bank account in your own name** if you don't have one — Jan Dhan accounts are accepted and can be opened for free at any bank
3. **Keep your mobile number active** — OTP verification and payment notifications are sent to your registered mobile
4. **Contact your Anganwadi worker** for help — they assist with form filling and can guide you through the application process
5. **Update your details** if you move, change bank accounts, or if your marital status changes

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. How much money do women get under Mahtari Vandana Yojana?
Eligible women receive ₹1,000 per month (₹12,000 per year) directly in their bank account via DBT.

### Q2. Are unmarried women eligible for the scheme?
No, the scheme is specifically for married women, including widows and divorced/separated women. Unmarried women are not currently eligible.

### Q3. Is there an income limit for the scheme?
There is no strict income ceiling, but the woman herself should not be an income tax payer and should not hold a government job. The scheme broadly targets all married women of Chhattisgarh.

### Q4. Can widows and divorced women apply?
Yes, widows (with husband's death certificate) and divorced/separated women (with legal proof) are eligible for the scheme.

### Q5. How do I check if my payment has been credited?
Check your bank account statement or passbook. You can also track your payment status on the [official portal](https://mahtarivandan.cgstate.gov.in) using your application number or Aadhaar.

### Q6. What if my application is rejected?
Common reasons include incorrect Aadhaar details, bank account not linked to Aadhaar, or missing marriage proof. Correct the issues and re-apply, or visit your local Anganwadi centre for assistance.

### Q7. Do I need to re-apply every year?
No, once approved, the monthly payment continues automatically as long as you remain eligible. However, you should update your details if there are any changes.
