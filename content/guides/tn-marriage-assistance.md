# Moovalur Ramamirtham Marriage Assistance Scheme – Tamil Nadu

## What is this Scheme?

The Moovalur Ramamirtham Ammaiyar Ninaivu Marriage Assistance Scheme is a Tamil Nadu government scheme providing **₹25,000 cash assistance and 8 grams of gold** (as a gold coin) to women from economically weaker sections at the time of their marriage. The scheme is administered by the Social Welfare and Women Empowerment Department.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Bride** must be a **resident of Tamil Nadu**
2. **Age:** Bride must be **18 years or above** at the time of marriage
3. **Education:** Bride must have studied up to at least **Class 10** (SSLC)
4. **Family Income:** Annual family income must be below **₹72,000**
5. **First Marriage:** Assistance is for the **first marriage** only
6. The bride's family must hold a **rice ration card**
7. **Religion/Caste:** Open to all communities (separate sub-schemes exist for SC/ST, BC/MBC, minorities)

### Enhanced Benefits by Category

| Category | Cash Assistance | Gold |
|----------|----------------|------|
| General (SSLC pass) | ₹25,000 | 8g gold coin |
| Graduate | ₹50,000 | 8g gold coin |
| SC/ST (degree holder) | ₹50,000 | 8g gold coin |
| Differently-abled | ₹25,000 | 8g gold coin |

## Documents Required

- **Marriage Invitation / Registration** as proof of upcoming marriage
- **Bride's Age Proof** (birth certificate, school certificate, or Aadhaar)
- **SSLC Marks Sheet** (Class 10 pass certificate)
- **Degree Certificate** (if applying for graduate benefit)
- **Family Income Certificate** (from Tahsildar, below ₹72,000/year)
- **Rice Ration Card**
- **Aadhaar Card** of the bride
- **Bank Account** in bride's name (linked to Aadhaar)
- **Community Certificate** (for SC/ST/BC/MBC categories)
- **Passport Size Photos** of bride and groom
- **Marriage Registration Certificate** (for post-marriage claims)

## How to Apply

### Before Marriage

1. **Visit** the nearest **District Social Welfare Office** or **Revenue Divisional Office**
2. **Collect** the marriage assistance application form
3. **Fill** in all details — bride's education, family income, marriage date
4. **Attach** all required documents
5. **Submit** the application at least **40 days before the marriage date**
6. **Collect** the acknowledgment receipt

### After Marriage (Within 3 Months)

1. Submit the **marriage registration certificate** to the same office
2. After verification, the **₹25,000 cash + 8g gold coin** is provided
3. Cash is transferred to the bride's bank account via DBT

### Online Application

1. Visit the Tamil Nadu e-Sevai portal or Social Welfare Department website
2. Fill the online application form
3. Upload all required documents
4. Submit and track using the reference number

## Processing Time

- **Application Submission:** At least 40 days before marriage
- **Verification:** 15-30 days
- **Benefit Disbursement:** Within 1-2 months after marriage (post submission of marriage certificate)

## Important Tips

1. **Apply Early:** Submit the application at least 40 days before the wedding. Late applications may be rejected.
2. **Income Certificate is Key:** Get it from the Tahsildar's office. It must show income below ₹72,000/year.
3. **Education Matters:** If the bride is a graduate, she gets ₹50,000 instead of ₹25,000 — ensure the degree certificate is submitted.
4. **Register the Marriage:** Marriage registration is mandatory to receive the benefit. Register within 3 months at the registrar's office.
5. **No Duplicate Claims:** Only one claim per bride. Re-marriage after divorce/widowhood has separate provisions.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Can the groom's family apply?
**No.** The application must be in the **bride's name** and submitted by the bride or her family.

### Q2: Is this only for Hindu marriages?
**No.** The scheme is available for all religions and communities in Tamil Nadu.

### Q3: What if the bride didn't pass Class 10?
She is **not eligible** under this specific scheme. Class 10 (SSLC) pass is a mandatory requirement.

### Q4: Can I get the gold coin in cash instead?
**No.** The 8g gold coin is provided as a physical gold coin — it cannot be converted to cash.

### Q5: What if my family income is slightly above ₹72,000?
You will **not be eligible**. The income limit is strictly enforced based on the income certificate.

### Q6: Can inter-state marriages qualify?
If the **bride is from Tamil Nadu** and meets all eligibility criteria, inter-state marriages may qualify. Consult the district office.
