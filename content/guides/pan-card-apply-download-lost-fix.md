# PAN Card Apply Online, Download & Lost PAN Fix — Complete Guide (2026)

PAN (Permanent Account Number) is mandatory for all financial transactions above ₹50,000, ITR filing, bank account opening, investment accounts, and income-generating activities. This guide covers everything — new PAN application, e-PAN download, lost PAN recovery, and corrections.

---

## New PAN Card — Apply Online (2026)

### Method 1: Instant e-PAN via Income Tax Portal (Free, For Aadhaar Holders)

**Eligibility:** Aadhaar card + Aadhaar-linked mobile number

1. Go to **[incometax.gov.in](https://www.incometax.gov.in)**
2. Click **"Instant e-PAN"** (on homepage)
3. Click **"Get New e-PAN"**
4. Enter **Aadhaar number** + accept terms
5. OTP sent to Aadhaar-linked mobile → Enter OTP
6. Verify Aadhaar details (name, DOB, gender)
7. **PAN allotted instantly** — e-PAN PDF sent to registered email
8. **Cost: FREE**

**Note:** Instant e-PAN is a digital-only PAN. You get a PDF PAN card. If you need a physical PAN card, apply via NSDL or UTIITSL.

---

### Method 2: NSDL (Physical PAN Card — ₹107)

1. Go to **[onlineservices.nsdl.com](https://onlineservices.nsdl.com/paam/endUserRegisterContact.html)**
2. Select **Application Type:** New PAN — Indian Citizen (Form 49A)
3. Fill form: name, DOB, address, contact, documents
4. Upload: Photo, signature, Aadhaar (for address + identity proof)
5. Pay ₹107 (within India) via net banking / debit card / UPI
6. Note the **15-digit acknowledgment number**
7. Physical PAN card delivered to address in **15–20 working days**
8. **e-PAN sent to email within 24–48 hours** after application

---

### Method 3: UTIITSL (Physical PAN — ₹107)

1. Go to **[www.utiitsl.com](https://www.utiitsl.com)** → PAN Services
2. Select **New PAN** → Fill Form 49A online
3. Upload documents + pay
4. Physical card in 15–20 days

## Download e-PAN (Existing PAN)

### Method 1: Income Tax Portal

1. Go to **[incometax.gov.in](https://www.incometax.gov.in)**
2. Click **"Instant e-PAN"** → **"Check Status / Download e-PAN"**
3. Enter Aadhaar number + OTP
4. **Download e-PAN PDF**

### Method 2: NSDL

1. Go to **[tin.tin.nsdl.com](https://www.tin.tin.nsdl.com)**
2. Click **"Download e-PAN"**
3. Enter: Acknowledgment number / PAN number + DOB
4. OTP + download PDF

### Method 3: UTIITSL

1. **[www.utiitsl.com](https://www.utiitsl.com)** → Download e-PAN
2. Enter PAN + DOB + GST/email/Aadhaar
3. Pay ₹8.26 (if not applied through UTIITSL) → Download

## Lost PAN Card — Reprint/Duplicate

**Option 1: Reprint via NSDL (Physical Card)**
1. NSDL website → **"Reprint of PAN Card"**
2. Enter PAN number + Aadhaar + DOB
3. OTP on Aadhaar-linked mobile
4. Pay ₹50 (within India) → New physical card delivered in 15 days

**Option 2: Download e-PAN (Free/₹8)**
For most purposes (bank KYC, ITR, investments), e-PAN PDF is accepted. Download from IT portal using Aadhaar + OTP (see above — free).

**Option 3: UTIITSL Reprint**
- UTIITSL website → PAN Reprint → Fill details + pay → Physical card in 15 days

## PAN Correction — Change Name, DOB, Address

If your PAN has wrong name, date of birth, or other errors:

1. Go to **NSDL** or **UTIITSL** → **"Changes/Corrections in PAN"**
2. Select: Form for correction (Indian Citizen: 49A for changes)
3. Tick the fields you want to correct
4. Upload supporting documents:
   - Name change: Marriage certificate / gazette notification
   - DOB change: Birth certificate / school certificate
   - Address: Not reflected on PAN card (only on records)
5. Pay ₹107 + submit
6. Corrected PAN card delivered in 15–20 working days

## Aadhaar-PAN Linking (Mandatory)

PAN-Aadhaar linking is mandatory. If not linked, your PAN becomes **inoperative** (cannot file ITR, make high-value transactions).

**How to Link Aadhaar to PAN:**

### Via Income Tax Portal
1. Login to **incometax.gov.in**
2. **"Link Aadhaar"** (visible on dashboard if not linked)
3. Enter PAN + Aadhaar number + name
4. OTP on Aadhaar-linked mobile → Confirm
5. Link confirmed instantly (or within 3–5 days for verification)

### Without Login (e-Filing SMS)
- SMS **UIDPAN<12-digit Aadhaar><10-digit PAN>** to **567678** or **56161**
- Response confirms link status

**Fee for late PAN-Aadhaar linking:** ₹1,000 penalty applicable if not linked by the due date. Currently applicable fee must be paid via challan before linking.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does it take to get a new PAN card?**
Instant e-PAN: Within 30 minutes. Physical PAN card: 15–20 working days via NSDL/UTIITSL.

**Is e-PAN valid for bank KYC?**
Yes — e-PAN (PDF) is legally equivalent to physical PAN for KYC purposes. Banks, brokers, and most institutions accept e-PAN.

**I have two PAN cards — what should I do?**
Having two PANs is illegal and attracts a penalty of ₹10,000. Surrender the duplicate PAN at a NSDL office or via your Assessing Officer. Keep only one.

**Can I get PAN without Aadhaar?**
Yes — but the process is longer. You'll need offline document submission (passport-size photo, address proof, identity proof) via NSDL physical form or agent.

**Is PAN card the same as PAN number?**
PAN (Permanent Account Number) is the 10-digit alphanumeric number (e.g., ABCDE1234F). The PAN card is the physical/digital card showing this number. Both terms are often used interchangeably.

**My PAN shows wrong date of birth in bank records — how to fix?**
First correct the PAN at NSDL/UTIITSL (document correction with DOB proof). Then update the corrected PAN details with your bank via KYC update form.
