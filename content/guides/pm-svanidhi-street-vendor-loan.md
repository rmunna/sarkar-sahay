# PM SVANidhi — Street Vendor Loan Scheme

## What is PM SVANidhi?

PM SVANidhi (Pradhan Mantri Street Vendor's AtmaNirbhar Nidhi) is a **government scheme to give small loans to street vendors**. If you sell vegetables, fruits, chai, food, clothes, or any goods on the street — this scheme is for you.

The government gives you a **working capital loan** so you can grow your business. The best part? You get **7% interest subsidy** — meaning the government pays part of your interest!

### How Much Loan Can You Get?

PM SVANidhi gives loans in **3 stages (tranches)**:

| Loan Stage | Loan Amount | When You Can Apply |
|---|---|---|
| **First Loan** | ₹10,000 | When you apply first time |
| **Second Loan** | ₹20,000 | After repaying first loan on time |
| **Third Loan** | ₹50,000 | After repaying second loan on time |

👉 You start with ₹10,000. Pay it back on time, and you can get ₹20,000 next. Pay that back, and you can get up to ₹50,000!

## Who Can Apply? (Eligibility)

You can apply for PM SVANidhi if:

- ✅ You are a **street vendor** (selling goods or services on the street)
- ✅ You were vending **on or before 24 March 2020**
- ✅ You have a **Vending Certificate** from your city/town municipality
- ✅ OR you have a **Letter of Recommendation (LoR)** from the Urban Local Body (ULB)
- ✅ You have an **Aadhaar card**
- ✅ You have a **bank account**

**Who counts as a street vendor?** Anyone who sells goods on the street — vegetable sellers, fruit sellers, chai wallahs, food stall owners, cobblers, barbers, laundry workers, pan shops, and more.

## Benefits of PM SVANidhi

1. **₹10,000 to ₹50,000 loan** — No big paperwork, easy process
2. **7% interest subsidy** — Government pays 7% per year of your interest directly to your account
3. **Digital payment cashback** — Use digital payments (UPI, etc.) and get up to **₹1,200 per year** as cashback
4. **No collateral needed** — You don't need to give any property or gold as security
5. **Build your credit score** — Timely repayment helps you get bigger loans in the future

## Documents Required

Keep these documents ready before you apply:

1. **Aadhaar Card** (linked with mobile number)
2. **Vending Certificate** or **Letter of Recommendation** from ULB
3. **Bank account details** (passbook or cancelled cheque)
4. **Passport size photo**
5. **Mobile number** (registered with Aadhaar)

## How to Get a Vending Certificate

If you don't have a vending certificate yet, don't worry. Follow these steps:

1. Go to your **nearest Municipal Corporation / Nagar Palika / Town Panchayat** office
2. Ask for a **Vending Certificate** application form
3. Fill the form with your details — name, Aadhaar number, what you sell, where you sell
4. Submit along with your Aadhaar copy and photo
5. The **Town Vending Committee (TVC)** will verify your details
6. If you are a genuine street vendor, they will issue you a **Vending Certificate** or a **Letter of Recommendation (LoR)**

💡 **Tip:** Many ULBs now issue LoR directly through the PM SVANidhi portal. Ask your local municipal office about this.

## How to Apply for PM SVANidhi Online (Step by Step)

### Step 1: Go to the Official Website

Open **[pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in](https://pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in)** in your phone or computer browser.

### Step 2: Click on "Apply for Loan"

On the homepage, look for the **"Apply for Loan"** button and click it.

### Step 3: Enter Your Mobile Number

Enter your mobile number linked with Aadhaar. You will get an **OTP**. Enter the OTP to verify.

### Step 4: Fill Your Details

Fill in your personal details:
- Full name (as per Aadhaar)
- Aadhaar number
- Category of vending (what you sell)
- Address where you vend

### Step 5: Upload Documents

Upload your:
- Aadhaar card photo
- Vending Certificate or LoR
- Bank passbook first page photo

### Step 6: Choose Your Bank

Select the bank where you want to receive the loan. You can choose from:
- Public sector banks (SBI, PNB, etc.)
- Private banks
- Regional Rural Banks
- Cooperative Banks
- NBFCs & MFIs

### Step 7: Submit Application

Review all details and click **Submit**. You will get an **application reference number**. **Save this number!**

### Step 8: Wait for Approval

Your application goes to the ULB for verification, then to the bank for approval. This usually takes **15-30 days**.

## How to Check Application Status

1. Go to **[pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in](https://pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in)**
2. Click on **"Know Your Application Status"**
3. Enter your **application number** or **mobile number**
4. Your current status will be shown — pending, approved, or disbursed

## Repayment Terms

- **Repayment period:** 12 months from the date you get the loan
- **EMI:** You can pay in monthly instalments
- **Early repayment:** You can pay back early — no penalty
- **Interest subsidy:** 7% per annum subsidy is credited to your bank account every quarter
- **On-time repayment bonus:** If you repay on time, you become eligible for the next (bigger) loan

## Digital Payment Incentive — Get ₹1,200/Year Cashback

The government wants to encourage digital payments. If you use **UPI, BHIM, or any digital payment** for your business:

- Do at least **50 digital transactions per month**
- You will get **₹100 cashback per month** (₹1,200 per year)
- Cashback is credited directly to your bank account

💡 **Tip:** Ask your customers to pay via UPI. It's easy and you earn cashback!

## ULB Recommendation Process

If your city does not have a Town Vending Committee survey, the **Urban Local Body (ULB)** can give you a **Letter of Recommendation (LoR)**. Here's how:

1. Visit your nearest **Municipal Corporation / Nagar Palika** office
2. Tell them you want LoR for PM SVANidhi
3. They will verify that you are a genuine street vendor
4. The ULB officer will issue you the LoR
5. Use this LoR to apply for the loan online

## Important Tips

- 🔒 **Never pay any agent or middleman** to apply. Application is **100% free**
- 📱 You can apply from your **own mobile phone** — no need to visit any office
- 🏦 If the bank rejects your application, you can **apply again**
- 📞 For help, call the PM SVANidhi helpline or visit your nearest Common Service Centre (CSC)

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: I don't have a vending certificate. Can I still apply?**
A: Yes! Your ULB (Municipal Corporation) can issue a Letter of Recommendation (LoR). Go to your local municipal office and ask for it.

**Q: Is there any processing fee?**
A: No. There is **no processing fee** for PM SVANidhi loan.

**Q: Can I apply if I already have another loan?**
A: Yes, you can apply for PM SVANidhi even if you have another loan.

**Q: What if I can't repay on time?**
A: Try your best to repay on time. Late repayment means you won't get the interest subsidy and won't be eligible for the next bigger loan.

**Q: I live in a village. Can I apply?**
A: PM SVANidhi is mainly for **urban areas** (cities and towns). If you vend in a town or city, you can apply. Rural vendors can check the [Mudra Loan](/guide/mudra-loan-apply-online) scheme instead.
