# RBI Banking Ombudsman Complaint 2026 — File Against Bank, NBFC, UPI

If your bank has not resolved your complaint within **30 days** (or given an unsatisfactory response), you can escalate to the **RBI Integrated Ombudsman** — a free, independent grievance redressal mechanism. The Ombudsman can direct banks to refund money and can award compensation up to **₹20 lakh**.

## Who Can You Complain Against?

Under the **RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme (RB-IOS, November 2021)**:

| Entity | Yes/No |
|--------|--------|
| **All commercial banks** (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, PNB, etc.) | ✅ Yes |
| **RRBs (Regional Rural Banks)** | ✅ Yes |
| **NBFCs** (Non-Banking Financial Companies) | ✅ Yes |
| **Scheduled Co-operative Banks** | ✅ Yes |
| **Payment System Operators** (UPI, PPI wallets) | ✅ Yes |
| **Crypto exchanges** | ❌ No |
| **Insurance companies** | ❌ No — IRDAI |
| **Mutual funds / brokers** | ❌ No — SEBI SCORES |

## Common Complaints the Ombudsman Handles

- **Unauthorized transactions**: Money debited without your authorization (fraud/ATM/net banking)
- **Failed ATM — cash not dispensed**: ATM shows "successful" but no cash came out
- **UPI failed but money deducted**: Failed payment where amount hasn't been refunded
- **Home loan issues**: Bank charging wrong interest rate, not giving NOC after full repayment
- **Credit card issues**: Disputes on credit card charges, unfair interest calculation
- **EMI not adjusted**: You paid EMI but loan balance not reducing
- **Locker not accessible**: Bank denying access to your safe deposit locker
- **Delayed cheque/transfer**: Bank delayed processing beyond RBI timelines
- **Bank refusing to close account**: Bank not processing account closure request
- **Mis-selling of insurance/investment**: Bank staff sold you a product misrepresenting it

## Prerequisites Before Filing

You can only file with the Ombudsman if:
1. You have **already complained to the bank** in writing (email counts)
2. The bank has either:
   - Not responded within **30 days**, OR
   - Given an unsatisfactory/partial resolution

> Keep your complaint email and any bank response as evidence. The Ombudsman will ask for these.

## Step-by-Step: How to File Complaint at cms.rbi.org.in

### Step 1: Register on the CMS Portal

1. Visit **[cms.rbi.org.in](https://cms.rbi.org.in/)**
2. Click **"File a Complaint"**
3. Enter your mobile number + email
4. Verify OTP
5. Create your account

### Step 2: File the Complaint

1. Login → click **"Lodge a Complaint"**
2. Select the **entity** (Bank Name, NBFC, Payment System Operator)
3. Select **complaint category** (e.g., "Unauthorized Electronic Fund Transfer", "ATM", "Loan/Advance")
4. Provide complaint details:
   - Date of transaction/issue
   - Amount involved
   - Steps taken with bank and response
5. Attach supporting documents (PDF/JPG):
   - Bank complaint email and their response (or proof of no response after 30 days)
   - Bank statement showing the transaction
   - ATM transaction receipt
   - Any relevant screenshots
6. Submit → receive a **Complaint Registration Number**

### Step 3: Track Your Complaint

1. Login to [cms.rbi.org.in](https://cms.rbi.org.in/) → "Track Complaint"
2. Enter your registration number
3. Status updates: Under Review → Entity Response → Ombudsman Decision

## What the Ombudsman Can Do

| Action | Possible |
|--------|---------|
| **Direct bank to credit your money back** | ✅ Yes |
| **Award compensation** (up to ₹20L) | ✅ Yes — for harassment, mental agony, etc. |
| **Order interest payment on delayed refund** | ✅ Yes |
| **Waive unfair bank charges** | ✅ Yes |
| **Cancel CIBIL/credit score damage** | ❌ Not directly — but directs bank to correct |
| **Criminal prosecution of fraudsters** | ❌ No — file cyber crime for that |

## What to Do for Unauthorized Transactions (Fraud)

For money stolen from your account:

1. **Immediately** call your bank's 24/7 helpline and block the card/account
2. File a complaint with your bank **within 3 working days** of noticing unauthorized transaction
3. The bank has limited liability under RBI guidelines — if you report in 3 days, the liability is zero or limited
4. **Also file an FIR** with Cyber Crime at [cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in/) (police investigation)
5. If bank refuses to refund after 30 days: file Ombudsman complaint

**Limited Liability Guidelines** (RBI):
- Fraud due to bank's system failure: Full refund
- 3rd party fraud (not your fault): Reported within 3 days → Zero liability; 4-7 days → up to ₹10,000 deductible; 7+ days → as per bank policy

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How is the RBI Ombudsman different from consumer court?

RBI Ombudsman is FREE, faster (60-90 days), and specifically for banking/financial issues. Consumer court may take 6-18 months, requires filing fee, and you need a lawyer for complex cases. Use Ombudsman first; if unsatisfied, you can still go to consumer court.

### Can I file complaint for CIBIL credit score wrongly damaged by bank?

Yes. If the bank wrongly reported a default that damaged your credit score, complain to the Ombudsman. The Ombudsman can direct the bank to correct CIBIL reporting.

### My home loan EMI is correct but the bank is charging extra fees without notice. Can I complain?

Yes — unauthorized fee addition is a valid Ombudsman complaint. Category: "Loans and Advances" → "Recovery Agents" or "Interest Rate" depending on the specific issue.

### The bank sold me a ULiP/insurance product saying it was a fixed deposit. Can I complain?

Yes — this is mis-selling. File under "Mis-selling of financial products." The Ombudsman can direct the bank to refund with surrender charges waived.

### My complaint was dismissed by the Ombudsman. What now?

You can appeal within **30 days** to the **Appellate Authority** (RBI Deputy Governor) or approach consumer court/civil court for monetary disputes.
