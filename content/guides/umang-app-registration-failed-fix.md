# UMANG App Registration Failed? Fix OTP & Login Errors

The **UMANG (Unified Mobile Application for New-age Governance)** app provides access to 1,500+ government services from over 200 departments. If you're facing registration failures, OTP errors, or login issues, this guide will help you fix them.

## Common UMANG Registration & Login Errors

Users frequently encounter these issues when trying to register or log in to the UMANG app:

1. **OTP not received** on registered mobile number
2. **Aadhaar verification failed** during registration
3. **App crash** during registration or login
4. **Server error** or "Something went wrong" message
5. **Unable to re-register** with the same mobile number

---

## Fix 1: OTP Not Received on UMANG App

If you're not receiving the OTP during registration or login:

1. **Check your mobile number** — Ensure you're entering the correct number linked to your Aadhaar
2. **Wait for 2-3 minutes** — OTP delivery can be delayed during peak hours
3. **Check SMS inbox and spam folder** — The OTP comes from a government sender ID
4. **Ensure network connectivity** — Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data
5. **Click "Resend OTP"** — Wait for the timer to expire, then request a new OTP
6. **Check DND status** — Call your telecom provider or SMS "STOP DND" to 1909 to disable Do Not Disturb
7. **Try after some time** — If the UMANG server is overloaded, OTP generation may fail temporarily

> **Tip:** The OTP is valid for 10 minutes. Do not close the app while waiting for the OTP.

---

## Fix 2: Aadhaar Verification Failed

If Aadhaar verification fails during UMANG registration:

1. **Verify your Aadhaar number** — Re-enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number carefully
2. **Check mobile number linkage** — Your mobile number must be linked to your Aadhaar. Check at [myAadhaar portal](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/)
3. **Update Aadhaar mobile number** — If your current number is not linked, visit the nearest Aadhaar enrolment centre to update it. See our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) for details
4. **Check UIDAI server status** — Aadhaar verification depends on UIDAI servers. Try again during non-peak hours (early morning or late evening)
5. **Ensure Aadhaar is active** — Deactivated or suspended Aadhaar numbers will fail verification

---

## Fix 3: UMANG App Crash During Registration

If the app crashes or freezes during registration:

1. **Update the app** — Go to Google Play Store or Apple App Store and update UMANG to the latest version
2. **Clear app cache and data:**
   - Go to **Settings → Apps → UMANG → Storage**
   - Tap **Clear Cache** first, then **Clear Data** if needed
3. **Restart your phone** — A simple restart can fix temporary glitches
4. **Check storage space** — Ensure your phone has at least 500 MB free space
5. **Reinstall the app** — Uninstall UMANG completely and reinstall from the official store
6. **Check OS compatibility** — UMANG requires Android 5.0+ or iOS 11.0+

---

## Fix 4: Server Error or "Something Went Wrong"

If you see server errors or generic error messages:

1. **Check internet connection** — Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data to test
2. **Try the web version** — Visit [web.umang.gov.in](https://web.umang.gov.in/) as an alternative
3. **Wait and retry** — Government servers experience high traffic during working hours (10 AM – 5 PM). Try during off-peak hours
4. **Disable VPN** — VPN connections can interfere with government app servers
5. **Check for maintenance** — Visit the official UMANG social media handles for scheduled maintenance announcements
6. **Update the app** — Older app versions may have API compatibility issues with updated servers

---

## Fix 5: Unable to Re-Register with Same Mobile Number

If you're trying to re-register and facing errors:

1. **Your old account may still be active** — Try logging in with your existing credentials instead of creating a new account
2. **Use "Forgot MPIN" option** — On the login screen, tap "Forgot MPIN" to reset your credentials
3. **Contact UMANG support:**
   - **Toll-free number:** 1800-115-565
   - **Email:** support-umang@digitalindia.gov.in
4. **Wait 24 hours** — If you recently deleted your account, the system may take time to release the mobile number
5. **Try with a different number** — If urgent, register with an alternative mobile number

## How to Register on UMANG App (Step-by-Step)

Follow these steps for a fresh registration:

1. Download **UMANG** from [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=in.gov.umang.negd.g2c) or Apple App Store
2. Open the app and tap **Register**
3. Enter your **mobile number** and tap **Get OTP**
4. Enter the **OTP** received via SMS
5. Set a **4-digit MPIN** for future logins
6. Optionally, link your **Aadhaar** for enhanced services
7. Complete your **profile** with name and email

> **Note:** Aadhaar linking is optional for basic services but required for Aadhaar-authenticated services like EPF, passport, and pension.

## Important Tips

1. Always download UMANG from official app stores only — avoid APK files from unknown sources
2. Keep your app updated to the latest version to avoid compatibility issues
3. Register during off-peak hours (before 10 AM or after 6 PM) for smoother experience
4. Save your MPIN securely — you'll need it for every login
5. If issues persist for more than 24 hours, contact UMANG helpline at 1800-115-565

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Why is UMANG OTP not coming to my phone?
OTP delays can occur due to network congestion, DND activation, or server overload. Disable DND by sending "STOP DND" to 1909, check your network, and try resending the OTP after the timer expires.

### Q2: Can I use UMANG without Aadhaar?
Yes, basic registration requires only a mobile number. However, services like EPF balance check, passport tracking, and pension require Aadhaar authentication.

### Q3: What should I do if UMANG app shows "Server Error"?
Clear the app cache, check your internet connection, and try during off-peak hours. You can also use the web version at [web.umang.gov.in](https://web.umang.gov.in/).

### Q4: How do I reset my UMANG MPIN?
On the login screen, tap "Forgot MPIN," enter your registered mobile number, verify with OTP, and set a new 4-digit MPIN.

### Q5: Is UMANG app safe to use?
Yes, UMANG is developed by the National e-Governance Division (NeGD) under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology. It uses secure authentication and encrypted data transmission.

### Q6: Can I register on UMANG with a different mobile number than my Aadhaar?
Yes, you can register with any mobile number. But for Aadhaar-based services, the OTP will be sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile number for verification.

### Q7: UMANG app is not available in my region. What should I do?
UMANG is available across India. If you cannot find it on your app store, try the web version at [web.umang.gov.in](https://web.umang.gov.in/) or check if your device meets the minimum OS requirements.
