# How to Correct Name in Aadhaar Card Online

Is your name spelled wrong in your Aadhaar card? Or did you change your name after marriage? Don't worry — UIDAI allows you to correct or update your name in Aadhaar both online and offline. This guide covers the complete process, documents needed, fees, and common mistakes to avoid.

## When Do You Need Aadhaar Name Correction?

You may need to correct or update your name in Aadhaar in these situations:

- **Spelling mistake** — Your name was entered incorrectly during enrolment (e.g., "Raaj" instead of "Raj")
- **Name change after marriage** — You want to update your surname
- **Name change by gazette notification** — Legal name change
- **Mismatch with other documents** — Your Aadhaar name doesn't match PAN, passport, or bank records
- **Incomplete name** — Only first name was entered, and you want full name

### Name Correction vs Name Update — What's the Difference?

| | Name Correction | Name Update |
|---|---|---|
| **What it means** | Fixing a spelling mistake or typo in your existing name | Changing your name to a completely different name |
| **Example** | "Suresh Kmar" → "Suresh Kumar" | "Priya Sharma" → "Priya Verma" (after marriage) |
| **Process** | Same online/offline process | Same online/offline process |
| **Documents needed** | Valid Proof of Identity (POI) document | Valid POI document with new name |

> **Note:** UIDAI treats both correction and update through the same process. You submit a request with supporting documents, and UIDAI verifies and updates accordingly.

## Online Process — Step by Step

You can correct your name in Aadhaar online through the **myAadhaar portal**. Here's how:

### Requirements Before You Start

- Your **mobile number must be registered** with your Aadhaar
- A **scanned copy** of a valid name proof document (clear, coloured scan of original)
- File size: **max 2 MB**, format: **JPEG/PNG/PDF**

### Steps to Correct Name Online

1. Go to [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Login"** and enter your **12-digit Aadhaar number**
3. Enter the **OTP** sent to your registered mobile number
4. After login, click on **"Update Aadhaar Online"** or **"Document Update"**
5. Select **"Name"** as the field you want to update
6. Enter your **correct name** as it appears in your supporting document
7. Select the **document type** you are uploading as proof
8. **Upload a clear scanned copy** of your name proof document
9. **Review all details** carefully before submitting
10. **Pay the fee of ₹50** using credit card, debit card, or net banking
11. After successful payment, you will receive a **Service Request Number (SRN)**
12. **Download and save the invoice** — it contains your SRN for tracking

> **Important:** Currently, UIDAI allows **only address and document updates** through the online portal. For **name, date of birth, and gender updates**, you may need to visit an Aadhaar Enrolment Centre. Check the myAadhaar portal for the latest available services, as UIDAI periodically enables/disables online demographic updates.

## Offline Process — At Aadhaar Enrolment Centre

If you cannot update your name online (mobile not registered, or online service unavailable), visit an Aadhaar Enrolment Centre:

1. **Locate your nearest centre** at [bhuvan-app3.nrsc.gov.in/aadhaar](https://bhuvan-app3.nrsc.gov.in/aadhaar/)
2. **Carry original documents** — at least one valid Proof of Identity document and a photocopy
3. Visit the centre and fill the **Aadhaar Update/Correction Form**
4. Select **"Name"** as the field to update
5. Write your **correct name** clearly in the form
6. Submit the form along with your **document photocopy**
7. The operator will take your **biometrics** (fingerprint, iris, photo)
8. Pay the **fee of ₹50** at the centre
9. Collect your **Update Request Number (URN)** from the acknowledgement slip
10. Your name will be updated within **7–90 days** after verification

> **Tip:** Book an appointment online at [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in) to avoid long queues at the enrolment centre.

## Documents Accepted for Name Proof

UIDAI accepts the following documents as Proof of Identity (POI) for name correction:

### Government-Issued Photo ID (Any One)

1. **Passport**
2. **PAN Card**
3. **Voter ID (EPIC)**
4. **Driving Licence**
5. **NREGA Job Card**
6. **Government Photo ID Card** (Central/State)
7. **Service Photo ID Card** (issued by PSU)

### Other Accepted Documents

8. **Ration Card**
9. **Bank/Post Office Passbook** with photo
10. **Arms Licence**
11. **Pensioner Card**
12. **Freedom Fighter Card**
13. **Kissan Photo Passbook**
14. **CGHS/ECHS Card**
15. **Certificate of Identity** (signed by MP, MLA, Gazetted Officer, or Tehsildar)
16. **Marriage Certificate** (for name change after marriage)
17. **Gazette Notification** (for legal name change)

> **Download the full document list:** [UIDAI Supporting Documents PDF](https://uidai.gov.in/images/commdoc/List_of_Supporting_Document_for_Aadhaar_Enrolment_and_Update.pdf)

### Document Upload Tips

- Upload a **coloured scan** of the original document (not a photocopy)
- Ensure the document is **clearly readable** — no blur, no shadows
- The **name on the document must match** the name you are requesting
- Keep file size under **2 MB**

## Fees and Processing Time

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| **Fee** | ₹50 (online and offline) |
| **Payment modes (online)** | Credit card, debit card, net banking, UPI |
| **Payment mode (offline)** | Cash/card at enrolment centre |
| **Processing time** | 7 to 90 days |
| **Average time** | 10–30 days for most cases |

> **Note:** The ₹50 fee applies per update request, regardless of how many fields you update in one request. There is no additional GST.

## How Many Times Can You Change Your Name in Aadhaar?

UIDAI allows a **maximum of 2 name changes** in your Aadhaar lifetime.

- **First update:** Allowed freely with valid documents
- **Second update:** Allowed with valid documents
- **Third update onwards:** Not permitted through self-service; you may need to approach UIDAI's regional office with a valid reason

> **Plan carefully:** Since you only get 2 chances, make sure the name you submit is exactly correct and matches your other documents (PAN, passport, etc.).

## How to Track Aadhaar Name Correction Status

After submitting your update request, you can track the status:

### Online Tracking

1. Go to [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in)
2. Log in with your Aadhaar number and OTP
3. Click on **"Check Aadhaar Update Status"**
4. Enter your **SRN (Service Request Number)** or **URN (Update Request Number)**
5. The status will show as: **Submitted → Under Review → Accepted/Rejected**

### Status Meanings

- **Submitted** — Your request has been received
- **Under Review** — UIDAI is verifying your documents
- **Accepted** — Name has been updated successfully
- **Rejected** — Document was not accepted (see reasons below)

> You will also receive an **SMS on your registered mobile** when the update is processed.

## Common Rejection Reasons and Solutions

| Rejection Reason | Solution |
|---|---|
| **Blurry or unclear document** | Upload a high-quality, coloured scan of the original document |
| **Name on document doesn't match** | Ensure the name in your POI document exactly matches what you entered |
| **Invalid or expired document** | Use a currently valid, government-issued document |
| **Document not in accepted list** | Check UIDAI's official document list and use an accepted POI |
| **Multiple fields changed at once** | Try updating only the name field in one request |
| **Photo of photocopy uploaded** | Always scan the original document, not a xerox copy |
| **Name entered in wrong language** | Enter name in English; regional language will be auto-transliterated |

### What to Do If Your Request Is Rejected

1. **Read the rejection reason** carefully in the status page
2. **Get a better quality scan** of your document
3. **Try a different POI document** from the accepted list
4. **Re-submit** a fresh request with ₹50 fee
5. If rejected multiple times, **visit an enrolment centre** for offline update with biometric verification

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Can I change my name in Aadhaar without a registered mobile number?

No, for online update you need a registered mobile number to receive OTP. If your mobile is not registered, visit an **Aadhaar Enrolment Centre** for offline update where biometric authentication is used instead.

### 2. How long does Aadhaar name correction take?

Typically **10 to 30 days**, but it can take up to **90 days** in some cases. You can track the status online using your SRN/URN.

### 3. Can I update my name and address at the same time?

Yes, you can update multiple fields in a single request. You will still pay only **₹50** for the entire request, not per field.

### 4. Is Aadhaar name correction free?

No. UIDAI charges a fee of **₹50** for any update request, whether online or offline. This fee applies regardless of the number of fields you update.

### 5. My Aadhaar name doesn't match my PAN card. Which one should I correct?

It depends on which document has the correct name. If your **PAN has the correct name**, update your Aadhaar to match. If your **Aadhaar has the correct name**, update your PAN instead. It's important that both documents have the same name to avoid issues with ITR filing and bank KYC.

### 6. Can I correct my child's name in Aadhaar?

Yes. For children below 5 years (Baal Aadhaar), the parent or guardian can request a name update by visiting an enrolment centre with the child's birth certificate or school ID as proof.

### 7. What if I changed my name after marriage — do I need a gazette notification?

Not necessarily. A **marriage certificate** issued by the registrar is sufficient proof for updating your name after marriage. A gazette notification is needed only for other types of legal name changes.
