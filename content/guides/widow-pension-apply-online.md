# Widow Pension Apply Online: Eligibility, Documents & Process

The **Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme (IGNWPS)** provides monthly financial assistance to widows from BPL families across India. Run under the **National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP)**, this central scheme is supplemented by state governments with additional amounts. This guide covers everything you need to apply for widow pension online.

## What is IGNWPS (Widow Pension)?

IGNWPS is a centrally sponsored pension scheme for widows belonging to Below Poverty Line (BPL) households. The scheme is managed by the Ministry of Rural Development through the [NSAP portal](https://nsap.nic.in/).

### Central Government Pension Amount

| Age Group | Central Share (per month) |
|-----------|--------------------------|
| 40–79 years | ₹300 |
| 80 years and above | ₹500 |

States add their own contribution, making the total pension range from ₹500 to ₹2,500/month depending on the state.

## Who is Eligible for Widow Pension?

### IGNWPS (Central Scheme) Eligibility

- **Gender:** Women only
- **Marital status:** Widow (husband deceased)
- **Age:** 40–79 years (age 80+ covered under IGNOAPS)
- **Income:** Must belong to a BPL family
- **Citizenship:** Indian citizen
- **Residence:** Permanent resident of the state where applying
- **Remarriage:** Must not have remarried (in most states)

### State-Specific Variations

- **Delhi:** Age 18+, annual family income below ₹1,00,000
- **Rajasthan:** Age 18+ with no income limit for destitute widows
- **UP:** Age 18–60, annual income below ₹2,00,000 under state scheme
- **Maharashtra:** Age 18+, annual income below ₹1,00,000 (Sanjay Gandhi Niradhar Yojana)

## Documents Required

### Mandatory Documents
- **Husband's death certificate** — issued by municipal corporation or Gram Panchayat
- **Age proof** — [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online), voter ID, or birth certificate
- **Identity proof** — Aadhaar card or voter ID
- **Address proof** — Aadhaar card, voter ID, or [ration card](/guide/ration-card-apply-online)
- **BPL certificate** or BPL ration card (for IGNWPS)
- **Income certificate** — from Tehsildar/SDM (for state schemes)
- **Bank passbook** — first page showing account number, IFSC, and name

### Additional Documents
- Passport-size photographs (2–4)
- FIR copy (if husband died in accident/unnatural death — some states)
- Self-declaration of not having remarried
- Caste certificate (if applicable)

## How to Apply for Widow Pension Online

### Step-by-Step Process (Most States)

1. **Visit your state's social security portal** (see links below)
2. **Register/Login** — Create an account using mobile number and Aadhaar
3. **Select scheme** — Choose "Widow Pension" or "Vidhwa Pension Yojana"
4. **Fill the form** — Enter personal details, husband's death details, income, bank account
5. **Upload documents** — Death certificate, Aadhaar, income certificate, bank passbook, photo
6. **Submit application** — Save the application number for tracking
7. **Track status** — Use the reference number on the same portal

### Offline Application Process

1. Visit your **Block Development Office (BDO)**, Tehsil, or District Social Welfare Office
2. Collect the widow pension application form
3. Fill and attach all required documents
4. Submit the form; collect the receipt/acknowledgement
5. Field verification will be done by local authorities
6. After approval, pension is credited to your bank account via DBT

## Processing Time

- **Online applications:** 30–60 days in most states
- **Offline applications:** 60–90 days
- **Verification:** 15–30 days by local committee
- **Pension credit:** Monthly via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to bank account after approval

---

## Important Tips

1. **Get death certificate first** — This is the most critical document. Apply at your municipal office or Gram Panchayat within 21 days of death
2. **Aadhaar-bank linking is mandatory** — Pension is transferred via DBT, so ensure your Aadhaar is linked to your bank account
3. **Submit life certificate annually** — Use [Jeevan Pramaan](https://jeevanpramaan.gov.in/) to submit digital life certificate
4. **Apply through CSC** — If you cannot apply online yourself, visit your nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) for assistance
5. **No duplicate pension** — You cannot receive widow pension if you already get pension from another government scheme

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. What is the age limit for widow pension?
Under IGNWPS, the age range is **40–79 years**. However, many state schemes allow applications from age 18. After age 80, widows are covered under the old age pension scheme ([IGNOAPS](/guide/old-age-pension-apply-online)).

### Q2. Is BPL card mandatory for widow pension?
For the central IGNWPS scheme, yes. But many state schemes use income criteria instead — for example, Delhi requires annual income below ₹1,00,000.

### Q3. How much pension does a widow get per month?
The central government pays ₹300/month (age 40–79). With state top-up, total ranges from ₹400 to ₹2,500/month depending on the state.

### Q4. Can a widow get pension if she remarries?
In most states, no — remarriage makes you ineligible for widow pension. However, some state schemes may have different rules.

### Q5. What if I don't have my husband's death certificate?
You must obtain a death certificate. Apply at your municipal corporation, Gram Panchayat, or through the [CRS portal](https://crsorgi.gov.in/). Late registration may require an affidavit.

### Q6. Can I apply for widow pension at a CSC centre?
Yes, you can visit any **Common Service Centre (CSC)** near you. They will help fill the online form and upload documents for a nominal fee.

### Q7. How to check widow pension status online?
Visit your state's pension portal (links above), go to "Track Application" or "Application Status", and enter your reference number or Aadhaar number.
