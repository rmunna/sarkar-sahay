# Widow Pension Apply Online: Eligibility, Documents & State-Wise Process

The **Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme (IGNWPS)** provides monthly financial assistance to widows from BPL families. Along with the central scheme, every state runs its own widow pension programme with additional benefits. This guide covers everything you need to apply for widow pension online or offline.

## What is IGNWPS (Widow Pension Scheme)?

The **Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme** is part of the **National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP)** run by the Ministry of Rural Development. It provides a basic pension to widows belonging to Below Poverty Line (BPL) families.

**Central government contribution:**
- **Age 40-79 years:** ₹300 per month
- **Age 80+ years:** ₹500 per month

Most states provide additional top-up amounts, making total pension range from ₹500 to ₹2,500 per month.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Marital status:** Must be a widow (husband deceased)
2. **Age:** Typically 40-79 years for ₹300/month; 80+ for ₹500/month (age varies by state — some states start from 18 years)
3. **Income/BPL:** Must belong to BPL family or meet state income criteria
4. **Residency:** Must be a permanent resident of the state
5. **Not remarried:** Should not have remarried (applicable in most states)
6. **Not receiving other pension:** Should not be receiving any other government pension
7. **Bank account:** Must have [Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)-linked bank account

## Documents Required

### Mandatory Documents
- **Death certificate of husband** (issued by municipal/gram panchayat)
- **Age proof:** [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online), birth certificate, voter ID, or school leaving certificate
- **Identity proof:** Aadhaar card, voter ID
- **Address proof:** Aadhaar card, [ration card](/guide/ration-card-apply-online), voter ID
- **Income certificate** or **BPL card** (from Tehsildar/Block office)
- **Bank passbook** (first page with account details)
- **Passport-size photographs** (2-4 copies)

### Additional Documents (State-Specific)
- **Ration card** (UP, Bihar, MP)
- **Samagra ID** (Madhya Pradesh)
- **FIR copy** (if death was unnatural — some states)
- **Self-declaration** that applicant has not remarried

## How to Apply for Widow Pension Online

### Step 1: Visit Your State Portal

- **Uttar Pradesh:** [sspy-up.gov.in](https://sspy-up.gov.in/) → Widow Pension (निराश्रित महिला पेंशन)
- **Bihar:** [elabharthi.bih.nic.in](https://elabharthi.bih.nic.in/)
- **Rajasthan:** [rajssp.rajasthan.gov.in](https://rajssp.rajasthan.gov.in/)
- **Madhya Pradesh:** [pension.socialsecurity.mp.gov.in](https://pension.socialsecurity.mp.gov.in/)
- **Delhi:** [edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in](https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in/)
- **Haryana:** [pension.socialjusticehry.gov.in](https://pension.socialjusticehry.gov.in/)
- **Maharashtra:** [aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in](https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in/)
- **Karnataka:** [sw.kar.nic.in](https://sw.kar.nic.in/)

### Step 2: Register and Login

1. Click on "Widow Pension" or "Vidhwa Pension" section
2. Register with your mobile number (new users)
3. Verify OTP and set password
4. Login with your credentials

### Step 3: Fill Application Form

1. Enter personal details: name, date of birth, gender, husband's name
2. Enter husband's death date and death certificate number
3. Enter address: district, block/tehsil, village/ward
4. Enter bank account details with IFSC code
5. Enter Aadhaar number

### Step 4: Upload Documents

1. Upload husband's death certificate
2. Upload identity and address proof
3. Upload income certificate / BPL card
4. Upload bank passbook photo
5. Upload passport-size photograph

### Step 5: Submit and Note Reference Number

1. Review all details
2. Submit the application
3. Save your **application/reference number**
4. Track status on the same portal

## How to Apply Offline

1. Visit your **Block Development Office (BDO)** or **District Social Welfare Office**
2. Collect widow pension application form (free)
3. Fill the form and attach required documents
4. Submit and collect acknowledgement receipt
5. Block office verifies and forwards to sanctioning authority

**Processing time:** 1-3 months typically

## Important Tips

1. **Get death certificate first** — This is the most critical document; apply at your municipal office or gram panchayat immediately after the death
2. **Ensure Aadhaar-bank linking** — Without DBT linking, pension cannot be credited
3. **Visit CSC for help** — Common Service Centres can help fill and submit the online application
4. **Complete Jeevan Pramaan annually** — Life certificate is mandatory to continue receiving pension
5. **Check for one-time assistance** — Many states offer a one-time lump sum (₹20,000-₹50,000) for widows in addition to monthly pension

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: What is the minimum age to apply for widow pension?
**A:** Under IGNWPS (central), the minimum age is 40 years. However, most states like UP, Bihar, Delhi, and Rajasthan allow widows from age 18 to apply under their state schemes.

### Q2: How much pension does a widow get per month?
**A:** The central IGNWPS gives ₹300/month (40-79 years) and ₹500/month (80+). With state top-up, total ranges from ₹400 (Bihar) to ₹2,750 (Haryana) depending on your state.

### Q3: Can I get widow pension if I remarry?
**A:** In most states, widow pension stops upon remarriage. However, a few states have provisions for destitute women regardless of marital status. Check your state's specific rules.

### Q4: My husband's death certificate is not available. Can I still apply?
**A:** Death certificate is mandatory. If not available, apply for it at your municipal corporation or gram panchayat first. Some states accept an FIR copy or hospital death report temporarily.

### Q5: How do I check widow pension status online?
**A:** Visit your state pension portal, go to the "Check Application Status" section, and enter your application/reference number to see the current status.

### Q6: Can government employees' widows apply for this scheme?
**A:** No. IGNWPS and state widow pension schemes are for BPL/low-income families. Government employees' widows receive [family pension](/guide/family-pension-apply-guide) under different rules.

### Q7: What if my pension payment is delayed or stopped?
**A:** Visit your block/district social welfare office. Common reasons include: bank account issues, Aadhaar mismatch, or missed life certificate. Carry all documents for resolution.
