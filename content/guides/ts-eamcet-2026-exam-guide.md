# TS EAMCET 2026 (TSEAPCET) — Telangana State Engineering Agriculture & Pharmacy CET

**TS EAMCET 2026** — officially **TSEAPCET (Telangana State Engineering, Agriculture and Pharmacy Common Entrance Test)** — is conducted by **JNTUH (Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University Hyderabad)** on behalf of **TSCHE (Telangana State Council of Higher Education)** for engineering (PCM) and agriculture/pharmacy (PCB) admissions in Telangana colleges.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [tseamcet.nic.in](https://tseamcet.nic.in). Telangana domicile (10-year residence OR 7-year school study) required for state quota. Two streams: Engineering and Agriculture/Pharmacy.

## Eligibility Criteria

| Criteria | Engineering | Agriculture / Pharmacy |
|----------|-------------|----------------------|
| **Qualification** | Class 12 with PCM | Class 12 with PCB |
| **Minimum marks** | 45% in PCM (40% SC/ST) | 45% in PCB |
| **TS nativity** | 10 years residence OR 7 years school study in TS | Same |

### Telangana Nativity — Two Routes

For state quota seats in TS EAMCET:
1. **10 years continuous residence** in Telangana (supported by voter ID, ration card, property records)
2. **Studied in Telangana schools for 7+ years** (Transfer Certificates from TS schools)

**Agriculture / Pharmacy Stream (PCB):**

| Subject | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| Biology | 80 | 80 |
| Physics | 40 | 40 |
| Chemistry | 40 | 40 |
| **Total** | **160** | **160** |

- **Mode**: Computer-Based Test (CBT)
- **Duration**: 3 hours
- **Negative marking**: No negative marking
- **Marks**: 1 mark per correct answer

## Syllabus 2026

Based on **Telangana Board of Intermediate Education (TSBIE) Class 11 & 12 syllabus**:

**Mathematics**: Algebra, Trigonometry, Vector Algebra, Differential & Integral Calculus, Coordinate Geometry, Probability

**Physics**: Physical World, Kinematics, Laws of Motion, Work & Energy, Thermal Properties, Electrostatics, Current Electricity, Magnetism, Optics, Modern Physics

**Chemistry**: Atomic Structure, Classification of Elements, Chemical Bonding, States of Matter, Thermodynamics, Equilibrium, Organic Chemistry, Amines, Biomolecules

## Local Area Quota in TS EAMCET

Telangana reserves seats based on study location:
- **Telangana Area**: Students who studied in Telangana districts
- **Hyderabad**: Special provisions for students who studied in GHMC area

## TS EAMCET vs AP EAMCET — Key Differences

| Aspect | TS EAMCET | AP EAMCET (APEAPCET) |
|--------|-----------|---------------------|
| Conducted by | JNTUH on behalf TSCHE | JNTU Kakinada on behalf APSCHE |
| State | Telangana | Andhra Pradesh |
| Exam pattern | 160 questions / 160 marks | 160 questions / 160 marks |
| Domicile rule | 10 yrs residence OR 7 yrs school | 10 yrs residence OR 7 yrs school |

Both exams have similar patterns since AP and Telangana bifurcated in 2014.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I appear in both TS EAMCET and AP EAMCET?

**Yes** — you can apply to both. TS EAMCET for Telangana colleges, AP EAMCET for Andhra Pradesh colleges. Each exam has its own fee and application. Joint counselling between the two states is not available.

### NEET for MBBS in Telangana — is TS EAMCET needed?

**No** — MBBS/BDS in Telangana uses NEET score (not TS EAMCET). TS EAMCET is for engineering, pharmacy (B.Pharm), and agriculture courses only.

### TS EAMCET rank calculation

TS EAMCET rank = based on raw exam score. **Bonus marks** are added for:
- Students from Telugu medium schools
- NCC cadets
- Children of ex-servicemen
