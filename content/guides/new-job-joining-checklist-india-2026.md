# New Job Joining Checklist India 2026 — Everything You Must Do

Starting a new job involves more than just showing up on day one. Tax implications, PF transfer, insurance gaps, and investment declarations — missing any of these creates problems. Here's the complete checklist.

---

## Before Day 1 — Verify These

| Item | Action |
|------|--------|
| **Offer letter vs Appointment letter** | Confirm you've received the appointment letter (formal employment document) — the offer letter is just an intent. Your appointment letter should match the agreed terms |
| **Salary structure in writing** | CTC breakup, fixed/variable split, joining bonus terms (clawback period?) |
| **Joining bonus clawback clause** | Some joining bonuses must be returned if you leave within 12–24 months — read before signing |
| **Notice period clause** | Your new employer's notice period for you to leave; must be acceptable |
| **Non-compete clause** | Review if it restricts your future employment unfairly; some are legally unenforceable |
| **NDA terms** | Read what information is classified and what you can discuss publicly |

---

## Day 1 — Documents to Carry

Most companies require a physical document submission on joining:

| Document | Why Needed |
|----------|-----------|
| **PAN card** | TDS deduction and Form 16 |
| **Aadhaar card** | Identity, KYC |
| **2 passport photos** | ID card, HR records |
| **Previous employer's relieving letter** | Confirms clean exit; some companies verify |
| **Previous employer's experience letter** | Employment history proof |
| **Last 3 payslips** | Salary verification |
| **Last 3 months bank statements** | Some companies ask for this |
| **Form 16 (last 2 years)** | For tax verification |
| **Educational certificates** | Original + photocopies (degree, 10th, 12th) |
| **Cancelled cheque** | For salary account setup |
| **Passport** (if required for background check) | BGV (Background Verification) |
| **Current UAN number** | PF transfer process |

## First Week — Tax Actions

### Submit Form 12B to New Employer (Critical)

**Form 12B**: Declaration of salary received from previous employer in the same financial year.

- Why it matters: Without this, your new employer doesn't know your income from the old job and may calculate wrong TDS
- Common result of missing Form 12B: Under-deduction of TDS from April → large TDS deduction in February-March to compensate, OR short payment of taxes

**What to include in Form 12B**:
- Name and address of previous employer
- PAN of previous employer
- Duration of employment (months)
- Gross salary received
- Allowances claimed (HRA, LTA, etc.)
- Deductions under Chapter VI-A (80C, 80D etc.)
- TDS already deducted by previous employer

Download Form 12B from incometax.gov.in and submit to new employer's payroll/finance team.

### Submit Investment Declaration — Form 12BB

**Form 12BB**: You declare planned investments for TDS calculation for the year.

Tell your new employer what deductions you plan to claim:
- Section 80C: PPF, ELSS, LIC, home loan principal, tuition fees
- Section 80D: Health insurance premium (self, family, parents)
- HRA: If applicable (self-declaration of rent paid)
- Home loan interest (Section 24(b))
- LTA: Leave Travel Allowance if claiming

Without declaration: Employer calculates TDS assuming no deductions → excess TDS deducted → refund only via ITR filing.

### Health Insurance: Group Policy Activation Gap

**Critical gap**: Old company group health insurance stops on last working day. New company group insurance activates after enrollment (which can take 30–60 days).

**Gap risk**: Medical emergency during the gap = out-of-pocket expense.

**Solution**:
1. Buy a short-term individual health insurance policy to cover the gap
2. Or check if your new company allows immediate enrollment from day 1
3. Activate new group insurance enrollment as soon as HR portal opens

## PF and Retirement — Actions Required

### Transfer PF from Previous Employer

Don't withdraw PF if changing jobs — transfer it to maintain continuous service for gratuity and higher interest.

**How to transfer PF (online)**:
1. Log in to [unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in)
2. Go to Online Services → One Member – One EPF Account (Transfer Request)
3. Enter previous and new employer details
4. Previous employer's PF account number automatically populated
5. Submit transfer request (authorized by your old or new employer)
6. Transfer completes in 20–30 days

**UAN is yours** — same UAN across all employers. Only the PF account number changes with each employer.

### If Employer Joins You in EPFO

Confirm new employer has linked your UAN to their establishment on EPFO. They do this while activating your PF contribution. Without this, your PF contributions don't get deposited.

### NPS (National Pension System)

If you had NPS through your previous employer (corporate plan), inform new employer's HR to map your PRAN to their corporate NPS account. PRAN is permanent across employers — just needs mapping.

## Aadhaar Update if You Relocated

If you moved cities for the new job:
- Update new address in Aadhaar via [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in) or nearest Aadhaar seva kendra
- Address in Aadhaar affects KYC for bank accounts, mutual fund folios opened with the new address

## Salary Account — New Bank or Existing?

Most companies open a salary account at their partner bank. You can also request your existing bank account if the employer allows.

**If new bank account opened**: Set up necessary bill payments and standing instructions (SIP auto-debit, loan EMI, LIC premium) in the new account as soon as it activates.

**Old salary account**: Convert to a savings account (zero balance, but no longer salary account). This changes the interest rate and some features — check with the bank.

## Background Verification — What to Expect

Most mid-to-large companies run BGV (Background Verification) after joining:
- **Employment history check**: Verifies you worked where you said
- **Education check**: Degree certificates verified with universities
- **Criminal record check**: Police records
- **Reference check**: HR may call your provided references
- **Address verification**: Field agent visits your address

**Do not misrepresent anything on your resume** — BGV catches discrepancies and employment can be terminated.

**BGV takes**: 2–6 weeks; your employment is "conditional" until completed.

## First Salary — Verify These

When you receive the first payslip:
- Is the salary structure what was agreed?
- Is TDS correctly calculated (considering your Form 12B submission)?
- Is PF being deducted at correct rate?
- Is any joining bonus reflected (or on a separate timeline)?

If anything is wrong, flag it immediately to HR/payroll.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My previous company hasn't given my relieving letter. Will the new company reject my joining?

Most companies require it but allow a grace period. Many do BGV only after 2–3 months — you have time to chase the old employer for the relieving letter. If old employer is deliberately withholding, see our guide on dealing with withheld experience letters.

### I'm joining in November. How does Form 12B work?

You joined in November and worked for the old company April–October. Fill Form 12B with that 7-month income. Your new employer will add their income for November–March and calculate correct annual TDS.

### My new company says they can't take the previous PF account for transfer because the number is wrong. What to do?

Login to EPFO portal with your UAN and verify the previous employer's PF account number. If there's an error, raise a grievance on EPFO portal or visit local PF office with proof.

### Should I submit investment declarations on the first day or wait?

Submit as early as possible. The sooner you declare, the more evenly your TDS is distributed across months. If you declare in December, your January–March TDS will be heavy to compensate.
