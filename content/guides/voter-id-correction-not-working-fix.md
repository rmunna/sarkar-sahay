# Voter ID Card Online Correction Not Working — How to Fix

Need to fix your name, photo, address, or date of birth on your Voter ID (EPIC) card? The Election Commission of India allows corrections through Form 8 on the [NVSP portal](https://www.nvsp.in/) and the Voter Helpline app. However, the process frequently fails due to portal errors, document rejections, and verification delays. This guide covers every common correction problem and how to resolve it.

## Common Error Messages and Symptoms

- **"Unable to submit Form 8"** — form submission fails on NVSP
- **"Photo upload failed"** — image rejected during correction
- **"OTP not received"** — mobile verification OTP doesn't arrive
- **"Application rejected by ERO"** — Electoral Registration Officer rejected Form 8
- **"BLO verification pending"** — correction stuck at field verification stage
- **"Invalid document format"** — uploaded proof not accepted
- Portal shows "Service temporarily unavailable" during correction
- Form 8 submitted but correction status shows no progress for weeks
- Correction approved but old details still showing on e-EPIC download

## Why Voter ID Correction Fails Online

| Reason | Impact |
|--------|--------|
| NVSP server overload during election season | Form submission timeout or error |
| Photo doesn't meet size/format requirements | Upload rejected instantly |
| Supporting documents unclear or mismatched | ERO rejects Form 8 |
| Mobile number not linked to Voter ID | OTP delivery fails |
| BLO unable to visit or verify | Application stuck indefinitely |
| Duplicate or conflicting records | System flags correction as suspicious |
| Browser compatibility issues | Form doesn't load or submit properly |

## Fix 1: NVSP Portal Errors During Correction

If the NVSP portal throws errors or doesn't load the correction form:

1. **Use a desktop browser** — Chrome or Firefox on a laptop/PC works best. Avoid mobile browsers for Form 8.
2. **Clear browser cache** — Old session data causes submission failures. Clear cache and cookies, then retry.
3. **Try off-peak hours** — The portal is least loaded between 10 PM and 8 AM IST.
4. **Disable browser extensions** — Ad blockers and VPNs can interfere with the NVSP portal.
5. **Use the Voter Helpline app** — Download the official app ([Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.eci.citizen) / iOS) as an alternative to the website.
6. **Check portal status** — Visit [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in/) to see if services are under maintenance.

> **Tip:** If the portal shows "Session Expired" repeatedly, try using an incognito/private browsing window.

## Fix 2: Form 8 Rejected by ERO

Form 8 (Application for Correction of Entries in Electoral Roll) can be rejected by the Electoral Registration Officer for several reasons:

### Common Rejection Reasons and Solutions

- **"Insufficient proof for name change"** — Submit a gazette notification, marriage certificate, or court order along with an updated Aadhaar/passport showing the new name.
- **"Address proof doesn't match constituency"** — Your address proof must show an address within the same assembly constituency. Use a utility bill, bank statement, or rent agreement for the current address.
- **"Photo mismatch"** — The photo on your correction form must clearly match you. Submit a recent passport-sized photo with white background.
- **"Date of birth proof not acceptable"** — Use your birth certificate, school leaving certificate (Class 10), or passport. Aadhaar alone may not be accepted for DOB correction in some states.

### How to Resubmit After Rejection

1. Log in to [NVSP](https://www.nvsp.in/) → Check application status → Note the rejection reason
2. Prepare the correct/missing documents
3. File a fresh Form 8 with the corrected information and stronger supporting documents
4. You can also visit your [ERO office directly](#fix-7-offline-correction-at-ero-office) for faster processing

## Fix 3: Photo Upload Failed

The NVSP portal has strict photo requirements. Your upload will fail if:

- **File size exceeds 200 KB** — Compress using [TinyPNG](https://tinypng.com/) or any image compressor
- **Format is not JPG/JPEG** — Convert PNG, HEIC, or other formats to JPG before uploading
- **Dimensions are wrong** — Use a passport-sized photo (3.5 cm × 4.5 cm / 200×250 pixels minimum)
- **Background is not plain** — Use a white or light-coloured background
- **Face is not clearly visible** — Remove sunglasses, ensure good lighting, face the camera directly

### Working Photo Upload Steps

1. Take a recent photo against a white wall with good lighting
2. Crop to passport size (use any free photo editor)
3. Save as `.jpg` format, under 200 KB
4. If upload still fails, try a different browser or the Voter Helpline app
5. As a last resort, submit the correction offline with a physical photo at the ERO office

## Fix 4: OTP Not Received

OTP verification is required to submit corrections online. If you're not receiving the OTP:

1. **Check your registered mobile number** — The OTP goes to the number linked to your Voter ID, not necessarily your current number. Verify on [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in/).
2. **Wait 2-3 minutes** — NVSP OTPs can be delayed during high traffic.
3. **Check SMS filters** — Look in spam/blocked messages. Add NVSP sender IDs to your safe list.
4. **Request OTP again** — Wait for the cooldown timer and click "Resend OTP." Don't request more than 3 times in a row.
5. **Try after some time** — If multiple resends fail, wait 30 minutes and try again.
6. **Update your mobile number first** — If your number has changed, you must first update it on the Voter Portal or visit the ERO office with ID proof and new SIM documentation.

> **Important:** If your mobile number is not linked to your Voter ID at all, you cannot complete online correction. Visit the ERO office to link your number first.

## Fix 5: BLO Verification Pending

After Form 8 submission, the Booth Level Officer (BLO) may need to verify your details in person. This step often causes delays:

### Why BLO Verification Gets Stuck

- BLO assigned to your area hasn't visited yet
- You weren't available when the BLO came
- BLO workload is high during election preparation periods
- Your application fell through the cracks in the system

### How to Speed Up BLO Verification

1. **Track your application** — Check status on [NVSP](https://www.nvsp.in/) or the Voter Helpline app using your reference number
2. **Contact your BLO directly** — Find BLO details on [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in/) → "Know Your BLO" → Enter your EPIC number or address
3. **Call the Voter Helpline** — Dial **1950** (toll-free) and provide your application reference number
4. **Be available at home** — BLOs typically visit between 10 AM and 5 PM on weekdays
5. **Visit the ERO office** — If BLO verification has been pending for more than 30 days, go to your ERO/AERO office with your documents and request expedited processing
6. **Keep your phone accessible** — The BLO may call before visiting; answer calls from unknown numbers during this period

## Fix 6: How to Track Correction Status

You can track your Form 8 correction application through multiple channels:

### Online Tracking

1. **NVSP Portal:** Go to [nvsp.in](https://www.nvsp.in/) → "Track Application Status" → Enter your Reference ID
2. **Voter Portal:** Visit [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in/) → Log in → "Track Application"
3. **Voter Helpline App:** Open the app → "Track Application" → Enter reference number

### Status Meanings

| Status | What It Means |
|--------|--------------|
| Submitted | Form received, pending review |
| Under Verification | BLO/ERO is reviewing your application |
| BLO Verification Pending | Awaiting field visit by BLO |
| Approved | Correction accepted, updated roll will reflect changes |
| Rejected | ERO rejected — check reason and resubmit |
| Disposed | Application processed (check if approved or rejected) |

### Other Ways to Check

- **SMS Updates:** You'll receive SMS notifications at your registered number for status changes
- **Voter Helpline:** Call **1950** with your reference number
- **ERO Office:** Visit in person with your reference number and original EPIC card

> **Note:** After approval, changes reflect in the electoral roll during the next revision. Download your updated [e-EPIC from the Voter Portal](/guide/voter-id-download-not-working-fix) once the roll is updated.

## Fix 7: Offline Correction at ERO Office {#fix-7-offline-correction-at-ero-office}

If online correction repeatedly fails, the offline route through the ERO (Electoral Registration Officer) office is reliable and often faster:

### Documents to Carry

- Original Voter ID card (EPIC)
- Filled Form 8 (download from [nvsp.in](https://www.nvsp.in/) or collect from ERO office)
- Supporting documents for the correction:
  - **Name correction:** Aadhaar, passport, PAN, gazette notification, marriage certificate
  - **Address correction:** Utility bill, bank statement, rent agreement, Aadhaar with new address
  - **DOB correction:** Birth certificate, Class 10 marksheet, passport
  - **Photo correction:** Two recent passport-sized photographs
- Photo ID proof (Aadhaar, passport, or driving licence)

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Find your ERO office** — Search on [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in/) → "Know Your Electoral Registration Officer"
2. **Fill Form 8** — Complete all fields. Mention your EPIC number and the specific correction needed
3. **Attach documents** — Self-attested photocopies of supporting documents
4. **Submit at ERO office** — Get an acknowledgement receipt with a reference number
5. **BLO visit** — A BLO may visit your address for verification (keep documents ready)
6. **Track status** — Use the reference number to track online or call 1950

> **Pro tip:** Visit the ERO office early morning (10-11 AM) on working days to avoid long queues. Carry originals for verification even though you submit photocopies.

## What Corrections Can Be Made via Form 8?

- Full name (first name, middle name, surname)
- Father's/mother's/husband's name
- Date of birth / age
- Gender
- Photograph on the EPIC card
- Address (within the same constituency)
- Mobile number and email ID

> **Note:** For address change to a different constituency, you need to file **Form 6** (new registration) instead of Form 8. Your old entry will be deleted via Form 7. If your [application gets rejected](/guide/voter-id-application-rejected-fix), check the linked guide for solutions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does Voter ID correction take?
Online corrections typically take 15-30 days. During election season or roll revision, it may take up to 45-60 days.

### Can I correct multiple fields at once?
Yes, you can request corrections for multiple fields (name, DOB, photo, etc.) in a single Form 8 submission.

### Is there a fee for Voter ID correction?
No, Voter ID correction through Form 8 is completely free — both online and offline.

### My correction was approved but the old photo/name still shows. What do I do?
Changes appear after the next electoral roll revision. Download a fresh [e-EPIC](/guide/voter-id-download-not-working-fix) after the roll is updated. If it still shows old details after 60 days, contact your ERO office.

### Can I correct my Voter ID without Aadhaar?
Yes. Aadhaar is not mandatory. You can use passport, driving licence, PAN card, or other government-issued ID as supporting proof.

## Still Not Working?

If none of the above solutions work:

1. **File a grievance** on the [ECI Grievance Portal](https://eci.gov.in/) or through the Voter Helpline app
2. **Call 1950** (National Voter Helpline) — available Monday to Saturday, 10 AM to 5 PM
3. **Email your state CEO office** — Find email addresses on [eci.gov.in](https://eci.gov.in/)
4. **Visit the ERO office in person** — This remains the most reliable method for corrections
