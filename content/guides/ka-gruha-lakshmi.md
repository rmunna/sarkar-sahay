# Gruha Lakshmi Scheme Karnataka – ₹2,000/Month for Women

## What is Gruha Lakshmi Scheme?

Gruha Lakshmi is a flagship welfare scheme launched by the Government of Karnataka to provide **₹2,000 per month** (₹24,000 per year) to women who are the head of their household. This scheme is one of the five guarantee schemes introduced by the Karnataka government to support women financially and promote their economic independence.

The amount is directly transferred to the beneficiary's bank account every month through **Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)**.

## Who is Eligible for Gruha Lakshmi?

To receive benefits under Gruha Lakshmi, the applicant must meet the following criteria:

1. **Gender:** Must be a **woman** who is the head of the household
2. **Residency:** Must be a **resident of Karnataka**
3. **Ration Card:** Must hold a valid **BPL (Below Poverty Line)**, **Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY)**, or **Above Poverty Line (APL)** ration card issued by the Karnataka government
4. **One per household:** Only **one woman per household** (as per ration card) can avail the benefit
5. **Age:** Must be **18 years or above**
6. **Income:** No specific income limit, but linked to ration card category

### Who is NOT Eligible?

- Women whose family members are income tax payers
- Women who are already receiving pension from the government (above ₹2,000/month)
- Women whose family members hold government jobs (Group A or B officers)
- More than one woman from the same ration card household

## Documents Required

### Mandatory Documents
- **Ration Card** (BPL/AAY/APL) — Karnataka issued
- **Aadhaar Card** of the applicant
- **Bank Account Details** — must be linked to Aadhaar for DBT
- **Mobile Number** linked to Aadhaar

### Supporting Documents
- **Voter ID** or other address proof (if needed)
- **Recent Passport Size Photo**

> 💡 **Tip:** Ensure your Aadhaar is linked to your bank account. See our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) for help with Aadhaar-related issues.

## How to Apply for Gruha Lakshmi Scheme

### Online Application

1. **Visit** the official Gruha Lakshmi portal at [gruhalakshmi.karnataka.gov.in](https://gruhalakshmi.karnataka.gov.in)
2. **Click** on "Apply Now" or "New Registration"
3. **Enter** your ration card number to fetch household details
4. **Select** the woman head of household from the ration card
5. **Enter Aadhaar number** and verify with OTP
6. **Fill in bank account details** (account number, IFSC code)
7. **Upload** required documents (photo, Aadhaar, ration card)
8. **Submit** the application
9. **Note down** the application reference number for tracking

### Offline Application

1. **Visit** your nearest **Bangalore One / Karnataka One** centre or **Gram Panchayat office**
2. **Carry** original documents and photocopies
3. **Fill** the Gruha Lakshmi application form
4. **Submit** along with required documents
5. **Collect** the acknowledgment receipt

### Through Seva Sindhu Portal

1. Visit [sevasindhuservices.karnataka.gov.in](https://sevasindhuservices.karnataka.gov.in)
2. Register/login with your mobile number
3. Search for "Gruha Lakshmi"
4. Fill the application form and submit

## How to Check Gruha Lakshmi Application Status

1. Visit the official portal
2. Click on "Track Application" or "Check Status"
3. Enter your application reference number or ration card number
4. View the current status of your application

## Processing Time

- **Online Application:** 15-30 days for verification
- **First Payment:** Within 30-45 days of approval
- **Subsequent Payments:** Monthly credit to bank account

## Important Tips

1. **Link Aadhaar to Bank:** Ensure your Aadhaar is seeded with your bank account before applying. Without this, DBT payments will fail.
2. **One Application Per Ration Card:** Only one woman per ration card can apply. If multiple women are listed, the head of household gets priority.
3. **Keep Documents Ready:** Have all original documents when visiting offline centres to avoid multiple trips.
4. **Track Your Application:** Regularly check your application status on the portal using your reference number.
5. **Report Issues:** If payment is delayed beyond 2 months, visit your nearest Gram Panchayat or taluk office with your acknowledgment receipt.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Can more than one woman from the same family apply?
**No.** Only one woman per ration card (household) is eligible. The woman listed as head of household on the ration card gets priority.

### Q2: What if I don't have a ration card?
You must have a valid Karnataka ration card to apply. If you don't have one, apply for a ration card first through the [Karnataka Food Department portal](https://ahara.kar.nic.in).

### Q3: Is there an income limit for Gruha Lakshmi?
There is no direct income limit. However, if any family member is an income tax payer or a Group A/B government officer, the household is not eligible.

### Q4: Can I apply if I already receive a widow pension?
If you receive a government pension of more than ₹2,000/month, you may not be eligible. If the pension is less than ₹2,000, you may receive the difference. Check with your local office for clarity.

### Q5: How will I receive the money?
The ₹2,000 is directly transferred to your Aadhaar-linked bank account every month via DBT.

### Q6: Is this scheme only for BPL families?
No. Women from BPL, AAY, and APL ration card categories can all apply, subject to eligibility conditions.

### Q7: What is the last date to apply?
There is no fixed last date. The scheme is ongoing and applications are accepted continuously.
