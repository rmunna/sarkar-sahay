## What is GST Registration?

GST (Goods and Services Tax) is India's unified indirect tax that replaced multiple taxes like VAT, Service Tax, and Excise Duty. GST registration gives you a unique 15-digit **GSTIN** (GST Identification Number) that allows you to collect tax from customers, claim input tax credit, and file GST returns.

GST registration is done online through the official GST portal at **gst.gov.in** and is completely **free of cost**. The process typically takes **3-7 working days** after submission.

## Who Needs GST Registration?

GST registration is **mandatory** if:

- Your **annual turnover exceeds ₹40 lakh** (₹20 lakh for special category states)
- For **service providers**, the threshold is **₹20 lakh** (₹10 lakh for special category states)
- You make **inter-state supply** of goods or services (no threshold — registration mandatory from day one)
- You sell on **e-commerce platforms** (Amazon, Flipkart, etc.)
- You are an **e-commerce operator**
- You are a **casual taxable person** or **non-resident taxable person**
- You are required to **deduct TDS** under GST
- You are an **input service distributor**
- You supply goods/services through an **agent or broker**

> **Special Category States:** Arunachal Pradesh, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Puducherry, Sikkim, Telangana, Tripura, and Uttarakhand have lower thresholds.

## Documents Required

### For Proprietorship
- **PAN card** of the proprietor — [Apply for PAN card](/guide/pan-card-apply-online)
- **Aadhaar card** of the proprietor — [Apply for Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
- Photograph (passport size)
- Bank account details (cancelled cheque or passbook first page)
- Address proof of business premises (electricity bill, rent agreement, or property tax receipt)

### For Partnership / LLP / Company
- PAN card of the entity
- PAN and Aadhaar of all partners/directors
- Partnership deed / Certificate of Incorporation
- Authorisation letter or board resolution
- Photograph of authorised signatory
- Bank account details of the entity
- Address proof of business premises

### Address Proof of Business (any one)
- Electricity bill (not older than 2 months)
- Rent/lease agreement with NOC from owner
- Property tax receipt
- Municipal Khata copy
- Ownership deed / Sale deed

## Step-by-Step Online Process

### Step 1: Generate TRN
1. Visit **[gst.gov.in](https://www.gst.gov.in)**
2. Click **"Register Now"** under the "Taxpayers" section
3. Select **"New Registration"**
4. Choose your state and district
5. Enter your **business name**, **PAN**, **email**, and **mobile number**
6. Verify email and mobile with OTP
7. You will receive a **TRN (Temporary Reference Number)**

### Step 2: Complete Application
1. Go to gst.gov.in → **Services** → **Registration** → **New Registration**
2. Select **"Temporary Reference Number (TRN)"** and enter your TRN
3. Verify with OTP sent to registered email/mobile
4. You will see your saved application — click **Edit** to continue

### Step 3: Fill Business Details
Complete all 10 tabs in the application:
1. **Business Details** — Legal name, trade name, constitution of business
2. **Promoter/Partner Details** — Personal info, PAN, Aadhaar, photo
3. **Authorised Signatory** — Details of the primary authorised person
4. **Authorised Representative** — If appointing a GST practitioner
5. **Principal Place of Business** — Address, contact, nature of premises
6. **Additional Place of Business** — If any branch offices
7. **Goods and Services** — HSN codes for goods, SAC codes for services (up to 5 each)
8. **State Specific Information** — Professional tax number etc.
9. **Aadhaar Authentication** — Choose to authenticate via Aadhaar
10. **Verification** — Review and submit using DSC/EVC/e-Sign

### Step 4: Submit and Get ARN
1. After verification, submit the application
2. You will receive an **ARN (Application Reference Number)** via email and SMS
3. Save this ARN — use it to track your application status

### Step 5: Track Application
1. Visit gst.gov.in → **Services** → **Registration** → **Track Application Status**
2. Enter your ARN
3. Check status: Pending, Approved, Query Raised, or Rejected

### Step 6: Receive GSTIN
- If approved, you will receive your **15-digit GSTIN** via email
- Download your GST registration certificate from the portal
- GSTIN format: First 2 digits = State code, next 10 = PAN, next 2 = entity code, last 1 = checksum

## Aadhaar Authentication

Since 2020, **Aadhaar authentication is mandatory** for GST registration (for proprietors, partners, and directors). If you opt for Aadhaar authentication:
- Registration is processed within **3 working days**
- If you skip Aadhaar authentication, the officer may conduct **physical verification** of business premises, which takes longer

## Important Tips

1. **Use Aadhaar authentication** — it speeds up approval significantly (3 days vs weeks)
2. **Keep business address proof ready** — most applications are queried for address proof issues
3. **HSN/SAC codes** — research the correct codes for your goods/services before applying
4. **Composition Scheme** — if your turnover is under ₹1.5 crore, consider the Composition Scheme for simpler compliance (apply during or after registration)
5. **Multiple registrations** — you need separate GSTIN for each state where you have a business presence

## FAQs

### Q1: Is GST registration free?
**Yes**, GST registration on gst.gov.in is completely free. There is no government fee. If anyone charges you, they are charging for their service, not a government fee.

### Q2: How long does GST registration take?
With Aadhaar authentication, it takes about **3 working days**. Without Aadhaar authentication, it can take **7-21 days** as physical verification may be required.

### Q3: What is ARN in GST?
ARN (Application Reference Number) is a unique number generated after you submit your GST registration application. Use it to track your application status on gst.gov.in.

### Q4: What is GSTIN and how is it structured?
GSTIN is your 15-digit GST Identification Number. It contains your state code (2 digits) + PAN (10 characters) + entity number (2 digits) + checksum (1 digit). Example: 29ABCDE1234F1Z5.

### Q5: Can I register for GST voluntarily?
**Yes**, you can register for GST even if your turnover is below the threshold. This is useful if you want to claim input tax credit or sell on e-commerce platforms.

### Q6: What happens if I don't register for GST when required?
You may face a **penalty of 10% of tax due or ₹10,000** (whichever is higher). In case of deliberate evasion, the penalty can be **100% of tax due**.

### Q7: Can I have multiple GSTINs?
Yes, you need a separate GSTIN for each state where you operate. Within a state, you can also get multiple registrations for different business verticals.
