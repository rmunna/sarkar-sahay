## What is MGNREGS?

**Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Scheme (MGNREGS)**, also known as **MGNREGA or NREGA**, is a central government scheme that **legally guarantees 100 days of unskilled manual work per year** to every rural household in India whose adult members are willing to do unskilled manual work.

The scheme is a legal right — if the government cannot provide work within 15 days of demand, the applicant is entitled to an **unemployment allowance**. Payment is made directly to the worker's bank account within 15 days of work completion.

**Key fact:** MGNREGS is the **world's largest employment guarantee programme**, covering over 15 crore households annually.

## Key Benefits

- **100 days of guaranteed work** per financial year (April–March)
- Work is always within **5 km of the village** (if further, extra 10% wages)
- **Wage rate** (2024-25) varies by state: ₹230–₹357 per day
  - Haryana: ₹374/day (highest)
  - Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh: ₹243/day
  - Check nrega.nic.in for your state's current rate
- Wages directly to **bank account within 15 working days**
- Unemployment allowance if work not given within 15 days
- **30% of work reserved for women** (often exceeds 50% in practice)
- Work includes: road building, pond digging, tree plantation, irrigation, watershed, Anganwadi construction

## Who is Eligible?

- Must be a resident of a **rural area** (village, hamlet)
- Must be an **adult** (18 years or older)
- Willing to do **unskilled manual work**
- Must have a **MGNREGA Job Card** (Rozgar Panjika)

> **Urban residents are not eligible.** MGNREGS is only for rural areas. Workers in towns and cities should check PM SVANidhi (street vendors) or urban employment schemes.

## How to Get a Job Card (Rozgar Panjika)

The Job Card is your official MGNREGS identity. Without it, you cannot get work.

**Application process:**
1. Visit your **Gram Panchayat office** with:
   - Aadhaar card (all adult family members)
   - Bank account details (your own account)
   - Passport-size photographs of all adult members
   - Application form (available at gram panchayat)
2. The Gram Panchayat is mandated to issue the Job Card within **15 days** of application
3. Job Card contains: photo, name, address, MGNREGS registration number, bank account
4. It is **free of cost** — do not pay anyone for getting a job card

**Online registration:** Many states now allow online job card applications via their state NREGASoft portals (e.g., UP, Bihar). Check your state's rural development department website.

## How to Demand Work (Rozgar Maango)

After getting your Job Card:

1. Submit a written **work demand application** at your Gram Panchayat
2. Clearly state: "I/We demand work for ___ days from (date)"
3. The Gram Panchayat must provide work within **15 days** from the date of demand
4. If they cannot, they must give an **unemployment allowance** (typically 25% of minimum wage for first 30 days, 50% thereafter)
5. Work will be assigned at a site within 5 km of your village

> Keep a copy of your written demand. The date stamp matters for the 15-day deadline.

## Types of Work Under MGNREGS

- **Water conservation:** Pond desilting, check dams, watershed management
- **Rural roads:** All-weather connectivity roads, farm roads
- **Land development:** Terracing, bunding, drains for farmland
- **Agriculture:** Irrigation canals, soil conservation for small farmers
- **Rural infrastructure:** Anganwadi buildings, school toilets, playgrounds
- **Plantation:** Social forestry, afforestation, fruit orchards
- **Individual works:** Wells, ponds, vermicompost pits, cattle sheds for SC/ST/BPL

## Payment — How and When

- Wages are calculated based on **work measurement** (by the Mate/Rozgar Sevak)
- Wages are paid **within 15 days** of work completion
- Payment is **directly to your bank account** (Aadhaar-linked preferred)
- If payment is delayed beyond 15 days, you are entitled to a **delay compensation** (0.05% of wages per day of delay)

## How to Check MGNREGS Payment Status Online

**At nrega.nic.in:**
1. Go to **[nrega.nic.in](https://nrega.nic.in)**
2. Select your state → district → block → panchayat
3. Click "Job Card" → search your name
4. View your work history and payment status

**UMANG App:**
- Download UMANG → search "MGNREGA" → enter job card number → view payments

**Bank Passbook:**
- Wages are credited as "MGNREGS" or "NREGA" in your passbook

## Grievance — If Work/Payment is Denied

**If work not given within 15 days:**
1. Ask for written order of rejection/postponement
2. Apply for unemployment allowance in writing to Gram Panchayat

**If payment not received:**
1. Check payment status on nrega.nic.in
2. File online grievance at nrega.nic.in → "Public Grievance"
3. Contact the District Programme Coordinator (DPC) at your district collectorate
4. Escalate to state ombudsman (MGNREGA has a mandatory social audit and ombudsman system)

**Social Audit:** MGNREGS is the only scheme with mandatory social audits — the community reviews all work done and payments made at a public meeting. You can raise objections during the social audit.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I demand work at any time of the year?
Yes — work can be demanded any time in the financial year (April–March). However, applications after March have no guarantee and carry over to the next year.

### Can all adult family members work under MGNREGS?
Yes — all adult members listed on the Job Card can work. The 100-day guarantee is **per household**, not per person. Multiple family members can work simultaneously, but total days cannot exceed 100 in a financial year.

### I live in a small town. Am I eligible?
No — MGNREGS is only for rural areas as defined by the government. Residents of municipalities/town panchayats/municipal councils are not eligible.

### What is the role of the Rozgar Sevak / Mate?
The Mate is a local MGNREGS worker who supervises a group of 40 workers, measures work done, and maintains attendance records. Getting accurate work measurement from the Mate is important for correct payment.

### My village doesn't have any MGNREGS work going on. Can I demand new work?
Yes — if there is a need for a work like pond digging, road repair, or irrigation channel, you can make a formal demand to the Gram Panchayat. The GP must include your demand in the labour budget/shelf of projects.
