# Car Insurance 2026 — Compare, Buy & Renew Online (Complete Guide)

Car insurance is **mandatory under Indian law** — driving without valid third-party (TP) insurance attracts a fine of ₹2,000 and up to 3 months imprisonment. But beyond legal compliance, a good comprehensive policy protects your car against accidents, theft, natural disasters, and more.

This guide helps you compare the best car insurance plans and buy in under 5 minutes online.

---

## Types of Car Insurance

| Type | Covers | Mandatory | Premium |
|------|--------|-----------|---------|
| **Third Party (TP)** | Damage to others (person/vehicle/property) | **Yes** | ₹2,094–₹7,897/year |
| **Own Damage (OD)** | Your car's damage (accident, fire, etc.) | No | Separate |
| **Comprehensive** | TP + OD combined | No (recommended) | ₹3,000–₹30,000+/year |
| **Standalone OD** | Only own damage (need TP separately) | No | Varies |

## Understanding IDV (Insured Declared Value)

IDV = Market value of your car. Maximum you'll receive if car is totally destroyed or stolen.

| Car Age | Depreciation on IDV |
|---------|---------------------|
| New to 6 months | 5% |
| 6 months – 1 year | 15% |
| 1–2 years | 20% |
| 2–3 years | 30% |
| 3–4 years | 40% |
| 4–5 years | 50% |
| Above 5 years | Mutually agreed |

**Never reduce IDV just to save ₹500 in premium.** If car is stolen or totalled, you get only the IDV.

## NCB (No Claim Bonus) — Save Up to 50%

NCB is a reward for not making claims:

| Claim-free Years | NCB Discount |
|----------------|-------------|
| 1 year | 20% |
| 2 years | 25% |
| 3 years | 35% |
| 4 years | 45% |
| 5 years | **50%** |

5 years of no claims = 50% discount on OD premium. For a ₹15,000 OD premium, NCB saves ₹7,500/year.

NCB is transferable when you change insurer. Get NCB certificate from old insurer before switching.

## How to File a Car Insurance Claim

### Accident Claim

1. **Ensure safety** → move car to safe spot → call police if needed (injuries, serious damage)
2. **Photos first** — document accident scene, damage, other vehicle
3. **Call insurer helpline** (within 24–48 hours)
4. **Cashless option:** Take to network garage → surveyor visits → insurer pays directly
5. **Reimbursement:** Get repaired anywhere → submit bills + photos → insurer reimburses

### Theft Claim

1. File **FIR immediately** at police station (mandatory)
2. Inform insurer within 24 hours
3. Submit: FIR copy, RC, insurance documents, all keys
4. 90-day waiting period (law requires search time)
5. IDV amount paid after settlement

## Car Insurance vs Own Damage — Which is Better?

In 2019, IRDAI allowed separate TP and OD policies. You can:
1. Buy **TP from any insurer** (all charge the same regulated rate)
2. Buy **OD from a different insurer** (shop for best OD rates)

This flexibility lets you optimize — get the best OD rates and still have TP compliance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is comprehensive car insurance mandatory?**
No — only third-party insurance is legally mandatory. But comprehensive is highly recommended. One accident can cost ₹50,000–₹2 lakh in repairs.

**How do I transfer insurance when I sell my car?**
Insurance can be transferred to the buyer within 14 days of car sale. Alternatively, buyer can get their own policy. Seller can transfer NCB to a new car or get NCB protection.

**My car insurance expired 3 months ago — can I renew online?**
If expired under 90 days: Online renewal possible. Over 90 days: Physical inspection of car required by insurer before renewal.

**Does raising a claim affect my NCB?**
Yes — a claim resets your NCB progress (back to 0% or previous level depending on claim type). NCB Protect add-on prevents this for one claim per year.

**What is the difference between Acko and traditional insurers?**
Acko is a digital-native insurer with no agent commissions. Claims are fully digital. No paperwork. Equivalent coverage, slightly lower premiums than traditional insurers. CSR is good (98%+). Safe to use for straightforward claims.
