## What is Vhali Dikri Yojana?

**Vhali Dikri Yojana** (meaning "Dear Daughter Scheme") is a Gujarat government scheme launched in 2019 to provide financial assistance to families with daughters. The scheme aims to improve the **sex ratio**, promote **girl child education**, and reduce the financial burden on families during a daughter's marriage.

Under this scheme, the Gujarat government provides financial assistance at **three key stages** of a girl's life:

| Stage | When | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| First installment | When daughter enters Class 1 | ₹4,000 |
| Second installment | When daughter enters Class 9 | ₹6,000 |
| Third installment | When daughter turns 18 years old | ₹1,00,000 |
| **Total** | | **₹1,10,000** |

The scheme applies to the **first two daughters** of a family. The third installment of ₹1 lakh can be used for higher education or marriage expenses.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Domicile:** Family must be a permanent resident of Gujarat
- **Income:** Annual family income should not exceed ₹2 lakh
- **Daughters:** Applicable for first two daughters of the family
- **Birth registration:** Daughter's birth must be registered
- **Bank account:** Family must have a bank account (preferably in the daughter's name or joint with parent)
- **BPL/EWS:** Priority given to Below Poverty Line and Economically Weaker Section families

## Documents Required

**For registration (at birth/Class 1):**
- Birth certificate of the daughter
- Aadhaar Card of parents (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- Income certificate (annual income below ₹2 lakh)
- Ration card / BPL card
- Bank account passbook (Aadhaar-linked)
- Domicile certificate / Address proof in Gujarat
- Passport-size photographs of daughter and parents
- Caste certificate (if applicable)

**For subsequent installments:**
- School admission proof (for Class 1 and Class 9 installments)
- Age proof (for 18-year installment)
- Continued enrollment certificate from school/college

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Online Application

1. **Visit the portal:** Go to the Gujarat Women & Child Development Department website or Digital Gujarat portal
2. **Download application form:** Download the Vhali Dikri Yojana application form
3. **Fill the form:** Enter daughter's details, parents' details, income, and bank account information
4. **Attach documents:** Attach photocopies of all required documents
5. **Submit at Anganwadi or CDPO office:** Submit the completed form at your nearest **Anganwadi Centre** or **Child Development Project Officer (CDPO)** office

### Offline Application

1. Visit your nearest **Anganwadi Centre** or **Mamlatdar office**
2. Collect the Vhali Dikri Yojana application form
3. Fill in all details and attach required documents
4. Submit the form to the Anganwadi worker or CDPO
5. Collect the acknowledgement receipt
6. The Anganwadi worker will forward your application to the Taluka/District level

### Application Timeline

- **First installment:** Apply when daughter is born or entering Class 1
- **Second installment:** Apply when daughter enters Class 9 (renewal with school proof)
- **Third installment:** Apply when daughter turns 18 (with age proof and educational/marriage documents)

## Processing Time

| Stage | Timeline |
|-------|----------|
| Application verification | 30-60 days |
| First installment disbursement | Within 3 months of approval |
| Subsequent installments | Disbursed at the respective stage automatically (if registered) |

## Fees

There is **no application fee** for this scheme. All services are free.

## How the Money is Disbursed

- Amount is transferred directly to the **bank account** via DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer)
- For the first two installments (₹4,000 and ₹6,000), the amount is credited to the parent's bank account
- For the third installment (₹1,00,000), the amount is credited to the **daughter's own bank account** after she turns 18
- The daughter must have her own bank account by the time she turns 18

## Important Tips

1. **Register early** — Apply as soon as your daughter is born or enters Class 1 to ensure you receive all three installments.
2. **Keep documents updated** — Ensure school enrollment certificates and bank details are current for smooth disbursement.
3. **Open bank account for daughter** — Before she turns 18, open a bank account in her name for the ₹1 lakh final installment.
4. **Track application status** — Follow up with the Anganwadi worker or CDPO office regularly.
5. **Combine with other schemes** — Your daughter may also be eligible for Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (central scheme) and other state education scholarships.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. How much total money will I get under Vhali Dikri Yojana?

A total of **₹1,10,000** across three installments: ₹4,000 at Class 1, ₹6,000 at Class 9, and ₹1,00,000 at age 18.

### Q2. Is the scheme only for BPL families?

The scheme is for families with **annual income up to ₹2 lakh**. Both BPL and above-BPL families within the income limit can apply.

### Q3. Can I apply for my third daughter?

No, the scheme is applicable only for the **first two daughters** of a family.

### Q4. What if my daughter drops out of school?

The education-linked installments (Class 1 and Class 9) require proof of enrollment. If your daughter discontinues education, the corresponding installment may not be released. The age-18 installment is based on age, not education status.

### Q5. Can NRI Gujaratis apply?

No, the family must be **domiciled in Gujarat** and the daughter must be residing and studying in Gujarat.

### Q6. Is there a last date to apply?

There is no fixed last date. The scheme is ongoing and you can apply at any time. However, apply as early as possible to avail all three installments.

### Q7. How will I know my application is approved?

You will receive communication through the Anganwadi worker or CDPO office. The payment will be credited to your bank account. You can also check with the District Social Welfare office.
