# Chhattisgarh Scheme Renaming 2024 — BJP vs Congress: What Changed After December 2023 Election

**BJP won Chhattisgarh** in December 2023, ending 5 years of Congress rule under Bhupesh Baghel. CM **Vishnu Deo Sai** took charge in December 2023. The BJP government systematically renamed or discontinued Congress schemes — especially those named after the Gandhi-Nehru family — and launched new BJP-aligned flagship schemes.

> **Key search issue**: If you're looking for a "Rajiv Gandhi Kisan Nyay" payment or "Rajiv Gandhi Bhoomihin Mazdoor" beneficiary status, these schemes are **discontinued** or significantly modified. This guide tells you what replaced them.

## Old Congress Schemes — Status Under BJP

### 1. Rajiv Gandhi Kisan Nyay Yojana (RGKNY)

**Congress scheme**: Bhupesh Baghel's flagship agricultural scheme. Provided input subsidy to farmers for paddy, corn, and other crops. Farmers received ₹9,000–₹10,000/acre per year.

**BJP status**: **Discontinued / Not extended.**

**What BJP launched instead**:
- **PM Kisan** (central scheme): ₹6,000/year to all farmers
- **Rajya Kisan Nyay Yojana (state supplement)**: BJP launched their own version providing additional support, but without the Rajiv Gandhi branding
- If you were receiving RGKNY payments, check if you're registered under PM Kisan — the state has linked some former beneficiaries

**Portal**: [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in/) for PM Kisan status

### 2. Rajiv Gandhi Gramin Bhoomihin Krishi Mazdoor Nyay Yojana

**Congress scheme**: Annual cash support of ₹6,000/year for landless agricultural laborers in rural Chhattisgarh. Covered approximately 3.5 lakh beneficiaries.

**BJP status**: **Discontinued.** No direct replacement announced.

**Alternative**: Landless agricultural laborers may access:
- **MGNREGA** (employment guarantee) for wages
- **PM Shram Yogi Maandhan** for pension if informal worker aged 18-40

### 3. Rajiv Gandhi Nyay Yojana (Urban)

**Congress scheme**: Urban livelihood support scheme

**BJP status**: Discontinued or quietly phased out.

### 4. Godhan Nyay Yojana (Cow Dung Purchase Scheme)

**Congress scheme**: Bhupesh Baghel's innovative scheme where the state bought cow dung from farmers/households at ₹2/kg to make organic fertilizers and biogas. Very popular with rural households.

**BJP status**: **Continued with modifications.** BJP retained this scheme as it was genuinely popular. However:
- Implementation has been patchy in some districts after the government change
- Payments to Gau Mata organizations slowed in 2024

**Check status**: Contact your local Gaushala or Janpad Panchayat.

### 5. Swami Atmanand Free English/Hindi Medium Schools

**Congress scheme**: Started under Bhupesh Baghel, providing free high-quality English and Hindi medium schools in government buildings across Chhattisgarh. Very popular.

**BJP status**: **Continued.** BJP renamed and expanded these. Rebranded as **PM Shri Schools** in alignment with central government's PM SHRI scheme, but the local Swami Atmanand branding is still used.

### 6. Noni Sashaktikaran Sahayata Yojana

**Congress scheme**: One-time assistance of ₹20,000 to two daughters of building and other construction workers registered with the Labour Welfare Board.

**BJP status**: Largely **continued** — this was a welfare scheme with less political branding. Check with the district Labour office.

## New BJP Schemes Launched in 2024

### 1. Mahtari Vandan Yojana (₹1,000/month for Women)

BJP's biggest flagship scheme in Chhattisgarh — inspired by similar schemes in Madhya Pradesh (Ladli Bahna) and other BJP states.

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Benefit** | ₹1,000/month (₹12,000/year) |
| **Eligible** | Married women of Chhattisgarh, aged 21+, family income ≤ ₹5 lakh |
| **Portal** | beneficiary.mahtarivandan.cgstate.gov.in |
| **Payment date** | Around the 1st of each month |

**If not receiving payment**: Check at [beneficiary.mahtarivandan.cgstate.gov.in](https://beneficiary.mahtarivandan.cgstate.gov.in/) → Status Check using mobile/Aadhaar.

Common reasons for non-payment:
- Aadhaar not seeded to bank (NPCI mapper issue)
- Application still under verification
- Bank account mismatch

**Helpline**: 14447

### 2. PM Awas Yojana (Expansion)

BJP prioritized PMAY housing for Chhattisgarh beneficiaries, linking some existing beneficiaries from Congress era housing lists.

### 3. Chhattisgarh Agriculture Scheme Reforms

BJP reorganized the agriculture input subsidy model. For current scheme status, contact the **Krishi Vibhag** (Agriculture Department) at [agri.cg.gov.in](https://agri.cg.gov.in/).

## Impact on Existing Beneficiaries

### If You Were Getting RGKNY Payments

- Check your bank account — last payment under RGKNY was likely around January–February 2024
- For current agricultural support, register under PM Kisan if not already done at [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in/)
- Visit your local **Krishi Seva Kendra** to understand the current state supplement scheme

### If You Were Getting Bhoomihin Mazdoor Nyay Payments

- Scheme is discontinued — no payments after early 2024
- MGNREGA job card is the primary safety net for agricultural laborers

### If You Were Getting Godhan Nyay Payments

- Scheme is operational but with reduced frequency/coverage in some areas
- Contact local Gaushala or Gram Panchayat to verify current status

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I'm a farmer in Chhattisgarh who was getting RGKNY money. Will I get it this year (2025)?

No — the Rajiv Gandhi Kisan Nyay Yojana was discontinued by the BJP government. The BJP has its own state agricultural supplement scheme but under a different name and structure. Visit your local Krishi Seva Kendra for current status of farmer support in your district.

### Is Mahtari Vandan Yojana the same as Ladli Bahna (MP)?

Different scheme, same concept. Ladli Bahna is in Madhya Pradesh (₹1,250/month), Mahtari Vandan is in Chhattisgarh (₹1,000/month). Both are BJP women welfare schemes providing monthly cash to married women.

### Where did the Rajiv Gandhi Kisan Nyay Yojana portal (rgkny.cg.gov.in) go?

The portal is likely discontinued or no longer updated. Old beneficiary data may still be on the portal but no new payments are being processed.

### Can I still apply for Godhan Nyay Yojana?

The scheme has been continued nominally but implementation varies. Contact your local Gram Panchayat or Gaushala to check if cow dung purchases are active in your area.
