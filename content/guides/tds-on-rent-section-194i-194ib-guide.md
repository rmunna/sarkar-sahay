# TDS on Rent India 2026 — Section 194I and 194IB Complete Guide

If you pay rent above certain thresholds in India, you are legally required to **deduct TDS (Tax Deducted at Source)** before paying your landlord. This applies to both individual tenants and businesses. Not deducting TDS when required makes you liable for the full TDS amount plus interest and penalties.

## Two TDS Sections on Rent

| Section | Who It Applies To | Threshold | TDS Rate |
|---------|------------------|-----------|---------|
| **194I** | Companies, firms, trusts, HUF, individuals/HUF with tax audit | ₹2,40,000 per year (₹20,000/month) | 2% (for plant/equipment) or **2% for land/building** |
| **194IB** | Individuals/HUF NOT under tax audit | ₹50,000 per month | **2%** |

> **Budget 2024 change**: TDS rate on rent for 194I (land/building) was **reduced from 10% to 2%** (effective October 1, 2024).

## Section 194IB — For Most Salaried Individuals & Small Business Owners

This is the section that affects most individual tenants paying high rent.

**Applies if**:
- You are an individual or HUF
- You are NOT required to get a tax audit (i.e., your business turnover is below ₹1 crore / professional income below ₹50 lakh)
- Monthly rent paid to a single landlord exceeds **₹50,000**

**TDS rate**: 2% of rent

**No TAN required** — unlike most TDS, Section 194IB does not need a Tax Deduction Account Number (TAN). You use your PAN.

**When to deduct**: At the end of the financial year OR when vacating (whichever is earlier) — one-time annual deduction, not monthly.

**Example**:
- Rent = ₹60,000/month
- Annual rent = ₹7,20,000
- TDS under 194IB = ₹7,20,000 × 2% = **₹14,400** (deduct once at year-end/vacation)

## Section 194I — For Businesses, Companies, HUF Under Tax Audit

**Applies if**:
- You are a company, firm, LLP, trust, or
- An individual/HUF whose accounts are required to be audited under Section 44AB

**Threshold**: Rent payments to a single landlord exceeding **₹2,40,000 per year** (₹20,000/month)

**TDS rates** (post October 1, 2024):
- Land, building, furniture: **2%**
- Plant and machinery, equipment: **2%**

(Previously land/building was 10% — major reduction in Budget 2024)

**When to deduct**: Monthly (each rent payment), unlike 194IB which is annual.

**TAN required**: Yes — businesses need TAN for 194I. Apply at [tin.egov-nsdl.com](https://onlineservices.tin.egov-nsdl.com/).

## How to Pay TDS on Rent (Step by Step)

### For Individuals (Section 194IB) — Form 26QC

1. Go to **[onlineservices.tin.egov-nsdl.com](https://onlineservices.tin.egov-nsdl.com/)**
2. Under "TDS" → Select **Form 26QC** (TDS on Rent of Property)
3. Fill in:
   - Your PAN (tenant/payer)
   - Landlord PAN (payee)
   - Rental period
   - Total rent paid during the year
   - TDS deducted amount (2% of total rent)
4. Pay online via net banking/UPI
5. Download **Form 16C** (TDS certificate for landlord)

**Deadline to file Form 26QC**: Within **30 days** from the end of the month in which TDS was deducted.

### For Businesses (Section 194I) — Regular TDS Challan

1. Deduct 2% from each monthly rent payment
2. Deposit via Challan 281 (TDS payment) by 7th of next month (or by April 30 for March deductions)
3. File quarterly TDS returns (Form 24Q for salary, **Form 26Q** for non-salary payments including rent)
4. Issue Form 16A to landlord after each quarter

## Landlord's Side — How to Claim TDS Credit

The landlord does not need to do anything special to claim this credit:

1. The TDS deposited by the tenant automatically appears in the landlord's **Form 26AS** (after processing)
2. When the landlord files ITR, they declare the full rent income
3. TDS credit automatically shows in ITR — deducted from tax payable
4. If TDS > tax liability: landlord gets a refund

**Landlord's document**: Request **Form 16C** (194IB) or **Form 16A** (194I) from your tenant — this is their responsibility to provide.

## What If You Didn't Deduct TDS?

If you were required to deduct TDS and didn't:

| Consequence | Amount |
|------------|--------|
| **Interest for non-deduction** | 1% per month from date TDS should have been deducted |
| **Interest for non-deposit** (if deducted but not paid) | 1.5% per month |
| **Penalty under Section 271C** | Equal to TDS amount (100% penalty) |
| **AO can treat entire rent as disallowed expense** (for businesses) | 30% of rent disallowed under Section 40(a)(ia) |

**Exception**: If landlord has paid the tax themselves on the income, the tenant's penalty may be reduced.

## Common Questions

### My rent is ₹45,000/month. Do I need to deduct TDS?

No — Section 194IB threshold is ₹50,000/month. At ₹45,000, TDS is not required.

### My rent is ₹52,000/month but I increased it from ₹45,000 in October. When does TDS kick in?

TDS applies from the point monthly rent **crosses ₹50,000**. Once it crosses, you deduct TDS on the total annual rent (including months before the threshold was crossed, if paid to the same landlord in the same year).

### My landlord refuses to give their PAN. Can I still pay without PAN?

If the landlord doesn't provide PAN, you must deduct TDS at **20%** (not 2%) under Section 206AA. It's the tenant's burden to obtain landlord PAN.

### I'm paying rent to two landlords (joint property). Threshold applies per landlord?

Yes — if the property has two owners and rent is split, TDS threshold and deduction is calculated per landlord. If each gets ₹30,000, no TDS required (below ₹50K each).

### I forgot to deduct TDS at year-end. Can I deduct now?

You can deduct TDS from the final month's rent even if you missed earlier months. File Form 26QC with the full year's rent and 2% TDS. Pay interest for the delayed deduction period along with the TDS.
