# How to Check PM Awas Yojana (PMAY) Application Status Online

Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) is the Government of India's flagship housing scheme that aims to provide affordable housing to all eligible citizens. If you have applied under PMAY-Urban or PMAY-Gramin, you can easily check your application status online. This guide covers all methods to track your PMAY application.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent platform and is not affiliated with the Government of India. Information provided here is for guidance only. Always verify details on official government portals.

---

## What is PM Awas Yojana?

PM Awas Yojana has two components:

- **PMAY-Urban (PMAY-U):** For urban areas — provides interest subsidy on home loans and direct financial assistance for house construction/enhancement.
- **PMAY-Gramin (PMAY-G):** For rural areas — provides financial assistance of ₹1.20 lakh (plain areas) to ₹1.30 lakh (hilly/difficult areas) for constructing pucca houses.

---

## Who is Eligible?

### PMAY-Urban
- Families in Economically Weaker Section (EWS) with annual income up to ₹3 lakh
- Low Income Group (LIG) with annual income ₹3-6 lakh
- Middle Income Group-I (MIG-I) with annual income ₹6-12 lakh
- Middle Income Group-II (MIG-II) with annual income ₹12-18 lakh
- Beneficiary family should not own a pucca house anywhere in India

### PMAY-Gramin
- Identified from Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC) 2011 data
- Houseless families or families living in kutcha/dilapidated houses
- Priority to SC/ST, minorities, and freed bonded labourers

## How to Check PMAY-Urban Status Online

### Method 1: Using PMAY MIS Portal

1. Visit the official PMAY-Urban portal at [pmaymis.gov.in](https://pmaymis.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Citizen Assessment"** in the top menu
3. Select **"Track Your Assessment Status"**
4. Enter your **Father's Name** and **Assessment ID**
5. Click **"Submit"**
6. Your application status will be displayed on screen

### Method 2: Using Aadhaar Number

1. Go to [pmaymis.gov.in](https://pmaymis.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Search Beneficiary"**
3. Enter your **Aadhaar number** (first 3 digits)
4. Click **"Show"**
5. If your name appears, your application has been approved

### Method 3: Check PMAY-U Beneficiary List

1. Visit [pmaymis.gov.in](https://pmaymis.gov.in)
2. Go to **"Search Beneficiary"** section
3. You can search by name or Aadhaar number
4. Select your state and city
5. Check if your name is in the approved beneficiary list

---

## How to Check PMAY-Gramin Status Online

### Method 1: Using PMAY-G Portal

1. Visit the PMAY-G portal at [pmayg.nic.in](https://pmayg.nic.in)
2. Click on **"Awaassoft"** in the menu
3. Select **"FTO Tracking"** or **"Beneficiary Details"**
4. Enter your **Registration Number**
5. Click **"Submit"** to view your application status

### Method 2: Using Aadhaar Number on PMAY-G

1. Visit [pmayg.nic.in](https://pmayg.nic.in)
2. Go to **"Stakeholders"** → **"IAY/PMAYG Beneficiary"**
3. Enter your **Registration Number** or **Aadhaar Number**
4. Your complete application details and payment status will be displayed

### Method 3: Check PMAY-G Beneficiary List

1. Visit the reporting portal at [rhreporting.nic.in](https://rhreporting.nic.in/netiay/newreport.aspx)
2. Select your **State → District → Block → Panchayat**
3. The village-wise beneficiary list will be displayed
4. Search for your name in the list

## Documents Required for PMAY Application

- **Identity Proof:** Aadhaar card (mandatory)
- **Income Proof:** Income certificate, salary slips, or ITR
- **Address Proof:** Ration card, voter ID, or utility bills
- **Bank Details:** Bank passbook or cancelled cheque
- **Property Documents:** Land ownership papers (for PMAY-G)
- **Photographs:** Passport-size photos of all family members
- **Affidavit:** Declaring that you don't own a pucca house (for PMAY-U)

## PMAY Application Status Meanings

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Under Verification | Application is being reviewed by local authority |
| Approved | Application approved, funds will be released |
| Sanctioned | Financial assistance has been sanctioned |
| First Instalment Released | First payment credited to beneficiary |
| Second Instalment Released | Second payment after construction progress |
| Third Instalment Released | Final payment after completion verification |
| Rejected | Application did not meet eligibility criteria |

## How to Check PMAY Payment Status

1. Visit [pfms.nic.in](https://pfms.nic.in) (Public Financial Management System)
2. Click on **"Know Your Payments"**
3. Enter your **Bank Account Number** and **Bank Name**
4. Enter the CAPTCHA and click **"Search"**
5. All PMAY payments credited to your account will be displayed

---

## Processing Time

- **PMAY-Urban:** 3-6 months for approval after application submission
- **PMAY-Gramin:** Beneficiary selection is based on SECC data; construction timeline is 12 months from sanction
- **Fund Release:** Payments are released in 3-4 instalments based on construction progress

---

## Important Tips

1. **Keep your Aadhaar linked** to your bank account — all PMAY payments are transferred via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)
2. **Complete Geo-tagging** — PMAY-G requires geotagged photos at each construction stage for instalment release
3. **Check beneficiary list first** — Before applying, check if your name already exists in the SECC-based PMAY-G list
4. **Don't pay middlemen** — PMAY is a free government scheme; no fees are charged for application or approval
5. **Contact Gram Panchayat/ULB** — For any issues, visit your local Gram Panchayat (rural) or Urban Local Body (urban) office

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: How can I check my PM Awas Yojana status by Aadhaar number?
Visit [pmaymis.gov.in](https://pmaymis.gov.in), go to "Search Beneficiary," enter the first 3 digits of your Aadhaar number, and click "Show" to check your status.

### Q2: What is the PMAY Assessment ID and where do I find it?
The Assessment ID is a unique number assigned when you submit your PMAY-U application. It is printed on your application acknowledgement slip. You need it to track your status.

### Q3: How many instalments are released under PMAY-Gramin?
PMAY-G releases financial assistance in 3 instalments based on construction progress — at foundation level, lintel level, and after completion with roof casting.

### Q4: My PMAY application was rejected. Can I apply again?
Yes, you can apply again if the rejection was due to incorrect information. Correct the errors and re-submit through your local body. If rejected due to ineligibility, you cannot re-apply.

### Q5: How long does it take to get the first instalment under PMAY-G?
After your name appears in the beneficiary list and the sanction order is issued, the first instalment is typically released within 1-2 months. The amount is directly credited to your Aadhaar-linked bank account.

### Q6: Is there a helpline for PMAY complaints?
Yes, you can call the PMAY toll-free helpline at **1800-11-6163** (PMAY-Urban) or **1800-11-6446** (PMAY-Gramin) for queries and complaints.
