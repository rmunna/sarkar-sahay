## What is CM Anuprati Coaching Yojana?

Chief Minister Anuprati Coaching Yojana is a flagship scheme by the Government of Rajasthan that provides **free coaching** to students from economically weaker sections for various competitive examinations. The scheme covers coaching fees, hostel, and living expenses for students preparing for civil services, engineering, medical, and other competitive exams.

### Exams Covered

- **UPSC** Civil Services (IAS, IPS, IFS)
- **RPSC** (Rajasthan State Services — RAS/RTS)
- **SI, Constable** (Rajasthan Police)
- **Patwari, Gram Sevak** and other state-level exams
- **Engineering** entrance (JEE)
- **Medical** entrance (NEET)
- **CLAT** (Law entrance)
- **REET** (Teacher eligibility)
- **CA, CS, CMA** professional courses

### Benefits

| Component | Amount |
|-----------|--------|
| Coaching fee | Paid directly to empanelled coaching institute |
| Hostel/mess charges | Up to ₹40,000/year |
| Other expenses | Up to ₹10,000/year |

> Total benefit varies by exam category. Duration: 1-2 years depending on the exam.

## Who is Eligible?

- Must belong to **SC, ST, OBC, MBC, EWS, or minority** community
- Must be a **resident of Rajasthan**
- Family annual income must not exceed **₹8 lakh**
- Age limit varies by exam:
  - UPSC: Up to **30 years** (relaxation for reserved categories)
  - RPSC: Up to **35 years**
  - JEE/NEET: As per exam eligibility
- Must have cleared the required educational qualification for the target exam
- Must not be availing similar coaching benefit from another scheme
- **Minimum marks** in qualifying exam (varies — typically Class 10/12 with 60%+)

## Documents Required

- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
- Jan Aadhaar card
- Caste certificate (SC/ST/OBC/MBC/EWS)
- Income certificate (annual income ≤ ₹8 lakh)
- Educational qualification marksheets (Class 10, 12, graduation as applicable)
- Domicile certificate of Rajasthan
- Bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked)
- Passport-size photograph
- Undertaking of not availing similar benefit from other schemes

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Online Application

1. **Visit** [SSO Rajasthan portal](https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in)
2. **Login** with SSO ID (create one if you don't have)
3. **Navigate** to SJE Department → CM Anuprati Coaching Yojana
4. **Select** the exam category you want coaching for
5. **Fill** the application with personal, educational, income details
6. **Choose** preferred coaching institute from the empanelled list
7. **Upload** all required documents
8. **Submit** and note application number
9. **Track** status on SSO portal

### Selection Process

1. Applications are **merit-based** — ranked by qualifying exam marks
2. **District-wise allocation** with reserved seats for SC/ST/OBC/women
3. **Selection list** published on the SJE portal
4. Selected students join the **empanelled coaching institute**
5. Coaching fees paid directly to the institute by the government

## Processing Time

- Application window: Usually **1-2 months** (annual cycle)
- Selection list: **1-2 months** after application deadline
- Coaching begins: As per the institute's schedule

## Fees

**No fees** from the student. Coaching is fully funded by the government.

## Important Tips

1. **Apply early** — Seats are limited; merit-based selection means higher marks get preference
2. **Score well in qualifying exam** — Your Class 10/12/graduation marks determine your rank
3. **Choose institute wisely** — Research empanelled coaching centres before selecting
4. **Attend regularly** — Irregular attendance may lead to cancellation of benefits
5. **Focus on the exam** — Use this opportunity fully; free coaching of this quality is rare
6. **Women get priority** — A significant percentage of seats are reserved for women candidates

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Can general category students apply?
**EWS (Economically Weaker Section)** students from general category with income ≤ ₹8 lakh are eligible.

### Q2. Is there a seat limit?
Yes. The scheme has a fixed number of seats per exam category, allocated district-wise. Selection is merit-based.

### Q3. Can I change my coaching institute?
Generally, no. You must join the institute selected during application. Contact SJE department for exceptional cases.

### Q4. What if I don't clear the exam?
The coaching benefit is for a fixed duration (1-2 years). There is no penalty for not clearing, but you cannot re-apply for the same exam coaching.

### Q5. Is hostel accommodation provided?
Hostel expenses up to **₹40,000/year** are covered. The student must arrange accommodation and submit bills.

### Q6. Can I apply for coaching for multiple exams?
No. You can apply for coaching for **one exam category** at a time.
