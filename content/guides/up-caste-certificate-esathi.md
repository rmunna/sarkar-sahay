# UP Caste Certificate Online — Apply via eSathi Portal (2026)

A **caste certificate** (जाति प्रमाण पत्र) is an essential document for citizens belonging to Scheduled Castes (SC), Scheduled Tribes (ST), and Other Backward Classes (OBC) in Uttar Pradesh. It is required for reservation benefits in education, government jobs, and various welfare schemes.

The **eSathi portal** ([esathi.up.gov.in](https://esathi.up.gov.in)) is the official online platform by the Government of Uttar Pradesh for applying for caste certificates and other revenue department documents from the comfort of your home.

> See our [national caste certificate guide](/guide/caste-certificate-online) for general information on caste certificates across India.

---

## Types of Caste Certificates in UP

| Category | Description | Issuing Authority |
|----------|-------------|-------------------|
| **SC (अनुसूचित जाति)** | Scheduled Caste certificate | Tehsildar / SDM |
| **ST (अनुसूचित जनजाति)** | Scheduled Tribe certificate | Tehsildar / SDM |
| **OBC (अन्य पिछड़ा वर्ग)** | Other Backward Classes certificate | Tehsildar / SDM |

> **Note:** In UP, the Tehsildar (तहसीलदार) is the primary issuing authority for caste certificates. For some categories, the Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) may also issue.

---

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a **permanent resident of Uttar Pradesh**.
- The applicant must belong to a caste listed under the SC, ST, or OBC category in the **UP Government's official caste list**.
- Both **new applicants and renewals** can apply online.
- There is **no age restriction** — certificates can be applied for minors through a parent or guardian.

---

## Documents Required (आवश्यक दस्तावेज़)

Keep scanned copies (JPEG/PDF, under 100 KB each) of the following:

1. **Aadhaar Card** (आधार कार्ड) of the applicant
2. **Family Register / Parivar Register** (कुटुम्ब रजिस्टर / परिवार रजिस्टर नकल)
3. **Previous Caste Certificate** — if held by father, grandfather, or any family member
4. **School Records** — High School marksheet or Transfer Certificate showing father's name and caste
5. **Passport-size Photograph** of the applicant
6. **Self-declaration / Affidavit** (शपथ पत्र) — in some cases
7. **Ration Card** — as additional address/family proof
8. **Voter ID / Domicile Certificate** — as supporting identity proof

> **Tip:** If you don't have an older family caste certificate, a sworn affidavit and verification by the village head (Gram Pradhan) or ward member can substitute in most tehsils.

## How to Apply Online via eSathi Portal (Step-by-Step)

### Step 1: Register on eSathi Portal

1. Visit [https://esathi.up.gov.in](https://esathi.up.gov.in).
2. Click **"नवीन उपयोगकर्ता पंजीकरण"** (New User Registration).
3. Fill in your name, date of birth, mobile number, and create a login ID and password.
4. Verify via **OTP** sent to your registered mobile number.
5. Log in with your new credentials.

### Step 2: Select the Caste Certificate Service

1. After login, navigate to **"आवेदन पत्र → सेवा चुनें"**.
2. Under the **Revenue Department (राजस्व विभाग)** section, select:
   - **जाति प्रमाण पत्र** (Caste Certificate)
3. Choose your district (जिला), tehsil (तहसील), and category (SC / ST / OBC).

### Step 3: Fill the Application Form

1. Enter personal details: name, father's name, mother's name, date of birth, gender.
2. Enter address details: village/ward, block/tehsil, district, PIN code.
3. Enter caste name as per the official UP Government list.
4. Fill in additional details as prompted (occupation, purpose of certificate, etc.).

### Step 4: Upload Documents

1. Upload scanned copies of all required documents.
2. Ensure each file is in **JPEG or PDF** format and **under 100 KB**.
3. Upload your **passport-size photo** as well.

### Step 5: Pay the Fee

1. Review the application and click **"शुल्क भुगतान"** (Fee Payment).
2. Pay online via:
   - **Net Banking**
   - **Debit/Credit Card**
   - **UPI**
   - **CSC Wallet** (if applying through a Common Service Centre)
3. Fee amount: **₹10 to ₹25** (varies by service and processing level).

### Step 6: Submit and Note Application Number

1. After successful payment, click **"Submit"**.
2. Note down your **Application Number** (आवेदन संख्या) — you'll need this to track status and download the certificate.
3. Take a **printout of the acknowledgement** for your records.

## How to Apply Offline (via Tehsil Office)

If you prefer the offline route:

1. Visit your nearest **Tehsil Office** (तहसील कार्यालय) or **Jan Seva Kendra**.
2. Obtain the caste certificate application form (or download from the eSathi website).
3. Fill in all details and attach **self-attested copies** of the required documents.
4. Submit the form along with the prescribed fee.
5. The Tehsildar will initiate a **field verification** (स्थानीय जांच) through the Lekhpal/Revenue Inspector.
6. After verification, the certificate is issued and can be collected from the tehsil.

> **Processing time for offline:** 15–30 days depending on verification workload.

## How to Track Application Status

1. Visit [https://esathi.up.gov.in](https://esathi.up.gov.in).
2. Click **"आवेदन की स्थिति"** (Application Status).
3. Enter your **Application Number**.
4. The portal will show the current status — pending, under verification, approved, or rejected.

---

## How to Download Your Caste Certificate

1. Log in to the eSathi portal.
2. Go to **"पूर्ण किये गए आवेदन"** (Completed Applications).
3. Find your caste certificate application.
4. Click **"प्रमाण पत्र डाउनलोड करें"** (Download Certificate).
5. The certificate is a **digitally signed PDF** — valid for official use.

You can also download it from the **DigiLocker** app if your Aadhaar is linked.

---

## How to Verify a Caste Certificate

To verify the authenticity of an issued caste certificate:

1. Visit [https://esathi.up.gov.in](https://esathi.up.gov.in) or [https://edistrict.up.gov.in](https://edistrict.up.gov.in).
2. Click **"प्रमाण पत्र सत्यापन"** (Certificate Verification).
3. Enter the **Certificate Number** or **Application Number**.
4. The portal will display the certificate details if valid.

> Employers, educational institutions, and government departments can use this to verify certificates online.

## Common Issues and Solutions

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| OTP not received during registration | Check mobile number; retry after a few minutes; try on a different browser |
| Document upload failing | Ensure file is JPEG/PDF and under 100 KB; compress using online tools |
| Application stuck at "Under Verification" | Contact your Tehsil office or call the UP helpline: **1076** |
| Certificate shows incorrect caste name | Apply for correction through the same eSathi portal or visit Tehsil office |
| Fee payment failed but money deducted | Wait 48 hours for auto-refund; if not, raise a complaint on the portal |

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is the eSathi caste certificate valid for central government jobs?
Yes. The digitally signed certificate from eSathi is valid for UPSC, SSC, Railways, and all central/state government recruitment.

### Can I apply for someone else's caste certificate?
Yes, parents or guardians can apply for minors. For adults, you need their consent and documents.

### How long is the caste certificate valid?
In UP, the caste certificate has **lifetime validity** — it does not expire. However, some institutions may ask for a recently issued one.

### Can NRIs apply for a UP caste certificate?
If your family is from UP and you can provide the required documents (family register, etc.), you can apply through a family member or authorized person.

### What if my caste is not in the official list?
Only castes listed in the UP Government's official SC/ST/OBC list are eligible. Check the list on the [Social Welfare Department](https://samajkalyan.up.gov.in) website.
