# Telangana Nativity/Domicile Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **nativity/residence certificate** (domicile certificate) in Telangana is issued by the Revenue Department (Mandal Revenue Officer) and is required for EAMCET/NEET Telangana state quota (85%) medical and engineering admissions, TS government jobs (TSPSC), and state scheme eligibility. Telangana requires **10 years of residence OR 7 years of school study** for state quota eligibility. Applications go through **MeeSeva** — Telangana's service delivery platform.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at any **MeeSeva centre** or [ts.meeseva.gov.in](https://ts.meeseva.gov.in). Issued by MRO within **7–21 days**.

---

## Telangana Nativity — Residence Requirements

For **EAMCET/NEET Telangana state quota**:

| Eligibility Route | Requirement |
|------------------|-------------|
| **Residence-based** | 10 years continuous residence in Telangana |
| **Study-based** | 7 years of schooling in Telangana schools (any combination from Class 1–12) |
| **Parents** | If parents are Telangana locals (local area residents for 10 years) |

> Local area rules in Telangana are tied to **district-level local area definitions** (former Telangana districts vs other districts). This is important for engineering admissions where "local" vs "non-local" quota distinctions apply within Telangana itself.

## Why You Need Telangana Nativity Certificate

- **EAMCET TS state quota** — 85% engineering, pharmacy, agriculture seats
- **NEET Telangana state quota** — 85% government medical college seats
- **TSPSC (Telangana State PSC)** — government job reservation for Telangana residents
- **TS scholarships** — BC/SC/ST welfare department scholarships for TS residents
- **Kalyanalakshmi / Shaadi Mubarak** scheme — TS domicile verification
- **Telangana state scheme eligibility** — Rythu Bandhu, Aarogyasri

---

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory)
- **Ration card** (shows Telangana address — rice/white card)
- **Voter ID** (Telangana voter enrollment)
- **School certificates** — Transfer Certificates from TS schools (showing study period)
- **Land records / Pattadar Passbook** (if claiming local through land ownership)
- **Utility bills** (electricity/water at TS address covering residence period)
- Self-declaration of residence/nativity
- **Voter ID of parent** (if claiming through parental local status)
- Passport-size photograph

## How to Apply via MeeSeva

**Method 1: MeeSeva Kiosk (Recommended)**

1. Visit nearest **MeeSeva centre**
2. Ask for **"Residence/Nativity Certificate"** or **"Domicile Certificate"**
3. Provide Aadhaar, ration card, school TCs, utility bills
4. Operator submits application
5. Fee: approximately **₹35–60**
6. Receive acknowledgement

**Method 2: MeeSeva Online Portal**

1. Go to [ts.meeseva.gov.in](https://ts.meeseva.gov.in)
2. Log in / register
3. Search **"Nativity Certificate"** or **"Residence Certificate"**
4. Fill form, upload documents, submit

---

## Apply via Ward/Village Secretariat

Telangana's **Ward Secretariat** (urban) and **Village Secretariat** (rural) assist:
1. Visit your **Ward/Village Secretariat**
2. Submit nativity certificate application with documents
3. Free of charge at secretariat
4. Application forwarded to MRO for processing

---

## Status Check & Download

1. Log in to [ts.meeseva.gov.in](https://ts.meeseva.gov.in)
2. Click **"My Applications"** → view nativity certificate status
3. Once approved: download digitally signed PDF

## EAMCET TS State Quota — Local Area Rules

For EAMCET Telangana, there are **local area quotas within Telangana**:
- **70% local area quota**: For students who studied in local district's schools
- **15% non-local quota**: For TS students who studied outside their home district but within TS
- **15% all-India quota**: Open to all

Verify the specific local area rules in the TSCHE EAMCET notification each year.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I studied in Hyderabad for 8 years — am I Telangana local?

If you studied in Hyderabad (Telangana) for **7+ years in school** (Classes 1–12), you qualify for TS state quota through the study-based route. Apply for nativity certificate based on your Hyderabad school study records.

### Telangana nativity for TSPSC — same as residence certificate?

Yes, for **TSPSC government jobs**, the nativity/domicile certificate proving Telangana residence is the required document. The MeeSeva-issued domicile/residence certificate serves this purpose.

### Andhra Pradesh native studying in Telangana — EAMCET eligibility?

If you are originally from AP but have studied in TS schools for **7+ years**, you may qualify for TS EAMCET state quota through the study-based route. Your nativity/domicile for state quota is based on study period, not birth state.
