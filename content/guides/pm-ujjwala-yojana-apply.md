# PM Ujjwala Yojana Apply Online: Free Gas Connection

Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY) is a flagship scheme of the Government of India that provides free LPG gas connections to women from Below Poverty Line (BPL) families. Launched in 2016 and expanded as Ujjwala 2.0 in 2021, the scheme aims to replace unhealthy cooking fuels like wood, coal, and dung with clean LPG fuel.

## What is PM Ujjwala Yojana?

Under PMUY, the government provides:
- **Free LPG gas connection** (no security deposit for cylinder and regulator)
- **₹1,600 subsidy** for the first refill and stove (under Ujjwala 2.0)
- **Ongoing LPG subsidy** on refills directly to bank account via DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer)
- The connection is issued in the **name of the woman** of the household

The scheme has provided over **10 crore free LPG connections** since launch.

## Who is Eligible?

### Ujjwala 2.0 Eligibility

1. **Adult woman** (18 years or above) from the household
2. Belonging to any of these categories:
   - BPL (Below Poverty Line) household listed in SECC 2011 data
   - SC/ST household
   - Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) beneficiary
   - Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) beneficiary
   - Forest dweller
   - Most Backward Classes (MBC)
   - Tea garden and ex-tea garden tribe
   - People living in islands and river islands
3. **No existing LPG connection** in the household (from any oil marketing company)
4. Must have a **bank account** and [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)

## Documents Required

**Mandatory Documents:**
- Aadhaar card of the applicant (adult woman)
- BPL ration card or proof of belonging to eligible category
- Bank account passbook or cancelled cheque (in applicant's name)
- Passport-size photograph

**Additional Documents (Ujjwala 2.0):**
- Self-declaration of address (for migrants without address proof)
- Ration card or family composition certificate
- KYC form (provided at the distributor)

**Note:** Under Ujjwala 2.0, address proof requirement has been relaxed — a self-declaration is accepted for migrant families.

## How to Apply for PM Ujjwala Yojana

### Online Application

1. Visit the official portal [pmuy.gov.in](https://www.pmuy.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"Apply for New Connection"**
3. Select your oil marketing company (Indian Oil, Bharat Petroleum, or HP Gas)
4. Fill in the application form with personal details
5. Enter **Aadhaar number** and verify with OTP
6. Upload required documents
7. Submit the application and note the reference number
8. The nearest LPG distributor will contact you for verification

### Offline Application (At LPG Distributor)

1. Visit the nearest **LPG gas agency** (Indane, Bharat Gas, or HP Gas distributor)
2. Ask for the **Ujjwala Yojana application form** (Form available free of cost)
3. Fill in the form with personal and family details
4. Attach photocopies of Aadhaar card, BPL card, bank passbook, and photograph
5. Submit the KYC form along with the application
6. The distributor will verify your documents and eligibility via SECC data
7. If approved, you will receive the **LPG connection within 7-15 days**

## Subsidy Details

| Component | Amount |
|-----------|--------|
| Security deposit for cylinder | Waived (free) |
| Security deposit for regulator | Waived (free) |
| First refill + hot plate (stove) | ₹1,600 (government-funded) |
| LPG refill subsidy | As per government rates, credited to bank via DBT |
| EMI option for stove (if not free) | Up to ₹500 per refill adjusted from subsidy |

### How Refill Subsidy Works

- You pay the **market price** for each LPG refill at the time of delivery
- The **subsidy amount** is credited directly to your linked bank account within a few days
- You can track your subsidy status on the [MyLPG portal](https://www.mylpg.in/) or PAHAL DBT portal
- Ensure your bank account is linked to your Aadhaar for DBT

## KYC Process

eKYC is mandatory for Ujjwala beneficiaries:

1. Visit your LPG distributor with your **Aadhaar card**
2. Complete **biometric verification** (fingerprint) at the distributor's point
3. Your Aadhaar will be linked to your LPG consumer number
4. This ensures subsidy reaches your bank account directly
5. You can also complete KYC via the oil company's portal or app

## How to Check Application Status

1. Visit [pmuy.gov.in](https://www.pmuy.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"Track Application"** or **"Check Status"**
3. Enter your application reference number or Aadhaar number
4. The portal will show your current application status

## Important Tips

1. **Apply through the woman member** of the household — connections are issued only in women's names
2. **Link your Aadhaar to your bank account** before applying to receive subsidy without delay
3. **Keep your BPL/ration card handy** — eligibility is verified against SECC 2011 data
4. **Choose the nearest LPG distributor** — this determines your delivery area for refills
5. **Don't pay any agent or middleman** — the application is completely free; report any demand for money to the helpline at 1906

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I apply for PM Ujjwala Yojana online?
Visit [pmuy.gov.in](https://www.pmuy.gov.in/), click "Apply for New Connection," select your oil company, fill in the form with Aadhaar and bank details, and submit with supporting documents.

### Who is eligible for Ujjwala Yojana 2.0?
Adult women (18+) from BPL families, SC/ST households, PMAY beneficiaries, AAY cardholders, forest dwellers, and MBC families who don't have an existing LPG connection are eligible.

### Is Ujjwala Yojana completely free?
Yes, the gas connection (cylinder + regulator) is provided free. Under Ujjwala 2.0, the first refill and hot plate are also free (worth ₹1,600). Subsequent refills are at market price with subsidy credited to your bank.

### How much subsidy do Ujjwala beneficiaries get per refill?
The subsidy amount varies and is decided by the government periodically. It is credited directly to your Aadhaar-linked bank account after each refill purchase. Check [mylpg.in](https://www.mylpg.in/) for current rates.

### Can I apply if I already have a gas connection?
No, only households without any existing LPG connection (from any oil marketing company) are eligible. If your previous connection was surrendered, you may be eligible — check with the distributor.

### What is the helpline number for Ujjwala Yojana?
You can call the LPG helpline at **1906** for queries related to Ujjwala Yojana, LPG subsidy, and gas connection issues.

### How many free refills do Ujjwala beneficiaries get?
Under Ujjwala 2.0, the first refill is free. The government has also periodically provided free refills (e.g., 12 free refills per year during specific periods) — check the latest announcements on pmuy.gov.in.
