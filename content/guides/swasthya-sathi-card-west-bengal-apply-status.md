# Swasthya Sathi Card West Bengal 2026 — Complete Guide

**Swasthya Sathi** is West Bengal's flagship health insurance scheme providing **₹5 lakh cashless health coverage per family per year** at government and empanelled private hospitals. Over 2.25 crore families in WB are enrolled.

> **Quick answer:** Apply at [swasthyasathi.gov.in](https://swasthyasathi.gov.in) or the nearest Duare Sarkar camp. The card is in the woman's name (head of household). Existing cardholders can download the e-card from the portal.

---

## Key Benefits

| Benefit | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Coverage** | ₹5 lakh per family per year (cashless) |
| **Card holder** | Senior-most woman in the family |
| **Hospitals** | All government hospitals + 1,800+ empanelled private hospitals |
| **Conditions covered** | Cancer, cardiac surgery, orthopaedics, kidney, eye — 1,653+ packages |
| **Cost** | FREE — fully funded by WB government |
| **Portability** | Valid at empanelled hospitals across West Bengal |

---

## Eligibility

- **Permanent resident of West Bengal**
- **All families** are eligible — no income ceiling for WB residents
- The card is in the **senior-most woman's name** in the household
- Must have a **ration card** (used for household identification)

## Documents Required

| # | Document |
|---|---|

| 4 | **Passport-size photograph** |
| 5 | **Mobile number** (for OTP and alerts) |

## How to Apply for Swasthya Sathi Card

### Method 1: Online at swasthyasathi.gov.in

**Step 1:** Visit [swasthyasathi.gov.in](https://swasthyasathi.gov.in)

**Step 2:** Click **"Apply Online"** / **"New Registration"**

**Step 3:** Enter ration card number and Aadhaar — OTP verify

**Step 4:** Fill in family member details, address, and mobile number

**Step 5:** Upload photograph and documents

**Step 6:** Submit — note the application reference number

**Step 7:** Card is delivered within 30–60 days or collect from designated centre

### Method 2: Duare Sarkar Camp

- Attend the next **Duare Sarkar camp** in your panchayat or ward
- Bring original ration card, Aadhaar, voter ID and photo
- Camp officials handle the enrollment on the spot
- Acknowledgment slip given — card delivered within a month

### Method 3: Bangla Sahayata Kendra (BSK)

- Visit the nearest BSK in your area
- Staff will assist with online registration
- Carry all original documents

## How to Check Swasthya Sathi Card Status

**Step 1:** Go to [swasthyasathi.gov.in](https://swasthyasathi.gov.in)

**Step 2:** Click **"Track Application"** or **"Beneficiary Status"**

**Step 3:** Enter your ration card number or application reference number

**Step 4:** Status shows: Applied / Under Verification / Approved / Card Dispatched

---

## How to Download Swasthya Sathi e-Card

**Step 1:** Visit [swasthyasathi.gov.in](https://swasthyasathi.gov.in)

**Step 2:** Click **"Download e-Card"**

**Step 3:** Enter Aadhaar number of the woman cardholder + OTP

**Step 4:** Download the PDF e-card — valid for cashless treatment

You can show the e-card on your mobile at empanelled hospitals.

## Swasthya Sathi Card Not Working at Hospital — Fix

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| **Card shows "expired"** | Renew at swasthyasathi.gov.in or Duare Sarkar camp — renewal is free |
| **Card rejected at hospital** | Check if hospital is empanelled: swasthyasathi.gov.in → "Find Hospital" |
| **Name mismatch on card** | Visit BSK with Aadhaar + ration card to correct details |
| **Card not delivered** | Track status online; if approved but not received, collect from block office |
| **₹5L limit exhausted** | No extension for that year — resets annually |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Swasthya Sathi different from Ayushman Bharat?**
Yes. Swasthya Sathi is a West Bengal state scheme. Ayushman Bharat (PMJAY) is central. WB has its own Swasthya Sathi and does not participate in PMJAY. For WB residents, use Swasthya Sathi.

**Can I use Swasthya Sathi for pre-existing conditions?**
Yes — pre-existing conditions ARE covered from day 1. No waiting period for most packages.

**Does Swasthya Sathi cover medicines and OPD?**
No — it covers only inpatient (admitted) treatment at empanelled hospitals. OPD visits and outpatient medicines are not covered.

**Can I add a new family member?**
Yes — visit your nearest Duare Sarkar camp or BSK with the member's Aadhaar to add them to the card.
