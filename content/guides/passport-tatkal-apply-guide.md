# Tatkal Passport Apply: Process, Fees & Documents

Need a passport urgently? The **Tatkal scheme** by the Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) lets you get a passport within **1–3 working days** by paying an additional fee. This guide covers the complete tatkal passport application process, fees, documents, and tips.

## What is Tatkal Passport?

Tatkal passport is an expedited passport issuance service offered by the Government of India through the Passport Seva portal. Under the normal scheme, a passport takes 15–30 days. Under tatkal, you can receive it in **1–3 working days** after police verification (if applicable).

Tatkal service is available for:
- Fresh passport applications
- Re-issue of passports (renewal, lost, damaged)

---

## Who is Eligible for Tatkal Passport?

Any Indian citizen who is eligible for a regular passport can apply under the tatkal scheme. However:

- You must have a valid reason for urgency (though no proof of urgency is required)
- Minors (below 18) can also apply under tatkal
- If you are applying for the first time, police verification happens **after** passport issuance
- If you are re-issuing with the same details, police verification may be waived

## Documents Required for Tatkal Passport

### Mandatory Documents

1. **Proof of Present Address** (any one):
   - Aadhaar card (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
   - Electricity bill (in applicant's name, not older than 3 months)
   - Water bill / Telephone bill / Gas connection bill
   - Voter ID card
   - Bank account passbook (with address)
   - Income Tax Assessment Order

2. **Proof of Date of Birth** (any one):
   - Birth certificate issued by Registrar of Births and Deaths
   - Transfer/School leaving certificate with DOB
   - PAN card
   - Aadhaar card
   - Voter ID (for age proof)

3. **Annexure F** (Verification Certificate):
   - This is **mandatory for tatkal fresh passport** applications
   - Must be signed by any one of the specified verification authorities:
     - Gazetted officer of Central/State government
     - Member of Parliament or MLA
     - Officer of PSU or statutory body
     - Notary public
   - Annexure F certifies the identity and address of the applicant
   - Download from: [passportindia.gov.in](https://www.passportindia.gov.in/)

4. **Self-attested copies of supporting documents**

### Additional Documents (if applicable)
- Marriage certificate (for married women with changed name)
- Divorce decree (if applicable)
- NOC from employer (for government employees)

## Tatkal Passport Fees

| Service | 36 Pages | 60 Pages |
|---------|----------|----------|
| Fresh Tatkal Passport (Age 18+) | ₹3,500 | ₹4,000 |
| Fresh Tatkal Passport (Minor below 18) | ₹3,500 | N/A |
| Re-issue Tatkal Passport (Age 18+) | ₹3,500 | ₹4,000 |
| Tatkal fee is **in addition** to the normal passport fee | | |

**Normal passport fee:** ₹1,500 (36 pages) / ₹2,000 (60 pages)

**Total cost for fresh tatkal passport (36 pages):** ₹1,500 + ₹2,000 (tatkal fee) = **₹3,500**

> **Note:** Fees are subject to revision. Check the [Passport Seva portal](https://www.passportindia.gov.in/) for latest fee structure.

## Step-by-Step Process to Apply for Tatkal Passport

### Online Application

1. **Visit** [Passport Seva Online Portal](https://www.passportindia.gov.in/) and register/login
2. **Click** "Apply for Fresh Passport / Re-issue of Passport"
3. **Select** application type — choose **"Tatkal"** under the scheme
4. **Fill** the application form with personal details, family information, and address
5. **Upload** the required documents (photograph and signature as per specifications)
6. **Pay** the tatkal fee online (credit/debit card, net banking, UPI, or SBI challan)
7. **Schedule appointment** at your nearest Passport Seva Kendra (PSK) or Regional Passport Office (RPO)
8. **Print** the Application Receipt Number (ARN)

### At the Passport Seva Kendra

1. **Arrive** at your scheduled appointment time with all original documents
2. **Token counter** — submit your ARN and get a token
3. **Counter A** — Document verification by officials
4. **Counter B** — Biometric data capture (photo, fingerprints, signature)
5. **Counter C** — Granting officer reviews and approves the application
6. **Collect** the acknowledgement receipt

### After PSK Visit

- For **re-issue with same details**: Passport may be printed and dispatched the same day or next day
- For **fresh passport**: Police verification is initiated and passport is issued within 1–3 days (verification may happen post-issuance)
- Passport is delivered via **Speed Post** to your address

## Important Tips

1. **Book appointment early** — Tatkal slots fill up fast, especially in metro cities. Book as soon as the portal opens new slots.
2. **Keep Annexure F ready** — For fresh tatkal passports, Annexure F is mandatory. Get it signed in advance.
3. **Carry all originals** — Bring original documents along with self-attested photocopies.
4. **Check document specifications** — Photo must be 2x2 inches, white background, and signature in black ink.
5. **Track your application** — Use the ARN on the Passport Seva portal or mPassport Seva app to track status.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Can I apply for tatkal passport online?
Yes. Visit [passportindia.gov.in](https://www.passportindia.gov.in/), register, fill the form, select "Tatkal" as the scheme, pay the fees, and book an appointment at a PSK.

### 2. What is Annexure F and when is it needed?
Annexure F is a verification certificate required for all **fresh tatkal passport** applications. It must be signed by a gazetted officer, MP, MLA, notary, or other specified authorities confirming your identity and address.

### 3. How much does a tatkal passport cost?
The total fee for a fresh 36-page tatkal passport is approximately **₹3,500** (normal fee + tatkal fee). For 60-page booklet, it is approximately ₹4,000.

### 4. How many days does it take to get a tatkal passport?
Typically **1–3 working days** after your PSK appointment. If police verification is required before issuance, it may take longer.

### 5. Is police verification required for tatkal passport?
For **fresh passports**, police verification is done **after** passport issuance (post-issuance verification). For **re-issue** with same details, police verification may be waived entirely.

### 6. Can minors apply for tatkal passport?
Yes. Minors can apply under the tatkal scheme. A parent or guardian must apply on their behalf, and Annexure F is needed for fresh applications.

### 7. What is the difference between tatkal and normal passport?
The only difference is **processing speed and fees**. A tatkal passport is processed in 1–3 days instead of 15–30 days. The passport document itself is the same.

### 8. Can I convert my normal passport application to tatkal?
No. You cannot convert an already submitted normal application to tatkal. You would need to apply afresh under the tatkal scheme.
