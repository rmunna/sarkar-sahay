# Maharashtra Government Schemes and Services – Complete Guide

Maharashtra, India's most industrialized state, runs an extensive network of welfare schemes covering women's empowerment, housing, education, health, agriculture, and social security. Whether you are looking for financial assistance, scholarships, pension, or government certificates, this guide covers every major Maharashtra government scheme with eligibility details, benefits, and application process.

## Key Government Portals in Maharashtra

Before applying for any scheme, familiarize yourself with these essential portals:

- **Aaple Sarkar Portal** – [aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in](https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in/) – Single window for 400+ government services including certificates, licenses, and permits. Read our detailed guide on [Aaple Sarkar Maharashtra Certificates](/guide/aaple-sarkar-maharashtra-certificates).
- **MahaDBT Portal** – [mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in](https://mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in/) – Direct Benefit Transfer portal for scholarships, pensions, and scheme benefits.
- **IGRS Maharashtra** – [igrmaharashtra.gov.in](https://igrmaharashtra.gov.in/) – Property registration, stamp duty, and e-search. See our guide on [IGRS Maharashtra Property Registration](/guide/igrs-maharashtra-property-registration).
- **Maharashtra Government Official Website** – [maharashtra.gov.in](https://www.maharashtra.gov.in/) – Central hub for all government departments and notifications.

## Women Empowerment Schemes

### Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana

One of Maharashtra's most popular schemes providing monthly financial assistance to women from economically weaker sections. The scheme aims to promote women's financial independence and well-being.

**Key benefits:**
- Monthly financial assistance of ₹1,500 transferred directly to bank accounts
- Covers women aged 21-65 years from eligible income groups
- No middlemen – direct bank transfer via DBT

Read the complete guide: [Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana Maharashtra](/guide/majhi-ladki-bahin-yojana-maharashtra)

Also see: [Maharashtra Lek Ladki Yojana](/guide/maharashtra-lek-ladki-yojana) – financial assistance for girl children from birth to age 18, with total benefits up to ₹1,01,000.

### Maharashtra Lek Ladki Yojana

This scheme provides staggered financial assistance to families of girl children born in economically weaker households. Benefits are released at key milestones – birth, school admission, class 6, class 9, class 12, and age 18.

Read more: [Maharashtra Lek Ladki Yojana](/guide/maharashtra-lek-ladki-yojana)

## Housing Schemes

### Gharkul Yojana (Ramai Awas Yojana)

Maharashtra's flagship rural housing scheme provides financial assistance for constructing pucca houses for families from SC, ST, and Neo-Buddhist communities living below the poverty line.

**Key benefits:**
- Financial assistance of ₹1.20 lakh to ₹2.50 lakh for house construction
- Priority for homeless families, those living in kutcha houses
- Convergence with MGNREGA for additional wage component

Read the complete guide: [Maharashtra Gharkul Yojana](/guide/maharashtra-gharkul-yojana)

### Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (Urban) in Maharashtra

Under PMAY-Urban, eligible urban households in Maharashtra can get subsidized housing with interest subsidy on home loans ranging from 3% to 6.5% depending on income category.

## Social Security and Pension Schemes

### Shravan Bal Seva Rajya Nivruttivetan Yojana

Maharashtra's state pension scheme for senior citizens provides monthly pension to elderly persons who do not have adequate means of livelihood.

**Key benefits:**
- Monthly pension of ₹1,500 for persons aged 65 and above (BPL or annual income below ₹21,000)
- Higher pension amount for those aged 80+
- Direct bank transfer

Read the complete guide: [Maharashtra Shravan Bal Pension](/guide/maharashtra-shravan-bal-pension)

### Sanjay Gandhi Niradhar Anudan Yojana

This scheme provides financial assistance to destitute persons including widows, orphans, disabled persons, and those suffering from serious illnesses who have no means of livelihood.

### Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension in Maharashtra

Central government pension scheme implemented through the state, providing ₹200-500 per month to BPL senior citizens. The state adds a top-up amount.

## Health Schemes

### Mahatma Jyotiba Phule Jan Arogya Yojana (MJPJAY)

Maharashtra's state health insurance scheme provides cashless treatment to families across the state. Under MJPJAY 2.0 (launched July 2024), coverage has been increased to ₹5 lakh per family per year, integrated with Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY.

**Key features:**
- Covers 1,356+ medical procedures across 34 specialties including major surgeries
- Cashless treatment at 1,900+ government and private empanelled hospitals
- Ration card and domicile certificate are the primary eligibility documents
- Available to all Maharashtra residents with valid ration card

See also: [Ayushman Bharat vs State Health Schemes](/guide/ayushman-bharat-vs-state-health-schemes)

## Agriculture Schemes

### Nanaji Deshmukh Krishi Sanjivani Yojana

A World Bank-assisted project for climate-resilient agriculture in Maharashtra, focusing on drought-prone areas. Benefits include:
- Soil health improvement
- Water-use efficiency through micro-irrigation
- Crop diversification support
- Post-harvest management

### Birsa Munda Krishi Kranti Yojana

Financial assistance for tribal farmers in Maharashtra for purchasing farm equipment, seeds, and other agricultural inputs.

## How to Apply for Maharashtra Government Schemes

Most Maharashtra government schemes follow a common application process:

### Online Application (Recommended)

1. **Visit the relevant portal** – Aaple Sarkar for certificates, MahaDBT for scholarships and pensions
2. **Register** – Create an account using your Aadhaar number and mobile number
3. **Fill the application form** – Enter personal, income, and category details
4. **Upload documents** – Aadhaar card, income certificate, caste certificate, bank passbook, photographs
5. **Submit and track** – Note the application number for tracking status

### Offline Application

1. Visit the nearest **Setu Suvidha Kendra** or **Common Service Centre (CSC)**
2. Carry original documents and photocopies
3. Fill the application form available at the centre
4. Submit with required documents and collect the acknowledgment receipt

### Common Documents Required

- Aadhaar card
- Ration card
- Income certificate
- Caste certificate (for reserved category schemes)
- Bank passbook (for DBT schemes)
- Domicile certificate
- Passport-size photographs

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How can I check my Maharashtra scheme application status?**
Visit the respective portal (Aaple Sarkar or MahaDBT), log in with your credentials, and check the application status using your application number.

**Is Aadhaar mandatory for Maharashtra government schemes?**
Yes, most schemes require Aadhaar for identity verification and DBT transfers. Aadhaar-linked bank accounts are mandatory for receiving benefits.

**Can I apply for multiple schemes simultaneously?**
Yes, you can apply for multiple schemes as long as you meet the eligibility criteria for each. However, some schemes may have exclusion clauses.

**Where can I complain about scheme-related issues?**
You can raise grievances on the Aaple Sarkar portal, call the respective helpline numbers, or visit the district collector's office.
