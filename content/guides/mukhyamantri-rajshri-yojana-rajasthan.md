## What is Mukhyamantri Rajshri Yojana?

Mukhyamantri Rajshri Yojana is a Rajasthan government scheme that provides financial assistance of ₹50,000 to families with a girl child, paid in 6 installments from birth to completion of Class 12. The scheme aims to promote the birth, survival, and education of girl children in Rajasthan, and to reduce the gender gap.

The scheme is applicable for girl children born in **government hospitals or accredited private hospitals on or after June 1, 2016**.

## ₹50,000 in 6 Installments

| Installment | When | Amount |
|-------------|------|--------|
| 1st | At birth (in government/accredited hospital) | ₹2,500 |
| 2nd | 1 year after birth (vaccination complete + Aadhaar enrolled) | ₹2,500 |
| 3rd | Admission to Class 1 (government school) | ₹4,000 |
| 4th | Admission to Class 6 (government school) | ₹5,000 |
| 5th | Admission to Class 10 (government school) | ₹11,000 |
| 6th | Passing Class 12 (from government school) | ₹25,000 |
| **Total** | | **₹50,000** |

Money is transferred directly to the **mother's bank account** (or father's if mother has no account).

## Who is Eligible?

- Girl child must be born in a **government hospital or accredited private hospital** in Rajasthan on or after **June 1, 2016**
- Must be a **permanent resident of Rajasthan**
- Mother/father must have **Bhamashah card / Jan Aadhaar card** (Rajasthan's family identity card)
- The family should be a **Bhamashah enrolled family**
- Third girl child and beyond are **not eligible** — only the first two girl children in a family qualify

**Important:** The installments for Class 1, 6, 10, and 12 are paid only if the girl is studying in a **government school** in Rajasthan.

## Documents Required

**For 1st and 2nd installment (at birth and 1 year):**
1. Jan Aadhaar card / Bhamashah card of family
2. Birth certificate of the girl child (from hospital)
3. Mother's Aadhaar card
4. Mother's bank account details (Aadhaar-linked)
5. Hospital discharge summary

**For 3rd to 6th installment (school admissions):**
1. Jan Aadhaar card
2. Girl child's Aadhaar card
3. Previous installment details (installment ID)
4. School enrollment certificate / transfer certificate
5. For 6th installment: Class 12 passing certificate

## How to Apply — Step by Step

**1st and 2nd installment (Birth and 1-year):**
1. Deliver at a **government hospital** or accredited private hospital
2. At the hospital itself, the Rajshri Yojana 1st installment is **automatically processed** by the hospital staff
3. Mother's bank account details are submitted at the hospital
4. Within 2–4 weeks, ₹2,500 is credited to the mother's account
5. After 1 year, when the child gets all vaccinations (BCG, DPT, OPV, measles) and Aadhaar is enrolled, the 2nd installment is processed through the Anganwadi Worker or health worker

**3rd installment (Class 1 admission):**
1. When the girl is admitted to **Class 1 in a government school**
2. The school's principal or teacher submits the application through the **Shala Darpan portal** (rajshaladarpan.nic.in) on behalf of the student
3. Or parents can visit the school and ask the school to file the installment claim
4. ₹4,000 is credited to the mother's account within 1–2 months

**4th, 5th, and 6th installments (Class 6, 10, 12):**
- Same process as 3rd installment
- The school submits the claim through Shala Darpan portal
- Parents should ensure the school has their correct Jan Aadhaar number and bank account

## How to Check Application Status

1. Visit **rajshaladarpan.nic.in** → Rajshri section
2. Or visit the nearest **District Education Office (DEO)** or **Women and Child Development (WCD) office**
3. Or contact your school principal for school-submitted installments
4. Or call the **Rajshri helpline** or WCD district office

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My daughter was born in a private hospital. Is she eligible?
Only if the private hospital is **accredited** (empanelled) under the Janani Suraksha Yojana or PM Matru Vandana Yojana scheme. Most small private hospitals or nursing homes may not be accredited — verify with your district hospital before delivery.

### Can I apply if I missed the first installment?
If the 1st installment was not processed at the hospital, you can approach the Chief Medical and Health Officer (CMHO) or the District Women and Child Development Officer with documents to apply for missed installments.

### What if my daughter is studying in a private school?
Installments 3 through 6 are only given for girls studying in **government schools**. If the girl is in a private school, the installments for those stages are not paid. Installments 1 and 2 (birth and 1-year) are not affected by school type.

### Is the third girl child eligible?
No — only the **first two girl children** in a family are eligible. The third and subsequent girl children are not covered.

### What is Bhamashah / Jan Aadhaar card?
Bhamashah was replaced by Jan Aadhaar card in 2019–20. Jan Aadhaar is Rajasthan's family identity card that serves as the basis for DBT (direct benefit transfer). Apply for Jan Aadhaar at janaadhaar.rajasthan.gov.in or any CSC/e-Mitra center.

### My daughter is in Class 1 now but the school has not applied for the installment. What should I do?
Speak to the school principal. The school is supposed to submit the Rajshri installment claim through the Shala Darpan portal. If the school is unaware or not doing it, escalate to the Block Education Officer (BEO) or District Education Officer (DEO).
