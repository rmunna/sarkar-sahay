# TVK Youth Unemployment Allowance 2026 — ₹4,000/Month for Graduates

CM Vijay's **TVK government** has announced a monthly allowance for unemployed Tamil Nadu youth — a bridging support while they search for jobs or pursue skill development.

| Category | Monthly Allowance |
|----------|------------------|
| **Unemployed Graduates (Degree holders)** | **₹4,000/month** |
| **Unemployed Diploma Holders** | **₹2,500/month** |
| **Age Group** | 21 to 29 years |

> **Implementation:** Within 100 days of government formation (by August 2026). CM Vijay was sworn in on **18 May 2026**.

---

## What is the TVK Youth Unemployment Allowance?

The TVK Youth Unemployment Allowance is a monthly stipend provided to educated but unemployed youth while they are between jobs or preparing for competitive exams and placement opportunities. It is part of the broader **Kalvi & Thozhil Urimai (Education & Employment Rights)** pillar of the TVK governing manifesto.

This is NOT a job — it is a **bridging financial support** for the period of unemployment. Recipients are expected to:
- Actively seek employment
- Optionally enrol in government skill development programmes
- Register at their district Employment Exchange

## Eligibility Criteria

| Criteria | Details |
|----------|---------|
| **Qualification** | Degree (any stream) or Diploma |
| **Age** | 21 to 29 years |
| **Residence** | Resident of Tamil Nadu |
| **Employment Status** | Currently unemployed (not in government or private salaried job) |
| **Registration** | Must register at District Employment Exchange |

### Additional Likely Conditions (from TVK manifesto):
- Family income below a prescribed annual limit
- Not enrolled in full-time higher education (part-time courses may be allowed — await G.O.)
- Must not be receiving any other government monthly stipend

## Required Documents

- **Aadhaar Card** (mandatory)
- **Degree Certificate / Diploma Certificate** (as applicable)
- **Marksheets** (Class 10, 12, and final qualifying exam)
- **Employment Exchange Registration Card**
- **Income Certificate** (from Tahsildar / VAO)
- **Bank Passbook** (Aadhaar-linked account)
- **Residence Proof** (ration card / voter ID)
- **Affidavit of unemployment** (simple declaration — format to be provided)

## How to Apply

### Step 1 — Register at District Employment Exchange
This is mandatory. Visit the **District Employment Exchange** in your district and register with your educational certificates. You will receive an Employment Exchange Registration Number.

### Step 2 — Apply Online (When Portal Goes Live)
Visit [eservices.tn.gov.in](https://eservices.tn.gov.in/) → Search for "TVK Youth Unemployment Allowance" → Fill application form → Upload documents → Submit.

### Step 3 — Via Mudhalvar Makkal Sevai Nanbar (Doorstep)
The TVK government has deployed **5 lakh youth workers** across Tamil Nadu to help citizens register for schemes at their doorstep. A worker will come to your area — carry your documents and get registered.

### Step 4 — Receive Monthly Payment
Once approved, ₹4,000 (or ₹2,500 for diploma holders) is credited to your Aadhaar-linked bank account every month.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When will the TVK ₹4,000/month allowance start?
Implementation is expected within 100 days of the TVK government formation (by August 2026). Official notification will be published at [tn.gov.in](https://www.tn.gov.in/).

### Who gets ₹4,000 and who gets ₹2,500?
**Degree holders** (BA, B.Sc, B.Com, BE, MBBS, LLB, etc.) receive **₹4,000/month**. **Diploma holders** (polytechnic diplomas, ITI certificates, etc.) receive **₹2,500/month**.

### Is there an income limit to apply?
The official G.O. will specify the income ceiling. Based on the TVK manifesto, it targets youth from ordinary households — not from affluent families.

### Can someone studying part-time and working part-time apply?
The scheme is for the **unemployed** — people without a regular salaried job. Part-time working students may or may not qualify depending on the official conditions. Await the G.O.

### Can I continue receiving the allowance if I get a job?
No. You must inform the relevant authority and the allowance will stop from the month you take up employment.

### What if I'm 30 years old — can I still apply?
The announced age bracket is **21 to 29 years**. The G.O. may have a specific cut-off date (e.g., "age as of 1 June 2026"). Above 29 years is not covered under this specific scheme.

### Is Employment Exchange registration mandatory?
Based on similar schemes in other states, Employment Exchange registration is typically mandatory as it verifies your unemployment status. Register early at your **District Employment Exchange**.

### What is the ₹10,000/month skill stipend?
Separately from the unemployment allowance, youth enrolled in TVK **government-backed skill development programmes** (at TNSDC / ASDM-equivalent skill centres) receive ₹10,000/month as a training stipend. This is a higher amount available to those actively upskilling.
