# SSC GD Constable 2026 — Complete Guide (CISF, BSF, CRPF, ITBP, SSB)

SSC GD Constable is one of the **biggest central government recruitment drives in India** — with 50,000–80,000 vacancies across paramilitary forces in each cycle. Conducted by the **Staff Selection Commission (SSC)**, this is a top opportunity for 10th-pass candidates to join India's central armed police forces.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is independent. Verify all details at [ssc.gov.in](https://ssc.gov.in).

## Exam Pattern (CBT)

### Computer Based Test

| Subject | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| General Intelligence & Reasoning | 20 | 40 |
| General Knowledge & General Awareness | 20 | 40 |
| Elementary Mathematics | 20 | 40 |
| English / Hindi | 20 | 40 |
| **Total** | **80** | **160** |

- **Duration:** 60 minutes
- **Negative Marking:** 0.50 marks per wrong answer
- **Mode:** Online (Computer Based Test)
- **Qualifying:** 35% (General), 33% (OBC), 30% (SC/ST/Ex-SM)
- **Language:** Question paper in Hindi and English (you choose)

## Physical Standard Test (PST)

## Medical Standards

- Minimum visual acuity: 6/6 in one eye, 6/9 in other (without glasses)
- No colour blindness (Ishihara test)
- No flat foot, knock knee, squint
- No hearing deficiency
- No chronic disease or disability

## Detailed Syllabus

### General Intelligence & Reasoning
Analogies, alpha-numeric series, coding-decoding, blood relations, directions, spatial visualization, Venn diagrams, syllogisms, classification, missing numbers, statement-conclusion.

### General Knowledge & Awareness
Current events (national + international), Indian history, geography, economy, polity, science, culture, sports. Focus on last 6 months current affairs before exam.

### Elementary Mathematics
Number system, LCM/HCF, simplification, percentage, ratio & proportion, profit/loss, SI/CI, time & work, speed-distance-time, mensuration (area/volume), trigonometry basics.

### English / Hindi
For English: Reading comprehension, fill in blanks, synonyms/antonyms, error detection, one-word substitution.
For Hindi: Same in Hindi — comprehension, grammar, idioms.

## Application Process

1. Visit **[ssc.gov.in](https://ssc.gov.in)**

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the 2026 SSC GD Constable vacancy count?**
Final vacancy count will be announced with the official notification. Previous cycles: 2023 had 26,146 vacancies; 2024–25 had 39,481 vacancies. Expect 40,000–60,000+ in 2026.

**Is SSC GD constable only for 10th pass?**
Yes — minimum qualification is Class 10 pass. There is no advantage for higher-educated candidates (salary, selection process is the same regardless of whether you have a degree).

**Which force is best to choose — CRPF, CISF, or BSF?**
It depends on your preference. CISF has industrial security postings (airports, PSUs — better quality of life). BSF and CRPF have border/conflict zone postings (higher risk but also higher allowances). SSB is posted along Nepal/Bhutan borders.

**Can girls apply for SSC GD Constable?**
Yes — female candidates can apply for all forces with different physical standards. Female candidates are exempt from application fee.

**Is there a bond period after joining SSC GD?**
No mandatory bond, but there is typically a service commitment period before you can apply for government jobs again. Force-specific rules vary — check with the respective force.
