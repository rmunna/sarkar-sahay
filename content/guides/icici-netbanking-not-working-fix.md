# ICICI NetBanking Not Working? Fix Login & Transaction Errors

ICICI Bank's Internet Banking (NetBanking) is used by millions of customers for fund transfers, bill payments, and account management. If you're facing login errors, password lockouts, OTP delays, or transaction failures, this guide covers all common problems and their fixes.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent platform and is not affiliated with ICICI Bank. Information is based on publicly available details from ICICI Bank's official website.

## Common ICICI NetBanking Problems and Fixes

### 1. Password Locked or Login Failed

Your ICICI NetBanking password gets locked after 3-5 incorrect login attempts.

**How to Fix:**

1. Go to [ICICI NetBanking login page](https://infinity.icicibank.com/)
2. Click on **"Forgot Password"**
3. Enter your **User ID** (usually your Customer ID or registered mobile number)
4. Verify via **OTP** sent to your registered mobile number
5. Set a new password following the password rules (8-28 characters, mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, special characters)
6. Log in with your new password

**If Forgot Password doesn't work:**
- Visit your nearest ICICI Bank branch with your PAN card and debit card
- Call ICICI customer care at **1800 1080** (toll-free) or **1860 120 7777**

### 2. OTP Not Received

OTP issues are the most common NetBanking complaint.

**How to Fix:**

1. **Wait 2-3 minutes** — OTPs can be delayed during peak hours
2. Check if your **mobile number is registered** with the bank — send SMS **"ITREG"** to 9215676766
3. Ensure your phone has **network signal** and is not in DND (Do Not Disturb) mode
4. Check your **SMS inbox and spam folder**
5. Try the **"Resend OTP"** option on the banking page
6. If using iMobile, check if OTP is appearing as an **in-app notification**
7. Restart your phone and try again

**If OTP still doesn't come:**
- Contact ICICI at **1800 1080** to verify your registered mobile number
- Visit a branch to update your mobile number if it has changed

### 3. Device Binding / Device Registration Issues

ICICI Bank requires device binding for iMobile Pay and sometimes for NetBanking on new devices.

**How to Fix:**

1. Open the **iMobile Pay** app on your new device
2. Enter your registered mobile number
3. You will receive a **debit card verification** prompt — enter your debit card number, expiry, and CVV
4. Complete the OTP verification
5. Your new device will be bound to your account

**Common errors:**
- **"Device not registered"** — Reinstall the iMobile app and start fresh registration
- **"Maximum device limit reached"** — You can bind up to 2 devices. Deregister old devices via NetBanking under **Settings > Device Management** or call customer care

### 4. iMobile Pay App Not Working

**Common issues and fixes:**

| Problem | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| App crashing on launch | Update to latest version from Play Store / App Store |
| Login loop / stuck on loading | Clear app cache: Settings > Apps > iMobile Pay > Clear Cache |
| Fingerprint/Face ID not working | Re-register biometric in app Settings > Login Preferences |
| UPI transaction failing | Check UPI server status; try after some time |
| App showing old balance | Pull down to refresh or log out and log in again |

### 5. Virtual Keyboard Not Loading

ICICI NetBanking uses a virtual keyboard for password entry for security.

**How to Fix:**

1. **Enable JavaScript** in your browser (mandatory for virtual keyboard)
2. **Disable ad blockers** — they can block the keyboard script
3. **Clear browser cache and cookies**
4. Try a different browser — Chrome or Firefox recommended
5. Disable any **browser extensions** that may interfere
6. Check if **Java/Flash plugins** are blocked (older systems)
7. Use **incognito/private browsing** mode

### 6. Transaction Failed but Amount Debited

**Steps to follow:**

1. **Wait 24-48 hours** — most failed transactions auto-reverse
2. Check your **account statement** in NetBanking for the reversal
3. If not reversed in 48 hours, raise a complaint:
   - NetBanking: Go to **Customer Service > Service Requests > Transaction Dispute**
   - Call **1800 1080** with transaction reference number
4. You can also file a complaint on the [ICICI Bank helpdesk](https://www.icicibank.com/helpdesk)

### 7. NetBanking Page Not Loading or Showing Error

**How to Fix:**

1. Check [ICICI Bank's official website](https://www.icicibank.com/) — if the main site is down, NetBanking will also be unavailable
2. Clear browser cache and cookies
3. Try a different browser or device
4. Disable VPN if you're using one
5. Check your internet connection
6. Try accessing during non-peak hours (avoid 1st and last day of month)

## Important Tips

1. **Never share** your password, OTP, CVV, or PIN with anyone — ICICI Bank will never ask for these
2. **Update your mobile number** immediately if you change it — most login issues stem from outdated mobile numbers
3. **Use the official ICICI website** (icicibank.com) — avoid clicking links from SMS or email to prevent phishing
4. **Keep iMobile app updated** — older versions frequently cause errors
5. **Set transaction limits** in NetBanking to protect against unauthorized transactions

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I reset my ICICI NetBanking password?
Go to the [ICICI NetBanking login page](https://infinity.icicibank.com/), click "Forgot Password," enter your User ID, verify with OTP, and set a new password.

### Why is my ICICI NetBanking account locked?
Your account gets locked after multiple incorrect password attempts. Use the "Forgot Password" option or call 1800 1080 to unlock it.

### How do I register for ICICI NetBanking?
Visit [ICICI NetBanking registration](https://infinity.icicibank.com/) and click "First Time User." You need your account number, registered mobile number, and debit card details to register.

### Can I use ICICI NetBanking on multiple devices?
Yes, you can access NetBanking via browser on any device. For iMobile Pay, you can register up to 2 devices at a time.

### What should I do if my ICICI UPI transaction fails?
Wait for auto-reversal (usually within 24-48 hours). If the amount is not refunded, raise a dispute through NetBanking or call 1800 1080.

### Is ICICI NetBanking available 24/7?
Yes, except during scheduled maintenance windows (usually late night on weekends). Fund transfers via NEFT have specific banking hour cutoffs.

### How do I update my mobile number for ICICI NetBanking?
You must visit your nearest ICICI Bank branch with your PAN card, Aadhaar card, and a signed request letter to update your registered mobile number.
