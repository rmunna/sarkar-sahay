# ITR Verification Pending? How to e-Verify Your Return

Filed your Income Tax Return but it still shows "Verification Pending"? Your ITR is **not valid** until it is verified. The Income Tax Department treats an unverified return as **not filed**, which means no processing, no refund, and potential penalties.

You must verify your ITR within **30 days** of filing. This guide explains all 6 methods to e-verify your return and what to do if the deadline has passed.

## Why Is ITR Verification Required?

When you file your ITR online, you need to confirm that it was actually filed by you. Verification is the digital equivalent of signing your return. Without it:

- Your return will **not be processed** by CPC Bengaluru
- Any [refund due will not be issued](/guide/itr-refund-not-received-fix)
- The return is treated as if it was **never filed**
- Late filing penalties under Section 234F may apply

## 30-Day Verification Deadline

- You have **30 days from the date of filing** to verify your ITR
- If you miss the deadline, the return may be treated as filed late (with the date of verification as the filing date)
- Late verification can result in a **late filing fee** of ₹1,000 to ₹5,000
- The IT Department may send a notice asking you to verify

## How to Check Verification Status

1. Log in to [incometax.gov.in](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/)
2. Go to **e-File** → **Income Tax Returns** → **View Filed Returns**
3. Check the status column — it will show:
   - **Successfully e-Verified** — no action needed
   - **Pending for e-Verification** — verify immediately
   - **ITR-V received by CPC** — physical verification received

## 6 Methods to e-Verify Your ITR

### Method 1: Aadhaar OTP (Most Popular)

1. Log in to the e-Filing portal
2. Go to **e-File** → **Income Tax Returns** → **e-Verify Return**
3. Select the return you want to verify
4. Choose **I would like to verify using OTP on mobile number registered with Aadhaar**
5. Click **Generate Aadhaar OTP**
6. Enter the 6-digit OTP received on your Aadhaar-linked mobile number
7. Click **Submit** — your return is now verified

> **Requirement:** Your PAN must be linked with [Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online), and your mobile number must be updated in Aadhaar.

### Method 2: Net Banking

1. Log in to your **bank's net banking portal** (major banks like SBI, HDFC, ICICI, PNB support this)
2. Look for the **Tax Filing** or **e-Verify** option (usually under Tax Services)
3. You will be redirected to the Income Tax e-Filing portal
4. The portal will automatically identify your pending return
5. Click **e-Verify** to complete the process

> **Tip:** This method works even if your Aadhaar is not linked with PAN.

### Method 3: Bank Account EVC

1. Log in to the e-Filing portal
2. Go to **e-Verify Return**
3. Select **I would like to verify through Electronic Verification Code (EVC) generated through my bank account**
4. Your pre-validated bank account's registered mobile and email will receive the EVC
5. Enter the EVC on the portal
6. Click **Submit**

> **Requirement:** Your bank account must be [pre-validated](/guide/itr-refund-not-received-fix) on the e-Filing portal.

### Method 4: Demat Account EVC

1. Log in to the e-Filing portal
2. Go to **e-Verify Return**
3. Select **EVC through Demat Account**
4. The EVC will be sent to the mobile number and email registered with your depository (NSDL/CDSL)
5. Enter the EVC and submit

> **Requirement:** Your Demat account must be pre-validated on the e-Filing portal.

### Method 5: ATM (Offline EVC)

1. Visit an ATM of your bank (supported banks include SBI, Canara Bank, IDBI, and others)
2. Insert your **debit card** and enter your PIN
3. Select **Generate EVC for Income Tax Filing** option
4. An EVC will be sent to your registered mobile number and email
5. Log in to the e-Filing portal and use this EVC to verify your return
6. The EVC is valid for **72 hours**

> **Note:** Not all banks support ATM-based EVC. Check with your bank.

### Method 6: Send Signed ITR-V by Post (Physical Verification)

If none of the electronic methods work, you can send a physical copy:

1. After filing, download the **ITR-V** (Acknowledgment) from the e-Filing portal
2. Go to **e-File** → **Income Tax Returns** → **View Filed Returns** → **Download ITR-V**
3. **Print the ITR-V** — do NOT fold it
4. **Sign it in blue ink** within the designated box
5. Send it by **ordinary post or speed post** to:

   > **CPC, Income Tax Department,**
   > **Centralized Processing Centre,**
   > **Bengaluru – 560500, Karnataka**

6. The ITR-V must reach CPC within **30 days of filing**
7. You will receive an email confirmation once CPC receives and processes the ITR-V

> **Important:** Do not send by courier or registered post — only ordinary or speed post is accepted.

## What If You Missed the 30-Day Deadline?

1. You can still e-verify your return after 30 days
2. However, the **date of e-verification** may be treated as the **date of filing**
3. This means your return could be considered **late filed**
4. Late filing penalties under Section 234F may apply:
   - ₹1,000 if total income is up to ₹5 lakh
   - ₹5,000 if total income exceeds ₹5 lakh
5. You may also lose the benefit of **carrying forward losses**
6. **Verify as soon as possible** — do not delay further

## Important Tips

1. **Verify immediately** after filing — don't wait for the deadline
2. **Aadhaar OTP is the fastest** method — takes less than 2 minutes
3. **Keep your mobile number updated** in Aadhaar and bank records for OTP/EVC
4. **Pre-validate your bank account** on the portal before filing season
5. **Save a copy of ITR-V** even after e-verification for your records

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: What happens if I don't verify my ITR?
Your return will be treated as not filed. You will not receive any refund, and you may face penalties for non-filing. The IT Department may also send a notice.

### Q2: Can I e-verify an old ITR that was never verified?
Yes, you can e-verify returns from previous years. However, the return will be treated as filed on the date of verification, which means late filing rules apply.

### Q3: I'm not receiving the Aadhaar OTP. What should I do?
Ensure your mobile number is updated in Aadhaar. If the number is old, visit an Aadhaar centre to update it. Alternatively, use net banking or bank account EVC method.

### Q4: How do I know if CPC received my physical ITR-V?
You will receive a confirmation email from CPC once they process the ITR-V. You can also check the status on the e-Filing portal under **View Filed Returns**.

### Q5: Can I e-verify after sending ITR-V by post?
Yes, you can e-verify even after sending the physical ITR-V. The electronic verification will override the physical process if it is completed first.

### Q6: Is e-verification mandatory for all ITR forms?
Yes, e-verification (or physical ITR-V submission) is mandatory for all ITR forms — ITR-1 through ITR-7.
