# Personal Loan EMI Calculator — How to Calculate & Best Rates (2026)

A personal loan EMI (Equated Monthly Instalment) depends on three things: **loan amount, interest rate, and tenure**. This guide gives you the EMI formula, ready-reference EMI tables, and the best personal loan rates in India 2026.

---

## Personal Loan EMI Formula

```
EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n / [(1+r)^n - 1]
```

Where:
- **P** = Principal loan amount
- **r** = Monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100)
- **n** = Tenure in months

**Example:** ₹5 lakh loan at 12% for 3 years (36 months)
- r = 12 / 12 / 100 = 0.01
- EMI = 5,00,000 × 0.01 × (1.01)^36 / [(1.01)^36 - 1]
- **EMI = ₹16,607/month**
- Total payment = ₹16,607 × 36 = ₹5,97,852
- Total interest paid = ₹97,852

## How to Reduce Personal Loan Interest Burden

### 1. Prepayment

Most banks allow partial prepayment after 1 EMI (some after 6 EMIs). Even paying 1 extra EMI per year can reduce tenure by 6–12 months.

**Prepayment penalty:** 0%–5% on prepaid amount (check your loan terms).

### 2. Balance Transfer

If you're paying 18%+ interest, transfer to a bank offering 11%:

For ₹5 lakh balance at 18% → 3 years remaining → EMI = ₹18,067
Transfer to 11%: EMI = ₹16,389
**Saving: ₹1,678/month = ₹60,408 over 3 years**

Balance transfer cost: Processing fee (1%–2%) + pre-closure penalty. Calculate net savings before transferring.

### 3. Negotiate Rate Reduction

If your CIBIL score improved since taking the loan or you got a salary hike:
- Write to bank requesting rate reduction
- Mention your improved score / better profile
- Many banks revise rates on request for good customers

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I calculate personal loan EMI without formula?**
Use any bank's online EMI calculator (available on HDFC, ICICI, Axis website). Enter: loan amount, rate, tenure → EMI displayed instantly.

**My personal loan EMI increased without notice — is that legal?**
If you have a floating rate personal loan (rare), the rate can change. Most personal loans are fixed rate — EMI is fixed. If bank changed your EMI without notice, file a complaint at [cms.rbi.org.in](https://cms.rbi.org.in).

**Can I get a personal loan with 600 CIBIL score?**
Very difficult with traditional banks. Some NBFCs lend to 600+ but at 25%–35% interest. Better to improve score first (6 months) and then apply.

**What's better — bank personal loan or NBFC personal loan?**
Banks typically offer lower rates but stricter eligibility. NBFCs approve faster with more flexible eligibility but higher rates. For urgent needs: NBFC. For planned expenses: Bank.

**Is there any tax benefit on personal loan?**
No tax deduction on personal loans (unlike home loans). Exception: If personal loan was used for home construction/purchase, you can claim interest under Section 24.
