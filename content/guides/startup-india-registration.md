# Startup India Registration 2025 – Complete Guide

## What is Startup India?

Startup India is a flagship initiative launched by the Government of India on 16th January 2016 to build a strong ecosystem for nurturing innovation and startups in the country. The scheme offers DPIIT (Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade) recognition, which unlocks tax benefits, easier compliance, funding access, and intellectual property support.

Any entity incorporated as a private limited company, partnership firm, or LLP that is innovative, scalable, and less than 10 years old can apply for recognition under Startup India.

## Key Benefits of Startup India Recognition

- **Tax exemption** — Eligible startups can get income tax exemption for 3 consecutive years out of the first 10 years (Section 80-IAC)
- **Angel tax exemption** — Investments from angel investors are exempt from tax under Section 56(2)(viib)
- **Self-certification** — Simplified compliance under 6 labour laws and 3 environmental laws
- **Fast-track patent filing** — 80% rebate on patent filing fees
- **Easy winding up** — Company can be wound up within 90 days under Insolvency & Bankruptcy Code
- **Fund of Funds** — Access to ₹10,000 crore government fund through SIDBI
- **Government e-Marketplace** — Exemption from prior experience/turnover criteria for government tenders on GeM

## Who is Eligible?

To get DPIIT recognition as a startup, your entity must meet ALL of the following:

- **Incorporated as** a Private Limited Company, Registered Partnership Firm, or Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)
- **Age:** Not more than **10 years** from the date of incorporation
- **Turnover:** Annual turnover has not exceeded **₹100 crore** in any financial year since incorporation
- **Innovation:** The entity is working towards innovation, development, or improvement of products/processes/services OR has a scalable business model with high potential for employment generation or wealth creation
- **Not formed** by splitting up or reconstruction of an existing business

### Who Cannot Apply?
- Sole proprietorships (must be a registered entity)
- Companies older than 10 years
- Entities formed by splitting/restructuring existing businesses

## Documents Required

### Entity Documents
- Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
- PAN of the entity
- **Udyam Registration** (recommended but not mandatory)

### Founder Documents
- Aadhaar card of authorized representative (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- PAN card of founders
- Mobile number and email ID

### Business Documents
- Brief description of the innovation / business idea
- Website URL or pitch deck (optional but recommended)
- Awards/recognition/patents (if any)
- **No business plan or financial statements required** for basic DPIIT recognition

## Step-by-Step Registration Process

### Online Registration (Only Method)

1. **Visit** the Startup India portal at [startupindia.gov.in](https://www.startupindia.gov.in)
2. **Click** on "Register" and create an account using your email/mobile
3. **Verify** your email and mobile with OTP
4. **Login** and click on "Get Recognized as a Startup"
5. **Fill** the recognition application form:
   - Entity details (name, type, incorporation date, CIN/LLPIN)
   - Authorized representative details
   - Business activity description
   - Innovation description (how your product/service is unique)
6. **Upload** Certificate of Incorporation
7. **Upload** supporting documents (pitch deck, awards, recommendation letters — optional)
8. **Self-certify** that you meet eligibility criteria
9. **Submit** the application
10. **Receive** DPIIT recognition certificate (typically within 2-5 working days)

### After Recognition — Apply for Tax Exemption (Optional)

1. **Login** to Startup India portal
2. **Navigate** to "Apply for Tax Exemption" under the 80-IAC section
3. **Submit** application to the Inter-Ministerial Board (IMB)
4. **IMB reviews** your application (may request additional documents)
5. Upon approval, you get **3-year income tax exemption** certificate

- **Angel Tax Exemption:** 15-30 working days after recognition

## Startup India Fund of Funds

The government has set up a **₹10,000 crore Fund of Funds for Startups (FFS)** managed by SIDBI. Key points:

- Government doesn't invest directly — funds are routed through SEBI-registered Alternative Investment Funds (AIFs)
- Over 130 AIFs empaneled as of 2024
- Startups with DPIIT recognition are eligible
- Apply through your chosen AIF or investor network

## Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS)

For early-stage startups needing initial capital:

- **Grant up to ₹20 lakh** for proof of concept, prototype, or product trials
- **Investment up to ₹50 lakh** for market entry, commercialization
- Apply through selected incubators across India
- Must have DPIIT recognition and be within 2 years of incorporation

## Important Tips

1. **Register your entity first** — You need a valid CIN (Company) or LLPIN (LLP) before applying for DPIIT recognition. Sole proprietorships are not eligible.
2. **Focus on the innovation description** — This is the most critical part of the application. Clearly explain what makes your product/service innovative and scalable.
3. **Tax exemption is separate** — DPIIT recognition alone doesn't give tax benefits. You must separately apply for 80-IAC exemption through the IMB.
4. **Keep your startup profile updated** — Regularly update your profile on the Startup India portal with milestones, funding raised, and revenue growth.
5. **Leverage the ecosystem** — Beyond funding, use Startup India's mentorship programs, learning modules, and networking events through the portal.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Is Startup India registration free?
**Yes.** Both DPIIT recognition and tax exemption applications are completely free. There are no government fees involved.

### Q2: Can a sole proprietorship register under Startup India?
**No.** Only Private Limited Companies, Registered Partnership Firms, and LLPs are eligible. You need to first incorporate your business as one of these entities.

### Q3: What is the difference between DPIIT recognition and tax exemption?
**DPIIT recognition** is the basic certification that identifies your entity as a startup — it gives access to self-certification, patent rebates, and government tenders. **Tax exemption (80-IAC)** is an additional approval from the Inter-Ministerial Board that gives 3-year income tax exemption.

### Q4: Can I get Startup India recognition if my company is 8 years old?
**Yes**, as long as your company is within 10 years of incorporation and meets all other eligibility criteria (turnover under ₹100 crore, innovative business).

### Q5: Does DPIIT recognition guarantee funding?
**No.** Recognition makes you eligible for government-backed funds like Fund of Funds and Seed Fund, but funding decisions are made by individual AIFs and incubators based on your business viability.

### Q6: Can NRIs register a startup under Startup India?
**Yes**, if they are founders/directors of an Indian-incorporated entity (Private Limited Company or LLP registered in India). The entity must be based in India.

### Q7: How do I apply for the patent fast-track facility?
After getting DPIIT recognition, file your patent application at [ipindia.gov.in](https://ipindia.gov.in) and select the "Startup" category. You'll get 80% rebate on filing fees and expedited examination.
