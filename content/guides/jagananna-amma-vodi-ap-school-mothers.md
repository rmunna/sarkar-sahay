## What is Jagananna Amma Vodi?

**Jagananna Amma Vodi** (meaning "Jagan's gift for mothers") is an Andhra Pradesh government scheme that gives **₹15,000 per year** to mothers (and eligible guardians) who send their children to government schools. The money is given as **financial support** to incentivize continued school attendance and prevent dropouts.

The scheme is implemented through the school system — enrollment happens through the child's government school.

---

## Key Benefits

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Annual assistance | ₹15,000 per year per enrolled child |
| Who receives | Mother (or eligible guardian) of the child |
| Payment | DBT to mother's Aadhaar-linked bank account |
| Payment timing | January each year (beginning of school year) |
| Classes covered | Class 1 to 12 (government and government-aided schools) |
| Income limit | BPL families and white/pink ration card holders |

---

## Who is Eligible?

**The mother/guardian is eligible if:**
- Her child is enrolled in a **government or government-aided school**
- The family holds a **white or pink ration card** (below poverty line card)
- The child is studying in **Classes 1 to 12**
- The mother is a **Andhra Pradesh resident**

> Even if the father is not present (widow/single mother), the eligible guardian can receive the benefit. In absence of mother, the primary guardian gets the payment.

---

## How to Get Enrolled

### Step 1 — Child Must Be in Government School

Ensure your child is enrolled in a **government or government-aided school** in Andhra Pradesh.

### Step 2 — School Collects Data

The school collects mother's details:
- Mother's Aadhaar number
- Bank account (Aadhaar-linked)
- Ration card type (white/pink)

The school submits this data to the AP Education Department.

### Step 3 — Verification

- Revenue and Education departments cross-verify eligibility (ration card, school enrollment)
- Beneficiary list is published at the school notice board

### Step 4 — Annual Payment (January)

On a designated date in January, ₹15,000 is credited directly to the **mother's Aadhaar-linked bank account**.

### Step 5 — Subsequent Years

The same process repeats each year — school confirms the child is still enrolled and in good attendance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. My child goes to a private school. Can I get Amma Vodi?**

No. Amma Vodi is only for children in **government or government-aided schools**. Private unaided schools are not covered.

**2. I have 2 children in government school. Do I get ₹30,000?**

Yes — ₹15,000 per enrolled child. For 2 children in government school, the mother receives ₹30,000 per year.

**3. I am the grandmother raising my grandchild. Can I receive Amma Vodi?**

If the mother is deceased or absent and you are the primary guardian, you may be eligible as the guardian. Approach the school to verify and update guardian details.

**4. My child transferred from a private school to government school this year. Can I get Amma Vodi this year?**

Yes, from the year the child joins the government school. The school will collect your details during the Amma Vodi enrollment process.

**5. The money was not credited even though I'm on the list. What do I do?**

Visit the school and ask the HM to check your application status. Also verify at [navasakam.ap.gov.in](https://navasakam.ap.gov.in) with your Aadhaar number. Common issue: Aadhaar not seeded to bank account.
