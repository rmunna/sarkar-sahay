# e-EPIC Download Failed? How to Fix Digital Voter ID Download Issues

The e-EPIC (Electronic Electoral Photo Identity Card) is the digital version of your voter ID that you can carry on your phone. But downloading it from the NVSP portal or Voter Helpline app often fails. Here's how to fix every common issue.

## Common Error Messages

- **"e-EPIC download is not available for your EPIC number."**
- **"Your details could not be verified. Please check and try again."**
- **"e-EPIC is not generated yet. Please try after some time."**
- **"OTP not received on registered mobile number."**
- **"Service temporarily unavailable. Please try later."**
- **"EPIC number not found in the database."**
- **"Face authentication failed."**
- **"You are not eligible for e-EPIC download."**
- **"PDF generation failed. Please retry."**

## Why e-EPIC Download Fails

### 1. Mobile Number Not Registered with Voter ID
e-EPIC download requires OTP verification on the mobile number linked to your voter registration. If no mobile was provided or it's outdated, download fails.

### 2. e-EPIC Not Yet Generated
If you recently registered as a voter or your voter ID was recently issued, the e-EPIC may not be generated yet. It takes **7-15 days** after your physical voter ID is ready.

### 3. Voter ID Still Under Processing
If your Form 6 application is still being processed or your name hasn't been added to the electoral roll, e-EPIC won't be available.

### 4. Old Voter IDs Without Photo
Pre-2010 voter IDs without digital photos are not eligible for e-EPIC.

### 5. Server Overload
During election season, the portal experiences massive traffic spikes.

## Step-by-Step Fix

### Method 1: Download via Voter Portal (Recommended)

1. Visit [voterportal.eci.gov.in](https://voterportal.eci.gov.in)
2. Click **"Login/Register"**
3. Register with your **mobile number** and verify OTP
4. After login, click **"Download e-EPIC"** from the dashboard
5. Enter your **EPIC number** (printed on your physical voter ID, starts with 3 letters)
6. Verify with OTP or **face authentication**
7. Your e-EPIC PDF will download — save it on your phone

### Method 2: Download via Voter Helpline App

1. Download **"Voter Helpline"** app from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.eci.citizen) or App Store
2. Register/login with your mobile number
3. Go to **"e-EPIC Download"** section
4. Enter your EPIC number
5. Complete OTP or face verification
6. Download the PDF

### Method 3: Download via NVSP Portal

1. Visit [www.nvsp.in](https://www.nvsp.in)
2. Click **"e-EPIC Download"**
3. Enter your **EPIC number** or **Form Reference Number**
4. Enter registered mobile number
5. Verify OTP
6. Download the e-EPIC

## Fixing Specific Errors

### "e-EPIC Not Available for Your EPIC Number"

This usually means your e-EPIC hasn't been generated yet:
1. **Check your voter registration status** at [voterportal.eci.gov.in](https://voterportal.eci.gov.in) — search by name or EPIC number
2. If registered but e-EPIC unavailable, **wait 7-15 days** — it's being generated
3. If your voter ID is very old (pre-2010), visit your local **BLO (Booth Level Officer)** to get a new photo-based voter ID issued

### "Details Could Not Be Verified"

1. Enter your EPIC number **exactly as printed** on your voter ID (including letters)
2. Ensure your name and DOB match the electoral roll
3. **Check your details** on the electoral roll at [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in)
4. If details don't match, file **Form 8** for correction at [voterportal.eci.gov.in](https://voterportal.eci.gov.in)

### "OTP Not Received"

1. Ensure the mobile number is the one **registered with your voter ID**
2. If you didn't provide a mobile number during registration, you need to **update it first**:
   - Visit [voterportal.eci.gov.in](https://voterportal.eci.gov.in) > **"Link your EPIC with Mobile"**
   - Or contact your BLO to update your mobile number
3. Disable DND on your mobile
4. Wait 5-10 minutes before requesting again

### "Face Authentication Failed"

1. Ensure **good lighting** — face clearly visible
2. Remove glasses, mask, or anything covering your face
3. Hold the phone at **eye level** and look directly at the camera
4. Your current appearance should reasonably match your voter ID photo
5. If face auth keeps failing, use **OTP method** instead

### "EPIC Number Not Found"

1. Verify your EPIC number from your **physical voter ID card**
2. Search the **electoral roll** at [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in) using your name and constituency
3. If not found, your registration may be pending — check application status with your Form Reference Number
4. You may need to **re-apply** using Form 6

## How to Find Your EPIC Number

If you don't have your physical voter ID:
1. Go to [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in)
2. Click **"Search in Electoral Roll"**
3. Search by **Name** — enter your name, father's name, age, state, and constituency
4. Your EPIC number will be displayed in the search results
5. Alternatively, search by **Form Reference Number** if you recently applied

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is e-EPIC valid as ID proof for voting?
Yes, **e-EPIC is a valid identity document** for voting at polling booths. You can show it on your phone. It has a secure QR code that officials can scan for verification.

### Can I download e-EPIC without my physical voter ID card?
Yes, but you need to know your **EPIC number**. You can find it by searching the electoral roll at [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in) using your name and other details.

### How is e-EPIC different from the physical voter ID?
e-EPIC is a **PDF version** of your voter ID stored digitally on your phone. It contains a secure QR code and is legally valid just like the physical card. It's portable and can't be lost.

### Can I use e-EPIC as address proof?
Yes, e-EPIC can be used as **address and identity proof** for many purposes including bank account opening, SIM card purchase, and other KYC requirements, just like a physical voter ID.

### My voter ID has wrong details. Can I still download e-EPIC?
The e-EPIC will show the **same details** as your electoral roll entry. If there are errors, first correct them by filing **Form 8** (Correction in Electoral Roll) through the Voter Portal, then download the updated e-EPIC.

### Is there a fee for downloading e-EPIC?
No, e-EPIC download is **completely free**. You can download it unlimited times from the Voter Portal or Voter Helpline app.
