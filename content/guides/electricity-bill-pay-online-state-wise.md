# How to Pay Electricity Bill Online — State-Wise Guide for All Discoms

Paying your electricity bill online saves time, avoids queues, and gives you an instant receipt. Every major DISCOM (Distribution Company) in India now offers online payment through their official portal, and you can also pay via UPI apps like Paytm, PhonePe, and Google Pay.

This guide covers **state-wise DISCOM portals**, step-by-step payment instructions, how to read your meter, understand bill components, and file complaints — all in one place.

## Step-by-Step: Pay Electricity Bill on BSES Delhi

1. Visit [bsesdelhi.com](https://www.bsesdelhi.com) and click **"Pay Bill"** or **"Quick Pay"**.
2. Enter your **CA Number** (Consumer Account Number) — printed on your bill.
3. Your outstanding amount and bill details will appear on screen.
4. Choose a payment method: **credit/debit card, net banking, UPI, or wallet**.
5. Complete the payment and **download your receipt**.

Your CA Number is a 9-digit code found at the top of your BSES bill. Keep it saved for future payments.

## Step-by-Step: Pay Electricity Bill on MSEDCL Maharashtra

1. Go to [mahadiscom.in](https://www.mahadiscom.in) and click **"Online Bill Payment"**.
2. Enter your **Consumer Number** (12-digit number on your bill).
3. Review the bill amount, due date, and any arrears.
4. Select payment mode: **UPI, debit/credit card, net banking, or NEFT**.
5. Confirm and pay. A receipt is generated instantly — save or print it.

MSEDCL also has a mobile app called **"MSEDCL Mobile"** available on Android and iOS for quick bill view and payment.

## Step-by-Step: Pay Electricity Bill on BESCOM Karnataka

1. Visit [bescom.karnataka.gov.in](https://bescom.karnataka.gov.in) and select **"Online Payment"**.
2. Enter your **RR Number** (10-digit account number from your BESCOM bill).
3. The system displays your current bill amount and due date.
4. Choose your payment option: **card, net banking, or UPI**.
5. Complete payment and save the transaction receipt.

BESCOM also supports bill payment through the **BESCOM Mithra** mobile app.

## Pay Electricity Bill via Paytm, PhonePe, or Google Pay

You don't need to visit individual DISCOM websites. All major UPI apps support electricity bill payment for most Indian DISCOMs.

### Using Paytm

1. Open Paytm → tap **"Electricity"** under Recharge & Pay Bills.
2. Select your **state and DISCOM** from the dropdown.
3. Enter your **consumer/CA/RR number**.
4. Review the bill amount and tap **"Pay"**.

### Using PhonePe

1. Open PhonePe → go to **"Electricity"** in the Bills section.
2. Choose your DISCOM (e.g., BSES Rajdhani, MSEDCL, TANGEDCO).
3. Enter your consumer number and fetch the bill.
4. Pay using UPI, wallet, or linked bank account.

### Using Google Pay

1. Open Google Pay → tap **"Electricity"** under Bill Payments.
2. Search for your DISCOM by name.
3. Enter your consumer number and view the bill.
4. Pay directly from your linked bank account via UPI.

> **Advantages of UPI payment:** Instant confirmation, automatic bill fetch, payment history saved, and cashback offers on select apps.

## How to Read Your Electricity Meter

Understanding your meter helps you verify bill accuracy:

- **Digital meter:** Displays total units (kWh) consumed on an LCD screen. Note the reading and subtract last month's reading to get current consumption.
- **Smart meter:** Automatically transmits readings to the DISCOM. You can check real-time usage via your DISCOM's app or portal.
- **Old analog meter:** Read the dials from left to right. Each dial alternates between clockwise and counter-clockwise rotation. Note the lower number when the pointer is between two digits.

Record your meter reading on the billing date each month to cross-check with the DISCOM bill.

## Understanding Your Electricity Bill Components

Every electricity bill has these key components:

| Component | What It Means |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Energy Charges** | Cost per unit (kWh) of electricity consumed. Rates increase in slabs — higher usage means higher per-unit cost. |
| **Fixed/Demand Charges** | A flat monthly charge based on your sanctioned load (in kW), regardless of consumption. |
| **Fuel Adjustment Charge (FAC)** | Variable surcharge to cover fluctuating fuel costs for power generation. |
| **Electricity Duty** | State government tax on electricity consumption, usually 5-10% of energy charges. |
| **Meter Rent** | Small monthly fee for the meter installed at your premises. |
| **Arrears** | Any unpaid amount carried forward from previous bills. |
| **Late Payment Surcharge** | Penalty for paying after the due date, typically 1.5-2% per month. |
| **Subsidy/Rebate** | Government subsidies (if applicable) that reduce your payable amount. |

> **Pro tip:** If your bill seems unusually high, compare the "units consumed" figure with your own meter reading. A mismatch could indicate a faulty meter or estimated billing.

## How to Register a Complaint for Electricity Issues

If you face billing errors, power outages, or meter problems, you can file a complaint online:

### Through Your DISCOM Portal

1. Visit your DISCOM's website and navigate to the **"Grievance"** or **"Complaint"** section.
2. Log in or register with your consumer number.
3. Select the complaint type: **billing dispute, meter fault, power outage, voltage issue**, etc.
4. Describe the problem, upload supporting documents if needed, and submit.
5. Note down the **complaint/docket number** for tracking.

### Through Government Portals

- **Integrated Power Development Scheme (IPDS):** Most states have a centralized grievance portal.
- **CGRF (Consumer Grievance Redressal Forum):** For unresolved complaints, escalate to your state's CGRF.
- **National Consumer Helpline:** Call **1800-11-4000** (toll-free) or visit [consumerhelpline.gov.in](https://consumerhelpline.gov.in).

## Tips for Managing Your Electricity Bills

- **Set up auto-pay:** Most DISCOMs and UPI apps let you enable automatic bill payment on the due date.
- **Go paperless:** Register your email and mobile on the DISCOM portal to get e-bills.
- **Track consumption:** Use your DISCOM's app to monitor monthly usage trends and avoid bill shocks.
- **Pay before the due date:** Avoid late payment surcharges that add up over time.
- **Check for subsidies:** Many states offer subsidies for consumers below a certain usage threshold — verify eligibility on your DISCOM portal.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What details do I need to pay my electricity bill online?

You need your **consumer number** (also called CA Number, RR Number, or Account ID depending on your DISCOM). This is printed on your physical bill. Some portals also accept your registered mobile number for bill lookup.

### Can I pay electricity bills for any state using Paytm or PhonePe?

Yes, Paytm, PhonePe, and Google Pay support bill payment for most major DISCOMs across all Indian states. Simply search for your DISCOM within the app and enter your consumer number.

### What happens if I miss the electricity bill due date?

A **late payment surcharge** (typically 1.5-2% per month on the outstanding amount) is added to your next bill. Prolonged non-payment can lead to disconnection of supply. You can still pay overdue bills online — the portal will show the total amount including arrears and surcharges.

### How do I check my electricity bill online without paying?

Visit your DISCOM's website, enter your consumer number in the **"View Bill"** or **"Bill Details"** section, and your current and past bills will be displayed. Most UPI apps also let you fetch and view the bill without completing payment.

### Is online electricity bill payment safe?

Yes. All DISCOM portals and major UPI apps use **SSL encryption and RBI-compliant payment gateways**. Always pay through official websites or verified apps. Avoid clicking on bill payment links from unknown SMS or email sources.

### Can I get a receipt after paying my electricity bill online?

Yes. Every online payment generates a digital receipt that you can download or screenshot. DISCOM portals also maintain a payment history section where you can access past receipts anytime.
