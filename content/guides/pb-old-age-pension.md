## What is Punjab Old Age Pension Scheme?

The Punjab Old Age Pension Scheme provides **monthly financial assistance** to senior citizens who are destitute or from economically weaker families. The scheme operates in convergence with the central **Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS)** and adds a state top-up.

### Pension Amount

| Age Group | Monthly Pension |
|-----------|----------------|
| 58-79 years | ₹750 – ₹1,000 |
| 80+ years | ₹1,000 – ₹1,500 |

> Amounts include both central and state contributions and may vary based on latest revisions.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Age:** 58 years or above
- Must be a **resident of Punjab**
- Annual income must be below the **BPL threshold** or as specified
- Must not be receiving any other government pension
- Must not be an income tax payer
- Must have a bank account in Punjab

## Documents Required

- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
- Age proof (birth certificate, voter ID, school records)
- Income certificate or BPL card
- Residence proof of Punjab
- Bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked)
- Passport-size photographs (2)
- Self-declaration of not receiving other pension

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Online Application

1. **Visit** Punjab Social Security portal or [Sewa Kendra portal](https://connect.punjab.gov.in)
2. **Register** with mobile number
3. **Select** Old Age Pension
4. **Fill** application with personal, age, income, and bank details
5. **Upload** documents
6. **Submit** and note application number
7. **Track** status online

### Through Sewa Kendra

1. **Visit** the nearest Sewa Kendra (Punjab's citizen service centre)
2. **Carry** all original documents
3. **Operator** fills and submits the application
4. **Collect** receipt

### Offline Application

1. Visit the **SDM office** or **District Social Security Office**
2. Collect application form
3. Fill and attach documents
4. Submit and collect acknowledgement

## Processing Time

- Verification: **1-2 months**
- Approval: **2-3 months**
- Monthly pension via DBT after approval

## Fees

**No fees** for application. Sewa Kendra may charge a nominal service fee.

## Important Tips

1. **Submit life certificate annually** — Use [Jeevan Pramaan](https://jeevanpramaan.gov.in) for digital submission
2. **Keep bank account active** — Pension is via DBT; inactive accounts delay payment
3. **Apply through Sewa Kendra** — Easiest option for senior citizens who are not tech-savvy
4. **Update Aadhaar** — Ensure Aadhaar address matches Punjab residence
5. **Check for health coverage** — Register for [Bhagat Puran Singh Sehat Bima](/guide/pb-bhagat-puran-singh-sehat-bima) for medical coverage

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. What is the minimum age for old age pension in Punjab?
**58 years** for both men and women.

### Q2. Can I get both old age and widow pension?
No. Only **one** social security pension is allowed at a time.

### Q3. How do I submit life certificate?
Visit your bank branch with Aadhaar, or use [Jeevan Pramaan](https://jeevanpramaan.gov.in) for biometric digital submission.

### Q4. Is the pension amount the same for all?
No. Pension increases for those **80 years and above**. Amounts also depend on central + state contribution.

### Q5. What if my pension stops?
Common reasons: failure to submit life certificate, bank account closure, or changes in eligibility. Contact the District Social Security Office.

### Q6. Can NRIs from Punjab apply?
No. You must be a **current resident** of Punjab.
