## What is Subhadra Yojana?

Subhadra Yojana is a flagship women empowerment scheme launched by the Government of Odisha. It provides direct financial assistance to eligible women across the state. The scheme aims to promote economic independence and social security for women in Odisha.

For a complete overview of the scheme including eligibility and how to apply, see our [Odisha Subhadra Yojana Women Scheme guide](/guide/odisha-subhadra-yojana-women-scheme).

## How to Check Subhadra Yojana Status Online

Follow these steps to check your application and payment status:

### Step 1: Visit the Official Portal

Go to the official Subhadra Yojana portal: [https://subhadra.odisha.gov.in](https://subhadra.odisha.gov.in)

### Step 2: Click on "Check Status" or "Beneficiary Status"

On the homepage, look for the **"Check Status"** or **"Beneficiary Status"** option and click on it.

### Step 3: Enter Your Details

Enter one of the following identifiers:
- **Aadhaar Number** (12 digits)
- **Registered Mobile Number**
- **Application Reference Number** (if available)

### Step 4: Verify with OTP

An OTP will be sent to your registered mobile number. Enter the OTP to proceed.

### Step 5: View Your Status

Your application status and payment details will be displayed, including:
- **Application Status** — Submitted, Under Review, Approved, or Rejected
- **Payment Status** — Pending, Processed, or Credited
- **Payment Date** and **Amount** (if disbursed)
- **Bank Account Details** (masked) linked to your application

## How to Check Payment Status via Bank

You can also verify if the payment has been credited:

1. **Check your bank passbook** — Visit your bank and get the passbook updated
2. **Net banking or mobile banking** — Log in and check recent credit transactions from government sources
3. **Call your bank's toll-free number** — Ask about recent DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer) credits
4. **Check via PFMS** — Visit [https://pfms.nic.in](https://pfms.nic.in) and use "Know Your Payment" to track DBT payments

## Common Errors and Fixes

### "Aadhaar Number Not Found"

- **Cause:** Your Aadhaar may not be linked to the scheme or was entered incorrectly
- **Fix:** Double-check your Aadhaar number. If correct, visit your nearest Anganwadi centre or block office to verify your registration

### "Mobile Number Mismatch"

- **Cause:** The mobile number on the portal doesn't match your Aadhaar-linked number
- **Fix:** Update your mobile number in Aadhaar at the nearest Aadhaar Seva Kendra, then re-register

### "Payment Status Shows Pending"

- **Cause:** Payment processing can take 7–15 working days after approval
- **Fix:** Wait for the payment cycle. If pending for more than 30 days, contact the helpline or visit the block office

### "Application Rejected"

- **Cause:** Incomplete documents, ineligible criteria, or duplicate application
- **Fix:** Check the rejection reason on the portal. Correct the issue and re-apply through your Anganwadi worker or block office

### "OTP Not Received"

- **Cause:** Network issues or incorrect mobile number linked
- **Fix:** Ensure your mobile has network coverage. Wait 2–3 minutes and click "Resend OTP." If the number is wrong, update it at the block office

### "Website Not Loading or Server Error"

- **Cause:** Heavy traffic on the portal, especially during payment dates
- **Fix:** Try during off-peak hours (early morning or late night). Clear browser cache or try a different browser

## Important Tips

1. **Keep your Aadhaar linked to your bank account** — Payment is sent via DBT to your Aadhaar-linked bank account
2. **Ensure your bank account is active** — Dormant accounts will cause payment failures
3. **Save your application reference number** — It helps in tracking and resolving issues faster
4. **Check status regularly** — Especially after applying or during announced payment cycles
5. **Contact the helpline for persistent issues** — Call the Odisha government helpline for scheme-related queries

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: How long does it take for Subhadra Yojana payment to be credited?

Payments are typically credited within 7–15 working days after approval. During peak periods, it may take up to 30 days.

### Q2: Can I check Subhadra Yojana status without Aadhaar?

Yes, you can check using your registered mobile number or application reference number on the official portal.

### Q3: What should I do if my payment is credited to the wrong account?

Contact the block office immediately with your Aadhaar card and bank passbook. You may need to update your Aadhaar-bank linking.

### Q4: Is there a helpline for Subhadra Yojana?

Yes, you can contact the Odisha government's integrated helpline or visit your nearest block/district office for assistance.

### Q5: Can I apply for Subhadra Yojana online?

Registration is primarily done through Anganwadi centres and block offices. Check our [Subhadra Yojana complete guide](/guide/odisha-subhadra-yojana-women-scheme) for application details.

### Q6: Will I get an SMS when payment is credited?

Yes, if your mobile number is linked to your bank account, you will receive an SMS notification when the payment is credited.

### Q7: Can men apply for Subhadra Yojana?

No, Subhadra Yojana is exclusively for eligible women residents of Odisha.
