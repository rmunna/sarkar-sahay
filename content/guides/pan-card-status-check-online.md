## How to Check PAN Card Application Status

Whether you've applied for a **new PAN card, correction, reprint, or Aadhaar-PAN linking**, you can track the status online. The tracking method depends on which portal you used to apply — **NSDL (Protean)** or **UTIITSL**.

## Method 1: Track PAN Status on NSDL (Protean)

### Using Acknowledgement Number

1. Visit [NSDL PAN Status Track](https://tin.tin.nsdl.com/pantan/StatusTrack.html)
2. Select **"PAN - New/Change Request"**
3. Enter your **15-digit acknowledgement number** (starts with a letter, e.g., N12345678901234)
4. Enter the **captcha code**
5. Click **"Submit"**

### Status Messages You May See

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Request received | Application submitted, not yet processed |
| Under process | Documents being verified |
| PAN number allotted | PAN generated — number will be displayed |
| Card dispatched | Physical card sent via Speed Post |
| Returned by post | Card could not be delivered — address issue |
| Request rejected | Documents insufficient or application has errors |

### Using PAN Number (For Existing PAN)

1. Visit [NSDL portal](https://www.onlineservices.nsdl.com/paam/endUserRegisterContact.html)
2. Click **"Know Your PAN"**
3. Enter your **name, date of birth, and mobile number**
4. Verify with OTP
5. Your PAN number and status will be displayed

## Method 2: Track PAN Status on UTIITSL

1. Visit [UTIITSL PAN Tracking](https://www.trackpan.utiitsl.com/PANONLINE)
2. Select **"PAN Card Status"**
3. Enter your **application coupon number** (provided at the time of application)
4. Click **"Submit"**
5. Your application status and PAN number (if allotted) will be displayed

## Method 3: Check PAN Status via Income Tax Portal

1. Visit [Income Tax Portal](https://www.incometax.gov.in)
2. Click **"Quick Links"** → **"Know Your PAN"** or **"Verify PAN"**
3. Enter your **name, DOB, and mobile number**
4. Verify via OTP
5. Shows whether PAN is active, inactive, or linked with Aadhaar

## Method 4: Check via SMS

- **For NSDL:** Send SMS **NSDLPAN** followed by your **15-digit acknowledgement number** to **57575**
  - Example: `NSDLPAN N12345678901234`
- You'll receive a reply SMS with the current status

## How to Track PAN Card Delivery (Speed Post)

Once the status shows **"Card Dispatched"**:

1. Note the **Speed Post tracking number** (shown on the NSDL/UTIITSL status page)
2. Visit [India Post Tracking](https://www.indiapost.gov.in/VAS/Pages/trackconsignment.aspx)
3. Enter the Speed Post tracking number
4. Track the delivery of your PAN card

## Common Status Issues and Fixes

### Status Shows "Request Rejected"

**Reasons:**
- Incomplete or unclear documents
- Mismatch between form details and supporting documents
- Photograph or signature not as per specifications

**Fix:**
- Read the rejection reason on the status page
- Correct the issue and **reapply** with proper documents
- See our [PAN correction guide](/guide/pan-card-correction-online) for help

### Status Shows "Returned by Post"

**Reasons:**
- Incorrect or incomplete address
- Nobody available to receive at the address
- Address not reachable by Speed Post

**Fix:**
- Apply for **PAN reprint** with the correct address on NSDL/UTIITSL portal
- Fee: ₹50 (+ GST) for reprint
- Meanwhile, download **e-PAN** from the Income Tax portal for immediate use

### Status Stuck on "Under Process" for Weeks

**Possible reasons:**
- Physical documents not received by NSDL/UTIITSL (if you used the physical mode)
- High volume of applications causing delays

**Fix:**
- If you sent documents by post, check if they've been delivered
- If it's been more than **30 days**, contact NSDL helpline: **020-27218080** or UTIITSL: **033-40802999**
- Consider reapplying with **e-KYC mode** (Aadhaar OTP) for faster processing

## How to Download e-PAN (Instant Access)

Don't want to wait for the physical card? Download your e-PAN:

1. Visit [Income Tax Portal](https://www.incometax.gov.in)
2. Click **"Instant e-PAN"** → **"Check Status / Download e-PAN"**
3. Enter your **PAN number** or **Aadhaar number**
4. Verify with OTP
5. Download the **PDF e-PAN card**
6. This is legally valid as original PAN

## Processing Timelines

| Application Type | Processing Time |
|-----------------|----------------|
| New PAN (e-KYC) | 7-10 working days |
| New PAN (physical docs) | 15-20 working days |
| PAN Correction | 15-20 working days |
| PAN Reprint | 15-20 working days |
| e-PAN Download | Instant (if PAN already allotted) |
| Physical card delivery | 5-7 days after dispatch |

## Important Tips

1. **Save your acknowledgement number** — you can't track status without it
2. **Download e-PAN** immediately after PAN is allotted — don't wait for physical card
3. **Check Speed Post tracking** once card is dispatched for delivery updates
4. **If your card is returned**, apply for reprint quickly with correct address
5. **NSDL helpline (020-27218080)** is helpful for stuck applications — call during business hours

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. I lost my acknowledgement number. How do I check PAN status?

Use the "Know Your PAN" option on NSDL or Income Tax portal. Enter your name, DOB, and mobile number to find your PAN details and application status.

### Q2. How long does it take to get a PAN card after applying?

7-10 days with e-KYC (Aadhaar OTP mode), 15-20 days with physical documents. Add 5-7 days for Speed Post delivery after dispatch.

### Q3. Can I use e-PAN before receiving the physical card?

Yes. The e-PAN downloaded from the Income Tax portal is legally valid and accepted for all purposes including bank KYC, ITR filing, and investments.

### Q4. My PAN status shows "allotted" but I haven't received the card. What do I do?

Check the Speed Post tracking number on the status page. If the card was returned, apply for a reprint. Meanwhile, download e-PAN for immediate use.

### Q5. How do I check if my PAN is linked with Aadhaar?

Visit [Income Tax Portal](https://www.incometax.gov.in) → "Quick Links" → "Link Aadhaar Status". Enter PAN and Aadhaar to check linking status.

### Q6. Can I track PAN correction status the same way?

Yes. Use the same acknowledgement number tracking on NSDL or UTIITSL portal. The process is identical for new PAN and correction applications.

### Q7. What if the PAN status page shows an error?

Try after some time — the tracking servers can be slow during peak hours. If the error persists, try the SMS tracking method or call the NSDL/UTIITSL helpline.
