SBI Card (issued by SBI Cards & Payment Services Ltd.) offers a wide range of credit cards for shopping, travel, cashback, and rewards. Existing SBI customers can check for pre-approved card offers on YONO SBI, while new applicants can apply directly on sbicard.com.

## Who is Eligible?

### Salaried Individuals
- Age: 21-60 years
- Minimum income: ₹3-6 lakh per annum (varies by card)
- Credit score: 700+ recommended

### Self-Employed Individuals
- Age: 21-65 years
- Minimum income: ₹6 lakh+ per annum (ITR-based)
- Stable business for 2+ years

### Pre-Approved Offers (YONO)
- Existing SBI account holders may get pre-approved offers
- Based on account relationship, balance history, and credit profile
- Faster processing — minimal documentation needed

## Documents Required

### For Salaried Applicants
- **Identity Proof:** Aadhaar card, PAN card (mandatory), passport
- **Address Proof:** Aadhaar, utility bills, passport
- **Income Proof:** Latest 3 months' salary slips, last 2 months' bank statements
- **Employment Proof:** Company ID card or offer letter

### For Self-Employed Applicants
- **Identity & Address Proof:** Same as above
- **Income Proof:** Last 2 years' ITR, computation of income
- **Business Proof:** GST registration, business licence

## Step-by-Step: Apply via YONO SBI (Pre-Approved)

1. **Log in to YONO SBI** app
2. **Check "Shop" or "Cards" section** for pre-approved credit card offers
3. **If an offer is available**, tap on it to see card options and credit limit
4. **Select the card** and review terms
5. **Verify details** — KYC is often pre-filled from your SBI account
6. **Accept terms and confirm** with MPIN/OTP
7. **Card dispatched** within 7-10 working days to your registered address
8. **Activate the card** via SMS, SBI Card app, or by calling customer care

## Step-by-Step: Apply on sbicard.com

1. **Visit** [sbicard.com](https://www.sbicard.com/)
2. **Browse cards** and select the one that suits your needs
3. **Click "Apply Now"** on the card page
4. **Fill the application form** — personal details, employment, income
5. **Upload documents** — PAN, Aadhaar, salary slips
6. **Submit application** — receive application reference number
7. **Verification** — SBI Card may call for employment/address verification
8. **Approval** — decision communicated within 7-14 working days
9. **Card delivered** to your address via courier

## Important Tips

1. **Always pay your full bill** by the due date — revolving credit at 42% p.a. is extremely expensive
2. **Check YONO first for pre-approved offers** — these have faster approval and often better terms
3. **Track your annual spend** — many SBI cards waive the annual fee if you meet the spend threshold
4. **Use the right card for the right spend** — SimplyCLICK for online shopping, CASHBACK for maximum savings, ELITE for premium benefits
5. **Set up auto-pay** via Net Banking to ensure you never miss a payment and maintain a good credit score

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: How do I check if I have a pre-approved SBI credit card offer?
Log in to YONO SBI app and check the "Cards" or "Shop" section. Pre-approved offers appear automatically for eligible customers.

### Q2: What credit score is needed for SBI credit card?
A CIBIL score of 700+ is generally recommended. Higher scores (750+) improve approval chances and may qualify you for premium cards.

### Q3: Can I apply for SBI credit card without being an SBI account holder?
Yes, you can apply directly on sbicard.com. Being an SBI customer is not mandatory, though it helps with pre-approved offers.

### Q4: How long does it take to get an SBI credit card?
Pre-approved via YONO: 7-10 working days. Fresh application via sbicard.com: 14-21 working days including verification.

### Q5: Which is the best SBI credit card for cashback?
The SBI CASHBACK card offers flat 5% cashback on all online transactions, making it one of the best cashback cards in India.

### Q6: Can I increase my SBI credit card limit?
Yes, you can request a limit increase through the SBI Card app, sbicard.com, or by calling customer care. Approval depends on your usage pattern and income.

### Q7: Is there a lifetime free SBI credit card?
SBI occasionally offers lifetime free cards through special promotions. Most regular cards have annual fees that are waived upon meeting spend thresholds.
