# MGNREGA – 100 Days Employment Guarantee Scheme

The Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA) provides a **legal guarantee of 100 days of wage employment per year** to every rural household whose adult members volunteer to do unskilled manual work. It is one of the largest employment programmes in the world, covering all rural districts of India.

## What is MGNREGA?

MGNREGA is a **demand-driven** employment programme that:

- Guarantees **100 days of employment** per financial year to every rural household
- Provides **unskilled manual work** within 5 km of the worker's residence
- Pays **minimum wages** as notified by the state government
- Provides **unemployment allowance** if work is not provided within 15 days of demand
- Creates **durable assets** — roads, water conservation structures, land development, etc.
- Employment must be provided **within 15 days** of demand, otherwise unemployment allowance is due

## Who is Eligible?

- **Any adult member** (18 years and above) of a rural household
- Must be **willing to do unskilled manual work**
- Must reside in a **rural area** (Gram Panchayat)
- **No income or BPL criteria** — all rural households are eligible
- Both men and women eligible — **one-third** of beneficiaries must be women
- **Indian citizen** with residence in the Gram Panchayat

## How to Get a MGNREGA Job Card

### Step-by-Step Process

1. Visit your **Gram Panchayat office**
2. **Apply for a Job Card** — submit a written application with:
   - Name and age of all adult household members willing to work
   - Photograph of the household
   - Address proof
3. Gram Panchayat verifies and **issues the Job Card within 15 days**
4. The Job Card is **free of cost** — no charges at any stage
5. Job Card is valid for **5 years** and can be renewed
6. Each household gets **one Job Card** with names of all adult members

### Documents Required
- Application form (available at Gram Panchayat)
- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) of all adult members
- Household photograph
- Address proof (ration card / voter ID)
- Bank account details (for wage payment via DBT)

## How to Demand Work

1. Submit a **written application** for work to the Gram Panchayat
2. Mention the **number of days** of work needed and names of family members
3. Get a **dated receipt** of your application — this is important for claiming unemployment allowance
4. Gram Panchayat must provide work **within 15 days** of receiving your demand
5. If work is not provided within 15 days, you are entitled to **unemployment allowance**

### Important: Keep the Dated Receipt
The dated receipt is proof that you demanded work. Without it, you cannot claim unemployment allowance.

## Types of Works Under MGNREGA

- **Water conservation** — check dams, ponds, rainwater harvesting, desilting of tanks
- **Drought proofing** — afforestation, tree plantation
- **Irrigation** — micro-irrigation, canal repair
- **Land development** — land levelling, field bunding
- **Rural roads** — construction of roads connecting habitations
- **Flood control** — drainage channels, embankment repair
- **Individual beneficiary works** — farm ponds, cattle sheds, goat sheds, vermi-compost pits (for SC/ST/BPL/small farmers)

## How to Check Payment Status

### Online

1. Visit [nrega.nic.in](https://nrega.nic.in/)
2. Select your **State → District → Block → Panchayat**
3. Click on **"Job Card / Employment Register"**
4. Find your name and view **work days and payment details**

### Via Aadhaar-based Payment

- Wages are paid directly to your **Aadhaar-linked bank account** via DBT
- Check payment in your **bank passbook** or via bank's mobile app
- Payment must be made **within 15 days** of work completion

### Helpline
- Call **1800-345-2244** (toll-free) for MGNREGA-related queries

## Key Benefits

- **Legal right to work** — not a welfare handout, but a legal guarantee
- **100 days guaranteed** employment per household per year
- **No skill required** — unskilled manual work available for all
- **Equal wages** for men and women
- **Work near home** — within 5 km of residence
- **Unemployment allowance** if work not provided within 15 days
- **Worksite facilities** — drinking water, shade, first-aid, crèche (for children of workers)
- **Wage payment via DBT** — directly to bank account, reducing corruption

## Important Tips

1. **Always get a dated receipt** when you demand work — this is your legal proof for claiming unemployment allowance
2. **Open a bank account** and link it to Aadhaar for smooth wage payment
3. **Attend Gram Sabha meetings** — MGNREGA work plans are discussed and approved in Gram Sabhas
4. **Check the NREGA website** regularly to verify your attendance and payment records
5. **Report irregularities** — if the muster roll shows your name but you didn't work, or if wages are delayed beyond 15 days, complain to the Block Development Officer or call the helpline

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many days of work are guaranteed under MGNREGA?
Every rural household is guaranteed **100 days of wage employment** per financial year. All adult members of the household can share these 100 days.

### What is the daily wage under MGNREGA?
Wages vary by state, ranging from **₹237 to ₹344 per day** (2024-25 rates). The Central Government revises wages annually.

### Do I need a BPL card to get MGNREGA work?
No. MGNREGA is a **universal scheme** for all rural households. There is no income or BPL requirement. Any adult willing to do unskilled manual work can demand employment.

### What happens if work is not provided within 15 days?
You are entitled to **unemployment allowance** — one-fourth of the wage rate for the first 30 days and one-half thereafter. The dated receipt of your work demand application is required to claim this.

### Can women work under MGNREGA?
Yes. At least **one-third of all MGNREGA workers** must be women. Women receive equal wages as men for the same work.

### How do I get a MGNREGA Job Card?
Apply at your Gram Panchayat with a household photograph and Aadhaar cards of adult family members. The Job Card is issued **free of cost within 15 days**.
