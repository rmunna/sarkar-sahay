# Chhattisgarh Domicile Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **Mool Niwas Praman Patra** (मूल निवास प्रमाण पत्र) in Chhattisgarh — the domicile certificate — is issued by the Revenue Department (Tehsildar) and is required for NEET Chhattisgarh state quota (85%) admissions, CGPSC government job applications, Chhattisgarh state scholarships, and scheme eligibility. Applications go through **CG e-District**.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [edistrict.cgstate.gov.in](https://edistrict.cgstate.gov.in) or **Lok Sewa Kendra**. Issued by Tehsildar within **15–30 days**. Chhattisgarh's **Lok Sewa Guarantee Act** protects your right to this certificate.

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Local name** | Mool Niwas Praman Patra (मूल निवास प्रमाण पत्र) |
| **Eligibility** | Born in CG OR permanent resident |
| **Issuing authority** | Tehsildar / Sub-Divisional Officer |
| **Processing time** | 15–30 days |
| **Fee** | ₹10–₹20 |
| **Validity** | Permanent once issued |
| **Portal** | edistrict.cgstate.gov.in |

## Lok Sewa Guarantee Act — Your Rights

Chhattisgarh's **Lok Sewa Guarantee Act** guarantees delivery of domicile certificate within the prescribed period. If not delivered on time:
- File an appeal to the Appellate Authority (SDO/Collector)
- Penalty imposed on defaulting officer
- Citizens empowered to escalate via the Act

- **NEET Chhattisgarh state quota (85%)** — mandatory for CGDME (Directorate of Medical Education) counselling
- **CGPSC (Chhattisgarh PSC)** — CG government jobs (most posts require CG domicile)
- **CGVYAPAM** — state-level competitive exams
- **CG Post-Matric Scholarship** — SC/ST/OBC scholarships for CG residents
- **Mukhyamantri Suposhan Abhiyan / Didi schemes** — scheme eligibility

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory — with Chhattisgarh address)
- **Ration card** (CG ration card)
- **Voter ID** (CG voter enrollment)
- **Birth certificate** — showing birth in Chhattisgarh
- **School Transfer Certificate (TC)** — from CG schools
- **Land Records / Khasra-Khatauni** (for rural/agricultural families)
- Utility bills at CG address
- Self-declaration of CG residency period
- Passport-size photograph

## How to Apply Online — CG e-District Portal

1. Go to [edistrict.cgstate.gov.in](https://edistrict.cgstate.gov.in)
2. Register / log in with mobile number (OTP)
3. Navigate to **"Revenue Department"** → **"Mool Niwas Praman Patra"**
4. Fill form:
   - Applicant name, father's name, date of birth
   - District, Tehsil, Village/Ward
   - Duration of residence in Chhattisgarh
   - Purpose of certificate
5. Upload documents (Aadhaar, ration card, birth certificate, school TC)
6. Pay fee: **₹10–₹20** (online)
7. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via Lok Sewa Kendra

1. Visit nearest **Lok Sewa Kendra** (at every tehsil/block level)
2. Carry Aadhaar, ration card, voter ID, school TC
3. Operator fills and submits on CG e-District
4. Service charge: approximately ₹30–50
5. Get receipt with Lok Sewa guarantee timeline

---

## Status Check & Download

1. Go to [edistrict.cgstate.gov.in](https://edistrict.cgstate.gov.in) → **"Track Application"**
2. Enter application reference number
3. Once approved: download digitally signed PDF

## NEET Chhattisgarh State Quota 2026

For **NEET 2026 Chhattisgarh state quota** (CGDME medical/dental counselling):
1. CG Mool Niwas Praman Patra — proof of CG residency
2. OR studied in CG schools for prescribed period (check CGDME notification)

4. Apply well before NEET results (15–30 day processing)
5. CGDME verifies during document round

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Chhattisgarh has 32% ST reservation — does domicile help ST applicants?

For **ST reservation benefits** in CG, you need both:
1. **Caste certificate** (proving ST community)
2. **Domicile certificate** (proving CG residency)

The domicile alone doesn't grant ST benefits — both are required. CG has the 3rd highest ST proportion among states (32%), making caste + domicile combination important for many schemes and quotas.

### CGPSC — is CG domicile mandatory for all posts?

**Yes, for most CG state government posts** (CGPSC/CGVYAPAM), Mool Niwas Praman Patra is required. Check the specific CGPSC advertisement.

### Lok Sewa Guarantee Act — what if my application expires?

Under the Lok Sewa Guarantee Act, if your application is not processed within the guaranteed timeline, you can:
1. File an **appeal to the Appellate Authority** (SDO/Collector)
2. Demand the certificate delivery
3. The defaulting officer may face a fine (₹250–₹500 per day of delay in some provisions)
