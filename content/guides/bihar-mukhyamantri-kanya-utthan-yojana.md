# Bihar Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana 2026 — ₹94,100 for Girls from Birth to Graduation

**Bihar Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana** is a state welfare scheme that provides financial support to girl children from birth through graduation, disbursed in installments at key life stages. The total benefit amounts to **₹94,100 per girl child** — making it one of India's most comprehensive girl-child incentive schemes.

**Quick answer:** The scheme pays installments at birth, immunisation, Class 1, Class 2, Class 10 pass, Class 12 pass, and graduation. Graduation installment (₹50,000) is the largest — apply at [medhasoft.bih.nic.in](https://medhasoft.bih.nic.in). Check status online using registration number.

## Installment Breakdown — ₹94,100 Total

| Stage | Installment Amount | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Birth registration | ₹2,000 | Must register birth within 1 year |
| Complete immunisation (1 year) | ₹1,000 | All vaccines completed |
| Class 1 enrolment | ₹2,000 | Girl enrolled in government school |
| Class 2 pass | ₹2,000 | |
| Sanitary napkin scheme (14-18 yrs) | ₹300/year | Through school |
| Uniform (Class 1-2) | ₹600 | |
| Uniform (Class 3-5) | ₹700 | |
| Uniform (Class 6-8) | ₹1,000 | |
| Uniform (Class 9-12) | ₹1,500 | |
| Class 10 (Matric) pass | ₹10,000 | Must pass Bihar Board |
| Class 12 (Inter) pass (unmarried) | ₹25,000 | Must be unmarried at the time |
| **Graduation pass (unmarried)** | **₹50,000** | **Largest installment — apply online** |

## Eligibility Criteria

**For birth to Class 12 installments:**
- Girl child born in Bihar
- Family must be from the **Below Poverty Line (BPL)** category or economically weaker section
- Parents must be residents of Bihar
- Girl must be unmarried for the Class 12 and graduation installments

**For graduation installment (₹50,000):**
- The girl must have passed graduation (Bachelor's degree) from a recognised university in Bihar
- Must be **unmarried at the time of graduation**
- Must have a bank account in her own name
- Aadhaar must be linked to the bank account

> **Important:** The graduation installment of ₹50,000 is the most claimed component. Applications are accepted online at medhasoft.bih.nic.in after results are declared.

---

## Documents Required

**For birth to school-stage installments:**
- Child's birth certificate
- Parents' Aadhaar card
- BPL ration card or income certificate
- Bank account details (parents' account for early stages)
- Immunisation certificate (from ASHA/Anganwadi worker)

**For graduation installment (₹50,000):**
- Graduation marksheet and passing certificate
- Aadhaar card of the girl
- Bank account passbook (in the girl's own name, Aadhaar-linked)
- Registration number from BSEB/university portal
- Caste certificate (if SC/ST — additional quota)
- Unmarried status certificate (from Ward/Gram Panchayat)
- Passport-size photograph

## How to Apply for Graduation Installment (₹50,000)

This is the most common application. The process is fully online:

### Step 1: Register on Medhasoft Portal

1. Go to [medhasoft.bih.nic.in](https://medhasoft.bih.nic.in)
2. Click **"Students click here to apply"** under Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana
3. Select your university from the dropdown
4. Enter your **registration number** and **date of birth**
5. Click **"Login"**

### Step 2: Fill Application Form

1. Verify your personal details — name, date of birth, university, course
2. Enter **Aadhaar number** and verify via OTP
3. Enter **bank account number** and IFSC code (must be in your name)
4. Upload documents: marksheet, Aadhaar, bank passbook, photograph
5. Enter your mobile number for communication

### Step 3: Submit and Note Application ID

1. Review all details carefully
2. Click **"Submit"**
3. Note down the **application reference number**
4. Take a printout of the submitted form

### Step 4: University Verification

1. Your application is forwarded to your university for verification
2. University confirms graduation details
3. After university confirmation, the application goes to the Department of Women & Child Development

### Step 5: Payment

- After approval, ₹50,000 is credited directly to your bank account via PFMS
- Processing typically takes **30–90 days** after university verification

## How to Check Application Status

### Online Status Check

1. Go to [medhasoft.bih.nic.in](https://medhasoft.bih.nic.in)
2. Click **"Application Status"**
3. Enter your **registration number** or **Aadhaar number**
4. View current status: applied / university verified / approved / payment released

### Through eKalyan Portal

1. Visit [ekalyan.bih.nic.in](https://ekalyan.bih.nic.in)

## For Earlier-Stage Installments (Birth to Class 12)

For installments at birth, immunisation, and school stages, the process goes through:

1. **Anganwadi centre** (for birth and immunisation stages) — Anganwadi worker or ASHA submits on behalf of the family
2. **School Headmaster** (for uniform and school-stage installments) — Submit documents to your school; the headmaster forwards to the district education office
3. **District Education Office / Block Education Office** — For Class 10 and Class 12 stage installments

The Class 10 (₹10,000) and Class 12 (₹25,000) installments are now also available online at [ekalyan.bih.nic.in](https://ekalyan.bih.nic.in) after Bihar Board results are declared.

## Common Problems & Fixes

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Application not found on Medhasoft | Ensure your university has uploaded results to the portal; wait 2–4 weeks after result declaration |
| Bank account mismatch | Bank account must be in the **girl's own name**, not parents'. Open one if needed. |
| Aadhaar not linked to bank | Visit your bank with Aadhaar card to seed it; takes 3–7 days |
| University not in dropdown | Contact your university's examination section; they may need to register with the Bihar government portal |
| Payment credited but wrong amount | Check with District Women Development Project Office (DWDPO) |
| Application rejected | Get rejection reason in writing; common causes are unmarried certificate missing or document mismatch |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can a married woman get the graduation installment?**
No — the graduation installment (₹50,000) and the Class 12 installment (₹25,000) require the girl to be **unmarried** at the time of application. Marriage before graduation disqualifies from these stages.

**Is the scheme only for BPL families?**
The birth-to-Class 12 stages target BPL families. However, the graduation installment of ₹50,000 is available to all girls in Bihar who pass graduation from a recognised university — there is no income restriction for this stage.

**What if the girl studies outside Bihar?**
The graduation installment is for graduation from a recognised university. Some girls studying in other states may also be eligible — contact the Bihar Women Development Corporation (BWDC) helpline for clarification.

**When should I apply after graduation?**
Apply as soon as the result is declared and the portal is open. The portal typically opens within 2–3 months of result declaration.

**Can I check if my Aadhaar is linked to my bank?**
Visit your bank or use the NPCI mapper check at [npci.org.in](https://www.npci.org.in/what-we-do/aadhaar-mapper/aadhaar-mapper-bank-resolve-issues/find-your-bank) to verify Aadhaar-bank seeding status.

**Helpline:** Bihar Women Development Corporation — **0612-2506068 / 2506078**

> For grievances: Visit your District Women Development Project Office (DWDPO) or contact the Bihar Women Development Corporation, Patna.
