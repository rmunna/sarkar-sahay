## What is the Odisha Free Rice Scheme?

The Odisha Free Rice Scheme provides **free rice** to eligible families through the Public Distribution System (PDS) via Fair Price Shops across the state. Originally, beneficiaries under the **National Food Security Act (NFSA)** and **State Food Security Scheme (SFSS)** received rice at a subsidized rate of ₹1 per kg. Since **2023**, the Odisha government made this rice completely **free of cost**, in line with the Centre's decision to provide free foodgrains under NFSA.

The scheme covers approximately **2.96 crore people** (about 96 lakh families) in Odisha, ensuring food security for the most vulnerable sections of society. Each eligible person receives **5 kg of rice per month** free of cost through their ration card.

## Key Benefits

| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Rice per person | 5 kg per month (free) |
| For AAY families | 35 kg per family per month (free) |
| Cost to beneficiary | ₹0 (completely free) |
| Distribution channel | Fair Price Shops (PDS) |
| Coverage | ~2.96 crore people in Odisha |

### Categories of Beneficiaries

- **NFSA (Priority Household - PHH):** 5 kg rice per person per month — free
- **NFSA (Antyodaya Anna Yojana - AAY):** 35 kg per family per month — free
- **SFSS (State Food Security Scheme):** 5 kg rice per person per month — free (for families not covered under NFSA but identified by the state)

## Who is Eligible?

### NFSA Beneficiaries
- Families identified in the **NFSA beneficiary list** by the Government of Odisha
- **Priority Household (PHH)** card holders
- **Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY)** card holders — the poorest of the poor

### SFSS Beneficiaries
- Families **not covered under NFSA** but identified by the state government as food-insecure
- Approximately **18 lakh additional people** covered under SFSS
- Must hold a valid **SFSS ration card**

### General Eligibility Criteria
- Must be a **resident of Odisha**
- Must hold a valid **ration card** (AAY, PHH, or SFSS category)
- Family should be **below poverty line** or meet the prescribed income criteria
- Must not be excluded under NFSA exclusion criteria (income tax payer, government employee, etc.)

## Documents Required

### For New Ration Card Application
- **Aadhaar Card** of all family members
- **Residence proof** — Voter ID, electricity bill, or rent agreement
- **Income Certificate** from Tehsildar
- **Family photo** (passport size)
- **BPL Certificate** (if available)
- **Bank account details**
- **Existing ration card** (if applying for modification/addition)

## How to Get Free Rice in Odisha

### Step 1: Check if You Have a Ration Card

1. Visit [pdsodisha.gov.in](https://pdsodisha.gov.in) or [oscsc.in](https://oscsc.in)
2. Click on **"Ration Card Details"** or **"Search Ration Card"**
3. Enter your **ration card number** or **Aadhaar number**
4. Check if your card is active and the category (AAY/PHH/SFSS)

### Step 2: Collect Rice from Fair Price Shop

1. Visit your **designated Fair Price Shop** (mentioned on your ration card)
2. Carry your **ration card** and **Aadhaar Card**
3. Complete **Aadhaar-based biometric authentication** (fingerprint/iris)
4. The dealer will issue your entitled quantity of free rice
5. Verify the **quantity and quality** before leaving
6. Collect the **receipt/transaction slip**

### If You Don't Have a Ration Card

1. Visit the **Tehsil office** or **District Supply Officer (DSO)** office
2. Collect the **ration card application form** (or download from pdsodisha.gov.in)
3. Fill in family details — name, age, Aadhaar number of all members
4. Attach required documents
5. Submit the form at the Tehsil/DSO office
6. After verification, the ration card will be issued within **30-45 days**

### Online Ration Card Application

1. Visit [pdsodisha.gov.in](https://pdsodisha.gov.in)
2. Navigate to **"Apply for Ration Card"**
3. Fill in family details with Aadhaar numbers
4. Upload documents
5. Submit and note the reference number
6. Track status online

## Fees

- **Ration card application** — Free or nominal fee (₹5-25 depending on category)
- **Rice** — Completely free (₹0 per kg)
- **Aadhaar authentication** — Free

## Important Details

### Distribution Schedule
- Rice is distributed **monthly** at Fair Price Shops
- Each district announces the **distribution dates** (usually 1st-15th of each month)
- Check with your local Fair Price Shop for exact dates

### Quality and Quantity
- Rice quality must meet **FCI (Food Corporation of India)** standards
- If you receive **less quantity or poor quality**, complain to the helpline
- **Odisha Food Helpline: 1967** or **1800-345-6724** (toll-free)

## Processing Time

- Ration card application: **30-45 days**
- Monthly rice distribution: As per announced schedule
- Grievance resolution: **15-30 days**

## Important Tips

1. **Get Aadhaar of all family members** — Aadhaar-based authentication is now mandatory at Fair Price Shops. See our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
2. **Know your Fair Price Shop** — Your ration card mentions the assigned FPS. Visit only that shop
3. **Check entitlement** — Visit pdsodisha.gov.in to know exactly how much rice your family is entitled to
4. **Report black marketing** — If the FPS dealer asks for money or gives less rice, report to **1967** helpline
5. **Use portability** — Under One Nation One Ration Card, you can collect ration from any FPS in India using Aadhaar authentication

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. How much free rice can I get in Odisha?
Under NFSA, each eligible person gets **5 kg of rice per month free**. AAY card holders get **35 kg per family per month free**.

### Q2. Is the rice really free? No cost at all?
Yes. Since 2023, rice under both NFSA and SFSS is provided at **₹0 (completely free)** in Odisha.

### Q3. What is the difference between NFSA and SFSS?
NFSA is the central government's food security scheme. SFSS is Odisha's additional state scheme that covers families left out of NFSA, ensuring wider food security coverage.

### Q4. How do I check my ration card status?
Visit [pdsodisha.gov.in](https://pdsodisha.gov.in) and search by ration card number or Aadhaar number to check your card status and entitlement details.

### Q5. What if the Fair Price Shop denies me rice?
Contact the **Odisha Food Helpline at 1967** or **1800-345-6724** (toll-free) to register a complaint. You can also complain to the District Supply Officer.

### Q6. Can I get ration from any Fair Price Shop in India?
Yes. Under the **One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC)** scheme, you can collect your entitled ration from any ePoS-enabled Fair Price Shop in India using Aadhaar authentication.
