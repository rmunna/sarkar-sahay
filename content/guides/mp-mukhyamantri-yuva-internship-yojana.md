## What is Mukhyamantri Yuva Internship Yojana?

**Mukhyamantri Yuva Internship Yojana** is a Madhya Pradesh government initiative to provide practical work experience and skill development to unemployed youth. Under this scheme, selected youth are placed as interns in **government departments and offices** across MP for a period of **one year**, during which they receive a **monthly stipend**.

The scheme helps bridge the gap between education and employment by giving young graduates hands-on exposure to government functioning, administrative processes, and public service delivery.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Age:** 18 to 29 years
- **Education:** Graduate (minimum bachelor's degree from a recognized university)
- **Domicile:** Must be a permanent resident of Madhya Pradesh
- **Employment:** Must be currently unemployed (not employed in any government or private organization)
- **Income:** Annual family income should not exceed ₹6 lakh (for priority selection)
- **Not a government employee's child:** In some phases, preference given to first-generation graduates

## Benefits

| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Monthly stipend | ₹8,000 per month |
| Duration | 1 year (12 months) |
| Certificate | Certificate of completion from MP government |
| Skill development | Hands-on experience in government offices |
| Preference in recruitment | Experience certificate may help in future government job applications |

> **Note:** The stipend amount and scheme details may vary by announcement year. Check the latest notification on the official portal.

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar Card (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- Graduation degree / marksheet (final year)
- Domicile certificate of Madhya Pradesh
- Samagra ID (MP's family/individual identity)
- Income certificate (annual family income proof)
- Passport-size photographs
- Bank account passbook (Aadhaar-linked)
- Active mobile number and email ID
- Caste certificate (if applicable, for reservation benefits)

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Online Application

1. **Visit the official portal:** Go to the MP Yuva Internship Yojana website (check latest notification for exact URL)
2. **Register:** Click on "New Registration" and enter your Samagra ID, Aadhaar number, and mobile number
3. **OTP verification:** Verify your mobile number via OTP
4. **Fill application form:** Enter personal details, educational qualifications, preferred department/district for internship
5. **Upload documents:** Upload scanned copies of degree, Aadhaar, domicile certificate, income certificate, and photograph
6. **Select preferences:** Choose up to 3 preferred government departments and districts for placement
7. **Submit application:** Review all details and submit
8. **Download acknowledgement:** Save/print the application acknowledgement for your records

### Selection Process

1. **Application screening:** Applications are screened for eligibility at the district level
2. **Merit-based selection:** Selection is based on educational merit (graduation percentage)
3. **Reservation:** SC/ST/OBC/EWS/Women candidates get reservation as per MP government norms
4. **Placement:** Selected candidates are placed in government departments based on vacancies and preferences
5. **Joining:** Selected interns receive an offer letter and must join within the specified date

## Internship Details

### What Interns Do:
- Assist in day-to-day administrative work of government offices
- Help with data entry, documentation, and record management
- Support public grievance redressal processes
- Assist in scheme implementation and monitoring
- Participate in district-level government programs and events

### Departments Where Interns Are Placed:
- District Collector's office
- Tehsil and Block offices
- Public Works Department
- Agriculture Department
- Education Department
- Health Department
- Revenue Department
- And other state government departments

## Stipend Payment

- Stipend of **₹8,000 per month** is credited directly to the intern's bank account via DBT
- Payment is made **monthly** after attendance verification by the supervising officer
- No additional allowances (travel, accommodation) are provided
- TDS is not deducted as the stipend is below taxable threshold

## Fees

There is **no application fee** for this scheme. Registration is completely free.

## Important Tips

1. **Watch for notifications** — The scheme is announced periodically. Follow the MP government's official website and local newspapers for the latest notification.
2. **Keep Samagra ID updated** — Your Samagra ID must have correct personal and family details, as the system auto-verifies against it.
3. **Choose departments wisely** — Select departments aligned with your career interest for more relevant experience.
4. **Maintain attendance** — Regular attendance is mandatory. Excessive absences can lead to termination and stipend deduction.
5. **Get your certificate** — After completing 12 months, ensure you collect the experience certificate. It adds value to your resume for future government job applications.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. What is the monthly stipend under MP Yuva Internship Yojana?

The monthly stipend is **₹8,000**, paid directly to your bank account. This amount may be revised in future announcements.

### Q2. Is there any age relaxation for reserved categories?

Yes, SC/ST/OBC candidates typically get **3-5 years of age relaxation** as per MP government reservation policy.

### Q3. Can final year students apply?

Generally, you need a **completed graduation degree** at the time of application. Final year students awaiting results may not be eligible unless the notification specifically allows it.

### Q4. Will this internship lead to a permanent government job?

No, the internship does **not guarantee** a permanent job. However, the experience certificate and skills gained can help in competitive exam preparation and interviews.

### Q5. Can I do the internship in my home district?

Yes, you can select your preferred district during application. Placement is subject to availability of positions in your chosen district.

### Q6. What happens if I want to leave the internship midway?

You can resign from the internship by giving notice to the supervising officer. However, the certificate will mention the actual duration served, not the full 12 months.

### Q7. Is Samagra ID mandatory?

Yes, **Samagra ID is mandatory** for all MP government schemes including this internship. Apply at [samagra.gov.in](https://samagra.gov.in) if you don't have one.
