# MP Police Constable Exam Guide — Eligibility, Pattern, Syllabus & Tips

The Madhya Pradesh Professional Examination Board (MPPEB/Vyapam) conducts the recruitment exam for Constable (GD) and Constable (Radio) posts in Madhya Pradesh Police. This is a highly popular recruitment among youth in MP, with thousands of vacancies announced in each cycle.

MP Police Constable offers a stable government career with decent salary, uniform pride, and departmental promotion opportunities.

## Eligibility Criteria

### Age Limit

| Category | Minimum Age | Maximum Age |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| General | 18 years | 33 years |
| OBC (MP domicile) | 18 years | 36 years |
| SC/ST (MP domicile) | 18 years | 38 years |

*Age calculated as on 1st January of the recruitment year.*

### Educational Qualification

- **Minimum:** 10th pass (Matriculation) or 12th pass (varies by notification)
- Recent notifications have required 12th pass
- Check the specific notification for exact requirement

### Nationality & Domicile

- Must be a citizen of India
- Must be a permanent resident of Madhya Pradesh

### Physical Standards

**For Male Candidates:**

| Parameter | General/OBC/SC | ST |
|-----------|---------------|-----|
| Height | 167.5 cm | 160 cm |
| Chest (unexpanded) | 81 cm | 76 cm |
| Chest (expanded) | 86 cm | 81 cm |

**For Female Candidates:**

| Parameter | General/OBC/SC | ST |
|-----------|---------------|-----|
| Height | 152.4 cm | 145 cm |
| Weight | Proportionate | Proportionate |

## Exam Pattern

### Stage 1: Written Examination (Online)

| Subject | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| General Knowledge & Reasoning | 40 | 40 |
| Intellectual Ability & Mental Aptitude | 30 | 30 |
| Science & Simple Arithmetic | 30 | 30 |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** |

- **Duration:** 2 hours
- **Type:** Objective (MCQ)
- **Negative marking:** No negative marking (generally)
- **Difficulty level:** 10th/12th standard

### Stage 2: Physical Standards Test (PST)

Measurement of height, chest, and weight as per eligibility criteria.

### Stage 3: Physical Efficiency Test (PET)

**For Male Candidates:**
- 800 metres running in 2 minutes 45 seconds
- Long Jump: 13 feet (2 chances)
- Shot Put (16 lbs): 19 feet (2 chances)

**For Female Candidates:**
- 800 metres running in 3 minutes 30 seconds
- Long Jump: 10 feet (2 chances)
- Shot Put (8 lbs): 15 feet (2 chances)

### Stage 4: Document Verification & Medical Examination

## Syllabus Overview

### General Knowledge & Reasoning
- Indian History — Ancient, Medieval, Modern; MP history
- Indian Geography — Physical, Economic; MP geography
- Indian Polity — Constitution, Fundamental Rights
- Indian Economy — Basic concepts
- Current Affairs — National and MP-specific
- Logical Reasoning — Analogy, Classification, Series

### Intellectual Ability & Mental Aptitude
- Coding-Decoding
- Direction Sense
- Blood Relations
- Syllogism
- Venn Diagrams
- Number Series, Alphabet Series
- Mirror/Water Image
- Non-Verbal Reasoning
- Community attitude and law awareness (police-specific)

### Science & Simple Arithmetic
- **Science:** Physics, Chemistry, Biology basics (Class 10 level)
- **Arithmetic:**
  - Number System, Simplification
  - Percentage, Profit & Loss
  - Ratio & Proportion
  - Simple & Compound Interest
  - Time & Work, Time & Distance
  - Average, LCM & HCF

## Important Dates Pattern

- **Notification:** Periodic (every 1–3 years)
- **Application window:** 3–4 weeks
- **Written exam:** 3–6 months after notification
- **PET/PST:** 2–4 months after written results
- **Medical exam & DV:** After PET/PST
- **Final result:** 12–18 months from notification

## Preparation Tips

### 1. Focus on GK and Reasoning
These two areas carry the highest weightage. Study MP-specific GK thoroughly — history, geography, tribes, culture, and government schemes.

### 2. Practice Basic Maths and Science
Questions are based on 10th/12th level. NCERT Science textbooks (Class 9–10) and basic arithmetic practice are sufficient.

### 3. No Negative Marking — Attempt All
Since there is generally no negative marking, attempt every question. Even a few extra marks from guessing can make a difference.

### 4. Physical Preparation is Key
MP Police PET includes running, long jump, and shot put. Start training at least 4–6 months before the expected PET date. Focus on stamina and strength.

### 5. Study MP-Specific Topics
Questions on MP geography, districts, rivers, national parks, tribal areas, and state government schemes are frequently asked.

### 6. Solve Previous Year Papers
MPPEB/Vyapam previous year papers for police constable are widely available. Solving them helps understand the pattern.

### 7. Practice with Mock Tests
Online mock tests help improve speed and familiarize you with the computer-based exam format.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. What is the salary of MP Police Constable?
MP Police Constable salary is approximately ₹19,500–₹62,000 (Pay Scale as per MP government). With allowances, the initial gross salary is around ₹25,000–₹30,000 per month.

### 2. What is the minimum qualification for MP Police Constable?
The minimum qualification is typically 10th or 12th pass (varies by notification). Recent notifications have required 12th pass. Always check the specific notification.

### 3. Is there negative marking in the MP Police Constable exam?
Generally, there is no negative marking in MPPEB Police Constable exams. Verify from the official notification.

### 4. Can candidates from other states apply?
No, MP Police Constable recruitment is open only to permanent residents of Madhya Pradesh.

### 5. What is the PET standard for running?
Male candidates must complete 800 metres in 2 minutes 45 seconds. Female candidates must complete 800 metres in 3 minutes 30 seconds.

### 6. How many vacancies are usually announced?
MP Police Constable recruitment typically announces 4,000–15,000+ vacancies per cycle.

### 7. What is the selection process order?
Written Exam → PST (Physical Standards) → PET (Physical Efficiency) → Document Verification → Medical Examination → Final Merit List.
