## What is J&K Tejaswini Scheme?

The **Tejaswini Scheme** (also called "Tejaswini — The Radiant") is a women empowerment initiative launched by the **Jammu & Kashmir government** under **Mission Youth**. It provides **financial assistance up to ₹5 lakh** as an interest-free loan to young women in J&K for starting self-employment ventures.

The scheme aims to:

- **Empower women** to start their own business in manufacturing, services, trading, or small business
- **Provide soft finance** for setting up a new unit or expanding/modernising an existing enterprise
- **Support asset acquisition** — plant & machinery, equipment, furniture & fixtures
- **Meet working capital needs** for purchase of products, equipment, and tools

Tejaswini is one of the flagship livelihood generation schemes under Mission Youth, J&K, and has helped thousands of women across the Union Territory become financially independent.

## Who is Eligible?

To apply for the J&K Tejaswini Scheme, you must meet these eligibility criteria:

- **Gender:** Only women applicants
- **Age:** Between **18 to 35 years**
- **Education:** Minimum **10th pass** (matriculation)
- **Domicile:** Must be a **domicile of Jammu & Kashmir**
- **Registration:** Must be registered with **Mission Youth, J&K**
- **Ownership:** Individual women or women entrepreneur(s) holding **not less than 50% financial holding** in the enterprise
- **No default:** Applicant should **not be a defaulter** to any bank or financial institution
- **Unemployed:** Only unemployed women are eligible

> **Preference** is given to women entrepreneurs trained at R-SETIs (Rural Self Employment Training Institutes), Skill Development Institutions, or other recognised training institutes.

## Documents Required

Keep these documents ready before applying:

1. **Aadhaar Card** — for identity verification
2. **Domicile Certificate** — proof of J&K residency
3. **Age Proof** — birth certificate, 10th marksheet, or Aadhaar
4. **Educational Qualification Certificate** — 10th pass certificate or above
5. **Passport-size Photographs** — recent colour photos
6. **Bank Account Details** — account number, IFSC code, passbook copy
7. **Business Plan / Project Report** — details of the proposed business activity
8. **Training Certificate** (if any) — from R-SETI, skill development institute, etc.
9. **Residential Proof** — ration card, voter ID, or utility bill
10. **Self-declaration** — stating you are not a defaulter to any bank/financial institution

> All documents should be self-attested. Keep both original and photocopies handy for verification.

## Step-by-Step Application Process

You can apply for the Tejaswini Scheme online through the Mission Youth portal. Here's how:

### Step 1: Visit the Official Website

Go to the Mission Youth website at [missionyouth.jk.gov.in](https://missionyouth.jk.gov.in). Click on the **"Schemes"** tab on the homepage.

### Step 2: Find Tejaswini Scheme

On the Schemes page, look for **"Tejaswini — The Radiant"** under the **Livelihood Generation** section. Click on it to see the scheme details.

### Step 3: Click "Apply for Tejaswini"

Click the **"Apply for Tejaswini"** button. You will be redirected to the J&K Government Scholarship/Scheme portal at [jkgss.jk.gov.in](https://jkgss.jk.gov.in).

### Step 4: Start Online Application

On the JKGSS portal, find the **"Tejaswini The Radiant"** section and click **"Apply Now"**.

### Step 5: Verify Your Identity

Enter your **Aadhaar number** and **mobile number**. Click **"Send OTP"** and enter the OTP received on your mobile to verify your identity.

### Step 6: Fill the Application Form

Complete all the required details in the application form:
- Personal information (name, age, address)
- Educational qualifications
- Business/project details
- Bank account information
- Upload required documents

### Step 7: Submit the Application

Review all details carefully and click **"Submit"**. Save or print the acknowledgement receipt for future reference.

### Step 8: Wait for Processing

After submission, your application will be reviewed by the concerned authorities. You can track your application status on the same portal.

## Important Tips

1. **Register on Mission Youth first** — You must be registered on the Mission Youth portal before applying for Tejaswini. Visit [missionyouth.jk.gov.in](https://missionyouth.jk.gov.in) and create your profile.

2. **Prepare a strong project report** — A clear, realistic business plan increases your chances of approval. Get help from your nearest R-SETI or District Industries Centre if needed.

3. **Check for training opportunities** — Applicants with training certificates from recognised institutes get preference. Look for free skill development programmes in your district.

4. **Keep all documents updated** — Ensure your Aadhaar, domicile certificate, and bank details are current and matching. Mismatched information can delay your application.

5. **Track your application regularly** — After submission, check the JKGSS portal periodically for updates on your application status.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What is the maximum loan amount under the Tejaswini Scheme?

The maximum loan amount is **₹5 lakh** (₹5,00,000). The actual amount sanctioned depends on your project report and business requirements.

### Is the Tejaswini Scheme loan completely interest-free?

Yes, the loan is **interest-free**. The J&K government provides interest subvention, so you only need to repay the principal amount.

### What is the age limit for J&K Tejaswini Scheme?

The applicant must be between **18 to 35 years** of age at the time of applying.

### Can married women apply for the Tejaswini Scheme?

Yes, both married and unmarried women can apply, as long as they meet the eligibility criteria (age, education, domicile, and unemployment status).

### What type of businesses can I start with this loan?

You can start any gainful self-employment activity including **manufacturing, services, trading, retail shops, food processing, handicrafts, beauty parlours, tailoring units**, and other small businesses suited to your skills and local conditions.

### How long does it take to get the loan approved?

The processing time varies, but typically it takes **4 to 8 weeks** after submission of the complete application with all documents.

### Can I apply if I already have a small business?

Yes, the scheme also covers **expansion and modernisation** of existing businesses run by women, provided you meet the eligibility criteria and are not a defaulter to any bank.

### Where can I get help with my application?

You can visit your nearest **District Employment & Counselling Centre**, **R-SETI**, or the **Mission Youth office** in your district for guidance and support with the application process.
