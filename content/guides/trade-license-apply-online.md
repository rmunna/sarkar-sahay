# Trade License Apply Online: Municipal Corporation Process & Documents

A trade license is a permit issued by the local municipal corporation or municipality that allows you to carry out a specific trade, business, or profession within its jurisdiction. It is mandatory for all businesses operating from a physical premises in India.

## What is a Trade License?

A trade license is issued under the respective Municipal Corporation Act of each state/city. It certifies that a business complies with local regulations related to health, safety, and environment. Key points:

- It is NOT a registration of business — it is a permission to operate
- Required for shops, restaurants, factories, warehouses, offices, and all commercial activities
- Must be renewed annually or as per the local municipality rules
- Different from [FSSAI license](/guide/food-license-fssai-apply) or [shop & establishment registration](/guide/shop-establishment-registration)

## Categories of Trade

Most municipalities classify trades into three categories:

| Category | Examples |
|----------|---------|
| **Non-hazardous / Green** | Retail shops, offices, tailoring, IT services, tuition centres |
| **Semi-hazardous / Orange** | Restaurants, bakeries, laundries, salons, printing press |
| **Hazardous / Red** | Chemical storage, crackers, petroleum, LPG, industrial manufacturing |

> Fees and scrutiny increase with hazard level.

## Who Needs a Trade License?

- All shops, restaurants, and commercial establishments
- Manufacturing units and factories
- Warehouses and storage facilities
- Hawkers and street vendors (in many cities)
- Home-based businesses operating commercially
- Professionals (doctors, lawyers, CAs) with office premises

## Documents Required

- **Identity proof:** Aadhaar card / PAN card / voter ID of the applicant
- **Address proof of business premises:** Rent agreement / ownership deed / property tax receipt
- **Photographs:** Passport-size photos of the proprietor/partners/directors
- **Business registration proof:** GST certificate / partnership deed / certificate of incorporation
- **NOC from property owner** (if rented premises)
- **Fire NOC** (for hazardous and semi-hazardous trades)
- **Pollution NOC** (for manufacturing and hazardous trades)
- **FSSAI license** (for food-related businesses)
- **Property tax paid receipt** for the premises

## Step-by-Step Online Process

The process varies by city. Below is the general procedure:

### Online Application

1. Visit your **municipal corporation's official website** (see city-wise links below)
2. Navigate to **"Trade License"** or **"License Management System"**
3. **Register/create an account** using mobile number and email
4. Select **"New Trade License Application"**
5. Fill in the application form:
   - Trade/business name and type
   - Category of trade (non-hazardous/semi-hazardous/hazardous)
   - Address of business premises
   - Applicant/proprietor details
   - Number of employees
6. **Upload required documents** in the specified format
7. **Pay the fee** online (amount depends on trade category and city)
8. Submit the application and note the **application/reference number**
9. Municipal inspector may conduct a **site inspection**
10. Upon approval, download/collect the **trade license**

### Offline Process

1. Visit the local municipal corporation/ward office
2. Obtain the trade license application form
3. Fill and submit with required documents and fee
4. Inspector visits premises for verification
5. License issued after approval

## Processing Time

- **Online application:** 7–15 working days
- **Offline application:** 15–30 working days
- Inspection may add additional time

## Renewal

- Trade license is usually valid for **1 year** (April to March in most cities)
- Apply for renewal **before 31st March** each year
- Late renewal attracts **penalty fees** (varies by city, typically 2–5% per month)
- Renewal can be done online on most municipal portals
- Documents for renewal: previous year's license, property tax receipt, updated business details

## Important Tips

1. Apply for trade license **before starting operations** — operating without one attracts fines and potential closure
2. Keep the license displayed **prominently** at your business premises
3. Different trades at the same premises may need **separate licenses**
4. Change of trade activity requires a **new application or amendment**
5. Some states allow **combined trade + shop & establishment registration** — check your state portal

## FAQs

### Is trade license mandatory for all businesses?
Yes. Any business operating from a physical premises within a municipal area needs a trade license from the local municipal corporation.

### What is the difference between trade license and shop & establishment registration?
Trade license is issued by the municipal corporation for permission to conduct a specific trade. [Shop & establishment registration](/guide/shop-establishment-registration) is under state labour law and covers employee working conditions, hours, and leave.

### How much does a trade license cost?
Fees vary by city and trade category, ranging from ₹500 to ₹25,000 or more for hazardous trades in major cities.

### Can I apply for trade license online?
Yes. Most major municipal corporations now offer online application through their portals.

### What happens if I don't have a trade license?
Operating without a trade license can lead to fines, closure of business, and legal action by the municipal corporation.

### How often do I need to renew my trade license?
Most trade licenses need annual renewal before 31st March. Some cities offer multi-year licenses.

### Do I need a trade license for online/e-commerce business?
If you operate from a physical premises (office, warehouse, fulfilment centre), you generally need a trade license for that location.
