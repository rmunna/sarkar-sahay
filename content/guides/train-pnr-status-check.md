# PNR Status Check: Track Railway Ticket Live Status

Every Indian Railways ticket has a unique 10-digit PNR (Passenger Name Record) number. You can use this PNR to check the live booking status of your ticket — whether it is confirmed (CNF), waitlisted (WL), or RAC. This guide covers all official methods to check your PNR status.

## What is a PNR Number?

PNR stands for **Passenger Name Record**. It is a 10-digit number printed on the top-left corner of your railway ticket (both e-ticket and counter ticket). The PNR stores all details of your booking — passenger names, train number, date of journey, class, and current booking status.

**Where to find your PNR:**
- **E-ticket:** In the booking confirmation email/SMS from IRCTC, or on the ticket PDF
- **Counter ticket:** Printed on the top-left of the physical ticket

---

## How to Check PNR Status Online

### Method 1: Indian Railways Official Website

1. Go to [indianrail.gov.in PNR Enquiry](https://www.indianrail.gov.in/enquiry/PNR/PnrEnquiry.html)
2. Enter your 10-digit PNR number
3. Enter the captcha code
4. Click **Get Status**
5. Your current booking status will be displayed

### Method 2: IRCTC Website or App

1. Log in to [irctc.co.in](https://www.irctc.co.in/)
2. Go to **PNR Status** section (or My Transactions > Booked Ticket History for e-tickets)
3. Enter your PNR number
4. View the live status of each passenger

### Method 3: NTES (National Train Enquiry System) App

1. Download the **NTES** app from Google Play Store or Apple App Store
2. Open the app and select **PNR Enquiry**
3. Enter the 10-digit PNR number
4. Tap **Get Status**

### Method 4: SMS (No Internet Required)

Send an SMS in this format:

**PNR \<10-digit PNR number\>** to **139**

Example: `PNR 2612345678` → Send to **139**

You will receive an SMS reply with your current booking status. Standard SMS charges apply.

### Method 5: IVRS (Phone Call)

Call **139** from any phone. Follow the voice prompts and enter your PNR number. The system will read out your current status.

## Understanding PNR Status Results

| Status Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| CNF / Confirmed | Seat is confirmed. You have a reserved berth. |
| RAC (R1, R2…) | Reservation Against Cancellation. You get a shared berth. |
| WL (W/L 15) | Waitlisted. No berth allotted yet. Depends on cancellations. |
| CAN | Ticket has been cancelled. |
| GNWL | General Waitlist — for general quota. |
| RLWL | Remote Location Waitlist — boarding from intermediate station. |
| PQWL | Pooled Quota Waitlist — for short-distance travel on long-route trains. |
| REGRET/WL | Waitlist not confirmed; cannot travel. |

**Tip:** If your e-ticket status shows WL after chart preparation, the ticket is automatically cancelled and refund is processed to your account.

## PNR Prediction: Will My Ticket Get Confirmed?

PNR prediction estimates your chances of ticket confirmation based on historical data. While no prediction is 100% accurate, these factors affect confirmation:

- **Waitlist number:** Lower WL numbers have higher chances
- **Route and season:** Popular routes during festivals have lower confirmation rates
- **Quota type:** GNWL confirms faster than RLWL or PQWL
- **Days to departure:** More days = more time for cancellations
- **Train popularity:** Less popular trains confirm faster

**Note:** There is no official government PNR prediction tool. Use only the official PNR status check for confirmed information.

## Chart Preparation: When is Final Status Decided?

Indian Railways prepares the reservation chart in two stages:

1. **First chart:** Usually prepared 4-6 hours before the train's departure from its originating station
2. **Second (final) chart:** Prepared about 30 minutes before departure

**Important points about chart preparation:**
- After chart preparation, your PNR status becomes final
- If your e-ticket is still WL after chart preparation, it is automatically cancelled
- If your counter ticket is WL after chart, you cannot board the train
- RAC passengers can board the train and get a shared seat
- Sometimes additional confirmations happen between first and second chart

## Important Tips

1. **Check status regularly** as your journey date approaches — WL positions change frequently due to cancellations
2. **Keep your PNR handy** — save the SMS or screenshot your e-ticket confirmation
3. **Auto-cancellation for e-tickets:** WL e-tickets are auto-cancelled after chart preparation; refund is credited within 5-7 working days
4. **For counter tickets on WL,** visit the reservation counter before chart preparation to cancel and get a refund
5. **Use official sources only** — always check PNR status from indianrail.gov.in or IRCTC to avoid scams

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. How many times can I check my PNR status?
There is no limit on checking PNR status through the official website. You can check as many times as needed before and after chart preparation.

### Q2. Can I check PNR status after the journey date?
Yes, PNR status remains available for a few days after the journey date on the official website.

### Q3. What happens if my PNR shows WL after chart preparation?
For e-tickets, the ticket is automatically cancelled and the refund is processed to your payment method. For counter tickets, you should cancel at the counter; travelling on a WL ticket is not permitted.

### Q4. Is PNR status available for tatkal tickets?
Yes, [tatkal tickets](/guide/tatkal-ticket-booking-tips) also have a PNR number and you can check their status using the same methods.

### Q5. What does "Current Status" vs "Booking Status" mean?
**Booking Status** shows your position at the time of booking. **Current Status** shows the live/latest position. Current status is what matters for travel.

### Q6. Can I check someone else's PNR status?
Yes, PNR status can be checked by anyone who has the 10-digit PNR number. No login is required on indianrail.gov.in.

### Q7. What is the difference between GNWL, RLWL, and PQWL?
These are different [waitlist types](/guide/train-ticket-waitlist-rac-guide) based on your boarding and destination stations. GNWL (General) has the highest confirmation chances, while RLWL and PQWL have separate quotas with lower chances.
