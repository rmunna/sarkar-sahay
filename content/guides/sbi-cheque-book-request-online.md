## What is SBI Cheque Book?

SBI issues CTS-2010 compliant cheque books to savings and current account holders. Cheques are used for payments, fund transfers, and demand draft requests. You can request a cheque book online via YONO SBI, net banking, SMS, or by visiting your branch.

## Who is Eligible?

- SBI savings account or current account holders
- Account must be active and KYC-compliant
- Cheque book facility must be enabled on the account
- Basic Savings Bank Deposit (BSBD) accounts may have limited cheque book access

## How to Request SBI Cheque Book Online

### Method 1: Via YONO SBI App

1. Open the **YONO SBI** app and log in
2. Tap **Service Requests** from the menu
3. Select **Cheque Book Request**
4. Choose your account number
5. Select number of leaves: **25 leaves** or **50 leaves**
6. Confirm the delivery address (branch or registered address)
7. Submit the request
8. Note the reference number for tracking

### Method 2: Via SBI Net Banking (OnlineSBI)

1. Log in to [www.onlinesbi.sbi](https://www.onlinesbi.sbi/)
2. Go to **e-Services** → **Cheque Book Request**
3. Select the account number
4. Choose the number of cheque leaves (25 or 50)
5. Select delivery mode: **Branch pickup** or **Postal delivery**
6. Click **Submit**
7. Save the acknowledgement reference number

### Method 3: Via SMS

Send an SMS from your registered mobile number:

**SMS format:** `CHQBK <Account Number>` to **567676**

Example: `CHQBK 12345678901` to **567676**

> The cheque book will be dispatched to your home branch for collection.

### Method 4: Via SBI ATM

1. Insert your SBI debit card at any SBI ATM
2. Enter your PIN
3. Select **Other Services** or **More Options**
4. Select **Cheque Book Request**
5. Choose the account and confirm

### Method 5: Visit SBI Branch (Offline)

1. Visit your SBI branch
2. Fill the cheque book requisition slip (available at the counter)
3. Submit with your account number and signature
4. Collect the cheque book when ready (usually 3-5 days)

## CTS-2010 Cheque Format

All SBI cheques follow the **CTS-2010** (Cheque Truncation System) format mandated by RBI:

- **Security features:** Watermark, UV ink, microprinting, void pantograph
- **Payable at par:** Multi-city cheques valid across all bank branches in India
- **Machine-readable:** MICR code at the bottom for automated processing
- **Standard size:** 8" × 3.66" (CTS-2010 standard dimensions)

> **Important:** Old non-CTS cheques are no longer accepted by banks. Ensure you're using CTS-2010 format cheques only.

## How to Track Cheque Book Delivery

1. Log in to **YONO SBI** or **OnlineSBI**
2. Go to **Service Requests** → **Track Requests**
3. Check the status using your reference number
4. You'll also receive an SMS when the cheque book is dispatched

## Important Tips

1. **Keep cheque books secure** — treat blank cheques like cash; never pre-sign cheques
2. **Write "A/C Payee" crossing** on cheques to ensure they can only be deposited, not encashed
3. **Use blue or black ink** only when writing cheques — other colours may cause rejection
4. **Do not overwrite** — corrections on cheques lead to rejection; write a new cheque instead
5. **Record cheque numbers** — maintain a register of cheque numbers issued for tracking

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. How many free cheque leaves does SBI give per year?

SBI provides the first cheque book (up to 25 leaves) free per financial year for regular savings accounts. BSBD accounts get 10 free leaves.

### Q2. Can I request SBI cheque book online without net banking?

Yes. You can send an SMS `CHQBK <Account Number>` to **567676** from your registered mobile number.

### Q3. How long does SBI cheque book delivery take?

Online requests typically take 5-7 working days. Branch requests are faster at 3-5 working days. Delivery is via Speed Post.

### Q4. What is CTS-2010 cheque format?

CTS-2010 is the standardized cheque format mandated by RBI with enhanced security features. All bank cheques in India must follow this format. Old non-CTS cheques are no longer valid.

### Q5. Can I get SBI cheque book delivered to my home?

Yes. When requesting via YONO or net banking, select the postal delivery option. The cheque book will be sent to your registered address via Speed Post.

### Q6. How do I know my SBI cheque book has been dispatched?

SBI sends an SMS notification when the cheque book is dispatched. You can also track the status via YONO SBI or net banking under Service Requests.

### Q7. Can I request a cheque book for SBI BSBD account?

Yes, but BSBD (Basic Savings Bank Deposit) accounts are limited to 10 free cheque leaves per year. Additional leaves may attract charges.
