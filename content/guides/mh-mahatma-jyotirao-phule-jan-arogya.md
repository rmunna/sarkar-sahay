## What is Mahatma Jyotirao Phule Jan Arogya Yojana (MJPJAY)?

Mahatma Jyotirao Phule Jan Arogya Yojana (MJPJAY) is the flagship government health insurance scheme of Maharashtra. Originally launched as Rajiv Gandhi Jeevandayee Arogya Yojana in 2012, it was renamed in 2017. The scheme now operates in convergence with **Ayushman Bharat – Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB-PMJAY)** at the central level.

The scheme provides **cashless treatment up to ₹5 lakh per family per year** for serious illnesses and surgeries at empanelled hospitals across Maharashtra. It covers over **1,000 medical procedures** including heart surgeries, cancer treatment, kidney transplants, knee replacements, and more.

## Who is Eligible?

- Families holding **Yellow (BPL) or Orange ration card** in Maharashtra
- Families identified under **SECC (Socio-Economic Caste Census)** data for AB-PMJAY
- **All farmers** in Maharashtra (including those with White ration cards) — a unique Maharashtra addition
- Annual family income below the defined threshold as per ration card category
- Must be a **resident of Maharashtra** with a valid ration card

### Who is NOT Eligible?
- Families with Saffron/White ration cards (unless farmers)
- Government employees covered under CGHS/state health schemes
- Families already covered under ESI (Employees' State Insurance)

## Coverage & Benefits

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|

| Number of procedures covered | 1,000+ |

### Major Treatments Covered
- Cardiac surgeries (bypass, angioplasty)
- Cancer treatment (chemotherapy, surgery)
- Kidney transplant and dialysis
- Joint replacement (knee, hip)
- Neurosurgery
- Burns treatment
- Neonatal care

## Documents Required

- Valid Maharashtra ration card (Yellow/Orange, or any for farmers)
- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) of the patient
- Ration card with patient's name listed
- Doctor's referral letter or prescription recommending the procedure
- Previous medical records (if any)
- 7/12 extract or farmer certificate (for farmer families on White ration cards)

> **Note:** At the empanelled hospital, the Arogya Mitra (scheme coordinator) helps with document verification. No advance payment is needed.

## How to Avail Benefits — Step-by-Step

### At Empanelled Hospital

1. **Visit** any MJPJAY/AB-PMJAY empanelled hospital in Maharashtra
2. **Meet the Arogya Mitra** at the hospital's dedicated help desk
3. **Show your ration card and Aadhaar** for eligibility verification
4. **Biometric verification** — fingerprint scan to confirm identity
5. **Pre-authorization** — the hospital sends a treatment request to the insurance company/TPA
6. **Get treated** — once approved, receive cashless treatment
7. **Post-discharge** — follow-up care as prescribed; expenses covered for 15 days post-discharge

### Finding Empanelled Hospitals

1. Visit [jeevandayee.gov.in](https://www.jeevandayee.gov.in)
2. Click on "Hospital List" or "Find Hospital"
3. Select your district and search
4. Or call the helpline: **155388** or **1800-233-2200** (toll-free)

### Checking Eligibility Online

1. Visit [pmjay.gov.in](https://pmjay.gov.in) or [mera.pmjay.gov.in](https://mera.pmjay.gov.in)
2. Enter your mobile number linked to ration card
3. Enter OTP
4. Search by name, ration card number, or SECC data
5. If eligible, your name will appear with the scheme details

## Fees

**Zero fees.** The entire treatment is cashless. The hospital cannot charge the patient for any covered procedure. If any hospital demands payment for a covered procedure, report it on the helpline.

## Processing Time

- Eligibility verification: **Instant** (biometric at hospital)
- Pre-authorization approval: **6-12 hours** (emergency cases: faster)
- Treatment: As per medical requirement
- No claim filing needed by the patient — hospital settles directly

## Important Tips

1. **Carry ration card and Aadhaar always** — These are the primary documents for verification at the hospital
2. **Use only empanelled hospitals** — Treatment at non-empanelled hospitals is not covered
3. **No middlemen needed** — Approach the Arogya Mitra directly; do not pay any agent
4. **Report overcharging** — If any empanelled hospital demands money, call **155388** immediately
5. **Farmers benefit** — Even if you have a White ration card, farmers are covered; carry your 7/12 extract

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Is MJPJAY the same as Ayushman Bharat?
MJPJAY is Maharashtra's state health scheme that now operates in convergence with Ayushman Bharat (AB-PMJAY). Eligible families get coverage under both. The combined benefit is ₹5 lakh per family per year.

### Q2. Can I use this scheme outside Maharashtra?
Yes, under the AB-PMJAY convergence, you can avail cashless treatment at any **empanelled hospital across India**.

### Q3. Are pre-existing diseases covered?
Yes. There is **no waiting period** for pre-existing conditions under this scheme.

### Q4. How many family members are covered?
**All members** listed on the ration card are covered. There is no cap on family size.

### Q5. What if the treatment cost exceeds ₹5 lakh?
The scheme covers up to ₹5 lakh per family per year. Any amount beyond this must be borne by the family. However, many procedures are fully covered within this limit.

### Q6. Do I need to register separately?
If you are already on the SECC list or have a Yellow/Orange ration card, you are **automatically eligible**. No separate registration is needed.

### Q7. What is the helpline number?
Call **155388** or **1800-233-2200** (toll-free) for any queries or complaints.
