# NOC Certificate Apply Online: How to Get No Objection Certificate

A No Objection Certificate (NOC) is an official document stating that the issuing authority has no objection to the applicant proceeding with a particular activity. NOCs are required in many situations in India — from vehicle transfers and property sales to passport applications and job changes. This guide covers all major types of NOCs, how to apply, and downloadable formats.

## What is a No Objection Certificate (NOC)?

An NOC is a legal document issued by an organization, institution, or government body confirming that they have no objection to the bearer carrying out a specified activity. It acts as a clearance or permission letter.

### Common Types of NOC in India

| Type | Issued By | Purpose |
|------|-----------|---------|
| NOC for Vehicle Transfer | Regional Transport Office (RTO) | Transfer vehicle registration to another state/owner |
| NOC from Employer | Current employer | For higher studies, passport, side business |
| NOC from Society | Housing society | For renting, renovation, property sale |
| NOC from Police | Police station | For events, passport verification |
| NOC from Bank | Bank/lender | After loan closure for property/vehicle |
| NOC from Municipality | Municipal corporation | For construction, trade licence |
| NOC for Passport | Employer (govt.) | Government employees applying for passport |

## NOC for Vehicle Transfer (RTO NOC)

This is the most commonly searched NOC. When you sell a vehicle or move it to another state, you need an NOC from the RTO where the vehicle is currently registered.

### How to Apply Online (Parivahan Portal)

1. Visit [parivahan.gov.in](https://parivahan.gov.in/)
2. Go to **Online Services** → **Vehicle Related Services**
3. Select your state and RTO
4. Choose **"NOC"** from the services list
5. Enter your vehicle registration number and chassis number
6. Fill in the application form with buyer/destination details
7. Upload documents — RC copy, insurance, PUC, ID proof
8. Pay the fee online (₹100–500 depending on state)
9. Submit and note the application number
10. Visit RTO for biometric verification if required

### Documents Required for RTO NOC

- Original Registration Certificate (RC)
- Valid insurance certificate
- PUC (Pollution Under Control) certificate
- ID proof of vehicle owner
- Address proof
- Tax clearance certificate
- No dues certificate for any pending challans
- Form 28 (NOC application form)

### Processing Time

RTO NOCs are typically issued within 7–15 working days. Check for pending challans or tax dues, as these can delay the process.

## NOC from Employer

### When is it Needed?

- Applying for [passport](/guides/passport-apply-online) (mandatory for government employees)
- Pursuing higher education while employed
- Taking up freelance or part-time work
- Applying for a job in another organization (government sector)

### How to Get It

1. Write a formal application to your HR department or reporting officer
2. State the purpose clearly — passport, higher studies, etc.
3. Attach relevant supporting documents
4. HR processes the request (usually 3–7 working days)
5. NOC is issued on company letterhead with authorized signature

### NOC from Employer — Sample Format

```
[Company Letterhead]

NO OBJECTION CERTIFICATE

Date: ___________

To Whom It May Concern,

This is to certify that Mr./Ms. [Full Name], Employee ID: [ID],
working as [Designation] in [Department] since [Joining Date],
is a bonafide employee of [Company Name].

We have no objection to him/her applying for [purpose: passport /
higher studies / etc.].

[Authorized Signatory]
[Name, Designation]
[Company Seal]
```

## NOC from Housing Society

### When is it Needed?

- Selling or transferring property
- Renting out your flat
- Major renovations or structural changes
- Getting a [property registration](/guides/property-registration-online) done

### How to Get It

1. Write an application to the Society Secretary/Chairman
2. State the purpose (sale, rent, renovation)
3. Provide details of the buyer/tenant if applicable
4. Society may call a meeting or seek managing committee approval
5. NOC is issued with society seal and secretary's signature
6. Processing time: 7–15 days

### Documents to Submit

- Ownership documents (sale deed, share certificate)
- Society dues clearance certificate
- ID proof of owner and buyer/tenant
- Copy of agreement (sale/rent)

## NOC from Police

Required for organizing public events, running certain businesses, or during passport verification.

### How to Apply

- **For events:** Apply to the local police station or through the state police portal at least 15 days before the event
- **For passport:** The police verification officer may request an NOC during address verification — keep documents ready
- **For business:** Apply through the municipal corporation or district administration portal

## NOC from Bank (After Loan Closure)

After you repay a vehicle loan or home loan fully, the bank issues an NOC confirming no dues remain. This is essential for:

- Removing the bank's lien from the RC book (vehicle loan)
- Registering property transfer (home loan)
- Updating records with RTO or sub-registrar

### How to Get It

1. Ensure all EMIs and charges are paid
2. Request NOC from the bank branch or through net banking
3. Bank issues NOC within 7–21 days of loan closure
4. For vehicle loans, send the NOC to RTO to update the RC

## Tips for Getting NOC Quickly

- Apply online wherever possible — it's faster and trackable
- Clear all pending dues (tax, society charges, challans) before applying
- Keep all original documents handy for verification
- Follow up with the issuing authority if processing exceeds the stated timeline
- For urgent needs, check if the authority offers a tatkal/express service

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Is there a standard format for NOC?

There is no single universal format. The format depends on the issuing authority and purpose. However, every NOC should contain: the issuer's name and details, the beneficiary's name, the specific purpose, date, and authorized signature with seal.

### 2. How long is an NOC valid?

Validity depends on the type. RTO NOCs are typically valid for 6 months. Employer NOCs for passport are valid for the duration of the passport process. Bank loan NOCs have no expiry.

### 3. Can an NOC be revoked?

Yes, in certain cases. If the conditions under which the NOC was issued change (e.g., new dues arise), the issuing authority may revoke it. However, this is uncommon.

### 4. Is NOC required for vehicle transfer within the same state?

Generally, no. NOC is primarily required when transferring a vehicle to a different state. For within-state transfers, only a transfer of ownership application is needed.

### 5. Can I get an NOC online from my employer?

Many large companies and government organizations now issue NOCs through their internal HR portals. Check with your HR department for the digital process.

### 6. What if the housing society refuses to give NOC?

If the society unreasonably refuses, you can approach the Cooperative Housing Society Registrar or file a complaint with the district consumer forum. Societies cannot withhold NOC without valid reasons.
