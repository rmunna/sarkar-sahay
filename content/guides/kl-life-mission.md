# LIFE Mission Kerala – Free Houses for Homeless Families

## What is LIFE Mission?

LIFE (Livelihood, Inclusion, and Financial Empowerment) Mission is Kerala's flagship housing scheme to provide **free houses to all homeless and landless families** in the state. The scheme aims to make Kerala the first state in India with **no homeless families**. Houses are constructed either on the beneficiary's own land or on government-allotted land.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Homeless families** — living without a permanent shelter
2. **Families living in dilapidated houses** that are unfit for habitation
3. **Landless families** — the government provides land along with the house
4. Must be a **resident of Kerala**
5. Must be identified in the **LIFE beneficiary list** prepared by the local Grama Panchayat/Municipality
6. **Income:** Targeted at BPL and economically weaker section families

### Priority Categories

- SC/ST families
- Fishermen communities
- Families headed by single women
- Families with differently-abled members
- Families displaced by natural disasters

## Benefits Provided

| Category | Benefit |
|----------|---------|
| House with own land | ₹4 lakh financial assistance for house construction |
| Landless (house + land) | Land + ₹6 lakh for house construction |
| Flat (in urban areas) | Free flat in LIFE apartment complexes |

*Note: Amounts may be revised. Check the latest government order.*

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar Card** of all family members
- **Ration Card**
- **Income Certificate**
- **Land Documents** (if applicant has land)
- **Photo of Existing House** (if claiming dilapidated house)
- **Caste Certificate** (for SC/ST priority)
- **Bank Account Details**
- **Application Form** from Panchayat

## How to Apply

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Check the LIFE Beneficiary List:** Visit [lifemission.lsgkerala.gov.in](https://lifemission.lsgkerala.gov.in) or your local Panchayat office to check if your name is on the LIFE beneficiary list
2. **If not on the list:** Submit an application to your **Grama Panchayat/Municipality** requesting inclusion
3. **Panchayat Verification:** The local body verifies your housing status and eligibility
4. **Grama Sabha Approval:** The beneficiary list is discussed and approved at the Grama Sabha (village assembly)
5. **Final List Published:** The approved list is published by the Panchayat
6. **House Construction Begins:** Once approved, construction starts either on your land or government-allotted land
7. **Funds Released in Installments:** The financial assistance is released in stages as construction progresses

### For Flats (Urban Areas)

1. Apply at your local Municipality/Corporation
2. If selected, you'll be allotted a flat in a LIFE apartment complex
3. The flat is provided free of cost

## Construction Process

1. **First Installment:** Released at foundation stage
2. **Second Installment:** Released at lintel level
3. **Third Installment:** Released at roof level
4. **Final Installment:** Released after completion
5. The beneficiary participates in house design selection from approved models

## Processing Time

- **List Preparation:** 3-6 months (Grama Sabha approval cycle)
- **Construction:** 6-12 months after approval
- **Total Process:** 1-2 years from application to house handover

## Important Tips

1. **Attend Grama Sabha:** Your presence at the Grama Sabha where the beneficiary list is discussed is important.
2. **Verify Your Name:** Regularly check the LIFE beneficiary list at the Panchayat or online portal.
3. **No Middlemen:** LIFE Mission is a government program — do not pay any agent or middleman.
4. **Quality Construction:** Report any poor-quality construction to the LIFE Mission helpline or Panchayat.
5. **Document Everything:** Keep copies of all submitted documents and receipts.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Can I apply if I already own a small house?
If your existing house is **dilapidated and unfit for living**, you may be eligible for a new house under LIFE.

### Q2: Can I choose the design of my house?
**Partially.** The government provides approved house models. You can choose from these models.

### Q3: What if my name is not on the beneficiary list?
Submit a written application to your Panchayat/Municipality requesting inclusion. The next Grama Sabha may consider it.

### Q4: Can I sell the LIFE house?
**No.** LIFE houses have a **lock-in period** (usually 10-20 years) during which you cannot sell or transfer the property.

### Q5: Is this only for rural areas?
**No.** LIFE Mission operates in both rural (Panchayats) and urban areas (Municipalities/Corporations). Urban beneficiaries may get flats.

### Q6: What if I have land but no money to build?
If you have land, you can receive **₹4 lakh** financial assistance for house construction under LIFE.
