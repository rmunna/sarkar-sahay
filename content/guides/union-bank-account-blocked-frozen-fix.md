# Union Bank of India Account Blocked or Frozen — How to Fix It (2026)

If your Union Bank of India account shows "Account Blocked", "Debit Freeze", "Account Restricted" or you cannot make withdrawals, UPI payments or net banking transactions, this guide walks you through all the fixes step by step.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is independent and not affiliated with Union Bank of India. For account-specific help, contact Union Bank directly.

---

## Common Reasons Your Union Bank Account Is Blocked

| Reason | What Happens |
|--------|-------------|
| **KYC Not Updated** | RBI mandates periodic KYC updates — non-compliance causes partial or full freeze |
| **Aadhaar / PAN Not Linked** | Accounts without mandatory Aadhaar/PAN linking are frozen by RBI directive |
| **Too Many Failed Login Attempts** | 3–5 wrong password attempts blocks Union Bank net banking (UNI@TION) |
| **Suspicious / Large Transactions** | Sudden or unusual transactions trigger fraud detection block |
| **Income Tax Department Order** | Tax freeze for pending demands or notices |
| **Dormant Account** | No transactions for 2+ years → account becomes dormant |
| **Court Order / Loan Recovery** | Freeze due to court order, DRT (Debt Recovery Tribunal) or legal recovery process |
| **Minimum Balance Restriction** | Low balance in some accounts leads to restricted (not full block) functionality |

---

## Type of Block — What's Affected

| Block Type | Transactions Affected |
|-----------|-----------------------|
| **Debit Freeze** | Withdrawals, transfers, debit card, UPI blocked. Incoming credits may still work |
| **Credit + Debit Freeze** | All transactions stopped — no in/out |
| **Net Banking Block** | Only Union Bank net banking (UNI@TION) locked |
| **UPI / BHIM Block** | UPI linked to Union Bank account blocked |

## Fix 1: KYC Freeze

**Via Union Bank Mobile Banking App (Vyom):**
1. Open **Union Bank Vyom** app
2. Look for a **KYC Update** prompt or notification on home screen
3. Follow the instructions to complete KYC online
4. Upload Aadhaar, PAN and a selfie

**Via Union Bank Net Banking (UNI@TION):**
1. Go to **[unionbankofindia.co.in](https://www.unionbankofindia.co.in)** → Login → Net Banking
2. Navigate to **Services → Update KYC**
3. Upload documents: Aadhaar card, PAN card, address proof if different
4. Submit — verification takes 1–3 working days

**At Branch (Recommended for complete freeze):**
1. Visit your **home branch** with original Aadhaar + PAN + address proof
2. Fill the **KYC Update Form** at the counter
3. Submit — account is typically unblocked in 1–3 working days

---

## Fix 2: Aadhaar/PAN Linking (If Not Done)

1. Login to **Union Bank Net Banking (UNI@TION)** or **Vyom app**
2. Go to **Services → Link Aadhaar** or **Link PAN**
3. Enter your Aadhaar/PAN number and verify via OTP
4. Alternatively, visit any Union Bank branch with Aadhaar + PAN for in-person linking

**For Aadhaar linking via SMS:**
Send SMS: `UID <12-digit Aadhaar> <Account Number>` to **09223008486**

---

## Fix 3: Net Banking (UNI@TION) Blocked — Wrong Password

1. Go to **[unionbankofindia.co.in](https://www.unionbankofindia.co.in)** → Login
2. Click **"Forgot Password"**
3. Enter User ID and registered mobile number
4. Verify via OTP
5. Set a new password — access restored immediately

**For Vyom App MPIN Reset:**
1. Open **Union Bank Vyom** app
2. Tap **"Forgot MPIN"** on the login screen
3. Verify using OTP + debit card details
4. Set new MPIN

---

## Fix 4: Suspicious Activity Block

1. Call **Union Bank Customer Care: 1800-22-2244** (24×7, toll-free) or **1800-208-2244**
2. Explain that your account has been blocked
3. Complete identity verification over the phone (OTP + security questions)
4. Request a temporary unblock for urgent needs
5. For permanent unblock, visit your home branch with:
   - Aadhaar + PAN (originals)
   - A written explanation of the flagged transactions
   - Bank statement if available

---

## Fix 5: Income Tax Department Freeze

1. Visit **[incometax.gov.in](https://www.incometax.gov.in)** → e-Filing portal → Login
2. Go to **Pending Actions → Outstanding Demand** to see pending notices
3. Pay the demand or file a rectification/response online
4. Obtain the IT department's defreeze order (or "NIL outstanding" certificate)
5. Submit the order to your Union Bank home branch
6. Branch will process the defreeze in 3–7 working days

---

## Fix 6: Dormant Account Reactivation

1. Visit your **home branch** (or any branch with supporting documents) with:
   - Original Aadhaar
   - Original PAN
   - Passport photo (1–2 copies)
2. Fill the **Account Reactivation Form**
3. Make a small transaction (minimum cash deposit)
4. Account is typically reactivated in 1–3 working days

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is my money safe when my Union Bank account is frozen?**
Yes — your funds are completely safe. A freeze only restricts transactions, it does not affect your balance. DICGC insurance covers up to ₹5 lakh per depositor.

**Can I still receive salary if my Union Bank account is on debit freeze?**
Usually yes — a debit freeze blocks outgoing transactions but incoming credits (salary, transfers) typically still work. A full credit + debit freeze blocks everything.

**Will my Union Bank loan EMI fail during a freeze?**
Yes — auto-debits for EMI, SIP and insurance premiums will fail during debit freeze. Immediately inform your bank/NBFC/insurer to avoid penalty, NPA status or policy lapse.

**How do I escalate if Union Bank does not unblock my account?**
1. Write to Union Bank Nodal Officer via [unionbankofindia.co.in/english/grievanceredressal.aspx](https://www.unionbankofindia.co.in)
2. If unresolved within 30 days, file a complaint with the **Banking Ombudsman** at [bankingombudsman.rbi.org.in](https://bankingombudsman.rbi.org.in)

**Union Bank merged with Andhra Bank and Corporation Bank — does this affect my account?**
The merger happened in April 2020. All Andhra Bank and Corporation Bank accounts are now Union Bank accounts. Your IFSC code, account number, or branch may have changed — contact Union Bank if you're facing issues related to the merger.
