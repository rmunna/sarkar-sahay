## What is Punjab Post Matric Scholarship for SC Students?

**Punjab Post Matric Scholarship for Scheduled Caste (SC) Students** is a central government scheme implemented by Punjab's Social Justice, Empowerment, and Minorities department. It provides **full tuition fee + maintenance allowance** to SC students pursuing education after Class 10 (Class 11, 12, ITI, Diploma, graduation, post-graduation).

---

## Key Benefits

| Benefit | Amount |
|---------|--------|
| Tuition fee | 100% reimbursement (as per state government rate) |
| Admission fee | Fully covered |
| Study tour / field trips | Covered (for professional courses) |
| Thesis typing, printing | Covered (for research scholars) |
| Maintenance allowance | ₹380–1,200/month (see table below) |

### Maintenance Allowance Rates

| Level of Study | Hostel | Day Scholar |
|----------------|--------|-------------|
| Degree/PG (Group I — Medical, Engineering) | ₹1,200/month | ₹550/month |
| Degree/PG (Group II — Other professional) | ₹820/month | ₹530/month |
| Degree/PG (Group III — General) | ₹570/month | ₹300/month |
| Class 11 & 12 / ITI / Diploma | ₹380/month | ₹230/month |

---

## Who is Eligible?

- **SC (Scheduled Caste) student**
- Parent/guardian's annual income: **₹2.5 lakh or less** (for central component)
- Enrolled in Punjab recognized institution, Class 11 onwards
- For state-funded component: Punjab resident
- **Full-time course** (not distance/correspondence for maintenance allowance)

---

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card**
2. **SC Caste certificate** — from competent authority (SDM/Tehsildar)
3. **Income certificate** — from SDM/Tehsildar (annual income ≤₹2.5 lakh)
4. **Admission receipt** (current year)
5. **Bank passbook** — student's Aadhaar-linked account
6. **Bonafide certificate** from institution
7. **Previous year mark sheet**
8. **Fee receipt** from institution

---

## How to Apply

### Step 1 — Online Application

Go to **[scholarship.punjab.gov.in](https://scholarship.punjab.gov.in)**:
- Register with Aadhaar
- Fill the scholarship application form
- Upload all required documents
- Submit before the deadline (usually September–October each year)

### Step 2 — Institution Verification

Your college/institution verifies your enrollment on the portal and confirms fee structure.

### Step 3 — Welfare Officer Verification

The **District Welfare Officer** verifies caste certificate and income certificate.

### Step 4 — Scholarship Disbursement

After central/state approval:
- Tuition fee directly paid to institution OR reimbursed to student
- Maintenance allowance credited monthly to student's bank account

---

## Application Deadline

**Usually September–October** each academic year for the upcoming year. Late applications may be accepted until December in some years. Check scholarship.punjab.gov.in each August for the current year's deadline.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. I am an SC student in a private engineering college in Chandigarh. Am I eligible?**

Yes, if your family income is ₹2.5 lakh or below and you have a valid Punjab SC caste certificate. Private recognized colleges are covered — tuition fees (within state rate cap) are reimbursed. Apply at scholarship.punjab.gov.in.

**2. My parents' combined income is ₹3 lakh/year. Am I eligible?**

The income limit for the central post-matric scholarship is ₹2.5 lakh/year. At ₹3 lakh, you exceed this. However, Punjab may have a state-funded component for slightly higher incomes — check with the District Welfare Office for alternatives.

**3. I missed last year's deadline and got no scholarship. What can I do now?**

Apply for the current academic year during the application window (August–October). Missed years cannot be recovered retroactively. Ensure you apply in August each year going forward.

**4. The scholarship amount in my account is less than what I expected. Why?**

Maintenance allowance is only for months during the academic session. Also, scholarship for private college tuition fees may be capped at the government college rate, not the actual private tuition. Discuss with the District Welfare Office for a breakdown.

**5. Can I get this scholarship for an online/distance degree?**

Maintenance allowance is generally for full-time regular courses. Distance education students may get tuition fee support but not maintenance. Check the current year's guidelines.
