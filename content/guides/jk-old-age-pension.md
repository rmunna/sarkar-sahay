## What is J&K Old Age Pension Scheme?

The **J&K Old Age Pension Scheme** provides monthly financial assistance to senior citizens in Jammu & Kashmir who are **60 years and above** and belong to economically weaker sections. The scheme combines the **Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS)** under NSAP with the UT-level supplementary pension.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Age:** Must be **60 years or older**
2. **Residency:** Must be a permanent resident of Jammu & Kashmir
3. **Income:** Must be from BPL family or below prescribed income limit
4. **Other Pension:** Must not be receiving any other government pension
5. **Aadhaar:** Required for DBT

## Pension Amount

| Age Group | Monthly Pension |
|-----------|----------------|
| 60-79 years | ₹1,000 (NSAP ₹200 + UT top-up) |
| 80+ years | ₹1,500+ (NSAP ₹500 + UT top-up) |

> Amounts reflect combined central + UT components. Check the latest notification for updated amounts.

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar Card** (linked to bank account)
- **Age Proof** (Birth Certificate / Aadhaar / School records)
- **Domicile Certificate** of J&K
- **BPL Certificate** / Income Certificate
- **Bank Passbook** (Aadhaar-linked)
- **Passport-size photographs**
- **Ration Card**

## How to Apply

### Online

1. **Visit** [jksocialwelfare.nic.in](https://jksocialwelfare.nic.in) or the J&K e-services portal
2. **Register** / Login
3. **Navigate** to Social Security Pension → Old Age Pension
4. **Fill** the application with personal, age, and bank details
5. **Upload** documents
6. **Submit** and save application ID
7. **Track** status online

### Offline

1. **Visit** the **Block Development Office** or **Tehsil Welfare Office**
2. **Collect** the pension application form
3. **Fill** and attach documents
4. **Submit** to the BDO or Social Welfare Officer
5. **Gram Sabha / local body** verification
6. **Get** acknowledgement receipt

## Processing Time

- **Verification:** 30-60 days
- **Approval:** After field and local body verification
- **First Payment:** 1-2 months after approval
- **Ongoing:** Monthly/quarterly via DBT

## Important Tips

1. **BPL Status:** BPL families are prioritized — ensure your BPL card is valid
2. **Aadhaar-Bank Link:** Mandatory for DBT
3. **Life Certificate:** Submit annually (Jeevan Pramaan)
4. **One Pension Only:** Cannot avail widow or disability pension simultaneously
5. **Block Office:** For rural areas, the BDO office is the primary point of contact

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. What is the pension amount?
₹1,000-₹1,500/month depending on age (combined central + UT components).

### Q2. What is the age requirement?
Must be **60 years or older**.

### Q3. Can I apply online?
Yes, through the J&K Social Welfare portal or e-services portal.

### Q4. Is Aadhaar mandatory?
Yes, for DBT payments.

### Q5. Can I get Old Age Pension and SEHAT health card?
Yes — SEHAT is a health scheme and doesn't conflict with pension.

### Q6. How often is pension paid?
Monthly or quarterly, directly to bank account via DBT.

### Q7. What if my application is rejected?
Contact the District Social Welfare Officer for clarification and re-apply.
