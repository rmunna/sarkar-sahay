## What is YSR Aarogyasri?

**Dr. YSR Aarogyasri Health Care Trust** is the flagship health insurance scheme of the Government of Andhra Pradesh. It provides **free cashless treatment up to ₹5 lakh per family per year** at empanelled network hospitals for Below Poverty Line (BPL) and eligible families.

Originally launched as Rajiv Aarogyasri in 2007, the scheme was restructured and renamed after Dr. Y.S. Rajasekhara Reddy. It covers over 2,000 medical procedures including surgeries, therapies, and treatments for serious illnesses — ensuring that no poor family is denied quality healthcare due to financial constraints.

> Similar to [Ayushman Bharat](/guide/ayushman-bharat-health-card) at the central level, YSR Aarogyasri is a state-level health protection scheme specifically for Andhra Pradesh residents.

## Who is Eligible?

To avail benefits under YSR Aarogyasri, you must meet **all** of the following criteria:

1. **Resident of Andhra Pradesh** — must be a permanent resident of AP
2. **White Ration Card Holder** — the family must hold a white ration card (issued to BPL families)
3. **Annual Family Income** — below the BPL threshold as defined by the state government
4. **Aadhaar Linked** — Aadhaar number must be linked for identity verification

### Who is NOT Eligible?

- Families holding pink/other colour ration cards (above poverty line)
- Government employees and their dependents (covered under separate schemes)
- Families already covered under ESIC or CGHS
- Non-residents of Andhra Pradesh

## How to Get a YSR Aarogyasri Card

If you hold a white ration card in Andhra Pradesh, you are **automatically eligible**. Here's how to get your Aarogyasri health card:

### Step 1: Check Your Eligibility Online

1. Visit the official portal at [aarogyasri.ap.gov.in](https://aarogyasri.ap.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Check Eligibility"** or **"Beneficiary Search"**
3. Enter your Aadhaar number or ration card number
4. Your eligibility status will be displayed

### Step 2: Visit a Meeseva / Village Secretariat

1. Go to your nearest **Village Secretariat**, **Ward Secretariat**, or **Meeseva Centre**
2. Carry the following documents:
   - White ration card (original + photocopy)
   - Aadhaar card of all family members
   - Recent passport-size photographs
3. The volunteer/operator will verify your details and register you

### Step 3: Receive Your Health Card

- Once verified, the Aarogyasri health card is issued
- The card is linked to your family's ration card and Aadhaar
- You can also download/check your card status on the official portal

> **Tip:** In many cases, eligible white ration card holders are auto-enrolled. Check the portal with your Aadhaar number before visiting any office.

## How to Check the Hospital List

To find YSR Aarogyasri empanelled hospitals near you:

### Online Method

1. Go to [aarogyasri.ap.gov.in](https://aarogyasri.ap.gov.in)
2. Navigate to **"Network Hospitals"** or **"Hospital List"**
3. Select your **district** and **hospital type** (government/private)
4. The list of empanelled hospitals with addresses and contact details will appear

### At the Hospital

- All empanelled hospitals display the **YSR Aarogyasri board** at the entrance
- They have a dedicated **Aarogyasri Help Desk** (Aarogyamitra counter) for beneficiaries

### Helpline

- Call **104** (AP Health Helpline) to enquire about nearest network hospitals
- You can also call **1902** for Aarogyasri-specific queries

## How to Use Aarogyasri at a Hospital

Follow these steps when you need treatment:

### Step 1: Visit an Empanelled Hospital

- Go to any YSR Aarogyasri network hospital (government or private)
- Approach the **Aarogyamitra** (scheme coordinator) stationed at the hospital

### Step 2: Verify Your Identity

- Show your **Aadhaar card** and **white ration card**
- The Aarogyamitra will verify your eligibility through biometric/online authentication

### Step 3: Get Diagnosed

- The hospital's medical team will examine you and recommend treatment
- If the required procedure is covered under Aarogyasri, the process moves forward

### Step 4: Pre-authorisation

- The hospital submits a **pre-authorisation request** to the Aarogyasri Trust
- Approval is typically granted within **24–48 hours** (emergency cases get faster approval)

### Step 5: Receive Cashless Treatment

- Once approved, your treatment/surgery is performed **completely free of cost**
- You pay nothing — the hospital claims the cost directly from the Trust

### Step 6: Post-Treatment Follow-up

- Follow-up consultations and medications for up to **1 year** are covered
- Transport allowance of ₹200–₹1,000 is provided for travel

## Covered Diseases & Procedures

YSR Aarogyasri covers **2,000+ procedures** across multiple specialities:

### Major Categories

- **Cardiology** — bypass surgery, angioplasty, valve replacement
- **Oncology (Cancer)** — chemotherapy, radiation, surgical removal of tumours
- **Neurology** — brain surgery, spinal procedures, epilepsy treatment
- **Nephrology** — kidney transplant, dialysis
- **Orthopaedics** — joint replacement (hip/knee), fracture surgeries
- **Ophthalmology** — cataract surgery, retinal procedures
- **Gastroenterology** — liver transplant, gastrointestinal surgeries
- **Pulmonology** — lung surgeries, treatment for chronic respiratory conditions
- **Gynaecology** — hysterectomy, treatment for reproductive disorders
- **Paediatric Surgery** — congenital heart defects, neonatal surgeries
- **Burns & Plastic Surgery** — reconstruction, skin grafting
- **ENT** — cochlear implant, tonsillectomy, sinus surgery

### What is NOT Covered?

- Outpatient (OPD) consultations and minor ailments
- Cosmetic/aesthetic procedures
- Treatment for substance abuse/de-addiction
- Dental procedures (except maxillofacial surgery)
- Infertility treatments
- Procedures not listed in the approved package list

> **Full list:** The complete list of covered procedures and package rates is available on the [official Aarogyasri portal](https://aarogyasri.ap.gov.in).

## Claim Process

### For Beneficiaries (Patients)

You **do not need to file any claim**. The process is entirely cashless:

1. Visit an empanelled hospital → get verified → receive treatment
2. The hospital directly claims the cost from the Aarogyasri Trust
3. You receive treatment with **zero out-of-pocket expense**

### For Hospitals

1. Hospital submits pre-authorisation request via the Aarogyasri portal
2. Trust reviews and approves/rejects the request
3. After treatment, hospital uploads discharge summary and bills
4. Trust processes the payment to the hospital directly

### If Claim is Denied

- Ask the Aarogyamitra for the reason
- You can file a **grievance** on the official portal or call **104 / 1902**
- Escalate to the District Aarogyasri Coordinator if unresolved

## Renewal & Validity

- YSR Aarogyasri coverage is **renewed automatically each year** as long as you hold a valid white ration card
- No separate renewal application is needed
- If your ration card status changes (e.g., from white to another category), eligibility may be affected
- Keep your Aadhaar and ration card details updated to avoid issues

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is YSR Aarogyasri the same as Ayushman Bharat?

No. YSR Aarogyasri is a **state scheme** run by the AP government. Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY) is a **central scheme**. However, AP has integrated both — eligible families may receive benefits under either or both schemes. The state scheme often covers additional procedures not included in PM-JAY.

### Can I use Aarogyasri in another state?

No. YSR Aarogyasri is valid only at empanelled hospitals within Andhra Pradesh (and select hospitals in neighbouring states like Hyderabad for tertiary care, under special arrangements).

### Is there any age limit?

No. All members of an eligible family — from newborns to senior citizens — are covered.

### How many times can I use it in a year?

You can avail treatment multiple times, as long as the total cost does not exceed **₹5 lakh per family per year**.

### What if I need treatment costing more than ₹5 lakh?

For treatment exceeding the annual cap, the Aarogyasri Trust may consider additional support on a case-by-case basis, especially for life-threatening conditions. Contact the helpline for guidance.
