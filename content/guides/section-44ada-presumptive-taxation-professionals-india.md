# Section 44ADA India 2026 — Presumptive Taxation for Professionals

If you're a doctor, lawyer, chartered accountant, architect, engineer, or technical consultant, Section 44ADA lets you simplify your tax filing dramatically: declare **50% of your gross receipts as your income** and pay tax on that — no need to maintain detailed books of accounts, no audit requirement.

## Who Is Covered Under Section 44ADA?

**Eligible professionals** (as per Section 44AA):
- Doctor (including hospital owner)
- Lawyer / Advocate
- Chartered Accountant (CA)
- Company Secretary (CS)
- Cost Accountant (CWA)
- Architect
- Engineer (consulting)
- Technical Consultant
- Interior Decorator
- Any other notified profession

**Not covered**: Software professionals who work as employees (they have salary income). But **freelance software developers / IT consultants** working as self-employed are covered under "technical consultant."

## The Core Benefit

| Feature | Regular Tax | Section 44ADA |
|---------|------------|--------------|
| Books of accounts | Must maintain | Not required |
| Audit | Required if receipts >₹75L | Not required (up to ₹75L) |
| Income declared | Actual profit (revenue minus all expenses) | 50% of gross receipts (automatic) |
| Advance tax | 4 installments | Single installment by March 15 |
| Form | ITR-3 | **ITR-4 (Sugam)** — simpler |

## Threshold Limits (FY 2025-26, AY 2026-27)

| Condition | Limit |
|-----------|-------|
| Gross receipts for 44ADA eligibility | **≤ ₹75 lakh** |
| If receipts are above ₹75L | Must use regular accounting (ITR-3) |
| Cash receipts threshold | If >5% of total receipts is cash: separate audit trigger (Tax Audit applies when cash exceeds 5% and total > ₹75L... actually for 44ADA, the ₹75L threshold is uniform) |

**Budget 2023 change**: Threshold raised from ₹50L to ₹75L. For FY 2025-26, the ₹75L limit applies.

## How Section 44ADA Works

**Step 1**: Calculate your total professional receipts (all fees received during FY 2025-26).

**Step 2**: Declare 50% of receipts as your taxable income.
- If receipts = ₹40L → Declare ₹20L as income
- The other ₹20L is assumed as your expenses — whether actual or not

**Step 3**: Pay tax on ₹20L at applicable slab rates (new or old regime).

**Step 4**: You can declare MORE than 50% if your actual profit is higher — but you cannot declare less than 50% without opting out of the scheme.

## What About Actual Expenses?

Under 44ADA, you **don't need to prove your expenses**. The 50% deduction is **presumed** — whether your actual expenses are 20% or 60%, the law says 50% is your "presumed" cost.

**If actual profit < 50%** (your actual expenses are more than 50%): You can still declare 50% — you can't declare less than 50% under this scheme. If your actual margin is below 50%, opting into 44ADA means paying more tax than necessary. In this case, opt out and do regular accounting.

**If actual profit > 50%** (low expenses): Declare the actual higher profit. 44ADA just sets the minimum floor.

## Advance Tax Simplification

Normal taxpayers: 4 advance tax installments (June 15 / September 15 / December 15 / March 15).

Under Section 44ADA: Only **one installment — 100% by March 15** of the financial year.

This is a major cash flow benefit. You estimate income at year-end and pay once, not across 4 quarters.

## What You MUST Do Under 44ADA

Even without books, you must:
1. Maintain receipts/invoices (in case of scrutiny)
2. File ITR-4 (not ITR-1 or ITR-3) by **July 31** of the AY
3. Pay advance tax (100% by March 15)
4. Collect TDS certificates from clients (clients who pay >₹30,000/year must deduct 10% TDS — claim this in ITR)

## TDS on Professional Fees

If any client pays you more than ₹30,000 in a year, they deduct **10% TDS** under Section 194J.

- You receive: ₹90,000 (after 10% TDS deducted on ₹1,00,000)
- Your gross receipt for 44ADA: ₹1,00,000 (full amount, before TDS)
- TDS of ₹10,000 shows in Form 26AS — you claim it against tax liability in ITR

Even if TDS was deducted by the client, you declare the gross amount as your receipt.

## When Should You NOT Use Section 44ADA?

### Case 1: Actual profit below 50%
If your professional business has high expenses (staff, office rent, equipment), and your actual profit margin is 25–30%, opting into 44ADA forces you to pay tax on 50% — overpaying.

### Case 2: Want to Claim Losses
Under 44ADA, if you want to carry forward business losses, you can't use this scheme. Regular books are required for loss carry-forward.

### Case 3: Receipts above ₹75L
Above ₹75L, 44ADA doesn't apply — you must maintain books and get a tax audit.

### Case 4: Partnership Firms / Companies
Section 44ADA applies only to **individuals and HUFs**. Partnerships, LLPs, and companies are excluded.

## Practical Example

**Dr. Priya (Dermatologist, self-employed clinic, FY 2025-26)**:
- Gross receipts: ₹55,00,000
- Actual expenses (staff, medicines, rent, equipment): ₹30,00,000
- Actual profit: ₹25,00,000 (45%)

Under 44ADA:
- Declared income: 50% of ₹55L = **₹27,50,000**
- Tax (new regime, after standard deduction): On ₹26,75,000
- Tax: ~₹5,37,500

If she used regular books:
- Actual profit: ₹25,00,000
- Tax would be lower

**Decision**: Regular accounting gives lower tax (since actual margin is 45% < 50%). 44ADA is not optimal here.

**Better candidate**: Architect Rahul with ₹30L receipts, ₹3L expenses (95% margin). Under 44ADA, declares ₹15L income. Under regular: declares ₹27L income. 44ADA saves significantly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I'm a freelance IT developer. Does 44ADA apply to me?

Yes — software/IT consultants are covered as "technical consultants" under Section 44ADA, provided they work as self-employed (not as an employee). If your gross receipts are ≤ ₹75L, you can opt for 44ADA.

### My client deducted TDS at 10%. My actual income tax is lower. How do I get the refund?

File ITR-4 declaring 50% of gross receipts as income. Add TDS credit from clients (shown in Form 26AS). If TDS credit > actual tax liability, you get a refund. The refund is processed after ITR is filed and verified.

### Can I switch between 44ADA and regular taxation every year?

Yes — you can opt in or out of 44ADA each year (unlike 44AD for businesses, which has a 5-year lock-in if you opt out). Each year, professionals decide whether 44ADA makes sense based on their income structure.
