## What is Annasaheb Patil Loan Scheme?

**Annasaheb Patil Arthik Vikas Mahamandal (APAVMC)** is a Maharashtra government corporation that provides **interest-free or interest-subsidy loans** to individuals from the **Maratha (and general/unreserved) community** for setting up businesses, self-employment, and education. The scheme was established specifically to address the economic needs of the Maratha community and other OBC communities not covered by existing backward class schemes.

---

## Key Loan Products

| Loan Type | Amount | Terms |
|-----------|--------|-------|
| Individual business loan | ₹10 lakh | 0% interest (interest paid by govt) |
| Group/SHG business loan | ₹20 lakh | 0% interest (group of 5+) |
| Large project loan | Up to ₹50 lakh | Interest subsidy (5–6% reimbursement) |
| Education loan guarantee | Up to ₹5 lakh | Government guarantee; interest subsidy |

---

## Who is Eligible?

**For APAVMC loans:**
- Belongs to **Maratha / Kunbi / Kunbi-Maratha** community OR **open category (unreserved)** residents not covered by SC/ST/OBC schemes
- **Maharashtra resident** — domicile of 15 years
- Age **18–50 years**
- No existing loan default with any nationalized bank
- Annual family income **below ₹8 lakh/year**

**For self-employment loans (individual):**
- Applicant must have a **business plan** or existing small business
- Business should be within Maharashtra

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card**
2. **Domicile certificate** (Maharashtra — 15 years)
3. **Caste certificate** — Maratha/Kunbi OR non-backward class certificate for unreserved
4. **Income certificate** (annual income < ₹8 lakh)
5. **Business plan / project report** (prepared or with help of APAVMC)
6. **Bank statement** (last 6 months)
7. **Education proof**
8. **Quotations** for machinery/equipment (if applicable)

## How to Apply

### Step 1 — Online Application

Go to **[mahaswayam.gov.in](https://mahaswayam.gov.in)** — Maharashtra's employment and self-employment portal:
- Login with Aadhaar
- Navigate to APAVMC / Annasaheb Patil Loan section
- Fill application with personal, business, and income details
- Upload documents

### Step 2 — District Office Verification

APAVMC district office verifies caste, domicile, and income documents.

### Step 3 — Business Plan Review

For loans above ₹5 lakh, a committee reviews the business plan. You may be called for an interview.

### Step 4 — Bank Loan Sanction

APAVMC facilitates the loan through empanelled banks:
- Bank sanctions the loan
- APAVMC pays the interest on your behalf to the bank

### Step 5 — Disbursement

Loan amount disbursed to your bank account or directly to vendor/supplier.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. I am from the Maratha community but my family income is ₹10 lakh/year. Am I eligible?**

The income limit is ₹8 lakh/year. At ₹10 lakh, you exceed the limit. Some APAVMC loan products may have higher income caps — check current year's guidelines at mahaswayam.gov.in.

**2. Is this scheme only for Marathas or for other communities too?**

The scheme is for Maratha community AND the unreserved/open category — those who are not covered under SC/ST/OBC reservation. This is a key distinction.

**3. I already have a bank loan. Can I take an APAVMC loan too?**

You can take an APAVMC loan if you do not have a default on any existing loan. Existing loans should be regular (no overdue). APAVMC facilitates the loan through a bank — the bank will check your CIBIL score.

**4. Can women apply for APAVMC loans?**

Yes. Women from the eligible communities can apply. Women-run businesses may get additional preference and relaxation in documentation.

**5. What is Mahaswayam portal?**

Mahaswayam (mahaswayam.gov.in) is Maharashtra's integrated portal for skill training, employment, and self-employment schemes including APAVMC, Startup Maharashtra, and other business loan schemes.
