# Aadhaar Address Update Online Not Working — How to Fix

Updating your address on Aadhaar online through the SSUP (Self-Service Update Portal) or myAadhaar portal should be straightforward, but many residents face errors at different stages — from OTP generation failures to document rejections and requests stuck at "in progress" for weeks. This guide walks through **every common problem** and its fix so you can successfully update your Aadhaar address.

## Common Errors and How to Fix Them

### 1. SSUP Portal Not Loading or Showing Errors

**Symptoms:**
- "Service temporarily unavailable" message
- Page loads but buttons don't respond
- Blank screen or timeout error after login

**Fixes:**
- **Clear browser cache and cookies**, then retry. Use Chrome or Edge (latest version).
- **Disable browser extensions** — ad blockers and VPNs can interfere with UIDAI portals.
- **Try during off-peak hours** — SSUP handles heavy traffic. Best time: early morning (6–9 AM) or late evening (after 9 PM).
- **Check UIDAI server status** — if the portal is down for maintenance, wait and retry later. UIDAI sometimes announces downtime on [uidai.gov.in](https://uidai.gov.in).
- Use the **myAadhaar portal** ([myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in)) as an alternative — it supports address update and is often more stable than SSUP.

### 2. OTP Not Received or OTP Verification Failed

**Symptoms:**
- OTP sent but not received on registered mobile
- "OTP generation failed" error
- "Invalid OTP" even when entered correctly

**Fixes:**
- Ensure your **mobile number is linked to Aadhaar**. If not, you must visit an Aadhaar centre to update your mobile first — online OTP-based update won't work without a registered number.
- Wait at least **2–3 minutes** before requesting a new OTP. Don't spam the resend button.
- Check if your **phone has SMS storage space** and network signal.
- If OTP arrives late, note that it is **valid for 10 minutes** from generation.
- If the issue persists, try again after 24 hours — UIDAI may temporarily block OTP after multiple failed attempts.

> 📖 **Detailed fix:** [Aadhaar OTP Not Received — Complete Fix Guide](/guides/aadhaar-otp-not-received-fix)

### 3. Address Proof Document Rejected

**Symptoms:**
- "Invalid document" or "Document not accepted"
- "Document not readable" error
- Upload succeeds but request rejected later

**Fixes:**
- **Use only UIDAI-approved address proof documents.** Accepted documents include:
  - Passport
  - Bank statement or passbook (less than 3 months old)
  - Voter ID
  - Driving licence
  - Electricity / water / gas bill (less than 3 months old)
  - Property tax receipt
  - Government-issued address proof letter
  - Ration card
- **Scan at 300 DPI minimum** in JPEG or PNG format, under **2 MB** file size.
- Ensure all **four corners are visible**, text is legible, and the document is not cropped or blurred.
- The address on the document must **exactly match** what you type in the update form — including pin code, spelling, and locality name.

> 📖 **Related:** [Aadhaar Address Update Rejected — Reasons & Fix](/guides/aadhaar-address-update-rejected-fix)

### 4. VID (Virtual ID) Not Accepted

**Symptoms:**
- "Invalid VID" error when entering Virtual ID
- VID field shows validation error

**Fixes:**
- **Generate a fresh VID** from [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in) → "VID Generator". Old VIDs expire after a set period or when a new one is generated.
- Ensure you're entering the **16-digit VID**, not your 12-digit Aadhaar number, in the VID field.
- If VID generation itself fails, use your **Aadhaar number directly** (where the portal allows it) or try regenerating after some time.
- VID works only when your **mobile number is registered** with Aadhaar.

### 5. Landlord Validation Request Failed

**Symptoms:**
- "Landlord verification request failed" error
- Landlord didn't receive the validation request
- Landlord approved but address update still not processed

**Fixes:**
- The **Address Validation Letter** feature requires the landlord (Head of Family) to have their **mobile number linked** to their Aadhaar.
- Ensure the landlord's **Aadhaar number is entered correctly** in the request form.
- The landlord must **approve the request within 30 days** via the link/OTP sent to their registered mobile. If expired, you must re-initiate.
- If the landlord's mobile number is not linked to Aadhaar or they're unable to complete verification, **use a different address proof method** instead — upload a valid document directly.
- This feature has limited success rates. If it fails repeatedly, **visit an Aadhaar Enrolment Centre** with your landlord and both your Aadhaar cards.

### 6. Request Stuck at "In Progress" for Weeks

**Symptoms:**
- Update Request Number (URN) shows status "in progress" for more than 30 days
- No rejection or success notification received

**Fixes:**
- UIDAI typically processes online address updates within **7–30 days**. During peak periods, it may take longer.
- **Track your request** using the URN:
  - Visit [resident.uidai.gov.in/aadhaar-update-status](https://resident.uidai.gov.in/aadhaar-update-status)
  - Or use the **myAadhaar app** → "Check Update Status"
- If stuck beyond **90 days**, file a complaint:
  - Call **UIDAI helpline: 1947**
  - Email: help@uidai.gov.in
  - Use the **grievance portal** at [uidai.gov.in](https://uidai.gov.in) → "File a Complaint"
- If the request was silently rejected (no notification), re-apply with a clearer document or visit an Aadhaar centre.

### 7. Payment Failed but Amount Deducted

**Symptoms:**
- ₹50 address update fee deducted but transaction shows failed
- "Payment not received" error on portal

**Fixes:**
- Wait **48–72 hours** — most failed transactions are auto-refunded.
- If not refunded, note the **transaction reference number** and contact your bank.
- You can also raise a complaint on UIDAI helpline **1947** with your URN and payment details.
- Retry the update after the refund is processed.

## How to Track Aadhaar Address Update Status

1. Visit [resident.uidai.gov.in/aadhaar-update-status](https://resident.uidai.gov.in/aadhaar-update-status)
2. Enter your **URN (Update Request Number)** — this was sent to your registered mobile/email when you submitted the request
3. Enter your **Aadhaar number or VID** and the captcha
4. Click **Check Status**
5. Possible statuses:
   - **Submitted** — request received, processing not started
   - **In Progress** — under review
   - **Completed** — address updated successfully; download updated e-Aadhaar
   - **Rejected** — document or data issue; re-apply with correct documents

## Offline Alternative: Update at Aadhaar Enrolment Centre

If the online method keeps failing, visit an Aadhaar centre:

1. **Book an appointment** at [appointments.uidai.gov.in](https://appointments.uidai.gov.in) (walk-in also accepted at most centres)
2. **Carry original + photocopy** of your address proof document
3. **Fee:** ₹50 (paid at the centre)
4. Biometric verification is done on the spot — no OTP needed
5. You'll receive a URN; address update is typically processed within **7–15 days**
6. This method has a **higher success rate** than online, especially for landlord-based or complex address changes

> 💡 **Tip:** If you don't have any address proof in your name, carry your landlord along with their Aadhaar and a **rental agreement or NOC** to the centre.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I update my Aadhaar address without a registered mobile number?
No, online address update requires OTP on your registered mobile. If your mobile is not linked, visit an Aadhaar Enrolment Centre where biometric verification is used instead.

### How many times can I update my address on Aadhaar?
There is **no limit** on the number of address updates. However, each online update costs ₹50.

### Can I use someone else's utility bill as address proof?
Yes, if you're living at that address. You can use the **Address Validation Letter** method where the bill holder (landlord/family member) verifies your address via their Aadhaar-linked mobile.

### My address update was rejected. Can I re-apply immediately?
Yes, you can re-apply right away. Fix the issue that caused rejection — typically a document quality or mismatch problem — before resubmitting.

### Is the ₹50 fee refunded if my update is rejected?
No, the fee is non-refundable regardless of the outcome. Ensure your documents are correct before submitting.
