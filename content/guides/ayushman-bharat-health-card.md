## What is Ayushman Bharat (PMJAY)?

Ayushman Bharat - Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PMJAY) is the world's largest health insurance scheme by the Government of India. It provides **₹5 lakh free health coverage per family per year** for secondary and tertiary hospitalization.

## Key Benefits

- **₹5 lakh health cover** per family per year
- **Cashless treatment** at empaneled hospitals
- Covers **1,949+ treatment packages** including surgery, day care, and diagnostics
- **No cap on family size** — entire family covered
- **Pre-existing diseases covered** from day one
- **No enrollment fee** — completely free
- Works across India — **portability** between states

## Who is Eligible?

Eligibility is based on **SECC 2011** (Socio-Economic Caste Census) data:

### Rural Families (any one criterion):
- Households with only one room, kucha walls, and kucha roof
- No adult member aged 16-59
- Female-headed household with no adult male member aged 16-59
- Disabled member and no able-bodied adult
- SC/ST households
- Landless households earning majority from manual casual labour
- Destitute / living on alms
- Manual scavenger families
- Primitive tribal groups
- Legally released bonded labour

### Urban Families (occupational categories):
- Rag picker
- Beggar
- Domestic worker
- Street vendor / cobbler / hawker
- Construction worker / plumber / mason / painter
- Sweeper / sanitation worker / gardener
- Home-based artisan / handicraft worker
- Transport worker (driver, conductor, rickshaw puller)
- Shop worker / assistant / waiter / mechanic

> **Recently expanded:** Senior citizens (70+) are now covered regardless of income under the expanded Ayushman Bharat scheme.

## How to Check Eligibility

### Method 1: Online Portal
1. Visit [pmjay.gov.in](https://pmjay.gov.in) → **"Am I Eligible?"**
2. Enter mobile number → Get OTP
3. Enter name, state, and other details
4. System checks against SECC database

### Method 2: Helpline
Call **14555** (toll-free) — Check eligibility over phone

### Method 3: Visit CSC
Go to nearest **Common Service Center** with Aadhaar card

## How to Get Ayushman Bharat Health Card

### Step 1: Check Eligibility (see above)

### Step 2: Visit Nearest Point
- **Empaneled hospital** — Arogya Mitra desk
- **Common Service Center (CSC)**
- **PMJAY Kiosk** at government hospitals

### Step 3: Carry Documents
- **Aadhaar Card** (mandatory)
- **Ration Card** (if available)
- **SECC/RSBY card** (if available)

### Step 4: e-KYC Verification
- Aadhaar-based biometric verification (fingerprint)
- Family details verified against SECC database

### Step 5: Card Generated
- **Ayushman Card** generated on the spot or within a few days
- Card has a unique **PMJAY ID** for each family member
- Download from [beneficiary.nha.gov.in](https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in)

## How to Download Ayushman Card Online

1. Visit [beneficiary.nha.gov.in](https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in)
2. Login with **Aadhaar + OTP** or **PMJAY ID**
3. Click **"Download Card"**
4. Verify via OTP
5. Download and print your Ayushman card

## How to Use — Getting Cashless Treatment

### Step 1: Find Empaneled Hospital
Visit [pmjay.gov.in](https://pmjay.gov.in) → **"Find Hospital"** → Search by state, district, and specialty

### Step 2: Visit Hospital with Ayushman Card
- Go to the **Ayushman Mitra / Help Desk** at the hospital
- Show your Ayushman card or Aadhaar

### Step 3: Pre-Authorization
- Hospital verifies your identity
- Submits treatment request to insurance company
- **Pre-authorization approved** (usually within hours)

### Step 4: Cashless Treatment
- Receive treatment — **no payment required**
- Hospital bills the insurance company directly
- You sign discharge form after treatment

## What's Covered?

- **1,949+ packages** including:
  - Heart surgeries (bypass, angioplasty)
  - Joint replacements
  - Cancer treatment
  - Kidney dialysis
  - Cataract surgery
  - Maternity and newborn care
  - All medical, surgical, and day-care treatments

### What's NOT Covered?
- OPD (outpatient consultations)
- Fertility treatment
- Cosmetic surgery
- Organ transplant

## Important Tips

1. **Get your card made before you need it** — Don't wait for an emergency
2. **Check hospital empanelment** before going — Not all hospitals accept PMJAY
3. **Senior citizens (70+)** — Check if you're eligible under the expanded scheme even if not in SECC
4. **Carry Aadhaar always** — Hospitals verify identity through Aadhaar
5. **Report fraud** — Call 14555 if a hospital demands payment or denies treatment

## FAQs

**Q: How do I check if I'm eligible for Ayushman Bharat?**
A: Visit pmjay.gov.in → "Am I Eligible?" → Enter mobile number and details. Or call 14555.

**Q: Is there any fee for Ayushman Bharat card?**
A: No. The card and all services are completely free.

**Q: How much health cover does Ayushman Bharat provide?**
A: ₹5 lakh per family per year for hospitalization at empaneled hospitals.

**Q: Can I use Ayushman card in a private hospital?**
A: Yes, if the private hospital is empaneled under PMJAY. Check the hospital list on pmjay.gov.in.

**Q: Is Ayushman Bharat available for senior citizens?**
A: Yes. The scheme has been expanded to cover all senior citizens aged 70 and above, regardless of income or SECC status.

**Q: What if the hospital refuses to provide cashless treatment?**
A: Call the PMJAY helpline 14555 immediately. Hospitals empaneled under PMJAY cannot deny treatment to eligible beneficiaries.
