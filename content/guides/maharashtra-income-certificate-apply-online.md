# Maharashtra Income Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **income certificate** in Maharashtra is officially called **Utkanna Praman Patra** (उत्पन्नाचे प्रमाणपत्र). It is issued by the Revenue Department of Maharashtra and is essential for scholarships, OBC/NT non-creamy layer certificates, government scheme eligibility, and admission fee waivers. Applications are processed through **Aaple Sarkar** — Maharashtra's multi-service citizen portal.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in](https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in) → Revenue Department → Income Certificate. Typically processed in **15–30 days** by Tahsildar.

---

## Why You Need Maharashtra Income Certificate

- **Scholarship applications** — state and national (NSP, mahadbtonline, state scholarship portal)
- **OBC/NT Non-Creamy Layer certificate** — income criterion required
- **EWS (Economically Weaker Section)** reservation eligibility
- **Government scheme benefits** — PMAY, PM Kisan, etc.
- **Fee concession** in colleges (income below ₹1 lakh/year)
- **Legal affidavits** and court proceedings

---

## Income Certificate vs Non-Creamy Layer Certificate in Maharashtra

| Certificate | Purpose | Authority |
|-------------|---------|-----------|
| **Income Certificate (Utkanna Praman Patra)** | Proves annual family income | Tahsildar |
| **Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) Certificate** | OBC families below income ceiling | Tahsildar |
| **EWS Certificate** | General category income < ₹8 lakh/year | SDO/Tahsildar |

> The **Income Certificate** is needed to obtain the **NCL certificate** for OBC and NT categories.

---

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (all family members — mandatory)
- **Ration card** (shows family composition)
- **7/12 extract** (Satbara — for agricultural income)
- Property tax receipt (for urban areas)
- **Salary certificate / Employer letter** (if salaried)
- **Form 16 / ITR** (if income taxpayer)
- Photograph
- Self-declaration of income (Affidavit or online declaration)

## How to Apply Online — Aaple Sarkar Portal

1. Go to [aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in](https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in)
2. Click **"New User? Register here"** → Register with mobile number (OTP)
3. Log in with registered mobile + OTP
4. Go to **"Revenue Department"** → **"Income Certificate"** (Utkanna Praman Patra)
5. Fill the application form:
   - Name, address, district, taluka, village
   - Annual family income (all sources)
   - Purpose of certificate
6. Upload required documents
7. Pay the fee: **₹15–35** (online via Aaple Sarkar)
8. Submit → note the **Application Reference Number**

---

## How to Apply via Setuseva / CSC

Maharashtra's **Setuseva centres** (also called Aaple Sarkar Seva Kendras) assist with online applications:

1. Visit nearest **Setuseva centre** or **CSC** in your taluka
2. Provide Aadhaar, income documents, and ration card
3. Operator fills and submits the form
4. Service charge: approximately ₹40–60
5. Collect acknowledgement slip

---

## Status Check

1. Go to [aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in](https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in)
2. Click **"Track Application"** → Enter application reference number
3. Or log in → **"My Applications"** → view status
4. Status: Submitted → Under Verification → Approved / Rejected

---

## Download Income Certificate Maharashtra

Once approved:
1. Log in to Aaple Sarkar portal
2. Go to **"My Applications"** → Click the approved income certificate
3. Click **"Download Certificate"**
4. PDF is digitally signed with government seal — valid for all official purposes
5. Verify at Aaple Sarkar → "Verify Certificate" using certificate number

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What income is counted for Maharashtra income certificate?

Total annual income of **all earning family members** (head of family + spouse + earning children). Sources: salary, business profit, agricultural income, rent income, pension. Non-earning student siblings are not counted.

### What is the income limit for Maharashtra scholarship?

For state scholarships, income limit varies: typically ₹1 lakh/year (some schemes up to ₹2.5 lakh). Check your specific scholarship scheme at [mahadbtmahait.gov.in](https://mahadbtmahait.gov.in).

### Can I get income certificate at district collectorate?

Yes — you can apply in person at the **Tahsildar office** in your taluka. Submit paper application with all documents. Process is same but online via Aaple Sarkar is faster.

### Income certificate rejected Maharashtra — what to do?

Check rejection reason in Aaple Sarkar → "My Applications". Common reasons: Satbara not attached (for agricultural families), income declaration mismatch, documents blurry. Correct and re-apply. You can also appeal to the Collector's office.
