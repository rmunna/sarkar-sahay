## What is Duare Sarkar?

**Duare Sarkar** (meaning "Government at Your Doorstep") is West Bengal government's initiative to bring **18+ government scheme services** directly to citizens through camps organized at panchayat offices, schools, and community centres. Rather than citizens travelling to multiple government offices, officials come to the neighbourhood.

Services are available at **Duare Sarkar camps** held periodically across the state.

## Who Can Use Duare Sarkar?

**All West Bengal residents.** The camps are open to everyone. No prior registration is needed to attend.

---

## How to Use Duare Sarkar

### Step 1 — Find the Camp Near You

Duare Sarkar camps are held periodically — typically in phases (1–3 months long campaigns). Check camp schedules at:
- **[bangla.wb.gov.in](https://bangla.wb.gov.in)**
- Your local **Gram Panchayat office**
- Your **BDO office**
- News announcements on West Bengal government portals

### Step 2 — Visit the Camp with Documents

At each Duare Sarkar camp:
- Different service stalls are set up
- Officials for each scheme are present
- You approach the relevant stall for the service you need

**Commonly needed documents:**
- Aadhaar Card
- Voter ID
- Ration card
- Bank passbook
- Relevant scheme-specific documents

### Step 3 — Apply at the Camp

Officials fill forms on your behalf, verify documents, and submit applications on the spot. For some services (like Aadhaar update or caste certificate), the process is completed at the camp itself.

### Step 4 — Receive Confirmation

You get an application receipt or a service confirmation. Scheme benefits (pensions, insurance, grants) follow the regular processing timelines after camp enrollment.

## Laxmi Bhandar — Key New Service at Duare Sarkar

**Laxmi Bhandar** is West Bengal's popular women's financial assistance scheme:
- ₹500/month for general category women heads of household
- ₹1,000/month for SC/ST women heads of household

Age: 25–60 years. No income limit. Application is done at Duare Sarkar camps using Aadhaar and bank account details.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. I missed the last Duare Sarkar camp. When is the next one?**

Duare Sarkar runs in phases — typically 2–3 times a year. Check [bangla.wb.gov.in](https://bangla.wb.gov.in) or your panchayat notice board for the next phase schedule.

**2. Can I apply for Swasthya Sathi (health insurance) only at Duare Sarkar?**

No. Swasthya Sathi applications are also accepted at District Health and Family Welfare Samiti offices and online. But Duare Sarkar camps are the most convenient option for rural residents.

**3. Is the Duare Sarkar service really free? Some camps charge money.**

All Duare Sarkar services are **free of cost**. If anyone demands money at a camp, refuse and report to the camp supervisor or your BDO office.

**4. I applied at the camp for pension. How do I know if I am approved?**

Check your application status at the relevant department portal (e.g., Kanyashree: [wbkanyashree.gov.in](https://wbkanyashree.gov.in), pension: District Social Welfare office). The camp gives you an application receipt number for tracking.

**5. My village is not near any panchayat office. Will Duare Sarkar come to my area?**

Duare Sarkar camps are organized at panchayat and ward levels across all districts of West Bengal. Contact your nearest BDO office for the specific schedule for your panchayat.
