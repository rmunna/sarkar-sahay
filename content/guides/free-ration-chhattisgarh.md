**Chhattisgarh's free ration scheme** operates under the **National Food Security Act (NFSA), 2013** and the state's own food security provisions. Chhattisgarh is known for its robust Public Distribution System (PDS), providing **rice at ₹1 per kg** (among the cheapest in India) and free food grains under PMGKAY to millions of families. The state covers nearly 80% of its rural and 60% of its urban population under NFSA.

## What is the Chhattisgarh Free Ration Scheme?

Chhattisgarh provides food grains to eligible families through a network of Fair Price Shops (FPS) using Aadhaar-authenticated PDS. Under the state's food security initiative, **rice is provided at just ₹1 per kg** to eligible ration card holders. Additionally, under PMGKAY provisions, **5 kg free food grains per person per month** are provided to NFSA beneficiaries. Antyodaya families receive 35 kg per household monthly.

## Types of Ration Cards in Chhattisgarh

| Card Type | Entitlement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) | 35 kg rice per household/month |
| Priority Household (PHH) | 5 kg per person per month |
| Above Poverty Line (APL) | Subsidised grains based on availability |

> Chhattisgarh also provides additional items like salt, pulses and fortified rice through its PDS.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Permanent resident** of Chhattisgarh
- **BPL/AAY families:** Destitute households, landless labourers, widows, disabled persons, elderly-headed households
- **PHH families:** Identified through SECC or state-level surveys
- Must possess a valid **ration card** issued by the CG Food & Civil Supplies Department
- **Annual family income** within prescribed state limits
- Must not be an income tax payer or government employee (for BPL/AAY)

## Documents Required

### For New Ration Card
- Aadhaar card of family head and all members
- Income certificate
- Address proof (electricity bill, rent agreement)
- Passport-size photograph of family head
- Self-declaration form
- Mobile number linked to Aadhaar
- Bank account details

## Step-by-Step Process

### Apply for Ration Card

1. **Visit** the Chhattisgarh Food Department portal at [khadya.cg.nic.in](https://khadya.cg.nic.in/)
2. **Navigate** to the ration card application section
3. **Fill** the online form or download the application for offline submission
4. **Alternatively**, visit your nearest **Lok Seva Kendra** or district Food Office
5. **Submit** the application with required documents
6. **Field verification** is conducted by the food inspector
7. **Ration card issued** within 15–30 days after successful verification
8. **Card is linked** to your designated Fair Price Shop

### Check Beneficiary List

1. **Visit** [khadya.cg.nic.in](https://khadya.cg.nic.in/)
2. **Click** on "Ration Card Details" or "NFSA Beneficiary List"
3. **Select** your district, block and FPS
4. **Search** by name or ration card number
5. **Download** or view your ration card details and entitlements

### Collect Monthly Ration

1. **Visit** your designated Fair Price Shop on the distribution day
2. **Carry** your ration card and Aadhaar card
3. **Authenticate** via biometric (fingerprint/iris) at the ePoS machine
4. **Collect** your entitled food grains — rice, salt, pulses
5. **Verify** the quantity before leaving

## Fees

- **Ration card application:** Free
- **Rice:** ₹1 per kg (state subsidised rate) for eligible families
- **Under PMGKAY:** Free for NFSA beneficiaries

## Processing Time

- **New ration card:** 15–30 days
- **Name addition/correction:** 7–15 days
- **Monthly distribution:** 1st–end of each month

## Important Tips

1. **Complete Aadhaar eKYC** for all family members — it's mandatory for biometric authentication at FPS
2. **Chhattisgarh's ₹1/kg rice** is one of the best food security programmes in India — ensure your ration card is active
3. **Report any FPS irregularity** on the toll-free helpline or the food department portal
4. **Use One Nation One Ration Card** if you migrate — collect your ration at any FPS across India
5. **Update your ration card** promptly for births, deaths or marriages in the family

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much rice do I get per month in Chhattisgarh?

PHH cardholders get **5 kg per person per month**. AAY families receive **35 kg per household per month**. Rice is provided at ₹1/kg under the state scheme or free under PMGKAY.

### How do I apply for a new ration card in Chhattisgarh?

Apply online at [khadya.cg.nic.in](https://khadya.cg.nic.in/) or visit your nearest Lok Seva Kendra/district Food Office with Aadhaar, income certificate and address proof.

### Can I transfer my ration card from another state to Chhattisgarh?

You need to surrender your old ration card in the previous state and apply for a new one in Chhattisgarh. Under ONORC, you can collect ration across states without transfer during the interim period.

### What items are distributed through PDS in Chhattisgarh?

Rice (primary grain), salt, pulses (dal), and fortified rice are distributed through the Chhattisgarh PDS system. Some FPS also provide iodised salt free of charge.

### What if the biometric authentication fails at the FPS?

If fingerprint authentication fails, **iris scan** is used as an alternative. If both fail, the FPS dealer can use **exception handling** through the ePoS system. Contact the district food office if issues persist.

### Is there a helpline for ration-related complaints?

Yes. Call the Chhattisgarh food helpline or use the grievance section on [khadya.cg.nic.in](https://khadya.cg.nic.in/). You can also contact the District Supply Officer directly.
