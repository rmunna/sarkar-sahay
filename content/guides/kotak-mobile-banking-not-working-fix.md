# Kotak Mobile Banking App Not Working? (2026 Fix Guide)

**Kotak Mobile Banking** (and the **811 by Kotak Mahindra Bank** app for digital-first accounts) are popular banking apps with over 10 crore users. Common issues include login failures, OTP delays, biometric failures, and the app not opening.

> **Quick check:** Call **1860-266-2666** or check @KotakBankLtd on Twitter/X for outage status before troubleshooting your device.

---

## Common Kotak Mobile Banking Issues

| Issue | Likely Cause |
|-------|-------------|
| Login failed | Wrong MPIN; account locked after multiple failures |
| OTP not received | Network issue; registered number not active |
| Black screen / app not loading | Outdated app; corrupted cache |
| Biometric not working | OS update; biometric permissions revoked |
| Transaction declined | Insufficient funds; transaction limit reached |
| App crashes on launch | Old version; phone storage full |
| 811 app showing error | Server issue; KYC pending |

## Fixes — Try in Order

### Fix 1: Check Kotak Server Status

- Check Twitter/X: @KotakBankLtd or search "Kotak app down"
- Check [downdetector.in](https://downdetector.in) → search "Kotak Mahindra Bank"
- If there's an outage, wait 15–60 minutes

### Fix 2: Update the App

1. Open **Google Play Store** or **App Store**
2. Search **"Kotak Mobile Banking"** or **"811 by Kotak"**
3. Tap **Update** if available
4. Relaunch and log in

### Fix 3: Clear App Cache (Android)

1. Go to **Settings → Apps → Kotak Mobile Banking** (or 811)
2. Tap **Storage → Clear Cache**
3. Reopen the app
4. **Do not** clear app data (you'll be logged out)

> On iOS: Delete and reinstall the app.

### Fix 4: OTP Not Received

- Ensure registered mobile number has **active SMS service**
- Disable **DND mode** temporarily
- Wait 3–5 minutes — OTP can be delayed in network congestion
- Request OTP again **only once** — multiple requests can lock the system
- If number has changed: call **1860-266-2666** to update before app login

### Fix 5: Reset MPIN

If MPIN is forgotten or account is locked:

1. On login screen → tap **"Forgot MPIN"** or **"Reset MPIN"**
2. Enter your **Debit Card number** and **Card Expiry / ATM PIN**
3. Enter OTP sent to registered number
4. Set new 6-digit MPIN

**Via Net Banking:**
- Login to [kotak.com](https://www.kotak.com) → NetBanking
- Go to **Service Requests → Mobile Banking → Reset MPIN**

### Fix 6: Re-register Biometric (Fingerprint / Face ID)

1. Open Kotak Mobile Banking → **Settings → Security**
2. **Disable fingerprint / Face ID** login
3. Log in manually with MPIN once
4. Re-enable biometric → rescan fingerprint

### Fix 7: Switch Networks

- Turn off Wi-Fi and use **4G mobile data**
- Or disable VPN (VPNs often interfere with banking apps)
- Try again

### Fix 8: Reinstall the App

If the above don't work:
1. **Uninstall** Kotak Mobile Banking or 811 app
2. Restart your phone
3. **Reinstall** from Play Store / App Store
4. Register again with your Customer ID, Aadhaar OTP, and new MPIN

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is Kotak Mobile Banking showing "User ID does not exist"?

Your Customer Relationship Number (CRN) / Customer ID might have been entered incorrectly. Find your CRN on your bank statement, welcome letter, or passbook. If the app still shows this error, call **1860-266-2666**.

### Kotak app transaction shows "Pending" for over 30 minutes — what to do?

**Do not retry the transaction** — check your statement first. If money is deducted but not credited to recipient, call customer care within 5 working days. Most pending UPI/NEFT transactions auto-settle within 30 minutes.

### My Kotak account is locked after too many wrong MPIN attempts — how to unlock?

Call **1860-266-2666** or visit your nearest Kotak Bank branch with your passbook and Aadhaar/PAN to reset MPIN and unlock the account.

### How to use Kotak banking if the app is down?

- Use **[kotak.com](https://www.kotak.com) NetBanking** for fund transfers, FD booking
- Use **Kotak ATM** for cash withdrawals and balance check
- Use **UPI apps** (GPay / PhonePe / Paytm) if your Kotak account is linked
