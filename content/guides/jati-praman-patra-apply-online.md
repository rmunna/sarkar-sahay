# Jati Praman Patra: How to Apply for Caste Certificate Online (State-Wise)

Jati Praman Patra (जाति प्रमाण पत्र) is the Hindi term for caste certificate — one of the most important documents in India for accessing reservation benefits in education, employment, and government welfare schemes. Whether you belong to SC, ST, OBC, or any other listed category, this guide covers the complete process to apply for your caste certificate online across all major Indian states.

## What is Jati Praman Patra (Caste Certificate)?

A Jati Praman Patra is a government-issued document that certifies a person's caste or community. It is issued by the revenue department of the state government, usually through the Tehsildar, Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM), or District Magistrate (DM).

### Why Do You Need a Caste Certificate?

- **Government job reservations** — SC (15%), ST (7.5%), OBC (27%) in central services
- **Educational admissions** — reserved seats in schools, colleges, IITs, IIMs, medical colleges
- **Scholarship eligibility** — Post Matric Scholarship, National Fellowship for SC/ST, etc.
- **Competitive exams** — age relaxation, fee exemption, and reserved vacancies
- **Government welfare schemes** — subsidized housing, loans, skill development
- **Political representation** — contesting elections from reserved constituencies

## Documents Required for Jati Praman Patra

| Document | Details |
|----------|---------|
| Aadhaar Card | Identity verification |
| Father's/family caste certificate | Primary evidence of caste |
| Birth certificate | Age and parentage proof |
| School records | TC/marksheet showing caste |
| Ration card | Family details |
| Address proof | Voter ID, utility bill, [domicile certificate](/guides/domicile-certificate-apply-online) |
| Passport photos | 2–4 recent photographs |
| Affidavit | Self-declaration if father's certificate unavailable |

## How to Apply for Jati Praman Patra Online

### Uttar Pradesh (e-District UP)

1. Visit [edistrict.up.gov.in](https://edistrict.up.gov.in/)
2. Register with mobile number and create login
3. Go to **"आवेदन करें"** → Select **"जाति प्रमाण पत्र"**
4. Fill the form with personal and caste details
5. Upload Aadhaar, photo, and supporting documents
6. Submit — no fee for SC/ST/OBC applicants
7. Track status under **"आवेदन की स्थिति"**
8. Download certificate once approved (usually 7–15 days)

### Bihar (ServiceOnline / RTPS)

1. Visit [serviceonline.bihar.gov.in](https://serviceonline.bihar.gov.in/)
2. Register/login
3. Select **"RTPS Services"** → **"Caste Certificate"**
4. Choose the issuing level (Block, Sub-Division, or District)
5. Fill the form and upload documents
6. Submit — typically free of charge
7. Certificate issued within the RTPS timeline (3–15 days depending on level)

### Maharashtra (Aaple Sarkar)

1. Visit [aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in](https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in/)
2. Login with registered mobile or create account
3. Select **Revenue Department** → **"Caste Certificate"**
4. Fill in details, upload documents
5. Pay fee (₹15–25)
6. Submit and track using application ID

### Madhya Pradesh (MP e-District)

1. Visit [mpedistrict.gov.in](https://mpedistrict.gov.in/)
2. Register with Samagra ID or Aadhaar
3. Select **"जाति प्रमाण पत्र"**
4. Fill form, upload documents
5. Submit (free for SC/ST/OBC)
6. Track and download from the portal

### Rajasthan (e-Mitra)

1. Visit [emitra.rajasthan.gov.in](https://emitra.rajasthan.gov.in/)
2. Login with Jan Aadhaar or SSO ID
3. Select Caste Certificate service
4. Fill form and upload documents
5. Pay fee and submit
6. Alternatively, visit any e-Mitra kiosk for assisted application

## Offline Application Process

1. Visit the Tehsildar / SDM / Block office
2. Obtain the caste certificate application form
3. Fill in all details including exact caste name as per government list
4. Attach self-attested copies of all documents
5. Submit with an affidavit if father's caste certificate is unavailable
6. Receive acknowledgement receipt
7. Field verification may be conducted by Patwari/Lekhpal
8. Certificate issued after 15–30 days

## How to Download and Verify Caste Certificate

Once your application is approved:

1. Login to the respective state e-District portal
2. Go to **"Track Application"** or **"Download Certificate"**
3. Enter your application number
4. Download the digitally signed certificate in PDF format
5. The certificate is also available on [DigiLocker](https://digitallocker.gov.in/) for most states
6. Verify using the QR code or verification link printed on the certificate

## Common Issues and Fixes

- **Application rejected:** Reapply with additional supporting documents; common reasons include missing father's certificate or caste name mismatch
- **Caste not in list:** Ensure you're checking the correct state list — caste classifications vary by state
- **Spelling errors:** Apply for correction through the same portal or visit the issuing office
- **Long delays:** File an RTI application or escalate through the state grievance portal

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. जाति प्रमाण पत्र कितने दिन में बनता है? (How long does a caste certificate take?)

Online applications are processed within 7–15 days in most states. Bihar's RTPS service at block level can issue it in 3–7 days. Offline processing takes 15–30 days.

### 2. Can I apply for caste certificate without father's caste certificate?

Yes. You can submit an affidavit, school records showing caste, and community verification. The process may take longer as additional verification is required.

### 3. Is the caste certificate valid for life?

In most states, the caste certificate has no expiry and is valid permanently. However, some states issue it with a validity period, and for certain jobs, fresh certificates may be requested.

### 4. Can I apply for caste certificate from a different state?

No. You must apply from the state where your family originates and where your caste is listed. If you've migrated, apply from your home state.

### 5. What is the difference between Jati Praman Patra and Caste Validity Certificate?

Jati Praman Patra is the basic caste certificate issued by the revenue department. Caste Validity Certificate is an additional verification done by the Caste Scrutiny Committee (especially in Maharashtra) and is required for government job appointments.

### 6. Is there any fee for caste certificate?

Most states issue caste certificates free for SC/ST/OBC applicants. Some states charge a nominal fee of ₹10–50.
