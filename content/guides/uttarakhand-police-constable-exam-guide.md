# Uttarakhand Police Constable Exam Guide — Eligibility, Pattern & Preparation Tips

The Uttarakhand Police Recruitment Board conducts the recruitment exam for Police Constable posts in Uttarakhand. This is one of the most popular government jobs in the state, attracting lakhs of candidates every recruitment cycle.

Uttarakhand Police Constable offers a stable government career with regular salary, social respect, and opportunities for departmental promotion to Head Constable and beyond.

## Eligibility Criteria

### Age Limit

| Category | Minimum Age | Maximum Age |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| General | 18 years | 25 years |
| OBC | 18 years | 28 years |
| SC/ST | 18 years | 30 years |

*Age relaxation as per Uttarakhand Government norms.*

### Educational Qualification

- **Minimum:** Intermediate (12th pass) from a recognized board
- Any stream accepted

### Nationality & Domicile

- Must be a citizen of India
- Domicile of Uttarakhand (for certain reserved categories)

### Physical Standards

**For Male Candidates:**

| Parameter | General/OBC/SC | ST |
|-----------|---------------|-----|
| Height | 165 cm | 160 cm |
| Chest (unexpanded) | 79 cm | 77 cm |
| Chest (expanded) | 84 cm | 82 cm |

**For Female Candidates:**

| Parameter | All Categories |
|-----------|---------------|
| Height | 152 cm |

## Exam Pattern

### Written Examination

| Subject | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| General Knowledge | 25 | 25 |
| Reasoning / Logic | 25 | 25 |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 25 | 25 |
| Language (Hindi/English/Hindi) | 25 | 25 |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** |

- **Duration:** 1-2 hours (varies by notification)
- **Negative marking:** Varies — check notification
- Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST) follow the written exam

### Physical Efficiency Test

**Male:** Running (varies by state), Long Jump, High Jump
**Female:** Running (shorter distance), Long Jump

### Document Verification & Medical Exam

Final stage before appointment.

## Syllabus Overview

### General Knowledge
- Uttarakhand History & Culture, Indian History, Geography, Polity, Economy, Science, Current Affairs

### Reasoning / Logic
- Analogies, Coding-decoding, Series, Blood relations, Directions, Venn diagrams

### Quantitative Aptitude
- Number system, Percentage, Profit & Loss, Average, Ratio, Time & Distance, Data Interpretation

### Language
- Grammar, Comprehension, Vocabulary, Sentence correction

## How to Apply

1. Visit the official Uttarakhand Police Recruitment Board website at [uttarakhandpolice.uk.gov.in](https://uttarakhandpolice.uk.gov.in)
2. Find the Police Constable recruitment notification
3. Register with valid email and mobile number
4. Fill in personal, educational, and category details
5. Upload photo, signature, and required certificates
6. Pay application fee online
7. Submit and download confirmation

## Application Fee

| Category | Fee |
|----------|-----|
| General / OBC | ₹250 - ₹500 |
| SC / ST | ₹100 - ₹250 |

*Exact fee varies per notification. Check the official advertisement.*

## Salary & Benefits

- **Pay Scale:** ₹21,700 – ₹69,100 (varies by state pay commission)
- DA, HRA, and other allowances
- Uniform allowance, medical benefits
- Pension (NPS), group insurance
- Promotion opportunities to Head Constable, ASI

## Preparation Tips

1. **State-specific GK:** Focus on Uttarakhand history, geography, and government schemes
2. **Physical fitness:** Start training early — PET eliminates many candidates
3. **Basic math:** Practice arithmetic speed calculations daily
4. **Current affairs:** Read newspapers daily for national and state-level news
5. **Mock tests:** Solve previous year papers for pattern understanding

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the minimum qualification for Uttarakhand Police Constable?
12th pass (Intermediate) from a recognized board is the minimum requirement.

### Is there negative marking?
It varies by notification — check the specific recruitment advertisement.

### Is physical fitness test mandatory?
Yes, PET/PST is mandatory and eliminates candidates who don't meet standards.

### Can candidates from other states apply?
Generally yes, but reserved category benefits require Uttarakhand domicile.

### What is the age limit for Uttarakhand Police Constable?
General category: 18-25 years, with relaxation for OBC/SC/ST candidates.

### How often is recruitment conducted?
Recruitment is based on vacancies — typically every 1-3 years.
