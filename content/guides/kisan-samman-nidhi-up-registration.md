## What is PM Kisan Samman Nidhi?

**Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)** is a central government scheme that provides income support of **₹6,000 per year** to all landholding farmer families across India. The amount is paid in **three equal installments of ₹2,000** every four months, directly into the farmer's bank account through DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer).

Uttar Pradesh has the **highest number of PM-KISAN beneficiaries** in the country, with over 2.5 crore farmers registered. The UP state government also supplements this through various state agricultural welfare schemes managed via the **UP Agriculture Department portal (upagriculture.com)**.

## Who is Eligible?

**Eligible:**
- All landholding farmer families in Uttar Pradesh
- Small and marginal farmers with cultivable land
- Both individual and joint landholders

**Not eligible (exclusion criteria):**
- Institutional landholders (trusts, companies, etc.)
- Former/present holders of constitutional posts
- Serving or retired officers of state/central government (with pension above ₹10,000/month)
- Income tax payers in the last assessment year
- Professionals (doctors, engineers, lawyers, CAs) registered with professional bodies
- Retired pensioners with monthly pension of ₹10,000 or more

## Benefits

| Benefit | Amount | Frequency |
|---------|--------|-----------|
| PM-KISAN installment | ₹2,000 | Every 4 months (3 times/year) |
| Annual total | ₹6,000 | Per farmer family |

**UP State Support (via UP Agriculture Department):**
- Subsidized seeds and fertilizers
- Crop insurance under PMFBY
- Soil health card
- Agricultural equipment subsidy

## Documents Required

**Mandatory:**
- Aadhaar Card (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- Land ownership documents (Khatauni / land record)
- Bank account passbook (Aadhaar-linked)
- Active mobile number

**For eKYC (mandatory for continued payments):**
- Aadhaar number
- Aadhaar-linked mobile number (for OTP)
- Or visit CSC center for biometric eKYC

## How to Register for PM Kisan in UP — Step-by-Step

### Online Self-Registration

1. **Visit PM-KISAN portal:** Go to [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in)
2. **Click "New Farmer Registration":** On the homepage, click on the registration link
3. **Enter Aadhaar number:** Type your Aadhaar number and select State as "Uttar Pradesh"
4. **OTP verification:** Enter the OTP sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile number
5. **Fill personal details:** Enter name (as per Aadhaar), father's name, date of birth, gender, category (SC/ST/OBC/General)
6. **Enter land details:** Add Khasra number, Khata number, area of land, survey number from your Khatauni
7. **Bank account details:** Enter bank name, branch, IFSC code, and account number
8. **Upload documents:** Upload scanned copies of land records and bank passbook
9. **Submit application:** Review all details and click Submit
10. **Note registration number:** Save your registration number for tracking

### Registration via CSC (Common Service Centre)

1. Visit your nearest **CSC / Jan Seva Kendra**
2. Carry original Aadhaar, land records, and bank passbook
3. The CSC operator will fill and submit your application
4. CSC may charge a **nominal fee of ₹20-50** for the service
5. Collect the acknowledgement receipt

### Registration via Lekhpal / Agriculture Department

1. Contact your village **Lekhpal** or **Block Agriculture Officer**
2. They can verify your land records and submit your application
3. This is helpful if you face issues with online registration

## eKYC — Mandatory for Receiving Payments

eKYC is **mandatory** for all PM-KISAN beneficiaries. Without eKYC, your installments will be stopped.

### How to Complete eKYC:

**Online (OTP-based):**
1. Go to [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in)
2. Click on "eKYC" option
3. Enter your Aadhaar number
4. Enter OTP received on Aadhaar-linked mobile
5. eKYC completed — status will show "eKYC done"

**Offline (Biometric at CSC):**
1. Visit nearest CSC center with Aadhaar card
2. CSC operator will do biometric authentication
3. This is useful if your mobile number is not linked with Aadhaar

## How to Check Beneficiary Status

1. Go to [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Beneficiary Status"**
3. Enter your Aadhaar number or mobile number or account number
4. Click "Get Data" to see your payment history and pending installments

## Processing Time

| Stage | Timeline |
|-------|----------|
| Application verification (state level) | 30-60 days |
| Land record verification | Done at Tehsil/Block level |
| First installment after approval | Next payment cycle (installments are released 3 times a year: Apr-Jul, Aug-Nov, Dec-Mar) |

## Fees

Registration on PM-KISAN portal is **completely free**. CSC centers may charge ₹20-50 as service fee.

## Important Tips

1. **Complete eKYC immediately** — Without eKYC, you won't receive any installments. Do it online or visit a CSC center.
2. **Ensure Aadhaar-bank linking** — Your bank account must be linked with Aadhaar for DBT. Visit your bank if not already linked (see our [Aadhaar linking guide](/guide/aadhaar-linking-guide)).
3. **Update land records** — If your Khatauni has errors, get them corrected through the Tehsil office before applying.
4. **Check UP Agriculture portal** — Register on [upagriculture.com](https://upagriculture.com) for additional state agricultural benefits and subsidies.
5. **Beware of middlemen** — Registration is free. Don't pay anyone for PM-KISAN registration beyond nominal CSC charges.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. How much money do farmers get under PM Kisan?

Farmers receive **₹6,000 per year** in three installments of ₹2,000 each, directly in their bank accounts.

### Q2. Is PM Kisan eKYC mandatory?

Yes, **eKYC is mandatory** for all beneficiaries. You can do it online via OTP or offline at a CSC center using biometric authentication.

### Q3. Can tenant farmers (without land ownership) apply?

Currently, PM-KISAN is for **landholding farmers only**. Tenant farmers or sharecroppers without land records in their name are not eligible under the central scheme.

### Q4. My installment is not coming. What should I do?

Check your beneficiary status on pmkisan.gov.in. Common reasons for payment failure: eKYC not done, Aadhaar-bank not linked, land records not verified, or bank account details incorrect. Resolve the specific issue and contact your Block Agriculture Officer.

### Q5. Can I apply if I have land in multiple districts of UP?

Yes, but you should register using the land records of one district. The total landholding across all locations is considered for eligibility.

### Q6. What is the last date to register for PM Kisan?

There is **no last date** — PM-KISAN registration is open throughout the year. However, to get the current installment, your registration and verification must be completed before the installment release date.

### Q7. How can I update my bank account details in PM Kisan?

Log in to pmkisan.gov.in, go to "Updation of Self Registered Farmer" section, and update your bank details. Changes are verified by the state agriculture department before taking effect.
