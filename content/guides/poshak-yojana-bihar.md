# Bihar Poshak Yojana: School Uniform Allowance for Class 1 to 12 Students

The Bihar Poshak Yojana (Uniform Allowance Scheme) provides financial assistance to students of government and government-aided schools in Bihar for purchasing school uniforms. The scheme covers students from **Class 1 to Class 12** and the allowance ranges from **₹600 to ₹1,500** depending on the class.

## What is Bihar Poshak Yojana?

The Bihar government introduced Poshak Yojana to ensure that students from economically weaker families can attend school in proper uniforms. The money is transferred directly to the student's or parent's bank account through DBT. The scheme benefits over **1.5 crore students** across Bihar every year.

### Uniform Allowance Amount

| Class | Annual Amount |
|-------|--------------|
| Class 1-2 | ₹600 |
| Class 3-5 | ₹700 |
| Class 6-8 | ₹1,000 |
| Class 9-12 | ₹1,500 |

## Who is Eligible?

- Student must be enrolled in a **government or government-aided school** in Bihar
- Students from **Class 1 to Class 12** are eligible
- The student must be a **resident of Bihar**
- The student or parent must have a **bank account**
- Student's details must be registered on the **Medhasoft portal** by the school

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** of the student (or parent for younger children)
- **Bank account passbook** (student's or parent's)
- **School enrolment proof**
- **Passport-size photograph**

## Step-by-Step Process

### How the Scheme Works

The Poshak Yojana amount is disbursed **through the school** — students do not apply individually.

1. **School Registration:** The school headmaster registers on the **[Medhasoft portal](https://medhasoft.bihar.gov.in/)**
2. **Student Data Upload:** School uploads the list of eligible students with Aadhaar, bank details, and class information
3. **Verification:** Block Education Officer (BEO) verifies the data
4. **Approval:** District-level approval of the student list
5. **DBT Transfer:** The allowance amount is transferred to the student's/parent's bank account
6. **Status Check:** Payment status can be tracked on the Medhasoft portal

### What Parents Should Do

1. Ensure your child has an **Aadhaar card**
2. Open a **bank account** in the child's name (or use parent's account for younger children)
3. **Link Aadhaar** to the bank account
4. Submit documents to the **school office** at the beginning of the academic session
5. Verify with the school that your child's name appears in the **Medhasoft list**
6. Check payment status on [medhasoft.bihar.gov.in](https://medhasoft.bihar.gov.in/)

## Fees

The scheme is completely **free**. No application fee or processing charges.

## Processing Time

- Schools upload data at the start of the academic session
- DBT transfer typically happens within **2-4 months** of data verification
- Sometimes disbursement may be delayed depending on government fund release

## Important Tips

1. **Ensure Aadhaar-bank linking** — without this, DBT transfer will fail
2. **Check with school regularly** — ensure your child's data is uploaded correctly on Medhasoft
3. **Both boys and girls** receive this benefit equally
4. **Private school students are not eligible** — only government and government-aided schools
5. **If payment is delayed**, approach the Block Education Officer with your child's details

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Do students need to apply separately for Poshak Yojana?
No, the school processes the application through the Medhasoft portal. Students/parents just need to submit their documents to the school.

### Q2: Is the money given for buying uniforms or are uniforms distributed?
The money is transferred directly to the bank account via DBT. Students can purchase uniforms themselves.

### Q3: Can a student get both Cycle Yojana and Poshak Yojana benefits?
Yes, a Class 9 student can receive both the [Cycle Yojana](/guide/cycle-yojana-bihar) bicycle allowance (₹3,000) and the Poshak Yojana uniform allowance (₹1,500) as they are separate schemes.

### Q4: What if my child doesn't have an Aadhaar card?
Get an [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) made for your child at the nearest Aadhaar enrolment centre. It is mandatory for DBT benefits.

### Q5: Is this scheme available for madrasa and Sanskrit school students?
Yes, students enrolled in government-recognised madrasas and Sanskrit schools in Bihar are also eligible.

### Q6: My child's name was not in the school list. What should I do?
Speak to the school headmaster immediately. If unresolved, contact the Block Education Officer (BEO) of your area.

### Q7: Is the amount given every year?
Yes, the Poshak Yojana benefit is provided **every academic year** to eligible students.
