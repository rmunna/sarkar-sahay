## What is Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)?

Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY) is a **government-backed savings scheme** for the girl child, launched under the **Beti Bachao Beti Padhao** campaign. It offers one of the highest interest rates among small savings schemes.

Key features:
- **Interest rate:** 8.2% per annum (Q1 FY 2024-25, revised quarterly)
- **Minimum deposit:** ₹250 per year
- **Maximum deposit:** ₹1.5 lakh per year
- **Account maturity:** 21 years from the date of opening
- **Tax benefit:** Deposits qualify for deduction under **Section 80C**; interest and maturity amount are tax-free (EEE status)
- **Eligibility:** Girl child below 10 years of age; maximum 2 accounts per family

SSY accounts can be opened at any **post office** or participating **banks** (SBI, PNB, BOB, ICICI, Axis, etc.).

## How to Check SSY Balance Online

### Method 1: DOP Internet Banking (Post Office Accounts)

1. Visit [https://dopfinancialservices.gov.in](https://dopfinancialservices.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Customer Login"**
3. Enter your **username and password** (register first if you haven't)
4. After login, navigate to **"Accounts"** → **"SSY Account"**
5. Your current balance, transaction history, and interest credited will be displayed
6. You can also download a statement

**First-time registration:**
1. Visit your post office and request **internet banking activation** for your SSY account
2. You will receive a username and temporary password
3. Log in at the DOP portal and change your password

### Method 2: IPPB Mobile App (India Post Payments Bank)

1. Download the **IPPB Mobile Banking app** from Google Play Store or App Store
2. Log in with your registered mobile number
3. If your SSY account is linked to your IPPB account, balance will be visible
4. Navigate to **"Accounts"** to view SSY balance and recent transactions

### Method 3: Bank Internet/Mobile Banking (Bank Accounts)

If your SSY account is with a **bank** (SBI, PNB, ICICI, etc.):

1. Log in to your bank's **internet banking or mobile app**
2. Navigate to **"Deposits"** or **"Other Accounts"** section
3. Your SSY account will be listed along with the current balance
4. Some banks show SSY under **"Linked Accounts"** or **"Term Deposits"**

**For SBI specifically:**
1. Log in to SBI YONO app or online banking
2. Go to **"Deposits & Investments"** → **"Sukanya Samriddhi"**
3. View balance, transaction history, and maturity details

### Method 4: Passbook Update (Offline)

1. Visit your **post office branch** or **bank branch** where the SSY account is held
2. Submit your SSY **passbook** for updating
3. The updated passbook will show all deposits, interest credited, and current balance
4. This is the simplest method if you don't have internet banking

## Check SSY Maturity Amount

SSY does not have an online maturity calculator on the official portal, but you can estimate it:

- **Deposit period:** First 15 years (deposits mandatory)
- **Maturity:** 21 years from account opening
- **Partial withdrawal:** Up to 50% of balance allowed after the girl turns 18 (for higher education)
- The maturity amount depends on your yearly deposits and the prevailing interest rate

## SMS / Missed Call Balance Check

There is no official SMS or missed call service specifically for SSY balance. However:

- **SBI customers** can SMS **BAL** to **09223766666** to get account summary (SSY linked accounts may show)
- **IPPB customers** can use the IPPB app for instant balance
- For post office accounts, **passbook update** at the branch remains the most reliable offline method

## Common Issues and Fixes

### 1. Cannot Access DOP Internet Banking
- You must first **register for internet banking** at your post office branch
- Carry your **Aadhaar, passbook, and mobile number** to the post office
- After registration, activate your account online within 30 days

### 2. SSY Account Not Showing in Bank App
- Ensure your SSY account is **linked to your customer ID** in the bank
- Visit the bank branch and request linking if it's not visible
- Some banks show SSY under "Other Deposits" rather than main savings

### 3. Interest Not Credited
- SSY interest is calculated monthly but **credited annually** (at the end of each financial year, March 31)
- If interest is not credited by April, contact your branch
- Ensure the minimum annual deposit of ₹250 was made — default accounts don't earn full interest

### 4. Account Marked as Default / Inactive
- If minimum ₹250 is not deposited in a financial year, the account becomes **default**
- To reactivate: pay ₹250 (minimum deposit) + ₹50 penalty per year of default at the branch
- Reactivation is possible until the girl child turns 15

### 5. Want to Transfer SSY Account
- SSY accounts can be transferred between **post offices** and between **post office and bank** (and vice versa)
- Submit a transfer request at the current branch with the new branch details
- Transfer is free of charge

## Important Tips

1. **Deposit early in the financial year** (April) to earn maximum interest for the full year
2. **Set up a yearly reminder** — missing deposits leads to penalty and default status
3. **Keep the passbook safe** — it is the primary proof of your SSY account
4. **Register for internet banking** to track your balance conveniently
5. **Plan for partial withdrawal** — 50% can be withdrawn after the girl turns 18 for education

## FAQs

### Q1: How do I check my Sukanya Samriddhi account balance online?
For post office accounts, log in at [dopfinancialservices.gov.in](https://dopfinancialservices.gov.in). For bank accounts, use your bank's internet banking or mobile app to view SSY balance.

### Q2: Can I check SSY balance using mobile number or Aadhaar?
No direct Aadhaar-based or mobile-based balance check is available for SSY. You need internet banking login credentials or must visit the branch for a passbook update.

### Q3: What is the current SSY interest rate?
The interest rate is **8.2% per annum** (as of Q1 FY 2024-25). It is revised quarterly by the government.

### Q4: When does the SSY account mature?
The account matures **21 years from the date of opening**. Deposits are required only for the first 15 years; the remaining 6 years earn interest on the accumulated balance.

### Q5: Can I withdraw money before maturity?
**Partial withdrawal** of up to 50% of the balance is allowed once the girl turns 18, for higher education purposes. **Premature closure** is allowed after the girl turns 18 for marriage (at least 1 month before marriage).

### Q6: What happens if I miss a yearly deposit?
The account becomes **default**. You can reactivate it by paying the minimum deposit (₹250) plus a penalty of ₹50 per year of default, at the branch.

### Q7: Can I open SSY account in both post office and bank?
You can open only **one SSY account per girl child**. It can be at either a post office or a bank, not both. You can transfer between them if needed.
