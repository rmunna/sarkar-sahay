# Punjab Women ₹1,000/Month Scheme 2026 — ₹1,500 for Dalit Women, Starts July 1

The Punjab government under Chief Minister Bhagwant Singh Mann has launched a near-universal monthly financial support scheme for women, with **₹1,000 per month** for general category women and **₹1,500 per month** for Scheduled Caste (Dalit) women. Payments start from **July 1, 2026**.

**Quick answer:** Eligible women of Punjab aged 18+ receive ₹1,000–₹1,500 per month directly in their bank account. Only 3 documents needed: **Aadhaar card with Punjab address + Punjab voter ID + bank passbook**. No income limit. No deadline for registration announced yet.

---

## Benefit Summary

| Category | Monthly Amount |
|---|---|
| General category women | ₹1,000/month |
| Scheduled Caste (Dalit) women | ₹1,500/month |
| **Annual total (General)** | **₹12,000/year** |
| **Annual total (SC/Dalit)** | **₹18,000/year** |

- **Payment mode:** Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to Aadhaar-linked bank account
- **Payment start:** July 1, 2026 (first disbursement)
- **Coverage:** Approximately 52 lakh women across Punjab (97% of eligible women in the state)

---

## Eligibility

- **Female** resident of Punjab
- Aged **18 years or above**
- Must have a **Punjab address on Aadhaar card**
- Must have a **Punjab voter ID**
- Must have an **Aadhaar-linked bank account** for DBT

**No income limit** — the scheme is near-universal (targeting 97% of women in the state). No BPL or below-threshold criteria required.

---

## Documents Required

Only 3 documents are needed to register:

1. **Aadhaar card** — with Punjab address
2. **Punjab voter ID** — confirms Punjab residency
3. **Bank passbook** — showing account linked to Aadhaar

For SC/Dalit women who want the ₹1,500 rate:
- **Caste certificate** issued by Punjab government

---

## How to Apply / Register

The scheme was announced on **Babasaheb Ambedkar Jayanti (April 14, 2026)** and registrations are being conducted through government camps and official channels.

### Step 1: Check Registration Camps in Your Area

The Punjab government is registering women through local camps at panchayat offices, municipality offices, and ward-level collection points. Watch for announcements:
- Local panchayat / sarpanch
- Ward councillor (urban areas)
- Official Punjab government social media and punjab.gov.in

### Step 2: Visit the Nearest Registration Point

Bring original documents + photocopies:
- Aadhaar card
- Punjab voter ID
- Bank passbook

For SC/Dalit women — also bring caste certificate for the ₹1,500 rate.

### Step 3: Complete Registration

Officials will:
- Verify Aadhaar-Punjab voter ID match
- Confirm bank account is Aadhaar-seeded
- Register you in the beneficiary database

**No fees charged for registration.**

### Step 4: Receive Monthly Credit from July 1, 2026

Once registered, ₹1,000 or ₹1,500 is credited to your bank account on/around the 1st of every month starting July 2026.

## Who Is This Scheme For?

This scheme targets nearly every adult woman in Punjab — 97% of the state's women population. Unlike many welfare schemes, there is:
- **No income ceiling** (not BPL-restricted)
- **No maximum age** (18+ without upper limit)
- **No education requirement**
- **No employment condition**

Married, unmarried, widowed, and divorced women are all eligible.

---

## How Dalit Women Get ₹1,500 Instead of ₹1,000

Women from **Scheduled Caste (SC/Dalit) communities** receive ₹500 extra per month:

- ₹1,000 (base grant) + ₹500 (SC supplement) = **₹1,500/month**
- Requires valid SC/Dalit caste certificate issued by Punjab government
- The caste certificate must show the applicant's name (not just parents')
- If you don't have a caste certificate, you'll receive the base ₹1,000 until you submit one

---

## If Your Aadhaar Is Not Linked to Bank Account

DBT cannot be processed to an unlinked account. To link:
1. Visit your bank branch with Aadhaar card + passbook
2. Request Aadhaar-bank account seeding
3. Or use your bank's mobile app / net banking under "Link Aadhaar"
4. Check seeding status at [NPCI](https://resident.uidai.gov.in/bank-mapper)

Allow 2–5 working days for linkage to activate.

---

## How to Check If Your Payment Arrived

After July 1, 2026:
- **Bank SMS alert** — if you have mobile number registered with bank
- **Net banking / mobile banking** — check latest transactions
- **PFMS portal** — pfms.nic.in → search by Aadhaar number

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I apply if I already receive other Punjab government benefits (ration, health scheme, etc.)?**
Yes — this monthly allowance is separate from other schemes like Punjab free ration or Aam Aadmi Clinic. You can benefit from multiple schemes simultaneously.

**What if I have Aadhaar but my address is from another state?**
You need a Punjab address on Aadhaar. If you've moved to Punjab and your Aadhaar shows a different state, update your Aadhaar address first at the nearest Aadhaar Seva Kendra or via the UIDAI portal.

**Is there an age limit (upper)?**
No upper age limit mentioned in official announcements — the scheme covers women aged 18 and above.

**Do working women qualify?**
No employment-based exclusion has been announced. The scheme appears to cover employed and unemployed women both. Confirm at your local registration camp.

**Is there a last date to register?**
As of June 2026, no registration deadline has been announced. However, registering early ensures you start receiving payments from July 2026 onwards.

**What if my village camp is over — can I still register?**
Check with your panchayat office or nearest district block development office. Registration is expected to remain open even after initial camps.
