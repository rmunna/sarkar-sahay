## What is the Atta Dal Scheme?

The Atta Dal Scheme is a food security programme by the Government of Punjab that provides **free wheat flour (atta) and pulses (dal)** to Below Poverty Line (BPL) families, particularly those headed by women, elderly, disabled, and other vulnerable groups. The scheme supplements the existing Public Distribution System (PDS) to ensure food security.

### Benefits

| Item | Quantity per Family/Month |
|------|--------------------------|
| Wheat flour (atta) | 4 kg per eligible member |
| Dal (pulses) | 1 kg per family |

> Quantities and items may vary based on government revisions. The scheme has been modified over the years.

## Who is Eligible?

- **BPL families** in Punjab (Blue ration card holders)
- **Priority Household (PHH)** cardholders under NFSA
- **Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY)** cardholders
- Must be a **resident of Punjab**
- Special priority: widows, elderly, disabled, and destitute families

## Documents Required

- **BPL/Blue ration card** (primary document)
- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) of family members
- If new to PDS: residence proof, income certificate, family details

## How to Avail Benefits

### Through Fair Price Shops (PDS)

1. **Locate** your nearest Fair Price Shop (ration shop)
2. **Visit** on the designated distribution day (varies by area)
3. **Carry** your ration card and Aadhaar card
4. **Biometric verification** — Aadhaar-based authentication at the ePOS machine
5. **Collect** your entitled atta and dal
6. **Verify** the quantity and sign the register

### Checking Entitlement

1. Visit [foodsuppb.gov.in](https://foodsuppb.gov.in) or Punjab PDS portal
2. Search with your ration card number
3. Check your entitlement and distribution history

## If You Don't Have a Ration Card

1. **Apply online** through the Punjab Food and Civil Supplies portal or visit the District Food Supply Controller's office
2. **Submit** Aadhaar, address proof, income proof, family details
3. **Get BPL survey** done if applicable
4. **Collect** ration card after verification
5. See our [ration card guide](/guide/ration-card-apply-online) for detailed steps

## Processing Time

- Ration collection: **Immediate** (at fair price shop with valid card)
- New ration card application: **1-3 months**

## Fees

**Free.** The atta and dal are provided at no cost to eligible BPL families.

## Important Tips

1. **Keep ration card updated** — Add new family members and remove deceased members promptly
2. **Link Aadhaar** — Aadhaar-linked ration card is required for biometric authentication
3. **Collect on time** — Visit the fair price shop during the designated distribution period
4. **Report irregularities** — If the shopkeeper denies ration or gives less quantity, complain to the District Food Supply Controller
5. **Check ePOS receipt** — Always collect the receipt from the ePOS machine as proof of distribution

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Who gets free atta and dal in Punjab?
**BPL families** with Blue ration cards, AAY cardholders, and PHH cardholders under NFSA.

### Q2. How much atta do I get?
Approximately **4 kg per eligible member** per month of wheat flour, plus 1 kg dal per family.

### Q3. Is this different from regular PDS ration?
The Atta Dal Scheme supplements regular PDS. BPL families get free atta and dal **in addition to** their regular subsidized ration.

### Q4. What if the fair price shop refuses to give ration?
Report to the **District Food Supply Controller** or call the state food helpline. Every eligible cardholder has a legal right to their entitlement.

### Q5. Can I get ration from any fair price shop?
No. You must collect from the **fair price shop assigned** to your ration card. For portability, check if Punjab has enabled One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC).
