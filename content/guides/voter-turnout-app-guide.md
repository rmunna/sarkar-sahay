## What is the Voter Turnout App?

The **Voter Turnout App** is an official mobile application developed by the **Election Commission of India (ECI)**. It provides real-time voter turnout data on election day, allowing citizens, media, and political parties to track voting percentages across constituencies and states.

The app was launched to bring transparency to the electoral process and help citizens stay informed about democratic participation in their area.

### Key Features

- **Live turnout data** updated throughout election day
- **Constituency-wise search** for specific polling areas
- **Phase-wise data** for multi-phase elections
- **State and national level** turnout statistics
- **Historical data** from past elections for comparison
- **Gender-wise turnout** breakdown (male, female, third gender)

## How to Download the Voter Turnout App

### For Android

1. Open **Google Play Store** on your phone
2. Search for **"Voter Turnout"** by Election Commission of India
3. Tap **Install** and wait for download to complete
4. Open the app after installation

### For iPhone (iOS)

1. Open **Apple App Store**
2. Search for **"Voter Turnout"** by ECI
3. Tap **Get** and install the app
4. Open after installation

You can also access turnout data on the official ECI website:

## How to Check Live Turnout on Election Day

### Step 1: Open the App

Launch the Voter Turnout App on your phone. The home screen shows the **current election** with overall national turnout percentage.

### Step 2: Select Election Phase

If the election has multiple phases, select the **relevant phase** from the dropdown menu. For example, Lok Sabha elections are typically held in 7 phases.

### Step 3: Search by Constituency

1. Tap the **Search** icon
2. Enter your **constituency name** or **state name**
3. Select from the results list
4. View detailed turnout data including:
   - Total registered voters
   - Votes polled so far
   - Turnout percentage
   - Gender-wise breakdown

### Step 4: View Phase-Wise Data

1. Go to the **Phase-Wise** tab
2. Select the specific phase number
3. View all constituencies voting in that phase
4. Compare turnout across different areas

### Step 5: Check Historical Data

1. Navigate to **Past Elections** section
2. Select the election year (e.g., 2019 Lok Sabha, 2024 State Elections)
3. Compare current turnout with previous election data

## Understanding Turnout Data

| Data Point | What It Means |
|---|---|
| Turnout % | Percentage of registered voters who have voted |
| Registered Voters | Total eligible voters in the constituency |
| Votes Polled | Number of votes cast so far |
| Male/Female Turnout | Gender-wise voting percentage |
| Updated Time | Last time the data was refreshed |

### How Often is Data Updated?

- Data is updated **every 2 hours** during polling
- **Final turnout** is published after polling ends
- Minor revisions may happen in the days following the election

## Important Tips

1. **Data is approximate** — Final turnout numbers may differ slightly from app data shown during the day
2. **Use official app only** — Download only from Play Store/App Store to avoid fake apps
3. **No login required** — The app is free and does not require registration
4. **Check after 10 AM** — Meaningful turnout data typically appears a few hours after polling begins
5. **Compare with past data** — Use historical comparison to understand voting trends in your area

## FAQs

### Q1: Is the Voter Turnout App free to use?

Yes, the app is completely free. It is developed and maintained by the Election Commission of India with no charges for download or usage.

### Q2: Can I check turnout data for past elections?

Yes, the app provides historical turnout data for previous Lok Sabha and State Assembly elections. You can compare current and past turnout for any constituency.

### Q3: How accurate is the real-time turnout data?

The data is based on reports from polling stations and is updated periodically (approximately every 2 hours). Final official figures may show minor variations after all data is compiled.

### Q4: Does the app work on election day only?

No, the app is available year-round. However, live turnout data is only updated during active polling. Between elections, you can access historical data and constituency information.

### Q5: Can I find my polling booth through this app?

The Voter Turnout App focuses on turnout data. To find your polling booth, use the **Voter Helpline App** or check your details on the [National Voters' Service Portal](https://voters.eci.gov.in). See our [voter ID guide](/guide/voter-id-card-apply-online) for registration details.

### Q6: Is the app available in regional languages?

The app primarily displays data in English and Hindi. Constituency and state names may appear in regional languages depending on the election.

### Q7: What is the difference between Voter Turnout App and Voter Helpline App?

The **Voter Turnout App** shows live voting percentages and turnout statistics. The **Voter Helpline App** helps you find your name on the voter list, locate your polling booth, and file complaints.
