# DigiLocker Verification Not Working: Fix Authentication & Login Errors

DigiLocker uses Aadhaar-based verification for account creation, login, and document access. When verification fails, you're completely locked out of your digital documents. This guide covers all verification and authentication failures — different from simple [login issues](/guide/digilocker-login-not-working-fix) or the ["Aadhaar already registered" error](/guide/digilocker-aadhaar-verification-failed-fix).

---

## Types of DigiLocker Verification Failures

| Error Type | When It Happens |
|---|---|
| **Aadhaar verification failed** | During new account creation or re-verification |
| **Authentication failed** | During login after entering OTP |
| **Face verification failed** | When face match is required for high-security documents |
| **KYC verification failed** | When a third-party service requests DigiLocker KYC |
| **"Unable to verify your identity"** | Generic error during any verification step |
| **"Verification timed out"** | Server took too long to verify |

---

## Fix 1: Aadhaar Verification Failed During Sign-Up

When creating a new DigiLocker account, Aadhaar verification can fail for these reasons:

### Mobile Number Mismatch
- DigiLocker sends OTP to your **Aadhaar-linked mobile number**
- If your current phone number is different from what's in Aadhaar, verification will fail
- **Fix:** Update your mobile number in Aadhaar by visiting an Enrolment Centre. See our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)

### Aadhaar Already Used
- Each Aadhaar can be linked to **only one DigiLocker account**
- If you (or someone) already created an account with your Aadhaar, new verification fails
- **Fix:** See our [Aadhaar already registered guide](/guide/digilocker-aadhaar-verification-failed-fix) for recovery steps

### UIDAI Server Down
- DigiLocker verifies against UIDAI servers in real-time
- If UIDAI authentication service is down, all verification fails
- **Fix:** Wait 1-2 hours and retry. Check [uidai.gov.in](https://uidai.gov.in/) for maintenance notices

## Fix 2: Authentication Failed During Login

After entering your DigiLocker username/Aadhaar and OTP, you may see "Authentication Failed."

### Wrong OTP
- OTPs expire in **3 minutes**. Enter quickly
- Don't confuse DigiLocker OTP with OTPs from other services arriving simultaneously
- **Fix:** Click "Resend OTP" and enter the new one immediately

### Session Expired
- If you take too long on the login page, the session expires
- **Fix:** Refresh the page completely (Ctrl+Shift+R) and start over

### Browser Issues
- DigiLocker works best on **Chrome** or **Edge**
- Safari and Firefox sometimes fail on the OTP verification step
- **Fix:** Switch to Chrome, disable extensions, clear cookies for digilocker.gov.in

### Account Locked
- Too many failed login attempts (5+) can temporarily lock your account
- **Fix:** Wait **30 minutes** and try again. If still locked, use the "Forgot Security PIN" option

## Fix 3: Face Verification Not Working

Some services require face verification through the DigiLocker app (matching your face with Aadhaar photo).

### Common Issues:
- **Poor lighting** — face not clearly visible to camera
- **Glasses/mask** — remove accessories that obscure your face
- **Old Aadhaar photo** — if your appearance has changed significantly, face match may fail
- **Phone camera quality** — front camera must be reasonably clear

### Fixes:
1. Use the verification in a **well-lit room** with natural light facing you
2. **Remove glasses, caps, and masks**
3. Hold the phone at **arm's length, face centered**
4. If it repeatedly fails, **update your Aadhaar photo** at an Enrolment Centre
5. Try on a **different phone** with a better front camera

## Fix 4: KYC Verification Failed (Third-Party Services)

When banks, insurance companies, or other services request DigiLocker KYC verification, it can fail even if DigiLocker itself works fine.

### Causes:
- The third-party service's API integration with DigiLocker is broken
- Your DigiLocker documents don't match the details the service expects (name spelling, DOB format)
- The service's KYC request has expired

### Fixes:
1. **Re-initiate KYC** from the third-party service (not from DigiLocker)
2. Check if your **name and DOB in DigiLocker** match what the service has on record
3. Try **linking the document again** in DigiLocker before re-attempting KYC
4. Contact the third-party service's support — the issue may be on their end

## Fix 5: "Unable to Verify Your Identity" — Generic Error

This catch-all error can mean multiple things:

### Systematic Fixes (Try in Order):
1. **Check internet connection** — DigiLocker needs stable connectivity
2. **Update the DigiLocker app** — old versions may have authentication bugs
3. **Clear app cache** — Settings → Apps → DigiLocker → Clear Cache (don't clear data)
4. **Try the website instead of app** (or vice versa)
5. **Try mobile data instead of Wi-Fi** — some corporate/college Wi-Fi blocks DigiLocker
6. **Disable VPN** if active
7. **Check system date/time** — incorrect date causes SSL/certificate verification failure
8. **Try after some time** — DigiLocker servers handle millions of requests and occasionally slow down

## Fix 6: Verification Works But Documents Don't Load

If you can log in but documents show "Verification pending" or don't appear:

- **Pull to refresh** the documents list
- **Re-link the document** — go to Issued Documents → Search → select the issuer → fetch again
- **Check if the issuing department** has uploaded your document to DigiLocker (not all departments have digitized records)
- For education documents, check if your university is a **DigiLocker partner** at [digilocker.gov.in/about-us/partners](https://digilocker.gov.in/about-us/partners)

For document-specific issues, see our [DigiLocker document not showing guide](/guide/digilocker-document-not-showing-fix).

## Important Tips

1. **Keep your Aadhaar mobile number active** — all DigiLocker verification depends on it
2. **Use the latest app version** — old versions cause authentication failures
3. **One Aadhaar = one DigiLocker account** — you cannot create multiple accounts
4. **DigiLocker security PIN is different from your Aadhaar OTP** — don't confuse them
5. **Save your DigiLocker credentials** in a password manager — repeated login failures lead to account locks

## Frequently Asked Questions

### DigiLocker says "Aadhaar verification failed" but my Aadhaar is fine. Why?
The issue is likely with UIDAI's authentication server, not your Aadhaar. Try again after a few hours. If it persists for more than 24 hours, contact DigiLocker support.

### Can I use DigiLocker without Aadhaar?
No. DigiLocker requires Aadhaar for account creation and primary verification. There is currently no alternative authentication method.

### My face verification fails every time. Is there an alternative?
Some services allow OTP-based verification instead of face match. Check if the requesting service offers an alternative. If face match is mandatory, update your Aadhaar photo to a recent one.

### DigiLocker verification worked yesterday but fails today. What changed?
Usually a server-side issue. DigiLocker handles heavy load and may have intermittent failures. Retry after a few hours. Also check if an app update is pending.

### I forgot my DigiLocker security PIN. How do I reset it?
On the login page, click **"Forgot Security PIN"** → enter your Aadhaar or username → verify with OTP → set a new PIN.

### Can I access DigiLocker from multiple devices?
Yes. DigiLocker works on multiple devices simultaneously. However, logging in on a new device triggers fresh verification.

### Is DigiLocker down right now?
Check DigiLocker's official social media or try accessing [digilocker.gov.in](https://www.digilocker.gov.in/). If the site loads but verification fails, it's likely a UIDAI authentication issue rather than DigiLocker being down.
