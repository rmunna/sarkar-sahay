## What is e-EPIC?

The **e-EPIC (Electronic Electors Photo Identity Card)** is the digital version of your physical Voter ID card issued by the **Election Commission of India**. It is a **PDF document** with a secure **QR code** that contains your electoral details and can be verified digitally.

The e-EPIC was launched in **January 2021** to provide citizens with instant access to their Voter ID in digital format.

### Key Features of e-EPIC

- **PDF format** with secure QR code
- Contains your name, photo, EPIC number, constituency, and other details
- **Legally valid** as identity proof
- Can be stored on your **smartphone** for easy access
- Available for **free** download anytime

## Who Can Download e-EPIC?

- Any registered voter whose name is on the **electoral roll**
- Must have a **registered mobile number** linked to the Voter ID for OTP verification
- Available for voters across **all states and union territories** of India

## How to Download e-EPIC from voters.eci.gov.in

### Step-by-Step Process

1. Visit the official portal at [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in)
2. **Login** with your registered mobile number/email and password
3. If you don't have an account, click **"Register"** and create one using your mobile number (OTP verification required)
4. After logging in, click on **"Download e-EPIC"** from the dashboard
5. Enter your **EPIC number** (Voter ID number — printed on your physical card, starts with 3 letters)
6. Verify via **OTP** sent to your registered mobile number
7. Click **"Download e-EPIC"**
8. The **PDF file** will be downloaded to your device

### Password for e-EPIC PDF

The downloaded e-EPIC PDF is **not password-protected** in most cases. If prompted, try:
- Your **EPIC number** in capital letters
- Your **date of birth** in DDMMYYYY format

## How to Download e-EPIC via Voter Helpline App

1. Download the **Voter Helpline App** from:
   - [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.eci.citizen) (Android)
   - [Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/voter-helpline/id1456535429) (iOS)
2. Open the app and **register/login** with your mobile number
3. Complete **OTP verification**
4. Go to **"e-EPIC Download"** section
5. Enter your **EPIC number**
6. Verify via OTP
7. Tap **"Download"** — the PDF will be saved to your phone

> **Tip:** The Voter Helpline App also lets you search your name in the electoral roll, find your polling booth, and file complaints.

## QR Code on e-EPIC

The e-EPIC contains a **secure QR code** that stores:

- Your EPIC number
- Name and photo
- Constituency details
- Part number and serial number in the electoral roll

The QR code can be **scanned and verified** by officials using the ECI's verification tools. This makes the e-EPIC tamper-proof and digitally verifiable.

## Is e-EPIC Valid as ID Proof?

Yes, the **e-EPIC is legally valid** as proof of identity. It is accepted for:

- **Voting** at polling booths
- **Identity verification** at banks, government offices, and private institutions
- **Address proof** (in combination with other documents)
- **KYC** for financial services

> **Note:** While the e-EPIC is valid, some institutions may still ask for the physical card. It is recommended to carry both when possible.

## How to Re-Download e-EPIC

You can download your e-EPIC **unlimited times** from:

- [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in) — login and click "Download e-EPIC"
- **Voter Helpline App** — same process as described above

There is **no limit** on the number of times you can download your e-EPIC.

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### "EPIC Number Not Found"
- Verify your EPIC number is correct (check physical card or search on [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in))
- Ensure your name is on the electoral roll

### "OTP Not Received"
- Check if your mobile number is registered with the Election Commission
- Wait 2–3 minutes before requesting a new OTP
- Ensure your phone has network coverage

### "Download Failed"
- Try using a different browser (Chrome recommended)
- Clear browser cache and try again
- Use the Voter Helpline App as an alternative

For persistent issues, see our guide on [Voter ID download not working](/guide/voter-id-download-not-working-fix).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is e-EPIC the same as the physical Voter ID card?
Yes, it contains the same information and is legally equivalent. The e-EPIC has an additional QR code for digital verification.

### Can I use e-EPIC to vote?
Yes, you can show your e-EPIC (on your phone or printed) at the polling booth as identity proof for voting.

### Do I need to apply separately for e-EPIC?
No, if you are already a registered voter, you can directly download your e-EPIC from the portal. No separate application is needed.

### Can I download e-EPIC without a registered mobile number?
No, OTP verification on your registered mobile number is mandatory. If your mobile number is not registered, visit your local ERO office to update it.

### What if my details are wrong on the e-EPIC?
First correct your details using [Form 8 online](/guide/voter-id-correction-online), then download the updated e-EPIC after the correction is approved.

### Is e-EPIC available in all states?
Yes, e-EPIC is available for registered voters across all states and union territories of India.
