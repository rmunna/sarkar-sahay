# UMANG Scholarship Status Not Showing? Fix NSP Tracking Errors

The UMANG app integrates with the **National Scholarship Portal (NSP)** to let students check their scholarship application status, payment tracking, and more. If your scholarship status is not showing or you're facing errors, this guide will help you troubleshoot.

## Common UMANG Scholarship Service Errors

1. **Scholarship status not showing** or "No records found"
2. **NSP account not linking** with UMANG
3. **Payment tracking not updating** or showing pending
4. **Institute verification pending** for extended periods
5. **Data not refreshing** or showing outdated information

---

## Fix 1: Scholarship Status Not Showing on UMANG

If UMANG shows no records for your scholarship:

1. **Verify your NSP application ID** — Log in to [scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in/) and confirm your application ID
2. **Check application year** — Ensure you're searching for the correct academic year
3. **Application may not be submitted** — Draft or incomplete applications won't show status. Verify on the NSP portal that your application is submitted (not just saved)
4. **Verify with Aadhaar number** — Some scholarship tracking features require your Aadhaar number instead of application ID
5. **Wait for processing** — Freshly submitted applications may take 7-15 days to appear in the tracking system
6. **Check on NSP directly** — Visit [scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in/) → "Check Your Status" for the most up-to-date information

---

## Fix 2: NSP Account Not Linking with UMANG

If you cannot link your NSP scholarship account on UMANG:

1. **Ensure NSP registration is complete** — You must have a fully registered NSP account at [scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in/)
2. **Check mobile number match** — Your NSP registered mobile number should match the one on UMANG
3. **Verify Aadhaar linking on NSP** — Your Aadhaar must be verified on the NSP portal
4. **Try linking with application ID** — Use your scholarship application ID instead of registration ID
5. **Clear UMANG cache** — Settings → Apps → UMANG → Storage → Clear Cache, then try again
6. **Update both apps** — Ensure UMANG is updated to the latest version

---

## Fix 3: Scholarship Payment Tracking Not Updating

If your scholarship payment status shows pending or is not updating:

1. **Understand the payment flow:**
   - Application submitted → Institute verified → District/State verified → Central ministry approved → Payment disbursed
   - Each stage can take 2-4 weeks
2. **Check bank account details** — Verify that your bank account and IFSC code are correct on the NSP portal
3. **Aadhaar-bank linking required** — Your Aadhaar must be linked to your bank account for DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer)
4. **Check PFMS portal** — Visit [pfms.nic.in](https://pfms.nic.in/) → "Know Your Payments" to check if the payment has been processed
5. **Payment rejection reasons:**
   - Inactive or closed bank account
   - Aadhaar not seeded with bank account
   - Name mismatch between Aadhaar and bank account
6. **Contact your bank** — If PFMS shows payment sent but you haven't received it, check with your bank

> **Tip:** Most scholarship payments are disbursed between January and March for the current academic year.

---

## Fix 4: Institute Verification Pending

If your scholarship application is stuck at "Institute Verification Pending":

1. **Contact your institute** — The nodal officer at your college/school must verify your application on the NSP portal
2. **Check deadline** — Institute verification has a deadline. If missed, the application may be rejected
3. **Verify submitted documents** — Ensure all required documents (fee receipts, bonafide certificate, income certificate) are uploaded correctly on NSP
4. **Follow up with nodal officer** — Visit the scholarship section of your institution and ask for the status
5. **Check for rejection** — Your institute may have rejected the application. Log in to NSP to check remarks
6. **Escalate if needed** — If your institute is unresponsive, contact the district nodal officer or state scholarship authority

---

## Fix 5: Data Not Refreshing on UMANG

If UMANG shows outdated scholarship information:

1. **Force refresh** — Pull down to refresh on the scholarship status screen
2. **Clear cache and re-login** — Clear UMANG cache, log out, and log back in
3. **UMANG sync delay** — UMANG pulls data from NSP servers periodically. There can be a 24-48 hour delay
4. **Check NSP portal directly** — For the most current status, always verify on [scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in/)
5. **Reinstall the app** — If data consistently shows outdated info, reinstall UMANG

---

## How to Check Scholarship Status on UMANG (Step-by-Step)

1. Open the **UMANG** app and log in
2. Search for **"National Scholarship"** or **"NSP"** in the search bar
3. Select the relevant scholarship scheme
4. Enter your **Application ID** or **Aadhaar number**
5. Enter the **Academic Year**
6. Tap **"Check Status"**
7. Your scholarship application status and payment details will be displayed

## Important Tips

1. Always apply for scholarships before the NSP deadline — late applications are not accepted
2. Keep your Aadhaar linked to your bank account for smooth DBT payment. See our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) for linking details
3. Save your NSP application ID and registration number — you'll need them for all tracking
4. Check the NSP portal regularly during the verification period (October to February)
5. For payment issues, check [pfms.nic.in](https://pfms.nic.in/) before contacting the helpline

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Can I apply for scholarships through the UMANG app?
No, scholarship applications must be submitted through the [National Scholarship Portal](https://scholarships.gov.in/). UMANG only provides status tracking and payment information.

### Q2: Why does UMANG show "No Records Found" for my scholarship?
Your application may not be fully submitted, you may be entering the wrong application ID, or the application data may not have synced with UMANG yet. Verify on the NSP portal first.

### Q3: How long does it take to receive scholarship payment after approval?
After final approval by the central ministry, payment is typically disbursed within 30-60 days via DBT to your linked bank account.

### Q4: My institute rejected my scholarship application. Can I appeal?
Check the rejection reason on the NSP portal. If documents were incomplete, you may be able to re-submit during the renewal window. Contact your institute's nodal officer for guidance.

### Q5: What scholarships are available through NSP on UMANG?
NSP covers pre-matric, post-matric, merit-cum-means, and top-class scholarships from the Ministry of Minority Affairs, Ministry of Social Justice, and various state governments.

### Q6: How do I check if my Aadhaar is linked to my bank account?
Visit your bank branch or use net banking to check Aadhaar seeding status. You can also check via [UIDAI's Aadhaar-bank linking status](https://resident.uidai.gov.in/).

### Q7: What is the NSP helpline number?
The NSP helpline is 0120-6619540. You can also email at helpdesk@nsp.gov.in. For UMANG issues, call 1800-115-565.
