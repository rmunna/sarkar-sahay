## What is the Dayanand Social Security Scheme?

The **Dayanand Social Security Scheme (DSSS)** is a comprehensive social security pension scheme by the **Government of Goa** under the **Directorate of Social Welfare**. It provides monthly pensions to senior citizens, persons with disabilities, widows, and other vulnerable groups in Goa.

This is one of the most generous state pension schemes in India, with pension amounts significantly higher than many other states.

## Who is Eligible?

### Old Age Pension
1. **Age:** Must be **60 years or older**
2. **Residency:** Must be a resident of Goa for at least **15 years**
3. **Income:** Annual family income should not exceed **₹3,00,000**

### Widow Pension
1. **Marital Status:** Must be a widow
2. **Age:** 18 years or above
3. **Residency:** 15 years in Goa
4. **Income:** Annual income ≤ ₹3,00,000
5. **Not remarried**

### Disability Pension
1. **Disability:** Minimum **40% disability** certified by medical authority
2. **Residency:** 15 years in Goa
3. **Income:** Annual income ≤ ₹3,00,000

## Pension Amount

| Category | Monthly Pension |
|----------|----------------|
| Old Age (60+) | ₹2,500 |
| Widow | ₹2,500 |
| Persons with Disability | ₹2,500 |
| Single Women (40+) | ₹2,500 |

> Pension amounts may be updated. Check [dsw.goa.gov.in](https://dsw.goa.gov.in) for latest rates.

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar Card**
- **Goa Residence Certificate** (15 years)
- **Age Proof** (for old age pension)
- **Death Certificate of husband** (for widow pension)
- **Disability Certificate** (for disability pension)
- **Income Certificate** (annual income ≤ ₹3,00,000)
- **Bank Passbook** (Aadhaar-linked)
- **Passport-size photographs**
- **Ration Card** (Goa)

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Online Application (Goa Online)

1. **Visit** [goaonline.gov.in](https://goaonline.gov.in)
2. **Register** / Login
3. **Navigate** to Social Welfare → Dayanand Social Security Scheme
4. **Select** the category (Old Age / Widow / Disability)
5. **Fill** application form with personal, income, and bank details
6. **Upload** required documents
7. **Submit** and note the application number
8. **Track** status on Goa Online portal

### Offline Application

1. **Visit** the **Directorate of Social Welfare** or **Mamlatdar's Office**
2. **Collect** the DSSS application form
3. **Fill** and attach all documents
4. **Submit** to the concerned officer
5. **Get** acknowledgement receipt
6. **Verification** by taluka-level officer

## Processing Time

- **Verification:** 30-60 days
- **Approval:** After field verification by social welfare inspector
- **First Payment:** Within 1-2 months of approval
- **Ongoing:** Monthly via bank transfer

## Important Tips

1. **15-Year Residency:** This is strictly enforced — keep residence proof ready
2. **Income Limit is Higher:** Goa's income limit of ₹3,00,000 is more generous than most states
3. **Goa Online Portal:** Use [goaonline.gov.in](https://goaonline.gov.in) for convenient online application
4. **Aadhaar-Bank Link:** Required for pension disbursement
5. **Renewal:** Pension may require annual renewal/verification

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. What is the pension amount under DSSS?
**₹2,500 per month** for all categories (old age, widow, disability).

### Q2. What is the residency requirement?
Must have lived in Goa for at least **15 years**.

### Q3. What is the income limit?
Annual family income should not exceed **₹3,00,000**.

### Q4. Can I apply online?
Yes, through [goaonline.gov.in](https://goaonline.gov.in).

### Q5. Is the scheme available for single women?
Yes, unmarried/single women aged 40+ are also eligible under this scheme.

### Q6. Can I get both old age and disability pension?
No, only one pension can be availed at a time.

### Q7. How is the pension paid?
Monthly, directly to your bank account.
