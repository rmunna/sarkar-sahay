# HDFC Home Loan Apply Online: Interest Rate, EMI & Eligibility

HDFC Bank (after the merger of HDFC Ltd with HDFC Bank in July 2023) is India's largest home loan provider. HDFC offers competitive interest rates, flexible tenures up to 30 years, and a fully digital application process. Whether you're buying your first home or a second property, this guide covers everything you need.

## Who Is Eligible for HDFC Home Loan?

### Salaried Individuals
- Age: 21–65 years (at loan maturity)
- Minimum monthly income: ₹25,000
- Employment stability: Minimum 2 years of total work experience, 1 year with current employer
- CIBIL score: 700+ (750+ for best rates)

### Self-Employed Individuals
- Age: 21–65 years (at loan maturity)
- Business vintage: Minimum 3 years
- Minimum annual income: ₹3 lakh (profit after tax)
- ITR filed for last 3 years
- CIBIL score: 700+

### Loan Amount & Tenure
- **Minimum loan:** ₹3 lakh
- **Maximum loan:** Up to ₹10 crore+ (based on eligibility)
- **Maximum tenure:** 30 years
- **Loan-to-Value (LTV):** Up to 90% for loans up to ₹30 lakh; 80% for loans above ₹75 lakh

## Documents Required

### For Salaried Applicants
- **Identity Proof:** [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online), PAN card, passport
- **Address Proof:** Aadhaar, utility bill, passport
- **Income Proof:** Latest 3 months' salary slips, 6 months' bank statement, Form 16 (last 2 years)
- **Employment Proof:** Appointment letter, experience certificate
- **Property Documents:** Sale agreement, title deed, approved building plan, NOC

### For Self-Employed Applicants
- **Identity & Address Proof:** Same as salaried
- **Income Proof:** Last 3 years' ITR with computation of income, 12 months' bank statement, audited balance sheet & P&L
- **Business Proof:** GST registration, shop establishment certificate, partnership deed (if applicable)
- **Property Documents:** Same as salaried

## How to Apply for HDFC Home Loan Online

1. Visit [HDFC Bank Home Loan page](https://www.hdfcbank.com/personal/borrow/popular-loans/home-loan)
2. Click **"Apply Now"** or **"Check Eligibility"**
3. Enter your mobile number and verify with OTP
4. Fill personal details — name, date of birth, PAN, current address
5. Enter employment and income details
6. Specify loan amount required and property details
7. Upload scanned documents (income proof, identity, property papers)
8. Submit the application
9. An HDFC Bank representative will contact you within 24–48 hours for verification
10. After document verification and property valuation, loan is sanctioned
11. Sign the loan agreement and complete disbursement formalities

## Disbursement Process

1. **Sanction Letter** issued after document verification and property legal check
2. **Property valuation** done by HDFC-empanelled valuers
3. **Loan agreement** signed with stamp duty
4. **Post-dated cheques / NACH mandate** submitted for EMI
5. **Disbursement** — For ready properties, full disbursement in one go. For under-construction, disbursed in stages as per construction progress

Typical timeline: 7–15 working days from complete document submission to disbursement.

## Important Tips

1. **Compare with other banks** — Don't assume HDFC always has the best rate; compare with SBI, Bank of Baroda, and other lenders
2. **Negotiate processing fee** — HDFC often runs offers with reduced or zero processing fee; ask before paying
3. **Maintain high CIBIL score** — A score of 750+ can get you 0.10%–0.25% better interest rate
4. **Women co-applicants save money** — Add a woman co-borrower to get 0.05% interest rate concession and stamp duty benefits in some states
5. **Prepay whenever possible** — No prepayment penalty on floating rate loans; even small annual prepayments significantly reduce total interest

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. What is the current HDFC home loan interest rate?
HDFC home loan floating interest rates currently start from approximately 8.50% p.a. for salaried individuals. Actual rate depends on loan amount, CIBIL score, and profile. Check the HDFC Bank website for latest rates.

### Q2. How much home loan can I get from HDFC Bank?
Your loan eligibility depends on income, existing liabilities, age, and property value. Generally, HDFC allows EMI up to 50%–60% of net monthly income. Use the HDFC eligibility calculator on their website.

### Q3. Can I prepay HDFC home loan without penalty?
Yes, for floating-rate home loans taken by individual borrowers, there is no prepayment or foreclosure penalty as per RBI guidelines. Fixed-rate loans may attract up to 2% penalty.

### Q4. How long does HDFC home loan approval take?
Typically 7–15 working days from submission of complete documents. Pre-approved customers or existing HDFC account holders may get faster processing.

### Q5. Can I transfer my home loan to HDFC Bank?
Yes, HDFC offers a Balance Transfer facility. If another bank is charging a higher interest rate, you can transfer the outstanding loan to HDFC at their prevailing rates. Processing fee may apply.

### Q6. Is HDFC home loan and HDFC Bank home loan the same?
Yes, after the merger of HDFC Ltd with HDFC Bank in July 2023, all home loans are now under HDFC Bank. Existing HDFC Ltd home loan customers have been migrated to HDFC Bank.

### Q7. What happens if I miss an HDFC home loan EMI?
A penalty/late charge is levied on the overdue EMI amount. Continuous defaults can lead to lower CIBIL score, legal notice, and eventually property auction. Always inform the bank in advance if you're facing financial difficulty.
