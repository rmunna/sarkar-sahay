## What is the Uttarakhand Old Age Pension?

**Vridhavastha Pension Yojana** is Uttarakhand government's monthly pension for **senior citizens aged 60 years and above** who belong to BPL or poor families. The current pension amount is:

- **₹1,200/month** per beneficiary

The scheme is implemented by the **Department of Social Welfare, Uttarakhand** and payments go directly to beneficiaries' Aadhaar-linked bank accounts via DBT.

## Who is Eligible?

**You ARE eligible if:**
- You are **60 years or older**
- You are a **permanent resident of Uttarakhand** (minimum 10 years)
- You belong to a **BPL (Below Poverty Line) family** or have no income
- You are **not a government servant** receiving pension
- You are **not already covered** under another old age pension scheme

---

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar Card** — age and identity proof
2. **Age proof** — Aadhaar (with DoB), school leaving certificate, or birth certificate
3. **BPL ration card** — or income certificate (issued by SDM/Tahsildar)
4. **Domicile certificate** — Uttarakhand residency
5. **Bank passbook** — Aadhaar-linked account in beneficiary's name
6. **Passport-size photograph**
7. **Self-declaration** — no other pension

## How to Apply — Step by Step

### Step 1 — Apply Online

Go to **[ssp.uk.gov.in](https://ssp.uk.gov.in)** (Uttarakhand Social Security Portal):
1. Click on **"Apply for Pension"**
2. Register with Aadhaar and mobile number
3. Select **Vridhavastha (Old Age) Pension**
4. Fill in details and upload documents
5. Submit and note the Application ID

### Step 2 — Apply at District Social Welfare Office

Visit the **District Social Welfare Office (DSWO)** with all documents. The officer fills the application on your behalf.

### Step 3 — Block-Level Verification

Application is sent to the **Block Development Officer (BDO)** who:
- Verifies your age, income, and BPL status
- Confirms you are not receiving other pensions

### Step 4 — Approval and Pension Start

After approval by the DPO/DSWO, monthly pension is credited to your bank account.

## Integrated with IGNOAPS

Uttarakhand integrates the **Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS)** with the state pension:
- Central IGNOAPS: ₹200–₹500/month (from Government of India)
- State top-up: Remaining amount to reach ₹1,200/month total

Combined, you get ₹1,200/month.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. My grandfather is 63 and belongs to a BPL family. He lives in a mountain village in Chamoli. Can he apply?**

Yes. Uttarakhand Vridhavastha Pension covers rural residents including those in mountain villages. He can apply through the Block Development Officer's office in Chamoli.

**2. Is the income limit ₹1 lakh or is it strictly BPL card?**

Primarily BPL card holders are eligible. However, in Uttarakhand, persons with no income (even without a formal BPL card) can apply and the DSWO assesses on a case-by-case basis. An income certificate from Tahsildar is needed.

**3. My pension has not been received for 3 months. What should I do?**

Check [ssp.uk.gov.in](https://ssp.uk.gov.in) for payment status. Visit the DSWO office with your bank passbook. Common issue: Aadhaar not seeded to bank. Get Aadhaar seeding done at your bank.

**4. My father is in his 60s and very ill. Can we do proxy application?**

You can apply on behalf of an elderly parent who cannot physically visit — submit a letter of authorization and their documents. The verification officer may visit the home.

**5. I get ₹600/month from IGNOAPS. Will Uttarakhand add more to reach ₹1,200?**

Yes — the state tops up to ensure the combined pension is ₹1,200/month. If you receive ₹600 from Central, the state contributes ₹600.
