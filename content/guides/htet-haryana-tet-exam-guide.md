# Haryana Teacher Eligibility Test (HTET) Exam Guide — Eligibility, Pattern & Preparation Tips

The Board of School Education Haryana (BSEH) conducts the Haryana Teacher Eligibility Test (HTET) for candidates aspiring to become teachers in government schools in Haryana. Clearing this exam is mandatory for teaching positions in state government schools.

Teaching in Haryana government schools offers job security, a respectable salary, and the opportunity to shape young minds across the state.

## Eligibility Criteria

### Age Limit

| Category | Minimum Age | Maximum Age |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| General | 18 years | 35 years |

*Age relaxation applicable for reserved categories as per Haryana Government norms.*

### Educational Qualification

**Paper 1 (Primary Level: Classes 1-5):**
- Senior Secondary (12th) with at least 50% marks AND D.El.Ed/D.Ed/BTC
- OR Graduation with B.Ed from NCTE recognized institution

**Paper 2 (Upper Primary: Classes 6-8):**
- Graduation with at least 50% marks AND B.Ed from NCTE recognized institution

## Exam Pattern

### Paper 1 (Primary Level)

| Subject | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| Child Development & Pedagogy | 30 | 30 |
| Language I | 30 | 30 |
| Language II | 30 | 30 |
| Mathematics | 30 | 30 |
| Environmental Studies | 30 | 30 |
| **Total** | **150** | **150** |

### Paper 2 (Upper Primary Level)

| Subject | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| Child Development & Pedagogy | 30 | 30 |
| Language I | 30 | 30 |
| Language II | 30 | 30 |
| Mathematics & Science OR Social Studies | 60 | 60 |
| **Total** | **150** | **150** |

- **Duration:** 2 hours 30 minutes per paper
- **No negative marking**
- **Qualifying marks:** General 60%, Reserved categories 55%

## Syllabus Overview

### Child Development & Pedagogy
- Theories of child development (Piaget, Vygotsky, Kohlberg), learning and motivation, inclusive education, assessment and evaluation, NCF 2005, RTE Act 2009

### Language I & II
- Comprehension, grammar, vocabulary, pedagogy of language development

### Mathematics
- Number system, operations, geometry, measurement, data handling, pedagogy of mathematics

### Environmental Studies (Paper 1)
- Family, food, shelter, water, animals, travel, things we make, pedagogy of EVS

### Mathematics & Science / Social Studies (Paper 2)
- Subject content and pedagogy for upper primary level

## How to Apply

1. Visit the official website at [haryanatet.in](https://haryanatet.in)
2. Click on the HTET TET notification
3. Register with email and mobile number
4. Fill in personal, educational details and select paper
5. Upload photo and signature
6. Pay application fee and submit

## Application Fee

| Category | Fee |
|----------|-----|
| General | ₹500 - ₹700 |
| OBC | ₹400 - ₹500 |
| SC/ST | ₹200 - ₹300 |

*Exact fee varies per notification cycle.*

## Salary & Benefits

After clearing TET and subsequent teacher recruitment:
- **Primary Teacher:** ₹25,000 – ₹45,000 per month (varies by state)
- **Upper Primary Teacher:** ₹30,000 – ₹55,000 per month
- DA, HRA, medical benefits, pension (NPS)
- Summer and winter vacations

## Preparation Tips

1. **NCERT is foundation:** Study NCERT textbooks for relevant classes thoroughly
2. **Child Development theories:** Master key theories — this is high-scoring
3. **Pedagogy matters:** Don't just study content — understand teaching methods
4. **Previous papers:** Solve past HTET TET papers for pattern familiarity
5. **Time management:** Practice completing 150 questions within 150 minutes

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the validity of HTET TET certificate?
Validity varies — typically 5-7 years. Check the latest notification for current rules.

### Can I appear for both Paper 1 and Paper 2?
Yes, you can appear for both papers in the same exam cycle.

### Is HTET TET enough to get a teaching job?
No, TET is only an eligibility test. You must separately apply for teacher recruitment.

### Is there negative marking in HTET TET?
No, there is no negative marking.

### What is the qualifying percentage?
General: 60% (90/150), Reserved categories: 55% (82.5/150).

### Can candidates from other states appear?
Yes, HTET TET is generally open to all Indian citizens.
