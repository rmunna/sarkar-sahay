# Telangana Government Schemes and Services – Complete Guide

Telangana, India's youngest state formed in 2014, has developed an extensive welfare framework covering farmer support, social pensions, women's empowerment, and education. Following the Congress government's election in December 2023 under CM Revanth Reddy, several flagship schemes were renamed or restructured — Rythu Bandhu became Rythu Bharosa, and new programmes like Gruha Jyothi, Mahalakshmi, and Indiramma Indlu were launched as part of six poll guarantees. This guide covers every major Telangana government scheme with eligibility, benefits, and application details.

## Key Government Portals in Telangana

- **Telangana State Portal** – [telangana.gov.in](https://www.telangana.gov.in/) – Official state government website

## Agriculture Schemes

### Rythu Bharosa (formerly Rythu Bandhu) – Farmer Investment Support

Telangana's flagship agriculture scheme, **renamed from Rythu Bandhu to Rythu Bharosa on 26 January 2025** by the Congress government. Now provides **₹12,000 per acre per year** (₹6,000 per season) directly to farmer bank accounts for crop investment. Lands suitable for cultivation only are eligible — non-agricultural and commercial lands are excluded under the new guidelines.

**Eligibility:**
- All land-owning farmers in Telangana with cultivable land
- Land must be recorded in Dharani portal
- Both small and large farmers eligible (non-cultivable land excluded)
- Tenant farmers excluded (separate schemes available)

**Official Portal:** [rythubharosa.telangana.gov.in](https://rythubharosa.telangana.gov.in/)

Read the complete guide: [Rythu Bandhu Telangana](/guide/rythu-bandhu-telangana) | [Telangana Rythu Bandhu Scheme](/guide/telangana-rythu-bandhu-scheme)

### Rythu Bima – Farmer Life Insurance

Free life insurance of ₹5 lakh for enrolled farmers aged 18-59. Premium is paid entirely by the state government. In case of a farmer's death (natural or accidental), the nominee receives the insured amount.

### Agriculture Subsidies

- Subsidized seeds, fertilizers, and pesticides
- Farm mechanization support
- Micro-irrigation (drip and sprinkler) subsidies
- Soil health cards

## Social Security and Pension – Aasara Pensions

Telangana's Aasara Pension scheme is one of India's most generous social security programmes:

### Pension Categories and Amounts

- **Old Age Pension** – ₹4,016 per month for persons aged 57+ (women) / 65+ (men)
- **Widow Pension** – ₹4,016 per month
- **Disability Pension** – ₹4,016 per month for 40%+ disability
- **Toddy Tappers Pension** – ₹4,016 per month
- **Filaria Patients Pension** – ₹4,016 per month
- **AIDS Patients Pension** – ₹4,016 per month
- **Single Women Pension** – ₹4,016 per month

## Women and Child Welfare Schemes

### Kalyana Lakshmi / Shaadi Mubarak

Marriage assistance of **₹1,00,116** for women from SC, ST, BC, and minority communities. Kalyana Lakshmi is for Hindu beneficiaries and Shaadi Mubarak for Muslim beneficiaries.

**Eligibility:**
- Bride must be 18+ years old
- Family income below ₹2 lakh per annum
- Must belong to SC, ST, BC, EBC, or minority community
- Apply within one year of marriage

Read the complete guide: [Kalyana Lakshmi Shaadi Mubarak Telangana](/guide/kalyana-lakshmi-shaadi-mubarak-telangana)

### MCH Kit (formerly KCR Kit / Amma Odi) – Mother and Child Health Kit

Renamed from KCR Kit to **MCH Kit (Mother and Child Health Kit)** by the Congress government in January 2024. Nutrition and baby care kit with 15 items for mothers who deliver in government hospitals, along with cash incentive (₹13,000 for girl child, ₹12,000 for boy child).

### Mahalakshmi Scheme

A flagship Congress government scheme providing **₹2,500 per month** to women, **LPG gas cylinders at ₹500**, and **free travel in TSRTC buses**. Part of the six poll guarantees.

### She Teams

Anti-eve-teasing and women's safety initiative by Telangana Police. Women can report harassment through WhatsApp, Hawk Eye app, or by dialing 100.

## Education Schemes

### Telangana Overseas Scholarship

Scholarships for SC, ST, BC, and minority students to pursue higher education abroad. Covers tuition, living expenses, and travel.

### Fee Reimbursement

Tuition fee reimbursement for students from economically weaker families pursuing professional and degree courses in Telangana.

### Gurukul Schools (Residential)

Free residential schooling for SC, ST, BC, and minority students through:
- **TSWREIS** – Telangana Social Welfare Residential Schools
- **TTWREIS** – Telangana Tribal Welfare Residential Schools
- **TMREIS** – Telangana Minorities Residential Schools

### Scholarships

- Pre-Matric and Post-Matric scholarships for SC, ST, BC, minority students

## Health Schemes

### Aarogyasri / Rajiv Aarogyasri

Cashless health insurance providing treatment up to ₹5 lakh per family at empanelled hospitals. Integrated with Ayushman Bharat.

**Coverage:**
- 2,000+ medical procedures
- Cashless at government and empanelled private hospitals
- White ration card holders eligible

See: [Ayushman Bharat vs State Health Schemes](/guide/ayushman-bharat-vs-state-health-schemes)

### 108 and 104 Services

- **108 Emergency Ambulance** – Free emergency transport
- **104 Health Helpline** – Medical advice and grievance redressal

### Telangana Diagnostic Hubs

Free diagnostic services including blood tests, X-rays, ECG, and ultrasound at government diagnostic centres across all districts.

## Housing Schemes

### Indiramma Indlu – Housing Scheme

The Congress government's flagship housing initiative replacing the earlier 2BHK Housing scheme. Aims to build **4.5 lakh permanent homes** with a budget of **₹22,000 crore**. "Mana Illu… Mana Kutumbam" — providing housing to economically weaker families.

**Eligibility:**
- Houseless families
- Income below prescribed limits
- Priority to SC, ST, minorities, and widows

**Official Portal:** [indirammaindlu.telangana.gov.in](https://indirammaindlu.telangana.gov.in/)

## Gruha Jyothi – Free Electricity

Congress government scheme providing **free electricity up to 200 units per month** to eligible domestic households. Households consuming 200 units or less receive a zero electricity bill. Consumption exceeding 200 units is charged at regular tariff. Launched in February 2024 as part of the six poll guarantees.

## Dalit Bandhu – SC Empowerment

A revolutionary scheme providing **₹10 lakh** as a direct grant to every Dalit (SC) family for setting up a business or livelihood enterprise. No bank loan or subsidy — a straight grant.

Read the complete guide: [Telangana Dalit Bandhu Scheme](/guide/telangana-dalit-bandhu-scheme)

## How to Apply for Telangana Government Schemes

### Online Application

1. **Visit MeeSeva Portal** – [meeseva.telangana.gov.in](https://meeseva.telangana.gov.in/) or scheme-specific portal
2. **Register/Login** – Use Aadhaar, mobile number, or MeeSeva ID
3. **Select the scheme** – Browse by department
4. **Fill application** – Enter personal and eligibility details
5. **Upload documents** – Aadhaar, ration card, income certificate, bank passbook
6. **Submit and track** – Use application number for status checks

### Offline Application

1. Visit the nearest **MeeSeva centre** or **Tahsildar office**
2. Carry original documents and photocopies
3. Fill the application form
4. Submit with required enclosures
5. Collect acknowledgment receipt

### Common Documents Required

- Aadhaar card
- White ration card
- Income certificate
- Caste certificate (for category-based schemes)
- Bank passbook with IFSC
- Passport-size photographs
- Land documents (for Rythu Bandhu)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I check my Rythu Bharosa (formerly Rythu Bandhu) status?**
Visit the [Rythu Bharosa portal](https://rythubharosa.telangana.gov.in/) or check with your local agriculture officer. Payment status is also available through Dharani portal.

**Can tenant farmers get Rythu Bharosa?**
Currently, Rythu Bharosa is only for land-owning farmers with cultivable land. Tenant farmers can access other central schemes like PM-KISAN.

**How do I apply for Aasara Pension?**
Apply through the MeeSeva portal or visit the Tahsildar office with age proof, income certificate, and Aadhaar.
