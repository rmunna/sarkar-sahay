# Passport Application Status Check Online: Track Your File

After submitting your passport application at the Passport Seva Kendra, the wait can feel long. Fortunately, the Ministry of External Affairs provides multiple ways to track your passport application status — online, via SMS, and through the mPassport Seva app. This guide explains every method and helps you understand each stage of processing.

## What You Need to Track Status

- **File Number** — A 15-character alphanumeric code (format: XX-1234567890123) printed on your acknowledgement receipt
- **Date of Birth** — As mentioned in your application

> **Don't have your file number?** Check the email/SMS you received after booking your PSK appointment, or log in to your Passport Seva account.

---

## Method 1: Track on Passport Seva Website

This is the most reliable and detailed tracking method:

1. Visit [Passport Seva Status Tracker](https://www.passportindia.gov.in/AppOnlineProject/statusTracker/trackStatusInp498498)
2. Select **Application Type** → Normal/Tatkal Passport
3. Enter your **File Number**
4. Enter your **Date of Birth**
5. Click **Track Status**

The page will display:
- Current application status
- Date of each status change
- Police verification status
- Dispatch details (if passport has been printed and sent)

---

## Method 2: Track via mPassport Seva App

The official **mPassport Seva** app provides status tracking on your phone:

1. Download from [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mpassport.seva) or Apple App Store
2. Open the app and tap **Track Application Status**
3. Select application type
4. Enter **File Number** and **Date of Birth**
5. Tap **Track Status**

The app also allows you to:
- Book appointments
- Locate nearest PSK/POPSK
- Calculate fees
- Check document requirements

---

## Method 3: Track via SMS

You can check passport status via SMS without internet:

**Send an SMS in this format:**

```
STATUS <File Number>
```

**Send to:** 9704100100

**Example:** `STATUS AB-1234567890123`

You'll receive an SMS reply with your current application status.

---

## Method 4: Track via Passport Seva Account

If you have a registered account:

1. Visit [passportindia.gov.in](https://passportindia.gov.in/)
2. **Log in** with your credentials
3. Go to **View Saved/Submitted Applications**
4. Your application status will be visible next to each application

This method also lets you view your complete application history.

---

## Method 5: Call Passport Seva Helpline

- **Toll-free number:** 1800-258-1800
- **Available:** Monday to Saturday, 8 AM to 10 PM
- Provide your file number and the agent will share your status

## Understanding Passport Application Stages

Your passport application goes through these stages in order:

| Stage | Status Message | What It Means |
|-------|---------------|---------------|
| 1 | Application Registered | Application submitted online |
| 2 | Appointment Booked | PSK visit date confirmed |
| 3 | Application Processed at PSK | Documents verified at Passport Seva Kendra |
| 4 | Granted | Application approved by Passport Officer |
| 5 | Pending for Police Verification | Verification request sent to police |
| 6 | Police Verification Initiated | SP office received the request |
| 7 | Police Verification Report Received – Clear | Police gave positive report |
| 8 | Passport Printed | Physical passport printed |
| 9 | Passport Dispatched | Sent via Speed Post |
| 10 | Passport Delivered | Received by applicant |

### Special Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Under Review with Senior Officer | Needs additional review (not necessarily a problem) |
| Pending for Additional Documents | You need to submit more documents |
| Police Verification – Adverse | Police report has concerns; clarification needed |
| Application Suspended | Serious issue found; contact RPO |

## How to Track Passport Delivery After Dispatch

Once your status shows **"Passport Dispatched"**, a Speed Post tracking number is provided:

1. Note the **speed post consignment number** from the status page
2. Visit [India Post Tracking](https://www.indiapost.gov.in/_layouts/15/dop.portal.tracking/trackconsignment.aspx)
3. Enter the consignment number and track delivery

Passport delivery via speed post typically takes **3–7 days** after dispatch.

## Processing Timelines

| Application Type | Typical Timeline |
|-----------------|-----------------|
| Normal (Fresh) | 30–45 days |
| Normal (Reissue, same address) | 15–20 days |
| Tatkal (Fresh) | 1–3 days (passport issued before police verification) |
| Tatkal (Reissue) | 1–3 days |

> **Note:** These are typical timelines. Actual processing depends on police verification speed and RPO workload.

## Important Tips

1. **Check status once a week** — checking daily won't speed up the process
2. **Save your file number** in a safe place — you'll need it for all tracking
3. **If stuck at police verification**, see our [police verification delay guide](/guide/passport-police-verification-delayed-fix) for escalation steps
4. **Status not updating?** Allow 2–3 working days for status updates after each stage
5. **For Tatkal passports**, the passport is dispatched first and police verification happens later

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I find my passport file number?

Your file number is printed on the **acknowledgement receipt** given at the PSK. It's also sent via email/SMS when you submit your online application. Format: XX-1234567890123.

### Why does my status show "Pending for Police Verification" for weeks?

Police verification delays are common due to workload at local police stations. If it's been more than 3 weeks, visit your local police station or escalate to the SP office.

### Can I track someone else's passport status?

Yes. You only need the **file number** and **date of birth** to track any passport application. No login is required for the public status tracker.

### What does "Granted" status mean?

"Granted" means the Passport Officer has approved your application. The passport will be printed and dispatched soon. This is a positive status.

### How long after "Passport Printed" will I receive it?

After printing, the passport is dispatched within **1–2 days**. Speed post delivery takes **3–7 days** depending on your location.

### My status shows "Application Suspended." What should I do?

Contact the Regional Passport Office (RPO) immediately. You may need to visit the RPO with original documents. Call the Passport Seva helpline (1800-258-1800) for guidance.

### Can I track my passport application without internet?

Yes. Send an SMS in the format `STATUS <File Number>` to **9704100100** to receive your current status via SMS.
