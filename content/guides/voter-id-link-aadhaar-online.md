# Link Voter ID with Aadhaar: How to Link Online and Offline

The Election Commission of India has introduced linking of Voter ID (EPIC) with [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) to clean up the electoral roll by removing duplicate entries. This linking is done through **Form 6B** and is currently **voluntary**. This guide covers all methods to link your Voter ID with Aadhaar.

## Why Link Voter ID with Aadhaar?

The primary purpose of Voter ID-Aadhaar linking is to:

- **Remove duplicate voter entries** across multiple constituencies
- **Authenticate voter identity** using Aadhaar's biometric database
- **Clean the electoral roll** of fake and multiple registrations
- **Improve electoral integrity** across India

**Important:** This linking is **voluntary** and not mandatory. Your voter ID will NOT be cancelled if you do not link it with Aadhaar.

---

## Who Should Link?

- All registered voters with a valid [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) and Voter ID
- It is especially useful if you suspect duplicate registrations in your name
- New voters can link during the registration process itself

---

## Method 1: Link Online at Voters.eci.gov.in

### Step 1: Visit the Portal
Go to [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in/) and click on **"Aadhaar Authentication"** or **"Form 6B"**.

### Step 2: Login
- Login with your registered mobile number and OTP
- Or create a new account if you don't have one

### Step 3: Enter Voter ID Details
- Enter your **EPIC number** (Voter ID card number)
- Your registered details will be fetched automatically

### Step 4: Enter Aadhaar Number
- Enter your **12-digit Aadhaar number**
- Verify the details match (name, date of birth)

### Step 5: OTP Verification
- An OTP will be sent to your **Aadhaar-registered mobile number**
- Enter the OTP to authenticate

### Step 6: Submit
- Confirm the linking
- You will receive a confirmation message
- **Save the acknowledgement** for your records

---

## Method 2: Link via SMS

You can link your Voter ID with Aadhaar by sending an SMS.

**SMS format:**
```
UIDLINK <EPIC Number> <Aadhaar Number>
```

**Send to:** 166 or 51969 (check the latest number on [eci.gov.in](https://www.eci.gov.in/))

**Example:**
```
UIDLINK ABC1234567 123456789012
```

You will receive a confirmation SMS after successful linking.

**Note:** The SMS must be sent from the mobile number registered with your Aadhaar.

---

## Method 3: Link via Voter Helpline App

### Steps:

1. Download the **Voter Helpline** app from Google Play Store or Apple App Store
2. Open the app and login with your mobile number
3. Navigate to **"Aadhaar Linking"** or **"Form 6B"** section
4. Enter your **EPIC number**
5. Enter your **12-digit Aadhaar number**
6. Verify via OTP sent to Aadhaar-registered mobile
7. Submit and save the confirmation

---

## Method 4: Link Offline

### At the ERO/BLO Office

1. Visit your local **Electoral Registration Officer (ERO)** office or contact your **BLO**
2. Fill **Form 6B** (available at the office or download from voters.eci.gov.in)
3. Provide your EPIC number and Aadhaar number on the form
4. Submit with your signature
5. The ERO will process the linking

### At Common Service Centres (CSC)

Visit your nearest **CSC** with your Voter ID and Aadhaar card. The CSC operator can submit Form 6B on your behalf.

---

## How to Check Aadhaar-Voter ID Linking Status

1. Visit [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in/)
2. Login to your account
3. Check under "Aadhaar Authentication" section for linking status
4. Or call helpline **1950** to verify the status

---

## How to Delink Voter ID from Aadhaar

If you want to remove the Aadhaar linking from your Voter ID:

1. Visit [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in/) and login
2. Navigate to the Aadhaar linking section
3. Select the option to **delink** or **remove Aadhaar association**
4. Submit the request
5. You can also contact your ERO office to request delinking

**Note:** Since the linking is voluntary, you have the right to delink at any time. Your voter registration remains valid regardless of Aadhaar linking status.

## Important Tips

1. **Linking is voluntary** — your voter ID will not be cancelled if you don't link
2. **Aadhaar-registered mobile number is needed** for OTP verification during online linking
3. **Name mismatch may cause issues** — if your name differs significantly between Voter ID and Aadhaar, update one of them first
4. **One Aadhaar can link to only one Voter ID** — this helps eliminate duplicate registrations
5. **You can delink anytime** — the process is reversible

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. Is it mandatory to link Voter ID with Aadhaar?

No. Linking Voter ID with Aadhaar is **voluntary**. The Election Commission collects Aadhaar information to clean electoral rolls, but non-linking does not affect your voter registration or voting rights.

### Q2. Will my voter ID be cancelled if I don't link Aadhaar?

No. Your voter ID will not be cancelled for not linking with Aadhaar. However, if duplicates are found during electoral roll revision, the ECI may seek verification.

### Q3. What is Form 6B?

Form 6B is the official form for furnishing Aadhaar number for Voter ID-Aadhaar linking. It can be submitted online at voters.eci.gov.in, through the Voter Helpline App, or offline at the ERO office.

### Q4. My name is different in Voter ID and Aadhaar. Can I still link?

Minor spelling variations may be accepted. However, if the name is significantly different, it is recommended to first correct the name in either your [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) or Voter ID before attempting to link.

### Q5. Can I link my family members' Voter IDs with their Aadhaar?

Each person must link their own Voter ID with their own Aadhaar. You cannot link on someone else's behalf online (as OTP goes to their Aadhaar-registered mobile). For offline linking, each person must sign their own Form 6B.

### Q6. I linked my Aadhaar but want to delink now. Is it possible?

Yes. You can delink your Aadhaar from your Voter ID at any time through the voters.eci.gov.in portal or by contacting your ERO office. The linking is reversible.

### Q7. What happens to my data after Aadhaar-Voter ID linking?

The ECI uses the Aadhaar data only for de-duplication of the electoral roll. As per the ECI's policy, Aadhaar data is kept confidential and is not shared with any third party. The linking is governed by the provisions of the Registration of Electors Rules.
