# NSP Scholarship Amount Not Received? Track & Fix

Your NSP scholarship application was sanctioned, but the money hasn't arrived in your bank account. This is frustrating but fixable. This guide covers how to track your payment and resolve common disbursement failures.

## How NSP Scholarship Payment Works

After your application is sanctioned on the National Scholarship Portal:

1. The sanctioned amount is sent to **PFMS** (Public Financial Management System)
2. PFMS processes the payment through **DBT** (Direct Benefit Transfer)
3. The amount is credited directly to the student's bank account linked with [Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)

This process can take **1–3 months** after sanction, depending on the scheme and state.

## How to Track Your Scholarship Payment

### Method 1: Check on NSP Portal
1. Log in at [scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in/)
2. Go to **"Application Status"**
3. Check if the status shows **"Sanctioned and Disbursed"** or **"Sanctioned — Payment Pending"**

### Method 2: Track on PFMS
1. Visit [pfms.nic.in](https://pfms.nic.in/)
2. Click **"Know Your Payment"**
3. Select the payment type (search by bank account number or Aadhaar)
4. Enter your details and captcha
5. The result will show if payment was processed, pending, or failed

### Method 3: Check Bank Statement
- Log in to your net banking or check your passbook
- Look for a credit from "PFMS" or the scheme name
- DBT credits show the scheme code in the transaction narration

## Common Payment Issues and Fixes

### 1. Bank Account Mismatch

**Problem:** The bank account number on NSP doesn't match your actual account, or you changed accounts after submission.

**Fix:**
1. Log in to NSP and check the bank account details in your profile
2. If the account number has a typo, contact the NSP helpline (0120-6619540) to request correction
3. If you have closed the old account, you need to update to a new account — this may require contacting the state nodal agency
4. The bank account **must be in the student's name** (not parent's)
5. Joint accounts are generally not accepted

### 2. Aadhaar Not Seeded with Bank Account

**Problem:** Your Aadhaar number is not linked (seeded) with the bank account, causing DBT failure.

**Why It Matters:** All NSP scholarship payments through DBT require Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts. Without this, PFMS cannot route the payment.

**Fix:**
1. Visit your bank branch with your [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) and passbook
2. Fill the Aadhaar seeding form and submit
3. The bank will link your Aadhaar to your account within 2–7 days
4. Verify seeding status at [resident.uidai.gov.in](https://resident.uidai.gov.in/) → "Check Aadhaar & Bank Account Linking Status"
5. After seeding is confirmed, inform the NSP helpline or state nodal officer to reprocess the payment

### 3. DBT Payment Failed

**Problem:** PFMS shows "Payment Failed" or "Transaction Returned."

**Common Reasons:**
- Bank account is inactive or frozen
- Account is KYC non-compliant
- IFSC code is wrong or bank branch has merged
- Aadhaar seeding mismatch

**Fix:**
1. Ensure your bank account is active — make a small transaction
2. Complete KYC at your bank if pending
3. Check if your bank branch IFSC has changed (common after bank mergers)
4. If the IFSC changed, update it by contacting NSP helpline
5. Once the issue is resolved, the payment will be reprocessed in the next cycle

### 4. State vs Central Scholarship Payment Differences

**Important to Know:**
- **Central schemes** (Post-Matric, Pre-Matric, Merit-cum-Means under central ministries) are paid through PFMS directly
- **State schemes** listed on NSP are sanctioned centrally but disbursed by the respective state government
- State scheme payments may go through state treasury systems, not PFMS

**If Your Scheme Is State-Administered:**
1. Check with your state's scholarship portal (e.g., ekalyan.cg.nic.in, scholarships.up.gov.in)
2. Contact the State Nodal Agency listed on NSP under your scheme details
3. State disbursements often have different (longer) timelines

### 5. Scholarship Sanctioned but Amount Shows Zero

**Problem:** Status shows "Sanctioned" but the amount is ₹0 or much lower than expected.

**Possible Reasons:**
- Maintenance allowance, tuition fee, and other components are disbursed separately
- The institute fee component goes to the institute, not the student
- The "Amount Disbursed" may only show the student's share

**Fix:**
1. Check the scheme guidelines for what components are paid to the student vs institute
2. Contact the institute accounts section to confirm if they received their share
3. If the amount seems wrong, file a grievance on NSP with the calculation details

## Payment Disbursement Timeline

| Stage | Typical Duration |
|---|---|
| Application sanctioned | After district/state verification |
| Payment order to PFMS | 2–6 weeks after sanction |
| PFMS processes to bank | 1–2 weeks after payment order |
| Credit in bank account | 2–5 working days after PFMS processing |

> Delays are common during October–March when most scholarships are disbursed simultaneously.

## Important Tips

1. **Keep your bank account active** — dormant accounts cause payment failure
2. **Complete Aadhaar-bank seeding** before applying; don't wait for sanction
3. **Check PFMS regularly** at [pfms.nic.in](https://pfms.nic.in/) for payment status
4. **Don't change bank accounts** after application submission unless absolutely necessary
5. **Save your Application ID and all receipts** — you'll need them for grievance filing

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. How long does NSP scholarship payment take after sanction?
Typically 1–3 months. Central scheme payments are usually faster than state scheme payments.

### Q2. Can I receive the scholarship in someone else's bank account?
No. The bank account must be in the student's own name and linked with the student's Aadhaar.

### Q3. PFMS shows "No Record Found" when I search — what does this mean?
Either the payment has not been initiated yet, or you are searching with incorrect details. Double-check your bank account number and try searching by Aadhaar number.

### Q4. I received a partial amount. Where is the rest?
Many schemes disburse in installments. Also, the tuition fee component may be sent directly to your institute. Check scheme guidelines.

### Q5. My scholarship was sanctioned last year but I still haven't received payment.
File a grievance on NSP immediately. Also contact the state nodal agency and the NSP helpline at 0120-6619540.

### Q6. Can I link a post office savings account for NSP scholarship?
Yes, if the post office account supports DBT and is Aadhaar-seeded. However, bank accounts are more reliable for DBT payments.
