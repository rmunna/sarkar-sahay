# How to Check Driving Licence Application Status Online

Whether you have applied for a new driving licence, learner's licence, or renewal, you can track your application status online through the Parivahan Sewa (Sarathi) portal. This guide covers all methods to check your DL status.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent platform and is not affiliated with the Ministry of Road Transport or any RTO office. Always verify on official portals.

---

## What is a Driving Licence?

A Driving Licence (DL) is a mandatory document for driving any motor vehicle on Indian roads. It is issued by the Regional Transport Office (RTO) of your state. Types include:

- **Learner's Licence (LL):** Temporary permit valid for 6 months to learn driving
- **Permanent Driving Licence:** Issued after passing the driving test
- **International Driving Permit (IDP):** For driving abroad
- **Commercial DL:** For transport vehicles (LMV-T, HMV, etc.)

---

## How to Check DL Application Status Online

### Method 1: Via Sarathi Parivahan Portal

1. Visit the Sarathi portal at [sarathi.parivahan.gov.in](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in)
2. Select your **State** from the dropdown
3. Click on **"Application Status"** from the menu
4. Enter your **Application Number** (received during application)
5. Enter your **Date of Birth**
6. Enter the CAPTCHA
7. Click **"Submit"**
8. Your DL application status will be displayed

### Method 2: Via Parivahan Sewa Portal

1. Visit [parivahan.gov.in](https://parivahan.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Online Services"** → **"Driving Licence Related Services"**
3. Select your state
4. Navigate to **"Application Status"**
5. Enter your application number and date of birth
6. View your current application status

### Method 3: Check DL Status Using DL Number

If you already have a DL and want to check its validity or details:

1. Visit [parivahan.gov.in](https://parivahan.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Know Your Driving Licence Details"** (under Informational Services)
3. Enter your **DL Number** and **Date of Birth**
4. Enter the CAPTCHA and submit
5. Your DL details, validity, and vehicle class endorsements will be displayed

### Method 4: Via mParivahan App

1. Download the **mParivahan** app from Google Play Store or App Store
2. Register with your mobile number
3. Add your DL by entering the **DL Number** and **Date of Birth**
4. View your DL details, validity, and status
5. You can also store a **digital copy of your DL** in the app (accepted by traffic police)

### Method 5: Visit the RTO Office

If online methods don't work:
1. Visit the RTO where you submitted your application
2. Carry your **application acknowledgement receipt**
3. Enquire at the information counter about your application status

## DL Application Status Meanings

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Submitted** | Application received by the system |
| **Approved** | Application approved; DL will be printed |
| **Slot Booked** | Driving test date has been assigned |
| **Test Passed** | Driving test cleared; DL processing |
| **Printed** | DL card printed; will be dispatched |
| **Dispatched** | DL sent via post to your registered address |
| **Rejected** | Application rejected (reason will be shown) |
| **Pending at RTO** | Awaiting action from the RTO officer |

## Documents Required for Driving Licence

### For Learner's Licence
- **Age Proof:** Birth certificate, class 10 marksheet, or Aadhaar
- **Address Proof:** Aadhaar, passport, voter ID, or utility bill
- **Passport-size Photos:** Recent photographs
- **Medical Certificate:** Form 1A (for transport vehicle applicants)

### For Permanent Driving Licence
- **Learner's Licence:** Valid LL (must wait 30 days after LL issuance)
- **Age & Address Proof:** Same as above
- **LL Test Certificate:** Proof of passing the LL test
- **Driving School Certificate:** From a registered driving school (recommended)

## Processing Time

- **Learner's Licence:** 1-7 days (often same day in some states)
- **Permanent DL (after test):** 7-30 days
- **DL Renewal:** 7-15 days
- **DL Dispatch via Post:** Additional 7-15 days after printing

## Important Tips

1. **Book slot early** — Driving test slots fill up quickly. Book your test date as soon as your LL completes 30 days
2. **Carry original documents** — Take all original documents to the RTO on your test day
3. **Use mParivahan app** — Your digital DL on the mParivahan app is accepted by traffic police as a valid document
4. **Renew before expiry** — DL renewal can be done up to 1 year before and 1 year after expiry. Late renewal may require re-testing
5. **Apply for correct vehicle class** — Ensure you apply for the right category (LMV, MCWG, etc.) to avoid issues later

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: How can I check my DL application status without the application number?
You cannot check status without the application number on the Sarathi portal. If you've lost it, visit the RTO office with your ID proof to retrieve your application details.

### Q2: How long does it take to receive the driving licence after passing the test?
After passing the driving test, the DL is usually processed and dispatched within 7-30 days. You can track delivery status on the Sarathi portal.

### Q3: Can I drive with a digital DL on the mParivahan app?
Yes, the digital DL displayed on the mParivahan app is accepted by traffic police across India as per the Motor Vehicles Act amendment. However, carry the physical card for non-traffic situations.

### Q4: My DL application shows "Rejected." What should I do?
Check the rejection reason on the portal. Common reasons include failed driving test, incomplete documents, or age eligibility issues. You can reapply after addressing the reason.

### Q5: Can I apply for DL in a different state from where I have my LL?
Yes, you can apply for a permanent DL in any state. However, you may need to produce your LL and complete the driving test at the RTO of the new state.

### Q6: How do I renew my expired driving licence?
Visit [sarathi.parivahan.gov.in](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in), select your state, and apply for DL renewal under "DL Services." You can renew online and may need to visit the RTO only for biometrics.
