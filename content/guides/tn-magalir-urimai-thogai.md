# Kalaignar Magalir Urimai Thogai – ₹1,000/Month for Women in Tamil Nadu

## What is Kalaignar Magalir Urimai Thogai?

Kalaignar Magalir Urimai Thogai (Women's Right Grant) is a flagship scheme of the Tamil Nadu government providing **₹1,000 per month** to eligible women who are the head of their household. The amount is credited directly to the beneficiary's bank account via DBT. This scheme aims to empower women financially and provide economic security.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Gender:** Must be a **woman** who is the head of the family
2. **Age:** Must be **21 years or above**
3. **Residency:** Must be a **resident of Tamil Nadu**
4. **Ration Card:** Must hold a valid **rice ration card** issued by the Tamil Nadu government
5. **Income:** Annual family income must be below **₹2.5 lakh**
6. **One per family:** Only one woman per ration card/family is eligible

### Who is NOT Eligible?

- Women whose family members are income tax payers
- Women who are government employees or pensioners
- Women whose family owns more than 5 acres of wet land or 10 acres of dry land
- Families owning four-wheelers (excluding autorickshaws used for livelihood)

## Documents Required

- **Ration Card** (Tamil Nadu rice card)
- **Aadhaar Card**
- **Bank Account Passbook** (linked to Aadhaar)
- **Mobile Number** linked to Aadhaar
- **Voter ID** (optional, for address verification)
- **Recent Passport Size Photo**

## How to Apply

### Online Application

1. **Visit** the official Tamil Nadu social welfare portal
2. **Register** with your Aadhaar number and mobile number
3. **Enter** ration card number to fetch family details
4. **Select** the woman applicant from the family list
5. **Upload** required documents
6. **Enter** bank account details
7. **Submit** the application
8. **Note** the application reference number

### Offline Application (Camp Mode)

1. The Tamil Nadu government conducts **special registration camps** at ward/village level
2. **Attend** the camp with original documents and photocopies
3. **Fill** the application form with help from camp officials
4. **Submit** documents and get fingerprint/photo captured
5. **Collect** acknowledgment slip

### At Taluk Office

1. Visit the **Revenue Divisional Office** or **Taluk Office**
2. Submit the application form with all required documents
3. Collect acknowledgment receipt

## Payment Details

| Detail | Information |
|--------|-------------|
| Monthly Amount | ₹1,000 |
| Annual Amount | ₹12,000 |
| Payment Mode | Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) |
| Credited To | Aadhaar-linked bank account |

## Processing Time

- **Verification:** 15-30 days after application
- **First Payment:** 30-45 days after approval
- **Monthly Credit:** Regular monthly transfer

## Important Tips

1. **Aadhaar-Bank Linkage is Mandatory:** Without this, DBT will fail. Ensure your Aadhaar is seeded with your bank account.
2. **Attend Camp Registrations:** The government often conducts special camps — these are the easiest way to apply.
3. **Update Ration Card:** Ensure your ration card details are correct and you are listed as the head of family.
4. **Track Status:** Check your application status through the official portal or by visiting the taluk office.
5. **No Middlemen:** Apply directly — there is no fee for this scheme. Do not pay anyone for applying.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Can unmarried women apply?
**Yes**, if they are 21+ and the head of their household as per the ration card.

### Q2: What if my ration card is in my husband's name?
The applicant must be listed as the head of the family on the ration card. You may need to update the ration card.

### Q3: Can I get both this and the old age pension?
You may need to choose one. Check with the local welfare office for the latest rules on combining benefits.

### Q4: Is the scheme available for all castes?
**Yes.** There is no caste restriction. All women meeting the eligibility criteria can apply regardless of caste or religion.

### Q5: How do I check my payment status?
Visit the official welfare portal or check your bank account statement. You can also call the helpline or visit the taluk office.

### Q6: Will this scheme continue every year?
The scheme is subject to government policy. As of now, it is an ongoing scheme with no announced end date.
