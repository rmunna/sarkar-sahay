# EPFO KYC Not Approved — How to Fix

KYC (Know Your Customer) verification on the EPFO Member Portal is mandatory for filing EPF withdrawals, transfers, and advances online. Your Aadhaar, PAN, and bank account details must all be verified and approved before you can submit any claim. When KYC stays pending or gets rejected, your EPF services are effectively blocked.

This guide covers every common KYC approval failure and the exact steps to fix each one.

## How EPFO KYC Approval Works

There are two KYC approval methods:

1. **Employer-approved KYC** — You upload KYC documents on the Member Portal. Your employer's authorised signatory reviews and approves them via the Employer Portal.
2. **Digitally signed (auto-approved) KYC** — If your Aadhaar is seeded and verified against UAN via UIDAI, the KYC is auto-approved digitally without employer intervention.

You can check your KYC status at: **Member Portal → Manage → KYC**

## Common KYC Status Messages

- **"Pending"** — KYC submitted but employer has not approved yet
- **"Digitally Approved"** — auto-verified via UIDAI/bank — no employer action needed
- **"Rejected"** — employer or system rejected the KYC entry
- **"Available"** — document number entered but not yet submitted for verification
- **"Verified"** — approved and linked to your UAN

## Problem 1: Employer Not Approving KYC

This is the most common KYC issue. You uploaded your Aadhaar, PAN, or bank details but the status stays "Pending" for weeks.

### Why It Happens

- Employer HR has not logged in to the Employer Portal
- Employer does not have a Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) configured
- Your establishment is small and unaware of the approval process
- Employer is deliberately delaying (especially after you resign)

### How to Fix

1. **Contact your HR/accounts department** — Ask them to log in to the EPFO Employer Portal and approve your pending KYC under **Members → KYC Approval**
2. **Use Aadhaar-based digital KYC instead** — If your Aadhaar details (name, date of birth) exactly match your UAN records, submit Aadhaar KYC. It gets auto-approved digitally without employer intervention
3. **If employer is unreachable or has shut down:**
   - File a grievance on [EPF iGMS](https://epfigms.gov.in/) selecting "KYC Related" as the category
   - Visit your regional EPFO office with your UAN, Aadhaar, and employment proof
   - The EPFO field officer can approve KYC directly

### Timeline

- Employer-approved KYC: 2–7 working days (depends entirely on employer)
- Aadhaar digital KYC: instant to 24 hours
- EPFO field office approval: 3–10 working days after visit

## Problem 2: Aadhaar Name Mismatch with UAN

KYC gets rejected because the name on your Aadhaar does not exactly match the name registered against your UAN.

### Common Mismatch Examples

| Aadhaar Name | UAN Name | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| Rajesh Kumar | Rajesh Kumar Singh | Missing surname |
| Mohammed Irfan | Md. Irfan | Abbreviation |
| Priya S | Priya Subramaniam | Initials vs full name |
| Ramesh Chandra | RAMESH CHAND | Spelling difference |

### How to Fix

**Option A: Correct your Aadhaar name** (if UAN name is correct)
1. Visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/) → Update Aadhaar
2. Or visit an Aadhaar Enrolment Centre with supporting ID proof
3. After Aadhaar update (takes 5–10 days), resubmit KYC on EPFO

**Option B: Correct your UAN name** (if Aadhaar name is correct)
1. Log in to the EPFO Member Portal
2. Go to **Manage → Mark Exit** — check if your name is correct
3. Request your employer to correct the name on the Employer Portal under **Member → Modify Basic Details**
4. Alternatively, file a joint declaration (Form 2 with employer attestation) at your EPFO regional office

**Option C: Both names are slightly off**
- Fix whichever is easier/faster first, then resubmit KYC

> **Tip:** Name matching is strict. Even extra spaces, initials vs full names, or middle name presence/absence causes rejection. Make names character-for-character identical.

## Problem 3: Bank Account Verification Failed

Your bank KYC shows "Rejected" or stays stuck on "Pending."

### Common Causes

- Bank account IFSC code is wrong or branch has merged with another bank
- Account holder name does not match UAN name
- Account is a joint account (only individual accounts are accepted)
- Bank account is closed or dormant
- Account type is not savings (salary accounts usually work)

### How to Fix

1. **Verify IFSC code** — Search the correct IFSC on [RBI's IFSC lookup](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/IFSCMICRDetails.aspx) or your bank's website. Many bank branches have merged and IFSC codes changed
2. **Ensure the account is in your name only** — Joint accounts are rejected
3. **Name on bank account must match UAN name** — If different, update your name at the bank first
4. **Re-enter bank details on the portal:**
   - Member Portal → Manage → KYC → Bank Account
   - Delete the old entry if rejected, and add a fresh one with correct details
5. **Use a different bank account** if the current one has issues
6. **Penny-drop verification:** EPFO verifies bank accounts by depositing ₹1 to confirm the account is active and name matches. If this fails, you get rejection

## Problem 4: PAN Not Verified

PAN KYC shows "Pending" or "Rejected."

### Common Causes

- PAN name does not match UAN name (same strict matching as Aadhaar)
- PAN number entered incorrectly (typo)
- PAN is deactivated or marked as duplicate by Income Tax department
- PAN is not linked to Aadhaar (mandatory since July 2023 — inactive PANs cannot be verified)

### How to Fix

1. **Check PAN-Aadhaar linkage** — Visit [incometax.gov.in](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/link-aadhaar-faq) and verify your PAN is active. Inactive PANs fail verification
2. **Link PAN to Aadhaar** if not done — pay the ₹1,000 fee if applicable and link via the Income Tax portal
3. **Correct PAN name** — If the name on PAN differs from UAN, request a PAN correction at [NSDL/Protean](https://www.onlineservices.nsdl.com/paam/endUserRegisterContact.html) or [UTIITSL](https://www.pan.utiitsl.com/)
4. **Re-enter PAN on EPFO portal** after corrections

## Problem 5: Digital Signing Failed

Aadhaar-based KYC approval fails with errors like "Digital Signing Failed" or "e-Sign not completed."

### Common Causes

- UIDAI server is down or overloaded
- Aadhaar mobile number is not updated (OTP goes to old number)
- Browser compatibility issues (EPFO portal works best on Chrome/Edge)
- Pop-up blocker preventing the e-Sign window

### How to Fix

1. **Try during off-peak hours** — Early morning (6–9 AM) or late evening. UIDAI servers are least loaded then
2. **Ensure Aadhaar mobile number is current** — OTP for digital signing goes to your Aadhaar-registered mobile. Update it at the nearest Aadhaar centre if changed
3. **Use Chrome or Edge browser** — Disable pop-up blockers for `epfindia.gov.in`
4. **Clear browser cache and cookies** before retrying
5. **If persistent:** Try after 24–48 hours. UIDAI server issues are usually temporary

## Problem 6: How to Update or Re-submit KYC Details

If your KYC was rejected or has wrong details:

1. Log in to [EPFO Member Portal](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in/memberinterface/)
2. Go to **Manage → KYC**
3. For rejected entries: click on the document, correct the details, and re-submit
4. For new entries: select the document type (Aadhaar/PAN/Bank), enter details, and click **Save**
5. If Aadhaar details match UAN exactly, it auto-verifies. Otherwise, it goes to your employer for approval

> **You cannot edit approved KYC.** If an approved entry has wrong details, you need to contact EPFO or your employer to de-link it first.

## How to Complain If Employer Delays KYC Approval

If your employer ignores repeated requests:

### Step 1: File Grievance on EPF iGMS
1. Visit [epfigms.gov.in](https://epfigms.gov.in/)
2. Register/login with your UAN
3. Select grievance category: **KYC Change Request / KYC Approval Pending**
4. Describe the issue clearly with your UAN, establishment code, and how long KYC has been pending
5. You get a grievance number — EPFO typically responds within 15–30 days

### Step 2: Contact EPFO via Other Channels
- **EPFO Helpline:** 1800-118-005 (toll-free)
- **Email:** Contact your regional PF office — find the email at [epfindia.gov.in → Regional Offices](https://www.epfindia.gov.in/site_en/Regional_offices.php)
- **Visit EPFO office** in person with:
  - UAN printout
  - Aadhaar card
  - Latest salary slip showing PF deduction
  - Bank passbook
  - Any communication with employer about KYC

### Step 3: Escalate If No Response
- File complaint on [CPGRAMS](https://pgportal.gov.in/) (Central Government grievance portal)
- Contact the EPFO Zonal/Head Office

## Quick Checklist Before Submitting KYC

- [ ] Name on Aadhaar, PAN, bank account, and UAN are **exactly identical**
- [ ] Aadhaar is linked to your current mobile number
- [ ] PAN is linked to Aadhaar and is active
- [ ] Bank account is individual (not joint), active, and savings type
- [ ] IFSC code is current (check for branch mergers)
- [ ] Using Chrome or Edge browser with pop-ups allowed

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does EPFO KYC approval take?
Aadhaar-based digital KYC is approved within minutes to 24 hours. Employer-approved KYC depends on how fast your employer acts — typically 2–7 working days, but can take longer.

### Can I withdraw PF without KYC approval?
No. Aadhaar, PAN, and bank KYC must all be approved before you can file any online claim (withdrawal, transfer, or advance).

### My employer shut down. How do I get KYC approved?
File a grievance on [EPF iGMS](https://epfigms.gov.in/) or visit your EPFO regional office. The field officer can approve KYC when the employer is non-functional.

### KYC shows "Available" but not "Verified" — what does this mean?
"Available" means you entered the document number but it has not been submitted for verification. Click on it and submit it for approval.

### Can I approve my own KYC without employer?
Yes, if you use Aadhaar-based KYC and your Aadhaar details exactly match your UAN details. This gets digitally verified without employer involvement.
