# Maharashtra Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana Payment Not Received? Fix Guide

**Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana** gives ₹1,500/month (₹18,000/year) to eligible women in Maharashtra aged 21–65 with household income below ₹2.5 lakh. If your payment isn't coming, here's how to track and fix the issue.

> **Quick check**: Payment usually arrives between the 1st and 10th of each month via DBT. Check your bank statement for transactions from "Maharashtra Gov" or "PFMS."

## Check Payment Status Online

### ladakibahin.maharashtra.gov.in — Official Portal

1. Visit **[ladakibahin.maharashtra.gov.in](https://ladakibahin.maharashtra.gov.in/)**
2. Click **"Application Status"** or **"Payment Status"**
3. Enter your **Application Number** or **Aadhaar Number**
4. View: Application approval, payment release dates, credited amount

### Via PFMS

1. Visit **[pfms.nic.in](https://pfms.nic.in/)** → **"Know Your Payment"**
2. Enter your bank account number
3. Check if Maharashtra government DBT has been credited

## Common Reasons Payment Doesn't Come

| Reason | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| **Aadhaar not seeded in bank (NPCI mapper)** | Visit bank branch; request "Aadhaar seeding for DBT" |
| **Bank account inactive/dormant** | Reactivate by making any transaction at branch |
| **Application status still "Pending"** | Visit Aapli Sarkar Seva Kendra; application under review |
| **Application rejected — ineligible** | Check rejection reason; appeal at district women's welfare office |
| **Bank account number changed** | Update at Aapli Sarkar Seva Kendra with new passbook |
| **Wrong bank account registered** | Same fix — update through official channel |
| **Age/income document mismatch** | Visit Sevakendra with correct documents |

## Fix 1: Aadhaar Not Linked to Bank (NPCI Mapper)

**Most common issue for missed payments:**

1. Visit your **bank branch** with Aadhaar card + bank passbook
2. Tell the bank staff: **"I want to seed my Aadhaar to NPCI mapper for DBT"**
3. Bank updates the NPCI system within 24–48 hours
4. Next month's payment will come to your account; missed months may be credited together

## Fix 2: Application Shows "Pending" for Months

If your application was submitted but status remains "Pending" for more than 60 days:

1. Visit your nearest **Aapli Sarkar Seva Kendra** (government service centre) with:
   - Application acknowledgement receipt
   - Aadhaar card
   - Bank passbook
2. Ask staff to check why the application is stuck
3. Alternatively, visit **Tahsildar's office** or **Women and Child Development (WCD) district office**
4. If rejected: get the rejection reason in writing; appeal to district-level officer

## Fix 3: "Payment Successful" at Portal But Money Not in Bank

If the portal shows payment released but your bank account doesn't show it:

1. Check PFMS → Know Your Payment to see if government sent the money
2. If PFMS shows "Credit to Bank": The bank received the transfer. Check if:
   - You're looking at the correct account (check last 4 digits of account number in PFMS)
   - Bank hasn't applied any charges that offset the credit
3. Visit your bank branch with the PFMS transaction ID and request the bank to trace it
4. Typical bank processing delay: 1–3 working days after government release

## Fix 4: Payment Stopped After First Few Months

If you received payments initially but they stopped:

1. **Re-verification**: Maharashtra periodically re-verifies beneficiary eligibility. If you didn't respond to a verification request, your payments may be paused
2. **Income increase**: If household income exceeded ₹2.5 lakh and the government data was updated, you may have been removed
3. **eKYC pending**: If Aadhaar-based eKYC was required and not completed
4. **Visit Aapli Sarkar Seva Kendra** immediately with all documents to resolve

## Fix 5: Bank Account Changed

If you changed your bank account after enrolling:

1. Visit **Aapli Sarkar Seva Kendra** with:
   - Old bank account closure letter (or passbook showing zero balance/closed)
   - New bank passbook (with Aadhaar already seeded in new bank)
   - Aadhaar card
2. Staff will update the bank account in the scheme database
3. Takes 15–30 days for the update to reflect and payments to resume

## Majhi Ladki Bahin Eligibility — Quick Reference

| Criteria | Requirement |
|----------|-------------|
| **Age** | 21–65 years |
| **Household Income** | Below ₹2.5 lakh/year |
| **Residency** | Domicile of Maharashtra |
| **Exclusion** | Government employee families, income taxpayers, families with 4-wheel vehicles |
| **Document** | Yellow/Orange ration card or below-income certificate |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many months can be credited at once if payment was missed?

If payment was missed due to bank/Aadhaar issues, the pending months are typically credited together once the issue is resolved — either as a lump sum or over 2–3 payment cycles. The government does not cap arrear payments.

### My application is "Approved" but payment never started. Who should I contact?

First check if your bank account is correctly registered and Aadhaar-seeded. If yes, visit the WCD district office — there may be a technical issue in the database that field staff can resolve but the helpline may not be aware of.

### The portal shows wrong bank account. How to update?

Visit Aapli Sarkar Seva Kendra with your correct bank passbook + Aadhaar. Don't update bank account details anywhere else — only through official channels to avoid fraud.

### I'm 66 years old. Am I still eligible?

Majhi Ladki Bahin covers ages 21–65. At 66, you are no longer in the eligible age range (unless the government extended the age — verify at ladakibahin.maharashtra.gov.in for current eligibility).
