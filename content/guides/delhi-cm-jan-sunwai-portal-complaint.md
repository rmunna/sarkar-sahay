# Delhi CM Jan Sunwai Portal — How to File Complaints Online

The **CM Jan Sunwai Portal** is a unified digital grievance redressal platform launched by Delhi Chief Minister Rekha Gupta on **21 February 2026**. It allows residents of Delhi to register complaints related to **all Delhi government departments** — including the Municipal Corporation of Delhi (MCD), Delhi Development Authority (DDA), Delhi Police, and others — on a single platform.

The portal replaces the earlier fragmented complaint systems with a streamlined, transparent mechanism that includes real-time tracking, SMS updates, and a three-tier escalation system.

> **Official Portal:** [https://cmjansunwai.delhi.gov.in](https://cmjansunwai.delhi.gov.in)

## What Complaints Can You File?

You can register complaints related to any Delhi government department, including but not limited to:

- **Water supply** issues (Delhi Jal Board)
- **Electricity** problems (BSES, Tata Power Delhi)
- **Roads and infrastructure** (PWD, MCD)
- **Sanitation and garbage** (MCD)
- **Pension** delays
- **DDA** services
- **Delhi Police** matters
- **Education and school** issues
- **Healthcare** services
- **Social welfare** schemes

## How to Register a Complaint — Step-by-Step (Online)

### Method 1: Via the CM Jan Sunwai Portal

1. Visit [https://cmjansunwai.delhi.gov.in](https://cmjansunwai.delhi.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Register a Complaint"**
3. Enter your **mobile number** — an OTP will be sent for verification
4. Enter the OTP and proceed
5. Fill in the complaint details:
   - **Description** of the issue
   - **Concerned department** (water, electricity, pension, etc.)
   - **Locality/area** of the complaint
6. **Upload supporting documents** (photos, bills, etc.) if available
7. Submit the complaint
8. A **unique reference/registration number** will be generated — save it for tracking

### Method 2: Via the CM Jansunwai Mobile App

1. Download the **"CM Jansunwai"** app from the [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store) (search for "CM Jansunwai")
2. Register with your mobile number and verify via OTP
3. Follow the same steps as the portal to file your complaint
4. The app also allows you to track complaint status and submit feedback

### Method 3: Via Helpline (Call Centre)

- Call the dedicated helpline number **1902**
- Provide your complaint details to the operator
- A reference number will be shared via SMS

### Method 4: Offline Submission

- Visit the **Chief Minister's Office** in person to submit your complaint
- Physical **Jan Sunwai (public hearing) sessions** continue alongside the digital system — from the CM to district-level officers

## How to Track Your Complaint

1. Visit [https://cmjansunwai.delhi.gov.in](https://cmjansunwai.delhi.gov.in) or open the CM Jansunwai app
2. Click on **"Check Complaint Status"**
3. Enter your **reference/registration number**
4. View the current status and updates
5. You can also **send reminders** if there are delays and **submit feedback** on the resolution

## Three-Tier Grievance Redressal Mechanism

The portal uses a structured escalation system to ensure accountability:

| Level | Authority | Role |
|-------|-----------|------|
| Level 1 | Grievance Redressal Officer (GRO) | First point of resolution |
| Level 2 | Appellate Authority (AA) | Handles appeals if GRO doesn't resolve |
| Level 3 | Final Appellate Authority (FAA) | Final escalation for unresolved cases |

**Key features:**
- Each complaint gets a **unique reference ID**
- **SMS updates** at every stage of resolution
- **Negative feedback auto-escalation** — if you rate the resolution poorly, the case is automatically escalated
- Complaints, jurisdictions, and departmental officers are **pre-mapped** in the system

## e-District Services via Common Service Centres (CSC)

Alongside the Jan Sunwai Portal, the Delhi government has integrated **e-District services** with the **Common Service Centre (CSC) Digital Seva Portal**. This means:

- **75+ government services** are now available at over **7,000 active CSC centres** across Delhi
- Services available include:
  - Income certificate
  - Caste certificate
  - Residence/domicile certificate
  - Birth and death certificates
  - Social welfare scheme applications
  - Labour department services
  - Education-related services
- **Nominal fee of ₹30** for most services
- No need to visit multiple government offices — one CSC centre handles it all

## Important Tips

1. **Save your reference number** — you'll need it to track your complaint and for any follow-up
2. **Upload clear photos** when filing complaints about roads, water leakage, or sanitation — visual evidence speeds up resolution
3. **Provide accurate location details** — the system maps complaints to the correct department based on jurisdiction
4. **Use the feedback option** — negative feedback triggers automatic escalation, which holds officers accountable
5. **Try the mobile app** for convenience — file complaints and track them on the go

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. What is the CM Jan Sunwai Portal?
The CM Jan Sunwai Portal ([cmjansunwai.delhi.gov.in](https://cmjansunwai.delhi.gov.in)) is a unified digital platform launched by the Delhi government on 21 February 2026 for residents to file complaints related to all government departments in Delhi, including MCD, DDA, and Delhi Police.

### Q2. How can I file a complaint on the CM Jan Sunwai Portal?
You can file a complaint through four modes: the online portal at cmjansunwai.delhi.gov.in, the CM Jansunwai mobile app (available on Google Play Store), calling the helpline number 1902, or visiting the Chief Minister's Office in person.

### Q3. Is there any fee to file a complaint?
No. Filing a complaint on the CM Jan Sunwai Portal, mobile app, or helpline is completely free.

### Q4. How do I track my complaint status?
Visit cmjansunwai.delhi.gov.in or open the CM Jansunwai app and use your unique reference/registration number to check the current status of your complaint.

### Q5. What happens if my complaint is not resolved?
The portal has a three-tier escalation mechanism. If the Grievance Redressal Officer (GRO) doesn't resolve your issue, it goes to the Appellate Authority (AA), and then to the Final Appellate Authority (FAA). Negative feedback also triggers automatic escalation.

### Q6. Can I file complaints about MCD, DDA, and Delhi Police on this portal?
Yes. The CM Jan Sunwai Portal is a unified platform covering all Delhi government departments, including MCD, DDA, Delhi Police, Delhi Jal Board, PWD, and others.

### Q7. What are the e-District services available at CSC centres?
Over 75 government services are available at 7,000+ CSC centres across Delhi, including income certificates, caste certificates, residence certificates, birth/death certificates, and social welfare scheme applications — at a nominal fee of approximately ₹30 per service.

### Q8. Is the CM Jansunwai app available for iPhone (iOS)?
As of the launch on 21 February 2026, the CM Jansunwai app is available for **Android** users on the Google Play Store. iOS availability has not been confirmed yet — check the official portal for updates.
