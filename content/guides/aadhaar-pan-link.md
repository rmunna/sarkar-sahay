## Why Link Aadhaar with PAN?

Aadhaar-PAN linking is **mandatory** under Section 139AA of the [income tax](/guide/income-tax-return-file-online) Act. If your PAN is not linked with Aadhaar:
- Your **PAN becomes inoperative** — it's as good as not having a PAN
- You **cannot file income tax returns**
- **Higher TDS** is deducted on your income (at 20% instead of normal rates under Section 206AA)
- You cannot open new bank accounts or demat accounts using PAN
- Mutual fund and stock transactions are affected
- You cannot make high-value transactions (above ₹50,000) that require PAN
- Existing SIPs and investments may face issues

> **Who is exempt?** NRIs, residents of Assam/Meghalaya/J&K (as per old notification), persons above 80 years (super senior citizens), and foreign citizens with PAN are exempt from mandatory linking.

## Step-by-Step Process — Online (Income Tax Portal)

### Step 1: Visit the Income Tax Portal
Go to [incometax.gov.in](https://www.incometax.gov.in)

### Step 2: Go to Link Aadhaar Section
On the homepage, click **"Link Aadhaar"** (available without login)

Or directly visit: `eportal.incometax.gov.in/iec/foservices/#/pre-login/link-aadhaar-status`

### Step 3: Enter Details
- **PAN Number**
- **Aadhaar Number**
- **Name as per Aadhaar**
- **Mobile Number** linked to Aadhaar (for OTP)

### Step 4: Pay Fee (if applicable)
If you missed previous deadlines, a fee of **₹1,000** is required before linking.
- Pay via **e-Pay Tax** on the portal
- Select Challan → **AY → Other Receipts (500)** → **Fee** → ₹1,000
- Wait 4-5 working days for payment to reflect before attempting linking
- You can check payment status under "e-Pay Tax" → "Payment History"

### Step 5: Validate with OTP
Enter the OTP received on your Aadhaar-registered mobile number. The OTP is valid for 10 minutes.

### Step 6: Linking Request Submitted
You'll see a confirmation message with a request number. The linking is processed within **2-7 working days**.

## Link Aadhaar with PAN via SMS

If you can't access the portal, you can link via SMS:

1. Open the SMS app on your phone
2. Type: **UIDPAN <12-digit Aadhaar> <10-digit PAN>**
3. Send to **567678** or **56161**
4. Example: `UIDPAN 123456789012 ABCDE1234F`
5. You'll receive a confirmation SMS

> **Limitations of SMS method:** This works only for basic linking where the name in Aadhaar and PAN matches. If there is any name mismatch, you must use the online portal. Also, the ₹1,000 fee (if applicable) must be paid online before SMS linking will work.

## Link Aadhaar with PAN — Offline Method

If you cannot use the online portal or SMS:

1. Visit any **NSDL TIN Facilitation Center** or **UTIITSL PAN Center**
2. Fill **Form for Aadhaar-PAN Linking** (available at the center)
3. Submit along with copies of [PAN card](/guide/pan-card-apply-online) and Aadhaar card
4. Pay the fee of ₹1,000 (if applicable) at the center
5. The center will process your linking request

> Find NSDL centers at [tin.tin.nsdl.com/pan/index.html](https://tin.tin.nsdl.com/pan/index.html)

## How to Check Aadhaar-PAN Link Status

### Method 1: Online Portal
1. Visit [incometax.gov.in](https://www.incometax.gov.in)
2. Click **"Link Aadhaar Status"**
3. Enter PAN and Aadhaar numbers
4. View status — it will show "already linked" or "linking request submitted" or "not linked"

### Method 2: SMS
Send SMS: **UIDPAN <12-digit Aadhaar> <10-digit PAN>** to **567678** or **56161**. You'll receive a reply with the current linking status.

## Deadline and Penalty Information

| Timeline | Status |

| 1 April 2022 to 30 June 2022 | ₹500 late fee |

| After deadline (PAN not linked) | PAN becomes **inoperative** |

**Current status:** If your PAN is not yet linked with Aadhaar, you must pay ₹1,000 fee and complete the linking. Your PAN will remain inoperative until linking is done.

## What If Names Don't Match?

If your name in Aadhaar and PAN don't match:
1. **Minor differences** (spelling variations like Rajesh vs Rajeshkumar) — The portal usually handles these with fuzzy matching
2. **Major differences** (completely different names) — Update your name in either:
   - **Aadhaar:** Visit myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in or nearest Aadhaar center (₹75 fee)
   - **PAN:** Apply for PAN correction at [onlineservices.nsdl.com](https://onlineservices.nsdl.com) (₹107 fee for Indian address)
3. Then retry linking after the correction is processed

> **Tip:** It's easier to correct the name in whichever document has the error. Make sure both documents have the same name before retrying.

## What If PAN Is Already Inoperative?

If your PAN became inoperative due to non-linking:
1. Pay the ₹1,000 fee via e-Pay Tax on the income tax portal
2. Wait 4-5 working days for the payment to reflect
3. Complete the Aadhaar-PAN linking online
4. PAN will become **operative again within 30 days** of successful linking
5. During this 30-day period, TDS will still be deducted at higher rates

## Common Errors and Solutions

| Error | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| "Aadhaar number is not valid" | Double-check your 12-digit Aadhaar number. Ensure no typos. |
| "PAN not found in database" | Verify your 10-digit PAN. If it's a new PAN, wait a few days for database update. |
| "Aadhaar-PAN already linked" | No action needed — your linking is already done. |
| "Payment not found" | Wait 4-5 working days after paying ₹1,000 fee. Payment takes time to reflect. |
| "Name mismatch" | Update name in Aadhaar or PAN to match, then retry. |
| "OTP not received" | Ensure your Aadhaar mobile number is active. Try again after 60 seconds. Use landline OTP option if available. |
| "Technical error / server busy" | Try during off-peak hours (early morning or late night). The portal gets heavy traffic near deadlines. |

## Important Tips

1. **Don't delay** — An inoperative PAN causes problems across all financial transactions
2. **Pay fee first, wait, then link** — The ₹1,000 fee takes 4-5 working days to reflect in the system
3. **Keep Aadhaar mobile number active** — You'll need OTP for verification
4. **Check status after a few days** — Linking isn't always instant, it can take up to 7 working days
5. **Save the confirmation** — Take a screenshot of the linking confirmation for your records
6. **File ITR on time** — Even after linking, file your pending ITR before the deadline to avoid penalties
7. **Inform your employer** — If PAN was inoperative, inform HR/accounts team once it's reactivated so they adjust TDS

## FAQs

**Q: Is Aadhaar-PAN linking mandatory?**
A: Yes. Under Section 139AA, all PAN holders (except exempt categories) must link with Aadhaar. Non-linked PANs become inoperative.

**Q: What is the fee for linking Aadhaar with PAN?**
A: ₹1,000 (for linking after the deadline). Pay via e-Pay Tax on the income tax portal before attempting to link.

**Q: How long does it take for Aadhaar-PAN linking?**
A: Usually processed within 2-7 working days after submitting the request.

**Q: What happens if PAN becomes inoperative?**
A: You cannot file ITR, higher TDS is deducted (20%), and PAN cannot be used for financial transactions above ₹50,000. Link Aadhaar and pay fee to reactivate within 30 days.

**Q: Can I link Aadhaar-PAN via SMS?**
A: Yes. Send UIDPAN <Aadhaar> <PAN> to 567678 or 56161. Works only when names match and fee is already paid.

**Q: Who is exempt from Aadhaar-PAN linking?**
A: NRIs, super senior citizens (80+ years), residents of certain states (as per notification), and foreign citizens with PAN are exempt.

**Q: Can I link Aadhaar-PAN without login on the portal?**
A: Yes. The Link Aadhaar option is available on the homepage without requiring login.

**Q: What if I have two PANs?**
A: Having two PANs is illegal. Surrender the duplicate PAN using Form 49A on the NSDL website, then link the remaining PAN with Aadhaar.
