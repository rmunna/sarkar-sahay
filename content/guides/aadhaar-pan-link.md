## Why Link Aadhaar with PAN?

Aadhaar-PAN linking is **mandatory** under Section 139AA of the Income Tax Act. If your PAN is not linked with Aadhaar:
- Your **PAN becomes inoperative**
- You **cannot file income tax returns**
- **Higher TDS** is deducted on your income
- You cannot open new bank accounts or demat accounts using PAN
- Mutual fund and stock transactions are affected

## Step-by-Step Process — Online

### Step 1: Visit the Income Tax Portal
Go to [incometax.gov.in](https://www.incometax.gov.in)

### Step 2: Go to Link Aadhaar Section
On the homepage, click **"Link Aadhaar"** (available without login)

Or directly visit: `eportal.incometax.gov.in/iec/foservices/#/pre-login/link-aadhaar-status`

### Step 3: Enter Details
- **PAN Number**
- **Aadhaar Number**
- **Name as per Aadhaar**
- **Mobile Number** linked to Aadhaar (for OTP)

### Step 4: Pay Fee (if applicable)
If you missed previous deadlines, a fee of **₹1,000** is required before linking.
- Pay via **e-Pay Tax** on the portal
- Select Challan → **AY → Other Receipts (500)** → **Fee** → ₹1,000
- Wait 4-5 working days for payment to reflect before attempting linking

### Step 5: Validate with OTP
Enter the OTP received on your Aadhaar-registered mobile number.

### Step 6: Linking Request Submitted
You'll see a confirmation. The linking is processed within **a few days**.

## How to Check Link Status

### Method 1: Online Portal
1. Visit [incometax.gov.in](https://www.incometax.gov.in)
2. Click **"Link Aadhaar Status"**
3. Enter PAN and Aadhaar numbers
4. View status

### Method 2: SMS
Send SMS: **UIDPAN <12-digit Aadhaar> <10-digit PAN>** to **567678** or **56161**

## Via SMS (Alternative Method)

If you can't access the portal:
Send SMS: **UIDPAN <Aadhaar Number> <PAN Number>** to **567678** or **56161**

Example: `UIDPAN 123456789012 ABCDE1234F`

> This method works for basic linking. For cases with name mismatch, use the online portal.

## Fees

| Scenario | Fee |
|----------|-----|
| Linking before original deadline | **Free** |
| Linking after deadline (current) | **₹1,000** |

## What If Names Don't Match?

If your name in Aadhaar and PAN don't match:
1. **Minor differences** (spelling variations) — The portal usually handles these
2. **Major differences** — Update your name in either:
   - **Aadhaar:** Visit uidai.gov.in or nearest Aadhaar center
   - **PAN:** Apply for PAN correction at onlineservices.nsdl.com
3. Then retry linking

## What If PAN Is Already Inoperative?

If your PAN became inoperative due to non-linking:
1. Pay the ₹1,000 fee via e-Pay Tax
2. Complete the Aadhaar-PAN linking
3. PAN will become **operative again within 30 days** of successful linking

## Important Tips

1. **Don't delay** — An inoperative PAN causes problems across all financial transactions
2. **Pay fee first, wait, then link** — The ₹1,000 fee takes 4-5 days to reflect
3. **Keep Aadhaar mobile number active** — You'll need OTP
4. **Check status after a few days** — Linking isn't always instant

## FAQs

**Q: Is Aadhaar-PAN linking mandatory?**
A: Yes. Under Section 139AA, all PAN holders must link with Aadhaar. Non-linked PANs become inoperative.

**Q: What is the fee for linking Aadhaar with PAN?**
A: ₹1,000 (for linking after the deadline). Pay via e-Pay Tax on the income tax portal.

**Q: How long does it take for Aadhaar-PAN linking?**
A: Usually processed within 2-7 working days after submitting the request.

**Q: What happens if PAN becomes inoperative?**
A: You cannot file ITR, higher TDS is deducted, and PAN cannot be used for financial transactions. Link Aadhaar + pay fee to reactivate.

**Q: Can I link Aadhaar-PAN via SMS?**
A: Yes. Send UIDPAN <Aadhaar> <PAN> to 567678 or 56161.
