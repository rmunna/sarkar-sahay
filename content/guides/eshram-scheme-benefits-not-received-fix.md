# e-Shram Benefits Not Received? Check Eligibility & Fix

Registered on e-Shram but **not receiving any benefits**? Many workers are confused about what e-Shram actually provides. This guide clarifies the real benefits, how to check eligibility, track payments, and file claims.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent platform, not affiliated with any government body. Verify information on [eshram.gov.in](https://eshram.gov.in).

## What Benefits Does e-Shram Actually Provide?

Let's clear up the confusion first:

### Confirmed Benefits

| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **PMSBY** (Accident Insurance) | ₹2 lakh (death), ₹1 lakh (partial disability) |
| **PMJJBY** (Life Insurance) | ₹2 lakh on death (for age 18-50) |
| **Scheme eligibility** | Priority access to various government schemes |
| **Identity proof** | Official recognition as unorganized worker |

### Common Misconceptions

- ❌ **₹500/₹1000 monthly payment** — No regular monthly payment exists for all e-Shram holders
- ❌ **₹3000 pension** — e-Shram alone does not provide pension (separate PM-SYM scheme exists)
- ❌ **Free ration** — Requires separate ration card; e-Shram may help with eligibility
- ❌ **Government job** — e-Shram does not guarantee employment

> **Warning:** Many fake websites and WhatsApp messages claim "e-Shram ₹1000" or "check balance." These are scams. Only trust [eshram.gov.in](https://eshram.gov.in).

## Check Your PMJJBY/PMSBY Enrollment

When you register on e-Shram, you may be auto-enrolled in insurance schemes. Here's how to verify:

### Method 1: Check Bank Statement
1. Get your bank passbook updated or check online statement
2. Look for deductions:
   - **PMSBY:** ₹20/year (deducted around June 1)
   - **PMJJBY:** ₹436/year (deducted around June 1)
3. If deducted, you're enrolled and covered

### Method 2: Contact Bank
1. Call your bank's customer care
2. Ask: "Am I enrolled in PMJJBY and PMSBY schemes?"
3. Provide your account number and Aadhaar

### Method 3: Visit Bank Branch
1. Go to the branch where your account is held
2. Ask for PMJJBY/PMSBY enrollment confirmation
3. Get a certificate if needed for claims

## Why Benefits May Not Be Coming

### Reason 1: Bank Account Issues
- **Insufficient balance** — Need minimum balance for premium deduction
- **Inactive account** — Account dormant if unused for 2 years
- **Wrong bank linked** — e-Shram may have a different bank than your active one

**Fix:** Update bank details on e-Shram portal (see our [update guide](/guide/eshram-card-update-mobile-name-fix))

### Reason 2: Aadhaar-Bank Not Linked
- DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer) requires Aadhaar-bank linking
- Without this, payments can't reach your account

**Fix:**
1. Visit your bank branch
2. Request Aadhaar-bank account linking
3. Carry Aadhaar card and bank passbook
4. This is free and takes 1-2 days

### Reason 3: Not Eligible for Specific Scheme
Each scheme has its own criteria:

| Scheme | Eligibility |
|--------|------------|
| **PMSBY** | Age 18-70, savings bank account |
| **PMJJBY** | Age 18-50, savings bank account |
| **PM-SYM** (Pension) | Age 18-40, monthly income below ₹15,000, separate enrollment needed |

### Reason 4: Auto-Enrollment Didn't Happen
- Not all states auto-enroll e-Shram workers in PMJJBY/PMSBY
- You may need to **separately consent** through your bank

**Fix:** Visit bank and explicitly request PMJJBY/PMSBY enrollment. Carry your e-Shram card.

## How to File Accident Insurance Claim (PMSBY)

If an e-Shram registered worker suffers an accident:

### For Accidental Death Claim (₹2 Lakh)
1. Nominee visits the bank branch where the worker had an account
2. Submit:
   - PMSBY claim form (available at bank)
   - Death certificate
   - FIR copy (if applicable)
   - Nominee's Aadhaar and bank account details
   - Worker's e-Shram card copy
3. Bank processes claim through insurance company
4. Amount credited to nominee's account in 30-60 days

### For Partial Disability Claim (₹1 Lakh)
1. Worker visits their bank branch
2. Submit:
   - PMSBY claim form
   - Disability certificate from **government hospital**
   - FIR copy (if accident-related)
   - Medical reports
   - e-Shram card copy
3. Claim processed in 30-60 days

> **Important:** Claim must be filed within **30 days** of the incident (extendable in special cases).

## Track Payment/Benefit Status

### For DBT Payments
1. Check your bank account statement regularly
2. Visit [pfms.nic.in](https://pfms.nic.in) → "Know Your Payments" → enter bank account details
3. This shows all government payments received

### For Insurance Status
1. Contact your bank for enrollment confirmation
2. Visit [jansuraksha.gov.in](https://jansuraksha.gov.in) for PMJJBY/PMSBY details

## State-Specific e-Shram Benefits

Some states provide additional benefits:

- **Uttar Pradesh:** One-time ₹1,000 assistance (during COVID, now discontinued)
- **Bihar:** Priority in state schemes
- **Jharkhand:** Additional insurance coverage
- Various states: Priority in housing, education, and welfare schemes

> Check your state labour department website for state-specific benefits.

## Important Tips

- **Don't fall for scams** — no website other than eshram.gov.in can give you money
- **Keep bank account active** — transact at least once every 6 months
- **Link Aadhaar to bank** — mandatory for DBT
- **Renew insurance** — PMJJBY/PMSBY are annual; ensure premium is deducted each year
- **File claims promptly** — accident claims have time limits

## Helpline & Support

- **e-Shram helpline:** 14434 (toll-free)
- **PMJJBY/PMSBY helpline:** Contact your bank
- **Jan Suraksha portal:** [jansuraksha.gov.in](https://jansuraksha.gov.in)
- **Email:** helpdesk-eshram@gov.in
