## What is Mukhyamantri Mahila Samriddhi Yojana?

The **Mukhyamantri Mahila Samriddhi Yojana** is a welfare scheme by the **Government of Delhi** that provides **₹2,500 per month** to eligible women residents of Delhi. The scheme aims to empower women financially and support their household needs.

This scheme is part of Delhi government's broader initiative to provide direct financial assistance to women, ensuring economic independence and social security.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Gender:** Must be a woman
2. **Age:** Must be **18 years or older**
3. **Residency:** Must be a permanent resident of Delhi (voter ID registered in Delhi)
4. **Income:** Family income below the prescribed limit
5. **Exclusions:**
   - Government employees (state/central)
   - Women already receiving pension from any government scheme
   - Income tax payees
6. **Aadhaar:** Must have Aadhaar Card linked to bank account

## Monthly Benefit

| Benefit | Amount |
|---------|--------|
| Monthly payment | ₹2,500 |
| Annual total | ₹30,000 |
| Mode | Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) |

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar Card** (linked to bank account and mobile)
- **Voter ID Card** (registered in Delhi) — proves Delhi residency
- **Bank Passbook** (account in own name, Aadhaar-linked)
- **Passport-size photographs**
- **Self-declaration** (not a government employee, not an income tax payee)
- **Ration Card** (if available)

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Online Application

1. **Visit** [edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in](https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in) or the dedicated scheme portal
2. **Register** or login with your credentials
3. **Search** for "Mahila Samriddhi Yojana"
4. **Fill** the application form with personal details, Aadhaar, and bank account
5. **Upload** required documents
6. **Submit** and note the application reference number
7. **Track** status online

### Offline Application

1. **Visit** the nearest **District Office** or **e-District Centre**
2. **Collect** the application form
3. **Fill** and attach all documents
4. **Submit** to the concerned official
5. **Get** acknowledgement receipt

## Processing Time

- **Verification:** 30-45 days
- **Approval:** After successful verification
- **First Payment:** Within 1-2 months of approval
- **Ongoing:** Monthly via DBT

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. How much do women get under this scheme?
**₹2,500 per month** directly to the bank account via DBT.

### Q2. Is there an age limit?
Women must be **18 years or older**. No upper age limit (unless receiving old age pension).

### Q3. Can government employees apply?
No, government employees and income tax payees are excluded.

### Q4. Do I need to be a Delhi voter?
Yes, you must be a registered voter in Delhi or have proof of Delhi residency.

### Q5. Can I get this along with Delhi Old Age Pension?
Generally no — you can avail only one government financial assistance scheme at a time.

### Q6. How is the money paid?
Via **Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)** to your Aadhaar-linked bank account every month.

### Q7. Where can I check application status?
At [edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in](https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in) using your application reference number.
