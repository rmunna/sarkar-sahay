# EPF Withdrawal Tax Rules India 2026 — Complete Guide

When you resign or change jobs, you have a choice: withdraw your EPF balance or transfer it to the new employer's account. The tax treatment depends heavily on how long you've been in service.

## The 5-Year Rule

**5+ years of continuous service**: EPF withdrawal is **completely tax-free** under Section 10(12). No TDS, no tax — even the interest earned is exempt.

**Below 5 years of service**: EPF withdrawal is **taxable**. TDS is deducted, and the amount is added to your income.

**"Continuous service"** means service counted across multiple employers — as long as you transferred the EPF balance (not withdrawn) when changing jobs. The 5-year clock runs from your first EPF contribution, not from joining each new company.

## What Gets Taxed When You Withdraw Before 5 Years

| Component | Tax Treatment (Pre-5 Year Withdrawal) |
|-----------|--------------------------------------|
| **Employee contribution** | NOT taxable (you already paid tax on this salary) |
| **Employee contribution interest** | Taxable as "Income from Other Sources" |
| **Employer contribution** | Taxable as "Salary" income |
| **Employer contribution interest** | Taxable as "Income from Other Sources" |

## TDS on Premature EPF Withdrawal

| Condition | TDS Rate |
|-----------|---------|
| PAN submitted, service < 5 years, withdrawal ≥ ₹50,000 | **10%** |
| PAN NOT submitted, service < 5 years, withdrawal ≥ ₹50,000 | **30% + surcharge** |
| Service ≥ 5 years (or < ₹50,000 withdrawal, or medical/other specified reasons) | **No TDS** |

### Special Exceptions — No TDS Even Below 5 Years

TDS is NOT deducted if withdrawal is due to:
- Termination of service due to ill-health / medical reasons
- Non-continuation of business (employer closed)
- Circumstances beyond employee's control

These require Form 15G/15H submission or documentary evidence to EPFO to avoid TDS.

## Tax Impact: Example

**Situation**: Ravi resigns after 3 years 2 months. EPF balance at withdrawal:

| Component | Amount |
|-----------|--------|
| Employee contribution | ₹1,80,000 |
| Employee contribution interest | ₹28,000 |
| Employer contribution | ₹1,80,000 |
| Employer contribution interest | ₹28,000 |
| **Total EPF balance** | **₹4,16,000** |

**Tax at withdrawal**:
- Employee contribution (₹1,80,000): Not taxable
- Employee interest (₹28,000): Taxable as income from other sources
- Employer contribution (₹1,80,000): Taxable as salary
- Employer interest (₹28,000): Taxable as income from other sources
- **Taxable amount**: ₹2,36,000

**TDS deducted by EPFO**: ₹23,600 (10% if PAN submitted)

**In ITR**: Add ₹2,36,000 to your income. If your total income (salary for remaining months + ₹2,36,000) crosses a bracket, pay additional tax.

## What to Do When Changing Jobs (The Smart Move)

**Transfer EPF, don't withdraw**. When you join a new employer:
1. Provide your UAN (Universal Account Number) to the new employer
2. Request EPF transfer from previous employer to current employer using Form 13 (online through EPFO portal)
3. Your service continuity is maintained
4. The clock toward 5 years keeps running

**Benefits of transfer vs withdrawal**:
- Service continuity (toward 5-year tax-free threshold)
- EPF balance continues earning 8.15% interest
- Avoid TDS hassle
- No immediate tax impact

## EPF at Maturity / Retirement (58 Years or 10+ Years Service)

EPF maturity at retirement: **Completely tax-free** if:
- You've completed 5+ years of service, OR
- You've retired at/after age 58

No TDS, no tax liability. The full EPF corpus (including employer contribution + all interest) is tax-free.

## EPF Interest Above ₹2.5L Per Year — Budget 2021 Change

Budget 2021 introduced a tax on EPF interest for high-income earners:

If your **employee EPF contributions** in a financial year exceed ₹2.5L:
- Interest on the excess amount (above ₹2.5L) is taxable at your slab rate
- For government employees: Threshold is ₹5L (higher limit)

This affects people with basic salary above ~₹1.72L/month (₹2.5L annual EPF contribution requires 12% × basic ≥ ₹2.5L → basic ≥ ₹20,833/month).

Voluntary Provident Fund (VPF) contributions also count toward the ₹2.5L limit.

## EPF Withdrawal — Process and Tax Certificate

When you withdraw EPF (Form 19 + Form 10C for EPS):
1. Withdraw online via EPFO member portal using UAN
2. EPFO processes within 20 working days
3. Amount credited to your bank account
4. Form 16A (TDS certificate) issued by EPFO if TDS was deducted

When filing ITR:
- Include TDS from EPFO in "Tax Payments" section (TDS from others)
- Declare taxable EPF components in respective income heads
- The taxable employer contribution and interest goes to the appropriate schedule

## Form 15G/15H for EPF Withdrawal

If your total income (including EPF withdrawal) for the year is below the taxable limit, you can submit:
- **Form 15G** (below 60 years): Self-declaration that tax is nil → EPFO won't deduct TDS
- **Form 15H** (60+ years): Same, for senior citizens

Submit Form 15G/15H to EPFO at the time of withdrawal application. If submitted but your income eventually exceeds the limit, you must pay tax in ITR.

## Special Cases

### Partial EPF Withdrawal (Not Full)
- For specific purposes (medical, house purchase, education, marriage): Partial withdrawal allowed
- Tax treatment: Same 5-year rule applies for any withdrawal before 5 years
- Medical reason partials: TDS waived even before 5 years

### EPF After Death
- EPF paid to legal heirs/nominees upon member's death: **Tax-free** regardless of service length (Section 10(12) and 12A)

### EPS (Pension) Received Monthly
- Monthly pension from EPS after retirement: Taxable as "Pension" income under salary head
- Standard deduction of ₹50,000 (for AY 2026-27, new regime: ₹75K) applies against pension

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I changed jobs 4 times in 7 years but transferred EPF each time. Is my withdrawal tax-free now?

Yes — service continuity is maintained through EPF transfers. If total service (across all employers) is 7 years and you transferred EPF each time without withdrawing, your withdrawal is tax-free under Section 10(12). The 5-year clock runs continuously from your first contribution.

### I withdrew EPF after 2 years (resigned during COVID). Now the company says EPFO deducted 30% TDS because I didn't submit PAN. What can I do?

You can claim the TDS as credit in your ITR. If your actual tax liability on the withdrawal is less than 30%, the difference is refunded. The 30% TDS is only because PAN was missing — at ITR time, you pay your actual slab rate, not 30% flat.

### My EPF balance is ₹15L. I'm changing jobs. Should I withdraw or transfer?

**Transfer**. If you withdraw: (a) Below 5 years service: Potentially ₹7–8L taxable at your slab; (b) No compounding going forward. If you transfer: Service clock continues, EPF earns 8.15%, and future withdrawal (after 5+ years total) is fully tax-free. The math heavily favors transfer.
