# Domicile Certificate Apply Online 2026

A **domicile certificate** (also called **residence certificate**, **nativity certificate**, or **mool niwas praman patra**) is an official document proving that you are a permanent resident of a particular state or union territory. It is required for NEET 85% state quota medical admissions, state government jobs, state college admissions, and state-specific schemes.

> **NEET state quota:** The 85% state quota in NEET requires a valid domicile/nativity certificate from that state. Without it, you compete only in the 15% all-India quota.

---

## Domicile vs Nativity vs Residence Certificate

| Term | States That Use It | Meaning |
|------|-------------------|---------|
| **Domicile Certificate** | UP, Bihar, Delhi, MP, WB, Rajasthan | Permanent residence in the state |
| **Nativity Certificate** | Tamil Nadu, Kerala, AP, Telangana | Born in / native of the state |
| **Residence Certificate** | Gujarat, Haryana, Punjab, Odisha | Current/long-term residence |
| **Mool Niwas Praman Patra** | MP, Chhattisgarh, Rajasthan | Permanent/original resident |

## Who Needs a Domicile Certificate?

You need a domicile certificate if you are:

- Applying for **state quota seats** in colleges and universities
- Applying for **state government jobs** that require domicile proof
- Availing **state-specific scholarships** or fee concessions
- Applying for certain **government schemes** reserved for state residents
- Seeking **reservation benefits** under state quota

## Eligibility Criteria

To obtain a domicile certificate, you generally must:

1. Be a **permanent resident** of the state for a continuous period (typically 3-15 years depending on the state)
2. Own property or have a permanent address in the state
3. Have studied in an educational institution within the state (for student applicants)
4. Be born in the state OR have parents who are domiciled in the state

> **Note:** Eligibility criteria vary from state to state. Check your state's e-district portal for specific requirements.

## Documents Required

### Mandatory Documents

- **Identity proof** — Aadhaar card, voter ID, or passport
- **Address proof** — Ration card, electricity bill, or property documents
- **Birth certificate** or school leaving certificate showing place of birth
- **Passport-size photographs** (2-4 copies)
- **Aadhaar card** of the applicant

### Additional Documents (May Be Required)

- **10th/12th marksheet** — as proof of studying in the state
- **Property documents** — if claiming domicile through property ownership
- **Parents' domicile certificate** — for minor applicants
- **Affidavit** — self-declaration of permanent residence
- **Ration card** — showing family details and address in the state

## Step-by-Step Online Process

### Step 1: Visit Your State's e-District Portal

Go to your state's official e-district website. Some major portals:

| State | Portal |
|-------|--------|
| Uttar Pradesh | edistrict.up.gov.in |
| Delhi | edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in |
| Bihar | serviceonline.bihar.gov.in |
| Maharashtra | aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in |
| Madhya Pradesh | mpedistrict.gov.in |
| Rajasthan | emitra.rajasthan.gov.in |

### Step 2: Register / Login

- Create an account using your mobile number and email ID
- Verify via OTP
- Log in to the portal

### Step 3: Select "Domicile Certificate" Service

- Navigate to **Certificate Services** or **Revenue Department**
- Select **Domicile Certificate / Niwas Praman Patra**

### Step 4: Fill the Application Form

- Enter personal details — name, father's name, date of birth, address
- Enter duration of residence in the state
- Select purpose of the certificate

### Step 5: Upload Documents

- Upload scanned copies of required documents
- Ensure documents are in the specified format (usually PDF/JPEG, under 200KB-1MB)

### Step 6: Pay the Fee

- Pay the application fee online via net banking, debit card, or UPI
- Fee ranges from **₹10 to ₹50** depending on the state

### Step 7: Submit and Note Application Number

- Submit the application and save the acknowledgement receipt
- Note down the **application/reference number** for tracking

## Offline Process

1. Visit your **Tehsildar office / SDM office / District Magistrate office**
2. Collect the domicile certificate application form (or download from the e-district portal)
3. Fill the form and attach self-attested copies of required documents
4. Submit the form along with the **prescribed fee**
5. A **field verification** may be conducted by the revenue department
6. Collect the certificate after processing (7-30 days)

## Processing Time

- **Online applications:** 7 to 15 working days
- **Offline applications:** 15 to 30 working days
- **Urgent/Tatkal:** Some states offer faster processing for an additional fee

## Validity

- In most states, the domicile certificate has **lifetime validity** once issued
- Some states may issue it with a **specific validity period** (3-5 years)
- For admission purposes, institutions may ask for a **recently issued** certificate

## How to Track Your Application

2. Click on **Track Application Status**
3. Enter your application/reference number

## Important Tips

1. **Apply early** — Start the process at least 1-2 months before you need the certificate
2. **Keep originals ready** — You may need to show original documents during verification
3. **Check state-specific rules** — Each state has different residency requirements and document lists
4. **Use CSC centres** — If you cannot apply online, visit a Common Service Centre (CSC) near you
5. **Download digital copy** — Once approved, download the digitally signed certificate from the portal

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: What is the difference between a domicile certificate and a residence certificate?

In most states, domicile certificate and residence certificate are the same document. Some states use different names — domicile certificate, niwas praman patra, or mool niwas praman patra — but they serve the same purpose of proving permanent residency.

### Q2: How long does it take to get a domicile certificate?

Online applications are typically processed within **7-15 working days**. Offline applications may take **15-30 working days**. Processing time varies by state and workload.

### Q3: Can I apply for a domicile certificate of a state where I was not born?

Yes, if you have been a **continuous resident** of that state for the required period (usually 3-15 years depending on the state) and can provide proof of residence, you can apply for a domicile certificate.

### Q4: Is domicile certificate mandatory for college admission?

For **state quota seats**, most colleges and universities require a domicile certificate. For **all-India quota seats**, it is generally not required. Check the specific institution's admission requirements.

### Q5: Can I get a domicile certificate from the e-district portal of any state?

No, you must apply through the e-district portal of the **state where you are a permanent resident**. Each state has its own portal and process.

### Q6: What if my domicile certificate application is rejected?

If rejected, check the reason for rejection on the portal. Common reasons include incomplete documents or incorrect information. You can **reapply** after correcting the issues or **file an appeal** with the concerned authority.

### Q7: Is Aadhaar card mandatory for domicile certificate?

In most states, Aadhaar card is required as identity and address proof. However, you can also use other documents like voter ID, passport, or ration card as alternatives.
