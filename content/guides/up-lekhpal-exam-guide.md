# UP Lekhpal Exam Guide — Eligibility, Syllabus, Pattern & How to Apply

The Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) conducts the recruitment exam for the post of Lekhpal (Revenue Inspector/Accountant) under the Board of Revenue, UP. The UP Lekhpal is a coveted government job responsible for maintaining land records at the village level.

This role offers job security, a government salary, and the opportunity to serve at the grassroots level of revenue administration.

## Eligibility Criteria

### Age Limit

| Category | Minimum Age | Maximum Age |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| General | 18 years | 40 years |
| OBC (UP) | 18 years | 43 years |
| SC/ST (UP) | 18 years | 45 years |

*Age relaxation applicable as per UP Government rules.*

### Educational Qualification

- **Minimum:** Intermediate (12th pass) from a recognized board
- Must have passed UPSSSC PET (Preliminary Eligibility Test)
- Basic computer knowledge (CCC certificate or equivalent)

### Nationality & Domicile

- Must be a citizen of India
- Domicile of Uttar Pradesh

## Exam Pattern

### UPSSSC PET (Preliminary Eligibility Test) — Qualifying

Candidates must first clear the UPSSSC PET before appearing for the Lekhpal main exam.

### Lekhpal Main Exam

| Subject | Marks |
|---------|-------|
| General Hindi | 25 |
| Mathematics | 25 |
| General Knowledge | 25 |
| Village Society & Development | 25 |

- **Total:** 100 marks
- **Duration:** 1.5 hours (90 minutes)
- **Type:** Objective (MCQ)
- **Negative marking:** 0.25 marks per wrong answer

## Syllabus Overview

### General Hindi
- Grammar, Comprehension, Vilom Shabd, Paryayvachi, Muhavare, Lokoktiyan, Fill in the blanks

### Mathematics
- Arithmetic: Number system, Percentage, Profit & Loss, SI/CI, Ratio, Average
- Statistics: Basic data interpretation, graphs, tables

### General Knowledge
- Indian History, Geography, Polity, Current Affairs, Economy, Science

### Village Society & Development
- Rural development schemes, Panchayati Raj, Land records & revenue system, Social welfare programmes, Agriculture basics

## How to Apply

1. Visit the UPSSSC official website at [upsssc.gov.in](https://upsssc.gov.in)
2. Ensure you have cleared the UPSSSC PET
3. Click on the Lekhpal recruitment notification
4. Login with your PET registration credentials
5. Fill in educational and personal details
6. Upload required documents (photo, signature, certificates)
7. Pay the application fee and submit

## Application Fee

| Category | Fee |
|----------|-----|
| General / OBC | ₹25 (exam fee via PET) |
| SC / ST | ₹25 |

*Actual fee may vary per notification cycle. PET fee is separate.*

## Salary & Benefits

- **Pay Scale:** ₹21,700 – ₹69,100 (Level 3)
- DA, HRA, and other allowances as per UP Government rules
- Pension (NPS), medical benefits
- Opportunities for promotion to Naib Tehsildar

## Preparation Tips

1. **Focus on Village Society:** This is unique to Lekhpal — study Panchayati Raj, land revenue systems, and rural development schemes
2. **Basic math:** Keep arithmetic strong — practice speed calculations
3. **Hindi grammar:** Spend time on Muhavare, Lokoktiyan, and comprehension
4. **Current affairs:** Focus on UP-specific schemes and national policies
5. **Previous papers:** Solve past year papers to understand question patterns

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is PET mandatory for UP Lekhpal?
Yes, candidates must clear the UPSSSC PET before applying for the Lekhpal exam.

### Is there any interview for UP Lekhpal?
No, selection is based purely on the written exam merit. There is no interview.

### What is the role of a Lekhpal?
A Lekhpal maintains village-level land records (Khasra, Khatauni), assists in revenue collection, and helps in crop inspection and disaster surveys.

### Can female candidates apply for UP Lekhpal?
Yes, female candidates are eligible and encouraged to apply.

### Is computer knowledge mandatory?
Yes, a CCC (Course on Computer Concepts) certificate or equivalent is typically required.

### How often is UP Lekhpal recruitment conducted?
Recruitment depends on vacancies — it has been conducted irregularly, roughly every 3-5 years.
