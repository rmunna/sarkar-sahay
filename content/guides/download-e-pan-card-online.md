# How to Download e-PAN Card Online (Instant e-PAN via Aadhaar)

Lost your PAN card? Need a digital copy urgently? You can **download your e-PAN card online** in just a few minutes — completely free of cost. This guide covers all the methods to get your e-PAN, check PAN status, and order a physical reprint.

## What is an e-PAN Card?

An **e-PAN** is a digitally signed PAN card issued by the Income Tax Department in **PDF format**. Key points:

- It is a **legally valid** document, equivalent to a physical PAN card
- Contains a **QR code** with your name, date of birth, and PAN details
- Can be used for **KYC verification**, bank account opening, ITR filing, and more
- Available as a downloadable PDF from official government portals
- Your **10-digit PAN number** remains the same on both physical and e-PAN

---

## Method 1: Instant e-PAN from Income Tax Portal (Free, Aadhaar-Based)

This is the **fastest and completely free** method. The Income Tax Department provides instant e-PAN if your PAN is linked with Aadhaar.

**Requirements:**
- Valid Aadhaar number linked to your PAN
- Mobile number registered with Aadhaar (for OTP)
- PAN must already be allotted to you

**Steps to Download:**

1. Go to the Income Tax e-Filing portal: [https://eportal.incometax.gov.in](https://eportal.incometax.gov.in)
2. On the homepage, click on **"Instant e-PAN"** under Quick Links
3. You will see two options — select **"Check Status / Download e-PAN"**
4. Enter your **12-digit Aadhaar number** and click **Continue**
5. An **OTP** will be sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile number
6. Enter the OTP and click **Continue**
7. Your e-PAN will be displayed — click **"Download e-PAN"**
8. A **PDF file** will be downloaded to your device

**Note:** If you don't have a PAN yet, you can also use the **"Get New e-PAN"** option on the same page to apply for a new PAN using Aadhaar — also free of cost.

> **Tip:** The downloaded e-PAN PDF is password-protected. The password is your **date of birth in DDMMYYYY format** (e.g., 15081990).

## Method 2: Download e-PAN from Protean (formerly NSDL)

If you applied for PAN through NSDL/Protean, you can download your e-PAN from their portal.

**Requirements:**
- Your PAN acknowledgement number or PAN number
- Date of birth

**Steps to Download:**

1. Visit the Protean PAN portal: [https://tinpan.proteantech.in](https://tinpan.proteantech.in)
2. Look for the **"Reprint of e-PAN"** or **"Download e-PAN"** option
3. Select whether you want to search by **PAN number** or **Acknowledgement number**
4. Enter your **PAN number** and **date of birth**
5. An **OTP** will be sent to your registered email or mobile
6. Enter the OTP and submit
7. Your e-PAN PDF will be available for download

**Charges:** Protean may charge **₹8.26 (inclusive of GST)** for e-PAN reprint/download. Payment can be made online.

---

## Method 3: Download e-PAN from UTIITSL

If your PAN was issued through UTIITSL, you can download it from their portal.

**Requirements:**
- PAN number
- Date of birth
- Registered email ID

**Steps to Download:**

1. Visit the UTIITSL PAN portal: [https://www.pan.utiitsl.com](https://www.pan.utiitsl.com)
2. Navigate to **"PAN Card Services"** → **"Reprint of e-PAN Card"**
3. Enter your **PAN number** and **date of birth**
4. Verify via **OTP** sent to your registered email/mobile
5. Pay the applicable fee (if any) through online payment
6. Download the **e-PAN PDF** from the confirmation page

**Charges:** UTIITSL may charge a nominal fee of around **₹8.26 (inclusive of GST)** for the e-PAN download.

## How to Check PAN Card Status

If you have applied for a new PAN or correction and want to track the status:

### Through Income Tax Portal:
1. Visit [https://eportal.incometax.gov.in](https://eportal.incometax.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Instant e-PAN"** → **"Check Status / Download e-PAN"**
3. Enter your Aadhaar number and verify with OTP
4. The current status of your PAN application will be displayed

### Through Protean (NSDL):
1. Visit [https://tinpan.proteantech.in](https://tinpan.proteantech.in)
2. Click on **"Track PAN/TAN Application Status"**
3. Enter your **15-digit acknowledgement number**
4. Enter the captcha and click **Submit**
5. Your application status will be shown

### Through UTIITSL:
1. Visit [https://www.pan.utiitsl.com](https://www.pan.utiitsl.com)
2. Go to **"Track PAN Application"**
3. Enter your **application coupon number**
4. View the current status of your application

## How to Know Your PAN Number

Forgot your PAN number? Here's how to find it:

### Method A: Know Your PAN on Income Tax Portal
1. Visit [https://eportal.incometax.gov.in/iec/foservices/#/pre-login/knowYourPan](https://eportal.incometax.gov.in/iec/foservices/#/pre-login/knowYourPan)
2. Enter your **name, date of birth, and mobile number** as per PAN records
3. Verify with **OTP**
4. Your PAN number will be displayed on the screen

### Method B: Check on Protean Portal
1. Visit the Protean website
2. Use the **"Know Your PAN"** service
3. Enter basic details (name, date of birth, etc.)
4. Your PAN details will be shown after verification

### Method C: Check Your Form 26AS
1. Log in to the Income Tax e-Filing portal
2. Go to **e-File** → **Income Tax Returns** → **View Form 26AS**
3. Your PAN number is displayed at the top of the form

## How to Reprint / Order a Physical PAN Card

If you need a new physical PAN card (plastic card delivered by post):

### Through Protean (NSDL):
1. Visit [https://tinpan.proteantech.in](https://tinpan.proteantech.in)
2. Select **"Reprint of PAN Card"**
3. Enter your PAN number and other details
4. Pay the fee:
   - **₹50 + GST** for delivery within India
   - **₹959 + GST** for delivery outside India
5. The physical PAN card will be dispatched to your registered address

### Through UTIITSL:
1. Visit [https://www.pan.utiitsl.com](https://www.pan.utiitsl.com)
2. Select **"Reprint of PAN Card"**
3. Fill in your PAN number and verify details
4. Pay the applicable fee online
5. PAN card will be delivered to your address within 15-20 working days

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Is the e-PAN card legally valid?
**Yes.** The e-PAN issued by the Income Tax Department is legally valid and equivalent to a physical PAN card. It is digitally signed and contains a QR code for verification.

### Q2: Is downloading e-PAN from the Income Tax portal free?
**Yes.** Downloading e-PAN from the Income Tax e-Filing portal ([eportal.incometax.gov.in](https://eportal.incometax.gov.in)) using Aadhaar is **completely free of cost**. No charges apply.

### Q3: What is the password for the e-PAN PDF?
The e-PAN PDF is password-protected. The password is your **date of birth in DDMMYYYY format**. For example, if your date of birth is 15 August 1990, the password would be **15081990**.

### Q4: Can I get instant e-PAN without Aadhaar?
The **instant e-PAN** service on the Income Tax portal requires Aadhaar. However, you can download your e-PAN from the **Protean (NSDL)** or **UTIITSL** portals using your PAN number and date of birth.

### Q5: How long does it take to get an instant e-PAN?
The instant e-PAN through the Income Tax portal is generated **within 10 minutes** after Aadhaar OTP verification. In most cases, it is available instantly.

### Q6: Can I use e-PAN for opening a bank account?
**Yes.** e-PAN is accepted as valid KYC proof by banks, mutual fund companies, demat account providers, and other financial institutions.

### Q7: My PAN is not linked with Aadhaar. Can I still download e-PAN?
If your PAN is not linked with Aadhaar, the instant e-PAN service on the Income Tax portal may not work. You can try downloading from **Protean** or **UTIITSL** portals instead. It is also advisable to link your PAN with Aadhaar as it is now mandatory.

### Q8: How many times can I download my e-PAN?
There is **no limit** on the number of times you can download your e-PAN from the Income Tax portal. You can download it as many times as needed.
