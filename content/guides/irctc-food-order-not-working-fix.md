# IRCTC e-Catering Food Order Not Working? Fix It Now

IRCTC's e-Catering service lets you order food from restaurants and have it delivered to your seat during your train journey. However, many passengers face issues — orders getting auto-cancelled, stations not available, or refunds not processed. This guide covers all common e-Catering problems and how to fix them.

## How IRCTC e-Catering Works

1. Visit [ecatering.irctc.co.in](https://www.ecatering.irctc.co.in/) or use the IRCTC Rail Connect app
2. Enter your **PNR number** or **train number**
3. Select the **station** where you want food delivered
4. Choose a **restaurant** and place your order
5. Food is delivered to your **seat/berth** when the train arrives at that station

## Common e-Catering Error Messages

- **"No food delivery available at this station"** — station not in e-Catering network
- **"Order auto-cancelled"** — order cancelled without your request
- **"Restaurant not available"** — no restaurants listed for your station
- **"Train not eligible for e-Catering"** — certain trains are excluded
- **"PNR not valid"** — PNR number not recognized
- **"Order failed — please try again"** — server or payment error
- Payment deducted but no order confirmation
- Refund not received after cancellation

## Fix 1: Station Not Available for Food Delivery

Not all stations support e-Catering delivery:

1. e-Catering is available at **select major stations** with a minimum halt time (usually 2+ minutes)
2. Check available stations: Enter your PNR on [ecatering.irctc.co.in](https://www.ecatering.irctc.co.in/) — it will show only eligible stations
3. If your preferred station isn't listed:
   - Check if the **next station** on the route is available
   - The train must have a **scheduled halt of 2+ minutes** at the station
   - Some stations are added/removed seasonally based on vendor availability
4. **Alternative:** Use Zomato or Swiggy station delivery at major junctions, or pre-order from railway pantry car

## Fix 2: Order Auto-Cancelled

Your e-Catering order may be auto-cancelled due to:

1. **Train running late** — if the delay exceeds a threshold, the order is auto-cancelled
2. **Restaurant closed or unable to fulfill** — the vendor cancelled from their end
3. **Train rescheduled or cancelled** — route/timing changes trigger auto-cancellation
4. **PNR cancelled or waitlisted** — if your ticket is cancelled or not confirmed, the food order is cancelled too

**What to do:**
- Check your **email/SMS** for the cancellation reason
- If cancelled due to delay: The refund is auto-processed
- If the train is running and you still want food: Place a **new order** for the next available station
- Contact e-Catering support: **1323** (toll-free) or email **ecatering@irctc.co.in**

## Fix 3: Refund Not Received After Cancellation

If your e-Catering order was cancelled but the refund hasn't come:

1. **Auto-refund timeline:** 5-7 working days to your original payment method
2. Check your **bank account/UPI app** — refunds may appear as a separate credit
3. If not received after 7 working days:
   - Call **1323** (e-Catering helpline)
   - Email **ecatering@irctc.co.in** with: Order ID, PNR, payment details, date
   - Use the **feedback/complaint** option on ecatering.irctc.co.in
4. Keep your **order confirmation email/SMS** as reference for the complaint

## Fix 4: PNR Not Valid or Not Recognized

1. Make sure you're entering the **10-digit PNR number** correctly
2. e-Catering only works with **confirmed or RAC tickets** — waitlisted PNRs may not be accepted
3. The PNR must be for a **future journey** — past journey PNRs won't work
4. If you booked through a third-party app (Paytm, MakeMyTrip), the PNR is still valid on e-Catering
5. **Counter tickets** (booked at railway station) are also eligible — enter the PNR printed on your ticket

## Fix 5: Train Not Eligible for e-Catering

Some trains don't support e-Catering:

1. **Local/suburban trains** — e-Catering is only for long-distance trains
2. **Trains without scheduled halts** at e-Catering stations
3. **Special/holiday special trains** — may not be in the e-Catering system yet
4. Check eligibility by entering your train number on [ecatering.irctc.co.in](https://www.ecatering.irctc.co.in/)

## Fix 6: Payment Failed or Order Not Confirmed

1. **Check your email** — sometimes the order is confirmed even if the page showed an error
2. If payment was deducted but no order:
   - Wait 30 minutes for auto-resolution
   - Check **My Orders** on ecatering.irctc.co.in
   - If no order exists, the refund will be processed automatically in 5-7 working days
3. For future orders, use **UPI** for the most reliable payments
4. Avoid ordering during server-heavy periods

## Fix 7: Food Quality or Delivery Issues

If food was delivered but there are quality/quantity issues:

1. **Take photos** of the food as evidence
2. **Call 1323** immediately while on the train
3. Register a complaint on [ecatering.irctc.co.in](https://www.ecatering.irctc.co.in/) under **My Orders → Feedback**
4. You can also tweet to **@IRCTCofficial** with your order details for faster resolution
5. IRCTC may issue a **partial or full refund** based on the complaint

## Important Tips

- Order at least **2 hours before** the station arrival time for guaranteed delivery
- Keep your **phone charged and reachable** — the delivery person will call you
- **UPI payment** is the fastest and most reliable option
- Save the **1323 helpline number** for any mid-journey food order issues
- Check our [IRCTC login fix guide](/guide/irctc-account-login-problems-fix) if you can't log in to place food orders

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Can I order food on IRCTC without a confirmed ticket?
No. You need a confirmed or RAC ticket with a valid PNR to order food through e-Catering. Waitlisted tickets are not eligible.

### Q2: What is the e-Catering helpline number?
Call **1323** (toll-free) for e-Catering support. You can also email ecatering@irctc.co.in.

### Q3: How early should I order food on IRCTC?
Order at least 2 hours before the train reaches the delivery station. Some restaurants have cut-off times, so ordering earlier is better.

### Q4: Will I get a refund if my food order is auto-cancelled?
Yes. Auto-cancelled orders are automatically refunded to your original payment method within 5-7 working days.

### Q5: Can I cancel my e-Catering order myself?
Yes, you can cancel through **My Orders** on ecatering.irctc.co.in, but only before the restaurant starts preparing your food (usually up to 1-2 hours before delivery time).

### Q6: Is e-Catering available on Rajdhani and Shatabdi trains?
Rajdhani and Shatabdi trains have onboard catering included in the ticket price. e-Catering is primarily for trains without included meals (Superfast, Mail/Express, etc.).

### Q7: Can I order food for someone else travelling on a different PNR?
Yes. You can order food for any valid PNR — you don't need to be the ticket holder. Just enter the PNR and delivery details.
