# IRCTC OTP Not Received — How to Fix

IRCTC sends a One-Time Password (OTP) to your registered mobile number or email during login, new registration, and ticket booking. If the OTP does not arrive, you cannot proceed. This is one of the most common IRCTC complaints, especially during Tatkal booking hours.

This guide covers every reason why IRCTC OTP may not arrive and gives you working fixes.

## When Does IRCTC Send an OTP?

IRCTC sends OTP verification in these situations:

1. **Login** — OTP sent to registered mobile after entering username and password
2. **New registration** — OTP sent to verify your mobile number and email
3. **Ticket booking** — OTP sent before final payment confirmation
4. **Password reset** — OTP sent to verify identity before allowing password change
5. **Profile update** — OTP sent when changing mobile number or email

## Why IRCTC OTP Is Not Received

### 1. DND (Do Not Disturb) Is Active

If DND is activated on your mobile number, your telecom operator may block transactional SMS from IRCTC. This is the **most common cause** of missing OTPs.

**How to check:** Dial your operator's DND status number or send an SMS:
- **Jio:** SMS `START` to 1909
- **Airtel:** SMS `STOP` to 1909
- **Vi (Vodafone-Idea):** SMS `STOP` to 1909
- **BSNL:** SMS `STOP` to 1909

Even after deactivating DND, it can take **24-72 hours** to fully take effect.

### 2. Network Delay or Poor Signal

Weak mobile network signal causes SMS delivery delays. OTPs have a short validity window (typically 2-5 minutes), so delays can make them expire before arrival.

### 3. IRCTC Server Overload

During peak hours, IRCTC servers handle millions of requests simultaneously. The OTP generation and delivery system slows down significantly.

**Peak hours when OTP delays are worst:**
- **8:00 AM – 10:00 AM** — Tatkal booking window (AC classes open at 10 AM, sleeper at 11 AM)
- **9:45 AM – 10:15 AM** — Heaviest load (everyone preparing for Tatkal)
- **11:00 AM – 11:30 AM** — Sleeper Tatkal window
- **Evening 6:00 PM – 9:00 PM** — General booking rush after work hours
- **Festive seasons** — Diwali, Chhath, Holi travel booking spikes

### 4. Wrong Mobile Number Linked to IRCTC Account

If your registered mobile number on IRCTC is different from the phone you are checking, the OTP will go to the old number. This happens when people change SIM cards but forget to update IRCTC.

### 5. SMS Inbox Full

Some older phones have an SMS storage limit. If your inbox is full, new messages (including OTPs) cannot be delivered.

### 6. IRCTC System Issue

Occasionally, IRCTC's OTP service itself faces technical problems. This affects all users simultaneously and is resolved by IRCTC's technical team.

## Step-by-Step Fixes for IRCTC OTP Not Received

### Fix 1: Check and Disable DND

1. Open your phone's dialer
2. Call **1909** (TRAI DND helpline — works for all operators)
3. Follow the IVR prompts to check DND status
4. If DND is active, request deactivation
5. Alternatively, SMS `STOP` to **1909**
6. Wait at least 24 hours for DND deactivation to take effect
7. Try IRCTC OTP again after DND is fully deactivated

### Fix 2: Wait and Retry

1. After requesting OTP, **wait at least 2 minutes** before clicking "Resend OTP"
2. OTP may be delayed but still on its way
3. Click **"Resend OTP"** on the IRCTC page if available
4. You usually get 3-5 resend attempts before being temporarily blocked
5. If OTP arrives late, use the **latest OTP** (earlier ones get invalidated)

### Fix 3: Use Email OTP Option

IRCTC allows switching from mobile OTP to email OTP:

1. On the OTP verification screen, look for **"Send OTP on Email"** or **"Get OTP via Email"** link
2. Click it — IRCTC will send OTP to your registered email address
3. Check your email inbox (also check **Spam/Junk** folder)
4. Enter the email OTP on the IRCTC page
5. This bypasses mobile network issues entirely

> **Tip:** Email OTP is more reliable during peak hours since it does not depend on mobile networks.

### Fix 4: Clear Browser Cache and Cookies

Corrupted cache can cause the OTP request to fail silently:

1. Open your browser settings (Chrome: three dots → Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data)
2. Select **"Cookies and other site data"** and **"Cached images and files"**
3. Clear data for at least the **last 24 hours**
4. Close and reopen the browser
5. Go to [irctc.co.in](https://www.irctc.co.in/) and try logging in again
6. The fresh session often resolves OTP delivery issues

### Fix 5: Try IRCTC App Instead of Website

The IRCTC Rail Connect app often handles OTP delivery better than the website:

1. Download **IRCTC Rail Connect** from Google Play Store or Apple App Store
2. Open the app and log in with your IRCTC credentials
3. The app uses a different OTP delivery pathway that is sometimes faster
4. App also supports **biometric login** (fingerprint/face) on some devices, which skips OTP entirely

**Why the app works better:**
- Dedicated servers separate from website traffic
- Push notification-based OTP delivery (not just SMS)
- Less affected during Tatkal rush since fewer users are on the app

### Fix 6: Check if Mobile Number Is Correct on IRCTC

1. Visit [www.irctc.co.in](https://www.irctc.co.in/)
2. Log in to your account (use email OTP if mobile OTP is not working)
3. Go to **My Profile** → **Personal Details**
4. Verify the mobile number shown is your current active number
5. If the number is wrong, you need to update it (see section below)

### Fix 7: Basic Phone Checks

- **Restart your phone** — clears temporary network glitches
- **Check if your SIM is active** — try making a call
- **Delete old SMS** — free up inbox space if your phone is old
- **Check if you have network signal** — OTP needs SMS network, not just data
- **Disable any SMS-blocking apps** — some apps filter "spam" and may block OTP messages

## How to Update Mobile Number on IRCTC Account

If your IRCTC account has an old or wrong mobile number, follow these steps:

### Method 1: Update via IRCTC Website

1. Go to [www.irctc.co.in](https://www.irctc.co.in/) and log in
2. Click on your **username** (top right) → **My Profile**
3. Click **"Edit"** next to Personal Details
4. Update your **mobile number** to the new one
5. IRCTC will send an OTP to the **new mobile number** for verification
6. Enter the OTP and save changes

### Method 2: Update via IRCTC App

1. Open IRCTC Rail Connect app
2. Go to **My Account** → **Profile**
3. Tap **Edit** and change the mobile number
4. Verify with OTP sent to the new number

### Method 3: Contact IRCTC Care (If You Cannot Log In)

If you cannot log in at all because OTP goes to an old number you no longer have:

1. Email **care@irctc.co.in** with subject: "Mobile Number Update Request"
2. Include: Full name, User ID, registered email, old mobile number, new mobile number
3. Attach a copy of your **Aadhaar card** or **ID proof** for verification
4. IRCTC support typically responds within **3-5 working days**
5. You can also call IRCTC helpline: **14646** or **011-23340000**

## Tatkal Booking OTP Tips

During Tatkal hours, every second matters. OTP delays can cost you a confirmed ticket.

**Preparation tips:**
1. **Log in 15 minutes before Tatkal opens** — complete OTP verification early
2. **Keep your session active** — do not let the browser tab go idle
3. **Pre-fill passenger details** — use the master passenger list feature
4. **Use IRCTC app** — generally faster OTP delivery during Tatkal
5. **Have email open** — switch to email OTP instantly if mobile OTP is delayed
6. **Use a stable internet connection** — Wi-Fi preferred over mobile data
7. **Avoid peak overload** — Tatkal for AC classes (10:00 AM) has slightly less rush than Sleeper (11:00 AM) on some routes

## Important Tips

1. **Never share your OTP** with anyone — IRCTC will never call and ask for your OTP
2. **OTPs expire in 2-5 minutes** — enter them quickly once received
3. **Use email OTP as backup** — always keep your email updated on IRCTC
4. **Deactivate DND permanently** if you use IRCTC regularly
5. **Bookmark the correct URL** — always use [www.irctc.co.in](https://www.irctc.co.in/), not third-party sites

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Why is IRCTC OTP not coming on my mobile?
The most common reasons are: DND (Do Not Disturb) active on your number, IRCTC server overload during peak hours, network issues, or a wrong mobile number linked to your account. Follow the step-by-step fixes above to resolve it.

### Q2: How do I get IRCTC OTP on email instead of mobile?
On the OTP verification screen, click "Send OTP on Email" or "Get OTP via Email." IRCTC will send the OTP to your registered email address. Check your spam folder if you don't see it in inbox.

### Q3: IRCTC OTP received but showing "Invalid OTP" — what to do?
This happens when you enter an expired OTP or an old OTP (if you clicked Resend). Always use the **latest OTP** received. Also ensure you are entering it on the same browser tab/session where you requested it.

### Q4: Can I book Tatkal tickets without OTP?
No. OTP verification is mandatory for all IRCTC ticket bookings. You cannot skip it. The best approach is to log in early (before 10 AM) so that the login OTP is already verified, and only the payment OTP remains during actual booking.

### Q5: How long does IRCTC OTP take to arrive?
Normally, IRCTC OTP arrives within **30 seconds to 2 minutes**. During peak hours (Tatkal timing), it can take **5-10 minutes** or more. If it doesn't arrive in 5 minutes, try the email OTP option.

### Q6: I changed my phone number. How do I update it on IRCTC?
Log in to IRCTC → My Profile → Edit Personal Details → Update mobile number → Verify with OTP on new number. If you cannot log in, email care@irctc.co.in with your details and ID proof.

### Q7: Does IRCTC OTP work on dual SIM phones?
Yes, OTP is sent via SMS to your registered number regardless of SIM slot. Ensure the registered SIM is active and has network coverage. Some phones redirect SMS to a specific SIM — check your phone's SIM settings.
