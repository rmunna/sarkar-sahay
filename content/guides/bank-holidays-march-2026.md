# Bank Holidays in March 2026 — Complete List of Bank Holidays in India

March 2026 is one of the busiest months for bank holidays in India. With festivals like Holi, Eid-Ul-Fitr, Ram Navami, and Mahavir Jayanti falling in this month, banks across India will remain closed for up to **11 days** (excluding regular weekend closures). Plan your bank visits carefully using this complete RBI holiday calendar for March 2026.

## How Bank Weekend Closures Work

Before looking at festival holidays, understand the regular weekly closures:

- **All Sundays** — Banks are closed every Sunday
- **2nd and 4th Saturdays** — Banks are closed on the 2nd Saturday (March 14) and 4th Saturday (March 28)
- **1st, 3rd, and 5th Saturdays** — Banks remain **open** on March 7, 21

This means in March 2026, there are **7 regular weekend closures**: 5 Sundays + 2 Saturdays.

## Total Working Days in March 2026

For most cities, the total working days in March 2026 will be approximately **17-19 days** depending on which regional holidays apply to your state. Major metro cities like Mumbai and Delhi will have fewer working days due to multiple festival closures.

## Important Tips for Planning Bank Visits in March 2026

1. **Plan around Holi week** — Banks in North India will be closed March 2-4. Complete urgent transactions before February 28.

2. **Use digital banking** — NEFT, RTGS, UPI, and internet banking work even on bank holidays. Use them for urgent transfers.

3. **ATM withdrawals** — ATMs remain functional on holidays, but may run out of cash during long weekends. Withdraw cash in advance.

4. **Financial year-end rush** — March 31 is the last day of the financial year AND a bank holiday (Mahavir Jayanti) in many cities. Complete all FY-end banking work by March 30.

5. **Check your specific city** — Bank holidays vary by city. Always confirm with your local branch or check the [RBI Holiday Calendar](https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/HolidayMatrixDisplay.aspx).

6. **Clearing delays** — Cheque clearances may be delayed during holiday periods. Allow extra processing time for cheque deposits.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are banks closed on Holi 2026?
Yes. Banks are closed on March 3, 2026 (Holi) in most cities across India. In Uttar Pradesh, banks also close on March 2 for Holika Dahan. In North India and several other states, March 4 is also a holiday for Holi 2nd Day.

### How many days are banks closed in March 2026?
Banks across India will be closed for up to **11 days** in March 2026 due to festival holidays (excluding regular Sunday and Saturday closures). The exact number depends on your city and state.

### Is March 31, 2026 a bank holiday?
Yes, March 31, 2026 is a bank holiday in many cities due to Mahavir Jayanti. This is significant as it is also the last day of the financial year. Complete all FY-end banking before March 30.

### Do ATMs work on bank holidays?
Yes, ATMs continue to function on bank holidays. However, if an ATM runs out of cash, it may not be refilled until the next working day. Plan your cash withdrawals accordingly.

### Can I do online banking on bank holidays?
Yes. Internet banking, mobile banking, UPI payments, NEFT, and RTGS services are available on most bank holidays. Only branch-level services are affected by bank closures.

### Is Eid-Ul-Fitr a bank holiday in 2026?
Yes. Eid-Ul-Fitr (Ramzan-Id) falls on March 20-21, 2026. March 21 is a near-nationwide bank holiday observed in 30 cities. March 20 is observed in select cities including Jammu, Kochi, Srinagar, Thiruvananthapuram, and Vijayawada.

### When is the longest bank closure streak in March 2026?
The longest streak depends on your city. In North India, banks may be closed from **February 28 (4th Saturday) through March 4 (Holi 2nd Day)** — a stretch of 5 consecutive days including the Sunday.

### Where can I check the official RBI holiday list?
Visit the official RBI Holiday Matrix at [rbi.org.in](https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/HolidayMatrixDisplay.aspx) to check city-wise holiday details.
