## What is Bihar Krishi Input Anudan?

Bihar Krishi Input Anudan (Agricultural Input Grant) is a state government scheme that provides **direct cash compensation** to farmers in Bihar whose crops are damaged by **floods, excess rains, or drought** (crop loss ≥33%). The scheme is implemented through the **DBT Agriculture Bihar portal** and payments are made directly to the farmer's bank account.

---

## Key Benefits

| Crop Type / Damage Condition | Rate per Hectare |
|-----------------------------|-----------------|
| Irrigated agricultural land (crop loss ≥33%) | ₹13,500/ha |
| Unirrigated (rainfed) agricultural land | ₹6,800/ha |
| Perennial crops (orchards, plantations) | ₹18,000/ha |
| Max area covered per farmer | Up to 2 hectares |

**Maximum total benefit:** 2 ha × ₹13,500 = **₹27,000** (for irrigated land with total crop loss)

---

## Who is Eligible?

**You ARE eligible if:**
- You are a **farmer in Bihar** with your own or tenant-cultivated agricultural land
- Your **crop loss is at least 33%** due to flood, excess rain, or drought (as per government crop loss survey)
- The damage is in a **notified block/district** — the government officially declares affected areas
- You are registered on **DBT Agriculture Bihar portal**

**You are NOT eligible if:**
- Your block is not in the officially notified disaster-affected area
- Your crop loss is less than 33%
- You have already received PMFBY (crop insurance) for the same loss in the same season

---

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar Card** — mandatory for DBT
2. **DBT Agriculture registration** — at dbtagriculture.bihar.gov.in
3. **Land records** — khasra, khatauni/jamabandi (proof of land ownership or tenancy)
4. **Bank passbook** — Aadhaar-linked
5. **Photograph** of damaged crop field (in some applications)

## How to Apply — Step by Step

### Step 1 — Register on DBT Agriculture Bihar Portal

If not already registered:
1. Go to **[dbtagriculture.bihar.gov.in](https://dbtagriculture.bihar.gov.in)**
2. Click **"Farmer Registration"**
3. Register with Aadhaar and mobile number
4. Get your **Farmer Registration Number**

### Step 2 — Wait for Government Notification

After a flood or drought event, the Bihar government:
1. Surveys affected blocks
2. Officially **notifies affected blocks** for Krishi Input Anudan
3. Opens the application window (usually 30–45 days)

Check the portal or contact your local **Block Agriculture Officer (BAO)** to know when applications open.

### Step 3 — Apply Online

Once the application window is open:
1. Login to **[dbtagriculture.bihar.gov.in](https://dbtagriculture.bihar.gov.in)**
2. Go to **"Krishi Input Anudan"** → Apply
3. Select the relevant season/notification
4. Enter your land details (khasra number, area, crop type)
5. Submit application

### Step 4 — Verification

- **Panchayat level verification** by BAO/Patwari
- Crop loss is verified against the government's block-level survey report

### Step 5 — DBT Payment

After verification, the grant amount is transferred directly to your Aadhaar-linked bank account, typically within 30–60 days of application.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. My crops were damaged by floods but my block is not in the notified list. What can I do?**

The compensation is only for officially notified blocks. If your block is not notified, you cannot apply for Krishi Input Anudan. You can raise a representation to the DM/District Agriculture Officer requesting block-level assessment.

**2. I am a tenant farmer. Can I apply?**

Yes, tenant farmers (sharecroppers/bataidars) can apply, but they must have valid land records showing tenancy or a declaration from the land owner. The limit is up to 2 hectares of cultivated area.

**3. I have 4 hectares of land. Do I get compensation for all 4 hectares?**

No. The maximum compensable area is **2 hectares per farmer**.

**4. Can I apply for both PMFBY (crop insurance) and Krishi Input Anudan for the same crop loss?**

Generally, you should not receive double compensation for the same loss. If you have a crop insurance claim under PMFBY, check with your BAO about whether Krishi Input Anudan is also applicable — some cases allow both.

**5. I don't have a smartphone. How do I apply?**

Visit the nearest **Common Service Centre (CSC/Jan Seva Kendra)** or **Block Agriculture Office**. The VLE at CSC can apply on your behalf on the DBT Agriculture portal.
