# Income Tax Section 245 Notice — Refund Adjusted Against Old Demand

If you filed ITR and expected a refund, but instead got an email with subject **"Proposed adjustment of demand/refund under Section 245"**, the IT Department wants to use your current refund to settle an old tax demand from a previous year.

> **What this means**: You're getting ₹X refund this year, and the IT department has an old demand of ₹Y from a past year. Before releasing your refund, they're asking if you agree to let them net it off.

## Why Did You Get This Notice?

The IT department uses Section 245 to set off outstanding demands against current-year refunds. Common reasons your refund may be held:

| Reason | Example |
|--------|---------|
| Old 143(1) demand | You owed ₹5,000 in AY 2021-22, never paid |
| Old 143(3) scrutiny demand | An old assessment added income you owe tax on |
| Old 148 reassessment demand | A previous year was reopened and assessed |
| Old penalty order | Section 271(1)(c) penalty was imposed in a past year |
| Interest demand | 234A/234B interest from a previous return |

## You Have 30 Days to Respond

The notice gives you **30 days** to respond. If you don't respond within 30 days, the IT department may:
1. Proceed with the refund adjustment (reduce your refund by the demand amount)
2. Or withhold the refund entirely if demand > refund

Your response options are:
1. **Agree to adjustment** (if the demand is valid)
2. **Disagree — demand is wrong** (dispute the demand)

## How to Respond to Section 245 Notice Online

### Step 1: View the Notice

1. Login to **[incometax.gov.in](https://incometax.gov.in/)**
2. Go to **"Pending Actions"** → **"e-Proceedings"**
3. Find your Section 245 notice
4. Click to view: It shows the current refund amount, the outstanding demand year, and the demand amount

### Step 2: Choose Your Response

**Option A — Agree to Adjustment**

If you accept the demand:
1. In e-Proceedings, find your 245 notice → "Submit Response"
2. Select: "I agree to the proposed adjustment"
3. The refund (minus adjusted demand) will be credited to your bank account
4. Remaining demand: If demand > refund, the balance demand remains on your record

**Option B — Disagree (Demand is Wrong)**

If you believe the demand is incorrect:
1. In e-Proceedings → Submit Response
2. Select: "I disagree with the proposed adjustment"
3. Provide reason (e.g., "Demand has been paid and challan uploaded", "Demand was rectified", "Demand is under appeal")
4. Upload supporting documents (payment challan, appeal order, rectification acknowledgement)

The IT officer reviews your response. If accepted, your refund proceeds without adjustment.

## Common Scenarios and What to Do

### Scenario 1: Old Demand That Was Already Paid

You paid the demand 2 years ago but the portal still shows it outstanding.

**Fix**:
1. Find your old payment challan (BSR Code + Challan Serial Number + Date)
2. Login to incometax.gov.in → **Update Challan** (if not already linked to that year's return)
3. OR respond to 245 notice with challan details as proof
4. The demand status should update once verified

### Scenario 2: Old Demand Is Under Appeal

You filed an appeal (Form 35) against the demand and it's pending at CIT(A).

**Fix**:
1. In your 245 response, select "Disagree" and state: "Appeal pending before CIT(A)"
2. Upload a copy of your appeal acknowledgement
3. The adjustment should be stayed pending appeal outcome

### Scenario 3: Demand Is From a TDS Mismatch You've Already Fixed

You got 143(1) demand due to TDS mismatch, but the TDS was later deposited and 26AS updated.

**Fix**:
1. File a rectification request on the old demand year (not the 245 notice — the source demand)
2. Once rectification clears the demand, it disappears from the 245 notice list
3. OR respond to 245 notice with updated 26AS as evidence

### Scenario 4: You Have No Knowledge of the Demand

Sometimes demands from old years are unknown (e.g., you changed CA, didn't know about a scrutiny notice).

**Fix**:
1. Download the old demand year's 143(1) or assessment order from e-Proceedings (change the Assessment Year filter)
2. Check what the demand is for
3. If valid: Pay it and update challan
4. If invalid: File rectification or appeal

## After Responding — What Happens to Your Refund?

| Outcome | Timeline |
|---------|---------|
| You agree to adjustment | Adjusted refund credited in 7-14 working days |
| You disagree — officer accepts | Full refund within 30-45 days |
| You disagree — officer rejects | Demand adjusted from refund; you need to appeal further |
| No response in 30 days | IT Dept may proceed with adjustment |

## How to See All Your Outstanding Demands

1. Login to incometax.gov.in → **"Pending Actions"** → **"Response to Outstanding Demand"**
2. All outstanding demands across all years are listed here
3. For each demand, you can see the amount, assessment year, and current status

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I expected a ₹40,000 refund but only received ₹15,000. Was a 245 adjustment made?

Check your email for a Section 245 notice. Also check Form 26AS or incometax.gov.in under "View Tax Credit" → "Refund Status" — it shows the refund amount and if any adjustment was made.

### Can the IT department adjust a refund without sending a 245 notice?

The law requires them to issue a 245 notice and give you an opportunity to respond before adjusting. If you didn't receive a notice and your refund was still adjusted, file a grievance at incometax.gov.in → "Grievance" → "e-Nivaran."

### My refund is for ₹50,000 but the old demand is ₹1 lakh. What happens?

The entire ₹50,000 refund is adjusted against the ₹1 lakh demand, leaving ₹50,000 balance demand still on your record. This balance can be recovered through other means (future refunds, TRO recovery).

### The 245 notice shows a demand from 10 years ago. Is it valid?

Old demands can remain on record for years. However, the IT department has limitations on when they can actively recover demands. If the demand is very old and you believe it's time-barred, consult a tax professional.
