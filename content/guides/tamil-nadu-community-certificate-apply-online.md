# Tamil Nadu Community Certificate Apply Online 2026

In Tamil Nadu, the term used for caste certificate is **"Community Certificate"** (சமுதாய சான்றிதழ்). It is issued by the Revenue Department and is required for TN government college admissions, NEET counselling (state quota), scholarship applications, government job reservations, and scheme eligibility. Applications are processed through **TN edistrict** portal.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [tnedistrict.tn.gov.in](https://tnedistrict.tn.gov.in) or nearest **CSC / Arasu Kalavai (Village Kiosk)**. Issued by Tahsildar within **15–30 days**.

---

## Community Categories in Tamil Nadu

| Category | Full Form | Reservation % |
|----------|-----------|---------------|
| **BC** | Backward Classes | 26.5% |
| **MBC/DNC** | Most Backward Classes & Denotified Communities | 20% |
| **BCM** | Backward Classes (Muslim) | 3.5% |
| **SC** | Scheduled Castes | 18% |
| **SCA** | Scheduled Castes (Arunthathiyar) | 3% |
| **ST** | Scheduled Tribes | 1% |
| **OC** | Open Category / Forward | No reservation |

> **SCA (Arunthathiyar)** is a special sub-category within SC in Tamil Nadu — additional 3% reservation within the SC 18%.

## Why You Need TN Community Certificate

- **Government college admission** (engineering, medicine, polytechnic) — mandatory for reserved seats
- **NEET counselling state quota** — community certificate required
- **TN government scholarships** — BC/MBC/SC/ST post-matric
- **Tamil Nadu BC, MBC, SC/ST scholarship** applications
- **Government job reservation** — state PSC, TNPSC, group exams
- **Fee concession** in government colleges (100% fee waiver for SC/ST, 50% for BC/MBC in some schemes)
- **Kalaignar/Amma schemes** eligibility

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory)
- **Ration card** (important — community is listed on TN ration card)
- **Father's / grandfather's community proof** (school TC/SSLC certificate with community, ration card)
- **Birth certificate** or school records showing family details
- Self-declaration affidavit (community declaration)
- Voter ID (additional address proof)
- Passport-size photograph

> **Key document:** The community listed on your **father's ration card or school TC** is the primary evidence. In TN, the ration card prominently lists community category.

## How to Apply Online — TN edistrict Portal

1. Go to [tnedistrict.tn.gov.in](https://tnedistrict.tn.gov.in)
2. Click **"New User? Register"** → register with mobile number
3. Log in → click **"Revenue"** → **"Community Certificate"**
4. Fill form:
   - Applicant name, address (district, taluk, revenue village)
   - Community/category declaration
   - Father's community details
5. Upload documents (Aadhaar, father's TC/ration card, photograph)
6. Pay fee: **₹20–30** (online payment)
7. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via Common Service Centre (CSC) / Arasu Kalavai

Tamil Nadu's **Arasu Kalavai** (Government Kiosks) and CSCs assist with edistrict applications:

1. Visit nearest **Arasu Kalavai** or CSC
2. Provide Aadhaar, ration card, father's TC/community proof
3. Operator fills and submits application
4. Service charge: approximately ₹30–60
5. Collect acknowledgement

## Download TN Community Certificate

Once approved:
1. Log in to tnedistrict.tn.gov.in
2. Go to **"My Certificates"** → click Community Certificate
3. Download digitally signed PDF
4. Valid for all official purposes in Tamil Nadu and central schemes

## Nativity Certificate vs Community Certificate in TN

| Certificate | Purpose |
|-------------|---------|
| **Community Certificate** | Proves your caste/community (SC/BC/MBC etc.) |
| **Nativity Certificate** | Proves you're a native/resident of Tamil Nadu (for admissions) |
| **Income Certificate** | Proves annual family income |

> For **government college admissions in TN**, you need **all three** — community, nativity, and income certificates.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My father's ration card shows "BC" but I applied for MBC — will it be rejected?

If your community is in the **MBC/DNC list** (Tamil Nadu MBC list), you can apply for MBC even if ration card shows "BC" broadly. The actual community name listed must match the MBC/DNC schedule. The Tahsildar verifies this.

### Community certificate for NEET counselling — what validity?

For NEET counselling, TN requires community certificate issued **in the current academic year**. Old certificates are not accepted for medical admissions.

### Can I download my TN community certificate without visiting the office?

Yes — once approved, download from tnedistrict.tn.gov.in → "My Certificates." The digitally signed PDF is valid everywhere.
