# Maharashtra Shetkari Karjmukti Yojana 2026 — Farmer Loan Waiver Apply Online

The Maharashtra Cabinet on **June 2, 2026** approved the **Punyashlok Ahilyadevi Holkar Shetkari Karjmukti Yojana 2026** — a ₹36,585 crore farm loan waiver scheme covering **55.72 lakh farmers** (approximately 56 lakh) across Maharashtra.

> **Quick answer:** If you have an outstanding short-term crop loan up to ₹2 lakh (disbursed April 2019–March 2025, overdue as of March 31, 2025), your entire loan will be waived. Apply via [mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in](https://mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in) or at your bank/Common Service Centre.

---

## Scheme Components — 3 Types of Relief

| Component | Who benefits | Relief |
|---|---|---|
| **Full waiver** | Outstanding crop loan ≤ ₹2 lakh | **100% waiver** |
| **Settlement (one-time)** | Loan > ₹2 lakh (partial coverage) | ₹2 lakh waived; farmer pays remainder |
| **Interest incentive** | Farmers who repaid on time | **Interest subvention** (reward for regular repayment) |

---

## Eligibility

| Criteria | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **State** | Maharashtra resident |
| **Occupation** | Farmer / cultivator |
| **Loan type** | Short-term crop loan (Kharif/Rabi) |
| **Loan period** | Disbursed between **April 1, 2019 and March 31, 2025** |
| **Loan status** | Outstanding (overdue) as of **March 31, 2025** |
| **Loan ceiling** | Loans up to ₹2 lakh waived fully; above ₹2L — partial |
| **Land** | Must have agricultural land in Maharashtra (in farmer's name/family) |

**Who is NOT eligible:**
- Income tax payers
- Central/state government employees and their family members
- Elected representatives (MPs, MLAs, Zila Parishad members)
- Loans taken after March 31, 2025
- Farmers who restructured their loans under other schemes and are current

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Method 1: Online via MahaDBT Portal

**Step 1:** Go to [mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in](https://mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in)

**Step 2:** Click **"Shetkari Karjmukti Yojana"** under Farm Schemes.

**Step 3:** Register / login with your Aadhaar + mobile OTP.

**Step 4:** Fill in your details:
- Personal info (name, address, village, district, taluka)
- Land details (survey number, gut number from 7/12)
- Bank loan account number, bank name, branch, IFSC
- Outstanding loan amount

**Step 5:** Upload documents — 7/12 extract, Aadhaar, bank passbook, loan passbook.

**Step 6:** Submit and note your application reference number.

---

### Method 2: At Your Bank / District Cooperative Bank

**Step 1:** Visit the bank where your crop loan is held (District Central Cooperative Bank, nationalized bank, or rural bank).

**Step 2:** Ask for the Karjmukti Yojana application form.

**Step 3:** Fill and submit with document copies.

**Step 4:** The bank coordinates with the government portal — your loan account is flagged for waiver after verification.

---

### Method 3: Common Service Centre (CSC) / Maha e-Seva Kendra

If you don't have internet access, visit the nearest CSC/Maha Seva Kendra and ask for help applying under Karjmukti Yojana.

---

## How the Waiver Works

1. Application submitted → bank forwards to state portal
2. Eligibility verified against land records + bank records
3. Approved → state government pays the waiver amount **directly to the bank** (the bank adjusts your loan account)
4. Farmer's loan account shows **₹0 outstanding** (for waived amount)
5. **No cash given to farmer** — the money goes directly from state treasury to the lending bank

## Frequently Asked Questions

**My loan is from a nationalized bank (SBI/Bank of Maharashtra) — am I eligible?**
Yes — the scheme covers loans from cooperative banks, nationalized banks, regional rural banks, and other institutional lenders.

**My loan is above ₹2 lakh — will I get any relief?**
Yes — under the settlement component, ₹2 lakh of your loan is waived. You need to pay the remaining amount (above ₹2 lakh) under a one-time settlement.

**I repaid my loan on time — do I get anything?**
Yes — the interest incentive component rewards on-time payers with interest subvention. Visit your bank branch to check if you qualify.

**How long will verification take?**
Verification involves cross-checking land records (7/12) with loan records. Typically 1–3 months for the full cycle. Watch for SMS notifications.

**My name is different on 7/12 and Aadhaar — what do I do?**
Visit the taluka office to get a certified name-match certificate, then apply with both documents.
