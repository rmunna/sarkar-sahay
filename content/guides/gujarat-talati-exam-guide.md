# Gujarat Talati Exam Guide — Eligibility, Pattern & Preparation Tips

The Gujarat Panchayat Service Selection Board (GPSSB) conducts the recruitment exam for Talati cum Mantri posts in Gujarat. The Talati is a village-level revenue and administrative officer responsible for maintaining land records and assisting in panchayat administration.

Gujarat Talati is one of the most popular government exams in the state with thousands of vacancies in each recruitment cycle.

## Eligibility Criteria

### Age Limit

| Category | Minimum Age | Maximum Age |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| General | 18 years | 33 years |
| OBC | 18 years | 36 years |
| SC/ST | 18 years | 38 years |

### Educational Qualification

- **Minimum:** Graduate (Bachelor's degree) from a recognized university
- Computer knowledge (CCC or equivalent)
- Knowledge of Gujarati language

## Exam Pattern

| Subject | Marks |
|---------|-------|
| Gujarati Language & Grammar | 50 |
| English Language & Grammar | 50 |
| General Knowledge | 50 |
| Mathematics / Reasoning | 50 |
| **Total** | **200** |

- **Duration:** 1 hour
- **Negative marking:** 0.25 per wrong answer
- **Type:** Online (Computer-based)

## Syllabus Overview

### Gujarati Language
- Grammar, Comprehension, Vocabulary, Muhavare, Translation

### English Language
- Grammar, Comprehension, Vocabulary, Sentence correction

### General Knowledge
- History (India & Gujarat), Geography, Polity, Economy, Science, Current Affairs, Gujarat-specific GK

### Mathematics / Reasoning
- Number system, Percentage, Ratio, Average, Profit & Loss, Logical reasoning, Series, Coding-decoding

## How to Apply

1. Visit GPSSB at [gpssb.gujarat.gov.in](https://gpssb.gujarat.gov.in) or OJAS portal
2. Find Talati cum Mantri notification
3. Register with OJAS (if not already)
4. Fill the application form
5. Upload photo and signature
6. Pay fee and submit

## Application Fee

| Category | Fee |
|----------|-----|
| General / OBC | ₹100 + portal charges |
| SC / ST | Free |

## Salary & Benefits

- **Pay Scale:** ₹19,950 – ₹63,200 (Level 2)
- DA, HRA, and other allowances as per Gujarat Government
- Pension (NPS), medical benefits
- Promotion opportunities

## Preparation Tips

1. **Gujarat GK:** Focus on Gujarat history, geography, and state schemes
2. **Gujarati language:** Strong grammar and vocabulary skills
3. **Speed matters:** 200 questions in 60 minutes — practice rapid answering
4. **Current affairs:** Follow Gujarat and national news daily
5. **Previous papers:** Solve past Talati papers from GPSSB

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is graduation mandatory for Gujarat Talati?
Yes, a Bachelor's degree is the minimum requirement.

### What is the role of Talati cum Mantri?
Maintains village land records, assists in revenue collection, and acts as secretary of the Gram Panchayat.

### Is there an interview?
No, selection is purely based on the written exam.

### Is Gujarati knowledge mandatory?
Yes, knowledge of Gujarati is required.

### How many vacancies are typically released?
Recent cycles have had 3,000-6,000+ vacancies.

### Is computer certificate required?
Yes, CCC (Course on Computer Concepts) or equivalent is needed.
