## What Are Central Government Jobs?

Central government jobs are positions in ministries, departments, and organizations under the Government of India. These are among the most sought-after careers in India due to **job security, pension benefits, housing allowance, and social status**.

Recruitment is conducted through four major bodies:

| Body | Full Name | Recruits For |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| **SSC** | Staff Selection Commission | Group B & C posts (clerks, assistants, SI, auditors) |
| **UPSC** | Union Public Service Commission | Group A & B posts (IAS, IPS, IFS, CDS, NDA) |
| **RRB** | Railway Recruitment Boards | Indian Railways (NTPC, Group D, ALP, JE) |
| **IBPS** | Institute of Banking Personnel Selection | PSU banks (clerks, PO, SO) |

## SSC — Staff Selection Commission

### Major Exams

| Exam | Posts | Eligibility | Age |
|------|-------|-------------|-----|
| **SSC CGL** | Tax Inspector, Auditor, SI, ASO | Graduate | 18–32 years |
| **SSC CHSL** | DEO, LDC, PA/SA | 12th pass | 18–27 years |
| **SSC MTS** | Multi Tasking Staff | 10th pass | 18–25 years |
| **SSC GD** | CRPF, BSF, CISF, SSB Constable | 10th pass | 18–23 years |
| **SSC JE** | Junior Engineer | Engineering diploma/degree | 18–32 years |
| **SSC Stenographer** | Stenographer Grade C & D | 12th pass | 18–30 years |

### How to Apply on SSC

1. Visit [ssc.gov.in](https://ssc.gov.in)
2. Click **"Register"** → Create a new account with email and mobile
3. Fill the **One Time Registration (OTR)** form with personal, educational, and preference details
4. Upload photo (recent, 20–50 KB, JPG) and signature (10–20 KB, JPG)
5. When a notification is released, log in and click **"Apply"**
6. Fill the exam-specific form, select exam centre preferences
7. Pay the application fee (₹100 for General/OBC; **free for SC/ST/Women/Ex-Servicemen**)
8. Download and save the application confirmation

## UPSC — Union Public Service Commission

### Major Exams

| Exam | Posts | Eligibility | Age |
|------|-------|-------------|-----|
| **Civil Services (CSE)** | IAS, IPS, IFS, IRS | Graduate (any stream) | 21–32 years |
| **CDS** | Army, Navy, Air Force officers | Graduate / Engineering | 19–25 years |
| **NDA** | Defence Academy entry | 12th pass (appeared) | 16.5–19.5 years |
| **CAPF** | Central Armed Police Forces ACs | Graduate | 20–25 years |
| **IES/ISS** | Indian Economic/Statistical Service | PG in Economics/Statistics | 21–30 years |

### How to Apply on UPSC

1. Visit [upsc.gov.in](https://upsc.gov.in)
2. Click **"Online Application for Various Examinations"**
3. Select the exam you want to apply for
4. **Part I:** Fill basic details (name, DOB, education, category)
5. **Part II:** Upload photo and signature, fill detailed form
6. Pay the fee (₹100 for Prelims; **free for SC/ST/PwD/Women**)
7. Submit and download the confirmation page

> **Attempts:** General — 6, OBC — 9, SC/ST — unlimited (until age limit). See our [exam preparation guide](/guide/government-exam-preparation-guide) for strategy.

## RRB — Railway Recruitment Boards

### Major Exams

| Exam | Posts | Eligibility | Age |
|------|-------|-------------|-----|
| **RRB NTPC** | Station Master, TA, CA, Clerk | Graduate | 18–33 years |
| **RRB Group D** | Track Maintainer, Helper, Porter | 10th pass | 18–33 years |
| **RRB ALP** | Assistant Loco Pilot | 10th + ITI/Diploma | 18–30 years |
| **RRB JE** | Junior Engineer | Engineering diploma/degree | 18–36 years |

### How to Apply on RRB

1. Visit the RRB website for your region (e.g., rrbcdg.gov.in for Delhi, rrbchennai.gov.in)
2. Click on the active recruitment notification
3. Click **"New Registration"** and fill basic details
4. Upload photo, signature, and [Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) details
5. Fill educational and personal details
6. Pay the fee (₹500 for General/OBC; ₹250 for SC/ST/Ex-SM/PwD/Women — **refunded on appearing**)
7. Submit and save the registration number

## IBPS — Banking Recruitment

### Major Exams

| Exam | Posts | Eligibility | Age |
|------|-------|-------------|-----|
| **IBPS PO** | Probationary Officer | Graduate | 20–30 years |
| **IBPS Clerk** | Clerical Cadre | Graduate | 20–28 years |
| **IBPS SO** | Specialist Officer | Relevant graduate degree | 20–30 years |
| **IBPS RRB** | Regional Rural Bank (PO/Clerk) | Graduate | 18–40 years |

### How to Apply on IBPS

1. Visit [ibps.in](https://ibps.in)
2. Click on the active recruitment → **"Click here to Apply Online"**
3. Register with basic details and get a registration number
4. Fill the detailed application form
5. Upload photo, signature, left thumb impression, and handwritten declaration
6. Pay the fee (₹850 for General/OBC; ₹175 for SC/ST/PwD)
7. Download the application form

## Age Relaxation & Reservation

### Age Relaxation

| Category | Relaxation |
|----------|-----------|
| SC/ST | +5 years |
| OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) | +3 years |

| PwD (OBC) | +13 years |
| PwD (SC/ST) | +15 years |
| Ex-Servicemen | +5 years (after deducting military service) |

## Important Tips

1. **Bookmark official sites** — Only apply from official portals (ssc.gov.in, upsc.gov.in, ibps.in). Beware of fake websites and scams
2. **Apply early** — Don't wait for the last date; servers crash and payment gateways may fail near deadlines
3. **Save everything** — Download and print your application form, fee receipt, and admit card
4. **Check eligibility carefully** — Age, education, and category criteria are strictly enforced; providing false information leads to disqualification

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is there any fee for applying to government exams?
Yes, but fees are nominal (₹100–₹850) and **SC/ST/PwD/Women candidates are often exempted** or get fee refunds (RRB). Check each exam notification for exact fee details.

### Can I apply for multiple exams simultaneously?
Yes. You can apply for SSC, UPSC, RRB, and IBPS exams simultaneously as long as exam dates don't clash. There is no restriction on applying to multiple bodies.

### What is the OBC Non-Creamy Layer certificate?
OBC candidates must have a **Non-Creamy Layer (NCL)** certificate stating their family income is below ₹8 lakh/year to avail reservation benefits. This certificate must be recent (within 1 year for most exams).

### How do I check if a government job notification is genuine?
Only check official websites (ssc.gov.in, upsc.gov.in, ibps.in, indianrailways.gov.in). Government never charges for job offers or asks for money via calls/messages. Report fraud at [cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in).

### What is the minimum qualification for government jobs?
It varies: 10th pass (SSC MTS, RRB Group D), 12th pass (SSC CHSL, NDA), Graduate (SSC CGL, UPSC CSE, IBPS PO/Clerk). Some posts require specific professional degrees.

### Can I apply for government jobs after apprenticeship?
Yes. The [National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC)](/guide/apprenticeship-apply-online) is recognized for many government job recruitments requiring ITI or trade qualifications.

### Is there an upper age limit relaxation for state domicile candidates?
Some state-level posts under central government offer domicile-based relaxation. However, for SSC/UPSC/IBPS, the relaxation is primarily based on category (SC/ST/OBC/PwD), not domicile.
