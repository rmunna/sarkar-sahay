# LPG Gas Cylinder Booking Online: Indane, HP, Bharat Gas

Booking an LPG cylinder refill in India is quick and easy — you can do it via SMS, missed call, mobile app, WhatsApp, or the online portal. This guide covers all booking methods for Indane (IOCL), HP Gas (HPCL), and Bharat Gas (BPCL).

## Method 1: Book via SMS

### Indane Gas (IOCL)
- Send SMS **"IVRS"** followed by your **17-digit consumer number** to **7718955555**
- Example: `IVRS 12345678901234567` to 7718955555
- You will receive a confirmation SMS with booking reference

### HP Gas (HPCL)
- Send SMS **"HPGAS"** followed by your **17-digit consumer number** to **7666677777**
- Example: `HPGAS 12345678901234567` to 7666677777

### Bharat Gas (BPCL)
- Send SMS **"Smart"** followed by your **17-digit consumer number** to **7200027200**
- Example: `Smart 12345678901234567` to 7200027200

> **Tip:** Send the SMS from your **registered mobile number** for instant processing.

The system automatically identifies your consumer number from your registered mobile and places the booking. You will receive a confirmation SMS.

## Method 3: Book via Mobile App

### Indane Gas
1. Download **"Indane"** app from Google Play or App Store
2. Register with your consumer number and mobile number
3. Tap **"Book Refill"**
4. Confirm booking and pay online (optional — you can pay on delivery)

### HP Gas
1. Download **"HP Pay"** app
2. Register and link your HP Gas consumer account
3. Tap **"Book Cylinder"**
4. Choose payment mode — online or cash on delivery

### Bharat Gas
1. Download **"Bharat Gas"** app
2. Register with consumer number
3. Tap **"Book Refill"**
4. Confirm and track delivery status

## Method 4: Book via Online Portal

### Indane
1. Visit [iocl.com/indane-for-you](https://www.iocl.com/indane-for-you)
2. Log in with consumer number and registered mobile
3. Click **"Book Refill"**
4. Confirm the order

### HP Gas
1. Visit [hindustanpetroleum.com/hpgas](https://www.hindustanpetroleum.com/hpgas)
2. Log in and click **"Book My Refill"**
3. Complete the booking

### Bharat Gas
1. Visit [bharatgas.com](https://www.bharatgas.com/)
2. Log in to your account
3. Click **"Quick Refill Booking"**

## Refill Schedule and Delivery

- **Minimum gap between refills:** Typically 20–25 days (varies by company)
- **Delivery time:** 3–7 days after booking in urban areas; may take longer in rural areas
- **Maximum refills per year:** No limit on total refills, but only **12 cylinders per year** get the subsidy
- **Delivery hours:** Usually 9 AM to 6 PM on working days

### Track Delivery Status
- Check via the mobile app or OMC portal
- SMS updates are sent at each stage — booking confirmed, out for delivery, delivered

## Composite Cylinders

Composite LPG cylinders are a modern alternative to traditional metal cylinders:

| Feature | Traditional (Metal) | Composite |
|---------|-------------------|-----------|
| Weight (empty) | ~15 kg | ~5.5 kg |
| Transparency | Cannot see gas level | Translucent — visible gas level |
| Rust | Can rust over time | Rust-proof |
| Explosion risk | Higher | Safer — does not explode |
| Availability | Widely available | Limited availability |
| Security deposit | ₹1,450–₹1,600 | ₹3,500–₹4,000 |

> **Note:** Composite cylinders are available in select areas. Contact your distributor to check availability.

## Important Tips

1. **Register your mobile number** with your LPG connection — all booking methods require it.
2. **Check your refill due date** before booking — bookings placed too early may get rejected.
3. **Pay online** to avoid cash handling and get instant payment confirmation.
4. **Verify the delivery person's ID** and get a proper receipt for every delivery.
5. **Report non-delivery** within 7 days by calling the distributor or OMC helpline.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Can I book a cylinder without a registered mobile number?
No, all automated booking methods (SMS, missed call, WhatsApp, app) require your registered mobile number. Visit your distributor to update your mobile number.

### 2. How do I know if my booking is confirmed?
You will receive a **confirmation SMS** with a booking reference number within minutes of placing the order.

### 3. Can I cancel a cylinder booking?
Yes, contact your distributor directly or use the OMC portal/app to cancel before delivery.

### 4. What is the minimum gap between two refill bookings?
The minimum gap is typically **20–25 days**, but it can vary. The system will reject bookings placed too early.

### 5. Can I book a 5 kg cylinder online?
Yes, if you have a 5 kg cylinder connection, you can book refills using the same methods listed above.

### 6. What if I don't receive a confirmation SMS?
Wait 15–30 minutes. If no SMS arrives, try booking again or call the OMC helpline to confirm.

### 7. Can I pay for the cylinder online?
Yes, all three OMCs support online payment via their apps and portals using UPI, debit card, credit card, or net banking.
