# Online LPG Gas Cylinder Booking — Indane, HP Gas, Bharat Gas

Booking an LPG gas cylinder refill in India is now very easy. You can book your next cylinder from home using a phone call, SMS, mobile app, or website. This guide covers all three major LPG providers — **Indian Oil (Indane)**, **Hindustan Petroleum (HP Gas)**, and **Bharat Petroleum (Bharat Gas)**.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent informational website. We are not affiliated with any government body, oil marketing company, or LPG distributor. Always verify details on the official websites.

---

## What is LPG Gas Cylinder Booking?

LPG (Liquefied Petroleum Gas) is the cooking gas used in most Indian households. When your cylinder runs out, you need to place a refill order with your distributor. Earlier, people had to visit the agency or call the local dealer. Now, you can book online through apps, websites, missed calls, or SMS — 24/7.

India has three public sector oil marketing companies (OMCs) that supply domestic LPG:

- **Indane** (Indian Oil Corporation)
- **HP Gas** (Hindustan Petroleum)
- **Bharat Gas** (Bharat Petroleum)

---

## Eligibility

- You must have an **active LPG connection** with any of the three providers.
- Your **registered mobile number** must be linked to your LPG account.
- Your previous cylinder should be at least **15 days old** (for subsidised refills).
- No outstanding dues on your account.

If you don't have a connection yet, check our guide on [getting a new LPG connection](/guide/lpg-gas-booking-online).

---

## Documents Needed

For booking a refill, no documents are needed — just your **registered mobile number** or **LPG consumer number** (17-digit ID).

For a **new connection**, you will need:
- Aadhaar card
- Address proof
- Passport-size photo
- Bank account details (for subsidy via PAHAL/DBT)

## Step-by-Step: Book LPG Cylinder Online

### Method 1: Missed Call Booking

The simplest way. Just give a missed call from your registered mobile number:

| Provider | Missed Call Number |
|---|---|
| Indane | 7718955555 |
| HP Gas | 9222201122 |
| Bharat Gas | 7710955555 |

You will receive an SMS confirming your booking and expected delivery date.

### Method 2: Book via Mobile App

**Indane Gas:**
1. Download the **IndianOil ONE** app from Play Store or App Store.
2. Log in with your registered mobile number.
3. Go to **LPG Services** → **Book Refill**.
4. Confirm your consumer number and tap **Book Now**.
5. You will get instant confirmation with a booking reference number.

**HP Gas:**
1. Download the **HP Pay** app.
2. Register with your mobile number linked to HP Gas.
3. Select **Book My LPG Refill**.
4. Confirm and pay online if required.

**Bharat Gas:**
1. Download the **Bharat Gas** app.
2. Log in and select **Book Refill**.
3. Choose your connection and confirm.

### Method 3: Book via Website

- **Indane:** Visit [indane.co.in](https://www.indane.co.in) → LPG Booking → Enter consumer number → Submit.
- **HP Gas:** Visit [myhpgas.in](https://myhpgas.in) → Quick Refill Booking.
- **Bharat Gas:** Visit [bharatgas.in](https://www.bharatgas.in) → Online Refill Booking.

### Method 4: Book via SMS

Send an SMS with your **LPG consumer number** to:

| Provider | SMS Number |
|---|---|
| Indane | 7718955555 |
| HP Gas | 9222201122 |
| Bharat Gas | 7710955555 |

### Method 5: Book via WhatsApp

Some providers now support WhatsApp booking. Save the number and send "BOOK" or your consumer number:

- **Indane:** 7588888824
- **HP Gas:** 9222201122

## Offline Method

1. Visit your nearest LPG distributor or gas agency.
2. Provide your consumer number or registered name.
3. The agency will place the booking for you.
4. You can also call your local distributor directly.

## Fees and Cylinder Prices

LPG cylinder prices are revised on the **1st of every month** by the OMCs. As of early 2026:

- **14.2 kg domestic cylinder:** ₹800–₹900 (varies by city)
- **19 kg commercial cylinder:** ₹1,800–₹1,900
- **5 kg FTL (Free Trade LPG):** ₹500–₹600

Subsidised consumers receive the subsidy directly in their bank account under the **PAHAL (DBTL)** scheme.

> Prices change monthly. Check the official website of your provider for the latest rates.

## Processing Time / Delivery

- **Normal booking:** Delivery within **3–7 days** depending on your area and demand.
- **Emergency booking:** Some distributors offer same-day or next-day delivery at extra charges.
- **Festive season / winter:** Expect delays of up to 10 days in some regions.

You can track your delivery status on the respective app or website.

## Tips and Best Practices

1. **Keep your mobile number updated** — All bookings and OTPs go to your registered number. Visit your distributor to update it.
2. **Link your Aadhaar and bank account** to receive LPG subsidy (PAHAL/DBT). Check status at [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in).
3. **Book early** — Don't wait until the cylinder is completely empty. Book when you have 2–3 days of gas left.
4. **Check delivery status** regularly on the app to plan your cooking.
5. **Pay online** to avoid cash hassles during delivery. UPI payments are accepted by most distributors.
6. **Safety first** — Always check the seal and weight of the delivered cylinder. Report any leaks immediately.
7. If you're moving to a new city, you can **transfer your LPG connection** — see our guide on [address change for LPG](/guide/lpg-gas-booking-online).

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Can I book a refill if my previous cylinder is less than 15 days old?

Generally no, for subsidised cylinders there is a minimum gap. However, you can book a non-subsidised (market price) cylinder anytime.

### 2. How do I check my LPG subsidy status?

Visit [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in) and log in with your consumer number. You can also check via the UMANG app or your bank passbook.

### 3. What if I don't receive the cylinder within 7 days?

Contact your local distributor first. If unresolved, file a complaint on the provider's website or call their customer care:
- Indane: 1800-2333-555
- HP Gas: 1800-233-3555
- Bharat Gas: 1800-22-4344

### 4. Can I book for someone else's connection from my phone?

No. The booking must be placed from the **registered mobile number** linked to that LPG connection.

### 5. How do I surrender or cancel my LPG connection?

Visit your distributor with your consumer card and passbook. The security deposit on the cylinder and regulator will be refunded. See our guide on [surrendering LPG connection](/guide/lpg-gas-booking-online).

### 6. How many subsidised cylinders can I get per year?

As per the current policy, consumers are entitled to **12 subsidised cylinders per year**. Beyond that, you pay the market price.

### 7. Can I change my LPG provider (e.g., from Indane to HP Gas)?

Yes, portability is allowed. Visit the new distributor with your documents and they will process the transfer.
