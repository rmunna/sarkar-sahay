## What is an Income Certificate?

An Income Certificate (Aay Praman Patra) is an official document issued by the state government that certifies the annual income of a person or family. It is essential for availing various government benefits, scholarships, and fee waivers.

## Why Do You Need It?

- **Educational fee waiver** and concessions
- **Scholarship applications** (central and state)
- **Government scheme eligibility** (BPL, EWS benefits)
- **OBC non-creamy layer** certification
- **Subsidized housing** and government allotments
- **Legal aid** (free legal services)
- **Medical treatment** concessions at government hospitals
- **EWS reservation** (10% quota — requires income below ₹8 lakh)

## Who is Eligible?

- Any **resident of the state** where they are applying
- Can be issued to individuals or families (head of household)
- No restriction on income level — the certificate simply states your income

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar Card**
- **Self-declaration / Affidavit** on stamp paper declaring income sources
- **Salary slip** (for salaried individuals)
- **ITR acknowledgment** (if filed)
- **Ration Card** (shows family details)
- **Bank statement** (last 6 months)
- **Proof of Address** (Voter ID, utility bill)
- **Passport-size photographs**

> If you are a farmer or self-employed, the self-declaration/affidavit is the primary proof. Revenue officer may verify.

## Step-by-Step Process — Online

### Step 1: Visit Your State e-District Portal

Common portals:
- **Uttar Pradesh:** edistrict.up.gov.in
- **Bihar:** serviceonline.bihar.gov.in
- **Delhi:** edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in
- **Maharashtra:** aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in
- **Rajasthan:** emitra.rajasthan.gov.in
- **Madhya Pradesh:** mpedistrict.gov.in
- **Tamil Nadu:** tnedistrict.tn.gov.in
- **Other states:** Search "[your state] income certificate apply online"

### Step 2: Register / Login
Create account with mobile number and Aadhaar OTP.

### Step 3: Select "Income Certificate"
Navigate to Certificate Services → Income Certificate / Aay Praman Patra

### Step 4: Fill Application Form
- Personal details (name, father's name, DOB)
- Address (permanent and current)
- **Income details** — list all sources:
  - Salary/wages
  - Agriculture income
  - Business income
  - Rental income
  - Pension
  - Other sources
- **Total annual income** of the family

### Step 5: Upload Documents
- Aadhaar Card
- Self-declaration/affidavit
- Salary slip or ITR (if applicable)
- Ration Card
- Address proof
- Photograph

### Step 6: Pay Fee and Submit
Pay nominal fee online. Note application/reference number.

### Step 7: Verification
- Tehsildar/Revenue Inspector verifies the declared income
- May involve field enquiry for self-employed/farmers
- Cross-verification with tax records

### Step 8: Certificate Issued
- Download from portal after approval
- Physical copy available at Tehsil office

## Offline Process

1. Visit **Tehsil Office / SDM Office**
2. Collect income certificate application form
3. Fill and attach required documents + affidavit
4. Submit at the counter with fee
5. Revenue officer verifies
6. Certificate issued after approval (7-15 days)

## Processing Time

- **Online:** 7-15 working days
- **Offline:** 15-30 working days
- **Express/Tatkal (some states):** 3-7 days (additional fee)

## Validity

- **Typically:** 6 months to 1 year (varies by state)
- **For specific purposes:** Issuing authority may specify validity
- **EWS certificate:** Valid for 1 financial year
- **Always check** with the institution requesting the certificate

## Important Tips

1. **Declare income honestly** — False declaration is a criminal offence
2. **Include ALL family members' income** — Family income certificate covers the entire household
3. **Apply fresh each year** for recurring needs (scholarships, etc.)
4. **Keep affidavit on stamp paper** ready — Most states require this
5. **For EWS:** Income must be below ₹8 lakh per annum for the certificate to be useful for reservation benefits

## FAQs

**Q: Can I apply for income certificate online?**
A: Yes. Visit your state's e-District portal to apply online with document upload.

**Q: What documents are needed for income certificate?**
A: Aadhaar, self-declaration/affidavit, salary slip or ITR (if applicable), ration card, address proof, and photographs.

**Q: How long is an income certificate valid?**
A: Typically 6 months to 1 year. For EWS certificates, validity is 1 financial year. Check with the requesting institution.

**Q: What is the fee for income certificate?**
A: ₹10-₹50 in most states. Some states issue it for free.

**Q: What is the difference between income certificate and EWS certificate?**
A: Income certificate states your income regardless of amount. EWS certificate specifically certifies that your family income is below ₹8 lakh for availing 10% EWS reservation.

**Q: Can I get income certificate if I don't file ITR?**
A: Yes. A self-declaration/affidavit is sufficient. ITR filing is not mandatory for getting an income certificate, though it helps speed up verification.
