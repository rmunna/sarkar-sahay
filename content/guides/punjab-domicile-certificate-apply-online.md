# Punjab Domicile Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **domicile / residence certificate** (ਨਿਵਾਸ ਪ੍ਰਮਾਣ ਪੱਤਰ — Niwas Praman Patra) in Punjab is issued by the Revenue Department (SDM / Tehsildar) and is required for NEET Punjab state quota (85%) admissions, PPSC/PSSSB government job applications, Punjab state scholarships, and scheme eligibility. Applications go through the **Punjab e-District portal**.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [punjabedistrictservices.gov.in](https://punjabedistrictservices.gov.in) or nearest **eSewa Kendra**. Issued by SDM within **15–21 days**.

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|

| **Issuing authority** | SDM / Tehsildar |
| **Processing time** | 15–21 days |
| **Fee** | ₹20–₹30 (Punjab e-District) |
| **Validity** | Permanent once issued |
| **Portal** | punjabedistrictservices.gov.in |

## Why You Need Punjab Domicile Certificate

- **NEET Punjab state quota (85%)** — domicile mandatory for BFUHS (Baba Farid University of Health Sciences) / GMCH-32 counselling
- **PPSC (Punjab PSC)** — Punjab government jobs (preference for Punjab domicile holders)
- **PSSSB (Punjab Subordinate Services Selection Board)** — state recruitment
- **Punjab state scholarship** — Post-Matric Scholarship for Punjab resident SC/OBC/minority students
- **Dr. Ambedkar Post-Matric Scholarship** — Punjab SC students (25% SC reservation — highest in India)
- **Bhagat Puran Singh Health Insurance** — scheme eligibility

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory — with Punjab address)
- **Ration card** (Punjab ration card)
- **Voter ID** (Punjab voter enrollment)
- **Birth certificate** — showing birth in Punjab (if claiming by birth)
- **School Transfer Certificate (TC)** — from Punjab schools
- **Utility bills** — electricity/water bills at Punjab address
- **Jamabandi / Fard** — land records (for rural/agricultural family)
- Self-declaration of Punjab residency
- Passport-size photograph

## How to Apply Online — Punjab e-District Portal

1. Go to [punjabedistrictservices.gov.in](https://punjabedistrictservices.gov.in)
2. Register / log in with mobile number (OTP)
3. Navigate to **"Revenue Department"** → **"Domicile Certificate"** or **"Residence Certificate"**
4. Fill form:
   - Applicant name, father's name, date of birth
   - District, Sub-Division, Tehsil, Village/Ward
   - Duration of residence in Punjab
   - Purpose of certificate
5. Upload documents (Aadhaar, ration card, voter ID, school TC)
6. Pay fee: **₹20–₹30** (online)
7. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via eSewa Kendra / Suwidha Centre

1. Visit nearest **eSewa Kendra** or **Suwidha Centre** (Punjab government service kiosk)
2. Carry Aadhaar, ration card, voter ID, school TCs
3. Operator fills and submits on Punjab e-District
4. Service charge: approximately ₹30–60
5. Collect acknowledgement

---

## Status Check & Download

1. Go to [punjabedistrictservices.gov.in](https://punjabedistrictservices.gov.in) → **"Track Application"**
2. Enter application reference number
3. Once approved: download digitally signed PDF
4. Valid for BFUHS NEET counselling, PPSC, PSSSB applications

## NEET Punjab State Quota 2026

For **NEET 2026 Punjab state quota** (BFUHS / Punjab government medical college counselling):
1. Punjab domicile certificate — proof of Punjab residency
2. OR studied in Punjab schools for prescribed period (check BFUHS NEET notification)
3. Certificate must be SDM/Tehsildar issued — Punjab e-District digital certificate accepted
4. Apply well before NEET results — 15–21 day processing
5. BFUHS verifies during counselling document round

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Punjab residence vs domicile certificate — same document?

In Punjab, the certificate issued by the SDM/Tehsildar serves both as **domicile** and **residence** proof. It's sometimes called "Niwas Praman Patra" or "Residence Certificate" — both are the same document for NEET/scholarship purposes.

### PPSC — is Punjab domicile mandatory for all posts?

**Yes, for most Punjab state government posts** (PPSC/PSSSB), Punjab domicile is required. Check the specific PPSC advertisement — some All-India or Punjab Public Service Act-governed posts may not require domicile.

### How many years for Punjab domicile?

Punjab requires approximately **3–5 years** of continuous residence. If you have school TCs from Punjab schools + ration card + voter ID, the requirement is easily met. Students born in Punjab can use birth certificate instead.
