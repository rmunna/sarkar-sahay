# Ration Shop Change: How to Transfer to New PDS Shop

If you have shifted to a new locality or are unhappy with your current Fair Price Shop (FPS) dealer, you can request a transfer of your ration card to a different PDS shop. This guide covers the complete process — online and offline — to change your allotted ration shop.

## What is Ration Shop Change (FPS Transfer)?

Every ration card is linked to a specific Fair Price Shop (also called a ration shop or FPS) in your area. When you move to a new address or want to switch to a different shop within your jurisdiction, you need to apply for an FPS transfer. This ensures you can collect your subsidised rations from a shop convenient to you.

Under the **One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC)** scheme, you can also buy rations from any FPS across India using Aadhaar-based authentication — without formally transferring your card. However, for permanent relocation, a formal shop change is recommended.

## Who Can Apply for FPS Transfer?

- Any ration card holder who has shifted residence within the same district or state
- Families who want to switch to a nearby or better-managed FPS
- Beneficiaries facing issues like irregular supply, overcharging, or misbehaviour at their current FPS

## Documents Required

### Mandatory Documents
- **Ration card** (original and photocopy)
- **Aadhaar card** of the head of family
- **Address proof** of new residence (if relocating) — electricity bill, rent agreement, voter ID, or any government-issued document

### Supporting Documents (if applicable)
- **Application letter** addressed to the Food Inspector or Taluk Supply Officer
- **No Objection Certificate (NOC)** from the current FPS dealer (required in some states)
- **Passport-size photographs** (1-2)

## Step-by-Step Process

### Online Process (State Portal)

Most states now allow FPS transfer through their online PDS portals. The general process is:

1. **Visit your state's PDS portal** (e.g., epds.nic.in for central, or state-specific portals like rcms.mahafood.gov.in for Maharashtra, nfs.delhi.gov.in for Delhi)
2. **Log in** using your ration card number, Aadhaar number, or registered mobile number
3. **Navigate to "Services"** or "Ration Card Modification" section
4. **Select "Change FPS / Shop Transfer"** option
5. **Enter details** of your current FPS and the new FPS you wish to transfer to
6. **Upload documents** — address proof, Aadhaar copy, application letter
7. **Submit the application** and note down the application/reference number
8. **Track status** online using the reference number

### Offline Process

1. **Write an application** addressed to the Food Inspector or Taluk Supply Officer of your area requesting FPS transfer
2. **Mention details** — your ration card number, current FPS name and code, new FPS name and code, reason for transfer
3. **Attach photocopies** of ration card, Aadhaar card, and address proof
4. **Submit the application** at the local Food & Civil Supplies office or Taluk Supply Office
5. **Collect acknowledgement receipt** with reference number
6. **Follow up** after 15-30 days if no update is received

### Using ONORC for Temporary Access

If you don't want a permanent transfer but need rations from a different shop:

1. Visit **any FPS across India** with your Aadhaar card
2. Authenticate using **biometric (fingerprint)** at the electronic Point of Sale (ePoS) machine
3. Collect your entitled rations — the subsidy follows you across states
4. No application or paperwork needed for ONORC

> **Note:** ONORC works only if your Aadhaar is seeded (linked) to your ration card. See our [Ration Card eKYC guide](/guide/ration-card-ekyc-online) for Aadhaar linking steps.

## Important Tips

1. **Always get acknowledgement** — whether applying online or offline, keep the reference number safe for tracking
2. **Check FPS capacity** — the new shop must have capacity to add more beneficiaries; if the shop is overcrowded, your request may be redirected
3. **ONORC is your backup** — even while your transfer is being processed, use ONORC to collect rations from any shop using Aadhaar biometric
4. **Update address first** — if you've moved, update your ration card address before or along with the FPS transfer. See our [Ration Card Address Change guide](/guide/ration-card-address-change)
5. **Escalate if delayed** — if your application is pending beyond 30 days, file a complaint on the state PDS portal or call the **1967 PDS helpline**

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Can I change my ration shop without changing my address?
**A:** Yes. You can request an FPS transfer to a different shop within your area even without moving. You need to submit an application with a valid reason (inconvenience, poor service, etc.).

### Q2: Is NOC from current FPS dealer mandatory?
**A:** In most states, NOC is not mandatory. However, some states like Uttar Pradesh and Rajasthan may require it. If the dealer refuses to give NOC, mention this in your application — the food inspector can process the transfer without it.

### Q3: Can I buy rations from a different shop without transferring?
**A:** Yes, under the ONORC scheme, you can buy rations from any FPS across India using Aadhaar biometric authentication. No formal transfer is needed for this.

### Q4: How do I find the FPS code of my new shop?
**A:** Visit your state PDS portal and search for FPS by area/pin code. You can also ask at the new FPS or contact the local Food & Civil Supplies office.

### Q5: What if my FPS transfer request is rejected?
**A:** Rejection usually happens if the new FPS is overcrowded or the documents are incomplete. Resubmit with correct documents, or file a grievance on the state portal. You can also call the **1967 helpline** for assistance.

### Q6: How long does ONORC portability take to activate?
**A:** ONORC is available immediately once your Aadhaar is linked (seeded) to your ration card. There is no separate activation process.

### Q7: Can I transfer my ration card to a shop in another state?
**A:** For inter-state permanent transfer, you need to apply for ration card transfer to the new state. See our [Ration Card Transfer to Another State guide](/guide/ration-card-transfer-another-state). For temporary access, use ONORC.
