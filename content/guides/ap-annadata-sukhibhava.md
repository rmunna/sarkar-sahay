# Annadata Sukhibhava – Free Crop Insurance for AP Farmers

Annadata Sukhibhava is a crop insurance scheme by the Government of Andhra Pradesh that provides free crop insurance to farmers. Under this scheme, the state government pays the entire farmer's share of the crop insurance premium, ensuring that farmers are protected against crop losses due to natural calamities, pests, and diseases without any financial burden.

## What is Annadata Sukhibhava?

The scheme replaces/supplements the central Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) framework in AP. The government bears the farmer's premium share so that all enrolled farmers get insurance coverage at zero cost. If crops are damaged due to drought, floods, cyclones, pest attacks, or other notified calamities, farmers receive compensation directly in their bank accounts.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Farmers of AP:** All farmers (landowners and tenant farmers) cultivating notified crops in Andhra Pradesh
2. **Loanee farmers:** Farmers who have taken crop loans from banks are automatically enrolled
3. **Non-loanee farmers:** Can voluntarily enroll through the Village/Ward Secretariat or nearest bank
4. **Crops covered:** All notified Kharif and Rabi crops as per the seasonal notification
5. **No income limit:** The scheme is available to all farmers regardless of income or landholding size

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar card (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- Land records / Pattadar passbook / land ownership documents
- Bank account passbook (Aadhaar-linked)
- Crop sowing certificate from Village Revenue Officer (VRO)
- Loan account details (for loanee farmers)
- Tenant farmer certificate (for tenant/sharecropper farmers)
- Passport-size photograph

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### For Loanee Farmers (Automatic)

1. **Automatic enrollment:** If you have taken a crop loan from a bank, you are automatically enrolled under the scheme
2. **Premium paid by government:** The farmer's share of premium is paid by the AP government — no deduction from your account
3. **Verify enrollment:** Check with your bank or at the Village Secretariat to confirm enrollment

### For Non-Loanee Farmers

1. **Visit Village/Ward Secretariat:** Approach the Agriculture Assistant at your nearest Secretariat
2. **Submit details:** Provide Aadhaar, land records, crop details, and bank account information
3. **Or visit bank branch:** Submit crop insurance enrollment form at your nearest bank
4. **Verification:** The Agriculture department verifies land and crop details
5. **Enrollment confirmed:** You will receive confirmation of enrollment via SMS

### Claiming Insurance

1. **Report crop loss:** Inform the Village Secretariat or call the toll-free helpline within 72 hours of crop damage
2. **Field inspection:** Insurance company and agriculture department officials conduct field-level assessment
3. **Claim processing:** Based on crop-cutting experiments and damage assessment, claims are processed
4. **Compensation credited:** Insurance compensation is directly credited to your Aadhaar-linked bank account

## Coverage Details

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Premium cost to farmer | ₹0 (fully paid by government) |
| Risks covered | Drought, flood, cyclone, pest attack, hailstorm, landslide |
| Coverage period | Sowing to post-harvest (including prevented sowing) |
| Claim settlement | Based on area-approach and individual assessment for localized calamities |

## Processing Time

- **Enrollment window:** Before sowing season cutoff dates (as notified)
- **Claim reporting:** Within 72 hours of crop damage
- **Claim settlement:** 45–90 days after assessment completion

## Important Tips

1. **Enroll before cutoff:** Non-loanee farmers must enroll before the seasonal cutoff date — check dates at your Secretariat
2. **Report losses immediately:** Report crop damage within 72 hours to be eligible for claims
3. **Keep land records updated:** Ensure your Pattadar passbook and land records are current
4. **Tenant farmers are covered:** Even if you don't own the land, you can enroll with a tenant certificate
5. **No premium to pay:** Do not pay any amount to middlemen — the insurance is completely free for farmers

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Do farmers need to pay any premium?
No. Under Annadata Sukhibhava, the entire farmer's premium share is borne by the AP government. Farmers get free crop insurance.

### Q2: Which crops are covered?
All notified Kharif and Rabi crops in your area are covered. Check the seasonal notification at your Village Secretariat for the specific crop list.

### Q3: How is compensation calculated?
Compensation is calculated based on crop-cutting experiments (area approach) and individual assessment for localized calamities like hailstorms.

### Q4: Are tenant farmers eligible?
Yes, tenant farmers can enroll by providing a tenant farmer certificate from the concerned authority.

### Q5: What if I miss the enrollment deadline?
You will not be covered for that season. Enrollment must be completed before the notified cutoff date each season.

### Q6: Can I get insurance for multiple crops?
Yes, you can enroll for all notified crops you are cultivating in a given season.

### Q7: Where do I report crop damage?
Report crop damage at your Village/Ward Secretariat, through the agriculture helpline, or via the crop insurance app.
