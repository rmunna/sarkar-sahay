## What is Maharashtra Saur Krishi Pump Yojana?

**Mukhyamantri Saur Krishi Pump Yojana** is Maharashtra's solar irrigation pump scheme that provides **3HP and 5HP solar pumps at 95% government subsidy** to farmers — especially those without electricity connections in remote areas. Implemented through MSEDCL (Mahadiscom) under PM KUSUM (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Urja Suraksha evam Utthaan Mahabhiyan).

---

## Key Benefits

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Subsidy | 95% (for SC/ST farmers); 90% (general farmers) |
| Pump capacity | 3HP (AC/DC), 5HP, 7.5HP |
| Eligible farmers | Priority to farmers without electricity connection |
| Complete system | Solar panels + pump + motor + controller |
| Maintenance | 5-year comprehensive maintenance by vendor |
| Portal | mahadiscom.in / agrimahascheme.gov.in |

---

## Farmer Contribution

| Category | Farmer Pays |
|----------|------------|
| SC/ST farmers | 5% of total cost |
| General small/marginal farmers | 10% of total cost |
| General large farmers | Varies (may be higher) |

For a 3HP solar pump with approximate total cost ₹2.5 lakh, SC/ST farmer pays only ~₹12,500.

---

## Who is Eligible?

- **Maharashtra farmer** with agricultural land
- **Priority 1:** Farmers who are pending electricity connection waitlisted for 5+ years
- **Priority 2:** Farmers with land on a canal command area
- **Priority 3:** Farmers with no electricity pump / dependent on diesel
- No electricity pending application required for SC/ST in remote areas

---

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card**
2. **7/12 extract** (satbara) — land ownership and area
3. **8A extract** — khata proof
4. **Caste certificate** (for SC/ST)
5. **Bank passbook** — Aadhaar-linked
6. **Photograph of water source** (borewell, open well, canal)

## How to Apply

### Step 1 — Online Application

Visit **[mahadiscom.in](https://mahadiscom.in)** → "Saur Krishi Pump" or the state portal:
- Register with Aadhaar
- Fill farm and water source details
- Upload 7/12, 8A, Aadhaar, bank proof
- Submit

Alternatively, apply at the **MSEDCL Sub-Division Office** or **Agriculture Office** in your tehsil.

### Step 2 — Field Survey

MSEDCL engineer visits your field to:
- Verify land and water source
- Assess pump capacity requirement
- Confirm solar panel mounting space

### Step 3 — Pay Farmer Share

After survey approval:
- Pay your 5–10% share (online or at MSEDCL office)
- Receipt issued

### Step 4 — Installation

Empanelled vendor installs the complete solar pump system within 60–90 days of payment.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. I applied for electricity connection 8 years ago and it never came. Am I priority 1?**

Yes. Farmers with pending electricity applications are given top priority. Bring your electricity connection application number to the MSEDCL office to verify your priority status.

**2. Can I use the solar pump for drip irrigation?**

Yes. Solar pump output can be connected to drip or sprinkler irrigation systems. This combination maximizes water and energy efficiency. Many farmers combine solar pump with drip irrigation subsidy (PM KUSUM + drip subsidy under PMKSY-PDMC).

**3. What if the solar pump breaks down after 2 years? Who repairs it?**

The installing vendor provides 5-year comprehensive maintenance under the scheme. Report breakdown to MSEDCL or the vendor — it is repaired at no extra cost within the warranty period.

**4. The water level in my well drops in summer. Will the solar pump still work?**

The pump motor switches off automatically if water level drops below the pump inlet (dry-run protection is standard). The pump will restart when water level rises. This protects the motor from damage.

**5. Can I sell excess solar energy back to the grid?**

This scheme is for a standalone off-grid pump — it does not connect to the grid for energy export. To sell solar power to the grid, the PM KUSUM Component A (solar feeder/plant) is different. This scheme is purely for irrigation pumping.
