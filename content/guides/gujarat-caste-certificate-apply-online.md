# Gujarat Caste Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **caste certificate** (જ્ઞાતિ પ્રમાણ પત્ર — Jnati Praman Patra) in Gujarat is issued by the Revenue Department (Mamlatdar) and is required for OBC/SEBC/SC/ST reservations, post-matric scholarships (e-Samaj Kalyan), NEET counselling, and government job applications. Applications go through **Digital Gujarat** — the state's integrated services portal.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [digitalgujarat.gov.in](https://digitalgujarat.gov.in) or **Antyodaya Seva Kendra / Seva Setu camps**. Issued by Mamlatdar within **15–30 days**.

---

## Caste Categories in Gujarat

| Category | Reservation % | Notes |
|----------|---------------|-------|
| **SC** | 7% | Scheduled Castes — state + central list |
| **ST** | 14% | Scheduled Tribes — Gujarat tribal communities |
| **OBC (SEBC)** | 27% | Other Backward Classes (called SEBC in Gujarat) |
| **EWS** | 10% | Economically Weaker Section — general category |

> In Gujarat, OBC is often referred to as **SEBC (Socially and Educationally Backward Classes)**. The SEBC reservation was a major political issue (Patidar quota movement). Current SEBC reservations include many communities per Gujarat government orders.

## Why You Need Gujarat Caste Certificate

- **Post-matric scholarship** via **e-Samaj Kalyan** (esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in) — mandatory
- **NEET state quota counselling** — community certificate required
- **ACPC / GUJCET engineering admissions** — reserved category proof
- **Government job reservation** — GPSC, GSSSB, district recruitment
- **Central OBC NCL certification** — for central jobs/UPSC
- **Vanvasi Kalyan / SC/ST welfare scheme eligibility**

---

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory)
- **Ration card** (shows family caste/community)
- **Father's school leaving certificate** (TC/SSLC showing caste)
- **Father's / grandfather's caste proof** (old voter ID, service record with caste)
- Self-declaration affidavit of caste
- **Voter ID** (address proof)
- Passport-size photograph

---

## How to Apply Online — Digital Gujarat Portal

1. Go to [digitalgujarat.gov.in](https://digitalgujarat.gov.in)
2. Register / log in with Aadhaar-linked mobile number
3. Click **"Revenue Department"** → **"Caste Certificate"**
4. Fill form:
   - Applicant name, address (district, taluka, village)
   - Community/caste declaration
   - Father's community details
5. Upload documents (Aadhaar, father's school TC, ration card)
6. Pay fee: **₹20–30** (online)
7. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via Antyodaya Seva Kendra / Seva Setu

1. Visit nearest **Antyodaya Seva Kendra** (jan seva kendra) or **Seva Setu camp**
2. Carry Aadhaar, ration card, father's TC / caste proof
3. Operator submits application on Digital Gujarat portal
4. Service charge: approximately ₹30–50
5. Collect acknowledgement receipt

## e-Samaj Kalyan — Scholarship Link

For **SC/ST/OBC post-matric scholarships in Gujarat**, the caste certificate must be uploaded on [esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in). Ensure:
- Certificate is digitally signed
- Issued within the current academic/financial year
- Community matches the scholarship category

---

## Status Check & Download

1. Log in to [digitalgujarat.gov.in](https://digitalgujarat.gov.in)
2. Click **"My Applications"** → view caste certificate status
3. Once approved: download digitally signed PDF
4. PDF is valid for scholarships, ACPC admissions, and government jobs

## OBC NCL (Non-Creamy Layer) Certificate — Gujarat

For **central government OBC reservation** (UPSC, SSC, railways), you need a separate **OBC NCL certificate**:
- Income < ₹8 lakh/year from non-agricultural sources
- Apply at Digital Gujarat under "OBC NCL Certificate"
- Issued by Mamlatdar / SDM
- Valid for **1 year**

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is SEBC in Gujarat — same as OBC?

**SEBC (Socially and Educationally Backward Classes)** is the Gujarat-specific term for OBC. For state government jobs and admissions, use the Gujarat SEBC/OBC certificate. For central jobs, you need the central OBC NCL certificate — they are different documents, both applied at Digital Gujarat.

### My community is in the SEBC list but not in central OBC list — what to do?

Some communities are on Gujarat's SEBC list but not on the central OBC list. For **Gujarat state jobs**: SEBC certificate applies. For **central government jobs/UPSC**: check central OBC list — if not listed, you don't get central OBC reservation even with a state certificate.

### Caste certificate for ACPC / GUJCET admissions in Gujarat?

ACPC (Admission Committee for Professional Courses) requires the caste certificate uploaded on the ACPC portal. The certificate must be issued by Mamlatdar and must be from the **current academic year** for admission purposes.
