## What is YSR Pension Kanuka?

**YSR Pension Kanuka** is Andhra Pradesh's social security pension scheme that delivers monthly pensions **directly to beneficiaries' homes** through the Village/Ward Secretariat system. It covers multiple categories of vulnerable citizens.

"Kanuka" means "gift" — the pension is delivered as a gift to beneficiaries at their doorstep by the Village Volunteer/Secretariat.

---

## Categories and Pension Amounts

| Category | Monthly Pension |
|----------|---------------|
| Old Age (60+ years) | ₹3,000/month |
| Widows and single women | ₹3,000/month |
| Persons with Disability (40%+) | ₹3,000/month |
| Toddy tappers | ₹3,000/month |
| Weavers (EBC) | ₹3,000/month |
| Fishermen (EBC) | ₹3,000/month |
| Dappu artists | ₹3,000/month |
| Christian converts (SC) | ₹3,000/month |
| HIV+ persons | ₹3,000/month |
| Transgenders | ₹3,500/month |

## Who is Eligible?

**General criteria:**
- **Permanent resident of Andhra Pradesh** (minimum 3 years)
- **Not a government employee or pensioner**
- **Not an income tax payer**
- Annual family income within specified limits (typically ≤ ₹10,000/month for most categories)

**Age/category requirements:**
- Old Age: 60+ years
- Widow: Any age after husband's death
- Disability: 40%+ disability (certified)

---

## How the Pension is Delivered — Village Secretariat System

Unlike most states where beneficiaries collect pension from bank/CSC, AP's YSR Pension Kanuka is delivered **at home**:

1. Village/Ward Secretariat maintains the beneficiary database
2. Village Volunteer visits the beneficiary's home on the pension distribution day
3. Pension is paid **in cash** or via **mobile banking** through the volunteer's banking device

This ensures even bedridden elderly persons receive their pension without going anywhere.

## How to Apply

### Step 1 — Visit Village/Ward Secretariat

Go to your **Village Secretariat** (rural) or **Ward Secretariat** (urban). Tell the Sachivalayam employee that you want to apply for YSR Pension.

### Step 2 — Submit Documents

At the secretariat, provide:
- Aadhaar Card
- Category certificate (old age: age proof; widow: death certificate; disability: certificate)
- Income certificate
- Bank passbook (optional if payment is cash)

### Step 3 — Application Submission

The Secretariat employee enters your details in the AP Government system and submits the application.

### Step 4 — Verification by Volunteer

The Village Volunteer verifies your eligibility through a home visit.

### Step 5 — Pension Starts

After approval and inclusion in the beneficiary list, pension is delivered monthly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. I didn't receive my pension last month. What do I do?**

Contact your Village Volunteer immediately. Also contact the Village Secretariat. The volunteer is responsible for pension delivery — track through them first.

**2. My mother is bedridden. Will she still get the pension?**

Yes. The entire purpose of the door delivery model is to serve bedridden and mobility-impaired beneficiaries. The Village Volunteer specifically visits their homes.

**3. Is pension available to all elderly or only BPL?**

The scheme targets economically backward individuals. An income screen is done — not strictly BPL card. Contact your Village Secretariat for the current income criteria.

**4. I am a transgender person. What amount do I get?**

Transgenders receive ₹3,500/month — the highest amount in the YSR Pension Kanuka scheme.

**5. Can I get YSR Pension and PM-JAY health insurance at the same time?**

Yes. YSR Pension and PM-JAY / YSR Aarogyasri health insurance are separate benefits — you can receive both simultaneously.
