# Dormant Bank Account Reactivation — All Banks 2026

A bank account becomes **dormant (inoperative)** when there is no customer-initiated transaction for **24 consecutive months**. The account isn't closed — but ATM, internet banking, UPI, and cheque payments stop working until you reactivate it.

This guide covers how to reactivate a dormant account at every major Indian bank, what documents you need, and how long it takes.

> **RBI Rule:** Banks must notify customers before marking an account dormant. Interest on savings accounts continues even in dormant status.

---

## Why Does an Account Go Dormant?

| Condition | RBI Rule |
|-----------|---------|
| No customer-initiated transaction for **24 months** | Account classified as "Inoperative" |
| No transaction for **10 years** | Account transferred to RBI Depositor Education and Awareness Fund (DEAF) |
| Automatic system-triggered credits (interest, ECS) | Do NOT count as customer-initiated transactions |
| ATM withdrawal, UPI, NEFT, IMPS, cheque | DOES count — resets the 24-month clock |

---

## What Stops Working in a Dormant Account

- ❌ ATM/Debit card transactions
- ❌ Internet banking / mobile banking transfers
- ❌ UPI payments
- ❌ Cheque payments
- ✅ Interest crediting continues
- ✅ ECS credits (pension, salary) continue
- ✅ Balance viewable (read-only access may work)

## Documents Required (All Banks)

| Document | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Aadhaar (original + copy) | Identity + KYC |
| PAN Card (original + copy) | Mandatory for accounts with any transaction |
| Recent passport photo (2 copies) | Photo update |
| Passbook (original) | Account verification |
| KYC Form / Account Reactivation Form | Bank's own form |

Some banks may also ask for:
- Latest address proof (utility bill, rent agreement) if address changed
- Mobile number update form if number changed

## SBI Dormant Account Reactivation

**Process:** Branch visit mandatory (SBI does not allow dormant reactivation online/digitally)

**Steps:**
1. Visit your **home branch** (where account was opened)
2. Carry: Aadhaar, PAN, passbook, 2 passport photos
3. Fill **"Request for Activation of Inoperative Account"** form
4. Submit with self-attested KYC documents
5. Do **one transaction** (even ₹1 cash deposit)
6. Account reactivated within **same day to 1 working day**

**Tip:** If account has been inoperative for 10+ years, it may have been transferred to RBI DEAF fund. File a separate DEAF claim at SBI branch.

**Customer Care:** 1800-11-2211

## HDFC Bank Dormant Account Reactivation

**Via Branch (Recommended):**
1. Visit nearest HDFC branch (not necessarily home branch)
2. Fill **"KYC / Account Reactivation Form"**
3. Submit Aadhaar + PAN + photo
4. Do a cash deposit or withdrawal (₹1 minimum)
5. Account reactivated: **same day**

**Via Netbanking/App (Partial — for KYC completion only):**
- HDFC allows KYC update via Video KYC for accounts where only KYC is pending
- For true dormant reactivation with transaction, branch visit is required

**Customer Care:** 1800-202-6161

## Bank Account Transferred to RBI DEAF Fund — How to Recover

If the account has had no activity for **10 years**, the balance is transferred to RBI's **Depositor Education and Awareness Fund (DEAF)**. You can still claim it:

1. Visit your bank branch
2. Bank verifies your identity + account history
3. Bank files DEAF claim with RBI on your behalf
4. Money credited back to your account in **3–6 weeks**
5. No penalty — you get the full amount with applicable interest

**Check unclaimed deposits:** [rbi.org.in/uddesh.aspx](https://rbi.org.in/uddesh.aspx) — RBI UDGAM portal lets you search unclaimed deposits across all banks.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does my dormant account lose money?**
No — balance is preserved and interest continues to accrue. Only transaction capabilities are suspended.

**Can I reactivate a dormant account from another city?**
For public sector banks (SBI, PNB, Union Bank), you typically need to visit the home branch. Private banks (HDFC, ICICI, Axis) often allow any branch or digital reactivation.

**What if my mobile number or address changed since I opened the account?**
Update both when you visit the branch for reactivation. Bring new address proof and your new mobile number.

**Will my debit card work again after reactivation?**
Yes — once the account is reactivated, the debit card usually works within 24 hours. If not, call customer care to activate the card separately.

**My account shows ₹0 balance but it was dormant, not closed — where did the money go?**
Check if the bank deducted non-maintenance charges (which some banks do even on dormant accounts). If the balance was above ₹0 and account has been dormant 10+ years, it may have gone to RBI DEAF fund. File a DEAF claim at branch.

**Can I avoid my account going dormant?**
Yes — make at least one customer-initiated transaction every year: ATM withdrawal, UPI payment, NEFT transfer, or even a small cash deposit. Calendar reminder every 6 months is good practice.
