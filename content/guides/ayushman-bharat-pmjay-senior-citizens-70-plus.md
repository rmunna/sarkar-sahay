# Ayushman Bharat for Senior Citizens 70+ — Free ₹5 Lakh Health Cover

From **October 29, 2024**, the central government extended **Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY** coverage to all senior citizens aged **70 years and above** — with **no income limit**. Whether you are rich, middle class, or poor, if you are 70+, you are entitled to a ₹5 lakh annual health cover under a new card called the **Ayushman Vay Vandana Card**.

> This is one of India's largest healthcare expansions — covering an estimated **6 crore senior citizens** who previously fell outside the SECC-based eligibility of regular Ayushman Bharat.

## Key Features at a Glance

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Who is eligible** | ALL senior citizens aged 70 years or above |
| **Income limit** | None — open to all income levels |
| **Coverage per year** | ₹5 lakh per senior citizen (separate from family coverage) |
| **Card name** | **Ayushman Vay Vandana Card** |
| **Existing private insurance** | Can have both — these are separate entitlements |
| **Existing Ayushman card** | If already PMJAY beneficiary, get an additional ₹5L top-up |
| **Effective from** | October 29, 2024 |
| **Hospitals accepted** | All empanelled AB-PMJAY hospitals across India |

## Who Qualifies?

**Broad rule**: Anyone aged **70 years or above** as of the date of enrollment.

**Specific categories**:
1. **Not currently under AB-PMJAY**: Senior citizens 70+ from any economic background — middle class, upper class — who weren't covered before. Now get full ₹5L coverage.
2. **Already under AB-PMJAY** (existing beneficiaries): Get an **additional** ₹5L top-up per senior member, separate from the family's ₹5L cover.
3. **Covered under private/employer health insurance**: Still entitled to the Vay Vandana Card. Both the private insurance and AB-PMJAY 70+ run in parallel.
4. **Central/State government pensioners** (covered by CGHS/ECHS/state schemes): **Excluded** — government employees with their own health schemes are not eligible.

> **Exclusion**: Senior citizens covered under Central Government Health Scheme (CGHS), Ex-Servicemen Contributory Health Scheme (ECHS), or equivalent state government employee health schemes are NOT eligible for this scheme.

## How to Apply for Ayushman Vay Vandana Card

### Method 1: Online at beneficiary.nha.gov.in

1. Visit **[beneficiary.nha.gov.in](https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in/)**
2. Click **"Check Eligibility"** or **"New 70+ Registration"**
3. Enter Aadhaar number of the senior citizen
4. Enter OTP received on Aadhaar-linked mobile
5. Verify eligibility — if 70+ and not in excluded category, eligible
6. **Download or print the Ayushman Vay Vandana Card** (PDF)
7. The card can be used at all AB-PMJAY empanelled hospitals

### Method 2: At Nearest Government Hospital (Ayushman Mitra)

1. Visit any **government or empanelled private hospital** with an Ayushman Mitra desk
2. Carry the senior citizen's **Aadhaar card**
3. The Ayushman Mitra will verify eligibility on the NHA portal and generate the card on the spot
4. The card is typically printed and given immediately

### Method 3: Via UMANG App or Common Service Centre (CSC)

1. Open **UMANG App** → search "Ayushman Bharat" → 70+ registration
2. Or visit nearest **CSC (Jan Seva Kendra)** with Aadhaar for assisted enrollment

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** of the senior citizen (must be 70+ years; age verified via Aadhaar)
- **Mobile number linked to Aadhaar** (for OTP verification)
- No income certificate needed
- No BPL card needed

## Relationship to Regular AB-PMJAY Family Card

| Scenario | How it Works |
|----------|-------------|
| Family already has Ayushman card | Senior 70+ gets **extra ₹5L** on top of family's ₹5L (total ₹10L for the family if one member is 70+) |
| No Ayushman card in family | Senior 70+ gets new card with ₹5L; rest of family (if SECC eligible) can still apply separately |
| Senior is sole member | Gets ₹5L individual cover |

The **Vay Vandana Card is separate** from the regular AB-PMJAY family card. It tracks usage independently.

## Which Hospitals Accept Vay Vandana Card?

All hospitals empanelled under AB-PMJAY accept the Vay Vandana Card. To find empanelled hospitals near you:
1. Visit **[pmjay.gov.in](https://pmjay.gov.in/)** → Hospital Finder
2. Enter your city/district/pincode
3. Filter by specialty (cardiac, ortho, oncology, etc.)

## Common Questions

### Can I get Ayushman Vay Vandana Card if I have a private health insurance policy?

**Yes**. Private insurance and AB-PMJAY 70+ are separate entitlements. You can use whichever is more convenient for a given treatment. They do not subtract from each other.

### Does the ₹5L cover reset annually?

Yes — ₹5 lakh per year. Unused coverage does not roll over.

### What if I turn 70 during the year?

Apply as soon as you turn 70. Coverage starts from enrollment date, not from birthday.

### I'm 70+ but a retired government employee. Am I eligible?

If you receive benefits under CGHS, ECHS, or equivalent state government health scheme, you are **not eligible** for this new scheme. If your government health coverage lapsed or you opted out, you may be eligible — verify at beneficiary.nha.gov.in.

### Can the card be used for pre-existing conditions?

Yes. Unlike most private insurance, AB-PMJAY covers **pre-existing conditions from day one**. There's no waiting period.

### My Aadhaar has wrong age. Can I still apply?

If Aadhaar shows you as under 70 due to data entry error, your application will fail. First update your Aadhaar date of birth at a nearby Aadhaar Seva Kendra using birth certificate or school leaving certificate.

## List of Covered Treatments

AB-PMJAY covers **1,961 treatment packages** across all major specialties:
- Cardiac surgeries (heart bypass, stents)
- Cancer treatment (chemotherapy, surgery)
- Kidney/liver transplants
- Orthopedic surgeries (knee/hip replacement)
- Neurological conditions
- Dialysis
- All major surgeries and treatments

For the complete list, visit pmjay.gov.in → Coverage → Package List.
