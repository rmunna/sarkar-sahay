## What is the Haryana Ladli Scheme?

The Haryana Ladli scheme is a state government initiative to promote the welfare of the girl child and combat the declining sex ratio. Under this scheme, the government provides financial assistance of **₹5,000 per year** for 5 years to families where a second girl child is born. The total benefit accumulates to **₹25,000** plus interest, which the family can withdraw when the girls turn 18.

The scheme specifically targets the second daughter to discourage families from viewing additional girl children as a financial burden.

## Who is Eligible?

### Eligibility Criteria
- The scheme is for the **second girl child** born in the family
- Both the first and second child must be **girls**
- The family must be a **resident of Haryana**
- No income limit for eligibility
- Applicable to all castes and religions
- The second daughter must be born on or after a notified date

### Important Conditions
- Both daughters must survive for the family to continue receiving benefits
- The family must get both daughters vaccinated as per the immunization schedule
- The family must ensure both daughters are enrolled in school

## Benefits

| Year | Annual Deposit | Cumulative Total |
|------|---------------|-----------------|
| 1st Year | ₹5,000 | ₹5,000 |
| 2nd Year | ₹5,000 | ₹10,000 |
| 3rd Year | ₹5,000 | ₹15,000 |
| 4th Year | ₹5,000 | ₹20,000 |
| 5th Year | ₹5,000 | ₹25,000 |

- **Total deposit:** ₹25,000 over 5 years
- **Interest:** Accumulated at prevailing rates
- **Maturity:** When both girls turn 18 years of age
- **Estimated maturity amount:** ₹40,000-50,000 (including interest, depending on when started)

The amount is deposited in a **Kisan Vikas Patra (KVP)** in the name of the mother. If the mother is not alive, it is deposited in the father's name.

## Documents Required

- **Birth certificate** of the second girl child
- **Birth certificate** of the first girl child
- **Aadhaar card** of both parents
- **Family ID (PPP ID)** or residence proof
- **Bank account details** of the mother
- **Immunization card** of both daughters
- **BPL certificate** (if applicable, for priority processing)
- **Photograph** of the family

## How to Apply

### Application Process
1. Visit the nearest **Anganwadi Centre** after the birth of the second daughter
2. Collect the Ladli scheme application form from the Anganwadi worker
3. Fill in details of both daughters and parents
4. Attach all required documents
5. Submit the form through the Anganwadi worker
6. The Anganwadi worker forwards it to the **CDPO (Child Development Project Officer)**
7. After verification, the form reaches the **District Women & Child Development Office**
8. Upon approval, the first KVP is purchased in the mother's name

### Through Online Portal
1. Visit [socialjusticehry.gov.in](https://socialjusticehry.gov.in) or the WCD portal
2. Register and navigate to the Ladli scheme section
3. Fill the application form online
4. Upload all required documents
5. Submit and track using the reference number

### Timeline
- Apply **within 1 year** of the second daughter's birth
- Late applications may be considered on case-by-case basis
- KVP deposits continue for 5 years from the date of approval

## How to Claim the Maturity Amount

1. When both daughters turn 18 years of age
2. Visit the District Women & Child Development Office
3. Carry original KVPs, Aadhaar of daughters and mother, and bank passbook
4. Fill the claim form
5. The matured amount is transferred to the mother's bank account
6. Both daughters must be alive and should have been enrolled in school

## Important Tips

- **Register the birth immediately** — birth certificate is mandatory; register at the hospital or municipal office within 21 days
- **Immunization is compulsory** — keep the immunization card updated for both daughters
- **School enrollment** — ensure both girls are enrolled in school; this is a continuing condition
- **KVP is in mother's name** — the deposit is specifically for the mother to empower women
- **Don't delay the application** — apply within 1 year of the second daughter's birth to avoid complications

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What if the first child is a boy and the second is a girl?
The scheme specifically applies when both the first AND second children are girls. If the first child is a boy and the second is a girl, this scheme is not applicable.

### Is this scheme only for BPL families?
No, the Ladli scheme is available to all Haryana families regardless of income. There is no income limit for eligibility.

### What if one of the daughters passes away?
If either daughter passes away during the scheme period, the benefits stop. The already deposited KVPs remain and can be claimed at maturity.

### Can the money be withdrawn before both daughters turn 18?
No, the KVPs mature only when both daughters turn 18. Early withdrawal is not permitted under the scheme guidelines.

### Is this different from Ladli Laxmi Yojana?
Yes, Ladli Laxmi Yojana is a Madhya Pradesh scheme with different benefits and eligibility. Haryana's Ladli scheme is specific to the state and focuses on the second girl child.

### What if I already have two sons and then a daughter?
The scheme is for families where the first two children are both girls. If you already have sons, the scheme does not apply to subsequent daughters.
