# Bihar Daroga (SI) Exam Guide — Eligibility, Pattern & Preparation Tips

The Bihar Police Sub-ordinate Services Commission (BPSSC) conducts the Bihar Daroga (Sub-Inspector) exam for recruitment to police and other law enforcement positions. "Daroga" is the colloquial term for Sub-Inspector in Bihar. This is one of the most popular government exams in the state.

The Bihar Daroga exam covers posts in Bihar Police, Bihar Military Police, and Special Branch.

## Eligibility Criteria

### Age Limit

| Category | Minimum Age | Maximum Age |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| General (Male) | 20 years | 25 years |
| General (Female) | 20 years | 25 years |
| OBC | 20 years | 27 years |
| SC/ST | 20 years | 30 years |

### Educational Qualification

- **Minimum:** Bachelor's degree from a recognized university
- Any stream accepted

### Physical Standards

**Male:** Height 165 cm (Gen/OBC/SC), 160 cm (ST); Chest 79/84 cm
**Female:** Height 155 cm (Gen), 150 cm (ST)

## Exam Pattern

### Stage 1: Prelims (Written)

| Subject | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| General Knowledge | 100 | 200 | 2 hours |

- Negative marking: 0.2 per wrong answer

### Stage 2: Mains (Written)

| Paper | Subject | Marks | Duration |
|-------|---------|-------|----------|
| Paper 1 | General Hindi | 200 | 2 hours |
| Paper 2 | General Studies | 200 | 2 hours |

### Stage 3: Physical Efficiency Test

**Male:** 1.6 km in 6 min, Long Jump 12 ft, High Jump 4 ft
**Female:** 1 km in 5 min, Long Jump 9 ft, High Jump 3 ft

## Syllabus Overview

### General Knowledge
- History, Geography, Polity, Science, Economy, Current Affairs, Bihar GK

### General Hindi
- Grammar, Essay, Translation, Comprehension, Letter writing

### General Studies (Mains)
- Constitution, Law & Order, Current Events, Bihar-specific topics

## How to Apply

1. Visit BPSSC at [bpssc.bih.nic.in](https://bpssc.bih.nic.in)
2. Find the Daroga recruitment notification
3. Register and fill the application form
4. Upload documents
5. Pay fee and submit

| Category | Fee |

| SC/ST/OBC | ₹175 |

## Salary & Benefits

- **Pay Scale:** ₹35,400 – ₹1,12,400
- Grade Pay: ₹4,200
- All government benefits including DA, HRA, pension

## Preparation Tips

1. **Bihar-centric GK:** Focus heavily on Bihar history, culture, and schemes
2. **Hindi writing skills:** Mains has descriptive Hindi — practice essays and translations
3. **Physical preparation:** Start running and fitness training 6 months before exam
4. **Previous papers:** BPSSC Daroga papers follow a pattern — study them
5. **Current affairs:** National + Bihar state affairs are crucial

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Bihar Daroga the same as Bihar SI?
Yes, "Daroga" is the local term for Sub-Inspector in Bihar.

### What is the selection process?
Prelims → Mains → PET → Document Verification → Medical Exam.

### Is there an interview?
No, there is no interview round.

### Can women apply for Bihar Daroga?
Yes, women are eligible with relaxed physical standards.

### What is the difference between Bihar SI and Bihar Daroga exams?
They are the same exam — BPSSC conducts the Daroga/SI recruitment as one process.

### How many vacancies are usually released?
It varies — recent cycles have had 2,000-3,000+ vacancies.
