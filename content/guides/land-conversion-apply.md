# Land Conversion Apply Online: Agriculture to Non-Agriculture

If you own agricultural land in India and want to use it for residential, commercial, or industrial purposes, you must first obtain **land conversion permission** (also called **NA permission** — Non-Agriculture order). Using agricultural land for non-agricultural purposes without conversion is illegal and can attract heavy penalties.

This guide explains the complete process of applying for land conversion, state-wise procedures, documents required, fees, restrictions, and timelines.

---

## What is Land Conversion (NA Permission)?

Land conversion is the legal process of changing the designated use of land from **agricultural** to **non-agricultural** purposes. Every piece of land in India is classified under a specific land use category in revenue records. To change this classification, you need permission from the revenue or district authority.

### Why is NA Permission Needed?

- To construct a house, factory, or commercial building on agricultural land
- To set up an industrial unit or warehouse
- To develop a residential layout or township
- To use the land for any purpose other than farming

---

## Documents Required

- **Application form** (prescribed by the state)
- **7/12 extract** or **khata certificate** (land record)
- **Property tax receipts** (latest)
- **Mutation entries** (showing current ownership)
- **Survey map** and **site plan** of the land
- **Registered sale deed** (if recently purchased)
- **ID proof** and **address proof** of the applicant
- **No Objection Certificate** from the Gram Panchayat (for rural areas)
- **Soil test report** (in some states)
- **Town planning scheme map** (if in a planning authority area)
- **Affidavit** stating the purpose of conversion

## State-Wise Online Application Process

### Karnataka

1. Visit the revenue department portal at [revenuedepartment.karnataka.gov.in](https://revenuedepartment.karnataka.gov.in/)
2. Navigate to the **Land Conversion (DC Conversion)** section
3. Submit the application with 7/12 extract, survey map, and site plan
4. Pay the conversion fee online
5. The Deputy Commissioner's office will verify and process
6. If approved, the NA order is issued and land records are updated

### Maharashtra

1. Visit the district collectorate portal or [mahabhulekh.maharashtra.gov.in](https://mahabhulekh.maharashtra.gov.in/)
2. Apply for **NA (Non-Agriculture) permission** under Section 44 of the Maharashtra Land Revenue Code
3. Upload 7/12 extract, property card, village map, and site plan
4. Pay the premium (conversion tax) based on the zone and land area
5. Track status online using application number

### Rajasthan

1. Visit [iora.rajasthan.gov.in](https://iora.rajasthan.gov.in/) or apply through e-Mitra
2. Submit conversion application under Rajasthan Land Revenue Act
3. Upload jamabandi, khasra map, and site plan
4. Pay the fee through e-Mitra or online portal
5. District Collector office processes the application

### Gujarat

1. Visit the Revenue Department portal at [revenuedepartment.gujarat.gov.in](https://revenuedepartment.gujarat.gov.in/)
2. Apply for NA permission under Section 65 of Gujarat Land Revenue Code
3. Upload required documents including village form 7/12 and 8-A
4. Pay the NA premium online
5. Collector's office will inspect and process the application

### Madhya Pradesh

1. Visit [mpbhulekh.gov.in](https://mpbhulekh.gov.in/)
2. Apply for diversion of land use
3. Upload khasra, B-1 record, and site plan
4. Pay the prescribed conversion fee
5. Track application status on the portal

### Other States

Most states process land conversion through the **District Collector's office**. Check your state's revenue department portal or visit [dilrmp.gov.in](https://dilrmp.gov.in/) for links.

## Processing Time

- **Karnataka:** 30–60 days
- **Maharashtra:** 60–90 days
- **Rajasthan:** 30–60 days
- **Gujarat:** 45–90 days
- **Other states:** Typically 30–120 days

The timeline depends on the location, land size, and whether any objections are raised during the process.

## Restrictions on Land Conversion

Not all agricultural land can be converted. Key restrictions include:

1. **Irrigated/fertile land:** Many states restrict conversion of highly fertile or irrigated agricultural land
2. **Land ceiling limits:** Conversion may be denied if it exceeds permissible land ceiling limits
3. **Green belt/agricultural zone:** Land in designated agricultural zones under master plans cannot be converted
4. **Forest land:** Land classified as forest cannot be converted without Central Government approval under Forest Conservation Act
5. **Tribal land:** Special restrictions apply to tribal land under Fifth/Sixth Schedule areas
6. **Ceiling surplus land:** Land acquired under ceiling laws cannot be converted
7. **Revenue village restrictions:** Some states restrict conversion within a certain distance of revenue village boundaries

## Penalties for Using Agricultural Land Without Conversion

Using agricultural land for non-agricultural purposes without NA permission can result in:

- **Heavy fines** — ranging from ₹5,000 to several lakhs depending on the state
- **Demolition orders** for any unauthorized construction
- **Criminal prosecution** under the respective state's Land Revenue Act
- **Cancellation of any approvals** obtained without proper land conversion
- **Property cannot be registered** for sale if used illegally

## Important Tips

1. **Check the master plan** of your area before purchasing agricultural land for non-agricultural use
2. **Get conversion done BEFORE construction** — building without NA permission is a serious offence
3. **Verify no litigation** on the land before applying for conversion
4. **Keep all receipts** — conversion fee payment receipts are needed for future transactions
5. **Update mutation records** after receiving the NA order

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I build a house on agricultural land without conversion?

No. Building any permanent structure on agricultural land without obtaining NA permission is illegal. You must first get the land converted to non-agricultural (residential) use before starting construction.

### How much does land conversion cost?

The cost varies significantly by state and location. It is typically calculated as a percentage of the guideline value of the land. In urban areas, the premium can be 10% or more of the land value. Check your state's revenue department portal for exact rates.

### Can agricultural land be converted for commercial use?

Yes, agricultural land can be converted for commercial, industrial, or residential use, subject to zoning regulations, master plan provisions, and state-specific restrictions. Some areas may not permit commercial conversion.

### Is NA permission permanent?

Yes, once the NA order is issued and land records are updated, the conversion is permanent. However, you must use the land for the purpose stated in the application. Using it for a different purpose may require a separate permission.

### Can I sell agricultural land to a non-farmer?

This depends on state law. Many states (Karnataka, Maharashtra, etc.) restrict the sale of agricultural land to non-agriculturists. You may need to convert the land first or obtain special permission.

### What is the difference between land conversion and land use change?

They are essentially the same — both refer to changing the designated purpose of land from agricultural to non-agricultural. The terminology varies by state (NA permission, diversion, conversion, etc.).

### Can the government reject my conversion application?

Yes. Common reasons for rejection include: land falls in agricultural zone of the master plan, the land is fertile irrigated land, pending litigation, improper documents, or the proposed use violates local planning regulations.
