## Why Update Your Voter ID After Marriage?

After marriage, many women (and sometimes men) change their surname or full name. It is important to update your Voter ID (EPIC) to reflect your new legal name because:

- **Voting:** Mismatch between your name and electoral roll entry may cause issues at the polling booth
- **ID Proof:** Voter ID is widely used as identity proof — an outdated name creates problems
- **Legal Consistency:** Keeping all documents updated with the same name avoids complications in government services

The name change is done through **Form 8** (Application for Correction of Entries in Electoral Roll).

## Documents Required

**Mandatory:**
- Existing Voter ID card (EPIC) with old name
- **Marriage Certificate** (issued by the local municipal body or registrar)
- Aadhaar Card (updated with new name, if available)

**Supporting (any one with new name):**
- Passport with new name
- [PAN Card](/guide/pan-card-apply-online) with new name
- Husband's Voter ID card (to verify husband's details and address)
- Gazette Notification (if applicable)

**Photo:**
- Recent passport-size photograph (JPEG, 10–200 KB)

> **Tip:** Get your [marriage certificate](/guide/marriage-certificate-apply-online) first. It is the primary document for name change across all government IDs.

## Step-by-Step Process — Online (Form 8)

### Step 1: Visit the Voter Portal
Go to [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in) and log in with your registered mobile number.

### Step 2: Navigate to Form 8
Click on **"Correction of Entries"** or select **Form 8** from the Forms section.

### Step 3: Search Your Electoral Entry
Enter your **EPIC number** to pull up your current voter registration details.

### Step 4: Select Fields to Correct
Select the following fields for correction:
- **Name** — Enter your new name (after marriage)
- **Surname** — Update if changed
- **Relation Name** — Change from father's name to **husband's name** (if applicable)
- **Relation Type** — Change from "Father" to "Husband" (if applicable)

### Step 5: Upload Supporting Documents
Upload scanned copies of:
- Marriage Certificate (PDF/JPEG, under 2 MB)
- Aadhaar Card with new name (if updated)
- New passport-size photograph

### Step 6: Review and Submit
- Double-check all entries — old name vs. new name
- Submit the form
- Note the **Reference ID** for tracking
- SMS confirmation will be sent to your registered number

## Step-by-Step Process — Offline

### Step 1: Obtain Form 8
Download from [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in) or collect from the ERO/BLO office.

### Step 2: Fill the Form
- Enter your current EPIC details
- In the correction section, clearly mention old name and new name
- Indicate reason: **"Name change after marriage"**

### Step 3: Attach Documents
Attach photocopies of:
- Marriage Certificate
- Updated Aadhaar or other ID with new name
- Husband's Voter ID (for reference)
- Two passport-size photographs

### Step 4: Submit to ERO/BLO
Submit the filled form and documents to your **Booth Level Officer (BLO)** or the **Electoral Registration Officer (ERO)** at the district collectorate.

### Step 5: Verification
The BLO may visit your address for verification. Keep your original documents ready for inspection.

## What About Address Change?

If you have moved to your husband's address after marriage, you have two options:

1. **Same constituency:** File Form 8 for name correction + address update in a single application
2. **Different constituency:** File **Form 6** for fresh registration at the new address AND **Form 7** to delete your entry from the old constituency

> See our [address change guide](/guide/voter-id-address-change-transfer-fix) for the detailed process.

## Processing Time

| Stage | Timeline |
|-------|----------|
| Online submission to BLO verification | 7–15 days |
| ERO approval | 15–30 days |
| Updated e-EPIC available | After approval |
| Physical EPIC card dispatch | 30–60 days |

## Fees

The name correction process is **completely free**. There are no charges for Form 8 — online or offline.

## Important Tips

1. **Update Aadhaar first** — If possible, update your name on [Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) before filing Form 8. It speeds up verification.
2. **Marriage certificate is essential** — Without it, your name change request is likely to be rejected. Apply for one if you don't have it yet.
3. **Change relation name too** — Don't forget to update the "Relation Name" field from father to husband if required.
4. **Download e-EPIC after approval** — The digital Voter ID is available immediately after ERO approval on [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in).
5. **Update all IDs together** — When changing your name, update Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, passport, and bank accounts in one go to maintain consistency.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I change my name on Voter ID after marriage online?
Yes. Use Form 8 on [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in) to submit a name correction request with your marriage certificate as proof.

### What documents are needed for Voter ID name change after marriage?
You need your existing Voter ID, marriage certificate, Aadhaar card (preferably updated), and a recent passport-size photo.

### Is marriage certificate mandatory for name change?
Yes, the marriage certificate is the primary proof for a post-marriage name change. Without it, the ERO may reject your application.

### Can I change my name and address together?
Yes, if you are within the same constituency. Use Form 8 and select both "Name" and "Address" fields for correction. For a different constituency, you need Form 6 + Form 7.

### How long does the name change process take?
The entire process takes **15–60 days** from submission to approval. The e-EPIC is available sooner than the physical card.

### Do I need my husband's Voter ID for the process?
It is not mandatory but helpful as supporting proof, especially if you are changing your relation name to your husband's name.

### Is there any fee for changing the name on Voter ID?
No. Name correction via Form 8 is completely free of charge.
