# Axis Bank Dormant Account Reactivation 2026 — Complete Guide

Axis Bank follows RBI's rule: accounts with no customer-initiated transactions for **24 consecutive months** are classified as dormant. Incoming credits (salary, transfers received) do not keep the account active. Only your own debit transactions count.

---

## Axis Bank Dormant Account — What Gets Restricted

| Service | Active | Dormant |
|---------|--------|---------|
| ATM withdrawals | ✅ | ❌ |
| Debit card payments | ✅ | ❌ |
| UPI / Axis Mobile | ✅ | ❌ |
| Net banking debits | ✅ | ❌ |
| Salary/NEFT credits | ✅ | ✅ Continues |
| Balance enquiry | ✅ | ✅ Available |

---

## Method 1: Axis Mobile App (Online Reactivation — Select Accounts)

1. Open **Axis Mobile** app → Login
2. **"Accounts"** → Select dormant account → **"Services"**
3. If **"Reactivate Account"** option shows: Tap it
4. Complete Aadhaar OTP KYC if required
5. Submit reactivation request
6. **Processing: 24–48 hours**

If option absent in app: Branch visit required.

---

## Method 2: Axis Bank Net Banking

1. Login at **[axisbank.com](https://www.axisbank.com)** → NetBanking
2. **"My Accounts"** → Dormant account → **"Account Services"**
3. Select **"Reactivate Account"**
4. Upload KYC documents if prompted (Aadhaar + PAN)
5. Submit → Confirmation in 2–3 working days

---

## Method 3: Axis Bank Branch (Most Effective)

1. Visit any Axis Bank branch
2. Carry:
   - Aadhaar card (original + self-attested copy)
   - PAN card (original + copy)
   - Account passbook or account number
3. At service desk: Request **"Dormant Account Reactivation"**
4. Fill the reactivation form
5. Make a small deposit or withdrawal at the counter (triggers reactivation)
6. **Same-day processing**

**Axis Customer Care:** **1860-419-5555** or **1800-419-5577** (toll-free)

## Axis Bank Dormant Account — KYC Check

Before processing reactivation, Axis verifies KYC:
- If Aadhaar + PAN already linked: Reactivation is faster
- If KYC is outdated: Update Aadhaar + PAN simultaneously at branch
- No KYC = No reactivation — ensure you carry both documents

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Axis Bank account dormant for 4 years — can it be reactivated?**
Yes — up to 10 years of dormancy, Axis accounts are reactivable. After 10 years, balance moves to DEAF but is claimable. Visit branch with valid KYC documents immediately.

**Axis Bank says "system cannot process reactivation" at branch — what now?**
Ask the branch manager to raise a manual request to the operations team. Get a reference number. It should be processed within 2–3 working days. Escalate to Axis customer care if not resolved.

**Can I reactivate Axis dormant account via phone banking?**
Phone banking (1860-419-5555) cannot process reactivation remotely. They can check your account status and guide you, but physical transaction/branch visit is needed.

**My Axis dormant account has a ₹1,200 maintenance charge deducted — normal?**
Axis deducts AMB (Average Monthly Balance) charges even on dormant accounts. The charges are valid per Axis's schedule. For waiver: Raise a request at branch — sometimes waived if account was genuinely dormant due to medical or other reasons.

**Axis Bank — will they send a notice before marking my account dormant?**
Yes — Axis sends SMS/email notification when an account is approaching dormancy (typically at 18 months of inactivity). Make one UPI payment or ATM withdrawal to reset the dormancy clock before the 24-month mark.
