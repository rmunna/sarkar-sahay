# Jammu and Kashmir Government Schemes and Services – Complete Guide

Jammu and Kashmir (J&K) became a Union Territory with a legislature on 31 October 2019, following the reorganisation of the erstwhile state under the Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019. The UT has an elected government headed by a Chief Minister, with a Lieutenant Governor as the constitutional head. J&K's administration runs numerous welfare schemes spanning livelihood, women's empowerment, healthcare, housing, skill development, agriculture, and tourism. This guide covers every major J&K government scheme with eligibility, benefits, and how to apply.

## Key Government Portals

- **J&K Official Portal** – [jk.gov.in](https://jk.gov.in/) – Main government website for all departments and notifications
- **JanSugam Portal** – [jansugam.jk.gov.in](https://jansugam.jk.gov.in/) – Single-window e-Services platform for certificates, permissions, and government services
- **J&K e-Services (UMANG)** – [umang.gov.in](https://web.umang.gov.in/landing/department/jammu-and-kashmir-e-services.html) – Pan-India portal offering J&K e-Services

- **JKPSC** – [jkpsc.nic.in](https://jkpsc.nic.in/) – J&K Public Service Commission for government recruitment exams
- **J&K Bank** – [jkbank.com](https://www.jkbank.com/) – Major banking partner for scheme disbursements

## Livelihood and Entrepreneurship Schemes

### Mumkin Scheme (Livelihood Generation)

Mumkin ("possible" in Urdu) is J&K's flagship livelihood scheme aimed at providing self-employment to youth through commercial vehicle ownership.

**Key features:**
- Subsidy of up to ₹80,000 on purchase of small commercial vehicles
- Targets unemployed youth aged 18–35 years
- Vehicle loan facilitated through J&K Bank
- Beneficiary must be a permanent resident/domicile of J&K
- Vehicles include load carriers, passenger vehicles, and tempos

**How to apply:** Apply through the District Employment and Counselling Centre or the concerned district administration office.

### Hausla Scheme (Women Entrepreneurs)

The Hausla scheme encourages women entrepreneurship in J&K by providing financial and institutional support for women-led enterprises.

**Key features:**
- Financial assistance and subsidised loans for women starting or expanding businesses
- Incubation support and mentoring through DIC (District Industries Centre)
- Covers manufacturing, services, handicrafts, food processing, and agri-enterprises
- Special focus on women in remote and border areas
- Linkage with J&K Bank and other financial institutions for credit support

**How to apply:** Register through the District Industries Centre (DIC) or the Department of Industries and Commerce, J&K.

### Tejaswini Scheme (Young Women Empowerment)

Tejaswini is a dedicated scheme for young women in J&K, providing soft loans to help them become self-reliant.

**Key features:**
- Loan of up to ₹5 lakh at subsidised interest rate
- For women aged 18–35 years
- Applicable for setting up small business, pursuing higher education, or skill training
- Loan facilitated through J&K Bank
- No collateral required for loans up to ₹5 lakh
- Must be a domicile of J&K

**How to apply:** Visit the nearest J&K Bank branch with your domicile certificate, Aadhaar card, business plan or admission letter, and bank account details. See also: [Aadhaar Card – How to Apply](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)

## Skill Development and Employment

### Himayat – Skill Training for Youth

Himayat (meaning "support") is a placement-linked skill development programme under the Ministry of Rural Development, implemented in J&K to train and place youth in organised-sector jobs.

**Key features:**
- Free skill training in sectors like IT, retail, hospitality, healthcare, BFSI, and construction
- Training duration: 3–12 months depending on the trade
- Stipend provided during training
- Guaranteed placement assistance after course completion
- Targets youth aged 18–35, with preference for BPL families and rural youth
- Over 1 lakh youth targeted for training and placement

**How to apply:** Register through the [Himayat portal](https://www.integratedindia.in/) or contact the District Skill Employment Centre.

### JKPSC Examinations

The Jammu & Kashmir Public Service Commission (JKPSC) conducts recruitment examinations for gazetted and non-gazetted posts in the UT government.

**Key exams:**
- **J&K Combined Competitive Examination (KAS)** – Equivalent to UPSC for J&K administrative services
- **J&K Medical Officer recruitment**
- **Assistant Professor and Lecturer recruitment**
- **Junior Scale JKAS and various departmental exams**

**How to apply:** Visit [jkpsc.nic.in](https://jkpsc.nic.in/), register for One-Time Registration (OTR), and apply for notified posts. See also: [Admit Card Download Guide](/guide/admit-card-download-online-guide)

### J&K SSRB (Services Selection Recruitment Board)

JKSSRB handles recruitment for Class IV and non-gazetted posts. Apply through [jkssb.nic.in](https://jkssb.nic.in/).

## Healthcare Schemes

### Ayushman Bharat – SEHAT Scheme

Ayushman Bharat – Social Endeavour for Health and Telemedicine (SEHAT) extends universal health coverage to all residents of J&K, going beyond the national PMJAY eligibility criteria.

**Key features:**
- Health insurance cover of ₹5 lakh per family per year
- Covers all residents of J&K – universal coverage (not limited to SECC data like PMJAY)
- Cashless treatment at empanelled government and private hospitals
- Covers hospitalisation, surgery, diagnostics, and follow-up care
- Golden Card (Ayushman Card) required for availing benefits

**How to apply:** Visit the nearest Ayushman Bharat centre, Common Service Centre (CSC), or empanelled hospital with Aadhaar, domicile, and ration card. See also: [Ayushman Bharat Card – Apply Online](/guide/ayushman-bharat-card-download-eligibility) | [ABHA Health ID](/guide/abha-health-id-create-online)

### PMJAY in J&K

The national Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PMJAY) is also applicable in J&K alongside SEHAT. Eligible families under SECC data get automatic coverage. See: [Ayushman Bharat vs State Health Schemes](/guide/ayushman-bharat-vs-state-health-schemes)

## Housing Schemes

### PMAY – Gramin and Urban (J&K)

The Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) is implemented in J&K under both Gramin (rural) and Urban components to provide pucca houses to homeless and kutcha-house-dwelling families.

**Key features:**
- **PMAY-G (Rural):** ₹1.30 lakh assistance in plains, ₹1.50 lakh in hilly/difficult areas (J&K qualifies for higher amount)
- **PMAY-U (Urban):** Subsidy of ₹2.67 lakh under CLSS for EWS/LIG categories
- Beneficiary selection through SECC data and Awaas+ survey
- DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer) to beneficiary's bank account

**How to apply:** Apply through the Gram Panchayat (rural) or Urban Local Body (urban). Check eligibility at [pmaymis.gov.in](https://pmaymis.gov.in/). See also: [PMAY Apply Online Guide](/guide/pm-awas-yojana-apply)

## Agriculture and Horticulture Schemes

### Apple Industry Support

J&K produces over 75% of India's apples, and the government runs several schemes to support apple growers:

- **High-Density Apple Plantation Scheme** – Subsidy for establishing high-density orchards with imported rootstock
- **Market Intervention Scheme (MIS)** – Minimum price support during glut seasons
- **Cold Storage and CA Store subsidies** – Under MIDH for post-harvest infrastructure
- **Apple transport subsidy** – Freight support for transporting apples to distant markets

Contact the Department of Horticulture, J&K or visit the nearest Horticulture Development Office.

### Saffron Mission

J&K accounts for nearly all of India's saffron production (primarily in Pampore, Pulwama district). The National Saffron Mission provides:

- Rejuvenation of saffron fields through drip irrigation and soil treatment
- GI-tagged "Kashmir Saffron" protection and branding support
- Subsidy on corm procurement and planting material
- Training for saffron growers on modern techniques

### Handicraft and Handloom Schemes

J&K's handicraft sector (Pashmina, Papier-mâché, Walnut wood, Carpet weaving) receives support through:

- **Artisan Credit Card** – Loans up to ₹2 lakh at subsidised rates through J&K Bank
- **Design and Technology Upgradation** – Training through Indian Institute of Carpet Technology, Srinagar
- **Marketing support** – Participation in national/international trade fairs, GI tagging for Kashmir Pashmina and Kani Shawl
- **Craft Development Institute (CDI)** – Skill training and certification for young artisans

## Power and Utilities

### JKPDD (J&K Power Development Department)

The J&K Power Development Department (previously PDD, now restructured) manages electricity generation, transmission, and distribution across the UT.

**Key services:**
- Online bill payment through [jkpdd.in](https://jkpdd.in/) or [jkpddcl.in](https://jkpddcl.in/)
- New connection application
- Complaint registration and outage tracking
- Prepaid smart metering (being rolled out across J&K)
- Subsidy for BPL households on electricity consumption

**Saubhagya Scheme:** Free electricity connections to all remaining un-electrified households under the central Saubhagya scheme.

## Domicile Certificate Process

The J&K Domicile Certificate is a crucial document required for government jobs, educational admissions, and scheme eligibility within the UT.

**Eligibility for J&K Domicile:**
- Resided in J&K for 15+ years, OR
- Studied in J&K for 7+ years, OR
- Appeared in Class 10 or 12 exams from a J&K-based institution, OR
- Registered as a migrant by the Relief and Rehabilitation Commissioner

**Documents required:**
- Aadhaar card – See: [Aadhaar Card Apply Guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
- 10th/12th mark sheet from a J&K educational institution
- Proof of residence (ration card, electricity bill, etc.)
- Passport-size photographs

**How to apply:** Apply through the **JanSugam Portal** ([jansugam.jk.gov.in](https://jansugam.jk.gov.in/)) or the office of the concerned Tehsildar. The certificate is issued by the Deputy Commissioner's office.

## Tourism and Employment

J&K's tourism sector is a major employment generator. Key government initiatives:

- **Registration of homestays and guest houses** – Simplified online registration to promote rural tourism
- **Skill training for tourism workers** – Through J&K Tourism Development Corporation and Himayat
- **Pilgrimage tourism support** – Infrastructure development for Amarnath Yatra, Vaishno Devi, and Sufi shrines
- **Adventure tourism promotion** – Subsidies for starting adventure tourism ventures (trekking, skiing, water sports)
- **Back to Village / My Town My Pride** – Government outreach programmes reaching every panchayat with scheme information and on-the-spot registrations

## How to Apply for J&K Government Schemes

Most J&K government schemes follow a common application process:

1. **Check eligibility** – Visit the official scheme page on [jk.gov.in](https://jk.gov.in/) or the concerned department website
2. **Gather documents** – Domicile certificate, Aadhaar card, income certificate, bank passbook, passport photos. See: [Documents Required Guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
3. **Register on JanSugam** – Create an account on [jansugam.jk.gov.in](https://jansugam.jk.gov.in/) for certificate and e-service applications
4. **Apply online or offline** – Some schemes accept online applications; others require visiting the District Office, DIC, or BDO office
5. **Track application** – Use the JanSugam portal or the concerned department's tracking system
6. **Receive benefits** – Subsidy/assistance credited to your bank account via DBT

**Pro tip:** Keep your Aadhaar linked to your bank account for seamless DBT. See: [Aadhaar Linking Guide](/guide/aadhaar-linking-guide)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is J&K a state or a Union Territory?

J&K is a Union Territory with a legislature since 31 October 2019. It has an elected government with a Chief Minister and a Lieutenant Governor appointed by the Centre. Ladakh was carved out as a separate UT without a legislature.

### Who is eligible for J&K domicile certificate?

Anyone who has resided in J&K for 15+ years, studied for 7+ years in J&K institutions, or appeared for Class 10/12 exams from J&K is eligible. Kashmiri migrants registered with the Relief Commissioner also qualify.

### How can I apply for Tejaswini scheme?

Visit the nearest J&K Bank branch with your domicile certificate, Aadhaar card, and a business plan or admission letter. The scheme provides loans up to ₹5 lakh for women aged 18–35.

### Is Ayushman Bharat available in J&K?

Yes. J&K has the Ayushman Bharat – SEHAT scheme which provides universal health coverage of ₹5 lakh per family to ALL residents of J&K, not just SECC-listed families.

### How do I check JKPSC exam notifications?

Visit [jkpsc.nic.in](https://jkpsc.nic.in/) regularly and complete One-Time Registration (OTR). Notifications for KAS, Medical Officer, and other posts are published on the website.

### What schemes are available for apple growers in J&K?

Apple growers can avail the High-Density Plantation Scheme, Market Intervention Scheme, cold storage subsidies, and transport subsidies. Contact the Department of Horticulture or the nearest Horticulture Development Office.

### How do I apply for PMAY in J&K?

For PMAY-Gramin, apply through your Gram Panchayat. For PMAY-Urban, apply through the Urban Local Body or online at [pmaymis.gov.in](https://pmaymis.gov.in/). J&K qualifies for higher assistance (₹1.50 lakh) under the hilly area category.

### Can non-domiciles apply for J&K government schemes?

Most J&K-specific schemes (Mumkin, Tejaswini, Hausla) require a J&K domicile certificate. However, centrally-sponsored schemes like PMAY, PMJAY, and MUDRA are available to all eligible Indian citizens residing in J&K.
