# Marriage Certificate Apply Online — Registration Process (2026)

A marriage certificate is an essential legal document proving your marriage. It's required for passport name change, spouse visa, joint property, insurance nomination, and many government services. Most Indian states now allow online application for marriage registration. This guide covers the complete process, documents, and state-wise portals.

## Why Do You Need a Marriage Certificate?

- **Legal proof of marriage** — recognized in courts
- **Passport** — required for spouse name addition
- **Visa applications** — embassies require it for dependent/spouse visas
- **Joint bank accounts** and property registration
- **Insurance** and pension nomination
- **Government schemes** for married couples
- **Prevention of child marriage** and bigamy
- **Name change** after marriage

## Types of Marriage Registration in India

### 1. Hindu Marriage Act, 1955
For marriages performed under Hindu customs (includes Hindus, Buddhists, Jains, Sikhs).

### 2. Special Marriage Act, 1954
For inter-faith marriages, court marriages, and civil marriages regardless of religion. Requires 30-day notice period.

### 3. Other Personal Laws
Muslim, Christian, and Parsi marriages registered under respective personal laws.

## Documents Required

### For Both Bride and Groom:
- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory in most states)
- **Age proof** — birth certificate, 10th marksheet, or passport
- **Address proof** — Aadhaar, voter ID, passport, or utility bill
- **Passport-size photographs** (individual + joint/couple photograph)
- **PAN card** (some states require it)

### Marriage-Related Documents:
- **Marriage invitation card** (if available)
- **Marriage photographs** (ceremony photos)
- **Affidavit** — sworn affidavit stating date, place, and details of marriage (on stamp paper)
- **Priest certificate** (letter from the priest who performed the ceremony) — for religious marriages

### Witness Documents:
- **Two witnesses** with valid ID proof (Aadhaar/voter ID)
- Witnesses must be present during registration

### Additional (If Applicable):
- **Divorce decree** — if either party was previously married
- **Death certificate of previous spouse** — if widowed
- **NOC from embassy** — for marriages involving foreign nationals
- **Conversion certificate** — if religious conversion involved

### Step 2: Register/Login
1. Create an account with mobile number and email
2. Verify via OTP
3. Log in to the portal

### Step 3: Fill Marriage Registration Form
1. Select **"Marriage Registration"** or **"Apply for Marriage Certificate"**
2. Select the **Act** under which the marriage is registered (Hindu Marriage Act / Special Marriage Act)
3. Enter **groom's details** — name, father's name, DOB, religion, occupation, address
4. Enter **bride's details** — same information
5. Enter **marriage details** — date of marriage, place, type of ceremony
6. Enter **witness details** — name, age, address, relationship

### Step 4: Upload Documents
Upload scanned copies:
- Aadhaar cards (both parties)
- Age proof documents
- Marriage photographs (ceremony + couple photo)
- Affidavit
- Witness ID proofs
- Any other required documents

### Step 5: Book Appointment
1. Select the **Sub-Registrar office / Marriage Registrar office**
2. Choose an available **date and time slot**
3. Note the appointment date — both parties and witnesses must attend

### Step 6: Pay Registration Fee

| State | Fee (Approximate) |
|-------|-------------------|
| Delhi | ₹100 (within 1 year) / ₹200 (after 1 year) |
| UP | ₹100-500 |
| Maharashtra | ₹100-500 |
| Karnataka | ₹500 |
| Tamil Nadu | ₹100 |
| Rajasthan | ₹100 |

> Additional charges may apply for stamp paper, affidavit, etc.

### Step 7: Visit Registrar Office
On the appointment date:
1. **Both bride and groom** must be present with original documents
2. **Two witnesses** must attend with their original ID proofs
3. **Gazetted officer** may need to attest the application (varies by state)
4. Biometric verification of all parties
5. Registrar verifies documents and records the marriage

### Step 8: Receive Marriage Certificate
- In some states, the certificate is issued on the same day
- In others, it takes 7-15 working days
- Download the digitally signed certificate from the portal
- Or collect the physical copy from the Registrar office

## Court Marriage Process (Special Marriage Act)

For court marriages, the process is slightly different:

1. **Submit notice** to the Marriage Registrar 30 days before the intended date
2. The notice is **displayed publicly** for 30 days inviting objections
3. If no valid objections, the marriage is solemnized on the scheduled date
4. Both parties, **three witnesses**, and the Marriage Registrar must be present
5. Certificate is issued after solemnization

> Court marriages under the Special Marriage Act require a **30-day notice period** — plan accordingly.

## How to Download Marriage Certificate Online

If your marriage was registered online:
1. Visit your state portal
2. Log in with your credentials
3. Go to **"Download Certificate"** or **"My Applications"**
4. Download the digitally signed marriage certificate

## Important Tips

1. **Register within 1 year** of marriage — late registration may attract additional fees or require court order in some states
2. **Both parties must be of legal age** — 21 for groom, 18 for bride
3. **Keep multiple copies** — you'll need the certificate for various purposes
4. **DigiLocker** — check if your state pushes the certificate to DigiLocker for easy access
5. **Married abroad?** Register under the Foreign Marriage Act or get it attested by the Indian embassy

## FAQs

### 1. Is marriage registration mandatory in India?
While not universally mandatory by central law, most states have made marriage registration compulsory. It's highly recommended for legal protection and accessing government services.

### 2. How much does marriage registration cost?
Typically ₹100-500 as registration fee, plus ₹50-100 for stamp paper/affidavit. Total cost is usually under ₹1,000.

### 3. Can I register my marriage online without visiting the office?
No, physical presence of both parties and witnesses is required at the Registrar office for verification. The application and appointment can be done online.

### 4. What is the difference between marriage certificate and marriage registration?
They're essentially the same — marriage registration is the process, and the marriage certificate is the document issued after registration.

### 5. How long does it take to get a marriage certificate?
Same day to 15 working days depending on the state. Online application with appointment booking speeds up the process.

### 6. Can I register a marriage that took place years ago?
Yes, but the process may require additional documents like an affidavit, newspaper advertisement, or court order depending on the delay and state rules.

### 7. Do both parties need to be Indian citizens?
No, marriages involving foreign nationals can be registered in India. Additional documents like passport, visa, and embassy NOC may be required.
