# India Post Tracking: Track Speed Post & Registered Mail

## What is India Post Tracking?

India Post provides online tracking for **Speed Post**, **Registered Post**, **Express Parcel**, and other trackable mail services. Using your **13-digit consignment number**, you can check real-time delivery status on the official India Post website or mobile app.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent informational platform and is not affiliated with India Post or the Government of India.

## What Can You Track?

| Service | Trackable | Consignment Number Format |
|---|---|---|
| Speed Post | ✅ Yes | EE123456789IN |
| Registered Post | ✅ Yes | RR123456789IN |
| Express Parcel | ✅ Yes | EP123456789IN |
| Ordinary Post | ❌ No | No tracking available |
| Book Post (Registered) | ✅ Yes | RB123456789IN |

## How to Track Online — Step-by-Step

### Method 1: India Post Website

1. Visit [India Post Tracking](https://www.indiapost.gov.in/_layouts/15/dop.portal.tracking/trackconsignment.aspx)
2. Enter your **13-digit consignment number** (e.g., EE123456789IN)
3. Click **"Track"**
4. View the tracking details — booking date, current location, delivery status
5. You can track **up to 10 consignments** at once (comma-separated)

### Method 2: India Post Mobile App

1. Download **India Post Mobile App** from [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=in.gov.cept.postinfo) or Apple App Store
2. Open the app and go to **"Track Consignment"**
3. Enter the consignment number
4. View real-time status updates

### Method 3: SMS Tracking

1. Send an SMS: **POST TRACK <consignment number>**
2. Send to **166** (India Post SMS service)
3. Receive delivery status via SMS reply

*Example: Send "POST TRACK EE123456789IN" to 166*

## Where to Find Your Consignment Number

- **Speed Post receipt** — printed on the booking receipt at the post office
- **Online booking confirmation** — if booked via ePost or India Post portal
- **Sender's notification** — ask the sender for the tracking number
- The number is **13 characters** — starts with 2 letters, followed by 9 digits, ending with "IN"

## Understanding Tracking Status

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Item Booked | Parcel accepted at source post office |
| In Transit | Moving between postal sorting centers |
| Arrived at Delivery Office | Reached your local post office |
| Out for Delivery | Postman is delivering it today |
| Item Delivered | Successfully delivered |
| Item Returned | Could not deliver, returning to sender |
| Held at Post Office | Waiting for you to collect (if absent) |

## Speed Post Delivery Timelines

| Route | Expected Delivery |
|---|---|
| Within same city | 1-2 days |
| Metro to Metro | 2-3 days |
| Metro to State Capital | 2-4 days |
| State Capital to District | 3-5 days |
| Remote/Rural areas | 4-7 days |

**Note:** These are indicative timelines. Actual delivery depends on distance and logistics.

## Important Tips

1. **Save your receipt** — the consignment number is your only tracking reference
2. **Track early and often** — tracking updates may take 12-24 hours to reflect
3. **Use the app** — it sends push notifications for status changes
4. **Multiple items** — track up to 10 consignments at once on the website
5. **No tracking for ordinary post** — only registered and speed post services are trackable

## FAQs

### How do I track my Speed Post?

Visit [indiapost.gov.in tracking page](https://www.indiapost.gov.in/_layouts/15/dop.portal.tracking/trackconsignment.aspx), enter your 13-digit consignment number (e.g., EE123456789IN), and click Track.

### What if tracking shows no information?

Tracking data may take **12-24 hours** to appear after booking. If no data shows after 48 hours, contact your booking post office or call India Post helpline at **1800-11-2011** (toll-free).

### How long does Speed Post take to deliver?

Speed Post typically takes **1-5 days** depending on the route — same city (1-2 days), metro to metro (2-3 days), and remote areas (4-7 days).

### Can I track Registered Post the same way?

Yes, Registered Post tracking works the same way. Enter the **RR-prefix consignment number** on the tracking page.

### Is there an India Post tracking app?

Yes, the **India Post Mobile App** is available on Google Play Store and Apple App Store. It offers consignment tracking, post office locator, and PIN code search.

### What does "Item Held" mean in tracking?

It means the postman attempted delivery but no one was available. The item is held at your local post office for **7 days** before being returned. Visit the post office with your ID to collect it.

### Can I track international Speed Post?

Yes, international EMS (Speed Post) items can be tracked on the same India Post tracking page using the consignment number.
