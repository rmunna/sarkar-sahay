# SSC CPO SI 2026 — Complete Exam Guide (Delhi Police & CRPF)

**SSC CPO (Central Police Organizations)** recruits **Sub-Inspectors** for Delhi Police and **Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs)** — BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP, SSB. It's a prestigious exam offering one of the best police officer jobs with salary, stability, and career growth.

**Conducting Body:** Staff Selection Commission (SSC)  
**Notification expected:** September–October 2026

---

| Organization | Post | Pay Level |
|-------------|------|----------|
| **Delhi Police** | Sub-Inspector (Executive) | Level 6 (₹35,400–₹1,12,400) |
| **BSF** | Sub-Inspector (GD) | Level 6 |
| **CRPF** | Sub-Inspector (GD) | Level 6 |
| **CISF** | Sub-Inspector (GD) | Level 6 |
| **ITBP** | Sub-Inspector (GD) | Level 6 |
| **SSB** | Sub-Inspector (GD) | Level 6 |

## Eligibility — SSC CPO 2026

| Criteria | Requirement |
|----------|------------|
| **Education** | **Graduate** (any stream from recognized university) |
| **Age** | 20–25 years (General); SC/ST +5 years; OBC +3 years |
| **Gender** | Male + Female (Delhi Police has separate female quota) |
| **Physical — Male** | Height 170 cm (Gen/OBC/SC), 162.5 cm (ST), 157 cm (hills/ST from hilly states) |
| **Physical — Female** | Height 157 cm (Gen/OBC/SC), 154 cm (hilly ST) |
| **Chest (Male only)** | 80 cm (unexpanded) / 85 cm (expanded) |
| **Vision** | 6/6 or 6/9 for better eye; 6/12 or 6/9 for worse eye |
| **Medical** | Fit as per CAPFs medical standards |

## Selection Process — 4 Stages

1. **Paper I** — Computer Based Test (CBT)
2. **PST (Physical Standard Test)** — Height + chest measurement
3. **PET (Physical Efficiency Test)** — Running + long jump
4. **Paper II** — CBT
5. **Medical Examination**
6. **DV (Document Verification)**

---

## Paper I Pattern (CBT)

| Section | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| General Intelligence & Reasoning | 50 | 50 |
| General Knowledge & Current Affairs | 50 | 50 |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 50 | 50 |
| English Comprehension | 50 | 50 |
| **Total** | **200** | **200** |

- Duration: **2 hours**
- Negative marking: **0.25 per wrong answer**
- Merit from Paper I + Paper II combined

---

## Paper II Pattern

| Section | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| English Language & Comprehension | 200 | 200 |

- Duration: **2 hours**
- No negative marking in Paper II
- Only qualifying — Paper I + Paper II marks combined for merit

## Syllabus — Key Areas

### General Intelligence & Reasoning:
Classification, Series, Analogies, Coding-decoding, Blood relations, Direction test, Syllogism, Venn diagrams, Matrix, Nonverbal (pattern-based)

### Quantitative Aptitude:
Number system, Percentage, Profit-Loss, SI/CI, Trigonometry, Geometry, Time-Work, Time-Distance, Data Interpretation

### General Knowledge:
History, Polity, Geography, Economy, Science & Technology, Current Affairs (6 months), Defence/police related GK, Indian constitution, Important government schemes

### English Comprehension (Paper II):
Reading comprehension, Cloze test, Para jumbles, Error detection, Sentence correction, Fill in the blanks, Vocabulary (synonyms, antonyms, idioms)

## Preparation Strategy (4 Months)

**Month 1: Reasoning + GK**
- Reasoning: Complete all major topics (syllogism, classification, coding — 30Q daily)
- GK: Indian history, polity, geography (NCERT class 9–12)

**Month 2: Quant + English**
- Quant: Data interpretation (high marks), percentage, trigonometry
- English: Grammar foundation + daily editorial reading

**Month 3: Paper II English Focus**
- English Paper II — 200 questions: Practice comprehension sets daily
- Vocabulary: 30 words daily + idioms + one-word substitution
- Mock Paper II: Complete in 1:45 minutes target

**Month 4: Mocks + Physical**
- 2 full mocks (Paper I + II together) per week
- Physical: 1.6 km run daily (target 6 minutes), long jump practice
- Current affairs: Last 6 months intensive

## Frequently Asked Questions

**SSC CPO vs UPSC CAPF (AC) — which is better?**
SSC CPO: Sub-Inspector (directly commissioned). UPSC CAPF AC: Assistant Commandant (officer above SI). CAPF AC is higher rank with better salary and faster promotion. But CAPF AC is much harder (UPSC exam + interview). For graduates who clear SSC: CPO first, then prepare for CAPF AC simultaneously.

**Delhi Police SI vs CRPF/BSF SI — which is better posting?**
Delhi Police: Same city (Delhi), non-field posting mostly, no fencing duty. CRPF/BSF/CISF: Paramilitary duties, postings in difficult areas, border/counter-insurgency. Delhi Police is generally preferred for stable city life.

**SSC CPO — is physical fitness tested after Paper I only?**
PET + PST are conducted after Paper I qualifying. Medical is after Paper II. So: Clear Paper I → Appear for PST/PET → Clear Paper II → Medical.

**Female candidates — what are the physical requirements for SSC CPO?**
Height 157 cm (General). Weight: Proportionate to height. Running: 800m in 4 minutes. Long jump: 2.7 metres. High jump: 0.9 metres. No chest measurement. Separate female vacancies in Delhi Police and CAPFs.

**Can 12th-pass candidates apply for SSC CPO?**
No — SSC CPO requires graduate degree (any discipline). 12th-pass candidates: Apply for SSC GD Constable (the level below SI). Complete graduation first, then appear for SSC CPO.
