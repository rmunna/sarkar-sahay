# Maharashtra Caste Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **caste certificate** (जाति प्रमाण पत्र / Jati Praman Patra) in Maharashtra is issued by the Revenue Department and certifies your caste/community for reservations, scholarships, government scheme eligibility, and government job applications. Maharashtra has specific categories: SC, ST, OBC, NT (Nomadic Tribes — NT-A, NT-B, NT-C, NT-D), SBC, SEBC, and VJNT. Applications are submitted through **Aaple Sarkar**.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in](https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in) → Revenue Department → Caste Certificate. Issued by Tahsildar. Processing: **15–30 days**.

## Types of Certificates in Maharashtra

1. **Caste Certificate** — certifies your caste (for all purposes)
2. **Caste Validity Certificate** — required for government jobs (issued by Scrutiny Committee — separate process)
3. **Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) Certificate** — for OBC/NT — certifies income below ₹8 lakh/year (requires income certificate)

> For **government jobs**, you need both Caste Certificate + Caste Validity Certificate (from District Caste Verification Committee).

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory)
- **Ration card** (shows family details and caste)
- **Father's / grandfather's caste proof** (old school leaving certificate, father's government document, or gazetted record showing caste)
- **Birth certificate** or school leaving certificate of applicant
- **Income certificate** (if applying for OBC/NT NCL certificate)
- Affidavit on stamp paper (in some cases)
- Passport-size photograph

## How to Apply Online — Aaple Sarkar

1. Go to [aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in](https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in)
2. Register / log in with mobile number (OTP)
3. Go to **"Revenue Department"** → **"Caste Certificate"**
4. Select your category (SC/ST/OBC/NT-A/B/C/D/SBC)
5. Fill form: applicant details, caste declaration, address
6. Upload documents (father's caste proof is most important)
7. Pay fee: **₹25–50** (online)
8. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via Setuseva / CSC

1. Visit nearest **Setuseva centre** or CSC
2. Provide Aadhaar, father's caste documents, ration card
3. Operator fills and submits form
4. Service charge: approximately ₹50–80
5. Collect acknowledgement

## Status Check & Download

1. Go to [aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in](https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in)
2. Click **"Track Application"** → enter reference number
3. Once approved: log in → **"My Applications"** → **Download Certificate**
4. Digitally signed PDF — valid for scholarship, college admission, government schemes

## OBC Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) Certificate — Separate Process

The NCL certificate is issued separately and is required for **OBC reservation in central government jobs and colleges**:

1. First, get your **income certificate** (Utkanna Praman Patra)
2. Then apply for **OBC NCL Certificate** separately at Aaple Sarkar
3. Income must be below **₹8 lakh per annum** (central government standard)
4. Valid for **3 years**

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between caste certificate and caste validity certificate in Maharashtra?

**Caste certificate** is issued by Tahsildar — for scholarships, schemes, and general use. **Caste Validity Certificate** is issued by the **District Caste Certificate Scrutiny Committee** — mandatory for government job/promotion under reservation. The two are separate; the scrutiny committee does a thorough ancestral caste verification.

### My caste certificate was issued 5 years ago. Is it still valid?

Maharashtra caste certificates do not have a fixed expiry for identity/scholarship purposes (unless the issuing authority specifies one). However, for **non-creamy layer** certificates, validity is **3 years**. Government job applications may require fresh certificates — check the specific advertisement.
