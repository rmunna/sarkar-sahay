# West Bengal Ration Card Apply Online 2026

The West Bengal ration card is issued by the **Department of Food and Supplies, Government of West Bengal**. West Bengal uses the **WBPDS** (West Bengal Public Distribution System) portal and has its own categories — including RKSY-I and RKSY-II — in addition to the national NFSA categories.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [wbpds.gov.in](https://wbpds.gov.in) or visit your nearest Block Development Office (BDO) or Ward Office. Duare Sarkar camps are periodically held for ration card applications.

---

## Ration Card Types in West Bengal

| Type | Category | Entitlement |
|------|----------|-------------|
| **PHH (Priority Household / NFSA)** | BPL families under central NFSA | 5 kg grain/person/month @ ₹2/kg (rice) |
| **AAY (Antyodaya Anna Yojana)** | Poorest families | 35 kg/family/month @ ₹2/kg |
| **RKSY-I** | State BPL — Priority | 5 kg rice/person/month @ ₹2/kg (state scheme) |
| **RKSY-II** | State BPL — Lower priority | 2 kg rice/person/month @ ₹2/kg |
| **SPHH (Special Priority Household)** | Special categories (widows, disabled, etc.) | As notified |

> **RKSY** stands for Rajya Khadya Suraksha Yojana — West Bengal's own food security scheme beyond the central NFSA quota.

---

## Eligibility

**PHH / RKSY-I:**
- Annual family income below ₹1.20 lakh (rural) or ₹1.50 lakh (urban)
- Not a government employee (central or state)
- No income taxpayer
- No 4-wheeler vehicle
- Aadhaar linked to ration card database

**RKSY-II:**
- Families that don't qualify for PHH but are economically weaker
- Income between ₹1.50 lakh and ₹2.50 lakh

**AAY:**
- Destitute households, widows living alone
- Elderly persons with no income
- Landless agricultural labourers

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar cards (all family members — mandatory)
- Voter ID or other photo ID proof
- Residence proof (electricity bill, bank passbook, municipality/panchayat certificate)
- Passport-size photographs
- Income certificate from BDO/SDO
- Caste certificate (if SC/ST)
- BPL certificate (if applicable)

## How to Apply

**Method 1: WBPDS Online Portal**

1. Go to [wbpds.gov.in](https://wbpds.gov.in)
2. Click **"Apply Online"** or **"New Ration Card Application"**
3. Log in with mobile number (OTP)
4. Fill in household and member details with Aadhaar numbers
5. Upload documents
6. Submit and download acknowledgement

**Method 2: Duare Sarkar Camp**

West Bengal holds periodic **Duare Sarkar** (Government at Your Doorstep) camps where you can apply for ration cards and other services. Watch for announcements in your local panchayat or municipality.

**Method 3: Block Development Office (BDO) / Ward Office**

1. Visit your BDO office (rural) or Ward Office (urban)
2. Submit filled application form with documents
3. BDO/Supply Inspector does field verification
4. Forwarded to District Supply Officer for approval

## Status Check (SAHA Portal)

1. Go to [wbpds.gov.in](https://wbpds.gov.in)
2. Click **"Application Status"** or use the SAHA portal link
3. Enter application number or Aadhaar number
4. View status — submitted / under verification / approved / rejected

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is RKSY-1 ration card in West Bengal?

RKSY-1 (Rajya Khadya Suraksha Yojana-I) is a West Bengal state ration card for BPL families not covered under central NFSA quota. It gives 5 kg rice per person per month at ₹2/kg — same as PHH in terms of quantity.

### How is RKSY-1 different from PHH?

Both give the same quantity (5 kg/person/month). PHH is funded by the central NFSA scheme; RKSY-I is funded by the West Bengal state government. Benefits are similar for the cardholder.

### Can I apply from outside West Bengal?

No — you need to be a resident of West Bengal. If you've moved from another state, surrender your old card and apply fresh in WB.

### Duare Sarkar — when is the next camp?

Camps are announced by the West Bengal government. Check [wb.gov.in](https://wb.gov.in) or ask at your local panchayat/municipality for schedule.
