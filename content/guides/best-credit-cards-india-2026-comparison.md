## How to Choose the Right Credit Card

**Step 1 — Identify your highest spend category:**
- Online shopping → SBI SimplyCLICK or Axis Flipkart
- Travel/flights → Axis Atlas
- Dining + everything → Amex MRCC or HDFC Regalia

**Step 2 — Check eligibility:**
- Premium cards (Regalia, Atlas): Minimum salary ₹6–12 lakh/year or existing relationship
- Mid-range (SimplyCLICK, Millennia): ₹3–5 lakh/year income
- Entry-level (IndusInd Platinum, IDFC Millennia): ₹2.5 lakh/year

**Step 3 — Calculate net benefit:**
Annual fee – Rewards earned – Lounge value = Net benefit

## Credit Card — Key Things to NEVER Do

| Mistake | Consequence |
|---------|-------------|
| Pay only minimum due | 36–42% effective annual interest on remaining balance |
| Take cash advance | 2.5–3% cash advance fee + 42% APR from day 1 |
| Exceed credit limit | Penalty fee + score impact |
| Miss payment by 1 day | Late payment fee (₹500–₹1,200) + CIBIL impact |
| Apply for 5 cards at once | Multiple hard inquiries → score drops |

**Always:** Pay FULL outstanding amount before due date. Credit cards are interest-free if paid in full.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How many credit cards should I have?**
2–3 credit cards is optimal — one for online, one for travel, one for everyday use. More than 4 is hard to manage and increases default risk. Each card is checked in CIBIL.

**Can I get a credit card with CIBIL score 650?**
Difficult for premium cards. Options: IDFC FIRST (more lenient), secured credit card against FD (SBI/HDFC), small bank cards. Build credit for 12 months to cross 700+ for mainstream cards.

**Is lifetime free credit card worth it vs annual fee card?**
Depends on spend. IDFC Millennia (free) gives ~1.5% rewards. HDFC Regalia (₹2,500 fee, waivable) gives 1.3% + 4 lounge visits/quarter. If you travel, Regalia is better. For non-travelers, Millennia is excellent.

**Axis Atlas card — how many free flights can I earn in a year?**
At 5x EDGE miles on general spend: For ₹5 lakh spend = 25,000 EDGE miles. One IndiGo ticket (domestic) costs ~6,500 miles → ~3–4 free flights/year on ₹5L spend. Travel spend gives even more miles.

**HDFC Regalia vs Regalia Gold — which is better in 2026?**
Regalia Gold (2024 version) replaced Regalia. Gold version has slightly better reward rate and more lounge access. If you have old Regalia, upgrade to Regalia Gold — HDFC allows upgrades.
