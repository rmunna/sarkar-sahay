# SBI Mini Statement: Check Last 5 Transactions

Want to quickly check your recent SBI transactions? A mini statement gives you the **last 5 transactions** on your account — including deposits, withdrawals, and transfers. SBI provides multiple free ways to get your mini statement without visiting the branch.

---

## Method 1: Missed Call — 09223866666

The easiest way to get your SBI mini statement. No internet required.

### Steps:

1. Open the **dialer** on your phone
2. Call **09223866666** from your **registered mobile number**
3. The call will **disconnect automatically**
4. You will receive an **SMS** with your last **5 transactions** within a few seconds

**Example SMS:**
```
Your a/c XXXX1234:
06Mar CR 5,000.00
05Mar DR 1,200.00
04Mar CR 15,000.00
03Mar DR 500.00
02Mar DR 2,000.00
Bal: 25,300.00
```

**Note:** This service only works with your **registered mobile number**. If you need to check your [account balance](/guide/sbi-account-balance-check) only, call 09223766666 instead.

---

## Method 2: SMS — Send MSTMT

Get your mini statement by sending a simple SMS.

### Steps:

1. Open the **SMS/Messages** app
2. Type **MSTMT** and send to **09223866666**
3. You will receive a reply SMS with your **last 5 transactions**

**For specific account:** If you have multiple SBI accounts, type **MSTMT XXXX** (last 4 digits of account number) and send to 09223866666.

**Note:** Standard SMS charges may apply based on your telecom plan.

---

## Method 3: YONO SBI App

View detailed transaction history on your smartphone.

### Steps:

1. Open the **YONO SBI** app
2. Log in with your **credentials**
3. Tap on your **savings account** on the dashboard
4. Tap **"View Transactions"**
5. Your recent transactions are displayed — you can scroll to see more

**Advantage over SMS:** YONO shows more than 5 transactions and lets you filter by **date range**, **transaction type**, and download statements. See our [SBI passbook update guide](/guide/sbi-passbook-update-online) for downloading full statements.

---

## Method 4: SBI Net Banking

View detailed transaction history through the SBI internet banking portal.

### Steps:

1. Visit [https://www.onlinesbi.sbi/](https://www.onlinesbi.sbi/)
2. Log in with your **username, password, and OTP**
3. Go to **"Account Statement"** under **"My Accounts & Profile"**
4. Select your **account** and choose a short date range (e.g., last 7 days)
5. Click **"Submit"** to view recent transactions

---

## Method 5: ATM Mini Statement

Get a printed mini statement slip from any SBI ATM.

### Steps:

1. Insert your **SBI ATM/debit card**
2. Enter your **4-digit ATM PIN**
3. Select **"Mini Statement"** from the menu
4. The ATM will print a slip with your **last 5-10 transactions**
5. Collect the printed slip

**Note:**
- Mini statement at **SBI ATMs** is **free**
- At **other bank ATMs**, it counts as a non-financial transaction (5 free transactions per month at other bank ATMs, after which ₹20 + GST per transaction applies)

---

## Method 6: USSD Code *99#

Works on any phone without internet.

### Steps:

1. Dial **\*99#** from your **registered mobile number**
2. Select option **2** — "Mini Statement"
3. Enter bank short code: **SBI** or **SBIN**
4. Your last few transactions will be displayed on screen

**Note:** USSD charges ₹0.50 per session (charged by telecom provider). Display is limited by screen size on feature phones.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: What is the SBI mini statement number?

The SBI mini statement number is **09223866666**. Give a missed call from your registered mobile number to receive your last 5 transactions via SMS.

### Q2: What is the difference between SBI mini statement and account statement?

A **mini statement** shows only the last **5 transactions**, while an **account statement** shows all transactions for a selected date range. Mini statement is available via missed call/SMS; full statements require YONO app, net banking, or branch visit.

### Q3: Why am I not receiving SBI mini statement SMS?

Common reasons: (a) your **mobile number is not registered** with SBI, (b) **network delay** — wait a few minutes, (c) **DND (Do Not Disturb)** is blocking banking SMS — contact your telecom provider to whitelist banking SMS, (d) **SBI server issue** — try again later or call 1800-11-2211.

### Q4: Can I get SBI mini statement for the last 10 transactions?

The missed call and SMS methods show only the **last 5 transactions**. ATMs may show up to **10 transactions**. For more transactions, use the **YONO app** or **SBI net banking** where you can view months or years of history.

### Q5: Is SBI mini statement service free?

Yes, the missed call mini statement is **free**. SMS may incur standard carrier charges. ATM mini statement is free at SBI ATMs. USSD charges ₹0.50 per session.

### Q6: How do I register my mobile number for SBI mini statement?

Visit your nearest SBI branch with your **passbook and Aadhaar card**. Fill the mobile number registration form. Activation takes **1-2 working days**. See our [SBI mobile number change guide](/guide/sbi-mobile-number-change) for the full process.
