# FASTag Recharge Online — How to Buy, Recharge, Check Balance & Link to Vehicle

FASTag is India's electronic toll collection system that allows cashless payments at highway toll plazas. Since February 2021, FASTag has been **mandatory for all four-wheeled vehicles** in India. This guide covers everything — from buying a FASTag online to recharging it, checking your balance, linking it to your vehicle, and fixing common issues.

---

## What is FASTag?

FASTag is a prepaid RFID (Radio Frequency Identification) tag that is affixed to a vehicle's windshield. When you drive through a toll plaza, the tag is scanned automatically and the toll amount is deducted from your linked prepaid wallet or bank account.

**Key facts about FASTag:**

- Operated under **NETC (National Electronic Toll Collection)** by NPCI
- Managed by **IHMCL** (Indian Highways Management Company Limited) under NHAI
- Valid for **5 years** from date of issuance
- Works across **800+ toll plazas** on National Highways
- Vehicles without a valid FASTag are charged **double toll** at plazas
- Available for cars, buses, trucks, and commercial vehicles

---

## Why is FASTag Mandatory?

The Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH) made FASTag mandatory from **15 February 2021** for all four-wheeled vehicles. Here's why:

1. **Reduces congestion** — vehicles pass through toll plazas in seconds
2. **Cashless and transparent** — every transaction is digitally recorded
3. **Saves fuel** — no idling in long toll queues
4. **Double toll penalty** — vehicles without FASTag pay 2x the toll fee
5. **Required for vehicle fitness** — new vehicles come with FASTag pre-fitted

> **Note:** If you're buying a new vehicle, the dealer typically provides a FASTag. For existing vehicles, you need to buy one from an authorized bank or issuer.

## How to Buy FASTag Online

You can purchase a FASTag from any of the **30+ authorized issuer banks** and digital platforms. Here are the most popular options:

### Option 1: Buy from Bank Websites/Apps

1. Visit your bank's FASTag page (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, etc.)
2. Fill in vehicle details — registration number, vehicle class, RC details
3. Upload KYC documents (Aadhaar, PAN, vehicle RC)
4. Pay the issuance fee (₹100–₹500 depending on the bank)
5. FASTag is delivered to your address in 3–7 working days

You can also buy FASTag in person at any NHAI toll plaza or authorized bank branch. Carry your vehicle RC, Aadhaar/PAN, and passport-size photos.

## How to Recharge FASTag Online

FASTag works on a prepaid wallet model — you need to keep sufficient balance for toll deductions. Here's how to recharge:

### Method 1: Using the Issuing Bank's App

1. Open your bank's mobile app (SBI YONO, iMobile, HDFC Mobile Banking, etc.)
2. Navigate to **FASTag** section
3. Select **"Recharge"** or **"Top Up"**
4. Enter the recharge amount (minimum ₹100)
5. Confirm payment via UPI PIN, net banking, or debit card

### Method 2: Using Paytm / PhonePe / Google Pay

1. Open the app and search for **"FASTag Recharge"**
2. Enter your **vehicle number** or **FASTag ID**
3. Select the issuing bank
4. Enter recharge amount
5. Pay using UPI, wallet, or debit/credit card

### Method 3: Using the MyFASTag App (IHMCL)

1. Download **MyFASTag** app from Play Store or App Store
2. Register with your mobile number linked to FASTag
3. Go to **"Recharge"**
4. Enter amount and pay via UPI or net banking

### Method 4: Auto-Recharge (Recommended)

Most banks offer **auto-top-up** — when your balance falls below a threshold (e.g., ₹100), the bank automatically recharges from your linked bank account. Enable this in your bank's FASTag settings to avoid toll failures.

### Method 5: Via NHAI Website

1. Visit **ihmcl.co.in**
2. Log in with your registered mobile number
3. Select your FASTag and recharge using available payment methods

## How to Link FASTag to Vehicle RC

FASTag must be linked to your vehicle's Registration Certificate (RC). Here's how:

### For New FASTag Purchase

When you buy a FASTag online, you provide your vehicle registration number during the application. The tag is automatically linked to your vehicle RC.

### For Transferring FASTag to a New Vehicle

If you've sold your old vehicle or bought a new one, you need to update the FASTag:

1. **Contact your issuing bank** — call customer care or visit a branch
2. Provide the **new vehicle RC** copy
3. Submit a **tag replacement request** (a new FASTag sticker will be issued)
4. The old tag will be deactivated

> **Important:** One FASTag = One Vehicle. You cannot use the same FASTag on multiple vehicles. If you own multiple vehicles, buy a separate FASTag for each.

### Documents Needed for Linking

- Vehicle Registration Certificate (RC) copy
- Vehicle owner's Aadhaar card
- PAN card (for KYC)
- Passport-size photograph
- Mobile number linked to the FASTag account

For related vehicle documentation, see our guide on [vehicle RC transfer online](/guide/vehicle-rc-transfer-online).

## KYC Requirements for FASTag

FASTag KYC is mandatory under RBI norms. Without valid KYC, your FASTag may be **deactivated or blacklisted**.

### Documents Required

- **Identity proof:** Aadhaar card, PAN card, Voter ID, or Passport
- **Address proof:** Aadhaar, utility bill, or bank statement
- **Vehicle proof:** Registration Certificate (RC)
- **Photograph:** Passport-size photo of vehicle owner

### How to Complete KYC

1. **Online:** Upload documents through the issuing bank's portal or app
2. **At bank branch:** Visit the nearest branch of your FASTag issuer with original documents
3. **At toll plaza:** Some plazas have KYC update counters

### Minimum KYC vs Full KYC

- **Minimum KYC:** Allows wallet balance up to ₹10,000. Requires basic ID verification.
- **Full KYC:** No wallet limit restrictions. Requires Aadhaar-based verification (biometric or OTP).

> **Deadline alert:** RBI periodically mandates KYC completion deadlines. FASTag accounts without full KYC may be suspended. Check with your issuer for current deadlines.

## FASTag Not Working at Toll — What to Do

If your FASTag fails at a toll plaza, here's a step-by-step troubleshooting guide:

### Common Reasons for Failure

1. **Insufficient balance** — most common reason
2. **Tag not readable** — damaged, faded, or improperly placed
3. **KYC incomplete** — tag deactivated by the bank
4. **Blacklisted tag** — due to unpaid dues or fraud
5. **Vehicle mismatch** — FASTag linked to a different vehicle
6. **Technical glitch** — scanner malfunction at toll plaza

### Immediate Steps

1. **Pay in cash** at the toll (you may be charged double toll)
2. **Keep the receipt** — you can claim a refund if the failure wasn't your fault
3. **Check your balance** via app or SMS immediately
4. **Recharge** if balance is low

### Long-Term Fixes

- **Reposition the tag** — it should be on the centre of the windshield, behind the rear-view mirror
- **Replace damaged tags** — contact your issuing bank for a replacement
- **Complete KYC** — if your tag was deactivated due to pending KYC
- **Update vehicle details** — ensure RC number matches the tag registration

### File a Complaint

If the toll deduction was incorrect or the tag was wrongly charged:

1. Call your issuing bank's FASTag helpline
2. Use the **MyFASTag app** → "Raise Grievance"
3. Email **ihmcl.co.in** support
4. File a complaint on the **NHAI toll-free number: 1033**

## Blacklisted FASTag — How to Fix

A blacklisted FASTag won't work at any toll plaza. Here's why it happens and how to fix it:

### Why FASTag Gets Blacklisted

- **Insufficient balance** for an extended period
- **KYC not completed** within the deadline
- **Duplicate or fraudulent** tag detected
- **Vehicle sold** but FASTag not transferred or closed
- **Bank account closed** that was linked to the FASTag

### How to Reactivate a Blacklisted FASTag

1. **Check status** — call your issuing bank or check on the MyFASTag app
2. **Recharge your wallet** — if blacklisted due to low balance, adding funds may auto-reactivate it
3. **Complete KYC** — if blacklisted due to pending KYC, submit your documents
4. **Visit the bank branch** — for complex issues, visit your issuing bank with RC, Aadhaar, and PAN
5. **Request a new FASTag** — if the old one can't be reactivated, apply for a fresh tag (the old one will be permanently deactivated)

### How to Check if Your FASTag is Blacklisted

- Open the **MyFASTag app** → Check tag status
- Call the **NHAI helpline: 1033**
- Contact your issuing bank's customer care

> **Pro tip:** Enable auto-recharge to prevent your FASTag from getting blacklisted due to low balance.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Is FASTag mandatory for all vehicles?

Yes, FASTag is mandatory for all four-wheeled vehicles since February 2021. Vehicles without a valid FASTag are charged double toll at NHAI plazas.

### 2. How much does a FASTag cost?

The tag itself costs ₹100–₹150. Banks charge a security deposit of ₹150–₹200 (refundable on tag closure). Total cost is typically ₹250–₹500 depending on the issuer.

### 3. Can I use one FASTag for multiple vehicles?

No. Each FASTag is linked to a single vehicle registration number. You need a separate FASTag for each vehicle.

### 4. What is the minimum recharge amount for FASTag?

Most banks and apps allow a minimum recharge of ₹100. There is no maximum limit for full-KYC wallets.

### 5. How long is a FASTag valid?

FASTag is valid for **5 years** from the date of issuance. After expiry, you need to buy a new one.

### 6. Can I recharge FASTag using UPI?

Yes. You can recharge FASTag using UPI through Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, your bank app, or the MyFASTag app.

### 7. What happens if FASTag balance is zero at the toll?

You'll need to pay cash and may be charged **double the toll fee**. The toll plaza will also record the event, and repeated failures may lead to blacklisting.

### 8. How do I transfer FASTag when selling my vehicle?

Contact your issuing bank to close your FASTag account. The new owner should purchase a new FASTag for the vehicle. You'll receive your security deposit refund.

### 9. Can I get a refund of the FASTag security deposit?

Yes. When you close your FASTag account, the security deposit and any remaining wallet balance are refunded to your linked bank account.

### 10. Is FASTag required for two-wheelers?

Currently, FASTag is **not mandatory for two-wheelers**. However, NHAI has piloted FASTag for bikes in some regions.

### 11. How do I replace a damaged FASTag?

Contact your issuing bank's customer care. They will deactivate the old tag and issue a replacement. You'll need to pay the tag issuance fee again but your wallet balance will be transferred.

### 12. Can I buy FASTag without a bank account?

Yes. Paytm and some prepaid wallet-based issuers allow you to buy and use FASTag with just KYC documents, without a traditional bank account.
