# How to Check PAN Card Status Online — Track PAN Application

Applied for a new PAN card or made a correction? Want to know if your PAN is active or linked with Aadhaar? This guide covers **every method** to check your PAN card status online — whether you applied through NSDL (Protean) or UTIITSL.

## Method 1: Track PAN Application via NSDL / Protean

If you applied for a PAN card through **Protean (formerly NSDL)**, you can track your application using the **15-digit acknowledgment number** you received after submitting the form.

### Steps to Check

1. Visit the Protean PAN status tracking page: [https://tin.tin.nsdl.com/pantan/StatusTrack.html](https://tin.tin.nsdl.com/pantan/StatusTrack.html)
2. Select **PAN - New/Change Request** from the application type dropdown
3. Enter your **15-digit acknowledgment number** (starts with your application date, e.g., `080220261234567`)
4. Enter the **captcha** code shown on the screen
5. Click **Submit**

### What the Status Means

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **PAN application is under process** | Your form is being reviewed |
| **PAN card is printed and dispatched** | Card has been sent by post — usually arrives in 15–20 days |
| **PAN is allotted** | Your PAN number has been generated but card is not yet dispatched |
| **Application returned/rejected** | Some issue with your documents or form — you need to reapply |
| **PAN card dispatched to the applicant** | Card is on its way; track via the Speed Post number shown |

> **Tip:** If the status says "dispatched", note the **Speed Post tracking number** and track it on [India Post](https://www.indiapost.gov.in/VAS/Pages/trackconsignment.aspx).

## Method 2: Track PAN Application via UTIITSL

If you applied through **UTIITSL**, use the **application coupon number** to check your status.

### Steps to Check

1. Visit: [https://www.pan.utiitsl.com/PAN/mainform.html](https://www.pan.utiitsl.com/PAN/mainform.html)
2. Click on **Track PAN Application** or **PAN Card Status**
3. Enter your **application coupon number** (provided at the time of application)
4. Enter your **date of birth**
5. Enter the **captcha** and click **Submit**

You will see the current processing status of your PAN application — whether it is under process, printed, or dispatched.

## Method 3: Know Your PAN — Find PAN Number by Name and Date of Birth

Forgot or lost your PAN number? You can find it using your **name, date of birth, and mobile number** on the Income Tax e-Filing portal.

### Steps to Find Your PAN

1. Visit: [https://eportal.incometax.gov.in/iec/foservices/#/pre-login/knowYourPAN](https://eportal.incometax.gov.in/iec/foservices/#/pre-login/knowYourPAN)
2. Enter your **PAN type** (Individual, Company, etc.)
3. Fill in your **full name** (as per PAN records), **date of birth**, and **mobile number**
4. Click **Submit** — an **OTP** will be sent to your registered mobile
5. Enter the OTP to view your **PAN number** on screen

> **Note:** Your mobile number must be registered with the Income Tax department or linked with Aadhaar for OTP verification.

## Method 4: Verify PAN Details Online

You can verify whether a PAN number is valid and check the details (name, status) associated with it.

### Steps to Verify

1. Visit: [https://eportal.incometax.gov.in/iec/foservices/#/pre-login/verifyYourPAN](https://eportal.incometax.gov.in/iec/foservices/#/pre-login/verifyYourPAN)
2. Enter the **PAN number**, **full name**, **date of birth**, and **mobile number**
3. Submit and verify via OTP
4. The system will confirm if the PAN is **valid** and show the **status** (Active / Inactive)

This is useful to confirm PAN details before filing returns or submitting it for KYC.

## Method 5: Check if PAN is Active or Inoperative (Deactivated)

As per government rules, if your PAN is **not linked with Aadhaar**, it may have become **inoperative** (deactivated). An inoperative PAN cannot be used for financial transactions, ITR filing, or KYC.

### How to Check

1. Visit: [https://eportal.incometax.gov.in/iec/foservices/#/pre-login/verifyYourPAN](https://eportal.incometax.gov.in/iec/foservices/#/pre-login/verifyYourPAN)
2. Enter your PAN details and verify via OTP
3. The result will show your PAN status as **Active** or **Inoperative**

### What Does "Inoperative PAN" Mean?

If your PAN is inoperative:
- You **cannot file income tax returns**
- **TDS will be deducted at a higher rate** (as per Section 206AA)
- You **cannot use it for bank account opening, mutual fund KYC**, or other financial purposes
- Existing bank accounts and demat accounts may face restrictions

## Method 6: Check PAN-Aadhaar Link Status

To keep your PAN active, it must be linked with your Aadhaar number. Here's how to check the link status:

### Steps to Check

1. Visit: [https://eportal.incometax.gov.in/iec/foservices/#/pre-login/link-aadhaar-status](https://eportal.incometax.gov.in/iec/foservices/#/pre-login/link-aadhaar-status)
2. Enter your **PAN number** and **Aadhaar number**
3. Click **View Link Aadhaar Status**

### Possible Results

- **Your PAN is linked with Aadhaar** — No action needed
- **Your PAN is not linked with Aadhaar** — You need to link it immediately to avoid PAN becoming inoperative

👉 **Need to link?** Follow our step-by-step guide: [How to Link Aadhaar with PAN](/guide/aadhaar-pan-link)

## Method 7: How to Reactivate an Inoperative PAN

If your PAN has become inoperative because it was not linked with Aadhaar, you can reactivate it by completing the linking process with a late fee.

### Steps to Reactivate

1. **Pay the late fee of ₹1,000** through the [e-Pay Tax portal](https://eportal.incometax.gov.in/iec/foservices/#/pre-login/e-pay-tax-prelogin)
   - Select challan **"AY 2025-26"** → Major Head **0021** → Minor Head **500** → Fee type **Others**
   - Complete payment via net banking, debit card, or UPI
2. **Link Aadhaar with PAN** on the Income Tax portal after payment
3. Your PAN will be **reactivated within 30 days** of successful Aadhaar linking

> **Important:** Keep the challan receipt safe. The reactivation process may take up to 30 days from the date of linking.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### How long does it take to get a PAN card after applying online?

After successful application, your PAN number is usually allotted within **2–3 working days**. The physical PAN card is dispatched within **15–20 working days** to your address via Speed Post.

### Can I check PAN card status without acknowledgment number?

If you applied through **NSDL/Protean**, the acknowledgment number is required. However, you can use the **"Know Your PAN"** feature on the Income Tax portal to find your PAN number using your name, date of birth, and mobile number.

### My PAN application was rejected. What should I do?

Check the rejection reason in the email sent by NSDL/UTIITSL. Common reasons include unclear documents, name mismatch, or incomplete form. Correct the issues and [apply again for a new PAN card](/guide/pan-card-apply-online).

### How do I check if my PAN card is active or deactivated?

Visit the **Verify Your PAN** page on the Income Tax portal. Enter your PAN, name, date of birth, and verify via OTP. The result will show whether your PAN is **Active** or **Inoperative**.

### What happens if I don't link PAN with Aadhaar?

Your PAN will become **inoperative**. You won't be able to file ITR, and TDS will be deducted at a higher rate on your income. To reactivate, link Aadhaar with PAN after paying a ₹1,000 late fee.

### Can I track my PAN card delivery after dispatch?

Yes. Once your PAN card status shows "Dispatched", you will get a **Speed Post tracking number**. Use it on the [India Post tracking website](https://www.indiapost.gov.in/VAS/Pages/trackconsignment.aspx) to check delivery status.

### How to find PAN number of a company or firm?

Use the **"Know Your PAN"** feature on the Income Tax portal. Select the entity type (Company, Firm, etc.), enter the name and date of incorporation, and verify via OTP to get the PAN number.
