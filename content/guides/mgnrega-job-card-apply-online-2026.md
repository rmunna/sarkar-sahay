# MGNREGA Job Card 2026 — Apply, Check Status & Track Payment

**MGNREGA (Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act)** guarantees **100 days of paid employment per year** to every rural household in India. The wage ranges from **₹200–₹375/day** depending on your state.

> **Quick answer:** Apply for a job card at your **Gram Panchayat office** (most common) or check if your state has an online portal. Check your job card status and payment at [nreganet.nic.in](https://nreganet.nic.in) → Reports → Job Card.

## Who is Eligible for MGNREGA?

- Must be an **adult member** (18+ years) of a **rural household**
- Must be willing to do **unskilled manual work**
- Must be a resident of the Gram Panchayat where applying
- **No income limit** — any rural family can apply
- Women are given priority (at least 1/3 of MGNREGA workforce must be women)

## How to Apply for MGNREGA Job Card

### Method 1: Gram Panchayat Office (Most Common)

**Step 1:** Visit your local **Gram Panchayat** office

**Step 2:** Ask for **MGNREGA Job Card Registration Form** (Form No. 1)

**Step 3:** Fill in: name, age, address, household members who want work, bank account

**Step 4:** Attach Aadhaar copies and photographs for each adult member applying

**Step 5:** Submit to the Gram Panchayat Secretary

**Step 6:** Within **15 days**, the Gram Panchayat must issue a job card

**Step 7:** Once work is available, you'll be notified — or demand work directly at the GP

### Method 2: Online Application (State-Specific)

Some states have online job card registration. Check your state's MGNREGA portal:

| State | Online Portal |
|---|---|
| Andhra Pradesh | apnrega.ap.gov.in |
| Telangana | nrega.telangana.gov.in |
| Karnataka | mgnregs.karnataka.gov.in |
| Rajasthan | rajnrega.rajasthan.gov.in |
| UP | nrega.up.nic.in |

For most states: apply at the Gram Panchayat directly.

## How to Check Job Card Status Online

**Step 1:** Go to [nreganet.nic.in](https://nreganet.nic.in)

**Step 2:** Click **"Reports"** → **"Job Card"**

**Step 3:** Select your **State → District → Block → Gram Panchayat**

**Step 4:** Search by name or click "Job Card Register" — find your name in the list

**Step 5:** Click on your job card number to see all details (work history, wages, days worked)

---

## How to Check MGNREGA Payment Status

**Step 1:** Go to [nreganet.nic.in](https://nreganet.nic.in) → **"Reports"** → **"Financial"**

**Step 2:** Or visit [pfms.nic.in](https://pfms.nic.in) → **"Know Your Payment"**

**Step 3:** Enter your job card number or Aadhaar number

Wages must be paid within **15 days** of completing work. If delayed:
- Contact your Gram Panchayat
- File a complaint at the Block Development Office
- Or call **1800-110-707** (MGNREGA helpline, toll-free)

---

## How to Demand Work Under MGNREGA

You have the **right to demand work** — the GP must provide employment within 15 days of demand or pay unemployment allowance.

**Step 1:** Submit a written application to your **Gram Panchayat** requesting work (minimum 14 days of work must be demanded at a time)

**Step 2:** GP must give a **dated receipt** of your demand

**Step 3:** If work is not provided within **15 days**, you are entitled to unemployment allowance (25% of wage for first 30 days, 50% thereafter)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can urban residents get MGNREGA job cards?**
No — MGNREGA is only for **rural areas** (villages and Gram Panchayats). Urban residents have NULM/urban employment schemes.

**How many family members can work under one job card?**
All adult members of the household can work — but the job card is one per household. Each member can get up to 100 days of work per year.

**My MGNREGA wages haven't come for 2 months. What to do?**
1. Check payment status at nreganet.nic.in
2. Ensure Aadhaar is linked to your bank account (visit bank if not)
3. File grievance at the Block Program Officer
4. Call helpline: **1800-110-707**

**Can I get work in a different village than where my job card is?**
You can request work in a different GP within the same district, but priority is given to your own GP's work.
