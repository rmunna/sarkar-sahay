## What is Mukhyamantri Parivar Samridhi Yojana (MMPSY)?

**Mukhyamantri Parivar Samridhi Yojana (MMPSY)** is a Haryana government scheme that provides **₹6,000 per year** (₹500/month) to BPL and low-income families. The benefit is used to fund:

- **Pension** for family members (PM-SYM, PM-LVM, PMMVY)
- **Life and accident insurance** premiums (PM Jeevan Jyoti, PM Suraksha Bima)
- **Direct cash** to the family

The scheme is bundled with **Parivar Pehchaan Patra (PPP)** — Haryana's family ID — and auto-enrolls eligible families.

---

## Key Benefits

| Component | Benefit |
|-----------|---------|
| Annual cash/benefit | ₹6,000/year per family |
| PM Jeevan Jyoti Bima | ₹2 lakh life insurance |
| PM Suraksha Bima | ₹2 lakh accident insurance |
| PM-SYM pension fund | Pension contribution for 18–40 year workers |
| PM-LVM (traders) | Pension contribution for trader members |
| PMMVY | Maternity benefit for pregnant women |

The ₹6,000 is essentially used to pay insurance premiums and pension contributions for all eligible schemes — family members get coverage without any out-of-pocket expense.

---

## Who is Eligible?

**You ARE eligible if:**
- Your family is a **Haryana resident** with a **Parivar Pehchaan Patra (PPP)**
- Your family's **annual income is ₹1.80 lakh or less**
- Your family has **5 acres of land or less**
- You are **not a government employee**

---

## How to Get MMPSY Benefits

The scheme uses **auto-enrollment** through the Parivar Pehchaan Patra system:

### Step 1 — Ensure PPP is Made

Get your **Parivar Pehchaan Patra** (Haryana family ID) — visit any CSC/Atal Sewa Kendra.

### Step 2 — Income Verification in PPP

Your family's income details in the PPP database are verified. Families with annual income ≤ ₹1.80 lakh and land ≤ 5 acres are automatically identified as eligible.

### Step 3 — Scheme Enrollment

Eligible families are auto-enrolled into the sub-schemes:
- PM Jeevan Jyoti Bima: ₹330/year premium deducted, ₹2L life cover
- PM Suraksha Bima: ₹12/year premium deducted, ₹2L accident cover
- PM-SYM: pension contributions for working-age members (18–40 years)

### Step 4 — Remaining Cash Transfer

After premium deductions, remaining ₹6,000 amount (or portion) may be credited directly to the family's account — check with Saral Haryana for current status.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. We are a 5-member family and our income is ₹1.5 lakh. Will we get ₹6,000 per person?**

No. The ₹6,000 is **per family** per year, not per member. It covers insurance premiums and pension contributions for eligible family members.

**2. I am 35 years old and a laborer. Do I get a pension fund under MMPSY?**

Yes. Working-age members (18–40 years) in eligible MMPSY families are enrolled under PM-SYM (₹3,000/month pension at age 60). The government funds your contribution.

**3. Our family income is ₹2.5 lakh. Are we excluded?**

Yes. The income limit is ₹1.80 lakh/year. Families above this threshold are not covered under MMPSY.

**4. We have 7 acres of land. Are we excluded?**

Yes. Land holding above 5 acres is an exclusion criterion.

**5. I am a widow with no income. Am I automatically covered?**

If you have a PPP and your family income is below ₹1.80 lakh, you should be auto-enrolled. Visit the Atal Sewa Kendra to verify your PPP income data and enrollment status.
