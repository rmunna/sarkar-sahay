## What is Haryana Old Age Samman Allowance?

The Old Age Samman Allowance is a social security pension scheme by the Government of Haryana that provides monthly financial assistance to senior citizens of the state. Under this scheme, elderly residents aged 60 years and above who meet the income criteria receive a monthly pension directly in their bank accounts.

The scheme is managed by the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment, Haryana, and aims to ensure financial independence and dignity for senior citizens who may not have a regular source of income.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Age:** Must be 60 years or above
- **Residence:** Must be a permanent resident of Haryana
- **Income limit:** Annual family income should not exceed ₹2,00,000 (₹2 lakh)
- **Parivar Pehchan Patra:** Must have a valid Haryana Family ID (see our [Parivar Pehchan Patra guide](/guide/haryana-family-id-parivar-pehchan-patra))
- **Not receiving** any other government pension (central or state) for the same purpose
- **Both men and women** are eligible

### Pension Amount

| Age Group | Monthly Pension |
|---|---|
| 60 years and above | ₹2,750 per month |

*Note: The pension amount is revised periodically by the state government. The above amount is as per the latest available information.*

## Documents Required

### Mandatory Documents
- **Aadhaar Card** (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- **Parivar Pehchan Patra** (Haryana Family ID) — mandatory
- **Age proof** — Aadhaar card, voter ID, birth certificate, or school leaving certificate
- **Bank account details** — passbook or cancelled cheque (account must be in applicant's name)
- **Passport-size photograph**
- **Address proof** — ration card, voter ID, or utility bill

### Additional Documents
- **Income certificate** — issued by the Tehsildar/SDM (if not auto-verified via Family ID)
- **BPL card** (if applicable)

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Online Process (via Saral Haryana / Parivar Pehchan Patra)

1. **Visit** the Saral Haryana portal at [saralharyana.gov.in](https://saralharyana.gov.in)
2. **Register/Login** using your mobile number and OTP
3. **Search for** "Old Age Samman Allowance" in the services list
4. **Your details will auto-populate** from the Parivar Pehchan Patra database
5. **Verify your personal information** — name, date of birth, address, family income
6. **Enter bank account details** (IFSC code and account number)
7. **Upload required documents** — photo, Aadhaar, age proof, bank passbook
8. **Review and submit** the application
9. **Note the Service Request Number** for tracking
10. **Application will be verified** by the Sarpanch/MC and then the BDPO/SDM

### Offline Process

1. **Visit your nearest** Atal Seva Kendra, CSC, or the office of the Block Development & Panchayat Officer (BDPO)
2. **Collect the pension application form** or download it from the Saral portal
3. **Fill the form** with your personal details, age, income, and bank information
4. **Attach self-attested copies** of all required documents
5. **Submit the form** to the BDPO/SDM office or the Atal Seva Kendra
6. **Get acknowledgement receipt** with the reference number
7. **Your application will be verified** at the block/district level before approval

### Auto-Identification via Family ID

With the Parivar Pehchan Patra system, many eligible citizens are **auto-identified** based on their Family ID data (age and income). If you receive a notification about your eligibility, you can directly confirm your details and provide bank information to start receiving the pension.

## Processing Time

- **Verification:** 30–60 days after submission
- **First pension disbursement:** Within 1–2 months after approval
- **Pension credited:** Monthly, directly to bank account via DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer)

## Important Tips

1. **Get your Parivar Pehchan Patra first** — it's mandatory for pension applications in Haryana. Apply for it at [meraparivar.haryana.gov.in](https://meraparivar.haryana.gov.in)
2. **Link your Aadhaar to your bank account** — pension is disbursed via DBT, so Aadhaar-bank linking is essential (see our [Aadhaar linking guide](/guide/aadhaar-linking-guide))
3. **Keep your bank account active** — dormant accounts may cause payment failures; do at least one transaction every 6 months
4. **Update family income** in PPP if it changes — if your family income rises above ₹2 lakh, you may become ineligible
5. **Track your application** on Saral Haryana using your Service Request Number

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. What is the current pension amount under Haryana Old Age Samman Allowance?
The current pension amount is ₹2,750 per month for all eligible senior citizens aged 60 and above. This amount is subject to revision by the state government.

### Q2. Is Parivar Pehchan Patra mandatory for old age pension in Haryana?
Yes, the Haryana Family ID (Parivar Pehchan Patra) is mandatory for applying for the Old Age Samman Allowance. Your eligibility is verified through the Family ID database.

### Q3. Can I receive both central and state old age pension?
No. If you are already receiving old age pension from the central government (e.g., IGNOAPS under NSAP), you are generally not eligible for the state Samman Allowance for the same purpose. However, check the latest guidelines as rules may vary.

### Q4. How do I check the status of my pension application?
Visit [saralharyana.gov.in](https://saralharyana.gov.in), go to "Track Application," and enter your Service Request Number to check the current status.

### Q5. What if my pension payment is delayed or stopped?
Common reasons include Aadhaar-bank linking issues, inactive bank account, or changes in Family ID data. Visit your nearest BDPO office or Atal Seva Kendra to resolve the issue. Ensure your Aadhaar is linked to your bank account.

### Q6. Can I apply on behalf of an elderly family member?
Yes. A family member or caretaker can submit the application on behalf of the senior citizen, either online or at a CSC. However, the pension will be credited only to the applicant's own bank account.

### Q7. Is there a separate pension for widows in Haryana?
Yes. Haryana also has a Widow & Destitute Women Pension scheme with similar eligibility criteria. You can apply through the same Saral portal.
