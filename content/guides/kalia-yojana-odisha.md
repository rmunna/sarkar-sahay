## What is KALIA Yojana?

KALIA (Krushak Assistance for Livelihood and Income Augmentation) is a flagship agricultural welfare scheme launched by the Government of Odisha in December 2018. The scheme provides direct financial assistance to small and marginal farmers, landless agricultural households, and vulnerable agricultural families to support their livelihood and improve farm productivity.

Unlike loan waiver schemes, KALIA focuses on empowering farmers with direct financial support for cultivation, livelihood, and life insurance coverage. The scheme has benefited over **50 lakh farmer families** across Odisha since its launch.

## Key Benefits

| Component | Benefit Amount | Frequency |
|-----------|---------------|-----------|
| Support for Cultivation | ₹25,000 per family | Over 5 crop seasons |
| Livelihood Support (Landless) | ₹12,500 per family | For allied activities |
| Life Insurance | ₹2,00,000 cover | Annual premium paid by govt |
| Personal Accident Cover | ₹2,00,000 | For age group 18-50 |
| Financial Assistance (Vulnerable) | ₹10,000 per year | For elderly/disabled farmers |

## Who is Eligible?

### For Cultivation Support (₹25,000)
- **Small and marginal farmers** of Odisha
- Must own cultivable land
- Family income should not exceed the prescribed limit
- Must not be an income tax payer

### For Livelihood Support (₹12,500)
- **Landless agricultural labourers** in Odisha
- Engaged in activities like goat rearing, mushroom cultivation, beekeeping, fishery, etc.
- Must be below the prescribed income limit

### For Vulnerable Section Support (₹10,000/year)
- **Old, disabled, or disease-affected farmers** who cannot cultivate
- Age 60 years and above or permanently disabled

### Who is NOT Eligible
- Government employees (current or retired)
- Income tax payers
- Professionals like doctors, engineers, lawyers, architects
- Holders of Constitutional posts
- Anyone receiving pension from government (except under social security)

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar Card** (mandatory for DBT)
- **Land ownership documents** (ROR/Patta for farmer category)
- **Bank account details** (Aadhaar-linked)
- **Ration Card**
- **Voter ID Card**
- **Passport-size photographs**
- **Mobile number** linked to Aadhaar

## How to Apply for KALIA Yojana

### Online Process

1. Visit the **KALIA Portal** at [kalia.odisha.gov.in](https://kalia.odisha.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Online Grievance Application Form"** or **"New Application"**
3. Enter your **Aadhaar number** for verification
4. Fill in personal details — name, father's name, address, district, block, GP
5. Enter **land details** (for farmer category) or **livelihood activity** (for landless category)
6. Upload required documents
7. Enter bank account details (must be Aadhaar-linked)
8. Submit the application and note the reference number
9. Track application status on the portal

### Offline Process

1. Visit the **nearest Gram Panchayat office** or **Block Development Office**
2. Collect the KALIA application form
3. Fill in all details accurately
4. Attach self-attested copies of required documents
5. Submit to the Gram Panchayat or Block office
6. Collect the acknowledgement receipt
7. The Panchayat-level committee will verify your application

### How to Check KALIA Beneficiary List

1. Go to [kalia.odisha.gov.in](https://kalia.odisha.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Beneficiary List"**
3. Select your **District → Block → GP**
4. Search by **name or Aadhaar number**
5. View your beneficiary status and payment details

## Payment Details

- Payments are made through **Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)** to Aadhaar-linked bank accounts
- Cultivation support of ₹25,000 is paid in **5 installments of ₹5,000** over crop seasons (Kharif and Rabi)
- Life insurance premium of ₹330/year (for ₹2 lakh cover) is paid by the state government under PMJJBY
- Accident insurance premium of ₹12/year (for ₹2 lakh cover) is paid under PMSBY

## Processing Time

- New applications: **30-60 days** for verification and approval
- Payment disbursement: Within **15 days** of approval, subject to government schedule
- Beneficiary list updates happen before each crop season

## Important Tips

1. **Ensure Aadhaar-bank linking** — Payments are only made through Aadhaar-enabled DBT. See our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) for help
2. **Keep land records updated** — Your ROR (Record of Rights) must be current for farmer category eligibility
3. **File grievances online** — If you're eligible but not in the list, use the grievance portal on the KALIA website
4. **Don't pay middlemen** — KALIA registration is free; never pay anyone for inclusion in the beneficiary list
5. **Check status regularly** — Visit the portal before each crop season to confirm your payment status

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. What is the full form of KALIA?
KALIA stands for **Krushak Assistance for Livelihood and Income Augmentation**. It is an Odisha state government scheme for farmers.

### Q2. How much money do farmers get under KALIA?
Small and marginal farmers receive ₹25,000 over 5 crop seasons (₹5,000 per season). Landless agricultural households get ₹12,500 for livelihood activities.

### Q3. Can landless labourers get KALIA benefits?
Yes. Landless agricultural labourers are eligible for ₹12,500 livelihood support for allied activities like goat rearing, mushroom cultivation, fishery, etc.

### Q4. How can I check if my name is in the KALIA list?
Visit [kalia.odisha.gov.in](https://kalia.odisha.gov.in), click on "Beneficiary List", select your district, block, and GP, then search by name or Aadhaar number.

### Q5. Is KALIA scheme still active in 2025-26?
The KALIA scheme continues to operate in Odisha. However, with the change in government in 2024, some modifications may be made. Check the official portal for the latest updates.

### Q6. Can both husband and wife apply separately?
No. KALIA benefits are provided per family, not per individual. Only one member per family can be a beneficiary.

### Q7. What if I was wrongly excluded from KALIA?
You can file a grievance through the online portal at kalia.odisha.gov.in or visit your Gram Panchayat office to submit a written complaint.
