# Aadhaar Card Reprint Online: Download e-Aadhaar & Order PVC Card

Lost your Aadhaar card or need a fresh copy? You can download your e-Aadhaar PDF instantly for free or order a PVC Aadhaar card online for just ₹50. Both options are available through the official UIDAI portal. This guide covers both methods step by step.

## What is Aadhaar Reprint?

Aadhaar reprint allows you to get a new copy of your Aadhaar card without visiting any centre. UIDAI provides two options:

- **e-Aadhaar (Free)** — Download a digitally signed PDF of your Aadhaar from the UIDAI portal. It is legally valid and accepted everywhere.
- **PVC Aadhaar Card (₹50)** — Order a durable, wallet-sized plastic card with a QR code. Delivered to your registered address by India Post.

Both options require your Aadhaar number or Enrolment ID (EID) and a registered mobile number for OTP verification.

---

## How to Download e-Aadhaar PDF (Free)

### Method 1: Via myAadhaar Portal

1. Visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/)
2. Click **Login** and enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number
3. Enter the OTP received on your registered mobile number
4. After login, click **Download Aadhaar** from the dashboard
5. Select Aadhaar type — choose **Aadhaar** (full) or **Masked Aadhaar** (last 4 digits visible)
6. Click **Download** — the PDF will be saved to your device
7. Open the PDF using the password: **first 4 letters of your name in CAPITALS + year of birth** (e.g., RAJA1990)

### Method 2: Via eaadhaar.uidai.gov.in

1. Visit [eaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://eaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/)
2. Select **Aadhaar Number** or **Enrolment ID (EID)**
3. Enter the number, captcha, and click **Send OTP**
4. Enter OTP and click **Download Aadhaar**
5. Open the PDF with the same password format as above

**Note:** You can download e-Aadhaar unlimited times. There is no fee.

## How to Order PVC Aadhaar Card Online (₹50)

The PVC Aadhaar card is a durable plastic card similar to a debit card. It contains your photo, details, and a secure QR code.

### Steps to Order:

1. Visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/)
2. Click **Login** with your Aadhaar number and OTP
3. From the dashboard, select **Order Aadhaar Reprint**
4. Verify your details (name, address, photo) shown on screen
5. Click **Make Payment** — pay ₹50 (inclusive of GST and speed post charges)
6. Payment options: Credit card, debit card, net banking, or UPI
7. After successful payment, you will receive a **Service Request Number (SRN)**
8. Save the SRN to track your order status

### What You Get:

- A PVC card with your photo, name, DOB, gender, and address
- Secure QR code for offline verification
- Delivered via India Post Speed Post to your Aadhaar-registered address

## How to Track PVC Aadhaar Card Order

1. Visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/)
2. Login with Aadhaar number and OTP
3. Click **Check Aadhaar PVC Card Status** or **Track Order**
4. Enter your **SRN (Service Request Number)** received during order
5. The status will show: Processing → Dispatched → Delivered

You can also track using India Post tracking once the card is dispatched.

## Important Tips

1. **e-Aadhaar is equally valid** — e-Aadhaar PDF is legally accepted everywhere as proof of identity and address, just like the physical card.
2. **Mobile number must be registered** — Both download and PVC order require OTP on your Aadhaar-linked mobile. If your mobile is not linked, visit an [Aadhaar enrolment centre](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) first.
3. **Address must be correct** — The PVC card is delivered to your Aadhaar-registered address. [Update your address](/guide/aadhaar-address-change-online) before ordering if it has changed.
4. **Keep the SRN safe** — You need the Service Request Number to track your PVC card order.
5. **Masked Aadhaar for safety** — Download the masked version (only last 4 digits visible) when sharing with private entities for better privacy.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Is e-Aadhaar valid as an official document?
Yes. The e-Aadhaar PDF is digitally signed by UIDAI and is legally valid. It is accepted by all government and private agencies as proof of identity and address.

### Q2. What is the password to open e-Aadhaar PDF?
The password is the first 4 letters of your name in CAPITAL letters followed by your year of birth. For example, if your name is Rajesh and birth year is 1990, the password is RAJE1990.

### Q3. Can I order a PVC Aadhaar card without a registered mobile number?
No. OTP verification on your registered mobile is mandatory. If your mobile is not linked to Aadhaar, visit an Aadhaar enrolment centre to register it first.

### Q4. What if my PVC Aadhaar card is not delivered within 30 days?
You can raise a complaint at [uidai.gov.in](https://uidai.gov.in/) or call the UIDAI helpline at 1947. If the card is returned undelivered, you may need to reorder.

### Q5. Can I order PVC Aadhaar card for a child?
Yes. A parent or guardian can order a PVC card for a child using the child's Aadhaar number and the registered mobile number linked to that Aadhaar.

### Q6. How many times can I download e-Aadhaar?
There is no limit. You can download e-Aadhaar as many times as needed from the UIDAI portal.

### Q7. Is the PVC Aadhaar card different from the Aadhaar letter?
Yes. The PVC card is a durable plastic card (like a debit card), while the Aadhaar letter is printed on paper and sent during initial enrolment. Both are valid.
