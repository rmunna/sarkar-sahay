# Odisha Caste Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **caste certificate** (ଜାତି ପ୍ରମାଣ ପତ୍ର — Jaati Praman Patra) in Odisha is issued by the Revenue Department (Tahasildar) and is required for OBC/SC/ST reservations in education and government jobs, OJEE (Odisha JEE) reserved category admissions, NEET state quota, Odisha government scholarships (PRERANA, SC/ST), and central scheme benefits. Applications go through **serviceonline.odisha.gov.in** — Odisha's integrated public service portal.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [serviceonline.odisha.gov.in](https://serviceonline.odisha.gov.in) or at **Mo Seva Kendra / CSC**. Issued by Tahasildar within **15–25 days**.

---

## Caste Categories in Odisha

| Category | Reservation % | Notes |
|----------|---------------|-------|
| **SC** | 16.25% | Scheduled Castes — Odisha state list |
| **ST** | 22.5% | Scheduled Tribes — Odisha has large tribal population |
| **SEBC (OBC)** | 11.25% | Socially and Educationally Backward Classes |
| **EWS** | 10% | General category, income < ₹8 lakh/year |

> Odisha uses the term **SEBC (Socially and Educationally Backward Classes)** for OBC. Odisha has one of the highest ST reservations (22.5%) due to its large Adivasi population — Kondhs, Santals, Juangs, Bondas, Dongria Kondhs, etc.

## Why You Need Odisha Caste Certificate

- **OJEE (Odisha JEE)** — lateral entry and diploma admissions, reserved category seats
- **NEET Odisha state counselling** — community certificate required
- **PRERANA Scholarship** (tribal/ST students) — caste proof mandatory
- **Odisha SC/ST post-matric scholarship** — state and NSP
- **Government job reservation** — OPSC (Odisha PSC), Odisha staff selection
- **Central OBC NCL** — for UPSC/SSC/railways (if community is in central OBC list)

---

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory)
- **Ration card** (shows family caste/community)
- **Father's school certificate** (TC or SSLC with caste)
- **ROR (Record of Rights) / patta** (for ST applicants — land records showing tribal community)
- Self-declaration of caste
- **Voter ID** (address proof)
- Passport-size photograph

> For **ST applicants**: the Revenue Inspector may additionally verify tribal community through village records and Gram Panchayat records. A letter from the Gram Pradhan or Sarpanch supporting the tribal identity is helpful.

## How to Apply Online — Odisha Service Online

1. Go to [serviceonline.odisha.gov.in](https://serviceonline.odisha.gov.in)
2. Register / log in with mobile number (OTP)
3. Click **"Revenue & Disaster Management"** → **"Caste Certificate"**
4. Fill form:
   - Applicant name, address (district, sub-division, block, village)
   - Caste/community declaration
   - Father's community details
5. Upload documents (Aadhaar, father's TC/ration card, photograph)
6. Pay fee: **₹20–30** (online)
7. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via Mo Seva Kendra / CSC

1. Visit nearest **Mo Seva Kendra** or CSC (Common Service Centre)
2. Carry Aadhaar, ration card, father's caste proof
3. Operator submits application on Odisha Service Online
4. Service charge: approximately ₹30–50
5. Collect acknowledgement receipt

---

## Status Check & Download

1. Go to [serviceonline.odisha.gov.in](https://serviceonline.odisha.gov.in)
2. Click **"Track Application"** → enter Application Reference Number
3. Once approved: download digitally signed PDF certificate

## Frequently Asked Questions

### SEBC certificate Odisha vs central OBC — same?

**Odisha SEBC** is the state-specific OBC term. For **state government jobs (OPSC)**: SEBC certificate applies. For **central government jobs (UPSC, SSC)**: you need the **central OBC NCL certificate** — your community must also be in the **central OBC list** ([ncbc.nic.in](https://ncbc.nic.in)). Many Odisha SEBC communities may not be in the central list.

### ST caste certificate for NEET Odisha — validity?

For **NEET Odisha state quota counselling**, the caste certificate should be from the current academic year as specified in the OJEE/NEET counselling notification. SC/ST certificates are generally permanent — once issued, they don't expire for community identification purposes. However, counselling authorities may specify recency requirements.
