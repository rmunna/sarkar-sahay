## What is PM Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY)?

Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY) is a central government maternity benefit programme under the **Ministry of Women and Child Development**. It provides a cash benefit of **₹5,000** directly to the bank account of pregnant and lactating women for the first living child.

The scheme compensates for wage loss during pregnancy and childbirth, and encourages safe delivery, good nutrition, and breastfeeding practices. Originally launched in 2010 as Indira Gandhi Matritva Sahyog Yojana, it was renamed PMMVY in 2017 and expanded nationwide across all districts of India.

PMMVY is a **conditional cash transfer** — the benefit is released in installments linked to specific health conditions like registration, ante-natal check-ups, and child birth registration.

## Who is Eligible for PMMVY?

To receive the PMMVY benefit, a woman must meet **all** of the following criteria:

- **Age:** 19 years or above at the time of pregnancy
- **First live birth:** The benefit applies to the first living child of the family
- **Employment:** Not employed in the central or state government, or in any public sector undertaking (those already receiving maternity benefits from employer are excluded)
- **Registration:** Must register at an Anganwadi Centre (AWC) or an approved health facility

> **Note:** Women who experience miscarriage or stillbirth are eligible to claim the benefit again in a future pregnancy, as the condition is "first live birth."

### Who is NOT Eligible?

- Women employed in government or PSU jobs receiving paid maternity leave
- Women who have already received PMMVY benefit for their first child
- Second or subsequent pregnancies (unless the first resulted in no live birth)

## PMMVY Benefit Amount — ₹5,000 in 3 Installments

The total cash benefit of ₹5,000 is paid in **three installments**, each linked to a specific condition:

| Installment | Amount | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| **1st Installment** | ₹1,000 | Early registration of pregnancy at AWC or health facility (within 150 days of LMP) |
| **2nd Installment** | ₹2,000 | At least one ante-natal check-up after 6 months of pregnancy |
| **3rd Installment** | ₹2,000 | Child birth is registered and the child has received the first cycle of vaccination (BCG, OPV, DPT, Hepatitis B) |

> **Total:** ₹5,000. Additionally, eligible women who deliver in a government or accredited hospital also receive ₹1,000 under the **Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY)**, making the effective benefit ₹6,000.

## Documents Required for PMMVY

Keep these documents ready before applying:

- **Aadhaar card** of the beneficiary (mother)
- **Aadhaar card** of the husband/family member
- **Bank account passbook** (account must be in the woman's name or joint account)
- **MCP card** (Mother and Child Protection card) issued by the health facility
- **Identity proof** (Voter ID, PAN, or driving licence — if Aadhaar is not available)
- **Pregnancy registration certificate** from AWC or health facility
- **Child birth certificate** (for 3rd installment)
- **Vaccination record** of the child (for 3rd installment)

### Bank Account & Aadhaar Linking

The PMMVY benefit is transferred via **Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)** into your bank account. Ensure:

- Your **bank account is linked to your Aadhaar number**
- The account is active and has correct IFSC details
- If you don't have a bank account, open one under [PM Jan Dhan Yojana](/guide/pm-jan-dhan-yojana) — it's free and requires only Aadhaar
- You can link Aadhaar to your bank account by visiting the bank branch with your [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)

## How to Apply for PMMVY

There are two ways to apply — **offline at Anganwadi Centre** or **online through the PMMVY-CAS portal**.

### Option 1: Apply at Anganwadi Centre (Offline)

1. Visit your nearest **Anganwadi Centre (AWC)** or approved health facility
2. Carry all required documents (Aadhaar, bank passbook, MCP card)
3. Fill out the **PMMVY application form** (Form 1-A for first installment, Form 1-B for second, Form 1-C for third)
4. The Anganwadi worker or ANM (Auxiliary Nurse Midwife) will help you fill and submit the form
5. Collect the **acknowledgement receipt** with your registration number
6. Repeat the process for 2nd and 3rd installments when eligible

### Option 2: Apply Online via PMMVY-CAS Portal

1. Visit the official portal: [pmmvy.wcd.gov.in](https://pmmvy.wcd.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Beneficiary Login"** or register as a new user
3. Enter your **mobile number** and verify with OTP
4. Fill in your personal details, pregnancy details, and bank information
5. Upload scanned copies of required documents
6. Submit the application and note down your **registration ID**
7. Apply for subsequent installments through the same portal

> **Tip:** The online portal (PMMVY-CAS or PMMVY Soft MIS) allows women to independently apply without visiting an Anganwadi centre. However, if you face issues, visiting the AWC is recommended.

## How to Track PMMVY Application Status

You can check the status of your application and installment payments:

- **Online:** Log in to [pmmvy.wcd.gov.in](https://pmmvy.wcd.gov.in) with your registered mobile number and check under "Application Status"
- **Via Anganwadi Worker:** Contact your local AWC and share your registration number
- **Helpline:** Call the **Women Helpline at 181** or the PMMVY helpline for your state
- **UMANG App:** The PMMVY service is also available on the UMANG app for status tracking

## What if Your PMMVY Claim is Rejected?

If your application is rejected or an installment is not credited:

1. **Check the rejection reason** on the portal or through your Anganwadi worker — common reasons include Aadhaar mismatch, incomplete documents, or bank account issues
2. **Correct the error** — update your Aadhaar details, re-link your bank account, or submit missing documents
3. **Re-apply** with corrected information at the AWC or online portal
4. **Escalate** if the issue persists — contact the **District Programme Officer (DPO)** or the **Child Development Project Officer (CDPO)** of your block
5. **File a grievance** on the portal or through the [Centralized Public Grievance Redress and Monitoring System (CPGRAMS)](https://pgportal.gov.in)

> **Common rejection reasons:** Aadhaar not linked to bank, duplicate application, incorrect pregnancy dates, missing vaccination proof, or applying for second child.

## Important Points to Remember

- PMMVY is a **one-time benefit per family** for the first live birth
- The benefit is **not taxable** and comes directly to the mother's bank account
- There is **no income limit** — all eligible women regardless of economic status can apply (except government employees)
- Apply **as early as possible** — ideally within 150 days of the last menstrual period for the first installment
- Keep your **MCP card updated** with all ante-natal visits and vaccination records
- If you have a girl child, you can also benefit from [Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana](/guide/sukanya-samriddhi-yojana) for her future education and marriage savings
- For health coverage during pregnancy, check if you're eligible for [Ayushman Bharat](/guide/ayushman-bharat-health-card)

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I apply for PMMVY for my second child?

No, PMMVY benefit is available only for the **first live birth**. If your first pregnancy resulted in miscarriage or stillbirth, you can claim the benefit for the next pregnancy that results in a live birth.

### Is PMMVY available for all states in India?

Yes, PMMVY is a **centrally sponsored scheme** implemented across all states and union territories of India. The central government bears the major share of the funding.

### What if I don't have an Aadhaar card?

You can apply using an alternate ID (Voter ID, PAN, driving licence) initially. However, Aadhaar is mandatory for DBT payment. Apply for [Aadhaar online](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) as soon as possible to avoid delays.

### How long does it take to receive the PMMVY payment?

Once your application and documents are verified, the payment is typically credited within **30 to 45 days** via DBT to your linked bank account. Delays may occur due to document verification or Aadhaar-bank linking issues.

### Can unmarried mothers apply for PMMVY?

Yes, PMMVY does not require the applicant to be married. Any pregnant woman aged 19 or above expecting her first child can apply, regardless of marital status.

### What is the PMMVY helpline number?

You can call the **Women Helpline at 181** or contact your local Anganwadi centre for assistance. The state-level helpline numbers are also available on the PMMVY portal.

### Can NRI women or women living abroad apply?

No, PMMVY is only for women **residing in India**. The registration and ante-natal check-ups must be done at Indian health facilities or Anganwadi centres.
