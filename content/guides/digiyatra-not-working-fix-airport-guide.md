# DigiYatra Not Working? Fix Airport Face Recognition and App Issues

**DigiYatra** is India's airport face-recognition boarding system that lets you enter the airport and board your flight without showing physical documents — just walk through and the camera recognizes your face. If DigiYatra isn't working at the airport or the app has registration issues, here's how to fix each problem.

## What Is DigiYatra?

DigiYatra uses **Aadhaar-linked facial biometrics** to:
- Enter the **terminal building** (security check) without showing ID
- Pass **boarding gate** without showing a printed/digital boarding pass
- Your face becomes your boarding pass

Operated by DigiYatra Foundation (joint venture of AAI and private airports). Available at 24+ major Indian airports (Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Chennai, Pune, Cochin, etc.) as of 2026.

## How DigiYatra Works — Quick Walkthrough

1. **Register**: Download **DigiYatra app** → register with Aadhaar OTP → upload selfie
2. **Link PNR**: Before flying, open app → link your flight PNR/booking reference
3. **At airport entrance**: Look at the camera at the e-gate → face recognized → gate opens
4. **At security**: Again face scan → passes without showing ID
5. **At boarding gate**: Face scan → board without showing boarding pass

## Fix 1: Face Not Recognized at Airport Gate

**Most common problem.** The camera at the airport e-gate fails to match your face.

**Causes and fixes:**
- **Changed appearance since registration**: If you cut/grew beard, got glasses, or changed hairstyle significantly after registration, your profile photo may not match. Update your DigiYatra profile selfie.
- **Wearing heavy makeup or face mask**: Temporary fix — remove for the face scan moment
- **Glasses glare**: Remove glasses when looking at the camera
- **Poor lighting**: Look directly at the camera; position your face straight (not at an angle)
- **Photo quality in app**: Open app → profile → update selfie with a clear, well-lit photo
- **Re-register**: Delete app account and re-register with a fresh selfie if photo is old

If face scan fails **3 times** at an e-gate, staff will manually verify your documents. You'll still board your flight — DigiYatra failure doesn't mean you can't travel.

## Fix 2: DigiYatra Registration Failed

**Causes and fixes:**
1. **OTP not received**: Try with a different browser (use Chrome, not in-app browser). Ensure mobile is connected to 4G/5G (not just WiFi for OTP delivery)
2. **Aadhaar verification failed**: Your mobile number must be linked to Aadhaar. Verify at myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in → Mobile linked status
3. **"User already exists" error**: You may have an old account. Try "Forgot Password" or login with Aadhaar number
4. **Camera permission denied**: Allow camera access for DigiYatra app in your phone's app settings
5. **App crash during selfie**: Update the DigiYatra app to latest version; try in airplane mode (selfie upload) and then reconnect

## Fix 3: PNR Not Linking or "Flight Not Found"

When you try to link your booking PNR to DigiYatra and it fails:

1. **Try alphanumeric PNR format**: Enter exactly as shown in your e-ticket (some airlines use 6-char PNR; enter without spaces)
2. **Wait closer to travel date**: You can only link PNR within **48 hours** of travel on some airline-airport combinations
3. **Airline compatibility**: Not all airlines have linked their PNR systems to DigiYatra. Currently integrated: Air India, IndiGo, Vistara/Air India Express, SpiceJet, AirAsia India. Check DigiYatra app for current list.
4. **Re-sync**: Log out and log back in; try linking PNR from the flight details screen
5. **Use Flight Number + Date**: Some airports' e-gates work with face alone after you've registered once — PNR linking is for pre-filling data, not strictly required

## Fix 4: DigiYatra Login OTP Not Received

1. Ensure your phone is on **4G/5G network** (not just WiFi — OTPs often need cellular)
2. Check **DND** — disable if active (though government OTPs should bypass DND)
3. Wait **2 minutes** before resending OTP
4. If still not received after 3 attempts, use **"Login with Aadhaar"** option instead of mobile OTP

## Fix 5: App Not Loading or Blank Screen

1. **Update the app**: Go to Play Store / App Store → update DigiYatra to latest version
2. **Clear cache**: Settings → Apps → DigiYatra → Clear Cache → reopen
3. **Uninstall and reinstall**: Reinstallation often fixes persistent issues
4. **Check Android version**: DigiYatra requires Android 8.0 or iOS 13.0 minimum
5. **VPN conflict**: If VPN is active, disable it — DigiYatra verifies Aadhaar via Indian servers and may block with VPN

## Privacy — Is DigiYatra Safe?

DigiYatra is a common concern:
- **No central database**: Your facial data is stored in a **decentralized wallet** on your phone, not on government servers
- **Data auto-deleted**: Facial data at airports is deleted within 24 hours of travel
- **No tracking post-travel**: No persistent facial recognition database is maintained
- **Aadhaar-linked but not stored centrally**: UIDAI verifies your identity but doesn't share biometrics with DigiYatra Foundation

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I still need to carry my boarding pass if using DigiYatra?

Recommended yes — carry a digital boarding pass (on IndiGo app, Air India app, etc.) as backup. DigiYatra is convenient but if any system is down at the airport, you'll need your boarding pass. Face recognition failure doesn't affect your right to fly.

### Is DigiYatra mandatory?

No. DigiYatra is optional and voluntary. You can always travel using your traditional boarding pass and Aadhaar/ID at security check.

### Does DigiYatra work for international flights?

Currently, DigiYatra operates for **domestic flights only**. International departures (immigration) are separate.

### My flight is via IndiGo but DigiYatra app says "airline not supported"

Check the in-app list. IndiGo was one of the early adopters. If the specific route or terminal is not integrated, DigiYatra may show that error. The system is still expanding airport-airline integrations.

### Can I use DigiYatra without Aadhaar?

Currently, DigiYatra registration requires Aadhaar OTP verification. If you don't have Aadhaar or your mobile isn't linked to Aadhaar, you cannot register for DigiYatra.
