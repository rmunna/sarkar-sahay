# Home Loan Process India 2026 — Complete Step-by-Step Guide

Getting a home loan in India takes 45–90 days from application to disbursement for a resale property. New construction from reputed builders can be faster (30–45 days). Here's the exact process.

---

## Step 1: Check Your Eligibility (Before Applying)

Banks use these parameters to determine how much you can borrow:

**Loan amount eligibility rules**:
- Generally: **EMI should not exceed 50–55% of net monthly income**
- Age: Loan must close before 70 years (65 for some banks) — limits tenure for older borrowers
- CIBIL score: 750+ is ideal; below 650 = likely rejection; below 700 = higher interest rate

**Quick estimation**:
- Net monthly salary ₹1,00,000 → Max EMI allowed: ₹50,000
- At 9% for 20 years: ₹50K EMI = approximately ₹55L loan
- Adjust down for existing loan EMIs (personal loan, car loan, credit card minimum)

**Calculate on bank websites**: SBI, HDFC, ICICI all have EMI calculators. Or use this formula:
- Monthly EMI for ₹1L at 9% for 20 years = ₹900/month
- So ₹50L loan at 9% × 20 years → ₹45,000 EMI

## Step 2: Choose the Right Lender

| Lender Type | Typical Rate (June 2026) | Best For |
|-------------|-------------------------|---------|
| PSU Banks (SBI, Bank of Baroda) | 8.5–9.2% | Transparent rates, PMAY eligibility |
| Private Banks (HDFC, ICICI, Axis) | 8.7–9.5% | Faster processing, online tracking |
| HFCs (PNB Housing, LIC Housing) | 8.8–9.5% | Self-employed, non-standard profiles |
| NBFCs (Bajaj Finance, Tata Capital) | 9–10.5% | Poor CIBIL borrowers (last resort) |

**Rate types**:
- **EBLR (External Benchmark Linked Rate)**: Linked to RBI repo rate. When RBI cuts rate, your EMI automatically reduces. Faster transmission. (Mandatory for banks since 2019)
- **PLR / BPLR** (older): Internal benchmarks, slower rate reduction. Mostly HFCs/NBFCs.

**Choose**: Bank over NBFC if CIBIL is above 700. EBLR-linked is standard for banks — ensures benefit of future rate cuts.

## Step 3: Documents Required

### For Salaried Employees

**Identity & Address**:
- Aadhaar card (identity + address)
- PAN card (mandatory)
- Passport / Voter ID (additional ID if needed)

**Income Proof**:
- Last 3 months' salary slips
- Form 16 for last 2 years (or both employers if switched job)
- Last 2 years' ITR with computation (acknowledged copy or TRACES)
- Last 6 months' salary bank statement (showing salary credits)

**Property Documents** (for resale flat/house):
- Agreement to sell / MOU
- Title deed (all chain documents — ideally 30 years)
- Previous sale deeds (at least last 2 transfers)
- Property tax receipts (latest)
- Encumbrance certificate (past 13–30 years, from sub-registrar office)
- Approved plan and completion certificate (for constructed property)
- Society NOC (for apartment)
- Builder's RERA registration (for new projects)

### For Self-Employed

All of the above, plus:
- ITR for last 3 years with all schedules + P&L + Balance Sheet (CA-certified)
- Business registration proof (GST certificate, company registration)
- Bank statements for business account (last 12 months)
- Professional license (if applicable)

---

## Step 4: Application and Processing Fee

Submit application with documents to chosen bank. Most allow online upload + branch visit.

**Processing fee**: 0.25%–1% of loan amount (non-refundable even if loan is rejected).
- SBI: ₹10,000 + GST flat (recently; check current fee)
- HDFC: 0.50% of loan amount
- ICICI: 0.25–0.50% + GST

**Negotiate**: Processing fee is negotiable, especially for salary accounts or existing relationships. Banks routinely waive 25–50% if you ask.

---

## Step 5: Verification Phase (Bank's Internal Process)

After application submission, bank runs parallel checks:

### 5a. Credit Check
- CIBIL/Experian score pulled (you should check yours first — free check at [CIBIL portal](/guide/cibil-score-check-free-improve-guide))
- All existing loans, credit card payment history reviewed
- RBI defaulter list checked

### 5b. Income Verification
- Salary slip verification with employer (HR call or email)
- Bank statement analysis (salary credits matching pay slips)
- ITR verified with IT department (Form 26AS match)

### 5c. Technical Valuation (Physical Inspection)
- Bank's panel valuer visits the property
- Values the property independently (may value lower than sale price)
- Checks structural condition, legal compliance, approved plan match
- Loan-to-Value (LTV): Banks lend up to 75–80% of valuation (not sale price)

**Important**: If bank valuation < sale price, you fund the gap from your own pocket.

### 5d. Legal Verification
- Bank's panel lawyer verifies title documents
- Checks for encumbrances (existing mortgage, court orders)
- Ensures clear and marketable title
- Takes 7–15 days
- If any defect found: Bank may ask for more documents or reject

**Timeline for steps 5a–5d**: 15–30 days (varies by bank and property complexity)

---

## Step 6: Sanction Letter

Once all verifications pass, bank issues **Sanction Letter** stating:
- Sanctioned loan amount
- Interest rate (floating/fixed)
- Tenure
- EMI amount
- Processing fee paid/payable
- Validity (usually 3–6 months)

**Sanction ≠ Disbursement**: Sanction just means bank has approved. Disbursement happens after property registration.

---

## Step 7: Property Registration and Disbursement

### For Resale Property

1. Both buyer and seller sign sale deed at sub-registrar office
2. Stamp duty and registration fee paid (4–8% depending on state)
3. Registered sale deed handed over
4. Buyer brings registered deed to bank
5. Bank disburses loan directly to seller (cheque or NEFT — never to buyer)
6. EMI starts from next month

### For Under-Construction Property

- Loan is disbursed in **tranches** as construction progresses
- You pay only **Pre-EMI** (interest on disbursed amount) during construction
- Full EMI starts after possession OR after full disbursement (check loan terms)

**Pre-EMI example**: Disbursed ₹30L out of ₹60L sanctioned, at 9%.
- Pre-EMI = ₹30L × 9% / 12 = ₹22,500/month
- Pre-EMI period can last 2–5 years for long-gestation projects

## Home Loan Rejection — Common Reasons and Fixes

| Rejection Reason | Fix |
|-----------------|-----|
| Low CIBIL score (<700) | Pay all EMIs on time for 6–12 months; clear credit card dues |
| High existing EMIs (FOIR >50%) | Close other loans before applying; increase income via spouse co-applicant |
| Job change within 6 months | Wait 6–12 months; banks prefer stability |
| Self-employed with inconsistent ITR | Show 3 consistent years of ITR; CA-certified financials |
| Property legal issues | Fix title, get proper documents; may need new property |
| Loan amount > eligible | Add co-applicant (spouse); choose longer tenure to reduce EMI |
| Property in unauthorized area | Some properties in gram panchayat/unauthorized colonies have lower LTV or are ineligible |

## EMI vs Pre-EMI — Tax Implications

**Pre-EMI interest** (during construction):
- Not deductible in the year paid
- Clubbed and deductible in **5 equal installments** from the year of possession
- Same ₹2L annual cap (Section 24b for self-occupied)

**Regular EMI after possession**:
- Interest: Section 24b (up to ₹2L for self-occupied, unlimited for let-out)
- Principal: Section 80C (up to ₹1.5L combined)

**New regime**: No Section 24b deduction for self-occupied property. If you're in new regime, home loan interest on self-occupied property gives zero tax benefit. But for let-out property, interest is still deductible against rental income (even in new regime).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does home loan approval take?

15–45 days for salaried applicants with clean documents and clear property. 30–60 days for self-employed. Incomplete documents or property issues can extend to 90 days.

### What is the minimum CIBIL score for home loan?

Most banks require 700+. SBI, HDFC, ICICI ideally want 750+. Below 700, some HFCs/NBFCs may lend but at higher rates. Below 650 = rejection at most lenders. [Check and improve your CIBIL](/guide/cibil-score-check-free-improve-guide).

### Can I get home loan without ITR (filed ITR)?

Difficult. Most banks require 2 years' ITR for salaried, 3 years for self-employed. Some banks accept Form 16 + 26AS in lieu of ITR for salaried, but ITR is standard. If you haven't filed ITR, file immediately.

### Is co-applicant mandatory?

No — but adding a co-applicant (spouse or parent) increases loan eligibility if their income is also considered. Also, joint home loan gives both co-applicants tax benefits (each gets Section 24b + 80C deduction, subject to being co-owners in sale deed).

### When does EMI start after disbursement?

EMI typically starts 1–2 months after first disbursement. For partial disbursement (under-construction), Pre-EMI on disbursed portion starts immediately; full EMI starts after final disbursement or possession, whichever is earlier.
