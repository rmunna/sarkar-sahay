# Aadhaar Date of Birth Change: How to Correct DOB Online & Offline

If your date of birth is recorded incorrectly in Aadhaar, you can get it corrected. However, UIDAI has strict rules around DOB changes — it can only be changed a limited number of times, and once locked, it cannot be changed again. This guide explains the complete process, accepted documents, and important restrictions.

## Key Rules for Aadhaar DOB Change

Before you begin, understand these critical rules:

- **DOB can be changed only once** if your Aadhaar was enrolled with a "Declared" or "Approximate" date of birth
- If you provided a valid DOB proof during original enrolment ("Verified" status), changes are more restricted
- **DOB lock:** Once you update DOB with a valid document and it is verified, UIDAI locks the DOB field permanently
- After locking, no further DOB changes are possible — even at an enrolment centre
- You can check if your DOB is verified/declared by looking at your Aadhaar letter or e-Aadhaar

## Documents Accepted for DOB Change

UIDAI accepts the following documents as Date of Birth proof:

### Primary DOB Documents:
1. **Birth Certificate** issued by Municipal Corporation or Registrar of Births & Deaths
2. **Passport** (valid or expired)
3. **SSLC / Class 10 Certificate or Mark Sheet** with DOB
4. **PAN Card** (shows DOB)
5. **Central/State Government Photo ID with DOB**

### Other Accepted Documents:
6. School leaving certificate with DOB
7. Certificate of Date of Birth from Group A Gazetted Officer on letterhead
8. Government hospital or health institution discharge/birth record
9. Pension Payment Order (PPO) with DOB

**Important:** The DOB on the proof document must exactly match the DOB you want to update in Aadhaar. Any mismatch will lead to rejection.

## How to Change DOB in Aadhaar Online

### Prerequisites:
- Mobile number registered with Aadhaar
- Valid DOB proof document (scanned copy in JPEG/PDF, max 2 MB)

### Steps:

1. Visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/)
2. Login with your 12-digit Aadhaar number and OTP
3. Click **Update Aadhaar** from the dashboard
4. Select **Date of Birth** as the field to update
5. Enter the correct date of birth
6. Upload the DOB proof document (JPEG or PDF, max 2 MB)
7. Review the details carefully — this change may be permanent
8. Click **Submit** and pay ₹50 via card, net banking, or UPI
9. Note down the **URN (Update Request Number)**
10. [Track your request status](/guide/aadhaar-card-track-status) after 5-7 working days

## How to Change DOB at Aadhaar Enrolment Centre

If online update is not possible (mobile not linked, or document upload issues):

1. Locate the nearest Aadhaar enrolment centre at [uidai.gov.in](https://uidai.gov.in/) → **Locate Enrolment Centre**
2. Carry **original DOB proof document** and one photocopy
3. Carry your existing Aadhaar card or number
4. Visit the centre and fill the **Aadhaar Update/Correction Form**
5. Select "Date of Birth" as the field to update
6. Submit the form with document photocopy to the operator
7. The operator will verify your biometrics (fingerprint/iris)
8. Pay ₹50 update fee
9. Collect the **acknowledgement slip** with EID
10. [Track status](/guide/aadhaar-card-track-status) using the EID after 10-15 working days

## DOB Lock — What You Need to Know

| Scenario | Can DOB Be Changed? |
|----------|-------------------|
| DOB status is "Declared" (no proof submitted earlier) | Yes — one time with valid document |
| DOB status is "Approximate" (year only) | Yes — can update to exact date with proof |
| DOB was "Verified" with proof at enrolment | Very restricted — only if original was clearly wrong |
| DOB has been updated once with document proof | No — DOB is locked permanently |

### How to Check if DOB is Locked:
- Download your [e-Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-card-reprint-online) and check the DOB verification status
- If it shows "Verified", the DOB is likely locked

## Important Tips

1. **Double-check before submitting** — DOB change is often a one-time opportunity. Ensure the date is correct and matches your document exactly.
2. **Use birth certificate or Class 10 marksheet** — These are the strongest DOB proofs and least likely to be rejected.
3. **Don't change DOB without valid reason** — UIDAI may flag and reject frivolous DOB change requests. Only apply if there is a genuine error.
4. **Update other documents after DOB change** — Once Aadhaar DOB is corrected, update your PAN, bank accounts, and other linked services.
5. **Keep the old and new records** — Save a copy of your old e-Aadhaar and the new one for reference during the transition period.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. How many times can I change my date of birth in Aadhaar?
UIDAI generally allows DOB change only once. After the DOB is verified with a valid proof document, it gets locked and cannot be changed again.

### Q2. Can I change DOB in Aadhaar without documents?
No. A valid date of birth proof document is mandatory for DOB change. Self-declaration alone is not accepted for this field.

### Q3. My Aadhaar shows only year of birth. Can I add the full date?
Yes. If your Aadhaar shows only the year (approximate DOB), you can update it to the exact date by providing a valid DOB proof document.

### Q4. What if my DOB change request is rejected?
Check the rejection reason on the [status tracking page](/guide/aadhaar-card-track-status). Common reasons are document mismatch, poor scan quality, or invalid document type. Resubmit with the correct document.

### Q5. Can I change DOB after DOB lock?
No. Once DOB is locked in Aadhaar, it cannot be changed through any method — online or at a centre. This is a permanent lock by UIDAI.

### Q6. Is DOB change free of cost?
No. UIDAI charges ₹50 for any demographic update including DOB, whether done online or at an enrolment centre.

### Q7. Can I change DOB for a child's Aadhaar?
Yes. A parent or guardian can request DOB correction for a child's Aadhaar at an enrolment centre with the child's birth certificate.
