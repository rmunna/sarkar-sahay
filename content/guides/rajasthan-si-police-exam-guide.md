# Rajasthan SI (Sub-Inspector) Exam Guide — Eligibility, Pattern & Preparation Tips

The Rajasthan Public Service Commission (RPSC) conducts the recruitment exam for Sub-Inspector posts in Rajasthan Police. The SI post is a direct officer-rank entry into the police force, making it one of the most competitive state-level exams.

Rajasthan Police SI offers a commanding position with Grade Pay ₹4,200, opportunities for promotion, and the responsibility of maintaining law and order.

## Eligibility Criteria

### Age Limit

| Category | Minimum Age | Maximum Age |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| General | 21 years | 28 years |
| OBC | 21 years | 31 years |
| SC/ST | 21 years | 33 years |

### Educational Qualification

- **Minimum:** Bachelor's degree from a recognized university
- Any discipline accepted

### Physical Standards

**Male:** Height 165-170 cm (varies by category), Chest 79/84 cm
**Female:** Height 150-157 cm (varies by category)

## Exam Pattern

### Stage 1: Written Examination

| Subject | Marks |
|---------|-------|
| General Knowledge & Current Affairs | 100 |
| Reasoning & Mental Ability | 100 |
| Quantitative Aptitude / Numerical | 100 |
| Language (Hindi/English/Regional) | 100 |
| **Total** | **400** |

*Exact pattern varies by notification — some states use different mark distributions.*

- **Duration:** 2-3 hours
- **Type:** Objective (MCQ)
- **Negative marking:** Typically 0.25 per wrong answer

### Stage 2: Physical Efficiency Test

**Male:** Running (1.6-4.8 km varies by state), Long Jump, High Jump, Shot Put
**Female:** Running (shorter distance), Long Jump

### Stage 3: Document Verification & Medical

## Syllabus Overview

### General Knowledge
- History, Geography, Polity, Economy, Science, Rajasthan-specific GK, Current Affairs

### Reasoning & Mental Ability
- Logical reasoning, Analogies, Series, Blood relations, Coding-decoding, Directions

### Quantitative Aptitude
- Arithmetic, Number system, Percentage, Ratio, Time & Work, Data Interpretation

### Language
- Grammar, Comprehension, Vocabulary, Sentence correction

## How to Apply

1. Visit the official website at [police.rajasthan.gov.in](https://police.rajasthan.gov.in)
2. Find the SI recruitment notification
3. Register with valid credentials
4. Fill the application form completely
5. Upload photo and signature
6. Pay fee and submit

## Application Fee

| Category | Fee |
|----------|-----|
| General / OBC | ₹400 - ₹600 |
| SC / ST | ₹200 - ₹400 |

*Exact fee as per notification.*

## Salary & Benefits

- **Pay Scale:** ₹35,400 – ₹1,12,400 (7th CPC equivalent)
- Grade Pay: ₹4,200
- DA, HRA, uniform allowance, and other benefits
- Medical benefits, pension (NPS)
- Promotion path: SI → Inspector → DSP

## Preparation Tips

1. **State GK:** Study Rajasthan-specific history, geography, and government schemes
2. **Reasoning practice:** Daily practice of reasoning puzzles and series
3. **Physical fitness:** Start running and stamina building at least 6 months early
4. **Mock tests:** Take weekly full-length tests
5. **Current affairs:** Follow national and state news daily

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is graduation mandatory for Rajasthan SI?
Yes, a Bachelor's degree is required for the SI post.

### Is there an interview?
Most states have eliminated the interview. Selection is based on written exam + PET.

### What is the physical test for female candidates?
Female candidates have relaxed physical standards — shorter running distance and lower height requirements.

### Can other state candidates apply?
Yes, but reserved category benefits typically require state domicile.

### How is the final merit prepared?
Final merit is based on written exam marks (Mains) with PET being qualifying.

### What is the promotion path for SI?
SI → Inspector → Deputy Superintendent of Police (DSP) through departmental exams and seniority.
