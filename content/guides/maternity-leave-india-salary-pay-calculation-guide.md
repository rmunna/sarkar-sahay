# Maternity Leave India 2026 — Your Rights, Salary, and What to Do If Employer Refuses

India's Maternity Benefit (Amendment) Act, 2017 gives working women one of the strongest maternity protections in Asia. But most employees don't know their exact rights — and many employers violate them. Here's everything you need to know.

---

## How Many Weeks of Maternity Leave?

| Situation | Paid Leave |
|-----------|-----------|
| First or second child | **26 weeks** (6.5 months) |
| Third child onwards | **12 weeks** |
| Adoption (child under 3 months) | 12 weeks from date of adoption |
| Surrogacy (commissioning mother) | 12 weeks |
| Miscarriage / medical termination | **6 weeks** (from date of event) |
| Tubectomy (post-delivery) | 2 weeks |
| Illness arising from delivery | Additional leave as prescribed by medical authority |

**Pre-natal leave**: Of the 26 weeks, up to 8 weeks can be taken before the expected delivery date. Remaining 18 weeks are post-delivery.

---

## Which Employers Must Give Maternity Leave?

**Mandatory for**: Every establishment employing **10 or more employees** (factories, mines, shops, offices, etc.)

**Not covered**: Domestic workers, establishments with <10 employees, casual/contract workers without 80-day minimum service

**Eligibility for the employee**: You must have worked for the employer for at least **80 days in the 12 months immediately preceding the date of delivery**.

---

## Maternity Salary Calculation — How Much Will You Get?

**Your maternity salary = average daily wages × number of leave days**

**Average daily wage calculation**:
Total wages paid in 3 calendar months immediately preceding maternity leave ÷ total working days in those 3 months

**Example**:
- April salary: ₹50,000 (22 working days)
- May salary: ₹50,000 (23 working days)
- June salary: ₹55,000 (24 working days) — increment received
- Total wages: ₹1,55,000
- Total working days: 69
- Average daily wage: ₹1,55,000 ÷ 69 = **₹2,246/day**

For 26 weeks (182 days):
₹2,246 × 182 = **₹4,08,772 total maternity benefit**

**Important**: All allowances that form part of your compensation are included in the wage calculation — basic salary + HRA + transport allowance + any other regular allowances. Only variable components (incentives, overtime) are typically excluded.

## ESI Employees — Separate Maternity Benefit

If you're covered under **ESIC (Employees' State Insurance)**, maternity benefit is paid by ESI, not the employer.

**ESI maternity benefit**:
- **Amount**: Full average daily wages × leave days (same calculation)
- **Payment**: Directly from ESIC to your bank account
- **Condition**: Must have contributed to ESI for at least 70 days in 2 contributory periods before delivery

**How to claim ESI maternity benefit**:
1. Fill Form 19 (Maternity Benefit claim) — available at ESIC office or esic.nic.in
2. Attach: ESI card, pregnancy certificate from registered medical practitioner, birth certificate after delivery
3. Submit to your nearest ESIC branch office
4. Payment credited within 2–4 weeks

If your employer has deducted ESI contributions but hasn't filed on your behalf, file directly at ESIC office with proof of contribution.

## Work From Home Extension After Maternity Leave

**2017 Amendment** added: After the 26-week maternity leave, women working in establishments with 50+ employees can request **work from home** (WFH) arrangement. The terms (duration, nature of work) are mutually agreed between employer and employee.

This is not an absolute right — the employer can decline if the nature of work doesn't permit WFH. But refusal must be documented, and the employer cannot take adverse action for requesting WFH.

---

## Creche Facility — Employer's Obligation

Establishments with **50 or more employees** must provide:
- A creche facility within the prescribed distance
- **4 visits per day** to the creche (nursing breaks in addition to regular breaks)
- Employees must be informed about creche availability at the time of appointment

---

## What the Employer CANNOT Do During/After Maternity Leave

**Legally prohibited**:
- **Termination of employment** during maternity leave or for reason of maternity
- **Reduction in salary** or benefits because of pregnancy/maternity
- **Demotion** or change in job role adversely affecting the employee
- **Deduction of maternity benefit** from the salary (it's additional, not adjustment)
- **Forcing resignation** directly or indirectly
- **Refusing to reinstate** to the same or equivalent position after leave

**If employer fires you during or because of maternity**: File a complaint with the **Labour Commissioner** of your state. This is a criminal offence under Section 21 of the Maternity Benefit Act — punishment up to 1 year imprisonment and/or fine.

## How to Apply for Maternity Leave

### Step 1: Give Written Notice

At least 7 weeks before expected delivery, inform your employer in writing (email is valid). State the expected date of delivery and that you're claiming maternity benefit under the Maternity Benefit Act.

### Step 2: Attach Medical Certificate

Attach a certificate from a registered medical practitioner (gynaecologist/MBBS) confirming pregnancy and expected delivery date.

### Step 3: Post-Delivery Notice

After delivery, submit the birth certificate to HR within 48 hours of being able to do so (no strict deadline during hospital stay).

### Step 4: Receive Benefit

Employer must pay maternity benefit in advance for the pre-natal period and within 48 hours of receiving post-delivery notice for the remaining period.

---

## If Your Employer Refuses or Delays — What to Do

### Step 1: Send Written Reminder

Email HR and your manager a reminder citing "Section 5 of the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961 (as amended 2017)" and your right to 26 weeks paid leave.

### Step 2: Approach Your HR Grievance Cell

Most companies have an internal grievance mechanism. File a complaint in writing; keep records.

### Step 3: File Complaint with State Labour Department

**Labour Commissioner's Office**: Every state has one. File a written complaint. The commissioner can investigate and penalize the employer.

**Online complaint portals**:
- Central: [shramsuvidha.gov.in](https://shramsuvidha.gov.in) → Grievances
- State-specific portals (search "[your state] labour department complaint")

### Step 4: Approach the Inspector Under the Act

District-level inspectors are appointed under the Maternity Benefit Act. They have powers to inspect establishments, examine records, and penalize violations.

### Step 5: Employment Tribunal / Labour Court

For serious violations (termination, unpaid maternity benefit), file a case in the Labour Court. Legal aid is available through District Legal Services Authority (DLSA) for free representation.

## Common Employer Violations and Your Response

| Violation | Your Right | Action |
|-----------|-----------|--------|
| "You're on contract, not eligible" | If employed for 80+ days continuously, contract status doesn't override Act | Show employment record to Labour Inspector |
| "Your salary stays the same so benefit is adjusted" | Maternity benefit is IN ADDITION to salary — not an adjustment | Quote Section 5(1) — file complaint |
| "We can only give 12 weeks" | 26 weeks is the law for first/second child | Quote Section 5(3) — file complaint |
| "We need you to resign before maternity" | Forced resignation due to pregnancy is illegal | File complaint for unfair labor practice |
| "WFH is not possible for your role" | WFH request can be declined for valid reasons, but in writing | Get rejection in writing; not actionable unless discriminatory |

## Tax on Maternity Benefit

**Maternity benefit received from employer**: Fully taxable as salary income — included in Form 16 and taxed at slab rate.

**Maternity benefit received from ESIC**: Exempt from income tax under Section 10(23AAB) — ESIC maternity benefit is NOT taxable.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I just joined the company 3 months ago. Am I eligible?

No — you need 80 days of service in the 12 months before your delivery date. If you've worked fewer than 80 days with this employer, you're not eligible for maternity benefit from them (though previous employer's service doesn't count). You may still be covered by ESIC if contributions were made.

### Can the employer ask me to work during maternity leave?

No — the employer cannot require any work during maternity leave. Even remote/email work requests are prohibited. Willingly doing some work is your choice, but it cannot be coerced.

### What if I lose my baby? Am I still entitled to leave?

Yes — in case of stillbirth or the child dying during delivery, you're entitled to full 26 weeks of maternity leave. For miscarriage or medical termination, 6 weeks applies.

### Does maternity leave count toward my total experience / seniority?

Yes — the period of maternity leave counts as service for purposes of seniority, promotions, and increment calculations under the Act.

### Can I extend maternity leave beyond 26 weeks?

You can apply for additional leave (earned leave, casual leave) or unpaid leave — but only with employer consent. The law only mandates 26 weeks paid. Any extension is by mutual agreement.
