## What is Antyodaya Parivar Utthan Yojana (APUY)?

**Antyodaya Parivar Utthan Yojana (APUY)** is a Haryana government programme that identifies the **poorest families** (annual income ≤ ₹1 lakh) from the **Parivar Pehchaan Patra (PPP)** database and specifically targets them with:

- Skill training and employment
- Self-employment loans at zero or minimal interest
- Direct linkage to all applicable government schemes
- Saturation of benefits (no family is left out)

The scheme targets **1 lakh families** with annual income below ₹1 lakh — the absolute poorest in Haryana.

---

## Key Benefits

| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Target group | Families with annual income ≤ ₹1 lakh |
| Skill training | Free vocational training via Haryana Skill Development Mission |
| Employment | Priority placement through government job portals |
| Self-employment loan | Zero-interest or minimal-interest loans |
| Scheme saturation | Automatic enrollment in pension, insurance, ration, PMAY |
| Family identification | Via PPP database (automatic) |

---

## How APUY Works

Unlike traditional schemes where you apply and wait, APUY uses **proactive outreach**:

1. The government identifies APUY-eligible families from PPP income data
2. District-level officials reach out to the families
3. A **District Antyodaya Parivar Utthan Mela** (camp) is organized
4. At the camp — families receive:
   - Skill training registration
   - Loan applications (HSCBC, HDFC, etc.)
   - Enrollment in missing schemes (pension, Ayushman, etc.)

---

## Who is Eligible?

**Automatic eligibility if:**
- Your family's **annual income is ₹1 lakh or less** (as per PPP data)
- You are a **Haryana domicile**
- Your family has a **valid PPP (Parivar Pehchaan Patra)**

**No separate application** is needed — the government finds you through the PPP database.

---

## Ensure You Are in the System

### Step 1 — Make Sure Your PPP is Correct

Visit an **Atal Sewa Kendra / CSC / E-Disha** and ensure:
- All family members are listed in your PPP
- Income details are correct and updated
- Bank account is linked

If your PPP shows higher income than actual, get it corrected — this affects your APUY eligibility.

### Step 2 — Attend the Antyodaya Camp When Called

District officials identify eligible families and invite them to an **Antyodaya Mela**. When you receive notice, attend with all your PPP and Aadhaar documents.

### Step 3 — Enroll in Skills Training and Loans

At the mela, register for:
- Free skill training (ITI, Haryana Skill Mission)
- HSCBC self-employment loans
- Employment registration on Saksham Yuva/Employment Exchange portal

---

## Skills Covered Under APUY

Eligible family members get free training in:
- Electrician, plumbing, welding (ITI trades)
- Sewing/tailoring
- Computer basics and digital literacy
- Beautician and spa services
- Driving (LMV/HMV)
- Agriculture/horticulture skills

After training: placement support in Haryana industries or self-employment.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. I am very poor but my PPP shows higher income. How do I correct this?**

Visit your nearest Atal Sewa Kendra with income proof (ration card, NREGA job card). The operator can initiate a correction request in the PPP. Income verification is done and corrected.

**2. I haven't been contacted for APUY even though my income is ₹70,000/year. What do I do?**

Visit your Block Development and Panchayat Officer (BDPO) office with your PPP and income proof. Request to be added to the APUY list for your block.

**3. Does APUY give direct cash?**

APUY does not give direct cash — it connects families to training, loans, and existing schemes. The cash benefits come from HSCBC loans, pension schemes, and other programmes that APUY facilitates enrollment in.

**4. Are there age restrictions for APUY skill training?**

Generally, youth between 18–45 years are prioritized for skill training. Older family members may be enrolled in pension/social security schemes instead.

**5. My family is in the poorest 1 lakh list. What schemes will we automatically get?**

APUY families are saturated with: Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY, MMPSY social security, state pension (if eligible), Ration card (if missing), PMAY housing (if homeless), and priority for HSCBC loans.
