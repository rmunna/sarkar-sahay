# Tamil Nadu Income Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **income certificate** (வருமான சான்றிதழ் — Varumaana Saanrithal) in Tamil Nadu is issued by the Revenue Department and is required for BC/MBC/SC/ST scholarships, NEET counselling (government quota), EWS reservation, and fee waiver in government colleges. Applications are processed through **TN edistrict** or directly at the **Village Office** (now known as Revenue Divisional Offices).

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [tnedistrict.tn.gov.in](https://tnedistrict.tn.gov.in) or visit your **Taluk Office (VAO / Village Administrative Officer)**. Issued by Tahsildar within **15–30 days**.

---

## Why You Need TN Income Certificate

- **BC/MBC/SC/ST Scholarship (Tamil Nadu)** — income proof mandatory
- **NSP National Scholarship** — for all categories
- **NEET State Counselling** — annual income certificate required for government seat
- **EWS (Economically Weaker Section)** reservation — income < ₹8 lakh/year
- **Fee concession in government colleges** — income-based scholarship
- **Government scheme eligibility** — Kalaignar schemes, housing, etc.

---

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory for applicant and family)
- **Ration card** (important — shows family and category)
- Self-declaration of annual income
- For salaried: **salary certificate** from employer or Form 16
- For farmers: **A-Register / land record** (from TNTANGEDCO or Village Administrative Officer)
- **Community certificate** (if applying for community-based scholarship)
- Passport-size photograph

## How to Apply Online — TN edistrict Portal

1. Go to [tnedistrict.tn.gov.in](https://tnedistrict.tn.gov.in)
2. Click **"New User? Register"** → register with mobile number (OTP)
3. Log in → click **"Revenue Department"** → **"Income Certificate"**
4. Fill form:
   - Applicant name, address (district, taluk, revenue village)
   - Annual family income (all sources declared)
   - Purpose of certificate
5. Upload documents (Aadhaar, ration card, income proof)
6. Pay fee: **₹20–30** (online payment)
7. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via VAO (Village Administrative Officer)

Tamil Nadu's traditional route is through the **VAO (Village Administrative Officer)**:

1. Visit your **Village Administrative Office**
2. Submit income certificate application form with documents
3. VAO verifies income at village level
4. Forwards to **Tahsildar** for approval
5. Collect from Taluk office after approval

---

## Apply via CSC / Arasu Kalavai

1. Visit nearest **Arasu Kalavai** (Government Kiosk) or CSC
2. Operator submits form on TN edistrict portal
3. Service charge: approximately ₹30–50
4. Collect acknowledgement

---

## Status Check

1. Go to [tnedistrict.tn.gov.in](https://tnedistrict.tn.gov.in)
2. Click **"Application Status"** → enter reference number
3. Or log in → **"My Applications"** → view status

---

## Download TN Income Certificate

Once approved:
1. Log in to tnedistrict.tn.gov.in
2. Go to **"My Certificates"** → click Income Certificate
3. Download digitally signed PDF
4. Valid for all official purposes

## Validity in Tamil Nadu

| Purpose | Validity |
|---------|---------|
| Scholarship (state/NSP) | Current financial year only |
| NEET counselling | Issued in the academic year of counselling |
| EWS certificate | 1 year |
| General official use | As specified |

> **Important:** For NEET counselling and scholarship applications, the income certificate must be issued **in the same academic/financial year**. Old certificates are not accepted.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What annual income should I declare for TN scholarship?

For **BC/MBC scholarships** (Tamil Nadu state): family income below ₹2 lakh/year for most schemes. For **SC/ST fee concession**: no income limit but certificate is still required. Check the specific scholarship scheme guidelines.

### TN income certificate vs income affidavit — which is accepted?

For official purposes (NEET, scholarship, government job EWS), the **income certificate from Tahsildar** is accepted. A notarized affidavit alone is generally **not accepted** for high-stakes applications.
