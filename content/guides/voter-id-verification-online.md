## What is Voter ID Verification?

Voter ID verification is the process of checking whether your name is registered in the electoral roll (voter list) and confirming that your Voter ID card (EPIC — Electors Photo Identity Card) details are correct. The Election Commission of India (ECI) provides free online tools to verify your Voter ID through the **Voters' Services Portal** (formerly known as NVSP — National Voter's Service Portal).

Voter ID verification is useful for:
- **Checking if your name is in the voter list** before elections
- **Verifying your polling booth** and constituency details
- **Confirming your EPIC number** and personal details
- **Downloading your e-EPIC** (digital Voter ID card)
- **Finding your Booth Level Officer (BLO)** contact information

## Who Can Verify Voter ID Online?

- Any Indian citizen aged 18 years or above who has applied for a Voter ID card
- Citizens who have already been enrolled in the electoral roll
- Anyone who wants to check if their name is in the voter list of any state or union territory

> **Note:** You do not need to log in or create an account to search the electoral roll. However, downloading e-EPIC requires login with your registered mobile number.

## Methods to Verify Voter ID Online

There are **three main ways** to verify your Voter ID card online:

### Method 1: Search by EPIC Number (Voter ID Number)

If you have your Voter ID card number (EPIC number), this is the fastest method.

1. Visit the **Electoral Search** portal at [electoralsearch.eci.gov.in](https://electoralsearch.eci.gov.in)
2. Select the **"Search by EPIC No."** tab
3. Enter your **EPIC number** (the alphanumeric number on your Voter ID card, e.g., ABC1234567)
4. Enter the **captcha code** shown on screen
5. Click **"Search"**
6. Your voter details will be displayed including:
   - Full name and father's/husband's name
   - Age and gender
   - Assembly constituency and part number
   - Polling station name and address

### Method 2: Search by Personal Details

If you don't have your EPIC number, you can search using personal details.

1. Visit [electoralsearch.eci.gov.in](https://electoralsearch.eci.gov.in)
2. Select the **"Search by Details"** tab
3. Fill in the required details:
   - **Name** (as registered in voter list)
   - **Father's/Husband's name**
   - **Age** or **Date of Birth**
   - **Gender**
   - **State**
   - **District**
   - **Assembly Constituency**
4. Enter the **captcha code**
5. Click **"Search"**
6. A list of matching records will appear — identify your entry and click to view full details

### Method 3: Using the Voter Helpline App

The ECI also provides a mobile app for voter verification.

1. Download the **Voter Helpline** app from [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.eci.citizen) or Apple App Store
2. Open the app and tap **"Search in Electoral Roll"**
3. Choose to search by **EPIC number** or **personal details**
4. Enter the required information
5. View your voter registration details

## How to Download e-EPIC (Digital Voter ID)

The Election Commission allows you to download your Voter ID card digitally as an **e-EPIC**.

1. Visit the **Voters' Services Portal** at [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Login/Register"**
3. Enter your **registered mobile number** and verify with OTP
4. After login, click on **"Download e-EPIC"**
5. Enter your **EPIC number**
6. Verify your identity using **OTP** or **face verification**
7. Download the e-EPIC in **PDF format**

> **Important:** Your mobile number must be linked with your Voter ID to download e-EPIC. If it is not linked, you can update it by filing Form 6B on the Voters' Services Portal.

## How to Check Voter ID Application Status

If you have recently applied for a new Voter ID or made corrections, you can track the status:

1. Visit [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Track Application Status"**
3. Enter your **Reference ID** (received at the time of application)
4. View the current status of your application

## Processing Time

- **Electoral roll search:** Instant results
- **e-EPIC download:** Instant (if mobile number is linked)
- **New Voter ID registration:** 15–30 days after application
- **Corrections in Voter ID:** 15–30 days after application

## Important Tips

1. **Check before every election** — Electoral rolls are updated periodically. Verify your name is in the voter list well before election day to avoid last-minute issues.
2. **Keep your mobile number updated** — Link your mobile number with your Voter ID for OTP-based services like e-EPIC download. File Form 6B to update it.
3. **Use the correct spelling** — When searching by details, enter your name exactly as registered. Try variations if your name is not found.
4. **EPIC number format** — Your Voter ID number is a 10-character alphanumeric code (3 letters followed by 7 digits, e.g., XYZ1234567). It is printed on the front of your physical Voter ID card.
5. **Report errors immediately** — If you find incorrect details in your voter record, file **Form 8** on the Voters' Services Portal to request corrections.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I find my Voter ID (EPIC) number?

Your EPIC number is printed on the front of your physical Voter ID card. It is a 10-character alphanumeric code (e.g., ABC1234567). If you don't have the physical card, search by personal details on [electoralsearch.eci.gov.in](https://electoralsearch.eci.gov.in) to find it.

### Can I verify my Voter ID without the EPIC number?

Yes. You can search the electoral roll using your personal details (name, father's name, age, gender, state, district, and constituency) on the Electoral Search portal.

### What should I do if my name is not in the voter list?

If your name is missing from the electoral roll, you need to apply for a new Voter ID by filing **Form 6** on [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in). You will need proof of age, proof of address, and a passport-size photograph. See our [Voter ID application guide](/guide/voter-id-card-apply-online) for detailed steps.

### How do I download my e-EPIC?

Log in to [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in) with your registered mobile number, click "Download e-EPIC", enter your EPIC number, verify via OTP or face recognition, and download the PDF.

### Is the e-EPIC valid as an official ID proof?

Yes. The e-EPIC (digital Voter ID) is a valid identity document and is accepted as proof of identity for various government and non-government purposes, just like the physical Voter ID card.

### Can I verify Voter ID for any state in India?

Yes. The Electoral Search portal at [electoralsearch.eci.gov.in](https://electoralsearch.eci.gov.in) contains electoral roll data for all states and union territories of India.

### What is the difference between NVSP and Voters' Services Portal?

The National Voter's Service Portal (NVSP) at nvsp.in has been replaced by the new **Voters' Services Portal** at [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in). All services previously available on NVSP are now accessible on the new portal.
