# Personal Loan Apply Online — Interest Rates, Eligibility & Best Banks Comparison

A **personal loan** is an unsecured loan offered by banks and NBFCs for any legitimate personal purpose — medical emergencies, weddings, home renovation, travel, debt consolidation, or higher education. Since no collateral is required, approval depends on your income, credit score, and employment profile.

In this comprehensive guide, we compare **interest rates from 10+ banks**, explain eligibility criteria, walk you through the online application process, and help you choose the best personal loan in India.

---

## What Is a Personal Loan?

A personal loan is a **multipurpose, unsecured loan** that you can use for virtually any personal expense. Unlike a [home loan](/guide/home-loan-guide-india) or [gold loan](/guide/gold-loan-comparison-guide), you don't need to pledge any asset as security.

### Key Features

- **Loan amount:** ₹50,000 to ₹40 lakh (up to ₹75 lakh for premium salaried)
- **Tenure:** 12 to 60 months (1–5 years)
- **Interest rate:** 10.25% to 24% p.a. (depends on profile)
- **No collateral** required
- **Quick disbursal** — often within 24–48 hours
- **Minimal documentation** for existing bank customers

### When Should You Take a Personal Loan?

- **Medical emergencies** — immediate funds without liquidating investments
- **Wedding expenses** — cover costs without draining savings
- **Home renovation** — when amount is too small for a home loan
- **Debt consolidation** — replace high-interest credit card debt
- **Travel or education** — when dedicated loan products don't fit
- **Urgent business needs** — bridge short-term gaps (also consider [Mudra Loan](/guide/mudra-loan-apply-online))

> **Tip:** Avoid personal loans for speculative investments or discretionary spending. The interest cost adds up quickly.

## Personal Loan Eligibility Criteria

Banks evaluate the following factors when processing your personal loan application:

### Basic Eligibility

| Parameter | Salaried | Self-Employed |
|-----------|----------|---------------|
| **Age** | 21–60 years | 25–65 years |
| **Minimum income** | ₹15,000–₹25,000/month | ₹3–5 lakh/year |
| **Employment** | Minimum 1–2 years total, 6 months in current job | 3+ years in business |
| **Credit score** | 700+ (750+ for best rates) | 700+ |
| **Nationality** | Indian resident | Indian resident |

### Factors That Improve Your Chances

- **Credit score above 750** — significantly lower interest rates
- **Working for a listed/MNC/government employer** — banks have preferred employer lists
- **Existing relationship** with the bank (salary account, FD, etc.)
- **Low existing EMI burden** — FOIR (Fixed Obligations to Income Ratio) below 50%
- **Stable employment history** — no frequent job changes

## How to Apply for a Personal Loan Online

### Option 1: SBI YONO App

1. Download the **SBI YONO** app and log in with your credentials
2. Go to **"Loans"** → **"Personal Loan"**
3. Check your **pre-approved offer** (if available, approval is instant)
4. Enter the **loan amount** and preferred **tenure**
5. Verify your details — KYC, income, and employment
6. Accept the terms and **e-sign** the loan agreement
7. Loan amount credited to your SBI account within **minutes** (pre-approved) or **2–3 working days**

### Option 2: HDFC Bank Online

1. Visit [HDFC Bank Personal Loan page](https://www.hdfcbank.com/personal/borrow/popular-loans/personal-loan)
2. Click **"Apply Now"** and enter your **mobile number** linked to HDFC
3. Complete **OTP verification**
4. Fill in employment details, income, and desired loan amount
5. Upload required **documents** (salary slips, bank statements)
6. HDFC will run a **credit check** and share the offer
7. Accept and **e-sign** — disbursement within **4 hours** for pre-approved customers

### Option 3: ICICI Bank iMobile / Website

1. Log in to **iMobile Pay** app or visit ICICI Bank website
2. Navigate to **"Loans"** → **"Personal Loan"**
3. Check **instant loan offers** for existing customers
4. Enter loan amount, tenure, and purpose
5. Upload documents if prompted
6. Accept the offer and complete **video KYC** (if required)
7. Funds credited within **24 hours**

> **Pro tip:** If you have a salary account with a bank, always check that bank first. Pre-approved offers have lower rates and instant disbursal.

## Personal Loan EMI Calculation Examples

Use this formula: **EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n / [(1 + r)^n – 1]**

Where P = loan amount, r = monthly interest rate, n = number of months.

### Example 1: ₹5 Lakh Loan

| Tenure | Interest Rate | Monthly EMI | Total Interest Paid |
|--------|--------------|-------------|-------------------|
| 3 years | 11.00% | ₹16,369 | ₹89,284 |
| 5 years | 11.00% | ₹10,871 | ₹1,52,260 |

> **Shorter tenure = higher EMI but lower total interest.** Choose based on your repayment capacity.

## Tips to Get the Lowest Personal Loan Interest Rate

1. **Maintain a credit score above 750** — this is the single biggest factor
2. **Apply through your salary account bank** — pre-approved offers are 1–3% cheaper
3. **Compare across 4–5 lenders** before applying (use aggregator sites)
4. **Avoid multiple applications** — each hard inquiry reduces your credit score
5. **Negotiate with your bank** — especially if you have FDs, insurance, or investments with them
6. **Choose shorter tenure** — some banks offer lower rates for shorter repayment periods
7. **Look for festive offers** — Diwali, New Year, and year-end often have discounted rates
8. **Consider government banks** — SBI, BOB, PNB often have lower rates than private banks
9. **Add a co-applicant** with good credit if your profile is weak
10. **Consolidate existing debt** — lower your FOIR before applying

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What is the minimum salary required for a personal loan?

Most banks require a minimum monthly salary of ₹15,000–₹25,000 for salaried individuals. Government bank requirements are generally lower.

### Can I get a personal loan with a credit score below 700?

It is difficult but possible. Some NBFCs like Bajaj Finserv and Tata Capital may approve loans at higher interest rates (18%–26%) for scores between 650–700. Below 650, approval is unlikely.

### How fast can I get a personal loan?

Pre-approved customers of HDFC, ICICI, and SBI can get loans disbursed within **minutes to 4 hours**. For new customers, it typically takes **2–5 working days** after document verification.

### Is personal loan interest tax deductible?

Personal loan interest is **not tax deductible** unless the loan is used for home renovation (Section 24), business purposes (business expense), or higher education (Section 80E applies only to education loans, not personal loans used for education).

### What is FOIR and why does it matter?

FOIR (Fixed Obligations to Income Ratio) is the percentage of your income going towards existing EMIs. Banks prefer FOIR below 40–50%. If your FOIR is high, your loan may be rejected or offered at a higher rate.

### Can I prepay my personal loan early?

Yes. For floating-rate personal loans, RBI prohibits prepayment penalties for individual borrowers. For fixed-rate loans, banks may charge 2–5% of the outstanding amount.

### What happens if I miss a personal loan EMI?

Missing an EMI results in a **late payment fee** (₹500–₹1,000 + GST), a negative mark on your credit report, and potential increase in interest rate. Three consecutive missed EMIs can lead to the loan being classified as NPA.

### Can self-employed individuals get a personal loan?

Yes. Self-employed professionals (doctors, CAs, engineers) and business owners can apply. They need to provide ITR for the last 2–3 years, business registration, and bank statements.

### Is it better to take a personal loan or use a credit card?

For amounts above ₹1 lakh and tenure beyond 12 months, a personal loan is almost always cheaper. Credit card revolving interest can be 36–42% p.a. — one of the most expensive forms of borrowing.

### How many personal loans can I have at once?

There is no legal limit, but having multiple active personal loans increases your FOIR and reduces your credit score. Most banks will be reluctant to approve a second personal loan if one is already active.

### Can NRIs apply for personal loans in India?

Some banks like SBI, HDFC, and ICICI offer personal loans to NRIs, but eligibility criteria are stricter, rates may be higher, and you need an NRE/NRO account.

### What is the difference between flat and reducing interest rate?

A **flat rate** calculates interest on the full loan amount throughout tenure. A **reducing rate** calculates interest only on the outstanding balance, making it cheaper. A 12% flat rate is roughly equivalent to a 21–22% reducing rate. Always compare in reducing rate terms.
