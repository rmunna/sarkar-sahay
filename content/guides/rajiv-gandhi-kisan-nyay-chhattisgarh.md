## What is Rajiv Gandhi Kisan Nyay Yojana?

Rajiv Gandhi Kisan Nyay Yojana (RGKNY) is a farmer welfare scheme launched by the Government of Chhattisgarh in **May 2020** to provide input subsidy directly to farmers. The scheme aims to ensure that farmers receive fair returns on their crops by providing an input cost subsidy per acre of cultivated land through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

Under RGKNY, registered farmers receive **₹9,000 per acre per year** (for paddy and other notified crops) as input subsidy. The scheme covers **paddy, maize, sugarcane, soybean, groundnut, and other notified crops** grown in Chhattisgarh. For sugarcane, farmers receive the difference between the Fair and Remunerative Price (FRP) and the actual payment, up to ₹355 per quintal.

> **Note:** This scheme was launched by the Congress government. With the change in government in December 2023, the scheme's operational status may be subject to changes. Check the official portal for the latest updates.

## Key Benefits

| Crop | Input Subsidy |
|------|--------------|
| Paddy | ₹9,000 per acre/year |
| Maize | ₹9,000 per acre/year |
| Sugarcane | FRP difference (up to ₹355/quintal) |
| Soybean | ₹9,000 per acre/year |
| Groundnut | ₹9,000 per acre/year |
| Other notified crops | As per government notification |

### Additional Benefits
- Encourages **crop diversification** — Farmers growing crops other than paddy also receive input subsidy
- **Landless cultivators** who cultivate on lease land are also covered
- Payments through **DBT** ensure transparency
- Supports both **Kharif and Rabi** season crops

## Who is Eligible?

### Eligible Farmers
- Must be a **resident of Chhattisgarh**
- Must be a **registered farmer** on the Integrated Kisan Portal (kisan.cg.nic.in)
- Must cultivate **notified crops** (paddy, maize, sugarcane, soybean, groundnut, etc.)
- Both **landowner farmers** and **tenant/lease farmers** are eligible
- Must have sold crops through **registered procurement centres** or cooperative societies

### Who is NOT Eligible
- Farmers who have not registered on the Kisan Portal
- Farmers who have not sown notified crops
- Institutional landholders (trusts, societies, etc.)
- Farmers whose land records are not updated

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar Card** (mandatory for DBT)
- **Land ownership documents** — Khasra/B1 (for landowners)
- **Lease agreement** (for tenant farmers — registered with Patwari)
- **Bank passbook** (Aadhaar-linked)
- **Ration Card**
- **Sowing certificate** from the Patwari/Gram Panchayat
- **Crop sale receipt** from procurement centre
- **Mobile number** linked to Aadhaar

## How to Register and Apply

### Online Registration on Kisan Portal

1. Visit the **Integrated Kisan Portal** at [kisan.cg.nic.in](https://kisan.cg.nic.in)
2. Click on **"नवीन पंजीयन" (New Registration)**
3. Enter your **Aadhaar number** and complete OTP verification
4. Fill in personal details — name, father's name, address, district, block, village
5. Enter **land details** — Khasra number, area, crop sown
6. Enter **bank account details** (must be Aadhaar-linked)
7. Upload documents (land record, Aadhaar, passbook)
8. Submit the registration
9. Note the **registration number** for tracking

### Crop Declaration (Every Season)

1. Login to [kisan.cg.nic.in](https://kisan.cg.nic.in)
2. Go to **"फसल का विवरण" (Crop Details)**
3. Enter **current season crop details** — crop name, area (in acres), Khasra number
4. Submit the declaration before the **prescribed deadline**
5. The Patwari will verify the sowing details
6. After verification, your name is included in the beneficiary list

### Offline Registration

1. Visit the **nearest Cooperative Society** or **Krishi Upaj Mandi**
2. Collect the **registration form**
3. Fill in all details with land and crop information
4. Attach document copies
5. Submit to the society/mandi office
6. They will register you on the Kisan Portal

### Check Payment Status

1. Visit [kisan.cg.nic.in](https://kisan.cg.nic.in)
2. Click on **"भुगतान की स्थिति" (Payment Status)**
3. Enter your **registration number** or **Aadhaar number**
4. View payment history and pending amounts

## Payment Schedule

- Payments are disbursed in **4 installments** per year
- Disbursement is done after verification of crop sowing and land records
- Payments credited to **Aadhaar-linked bank accounts** via DBT
- Each installment is approximately **₹2,250 per acre** (for annual total of ₹9,000)

## Fees

- **Registration** — Free of cost
- **Application** — No charges
- **Portal access** — Free

## Processing Time

- Registration: **Immediate** (online) or **7-15 days** (offline)
- Crop verification by Patwari: **15-30 days** after declaration
- Payment after verification: **30-60 days** per government schedule

## Important Tips

1. **Register before the deadline** — Each crop season has a registration deadline. Late registration may disqualify you for that season's payment
2. **Update crop details every season** — You must declare your crop for both Kharif and Rabi seasons to receive the full annual subsidy
3. **Keep land records updated** — Ensure your Khasra/B1 records are current at the Tehsil office
4. **Tenant farmers must register leases** — If you farm on leased land, get the lease registered with the Patwari to be eligible
5. **Sell through official channels** — Selling crops through registered procurement centres strengthens your claim

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. How much money do farmers get under RGKNY?
Farmers receive **₹9,000 per acre per year** as input subsidy for cultivating notified crops like paddy, maize, soybean, etc.

### Q2. Can tenant farmers apply?
Yes. Tenant/lease farmers are eligible if they have a **registered lease agreement** with the landowner, certified by the Patwari.

### Q3. Where do I register for RGKNY?
Register on the **Integrated Kisan Portal** at [kisan.cg.nic.in](https://kisan.cg.nic.in) or visit your nearest Cooperative Society office.

### Q4. Do I need to register every year?
You need to register once. However, you must **declare your crop details every season** (Kharif and Rabi) on the portal.

### Q5. Is RGKNY similar to PM Kisan?
Both provide financial support to farmers, but they are different schemes. PM Kisan gives ₹6,000/year from the central government, while RGKNY gives ₹9,000/acre/year from the Chhattisgarh government. Eligible farmers can receive both.

### Q6. Is the scheme still running after the 2023 election?
The scheme was a Congress government initiative. With the BJP government in Chhattisgarh from December 2023, check [kisan.cg.nic.in](https://kisan.cg.nic.in) for the latest status and any modifications.
