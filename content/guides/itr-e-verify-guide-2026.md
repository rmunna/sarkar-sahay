# ITR E-Verification 2026 — How to Verify ITR in 30 Days (All Methods)

Filing your ITR is only half the job. After filing, you must **e-verify** (electronically verify) it within **30 days**. An unverified ITR is treated as if it was never filed — no refund, no losses carried forward, and you could be deemed a non-filer.

> **Budget 2022 change**: The verification window was reduced from 120 days to **30 days** (effective August 1, 2022). For ITRs filed from August 2022 onwards, you have only 30 days to verify.

## What Is E-Verification?

E-verification is your digital signature confirming that the ITR was filed by you (not by someone else using your credentials). It replaces the physical signature requirement.

**Before e-verify**: ITR is in "pending verification" status — not processed, no refund.
**After e-verify**: ITR moves to "successfully verified" — processing begins, refund credited.

## E-Verify Methods — All Options

### Method 1: Aadhaar OTP (Easiest — Recommended)

**Requirement**: Your Aadhaar must be linked to your PAN (mandatory since July 1, 2023).

**Steps**:
1. Log in to incometax.gov.in
2. Go to e-File → Income Tax Returns → e-Verify Return
3. Select your ITR → Click "e-Verify"
4. Choose "Generate Aadhaar OTP"
5. OTP is sent to your Aadhaar-registered mobile number
6. Enter OTP → Verified ✅

**Time**: 2 minutes. No preparation needed.

**If Aadhaar mobile number is outdated**: You must update your mobile in Aadhaar at an Aadhaar Seva Kendra (not online) before using this method.

### Method 2: Net Banking EVC

**Requirement**: Net banking with a bank that offers EVC (most major banks do).

**Steps**:
1. Log in to your bank's net banking portal
2. Go to IT Department → Generate EVC (or Tax → e-Verify ITR)
3. An EVC (Electronic Verification Code) is generated — valid for 72 hours
4. Copy the EVC and go to incometax.gov.in → e-Verify ITR
5. Enter the EVC → Verified ✅

**Banks that support EVC**: SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, Bank of Baroda, Canara Bank, PNB, and most other major banks.

### Method 3: DEMAT Account EVC

**Requirement**: DEMAT account with CDSL or NSDL where your PAN is linked.

**Steps**:
1. Log in to your broker's platform (Zerodha, Groww, Angel, etc.)
2. Find the "Generate EVC for ITR" option (usually under Profile or Tax Tools)
3. Generate EVC → Copy the code
4. Enter at incometax.gov.in → e-Verify ITR ✅

### Method 4: Bank Account EVC (via IT Portal)

**Requirement**: Bank account pre-validated on incometax.gov.in.

**Steps**:
1. On incometax.gov.in, go to Profile → Bank Accounts → Pre-validate
2. Enter your bank account details and OTP
3. Once pre-validated, the bank account appears as an EVC option
4. Generate EVC → Enter on e-verify page ✅

### Method 5: Bank ATM

**Requirement**: ATM of your bank that supports EVC generation.

**Steps**:
1. Visit your bank's ATM
2. Go to "Generate EVC for Income Tax" option
3. OTP or EVC is sent to your registered mobile
4. Enter at incometax.gov.in → e-Verify ✅

**Banks offering ATM EVC**: SBI, Canara Bank, Union Bank (ATM availability varies; check your bank's website).

### Method 6: DSC (Digital Signature Certificate)

**For**: Individuals required to get accounts audited (business income above threshold), or those who prefer DSC for security.

**Requirement**: DSC token (USB dongle) registered with the IT department.

**Steps**:
1. Download and install ITD e-Filing DSC Management Utility
2. Generate a signature file using your DSC token
3. Upload on incometax.gov.in → e-Verify → Upload DSC Signature ✅

DSC is typically used by companies and auditors, not ordinary individual taxpayers.

### Method 7: Physical Verification (Last Resort)

**If none of the above work**:
1. Download ITR-V (the acknowledgement PDF) from incometax.gov.in → e-File → View Filed Returns → Download
2. Print the ITR-V (single page, A4)
3. Sign with blue ink
4. **Speed Post** to:
   - CPC, Income Tax Department, Bengaluru – 560 500
5. Must reach CPC within **30 days** of ITR filing

**Note**: Ordinary post or courier is not accepted — only **Speed Post**. Registered post is also acceptable.

## What Happens If You Don't E-Verify in 30 Days?

- Your ITR is treated as **invalid** — as if never filed
- No refund will be processed
- You cannot carry forward capital losses
- You may receive a notice for non-filing

**To revive a lapsed ITR**:
- You can still verify it later by submitting a **condonation request** to your Jurisdictional Income Tax Officer or PCIT
- Or file a belated ITR if the December 31 deadline hasn't passed

## How to Check If Your ITR Is Verified

1. Log in to incometax.gov.in
2. Go to e-File → Income Tax Returns → View Filed Returns
3. Status should show: **"ITR Status: Successfully e-Verified"**
4. Also check for: "Return Processed" (comes later, after CPC processes it)

**Other statuses you may see**:
- **"Pending e-Verification"**: You haven't verified yet — do it now
- **"Verification Pending (Physical)"**: You chose physical ITR-V route
- **"Under Processing"**: Verified, CPC is processing — wait
- **"Processed with Refund"**: Your refund is coming
- **"Processed with Demand"**: You owe tax — pay via Challan 280

## Aadhaar OTP Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|---------|
| "Aadhaar not linked to PAN" | Link at incometax.gov.in first (fees apply if linked late) |
| OTP not received | Check Aadhaar-registered mobile number; if outdated, update at Aadhaar Seva Kendra |
| OTP expired (10 minutes) | Generate a new OTP and try again immediately |
| "Service unavailable" | IT portal downtime — try after 1–2 hours |
| Wrong OTP entered 3 times | Session locked; wait 30 minutes and try again |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I filed ITR in July but forgot to verify. It's now September (more than 30 days). What do I do?

Submit a **condonation of delay request** on incometax.gov.in under "Services → Condonation Request". Explain the reason for delay. If approved, the department may allow you to verify the pending ITR. Alternatively, file a belated/revised ITR (if within December 31).

### Can I e-verify someone else's ITR (e.g., my parent's)?

If you are the authorized representative for a taxpayer (e.g., for a deceased or legally incapacitated person), you can use the "Representative" login. For ordinary cases, each taxpayer must verify their own ITR using their Aadhaar OTP or net banking.

### I live abroad. How do I e-verify my ITR?

If you have an Indian SIM card with the Aadhaar-registered number on roaming, Aadhaar OTP works. If not, use Net Banking EVC (any Indian bank's net banking from abroad works). If neither is accessible, send the signed ITR-V via Speed Post from India (ask a family member).

### My refund was supposed to come but I see "pending verification" in my ITR. Is my refund stuck?

Yes — the IT Department doesn't process returns or release refunds until the ITR is verified. Verify immediately using any of the methods above. Refund processing typically begins after successful verification.
