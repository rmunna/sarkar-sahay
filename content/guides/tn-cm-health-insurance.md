# CM's Comprehensive Health Insurance Scheme (CMCHIS) Tamil Nadu

## What is CMCHIS?

The Chief Minister's Comprehensive Health Insurance Scheme (CMCHIS) provides **free medical and surgical treatment** up to **₹5 lakh per family per year** at government and empanelled private hospitals. The scheme covers over **1,000 medical procedures** including surgeries, diagnostics, and follow-up care. It has been integrated with the central Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY scheme.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **All families** with annual income below **₹1,20,000** (₹1.2 lakh)
2. Must hold a valid **rice ration card** issued by Tamil Nadu
3. Must be a **resident of Tamil Nadu**
4. **All family members** listed on the ration card are covered
5. No age limit — covers newborns to senior citizens

## Coverage Details

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Coverage Amount | ₹5,00,000 per family per year |
| Procedures Covered | 1,000+ medical/surgical procedures |
| Hospitals | Government + empanelled private hospitals |
| Pre-existing Conditions | Covered from day one |
| Follow-up Care | Covered (including medicines for 10 days post-discharge) |

### What is Covered?

- Heart surgeries (bypass, valve replacement)
- Cancer treatment (surgery, chemotherapy, radiation)
- Kidney transplant and dialysis
- Joint replacement
- Neurosurgery
- Eye surgeries (cataract, retina)
- Burns treatment
- Accident and trauma care
- And many more (1,000+ procedures)

## Documents Required

- **Rice Ration Card** (Tamil Nadu)
- **Aadhaar Card** of the patient
- **CMCHIS Card** (if already enrolled)
- **Hospital referral** (if going from government to private hospital)
- **Medical records** of the condition

## How to Avail CMCHIS Treatment

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Visit** a government hospital or any CMCHIS-empanelled private hospital
2. **Show** your ration card and Aadhaar card at the **CMCHIS help desk** in the hospital
3. **Biometric verification** — fingerprint authentication to confirm identity
4. **Doctor examines** and recommends the required procedure
5. **Pre-authorization** — the hospital sends the treatment request to CMCHIS for approval
6. **Approval received** (usually within hours for emergencies)
7. **Treatment performed** — completely free for the patient
8. **Post-treatment** — medicines for 10 days and follow-up consultation covered

### Finding Empanelled Hospitals

1. Visit [cmchistn.com](https://www.cmchistn.com)
2. Click on "Hospital List" or "Network Hospitals"
3. Search by district to find empanelled hospitals near you

## How to Get the CMCHIS Card

- If you have a valid rice ration card, you are **automatically eligible**
- Visit any government hospital or CMCHIS centre with your ration card and Aadhaar
- The CMCHIS card/e-card is generated instantly after biometric verification
- In many cases, just the ration card + Aadhaar is sufficient (no separate card needed)

## Processing Time

- **Emergency Treatment:** Immediate (no pre-authorization needed)
- **Planned Surgery:** Pre-authorization takes 1-3 days
- **CMCHIS Card:** Instant (at hospital help desk)

## Important Tips

1. **Carry Ration Card Always:** This is your primary proof of eligibility. Keep it accessible.
2. **Emergency = No Waiting:** For emergencies, hospitals must treat first. Pre-authorization happens in parallel.
3. **Check Hospital Empanelment:** Not all private hospitals are empanelled. Verify on the website before going.
4. **Use Government Hospitals First:** Government hospitals are always covered. For private hospitals, a referral may be needed.
5. **Keep Medical Records:** Maintain all previous treatment records — they help in faster approval.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Is CMCHIS the same as Ayushman Bharat?
CMCHIS has been **integrated with Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY** in Tamil Nadu. The combined scheme provides ₹5 lakh coverage.

### Q2: Can I use CMCHIS at any private hospital?
**No.** Only at CMCHIS-empanelled private hospitals. Check the list at [cmchistn.com](https://www.cmchistn.com).

### Q3: Is there a waiting period for pre-existing diseases?
**No.** Pre-existing conditions are covered from day one.

### Q4: What if my ₹5 lakh limit is exhausted?
If the ₹5 lakh annual limit is exhausted, you can seek treatment at government hospitals where treatment continues to be free.

### Q5: Are medicines covered?
Post-discharge medicines for up to **10 days** and follow-up consultations are covered under the scheme.

### Q6: Can senior citizens use CMCHIS?
**Yes.** There is no age limit. All family members on the ration card, including senior citizens, are covered.
