# Bhulekh UP: Check Land Record Online Uttar Pradesh

**Bhulekh UP** is the official online portal of the Uttar Pradesh government for checking land records. Through [upbhulekh.gov.in](https://upbhulekh.gov.in/), you can view Khasra Khatauni records, search by Gata number, check revenue court status, and access village-wise land details. This guide covers everything you need to know.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent informational platform and is not affiliated with any government department. Always verify details on official government portals.

## Key Terms in UP Land Records

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Khasra** | Plot-level record showing area, soil type, crop, and owner |
| **Khatauni** | Ownership record linking multiple Khasra plots to an owner |
| **Gata Number** | Unique plot identification number (same as Khasra number in UP) |
| **Khata Number** | Account number linking an owner to their land holdings |
| **Fasli Year** | Revenue year used in UP land records (starts from July) |
| **Bhulekh** | Literally "land record" in Hindi |

## How to Check Khasra Khatauni Online

### Step-by-Step Process

1. Visit [upbhulekh.gov.in](https://upbhulekh.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"खतौनी (अधिकार अभिलेख) की नकल देखें"** (View Khatauni Copy)
3. Enter the **captcha code**
4. Select **जनपद (District)** from the dropdown
5. Select **तहसील (Tehsil)**
6. Select **ग्राम (Village)** — you can search by village name or village code
7. Choose your search method:
   - **खसरा/गाटा संख्या द्वारा** — By Khasra/Gata Number
   - **खाता संख्या द्वारा** — By Khata Number
   - **खातेदार के नाम द्वारा** — By Owner Name
   - **नामांतरण दिनांक से** — By Mutation Date
8. Enter the relevant number or name
9. Click **"उद्धरण देखें"** (View Extract)
10. The Khatauni record will display with owner name, area, and plot details
11. Click **"Print"** to save or download

## How to Search by Gata Number

The **Gata number** is the most reliable way to search for a specific plot:

1. Go to [upbhulekh.gov.in](https://upbhulekh.gov.in/)
2. Select **Khatauni (Record of Rights)**
3. Navigate: **District → Tehsil → Village**
4. Select **"खसरा/गाटा संख्या द्वारा"** (By Khasra/Gata Number)
5. Enter the Gata number (e.g., 123 or 123/1 for sub-division)
6. Click submit to view the complete record

**Where to find Gata number:** Your sale deed, property tax receipt, or previous Khatauni copy will have the Gata number.

## How to Check Village Map (Bhu Naksha UP)

1. Visit [bhunaksha.up.nic.in](https://bhunaksha.up.nic.in/)
2. Select **District → Tehsil → RI → Halka → Village** from the dropdowns
3. The village map loads with all plots marked
4. Click on any plot to view:
   - Gata/Khasra number
   - Owner name
   - Land area
   - Land use type
5. Use **"Map Report"** to download the plot map
6. Use **"Print"** for a printable version

## How to Check Revenue Court Status

If there is a land dispute or revenue case pending, you can check its status online:

1. Visit [upbhulekh.gov.in](https://upbhulekh.gov.in/)
2. Look for **"राजस्व न्यायालय"** (Revenue Court) section
3. Search by:
   - **Case number**
   - **Party name**
   - **Village and Gata number**
4. View case status, hearing dates, and orders

This is useful to verify that land you are purchasing does not have any **pending revenue disputes**.

## How to Check Land Record by Owner Name

1. Visit [upbhulekh.gov.in](https://upbhulekh.gov.in/)
2. Go to **Khatauni section**
3. Select **District → Tehsil → Village**
4. Choose **"खातेदार के नाम द्वारा"** (By Owner Name)
5. Type the owner's name (in Hindi or English depending on the record)
6. Select the matching name from the suggestions
7. View all land holdings of that owner in the village

## Important Tips

1. **Use Hindi names** when searching by owner name — most UP records are in Hindi (Devanagari script)
2. **Check the Fasli year** — Ensure you are viewing the latest revenue year's records
3. **Cross-verify with Bhu Naksha** — Match the Khatauni details with the village map for boundary confirmation
4. **Revenue court check is essential** before purchasing any property — this confirms no pending disputes
5. **For certified copies**, visit the **Tehsil office** or use the [UP e-District portal](https://edistrict.up.gov.in/) for online applications

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between Khasra and Khatauni?

**Khasra** is the plot-level record showing area, soil type, crops grown, and land use for each Gata number. **Khatauni** is the ownership record that groups multiple Khasra plots under an owner's name (Khata).

### Can I download Khatauni from Bhulekh UP?

Yes, you can view and print the Khatauni from [upbhulekh.gov.in](https://upbhulekh.gov.in/). Use the print/save as PDF option in your browser. However, for official use, get a **certified copy** from the Tehsil office.

### What is Gata number in UP land records?

**Gata number** is the unique plot identification number assigned to each land parcel in UP. It is the same as the Khasra number and is the most reliable way to search for a specific plot.

### How to check if land has any dispute in UP?

Check the **Revenue Court Status** section on upbhulekh.gov.in. Also get an **Encumbrance Certificate** from the Sub-Registrar office. See our [Land Dispute Resolution guide](/guide/land-dispute-resolution-guide) for more details.

### Is Bhulekh UP available in English?

The portal interface has some English elements, but most land records are maintained in **Hindi (Devanagari script)**. Owner names, village names, and land details will be in Hindi.

### What if my name is not showing in Khatauni?

If you recently purchased land, the **mutation (Namantar)** may not be completed yet. Apply for mutation at the Tehsil office with your registered sale deed. Once mutation is done, your name will appear in the Khatauni.

### How to get land record correction in UP?

Visit the **Tehsildar office** with supporting documents (sale deed, Aadhaar, previous records). File an application for correction. The Tehsildar will verify and update the records. For disputes, approach the **Revenue Court** or **SDM (Sub-Divisional Magistrate)**.
