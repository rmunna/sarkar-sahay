## What is Aadhaar eSign?

Aadhaar eSign is an online electronic signature service that allows any Aadhaar holder to digitally sign documents using OTP (One-Time Password) verification. It is a legally valid way to sign documents without needing a physical Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) dongle.

Unlike a [Digital Signature Certificate (DSC)](/guide/digital-signature-certificate-dsc) which requires purchasing a USB token, Aadhaar eSign is simple, fast, and works entirely online — you just need your Aadhaar number and a linked mobile number.

Aadhaar eSign is:
- **OTP-based** — no hardware token or USB dongle needed
- **Legally valid** — recognized under the IT Act, 2000 and its amendments
- **Instant** — the entire signing process takes under 2 minutes
- **Paperless** — no printing, scanning, or courier required

## How Does Aadhaar eSign Work?

The Aadhaar eSign process involves three parties — you (the signer), the application service provider (ASP), and UIDAI's authentication system.

**Step-by-step flow:**

1. You upload a document on a platform that supports Aadhaar eSign
2. The platform redirects you to an eSign service provider (ESP) page
3. You enter your Aadhaar number
4. UIDAI sends an OTP to your Aadhaar-linked mobile number
5. You enter the OTP to authenticate your identity
6. A digital signature is generated and attached to the document
7. The signed document (PDF) is returned to you

The entire process is powered by **eSign API** developed by **C-DAC** and governed by the **Controller of Certifying Authorities (CCA)** under the Ministry of Electronics & IT.

## Legal Validity of Aadhaar eSign

Aadhaar eSign is **legally valid** across India. Here's the legal framework:

| Law / Regulation | Relevance |
|---|---|
| **IT Act, 2000 (Section 3A)** | Recognizes electronic signatures as valid |
| **IT Act Amendment, 2008** | Introduced the concept of "electronic signature" beyond just digital signature |
| **eSign Guidelines (CCA, 2015)** | Governs the eSign ecosystem, ESP licensing |
| **Aadhaar Act, 2016** | Enables Aadhaar-based authentication for eSign |
| **Indian Evidence Act** | e-Signed documents are admissible as evidence |

**Key point:** An Aadhaar eSign carries the same legal weight as a wet (physical) signature for most documents. However, some documents like property registration and certain court filings may still require physical signatures or registered DSC as per state-specific rules.

## Who Can Use Aadhaar eSign?

**Eligibility:**
- Any Indian resident with a valid Aadhaar card
- Mobile number must be linked to Aadhaar (for OTP verification)
- No age restriction — minors with Aadhaar can also use eSign (through guardian consent where applicable)

**To check if your mobile is linked to Aadhaar**, visit the [UIDAI portal](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in) or see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) for help.

## How to Use Aadhaar eSign — Step-by-Step Process

### Method 1: eSign via DigiLocker

[DigiLocker](/guide/digilocker-document-download-guide) is a government platform that supports free Aadhaar eSign.

1. Visit [digilocker.gov.in](https://www.digilocker.gov.in) and log in
2. Go to **Upload Documents** section
3. Upload the PDF you want to sign
4. Click **eSign** on the uploaded document
5. Enter your Aadhaar number when prompted
6. Enter the OTP received on your Aadhaar-linked mobile
7. The document is signed and stored in your DigiLocker
8. Download the eSigned PDF

**Cost:** Free

### Method 2: eSign via Leegality

Leegality is a popular private platform for eSigning legal documents.

1. Visit [leegality.com](https://leegality.com) and create an account
2. Upload your document (PDF, Word, or image)
3. Add signature fields where required
4. Choose **Aadhaar eSign** as the signing method
5. Enter your Aadhaar number
6. Verify with OTP
7. Download the signed document

**Cost:** Free for basic use; paid plans for bulk signing (₹5–15 per signature on business plans)

### Method 3: eSign via SignDesk

SignDesk is an enterprise-grade eSigning platform.

1. Visit [signdesk.com](https://signdesk.com) and register
2. Upload the document to be signed
3. Add signatories and place signature fields
4. Select **Aadhaar eSign** option
5. Signatories receive an email/SMS with the signing link
6. Each signer authenticates via Aadhaar OTP
7. Signed document is emailed to all parties

**Cost:** ₹5–15 per eSign (varies by plan)

### Method 4: eSign on eStamp/eRegistration Portals

Many state government portals for [rent agreements](/guide/rent-agreement-registration) and stamp duty now support Aadhaar eSign directly:

1. Visit your state's eStamp or eRegistration portal
2. Fill in the agreement/document details
3. Pay stamp duty online
4. At the signing stage, choose **Aadhaar eSign**
5. Both parties authenticate with Aadhaar OTP
6. The eSigned, eStamped document is generated

**Cost:** Free (only stamp duty charges apply)

## Aadhaar eSign vs Digital Signature Certificate (DSC)

| Feature | Aadhaar eSign | DSC |
|---|---|---|
| **Method** | OTP-based | USB token/dongle |
| **Cost** | Free to ₹15 | ₹500–2,000/year |
| **Hardware needed** | No | Yes (USB token) |
| **Legal validity** | Class 2 equivalent | Class 2 or Class 3 |
| **Best for** | Individuals, one-time signing | Businesses, repeated signing |
| **Processing time** | Instant | 1–3 days to obtain |
| **Requires Aadhaar** | Yes | No |

For more on DSC, see our [Digital Signature Certificate guide](/guide/digital-signature-certificate-dsc).

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Is Aadhaar eSign legally valid in India?

Yes. Aadhaar eSign is legally valid under Section 3A of the IT Act, 2000. It is recognized by courts, government departments, banks, and all regulatory bodies in India.

### Can I use Aadhaar eSign without a linked mobile number?

No. Aadhaar eSign requires OTP verification, which is sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile number. If your mobile is not linked, visit an [Aadhaar enrollment center](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) to update it.

### Is Aadhaar eSign free?

On government platforms like DigiLocker and state eStamp portals, Aadhaar eSign is free. Private platforms like Leegality and SignDesk charge ₹5–15 per signature on their paid plans.

### Can Aadhaar eSign be used for property registration?

It depends on the state. Some states like Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Rajasthan allow eSigned rent agreements and leave & license agreements. However, sale deed registration typically still requires physical presence and biometric verification at the Sub-Registrar office.

### How many times can I use Aadhaar eSign?

There is no limit on the number of times you can use Aadhaar eSign. Each signing session generates a fresh OTP.

### Can NRIs use Aadhaar eSign?

Yes, as long as they have a valid Aadhaar number with a linked Indian mobile number that can receive OTP. NRIs with foreign mobile numbers linked to Aadhaar may face issues receiving OTP.

### Is Aadhaar eSign the same as Digital Signature?

Not exactly. Aadhaar eSign is a type of electronic signature that uses Aadhaar OTP for authentication. A Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) uses a cryptographic USB token. Both are legally valid, but they use different technology. See the comparison table above.

### Can a company use Aadhaar eSign for business documents?

Yes. Many companies use Aadhaar eSign for HR documents, vendor agreements, and customer onboarding. Platforms like Leegality and SignDesk offer business plans with bulk eSigning capabilities.
