# Flexi Cap vs Multi Cap Fund India 2026 — Complete Guide

If you want exposure to companies of all sizes (large, mid, small cap) in one fund, flexi cap and multi cap are your options. They sound similar but have an important structural difference that affects risk and returns.

---

## What is a Flexi Cap Fund?

**SEBI definition**: A flexi cap fund must invest minimum 65% in equities. The **allocation across large, mid, and small cap is entirely the fund manager's decision** — no fixed % required.

**In practice**: Most flexi cap funds hold 50–70% in large cap, 20–30% in mid cap, 10–20% in small cap. But during market uncertainty, they can increase large cap to 80%+ for safety.

**Advantage**: The fund manager can reduce small/mid cap exposure during downturns and increase during recoveries — lower volatility than pure mid cap or small cap funds.

---

## What is a Multi Cap Fund?

**SEBI definition** (post 2020 circular): Multi cap funds must maintain minimum:
- **25% in large cap** (top 100 companies)
- **25% in mid cap** (companies 101–250)
- **25% in small cap** (companies 251+)
- Remaining 25%: any combination

**The rigid allocation** means even if small caps are crashing, the fund can't exit below 25%. This makes multi cap inherently higher risk than flexi cap.

## When to Choose Flexi Cap

- **Conservative equity investor**: You want equity returns but can't stomach extreme volatility
- **Primary equity holding**: If flexi cap is your main equity fund (not one of several)
- **Near 5-year goal**: For 5–7 year goals, flexi cap's safer positioning reduces crash risk
- **Want global diversification**: Parag Parikh specifically
- **First equity fund**: Less scary than mid/small cap for first-time investors

---

## When to Choose Multi Cap

- **Long-term aggressive investor**: 10+ year horizon, can ride out 40%+ crashes
- **You already have large cap covered**: Adding multi cap gives mandatory mid/small exposure
- **Want systematic exposure to all segments**: No fund manager bias
- **Complementing a large cap index fund**: Multi cap's mid/small cap mandatory allocation diversifies the large cap heavy index fund

---

## Can I Hold Both Flexi Cap and Multi Cap?

Holding both creates significant overlap — both invest across all cap sizes. The multi cap's mandatory 25% small cap differentiation is the main difference.

**Better portfolio structure than holding both**:

| Portfolio | Allocation |
|----------|-----------|
| Large cap index fund | 40% |
| Flexi cap OR multi cap (not both) | 30% |
| Small cap OR mid cap fund | 20% |
| Gold ETF | 10% |

## Tax on Flexi Cap / Multi Cap Returns

Both are equity funds:
- **LTCG (>12 months)**: 12.5% on gains above ₹1.25L
- **STCG (<12 months)**: 20%
- Both count toward your ₹1.25L annual LTCG exemption

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Parag Parikh Flexi Cap has a US exposure — is there extra tax on that?

Parag Parikh invests in US stocks through its overseas investment quota. The fund is taxed as an Indian equity fund (because its overall equity exposure >65% India + overseas combined). So same LTCG/STCG rates apply — no separate foreign income tax for you.

### Is multi cap better than large cap + mid cap + small cap separately?

One multi cap fund is simpler (one SIP, one folio, one decision). Three separate funds (large/mid/small) give you more control and potentially lower expense ratio if you use index funds. For simplicity: multi cap. For cost efficiency: separate index funds.

### Which is better — flexi cap SIP or multi cap SIP?

Depends on your risk tolerance. Flexi cap is more stable but may slightly underperform multi cap in strong small cap bull markets. Multi cap will outperform in bull markets and underperform (more) in bear markets. If you can't decide, flexi cap is the safer default for most investors.
