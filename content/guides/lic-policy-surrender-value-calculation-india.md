# LIC Policy Surrender Value India 2026 — When to Surrender, How to Calculate, Better Alternatives

Most people holding LIC endowment or money-back policies want to know: should I keep paying, convert to paid-up, or surrender? Here's how to think through it.

---

## What is Surrender Value?

Surrender value is the amount LIC pays you if you cancel your policy before its maturity date.

Two components determine surrender value:

### 1. Guaranteed Surrender Value (GSV)

**Formula** (IRDAI standard):
- Year 1 to 2: Zero (cannot surrender in first 3 years if premiums paid, for most policies)
- From Year 3 onwards: **30% of total premiums paid** (excluding first year's premium and rider premiums)

**Example**: ₹50,000 annual premium, 20-year policy.
- You've paid for 5 years = ₹2,50,000 total premiums
- Exclude first year = ₹2,00,000 (4 years)
- GSV = 30% × ₹2,00,000 = **₹60,000**

This seems low — it is. GSV is the minimum. The actual value is usually higher via Special Surrender Value.

### 2. Special Surrender Value (SSV) — Usually Higher

**Formula**:
- SSV = Paid-up value × applicable surrender value factor

Where:
- **Paid-up value** = (Premiums paid / Total premiums in policy term) × Sum Assured + bonuses accrued
- **Surrender value factor**: Depends on policy duration, age, plan type. Increases with years paid.

LIC doesn't publish a simple SSV formula — you must contact LIC branch or use their online calculator.

**Surrender value = Higher of GSV and SSV**

## Surrender Value by Years Paid (Approximate)

For a standard LIC Jeevan Anand or similar endowment policy:

| Years Premiums Paid | Approximate Surrender % of Total Premiums |
|--------------------|------------------------------------------|
| Less than 3 years | Zero (cannot surrender) |
| 3 years | ~30–35% |
| 5 years | ~40–50% |
| 7 years | ~50–60% |
| 10 years | ~60–70% |
| 15 years | ~75–85% |
| 18+ years | ~85–95% |

**Key insight**: Even at 15 years, you recover only 75–85% of premiums paid. The investment return on LIC endowment is effectively 4–6% — much lower than PPF (7.1%) or ELSS (12–15%).

## Paid-Up Policy — Better Than Surrendering

If you stop paying premiums but DON'T surrender:
- Policy converts to **Paid-Up status** automatically (after 3 years of premiums paid)
- Paid-up sum assured = (Premiums paid / Total premiums) × Original sum assured
- Policy continues for remaining term, just with reduced sum assured
- Bonuses already accrued are also proportionately reduced
- You get paid-up value + reduced bonus at maturity (not surrender value now)

**Example**: ₹5L sum assured, 20-year plan, ₹50K/year premium.
- You've paid for 7 years (₹3.5L paid)
- Paid-up sum assured = 7/20 × ₹5L = **₹1,75,000**
- You get ₹1,75,000 at maturity (20 years from start) without paying any more premiums

**When is Paid-Up better than Surrender?**
- If you have 8+ years remaining to maturity AND you need the life coverage
- If surrender value is very low (below 50% of premiums paid)
- If you have a reason to believe you'll need the death benefit (no term plan)

**When is Surrender better?**
- Policy is losing money relative to alternatives (almost always true)
- You can redeploy the surrender value in something better (ELSS, PPF)
- You have a term plan already (don't need LIC's death benefit)
- Early years where paid-up value is very small

## Tax on LIC Policy Surrender

Section 10(10D) grants tax-free treatment to LIC maturity/surrender proceeds IF:
- Premium is ≤ 10% of sum assured (for policies issued on/after 1 April 2012)
- Premium is ≤ 20% of sum assured (for policies before 1 April 2012)

**If premium > 10% of sum assured**: Surrender/maturity value is taxable as "Income from Other Sources" at slab rate.

**Example**: ₹5L sum assured, annual premium ₹75,000.
- 10% of ₹5L = ₹50,000
- Your premium ₹75,000 > ₹50,000
- **Surrender value is FULLY TAXABLE at your slab rate**

Most money-back and endowment policies sold before 2020 have premiums within the 10% limit — so maturity/surrender is usually tax-free for those. But newer ULIP policies with high coverage often breach this.

## LIC ULIP Surrender vs Endowment Surrender

### LIC Endowment / Money-Back (Traditional)
- 3-year minimum before surrender
- Guaranteed surrender value applies
- Usually better to wait 10+ years before surrendering (value compounds slowly)

### LIC ULIP (Market-linked)
- Higher charges in early years (mortality, fund management, admin)
- Cannot surrender in first 5 years (lock-in under IRDAI guidelines)
- After 5 years: Fund value credited to you minus any outstanding charges
- ULIPs have discontinuance fund during lock-in (money not accessible, earns ~4%)

**Tax on ULIP surrender after 5 years**: If aggregate annual ULIP premiums > ₹2.5L, gains are taxable as LTCG (Budget 2021). Otherwise tax-free under 10(10D).

## How to Surrender LIC Policy

### Online (via LIC Portal)

1. Visit **licindia.in** → Customer Zone → Policy Surrender Online
2. Login with policy number + registered mobile
3. Submit surrender request with bank account details (IFSC + account number)
4. Upload required documents (identity proof, cancelled cheque)
5. LIC processes within 7–15 working days

### At LIC Branch (Walk-in)

1. Visit your nearest LIC branch (wherever policy was issued — can be transferred)
2. Carry: Policy bond (original), identity proof (Aadhaar/PAN), cancelled cheque, Form 3801 (Surrender Form — get from branch)
3. Submit documents
4. Amount typically credited within 15–30 days

## The Real Question: Should You Keep This LIC Policy?

Compare your LIC endowment's effective return:

**Example calculation**:
- Pay ₹50,000/year for 20 years = ₹10,00,000 total premium paid
- Maturity value (including bonus): ~₹13–14L (typical for 20-year endowment)
- CAGR on ₹10L invested over 20 years to get ₹14L: approximately **1.7% CAGR**

Compare to: PPF at 7.1%, ELSS at 12–15%. LIC endowment returns are generally 4–6% pre-bonus, 1–3% when you account for the time value of money.

**The honest calculation**: Almost always, surrender the LIC endowment (after recovering reasonable value), buy a cheap term plan (₹1 crore cover for ₹10,000–₹15,000/year), and invest the remaining premium in PPF + ELSS.

This typically results in 3–5x more wealth at retirement AND higher insurance coverage.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the LIC surrender value after 5 years?

For most traditional LIC policies, surrender value after 5 years is approximately 40–50% of total premiums paid. Exact value depends on plan type, sum assured, and bonuses accrued. Check via licindia.in calculator or visit branch.

### Can I surrender LIC policy after 3 years?

Yes — most LIC traditional policies can be surrendered after 3 years of premium payment. The policy acquires surrender value from the start of the 3rd policy year.

### Is LIC policy surrender value taxable?

If annual premium ≤ 10% of sum assured: Surrender value is TAX-FREE under Section 10(10D). If premium > 10% of sum assured: Entire surrender proceeds are taxable at your slab rate.

### Should I surrender LIC policy or make it paid-up?

Compare: Surrender now (get cash, redeploy) vs Paid-Up (keep coverage, reduced maturity benefit, no more premium). If you have 8+ years to maturity and need the life cover AND have no term plan: Paid-Up may be better. If you have a term plan and can redeploy surrender value: Surrender is usually better.

### How to surrender LIC policy without visiting branch?

Visit licindia.in → Customer Zone → Policy Surrender Online. Requires: policy number, mobile OTP, bank account details. Some policies may require branch visit for original bond submission.
