# Yuva Nidhi Scheme Karnataka – ₹3,000/Month for Unemployed Graduates

## What is Yuva Nidhi Scheme?

Yuva Nidhi is a Karnataka government scheme providing **monthly financial assistance** to unemployed graduates and diploma holders while they search for employment. Graduates receive **₹3,000 per month** and diploma holders receive **₹1,500 per month** for up to **2 years** after completing their degree/diploma.

The scheme aims to support young job seekers during the transition period after education.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Education:** Must have completed a **degree (graduation)** or **diploma** from a recognized institution in Karnataka
2. **Employment Status:** Must be **unemployed** — not employed anywhere (government or private)
3. **Age:** Must have passed the qualifying exam in the **current or immediately preceding academic year**
4. **Residency:** Must be a **domicile of Karnataka**
5. **Family Income:** Annual family income must be below **₹6 lakh**
6. **Registration:** Must be registered on the **Karnataka Employment Exchange**
7. Must not be pursuing further higher education (in some conditions)

### Benefit Amount

| Category | Monthly Amount | Maximum Duration |
|----------|---------------|-----------------|
| Degree Graduates | ₹3,000/month | 2 years |
| Diploma Holders | ₹1,500/month | 2 years |

## Documents Required

- **Degree/Diploma Certificate** or provisional certificate
- **Marks Card** (final year)
- **Aadhaar Card**
- **Bank Account Details** (linked to Aadhaar)
- **Income Certificate** (family income below ₹6 lakh)
- **Domicile Certificate** of Karnataka
- **Employment Exchange Registration** card/number
- **Caste Certificate** (if applicable)
- **Recent Passport Size Photo**

## How to Apply for Yuva Nidhi

### Online Application

1. **Visit** [yuvanidhi.karnataka.gov.in](https://yuvanidhi.karnataka.gov.in)
2. **Register** with your mobile number and create an account
3. **Login** and click on "Apply for Yuva Nidhi"
4. **Enter** personal details — name, address, date of birth
5. **Enter** educational details — degree/diploma, university, year of passing
6. **Upload** documents — degree certificate, marks card, Aadhaar, income certificate
7. **Provide** bank account details for DBT
8. **Submit** the application
9. **Note** the application reference number

### Offline Application

1. Visit the **District Employment Exchange** office
2. Register as a job seeker if not already registered
3. Fill the Yuva Nidhi application form
4. Submit all required documents
5. Collect the acknowledgment receipt

## Verification Process

- Applications are verified by the **Department of Skill Development, Entrepreneurship, and Livelihood**
- University/college records are cross-verified
- Employment status is checked against government databases
- Bank account and Aadhaar linkage is verified

## Processing Time

- **Application Review:** 15-30 days
- **First Payment:** Within 30-45 days of approval
- **Ongoing Payments:** Monthly via DBT

## Important Tips

1. **Register at Employment Exchange:** This is a mandatory requirement. Do it immediately after graduation.
2. **Benefit Stops on Employment:** If you get a job during the 2-year period, the benefit stops. You must report your employment status.
3. **Keep Documents Updated:** If your bank account or address changes, update it on the portal.
4. **Attend Skill Training:** The government may offer skill development programs alongside Yuva Nidhi. Participating can improve your job prospects.
5. **Apply Early:** Benefits are calculated from the date of application, not from the date of degree completion.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Can I get Yuva Nidhi if I'm doing a Master's degree?
Generally, **no**. If you are pursuing further full-time education, you may not be eligible. However, part-time or distance education may be allowed. Check the official guidelines.

### Q2: What if I get a job after 6 months?
Your Yuva Nidhi payments will **stop from the month you start employment**. You are expected to self-report your employment status.

### Q3: Is there a specific percentage required in graduation?
The scheme does not mandate a minimum percentage. However, you must have **passed** the degree/diploma examination.

### Q4: Can I apply if I graduated from a university outside Karnataka?
**No.** You must have completed your degree/diploma from a **recognized institution within Karnataka**.

### Q5: Will the amount be increased?
The amount is set by government policy and may be revised periodically. Currently, it is ₹3,000 for graduates and ₹1,500 for diploma holders.

### Q6: Can I reapply after the 2-year period ends?
**No.** The scheme provides benefits for a maximum of 2 years. After that, the support ends.
