# Income Tax Notice for 26AS / AIS Mismatch — How to Respond (2026)

If your ITR doesn't match the income/TDS data in your AIS (Annual Information Statement) or Form 26AS, the Income Tax Department will send you a notice. Here's exactly what to do.

---

## Why Does This Mismatch Happen?

The IT Department cross-checks your filed ITR against:
- **Form 26AS**: TDS/TCS deducted and deposited, advance tax paid, high-value transactions
- **AIS**: Expanded data — salary, dividends, mutual fund redemptions, property purchase/sale, foreign remittances, savings interest, GST turnover, EPF withdrawals, and 45+ more sources

Common reasons for mismatch:
- You forgot to report freelance/consultant income (TDS at 10% was deducted but you didn't show this income in ITR)
- Dividend income not reported (companies file SFT with IT department; if you received ₹5K+ dividends, they know)
- Interest income from FD not reported (bank filed TDS; you used Form 15G/H but didn't mention interest in ITR)
- Property sale — seller deducted TDS at 1% under Section 194IA; you didn't report capital gains
- Foreign remittances (LRS) — banks file SFT showing money sent abroad; IT department cross-checks for income
- EPF withdrawal shown in AIS but not in ITR (even if tax-exempt, you may need to mention it)
- Mutual fund redemption — CAMS/KFintech file SFT with redemption data; if you redeemed and didn't show capital gains in ITR

## Types of Notices You Might Receive

### Section 143(1)(a) Intimation — Most Common

This is an **intimation** (not a full notice). It comes after processing your ITR and says:
- "Your ITR shows income of ₹X but our records show income/TDS of ₹Y"
- A tax demand is raised or adjustment made

**This is the most common scenario** — IT systems auto-generate this when they spot a discrepancy.

**How to respond**: Within 15 days. You can:
1. Agree with the adjustment (pay the demand)
2. Disagree and file a response explaining the discrepancy

### Section 139(9) Defective Notice

Your return has been considered defective — wrong ITR form, required schedules missing, or income shown doesn't match TDS.

**How to respond**: File a corrected return within 15 days. If you miss it, the return is treated as never filed.

### Section 148A / 148 — Reassessment Notice

More serious. IT department believes income escaped assessment. Usually sent for:
- Large discrepancies (>₹50L in recent years for reassessment to go back 3+ years)
- Foreign assets not disclosed
- High-value transactions with no corresponding income

**This requires careful response** — consider a CA for this notice.

## Step-by-Step Response Process

### Step 1: Download and Compare AIS vs Your ITR

1. Login to [incometax.gov.in](https://incometax.gov.in) with PAN + OTP
2. Go to **Services → Annual Information Statement (AIS)**
3. Download your AIS (full PDF or JSON)
4. Compare each income head in AIS against what you filed in ITR:
   - Salary: Match with Form 16 / 26AS TDS entries
   - Dividends: AIS shows company-wise dividend received
   - Interest income: FD interest, savings account interest
   - Capital gains: MF redemptions, stock sales, property sales
   - Other sources: Freelance TDS, rental income, EPF withdrawal

### Step 2: Identify the Mismatch

Two scenarios:

**Scenario A — AIS is correct, you missed income in ITR**
→ You need to file a revised ITR including the missed income

**Scenario B — AIS has wrong data**
→ You need to raise feedback in AIS portal + explain in notice response

### Step 3A: File Revised ITR (if AIS is correct)

If you forgot to report income:
1. File a **Revised ITR** under Section 139(5)
2. Deadline: December 31 of the Assessment Year (e.g., Dec 31, 2026 for AY 2026-27)
3. In the revised ITR, include the missed income, recalculate tax, and pay any due tax + interest (Section 234A/234B)
4. After filing revised ITR, respond to the notice referencing the revised ITR number

**Filing revised ITR stops most 143(1)(a) demands** — the system reprocesses and if numbers match, demand is withdrawn.

### Step 3B: Raise Feedback in AIS (if AIS is wrong)

If the AIS data is incorrect (e.g., a property purchase was incorrectly shown as sale, or TDS was deducted twice, or someone else's TDS shows in your AIS due to PAN linkage error):

1. Go to AIS portal
2. Click on the incorrect entry
3. Click **"Feedback"** button next to the entry
4. Select the reason: "Income is not taxable", "Income is included in other head", "Duplicate entry", "Income belongs to other person", etc.
5. Submit feedback with explanation
6. IT department reviews feedback — correction can take 15–30 days

**Keep a record** of your feedback submission date and details.

### Step 4: Respond to the Notice Online

1. Login to incometax.gov.in → **Pending Actions → Response to Outstanding Demand**
2. Or go to **e-Proceedings** for notices under Section 143(1), 148, etc.
3. Click on the notice
4. Choose: "Agree" or "Partially Agree" or "Disagree"
5. If disagree: Upload supporting documents (revised ITR acknowledgement, AIS feedback screenshot, bank statements, Form 16, etc.)
6. Write a brief explanation in the text field
7. Submit response

**Deadline**: Always respond within the time mentioned in the notice (usually 15–30 days). Ignoring notices leads to ex-parte assessment and higher penalties.

## Common Scenarios and What To Do

### Scenario 1: FD Interest Not Reported

Your FD interest was ₹52,000. You filed Form 15G/H with the bank, so no TDS was deducted. But the bank still reported the interest in SFT to IT department. AIS shows ₹52,000 interest; your ITR doesn't.

**Fix**: File revised ITR. Add ₹52,000 under "Income from Other Sources → Interest Income". The 80TTA deduction (₹10K on savings bank interest only, not FD) doesn't apply here. Pay tax at slab rate on the full ₹52,000.

### Scenario 2: Dividend Income Missed

You received ₹25,000 in dividends from 5 different stocks. You didn't notice/remember. AIS shows all 5 companies' dividends. ITR shows ₹0 dividend income.

**Fix**: File revised ITR. Add ₹25,000 under "Income from Other Sources → Dividend". Dividends are taxable at slab rate since April 2020 (no longer tax-free). TDS at 10% may have been deducted by companies — claim it as TDS credit.

### Scenario 3: MF Redemption Capital Gains

You redeemed mutual fund units worth ₹1.8L (invested ₹1.4L → profit ₹40K LTCG). CAMS/KFintech reported the redemption in SFT. You filed ITR-1 (which doesn't have a capital gains schedule).

**Fix**: You should have filed ITR-2 (for capital gains). File revised ITR as ITR-2. Declare ₹40K LTCG. Under ₹1.25L exemption, so tax = ₹0. But you need to declare it — the ITR form mismatch itself can trigger notice.

### Scenario 4: Freelance TDS Not Reported

Client deducted TDS at 10% (₹20,000 TDS) on your consulting fee of ₹2,00,000. You forgot to report the ₹2L income. AIS shows the TDS; your ITR shows you claimed the ₹20K TDS credit without showing the income.

**Fix**: Serious issue — claiming TDS credit without showing underlying income triggers automatic flags. File revised ITR with ₹2L added to income. Under 44ADA (if eligible), declare 50% (₹1L) as income, pay tax on it, and claim the ₹20K TDS.

### Scenario 5: Wrong Entry in AIS

A property you bought for ₹80L is showing as a sale (not a purchase) in AIS. This happens when the registrar erroneously files the transaction type.

**Fix**: Raise AIS feedback → "Income belongs to other person" or "Transaction type is incorrect". Also respond to notice explaining the property was purchased (not sold) and attach sale deed.

## Penalty for Ignoring Notices

- **Section 270A**: Penalty for underreporting income = 50% of the tax due on underreported income
- **Section 270A(9)**: Misreporting penalty (more serious) = 200% of tax due
- **Interest**: Section 234A (delay in filing) + 234B (advance tax shortfall) — 1% per month

**The safe approach**: Even if you believe the notice is wrong, always respond within the deadline. A response defending your position is infinitely better than no response, which gets treated as acceptance of the demand.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I got a 143(1)(a) notice with a demand for ₹12,000. What's the fastest way to resolve?

If you missed income: File a revised ITR immediately, then respond to notice citing the revised ITR. If you believe it's wrong: Respond via e-Proceedings with explanation + supporting documents. The demand is usually auto-adjusted once revised ITR is filed and processed.

### Can I respond to income tax notice without a CA?

Yes, for straightforward 143(1)(a) intimations. Log in to incometax.gov.in → e-Proceedings → respond online. For complex notices (148, 263, search notices), use a CA.

### How long does it take for IT department to process my response?

143(1)(a) responses: 30–90 days typically. AIS feedback: 15–30 days. For complex proceedings, it can take 6–12 months.

### My response was submitted but the demand still shows. Is that normal?

Yes — IT systems update slowly. Keep the response submission confirmation. If the demand shows as "outstanding" 3+ months after a valid response, file a grievance at the IT portal or visit your jurisdictional AO.

### What if the AIS shows income from someone else's PAN (PAN linkage error)?

This happens when Aadhaar is linked to multiple PANs or due to data entry errors by deductors. Raise AIS feedback → "PAN of deductee / payee is different". Also contact the deductor (company that deducted TDS) to file a TDS correction statement.
