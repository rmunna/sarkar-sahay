# UP Vridha Pension Yojana: ₹1,000 Monthly Pension for Senior Citizens

UP Vridha Pension Yojana (Uttar Pradesh Old Age Pension Scheme) provides a **monthly pension of ₹1,000** to senior citizens aged 60 years and above belonging to economically weaker sections. The scheme is managed by the Social Welfare Department, Government of Uttar Pradesh, and applications are processed through the SSPY (Samajik Suraksha Pension Yojana) portal.

## What is UP Vridha Pension?

The Uttar Pradesh government provides old age pension to elderly citizens who are below the poverty line or economically weak. The pension amount includes both the Central Government's share under [IGNOAPS](/guide/nsap-old-age-pension) and the state government's additional contribution, totaling **₹1,000 per month** (revised from ₹500).

---

## Who is Eligible?

- **Age:** 60 years or above
- **Resident:** Permanent resident of Uttar Pradesh
- **Income:** Below Poverty Line (BPL) or economically weaker section
- **Not receiving pension** from any other government pension scheme (central/state/PSU)
- **Indian citizen**
- Should be listed in the BPL list or have income below the prescribed limit

---

## Pension Amount

| Category | Monthly Pension |
|----------|----------------|
| Age 60-79 years | ₹1,000 per month |
| Age 80 years and above | ₹1,000 per month (+ Central share of ₹500 under IGNOAPS) |

*Pension is paid quarterly (every 3 months) directly to the bank account.*

## Documents Required

- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
- Age proof (birth certificate, voter ID, Aadhaar, school certificate)
- Income certificate (from Tehsildar/SDM)
- BPL card / [Ration card](/guide/ration-card-apply-online)
- Bank account passbook (Aadhaar-linked, UP-based bank)
- Passport-size photograph (recent)
- Domicile / residence proof of UP
- Mobile number

## How to Apply Online

1. **Visit SSPY portal** — Go to [sspy-up.gov.in](https://sspy-up.gov.in/)
2. **Select "वृद्धावस्था पेंशन"** — Click on Old Age Pension
3. **Click "ऑनलाइन आवेदन करें"** — Apply Online
4. **Fill application form** — Enter personal details (name, date of birth, gender, address)
5. **Enter income details** — Annual household income
6. **Upload documents** — Upload photograph, age proof, and income certificate
7. **Enter bank details** — Bank account number and IFSC code
8. **Submit** — Review and submit the application
9. **Note registration number** — Save the registration number for tracking
10. **Verification** — The application is verified at Block/District level
11. **Approval** — After verification, pension is approved and payments begin

### Offline Application

1. Visit the **Block Development Office (BDO)** or **District Social Welfare Office**
2. Collect the Vridha Pension application form
3. Fill the form and attach all required documents
4. Submit at the BDO office
5. Verification is done at the block level
6. After approval, pension is credited to your bank account

## How to Check Pension Status

1. Visit [sspy-up.gov.in](https://sspy-up.gov.in/)
2. Click on "वृद्धावस्था पेंशन" (Old Age Pension)
3. Select "आवेदन की स्थिति" (Application Status)
4. Enter your registration number
5. View the current status of your application

---

## Processing Time

- **Online application to verification:** 30-60 days
- **Verification to approval:** 30-60 days
- **First payment:** 2-4 months from application (varies by district)

---

## Important Tips

1. **Apply online** — Online applications at sspy-up.gov.in are faster than offline
2. **Correct age proof** — Ensure your date of birth is accurately reflected in Aadhaar and other documents. Age is the primary eligibility criterion
3. **Income certificate** — Get a fresh income certificate from your Tehsildar's office. Expired certificates may lead to rejection
4. **UP bank account** — Your bank account should be in a UP-based bank branch for smooth processing
5. **Check pension list** — After applying, check the pension list on SSPY portal to see if your name appears in the approved list

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. What is the pension amount under UP Vridha Pension?
₹1,000 per month, paid quarterly (₹3,000 every 3 months) directly to your bank account.

### Q2. Can I apply if I am receiving PM-KISAN?
Yes, PM-KISAN is a farmer income support scheme, not a pension. You can receive both UP Vridha Pension and PM-KISAN benefits.

### Q3. Is there an income limit?
Yes, the applicant should be from a BPL family or economically weaker section. The exact income limit is determined by the state government.

### Q4. How do I check if my pension has been credited?
Check your bank account statement or visit the SSPY portal to check payment status.

### Q5. Can I apply if I don't have a BPL card?
You can apply with an income certificate from the Tehsildar showing your family income is below the prescribed limit.

### Q6. Is Aadhaar mandatory?
Yes, Aadhaar card is mandatory for pension application and for receiving payment through DBT.

### Q7. What if my application is rejected?
Check the rejection reason on SSPY portal. Common reasons include incorrect age, incomplete documents, or name mismatch. Correct the issue and reapply.
