# Bihar Mukhyamantri Balak/Balika Cycle Yojana 2025 – Complete Guide

## What is the Mukhyamantri Cycle Yojana?

The Mukhyamantri Balak/Balika Cycle Yojana is a flagship education scheme by the Government of Bihar. Under this scheme, **₹3,000 is provided** to every student admitted to **Class 9** in government and government-aided schools to purchase a bicycle.

Launched in 2007, the scheme was revolutionary — it was one of the first state-level initiatives in India to address school dropout caused by long commuting distances. Studies have shown that the Bihar Cycle Yojana significantly **increased enrollment and attendance**, especially among girls in rural areas.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Class:** Must be admitted to **Class 9** in the current academic session
- **School Type:** Must be enrolled in a **government or government-aided school** in Bihar
- **Resident:** Must be a resident of Bihar
- **Gender:** Both **boys (Balak) and girls (Balika)** are eligible
- **No Repeat Benefit:** Should not have received the cycle benefit before

### Who is NOT Eligible?

- Students in private unaided schools
- Students in classes other than Class 9
- Students who already received the benefit in a previous year
- Students studying outside Bihar

## Amount and Benefit

| Detail | Information |
|--------|-------------|
| Amount | ₹3,000 |
| Purpose | To purchase a bicycle |
| Payment Mode | Direct Bank Transfer (DBT) |
| Bank Account | Student's own account (or parent/guardian's if minor doesn't have one) |

**Note:** The amount was initially ₹2,500 and was later revised to ₹3,000.

## Documents Required

### Mandatory Documents
- Aadhaar card of the student
- Class 9 admission proof / school enrollment certificate
- Bank account passbook (student's own or parent/guardian's)
- Passport-size photograph
- School identity card

### Additional Documents
- Parent/guardian's Aadhaar card
- Residential proof (if required by the school)

## How to Claim – Step-by-Step

### Step 1: Get Admitted to Class 9
Secure admission in Class 9 at a government or government-aided school in Bihar.

### Step 2: Open a Bank Account
If you don't have a bank account, open one. Many schools facilitate **zero-balance accounts** for students at nearby banks. Ensure the account is linked to Aadhaar — see our [Aadhaar linking guide](/guide/aadhaar-linking-guide).

### Step 3: Submit Details to School
Provide your **Aadhaar number, bank account details, and photograph** to your school office. The school collects this information from all eligible Class 9 students.

### Step 4: School Prepares Beneficiary List
Your headmaster/principal compiles the list of all eligible students and submits it to the **District Education Officer (DEO)**.

### Step 5: Verification
The DEO's office verifies the student list against enrollment records and Aadhaar/bank details.

### Step 6: Funds Transfer
After verification, **₹3,000 is credited** directly to the student's bank account via DBT.

### Step 7: Purchase Your Bicycle
Use the amount to purchase a bicycle of your choice from any shop.

## Processing Time

- **List Compilation:** Within 1–2 months of the academic session start
- **Verification:** 1–2 months
- **Fund Transfer:** Usually within the first half of the academic year
- **Total:** 2–4 months from enrollment

## Important Tips

1. **Open a bank account early** — don't wait for the school to ask. Having an Aadhaar-linked account speeds up the process.
2. **Submit documents promptly** when your school asks — delays in submission mean delays in receiving funds.
3. **Keep your Aadhaar updated** with current address and mobile number. See our [Aadhaar address change guide](/guide/aadhaar-address-change-online).
4. **The amount is for buying a bicycle** — schools may verify that the money was used for its intended purpose.
5. **Girls should especially avail this benefit** — the scheme has been shown to significantly improve female enrollment in secondary education.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: How much money do I get under the Cycle Yojana?
**A:** You receive **₹3,000** directly in your bank account to purchase a bicycle.

### Q2: Do I get an actual bicycle or cash?
**A:** You receive **cash (₹3,000) via bank transfer**. You purchase the bicycle yourself.

### Q3: Can Class 10, 11, or 12 students also apply?
**A:** No. The Cycle Yojana is specifically for students entering **Class 9 only**. However, Bihar has separate schemes for higher classes.

### Q4: I'm in a private school. Can I get this benefit?
**A:** No. Only students in **government and government-aided schools** are eligible.

### Q5: What if I don't have a bank account?
**A:** You can use your **parent or guardian's bank account**. However, it's best to open your own account — many banks offer zero-balance accounts for students.

### Q6: Do I need to submit any proof of buying the bicycle?
**A:** Some schools may ask for a purchase receipt as proof. Keep the receipt safe after buying your bicycle.

### Q7: Is this scheme available every year?
**A:** Yes, the Mukhyamantri Cycle Yojana is an **ongoing annual scheme**. Every batch of Class 9 students is eligible each year.
