# SBI RTGS/NEFT Transfer: How to Send Money Online

RTGS (Real Time Gross Settlement) and NEFT (National Electronic Funds Transfer) are RBI-operated fund transfer systems available through SBI. Both are now available **24x7, 365 days** and SBI charges **zero fees** for online transactions. This guide covers how to add a beneficiary and send money via SBI net banking and YONO app.

## RTGS vs NEFT: Key Differences

| Feature | RTGS | NEFT |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum amount | ₹2,00,000 | No minimum (₹1 onwards) |
| Maximum amount | No upper limit | No upper limit (per transaction limits may apply) |
| Settlement | Real-time (instant) | In batches (every 30 minutes, effectively near-instant) |
| Availability | 24x7, 365 days | 24x7, 365 days |
| SBI online charges | **Nil** | **Nil** |
| Best for | Large transfers (₹2L+) | Any amount below ₹2L |

> **Note:** As per RBI mandate, banks cannot charge for NEFT and RTGS transactions done online. SBI online RTGS/NEFT is completely free.

## Documents Required

To set up NEFT/RTGS online, you need:

- Active **SBI savings/current account**
- [SBI internet banking](/guide/sbi-net-banking-registration-login) or **YONO app** registered and activated
- Beneficiary's **account number** and **IFSC code**
- Registered **mobile number** for OTP verification

No physical documents are needed for online transfers — everything is done digitally.

## Step 1: Add Beneficiary in SBI Net Banking

Before sending money, you must add the recipient as a beneficiary. This is a one-time process.

### Via SBI Net Banking (OnlineSBI):

1. Log in to [OnlineSBI](https://www.onlinesbi.sbi/) with your username and password
2. Go to **"Payments/Transfers"** → **"Manage Beneficiary"**
3. Click **"Add Beneficiary"**
4. Enter the beneficiary details:
   - Beneficiary name (as per bank records)
   - Account number
   - IFSC code of the beneficiary's bank branch
   - Transfer limit (optional)
5. Confirm with **OTP** sent to your registered mobile
6. Beneficiary is added but **activated after a cooling period of 4-24 hours**
7. You will receive an SMS once the beneficiary is activated

### Via YONO App:

1. Open **YONO SBI** and log in
2. Go to **"Money Transfer"** → **"Add Beneficiary"**
3. Enter account number, IFSC code, and name
4. Verify with OTP
5. Wait for the **cooling period** (4–24 hours) before making the first transfer

> **Important:** The cooling period is a security measure. Plan ahead if you need to make an urgent transfer.

## Step 2: Send Money via NEFT (Below ₹2 Lakh)

### Via SBI Net Banking:

1. Log in to [OnlineSBI](https://www.onlinesbi.sbi/)
2. Go to **"Payments/Transfers"** → **"NEFT"**
3. Select the **beneficiary** from your added list
4. Enter the **transfer amount**
5. Add remarks/narration (optional but recommended)
6. Confirm the transaction details
7. Authenticate with **OTP** or **profile password**
8. Transaction is submitted — you'll receive a **UTR number** (Unique Transaction Reference)
9. Money is credited to the beneficiary's account within **30 minutes** (usually faster)

### Via YONO App:

1. Open YONO → **"Money Transfer"**
2. Select **"NEFT"** and choose the beneficiary
3. Enter amount and remarks
4. Confirm with **YONO PIN** or **OTP**
5. Done — check transaction status under "Transaction History"

## Step 3: Send Money via RTGS (₹2 Lakh and Above)

### Via SBI Net Banking:

1. Log in to [OnlineSBI](https://www.onlinesbi.sbi/)
2. Go to **"Payments/Transfers"** → **"RTGS"**
3. Select the **beneficiary**
4. Enter the amount (minimum ₹2,00,000)
5. Add remarks (optional)
6. Confirm and authenticate with **OTP**
7. Money is transferred **instantly** — credited to the beneficiary's account in real-time
8. You receive the **UTR number** for tracking

### Via YONO App:

1. Open YONO → **"Money Transfer"**
2. Select **"RTGS"** and choose the beneficiary
3. Enter amount (minimum ₹2 lakh)
4. Confirm with YONO PIN
5. Transfer happens instantly

## Charges for RTGS/NEFT via SBI

| Channel | NEFT Charges | RTGS Charges |
|---|---|---|
| Online (Net banking/YONO) | **Nil** | **Nil** |
| Branch (up to ₹10,000) | ₹2 + GST | N/A |
| Branch (₹10,001–₹1L) | ₹5 + GST | N/A |
| Branch (₹1L–₹2L) | ₹15 + GST | N/A |
| Branch (₹2L–₹5L) | N/A | ₹25 + GST |
| Branch (₹5L and above) | N/A | ₹50 + GST |

> **Bottom line:** Always use net banking or YONO to transfer via NEFT/RTGS — it's completely free.

## Transaction Limits

| Channel | NEFT Limit | RTGS Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Net banking | Up to ₹10 lakh per transaction | Up to ₹25 lakh per transaction |
| YONO | Up to ₹10 lakh per transaction | Up to ₹25 lakh per transaction |

> **Note:** These are default limits. You can request higher limits at your SBI branch with additional verification.

## NEFT/RTGS Timing

Both NEFT and RTGS are available **24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including holidays** as per RBI guidelines. There is no downtime except for brief maintenance windows announced by RBI.

## How to Track Your NEFT/RTGS Transaction

1. Note the **UTR number** provided after the transaction
2. Check status via:
   - **OnlineSBI:** Account Statement → filter by date
   - **YONO:** Transaction History
   - **SMS:** Transaction confirmation is sent to registered mobile
3. If the beneficiary hasn't received the amount, contact SBI customer care at **1800-11-2211**
4. For NEFT, if the beneficiary account details are wrong, the amount is **returned within 2 hours**

## Important Tips

1. **Always use online channels** — NEFT/RTGS is free via net banking and YONO, but branches charge fees
2. **Double-check account number and IFSC** — Wrong details can delay or misdirect your payment
3. **Add beneficiary in advance** — There's a 4–24 hour cooling period before you can transfer
4. **Use NEFT for amounts below ₹2 lakh** — It's practically instant now with 24x7 settlement
5. **Save the UTR number** — This is your proof of transfer and needed for any dispute resolution

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Is SBI NEFT/RTGS available 24x7?
Yes, both NEFT and RTGS are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including Sundays and bank holidays.

### Q2. Does SBI charge for NEFT/RTGS online?
No, SBI does not charge any fee for NEFT and RTGS transactions done through net banking or YONO app. Branch transactions have nominal charges.

### Q3. What is the minimum amount for RTGS transfer?
The minimum amount for RTGS is ₹2,00,000 (₹2 lakh). For amounts below ₹2 lakh, use NEFT.

### Q4. How long does SBI NEFT take to credit?
NEFT transfers are settled in batches every 30 minutes. In practice, most NEFT transfers are credited within 30 minutes to 1 hour.

### Q5. What is the beneficiary cooling period in SBI?
After adding a new beneficiary in SBI net banking or YONO, there is a cooling period of 4–24 hours before you can make the first transfer. This is a security measure.

### Q6. Can I cancel an NEFT/RTGS transaction after submission?
Once submitted, NEFT/RTGS transactions cannot be cancelled. If the beneficiary details are incorrect, NEFT returns the amount automatically within 2 hours. For RTGS, contact SBI immediately.

### Q7. What if I enter the wrong account number?
If the account number is valid but belongs to a different person, recovering the money can be difficult. Always verify account details before transferring. If the account doesn't exist, the money is automatically returned.
