# Assam Government Schemes and Services – Complete Guide

Assam, governed by the BJP under Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma, has launched numerous welfare schemes targeting women empowerment, education, healthcare, housing, and rural development. The state has embraced Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to ensure transparent delivery of benefits to citizens.

This comprehensive hub covers all major Assam government schemes and services — from financial assistance programmes to online citizen portals — so you can find the right scheme and apply with confidence.

## Women Empowerment Schemes

### Orunodoi Scheme

Orunodoi is the flagship DBT scheme of the Assam government providing monthly financial assistance of **₹1,250** to economically weaker families. The amount is transferred directly to the bank account of a nominated woman member of each eligible household.

**Key Details:**
- **Benefit:** ₹1,250 per month via DBT
- **Eligibility:** BPL families; woman nominee must be 18+ years
- **Income Limit:** Annual family income below ₹2 lakh
- **Exclusions:** Government employees, taxpayers, families owning four-wheelers

📖 **Read our detailed guide:** [Assam Orunodoi Scheme – Complete Guide](/guide/assam-orunodoi-scheme)

### Arundhati Gold Scheme

Under this scheme, the government provides **10 grams of gold** (or equivalent cash of approximately ₹38,000) to brides from economically weaker families at the time of their registered marriage.

**Key Details:**
- **Benefit:** 10 grams of gold or cash equivalent
- **Eligibility:** Bride's family annual income below ₹5 lakh; marriage must be registered under Special Marriage Act
- **Age Requirement:** Bride must be 18+ and groom 21+

📖 **Read our detailed guide:** [Assam Arundhati Gold Scheme – Complete Guide](/guide/assam-arundhati-gold-scheme)

## Education and Youth Schemes

### Pragyan Bharati Scooty Scheme

The state government provides **free scooties** to meritorious girl students who score 60% or above in their Higher Secondary (Class 12) examinations, promoting higher education among women.

**Key Details:**
- **Benefit:** Free scooty for eligible girl students
- **Eligibility:** Girls scoring 60%+ in HS exams; family income below ₹2 lakh per annum
- **Application:** Through college or education department

📖 **Read our detailed guide:** [Assam Pragyan Bharati Scooty Scheme](/guide/assam-pragyan-bharati-scooty-scheme)

### Chief Minister's Special Scholarship Scheme

This scholarship supports meritorious students from economically weaker sections pursuing higher education. It covers tuition fees and provides a maintenance allowance for students admitted to recognised institutions across India.

**Key Details:**
- **Benefit:** Tuition fee reimbursement + monthly stipend
- **Eligibility:** Students from Assam admitted to top national institutions; family income criteria apply
- **Application:** Through Directorate of Scholarships, Assam

### Swami Vivekananda Assam Youth Empowerment (SVAYEM) Scheme

SVAYEM provides a one-time financial grant of **₹50,000** to unemployed youth of Assam to set up self-employment ventures such as small businesses, piggery, fishery, dairy, and handloom.

**Key Details:**
- **Benefit:** ₹50,000 one-time grant (no repayment required)
- **Eligibility:** Unemployed youth aged 18–40 years; must be Assam resident
- **Income Limit:** Family income below ₹2 lakh per annum
- **Application:** Through District Commissioner office or online portal

### Assam TET and Competitive Exams

Assam conducts its own Teacher Eligibility Test (TET) and Police Constable recruitment regularly. These exams are a major pathway for government employment in the state.

## Healthcare Schemes

### Assam Arogya Nidhi

Assam Arogya Nidhi provides financial assistance up to **₹3 lakh** for treatment of critical illnesses to patients from BPL families. The assistance covers treatment at empanelled government and private hospitals.

**Key Details:**
- **Benefit:** Up to ₹3 lakh for critical illness treatment
- **Eligibility:** BPL families; diseases covered include cancer, kidney failure, heart disease, and other life-threatening conditions
- **Application:** Through the Chief Minister's Office or Health Department with medical documents and BPL certificate

### Atal Amrit Abhiyan

This health insurance scheme covers treatment costs of up to **₹2 lakh** for six critical diseases — cancer, heart disease, kidney failure, liver failure, neurological conditions, and neonatal diseases. Available for families with annual income below ₹5 lakh.

## Housing Schemes

### Apon Ghar Housing Scheme

Apon Ghar (My Home) provides financial assistance for construction of pucca houses for homeless and economically weaker families in Assam. This state-level complement to PMAY targets families not covered under central housing schemes.

**Key Details:**
- **Benefit:** Financial assistance for house construction
- **Eligibility:** Homeless families or those living in kutcha houses; BPL category
- **Application:** Through district administration or block development office

## Land and Revenue Services

### Mission Basundhara

Mission Basundhara is a landmark land reform initiative that simplifies land-related services in Assam. It enables citizens to apply online for land mutation, conversion, and settlement — processes that previously required multiple visits to revenue offices.

**Services Available:**
- Land mutation (name transfer after purchase or inheritance)
- Land conversion (agricultural to non-agricultural)
- Settlement of government land to eligible landless families
- Correction of land records
- Issue of certified copies of land documents

**Portal:** missionbasundhara.assam.gov.in

## Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) in Assam

Assam has aggressively adopted DBT for most welfare schemes. The Assam DBT framework covers:

- **Orunodoi** – Monthly ₹1,250 to women
- **SVAYEM** – ₹50,000 youth grant
- **Scholarship disbursements** – Directly to student bank accounts
- **Arundhati Gold Scheme** – Cash equivalent of gold
- **Farm input subsidies** – Kisan Credit and seed/fertiliser support

All DBT payments require an **Aadhaar-linked bank account**. Ensure your bank account is seeded with Aadhaar to receive benefits without delays.

## Farmer and Agriculture Schemes

### Assam Farmer Welfare Schemes

The state government runs several schemes for the farming community:

- **Chief Minister's Samagra Gramya Unnayan Yojana (CMSGUY):** Integrated rural development covering agriculture, fishery, dairy, and handloom at village level
- **Mukhya Mantrir Krishi Sa-Sajuli Yojana:** Provides farm equipment and tools to small and marginal farmers
- **Kisan Credit Card (KCC):** Interest-subvented crop loans through cooperative banks
- **Assam Agricultural University Extension Services:** Training and advisory for modern farming techniques

**Assam Darshan** is a state-funded tourism initiative aimed at promoting domestic tourism by offering subsidised travel packages to Assam residents for visiting tourist destinations within the state. It encourages exploration of heritage sites, national parks (Kaziranga, Manas), tea gardens, and cultural landmarks.

## Citizen Services and Governance

### Assam Right to Public Services (RTPS)

The RTPS Act guarantees time-bound delivery of notified public services. Citizens can track applications and escalate delays through the RTPS portal.

**Services Covered:**
- Caste, income, and residence certificates
- Domicile certificates
- Land records and NOCs
- Driving licence and vehicle registration services
- Birth and death certificates

**Portal:** rtps.assam.gov.in

### Seva Setu

Seva Setu is the single-window service delivery platform for Assam, bringing multiple government services under one roof. Citizens can apply for certificates, permits, and licences without visiting multiple offices.

**Portal:** sewasetu.assam.gov.in

## How to Apply for Assam Government Schemes

Most Assam government schemes now accept online applications. Here's the general process:

1. **Check Eligibility:** Visit the scheme's official portal or Seva Setu to verify eligibility criteria
2. **Gather Documents:** Keep the following ready — Aadhaar card, BPL certificate (if applicable), income certificate, bank passbook, passport-size photographs, and scheme-specific documents
3. **Apply Online:** Visit the relevant portal (Orunodoi, Mission Basundhara, RTPS, or Seva Setu) and fill the application form
4. **Offline Option:** Visit your nearest Circle Office, Block Development Office, or District Commissioner's office for assisted application
5. **Track Status:** Use the application receipt number on the portal to track your application
6. **Receive Benefits:** DBT amounts are credited directly to your Aadhaar-linked bank account

**Pro Tip:** Ensure your Aadhaar is linked to your mobile number and bank account before applying. Many applications get rejected due to Aadhaar-bank linking issues.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for Orunodoi scheme?
BPL families in Assam with annual income below ₹2 lakh are eligible. The benefit of ₹1,250 per month is transferred to a woman nominee (18+ years) in the household. Government employees and taxpayers are excluded.

### How can I apply for the Arundhati Gold Scheme?
Register your marriage under the Special Marriage Act, then apply through the Deputy Commissioner's office with marriage certificate, income certificate, Aadhaar, and bank details. The bride's family income must be below ₹5 lakh per annum.

### What is Seva Setu and what services does it provide?
Seva Setu is Assam's single-window service delivery platform at sewasetu.assam.gov.in. It provides over 40 government services including certificates (caste, income, residence), land records, and various NOCs — all from a single portal.

### How do I get land mutation done through Mission Basundhara?
Visit missionbasundhara.assam.gov.in, register an account, select the land mutation service, fill in property details, upload the sale deed or inheritance documents, and submit. The application is processed at the circle revenue office level.

### What is the SVAYEM scheme and how much grant does it provide?
Swami Vivekananda Assam Youth Empowerment (SVAYEM) provides a one-time grant of ₹50,000 to unemployed youth aged 18–40 for self-employment ventures. No repayment is required. Apply through the District Commissioner's office.

### Can I track my RTPS application status online?
Yes. Visit rtps.assam.gov.in, click on "Track Application," and enter your application reference number to check the current status and expected delivery date of your service.

### Is Aadhaar mandatory for Assam government scheme benefits?
For most DBT-based schemes (Orunodoi, SVAYEM, scholarships), Aadhaar-linked bank account is mandatory. Ensure your Aadhaar is seeded with your bank and linked to your active mobile number for seamless benefit transfer.
