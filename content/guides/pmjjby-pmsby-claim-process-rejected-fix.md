# PMJJBY & PMSBY Claim Process 2026 — File Death & Accident Insurance Claims

If a PMJJBY or PMSBY subscriber has died or met with an accident, the **nominee or subscriber** can claim ₹2 lakh in insurance benefits. Both schemes are administered through the bank where the insured person held their account. Here's how to file the claim step by step.

## Quick Reference

| Scheme | Event Covered | Amount | Who Claims | Deadline |
|--------|--------------|--------|------------|---------|
| **PMJJBY** | Death (any cause) | ₹2 lakh | Nominee | 30 days of death |
| **PMSBY** | Accidental death | ₹2 lakh | Nominee | 30 days of accident |
| **PMSBY** | Permanent total disability | ₹2 lakh | Subscriber | 30 days of discharge |
| **PMSBY** | Permanent partial disability | ₹1 lakh | Subscriber | 30 days of discharge |

---

## PMJJBY Claim — Death Insurance

**PMJJBY (Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana)** pays ₹2 lakh to the nominee on the death of the subscriber.

### Step 1: Notify the Bank Immediately

The **nominee** should visit the bank branch where the deceased subscriber held their account as soon as possible — preferably within 30 days of death.

### Step 2: Documents to Collect

| Document | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| **Death Certificate** | Issued by hospital or local civic authority (municipality/gram panchayat) |
| **Nominee's Aadhaar Card** | For nominee identity |
| **Nominee's Bank Passbook** | Claim will be credited to nominee's bank account |
| **Deceased's Bank Account Details** | Account number where PMJJBY premium was deducted |
| **PMJJBY Enrolment Proof** | If available (bank can verify from records) |
| **Indemnity Bond** | Small declaration form — the bank will provide this |

### Step 3: Fill Claim Form

The bank will provide the **PMJJBY Claim Form**. Download also from [jansuraksha.gov.in](https://jansuraksha.gov.in/) → PMJJBY → Claim forms.

Submit all documents at the bank. The bank forwards the claim to the insurance company (LIC or other empanelled insurer, depending on your bank's tie-up).

### Step 4: Claim Processing Timeline

- Bank submits to insurer within **30 days** of receiving documents
- Insurer processes within **30 working days** of receipt
- Total typical time: **60–90 days** from claim filing to credit in nominee's account

## PMSBY Claim — Accident Insurance

**PMSBY (Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana)** covers accidental death and disability.

### For Accidental Death Claim:

**Documents needed:**
1. **FIR** (First Information Report) from police — mandatory for accidents
2. **Post-mortem report** (if available)
3. **Death Certificate**
4. **Nominee's Aadhaar + bank account details**
5. **PMSBY Claim Form** (from bank or jansuraksha.gov.in)
6. **Hospital discharge summary** (if treated before death)

### For Disability Claim (Subscriber Survives):

**Documents needed:**
1. **FIR / Accident report**
2. **Hospital discharge certificate** with diagnosis of permanent disability
3. **Doctor's certificate** confirming nature and extent of disability (total vs partial)
4. **Subscriber's own Aadhaar + bank account**
5. **PMSBY Claim Form**

> **Disability criteria**: "Total permanent disability" means loss of use of both eyes, hands, or feet. "Partial permanent disability" means loss of one eye, hand, or foot.

### Step-by-Step PMSBY Claim:

1. Visit the **same bank branch** where PMSBY premium was deducted
2. Inform them of the accident and initiate claim
3. Collect and submit all documents within **30 days of the event**
4. Bank submits to insurer (non-life insurance company)
5. Insurer investigates and pays within **30 working days**

## Common Reasons for Rejection — and How to Fix

### Rejection: "Nominee not registered"

**Problem**: The subscriber enrolled in PMJJBY/PMSBY but never officially registered a nominee.

**Fix**: This is unfortunately the most common rejection. If the bank has no nominee on record, the legal heir can still claim by:
- Obtaining a **Legal Heir Certificate** from the Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) office
- Or applying for a **Succession Certificate** from a civil court (for larger amounts)
- Submit these along with the claim form — insurers generally accept legal heir claims with proper documentation

### Rejection: "Premium lapsed — coverage not active"

**Problem**: PMJJBY and PMSBY need annual renewal. If the subscriber's bank account didn't have sufficient balance on **May 31** each year, the premium auto-debit fails and coverage lapses.

**Fix**: 
- If the death/accident occurred AFTER the lapse date and before renewal, the claim can be rejected
- Check bank statement for "PMJJBY" or "PMSBY" debit entries — the last successful debit date tells you if coverage was active
- If premium was missed due to insufficient balance, there is **no retroactive fix** — coverage was not active

### Rejection: "Subscriber not eligible at time of death"

**Problem**: Subscriber may have exceeded age limit (PMSBY: 70 years; PMJJBY: 55 years) or account was closed.

**Fix**: Limited recourse. If age was at boundary, appeal with Aadhaar birth date documentation.

### Rejection: "Suicide" (PMJJBY only)

**Problem**: PMJJBY does NOT cover suicide in the first year of enrolment.
- **After completing 1 full year**: suicide is covered under PMJJBY

**Fix**: If death is classified as suicide, get the exact date of PMJJBY enrollment from the bank and compare with date of death. If 1+ year has passed since enrollment, appeal the rejection citing this rule.

### Rejection: "FIR not filed" (PMSBY)

**Problem**: PMSBY requires FIR for accidental claims. If the accident wasn't reported to police, claim may be denied.

**Fix**: 
- FIR can sometimes be filed retrospectively (with police cooperation) for genuine accidents
- Road accidents are typically reported automatically; workplace accidents need separate FIR
- If FIR delay was due to hospitalization, submit hospital records + explanation letter

## How to Appeal a Rejection

1. **First appeal**: Write to the bank's **regional head of insurance operations** citing the rejection reason and your counter-argument
2. **Insurance Ombudsman**: If bank/insurer reject twice, approach the Insurance Ombudsman at [cioins.co.in](https://cioins.co.in/) — free of charge, covers claims up to ₹30 lakh
3. **IRDAI Bima Bharosa**: Call **155255** or file at bimabharosa.irdai.gov.in
4. **Consumer Court**: For delays beyond 3 months without reason

## Claiming After the 30-Day Deadline

The 30-day deadline is for initiating the claim, not completing it. If circumstances (hospitalization, travel, etc.) prevented timely filing, many insurers accept delayed claims with a valid reason letter. Do NOT assume it's too late — file immediately and explain the delay.

## Bank Contact for Claims

Your claim must be filed at **the specific bank branch** that holds the insured person's account (where the premium was deducted). Different banks have different insurance partners:
- **SBI**: SBI Life (PMJJBY) / New India Assurance (PMSBY)
- **PNB, BOI, BOB, other PSU banks**: Own insurance tie-ups
- All claims go through the bank, not directly to insurer

Call your bank's **general helpline** and ask for the "PMJJBY/PMSBY insurance claim desk."

## National Insurance Portal

For additional guidance: [jansuraksha.gov.in](https://jansuraksha.gov.in/) → PMJJBY or PMSBY → Claims section has downloadable claim forms and FAQs.
