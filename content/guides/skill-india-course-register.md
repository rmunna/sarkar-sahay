## What is Skill India / PMKVY?

Skill India is the Government of India's flagship initiative to train youth in industry-relevant skills and provide them with government-recognized certification. The primary scheme under this mission is **Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY)**, implemented by the **National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC)** under the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship.

PMKVY offers:
- **Free short-term training** (2-6 months) in 600+ job roles
- **Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL)** for experienced workers
- **Government certificate** recognized by employers across India
- **Job placement assistance** after course completion
- **Financial reward** of ₹500-₹8,000 upon successful certification

> **PMKVY 4.0** (2023-2026) has a target of skilling 12 lakh+ youth with a budget of ₹6,000+ crore.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Age:** 15-45 years (varies by course)
- **Education:** No minimum qualification for most courses; some advanced courses require 10th/12th pass
- **Nationality:** Indian citizens only
- **Aadhaar:** Required for registration (linked to bank account for rewards)
- **Employment status:** Unemployed youth, school/college dropouts, and existing workers seeking certification

### Priority Groups
- SC/ST/OBC candidates
- Women and transgender persons
- Persons with disabilities (PwD)
- Candidates from aspirational districts

## Documents Required

### For Registration
- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory)
- Recent **passport-size photograph**
- **Bank account details** (for reward transfer)
- **Educational certificates** (if applicable to the course)
- **Caste/category certificate** (for SC/ST/OBC candidates)

### For Certification Exam
- Aadhaar card (original for verification)
- Training completion certificate from the training centre

## Step-by-Step Registration Process

### Online Registration

1. **Visit** the Skill India portal at [skillindia.nsdcindia.org](https://skillindia.nsdcindia.org)
2. **Click** on "Candidate Registration" or "Register as Candidate"
3. **Enter your Aadhaar number** and verify via OTP sent to your linked mobile
4. **Fill in personal details** — name, date of birth, gender, address, education
5. **Select your preferred sector and course** from the available list
6. **Choose a training centre** near your location
7. **Submit the registration** and note your candidate ID
8. **Wait for confirmation** — the training centre will contact you with batch details

### Offline Registration

1. **Locate a PMKVY training centre** near you via [pmkvyofficial.org](https://www.pmkvyofficial.org) → "Find a Training Centre"
2. **Visit the centre** with your Aadhaar card, photograph, and educational documents
3. **Fill the registration form** provided at the centre
4. **Complete biometric verification** at the centre
5. **Enrol in the batch** — training starts as per schedule

### Through Common Service Centre (CSC)

1. Visit your nearest [CSC centre](https://www.csc.gov.in)
2. Ask for Skill India / PMKVY registration
3. Provide your Aadhaar and other documents
4. The operator will complete registration on your behalf

## Training & Certification Process

1. **Training Duration:** 2-6 months depending on the course (150-600 hours)
2. **Training is free** — no fees charged to candidates
3. **Classroom + practical training** at authorized centres
4. **Assessment** by SSC-empanelled assessment agencies
5. **Certification** issued by NSDC and the relevant Sector Skill Council (SSC)
6. **Monetary reward** credited to bank account after certification
7. **Placement support** — training partners assist with job placement

## How to Check Training Centre Quality

- Verify the centre is listed on [pmkvyofficial.org](https://www.pmkvyofficial.org)
- Check for **NSDC affiliation** and **Sector Skill Council** authorization
- Look for the **PMKVY branding** at the centre
- Ask for the centre's **Training Partner ID**

## Important Tips

1. **Always verify the training centre** on the official PMKVY website before enrolling — many fake centres operate under the Skill India name
2. **No money should be collected** from you at any stage — training, assessment, and certification are completely free
3. **Keep your Aadhaar linked to your bank account** for smooth reward transfer
4. **Attend all training sessions** — minimum 80% attendance is required for certification eligibility
5. **After certification**, actively follow up with the training partner for placement assistance

## FAQs

### Is Skill India training really free?
Yes, all training under PMKVY is fully funded by the Government of India. No fee is charged for training, assessment, or certification. If any centre asks for money, report it to the helpline at 1800-123-9626.

### What certificate do I get after completing the course?
You receive a government-recognized certificate issued jointly by NSDC and the relevant Sector Skill Council (SSC). This certificate is valid across India and recognized by employers in the respective sector.

### Can I do Skill India course after 10th or 12th?
Yes. Most short-term courses have no minimum education requirement. Some specialized courses may require 10th or 12th pass. Check specific course eligibility on the PMKVY portal.

### Will I get a job after Skill India training?
Training partners are required to provide placement assistance. While job placement is not guaranteed, PMKVY reports placement rates of 50-70% depending on the sector and location. IT, healthcare, and retail sectors typically have higher placement rates.

### How long is the Skill India course?
Course duration ranges from 2 to 6 months (150-600 hours) depending on the job role and sector. Some Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) courses can be completed in 12-80 hours.

### Can women apply for Skill India courses?
Yes, women are encouraged to apply and are given priority in enrollment. Special courses in beauty & wellness, healthcare, apparel, and electronics are popular among women candidates. Some centres offer women-only batches.

### Is there an age limit for PMKVY?
The general age range is 15-45 years, but this varies by specific course. Some courses accept candidates up to 59 years of age. Check the specific job role requirements on the portal.
