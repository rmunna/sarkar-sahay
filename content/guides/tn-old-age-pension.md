# Old Age Pension Scheme Tamil Nadu – ₹1,000/Month for Senior Citizens

## What is the Tamil Nadu Old Age Pension?

The Tamil Nadu Old Age Pension scheme provides **₹1,000 per month** to senior citizens from economically weaker sections. This includes both the state contribution and the central government's share under the **Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS)**. The pension is credited directly to the beneficiary's bank/post office account.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Age:** Must be **60 years or above**
2. **Income:** Must belong to a **BPL family** or have income below the prescribed limit
3. **Residency:** Must be a **resident of Tamil Nadu** for at least 5 years
4. **Ration Card:** Must hold a rice ration card
5. **Not receiving other pension:** Must not be receiving any other government pension
6. Should not be a beneficiary of any other social security pension scheme

## Pension Amount

| Age Group | Monthly Amount |
|-----------|---------------|
| 60-79 years | ₹1,000/month |
| 80 years and above | ₹1,000/month (may include enhanced central component) |

## Documents Required

- **Age Proof** — Aadhaar card, voter ID, birth certificate, or school certificate
- **Aadhaar Card**
- **Ration Card** (rice card)
- **Income Certificate** from Tahsildar
- **Bank/Post Office Passbook** (linked to Aadhaar)
- **Residence Proof** (voter ID, utility bill)
- **Passport Size Photo**

## How to Apply

### Online Application

1. Visit the Tamil Nadu e-Sevai portal or the Social Welfare Department website
2. Register with mobile number
3. Search for "Old Age Pension"
4. Fill the application form with personal details
5. Upload age proof, Aadhaar, income certificate, photo
6. Enter bank/post office account details
7. Submit and note the reference number

### Offline Application

1. **Visit** the **Revenue Divisional Office** or **Taluk Office** or **Village Administrative Office**
2. **Collect** the old age pension application form
3. **Fill** in personal details including age, income, bank details
4. **Attach** copies of all documents
5. **Submit** to the concerned officer
6. **Collect** the acknowledgment receipt

## Verification & Disbursement

1. The Revenue Inspector verifies the applicant's age, income, and residence
2. Village Administrative Officer provides a recommendation
3. Revenue Divisional Officer sanctions the pension
4. Pension is credited monthly to the bank/post office account

## Processing Time

- **Verification:** 30-60 days
- **First Payment:** Within 2-3 months of approval
- **Ongoing:** Monthly credit

## Important Tips

1. **Annual Life Certificate:** Submit a life certificate (Jeevan Pramaan) every year in November to continue the pension. See our [Jeevan Pramaan guide](/guide/jeevan-pramaan-life-certificate).
2. **Aadhaar Linkage:** Ensure Aadhaar is linked to your bank account for uninterrupted DBT.
3. **Report Change of Address:** If you shift residence, update your address at the taluk office.
4. **No Middlemen:** Apply directly at government offices. There is no fee.
5. **Nominee Information:** Provide nominee details at the time of application.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Can I get the pension if I receive a family pension from my spouse's employer?
**No.** If you receive any government pension (including family pension), you are not eligible.

### Q2: What if I don't have a birth certificate to prove my age?
Your **school leaving certificate, voter ID, or Aadhaar card** mentioning your date of birth can be used as age proof.

### Q3: Can I receive the pension in my post office account?
**Yes.** You can choose either a bank account or post office savings account.

### Q4: Is the pension taxable?
**No.** Social security pensions from the government are not subject to income tax for BPL beneficiaries.

### Q5: What happens to the pension after the beneficiary's death?
The pension **stops** from the month following the beneficiary's death. Family members must inform the authorities.

### Q6: Can I apply if I'm from another state but living in Tamil Nadu?
You must have been a **resident of Tamil Nadu for at least 5 years** and have a Tamil Nadu ration card.
