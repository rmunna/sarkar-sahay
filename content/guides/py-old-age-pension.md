# Old Age Pension – Puducherry

The Government of Puducherry provides monthly old age pension to senior citizens aged 60 years and above. This social security pension supports elderly residents who lack adequate income or family support, ensuring a basic standard of living.

## What is the Old Age Pension Scheme?

The Puducherry Old Age Pension is a monthly financial assistance provided to senior citizens from economically weaker sections. The pension is credited directly to the beneficiary's bank account or distributed through the Department of Social Welfare.

### Pension Amount

| Age Group | Monthly Pension |
|-----------|----------------|
| 60–79 years | ₹2,000 – ₹3,000* |
| 80 years and above | ₹2,500 – ₹3,500* |

*Exact amounts may vary as per latest government orders. Puducherry periodically revises pension amounts — verify on the official portal.*

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Age:** 60 years or above
2. **Residency:** Must be a resident of Puducherry UT (Union Territory) for at least 5 years
3. **Income:** Annual income should not exceed the prescribed limit (typically ₹1,00,000 or as notified)
4. **Not receiving other pensions:** Should not be receiving any other government pension (central/state)
5. **BPL/EWS:** Priority given to BPL card holders

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar card (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- Age proof (birth certificate / voter ID / Aadhaar)
- Residence proof (5+ years in Puducherry)
- Income certificate from Revenue Department
- Ration card
- Bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked)
- Passport-size photographs (2 copies)
- BPL certificate (if applicable)

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Online Method (via e-District Portal)

1. **Visit e-District portal:** Go to [edistrict.py.gov.in](https://edistrict.py.gov.in/)
2. **Register/Login:** Create an account or log in
3. **Select Old Age Pension:** Navigate to Social Welfare → Old Age Pension
4. **Fill application:** Enter personal details, age, income, bank account, and residence information
5. **Upload documents:** Upload scanned copies of all required documents
6. **Submit:** Review and submit the application
7. **Track status:** Use the application number to track progress

### Offline Method

1. **Visit the Commune Panchayat / Municipal office** in your area
2. **Collect application form** from the Social Welfare section
3. **Fill the form** with all required details
4. **Attach documents** — self-attested copies
5. **Submit** the form at the office
6. **Receive acknowledgment** with reference number
7. **Verification:** Social welfare inspector conducts field verification
8. **Approval:** Application approved by competent authority
9. **Pension starts:** Monthly pension credited to bank account

## Processing Time

- **Verification:** 15–30 days
- **Approval:** 30–60 days from submission
- **First payment:** Within 1–2 months of approval

## Important Tips

1. **Apply as soon as you turn 60:** Don't wait — apply early to start receiving pension sooner
2. **Life certificate annually:** Submit a life certificate each year (usually in November) to continue receiving pension
3. **Update bank details:** Notify the office if you change your bank account
4. **No middlemen:** Apply directly at the government office or online portal
5. **Combine with NSAP:** Check eligibility for the central National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP) old age pension as well

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: How much pension will I get?
The pension amount depends on your age and current government orders. It is typically ₹2,000–₹3,500 per month.

### Q2: Can NRIs or people from other states apply?
No, you must be a resident of Puducherry UT for at least 5 years.

### Q3: What is a life certificate and when do I need it?
A life certificate (Jeevan Pramaan) proves you are alive. It must be submitted annually (usually November) to continue pension. You can submit it digitally via Jeevan Pramaan portal.

### Q4: Can I get both central and Puducherry old age pension?
Generally, you can receive only one government pension. However, the central NSAP amount may be supplemented by the state. Check with the local office.

### Q5: What happens to the pension after the beneficiary's death?
The pension stops upon death. Family members should inform the office to avoid legal issues.

### Q6: Is there an income limit?
Yes, annual income should typically not exceed ₹1,00,000 or the limit specified in the latest government order.

### Q7: Can I apply online?
Yes, through the [e-District portal](https://edistrict.py.gov.in/) of Puducherry.
