# CSC Registration 2026 — How to Apply for Digital Seva (VLE)

Common Service Centres (CSCs) are the backbone of India's digital governance at the grassroots level. As a Village Level Entrepreneur (VLE), you can deliver over 400 government and private services to citizens in your area — from Aadhaar to banking, bill payments to insurance. This guide covers how to register as a new CSC VLE through the Digital Seva portal in 2026.

## What is CSC (Common Service Centre)?

CSC is a Government of India initiative under the Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology (MeitY). Each centre is run by a VLE who acts as an entrepreneur delivering digital services. There are over 5.3 lakh CSCs across India covering virtually every gram panchayat.

## Who is Eligible for CSC VLE Registration?

- **Age:** Minimum 18 years
- **Education:** At least 10th pass (secondary school certificate)
- **Infrastructure:** Must have a physical premises (minimum 100–150 sq ft) with:
  - At least 2 computers with internet connectivity
  - Printer, scanner, and web camera
  - Biometric device (for Aadhaar services)
- **Location:** Priority given to uncovered gram panchayats (one CSC per panchayat)
- **TEC Certificate:** Telecentre Entrepreneur Course (TEC) certification is mandatory before applying

## What is TEC Certificate and How to Get It?

The **Telecentre Entrepreneur Course (TEC)** is a mandatory certification for CSC VLE applicants:

1. Visit the TEC portal at [https://www.cscentrepreneur.in](https://www.cscentrepreneur.in)
2. Register with your Aadhaar number and mobile number
3. Pay the TEC course fee of approximately ₹1,479 (inclusive of GST)
4. Complete the online course modules (10 modules covering digital literacy, entrepreneurship, etc.)
5. Pass the online examination
6. Download your TEC certificate after successful completion

> **Important:** Without a valid TEC certificate, your CSC application will not be processed.

## Documents Required for CSC Registration

- **Identity Proof:** Aadhaar card (mandatory)
- **Education Proof:** 10th marksheet or certificate
- **Address Proof:** Aadhaar, voter ID, or utility bill
- **TEC Certificate:** Downloaded from cscentrepreneur.in
- **Passport-size Photograph** (recent, white background)
- **PAN Card** (for financial transactions)
- **Bank Account Details:** Cancelled cheque or passbook copy
- **Infrastructure Proof:** Photos of your centre (interior and exterior)
- **Kiosk/Centre Address Proof:** Rent agreement or ownership document

## Step-by-Step CSC Registration Process (Online)

### Step 1: Complete TEC Certification
Complete the TEC course and download your certificate (see section above).

### Step 2: Visit the CSC Registration Portal
Go to [https://register.csc.gov.in](https://register.csc.gov.in).

### Step 3: Select Application Type
Choose **"New Registration"** and select the type:
- **CSC VLE** — for individual entrepreneurs
- **SHG (Self Help Group)** — for group-based centres

### Step 4: Enter Aadhaar Number
Enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number and verify via OTP sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile.

### Step 5: Fill Application Form
Provide the following details:
- Personal information (name, DOB, gender, email, mobile)
- Educational qualification
- TEC certificate number
- Proposed kiosk/centre address with GPS coordinates
- Banking details (account number, IFSC code)

### Step 6: Upload Documents
Upload scanned copies of:
- Passport photo
- PAN card
- 10th certificate
- Centre photographs (interior/exterior — at least 3 photos)
- TEC certificate

### Step 7: Submit Application
Review all details and submit. You'll receive an application reference number.

### Step 8: Verification and Approval
- Your application is reviewed by the District Manager
- Physical verification of your centre may be conducted
- Approval typically takes 30–60 days
- You'll receive login credentials via SMS/email upon approval

## Fees for CSC Registration

| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| TEC Course Fee | ₹1,479 (approx., inclusive of GST) |
| CSC Registration | Free (no registration fee) |
| Biometric Device | ₹2,000–5,000 (purchased separately) |
| Infrastructure Setup | Variable (computers, printer, internet) |

## After Approval — Getting Started

1. **Login** to the Digital Seva portal at [https://digitalseva.csc.gov.in](https://digitalseva.csc.gov.in)
2. **Complete KYC** by uploading required documents
3. **Load wallet balance** to start offering services
4. **Activate services** — choose from 400+ available services
5. **Start earning** commissions on each transaction

## Important Tips

1. **Check gram panchayat availability** before applying — only one CSC per GP is typically allowed
2. **Keep your Aadhaar mobile number active** as OTP verification is required at multiple steps
3. **Invest in reliable internet** — minimum 1 Mbps broadband recommended
4. **Don't pay agents or middlemen** — registration is free and done directly online
5. **Maintain proper records** of all transactions for audit purposes

## Common Issues During Registration

- **"GP already allocated"** — Another VLE is already registered in your gram panchayat. Try a nearby uncovered GP.
- **TEC certificate not accepted** — Ensure the certificate number matches exactly. Re-download if needed.
- **OTP not received** — Check your Aadhaar-linked mobile. Update mobile in Aadhaar if changed.
- **Application stuck in pending** — Contact your District Manager or call CSC helpline at 1800-121-3468.

## FAQs

### 1. Is CSC registration free?
Yes, CSC VLE registration is completely free. You only need to pay for the TEC course (approx. ₹1,479). Beware of fraudulent websites charging registration fees.

### 2. What is the TEC certificate and is it mandatory?
TEC (Telecentre Entrepreneur Course) is an online certification program. Yes, it is mandatory for all new CSC VLE applications. You can complete it at cscentrepreneur.in.

### 3. How long does CSC approval take?
Typically 30–60 days after application submission. It depends on verification by the District Manager and availability of GPs in your area.

### 4. Can I open a CSC in a city or urban area?
Yes, CSCs can be opened in urban areas as well. Urban centres are registered under the Urban CSC scheme.

### 5. How much can a CSC VLE earn?
Earnings vary based on services offered and location. Active VLEs typically earn ₹15,000–50,000 per month through commissions on various services.

### 6. What services can I offer through CSC?
You can offer Aadhaar enrollment/update, PAN card, passport, insurance (PMJJBY/PMSBY), banking services, bill payments, scholarship applications, and 400+ other government and private services.

### 7. Can I check my CSC application status?
Yes, visit [https://register.csc.gov.in](https://register.csc.gov.in) and click on "Application Status" to track your application using your Aadhaar number or reference ID.

### 8. What if my CSC application is rejected?
You can re-apply after addressing the rejection reason. Common reasons include incomplete documentation, already-allocated GP, or failing infrastructure verification.
