# CSC Registration Apply Online: How to Open a CSC Center

Common Service Centres (CSC) are government-authorized centres that provide digital services to citizens in rural and urban areas. If you want to become a CSC VLE (Village Level Entrepreneur) and open your own CSC center, this guide explains the complete registration process, eligibility, documents, and requirements.

## What is a CSC Center?

A CSC (Common Service Centre) is part of the Digital India initiative under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY). CSC VLEs provide government services like:

- [Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) enrollment and updates
- [PAN card](/guide/pan-card-apply-online) applications
- Passport services
- Banking and insurance services
- [Ration card](/guide/ration-card-apply-online) services
- Utility bill payments
- Education and skill development courses

## Eligibility Criteria

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| Age | Minimum 18 years |
| Education | Minimum 10th pass (Matriculation) |
| Nationality | Indian citizen |
| Infrastructure | At least one computer, printer, scanner, and internet connection |
| Location | Should have a physical shop/centre accessible to public |
| Biometric Device | STQC-certified biometric device required |

**Note:** Only one CSC center is allowed per village/ward. Priority is given to locations without an existing CSC.

## Documents Required for CSC Registration

**Personal Documents:**
- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) (mandatory)
- PAN card
- Bank account passbook or cancelled cheque
- Passport-size photograph
- Educational certificate (10th marksheet minimum)

**Business/Centre Documents:**
- TEC (Telecentre Entrepreneur Course) certificate
- Photograph of your centre/shop (interior and exterior)
- Proof of address for the centre location

## How to Get TEC Certificate

TEC (Telecentre Entrepreneur Course) is a mandatory certification for CSC registration:

1. Visit the CSC Academy portal or TEC registration link on [register.csc.gov.in](https://register.csc.gov.in/)
2. Register using your Aadhaar number and mobile number
3. Pay the TEC course fee (approximately ₹1,479 including GST)
4. Complete the online course modules
5. Pass the online examination
6. Download your TEC certificate after successful completion

**Important:** TEC certificate is mandatory. Applications without TEC certificate will be rejected.

## Step-by-Step CSC Registration Process (Online)

### Step 1: Visit the Registration Portal

1. Open [register.csc.gov.in](https://register.csc.gov.in/) in your browser
2. Select **"New VLE Registration"** or **"Apply"**

### Step 2: Choose Application Type

Select the type of CSC you want to register:
- **CSC VLE** — Individual entrepreneur (most common)
- **SHG (Self Help Group)** — For women-led self-help groups
- **Banking Correspondent** — For banking services

### Step 3: Enter Personal Details

1. Enter your **Aadhaar number**
2. Authenticate via **biometric** or **OTP**
3. Your name, address, and photo will auto-populate from Aadhaar
4. Enter your **mobile number** and **email address**
5. Fill in educational qualification details
6. Upload your **TEC certificate number**

### Step 4: Enter Centre/Kiosk Details

1. Select your **State**, **District**, **Block**, and **Village/Ward**
2. Enter the full address of your proposed CSC centre
3. Upload **photographs of your centre** (interior and exterior)
4. Mark your centre location on the map (GPS coordinates)
5. Provide details of available infrastructure (computers, printers, internet)

### Step 5: Upload Documents

Upload scanned copies of:
1. PAN card
2. Bank passbook front page or cancelled cheque
3. Educational certificate
4. Centre photographs
5. Any additional documents requested

### Step 6: Enter Banking Details

1. Enter your **bank account number**
2. Enter **IFSC code**
3. Enter **bank name** and **branch**
4. This account will be used for CSC commission payouts

### Step 7: Review and Submit

1. Review all entered information carefully
2. Accept the terms and conditions
3. Click **Submit Application**
4. Note down your **application reference number**

### Step 8: Application Verification

1. Your application will be reviewed by the **CSC District Manager**
2. Physical verification of your centre may be conducted
3. You will receive updates via SMS and email
4. Approval typically takes **30-45 days**

### Step 9: Receive CSC ID

Once approved:
1. You will receive your **CSC VLE ID** via email and SMS
2. Log in to [digitalseva.csc.gov.in](https://digitalseva.csc.gov.in/) with your new ID
3. Set your password and start offering services

## Important Tips

1. Complete the TEC certification before starting the CSC registration
2. Ensure your centre has stable internet and backup power supply
3. Keep all original documents ready for physical verification
4. Only one CSC is allowed per Gram Panchayat — check availability first
5. Do not pay any agent or middleman for CSC registration — the process is free

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is CSC registration free?

Yes, CSC VLE registration is completely free. You only need to pay for the TEC certificate (₹1,479). Do not pay any agent or middleman for registration.

### What is the TEC certificate for CSC?

TEC (Telecentre Entrepreneur Course) is a mandatory online course and certification required before applying for CSC registration. It costs approximately ₹1,479.

### How long does CSC registration take?

The complete process from application to CSC ID activation typically takes 30-45 days, depending on district manager review and physical verification timelines.

### Can I open a CSC center in a city?

Yes, CSC centers can be opened in both rural and urban areas. Urban CSC centers are registered under the urban ward system.

### What is the income from a CSC center?

Income varies based on services offered and location. VLEs earn commissions on each service. Active CSC operators can earn ₹15,000 to ₹50,000+ per month depending on the volume of services.

### How do I check my CSC registration status?

Visit [register.csc.gov.in](https://register.csc.gov.in/) and check the status using your application reference number or Aadhaar number.
