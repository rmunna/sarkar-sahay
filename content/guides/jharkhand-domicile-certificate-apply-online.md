# Jharkhand Domicile Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **local resident certificate** (स्थानीय निवासी प्रमाण पत्र) in Jharkhand — the state's equivalent of a domicile certificate — is issued by the Revenue Department (Circle Officer / Anchal Adhikari) and is required for NEET/JCECE Jharkhand state quota admissions, JPSC government job applications, Jharkhand state scholarships, and scheme eligibility. Applications go through **Jharsewa** — Jharkhand's integrated e-services platform.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [jharsewa.jharkhand.gov.in](https://jharsewa.jharkhand.gov.in) or **Pragya Kendra**. Issued by Circle Officer within **15–30 days**.

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Local name** | Sthaniya Niwasi Praman Patra (स्थानीय निवासी प्रमाण पत्र) |
| **Eligibility** | Born in Jharkhand OR permanent resident |
| **Issuing authority** | Circle Officer (Anchal Adhikari) |
| **Processing time** | 15–30 days |
| **Fee** | ₹10–₹20 |
| **Validity** | Usually valid 1–3 years (reissue for admissions) |
| **Portal** | jharsewa.jharkhand.gov.in |

## Why You Need Jharkhand Domicile Certificate

- **NEET Jharkhand state quota (85%)** — local resident certificate mandatory for JCECE (Jharkhand Combined Entrance Competitive Examination Board) counselling
- **JPSC (Jharkhand PSC)** — Jharkhand government jobs
- **JSSC (Jharkhand Staff Selection Commission)** — state recruitment
- **Jharkhand Post-Matric Scholarship** — SC/ST/OBC state scholarship
- **PM Awas Yojana / Abua Awas Yojana** — Jharkhand housing scheme
- **Mukhyamantri Sarkar Aapke Dwar** — scheme eligibility

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory — with Jharkhand address)
- **Ration card** (Jharkhand ration card)
- **Voter ID** (Jharkhand voter enrollment)
- **Birth certificate** — showing birth in Jharkhand
- **School Transfer Certificate (TC)** — from Jharkhand schools
- **Khatian / Land Records** — for tribal or rural applicants
- **Tribal Panchayat letter** — for remote tribal areas where documents are scarce (accepted at Circle Officer discretion)
- Utility bills at Jharkhand address
- Self-declaration of Jharkhand residency
- Passport-size photograph

## How to Apply Online — Jharsewa Portal

1. Go to [jharsewa.jharkhand.gov.in](https://jharsewa.jharkhand.gov.in)
2. Register / log in with mobile number (OTP)
3. Navigate to **"Revenue Department"** → **"Local Resident Certificate"** or **"Residential Certificate"**
4. Fill form:
   - Applicant name, father's name, date of birth
   - District, Sub-Division, Anchal (Circle), Village/Ward
   - Duration of residence in Jharkhand
   - Purpose of certificate
5. Upload documents (Aadhaar, ration card, birth certificate, school TC)
6. Pay fee: **₹10–₹20** (online)
7. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via Pragya Kendra

1. Visit nearest **Pragya Kendra** (Jharkhand government CSC kiosk at block level)
2. Carry Aadhaar, ration card, voter ID, school TC
3. Operator fills and submits on Jharsewa
4. Service charge: approximately ₹30–60
5. Collect acknowledgement

---

## Status Check & Download

1. Go to [jharsewa.jharkhand.gov.in](https://jharsewa.jharkhand.gov.in) → **"Track Application"**
2. Enter application reference number
3. Once approved: download digitally signed PDF

## NEET / JCECE Jharkhand State Quota 2026

For **NEET 2026 Jharkhand state quota** (JCECE medical/dental counselling):
1. Jharkhand local resident certificate — proof of Jharkhand residency
2. OR studied in Jharkhand schools for prescribed period (check JCECE notification)
3. Certificate must be Circle Officer/Anchal Adhikari issued
4. Apply well before NEET results (15–30 day processing)
5. JCECE verifies during counselling document check

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Tribal khatian letter — when is it accepted?

For **Scheduled Tribe (ST) applicants** in remote areas where formal documents are scarce, the **tribal panchayat / mukhiya letter** certifying residency is sometimes accepted at the Circle Officer's discretion. However, it's better to have Aadhaar + ration card + school TC as primary documents.

### JPSC — is Jharkhand domicile mandatory?

**Yes, for most Jharkhand state government posts** (JPSC/JSSC), Jharkhand local resident certificate is required. Check the specific JPSC advertisement — some centrally funded posts may not require state domicile.

### How many years of residence for Jharkhand domicile?

Jharkhand doesn't have a rigidly enforced fixed-year rule (unlike Rajasthan's 10 years or Maharashtra's 15 years). **Birth in Jharkhand** or **permanent residence** with supporting documents is the standard. Long-term residents (3+ years with consistent utility/school records) qualify.
