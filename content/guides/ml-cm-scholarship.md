# CM's Scholarship Scheme Meghalaya – Complete Guide 2026

## What is the CM's Scholarship Scheme?

The **Chief Minister's Scholarship Scheme (CMSS)** is an education support initiative by the Government of Meghalaya. It provides financial assistance to meritorious and economically disadvantaged students from Meghalaya pursuing higher education. The scheme covers tuition fees, maintenance allowance, and other educational expenses.

## Scholarship Benefits

| Component | Approximate Amount |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Tuition fee coverage | Up to actual fees (capped amount) |
| Maintenance allowance | ₹5,000-10,000 per year |
| Book grant | ₹2,000-5,000 per year |
| Total annual benefit | Varies by course level |

> Exact amounts vary by course and year. Verify with the Directorate of Higher & Technical Education.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Residency:** Permanent resident of Meghalaya (domicile certificate required)
- **Education:** Pursuing graduation, post-graduation, or professional courses
- **Academic merit:** Minimum percentage in qualifying examination (typically 50-60%)
- **Income:** Annual family income below ₹2-2.5 lakh
- **Institution:** Enrolled in a recognised institution (within or outside Meghalaya)
- **Not availing** other state scholarships simultaneously (some exceptions apply)

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar Card
- Domicile / permanent resident certificate of Meghalaya
- Previous qualifying exam marksheet
- Admission letter / bonafide certificate from institution
- Income certificate (from Deputy Commissioner's office)
- Bank passbook (student's name, Aadhaar-linked)
- Passport-size photographs
- Caste / tribe certificate (if applicable)
- Course fee receipt

## How to Apply – Step-by-Step

### Online Application

1. Visit the Meghalaya scholarship portal or [megeducation.gov.in](https://megeducation.gov.in)
2. Register with your mobile number and email
3. Fill in personal, academic, and family income details
4. Upload scanned copies of all documents
5. Select the scholarship scheme — **CM's Scholarship**
6. Review and submit the application
7. Note the **application ID** for tracking
8. Applications are verified by the education department and institution

### Offline Application

1. Visit the **Directorate of Higher & Technical Education** or **District Education Office**
2. Collect the scholarship form
3. Fill in all details and attach documents
4. Get the form attested by the **institution head**
5. Submit at the designated office before the deadline

## Scholarship Renewal

- Renewal is required **each academic year**
- Student must pass the previous year with the minimum required percentage
- Submit updated marksheet and continuation certificate
- Apply before the renewal deadline

## Processing Time

- **Application review:** 1-2 months
- **Disbursement:** Typically within the academic session (may take 2-4 months)

## Important Tips

1. **Apply before the deadline** — late applications are not accepted
2. **Maintain minimum academic performance** for renewal
3. **Keep all fee receipts** — they may be needed for reimbursement claims
4. **Check for additional scholarships** — you may be eligible for central tribal/minority scholarships too
5. **Update contact details** if you change your phone number or email

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Can students studying outside Meghalaya apply?
**A:** Yes, students from Meghalaya studying in **recognised institutions outside the state** are generally eligible.

### Q2: Is there a specific academic cutoff?
**A:** Typically **50-60% in the qualifying exam**. Check the specific notification for exact requirements.

### Q3: Can I avail this with NSP scholarships?
**A:** Some central scholarships through the **National Scholarship Portal (NSP)** may not be clubbed. Check scheme-specific rules.

### Q4: What courses are covered?
**A:** Graduation, post-graduation, and professional courses (engineering, medical, law, etc.) in recognised institutions.

### Q5: How do I check my scholarship status?
**A:** Log in to the scholarship portal with your application ID, or contact the **District Education Office**.
