# Jio Fiber vs Airtel Xstream — Plans, Speed & How to Apply

Choosing a broadband connection for your home? **Jio Fiber** and **Airtel Xstream Fiber** are the two most popular private fiber broadband providers in India. Both offer high-speed internet, OTT bundles, and wide coverage across cities and towns. This guide compares their plans, helps you decide, and walks you through the application process.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent informational website. We are not affiliated with Jio, Airtel, or any telecom provider. Always verify plans and pricing on the official websites.

---

## What are Jio Fiber and Airtel Xstream?

**Jio Fiber** (by Reliance Jio) is a fiber-to-the-home (FTTH) broadband service launched in 2019. It offers speeds up to 1 Gbps with unlimited data on most plans, plus bundled OTT apps and gaming content.

**Airtel Xstream Fiber** (by Bharti Airtel) is Airtel's FTTH broadband service. It also offers speeds up to 1 Gbps with unlimited data, OTT bundles, and a strong customer service network.

Both require fiber infrastructure in your area for installation.

---

## Eligibility

- Any resident in a **serviceable area** can apply.
- Applicant must be **18 years or older**.
- Valid **ID proof and address proof** required.
- Your building / society must allow fiber installation (cabling through common areas).
- Both offer **residential and business** plans.

---

## Documents Needed

For both Jio Fiber and Airtel Xstream:

- **Aadhaar card** (for identity and address verification)
- **PAN card or Voter ID** (alternative ID proof)
- **Passport-size photo** (usually taken digitally during installation)
- **Mobile number** for OTP verification

Most applications are processed via **Aadhaar eKYC** — just your Aadhaar number and OTP on your linked mobile.

## Step-by-Step: Apply for Jio Fiber

### Online

1. Visit [jio.com/fiber](https://www.jio.com/fiber).
2. Click **"Get Jio Fiber"** or **"Book Now"**.
3. Enter your **address / pincode** to check availability.
4. If available, fill in your **name, mobile number, and address**.
5. Select your preferred **plan**.
6. Submit the request.
7. A Jio representative will call you to confirm and schedule installation.
8. **Installation:** The engineer will visit, lay the fiber cable, install the ONT and router, and activate your connection. Usually takes **3–7 days** from booking.

### Via MyJio App

1. Download the **MyJio** app.
2. Go to **Jio Fiber** section.
3. Check availability and apply.

---

## Step-by-Step: Apply for Airtel Xstream Fiber

### Online

1. Visit [airtel.in/xstream-fiber](https://www.airtel.in/xstream-fiber).
2. Click **"Buy Now"** or **"Get Connection"**.
3. Enter your **address / pincode**.
4. Choose your **plan**.
5. Fill in personal details and submit.
6. An Airtel engineer will be assigned and will contact you.
7. **Installation:** Engineer installs the fiber, ONT, and router at your home. Usually **2–5 days** in cities.

### Via Airtel Thanks App

1. Download the **Airtel Thanks** app.
2. Go to **Xstream Fiber** section.
3. Apply and track your installation.

### Visit Airtel Store

You can also walk into any **Airtel retail store** and apply in person.

## Offline Application

For both providers:

## Processing / Installation Time

- **Metro cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, etc.):** **2–5 working days**.
- **Tier 2/3 cities:** **5–10 working days**.
- **Building permission delays:** If your society doesn't allow cabling, it may take longer. Talk to your RWA/society management.

## Tips and Best Practices

1. **Check both for availability** at your address — one might be available where the other isn't.
2. **Compare OTT bundles** — if you already subscribe to Netflix or Hotstar, pick the plan that includes it.
3. **Speed matters for your use case** — 100 Mbps is enough for most families (4K streaming, WFH, gaming). 300+ Mbps is for heavy users.
4. **Test before committing** — both offer monthly plans without long lock-in periods.
5. **Use the router they provide** initially; upgrade to a Wi-Fi 6 mesh system if your house is large.
6. **Negotiate** — especially in competitive areas, representatives may offer extra months free or waive the deposit.
7. **Set up auto-pay** to avoid disconnection due to missed bills.
8. **For government broadband option**, see our guide on [BSNL Broadband — Apply Online](/guide/broadband-bsnl-apply).

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Which is better — Jio Fiber or Airtel Xstream?

Both are excellent. **Jio Fiber** is slightly cheaper at entry level and has wider coverage. **Airtel Xstream** often has better customer service and includes Netflix on premium plans. Choose based on availability, price, and the OTT apps you want.

### 2. Can I use my own router?

Yes. Both provide an ONT (Optical Network Terminal). You can connect your own router to the ONT's LAN port for better Wi-Fi coverage and performance.

### 3. Is there a lock-in period?

Most plans are **monthly** with no lock-in. However, if you took a discounted annual plan or got a free router, there might be terms. Read the fine print before signing.

### 4. What if Jio Fiber or Airtel is not available in my area?

Check **BSNL Bharat Fiber** — it has wider reach in semi-urban and rural areas. See our [BSNL Broadband guide](/guide/broadband-bsnl-apply). Other options include local ISPs, ACT Fibernet, Excitel, or Hathway depending on your city.

### 5. Can I shift my connection to a new address?

Yes. Both Jio and Airtel allow shifting requests through their app or customer care. The new address must be serviceable. A small shifting charge may apply.

### 6. How do I pay my Jio Fiber or Airtel broadband bill?

Use the **MyJio app** or **Airtel Thanks app** respectively. You can also pay via Paytm, PhonePe, Google Pay, or net banking.

### 7. What happens if I don't pay my broadband bill?

After the due date, you'll get reminders. If unpaid for **15–30 days**, your connection will be **temporarily suspended**. After 60–90 days, it may be **permanently disconnected** and your deposit forfeited.
