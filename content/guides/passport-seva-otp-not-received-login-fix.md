# Passport Seva OTP Not Received 2026? Fix All Login & Portal Errors

The Passport Seva Online Portal (passportindia.gov.in) has two OTP flows — one for registration and one for login. Many users get stuck because the OTP goes to an old mobile/email or the portal is overloaded. This guide covers every fix.

## Common Passport Seva Portal Errors

| Error | Likely Cause |
|-------|-------------|
| OTP not received on mobile | Wrong mobile registered, or SMS delay |
| OTP not received on email | Wrong email, or spam folder |
| "Invalid OTP entered" | OTP expired (valid only 30 seconds) |
| "Account locked" | 3 failed login attempts |
| Portal not loading | Server overloaded (peak hours) |
| "User already registered" | Account exists with your email |
| Appointment booking page blank | Browser cache or JS issue |

## Fix 1: OTP Not Received on Mobile

**Passport Seva sends OTP to BOTH email and mobile.** If mobile OTP is delayed:

1. **Check your email first** — the same OTP is sent to your registered email
2. **Wait 2–3 minutes** — OTP gateway is slow during 10 AM–5 PM
3. Click **"Resend OTP"** — available after 60 seconds
4. **Check spam/DND**: mobile transactional SMS may be filtered. Check "Promotional/DND" folder
5. **If still not received**: use the email OTP to proceed

## Fix 2: Mobile Number or Email Changed Since Registration

If you no longer have access to the mobile/email used during registration:

**If you still have email access but not mobile:**
1. Login using email OTP only (the portal allows email OTP for most actions)
2. Once logged in, go to **Profile → Edit** and update your mobile number

**If you lost access to both email and mobile:**
1. Visit your nearest **Passport Seva Kendra (PSK)** in person
2. Carry your **Aadhaar card** and the passport (old/applied-for)
3. Request staff to update your registered email/mobile on the spot
4. There's no online self-service route for changing both credentials at once

## Fix 3: Account Locked After Failed Attempts

If you entered the wrong password 3 times, your account is locked:

1. Go to [passportindia.gov.in](https://passportindia.gov.in/) → **Login**
2. Click **"Forgot Password"**
3. Enter your registered email address
4. You'll receive a password reset link on email (valid for 30 minutes)
5. Set a new password — minimum 8 characters, must include a number and special character

> **Do not share your OTP or password** — Passport Seva staff never ask for these. Scammers impersonate passport officers.

## Fix 4: Portal Not Loading or Appointment Page Blank

The portal is heavily loaded on Monday mornings and after public holidays when appointment slots open:

1. **Best time to access**: 8 AM – 9:30 AM or after 10 PM
2. **Clear browser cache**: Chrome → Ctrl+Shift+Delete → Cached images and files
3. **Use Chrome in private mode** (Ctrl+Shift+N): avoids cache conflicts
4. **Disable browser extensions**: some extensions break the Passport Seva JavaScript
5. **Try Firefox**: Passport Seva works reliably on Firefox even when Chrome has issues

## Fix 5: "User Already Registered" Error During Registration

This means your email is already linked to an account:

1. Try logging in with that email + "Forgot Password" if you don't remember the password
2. If you created the account long ago, try common passwords or use "Forgot Password"
3. If someone else used your email by mistake, contact **1800-258-1800** (helpline)

## Fix 6: OTP Valid for Only 30 Seconds — "Invalid OTP"

The Passport Seva OTP expires in **30 seconds** — faster than most portals:

1. Enter the OTP immediately after receiving it
2. If it expires: click "Resend OTP" and enter it within 30 seconds
3. **Do not use copy-paste** if delayed — type it directly
4. If on email: keep the inbox tab open so you can switch tabs quickly

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I book a passport appointment without logging in?

No. Login is mandatory to book a Passport Seva Kendra appointment. Create an account first with a valid email and mobile, then login to book.

### My OTP comes on email but not on mobile. Can I still proceed?

Yes. The email OTP and mobile OTP are the same — entering the OTP from your email on the mobile OTP field works. You do not need both separately.

### How do I change my registered email on Passport Seva?

Login → Go to your **Profile (top right)** → Click **"Edit"** → Update email → Verify new email with OTP sent to new address. This can be done online without visiting a PSK.

### The portal says "Service Temporarily Unavailable." When will it work?

This usually happens during maintenance (midnight – 4 AM) or server overload (peak hours). Wait 2–3 hours or try early morning. Passport Seva maintenance is usually announced on their Twitter/social media.

### Can I use the same Passport Seva account to apply for a family member?

Yes. Under one account, you can create multiple **ARN (Application Reference Numbers)** for family members. Go to **Apply for Fresh Passport** or **Re-issue** and fill in the family member's details.
