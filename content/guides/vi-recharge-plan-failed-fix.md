# Vi Recharge Failed? Fix Payment & Plan Errors

Did your Vi recharge fail but money got deducted? Or is the Vi app showing errors during payment? Recharge failures on Vi (Vodafone Idea) are common and usually fixable. This guide covers all scenarios — from payment failures to plan not activating — with solutions.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent platform and is not affiliated with Vi (Vodafone Idea). Information is for guidance purposes only.

## Common Vi Recharge Problems

- Payment successful but plan not activated
- Money deducted from bank, recharge failed
- Vi app showing "Transaction Failed" or "Payment Error"
- UPI payment declined for Vi recharge
- Credit/debit card payment not going through
- Double deduction for single recharge
- Wrong plan recharged

## Step-by-Step Fixes

### 1. Wait 15-30 Minutes

Sometimes there's a delay between payment and plan activation. Bank transactions can take time to reflect. Wait at least 30 minutes before taking further action. You should receive an SMS from Vi confirming the recharge.

### 2. Check Plan Status

**Via Vi App:**
1. Open **Vi App** → Go to **My Account**
2. Check **Active Plans** section
3. If your new plan appears, the recharge was successful

**Via SMS/USSD:**
- Dial **\*199#** → Check balance and active plans
- Or SMS **BAL** to **199**

### 3. Fix Vi App Recharge Errors

If the Vi app keeps failing:

1. **Clear app cache:** Settings → Apps → Vi → Clear Cache
2. **Update the app:** Go to Play Store / App Store → Update Vi app
3. **Reinstall:** Uninstall and reinstall the Vi app
4. **Try mobile data instead of WiFi** (or vice versa)
5. **Try the Vi website** instead: [www.myvi.in/recharge](https://www.myvi.in/recharge)

### 4. Fix UPI Payment Failures

| Issue | Fix |
|-------|-----|
| UPI PIN wrong | Re-enter correct PIN; reset via your bank app if forgotten |
| Daily UPI limit reached | Try next day or use card/net banking |
| UPI app not responding | Close and reopen the UPI app |
| Bank server down | Try after some time or use a different bank account |
| Vi not accepting UPI | Try Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm directly |

### 5. Fix Card Payment Failures

- **Insufficient balance** — Check account balance before retrying
- **International transaction block** — Online transactions must be enabled; check with your bank
- **OTP not received** — Wait 2 minutes; ensure your registered mobile is reachable
- **Card expired** — Use a different card
- **Daily limit exceeded** — Contact bank to increase online transaction limit

### 6. Money Deducted but Recharge Not Done

This is the most frustrating scenario. Here's what to do:

1. **Check SMS** — Look for a failure SMS from Vi or your bank
2. **Check bank statement** — See if the amount was actually debited (not just "on hold")
3. **Wait 24-48 hours** — Failed transactions often auto-reverse to your bank account
4. **If not reversed after 48 hours:**
   - Call **199** and report with your transaction ID
   - Also contact your bank with the transaction reference number
   - The refund typically takes 5-7 working days

### 7. Wrong Plan Recharged

If you accidentally recharged with the wrong plan:

- Vi does **not** allow plan cancellation after activation
- Call **199** immediately and explain the situation
- In some cases, Vi may offer a one-time adjustment or credit
- For future, always double-check the plan details before confirming payment

## How to Recharge Vi Without Errors

For the most reliable recharge experience:

1. **Use the Vi website** — [www.myvi.in/recharge](https://www.myvi.in/recharge) is often more stable than the app
2. **Use third-party apps** — Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, Amazon Pay all support Vi recharge
3. **Recharge via bank app** — Most bank apps (SBI YONO, iMobile, etc.) have bill pay / recharge options
4. **Offline recharge** — Visit any Vi store or local recharge shop

## How to Get Refund from Vi

1. **Call 199** → Select "Recharge Issue" → Provide transaction ID
2. **Vi App** → Help → Recharge Issue → Raise Complaint
3. Note the **complaint number**
4. If not resolved in 7 days, escalate to Vi's **Appellate Authority**
5. If still unresolved after 30 days, file a complaint on [PGPORTAL](https://pgportal.gov.in/) under Department of Telecommunications

## Important Tips

1. **Screenshot before paying** — Always screenshot the plan details and payment confirmation page.
2. **Save transaction ID** — Note the UPI transaction ID or bank reference number for every recharge.
3. **Avoid peak hours** — Recharge failures are more common during peak evening hours (7-10 PM). Try early morning.
4. **Keep your Vi app updated** — Outdated app versions cause the most payment errors.
5. **Use auto-recharge cautiously** — Enable it only if you're sure about your plan preference to avoid unwanted charges.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Vi recharge failed but money was deducted. What should I do?
Wait 24-48 hours for automatic reversal. If not refunded, call 199 with your transaction ID. Also contact your bank. Refunds typically take 5-7 working days.

### Why does Vi app show "Transaction Failed" every time?
Clear the Vi app cache, update to the latest version, or try the Vi website at myvi.in. Also check if your payment method (UPI/card) is working by testing a small transaction elsewhere.

### Can I get a refund for a wrong Vi recharge?
Vi generally does not refund activated plans. Call 199 immediately — in some cases they offer a one-time courtesy adjustment. Act fast, as chances decrease after the plan is used.

### How long does a Vi recharge refund take?
If the transaction failed, auto-reversal happens in 24-48 hours. For manually raised complaints, refunds take 5-7 working days to reflect in your bank account.

### Why is my Vi recharge not showing after payment?
Check your active plans in the Vi app or dial *199#. If the plan doesn't show after 30 minutes and money was deducted, call 199 to report the issue with your payment reference.

### How do I recharge Vi if the app is not working?
Use the Vi website (myvi.in/recharge), third-party apps like Google Pay or PhonePe, your bank's app, or visit a local recharge shop for offline recharge.

### How do I complain about Vi recharge issues to TRAI?
First exhaust Vi's complaint process (199 → Appellate Authority). If unresolved after 30 days, file a complaint on [pgportal.gov.in](https://pgportal.gov.in/) under the Department of Telecommunications.
