# Yuva Sathi Scheme West Bengal — Application Status Check, Eligibility & Benefits

The **Yuva Sathi Scheme** (officially called Banglar Yuva Sathi Prakalpa) is a financial assistance programme by the West Bengal government that provides **₹1,500 per month** to unemployed youth. Announced by Chief Minister Mamata Banerjee on **5 February 2026**, the scheme targets educated youth aged 21–40 years who are currently without employment.

If you have already applied through the Duare Sarkar camps held in February 2026 and want to **check your application status**, or if you want to understand eligibility and benefits before applying online, this guide covers everything you need.

> **Also read:** [Banglar Yuva Sathi Scheme — Complete Application Guide](/guide/banglar-yuva-sathi-scheme-west-bengal) for detailed application steps and payment information.

---

## What Is the Yuva Sathi Scheme?

The Yuva Sathi Scheme (also known as Bekar Bhata or unemployment allowance) is designed to provide temporary financial support to unemployed youth in West Bengal until they find employment. Key features include:

- **Monthly allowance:** ₹1,500 credited directly to your bank account via DBT
- **Maximum duration:** 5 years or until you get employed — whichever comes first
- **Total potential benefit:** Up to ₹90,000 over the full period
- **Administered by:** Sports and Youth Services Department, Government of West Bengal
- **First payment date:** 1 April 2026

The scheme replaces and expands upon earlier initiatives like Yuva Utsaha Prakolpo (YUP) and is distinct from the Yuvashree Arpan scheme.

## How to Check Yuva Sathi Application Status

If you applied at a Duare Sarkar camp between 15–26 February 2026, follow these steps to track your application:

### Method 1: Official Portal

1. Visit the official website at **[banglaryuvasathi.gov.in](https://banglaryuvasathi.gov.in/)**.
2. Click on **"Check Application Status"** or **"Track Status"** on the homepage.
3. Enter your **registered mobile number** and verify with the **OTP** sent to your phone.
4. Enter your **reference ID** or **application number** (from the acknowledgement slip you received at the camp).
5. Your current status will be displayed.

### Application Status Stages

Your application moves through these stages:

| Stage | What It Means |
|---|---|
| **Submitted** | Your application has been received |
| **Under Verification** | Documents are being verified by the BDO or municipal authority |
| **Verified** | All documents and details have been confirmed |
| **Approved** | You are eligible and approved for monthly payments |
| **Payment Initiated** | ₹1,500 has been credited or is being processed to your bank account |
| **Rejected** | Application was rejected — check reason and re-apply if eligible |

### Method 2: West Bengal Government Portal

1. Go to **[wb.gov.in](https://wb.gov.in/)** and navigate to the Banglar Yuva Sathi section.
2. Use the status tracking feature with your application number.

### Method 3: Visit Your Local Office

If online tracking is unavailable or you face issues, visit the **Block Development Office (BDO)** in rural areas or your **municipal office** in urban areas with your acknowledgement slip.

> **Tip:** Keep your acknowledgement slip safe — it contains the reference ID needed for all status checks.

## Eligibility Criteria

To qualify for the Yuva Sathi Scheme, you must meet **all** of the following conditions:

### Basic Requirements

- **Age:** Between 21 and 40 years on the date of application
- **Residency:** Permanent resident of West Bengal (proof required)
- **Education:** Passed Class 10 (Madhyamik) or equivalent from a recognised board — WBBSE, CBSE, ICSE, or equivalent
- **Employment status:** Currently unemployed (self-declaration required)

### Who Cannot Apply?

- Persons already receiving benefits under **Yuvashree Arpan** or **Yuva Utsaha** schemes
- Residents of other states
- Applicants who have not cleared Class 10
- Those who are employed full-time or part-time

> **Note:** No specific family income ceiling has been announced. College students who are unemployed can apply, even if they receive a scholarship.

## Benefits and Financial Assistance Amount

| Benefit Detail | Amount / Duration |
|---|---|
| **Monthly payment** | ₹1,500 |
| **Payment mode** | Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to Aadhaar-linked bank account |
| **Maximum duration** | 5 years from date of approval |
| **Maximum total benefit** | ₹90,000 |
| **First disbursement** | 1 April 2026 |

Payments stop automatically when you secure employment or self-employment, or after 5 years — whichever comes first.

## How to Apply for Yuva Sathi Scheme

### Offline Application (Camp-Based) — Window Closed

The initial offline application window ran from **15 February to 26 February 2026** at 294 designated centres across West Bengal through Duare Sarkar camps. This window is now closed.

**What the process involved:**
1. Visit the nearest block office, municipal office, or Duare Sarkar camp
2. Collect and fill Form-A, Self-Declaration Form, and bank mandate form
3. Submit documents (originals for verification, photocopies to attach)
4. Collect acknowledgement slip with reference ID

### Online Application — Opens August 2026

Online applications through the official portal are expected to open from **15 August 2026**.

1. Visit **[banglaryuvasathi.gov.in](https://banglaryuvasathi.gov.in/)**
2. Register with your mobile number and OTP
3. Fill in personal details, education, and bank account information
4. Upload scanned copies of required documents
5. Submit and save your reference ID

## Documents Required

Keep these documents ready for application or verification:

- **Passport-size photograph** (recent)
- **Aadhaar Card** — mandatory for identity and DBT linkage
- **Voter ID** — additional identity proof (if available)
- **Ration Card or Domicile Certificate** — proof of West Bengal residency
- **Class 10 (Madhyamik) Marksheet and Admit Card** — proof of minimum education
- **Highest Qualification Certificate** — if education is above Class 10
- **Income Certificate** — issued by local authority
- **Caste Certificate** — SC/ST/OBC, if applicable
- **Bank Passbook or Cancelled Cheque** — showing Aadhaar-linked account details

> **Important:** Your bank account must be linked to your Aadhaar number. If it isn't, visit your bank and get Aadhaar seeding done before the payment cycle starts.

## Important Tips

1. **Link Aadhaar to your bank account** before the payment date — DBT will fail without Aadhaar seeding.
2. **Keep your acknowledgement slip safe** — you need the reference ID for status checks.
3. **Check status regularly** on the official portal after applying.
4. **Don't fall for scams** — the government does not charge any fee for this scheme. If someone asks for money, it is a fraud.
5. **Update your mobile number** if it changes — OTP verification is needed for status checks and portal access.
6. **You cannot receive both** Yuva Sathi and Yuvashree Arpan/Yuva Utsaha benefits simultaneously.
7. **Report employment** honestly — if you get a job, update your status. Failure to do so may lead to recovery of payments.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How can I check my Yuva Sathi application status?
Visit [banglaryuvasathi.gov.in](https://banglaryuvasathi.gov.in/), click "Check Application Status", enter your mobile number and OTP, then provide your reference ID from the acknowledgement slip. Your current status will be displayed.

### I missed the February camp. Can I still apply?
The offline camp window (15–26 February 2026) is closed. However, online applications are expected to open from **15 August 2026** on the official portal. Keep checking [banglaryuvasathi.gov.in](https://banglaryuvasathi.gov.in/) for updates.

### Is there any application fee?
No. The Yuva Sathi Scheme is completely free. Do not pay anyone who claims to process your application for a fee.

### What if my application is rejected?
Check the rejection reason on the portal. Common reasons include incorrect documents, age mismatch, or already being a beneficiary of another scheme. Fix the issue and re-apply when the online window opens.

### Can I apply if I have a graduation degree?
Yes. The minimum qualification is Class 10 pass. Higher education qualifications like graduation or post-graduation do not disqualify you — as long as you are unemployed.

### When will I receive the first payment?
The government has announced that the first payment cycle begins from **1 April 2026**. Payments will be credited directly to your Aadhaar-linked bank account via DBT.

### Can both siblings from the same family apply?
Yes, each eligible individual can apply independently. There is no restriction on the number of applicants per family, provided each person meets the eligibility criteria individually.
