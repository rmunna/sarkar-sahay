# West Bengal Caste Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **caste certificate** in West Bengal is issued by the Revenue Department (Sub-Divisional Officer / Block Development Officer) and is required for OBC-A/OBC-B/SC/ST reservations, WB government scholarships (Aikyashree, Swami Vivekananda), WBJEAM/NEET counselling, and government job applications. West Bengal has a dedicated portal — **castcertificatewb.gov.in** — specifically for caste certificate applications.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [castcertificatewb.gov.in](https://castcertificatewb.gov.in) or [edistrict.wb.gov.in](https://edistrict.wb.gov.in). Issued by SDO/BDO within **15–30 days**.

---

## Caste Categories in West Bengal

| Category | Reservation % | Notes |
|----------|---------------|-------|
| **SC** | 22% | Scheduled Castes — WB state list |
| **ST** | 6% | Scheduled Tribes (Santhali, Bodo, Munda communities) |
| **OBC-A** | 10% | Other Backward Classes-A (Muslim OBC communities) |
| **OBC-B** | 7% | Other Backward Classes-B (Hindu OBC communities) |

> West Bengal's OBC is split into **OBC-A** (predominantly Muslim communities) and **OBC-B** (Hindu and other communities). This split is unique to West Bengal. For central OBC purposes, WB OBC holders apply for central OBC NCL separately.

## Why You Need WB Caste Certificate

- **Swami Vivekananda Scholarship** — for SC/ST/OBC students
- **Aikyashree Scholarship** — for minority communities (OBC-A mostly)
- **WBJEAM engineering admissions** — reserved category proof
- **NEET WB state quota** — community certificate required
- **Government job reservation** — WBPSC, district recruitment
- **Central OBC NCL certificate** — for UPSC, SSC, railways
- **Kanyashree / other state schemes** — eligibility check

---

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory)
- **Ration card** (shows family caste/community — WB ration card has caste column)
- **Father's school leaving certificate** (TC with caste listed)
- **Father's / grandfather's caste proof** (old ration card, voter ID with caste, govt service record)
- Self-declaration of caste
- **Voter ID** (address proof)
- Passport-size photograph

## How to Apply — Dedicated WB Caste Certificate Portal

1. Go to [castcertificatewb.gov.in](https://castcertificatewb.gov.in)
2. Click **"New Application"** → register with mobile number
3. Select category: **OBC-A / OBC-B / SC / ST**
4. Fill form:
   - Applicant name, address (district, sub-division, block/ward)
   - Caste/community declaration
   - Father's community details and documents
5. Upload documents (Aadhaar, ration card, father's TC)
6. Pay fee: **₹20–30** (online)
7. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via e-District WB Portal

1. Go to [edistrict.wb.gov.in](https://edistrict.wb.gov.in)
2. Log in / register
3. Click **"Revenue Department"** → **"Caste Certificate"**
4. Follow same form-filling and document upload process
5. Alternative to the dedicated caste certificate portal

---

## Apply via Duare Sarkar Camp

WB's **Duare Sarkar** (government at doorstep) camps are held regularly in every GP/ward:
1. Visit the nearest **Duare Sarkar camp** (schedule on wb.gov.in)
2. Carry all documents
3. Government officials help fill and submit the application
4. Free of charge
5. Certificates often delivered at home or GP office

## Status Check

2. Click **"Track Application"** → enter reference number
3. Or log in → **"My Applications"** → view status
4. Status flow: Submitted → BDO/SDO Verification → Approved

## OBC NCL Certificate — West Bengal

For **central government OBC reservation** (UPSC, SSC, IIT admissions):
- Apply for "OBC NCL Certificate" at e-District WB or SDO office
- WB OBC-A and OBC-B holders may or may not be on the **central OBC list** — verify separately
- Income < ₹8 lakh/year required
- Valid for **3 years** (central NCL)

> **Important:** Many WB OBC-A/B communities are **not on the central OBC list**. Before applying for a central job OBC reservation, check if your community appears in the Central OBC list (Ministry of Social Justice — OBC list for WB).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### OBC-A vs OBC-B in West Bengal — which applies to me?

**OBC-A** includes predominantly Muslim OBC communities in WB (e.g., Atishudra Hindu communities and Muslim artisan communities). **OBC-B** includes Hindu OBC communities (Teli, Mahishya, Goala, Suri, etc.). Check the WB government OBC-A and OBC-B schedule to identify your community.

### WB OBC certificate valid for central OBC quota?

**Not automatically.** West Bengal's OBC list does not fully overlap with the central OBC list. Verify your community at [ncbc.nic.in](https://ncbc.nic.in) (National Commission for Backward Classes). If your community is on the central OBC list, you can claim central OBC reservation using a separate OBC NCL certificate.

### Caste certificate for WBJEAM — what validity?

WBJEAM counselling requires a caste certificate issued **within the same academic year** or as specified in the counselling notice. Old certificates may not be accepted.
