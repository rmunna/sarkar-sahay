## What is Haryana Free Electricity Scheme?

**Haryana Free Electricity Scheme for BPL Families** provides **2,000 free electricity units (kWh) per year** to households identified as Below Poverty Line (BPL) through the **Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP)** database. This translates to approximately **166 units per month** — enough to cover basic household electricity use.

| Feature | Details |

## Who is Eligible?

- **Haryana resident family** with electricity connection
- Family identified as **BPL (Below Poverty Line)** in the **PPP (Parivar Pehchan Patra)** database
- Annual family income **below ₹1.80 lakh/year** as per PPP

The BPL status is determined automatically via the PPP database. If your family's PPP shows income below ₹1.80 lakh, you are in the BPL category and eligible.

---

## How the Scheme Works

**For PPP-verified BPL families:**
1. Electricity consumption is tracked on your meter
2. Up to 2,000 units per year — **zero bill** (state subsidy covers cost)
3. Beyond 2,000 units — normal billing rate applies
4. No cash payment needed — subsidy applied automatically at billing

---

## How to Ensure You Get the Benefit

### Step 1 — Verify Your PPP Status

Check your family's PPP income status at **[meraparivar.haryana.gov.in](https://meraparivar.haryana.gov.in)**:
- Login with PPP ID
- Check income category shown on the profile

### Step 2 — Link PPP with Electricity Connection

Visit your nearest **DHBVN / UHBVN sub-divisional office**:
- Bring PPP ID, Aadhaar, electricity consumer number
- Request linking of BPL status to your electricity connection
- Once linked, free units are automatically applied in billing

### Step 3 — Check Your Bills

After linking, your electricity bills should show "0 payable" for usage below 166 units/month (2,000/year). If being charged despite BPL status, visit the SDO office with your PPP.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. How do I know if my family is BPL as per PPP?**

Check at meraparivar.haryana.gov.in or visit your nearest SARAL Centre / CSC with your PPP number. The portal shows income category. If below ₹1.80 lakh, you are BPL.

**2. My family is BPL but I still get electricity bills. What do I do?**

Visit your DHBVN or UHBVN sub-divisional office with your PPP, Aadhaar, and electricity bill. Request linking of BPL status. If already linked but still billed, raise a formal complaint and ask for billing correction.

**3. We use about 200 units a month. Will we still get any benefit?**

Yes. 2,000 units/year = 166 units/month free. If you use 200 units/month (2,400/year), you get 2,000 free and pay for the remaining 400 units at normal rate. Significant savings.

**4. Is this different from the PM UJJWALA scheme?**

Yes. PM Ujjwala gives free LPG gas connections to BPL women. Haryana free electricity scheme gives free electricity units to BPL households. Both are separate and can be availed simultaneously.

**5. Can I apply if I don't have PPP yet?**

You need PPP to access most Haryana BPL schemes. Apply for PPP at any SARAL Centre or CSC in Haryana — it's free. Once enrolled, your income details are entered and BPL status determined.
