# Assam Mukhyamantri Mahila Udyamita Abhiyan 2026 — Women Entrepreneurs to Earn ₹1 Lakh/Year

The **Mukhyamantri Mahila Udyamita Abhiyan (MMUA)** is the Assam government's flagship women entrepreneurship programme — described as one of the biggest of its kind. The scheme provides **three-stage financial assistance, training, and resources** to women members of Self Help Groups (SHGs) with the goal of helping each beneficiary earn **₹1 lakh per year** through self-employment.

**Quick answer:** Assam SHG women can apply through their SHG group to receive phased financial support (up to ₹1 lakh) + business training + market linkage. The scheme targets economically weaker SHG members. Contact your nearest Block Development Office (BDO) or Assam Rural Infrastructure Development Society (ARIDS) for enrollment.

---

## Scheme Overview

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Scheme name | Mukhyamantri Mahila Udyamita Abhiyan (MMUA) |
| State | Assam |
| Target | Women SHG members (economically weaker) |
| Goal | ₹1 lakh annual income per beneficiary |
| Support type | Three-stage financial assistance + training + market linkage |
| Department | Rural Development / SHG Mission, Assam |

---

## Who Is Eligible?

- **Women members of registered Self Help Groups (SHGs)** in Assam
- SHGs that are active, credit-linked, and have a positive grading
- Preference to **economically weaker women** — BPL and low-income households
- The SHG must be functioning for a minimum period (typically 6 months to 1 year)
- Women engaged in micro-enterprise or willing to start one

**Not eligible:**
- Women who are not part of a registered SHG
- SHGs with poor repayment or inactive track record

---

## Three-Stage Financial Support

The MMUA provides funding in three phases, ensuring women have the resources to start, stabilise, and grow a micro-enterprise:

### Stage 1: Seed Capital (Start)

- Initial financial support to purchase raw materials, tools, or equipment for the chosen micro-business
- Grants / soft loans at this stage to reduce initial burden

### Stage 2: Working Capital (Grow)

- Second tranche for working capital needs once the business is established
- Helps the enterprise sustain operations and expand production/service capacity

### Stage 3: Scaling Support (Sustain)

- Third stage links beneficiaries to formal credit (bank loans, MUDRA), market networks, and government procurement programs
- Facilitates income stabilisation so the annual income target of ₹1 lakh is achieved

---

## Training and Skilling

Beyond financial support, the scheme provides:

- **Skill training** in the chosen trade / micro-enterprise (tailoring, food processing, handicrafts, livestock, etc.)
- **Financial literacy** and bookkeeping training
- **Digital literacy** — to enable digital payments and e-commerce
- **Market linkage** — connection to fairs, melas, local government purchase mandates, and e-commerce platforms

---

## How to Apply / Enroll

The scheme operates through **Self Help Groups** as the delivery mechanism:

### Step 1: Join or Activate Your SHG

If you're not in an SHG, join or form one in your village:
- A minimum of 10-15 women from the same village/locality
- Register with the Gram Panchayat or Block Development Office
- Open a bank account and start regular savings

### Step 2: SHG Gets Graded

Your SHG's performance is evaluated (savings regularity, meeting attendance, loan repayment). Higher-grade SHGs get priority access to MMUA funds.

### Step 3: Apply for MMUA Benefit via BDO

1. Visit your **Block Development Office (BDO)** or the Assam SHG Mission office in your district
2. The BDO or District Mission Coordinator will shortlist SHGs and members
3. Eligible women receive a **call letter / notice** for document submission
4. Submit required documents and fill in the MMUA application form
5. Upon verification, financial support is disbursed in stages to the SHG's bank account

---

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar card
- SHG membership certificate / SHG passbook
- SHG bank account details (group account)
- Income/BPL certificate (if applicable)
- Residential proof (Assam address)
- Passport-size photograph
- Details of proposed/existing micro-enterprise (product/service, location, estimated investment)

---

## Types of Micro-Enterprises Supported

- **Agro-based:** Vegetable cultivation, mushroom farming, fish farming, poultry, goatry
- **Food processing:** Rice milling, dal processing, pickle making, bakery
- **Handicrafts:** Bamboo craft, handloom weaving, pottery
- **Tailoring and apparel:** Dress making, block printing, embroidery
- **Livestock:** Dairy, piggery, sericulture (silk)
- **Services:** Beauty parlour, phone/electronics repair, vehicle service support

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need to be in an SHG to apply?**
Yes — MMUA is delivered through SHGs. If you're not in one, form or join an SHG first. Contact your BDO for guidance on forming a new SHG.

**Is the financial support a loan or a grant?**
The scheme has a mix of grants and soft loans. Stage 1 seed capital is typically grant-based; later stages may involve bank-linked credit. Specific terms vary by cohort.

**What if my business doesn't reach ₹1 lakh income?**
The scheme's goal is ₹1 lakh annually per beneficiary, but it's an aspiration. The scheme provides tools and support; actual income depends on the enterprise, effort, and market conditions.

**Can women from tea garden communities apply?**
Tea garden worker families are part of Assam's SHG network. Check with your BDO — MMUA eligibility covers rural and semi-rural women across districts.

**How long does the scheme run?**
The MMUA is part of the Assam government's multi-year plan for women's financial inclusion. Check with the BDO or State Rural Livelihood Mission (SRLM) Assam for current batch enrollment status.

**Where can I check the MMUA beneficiary list?**
The district-wise MMUA list is published periodically by the Rural Development department. Check your Block Development Office notice board or the CM Assam website.
