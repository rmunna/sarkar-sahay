# Axis Bank NetBanking Not Working? Fix Login & OTP Issues

Axis Bank Internet Banking allows customers to manage accounts, transfer funds, pay bills, and more online. If you're facing login failures, OTP delays, app errors, or transaction issues, follow this troubleshooting guide to fix them.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent platform and is not affiliated with Axis Bank. Information is based on publicly available details from Axis Bank's official website.

## Common Axis Bank NetBanking Problems and Fixes

### 1. Password Reset / Forgot Password

If you've forgotten your Axis Bank NetBanking password or it's locked:

**How to Fix:**

1. Go to the [Axis Bank NetBanking login page](https://retail.axisbank.co.in/wps/portal/rBanking/axisebanking/AxisRetailLogin)
2. Click **"Forgot/Reset Password"**
3. Select **"Login Password"**
4. Enter your **Customer ID** and registered mobile number
5. Verify with **OTP** and **debit card** details (last 6 digits + expiry)
6. Create a new password (8-20 characters with uppercase, lowercase, number, special character)
7. Log in with your new password

**If this doesn't work:**
- Visit your nearest Axis Bank branch with PAN card and photo ID
- Call Axis Bank at **1860 419 5555** or **1860 500 5555**

### 2. OTP Not Received or Delayed

**How to Fix:**

1. **Wait 3-5 minutes** — OTPs may be delayed during high-traffic periods
2. Check if your **registered mobile number** is active and has signal
3. Ensure **DND (Do Not Disturb)** is not enabled — send **STOP DND** to 1909 from your number
4. Check if your **SMS inbox is full** — delete old messages and retry
5. Click **"Resend OTP"** on the banking page
6. Restart your phone and try again
7. For Axis Mobile app, OTP may arrive as an **in-app notification**

**If OTP still doesn't come:**
- Your mobile number may not be updated with the bank — visit a branch to update
- Call **1860 419 5555** to verify your registered mobile number

### 3. Axis Mobile App Crash / Not Working

| Problem | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| App crashing on launch | Update app from Play Store / App Store |
| Stuck on loading screen | Clear cache: Settings > Apps > Axis Mobile > Clear Cache |
| Login error after update | Uninstall and reinstall the app |
| Biometric login failing | Re-register fingerprint/face in app settings |
| UPI not working | Check UPI server status; try after 30 minutes |
| "Session expired" error | Log in again; avoid keeping app idle for long |

### 4. Device Registration / New Device Login

Axis Bank requires device registration when you log in from a new device or after reinstalling the app.

**How to Fix:**

1. Open **Axis Mobile** app on your new device
2. Enter your **registered mobile number**
3. Enter your **Customer ID** or account number
4. Verify using **debit card details** — card number, expiry date, CVV
5. Enter the **OTP** received on your registered mobile
6. Set up your **MPIN** and **login password**
7. Registration is complete

**If device registration fails:**
- Ensure you're using the **latest version** of Axis Mobile
- Check that your mobile number matches the bank records
- If you've changed your phone number, visit a branch first to update it

### 5. Axis Bank Secure Access Issues

Axis Secure Access is required for high-value NetBanking transactions.

**How to Fix:**

1. Download the **Axis Secure Access** feature within the Axis Mobile app
2. Enable it via NetBanking: **Settings > Secure Access > Enable**
3. For each high-value transaction, you'll receive a push notification on Axis Mobile
4. **Approve the transaction** on your phone to complete it

**Common issues:**
- **Not receiving push notifications** — Enable notifications for Axis Mobile in phone settings
- **"Secure Access not registered"** — Re-register via NetBanking Settings
- **Notification expired** — Complete the transaction within the time limit (usually 3 minutes)

### 6. Transaction Failed but Amount Debited

1. **Wait 24-48 hours** for auto-reversal of the failed transaction
2. Check your **mini statement** or account statement for reversal entry
3. If not reversed, raise a complaint:
   - NetBanking: **Service Requests > Transaction Dispute**
   - Axis Mobile: **Help > Raise a Complaint**
   - Call **1860 419 5555** with transaction details
4. For UPI transaction disputes, you can also complain through the NPCI portal

### 7. NetBanking Page Not Loading

1. Check if [Axis Bank's website](https://www.axisbank.com/) is accessible
2. Clear browser **cache and cookies**
3. Try **Chrome or Firefox** (recommended browsers)
4. Disable **VPN** and **ad-blockers**
5. Try **incognito/private browsing** mode
6. Check your internet connection
7. Scheduled maintenance is usually between **11 PM to 5 AM** on weekends

## Important Tips

1. **Never share** OTP, password, CVV, or PIN with anyone — Axis Bank will never call and ask for these
2. **Update your registered mobile number** promptly if you change it — visit the branch with ID proof
3. **Bookmark the official login page** to avoid phishing sites — the URL should start with `axisbank.co.in`
4. **Keep Axis Mobile app updated** — older versions frequently cause login and transaction errors
5. **Set daily transaction limits** in NetBanking to add an extra layer of security

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I reset my Axis Bank NetBanking password?
Go to the Axis Bank NetBanking login page, click "Forgot/Reset Password," enter your Customer ID, verify with OTP and debit card details, and create a new password.

### Why is my Axis Bank NetBanking account locked?
Your account gets locked after 3-5 incorrect password attempts. Use the "Forgot Password" option to reset, or call 1860 419 5555 for help.

### How do I register for Axis Bank NetBanking for the first time?
Visit the Axis Bank NetBanking login page and click "Register." You need your account number, registered mobile number, and debit card details to complete registration.

### Can I access Axis Bank NetBanking on my phone?
Yes, you can use Axis Bank NetBanking through your mobile browser or download the Axis Mobile app from Play Store or App Store.

### What should I do if my Axis Bank UPI payment fails?
Wait 24-48 hours for auto-reversal. If the refund doesn't appear, raise a complaint through Axis Mobile app or call 1860 419 5555.

### How do I update my mobile number in Axis Bank?
You must visit your nearest Axis Bank branch with your PAN card, Aadhaar card, and a signed request letter. Mobile number updates cannot be done online for security.

### What are Axis Bank NetBanking working hours?
Axis Bank NetBanking is available 24/7 except during scheduled maintenance (usually late-night weekends). NEFT transfers follow RBI banking hours.
