# CM Widow Pension Scheme Manipur – Complete Guide 2026

## What is the CM Widow Pension Scheme?

The **CM Widow Pension Scheme** in Manipur provides monthly financial assistance to widowed women from economically weaker sections. The scheme combines the central **IGNWPS** component with a state top-up from the Manipur government.

## Pension Amount

| Category | Approximate Monthly Pension |
|----------|---------------------------|
| Widows aged 40-79 | ₹500-1,000 |
| Widows aged 80+ | ₹800-1,500 |

> Includes central and state components. Verify with District Social Welfare Office.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Status:** Widow (husband deceased), not remarried
- **Age:** 40 years and above (IGNWPS); state scheme may cover younger widows
- **Residency:** Permanent resident of Manipur
- **Income:** BPL or below prescribed income limit
- **Not receiving** any other government pension

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar Card
- Husband's death certificate
- Age proof
- Income certificate / BPL card
- Bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked)
- Residential proof (voter ID / ration card)
- Passport-size photographs (2)
- Self-declaration of not being remarried

## How to Apply – Step-by-Step

1. Visit the **District Social Welfare Office** or **Panchayat office**
2. Collect the **Widow Pension application form**
3. Fill in all personal, income, and bank details
4. Attach self-attested copies of documents
5. Get the form verified by the **Village Authority / Ward Member**
6. Submit at the Social Welfare Office
7. After verification and approval, pension is credited via DBT

## Processing Time

- **Verification to first payment:** 2-4 months

## Important Tips

1. **Obtain death certificate first** — essential for application
2. **Pension stops on remarriage**
3. **Annual life verification** is required
4. **Keep bank account active** to avoid payment issues
5. **Contact the BDO** for any payment-related issues

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Can I get both widow pension and old age pension?
**A:** Generally, only **one NSAP pension** at a time. See our [Manipur Old Age Pension guide](/guide/mn-old-age-pension).

### Q2: What if I don't have a death certificate?
**A:** Obtain one from the **Municipal/Panchayat office** where the death occurred.

### Q3: Is there an online application?
**A:** Check [nsap.nic.in](https://nsap.nic.in). Primary method is offline through the Social Welfare Office.

### Q4: Can I receive this along with CMST?
**A:** Check with the **District Social Welfare Office** for compatibility. See our [CMST guide](/guide/mn-cmst).

### Q5: How is the pension paid?
**A:** Through **DBT** to the beneficiary's Aadhaar-linked bank account.
