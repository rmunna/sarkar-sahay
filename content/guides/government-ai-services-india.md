## What Is Actually Live?

These services are not all the same kind of product. Some are citizen-facing, some are developer infrastructure, and some are platform layers used by other services.

| Layer | Examples | What to expect |
|---|---|---|
| Citizen help | Kar Saathi, Samadhan Didi | Guided answers, routing help or voice-assisted submission |
| Farmer services | Bharat-VISTAAR | Advisory and scheme-support layer as rollout expands |
| Language AI | BHASHINI, ONDC Saarthi | Speech, translation and multilingual interface capabilities |
| Developer infrastructure | IndiaAI Compute, AIKosha | Compute requests, datasets, models and AI development resources |

Do not assume every AI service can accept applications, issue benefits or solve complaints by itself. Always check the official portal and final submission screen.

## Safety Checklist Before Using Any AI Service

- Check that the domain is official, such as `.gov.in`, `.nic.in`, `indiaai.gov.in`, `bhashini.gov.in`, `ondc.org`, `pgportal.gov.in` or `incometax.gov.in`.
- Do not share Aadhaar OTP, bank OTP, passwords or DigiLocker credentials with a chatbot.
- Treat AI output as assistance, not a final government order.
- Save application numbers, grievance IDs and screenshots after final submission.
- For tax, health, legal, subsidy or loan decisions, cross-check against official forms and guidelines.

## Service-by-Service Notes

### Kar Saathi for Taxpayers

Kar Saathi is useful for understanding tax concepts, portal navigation and common direct-tax questions. It should not replace the official e-filing flow or professional advice for complex cases.

Best next guide: [Kar Saathi AI Chatbot and Income Tax Website 2.0 Guide](/guide/kar-saathi-income-tax-ai-chatbot-guide).

### Samadhan Didi for Grievances

Samadhan Didi is designed to make CPGRAMS easier for citizens who prefer voice-based complaint filing. Prepare your reference number, office name, date and exact relief requested before using the grievance flow.

Best next guide: [Samadhan Didi CPGRAMS AI Voice Bot Guide](/guide/samadhan-didi-cpgrams-ai-voice-bot-guide).

### Bharat-VISTAAR for Farmers

Bharat-VISTAAR is positioned around AI-enabled agricultural support: schemes, crop advisories, weather, markets and local-language access. Farmers should still verify pesticide, loan and subsidy actions through official agriculture channels.

Best next guide: [Bharat-VISTAAR AI for Farmers Guide](/guide/bharat-vistaar-ai-farmers-guide).

### BHASHINI and ONDC Saarthi

BHASHINI is the language AI layer. ONDC Saarthi applies multilingual and voice-first ideas to digital commerce. This is important for local-language access, small sellers and citizen services that cannot depend only on English forms.

Best next guide: [BHASHINI and ONDC Saarthi Multilingual AI Guide](/guide/bhashini-ondc-saarthi-multilingual-ai-guide).

### IndiaAI Compute and AIKosha

IndiaAI Compute is for eligible students, researchers, startups, MSMEs and government entities that need AI compute resources. AIKosha is the resource layer for datasets, models and AI artefacts.

Best next guide: [IndiaAI Compute and AIKosha Guide for Students and Developers](/guide/indiaai-compute-aikosha-students-developers-guide).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which government AI service should citizens use first?

It depends on the problem. Use CPGRAMS or Samadhan Didi for central government grievances, Kar Saathi for tax guidance, Bharat-VISTAAR for agriculture AI updates, and BHASHINI-related tools for language or voice-first services.

### Are government AI answers legally final?

No. Treat AI answers as guidance. Final decisions still come from official forms, orders, notices, approvals and department responses.

### Can I trust a WhatsApp link claiming to be a government AI bot?

Only if the link is published by an official government portal or verified official communication. Never enter OTPs, bank details or passwords through forwarded links.

### Is IndiaAI Compute for every student?

No. The compute portal lists student and fellowship categories, but access depends on eligibility, documents, project details and approval.

### Why is CitizenNest building a government AI hub?

Government AI services are spreading across tax, agriculture, grievance redressal, language access and developer infrastructure. A hub helps citizens find the right official route without confusing policy news with usable services.
