# Odisha Income Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **income certificate** (ଆୟ ପ୍ରମାଣ ପତ୍ର — Aay Praman Patra) in Odisha is issued by the Revenue Department (Tahasildar) and is required for PRERANA/NSP scholarships, NEET counselling (state quota), EWS reservation, government scheme eligibility, and government college fee concessions. Applications are processed through **serviceonline.odisha.gov.in** — Odisha's integrated online public service platform.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [serviceonline.odisha.gov.in](https://serviceonline.odisha.gov.in) or at **Mo Seva Kendra / CSC**. Issued by Tahasildar within **15–20 days**.

---

## Why You Need Odisha Income Certificate

- **PRERANA Scholarship** (Odisha state — for ST students) — income proof required
- **NSP Post-Matric Scholarship** — OBC/SC/ST categories
- **Odisha SC/ST Development Scholarship** — income certificate mandatory
- **NEET state counselling** (Odisha) — income certificate for government quota
- **EWS reservation** — annual family income < ₹8 lakh/year
- **Government college fee waiver** — income-based scholarship
- **Kalia Yojana / other state schemes** — eligibility verification

---

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory)
- **Ration card** (if available — NFSA or state ration card)
- Self-declaration of annual income (all sources)
- For salaried: **salary certificate** or Form 16 from employer
- For farmers: **ROR (Record of Rights) / land records** — from RI (Revenue Inspector)
- **Voter ID** (additional address proof)
- Passport-size photograph

---

## How to Apply Online — Odisha Service Online Portal

1. Go to [serviceonline.odisha.gov.in](https://serviceonline.odisha.gov.in)
2. Click **"New User? Register"** → register with mobile number (OTP)
3. Log in → click **"Revenue & Disaster Management"** → **"Income Certificate"**
4. Fill form:
   - Applicant name, address (district, sub-division, block, village)
   - Annual family income (all sources declared)
   - Purpose of certificate
5. Upload documents (Aadhaar, income proof, photograph)
6. Pay fee: **₹20–30** (online payment or challan)
7. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via Mo Seva Kendra / CSC

Odisha's **Mo Seva Kendra** (government service centres) handle serviceonline applications:

1. Visit nearest **Mo Seva Kendra** or CSC
2. Carry Aadhaar, ration card, income proof documents
3. Operator fills and submits income certificate form
4. Service charge: approximately ₹30–50
5. Collect acknowledgement receipt

---

## Apply at Revenue Inspector (RI) Office / Tahasil

1. Visit your **Tahasil office** or Revenue Inspector's office
2. Obtain income certificate application form
3. Submit with supporting income documents
4. RI verifies income at village/block level
5. Forward to Tahasildar for final approval
6. Collect certificate from Tahasil after approval

---

## Status Check

1. Go to [serviceonline.odisha.gov.in](https://serviceonline.odisha.gov.in)
2. Click **"Track Application"** → enter Application Reference Number
3. Or log in → **"My Applications"** → view income certificate status

---

## Download Odisha Income Certificate

Once approved:
1. Log in to serviceonline.odisha.gov.in → **"My Applications"**
2. Click approved income certificate
3. Download digitally signed PDF
4. Valid for all official purposes — Odisha and central government schemes

## Frequently Asked Questions

### PRERANA scholarship — what income limit in Odisha?

**PRERANA Scholarship** (for tribal/ST students under Odisha government): family income below **₹2.5 lakh/year**. For **NSP Post-Matric SC scholarship**: income below ₹2.5 lakh/year. For **NSP Post-Matric OBC**: income below ₹1.5 lakh/year (check latest NSP guidelines).

### ROR vs ration card — which is better income proof for farmers in Odisha?

The **ROR (Record of Rights)** showing your land holdings is the primary income document for farming families — it helps RI estimate agricultural income. Ration card supports family identity and composition. Both should be submitted together for farming households.

### Can I use Odisha income certificate for AIIMS / central college admission?

Yes — income certificate from the Tahasildar of Odisha is valid for central government schemes, NSP, and central college admissions. Ensure it is digitally signed and issued within the required financial year.
