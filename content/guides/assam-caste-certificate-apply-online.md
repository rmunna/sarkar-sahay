# Assam Caste Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **caste certificate** in Assam is issued by the Revenue Department (Circle Officer) and is required for OBC/SC/ST reservations, Assam government scholarships, SEBA/AHSEC post-matric scholarships, government job reservations (APSC, SLRC), and tribal welfare scheme eligibility. Assam has a distinctive tribal classification with **ST(Plains)** and **ST(Hills)** as separate categories. Applications go through **Assam e-District** portal.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [edistrict.assam.gov.in](https://edistrict.assam.gov.in) or at **Seva Setu camp / CSC**. Issued by Circle Officer within **15–30 days**.

---

## Caste Categories in Assam

| Category | Reservation % | Key Communities |
|----------|---------------|-----------------|
| **SC** | 7% | Scheduled Castes (Namshudra, Mala, etc.) |
| **ST (Plains)** | 10% | Bodo, Mising, Rabha, Tiwa, Sonowal Kachari, etc. |
| **ST (Hills)** | 5% | Karbi, Dimasa, Chakma, Deori, Hmar, etc. |
| **OBC/MOBC** | 27% | Other Backward Classes / More OBC (Koch-Rajbongshi, Ahom, Chutia, etc.) |
| **EWS** | 10% | General category, income < ₹8 lakh/year |

> **ST(Plains) and ST(Hills)** are separate categories in Assam. Bodo, Mising, Rabha are Plains tribes; Karbi (Mikir), Dimasa are Hill tribes. Make sure to apply under the correct ST sub-category.
> **MOBC (More OBC)**: Koch-Rajbongshi, Ahom, Chutia, and other indigenous Assamese communities fall under MOBC — a state-specific category.

## Why You Need Assam Caste Certificate

- **Assam government scholarship** — SC/ST/OBC/MOBC post-matric
- **SEBA/AHSEC state scholarships** — caste proof required
- **Government job reservation** — APSC (Assam PSC), SLRC (State Level Recruitment Commission)
- **Assam Direct Recruitment** — Group C, Group D posts (ST/SC/OBC quota)
- **Central OBC NCL** — for UPSC/SSC (if community in central OBC list)
- **Tea garden worker community benefits** — garden worker caste cert for scheme access

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory)
- **Ration card** (shows family caste/community)
- **Father's school leaving certificate** (TC with caste)
- **Patta / land records** (for ST applicants — especially Plains tribes)
- Self-declaration of caste
- **Voter ID** (address proof)
- Passport-size photograph

## How to Apply Online — Assam e-District

1. Go to [edistrict.assam.gov.in](https://edistrict.assam.gov.in)
2. Register / log in with mobile number (OTP)
3. Click **"Revenue Department"** → **"Caste Certificate"**
4. Fill form:
   - Applicant name, address (district, sub-division, circle, village)
   - Caste/category declaration (SC / ST-P / ST-H / OBC / MOBC)
   - Father's community details
5. Upload documents (Aadhaar, father's TC/ration card, photograph)
6. Pay fee: **₹20–30** (online)
7. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via Seva Setu / CSC

1. Visit nearest **Seva Setu camp** (organized at GP/ward level) or CSC
2. Carry Aadhaar, ration card, father's caste proof
3. Government staff helps fill and submit application
4. Seva Setu camps: free of charge; CSC: ₹30–50
5. Collect acknowledgement

---

## Apply at Revenue Circle Office

1. Visit your **Revenue Circle Office** (Circle Officer's office at block/circle level)
2. Obtain caste certificate application form
3. Submit with documents
4. Circle Officer verifies community through local records
5. Collect certificate after approval

---

## Status Check & Download

1. Go to [edistrict.assam.gov.in](https://edistrict.assam.gov.in)
2. Click **"Track Application Status"** → enter reference number
3. Once approved: download digitally signed PDF

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Bodo community — ST(Plains) or ST(Hills) in Assam?

**Bodo** (and allied communities — Boro, Bodo Kachari) are classified under **ST (Plains)** in Assam. They have their own autonomous Bodoland Territorial Council (BTC) area. Apply for ST(Plains) caste certificate if you are Bodo/Boro.

### Karbi community — which ST category?

**Karbi (Mikir)** and **Dimasa** are classified under **ST (Hills)** in Assam, as they traditionally inhabit hilly areas (Karbi Anglong and Dima Hasao districts).

### MOBC certificate Assam — for central government jobs?

**MOBC (More OBC)** is a state-specific category for Assam state government jobs and scholarships. For **central government jobs (UPSC, SSC, railways)**: your community must be in the **central OBC list** ([ncbc.nic.in](https://ncbc.nic.in)). Many Assam MOBC communities (like Ahom, Koch-Rajbongshi) may not be in the central OBC list. Check before applying.
