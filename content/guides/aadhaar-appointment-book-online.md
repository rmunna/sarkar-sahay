# Book Aadhaar Appointment Online: Step-by-Step Guide

Tired of waiting in long queues at Aadhaar centres? UIDAI allows you to book a free appointment online so you get a guaranteed time slot. Whether you need a new Aadhaar, biometric update, or demographic correction — booking online is the smartest way.

This guide walks you through the entire booking process step by step.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent platform and is not affiliated with UIDAI or any government body.

## Why Book an Appointment?

| Walk-In | Online Appointment |
|---|---|
| Wait time: 1–3 hours | Guaranteed time slot |
| Risk of centre being full | Slot reserved for you |
| No confirmation | Booking receipt with reference number |
| Available at all centres | Available at Aadhaar Seva Kendras and select centres |

**Bottom line:** If an appointment slot is available, always book it.

## Step-by-Step: Book Aadhaar Appointment Online

### Step 1: Visit the Appointment Portal

Go to [appointments.uidai.gov.in](https://appointments.uidai.gov.in/)

### Step 2: Select Your City

- Choose your **State** and **City** from the dropdown
- The portal shows available Aadhaar Seva Kendras and enrollment centres

### Step 3: Choose a Centre

- Review the list of centres with addresses
- Select the most convenient centre near you

### Step 4: Pick a Date and Time Slot

- Available dates are shown on a calendar (typically 1–5 days ahead)
- Time slots are usually in 30-minute windows (e.g., 10:00 AM, 10:30 AM)
- Grey/unavailable slots are already booked — choose a green/available slot

### Step 5: Enter Your Details

- Enter your **mobile number** (required for OTP verification)
- You'll receive a **4-digit OTP** via SMS
- Enter the OTP to verify

### Step 6: Confirm and Save

- Review your booking details: centre, date, time
- Click **Confirm**
- **Save or screenshot your appointment slip** — it contains your booking reference number

### Step 7: Visit the Centre

- Arrive **15 minutes before** your slot time
- Show your appointment slip (printed or on phone) at the counter
- Carry all required documents (see below)

## What to Carry to Your Appointment

### For New Aadhaar Enrollment
- **Proof of Identity:** PAN card, passport, voter ID, driving licence
- **Proof of Address:** Passport, bank passbook, utility bill, voter ID, ration card
- **Proof of Date of Birth:** Birth certificate, SSLC marksheet, passport
- **For children under 5:** Parent's Aadhaar card + child's birth certificate

### For Aadhaar Update
- Your **Aadhaar number** or **Enrollment ID**
- **Supporting document** for the field being updated
- For biometric update: just carry your Aadhaar number — no documents needed

### General Tips
- Carry **originals + one photocopy** of each document
- Remove contact lenses before iris scan (if applicable)
- Ensure fingers are clean and dry for fingerprint capture

## How to Check Slot Availability

Slots open on a rolling basis (usually 3–5 days in advance). Here's how to find available slots:

1. Visit [appointments.uidai.gov.in](https://appointments.uidai.gov.in/)
2. Select your city — the calendar shows availability
3. **Morning slots (9:30–11:00 AM) fill up fastest** — book early
4. Check back daily if no slots are available — new slots open each morning
5. Smaller cities and newer centres tend to have better availability

## How to Reschedule or Cancel

- Currently, the UIDAI portal **does not offer direct rescheduling**
- If you miss your appointment, simply book a new one
- Missed appointments have **no penalty** — you can rebook immediately
- Walk-in is always available as a backup if you can't get an online slot

## Fees

| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| New enrollment | **Free** |
| Appointment booking | **Free** |
| Demographic update (name, address, DOB) | **₹50** |
| Biometric update (photo, fingerprints, iris) | **₹100** |
| Mandatory biometric update (at age 5 & 15) | **Free** |

*Appointment booking itself is always free. Never pay anyone for "booking help."*

## Important Tips

1. **Book 2–3 days in advance** — same-day slots are rarely available at busy centres
2. **Early morning slots are best** — less crowding and centres run on time
3. **Keep your phone handy** — you'll need OTP during booking and possibly at the centre
4. **Don't rely only on appointment** — if urgent, walk-in early morning at an Aadhaar Seva Kendra
5. **Save booking reference** — you may need it if there's a dispute about your slot

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Aadhaar appointment booking really free?
Yes, 100% free. UIDAI does not charge for appointment booking. If any website or agent asks for payment to book a slot, it is a scam.

### Can I book an appointment for someone else?
Yes, you can book using anyone's mobile number. The person just needs to visit the centre at the scheduled time with their documents.

### What if I miss my appointment?
Nothing happens — there's no penalty. You can either book a new appointment or visit the same centre as a walk-in.

### How far in advance can I book?
Typically 3–5 days in advance. Slots open daily. If your preferred centre shows no availability, check back the next morning.

### Do all Aadhaar centres accept online appointments?
No, online appointment booking is primarily available at **Aadhaar Seva Kendras** and select larger centres. Smaller or temporary centres may be walk-in only.

### Can I book an appointment through the mAadhaar app?
The mAadhaar app currently does not support appointment booking. Use [appointments.uidai.gov.in](https://appointments.uidai.gov.in/) on any web browser. For other mAadhaar services, see our [mAadhaar App guide](/guide/aadhaar-maadhaar-app-guide).

### What happens if the centre is closed on my appointment day?
In rare cases, centres may close unexpectedly. There's no automatic notification — check the UIDAI portal or call the centre before visiting. You can rebook for another date at no cost.
