# Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana Bihar: Complete Guide to Apply Online

Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana (MKUY) is a flagship scheme of the Bihar government aimed at empowering the girl child through financial assistance from birth to graduation. Under this scheme, eligible girls can receive up to **₹50,000** in multiple installments at different stages of their education and life. The scheme benefits approximately 1.5 crore girls across Bihar.

## What is Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana?

Launched by the Bihar government, this scheme provides financial support to every girl child born in the state. The assistance is given at various milestones — birth, immunisation completion, school uniform purchase, intermediate (12th) pass, and graduation. The aim is to reduce gender disparity, improve female literacy, and discourage child marriage.

### Benefit Breakdown

| Stage | Amount |
|-------|--------|
| At Birth | ₹2,000 |
| After 1 year (Aadhaar generation) | ₹1,000 |
| After 2 years (Immunisation complete) | ₹2,000 |
| Class 1-2 (Uniform) | ₹600 |
| Class 3-5 (Uniform) | ₹700 |
| Class 6-8 (Uniform) | ₹1,000 |
| Class 9-12 (Uniform) | ₹1,500 |
| After passing Inter (12th) | ₹25,000 |
| After Graduation | ₹50,000 |

> **Note:** The ₹50,000 for graduation is available only for unmarried girls up to their first degree.

## Who is Eligible?

- The girl child must be a **permanent resident of Bihar**
- Family annual income should be **less than ₹2 lakh** (for graduation component)
- Only **two daughters** per family are eligible
- The girl must be **unmarried** (for 12th pass and graduation benefits)
- Must be studying in a **recognised school/college** in Bihar
- For graduation benefit, the girl must have passed graduation from a recognised university in Bihar

## Documents Required

### For Birth-Related Benefits
- Birth certificate of the girl child
- Aadhaar card of parent/guardian
- Bank account details (mother's or girl's)
- Immunisation card

### For Education-Related Benefits (12th Pass / Graduation)
- Aadhaar card of the applicant
- Marksheet of 12th / Graduation
- Passport-size photograph
- Bank account details (in the girl's name)
- Income certificate of parents
- Residential certificate of Bihar
- Mobile number linked with Aadhaar

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Online Application (for 12th Pass / Graduation)

1. Visit the official portal at **[medhasoft.bihar.gov.in](https://medhasoft.bihar.gov.in/)** or **[ekalyan.bih.nic.in](https://ekalyan.bih.nic.in/)**
2. Click on **"Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana"** link
3. Select the appropriate category (12th Pass or Graduation)
4. Click on **"Click here to Apply"**
5. Enter your **Registration Number** and **Date of Birth** or total marks
6. Fill in the application form with personal details, address, and bank information
7. Upload required documents — photo, Aadhaar, marksheet
8. Verify all details and submit the application
9. Note down the **Application ID** for future reference
10. Check status on the same portal using your Application ID

### For Birth and Immunisation Benefits

These benefits are processed through **ICDS (Integrated Child Development Services)** at the Anganwadi centre level. Parents should:

1. Register the girl child at the nearest **Anganwadi centre**
2. Submit birth certificate and Aadhaar details
3. Ensure timely immunisation as per schedule
4. The amount is credited directly to the mother's/guardian's bank account

## Fees

There are **no fees** for applying under this scheme. The application is completely free.

## Processing Time

- **12th Pass / Graduation benefits:** 2-4 months after application submission
- **Birth/Immunisation benefits:** Processed through Anganwadi centres, usually within 1-3 months
- Amount is transferred directly to the bank account via **DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer)**

## Important Tips

1. **Ensure Aadhaar is linked** to your bank account for seamless DBT transfer
2. **Apply within the deadline** — the government announces specific dates for each batch. Check the official portal regularly
3. **Only one application** can be submitted per beneficiary. Duplicate applications are rejected
4. **Keep your mobile number updated** as OTP verification is required during application
5. If your application is rejected, check the reason on the portal and re-apply with corrected documents

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Can married girls apply for the graduation benefit?
No, the graduation benefit of ₹50,000 is available only to unmarried girls. However, the earlier benefits (birth, immunisation, uniform) are available to all girls.

### Q2: What if I passed 12th from Bihar but graduated from another state?
The graduation benefit is available only if you completed graduation from a **recognised university within Bihar**.

### Q3: Is there an income limit for the 12th pass benefit?
For the 12th pass benefit of ₹25,000, there is no strict income criterion. However, for the graduation component, the family income should be below ₹2 lakh per year.

### Q4: Can I apply offline?
For 12th pass and graduation benefits, the application is **online only** through the official portal. Birth and immunisation benefits are processed through Anganwadi centres.

### Q5: How do I check my application status?
Visit [medhasoft.bihar.gov.in](https://medhasoft.bihar.gov.in/) or [ekalyan.bih.nic.in](https://ekalyan.bih.nic.in/), click on "Application Status," and enter your Registration Number or Application ID.

### Q6: What if the money is not credited to my account?
Ensure your Aadhaar is linked to your bank account and the bank details entered during application are correct. Contact the district education officer or helpline for assistance.

### Q7: Is this scheme available for girls from all castes and religions?
Yes, Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana is available for **all girls in Bihar** regardless of caste, religion, or community, subject to the eligibility criteria.
