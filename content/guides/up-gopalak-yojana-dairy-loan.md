## What is UP Gopalak Yojana?

**Mukhyamantri Gopalak Yojana** is an Uttar Pradesh government scheme to promote **dairy farming** as a livelihood for unemployed youth. The scheme provides bank loans for purchasing **5 to 20 milch animals** (cows or buffaloes) with a **subsidised bank loan + government subsidy**.

The scheme is implemented through banks with the **Animal Husbandry Department, UP**.

---

## Key Benefits

| Scale | Loan Amount | Bank Loan | Govt Subsidy |
|-------|------------|-----------|-------------|
| 5 animals | ₹1.80 lakh | 100% (bank) | ₹10,000 per animal (max ₹40K total) |
| 10 animals | ₹3.60 lakh | 100% (bank) | ₹10,000 per animal |
| 15 animals | ₹5.40 lakh | 100% (bank) | ₹10,000 per animal |
| 20 animals | ₹9.00 lakh | 100% (bank) | ₹10,000 per animal |

Subsidy of **₹40,000** (for 5 animals — 4 animals × ₹10,000) is given in the second year after successful first year operation.

---

## Who is Eligible?

**You ARE eligible if:**
- You are an **unemployed youth** — age 18–40 years (approx.)
- You are a **permanent resident of Uttar Pradesh**
- You have **land available** (own/leased) for keeping the animals and constructing a shed
- Your family income is within limits (check current criteria with DPO)

> SC/ST and BPL candidates get additional priority

---

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar Card** — identity
2. **Land records** — proof of land for animal shed
3. **Caste certificate** — SC/ST priority
4. **Bank account details** — Aadhaar-linked
5. **Photograph**
6. **Application form** — from District Animal Husbandry Officer

---

## How to Apply — Step by Step

### Step 1 — Contact District Animal Husbandry Officer

Visit the **District Animal Husbandry Officer (DAHO)** in your district. Ask for the Gopalak Yojana application form.

### Step 2 — Submit Application

Fill the form with:
- Personal details and land information
- Number of animals to be purchased (5/10/15/20)
- Bank preference

Submit to DAHO office.

### Step 3 — DAHO Verification

The officer verifies your land, documents, and applicant background.

### Step 4 — Bank Loan Processing

DAHO forwards your application to the selected bank. Bank:
- Appraises the dairy project
- Sanctions the loan
- Disburses to purchase animals

### Step 5 — Purchase Animals

Buy high-yield milch animals (specified in the scheme — should be from authorized breeding farms or markets).

### Step 6 — Subsidy Release (Year 2)

After successfully operating the dairy for the first year:
- DAHO verifies the animals are alive and the dairy is functional
- Subsidy (₹40,000 for 5-animal unit) is released to your bank account

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. Can I choose any breed of cow or buffalo?**

The scheme recommends **high-milk-yielding breeds** — Sahiwal, Gir, Murrah buffalo, etc. The DAHO will guide you on approved breeds for the scheme.

**2. I have 10 animals already. Can I get a loan to expand?**

The scheme is primarily for new units. Expansion may be possible — check with the DAHO. New applicants with no existing dairy get priority.

**3. What is the repayment period for the loan?**

Typically 5 years (including 1-year moratorium). Confirm with the lending bank as terms vary.

**4. I don't have land for a shed. Can I still apply?**

You need at least a small plot to construct the animal shed. Leased land may be accepted. Without land, the project is not feasible — this is a key requirement.

**5. Is there an insurance for animals under this scheme?**

Animal insurance is available separately under the **Rashtriya Pashu Bima Yojana** or state insurance schemes. The Gopalak Yojana itself does not include animal insurance, but you should insure your animals after purchase.
