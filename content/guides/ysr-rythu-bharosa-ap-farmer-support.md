## What is YSR Rythu Bharosa?

**YSR Rythu Bharosa** is the Andhra Pradesh government scheme providing **annual income support** to farmers and agricultural labourers. Launched in 2019, it delivers direct cash transfers to farmers without any middlemen.

The scheme integrates state funds with central PM-Kisan for a **combined annual support of ₹13,500 per farmer household.**

## Annual Benefits Breakdown

| Component | Amount | Source |
|---|---|---|
| AP state investment support | ₹5,500/year | Andhra Pradesh government |
| PM-Kisan Samman Nidhi | ₹6,000/year | Central government |
| Input cost subsidy (Zaid season) | ₹2,000/year | AP state |
| **Total** | **₹13,500/year** | Combined |

**Who gets the ₹13,500:**
- Farmers (landholders) get the investment support
- Agricultural labourers (tenant farmers) may get some components

**Payment schedule:** Paid in 3–4 installments throughout the year.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Land-owning farmers** in Andhra Pradesh (own agricultural land)
- **Tenant/lease farmers** (those who cultivate others' land) — also eligible for state component
- **Agricultural labourers** (landless) — get a separate component under YSR Rythu Bharosa (₹5,500 specifically designated for them)
- Must be a **registered farmer** in the AP Agriculture Department database

**Not eligible:**
- Government employees (central/state)
- Income tax payers
- Farmers owning more than a specified land threshold (institutional farmers/large corporations)

## How to Apply

YSR Rythu Bharosa enrollment happens primarily through:

1. **Village Secretariat** — the YSRCP government set up village secretariats for every 2,000 households. Farmers register at their village secretariat.
2. **Gram Sachivalayam officers** (Agriculture, Revenue, Welfare officers) visit door-to-door for enrollment
3. **Online:** Check [rythubharosa.ap.gov.in](https://rythubharosa.ap.gov.in) for current enrollment portal

For **new farmers** (not yet enrolled):
1. Go to your **Village Secretariat** (Gram Sachivalayam)
2. Submit land documents + Aadhaar + bank account
3. Registration officer enrolls you in the system
4. PM-Kisan registration is done simultaneously

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card**
2. **Land documents** — Pattadar Passbook (land records), Adangal (crop details)
3. **Bank account** — Aadhaar-linked (ideally with the bank where Pattadar Passbook is maintained)
4. **Mobile number** (registered with Aadhaar)
5. **Caste certificate** (for SC/ST farmers for relevant schemes)
6. Passport-size photograph

## Checking Payment Status

1. Go to [rythubharosa.ap.gov.in](https://rythubharosa.ap.gov.in)
2. Enter Aadhaar number or farmer ID
3. View payment history — all installments

OR check your bank account for DBT credits.

## YSR Rythu Bharosa for Agricultural Labourers

**Agricultural labourers** (landless workers) who depend on farm work are given **₹5,500/year** separately. Enrollment is also through the Village Secretariat — bring Aadhaar and job card (MGNREGS card as supporting document).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I own 0.5 acres. Am I still eligible for the full ₹13,500?
Yes — even small landholders (including those with fractional acre holdings) are eligible for the full support amount. The amount is per household, not per acre.

### My land is registered in my father's name but I cultivate it. Who gets the money?
The support goes to the **Pattadar (registered landowner)** — your father. However, if you can prove tenant/lease cultivation, the state component for tenant farmers may be available to you. Register as a tenant farmer at the village secretariat.

### I already get PM-Kisan. Will I now get an extra ₹13,500 or only ₹7,500 (state part)?
You get both — PM-Kisan gives ₹6,000/year (from central government) and Rythu Bharosa state component gives ₹7,500 (₹5,500 + ₹2,000). Total = ₹13,500/year if you're enrolled in both.

### I moved to another state but my land is in AP. Am I still eligible?
Rythu Bharosa is for actively cultivating farmers. If you live outside AP but still farm your AP land (or have a tenant farming it), eligibility may vary. Consult the village secretariat for your land.

### Are schemes under YSRCP government continuing under TDP (new government)?
Check current notifications — state scheme names and implementation may change between governments. The underlying PM-Kisan central scheme continues regardless of state government.
