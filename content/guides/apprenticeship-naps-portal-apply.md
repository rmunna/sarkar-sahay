# How to Apply for Apprenticeship in India — NAPS Portal Registration

Apprenticeship training is one of the most practical ways to gain industry-ready skills while earning a stipend. The Government of India, through the **Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship (MSDE)**, runs a structured apprenticeship framework backed by the **Apprentices Act, 1961**. The **National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme (NAPS)** further incentivises employers to hire apprentices by sharing stipend costs.

This guide covers everything — types of apprenticeships, how to register on the official portal, stipend details, certification, and benefits.

---

## What Is Apprenticeship Under the Apprentices Act?

An apprenticeship is a **dual-mode training programme** where a candidate learns on the job inside an establishment while also receiving related theoretical instruction. It is governed by the **Apprentices Act, 1961** (amended in 2014) and regulated by the Directorate General of Training (DGT) under MSDE.

Key features:

- **On-the-job training** in real workplaces (factories, offices, workshops)
- **Stipend paid by the employer** (with partial reimbursement under NAPS)
- **Certificate recognised nationally** by NCVT or RDAT
- **No bond or employment guarantee** — but high employability after completion

---

## Types of Apprenticeships in India

| Type | Eligibility | Duration |
|------|------------|----------|
| **Trade Apprentice** | Class 8th / 10th pass or ITI certificate holder | 6 months – 3 years |
| **Graduate Apprentice** | Degree in Engineering / Technology | 1 year |
| **Technician Apprentice** | Diploma in Engineering / Technology | 1 year |
| **Technician (Vocational) Apprentice** | Vocational certificate after 10+2 | 1 year |
| **Optional Trade Apprentice** | Class 5th–12th pass (varies by trade) | 6 months – 1 year |

> **Optional Trade Apprentices** are the most flexible category — employers can design their own training modules and hire candidates with minimal educational qualifications.

## What Is NAPS (National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme)?

NAPS is a **Central Government scheme** launched to promote apprenticeship training across India. It provides financial support to employers who engage apprentices.

### NAPS Benefits for Employers

- **25% of prescribed stipend** reimbursed by the government (up to ₹1,500 per month per apprentice)
- **₹7,500 one-time assistance** for fresh candidates requiring basic training (shared cost of basic training)
- Simplified online compliance through the apprenticeship portal

### NAPS Benefits for Candidates

- Guaranteed monthly stipend during training
- Nationally recognised certificate on completion
- Practical industry exposure and skill development
- No fees — apprenticeship training is free for the candidate

## How to Register as a Candidate on the Apprenticeship Portal

The official portal is **[apprenticeshipindia.gov.in](https://www.apprenticeshipindia.gov.in)**. Follow these steps:

### Step 1: Visit the Portal

Go to [https://www.apprenticeshipindia.gov.in](https://www.apprenticeshipindia.gov.in) and click on **"Candidate"** under the registration section.

### Step 2: Enter Basic Details

- Full name, date of birth, gender
- Mobile number and email ID
- Aadhaar number (mandatory for identity verification)

### Step 3: Choose Education and Trade

- Select your highest qualification (5th, 8th, 10th, 12th, ITI, Diploma, Degree)
- If you are an **ITI pass-out**, enter your ITI details and trade name
- Choose your **preferred apprenticeship trade** and location

### Step 4: Upload Documents

- Passport-size photograph
- Educational certificates (marksheets)
- Aadhaar card
- ITI certificate (if applicable)
- Caste certificate (if applying under SC/ST/OBC category)

> 💡 Keep scanned copies ready in **JPG/PDF format** (under 500 KB each). You can use [DigiLocker](/guide/digilocker-guide) to access verified digital documents.

### Step 5: Submit and Get Registration Number

After submission, you receive a **unique registration number**. Use this to:

- Search and apply for apprenticeship openings
- Track your application status
- Download your offer letter once selected

### Step 6: Apply to Establishments

Browse available apprenticeship vacancies on the portal, filter by trade/location/sector, and **apply directly**. Employers will review your profile and issue a **contract of apprenticeship** if selected.

## How Employers Register on the NAPS Portal

Employers (establishments) must also register to engage apprentices under NAPS:

1. **Visit** [apprenticeshipindia.gov.in](https://www.apprenticeshipindia.gov.in) → click **"Establishment"**
2. **Enter establishment details** — name, address, type, sector, GSTIN/PAN
3. **Specify apprentice seats** — number of apprentices the establishment can train (based on workforce size)
4. **Upload documents** — registration certificate, PAN, bank details for NAPS reimbursement
5. **Get approval** from the Regional Directorate of Apprenticeship Training (RDAT) or State Apprenticeship Adviser (SAA)
6. **Post vacancies** and select candidates from the portal

> Establishments with **30 or more workers** (including contract workers) are mandated to engage apprentices — between **2.5% to 15%** of their total workforce.

## Stipend Structure for Apprentices

The government prescribes **minimum stipend** rates. Employers may pay more, but cannot pay less:

| Apprentice Category | Minimum Monthly Stipend |
|---------------------|------------------------|
| School pass-outs (5th–9th) | ₹5,000 |
| School pass-outs (10th) | ₹6,000 |
| School pass-outs (12th) | ₹7,000 |
| ITI pass-outs | ₹7,000 – ₹8,000 |
| Technician (Diploma) Apprentice | ₹8,000 |
| Graduate Apprentice | ₹9,000 |

> Stipend rates are revised periodically by the government. Under NAPS, the government reimburses **25% of the stipend (max ₹1,500/month)** directly to the employer.

## Duration of Apprenticeship Training

- **Trade Apprentices:** 6 months to 3 years (depends on the designated trade)
- **Graduate / Technician Apprentices:** 1 year
- **Optional Trade Apprentices:** 6 months to 1 year (employer-defined)

The training period includes both **practical training** at the workplace and **related instruction** (classroom or online modules).

---

## Certification — NCVT and RDAT

On successful completion of apprenticeship training:

- **Trade Apprentices** receive a **National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC)** issued by the **National Council for Vocational Training (NCVT)** after passing the All India Trade Test (AITT)
- **Graduate and Technician Apprentices** receive a **Proficiency Certificate** issued by the respective **RDAT/SAA**
- **Optional Trade Apprentices** receive a certificate from the **employer and DGT**

These certificates are **recognised across India** for government jobs, PSU recruitment, and further skill development programmes. Many [government job applications](/guide/government-jobs-apply-online) accept apprenticeship certificates as valid experience.

## FAQ — Frequently Asked Questions

### Is there any fee to register for apprenticeship on the NAPS portal?

No. Registration on apprenticeshipindia.gov.in is completely **free** for candidates. You do not need to pay any fee at any stage of the apprenticeship process.

### Can I do an apprenticeship after completing ITI?

Yes. ITI pass-outs are highly preferred for **Trade Apprenticeship** positions. Your ITI trade and marks are considered during selection. Many government and private establishments specifically seek ITI-certified candidates.

### What is the difference between NAPS and NATS?

**NAPS** (National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme) covers trade and optional trade apprentices and is managed by DGT under MSDE. **NATS** (National Apprenticeship Training Scheme) covers graduate, technician, and technician (vocational) apprentices and is managed by BOAT (Board of Apprenticeship Training) under the Ministry of Education. Both use the same portal for registration.

### Will I get a job after completing apprenticeship?

There is **no legal obligation** on the employer to provide permanent employment after apprenticeship. However, many employers prefer to absorb trained apprentices. The NAC certificate and practical experience significantly boost your employability for both private and [government jobs](/guide/government-jobs-apply-online).

### Can I apply for apprenticeship in a different state?

Yes. The apprenticeship portal allows you to search and apply for vacancies **across India**. You can filter by state, district, sector, and trade to find opportunities anywhere in the country.

### What documents are needed for apprenticeship registration?

You need Aadhaar card, educational certificates (marksheets), passport-size photo, and category certificate (if applicable). ITI pass-outs should keep their ITI certificate ready. Use [DigiLocker](/guide/digilocker-guide) to access verified digital copies of your documents.

### Is there an age limit for apprenticeship?

The minimum age is **14 years** (for trades not involving hazardous work) and **18 years** for trades involving hazardous processes. There is **no upper age limit** prescribed under the Apprentices Act, though individual establishments may set their own criteria.
