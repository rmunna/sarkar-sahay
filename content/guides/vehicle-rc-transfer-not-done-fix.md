# Vehicle RC Transfer Not Done — How to Fix

Bought a used car or bike but the Registration Certificate (RC) is still in the previous owner's name? An incomplete RC transfer can lead to challan issues, insurance claim rejections, and legal trouble. This guide covers every reason your RC transfer may be stuck and exactly how to fix it.

## Why RC Transfer Is Mandatory

Under **Section 50 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988**, when a vehicle changes ownership, both the buyer and seller must apply for transfer of registration within **14 days** of the sale. Failure to do so attracts penalties and can create legal complications for both parties.

## Documents Required for RC Transfer

Before troubleshooting, make sure you have all the required documents:

- **Form 29** (Notice of transfer of ownership) — signed by both buyer and seller
- **Form 30** (Report of transfer of ownership) — signed by both buyer and seller
- **Original RC** (Registration Certificate) of the vehicle
- **Valid insurance** in the buyer's name
- **Address proof** of the buyer (Aadhaar, passport, utility bill)
- **PUC (Pollution Under Control) certificate**
- **Sale deed / sale receipt** between buyer and seller
- **ID proof** of buyer and seller
- **NOC from financier** (if the vehicle had a loan / hypothecation)
- **NOC from the registering RTO** (if transferring to a different state)

## Common RC Transfer Problems and How to Fix Them

### 1. Seller Not Cooperating for Form 29/30

The biggest hurdle — the previous owner is unreachable or refuses to sign Form 29 and Form 30.

**How to fix:**
- Send a **legal notice** via registered post to the seller demanding cooperation
- File a complaint at the **local police station** with a copy of the sale receipt
- Approach the **RTO directly** with proof of purchase (sale deed, payment receipts, bank transaction records) — many RTOs accept unilateral transfer applications with sufficient proof
- As a last resort, file a **civil suit** for specific performance

> **Tip:** Always get Form 29 and Form 30 signed at the time of purchase, ideally attested by a notary.

### 2. Parivahan / Vahan Portal Errors

Common errors on the Vahan portal when applying for RC transfer online:

| Error | Cause | Fix |
|-------|-------|-----|
| "Vehicle data not found" | Vehicle not digitized by RTO | Visit RTO in person to get records digitized first |
| "Chassis/Engine number mismatch" | Data entry error in RTO records | File a correction application at the RTO with original documents |
| OTP not received | Server load / wrong mobile number | Try during off-peak hours (before 10 AM); verify mobile number linked to Aadhaar |
| Payment failed but amount deducted | Gateway timeout | Wait 48 hours for auto-refund; if not, raise a grievance on parivahan.gov.in |
| "Application already exists" | Duplicate submission | Check status of existing application; don't create a new one |
| "Hypothecation not cleared" | Loan termination not updated | Get HP termination endorsement from RTO first (see Section 5 below) |

**General portal tips:**
- Use **Chrome or Edge** browser — other browsers may have compatibility issues
- Clear cache before starting
- Keep scanned documents under **200 KB each** (JPEG/PDF)
- Apply during **weekday mornings** for better server response

### 3. NOC from Previous RTO Not Received

When transferring a vehicle registered in a different state, you need a **No Objection Certificate (NOC)** from the original RTO. This is often the most delayed step.

**How to fix:**
- **Apply online** via Parivahan portal → Services → NOC/Transfer to another state
- If applied offline, visit the **original RTO** with a copy of your application and follow up
- NOC is typically issued within **7–15 working days**, but delays of 1–3 months are common
- File an **RTI application** to the RTO asking for the status and reason for delay
- Escalate to the **Regional Transport Commissioner** if delayed beyond 30 days
- Some states now issue **electronic NOC** that is sent directly to the destination RTO — check if applicable

**Important:** NOC is valid for **6 months** from the date of issue. Complete your re-registration at the new RTO before it expires.

### 4. Form 29 / Form 30 Rejection by RTO

Your transfer application may be rejected by the RTO for various reasons:

**Common rejection reasons:**
- **Incomplete or incorrect forms** — name, address, or vehicle details don't match RC
- **Missing signatures** — both buyer and seller must sign
- **Insurance not transferred** — insurance must be in buyer's name or at least endorsed
- **Pending challans/e-challans** — outstanding traffic violations on the vehicle
- **Tax arrears** — unpaid road tax or green tax
- **Fitness certificate expired** — for commercial vehicles

**How to fix:**
- Check the **rejection reason** on the Vahan portal or at the RTO counter
- Correct the specific issue and **re-apply** — you don't need to pay fees again in most states if resubmitting within 30 days
- Clear all **pending challans** on [echallan.parivahan.gov.in](https://echallan.parivahan.gov.in)
- Pay any **road tax arrears** at the RTO
- Get **insurance transferred** to your name — contact your insurance company with the sale deed

### 5. Hypothecation (HP) Not Removed

If the previous owner had a vehicle loan, the RC will show a **hypothecation endorsement** (HP marked to the bank/financier). RC transfer cannot proceed until this is cleared.

**How to fix:**
1. Get a **loan closure letter / NOC** from the bank or financier
2. Obtain **Form 35** (notice of HP termination) signed by the financier
3. Submit Form 35 + NOC + original RC at the RTO
4. RTO will endorse **"HP Terminated"** on the RC — this takes 3–7 working days
5. Now proceed with the regular RC transfer process

**If the bank is not cooperating:**
- Send a written request to the bank's **nodal officer**
- Escalate to the **Banking Ombudsman** (RBI)
- File a complaint on the **RBI CMS portal** (cms.rbi.org.in)

> **Note:** Some states now allow HP termination and RC transfer to be processed simultaneously. Check with your local RTO.

### 6. Inter-State Transfer Issues

Transferring a vehicle from one state to another involves additional steps and common delays:

**Process:**
1. Apply for **NOC** at the original (home) RTO
2. Once NOC is received, apply for **re-registration** at the new state's RTO within the NOC validity period
3. Pay **road tax** in the new state (some states offer adjustment for tax already paid)
4. The new RTO issues a **new registration number** for the vehicle

**Common issues and fixes:**
- **Road tax double-payment:** Some states (Delhi, Maharashtra, Karnataka) have bilateral agreements — carry the original tax receipt from the old state
- **BH Series vehicles:** If your vehicle has a **Bharat (BH) series** registration, you do NOT need an NOC or re-registration — it's valid across India
- **Different fee structures:** Each state has its own transfer and re-registration fees — check the specific state transport department website
- **Delay in old RTO sending records:** Follow up with old RTO; file RTI if necessary

### 7. Penalty for Delayed RC Transfer

If you delay the RC transfer beyond the **14-day window**, penalties apply:

| Delay Period | Typical Penalty |
|-------------|----------------|
| 15–30 days | ₹500–₹1,000 |
| 1–3 months | ₹1,000–₹2,500 |
| 3–6 months | ₹2,500–₹5,000 |
| 6–12 months | ₹5,000–₹10,000 |
| Over 1 year | ₹10,000+ (varies by state) |

> Penalty amounts vary significantly by state. Some states charge a flat late fee while others calculate it as a percentage of the vehicle's value.

**How to minimize penalties:**
- Apply as soon as possible — the longer you wait, the higher the penalty
- Some RTOs offer **amnesty schemes** periodically — check your state transport department website
- If the delay was due to the **seller's non-cooperation**, document this (legal notice, police complaint) to potentially reduce penalty

## How to Track RC Transfer Status

### Online (Vahan Portal)
1. Visit [vahan.parivahan.gov.in](https://vahan.parivahan.gov.in)
2. Go to **"Know Your Vehicle Details"** or **"RC Status"**
3. Enter your **vehicle registration number**
4. Check if ownership name has been updated

### Via mParivahan App
1. Download the **mParivahan** app (available on Android and iOS)
2. Enter the vehicle registration number
3. The app shows current owner name, RC status, and insurance details

### Via SMS
- Send an SMS: **VAHAN <space> Vehicle Number** to **7738299899**
- You'll receive basic RC details including owner name

### At RTO
- Visit the RTO with your application receipt number and check status at the counter

## Step-by-Step: Complete RC Transfer Process

### For Same-State Transfer (Online)
1. Visit [parivahan.gov.in](https://parivahan.gov.in) → Vahan Services
2. Select your **state and RTO**
3. Choose **"Transfer of Ownership"**
4. Enter vehicle registration number
5. Upload scanned copies of Form 29, Form 30, insurance, ID proof, address proof
6. Pay the **transfer fee** online (₹300–₹500 depending on state)
7. Book an **appointment** at the RTO (if required)
8. Visit RTO with **original documents** for verification
9. RC with updated ownership is dispatched within **7–15 working days**

### For Same-State Transfer (Offline)
1. Collect Form 29 and Form 30 from the RTO or download from parivahan.gov.in
2. Fill and get **both buyer and seller** to sign
3. Visit the RTO with all original documents
4. Pay the transfer fee at the RTO counter
5. Submit application and collect the **acknowledgment receipt**
6. Collect the updated RC or receive it by post

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I drive the vehicle without RC transfer?**
A: Technically, you need the RC in your name. Driving with RC in someone else's name can result in fines and complications during insurance claims. Carry the sale deed as interim proof of ownership.

**Q: What if the previous owner has passed away?**
A: You'll need the **death certificate**, **legal heir certificate**, and a **succession certificate** or **probate of will**. The legal heir must sign Form 29/30 on behalf of the deceased.

**Q: Can I transfer RC for a vehicle with expired fitness/PUC?**
A: No. You must first renew the fitness certificate (for commercial vehicles) and get a valid PUC before applying for transfer.

**Q: Is RC transfer required for gifted vehicles?**
A: Yes. The process is the same, but instead of a sale deed, you submit a **gift deed** (ideally notarized).

**Q: How much does RC transfer cost?**
A: The transfer fee is typically ₹300–₹500 plus any applicable road tax (for inter-state transfers). Late fees are additional.
