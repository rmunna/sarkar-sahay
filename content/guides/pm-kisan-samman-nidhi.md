## What is PM Kisan Samman Nidhi?

PM Kisan Samman Nidhi Yojana is a Central Government scheme that provides **₹6,000 per year** directly to the bank accounts of small and marginal farmer families across India. The amount is paid in **3 installments of ₹2,000 each**.

## Key Benefits

- **₹6,000 per year** (₹2,000 × 3 installments)
- Directly credited to bank account (DBT)
- No middlemen — money comes from Central Government
- Both husband and wife can register if they own land separately

## Payment Schedule

| Installment | Period | Payment Month |
|-------------|--------|---------------|
| 1st | April - July | April onwards |
| 2nd | August - November | August onwards |
| 3rd | December - March | December onwards |

## Who is Eligible?

**Eligible:**
- All farmer families with cultivable landholding
- Both small and marginal farmers
- Land must be in the farmer's name (or spouse's name in some states)

**NOT Eligible:**
- Institutional landholders
- Farmer families where one or more members are:
  - Former/present holders of constitutional posts
  - Former/present Ministers, MPs, MLAs, Mayors
  - Government/PSU employees (except multi-tasking staff/Group D)
  - Income tax payers (in last assessment year)
  - Professionals (doctors, engineers, lawyers, CAs) with professional bodies
  - Pensioners receiving ₹10,000+/month

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar Card** (mandatory)
- **Land ownership documents** (Khasra/Khatauni/land records)
- **Bank account details** (account number, IFSC)
- **Mobile number** linked to Aadhaar

## How to Register — Online

### Step 1: Visit PM Kisan Portal
Go to [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in)

### Step 2: Click "New Farmer Registration"
On the homepage, click **"New Farmer Registration"**

### Step 3: Enter Aadhaar Number
- Enter your Aadhaar number
- Select your state
- Enter captcha
- Verify via OTP on Aadhaar-linked mobile

### Step 4: Fill Registration Form
- Name (as per Aadhaar)
- Address and village details
- **Land details** (survey number, area in hectares)
- Bank account number and IFSC code
- Mobile number

### Step 5: Upload Documents
- Land ownership proof
- Bank passbook first page

### Step 6: Submit
Note the registration number. Your application goes for verification.

### Step 7: Verification
- Local revenue officer/patwari verifies land records
- State government approves the application
- Once approved, payments start from the next installment cycle

## How to Register — Offline

1. Visit nearest **Common Service Center (CSC)**
2. Or contact your local **Patwari / Lekhpal / Agriculture Officer**
3. They will assist with registration
4. You can also register through **PM Kisan mobile app**

## How to Check Payment Status

### Method 1: Online
1. Visit [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in)
2. Click **"Beneficiary Status"**
3. Enter Aadhaar number or account number or mobile number
4. View payment history and upcoming installment status

### Method 2: Helpline
Call **155261** or **011-24300606**

## How to Check Beneficiary List

1. Visit [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in)
2. Click **"Beneficiary List"**
3. Select State → District → Sub-District → Block → Village
4. Check if your name appears in the list

## e-KYC (Mandatory)

**e-KYC is mandatory** to continue receiving payments:
1. Visit [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in) → **"e-KYC"**
2. Enter Aadhaar number
3. Verify via OTP on Aadhaar-linked mobile
4. e-KYC done!

> Without e-KYC, your installments will be held.

## Fees

All PM Kisan services are **completely free**. No registration fee, no processing fee.

## Important Tips

1. **Complete e-KYC immediately** — Payments are held without it
2. **Keep land records updated** — Ensure your name is on the latest revenue records
3. **Link Aadhaar to bank account** — Required for DBT payment
4. **Check beneficiary status regularly** — Installments can be held for various reasons
5. **Update bank details** if you change accounts — Do it on the portal under "Updation of Self-Registered Farmer"

## FAQs

**Q: How much money do farmers get under PM Kisan?**
A: ₹6,000 per year in 3 installments of ₹2,000 each, directly to bank account.

**Q: Can I register for PM Kisan online?**
A: Yes. Visit pmkisan.gov.in → New Farmer Registration. You need Aadhaar, land documents, and bank details.

**Q: How to check PM Kisan payment status?**
A: Visit pmkisan.gov.in → Beneficiary Status → Enter Aadhaar/account number/mobile number.

**Q: Is e-KYC mandatory for PM Kisan?**
A: Yes. Without e-KYC, your installment payments will be held. Complete it on the PM Kisan portal.

**Q: Can tenant farmers apply for PM Kisan?**
A: PM Kisan is for landholding farmers. Tenant farmers (who don't own land) are generally not eligible, though some states have included them.

**Q: What if my PM Kisan payment is not received?**
A: Check if e-KYC is done, Aadhaar is linked to bank, and land records are verified. Contact helpline 155261 or your local agriculture officer.
