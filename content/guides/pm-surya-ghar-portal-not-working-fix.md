# PM Surya Ghar Portal Not Working 2026? Fix All Login & Application Errors

The PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana portal (**pmsuryaghar.gov.in**) has three separate sub-portals for different actions. Many users face errors because they use the wrong portal or face session/OTP issues. This guide fixes all common errors.

## PM Surya Ghar Portal URLs — Which One to Use

| Action | Portal URL |
|--------|-----------|
| **New registration** | [registration.pmsuryaghar.gov.in](https://registration.pmsuryaghar.gov.in/) |
| **Consumer login** (track application) | [consumer.pmsuryaghar.gov.in](https://consumer.pmsuryaghar.gov.in/) |
| **DISCOM login** (for solar vendor/DISCOM staff) | [discom.pmsuryaghar.gov.in](https://discom.pmsuryaghar.gov.in/) |
| **Main homepage** | [pmsuryaghar.gov.in](https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in/) |

**Using the wrong portal is the #1 cause of login failures.** Consumers who already registered should use `consumer.pmsuryaghar.gov.in`, not the main homepage.

## Fix 1: "Session Expired" or "Login Timeout" Error

This happens when the portal's session times out or there's a stale cookie.

1. **Open Incognito/Private mode**: Chrome → Ctrl+Shift+N (Mac: Cmd+Shift+N)
2. Go directly to [consumer.pmsuryaghar.gov.in](https://consumer.pmsuryaghar.gov.in/)
3. Login with your registered mobile number and OTP
4. **Do not leave the tab idle** — the portal times out in ~10 minutes

If the session keeps expiring, try a **different browser** (Firefox works more stably than Chrome for this portal).

## Fix 2: OTP Not Received

If the OTP for login isn't coming to your mobile:

1. **Wait 3 minutes** — OTP is delayed during peak hours (10 AM–5 PM)
2. **Check the mobile number**: the OTP goes to the number you registered with your DISCOM (electricity provider), not necessarily your Aadhaar number
3. **Check SMS spam/DND**: if your number is on DND, government transactional SMS may still work but check anyway
4. **Resend OTP**: click "Resend OTP" after 60 seconds
5. **If nothing works**: contact your electricity provider (DISCOM) to confirm which mobile is registered against your Consumer ID

## Fix 3: "Consumer ID Not Found" or "Invalid Consumer ID"

Your Consumer ID (electricity bill number) must exactly match what your DISCOM has on record:

1. Check your **electricity bill** — Consumer ID is printed prominently on the bill
2. Ensure you're selecting the **correct DISCOM** (electricity company) for your state
3. Some DISCOMs use Meter Number instead of Consumer ID — try both
4. If recently changed address or meter: update Consumer ID with your DISCOM first

## Fix 4: DISCOM Approval Pending — Application Stuck

After submitting your application on the portal, it goes to your DISCOM for approval. This is the most common delay:

- **Normal wait time**: 7–30 days for DISCOM to send a technical feasibility survey team
- **What happens**: DISCOM checks if your roof/location is suitable for solar installation
- **After DISCOM approval**: you can select a registered solar vendor and proceed with installation

**If stuck for more than 30 days:**
1. Call your DISCOM helpline and quote your Application Reference Number
2. Alternatively raise a ticket at [consumer.pmsuryaghar.gov.in](https://consumer.pmsuryaghar.gov.in/) → "Raise Complaint"
3. Central helpline: **1800-180-3333** (toll-free)

## Fix 5: Subsidy Not Credited After Installation

Subsidy is credited **after** all these steps are completed:
1. ✅ DISCOM approval
2. ✅ Solar installation by registered vendor
3. ✅ Net meter installation by DISCOM
4. ✅ Inspection and commissioning by DISCOM engineer
5. ✅ You submit final inspection certificate on the portal
6. ✅ DISCOM uploads commissioning report to portal

**Timeline after commissioning**: 30–60 days for subsidy to arrive in your bank account.

If subsidy is delayed beyond 60 days after commissioning:
- Raise a grievance at **[consumer.pmsuryaghar.gov.in](https://consumer.pmsuryaghar.gov.in/)** → Complaint section
- Contact your DISCOM's solar grievance cell
- Escalate to MNRE (Ministry of New and Renewable Energy) at **grievance.mnre.gov.in**

## Fix 6: Portal Not Loading at All

If pmsuryaghar.gov.in itself doesn't open:

1. **Check server status**: visit [downforeveryoneorjustme.com](https://downforeveryoneorjustme.com/) and search "pmsuryaghar.gov.in"
2. **Try a different network**: switch from WiFi to mobile data (or vice versa)
3. **DNS issue**: change DNS to 8.8.8.8 (Google) in your network settings
4. **Best time**: 8 AM–10 AM or after 8 PM for lowest server load

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I registered but can't log in again — what's my login ID?

Your login credentials for consumer.pmsuryaghar.gov.in are your **registered mobile number** (used as username) and an OTP each time — there's no permanent password. Use the same mobile you used during registration.

### Can I apply on the portal if my state's DISCOM is not listed?

Some smaller DISCOMs are not yet integrated. In that case, apply **offline** through your DISCOM's own solar subsidy portal (most state DISCOMs have one). Central subsidy is the same — it's just routed through the state DISCOM.

### How do I check my application status without login?

Go to [pmsuryaghar.gov.in](https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in/) → "Check Application Status" → enter your registered mobile number. Status shows: Submitted, DISCOM Approved, Vendor Selected, Installed, Net Meter Applied, Commissioned, Subsidy Released.

### The solar vendor I selected is not showing on the portal. Why?

Vendors must be registered with the national portal. If your vendor isn't showing, they may not be empanelled. Use only vendors who appear in the portal's vendor list for your district — subsidy requires installation by an empanelled vendor.
