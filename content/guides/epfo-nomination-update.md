## What is EPFO Nomination?

EPFO nomination is the process of designating a person (nominee) who will receive your **Employees' Provident Fund (EPF)** balance, **Employee Pension Scheme (EPS)** benefits, and **Employee Deposit Linked Insurance Scheme (EDLI)** benefits in the event of your death.

Updating your nomination is crucial because:
- Without a valid nomination, your family may face delays in claiming your PF money
- Outdated nominations (naming parents when you're married) can cause legal complications
- **E-nomination is now mandatory** — Physical Form 2 nominations are being phased out

## Who Can Be a Nominee?

### If You Are Married
- **Spouse** (husband/wife)
- **Children** (minor or adult)
- Both spouse and children can be co-nominees with percentage allocation
- Parents **cannot** be nominees if you are married (unless spouse and children are legally excluded)

### If You Are Unmarried
- **Father**
- **Mother**
- **Brother or sister** (if parents are not alive)
- After marriage, you must update the nomination to include your spouse

## How to Update EPFO Nomination Online (E-Nomination)

### Prerequisites
- Active **UAN (Universal Account Number)** — linked to Aadhaar
- **Aadhaar** linked to UAN and verified — see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
- **Mobile number** linked to Aadhaar for OTP
- **Bank account** linked to UAN

### Step 1: Log In to EPFO Member Portal
1. Visit [unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in)
2. Log in with your **UAN** and password
3. If you don't have a password, click "Activate UAN" to set one

### Step 2: Go to E-Nomination
1. Click on **"Online Services"** in the top menu
2. Select **"E-Nomination"**
3. You'll see the nomination page with your profile details

### Step 3: Add Family Details
1. Click **"Add Family Details"**
2. Click **"Add Row"** to add a family member
3. Fill in:
   - Name of family member
   - Relationship (Spouse, Son, Daughter, Father, Mother)
   - Date of birth
   - Gender
   - Aadhaar number (optional but recommended)
   - Address
   - Guardian details (if nominee is a minor)
4. Upload **photo of the family member** (optional)
5. Click **"Save"**

### Step 4: Add Nomination
1. After saving family details, click **"Nomination Details"**
2. Select the family member(s) as nominee(s)
3. Assign **percentage share** for each nominee (must total 100%)
4. Specify nomination for:
   - **EPF** — Provident Fund balance
   - **EPS** — Pension benefits
   - **EDLI** — Insurance benefits (₹7 lakh coverage)
5. Click **"Save EPF Nomination"**

### Step 5: E-Sign with Aadhaar
1. After saving, click **"E-Sign"**
2. Enter your **Aadhaar number**
3. An OTP is sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile
4. Enter the OTP to digitally sign the nomination
5. Nomination is submitted and confirmed

### Step 6: Verify Nomination
1. Go back to E-Nomination page
2. Check status — should show **"Nomination Submitted"**
3. Download the nomination acknowledgement for your records

## How to Change Existing Nomination

1. Log in to the EPFO member portal
2. Go to **Online Services** → **E-Nomination**
3. You'll see existing nomination details
4. Click **"Update"** or **"Modify"**
5. Add new family members or change percentages
6. Remove old nominees if needed (e.g., add spouse after marriage)
7. E-Sign with Aadhaar OTP
8. New nomination replaces the old one

## When Should You Update Nomination?

- **After marriage** — Add spouse as primary nominee
- **After birth of children** — Include children as co-nominees
- **After divorce** — Remove ex-spouse and update
- **Death of a nominee** — Update to include other family members
- **Change of employer** — Verify nomination carries over with UAN
- **Periodically** — Review every 2-3 years even if no life changes

## Nomination for Different EPFO Schemes

### EPF (Provident Fund)
- Nominee receives the full PF balance (employee + employer contribution + interest)
- Multiple nominees can share in specified percentages

### EPS (Employee Pension Scheme)
- Nominee (spouse) receives **widow/widower pension** for life
- Children receive **children pension** until age 25
- Orphan pension if both parents die

### EDLI (Employee Deposit Linked Insurance)
- Nominee receives insurance benefit up to **₹7 lakh**
- Calculated as: 30 times the average monthly wages (capped at ₹15,000) + bonus of ₹1.75 lakh
- Minimum benefit: ₹2.5 lakh

## Common Issues and Solutions

### "E-Nomination option not visible"
- Ensure Aadhaar is linked and verified with UAN
- Check if KYC is approved by employer
- Try after 24 hours if Aadhaar was recently linked

### "Aadhaar OTP not received"
- Ensure Aadhaar-linked mobile is active
- Wait a few minutes and retry
- Check if Aadhaar authentication service is down (try later)

### "Employer approval pending"
- E-nomination through the portal typically doesn't require employer approval
- If using physical Form 2, employer attestation is needed
- Contact your HR department for status

### "Previous nomination still showing"
- New e-nomination automatically supersedes the old one
- If old physical nomination shows, submit e-nomination which takes precedence

## Important Tips

1. **Complete e-nomination ASAP** — It's now mandatory and ensures your family can claim benefits smoothly
2. **Keep Aadhaar-mobile active** — You need the OTP for e-signing; don't change your Aadhaar-linked mobile without updating first
3. **Inform your nominees** — They should know about your PF account, UAN, and the claim process
4. **Update after every life event** — Marriage, birth, divorce, or death of nominee
5. **Download acknowledgement** — Save the e-nomination confirmation as proof

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is e-nomination mandatory for EPFO?
Yes, EPFO has made e-nomination mandatory. Members without e-nomination may face delays in claim settlement for their nominees. Complete it even if you have an old physical Form 2 nomination.

### Can I nominate my parents if I'm married?
No, if you are married, only your spouse and children can be nominees for EPF. Parents can be nominees only if you are unmarried or if your spouse and children are legally excluded by court order.

### What happens if I don't have any nomination?
If there's no nomination and you pass away, your PF money will be distributed to legal heirs as per succession laws. This involves producing a legal heir certificate and can take months — see our [legal heir certificate guide](/guide/legal-heir-certificate-apply).

### Can I nominate someone outside my family?
For EPF, you can only nominate family members (as defined by EPF rules). If you have no family members, you can nominate any person.

### Does nomination change when I change jobs?
Your UAN and nomination remain the same across job changes. However, it's good practice to verify your nomination whenever you join a new employer.

### Can a minor be a nominee for EPF?
Yes, minors (children) can be nominees. However, you must specify a **guardian** who will receive the money on the minor's behalf until they turn 18.
