# Punjab Ration Card Apply Online 2026

The Punjab ration card is issued by the **Department of Food, Civil Supplies and Consumer Affairs, Government of Punjab**. Punjab manages PDS through the **EPOS Punjab** (Electronic Point of Sale) portal. Punjab uses a colour-coded card system — Blue (BPL/NFSA), Yellow (APL), and Pink (AAY). The state is one of the few where the PDS system covers a large proportion of the population under NFSA.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [epos.punjab.gov.in](https://epos.punjab.gov.in) or your nearest Sewa Kendra. Blue card (BPL/PHH) holders get subsidised/free grain under PM GKAY. Punjab also provides sugar and kerosene through PDS.

---

## Ration Card Types in Punjab

| Card | Category | Colour | Entitlement |
|------|----------|--------|-------------|
| **PHH / Blue Card** | Priority Household BPL (NFSA) | Blue | 5 kg grain/person/month free (PM GKAY) |
| **AAY Card** | Antyodaya — Poorest families | Pink | 35 kg/family/month free |
| **APL / Yellow Card** | Above poverty line | Yellow | Subsidised sugar; kerosene; identity use |

> Punjab also distributes **sugar** and **kerosene** through PDS to eligible cardholders — check with your local FPS dealer for current allotments.

---

## Eligibility

**PHH / Blue Card (BPL):**
- Annual household income below ₹1.50 lakh (rural) or ₹2 lakh (urban)
- Not a government employee or PSU employee
- No income taxpayer in family
- No motorised 4-wheeler vehicle
- No agricultural land above 5 acres
- Aadhaar card mandatory for all members

**AAY / Pink Card:**
- Landless agricultural labourers with no stable income
- Widow / elderly living alone with no income source
- Disabled persons without family support
- Migrant workers and bonded labourers
- Tribal families (in applicable areas)

**APL / Yellow Card:**
- Families above BPL threshold
- Mainly used for PDS sugar, kerosene access, and identity/residence proof

---

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar cards (all family members — mandatory)
- Residence proof (electricity bill, water bill, property tax, rent agreement)
- Passport-size photographs
- Income certificate from Tehsildar or SDM
- Caste certificate (if SC/ST)
- Bank account passbook (for DBT linkage)
- Voter ID (additional identity proof)

## How to Apply

**Method 1: Sewa Kendra (Recommended)**

Punjab's **Sewa Kendras** (multi-service delivery centres) are the primary offline channel:

1. Visit your nearest **Sewa Kendra**
2. Ask for "New Ration Card Application"
3. Provide Aadhaar details, income certificate, and address proof
4. Operator fills and submits the online application
5. Service fee: approximately ₹30–50
6. Receive acknowledgement slip with application number

**Method 2: EPOS Punjab Online Portal**

1. Go to [epos.punjab.gov.in](https://epos.punjab.gov.in)
2. Click **"Citizen Services"** or **"Apply for Ration Card"**
3. Log in with your Aadhaar-linked mobile number
4. Fill in household and member details
5. Upload documents (income certificate, address proof, photographs)
6. Submit and save acknowledgement number

**Method 3: Block Food Supply Officer / SDM Office**

1. Visit your Block Food Supply Officer or SDM office
2. Collect and fill application form
3. Submit with all documents
4. Field verification by Inspector before forwarding to DFSO (District Food Supply Officer)

## Status Check

1. Go to [epos.punjab.gov.in](https://epos.punjab.gov.in)
2. Click **"Application Status"**
3. Enter acknowledgement number or Aadhaar number
4. View current status

**Via Sewa Kendra:** Ask at any Sewa Kendra with your acknowledgement number.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a Blue Card in Punjab?

Blue card = Priority Household (PHH/BPL) under NFSA. It entitles the family to 5 kg free grain per person per month. This is the main subsidised ration card in Punjab.

### How is Blue Card different from Yellow Card in Punjab?

Blue card = BPL / NFSA eligible → free grain. Yellow card = APL → no free grain but access to PDS sugar, kerosene, and useful as identity document.

### I'm a migrant worker in Punjab from another state. Can I apply?

Yes — if you're a resident of Punjab, apply with current Punjab address proof. Portable ration under "One Nation One Ration Card" (ONORC) also allows you to use your home state ration card at Punjab FPS shops.

### What is ONORC and does it work in Punjab?

Yes — Punjab is part of **One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC)**. Migrant workers can use their ration card from any state at Punjab FPS shops using Aadhaar biometric authentication. No separate Punjab ration card needed if you already have one from your home state.
