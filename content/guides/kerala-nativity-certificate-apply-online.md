# Kerala Nativity Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **nativity certificate** (ജൻമദേശ സർട്ടിഫിക്കറ്റ് — Janmadesham Sartteefikkattu) in Kerala proves that you are a native/resident of Kerala. It is required for the **85% state quota** in KEAM (Kerala Engineering Architecture Medical) admissions, NEET Kerala state counselling (Commissioner for Entrance Examinations — CEE), Kerala government jobs (Kerala PSC), and state scheme eligibility. Applications go through the **Kerala e-District** portal.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [edistrict.kerala.gov.in](https://edistrict.kerala.gov.in) or nearest **Akshaya Centre**. Issued by Village Officer / Tahsildar within **7–15 days**.

---

## Kerala State Quota — Nativity Rules (KEAM/NEET)

For **KEAM 2026 and NEET Kerala state quota** (CEE Kerala counselling):

Kerala uses **birth-based or study-based** nativity:

| Route | Requirement |
|-------|------------|
| **Born in Kerala** | Nativity certificate showing birth in Kerala |
| **Parent is Kerala native** | Parent's nativity + your residence in Kerala |
| **Studied in Kerala schools** | Transfer Certificate from Kerala schools covering minimum study period (check CEE Kerala notification) |

> Kerala's state quota rules are set annually by **CEE Kerala** (Commissioner for Entrance Examinations). Always verify current year's KEAM/NEET notification at [cee.kerala.gov.in](https://cee.kerala.gov.in).

## Why You Need Kerala Nativity Certificate

- **KEAM state quota** — engineering, architecture, pharmacy admissions in Kerala
- **NEET Kerala state quota** — 85% government medical college seats
- **Kerala PSC** — government job reservation for Kerala natives
- **Kerala state scholarship** — SSLC/HSE merit scholarships for Kerala students
- **DC (Directorate of Collegiate Education)** scholarships — for Kerala residents
- **Ration card related** — nativity supports Kerala ration card eligibility

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory — with Kerala address)
- **Ration card** (Kerala ration card)
- **Birth certificate** — showing birth in Kerala (if claiming nativity by birth)
- **School Transfer Certificates (TC)** — from Kerala schools
- **Voter ID** (Kerala voter enrollment)
- **Parent's nativity certificate** (if claiming through parent)
- Self-declaration of Kerala nativity
- Passport-size photograph

## How to Apply Online — Kerala e-District

1. Go to [edistrict.kerala.gov.in](https://edistrict.kerala.gov.in)
2. Register / log in with mobile number (OTP)
3. Click **"Revenue"** → **"Nativity Certificate"**
4. Fill form:
   - Applicant name, address (district, taluk, village)
   - Basis of nativity (born in Kerala / school study)
   - Purpose of certificate
5. Upload documents (Aadhaar, ration card, birth certificate or school TCs)
6. Pay fee: **₹15–30** (online)
7. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via Akshaya Centre

1. Visit nearest **Akshaya Centre** (Kerala government digital service kiosks)
2. Carry Aadhaar, ration card, birth certificate / school TCs
3. Operator fills and submits on e-District portal
4. Service charge: approximately ₹30–60
5. Collect acknowledgement

---

## Apply at Village Office

1. Visit your **Village Office** (Desam office)
2. Meet the **Village Officer**
3. Submit nativity certificate application with documents
4. Village Officer verifies nativity from local records
5. Certificate issued after Tahsildar approval

---

## Status Check & Download

1. Go to [edistrict.kerala.gov.in](https://edistrict.kerala.gov.in)
2. Click **"Application Status"** → enter reference number
3. Once approved: download digitally signed PDF
4. Valid for KEAM, NEET Kerala CEE, Kerala PSC applications

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I was born in Kerala but studied outside — do I qualify for KEAM state quota?

Being **born in Kerala** is one of the eligibility routes. Additionally, if you've studied in Kerala schools for the minimum period required by CEE Kerala, you may also qualify. Check the **current year's KEAM notification** for precise eligibility criteria — rules can change.

### Kerala nativity vs community certificate — different?

Yes. **Nativity certificate** = proves you are a Kerala native/resident. **Community certificate** = proves your caste (OBC/SC/ST). For KEAM/NEET reserved category: you need **both** — nativity (for state quota) + community (for reservation within state quota).

### Akshaya Centre for nativity certificate — fast processing?

Akshaya Centre submits your application electronically — processing time after submission is the same (7–15 days). Akshaya Centre helps with form filling and document scanning. You cannot expedite the Village Officer / Tahsildar verification process.
