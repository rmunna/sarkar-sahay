## What is Medha Protsahan Yojana?

**Medha Protsahan Yojana** is a scheme by the **Government of Himachal Pradesh** under the **Department of Higher Education** that provides financial assistance to meritorious students from economically weaker families for coaching for competitive exams like **UPSC, JEE, NEET, CLAT, UGC-NET**, and other national-level examinations.

Under this scheme, eligible students receive up to **₹1,00,000** as coaching assistance to prepare at reputed coaching institutes.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Residency:** Must be a permanent resident of Himachal Pradesh
2. **Income:** Annual family income should not exceed **₹2,50,000**
3. **Education:**
   - For UPSC/State PCS: Must be a graduate
   - For JEE/NEET: Must have passed 10+2 with science
   - For CLAT: Must have passed 10+2
   - For UGC-NET: Must be a postgraduate
4. **Age:** As per the respective exam eligibility
5. **Merit:** Selection is based on academic merit — preference given to higher marks
6. **One-Time Benefit:** The assistance can be availed only once

## Financial Assistance

| Exam Category | Maximum Assistance |
|---------------|-------------------|
| UPSC Civil Services | Up to ₹1,00,000 |
| State PCS | Up to ₹75,000 |
| JEE / NEET | Up to ₹75,000 |
| CLAT | Up to ₹50,000 |
| UGC-NET / Other | Up to ₹50,000 |

> Amounts may vary based on actual coaching fees and government notifications.

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar Card**
- **HP Bonafide / Domicile Certificate**
- **Income Certificate** (annual family income ≤ ₹2,50,000) from SDM/Tehsildar
- **Educational Certificates** (marksheets of qualifying exam)
- **Bank Account Details** (Aadhaar-linked)
- **Coaching Institute Admission Proof** (fee receipt / admission letter from a recognized coaching institute)
- **Passport-size photographs**
- **Caste Certificate** (if applicable, for reservation category)

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Online Application

1. **Visit** [emeritus.hp.gov.in](https://emeritus.hp.gov.in) or the HP Higher Education Department portal
2. **Register** with your details and mobile number
3. **Fill** the application form: personal details, educational qualification, exam you're preparing for
4. **Upload** all required documents
5. **Select** the coaching institute where you plan to take coaching
6. **Submit** the application before the deadline
7. **Track** status online using application ID

### Offline Application

1. **Visit** the **Director of Higher Education Office** or **District Education Office**
2. **Collect** the Medha Protsahan application form
3. **Fill** and attach all documents
4. **Submit** before the announced deadline
5. **Selection** is based on merit list

## Selection Process

1. Applications are reviewed by the Higher Education Department
2. A **merit list** is prepared based on academic performance
3. Shortlisted candidates are notified via email/SMS
4. Financial assistance is released after joining the coaching institute

## Processing Time

- **Application Window:** Usually opens once a year (check notifications)
- **Merit List:** Published within 30-60 days of deadline
- **Disbursement:** After verification and joining coaching institute

## Important Tips

1. **Apply Early:** The scheme has limited seats — apply as soon as notifications are released
2. **Choose Recognized Institutes:** Coaching must be at a reputed, recognized institute
3. **Keep Marksheets Ready:** Your academic performance determines selection
4. **Check Notifications:** Visit the HP Higher Education website regularly for announcements
5. **One-Time Only:** This benefit is available only once — make the most of it

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. How much financial assistance is provided?
Up to **₹1,00,000** depending on the exam category and coaching fees.

### Q2. Can I take coaching anywhere in India?
Yes, you can enroll at any recognized coaching institute in India.

### Q3. What is the income limit?
Annual family income should not exceed **₹2,50,000**.

### Q4. Can I apply for multiple exams?
No, the benefit is one-time and for one specific exam preparation.

### Q5. When does the application window open?
Usually once a year. Check [emeritus.hp.gov.in](https://emeritus.hp.gov.in) for the latest notification.

### Q6. Is the money given to the student or the coaching institute?
The financial assistance is typically released to the student's bank account or in some cases directly to the coaching institute.

### Q7. What if I don't get selected?
Selection is merit-based. You can re-apply in the next cycle if you improve your academic record.
