## What is Punjab Widow Pension Scheme?

The Punjab Widow Pension Scheme is a social security initiative by the **Department of Social Security and Women & Child Development, Government of Punjab**. Under this scheme, widowed women in Punjab receive a monthly pension of **₹1,500** to support their basic livelihood.

This scheme is part of the National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP) combined with state-level top-up. The aim is to provide financial independence to women who have lost their husbands and may not have other sources of income.

## Who is Eligible?

To qualify for the Punjab Widow Pension, applicants must meet these criteria:

1. **Residency:** Must be a permanent resident of Punjab
2. **Marital Status:** Must be a widow (husband deceased)
3. **Age:** Minimum 18 years of age
4. **Income:** Annual family income should not exceed **₹60,000** from all sources
5. **Re-marriage:** The applicant should not have remarried
6. **Other Pension:** Should not be receiving any other government pension

## Documents Required

### Identity & Address Proof
- Aadhaar Card (linked with mobile number)
- Voter ID or Ration Card

### Death & Marriage Proof
- Death Certificate of husband (issued by Municipal Corporation or Gram Panchayat)
- Marriage Certificate or any proof of marriage

### Income Proof
- Income Certificate from Tehsildar/SDM (annual income below ₹60,000)

### Bank Details
- Bank passbook copy (account in applicant's name)
- Account must be linked with Aadhaar for DBT

### Photographs
- Passport-size photographs (2 copies)

### Residence Proof
- Domicile Certificate or Residential Proof from Punjab

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Online Application (via Sewa Kendra / Connect Punjab)

1. **Visit** the Connect Punjab portal at [connect.punjab.gov.in](https://connect.punjab.gov.in)
2. **Navigate** to Social Security services or search for "Widow Pension"
3. **Fill** the application form with personal details, husband's death certificate number, and bank account details
4. **Upload** scanned copies of all required documents
5. **Submit** the application and note down the reference number
6. **Track** your application status on the same portal

### Offline Application (via Sewa Kendra or District Office)

1. **Visit** your nearest **Sewa Kendra** or **District Social Security Office**
2. **Collect** the widow pension application form
3. **Fill** the form with accurate details
4. **Attach** self-attested photocopies of all required documents
5. **Submit** the form at the Sewa Kendra counter
6. **Collect** the acknowledgement receipt with application number
7. **Verification** will be done by the Social Security Officer at the block level

## Pension Amount

| Category | Monthly Pension |
|----------|----------------|
| Widow aged 18-57 years | ₹1,500 |
| Widow aged 58-79 years | ₹1,500 |
| Widow aged 80+ years | ₹1,500 (may vary with NSAP top-up) |

> The pension amount is credited directly to the beneficiary's bank account via **Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)** on a monthly basis.

## Processing Time

- **Application Review:** 30-45 days from submission
- **Verification:** District Social Security Officer conducts field verification
- **Approval:** After verification, pension starts from the month of approval
- **Disbursement:** Monthly, directly to bank account

## Important Tips

1. **Aadhaar-Bank Linking:** Ensure your Aadhaar is linked to your bank account for seamless DBT payments
2. **Annual Verification:** Beneficiaries may need to submit a life certificate annually to continue receiving pension
3. **Track Online:** Use Connect Punjab portal to check your application status regularly
4. **Sewa Kendra Help:** If you face difficulty with online application, visit any Sewa Kendra for assisted filing — the service fee is nominal
5. **Update Details:** If you change your bank account or address, update it immediately at the District Social Security Office

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. What is the current pension amount under Punjab Widow Pension Scheme?
The current monthly pension is **₹1,500**, paid directly to the beneficiary's bank account via DBT.

### Q2. Can I apply online for Punjab Widow Pension?
Yes, you can apply online through the [Connect Punjab portal](https://connect.punjab.gov.in) or visit your nearest Sewa Kendra for assisted filing.

### Q3. What is the income limit for Punjab Widow Pension eligibility?
The annual family income should not exceed **₹60,000** from all sources.

### Q4. How long does it take to get the pension approved?
Typically **30-45 days** after submission, including field verification by the Social Security Officer.

### Q5. Will the pension stop if I remarry?
Yes, the widow pension is discontinued if the beneficiary remarries.

### Q6. What documents are needed to prove widow status?
You need the **Death Certificate** of your husband issued by the competent authority (Municipal Corporation or Gram Panchayat).

### Q7. Can I receive both Old Age Pension and Widow Pension?
No, you can receive only one social security pension at a time from the Punjab government.
