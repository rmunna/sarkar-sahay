# Tripura Old Age Pension – Complete Guide 2026

## What is the Tripura Old Age Pension?

The **Tripura Old Age Pension** provides monthly financial assistance to senior citizens from BPL and economically weaker families. It operates under the **Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS)** with a state top-up from the Tripura government.

## Pension Amount

| Age Group | Central Share | State Top-up | Total (Approx.) |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|-----------------|
| 60-79 years | ₹200 | ₹200-500 | ₹400-700 |
| 80+ years | ₹500 | ₹200-500 | ₹700-1,000 |

> Verify current state top-up amounts with the District Social Welfare Office.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Age:** 60 years or above
- **Residency:** Permanent resident of Tripura
- **Income:** BPL category or below prescribed income limit
- **Not receiving** any other government pension or retirement benefits

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar Card
- Age proof (birth certificate / voter ID / school certificate)
- BPL card or income certificate
- Bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked)
- Residential proof
- Passport-size photographs (2)
- Self-declaration form

## How to Apply – Step-by-Step

### Offline Application

1. Visit the **Gram Panchayat office** (rural) or **Municipal office** (urban)
2. Collect the **Old Age Pension application form**
3. Fill in personal, age, income, and bank details
4. Attach self-attested document copies
5. Get the form verified by the **Gram Pradhan / Ward Member**
6. Submit at the **Block Development Office** or **Sub-Divisional Magistrate office**
7. After verification and district-level approval, pension is credited via DBT

### Online

- Check [nsap.nic.in](https://nsap.nic.in) for central IGNOAPS portal
- Some applications may be accepted through [tripura.gov.in](https://tripura.gov.in) e-services

## Processing Time

- **Verification to approval:** 1-3 months
- **First credit:** Within 1-2 months of approval

## Important Tips

1. **Ensure accurate age proof** — pension amount varies by age bracket
2. **Link Aadhaar to bank account** before applying
3. **Annual life verification** is mandatory
4. **Apply through Gram Panchayat** for faster processing
5. **Keep acknowledgment receipt** safe for tracking

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: What is the pension amount in Tripura?
**A:** Approximately **₹400-700 per month** for ages 60-79, and **₹700-1,000** for 80+, including central and state shares.

### Q2: Can I apply online?
**A:** Primary method is **offline** through Gram Panchayat/Municipal office. The NSAP portal ([nsap.nic.in](https://nsap.nic.in)) also accepts applications.

### Q3: Is BPL card mandatory?
**A:** If you don't have a BPL card, an **income certificate** from the SDM office can substitute.

### Q4: How is the pension paid?
**A:** Through **DBT** to the beneficiary's Aadhaar-linked bank account.

### Q5: Can I receive this along with widow pension?
**A:** Generally, only **one NSAP pension** is allowed. Choose whichever offers higher benefit. See our [Tripura Widow Pension guide](/guide/tr-widow-pension).
