# Sovereign Gold Bond (SGB) 2026 — Complete Buying Guide

**Sovereign Gold Bond (SGB)** is the smartest way to invest in gold in India. Issued by the **Reserve Bank of India** on behalf of the Government of India, SGBs give you:

- **Gold price appreciation** (same as physical gold)
- **+2.5% annual interest** on the investment amount (paid every 6 months)
- **Zero capital gains tax** if held till maturity (8 years)
- No storage risk, no making charges, no purity worry

> **Note:** RBI has paused fresh SGB issuances since February 2024. You can still buy existing SGBs from the secondary market via stock exchanges (NSE/BSE). This guide covers both new issuance (when available) and secondary market buying.

---

## SGB vs Physical Gold vs Gold ETF

| Factor | SGB | Physical Gold | Gold ETF |
|--------|-----|--------------|---------|
| **Returns** | Gold price + 2.5% interest | Gold price only | Gold price only |
| **Storage** | No (electronic) | Risk (locker/home) | No (demat) |
| **Tax on maturity** | **Zero** (if held 8 years) | LTCG 20% + indexation | LTCG 20% |
| **Making charges** | None | 10–25% | None |
| **Purity risk** | None (sovereign guarantee) | Yes | None |
| **Liquidity** | After 5 years or secondary market | High | High (stock exchange) |
| **Minimum** | 1 gram | 1 gram (jewellery) | Varies by ETF |
| **Maximum** | 4 kg/year (individual) | No limit | No limit |

**Verdict:** SGB is mathematically superior to physical gold in every dimension. The 2.5% extra interest alone justifies choosing SGB over gold jewellery or gold coins.

## How SGB Works

1. **Buy:** At issuance price (gold rate − ₹50 discount online) OR from secondary market
2. **Interest:** 2.5% per annum on the **issue price** (not current market price), paid every 6 months
3. **Tenure:** 8 years (but can exit after 5 years on coupon payment dates)
4. **Maturity:** Receive prevailing gold price × quantity — **zero capital gains tax**
5. **Before 5 years:** Sell on stock exchange (taxed as per normal LTCG/STCG rules)

## How to Buy SGB Online

### Method 1: Zerodha (Kite)

1. Login to **Zerodha Kite** → **Mutual Funds** → **Gold Bonds**
2. During issuance period: Click on current series → Buy
3. Secondary market: Go to **NSE** → Search **"SGB"** → Buy like a stock
4. Payment via Zerodha account balance or UPI
5. Units reflect in demat in **T+2 days**

### Method 2: Groww

1. Open Groww → **"Explore"** → Search **"SGB"** or **"Gold"**
2. During issuance: Buy SGB directly
3. Secondary market: Groww shows listed SGBs from NSE/BSE
4. Complete KYC if needed → Buy

### Method 3: Bank (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, etc.)

1. Login to net banking → **"Invest"** → **"Sovereign Gold Bond"**
2. During issuance window: Fill form, enter quantity, confirm
3. **₹50 per gram discount** for online buyers (official RBI discount)
4. Debit from account → Bond credited to demat in 2–3 working days

**SBI Net Banking:** onlinesbi.sbi.co.in → e-Invest → SGB

### Method 4: Post Office

1. Visit any Post Office during issuance window
2. Fill SGB application form
3. Pay by cash/cheque
4. Bond certificate issued (no demat required)

## Buying SGB from Secondary Market (When No Fresh Issue)

Even when RBI is not issuing new SGBs, older series trade on NSE/BSE. You can buy them like stocks:

1. Open your **trading account** (Zerodha, Upstox, Groww)
2. Search for **"SGB"** in the stock search
3. You'll see multiple series: SGBJAN27, SGBMAR28, etc.
4. Choose a series with maturity date that suits you
5. Buy at market price (may be slight premium or discount to spot gold)
6. Held in your demat account

**Secondary market SGB:** You get the same 2.5% interest. Tax treatment depends on holding period from your purchase date (not original issue date).

## SGB Interest — How It Works

- Interest rate: **2.5% per annum** on the **issue price** (fixed at time of purchase)
- **Not on current market value** — so as gold prices rise, the percentage return from interest decreases in absolute terms but the capital appreciation compensates massively
- Interest paid: Every **6 months** (credited to your bank account)
- **Tax on interest:** Taxable as per your income tax slab (added to income)
- **No TDS** on SGB interest

**Example:** Buy 10 grams at ₹6,000/gram = ₹60,000 investment
Annual interest = 2.5% × ₹60,000 = ₹1,500/year (₹750 every 6 months)

## SGB Maturity Calculation Example

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Purchase | 10 grams at ₹6,000 = ₹60,000 |
| Annual interest | ₹1,500 × 8 years = ₹12,000 |
| Gold price at maturity | ₹9,000/gram |
| Maturity value | 10 × ₹9,000 = ₹90,000 |
| Capital gain | ₹90,000 − ₹60,000 = ₹30,000 |
| **Tax on capital gain** | **ZERO (maturity = tax-free)** |
| **Total return** | ₹42,000 on ₹60,000 = **70% over 8 years** |

Compare: Physical gold would give the same ₹30,000 appreciation but **no interest** and LTCG tax on the gain.

## When Can You Exit SGB Early?

| Exit Option | When Available | Tax |
|-------------|----------------|-----|
| **Maturity exit** | After 8 years | Zero capital gains |
| **Premature redemption** | After 5 years (on coupon payment date) | Zero capital gains |
| **Secondary market sale** | Anytime (NSE/BSE) | STCG or LTCG based on holding |

For early exit before 5 years: Sell on stock exchange. If held < 3 years: STCG (slab rate). If held > 3 years: LTCG (20% with indexation).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is SGB safe? What if the government defaults?**
SGB is issued by the Government of India and backed by RBI. It is as safe as Indian government bonds — the safest asset class in India. Default risk is negligible.

**Can I use SGB as collateral for a loan?**
Yes — SGBs are eligible as collateral for loans from banks and NBFCs. Typically up to 75% of SGB value as loan (Loan-to-Value ratio).

**How many grams can I buy?**
Minimum: 1 gram. Maximum: 4 kg per financial year for individuals, 20 kg for trusts/entities. HUF is treated as individual (4 kg limit).

**What happens to SGB if RBI issues are suspended?**
Your existing SGBs are not affected — you continue to receive interest and can redeem at maturity. Only new issuances are paused. Secondary market trading continues.

**Is SGB better than gold mutual funds or gold ETFs?**
Yes — SGBs give the extra 2.5% interest and zero tax at maturity. Gold ETFs and gold funds are convenient (more liquid) but don't have the extra interest or tax-free maturity. If you can lock in for 5–8 years, SGB wins.
