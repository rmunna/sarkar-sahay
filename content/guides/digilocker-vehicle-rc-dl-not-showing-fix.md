# DigiLocker Driving Licence or RC Not Showing? Fix It Now

Many users search for their Driving Licence (DL) or Vehicle Registration Certificate (RC) on [DigiLocker](https://www.digilocker.gov.in/) but find that the document is not available or not showing. This is a common issue and is usually caused by sync delays with the Parivahan (Ministry of Road Transport) database. Here's how to fix it.

## Why Your DL or RC Is Not Showing in DigiLocker

DigiLocker fetches DL and RC data directly from the **Parivahan/Sarathi database** maintained by the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways. Your document may not appear due to:

- **Parivahan database sync delay** — Your state RTO may not have updated the central database yet
- **Name mismatch** — The name on your DL/RC doesn't match your Aadhaar name in DigiLocker
- **State RTO not fully integrated** — Some state RTOs have not completed integration with DigiLocker
- **Incorrect DL/RC number** — Entering the wrong number during manual fetch
- **Recently issued document** — New DLs and RCs may take 7–30 days to appear in DigiLocker

## Fix 1: Manually Pull Your DL or RC

Instead of waiting for auto-fetch, pull the document manually:

### For Driving Licence (DL):

1. Open **DigiLocker** app or visit [digilocker.gov.in](https://www.digilocker.gov.in/)
2. Go to **Search Documents** or **Get Issued Documents**
3. Search for **Ministry of Road Transport and Highways**
4. Select **Driving Licence**
5. Enter your **DL number** and **Date of Birth**
6. Tap **Get Document**

### For Vehicle RC:

1. Open **DigiLocker** app or website
2. Go to **Search Documents** or **Get Issued Documents**
3. Search for **Ministry of Road Transport and Highways**
4. Select **Vehicle Registration Certificate (RC)**
5. Enter your **Vehicle Registration Number**
6. Tap **Get Document**

> **Tip:** Make sure to enter the DL/RC number exactly as printed on your physical document, including any hyphens or spaces.

## Fix 2: Check and Fix Name Mismatch

DigiLocker matches your name with the issuing department's database. If your name on the DL/RC doesn't match your Aadhaar name:

1. Check the **exact spelling** of your name on your physical DL/RC
2. Compare with your name in DigiLocker (linked to Aadhaar)
3. If there's a mismatch, you have two options:
   - **Update your name in Aadhaar** to match your DL/RC — visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/)
   - **Update your name on DL/RC** at your local RTO to match Aadhaar

See our [Aadhaar card guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) for name correction steps.

## Fix 3: Check If Your State RTO Is Integrated

Not all state RTOs have completed integration with DigiLocker. To check:

1. Visit [parivahan.gov.in](https://parivahan.gov.in/)
2. Check if your state's transport department appears under the online services
3. You can also check the DigiLocker partner list at [digilocker.gov.in](https://www.digilocker.gov.in/) under **Partner Issuers**

**States with known full integration:** Delhi, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan, Gujarat, Telangana, Madhya Pradesh, and most other major states.

**States with partial/delayed integration:** Some northeastern states and smaller union territories may have delays.

## Fix 4: Verify Your DL/RC on Parivahan Portal

If your document doesn't show in DigiLocker, check if it exists in the Parivahan database first:

1. Visit [parivahan.gov.in](https://parivahan.gov.in/)
2. Go to **Know Your DL** or **Know Your Vehicle Details**
3. Enter your DL number or vehicle registration number
4. If your details appear here but not in DigiLocker, it's a sync issue — wait 24–48 hours and try again
5. If your details don't appear here either, contact your local RTO

## Fix 5: Wait for New Documents

If you recently obtained your DL or registered a vehicle:

- **New Driving Licence:** May take **7–15 days** to appear in DigiLocker after issuance
- **New Vehicle RC:** May take **15–30 days** to appear after registration
- **DL Renewal:** Updated DL may take **7–10 days** to sync

During this waiting period, use your physical document or the Parivahan portal's **mParivahan app** as an alternative digital copy.

## Fix 6: Use mParivahan as an Alternative

The **mParivahan** app by the Ministry of Road Transport is another official app for accessing your DL and RC digitally:

1. Download **mParivahan** from Google Play Store or Apple App Store
2. Register with your mobile number
3. Add your DL or RC using the document number
4. Your digital DL/RC in mParivahan is legally valid for traffic police verification

## Important Tips

- DigiLocker DL and RC are legally valid and accepted by traffic police across India under the Motor Vehicles (Amendment) Act, 2019
- If you have a **learner's licence**, it may not be available in DigiLocker — only permanent DLs are usually synced
- Keep the **mParivahan** app as a backup for DL and RC access
- For persistent issues, raise a grievance at the [Parivahan Sewa portal](https://parivahan.gov.in/)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is DigiLocker DL valid for traffic police verification?
Yes. A DL or RC stored in DigiLocker is legally valid and accepted by traffic police across India under the IT Act, 2000 and Motor Vehicles (Amendment) Act, 2019.

### My DL shows in Parivahan but not in DigiLocker. What should I do?
This is a sync delay between the Parivahan database and DigiLocker. Try manually pulling the document in DigiLocker. If it still doesn't work, wait 24–48 hours and try again.

### Can I download my DL PDF from DigiLocker?
Yes. Once your DL appears in DigiLocker, you can download it as a digitally signed PDF that is legally valid.

### My RC shows the previous owner's name. How do I fix it?
You need to complete the **transfer of ownership** at your local RTO first. Once updated in the Parivahan database, the new details will sync to DigiLocker.

### How long does it take for a renewed DL to appear in DigiLocker?
A renewed DL typically takes 7–15 days to sync with DigiLocker after the RTO updates the Parivahan database.

### Does DigiLocker show international driving permits?
No. DigiLocker only shows domestic Indian driving licences issued by state RTOs through the Sarathi portal.
