# ITR-U (Updated Return) India 2026 — Complete Guide

ITR-U (Updated Return under Section 139(8A)) is a second chance: if you missed the ITR deadline, filed with errors, or didn't declare some income — you can correct it within 2 years of the assessment year. Here's how it works and what it costs.

---

## What Is ITR-U?

ITR-U allows you to file or revise your income tax return **up to 2 years after the assessment year ends**, even after the regular revised return deadline has passed.

**Key differences from regular revised return**:

| Feature | Revised Return (139(5)) | Updated Return ITR-U (139(8A)) |
|---------|------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Deadline | December 31 of the assessment year | 2 years from end of assessment year |
| Additional tax | None | 25–50% extra on additional income tax |
| Purpose | Fix any error | Can only INCREASE tax liability (not decrease) |
| Refund | Can claim | Cannot file ITR-U for refund |
| Available if notice received | No | No (if proceedings pending) |

---

## ITR-U Deadlines — Which AY Can You Still File?

| Assessment Year (AY) | FY of Income | ITR-U Deadline |
|---------------------|-------------|---------------|
| AY 2023-24 | FY 2022-23 | **March 31, 2026** |
| AY 2024-25 | FY 2023-24 | March 31, 2027 |
| AY 2025-26 | FY 2024-25 | March 31, 2028 |
| AY 2026-27 | FY 2025-26 | March 31, 2029 |

**Important**: AY 2023-24 deadline is **March 31, 2026** — if you have missed income from FY 2022-23, file before this date.

---

## Additional Tax in ITR-U — What You Pay

Filing ITR-U requires paying an **additional tax surcharge** on top of the normal tax on the added income:

| When You File ITR-U | Additional Tax on Incremental Income |
|--------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Within 1 year of AY end (AY 2024-25 → by March 31, 2025) | 25% extra |
| Between 1–2 years of AY end (AY 2024-25 → April 2025 to March 2026) | 50% extra |

**Example**:
You forgot to declare ₹2,00,000 freelance income in FY 2023-24 (AY 2024-25). Your tax slab is 30%.

Tax on ₹2,00,000 at 30% = ₹60,000
If filing ITR-U after April 1, 2025 (1+ year mark): Additional 50% = ₹30,000
Total extra payment: **₹90,000** (+ original tax)

## When Should You File ITR-U?

**Use ITR-U when**:
- You missed the original July 31 ITR deadline and also the December 31 belated return deadline
- You filed ITR but missed declaring some income (second job, freelance, capital gains, fixed deposit interest)
- You filed under wrong regime and it resulted in lower tax (though you can't change regime via ITR-U — you'd need to recalculate under correct rules)
- You claimed excessive deductions (wrong HRA calculation, extra 80C) — file ITR-U to correct and pay difference

**Cannot use ITR-U if**:
- You want to claim a refund or increase refund
- You want to reduce your tax liability
- An income tax notice/proceeding for that AY is already pending
- Search/survey proceedings have been initiated for that year
- Prosecution proceedings started for that AY

## Step-by-Step: How to File ITR-U on Income Tax Portal

### Step 1: Login

1. Go to [incometax.gov.in](https://incometax.gov.in)
2. Login with PAN + password (or Aadhaar OTP)

### Step 2: Navigate to ITR-U

1. Dashboard → "File Income Tax Return"
2. Select Assessment Year (e.g., AY 2024-25)
3. The system will show ITR-U option if belated/revised return window has closed

**Alternatively**:
- e-File → Income Tax Returns → File Income Tax Return → Select AY → ITR-U will appear as an option

### Step 3: Download and Fill ITR-U Form

ITR-U is filed using the same ITR form you originally used (ITR-1, ITR-2, ITR-3, or ITR-4) — but with an additional schedule for:
- Part A-GEN: State the reason for filing ITR-U (choose from dropdown)
- Schedule of additional income/correction
- Computation of additional tax (25% or 50% on extra tax)

**Offline utility**: Download the ITR form Excel/JSON utility from the portal → fill → upload.

### Step 4: Calculate Additional Tax

The portal auto-calculates but verify:
1. Additional income (income not declared in original return)
2. Tax on additional income at your slab rate
3. Interest under 234A, 234B, 234C (if applicable)
4. Additional 25% or 50% as applicable
5. Total amount payable

### Step 5: Pay Additional Tax

1. Go to: e-Pay Tax → New Payment
2. Select: "Self-Assessment Tax" (challan 280)
3. AY: [relevant assessment year]
4. Enter amount and pay via net banking/UPI

### Step 6: Upload ITR-U with Challan

1. Fill ITR-U form with all corrections
2. Enter challan details (BSR code, date, amount, serial number)
3. Upload ITR-U JSON to portal
4. e-Verify via Aadhaar OTP, net banking, or DSC

| "Return not filed within due date" | Never filed ITR for that year |
| "Income taxable at special rate" | LTCG/STCG not properly declared |

## ITR-U vs Revised Return — Which to Use?

| Your Situation | Use This |
|---------------|---------|
| It's before December 31 of the assessment year | Revised return (free, no extra tax) |
| December 31 has passed, less than 2 years since AY end | ITR-U (with extra 25–50% tax) |
| Trying to increase refund | Revised return only (before Dec 31) — ITR-U won't help |
| Never filed ITR for that year | ITR-U (if within 2-year window) |

## What Happens If You Don't File ITR-U?

If the Income Tax Department later discovers undeclared income (via Form 26AS, AIS, financial institution reports):
- **Section 147 / 148 Notice**: Department can "reopen" your assessment and tax the income
- **Penalty**: 50–200% of tax evaded (vs 25–50% if you proactively file ITR-U)
- **Interest**: 12% per annum on unpaid tax (compounding from due date)

**Proactive ITR-U = lower penalty, closed loop.** Department notices = higher penalty + legal proceedings.

## AY 2023-24 Deadline Alert

The ITR-U deadline for **AY 2023-24 (FY 2022-23 income) is March 31, 2026**.

If you missed any income for FY 2022-23 (salary from previous job, freelance payments, FD interest you forgot, crypto transactions, capital gains from shares) — you have until March 31, 2026.

After that, there's no legal way to self-correct. The department can still find and tax it with higher penalties.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I filed ITR-1 but should have filed ITR-2 (capital gains). Can ITR-U fix this?

Yes — you can file ITR-U in the correct form (ITR-2) with capital gains properly reported. This declares additional income and triggers the 25–50% extra tax. It corrects the record without waiting for a department notice.

### I have a notice pending for AY 2024-25. Can I still file ITR-U?

No — if notice under Section 148/148A or 131 is already issued for that AY, ITR-U cannot be filed. Respond to the notice directly.

### Can I use ITR-U to switch tax regime?

No — you cannot change your tax regime via ITR-U. Regime choice is fixed for the year once the return is filed (or for belated return, the default applies). ITR-U only allows you to add income, not restructure your tax computation.

### What if I realize I paid too much tax and want a refund?

ITR-U cannot generate or increase a refund. For refund claims: use revised return (within December 31 of assessment year) or wait for department to process if it shows in their records.
