# Yes Bank Account Blocked or Frozen — How to Fix It (2026)

If your Yes Bank account shows "Account Blocked", "Debit Freeze" or you cannot access YES PAY (mobile app), net banking or make transactions — this guide explains the fixes.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is independent and not affiliated with Yes Bank. Contact Yes Bank directly for account-specific help.

---

## Why Is Your Yes Bank Account Blocked?

| Reason | Explanation |
|--------|-------------|
| **KYC Not Updated** | Yes Bank, under RBI directions, has strict KYC compliance requirements post-2020 reconstruction |
| **Aadhaar / PAN Not Linked** | Mandatory compliance requirement |
| **Too Many Failed Login Attempts** | 3–5 wrong password attempts lock YES PAY / net banking |
| **Suspicious Transactions** | Fraud detection block |
| **Income Tax Department Order** | Tax freeze |
| **Dormant Account** | No transactions for 2+ years |
| **RBI 2020 Moratorium Legacy** | Some accounts from the 2020 Yes Bank crisis period may have compliance flags |

## Fix 1: KYC Freeze

**Via YES PAY App:**
1. Open **YES PAY** or **Yes Mobile** app
2. Look for KYC Update notification on home screen
3. Complete KYC via Video KYC (face + document) if available
4. Upload Aadhaar, PAN and selfie

**Via Yes Bank Net Banking:**
1. Login at **[yesbank.in](https://www.yesbank.in)** → Internet Banking
2. Go to **Profile → KYC Update**
3. Upload documents and submit
4. Verification: 1–3 working days

**At Branch:**
1. Visit Yes Bank branch with Aadhaar + PAN (originals)
2. Fill KYC form
3. Account unblocked in 1–3 working days

---

## Fix 2: YES PAY / Net Banking Blocked

**Net Banking Password Reset:**
1. Go to **[yesbank.in](https://www.yesbank.in)** → Login
2. Click **"Forgot Password"**
3. Enter Customer ID + registered mobile number
4. OTP verification → Set new password

**YES PAY App MPIN Reset:**
1. Open YES PAY → **"Forgot MPIN"**
2. Verify via OTP + debit card details
3. Set new MPIN

---

## Fix 3: Suspicious Activity / Other Block

1. Call **Yes Bank Customer Care: 1800-1200** (toll-free, 24×7)
2. Explain the issue and complete identity verification
3. For permanent unblock: visit branch with Aadhaar + PAN

---

## Fix 4: Dormant Account

1. Visit Yes Bank branch with Aadhaar + PAN (originals) + 2 passport photos
2. Fill Reactivation Form
3. Make a minimum transaction
4. Reactivated in 1–2 working days

## FAQ

**Is my money safe at Yes Bank after the 2020 crisis?**
Yes — Yes Bank was fully reconstructed under RBI supervision in March 2020. SBI and other banks infused capital. Your deposits are safe and DICGC-insured up to ₹5 lakh.

**Why was Yes Bank placed under moratorium in 2020?**
Yes Bank faced severe NPA (non-performing assets) issues. RBI placed it under temporary moratorium in March 2020, then reconstructed it with SBI's support. The bank is now fully operational.

**Escalation for unresolved freeze:** File at [yesbank.in/grievance](https://www.yesbank.in) or Banking Ombudsman at [bankingombudsman.rbi.org.in](https://bankingombudsman.rbi.org.in).
