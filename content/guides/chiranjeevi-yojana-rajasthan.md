## What is Chiranjeevi Yojana?

Mukhyamantri Chiranjeevi Swasthya Bima Yojana (MCSBY) is Rajasthan's flagship health insurance scheme launched on 1 May 2021. It provides **free cashless treatment up to ₹25 lakh per family per year** at government and empanelled private hospitals across Rajasthan and beyond.

The scheme covers all families of Rajasthan — there is no income limit or BPL requirement. Every family with a **Jan Aadhaar card** is eligible.

Similar to [Ayushman Bharat](/guide/ayushman-bharat-health-card) at the national level, Chiranjeevi Yojana is a state-level universal health coverage scheme.

## Coverage & Benefits

| Benefit | Details |
|---|---|
| **Coverage Amount** | ₹25 lakh per family per year |
| **Treatment Type** | Cashless at empanelled hospitals |
| **Who is Covered** | All family members on Jan Aadhaar card |
| **Hospitalization** | Covered (including 5 days pre & 15 days post) |
| **Packages Covered** | 1,597+ medical packages including surgeries, therapies, diagnostics |
| **Accidents** | Covered from day one (no waiting period) |
| **Pre-existing Diseases** | Covered from day one |
| **Organ Transplant** | Covered (heart, kidney, liver, etc.) |
| **Cancer Treatment** | Chemotherapy, radiation, surgery covered |
| **Dialysis** | Free dialysis at empanelled centres |
| **ICU Charges** | Fully covered |
| **Premium** | Free for all Rajasthan families (government pays) |

## Who is Eligible?

Chiranjeevi Yojana is a **universal scheme** — every family in Rajasthan is eligible regardless of income.

**Requirements:**
- Must be a resident of Rajasthan
- Must have a **Jan Aadhaar card** (Rajasthan's family identity card)
- All family members listed on Jan Aadhaar are automatically covered
- No income limit, no BPL/APL distinction
- No separate premium payment required

> **Note:** If you don't have a Jan Aadhaar card, apply first at [janaadhaar.rajasthan.gov.in](https://janaadhaar.rajasthan.gov.in) or your nearest e-Mitra centre.

## How to Register Online for Chiranjeevi Yojana

### Step 1 — Get Your Jan Aadhaar Card Ready
Ensure your Jan Aadhaar card is active with all family members listed. You can check status at [janaadhaar.rajasthan.gov.in](https://janaadhaar.rajasthan.gov.in).

### Step 2 — Visit the Chiranjeevi Portal
Go to [chiranjeevi.rajasthan.gov.in](https://chiranjeevi.rajasthan.gov.in) or log in via [sso.rajasthan.gov.in](https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in) (Rajasthan SSO).

### Step 3 — Log In Using SSO
- If you have an SSO ID, log in directly
- If not, create a new SSO account using your Jan Aadhaar number or Aadhaar number

### Step 4 — Select Chiranjeevi Yojana
After SSO login, find and click on **"Chiranjeevi Yojana (MCSBY)"** from the available services.

### Step 5 — Verify Your Jan Aadhaar Details
- Enter your Jan Aadhaar number
- Verify family member details
- All members on Jan Aadhaar will be registered automatically

### Step 6 — Complete Registration
- Confirm your details and submit
- Your Chiranjeevi digital health card / policy number will be generated
- Download or note your policy number for hospital visits

### Alternative: Register via e-Mitra
Visit any **e-Mitra kiosk** in Rajasthan with your Jan Aadhaar card and Aadhaar card. The operator will complete registration for you.

## How to Find Empanelled Hospitals

You can get cashless treatment only at **empanelled hospitals**. To find them:

1. Visit [chiranjeevi.rajasthan.gov.in](https://chiranjeevi.rajasthan.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Empanelled Hospital List"** or **"Hospital Search"**
3. Select your **district** and **city/town**
4. The list shows hospital name, address, contact number, and available packages
5. Both government hospitals and private hospitals are listed

**Key points:**
- All government hospitals in Rajasthan are empanelled by default
- 1,000+ private hospitals are also empanelled
- Treatment is available outside Rajasthan at empanelled hospitals in other states too
- The hospital list is updated regularly — check before visiting

## How to Use Chiranjeevi Card at Hospital

### At the Time of Admission
1. Visit any empanelled hospital
2. Go to the **Chiranjeevi / Ayushman Help Desk** (available at all empanelled hospitals)
3. Show your **Jan Aadhaar card** and **Aadhaar card**
4. The hospital will verify your eligibility online
5. Biometric verification (fingerprint) may be required
6. Once verified, you'll receive **cashless admission** — no payment needed

### During Treatment
- The hospital provides treatment under approved packages
- You don't need to pay anything covered under the scheme
- If additional treatment beyond the package is needed, the hospital will seek approval

### After Discharge
- Collect your discharge summary
- Medicines for up to 15 days post-discharge are covered
- Follow-up consultations as per the package are also covered
- No claim forms — the hospital settles directly with the insurance company

## What Diseases & Treatments are Covered?

Chiranjeevi Yojana covers **1,597+ treatment packages** including:

- **Heart:** Bypass surgery, angioplasty, valve replacement
- **Cancer:** Chemotherapy, radiation therapy, surgery
- **Kidney:** Dialysis, kidney transplant
- **Orthopaedic:** Joint replacement (knee, hip), fracture surgery
- **Neurosurgery:** Brain tumour, spine surgery
- **Eye:** Cataract surgery, retina treatment
- **Maternity:** Normal & caesarean delivery (at government hospitals)
- **General Surgery:** Hernia, appendix, gallbladder, etc.
- **Burns & Accidents:** Emergency treatment
- **Organ Transplant:** Heart, liver, kidney, bone marrow

> **Not covered:** OPD consultations (outpatient), cosmetic surgery, fertility treatment, substance abuse treatment.

## Important Tips

- **Always carry** your Jan Aadhaar card and Aadhaar card when visiting the hospital
- **Check empanelment** of the hospital before going — not all private hospitals are empanelled
- **No middlemen needed** — registration and treatment are completely free; don't pay anyone
- **Renewal is automatic** — no need to re-register every year
- **Emergency cases** can go to the nearest empanelled hospital without prior approval
- **Helpline:** Call **181** (Rajasthan government helpline) for Chiranjeevi-related queries
- If a hospital **denies cashless treatment** despite empanelment, complain on the Chiranjeevi portal or call the helpline

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Chiranjeevi Yojana free for everyone in Rajasthan?
Yes. Since 2021, the Rajasthan government pays the premium for all families. You don't need to pay anything for registration or treatment at empanelled hospitals.

### Do I need a separate Chiranjeevi card?
No physical card is issued. Your **Jan Aadhaar card** itself serves as your identity for availing benefits. You just need to register on the Chiranjeevi portal once.

### Can I use Chiranjeevi Yojana outside Rajasthan?
Yes, treatment is available at empanelled hospitals in other states as well. Check the hospital list on the portal for out-of-state options.

### What if my Jan Aadhaar doesn't have all family members?
Get your Jan Aadhaar updated first at [janaadhaar.rajasthan.gov.in](https://janaadhaar.rajasthan.gov.in) or an e-Mitra centre. Only members listed on Jan Aadhaar are covered.

### Is there a waiting period for Chiranjeevi Yojana?
No waiting period for accidents and emergencies. For planned surgeries and treatments, coverage is available from the date of registration.

### Can I use both Ayushman Bharat and Chiranjeevi Yojana?
Yes, both schemes can complement each other. Chiranjeevi covers all Rajasthan families regardless of SECC status, while Ayushman Bharat covers families identified under SECC data nationally. At empanelled hospitals, the portability between schemes is managed automatically.

### How do I check my Chiranjeevi Yojana registration status?
Log in to [chiranjeevi.rajasthan.gov.in](https://chiranjeevi.rajasthan.gov.in) using your SSO ID and check your policy status. You can also visit any e-Mitra centre for assistance.
