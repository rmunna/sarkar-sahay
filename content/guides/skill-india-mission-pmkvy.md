## What is PMKVY?

**Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY)** is the flagship skill development scheme of the Government of India, launched under the **Skill India Mission** in 2015. It is managed by the **National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC)** under the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship (MSDE).

The scheme aims to provide **free, industry-relevant skill training** to Indian youth so they can earn a livelihood and contribute to the economy. Since its launch, PMKVY has trained over 1.4 crore candidates across India.

### Key Features

- **Free training** — No fees for candidates
- **Government certification** — Recognized across industries
- **Placement assistance** — Help with job placement after training
- **Monetary reward** — Candidates receive ₹500–₹8,000 on successful certification
- **Short-term and Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL)** courses available
- **Available in 700+ districts** across India

> **Current Phase:** PMKVY 4.0 was launched in 2023 with a focus on new-age skills like AI, IoT, drones, coding, and green jobs alongside traditional trades.

## Who is Eligible?

PMKVY is open to a wide range of candidates. Here are the eligibility criteria:

- **Indian citizen** (must have [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- **Age:** 15–45 years (varies by course)
- **Education:** No strict minimum for many courses; Class 10 or 12 required for some technical courses
- **Unemployed or school/college dropouts** are specifically encouraged
- **No income limit** — open to all economic backgrounds

### Who Should Apply?

- School or college dropouts looking for a career
- Youth seeking employment-ready skills
- Workers wanting to upgrade their existing skills (RPL component)
- Anyone interested in learning a trade or technical skill for free

> **Note:** Prior training under PMKVY does not disqualify you from enrolling in a different course under the scheme.

## Courses Available

PMKVY offers training in **40+ sectors** with **600+ job roles**. Courses are aligned with the **National Skills Qualification Framework (NSQF)** and designed with industry input.

### Popular Sectors and Courses

### Types of Training

1. **Short-Term Training (STT):** 200–600 hours (2–6 months). For fresh candidates with no prior skills.
2. **Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL):** 12–80 hours. For people who already have a skill but no formal certification.
3. **Special Projects:** Customized training for specific regions, communities, or industries.

## How to Enroll

### Step 1: Visit Skill India Digital Portal

Go to [skillindiadigital.gov.in](https://www.skillindiadigital.gov.in) and create an account using your mobile number and Aadhaar.

### Step 2: Search for Courses

Browse available courses by sector, location, or keyword. Filter by your district to find nearby training centers.

### Step 3: Choose a Training Center

Select a **PMKVY-affiliated training center** near you. Each center listing shows the course duration, timings, and batch start dates.

### Step 4: Visit the Center with Documents

Go to the training center with the following documents:
- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
- Recent passport-size photographs
- Education certificates (if applicable)
- [Bank account details](/guide/jan-dhan-yojana-bank-account) (for receiving monetary reward)

### Step 5: Complete Enrollment

The training center will verify your documents, complete biometric verification, and enroll you in the batch.

> **Tip:** You can also walk into any PMKVY training center directly. Online registration is not mandatory but helps you find centers faster.

## Training Centers

PMKVY training is delivered through **NSDC-affiliated training centers** across India. These are called **Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Kendras (PMKKs)** and other affiliated centers.

### How to Find a Training Center

1. Visit [skillindiadigital.gov.in](https://www.skillindiadigital.gov.in)
2. Use the "Find a Training Center" feature
3. Search by state, district, or pincode
4. You can also call the **Skill India helpline: 1800-123-9626** (toll-free)

### What to Expect at the Center

- **Trained instructors** with industry experience
- **Practical labs** and equipment for hands-on learning
- **Classroom sessions** covering theory and soft skills
- **Regular assessments** to track progress
- **Placement cell** to help with job search after training

> **Important:** Only enroll at centers listed on the official Skill India portal. Beware of fake centers claiming to offer PMKVY training.

## Certification

After completing the training, candidates must pass an **assessment conducted by SSC-empanelled assessment agencies**. The assessment includes:

- **Theory test** — Written or computer-based
- **Practical test** — Hands-on demonstration of skills learned

### After Passing

- You receive an **NSQF-aligned certificate** recognized by the Government of India
- Certificate is digitally verifiable on the Skill India Digital portal
- Many employers accept PMKVY certificates for entry-level positions
- Certificate helps in applying for [government jobs](/guide/sarkari-naukri-apply-online) and apprenticeships

### If You Don't Pass

- You can retake the assessment (check with your training center for the next assessment date)
- Additional coaching may be provided by the center before reassessment

## Financial Support

PMKVY provides several forms of financial support to candidates:

### Monetary Reward

- Candidates who pass the assessment receive **₹500 per month** during training (for select courses)
- A **one-time reward of up to ₹8,000** is given on successful certification
- Amount varies based on course type and duration

### Other Benefits

- **Free training** — No tuition fees, no hidden charges
- **Free study material** and uniform (at most centers)
- **Boarding and lodging support** for candidates from remote areas (in special projects)
- **Insurance coverage** of ₹2 lakh under Skill India for 3 years after certification

### How Payment is Made

- Monetary rewards are transferred directly to your **bank account via DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer)**
- You need a bank account linked to Aadhaar — consider opening a [Jan Dhan account](/guide/jan-dhan-yojana-bank-account) if you don't have one

## Important Tips

1. **Always verify the training center** on the official Skill India portal before enrolling. Many fake centers operate under the PMKVY name.

2. **Keep your Aadhaar updated** — Your biometric and demographic details must match. [Update your Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-address-change-online) if needed.

3. **Attend all classes** — Regular attendance is mandatory. Most centers require 80%+ attendance to be eligible for assessment.

4. **Prepare for the assessment seriously** — Your certification and monetary reward depend on passing the final assessment.

5. **Ask about placement support** — Good training centers actively help with job placement. Ask about their placement record before enrolling.

6. **Explore RPL if you already have skills** — If you're already working in a trade (like plumbing, tailoring, etc.), the RPL component can get you a government-recognized certificate in just a few days.

7. **Check for state-specific add-on benefits** — Some states offer additional incentives on top of PMKVY benefits.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Is PMKVY training really free?

Yes, PMKVY training is completely free for candidates. The government bears all training costs. If any center asks you to pay fees, it is likely not a genuine PMKVY center. Report such cases to the Skill India helpline (1800-123-9626).

### Can I do PMKVY after graduation?

Yes. There is no upper education limit. Graduates, postgraduates, and even working professionals can enroll in PMKVY courses. The RPL component is especially useful for those already employed who want formal certification.

### What is the difference between PMKVY and ITI?

**ITI (Industrial Training Institute)** offers longer diploma courses (1–2 years) with a focus on manufacturing and engineering trades. **PMKVY** offers shorter courses (2–6 months) across a wider range of sectors. ITI requires formal admission and exams; PMKVY enrollment is simpler and training is free.

### Will I get a job after PMKVY training?

PMKVY training centers provide **placement assistance**, but a job is not guaranteed. Your chances depend on the course chosen, your performance, and local job market demand. Courses in IT, healthcare, and beauty & wellness tend to have better placement rates.

### Can I enroll in more than one PMKVY course?

Yes, you can enroll in a different course after completing one. However, you cannot enroll in two courses simultaneously. Each course must be completed (or formally withdrawn from) before starting another.

### Is PMKVY certificate valid for government jobs?

PMKVY certificates are NSQF-aligned and recognized by the Government of India. While they are valid credentials, specific government job eligibility depends on the job's requirements. The certificate is most useful for skill-based and contractual government positions.

### How long does the PMKVY course take?

Short-Term Training courses range from **2 to 6 months** (200–600 hours). RPL courses are much shorter — typically **12 to 80 hours** spread over a few days to a couple of weeks.
