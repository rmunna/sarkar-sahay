# UP Bhulekh — Check Land Records, Khasra Khatauni Online (2026)

UP Bhulekh (यूपी भूलेख) is the official online portal of the Uttar Pradesh Revenue Department for accessing digitised land records. Through **upbhulekh.gov.in**, anyone can view Khasra (plot-level record), Khatauni (ownership record), and other revenue documents — free of cost.

> See our [land records guide](/guide/land-records-bhulekh-online) for general information about checking Bhulekh records across Indian states.

---

## What Is UP Bhulekh?

**Bhulekh** (भूलेख) literally means "land record." The UP Bhulekh portal digitises the records maintained by Tehsil-level Lekhpals (revenue officials) across all 75 districts of Uttar Pradesh. Key records available include:

| Record | Hindi Name | What It Shows |
|--------|-----------|---------------|
| **Khasra** | खसरा | Plot-level details — area, crop, land type, survey number |
| **Khatauni** | खतौनी | Ownership record — names of all co-owners, share details |
| **Khasra Map (Naksha)** | खसरा नक्शा | Plot boundary map from revenue survey |

These records are essential for property verification, bank loans, mutation, sale deeds, and government scheme eligibility.

## How to Check Khasra Khatauni Online

### Step 1: Visit the Portal

Go to **[upbhulekh.gov.in](https://upbhulekh.gov.in)**. The homepage shows several options. Click **"खतौनी (अधिकार अभिलेख) की नकल देखें"** (View copy of Khatauni).

### Step 2: Enter the Captcha

A captcha code will appear. Type it correctly and click **Submit**.

### Step 3: Select Your Location

Choose the following from the dropdown menus:

1. **जनपद (District)** — e.g., Lucknow, Varanasi, Agra
2. **तहसील (Tehsil)** — sub-district within the selected district
3. **ग्राम (Village)** — type the first letter to search, or browse the list

### Step 4: Search the Record

You can search by any of these methods:

- **खसरा/गाटा संख्या (Khasra/Gata Number)** — Enter the plot/survey number
- **खाता संख्या (Khata Number)** — Enter the account number
- **खातेदार का नाम (Owner's Name)** — Type the owner's name (in Hindi)
- **नामांतरण दिनांक (Mutation Date)** — Search by date of mutation

### Step 5: View and Download

Click **"उद्धरण देखें"** (View Extract). The Khatauni will display with:

- Owner name(s) and father's/husband's name
- Khasra/Gata number(s) linked to the Khata
- Total area (in hectare/bigha)
- Land type (agricultural, residential, etc.)
- Remarks and mutation history

You can **print the page** or **save as PDF** using your browser's print function (Ctrl+P).

## Search by Owner Name (नाम से खोजें)

If you don't know the Gata or Khata number:

1. Select District → Tehsil → Village
2. Choose **"खातेदार के नाम द्वारा खोजें"**
3. Type the owner's name in **Hindi (Devanagari script)**
4. Select the matching name from the suggestions
5. Click to view the full Khatauni

> **Tip:** Use a Hindi typing tool or Google Input Tools if your keyboard doesn't support Devanagari.

## How to View Khasra Map (भू-नक्शा)

The plot map is available on a separate portal — **UP Bhu Naksha**.

1. Visit **[upbhunaksha.gov.in](https://upbhunaksha.gov.in)**
2. Select **District → Tehsil → RI Circle → Village** from the top panel
3. The village map will load showing all plots
4. **Click on a plot** or enter the **Khasra number** in the search box
5. The plot will be highlighted, showing area, owner name, and land type
6. Click **"Map Report"** or **"Naksha"** to download a PDF of the plot map

This map is useful for verifying plot boundaries, identifying adjacent plots, and cross-checking area with the Khatauni record.

## Revenue Village Directory Lookup

To find the correct village code or check which Tehsil a village falls under:

1. On the Bhulekh homepage, click **"राजस्व ग्राम खतौनी का कोड जानें"**
2. Select District and Tehsil
3. Browse or search the village list
4. The village name along with its **code number** will be displayed

This is helpful when villages have similar names across Tehsils.

## How to Get a Certified Copy (प्रमाणित प्रति)

The online Khatauni from Bhulekh is **for information only** — it is not a certified/legal copy. To get an officially certified copy:

### Option 1: Visit the Tehsil Office
- Apply at the **Tehsil/Revenue office** with a written application
- Pay the prescribed fee (₹10–₹25 per page)
- The Tehsildar or Naib Tehsildar will issue a certified copy with an official stamp

### Option 2: Through CSC (Common Service Centre)
- Visit your nearest **Jan Seva Kendra / CSC centre**
- Request a certified Khatauni copy
- Fee: ₹20–₹50 (varies by centre)

### Option 3: e-District Portal
- Visit **[edistrict.up.gov.in](https://edistrict.up.gov.in)**
- Apply online for a certified copy of land records
- Track your application status online

## Mutation / Dakhil Kharij (दाखिल खारिज) Process

**Dakhil Kharij** (mutation) is the process of updating the revenue record when land ownership changes — after a sale, inheritance, gift, or partition.

### How to Apply for Mutation in UP

1. **Register the sale deed** at the Sub-Registrar Office (via [igrsup.gov.in](https://igrsup.gov.in))
2. **Apply for mutation** at the Tehsil office or online through the Revenue Court portal
3. Submit supporting documents:
   - Registered sale deed / succession certificate / will
   - Aadhaar card of new owner
   - Previous Khatauni copy
   - Death certificate (for inheritance cases)
4. The **Lekhpal** will verify the documents and prepare a report
5. The **SDM / Tehsildar** will pass the mutation order after hearing
6. The updated Khatauni will reflect on UP Bhulekh within a few weeks

### Check Mutation Status

- On the Bhulekh portal, use the **"नामांतरण दिनांक से खोजें"** option to search records by mutation date
- You can also check at the Tehsil office or through the Revenue Court computerisation system

## Common Issues and Troubleshooting

### "Village name not showing"
- Ensure you selected the correct District and Tehsil first
- Type the **first Hindi letter** of the village name to filter
- Some newly created villages may not be listed yet

### "Captcha not loading or failing"
- Refresh the page and try again
- Clear your browser cache
- Try a different browser (Chrome/Firefox recommended)

### "Record not found for my Gata number"
- Double-check the Gata number from your physical record or previous Khatauni
- The number may have changed after consolidation (चकबंदी)
- Visit the Tehsil office to verify the current Gata number

### "Website is slow or not opening"
- The portal experiences heavy traffic, especially during office hours
- Try accessing early morning (before 9 AM) or late evening
- Use a stable internet connection

### "Owner name doesn't match"
- Name may be spelled differently in Hindi on the record
- Try partial name search or search by Gata number instead
- For recently mutated records, the update may still be pending

### "Khatauni shows old owner after sale"
- Mutation (Dakhil Kharij) must be completed separately after registration
- Check if the mutation application has been filed and processed
- Follow up at the Tehsil office

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is the UP Bhulekh record legally valid?**
A: The online copy is for **informational purposes only**. For legal or official use, obtain a certified copy from the Tehsil office.

**Q: Can I check records for any district in UP?**
A: Yes, UP Bhulekh covers all **75 districts** of Uttar Pradesh.

**Q: Is there any fee to check records online?**
A: No, viewing records on upbhulekh.gov.in is **completely free**.

**Q: How often are records updated?**
A: Records are updated regularly as mutations are processed. However, there can be a delay of a few weeks between the mutation order and the online update.

**Q: Can I check records in English?**
A: The portal is primarily in **Hindi**. Records are maintained in Hindi/Devanagari script.
