# Delhi Government Schemes and Services – Complete Guide

Delhi, India's National Capital Territory, offers a wide range of government schemes and services for its nearly 2 crore residents. From subsidised electricity and water to free healthcare at Mohalla Clinics, doorstep delivery of government documents, and robust public transport subsidies — Delhi's welfare ecosystem is among the most comprehensive in India. This guide covers every major Delhi government scheme with eligibility, benefits, and how to apply.

## Key Government Portals in Delhi

Before applying for any scheme, bookmark these essential portals:

- **Delhi Government Official Website** – [delhi.gov.in](https://delhi.gov.in/) – Central hub for all departments and notifications.
- **e-District Delhi** – [edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in](https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in/) – Online portal for certificates (caste, income, domicile), pension, and other services.
- **Delhi Doorstep Delivery** – [delhidoorstepdelivery.com](https://delhidoorstepdelivery.com/) – Get 100+ government services delivered at your doorstep for ₹50. Read our [complete guide to Delhi Doorstep Delivery Services](/guide/delhi-doorstep-delivery-services).
- **Delhi Jal Board** – [delhijalboard.delhi.gov.in](https://delhijalboard.delhi.gov.in/) – Water supply, new connections, and billing.
- **BSES / Tata Power-DDL** – Electricity distribution portals for billing, new connections, and subsidy registration.

## Women and Social Welfare Schemes

### Delhi Ladli Yojana

One of Delhi's longest-running schemes for the girl child, Ladli Yojana provides financial assistance to families upon the birth of a girl and at key education milestones. The accumulated amount (up to ₹1 lakh) can be claimed by the girl after turning 18.

**Key benefits:**
- ₹11,000 at birth (institutional delivery) or ₹10,000 (home delivery)
- ₹5,000 each at admission to Class 1, 6, 9, and 12
- Maturity amount available after age 18 for higher education or marriage

📖 Read the complete guide: [Delhi Ladli Yojana](/guide/delhi-ladli-yojana)

### Widow Pension Scheme

Women who have lost their spouse and belong to economically weaker sections can receive a monthly pension of ₹2,500 under the Delhi Widow Pension Scheme, applied through e-District portal.

### Old Age Pension Scheme

Senior citizens aged 60+ with annual income below ₹1 lakh can receive ₹2,000–₹2,500 per month. Applied through e-District Delhi.

### Disability Pension Scheme

Persons with 40%+ disability can apply for a monthly pension of ₹2,500 through the e-District portal.

## Education and Scholarship Schemes

### Delhi Government Schools – Free Quality Education

Delhi's government schools offer free education with modern infrastructure, smart classrooms, and a focus on holistic development including the Happiness Curriculum and Entrepreneurship Mindset Curriculum.

### Business Blasters Programme

An entrepreneurship programme for students of Delhi government schools where teams receive seed funding of ₹2,000 each to develop and pitch business ideas. The programme has supported thousands of student startups.

### Merit-cum-Means Scholarship

Higher education scholarships for students from economically weaker families studying in Delhi's colleges. Applied through the Directorate of Higher Education.

### Fee Reimbursement for SC/ST Students

Students from Scheduled Caste/Tribe communities studying in recognized institutions can get full fee reimbursement from the Delhi SC/ST Welfare Department.

## Health Schemes

### Mohalla Clinics (Aam Aadmi Mohalla Clinics)

Delhi's flagship primary healthcare initiative, Mohalla Clinics provide free OPD consultations, diagnostics (blood tests, X-rays), and medicines at neighbourhood level. Over 500 clinics operate across Delhi.

**Key benefits:**
- Completely free – no registration or consultation fees
- 212+ free diagnostic tests
- Free medicines dispensed on-site
- No referral needed – walk-in service

Also see: [Ayushman Bharat vs State Health Schemes](/guide/ayushman-bharat-vs-state-health-schemes) for comparing Delhi's health coverage with national schemes.

### Delhi Arogya Kosh (DAK)

A health fund providing financial assistance up to ₹5 lakh for serious illness treatment at empanelled hospitals for Delhi residents from economically weaker sections.

### Free Treatment at Government Hospitals

Delhi has an extensive network of government hospitals (LNJP, GTB, DDU, Deen Dayal Upadhyay, Sanjay Gandhi Memorial) offering free treatment including surgeries, dialysis, and cancer care.

## Food and Ration Services

### Delhi Ration Card and e-Coupon System

Delhi's Public Distribution System provides subsidised food grains (wheat, rice, sugar) to ration card holders. The e-Coupon system has digitised the distribution process.

**Key benefits:**
- 5 kg food grains per person per month under NFSA
- Digital e-Coupon for transparent distribution
- Apply online through the Food & Supplies Department portal

## Transport and Travel Schemes

### Free Bus Ride for Women

Women can travel for free on all DTC and cluster buses in Delhi. No registration or pass is required — simply board the bus.

**Key benefits:**
- Unlimited free rides on DTC and cluster buses
- No special card or registration needed
- Available to all women regardless of income

📖 Read our guide: [Delhi Free Bus Ride for Women](/guide/delhi-free-bus-ride-women)

### Mukhyamantri Tirth Yatra Yojana

A pilgrimage scheme for senior citizens of Delhi providing free travel (by train/air) to religious destinations across India. Beneficiaries aged 60+ get fully sponsored trips to destinations like Ayodhya, Mathura, Haridwar, Ajmer, Amritsar, and more.

**Key benefits:**
- Free travel and accommodation for senior citizens
- Covers multiple religious destinations across India
- One attendant can accompany if beneficiary is 70+

### Delhi Metro and Bus Smart Card

Delhi has an integrated smart card system for Metro and buses, with discounts for students and senior citizens.

## Utilities – Electricity and Water

### Free Electricity Scheme (Up to 200 Units)

Delhi residents consuming up to 200 units of electricity per billing cycle get a zero bill. Those consuming 201–400 units get a 50% subsidy.

**Key benefits:**
- 0–200 units: Completely free (100% subsidy)
- 201–400 units: 50% subsidy on total bill
- Applied automatically – no separate registration needed for BSES/Tata Power-DDL consumers
- Subsidy credited directly to electricity bill

### Free Water Scheme (Up to 20 KL)

Households with a functional water meter consuming up to 20,000 litres (20 KL) per month get free water supply from Delhi Jal Board.

**Key benefits:**
- Up to 20 KL per month: Completely free
- Metered connections only
- Automatically applied – no separate registration

### Delhi Jal Board – Sewer and Water Connections

Residents can apply for new water connections, sewer connections, and resolve billing disputes through the Delhi Jal Board portal. Subsidised rates apply for EWS and LIG categories.

## Housing Schemes

### Delhi Urban Shelter Improvement Board (DUSIB)

DUSIB manages housing for economically weaker sections, including night shelters (rain basera) and in-situ slum rehabilitation. EWS flats are allotted through periodic draws.

### Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (Urban) – Delhi

Eligible Delhi residents from EWS/LIG/MIG categories can get interest subsidy on home loans ranging from 3% to 6.5% under PMAY-Urban. Applied through banks and housing finance companies.

### Jahan Jhuggi Wahin Makaan

An in-situ rehabilitation policy for jhuggi clusters, aiming to provide permanent housing to slum dwellers at the same location.

## Jobs and Competitive Exams

### Delhi Ek Parivar Ek Naukri Yojana

A scheme aimed at providing at least one government job per family in Delhi through direct recruitment to various Delhi government departments.

DSSSB conducts recruitment exams for Group B and C posts in Delhi government departments — teachers, clerks, JEs, and more. Check [dsssb.delhi.gov.in](https://dsssb.delhi.gov.in/) for latest notifications.

### Delhi Police Constable Exam

One of the most sought-after exams for Delhi youth. Conducted by SSC for Delhi Police recruitment.

### Rozgar Bazaar

Delhi Government's job portal connecting job seekers with employers. Candidates can register, upload resumes, and apply for private sector jobs across Delhi.

## Startup and Business Schemes

### Delhi Startup Policy

The Delhi Startup Policy supports entrepreneurs with financial incentives, mentorship, incubation support, and collateral-free loans. It aims to establish Delhi as a leading startup hub.

**Key benefits:**
- Seed funding and collateral-free loans for eligible startups
- Incubation support through Delhi government-backed incubators
- Reimbursement of patent filing costs
- Mentorship and networking opportunities

### Dilli Bazaar

An e-commerce marketplace by the Delhi government to help local artisans, weavers, and small businesses sell products online without middlemen.

## Certificates and Documents via e-District

The e-District Delhi portal offers online application for a wide range of certificates and documents:

- **Income Certificate** – Required for scholarships, subsidies, and fee waivers
- **Caste Certificate** (SC/ST/OBC) – Required for reservations and government benefits
- **Domicile Certificate** – Proof of Delhi residency
- **Marriage Certificate** – Applied through SDM office or online
- **Birth and Death Certificate** – Through MCD or e-District
- **Surviving Member Certificate** – Required for pension and insurance claims

Most of these can also be availed through [Delhi Doorstep Delivery Services](/guide/delhi-doorstep-delivery-services) for just ₹50.

You'll also need your [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) for most applications.

## How to Apply for Delhi Government Schemes

1. **Online via e-District** – Visit [edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in](https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in/), register, and apply for certificates, pensions, and welfare schemes.
2. **Doorstep Delivery** – Call **1076** to avail 100+ services at your doorstep for ₹50. See our [Doorstep Delivery guide](/guide/delhi-doorstep-delivery-services).
3. **SDM/Tehsil Office** – Visit your nearest Sub-Divisional Magistrate office for certificates and verifications.
4. **CSC/Atal Seva Kendra** – Common Service Centres in every ward help with online applications.
5. **Department-Specific Portals** – Some schemes require application through specific department websites (e.g., Food & Supplies for ration card, DJB for water connection).

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How do I check if I am eligible for free electricity in Delhi?

If your electricity consumption is 200 units or less per billing cycle, you automatically get a zero bill. No separate registration is needed — the subsidy is applied directly by your electricity provider (BSES or Tata Power-DDL).

### How can I apply for a ration card in Delhi?

You can apply online through the Delhi Food & Supplies Department portal or use the [Doorstep Delivery service](/guide/delhi-doorstep-delivery-services) by calling 1076. You'll need Aadhaar, address proof, and family details. Read our [Delhi Ration Card e-Coupon guide](/guide/delhi-ration-card-e-coupon).

### Are Mohalla Clinics completely free?

Yes. Mohalla Clinics offer free OPD consultations, over 200 diagnostic tests, and medicines at no cost. No registration or referral is needed — simply walk in to the nearest clinic.

### How do I avail free bus rides for women in Delhi?

No registration or special pass is required. Women can simply board any DTC or cluster bus in Delhi and travel for free. Read our [detailed guide](/guide/delhi-free-bus-ride-women).

### Who is eligible for the Old Age Pension in Delhi?

Delhi residents aged 60 years and above with annual family income below ₹1 lakh can apply through the e-District portal. The pension amount ranges from ₹2,000 to ₹2,500 per month.

### How do I get a caste or income certificate in Delhi?

Apply online through [e-District Delhi](https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in/) or use [Doorstep Delivery](/guide/delhi-doorstep-delivery-services) (call 1076). Required documents include Aadhaar, address proof, and supporting documents. You can also visit your nearest SDM office.
