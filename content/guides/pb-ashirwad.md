## What is the Ashirwad Scheme (Punjab)?

The Ashirwad Scheme (also known as Shagun Scheme) is a marriage assistance programme by the Government of Punjab that provides **financial aid** to economically weaker families for the marriage of their daughters. The scheme specifically targets SC, BC, minority, and BPL families.

### Benefits

| Category | Assistance Amount |
|----------|------------------|
| SC/BC/Minority families (BPL) | ₹21,000 |
| Widows' daughters (any category) | ₹21,000 |
| Widows themselves (remarriage) | ₹21,000 |
| Daughters of registered construction workers | ₹31,000 |

> The Punjab government has periodically revised amounts. Recent proposals suggest increasing to ₹51,000. Check the latest notification.

## Who is Eligible?

- Family must belong to **SC, BC, or Minority community** and be BPL
- The **bride must be 18 years or above**
- The **groom must be 21 years or above**
- Family annual income must not exceed **₹32,790** (BPL threshold) — or as revised
- Must be a **resident of Punjab**
- Widows and their daughters are eligible regardless of caste (subject to income limit)
- The application must be submitted within **6 months** of the marriage

## Documents Required

- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) of bride and groom
- Marriage certificate / proof of marriage
- Caste certificate (SC/BC/Minority)
- BPL certificate or income certificate
- Age proof of bride (18+) and groom (21+)
- Bank passbook of the bride
- Passport-size photographs
- Father's/husband's death certificate (for widow category)
- Residence proof of Punjab
- Self-declaration of first marriage (or remarriage for widows)

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Online Application

1. **Visit** [Punjab Shagun Scheme portal](https://pbshagunscheme.punjab.gov.in)
2. **Register** with mobile number
3. **Fill** the application form with marriage details, family, caste, income
4. **Upload** documents — marriage certificate, caste certificate, income proof, Aadhaar, bank details
5. **Submit** and note application number
6. **Track** status online

### Offline Application

1. **Visit** the District Social Welfare Office or DC office
2. **Collect** the Ashirwad/Shagun scheme application form
3. **Fill** and attach all documents
4. **Submit** to the Social Welfare Officer
5. **Collect** acknowledgement receipt

## Processing Time

- Verification: **1-2 months**
- Disbursement: **2-4 months** after approval
- Amount credited to bride's bank account via DBT

## Fees

**No fees** for application.

## Important Tips

1. **Apply within 6 months** — Late applications may be rejected
2. **Register the marriage** — Marriage certificate is essential for the application
3. **Bride's bank account** — Amount is transferred to the bride's account; ensure it's active and Aadhaar-linked
4. **Multiple daughters** — Check if the scheme allows benefits for more than one daughter
5. **Construction workers** — If registered with the Punjab Building Workers Welfare Board, you may get a higher amount

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Can general category families apply?
The scheme is primarily for **SC, BC, and Minority BPL families**. However, widows' daughters from any category are eligible.

### Q2. Can I apply after the marriage?
Yes. You must apply **within 6 months** of the marriage date.

### Q3. Is the money given before or after marriage?
The financial assistance is disbursed **after the marriage** and submission of the marriage certificate.

### Q4. Can the groom's family apply?
No. The application is from the **bride's family** or the bride herself.

### Q5. What if the marriage is inter-state?
The bride must be a **resident of Punjab**. The groom can be from any state.

### Q6. Is second marriage eligible?
**Widow remarriage** is eligible. However, the scheme generally covers first marriages for daughters.
