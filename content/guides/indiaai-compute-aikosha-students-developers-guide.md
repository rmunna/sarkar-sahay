# IndiaAI Compute and AIKosha Guide for Students and Developers

IndiaAI is becoming the core government-backed AI ecosystem for students, researchers, startups, MSMEs and public institutions. Two high-value parts are IndiaAI Compute, for access to cloud AI infrastructure, and AIKosha, for datasets, models and development resources.

This hub is for students, founders, developers and academic teams who want to know what to apply for and how to avoid wasting time.

## IndiaAI Compute: Who It Is For

The official compute portal lists eligibility categories such as:

- Researchers and academia
- IndiaAI fellowship students
- Students with AI/ML projects
- Startups and MSMEs
- Early-stage startups and researchers
- Government entities

The exact document requirements depend on the category. Do not assume every student automatically gets GPU access. Prepare a project proposal and proof of eligibility before applying.

## Basic Application Flow

1. Open the IndiaAI Compute Portal.
2. Login using the supported identity flow.
3. Complete end-user registration.
4. Upload eligibility documents.
5. Submit a compute request with project details.
6. Review the draft bill or subsidy information.
7. Wait for verification or approval.

The portal indicates that smaller requests may follow a simpler approval path, while larger requests may need committee review. Always check the current portal instructions before applying.

## AIKosha: What Developers Can Use

AIKosha is positioned as a shared resource platform for:

- AI datasets
- AI models
- Development tools
- Sector-specific resources including health, agriculture and education

Good starter use cases:

| User | Useful project |
|---|---|
| Student | Build a multilingual FAQ bot using public datasets |
| Startup | Prototype an India-specific classifier or assistant |
| Researcher | Compare model behavior across Indian language data |
| NGO | Build a citizen-service helper with verified official content |

## Common Mistakes

- Applying for compute without a project plan
- Treating AIKosha data as automatically production-ready
- Ignoring privacy and consent obligations
- Building apps that ask citizens for OTPs or passwords
- Using government logos or names in a way that suggests official endorsement

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is IndiaAI Compute free?

The portal is designed to provide affordable and subsidized access, but users may still have payment obligations depending on approval, usage and subsidy terms.

### Can students apply?

Yes, the official portal lists student AI/ML projects and IndiaAI fellowship students among eligible categories, subject to criteria and documents.

### Is AIKosha only for government users?

No. It is meant as a shared AI resource platform, but access and permitted use depend on dataset/model terms and platform rules.

### Can I use IndiaAI resources for a commercial startup?

Startups and MSMEs are listed among eligible categories, but you must satisfy the portal criteria and comply with usage terms.
