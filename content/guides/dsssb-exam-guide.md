# DSSSB Exam Guide 2025: Complete Information

The **Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB)** conducts recruitment examinations for various Group B and Group C posts under the Government of NCT of Delhi. DSSSB is one of the most sought-after recruitment bodies for aspirants looking to work in Delhi's government departments, schools, hospitals, and municipal bodies.

## What is DSSSB?

DSSSB was established to recruit staff for various departments and local bodies of the Government of Delhi. It conducts examinations for posts like **Teaching**, **Clerk**, **Junior Engineer**, **Pharmacist**, **Patwari**, **Assistant Teacher (PRT/TGT/PGT)**, **Lab Technician**, and many more across departments like MCD, DJB, NDMC, Education, Health, and Revenue.

## DSSSB Eligibility Criteria

### Age Limit
- **Minimum Age:** 18 years
- **Maximum Age:** 27 years (varies by post; some posts allow up to 30 or 32 years)
- **Age Relaxation:** OBC: 3 years, SC/ST: 5 years, PwBD: 10 years, Ex-servicemen: as per rules

### Educational Qualification
- Varies by post:
  - **Grade IV/MTS:** 10th Pass
  - **Clerk/LDC:** 12th Pass with typing speed
  - **Teaching (PRT):** Graduate with D.El.Ed/B.Ed + CTET
  - **TGT:** Graduate with B.Ed + CTET
  - **PGT:** Post Graduate with B.Ed
  - **Junior Engineer:** Diploma/B.Tech in relevant discipline
  - **Pharmacist:** D.Pharm/B.Pharm

### Nationality
- Indian citizen (domicile of Delhi not mandatory for most posts)

## DSSSB Exam Pattern

### Tier 1 (One-tier for most posts)
| Section | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| General Awareness | 20 | 20 |
| General Intelligence & Reasoning | 20 | 20 |
| Arithmetical & Numerical Ability | 20 | 20 |
| Hindi Language & Comprehension | 20 | 20 |
| English Language & Comprehension | 20 | 20 |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** |

For teaching posts, an additional section on the concerned subject is included (200 marks total).

### Marking Scheme
- Each correct answer: 1 mark
- Negative marking: 0.25 marks deducted for wrong answers
- Duration: 2 hours (most posts)

## DSSSB Syllabus

### General Awareness
- Current affairs (India & Delhi specific)
- Indian history, geography, polity
- Indian economy
- Science & technology
- Delhi-specific GK (history, culture, governance)

### General Intelligence & Reasoning
- Analogies, coding-decoding, series
- Blood relations, direction sense
- Syllogism, Venn diagrams
- Non-verbal reasoning

### Arithmetical & Numerical Ability
- Number system, simplification
- Percentage, ratio & proportion
- Profit & loss, SI/CI
- Time, speed & distance
- Data interpretation

### Hindi Language
- Grammar (संधि, समास, मुहावरे)
- Comprehension passage
- Vocabulary & antonyms/synonyms

### English Language
- Grammar (articles, tenses, voice)
- Reading comprehension
- Vocabulary, idioms & phrases
- Error spotting, fill in the blanks

## DSSSB Preparation Strategy

### 1. Know Your Syllabus
Start by downloading the official syllabus for your specific post from the DSSSB website. Different posts have different subjects.

### 2. Focus on Delhi-Specific GK
DSSSB gives extra weightage to Delhi-related questions — Delhi's history, monuments, governance structure, recent policies, and schemes.

### 3. Practice Previous Year Papers
DSSSB tends to repeat question patterns. Solve at least 5 years of previous papers.

### 4. Time Management
With 100 questions in 2 hours, you get about 72 seconds per question. Practice with a timer.

### 5. Mock Tests
Take full-length mock tests weekly. Analyze your performance and work on weak areas.

### 6. For Teaching Posts
If applying for PRT/TGT/PGT, focus heavily on pedagogy, child development, and your subject expertise. CTET qualification is mandatory for PRT and TGT.

## Important DSSSB Recruitment Cycles

DSSSB releases multiple advertisements throughout the year for different posts. Key recruitment cycles include:
- **Teaching posts** (PRT, TGT, PGT) — largest recruitment, often 5000+ vacancies
- **Clerical posts** (LDC, Patwari) — regular recruitment
- **Technical posts** (JE, Pharmacist, Lab Tech) — periodic recruitment
- **Grade IV/MTS** — bulk recruitment

## How to Apply for DSSSB

1. Visit [dsssb.delhi.gov.in](https://dsssb.delhi.gov.in)
2. Click on "Online Registration" or the specific advertisement
3. Create an account and fill in personal details
4. Upload photograph, signature, and required documents
5. Pay the application fee (₹100 for General/OBC; free for SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-SM/Women)
6. Submit and download the confirmation page

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Delhi domicile required for DSSSB exams?
No, Delhi domicile is not required for most DSSSB posts. Any Indian citizen can apply. However, some specific posts under MCD or certain departments may have a domicile preference.

### What is the age limit for DSSSB teaching posts?
For PRT: 18-30 years, TGT: 18-32 years, PGT: 18-32 years. Age relaxation applies for reserved categories as per Delhi government rules.

### How many times can I attempt DSSSB exams?
There is no limit on the number of attempts for DSSSB exams, as long as you meet the age criteria for the specific post.

### Is CTET mandatory for DSSSB teaching posts?
Yes, CTET qualification is mandatory for PRT and TGT posts. For PGT posts, CTET is not required but having it may be advantageous.

### What is the selection process for DSSSB?
For most posts, selection is based on the written exam (one-tier or two-tier). Teaching posts may have an additional skill test or interview. Physical standards apply for posts like fire operator.

### How is DSSSB different from SSC?
SSC recruits for central government posts across India, while DSSSB recruits specifically for Delhi government departments, schools, and local bodies. DSSSB posts are based in Delhi with Delhi government pay scales and benefits.

### When does DSSSB release notifications?
DSSSB releases multiple notifications throughout the year. Major teaching recruitments typically happen annually. Check the official website regularly or follow CitizenNest for updates.
