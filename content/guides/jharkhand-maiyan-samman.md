## What is Maiyan Samman Yojana?

Maiyan Samman Yojana is a welfare scheme launched by the Jharkhand government to provide financial assistance of **₹1,000 per month (₹12,000 per year)** to women in the state. The scheme targets women aged 18-50 years to promote their financial empowerment and independence.

The word "Maiyan" refers to mother/sister in the local dialect, and the scheme recognizes women's contributions to family and society by providing them direct financial support.

## Who is Eligible?

### Eligibility Criteria
- **Women aged 18 to 50 years**
- Must be a **resident of Jharkhand**
- Must hold a valid **ration card** or family identification
- Annual family income should not exceed **₹3,00,000**
- Must have an **Aadhaar-linked bank account**

### Who is NOT Eligible
- Women who are income tax payers
- Government employees (state/central)
- Women receiving pension of ₹1,000+ from state schemes
- Women whose family owns more than the prescribed limit of agricultural land
- Family members of elected representatives

## Benefits

| Component | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Monthly assistance | ₹1,000 |
| Annual total | ₹12,000 |
| Payment mode | Direct Bank Transfer |
| Frequency | Monthly |

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card**
- **Bank account passbook** (in applicant's name, linked with Aadhaar)
- **Ration card** or family identification document
- **Voter ID** or residence proof
- **Age proof** (birth certificate, 10th marksheet, Aadhaar)
- **Caste certificate** (if applicable)
- **Income certificate** (if required)
- **Passport-size photograph**

## How to Apply

### Online Application
1. Visit the official Jharkhand government scheme portal
2. Navigate to the Maiyan Samman Yojana section
3. Enter Aadhaar number and verify through OTP
4. Fill in personal, family, and bank details
5. Upload supporting documents
6. Submit the application and save the reference number

### Offline Application
1. Visit your nearest **Block Development Office (BDO)** or **Gram Panchayat**
2. Collect the application form
3. Fill in all required details
4. Attach photocopies of documents
5. Submit and collect acknowledgment receipt

### Through Camp/Special Drives
- The Jharkhand government organizes special enrollment camps
- Attend camps with all original documents
- Officials assist with application filling and verification on the spot

## Verification and Approval

1. Application reviewed by local authorities
2. Aadhaar and bank account verified electronically
3. Family income and eligibility cross-checked
4. Approved applications receive payment within 30-45 days
5. Rejected applications can file grievance and reapply

## Important Tips

- **Ensure Aadhaar-bank linking** — without this, DBT payments will fail
- **Keep your ration card updated** — it serves as family identification and address proof
- **Apply through official channels** — avoid middlemen and unauthorized agents
- **Check enrollment camps** — these are the easiest way to apply with on-the-spot verification
- **Document readiness** — keep all documents (originals and copies) organized before applying

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can unmarried women apply for Maiyan Samman?
Yes, all women aged 18-50 regardless of marital status (unmarried, married, widowed, divorced) are eligible subject to other criteria.

### Is there a caste-based criteria?
The scheme is for all eligible women regardless of caste. However, SC/ST women may have relaxed income criteria.

### Can I get both Maiyan Samman and central government schemes?
Yes, this is a state scheme. You can simultaneously receive benefits from central schemes like PM-KISAN (if applicable) or other non-overlapping programs.

### How do I check my payment status?
Check on the official portal using your application number or Aadhaar number. You can also check your bank account statement for DBT credits.

### What if my application is rejected?
Check the rejection reason on the portal. Common reasons include wrong bank details, Aadhaar mismatch, or exceeding income limit. Correct the issue and reapply.

### Can women above 50 years get any similar benefit?
Women above 50 may be eligible for Jharkhand's old age pension or widow pension schemes. Check with your local BDO office for available options.
