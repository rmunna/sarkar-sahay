# Union Bank of India Dormant Account Reactivation 2026 — Complete Guide

Union Bank of India (which merged with Andhra Bank and Corporation Bank in 2020) marks accounts dormant after **24 months** of no customer-initiated debit transactions. Here's how to reactivate your Union Bank dormant account, including legacy Andhra/Corporation Bank accounts.

---

## Union Bank Dormant Account — Impact

| Feature | Active | Dormant |
|---------|--------|---------|
| ATM withdrawals | ✅ | ❌ |
| UPI / Debit card | ✅ | ❌ |
| NEFT/IMPS debits | ✅ | ❌ |
| Incoming salary/credit | ✅ | ✅ Continues |
| Balance enquiry | ✅ | ✅ |

---

## Method 1: Vyom Mobile App

1. Open **Union Bank Vyom** app → Login
2. **"Services"** → **"Account Services"** → **"Dormant Account Reactivation"** (if visible)
3. Complete KYC (Aadhaar OTP preferred)
4. Submit request
5. **Processing: 24–72 hours**

---

## Method 2: Union Bank Net Banking

1. Login at **[unionbankonline.co.in](https://www.unionbankonline.co.in)**
2. **"My Account"** → **"Account Services"** → **"Reactivate Dormant Account"**
3. Update KYC if prompted
4. Submit
5. **Processing: 2–5 working days**

---

## Method 3: Branch (Most Reliable)

1. Visit any Union Bank branch
2. Carry: Aadhaar + PAN (originals + copies)
3. Request **"Dormant Account Reactivation Form"**
4. Deposit ₹100–₹500 at counter → Triggers reactivation
5. **Same-day processing**

**Union Bank Customer Care:** **1800-22-2244** (toll-free)

---

## Legacy Andhra Bank / Corporation Bank Accounts

If your account was originally with Andhra Bank or Corporation Bank:
- Dormancy rules are same (24 months)
- Bring old passbook + Aadhaar + PAN to any Union Bank branch
- Branch will verify merger records before processing
- New Union Bank account number was assigned — confirm which number to use
- If you don't know the new account number: Give old Andhra/Corp account number at branch

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Union Bank account dormant — can I reactivate online without visiting branch?**
Try Vyom app first (reactivation option available for some accounts). If not available or if KYC is outdated, branch visit is required with Aadhaar + PAN.

**My Andhra Bank account (now Union Bank) is dormant for 8 years — can I claim it?**
Yes — visit any Union Bank branch. The merger records are maintained. Carry old Andhra Bank passbook + Aadhaar + PAN. Balance is recoverable until 10 years (then DEAF). Even post-DEAF, amount is claimable at branch.

**Union Bank dormant account — how long does branch reactivation take?**
At branch: Same day (2–4 hours). The core banking system updates in real-time. Test with ATM withdrawal by evening.

**DEAF transfer for Union Bank accounts — when does it happen?**
After 10 years of dormancy/unclaimed balance, Union Bank transfers funds to RBI's DEAF Fund. To claim: Visit Union Bank branch → Fill DEAF Claim Form → Recovery takes 3–7 working days.

**Union Bank dormant account with zero balance — will they close it?**
Union Bank may initiate closure for zero-balance dormant accounts after extended periods. Reactivate before this happens. If already closed, a fresh account opening is needed.
