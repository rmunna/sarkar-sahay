# PMFBY Crop Insurance Claim Rejected? Appeal & Fix Guide

If your **PMFBY (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana)** claim was rejected, it doesn't mean you've lost your chance. You have the right to appeal, and many claims are reversed after a review — especially when the rejection reason is procedural or a data mismatch.

## Top Reasons PMFBY Claims Are Rejected

| Reason | What It Means |
|--------|--------------|
| **Late crop loss notification** | You did not notify within 72 hours of crop damage |
| **Non-notified crop/area** | The specific crop or village is not covered under the scheme for that season |
| **Exclusion clause** | Loss caused by excluded perils: fire from burning, theft, war, nuclear risk |
| **Yield above threshold** | Cluster-level yield assessment showed yield above the indemnity trigger |
| **Premium not paid** | Your premium installment wasn't deducted/paid in time for the season |
| **Land not insured** | The specific land parcel was not enrolled under the policy |
| **Bank account mismatch** | Account details changed or Aadhaar not seeded in bank |
| **Duplicate claim** | Another claim was already filed for the same survey number |

## Step 1: Get the Rejection Reason in Writing

**Do this first — you cannot appeal without knowing the exact reason.**

1. Visit **[pmfby.gov.in](https://pmfby.gov.in/)** → Farmer Corner → Application Status
2. Enter your application number or policy number + Aadhaar
3. Check the claim status — if rejected, note the rejection code/reason
4. If the portal doesn't show the reason, contact your **bank branch** (the bank that collected your premium) — they have the rejection details

## Step 2: Fix Based on Rejection Reason

### If rejected for "Late Notification" (72-hour rule violation)

The 72-hour crop damage notification rule is strict, but has exceptions:
- **Localised/post-harvest losses**: These have a **48-hour window** from the loss event, not crop sowing
- **Pestilence/disease**: If the spread was gradual and notification was delayed due to crop identification time, document this and appeal
- **Natural disaster declaration**: If your district was declared a natural disaster zone, the notification window may be relaxed — check with your State Agriculture Department

**Appeal action**: Write to your district Agriculture Officer (DAO) with documentary evidence: photos, local witness statements, newspaper clippings of the calamity.

### If rejected for "Yield Above Threshold" (CCE — Crop Cutting Experiment)

This is the most disputed rejection reason. Insurance companies rely on Crop Cutting Experiment (CCE) data from state governments:

1. **Request the CCE data** for your village cluster (gram panchayat / village code)
2. CCE data is collected by Agriculture department officials; you can challenge it if it seems incorrect
3. File a complaint with the **District Level Monitoring Committee (DLMC)** at your district collectorate
4. If the DLMC dismisses, appeal to **State Level Grievance Redressal Committee**

### If rejected for "Premium Not Paid"

1. Check your bank statement — was the premium deducted at crop season start?
2. If premium was deducted but not passed to insurer: **complaint against your bank** to IRDAI (irdai.gov.in)
3. If premium was never deducted: you may not have been covered for that season (farm enrollment must be done before the deadline each season)

## Step 3: File a Formal Grievance

### Online via PMFBY Portal

1. Go to **[pmfby.gov.in](https://pmfby.gov.in/)** → Farmer Corner → **"File a Complaint"** or **"Grievance"**
2. Enter your policy number, rejection details, and reason for dispute
3. Upload supporting documents (photos, Aadhaar, bank statement, weather data)
4. Note the **grievance registration number**

### Via Crops Insurance Company

Find the insurer from your policy document (typically AIC, Reliance, ICICI Lombard, Bajaj Allianz, or a public sector insurer based on state):
1. Visit the insurer's grievance portal or walk into a district office
2. File a formal appeal within **30 days of rejection**

### Via State Agriculture Department

Your state's District Agriculture Officer (DAO) or Sub-Divisional Agriculture Officer (SDAO) can escalate on your behalf:
1. Submit a written application with your rejection notice
2. Ask for DLMC review
3. DLMC meets periodically to review disputed claims

### Via Insurance Ombudsman

If insurer and DLMC both fail, approach the **Insurance Ombudsman** (free of charge):
- Visit [cioins.co.in](https://cioins.co.in/) to find your nearest Ombudsman
- File complaint within **1 year of rejection**

## Step 4: Document Everything

For any appeal to succeed, gather:
- **Photos of crop damage** (with timestamps)
- **Satellite/weather data** for your district on the date of damage (check IMD or crop monitoring apps)
- **Gram Sabha / Panchayat letter** confirming widespread crop loss
- **Neighbor claims** — if your neighbors' claims were approved for the same event, your rejection is inconsistent

## Timeline for Appeals

| Stage | Deadline |
|-------|---------|
| Initial grievance to insurer | Within 30 days of rejection |
| DLMC hearing | 15 days after referral |
| State committee | 30 days after DLMC |
| Insurance Ombudsman | Within 1 year of rejection |

## Important: Re-Enrollment for Next Season

A claim rejection does **not** automatically remove you from PMFBY. Enroll again for the next crop season before the deadline (typically June 30 for Kharif, December 31 for Rabi) to maintain coverage.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does an appeal take?

DLMC reviews typically take 30–60 days. Insurance Ombudsman cases can take 3–6 months. Priority cases (natural disasters, large farmer groups) may be resolved faster.

### Can I file a claim after crop damage if I didn't report in 72 hours?

For widespread crop damage (flood, cyclone, official drought declaration), state governments often relax the notification requirement and do collective surveys. Contact your DAO immediately — don't self-assume you've missed the window.

### Will my premium be refunded if the claim is rejected?

No, the premium is non-refundable. PMFBY premiums are shared between farmer, state government, and central government. The premium stays with the insurer regardless of claim outcome.

### Can I file PMFBY claim online?

Crop loss notification must be filed online at pmfby.gov.in or via your bank/CSC where you enrolled, within 72 hours of loss. Grievances against rejections are also filed online at pmfby.gov.in → Grievance section.
