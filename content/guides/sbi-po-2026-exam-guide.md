# SBI PO 2026 — Complete Guide to India's Most Prestigious Bank PO Exam

The State Bank of India Probationary Officer (SBI PO) examination is India's most prestigious banking recruitment exam. Conducted directly by SBI (not IBPS), it offers 2,000–2,500 vacancies annually across SBI's vast branch network. An SBI PO earns ₹52,000–₹64,000/month gross and has access to one of the best career growth paths in Indian banking.

## Detailed Syllabus

### Reasoning & Computer Aptitude (Mains)
**Reasoning:** Puzzles (Linear, Circular, Floor, Box), Seating Arrangements, Blood Relations, Coding-Decoding, Syllogism, Input-Output, Data Sufficiency, Critical Reasoning, Logical Reasoning, Direction Sense, Order/Ranking, Verbal Reasoning

**Computer Aptitude:** Computer Fundamentals, Networking basics, MS Office, Internet, Security, DBMS basics, Programming Concepts (no coding)

### Quantitative Aptitude / Data Analysis
**Prelims:** Number Series, Simplification/Approximation, Quadratic Equations, Percentage, Ratio & Proportion, Profit/Loss, SI/CI, Time-Work, Time-Speed-Distance, Averages, Probability, Mensuration

**Mains DI:** Data Interpretation (Bar, Line, Pie, Table, Caselet, Mixed), Data Sufficiency, Quantity Comparison

### English Language
Reading Comprehension (3–4 passages in Mains), Error Detection, Sentence Correction, Para Jumbles, Fill in the Blanks, Cloze Test, Vocabulary in Context, Paragraph/Sentence Connectors, Summary Writing (Mains), Descriptive Writing

### General/Economy/Banking Awareness
- **Banking:** RBI, monetary policy, banking acts, financial inclusion, NPAs, Basel norms, credit/debit schemes
- **Economy:** GDP, inflation, Union Budget, Economic Survey, Five Year Plans (legacy), Make in India, PLI
- **Current Affairs:** Last 6 months — focusing on banking, economy, government schemes, appointments
- **Static Banking GK:** Headquarters of banks, taglines, MD/CEOs, financial regulators

## 4-Month Preparation Strategy

### Weeks 1–4: Foundation
- Quant: Chapters from Number System to Time-Work (NCERT 10 + R.S. Aggarwal)
- Reasoning: Seating arrangement + Puzzles (K. Kundan) — 1.5 hrs daily
- English: Grammar basics + RC practice (SP Bakshi + newspapers)
- GK: Open a daily banking GK app (Oliveboard/Testbook capsule)

### Weeks 5–8: Speed Building
- Full Prelims mocks daily (1 mock + full analysis)
- DI sets: 3 sets per day (bar, pie, caselet rotation)
- Vocabulary: 10 words/day from Word Power Made Easy
- Current affairs: 30 min daily (Hindu/Mint financial section)

### Weeks 9–12: Mains Preparation
- Mains-level puzzles (3+ variables, blood relation combination)
- Data Interpretation: Caselet + Mixed DI daily
- Descriptive: 1 essay + 1 letter per day (practice on paper)
- Banking GK: RBI policies, banking acts, financial year highlights

### Weeks 13–16: Revision + GE Prep
- Full Mains mocks (2 per week)
- Group Exercise: Practice in study group — current economic issues, banking reforms
- Interview prep: HR questions + banking knowledge + current affairs
- Descriptive final review: Review and time yourself

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is SBI PO tougher than IBPS PO?**
Yes — SBI PO is considered harder, especially in Data Interpretation and Reasoning sections. The competition is also intense given SBI's prestige.

**Can I apply for both SBI PO and IBPS PO?**
Yes — they are separate exams. You can and should apply for both to maximise chances.

**How many times can I attempt SBI PO?**
No official attempt limit. You can appear every year as long as you meet the age criteria (21–30 years, with relaxation for reserved categories).

**Is there a bond period after joining SBI as PO?**
Yes — SBI has a service bond. Typically you must serve for 3 years before you can resign or transfer to another organisation.

**What is the posting preference system?**
SBI has a zone-based posting system. You indicate preferences and are allotted a zone/circle based on merit. Transfers within the zone happen every 3–5 years.

**When does SBI PO notification release in 2026?**
Expected April–May 2026. Monitor [bank.sbi/careers](https://bank.sbi/careers) and set a Google Alert for "SBI PO notification 2026".
