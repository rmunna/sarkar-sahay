# Krishi Bhagya Scheme Karnataka – Farm Pond & Irrigation Subsidy

## What is Krishi Bhagya Scheme?

Krishi Bhagya is a Karnataka Agriculture Department scheme aimed at helping **rainfed farmers** in dry and drought-prone areas. The scheme provides **subsidies for farm ponds, polyhouse/shade net structures, drip/sprinkler irrigation, and diesel/electric pump sets**. The goal is to improve water harvesting and irrigation for smallholder farmers who depend on rainfall.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Farmers** with agricultural land in **rainfed/dry areas** of Karnataka
2. **Land Holding:** Small and marginal farmers with land up to **10 acres** get priority
3. Must own the land in their name (RTC — Record of Rights, Tenancy, and Crops must be in applicant's name)
4. **Residency:** Must be a Karnataka farmer with land records in Karnataka
5. Priority to farmers in **drought-prone taluks**

## Components and Subsidy Details

| Component | Subsidy for General | Subsidy for SC/ST |
|-----------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Farm Pond (plastic lining) | 80% of cost | 90% of cost |
| Diesel Pump Set | 50% of cost | 50% of cost |
| Drip/Sprinkler Irrigation | 90% of cost | 90% of cost |
| Shade Net / Polyhouse | 50-75% of cost | 75-90% of cost |

*Note: Exact subsidy percentages and maximum amounts may vary by year. Check the latest government notification.*

## Documents Required

- **RTC (Record of Rights)** — land ownership record
- **Aadhaar Card**
- **Bank Account Passbook**
- **Caste Certificate** (for SC/ST higher subsidy)
- **Land Survey Number Details**
- **Passport Size Photo**
- **Existing borewell/well details** (if any)

## How to Apply for Krishi Bhagya

### Online Application

1. **Visit** [raitamitra.karnataka.gov.in](https://raitamitra.karnataka.gov.in) or the Karnataka Agriculture Department portal
2. **Register** as a farmer using Aadhaar and mobile number
3. **Login** and select **Krishi Bhagya** under schemes
4. **Enter** land details — survey number, extent, taluk, district
5. **Select** the component you need (farm pond, irrigation, etc.)
6. **Upload** RTC, Aadhaar, bank passbook, photo
7. **Submit** the application
8. **Note** the reference number

### Offline Application

1. Visit the **Raitha Samparka Kendra (RSK)** at your taluk
2. Meet the **Assistant Director of Agriculture**
3. Fill the Krishi Bhagya application form
4. Submit all documents
5. The agriculture officer will inspect your land and forward the application

## After Application

1. **Land Inspection:** An agriculture officer visits your farm to verify the land and suitability
2. **Approval:** If eligible, work order is issued
3. **Construction/Installation:** You construct the farm pond or install equipment
4. **Verification:** Officer verifies the completed work
5. **Subsidy Disbursement:** Subsidy amount is credited to your bank account

## Processing Time

- **Application Review:** 15-30 days
- **Land Inspection:** Within 30 days
- **Subsidy Disbursement:** 30-60 days after work completion and verification

## Important Tips

1. **Apply Before Monsoon:** Submit applications well before the rainy season to get the farm pond ready for water harvesting.
2. **SC/ST Get Higher Subsidy:** If you belong to SC/ST category, ensure you submit caste certificate for the enhanced subsidy rate.
3. **Maintain the Farm Pond:** After construction, maintain the pond lining to prevent damage and ensure longevity.
4. **Combine with Other Schemes:** Krishi Bhagya can sometimes be combined with other agriculture schemes for maximum benefit.
5. **Keep Records:** Photograph all construction stages as proof for verification.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: What is the minimum land required?
There is no strict minimum, but having at least **1 acre of rainfed agricultural land** is generally expected.

### Q2: Can I apply if I already have a borewell?
**Yes.** Having a borewell does not disqualify you. Farm ponds supplement water availability.

### Q3: Do I need to pay anything upfront?
**Yes.** You may need to invest the farmer's share (10-50% depending on component) upfront. The subsidy is reimbursed after work completion.

### Q4: Can tenant farmers apply?
Generally, only **land-owning farmers** (with RTC in their name) are eligible. Tenant farmers may face difficulties.

### Q5: Is this available in all districts of Karnataka?
The scheme primarily targets **dry and drought-prone** taluks but is available across Karnataka for rainfed areas.

### Q6: Can I get both a farm pond and drip irrigation under this scheme?
**Yes.** You can apply for multiple components, subject to budget availability.
