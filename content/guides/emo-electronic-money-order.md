# Electronic Money Order (eMO): How to Send Money Online via India Post

The **Electronic Money Order (eMO)** is a digital version of the traditional money order offered by India Post. Instead of visiting a post office, you can send money online through the **epostoffice.gov.in** portal. The amount is delivered to the recipient's doorstep by the postman — no bank account needed for the receiver. This makes eMO ideal for sending money to people in rural areas without bank accounts.

## What is Electronic Money Order (eMO)?

eMO is an online money transfer service by the Department of Posts. The sender books the money order online, and the amount is physically delivered to the recipient's address by the local postman. It combines the convenience of digital booking with the last-mile delivery strength of India Post.

### eMO vs Regular Money Order

| Feature | Regular Money Order | Electronic Money Order (eMO) |
|---|---|---|
| Booking method | At post office counter | Online via epostoffice.gov.in |
| Payment | Cash at counter | Online (net banking, debit card) |
| Delivery | Postman delivers cash | Postman delivers cash |
| Tracking | Limited | Online tracking available |
| Commission | 5% of amount | 5% of amount |
| Speed | 3-5 days | 1-3 days (faster processing) |
| Maximum amount | ₹5,000 per MO | ₹5,000 per eMO |
| Receipt | Paper receipt | Digital receipt + email confirmation |

## Who Can Send eMO?

- Any Indian resident with a valid mobile number and email address
- No registration required on India Post portal (guest checkout available)
- Sender must have online payment facility (net banking / debit card / UPI)

## eMO Amount Limits and Charges

| Amount Slab | Commission |
|---|---|
| Up to ₹1,500 | ₹25 + GST |
| ₹1,501 to ₹5,000 | ₹35 + GST |

- **Minimum amount:** ₹1
- **Maximum amount:** ₹5,000 per single eMO
- You can send multiple eMOs to the same person
- Additional charges may apply for SMS notification

## How to Send Electronic Money Order (eMO) Online

### Step-by-Step Process

1. Visit [epostoffice.gov.in](https://www.epostoffice.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"eMO (Electronic Money Order)"** option
3. Enter **sender details:**
   - Full name
   - Mobile number
   - Address and PIN code
4. Enter **recipient details:**
   - Full name (as known locally)
   - Complete address with landmark
   - PIN code of delivery area
   - Mobile number (optional but recommended)
5. Enter the **amount** (₹1 to ₹5,000)
6. Add a **message** for the recipient (optional, up to 30 characters)
7. Review the details and **commission charges**
8. Choose **payment method:** Net banking, debit card, or UPI
9. Complete the payment
10. Download or note the **eMO booking number**
11. You will receive a confirmation email and SMS

### Important: Ensure Correct Details

- The **recipient's name must match** what is known at their local post office
- The **address must be complete** with house number, street, village/city, and PIN code
- Wrong PIN code can cause delivery to the wrong post office

## How to Track eMO Status

1. Visit [indiapost.gov.in](https://www.indiapost.gov.in/)
2. Go to **"Track & Trace"** section
3. Enter your **eMO booking number**
4. View the current delivery status

### eMO Status Meanings

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Booked | eMO has been generated |
| In Transit | Sent to destination post office |
| Out for Delivery | Postman has the MO for delivery |
| Delivered | Cash handed to recipient |
| Returned | Could not deliver — wrong address or recipient not found |

## Delivery Timeline

| Destination | Expected Delivery |
|---|---|
| Same city/district | 1-2 working days |
| Same state | 2-3 working days |
| Different state | 3-5 working days |
| Remote/rural areas | 5-7 working days |

> Delivery time depends on the destination post office workload. Metro areas are usually faster.

## How to Send Money Order Offline (At Post Office)

If you prefer the traditional method:

1. Visit your nearest post office
2. Fill the **Money Order form (MO form)**
3. Provide recipient's full name and complete address
4. Pay the amount + commission in cash
5. Get the receipt with MO number
6. Track using the MO number on indiapost.gov.in

## What If eMO Is Not Delivered?

If your eMO is not delivered within expected time:

1. **Track online** first — check if it shows "Returned" or still "In Transit"
2. **Call helpline:** 1800-11-2011 (toll-free)
3. **File complaint** on [pgportal.gov.in](https://pgportal.gov.in/) — see our [post office complaint guide](/guide/post-office-complaint-online) for detailed steps
4. **Visit the sender's post office** with the receipt for inquiry
5. If returned, the amount will be refunded to the sender's post office — collect with original receipt

## Important Tips

1. **Double-check the PIN code** — wrong PIN is the most common reason for eMO delivery failure
2. **Include recipient's mobile number** — the postman can call to confirm address and availability
3. **Keep the booking number safe** — you need it for tracking and complaints
4. **Send during working days** — eMOs booked on weekends/holidays are processed the next working day
5. **For large amounts, send multiple eMOs** — since the limit is ₹5,000 per eMO, split larger amounts

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the maximum amount I can send through eMO?
The maximum amount per single eMO is **₹5,000**. If you need to send more, you can book multiple eMOs to the same recipient.

### How much commission does India Post charge for eMO?
India Post charges ₹25 (+ GST) for amounts up to ₹1,500 and ₹35 (+ GST) for amounts between ₹1,501 and ₹5,000.

### How long does eMO delivery take?
eMO typically takes 1-3 working days within the same state and 3-5 working days for different states. Remote areas may take up to 7 days.

### Can I send eMO to any address in India?
Yes, eMO can be sent to any address in India where India Post delivers. The postman will deliver the cash to the recipient's doorstep.

### Does the recipient need a bank account to receive eMO?
No, the recipient does not need a bank account. The postman delivers cash directly to the recipient's address. This is the main advantage of money orders.

### How do I track my eMO?
Visit indiapost.gov.in and use the **Track & Trace** feature. Enter your eMO booking number to see the current delivery status.

### What happens if eMO is not delivered?
If the postman cannot deliver (wrong address, recipient not available after attempts), the eMO is returned to the sender's post office. The sender can collect the refund with the original receipt.
