# ONORC Not Working? Fix One Nation One Ration Card Portability Problems

**ONORC (One Nation One Ration Card)** lets ration card holders get their entitled grains (rice/wheat) from **any Fair Price Shop (FPS)** in India — not just from their home state. This helps migrant workers and people who move between states.

If your ONORC is not working at another state's ration shop, here are the most common reasons and how to fix each.

## Why ONORC May Not Work

| Problem | Cause |
|---------|-------|
| **Biometric failure at ePoS machine** | Fingerprints not matching (dry/worn fingers); old ePoS machine with bad sensor |
| **"Member not found" error** | Aadhaar not seeded in your home state's ration card database |
| **FPS dealer refusing** | Dealer doesn't want to serve out-of-state customers (not allowed) |
| **Wrong finger selected** | Using wrong finger — try multiple fingerprints |
| **Network failure at shop** | ePoS machine has poor connectivity |
| **Transaction limit reached** | You may have already used your monthly quota in your home state |
| **Ration card suspended/cancelled** | Your home state suspended the card for non-usage or re-verification |

## Fix 1: Biometric Failure at ePoS Machine

**Most common ONORC failure for migrant workers:**

1. **Try all 10 fingers** — don't stick to just one or two. Migrant workers often have dry/calloused fingers; the machine may recognize different fingers differently.
2. **Clean the finger** with a damp cloth and try again. Dust and calluses reduce fingerprint quality.
3. **Ask the shop owner** to try **Iris scan** if their ePoS machine supports it (not all machines have iris).
4. **Try ONORC at a different FPS shop** in the same area — machine quality varies.
5. **Use mAadhaar face authentication** if the FPS has a phone with mAadhaar — available in some states.

If biometric consistently fails, you may need to **re-seed biometrics in your home state** at the Aadhaar centre (biometric update).

## Fix 2: "Member Not Found" or "Aadhaar Not Seeded"

**Cause**: Your Aadhaar number is not linked to your ration card in the home state's database.

**Fix**:
1. Visit your **home state's nearest Ration Card office or Fair Price Shop** to link Aadhaar to your ration card
2. Or visit a **CSC (Common Service Centre)** in your home state or current city to initiate the seeding
3. After seeding, allow **7–10 days** for the data to sync across systems

**Verify your Aadhaar seeding**: Visit [mera.nic.in](https://mera.nic.in/) → enter your Aadhaar number to check if it's linked to a ration card in the ONORC database.

## Fix 3: FPS Dealer Refusing to Serve

Dealers are **legally required** to serve ONORC beneficiaries. Refusal is a violation. If a dealer refuses:

1. **Note the dealer's ID** (visible on the ePoS machine or the shop board)
2. **Call the National Food Helpline**: **14445** (toll-free)
3. **File online complaint**: nfsa.gov.in → Grievance portal
4. **Contact your current state's Food Department**: They can instruct the FPS dealer

After your complaint, the FPS dealer typically becomes cooperative.

## Fix 4: Transaction Limit Already Used

ONORC allocates your monthly grain quota once per month. If you:
- Already collected grain from your **home state's FPS** this month
- Then try to collect again at another state via ONORC

The second transaction will fail. ONORC portability is for **one collection per month** from any FPS — you can't collect twice.

**Fix**: Wait for next month's cycle (typically resets on the 1st of the month).

## Fix 5: Ration Card Suspended in Home State

If you've been away from your home state for months without using your ration card, some states **suspend** the card for prolonged non-usage or during re-verification drives.

**Fix**:
1. Check your card status at your **home state's ration card portal** or FPS
2. Visit home state's food office to reactivate suspended card
3. **Some states allow reactivation online** — check your state's PDS (Public Distribution System) portal

## How Many FPS Shops Can I Use ONORC At?

You can use **any FPS in any state where ONORC is active** (all 36 states/UTs as of 2024). You are NOT limited to specific shops.

However:
- **Some rural areas** may not have upgraded to ONORC-compatible ePoS machines — less common now
- **Check with the shop first** — most urban FPS shops support ONORC

## Monthly ONORC Allocation

Your monthly entitlement (kg of rice/wheat) remains the same as your home state's allocation. ONORC doesn't change the quantity — only where you collect it.

Example: If your home state (say Bihar) gives 5 kg of rice per member, you'll receive 5 kg at any ONORC FPS in any other state.

## ONORC for Seasonal Migrant Workers — Specific Tips

1. **Identify FPS shops near your workplace** before the month starts (get location from mera.nic.in)
2. **Register your employer address** with your home state's food department if they have a migrant worker registration scheme (some states like Kerala and Maharashtra have this)
3. **Carry a photocopy of your ration card** as backup — the ePoS machine may sometimes ask for manual verification

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I change my home state's FPS permanently to my current city?

No — ONORC is for portability, not permanent transfer. Your "home" FPS remains in your home state. To permanently change, you must **transfer/migrate your ration card** to your new state — a separate process involving surrendering the old card.

### I came back to my home state but ONORC transaction was done in another state this month. Can I still collect?

No. Your monthly quota is ONE collection from any FPS. If you collected in another state earlier this month, you cannot collect again in your home state this month. Wait for next month.

### My home state is Uttar Pradesh. Can I use ONORC in Tamil Nadu?

Yes. All 36 states/UTs are ONORC-connected. UP ration card holders can collect grains at any FPS in Tamil Nadu using Aadhaar biometric at the ePoS machine.

### My child is on the ration card. Can they collect separately?

Each family member on the ration card has an Aadhaar seeded. One family member's biometric can authorize the entire family's monthly allocation at once. You don't need each member present.
