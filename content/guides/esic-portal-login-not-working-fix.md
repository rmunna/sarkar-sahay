# ESIC Portal Login Not Working 2026? Fix All Employee & Employer Errors

The ESIC (Employees' State Insurance Corporation) has **separate portals** for employees (Insured Persons / IPs) and employers. Most login failures happen because users mix up the portals or don't have their IP number. This guide covers every fix.

## Which ESIC Portal to Use

| Who You Are | Portal URL | What You Can Do |
|-------------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Employee (Insured Person)** | [esic.gov.in/insured-person-employer-login](https://esic.gov.in/insured-person-employer-login) | Check contributions, download Pehchan card, view medical entitlements |
| **Employer** | [employer.esic.gov.in](https://employer.esic.gov.in/) | Pay contributions, add/remove employees, file returns |
| **Hospital/Dispensary** | [esic.gov.in](https://esic.gov.in/) → Hospital Module | Patient management, claims |

**Tip**: "IP" means Insured Person (employee). Your **IP number** is the unique 17-digit ESIC number assigned to you by your employer — it's printed on your Pehchan card.

## Fix 1: "IP Number Not Found" or "Invalid IP Number"

This is the most common error for employees:

**Why it happens**: Your employer may not have registered you with ESIC yet, or may have entered your Aadhaar/mobile incorrectly.

**How to fix**:
1. **Ask your HR/employer** to check your registration in their employer.esic.gov.in portal
2. Your employer must have added your Aadhaar and correct mobile number during employee registration
3. Once your employer confirms registration, wait **24–48 hours** for it to reflect on the employee portal
4. Your IP number is on your **salary slip** or ESIC Pehchan card — if you don't have it, ask your employer

## Fix 2: Password Errors / "Invalid Credentials"

**For Employees (IP login)**:
1. Go to [esic.gov.in/insured-person-employer-login](https://esic.gov.in/insured-person-employer-login)
2. Click **"Forgot Password"**
3. Enter your **IP number** and **registered mobile number**
4. Verify with OTP and set a new password

**For Employers**:
1. Go to [employer.esic.gov.in](https://employer.esic.gov.in/)
2. Click **"Forgot Password"** → enter **Employer Code** + registered email
3. Reset link will be sent to registered email

## Fix 3: OTP Not Received

If OTP for login or password reset isn't arriving:

1. The OTP goes to the **mobile registered with your employer** — not necessarily your personal number
2. Ask your HR what mobile number is registered for you in ESIC
3. If it's wrong, your employer must update it at employer.esic.gov.in → Employee Management → Edit Employee
4. **DND numbers**: ESIC OTPs are transactional SMS and should bypass DND, but contact your carrier if still blocked
5. **Delay**: ESIC OTP can take up to 5 minutes during peak hours

## Fix 4: ESIC Portal Not Loading / Server Error

1. **Check server status**: ESIC portal is frequently overloaded from 10 AM–3 PM
2. **Best time to access**: 7 AM–9 AM or after 7 PM
3. **Clear cache**: Chrome → Settings → Clear browsing data → check "Cached images"
4. **Use incognito mode**: Ctrl+Shift+N in Chrome
5. **If completely down**: check ESIC social media or wait 2–3 hours

## Fix 5: Pehchan Card Not Downloading

The ESIC Pehchan card is your health insurance identity card:

1. Login to [esic.gov.in/insured-person-employer-login](https://esic.gov.in/insured-person-employer-login)
2. Go to **"Download Pehchan Card"** under IP Services
3. The card generates as a PDF — ensure your browser allows PDF downloads
4. If not generating: contributions must have been paid by your employer for at least one wage period before the card activates
5. Contact your employer to confirm ESIC contributions have been paid

## Fix 6: Contribution History Not Showing

If your contribution history is blank or shows wrong amounts:

1. **Employer delay**: contributions must be uploaded by the employer by the 15th of the following month
2. **New employee**: it takes 1–2 months for the first contribution to appear
3. **Check wage period**: contributions are shown month-wise; check the correct month/year filter
4. If contributions are missing for 3+ months, ask your HR — employer may not be depositing on time (which is a legal violation)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I access ESIC without knowing my IP number?

No. Your IP (Insured Person) number is mandatory to login as an employee. Get it from your employer's HR department or from your salary slip. If you have your Aadhaar, your employer can look up your IP number from their employer portal.

### My employer says I am registered but I still can't login. What to do?

Ask your employer to check: (a) Aadhaar is correctly linked to your employee profile, (b) your registered mobile number, (c) that your employee status is "Active" (not "Separated"). If all correct and still failing, raise a grievance at the nearest ESIC Branch Office with a copy of your appointment letter and Aadhaar.

### I left a job. Can I still use ESIC?

Yes — ESIC coverage continues for 6 months after you leave a job (for medical benefits). You can still login with your IP number. After 6 months, coverage lapses unless you join a new ESIC-covered employer.

### My ESIC card photo is wrong/blurry. How to correct it?

Visit your nearest ESIC Branch Office with your Aadhaar card. They will update the photo and reissue the Pehchan card. The online portal does not allow photo self-correction.

### How to check which hospitals I can use under ESIC?

Login to [esic.gov.in](https://esic.gov.in/) → "Hospital Locator" → select your state and district. You can also search by medical specialty. Employees must use the ESI dispensary or hospital in their registered area.
