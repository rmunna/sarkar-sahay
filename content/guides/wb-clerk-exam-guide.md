# WB Clerkship Exam Guide — Eligibility, Pattern & Preparation Tips

The West Bengal Public Service Commission (WBPSC) conducts the Clerkship recruitment exam for Lower Division Clerk (LDC) posts in various government offices across West Bengal. This is one of the most popular entry-level government exams in the state.

WB Clerkship offers a stable government career with regular salary, benefits, and opportunities for promotion to Upper Division Clerk and beyond.

## Eligibility Criteria

### Age Limit

| Category | Minimum Age | Maximum Age |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| General | 18 years | 28 years |
| OBC | 18 years | 31 years |
| SC/ST | 18 years | 33 years |

### Educational Qualification

- **Minimum:** Madhyamik (10th pass) or equivalent
- Must have basic computer knowledge
- Knowledge of Bengali is essential

## Exam Pattern

### Written Examination

| Subject | Marks |
|---------|-------|
| English | 100 |
| Bengali / Nepali | 100 |
| General Studies | 100 |
| Arithmetic | 100 |
| **Total** | **400** |

- **Duration:** 2 hours per paper (4 sittings)
- **Type:** Descriptive + Objective (mixed)
- **No negative marking** (for objective portions)

### Computer Typing Test (Qualifying)

- English typing speed test on computer

## Syllabus Overview

### English
- Comprehension, Grammar, Vocabulary, Letter writing, Précis writing

### Bengali / Nepali
- Comprehension, Grammar, Essay writing, Translation

### General Studies
- History, Geography, Polity, Science, Current Affairs, West Bengal GK

### Arithmetic
- Number system, Fractions, Percentage, Profit & Loss, SI/CI, Ratio, Average, Time & Work

## How to Apply

1. Visit WBPSC at [wbpsc.gov.in](https://wbpsc.gov.in)
2. Register and create an account
3. Apply for the Clerkship exam notification
4. Fill in personal and educational details
5. Upload photo and signature
6. Pay fee and submit

## Application Fee

| Category | Fee |
|----------|-----|
| General | ₹210 |
| SC/ST | ₹50 |

## Salary & Benefits

- **Pay Scale:** ₹22,700 – ₹58,500 (Level 2)
- DA, HRA, and other allowances
- Pension (NPS), medical benefits
- Promotion path to Upper Division Clerk

## Preparation Tips

1. **Bengali language:** Strong Bengali is essential — practice essay and comprehension
2. **Arithmetic:** Focus on basic calculations and speed
3. **WB GK:** Study West Bengal history, geography, and current affairs
4. **English grammar:** Practice basic grammar and letter writing
5. **Previous papers:** Solve past WB Clerkship papers

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the minimum qualification for WB Clerkship?
10th pass (Madhyamik) is the minimum requirement.

### Is there an interview?
No, selection is based on written exam and typing test.

### Is typing test qualifying or merit-based?
The typing test is qualifying — it does not count for merit.

### Is Bengali mandatory?
Yes, knowledge of Bengali (or Nepali in hill areas) is required.

### How many vacancies are released?
It varies — recent cycles have had 2,000-5,000+ vacancies.

### Can other state candidates apply?
Knowledge of Bengali and West Bengal domicile are generally required.
