# LPG Gas Cylinder Price Today: Check Rates All States

LPG (Liquefied Petroleum Gas) cylinder prices in India are revised on the 1st of every month by the three Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs) — Indian Oil (Indane), Bharat Petroleum (Bharat Gas) and Hindustan Petroleum (HP Gas). This guide helps you check the latest prices, understand the pricing structure and track price history.

## How LPG Pricing Works in India

LPG prices are determined by:
- **International benchmark** — Saudi Aramco Contract Price (Saudi CP)
- **Exchange rate** — USD to INR
- **Local costs** — Bottling charges, dealer commission, GST, transportation
- **Government subsidy** (if applicable)

Prices are revised on the 1st of each month based on the average international price of the previous month.

## Why Prices Vary by State

LPG prices differ across states due to:
- **Transportation cost** — Distance from bottling plant to dealer
- **Local taxes** — State VAT/GST differences
- **Dealer commission** — Varies by location
- **Bottling charges** — Different for each plant

Northeastern states and island territories generally have higher prices due to transportation.

## LPG Subsidy — Current Status

- The government provides **₹200 per cylinder subsidy** to [Ujjwala Yojana](/guide/ujjwala-yojana-apply-status) beneficiaries for up to 12 refills per year
- For general consumers, direct subsidy was discontinued in 2020 for most households
- Subsidy, when applicable, is transferred via **DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer)** to the linked bank account
- Check subsidy status on [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in/)

## Important Tips

1. **Link Aadhaar and bank account** to receive any applicable subsidy via DBT.
2. **Compare prices** across OMCs — prices may differ by ₹5-15 in the same city.
3. **5 kg cylinders** are ideal for PG/hostel students and single-person households.
4. **Double cylinder connection** — You can have 2 cylinders under one connection for uninterrupted supply. See our [gas agency transfer guide](/guide/gas-agency-change-transfer).
5. **Check weight** — A full 14.2 kg cylinder weighs approximately 29.5 kg (14.2 kg gas + 15.3 kg tare weight printed on cylinder).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: When do LPG prices change?
LPG prices are revised on the 1st of every month by the three OMCs based on international crude oil prices.

### Q2: Is domestic and commercial LPG the same quality?
Yes, the gas composition is the same. The difference is only in cylinder size (14.2 kg vs 19 kg) and pricing.

### Q3: How do I check if I received my LPG subsidy?
Check your bank statement for DBTL (Direct Benefit Transfer for LPG) credit. You can also check on [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in/).

### Q4: Can I use a commercial cylinder at home?
Using commercial LPG cylinders for domestic purposes is illegal and can attract penalties. Always use domestic cylinders at home.

### Q5: Why is my LPG bill higher than the listed price?
The bill may include delivery charges (₹20-50 in some areas) and GST. If you have a double cylinder connection, you may be billed for two.

### Q6: How can I get a free LPG connection?
Eligible BPL families can get a free connection under [PM Ujjwala Yojana](/guide/ujjwala-yojana-apply-status).

### Q7: Is PNG cheaper than LPG?
Yes, [PNG (Piped Natural Gas)](/guide/png-cng-connection-apply) is generally 30-40% cheaper than LPG per unit of energy.
