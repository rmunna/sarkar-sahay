## What is Mukhyamantri Kanya Vivah-Nikah Yojana?

**Mukhyamantri Kanya Vivah-Nikah Yojana** is the Madhya Pradesh government scheme providing **₹51,000 in marriage assistance** to economically weak families at the time of their daughter's marriage. The scheme covers all religions — both Hindu (vivah) and Muslim (nikah) marriages.

The benefit is provided primarily through **state-organized Samuhik Vivah (mass marriage)** ceremonies, though individual registrations are also accepted.

## Benefits — What the ₹51,000 Includes

| Component | Amount | How Given |
|---|---|---|
| Household goods (grihasti) | ₹43,000 | Given as physical goods (utensils, furniture, etc.) |
| Cash cheque to bride | ₹8,000 | Bank cheque in bride's name |
| **Total** | **₹51,000** | |

**Note:** The ₹43,000 in goods is given to help the newly married couple set up their household. The physical goods list is fixed by the government.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Girl (bride) must be a Madhya Pradesh resident**
- **Family must be Below Poverty Line (BPL)** or economically weak
- **Bride's age: 18 years or above** (legal marriage age — girls under 18 are NOT eligible and registrations are not accepted)
- **Groom's age: 21 years or above**
- Marriage must be the **bride's first marriage** (widows/divorcees may be eligible under special provisions — confirm with local office)
- Families with daughters who are orphaned or handicapped get priority

### Who is Included (All Categories)
- SC/ST, OBC, and general category poor families
- Hindu, Muslim marriages (scheme covers "Vivah" for Hindu and "Nikah" for Muslim)
- Widows remarrying (under Punarvivah provision)

## How to Apply

### For Samuhik Vivah (Mass Marriage) — Preferred Route
1. Contact your **Gram Panchayat, Nagar Panchayat, or Municipal Corporation**
2. Register for the state-organized mass marriage ceremony
3. The government organizes Samuhik Vivah events — families apply in advance, ceremony is held on an auspicious date
4. Benefits distributed at the ceremony itself

### Online Application (Individual)
1. Go to **[vivah.mp.gov.in](https://vivah.mp.gov.in)**
2. Register with Aadhaar + mobile OTP
3. Fill application:
   - Bride and groom's details
   - Family income / BPL status
   - Marriage date (proposed)
4. Upload documents
5. Submit → District Social Justice office verifies and approves

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card** of bride and parents/guardian
2. **Age proof of bride** (birth certificate, Class 10 certificate) — showing 18+
3. **Age proof of groom** — showing 21+
4. **BPL ration card** or income certificate (poor families)
5. **Residence proof** (Aadhaar with MP address)
6. **Bank account details** of the bride (for ₹8,000 cheque)
7. Passport-size photographs of bride and groom

## Frequently Asked Questions

### We are not BPL. Can we still apply?
The scheme primarily targets BPL and economically weak families. Non-BPL families may not qualify unless they meet specific criteria (destitute/orphan status). Check with your local Gram Panchayat.

### Our family prefers a private wedding. Can we still get the benefit?
Yes — individual applications can be submitted online. However, the government prefers Samuhik Vivah as it ensures proper verification. Individual applications are also processed but may take longer.

### Can we register a marriage that already happened?
The scheme is for upcoming/recent marriages. Applications for marriages that happened more than 3 months ago may not be accepted. Apply before or immediately after the marriage.

### The bride is a widow. Can she apply?
Widows remarrying may be eligible under the Punarvivah (remarriage) provision. Check with the district Social Justice office for current eligibility norms for widows.

### We received goods worth ₹43,000 but they seem of lower quality. What can we do?
Goods are procured by the district administration. If you believe the quality or value is significantly below the declared ₹43,000, file a complaint with the Collector's office or the district Social Justice officer.
