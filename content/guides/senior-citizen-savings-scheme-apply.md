# Senior Citizen Savings Scheme: How to Apply, Interest Rate, Tax Benefits

The Senior Citizen Savings Scheme (SCSS) is the most popular fixed-income investment for retirees in India. With a high interest rate of **8.2% paid quarterly**, government backing, and Section 80C tax benefits, SCSS provides reliable income during retirement. Here's your complete guide.

## What is SCSS?

SCSS is a government-sponsored savings scheme designed for Indians aged 60 and above. It provides:
- **8.2% annual interest** paid quarterly — steady income every 3 months
- **Government guarantee** on both principal and interest
- **5-year tenure** extendable by 3 years
- **Section 80C tax deduction** on deposits

## SCSS Key Details

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Interest rate | 8.2% p.a. (Q1 FY 2026-27) |
| Interest payment | Quarterly (April 1, July 1, Oct 1, Jan 1) |
| Minimum deposit | ₹1,000 |
| Maximum deposit | ₹30,00,000 (₹30 lakh) |
| Tenure | 5 years (extendable by 3 years) |
| Tax benefit | Section 80C on deposits |
| TDS | If interest exceeds ₹50,000/year |

## Eligibility

### Who Can Open SCSS?

- **Indian residents aged 60 years or above**
- **Retired civilian government employees aged 55-60** — must invest within 1 month of receiving retirement benefits
- **Retired defense personnel aged 50-60** — must invest within 1 month of receiving retirement benefits
- **HUFs and NRIs** are **not eligible**

### Joint Account

- SCSS can be opened as a **joint account with spouse**
- The first holder must meet the age criteria
- The entire deposit is attributed to the first holder

## How to Apply for SCSS

### At Post Office

1. Visit any post office
2. Fill the **SCSS Account Opening Form**
3. Submit KYC documents:
   - **Age proof** (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, birth certificate)
   - **Address proof** (Aadhaar, utility bill)
   - **Passport-size photographs**
   - **Retirement proof** (if applying before 60)
4. Make the deposit by cheque or demand draft (amounts above ₹1 lakh must be by cheque/DD)
5. Receive the SCSS passbook

### At Bank

SCSS accounts can be opened at authorized banks including SBI, PNB, Bank of Baroda, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Canara Bank, and others.

1. Visit the bank branch with documents
2. Fill the SCSS application form
3. Submit KYC and age proof
4. Make the deposit
5. Account is activated and passbook is issued

### Documents Required

1. **SCSS account opening form**
2. **Identity proof** — Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, Voter ID
3. **Age proof** — Aadhaar, birth certificate, Passport, PAN
4. **Address proof** — Aadhaar, utility bill, bank statement
5. **Passport-size photographs** (2)
6. **Retirement proof** (for those between 55-60): Retirement order or proof of retirement benefits

## SCSS Interest Rate and Quarterly Income

The 8.2% interest is paid quarterly. Here's what you earn based on your deposit:

| Deposit Amount | Annual Interest | Quarterly Income |
|---------------|----------------|-----------------|
| ₹5,00,000 | ₹41,000 | ₹10,250 |
| ₹10,00,000 | ₹82,000 | ₹20,500 |
| ₹15,00,000 | ₹1,23,000 | ₹30,750 |
| ₹20,00,000 | ₹1,64,000 | ₹41,000 |
| ₹30,00,000 | ₹2,46,000 | ₹61,500 |

Investing the maximum ₹30 lakh gives **₹61,500 every quarter** — a solid retirement income.

## Tax Benefits and TDS

### Section 80C Deduction

SCSS deposits qualify for tax deduction under **Section 80C** up to ₹1,50,000 per financial year. This is the same section used by PPF, ELSS, and [other tax-saving instruments](/guide/income-tax-saving-tips-80c-investments).

### Tax on Interest

Unlike PPF and SSY, **SCSS interest is taxable** as per your income tax slab:

| Income Slab | Tax on Interest |
|------------|----------------|
| Up to ₹3,00,000 | Nil |
| ₹3-6 lakh | 5% |
| ₹6-9 lakh | 10% |
| ₹9-12 lakh | 15% |
| ₹12-15 lakh | 20% |
| Above ₹15 lakh | 30% |

### TDS Rules

- TDS is deducted if annual interest exceeds **₹50,000** (higher limit for senior citizens)
- Submit **Form 15H** to avoid TDS if your total income is below the taxable limit
- TDS rate: 10% (20% if PAN is not provided)

## Premature Withdrawal Rules

| Closure Time | Penalty |
|-------------|---------|
| Before 1 year | **Not allowed** |
| After 1 year, before 2 years | **1.5% of deposit** deducted |
| After 2 years, before 5 years | **1% of deposit** deducted |
| At maturity (5 years) | No penalty |

### Process for Premature Closure

1. Submit the premature closure form at the post office/bank
2. Provide SCSS passbook and identity proof
3. Deposit minus penalty is credited to your bank account

## Extension After Maturity

- SCSS can be **extended by 3 years** after the 5-year maturity
- Extension request must be made **within 1 year of maturity**
- Interest rate applicable is the rate at the time of extension
- During the extension, you can close the account **any time without penalty**

## SCSS vs Other Senior Citizen Options

| Interest | 8.2% | 7-7.75% | 7.1% | Market-linked |

| 80C benefit | Yes | Yes (5-yr FD) | Yes | Yes |
| Quarterly income | Yes | Option available | No | No |
| Max limit | ₹30 lakh | No limit | ₹1.5L/year | No limit |

## Tips for Maximizing SCSS Benefits

1. **Invest the maximum ₹30 lakh** if you have the funds — the 8.2% quarterly payout is excellent retirement income
2. **Both spouses should invest separately** — combined ₹60 lakh gives ₹1,23,000 quarterly income
3. **Submit Form 15H** if your total income is below the taxable limit to avoid TDS
4. **Invest retirement corpus immediately** — don't let funds sit idle in savings accounts
5. **Use the 3-year extension** at maturity to continue earning high interest
6. **Combine SCSS with PPF** — PPF for tax-free growth, SCSS for regular income. Read our [PPF guide](/guide/ppf-account-open-online)

## Nomination and Transfer

- **Nomination** is mandatory at the time of account opening
- Account can be **transferred** between post offices or from post office to bank (and vice versa)
- On death of the account holder, the **nominee receives the balance** with interest up to the date of death

The Senior Citizen Savings Scheme is the best fixed-income option for retirees in India. With 8.2% quarterly interest, government guarantee, and Section 80C benefits, it provides both income security and tax savings. If you're approaching retirement, plan to invest the maximum ₹30 lakh in SCSS on day one to start earning ₹61,500 every quarter.
