# Chhattisgarh Ration Card Apply Online 2026

The Chhattisgarh ration card is issued by the **Food, Civil Supplies & Consumer Protection Department, Government of Chhattisgarh**. Chhattisgarh manages PDS through the **khadya.cg.nic.in** portal. Chhattisgarh has one of the highest food security coverage rates in India — approximately 85% of the population is covered under its comprehensive food security scheme, including the state's own **Chhattisgarh Food Security Act (CFSA)** which goes beyond central NFSA.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [khadya.cg.nic.in](https://khadya.cg.nic.in) or visit your nearest Jan Seva Kendra / Gram Panchayat. PHH/BPL families get 35 kg rice per family per month @ ₹1/kg under Chhattisgarh's state scheme — significantly more than the national average.

---

## Ration Card Types in Chhattisgarh

| Type | Category | Entitlement |
|------|----------|-------------|
| **PHH (Priority Household / State BPL)** | BPL families under NFSA + CFSA | **35 kg rice/family/month @ ₹1/kg** (state scheme) |
| **AAY (Antyodaya Anna Yojana)** | Poorest families | 35 kg/family/month free |
| **APL (Above Poverty Line)** | Non-subsidised | Identity document; no grain subsidy |

> **Chhattisgarh is exceptional**: While central NFSA gives 5 kg/person, the state government tops up to provide **35 kg rice per family per month at ₹1/kg** — one of the most generous state food schemes in India.

---

## Eligibility

**PHH/BPL ration card:**
- Annual household income below ₹1.20 lakh (rural) or ₹1.50 lakh (urban)
- Not a regular government or PSU employee
- No income taxpayer in family
- No motorised 4-wheeler vehicle
- No land above 2 acres irrigated / 5 acres unirrigated (general)
- Tribal/forest dweller norms differ — consult local Gram Panchayat
- Aadhaar card mandatory for all members

**AAY:**
- Landless agricultural labourers with no regular income
- Widow / elderly living alone
- Disabled persons without family support
- Forest-dwelling tribal families with no other income
- Bonded labourers

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar cards (all family members — mandatory)
- Residence proof (electricity bill, panchayat certificate, water bill, rent agreement)
- Passport-size photographs
- Income certificate from Patwari / SDM
- Caste/tribe certificate (if SC/ST — important for forest dweller provisions)
- Bank account passbook (for DBT)
- Voter ID (additional identity proof)

## How to Apply

**Method 1: Khadya Portal (Online)**

1. Go to [khadya.cg.nic.in](https://khadya.cg.nic.in)
2. Click **"New Ration Card Application"** or Citizen Services
3. Register / log in with Aadhaar-linked mobile number
4. Fill in family details and add all members with Aadhaar
5. Select district, block, gram panchayat
6. Upload documents (income certificate, address proof, photographs)
7. Submit and note the acknowledgement number

**Method 2: Jan Seva Kendra (CSC)**

1. Visit nearest **Jan Seva Kendra** (Chhattisgarh's CSC)
2. Operator fills the form with your documents
3. Service fee: approximately ₹30–50
4. Collect printed acknowledgement

**Method 3: Gram Panchayat / Urban Local Body**

1. Visit your **Gram Panchayat** (rural) or **Municipal Corporation / Nagar Panchayat** (urban)
2. Submit filled application form with documents to Secretary/Supply Inspector
3. Panchayat forwards to Block Supply Officer
4. Block Supply Officer verifies and submits to DSO for approval

## Status Check

1. Go to [khadya.cg.nic.in](https://khadya.cg.nic.in)
2. Click **"Application Status"**
3. Enter acknowledgement number or Aadhaar number
4. View status — received / under verification / approved / rejected

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why does Chhattisgarh give 35 kg rice per family vs 5 kg per person in other states?

Chhattisgarh implemented its own **Chhattisgarh Food Security Act (CFSA)** which provides 35 kg rice per family per month at ₹1/kg — a state-funded top-up over the central NFSA. This makes CG one of the most food-secure states for BPL families.

### Do tribal/forest dwellers get any special ration card benefits in CG?

Yes — forest-dwelling Scheduled Tribes and those with **Van Adhikar Patta** (forest land rights) are given priority for PHH/AAY ration cards. Caste/tribe certificate and forest rights document help in the application.

### Can I check CG ration card by Aadhaar number?

Yes — at khadya.cg.nic.in, you can search your ration card status using your Aadhaar number or acknowledgement number.

### My CG ration card was cancelled. How to re-apply?

If your card was cancelled due to wrong data or non-Aadhaar seeding, visit your nearest Jan Seva Kendra or Gram Panchayat with Aadhaar and income documents to apply afresh. Enquire at the Block Supply Office about the reason for cancellation.
