# How to Order Aadhaar PVC Card Online — Plastic Aadhaar Card

Want a durable, wallet-sized Aadhaar card? You can order an **Aadhaar PVC card** online from the official UIDAI website for just **₹50**. This guide covers the complete process, fees, delivery time, and tracking.

> **Related:** [Download e-Aadhaar](/guide/download-e-aadhaar-card-online) | [Apply for Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) | [Aadhaar Common Problems](/guide/aadhaar-common-problems-solutions)

## What is Aadhaar PVC Card?

The Aadhaar PVC card is a **plastic, credit card-sized** version of your Aadhaar. It is:

- **Durable** — made of PVC plastic, doesn't tear or get damaged easily
- **Compact** — fits in your wallet like a debit or credit card
- **Secure** — has a QR code with your demographic details and photograph, plus a hologram for authenticity
- **Officially valid** — accepted as proof of identity everywhere Aadhaar is accepted

The PVC card contains your name, photo, date of birth, gender, and masked Aadhaar number. It also has a secure QR code that can be scanned for verification.

## PVC Card vs e-Aadhaar vs Letter Aadhaar

| Feature | Aadhaar PVC Card | e-Aadhaar (PDF) | Letter Aadhaar |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Format** | Plastic card | Digital PDF | Paper printout |
| **Size** | Credit card size | A4 page | A4 page |
| **Durability** | Very high | N/A (digital) | Low (paper tears) |
| **Cost** | ₹50 | Free | Free (at enrolment) |
| **QR Code** | Yes (with hologram) | Yes | Yes |
| **Delivery** | 7-15 days by speed post | Instant download | Given at enrolment centre |
| **Validity** | Same as Aadhaar | Same as Aadhaar | Same as Aadhaar |

**All three versions are equally valid** for identity verification. The PVC card is simply more convenient to carry.

## How to Order Aadhaar PVC Card Online

### Method 1: Via myAadhaar Portal (Recommended)

1. **Visit** [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in)
2. **Log in** with your 12-digit Aadhaar number
3. **Enter the OTP** sent to your registered mobile number
4. After login, click on **"Order Aadhaar PVC Card"** from the dashboard
5. **Verify your details** — check your name, address, photo, and other details shown on screen
6. **Make payment** of ₹50 (including GST and speed post charges)
7. Choose your payment method — UPI, debit card, credit card, or net banking
8. After successful payment, you will receive a **Service Request Number (SRN)**
9. **Save the SRN** — you will need it to track your order

### Method 2: Via resident.uidai.gov.in

1. **Visit** [resident.uidai.gov.in](https://resident.uidai.gov.in)
2. Navigate to **"Order Aadhaar Card"** or **"Order Aadhaar PVC Card"**
3. Enter your **12-digit Aadhaar number** or **16-digit Virtual ID (VID)**
4. Enter the **security code** (captcha)
5. Click **"Send OTP"** and enter the OTP received on your registered mobile
6. **Preview your card** and verify all details
7. **Pay ₹50** using any available payment method
8. Note down your **SRN** for tracking

> **Important:** Your mobile number must be registered with Aadhaar to receive OTP. If your mobile is not linked, visit the nearest Aadhaar enrolment centre first.

## Fees & Payment Methods

### How Much Does It Cost?

The Aadhaar PVC card costs **₹50 (inclusive of GST and speed post delivery charges)**. There are no hidden fees.

### Accepted Payment Methods

- **UPI** — Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, or any UPI app
- **Debit Card** — Visa, Mastercard, RuPay
- **Credit Card** — Visa, Mastercard
- **Net Banking** — Most major Indian banks supported

> **Tip:** UPI is the fastest and easiest payment method. Just scan the QR or enter your UPI ID.

## Delivery Time

- The Aadhaar PVC card is delivered by **India Post Speed Post**
- Expected delivery time: **7 to 15 working days** from the date of order
- The card is delivered to the **address registered in your Aadhaar**
- You cannot change the delivery address — it will be sent to your Aadhaar address only

> If you have recently updated your address, make sure the update is reflected before ordering the PVC card.

## How to Track Delivery

### Track Using SRN

1. Visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in) or [resident.uidai.gov.in](https://resident.uidai.gov.in)
2. Look for **"Check Aadhaar PVC Card Status"** or **"Track Order"**
3. Enter your **SRN (Service Request Number)** received at the time of order
4. View the current status of your card

### Track via India Post

Once your card is dispatched, you will receive a **Speed Post tracking number** via SMS on your registered mobile.

1. Visit [www.indiapost.gov.in](https://www.indiapost.gov.in)
2. Enter the **Speed Post consignment number**
3. Track real-time delivery status

## Order for Family Members

Yes, you can order Aadhaar PVC cards for your family members. Here's how:

- You need their **Aadhaar number** and access to their **registered mobile number** (for OTP)
- Log in to the myAadhaar portal using **their Aadhaar number**
- Follow the same ordering process
- Payment can be made from **any payment method** — it doesn't have to be in their name

> **Note:** Each order is for one Aadhaar PVC card. If you want cards for 4 family members, you need to place 4 separate orders and pay ₹50 for each.

## What If Card Not Delivered?

If your Aadhaar PVC card has not been delivered within 30 days:

1. **Check the status** using your SRN on the UIDAI website
2. **Track via Speed Post** using the consignment number
3. **Contact UIDAI helpline:**
   - **Toll-free number:** 1947
   - **Email:** help@uidai.gov.in
4. **Raise a grievance** on [uidai.gov.in](https://uidai.gov.in) under the grievance section
5. If the card was returned to sender (wrong address), **update your address first** and then re-order

### Common Reasons for Non-Delivery

- **Wrong address** in Aadhaar records
- **Incomplete address** — pin code or landmark missing
- **Nobody available** to receive the speed post
- **Address not serviceable** by India Post

> **Tip:** If your address has changed, [update it in Aadhaar first](/guide/aadhaar-common-problems-solutions) before ordering the PVC card.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q: Can I order Aadhaar PVC card without a registered mobile number?

**No.** You need a mobile number linked to your Aadhaar to receive the OTP. If your mobile is not registered, visit the nearest Aadhaar enrolment centre to update it.

### Q: Is the Aadhaar PVC card valid as an ID proof?

**Yes.** The Aadhaar PVC card is an officially valid identity document issued by UIDAI. It is accepted everywhere Aadhaar is accepted.

### Q: Can I order multiple PVC cards for the same Aadhaar number?

**Yes.** There is no limit. You can re-order anytime by paying ₹50 each time. This is useful if your card is lost or damaged.

### Q: Will the PVC card have my full Aadhaar number?

The PVC card displays a **masked Aadhaar number** (only last 4 digits visible) on the front. The full number is encoded in the QR code.

### Q: Can I order Aadhaar PVC card using Virtual ID (VID)?

**Yes.** On the resident.uidai.gov.in portal, you can use your 16-digit Virtual ID instead of your Aadhaar number to order the PVC card.

### Q: What if I entered wrong details while ordering?

The PVC card is printed with the details **already stored in your Aadhaar records**. You cannot change details during ordering. If your details are wrong, [update your Aadhaar first](/guide/aadhaar-common-problems-solutions).

### Q: Is there any way to get the PVC card faster?

**No.** The delivery is only through India Post Speed Post. There is no express or premium delivery option available.
