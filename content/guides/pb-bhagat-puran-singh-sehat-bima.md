## What is Bhagat Puran Singh Sehat Bima Yojana (BPSSBY)?

Bhagat Puran Singh Sehat Bima Yojana is Punjab's state health insurance scheme providing **cashless medical treatment** to families below the poverty line. Named after the renowned Sikh humanitarian Bhagat Puran Singh, the scheme now operates in convergence with **Ayushman Bharat – PMJAY**, providing coverage up to **₹5 lakh per family per year**.

### Coverage

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Coverage per family per year | ₹5,00,000 (₹5 lakh) |
| Procedures covered | 1,800+ medical/surgical |
| Pre-hospitalization | 5 days |
| Post-hospitalization | 15 days |
| Family members covered | All members on ration card |
| Treatment type | Cashless |

## Who is Eligible?

- **Blue card (BPL)** ration card holders in Punjab
- **SECC 2011** listed families
- Families covered under **AB-PMJAY** criteria
- **Yellow card** holders (NFSA/priority households)
- **Construction workers** registered with the Punjab Building Workers Welfare Board
- **Small and marginal farmers**
- Must be a **resident of Punjab**

## Documents Required

- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
- BPL ration card or NFSA card
- Ayushman Bharat card (if issued)
- Bank account details
- At hospital: Aadhaar for biometric verification

## How to Avail Benefits — Step-by-Step

### Checking Eligibility

1. Visit [pmjay.gov.in](https://pmjay.gov.in) or [mera.pmjay.gov.in](https://mera.pmjay.gov.in)
2. Enter mobile number and OTP
3. Search by name, ration card, or SECC data
4. Or call **14555** (Ayushman helpline) or **104**

### Getting Treatment

1. **Visit** any BPSSBY/AB-PMJAY empanelled hospital
2. **Go to** the Ayushman Mitra/help desk at the hospital
3. **Show** Aadhaar card and ration card
4. **Biometric verification** for identity confirmation
5. **Pre-authorization** — hospital sends treatment request
6. **Cashless treatment** — no payment for covered procedures
7. **Post-discharge** — follow-up covered for 15 days

### Getting Ayushman Card

1. Visit any **Common Service Centre (CSC)** or empanelled hospital
2. Carry Aadhaar and ration card
3. Biometric registration
4. **Ayushman card** issued (physical or digital)

## Finding Empanelled Hospitals

- Visit [sha.punjab.gov.in](https://sha.punjab.gov.in) for hospital list
- Call **14555** or **104** for assistance
- Search on [pmjay.gov.in](https://pmjay.gov.in)

## Processing Time

- Eligibility check: **Instant**
- Card issuance: **Same day** at CSC
- Pre-authorization at hospital: **2-6 hours**

## Fees

**Free.** No premium for BPL/NFSA families. Cashless treatment with no charges.

## Important Tips

1. **Get Ayushman card** — While not mandatory (Aadhaar works), having the card speeds up hospital verification
2. **Use empanelled hospitals** — Non-empanelled hospitals are not covered
3. **No middlemen** — Go directly to the hospital's Ayushman Mitra desk
4. **Report overcharging** — Call **14555** if any hospital demands money
5. **Works across India** — Under PMJAY portability, use at empanelled hospitals anywhere in India

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Is BPSSBY the same as Ayushman Bharat?
BPSSBY is Punjab's state scheme that now works in convergence with AB-PMJAY. Together, they provide ₹5 lakh coverage.

### Q2. Are pre-existing diseases covered?
Yes. **No waiting period** for pre-existing conditions.

### Q3. Can I use this scheme in another state?
Yes, under PMJAY portability at any **empanelled hospital across India**.

### Q4. How many family members are covered?
**All members** on the ration card. No cap on family size.

### Q5. Is maternity covered?
Yes. Normal and C-section deliveries are covered at empanelled hospitals.

### Q6. What if I don't have a ration card?
You may still be eligible if listed in the **SECC 2011** database. Check on the PMJAY portal.
