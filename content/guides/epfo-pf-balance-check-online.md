# How to Check PF/EPF Balance Online — 5 Easy Methods

Every salaried employee in India contributing to the Employee Provident Fund (EPF) can check their PF balance online. Whether you prefer using a website, app, SMS, or even a missed call — EPFO provides multiple ways to track your provident fund balance. Here is a complete guide.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent platform and is not affiliated with EPFO or the Government of India. Always verify details on official portals.

---

## What is EPF/PF Balance?

Your EPF (Employee Provident Fund) account receives monthly contributions from both you and your employer:

- **Employee Contribution:** 12% of basic salary + DA
- **Employer Contribution:** 12% of basic salary + DA (8.33% goes to EPS, 3.67% to EPF)
- **Interest Rate:** 8.25% per annum (FY 2024-25)

Your PF balance is the total accumulated amount including your contributions, employer's contributions, and interest earned.

---

## Method 1: Check PF Balance via EPFO Member Portal

1. Visit the EPFO member portal at [unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in)
2. Log in with your **UAN (Universal Account Number)** and **password**
3. Click on **"View"** → **"Passbook"**
4. You will be redirected to the passbook portal
5. Log in again with UAN and password
6. Select your **Member ID** from the dropdown
7. Your complete PF passbook with monthly contributions and balance will be displayed
8. You can **download the passbook as PDF**

**Note:** Your UAN must be activated and linked to your Aadhaar and mobile number.

---

## Method 2: Check PF Balance via EPFO Passbook Portal

1. Visit [passbook.epfindia.gov.in](https://passbook.epfindia.gov.in)
2. Enter your **UAN** and **password**
3. Enter the CAPTCHA
4. Click **"Log In"**
5. Select the relevant **Member ID** (if you have multiple employers)
6. Click **"View Passbook"**
7. Your month-wise EPF contributions and running balance will be shown

---

## Method 3: Check PF Balance via Umang App

1. Download the **UMANG app** from Google Play Store or Apple App Store
2. Register/log in with your mobile number
3. Search for **"EPFO"** in the app
4. Select **"Employee Centric Services"**
5. Tap on **"View Passbook"**
6. Enter your **UAN** and OTP received on registered mobile
7. Your PF balance and passbook will be displayed

---

## Method 4: Check PF Balance via SMS

Send an SMS from your UAN-registered mobile number:

**Format:** `EPFOHO UAN ENG` → Send to **7738299899**

- Replace `UAN` with your actual UAN number
- Replace `ENG` with your preferred language code:
  - ENG (English), HIN (Hindi), TAM (Tamil), TEL (Telugu), KAN (Kannada), MAL (Malayalam), BEN (Bengali), GUJ (Gujarati), MAR (Marathi), PUN (Punjabi)

You will receive an SMS with your PF balance details.

**Requirement:** Your UAN must be activated and linked to your Aadhaar, PAN, and bank account.

---

## Method 5: Check PF Balance via Missed Call

1. Give a **missed call to 011-22901406** from your UAN-registered mobile number
2. You will receive an **SMS** with your PF balance details within a few minutes

**Requirement:** Your UAN must be seeded with KYC (Aadhaar, PAN, and bank details).

## How to Find Your UAN Number

If you don't know your UAN:

1. **Check your salary slip** — UAN is usually mentioned
2. **Ask your HR department** — They can provide your UAN
3. **Visit EPFO portal** → **"Know Your UAN"** → Enter your member ID, Aadhaar, or PAN to retrieve it
4. **Check previous employer's records** — Your UAN remains the same across employers

---

## How to Activate Your UAN

1. Visit [unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Activate UAN"**
3. Enter your **UAN, Aadhaar, Name, Date of Birth, and Mobile Number**
4. Click **"Get Authorization Pin"**
5. Enter the OTP received on your mobile
6. Set your **password**
7. Your UAN is now activated for online access

## Understanding Your PF Passbook

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Wage Month | The month for which contribution was made |
| ECR (Employee) | Your contribution amount |
| ECR (Employer) | Employer's EPF contribution |
| EPS (Employer) | Employer's pension scheme contribution |
| Balance | Running total in your EPF account |

## Important Tips

1. **Link Aadhaar to UAN** — This is mandatory for online services, PF withdrawal, and transfer
2. **Check passbook quarterly** — Verify that your employer is depositing PF on time
3. **Transfer PF when changing jobs** — Use the online transfer facility to merge old and new PF accounts. See our PF Transfer guide if available
4. **Interest is credited annually** — EPF interest for the year is credited at the end of the financial year, so your balance may not reflect current year's interest until March
5. **Save your UAN** — Your UAN is permanent and stays with you throughout your career across all employers

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: Can I check PF balance without UAN?
No, UAN is essential for online PF balance check. If you don't have your UAN, ask your employer or use the "Know Your UAN" feature on the EPFO portal with your Aadhaar or PAN.

### Q2: Why is my PF passbook not showing recent contributions?
Employers upload ECR (Electronic Challan cum Return) monthly. If contributions are not showing, your employer may have delayed the filing. Contact your HR department to verify.

### Q3: Can I check PF balance for previous employers?
Yes, if your previous PF was linked to the same UAN. In the passbook portal, select the relevant Member ID from the dropdown to see balance from each employer.

### Q4: How often is PF balance updated online?
PF balance is updated once your employer files the monthly ECR. Typically, it reflects within 3-5 days of employer submission. Interest is updated at the end of the financial year.

### Q5: Is it safe to check PF balance online?
Yes, the EPFO portal uses secure authentication with UAN, password, and OTP verification. Always access through official portals only and avoid third-party websites.

### Q6: What should I do if my PF balance shows zero?
This could mean your employer hasn't filed contributions, your UAN is not linked to the correct member ID, or you have already withdrawn the balance. Contact your employer or the nearest EPFO office.
