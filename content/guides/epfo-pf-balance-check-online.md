## What is EPF (Employees' Provident Fund)?

EPF is a retirement savings scheme managed by **EPFO (Employees' Provident Fund Organisation)** under the Ministry of Labour and Employment. Both the employee and employer contribute **12% of basic salary** each month to the EPF account. Every EPF member gets a **Universal Account Number (UAN)** to access their PF account online.

EPF provides:
- **Retirement corpus** with compound interest (current rate: 8.25% for FY 2023-24)
- **Partial withdrawal** for housing, medical, education, and marriage
- **Pension** under EPS (Employees' Pension Scheme)
- **Insurance** under EDLI (Employees' Deposit Linked Insurance Scheme)

## How to Check EPF/PF Balance Online

### Method 1: EPFO Member Passbook (Recommended)

1. Visit [https://passbook.epfindia.gov.in](https://passbook.epfindia.gov.in)
2. Enter your **UAN (Universal Account Number)**
3. Enter your **passbook password** (set during UAN activation)
4. Enter the captcha and click **"Login"**
5. Select your **Member ID** (if you have multiple)
6. Your complete **EPF passbook** with monthly contributions will be displayed
7. You can see: employee share, employer share, pension contribution, and total balance
8. Click **"Download Passbook"** for a PDF copy

### Method 2: EPFO Unified Member Portal

1. Go to [https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in/memberinterface/](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in/memberinterface/)
2. Log in with your **UAN** and **password**
3. Click on **"View"** → **"Passbook"**
4. You will be redirected to the passbook portal
5. This portal also lets you check **claim status**, **KYC details**, and **service history**

### Method 3: UMANG App

1. Download the **UMANG app** from Google Play Store or Apple App Store
2. Register/login with your mobile number
3. Search for **"EPFO"** and select it
4. Choose **"View Passbook"** or **"Track Claim"**
5. Enter your **UAN** and verify via OTP
6. Your EPF balance and passbook details will be shown

## Check EPF Balance via SMS and Missed Call

### Missed Call Method
- Give a **missed call to 011-22901406** from your UAN-registered mobile number
- You will receive an SMS with your latest **EPF balance** and last contribution details
- Your UAN must be activated and mobile number linked

### SMS Method
- Send an SMS: **EPFOHO UAN** to **7738299899**
- Example: `EPFOHO 100123456789`
- You will receive your PF balance details via SMS
- If you prefer a specific language, send: **EPFOHO UAN ENG** (for English), replace ENG with HIN (Hindi), TAM (Tamil), etc.

## How to Check PF Claim Status

1. Visit [https://www.epfindia.gov.in](https://www.epfindia.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Services"** → **"Know Your Claim Status"**
3. Enter your **UAN number** and captcha
4. Click **"Search"**
5. Your claim status will show: Claim submitted → Under process → Settled → Amount credited
6. Alternatively, log in to the **Unified Member Portal** and go to **"Track Claim Status"** under the "Online Services" tab

## Common Issues and Fixes

### 1. "UAN Not Activated" Error
- Your employer must first generate your UAN
- Once generated, activate it at the **Unified Member Portal** using your UAN, Aadhaar, and mobile number
- You need Aadhaar-linked mobile for OTP verification

### 2. Passbook Showing "No Records Found"
- Your employer may not have uploaded the latest ECR (Electronic Challan cum Return)
- Wait until the 15th of the following month and check again
- Contact your employer's HR to confirm PF deposit

### 3. Forgot UAN or Password
- **Forgot UAN:** Visit the Unified Member Portal → click "Know Your UAN" → enter Aadhaar/PAN/member ID to retrieve it
- **Forgot Password:** Click "Forgot Password" on the login page → verify via UAN + Aadhaar/mobile OTP → reset password

### 4. PF Claim Rejected
- Common reasons: KYC not approved, bank details mismatch, Aadhaar not verified
- Ensure your **Aadhaar, PAN, and bank account** are verified in the UAN portal
- Check our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) if you need to update Aadhaar details

### 5. Old PF Account Not Visible
- If you changed jobs, your old PF account may be under a different Member ID
- Use **"Transfer Request"** on the Unified Member Portal to merge old and new accounts under one UAN

## Important Tips

1. **Activate your UAN immediately** after joining a new company — don't wait
2. **Link Aadhaar, PAN, and bank account** to your UAN for seamless withdrawals
3. **Check passbook monthly** to ensure your employer is depositing PF regularly
4. **Use the UMANG app** for quick balance checks on mobile
5. **Do not share your UAN password** with anyone, including your employer

## FAQs

### Q1: How do I check my PF balance without logging in?
Give a missed call to **011-22901406** from your registered mobile, or SMS **EPFOHO UAN** to **7738299899**. You'll get your balance via SMS.

### Q2: How often is the EPF passbook updated?
The passbook is updated once your employer uploads the monthly ECR. This typically happens by the **15th of the following month**.

### Q3: Can I check PF balance using Aadhaar number?
Not directly. You need your UAN to check PF balance. If you don't know your UAN, use the "Know Your UAN" feature with your Aadhaar on the EPFO portal.

### Q4: How long does a PF withdrawal claim take?
Online PF claims are typically processed within **10-20 working days** if KYC is complete and verified. Complex cases may take longer.

### Q5: What is the current EPF interest rate?
The EPF interest rate for FY 2023-24 is **8.25%** per annum. Interest is calculated monthly but credited annually.

### Q6: Can I withdraw my full PF balance while still employed?
No. Full withdrawal is allowed only after **retirement (age 58)** or **2 months of unemployment**. Partial withdrawals are allowed for specific purposes like home purchase, medical emergency, or education.

### Q7: How to check how much pension I will get from EPS?
Log in to the Unified Member Portal and check your **service history** and **pension eligibility**. You need a minimum of 10 years of service for pension under EPS.
