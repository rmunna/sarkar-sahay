# Kotak Mahindra Bank KYC Update Online 2026 — Without Branch Visit

Kotak Mahindra Bank requires KYC updates periodically. Kotak 811 (zero-balance digital account) customers specifically need to complete **Video KYC to upgrade from minimum KYC to full KYC** — which unlocks higher balance limits and full banking features.

---

## Kotak 811 KYC — Minimum vs Full KYC Difference

| Feature | Minimum KYC (811) | Full KYC (Video KYC done) |
|---------|-------------------|---------------------------|
| Balance limit | ₹1 lakh | No limit |
| Validity | 12 months | Lifetime (until next periodic re-KYC) |
| NEFT/IMPS sending | ₹2 lakh/month | Full limits |
| Debit card | Virtual only | Physical card eligible |
| Fixed deposits | Not available | Available |

**If you opened Kotak 811 online**, you need to complete Video KYC within 12 months to retain full functionality.

---

## Method 1: Video KYC via Kotak Mobile Banking App (Essential for 811)

1. Open **Kotak Mobile Banking** app → Login
2. Look for **"Complete Video KYC"** banner (appears for 811 minimum KYC holders)
3. OR: **"Profile"** → **"KYC Status"** → **"Start Video KYC"**
4. Grant camera + microphone permissions
5. Keep ready:
   - Original Aadhaar card (not photocopy)
   - PAN card
   - Good internet connection + bright lighting
6. Kotak executive joins for a ~5 minute video call
7. During call: Show Aadhaar (front + back), PAN, answer basic questions
8. **Full KYC approved on the call — limits upgraded immediately**

---

## Method 2: Document Upload for Periodic Re-KYC

For existing full-KYC customers receiving periodic re-KYC notice:

1. Kotak Mobile Banking app → **"Profile"** → **"Update KYC Documents"**
2. Upload:
   - Aadhaar (front + back)
   - PAN card
   - Live selfie (in-app camera)
3. Digital declaration accepted in-app
4. Submit → Reference number generated
5. **Processing: 24–72 hours**

---

## Method 3: Kotak Net Banking

1. Login at **[netbanking.kotak.com](https://netbanking.kotak.com)**
2. **"My Accounts"** → **"Profile Settings"** → **"KYC Documents"**
3. Upload scanned Aadhaar + PAN (self-attested)
4. Submit → Processing 2–4 working days

---

## Method 4: Kotak Bank Branch

1. Visit any Kotak Mahindra Bank branch
2. Carry original Aadhaar + PAN (originals for verification, give self-attested copies)
3. Fill **"KYC Update Form"** at the service desk
4. Same-day processing for standard re-KYC
5. Note: For 811 accounts, Video KYC can also be done at branch on their tablet

## Kotak NRI KYC Requirements

For NRI customers with Kotak NRE/NRO accounts:
- Passport copy (notarised or attested by Indian embassy)
- Foreign address proof (overseas bank statement / utility bill)
- Overseas bank-attested copies accepted
- Contact Kotak NRI services: **+91-22-6600-6022**

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Kotak 811 — what happens if I don't complete Video KYC within 12 months?**
Minimum KYC accounts expire after 12 months if full Video KYC is not done. Account is frozen — you can only withdraw existing balance. Complete Video KYC immediately to reactivate.

**Kotak Video KYC schedule — when is it available?**
Video KYC is available Monday to Saturday, 9 AM – 6 PM. Kotak executives are usually available within 5 minutes. Avoid trying on public holidays.

**I completed Kotak Video KYC but balance limit not increased yet?**
Allow up to 2 hours post-call for limit upgrade to reflect in-app. If not updated in 4 hours: Call **1860-266-0811** with Video KYC reference number.

**Kotak KYC rejected — Aadhaar name doesn't match account name?**
This is common if you opened account with a name variation. Solution: Visit branch with Aadhaar + PAN + account opening documents. Request name correction + KYC update simultaneously.

**Can I transfer my Kotak 811 Video KYC to another Kotak account?**
No — KYC is per-account. If you have multiple Kotak accounts, each needs independent KYC. However, Video KYC done once is recorded by Kotak and branch KYC updates are typically faster for subsequent accounts.
