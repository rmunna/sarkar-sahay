# IBPS PO 2026 — Complete Guide to Become a Bank Probationary Officer

The IBPS PO (Probationary Officer) examination is conducted by the Institute of Banking Personnel Selection to recruit Probationary Officers across 11 major public sector banks in India. It is one of the most coveted banking exams with 4,000–5,000 vacancies per year.

Getting selected as a Bank PO means a starting salary of ₹52,000+, prestigious career, pension benefits and rapid promotion prospects.

## Syllabus

### Reasoning Ability
Seating Arrangement (Linear/Circular), Puzzles, Blood Relations, Coding-Decoding, Syllogism, Inequality, Input-Output, Data Sufficiency, Logical Reasoning, Direction Sense

### Quantitative Aptitude / DI
Data Interpretation (Bar, Pie, Line, Table, Caselet), Number Series, Simplification/Approximation, Quadratic Equations, Percentage, Ratio, Profit/Loss, SI/CI, Time-Work, Time-Distance, Mensuration, Probability, Permutation-Combination

### English Language
Reading Comprehension, Error Detection, Sentence Correction, Fill in the Blanks, Para Jumbles, Cloze Test, Vocabulary, Connectors, Word Usage/Replacement

### Banking & Economy Awareness
RBI policies, Monetary Policy, Banking Acts, Financial Institutions, Stock Market basics, Budget & Economic Survey highlights, Insurance, Current Affairs (Banking & Economy focus)

## How to Apply

1. Go to **[ibps.in](https://www.ibps.in)**
2. Click on **"CRP PO/MT"** (Common Recruitment Process for PO/Management Trainee)
3. Register with email ID and mobile number
4. Fill the application form with personal, educational details
5. Upload photo (20–50 KB) and signature (10–20 KB)
6. Select exam city preferences
7. Pay application fee:
   - General/OBC/EWS: ₹850
   - SC/ST/PwBD: ₹175 (intimation charges only)
8. Submit and save the application form

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is IBPS PO easier than SBI PO?**
Both are similar in difficulty. SBI PO tends to have slightly higher difficulty in Reasoning and DI. IBPS PO is more predictable in pattern.

**Can I apply for multiple bank PO exams simultaneously?**
Yes — IBPS PO, SBI PO, and individual bank PO exams are separate. You can apply for all.

**Is there any bond period after joining as PO?**
No bond period in most banks. However, you may have to serve for 2–3 years before applying for specialist officer roles internally.

**How many times can I attempt IBPS PO?**
No attempt limit — you can appear every year as long as you're within the age limit.

**When is the salary hike for PO?**
DA is revised every quarter. Basic pay revision happens under bipartite settlement (typically every 5 years for PSBs).
