# SBI Net Banking Login Not Working — How to Fix Password, OTP & Locked Account Issues

State Bank of India's internet banking portal (OnlineSBI) lets you manage accounts, transfer funds, pay bills, and more — all from your browser. But login issues are extremely common. This guide walks you through **every** reason your SBI net banking login might fail and exactly how to fix it.

## Common SBI Net Banking Login Errors

| Error / Symptom | Likely Cause |
|---|---|
| "Invalid User ID or Password" | Wrong login password or username |
| "Your account is locked" | 3+ incorrect password attempts |
| OTP not received on mobile | Network issue, unregistered number, DND |
| "Kit number not valid" | Wrong Internet Banking Kit reference |
| Can't tell login password from profile password | Confusion between the two SBI passwords |
| Login works on YONO but not OnlineSBI (or vice versa) | Different credentials or activation status |

---

## Fix 1: Wrong Password — "Invalid User ID or Password"

**Why it happens:**
- You're entering the profile password instead of the login password (they are different — see Fix 5 below).
- Caps Lock is on or you're mistyping special characters.
- Password was recently changed and you're using the old one.

**How to fix:**

1. Go to [retail.onlinesbi.sbi](https://retail.onlinesbi.sbi/retail/login.htm).
2. Click **"Forgot Login Password"** on the login page.
3. Enter your **username**, verify with your registered mobile OTP or ATM card details.
4. Set a new login password following SBI's password rules:
   - 8–20 characters
   - At least 1 uppercase, 1 lowercase, 1 digit, and 1 special character
   - No spaces, no part of your username
5. Log in with the new password.

> **Tip:** If you also forgot your username, click **"Forgot Username"** on the same page and retrieve it via your registered mobile number + account number.

## Fix 2: Account Locked After 3 Incorrect Attempts

SBI automatically locks your internet banking account after **3 consecutive wrong password attempts** as a security measure.

**How to unlock:**

### Option A: Wait 24 Hours
The account unlocks automatically after 24 hours. Try logging in again with the correct password.

### Option B: Reset Password Immediately
1. Visit [retail.onlinesbi.sbi](https://retail.onlinesbi.sbi/retail/login.htm).
2. Click **"Forgot Login Password"**.
3. Authenticate via OTP or ATM card + PIN.
4. Create a new login password — this also unlocks the account.

### Option C: Visit Your SBI Branch
- Carry your **passbook, PAN card, and Aadhaar**.
- Request internet banking re-activation.
- They will issue new temporary credentials.

> **Note:** Account lock does NOT affect ATM, YONO MPIN, or UPI access — only OnlineSBI web login.

## Fix 3: OTP Not Received

OTPs are sent to your **registered mobile number** via SMS. If you're not receiving them:

**Check these first:**
- ✅ Mobile number registered with SBI (check passbook or call 1800-11-2211).
- ✅ Phone has network signal and SMS inbox isn't full.
- ✅ DND (Do Not Disturb) is not blocking transactional SMS — call your operator and whitelist SBI.
- ✅ SBI's SMS sender (e.g., SBI, ONLSBI) is not blocked in your phone's spam filter.

**Still not receiving?**

1. Wait 2–3 minutes — SBI OTPs can be delayed during peak hours.
2. Click **"Resend OTP"** on the login page.
3. Restart your phone and retry.
4. If your mobile number has changed, visit your SBI branch with Aadhaar and PAN to update it. OTP-based services won't work until the number is updated.

## Fix 4: Internet Banking Kit Number Issues

When you apply for SBI internet banking at a branch, you receive an **Internet Banking Kit** containing a temporary username and password.

**Common kit number problems:**

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Kit expired (not activated within 7 days) | Visit branch and request a fresh kit |
| Lost the kit slip | Request a reissue at branch with ID proof |
| "Kit number not valid" error | Ensure you're entering the **CIF number or kit reference** correctly, not your account number |
| Already activated but forgot credentials | Use "Forgot Password" / "Forgot Username" on OnlineSBI |

**First-time activation steps:**

1. Go to [retail.onlinesbi.sbi](https://retail.onlinesbi.sbi/retail/login.htm).
2. Click **"New User Registration / Activation"**.
3. Enter the temporary username from your kit.
4. Complete OTP verification and set your own username + login password + profile password.

## Fix 5: Login Password vs Profile Password — The Difference

SBI internet banking has **two separate passwords**, and confusing them is one of the most common login issues.

| | Login Password | Profile Password |
|---|---|---|
| **Used for** | Logging into OnlineSBI | Authorizing transactions (fund transfer, bill pay, etc.) |
| **When set** | During first-time activation | During first-time activation (set separately) |
| **Reset method** | "Forgot Login Password" on OnlineSBI | "Forgot Profile Password" after logging in, or via branch |
| **Locked separately?** | Yes — login lock ≠ profile lock | Yes |

**Key rule:** You log in with your **login password** and approve transactions with your **profile password**. If one works but the other doesn't, reset only the one that's failing.

## Fix 6: Reset SBI Net Banking Password via ATM

If you can't receive OTPs or access the online reset, use any SBI ATM:

1. Insert your **SBI ATM/debit card** and enter your PIN.
2. Select **"Registration"** from the menu.
3. Choose **"Internet Banking"**.
4. Select **"Generate OTP for Password Reset"** or similar option.
5. You'll receive a one-time password on your registered mobile.
6. Go to OnlineSBI → **"Forgot Login Password"** → use this OTP to set a new password.

> Not all SBI ATMs show this option. If unavailable, visit the branch.

## Fix 7: Internet Banking (OnlineSBI) vs YONO — Key Differences

Many users confuse SBI's two digital platforms. They share the same bank account but have **separate login credentials**.

| Feature | OnlineSBI (Internet Banking) | YONO App |
|---|---|---|
| **Platform** | Web browser | Mobile app (Android/iOS) |
| **Login** | Username + Login Password | Username + Password or MPIN/Biometric |
| **Passwords** | Login Password + Profile Password | App password + MPIN |
| **Registration** | Via branch kit or online | Through YONO app (needs net banking) |
| **Key use** | Full banking, PPF, FD, tax payments | Quick transfers, YONO Mall, investments |

**Important:**
- YONO requires **active internet banking** to register. If OnlineSBI login is broken, fix it first.
- Resetting your password on OnlineSBI does **not** change your YONO password or MPIN.
- If YONO is also not working, see our [YONO app troubleshooting guide](/guides/sbi-yono-app-not-working-fix).

## Other Quick Fixes

### Browser Issues
- Clear cookies and cache, or try **incognito mode**.
- Use **Chrome or Firefox** — OnlineSBI works best on these.
- Disable ad-blockers or VPN — they can interfere with SBI's security scripts.

### "Service Temporarily Unavailable" / Server Down
- SBI has scheduled maintenance windows (usually late night, Sundays).
- Check [sbi.co.in](https://www.sbi.co.in) for maintenance notices.
- Wait 30 minutes and retry.

### Virtual Keyboard Not Loading
- Enable JavaScript in your browser.
- Disable any browser extension that blocks scripts.
- Switch to a different browser.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many wrong attempts lock SBI net banking?
Three consecutive incorrect login password attempts will lock your account for 24 hours.

### Can I unlock SBI net banking without visiting the branch?
Yes. Use the **"Forgot Login Password"** option on OnlineSBI and authenticate via OTP or ATM card details.

### My mobile number is not registered with SBI. Can I still reset my password?
No. You need to visit your SBI branch with Aadhaar and PAN to update your mobile number first.

### Is SBI internet banking the same as YONO?
No. They share the same bank account but have separate credentials and platforms. See the comparison table above.

### I changed my password but still can't log in. What should I do?
Clear your browser cache, try incognito mode, and ensure you're on the correct site: [retail.onlinesbi.sbi](https://retail.onlinesbi.sbi/retail/login.htm). If the issue persists, call 1800-11-2211.
