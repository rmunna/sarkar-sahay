This guide gives you a head-to-head comparison so you can make the right call in under 5 minutes.

## App & Platform Quality

### Zerodha Kite
- **Web:** Kite.zerodha.com is the gold standard for Indian trading platforms
- **Charting:** 100+ indicators, advanced drawing tools
- **Screener:** Streak (algo trading), Smallcase, Sensibull (options) integrations
- **Learning:** Zerodha Varsity — best free trading education in India
- **Cons:** Can feel overwhelming for beginners; no phone support

### Groww App
- **Mobile-first design:** Cleanest, most intuitive app in India
- **Mutual funds:** Best MF discovery, portfolio tracking
- **Stocks:** Simple buy/sell, basic charts
- **FD, digital gold, US stocks:** All available
- **Cons:** Limited advanced charting; not ideal for intraday trading

### Upstox Pro
- **Web + Mobile:** Good balance of simplicity and features
- **Charts:** Adequate for most investors, TradingView integration
- **Options trading:** Upstox Options Analytics available
- **Cons:** Customer care can be slow; AMC charges kick in Year 2

✅ You're a beginner who also wants decent trading tools

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Zerodha or Groww better for beginners?**
Groww is better for absolute beginners — simpler app, free mutual fund SIP, no AMC charges. Zerodha is better once you want to trade actively and need advanced tools.

**Does Groww charge for stocks?**
Groww charges zero brokerage for equity delivery (buy and hold). For intraday trading, it charges ₹20 per order or 0.05% (whichever is lower), plus standard exchange/regulatory charges.

**Is Upstox safe after SEBI action in 2022?**
Yes — Upstox is fully operational. The 2022 SEBI action was related to system issues, not fraud. It has since been resolved and Upstox remains one of India's top 3 brokers.

**Which broker has the best customer support?**
Honestly, customer support is a known weakness across all three. Zerodha has a comprehensive support portal and chatbot. Groww has in-app chat. Upstox has call support. For urgent issues, visiting a branch (Zerodha offices in Bengaluru, Mumbai) is fastest.

**Can I transfer my demat account from one broker to another?**
Yes — you can transfer holdings using DIS (Delivery Instruction Slip) or CDSL Easiest/NSDL online transfer. Account transfer is possible but takes 7–15 days. Brokerage charges may apply.
