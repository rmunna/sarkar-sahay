# PPF Interest Calculation Rules India 2026 — The 5th Date Rule Explained

Most PPF investors don't know that depositing on the 6th of April instead of the 4th costs them money. Here's exactly how PPF interest is calculated and how to maximize your returns.

---

## The Rule: Interest on Minimum Balance Between 5th and Last Day

**RBI / Finance Ministry rule**: PPF interest is calculated on the **minimum balance in your account between the 5th and the last day of each month**.

This means:
- If you deposit BEFORE the 5th → your new balance is considered for that month's interest
- If you deposit ON or AFTER the 5th → only the OLD balance counts; new deposit earns no interest that month

**The money deposited after the 5th starts earning interest from NEXT month only.**

---

## How Much Does the 5th Rule Matter?

**Example**: Annual deposit of ₹1,50,000 in lump sum.

| Deposit Date | Months Earning Interest in FY | Annual Interest Difference |
|-------------|------------------------------|---------------------------|
| April 1 (before 5th) | 12 months of ₹1.5L balance | Maximum interest |
| April 6 (after 5th) | 11 months of ₹1.5L | Lose 1 month's interest |
| March 31 next year | 0 months on this deposit (counts for next FY) | No interest this FY |

**Exact calculation** (at 7.1%):
- ₹1,50,000 × 7.1% / 12 = **₹887.50 per month**
- One month lost by depositing after 5th = **₹887.50 forgone per year**
- Over 15 years compounding: This one-day difference compounds to roughly **₹25,000 less** at maturity

**Always deposit your PPF amount by the 4th of April** for maximum interest.

---

## Monthly Interest Calculation — Step by Step

PPF interest is calculated on minimum balance between 5th and end of each month.

**Formula (monthly)**: `Monthly Interest = Minimum Balance (5th to last day) × 7.1% / 12`

**Example**: April 2026

| Date | Event | Balance |
|------|-------|---------|
| April 1 | Start of month | ₹3,00,000 |
| April 4 | You deposit ₹1,50,000 | ₹4,50,000 |
| April 5 | Balance for interest calc | ₹4,50,000 |
| April 30 | End of month | ₹4,50,000 |

- Minimum balance (5th to 30th): ₹4,50,000
- Interest for April: ₹4,50,000 × 7.1% / 12 = **₹2,662**

**If you had deposited April 6:**

| Date | Event | Balance |
|------|-------|---------|
| April 1 | Start of month | ₹3,00,000 |
| April 5 | Balance for interest calc | ₹3,00,000 |
| April 6 | You deposit ₹1,50,000 | ₹4,50,000 |
| April 30 | End of month | ₹4,50,000 |

- Minimum balance (5th to 30th): ₹3,00,000 (your deposit came after 5th)
- Interest for April: ₹3,00,000 × 7.1% / 12 = **₹1,775**
- **Lost**: ₹2,662 − ₹1,775 = **₹887 in April alone**

## When is PPF Interest Credited?

**Common misconception**: PPF interest is not credited monthly.

**Reality**: 
- Interest is CALCULATED monthly (as above)
- Interest is CREDITED once a year — **on March 31** (end of financial year)

You see the credit in your PPF passbook only on March 31. The monthly calculated amounts accumulate silently and appear as one lump sum on March 31.

**Compounding**: Officially annual (interest earned each year is added to principal for next year's calculation).

---

## SIP in PPF vs Lump Sum — Which is Better?

**Lump sum on April 1**: Earns interest for all 12 months → Maximum interest.

**Monthly SIP**: Each monthly deposit earns interest only for months remaining.

| Method | Annual Interest (at ₹1.5L total, 7.1%) |
|--------|---------------------------------------|
| Lump sum on April 1 | ₹10,650 |
| ₹12,500/month (deposited on 1st each month) | ₹7,021 (weighted average ~6 months earning) |
| ₹12,500/month (deposited on 10th each month) | ₹5,250 (each deposit earns for following months only) |

**Lump sum on April 1 gives maximum interest**. Monthly SIP is better for cash flow management — but you lose ~₹3,600–5,400 in annual interest vs lump sum.

**Compromise**: If you can't do lump sum, do SIP but always before the 5th of each month.

## After 15 Years — Extension Options

At maturity (15 years), you have 3 choices:

1. **Close and withdraw**: Full amount, tax-free.
2. **Extend without contribution**: Keep balance earning 7.1% for 5-year blocks. Withdraw anytime. (Best if you don't need more 80C benefit)
3. **Extend with contribution**: Continue depositing ₹1.5L/year. Full withdrawal allowed only at end of 5-year extension. Partial withdrawal (one per year) of up to 60% of balance at start of extension allowed.

**Submit Form H** to bank/post office before maturity to choose options 2 or 3.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### If I deposit on April 5, does it count for that month?

Yes — April 5 is BEFORE the "after 5th" cutoff. The rule says no interest if deposited AFTER 5th. So April 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5 all qualify for that month's interest. Only April 6 onwards misses April's interest.

### I forgot to deposit in April. When should I deposit now?

Deposit before the 5th of any subsequent month to earn full interest for that month. Even if it's September, depositing by September 4th earns September + remaining months' interest.

### Can I deposit monthly via auto-debit/standing instruction?

Yes — most banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI etc.) allow standing instructions to auto-debit from savings to PPF monthly. Set it for the 1st or 2nd of each month to ensure it credits before the 5th.

### I deposited ₹1,50,000 in March. Did it earn interest for March?

Depends on the date. If deposited before March 5: Yes, earns for March (minimum balance on March 5 includes this). If after March 5: No interest for March — but this deposit counts toward the next financial year's balance.

### What happens if PPF rate changes mid-year?

New rate applies from the quarter of notification. Typically April 1 (Q1), July 1 (Q2), October 1 (Q3), January 1 (Q4). If rate changes on July 1, interest for April–June is at old rate, July onwards at new rate.
