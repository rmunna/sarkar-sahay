# Ration Card Biometric Authentication Failed at Shop? How to Fix and Get Ration

You stand in line at the fair price shop, place your finger on the machine, and it says **"Authentication Failed."** The dealer turns you away. For families who depend on subsidized ration — rice at ₹3/kg, wheat at ₹2/kg — this isn't just inconvenient. It means going hungry.

This guide covers every fix, from immediate workarounds to permanent solutions.

## Why Biometric Authentication Fails at Ration Shops

| Cause | Who It Affects Most | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Worn fingerprints | Manual labourers, elderly, brick workers | Very common |
| Dry/wet fingers | Everyone in extreme weather | Common |
| Dirty ePoS machine sensor | All beneficiaries at that shop | Common |
| Poor internet/connectivity | Rural and remote areas | Very common |
| Aadhaar not seeded with ration card | New card holders, migrants | Common |
| Wrong Aadhaar mapped | Data entry errors | Occasional |
| ePoS machine malfunction | All beneficiaries at that shop | Occasional |
| Aadhaar biometric locked | Security-conscious users | Rare |

## Immediate Fix: Get Your Ration Today

### Option 1: OTP-Based Authentication
If fingerprint fails, ask the dealer to try **OTP authentication**:

1. Request the dealer to switch to **OTP mode** on the ePoS machine
2. OTP is sent to your **Aadhaar-linked mobile number**
3. Tell the dealer the OTP
4. Authentication succeeds → Collect your ration

> ⚠️ **Prerequisite:** Your mobile number must be linked to your Aadhaar. If it's not, this won't work — see permanent fixes below.

### Option 2: Try Different Fingers
All 10 fingerprints are registered in Aadhaar. If one fails:
- Try **index finger** of the other hand
- Try **thumb** of either hand
- Try **middle finger**
- Clean your fingers and the scanner surface before each attempt

### Option 3: Another Family Member
Under NFSA rules, **any family member** whose Aadhaar is linked to the ration card can authenticate:
- Spouse, adult children, or any linked member
- Only one person needs to authenticate for the entire family's ration

### Option 4: Request Manual Distribution (Exception Handling)
Most states have a provision for **exception handling**:
- If biometric fails for a beneficiary, the dealer can distribute ration
- The transaction is recorded manually and reconciled later
- Ask the dealer to use **"exception list"** or **"biometric failure"** option on ePoS
- **If the dealer refuses**, note the date and report to the food department

## Permanent Fixes

### Fix 1: Link Aadhaar with Ration Card

If your Aadhaar isn't linked ("seeded") with your ration card, biometric won't work.

**How to check:**
1. Visit your state's **PDS/food department portal** (links below)
2. Enter ration card number
3. Check if Aadhaar numbers are shown against family members

**How to link:**
1. Visit your **Tehsil/Block food supply office**
2. Carry: Ration card + Aadhaar cards of all family members
3. Fill the **Aadhaar seeding form**
4. Biometric verification at the office
5. Linking takes **7-15 days**

**State PDS portals:**

| State | Portal |
|---|---|
| UP | fcs.up.gov.in |
| Bihar | sfc.bihar.gov.in |
| MP | nfsa.samagra.gov.in |

| Telangana | epds.telangana.gov.in |
| West Bengal | wbpds.gov.in |
| Odisha | pdsodisha.gov.in |

### Fix 2: Update Aadhaar Biometrics

For persistent fingerprint failures (elderly, labourers):

1. Visit nearest **Aadhaar Enrolment Centre**
2. Request **biometric update** — fresh fingerprint + iris + photo
3. Fee: **₹100**
4. Get **URN** (Update Request Number)
5. Wait **7-30 days** for update to process
6. After update, iris scan can be used as fallback at ration shops with iris-capable ePoS machines

### Fix 3: Link Mobile Number to Aadhaar

To enable OTP-based authentication as fallback:

1. Visit **Aadhaar Enrolment Centre** (cannot be done online)
2. Request mobile number update
3. Fee: **₹50**
4. Takes **7-30 days** to process
5. Once linked, OTP authentication works at ration shops

### Fix 4: Correct Wrong Aadhaar Mapping

If someone else's Aadhaar is linked to your ration card slot:

1. Visit **food supply office** with correct Aadhaar + ration card
2. Request **de-seeding** of wrong Aadhaar and **re-seeding** of correct one
3. Carry: Original ration card, correct Aadhaar, ID proof

## How to Report a Ration Shop That Refuses Service

If the dealer repeatedly denies ration despite the biometric failure being on their end:

1. **State food helpline:** Every state has one (often 1967 or 1800-xxx-xxxx)
2. **District Supply Officer (DSO):** Visit or call the district food supply office
3. **Grievance portals:**
   - Central: [pgportal.gov.in](https://pgportal.gov.in/) → Ministry of Consumer Affairs
   - State-specific food department grievance portals
4. **National Food Security portal:** [nfsa.gov.in](https://nfsa.gov.in/) → Grievance
5. **CM Helpline:** Most states have a CM helpline (e.g., UP: 1076, MP: 181)

## Your Rights Under NFSA

Under the **National Food Security Act, 2013**:

- **5 kg per person per month** for priority households (₹2/kg wheat, ₹3/kg rice, ₹1/kg coarse grains)
- **35 kg per household per month** for Antyodaya (AAY) families
- If ration is **not provided**, you can demand **food security allowance** from the state government
- **Biometric failure is not a valid reason** to deny ration — the dealer must use exception handling

## Portability: Get Ration from Any Shop

Under **One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC)**, you can collect ration from any fair price shop in India:

- Useful if your local shop's machine is always broken
- Biometric authentication works the same at any ePoS-enabled shop
- Just carry your Aadhaar card — the system pulls your ration card details automatically
