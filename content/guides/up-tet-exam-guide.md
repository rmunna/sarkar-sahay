# UP TET Exam Guide — Eligibility, Pattern, Syllabus & Preparation Tips

The Uttar Pradesh Teacher Eligibility Test (UPTET) is conducted by the UP Basic Education Board for candidates who wish to become teachers in government primary and upper primary schools in Uttar Pradesh. UPTET qualification is mandatory for teaching positions in Classes 1-8 in UP.

Clearing UPTET opens doors to lakhs of assistant teacher vacancies under the UP Basic Education Department.

## Eligibility Criteria

### Age Limit

| Category | Minimum Age | Maximum Age |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| General | 18 years | No upper limit |

*There is no upper age limit for UPTET. However, for teacher recruitment, age limits apply separately.*

### Educational Qualification

**Paper 1 (Classes 1-5 — Primary):**
- Graduation with at least 50% marks AND 2-year D.El.Ed/BTC/B.Ed
- OR Senior Secondary (12th) with at least 50% marks AND 4-year B.El.Ed
- OR Senior Secondary with at least 50% marks AND 2-year D.El.Ed (by NCTE recognized institution)

**Paper 2 (Classes 6-8 — Upper Primary):**
- Graduation with at least 50% marks AND B.Ed from NCTE recognized institution
- OR Graduation with 45% marks AND B.Ed (as per NCTE 2002 norms)

## Exam Pattern

### Paper 1 (Primary Level: Classes 1-5)

| Subject | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| Child Development & Pedagogy | 30 | 30 |
| Language I (Hindi) | 30 | 30 |
| Language II (English/Urdu/Sanskrit) | 30 | 30 |
| Mathematics | 30 | 30 |
| Environmental Studies | 30 | 30 |
| **Total** | **150** | **150** |

### Paper 2 (Upper Primary Level: Classes 6-8)

| Subject | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| Child Development & Pedagogy | 30 | 30 |
| Language I (Hindi) | 30 | 30 |
| Language II (English/Urdu/Sanskrit) | 30 | 30 |
| Mathematics / Science OR Social Studies | 60 | 60 |
| **Total** | **150** | **150** |

- **Duration:** 2 hours 30 minutes per paper
- **No negative marking**
- **Qualifying marks:** General — 60%, OBC — 55%, SC/ST — 55%

## Syllabus Overview

### Child Development & Pedagogy
- Growth and development concepts, learning theories, Piaget, Vygotsky, Kohlberg, inclusive education, assessment methods, NCF 2005

### Language I & II
- Reading comprehension, grammar, pedagogy of language teaching

### Mathematics (Paper 1)
- Number system, basic operations, geometry, shapes, measurement, data handling, pedagogy of mathematics

### Environmental Studies (Paper 1)
- Family, food, shelter, water, animals, plants, transport, social issues, pedagogy of EVS

### Mathematics & Science / Social Studies (Paper 2)
- Advanced math/science topics appropriate for Classes 6-8, or History, Geography, Civics with pedagogy

## How to Apply

1. Visit the official UPTET website at [updeled.gov.in](https://updeled.gov.in)
2. Click on "UPTET Online Application"
3. Register with valid email and mobile number
4. Fill in personal and educational details
5. Select Paper 1, Paper 2, or both
6. Upload photo and signature
7. Pay application fee and submit

## Application Fee

| Category | One Paper | Both Papers |
|----------|-----------|-------------|
| General / OBC | ₹600 | ₹1,200 |
| SC / ST | ₹400 | ₹800 |
| PH (Divyang) | ₹100 | ₹200 |

## Salary & Benefits

After clearing UPTET and subsequent teacher recruitment:
- **Assistant Teacher (Primary):** ₹23,700 – ₹73,000 per month
- **Assistant Teacher (Upper Primary):** ₹28,700 – ₹91,300 per month
- DA, HRA, medical benefits, pension (NPS)

## Preparation Tips

1. **Child Development is key:** This section is common in both papers — master theories of learning and NCF 2005
2. **Language pedagogy:** Don't just study grammar — understand teaching methodologies
3. **NCERT books:** Study NCERT textbooks for Classes 1-8 thoroughly
4. **Practice papers:** Solve previous year UPTET papers — question patterns repeat
5. **Time management:** 150 questions in 150 minutes means 1 minute per question

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the validity of UPTET certificate?
UPTET certificate is valid for life (as per Supreme Court order, earlier it was 5 years).

### Can I appear for both Paper 1 and Paper 2?
Yes, you can appear for both papers in the same exam cycle.

### Is UPTET enough to get a teaching job?
No, UPTET is only an eligibility test. You must separately apply for teacher recruitment conducted by UP Basic Education Board.

### Is there negative marking in UPTET?
No, there is no negative marking in UPTET.

### How many times can I attempt UPTET?
There is no limit on the number of attempts.

### What is the qualifying percentage for UPTET?
General category needs 60% (90 out of 150), while OBC/SC/ST need 55% (82.5 out of 150).
