# DigiLocker NAC Certificate: How to Download Academic Certificates Online

DigiLocker allows you to download your National Academic Certificates (NAC) — including CBSE marksheets, university degrees, and other academic documents — directly from the issuing authority. These digital certificates are legally valid and can be used for jobs, admissions, and verification.

## What is NAC (National Academic Certificate)?

The National Academic Certificate is a digitally issued academic document available through DigiLocker's partnership with educational boards and universities. NAC includes:

- **CBSE Class 10 and 12 marksheets and passing certificates**
- **University degree certificates and marksheets** (participating universities)
- **State board marksheets** (many state boards are now on DigiLocker)
- **Academic Bank of Credits (ABC)** records
- **Professional course certificates** from participating institutions

These documents are issued electronically by the respective boards/universities and stored in DigiLocker. They carry a digital signature and are equivalent to original documents under the IT Act, 2000.

---

## Who Can Access NAC on DigiLocker?

- Any student whose **board or university is registered as an issuer** on DigiLocker
- You need a **DigiLocker account** (free to create with Aadhaar or mobile number)
- CBSE students from **2004 onwards** can access their marksheets
- University students — availability depends on whether your university has integrated with DigiLocker

---

## How to Create a DigiLocker Account

If you don't have a DigiLocker account yet:

1. Visit [www.digilocker.gov.in](https://www.digilocker.gov.in/) or download the DigiLocker app
2. Click **"Sign Up"**
3. Enter your **mobile number** and verify with OTP
4. Enter your **Aadhaar number** for identity verification (or use without Aadhaar with limited features)
5. Set a **username and password** (or use a 6-digit security PIN)
6. Your DigiLocker account is ready

## How to Download NAC Certificate from DigiLocker

### Step-by-Step: Pull CBSE Marksheet

1. Log in to [www.digilocker.gov.in](https://www.digilocker.gov.in/) or open the DigiLocker app
2. Go to **"Search Documents"** or **"Pull Documents"** section
3. In the search bar, type **"CBSE"** or browse under **Education** category
4. Select **"Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE)"**
5. Choose the document type:
   - Class 10 Marksheet
   - Class 10 Passing Certificate
   - Class 12 Marksheet
   - Class 12 Passing Certificate
   - Migration Certificate
6. Enter your details:
   - **Roll Number**
   - **Year of Passing**
7. Click **"Get Document"**
8. The certificate is fetched from CBSE and added to your **Issued Documents**
9. Download as **PDF** or share directly from DigiLocker

### Step-by-Step: Pull University Certificate

1. Log in to DigiLocker
2. Go to **"Search Documents"**
3. Search for your **university name** (e.g., "Delhi University", "Anna University")
4. If your university is listed, select it
5. Choose the document type (Degree Certificate, Marksheet, etc.)
6. Enter required details — **Registration Number, Year**, etc.
7. Click **"Get Document"**
8. The document is pulled from the university's records

**Note:** Not all universities are on DigiLocker yet. Check the [DigiLocker partner list](https://www.digilocker.gov.in/) for available issuers.

### Step-by-Step: Pull State Board Marksheet

1. In DigiLocker, go to **"Search Documents"**
2. Search for your state board (e.g., "UP Board", "Maharashtra Board", "Kerala Board")
3. Select the board and document type
4. Enter your **roll number** and **year of passing**
5. Fetch and download the document

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Document Not Found
- **Check your roll number and year** — Even a small error will prevent fetching
- **Your board/university may not be on DigiLocker** — Check the issuer list
- **Older records** — Some boards only have records from a certain year onwards (CBSE: 2004+)

### "Document Already in Your Account"
- Check your **"Issued Documents"** section — it may already be fetched from a previous attempt

### Verification Failed
- Ensure your **DigiLocker profile name matches** your name on the academic document
- Complete **Aadhaar verification** in your DigiLocker account for full access

## Important Tips

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is DigiLocker free to use?
Yes. DigiLocker is a **free service** by the Government of India under the Ministry of Electronics and IT. There are no charges for creating an account or downloading documents.

### Are DigiLocker certificates accepted for government jobs?
Yes. DigiLocker documents are **legally valid** and accepted by most government departments, banks, and educational institutions. They carry digital signatures from the issuing authority.

### My university is not on DigiLocker. What can I do?
If your university hasn't partnered with DigiLocker yet, you cannot pull those documents. You can write to your university requesting them to join the DigiLocker ecosystem, or check the **Academic Bank of Credits (ABC)** at [abc.gov.in](https://abc.gov.in/).

### Can I download documents from DigiLocker without Aadhaar?
You can create a DigiLocker account with just a **mobile number**, but some issuers require Aadhaar verification to release documents. For full access, Aadhaar linking is recommended.

### How do I share my DigiLocker certificate with an employer?
Log in to DigiLocker → Go to the document → Click **"Share"** → Enter the recipient's email or generate a **shareable link** with an expiry date. The recipient can verify the document's authenticity via the DigiLocker verification page.

### What is the difference between "Uploaded Documents" and "Issued Documents"?
**Issued Documents** are pulled directly from the issuing authority (like CBSE) and carry their digital signature — these are equivalent to originals. **Uploaded Documents** are scanned copies you upload yourself — these are self-attested and may not be accepted everywhere.

### Can I access DigiLocker from multiple devices?
Yes. You can log in to DigiLocker from any device — phone, tablet, or computer. Your documents are stored in the cloud and accessible from anywhere.
