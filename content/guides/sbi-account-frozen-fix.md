# SBI Account Frozen or Blocked — How to Unfreeze

If your SBI savings or current account has been frozen, blocked, or marked as inoperative, you won't be able to withdraw, transfer, or use your funds. This guide explains every reason this happens and how to get your account reactivated.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent informational platform and is not affiliated with SBI or any government body.

---

## Why Is Your SBI Account Frozen?

### 1. KYC Not Updated (Most Common)

As per RBI guidelines, banks must periodically verify customer identity. If you haven't updated your KYC (Know Your Customer) documents, SBI will:
- First send SMS/email reminders
- Then apply **partial freeze** (only credits allowed, debits blocked)
- Finally apply **full freeze** (no transactions allowed)

### 2. Account Became Inoperative/Dormant

If there are **no customer-initiated transactions for 2 years**, the account is classified as inoperative (dormant). Key points:
- Standing instructions and auto-debits don't count as customer-initiated transactions
- Interest continues to accrue
- You can still receive credits (salary, government payments)

### 3. Missing Nomination Declaration

As per RBI's 2024 guidelines, all savings and fixed deposit accounts must have a **nomination or explicit opt-out declaration**. Accounts without this may face restrictions.

### 4. Court Order or Legal Hold

A court or government authority (Income Tax, ED, police) can order the bank to freeze your account. In such cases:
- The bank cannot unfreeze without a court order
- You need to resolve the legal matter first

### 5. Suspicious Transaction (AML Rules)

Under Anti-Money Laundering (AML) rules, if SBI detects unusual transaction patterns, they may temporarily freeze the account for investigation.

### 6. PAN Not Linked to Account

If PAN is not linked to your bank account and you conduct high-value transactions (above ₹50,000), the account may face restrictions.

## How to Unfreeze SBI Account Without Visiting Branch

Many SBI account freezes can be resolved **fully online** — no branch visit required:

| Method | Works For | How |
|--------|-----------|-----|
| **YONO App** | KYC update (partial freeze), nomination | App → Service Requests → KYC Updation |
| **SBI Net Banking** | Nomination, PAN linking | onlinesbi.sbi → e-Services |
| **Customer Care** | Raise complaint, get reason | Call **1800-11-2211** or **1800-425-3800** (toll-free 24×7) |
| **Video KYC** | KYC-related freezes | YONO App → Video KYC option |
| **SMS** | PAN linking | SMS **EPAN XXXXXXXXXX** (your PAN) to **567676** |

> **When branch visit is required:** Full freeze, inoperative accounts, court-ordered freezes, and suspicious transaction reviews usually need you to visit your home branch at least once. For all other freezes — try online first.

**Online steps for KYC freeze (YONO):**
1. Open YONO SBI app → Log in
2. Go to **Service Requests** → **KYC**
3. Upload Aadhaar, PAN, and photo
4. Complete **Video KYC** when prompted
5. Account unfreezes within **1–3 working days** without visiting branch

## How to Unfreeze Your SBI Account

### For KYC-Related Freeze

**Documents needed:**
- Aadhaar card (mandatory)
- PAN card
- Recent passport-size photograph
- Current address proof (if address has changed)

**Steps:**
1. Visit your **home branch** (the branch where your account was opened)
2. Carry original Aadhaar and PAN along with photocopies
3. Fill the **KYC updation form** available at the branch
4. Complete **biometric verification** (Aadhaar-based)
5. The branch will update KYC and unfreeze your account within **1-3 working days**

**Online KYC update (partial):**
1. Log in to [YONO app](/guide/sbi-yono-app-not-working-fix) or [Internet Banking](https://www.onlinesbi.sbi/)
2. Go to **Service Requests** → **KYC Updation**
3. Upload Aadhaar and PAN details
4. Complete Video KYC if prompted
5. Note: Online KYC may not suffice for fully frozen accounts — branch visit may still be required

### For Inoperative/Dormant Account

**Steps:**
1. Visit your **home branch** with:
   - Aadhaar card and PAN card (originals + copies)
   - Passbook
   - A written request to reactivate the account
2. Fill the **account reactivation form**
3. Make a small deposit or withdrawal to activate the account
4. Update KYC if it's also pending
5. The account is usually reactivated **same day or within 24 hours**

> **Tip:** To prevent your account from becoming dormant, make at least one transaction (deposit, withdrawal, or transfer) every 6 months.

### For Missing Nomination

1. Visit your home branch or log in to Internet Banking
2. **Online:** Go to **e-Services** → **Nomination** → Add nominee details
3. **Branch:** Fill the nomination form (DA-1) and submit with nominee's details
4. If you don't want to nominate anyone, submit a **declaration of non-nomination**

### For Court-Ordered Freeze

1. This cannot be resolved at the bank
2. Consult a **lawyer** to understand the order and your options
3. Once the court issues an **unfreeze order**, submit it to SBI
4. The bank will unfreeze the account within 1-2 working days of receiving the order

### For Suspicious Transaction Freeze

1. Contact your **home branch manager** to understand the reason
2. Provide any documents the bank requests (income proof, business documents, etc.)
3. Cooperate with the investigation — the freeze is usually temporary
4. If you believe it's a mistake, escalate to SBI's **Customer Grievance Redressal** or the Banking Ombudsman

## Important Tips

1. **Update KYC proactively** — don't wait for the bank to freeze your account. SBI sends reminders via SMS
2. Keep your **mobile number and email updated** with the bank to receive important notifications
3. Make at least **one transaction every 6 months** to keep your account active
4. **Add a nominee** to your account immediately if you haven't already — it's now an RBI requirement
5. If you have multiple SBI accounts, ensure KYC is updated on all of them

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I know if my SBI account is frozen?
You'll receive an SMS from SBI. You can also check by attempting a transaction via YONO or ATM — a frozen account will show "transaction not allowed" or similar error.

### Can I receive money in a frozen SBI account?
In a **partially frozen** (debit freeze) account, yes — credits like salary and government payments will still come in. In a **fully frozen** account, no transactions are allowed.

### How long does it take to unfreeze an SBI account?
For KYC-related freezes, 1-3 working days after submitting documents at the branch. For inoperative accounts, usually same day.

### Can I unfreeze my SBI account without visiting branch?
Yes — for KYC-related partial freezes: use YONO app or SBI Net Banking to update KYC and complete Video KYC. For nomination issues: add nominee online via Net Banking (e-Services → Nomination). Call 1800-11-2211 (toll-free) to raise a complaint and get guidance without visiting. Full freezes and inoperative accounts often need at least one branch visit, but many KYC freezes are fully resolvable online.

### Will I lose my money if my account is frozen?
No. Your money remains safe in the account. Interest continues to accrue on savings accounts. The freeze only restricts transactions.

### My SBI account was frozen but I've done KYC recently. What happened?
Check if your PAN is linked, nomination is declared, and there are no pending compliance requirements. Contact your branch for the specific reason.

### Can I close a frozen SBI account?
You need to unfreeze it first. Visit your branch, resolve the freeze reason, and then submit an account closure request if desired.
