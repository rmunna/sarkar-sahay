# Mukhyamantri Medhavriti Yojana Bihar: Scholarship for SC/ST Inter Pass Students

Mukhyamantri Medhavriti Yojana is a scholarship scheme by the Bihar government for **SC (Scheduled Caste) and ST (Scheduled Tribe)** students who pass the Intermediate (12th) examination. Students who pass in the **First Division receive ₹15,000** and those in the **Second Division receive ₹10,000** as one-time incentive.

## What is Medhavriti Yojana?

This scheme encourages meritorious SC/ST students to pursue higher education by rewarding them for their academic achievements at the Intermediate level. The scholarship is a one-time grant paid directly to the student's bank account via DBT.

### Scholarship Amount

| Division | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| First Division (60% and above) | ₹15,000 |
| Second Division (50-59.9%) | ₹10,000 |

> **Note:** Only unmarried girls were eligible in earlier versions. The scheme has been expanded — check the latest notification for current eligibility.

## Who is Eligible?

- Must belong to **SC (Scheduled Caste) or ST (Scheduled Tribe)** category
- Must have passed **Intermediate (12th) examination** from Bihar Board (BSEB)
- Must have passed in **First or Second Division**
- Must be a **permanent resident of Bihar**
- Must be **unmarried** (for female candidates — check latest criteria)
- Must have a **bank account** in their own name linked with Aadhaar
- Family annual income should be within the prescribed limit

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** of the student
- **12th (Intermediate) marksheet** from BSEB
- **Caste certificate** (SC/ST) issued by competent authority
- **Bank account passbook** (in student's name)
- **Income certificate** of parents/family
- **Residential certificate** of Bihar
- **Passport-size photograph**
- **Mobile number** linked with Aadhaar

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Online Application

1. Visit the official portal at **[medhasoft.bihar.gov.in](https://medhasoft.bihar.gov.in/)**
2. Look for the **"Mukhyamantri Medhavriti Yojana"** link
3. Click on **"Apply Online"** or **"Student Click Here to Apply"**
4. Enter your **BSEB Registration Number** and other required details
5. Verify your identity through **Aadhaar OTP**
6. Fill in personal details — name, father's name, caste, address
7. Upload documents — marksheet, caste certificate, photo, Aadhaar
8. Enter **bank account details** (account number, IFSC code)
9. Review all information and **submit the application**
10. Download and save the **application receipt** with your Application ID

### Check Application Status

1. Visit [medhasoft.bihar.gov.in](https://medhasoft.bihar.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"Application Status"** under Medhavriti Yojana
3. Enter your Registration Number or Application ID
4. Check payment status and any remarks

## Fees

There are **no fees** for applying. The application process is completely free.

## Processing Time

- Application verification: **1-3 months**
- DBT transfer after approval: **1-2 months**
- Total: Approximately **2-4 months** from application submission

## Important Tips

1. **Apply as soon as the portal opens** — there is usually a deadline. Don't wait until the last date
2. **Ensure caste certificate is valid** — it must be issued by the competent authority (SDO/Circle Officer)
3. **Bank account must be in student's name** — joint accounts or parent's accounts may cause rejection
4. **Keep Aadhaar linked to bank** — DBT transfer requires Aadhaar-bank linking
5. **Verify marksheet details** — ensure your name, roll number, and marks match across all documents

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Is this scheme only for girls?
Earlier versions were primarily for girls. However, **check the latest notification** on Medhasoft as the scheme may have been expanded to include all SC/ST students regardless of gender.

### Q2: Are CBSE/ICSE students eligible?
The scheme is primarily for students who passed 12th from **Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB)**. Students from CBSE/ICSE boards in Bihar should check the latest notification for eligibility.

### Q3: Can I apply if I passed with Third Division?
No, only **First Division (60%+) and Second Division (50-59.9%)** students are eligible.

### Q4: Is this different from Kanya Utthan Yojana?
Yes. [Kanya Utthan Yojana](/guide/kanya-utthan-yojana-bihar) provides ₹25,000 to all 12th pass girls regardless of caste. Medhavriti Yojana is specifically for **SC/ST students** and provides ₹10,000-₹15,000 based on merit division.

### Q5: Can I get both Medhavriti and Kanya Utthan Yojana?
SC/ST girls who pass 12th may be eligible for both schemes. Check eligibility criteria for each separately on the respective portals.

### Q6: What if my caste certificate is not ready?
Apply for a caste certificate immediately through **[serviceonline.bihar.gov.in](https://serviceonline.bihar.gov.in/)**. You will need a valid caste certificate to apply for Medhavriti Yojana.

### Q7: When does the application window open?
The application window typically opens a few months after BSEB results are declared. Check [medhasoft.bihar.gov.in](https://medhasoft.bihar.gov.in/) regularly for notifications.
