# Gruha Jyothi Scheme Karnataka – Free Electricity Up to 200 Units

## What is Gruha Jyothi Scheme?

Gruha Jyothi is one of Karnataka's five guarantee schemes providing **free electricity up to 200 units per month** to every household. Under this scheme, domestic consumers whose monthly electricity consumption does not exceed 200 units receive a **zero electricity bill** (excluding fixed/meter charges in some cases). The scheme covers all domestic connections across the state.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Domestic consumers** in Karnataka with a valid electricity connection
2. **One connection per household** (linked to ration card)
3. Monthly consumption must be **200 units or less** to get full benefit
4. Must have a valid **Karnataka ration card** linked to the electricity account
5. The connection must be a **domestic/residential** connection (not commercial or industrial)

### Who is NOT Eligible?

- Households consuming **more than 200 units/month** will pay the full bill (no partial benefit)
- Commercial, industrial, or agricultural connections
- Households where any member is a government employee of Group A/B rank
- Households where any member is an income tax payer

## Documents Required

- **Ration Card** (Karnataka-issued)
- **Electricity Account/RR Number**
- **Aadhaar Card** of the account holder
- **Mobile Number** linked to the electricity account

## How to Apply / Register for Gruha Jyothi

### Online Registration

1. **Visit** [gruhajyothi.karnataka.gov.in](https://gruhajyothi.karnataka.gov.in)
2. **Enter** your electricity **RR Number** (account number from your bill)
3. **Link** your **ration card number** to the electricity account
4. **Verify** using Aadhaar OTP
5. **Submit** the registration
6. Once verified, the free electricity benefit is automatically applied to your monthly bill

### Offline Registration

1. Visit your nearest **BESCOM / HESCOM / MESCOM / GESCOM / CESC** office (your electricity supply company)
2. Carry your ration card, Aadhaar card, and a recent electricity bill
3. Fill the Gruha Jyothi linkage form
4. Submit and collect the acknowledgment

### Through Electricity Bill Itself

- In many cases, the ESCOM (Electricity Supply Company) has **auto-linked** eligible consumers based on existing ration card data
- Check your monthly bill — if you see "Gruha Jyothi" credit, you're already enrolled

## How the Benefit Works

| Consumption | Benefit |
|------------|---------|
| 0–200 units/month | **Full bill waived** (free electricity) |
| Above 200 units/month | **No benefit** — pay the full bill |

> ⚡ **Important:** The 200-unit limit is strict. If you consume even 201 units, you pay the **entire bill** — there is no partial waiver.

## Processing Time

- **Registration:** Instant to 7 days for linkage verification
- **Benefit Application:** From the next billing cycle after successful registration

## Important Tips

1. **Monitor Your Usage:** Keep consumption below 200 units. Use energy-efficient appliances to stay within the limit.
2. **Link Ration Card Early:** The scheme requires ration card-to-meter linkage. Do this as soon as possible.
3. **Check Your Bill:** Verify that the Gruha Jyothi discount appears on your electricity bill after registration.
4. **One Connection Only:** If you have multiple electricity connections, only one (linked to ration card) gets the benefit.
5. **Fixed Charges May Apply:** Some ESCOMs may still charge a small fixed/meter charge even under Gruha Jyothi. Check your bill details.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: What happens if I use more than 200 units?
You will have to pay the **full electricity bill** for that month. There is no partial discount — the 200-unit limit is an all-or-nothing threshold.

### Q2: Do I need to apply every month?
**No.** Once registered, the benefit is automatically applied every month as long as your consumption stays within 200 units.

### Q3: Can I get Gruha Jyothi if I don't have a ration card?
**No.** A valid Karnataka ration card is mandatory. Apply for a ration card through [ahara.kar.nic.in](https://ahara.kar.nic.in) first.

### Q4: My bill still shows charges even though I use less than 200 units. Why?
Some ESCOMs charge a nominal **fixed charge or meter rent**. The Gruha Jyothi scheme covers the energy charges, but fixed charges may still appear.

### Q5: I have two houses with two connections. Can both get the benefit?
**No.** Only one domestic connection per ration card is eligible.

### Q6: Is this scheme available across all of Karnataka?
**Yes.** It covers all five ESCOMs — BESCOM, HESCOM, MESCOM, GESCOM, and CESC.

### Q7: Can tenants avail this scheme?
The benefit is linked to the ration card and electricity account. If the tenant's ration card is linked to that address and meter, they may be eligible.
