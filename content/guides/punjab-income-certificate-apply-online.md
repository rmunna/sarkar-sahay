# Punjab Income Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **income certificate** (ਆਮਦਨ ਸਰਟੀਫਿਕੇਟ — Aamdani Sarteefeekat) in Punjab is issued by the Revenue Department and is required for post-matric SC/BC scholarships, EWS reservation (income < ₹8 lakh), government college fee concessions, and central scheme eligibility. Applications are processed through **Punjab E-District Services** or eSewa centres.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [punjabedistrictservices.gov.in](https://punjabedistrictservices.gov.in) or visit nearest **eSewa centre / Seva Kendra**. Issued by Tehsildar/SDM within **15–30 days**.

---

## Why You Need Punjab Income Certificate

- **Post-matric SC scholarship (Punjab)** — income < ₹2.5 lakh/year
- **Post-matric BC scholarship** — income below scheme limit
- **NSP National Scholarship** — all categories need income proof
- **EWS (Economically Weaker Section)** reservation — income < ₹8 lakh/year
- **Punjab state fee concession** — government college scholarships
- **PMAY / housing scheme eligibility** — income-based eligibility
- **Government job EWS** — central and state recruitment

---

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory for applicant)
- **Ration card / family ID** (if available — shows family composition)
- Self-declaration of annual income (all sources)
- For salaried: **salary certificate** from employer or Form 16 / ITR
- For farmers: **Girdawari** (land record from Patwari showing cultivation)
- For business: **self-declaration** or municipal trade license
- **Voter ID** (address proof)
- Passport-size photograph

---

## How to Apply Online — Punjab E-District Portal

1. Go to [punjabedistrictservices.gov.in](https://punjabedistrictservices.gov.in)
2. Click **"New User? Register"** → register with mobile number (OTP)
3. Log in → select **"Revenue Department"** → **"Income Certificate"**
4. Fill form:
   - Applicant name, address (district, tehsil, village/ward)
   - Annual family income (declare all sources)
   - Purpose of certificate
5. Upload documents (Aadhaar, income proof)
6. Pay fee: **₹20–30** (online payment)
7. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via eSewa / Seva Kendra

Punjab's **eSewa centres** (Seva Kendras) handle edistrict applications:

1. Visit nearest **eSewa centre** or Seva Kendra
2. Carry Aadhaar, ration card, income proof documents
3. Operator fills and submits the income certificate form
4. Service charge: approximately ₹30–60
5. Collect acknowledgement receipt
6. Track status using reference number

---

## Apply at Tehsildar Office (Offline)

1. Visit your **Tehsil office**
2. Obtain income certificate application form
3. Submit with documents to Revenue Inspector / Naib Tehsildar
4. Patwari/Revenue Inspector verifies income at village level
5. Tehsildar issues certificate after verification

---

## Status Check

1. Go to [punjabedistrictservices.gov.in](https://punjabedistrictservices.gov.in)
2. Click **"Track Application"** → enter reference number
3. Or log in → **"My Applications"** → view status
4. Status flow: Submitted → Patwari Verification → Tehsildar → Approved

---

## Download Punjab Income Certificate

Once approved:
1. Log in to Punjab E-District portal → **"My Applications"**
2. Click the approved income certificate
3. Download digitally signed PDF
4. Valid for all official purposes — state and central schemes

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Income limit for Punjab post-matric SC scholarship?

For **SC post-matric scholarship (central)**: family income below **₹2.5 lakh/year**. For **Punjab state SC scholarship**: income limit may differ — check the specific scheme notification. For BC category scholarship, income limit is typically ₹1.5–2.5 lakh/year.

### Is Girdawari mandatory for farmers in Punjab?

Yes — for farming families, the **Girdawari** (crop and land record issued by Patwari) is the primary income proof. It shows the land area under cultivation and crop details, from which annual farming income is estimated.

### EWS income certificate vs OBC NCL — same?

No. **EWS** is for general/open category applicants with income < ₹8 lakh/year. **OBC NCL** (Non-Creamy Layer) is for OBC categories with similar income limit but different categories. Both require income certificate but serve different reservation types.
