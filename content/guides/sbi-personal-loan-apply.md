# SBI Personal Loan Apply Online: Interest Rate & Eligibility

SBI Xpress Credit is one of the most popular personal loan products in India. Available to salaried individuals, it offers quick disbursement with minimal documentation — especially through the YONO app where pre-approved customers can get instant loans. This guide covers the complete application process, interest rates, eligibility criteria, and disbursement timelines.

## What is SBI Xpress Credit Personal Loan?

SBI Xpress Credit is an unsecured personal loan offered by State Bank of India to salaried employees of Central/State Government, Defence, reputed PSUs, and select private companies. No collateral or security is required.

**Key highlights:**

- Loan amount: Up to ₹35 lakh
- Tenure: Up to 6 years (72 months)
- No collateral or guarantor required
- Pre-approved loans available via YONO with instant disbursement

## SBI Personal Loan Interest Rate 2026

| Category | Interest Rate (per annum) |
|---|---|
| SBI salary account holders | 11.15% onwards |
| Government employees (Central/State) | 11.15% onwards |
| Defence personnel | 11.15% onwards |
| Other salaried | 11.65% onwards |

> **Note:** Rates are linked to SBI's EBLR and depend on your credit profile, employer category, and loan amount. Check the [official SBI Xpress Credit page](https://sbi.co.in/web/personal-banking/loans/personal-loans/xpress-credit) for current rates.

## Who is Eligible?

**Basic eligibility:**
- Must be a salaried individual
- Age: 21–58 years (loan should be repaid before retirement)
- Minimum net monthly salary: ₹15,000
- Employer should be in SBI's approved list (Government, PSU, or select private companies)

**Loan amount calculation:**
- Up to 24 times the net monthly salary (for approved employer categories)
- Maximum ₹35 lakh
- Subject to repayment capacity and credit score

**Credit score:**
- Minimum CIBIL score of 700 recommended
- Higher score may qualify for better interest rates

> **Tip:** If you have a salary account with SBI, most documents are already available in the system — YONO pre-approved offers may not require any document upload.

## How to Apply for SBI Personal Loan Online

### Method 1: YONO App (Recommended — Pre-Approved Offers)

1. Open the **YONO SBI** app and log in
2. Go to the home screen — check for **"Pre-approved Personal Loan"** banner
3. If available, tap on it to see your eligible loan amount and interest rate
4. Select the desired loan amount and tenure
5. Verify your details (auto-filled from your SBI account)
6. Accept the terms and confirm with **OTP**
7. Loan is **disbursed instantly** to your SBI account
8. The entire process takes **under 5 minutes** for pre-approved customers

### Method 2: SBI Online Application

1. Visit the [SBI Xpress Credit page](https://sbi.co.in/web/personal-banking/loans/personal-loans/xpress-credit)
2. Click **"Apply Now"**
3. Fill in personal details, employment information, and loan amount
4. Upload scanned documents (Aadhaar, PAN, salary slips)
5. Submit the application
6. Note the reference number
7. SBI will verify and process your application within 5–7 working days

### Method 3: Branch Visit

1. Visit your nearest SBI branch with original documents
2. Fill in the Xpress Credit application form
3. Submit along with photocopies of all required documents
4. The branch will process your application and disburse after verification

## Important Tips

1. **Check YONO first** — Pre-approved personal loan offers on YONO give the best rate and instant disbursement
2. **Keep salary account with SBI** — This significantly improves approval chances and may lower the interest rate
3. **Don't apply at multiple banks** — Multiple loan inquiries within a short period can reduce your CIBIL score
4. **Choose shorter tenure if possible** — You'll pay significantly less total interest
5. **Prepay when you can** — No penalty on floating rate loans, so prepay surplus amounts to reduce interest burden

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. What is the maximum SBI personal loan amount I can get?
The maximum personal loan amount under SBI Xpress Credit is ₹35 lakh, subject to your salary, employer category, and repayment capacity.

### Q2. Can I get an SBI personal loan without salary account in SBI?
Yes, but having a salary account with SBI significantly improves your chances of approval and may qualify you for pre-approved offers via YONO.

### Q3. How long does SBI personal loan approval take?
Pre-approved loans via YONO are instant. Regular applications take 5–7 working days for approval and disbursement.

### Q4. Can self-employed individuals apply for SBI Xpress Credit?
No, SBI Xpress Credit is only for salaried individuals. Self-employed persons can explore SBI's other loan products like Xpress Flexi or loan against property.

### Q5. What is the minimum CIBIL score for SBI personal loan?
While SBI doesn't publish an official minimum, a CIBIL score of 700+ is generally recommended. Scores above 750 may get better interest rates.

### Q6. Can I foreclose my SBI personal loan early?
Yes, you can foreclose your SBI personal loan at any time without any prepayment penalty on floating rate loans, as per RBI guidelines.

### Q7. How do I check my SBI personal loan status?
Check your loan status via the YONO app, [SBI net banking](/guide/sbi-net-banking-registration-login), or call SBI customer care at 1800-11-2211 (toll-free).
