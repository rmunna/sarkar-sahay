# Airtel Recharge Failed? Fix Payment & Plan Errors

Did your Airtel recharge fail but money got deducted? Or is the Airtel Thanks app showing errors during payment? This guide covers all common recharge failures with fixes and how to get a refund.

## Common Airtel Recharge Problems

- **Payment deducted but plan not activated** — most common issue
- **Airtel Thanks app showing error** — app crash or payment page not loading
- **UPI payment failed** — transaction failed via Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm
- **Credit/debit card declined** — payment gateway rejection
- **Recharge successful but wrong plan activated**
- **Cashback or coupon not applied**

---

## Step-by-Step Fixes

### Fix 1: Payment Deducted but Plan Not Activated

This is usually a temporary delay. Follow these steps:

1. **Wait 15-30 minutes** — sometimes plan activation is delayed due to server load
2. **Check SMS** — look for a confirmation SMS from Airtel (sender: AirtelPB or similar)
3. **Check Airtel Thanks app** — Go to **My Account** → **My Plans** to see active plans
4. **Dial *121#** — this USSD code shows your current active plan and balance

If the plan is still not activated after 30 minutes:
1. Open the **Airtel Thanks** app
2. Go to **Help** → **Recharge & Payment Issues**
3. Select **Money deducted but recharge not done**
4. The app will auto-detect the failed transaction and initiate resolution

### Fix 2: Airtel Thanks App Errors

If the app is not working properly:

1. **Force close and reopen** the app
2. **Clear app cache:**
   - Android: Settings → Apps → Airtel Thanks → Storage → Clear Cache
   - iPhone: Delete and reinstall the app
3. **Update the app** from Play Store or App Store
4. **Check internet connection** — switch between WiFi and mobile data
5. **Try recharging via website** — visit [airtel.in/recharge-online](https://www.airtel.in/recharge-online)

### Fix 3: UPI Payment Failed

If recharge via UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, BHIM) failed:

1. **Check your bank account** — verify if money was actually deducted
2. **If money was deducted:** The amount is usually auto-refunded within 24-48 hours (up to 5 business days in some cases)
3. **Check UPI app transaction history** — look for "Pending" or "Failed" status
4. **Raise dispute in UPI app:** Open Google Pay/PhonePe → Transaction history → Select the transaction → Raise concern
5. **Contact your bank** if refund doesn't come in 5 business days

### Fix 4: Card Payment Declined

If your debit or credit card was declined:

1. **Check if online transactions are enabled** — many banks require you to enable online/e-commerce transactions in your banking app
2. **Check daily transaction limit** — your bank may have a low daily limit
3. **Ensure correct OTP entry** — OTP expires quickly, enter it within 2-3 minutes
4. **Try a different payment method** — use UPI, another card, or Airtel Payments Bank
5. **Try recharging from a third-party app** — Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or Amazon Pay

### Fix 5: Wrong Plan Activated

If a different plan was activated than what you selected:

1. Take a **screenshot** of the payment confirmation showing the plan you selected
2. Note the **transaction ID** from your bank statement or UPI app
3. Call **198** and explain the issue with your transaction ID
4. Airtel can deactivate the wrong plan and activate the correct one, or process a refund

## How to Get a Refund from Airtel

### For Failed Recharges (Money Deducted)

| Payment Method | Refund Timeline | Action Needed |
|---|---|---|
| UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, etc.) | 24-48 hours (up to 5 days) | Usually auto-refund; raise dispute in UPI app if delayed |
| Credit/Debit Card | 5-7 business days | Call 198 or raise ticket in Airtel Thanks app |
| Airtel Payments Bank | 24 hours | Auto-refund to Airtel Payments Bank wallet |
| Net Banking | 5-7 business days | Contact your bank if Airtel confirms non-receipt |

### Steps to Claim Refund

1. Open **Airtel Thanks** app → **Help** → **Recharge & Payment Issues**
2. Select **Refund for failed recharge**
3. Provide your **transaction ID** and **payment screenshot**
4. Note down the **complaint number**
5. If not resolved in 7 days, escalate:
   - Call **198** and quote your complaint number
   - Email: **121@in.airtel.com** (include transaction details)

## Important Tips

1. **Always save transaction IDs** — you'll need them for refund claims.
2. **Avoid recharging during peak hours** (9-11 PM) when servers are under heavy load.
3. **Enable auto-pay** in the Airtel Thanks app to avoid last-minute recharge failures.
4. **Check plan validity before recharging** — some plans cannot be stacked on top of active plans.
5. **Use official channels only** — avoid third-party websites claiming to offer discounted recharges.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My Airtel recharge failed but money was deducted. What should I do?
Wait 30 minutes for auto-processing. If the plan is still not activated, raise a complaint in the Airtel Thanks app under Recharge & Payment Issues. Refunds are typically processed within 24-48 hours for UPI and 5-7 business days for cards.

### How do I check my Airtel recharge status?
Dial *121# from your Airtel number or open the Airtel Thanks app → My Account → My Plans. You can also check by calling 121.

### Can I cancel an Airtel recharge and get a refund?
Once a recharge is successfully activated, it generally cannot be reversed. However, if a wrong plan was activated due to a system error, call 198 with your transaction details to request correction.

### Why does the Airtel Thanks app keep showing payment errors?
This could be due to an outdated app, poor internet connection, or server issues. Clear the app cache, update to the latest version, and try again. If the problem persists, use the Airtel website or a third-party app instead.

### How long does an Airtel recharge refund take?
UPI refunds take 24-48 hours (up to 5 business days in some cases). Credit/debit card refunds take 5-7 business days. If the refund is delayed beyond this, contact your bank with the transaction ID.

### Where can I complain if Airtel doesn't refund my money?
If Airtel doesn't resolve your complaint within 7 days, you can escalate to the **National Consumer Helpline** at [consumerhelpline.gov.in](https://consumerhelpline.gov.in/) or call **1800-11-4000**. You can also file a complaint with the Telecom Ombudsman.
