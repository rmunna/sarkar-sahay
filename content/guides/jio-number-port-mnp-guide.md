# Port Number to Jio: MNP Process & Step-by-Step Guide

Want to switch to Jio but keep your current mobile number? You can do this through MNP (Mobile Number Portability). The process takes 3-5 working days and your number stays the same. This guide explains the complete Jio porting process with every step.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent platform and is not affiliated with Reliance Jio or any government body. Information is for guidance only.

## What is MNP (Mobile Number Portability)?

MNP allows you to switch your mobile network operator (e.g., Airtel, Vi, BSNL) to Jio while **retaining your existing mobile number**. This is a TRAI-regulated process available to all Indian mobile users.

## Eligibility for Porting to Jio

- Your current number must be **at least 90 days old** with the existing operator
- **No pending dues** with your current operator
- You must not have ported in the **last 90 days**
- Your number should be **active** (not suspended or deactivated)

## Documents Required

- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) (for eKYC verification)
- Passport-size photograph
- UPC (Unique Porting Code) — generated via SMS

## Step-by-Step Process to Port Number to Jio

### Step 1: Generate UPC (Unique Porting Code)

Send an SMS from the number you want to port:

**SMS Format:** `PORT <your 10-digit number>` and send to **1900**

Example: `PORT 9876543210` → Send to **1900**

You'll receive a **UPC code via SMS** within a few minutes. Note this code.

**UPC Validity:** The UPC is valid for **4 days only**. Complete the process before it expires.

### Step 2: Visit a Jio Store

1. Go to your nearest **Jio Store** or authorized Jio retailer
2. Carry your **Aadhaar card**, **photograph**, and the **UPC code**
3. Tell the executive you want to **port your number to Jio**

### Step 3: Complete eKYC and Documentation

1. The Jio executive will perform **Aadhaar eKYC** (biometric or OTP-based)
2. Fill and sign the **Customer Acquisition Form (CAF)**
3. Submit your **UPC code**
4. Choose your **Jio plan** — you'll need to do a first recharge
5. You'll receive a **new Jio SIM card** with a temporary number

### Step 4: Wait for Porting (3-5 Working Days)

1. **Keep your old SIM active** — don't remove it until porting is complete
2. Jio processes the porting request with TRAI's MNP system
3. You'll receive an **SMS notification** when porting is scheduled
4. On the porting day, your old SIM will **stop working** (usually between 10 PM - 5 AM)
5. Insert your **new Jio SIM** and restart your phone
6. Your number is now active on Jio

### Step 5: Activate and Verify

1. After the port is complete, **restart your phone** with the Jio SIM
2. You may receive a **tele-verification call** — answer and follow instructions
3. Check network — you should see **Jio 4G** signal
4. Dial `*333#` to check your active plan
5. Download the **MyJio app** for plan management

## MNP Timeline

| Stage | Duration |
|-------|----------|
| UPC generation | Instant (SMS within minutes) |
| UPC validity | 4 days |
| Porting request processing | 3-5 working days |
| SIM switchover | 1-2 hours (usually late night) |
| Total process | 3-7 days |

## Common MNP Problems and Fixes

### UPC Not Received
- Check if you sent the SMS in correct format: `PORT <number>` to **1900**
- Try again after 30 minutes
- If still not received, contact your current operator's customer care

### Porting Request Rejected
Common rejection reasons:
- Number less than 90 days old with current operator
- Unpaid bills with current operator
- Already ported within last 90 days
- UPC expired (4-day validity)
- Incorrect details in the porting form

### Porting Delayed Beyond 7 Days
- Contact Jio at **199** with your porting request details
- You can also complain on the [TRAI MNP portal](https://trai.gov.in/release-publication/mobile-number-portability)

## Port to Jio Online (eSIM)

For select phones, you can initiate porting online:

1. Visit **Jio.com** or use the **MyJio app**
2. Select **Get Jio** → **Port your number**
3. Enter your number and UPC code
4. Complete Aadhaar eKYC online
5. Choose your plan and pay
6. For eSIM: scan the QR code to activate
7. For physical SIM: it will be delivered to your address

## Important Tips

1. Generate a **fresh UPC** just before visiting the Jio Store — it expires in 4 days
2. Clear all pending bills with your current operator before initiating port
3. Your number's **incoming calls and SMS will work** throughout the porting process (except during the brief switchover)
4. After porting, update your number on banking apps, UPI, and other services — they remain linked to your number
5. If you have **prepaid balance** with your old operator, use it up before porting — it won't be refunded

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does it take to port a number to Jio?
The complete MNP process takes 3-5 working days from the date of submitting the porting request at a Jio Store. The actual switchover happens overnight.

### Will I lose my number during porting?
No, your number remains the same. During the brief switchover (usually 1-2 hours at night), your old SIM stops and the new Jio SIM activates with the same number.

### How much does it cost to port to Jio?
The porting fee is ₹4 (one-time). The Jio SIM is free. You'll need to do a first recharge as per your chosen Jio plan (starting from ₹189).

### Can I port my number to Jio online?
Yes, you can initiate the process on Jio.com or MyJio app for select cities. You'll need to enter your UPC, complete eKYC, and choose a plan. The SIM is delivered to your address.

### What is UPC and how do I generate it?
UPC (Unique Porting Code) is a code needed to port your number. Send SMS `PORT <your number>` to 1900 from your current number. The UPC arrives via SMS and is valid for 4 days.

### Can I port if I have unpaid bills?
No, you must clear all dues with your current operator before porting. Unpaid bills are a common reason for MNP request rejection.

### Can I port a postpaid number to Jio prepaid?
Yes, you can port from any operator's postpaid to Jio prepaid (or postpaid). Clear your postpaid bills first and then follow the standard MNP process.
