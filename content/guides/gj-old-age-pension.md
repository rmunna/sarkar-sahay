## What is Gujarat Vrudh Sahay Old Age Pension?

Gujarat Vrudh Sahay (Old Age Pension) is a social security pension scheme by the Government of Gujarat for senior citizens who are destitute or have no regular income. The scheme operates in convergence with the central **Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS)**.

### Pension Amount

| Age Group | Monthly Pension |
|-----------|----------------|
| 60-79 years | ₹750 (State ₹550 + Centre ₹200) |
| 80+ years | ₹1,000 (State ₹500 + Centre ₹500) |

> Amounts may vary based on latest government revisions.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Age:** 60 years or above
- **Domicile:** Resident of Gujarat
- **Income:** BPL family or annual income below the poverty line threshold
- Must **not be receiving** any other government pension
- Must not be an income tax payer
- Not dependent on any earning adult family member (destitute)

## Documents Required

- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
- Age proof (birth certificate, school leaving certificate, or election card)
- BPL ration card or income certificate
- Domicile/residence proof (voter ID, ration card)
- Bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked, nationalized bank)
- Passport-size photographs (2)
- Self-declaration of not receiving other pension

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Online Application

1. **Visit** [Digital Gujarat portal](https://digitalgujarat.gov.in) or [e-Samaj Kalyan portal](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in)
2. **Register** with mobile number
3. **Select** Vrudh Sahay / Old Age Pension
4. **Fill** the form with personal, age, income, and bank details
5. **Upload** Aadhaar, age proof, income certificate, bank passbook
6. **Submit** and note application number
7. **Track** status online

### Offline Application

1. **Visit** the Mamlatdar office or Taluka Development Office
2. **Collect** the pension application form
3. **Fill** and attach all documents
4. **Submit** to the concerned officer
5. **Collect** acknowledgement receipt

## Processing Time

- Verification: **1-3 months**
- Approval and first pension: **2-4 months**
- Pension credited monthly via DBT

## Fees

**No fees** for application.

## Important Tips

1. **Submit life certificate annually** — Use [Jeevan Pramaan](https://jeevanpramaan.gov.in) for digital submission
2. **Keep bank account active** — DBT requires an active, Aadhaar-linked account
3. **Apply through CSC** — Common Service Centres can help with online applications
4. **Update Aadhaar address** — If you've moved, update your Aadhaar to match your Gujarat address
5. **Check for additional benefits** — Senior citizens may also qualify for [MA Amrutam health insurance](/guide/gj-ma-amrutam)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. What is the minimum age?
**60 years**. Age is verified from birth certificate or equivalent documents.

### Q2. Can I apply if my son earns well?
The scheme is for **destitute** senior citizens. If you have earning family members supporting you, eligibility may be affected.

### Q3. How do I submit life certificate?
Visit your bank branch with Aadhaar, or use [Jeevan Pramaan portal](https://jeevanpramaan.gov.in) for biometric digital submission.

### Q4. Can I get both old age pension and widow pension?
No. Only **one** social security pension is allowed at a time.

### Q5. Is the pension taxable?
No. Social security pension under this scheme is **not taxable**.
