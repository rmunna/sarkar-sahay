# Bandhan Bank Account Blocked or Frozen? How to Unblock (2026)

Bandhan Bank, one of India's newer private sector banks with 6,000+ banking outlets, primarily serves customers in East and Northeast India. If your Bandhan Bank account is blocked or frozen, this guide tells you how to fix it.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is independent. Contact Bandhan Bank directly at 1800-258-8181 for account-specific assistance.

---

## Why Is Your Bandhan Bank Account Blocked?

| Reason | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| KYC not updated | Complete re-KYC via app or branch |
| PAN not linked | Link PAN at branch or app |
| Dormant account | Branch reactivation |
| Suspicious transactions | Call customer care + branch visit |
| Government / court order | Legal resolution |

---

## Identify the Reason

1. **Check SMS alerts** — Bandhan Bank sends restriction notifications via SMS
2. **Call 1800-258-8181** — ask for exact block reason
3. **Bandhan Bank App** — may show KYC notification

---

## Unblock Bandhan Bank Account Online

### Via Bandhan Bank Mobile App

1. Open **Bandhan Bank** mobile app
2. Login → **Services → Account**
3. If KYC update prompt: Upload Aadhaar + PAN
4. Raise Service Request for other blocks

### Via Bandhan Bank Net Banking

1. Login at **[bandhanbank.com](https://www.bandhanbank.com)** → Net Banking
2. Profile → KYC Documents → Upload
3. Or submit service request

## Call Customer Care

**Toll-Free:** **1800-258-8181**
**Alternate:** 033-4409-9090

Say: "My Bandhan Bank account XXXX has debit restrictions. Can you confirm the reason and how to unblock it?"

## Visit Bandhan Bank Branch

1. Visit nearest Bandhan Bank branch (home branch preferred)
2. Carry: Aadhaar (original + copy), PAN, passbook, 2 passport photos
3. Fill **Account Reactivation / KYC Update** form
4. Submit documents + get acknowledgment
5. Processing: **1–2 working days**

---

## Bandhan Bank KYC Update

**Via App:**
1. Bandhan Bank App → Profile → KYC Update
2. Upload Aadhaar + PAN (clear photos, self-attested)
3. Timeline: 1–2 working days

**At Branch:**
- Fill KYC form + submit documents
- Same-day processing

---

## Dormant Account Reactivation (Bandhan Bank)

1. Visit home branch
2. Fill dormant reactivation form + submit KYC documents
3. Do one cash deposit
4. Active: 1 working day

**How long to unblock Bandhan Bank account?**

**Is Bandhan Bank safe? Is my money protected?**
Yes — Bandhan Bank is RBI-regulated and DICGC-insured. Your deposits are protected up to ₹5 lakh per depositor.

**Can I update Bandhan Bank KYC without branch visit?**
