# IRCTC App Not Working? Fix Connection & Crash Errors (2026)

The IRCTC Rail Connect app is the primary way millions of Indians book train tickets. But the app frequently crashes, shows connection errors, or fails to load — especially during Tatkal booking hours. This guide covers every common IRCTC app problem and how to fix it.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent platform and is not affiliated with Indian Railways or IRCTC. Information is for guidance only.

## Common IRCTC App Problems

Before diving into fixes, here are the most common issues users face:

- App crashes immediately after opening
- "Connection error" or "Server not responding" message
- App stuck on loading screen
- Login page not loading
- Payment page crashes mid-transaction
- OTP not received for login
- App showing blank white screen

## Fix 1: Clear App Cache and Data

This fixes most IRCTC app problems instantly.

**For Android:**
1. Go to **Settings → Apps → IRCTC Rail Connect**
2. Tap **Storage & Cache**
3. Tap **Clear Cache** first
4. If problem persists, tap **Clear Data** (you'll need to login again)
5. Reopen the app

**For iPhone:**
1. Go to **Settings → General → iPhone Storage**
2. Find **IRCTC Rail Connect**
3. Tap **Offload App** (this preserves your data)
4. Reinstall the app from App Store

## Fix 2: Update the App

An outdated app version causes most crashes and compatibility issues.

1. Open **Google Play Store** (Android) or **App Store** (iPhone)
2. Search for "IRCTC Rail Connect"
3. If an **Update** button appears, tap it
4. Wait for the update to complete
5. Open the app and try again

**Tip:** Enable auto-updates for the IRCTC app to avoid this issue in the future.

## Fix 3: Check Your Internet Connection

IRCTC app requires a stable internet connection. Here's what to try:

1. **Switch between WiFi and mobile data** — sometimes one works better
2. **Turn Airplane mode ON for 10 seconds**, then OFF
3. **Reset network settings:** Settings → General Management → Reset → Reset Network Settings
4. Try using a **different WiFi network** if available
5. Check if other apps are working — if not, the problem is your internet

## Fix 4: Check IRCTC Server Status

Sometimes the problem is not your app — IRCTC servers go down frequently.

- **Peak crash times:** 10:00 AM - 10:30 AM (Tatkal window) and 8:00 AM - 9:00 AM
- Visit [irctc.co.in](https://www.irctc.co.in) in your browser — if the website is also down, servers are the issue
- Check social media for "#IRCTCDown" trending
- **Solution:** Wait 15-30 minutes and try again

## Fix 5: Reinstall the App

If clearing cache didn't work, a fresh install often fixes persistent issues.

1. Uninstall IRCTC Rail Connect from your phone
2. Restart your phone
3. Install the app again from Play Store/App Store
4. Login with your IRCTC credentials
5. Try booking again

## Fix 6: Fix Login Issues

If you can't login to the IRCTC app:

1. **Forgot Password:** Tap "Forgot Password" on the login screen → Enter User ID → Verify via OTP → Set new password
2. **OTP not received:** Wait 2 minutes → Tap "Resend OTP" → Check if your mobile number is correct in your IRCTC profile
3. **Account locked:** Too many wrong attempts lock your account for 30 minutes. Wait and try again
4. **Captcha not loading:** Switch to mobile data or try a different network

## Fix 7: Fix Payment Failures

Payment crashes during booking are the most frustrating issue:

1. **Don't press back button** during payment — wait for timeout
2. If payment failed but money deducted, check our [IRCTC payment failed guide](/guide/irctc-payment-failed-booking-fix)
3. Use **UPI payment** instead of net banking — it's faster and more reliable
4. Avoid booking during 10:00-10:15 AM Tatkal rush

## Fix 8: Device-Specific Fixes

### For Older Android Phones (Android 8 or below):
- The latest IRCTC app may not support your phone
- Try booking via the [IRCTC website](https://www.irctc.co.in) in Chrome browser instead
- Enable "Desktop mode" in Chrome for better experience

### For iPhone:
- Force close the app: Swipe up from bottom, swipe the app away
- Check if iOS is updated to the latest version
- Reset network settings: Settings → General → Transfer or Reset → Reset → Reset Network Settings

## Fix 9: VPN and Proxy Issues

If you're using a VPN, it may block IRCTC:

1. **Disable VPN** before opening the IRCTC app
2. Disable any **ad blocker** apps
3. If your ISP is blocking IRCTC, try switching to mobile data
4. Remove any proxy settings from your WiFi connection

## Tips for Smooth IRCTC App Usage

- **Book during off-peak hours** (after 11 AM) when possible
- Keep your app **always updated** to the latest version
- Use **UPI payments** — they're faster than net banking
- **Save passenger details** in advance to speed up booking
- Keep your **IRCTC password simple but strong** — complex passwords cause login delays
- For Tatkal, open the app at **9:55 AM** and stay on the search page ready

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why does IRCTC app keep crashing?
The IRCTC app crashes mainly due to outdated app version, corrupted cache, or server overload during peak hours. Clear your cache, update the app, and try during off-peak hours.

### How do I fix IRCTC connection error?
Switch between WiFi and mobile data, turn airplane mode on/off, clear app cache, and check if IRCTC servers are down. If using VPN, disable it.

### Why is IRCTC app not loading after update?
Sometimes a new update has bugs. Clear the app cache and data, restart your phone, and if the issue persists, uninstall and reinstall the app.

### Can I book tickets if IRCTC app is not working?
Yes. Use the IRCTC website at [irctc.co.in](https://www.irctc.co.in) in your mobile or desktop browser. You can also book via authorized agents or the railway counter.

### Why is IRCTC app slow during Tatkal time?
Millions of users try to book Tatkal tickets simultaneously at 10 AM (AC) and 11 AM (Non-AC), causing massive server load. Use the website instead of the app for better chances.

### How do I fix IRCTC app blank screen?
Clear cache and data, update the app, check your internet connection, and restart your phone. If the blank screen persists, reinstall the app.

### Is there an alternative to the IRCTC app?
You can use the IRCTC website in any browser, book via authorized travel agents, or use the 139 railway enquiry number. Third-party apps like Paytm and MakeMyTrip also offer IRCTC bookings.

### Why is OTP not coming on IRCTC app?
OTP delays happen due to server load or telecom issues. Wait 2 minutes before requesting resend. Ensure your registered mobile number is active and has network coverage.
