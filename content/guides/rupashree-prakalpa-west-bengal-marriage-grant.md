## What is Rupashree Prakalpa?

**Rupashree Prakalpa** is a West Bengal government scheme that gives a **one-time ₹25,000 financial assistance** to economically backward families at the time of their **daughter's marriage**. The scheme is implemented by the Department of Women and Child Development and Social Welfare, West Bengal.

Applications must be submitted **before the marriage** — at least 30 days in advance.

---

## Key Benefits

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Grant amount | ₹25,000 (one-time) |
| When to apply | At least 30 days before marriage date |
| Payment | DBT to bride's bank account on or before marriage day |
| Target | Economically backward families |
| Income limit | Annual family income ≤ ₹1.5 lakh |
| Bride's age | 18 years or above |

---

## Who is Eligible?

**You ARE eligible if:**
- The bride is a **permanent resident of West Bengal** (3+ years)
- The bride is **18 years or older** on the date of marriage
- Annual family income is **₹1,50,000 or less**
- It is the bride's **first marriage**
- The bride and her family are not income tax payers

> There is **no caste/religion restriction** — any economically weaker family qualifies regardless of caste.

---

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar Card** — bride's
2. **Voter ID card** — bride's (West Bengal voter ID helps establish residency)
3. **Age proof** — Aadhaar with DoB, birth certificate, or school certificate
4. **Income certificate** — from Block Development Officer or SDO (family income ≤ ₹1.5L)
5. **Marriage invitation card** — showing date and venue (for advance application)
6. **Bank passbook** — bride's own Aadhaar-linked account
7. **Photograph** — bride's
8. **Residence proof** — ration card, electricity bill, or other WB residency proof

## How to Apply — Step by Step

### Step 1 — Apply at Least 30 Days Before Marriage

This is critical. Applications submitted less than 30 days before marriage may not be processed in time for pre-wedding disbursement.

### Step 2 — Apply Online

1. Go to **[webcsc.org](https://webcsc.org)** → Rupashree Prakalpa
2. Register with mobile number
3. Fill the online form with personal, family, and marriage details
4. Upload all documents
5. Submit and note Application Reference Number

### Step 3 — Apply at CSC / Duare Sarkar Camp

Visit the nearest **Common Service Centre (CSC)** or **Duare Sarkar camp** for assisted application. The operator fills the form on your behalf.

### Step 4 — Block Development Officer Verification

The BDO verifies:
- Income certificate
- Residency
- Age of bride

### Step 5 — Money Released Before Marriage

The ₹25,000 is credited to the bride's bank account **before or on the day of marriage**.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. Can I apply after the marriage?**

No. Rupashree Prakalpa requires application **before the marriage** — at least 30 days in advance. Post-marriage applications are not accepted.

**2. My daughter's marriage is in 15 days. Is it too late?**

Yes — 30 days advance is required. Apply immediately and contact your Block Development Officer's office for emergency processing, though approval in less than 30 days is not guaranteed.

**3. I applied in time but the money hasn't come. The marriage is tomorrow. What do I do?**

Contact the BDO office immediately. Also call the Rupashree helpline (if available via webcsc.org). In genuine cases, emergency disbursement may be arranged.

**4. My daughter is from an upper-caste family. Does income matter?**

Yes — the only criterion is **income (≤ ₹1.5 lakh/year)**. There is no caste restriction. Upper-caste BPL families are equally eligible.

**5. I have 2 daughters getting married (different times). Can I apply for both?**

Yes. The scheme allows one application per daughter's first marriage. Two daughters, two separate applications.
