## Why You May Need to Change Address on Your Passport

If you have moved to a new address — whether within the same city or to a different city or state — your passport should reflect your current residential address. An updated passport address is important for:

- **Visa applications** — Many embassies verify your current address
- **Police verification** — Conducted at your present address
- **Identity proof** — Passport serves as a primary ID document
- **Government services** — Some require address-matched documents

> **Important:** Changing the address on your passport is officially processed as a **"Re-issue of Passport"** under the reason "Change in existing personal particulars." You will receive a fresh passport with a new number.

## Same City vs Different City/State — Does It Matter?

Yes, the process differs slightly based on whether you're changing your address within the same city or moving to a different city/state.

### Address Change Within the Same City

- You can apply at the same **Passport Seva Kendra (PSK)** or **Post Office Passport Seva Kendra (POPSK)**
- Police verification may or may not be required (depends on whether the system already has your verification on file)
- Processing is generally faster

### Address Change to a Different City or State

- You must apply at a PSK/POPSK in your **new city of residence**
- **Police verification is mandatory** at your new address
- Processing may take slightly longer due to police verification at the new location
- No NOC is required from the old passport office

## Step-by-Step Process to Change Passport Address Online

### Step 1: Register or Log In on Passport Seva Portal

1. Visit [passportindia.gov.in](https://www.passportindia.gov.in)
2. If you're a new user, click **"Register Now"** and create an account
3. If you already have an account, log in with your credentials

### Step 2: Apply Online — Fill the Application Form

1. After logging in, click **"Apply for Re-issue of Passport"**
2. Select the reason as **"Change in existing personal particulars"**
3. Fill in your **new address** in the address fields
4. Ensure all other details (name, date of birth, etc.) are correct
5. Upload your recent **passport-size photograph** (as per specifications)
6. Review all details carefully and **submit the form**

### Step 3: Pay the Fees Online

Pay the application fee online using net banking, credit/debit card, or UPI. Fee details are covered below.

### Step 4: Book an Appointment

1. After payment, you'll be directed to **schedule an appointment**
2. Choose a **Passport Seva Kendra (PSK)** or **Post Office Passport Seva Kendra (POPSK)** near your new address
3. Select a convenient date and time slot
4. Download and print your **appointment confirmation**

### Step 5: Visit the PSK/POPSK

On your appointment date, visit the PSK/POPSK with:

- **Printed application form** (submitted online)
- **Appointment confirmation**
- **Original documents** and **self-attested photocopies**
- **Old passport** (original)

At the centre, you'll go through these counters:

| Counter | Activity |
|---------|----------|
| **A** | Application submission and token |
| **B** | Document verification |
| **C** | Biometrics (photo, fingerprint, signature) |

### Step 6: Police Verification

After your PSK visit, police verification is initiated at your **new address**. A police officer will visit your residence or you may be called to the local police station. This is standard procedure for address changes.

### Step 7: Passport Dispatch

Once police verification is complete and approved, your new passport is printed and dispatched via **Speed Post** to your new address.

## Documents Required for Passport Address Change

### Mandatory Documents

| Document | Details |
|----------|---------|
| **Old Passport** (original + photocopy) | First page, last page, and all stamped/visa pages |
| **Address Proof** (new address) | Any one from the list below |
| **Annexure D** | Self-declaration for change in personal particulars |

### Accepted Address Proof Documents

You need **one** of the following documents showing your **new address**:

- **Aadhaar Card** — Most commonly accepted and preferred
- **Electricity bill** (in your name or spouse's/parent's name, not older than 3 months)
- **Water bill** (not older than 3 months)
- **Telephone/mobile postpaid bill** (not older than 3 months)
- **Bank account statement/passbook** (with updated address)
- **Voter ID card** (with new address)
- **Income Tax Assessment Order**
- **Registered rent agreement** or **registered sale deed**
- **Gas connection bill** (in your name)

> **Pro tip:** If your [Aadhaar address is already updated](/guide/aadhaar-address-change-online), it serves as the strongest single document for address proof. Update your Aadhaar address first before applying for passport address change.

### Additional Documents (If Applicable)

- **Annexure E** — If applying on the basis of spouse's/parent's documents for address proof
- **Marriage certificate** — If address change is combined with name change after marriage

## Fees for Passport Address Change

The fee for changing address on passport is the same as a passport re-issue:

| Passport Type | Normal Fee | Tatkal Fee |
|---------------|-----------|------------|
| **36-page booklet** | ₹1,500 | ₹3,500 |
| **60-page booklet** (jumbo) | ₹2,000 | ₹4,000 |

> **Note:** Fees are subject to revision. Check the latest fees on [passportindia.gov.in](https://www.passportindia.gov.in) before applying.

## Short Validity Passport During Address Change

In some cases, you may be issued a **short validity passport** (valid for 1-3 years instead of the standard 10 years). This can happen when:

- Police verification reveals a **discrepancy** or is pending
- There is an **adverse police report** at the new address
- Documents are **insufficient** to fully establish your new address

A short validity passport is fully functional for travel. Once the issue is resolved, you can apply again for a **full validity (10-year) passport** without paying additional fees in certain cases.

> This is different from a regular re-issue. If you receive a short validity passport, contact the Regional Passport Office for clarification on next steps.

## Police Verification for Address Change

Police verification is a critical part of the passport address change process, especially when moving to a different city or state.

### What to Expect

- A **police officer** visits your new residential address
- They verify your **identity** and **residence** at the new address
- They may ask for **ID proof** and speak to **neighbours** or your **landlord**
- The verification report is submitted to the Passport Office

### How to Prepare

1. Keep your **original documents** ready at home
2. Inform your **landlord** (if renting) about the upcoming verification
3. Ensure your **name is on the doorbell/nameplate** if possible
4. Keep a copy of your **rent agreement** handy

### Timeline for Police Verification

- **Same city:** 1-2 weeks typically
- **Different city/state:** 2-4 weeks
- **Tatkal applications:** Police verification is done after passport issuance (post-verification)

For detailed information, read our guide on [passport police verification process](/guide/passport-police-verification-process).

## Tips for a Smooth Address Change Process

- **Update Aadhaar first** — [Change your Aadhaar address](/guide/aadhaar-address-change-online) before applying for passport address change. Aadhaar is the most accepted address proof.
- **Apply online** — The online process through [Passport Seva](/guide/passport-apply-online) is faster than offline submission
- **Book early morning slots** — Less crowded, faster processing at PSK
- **Carry extra photocopies** — Take 2 sets of all documents
- **Don't delay police verification** — Be available at your new address when the police officer visits

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I change my passport address online without visiting a PSK?

No. While the application and appointment booking are done online, you must visit a Passport Seva Kendra (PSK) or POPSK in person for document verification and biometrics.

### Do I get a new passport number after address change?

Yes. Address change is processed as a re-issue, and you receive a **new passport with a new passport number**. Your old passport will be cancelled (hole-punched and returned to you).

### Is police verification mandatory for passport address change?

Police verification is mandatory when you change your address to a **different city or state**. For address changes within the same city, it may be waived if prior police verification exists in the system.

### Can I apply for address change if my passport is expired?

Yes. You can apply for re-issue with address change even if your passport has expired. The process remains the same. You may also want to check our guide on [passport renewal online](/guide/passport-renewal-online-india).

### How long is the new passport valid after address change?

The new passport issued after address change is valid for **10 years** from the date of issue (5 years for minors below 18). Short validity passports are exceptions, as explained above.

### Can I change my address and name simultaneously on my passport?

Yes. When filling the re-issue application, you can select multiple reasons for change — including both name and address. You'll need to provide supporting documents for each change (e.g., marriage certificate for name change, address proof for address change).

### What if police verification fails or is negative?

If police verification returns an adverse report, your application may be put on hold or you may receive a short validity passport. You can appeal or provide additional documents to the Regional Passport Office to resolve the issue.
