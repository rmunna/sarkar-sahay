# NREGA Payment Pending or Not Credited 2026? Fix It Step by Step

Under MGNREGA (Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act), wages must be paid within **15 days** of work completion. If your wages are delayed, this guide explains exactly how to check the FTO (Fund Transfer Order) status and get the payment credited.

## Why Is Your NREGA Payment Delayed?

| Reason | What Happens |
|--------|-------------|
| **FTO not generated** | Gram Panchayat hasn't submitted attendance/muster roll |
| **FTO pending at bank** | FTO generated but bank hasn't processed it |
| **FTO rejected** | Bank rejected due to wrong account number or Aadhaar mismatch |
| **Aadhaar-bank link missing** | Payment can't go to DBT without Aadhaar-linked account |
| **Wrong bank account** | Account closed, merged, or wrong IFSC |
| **Complaint/objection filed** | Some administrative hold on your job card |

## Step 1: Check FTO Status at nrega.nic.in

1. Visit **[nrega.nic.in](https://nrega.nic.in/)**
2. Select your **State** from the map
3. Click **"FTO Tracking"** or **"Payment Performance"**
4. Select District → Block → Panchayat
5. Enter the date range of when you worked
6. Check your **FTO status**:
   - **FTO Generated**: submitted to bank, waiting
   - **1st Sign**: one officer approved, awaiting second signature
   - **2nd Sign**: both signatures done, sent to bank
   - **Processed**: bank transferred the amount
   - **Rejected**: bank returned the FTO (needs regeneration)

## Step 2: If FTO Is Rejected — What to Do

FTO rejection is the most common cause of payment delay. Reasons and fixes:

| Rejection Reason | What to Do |
|-----------------|-----------|
| Wrong account number | Visit BDO/GP office with correct bank passbook |
| Aadhaar not seeded in bank | Link Aadhaar at your bank branch |
| Account closed/inactive | Open new account + update at GP office |
| Name mismatch (Aadhaar vs bank) | Update name with bank OR correct Aadhaar (easier at bank) |
| IFSC code changed | Give updated IFSC to GP secretary (banks merge/change IFSC) |

After correcting details at the Gram Panchayat or BDO office, the FTO will be **regenerated and resubmitted** within 7–15 days.

## Step 3: If FTO Is Processed But Money Hasn't Come to Bank

If FTO shows "Processed" but your account hasn't received the money:

1. **Wait 2–3 bank working days** — bank processing takes time
2. **Check your passbook or PFMS app** — NREGA wages show as "PMGSFY" or "MGNREGS" credit
3. **Check at Aadhaar Payment Bridge**: your payment may have gone to a different account if Aadhaar is linked to multiple accounts
4. **Visit your bank branch** with your job card number and ask them to trace the PFMS transaction
5. **Check PFMS status** at [pfms.nic.in](https://pfms.nic.in/) → Know Your Payment → enter Aadhaar or bank account

## Step 4: Update Bank Account at BDO

If you need to change the bank account for NREGA wages:

1. Visit your **Gram Panchayat secretary** or **Block Development Office (BDO)**
2. Carry:
   - New bank passbook (with Aadhaar-linked account)
   - NREGA Job Card
   - Aadhaar card
3. Fill the **bank account update form**
4. BDO will update your account in the NREGA MIS system
5. New account activates for next wage payment cycle

> **Important**: Your new account MUST be Aadhaar-linked (Aadhaar seeded at the bank). NREGA wages are paid via Aadhaar Payment Bridge (APB) — accounts without Aadhaar seeding will get rejected again.

## Step 5: File a Complaint If Payment Is Delayed Beyond 15 Days

MGNREGA law mandates payment within 15 days. If delayed beyond that, you're entitled to **compensation at 0.05% of wages per day of delay**.

**How to file a complaint:**

1. **Written complaint to BDO**: submit in writing; they must respond within 15 days
2. **MGNREGA Ombudsman**: every district has an MGNREGA ombudsman (contact District Collector's office)
3. **Online Grievance at pgportal.gov.in**: under Ministry of Rural Development → MGNREGS
4. **NREGA Helpline**: **1800-345-5244** (toll-free)
5. **Social Audit**: MGNREGA social audits happen twice a year in each Gram Panchayat — raise your complaint during the audit

## Check Your NREGA Payment History Online

1. Go to [nrega.nic.in](https://nrega.nic.in/) → "Job Card" → Enter State
2. Enter your **Job Card Number** (format: state code + district code + number)
3. View all work history, muster rolls and payment dates
4. Download wage payment summary as PDF

Alternatively: use the **Mera Nrega app** (Play Store) to check payment status on mobile.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many days does NREGA payment take after work?

By law, NREGA wages must be paid within **15 days** of the date of closure of the muster roll (attendance register). In practice, it takes 10–20 days depending on how quickly the Gram Panchayat generates the FTO and whether the bank processes it without rejection.

### My NREGA payment came but the amount is less than expected. Why?

Check your muster roll (attendance register) — attendance data may be partially entered. Also, if you were absent on some days, those won't be counted. If you believe all days are correct, ask the GP secretary to show you the muster roll and count the entries.

### Can I change my NREGA payment to a Post Office savings account?

Yes. Post Office savings accounts (with Aadhaar seeding) can receive NREGA wages. The process is the same: visit BDO with your Post Office passbook and Aadhaar.

### What if my job card is in my deceased relative's name?

You need to first get the job card updated with the new household head's name. Visit the Gram Panchayat with the death certificate + Aadhaar of the new head. The GP will update the job card, and then the bank account can be updated.
