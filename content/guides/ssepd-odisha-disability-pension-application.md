# SSEPD Odisha — Disability Pension Application Status Check & Guide (2026)

The **Social Security & Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (SSEPD)** department, Government of Odisha, runs multiple disability pension and empowerment schemes for persons with disabilities (PwD), destitute persons, and other vulnerable groups.

This guide covers how to check your SSEPD application status by Aadhaar number, track pension payments, and apply for SSEPD schemes.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is independent and not affiliated with the Government of Odisha. For account-specific help, contact SSEPD Odisha directly.

## SSEPD Odisha Schemes at a Glance

| Scheme | Full Name | Beneficiary | Pension Amount |
|--------|-----------|-------------|---------------|
| **MODST** | Madhu Babu Disability Special Pension | Persons with 40%+ disability | ₹500–₹700/month |
| **NMDS** | National Scheme for PwD | Persons with 80%+ disability (BPL) | ₹300/month (central) |
| **ADWAS** | Assistance to Destitute Widows & Abandoned Women | Destitute widows | ₹500–₹700/month |
| **IGDPS** | Indira Gandhi Disability Pension Scheme | Severe PwD aged 18–79 (BPL) | ₹500/month |
| **Scholarship** | Pre/Post Matric Scholarship for PwD | Students with disability | As per norm |
| **NMBS** | National Maternity Benefit Scheme | PwD pregnant women | One-time ₹6,000 |

## Check SSEPD Application Status by Aadhaar

### Method 1: SSEPD Official Portal (Online Status Check)

1. Go to **[ssepd.odisha.gov.in](https://ssepd.odisha.gov.in)**
2. Click on **"Beneficiary Status"** or **"Application Status"** on the homepage
3. Select the scheme (MODST, ADWAS, IGDPS, etc.)
4. Enter your **Aadhaar number** or **Application ID**
5. Click **"Submit"** or **"Search"**
6. Your application status will show:
   - Application received
   - Under verification
   - Approved / Pending / Rejected
   - Pension disbursed (with last payment date)

### Method 2: Through ServicePlus / SOVA Odisha

1. Visit **[ssepd.odisha.gov.in/scheme_details](https://ssepd.odisha.gov.in/scheme_details)**
2. Click on the relevant scheme
3. Use the **"Track Application"** option
4. Enter your reference number or Aadhaar

### Method 3: Gram Panchayat / Block Office

1. Visit your nearest Gram Panchayat office or Block Development Office (BDO)
2. Carry your Aadhaar card and application acknowledgment slip
3. The Panchayat secretary or Social Welfare Officer can check status on their terminal

## How to Apply for SSEPD Schemes

### Documents Required

| Document | Details |
|----------|---------|
| **Disability Certificate** | From Chief District Medical Officer (CDMO) — minimum 40% disability |
| **Aadhaar Card** | Mandatory for all schemes |
| **Bank Account** | Linked to Aadhaar (for DBT payment) |
| **Income Certificate** | For income-based schemes (BPL or below income threshold) |
| **Residence Proof** | Voter ID, ration card or domicile certificate |
| **Passport Photo** | 2 recent colour photographs |
| **Ration Card** | For BPL-category benefits |

### Application Process — Online

1. Go to **[ssepd.odisha.gov.in](https://ssepd.odisha.gov.in)** → **"Apply Online"**
2. Select the scheme you're applying for
3. Register/login using mobile number and OTP
4. Fill the application form:
   - Personal details (name, DOB, gender, caste)
   - Disability details (type, percentage, certificate number)
   - Bank account details (account number, IFSC)
   - Aadhaar number
5. Upload scanned documents
6. Submit and note the **Application Reference Number**

### Application Process — Offline

1. Visit your **Block Development Office (BDO)** or **Gram Panchayat office**
2. Collect the SSEPD scheme application form
3. Fill in all details
4. Attach self-attested copies of all documents
5. Submit to the Panchayat Secretary or Social Welfare Extension Officer (SWEO)
6. Collect the acknowledgment receipt

## How SSEPD Pension is Paid

SSEPD pension is paid through **Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)** to the beneficiary's Aadhaar-linked bank account.

### Payment Schedule
- Pension is credited **monthly** (1st to 10th of each month)
- Delays may occur due to Aadhaar seeding issues or bank account validation failures

### Check If Pension Was Credited

1. **SMS:** Registered mobile gets an SMS from the bank when pension is credited
2. **Bank Passbook:** Check passbook/mini statement for credit from "SSEPD" or "PFMS"
3. **PFMS Portal:** Check at [pfms.nic.in](https://pfms.nic.in) → Know Your Payment → enter Aadhaar number
4. **SSEPD Portal:** Login → Beneficiary Dashboard → Payment History

## Common Issues and Fixes

### 1. Application Status Stuck at "Under Verification" for Months

**Possible reasons:**
- Disability certificate not verified by CDMO
- Bank account not Aadhaar-seeded
- Document mismatch

**Fix:**
- Visit BDO and request manual verification
- Re-submit Aadhaar-seeding consent to your bank
- Cross-check name spelling on Aadhaar vs bank account

### 2. Pension Not Received Despite Approval

**Fix:**
1. Check PFMS payment status at pfms.nic.in
2. Verify your bank account is Aadhaar-linked (call bank helpline)
3. Visit BDO with Aadhaar + bank passbook
4. If Aadhaar seeding is the issue — submit fresh seeding consent at bank
5. If bank account is closed — submit new account details to BDO

### 3. Application Rejected

**Possible reasons:**
- Disability percentage below threshold (below 40% for MODST)
- Income above limit
- Aadhaar not linked to bank account
- Duplicate beneficiary (already receiving another pension)

**Fix:**
1. Obtain rejection reason from BDO in writing
2. If disability certificate: get fresh certificate from CDMO with correct percentage
3. If income: obtain fresh income certificate from Revenue Department
4. File an appeal at the District Social Welfare Officer (DSWO)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the minimum disability percentage for SSEPD MODST pension?**
Minimum 40% disability as certified by the Chief District Medical Officer (CDMO). The disability certificate must be from a government hospital.

**How do I check my SSEPD application status by Aadhaar?**
Go to ssepd.odisha.gov.in, click "Beneficiary Status", select your scheme, and enter your Aadhaar number. You can also visit the BDO office with your Aadhaar card for offline status check.

**How long does SSEPD application approval take?**
Typically 30–90 days from submission. The BDO verifies documents, forwards to DSWO, who recommends to SSEPD for final approval.

**Can I receive both SSEPD pension and Madhu Babu Pension?**
No — beneficiaries cannot receive both simultaneously. MODST and Madhu Babu Pension are linked. You will receive whichever pension you are enrolled in first.

**What if my disability certificate has expired?**
You must renew the disability certificate from CDMO. Many types of permanent disabilities (congenital, etc.) get permanent certificates, while acquired disabilities may have time-limited certificates.

**My pension was stopped without notice — what should I do?**
Visit BDO immediately. Common reasons: Aadhaar seeding failure, bank account closed, annual verification not done, or duplicate beneficiary flag. Bring Aadhaar + bank passbook for resolution.
