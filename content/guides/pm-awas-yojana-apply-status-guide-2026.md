# PM Awas Yojana 2026 — Complete Apply & Eligibility Guide

**Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY)** is India's flagship affordable housing scheme. It provides subsidized housing to eligible urban and rural poor. PMAY has been extended with a new phase (PMAY 2.0) targeting 3 crore additional houses by 2029.

---

## PMAY — Two Components

| Component | Target | Administering Ministry |
|----------|--------|----------------------|
| **PMAY-Urban (PMAY-U)** | Urban areas — slum dwellers, EWS/LIG/MIG | MoHUA (Housing & Urban Affairs) |
| **PMAY-Gramin (PMAY-G)** | Rural villages | Ministry of Rural Development |

---

## PMAY-U (Urban) — Eligibility

| Category | Annual Income | Benefit |
|----------|-------------|---------|
| **EWS (Economically Weaker Section)** | Up to ₹3 lakh | Full subsidy (₹2.67 lakh) on home loan |
| **LIG (Low Income Group)** | ₹3–6 lakh | Subsidy on home loan |
| **MIG-I (Middle Income Group)** | ₹6–12 lakh | Subsidy on home loan |
| **MIG-II** | ₹12–18 lakh | Reduced subsidy |

**Key conditions:**
- Beneficiary family should NOT own a pucca house anywhere in India
- Must be first-time home buyer
- Beneficiary + spouse + children — none should have pucca house in their name
- House must be registered in woman's name (mandatory for EWS/LIG)

---

## CLSS Subsidy — How Much Can You Get?

**Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme (CLSS):** Interest subsidy on home loan

| Category | Loan Amount (Max) | Interest Subsidy | Max Subsidy (NPV) |
|----------|------------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| EWS/LIG | ₹6 lakh | 6.5% | ~₹2.67 lakh |
| MIG-I | ₹9 lakh | 4% | ~₹2.35 lakh |
| MIG-II | ₹12 lakh | 3% | ~₹2.30 lakh |

*Subsidy is given as upfront credit to your loan account, reducing your principal outstanding.*

**Example:** EWS household with ₹6 lakh home loan gets ₹2.67 lakh upfront subsidy → Effective loan: ₹3.33 lakh → Much lower EMI.

## How to Apply for PMAY-U

### Through PLI (Primary Lending Institution — Banks):

Most PMAY benefits are processed through banks when you take a home loan:

1. Approach any bank/NBFC that participates in PMAY (SBI, HDFC, LIC HFL, PNB HFL, Bank of Baroda — all participate)
2. Inform them you want PMAY subsidy
3. Bank applies on your behalf to NHB/HUDCO (nodal agencies)
4. Subsidy credited to your loan account within 3–4 months

### Through ULB (Urban Local Body):

For in-situ slum rehabilitation:
1. Contact your municipal corporation / town panchayat
2. Apply with Aadhaar + income proof + house documents
3. They process under PMAY-U BLC/AHP verticals

### Online Check at pmaymis.gov.in:
1. **[pmaymis.gov.in](https://pmaymis.gov.in)** → "Citizen Assessment"
2. Check eligibility → Fill assessment form
3. Assessment generates a reference for bank processing

## Check PMAY Beneficiary Status

1. **[pmaymis.gov.in](https://pmaymis.gov.in)** → **"Search Beneficiary"**
2. Enter: Aadhaar number / name / father's name / state
3. Application status shown: Received / Under Process / Approved / Rejected

## PMAY-G (Rural) — Grameen Housing

**PMAY-G** provides financial assistance to rural households without pucca housing:

| Category | Assistance |
|----------|-----------|
| Plain areas (general) | ₹1.20 lakh |
| Hilly / difficult areas | ₹1.30 lakh |
| North-East / IAP districts | ₹1.30 lakh |

**Additional:** ₹12,000 for toilet (SBM), MGNREGA wages for construction labour (90 days = ₹9,000–15,000)

### PMAY-G Eligibility:
- Rural household
- No pucca house (less than 25 sqm dwelling or none)
- Listed in SECC 2011 survey (Socio-Economic Caste Census)
- Priority: SC/ST, bonded labour, minorities, disabled-headed households

### PMAY-G Application:
Registered through **Gram Panchayat** — you don't apply directly. If eligible per SECC 2011, your name is in the waiting list. Contact village Pradhan / BDO (Block Development Officer) to check your eligibility + apply.

**Track PMAY-G payment:** [pmayg.nic.in](https://pmayg.nic.in) → Stakeholders → Awaassoft → Track payment

## PMAY 2.0 (2024–2029)

Government launched **PMAY 2.0** with fresh eligibility:
- Target: 3 crore more houses (1 crore urban + 2 crore rural)
- Fresh beneficiary identification: Those not covered in PMAY 1.0
- EWS/LIG: New applications being accepted at banks
- Subsidy amount: Under revision (check latest pmaymis.gov.in notification)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**I applied for PMAY through my bank but didn't receive subsidy — what to do?**
Steps: (1) Check with bank — was application submitted to NHB/HUDCO? Get application number, (2) Track at pmaymis.gov.in, (3) If application is approved but subsidy not credited: Call PMAY helpline: 1800-11-6163, (4) File CPGRAMS (grievance portal) at pgportal.gov.in.

**Can both husband and wife claim PMAY subsidy?**
No — PMAY subsidy is per household (not per person). One application per family. Both names can be on the property, but only one CLSS subsidy per household.

**I own land in village — can I get PMAY for urban house?**
PMAY eligibility is that the family should not own a pucca house anywhere in India. Owning land (without a pucca house) doesn't disqualify you. Owning a pucca house (completed construction) anywhere disqualifies you.

**My income is ₹15 lakh — can I benefit from PMAY?**
MIG-II upper limit is ₹18 lakh. You qualify for MIG-II subsidy (3% on ₹12 lakh loan = ~₹2.30 lakh subsidy). Apply through your home loan bank.

**PMAY CLSS subsidy — when does it get credited to loan?**
After bank application and NHB/HUDCO approval: Usually 2–4 months. The subsidy appears as "credit to loan" in your statement, directly reducing principal. Subsequent EMIs are lower.
