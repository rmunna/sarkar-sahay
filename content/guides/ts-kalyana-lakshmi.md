# Kalyana Lakshmi / Shaadi Mubarak – Marriage Assistance in Telangana

Kalyana Lakshmi and Shaadi Mubarak are marriage assistance schemes by the Government of Telangana providing ₹1,00,116 to brides from economically weaker families. Kalyana Lakshmi is for Hindu brides and Shaadi Mubarak is for Muslim brides — both offer the same benefit amount.

## What is Kalyana Lakshmi / Shaadi Mubarak?

These twin schemes provide one-time financial assistance of ₹1,00,116 to the bride at the time of marriage. The amount is transferred directly to the bride's bank account. The scheme reduces the financial burden on families during weddings and promotes women's financial independence.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Bride's age:** Must be 18 years or above at the time of marriage
2. **Community:** Must belong to SC, ST, BC, EBC, or Minority community
3. **Residency:** Bride's family must be residents of Telangana
4. **Income:** Annual family income should not exceed ₹2,00,000
5. **First marriage:** Generally applicable for the bride's first marriage
6. **Aadhaar:** Bride must have an Aadhaar card
7. **Kalyana Lakshmi:** For Hindu, Christian, and other non-Muslim brides
8. **Shaadi Mubarak:** For Muslim brides

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar card of bride (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- Age proof of bride (SSC certificate / birth certificate)
- Caste/community certificate
- Income certificate (family income below ₹2,00,000)
- Bank passbook of bride (Aadhaar-linked)
- Ration card
- Marriage invitation card or declaration of intended marriage
- Passport-size photographs of bride
- Bridegroom's Aadhaar card (in some cases)

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Online Application

1. **Visit ePASS portal:** Go to [telanganaepass.cgg.gov.in](https://telanganaepass.cgg.gov.in/KalyanaLakshmiLinks.do)
2. **Select scheme:** Choose Kalyana Lakshmi or Shaadi Mubarak
3. **Register:** Create an account with bride's Aadhaar and mobile number
4. **Fill application:** Enter bride's details, marriage details, family income, and bank information
5. **Upload documents:** Upload scanned copies of all required documents
6. **Submit application:** Review and submit — note the application ID
7. **Print application:** Take a printout of the submitted application
8. **Submit printout at MeeSeva:** Visit the nearest MeeSeva center with the printout and original documents
9. **Verification:** Revenue and social welfare officials verify the application
10. **Approval and payment:** After approval, ₹1,00,116 is credited to the bride's bank account

### Timeline for Application

- Apply **before the marriage date** (at least 8–10 days before)
- Post-marriage applications may also be accepted within a limited window — check current rules

## Benefit Details

| Detail | Information |
|--------|------------|
| Amount | ₹1,00,116 |
| Payment to | Bride's Aadhaar-linked bank account |
| Mode | Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) |
| Frequency | One-time (at marriage) |

## Processing Time

- **Application to verification:** 7–15 days
- **Approval to disbursement:** 15–30 days after verification
- **Total:** Approximately 3–6 weeks

## Important Tips

1. **Apply before marriage:** Submit the application well in advance of the wedding date
2. **Bride's own bank account:** The amount is credited to the bride's account, not the family's
3. **Keep originals ready:** You'll need to show original documents during verification
4. **One-time benefit:** This is a one-time payment — plan accordingly
5. **Check status online:** Track your application on the ePASS portal using your application ID

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: What is the difference between Kalyana Lakshmi and Shaadi Mubarak?
Both provide the same ₹1,00,116. Kalyana Lakshmi is for non-Muslim brides and Shaadi Mubarak is for Muslim brides.

### Q2: Can I apply after the marriage?
Ideally, apply before marriage. Post-marriage applications may be accepted within a limited period — check current rules on the ePASS portal.

### Q3: Is the money given to the bride or the family?
The amount is credited directly to the bride's personal bank account.

### Q4: Can inter-caste or inter-religion marriages apply?
Yes, as long as the bride belongs to an eligible community and meets all other criteria.

### Q5: What if the bride doesn't have a bank account?
Open a bank account before applying. The account must be linked to the bride's Aadhaar for DBT.

### Q6: Is there an income limit?
Yes, the annual family income should not exceed ₹2,00,000.

### Q7: Can the scheme be availed for a second marriage?
Generally, the scheme is for the first marriage. Specific cases may vary — consult the local welfare office.
