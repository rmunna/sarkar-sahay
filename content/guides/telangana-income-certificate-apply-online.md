# Telangana Income Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **income certificate** (also called **Aay Praman Patra**) in Telangana is issued by the Revenue Department and is required for scholarship applications, BC/OBC non-creamy layer verification, BPL ration card eligibility, and government scheme benefits. Applications are submitted through **MeeSeva** — Telangana's multi-service delivery platform.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at any MeeSeva centre or at [ts.meeseva.gov.in](https://ts.meeseva.gov.in). Issued by MRO (Mandal Revenue Officer) within **7–30 days**. Fee: approximately ₹35.

---

## Why You Need Telangana Income Certificate

- **BC/OBC scholarship** — mandatory (income below ₹2 lakh/year)
- **SC/ST scholarship** — required for central and state scholarships
- **Telangana State Scholarship portal** — income proof for all categories
- **Ration card** BPL category application
- **Fee reimbursement** for SC/ST/BC students in government colleges
- **Government scheme eligibility** — CM Relief Fund, Kalyana Lakshmi, etc.

---

## Income Certificate via MeeSeva — Overview

In Telangana, income certificates are issued through MeeSeva kiosks and the online portal. The revenue verification is done by the **Mandal Revenue Inspector (MRI)** or **Village Revenue Officer (VRO)** and approved by the **Mandal Revenue Officer (MRO)**.

---

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (all family members — mandatory)
- **Ration card** (if available — shows family members)
- Self-declaration of income (online form on MeeSeva or affidavit)
- For salaried: **salary slip** or employer letter
- For farmers: **Pahani** (land records from Dharani portal)
- Voter ID (additional ID proof)
- Passport-size photograph

---

## How to Apply via MeeSeva

**Method 1: MeeSeva Kiosk (Recommended)**

1. Visit nearest **MeeSeva centre** or **kiosk** in your mandal/town
2. Ask for **"Income Certificate"** service
3. Provide Aadhaar, ration card, income declaration
4. Operator submits the application online
5. Service fee: approximately **₹35**
6. Receive acknowledgement with application number

**Method 2: MeeSeva Online Portal**

1. Go to [ts.meeseva.gov.in](https://ts.meeseva.gov.in)
2. Log in (register if first time — mobile OTP)
3. Search **"Income Certificate"**
4. Select **"Income Certificate (Residential & Income)"**
5. Fill form: applicant details, annual income, address
6. Upload documents and pay fee online
7. Submit and note reference number

---

## Status Check

1. Go to [ts.meeseva.gov.in](https://ts.meeseva.gov.in) → Log in
2. Click **"My Applications"** → View status of income certificate application
3. Or: Track via MeeSeva → "Application Status" → enter application number
4. Status flow: Submitted → MRI Verification → MRO Approval → Issued

---

## Download Telangana Income Certificate

Once approved by MRO:
1. Log in to MeeSeva portal
2. Go to **"My Certificates"** or **"My Applications"**
3. Click **"Download"** next to approved income certificate
4. PDF with digital signature — valid for all official purposes
5. Or: collect printed copy from MeeSeva centre with application number

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What income should I declare for Telangana income certificate?

Declare **total annual household income** from all sources: salary, agriculture (Pahani-based), business/trade, pension, daily wages. Estimate realistically — MRI will verify during field visit.

### Telangana income certificate for BC scholarship — what limit?

For **BC/OBC Scholarship** (Telangana): family income below **₹2 lakh/year**. For **SC/ST fee reimbursement**: no strict income ceiling, but certificate is still required.

### Can I get Telangana income certificate without MeeSeva?

You can apply directly at the **Mandal Revenue Office (MRO)** by submitting a paper application. The officer will issue after verification. Online via MeeSeva is faster.

### Income certificate from another state — does it work in Telangana?

No. For Telangana scholarships and schemes, a **Telangana-issued** income certificate is required showing your current Telangana address and income.
