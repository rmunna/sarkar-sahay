# NPCI Aadhaar Bank Link Not Working? Fix DBT Payment Failures

If your government scheme payment (PM Kisan, pension, MGNREGA, PM Awas, scholarship) isn't coming to your bank account even though your Aadhaar is linked, the problem is usually the **NPCI Aadhaar Mapper** — the central system that maps Aadhaar to a bank account for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

## What Is the NPCI Aadhaar Mapper?

NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India) maintains a central database called the **Aadhaar Payment Bridge (APB)** or NPCI Mapper. Every time a government scheme sends a DBT payment:

1. Government sends payment to NPCI with your Aadhaar number
2. NPCI mapper looks up which bank account your Aadhaar is linked to
3. NPCI routes the payment to that bank account

**If your Aadhaar is NOT correctly seeded in this mapper, the payment fails — even if you think your bank account is linked to Aadhaar.**

> "Linking Aadhaar with bank" and "Aadhaar seeded in NPCI mapper" are two different things. You may have linked Aadhaar at your bank, but if the bank didn't update the NPCI mapper, DBT payments still fail.

## Common Symptoms of NPCI Mapper Failure

- PM Kisan payment shown as "transferred" but not in your account
- Pension credited to an old/closed bank account
- MGNREGA wages going to wrong account
- DBT payment at PFMS shows "APB Credit" but money never arrived
- Government shows payment made but you received nothing

## Fix 1: Seed Aadhaar at Your Current Bank Branch

The most reliable fix:

1. Visit your **current active bank branch** (the account where you want to receive payments)
2. Tell the bank staff: **"I want to seed my Aadhaar to NPCI mapper / Aadhaar Payment Bridge"**
3. Carry: **Aadhaar card + bank passbook**
4. The bank staff links your Aadhaar to their NPCI mapper at their terminal
5. Allow **24–72 hours** for it to reflect in the NPCI central system

**Important**: If you previously seeded Aadhaar with another bank and now want payments in a new bank, you MUST seed again at the new bank. The new seeding automatically supersedes the old one in the mapper.

## Fix 2: Check NPCI Mapper Status

To check which bank account your Aadhaar is currently mapped to:

**Method 1 — via UIDAI portal:**
1. Visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/)
2. Login with Aadhaar + OTP
3. Go to **"Bank Seeding Status"** or **"Aadhaar Bank Linking Status"**
4. This shows which bank your Aadhaar is currently mapped to in NPCI

**Method 2 — via UMANG App:**
1. Open UMANG app → login with mobile + OTP
2. Go to **UIDAI → Aadhaar Bank Seeding Status**
3. View current bank and seeding date

**Method 3 — via PFMS:**
1. Visit [pfms.nic.in](https://pfms.nic.in/) → **"Know Your Payment"**
2. Enter Aadhaar number
3. See last payment received and the bank it was credited to

## Fix 3: Aadhaar Mapped to Wrong/Old Bank Account

If the mapper shows an old bank you no longer use:

1. Visit your **new/current bank branch** and request NPCI mapper update
2. The new seeding will **overwrite the old mapping** within 24–48 hours
3. Once updated, all future DBT payments will go to the new account
4. **Pending/stuck payments**: these may need to be re-initiated by the scheme department — raise a complaint with the relevant scheme (PM Kisan helpline, pension office, etc.) after updating the mapper

## Fix 4: "Aadhaar not seeded" error at Ration Shop or ePoS Machine

If your ration is being denied because the ePoS machine shows "Aadhaar not seeded":

1. This is a **different seeding** — this is about PDS (Public Distribution System) Aadhaar linking, not bank mapper
2. Visit your **Gram Panchayat or Fair Price Shop owner** to get your ration card Aadhaar seeded
3. Or update at the nearest CSC (Common Service Centre) with Aadhaar biometric authentication

## Fix 5: After Seeding, Which Payments Will Come to New Account?

After successful NPCI mapper update:
- **Future payments**: go to new bank automatically
- **Payments already failed**: may be re-credited to old account OR held at NPCI/bank — vary by scheme
- **PM Kisan**: missed installment typically included in next installment after mapper update (with re-approval)
- **Pension**: next month's payment goes to new bank; missed months must be claimed separately at scheme office

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I linked Aadhaar with my bank app. Why isn't DBT coming?

Linking Aadhaar via mobile banking for Aadhaar OTP purposes is DIFFERENT from NPCI mapper seeding. For DBT, you need the NPCI mapper seeding which must be done at the bank branch (or via net banking in some banks → "Aadhaar Seeding" option). App-based linking doesn't always trigger NPCI mapper update.

### Can I link Aadhaar to two bank accounts for DBT?

No. NPCI mapper allows only ONE bank account per Aadhaar for DBT. The most recently seeded account is active. If you seed at two banks, only the latest one gets DBT payments.

### My Aadhaar shows "Linked" at both banks. Which one gets payments?

The most recently seeded account takes priority. Check UIDAI portal (myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in) → Bank Seeding Status to see which bank is the current active mapper entry.

### My scheme payment shows "Transaction Successful" but money isn't in account. Where is it?

Use PFMS → Know Your Payment with your Aadhaar. If it says "APB Credit to Bank" but you haven't received it, the bank received the credit but may not have posted it to your account (bank processing lag: 1–3 days). Visit the bank with the PFMS transaction ID if it doesn't arrive in 3 days.
