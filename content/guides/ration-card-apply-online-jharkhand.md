# Jharkhand Ration Card Apply Online 2026

The Jharkhand ration card is issued by the **Food, Public Distribution & Consumer Affairs Department, Government of Jharkhand**. The state manages PDS through the **AAHAR** portal — Jharkhand's food security management system. Jharkhand has a large tribal population and extensive BPL coverage, with a significant portion of the population covered under NFSA.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [aahar.jharkhand.gov.in](https://aahar.jharkhand.gov.in) or visit your nearest PRJA (Pragya Kendra) / block office. PHH households get 5 kg grain/person/month free under PM GKAY.

---

## Ration Card Types in Jharkhand

| Type | Category | Entitlement |
|------|----------|-------------|
| **PHH (Priority Household)** | NFSA BPL families | 5 kg grain/person/month free (PM GKAY) |
| **AAY (Antyodaya Anna Yojana)** | Poorest households | 35 kg/family/month free |
| **State BPL** | State-covered BPL beyond NFSA quota | As per state notification |
| **APL (Above Poverty Line)** | Non-subsidised | Identity document; limited food access |

> Jharkhand has a high tribal (Adivasi) population. Tribal families in remote/forest areas often qualify for AAY or PHH even with slightly higher land holdings due to subsistence farming.

---

## Eligibility

**PHH/BPL ration card:**
- Annual household income below ₹1.20 lakh (rural) or ₹1.50 lakh (urban)
- Not a regular government or PSU employee
- No income taxpayer in family
- No motorised 4-wheeler vehicle
- No agricultural land above 5 acres (general) — tribal land norms may differ
- Aadhaar card mandatory for all members

**AAY:**
- Landless agricultural labourers
- Widow / elderly person living alone with no income
- Tribal families in remote/forest areas with no regular income
- Disabled persons with no family support
- Bonded and migrant labourers

---

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar cards (all family members — mandatory)
- Residence proof (electricity bill, water bill, rent agreement, panchayat certificate)
- Passport-size photographs
- Income certificate from Block Development Officer (BDO) or Circle Officer
- Caste certificate (especially for SC/ST — required for tribal categories)
- Bank account passbook (for DBT)
- Voter ID (as additional identity proof)

## How to Apply

**Method 1: AAHAR Jharkhand Online Portal**

1. Go to [aahar.jharkhand.gov.in](https://aahar.jharkhand.gov.in)
2. Click **"Apply for New Ration Card"** or Citizen Services
3. Log in / register with your mobile number
4. Fill in household details with Aadhaar numbers
5. Select district, block, gram panchayat / ward
6. Upload documents (income certificate, address proof, photographs)
7. Submit and note the acknowledgement number

**Method 2: Pragya Kendra / CSC**

1. Visit nearest **Pragya Kendra** (Jharkhand's CSC / Jan Seva Kendra)
2. Operator fills the online application with your documents
3. Biometric verification may be done on site
4. Service fee: approximately ₹30–50
5. Collect printed acknowledgement

**Method 3: Block Office / Panchayat Office**

1. Visit your **Block Development Office** (BDO) or Gram Panchayat
2. Collect and fill the ration card application form
3. Submit with all documents to Block Supply Officer (BSO)
4. BSO verifies and forwards to District Supply Officer for approval

---

## Status Check

1. Go to [aahar.jharkhand.gov.in](https://aahar.jharkhand.gov.in)
2. Click **"Application Status"** or **"Track Application"**
3. Enter acknowledgement number or Aadhaar number
4. View status — submitted / under verification / approved / rejected

## Processing Time

| Stage | Duration |
|-------|----------|
| Block Supply Officer / field verification | 10–20 days |
| District Supply Officer approval | 15–30 days |
| Total | 30–45 days |

> In remote tribal/forest areas, verification may take longer due to travel distances. Follow up at the block office if no update after 45 days.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can tribal families in forest areas apply for ration cards?

Yes — tribal (Adivasi) families in Scheduled Areas and forest regions are given priority under AAY/PHH. Even families with some land may qualify if income is below threshold. Caste certificate and residence proof are important.

### How do I check my ration card status in Jharkhand?

Visit [aahar.jharkhand.gov.in](https://aahar.jharkhand.gov.in) and use the "Application Status" option with your acknowledgement number or Aadhaar number.

### My Aadhaar shows Bihar address but I live in Jharkhand. Can I apply?

Yes — apply with current Jharkhand residence proof (electricity bill, panchayat certificate, rent agreement). You should also update your Aadhaar address separately, but it is not mandatory before applying.

### How do I add my spouse to the Jharkhand ration card after marriage?

Visit your nearest Pragya Kendra or AAHAR portal for "Member Addition." You'll need the spouse's Aadhaar, marriage certificate, and the existing ration card number.
