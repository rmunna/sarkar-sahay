# LPG Gas Subsidy Not Received? Check Status & Fix

If your LPG subsidy is not being credited to your bank account, you are not alone. This is a common issue, usually caused by Aadhaar-bank linking problems or DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer) configuration errors. This guide explains how to check your subsidy status and fix the issue step by step.

## How Does LPG Subsidy Work?

Under the **PAHAL (Pratyaksh Hanstantrit Labh)** scheme, LPG cylinders are sold at market price, and the subsidy amount is directly transferred to your Aadhaar-linked bank account after each refill purchase. You are entitled to **12 subsidised cylinders per year** (14.2 kg each).

### Subsidy Flow
1. You book and pay for a cylinder at **market price**
2. After delivery, the subsidy amount is calculated
3. The subsidy is credited to your **Aadhaar-linked bank account** within 2–5 working days

## How to Check LPG Subsidy Status Online

### Method 1: MyLPG Portal
1. Visit [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in/)
2. Select your OMC — Indane, HP Gas, or Bharat Gas
3. Enter your **17-digit LPG Consumer Number**
4. View subsidy transaction history and status

### Method 2: Check via OMC Portals
- **Indane (IOCL):** Visit [iocl.com/indane-for-you](https://www.iocl.com/indane-for-you) → My Account → Subsidy Status
- **HP Gas (HPCL):** Visit [hindustanpetroleum.com/hpgas](https://www.hindustanpetroleum.com/hpgas) → Track Subsidy
- **Bharat Gas (BPCL):** Visit [bharatgas.com](https://www.bharatgas.com/) → My Account → Subsidy Details

### Method 3: Check Bank Statement
- Look for credits from **"PAHAL DBT"** or **"LPG SUBSIDY"** in your bank statement
- The credit usually appears 2–5 working days after cylinder delivery

## Common Reasons Why LPG Subsidy is Not Received

| # | Reason | Fix |
|---|--------|-----|
| 1 | Aadhaar not linked to bank account | Link Aadhaar to bank account |
| 2 | Wrong bank account in LPG records | Update bank account with distributor |
| 3 | Bank account closed or dormant | Reactivate account or update new account |
| 4 | Aadhaar not linked to LPG connection | Link Aadhaar via OMC portal |
| 5 | Exceeded 12 subsidised cylinders/year | No subsidy beyond 12 cylinders per year |
| 6 | KYC not completed | Complete eKYC at distributor or bank |
| 7 | Duplicate LPG connection detected | Surrender duplicate connection |
| 8 | Name mismatch between Aadhaar and bank | Correct name in Aadhaar or bank records |

## How to Fix — Step by Step

### Step 1: Verify Aadhaar-Bank Linking
1. Visit your bank branch or use net banking
2. Check if your [Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) is linked to your bank account
3. If not linked, submit an Aadhaar linking request at the bank
4. You can also check Aadhaar seeding status on [NPCI mapper](https://resident.uidai.gov.in/bank-mapper)

### Step 2: Link Aadhaar to LPG Connection
1. Visit [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in/) and log in
2. Go to **"Update Aadhaar"** section
3. Enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number
4. Verify with OTP sent to your Aadhaar-registered mobile
5. Alternatively, visit your LPG distributor with Aadhaar card and consumer number

### Step 3: Update Bank Account Details
1. Visit your LPG distributor with:
   - Bank account passbook (with Aadhaar-linked account)
   - Aadhaar card
   - LPG consumer card/slip
2. Fill in the bank account update form
3. The distributor will update your records — changes reflect within 7–15 days

### Step 4: Ensure KYC Completion
- Both **bank KYC** and **LPG KYC (eKYC)** must be complete
- Visit the distributor or use the OMC portal to complete eKYC
- Ensure your name, address, and Aadhaar match across all records

## How to File a Complaint for Missing Subsidy

### Option 1: Contact Your Distributor
- Visit or call your LPG distributor with consumer number and bank details
- Ask them to check the subsidy transfer status in their system

### Option 2: OMC Customer Care
| Company | Helpline Number | Email |
|---------|----------------|-------|
| Indane (IOCL) | 1800-233-3555 | indaborb@indianoil.in |
| HP Gas (HPCL) | 1800-233-3555 | hpgas@hpcl.in |
| Bharat Gas (BPCL) | 1800-22-4344 | bharatgas@bharatpetroleum.in |

### Option 3: PGPORTAL (Government Grievance Portal)
1. Visit [pgportal.gov.in](https://pgportal.gov.in/)
2. Register and file a grievance under **Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas**
3. Attach proof — bank statements showing no credit, delivery receipts
4. Track your grievance status online

### Option 4: MyLPG Portal Complaint
1. Visit [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in/)
2. Go to **"Lodge Complaint"**
3. Select issue type — "Subsidy not received"
4. Enter consumer number and details
5. Submit and note the complaint number

## Important Tips

1. **Always keep Aadhaar, bank, and LPG records consistent** — name mismatches are the most common cause of subsidy failure.
2. **Check NPCI Aadhaar-bank mapper** to verify which bank account is receiving your Aadhaar-based payments.
3. **Don't exceed 12 cylinders** per year if you want subsidised rates — the 13th cylinder onwards is at market price with no subsidy.
4. **Keep delivery receipts** — they serve as proof if you need to file a complaint for missing subsidy.
5. **Subsidy takes 2–5 working days** after delivery — don't panic immediately. Check after a week.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. How many subsidised LPG cylinders can I get per year?
You are entitled to **12 subsidised cylinders (14.2 kg)** per year under the PAHAL scheme. Beyond 12, you pay the full market price.

### 2. How do I check if my Aadhaar is linked to my LPG connection?
Visit [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in/) and check your consumer profile. It will show the Aadhaar linking status.

### 3. Can I receive LPG subsidy without Aadhaar?
No. Aadhaar is mandatory for receiving LPG subsidy under the PAHAL (DBT) scheme. You must link Aadhaar to both your bank account and LPG connection.

### 4. My subsidy was coming earlier but stopped suddenly. Why?
Common reasons: bank account became dormant, Aadhaar-bank linking got reset, exceeded 12 cylinders, or KYC expired. Check each of these and fix accordingly.

### 5. How much is the LPG subsidy per cylinder?
The subsidy amount varies every month based on the difference between the market price and the government-set subsidised price. It typically ranges from ₹150 to ₹300 per cylinder.

### 6. I gave up my subsidy voluntarily. Can I get it back?
Yes. Visit your distributor or the OMC portal and opt back into the PAHAL scheme. Subsidy will resume from the next refill.

### 7. Where is my LPG subsidy credited?
The subsidy is credited to the bank account linked to your Aadhaar via the **NPCI Aadhaar Payment Bridge (APB)** system.
