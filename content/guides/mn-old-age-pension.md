# Manipur Old Age Pension – Complete Guide 2026

## What is the Manipur Old Age Pension?

The **Manipur Old Age Pension** provides monthly financial assistance to senior citizens from economically weaker sections under the **IGNOAPS** with state top-up from the Manipur government.

## Pension Amount

| Age Group | Approximate Monthly Pension |
|-----------|---------------------------|
| 60-79 years | ₹400-700 |
| 80+ years | ₹700-1,000 |

> Includes central (₹200/₹500) and state top-up. Verify with District Social Welfare Office.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Age:** 60 years or above
- **Residency:** Permanent resident of Manipur
- **Income:** BPL or below prescribed income limit
- **Not receiving** any other government pension

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar Card
- Age proof (birth certificate / voter ID / school certificate)
- BPL card or income certificate
- Bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked)
- Residential proof
- Passport-size photographs (2)

## How to Apply – Step-by-Step

1. Visit the **Panchayat office** (rural) or **Municipal office** (urban)
2. Collect the **Old Age Pension form**
3. Fill in personal, age, income, and bank details
4. Attach document copies
5. Get verified by the **Village Authority / Ward Member**
6. Submit at the **Block Development Office** or **District Social Welfare Office**
7. After approval, pension is credited via DBT

## Processing Time

- **Verification to first payment:** 2-4 months

## Important Tips

1. **Accurate age proof** is critical — pension depends on age bracket
2. **Link Aadhaar to bank** before applying
3. **Annual life verification** is mandatory
4. **Apply through local Panchayat** for fastest processing
5. **Contact BDO** for payment issues

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: What is the pension for those above 80?
**A:** Higher pension (₹500 central + state top-up) for **80+ seniors**.

### Q2: Can I apply online?
**A:** Check [nsap.nic.in](https://nsap.nic.in). Primary method is offline.

### Q3: Is BPL card mandatory?
**A:** An **income certificate** from the SDM can substitute for BPL card.

### Q4: Can I receive this with CMST?
**A:** Check with the District Social Welfare Office. See our [CMST guide](/guide/mn-cmst).

### Q5: How is the pension paid?
**A:** Through **DBT** to the Aadhaar-linked bank account.
