## What is CM-KISAN (formerly KALIA)?

CM-KISAN is Odisha's state-level farmer support scheme, which replaced the earlier KALIA (Krushak Assistance for Livelihood and Income Augmentation) scheme. It provides financial assistance to farmers in Odisha for cultivation, livelihood support, and crop insurance. The scheme is aligned with PM-KISAN at the central level but offers additional state-level benefits.

## How to Check CM-KISAN Status Online

### Step 1: Visit the Official Portal

Go to: [https://cmkportal.odisha.gov.in](https://cmkportal.odisha.gov.in)

### Step 2: Click on "Beneficiary Status" or "Check Payment"

On the homepage, click on the **"Beneficiary Status"** or **"Payment Status"** link.

### Step 3: Enter Your Details

Provide one of the following:
- **Aadhaar Number** (12 digits)
- **Registered Mobile Number**
- **Farmer ID / KALIA ID** (if you have one from the earlier scheme)

### Step 4: Verify and Submit

Enter the captcha or OTP as prompted, then click **"Submit"** or **"Search"**.

### Step 5: View Your Status

The portal will display:
- **Beneficiary Status** — Active, Pending Verification, or Not Found
- **Payment History** — Instalment-wise payment details with dates and amounts
- **Bank Account** — Linked bank details (masked)
- **Land Records** — Linked land details (if applicable)

## How to Check Beneficiary List (District/Block-wise)

1. Visit [https://cmkportal.odisha.gov.in](https://cmkportal.odisha.gov.in)
2. Look for **"Beneficiary List"** or **"District-wise List"**
3. Select your **District → Block → Gram Panchayat → Village**
4. The list of beneficiaries for your village will be displayed
5. Search for your name in the list

## Alternative Ways to Check Payment

- **Bank Passbook** — Visit your bank and update your passbook to see DBT credits
- **PFMS Portal** — Visit [https://pfms.nic.in](https://pfms.nic.in) → "Know Your Payment" → Enter bank account details
- **PM-KISAN Portal** — If you also receive PM-KISAN, check at [https://pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in) for central payments

## Common Errors and Fixes

### "Aadhaar Not Found in Records"

- **Cause:** Your Aadhaar may not be seeded in the CM-KISAN database, or there's a name mismatch
- **Fix:** Visit your block agriculture office with Aadhaar card and land documents. Ask them to verify and update your records

### "Payment Status Shows Failed"

- **Cause:** Bank account is inactive, Aadhaar-bank linking issue, or incorrect IFSC code
- **Fix:**
  1. Visit your bank to ensure the account is active
  2. Link your Aadhaar to the bank account (NPCI mapping)
  3. Verify bank details at the agriculture office

### "Beneficiary Status: Not Eligible"

- **Cause:** You may not meet the updated eligibility criteria or your land records are not verified
- **Fix:** Check with your Gram Panchayat or block agriculture office. Ensure your land records on Bhulekh Odisha are updated

### "Duplicate Aadhaar Entry"

- **Cause:** Your Aadhaar is linked to multiple applications (e.g., family members applied separately)
- **Fix:** Visit the block office to resolve the duplicate entry. Only one beneficiary per Aadhaar is allowed

### "Website Not Loading"

- **Cause:** Server overload during payment periods
- **Fix:** Try during off-peak hours. Use Google Chrome and clear cache. Try on mobile data if Wi-Fi is slow

## Important Tips

1. **Keep Aadhaar linked to your bank account** — All payments are sent via Aadhaar-based DBT
2. **Update land records** — Ensure your name appears correctly in Bhulekh Odisha land records
3. **Don't miss verification drives** — Block offices periodically verify beneficiaries; attend when called
4. **Save your Farmer ID** — Your KALIA/CM-KISAN ID helps in faster tracking
5. **Check both state and central payments** — You may be eligible for both CM-KISAN (state) and PM-KISAN (central)

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Is KALIA and CM-KISAN the same scheme?

CM-KISAN is the successor to KALIA in Odisha. If you were a KALIA beneficiary, your records have been migrated to CM-KISAN. Check the new portal for updated status.

### Q2: How much money do farmers get under CM-KISAN?

The financial assistance amount varies by category (small farmer, marginal farmer, landless agricultural household). Check the portal for your specific entitlement.

### Q3: Can landless farmers apply for CM-KISAN?

Yes, landless agricultural households are eligible for livelihood support under the scheme. Contact your block agriculture office for details.

### Q4: When are CM-KISAN payments made?

Payments are typically made in instalments during the Kharif and Rabi seasons. Exact dates are announced by the Odisha government.

### Q5: I was getting KALIA but not CM-KISAN. What happened?

Your eligibility may have been reassessed during the transition. Visit your block agriculture office with your Aadhaar and previous KALIA documents to check.

### Q6: Can I get both PM-KISAN and CM-KISAN?

Yes, PM-KISAN is a central government scheme and CM-KISAN is state-level. Eligible farmers can receive benefits from both.

### Q7: How do I update my bank account for CM-KISAN?

Visit your block agriculture office with your new bank passbook and Aadhaar card. They will update the bank details in the system.
