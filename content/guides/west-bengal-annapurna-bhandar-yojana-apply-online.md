# West Bengal Annapurna Bhandar Yojana Apply Online — Step-by-Step Guide 2026

The **Annapurna Bhandar Yojana** (also called Annapurna Yojana) is West Bengal's new flagship welfare scheme launched by CM Mamata Banerjee's government from **June 1, 2026**. It replaces the earlier Lakshmir Bhandar scheme and gives **₹3,000 per month** directly to eligible women's bank accounts — regardless of social category (general, SC, ST, OBC all get the same amount).

> **Quick answer:** Application forms were released on **May 27, 2026**. Apply at your local Duare Sarkar camp, Block/Municipal office, or online at the official portal. Bring Aadhaar, ration card, bank passbook, and a photo.

---

## Key Benefits

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| **Monthly amount** | **₹3,000** (all eligible women — no category-wise difference) |
| **Payment method** | DBT — directly to Aadhaar-linked bank account |
| **Launched** | June 1, 2026 |
| **Replaces** | Lakshmir Bhandar (₹500–₹1,000/month) |
| **Application started** | May 27, 2026 |

---

## Eligibility

| Criteria | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Residence** | Permanent resident of West Bengal |
| **Gender** | Female |
| **Age** | 18–60 years |
| **Income** | Lower income households (BPL priority — exact income ceiling to be confirmed at application point) |
| **Bank account** | Must have an Aadhaar-linked bank account |
| **Ration card** | West Bengal ration card preferred (for residence proof) |

**Who is NOT eligible:**
- Central or state government employees and their spouses
- Income tax payers
- Women already receiving pension from other government schemes (verify at your local office)
- Residents of other states

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Method 1: Duare Sarkar Camp (Most Common)

**Step 1:** Wait for the next Duare Sarkar camp in your area (announced by local Panchayat/Municipality).

**Step 2:** Visit the camp with all original documents and self-attested photocopies.

**Step 3:** Fill the Annapurna Bhandar application form at the camp (forms available at the venue).

**Step 4:** Submit the form with documents. Collect your acknowledgment slip.

**Step 5:** After verification (1–4 weeks), ₹3,000 will start arriving in your bank account monthly.

---

### Method 2: Block/Municipal Office (Offline)

**Step 1:** Go to your Block Development Office (BDO) or Municipal office.

**Step 2:** Ask for the Annapurna Bhandar Yojana application form.

**Step 3:** Fill and submit with self-attested document copies.

**Step 4:** Note your reference number.

---

### Method 3: Online Portal

**Step 1:** Visit the official portal (when available): [annapurnabhandar.wb.gov.in](https://annapurnabhandar.wb.gov.in)

**Step 2:** Register with your Aadhaar number and mobile OTP.

**Step 3:** Fill in personal details, bank account info, income details.

**Step 4:** Upload documents (Aadhaar, ration card, bank passbook, photo).

**Step 5:** Submit and save the application reference number.

## How to Check Application Status

1. Visit the official portal
2. Enter your application reference number or Aadhaar number
3. Verify with OTP
4. Status shows: Pending / Under Verification / Approved / Payment Initiated

## Annapurna Bhandar vs Lakshmir Bhandar — Key Differences

| Feature | Lakshmir Bhandar (old) | Annapurna Bhandar (new 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly amount | ₹500 (General), ₹1,000 (SC/ST) | **₹3,000 (all categories)** |
| Category difference | Yes — SC/ST got more | **No — flat ₹3,000 for all** |
| Started | 2021 | June 2026 |
| Verification | Basic | Stricter (Aadhaar-linked validation) |

Women already enrolled under Lakshmir Bhandar will be migrated automatically — verify with your local office.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**If I was getting Lakshmir Bhandar, do I need to apply again?**
Check with your BDO office. The government announced automatic migration for existing beneficiaries, but a re-verification process is underway — attending a Duare Sarkar camp to confirm your Aadhaar linkage is recommended.

**Can women in Kolkata (urban) also apply?**
Yes — both rural (BDO) and urban (Municipality) women are eligible.

**When will the first payment arrive after applying?**
Typically 3–6 weeks after verification is complete and the application is approved.

**What if my bank account is not Aadhaar-linked?**
Visit your bank branch first to link your Aadhaar to your account. Without Aadhaar-DBT linkage, you cannot receive the ₹3,000.

**Can a widow or unmarried woman apply?**
Yes — marital status is not a criterion. Any eligible woman aged 18–60 can apply.
