# SBI Dormant Account Reactivation 2026 — Complete Guide

An SBI account becomes dormant if there are no customer-initiated transactions for **24 consecutive months**. Salary credits and bank charges don't count — only debits/withdrawals you make count. Here's exactly how to reactivate your SBI dormant account.

---

## SBI Dormant Account — What Changes

| Feature | Active Account | Dormant Account |
|---------|---------------|-----------------|
| ATM withdrawals | ✅ Available | ❌ Blocked |
| NEFT/IMPS transfers | ✅ Available | ❌ Blocked |
| UPI payments | ✅ Available | ❌ Blocked |
| Salary/credit receiving | ✅ Available | ✅ Still receives |
| Net banking login | ✅ Full access | ⚠️ Read-only |
| YONO app | ✅ Full access | ⚠️ Limited |

---

## Step-by-Step: SBI Dormant Reactivation at Branch (Primary Method)

1. Visit **any SBI branch** (home branch is faster; other branches can also process)
2. Carry original documents:
   - **Aadhaar card** (mandatory for KYC)
   - **PAN card** (if not updated)
   - **Passbook** (SBI passbook if available)
3. At the counter: Say **"I want to reactivate my dormant account"**
4. Fill **"Account Reactivation Request"** form (given at counter)
5. Perform a **fresh transaction**: Deposit any amount (even ₹100 cash deposit at counter)
6. Branch updates KYC if needed (Aadhaar + PAN)
7. **Reactivation: Same day** (within 2–4 hours)
8. Confirm reactivation via ATM withdrawal (test transaction)

---

## SBI Dormant Reactivation via YONO (If Net Banking Works)

Some SBI dormant accounts can be reactivated online if YONO access is not fully restricted:

1. Login to **YONO SBI** app
2. Navigate to **"Accounts"** → Your dormant account
3. If reactivation option available: Follow on-screen instructions
4. You may need to:
   - Update Aadhaar OTP KYC online
   - Submit self-declaration
5. If YONO shows limited access: Branch visit is required

---

## SBI Dormant Account — KYC Requirement

SBI requires KYC update at the time of reactivation:
- **Aadhaar linking** (mandatory if not already linked)
- **PAN linking** (for accounts transacting > ₹50,000/year)
- If KYC is already complete and valid: Only a fresh transaction needed

---

## SBI Dormant Account — After 10 Years (DEAF Transfer)

If SBI account is dormant/unclaimed for **10 years**:
- Balance transferred to **DEAF (Depositor Education and Awareness Fund)** maintained by RBI
- Account is NOT closed — it remains at SBI
- To claim back: Visit SBI branch → Fill DEAF claim form → Receive amount (takes 3–5 working days for DEAF-transferred amounts)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**My SBI account has been dormant for 5 years — can it still be reactivated?**
Yes — SBI accounts are reactivable up to 10 years. After 10 years, balance goes to DEAF but is still claimable. Visit branch immediately to reactivate.

**SBI net banking says "Your account is dormant" — what to do?**
Login works but full features are blocked. Visit your SBI home branch with Aadhaar + PAN. Deposit ₹100 at the counter. Reactivation done same day.

**SBI dormant account — is there any reactivation fee?**
No. SBI does not charge a reactivation fee. However, check if there are maintenance charges pending that reduced your balance during dormancy.

**My SBI dormant account shows ₹0 balance — where did my money go?**
SBI may deduct non-maintenance charges during dormancy. Check the last 5 years of statement (request at branch). If your balance was significant and dropped to ₹0 from charges only, dispute the charges at branch.

**Can salary be credited to dormant SBI account?**
Incoming credits (salary, NEFT received) continue even in dormant status. But you cannot withdraw until reactivated. Reactivate immediately to access accumulated salary.
