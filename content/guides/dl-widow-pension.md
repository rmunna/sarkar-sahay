## What is Delhi Widow Pension Scheme?

The **Delhi Widow Pension Scheme** provides a monthly pension of **₹2,500** to widowed women residing in Delhi. Administered by the **Department of Social Welfare, Government of NCT of Delhi**, this scheme supports women who have lost their husbands and need financial assistance.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Marital Status:** Must be a widow (husband deceased)
2. **Age:** Must be **18 years or older**
3. **Residency:** Must have lived in Delhi for at least **5 years**
4. **Income:** Annual family income should not exceed **₹1,00,000**
5. **Re-marriage:** Must not have remarried
6. **Other Pension:** Must not be receiving any other government pension

## Pension Amount

| Category | Monthly Pension |
|----------|----------------|
| Widow aged 18+ | ₹2,500 |

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar Card** (linked to bank account)
- **Death Certificate** of husband
- **Proof of Delhi Residence** for 5+ years (Voter ID / Ration Card)
- **Income Certificate** (annual income ≤ ₹1,00,000)
- **Bank Passbook** (Aadhaar-linked account)
- **Passport-size photographs** (2 copies)
- **Voter ID Card**

## How to Apply

### Online (e-District Delhi)

1. **Visit** [edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in](https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in)
2. **Register** / Login
3. **Select** "Widow Pension" under Social Welfare
4. **Fill** the application form: personal details, husband's death details, income, bank account
5. **Upload** documents (death certificate, Aadhaar, residence proof)
6. **Submit** and save application ID
7. **Track** status online

### Offline

1. **Visit** the **District Social Welfare Office** or **SDM Office**
2. **Collect** the application form
3. **Fill** and attach all documents
4. **Submit** and get acknowledgement receipt

## Processing Time

- **Verification:** 30-45 days (field verification by inspector)
- **Approval:** After verification
- **First Payment:** 1-2 months after approval
- **Ongoing:** Monthly via DBT

## Important Tips

1. **5-Year Residence:** Prove Delhi residence of at least 5 years
2. **Death Certificate:** Get it from the MCD if not already obtained — see our [Death Certificate guide](/guide/death-certificate-apply-online)
3. **One Pension Only:** Cannot combine with old age or disability pension
4. **Aadhaar-Bank Link:** Mandatory for DBT payments
5. **Life Certificate:** Submit Jeevan Pramaan annually

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. What is the pension amount?
**₹2,500 per month** for eligible widowed women.

### Q2. What is the income limit?
Annual family income should not exceed **₹1,00,000**.

### Q3. Is 5-year Delhi residency mandatory?
Yes, you must have lived in Delhi for at least 5 years.

### Q4. Can I apply online?
Yes, at [edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in](https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in).

### Q5. Will pension stop if I remarry?
Yes, the widow pension is discontinued upon remarriage.

### Q6. Can I get both Widow Pension and Old Age Pension?
No, only one pension can be availed at a time.

### Q7. What if I don't have my husband's death certificate?
Apply for the death certificate first through MCD. It is mandatory for widow pension.
