# Abua Awas Yojana Jharkhand: Free Pucca House for Rural and Urban Poor

Abua Awas Yojana (AAY) is a housing scheme launched by the Jharkhand government to provide **pucca (permanent) houses** to families who were left out of the Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY). Under this scheme, eligible families receive financial assistance of approximately **₹2 lakh** for constructing a three-room pucca house. "Abua" means "our own" in local dialect, signifying ownership and dignity.

## What is Abua Awas Yojana?

Launched in 2023, this is a state-funded housing scheme targeting families in Jharkhand who are houseless or living in kutcha houses but were not covered under PMAY-G or PMAY-U. The scheme aims to construct **lakhs of pucca houses** across both rural and urban areas of Jharkhand.

### Key Features

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Financial Assistance | Approximately ₹2,00,000 |
| House Type | 3-room pucca house |
| Toilet | Included (with Swachh Bharat Mission) |
| Target | Families left out of PMAY |
| Coverage | Both rural and urban areas |

## Who is Eligible?

- Must be a **permanent resident of Jharkhand**
- Must be **houseless** or living in a **kutcha/dilapidated house**
- Must not have received benefit under **PMAY-G or PMAY-U**
- Must not own a **pucca house** anywhere
- Family should be from **economically weaker sections**
- Must be listed in the **SECC 2011** data or identified through fresh surveys
- Priority given to **SC/ST, women-headed households, disabled persons, and minorities**

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** of the applicant and family members
- **Residential certificate** of Jharkhand
- **Ration card** / BPL card
- **Bank account passbook** linked with Aadhaar
- **Passport-size photograph**
- **Land ownership document** (if available)
- **Income certificate** (if required)
- **SECC inclusion proof** or survey data

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### How Beneficiaries Are Selected

1. **Survey and Identification:** The government conducts surveys to identify eligible families not covered under PMAY
2. **Gram Sabha / Ward Committee Verification:** The list is verified at the local level
3. **District-Level Approval:** The Deputy Commissioner approves the final beneficiary list
4. **Sanction Order:** Selected beneficiaries receive a sanction order

### Online Application (if portal is active)

1. Visit **[aay.jharkhand.gov.in](https://aay.jharkhand.gov.in/)**
2. Click on **"Apply Online"** or **"New Registration"**
3. Enter **Aadhaar number** and verify via OTP
4. Fill in personal details and address
5. Upload required documents
6. Submit the application
7. Note the **Application Reference Number**

### Offline Application

1. Visit the **Block Development Office (BDO)** for rural areas or **Municipal Office** for urban areas
2. Obtain the **Abua Awas Yojana application form**
3. Fill in details and attach required documents
4. Submit to the concerned officer
5. Collect the acknowledgement receipt

### After Approval

1. Beneficiary receives **first instalment** for foundation work
2. **Geotagged photographs** are taken at each construction stage
3. Subsequent instalments released after verification of progress
4. Final payment after house completion and inspection

### Check Beneficiary List

1. Visit [aay.jharkhand.gov.in](https://aay.jharkhand.gov.in/)
2. Search by district, block, and panchayat/ward
3. Check if your name appears in the beneficiary list

## Fees

There are **no fees** for applying. The scheme is entirely government-funded.

## Processing Time

- Survey and list preparation: Varies by area
- Approval after list finalisation: **1-2 months**
- House construction deadline: **12 months** from sanction
- Instalment disbursement: **1-2 months** per stage

## Important Tips

1. **Ensure Aadhaar-bank linking** — all payments are via DBT
2. **Attend Gram Sabha meetings** — beneficiary lists are verified here
3. **Complete construction on time** — delays may lead to instalment cancellation
4. **Don't pay brokers or middlemen** — selection is based on government data, not payments
5. **Report grievances** to the BDO or Deputy Commissioner if you face issues

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Can I apply if I already have a PMAY house?
No, families who have already received a house under PMAY-G or PMAY-U are **not eligible** for Abua Awas Yojana.

### Q2: Is the house given to men or women?
The house is preferably registered in the **name of the woman** of the household, similar to PMAY.

### Q3: What if I don't own land?
The government may provide a **homestead plot** to landless families under the scheme or through convergence with other land allocation schemes.

### Q4: How much total assistance is provided?
Approximately **₹2 lakh** for house construction, plus toilet assistance under Swachh Bharat Mission, and MGNREGA wages for labour component.

### Q5: Can urban families apply?
Yes, Abua Awas Yojana covers **both rural and urban** areas of Jharkhand, unlike PMAY which has separate rural and urban components.

### Q6: How do I check if my name is in the list?
Visit the official portal [aay.jharkhand.gov.in](https://aay.jharkhand.gov.in/) or check at your Block/Municipal office.

### Q7: Can I construct the house myself or do I need a contractor?
Beneficiaries can **construct the house themselves** or hire local labour. There is no mandatory requirement to use a contractor.
