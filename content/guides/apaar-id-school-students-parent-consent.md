# APAAR ID for School Students - Parent Consent, PEN ID and DigiLocker

**Quick answer:** School students usually do not create APAAR ID alone. The official APAAR flow says parents are informed by the school, consent is submitted voluntarily, the school verifies student details, UDISE generates APAAR ID against the student's PEN ID, and the APAAR ID is pushed to the student's DigiLocker account.

> **Verified on 7 June 2026:** The official APAAR portal lists four school-flow stages: educating parents, consent submission, APAAR ID generated against PEN ID in UDISE, and upload to DigiLocker.

## School Student APAAR Flow

| Stage | What Happens | Who Handles It |
|---|---|---|
| Parent awareness | School explains APAAR ID | School |
| Consent | Parent/guardian submits voluntary consent | Parent/guardian |
| Verification | Student details are checked | School |
| Generation | APAAR generated against PEN ID | UDISE/school system |
| DigiLocker | APAAR card pushed to DigiLocker | System/DigiLocker |

## What Parents Should Check Before Consent

Before signing or submitting consent, check:

- Student name in school records
- Name as per Aadhaar
- Date of birth
- Gender
- Parent names
- Mobile number
- Class/section/admission details
- PEN ID status if the school provides it

The official APAAR FAQ says the student name in UDISE+ records should match the student name as per Aadhaar, and PEN is mandatory for APAAR generation.

## What Is PEN ID?

PEN is the student identifier used in school education records. For school APAAR generation, the official APAAR FAQ says PEN is mandatory. Students or parents usually need to ask the school to check PEN/UDISE+ details; it is not something every student can self-correct from a public portal.

## Documents Usually Needed

The official APAAR FAQ lists the student information required for creation. Based on that, keep these details ready:

| Detail | Why Needed |
|---|---|
| Student name | Must match school and Aadhaar records |
| Aadhaar number/name as per Aadhaar | Identity matching |
| Date of birth | Demographic matching |
| Gender | Demographic matching |
| Mobile number | Contact/account flow |
| Mother and father name | School record matching |
| PEN / UDISE+ identifier | APAAR generation in school system |

Do not share Aadhaar or OTP with unofficial agents or websites.

## How to Check APAAR Status for a School Student

The official APAAR FAQ says:

- Once generated, APAAR is pushed to the student's DigiLocker account.
- Status can also be checked in the UDISE+ portal under the APAAR module.
- Students may request the school authority to check the status.

Practical steps:

1. Ask the class teacher/school office whether consent and verification are complete.
2. Ask if the student's PEN ID is available and correctly mapped.
3. Ask the school to check the UDISE+ APAAR module.
4. Log in to the student's DigiLocker account and check **Issued Documents**.
5. If not visible, wait for sync or ask the school whether generation succeeded.

## Common Problems and Fixes

| Problem | Likely Cause | What to Do |
|---|---|---|
| APAAR not generated | Consent not submitted or verification pending | Ask school to check APAAR module |
| Name mismatch | School name differs from Aadhaar | Correct school record or Aadhaar as required |
| PEN not available | Student record not updated in UDISE+ | Ask school to update/check PEN |
| Not showing in DigiLocker | Wrong DigiLocker account or sync delay | Check correct account; ask school for status |
| Parent consent not accepted | Form/details incomplete | Submit corrected consent through school |

## What Schools Should Not Ask

Schools may need verified student details for APAAR, but parents should be careful about privacy.

- Do not share OTP with anyone.
- Do not pay unofficial charges for APAAR creation.
- Do not submit documents on random third-party sites.
- Use official school communication and official portals only.

## Parent Checklist Before Leaving School

Before you leave the school office, note these details:

- Whether consent was accepted
- Whether the student's name matches Aadhaar
- Whether PEN ID is available
- Whether the school has submitted the APAAR request
- Whom to contact if DigiLocker does not show the card
- Whether the school expects any correction from the parent side

This matters because parents often check DigiLocker too early. If the school has not completed verification or if the PEN/student record is pending, the card will not appear in DigiLocker yet.

## FAQs

### Can parents create APAAR ID directly for a school student?
For school students, the official flow is school-led through consent, verification and UDISE/PEN mapping. Parents should coordinate with the school.

### Is parent consent compulsory?
The APAAR portal says parents submit consent voluntarily for APAAR ID creation in the school flow.

### Where will the school student's APAAR ID appear?
The official APAAR portal says APAAR ID is pushed into the student's DigiLocker account after generation.

### What if the school says PEN ID is missing?
Ask the school to check or update the student's UDISE+ record. The official FAQ says PEN is mandatory for APAAR generation.

### What if Aadhaar name and school name are different?
APAAR generation may fail or be delayed. Correct the record that is wrong before retrying.

### Should parents pay for APAAR ID?
The official APAAR portal does not list a student fee for APAAR generation. Avoid unofficial agents or paid websites that claim faster generation.
