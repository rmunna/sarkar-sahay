# Forex Card Buy Online: Best Cards & How to Use

Travelling abroad? A prepaid forex card is one of the smartest ways to carry foreign currency. It offers better exchange rates than cash, works like a debit card internationally, and is safer than carrying large amounts of foreign currency. This guide covers everything about buying and using forex cards in India.

## What is a Forex Card?

A forex card (also called a prepaid travel card or multicurrency card) is a prepaid card loaded with foreign currency. You can use it for:

- **Shopping** at international POS terminals
- **Online purchases** in foreign currency
- **ATM withdrawals** abroad
- **Contactless payments** where supported

Forex cards are issued by banks and authorised dealers regulated by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) under the Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS).

## RBI Limits on Forex

Under the **Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS)**, RBI allows resident individuals to remit up to **USD 250,000 per financial year** (April–March) for permitted transactions including:

- Travel abroad (business or personal)
- Education
- Medical treatment
- Gifts and donations
- Investment abroad

**Important:** This USD 250,000 limit covers ALL forex transactions — cash, forex card, wire transfer, and international debit card spends combined.

### Tax Collected at Source (TCS)

As per the Income Tax Act:
- **5% TCS** on forex remittance above ₹7 lakh per financial year (general)
- **20% TCS** on overseas tour packages (from ₹0, no threshold)
- **0.5% TCS** if remittance is for education funded by a loan
- TCS is adjustable against your income tax liability

## How to Buy a Forex Card Online

### Step 1: Choose Your Card

Compare exchange rates, supported currencies, loading fees, and ATM withdrawal charges across banks.

### Step 2: Apply Online

1. Visit your bank's website or the forex provider's portal
2. Select "Forex Card" or "Travel Card"
3. Enter your travel details — destination country, travel dates
4. Select the currency and amount to load
5. Upload documents (see below)

### Step 3: Submit KYC Documents

- **Passport** (mandatory for forex card)
- **PAN card**
- **Valid visa** (for the destination country)
- **Air ticket** or travel itinerary
- **Passport-size photograph**
- **Form A2** (declaration for forex purchase — the bank usually generates this)

### Step 4: Pay and Load

- Pay via net banking, UPI, or bank transfer
- The forex card is loaded at the exchange rate locked at the time of payment
- Card is delivered to your address or available for pickup at the branch

### Step 5: Activate the Card

- Set your ATM PIN via the bank's app or by calling customer care
- Register on the bank's forex card portal for balance tracking

## ATM Withdrawal Abroad

1. Insert your forex card at any Visa/Mastercard-enabled ATM
2. Select "Withdrawal" → Choose "Savings" or "Credit" (depends on ATM)
3. Enter the amount in **local currency**
4. **Decline** the ATM's conversion offer (DCC — Dynamic Currency Conversion) — always choose to be charged in the local currency
5. Collect your cash and receipt

**Daily ATM withdrawal limit:** Typically USD 1,000–USD 5,000 equivalent per day (varies by bank and card).

## Using Your Forex Card Online

- Use it for hotel bookings, Uber, online shopping abroad

- Enable international online transactions via the bank's app before use
- Some banks support setting transaction limits for added security

## Important Tips

1. **Load the primary currency** of your destination country to avoid cross-currency charges
2. **Keep a backup** — carry a second forex card or small amount of cash
3. **Inform your bank** about your travel dates to avoid card blocking
4. **Check balance regularly** via the bank's app or SMS alerts
5. **Encash unused balance** within 180 days of return (as per FEMA regulations)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Can I buy a forex card without a visa?
Some banks issue forex cards without a visa for certain countries (visa-on-arrival destinations). However, most banks require a valid visa or travel proof.

### Q2. What happens if my forex card is lost abroad?
Immediately call the bank's 24/7 helpline to block the card. Most banks provide an emergency replacement card or emergency cash within 24–48 hours.

### Q3. Can I use a forex card in India?
Forex cards are meant for international use. You cannot use them for domestic transactions within India. You must encash the remaining balance after returning.

### Q4. Is there a limit on how much forex I can load?
Yes, under the RBI's LRS, you can load up to USD 250,000 per financial year across all forex transactions combined.

### Q5. How do I check my forex card balance?
Use your bank's mobile app, internet banking portal, SMS service, or call customer care.

### Q6. Can I load multiple currencies on one card?
Yes, most multicurrency forex cards support 5–22 currencies on a single card. You can load different currencies and switch between them.

### Q7. What is cross-currency markup?
If you spend in a currency not loaded on your card, the bank converts from your loaded currency at a markup of 2%–3.5%. Always load the currency of your destination.
