## What is Chirayu Haryana Yojana?

**Chirayu Haryana Yojana** (also known as **Ayushman Bharat - Chirayu Haryana**) is a health insurance scheme by the **Government of Haryana** that provides cashless treatment of up to **₹5 lakh per family per year** at empanelled hospitals. It is the state extension of the central **Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY** scheme.

The scheme covers families identified through the **Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP)** database. It aims to provide quality healthcare to economically weaker families, covering hospitalization, surgeries, diagnostics, and medicines.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Residency:** Must be a resident of Haryana with a verified Parivar Pehchan Patra
2. **Income:** Families with annual income up to **₹1,80,000** (or as notified) are eligible
3. **PPP Verification:** Family must be verified in the Parivar Pehchan Patra system
4. **No Other Insurance:** Should not be covered under CGHS, ECHS, or similar central government health schemes
5. **SECC/Deprivation Criteria:** Families identified under SECC data or state deprivation criteria

## Coverage & Benefits

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Sum Insured | ₹5,00,000 per family per year |
| Hospitalization | Cashless at empanelled hospitals |
| Pre-existing Diseases | Covered from day one |
| Surgeries | 1,500+ procedures covered |
| Pre & Post Hospitalization | 3 days pre and 15 days post covered |
| Transport Allowance | ₹250 per hospitalization |

## Documents Required

- **Parivar Pehchan Patra** (Haryana Family ID) — mandatory
- **Aadhaar Card** of family members
- **Ration Card** or income proof
- **Any government-issued ID** for identity verification at hospital

> **Note:** If your family is already in the PPP database and meets income criteria, you may be auto-enrolled. No separate application may be needed.

## How to Get Your Chirayu Card

### Check Eligibility & Download Card

1. **Visit** [chirayuharyana.gov.in](https://chirayuharyana.gov.in)
2. **Enter** your Parivar Pehchan Patra (Family ID) number
3. **Check** if your family is eligible
4. **If eligible**, download or generate your Chirayu e-card
5. **Save** or print the card for use at hospitals

### At Empanelled Hospital

1. **Visit** any Chirayu-empanelled hospital (government or private)
2. **Show** your Aadhaar Card and Parivar Pehchan Patra
3. **Verification** is done at the hospital's Ayushman desk
4. **Get cashless treatment** — no out-of-pocket payment for covered procedures

### If Not Auto-Enrolled

1. **Visit** the nearest **Common Service Centre (CSC)** or **Atal Seva Kendra**
2. **Carry** Parivar Pehchan Patra, Aadhaar, and income proof
3. **Request** enrollment under Chirayu Haryana
4. **Biometric verification** will be done
5. **Card will be generated** after verification

## Empanelled Hospitals

- All **government hospitals** in Haryana are empanelled
- Many **private hospitals** are also empanelled
- Check the full list at [chirayuharyana.gov.in](https://chirayuharyana.gov.in)

## Processing Time

- **Eligibility Check:** Instant (if already in PPP database)
- **Card Generation:** Same day to 7 days
- **Hospital Admission:** Cashless treatment starts immediately upon verification

## Important Tips

1. **Always Carry Aadhaar:** Hospitals verify identity through Aadhaar biometric authentication
2. **Check Hospital List:** Before going, confirm that the hospital is empanelled under Chirayu
3. **No Cash Payment:** Do not pay anything for covered treatments — it's fully cashless
4. **Helpline:** Call **14555** (Ayushman Bharat helpline) or the Haryana helpline for queries
5. **Pre-existing Conditions:** All pre-existing diseases are covered — no waiting period

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. What is the coverage under Chirayu Haryana?
**₹5 lakh per family per year** for hospitalization, surgeries, and medical procedures.

### Q2. Is Parivar Pehchan Patra mandatory?
Yes, your family must be registered and verified in the PPP system.

### Q3. Do I need to pay any premium?
No, the scheme is completely **free** for eligible families. The state government pays the premium.

### Q4. Can I use it at private hospitals?
Yes, at any **empanelled private hospital**. Check the list on the official portal.

### Q5. What if the hospital refuses cashless treatment?
Call the Ayushman Bharat helpline **14555** or the hospital's Ayushman Mitra for assistance.

### Q6. Are pre-existing diseases covered?
Yes, all pre-existing conditions are covered from day one under Chirayu Haryana.

### Q7. How is this different from Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY?
Chirayu Haryana extends PM-JAY coverage to additional families in Haryana based on state income criteria and PPP data.
