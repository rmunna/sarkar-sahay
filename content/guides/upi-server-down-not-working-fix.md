# UPI Server Down Today? How to Fix "Service Temporarily Unavailable"

When UPI shows "Service Temporarily Unavailable" or your payments keep failing, it could be your bank's server, the NPCI system, or the UPI app itself. This guide helps you identify the exact issue and find alternatives so you're not stuck without a payment option.

## Why Is UPI Not Working?

UPI transactions involve three systems — your UPI app, NPCI's central server, and your bank's server. A failure in any one causes the "Service Temporarily Unavailable" error.

### Common Reasons:

1. **Bank server maintenance** — Banks perform scheduled maintenance, usually between 11:30 PM and 12:30 AM
2. **NPCI system overload** — During peak hours or festive sales, NPCI servers face heavy load
3. **UPI app issues** — App-specific bugs or updates can cause temporary failures
4. **Your internet connection** — Weak or unstable network causes timeout errors
5. **Bank-specific outages** — Individual banks may face unscheduled downtime

## How to Check If UPI Is Down

### Method 1: Check DownDetector

Visit [downdetector.in](https://downdetector.in/) and search for:
- Your UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm)
- Your bank name
- "UPI" in general

If many users report issues at the same time, it's a server-side problem — not your device.

### Method 2: Try a Small Test Transaction

Send ₹1 to a trusted contact. If it fails with a timeout or "service unavailable" error, the server is down. If it asks for PIN and then fails, the issue is bank-specific.

### Method 3: Check Your Bank's Social Media

Banks often post maintenance updates on their official Twitter/X handles:
- @TheOfficialSBI
- @HDFC_Bank
- @ICICIBank
- @aboraborabo (Axis Bank)
- @paboraborabo (PNB)

### Method 4: Check NPCI Status

NPCI doesn't have a real-time public status page, but you can check their ecosystem statistics at [npci.org.in](https://www.npci.org.in/what-we-do/upi/upi-ecosystem-statistics) for any reported disruptions.

## Scheduled Maintenance Windows

Most banks have a regular maintenance window. During these times, UPI transactions may fail:

| Time Period | What Happens |
|------------|--------------|
| 11:30 PM – 12:30 AM daily | Bank system settlement and reconciliation |
| 1st of month (morning) | Salary credit processing — high load |
| Last day of month | Month-end settlements — high load |
| Saturday 9 PM – Sunday 6 AM | Extended maintenance (some banks) |
| Festive seasons (Diwali, etc.) | NPCI peak load — intermittent failures |

## Step-by-Step Fix

### Step 1: Check Your Internet

- Switch between WiFi and mobile data
- Turn airplane mode on and off
- Test by opening any website in your browser

### Step 2: Wait 15-30 Minutes

If it's a server issue, most outages resolve within 15-30 minutes. Don't keep retrying — excessive attempts during outages can flag your account.

### Step 3: Try a Different UPI App

If Google Pay isn't working, try PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM. All UPI apps connect through NPCI but may route differently. If ALL apps fail, the issue is either NPCI-wide or bank-specific.

### Step 4: Try a Different Bank Account

If you have accounts in multiple banks linked to UPI, switch to a different bank's account. If one bank's server is down, another may work fine.

### Step 5: Clear App Cache

**Android:**
1. Go to Settings → Apps → [UPI App Name]
2. Tap Storage → Clear Cache (NOT Clear Data)
3. Reopen the app and try again

**iPhone:**
1. Delete and reinstall the app
2. Log in again with your mobile number
3. Your bank accounts will auto-link

### Step 6: Update the App

Go to Google Play Store or Apple App Store → Search for your UPI app → Tap Update if available. Outdated apps may have compatibility issues with latest NPCI protocols.

## Alternatives When UPI Is Down

Don't get stuck. Use these payment options:

| Alternative | How to Use |
|------------|-----------|
| **NEFT/RTGS** | Via net banking — works on different infrastructure |
| **IMPS** | Via mobile banking app — may work even if UPI is down |
| **Debit/Credit Card** | Swipe or tap at POS terminals |
| **Net Banking** | Direct bank transfer via browser |
| **Cash** | ATM withdrawal — old but reliable |
| **UPI Lite** | Pre-loaded wallet — works offline for small payments up to ₹500 |

**Pro tip:** Load your UPI Lite wallet when UPI is working, so you have a backup for small payments during outages.

## Important Tips

1. **Don't panic-retry** — Repeated failed attempts during outages can trigger account blocks
2. **Avoid peak hours** for large payments — early morning (6-8 AM) usually has lowest traffic
3. **Keep alternative payment methods** ready — at least one debit card and net banking access
4. **Enable UPI Lite** for small payments — it works even during server outages
5. **Follow your bank on social media** for real-time maintenance updates

## FAQs

### How long does UPI server downtime usually last?

Scheduled maintenance lasts 30-60 minutes (typically 11:30 PM to 12:30 AM). Unscheduled outages usually resolve within 1-4 hours. Major outages affecting all banks are rare and NPCI resolves them on priority.

### Is UPI down for everyone or just me?

Check DownDetector or ask a friend to try a transaction. If multiple people face the same issue with the same bank, it's a server problem. If only you're affected, check your internet, app, and bank account.

### Can I get a refund for a failed transaction during server downtime?

If money was debited during a server outage, it will be auto-reversed within 24-48 hours. See our [UPI money debited not credited guide](/guide/upi-money-debited-not-credited-fix) for detailed refund steps.

### Why does UPI fail every night around midnight?

Banks perform daily settlement and reconciliation between 11:30 PM and 12:30 AM. During this window, many transactions fail. This is normal and expected.

### Does UPI Lite work when UPI is down?

Yes, partially. UPI Lite transactions (up to ₹500) can work even during bank server outages because the money is deducted from your pre-loaded UPI Lite wallet, not directly from your bank account. However, you need UPI to be working to load the wallet.

### Why does Google Pay show "Something went wrong" but PhonePe works?

Different UPI apps may have different server-side issues. They all connect through NPCI but their own app servers can face problems independently. If one app fails, always try another.
