## What is UP Bhulekh?

UP Bhulekh is the official online land records portal of the Uttar Pradesh government, maintained by the Board of Revenue. It allows citizens to check land ownership details, khasra (plot) numbers, khatauni (ownership record), and other revenue records from anywhere without visiting the tehsil office.

Before this portal, checking land records required visiting the Lekhpal or tehsil office in person — a time-consuming and often frustrating process. UP Bhulekh has digitised land records for all 75 districts of Uttar Pradesh.

## Key Features of UP Bhulekh Portal

- **Khasra/Khatauni search** — View plot-level ownership details
- **Gata number search** — Find records using survey/plot number
- **Khata number search** — Look up all plots under one owner
- **Owner name search** — Search records by land owner's name
- **Revenue village maps** — View village-level land maps
- **Real-time data** — Records updated by revenue department regularly

## How to Check UP Land Records Online (Step-by-Step)

### Step 1: Visit UP Bhulekh Portal

Go to [upbhulekh.gov.in](https://upbhulekh.gov.in) and click on **"खतौनी (अधिकार अभिलेख) की नक़ल देखें"** (View Khatauni Copy).

### Step 2: Enter Captcha

Enter the captcha code displayed on the screen and click **Submit**.

### Step 3: Select District, Tehsil & Village

1. Select your **जनपद (District)** from the dropdown
2. Select your **तहसील (Tehsil)**
3. Select your **ग्राम (Village)** — you can search by village name or village code

### Step 4: Choose Search Method

You can search land records by:
- **खसरा/गाटा संख्या** (Khasra/Gata Number) — Enter the plot number
- **खाता संख्या** (Khata Number) — Enter the ownership account number
- **खातेदार के नाम से** (By Owner Name) — Enter landowner's name in Hindi

### Step 5: View & Download Khatauni

After entering details, click **"उद्धरण देखें"** (View Extract). Your khatauni record will display with:
- Owner name(s)
- Father's/husband's name
- Plot area (in hectare/bigha)
- Land type (agricultural, residential, etc.)
- Tenure details and remarks

You can **print** or **save as PDF** directly from the browser.

## How to Check Khasra Details

1. Visit [upbhulekh.gov.in](https://upbhulekh.gov.in)
2. Click on **"भूखण्ड/गाटे का यूनीक कोड जानें"** (Know Unique Code of Plot)
3. Select district → tehsil → village
4. Enter the gata number
5. View plot details including unique code, area, and land use

## How to Search by Owner Name

1. On the UP Bhulekh homepage, select **"खतौनी (अधिकार अभिलेख) की नक़ल देखें"**
2. Select district, tehsil, and village
3. Choose **"खातेदार के नाम से"** search option
4. Type the owner's name in **Hindi** (use the on-screen Hindi keyboard if needed)
5. Select the matching name from the suggestions
6. View the complete khatauni record

## Revenue Court Case Status

To check revenue court case status related to land disputes:

1. Visit [vaad.up.nic.in](https://vaad.up.nic.in) — UP Revenue Court Computerised Management System
2. Select the type of search (by case number, party name, or plot number)
3. Enter the required details (district, tehsil, case year)
4. View case status, hearing dates, and orders

## Important Tips

1. **Use Hindi for name search** — Owner names are stored in Hindi in UP land records. Use the Hindi keyboard provided on the portal for accurate results.
2. **Note your gata number** — Keep your khasra/gata number handy before searching. It's the fastest way to find your record.
3. **Cross-verify at tehsil** — Online records are for reference. For legal purposes (sale, loan, mutation), get a certified copy from your tehsil office.
4. **Check regularly** — Verify your land records periodically to ensure no unauthorised changes or mutations have been made.
5. **Use the unique code** — Each plot has a unique code that helps in verification during property transactions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is UP Bhulekh?
UP Bhulekh is the official online portal of Uttar Pradesh government to check land records including khasra, khatauni, and revenue village maps. The website is [upbhulekh.gov.in](https://upbhulekh.gov.in).

### Is UP Bhulekh free to use?
Yes, viewing land records online on UP Bhulekh is completely free. You only pay fees if you need a certified physical copy from the tehsil office.

### Can I download khatauni from UP Bhulekh?
You can view and print the khatauni online. However, for legal purposes, you need a certified copy from the tehsil office, as the online version is for informational purposes only.

### How do I find my khasra number?
You can search by owner name on UP Bhulekh to find your khasra/gata number. Alternatively, check your previous land documents or ask your village Lekhpal.

### Is the online khatauni valid for property registration?
The online khatauni is for reference only. For property registration, sale deeds, or bank loans, you need a certified copy issued by the tehsil/revenue office.

### What if my name is incorrect in the land record?
If there is an error in your name or other details, you need to apply for correction at your tehsil office. Submit an application with supporting documents (Aadhaar, previous records) to the concerned Lekhpal or tehsildar.

### Can I check land records for any district in UP?
Yes, UP Bhulekh covers all 75 districts of Uttar Pradesh. You can check records for any village in any district.
