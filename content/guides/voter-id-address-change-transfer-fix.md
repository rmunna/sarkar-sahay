# How to Transfer Voter ID to New Address — Form 8A Online (2025)

Moved to a new city, state, or even a different area within your city? You need to **transfer your Voter ID (EPIC)** to your new address so you can vote from your current location. This guide covers the complete process — which form to use, how to apply online on the NVSP portal, documents needed, and how to fix common problems.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent informational platform and is not affiliated with the Election Commission of India or any government body. Always verify details on [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in).

---

## When Do You Need to Transfer Your Voter ID?

You must transfer your voter registration if:

- You have **moved to a new city or state** and want to vote from your current address
- You have **shifted within the same city** but to a different Assembly Constituency
- You have **moved to a different area** within the same constituency and want your address updated
- Your **current address on the Voter ID is outdated** and doesn't match where you live

**Important:** If you don't transfer, you will have to travel back to your old constituency to vote. Your name will not appear in the voter list at your new address.

---

## Form 8A vs Form 6 — Which One Do You Need?

| Scenario | Form to Use | What It Does |
|---|---|---|
| Shifted **within the same Assembly Constituency** | **Form 8A** | Updates your address on existing voter registration |
| Shifted to a **different Assembly Constituency** (new city/state/district) | **Form 6** | New voter registration at the new constituency (old entry gets deleted) |

### How to check your Assembly Constituency:
1. Go to [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in)
2. Use the **"Know Your Electoral Details"** feature
3. Enter your EPIC number or search by name
4. Note the Assembly Constituency — compare it with your new area

**Rule of thumb:** If you moved to a different pin code area or different district, you likely need **Form 6**. If you moved within the same locality/area, use **Form 8A**.

## Documents Required

### Address Proof (any one — must show new address)
- Aadhaar card with updated address
- Electricity or water bill (recent, within 3 months)
- Gas connection bill
- Rent agreement (registered or notarized)
- Bank passbook with new address
- Passport with new address
- Property tax receipt

### Identity Proof (any one)
- Aadhaar card
- Passport
- PAN card
- Driving licence
- Bank passbook with photo

### Additional Requirements
- Recent passport-size photograph
- Existing EPIC (Voter ID) number
- Mobile number linked to your voter registration (for OTP)

## Step-by-Step: Transfer Voter ID Online via NVSP Portal

### For Form 8A (Within Same Constituency — Address Change Only)

**Step 1:** Visit [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in) and log in or register with your mobile number.

**Step 2:** After login, click on **"Correction in Entries"** or navigate to **Forms → Form 8A**.

**Step 3:** Enter your **EPIC number** to fetch your existing voter details.

**Step 4:** In the address section, update your **new address details** — house number, street, locality, pin code.

**Step 5:** Upload scanned copies of:
- Address proof (showing new address)
- Passport-size photo

**Step 6:** Verify all details and **submit the form**. You will receive a **Reference ID** — save this.

**Step 7:** You'll get an SMS/email confirmation. Track your application status using the Reference ID.

### For Form 6 (New Constituency — Fresh Registration)

**Step 1:** Visit [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in) and log in.

**Step 2:** Click on **"New Voter Registration"** or navigate to **Forms → Form 6**.

**Step 3:** Fill in your **personal details** — name, date of birth, gender, family member details.

**Step 4:** Enter your **new address** completely — including district, state, Assembly Constituency, and pin code.

**Step 5:** In the **"Have you been registered as a voter earlier?"** section, select **Yes** and provide:
- Your previous EPIC number
- Previous constituency details
- Previous address

This ensures your old registration is cancelled automatically.

**Step 6:** Upload:
- Address proof (new address)
- Identity proof
- Passport-size photo
- Age proof (if applicable)

**Step 7:** Review all details and **submit**. Save your **Reference ID**.

## Using the Voter Helpline App (Alternative Method)

You can also apply through the **Voter Helpline App** (available on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.eci.citizen) and App Store):

1. Download and register with your mobile number
2. Go to **Forms** section
3. Select **Form 8A** (same constituency) or **Form 6** (new constituency)
4. Fill in details, upload documents, and submit
5. Track status within the app

## BLO Verification Process

After you submit your application:

1. **Booth Level Officer (BLO)** is assigned to verify your application
2. The BLO will **visit your new address** for physical verification
3. They may ask to see **original documents** (address proof, ID proof)
4. They verify that you actually reside at the new address
5. The BLO submits a **verification report** to the Electoral Registration Officer (ERO)
6. The ERO approves or rejects the application

### Tips for BLO Visit
- Keep original documents ready at home
- Ensure someone is available at the address during working hours
- If BLO doesn't visit within 15 days, call the **voter helpline at 1950**
- You can check BLO details on the [Voter Portal](https://voterportal.eci.gov.in)

---

## Processing Time

| Stage | Timeline |
|---|---|
| Application submission to BLO assignment | 3–7 days |
| BLO verification visit | 7–15 days after assignment |
| ERO approval | 5–10 days after verification |
| Updated voter ID / e-EPIC available | 7–15 days after approval |
| **Total estimated time** | **3–6 weeks** |

During election season or revision periods, processing may be faster as drives are active.

## Fees

Voter ID transfer is **completely free**. There is **no charge** for Form 6 or Form 8A submission, whether online or offline.

## Common Issues and Fixes

### 1. Application Stuck at "Under Verification" for Weeks
- **Cause:** BLO has not visited or submitted the report
- **Fix:** Call the voter helpline at **1950** with your Reference ID. You can also visit the local ERO office in person. Check BLO contact details on the Voter Portal and call them directly.

### 2. Wrong Constituency Assigned After Transfer
- **Cause:** Incorrect pin code or address entered, or boundary changes
- **Fix:** File a fresh **Form 8A** (if within correct constituency) or **Form 6** (if different constituency). Double-check your constituency using the "Know Your Electoral Details" tool before applying.

### 3. Old Address Still Showing After Approval
- **Cause:** Backend update delay or only voter list updated but not EPIC card
- **Fix:** Download your updated **e-EPIC** from [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in) → "Download e-EPIC". If still showing old address, wait 2–3 weeks and try again. Contact your ERO if it persists.

### 4. Application Rejected Without Clear Reason
- **Cause:** Document mismatch, address proof doesn't match new area, or BLO couldn't verify
- **Fix:** Check rejection reason on the portal. Reapply with correct documents. Ensure your address proof clearly shows the new address. See our [Voter ID Application Rejected Fix guide](/guide/voter-id-application-rejected-fix) for detailed troubleshooting.

### 5. Old Voter ID Not Getting Deleted After Form 6
- **Cause:** Inter-state or inter-district transfers take longer for deletion
- **Fix:** After your new registration is confirmed, file **Form 7** (request for deletion) for the old constituency. This can also be done online on the NVSP portal.

### 6. OTP Not Received During Online Application
- **Cause:** Mobile number mismatch or network issue
- **Fix:** Ensure the mobile number matches what's registered. Try after some time. Use the Voter Helpline App as an alternative.

---

## Important Tips

1. **Apply well before elections** — don't wait for election announcements. Transfers take 3–6 weeks minimum.
2. **Update Aadhaar first** — if you use Aadhaar as address proof, update your Aadhaar address before applying for voter transfer.
3. **Keep your old EPIC number handy** — you'll need it for both Form 8A and Form 6.
4. **Screenshot your Reference ID** — this is essential for tracking your application.
5. **Don't apply on both forms** — use either Form 8A or Form 6, not both. Duplicate applications cause delays.

## FAQs

### Can I transfer my Voter ID online without visiting any office?
Yes, you can apply online through [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in) or the Voter Helpline App. However, a BLO will visit your new address for verification — you don't need to visit any office yourself.

### What is the difference between Form 6 and Form 8A?
Form 8A is for address changes **within the same Assembly Constituency**. Form 6 is for **new voter registration** when you move to a different constituency, district, or state. Form 6 creates a new entry and deletes the old one.

### How long does voter ID address transfer take?
The complete process typically takes **3 to 6 weeks** — this includes BLO verification and ERO approval. During active electoral roll revision periods, it may be faster.

### Will my old Voter ID become invalid after transfer?
If you used **Form 8A** (same constituency), your EPIC number stays the same — only the address is updated. If you used **Form 6** (new constituency), you get a new EPIC number, and the old one is deactivated.

### Can I vote at my old address while the transfer is processing?
Yes, until your transfer is approved, your name remains in the voter list at your old constituency. Once the new registration is approved and old one is deleted, you can only vote at the new address.

### What if the BLO doesn't visit my address?
If the BLO doesn't visit within 15 days of your application, call the voter helpline at **1950** or visit the ERO office in your constituency. You can find BLO contact details on the [Voter Portal](https://voterportal.eci.gov.in).

### Can I transfer my Voter ID from one state to another?
Yes, use **Form 6** for inter-state transfers. Fill in your new state address and mention your previous registration details. The old registration will be cancelled once the new one is approved.

### Is there any fee for voter ID address transfer?
No, voter ID transfer is completely free. Both Form 6 and Form 8A can be submitted online at no cost.
