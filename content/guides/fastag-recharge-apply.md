## What is FASTag?

FASTag is an electronic toll collection system operated by the **National Highway Authority of India (NHAI)** using RFID (Radio Frequency Identification) technology. It enables automatic toll deduction from a prepaid or linked account when your vehicle passes through a toll plaza — without stopping.

FASTag is **mandatory for all four-wheeled and above vehicles** on national highways. Vehicles without a valid FASTag are charged double the toll amount at designated FASTag lanes.

The system works on the **National Electronic Toll Collection (NETC)** programme managed by NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India) and IHMCL (Indian Highway Management Company Limited).

## How to Buy/Apply for FASTag

### Method 1: Online Through Bank
1. Visit your bank's website (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Paytm Payments Bank, etc.)
2. Navigate to **FASTag** section
3. Fill in vehicle details — registration number, vehicle class
4. Upload documents — RC copy, vehicle owner's photo, KYC
5. Pay issuance fee (₹100-500 depending on bank)
6. FASTag is delivered to your address in 3-7 days

### Method 2: Through Paytm / Amazon
1. Search for "FASTag" on Paytm or Amazon
2. Select the bank/issuer
3. Enter vehicle registration number and details
4. Upload RC copy and KYC documents
5. Pay and get FASTag delivered

### Method 3: At Toll Plaza
1. Visit any NHAI toll plaza with FASTag sales counter
2. Carry your vehicle RC, driving licence, and one KYC document
3. Pay the issuance fee and initial recharge amount
4. FASTag is affixed to your windshield immediately

### Method 4: At Bank Branch
1. Visit a bank branch that issues FASTag
2. Fill the application form
3. Submit RC copy, KYC document, and passport photo
4. Pay the fee and collect FASTag

### Method 5: Through My FASTag App
1. Download the **My FASTag** app (by IHMCL)
2. Register with mobile number
3. Add vehicle details and upload documents
4. Choose issuing bank
5. Pay and get FASTag delivered

## Documents Required

- **Vehicle Registration Certificate (RC)** — front and back
- **Vehicle owner's KYC** — Aadhaar, PAN, or passport
- **Passport-size photograph** of vehicle owner
- **Driving licence** (at some issuers)

## How to Recharge FASTag

### Method 1: UPI / Net Banking
1. Open your FASTag issuer's app or website
2. Log in with your registered mobile
3. Select **Recharge FASTag**
4. Enter amount and pay via UPI, net banking, or debit card

### Method 2: Through Paytm / PhonePe / Google Pay
1. Open the payment app
2. Search for **FASTag Recharge**
3. Enter vehicle number or FASTag ID
4. Enter amount and complete payment

### Method 3: My FASTag App
1. Open My FASTag app
2. Select your linked FASTag
3. Tap **Recharge**
4. Pay via UPI, cards, or net banking

### Method 4: At Toll Plaza
Some toll plazas accept cash top-up for FASTag. Ask at the customer service counter.

### Auto-Recharge
Many banks offer **auto-recharge** — when your FASTag balance falls below a threshold, it automatically recharges from your linked bank account or wallet.

## How to Affix FASTag to Vehicle

1. **Clean the windshield** inside surface (top centre area)
2. **Peel the adhesive** backing of the FASTag sticker
3. **Stick it on the inside** of the windshield, behind the rearview mirror
4. Ensure it's **clearly visible** from outside and not obstructed
5. **Do not tamper** — Removing and re-sticking may damage the RFID chip

## How to Link FASTag to Vehicle

If your FASTag is not pre-linked:
1. Log in to your FASTag issuer's portal
2. Go to **Tag Management** → **Link Vehicle**
3. Enter vehicle registration number
4. Upload RC copy
5. Verify and link

Each FASTag is linked to **one vehicle** only. If you change vehicles, you need a new FASTag.

## Common Issues and Solutions

### FASTag Not Reading at Toll
- Ensure FASTag is properly affixed and not blocked
- Check if balance is sufficient
- Vehicle class mismatch — contact issuer
- Move to the customer service lane at the toll plaza

### Double Deduction
- Check transaction history in the app
- Raise a dispute through the issuer bank's portal
- Refund is processed within 3-7 working days

### Blacklisted FASTag
- Happens due to KYC issues or insufficient balance
- Contact the issuing bank to resolve
- Complete KYC if pending

### FASTag for New Vehicle (Number Plate Change)
- Old FASTag becomes invalid with old registration
- Apply for a new FASTag with the new RC
- Transfer is not possible between vehicles

## Important Tips

1. **Keep minimum ₹500 balance** to avoid issues at toll plazas
2. **Enable auto-recharge** for hassle-free travel
3. **Don't transfer FASTag between vehicles** — it's linked to one vehicle permanently
4. **Save issuer's helpline number** — Useful for disputes at toll plazas
5. **Get FASTag before a long drive** — Buying at toll plazas during peak hours can mean long queues

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is FASTag mandatory for all vehicles?
FASTag is mandatory for all four-wheeled and above vehicles on national highways. Two-wheelers and three-wheelers are currently exempt. Vehicles without FASTag pay double toll.

### Can I use one FASTag for multiple vehicles?
No, each FASTag is linked to one specific vehicle registration number. You need a separate FASTag for each vehicle.

### What happens if I drive through a FASTag lane without FASTag?
You'll be charged **double the toll** amount. Some toll plazas may also redirect you to a cash lane with additional waiting time.

### How do I close or deactivate my FASTag?
Contact your issuing bank to close/deactivate the FASTag. The remaining balance and security deposit will be refunded to your bank account within 7-14 days.

### Can I get a FASTag refund if I sell my vehicle?
Yes, close your FASTag account through the issuer. The balance and security deposit are refunded. The new owner must get a fresh FASTag in their name.

### Does FASTag work at state toll plazas?
FASTag works at most national highway toll plazas. Some state highways have also adopted FASTag. However, a few state-level toll plazas may still accept only cash.
