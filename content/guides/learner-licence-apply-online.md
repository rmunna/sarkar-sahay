# How to Apply for Learner's Licence Online in India

A **Learner's Licence (LL)** is the first step to getting a driving licence in India. It is a temporary permit that allows you to learn driving on public roads under the supervision of a licensed driver. You must hold a valid LL before you can apply for a permanent [Driving Licence](/guide/driving-license-apply-online).

The entire application process can now be completed online through the **Sarathi Parivahan portal** — no need to visit the RTO office in most states.

## What is a Learner's Licence?

A Learner's Licence is an official document issued by the Regional Transport Office (RTO) that permits you to practice driving. Key points:

- It is **mandatory** before applying for a permanent Driving Licence (DL)
- You must hold the LL for at least **30 days** before applying for a DL
- The LL is valid for **6 months** from the date of issue
- You must drive with an "L" sign displayed on your vehicle while learning

## Eligibility for Learner's Licence

Your eligibility depends on the type of vehicle you want to drive:

| Vehicle Type | Minimum Age | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Motorcycle without gear (up to 50cc) | 16 years | Written consent from parent/guardian required |
| Motorcycle with gear | 18 years | — |
| Car (LMV) | 18 years | — |
| Commercial vehicle (Transport) | 18 years | Must have 8th class pass certificate |

**Other requirements:**
- You must know traffic signs and rules
- No disqualification order from any court or RTO should be in effect
- You must be physically and mentally fit to drive

## Documents Required for Learner's Licence

Keep these documents ready before starting your application:

### Mandatory Documents
1. **Proof of Age** (any one):
   - Birth certificate
   - 10th class mark sheet
   - Aadhaar card
   - Passport
   - PAN card

2. **Proof of Address** (any one):
   - Aadhaar card
   - Voter ID
   - Passport
   - Electricity / water bill (not older than 3 months)
   - Ration card

3. **Passport-size photographs** — recent colour photos

4. **Signature** — digital or scanned

### Additional Documents (if applicable)
- **Medical Certificate (Form 1A)** — required for transport/commercial vehicle licence, or if applicant has any physical disability
- **Parental consent** — if applicant is between 16–18 years
- **8th class pass certificate** — for transport vehicle LL

## How to Apply for Learner's Licence Online — Step by Step

Follow these steps on the Sarathi Parivahan portal:

### Step 1: Visit the Sarathi Portal
Go to [sarathi.parivahan.gov.in](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/) and select your **state** from the dropdown.

### Step 2: Select "New Learner's Licence"
On the state page, click on **"New Learner's Licence"** under the Learner's Licence section.

### Step 3: Fill the Application Form
- Enter your personal details — name, father's/mother's name, date of birth, address, mobile number, and email
- Select the **class of vehicle** (e.g., MC with gear, LMV, Transport)
- Fill in all mandatory fields marked with an asterisk (*)

### Step 4: Upload Documents
- Upload scanned copies of your age proof, address proof, photograph, and signature
- File size and format requirements will be shown on the portal (usually JPEG/PDF, under 1 MB)

### Step 5: Upload Medical Certificate (if needed)
- For transport vehicles, upload **Form 1A** signed by a registered medical practitioner
- For non-transport LMV/motorcycle, a self-declaration of fitness is usually sufficient

### Step 6: Book LL Test Slot
- Choose a **date and time** for your online LL test
- Some states allow you to take the test immediately; others require a scheduled appointment at the RTO

### Step 7: Pay the Fee
- Pay the LL fee online using net banking, debit card, credit card, or UPI
- You will receive a payment receipt — save it for your records

### Step 8: Take the LL Test
- Appear for the test on your scheduled date (online or at RTO, depending on state)
- On passing, your LL will be issued

### Step 9: Download Your Learner's Licence
- Once approved, download your LL from the Sarathi portal or check your registered email

## Learner's Licence Fees

| Fee Component | Amount (approx.) |
|---|---|
| LL application fee | ₹200 |
| LL test fee | ₹50 – ₹150 |
| **Total** | **₹200 – ₹350** |

> **Note:** Fees vary slightly by state. The exact amount will be shown during online payment.

## LL Test — Format and How to Pass

The Learner's Licence test checks your knowledge of Indian traffic rules and road signs.

### Test Format
- **Total questions:** 15 (multiple choice)
- **Passing score:** 9 correct answers (60%)
- **Time limit:** Usually 5–10 minutes
- **Language:** Available in English, Hindi, and regional languages
- **Mode:** Computer-based (at RTO or online, depending on your state)

### Topics Covered
- Traffic signs (mandatory, cautionary, informatory)
- Road markings and signals
- Right of way and overtaking rules
- Speed limits
- Drink driving laws
- Seat belt and helmet rules
- Pollution and vehicle fitness

### Tips to Pass the LL Test
1. **Study the official RTO handbook** — focus on traffic signs and their meanings
2. **Practice mock tests** — many free mock tests are available on the Parivahan website and third-party apps
3. **Learn all traffic signs** — at least 20–30 common signs are asked frequently
4. **Read questions carefully** — some questions have tricky wording
5. **Don't rush** — you have enough time, so read each question properly

## How to Download Your Learner's Licence

After passing the LL test:

1. Visit [sarathi.parivahan.gov.in](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/)
2. Select your state
3. Click on **"Print Learner's Licence"** or **"Download LL"**
4. Enter your **Application Number** and **Date of Birth**
5. Download and print the PDF

You can also check your email — the LL is often sent to your registered email address.

## How to Check Learner's Licence Status

1. Go to [parivahan.gov.in](https://parivahan.gov.in/parivahan/)
2. Navigate to **"Know Your LL Status"** or use the Sarathi portal
3. Enter your Application Number or LL Number and Date of Birth
4. Your current status (approved, pending, rejected) will be displayed

## Learner's Licence Validity and Rules

- **Validity:** 6 months from the date of issue
- **Renewal:** If your LL expires, you need to apply for a fresh LL — there is no renewal option
- **Rules while driving with LL:**
  - Always display an **"L" sign** on the front and back of the vehicle
  - Drive only under the supervision of a person who holds a valid permanent driving licence
  - Do not carry passengers (except the supervisor)
  - Do not drive on highways or restricted roads
  - Follow all traffic rules strictly

## Converting Learner's Licence to Permanent Driving Licence

After holding your LL for at least **30 days** (and before it expires in 6 months), you can apply for a permanent [Driving Licence](/guide/driving-license-apply-online):

1. Visit the Sarathi portal and select **"New Driving Licence"**
2. Enter your LL number
3. Book a **driving test slot** at your nearest RTO
4. Upload required documents
5. Pay the DL fee (approximately ₹300–₹500)
6. Appear for the **driving test** at the RTO on the scheduled date
7. On passing, your permanent DL will be issued

> **Related:** Already have a DL? Learn about [DL Renewal](/guide/driving-license-renewal-online) or applying for an [International Driving Permit](/guide/international-driving-permit-apply).

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Can I apply for a Learner's Licence fully online?
Yes, in most states you can complete the entire LL application online through the Sarathi Parivahan portal — including filling the form, uploading documents, paying fees, and even taking the LL test. Some states may still require you to visit the RTO for the test.

### How long does it take to get a Learner's Licence?
If you pass the LL test, the licence is usually issued on the **same day** or within **1–2 working days**. You can download it from the portal.

### Can I drive a car with a Learner's Licence?
Yes, but only under the supervision of a person who holds a valid permanent driving licence for that vehicle type. You must also display an "L" sign on the vehicle.

### What happens if I fail the LL test?
You can retake the test. Most states allow you to rebook and attempt the test again after a **7-day waiting period**. You may need to pay the test fee again.

### Is a medical certificate required for a Learner's Licence?
A medical certificate (Form 1A) is required only for **transport/commercial vehicle** LL applications. For personal vehicles (motorcycle, car), a self-declaration of physical fitness is usually enough.

### Can I apply for LL for multiple vehicle types at once?
Yes, you can select multiple vehicle classes (e.g., motorcycle with gear + LMV) in a single application. The fee may be slightly higher.

### What is the minimum age for a two-wheeler Learner's Licence?
You can apply at **16 years** for a motorcycle without gear (up to 50cc) with parental consent. For a geared motorcycle, the minimum age is **18 years**.
