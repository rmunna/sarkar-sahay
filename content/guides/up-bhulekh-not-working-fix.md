# UP Bhulekh Not Working 2026? Fix All Portal Errors

The UP Bhulekh portal (upbhulekh.gov.in) is Uttar Pradesh's land records portal for checking khasra, khatauni and RoR (Record of Rights). It faces frequent downtime and captcha issues. Here's how to access UP land records even when the main portal is down.

## Common UP Bhulekh Errors

| Error | Cause |
|-------|-------|
| Portal not opening / blank page | Server down or maintenance |
| Captcha image not loading | Browser JavaScript disabled or cache issue |
| "No record found" for khasra number | Wrong number entered or survey errors |
| Slow loading / timeout | Peak hours (10 AM – 3 PM) |
| "Server error 500" | Backend server overloaded |
| PDF not generating for nakal | PDF plugin issue in browser |

## Fix 1: Use Alternative UP Land Record Portals

When upbhulekh.gov.in is down, use these alternatives:

| Portal | URL | What's Available |
|--------|-----|-----------------|
| **eKhasra UP** | [ekhasra.up.gov.in](https://ekhasra.up.gov.in/) | Khasra nakal, khatauni search |
| **Bhulekh UP (mirror)** | [bhulekh.up.in](https://bhulekh.up.in/) | Land records search |
| **Vaad UP (certified copies)** | [vaad.up.nic.in](https://vaad.up.nic.in/) | Court-valid certified nakal |
| **UP eDistrict** | [edistrict.up.gov.in](https://edistrict.up.gov.in/) | Certified land record download with fees |

> **ekhasra.up.gov.in** is the most reliable alternative — it's maintained separately and is usually accessible even when the main portal is down.

## Fix 2: Captcha Not Loading

If the security captcha image doesn't appear on upbhulekh.gov.in:

1. **Clear browser cache**: Chrome → Ctrl+Shift+Delete → Cached images and files → Clear
2. **Disable ad blockers**: extensions like uBlock Origin block captcha images
3. **Try Incognito mode**: Chrome → Ctrl+Shift+N
4. **Switch to Chrome**: the portal is optimized for Chrome; Firefox and Edge sometimes show captcha issues
5. **Refresh page** once after captcha loads — sometimes the image appears on second load

## Fix 3: "No Record Found" for Khasra Number

If your khasra number returns no results:

1. **Check the district and tehsil**: a khasra number is only unique within a village; wrong tehsil = no match
2. **Try owner name search**: on upbhulekh.gov.in, select "Khatedar Ka Naam" (owner name) instead of khasra number
3. **Old vs new khasra numbers**: after re-surveys, khasra numbers may have changed. Ask at the local Lekhpal's office for the current number
4. **Check revenue village name**: your postal address village may differ from the revenue village — ask a neighbour or local Patwari/Lekhpal

## Fix 4: Best Time to Access UP Bhulekh

The portal is heavily loaded on weekday afternoons:

- **Best time**: 7 AM – 9 AM or after 8 PM
- **Avoid**: 11 AM – 4 PM (highest server load)
- **Avoid Mondays**: weekly peak after weekend closure

## How to Check UP Khasra/Khatauni Online (When Portal Works)

1. Go to **[upbhulekh.gov.in](https://upbhulekh.gov.in/)** → Click "जनपद चुनें" (Select District)
2. Select **District → Tehsil → Village (Gram)**
3. Complete the captcha
4. Choose search type: **Khasra/Gata number** OR **Khatedar name**
5. Enter the number or name and click search
6. View khasra details: owner name, area in hectares, land type, mutation status

## Get Certified Copy (Nakal) of Land Records

A plain printout of land records from the portal is NOT legally valid. For official/court use:

1. Visit **[vaad.up.nic.in](https://vaad.up.nic.in/)** or nearest tehsil Nakal Daftar
2. Apply for certified copy (Pratilipi) with a fee of ₹20–₹30 per page
3. Or use UP eDistrict (edistrict.up.gov.in) for online certified copy

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I download khasra nakal from UP Bhulekh as a PDF?

Yes. After the search results appear, click "अभिलेख देखें" (View Record) and then use Ctrl+P → Save as PDF in your browser. However, this is not a certified copy — it's for reference only.

### UP Bhulekh shows my land under a wrong owner's name. How to correct?

Wrong ownership shown on the portal means either an old record (before recent mutation) or an error. To correct: file a mutation application at the tehsil office with sale deed/court order as proof. The Lekhpal will verify and update the record. This takes 30–90 days.

### Is upbhulekh.gov.in and bhulekh.up.in the same?

Both show UP land records but are maintained by different departments. upbhulekh.gov.in is the official Revenue Department portal. bhulekh.up.in is a mirror/alternative version. The underlying data is the same.

### How do I check mutation (daakhil-kharij) status?

Visit [upbhulekh.gov.in](https://upbhulekh.gov.in/) → "खाता विवरण" → enter your khata number and look for "Daakhil Kharij" entry date. Alternatively, visit your tehsil office with your application receipt.
