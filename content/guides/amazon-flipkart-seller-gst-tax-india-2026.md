# Amazon / Flipkart Seller Tax & GST India 2026 — Complete Guide

If you sell products on Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, or any e-commerce marketplace, you have specific GST and income tax obligations. Many small sellers don't know that GST registration is mandatory for marketplace sellers regardless of turnover — and non-compliance attracts penalties.

---

## Is GST Registration Mandatory for E-Commerce Sellers?

**Yes** — and this is different from offline businesses.

| Seller Type | GST Threshold |
|-------------|--------------|
| **E-commerce marketplace seller** (Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, Myntra) | **Mandatory GST** — no turnover threshold |
| Offline small business | Exempt below ₹20L (services) / ₹40L (goods) |

**Why different?** Section 24(ix) of the CGST Act mandates GST registration for all e-commerce sellers (businesses supplying goods/services through electronic commerce operators). Even if you sell only ₹1,000/month.

**Exception**: If you sell ONLY exempt goods (fresh produce, unprocessed food, fresh milk, etc.) — but most products on Amazon/Flipkart are taxable.

---

## TCS — Marketplace Deducts 1% Before Paying You

**TCS (Tax Collected at Source)** under Section 52 of CGST Act:
- Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, etc. deduct **1% TCS** (0.5% CGST + 0.5% SGST or 1% IGST on interstate)
- Deducted from your settlement amount before payment
- Credited to your GST account by the marketplace (visible in GSTR-2A/2B)
- You can claim this as credit against your GST liability

**Example**: You sell goods for ₹1,00,000 in a month
- Marketplace deducts TCS 1%: ₹1,000
- Your net settlement: ₹99,000 (+ ₹18,000 GST which marketplace also remits after collecting from buyer)
- ₹1,000 TCS credit appears in your GST account

**Reconcile TCS**: Check your GSTR-2A or GST portal → Services → Returns → Auto-Populated for TCS credits from each marketplace.

## Getting GST Registration as E-Commerce Seller

1. Go to [gst.gov.in](https://www.gst.gov.in) → New Registration → Part A
2. Enter PAN, business name, mobile, email → OTP verify
3. Proceed to Part B:
   - Business type: Proprietorship/Partnership/Company
   - Business address proof (electricity bill, rent agreement)
   - Bank account details
   - Photographs
   - Authorization letter (if partnership/company)
4. Receive GSTIN within 7 working days (provisional GSTIN immediately)
5. Register your GSTIN on Amazon/Flipkart Seller Central

## GST Filing — What You Must File

| Return | Frequency | What It Contains |
|--------|-----------|-----------------|
| **GSTR-1** | Monthly (≥₹5Cr turnover) or Quarterly (IFF quarterly) | Your sales invoice details |
| **GSTR-3B** | Monthly | Summary of sales + purchases + TCS credit + net GST payment |
| **GSTR-9** | Annually | Annual GST reconciliation |

**Composition Scheme**: E-commerce sellers CANNOT opt for composition scheme (lower rate but no ITC). Mandatory full GSTR-1/3B filing.

---

## GST Input Tax Credit (ITC) for Sellers

You can claim ITC (deduct GST paid on purchases from GST liability):
- GST paid on packaging material
- GST paid on raw materials (if manufacturing)
- GST paid on warehouse services, logistics (18%)
- GST paid on professional services related to business

**Cannot claim ITC for**: Personal use purchases, personal vehicle, food/beverages.

## Income Tax for E-Commerce Sellers

### Which ITR Form?

| Seller Type | ITR Form |
|-------------|---------|
| Seller with business income (profit/loss) | **ITR-3** |
| Seller using presumptive taxation (Section 44AD) | **ITR-4 (Sugam)** |

### Section 44AD — Presumptive Taxation for Small Sellers

If your annual turnover is ≤ ₹3 crore AND you qualify:
- Declare **8% of gross turnover as profit** (6% if all receipts are digital/banking)
- No detailed books of accounts needed
- Pay tax on that 8%/6% profit

**Example**: ₹30L annual turnover on Amazon
- Section 44AD profit: 8% = ₹2.4L
- Tax in new regime: ₹2.4L in 0% slab → no tax!
- Or at 5% bracket if other income: ~₹12,000 tax

### Actual Books (ITR-3)

If turnover > ₹3Cr, or you want to declare actual profit (which may be lower than 8%), file ITR-3 with detailed P&L:

| Income | Revenue from marketplace settlements |
|--------|-------------------------------------|
| Expenses | Inventory cost, shipping, returns, packaging, marketplace fees, ads |
| Net Profit | Revenue - All expenses |
| Tax | On net profit at slab rate |

Amazon charges: Platform fee 2–20% + closing fee + fulfillment fee (if FBA) + ad spend. These are all deductible expenses.

## Common Mistakes E-Commerce Sellers Make

| Mistake | Consequence |
|---------|------------|
| Operating without GST registration | Penalties up to ₹10,000 or 10% of tax (whichever higher) + back taxes |
| Not filing GSTR-1 or 3B | Late fee ₹50/day (max ₹5,000) per return |
| Not claiming TCS credit | Paying higher GST than owed |
| Treating marketplace fees as not-deductible | Higher taxable income |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I sell on Meesho. Is GST also mandatory?

Yes — Meesho is an electronic commerce operator under CGST. All sellers on Meesho also need GST registration mandatorily.

### I sell handmade jewelry on Instagram. Do I need GST?

Instagram direct sales (not through a marketplace) follow the regular threshold: register only above ₹20L (services) or ₹40L (goods). If you also use Meesho/Amazon for the same products, you'd need GST registration regardless.

### My Amazon profits are very low because of returns and fees. Can I show actual loss?

Yes — with ITR-3 and actual books, you can show actual profit/loss. Section 44AD (presumptive) requires declaring minimum 8% — if your actual profit is lower, use actual books (ITR-3). Losses can also be set off against future income.

### When does Amazon/Flipkart deduct income tax TDS from seller payments?

Amazon and Flipkart generally do NOT deduct income tax TDS on seller payments (TCS is different — that's GST TCS). Income tax on your profits is your responsibility via advance tax/ITR.
