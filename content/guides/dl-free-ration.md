## What is Delhi Free Ration Scheme?

The **Delhi Free Ration Scheme** provides free foodgrains to eligible families in Delhi through the **Public Distribution System (PDS)**. Under this scheme, combined with central schemes like **PM Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana (PMGKAY)** and **National Food Security Act (NFSA)**, eligible Delhi residents receive **free wheat, rice, and other essential commodities** every month from designated ration shops (Fair Price Shops).

The scheme is managed by the **Department of Food, Supplies and Consumer Affairs, Government of NCT of Delhi**.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Delhi Resident:** Must be a resident of Delhi with a valid **Delhi ration card**
2. **Ration Card Category:**
   - **AAY (Antyodaya Anna Yojana):** Poorest of the poor families — 35 kg foodgrains/month
   - **Priority Household (PHH):** 5 kg per person per month
3. **NFSA Coverage:** Families covered under the National Food Security Act
4. **Aadhaar:** Aadhaar-linked ration card for biometric authentication at shops

## Monthly Entitlement

| Category | Wheat | Rice | Sugar |
|----------|-------|------|-------|
| AAY | 25 kg free | 10 kg free | As notified |
| PHH | 4 kg/person free | 1 kg/person free | As notified |

> Under PMGKAY extension and Delhi state subsidy, these quantities may be provided completely **free of cost**.

## Documents Required (for New Ration Card)

- **Aadhaar Card** of all family members
- **Proof of Delhi Residence** (Voter ID / Electricity Bill / Rent Agreement)
- **Passport-size photographs** of head of family
- **Income proof** (for AAY category)
- **Mobile number** linked to Aadhaar

## How to Get a Delhi Ration Card

### Online Application

1. **Visit** [nfs.delhi.gov.in](https://nfs.delhi.gov.in) or [edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in](https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in)
2. **Register** / Login
3. **Select** "New Ration Card" application
4. **Fill** family details, address, and Aadhaar numbers of all members
5. **Upload** documents
6. **Submit** and note application ID
7. **Verification** by Food Supply Officer at your residence
8. **Ration card issued** after successful verification

### Offline

1. **Visit** the **District Food & Supply Office** or **Circle Office**
2. **Collect** the ration card application form
3. **Fill** and attach documents
4. **Submit** to the Circle FSO
5. **Field verification** will be done
6. **Card issued** within 30-45 days

## How to Collect Free Ration

1. **Visit** your designated **Fair Price Shop (FPS)** on the scheduled date
2. **Carry** your ration card and **Aadhaar Card**
3. **Biometric authentication** (fingerprint) at the ePoS machine
4. **Collect** your monthly ration entitlement
5. **Check** the quantity and quality before leaving

## Important Tips

1. **Aadhaar Must Be Linked:** Your ration card must be linked with Aadhaar of all family members for biometric PDS
2. **Check Your FPS:** Find your designated Fair Price Shop at [nfs.delhi.gov.in](https://nfs.delhi.gov.in)
3. **Don't Miss Dates:** Ration is distributed on specific dates each month — check with your FPS
4. **Complain if Issues:** Use the Delhi government helpline **1967** for ration-related complaints
5. **Portability:** Under One Nation One Ration Card, you can collect ration from any FPS in India

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. Is ration really free in Delhi?
**Yes**, under NFSA and PMGKAY combined with Delhi state subsidy, eligible families receive ration at **zero cost**.

### Q2. How much ration do I get?
PHH families get **5 kg per person per month**. AAY families get **35 kg per family per month**.

### Q3. Do I need Aadhaar?
**Yes**, Aadhaar is mandatory for biometric authentication at the ration shop.

### Q4. How do I check if I have a ration card?
Visit [nfs.delhi.gov.in](https://nfs.delhi.gov.in) and search by Aadhaar or ration card number.

### Q5. Can I collect ration from any shop?
Under One Nation One Ration Card, yes — you can collect from any ePoS-enabled FPS in India.

### Q6. What if the shop refuses to give ration?
Call the Delhi government helpline **1967** or complaint online at [food.delhi.gov.in](https://food.delhi.gov.in).

### Q7. How do I add a family member to my ration card?
Apply for modification at [edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in](https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in) or visit the Circle Food & Supply Office.
