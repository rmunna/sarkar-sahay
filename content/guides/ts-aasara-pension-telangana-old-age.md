## What is Telangana Aasara Pension?

**Aasara Pension** is Telangana government's flagship social security pension scheme that gives **₹2,016 per month** to multiple categories of vulnerable citizens. "Aasara" means "support" — the pension goes to those who have no other income.

The pension amount is among the highest state social security pensions in India.

---

## Who Gets Aasara Pension?

| Category | Pension Amount |
|----------|---------------|
| Old Age (60+ years) | ₹2,016/month |
| Widows/abandoned women | ₹2,016/month |
| Persons with disability (40%+) | ₹2,016/month |
| Beedi workers | ₹2,016/month |
| Toddy tappers (EBC) | ₹2,016/month |
| Handloom weavers (EBC) | ₹2,016/month |
| Single women (deserted/separated) | ₹2,016/month |
| Fishermen (EBC) | ₹2,016/month |

---

## Who is Eligible?

**General criteria for all categories:**
- Must be a **permanent resident of Telangana**
- Must **not be an income tax payer**
- Must **not be a government employee or pensioner**
- No **private/government pension** from other sources
- Must not own or live in a **pucca house worth more than ₹2.5 lakh**

**Age criteria:**
- Old Age: **60 years+**
- All other categories: **18 years+ (no maximum age)**

---

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar Card** — mandatory
2. **Age proof** (for old age pension) — Aadhaar with DoB
3. **Community certificate** (for category-specific pension)
4. **Disability certificate** (for disability category) — 40%+ from CMO
5. **Occupation proof** (for toddy tappers, weavers, fishermen) — labour card, occupational certificate
6. **Bank passbook** — Aadhaar-seeded account in beneficiary's name
7. **Photograph**
8. **Widow proof** — husband's death certificate (for widow pension)

## How to Apply — Step by Step

### Step 1 — Apply at Gram Panchayat / Municipality

Applications for Aasara Pension are submitted at the local **Gram Panchayat office** (rural) or **Ward Office / Municipal Corporation** (urban).

### Step 2 — Fill Application

Get the Aasara Pension application form from the panchayat/ward office — it is free. Fill in your details and attach all documents.

### Step 3 — Mandal Parishad Development Officer (MPDO) Verification

The Mandal Revenue Officer (MRO) or MPDO verifies:
- Eligibility category
- Income and assets (no pucca house worth >₹2.5L)
- Residency

### Step 4 — District Collector Approval

After MPDO approval, the District Collector approves the beneficiary list.

### Step 5 — Monthly Pension Starts

After inclusion in the beneficiary list, pension is credited to your Aadhaar-linked bank account on the 1st of every month.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. My mother is 61 and lives alone in Hyderabad. She has no income. How does she apply?**

She should go to the Ward Office (in Hyderabad, the GHMC ward office) with her Aadhaar and other documents. The ward officer will help with the Aasara Pension application.

**2. My father receives ₹1,000/month from IGNOAPS. Will he get Aasara Pension on top?**

Generally, Telangana integrates IGNOAPS with Aasara and the total comes to ₹2,016/month (Central + State together). He may already be receiving this combined pension. Check the pension portal.

**3. My wife is 35 and a widow. Is she eligible?**

Yes. Widow/abandoned women are eligible from age 18. Being 35 and widowed, she qualifies for ₹2,016/month.

**4. I applied but my name is not on the pensioners list. What to do?**

Visit your Gram Panchayat Sachiv or Ward Development Secretary. They maintain the local pensioners list. If your application was processed but not included, escalate to the MPDO office.

**5. The pension stopped for 2 months. Why?**

Common reasons: biometric/Aadhaar verification not done, DBT failure, or periodic reverification. Visit the Ward/Panchayat office to check and rectify.
