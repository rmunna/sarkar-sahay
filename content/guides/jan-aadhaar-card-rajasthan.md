# Rajasthan Jan Aadhaar Card — Apply Online, Download & Link Services (2026)

**Jan Aadhaar** is Rajasthan's unified family and individual identity system. Launched by the Government of Rajasthan, it replaces the earlier Bhamashah Card and serves as the **single gateway to all state government schemes** — from healthcare and rations to scholarships and pensions.

Every family residing in Rajasthan needs a Jan Aadhaar Card. It assigns a **10-digit Family ID** and a unique **11-digit Member ID** to each individual in the family.

> **Key fact:** Without Jan Aadhaar, you cannot access Chiranjeevi Health Insurance, subsidised ration (PDS), state scholarships, pension schemes, or most Rajasthan government benefits.

---

## Why You Need Jan Aadhaar

Jan Aadhaar is mandatory for accessing virtually every Rajasthan government service:

- **[Chiranjeevi Health Insurance](/guide/chiranjeevi-yojana-rajasthan)** — ₹25 lakh cashless treatment (Jan Aadhaar required for registration)
- **Public Distribution System (PDS)** — subsidised ration through fair price shops
- **Scholarships** — state scholarships for school and college students
- **Pension schemes** — old age, widow, and disability pensions
- **Palanhar Yojana** — support for orphan children
- **Housing schemes** — Indira Awas and other housing benefits
- **DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer)** — all cash transfers routed through Jan Aadhaar-linked bank accounts

## What is a Jan Aadhaar Card?

| Detail | Description |
|---|---|
| **Full name** | Jan Aadhaar Yojana (जन आधार योजना) |
| **Launched** | 18 December 2019 |
| **Replaces** | Bhamashah Card |
| **Issuing authority** | Department of Economics & Statistics (DES), Government of Rajasthan |
| **Family ID** | 10-digit number |
| **Member ID** | 11-digit number (unique per person) |
| **Head of family** | Woman of the household (preferred) |
| **Eligibility** | All families residing in Rajasthan |
| **Cost** | Free |

### Jan Aadhaar vs Aadhaar (UIDAI)

- **Aadhaar** is a national individual identity (12-digit, issued by UIDAI)
- **Jan Aadhaar** is a Rajasthan state family identity (10-digit family + 11-digit member)
- Both are different — Jan Aadhaar **links with** your Aadhaar but does not replace it

## How to Register for Jan Aadhaar Online

### Prerequisites

- Aadhaar card of the head of family (preferably a woman)
- Aadhaar cards of all family members
- Mobile number linked to Aadhaar
- Bank account details (for DBT)
- Passport-size photograph

### Step-by-Step Online Registration

1. **Visit** the official portal: [janaadhaar.rajasthan.gov.in](https://janaadhaar.rajasthan.gov.in)
2. **Click** on "Jan Aadhaar Enrollment" or "Citizen Registration"
3. **Enter** your Aadhaar number and verify via OTP sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile
4. **Fill in** family head details — name, date of birth, gender, address
5. **Add family members** — enter each member's Aadhaar number and verify
6. **Upload** documents — photograph, bank passbook first page
7. **Enter bank account details** — account number, IFSC code, bank name
8. **Review** all details and **submit** the application
9. **Note down** your Jan Aadhaar Enrollment ID / acknowledgement number

### Via SSO (Single Sign-On) Portal

1. Register or log in at [sso.rajasthan.gov.in](https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in)
2. Search for "Jan Aadhaar" in the available services
3. Follow the same enrollment process as above
4. Track application status through the SSO dashboard

## How to Add Family Members

You can add new family members (newborn child, spouse after marriage, etc.) to an existing Jan Aadhaar:

1. Log in to [janaadhaar.rajasthan.gov.in](https://janaadhaar.rajasthan.gov.in)
2. Enter your **Jan Aadhaar Family ID** and authenticate via OTP
3. Select **"Add Member"**
4. Enter the new member's Aadhaar number and verify
5. Fill in details — name, date of birth, relationship with head
6. Submit — the member is added after verification

> **For newborns without Aadhaar:** Visit your nearest e-Mitra kiosk with the birth certificate. The child can be added with a pending Aadhaar link.

## How to Download Jan Aadhaar Card

### Download Digital Card (e-Jan Aadhaar)

1. Go to [janaadhaar.rajasthan.gov.in](https://janaadhaar.rajasthan.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Card Download"** or **"Jan Aadhaar Card Download"**
3. Enter your **Jan Aadhaar ID** (Family ID or Member ID)
4. Verify via OTP on registered mobile number
5. Click **Download** — your card downloads as a PDF
6. Print or save on your phone for use at hospitals, ration shops, etc.

### Download via SSO

1. Log in at [sso.rajasthan.gov.in](https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in)
2. Navigate to Jan Aadhaar services
3. Select "Download Card" and authenticate
4. Download the PDF

### Download via Rajasthan Jan Aadhaar App

1. Download the **Jan Aadhaar app** from Google Play Store
2. Log in with your registered mobile number
3. View and download your family card and individual member cards

## How to Update Jan Aadhaar Details

You can update the following details:

- **Name correction**
- **Date of birth**
- **Mobile number**
- **Address change**
- **Bank account details**
- **Photograph update**
- **Add/remove family members**

### Update Process

1. Visit [janaadhaar.rajasthan.gov.in](https://janaadhaar.rajasthan.gov.in)
2. Select **"Update"** or **"Jan Aadhaar Update"**
3. Enter your Jan Aadhaar ID and verify via OTP
4. Select the field you want to update
5. Enter new details and upload supporting documents if required
6. Submit the update request
7. Changes reflect after verification (typically 3–7 working days)

> **Important:** For name or date of birth changes, you may need to visit an e-Mitra kiosk with original documents for physical verification.

## How to Link Jan Aadhaar with Aadhaar (UIDAI)

Linking Jan Aadhaar with your UIDAI Aadhaar ensures seamless identity verification across state and central schemes.

1. Visit [janaadhaar.rajasthan.gov.in](https://janaadhaar.rajasthan.gov.in)
2. Select **"Aadhaar Linking"** or find the option under your profile
3. Enter your **Jan Aadhaar Member ID**
4. Enter your **12-digit Aadhaar number**
5. Verify via OTP sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile
6. Confirm — linking is done instantly

> **Note:** If your mobile number is different on Aadhaar and Jan Aadhaar, update one of them first to match, or visit an e-Mitra kiosk for biometric verification.

## e-Mitra Kiosk Process

If you cannot complete registration or updates online, visit any **e-Mitra kiosk** across Rajasthan:

### What You Need to Carry

- Original Aadhaar card (all family members)
- One passport-size photo of the family head
- Bank passbook or cancelled cheque
- Existing Bhamashah card (if any, for migration)
- Mobile phone (for OTP)

### At the Kiosk

1. Visit your nearest **e-Mitra centre** — find one at [emitra.rajasthan.gov.in](https://emitra.rajasthan.gov.in)
2. Tell the operator you want **Jan Aadhaar enrollment / update**
3. Provide your Aadhaar and documents
4. The operator will enter your details and capture biometrics (fingerprint/iris)
5. Verify details on screen and confirm
6. Collect your **acknowledgement receipt**
7. Card is generated within 7–15 working days
8. **Service charge:** ₹30–50 at e-Mitra (may vary)

## Jan Aadhaar Enrollment Camps

The Government of Rajasthan periodically organises **Jan Aadhaar enrollment camps** in:

- **Gram Panchayat offices** (rural areas)
- **Ward offices** (urban areas)
- **Government schools and colleges**
- **Community centres**

### How to Find Camps Near You

- Check announcements on [janaadhaar.rajasthan.gov.in](https://janaadhaar.rajasthan.gov.in)
- Contact your **local Gram Panchayat or Ward Councillor**
- Call the Jan Aadhaar helpline: **0141-2850287**
- Follow updates on local news and government social media

### What Happens at a Camp

- Free enrollment — no charges
- Biometric capture (fingerprint and iris scan)
- Instant acknowledgement slip
- Card dispatched or available for download after processing

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Jan Aadhaar mandatory?
Yes, for accessing any Rajasthan government scheme — including [Chiranjeevi Health Insurance](/guide/chiranjeevi-yojana-rajasthan), ration, scholarships, and pensions.

### Can men be the head of family?
Yes, but the government prefers a **woman as head of family** to promote women's empowerment and ensure benefits reach women directly.

### I have a Bhamashah Card. Do I still need Jan Aadhaar?
Yes. Bhamashah has been fully replaced by Jan Aadhaar. Your Bhamashah data may have been auto-migrated, but verify and update your Jan Aadhaar.

### Can I have Jan Aadhaar without Aadhaar (UIDAI)?
Enrollment requires Aadhaar for biometric verification. If you don't have Aadhaar, apply for it first at your nearest Aadhaar Enrollment Centre.

### How long does the card take?
- **Online registration:** Instant acknowledgement; card available for download in 7–15 days
- **e-Mitra:** 7–15 working days
- **Camps:** 15–30 days

### Is there a fee?
- **Online:** Free
- **e-Mitra:** ₹30–50 service charge
- **Camps:** Free
