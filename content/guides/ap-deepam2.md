# Deepam 2 Scheme – 3 Free LPG Cylinders Per Year in Andhra Pradesh

Deepam 2 is a welfare scheme by the Government of Andhra Pradesh that provides 3 free LPG gas cylinders per year to eligible households. The scheme aims to reduce the cooking fuel burden on poor families and promote clean cooking fuel usage, especially for women in BPL households.

## What is Deepam 2?

Building on the original Deepam scheme (which provided free LPG connections), Deepam 2 ensures ongoing support by providing 3 free LPG refill cylinders annually to eligible families. The cost of these 3 cylinders is fully borne by the state government. Beneficiaries can purchase additional cylinders at the normal subsidized rate.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **White ration card holders:** Families holding a white (BPL) ration card in Andhra Pradesh
2. **Women beneficiaries:** The LPG connection must be in the name of a woman in the household
3. **Existing LPG connection:** Must have an active LPG connection (Indane, HP Gas, or Bharat Gas)
4. **Residency:** Must be a resident of Andhra Pradesh
5. **Exclusions:**
   - Families with annual income above the BPL threshold
   - Government employees and income tax payers
   - Households without a ration card

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar card of the woman LPG consumer (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- White ration card (see our [ration card guide](/guide/ration-card-apply-online))
- LPG consumer number / subscription voucher
- Bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked)
- Mobile number linked to LPG account

## Step-by-Step Process

### How It Works

1. **Automatic identification:** The government identifies eligible beneficiaries based on white ration card and LPG connection data
2. **Linked to ration card:** Your ration card and LPG consumer number are linked in the government database
3. **Book cylinder normally:** Book your LPG refill as you normally do (through distributor, app, or call)
4. **Pay for the cylinder:** Pay the full amount at the time of delivery
5. **Reimbursement via DBT:** The cost of 3 cylinders per year is reimbursed directly to the woman's bank account
6. **Check credit:** The reimbursement amount will be credited within a few days of each eligible refill

### If Not Enrolled

1. **Visit Village/Ward Secretariat:** If you believe you're eligible but not receiving the benefit
2. **Carry documents:** Take your ration card, Aadhaar, LPG consumer details, and bank passbook
3. **Submit request:** The Secretariat staff will verify and help link your LPG details
4. **Verify Aadhaar-bank link:** Ensure your Aadhaar is seeded in your bank account for DBT

## Important Tips

1. **Keep LPG-Aadhaar linked:** Ensure your LPG connection and Aadhaar are linked for DBT
2. **Book regularly:** The 3 free cylinders must be booked within the financial year — unused benefits don't carry forward
3. **Check bank statements:** After each refill, verify that the reimbursement has been credited
4. **Update mobile number:** Keep your mobile number updated with both the gas agency and bank
5. **Free scheme — no agents needed:** Do not pay anyone to enroll you — this is automatic for eligible families

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Do I get 3 cylinders for free or is it a reimbursement?
You pay for the cylinder at delivery and the cost is reimbursed to your bank account via DBT within a few days.

### Q2: What if I don't have an LPG connection?
You need an active LPG connection first. Apply for a new connection under Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana or at your nearest LPG distributor.

### Q3: Can I get more than 3 free cylinders?
No, only 3 cylinders per year are free under Deepam 2. Additional refills are at the normal subsidized price.

### Q4: Is the connection required in a woman's name?
Yes, the LPG connection must be in the name of a woman family member.

### Q5: What if I have a pink (above poverty line) ration card?
Only white ration card holders are eligible for Deepam 2 free cylinders.

### Q6: When will I get the reimbursement?
Typically within 3–7 working days after the refill delivery, the amount is credited via DBT.

### Q7: I have a white ration card but didn't receive the benefit. What should I do?
Visit your Village/Ward Secretariat with your ration card, Aadhaar, and LPG details to get the issue resolved.
