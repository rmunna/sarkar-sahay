# PM Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) 2025 – Complete Guide

## What is PM Fasal Bima Yojana?

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) is a government-sponsored crop insurance scheme launched on 13th January 2016. It provides financial support to farmers suffering crop loss or damage due to natural calamities, pests, and diseases. The scheme is implemented by empaneled general insurance companies under the overall guidance of the Department of Agriculture.

PMFBY replaced the earlier National Agricultural Insurance Scheme (NAIS) and Modified NAIS with significantly lower premium rates and quicker claim settlements. As of 2024, the scheme has insured over 56 crore farmer applications and settled claims worth over ₹1.65 lakh crore.

## Key Features

- **Lowest premiums** in the world — farmers pay just 1.5% to 5% of the sum insured
- **Full coverage** against crop loss from sowing to post-harvest
- **Technology-driven** — uses satellite imagery, drones, and smartphones for crop loss assessment
- **Voluntary** for all farmers since Kharif 2020 (was mandatory for loanee farmers earlier)
- Covers **food crops, oilseeds, and annual commercial/horticultural crops**

## Premium Rates

| Season | Crop Type | Farmer's Premium | Government Subsidy |
|--------|-----------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **Kharif** | Food crops & oilseeds | 2% of sum insured | Remaining premium |
| **Rabi** | Food crops & oilseeds | 1.5% of sum insured | Remaining premium |
| **Kharif & Rabi** | Commercial/horticultural crops | 5% of sum insured | Remaining premium |

> The actual premium can be 15-25% of sum insured. The government (Centre + State) pays the rest as subsidy.

## Who is Eligible?

- **All farmers** — both loanee and non-loanee — growing notified crops in notified areas
- Sharecroppers and tenant farmers (with proof of cultivation)
- Farmers must have an **insurable interest** in the crop at the time of sowing
- Both **irrigated and rain-fed** crops are covered

### Crops Covered
- Food crops: Rice, wheat, millets, pulses
- Oilseeds: Soybean, groundnut, mustard, sunflower
- Commercial crops: Cotton, sugarcane, jute
- Horticultural crops: Fruits, vegetables, spices (where notified)

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Online Application

1. **Visit** the PMFBY portal at [pmfby.gov.in](https://pmfby.gov.in)
2. **Register** using your mobile number
3. **Login** and click on "Apply for Crop Insurance"
4. **Enter** personal details — name, Aadhaar, bank account
5. **Select** your state, district, block, and village
6. **Choose** the crop and season (Kharif/Rabi)
7. **Enter** land details — survey number, area sown (hectares)
8. **Upload** land records and sowing certificate
9. **Pay** the farmer's share of premium online
10. **Download** the insurance policy document after confirmation

### Through Bank (for Loanee Farmers)

1. If you have a **crop loan**, your bank will automatically enrol you (you can opt out if you wish)
2. Premium is **debited from your loan account**
3. Collect the **insurance policy certificate** from the bank
4. You can also opt out by submitting a declaration to your bank **before the cut-off date**

### Through CSC (Common Service Centre)

1. **Visit** your nearest CSC / Jan Seva Kendra
2. **Carry** all required documents
3. CSC operator will **fill the form** and upload documents
4. **Pay** the premium (cash/UPI)
5. **Collect** the acknowledgment receipt

### Application Deadlines

| Season | Deadline |
|--------|----------|
| Kharif | Usually July 31 (varies by state) |
| Rabi | Usually December 31 (varies by state) |

> Check the PMFBY portal for state-specific deadlines each season.

## How to File a Claim

1. **Report crop loss** within **72 hours** of the calamity
2. **Call** the Crop Insurance App helpline or report on PMFBY portal
3. You can also report via the **Crop Insurance App** (available on Android/iOS)
4. **Insurance company** will send a surveyor for loss assessment
5. **Assessment** is done at the village/area level using technology (satellite data, drones)
6. **Claim amount** is calculated based on yield data and deposited to your **Aadhaar-linked bank account**

### Claim Settlement Timeline

- **Individual claims** (post-harvest, localized): Within 30 days of loss assessment
- **Area-based claims** (widespread): Within 60 days of yield data finalization
- **Prevented sowing claims**: 25% of sum insured if >75% area affected

## Important Tips

1. **Report crop damage within 72 hours** — Late reporting can result in claim rejection. Use the Crop Insurance mobile app for quick reporting.
2. **Download the Crop Insurance App** — Available on Google Play and App Store. Use it to report losses, track claims, and check coverage details.
3. **Keep land records updated** — Ensure your khata/khasra entries match the actual crop sown. Mismatches can delay claims.
4. **Non-loanee farmers must apply before the deadline** — Don't wait until the last date. Portal gets heavy traffic near deadlines.
5. **Opt-out if not needed** — If you're a loanee farmer and don't want insurance, submit a written declaration to your bank before the cut-off date.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Is PMFBY mandatory for farmers with crop loans?
**No**, not anymore. Since Kharif 2020, the scheme is **voluntary for all farmers**, including loanee farmers. You can opt out by informing your bank before the cut-off date.

### Q2: How much premium do farmers pay?
Farmers pay **2% for Kharif, 1.5% for Rabi** (food crops and oilseeds), and **5% for commercial/horticultural crops**. The government pays the rest as subsidy.

### Q3: How is crop loss assessed?
PMFBY uses a combination of **Crop Cutting Experiments (CCEs), satellite remote sensing, drones, and smartphone-based assessments**. For localized calamities, individual farm-level assessment is done.

### Q4: When will I receive the claim amount?
For area-based claims, within **60 days** of yield data finalization. For individual claims (localized damage, post-harvest losses), within **30 days** of loss assessment.

### Q5: Can tenant farmers apply for PMFBY?
**Yes.** Tenant farmers and sharecroppers can apply with proof of cultivation — a seasonal agreement with the landowner and the landowner's land records.

### Q6: What if I miss the application deadline?
Unfortunately, applications submitted **after the cut-off date are not accepted**. You must apply before the state-specific deadline for each season.
