# Jan Samarth Portal 2026 — One Portal for 13 Government Loan Schemes

**Jan Samarth** (jansamarth.in) is a Government of India portal that lets you apply for **13 different government-backed loan schemes** in one place — without visiting a bank or contacting an agent. The portal matches your profile to eligible schemes and connects you with a lender within 7 working days.

## Schemes Available on Jan Samarth Portal

| Category | Scheme | Loan Amount |
|----------|--------|------------|
| **Street Vendors** | PM SVANidhi (₹10K → ₹20K → ₹50K) | ₹10,000–₹50,000 |
| **Business/Enterprise** | PM MUDRA Loan (Shishu/Kishore/Tarun) | ₹50,000–₹10 lakh |
| **Education** | Dr. Ambedkar Central Sector Scheme | Up to ₹20 lakh |
| **Education** | Padho Pradesh Interest Subsidy | Up to ₹20 lakh |
| **Agriculture** | Kisan Credit Card (KCC) | As per land/crop |
| **SC/OBC Business** | National Scheduled Castes Finance Corp | ₹2 lakh–₹30 lakh |
| **Tribal Entrepreneurs** | NSTFDC Loan | Up to ₹30 lakh |
| **Women Entrepreneurs** | NMDFC Loan for women | Up to ₹10 lakh |
| **Housing** | PM Awas Yojana housing loan subsidy | As per scheme |

## How to Apply on Jan Samarth Portal

### Step 1: Register

1. Visit **[jansamarth.in](https://www.jansamarth.in/)**
2. Click **"Login / Register"**
3. Enter your **mobile number** → verify with OTP
4. Optionally link your **Aadhaar** for auto-fill (recommended — speeds up application)

### Step 2: Check Eligibility

1. On the dashboard, click **"Check Eligibility"** or **"Apply for Loan"**
2. Select your loan purpose: education, business, agriculture, street vending, etc.
3. Answer eligibility questions (age, income, business type, caste category)
4. The portal shows which schemes you qualify for

### Step 3: Apply for a Scheme

1. Select your preferred scheme
2. Fill in the application form — most fields auto-fill from Aadhaar
3. Upload required documents (PDF/JPG under 2 MB each):
   - Aadhaar card
   - PAN card (or Aadhaar for amounts under ₹50,000)
   - Income proof / ITR
   - Business registration (for business loans)
   - Course/college admission proof (for education loans)
4. Submit application

### Step 4: Wait for Lender Connection

- Within **7 working days**, a bank/NBFC will be assigned to your application
- You'll receive an SMS on your registered mobile
- The lender may call for document verification or video KYC
- After lender verification, loan is sanctioned and disbursed

## Jan Samarth Portal Login Issues — Fix

**OTP not received:**
- OTP goes to mobile you registered. Try "Resend OTP" after 60 seconds
- Check for DND; switch to different mobile if stuck

**Portal not loading:**
- Try at 8–9 AM or after 7 PM (lower traffic)
- Use Chrome and clear cache (Ctrl+Shift+Delete)
- Avoid mobile browsers — use desktop for form-filling

**Application not submitted:**
- Check file sizes (max 2 MB each)
- Ensure Aadhaar is verified; update your name/DOB if mismatch
- Try Incognito mode if stuck on a particular step

## Jan Samarth vs Udyam Portal vs Bank Direct — Which to Use?

| Method | When to Use |
|--------|-------------|
| **Jan Samarth** | Multiple scheme options, not sure which bank, want comparison |
| **PM SVANidhi official site** | If you specifically want SVANidhi only |
| **Udyam Portal** | For MSME registration (prerequisite for some loans) |
| **Bank directly** | If you already have a relationship with a bank and know the scheme |

Jan Samarth is best for **first-time borrowers** or those who want to compare which scheme and lender suits them.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Jan Samarth portal free to use?

Yes. Registering and applying on Jan Samarth is completely free. You do not need to pay any agent or facilitation fee. The portal directly connects you to government-approved lenders.

### Can I apply for multiple loans at the same time?

You can apply for different scheme categories (e.g., one education loan and one business loan) if you are eligible. However, having multiple active loans may affect your credit score and lender's risk assessment.

### How long does it take to get the loan after applying?

- Lender assignment: 7 working days after application submission
- Lender verification and sanction: 7–30 days (varies by lender and scheme)
- Disbursal after sanction: 2–7 days

Total time from application to disbursal is typically **3–6 weeks** for most schemes.

### What is PM MUDRA Loan and how much can I get?

PM MUDRA Loan is for small businesses and entrepreneurs. Three categories:
- **Shishu**: up to ₹50,000 (new/micro businesses)
- **Kishore**: ₹50,000 – ₹5 lakh (existing businesses)
- **Tarun**: ₹5 lakh – ₹10 lakh (well-established businesses)

Apply via Jan Samarth, any scheduled bank, or MUDRA portal (mudra.org.in).

### I got a call from someone claiming to be from Jan Samarth asking for money. Is it genuine?

**No.** Jan Samarth and government schemes never call asking for processing fees, insurance premiums, or any advance payment. If you receive such calls, it's a scam. Report at cybercrime.gov.in.
