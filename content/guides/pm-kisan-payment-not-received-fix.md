# PM Kisan Payment Not Received — How to Fix

Crores of farmers depend on **PM Kisan Samman Nidhi** for ₹6,000 per year (₹2,000 per installment). But many beneficiaries face delayed or stuck payments every cycle. If your PM Kisan payment has not been received, this guide walks you through every possible reason and the exact steps to fix it.

> **Also read:** [Complete Guide to PM Kisan Samman Nidhi](/guides/pm-kisan-samman-nidhi) | [PM Kisan eKYC Failed — How to Fix](/guides/pm-kisan-ekyc-failed-fix)

---

## Why PM Kisan Payment Gets Stuck

Your installment can fail or get delayed due to any of these reasons:

| # | Reason | How Common |
|---|--------|------------|
| 1 | **eKYC not completed** | Very Common |
| 2 | **Bank account number mismatch** | Common |
| 3 | **Aadhaar-bank seeding failed** | Common |
| 4 | **Name mismatch** (Aadhaar vs bank vs land records) | Common |
| 5 | **Land record verification pending** | Moderate |
| 6 | **Account inactive or closed** | Moderate |
| 7 | **Duplicate registration** | Less Common |
| 8 | **State government has not sent data** | Occasional |

The PM Kisan portal shows a specific rejection reason for most cases. Check your beneficiary status first.

## How to Check PM Kisan Beneficiary Status

**Step 1:** Visit [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in).

**Step 2:** Click **"Know Your Status"** or go directly to the [Beneficiary Status page](https://pmkisan.gov.in/beneficiarystatus.aspx).

**Step 3:** Enter your **registered mobile number** and captcha.

**Step 4:** Click **Get Data**. You'll see:
- Installment history (which ones were paid)
- Current status and rejection reason (if any)
- Bank details and Aadhaar status

> **Tip:** If the status says **"Rft Signed by State"**, payment is on its way. If it says **"Pending at State"** or shows a rejection reason, action is needed.

## Fix for Each Reason

### 1. eKYC Not Done

Since 2022, **eKYC is mandatory** for all PM Kisan beneficiaries. No eKYC = no payment.

**How to fix:**
1. Go to the [PM Kisan eKYC page](https://pmkisan.gov.in/registrationformekyc.aspx)
2. Enter your **Aadhaar number** and captcha
3. Click **Search** → then **Get OTP**
4. Enter the OTP received on your Aadhaar-linked mobile
5. Submit — eKYC is done

**If OTP-based eKYC fails**, visit your nearest CSC (Common Service Centre) for biometric eKYC. Read our detailed guide: [PM Kisan eKYC Failed — How to Fix](/guides/pm-kisan-ekyc-failed-fix).

---

### 2. Bank Account Mismatch

If your bank account number in PM Kisan records is wrong, the payment will bounce.

**How to fix:**
1. Log in to [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in)
2. Go to **"Updation of Self Registered Farmer"**
3. Enter your Aadhaar number → verify with OTP
4. Update the **correct bank account number and IFSC code**
5. Submit the correction request

> **Important:** The updated account must be in **your name** (matching Aadhaar name) and must be **Aadhaar-seeded**.

---

### 3. Aadhaar-Bank Seeding Failed

Even if your bank account is correct, **Aadhaar must be linked (seeded) to that account** for DBT payments.

**How to check seeding status:**
- Visit [UIDAI Bank Mapper](https://resident.uidai.gov.in/bank-mapper) or dial **\*99\*99\*1#** from your registered mobile.

**How to fix:**
1. Visit your bank branch with **Aadhaar card** and **passbook**
2. Fill the Aadhaar seeding form
3. Bank will link your Aadhaar to the account (takes 2–7 days)
4. Verify seeding status after a week

---

### 4. Name Mismatch

Your name must match across **three records**: Aadhaar, bank account, and land/revenue records. Even a small spelling difference (e.g., "Rajesh" vs "Rajesh Kumar") can cause rejection.

**How to fix:**
- **Aadhaar name wrong?** → Update at [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in) or visit an Aadhaar Enrolment Centre
- **Bank name wrong?** → Visit your branch with ID proof and request a name correction
- **Land record name wrong?** → Contact your Tehsildar/Patwari office to update revenue records

> After correcting the name, allow 1–2 installment cycles for the system to re-verify.

---

### 5. Land Record Verification Pending

States upload land ownership data to the PM Kisan portal. If your land records are incomplete or not uploaded, payment stays pending.

**How to fix:**
1. Visit your **local Patwari or Tehsildar office**
2. Ensure your land is recorded in your name in revenue records
3. Get a **Khasra/Khatauni** copy as proof
4. Contact your **Agriculture Department** or **Block Development Officer** to push the data upload

## How to File a Complaint

If you've fixed all issues and still haven't received payment:

### Option 1: PM Kisan Helpline
- **Phone:** 155261 or 011-24300606
- **Email:** pmkisan-ict@gov.in
- Available Mon–Sat, 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM

### Option 2: Online Grievance on PM Kisan Portal
1. Go to [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in)
2. Click **"Help Desk"** at the bottom
3. Enter your Aadhaar/mobile/account number
4. Describe your issue and submit

### Option 3: CPGRAMS (Centralized Grievance Portal)
1. Visit [pgportal.gov.in](https://pgportal.gov.in)
2. Register and file a grievance under **Ministry of Agriculture**
3. You'll get a tracking number for follow-up

### Option 4: Visit Your Local Agriculture Office
- Meet the **Block/District Agriculture Officer** with your Aadhaar, bank passbook, and land documents
- They can check your status in the backend system and escalate

## Important Tips

- ✅ **Complete eKYC every year** if prompted — it's now a recurring requirement
- ✅ Keep your **mobile number updated** on both Aadhaar and PM Kisan portal
- ✅ Ensure your bank account is **active** — dormant accounts reject DBT transfers
- ✅ After any correction, wait **at least one installment cycle** (4 months) for payment to resume
- ✅ Save your **PM Kisan registration number** for quick status checks
- ❌ Do NOT create **duplicate registrations** — this will block both entries
- ❌ Do NOT use a **joint account** — only individual accounts linked to your Aadhaar work

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: How many days does it take to receive PM Kisan payment after status shows "Rft Signed"?

Usually **5–10 working days** after the status shows "Rft Signed by State." If it takes longer than 15 days, contact the helpline.

### Q2: Can I receive PM Kisan payment in a post office account?

**Yes.** India Post Payments Bank (IPPB) accounts and post office savings accounts linked to Aadhaar are eligible for PM Kisan DBT.

### Q3: My eKYC is done but payment is still pending. What should I do?

Check for other issues — bank account mismatch, Aadhaar seeding failure, or land record problems. Use the beneficiary status page to see the exact rejection reason. If no reason is shown, file a complaint via the helpline.

### Q4: I changed my bank account. When will the next payment come to the new account?

After updating your bank details on the portal, it takes **1–2 installment cycles** for verification. Ensure the new account is Aadhaar-seeded.

### Q5: What if my state has not uploaded my data?

Contact your **Block Development Officer** or **District Agriculture Officer**. They handle state-level data uploads. You can also raise a grievance on CPGRAMS targeting your state agriculture department.

### Q6: Is there a deadline for eKYC to receive the current installment?

Yes. The government announces eKYC deadlines before each installment. Check [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in) for the latest deadline. Missing it means your payment for that cycle will be held.

### Q7: Can PM Kisan payment be received in someone else's account?

**No.** The bank account must be in the **beneficiary's own name** and linked to their Aadhaar. Joint accounts or family member accounts are not accepted.
