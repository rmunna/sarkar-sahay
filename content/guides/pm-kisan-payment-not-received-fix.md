## What is PM Kisan?

PM Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) is a central government scheme that provides **₹6,000 per year** in three equal installments of ₹2,000 to eligible farmer families. The amount is directly transferred to the beneficiary's bank account.

If you haven't received your PM Kisan installment, this guide will help you identify the issue and fix it.

## Common Reasons for Non-Payment

1. **Aadhaar-bank account mismatch** — Name in Aadhaar doesn't match bank records
2. **eKYC not completed** — Mandatory Aadhaar-based eKYC is pending
3. **Aadhaar not linked to bank account** — DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer) requires Aadhaar-linked bank account
4. **Land record verification failed** — State government hasn't verified your land records
5. **Bank account invalid or closed** — Account is inactive, frozen, or closed
6. **Duplicate registration** — Multiple registrations with different details
7. **Ineligible category** — Income tax payers, government employees, and institutional landholders are excluded

## Step 1: Check Your Beneficiary Status

1. Visit [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Beneficiary Status"** or go directly to [pmkisan.gov.in/BeneficiaryStatus.aspx](https://pmkisan.gov.in/BeneficiaryStatus.aspx)
3. Enter your **Aadhaar number** or **mobile number** or **account number**
4. Click **"Get Data"**
5. Check your status:
   - **Registered:** Your application is in the system
   - **Pending for approval at State/District level:** Land verification is pending
   - **Rejected:** Your application has been rejected (reason will be shown)
   - **FTO is generated:** Payment is being processed
   - **Rft Signed by State:** Payment is approved and will be credited soon

## Step 2: Fix Aadhaar-Bank Account Mismatch

This is the **most common reason** for failed payments.

1. **Check if your name in Aadhaar matches your bank account name exactly**
2. If there's a mismatch:
   - Visit your **bank branch** and update your name to match Aadhaar, OR
   - Update your **Aadhaar name** at an Aadhaar Enrollment Centre to match bank records
3. **Link Aadhaar to your bank account** (if not already done):
   - Visit your bank branch with Aadhaar card
   - Fill the Aadhaar seeding form
   - The bank will link your Aadhaar to your account for DBT
4. Verify linking at [resident.uidai.gov.in](https://resident.uidai.gov.in/bank-mapper)

## Step 3: Complete eKYC

eKYC is **mandatory** for receiving PM Kisan payments.

### Online (OTP-based):
1. Visit [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in)
2. Click on **"eKYC"** option on the homepage
3. Enter your **Aadhaar number** and registered mobile number
4. Click **"Get OTP"** and enter the OTP received on your Aadhaar-linked mobile
5. eKYC will be completed instantly

### Offline (at CSC):
1. Visit your nearest **Common Service Centre (CSC)**
2. Carry your **Aadhaar card** and registered mobile
3. The CSC operator will complete biometric or OTP-based eKYC
4. You'll receive confirmation on your mobile

*If eKYC keeps failing, see our [PM Kisan eKYC Failed guide](/guide/pm-kisan-ekyc-failed-fix) for troubleshooting.*

## Step 4: Fix Land Record Issues

If your status shows **"Pending for approval at State/District level"**:

1. **Contact your local Patwari or Lekhpal** (revenue officer) to verify your land records
2. Ensure your **land records are updated** in the state land portal
3. If you have recently purchased land, ensure the **mutation** (name transfer) is complete
4. Contact your **District Agriculture Officer** for status updates
5. Some states allow online tracking — check your state's land records portal

## Step 5: Fix Bank Account Issues

1. **Ensure your bank account is active** — deposit a small amount or visit the branch
2. **Check IFSC code** — if your branch has merged, the IFSC may have changed
3. **Update bank details** on PM Kisan portal:
   - Visit [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in)
   - Use the **"Updation of Self Registered Farmer"** option
   - Update your bank account number and IFSC code

## Processing Time

- **eKYC verification:** Instant (OTP) or same day (CSC biometric)
- **Bank account update:** 2-5 working days to reflect
- **Land record verification:** Varies by state (1-4 weeks)
- **Payment processing after fix:** Next installment cycle (installments are released in April-July, August-November, December-March)

## Important Tips

1. **Complete eKYC immediately** — payments are blocked until eKYC is done
2. **Use the same mobile number** registered with both Aadhaar and PM Kisan
3. **Don't create duplicate registrations** — it will block both applications
4. **Check status before every installment** to catch issues early
5. **Keep your Aadhaar, bank account, and land records updated** and consistent

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When are PM Kisan installments released?

Installments are released in three periods: **April-July** (1st), **August-November** (2nd), and **December-March** (3rd). Each installment is ₹2,000.

### How do I know if my eKYC is complete?

Check on [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in) under the **eKYC** section. Enter your Aadhaar number — if eKYC is done, it will show **"eKYC is already done"**.

### My name is different in Aadhaar and bank account. What should I do?

You need to make the names match. Either update your name in Aadhaar at an enrollment centre, or update your name at the bank branch. Both must be identical for DBT to work.

### Can I receive PM Kisan in a joint bank account?

Yes, but **your Aadhaar must be linked** to that joint account. The account holder name linked via Aadhaar must match your PM Kisan registration.

### I was getting payments earlier but they stopped. Why?

Common reasons: eKYC became mandatory and you didn't complete it, Aadhaar-bank linking expired, land records need re-verification, or your status was flagged during verification. Check your beneficiary status online for the specific reason.

### My application was rejected. Can I re-apply?

If rejected due to incorrect details, you can correct the information and re-apply through the **"Updation of Self Registered Farmer"** option on the PM Kisan portal or visit your local agriculture office.
