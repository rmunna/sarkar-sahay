# Telangana Mahalakshmi Scheme 2026 — ₹2,500/Month Cash + Free Bus Travel for Women

The **Telangana Mahalakshmi Scheme** is a landmark welfare initiative launched by the Congress government under Chief Minister A. Revanth Reddy on **June 2, 2026** (Telangana Formation Day). The scheme has two distinct components:

1. **₹2,500/month cash transfer** — for eligible women heads of household with annual family income below ₹2 lakh
2. **Free TSRTC bus travel** — for ALL women and girls in Telangana, regardless of income

**Quick answer:** To get the ₹2,500/month cash, apply at your nearest **MeeSeva centre** with income proof and Aadhaar. For the free bus card only, collect the **Mahalakshmi Smart Card** from a **TGSRTC bus depot or MeeSeva centre** (₹50 fee). Check status at [mahalakshmi.telangana.gov.in](https://mahalakshmi.telangana.gov.in).

| Component | Benefit | Who Gets It |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly cash | ₹2,500/month via DBT | Women heads of household with annual family income < ₹2 lakh |
| Free bus travel | Free on all TSRTC ordinary/express buses | ALL women and girls in Telangana |

---

## Component 1: ₹2,500 Monthly Cash Transfer

### Eligibility

- **Female** head of household
- **Annual family income below ₹2 lakh** (family income, not individual)
- Resident of **Telangana state**
- Must have a **Telangana ration card** or domicile proof
- Aadhaar-linked bank account for DBT

**Not eligible:**
- Government employees (central or state)
- Women from families with annual income above ₹2 lakh
- Families already receiving certain other cash welfare benefits (check official guidelines)

### Documents Required (for ₹2,500 Cash)

- Aadhaar card
- Bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked account)
- Annual income certificate / family income proof (BPL card or certificate from sarpanch/MRO)
- Telangana ration card or domicile certificate
- Passport-size photograph

### How to Apply for ₹2,500 Cash

1. Visit your nearest **MeeSeva centre**
2. Request the Mahalakshmi Scheme cash transfer application form
3. Fill in: name, Aadhaar, bank details, household income details
4. Submit with self-attested copies of all documents
5. Pay standard MeeSeva service fee
6. Collect your application reference number
7. Once verified, ₹2,500 is credited monthly to your bank account via DBT

You can also apply online at **[mahalakshmi.telangana.gov.in](https://mahalakshmi.telangana.gov.in)** once the portal is fully live.

## Component 2: Free Bus Travel + Smart Card

### Who Gets Free Bus Travel?

**Every woman and girl in Telangana** is entitled to free travel on **TSRTC (TGSRTC) ordinary and express buses** — no income limit, no eligibility condition. This includes:
- Working women
- Students
- Government employees
- Senior women citizens
- Women from all income groups

### What Buses Are Covered?

- ✅ TSRTC ordinary (city and intercity routes)
- ✅ TSRTC express services
- ❌ AC sleeper / luxury / Garuda / Volvo / Metro Express (regular fare applies)

### Getting the Mahalakshmi Smart Card (for Free Bus)

1. Visit any **TSRTC bus depot** or **MeeSeva centre** in Telangana
2. Carry your Aadhaar card + one passport-size photo
3. Pay the ₹50 card fee
4. Smart Card is issued on the spot or collected within a few days
5. Show the Smart Card while boarding — no need to buy a ticket on eligible routes

> **Note:** Women without the Smart Card can still travel free by showing their Aadhaar card on TSRTC buses during the initial phase. The Smart Card streamlines the process and is recommended for regular travellers.

### MeeSeva Centres Near You

Find your nearest MeeSeva centre at [meeseva.telangana.gov.in](https://meeseva.telangana.gov.in) — search by district.

## Launch Context

The Mahalakshmi Scheme was launched on **June 2, 2026** — Telangana Formation Day — fulfilling a Congress election promise. Free TSRTC travel for women was implemented earlier (from 2023), and the scheme now formally expands with the cash transfer component and Smart Card.

The scheme name is inspired by Goddess Mahalakshmi, reflecting the financial empowerment (Lakshmi) angle of the programme.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**I am a salaried government employee — can I get the ₹2,500?**
No — government employees are excluded from the monthly cash component. However, you are eligible for the free TSRTC bus travel and can get the Smart Card.

**Can students get the Smart Card?**
Yes — all women, including students, are eligible for the Smart Card and free bus travel.

**Is this the same as the old free bus scheme for women?**
Free TSRTC travel for women was introduced earlier; the Mahalakshmi Scheme formally institutionalises and expands it with a Smart Card system and adds the monthly cash transfer for low-income households.

**What if my Aadhaar is not linked to a bank account?**
For the ₹2,500 cash, Aadhaar-bank linkage is required. Visit your bank to link Aadhaar before applying. For the Smart Card (free bus only), Aadhaar linkage to bank is not needed.

**Can I apply even if I don't live in Hyderabad?**
Yes — the scheme covers all of Telangana. Apply at the MeeSeva centre in your district. TSRTC depots are available in all districts for Smart Card collection.

**Is there an upper age limit?**
No upper age limit mentioned — women of any age above 18 are eligible.

**When does the ₹2,500 come to my account?**
After application, verification, and approval (typically 2–6 weeks), monthly payments begin on/around the 1st of the month. Credit goes directly to your Aadhaar-linked bank account.
