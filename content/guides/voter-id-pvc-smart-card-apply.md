# Voter ID PVC Smart Card: How to Apply & Get

The Voter ID PVC (Polyvinyl Chloride) smart card is the durable, credit-card-sized version of your EPIC (Electors Photo Identity Card). Unlike the older laminated paper voter ID, the PVC card has security features including a hologram and QR code. Here's everything you need to know about getting one.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent platform and is not affiliated with the Election Commission of India or any government body.

## e-EPIC vs PVC Smart Card: What's the Difference?

| Feature | e-EPIC (Digital) | PVC Smart Card (Physical) |
|---------|-----------------|--------------------------|
| Format | PDF/digital on phone | Physical plastic card |
| Availability | Instant download | Delivered by BLO or post |
| Cost | Free | Free (issued by ECI) |
| Validity | Valid as ID proof | Valid as ID proof |
| Security features | QR code, digital signature | Hologram, QR code, microtext |
| How to get | Download from voters.eci.gov.in | Applied through ERO office or distributed by BLO |
| Accepted everywhere | Some places may not accept digital | Universally accepted |

**Key point:** The e-EPIC is your digital voter ID that you can download instantly. The PVC card is the physical smart card that ECI prints and distributes. Both are valid identity proofs.

## Who Is Eligible for a PVC Smart Card?

- Any Indian citizen aged 18 years or above who is registered as a voter
- You must have an approved voter registration (Form 6 approved)
- Your EPIC number must be assigned in the electoral roll

## How to Get Your PVC Voter ID Card

### Method 1: Through BLO Distribution

The most common way to receive your PVC card is through your Booth Level Officer (BLO).

1. After your voter registration is approved, ECI prints PVC cards centrally
2. Cards are sent to the **District Election Office**
3. The **BLO (Booth Level Officer)** assigned to your area distributes cards door-to-door
4. BLO will visit your registered address and hand over the card
5. You may need to sign an acknowledgement receipt

**How to find your BLO:**
- Visit [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in/) → "Know Your BLO"
- Use the Voter Helpline App → "Know Your BLO/ERO" section
- Call the **1950 helpline**

### Method 2: Collect from ERO/AERO Office

If BLO hasn't delivered your card:

1. Visit the **Electoral Registration Officer (ERO)** office in your constituency
2. Carry your e-EPIC printout or EPIC number
3. Carry a government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar, passport, etc.)
4. Request your PVC card at the counter
5. If the card is available, you can collect it on the spot

### Method 3: Apply for Reprint/Replacement

If your PVC card is lost, damaged, or you never received one:

1. Visit [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in/) and log in
2. Look for the **"Replacement of EPIC"** or **"Print EPIC"** option
3. Enter your EPIC number and verify details
4. Submit the request
5. Your request will be processed by the ERO office
6. The reprinted PVC card will be dispatched to your BLO for delivery

### Method 4: Download e-EPIC (Instant Digital Alternative)

While waiting for the PVC card, get your digital voter ID:

1. Visit [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in/)
2. Log in with your registered mobile number
3. Go to **"Download e-EPIC"**
4. Verify your identity via OTP
5. Download the e-EPIC PDF
6. You can also download it via the **Voter Helpline App**

See our [Voter Helpline App guide](/guide/voter-helpline-app-guide) for detailed steps.

## Cost of Voter ID PVC Card

| Service | Cost |
|---------|------|
| First PVC card issuance | **Free** |
| e-EPIC download | **Free** |
| Replacement/reprint of lost card | **Free** (no fee charged by ECI) |

The Election Commission does not charge any fee for issuing voter ID cards. If anyone asks you to pay for a voter ID card, it is likely a scam.

## Processing and Delivery Timeline

| Stage | Expected Timeline |
|-------|-------------------|
| Form 6 approval | 15-30 days after submission |
| PVC card printing | 30-60 days after approval |
| BLO delivery | Within 30 days of card reaching district office |
| Total estimated time | 2-4 months from registration |
| e-EPIC availability | Immediately after registration approval |

**Note:** Timelines vary by state and district. During election periods, card printing and distribution may be faster.

## Important Tips

1. **Download e-EPIC immediately** after your registration is approved — it serves as valid ID proof while you wait for the physical PVC card
2. **Keep your mobile number updated** in the voter portal — OTP verification is needed for many services including e-EPIC download
3. **Contact your BLO** if you haven't received your PVC card within 3 months of registration approval
4. **Never pay anyone for a voter ID** — all ECI voter ID services are completely free
5. **Check your details on the e-EPIC** before the PVC card is printed — corrections are easier to make before printing

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. How do I know if my PVC card has been printed?
Check with your BLO or visit the ERO office in your constituency. You can find your BLO's contact details on [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in/) under "Know Your BLO."

### Q2. Can I use e-EPIC as ID proof at the polling booth?
Yes, e-EPIC is accepted as valid identification at polling stations. You can show it on your smartphone. However, carrying a printed copy is recommended as backup.

### Q3. My old voter ID is a laminated paper card. Will I get a PVC card automatically?
ECI has been gradually replacing old laminated cards with PVC smart cards. You may receive one during the next revision drive in your area. Contact your BLO to check if your PVC card is available.

### Q4. What should I do if my PVC card has incorrect details?
File a correction request using **Form 8** on [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in/) or through the Voter Helpline App. After correction is approved, a new PVC card will be issued. See our [Voter ID correction guide](/guide/voter-id-correction-online) for details.

### Q5. Can I get a PVC card for a family member?
Each voter must individually apply and collect their own PVC card. However, BLOs often deliver cards for all registered voters at an address together.

### Q6. Is the PVC smart card accepted for Aadhaar verification?
Yes, the Voter ID PVC card is accepted as valid identity proof for Aadhaar enrolment and update. See our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) for more information.

### Q7. What security features does the PVC smart card have?
The PVC smart card includes a hologram, QR code with encrypted voter data, microtext printing, and a unique serial number. These features make it tamper-proof and difficult to forge.
