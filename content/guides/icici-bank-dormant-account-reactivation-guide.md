# ICICI Bank Dormant Account Reactivation 2026 — Complete Guide

ICICI Bank classifies an account as dormant after **24 months** of no customer-initiated transactions. Salary credits, interest credits, and bank-initiated debits don't reset the dormancy clock — only a customer-initiated withdrawal/debit keeps it active.

---

## ICICI Dormant Account — Impact on Transactions

| Feature | Active Account | Dormant Account |
|---------|---------------|-----------------|
| ATM withdrawals | ✅ | ❌ Blocked |
| UPI / Debit card | ✅ | ❌ Blocked |
| Net banking debits | ✅ | ❌ Blocked |
| Salary / NEFT received | ✅ | ✅ Still credited |
| FD interest | ✅ | ✅ Still credited |
| Balance check | ✅ | ✅ Available |
| Cheque deposit | ✅ | ✅ Usually accepted |

## Method 1: iMobile Pay App (Online Reactivation)

ICICI iMobile Pay allows reactivation for select dormant accounts:

1. Open **iMobile Pay** → Login
2. **"Accounts"** → Select dormant account
3. Tap **"Activate Account"** or **"Account Services"** → **"Reactivate"** (if visible)
4. Complete KYC (Aadhaar OTP or Video KYC if prompted)
5. Submit reactivation
6. **Processing: 24–72 hours**

If option not visible: Branch visit or call centre escalation required.

---

## Method 2: ICICI Net Banking

1. Login at **[icicibank.com](https://www.icicibank.com)** → Net Banking
2. **"My Accounts"** → Dormant account → **"Services"** → **"Reactivate Account"**
3. Update KYC if prompted
4. Submit → Wait for confirmation email/SMS
5. **Processing: 2–4 working days**

---

## Method 3: ICICI Branch (Most Reliable)

1. Visit any **ICICI Bank branch**
2. Carry:
   - Aadhaar card (original + copy)
   - PAN card (original + copy)
   - Account details (account number or passbook)
3. Request: **"Dormant Account Reactivation"**
4. Fill reactivation form
5. Do a cash deposit (even ₹500): Triggers the fresh transaction required
6. **Reactivation: Same business day**

**ICICI Customer Care:** **1800-1080** (24×7, toll-free)

---

## Method 4: ICICI Video KYC + Reactivation

If KYC is expired/pending, ICICI allows Video KYC first:
1. iMobile Pay → KYC → Video KYC (complete KYC update)
2. After KYC done: Visit branch for reactivation transaction (or try iMobile reactivation again)

## ICICI Dormant Account & Linked Products

When ICICI account goes dormant:
- **ICICI Credit card**: Not affected (separate product — continues normally)
- **ICICI FD**: Not affected (continues earning interest)
- **SIP/Mutual funds**: Standing instructions fail → Resume after reactivation
- **ICICI Home Loan EMI**: May fail → Could cause EMI bounce charges. Reactivate ASAP.

---

## ICICI Dormant Account — 10-Year DEAF Rule

- After 10 years unclaimed: Balance transferred to RBI DEAF Fund
- Account still active at ICICI — not closed
- To claim: ICICI branch → DEAF claim form → Takes 3–5 working days

## Frequently Asked Questions

**ICICI account dormant — I'm abroad. How to reactivate?**
Options: (1) Authorize a trusted person with ICICI Power of Attorney to visit branch. (2) Try iMobile Pay online reactivation. (3) Contact ICICI NRI Services for NRE/NRO accounts: +91-22-3366-7777.

**ICICI dormant account still receives salary — can I continue like this?**
Not recommended — your account continues to receive, but you can't access funds. You're also accumulating risk. Reactivate immediately — takes 1 day at branch.

**ICICI charged ₹590/year maintenance fees on dormant account — valid?**
ICICI can deduct maintenance charges even on dormant accounts. Check ICICI's schedule of charges. Request waiver at branch if account became dormant due to circumstances beyond your control.

**Will ICICI close my dormant account without notice?**
ICICI typically doesn't close dormant accounts with a balance. Zero-balance dormant accounts may be marked for closure after extended dormancy. Reactivate to avoid any risk.

**ICICI dormant account reactivation — how many days does it take?**
Branch reactivation: Same day (when you do a cash transaction at counter). App/net banking: 1–3 working days. Timeline depends on whether KYC needs updating simultaneously.
