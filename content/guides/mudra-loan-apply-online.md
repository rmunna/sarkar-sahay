# Mudra Loan Apply Online: How to Apply for PMMY Mudra Yojana Loan

The **Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY)** provides loans up to ₹10 lakh to small and micro businesses across India. Whether you run a shop, a manufacturing unit, or a service business, you can apply for a Mudra loan through banks, NBFCs, and MFIs — both online and offline.

This guide covers the complete application process, eligibility criteria, and tips to improve your chances of approval.

---

## What is Mudra Loan?

**MUDRA** stands for **Micro Units Development and Refinance Agency Ltd.** It was set up by the Government of India to fund and promote micro enterprises. Under PMMY, loans are provided in three categories:

| Category | Loan Amount |
|----------|-------------|
| **Shishu** | Up to ₹50,000 |
| **Kishore** | ₹50,001 to ₹5,00,000 |
| **Tarun** | ₹5,00,001 to ₹10,00,000 |

👉 Learn more about each type in our [Mudra Loan Types: Shishu, Kishore, Tarun](/guide/mudra-loan-types-shishu-kishore-tarun) guide.

---

## Who Can Apply for Mudra Loan?

### Eligibility Criteria

- Any Indian citizen running or planning to start a **non-farm, non-corporate small/micro enterprise**
- Individuals, proprietorships, partnerships, LLPs, and private limited companies
- Business should be in **manufacturing, trading, services, or allied agriculture activities**
- No minimum turnover or income requirement for Shishu loans
- Existing businesses and new entrepreneurs are both eligible

### Who Cannot Apply?

- Corporate entities above MSME limits
- Businesses already defaulting on bank loans
- Agricultural farming activities (crop loans are excluded)

> 💡 **Tip:** If you're a startup, also check [Startup India Registration](/guide/startup-india-registration) for additional benefits and funding options.

## How to Apply for Mudra Loan Online

### Method 1: Apply via Udyami Mitra Portal

1. Visit [udyamimitra.in](https://udyamimitra.in/)
2. Click **"Apply for Loan"**
3. Register with your mobile number and verify OTP
4. Fill in business details — type, sector, turnover, loan amount needed
5. Upload [required documents](/guide/mudra-loan-documents-required)
6. Submit the application
7. You will receive offers from multiple lenders
8. Choose a lender and complete the process

### Method 2: Apply via PSB Loans in 59 Minutes

1. Go to [psbloansin59minutes.com](https://psbloansin59minutes.com/)
2. Register and link your PAN, Aadhaar, and bank account
3. Select **"Mudra Loan"** as product type
4. Fill the application and upload documents
5. Get in-principle approval within 59 minutes
6. Visit the assigned bank branch to complete formalities

### Method 3: Apply via Jan Samarth Portal

1. Visit [jansamarth.in](https://www.jansamarth.in/)
2. Select **"PMMY - Mudra Loan"**
3. Fill in the application form with personal and business details
4. Upload documents and submit
5. Track status through the portal dashboard

### Method 4: Apply Directly at Bank Branch

1. Visit the nearest branch of any public sector bank, private bank, or eligible NBFC/MFI
2. Ask for **Mudra Loan application form**
3. Fill the form and attach required documents
4. Submit and collect the acknowledgement receipt
5. Bank will process within 7–14 working days

You will need identity proof, address proof, business proof, and financial documents. The exact list varies by loan category.

- Business plan (for Kishore and Tarun)

## Processing Time and Loan Disbursement

| Step | Timeline |
|------|----------|
| Application submission | Day 1 |
| Document verification | 3–7 working days |
| Credit appraisal | 5–10 working days |
| Sanction and disbursement | 7–14 working days total |

- **Shishu loans** are typically processed faster (within a week)
- **Kishore and Tarun** loans may take 2–3 weeks
- Online applications through PSB 59 minutes get in-principle approval the same day

## Tips to Get Your Mudra Loan Approved

1. **Maintain a good CIBIL score** — 650+ improves chances significantly
2. **Prepare a clear business plan** — especially for Kishore/Tarun category
3. **Keep clean bank statements** — regular transactions, no bounced cheques
4. **Apply with complete documents** — incomplete applications cause delays
5. **Register on Udyam** — having an [MSME Udyam Registration](/guide/msme-udyam-registration) strengthens your application
6. **Start with Shishu** — if you're a first-time borrower, start small and build a repayment track record

## Interest Rates on Mudra Loans

Mudra loan [interest rates](/guide/mudra-loan-interest-rate-emi) are not fixed by the government — each bank sets its own rate. Generally:

- **Shishu:** 10%–12% per annum
- **Kishore:** 11%–14% per annum
- **Tarun:** 12%–16% per annum

No processing fee is charged for Shishu loans. Higher categories may have 0.5%–1% processing fee.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is there any collateral required for Mudra loan?

No. Mudra loans up to ₹10 lakh are **collateral-free**. Banks cannot ask for any security or guarantee for PMMY loans.

### Can I apply for Mudra loan if I don't have a business yet?

Yes. New entrepreneurs can apply for Mudra loans. You need to submit a business plan detailing what you intend to do with the funds.

### Which banks offer Mudra loans?

All public sector banks, most private banks, regional rural banks (RRBs), small finance banks, NBFCs, and MFIs offer Mudra loans. SBI, PNB, Bank of Baroda, Canara Bank, and HDFC are popular choices.

### Can I get a Mudra loan if my CIBIL score is low?

For **Shishu** loans (up to ₹50,000), CIBIL score requirements are relaxed. For Kishore and Tarun, a score of 650+ is generally expected. If your loan was [rejected due to low CIBIL](/guide/mudra-loan-rejected-fix), you can work on improving your score and reapply.

### Is Mudra loan available for women entrepreneurs?

Yes. Women entrepreneurs are given priority under PMMY. Some banks offer reduced interest rates for women borrowers.

### Can I take a second Mudra loan?

Yes, if you have repaid or are regularly repaying your first Mudra loan, you can apply for a second loan — even in a higher category (e.g., Shishu to Kishore).

### Is there a subsidy on Mudra loan?

No direct subsidy is provided on Mudra loans. However, interest rates are kept affordable and no collateral is needed, making it effectively subsidised for small borrowers.
