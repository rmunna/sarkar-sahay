# JEE Main 2026 Exam Guide — Engineering Entrance Exam

JEE Main is India's biggest engineering entrance exam. Every year, lakhs of students appear for this exam to get admission into NITs, IIITs, GFTIs and other top engineering colleges. It is also the gateway to JEE Advanced, which is required for IIT admission.

This guide covers everything you need to know about JEE Main 2026 — eligibility, exam pattern, syllabus, how to apply, preparation strategy and counselling process.

---

## What is JEE Main?

JEE Main (Joint Entrance Examination Main) is a national-level engineering entrance exam conducted by the **National Testing Agency (NTA)**. It serves two main purposes:

1. **Direct admission** to NITs, IIITs and Government Funded Technical Institutions (GFTIs) based on JEE Main rank.
2. **Eligibility for JEE Advanced** — only the top 2,50,000 JEE Main qualifiers can appear for JEE Advanced (for IIT admission).

JEE Main is conducted in two sessions each year (January and April). You can appear in one or both sessions, and your **best score** is considered for ranking.

### JEE Main Papers

| Paper | For Admission To |
|-------|-----------------|
| Paper 1 | B.E. / B.Tech programmes |
| Paper 2A | B.Arch programmes |
| Paper 2B | B.Planning programmes |

Most students appear for **Paper 1 (B.E./B.Tech)**, which is the focus of this guide.

## Eligibility Criteria

### Age Limit
- **No age limit** for appearing in JEE Main (as per recent NTA guidelines). However, individual institutions may have their own age criteria for admission.

### Educational Qualification
- You must have passed **Class 12 (or equivalent)** with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects, along with one of: Chemistry, Biology, Biotechnology, or Technical Vocational Subject.
- Students appearing for Class 12 exams in 2026 are also eligible.

### Number of Attempts
- You can attempt JEE Main for **3 consecutive years** from the year of passing Class 12.
- Both sessions in a year count as a single attempt.

### Qualifying Marks
- There is no minimum percentage required in Class 12 to appear for JEE Main. However, for admission to NITs/IIITs through JoSAA, you typically need **75% in Class 12** (or top 20 percentile in your board).

> **Note:** Always check the latest NTA notification for the most current eligibility criteria, as rules may change each year.

## Exam Pattern

JEE Main Paper 1 (B.E./B.Tech) is a **Computer Based Test (CBT)** conducted online.

### Structure

| Subject | Section A (MCQ) | Section B (Numerical) | Total Questions | To Attempt |
|---------|----------------|----------------------|----------------|------------|
| Physics | 20 | 10 | 30 | 25 |
| Chemistry | 20 | 10 | 30 | 25 |
| Mathematics | 20 | 10 | 30 | 25 |
| **Total** | **60** | **30** | **90** | **75** |

### Key Details
- **Duration:** 3 hours (180 minutes)
- **Total Marks:** 300
- **Section A (MCQ):** 4 marks for correct answer, **−1 for wrong answer**
- **Section B (Numerical):** 4 marks for correct answer, **no negative marking**
- **Language:** English, Hindi, and other regional languages (based on exam city)
- **Mode:** Online (Computer Based Test) only

In Section B, you need to attempt only **5 out of 10** numerical value questions per subject.

## Syllabus Overview

The JEE Main syllabus is based on the **NCERT Class 11 and 12** curriculum. Here is a topic-wise overview:

### Physics
- Mechanics (Laws of Motion, Work-Energy, Rotational Motion)
- Thermodynamics and Kinetic Theory
- Electrostatics and Current Electricity
- Magnetism and Electromagnetic Induction
- Optics (Ray and Wave)
- Modern Physics (Atoms, Nuclei, Semiconductors)
- Waves and Oscillations

### Chemistry
- **Physical Chemistry:** Mole Concept, Thermodynamics, Equilibrium, Electrochemistry, Chemical Kinetics
- **Organic Chemistry:** Hydrocarbons, Haloalkanes, Alcohols, Aldehydes, Amines, Polymers
- **Inorganic Chemistry:** Periodic Table, Chemical Bonding, Coordination Compounds, s/p/d-block Elements

### Mathematics
- Algebra (Complex Numbers, Matrices, Sequences & Series, Permutations)
- Calculus (Limits, Differentiation, Integration, Differential Equations)
- Coordinate Geometry (Straight Lines, Circles, Conics)
- Trigonometry
- Vectors and 3D Geometry
- Statistics and Probability

> **Tip:** NCERT textbooks should be your first priority. Most JEE Main questions are directly based on NCERT concepts.

## How to Apply

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Visit** the official NTA JEE Main website: [jeemain.nta.nic.in](https://jeemain.nta.nic.in)
2. **Register** with your email ID and mobile number
3. **Fill the application form** with personal, educational and exam details
4. **Upload documents** — passport-size photo, signature, Class 10 & 12 certificates
5. **Choose exam cities** (you can select up to 4 preferences)
6. **Pay the application fee** online

### Application Fee (Approximate)
- **General/OBC:** ₹1,000 (for one session)
- **SC/ST/PwD/Female:** ₹500 (for one session)

> Fees may vary. Check the official notification for exact amounts.

### Important Dates (Tentative for 2026)
- **Session 1:** January 2026
- **Session 2:** April 2026
- Application forms are usually released 2-3 months before the exam.

## Preparation Strategy

### 6-Month Plan

**Months 1-2: Build Foundations**
- Complete NCERT textbooks for all three subjects
- Make short notes and formula sheets
- Solve NCERT back exercises thoroughly

**Months 3-4: Practice and Deepen**
- Start solving previous year JEE Main papers (at least 10 years)
- Use reference books like HC Verma (Physics), MS Chouhan (Organic Chemistry), RD Sharma (Maths)
- Focus on weak topics — give extra time where needed

**Months 5-6: Mock Tests and Revision**
- Take full-length mock tests (at least 2 per week)
- Analyse each test — identify mistakes and revise those topics
- Revise formula sheets and short notes daily
- Solve NTA Abhyas app questions for practice

### Subject-Wise Tips

- **Physics:** Focus on understanding concepts, not just formulas. Numericals are very important.
- **Chemistry:** NCERT is king, especially for Inorganic and Organic Chemistry. Physical Chemistry needs regular practice.
- **Mathematics:** Practice is everything. Solve as many problems as you can daily.

### Best Resources
- NCERT Textbooks (Class 11 & 12)
- Previous Year Papers (freely available on NTA website)
- NTA Abhyas App (free mock tests)
- Online platforms like Unacademy, Physics Wallah, BYJU'S

## Counselling Process (JoSAA)

After JEE Main results are declared, admissions to **NITs, IIITs and GFTIs** happen through the **Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA)**.

### How JoSAA Counselling Works

1. **Register on JoSAA portal:** [josaa.nic.in](https://josaa.nic.in)
2. **Fill your choices** — select colleges and branches in order of preference
3. **Seat allotment** happens in multiple rounds (usually 5-6 rounds)
4. **Accept the seat** — pay the seat acceptance fee and report to the allotted college
5. **Document verification** at the reporting centre

### Key Points
- JoSAA counselling is **completely online** (choice filling and seat allotment)
- You must **report physically** to the allotted institute for document verification
- Carry all original documents: Class 10 & 12 marksheets, category certificate, JEE Main scorecard, photo ID
- If not satisfied with the allotted seat, you can **float** (keep current seat and wait for upgrades in next round) or **freeze** (accept and exit)

### CSAB Counselling
After JoSAA rounds, the **Central Seat Allocation Board (CSAB)** conducts additional rounds for vacant seats in NITs and IIITs.

## Important Tips

1. **Start with NCERT** — Don't jump to advanced books before mastering NCERT concepts. Most JEE Main questions come directly from NCERT.

2. **Take mock tests regularly** — Mock tests improve time management and help you get comfortable with the online exam format.

3. **Don't ignore negative marking** — In MCQ sections, avoid random guessing. If you can eliminate 2 options, then attempt the question.

4. **Focus on Section B** — Numerical value questions have no negative marking. Even partial attempts can boost your score.

5. **Keep Class 12 boards in mind** — JEE Main and board exams have overlapping syllabi. Preparing well for one helps with the other.

6. **Stay healthy and consistent** — Regular study (6-8 hours daily) is better than last-minute marathon sessions. Sleep well and eat right.

7. **Revise regularly** — Make a revision schedule. Revise formulas and key concepts every week.

8. **Use both sessions wisely** — Appear in both January and April sessions. Treat January as a practice run if needed.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Can I appear for JEE Main in both sessions?
Yes, you can appear in both Session 1 (January) and Session 2 (April). Your best score will be used for ranking.

### 2. Is there any age limit for JEE Main?
No, NTA has removed the upper age limit. However, check individual institute requirements for admission.

### 3. How many times can I attempt JEE Main?
You can attempt JEE Main for 3 consecutive years from the year you pass Class 12.

### 4. What is the minimum percentage needed in Class 12?
There is no minimum percentage to appear for JEE Main. But for NIT/IIIT admission via JoSAA, you generally need 75% in boards (or top 20 percentile).

### 5. Is JEE Main enough for NIT admission?
Yes, JEE Main score is used for admission to NITs, IIITs and GFTIs through JoSAA counselling. You do not need JEE Advanced for these colleges.

### 6. What is the difference between JEE Main and JEE Advanced?
JEE Main is for NITs/IIITs/GFTIs. JEE Advanced is for IITs only. You must qualify JEE Main first to be eligible for JEE Advanced.

### 7. Can I change my exam city after submitting the form?
NTA usually provides a correction window where you can modify certain details including exam city preferences. Check the official website for dates.
