## What is Krishak Bandhu?

**Krishak Bandhu** — meaning "Friend of Farmers" — is the West Bengal government's flagship agricultural support scheme providing **₹10,000 per year** to all land-owning farmers as direct farm investment assistance — without any income limit.

Additionally, Krishak Bandhu provides **death-cum-accidental insurance** of ₹2 lakh per enrolled farmer.

## Benefits

| Benefit | Details |
|---|---|
| **Farm assistance** | ₹10,000/year per family (₹5,000 per season in 2 installments) |
| **Death insurance** | ₹2 lakh for natural death OR accidental death of the farmer |
| **Seasonal payment** | Kharif: April–May; Rabi: October–November |

## Who is Eligible?

- **All land-owning farmers** in West Bengal with agricultural land
- **Sharecroppers (bargadars)** registered with WB Agriculture Department
- Land size: **Minimum 1 bigha** (approximately 0.33 acre) to qualify
- **No income limit** — all farmers regardless of size of holding
- Farmer must be 18–60 years for the insurance component

**Not eligible:**
- Government employees
- Income tax payers

## How to Apply

### Through Agriculture Department Office
Most Krishak Bandhu enrollments are done through:
1. Visit your local **Block Agriculture Office** or **GP-level Agriculture Extension Officer**
2. Show land records (record of rights) and Aadhaar
3. Get enrolled in the Krishak Bandhu beneficiary list

### Online Check/Application
1. Go to **[krishakbandhu.net](https://krishakbandhu.net)**
2. Check if your name is already in the beneficiary list
3. If not, contact your Block Agriculture Office for enrollment

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card**
2. **Land records** (Record of Rights / Khatiyan / Khatian with your name as landowner or registered sharecropper)
3. **Bank passbook** (Aadhaar-linked)
4. For sharecroppers: **Barga certificate** or registered tenancy documents

## Krishak Bandhu Insurance — Claiming ₹2 Lakh

In case of farmer's death:
1. Family members should inform the local **Block Agriculture Office** within the specified time (typically 60 days)
2. Submit: Death certificate, farmer's Aadhaar/Krishak Bandhu ID, insurance claim form
3. The insurance amount (₹2 lakh) is paid to the nominee/legal heir

## Checking Payment Status

1. Go to [krishakbandhu.net](https://krishakbandhu.net)
2. Enter your **Krishak Bandhu number** or Aadhaar
3. View payment history for Kharif and Rabi seasons

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I am a farmer but my land is very small (less than 1 bigha). Am I eligible?
Minimum land size for Krishak Bandhu is typically 1 bigha. Farmers with smaller plots may be enrolled under certain state provisions — check with your local Block Agriculture Officer.

### I am a sharecropper (bargadar) but my registration is old. Is it still valid?
Registered sharecroppers are eligible. Bring your Barga certificate to the Block Agriculture Office to confirm and update your Krishak Bandhu enrollment.

### My name appeared in the old beneficiary list but I'm not getting payments. What do I do?
Visit your Block Agriculture Office with your Krishak Bandhu ID/Aadhaar. Check if your bank account is correctly linked and Aadhaar-seeded. Payments may be on hold due to bank linkage issues.

### The insurance amount of ₹2 lakh — who gets it?
The nominated family member (spouse/child/parent) receives the insurance amount. If no nominee was registered, the legal heir (as per inheritance law) receives it through a claim process.

### Can I get both Krishak Bandhu and PM-Kisan simultaneously?
Yes — Krishak Bandhu is a state scheme; PM-Kisan is central. Both provide separate farm support. A West Bengal farmer can receive PM-Kisan ₹6,000/year AND Krishak Bandhu ₹10,000/year.
