## What is CM Swarozgar Yojana UP?

**Mukhyamantri Swarozgar Yojana (CMSY)** is the Uttar Pradesh government scheme providing **loans with margin money (subsidy)** to unemployed educated youth for starting self-employment ventures in manufacturing, service, or trading. It is similar in structure to Yuva Swarozgar Yojana but covers a broader age range and has different subsidy tiers.

## Loan Details

| Category | Max Loan | Margin Money (Subsidy) |
|---|---|---|
| General / OBC | **₹10 lakh** | 25% of project cost |
| SC / ST | **₹10 lakh** | **50% of project cost** |
| Women | **₹10 lakh** | **35% of project cost** |
| Ex-servicemen | **₹10 lakh** | **25% of project cost** |

**Margin money** = subsidy deposited by government into your loan account, reducing repayment.

## Key Features

- No collateral for loans up to ₹10 lakh (CGTMSE guarantee)
- Loan processed through **nationalized banks, RRBs, cooperative banks**
- Can cover: manufacturing, service, and trading businesses

## Who is Eligible?

- **Age:** 18 to 55 years
- **Education:** Minimum **Class 8 pass**
- **Residency:** Uttar Pradesh permanent resident
- **Employment status:** Unemployed or self-employed with no regular job
- Not a defaulter on any previous government loan
- **One scheme per family** (cannot have availed similar UP government loans earlier)

### Priority Categories
- **SC/ST applicants** — higher subsidy (50%)
- **Women applicants** — 35% subsidy
- **Ex-servicemen** — preference in selection
- **Minorities** — preference under specific notified sub-schemes

## What Businesses Are Covered?

**Manufacturing:**
- Furniture making, bamboo products
- Garment stitching, weaving
- Food processing (pickles, papad, dairy)
- Pottery, handicrafts

**Service:**
- Salons, tailoring shops
- Auto repair, welding workshop
- Computer/mobile repair, DTH/cable service
- Driving school, tour & travel

**Trading:**
- Grocery/general store
- Agricultural input shop
- Electronics retail

## How to Apply

1. Go to **[diupmsme.upsdc.gov.in](https://diupmsme.upsdc.gov.in)**
2. Click **"आवेदन करें"** under Mukhyamantri Swarozgar Yojana
3. Register with Aadhaar + mobile OTP
4. Fill in:
   - Personal details and education
   - Business type and project details
   - Project cost estimate
   - Preferred bank
5. Upload documents
6. Submit → note Application ID

### After Submission
- District Industries Centre (DIC) verifies and approves
- Application forwarded to selected bank
- Bank evaluates and sanctions loan
- Margin money released by government upon loan disbursement

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card**
2. **Class 8/10 certificate** (education proof)
3. **UP residence proof** (Aadhaar or voter ID with UP address)
4. **Caste certificate** (SC/ST/OBC for applicable subsidy)
5. **Affidavit of unemployment** (that applicant has no regular job)
6. **Business plan** (1–2 page description of business, cost estimate)
7. **Bank account** in own name
8. Passport-size photograph

## CMSY vs MYSY (Yuva Swarozgar Yojana) — Comparison

| Feature | CM Swarozgar (CMSY) | Yuva Swarozgar (MYSY) |
|---|---|---|
| Age limit | 18–55 years | 18–40 years |
| Education | Class 8 pass | Class 10 pass |
| Max loan | ₹10 lakh | ₹25 lakh (industry) |
| SC/ST subsidy | 50% | 25% |
| Focus | Broader self-employment | Youth entrepreneurship |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I am 52 years old and want to open a tailoring shop. Am I eligible?
Yes — CMSY covers ages up to 55 years. A tailoring shop falls under the service sector. You would need to show you are not in regular employment.

### Can a woman get both CMSY and another UP women's scheme?
You can access CMSY for a business loan. State women's skill/SHG schemes are separate. However, you cannot combine two government loans for the same business.

### The DIC approved my application but the bank rejected the loan. What can I do?
Visit your DIC office with the rejection letter. The DIC can help escalate with the bank, suggest an alternative bank, or issue a fresh referral. Under government schemes, banks are expected to give serious consideration to DIC-recommended applications.

### Is there an annual income limit for CMSY applicants?
There is no specific income cap mentioned — the eligibility is based on unemployment/self-employment status. The scheme is not exclusively for BPL families.
