# EPF Withdrawal Not Received? Track PF Claim Status (2026)

You filed an EPF withdrawal claim, the status shows "Settled" on the EPFO portal, but the money hasn't reached your bank account. This is a common problem faced by lakhs of EPF members. Here's how to track and fix it.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent platform and is not affiliated with EPFO.

## Why EPF Withdrawal Money Isn't Received

| Reason | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Bank account mismatch | Name or account number in EPFO doesn't match bank records |
| IFSC code changed | Bank merged or branch IFSC changed |
| Bank returned the transfer | Account closed, dormant, or KYC incomplete |
| NEFT/RTGS processing delay | Transfer pending at bank end |
| Wrong bank details in UAN | Incorrect account number linked to UAN |
| PF office processing delay | Claim approved but payment not yet initiated |

## Step 1: Check Your Claim Status

### On EPFO Member Portal:
1. Visit [unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in)
2. Login with UAN and password
3. Go to **Online Services → Track Claim Status**
4. Your claim will show one of these statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Under Process | Claim received, being reviewed |
| Settled | Claim approved and payment initiated |
| Payment Under Process | Money sent to bank, awaiting credit |
| Rejected | Claim rejected — check reason |
| Returned by Bank | Bank rejected the transfer |

### Via UMANG App:
1. Open UMANG app → EPFO → Track Claim
2. Enter UAN number
3. View current claim status

### Via SMS:
Send SMS: `EPFOHO UAN` to **7738299899**

## Step 2: If Status Shows "Settled"

"Settled" means EPFO has approved the claim and sent the money. If you haven't received it:

1. **Wait 3-5 working days** — bank processing takes time after EPFO transfers
2. **Check the bank account linked to your UAN** — login to EPFO portal → Manage → KYC → Check bank details
3. **Verify the amount** — check if partial amount was credited (TDS may be deducted)
4. **Contact your bank:**
   - Call customer care with the EPFO reference number
   - Ask if any NEFT/RTGS credit is pending or returned
   - Check if your account has any hold or freeze

## Step 3: If Money Was Returned by Bank

This is the most common reason. Money gets returned to EPFO when:

- Account number is wrong
- Account name doesn't match (spelling difference)
- Account is closed or dormant
- Bank IFSC code has changed (bank mergers)
- Account is not KYC-compliant

### How to Fix:

1. Login to EPFO portal → **Manage → KYC**
2. **Verify your bank account details** are correct
3. If wrong, update bank details:
   - Add correct bank account
   - Wait for employer/EPFO to approve
   - Resubmit the claim
4. If bank account was correct but still returned:
   - Contact bank to ensure account is active
   - Complete bank KYC if pending
   - Ask bank to accept the incoming NEFT transfer

## Step 4: File a Grievance on EPFO

If money isn't received within 10 working days of "Settled" status:

### Via EPF iGMS (Grievance Portal):
1. Visit [epfigms.gov.in](https://epfigms.gov.in)
2. Click **"Register Grievance"**
3. Select grievance type: **"PF Withdrawal / Settlement"**
4. Enter your UAN, PF account number, and claim details
5. Describe the issue clearly
6. Upload bank statement showing no credit received
7. Submit and note the **grievance registration number**
8. **Response time:** 7-15 working days

### Via EPFO Helpline:
- Call **1800-118-005** (toll-free)
- Call **14470** (PF helpline)
- Provide your UAN and claim reference number

### Via Email:
Email your regional EPFO office. Find the email at [epfindia.gov.in/site_en/Contact.php](https://www.epfindia.gov.in/site_en/Contact.php)

## Step 5: Escalate If No Response

If the grievance isn't resolved within 15 days:

1. **Re-escalate on EPFiGMS** — mark it as "Not Satisfied" and escalate
2. **Write to CPFC** (Central Provident Fund Commissioner) at cpfc@epfindia.gov.in
3. **Tweet to @aboraborabor** (EPF India official handle) with your grievance number
4. **File on CPGRAMS** — [pgportal.gov.in](https://pgportal.gov.in) under Ministry of Labour
5. **Visit your regional EPFO office** in person with all documents

## Step 6: Check for TDS Deduction

If you received less money than expected:

| Situation | TDS Rate |
|-----------|---------|
| Withdrawal before 5 years of service | 10% TDS (if PAN linked) |
| Withdrawal before 5 years, no PAN | 20% TDS |
| Withdrawal after 5 years | No TDS |
| Amount less than ₹50,000 | No TDS |

If TDS was deducted, you can claim it back while filing your Income Tax Return.

## How Long Does EPF Withdrawal Take?

| Stage | Timeline |
|-------|---------|
| Claim submission to approval | 3-10 working days |
| Approved to bank credit | 3-5 working days |
| Total end-to-end | 7-15 working days |
| If bank returns money | Add 15-30 more days after correction |

## Tips to Avoid EPF Withdrawal Delays

1. **Verify bank details** in your UAN profile before filing claim
2. **Link Aadhaar to UAN** — mandatory for online claims
3. **Ensure bank account name matches** your EPF records exactly
4. **Use the same bank account** that's verified in your UAN KYC
5. **Complete Form 15G/15H** to avoid TDS deduction (if eligible)
6. **Keep your UAN activated** and mobile number updated
7. **File claim online** — faster than physical submission

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does EPF withdrawal take after claim settled?
After claim status shows "Settled," money is typically credited to your bank account within 3-5 working days. If not received in 10 days, file a grievance on epfigms.gov.in.

### Why is my EPF claim settled but money not received?
Most common reasons: wrong bank account details in UAN, bank account inactive/frozen, IFSC code changed due to bank merger, or bank KYC incomplete. Verify bank details and contact your bank.

### How to track EPF withdrawal status?
Login to unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in → Online Services → Track Claim Status. You can also use UMANG app or send SMS "EPFOHO UAN" to 7738299899.

### What if EPF money is returned by bank?
Update your correct bank details on the EPFO portal, ensure the account is active, and resubmit the claim. Contact your bank to ensure they accept incoming NEFT transfers.

### Can I file EPF withdrawal claim again after rejection?
Yes, you can resubmit the claim after fixing the rejection reason. Common reasons include Aadhaar-UAN mismatch, incomplete KYC, or wrong bank details. Fix the issue and reapply.

### How to contact EPFO about pending withdrawal?
Call 1800-118-005 (toll-free) or 14470, file grievance on epfigms.gov.in, email your regional EPFO office, or visit the EPFO office in person.

### Is there any time limit for EPF withdrawal?
There's no time limit for EPF withdrawal. You can claim your PF balance anytime after leaving employment. However, unclaimed EPF earns interest only for 3 years after inactivity.

### Will I get interest on delayed EPF withdrawal?
EPFO pays interest on your PF balance until the claim is settled. However, no additional interest is paid for bank-side delays after settlement.
