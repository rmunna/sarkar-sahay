## What is a Domicile/Residence Certificate in Karnataka?

A **Domicile Certificate** (also called Residence Certificate) is an official document issued by the **Revenue Department of Karnataka** that certifies a person is a permanent resident of the state. It is issued by the **Tahsildar** of the respective taluk.

In Karnataka, this certificate is primarily issued through the **Nadakacheri portal** (for Atalji Janasnehi Kendra / Bangalore One / Nadakacheri centres) and the **Seva Sindhu portal** for online applications.

### Why Do You Need It?

- **Government jobs** — required for Karnataka state quota reservations
- **Education** — admission to state universities and colleges under domicile quota
- **Scholarships** — many Karnataka scholarships require proof of residence
- **Government schemes** — state welfare schemes like [Gruha Lakshmi](/guide/karnataka-gruha-lakshmi-scheme), [Yuva Nidhi](/guide/karnataka-yuva-nidhi-unemployment-allowance), and [Vidyasiri Scholarship](/guide/karnataka-vidyasiri-scholarship) require domicile proof
- **Property transactions** — some land-related processes require domicile verification

## Who is Eligible?

You can apply for a Karnataka Domicile/Residence Certificate if:

1. You are a **permanent resident of Karnataka** (born in Karnataka or residing continuously)
2. You have been **residing in Karnataka for a minimum period** (typically 7+ years for domicile, or 1+ year for residence certificate — varies by purpose)
3. You have **valid address proof** showing Karnataka residence

> **Note:** The eligibility criteria and minimum residence period may vary depending on whether you need a domicile certificate or a residence certificate. See our [Domicile vs Residence Certificate guide](/guide/domicile-vs-residence-certificate) for differences.

## Step-by-Step Online Process via Nadakacheri

The **Nadakacheri portal** is Karnataka's dedicated platform for revenue department certificates.

### Step 1: Visit Nadakacheri Portal

Go to **[nadakacheri.karnataka.gov.in](https://nadakacheri.karnataka.gov.in)**

### Step 2: Register / Login

- Click on **"Citizen Login"** or **"New User Registration"**
- Register using your **mobile number and Aadhaar**
- Complete OTP verification and create your account

### Step 3: Select Certificate Type

- After login, select **"Apply for Certificate"**
- Choose **"Residence Certificate"** or **"Domicile Certificate"** from the dropdown
- Select your **District, Taluk, and Village/Ward**

### Step 4: Fill Application Form

- Enter personal details — name, father's/mother's name, date of birth, address
- Fill in the **duration of residence** in Karnataka
- Provide **purpose** for which the certificate is required

### Step 5: Upload Documents

- Upload scanned copies of all required documents
- Accepted formats: **PDF, JPG, PNG** (file size typically under 2 MB each)
- Ensure documents are **clearly readable**

### Step 6: Pay Fees

- Pay the application fee online via **net banking, debit card, credit card, or UPI**
- Download the **payment receipt** for reference

### Step 7: Submit Application

- Review all details carefully
- Click **"Submit"**
- Note down the **Application Reference Number** for tracking

### Step 8: Track and Download

- Track your application at the Nadakacheri portal using your reference number
- Once approved, **download the digitally signed certificate** from the portal
- The certificate can also be verified online using the certificate number

## Alternative: Apply via Seva Sindhu Portal

You can also apply through **[sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in)**, Karnataka's integrated services portal.

1. Visit Seva Sindhu and click **"Apply for Services"**
2. Select **Revenue Department → Residence/Domicile Certificate**
3. Login using your **Aadhaar-linked mobile number**
4. Fill the application form and upload documents
5. Pay fees and submit
6. Track using the application ID on the Seva Sindhu dashboard

## Offline Process

If you prefer the offline method:

1. Visit your nearest **Nadakacheri centre, Atalji Janasnehi Kendra, or Bangalore One centre**
2. Collect the application form (or download from nadakacheri.karnataka.gov.in)
3. Fill the form and attach **self-attested photocopies** of all documents
4. Submit at the counter and collect the **acknowledgement slip**
5. The Tahsildar's office will verify your details (may include field verification)
6. Collect the certificate from the centre once approved

Processing time depends on verification requirements. If field verification is ordered by the Tahsildar, it may take additional time.

## How to Track Your Application

1. Visit **[nadakacheri.karnataka.gov.in](https://nadakacheri.karnataka.gov.in)**
2. Click on **"Track Application"** or **"Check Status"**
3. Enter your **Application Reference Number**
4. View the current status — Pending / Under Verification / Approved / Rejected

You can also track via the Seva Sindhu portal if you applied there.

## Important Tips

1. **Keep documents ready before starting** — incomplete applications get rejected and you'll need to reapply
2. **Ensure Aadhaar address matches** — your Aadhaar should show your Karnataka address. If not, [update your Aadhaar address](/guide/aadhaar-address-change-online) first
3. **Save your reference number** — you'll need it for tracking and downloading the certificate
4. **Digital certificates are legally valid** — the digitally signed certificate from Nadakacheri is accepted everywhere; no need for physical copies
5. **Apply well in advance** — if needed for admissions or job applications, apply at least 3–4 weeks before your deadline

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: What is the difference between a domicile certificate and a residence certificate in Karnataka?

A **domicile certificate** proves you are a permanent resident of Karnataka and is typically needed for government job reservations and educational admissions. A **residence certificate** confirms you currently live in Karnataka. For detailed differences, see our [Domicile vs Residence Certificate guide](/guide/domicile-vs-residence-certificate).

### Q2: How long is the Karnataka domicile certificate valid?

The domicile certificate issued in Karnataka is generally **valid for 3 to 5 years** from the date of issue, depending on the issuing authority. For some purposes (like education admissions), institutions may require a recently issued certificate.

### Q3: Can I apply for a domicile certificate if I was not born in Karnataka?

Yes. If you have been **continuously residing in Karnataka for the required period** (usually 7 years or more) and can provide supporting documents, you can apply for a domicile certificate regardless of your place of birth.

### Q4: Is the online domicile certificate from Nadakacheri accepted for government jobs?

Yes. The **digitally signed certificate** issued through the Nadakacheri portal is legally valid and accepted by all Karnataka government departments, educational institutions, and recruitment boards.

### Q5: What should I do if my application is rejected?

If rejected, check the **reason for rejection** on the portal. Common reasons include incomplete documents, mismatched details, or failed verification. Correct the issues and **reapply with proper documents**. You can also visit the Tahsildar office in person for clarification.

### Q6: Can NRIs or people living outside Karnataka get a domicile certificate?

If you were born in Karnataka or previously resided there, you may still be eligible. You'll need to provide **birth certificate, old address proofs, and family records** showing Karnataka connection. Contact the Tahsildar of your native place for specific requirements.

### Q7: Is Aadhaar mandatory for applying online?

Yes, **Aadhaar is mandatory** for online applications through both Nadakacheri and Seva Sindhu portals, as verification is done via Aadhaar-based OTP authentication.
