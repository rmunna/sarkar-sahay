# How to Apply for IPO Online in India 2026 — Complete Guide

Applying for an IPO takes about 5 minutes once you have a demat account. The entire process is digital — no physical forms, no cheque submission. Here's how it works.

## What You Need Before Applying

- **Demat account** — Zerodha, Groww, Angel One, HDFC Securities, SBI Cap, etc. ([Open demat account guide](/guide/demat-account-open-online-guide-india-2026))
- **UPI ID** — from any UPI app (PhonePe, BHIM, Google Pay, Paytm) or your bank's UPI
- **PAN card** — linked to your demat account
- **Bank account** — linked to your demat account; funds blocked (not debited) until allotment

---

## Step-by-Step: Apply for IPO Online (UPI Method)

### Step 1: Open Your Broker's App

Open Zerodha, Groww, Angel One, or whichever broker you use. Navigate to:
- **Zerodha**: Console → IPO
- **Groww**: Stocks → IPO
- **Angel One**: IPO section on homepage
- **Paytm Money**: Stocks → IPO

Most broker apps show the **current open IPOs** with issue dates, price band, and lot size.

### Step 2: Select the IPO and Check Details

Before applying, verify:
- **Price band**: e.g., ₹180–₹190 per share
- **Lot size**: Minimum shares per application (e.g., 75 shares = 1 lot)
- **Minimum investment**: Lot size × upper price band (e.g., 75 × ₹190 = ₹14,250)
- **IPO open/close dates**
- **Listing exchange**: NSE, BSE, or both

### Step 3: Enter Your Bid

You have two options:
- **Cut-off price**: Applies at whatever price is finally decided by the company (recommended for retail investors — you get allotment at the final price regardless of where it falls in the band)
- **Specific price**: Enter a price within the price band; if the final price is higher than your bid, your application is rejected

**For retail investors, always bid at cut-off price** unless you have a strong view on valuation.

Enter number of lots (minimum = 1 lot, maximum = 13 lots for retail category to stay within ₹2L investment limit for RII category — see below).

### Step 4: Enter UPI ID and Submit

Enter your UPI ID (e.g., `yourname@paytm`, `9876543210@ybl`) and submit the application. Your application is registered with the exchange.

**UPI limit for IPO**: ₹5 lakh per application (SEBI raised this from ₹2L in 2024 — but retail investor category has its own ₹2L investment cap for the RII segment's 35% quota benefit).

### Step 5: Approve the UPI Mandate

Within a few minutes (usually within an hour), you'll receive a **UPI payment mandate request** on your UPI app.

**Critical step**: Go to your UPI app → check pending requests → **approve the mandate**.

The mandate **blocks the funds** in your bank account — it does NOT debit them immediately. Money is only debited if you get allotment. If not allotted, the block is released on the allotment date.

**Mandate expiry**: If you don't approve within 12–24 hours (or before the IPO closes), your application is cancelled automatically.

## Retail vs HNI vs QIB — Which Category Are You?

| Category | Who | Investment Limit | IPO Quota |
|----------|-----|-----------------|-----------|
| **RII (Retail Individual Investor)** | Individuals investing ≤₹2L | ≤₹2,00,000 | 35% of issue |
| **NII (Non-Institutional / HNI)** | Individuals investing >₹2L | No limit | 15% of issue |
| **QIB (Qualified Institutional Buyer)** | Mutual funds, banks, FIIs | No limit | 50% of issue |

Most people apply under **RII** — the ₹2L cap means maximum 13 lots typically (exact lot count depends on lot size × price band).

**Applying above ₹2L moves you to NII**: higher investment, lower allotment probability (NII allotment is proportional, not lottery-based).

## IPO Allotment — How It Works

**Oversubscribed IPOs (most popular IPOs)**: SEBI mandates **1 lot per applicant** allotment in the retail category when oversubscribed. Your number of lots doesn't increase chances — applying 13 lots gives same chance as 1 lot. Only the number of unique applications (PAN cards) matters.

**Under-subscribed IPOs**: All applicants get full allotment.

**Allotment date**: Usually 6–7 days after the IPO closes.

### How to Check IPO Allotment Status

**Method 1 — Registrar's Website**

Find the registrar in the IPO's red herring prospectus. Most IPOs use:
- **Link Intime** (Linkintime): [linkintime.co.in](https://linkintime.co.in) → IPO → Allotment Status
- **KFin Technologies** (KFintech): [kfintech.com](https://kfintech.com) → Services → Allotment Status
- **Bigshare Services**: [bigshareonline.com](https://www.bigshareonline.com)

Enter: PAN number or Application number or DPID/Client ID → Submit

**Method 2 — BSE Website**
- [bseindia.com](https://www.bseindia.com) → Investors → Application Status

**Method 3 — Broker App**
Zerodha Console, Groww, and most brokers show allotment status directly in the IPO section.

## IPO Refund — When Does It Come?

- **If not allotted**: UPI mandate block is released on allotment date itself (T+6). Funds immediately available in your bank account.
- **If partially allotted** (NII category): Excess amount unblocked on allotment date.
- **No action needed from you** — refund is automatic via UPI unblock.

If funds are not released within 2–3 working days of allotment date, contact your broker.

## Key IPO Terms Explained

### GMP (Grey Market Premium)

GMP is the unofficial premium at which IPO shares trade before listing. It's not on any official platform — it's shared on Telegram groups, websites like investorgain.com, and YouTube channels.

**GMP of ₹50 on ₹190 issue price**: Expected listing around ₹240 (+26%). Not a guarantee. GMP can collapse on listing day if sentiment shifts.

**Don't make investment decisions purely based on GMP.** High GMP means high demand — but it's also an incentive for manipulators to hype GMP to create artificial demand.

### Cut-off Price

The final price at which the company issues shares, decided after the book-building process closes. Retail investors who bid at "cut-off price" are allocated shares at this price, regardless of where in the band it falls.

### Price Band

The range within which bids are accepted. E.g., ₹180–₹190. Company and investment bankers set this. Final price is at or below the upper end of the band (can be the exact upper end for most oversubscribed IPOs).

### Lot Size

Minimum number of shares per IPO application. SEBI mandates the minimum investment per lot to be approximately ₹13,000–₹15,000. If lot size is 75 and upper price band is ₹190: 1 lot = ₹14,250.

### SME IPO vs Mainboard IPO

**Mainboard IPO**: Listed on NSE/BSE, larger companies (≥₹10 crore post-issue net worth)
**SME IPO**: Listed on NSE Emerge or BSE SME, smaller companies, lower liquidity, often more speculative

Beginners should stick to mainboard IPOs. SME IPOs have fewer disclosures and much lower trading volumes post-listing.

## ASBA vs UPI — What's the Difference?

| Feature | ASBA (via bank) | UPI Method |
|---------|-----------------|------------|
| **Where** | Bank's net banking / branch | Broker app + UPI app |
| **How funds work** | Blocked in savings account | Blocked via UPI mandate |
| **Convenience** | Slightly cumbersome | Much faster (recommended) |
| **Who uses it** | HNI applicants (>₹2L) | Retail investors |

For retail investors, UPI method through broker apps is the standard. ASBA via bank branch is rarely used now.

## Common IPO Application Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Not Approving the UPI Mandate
Your application is void if you don't approve the mandate. Check your UPI app immediately after applying.

### Mistake 2: Entering Wrong UPI ID
Double-check your UPI ID. If it's wrong, mandate request goes to someone else or fails.

### Mistake 3: Insufficient Bank Balance
The UPI block will fail if your bank account balance is less than your application amount. Keep sufficient balance.

### Mistake 4: Multiple Applications from Same PAN
Only 1 application per PAN per IPO is allowed (across all platforms). Multiple applications get rejected.

### Mistake 5: Applying at Non-Cut-Off Price Below Final Price
If you bid at ₹185 and the final price is ₹190, your application is rejected.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I apply for IPO from Groww / Zerodha without visiting bank?

Yes. Broker apps (Groww, Zerodha, Angel One, Paytm Money) support full online IPO application via UPI. You never need to visit a bank branch.

### How much money is blocked for IPO application?

Your entire application amount is blocked in your bank account until allotment date. For a ₹14,250 application (1 lot × ₹190 × 75 shares), ₹14,250 is blocked. If not allotted, it's unblocked on allotment day.

### Can I apply for the same IPO through multiple brokers?

No. Only 1 application per PAN is allowed per IPO. Applying through multiple brokers with the same PAN results in all applications being rejected.

### When is the IPO mandate approval deadline?

Generally before the IPO closes — so if the IPO closes at 5:00 PM on the last date, approve the mandate by then. Check the exact timing in the broker app notification.

### How to check if my IPO application was submitted?

Your broker app will show the application status (Submitted / Pending Mandate / Rejected). You can also check on BSE/NSE websites using your PAN.

### Is IPO listing guaranteed to be above issue price?

No. IPOs can and do list below issue price. GMP, analyst views, and market conditions influence listing price, but none are guarantees. Don't apply to IPOs assuming listing gains are certain.

### What happens if IPO is undersubscribed?

If total applications × shares < total shares on offer, the IPO is undersubscribed. All applicants get full allotment. The company may still list, though at potentially lower prices.

### IPO refund not received — what to do?

Wait until T+6 (6 working days after subscription close). If still not received by T+8, contact your broker first, then the registrar (Linkintime/KFintech). Provide your application number and PAN.
