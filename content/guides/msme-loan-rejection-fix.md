# MSME Loan Rejected? Common Reasons & How to Fix and Reapply

Getting an MSME loan rejected can be frustrating, especially when you need funds urgently. Understanding why banks reject MSME loan applications helps you fix the issues and reapply successfully. This guide covers the most common rejection reasons and actionable fixes.

## Common Reasons for MSME Loan Rejection

### Reason 1: Low CIBIL/Credit Score

Banks check CIBIL scores for both the business owner (personal score) and the business (commercial score).

**Minimum CIBIL Score Requirements:**

| Lender Type | Minimum Score | Preferred Score |
|-------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Public Sector Banks (SBI, PNB, etc.) | 650+ | 700+ |
| Private Banks (HDFC, ICICI, etc.) | 700+ | 750+ |
| NBFCs (Bajaj, Tata Capital, etc.) | 600+ | 650+ |
| Mudra Loans (via banks) | 650+ | 700+ |

**How to Fix:**

1. **Check your CIBIL score** — Get free report from [cibil.com](https://www.cibil.com/) annually
2. **Pay off existing dues** — Clear credit card bills, EMIs, and overdue loans
3. **Dispute incorrect entries** — If your report shows wrong defaults, raise a dispute with CIBIL
4. **Build credit history** — If no credit history, take a small personal loan or credit card and repay on time
5. **Wait 3–6 months** — After clearing dues, your score takes a few months to improve
6. **Apply to NBFCs first** — They accept lower scores than banks

### Reason 2: Insufficient Turnover or Revenue

Banks assess your repayment capacity based on business turnover.

**Typical Requirements:**

- **Mudra Shishu (up to ₹50,000):** Minimal turnover proof needed
- **Mudra Kishor (₹50,000–₹5 lakh):** Annual turnover of ₹2–5 lakh+
- **Mudra Tarun (₹5–10 lakh):** Annual turnover of ₹10 lakh+
- **MSME Term Loans (₹10 lakh+):** Turnover typically 2–3x the loan amount
- **CGTMSE Loans (up to ₹5 crore):** Assessed case by case

**How to Fix:**

1. **Apply for a smaller amount** — Request a loan proportional to your demonstrated turnover
2. **Show GST returns** — Regular GST filing demonstrates consistent revenue
3. **Submit bank statements** — 12 months of healthy bank statements with regular credits strengthen your case
4. **Include all income sources** — Don't miss secondary revenue streams
5. **Wait for better financials** — If turnover genuinely increased, file updated ITR before reapplying

### Reason 3: Documentation Gaps

Incomplete or incorrect documentation is a top reason for rejection.

**Essential Documents Checklist:**

- ✅ [Udyam Registration Certificate](/guide/udyam-registration-not-working-fix) (mandatory for MSME benefits)
- ✅ PAN card (personal + business if applicable)
- ✅ [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
- ✅ Last 2–3 years Income Tax Returns (ITR)
- ✅ Last 12 months bank statements
- ✅ GST registration and returns (if applicable)
- ✅ Business proof (GST certificate, Shop & Establishment license, partnership deed, etc.)
- ✅ Address proof (utility bills, rent agreement)
- ✅ Business plan or project report (for larger loans)
- ✅ Proof of business vintage (2+ years preferred)

**How to Fix:**

1. **Get Udyam Registration** — If not registered, do it immediately at [udyamregistration.gov.in](https://udyamregistration.gov.in/) (it's free)
2. **File pending ITRs** — File returns for last 2–3 years before applying
3. **Get proper business proof** — Register for GST, get Shop Act license, or trade license
4. **Prepare a project report** — A simple document showing how you'll use the loan and repay it
5. **Organize documents** — Submit clear, legible copies. Incomplete forms are auto-rejected

### Reason 4: Business Too New (Low Vintage)

Most banks require 2–3 years of business existence.

**How to Fix:**

1. **Apply for Mudra Loan** — [Mudra Yojana](https://www.mudra.org.in/) specifically supports new and small businesses
2. **Try Startup India loans** — If you qualify as a startup, check [startupindia.gov.in](https://www.startupindia.gov.in/) for SIDBI Fund of Funds
3. **Apply to NBFCs** — Many accept businesses with 1+ year vintage
4. **Use CGTMSE guarantee** — Helps new businesses get loans without collateral (see below)

### Reason 5: Existing High Debt

If your current debt-to-income ratio is too high, banks will reject new loan applications.

**How to Fix:**

1. **Pay down existing loans** — Reduce outstanding balances
2. **Consolidate loans** — Combine multiple loans into one with better terms
3. **Show increased income** — Updated ITR with higher income improves your ratio
4. **Close unused credit facilities** — Even unused credit limits count against you

## CGTMSE Scheme: Get Collateral-Free MSME Loans

The **Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises (CGTMSE)** is a government scheme that helps MSMEs get loans without collateral or third-party guarantee.

### Key Details

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Maximum Loan | ₹5 crore |
| Collateral Required | No |
| Third-Party Guarantee | No |
| Eligible Enterprises | Micro and Small (not Medium) |
| Coverage | 75–85% of loan amount guaranteed by government |
| Available Through | All scheduled commercial banks, select NBFCs, and SFBs |

### How to Apply for CGTMSE Loan

1. Register on [Udyam portal](https://udyamregistration.gov.in/) — must be classified as Micro or Small
2. Approach any scheduled commercial bank (SBI, PNB, BOB, etc.)
3. Apply for an MSME term loan and specifically request **CGTMSE coverage**
4. The bank evaluates your proposal and, if approved, applies for CGTMSE guarantee
5. You don't need to apply to CGTMSE directly — the bank handles it
6. Official info: [cgtmse.in](https://www.cgtmse.in/)

> **Tip:** Mention CGTMSE proactively when applying. Some bank branches may not offer it unless you ask.

## Alternative Lending Options

If bank loans aren't working, consider these alternatives:

### 1. PSB Loans in 59 Minutes
- Apply at [psbloansin59minutes.com](https://psbloansin59minutes.com/)
- In-principle approval in 59 minutes
- Loans from ₹1 lakh to ₹5 crore
- Requires GST and ITR data

### 2. SIDBI Direct Lending
- [SIDBI](https://www.sidbi.in/) provides direct loans to MSMEs
- Special schemes for women entrepreneurs and startups

### 3. Microfinance Institutions
- For very small loans (₹10,000–₹1 lakh)
- Lower documentation requirements
- Higher interest rates but easier approval

### 4. Government Subsidy Schemes
- **PM Vishwakarma:** Loans up to ₹3 lakh for artisans and craftspeople
- **PM SVANidhi:** Loans up to ₹50,000 for street vendors
- **Stand-Up India:** ₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore for SC/ST and women entrepreneurs

### 5. Fintech MSME Lenders
- Lendingkart, NeoGrowth, FlexiLoans, etc.
- Faster approval, flexible eligibility
- Higher interest rates (14–24%) but useful when banks reject

## Steps to Reapply After Rejection

1. **Wait at least 30–90 days** — Multiple rejections in quick succession hurt your credit score
2. **Get rejection reason in writing** — Ask the bank for specific reason (you're entitled to know)
3. **Fix the specific issue** — Address the exact reason before reapplying
4. **Try a different bank or NBFC** — Each lender has different criteria
5. **Consider a smaller loan amount** — Start small, build a repayment track record, then apply for more

## Important Tips

1. Always get your [Udyam Registration](/guide/udyam-registration-not-working-fix) before applying — it's mandatory for MSME benefits
2. Maintain a clean bank statement — avoid cash deposits of large undocumented amounts
3. File GST returns on time — regular filing demonstrates business health
4. Don't apply to multiple banks simultaneously — each application triggers a credit inquiry
5. Ask about CGTMSE guarantee specifically — it eliminates the collateral barrier

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the minimum CIBIL score for MSME loan?
Most banks require 650–700+. NBFCs may accept 600+. Under CGTMSE scheme, the focus is more on project viability than credit score, but a decent score (650+) still helps.

### Can I get an MSME loan without collateral?
Yes, through the CGTMSE scheme. Loans up to ₹5 crore are available without collateral or third-party guarantee for micro and small enterprises.

### How many times can I reapply for an MSME loan after rejection?
There's no limit, but wait 30–90 days between applications. Each application creates a hard inquiry on your credit report, which temporarily lowers your score.

### Is Udyam registration mandatory for MSME loans?
While not always mandatory for basic bank loans, Udyam registration is required to avail MSME-specific benefits like priority sector lending, CGTMSE coverage, and government subsidy schemes.

### Can I get a loan for a new business with no turnover?
Yes. Mudra Shishu loans (up to ₹50,000) and PM SVANidhi loans are available for new/very small businesses. For larger amounts, you'll need a solid project report and some collateral or guarantee.

### What should I do if my loan is rejected but I urgently need funds?
Consider Mudra loans (easier approval), microfinance institutions, or fintech lenders as immediate alternatives. Also check state government schemes — many states have entrepreneur support funds with easier eligibility.
