# Ration Card Nominee Change: How to Add or Remove Family Member

Your ration card must accurately reflect all members of your family. Whether a child is born, you get married, or a family member passes away — updating the ration card ensures correct entitlements under the Public Distribution System (PDS). This guide covers the complete process to add or remove members.

## When Do You Need to Update Members?

### Adding a Member
- **Newborn baby** — add within 1 year of birth for best results
- **Spouse** — after marriage, add to either spouse's ration card
- **Adopted child** — with legal adoption documents
- **Dependent family member** — elderly parent or relative moving into the household

### Removing a Member
- **Deceased family member** — remove after death to avoid discrepancies
- **Married daughter/son** — when they move to spouse's household and ration card
- **Family member who moved out** — separated or relocated permanently
- **Duplicate entry** — if a member is listed on two ration cards

## Documents Required

### For Adding a Member

| Scenario | Documents Needed |
|----------|-----------------|
| **Newborn** | Birth certificate, hospital discharge summary, parents' Aadhaar |
| **Spouse (after marriage)** | Marriage certificate, spouse's Aadhaar, spouse's photo |
| **Adopted child** | Adoption deed/court order, child's birth certificate, Aadhaar (if available) |
| **Dependent/relative** | Aadhaar card, relationship proof, deletion proof from previous ration card |

### For Removing a Member

| Scenario | Documents Needed |
|----------|-----------------|
| **Deceased member** | Death certificate |
| **Married-out member** | Marriage certificate, proof of addition to spouse's ration card |
| **Relocated member** | Self-declaration, new address proof (if available) |

### Common Documents for All Cases
- **Current ration card** (original and photocopy)
- **Aadhaar card** of head of family
- **Application form** (from state portal or Food & Civil Supplies office)
- **Passport-size photograph** of the member being added

## Step-by-Step Process

### Online Process

1. **Visit your state's PDS portal** — search for "[your state] ration card online" or use epds.nic.in links
2. **Log in** with ration card number, Aadhaar, or registered mobile
3. **Go to "Services"** or "Ration Card Modification"
4. **Select "Add Member"** or **"Remove Member"**
5. **Enter member details** — name, date of birth, Aadhaar number, relationship with head of family
6. **Upload required documents** — birth certificate, death certificate, marriage certificate, Aadhaar, photo
7. **Review and submit** the application
8. **Note the reference number** for tracking
9. **Track status** on the same portal using reference number or ration card number

### Offline Process

1. **Get the application form** from the local Food & Civil Supplies office, Taluk Supply Office, or download from the state portal
2. **Fill in complete details** — ration card number, member details, reason for addition/removal
3. **Attach photocopies** of all required documents
4. **Submit the form** at the local Food & Civil Supplies office
5. **Collect the acknowledgement receipt** with reference number
6. **Verification** — a food inspector may verify the details
7. **Collect updated ration card** after approval (or download online)

### Through Common Service Centre (CSC)

If you are not comfortable with the online process:

1. Visit your nearest **Common Service Centre (CSC)** or **e-Seva Kendra**
2. Carry all original documents and photocopies
3. The CSC operator will fill and submit the application on your behalf
4. Nominal service charge of ₹20-50 may apply
5. Collect the receipt and track online

## Special Cases

### Adding a Newborn
- Apply as soon as you get the birth certificate (usually within 21 days of birth)
- No Aadhaar needed for infants under 5 years in most states
- The child's food entitlement starts from the month of addition

### Removing a Deceased Member
- Apply immediately after receiving the death certificate
- Rations allocated to the deceased member will be adjusted from the next distribution cycle
- If the deceased was the head of family, you must also apply for **head of family change**

### Head of Family Change
- Required when the current head of family passes away, leaves, or becomes incapacitated
- Submit death certificate or relevant proof along with the new head's Aadhaar and photo
- The new head must be an adult member already listed on the ration card

## Important Tips

1. **Don't delay** — update your ration card promptly after any family change to avoid issues during ration distribution
2. **One person, one card** — a person cannot be listed on two ration cards simultaneously. Ensure deletion from the old card before adding to a new one
3. **Aadhaar linking is essential** — new members must have their Aadhaar linked for biometric authentication at FPS. See our [eKYC guide](/guide/ration-card-ekyc-online)
4. **Track regularly** — check application status every week online; if stuck beyond 30 days, file a grievance
5. **Keep copies** — always keep photocopies of the updated ration card and all submitted documents

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Can I add my spouse to my ration card after marriage?
**A:** Yes. Submit the marriage certificate, spouse's Aadhaar card, and a photograph. Your spouse must first be removed from their parents' ration card.

### Q2: How do I add a newborn baby to my ration card?
**A:** Apply online or offline with the baby's birth certificate and parents' Aadhaar. Aadhaar is not mandatory for children under 5 in most states. The baby's entitlement starts from the month of addition.

### Q3: Is it mandatory to remove a deceased member?
**A:** While not legally mandatory with a strict deadline, it is important to remove deceased members to avoid discrepancies. Government verification drives may flag ration cards with deceased members, leading to complications.

### Q4: Can I add my elderly parents to my ration card?
**A:** Yes, if they live with you and are your dependents. They must first be removed from their existing ration card (if any). Submit their Aadhaar and a deletion certificate from their previous ration card.

### Q5: How many members can be added to one ration card?
**A:** There is no fixed upper limit set by NFSA. However, only genuine family members living together should be listed. Under PHH/BPL, each member gets 5 kg of grains per month.

### Q6: What if my name addition is not showing after approval?
**A:** Wait for 2-3 distribution cycles. If still not updated, visit the FPS with your approval letter/SMS. You can also check our [Name Addition Not Showing Fix guide](/guide/ration-card-name-addition-not-showing-fix).

### Q7: Can I add or remove members from any state's ration card?
**A:** Yes, the process is available in all states. Apply through your respective state's PDS portal or Food & Civil Supplies office.
