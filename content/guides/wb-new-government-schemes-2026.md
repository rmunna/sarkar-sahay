## Women Empowerment Schemes

### CM Women Income Support Scheme 2026 (New / Enhanced)

The new West Bengal government's flagship programme for women's financial empowerment — building on the model of income support that has been central to the state's welfare architecture.

**Expected Benefits:**
- Monthly Direct Benefit Transfer to women aged 25–60
- Enhanced amounts for SC/ST women
- Streamlined single-window application via e-District portal
- Coverage for both rural and urban women

📖 **Detailed guide:** [West Bengal CM Women Welfare Scheme 2026](/guide/wb-cm-women-welfare-scheme-2026)

---

### Lakshmir Bhandar (Continuing / Enhanced)

West Bengal's landmark women income support scheme remains one of the most popular welfare schemes in the state. The new government has signalled continuation with possible enhancements to the monthly benefit amount.

**Current Benefits (as last notified):**
- ₹1,000/month for General category women
- ₹1,200/month for SC/ST women
- Women aged 25–60, resident of West Bengal

📖 **Detailed guide:** [Lakshmir Bhandar West Bengal](/guide/lakshmir-bhandar-west-bengal)

---

### Kanyashree Prakalpa (Continuing)

West Bengal's internationally recognised scholarship scheme for girls aged 13–18 continues under all governments due to its UN Public Service Award recognition and deep implementation infrastructure.

**Key Benefits:**
- **K1:** ₹1,000/year annual scholarship for enrolled school girls (13–18 years)
- **K2:** ₹25,000 one-time grant at age 18 if unmarried and pursuing education
- **K3:** Support for higher education (UG/PG)
- No income bar — all girl students eligible

📖 **Detailed guide:** [Kanyashree Prakalpa West Bengal](/guide/kanyashree-prakalpa-west-bengal)

---

### Rupashree Prakalpa (Continuing)

One-time financial assistance to economically backward families for their daughter's marriage — ₹25,000 transferred to the bride's account within 10 days of application approval.

**Key Details:**
- **Benefit:** ₹25,000 one-time payment
- **Eligibility:** Bride must be 18+, family income below ₹1.5 lakh/year, domicile of WB
- **Application:** At Block Development Office or through e-District portal

📖 **Detailed guide:** [Rupashree Prakalpa West Bengal](/guide/rupashree-prakalpa-west-bengal)

## Student and Youth Schemes

### Swami Vivekananda Merit-cum-Means Scholarship (Continuing)

West Bengal's flagship merit scholarship for students from economically weaker sections pursing education after Class 12. Continues under the new government.

**Key Details:**
- ₹1,000–₹5,000/month depending on course level (Class 11 to PG)
- Family income below ₹2.5 lakh/year
- Available for students enrolled in WB colleges/universities
- Apply at [scholarships.wbsed.gov.in](https://scholarships.wbsed.gov.in/)

📖 **Detailed guide:** [WB Swami Vivekananda Scholarship](/guide/wb-swami-vivekananda-scholarship)

---

### Sabuj Sathi — Free Bicycle Scheme (Continuing)

One of the most successful welfare schemes of any Indian state government — providing free bicycles to Class 9–12 students in government and government-aided schools. The new government continues this scheme, with discussions on upgrading to e-bicycles for older students in some districts.

**Key Details:**
- All students in Class 9–12 studying in government/aided schools receive a free bicycle
- No application required — automatically distributed through school
- Over 1 crore bicycles distributed since the scheme's inception

📖 **Detailed guide:** [WB Sabuj Sathi Bicycle Scheme](/guide/wb-sabuj-sathi-bicycle-scheme)

---

### Aikyashree Scholarship for Minority Students (Continuing)

Post-matric scholarship for Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain, and Parsi students from economically weaker families in West Bengal.

**Key Details:**
- Pre-matric and post-matric scholarships
- Family income below ₹2 lakh/year
- Covers tuition fees and maintenance allowance
- Apply at [wbmdfcscholarship.in](https://wbmdfcscholarship.in/)

📖 **Detailed guide:** [Aikyashree Prakalpa West Bengal](/guide/aikyashree-prakalpa-west-bengal)

## Health Schemes

### Swasthya Sathi (Continuing / Enhanced)

West Bengal's universal health scheme providing cashless treatment coverage to all families in the state — one of the most comprehensive state health insurance programmes in India.

**Key Details:**
- Covers all West Bengal families — no income bar, no exclusion
- Cashless treatment up to ₹5 lakh per family per year
- 1,000+ medical procedures and packages covered
- Both government and empanelled private hospitals
- Smart card issued per family — apply at nearest camp or e-District portal

📖 **Detailed guide:** [Swasthya Sathi West Bengal](/guide/swasthya-sathi-west-bengal)

## Farmer Welfare Schemes

### Krishak Bandhu — Farmer Income Support (Continuing / Enhanced)

West Bengal's flagship farmer income support scheme, providing annual financial assistance to cultivating farmers based on landholding.

**Key Details (as last notified):**
- **₹10,000 per year** for farmers owning 1 acre or more of agricultural land
- Proportionate amount for farmers with less than 1 acre
- Includes death benefit of ₹2 lakh for natural or accidental death of farmer
- Over 75 lakh farmer beneficiaries enrolled

**Application:** BDO office or online at krish.wb.gov.in

---

### Bangla Shasya Bima — Crop Insurance (Continuing)

State-level crop insurance complementing PM Fasal Bima Yojana — covers weather-related and pest-related crop losses. Farmers receive insurance benefits directly in their bank accounts within weeks of crop loss.

## Housing Schemes

### Bangla Awas Yojana (Continuing)

State counterpart of PMAY (Gramin) — provides financial assistance for housing construction to homeless and kutcha-house families in rural West Bengal.

**Key Details:**
- ₹1.2–1.5 lakh for rural house construction
- Priority to SC/ST families, women-headed households, and differently-abled persons
- Apply through Gram Panchayat or BDO office

## How to Apply for West Bengal Government Schemes in 2026

### Step 1 — Visit WB e-District Portal
Go to [edistrict.wb.gov.in](https://edistrict.wb.gov.in/) for most scheme applications and certificates.

### Step 2 — Register Your Account
Register with mobile number and Aadhaar. Verify OTP. Keep Aadhaar and bank account linked before applying for DBT schemes.

### Step 3 — Select Scheme and Check Eligibility
Search for your scheme, read eligibility criteria carefully, and confirm you qualify before starting the application.

### Step 4 — Apply Online or at Duare Sarkar Camps
The West Bengal government regularly conducts **Duare Sarkar** (government at doorstep) camps at local schools, panchayat offices, and community halls. These camps help citizens apply for schemes without visiting government offices.

### Step 5 — Track Application
Use the application reference number to track status on the e-District portal or the specific scheme portal.

### Documents Commonly Required
- Aadhaar card
- Voter ID (EPIC card)
- Ration card
- Income certificate (from BDO or SDO)
- Bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked)
- Residence proof
- Caste certificate (SC/ST/OBC)
- Passport-size photographs

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the flagship schemes of the new West Bengal government 2026?
The new WB government (2026) is building on women income support (continuing or enhancing Lakshmir Bhandar), Kanyashree Prakalpa, Swasthya Sathi health coverage, and Krishak Bandhu farmer scheme. New flagship announcements will be made available at [wb.gov.in](https://wb.gov.in/).

### Will Lakshmir Bhandar continue under the new West Bengal government?
Yes. Women income support schemes have broad cross-party support in West Bengal and are unlikely to be discontinued. The new government is expected to continue or enhance the scheme.

### How do I apply for schemes at a Duare Sarkar camp?
Check your local Panchayat or Block Development Office for the camp schedule in your area. Carry your Aadhaar card, voter ID, income certificate, and bank passbook. Assistants at the camp will help you fill applications on the spot.

### Does Kanyashree Prakalpa continue under the new government?
Yes. Kanyashree has UN recognition, is deeply embedded in school administration, and benefits crores of girls. It continues regardless of the government in power. Apply through your school.

### How do I check if I received the Krishak Bandhu benefit?
Visit [krish.wb.gov.in](https://krish.wb.gov.in/), enter your Aadhaar or voter ID number, and check payment status. You can also call the Krishak Bandhu helpline at 1800-103-2253.

### Where can I get help applying for West Bengal government schemes?
Visit your nearest **Duare Sarkar camp**, **BDO office**, or **Common Service Centre (CSC)** for assisted application. WB helpline: **1800-103-0136** (toll-free).

### Is Aadhaar mandatory for WB government schemes?
For all DBT schemes (Lakshmir Bhandar, Krishak Bandhu, scholarships), an Aadhaar-linked bank account is mandatory. Ensure your bank account is seeded with Aadhaar before applying.
