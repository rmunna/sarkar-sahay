# West Bengal BJP Government Schemes 2026 — Complete Guide

The **BJP government led by CM Suvendu Adhikari** won the 2026 West Bengal Legislative Assembly election with a historic 206–207 seats (majority mark: 148) out of 294, defeating TMC which fell to 80 seats. Suvendu Adhikari was sworn in as West Bengal's **first-ever BJP Chief Minister** on **9 May 2026** at Brigade Parade Ground, Kolkata.

This guide covers every major WB scheme — new flagship programmes, continued schemes, and Day 1 decisions.

> CM Suvendu Adhikari was sworn in on **9 May 2026**. First cabinet meeting decisions were announced on **11 May 2026**. The Annapurna Bhandar women scheme launches **1 June 2026**.

---

## What the BJP Government Did on Day 1 (11 May 2026)

Six cabinet decisions announced at the very first cabinet meeting:

1. **Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY** — Signed with Union Health Ministry, ending years of deadlock. ₹5 lakh/family/year health insurance now active.
2. **Central Schemes Unlocked** — PM Ujjwala Yojana 3.0, PM Vishwakarma, PM SHRI schools, Beti Bachao Beti Padhao, PM Krishak Bima Yojana — all formally launched in WB for the first time.
3. **5-Year Age Relaxation** — Upper age limit for ALL government recruitment exams relaxed by 5 years (since no recruitment happened 2015–2026 under TMC).
4. **BSF Land Transfer** — ~600 acres to BSF within 45 days for India-Bangladesh border fencing.
5. **Census Operations** — Launched immediately (had been delayed 11 months by previous government).
6. **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS)** — New criminal code fully implemented across West Bengal.

## SECTOR 1 — Women's Welfare

### 1. Annapurna Bhandar — ₹3,000/Month (Flagship)

The BJP government's new women income support scheme — **replacing the TMC's Lakshmir Bhandar** (₹500–₹1,200/month) with a significantly higher ₹3,000/month.

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| **Monthly Benefit** | **₹3,000 via DBT** |
| **Previous Scheme** | Lakshmir Bhandar (TMC) was ₹1,000 (Gen) / ₹1,200 (SC/ST) |
| **Launch Date** | **1 June 2026** |
| **Official Notification** | Notification No. 2411-WCD/O/AB-4/2026, issued 19 May 2026 |
| **Eligibility** | Women 25–60 years, permanent WB resident, non-taxpayer, non-govt employee |
| **Application Portal** | edistrict.wb.gov.in |

📖 **Detailed guide:** [WB Annapurna Bhandar Scheme ₹3,000/Month 2026](/guide/wb-cm-women-welfare-scheme-2026)

### 2. Free Bus Travel for Women (Continuing)

All women travel free on state government buses — announced effective **1 June 2026** alongside Annapurna Bhandar.

### 3. Kanyashree Prakalpa (Continuing)

West Bengal's UN-award-winning girls scholarship scheme continues under the BJP government.

| Category | Benefit |
|----------|---------|
| K1 — Annual scholarship | ₹750/year for enrolled girls aged 13–18 |
| K2 — Lump sum at 18 | ₹25,000 one-time if unmarried and pursuing education |

- No income bar — all girl students in government/aided schools eligible
- Apply through your school or at [edistrict.wb.gov.in](https://edistrict.wb.gov.in/)

---

### 4. Rupashree Prakalpa (Continuing)

One-time marriage assistance for economically weaker families:

- **₹25,000** transferred to bride's account within 10 days of approval
- Bride must be 18+; family income below ₹1.5 lakh/year
- Apply at BDO office or e-District portal

## How to Apply for WB Government Schemes 2026

### Step 1 — Keep Documents Ready
- Aadhaar card (Aadhaar-linked bank account mandatory for DBT)
- Voter ID (EPIC card)
- Ration card (West Bengal)
- Income certificate (from BDO/SDO)
- Bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked)
- Caste certificate (SC/ST/OBC)

### Step 2 — Apply Online at WB e-District
[edistrict.wb.gov.in](https://edistrict.wb.gov.in/) → Register with mobile + Aadhaar → Find scheme → Fill application → Upload documents → Submit

### Step 3 — Duare Sarkar Camps (if continuing under BJP)
The BJP government may continue West Bengal's Duare Sarkar (government at doorstep) model — watch for camp announcements at your Gram Panchayat or BDO office.

**WB Helpline: 1800-103-0136** (toll-free)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When was Suvendu Adhikari sworn in as West Bengal CM?
Suvendu Adhikari (BJP) was sworn in as West Bengal's first BJP Chief Minister on **9 May 2026** at Brigade Parade Ground, Kolkata. PM Modi attended the ceremony.

### What replaced Lakshmir Bhandar in West Bengal 2026?
The BJP government replaced TMC's Lakshmir Bhandar (₹1,000–₹1,200/month) with **Annapurna Bhandar**, which pays **₹3,000/month** to eligible women. Launch date: **1 June 2026**.

### Is Ayushman Bharat now available in West Bengal?
Yes — for the **first time ever**. The TMC government had rejected Ayushman Bharat since 2018. The BJP cabinet approved it on **11 May 2026** (Day 1). WB families with SECC eligibility can now enrol for ₹5 lakh/year health coverage.

### How much is the West Bengal pension after 2026?
Old-age pension, widow pension, and disability pension have all been **doubled to ₹2,000/month** (from ₹1,000), effective 1 June 2026, by Cabinet Minister Agnimitra Paul.

### What happened to Swasthya Sathi under the BJP government?
Swasthya Sathi (TMC's health scheme, ₹5 lakh/family) is being transitioned to Ayushman Bharat. Note: Ayushman Bharat covers ~1.24 crore families under SECC eligibility, while Swasthya Sathi covered ~2.4 crore families. Check your eligibility at [pmjay.gov.in](https://pmjay.gov.in/).

### Where can I apply for Annapurna Bhandar?
Applications will open at [edistrict.wb.gov.in](https://edistrict.wb.gov.in/) from **1 June 2026**. Assist offline at BDO offices and CSC centres. Helpline: **1800-103-0136**.
