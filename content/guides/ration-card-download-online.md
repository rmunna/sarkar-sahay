# Ration Card Download Online: e-Ration Card State-Wise

You can now download your ration card online as an e-Ration Card from your state's food and civil supplies portal. The digital ration card (e-Ration Card) is a valid document and can be used at Fair Price Shops along with Aadhaar-based authentication. This guide covers state-wise download links, DigiLocker access, and how to get a print copy.

## What is an e-Ration Card?

An e-Ration Card is the digital version of your physical ration card. It contains the same details — ration card number, family members' names, category (AAY/PHH/NPHH), and address. The e-Ration Card is:

- Available as a downloadable PDF from state portals
- Legally valid for availing subsidized rations under NFSA
- Accessible through DigiLocker for central verification
- Useful as address proof in many government processes

## How to Download Ration Card Online — General Steps

Most state portals follow a similar process:

1. Visit your state's Food & Civil Supplies Department website (see table below).
2. Look for **"e-Ration Card"**, **"Download Ration Card"**, or **"RC Details"** option.
3. Enter your **ration card number**, **Aadhaar number**, or **mobile number**.
4. Complete OTP verification if required.
5. Your ration card details will appear on screen.
6. Click **"Download"** or **"Print"** to save the e-Ration Card as PDF.

## Download Ration Card via DigiLocker

DigiLocker provides verified digital copies of ration cards for participating states.

### Steps to Download via DigiLocker

1. Visit [digilocker.gov.in](https://www.digilocker.gov.in) or open the DigiLocker mobile app.
2. Log in with your registered mobile number and Aadhaar.
3. Go to **"Search Documents"** or **"Issued Documents"** section.
4. Search for **"Ration Card"** under the Food & Civil Supplies Department of your state.
5. Enter your ration card number or Aadhaar number.
6. The verified e-Ration Card will be fetched and stored in your DigiLocker.
7. Download the PDF from DigiLocker anytime.

> **Note:** DigiLocker ration card availability depends on your state's integration. Not all states have enabled this feature yet.

## e-Ration Card Validity

- The e-Ration Card downloaded from official state portals is **legally valid** under the IT Act, 2000.
- It can be used at Fair Price Shops along with biometric (Aadhaar) verification.
- DigiLocker documents are treated as original documents under Rule 9A of the IT Rules.
- The e-Ration Card reflects **real-time data** — any updates to your ration card (name change, member addition) will automatically reflect in the downloaded version.

## How to Get a Print Copy

If you need a physical printed copy of your ration card:

1. Download the e-Ration Card PDF from your state portal.
2. Print it on A4 paper (colour print recommended).
3. The printed copy is valid for use at Fair Price Shops with Aadhaar verification.
4. For an **official laminated card**, visit your nearest Taluk/Block Supply Office or CSC (Common Service Centre) with your ration card number and Aadhaar.

## Documents Needed for Download

No documents are required for downloading. You only need:

- Ration card number (RC number)
- Aadhaar number linked to the ration card
- Registered mobile number (for OTP)

## Important Tips

1. Always download from the **official state portal** — avoid third-party websites that may charge fees.
2. If your ration card details are not showing, ensure your [Aadhaar is linked](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) to your ration card.
3. Keep a screenshot or PDF saved on your phone for quick access at Fair Price Shops.
4. If you face issues downloading, contact your state's food helpline or visit the nearest Taluk Supply Office.
5. For [ration card portability](/guide/ration-card-portability) across states, the e-Ration Card works with One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC) scheme.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the e-Ration Card valid at Fair Price Shops?
Yes. The e-Ration Card downloaded from official portals is legally valid. You need to verify your identity via Aadhaar biometric at the Fair Price Shop.

### Can I download someone else's ration card?
You can view ration card details if you have the ration card number, but download with OTP verification requires access to the registered mobile number.

### Is there any fee to download the e-Ration Card?
No. Downloading from official state portals and DigiLocker is completely free. Do not pay any third-party website.

### What if my state portal is not working?
Try during non-peak hours (early morning or late evening). You can also try DigiLocker or visit your nearest CSC centre for assistance.

### Can I use the e-Ration Card as address proof?
Yes. The ration card (physical or digital) is accepted as address proof by most government departments and banks.

### How often should I download a fresh copy?
Download a new copy whenever you make changes to your ration card (member addition, address change, etc.) to ensure it reflects the latest information.
