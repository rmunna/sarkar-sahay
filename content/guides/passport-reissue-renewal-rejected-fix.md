# Passport Reissue or Renewal Rejected — How to Fix

Had your passport reissue or renewal application rejected? This is more common than you think. The Passport Seva system rejects applications for specific, fixable reasons — from damaged passport pages to missing gazette notifications for name changes.

This guide covers **every common rejection reason**, what documents you actually need, how to re-apply correctly, and how to escalate if you believe the rejection was wrong.

---

## Common Reasons for Passport Reissue/Renewal Rejection

### 1. Damaged Passport — Photo or Details Page Unreadable

If your existing passport's **photo page (first and last page)** is torn, water-damaged, faded, or the MRZ (machine-readable zone) is illegible, your reissue application will be rejected.

**Why it matters:** The Passport Office needs to verify your identity against the old passport. If the photo/details page is unreadable, they cannot process the reissue.

**How to fix:**
- Apply for a **fresh passport** instead of reissue if the damage is severe
- Attach a **police complaint (FIR)** reporting the damaged passport
- Carry the damaged passport physically to the PSK — do not dispose of it
- Provide **two additional identity proofs** (Aadhaar, PAN, voter ID) to compensate

### 2. Incomplete or Wrong ECR/ECNR Details

ECR (Emigration Check Required) and ECNR (Emigration Check Not Required) categorisation depends on your education and profession. Common mistakes:

- Selecting **ECNR without valid proof** (e.g., no matriculation certificate)
- Providing a degree certificate but **not the matriculation/10th certificate** (which is actually required for ECNR)
- Mismatch between education details in the form and supporting documents

**How to fix:**
- For ECNR: Submit your **10th pass certificate** or higher education proof (degree, diploma)
- If you don't have a 10th certificate, apply under **ECR category** — you can convert later
- Government employees must attach a **letter from their department** confirming employment
- Income tax payers with annual income above ₹50,000 need **ITR acknowledgement**

### 3. Name Change Without Gazette Notification

If you've changed your name (after marriage, divorce, or personal choice) and applied for reissue with the new name but **haven't published a gazette notification**, your application will be rejected.

**Required documents for name change:**
1. **Gazette of India notification** with the name change published — apply at [egazette.gov.in](https://egazette.gov.in/)
2. **Two newspaper advertisements** (one in a local and one in a regional newspaper) announcing the name change
3. **Marriage certificate** (if name change is due to marriage)
4. **Affidavit** on stamp paper declaring the name change

**How to fix:**
- Get the gazette notification published **before** re-applying — it takes 1–4 weeks
- For married women: a **marriage certificate alone is sufficient** for surname change; gazette is needed only for first name change
- Carry **original + photocopy** of all documents to the PSK

### 4. Wrong or Insufficient Supporting Documents

Common document mistakes that lead to rejection:

| Situation | Document Often Missed |
|---|---|
| Address change | New address proof (utility bill < 3 months, bank statement, Aadhaar with new address) |
| Spouse name addition | Marriage certificate + spouse's passport copy |
| Minor to adult reissue | Birth certificate + 10th marksheet + both parents' passports |
| Government employee | NOC (No Objection Certificate) or Prior Intimation Letter from department |

**How to fix:**
- Use the [Passport Document Advisor](https://www.passportindia.gov.in/AppOnlineProject/docAdvisor/docAdvisorST) tool before applying — enter your specific situation and it lists exact documents needed
- Always carry **originals + self-attested photocopies** of all documents
- If your address proof name doesn't match, carry a linking document (e.g., Aadhaar with both old and new name)

### 5. Adverse Police Verification Report

If your previous passport had an **adverse police verification** or there's a pending criminal case, the reissue will be rejected or put on hold.

**Common triggers:**
- Pending FIR or court case against you
- Previous passport flagged for misuse
- Address verification failed (you don't live at the declared address)
- Adverse report from a previous passport application

**How to fix:**
- Obtain a **clearance certificate from the court** if the case has been resolved
- For address mismatch: update your Aadhaar address first, then re-apply
- Request the **police verification report** through RTI if you suspect an error
- Visit the **SSP/SP office** in your district to get the police report corrected
- If wrongly flagged, file a grievance at [cpgrams.gov.in](https://cpgrams.gov.in/)

## Fresh Application vs. Reissue — Which Should You File?

| Factor | Reissue | Fresh Application |
|---|---|---|
| **When to use** | Passport expired, pages full, damaged (readable), name/address change | Passport lost, stolen, severely damaged (unreadable) |
| **Old passport needed** | Yes — must submit original | No — FIR/police complaint required instead |
| **Fee (36 pages)** | ₹1,500 | ₹1,500 |
| **Fee (60 pages)** | ₹2,000 | ₹2,000 |
| **Processing time** | 7–15 working days | 15–30 working days |
| **Police verification** | Usually skipped (if address unchanged) | Mandatory |

**Key rule:** If your old passport is so damaged that the photo page is unreadable, you **must** file a **fresh application** (not reissue) with an FIR for the damaged passport.

## How to Re-Apply After Rejection

### Step 1: Understand the Rejection Reason

- Log in at [passportindia.gov.in](https://www.passportindia.gov.in/AppOnlineProject/welcomeLink)
- Go to **View Saved/Submitted Applications**
- Check the **status remarks** — the rejection reason is noted here
- If unclear, call the **Passport Seva helpline: 1800-258-1800** (toll-free)

### Step 2: Fix the Issue

Based on the rejection reason, gather the correct/missing documents. Do **not** re-apply with the same documents — it will be rejected again.

### Step 3: Fill a New Application

1. Log in to the Passport Seva portal
2. Click **Apply for Fresh Passport/Reissue of Passport**
3. Select **Reissue** and choose the correct reason (validity expired, damaged, change in details, etc.)
4. Fill all details carefully — ensure ECR/ECNR, name, and address match your documents exactly
5. Upload documents if applying online; carry originals to PSK

### Step 4: Book an Appointment

- Select your nearest **PSK (Passport Seva Kendra)** or **POPSK (Post Office Passport Seva Kendra)**
- Pay the fee online (₹1,500 for normal 36-page booklet)
- If appointment slots are unavailable, see our guide: [Passport Appointment Not Available — How to Fix](/guides/passport-appointment-not-available-fix)

### Step 5: Visit the PSK

- Arrive **15 minutes before** your appointment
- Carry **all original documents** + self-attested photocopies
- Carry the **old/damaged passport** (even if damaged)
- The officer may ask for additional documents — bring extras

> **Tip:** If your previous application was rejected, mention it at **Counter A** so the officer can review the rejection notes and guide you.

## Tatkal Reissue — When You Need It Fast

If you need the reissued passport urgently (travel within 1–3 weeks), apply under the **Tatkal scheme**:

| Detail | Normal | Tatkal |
|---|---|---|
| **Processing time** | 7–15 working days | 1–3 working days |
| **Extra fee (36 pages)** | — | ₹2,000 |
| **Extra fee (60 pages)** | — | ₹2,500 |
| **Total fee (36 pages)** | ₹1,500 | ₹3,500 |
| **Documents needed** | Standard | Standard + Annexure F (verification certificate from gazetted officer/notary) |

**Important Tatkal rules:**
- Tatkal passports issued within the first year show **ECR category by default** regardless of qualifications
- Address change under Tatkal **requires police verification** — which may delay the process
- Tatkal is **not available** for applicants with adverse police verification

## How to Escalate — Appeal to RPO

If your reissue was rejected and you believe it was wrong, you can escalate:

### Level 1: Passport Seva Grievance

- Call **1800-258-1800** and register a formal complaint
- You'll receive a **grievance reference number** — note it down
- Expected response: **7 working days**

### Level 2: RPO (Regional Passport Officer) Escalation

- Find your RPO's contact: [RPO Contact Directory](https://www.passportindia.gov.in/AppOnlineProject/online/rpoContactUsAction)
- Write a formal letter/email to the RPO explaining:
  - Your application file number
  - The rejection reason stated
  - Why you believe it's incorrect
  - Attach supporting documents
- Request a **personal hearing** if the matter is complex

### Level 3: CPV Division, MEA

- File a grievance at [cpgrams.gov.in](https://cpgrams.gov.in/) — select **Ministry of External Affairs → CPV Division**
- Alternatively, use the [MEA Grievance Portal](https://www.mea.gov.in/grievance.htm)
- Attach all correspondence, rejection letter, and documents

### Level 4: RTI Application

If you're not getting a clear reason for rejection:
- File an **RTI** under the Right to Information Act
- Address it to the **CPIO, Passport Office** in your jurisdiction
- Ask for: "Reason for rejection of application [file number], copies of internal notes, and police verification report"
- Fee: ₹10 (online via [rtionline.gov.in](https://rtionline.gov.in/))

## Checklist Before Re-Applying

Use this checklist to avoid another rejection:

- [ ] Identified and fixed the exact rejection reason
- [ ] Used the [Document Advisor](https://www.passportindia.gov.in/AppOnlineProject/docAdvisor/docAdvisorST) to confirm required documents
- [ ] ECR/ECNR category matches your education certificates
- [ ] Name on application matches all supporting documents exactly
- [ ] Gazette notification obtained (if name change involved)
- [ ] Address proof is recent (within 3 months for utility bills)
- [ ] Old passport ready to submit (even if damaged)
- [ ] FIR filed (if applying fresh due to lost/severely damaged passport)
- [ ] NOC obtained (if government employee)
- [ ] Police verification issues resolved (if applicable)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I reissue a passport that expired more than 3 years ago?

Yes. You can apply for reissue regardless of how long ago it expired. However, if it expired **more than 3 years ago**, police verification is mandatory.

### My passport has my maiden name. Do I need a gazette notification for surname change after marriage?

No. For **surname change after marriage**, a marriage certificate is sufficient. Gazette notification is only needed for **first name changes**.

### Can I change my address and renew at the same time?

Yes. Select "Reissue" with reason "Change in existing personal particulars" and update the address. Carry new address proof. Note: address change triggers police verification.

### How many times can I re-apply after rejection?

There's **no limit**. You can re-apply as many times as needed after fixing the issue. Each application requires fresh fee payment.

### Will a rejected application affect my future passport applications?

No. A rejected reissue application does **not** create a negative record. Only adverse police verification or criminal cases affect future applications.

### Can I apply for reissue from a different city than where my passport was originally issued?

Yes. You can apply from **any PSK in India** regardless of where your original passport was issued. The system is centralized.
