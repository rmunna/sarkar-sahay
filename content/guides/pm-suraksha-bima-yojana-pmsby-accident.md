## What is PM Suraksha Bima Yojana?

**Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY)** is a government-backed **accidental death and disability insurance scheme** at an extremely affordable premium of just **₹20 per year** (approximately ₹1.67 per month).

Key features:
- Coverage: **₹2 lakh** on accidental death or total permanent disability
- Coverage: **₹1 lakh** on partial permanent disability
- Premium: **₹20/year** — auto-debited on June 1
- Available to all savings bank account holders
- No medical examination required
- Renewable annually

## Who is Eligible?

- Age: **18 to 70 years**
- Must have a **savings bank account** or **Jan Dhan account**
- Aadhaar should be linked to the bank account (recommended)

> **Note:** Unlike PMJJBY (which covers only up to age 50), PMSBY covers up to **70 years** — making it especially valuable for older adults.

## Premium and Coverage Details

| Coverage Type | Amount |
|---|---|
| Accidental death | **₹2,00,000** |
| Total permanent disability | **₹2,00,000** |
| Partial permanent disability | **₹1,00,000** |
| Annual premium | **₹20** |
| Coverage period | June 1 to May 31 |

**Total permanent disability** includes loss of both eyes, or loss of use of both hands/feet, or loss of one eye AND one hand/foot.

**Partial permanent disability** includes loss of one eye, or one hand, or one foot.

## How to Enroll — Step by Step

### Via Bank Branch

1. Visit your bank branch
2. Fill the **PMSBY enrollment/consent form** (Form available at bank)
3. Provide auto-debit consent for ₹20/year from your savings account
4. Submit Aadhaar copy (if not already linked)
5. Bank registers you and deducts premium on June 1

### Via Net Banking / Mobile Banking

1. Log in to net banking or bank mobile app
2. Navigate to **Insurance** or **Government Schemes** section
3. Find **PMSBY** and click **Enroll/Subscribe**
4. Confirm auto-debit consent
5. Receive confirmation SMS

### Via Jan Dhan / Post Office Account

Visit your Jan Dhan bank branch or post office. Submit the consent form. Premium will be auto-debited from your account.

## How to Claim

In case of accidental death or disability:

1. **Notify the bank** where enrolled within **30 days** of the accident (or as soon as possible)
2. Download/collect the **PMSBY claim form** from the bank
3. Submit:
   - **Death cases:** Death certificate, FIR/post-mortem report, claimant (nominee) identity + bank details
   - **Disability cases:** Disability certificate from government hospital, FIR, identity proof
4. Bank forwards claim to the insurer
5. Claim settled within **30 days** — amount credited to nominee's account

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does PMSBY cover death due to illness?
No — PMSBY only covers **accidental death**. Death due to illness, disease, or natural causes is not covered. For that, enroll in PMJJBY.

### What if my bank account has no balance on June 1?
The auto-debit of ₹20 will fail and your policy will lapse. Keep at least ₹20 in your account on May 31–June 1 each year.

### Can I have PMSBY from multiple bank accounts?
You can enroll from multiple accounts, but you can **claim from only one** policy per accident event. Duplicate claims are not allowed.

### I already had an accident before enrolling. Will it be covered?
No — only accidents occurring **after** the policy start date (enrollment or June 1, whichever is applicable) are covered.

### Is there a waiting period?
There is no general waiting period for PMSBY. Coverage starts from the date of enrollment.

### Can a housewife or farmer enroll?
Yes — anyone with a savings bank account aged 18–70 can enroll, regardless of occupation or employment status.
