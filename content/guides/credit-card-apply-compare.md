# Credit Card in India – How to Apply, Compare & Choose the Best Card

A credit card is a powerful financial tool when used responsibly. It offers convenience, rewards, cashback, and helps build your credit score. This guide covers how to choose the right card, compare options, apply online, and use credit cards wisely.

---

## What is a Credit Card?

A credit card lets you borrow money from the issuing bank to make purchases, with the obligation to repay within a billing cycle (usually 45–50 days interest-free). Key features:

- **Credit limit:** ₹10,000 to ₹10+ lakh (based on income and score)
- **Interest-free period:** Up to 45–50 days (if you pay full amount by due date)
- **Interest rate on revolving credit:** 24% – 48% per annum (very high!)
- **Annual fee:** ₹0 to ₹10,000+ depending on card type
- **Rewards:** Points, cashback, airline miles, lounge access

## Who is Eligible?

### Salaried
- Age: 21–60 years
- Minimum income: ₹15,000 – ₹25,000/month (entry-level cards)
- CIBIL score: 700+ (750+ for premium cards)
- Stable employment (6+ months with current employer)

### Self-Employed
- Age: 21–65 years
- ITR for 1–2 years
- Minimum annual income: ₹3–5 lakh
- CIBIL score: 700+

### First-Time Applicants (No Credit History)
- Secured credit card against Fixed Deposit
- Student credit cards (select banks)
- Add-on card on parent's existing card

## Documents Required

- **Identity proof:** Aadhaar card, PAN card
- **Address proof:** Aadhaar, utility bill, passport
- **Income proof:** Salary slips (3 months), bank statements (3 months), or ITR
- **Photographs:** Passport-size (1–2)
- **Employment proof:** Company ID or appointment letter (for salaried)

## How to Apply – Step-by-Step

### Online Application

1. **Check your CIBIL score** at [cibil.com](https://www.cibil.com/) — ensure 700+
2. **Compare cards** on bank websites or comparison sites
3. **Visit the bank's website** or app → Credit Cards section
4. **Fill the application form** with personal, income, and employment details
5. **Upload documents** (KYC, income proof)
6. **Submit application** — you'll receive a reference number
7. **Verification** — bank may call for tele-verification
8. **Approval** — typically 3–7 working days
9. **Card delivery** — sent by courier (5–10 days after approval)
10. **Activate card** — via SMS, net banking, or customer care

### At Bank Branch
1. Visit any branch of the issuing bank
2. Fill physical application form
3. Submit documents with photographs
4. Card processed in 7–14 working days

## Understanding Your Credit Card Bill

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Statement date** | Date when monthly bill is generated |
| **Due date** | Last date to pay (usually 18–20 days after statement) |
| **Total amount due** | Full outstanding amount |
| **Minimum amount due (MAD)** | Minimum to pay to avoid late fee (usually 5% of total) |
| **Interest-free period** | 45–50 days if you pay total due by due date |
| **Finance charge** | Interest on unpaid balance (2%–4% per month!) |

## Important Tips

1. **Always pay full amount by due date** — never just the minimum; revolving credit at 36%+ annual interest is extremely costly
2. **Start with a no-annual-fee card** if you're new to credit cards
3. **Keep credit utilization below 30%** of your limit to maintain a good CIBIL score
4. **Never use credit card for cash withdrawal** — interest starts from day one with no grace period
5. **Set up auto-pay** for at least the minimum amount to avoid late fees and CIBIL impact

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How is the credit limit decided?
Banks set your credit limit based on your income, CIBIL score, existing loans/obligations, and employment stability. Typically, the limit is 2–3 times your monthly income for salaried individuals.

### Will applying for a credit card affect my CIBIL score?
Each application triggers a hard inquiry, which may reduce your score by 5–10 points temporarily. Avoid applying to multiple banks simultaneously.

### Can I get a credit card with a low CIBIL score?
If your score is below 650, most unsecured cards will be rejected. You can apply for a secured credit card (against an FD) to build your credit history.

### What is a billing cycle?
A billing cycle is the period (usually 30 days) between two statement dates. All transactions during this period appear on that month's statement. You then get 18–20 days to pay.

### How do I increase my credit card limit?
Use your card regularly, pay full dues on time, and request a limit increase after 6–12 months. Banks also proactively increase limits based on usage patterns and income growth.

### Should I close unused credit cards?
Not immediately — closing an old card reduces your total credit limit and average account age, both of which can lower your CIBIL score. Keep it open unless it has a high annual fee.
