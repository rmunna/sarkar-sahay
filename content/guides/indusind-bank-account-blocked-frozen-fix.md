# IndusInd Bank Account Blocked or Frozen — How to Fix It (2026)

If your IndusInd Bank account shows "Account Blocked", "Debit Freeze" or you cannot access IndusMobile, IndusDirect (net banking) or make transactions — here is how to get it unblocked step by step.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is independent and not affiliated with IndusInd Bank. Contact IndusInd Bank directly for account-specific help.

---

## Why Is Your IndusInd Account Blocked?

| Reason | Explanation |
|--------|-------------|
| **KYC Not Updated** | RBI mandates periodic KYC compliance |
| **Aadhaar / PAN Not Linked** | Mandatory RBI requirement |
| **Too Many Failed Login Attempts** | 3–5 wrong attempts block IndusMobile / IndusDirect |
| **Suspicious Transactions** | Fraud detection block for unusual activity |
| **Income Tax Department Order** | Tax freeze for pending demands |
| **Dormant Account** | No transactions for 2+ years |

---

## Fix 1: KYC Freeze

**Via IndusMobile App:**
1. Open **IndusMobile** app
2. Look for KYC Update notification or banner
3. Complete Video KYC (face + document scan) if prompted
4. Upload Aadhaar and PAN

**Via IndusDirect (Net Banking):**
1. Login at **[indusind.com](https://www.indusind.com)** → Net Banking
2. Go to **Service Requests → KYC Update**
3. Upload Aadhaar, PAN, address proof and photo
4. Submit — 1–3 working days

**At Branch:**
1. Visit IndusInd Bank branch with original Aadhaar + PAN
2. Fill KYC Update form
3. Account unblocked in 1–3 working days

---

## Fix 2: IndusMobile / IndusDirect Blocked (Wrong Password)

**Net Banking (IndusDirect) Reset:**
1. Go to [indusind.com](https://www.indusind.com) → Internet Banking
2. Click **"Forgot Password"**
3. Enter User ID + registered mobile number
4. OTP verification → Set new password

**IndusMobile App MPIN Reset:**
1. Open IndusMobile → **"Forgot MPIN"**
2. Verify via OTP + debit card details
3. Set new MPIN

---

## Fix 3: Suspicious Activity / Other Block

1. Call **IndusInd Bank Customer Care: 1860-500-5004** (24×7)
2. Explain account is blocked and complete identity verification
3. Request unblock or branch visit as instructed

---

## Fix 4: Dormant Account Reactivation

1. Visit IndusInd Bank branch with Aadhaar + PAN (originals) + 2 passport photos
2. Fill Account Reactivation Form
3. Make a minimum deposit
4. Reactivated in 1–2 working days

## FAQ

**Is my money safe if IndusInd Bank freezes my account?**
Yes — your balance is safe and DICGC-insured up to ₹5 lakh. A freeze restricts transactions only.

**Will EMIs fail during an IndusInd freeze?**
Yes — auto-debits including EMI, SIP, insurance will fail. Inform lenders/insurers immediately.

**Escalation:** Raise grievance at [indusind.com/grievance](https://www.indusind.com) or contact Banking Ombudsman at [bankingombudsman.rbi.org.in](https://bankingombudsman.rbi.org.in).
