# E-Shram Card Registration: Benefits, Eligibility, and How to Apply Online

E-Shram is the **national database for unorganised workers** launched by the Ministry of Labour & Employment, Government of India. By registering on the E-Shram portal, workers get a **Universal Account Number (UAN)** and an E-Shram card, which enables them to access various social security and welfare schemes. Over **29 crore workers** have already registered on the portal.

## What is E-Shram Card?

The E-Shram card is a 12-digit unique identification number (UAN) issued to unorganised sector workers who register on the [eshram.gov.in](https://eshram.gov.in/) portal. It serves as a national database to identify and extend social security benefits to workers in the unorganised sector — including construction workers, street vendors, domestic workers, agricultural labourers, gig workers, and others.

---

## Who is Eligible?

- **Unorganised worker** — any worker engaged in the unorganised sector (home-based, self-employed, or wage worker)
- **Age:** 16 to 59 years
- **Indian citizen**
- **Not a member of EPFO or ESIC** — should not be a member of Employees' Provident Fund Organisation or Employees' State Insurance Corporation
- **Not an income tax payer** (for some linked benefits)

### Types of Workers Covered
- Construction workers, street vendors, domestic workers
- Agricultural labourers, fishermen, MGNREGA workers
- Rickshaw pullers, auto drivers, truck drivers
- Tailors, weavers, artisans, carpenters
- Mid-day meal workers, ASHA workers, Anganwadi workers
- Gig workers (delivery, platform workers)
- Migrant workers, beedi workers, leather workers
- And many more categories

## Benefits of E-Shram Card

1. **Accidental insurance** — ₹2 lakh for accidental death or permanent disability and ₹1 lakh for partial disability under PMSBY (Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana)
2. **Access to government schemes** — E-Shram registered workers can access benefits under PM-SYM, PMJJBY, PMSBY, Atal Pension Yojana, and more
3. **Building and Construction Workers (BoCW)** — data is shared with state BoCW boards for registration
4. **Emergency relief** — during COVID-19 and natural disasters, E-Shram registered workers received direct assistance
5. **Portability** — benefits can be availed across states as the UAN is national
6. **Future social security** — the database enables targeted delivery of welfare benefits

## Documents Required

- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) (mandatory — must be linked to mobile number)
- Active mobile number linked to Aadhaar
- Bank account details (account number, IFSC code)
- No other documents required for basic registration

## Step-by-Step Registration Process

### Online Self-Registration

1. **Visit the portal** — Go to [register.eshram.gov.in](https://register.eshram.gov.in/)
2. **Enter Aadhaar number** — Type your 12-digit Aadhaar number
3. **Enter mobile number** — The mobile number linked to your Aadhaar
4. **Verify captcha** — Enter the captcha code shown
5. **Send OTP** — Click "Send OTP" — OTP will be sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile
6. **Enter OTP** — Type the OTP received on your mobile
7. **Fill details** — Enter personal details (name, DOB, gender), occupation/skill, bank account details, nominee details
8. **Upload photo** — Upload a recent passport-size photograph (if needed)
9. **Review and submit** — Check all details and submit
10. **Download E-Shram card** — After successful registration, download your E-Shram card with UAN number

### Through CSC (Common Service Centre)

1. Visit your nearest **Common Service Centre (CSC)** or Jan Seva Kendra
2. Carry your Aadhaar card and bank passbook
3. The CSC operator will fill your details and complete biometric verification
4. Your E-Shram card will be generated
5. **Fee:** The registration is **free**. Do not pay any agent or CSC for registration charges

### Via Mobile App

1. Download the **Bharat e-Shram** app from Google Play Store
2. Follow the same registration process as online
3. Complete Aadhaar verification via OTP
4. Download your E-Shram card from the app

## Important Tips

1. **Aadhaar-mobile linking is mandatory** — Your mobile number must be linked to Aadhaar. If not linked, visit an Aadhaar centre first
2. **Free registration** — Do not pay any agent or centre for E-Shram registration. It is completely free
3. **Keep your UAN safe** — The 12-digit UAN is your unique identity. Use it to access benefits
4. **Update details** — If you change your bank account, mobile, or address, update on the E-Shram portal
5. **Link to other schemes** — After E-Shram registration, enrol in [Atal Pension Yojana](/guide/atal-pension-yojana) and [PM Shram Yogi Maandhan](/guide/pm-shram-yogi-maandhan) for pension benefits

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. Is E-Shram card mandatory for unorganised workers?
While not legally mandatory, registering gives access to social security benefits and government schemes. It is highly recommended.

### Q2. Do I get money directly for having an E-Shram card?
The E-Shram card itself does not provide direct cash payments. It is a registration that enables access to various schemes like PMSBY (accidental insurance), pension schemes, and emergency relief.

### Q3. Can organised sector workers register on E-Shram?
No. Workers who are members of EPFO or ESIC cannot register on E-Shram. It is only for unorganised sector workers.

### Q4. I don't have a smartphone. How can I register?
Visit your nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) or Jan Seva Kendra. The CSC operator will complete the registration for you at no charge.

### Q5. Can I update my E-Shram card details?
Yes, visit [eshram.gov.in](https://eshram.gov.in/) and login with your UAN to update bank account, mobile number, address, or occupation details.

### Q6. Is there an age limit for E-Shram registration?
Yes, you must be between 16 and 59 years of age to register.

### Q7. What is the accidental insurance benefit amount?
Under PMSBY linked to E-Shram: ₹2 lakh for accidental death or permanent disability, and ₹1 lakh for partial disability.
