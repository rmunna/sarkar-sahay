## What is the Guruji Student Credit Card Scheme?

The Guruji Student Credit Card (GSCC) is a flagship education loan scheme launched by the Government of Jharkhand to provide affordable education loans to students from the state for pursuing higher education. Named after tribal leader Sidho-Kanho, the scheme aims to ensure that no student in Jharkhand is deprived of higher education due to financial constraints.

Under this scheme, students can avail education loans of up to ₹15 lakh at highly subsidized interest rates (effectively 0% for many students, as the government provides interest subvention). The loan covers tuition fees, hostel charges, books, laptops, and other educational expenses.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Permanent resident of Jharkhand** (domicile certificate required)
- **Age:** Up to 25 years at the time of application
- **Education:** Must have passed Class 10 or Class 12 from a recognized board in Jharkhand
- **Admission:** Must have secured admission in a recognized institution for higher education (graduation, post-graduation, diploma, professional courses)
- **Income limit:** Annual family income should not exceed ₹4,00,000 (₹4 lakh)
- **Not a loan defaulter** — neither the student nor the family should have defaulted on any bank loan
- Courses covered: Engineering, Medical, Law, Management, Agriculture, Polytechnic, ITI, and other recognized courses in India

### Loan Details

| Parameter | Details |
|---|---|
| Maximum loan amount | ₹15,00,000 (₹15 lakh) |
| Interest rate | 4% (government subsidizes the interest for eligible students) |
| Repayment period | Starts 1 year after course completion; up to 15 years to repay |
| Collateral | No collateral required up to ₹10 lakh |
| Moratorium | During course duration + 1 year grace period |

## Documents Required

### Mandatory Documents
- **Aadhaar Card** of student and parent/guardian (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- **Domicile certificate** of Jharkhand
- **10th/12th marksheet** and passing certificate
- **Admission letter** from the educational institution
- **Fee structure** of the course (issued by the institution)
- **Income certificate** (annual family income below ₹4 lakh) — from Circle Officer/SDO
- **Bank account details** — student's bank passbook or cancelled cheque
- **Passport-size photographs** of student and co-applicant (parent/guardian)

### Additional Documents
- **Caste certificate** (for SC/ST/OBC students)
- **Previous year's marksheets** (for students already enrolled in a course)
- **Hostel allotment letter** (if claiming hostel expenses)
- **Affidavit** — no existing loan default

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Online Process

1. **Visit** the official GSCC portal at [jsgcc.jharkhand.gov.in](https://jsgcc.jharkhand.gov.in)
2. **Click on "Student Registration"** and register using your mobile number and Aadhaar
3. **Create your login** and verify via OTP
4. **Fill the application form** — personal details, educational background, course details, institution name, loan amount required
5. **Enter co-applicant details** — parent/guardian's information and income details
6. **Upload required documents** — Aadhaar, marksheets, admission letter, fee structure, income certificate, domicile, photos
7. **Select the bank branch** where you want to process the loan
8. **Review and submit** the application
9. **Note your application ID** for tracking
10. **Application is forwarded** to the selected bank for processing and disbursement

### Offline Process

1. **Visit** the District Welfare Office or the designated bank branch
2. **Collect the GSCC application form**
3. **Fill the form** with all student and co-applicant details
4. **Attach all required documents** (self-attested copies)
5. **Submit at the bank branch** designated under the scheme
6. **Bank will verify documents** and process the loan
7. **Loan amount disbursed** directly to the educational institution (tuition) and student's account (other expenses)

## Processing Time

- **Application review:** 15–30 days
- **Bank loan processing:** 30–60 days after document verification
- **Loan disbursement:** Before the start of each semester/academic year, directly to the institution

## Expenses Covered

- Tuition fees and college fees
- Hostel and mess charges
- Books, stationery, and study materials
- Laptop/computer (if required by the course)
- Examination fees
- Travel expenses (reasonable)
- Any other course-related expenses as approved

## Important Tips

1. **Apply as soon as you get the admission letter** — don't wait for classes to start; early applications get processed faster
2. **Choose the right bank branch** — select one near your home or institution for easier document verification
3. **Keep all original documents safe** — the bank may ask for originals during verification
4. **Track your application** on the GSCC portal regularly — respond quickly to any query raised by the bank
5. **Plan your repayment** — the grace period is 1 year after course completion; start planning how you'll repay even before you graduate

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. What is the maximum loan amount under Guruji Student Credit Card?
Students can avail loans up to ₹15 lakh under the GSCC scheme. The actual amount sanctioned depends on the course fee structure and institution.

### Q2. Is collateral required for the loan?
No collateral is required for loans up to ₹10 lakh. For amounts above ₹10 lakh, the bank may ask for collateral or a third-party guarantee.

### Q3. What is the interest rate on GSCC loans?
The interest rate is approximately 4%, with the government providing interest subvention. For many students from weaker economic backgrounds, the effective interest may be close to 0% during the study period.

### Q4. Can I use the loan for courses outside Jharkhand?
Yes, the loan covers higher education at recognized institutions across India, not just within Jharkhand.

### Q5. When do I start repaying the loan?
Repayment begins 1 year after the completion of the course (moratorium period covers the course duration + 1 year). You have up to 15 years to repay.

### Q6. What if I drop out of the course?
If you drop out, the loan repayment may become immediately due. Contact your bank branch to discuss options. The remaining loan amount will not be disbursed.

### Q7. Can I apply if I'm already studying in the middle of my course?
Yes, students already enrolled in an eligible course can apply for the remaining semesters/years. You'll need to provide previous marksheets along with the application.
