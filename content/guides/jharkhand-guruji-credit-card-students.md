## What is the Jharkhand Guruji Student Credit Card Scheme?

The **Guruji Student Credit Card (GSCC)** is a flagship education loan scheme by the Government of Jharkhand that provides affordable loans of **up to ₹15 lakh** to students from the state for pursuing higher education. The scheme aims to ensure that no student in Jharkhand is denied higher education due to financial constraints.

Under GSCC, students receive education loans at a **subsidized interest rate of 4% simple interest per annum**. The government provides interest subvention, making the effective cost of borrowing significantly lower than regular education loans. The loan covers tuition fees, hostel charges, books, laptops, and other educational expenses.

Each year, over **4 lakh students pass Class 10** and over **3 lakh students pass Class 12** in Jharkhand. The GSCC scheme was designed to support meritorious students from financially weaker sections among these graduates to continue their education without financial burden.

## Loan Amount and Interest Rate Details

| Parameter | Details |
|---|---|
| **Maximum loan amount** | ₹15,00,000 (₹15 lakh) |
| **Interest rate** | 4% simple interest per annum |
| **Interest subvention** | Government subsidizes interest for eligible students |
| **Collateral** | Not required for loans up to ₹10 lakh |
| **Collateral (above ₹10 lakh)** | Bank may require collateral or third-party guarantee |
| **Processing fee** | Nil (waived under the scheme) |
| **Moratorium period** | Course duration + 1 year grace period |
| **Repayment period** | Up to 15 years after moratorium ends |

The actual loan amount sanctioned depends on the fee structure of the course and institution. The loan is disbursed semester-wise or year-wise, directly to the educational institution for tuition and to the student's bank account for other approved expenses.

## Who is Eligible for GSCC?

- **Domicile:** Must be a permanent resident of Jharkhand (domicile certificate required)
- **Age:** Up to 25 years at the time of application
- **Education:** Must have passed Class 10 or Class 12 from a recognized board in Jharkhand
- **Admission:** Must have secured admission in a recognized institution for higher education
- **Family income:** Annual family income should not exceed **₹4,00,000 (₹4 lakh)**
- **No loan default:** Neither the student nor the family should have defaulted on any bank loan

### Eligible Courses

The GSCC scheme covers a wide range of higher education courses at recognized institutions across India:

- **Engineering & Technology** — B.Tech, B.E., Polytechnic, Diploma
- **Medical & Health Sciences** — MBBS, BDS, BAMS, B.Pharm, Nursing, Paramedical
- **Law** — BA LLB, LLB
- **Management** — BBA, MBA
- **Agriculture** — B.Sc Agriculture, B.V.Sc
- **Arts, Science & Commerce** — BA, B.Sc, B.Com, MA, M.Sc, M.Com
- **Professional courses** — CA, CS, Hotel Management
- **ITI and vocational** — Industrial Training Institute courses
- **Post-graduation** — Any recognized PG course in India

## Documents Required

### Mandatory Documents
- **Aadhaar Card** of student and parent/guardian (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- **Domicile certificate** of Jharkhand
- **10th/12th marksheet** and passing certificate
- **Admission letter** from the educational institution
- **Fee structure** of the course (issued by the institution)
- **Income certificate** (annual family income below ₹4 lakh) — from Circle Officer/SDO
- **Bank account details** — student's bank passbook or cancelled cheque
- **Passport-size photographs** of student and co-applicant (parent/guardian)

### Additional Documents
- **Caste certificate** (for SC/ST/OBC students)
- **Previous year's marksheets** (for students already enrolled in a course)
- **Hostel allotment letter** (if claiming hostel expenses)
- **Affidavit** — declaration of no existing loan default

## How to Apply for Guruji Student Credit Card Online

### Online Application Process

1. **Visit** the official GSCC portal at [gscc.jharkhand.gov.in](https://gscc.jharkhand.gov.in)
2. **Click on "Student Registration"** and register using your mobile number and Aadhaar
3. **Create your login** and verify via OTP sent to your registered mobile number
4. **Fill the application form** — enter personal details, educational background, course details, institution name, and loan amount required
5. **Enter co-applicant details** — parent/guardian's information, occupation, and income details
6. **Upload required documents** — scan and upload Aadhaar, marksheets, admission letter, fee structure, income certificate, domicile certificate, and photographs
7. **Select the bank branch** where you want to process the loan (choose one near your home or institution)
8. **Review and submit** the application carefully
9. **Note your application ID** — save this for tracking your application status
10. **Application is forwarded** to the selected bank for verification, processing, and disbursement

### Offline Application Process

Students can also apply through the **"Aapki Yojana, Aapki Sarkar — Aapke Dwar"** camps organized at block-level offices:

1. **Visit** the nearest block office and find out about the next camp schedule
2. **Collect the GSCC application form** at the camp or District Welfare Office
3. **Fill the form** with complete student and co-applicant details
4. **Attach all required documents** (self-attested copies)
5. **Submit at the designated bank branch** under the scheme
6. **Bank verifies documents** and processes the loan
7. **Loan disbursed** directly to the educational institution (tuition) and student's account (living expenses)

## How to Check GSCC Application Status

After submitting your application, you can track the status:

1. **Visit** [gscc.jharkhand.gov.in](https://gscc.jharkhand.gov.in)
2. **Log in** using your registered mobile number and password
3. **Go to "Application Status"** section in your dashboard
4. **View current status** — the portal shows whether your application is pending, under verification, approved, or disbursed
5. You can also **contact the designated bank branch** directly for updates on loan processing

**Typical processing timeline:**
- Application review by department: **15–30 days**
- Bank loan processing and verification: **30–60 days**
- Loan disbursement: Before the start of each semester/academic year

## Expenses Covered Under GSCC

The Guruji Student Credit Card loan covers the following education-related expenses:

- Tuition fees and college/university fees
- Hostel and mess charges
- Books, stationery, and study materials
- Laptop or computer (if required by the course)
- Examination and lab fees
- Travel expenses (reasonable, for outstation students)
- Any other course-related expenses approved by the institution

## Repayment Terms

Understanding the repayment structure is important before availing the loan:

- **During the course:** No repayment required. The moratorium period covers the entire course duration
- **Grace period:** 1 year after course completion — no EMI payments during this time
- **Repayment starts:** After moratorium (course duration + 1 year grace period)
- **Maximum repayment tenure:** Up to **15 years** from the date repayment begins
- **Interest rate:** 4% simple interest per annum on the outstanding amount
- **EMI calculation:** Monthly installments based on the total loan amount, interest, and chosen tenure
- **Prepayment:** Students can prepay the loan without any penalty
- **Default consequences:** Loan default may affect future credit eligibility of both student and co-applicant

**Example:** If you take a ₹10 lakh loan for a 4-year B.Tech course, your moratorium period is 5 years (4 years course + 1 year grace). Repayment starts in year 6. With a 15-year repayment tenure at 4% simple interest, your approximate monthly EMI would be around ₹5,800–₹6,500.

## GSCC vs Bihar Student Credit Card: A Comparison

Both Jharkhand and Bihar offer student credit card schemes. Here's how they compare:

| Feature | Jharkhand GSCC | Bihar Student Credit Card (BSCC) |
|---|---|---|
| **Loan amount** | Up to ₹15 lakh | Up to ₹4 lakh |
| **Interest rate** | 4% simple interest | 4% (1% for women/transgender/disabled) |
| **Income limit** | ₹4 lakh per annum | No income limit |
| **Age limit** | Up to 25 years | Up to 25 years |
| **Collateral** | Not required up to ₹10 lakh | No collateral required |
| **Repayment period** | Up to 15 years | Up to 15 years |
| **Portal** | gscc.jharkhand.gov.in | 7nishchay-yuvaupmission.bihar.gov.in |

**Key takeaway:** Jharkhand's GSCC offers a significantly **higher loan amount (₹15 lakh vs ₹4 lakh)** compared to Bihar's scheme. However, Bihar's scheme has **no income limit** and offers a **lower interest rate for women and disabled students**. ## Important Tips for Applicants

1. **Apply early** — submit your application as soon as you receive the admission letter; don't wait for classes to start
2. **Choose the right bank branch** — select one near your home or institution for easier document verification
3. **Keep originals safe** — the bank will verify original documents during processing
4. **Track regularly** — check your application status on the GSCC portal and respond quickly to any queries from the bank
5. **Maintain academic performance** — continued disbursement for subsequent semesters may depend on passing previous exams
6. **Plan repayment early** — start saving or planning your career before the grace period ends
7. **Check other scholarships** — you may also be eligible for scholarships through the [National Scholarship Portal](/guide/national-scholarship-portal-apply) alongside this loan

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. What is the maximum loan amount under Guruji Student Credit Card?
Students can get loans of **up to ₹15 lakh** under the GSCC scheme. The actual amount depends on the course fee structure and institution. The loan is disbursed semester-wise or year-wise.

### Q2. Is collateral required for the GSCC loan?
**No collateral is required for loans up to ₹10 lakh.** For loan amounts between ₹10–15 lakh, the bank may ask for collateral or a third-party guarantee.

### Q3. What is the interest rate on GSCC loans?
The interest rate is **4% simple interest per annum**. The government provides interest subvention, making this significantly cheaper than regular education loans (which typically charge 8–12%).

### Q4. Can I use the loan for courses outside Jharkhand?
**Yes.** The GSCC loan covers higher education at any recognized institution across India, not just within Jharkhand. The institution must be recognized by the relevant regulatory body (UGC, AICTE, etc.).

### Q5. When do I start repaying the loan?
Repayment begins **1 year after course completion**. The moratorium period covers your entire course duration plus a 1-year grace period. You then have up to 15 years to repay.

### Q6. What if I drop out of the course?
If you drop out, loan repayment may become **immediately due**. The remaining loan amount will not be disbursed. Contact your bank branch to discuss restructuring options.

### Q7. Can I apply if I'm already in the middle of my course?
**Yes.** Students already enrolled in an eligible course can apply for the remaining semesters or years. You'll need to provide previous marksheets along with your application.

### Q8. How is GSCC different from a regular education loan?
GSCC offers **4% interest** (vs 8–12% for bank education loans), **no processing fee**, and **no collateral up to ₹10 lakh**. Regular education loans have higher interest rates and often require collateral even for smaller amounts.

### Q9. Can I get both GSCC loan and a scholarship?
**Yes.** The GSCC loan and government scholarships are separate. If you are eligible, you can receive scholarships through the [National Scholarship Portal](/guide/national-scholarship-portal-apply) or state-specific schemes while also having a GSCC loan.

### Q10. What happens if the bank rejects my application?
If rejected, check the reason (usually incomplete documents or income above the limit). You can **reapply after fixing the issue**. Contact the District Welfare Office for assistance.
