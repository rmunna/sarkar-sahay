# Tripura Girl Child Incentive Scheme – Complete Guide 2026

## What is the Tripura Girl Child Incentive Scheme?

The **Tripura Girl Child Incentive Scheme** is a welfare initiative by the Government of Tripura aimed at promoting the welfare and education of the girl child. The scheme provides financial incentives at various stages — from birth to education milestones — to encourage families to educate their daughters and prevent child marriage.

The scheme works alongside central programmes like **Beti Bachao Beti Padhao** and aims to improve the status of girls in Tripura by reducing gender disparity.

## Benefits

The scheme provides financial assistance at key milestones:

| Stage | Approximate Benefit |
|-------|-------------------|
| Birth of girl child | ₹5,000 (one-time) |
| Admission to school | ₹5,000-10,000 |
| Completion of Class 10 | ₹10,000-15,000 |
| Completion of Class 12 | ₹15,000-20,000 |
| Higher education / marriage (after 18) | Additional benefits |

> **Note:** Exact amounts may vary based on current government notifications. Verify with the District Social Welfare Office.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Gender:** Girl child
- **Residency:** Born to a permanent resident of Tripura
- **Birth registration:** Birth must be registered
- **Family income:** BPL or economically weaker section families get priority
- **Family size:** Benefits may be limited to **first two girl children** per family
- **Immunization:** Girl child must complete prescribed immunization schedule

## Documents Required

### At Birth
- Birth certificate of the girl child
- Aadhaar Card of parent(s)
- Hospital discharge certificate / institutional delivery proof
- BPL card / income certificate
- Bank passbook of parent (Aadhaar-linked)

### At Education Milestones
- School admission certificate / bonafide certificate
- Marksheet of relevant class (for Class 10/12 benefits)
- Aadhaar Card of the girl (if available)
- Continuation certificate from school

## How to Apply – Step-by-Step

### At Birth

1. **Register the birth** at the nearest Municipal/Panchayat office
2. Visit the **Anganwadi Centre** or **Block Social Welfare Office**
3. Collect the **Girl Child Incentive application form**
4. Fill in details of parents, birth, and bank account
5. Attach birth certificate, parent Aadhaar, and income proof
6. Submit at the Anganwadi Centre or Block office
7. After verification, the incentive is credited to the parent's bank account

### At Education Milestones

1. Obtain the relevant **marksheet or admission proof** from the school
2. Visit the **District Social Welfare Office** or **Block office**
3. Fill the milestone-claim form
4. Attach marksheet, school certificate, and previous claim receipt
5. Submit and wait for verification and payment

## Processing Time

- **Verification:** 2-4 weeks
- **Payment:** 1-3 months after submission

## Important Tips

1. **Register the birth immediately** — delayed registration complicates applications
2. **Keep all educational certificates** safe for claiming milestone benefits
3. **Complete immunization** as per government schedule — it may be a prerequisite
4. **Ensure institutional delivery** — hospital births get priority under many schemes
5. **Don't delay milestone claims** — apply as soon as the girl reaches each stage

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Is this scheme only for BPL families?
**A:** BPL families get priority, but the scheme may be available to all families below a certain income threshold. Check with the Social Welfare Office.

### Q2: Can I claim for a third girl child?
**A:** Benefits are typically limited to **first two girl children** per family. Exceptions may apply in specific cases.

### Q3: Is birth in a government hospital mandatory?
**A:** Institutional delivery (government or private hospital) is generally preferred. Home births with registered birth certificates may also qualify.

### Q4: Can I avail this along with Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana?
**A:** Yes, the Tripura Girl Child Incentive is a **state scheme** and can generally be availed alongside central schemes like Sukanya Samriddhi.

### Q5: What if I missed claiming the birth-stage benefit?
**A:** Contact the **District Social Welfare Office** for late claims. Some flexibility may be available depending on current policy.

### Q6: How is the money paid?
**A:** Through **DBT** to the parent's or girl child's (when older) Aadhaar-linked bank account.
