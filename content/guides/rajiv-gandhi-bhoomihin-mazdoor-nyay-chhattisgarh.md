## What is Rajiv Gandhi Bhoomihin Mazdoor Nyay Yojana?

**Rajiv Gandhi Bhoomihin Mazdoor Nyay Yojana** is Chhattisgarh's income support scheme for **landless agricultural labourers** — those who earn their living by working on others' land but own no agricultural land themselves. The scheme provides **₹7,000 per year** directly to eligible landless workers through DBT.

Launched in 2021, this scheme specifically addresses the section of rural society not covered by farmer-focused schemes like PM-KISAN (which requires land ownership).

## Who is Eligible?

**You ARE eligible if:**
- You are a **Chhattisgarh resident**
- You work as an **agricultural labourer** (farm work on others' land)
- You and your family **do not own agricultural land**
- You are not employed in any government job
- You are **not an income tax payer**

**Not eligible:**
- Farmers who own land (they get Rajiv Gandhi Kisan Nyay Yojana instead)
- Non-agricultural workers (construction, factory, domestic)
- Government employees

---

## Rajiv Gandhi Kisan Nyay Yojana (Separate Scheme)

If you are a farmer with land, the correct scheme is **Rajiv Gandhi Kisan Nyay Yojana** which provides ₹6,000-9,000/year based on crop area to Chhattisgarh farmers. This is separate from the Bhoomihin Mazdoor scheme.

---

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card**
2. **Ration card** (CG)
3. **Residence proof** (voter ID, ration card with CG address)
4. **Declaration of landless status** — self-declaration + gram panchayat verification
5. **Bank passbook** — Aadhaar-linked
6. **Gram Sabha verification** — confirmation that applicant is an agricultural labourer

---

## How to Register

### Step 1 — Register at Gram Panchayat

Visit your **Gram Panchayat** with Aadhaar and ration card:
- Fill the Bhoomihin Mazdoor registration form
- Gram Panchayat verifies your landless status through local records
- Your name is forwarded to block/district for verification

### Step 2 — Online Registration

Also available at **[rgbmny.cg.nic.in](https://rgbmny.cg.nic.in)**:
- Enter Aadhaar, ration card details
- Self-certify landless agricultural labourer status
- Link bank account

### Step 3 — Gram Sabha Approval

The Gram Sabha (village assembly) approves the list of eligible beneficiaries. This community verification prevents fraud.

### Step 4 — Annual Payment

After approval, ₹7,000 is credited to your Aadhaar-linked bank account once a year. Check your bank account for the payment.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. I am a daily wage farm worker. I own a small house in the village but no farm land. Am I eligible?**

Yes. The scheme requires that you have no **agricultural land** — residential plot or house does not disqualify you. Being a daily wage farm labourer with no farm land makes you eligible.

**2. I do farm work 6 months and construction work 6 months. Am I still eligible?**

Agricultural labourers who also do other work are typically eligible if their primary identity and registration is as an agricultural labourer. The gram panchayat will verify. If construction work is your primary income, eligibility may be questioned.

**3. My wife is the registered agricultural labourer. Can she get the benefit?**

Yes. The benefit is individual — if your wife is the registered landless agricultural labourer, the ₹7,000 is credited to her Aadhaar-linked account.

**4. Is there a separate scheme for non-agricultural landless workers in CG?**

For landless construction workers, check **BOCW Chhattisgarh** — Building and Other Construction Workers welfare scheme. For other non-agricultural landless workers, check the Chhattisgarh Social Security schemes at the District Social Welfare office.

**5. How do I check if my name is on the beneficiary list?**

Check at rgbmny.cg.nic.in using Aadhaar or ration card number. Also ask at your Gram Panchayat or local agriculture office.
