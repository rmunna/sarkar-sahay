## What is Manav Garima Yojana?

Manav Garima Yojana (Human Dignity Scheme) is a welfare scheme by the Government of Gujarat that provides **free tool kits and equipment** to individuals from **Scheduled Castes (SC)** to help them start or improve their self-employment activities. The scheme aims to uplift the economic condition of SC families by enabling them to earn a livelihood with dignity.

The scheme is administered by the **Scheduled Caste Welfare Department** under the Director of Developing Caste Welfare, Gujarat. A similar scheme exists for **Scheduled Tribes (ST)** under the Tribal Development Department.

## Benefits

The scheme provides **tool kits/equipment worth up to ₹15,000** (for individual occupations) based on the trade or occupation of the applicant. The tools are provided **free of cost** — no repayment required.

### Occupations Covered

Tool kits are provided for the following self-employment activities:

- **Carpentry** — Saw, hammer, chisel, plane, measuring tools
- **Tailoring** — Sewing machine, scissors, measuring tape, thread
- **Plumbing** — Pipe wrench, cutter, fittings, tool box
- **Welding** — Welding machine, rods, safety gear
- **Electric work** — Multimeter, wire cutter, pliers, tester
- **Mobile repair** — Repair kit, soldering iron, tools
- **Beauty parlour** — Hair dryer, scissors, styling tools, chair
- **Pottery** — Potter's wheel, kiln accessories
- **Shoe making/repair** — Tools, leather, sewing equipment
- **Vegetable vending** — Weighing scale, baskets, cart
- **Masonry** — Trowel, level, hammer, plumb bob
- **Laundry/ironing** — Iron press, ironing board, hangers
- **And more** — Various trades as notified by the department

## Who is Eligible?

- Must belong to **Scheduled Caste (SC)** category
- Must be a **permanent resident of Gujarat**
- **Age:** 18 years or above
- **Income:** Family annual income must not exceed **₹47,000** (rural) or **₹68,000** (urban) — *these limits are revised periodically*
- Must be engaged in or intending to start the **specified occupation**
- Should **not have received** the same benefit under this scheme before
- Must have a valid **caste certificate** from the competent authority

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar Card** — [See our Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
- **Caste Certificate** (SC) issued by Mamlatdar/Collector
- **Income Certificate** (from Mamlatdar)
- **Domicile Certificate** (Gujarat resident proof)
- **Age proof** (birth certificate, school leaving certificate, or Aadhaar)
- **Bank account passbook** (Aadhaar-linked)
- **Passport-size photographs** (3 copies)
- **Self-declaration** that the applicant has not received the benefit earlier
- **Ration card** (if available)
- **Proof of occupation** or trade skill (if available — training certificate, experience letter)

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Online Application (e-Samaj Kalyan Portal)

1. **Visit the portal** — Go to [esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in)
2. **Register** — Create a new account with Aadhaar number, mobile, and email
3. **Login** and select **"Manav Garima Yojana"** from the scheme list
4. **Fill application** — Enter personal details, caste, income, chosen occupation/trade
5. **Upload documents** — Caste certificate, income certificate, Aadhaar, photo, bank passbook
6. **Submit** — Review and submit; note the application ID
7. **Verification** — District social welfare officer verifies documents
8. **Approval** — Application approved after verification
9. **Tool kit distribution** — Beneficiary is invited to collect the tool kit from the district office or a distribution event

### Offline Application

1. Visit the **District Social Welfare Office** or **Mamlatdar Office**
2. Collect the Manav Garima Yojana application form
3. Fill the form completely
4. Attach self-attested photocopies of all documents
5. Submit at the office
6. Collect acknowledgement receipt

## Processing Time

- **Application to verification:** 15-30 days
- **Verification to approval:** 15-30 days
- **Tool kit distribution:** 1-3 months after approval (depends on procurement cycle)
- **Total:** 2-4 months approximately

## How to Check Status

1. Login to [esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in)
2. Navigate to **"Application Status"**
3. Enter your application number
4. View the current status and any remarks

## Important Tips

1. **Choose the right trade** — Select an occupation you are genuinely skilled in or plan to pursue; the tools are specific to each trade
2. **Get income certificate fresh** — Income certificates older than 6 months may not be accepted; get a recent one
3. **Keep caste certificate handy** — The SC caste certificate must be issued by the Mamlatdar or District Collector of Gujarat
4. **Apply early in the financial year** — Funds are allocated annually; applying early increases your chances of selection in the current year
5. **Maintain the tools** — The tool kit is given free but is meant for productive use; some districts may verify that tools are being used for the intended purpose

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Can OBC/General category people apply?

No, Manav Garima Yojana is specifically for **Scheduled Caste (SC)** beneficiaries. OBC and General category individuals should check other self-employment schemes like PMEGP or Mudra Loan.

### Q2: Is there a similar scheme for ST communities?

Yes, the **Gujarat Tribal Development Department** runs a similar tool kit scheme for Scheduled Tribes. You can check the tribal welfare portal for details.

### Q3: Can women apply?

Absolutely. **Women are encouraged** to apply, especially for trades like tailoring, beauty parlour, and embroidery. There is no gender restriction.

### Q4: Can I apply for more than one trade?

No, you can apply for **only one trade/occupation** at a time. Choose the trade that best suits your skills and livelihood plans.

### Q5: What if my income certificate shows higher income?

If your income exceeds the prescribed limit (₹47,000 rural / ₹68,000 urban), you are **not eligible**. Ensure your income certificate accurately reflects your current family income.

### Q6: Do I need to return the tools?

No, the tool kit is a **grant (free)** and does not need to be returned. However, it is expected that you use the tools for the specified occupation and self-employment.

### Q7: Can I apply if I already have some tools?

Yes, you can apply even if you have some tools. The scheme is meant to supplement your equipment. However, if you have already received the **same scheme benefit** earlier, you cannot apply again.
