# REET (Rajasthan Eligibility Examination for Teachers) Guide — Eligibility, Pattern & Tips

The Board of Secondary Education, Rajasthan (BSER) conducts the Rajasthan Eligibility Examination for Teachers (REET) for candidates aspiring to become government school teachers in Rajasthan. REET qualification is mandatory for teaching posts in Rajasthan government schools.

REET is one of the largest state-level teacher eligibility exams in India, with lakhs of applicants each cycle.

## Eligibility Criteria

### Age Limit

| Category | Minimum Age | Maximum Age |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| General | 18 years | 40 years |
| OBC | 18 years | 43 years |
| SC/ST | 18 years | 45 years |

### Educational Qualification

**Level 1 (Classes 1-5):**
- Senior Secondary (12th) with at least 50% marks AND D.El.Ed/BTC
- OR Graduation with B.Ed

**Level 2 (Classes 6-8):**
- Graduation with at least 50% marks AND B.Ed from NCTE recognized institution

## Exam Pattern

### Level 1 (Primary: Classes 1-5)

| Subject | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| Child Development & Pedagogy | 30 | 30 |
| Language I (Hindi/English/etc.) | 30 | 30 |
| Language II | 30 | 30 |
| Mathematics | 30 | 30 |
| Environmental Studies | 30 | 30 |
| **Total** | **150** | **150** |

### Level 2 (Upper Primary: Classes 6-8)

| Subject | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| Child Development & Pedagogy | 30 | 30 |
| Language I | 30 | 30 |
| Language II | 30 | 30 |
| Maths & Science OR Social Studies | 60 | 60 |
| **Total** | **150** | **150** |

- **Duration:** 2 hours 30 minutes
- **No negative marking**
- **Qualifying:** General 60%, Reserved 55%

## Syllabus Overview

### Child Development & Pedagogy
- Piaget, Vygotsky, Kohlberg, learning theories, inclusive education, NCF 2005, RTE Act

### Languages
- Comprehension, grammar, pedagogy of language

### Mathematics & EVS
- Number system, geometry, measurement, environment, pedagogy

## How to Apply

1. Visit [reetbser.com](https://reetbser.com) or BSER website
2. Register and select Level 1, Level 2, or both
3. Fill in personal and educational details
4. Upload photo and signature
5. Pay fee and submit

## Application Fee

| Category | Fee (One Level) | Fee (Both Levels) |
|----------|-----------------|-------------------|
| General | ₹550 | ₹750 |
| OBC | ₹450 | ₹650 |
| SC/ST | ₹350 | ₹550 |

## Salary & Benefits

- **Level 1 Teacher (3rd Grade):** ₹23,700 per month + DA, HRA
- **Level 2 Teacher (3rd Grade):** ₹28,700 per month + DA, HRA
- Government pension (NPS), medical benefits

## Preparation Tips

1. **Rajasthan-specific content:** REET includes Rajasthan history and culture in GK portions
2. **Child Development:** Master key theories — highest scoring section
3. **NCERT books:** Study relevant NCERT textbooks
4. **Previous REET papers:** Pattern is consistent — solve at least 5 years
5. **Pedagogy focus:** Understand teaching methods, not just subject content

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the validity of REET certificate?
REET certificate is valid for 3 years from the date of result.

### Can I appear for both Level 1 and Level 2?
Yes, you can appear for both levels.

### Is REET enough to become a teacher?
No, REET is eligibility only. Teacher recruitment is conducted separately.

### Is there negative marking?
No, there is no negative marking.

### What is the qualifying score?
General: 60% (90/150), Reserved: 55% (82.5/150).

### How often is REET conducted?
REET is typically conducted every 2-3 years based on vacancy requirements.
