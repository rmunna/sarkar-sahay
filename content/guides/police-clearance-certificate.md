## What is a Police Clearance Certificate?

A Police Clearance Certificate (PCC) is an official document certifying that a person has **no criminal record** in India. It is issued by the **Passport Seva Kendra** or the **Regional Passport Office (RPO)** based on police verification records.

PCC is commonly required for:
- **Immigration/visa** applications to other countries
- **Employment abroad** — Employers in foreign countries require PCC
- **Foreign citizenship** or permanent residency applications
- **Adoption** proceedings (international)
- **Some domestic purposes** — Government jobs, security clearances

PCC is different from a character certificate or police verification. It specifically certifies the absence of criminal records.

## Eligibility

- Any **Indian citizen** holding a valid Indian passport
- PCC is issued based on the address mentioned in your **current passport**
- If you've lived at different addresses, police verification may be conducted at all addresses

## Documents Required

- **Valid Indian passport** (original and photocopy)
- **Online application form** from Passport Seva portal
- **Proof of present address** (if different from passport address):
  - Aadhaar card — see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
  - Utility bills (electricity, water, gas)
  - Bank statement
  - Rent agreement
- **Passport-size photographs** (2 copies)
- **Fee payment receipt**

If PCC is for a specific country, you may also need a letter from the embassy or employer specifying the requirement.

## How to Apply for PCC Online

### Step 1: Register on Passport Seva Portal
1. Visit [passportindia.gov.in](https://www.passportindia.gov.in)
2. Click **"Register Now"** if you don't have an account
3. Create account with email and password
4. Verify your email

### Step 2: Log In and Apply
1. Log in to your account
2. Click **"Apply for Police Clearance Certificate"**
3. Select the type of application

### Step 3: Fill the Application Form
- **Personal details** — Name, date of birth, place of birth
- **Family details** — Father's/mother's/spouse's name
- **Present and permanent address**
- **Passport details** — Passport number, date of issue, place of issue
- **Purpose of PCC** — Employment, immigration, education, etc.
- **Country for which PCC is required**

### Step 4: Upload Documents
Upload scanned copies of:
- Passport first and last page
- Address proof (if current address differs from passport)
- Supporting documents based on purpose

### Step 5: Pay the Fee
- PCC fee: **₹500**
- Pay online through credit/debit card, net banking, or SBI challan

### Step 6: Schedule Appointment
1. Select the **Passport Seva Kendra (PSK)** or RPO nearest to you
2. Choose a convenient date and time slot
3. Print the appointment confirmation

### Step 7: Visit PSK/RPO
On the appointment date:
1. Carry **original passport** and all documents
2. Arrive 15 minutes before your slot
3. Go through document verification at the counter
4. Biometric capture (if required)
5. Submission and acknowledgement

### Step 8: Receive PCC
- If police verification is **already done** (recent passport issuance): PCC may be issued **on the same day** or within 2-3 days
- If **new police verification** is needed: 2-4 weeks
- PCC is sent by Speed Post or can be collected from the RPO
- Also available for download on the Passport Seva portal

## PCC Processing Time

| Scenario | Time |
|----------|------|
| Clear police record (already verified) | **Same day to 3 days** |
| Police verification required | **2-4 weeks** |
| Address mismatch or multiple addresses | **3-6 weeks** |
| Re-issue (if original expired) | **1-3 days** |

## Validity of PCC

- PCC is typically valid for **6 months** to **1 year** from the date of issue
- Different countries may have their own validity requirements
- Check with the embassy/employer for the specific validity accepted

## PCC for Different Countries

Different countries have different PCC requirements:

| Country | PCC Name | Validity |
|---------|----------|---------|
| USA | Police Clearance Certificate | 6-12 months |
| Canada | Police Certificate | 6 months |
| Australia | National Police Check | 12 months |
| UK | ACRO Police Certificate | 6 months |
| UAE | Police Clearance | 3-6 months |
| Germany | Certificate of Good Conduct | 6 months |

**Note:** Some countries may require PCC from every country you've lived in for more than 6-12 months, not just India.

## What If Police Verification Reveals Issues?

If there's a pending case or FIR against you:
- PCC will **not be issued** until the case is resolved
- If the case resulted in acquittal, provide the court order
- Minor traffic violations generally don't affect PCC
- Withdrawn or compounded cases may not impact PCC (varies by RPO)

## Important Tips

1. **Apply well in advance** — Start the process at least 4-6 weeks before you need the PCC
2. **Ensure passport address is current** — If you've moved, update your passport first or carry strong address proof
3. **Check country-specific requirements** — Some countries need apostilled or embassy-attested PCC
4. **Same-day PCC** is possible if your police verification is clean and recent — apply early in the morning
5. **Carry original passport** to the PSK — Photocopies alone won't work

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I apply for PCC without a passport?
No, a valid Indian passport is mandatory for PCC application. The PCC is issued by the passport authority based on passport records. If you don't have a passport, apply for one first — see our [passport guide](/guide/passport-apply-online).

### How much does PCC cost?
The fee for PCC is **₹500**, payable online or through SBI challan. There are no additional charges unless you use the Tatkal (urgent) facility.

### Can I get PCC the same day?
Yes, if your police verification was done recently (during passport issuance) and the record is clear, PCC can be issued on the same day of your PSK visit.

### Is PCC and police verification the same thing?
No. Police verification is the background check conducted by the police. PCC is the certificate issued by the passport office based on (among other things) the police verification report.

### Can NRIs apply for PCC from India?
Yes, NRIs can apply for PCC at any PSK/RPO in India. They can also apply through Indian missions abroad (embassy/consulate) in the country where they reside.

### Does PCC mention specific details of criminal record?
If your record is clean, PCC simply states that no criminal record was found. If there are records, the PCC may be denied or issued with details of the case (this varies by RPO and purpose).
