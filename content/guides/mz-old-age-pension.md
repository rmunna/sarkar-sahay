# Mizoram Old Age Pension – Complete Guide 2026

## What is the Mizoram Old Age Pension?

The **Mizoram Old Age Pension** provides monthly financial assistance to senior citizens from BPL families under the **Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS)** with a state top-up from the Mizoram government.

## Pension Amount

| Age Group | Approximate Monthly Pension |
|-----------|---------------------------|
| 60-79 years | ₹400-800 |
| 80+ years | ₹700-1,200 |

> Includes central and state components. Verify with District Social Welfare Office.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Age:** 60 years or above
- **Residency:** Permanent resident of Mizoram
- **Income:** BPL category or below prescribed income limit
- **Not receiving** any other government pension or retirement benefits

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar Card
- Age proof (birth certificate / voter ID / church register extract)
- BPL card or income certificate
- Bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked)
- Residential proof (voter ID / inner line permit documentation)
- Passport-size photographs (2)
- Village Council certificate (for rural applicants)

## How to Apply – Step-by-Step

1. Visit the **Village Council office** (rural) or **Municipal office** (urban)
2. Collect the **Old Age Pension application form**
3. Fill in personal, age, income, and bank details
4. Attach self-attested document copies
5. Get verified by the **Village Council President / Ward Councillor**
6. Submit at the **District Social Welfare Office** or **Block office**
7. After verification and approval, pension is credited via DBT monthly

## Processing Time

- **Verification to first payment:** 2-4 months

## Important Tips

1. **Village Council verification** is important in Mizoram — get their endorsement
2. **Link Aadhaar to bank account** before applying
3. **Annual life verification** is mandatory
4. **Church register** can serve as age proof if birth certificate is unavailable
5. **Contact the District Social Welfare Officer** for any issues

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: What is the pension amount?
**A:** Approximately **₹400-800/month** for 60-79 years and **₹700-1,200** for 80+.

### Q2: Can I apply online?
**A:** Check [nsap.nic.in](https://nsap.nic.in). Primary method is offline through Village Council/Municipal office.

### Q3: Is Village Council certificate mandatory?
**A:** Yes, for rural applicants. The Village Council verifies residency and eligibility.

### Q4: What if I don't have a birth certificate?
**A:** Alternative age proofs like **voter ID, church register extract, or school certificate** are accepted.

### Q5: How is the pension paid?
**A:** Through **DBT** to the Aadhaar-linked bank account.
