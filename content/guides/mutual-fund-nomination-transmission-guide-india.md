# Mutual Fund Nomination & Transmission India 2026 — Complete Guide

SEBI mandated nominations for all mutual fund folios by December 31, 2023. If you haven't added a nominee, your folio may be frozen for new transactions. After an investor's death, nominees or legal heirs claim units through "transmission" — a structured process that avoids court proceedings for most situations.

---

## Why Nomination Is Critical

Without a valid nomination:
- Your folio may be restricted from new purchases (post December 2023 SEBI deadline)
- After your death: family needs legal heir certificate/succession certificate from court (3–6 months, expensive)
- With nomination: units transfer to nominee in 7–30 days with just identity documents

Nomination is not inheritance — it's a custodial arrangement. Nominee holds units on behalf of legal heirs (if nominee isn't the legal heir). But in practice, if the nominee is your spouse/child, the process is smooth and courts are not involved.

## Add Nominee to Mutual Fund Folio — Step by Step

### Via MF Central (mfcentral.com) — Best Option

MF Central allows adding/changing nomination for ALL your folios across ALL AMCs from one place:

1. Login at [mfcentral.com](https://mfcentral.com) using PAN + OTP
2. Services → Nomination → Add/Modify Nomination
3. Select folio(s) — you can bulk-add nomination to multiple folios at once
4. Enter nominee details:
   - Full name (as per ID)
   - Relationship with investor
   - Date of birth
   - Percentage of units (if multiple nominees)
   - Guardian details (if nominee is minor)
5. Upload nominee's ID proof (Aadhaar / PAN)
6. Authenticate with OTP or e-sign via Aadhaar
7. Done — nomination active within 24 hours

### Via Zerodha Coin

1. Open Coin → Account → Nomination
2. Add nominee details
3. e-Sign with Aadhaar OTP
4. Applies to all Zerodha Coin folios

### Via Groww

1. Groww → Account → Nominations
2. Add nominee — name, relationship, percentage, DOB
3. Confirm with OTP

### Via AMC Website Directly

Every AMC (SBI MF, HDFC AMC, ICICI Pru AMC, etc.) has a nomination section in their investor portal. Login with folio number + PAN → Services → Nomination.

## How Many Nominees Can You Add?

SEBI allows up to **3 nominees per folio**. Specify percentage allocation for each:
- Nominee 1: 50%
- Nominee 2: 30%
- Nominee 3: 20%
- Total must = 100%

**If only one nominee**: 100% allocation (default).

**Minor as nominee**: Allowed — add guardian's details (name + relationship). Guardian manages units until nominee turns 18.

---

## Opt-Out of Nomination (If You Choose)

If you don't want to add any nominee (rare — perhaps HUF accounts, joint accounts with survivorship):
- Submit "Declaration for not providing Nomination" (Form available at MF Central and AMC portals)
- This satisfies the SEBI compliance without actually naming a nominee

## Transmission of Mutual Fund Units After Investor's Death

### What Is Transmission?

Transmission = transfer of mutual fund units from a deceased investor's folio to the nominee's (or legal heir's) folio. This is NOT the same as redemption — units just transfer ownership, no selling.

The nominee can then hold the units, redeem them, or transfer to their own existing folio.

---

## Transmission Process — With Nomination

**Documents Required**:

| Document | Specification |
|----------|--------------|
| Transmission request letter | AMC's standard form (downloadable from AMC website) |
| Death certificate (original or notarized copy) | From Municipal Corporation / Panchayat |
| Nominee's KYC documents | PAN, Aadhaar, cancelled cheque |
| Nominee's Signature | On the request form |

**For folios up to ₹2 lakh**: Simplified — just the above documents.

**For folios above ₹2 lakh**: AMC may additionally require:
- Nominee's bank attestation (signature verified by bank)
- Indemnity bond on stamp paper (some AMCs waive this)

**Process**:
1. Contact the AMC (email, walk-in, or RTA — CAMS/KFintech)
2. Submit transmission request form + documents
3. AMC verifies documents (7–30 days)
4. Units transferred to nominee's folio (new folio number created for nominee)
5. Nominee receives statement showing units in their name

**Alternative**: File transmission request at [mfcentral.com](https://mfcentral.com) — unified platform, works for all AMCs.

---

## Transmission Process — WITHOUT Nomination (Legal Heir Claim)

If the investor died without nominating anyone (or nomination wasn't updated):

| Scenario | Documents Required |
|----------|-------------------|
| Folio ≤ ₹2 lakh | Succession certificate from court (or) Indemnity bond + affidavit + bank consent |
| Folio ₹2–₹5 lakh | Letter of Administration or Probate from court (some AMCs accept indemnity bond) |
| Folio > ₹5 lakh | Succession certificate / Probate / Letters of Administration from High Court |

**Succession Certificate cost**: ₹1,000–₹5,000 court fee (varies by state) + advocate fees + 2–6 months time.

**Practical reality**: For amounts above ₹5 lakh without nomination, the process is legally complex and time-consuming. This is exactly why SEBI mandated nominations.

## Joint Folio — What Happens After Primary Holder Dies?

For a joint folio (e.g., you and your spouse):
- **First holder dies**: Surviving holder becomes sole holder automatically (units continue in folio under survivor's name)
- No transmission required for joint folios where at least one holder is alive
- Documents: Death certificate submitted to AMC; folio updated to remove deceased holder's name

## Taxation of Transmitted Units — What Nominees Should Know

Units transmitted to nominee are NOT a sale. No capital gains tax at time of transmission.

**When nominee later REDEEMS the units**:
- Cost of acquisition for nominee = original investor's purchase price
- Holding period: Includes the deceased investor's holding period (nominee "inherits" the date of purchase)
- So if original investor bought Nifty 50 fund in 2020 and nominee sells in 2027: 7-year holding → LTCG at 12.5%

**No inheritance tax in India**: Transmission of mutual fund units to legal heirs/nominees is not taxed at the time of transfer. Only the eventual sale attracts capital gains.

## What to Do If AMC is Not Processing Transmission

If AMC delays beyond 30 days:

1. Escalate to CAMS or KFintech (the RTAs — Registrar and Transfer Agents)
   - CAMS: camsonline.com → Customer Service → Complaint
   - KFintech: kfintech.com → Investor Services

2. File SEBI SCORES complaint: [scores.sebi.gov.in](https://scores.sebi.gov.in) → Complaint against Mutual Fund
   SEBI can mandate AMC to process within 15 days

3. AMFI: [amfiindia.com](https://amfiindia.com) → Investor Helpline for escalation

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I have SIP running. Will it stop after investor's death?

Yes — the bank mandate for SIP typically cannot be honored once the account holder is deceased (bank may freeze the account). Notify the AMC to pause or cancel SIP. Nominee can start fresh SIP in their own name after transmission.

### My parent had MF but we don't know which AMC or folio.

Check:
1. **CAMS Mailback**: Send email to [investorservices@camsonline.com](mailto:investorservices@camsonline.com) with investor's PAN → CAMS will send list of all AMC folios they service
2. **KFintech**: [kfintech.com](https://kfintech.com) → Investor Services → Portfolio Statement → enter PAN
3. **NSDL CAS**: Login to [nsdl.co.in](https://nsdl.co.in) with PAN → Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) — shows all demat and MF holdings

These cover 95%+ of all mutual fund folios.

### Can nominee be changed after adding?

Yes — nomination can be changed anytime during the investor's lifetime. Login to MF Central or AMC portal → Nomination → Modify. Each change requires fresh authentication.

### Is nomination in mutual funds the same as Will?

No — they serve different purposes:
- **Nomination**: Immediate, court-free transfer of assets to nominee (nominee is custodian, not necessarily the owner by law)
- **Will**: Legal document for overall estate distribution (may override nomination in some circumstances)

For mutual funds specifically: AMFI guidance is that nominee has a valid claim to the units on investor's death, even if the Will says something different. Having both nomination (for quick access) + Will (for clarity on legal ownership) is ideal.
