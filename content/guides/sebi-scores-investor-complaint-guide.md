# SEBI SCORES — File Investor Complaint Against Broker, MF or Listed Company

**SEBI SCORES** (Securities and Exchange Board of India Complaint Redress System) is the official portal to file complaints against:
- Stock brokers (Zerodha, Groww, HDFC Securities, ICICI Direct, Upstox, Angel One, etc.)
- Mutual fund companies (SBI MF, HDFC MF, Nippon India MF, etc.)
- Listed companies (dividend not paid, share transfer issues)
- Depository Participants (CDSL/NSDL demat account issues)
- Portfolio Management Services (PMS)
- Registrar and Transfer Agents (RTA — like CAMS, KFintech)

> **Important**: SEBI SCORES does NOT handle crypto exchanges, P2P lending fraud, or Ponzi schemes. For those, report to Cyber Crime at [cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in/) or local police.

## When to File SEBI SCORES Complaint

| Situation | File SCORES |
|-----------|------------|
| Broker didn't execute your order or added charges without notice | ✅ Yes |
| Demat account shows wrong shares or missing shares | ✅ Yes |
| Mutual fund redemption not credited | ✅ Yes |
| Company didn't pay declared dividend | ✅ Yes |
| Rights issue / bonus shares not received | ✅ Yes |
| RTA (CAMS) lost your MF folio | ✅ Yes |
| Broker executed trades you didn't authorize | ✅ Yes |
| Crypto exchange not paying out | ❌ No — Cyber Crime |
| Unauthorized lending app | ❌ No — RBI / Cyber Crime |
| Stock tip fraud / investment advisory fraud | ❌ No — local police + Cyber Crime |

## Step-by-Step: How to File SCORES Complaint

### Step 1: Exhaust the Entity's Grievance Process First

Before filing with SEBI, **you must first complain to the entity itself** and wait 30 days (or get a rejection/no response):
1. Email your broker's customer service with a detailed written complaint
2. Keep a record of the complaint date and any responses
3. If no resolution in 30 days → proceed to SEBI SCORES

### Step 2: Register on SCORES Portal

1. Visit **[scores.sebi.gov.in](https://scores.sebi.gov.in/)**
2. Click **"Register"** → fill name, mobile, email, PAN
3. Verify email + mobile OTP
4. Your account is created

### Step 3: File Your Complaint

1. Login to SCORES → Click **"File a Complaint"**
2. Select **entity type**: Broker / Mutual Fund / Listed Company / Depository / RTA
3. Select the **specific entity name** from the dropdown
4. Describe the complaint in detail (limit: typically 1,000-2,000 characters)
5. Attach supporting documents:
   - Trade confirmation slips
   - Bank statements showing amounts
   - Email correspondence with the entity
   - Demat statement showing missing shares
6. Submit → receive **Registration Number** (e.g., SECO/G/2026/000XXXXX)

### Step 4: What Happens Next

| Timeline | What Happens |
|----------|-------------|
| Day 0 | You file complaint; entity is notified automatically |
| Day 1–21 | Entity must respond and try to resolve |
| Day 21 | If not resolved, SEBI reviews the complaint |
| Day 21–30 | SEBI may issue directions to the entity |
| Ongoing | SCORES system tracks and escalates |

**SEBI SCORES 2.0** (launched 2024) uses automated routing to the right regulator (SEBI, RBI, IRDAI) and AI-based priority handling. Complaints are tracked in real-time.

## What Can SEBI Do?

| Resolution | Possible |
|------------|---------|
| Force the broker to credit missing funds | ✅ (via penalty) |
| Direct company to pay dividend | ✅ |
| Revoke broker's license for repeated violations | ✅ |
| **Award compensation directly to you** | ❌ — SEBI can't order direct compensation; use court/ODR for that |

SEBI SCORES is for **regulatory action** — SEBI pressures the entity but doesn't directly compensate you. For monetary compensation:

## SEBI ODR (Online Dispute Resolution) — For Monetary Claims

SEBI launched **SMART ODR** ([smartodr.in](https://smartodr.in/)) in 2023:
- Handles **disputes up to ₹30 lakh** (expanded in 2025)
- Online arbitration/mediation with SEBI-empanelled arbitrators
- Legally binding resolution
- Faster than civil court (60-90 days typical)

**Process**:
1. File at [smartodr.in](https://smartodr.in/) → select dispute type
2. Choose mediation (cheaper) or arbitration (binding)
3. Pay nominal filing fee (₹1,000–₹5,000)
4. Get hearing date; present case online
5. Arbitrator issues binding award

**Use ODR when**: You want monetary compensation and the amount is under ₹30L. SCORES + ODR together is more powerful than either alone.

## Common Complaints and Tips

### Broker unauthorized trading

- **Evidence needed**: Your order history vs actual trade log; call recordings if available
- **File SCORES under**: "Broker/Sub-Broker" → unauthorized trading

### Mutual fund redemption not credited

- **Typical timeline**: 1-3 working days for redemption credit
- **Wait period before filing**: 7 working days (occasional delays)
- **Evidence needed**: Redemption confirmation email, bank statement showing no credit
- **File SCORES under**: "Mutual Fund"

### Listed company didn't pay dividend

- **File after**: Record date + 30 days (SEBI mandates payment within 30 days of dividend declaration)
- **Evidence needed**: Demat statement showing shareholding on record date, no credit to bank
- **File SCORES under**: "Listed Companies" → "Dividend"

### Wrong shares in demat or shares missing

- **File SCORES under**: "Depository Participant" (for demat issues) or "Listed Company" (for share split/bonus issues)
- **Evidence needed**: Before/after demat statements; contract note

## What SCORES Is NOT

- **Not for crypto**: Crypto is unregulated by SEBI; go to Cyber Crime
- **Not for UPI fraud**: That's handled by NPCI / your bank / Cyber Crime
- **Not for insurance fraud**: IRDAI Bima Bharosa (155255)
- **Not for bank fraud**: RBI Banking Ombudsman (bankingombudsman.rbi.org.in)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My broker Zerodha/Groww deducted wrong charges. Can I file SEBI SCORES?

Yes. First email their support with the specific charge dispute. If not resolved in 30 days, file SCORES under "Broker."

### How long does SEBI take to resolve SCORES complaints?

SEBI aims to resolve within 30–45 days from filing. However, complex disputes may take 90-120 days. If the entity appeals SEBI's direction, it can extend further.

### Can I file SEBI SCORES for a PMS (Portfolio Management Service) that lost all my money?

Yes — SEBI regulates PMS. File under "Portfolio Management Service (PMS)" on SCORES. If there was fraudulent mismanagement, also register a police FIR.

### I lost money in an unauthorized investment app. How to complain?

If the app is an unauthorized broker (no SEBI registration), file at Cyber Crime → cybercrime.gov.in and also report to SEBI at scores.sebi.gov.in. Provide screenshots, transaction details.

### What's the difference between SCORES and consumer court?

SCORES: Regulatory complaint — SEBI pressures the entity and can take action against their license. Consumer Court: Legal compensation claim — you can get monetary award. Both are complementary. For large amounts (>₹1L), use both simultaneously.
