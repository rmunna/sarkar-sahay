## What is Savitribai Phule Kishori Samridhi Yojana?

**Savitribai Phule Kishori Samridhi Yojana** is a Jharkhand government scheme that provides **financial assistance in installments to girls from Class 8 to 18 years of age** to encourage them to continue their education and prevent child marriage. The scheme was merged from two earlier schemes (Sukanya and Kishori Shakti) and targets girls from SECC-2011 listed and Antyodaya card families.

---

## Key Benefits

| Class / Stage | Amount |
|--------------|--------|
| Class 8 | ₹2,500 |
| Class 9 | ₹2,500 |
| Class 10 | ₹5,000 |
| Class 11 | ₹5,000 |
| Class 12 | ₹5,000 |
| On turning 18 (not married) | ₹20,000 lump sum |
| **Total benefit** | **₹40,000** |

The **₹20,000 at 18** is given only if the girl is **not married before 18** — it incentivizes avoiding child marriage.

---

## Who is Eligible?

**The girl is eligible if:**
- She is studying in **Class 8 or above** in a government school
- Her family is listed in **SECC-2011 database** OR has an **Antyodaya ration card**
- She is a **permanent resident of Jharkhand**
- She is **not married** before 18 years (for the ₹20,000 incentive)

> SECC-2011 criteria — this restricts the scheme to the most economically vulnerable families. Many families may not be in SECC-2011 even if poor — check with your local ICDS office.

---

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar Card** — girl's
2. **SECC-2011 enrollment proof** OR **Antyodaya ration card**
3. **School enrollment / marksheet** — current class
4. **Domicile certificate** — Jharkhand residency
5. **Bank passbook** — in the girl's name (Aadhaar-linked)
6. **Date of birth proof** — birth certificate or Aadhaar
7. **Self-declaration of unmarried status** — for the ₹20,000 incentive

---

## How to Apply — Step by Step

### Step 1 — Through Anganwadi / School

For girls in Class 8:
- The **school headmaster** or **Anganwadi worker** identifies eligible girls
- Forms are distributed at the school level

### Step 2 — Fill and Submit Form

Fill the form with all required details. Submit to:
- The school (for class-based installments), OR
- The **District Child Development Project Officer (CDPO)**

### Step 3 — Verification

The CDPO / Block Development Officer verifies SECC-2011 listing, residence, and school enrollment.

### Step 4 — Installments Credited

Upon approval:
- Class installments are credited to the girl's bank account via DBT
- The ₹20,000 at 18 is credited on verification that she is 18+ and unmarried

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. My daughter is in Class 10 in a private school. Is she eligible?**

No. The scheme is for **government school students only**. Private school students are not eligible.

**2. My family is not in SECC-2011 and we don't have an Antyodaya card. Can we apply?**

Unfortunately, the scheme strictly requires SECC-2011 listing or Antyodaya card. If your family meets the income criteria but is not listed, contact your Block Development Officer to check if you can be included.

**3. My daughter got married at 16. Will she get the ₹20,000 at 18?**

No. The ₹20,000 incentive is specifically for girls who remain unmarried until age 18. Early marriage disqualifies the final installment.

**4. I want to check if my family is in SECC-2011. How?**

Visit the SECC-2011 database (available at your Block office or CDPO office). Or ask the Anganwadi worker — they have lists for their area.

**5. My daughter received installments for Class 8 and 9 but the payment stopped in Class 10. Why?**

Check with your Block CDPO. Common issues: school transfer not updated, bank account Aadhaar link broken. Visit the CDPO with school certificate and bank passbook.
