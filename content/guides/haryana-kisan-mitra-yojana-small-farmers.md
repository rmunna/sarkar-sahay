## What is Haryana Kisan Mitra Yojana?

**Haryana Kisan Mitra Yojana** is a Haryana state government initiative specifically designed for **small and marginal farmers with 2 acres or less of land**. These farmers, who often lack access to institutional credit and government schemes, get a dedicated support package including financial assistance, free services, and preferential access to state agriculture schemes.

---

## Key Benefits

| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Annual financial support | ₹4,000/year (₹2,000 per season) |
| Soil health testing | Free soil testing at Krishi Vigyan Kendra |
| Crop advice | Free advisory from Agriculture Extension Officer |
| Preferential scheme access | Priority in horticulture, drip irrigation, equipment subsidies |
| Kisan Credit Card | Facilitation for KCC (short-term farm credit) |

---

## Who is Eligible?

- **Haryana resident farmer**
- Landholding of **2 acres or less** (small / marginal farmer)
- Land in **own name or leased** (with land records showing cultivator status)
- **Not an income tax payer**
- Registered with the **Haryana Agriculture Department**

---

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card**
2. **Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP)** — Haryana Family ID (mandatory)
3. **Jamabandi / Fard** — land records showing 2 acres or less
4. **Bank passbook** — Aadhaar-linked for DBT

---

## How to Apply / Register

### Step 1 — Register via PPP / Meri Fasal Mera Byora

Haryana farmers should first register on **Meri Fasal Mera Byora** portal (mfmb.haryana.gov.in) — the state's crop registration portal. Kisan Mitra eligibility is linked to this registration.

### Step 2 — Contact Block Agriculture Officer (BAO)

Visit the **Block Agriculture Officer** in your block to:
- Confirm eligibility as Kisan Mitra beneficiary
- Get soil testing voucher
- Access extension advisory services

### Step 3 — Financial Support Disbursement

After verification of land records and PPP ID:
- ₹2,000 per season credited to Aadhaar-linked bank account
- Credited before Kharif (May–June) and Rabi (October–November) seasons

---

## Free Soil Testing

One of the most practical benefits — **free soil health testing**:
- Visit the Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) or nearest Soil Testing Lab
- Provide 500g soil sample from your field (from 6-inch depth, multiple spots mixed)
- Receive **Soil Health Card** with nutrient levels and fertilizer recommendations
- Reduces fertilizer waste and improves yields

---

## Priority Access to Agriculture Schemes

Kisan Mitra beneficiaries get priority in:
- **Drip/sprinkler irrigation subsidy** (40–80% subsidy)
- **Farm equipment rental** at Haryana Agriculture Service Centre
- **Horticulture subsidy** for vegetable and fruit cultivation
- **PM Fasal Bima Yojana** premium subsidy
- **Subsidized seed distribution**

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. I have 1.5 acres in Haryana. Will I automatically get the ₹4,000/year?**

Registration is required — it's not fully automatic. Register on Meri Fasal Mera Byora and confirm with the BAO. Once verified as Kisan Mitra, the amount is credited via DBT.

**2. What is PPP (Parivar Pehchan Patra)?**

PPP is Haryana's Family ID — a unique 8-digit number assigned to each family. It is mandatory for almost all Haryana state scheme benefits. If you don't have it, apply at your nearest SARAL Centre or CSC.

**3. I am a tenant farmer (leased 1.5 acres). Can I qualify?**

Yes, tenant farmers can qualify if they are the registered cultivator. You may need a copy of the lease agreement and the land record showing you as the cultivator.

**4. My land is registered jointly with my brother — total 4 acres, my share is 2 acres. Am I eligible?**

Your individual share of 2 acres makes you eligible as a small farmer. Provide the jamabandi showing your share of the land.

**5. Is Kisan Mitra different from PM-KISAN?**

Yes. **PM-KISAN** (central scheme) gives ₹6,000/year to all farmers regardless of land size. **Kisan Mitra** (Haryana state scheme) gives ₹4,000 additional only to farmers with 2 acres or less, plus free soil testing and priority services.
