# Bhagyalakshmi Scheme Karnataka – ₹25,000 Bond at Girl Child's Birth

## What is Bhagyalakshmi Scheme?

Bhagyalakshmi is a Government of Karnataka scheme aimed at **empowering the girl child** in BPL families. Under this scheme, a **₹25,000 savings bond** is deposited in the name of the girl child at birth. This amount matures when the girl turns **18 years old**, growing to approximately **₹1,00,000 or more** due to interest accumulation.

Additionally, the scheme provides **annual scholarships** for the girl's education at various stages.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Family:** Must be a **BPL (Below Poverty Line)** family
2. **Birth:** The girl must be **born on or after the scheme's launch date** in a government or recognized hospital
3. **Registration:** Birth must be **registered within one year**
4. **Children Limit:** Applicable for up to **2 girl children** in a family
5. **Annual Income:** Family income must be below **₹50,000 per annum** (BPL criteria)
6. **Parents:** Parents must not have more than 2 children (family planning norm)

## Benefits Provided

| Stage | Benefit |
|-------|---------|
| At Birth | ₹25,000 savings bond |
| Class 1 | ₹2,000 scholarship |
| Class 2 | ₹2,000 scholarship |
| Class 3 | ₹2,000 scholarship |
| Class 4 | ₹2,000 scholarship |
| Class 5-7 | ₹2,500/year |
| Class 8-9 | ₹3,000/year |
| Class 10 | ₹4,000 |
| At Age 18 | Matured bond amount (~₹1,00,000+) |

*Conditions: The girl must be enrolled in school and unmarried to receive scholarships and the matured amount.*

## Documents Required

- **Birth Certificate** of the girl child
- **BPL Card / Ration Card** of the family
- **Hospital Discharge Certificate**
- **Aadhaar Card** of parents
- **Bank Account** in the name of the girl child (or mother's account for initial stages)
- **Caste Certificate** (if applicable)
- **Income Certificate**
- **Photo** of the girl child and parents

## How to Apply for Bhagyalakshmi Scheme

### Online Application

1. **Visit** [dwcd.karnataka.gov.in](https://dwcd.karnataka.gov.in) or Seva Sindhu portal
2. **Search** for "Bhagyalakshmi Scheme"
3. **Register** and fill the application form
4. **Upload** birth certificate, BPL card, hospital certificate
5. **Submit** the application
6. **Note** the reference number

### Offline Application

1. **Visit** the nearest **Anganwadi Centre** or **Women & Child Development Department** office
2. **Collect** the Bhagyalakshmi application form
3. **Fill** the form with required details
4. **Attach** photocopies of all documents
5. **Submit** to the Anganwadi worker or CDPO (Child Development Project Officer)
6. **Collect** acknowledgment receipt

## Processing Time

- **Application Review:** 30-60 days
- **Bond Issuance:** Within 3 months of approval
- **Scholarships:** Annually, upon proof of school enrollment

## Important Tips

1. **Apply Within 1 Year of Birth:** The application must be submitted within one year of the girl's birth.
2. **Keep the Girl in School:** Scholarships and the final maturity amount are conditional on the girl being enrolled in school.
3. **No Early Marriage:** If the girl marries before 18, the family loses the matured bond amount.
4. **Hospital Birth Mandatory:** The birth must be in a government or recognized private hospital.
5. **Two Girls Maximum:** The scheme covers a maximum of 2 girl children per family.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Can I apply if my daughter was born at home?
**No.** The birth must be in a government or recognized hospital. Home births are not eligible.

### Q2: What happens to the bond if the girl gets married before 18?
The family **forfeits the matured bond amount**. The scheme is designed to discourage child marriage.

### Q3: Can I apply for both my daughters?
**Yes**, up to 2 girl children per family can benefit from this scheme.

### Q4: Is this scheme only for BPL families?
**Yes.** Only families with a valid BPL card and income below ₹50,000/year are eligible.

### Q5: How much will the bond be worth at age 18?
The ₹25,000 bond matures to approximately **₹1,00,000 or more** depending on the prevailing interest rate.

### Q6: Can NRI or non-Karnataka families apply?
**No.** Only families domiciled in Karnataka with a Karnataka BPL card are eligible.
