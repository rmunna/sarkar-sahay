## What is Mukhyamantri Seekho Kamao Yojana?

Mukhyamantri Seekho Kamao Yojana (Chief Minister Learn and Earn Scheme) is a Madhya Pradesh government scheme launched in 2023 that provides on-the-job training to educated youth at private companies while paying them a monthly stipend. The idea is: the youth learns a skill at a company, gets paid while learning, and may even get a job offer at the end.

The scheme targets 1 lakh youth per year and covers over 700 types of work across all sectors — IT, engineering, hospitality, healthcare, retail, media, arts, and more.

## Monthly Stipend

| Education Level | Monthly Stipend |
|----------------|----------------|
| Class 12 pass | ₹8,000/month |
| ITI pass | ₹8,500/month |
| Diploma holder | ₹9,000/month |
| Graduate (B.A., B.Com, B.Sc., etc.) | ₹10,000/month |
| Postgraduate or higher | ₹10,000/month |

The stipend is paid **directly to the youth's bank account** for the duration of training (up to 1 year).

**75% is paid by the government; 25% is paid by the company.** So the youth gets 100% stipend regardless.

## Key Benefits

- Monthly stipend of ₹8,000–₹10,000 directly to bank account
- **Free on-the-job training** at established companies (700+ types of work)
- **Certificate** from the National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC) or state skill authority after completion
- **Possibility of placement** — companies may hire after training
- No bond or service obligation — you are free after training
- Training is **at the company premises**, so you gain real work experience

## Who is Eligible?

- **Permanent resident of Madhya Pradesh** (must have MP domicile)
- **Age:** 18 to 29 years
- **Education:** Minimum Class 12 pass (ITI / Diploma / Degree holders also eligible — higher stipend for higher qualification)
- **Employment status:** Not currently employed in any government or regular private sector job
- Must have a valid MP Samgra ID (integrated socio-economic database of MP families)

## Documents Required

1. Aadhaar card
2. MP domicile certificate (proving permanent resident of MP)
3. Education qualification certificates (Class 12 / ITI / Diploma / Degree marksheets)
4. Samgra ID card (8-digit Samgra ID — obtained from Samgra portal samagra.gov.in)
5. Bank passbook / cancelled cheque (for stipend transfer)
6. Mobile number and email ID
7. Passport size photograph

**If you don't have Samgra ID:** Register at samagra.gov.in or visit your nearest Gram Panchayat / Ward office.

## How to Apply Online — Step by Step

1. Go to **mmsky.mp.gov.in** (official portal)
2. Click **"Abhyarthi Panjeeyan"** (Applicant Registration)
3. Register using your **Samgra ID** and **mobile number** — OTP verification
4. Fill in your profile: education details, work preferences, location
5. Browse available training courses and companies — use filters for your area, education, and interest
6. Select a training course and click **"Apply"**
7. Your application is sent to the company; they review and may call you for an interview/selection
8. Once selected, you receive an offer letter on the portal
9. Join the company on the designated start date
10. Stipend is credited to your bank account every month

## How to Check Application Status

1. Visit **mmsky.mp.gov.in**
2. Click **"Abhyarthi Login"** and log in with Samgra ID
3. Your applications, status, and stipend payment history are visible in your dashboard

## For Companies — How to Register

Companies wishing to offer training under Seekho Kamao can register at mmsky.mp.gov.in and list training positions. There is no minimum company size requirement. Companies get a CSR benefit and tax advantages for participating.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is there a bond or commitment after training?
No — there is no bond. After training (up to 1 year), you are free to take up any job or continue your education. The company may offer you employment, but you are not obligated to join.

### Can I apply for training in a different city/district within MP?
Yes — the portal lists training opportunities across all MP districts. You can apply for training in any city in MP, but you need to be available at the company's location during training.

### What if the company does not pay the 25% stipend?
The government pays 75% directly. The remaining 25% (company's share) is also transferred through the portal after the company confirms attendance. If there is any issue, contact the Seekho Kamao helpline.

### Can a diploma holder get ₹9,000?
Yes — diploma holders receive ₹9,000/month. ITI passers get ₹8,500/month. Verify that your diploma is from a recognized institution.

### Is Samgra ID mandatory?
Yes — Samgra ID is mandatory to register on the MMSKY portal. If you don't have one, register at samagra.gov.in or visit your nearest Panchayat/Ward office to get your Samgra ID.

### Can a Class 10 pass youth apply?
No — the minimum education is Class 12 (12th pass). Students who have passed only Class 10 are not eligible currently.

### What is the training duration?
Training is for a minimum of 1 month and maximum of 1 year, depending on the course. Stipend is paid for the actual training period.
