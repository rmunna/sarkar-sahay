# Madhya Pradesh Caste Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **caste certificate** (जाति प्रमाण पत्र — Jaati Praman Patra) in Madhya Pradesh is issued by the Revenue Department (Sub-Divisional Officer / Tehsildar) and is required for OBC/SC/ST reservations, MP government scholarships, NEET state counselling, government job applications, and scheme eligibility. Applications are processed through **MP e-District** — and a **Samagra ID is typically required** for all MP government services.

> **Quick summary:** Get your **Samagra ID** first, then apply at [mpedistrict.gov.in](https://mpedistrict.gov.in) or **Lok Seva Kendra**. Approved by SDM/Tehsildar within **15–30 days**.

---

## Samagra ID — Mandatory First Step in MP

Before applying for a caste certificate in MP, you must have a **Samagra ID** (8-digit family ID):
- **Samagra Family ID** (8 digits) — for the entire family
- **Samagra Member ID** (9 digits) — for each family member

Register at [samagra.gov.in](https://samagra.gov.in) if you don't have one. Take your Aadhaar and ration card to a Gram Panchayat or Lok Seva Kendra for registration.

## Why You Need MP Caste Certificate

- **MP state scholarship (post-matric)** — OBC/SC/ST categories
- **NEET MP state counselling** — caste certificate for reserved seats
- **MPJPHS / DTE MP admissions** — community proof for reserved category
- **Government job reservation** — MPPSC, MPESB, district recruitment
- **Mukhyamantri Medhavi Vidyarthi Yojana** — caste proof required for non-general
- **Central OBC NCL certificate** — for UPSC, SSC, railways

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory)
- **Samagra ID** (mandatory for MP services)
- **Ration card** (family composition and caste proof)
- **Father's / grandfather's caste proof** (school TC, ration card, old voter ID with caste)
- Self-declaration of caste (online or on stamp paper)
- **Voter ID** (address proof)
- Passport-size photograph

## How to Apply Online — MP e-District Portal

1. Go to [mpedistrict.gov.in](https://mpedistrict.gov.in)
2. Register / log in (use Samagra ID or Aadhaar)
3. Click **"Revenue Department"** → **"Caste Certificate"** (जाति प्रमाण पत्र)
4. Fill form:
   - Applicant name, Samagra ID, address (district, tehsil, village)
   - Caste/community declaration
   - Father's community details
5. Upload documents (Aadhaar, Samagra, father's TC/caste proof)
6. Pay fee: **₹20–40** (online)
7. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via Lok Seva Kendra (LSK)

MP's **Lok Seva Kendra** (public service centres — one per block/tehsil):

1. Visit nearest **Lok Seva Kendra**
2. Carry Aadhaar, Samagra ID, ration card, father's caste proof
3. Operator fills and submits on e-District portal
4. Service charge: approximately ₹30–60
5. Collect acknowledgement receipt

---

## Apply via MP Online Kiosk

**MP Online** kiosks (at petrol pumps, shops) also handle e-District applications:
1. Visit MP Online kiosk
2. Provide documents
3. Operator submits application
4. Service charge: ₹30–70

## Status Check

1. Go to [mpedistrict.gov.in](https://mpedistrict.gov.in)
2. Click **"Track Application"** → enter Application Reference Number
3. Or log in → **"My Applications"** → view status
4. Status flow: Submitted → RI Verification → SDM/Tehsildar → Approved

---

## Download MP Caste Certificate

Once approved:
1. Log in to MP e-District → **"My Applications"** → click approved caste certificate
2. Download digitally signed PDF
3. Valid for MP scholarships, MPPSC, and central purposes (for SC/ST)

## OBC NCL Certificate — MP

For **central government OBC reservation** (UPSC, SSC, IIT-JEE, NIT admissions):
- Apply for "OBC Non-Creamy Layer Certificate" at MP e-District
- Income < ₹8 lakh/year
- Issued by Tahsildar
- Valid for **1 year** (renewable)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### ST certificate in MP — which authority issues?

**ST caste certificates** in MP are issued by the **Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM)** — same as other categories but may require additional tribal affiliation documents. For ST, the Gram Sabha panchayat resolution or tribal community leader letter can support the application.

### OBC certificate MP vs OBC NCL — same?

No. The **OBC caste certificate** proves you belong to an OBC community. The **OBC NCL (Non-Creamy Layer) certificate** additionally proves your family income is < ₹8 lakh/year. For central government OBC reservation, you need **OBC NCL**, not just OBC. Both applied at MP e-District.

### Samagra ID not available — what to do?

Register first at [samagra.gov.in](https://samagra.gov.in) or visit the nearest Gram Panchayat / Ward Office with Aadhaar and family documents. Samagra registration is free and mandatory for all MP services.
