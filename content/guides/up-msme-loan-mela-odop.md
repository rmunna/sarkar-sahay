## What is UP MSME Loan Mela?

**UP MSME Loan Mela** is a special event-based camp organized by the Government of Uttar Pradesh where **MSMEs (Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises)** can get **on-the-spot loan sanctions** from participating banks — collateral-free and with minimum documentation.

Loan Melas are held periodically at district headquarters across UP. Entrepreneurs, artisans, and small business owners attend and receive **loan sanction letters directly** at the event.

## What is ODOP?

**ODOP (One District One Product)** is the UP government's flagship programme that:
1. **Identifies one specialty product** for each district (e.g., Varanasi → Banarasi silk; Agra → leather footwear; Moradabad → brassware)
2. **Provides financial support** (subsidy on loans) to artisans and enterprises making that product
3. **Supports marketing** — domestic and international exhibitions

## ODOP — Financial Benefits

### For Micro Enterprises (making ODOP product):
- **25% margin money subsidy** on project loans (max ₹6.25 lakh)
- Loan facilitated through banks
- Common Facility Centres (CFCs) set up for shared equipment

### For Artisan-level (Vishwakarma-type):
- **Toolkits worth ₹10,000–₹15,000** (similar to PM Vishwakarma)
- Skill training linked to the district's ODOP product

## Who is Eligible?

### For MSME Loan Mela:
- Any MSME registered in Uttar Pradesh
- Should have Udyam registration (MSME registration)
- Attending the Loan Mela event in their district

### For ODOP Subsidy:
- Entrepreneur or artisan making the specific ODOP product of their district
- Registered unit (or willing to register)
- Age: 18 to 50 years typically

## How to Apply for ODOP Subsidy

1. Go to **[diupmsme.upsdc.gov.in](https://diupmsme.upsdc.gov.in)** or [odopup.in](https://odopup.in)
2. Find the ODOP subsidy scheme for your district
3. Register with Aadhaar + mobile OTP
4. Fill application: business type, ODOP product, project cost
5. Upload documents
6. The application goes to your District Industries Centre (DIC)
7. DIC forwards to bank for loan sanction
8. 25% margin money released by government to bank upon loan disbursement

## How to Attend MSME Loan Mela

MSME Loan Melas are announced via:
- District Magistrate's office notifications
- DIC (District Industries Centre) announcements
- UP government press releases

On the day:
1. Bring your business registration and loan application documents
2. Banks have stalls — submit application to your preferred bank
3. For eligible cases, on-the-spot sanction letters may be issued
4. Loan disbursement happens through normal bank process after the mela

## Documents Required (for both schemes)

1. **Aadhaar card** (proprietor/partners)
2. **Udyam Registration certificate**
3. **ODOP product proof** — type of product, machinery, workshop details
4. **Project report** — cost estimate, expected revenue
5. **Bank account** (nationalized/scheduled bank)
6. **GST registration** (if applicable)
7. **Caste certificate** (SC/ST for priority)
8. Passport-size photograph

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My district's ODOP product is silk, but I make leather goods. Can I still apply for ODOP?
ODOP subsidy is specifically for the designated product of your district. For non-ODOP products, apply under the UP Mukhyamantri Yuva Swarozgar Yojana (25% margin money for any business) or PMEGP.

### Can I combine ODOP subsidy with MSME Loan Mela?
Yes — you can apply for a loan at an MSME Loan Mela for your ODOP product and also claim the ODOP 25% subsidy. The Loan Mela helps fast-track loan sanction; the subsidy is applied separately.

### Is ODOP only for artisans, or can I start a factory making the ODOP product?
ODOP supports both artisans (small-scale) and enterprises (factory-level). Subsidy quantum differs — contact your DIC for the applicable tier for your scale.

### How do I check when the next MSME Loan Mela is in my district?
Check the [diupmsme.upsdc.gov.in](https://diupmsme.upsdc.gov.in) portal, or contact your District Industries Centre (DIC). Announcements are also made on the UP government's official social media.
