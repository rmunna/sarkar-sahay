## What is an LPG Gas Connection?

An LPG (Liquefied Petroleum Gas) connection allows you to receive cooking gas cylinders from one of India's three Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs) — **Indian Oil (Indane)**, **Hindustan Petroleum (HP Gas)**, or **Bharat Petroleum (Bharat Gas)**. Each household is entitled to one subsidised LPG connection, and you can now apply entirely online through the respective company portals.

## Who Can Apply for a New LPG Connection?

- Any Indian resident aged 18 years or above
- The applicant must not already have an LPG connection in their name at the same address
- Only one subsidised connection is allowed per household
- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) is mandatory for KYC and subsidy transfer (DBTL)

### Ujjwala Yojana Beneficiaries

Under the **Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY) 2.0**, eligible families can get a free LPG connection. You qualify if:

- Your household is listed in the SECC (Socio-Economic Caste Census) database
- You belong to an SC/ST household, are a beneficiary of PMAY, AAY, forest dweller, or tea garden worker
- You are a woman member of an eligible BPL household
- Migrants who do not have proof of address can use a self-declaration

Learn more about subsidy benefits in our [LPG Subsidy & Ujjwala Yojana guide](/guide/lpg-subsidy-ujjwala-yojana).

## Documents Required

| Document | Details |
|----------|---------|
| **Proof of Identity** | Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Passport, or Driving Licence |
| **Proof of Address** | Aadhaar, Electricity Bill, Passport, Ration Card, or Rent Agreement |
| **Passport-size Photo** | Recent colour photograph |
| **Ration Card** | Required to verify no existing connection at the household ([Apply for Ration Card](/guide/ration-card-apply-online)) |
| **Bank Account Details** | For DBTL subsidy credit (account must be linked with Aadhaar) |

> For Ujjwala applicants, a BPL certificate or SECC list inclusion proof is additionally required. Migrants can submit a self-declaration in lieu of address proof.

## How to Apply Online — Step-by-Step

### 1. Indian Oil Indane Gas — New Connection

1. Visit the Indian Oil Indane portal at **iocl.com** and navigate to "Indane for Home" → "New Connection"
2. Select your state, district, and preferred distributor
3. Fill in the online application form with personal details, Aadhaar number, and address
4. Upload scanned copies of identity proof, address proof, and photograph
5. Choose your cylinder type — 14.2 kg (regular) or 5 kg (FTL / Free Trade LPG)
6. Submit the form and note the reference number
7. Pay the security deposit online or at the distributor's office
8. The distributor will verify documents and schedule delivery within 7–15 days

### 2. HP Gas — New Connection

1. Go to **hindustanpetroleum.com** and click on "HP Gas" → "New Connection"
2. Enter your mobile number and verify via OTP
3. Fill in personal details, select your area, and choose a distributor
4. Upload KYC documents (Aadhaar, address proof, photo)
5. Select cylinder type and pay the security deposit
6. Your application is assigned to the nearest distributor for verification
7. Connection is delivered within 7–15 working days after verification

### 3. Bharat Gas — New Connection

1. Visit **bharatgas.com** and select "New Connection"
2. Register with your mobile number and complete OTP verification
3. Fill in the application form with Aadhaar and address details
4. Upload required documents
5. Select your preferred distributor and cylinder type
6. Complete the payment for the security deposit
7. Delivery is typically within 7–15 days post-verification

| **Pressure Regulator** | ₹150 – ₹200 |
| **LPG Hose Pipe** | Included or ₹100–₹150 |
| **First Refill (14.2 kg — subsidised)** | ₹803 (approximate, varies monthly) |
| **Installation Charges** | Nil (included with connection) |

> Ujjwala beneficiaries receive a **free connection** — the security deposit for the cylinder, regulator, and first refill are borne by the Government. EMI option is available for the stove cost.

**Note:** LPG refill prices are revised on the 1st of every month. Check the latest price on your OMC's website.

## How to Book an LPG Cylinder Refill

You can book refills through multiple channels:

- **Missed Call:** Give a missed call to your OMC's booking number (Indane: 7718955555, HP: 9222201122, Bharat Gas: 7710955555)
- **WhatsApp:** Send "Book" to the respective number
- **Online Portal:** Log in to your OMC's customer portal
- **Mobile App:** Use the Indane Gas, HP Gas, or Bharat Gas apps
- **IVRS:** Call the distributor's IVRS number

### Check Booking Status

After booking, check your refill status via:
- The OMC's mobile app or website using your consumer number
- SMS notification sent to your registered mobile number
- Calling your distributor directly

## How to Apply for a Double Cylinder Connection

If you need two cylinders, you can apply for a **Double Bottle Connection (DBC)**:

1. Log in to your OMC's customer portal
2. Navigate to "Request for Double Cylinder" or "DBC"
3. Pay the additional security deposit for the second cylinder (approximately ₹1,450–₹1,600)
4. The second cylinder will be delivered after approval

You are eligible for DBC if you have had a single-cylinder connection for at least one year and your consumption history is satisfactory.

## How to Transfer or Change Address on Your LPG Connection

### Address Change (Same Distributor Area)

1. Log in to your OMC portal
2. Submit an address change request with new address proof
3. The distributor verifies and updates the records within 7 days

### Transfer to Another City/Distributor (Transferring Out — TV-Out)

1. Visit your current distributor and submit a **Transfer Voucher (TV-Out)** request
2. Surrender the cylinder and regulator (security deposit is carried forward)
3. At the new location, approach the new distributor with the TV-Out letter
4. Complete formalities and receive a new connection within 7–15 days

> Keep your Aadhaar address updated. See our [Aadhaar address change guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) for help.

## How to Surrender Your LPG Connection

If you no longer need your LPG connection:

1. Visit or write to your distributor requesting surrender
2. Return the cylinder(s) and regulator in usable condition
3. Submit your original Subscription Voucher (SV)
4. The security deposit is refunded to your bank account within 15–30 days after inspection
5. If the cylinder or regulator is damaged, deduction is applied before refund

## Important Tips

- **Link Aadhaar to your LPG connection** to ensure subsidy is credited directly to your bank account via DBTL (PAHAL scheme)
- **One connection per household** — duplicate connections may lead to cancellation and recovery
- **Keep your KYC updated** — change of name, address, or bank details can be done online
- You can **port your connection** from one OMC to another if needed, similar to a mobile number port

## FAQ — Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does it take to get a new LPG connection?

After successful document verification, the connection is typically delivered within **7 to 15 working days**. The timeline depends on your distributor's queue and area.

### Can I apply for a new LPG connection without Aadhaar?

Aadhaar is mandatory for KYC verification and for receiving the DBTL subsidy. Without Aadhaar, you cannot avail of the subsidised rate. You can [apply for Aadhaar online](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) if you don't have one.

### What is the cost of a new gas connection?

The total upfront cost is approximately **₹1,600 to ₹2,200** including security deposit, regulator, and first refill for a 14.2 kg cylinder. Ujjwala beneficiaries get this free.

### Can I have connections from two different companies?

No. A household is entitled to only **one subsidised LPG connection**. However, you can take a second connection on a non-subsidised (market price) basis from any OMC.

### How do I check my LPG subsidy status?

Visit your OMC's website or the **MyLPG.in** portal and enter your consumer number to check subsidy credit status. You can also check via the PAHAL (DBTL) section on the respective OMC's site. Read our detailed [LPG Subsidy & Ujjwala guide](/guide/lpg-subsidy-ujjwala-yojana).

### Can I change my LPG distributor?

Yes. You can request a change of distributor within the same OMC through the customer portal or by visiting the current distributor. For switching OMCs, a Transfer Voucher process applies.

### What happens if I don't take a refill for a long time?

If no refill is booked for **more than one year**, the connection may be classified as dormant. You will need to contact your distributor to reactivate it with updated KYC.
