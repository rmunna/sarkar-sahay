## What is UMANG App?

**UMANG (Unified Mobile Application for New-age Governance)** is the Government of India's single mobile app that provides access to **2,000+ government services** from 300+ central and state departments — all on one platform, without switching between multiple apps.

Developed by **MeitY (Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology)** and **NIC (National Informatics Centre)**, UMANG is the most comprehensive government services app in India.

Key features:
- **2,000+ services** from 300+ departments
- Single login with **Aadhaar / mobile number**
- Available in **13 languages**
- Free for all Indian citizens
- Available on Android and iOS

## Key Services Available on UMANG

### EPFO Services
- Check PF balance
- View passbook
- Raise PF claim (Form 31, 19, 10C)
- Update KYC (UAN)
- EPS pension details

### Aadhaar Services
- Download e-Aadhaar
- Verify Aadhaar
- Update address (link to portal)
- Lock/unlock Aadhaar biometrics

### Income Tax
- File ITR (link to e-filing portal)
- Check ITR status
- View Form 26AS
- Download income tax certificate

### DigiLocker
- Access your documents
- Share certificates

### Health Services
- Locate government hospitals
- Cowin vaccination certificate download
- PM-JAY Ayushman Bharat health ID

### Education and Scholarships
- National Scholarship Portal (NSP) — apply and check status
- CBSE marksheet download
- Student-teacher directory

### Agriculture
- PM-KISAN status
- Crop insurance (PMFBY) claims
- Soil health card

### Pension
- NSAP pension status (old age, widow, disability)
- EPF pension details

### Driving / Vehicle
- Driving licence details
- Vehicle RC details
- Traffic violations and fine payment

### Banking
- Jan Dhan account details
- PMJJBY / PMSBY insurance status

## How to Download and Set Up UMANG

### Download the App
1. Go to Google Play Store or Apple App Store
2. Search **"UMANG"**
3. Download the official app (by MeitY / NIC — official government app)
4. Alternatively access at [web.umang.gov.in](https://web.umang.gov.in)

### Register / Login
1. Open the app → click **"Register"**
2. Enter your **mobile number** → OTP
3. Optionally link **Aadhaar** for more services (recommended)
4. Set a 4-digit MPIN for quick login

## Using UMANG for EPFO (Most Popular Use)

1. Open UMANG → search **"EPFO"**
2. Click **"Employee Centric Services"**
3. Select service: "View Passbook" or "Claim Status"
4. Enter **UAN** (Universal Account Number)
5. OTP on registered mobile
6. View PF balance, passbook, or claim status

## Using UMANG for Scholarships (NSP)

1. Open UMANG → search **"Scholarship"** or **"NSP"**
2. Access National Scholarship Portal through UMANG
3. Login with NSP credentials
4. Check application status and payment

## Using UMANG for PM-KISAN

1. Open UMANG → search **"PM-KISAN"**
2. Enter Aadhaar number
3. View your beneficiary status and payment history

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is UMANG the same as the government's individual department apps?
UMANG integrates services from many departments. However, some departments have their own dedicated apps (like DigiLocker, mAadhaar, mParivahan) with more features. UMANG is best for quick access across multiple services.

### Is UMANG secure? Can I trust it with my Aadhaar?
Yes — UMANG is an official Government of India app developed by NIC. Your data is not stored on UMANG servers when you access services — you are redirected to the respective department's API. Aadhaar linking is done via UIDAI's secure OTP.

### I can't find a service I need on UMANG. What to do?
UMANG has a search bar — type the service name. If not found, the service may not be integrated yet. Use the direct government portal for that service.

### Does UMANG work without internet?
No — UMANG requires internet connectivity to access government services. However, some downloaded documents (like e-Aadhaar once downloaded) can be viewed offline.

### Is there a desktop/web version of UMANG?
Yes — [web.umang.gov.in](https://web.umang.gov.in) is the web version with the same services accessible from any browser.

### Can senior citizens who don't use smartphones access UMANG?
UMANG has a basic mobile version. For non-smartphone users, **CSC (Common Service Centres)** provide assisted access to all these services — visit your nearest Jan Seva Kendra.
