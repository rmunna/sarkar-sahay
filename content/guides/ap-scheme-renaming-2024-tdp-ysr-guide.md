# AP Scheme Renaming 2024 — Find Your Old Scheme's New Name

When TDP (Telugu Desam Party) returned to power in June 2024 under CM N. Chandrababu Naidu, the government renamed all YSRCP-era welfare schemes. Schemes previously named "YSR" or "Jagananna" were rebranded — primarily after former CM N.T. Rama Rao (NTR) and CM Chandrababu Naidu ("Chandranna").

If you were receiving benefits under an old scheme name and are now confused, this guide maps every old name to the new name.

## Key AP Scheme Renamings (2024)

| Old Name (YSRCP) | New Name (TDP 2024) | Benefits |
|-----------------|---------------------|---------|
| **YSR Pension Kanuka** | **NTR Bharosa Pension** | Old age, widow, disability pension ₹3,000/month |
| **YSR Cheyutha** | Modified under NTR Bharosa | Women 45–60 from SC/ST/BC |
| **Jagananna Ammavodi** | **Chandranna Baata** | School mothers attendance incentive |
| **YSR Rythu Bharosa** | Modified / AP Rythu Bharosa | Farmer input subsidy |
| **Jagananna Housing (PMAY-AP)** | Continuing under PM Awas | Rural housing |
| **YSR Jagananna Chedodu** | Modified/Discontinued | Tailoring allowance for women |
| **Jagananna Thodu** | Modified / business support | Interest-free loan for small vendors |
| **YSR Vahana Mitra** | Continuing as Vahana Mitra | Auto/cab driver insurance support |
| **Jagananna Amma Vodi** | **Chandranna Baata** | School attendance incentive for mothers |
| **YSR Aasara** | Modified under NTR Bharosa | Self-help group (SHG) support |
| **AP Deepam** | **AP Deepam-2** | Free LPG cylinders |
| **Jagananna Smart Town** | Modified | Urban housing scheme |

> **Note**: Scheme structures may also have changed — not just names. Visit your Gram Panchayat or MeeSeva centre to confirm your current eligibility.

## NTR Bharosa Pension — Key Details

The largest scheme affecting most beneficiaries is the pension:

- **Old name**: YSR Pension Kanuka
- **New name**: NTR Bharosa Pension
- **Amount**: ₹3,000/month for Old Age, Widow, Disability categories
- **Payment**: monthly via DBT to Aadhaar-linked bank account
- **Portal**: pensionportals.ap.gov.in (check your payment status here)

If your pension stopped after June 2024, visit your Gram Panchayat secretary — you may need to re-verify your eligibility under the new government's data update.

## Chandranna Baata (formerly Jagananna Ammavodi)

- **Old name**: Jagananna Ammavodi (Amma Vodi)
- **New name**: Chandranna Baata
- **Amount**: ₹15,000/year (mother incentive for sending children to school)
- **Eligibility**: Same as before — mothers of children in classes 1–12 in government schools
- **Note**: School enrollment verification now through UIDAI/Aadhaar instead of previous methods

## AP Deepam-2 (New Scheme — Not Just a Rename)

AP Deepam-2 is a new TDP Super Six promise, not just a rename:
- **Benefit**: Free LPG cylinders (first cylinder free)
- **Portal**: apdeepam.ap.gov.in
- **Apply**: via MeeSeva centre with Aadhaar + ration card
- **For**: women who are ration card holders, BPL families

## How to Continue Receiving Benefits Under New Names

1. **Pension beneficiaries**: No re-application needed if already receiving YSR Pension Kanuka. Verify at your Gram Panchayat that your bank account and Aadhaar are still linked correctly
2. **New applicants**: apply under the new scheme names at nearest MeeSeva or Village Secretariat
3. **Discrepancies**: if payment stopped after June 2024, visit your Village Secretariat with Aadhaar + old scheme acknowledgement

## What Happened to "Jagananna" Prefix Schemes?

All schemes with "Jagananna" prefix (named after former CM Y.S. Jagan Mohan Reddy) were renamed. Physical boards, branding and portal names were also updated. However, the **underlying benefits and eligibility** for most core welfare schemes continued — only the name and branding changed.

Some schemes were also **restructured** (amounts or criteria modified) under the TDP government. For accurate current eligibility, always verify at MeeSeva or your nearest Village Secretariat.

## MeeSeva — One-Stop Point for All AP Scheme Queries

MeeSeva centres (in every Village and Ward Secretariat) handle:
- Scheme eligibility verification
- New enrollment under renamed schemes
- Updating bank account / Aadhaar details
- Grievance registration for stopped payments

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My YSR Pension Kanuka stopped in mid-2024. How do I restart?

The pension was renamed NTR Bharosa but continued for existing beneficiaries. If yours stopped, it could be due to: Aadhaar seeding issue, bank account mismatch, or a reverification drive. Visit your Village Secretariat with Aadhaar + bank passbook. The Secretariat staff will check your status and flag for revival.

### Do I need to reapply under new scheme names?

Generally no — existing beneficiaries were migrated automatically. However, new applicants should apply under the new names. If you were on a waiting list under the old government, check your status at MeeSeva as priorities may have changed.

### Is "Chandranna Baata" the same as "Jagananna Ammavodi"?

The spirit is similar — incentivizing mothers to send children to school. The brand has changed and some implementation details differ. The eligibility (mother of government school child) remains similar. Check at your school's HM (Headmaster) for the current year's enrollment.

### How do I know if a scheme was discontinued vs just renamed?

Check the AP government's official scheme portal at **ap.gov.in → Schemes**. Discontinued schemes will either not appear or show "Discontinued" status. Renamed schemes appear with new names. MeeSeva staff can also confirm.
