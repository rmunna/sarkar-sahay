# Gruha Jyothi – Free Electricity Up to 200 Units in Telangana

Gruha Jyothi is a scheme by the Government of Telangana providing free electricity up to 200 units per month to eligible domestic households. The scheme reduces the electricity bill burden on families and ensures affordable power access across the state.

## What is Gruha Jyothi?

Under Gruha Jyothi, domestic electricity consumers in Telangana who consume up to 200 units per month receive a zero electricity bill. The government bears the cost of consumption up to 200 units. Consumers exceeding 200 units pay the normal tariff for their entire consumption.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Domestic consumers:** Must have a domestic (residential) electricity connection in Telangana
2. **One connection per household:** One free connection per household (as per ration card / family unit)
3. **Consumption limit:** Monthly consumption should not exceed 200 units to get zero bill
4. **Aadhaar linkage:** Electricity consumer number must be linked to Aadhaar
5. **Exclusions:**
   - Commercial and industrial connections
   - Agricultural connections (covered under separate free power scheme)
   - Households with multiple connections (only one eligible)
   - Government quarters / official residences (in some cases)

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar card of the account holder (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- Electricity consumer number / account number
- Ration card (for household verification)
- Mobile number linked to electricity account

## How to Avail Gruha Jyothi

### Registration Process

1. **Visit Gruha Jyothi portal:** Go to [gruhajyothi.telangana.gov.in](https://gruhajyothi.telangana.gov.in/)
2. **Enter consumer number:** Provide your electricity consumer/account number
3. **Link Aadhaar:** Enter Aadhaar number of the household head
4. **OTP verification:** Verify via OTP on registered mobile number
5. **Ration card linking:** Link your ration card number for household verification
6. **Submit registration:** Complete the registration
7. **Automatic zero bill:** Once registered, if your consumption is within 200 units, your bill becomes zero

### Offline Registration

1. **Visit nearest electricity office** (TSSPDCL or TSNPDCL)
2. **Submit Aadhaar and consumer details**
3. **Fill registration form**
4. **Link ration card** for household verification
5. **Receive confirmation**

## Billing Details

| Consumption | Bill Amount |
|------------|-------------|
| 0–200 units/month | ₹0 (free under Gruha Jyothi) |
| Above 200 units/month | Normal tariff for full consumption |

**Important:** If you exceed 200 units even by 1 unit, you pay the full bill for all units consumed — not just the excess. Keep consumption within 200 units.

## Important Tips

1. **Monitor consumption:** Keep your monthly consumption under 200 units to benefit — use energy-efficient appliances
2. **One connection per family:** Only one domestic connection per ration card family is eligible
3. **Link Aadhaar promptly:** Without Aadhaar linkage, you won't receive the zero-bill benefit
4. **Check bill regularly:** Even with Gruha Jyothi, check your bill to ensure the benefit is applied
5. **200-unit threshold is strict:** Crossing 200 units means you pay for ALL units, not just the excess

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: How many units of free electricity do I get?
Up to 200 units per month. If your consumption is within this limit, your bill is zero.

### Q2: What if I use 201 units?
You will pay the full tariff for 201 units. The free benefit applies only if total consumption is 200 units or less.

### Q3: Do I need to register separately for Gruha Jyothi?
Yes, you need to register on the Gruha Jyothi portal or at your electricity office and link your Aadhaar.

### Q4: Is this available for rented houses?
Yes, as long as the electricity connection is a domestic connection and the account holder registers for the scheme.

### Q5: Can I get free electricity for two houses?
No, only one connection per household/family (as per ration card) is eligible.

### Q6: Are commercial shops eligible?
No, only domestic (residential) connections are covered under Gruha Jyothi.

### Q7: How do I check if my registration is successful?
Check on the [Gruha Jyothi portal](https://gruhajyothi.telangana.gov.in/) or your next electricity bill should reflect zero charges if you're within 200 units.
