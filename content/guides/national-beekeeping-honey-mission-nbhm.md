## What is the National Beekeeping and Honey Mission (NBHM)?

The National Beekeeping and Honey Mission (NBHM) is a Government of India initiative launched under the AtmaNirbhar Bharat package to promote scientific beekeeping and double honey production in India. The scheme provides **80–90% subsidy** on:

- **Bee colonies** (bee boxes with bees)
- **Honey processing equipment** (extractors, wax melters)
- **Queen rearing units**
- **Training for new beekeepers**

NBHM is implemented through the **National Bee Board (NBB)**, state agriculture/horticulture departments, and NABARD.

---

## Key Benefits

| Component | Subsidy Rate |
|-----------|-------------|
| Bee colonies (bee boxes + bees) | 80% for general, 90% for SC/ST/women |
| Honey processing equipment | 80–90% subsidy |
| Queen rearing units | 80–90% subsidy |
| Training and capacity building | 100% (free training provided) |
| Integrated bee breeding units | 80% subsidy |
| Apiary (bee house) construction | 50% subsidy |

> Subsidy is provided on **verified invoices** from approved suppliers — buy from NBB-approved vendors to claim subsidy.

---

## Who is Eligible?

**You ARE eligible if:**
- You are a farmer, landless labourer, or rural entrepreneur
- You want to take up beekeeping as a livelihood or supplementary income
- You have or can arrange land/orchard for placing hive boxes

**Priority categories:**
- **SC/ST applicants** — 90% subsidy (vs 80% for general)
- **Women beekeepers** — 90% subsidy
- **FPOs and cooperative societies** — eligible for group applications

**You are NOT eligible if:**
- You are an income tax payer (for highest subsidy slabs)
- You are already covered under another beekeeping subsidy scheme in the same year

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar Card** — identity and DBT
2. **Bank passbook** — Aadhaar-linked
3. **Caste certificate** — for SC/ST (90% subsidy)
4. **Land records or orchard certificate** — to show placement area
5. **Application form** — from state horticulture / NBB
6. **Photographs**

## How to Apply — Step by Step

### Step 1 — Contact NBB or State Department

Reach out to:
- **National Bee Board regional office** (contact via [nbb.gov.in](https://nbb.gov.in))
- Your **District Horticulture Officer**
- **NABARD district office** (for rural enterprise loan linkage)

### Step 2 — Attend Beekeeping Training

NBHM requires applicants to complete a **basic beekeeping training** (typically 5–7 days, free of cost) before receiving subsidised equipment. Training is provided at:
- State Agriculture Universities
- KVKs (Krishi Vigyan Kendras)
- NBB-empanelled training centres

### Step 3 — Submit Application

After training, submit the NBHM application form with:
- Training completion certificate
- Aadhaar, caste certificate, land documents
- Bank details

### Step 4 — Receive Approval

The district/state department verifies documents and issues a **work order** specifying how many bee boxes and equipment you can procure.

### Step 5 — Purchase from Approved Supplier

Buy bee colonies, boxes, and equipment from **NBB-approved suppliers** and collect original invoices.

### Step 6 — Subsidy Disbursement

Submit invoices and inspection report → subsidy credited to your bank account via DBT.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. I don't have a farm. Can I still do beekeeping?**

Yes. Bee boxes can be placed on any land — your own, leased, or even orchard margins. Many beekeepers are landless labourers who keep hives on contract with orchard owners.

**2. Is training mandatory before getting the subsidy?**

Yes. NBHM requires basic beekeeping training as a pre-condition. The training is free and typically 5–7 days. You will receive a certificate that is required for the application.

**3. How many bee boxes can I get under NBHM?**

Typically 10–50 boxes per beneficiary for initial setup (varies by state and available budget). Check with your District Horticulture Officer for the current limit.

**4. Where do I get NBB-approved suppliers?**

Visit [nbb.gov.in](https://nbb.gov.in) to find the list of registered bee suppliers, queen breeders, and equipment manufacturers in your region.

**5. Can a women's SHG apply as a group for beekeeping?**

Yes. Group applications are encouraged. An SHG can apply for a collective beekeeping unit and receive 90% subsidy as women beneficiaries.
