## What is ESIC?

The Employees' State Insurance Corporation (ESIC) administers the **ESI (Employees' State Insurance) scheme** — a comprehensive social security programme providing **medical, cash, maternity, disability, and death benefits** to employees and their dependents.

ESIC is governed by the **ESI Act, 1948** and managed by the Ministry of Labour and Employment. It is one of the largest social security systems in the world, covering over **14 crore beneficiaries**.

The scheme is funded by contributions from both **employers and employees**, and beneficiaries receive free medical treatment at ESI hospitals and dispensaries across India.

## Who Must Register?

### Employer Registration (Mandatory)
- **Factories** with 10 or more workers (20 in some states)
- **Establishments** with 10 or more employees (shops, hotels, restaurants, cinemas, road transport, newspaper establishments)
- **Any establishment** notified by the government under Section 1(5)

### Employee Coverage
- All employees earning up to **₹21,000 per month** (₹25,000 for persons with disability)
- Includes permanent, temporary, casual, and contract workers
- Apprentices under the Apprentices Act are excluded

### Exemptions
- Seasonal factories
- Mines covered under Mines Act
- Railway running sheds
- Government employees covered under separate schemes

## ESIC Benefits

### 1. Medical Benefit
- **Free medical care** at ESI hospitals, dispensaries, and panel clinics
- Covers the insured person and their dependents (spouse, children, parents)
- Includes OPD, IPD, surgery, medicines, tests, and specialist consultations
- Super-specialty treatment covered at tied-up hospitals
- No limit on medical expenses

### 2. Sickness Benefit
- **70% of average daily wages** for up to 91 days per year
- For sickness certified by an ESI doctor
- Extended sickness benefit: 80% wages for up to 2 years for serious diseases (TB, cancer, mental illness, etc.)

### 3. Maternity Benefit
- **100% of average daily wages** for 26 weeks (full salary)
- Extendable by 1 month on medical grounds
- Also covers miscarriage, medical termination, and adoption

### 4. Disablement Benefit
- **Temporary disablement:** 90% of wages during treatment
- **Permanent disablement:** Monthly pension based on disability percentage
- Covers work-related injuries and occupational diseases

### 5. Dependants' Benefit
- Monthly pension to dependents if the insured person dies due to employment injury
- **Spouse:** 60% of the full rate
- **Children:** 40% until age 25
- **Parents:** 40% if no spouse/children

### 6. Funeral Expenses
- ₹15,000 to the person who performs the funeral of the deceased insured person

### 7. Unemployment Allowance (Rajiv Gandhi Shramik Kalyan Yojana)
- **50% of wages** for up to 24 months if involuntarily unemployed
- Requires minimum 3 years of contribution
- Includes skill upgradation training during unemployment

## Contribution Rates

| Contributor | Rate | On Wages Up To |
|------------|------|---------------|
| Employer | **3.25%** of gross wages | ₹21,000/month |
| Employee | **0.75%** of gross wages | ₹21,000/month |
| **Total** | **4%** | |

**Exemption:** Employees earning up to **₹176/day** (approximately ₹4,576/month) are exempt from their 0.75% contribution. The employer's contribution still applies.

## How to Register as an Employer

### Online Registration on ESIC Portal
1. Visit [esic.gov.in](https://www.esic.gov.in)
2. Click **"Employer Login"** → **"Sign Up"**
3. Enter your details and create an account
4. Log in and select **"New Employer Registration"**
5. Fill the **Employer Registration Form (Form 01)**:
   - **Establishment details** — Name, address, type, date of setup
   - **Owner/partner/director details**
   - **Employee details** — Total count, wages
   - **Bank details** — For contribution payments
   - **PAN of establishment** — See our [PAN guide](/guide/pan-card-apply-online)
6. Upload documents:
   - Certificate of Incorporation / Partnership deed / Shop licence
   - PAN card
   - Address proof of establishment
   - Bank statement / Cancelled cheque
   - List of employees with Aadhaar numbers
7. Submit the form
8. Receive **Employer Code Number (ESIC Code)** — typically a 17-digit number
9. Registration is effective from the date the establishment becomes eligible

### Registration Timeline
- Must register **within 15 days** of becoming eligible (crossing 10 employee threshold)
- Delayed registration may attract penalties

## How to Register Employees

Once the employer is registered:

### Step 1: Collect Employee Details
- Full name, date of birth, gender
- Aadhaar number
- Bank account details
- Photograph
- Family/dependent details (for medical benefit)

### Step 2: Register on ESIC Portal
1. Log in to the employer portal
2. Go to **"Employee Registration"**
3. Enter employee's Aadhaar number — details auto-populate from Aadhaar
4. Fill remaining details — wages, date of joining, nominee
5. Upload employee photograph
6. Submit

### Step 3: Generate ESI Number (IP Number)
- Each employee gets a unique **Insurance Number (IP Number)**
- A temporary card is generated online
- **Pehchan Card (ESI smart card)** is issued later with biometric data

### Step 4: Add Dependents
Register employee's family members for medical benefit:
- Spouse, children (up to 2), and parents
- Upload family photos and details

## Monthly Compliance

### Contribution Payment
1. Log in to the ESIC employer portal
2. Generate the **monthly contribution challan**
3. Pay within **15th of the following month**
   - Example: April contributions due by 15th May
4. Pay via net banking, UPI, or cheque

### Return Filing
- **Half-yearly returns** — File within 42 days of the contribution period ending
- Period 1: April – September (due by 11th November)
- Period 2: October – March (due by 11th May)

### Penalties for Non-Compliance
- **Late payment interest:** 12% per annum
- **Penalty for non-registration:** Up to ₹50,000 or imprisonment
- **False reporting:** Penalty and prosecution under the ESI Act

## Important Tips

1. **Register within 15 days** of crossing the employee threshold — delays attract penalties
2. **Ensure all employees below ₹21,000 are covered** — Missing employees is a compliance risk
3. **Pay contributions on time** — The 15th deadline is strict; late payments attract 12% interest
4. **Educate employees** about ESI benefits — Many don't know they can get free medical treatment
5. **Use ESI hospitals and dispensaries** — They provide comprehensive treatment at zero cost for registered employees

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is ESIC registration mandatory for all companies?
ESIC registration is mandatory for establishments with 10 or more employees (in most states). Establishments with fewer employees are not required to register unless notified by the state government.

### Can an employee opt out of ESIC?
Employees earning up to ₹21,000/month cannot opt out — ESIC coverage is mandatory. However, once their salary exceeds ₹21,000, they continue to be covered until the end of the contribution period.

### What is the ESI Pehchan Card?
The Pehchan Card is a biometric smart card issued to ESI beneficiaries. It's used for identity verification at ESI hospitals. Until the card is issued, a temporary slip from the portal can be used.

### Can ESI benefits be used at private hospitals?
ESI benefits are primarily for ESI hospitals and dispensaries. However, ESIC has tie-ups with certain private hospitals for super-specialty treatment. In emergencies, reimbursement claims can be filed for private hospital treatment.

### What happens to ESI benefits after leaving a job?
Medical benefits continue for **3 months** after leaving employment (if you contributed for 78 days in the previous contribution period). Cash benefits for ongoing treatment continue until recovery.

### How do I find the nearest ESI hospital?
Visit [esic.gov.in](https://www.esic.gov.in) and use the hospital/dispensary locator. You can also call the ESIC helpline at **1800-11-2526** (toll-free).
