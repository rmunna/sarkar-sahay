# Odisha Mukhyamantri Annapurna Yojana 2026 — 10 kg Free Rice for 3.28 Crore Odisha Residents

The Odisha government under Chief Minister Mohan Charan Majhi launched the **Mukhyamantri Annapurna Yojana (MAPY)** on **June 13, 2026** — on the BJP government's second anniversary. The scheme provides **10 kg of free rice per month** to 3.28 crore beneficiaries across Odisha.

**Quick answer:** If you have a valid Odisha ration card and are in the state's food security list, you receive 10 kg of free rice per month automatically through your Fair Price Shop (FPS/ration shop). No separate application needed for existing ration card holders.

## Who Is Eligible?

The scheme targets **economically vulnerable households** in Odisha:

- Holders of **Odisha state ration cards** covered under food security lists
- Families under **NFSA (National Food Security Act)** and state-level food security schemes
- **BPL (Below Poverty Line)** households
- **Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY)** households — priority section
- **Orunodoi scheme** beneficiaries and similar vulnerable groups

The government is covering **3.28 crore beneficiaries** — a significant portion of Odisha's approximately 4.6 crore population. If your family is already getting subsidised ration through any Odisha government scheme, you likely qualify.

---

## What You Get

- **10 kg of rice per month** — free of cost
- Distributed through your existing **Fair Price Shop** (FPS)
- The 10 kg is **in addition to** the rice you already receive under central NFSA entitlements (if applicable), or as a standalone benefit for state-scheme beneficiaries
- Rice is standard PDS quality (medium grade / CMR grade)

## How to Get Your Free Rice

### For Existing Ration Card Holders

**No new application needed.** The entitlement is automatically added to your ration card:

1. Visit your **designated Fair Price Shop (ration shop)** on your allotted distribution day
2. Carry your **ration card** (physical card or digital copy on e-Ration card app)
3. Complete **biometric authentication** (Aadhaar fingerprint/OTP at FPS PoS machine)
4. Collect your **10 kg free rice**

Distribution schedule varies by FPS — check with your local ration shop or Panchayat for the date.

### For New Applicants (Not Yet on Ration Card)

If you are a resident of Odisha but don't yet have a ration card:

**Step 1:** Apply for a new ration card at:
- Your nearest **Block Supply Office** (rural) or **Urban Supply Officer office** (urban)
- OR online at **[foododisha.in](https://foododisha.in)**

**Step 2:** Documents required for new ration card:
- Aadhaar card (all family members)
- Proof of Odisha residence (voter ID, driving licence, electricity bill)
- Income proof (if applying for BPL/AAY category)
- Passport-size photos of head of household
- Recent family photo

**Step 3:** After ration card is issued and entered in food security database, Annapurna Yojana rice entitlement is automatically included.

## About the Scheme — Context

Odisha CM Mohan Charan Majhi announced the Mukhyamantri Annapurna Yojana as part of the BJP government's 2nd anniversary celebrations on June 13, 2026. The announcement was made alongside a commitment to **free education from KG to PG** for economically weaker students.

The scheme is designed to **ensure no family in Odisha goes to bed hungry** — complementing the central government's Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana (PMGKAY) which provides 5 kg of free foodgrain per person per month through NFSA.

Together with PMGKAY (5 kg per person) and Mukhyamantri Annapurna Yojana (10 kg per household), eligible families in Odisha now have significantly enhanced food security coverage.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**I already receive free rice under PMGKAY — will I get additional 10 kg under this scheme?**
Yes — Mukhyamantri Annapurna Yojana is a state scheme that runs alongside the central PMGKAY. Eligible beneficiaries get both. The 10 kg under MAPY is a state addition on top of your existing central entitlements.

**Which ration card categories are covered?**
Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) and Priority Household (PHH) categories under NFSA, plus Odisha state food security scheme beneficiaries. BPL card holders are included.

**Do I need to go to a different shop for this rice?**
No — the same Fair Price Shop (ration shop) where you currently collect your ration will distribute the Annapurna Yojana rice.

**What if my FPS says the scheme hasn't started yet?**
The scheme was announced June 13, 2026. Implementation may be staggered district-by-district. Check your district Food Supply Officer office or call the Odisha Food helpline.

**How is the rice quality — is it the same as regular PDS rice?**
PDS rice is standard government-issued CMR (Custom Milled Rice) which is generally medium grain. Quality is regulated by the state civil supplies department.

**I live outside Odisha but my family is there — can they get this benefit?**
The benefit goes to the registered household at the FPS. Your family members listed on the Odisha ration card will receive it. You don't need to be physically present.
