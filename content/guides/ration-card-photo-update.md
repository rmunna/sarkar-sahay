# Ration Card Photo Update: How to Change Photo Online

If the photo on your ration card is outdated, missing, or incorrect, you can update it through your state's Food & Civil Supplies portal or by visiting the local supply office. Many states now allow online photo updates as part of the ration card correction process.

## Why Update Your Ration Card Photo?

- **Old photo** — the photo no longer resembles the family member
- **Missing photo** — some older ration cards were issued without photographs
- **Wrong photo** — incorrect photo was uploaded during application
- **New member added** — a recently added family member needs their photo on the card
- **Biometric mismatch** — photo mismatch may cause issues at Fair Price Shops

## Photo Requirements

Before uploading or submitting a photo, ensure it meets these specifications:

| Requirement | Specification |
|-------------|--------------|
| Format | JPEG or PNG |
| Size | 10 KB to 100 KB (varies by state) |
| Dimensions | Passport-size (3.5 cm × 4.5 cm or as specified) |
| Background | White or light-coloured background |
| Recency | Taken within the last 6 months |
| Clarity | Face clearly visible, no sunglasses or caps |
| Resolution | Minimum 200 × 200 pixels |

> Requirements may vary slightly by state. Check your state portal for exact specifications.

## Online Process — Update Photo via State Portal

### Steps (General Process)

1. Visit your state's Food & Civil Supplies portal (see [state portals list](/guide/ration-card-download-online)).
2. Log in using your **ration card number**, **Aadhaar number**, or **registered mobile number**.
3. Navigate to **"Ration Card Correction"**, **"Update Details"**, or **"Photo Update"** section.
4. Select the **family member** whose photo needs to be updated.
5. Upload the **new passport-size photograph** in the required format.
6. If updating the head of family's photo, you may need to upload additional ID proof.
7. Submit the request and note the **application/reference number**.
8. The update will be processed after verification (typically 7-15 days).

## Offline Process — Update Photo at Supply Office

If your state does not support online photo updates or you prefer an in-person visit:

1. Visit the **Taluk Supply Office** or **Block Development Office** in your area.
2. Carry your **original ration card** and **Aadhaar card**.
3. Bring **2-3 passport-size photographs** of the family member.
4. Fill in the **ration card correction/update form**.
5. Submit the form with photographs and supporting documents.
6. Collect the **acknowledgment receipt**.
7. The updated card will be issued after verification (15-30 days).

## Documents Required

- Original ration card
- Aadhaar card of the family member whose photo is being updated
- New passport-size photographs (2-3 copies)
- Ration card correction application form
- Self-declaration (if head of family photo is being changed)

## Fee

Photo update on ration card is **free of charge** in most states. Some states may charge a nominal correction fee of ₹5-₹15.

## Processing Timeline

| Method | Timeline |
|--------|----------|
| Online submission | 7-15 days for verification and update |
| Offline (office visit) | 15-30 days |
| CSC centre submission | 10-20 days |

## Important Tips

1. Ensure the photo is **recent and clear** — blurry or old photos may be rejected during verification.
2. The photo should match the person's **Aadhaar photo** to avoid biometric authentication issues at Fair Price Shops.
3. If you are updating photos for multiple family members, submit all requests together to save time.
4. After the update is approved, [download your updated e-Ration Card](/guide/ration-card-download-online) to verify the change.
5. If the online portal shows an error during upload, try reducing the file size or converting to JPEG format.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I update the ration card photo online?
Yes, many states allow photo updates through the online correction module on their Food & Civil Supplies portal. Check your state's specific portal.

### Is there a fee for photo update?
Most states do not charge any fee for photo updates. Some states may charge ₹5-₹15 as a correction fee.

### Can I update photos of all family members at once?
Yes. Most portals allow you to update photos for multiple members in a single correction request.

### What if my photo update request is rejected?
Check the rejection reason (usually poor photo quality or format mismatch). Re-upload a photo that meets the specifications and re-submit.

### Do I need to visit the office for photo update?
Not necessarily. If your state supports online photo updates, you can complete the entire process from home. Otherwise, visit the Taluk Supply Office.

### Will my ration card number change after photo update?
No. Your ration card number and all other details remain the same. Only the photograph is updated.
