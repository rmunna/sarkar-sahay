# Dubai / UAE Visa from India 2026 — Complete Guide

Dubai is one of the most popular international destinations for Indians. The UAE visa process is relatively straightforward — and Indians holding valid US, UK, or Schengen visas can even get visa on arrival. Here's the complete guide.

---

## Do Indians Need a UAE Visa?

Indian passport holders require a UAE visa. However:

### Visa on Arrival (VoA) — Free If You Qualify

Indians holding **valid** (not expired) visas from:
- USA (B1/B2 or any valid non-immigrant visa)
- UK
- Any Schengen country (27 countries)
- European Union member states

Can get **UAE Visa on Arrival** at Dubai/Abu Dhabi/Sharjah airports:
- **Duration**: 14 days (extendable for 14 more days)
- **Fee**: Free
- **Process**: Immigration officer stamps on arrival — no prior application needed

**Conditions**: Visa must be valid for at least 6 months from date of UAE entry OR have been used at least once AND still valid.

If you don't qualify for VoA: Apply in advance online (see below).

## How to Apply for UAE Tourist Visa — Online

### Route 1: Through Emirates or Air Arabia Airlines

If flying on **Emirates** or **Air Arabia** (or their partner airlines), you can apply for a UAE visa directly through their website:
- Emirates: emiratres.com → Book → Visa Services
- Air Arabia: airarabia.com → Visa section

**Advantage**: Slightly faster processing (2–3 days); airline acts as sponsor.

**Cost**: Airline charges a processing fee (AED 100–150 on top of visa fee).

### Route 2: GDRFA (General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs) Dubai

Online application at [gdrfad.gov.ae](https://gdrfad.gov.ae):
1. Click "e-Services" → Online Visa Application
2. Fill application form
3. Upload documents
4. Pay fee online (credit/debit card)
5. Receive visa via email (PDF e-visa) within 3–4 working days

**GDRFA is the official UAE government portal** — use this if not flying Emirates/Air Arabia.

### Route 3: Through UAE Visa Agent in India

Multiple travel agents in India (Thomas Cook, VFS UAE, various travel agencies) submit UAE visa applications. Convenient but adds ₹500–1,500 service fee.

### Financial Documents

| Document | Notes |
|----------|-------|

| **Salary slips** | Income proof for salaried |
| **Business proof** | For self-employed |

**No strict financial minimum** is officially published, but ₹50,000–75,000+ visible in account for a 7–10 day trip is generally considered safe.

## UAE Entry Rules

- **Date of entry**: Must enter UAE within validity of your visa (usually visa is valid for 60 days from issue date; your actual stay is 30/60/90 days from entry)
- **Extension**: Tourist visa can be extended inside UAE at GDRFA office or via airline (if you flew on Emirates)
- **Multiple entry**: Regular tourist visas are single-entry. 5-year multiple entry visas allow multiple visits
- **Working in UAE**: Not permitted on tourist visa — separate employment visa needed

Your UAE visa is valid across ALL of UAE:

One UAE visa, travel freely across all emirates.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My US visa is expired. Can I still get UAE Visa on Arrival?

No — the qualifying visa (US, UK, Schengen) must be **currently valid** (not expired). Expired visas do not qualify for UAE VoA.

### I have a Schengen visa valid for 3 more weeks. Can I use it for UAE VoA?

Yes, if the Schengen visa has at least 1 month of validity remaining (rules require valid for at least 6 months OR have been used at least once — if it's been used once and still has any validity, you can use it). Best practice: have 3+ months of validity remaining.

### How long does UAE visa take from India?

Online applications via GDRFA or Emirates: 3–4 working days. Agent-assisted applications: 3–5 working days.

### Can I overstay in Dubai?

Absolutely not — overstay fine is AED 100 per day (₹2,300+/day). Overstay also appears in UAE records and can affect future visa applications. Leave before your authorized stay expires.

### Dubai 5-year multiple-entry visa — who should get it?

If you visit Dubai 2+ times a year for business or personal travel, the 5-year multiple-entry (AED 1,500 ≈ ₹34,000) pays off very quickly vs getting a fresh 30-day visa (AED 350) each time. Typically requires a clean previous UAE visit record.
