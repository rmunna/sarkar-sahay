## What is Professional Tax?

Professional Tax (PT) is a state-level tax levied on individuals earning income through employment, profession, trade, or calling. Despite its name, it applies not just to professionals but to all salaried employees and self-employed persons. Professional tax is governed by Article 276 of the Indian Constitution.

The maximum professional tax that any state can levy is **₹2,500 per year**. It is deducted by the employer from the employee's salary and deposited with the respective state government. Self-employed individuals must register and pay it themselves.

Professional tax is deductible under Section 16(iii) of the Income Tax Act while computing taxable income from salary — see our [income tax guide](/guide/income-tax-return-file-online) for more details.

## Which States Levy Professional Tax?

Professional tax is levied by the following states and union territories:

- **Maharashtra** — Up to ₹2,500/year
- **Karnataka** — Up to ₹2,400/year
- **West Bengal** — Up to ₹2,500/year
- **Andhra Pradesh** — Up to ₹2,500/year
- **Telangana** — Up to ₹2,500/year
- **Tamil Nadu** — Up to ₹2,500/year (half-yearly)
- **Gujarat** — Up to ₹2,500/year
- **Madhya Pradesh** — Up to ₹2,500/year
- **Kerala** — Up to ₹2,500/year
- **Assam** — Up to ₹2,500/year
- **Bihar** — Up to ₹2,500/year
- **Odisha** — Up to ₹2,500/year
- **Jharkhand** — Up to ₹2,500/year
- **Meghalaya, Tripura, Manipur, Sikkim, Mizoram** — Varying rates

**States that do NOT levy professional tax** include Delhi, Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan, Haryana, Punjab, Uttarakhand, Himachal Pradesh, and most northeastern states.

## Who is Exempt from Professional Tax?

- **Parents of children with permanent disability** (in some states)
- **Members of armed forces** (Army, Navy, Air Force)
- **Senior citizens above 65 years** (in some states)
- **Persons with disabilities** (typically 40% or more disability)
- **Women earning below specified threshold** (state-specific)
- **Foreign diplomats and consular staff**
- **Badli workers in textile industries** (Maharashtra)

Exemptions vary significantly by state — check your state's specific rules.

## How to Register for Professional Tax

### For Employers (PTEC — Professional Tax Enrolment Certificate)
Employers must register within **30 days** of hiring their first employee:

1. Visit your state's commercial tax or GST portal
2. Fill the application form for PTEC
3. Upload required documents — PAN, certificate of incorporation, address proof
4. Pay the registration fee (typically ₹2,000 – ₹5,000)
5. Receive PTEC number

### For Self-Employed (PTRC — Professional Tax Registration Certificate)
Self-employed professionals must register individually:

1. Visit the state's professional tax portal
2. Apply for PTRC with professional details
3. Submit PAN, qualification certificates, address proof
4. Receive PTRC number and pay tax periodically

## How to Pay Professional Tax Online

### Step 1: Visit State Portal
Go to your respective state's commercial tax or professional tax portal.

### Step 2: Log In
Use your PTEC/PTRC number and password to log in.

### Step 3: Select Payment Period
Choose the month or period for which you want to pay.

### Step 4: Enter Details
Fill in employee count, salary details, and tax amount.

### Step 5: Make Payment
Pay via net banking, debit card, or NEFT/RTGS.

### Step 6: Download Receipt
Save the payment receipt and challan for records.

## Filing Professional Tax Returns

Employers must file PT returns periodically:
- **Monthly** — If annual PT liability exceeds ₹50,000 (in most states)
- **Annually** — If annual PT liability is below ₹50,000

The return includes:
- Total number of employees
- Total salary paid
- Total PT deducted
- Challan details of PT deposited

## Penalties for Non-Compliance

- **Late registration** — Penalty of ₹5 per day of delay (varies by state)
- **Late payment** — Interest of 1-2% per month on the unpaid amount
- **Non-filing of returns** — Penalty up to ₹1,000 per month of default
- **Non-deduction** — Employer becomes personally liable for the tax plus penalty

## Important Tips

1. **Employers are responsible** for deducting and depositing PT from employees' salaries — failure makes the employer liable
2. **Claim deduction** — Professional tax paid is deductible under Section 16(iii) when filing your ITR
3. **Check your Form 16** — PT deducted should reflect in your Form 16 from the employer; see our [Form 16 guide](/guide/form-16-download-guide) for details
4. **Keep receipts** — Maintain PT payment receipts for at least 6 years for audit purposes
5. **State migration** — If you move to another state, ensure registration in the new state and deregistration from the old one

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is professional tax mandatory for all employees?
Professional tax is mandatory for all salaried employees in states that levy it. The employer is responsible for deducting it from salary. However, employees earning below the threshold slab are exempt.

### Can professional tax be claimed as a deduction in income tax?
Yes, professional tax paid is fully deductible under Section 16(iii) of the Income Tax Act. It reduces your taxable salary income. Ensure it's reflected in your Form 16.

### What is the maximum professional tax in India?
As per Article 276 of the Constitution, no state can levy professional tax exceeding ₹2,500 per person per year.

### Do freelancers need to pay professional tax?
Yes, self-employed professionals and freelancers in states that levy PT must register and pay it themselves. The rates depend on their annual income and the state they operate in.

### What happens if my employer doesn't deduct professional tax?
If your employer fails to deduct PT, they become liable for the tax amount plus penalties. As an employee, you should inform your HR department. The liability ultimately rests with the employer.

### Is professional tax applicable in Delhi?
No, Delhi does not levy professional tax. If you work in Delhi, no PT will be deducted from your salary, regardless of your income level.
