# Central Sector Scholarship 2026 — Up to ₹20,000/Year for Top College Students

The **Central Sector Scheme of Scholarships (CSSS)** is a merit-cum-means scholarship from the Government of India that provides annual financial support to **82,800 top students** who scored above the **80th percentile in their Class 12 board exam** and are pursuing degree courses at college or university.

**Quick answer:** If you scored above the 80th percentile in Class 12 and your family income is below ₹8 lakh/year, apply at [scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in) by **December 31, 2026**. You can receive ₹10,000–₹20,000 per year throughout your degree without reapplying each year (automatic renewal).

---

## Scholarship Amount

| Level | Annual Amount |
|---|---|
| Undergraduate (UG) — General courses | ₹10,000/year |
| Undergraduate (UG) — Technical/Professional (B.Tech, MBBS, B.Arch, BBA, etc.) | ₹20,000/year |
| Postgraduate (PG) | ₹20,000/year |

- Amount is paid annually by Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to your bank account
- Non-refundable (it's a grant, not a loan)
- Duration: throughout the degree, subject to renewal conditions

---

## Eligibility Criteria

### Academic Eligibility

- Must have scored in the **top 80th percentile of successful candidates** in your stream (Science / Commerce / Arts) in your Class 12 board exam
- Boards covered: CBSE, ICSE, and all state boards
- Must be enrolled in a **regular (non-correspondence/distance) degree course** at a UGC-recognised college or university
- Eligible courses: B.A., B.Sc., B.Com., B.Tech., B.E., MBBS, B.Arch., B.Pharma., B.Ed., MBA, M.Tech., etc.

> **What is the 80th percentile?** If 1,000 students passed the exam in your stream, the top 200 (20%) would be at or above the 80th percentile. It's stream and board specific — the Ministry of Education publishes the cut-off marks for each board annually.

### Income Eligibility

- Family annual income must be **below ₹8 lakh per year**
- All sources of income (salary, business, agriculture, rents) are counted
- Income certificate from a government authority (Tahsildar/SDM) is required

### Other Conditions

- Student must NOT be receiving any other central government scholarship
- Must be studying in a college/university recognized by AICTE, BCI, MCI, or UGC
- Not for students pursuing distance or correspondence courses
- Must be a regular full-time student

## How to Apply — Step by Step

### Step 1: Register on NSP

1. Go to **[scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in)**
2. Click **"New Registration"**
3. Select: State → Ministry of Education → Central Sector Scheme of Scholarship
4. Fill in basic details: name, date of birth, mobile number
5. Choose your bank account for DBT (Aadhaar-linked account strongly recommended)
6. Note down your Application ID

### Step 2: Login and Fill Application

1. Login with your Application ID and password
2. Click **"Fresh Application"** (for first-time applicants)
3. Fill in:
   - Personal details (name, Aadhaar, address)
   - Academic details: board name, 12th roll number, year of passing, % marks / score
   - College/University details: institution name, course, year of study
   - Bank account details

### Step 3: Upload Documents

Upload scanned/photo copies (JPG or PDF, max 200KB each):
- Class 12 marksheet / board result
- College/institution enrollment proof (bonafide certificate or admission letter)
- Income certificate issued by Tahsildar/SDM/Circle Officer
- Aadhaar card
- Bank passbook (first page showing account number + IFSC)
- Passport-size photograph

### Step 4: Submit and Verify at Institute

1. Submit the application — note the Application ID and print it
2. The application goes to your college/university for **institute verification**
3. Your college nodal officer approves the application on NSP
4. After institute verification, the state/central officers do final approval

### Step 5: DBT Payment

After full approval, ₹10,000 or ₹20,000 is credited directly to your Aadhaar-linked bank account.

## How Many Students Get This Scholarship?

- **Total seats: 82,000+** per year (nationwide)
- Distributed across all board examinations and streams
- Proportional to the number of Class 12 students in each state and stream

This means competition is significant but clear — if you meet the 80th percentile and income criteria, your chances are high since the scholarship is not a competitive exam but a criteria-based selection.

---

## Renewal (Continuing Students)

For students already receiving CSSS who want to continue in the next year:

1. Login to scholarships.gov.in
2. Click **"Renewal Application"**
3. Submit updated enrollment proof for the current year
4. Maintain progress in your course (no repeat year without valid reason)

You don't need to meet the 80th percentile again after the first year — the renewal requirement is just continued enrollment and academic progress.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the 80th percentile cut-off marks?**
It varies by board, year, and stream. After board results, the Ministry of Education calculates the 80th percentile mark for each board and stream. You'll know if you qualify based on the NSP portal's eligibility check when you enter your board and marks.

**Can I apply if I'm already in 2nd year of B.Tech?**
Yes — students already in their degree can apply for Fresh scholarship if they haven't applied before and meet the criteria. Apply in the 2026-27 cycle.

**Can I apply if I'm studying at a private college?**
Yes — CSSS is for any UGC/AICTE/MCI/BCI-recognised institution, including private colleges. Your institution must be on the approved list.

**What if my college isn't registered on NSP?**
Ask your college to register on the NSP institutional portal. Without institute verification, your application cannot be processed. Contact your college's scholarship coordinator.

**Can I get this if I'm also getting a state scholarship?**
The Central Sector Scholarship prohibits receiving other **central government** scholarships simultaneously. State scholarships in addition to CSSS may be allowed — check your state's rules.

**What if my Class 12 marksheet shows percentage, not percentile?**
You enter your marks on the NSP portal, and the system automatically checks if you meet the 80th percentile for your board and stream (using data from your board). You don't need to compute percentile yourself.

**How long does approval take?**
After you submit, institute verification typically takes 2-4 weeks. State/central officer approval may take additional weeks. Total: expect 2-4 months from submission to payment.
