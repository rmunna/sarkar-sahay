# Mahila Samman Savings Certificate — Maturity & How to Claim Your Money Back

The **Mahila Samman Savings Certificate (MSSC)** scheme, announced in Budget 2023 and opened for deposits from **April 1, 2023**, ran for 2 years. Accounts opened in April 2023 matured in **April 2025**. If you or a family member had opened an MSSC account, here's how to claim the maturity amount.

## Scheme at a Glance

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Scheme name** | Mahila Samman Savings Certificate (MSSC) |
| **Opened for** | April 1, 2023 – March 31, 2025 |
| **Tenure** | 2 years |
| **Interest rate** | 7.5% per annum, compounded quarterly |
| **Maximum deposit** | ₹2,00,000 per account |
| **Eligible** | Women of all ages (minor accounts allowed via guardian) |
| **Where** | Post offices across India |
| **Status** | Scheme ended March 31, 2025. No new accounts. Existing accounts at maturity. |

## Maturity Amount Calculation

If you deposited the **maximum ₹2,00,000** on April 1, 2023:

| Deposit | Rate | Compounding | Tenure | Maturity |
|---------|------|------------|--------|---------|
| ₹2,00,000 | 7.5% p.a. | Quarterly | 2 years | **₹2,32,044** |

Interest earned: ₹32,044

For other amounts:
- ₹1,00,000 at 7.5% quarterly for 2 years = **₹1,16,022**
- ₹50,000 at 7.5% quarterly for 2 years = **₹58,011**

## How to Claim Maturity Amount

### Step 1: Go to the Post Office Where Account Was Opened

Maturity of small savings schemes (including MSSC) is processed at the **originating post office** (where you opened the account).

### Step 2: Documents Required

Carry the following:
- **MSSC passbook** (original)
- **Government photo ID**: Aadhaar card, Voter ID, Passport, Driving Licence
- **Savings Account details** (if you want credit to bank account) — IFSC + account number

### Step 3: Fill the Closure/Maturity Form

At the counter:
1. Ask for the **Maturity/Closure form** for MSSC
2. Fill your account number, date of account opening, maturity date
3. Mention your withdrawal preference: Cash (up to ₹20,000) or Account Credit (above ₹20,000 goes to savings/bank account)

### Step 4: Receive Maturity Amount

The post office will credit the maturity amount either:
- **Cash at counter** (for amounts up to ₹20,000)
- **Transfer to your Post Office Savings Account** (for amounts above ₹20,000)
- **Transfer to linked bank account** via NEFT (may take 2-3 working days)

## Tax on MSSC Interest

| Tax Aspect | Details |
|------------|---------|
| **TDS** | Post office deducts 10% TDS on interest if interest > ₹40,000/year (₹50,000 for senior citizens) |
| **Income tax** | Interest is taxable as "Income from Other Sources" at your slab rate |
| **80C benefit** | MSSC deposit DOES NOT qualify for Section 80C deduction |
| **Form 26AS** | TDS (if deducted) will appear in Form 26AS linked to your PAN |

If TDS was deducted, claim the credit in your ITR. If no TDS but interest exceeds ₹10,000, you may need to pay advance tax on the interest income.

## Premature Closure (Before 2-Year Maturity)

MSSC allowed premature closure with conditions:

| Timing | Premature Closure Rule |
|--------|----------------------|
| Before 6 months | Not allowed at all |
| After 6 months, before 1 year | Allowed at **5.5% interest** (instead of 7.5%) |
| After 1 year, before 2 years | Allowed at **7.5% interest minus 1%** penalty = **6.5% interest** |
| In case of account holder's death | Allowed at full 7.5%, no penalty, at any time |

If you closed the account prematurely (after April 2023 and before April 2025), the interest you received would have been at the reduced rate above.

## What If I Haven't Claimed Yet?

If the 2-year maturity date passed in April 2025 and you haven't claimed:
- The maturity amount **sits in the account** — the post office does not automatically transfer it
- There is no additional interest earned after the maturity date in most cases (or it earns Post Office Savings Account rate which is 4% p.a.)
- **Go to the post office and claim it as soon as possible**

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I open a new MSSC account now?

No. The Mahila Samman Savings Certificate scheme **ended on March 31, 2025**. No new accounts can be opened. Existing accounts continue to maturity.

### My daughter's MSSC account matures — who claims it?

If the account was opened in the name of a minor (with the mother/guardian), the guardian submits the maturity form. The amount is credited to the minor's/guardian's account as applicable.

### I lost my MSSC passbook. Can I still claim maturity?

Yes. Visit the post office with your ID proof and PAN/Aadhaar. Formally request a passbook reissue and then proceed with maturity claim. The post office has account records.

### Will the interest be in Form 26AS automatically?

Only if TDS was deducted. For interest below ₹40,000, no TDS is deducted — you must check the passbook/statement and report interest in your ITR manually under "Income from Other Sources."

### Are there similar schemes open now in 2025-26?

Post-MSSC alternatives for women:
- **Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)**: For girls below 10 years; 8.2% interest; 21-year lock-in
- **Post Office Time Deposit 2 Year**: 7% interest; open to all; 2-year lock-in
- **NSC (National Savings Certificate)**: 7.7% for 5 years; 80C deductible
