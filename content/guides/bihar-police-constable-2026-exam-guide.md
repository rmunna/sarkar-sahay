# Bihar Police Constable 2026 — Complete Recruitment Guide

Bihar Police Constable recruitment is conducted by the **Central Selection Board of Constable (CSBC), Bihar**. With **20,000–30,000 vacancies** in each cycle, it is one of the largest state police recruitments in India.

With **12th pass (Intermediate) as the qualifying education**, Bihar Police Constable is accessible to a wide pool of candidates from Bihar.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is independent and not affiliated with CSBC Bihar. Verify all details at [csbc.bih.nic.in](https://csbc.bih.nic.in).

## Eligibility Criteria

| Criterion | Requirement |
|-----------|------------|
| **Education** | Class 12 (Intermediate) from a recognised board |
| **Age (Male — General/OBC)** | 18–25 years |
| **Age (Male — SC/ST)** | 18–27 years |
| **Age (Female — General/OBC)** | 18–25 years |
| **Age (Female — SC/ST)** | 18–27 years |
| **Height (Male — General/OBC/SC)** | 165 cm |
| **Height (Male — ST)** | 160 cm |
| **Height (Female — General/OBC/SC)** | 155 cm |
| **Chest (Male — un-expanded/expanded)** | 81/86 cm (General/OBC/SC); 79/84 cm (ST) |

## Salary & Benefits

| Component | Amount |
|-----------|--------|
| **Basic Pay** | ₹21,700/month (Level 3 — 7th CPC) |
| **DA** | ~₹5,000–₹7,000 |
| **HRA** | 8%–24% of basic |
| **Other allowances** | ₹3,000–₹5,000 |
| **Gross In-Hand** | ₹25,000–₹32,000/month |

**Benefits:** Government accommodation (police lines/barracks), uniform, pension, medical coverage.

**Career Growth:** Constable → Head Constable → ASI → SI → Inspector (through departmental exam/BPSSC)

## Syllabus

### Hindi
Grammar: संधि, समास, विलोम, पर्यायवाची, मुहावरे, Sentence correction, Reading comprehension

### English
Grammar, Synonyms/Antonyms, Fill in the blanks, Vocabulary, Simple RC

### Mathematics
Number system, LCM/HCF, Percentage, Ratio & Proportion, Simple/Compound Interest, Profit/Loss, Time-Work, Time-Distance, Mensuration

### Science (Class 10 Level)
Physics, Chemistry, Biology basics

### Social Studies
Indian History, Indian Geography, Indian Polity, Economics basics

### Current Affairs
Bihar-specific and national current events (last 6 months)

## Preparation Tips

1. **Bihar GK is crucial** — specific questions on Bihar history, geography, culture, schemes (Mukhyamantri schemes, BSSC exams, Nitish Kumar's initiatives)
2. **Written exam is relatively easier** than UP Police or SSC exams — focus on completing all questions accurately
3. **Physical test is strict** — 1 mile in 6 minutes requires serious running practice. Start from Month 1
4. **Hindi scoring** — strong Hindi (grammar + comprehension) can boost your rank significantly
5. **Mock tests** — previous CSBC Bihar question papers are available online; solve all of them

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the minimum age for Bihar Police Constable 2026?**
18 years for all categories. Maximum age varies by category (25–27 years). Check the official notification for exact age.

**Is there any restriction on number of attempts for Bihar Police Constable?**
No attempt limit — you can appear in every recruitment as long as you are within the age limit.

**Can female candidates apply for Bihar Police Constable?**
Yes — Bihar has separate vacancies for female constables (Mahila Constables). Physical standards are different for female candidates.

**What documents are required for Bihar Police Constable application?**
Class 12 marksheet, Aadhaar, caste certificate (if applicable), domicile certificate, and Bihar residency proof (for reserved category benefits).

**Is Bihar Police Constable a permanent job?**
Yes — it is a regular state government job with full pension, medical, and job security.
