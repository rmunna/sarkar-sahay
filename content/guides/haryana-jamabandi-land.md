## What is Haryana Jamabandi?

Jamabandi is the official online land records portal of the Government of Haryana. Managed by the Revenue Department, it provides digital access to land ownership records including jamabandi nakal (Record of Rights), mutation details, owner information, khasra girdawari, and other revenue records for all districts of Haryana.

"Jamabandi" refers to the Record of Rights document that contains details of land ownership, area, revenue assessment, and tenancy for each revenue estate (village) in Haryana.

## Key Services on Jamabandi Portal

- **Jamabandi Nakal** — View Record of Rights with owner, area, and khewat details
- **Mutation Status** — Track ownership transfer/mutation progress
- **Owner Details Search** — Find land records by owner name
- **Khasra/Girdawari** — View crop inspection and land use records
- **Nakal Verification** — Verify the authenticity of a jamabandi copy
- **Check Nakal** — View digitized copies of revenue records

## Who Can Use the Jamabandi Portal?

- Any citizen of India
- Land owners in Haryana verifying their records
- Property buyers checking ownership before purchase
- Farmers verifying crop and land records
- Banks for agriculture and mortgage loan verification
- Legal professionals handling property cases

No registration is required for searching land records on the Jamabandi portal.

## Understanding Haryana Land Record Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Jamabandi | Record of Rights — primary land ownership document |
| Khewat | Owner number — groups all lands under one owner |
| Khatoni | Cultivator number — groups all lands under one cultivator |
| Khasra | Plot/survey number |
| Girdawari | Crop inspection report (recorded twice yearly) |
| Mutation (Intiqal) | Transfer of ownership in revenue records |
| Murabba | A large block of land (25 acres standard) |
| Killa | Unit of land (approximately 1 acre) |
| Nakal | Certified copy of a revenue record |

## How to Check Land Records on Jamabandi — Step by Step

### Method 1: View Jamabandi Nakal (By Owner)

1. Visit the official portal at [jamabandi.nic.in](https://jamabandi.nic.in)
2. Click on **"Jamabandi Nakal"** from the home page
3. Select **"By Owner Name"** as search type
4. Select your **District** from the dropdown
5. Select your **Tehsil/Sub-Division**
6. Select the **Village**
7. Select the **Jamabandi Year** (latest available)
8. Enter the **Owner Name** (in Hindi or English)
9. Enter the **CAPTCHA** code
10. Click **"Submit"** to view records

### Method 2: View Jamabandi by Khewat/Khatoni

1. Go to [jamabandi.nic.in](https://jamabandi.nic.in)
2. Click on **"Jamabandi Nakal"**
3. Select **"By Khewat"** or **"By Khatoni"**
4. Select **District → Tehsil → Village → Jamabandi Year**
5. Enter the **Khewat Number** or **Khatoni Number**
6. Enter CAPTCHA and click **"Submit"**

The jamabandi nakal will display:
- Khewat and khatoni numbers
- Owner name(s) with father's name
- Khasra (plot) numbers
- Land area (in kanal, marla, or acres)
- Land classification
- Revenue assessment
- Mutation entries and remarks

### Method 3: Search by Khasra (Plot Number)

1. Visit [jamabandi.nic.in](https://jamabandi.nic.in)
2. Click on **"Jamabandi Nakal"**
3. Select **"By Khasra"**
4. Select **District → Tehsil → Village → Year**
5. Enter the **Murabba Number** and **Khasra Number**
6. Enter CAPTCHA and submit

### Method 4: Check Mutation Status

1. Go to [jamabandi.nic.in](https://jamabandi.nic.in)
2. Click on **"Check Mutation Status"**
3. Select your **District** and **Tehsil**
4. Enter the **Mutation Number** or search by date range
5. Click **"Search"**
6. View mutation status — pending, sanctioned, or rejected with dates

### Method 5: View Khasra Girdawari

1. Visit [jamabandi.nic.in](https://jamabandi.nic.in)
2. Navigate to **"Khasra Girdawari"**
3. Select **District → Tehsil → Village**
4. Select the **Year** and **Season** (Kharif/Rabi)
5. Enter **Murabba** and **Khasra Number**
6. View crop details, land condition, and cultivation status

## Important Tips

1. **Check the latest jamabandi year** — Always view the most recent year's jamabandi for current ownership details
2. **Know your khewat number** — It provides the most accurate search results; available on your previous jamabandi or revenue receipt
3. **Verify mutation after purchase** — After buying land, ensure mutation is completed within 6 months to update ownership in revenue records
4. **Girdawari matters for farmers** — Banks and government schemes check girdawari for crop loan eligibility and scheme benefits
5. **Use e-Disha for certified copies** — Haryana's e-Disha centres provide faster service than regular Tehsil offices

## FAQs

### 1. Is jamabandi.nic.in a government website?
Yes, jamabandi.nic.in is the official land records portal of the Government of Haryana, hosted by NIC (National Informatics Centre) under the Revenue Department.

### 2. What is jamabandi nakal?
Jamabandi nakal is a copy of the Record of Rights document that shows land ownership details, area, revenue assessment, and mutation history for a specific village in Haryana.

### 3. Can I use online jamabandi for court cases?
Online jamabandi is for informational purposes. For court cases or legal matters, you need a certified nakal (copy) from the Tehsildar office with an official seal and signature.

### 4. How do I apply for mutation in Haryana?
After a property transaction (sale, inheritance, gift), submit a mutation application at the Tehsil office with the registered deed, identity proof, and application form. You can also apply through the HARIS (Haryana Registration Information System) portal.

### 5. What is the difference between khewat and khatoni?
Khewat is the owner number — it groups all land owned by one person. Khatoni is the cultivator number — it groups land cultivated by one person. The owner and cultivator can be the same or different persons.

### 6. Can I search Haryana land records by owner name in Hindi?
Yes, the Jamabandi portal supports searching by owner name in both Hindi and English. However, records are typically maintained in Hindi, so searching in Hindi may give better results.

### 7. Are all Haryana districts available on jamabandi.nic.in?
Yes, the portal covers all 22 districts of Haryana. Select your district from the dropdown to begin searching.
