# EPFO Unified Portal: Complete Guide to All PF Services

The **EPFO Unified Portal** ([unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in)) is the official online platform by the Employees' Provident Fund Organisation for all PF-related services. Members can view their passbook, file claims, update KYC, transfer PF, and manage nominations — all from one place.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent informational resource and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government agency.

---

## What is the EPFO Unified Portal?

The EPFO Unified Portal is the member interface of EPFO's digital ecosystem. Launched to eliminate paperwork and physical visits to EPFO offices, it allows EPF members to:

- View and download **PF passbook**
- File **online claims** (withdrawal, advance, pension)
- Update **KYC details** (Aadhaar, PAN, bank account)
- Request **PF transfer** between old and new accounts
- File **e-Nomination** for EPF and EPS
- Track claim status

---

## Who Can Use the EPFO Unified Portal?

- **Salaried employees** covered under the EPF & MP Act, 1952
- Employees with an active **UAN (Universal Account Number)**
- Establishments with 20+ employees (mandatory) or voluntarily registered smaller establishments
- Both **active and retired** EPF members

## How to Log In to EPFO Unified Portal

1. **Visit** [unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in)
2. **Enter** your UAN number
3. **Enter** your password
4. **Fill** the captcha
5. **Click** "Sign In"

> If you haven't activated your UAN yet, see our [UAN Activation Guide](/guide/uan-activate-online).

## How to View and Download PF Passbook

1. **Log in** to the Unified Portal
2. **Click** on "View" → "Passbook" from the top menu
3. Alternatively, visit [passbook.epfindia.gov.in](https://passbook.epfindia.gov.in) directly
4. **Enter** your UAN and password
5. **Select** the Member ID (if you have multiple)
6. **View** month-wise contributions from employee and employer
7. **Click** "Download Passbook" to save as PDF

### Passbook Details Include:
- Monthly employee contribution (EPF)
- Monthly employer contribution (EPF + EPS)
- Interest credited
- Running balance
- Withdrawal/advance entries

## How to File Online PF Claims

### Types of Claims Available

| Claim Type | Purpose | Form |
|-----------|---------|------|
| PF Final Settlement | Full withdrawal after leaving job | Form 19 |
| Pension Withdrawal | EPS withdrawal (less than 10 years service) | Form 10C |
| Monthly Pension | Pension for 10+ years of service | Form 10D |
| PF Advance/Loan | Partial withdrawal for specific purposes | Form 31 |

### Steps to File a Claim

1. **Log in** to the Unified Portal
2. **Go to** "Online Services" → "Claim (Form-31, 19, 10C & 10D)"
3. **Verify** your bank account (last 4 digits)
4. **Click** "Proceed for Online Claim"
5. **Select** the claim type:
   - "PF Advance (Form 31)" for partial withdrawal
   - "Only PF Withdrawal (Form 19)" for full settlement
   - "Only Pension Withdrawal (Form 10C)" for EPS withdrawal
6. **Choose** the purpose (housing, medical, education, etc. for Form 31)
7. **Enter** the amount
8. **Upload** supporting documents if required
9. **Submit** with Aadhaar OTP verification
10. **Track** status under "Track Claim Status"

### PF Advance Purposes (Form 31)

- Medical treatment
- Marriage (self/children/siblings)
- Education (children)
- Home purchase/construction
- Home loan repayment
- Renovation/repair of house
- Natural calamity
- Disability/retirement (age 54+)

## How to Update KYC on EPFO Portal

KYC seeding is essential for filing online claims without employer approval.

1. **Log in** to the Unified Portal
2. **Go to** "Manage" → "KYC"
3. **Select** the document type:
   - **Aadhaar** (mandatory for online claims)
   - **PAN**
   - **Bank Account** (with IFSC)
   - **Passport** (optional)
4. **Enter** the document number and name as per document
5. **Click** "Save"
6. **Wait** for employer approval (status changes from "Pending" to "Verified")
7. Once verified by employer, EPFO digitally validates against UIDAI/Income Tax databases

> **Important:** Your name and date of birth on Aadhaar must match your EPFO records. If there's a mismatch, get it corrected first.

## How to Transfer PF Online

When changing jobs, transfer your old PF balance to the new account:

1. **Log in** to the Unified Portal
2. **Go to** "Online Services" → "One Member – One EPF Account (Transfer Request)"
3. **Verify** your personal details
4. **Select** the previous employer's Member ID
5. **Choose** which employer should attest (previous or current)
6. **Click** "Get OTP" on Aadhaar-linked mobile
7. **Submit** the transfer request
8. **Track** status under "Track Claim Status"

### Transfer Timeline
- **Online with Aadhaar:** 10-20 days
- **Without Aadhaar (manual):** 30-45 days

---

## How to File e-Nomination

e-Nomination lets you designate who receives your EPF and EPS benefits:

1. **Log in** to the Unified Portal
2. **Go to** "Manage" → "e-Nomination"
3. **Click** "Provide Details" under Family Declaration
4. **Add family members** — name, relation, date of birth, Aadhaar (optional)
5. **Specify** the share percentage for each nominee
6. **Upload** nominee's photo (optional)
7. **Save** and verify with Aadhaar OTP
8. **Download** the e-Nomination certificate

## Important Tips

1. **Seed your Aadhaar** with UAN — This enables direct online claims without employer attestation
2. **Keep your mobile number updated** — OTPs for claims and login are sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile
3. **Check passbook regularly** — Ensure your employer is depositing contributions every month
4. **File e-Nomination** — It simplifies the process for your family in case of an unfortunate event
5. **Don't withdraw PF** on every job change — Transfer instead to maintain your pension eligibility (10 years required for monthly pension)

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. What is UAN and how is it different from PF number?
UAN (Universal Account Number) is a permanent number that remains the same throughout your career. PF number (Member ID) changes with each employer. Multiple PF numbers are linked under one UAN.

### 2. Can I withdraw PF while still employed?
You can file for PF advance (Form 31) for specific purposes like medical emergency, housing, or education while still employed. Full withdrawal is only possible after leaving employment.

### 3. How do I check my PF balance without logging in?
You can check PF balance by: (a) Sending SMS "EPFOHO UAN" to 7738299899, (b) Giving a missed call to 011-22901406, or (c) Using the UMANG app.

### 4. Why is my KYC showing "Pending" for a long time?
KYC approval requires your employer to verify and approve it first. Contact your HR/employer to approve the pending KYC request on their employer portal.

### 5. Can I file PF claim online without Aadhaar?
No, Aadhaar seeding with UAN is mandatory for filing online claims. Without Aadhaar, you need to submit physical forms through your employer.

### 6. How much PF can I withdraw as advance?
The amount depends on the purpose. For example, for housing you can withdraw up to 36 months' wages or total employee+employer share (whichever is less). For medical emergencies, up to 6 months' basic wages.

### 7. What is the interest rate on EPF?
The EPF interest rate is declared annually by the government. For FY 2024-25, the rate is 8.25%. Interest is calculated monthly but credited at year-end.

### 8. How do I correct my name or date of birth in EPFO records?
Log in to the Unified Portal, go to "Manage" → "Modify Basic Details", enter the correct details, upload supporting documents (Aadhaar/PAN), and submit. Your employer and EPFO field office will approve the correction.
