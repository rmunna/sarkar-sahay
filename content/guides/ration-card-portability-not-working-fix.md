# Ration Card Portability Not Working: How to Fix

Under the One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC) scheme, ration card holders can buy subsidized food grains from any Fair Price Shop (FPS) across India. However, many beneficiaries face issues when trying to use portability — biometric failures, Aadhaar authentication errors, and shops refusing to serve migrants. This guide explains all common portability issues and how to fix them.

## Common Error Messages and Symptoms

- **"Aadhaar authentication failed"** — biometric or OTP verification fails at the FPS ePoS machine
- **"Beneficiary not found"** — your details not appearing in the portability system
- **"Entitlement exhausted"** — system shows you already collected your quota
- **"ePoS device error"** — the point-of-sale machine at the FPS is malfunctioning
- **"IMPDS transaction failed"** — Integrated Management of Public Distribution System error
- FPS dealer says portability is not available at their shop
- Ration is deducted from your quota but grains were not given
- Cannot buy ration in another state despite having a valid ration card
- Family members' names are not showing on the portability system
- Fingerprint does not match at the ePoS machine

## Why Ration Card Portability May Not Work

### 1. Aadhaar Not Seeded with Ration Card

ONORC requires Aadhaar to be linked (seeded) with your ration card. If Aadhaar is not linked, the ePoS system cannot authenticate you.

### 2. Biometric Mismatch

Worn fingerprints (common in manual labourers), injuries, or age-related changes can cause biometric authentication failure at the ePoS machine.

### 3. ePoS Machine Issues

The electronic Point of Sale device at the FPS may be malfunctioning, have poor internet connectivity, or have outdated software.

### 4. State System Not Updated

While ONORC is implemented across all states/UTs, some states' databases may not be fully synchronized with the central IMPDS system.

### 5. Entitlement Already Drawn

If someone has already collected your ration (possibly fraud or an error), the system shows your quota as exhausted.

### 6. FPS Dealer Refusal

Some FPS dealers are unaware of or unwilling to implement portability, especially for migrants from other states.

## Step-by-Step Troubleshooting

### Step 1: Check Aadhaar Seeding Status

1. Visit your state's food department portal (search: "[your state] ration card portal")
2. Look for "Know Your Ration Card" or "Aadhaar Seeding Status"
3. Enter your ration card number
4. Check if all family members' Aadhaar numbers are linked
5. If not linked, visit your local FPS or food department office with Aadhaar cards of all family members

### Step 2: Verify Portability Status Online

1. Visit [impds.nic.in](https://impds.nic.in/) — the national portability portal
2. Or visit [annavitran.nic.in](https://annavitran.nic.in/)
3. Search for your ration card details
4. Verify that your card is active and portability-enabled
5. Check your monthly entitlement and how much has been drawn

### Step 3: Fix Biometric Authentication Issues

If your fingerprint does not work at the ePoS:
1. Clean and dry your fingers before attempting
2. Try different fingers — the ePoS usually accepts any finger
3. Request the FPS dealer to try **iris scan** if the ePoS device supports it
4. Ask for **OTP-based authentication** as an alternative to biometric
5. If your fingerprints are permanently damaged, [update your biometrics at an Aadhaar centre](/guide/aadhaar-biometric-verification-failed-fix)

### Step 4: Handle ePoS Machine Errors

1. Ask the FPS dealer to restart the ePoS device
2. Check if the device has internet connectivity — portability requires an online connection
3. If the device is malfunctioning, report it to the food department helpline
4. Visit a different FPS that has a working ePoS machine
5. Come back at a different time when the network may be better

### Step 5: Fix "Entitlement Exhausted" Error

1. Check your transaction history at [impds.nic.in](https://impds.nic.in/) or your state portal
2. If ration was drawn without your knowledge, this may be fraud — report it immediately
3. Contact your **District Supply Officer** or state food helpline
4. File a complaint on the NFSA portal at [nfsa.gov.in](https://nfsa.gov.in/)
5. For the current month, you may not be able to draw again; the issue can be resolved for next month

### Step 6: Deal with FPS Dealer Refusal

If the dealer refuses to provide portability service:
1. Remind them that ONORC is mandatory at all FPS with ePoS devices
2. Note the FPS licence number and dealer name
3. Call the state food helpline and report the refusal
4. File a complaint at [nfsa.gov.in → Grievance](https://nfsa.gov.in/)
5. Contact your local MLA or district administration if the issue persists

### Step 7: Update Your Ration Card Details

If family members' names are not showing:
1. Visit your home state's food department office
2. Apply for ration card correction/addition
3. Submit Aadhaar cards and proof of relationship
4. The update typically takes 15-30 days
5. Once updated, all members can use portability

## Alternative Methods and Workarounds

### Apply for Ration Card in New State

If you have permanently migrated:
1. Surrender your ration card in the old state
2. Apply for a new ration card in the new state
3. This gives you full access to the new state's PDS without portability issues
4. Carry your old ration card surrender receipt and Aadhaar

### Use Mera Ration App

1. Download **Mera Ration** app from Google Play
2. Register with your ration card details
3. Find nearby FPS that support portability
4. Check your entitlement and transaction history
5. The app shows FPS that have functional ePoS machines

### Visit a Different FPS

1. ONORC allows you to buy from any FPS, not just the one assigned to you
2. If one FPS has issues, try another nearby
3. Check the Mera Ration app or state portal for FPS locations
4. Government-run FPS tend to be more compliant with portability rules

### File Online Grievance

1. Visit [nfsa.gov.in](https://nfsa.gov.in/) → Grievance section
2. Select your state and district
3. Describe the portability issue
4. Upload any evidence (photos of refusal, transaction receipts)
5. Note the grievance number for tracking

## Prevention Tips

1. **Link Aadhaar to ration card** — ensure all family members' Aadhaar numbers are seeded before you migrate
2. **Download Mera Ration app** — use it to find portability-enabled FPS and track transactions
3. **Carry both cards** — always carry your ration card and Aadhaar card when going to the FPS
4. **Check entitlement monthly** — monitor your account to detect any unauthorized withdrawals early
5. **Update biometrics** — if your fingerprints are worn, update your Aadhaar biometrics before migrating
6. **Know your rights** — under ONORC, any FPS with an ePoS device must serve you regardless of your home state

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC)?

ONORC is a government scheme that allows ration card holders to buy their entitled subsidized food grains from any Fair Price Shop (FPS) across India, not just the one assigned in their home state. It is designed to help migrant workers and families who move between states for work.

### Why does biometric authentication fail at the FPS?

Biometric failure at FPS ePoS machines is common due to worn fingerprints (especially for manual labourers), dirty or wet fingers, poor-quality ePoS devices, and network connectivity issues. Try cleaning your fingers, using different fingers, or request iris/OTP-based authentication.

### Can I get ration in another state without portability?

Without ONORC portability, you can only get ration from the FPS assigned to your ration card in your home state/district. To get ration in another state, you need to either use ONORC portability (with Aadhaar-linked ration card) or surrender your old ration card and apply for a new one in the new state.

### What should I do if someone else drew my ration?

Check your transaction history on the IMPDS portal or Mera Ration app. If ration was withdrawn without your knowledge, file a complaint at the NFSA portal (nfsa.gov.in), contact the national food helpline at 1967, and inform your District Supply Officer. This may be a case of fraud that needs investigation.

### How long does it take to link Aadhaar with ration card?

Aadhaar seeding with ration card is usually completed within 7-15 days after submitting the request at your local food department office or FPS. Some states offer online linking through their food department portals. Once linked, portability becomes active from the next month's distribution cycle.
