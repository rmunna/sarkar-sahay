# UP SI (Sub-Inspector) Exam Guide — Eligibility, Pattern & Preparation Tips

The Uttar Pradesh Police Bharti and Protsahan Board (UPPBPB) conducts the recruitment exam for Sub-Inspector (Civil Police) and other equivalent posts in UP Police. The UP SI exam is one of the most competitive state-level police exams in India, attracting millions of applicants.

A career as UP SI offers a commanding position in law enforcement with a Grade Pay of ₹4,200 and opportunities for promotion to Inspector and beyond.

## Eligibility Criteria

### Age Limit

| Category | Minimum Age | Maximum Age |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| General | 21 years | 28 years |
| OBC (UP) | 21 years | 31 years |
| SC/ST (UP) | 21 years | 33 years |

*Age relaxation as per UP Government norms.*

### Educational Qualification

- **Minimum:** Bachelor's degree from a recognized university
- Any discipline accepted

### Nationality & Domicile

- Must be a citizen of India
- Domicile of Uttar Pradesh preferred for certain reserved categories

### Physical Standards

**For Male Candidates:**

| Parameter | General/OBC/SC | ST |
|-----------|---------------|-----|
| Height | 168 cm | 160 cm |
| Chest (unexpanded) | 79 cm | 77 cm |
| Chest (expanded) | 84 cm | 82 cm |

**For Female Candidates:**

| Parameter | General/OBC | SC/ST |
|-----------|-------------|-------|
| Height | 152 cm | 147 cm |

## Exam Pattern

### Stage 1: Written Examination (Online)

| Subject | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| General Hindi | 40 | 100 | 2 hours |
| Basic Law / Constitution | 40 | 100 | 2 hours |
| Numerical & Mental Ability | 40 | 100 | 2 hours |
| Mental Aptitude / IQ / Reasoning | 40 | 100 | 2 hours |

- **Total:** 160 questions, 400 marks
- **Negative marking:** 0.25 marks deducted per wrong answer
- Exam conducted in Hindi and English

### Stage 2: Physical Efficiency Test (PET)

**For Male Candidates:**
- 4.8 km running in 28 minutes

**For Female Candidates:**
- 2.4 km running in 16 minutes

### Stage 3: Document Verification

Verification of educational certificates, caste certificates, domicile, and other relevant documents.

## Syllabus Overview

### General Hindi
- Hindi grammar, Vilom, Paryayvachi, Muhavare, Lokoktiyan, One-word substitution, Sandhi, Samas, Ras, Alankar

### Basic Law / Constitution
- Indian Constitution, Indian Penal Code (IPC), Criminal Procedure Code (CrPC), Women & Children related laws, SC/ST Prevention of Atrocities Act

### Numerical & Mental Ability
- Number systems, Simplification, Percentage, Profit & Loss, SI/CI, Ratio & Proportion, Time & Work, Data Interpretation

### Mental Aptitude / IQ / Reasoning
- Logical reasoning, Blood relations, Coding-decoding, Series completion, Directions, Venn diagrams, Analogies

## How to Apply

1. Visit the official UPPBPB website at [uppbpb.gov.in](https://uppbpb.gov.in)
2. Click on the active recruitment notification for SI
3. Register with a valid email ID and mobile number
4. Fill in personal, educational, and address details
5. Upload scanned photograph (with name & date) and signature
6. Pay the application fee online
7. Submit the form and take a printout

## Salary & Benefits

- **Pay Scale:** ₹35,400 – ₹1,12,400 (7th CPC)
- **Grade Pay:** ₹4,200
- DA, HRA, and other allowances as per UP Government rules
- Medical benefits, pension (NPS), and housing facility

## Preparation Tips

1. **Master the basics:** Focus on Hindi grammar and Constitutional provisions — these are high-scoring
2. **Practice math daily:** Speed and accuracy matter in Numerical Ability
3. **Read legal sections:** Focus on frequently asked IPC and CrPC sections
4. **Mock tests:** Take at least 2 full-length mock tests per week
5. **Stay fit:** Start physical training early — PET eliminates many candidates

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is graduation compulsory for UP SI?
Yes, a Bachelor's degree from a recognized university is mandatory to apply for UP SI.

### How many attempts are allowed for UP SI exam?
There is no limit on the number of attempts, as long as you meet the age criteria.

### Is there negative marking in UP SI exam?
Yes, 0.25 marks are deducted for each wrong answer.

### Can candidates from other states apply for UP SI?
Yes, candidates from all states can apply, but certain reserved category benefits are available only to UP domicile holders.

### What is the physical test for female candidates?
Female candidates must complete a 2.4 km run within 16 minutes.

### When is the UP SI exam usually conducted?
The exam is conducted as per vacancy cycle — typically every 2-3 years. Check the official website for the latest notification.
