# Mukhyamantri Protsahan Yojana Jharkhand: ₹5,000 for Unemployed Graduates

Mukhyamantri Protsahan Yojana is a scheme by the Jharkhand government that provides a **one-time financial assistance of ₹5,000** to unemployed graduates and post-graduates registered on the state employment exchange. The scheme aims to provide temporary financial relief to educated youth who are actively seeking employment.

## What is Mukhyamantri Protsahan Yojana?

This scheme was launched to support educated but unemployed youth in Jharkhand. Graduates and post-graduates who have registered on the state employment exchange and are actively looking for jobs receive a one-time payment of ₹5,000 as encouragement (protsahan) while they continue their job search.

### Key Details

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Benefit Amount | ₹5,000 (one-time) |
| Target Group | Unemployed graduates/post-graduates |
| Managed By | Department of Labour, Employment, Training & Skill Development |
| Payment Mode | DBT to bank account |

## Who is Eligible?

- Must be a **permanent resident of Jharkhand**
- Must have completed **graduation or post-graduation** from a recognised university
- Must be **registered on the Jharkhand Employment Exchange** (Rojgar portal)
- Must be **unemployed** — not employed in any government or private organisation
- Must not be receiving any other **employment or unemployment benefit** from the government
- Must have an **Aadhaar-linked bank account**
- Age: Typically **18-35 years** (check latest notification)

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card**
- **Graduation/Post-graduation degree or marksheet**
- **Employment Exchange registration certificate** (from rojgar.jharkhand.gov.in)
- **Bank account passbook** linked with Aadhaar
- **Passport-size photograph**
- **Residential certificate** of Jharkhand
- **Self-declaration** of being unemployed
- **Mobile number** linked with Aadhaar

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Online Application

1. Visit the Jharkhand Employment portal at **[rojgar.jharkhand.gov.in](https://rojgar.jharkhand.gov.in/)**
2. **Register on the employment exchange** if not already registered
3. Complete your profile with educational qualifications and employment status
4. Look for the **"Mukhyamantri Protsahan Yojana"** section
5. Click on **"Apply"** or **"Claim Protsahan Allowance"**
6. Verify your Aadhaar via OTP
7. Fill in required details and upload documents
8. Enter bank account details
9. Submit the application
10. Note the **Application Reference Number**

### After Submission

1. Application is verified by the **District Employment Officer**
2. Employment status is cross-checked
3. Approved applications are processed for payment
4. **₹5,000 is credited** to the bank account via DBT

## Fees

There are **no fees** for registration or application. The entire process is free.

## Processing Time

- Application verification: **2-4 weeks**
- Payment processing: **1-2 months** after approval
- Total: **1-3 months** from application to payment

## Important Tips

1. **Register on employment exchange first** — this is a prerequisite for applying
2. **Keep your employment exchange registration active** — renew it periodically as required
3. **This is a one-time benefit** — you receive ₹5,000 only once, not monthly
4. **Use the time for skill development** — consider enrolling in government skill training programmes
5. **Keep applying for jobs** — this is temporary support, not a permanent income source

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Is this a monthly allowance?
No, Mukhyamantri Protsahan Yojana provides a **one-time payment of ₹5,000** only. It is not a recurring monthly allowance.

### Q2: Can diploma holders apply?
The scheme is primarily for **graduates and post-graduates**. Diploma holders should check the latest notification for eligibility.

### Q3: Do I need to be registered on the employment exchange?
Yes, **registration on the Jharkhand Employment Exchange** (rojgar.jharkhand.gov.in) is mandatory.

### Q4: Can I apply if I'm self-employed?
If you are earning through self-employment, you may not qualify as "unemployed." The scheme is for those genuinely seeking employment.

### Q5: What if I get a job after applying but before receiving the money?
You should update your employment status. If found employed during verification, the application may be rejected.

### Q6: Can students in final year apply?
No, you must have **completed your graduation** and received the degree/marksheet to be eligible.

### Q7: Is there an age limit?
Check the latest notification on [rojgar.jharkhand.gov.in](https://rojgar.jharkhand.gov.in/). Generally, applicants should be within the working age group (18-35 years).
