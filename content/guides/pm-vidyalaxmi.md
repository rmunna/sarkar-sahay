# PM Vidyalaxmi Education Loan: Eligibility, Interest Rate, and How to Apply Online

PM Vidyalaxmi is a Central Government scheme launched in November 2024 to provide **collateral-free, guarantor-free education loans** to meritorious students pursuing higher education in India. The scheme ensures that financial constraints do not prevent talented youth from accessing quality higher education at top-ranked institutions.

## What is PM Vidyalaxmi Scheme?

Pradhan Mantri Vidyalaxmi (PM-Vidyalaxmi) is a centrally sponsored scheme under the Ministry of Education. It provides education loans of up to ₹10 lakh without any collateral or guarantor requirement. Students admitted to Quality Higher Education Institutions (QHEIs) — institutions ranked in top NIRF categories or accredited by NAAC with a minimum grade — can avail loans through participating banks via the PM Vidyalaxmi portal.

The scheme also provides **interest subvention (subsidy)** of 3% on education loans up to ₹10 lakh for students from families with annual income up to ₹8 lakh, during the moratorium period.

---

## Who is Eligible for PM Vidyalaxmi?

To avail the PM Vidyalaxmi education loan, you must meet the following criteria:

- **Indian citizen** — only Indian nationals are eligible
- **Admitted to a QHEI** — the institution must be in the top 100 NIRF-ranked institutions, or NAAC A++ / A+ accredited, or a centrally funded technical institution
- **First-time loan** — for pursuing the first higher education degree (graduation, post-graduation, diploma, or professional courses)
- **Family income** — for interest subsidy, family annual income must be up to ₹8 lakh; for loan (without subsidy), no income limit
- **Age** — no specific age limit, but must be a bonafide student admitted for the current academic year
- **Not an income tax payer** — the student's family should not be income tax payees (for interest subsidy benefit)

### Who Cannot Apply?

- Students already availing education loans under other government subsidy schemes
- Students admitted to institutions not listed as QHEIs
- Students who have already completed the same level of education

## Documents Required

### Mandatory Documents
- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) of student and parent/guardian
- Admission letter / bonafide certificate from the institution
- Fee structure from the institution
- 10th and 12th mark sheets
- Income certificate of the family (from competent authority)
- Bank account details (savings account with Aadhaar linked)

### Additional Documents
- Caste certificate (if applicable, for SC/ST/OBC students)
- Domicile certificate
- Passport-size photographs
- Previous education certificates and transcripts
- [PAN card](/guide/pan-card-apply-online) of student and co-applicant

## Step-by-Step Process to Apply Online

### Online Application (via PM Vidyalaxmi Portal)

1. **Visit the portal** — Go to [pmvidyalaxmi.co.in](https://pmvidyalaxmi.co.in/)
2. **Register** — Click on "Student Registration" and create an account using your Aadhaar number, mobile number, and email
3. **Verify OTP** — Enter the OTP sent to your registered mobile number
4. **Login** — Use your credentials to log into the portal
5. **Fill application form** — Enter personal details, educational details, institution name, course details, and fee structure
6. **Upload documents** — Upload scanned copies of all required documents
7. **Select banks** — Choose up to 3 banks from the list of participating banks where you want to apply for the loan
8. **Submit application** — Review all details and submit the application
9. **Track status** — Use the dashboard to track your loan application status with each bank
10. **Bank processing** — Selected banks will process your application and contact you for further verification

### Offline Process

There is no separate offline application process. All applications must be submitted through the PM Vidyalaxmi portal. However, you may visit the bank branch for document verification after submitting online.

| Repayment period | Up to 15 years after moratorium |

## Processing Time

- **Application submission to bank allocation:** 7-10 days
- **Bank verification and sanction:** 15-30 days
- **Total time from application to disbursement:** 30-45 days (approximately)

## Important Tips

1. **Check QHEI list** — Before applying, verify that your institution is listed as a Quality Higher Education Institution on the PM Vidyalaxmi portal
2. **Apply early** — Submit your application as soon as you receive the admission letter; do not wait until the last date for fee payment
3. **Keep documents ready** — Ensure all documents are clear, scanned copies are legible, and income certificate is recent
4. **Link Aadhaar to bank** — Your [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) must be linked to your bank account for direct benefit transfer
5. **Compare with other schemes** — If eligible, also check Vidyalakshmi portal (vidyalakshmi.co.in) for other education loan options from multiple banks

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. What is the maximum loan amount under PM Vidyalaxmi?
The scheme provides collateral-free loans up to ₹10 lakh. For loans above ₹10 lakh, banks may require collateral as per their existing norms.

### Q2. Is PM Vidyalaxmi only for government college students?
No, it covers students admitted to any institution ranked in top NIRF categories, NAAC A++/A+ accredited institutions, and centrally funded technical institutions — both government and private.

### Q3. Can I apply to multiple banks through PM Vidyalaxmi?
Yes, you can select up to 3 banks while applying through the portal. Each bank will independently evaluate your application.

### Q4. What is the interest rate under PM Vidyalaxmi?
The interest rate is as per the bank's education loan norms. However, the government provides a 3% interest subsidy during the moratorium period for students from families with annual income up to ₹8 lakh.

### Q5. Is PM Vidyalaxmi available for studying abroad?
Currently, the scheme is primarily for students admitted to Quality Higher Education Institutions within India. For study abroad, check other education loan schemes.

### Q6. Can postgraduate students apply?
Yes, students pursuing postgraduate courses (MA, MSc, MTech, MBA, etc.) at eligible institutions can apply under PM Vidyalaxmi.

### Q7. What happens if my loan application is rejected by all banks?
If all selected banks reject your application, you can re-apply after addressing the reasons for rejection. Contact the bank or use the grievance mechanism on the portal.
