# Link Aadhaar with Bank Account Online — All Banks (2026)

Linking Aadhaar with your bank account is mandatory for receiving government subsidies, DBT payments, and LPG benefits. It's also required under income tax rules for certain transactions. This guide covers all methods to link Aadhaar with your bank account — online, via SMS, ATM, and at the branch — for all major banks.

## Why Link Aadhaar with Bank Account?

- **Mandatory for DBT:** Government subsidies (LPG, PM Kisan, scholarships) require Aadhaar-linked bank accounts
- **Income Tax compliance:** PAN-Aadhaar linking is mandatory, and Aadhaar-linked bank accounts ensure smooth refunds
- **KYC requirement:** Banks require Aadhaar as part of KYC compliance
- **NPCI mapping:** Aadhaar-linked accounts enable Aadhaar-based payments through NPCI

## Method 1: Link via Net Banking (Online)

### SBI (State Bank of India)

1. Log in to [https://www.onlinesbi.sbi](https://www.onlinesbi.sbi)
2. Go to **"My Accounts"** → **"Update Aadhaar Number"**
3. Select your account from the dropdown
4. Enter your **12-digit Aadhaar number**
5. Confirm and submit
6. Aadhaar will be linked within 24–48 hours after verification

### PNB (Punjab National Bank)

1. Log in to PNB net banking at [https://www.pnbindia.in](https://www.pnbindia.in)
2. Go to **"Services"** → **"Aadhaar Seeding"**
3. Select your account number
4. Enter Aadhaar number and confirm
5. Submit the request

### HDFC Bank

1. Log in to HDFC Net Banking at [https://netbanking.hdfcbank.com](https://netbanking.hdfcbank.com)
2. Go to **"Update Aadhaar"** under **Accounts** section
3. Enter your Aadhaar number
4. Verify via OTP
5. Submit

### Bank of Baroda

1. Log in to BOB net banking
2. Navigate to **"Services"** → **"Aadhaar Linking"**
3. Enter Aadhaar number and confirm
4. Submit

### ICICI Bank

1. Log in to ICICI net banking
2. Go to **"Service Requests"** → **"Link Aadhaar"**
3. Enter Aadhaar number, verify via OTP
4. Submit

## Method 2: Link via SMS

Some banks support Aadhaar linking via SMS from your registered mobile:

| SBI | UID <space> Aadhaar No <space> Account No | 567676 |
| PNB | LINK <space> Aadhaar No <space> Account No | 9264092640 |

| Canara Bank | LINK <space> Aadhaar No <space> Account No | 9015483483 |

> **Important:** Send SMS only from your registered mobile number. SMS charges may apply.

## Method 3: Link via ATM

1. Visit your bank's ATM
2. Insert debit card and enter PIN
3. Select **"Services"** or **"Aadhaar Registration"**
4. Select **"Link Aadhaar"**
5. Enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number
6. Confirm and submit
7. You'll receive confirmation SMS

> Not all bank ATMs support this feature. SBI and PNB ATMs generally do.

## Method 4: Link via Mobile Banking App

### SBI YONO:
1. Open YONO SBI app → Login
2. Go to **"Service Requests"** → **"Aadhaar Linking"**
3. Enter Aadhaar number → Submit

### PNB ONE:
1. Open PNB ONE app
2. Go to **"Services"** → **"Aadhaar Seeding"**
3. Enter Aadhaar and submit

### HDFC Mobile Banking:
1. Open HDFC Mobile Banking app
2. Navigate to **"Requests"** → **"Link Aadhaar"**
3. Enter Aadhaar number → Verify → Submit

## Method 5: Visit Bank Branch (Offline)

This is the most reliable method, especially if online methods don't work:

1. Visit your bank branch with:
   - Original Aadhaar card
   - Photocopy of Aadhaar
   - Bank passbook
2. Fill the **Aadhaar seeding form** (available at the counter)
3. Submit the form with Aadhaar photocopy
4. Staff will update your account within 2–5 working days
5. You'll receive confirmation SMS

## How to Check if Aadhaar is Linked to Bank Account

### Method 1: NPCI Mapper (UIDAI)
1. Visit [https://resident.uidai.gov.in](https://resident.uidai.gov.in)
2. Go to **"Aadhaar Services"** → **"Check Aadhaar & Bank Account Linking Status"**
3. Enter Aadhaar number and security code
4. Request OTP on registered mobile
5. The linked bank name will be displayed

### Method 2: Contact Bank
- Call your bank's customer care
- Check via net banking under account details
- Visit branch and ask

## Common Issues and Fixes

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| "Aadhaar number invalid" | Check for typos. Ensure the same Aadhaar is used (not VID). |
| Name mismatch | Your bank name and Aadhaar name must match. Update whichever is incorrect. |
| "Already linked to another account" | One Aadhaar can be linked to one account per bank for DBT. Contact branch to change. |
| OTP not received | Check Aadhaar-linked mobile. Update mobile in Aadhaar if needed — see our [Aadhaar update guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online). |
| Linking pending for days | Visit branch. Online seeding sometimes requires manual verification. |
| DBT not received after linking | Check NPCI mapper to confirm which bank is mapped. It may take 7-10 days to reflect. |

## NPCI Aadhaar Mapper — Important for DBT

For receiving DBT payments, your Aadhaar must be mapped to a specific bank account via NPCI:

- If you have multiple bank accounts linked to Aadhaar, only **one** receives DBT
- The **last linked account** typically becomes the default for NPCI mapping
- To change the default, visit the desired bank and request **"Aadhaar mapper update"**

## Important Tips

1. **Ensure name match** between Aadhaar and bank records before linking
2. **One active mapping** — for DBT, only one account per Aadhaar receives payments through NPCI
3. **Keep confirmation SMS** as proof of successful linking
4. **Link before applying** for any government scheme to ensure smooth benefit transfer
5. **Update Aadhaar first** if your details have changed before linking

## FAQs

### 1. Is it mandatory to link Aadhaar with bank account?
Yes, it's mandatory for receiving government subsidies and DBT payments. RBI and government directives require Aadhaar linking for KYC compliance.

### 2. Can I link Aadhaar to multiple bank accounts?
Yes, you can link Aadhaar to accounts in different banks. However, for DBT/NPCI mapping, only one account will be the default recipient.

### 3. How long does Aadhaar-bank linking take?
Online methods: 24–48 hours. Branch visit: 2–5 working days. SMS/ATM: 24–72 hours.

### 4. What if my name in Aadhaar doesn't match my bank name?
You need to get the name corrected in either Aadhaar or your bank records before linking. Name mismatch will cause linking failure.

### 5. I linked Aadhaar but still not getting DBT. Why?
Check NPCI Aadhaar mapper to see which bank account is mapped. Your DBT-receiving account may be different from the one you just linked. Visit the correct bank to update the mapper.

### 6. Can I link VID (Virtual ID) instead of Aadhaar number?
Some banks accept VID for linking. Check with your bank. For NPCI mapping, the actual Aadhaar number is used in the backend.

### 7. Is there any charge for Aadhaar-bank linking?
No, linking Aadhaar with your bank account is completely free through all methods — online, SMS, ATM, or branch visit.
