# Senior Citizen Income Tax & ITR Filing Guide 2026

India's income tax laws have several special provisions for **senior citizens (60–79 years)** and **super senior citizens (80+ years)** — higher exemption limits, additional deductions, and even exemption from ITR filing in some cases.

## Who Is a Senior Citizen for Tax Purposes?

| Category | Age |
|----------|-----|
| **Senior Citizen** | 60 years or above (but below 80) during the financial year |
| **Super Senior Citizen** | 80 years or above during the financial year |

Age is determined by birthday during the **financial year** (April 1 – March 31). If you turn 60 on March 31, 2026, you qualify as senior citizen for FY 2025-26.

## Income Tax Slabs for Senior Citizens

### Old Tax Regime (Higher exemption for seniors)

| Income Range | Senior Citizen (60–79) | Super Senior (80+) |
|-------------|----------------------|-------------------|
| Up to ₹3,00,000 | **0%** | **0%** |
| Up to ₹5,00,000 | **0%** | **0%** |
| ₹3,00,001 – ₹5,00,000 | 5% | **0%** |
| ₹5,00,001 – ₹10,00,000 | 20% | 20% |
| Above ₹10,00,000 | 30% | 30% |

> Senior citizen basic exemption is ₹3L (vs ₹2.5L for non-seniors). Super seniors get ₹5L exemption.

### New Tax Regime (Same slabs as everyone else)

Under the new default regime, seniors and non-seniors have the same slabs. No age-based exemption enhancement.

| Income Range | Rate (New Regime) |
|-------------|-----------------|
| Up to ₹4,00,000 | 0% |
| ₹4,00,001 – ₹8,00,000 | 5% |
| ₹8,00,001 – ₹12,00,000 | 10% |
| Above ₹12,00,000 | 15%–30% |

The new regime may still work for seniors who have fewer deductions.

## Special Tax Benefits for Senior Citizens

### 1. Section 87A Rebate

Tax rebate of up to ₹25,000 for taxable income up to ₹7,00,000 (old regime) and up to ₹12,00,000 (new regime under Budget 2025).

### 2. Section 80TTB — ₹50,000 Deduction on Interest

Senior citizens can deduct up to **₹50,000** per year on interest income from:
- Bank savings accounts
- Fixed deposits (FD)
- Post office deposits
- Recurring deposits

**Non-senior citizens**: Only ₹10,000 deduction (Section 80TTA, only savings account interest, not FDs).

**Example**: Senior citizen earning ₹60,000 FD interest — deduction ₹50,000, taxable interest = ₹10,000.

### 3. Section 80D — Higher Health Insurance Deduction

| Who | Maximum Deduction |
|-----|-----------------|
| Self health insurance premium (60+) | ₹50,000 |
| Paying parents' premium (parents 60+) | ₹50,000 additional |

Senior citizens without health insurance: Can claim ₹50,000 deduction for medical expenses paid.

### 4. No Advance Tax for Senior Citizens (Section 207)

Senior citizens with **no business/professional income** are exempt from paying advance tax — no penalty under 234B/234C even if tax liability > ₹10,000.

**Exception**: If senior citizen has business income, advance tax is required.

Instead, pay all tax as **self-assessment tax** at ITR filing time.

### 5. No TDS on FD for Super Senior Citizens (80+) — Section 194P

Super senior citizens (80+) with only pension + FD interest income can have TDS handled by the designated bank (the "specified bank" where they receive pension):
- File a declaration with the bank
- Bank deducts TDS on pension + FD interest and directly pays to IT department
- No need to file ITR separately

## When Senior Citizens DON'T Need to File ITR

### Section 194P Exemption (75+ years, effective 2021)

Senior citizens **aged 75 or above** are NOT required to file ITR if:
1. Their income is ONLY from:
   - Pension (credited to same bank)
   - Interest from deposits in the SAME bank as pension
2. They submit a declaration to the **designated bank** (the pension bank)
3. The bank deducts the correct TDS and deposits it

> Note: "Specified bank" must be a scheduled bank specified by the government. Not all banks qualify.

**What still requires ITR for seniors**:
- Capital gains from selling property/stocks
- Rental income
- Business/professional income
- Income from multiple banks
- Claiming a refund (even for seniors, file ITR to get TDS refund)

## Which ITR Form for Senior Citizens?

| Income Type | ITR Form |
|-------------|---------|
| Pension + FD interest + savings interest only | **ITR-1** |
| Pension + capital gains from stocks/MF | **ITR-2** |
| Pension + rental income | **ITR-1** or **ITR-2** (if more than one property) |
| Business income | **ITR-3** or **ITR-4** |

## Common Senior Citizen Income — Tax Calculation Example

**Example**: Senior citizen, 68 years, old regime, FY 2025-26.
- Pension: ₹4,80,000/year
- FD interest: ₹80,000/year
- Standard deduction (salary/pension): ₹50,000
- Section 80TTB: ₹50,000 (on FD interest)
- Section 80D: ₹50,000 (health insurance premium for self, senior)

**Taxable income**:
- Pension: ₹4,80,000 − ₹50,000 (std deduction) = ₹4,30,000
- FD Interest: ₹80,000 − ₹50,000 (80TTB) = ₹30,000
- Less 80D: −₹50,000
- Total taxable: ₹4,30,000 + ₹30,000 − ₹50,000 = **₹4,10,000**
- Tax at senior citizen slab: ₹0 on ₹3L + 5% on ₹1.1L = **₹5,500** + 4% cess = ₹5,720
- Less 87A rebate: ₹5,720 (full rebate since income < ₹5L under old regime, tax < ₹12,500)
- **Final tax: ₹0**

## Filing ITR — How It Works for Seniors

1. Visit **[incometax.gov.in](https://incometax.gov.in/)** → File ITR → ITR-1
2. The pre-fill automatically loads pension + FD interest from Form 26AS/AIS
3. Check if the pre-filled amounts match your Form 16 (pension TDS) and bank statements
4. Add Section 80TTB deduction (₹50,000 limit on interest income)
5. Add 80D premium
6. Verify and submit

**Deadline**: July 31 of Assessment Year (same as non-seniors for self-filing).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My mother is 76 and gets only pension + FD interest from the same bank. Does she need to file ITR?

No — under Section 194P, the bank handles TDS, and she doesn't need to file ITR if all income is pension + interest from the same bank. She must submit Form 12BBA declaration to the bank for this exemption to apply.

### My father is 65 with ₹4 lakh pension. Is there any income tax?

Old regime: ₹4L pension − ₹50K standard deduction = ₹3.5L taxable. Senior citizen exemption is ₹3L. Taxable = ₹50,000 at 5% = ₹2,500. Section 87A rebate: tax < ₹12,500, so rebate = ₹2,500. **Final tax = ₹0.** New regime: Similar outcome with ₹4L below the ₹4L zero-tax threshold.

### Can a senior citizen use new tax regime?

Yes — seniors can file under the new regime. The advantage of old regime for seniors is the higher basic exemption (₹3L vs same ₹4L threshold in new regime) plus 80TTB/80D deductions. Calculate both to choose lower tax.

### TDS was deducted on my FD interest. How do I get a refund?

File ITR-1 claiming the 80TTB deduction (₹50,000 on FD interest). If your total tax after deductions is less than TDS deducted, the excess TDS is refunded to your bank account within 30-90 days.
