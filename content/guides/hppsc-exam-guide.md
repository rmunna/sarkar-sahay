# HPPSC Exam Guide 2025: Complete Information

The **Himachal Pradesh Public Service Commission (HPPSC)** conducts the **HP Administrative Services (HPAS)** Combined Competitive Examination for recruitment to various Group A and Group B posts in Himachal Pradesh government departments. It is the premier state-level civil services exam for HP.

## What is HPPSC?

HPPSC is a constitutional body established under Article 315 of the Indian Constitution. It recruits officers for the HP Administrative Service (HAS), HP Police Service (HPS), and other allied services through the combined competitive examination, commonly known as the HPAS exam.

## HPPSC Eligibility Criteria

### Age Limit
- **Minimum Age:** 21 years
- **Maximum Age:** 45 years (one of the highest among state PSCs)
- **Age Relaxation:**
  - OBC: 3 years
  - SC/ST: 5 years
  - PwBD: 10 years (additional to category relaxation)
  - Ex-Servicemen: as per HP government rules

### Educational Qualification
- **Graduate** from any recognized university
- Any discipline/stream accepted
- Final year students can also apply (must have degree before interview)

### Nationality
- Indian citizen
- Domicile of Himachal Pradesh required for most posts

### Number of Attempts
- General: 6 attempts
- OBC: 9 attempts
- SC/ST/PwBD: Unlimited (within age limit)

## HPPSC Exam Pattern

The HPAS exam is conducted in three stages:

### Preliminary Examination
| Paper | Subject | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|-------|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| Paper 1 | General Studies | 100 | 200 | 2 hours |
| Paper 2 | Aptitude (CSAT) | 80 | 200 | 2 hours |

- Paper 2 is qualifying (33% minimum)
- Only Paper 1 marks count for mains cutoff
- Negative marking: 1/3rd of marks for wrong answers

### Main Examination
| Paper | Subject | Marks | Duration |
|-------|---------|-------|----------|
| Paper 1 | General Studies I | 200 | 3 hours |
| Paper 2 | General Studies II | 200 | 3 hours |
| Paper 3 | General Studies III | 200 | 3 hours |
| Paper 4 | Optional Subject I | 200 | 3 hours |
| Paper 5 | Optional Subject II | 200 | 3 hours |
| Paper 6 | Hindi | 100 | 2 hours |
| Paper 7 | English | 100 | 2 hours |
| **Total** | | **1200** | |

Hindi and English papers are qualifying (minimum marks required).

### Interview / Personality Test
- **Marks:** 150
- **Total (Mains + Interview):** 1150 (1000 mains counted + 150 interview)

## HPPSC Syllabus

### Prelims — General Studies
- Current affairs (national, international, HP-specific)
- History of India and Himachal Pradesh
- Geography of India and HP (physical, economic, social)
- Indian polity and governance
- HP polity and administrative structure
- Economy — Indian and HP
- Environment, ecology, biodiversity (HP focus)
- General science
- Art, culture, and heritage of HP

### Prelims — CSAT
- Comprehension, interpersonal skills
- Logical reasoning, analytical ability
- Decision making, problem-solving
- General mental ability
- Basic numeracy, data interpretation

### Mains — General Studies Papers
**GS-I:** History, culture, geography, society of India and HP
**GS-II:** Governance, polity, international relations, social justice
**GS-III:** Technology, economic development, environment, disaster management, security

### HP-Specific Topics (High Weightage)
- History of Himachal Pradesh (from ancient to modern)
- HP's geography — rivers, passes, glaciers, national parks
- HP's economy — horticulture, tourism, hydropower
- HP governance — Panchayati Raj, administrative setup
- Folk culture, art, fairs, and festivals of HP
- HP current affairs and government schemes

## Optional Subjects Available
Public Administration, Geography, History, Political Science, Sociology, Hindi Literature, English Literature, Economics, Commerce, Law, Philosophy, Psychology, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Botany, Zoology, and more.

## Preparation Strategy

### 1. Master HP-Specific Content
HPPSC gives significant weightage to Himachal Pradesh — its history, geography, economy, culture, and current affairs. This is what differentiates it from UPSC. Dedicate 30-40% of study time to HP topics.

### 2. Standard GS Preparation
For general topics, follow the same approach as UPSC preparation. NCERT books (6th to 12th) form the foundation.

### 3. Choose Optional Wisely
Pick an optional subject you're strong in. Public Administration and Geography are popular choices with good scoring potential.

### 4. Current Affairs — Daily Habit
Read newspapers daily. Pay special attention to HP state news, government schemes, and policy decisions.

### 5. Answer Writing Practice
Mains is subjective. Practice answer writing daily — crisp, structured answers with HP-specific examples score well.

### 6. Previous Year Analysis
Solve 10 years of HPPSC prelims papers. Analyze the pattern — HP topics and current affairs dominate.

### 7. Mock Tests
Join a test series for prelims. For mains, get your answers evaluated by peers or mentors.

## How to Apply

1. Visit [hppsc.hp.gov.in](https://hppsc.hp.gov.in)
2. Click on "Online Recruitment Applications"
3. Register with email and mobile number
4. Fill the application form with personal and educational details
5. Upload photo, signature, and required certificates
6. Pay the fee: ₹400 (General), ₹100 (SC/ST/OBC of HP), free for PwBD/Ex-SM
7. Submit and download confirmation

## Career Growth

HAS Officer → SDM → ADM → DC (Deputy Commissioner). HPPSC officers can also get deputed to central government posts and All India Services through promotions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the age limit for HPPSC HPAS exam?
The age limit is 21-45 years for general category, which is one of the most relaxed among state PSCs. OBC gets 3 years and SC/ST gets 5 years additional relaxation.

### Is HP domicile mandatory for HPPSC?
Yes, Himachal Pradesh domicile is required for most HPPSC posts. However, some posts under the central deputation quota may be open to all Indian citizens.

### How many attempts are allowed in HPPSC?
General: 6 attempts, OBC: 9 attempts, SC/ST/PwBD: Unlimited (within age limit). Each appearance in prelims counts as one attempt.

### What is the difficulty level of HPPSC compared to UPSC?
HPPSC is considered moderate difficulty — easier than UPSC but competitive within HP. The syllabus is similar but with heavy HP-specific content. Competition is lower (fewer seats but also fewer applicants).

### Can I appear for HPPSC while preparing for UPSC?
Absolutely! Many aspirants prepare for both simultaneously. The GS overlap is significant. Just add HP-specific preparation for HPPSC. The higher age limit (45 years) gives ample time.

### What are the popular optional subjects for HPPSC?
Public Administration, Geography, and History are the most popular optionals. They have good overlap with GS syllabus and scoring potential. Choose based on your background and interest.

### How many vacancies does HPPSC announce each year?
HPPSC typically announces 30-80 vacancies per year for the combined competitive exam (HPAS + allied services). The number varies by year based on government requirements.
