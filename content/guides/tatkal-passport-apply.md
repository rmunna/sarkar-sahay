## What is Tatkal Passport?

Tatkal passport is an **expedited passport service** offered by the Ministry of External Affairs (MEA), Government of India. It allows Indian citizens to get a passport issued on an urgent basis — typically within **1-3 working days** of police verification being completed, compared to the regular processing time of 30-45 days.

The Tatkal scheme is ideal for:
- **Emergency travel** requirements
- **Medical emergencies** abroad
- **Urgent business travel**
- **Death of a family member** abroad
- **Job opportunities** with tight deadlines

## Eligibility for Tatkal Passport

- Any **Indian citizen** who is eligible for a regular passport
- Available for both **fresh passport** and **re-issue** applications
- Available for **adults and minors**
- Applicable to all passport types — ordinary, diplomatic, and official

> **Note:** Tatkal passports are issued with a **shorter validity** initially (sometimes with an address verification pending endorsement) and may have limited pages.

## Documents Required for Tatkal Passport

You need all documents required for a regular passport, **plus additional verification documents**:

### Standard Documents
- **Proof of identity** (any one): [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online), [PAN card](/guide/pan-card-apply-online), Voter ID, Driving License
- **Proof of address** (any one): Aadhaar, utility bills, bank statement, rent agreement
- **Proof of date of birth**: Birth certificate, school leaving certificate, or matriculation certificate

### Additional Documents for Tatkal (Annexure-Based Verification)

Since Tatkal applications skip prior police verification, you need **one of the following**:

**Option A — Annexure F (Verification Certificate)**
- A verification certificate from any one of these officials:
  - Gazetted officer
  - MP, MLA, or Municipal Councillor
  - Tehsildar/SDM
  - Head of recognized educational institution

**Option B — Public Sector Documents (any one)**
- [Income tax PAN card](/guide/pan-card-apply-online) with ITR acknowledgement
- Voter ID with photo
- Arms licence
- Government employee ID (central/state)
- Educational certificates from recognized institution

**Option C — Aadhaar-based**
- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) (accepted as both identity and address proof)

## Step-by-Step Process: How to Apply for Tatkal Passport Online

### Step 1: Register on Passport Seva Portal
1. Visit [passportindia.gov.in](https://www.passportindia.gov.in)
2. Click **"New Registration"** if you don't have an account
3. Fill in your details, create a login ID and password
4. Verify your email address

### Step 2: Login and Fill Application Form
1. Log in to the portal
2. Click **"Apply for Fresh Passport / Re-issue of Passport"**
3. Select **"Tatkal"** under the application type
4. Fill in all required details:
   - Personal details (name, DOB, place of birth)
   - Family details (father's and mother's name)
   - Present and permanent address
   - Emergency contact information
   - Other details (previous passport info if re-issue)
5. Upload a recent **passport-size photograph** (JPEG, 10KB-300KB)
6. Review and submit the form

### Step 3: Pay the Fees
1. After form submission, proceed to **"Pay and Schedule Appointment"**
2. Select the **Passport Seva Kendra (PSK)** or **Regional Passport Office (RPO)**
3. Choose your preferred appointment date and time
4. Pay the fee via **online banking, credit/debit card, or SBI challan**

### Step 4: Visit the Passport Seva Kendra
1. Visit the PSK/RPO on your scheduled date
2. Carry **original documents** + **self-attested photocopies**
3. Carry the **application receipt** printed from the portal
4. Complete the following counters:
   - **Counter A:** Document verification
   - **Counter B:** Biometric capture (photo, fingerprint, signature)
   - **Counter C:** Final verification by Passport Officer
5. Collect the **acknowledgement receipt**

### Step 5: Police Verification
- For Tatkal applications, police verification happens **after passport issuance** (post-issuance verification)
- The passport is issued first, and police verify your address later
- In some cases, if Annexure F or public documents are strong, verification may be waived

### Step 6: Receive Your Passport
- Passport is dispatched via **Speed Post** to your address
- Track delivery using the [passport status tracker](/guide/passport-status-check-online)

## Processing Time

| Application Type | Typical Time |
|------------------|--------------|
| Tatkal (with clear documents) | **1-3 working days** after PSK visit |
| Tatkal (if police verification pending) | **7-14 working days** |
| Regular application (for comparison) | **30-45 working days** |

## Important Tips

1. **Book appointment early** — Tatkal slots fill up fast, especially at popular PSKs. Book as soon as slots open (usually around midnight)
2. **Carry all originals** — Missing even one document can delay your application
3. **Aadhaar-based applications are faster** — If your address on Aadhaar matches the passport application, processing is quicker
4. **Avoid touts** — Never pay agents outside PSKs. The entire process is designed to be self-service
5. **Check status regularly** — Use the [passport status tracking](/guide/passport-status-check-online) feature on the portal

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Can I convert a regular passport application to Tatkal?
No, you cannot convert an already-submitted regular application to Tatkal. You would need to submit a new application under the Tatkal scheme.

### 2. Is police verification done before or after Tatkal passport issuance?
For Tatkal passports, police verification is typically done **after** the passport is issued (post-issuance verification). The passport is given on the basis of document verification at the PSK.

### 3. Can I apply for Tatkal passport for a minor/child?
Yes, Tatkal scheme is available for minors as well. A parent or legal guardian must apply on behalf of the child and accompany them to the PSK.

### 4. What is the validity of a Tatkal passport?
Tatkal passport has the same validity as a regular passport — **10 years for adults** and **5 years for minors** (under 18).

### 5. Can I get a Tatkal passport without Aadhaar?
Yes, but you need strong alternative documents like a verification certificate (Annexure F) from a gazetted officer or valid government-issued ID with public sector documents.

### 6. What if my Tatkal passport application is rejected?
If rejected, you will receive a reason for rejection. You can reapply after addressing the issues. The Tatkal fee is **non-refundable** even if the application is rejected.

### 7. Is there a super-Tatkal or emergency passport option?
There is no official "super-Tatkal" scheme. However, in genuine emergencies (medical, death), you can write to the RPO or MEA for expedited processing on compassionate grounds.
