## What is Mahtari Vandan Yojana?

Mahtari Vandan Yojana is a welfare scheme launched by the Chhattisgarh government to provide financial assistance of **₹1,000 per month (₹12,000 per year)** to married women in the state. The word "Mahtari" means mother in the Chhattisgarhi language, and the scheme aims to empower women financially and promote their economic independence.

The amount is transferred directly to the woman's bank account through DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer) on a monthly basis.

## Who is Eligible?

### Eligibility Criteria
- Must be a **married woman** (including widows and divorced women)
- Must be a **resident of Chhattisgarh**
- Must be aged **21 years or above**
- Must have a **bank account** in her own name linked with Aadhaar

### Who is NOT Eligible
- Unmarried women
- Women who are income tax payers
- Women who are government employees (state/central)
- Women whose family income exceeds the prescribed limit
- Women already receiving pension of ₹1,000+ from the state government

## Benefits

| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Monthly assistance | ₹1,000 |
| Annual total | ₹12,000 |
| Payment mode | Direct Bank Transfer (DBT) |
| Payment date | 1st of every month |

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** of the applicant
- **Marriage certificate** or marriage proof
- **Bank account passbook** (in the applicant's name)
- **Ration card** or family identification document
- **Voter ID** or residence proof
- **Caste certificate** (if applicable)
- **Recent passport-size photograph**
- **Mobile number** linked with Aadhaar

## How to Apply

### Online Application
1. Visit [mahtarivandan.cgstate.gov.in](https://mahtarivandan.cgstate.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Apply Online"** or **"आवेदन करें"**
3. Enter your Aadhaar number and verify through OTP
4. Fill in personal details — name, date of birth, marital status
5. Enter bank account details
6. Upload photograph and required documents
7. Submit the application and note the reference number

### Offline Application
1. Visit your local **Anganwadi Centre**, **Gram Panchayat**, or **Ward office**
2. Collect the Mahtari Vandan application form
3. Fill in all required details
4. Attach photocopies of required documents
5. Submit the form and collect acknowledgment receipt
6. Applications can also be submitted at **Lok Seva Kendras**

### Verification Process
- Applications verified by local authorities (Anganwadi worker / Gram Panchayat)
- Document authenticity confirmed through government databases
- Aadhaar-based verification of identity
- Approved applications added to the beneficiary list

## How to Check Application Status

1. Visit [mahtarivandan.cgstate.gov.in](https://mahtarivandan.cgstate.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Application Status"**
3. Enter your application number or Aadhaar number
4. View current status and payment history

## Important Tips

- **Marriage proof is essential** — ensure you have a marriage certificate; if not, get one issued from your local body
- **Aadhaar-bank linking** — mandatory for DBT; visit your bank branch to complete seeding
- **Apply early** — payments start from the month of approval, not the month of application
- **Keep originals safe** — you may need to show originals during verification
- **Update your details** — if you change your bank account or address, update it on the portal immediately

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can widows apply for Mahtari Vandan?
Yes, widows are eligible as they fall under the "married women" category (previously married). Divorced women are also eligible.

### Is there an income limit for this scheme?
Yes, income tax payers and government employees are excluded. The exact family income threshold is specified in the notification.

### Can I receive both Mahtari Vandan and Ladli Behna Yojana?
Mahtari Vandan is a Chhattisgarh scheme while Ladli Behna is from Madhya Pradesh. If you're a Chhattisgarh resident, you can only apply for Mahtari Vandan. See our [MP Ladli Behna guide](/guide/madhya-pradesh-ladli-behna) for comparison.

### What if I don't have a marriage certificate?
You can obtain a marriage certificate from your local municipal body or Gram Panchayat. Alternatively, some areas accept a joint affidavit from husband and wife as proof.

### Will the monthly amount increase in the future?
The government may revise the amount based on budget allocation and policy decisions. Currently, it is fixed at ₹1,000 per month.
