# Vi (Vodafone Idea) Network Not Working — How to Fix Signal & Data Issues

Vi (Vodafone Idea) users across India frequently face network problems — no signal, slow 4G data, call drops, and recharge failures. Many of these issues became worse after the Vodafone-Idea merger, especially in areas where network infrastructure consolidation is still ongoing. This guide covers every common Vi problem and how to fix it.

## 1. Vi No Signal or No Network in Your Area

This is the most common complaint. Your phone shows "No Service," "Emergency Calls Only," or no signal bars at all.

### Why This Happens

- **Network consolidation post-merger** — Vi shut down duplicate towers in many areas, reducing coverage
- **Tower maintenance or outage** — temporary issues in your locality
- **SIM card problem** — damaged or old SIM not supporting newer bands
- **Phone settings issue** — wrong network mode selected

### How to Fix

1. **Restart your phone** — this forces a fresh network search
2. **Check for outage** — dial **199** from another phone or check the Vi app for service alerts in your area
3. **Toggle Airplane Mode** — turn on for 30 seconds, then turn off
4. **Manual network selection:**
   - Go to **Settings → Mobile Network → Network Operators**
   - Select **Search manually** → choose **Vi** or **Vodafone IN** / **Idea**
   - Switch back to **Automatic** after selecting
5. **Check Network Mode** — go to Settings → Mobile Network → Preferred network type → select **4G/3G/2G (Auto)**
6. **Try your SIM in another phone** — if it works, your phone's antenna may have issues
7. **Get a SIM replacement** — if your SIM is old (pre-merger Vodafone or Idea SIM), visit a Vi store for a free 4G SIM upgrade

> **Note:** If Vi has poor coverage in your area permanently, consider porting to another network. See the MNP section below.

## 2. Vi Recharge Failed — Money Debited but Not Recharged

You paid for a recharge via UPI, card, or the Vi app, but the plan didn't activate and money was deducted.

### How to Fix

1. **Wait 30 minutes** — some recharges take time to reflect during high load
2. **Check SMS** — look for a confirmation or failure SMS from Vi
3. **Check the Vi app** — go to **My Account → My Plans** to see if the plan is active
4. **Check your bank/UPI app** — verify if the transaction was actually debited or is still "pending"
5. **If debited but not recharged:**
   - Open the **Vi app → Help → Recharge Issues**
   - Or call **199** → select recharge complaint
   - File a complaint on **https://www.myvi.in/** under Support
6. **Bank reversal** — if Vi confirms no recharge, the amount auto-reverses to your bank in 5-7 working days
7. **Escalation** — if unresolved after 7 days, file a complaint on the **TRAI DND/complaint portal** or email **appellate@vodafoneidea.com**

## 3. Vi Slow 4G Data — APN Settings Fix

If your Vi data is extremely slow despite having an active plan, incorrect APN settings could be the cause.

### Correct Vi APN Settings

| Setting | Value |
|---------|-------|
| **Name** | Vi Internet |
| **APN** | portalnmms |
| **Proxy** | Not set |
| **Port** | Not set |
| **Username** | Not set |
| **Password** | Not set |
| **MCC** | 404 or 405 |
| **MNC** | Depends on circle |
| **Authentication type** | Not set |
| **APN type** | default,supl |

### How to Set APN

1. Go to **Settings → Mobile Network → Access Point Names**
2. Tap **+** (Add new APN)
3. Enter the settings from the table above
4. Save and **select** the new APN
5. Restart your phone

### Other Fixes for Slow Data

- **Check data balance** — dial **\*199#** to check remaining data. Speed drops to 64 Kbps after limit
- **Disable data saver** — Settings → Network → Data Saver → turn off
- **Clear browser cache** — old cache can make browsing feel slow
- **Check background apps** — apps updating in background consume data
- **Reset network settings** — Settings → System → Reset → Reset Network Settings (this erases saved WiFi passwords)
- **Switch between 4G bands** — some areas work better on Band 3 vs Band 40/41

## 4. VoLTE / VoWiFi Not Working on Vi

VoLTE (Voice over LTE) enables HD calls without dropping to 2G/3G. VoWiFi (WiFi Calling) lets you make calls over WiFi when signal is weak.

### Common Issues

- VoLTE icon not showing in status bar
- Calls still dropping to 3G/2G
- WiFi Calling option missing in settings

### How to Fix VoLTE

1. **Check phone compatibility** — not all phones support Vi VoLTE. Check the list at **myvi.in**
2. **Enable VoLTE:**
   - **Android:** Settings → Mobile Network → VoLTE → Enable / "Enhanced 4G LTE Mode"
   - **iPhone:** Settings → Cellular → Cellular Data Options → Voice & Data → LTE, VoLTE On
3. **Update carrier settings** — on iPhone, go to Settings → General → About (carrier update prompt appears if available)
4. **Restart after enabling**
5. **Check if VoLTE is available in your area** — Vi VoLTE is not live in all circles

### How to Fix VoWiFi (WiFi Calling)

1. **Check eligibility** — Vi WiFi Calling works on select phones and circles only
2. **Enable:** Settings → Mobile Network → WiFi Calling → Turn On
3. **If option is missing** — your phone or circle may not support it yet
4. **Update your phone software** — WiFi Calling support sometimes comes via software updates

## 5. "Not Registered on Network" After SIM Swap

After getting a new SIM (SIM swap/replacement), you may see "Not registered on network" or "SIM not provisioned."

### How to Fix

1. **Wait up to 4 hours** — SIM activation takes time after swap
2. **Restart your phone** after waiting
3. **Check if the SIM swap is complete** — call **199** from another number and check with your mobile number
4. **Re-insert the SIM** — remove, clean contacts gently, re-insert
5. **Try in another phone** — rules out a phone-specific issue
6. **Visit a Vi store** — if the error persists after 24 hours, the SIM swap may not have been processed correctly. Carry your Aadhaar and the old SIM (if available)

> **Important:** During SIM swap, your old SIM stops working immediately. If the new SIM doesn't activate within 24 hours, escalate at the Vi store.

## 6. Vi App Login and Recharge Errors

The Vi app (Android/iOS) is often needed for recharges, plan checks, and support — but it has its own issues.

### Common Errors

- OTP not received for login
- App crashes on launch
- "Something went wrong" during recharge
- Unable to fetch plan details

### How to Fix

1. **OTP not received:**
   - Check if your Vi number has active incoming SMS
   - Wait 2-3 minutes — OTPs can be delayed
   - Try the **missed call verification** option if available
2. **App crashes:**
   - Update to the latest version from Play Store / App Store
   - Clear app cache: Settings → Apps → Vi → Clear Cache
   - Reinstall the app
3. **Recharge errors:**
   - Try recharging from **myvi.in** website instead
   - Use a different payment method (UPI vs card)
   - Check if your bank's payment server is down
4. **Can't fetch plans:**
   - Switch from WiFi to mobile data (or vice versa)
   - Try after some time — Vi servers face load during peak hours

## 7. Vi Postpaid Bill Dispute

If you've been overcharged on your Vi postpaid bill — unexpected charges, roaming fees, or data overage charges.

### How to Resolve

1. **Download itemized bill** — Vi app → My Account → Bills → View Bill Details
2. **Identify the disputed charges** — look for ISD calls, VAS (Value Added Services), or data overage
3. **Deactivate unwanted VAS** — send **STOP** to **155223** or call **199**
4. **Raise a complaint:**
   - Call **199** → Postpaid → Billing complaint
   - Vi app → Help → Billing Issues
   - Email: **customercare@vodafoneidea.com**
5. **Escalation path:**
   - **Level 1:** Call 199 or Vi app (resolution within 7 days)
   - **Level 2:** Appellate Officer — email **appellate@vodafoneidea.com** (if Level 1 doesn't resolve in 7 days)
   - **Level 3:** File complaint on **TRAI's TCCMS portal** — https://www.trai.gov.in/
   - **Level 4:** Consumer Forum — file case on **https://consumerhelpline.gov.in/**

## 8. MNP — Port Out from Vi to Another Network

If Vi network quality is consistently poor in your area, you can port your number to Jio, Airtel, or BSNL via Mobile Number Portability (MNP).

### Steps to Port Out

1. **Ensure eligibility:**
   - Your Vi number must be at least 90 days old
   - No pending dues (prepaid balance isn't an issue)
   - You haven't ported in the last 90 days
2. **Generate UPC (Unique Porting Code):**
   - Send SMS: **PORT <your 10-digit number>** to **1900**
   - You'll receive a UPC code valid for **4 days** (15 days for J&K/NE)
3. **Visit the new operator's store** (Jio, Airtel, or BSNL) with:
   - UPC code
   - Aadhaar card (for identity verification)
   - Passport photo
4. **Fill the CAF (Customer Acquisition Form)** and submit
5. **Porting completes in 3-7 working days** — you'll get an SMS before the switch

### Common MNP Issues

- **"Port out request rejected"** — check for outstanding dues or if 90-day rule isn't met
- **Corporate number** — need an NOC from your company
- **UPC expired** — generate a new one by sending the PORT SMS again
- **Network down during porting** — brief disconnection (up to a few hours) is normal during the switch

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Why does my Vi SIM show "No Service" suddenly?
This usually happens due to a local tower outage or maintenance. Toggle Airplane Mode, restart your phone, and wait 30 minutes. If the issue persists across multiple phones, it's likely a tower issue in your area — call 199 to report.

### Q2. My Vi recharge was debited but plan is not active. What should I do?
Wait 30 minutes first. Then check the Vi app under My Plans. If still not active, call 199 or raise a complaint on the Vi app. The amount auto-reverses to your bank within 5-7 days if the recharge truly failed.

### Q3. How do I fix slow Vi 4G data?
First check your remaining data balance (dial *199#). If data is exhausted, speed is throttled to 64 Kbps. If data is available, reset your APN settings (see APN table above), reset network settings, and restart your phone.

### Q4. Can I use VoLTE on Vi? How do I enable it?
Yes, Vi supports VoLTE in most circles on compatible phones. Enable it via Settings → Mobile Network → VoLTE/Enhanced 4G. If the option doesn't appear, your phone may not be compatible or VoLTE isn't live in your circle yet.

### Q5. How long does it take to port out from Vi?
MNP porting takes 3-7 working days. Send **PORT <your number>** to **1900** to get a UPC code, then visit the new operator's store. Your Vi number must be at least 90 days old with no outstanding dues.

### Q6. Vi app OTP not coming — what can I do?
Ensure your Vi number has active incoming SMS service. Wait 2-3 minutes. If still not received, try restarting your phone or logging in via the myvi.in website instead.

### Q7. How do I stop unwanted VAS charges on Vi postpaid?
Send **STOP** to **155223** from your Vi number. Also call 199 and ask them to deactivate all VAS services. Check your bill for recurring charges and dispute any unauthorized ones.

### Q8. My Vi SIM says "Not registered on network" after SIM swap. Is this normal?
Yes, SIM activation after swap can take up to 4 hours. Restart your phone after waiting. If it doesn't resolve in 24 hours, visit a Vi store with your Aadhaar for re-verification.
