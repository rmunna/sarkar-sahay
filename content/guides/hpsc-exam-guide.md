# HPSC Exam Guide — Haryana PSC HCS Eligibility, Syllabus & Tips

The Haryana Public Service Commission (HPSC) conducts the Haryana Civil Services (HCS) exam for recruitment to administrative and allied services in Haryana. The HCS exam is the gateway to top state government positions including SDM, DSP, DETC, and more. This guide covers the complete exam process.

## Overview

| Detail | Information |
|--------|-------------|
| Conducting Body | Haryana Public Service Commission (HPSC) |
| Exam Name | Haryana Civil Services (HCS) Exam |
| Posts | SDM, Dy.SP, DETC, ETO, DSP, Tehsildar, and more |
| Level | State Level |
| Selection Process | Prelims → Mains → Interview |
| Job Location | Haryana |

## Posts Under HCS

- Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM)
- Deputy Superintendent of Police (DSP)
- District Employment & Training Officer (DETC)
- Excise & Taxation Officer (ETO)
- Tehsildar
- Block Development & Panchayat Officer (BDPO)
- District Revenue Officer
- District Food & Supply Controller

## Eligibility Criteria

### Age Limit

| Category | Minimum Age | Maximum Age |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| General | 21 years | 42 years |
| SC / BC (Haryana) | 21 years | 47 years |
| PwD | 21 years | 52 years |

**Note:** Haryana has a relatively higher age limit compared to other state PSCs.

### Educational Qualification

- Bachelor's Degree from a recognised university
- For certain posts, relevant professional qualification may be required

### Nationality

- Indian citizen
- Knowledge of Hindi/Devanagari script is required
- Haryana domicile may be required for certain reserved categories

## Exam Pattern

### Prelims

| Paper | Subject | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|-------|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| Paper I | General Studies | 100 | 200 | 2 hours |
| Paper II | CSAT | 100 | 200 | 2 hours |

- Paper II is qualifying (33% minimum)
- Only Paper I counts for prelims merit
- **Negative marking:** 1/4th mark for each wrong answer
- Objective type (MCQ)

### Mains

| Paper | Subject | Marks | Duration |
|-------|---------|-------|----------|
| Paper I | General Hindi | 100 | 3 hours |
| Paper II | English Essay | 100 | 3 hours |
| Paper III | General Studies I | 200 | 3 hours |
| Paper IV | General Studies II | 200 | 3 hours |
| Paper V | General Studies III | 200 | 3 hours |
| Paper VI | Optional Subject Paper I | 200 | 3 hours |
| Paper VII | Optional Subject Paper II | 200 | 3 hours |
| **Total** | | **1200** | |

- Hindi and English papers are qualifying
- Merit based on GS (600) + Optional (400) = **1000 marks**

### Interview

- **Marks:** 150
- **Total for ranking:** 1000 + 150 = **1150**

## Syllabus Overview

### Prelims — General Studies
- Current Events — national, international, Haryana
- Indian History & Haryana History
- Geography of India and Haryana
- Indian Polity and Constitution
- Indian Economy and Haryana Economy
- General Science
- Environment and Ecology
- Haryana's culture, festivals, folk traditions

### Prelims — CSAT
- Comprehension, Logical reasoning, Data interpretation
- Decision-making, Problem-solving
- Basic numeracy, Analytical ability

### Mains — General Studies

**GS I:** Indian History, Culture, Geography, Society
**GS II:** Governance, Constitution, Polity, International Relations, Social Justice
**GS III:** Economy, Technology, Environment, Disaster Management, Security

### Optional Subjects
Candidates choose one optional subject (2 papers × 200 marks each). Popular options include:
- Public Administration
- Geography
- History
- Hindi Literature
- Sociology
- Political Science

## How to Apply

1. Visit [hpsc.gov.in](https://hpsc.gov.in)
2. Register with email and mobile number
3. When HCS notification is released, apply through the portal
4. Fill in personal, educational, and category details
5. Upload photograph, signature, certificates
6. Pay application fee online
7. Submit and save confirmation

### Application Fees

| Category | Fee |
|----------|-----|
| General (Male) | ₹500 |
| General (Female — Haryana) | ₹125 |
| SC / BC / ESM (Haryana) | ₹125 |
| PwD | Nil |

## Important Dates Pattern

- **Notification:** No fixed schedule; roughly once every 1–2 years
- **Application Window:** 30–45 days
- **Prelims:** 3–6 months after notification
- **Mains:** 4–8 months after prelims
- **Interview:** 2–4 months after mains
- **Final Result:** 18–24 months from notification

## Preparation Tips

### 1. Haryana GK is Critical
HPSC asks many questions on Haryana's history, geography, culture, and government schemes. Study *Haryana GK* by Arihant or Lucent and follow Haryana government announcements.

### 2. Choose Optional Wisely
Your optional subject contributes 400/1000 marks in Mains. Choose based on your interest, academic background, and scoring potential.

### 3. NCERT + Standard Books
Build your GS foundation with NCERT (Class 6–12) and standard references:
- Polity: M. Laxmikanth
- Economy: Ramesh Singh
- History: Bipan Chandra (Modern India)

### 4. Practice Descriptive Writing
Mains is entirely descriptive. Practice writing structured answers daily. Time yourself — 15 minutes per 200-mark answer.

### 5. Hindi and English Papers
These are qualifying but don't underestimate them. Practice essay writing, letter writing, and translation regularly.

### 6. Previous Year Papers
HCS papers from the last 5–10 years help identify trends. Pay attention to Haryana-centric questions — they're increasing.

### 7. Current Affairs with Haryana Focus
Read Dainik Jagran (Haryana edition) and follow Haryana Budget, Panchayati Raj news, and state scheme launches.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Is there negative marking in HCS Prelims?
Yes. 1/4th of the allotted marks are deducted per wrong answer.

### Can non-Haryana residents apply?
Yes, Indian citizens from any state can apply for unreserved posts. Haryana domicile is required for reserved categories.

### What is the HCS age limit?
General category: 21–42 years. SC/BC (Haryana): up to 47 years. This is higher than most state PSCs.

### How many vacancies are typically available?
Usually 100–200 posts across all services combined. Varies significantly by notification.

### Is coaching necessary for HCS?
Not mandatory. Many candidates succeed through self-study. However, coaching can help with optional subject preparation and answer writing practice.

### What are the popular optional subjects?
Public Administration, Geography, and History are the most popular choices due to overlap with GS and availability of study material.
