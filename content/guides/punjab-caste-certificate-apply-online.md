# Punjab Caste Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **caste certificate** (ਜਾਤੀ ਸਰਟੀਫਿਕੇਟ — Jaati Sarteefeekat) in Punjab is issued by the Revenue Department (Sub-Divisional Magistrate / Tehsildar) and is required for SC/BC/OBC reservations, Punjab government scholarships (Dr. Ambedkar Scholarship), government job applications (PPSC, Punjab police), and welfare scheme eligibility. Applications go through **Punjab E-District Services** or **eSewa centres**.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [punjabedistrictservices.gov.in](https://punjabedistrictservices.gov.in) or nearest **eSewa centre**. Issued by Tehsildar/SDM within **15–30 days**.

---

## Caste Categories in Punjab

| Category | Reservation % | Notes |
|----------|---------------|-------|
| **SC** | 25% | Scheduled Castes — Punjab has highest SC % |
| **BC** | 12% | Backward Classes (OBC equivalent in Punjab) |
| **EWS** | 10% | General category, income < ₹8 lakh/year |

> Punjab has a **high SC population** (31.9%) — one of the highest among all states. The SC reservation is correspondingly high at 25%. Punjab's "BC" is the equivalent of OBC in other states. **Ravidassia, Mazhabi Sikh, Valmiki, and Chamar** are major SC communities in Punjab.

## Why You Need Punjab Caste Certificate

- **Dr. Ambedkar Post-Matric Scholarship** (SC) — mandatory caste proof
- **Punjab OBC/BC scholarship** — caste certificate required
- **NSP National Scholarship** — all categories
- **Government job reservation** — PPSC, Punjab Police, PSPCL, Punjab government departments
- **Central OBC NCL** — for UPSC/SSC/railways (if community is in central OBC list)
- **PSEB / PTET admissions** — reserved category proof

---

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory)
- **Ration card** (shows family caste/community — Punjab ration card mentions caste)
- **Father's school leaving certificate (TC)** — caste listed in school records
- **Father's / grandfather's caste proof** (old voter ID with caste, service record)
- Self-declaration of caste
- **Voter ID** (address proof)
- Passport-size photograph

## How to Apply Online — Punjab E-District Services

1. Go to [punjabedistrictservices.gov.in](https://punjabedistrictservices.gov.in)
2. Register / log in with mobile number (OTP)
3. Click **"Revenue Department"** → **"Caste Certificate"**
4. Fill form:
   - Applicant name, address (district, tehsil, village)
   - Community/caste declaration (SC / BC)
   - Father's community details
5. Upload documents (Aadhaar, father's TC, ration card)
6. Pay fee: **₹20–30** (online)
7. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via eSewa / Seva Kendra

1. Visit nearest **eSewa centre** or Seva Kendra
2. Carry Aadhaar, ration card, father's TC / caste proof
3. Operator fills and submits application
4. Service charge: approximately ₹30–60
5. Collect acknowledgement receipt

---

## Status Check & Download

1. Go to [punjabedistrictservices.gov.in](https://punjabedistrictservices.gov.in)
2. Click **"Track Application"** → enter reference number
3. Or log in → **"My Applications"** → view status
4. Once approved: download digitally signed PDF

## Frequently Asked Questions

### BC certificate Punjab vs OBC NCL — for central government jobs?

The **Punjab BC certificate** is for state-level reservations. For **central government jobs** (UPSC, SSC, railways), you need an **OBC NCL certificate** with income < ₹8 lakh/year, and your community must be in the central OBC list. Apply for OBC NCL separately at Punjab E-District — it is a different service.

### Dr. Ambedkar SC Post-Matric Scholarship — income limit?

For **Punjab SC Post-Matric Scholarship (Dr. Ambedkar Scheme)**: income < **₹4 lakh/year** for most categories. For **NSP central SC scholarship**: income < ₹2.5 lakh/year. Both require the Punjab caste certificate.

### SC caste certificate Punjab for admission in other states?

SC caste certificate from Punjab is valid for **NSP** applications nationally. For **state-specific quotas in other states** (e.g., NEET state quota), the other state's authority typically issues the certificate if you're a resident there. Cross-state SC certificates are generally accepted for central reservations.
