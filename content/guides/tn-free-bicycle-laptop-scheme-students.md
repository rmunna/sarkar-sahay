## What is the TN Free Laptop & Bicycle Scheme?

The **Tamil Nadu Free Laptop Scheme** and **Free Bicycle Scheme** are flagship education welfare programs of the state government aimed at bridging the digital divide and improving school attendance among students from economically weaker backgrounds.

- **Free Laptop Scheme:** Laptops are distributed to students who pass **Class 11 and 12** in government and government-aided schools and colleges. Originally launched as the **Anna Laptop Scheme**, it has distributed crores of laptops since 2011.
- **Free Bicycle Scheme:** Bicycles are given to students of **Class 9 to 12** studying in government and government-aided schools to reduce dropouts caused by transportation difficulties, especially in rural areas.

Both schemes are administered by the **School Education Department, Government of Tamil Nadu**.

## Who is Eligible?

### Free Laptop Scheme
- Students who have **passed Class 12** (Higher Secondary) from government or government-aided schools
- Students enrolled in **government arts and science colleges, polytechnics, and ITIs**
- Must be a **resident of Tamil Nadu**
- Must be studying in a **Tamil Nadu state board** recognized institution
- No separate income criterion — all eligible students receive laptops

### Free Bicycle Scheme
- Students enrolled in **Class 9 to 12** in government and government-aided schools
- Must be a **resident of Tamil Nadu**
- Both boys and girls are eligible
- Students must have **regular attendance** in school
- No income limit — all eligible students benefit

> **Note:** Students in private unaided schools are generally not eligible for these schemes.

## Benefits Provided

| Scheme | Benefit | Value (Approx.) |
|---|---|---|
| Free Laptop | Laptop with pre-loaded educational software | ₹15,000-₹20,000 |
| Free Bicycle | Standard bicycle | ₹3,000-₹4,000 |

The laptops come pre-loaded with **Tamil Nadu state board study materials**, educational software, and internet browsing capability. Bicycles are standard models suitable for daily commuting.

## Step-by-Step Process

### How Students Receive Laptops

1. **Automatic enrollment:** Schools and colleges compile the list of eligible students through the **EMIS (Educational Management Information System)** portal
2. **Verification by school:** The headmaster/principal verifies student details and uploads to EMIS
3. **No separate application needed** — eligible students are automatically included
4. **Distribution event:** Laptops are distributed at school/college premises during designated distribution drives
5. **Acknowledgement:** Students sign a receipt upon receiving the laptop
6. **Activation:** Students activate the laptop using their credentials provided at distribution

### How Students Receive Bicycles

1. **School identifies eligible students** from Class 9-12 enrollment records
2. **List uploaded** to the EMIS portal by the school administration
3. **District education office** verifies and approves the list
4. **Bicycles delivered** to school premises
5. **Distribution ceremony** conducted at the school
6. **Students collect** bicycles with parent/guardian signature

> **Important:** Students do **not** need to apply separately. The school handles the entire process through EMIS.

## Distribution Timeline

- **Laptops:** Typically distributed within **3-6 months** of the academic year beginning, usually during special state-level events
- **Bicycles:** Distributed at the **start of each academic year** (June-August)
- Distribution schedule announced by the **District Education Officer**

## Important Tips

1. **Ensure EMIS registration** — Your school must have updated your details on the EMIS portal for you to be eligible
2. **Keep Aadhaar updated** — An active Aadhaar linked with correct details helps in smooth verification (see our [Aadhaar update guide](/guide/aadhaar-address-change-online))
3. **Maintain attendance** — Regular attendance is a practical requirement; schools may exclude chronic absentees
4. **Don't sell the laptop** — Laptops are meant for educational use; selling government-provided laptops can lead to legal action
5. **Report issues** — If you face any issues with the laptop (hardware defects), report to your school within the warranty period

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Do I need to apply separately for the free laptop?
**A:** No. Eligible students are **automatically identified** by their school/college through the EMIS system. No separate application is required.

### Q2: Can private school students get a free laptop or bicycle?
**A:** Generally, **no**. The scheme is for students in government and government-aided schools/colleges only. Private unaided school students are not eligible.

### Q3: What specifications do the free laptops have?
**A:** The laptops typically have a basic configuration suitable for educational use — around **4GB RAM, 500GB storage, Intel processor**, and a 14-inch screen. Specifications may vary each year based on procurement.

### Q4: Can I get both a bicycle and a laptop?
**A:** Yes. A student can receive a **bicycle in Class 9** and a **laptop after Class 12**. Both are separate schemes with no overlap restriction.

### Q5: What if my laptop is defective?
**A:** Report the defect to your **school/college administration** immediately. Laptops come with a manufacturer warranty (typically **1-3 years**). The school coordinates repairs.

### Q6: Is the bicycle scheme available for boys and girls?
**A:** Yes, **both boys and girls** studying in government and government-aided schools from Class 9 to 12 are eligible for the free bicycle.

### Q7: My school hasn't distributed laptops yet. Who should I contact?
**A:** Contact your **school headmaster** first. If unresolved, reach out to the **District Education Officer** or the **Chief Educational Officer** of your district.
