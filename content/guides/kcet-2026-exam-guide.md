# KCET 2026 — Karnataka Common Entrance Test

**KCET 2026** (Karnataka Common Entrance Test) is conducted by the **KEA (Karnataka Examinations Authority)** for admission to engineering, pharmacy, and other professional courses in Karnataka government and private aided colleges. It's one of India's largest state-level engineering entrance exams, with over 2 lakh students appearing annually.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [kea.kar.nic.in](https://kea.kar.nic.in). Exam held in **April–May 2026** (2 days). Karnataka domicile required for 85% state quota seats.

## Eligibility Criteria

| Criteria | Requirement |
|----------|-------------|
| **Nationality** | Indian citizen (Karnataka domicile required for state quota) |
| **Qualification** | PUC (12th) with PCM (for engineering) or PCB (for pharmacy/medical) |
| **Minimum marks** | 45% aggregate in PCM (40% for SC/ST/OBC Karnataka) |
| **Age limit** | No upper age limit for engineering; check pharmacy regulations |
| **Attempts** | No limit |

### Karnataka Domicile — Who Gets State Quota?

For the **85% state quota seats**, applicants must:
- Be born in Karnataka, OR
- Have studied in Karnataka schools for **7 continuous years** (Classes 1–12)
- OR be children of Karnataka state government employees

## KCET 2026 Application Process

1. Go to [kea.kar.nic.in](https://kea.kar.nic.in) → **"KCET 2026"**
2. Register with mobile number + email (OTP verification)
3. Fill application form:
   - Personal details, PUC school details
   - Select exam subjects (PCM/PCB or both)
   - Choose exam centre (district-wise)
4. Upload photo (passport size, white background) + signature
5. Pay fee:
   - General/OBC: **₹650** (2 subjects) | ₹750 (both PCM+PCB)
   - SC/ST Karnataka: **₹500**
6. Submit and download confirmation

## Exam Pattern

| Subject | Papers | Marks | Duration |
|---------|--------|-------|---------|
| Physics | 60 questions | 60 marks | 80 min |
| Chemistry | 60 questions | 60 marks | 80 min |
| Mathematics / Biology | 60 questions | 60 marks | 80 min |

- **Mode**: Offline (OMR-based)
- **Question type**: MCQs (1 mark each)
- **Negative marking**: **No negative marking** (attempt all questions)
- **Day 1**: Physics + Chemistry
- **Day 2**: Mathematics (engineering) or Biology (pharmacy/MBBS)

## KCET Syllabus 2026

KCET syllabus is based on **Karnataka PUC II (Class 12) syllabus**:

**Physics**: Electrostatics, Current Electricity, Magnetism, Optics, Semiconductors, Communication Systems, etc.

**Chemistry**: Solid State, Solutions, Electrochemistry, Coordination Compounds, Biomolecules, Polymers, Organic Chemistry, etc.

**Mathematics**: Relations & Functions, Inverse Trigonometric Functions, Matrices & Determinants, Calculus (Differentiation, Integration, Differential Equations), Vectors, 3D Geometry, Probability, etc.

**Biology** (for pharmacy/NEET backup): Reproduction, Genetics, Evolution, Biotechnology, Ecology, etc.

## KCET vs JEE Main — Which Is Easier?

| Aspect | KCET | JEE Main |
|--------|------|----------|
| Difficulty | **Easier** (PUC-level) | Harder (NCERT + advanced) |
| Questions | 60 per subject | 75 per subject |
| Negative marking | No | Yes (-1 for MCQ) |
| Syllabus | Karnataka PUC | NCERT + beyond |
| Uses for | Karnataka state colleges | NITs, IIITs, all states |

> Students often appear in **both KCET and JEE Main** — KCET for Karnataka colleges, JEE for national options.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can students from other states apply for KCET?

Yes — students from all states can apply. However, they are eligible only for the **15% all-India quota seats** (called Type 2A seats by KEA). The **85% state quota** is reserved for Karnataka domicile holders.

### Is KCET accepted for MBBS admissions?

KCET is primarily for **engineering and pharmacy**. For MBBS/BDS in Karnataka, you need **NEET** — KEA uses NEET scores for medical seat allotment (not KCET scores).

### Can I use both KCET and JEE scores for Karnataka colleges?

**KCET score is used** for Karnataka government and private aided colleges under state quota. **JEE Main score** may be used by some private unaided colleges (not KEA counselling). In KEA counselling, only KCET rank matters.
