# How to Pay Electricity Bill Online — All State Discoms

Paying your bijli (electricity) bill online saves time and avoids long queues at payment counters. Every state discom (distribution company) in India now offers online payment through their own website, app, or third-party platforms like Paytm, PhonePe, Google Pay, and BHIM UPI.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent informational website. We are not affiliated with any government body, discom, or electricity provider. Always verify details on your state discom's official website.

---

## What is Online Electricity Bill Payment?

Electricity in India is distributed by state-level distribution companies (discoms). Each state has one or more discoms. You receive a monthly or bimonthly bill based on your meter reading. Online payment lets you clear this bill from your phone or computer without visiting any office.

Some major discoms across India:

| State | Discom(s) |
|---|---|
| Delhi | BSES Rajdhani, BSES Yamuna, Tata Power DDL |
| Maharashtra | MSEDCL (Mahavitaran) |
| Uttar Pradesh | UPPCL (DVVNL, MVVNL, PuVVNL, KESCO) |
| Tamil Nadu | TNEB (Tangedco) |
| Karnataka | BESCOM, MESCOM, HESCOM, CESCOM, GESCOM |
| West Bengal | WBSEDCL, CESC |
| Rajasthan | JDVVNL, AVVNL, JdVVNL |
| Gujarat | UGVCL, PGVCL, MGVCL, DGVCL |

## Eligibility

- You must have an **active electricity connection** with a valid consumer/account number.
- Your **meter number** and **CA (Consumer Account) number** are printed on your bill.
- Any person can pay — you don't need to be the account holder.

---

## Documents / Details Needed

- **Consumer number / CA number** (printed on your bill)
- **Bill number** (for some discoms)
- **Mobile number** registered with your connection
- **Payment method** — debit card, credit card, net banking, UPI, or wallet

## Step-by-Step: Pay Electricity Bill Online

### Method 1: Pay on Your Discom's Website

1. Visit your state discom's official website (see table above or search "[your discom] bill payment").
2. Look for **"Online Bill Payment"** or **"Quick Pay"** option.
3. Enter your **Consumer Number / CA Number**.
4. Your pending bill amount will be displayed.
5. Choose your payment method (UPI, card, net banking).
6. Complete the payment and **save the receipt**.

### Method 2: Pay via Discom Mobile App

Most discoms have their own mobile app:
- **MSEDCL** → Mahadiscom app
- **UPPCL** → UPPCL Jhatpat app
- **BSES** → BSES Rajdhani/Yamuna app
- **TNEB** → Tangedco app
- **BESCOM** → BESCOM Mithra app

Steps:
1. Download the app from Play Store / App Store.
2. Register with your consumer number and mobile.
3. View your current bill.
4. Tap **Pay Now** and choose payment method.
5. Confirm payment — receipt is saved in the app.

### Method 3: Pay via UPI Apps (Paytm, PhonePe, Google Pay)

1. Open your UPI app (Paytm, PhonePe, Google Pay, BHIM, etc.).
2. Go to **Bill Payments** → **Electricity**.
3. Select your **state** and **discom/provider**.
4. Enter your **Consumer Number**.
5. The bill amount will auto-fetch.
6. Pay using UPI balance, linked bank account, or card.
7. You get instant confirmation and receipt.

### Method 4: Pay via Net Banking

1. Log in to your bank's internet banking portal.
2. Navigate to **Bill Payments** or **Utility Payments**.
3. Select **Electricity** and choose your discom.
4. Enter consumer number and amount.
5. Authorize the payment.

## Offline Methods

1. **Discom office / collection centre:** Visit with your bill and pay by cash, card, or cheque.
2. **Authorised collection agents:** Many discoms use CSC (Common Service Centres) or retail partners.
3. **Post office:** Some discoms accept payment at India Post counters.
4. **Drop box:** Some offices have cheque drop boxes for bill payment.

---

## Fees

- **Online payment:** Usually **no extra charges** when paying via UPI or net banking.
- **Credit card:** Some discoms or payment platforms charge **1–2% convenience fee**.
- **Third-party apps:** Paytm, PhonePe etc. may charge a small platform fee on some transactions.
- **Late payment surcharge:** If you miss the due date, a **1–2% monthly surcharge** is added to your next bill.

> Always pay before the **due date** printed on your bill to avoid surcharges and disconnection.

---

## Processing Time

- **UPI / debit card / net banking:** Bill updated within **minutes to 24 hours**.
- **Credit card:** May take **1–2 working days** to reflect.
- **Cheque:** Takes **3–5 working days** to clear.

If the payment doesn't reflect within 48 hours, contact your discom's customer care with the transaction receipt.

---

## How to Check Your Electricity Bill Online

Before paying, you can view and download your bill:

1. Visit your discom's website.
2. Go to **"View/Download Bill"** or **"Bill History"**.
3. Enter your consumer number.
4. Your latest bill PDF will be available for download.

You can also check on Paytm or PhonePe by entering your consumer number under Electricity.

## Tips and Best Practices

1. **Save your consumer number** in your UPI app for quick future payments.
2. **Set up auto-pay** if your discom supports it — never miss a due date.
3. **Download receipts** after every payment for your records.
4. **Compare your meter reading** with the bill to catch errors early.
5. **Submit meter readings** online if your discom allows self-reporting — avoids estimated billing.
6. **Apply for e-bill** to get paperless bills via email or SMS.
7. **Report outages** on your discom app — most have a dedicated fault reporting feature.
8. Check if you qualify for any **subsidy or free electricity scheme** under your state government (e.g., PM Surya Ghar for solar). See our guide on [PM Surya Ghar Yojana](/guide/pm-surya-ghar-muft-bijli).

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. What if I paid the bill but it still shows unpaid?

Wait 24–48 hours. If still showing unpaid, contact your discom with the payment receipt/transaction ID. Payments via UPI usually reflect within a few hours.

### 2. Can I pay someone else's electricity bill?

Yes. You just need their **consumer number**. Anyone can pay any consumer's bill.

### 3. What happens if I don't pay my electricity bill on time?

A **late payment surcharge** (typically 1–2% per month) is applied. If bills remain unpaid for 2–3 months, your connection may be **disconnected**. Reconnection involves additional fees.

### 4. How do I apply for a new electricity connection?

Visit your local discom office or apply online through the discom website. You'll need ID proof, address proof, and ownership/rental documents. See our guide on [new electricity connection](/guide/electricity-connection-apply-online).

### 5. Can I change my electricity meter or connection name?

Yes. You need to apply at your discom office with supporting documents (sale deed, death certificate, etc.). Some discoms allow online name transfer applications.

### 6. How do I report a faulty meter?

Call your discom's helpline or use their app/website to file a complaint. A technician will be assigned to inspect and replace the meter if needed.

### 7. Is there any cashback on electricity bill payments?

UPI apps like Paytm, PhonePe, and Google Pay occasionally offer **cashback or reward points** on bill payments. Check current offers in the app before paying.
