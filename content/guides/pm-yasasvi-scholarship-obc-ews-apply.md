## What is PM YASASVI Scholarship?

**PM YASASVI (Young Achievers Scholarship Award Scheme for Vibrant India)** is a central government scholarship scheme launched by the **Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment** to support talented students from **OBC (Other Backward Classes), EWS (Economically Weaker Sections), NT (Nomadic Tribes), and DNT (De-Notified Tribes)** communities.

Unlike most scholarships that are merit-based on board marks, YASASVI requires an **entrance test (YET — YASASVI Entrance Test)** conducted by **NTA (National Testing Agency)**.

Key features:
- **₹75,000/year** for Class 9 and 10 students
- **₹1,25,000/year** for Class 11 and 12 students
- Selection via **NTA entrance test**
- Renewable each year based on class promotion
- Covers school fees, books, accommodation, and other academic expenses

## Who is Eligible?

**Category:**
- OBC (Other Backward Classes)
- EWS (Economically Weaker Sections)
- NT (Nomadic Tribes)
- DNT (De-Notified Tribes)

**Class (at time of application):**
- For ₹75,000 scholarship: **Currently in Class 8** (scholarship for Class 9)
- For ₹1,25,000 scholarship: **Currently in Class 10** (scholarship for Class 11)

**Income:**
- Annual family income must be **below ₹2.5 lakh**

**School:**
- Must be enrolled in a government school, government-aided school, or government-recognised school

## Scholarship Amount

| Student Class | Annual Scholarship |
|---|---|
| Class 9 (entry) | **₹75,000/year** |
| Class 10 (continued) | **₹75,000/year** |
| Class 11 (entry) | **₹1,25,000/year** |
| Class 12 (continued) | **₹1,25,000/year** |

Scholarship is paid via **DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer)** to the student's bank account.

## Selection Process — YASASVI Entrance Test (YET)

1. **Online registration** at [yet.nta.ac.in](https://yet.nta.ac.in)
2. Download **admit card**
3. Appear for **YET (written test)** — conducted by NTA at exam centres across India
4. YET syllabus: Mathematics, Science, Social Science (Class 7/8 level for Group 1; Class 9/10 level for Group 2)
5. Results declared on [yet.nta.ac.in](https://yet.nta.ac.in)
6. Merit list prepared — top scorers from each state/category get scholarship
7. Document verification and scholarship disbursement

## How to Apply — Step by Step

1. Go to **[yet.nta.ac.in](https://yet.nta.ac.in)** when applications open
2. Click **"New Registration"**
3. Fill in basic details → create login
4. Fill application form:
   - Personal details
   - Category (OBC/EWS/NT/DNT) + caste certificate details
   - School details (name, district, state)
   - Income details
5. Upload documents:
   - Caste/category certificate
   - Income certificate
   - School ID / enrollment proof
   - Bank account details
6. Pay application fee (if applicable — many categories are exempt or have nominal fee)
7. Download and keep the **application number**
8. Download **admit card** when released
9. Appear for the YET exam

## Documents Required

1. **Caste certificate** — OBC/NT/DNT (issued by competent authority)
   OR **EWS certificate** (for EWS applicants)
2. **Income certificate** (annual family income below ₹2.5 lakh)
3. **Aadhaar card** of student
4. **School enrollment certificate** / current class ID card
5. **Bank account** in student's name (Aadhaar-linked for DBT)
6. **Passport-size photograph**

## YET Exam Pattern

| Group | Target Students | Subjects | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Group 1 | Class 8 (for Class 9 entry) | Maths, Science, Social Science (Class 7-8) | 2.5 hours |
| Group 2 | Class 10 (for Class 11 entry) | Maths, Science, Social Science (Class 9-10) | 2.5 hours |

- Total marks: 100
- Questions: MCQ (multiple choice)
- Medium: English, Hindi, and regional languages

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is there a reserved quota within YASASVI?
Yes — within the total scholarship seats, there is reservation for women, students with disabilities, and candidates from specific sub-categories within OBC/EWS/NT/DNT.

### I am an OBC student but studying in a private unaided school. Am I eligible?
For YASASVI, the student should be in a **government, government-aided, or government-recognised school**. Unaided private schools may not qualify — check the notification for the exact current year's criteria.

### Can I apply for YASASVI if I already have a state OBC scholarship?
YASASVI is a central scheme. Receiving a state scholarship may or may not disqualify you — check the current year's notification carefully. Generally, you should not hold two scholarship amounts for the same purpose.

### When does the YET exam happen each year?
NTA typically announces YASASVI in June–July each year with exams in August–September. Check [yet.nta.ac.in](https://yet.nta.ac.in) for the current year's schedule.

### What if I score well in the test but move to a private school?
The scholarship may be cancelled if you transfer to an ineligible school. Verify the rules before transferring.

### Is the scholarship for full year or per semester?
It is an **annual** scholarship — ₹75,000 or ₹1,25,000 paid per year directly to your bank account.
