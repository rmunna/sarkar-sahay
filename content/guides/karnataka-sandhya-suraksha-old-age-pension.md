## What is Sandhya Suraksha Yojana?

**Sandhya Suraksha Yojana** is the Karnataka government's **old age pension scheme** providing **₹600 per month** to elderly BPL citizens aged 60 and above in Karnataka. The pension is paid directly to the beneficiary's bank account via DBT.

The scheme integrates the central **IGNOAPS (Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme)** contribution with state top-up:
- Central share: ₹200 (60–79 years) / ₹500 (80+ years)
- State share: Supplementing to reach ₹600/month

## Pension Amount

| Age | Monthly Pension |
|---|---|
| 60 to 79 years | **₹600/month** |
| 80 years and above | **₹600/month** (central share higher, state adjusts) |

## Who is Eligible?

- **Karnataka resident** (permanent domicile)
- **Age:** 60 years or above
- **BPL (Below Poverty Line)** — as per state BPL list
- Not receiving any other government pension (state, central, or Army)
- Annual income below ₹12,000 per year (or as per current norms)

## How to Apply

### Online via Seva Sindhu
1. Go to **[sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in)**
2. Search for **"Sandhya Suraksha"** or "Old Age Pension"
3. Click **"Apply"**
4. Log in with Aadhaar OTP or register
5. Fill application:
   - Personal details (name, DOB, address)
   - BPL ration card details
   - Bank account (Aadhaar-linked)
6. Upload documents
7. Submit → application processed by District Social Security & Pension (DSSP) office

### Offline Application
- Visit your **Gram Panchayat (rural)** or **Ward Office (urban)**
- Or apply at the **District Social Security & Pension office**
- Application form available free of cost

### Through Atalji Janasnehi Kendras
Also accessible through Karnataka's citizen service centres (Atalji Janasnehi Kendras / Bapuji Seva Kendras).

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card** (with DOB showing 60+)
2. **BPL ration card**
3. **Bank passbook** (account in applicant's name, Aadhaar-linked)
4. **Karnataka domicile proof** — Aadhaar with Karnataka address, or voter ID
5. **Age proof** — birth certificate, school certificate (if Aadhaar DOB is incorrect)
6. Passport-size photograph

## Checking Pension Status

1. Go to **[sspdkarnataka.gov.in](https://sspdkarnataka.gov.in)** (Social Security & Pension Department)
2. Click "Pension Status"
3. Enter Aadhaar or beneficiary ID
4. View payment history

OR check your bank account — monthly DBT credit.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My grandmother is 75 and BPL. Can I apply on her behalf?
Yes — you can assist her in filing the application at a Gram Panchayat or Bapuji Seva Kendra. The pension will be paid directly to her bank account.

### My father has a small pension from a private company (EPF). Does he still qualify?
EPF (EPFO pension) is a private/government pension — having it typically disqualifies from Sandhya Suraksha. Verify with the district DSSP office — the rule is specifically for government employees' pension, not all EPF withdrawals.

### The pension has not been credited for 2 months. What should I do?
Check status at [sspdkarnataka.gov.in](https://sspdkarnataka.gov.in). Common issues: Aadhaar-bank linkage failure, account dormant. Re-activate bank account and re-link Aadhaar. Contact district DSSP office for further support.

### Can I get both Sandhya Suraksha and Raitha Siri (farmer insurance)? These are different schemes.
Yes — Sandhya Suraksha (old age pension) and Raitha Siri (farmer insurance) are separate schemes serving different purposes. You can be a beneficiary of both if eligible for each.

### Does Karnataka provide additional pension for those above 75 or 80?
The base Sandhya Suraksha amount is ₹600/month. The central share increases at 80+ (IGNOAPS: ₹500 instead of ₹200), but the total stays at ₹600 because the state adjusts its share. Some years, state budget revisions may increase total pension — check [sspdkarnataka.gov.in](https://sspdkarnataka.gov.in) for current year rates.
