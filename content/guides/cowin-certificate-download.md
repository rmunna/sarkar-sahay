# COVID Vaccination Certificate Download: CoWIN & DigiLocker Guide

Your COVID-19 vaccination certificate is an official proof of vaccination issued by the Government of India through the CoWIN portal. You can download it from CoWIN or DigiLocker. This guide covers downloading certificates for all doses including booster, getting the international travel certificate, and correcting errors.

## How to Download COVID Certificate from CoWIN

### Method 1: CoWIN Portal

1. Visit [www.cowin.gov.in](https://www.cowin.gov.in/)
2. Click **"Register/Sign In"** at the top right
3. Enter your **mobile number** (the one used during vaccination registration)
4. Enter the **OTP** received on your mobile
5. You'll see your vaccination details on the dashboard
6. Click **"Certificate"** or the **download icon** next to your dose details
7. The certificate downloads as a **PDF** with a QR code for verification

### Method 2: DigiLocker

1. Visit [www.digilocker.gov.in](https://www.digilocker.gov.in/) or open the DigiLocker app
2. Log in with your **Aadhaar** or **mobile number**
3. Go to **"Issued Documents"** section
4. Look for **"COVID-19 Vaccination Certificate"** issued by MoHFW
5. If not visible, go to **"Pull Documents"** → Search for **"Ministry of Health and Family Welfare"** → Select **"COVID-19 Vaccination Certificate"**
6. Enter your **13-digit Beneficiary Reference ID** (from CoWIN) or **mobile number**
7. The certificate is fetched and stored in your DigiLocker account
8. Download or share directly from DigiLocker

For more on DigiLocker, see our [DigiLocker NAC certificate guide](/guide/digilocker-nac-certificate-download).

### Method 3: UMANG App

1. Download the **UMANG app** from Play Store or App Store
2. Search for **"CoWIN"** in the app
3. Log in with your registered mobile number
4. Download your vaccination certificate

## How to Get International Travel Certificate

For international travel, you need the **internationally verifiable certificate** with your passport details.

1. Log in to [www.cowin.gov.in](https://www.cowin.gov.in/) with your registered mobile number
2. Click on **"Raise an Issue"** at the top menu
3. Select **"International Travel Certificate"**
4. Enter your **Passport Number**, **Date of Birth**, and **Nationality**
5. Submit the request
6. Once processed, your certificate will show passport details and comply with WHO standards
7. Download the updated certificate from the **Certificate** section

**Note:** The international certificate includes a QR code that is verifiable through the International COVID-19 Vaccination Certificate verification systems.

## How to Correct Errors in COVID Certificate

If your name, date of birth, or other details are wrong on the certificate:

1. Log in to [www.cowin.gov.in](https://www.cowin.gov.in/)
2. Click **"Raise an Issue"**
3. Select the type of correction needed:
   - **Name correction**
   - **Year of birth correction**
   - **Gender correction**
4. Upload supporting documents (Aadhaar, Passport, or other government ID)
5. Submit the request
6. Corrections are typically processed within **7-15 working days**
7. Download the corrected certificate after receiving confirmation

## How to Add Booster Dose to Certificate

Your booster (precaution) dose is automatically added to your certificate after vaccination:

1. Get your booster dose at any authorized vaccination centre
2. Ensure you provide the **same mobile number** used for earlier doses
3. Wait **24-48 hours** after vaccination
4. Log in to [www.cowin.gov.in](https://www.cowin.gov.in/)
5. Your certificate will now show **all doses** including the booster/precaution dose
6. Download the updated certificate

## Important Tips

1. **Use the registered mobile number** — You can only access certificates through the mobile number used during vaccination registration
2. **Save the Beneficiary Reference ID** — This 13-digit number (visible on CoWIN dashboard) is useful for pulling certificates on DigiLocker
3. **Certificate has QR code** — The QR code on the certificate can be scanned for verification; ensure it's readable when printing
4. **DigiLocker copy is legally valid** — The DigiLocker version is as valid as the original under the IT Act
5. **Keep a downloaded copy** — Save a PDF copy on your phone and email for easy access during travel

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I lost my registered mobile number. How do I download my certificate?
You can visit the vaccination centre where you got vaccinated and request them to update your mobile number in the CoWIN system. Alternatively, raise a request through the **CoWIN helpline at 1075**.

### Can I download the certificate without the CoWIN app?
Yes. CoWIN does not have an official app. You download certificates through the **CoWIN website** (cowin.gov.in), **DigiLocker**, or the **UMANG app**. No separate CoWIN app is needed.

### My certificate shows the wrong vaccine name. How to fix it?
Log in to CoWIN → **Raise an Issue** → Select **vaccine name correction**. This needs to be verified by the vaccination centre, so it may take 7-15 days.

### Is the COVID certificate still required for domestic travel in India?
As of the latest guidelines, COVID vaccination certificates are **not mandatory** for domestic travel within India. However, some international destinations may still require them. Check your destination country's requirements.

### Can I get a COVID certificate if I was vaccinated abroad?
Vaccinations administered outside India are **not reflected on CoWIN**. You would need to use the certificate issued by the country where you were vaccinated. Some countries have mutual recognition agreements with India.

### How do I verify if my certificate QR code is authentic?
Scan the QR code using any QR scanner — it will redirect to the official verification page showing your vaccination details. This is the same verification used at airports and international checkpoints.
