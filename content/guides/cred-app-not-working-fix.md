# CRED App Not Working — How to Fix Payment, Credit Score & Cashback Issues

CRED is a popular app for paying credit card bills, checking credit scores, and earning rewards. But users frequently face issues like payment failures, money deducted but bill not updated, wrong credit scores, and missing cashback. This troubleshooting guide covers all common CRED problems and how to fix them.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent informational platform and is not affiliated with CRED or any financial institution. Always verify details on the official CRED app or website.

---

## 1. CRED Credit Card Bill Payment Failed

This is the most common CRED complaint — you try to pay your credit card bill, but the transaction fails.

### Common Causes
- Bank server downtime or maintenance window
- Insufficient balance in the source account
- UPI daily transaction limit exceeded (₹1 lakh per day for most banks)
- CRED app version outdated
- Internet connectivity issues

### How to Fix

1. **Check your bank balance** — Ensure you have enough funds in the linked bank account, including any processing charges.
2. **Try a different payment method** — Switch between UPI, net banking, or another linked bank account.
3. **Check UPI limits** — Most banks cap UPI at ₹1 lakh/day. If you've done other transactions, the remaining limit may be insufficient.
4. **Update the app** — Go to Play Store / App Store and update CRED to the latest version.
5. **Clear app cache** — Go to phone Settings → Apps → CRED → Clear Cache. Then retry.
6. **Try during non-peak hours** — Bank servers are often busy between 8–10 AM and 6–9 PM. Try early morning or late night.
7. **Check bank app** — Open your bank's own app to see if UPI services are working. Some banks have scheduled maintenance windows.
8. **Wait and retry** — If the issue is on the bank's side, wait 30 minutes and try again.

> **Tip:** Always pay your credit card bill at least 2–3 days before the due date to account for any processing delays or failures.

## 2. Money Debited but Bill Not Paid — How to Get a Refund

Your bank account was debited, but CRED shows the bill as unpaid. This is a stressful situation but usually resolves automatically.

### What Happens

When money is debited but the transaction doesn't complete on the biller's end, it's called a "failed transaction with debit." RBI mandates auto-reversal within 5 business days.

### Steps to Resolve

1. **Wait 24–48 hours** — Most failed transactions auto-reverse. Check your bank statement after 1–2 business days.
2. **Check transaction status in CRED** — Go to CRED → Pay → Transaction History. Look for the specific transaction and check its status.
3. **Raise a complaint in CRED** — Go to CRED → Profile → Help → select the transaction → "Payment debited but bill not paid" → Submit.
4. **Contact your bank** — If CRED shows the transaction as failed but money hasn't returned after 5 business days, call your bank's customer care with the UTR/transaction reference number.
5. **File a UPI complaint on NPCI** — Visit [NPCI's dispute portal](https://www.npci.org.in/what-we-do/upi/dispute-redressal-mechanism) if the bank doesn't resolve within 15 days.
6. **RBI Ombudsman** — As a last resort, file a complaint with [RBI's Integrated Ombudsman](https://cms.rbi.org.in/) if unresolved beyond 30 days.

> **Important:** Keep screenshots of the CRED transaction page and bank SMS/statement as proof.

## 3. CRED Credit Score Not Loading or Showing Wrong Score

CRED shows your credit score (sourced from credit bureaus like Experian, CIBIL, etc.), but sometimes it doesn't load or displays an incorrect score.

### Why This Happens
- Credit bureau data refresh delays (scores update monthly, not real-time)
- New credit activity not yet reported by your bank
- CRED fetches from Experian by default — your CIBIL score may differ
- App cache showing stale data

### How to Fix

1. **Pull to refresh** — Open the credit score section and pull down to refresh.
2. **Clear cache** — Settings → Apps → CRED → Clear Cache, then reopen the score section.
3. **Understand the source** — CRED uses Experian. Your CIBIL score (from TransUnion) may be different. Both are valid — different bureaus, different algorithms.
4. **Wait for the monthly update** — Credit scores refresh once a month on CRED. A payment made today won't reflect for 30–45 days.
5. **Check directly with the bureau** — Visit [cibil.com](https://www.cibil.com/) for your free annual CIBIL report, or use [Experian](https://www.experian.in/) for the score CRED uses.
6. **Dispute errors** — If there's genuinely wrong information (a loan you didn't take, wrong balance), raise a dispute directly with the credit bureau, not CRED. CRED only displays the score — it doesn't control it.

> **Note:** A 20–50 point difference between Experian and CIBIL scores is normal.

## 4. CRED Coins / Cashback Not Credited

You paid your bill on CRED but didn't receive the promised coins, cashback, or rewards.

### Common Reasons
- Rewards are credited after the payment is confirmed (24–72 hours)
- The offer had specific conditions you didn't meet (minimum amount, specific card, first-time only)
- CRED coins and cashback are different — coins go to CRED's rewards store, cashback goes to your bank/CRED balance
- Promotional offers may have expired

### Steps to Resolve

1. **Wait 72 hours** — Rewards are usually credited within 1–3 business days after successful payment confirmation.
2. **Check the offer terms** — Go to the offer details and verify eligibility conditions (minimum bill amount, card type, new user only, etc.).
3. **Check CRED coins balance** — Open CRED → Rewards/Coins section. Your coins may already be there but not notified.
4. **Contact support** — CRED → Profile → Help → "Cashback/Rewards" → describe the issue with the transaction date and amount.
5. **Keep proof** — Screenshot the offer terms before claiming, as offers change frequently.

> **Tip:** CRED coins are not real money — they're redeemable only in CRED's store. Cashback offers are separate and usually credited to your CRED cash balance.

## 5. "Credit Card Not Supported" Error on CRED

You're trying to add your credit card but CRED says it's not supported.

### Why This Happens
- CRED requires a credit score of 750+ for membership (though this has relaxed over time)
- Some co-branded or store cards may not be supported
- Corporate/business credit cards are often not accepted
- Newly issued cards may take a few days to appear in bureau records

### How to Fix

1. **Check eligibility** — CRED requires a minimum credit score. If you're new to credit, your score may be below the threshold.
2. **Wait for bureau update** — If you just got a new card, wait 30–45 days for the bureau to reflect it, then try adding again.
3. **Try the full card number** — Enter the 16-digit card number manually instead of using any scan feature.
4. **Check card type** — Corporate cards, prepaid cards, and some co-branded store cards are not supported.
5. **Contact CRED support** — If your card is a regular bank-issued credit card and still not accepted, raise a ticket via CRED → Profile → Help.

## 6. Auto-Pay Setup Failed on CRED

CRED's auto-pay feature lets you automatically pay bills on the due date, but setup can fail.

### Common Causes
- Bank doesn't support e-mandate/NACH for the chosen method
- UPI auto-pay limit exceeded (UPI auto-pay cap is ₹1 lakh for most categories)
- Your bank's UPI app rejected the mandate request
- Incorrect UPI PIN entered during mandate registration

### How to Fix

1. **Check your bank's UPI app** — After setting up auto-pay on CRED, you may need to approve the mandate in your bank's UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, or bank app).
2. **Try a different bank account** — Some banks have better e-mandate support than others.
3. **Set a specific amount** — Instead of "total outstanding," try setting a fixed maximum amount.
4. **Remove and re-add** — Go to CRED → Auto-pay settings → Remove the existing setup → Set up fresh.
5. **Check with your bank** — Some banks require you to enable e-mandate from their internet banking portal first.

> **Note:** Auto-pay will fail if your bank balance is insufficient on the execution date. Keep enough balance 1 day before.

## 7. CRED UPI (CRED Pay) Transaction Errors

CRED Pay lets you use CRED as a UPI app for merchant payments. Transaction failures here are similar to regular UPI issues.

### Common Errors
- "Transaction declined by bank"
- "UPI PIN is incorrect"
- "Beneficiary bank is not available"
- "Transaction timeout"

### How to Fix

1. **Verify UPI PIN** — If you've recently changed your UPI PIN in another app, CRED's PIN also changes (it's the same PIN per bank).
2. **Check daily limits** — UPI has a ₹1 lakh daily limit. CRED Pay transactions count toward this.
3. **Try after some time** — "Beneficiary bank not available" means the merchant's bank is down. Wait and retry.
4. **Reset UPI on CRED** — CRED → UPI settings → Delink bank → Re-link with correct details.
5. **Check bank account status** — Ensure your linked bank account is active and KYC-compliant.
6. **For merchant-specific failures** — Some merchants may not accept CRED Pay. Try the merchant's own payment page or another UPI app.

## 8. CRED Mint / Personal Loan Issues

CRED Mint offers personal loans and credit lines. Common issues include loan disbursal delays and EMI payment failures.

### Common Issues and Fixes

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Loan approved but not disbursed | Wait 2–3 business days. Check your registered bank account. Contact CRED support with the loan ID. |
| EMI auto-debit failed | Ensure sufficient balance. Check if NACH mandate is active. Manually pay the EMI from CRED. |
| Higher interest rate than shown | The rate shown is indicative. Final rate depends on your profile. Check the loan agreement document. |
| Prepayment/foreclosure | Go to CRED → Mint → Active loan → Prepay option. Some loans have a lock-in period. |
| Loan not showing in CIBIL | CRED's lending partners report to bureaus. It may take 30–60 days to reflect. |

> **Important:** CRED Mint loans are offered by RBI-regulated NBFCs/banks through CRED's platform. For disputes about loan terms, contact the specific lender mentioned in your loan agreement.

## 9. How to Delete Your CRED Account

If you want to permanently delete your CRED account and all associated data:

### Steps to Delete

1. Open the **CRED app**.
2. Go to **Profile** (top-left icon).
3. Tap **Help & Support**.
4. Search for **"Delete account"**.
5. Select **"I want to delete my account"**.
6. Follow the on-screen prompts to confirm deletion.

### What Happens After Deletion
- All your CRED coins, rewards, and cashback balance will be forfeited.
- Linked cards and bank accounts will be removed.
- Any active auto-pay mandates should be cancelled separately with your bank.
- Active CRED Mint loans will NOT be cancelled — you must continue EMI payments to the lending partner.
- Account data is deleted as per CRED's privacy policy (typically within 90 days).

> **Alternative:** If you just want to stop using CRED without deleting data, simply unlink your cards and disable auto-pay.

## General Troubleshooting Tips for CRED

1. **Always keep the app updated** — Most bugs are fixed in newer versions.
2. **Use a stable internet connection** — Switch between WiFi and mobile data if transactions fail.
3. **Don't use VPN** — CRED may block transactions if a VPN is active.
4. **Check CRED's status page** — If multiple features are down, it may be a server-side issue. Check [CRED's Twitter/X](https://twitter.com/CABORNEINCRED) or [Downdetector](https://downdetector.in/status/cred/).
5. **Document everything** — Take screenshots of errors, transaction IDs, and timestamps before contacting support.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why did my CRED payment fail but money was deducted?
This happens when the bank debits the amount but the transaction doesn't complete on the biller's side. The money is usually auto-reversed within 3–5 business days. Check your CRED transaction history and bank statement. If not refunded within 5 days, raise a complaint in the CRED app.

### Is the credit score shown on CRED accurate?
CRED fetches your score from Experian, which is a legitimate credit bureau. However, it may differ from your CIBIL (TransUnion) score by 20–50 points. Both are valid — lenders use different bureaus for different products.

### Why are my CRED coins not showing after bill payment?
CRED coins are credited within 1–3 business days after payment confirmation. If they don't appear after 72 hours, contact CRED support through the app with your transaction details.

### Can I use CRED with a debit card?
No. CRED is specifically designed for credit card bill payments. You cannot add debit cards for bill payment. However, you can pay your credit card bill using money from your bank account (linked via UPI or net banking).

### How long does a CRED refund take?
For failed transactions where money was debited, auto-reversal typically happens within 3–5 business days as per RBI guidelines. If paying through UPI, the refund follows NPCI's dispute resolution timeline.

### Why does CRED say my credit card is not supported?
CRED has eligibility criteria based on your credit score and card type. Corporate cards, prepaid cards, and some co-branded cards may not be supported. New credit card holders should wait 30–45 days for bureau data to update.

### Is it safe to link my credit card on CRED?
CRED is PCI-DSS compliant and uses bank-grade encryption. However, as with any fintech app, only link cards you actively want to manage through CRED. You can always remove cards from the app.

### How do I cancel CRED auto-pay?
Open CRED → go to the card with auto-pay enabled → Auto-pay settings → Cancel/Remove. Also check your bank's UPI app to ensure the e-mandate is revoked from that end too.
