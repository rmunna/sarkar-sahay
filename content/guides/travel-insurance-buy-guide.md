# Travel Insurance: How to Buy & Best Plans in India

Travel insurance protects you against unexpected events during international or domestic trips — medical emergencies, trip cancellation, lost baggage, and more. For **Schengen visa** applicants, travel insurance is **mandatory**. This guide covers what to look for, how to buy, claim process, and comparison tips.

## What is Travel Insurance?

Travel insurance is a policy that covers financial losses or medical expenses incurred during a trip. It typically covers:

- Medical emergencies and hospitalization abroad
- Trip cancellation or interruption
- Lost or delayed baggage
- Passport loss
- Personal accident or death
- Flight delays

> If you're applying for a [Schengen visa](/guide/visa-apply-online-guide), travel insurance with minimum **€30,000** medical coverage is mandatory.

---

## Types of Travel Insurance Coverage

| Coverage Type | What It Covers |
|---------------|----------------|
| **Medical Emergency** | Hospitalization, surgery, doctor visits abroad |
| **Medical Evacuation** | Emergency transport to nearest hospital or repatriation |
| **Trip Cancellation** | Non-refundable expenses if trip is cancelled due to covered reasons |
| **Trip Interruption** | Costs if you must return early due to emergency |
| **Baggage Loss/Delay** | Compensation for lost, stolen, or delayed luggage |
| **Flight Delay** | Meals and accommodation if flight is delayed beyond specified hours |
| **Passport Loss** | Cost of obtaining emergency travel documents |
| **Personal Accident** | Lump sum for accidental death or disability during travel |
| **Personal Liability** | If you accidentally cause injury/damage to others |
| **Hijack Cover** | Compensation for each hour of hijack |

## Schengen Travel Insurance Requirements

For Schengen visa applications, your travel insurance **must** meet these criteria:

1. **Minimum coverage:** €30,000 (approximately ₹27 lakh)
2. **Valid in all 29 Schengen countries**
3. **Cover medical emergencies** including hospitalization and repatriation
4. **Cover the entire duration** of stay plus a few buffer days
5. **No deductible** (some embassies prefer zero-deductible policies)
6. Must be from a **recognized insurance company**

> Many Indian insurers offer Schengen-specific plans. Always verify the policy meets embassy requirements before applying for your visa.

## How to Buy Travel Insurance Online

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Compare plans** on aggregator websites or directly on insurer websites
2. **Select destination and travel dates** — this determines the plan options
3. **Choose coverage amount** — minimum €30,000 for Schengen; $50,000–$100,000 recommended for US
4. **Select plan type** — Individual, Family, Student, Senior Citizen, or Multi-trip
5. **Review coverage details** — check inclusions, exclusions, sub-limits, and deductibles
6. **Fill traveller details** — name (as on passport), date of birth, passport number
7. **Pay online** — credit/debit card, net banking, or UPI
8. **Download policy document** — you'll receive it instantly via email

### Where to Buy
- **Directly from insurers:** ICICI Lombard, Bajaj Allianz, HDFC ERGO, TATA AIG, Star Health
- **Aggregator websites:** Policybazaar, Coverfox, InsuranceDekho
- **Bank/credit card portals** — some banks offer complimentary travel insurance with premium credit cards

## Family Travel Insurance Plans

Family plans cover **all family members** under a single policy, often at a lower per-person cost than individual plans.

### Typical Family Plan Features
- Covers 2 adults + up to 2–3 children
- Sum insured shared (floater) or individual
- Children typically covered up to age 18–25
- Some plans cover dependent parents

### Benefits of Family Plans
- **Lower cost** compared to separate individual policies
- **Single policy management** — one document, one claim process
- **Floater sum insured** — higher coverage available for whoever needs it

## How to Claim Travel Insurance

### During Emergency (Cashless)
1. **Call the insurer's 24x7 helpline** (number on your policy card)
2. **Request cashless hospitalization** at a network hospital
3. **Share policy details** and hospital information
4. **Insurer coordinates** directly with the hospital for payment
5. **Keep all documents** — admission papers, discharge summary, bills

### After Returning (Reimbursement)
1. **Inform the insurer** within the specified time (usually 7–30 days)
2. **Gather documents:**
   - Original medical bills and receipts
   - Doctor's prescription and diagnosis report
   - Hospital discharge summary
   - FIR copy (for theft/loss claims)
   - Flight delay certificate from airline (for delay claims)
   - Passport with entry/exit stamps
3. **Fill claim form** on insurer's website or app
4. **Upload/submit documents**
5. **Claim processed** typically within 15–30 days

## Tips to Compare and Choose the Best Plan

1. **Check the sum insured** — ₹50 lakh+ for US/Europe trips; €30,000 minimum for Schengen
2. **Read exclusions carefully** — pre-existing diseases, adventure sports, and alcohol-related incidents are commonly excluded
3. **Check sub-limits** — some plans cap specific benefits (e.g., baggage loss limited to ₹25,000 even if total sum insured is ₹50 lakh)
4. **Look for zero deductible** — plans without deductible mean the insurer pays from the first dollar/rupee
5. **24x7 assistance** — ensure the insurer provides round-the-clock helpline and cashless hospital network
6. **Check claim settlement ratio** — choose insurers with 90%+ claim settlement ratio
7. **Read reviews** — check actual claim experiences, not just marketing promises

## Important Tips

1. **Buy before visa application** — For Schengen, you need the policy document at the time of visa submission
2. **Coverage dates matter** — Your policy must cover the entire trip duration including travel days
3. **Declare pre-existing conditions** — Not declaring can lead to claim rejection
4. **Keep digital copies** — Save your policy document, insurer helpline number, and claim form on your phone
5. **Check credit card coverage** — Some premium credit cards include complimentary travel insurance; verify the coverage amount and terms

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Is travel insurance mandatory for international travel?
It is **mandatory for Schengen visa** applications. For other countries, it is not mandatory but highly recommended.

### 2. How much travel insurance coverage do I need?
For Schengen: minimum **€30,000**. For US: at least **$50,000** recommended due to high medical costs. For Southeast Asia: ₹10–25 lakh is usually sufficient.

### 3. Does travel insurance cover COVID-19?
Most current travel insurance plans cover COVID-19 treatment abroad. Check the specific policy wording to confirm.

### 4. Can I buy travel insurance after reaching the destination?
Technically some plans allow it, but it's **not recommended**. Buy before departure to ensure coverage from day one. Schengen requires it before visa application.

### 5. What is not covered by travel insurance?
Common exclusions: pre-existing diseases, self-inflicted injuries, adventure sports (unless add-on purchased), war/terrorism, alcohol/drug-related incidents, and travelling against medical advice.

### 6. Can I cancel travel insurance and get a refund?
Yes, most insurers offer a **free-look period** (15–30 days) during which you can cancel and get a full refund if the trip hasn't started and no claim has been made.

### 7. Is domestic travel insurance available?
Yes. Several insurers offer domestic travel insurance covering medical emergencies, trip cancellation, and baggage loss within India, though it's less common.
