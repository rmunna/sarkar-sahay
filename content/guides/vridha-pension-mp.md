# MP Vridha Pension Yojana: Old Age Pension Eligibility, Amount & How to Apply

The Madhya Pradesh Vridha Pension Yojana provides monthly financial assistance to senior citizens belonging to economically weaker sections. Administered through the Social Security Pension Portal, this scheme ensures that elderly citizens of MP receive regular pension support for a dignified life.

---

## What is MP Vridha Pension Yojana?

MP Vridha Pension Yojana is the state implementation of pension schemes for senior citizens, combining the **Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS)** and the state's own old age pension provisions. The scheme provides monthly pension to BPL senior citizens aged 60 years and above through the Social Justice Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh.

---

## Pension Amount

| Age Group | Monthly Pension |
|-----------|----------------|
| 60-79 years | Rs 600 per month (Rs 300 Central + Rs 300 State) |
| 80 years and above | Rs 800 per month (Rs 500 Central + Rs 300 State) |

*Note: Amounts may vary based on latest government notifications.*

---

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Age:** Must be 60 years or above
2. **Residency:** Must be a permanent resident of Madhya Pradesh
3. **BPL status:** Must be a BPL card holder or from an economically weaker family
4. **Samagra ID:** Must have a valid Samagra ID
5. **Not receiving other pension:** Should not be receiving pension from any other government scheme or employment
6. **Not an income tax payer**

---

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- **Samagra ID** (family and member ID)
- **Age proof** (birth certificate, school certificate, or Aadhaar)
- **BPL ration card**
- **Bank account passbook** with IFSC code
- **Passport-size photograph**
- **Residence proof** (voter ID or domicile certificate)
- **Self-declaration** of not receiving any other pension

## How to Apply: Step-by-Step Process

### Online Application

1. Visit the MP Social Security Portal at [socialsecurity.mp.gov.in](https://socialsecurity.mp.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"पेंशन योजनाओं हेतु ऑनलाइन आवेदन"** (Online Application for Pension Schemes)
3. Select **Vridha Pension / Old Age Pension** scheme
4. Enter your **Samagra Member ID**
5. Verify your details and fill in any additional information
6. Upload required documents
7. Submit the application
8. Note down the application reference number

### Offline Application

1. Visit your **Gram Panchayat** (rural) or **Nagar Palika/Municipal office** (urban)
2. Collect the pension application form
3. Fill in all details with supporting documents
4. Submit the form to the local body
5. The application will be verified and forwarded to the District Social Justice office
6. Upon approval, pension will start from the next disbursement cycle

### Via MP e-District Portal

1. Visit [mpedistrict.gov.in](https://mpedistrict.gov.in/)
2. Register/login with your credentials
3. Navigate to Social Security Pension section
4. Select Vridha Pension and fill the application
5. Submit with required documents

---

## Processing Time

- **Verification:** 30-45 days
- **Approval:** 30-60 days after verification
- **First pension:** Within the next quarterly disbursement cycle after approval

---

## How to Check Pension Status

1. Visit [socialsecurity.mp.gov.in](https://socialsecurity.mp.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"पेंशन की स्वीकृति की स्थिति"** (Pension Approval Status)
3. Enter your Samagra ID or application number
4. View approval status and payment history

## Important Tips

1. **Samagra ID is mandatory** — Get your Samagra ID first if you don't have one
2. **Bank account must be in beneficiary's name** — Joint accounts are not accepted
3. **Annual verification** — Some local bodies require annual life verification (Jeevan Pramaan)
4. **Report death promptly** — Family must inform authorities to avoid recovery of excess pension
5. **e-KYC helps** — Complete Aadhaar-based e-KYC for faster processing

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. How much pension do senior citizens get in MP?
Senior citizens aged 60-79 get Rs 600/month and those aged 80+ get Rs 800/month.

### 2. Can I apply if I don't have a BPL card?
The scheme primarily targets BPL families. However, certain categories of destitute elderly may be considered at the discretion of local authorities.

### 3. Is Samagra ID mandatory?
Yes, Samagra ID is mandatory for all social security pension schemes in Madhya Pradesh.

### 4. Can I receive both Vridha Pension and Vidhwa Pension?
No, a person can receive pension under only one scheme at a time.

### 5. How is the pension paid?
Pension is transferred directly to the beneficiary's bank account via DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer).

### 6. What if my pension is stopped?
Visit the local Gram Panchayat or Municipal office to check the reason. Common reasons include incomplete e-KYC or bank account issues.

### 7. Can government employees apply?
No, retired government employees receiving pension are not eligible for this scheme.
