# Delhi Income Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **income certificate** in Delhi is issued by the Revenue Department (SDM office) and is required for EWS reservations, Delhi scholarships, fee waivers in government colleges, and various government schemes. Applications are processed through **e-District Delhi** at [edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in](https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in).

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in](https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in) under Revenue Department → Income Certificate. Verified by Tehsildar/SDM within **7–21 days**.

---

## Why You Need Delhi Income Certificate

- **Delhi Government Scholarship** (SC/ST/OBC/Minority) — income proof
- **EWS (Economically Weaker Section)** reservation in Delhi schools/colleges
- **NSP National Scholarship** — mandatory income certificate
- **Government hospital fee waiver** — Delhi government hospitals
- **DDA and housing scheme** eligibility (income-based)
- **Legal affidavits** and court submissions

---

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory — Delhi address preferred)
- **Voter ID with Delhi address** (important for Delhi residence proof)
- Ration card (if BPL/Delhi ration card holder)
- Self-declaration of income (online declaration or affidavit on stamp paper)
- For salaried: **salary slip** or employer certificate
- Passport-size photograph
- Proof of residence in Delhi (electricity bill, rent agreement)

---

## How to Apply Online — e-District Delhi

1. Go to [edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in](https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in)
2. Click **"Register"** if new user → Register with mobile number
3. Log in → click **"Revenue Department"**
4. Click **"Apply for Income Certificate"**
5. Fill form:
   - Name, address in Delhi (district, sub-division, ward)
   - Annual family income from all sources
   - Purpose of income certificate
6. Upload documents (Aadhaar, voter ID, income proof)
7. Submit (fee may apply online — ₹10–30)
8. Note the **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply at SDM (Sub-Divisional Magistrate) Office

1. Visit your area's **SDM office** (Sub-Divisional Magistrate)
2. Collect income certificate form
3. Submit with Aadhaar, voter ID, and income declaration
4. Tehsildar/Revenue Inspector verifies
5. SDM approves and issues certificate

---

## Status Check

1. Go to [edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in](https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in)
2. Click **"Track Application"** → enter application number
3. Or log in → **"My Applications"** → view status
4. Status: Applied → Verification → SDM Approval → Issued

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My Aadhaar shows another state address but I live in Delhi. Can I get Delhi income certificate?

Yes — you can apply with **Delhi Voter ID + Electricity Bill / Rent Agreement** as residence proof, even if Aadhaar shows another state. The SDM verifies current Delhi residence.

### EWS certificate vs income certificate Delhi — same?

They're different. **Income certificate** proves annual income. **EWS certificate** certifies that annual family income is below ₹8 lakh AND you don't own property above certain limits. EWS certificate requires income certificate as a supporting document.
