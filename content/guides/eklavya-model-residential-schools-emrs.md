## What is an Eklavya Model Residential School (EMRS)?

Eklavya Model Residential Schools (EMRS) are government-funded residential schools exclusively for **Scheduled Tribe (ST) students**, providing **free quality education from Class 6 to Class 12** including accommodation, food, uniforms, books, and all other facilities. The scheme is run by the **Ministry of Tribal Affairs** through the **National Education Society for Tribal Students (NESTS)**.

The goal is to bring quality education on par with Navodaya Vidyalayas to tribal students in remote areas.

## Who is Eligible?

**You ARE eligible if:**
- You belong to a **Scheduled Tribe (ST)** community
- You are applying for **Class 6 admission** (entry point)
- You have passed **Class 5 from a recognized school** in the same block/district
- You come from the **tribal sub-plan area** or block where the EMRS is located (priority to local tribal areas)
- Your family's **annual income is below ₹2 lakh** (preference to lower income — check specific school's criteria)

**Age limit:** Usually 10–12 years at the time of Class 6 admission.

**Lateral entry:** Some EMRS schools allow Class 7–9 lateral admissions if seats are vacant — check with the specific school.

## Documents Required

1. **Caste (Tribe) certificate** — ST certificate issued by competent authority
2. **Class 5 marksheet** and school leaving certificate
3. **Date of birth proof** (birth certificate or Aadhaar)
4. **Domicile/residence proof** — of the block/district
5. **Income certificate** — issued by tahsildar
6. **Aadhaar Card** — child and parent
7. **Passport-size photographs**

## How to Apply — Step by Step

### Step 1 — Find Your Nearest EMRS

EMRS schools are set up in blocks with ≥50% ST population. Find EMRS in your area:
- Visit **[emrs.tribal.gov.in](https://emrs.tribal.gov.in)** → School List
- Contact your **District Tribal Welfare Officer**

### Step 2 — Get the Admission Form

- Download from **[emrs.tribal.gov.in](https://emrs.tribal.gov.in)**
- Or collect from the EMRS school directly
- Or from the District Tribal Welfare Office

### Step 3 — Fill and Submit

Submit the completed form with all documents to:
- The EMRS Principal, OR
- District Tribal Welfare Officer

### Step 4 — Entrance Test

EMRS conducts a **written entrance examination** — covering:
- Mathematics
- Language (Hindi / regional)
- General Knowledge/Environment

Top-scoring eligible ST students are admitted based on merit.

### Step 5 — Merit List and Admission

A merit list is published. Selected students and parents are called for document verification and admission formalities.

## NESTS — Centralized Management

From 2018–19, EMRS schools are managed centrally by the **National Education Society for Tribal Students (NESTS)**. NESTS standardized curriculum, teacher recruitment, and quality assurance across all EMRS schools.

Check school-specific admission notices at: [emrs.tribal.gov.in](https://emrs.tribal.gov.in)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. Is there an EMRS in every district?**

The government aims to have one EMRS in every block with ≥50% ST population. By 2026, over 700 EMRS schools are planned across India. Check emrs.tribal.gov.in for the current list.

**2. What class does EMRS start admission for?**

The primary entry point is **Class 6**. Some schools also have Class 9 entry if seats are available.

**3. My daughter is ST but we live in a non-tribal area. Is she eligible?**

Eligibility typically requires that the student's **school** (Class 5) was in the block or tribal sub-plan area of that EMRS. Students from non-tribal urban areas generally are not the target — but contact the District Tribal Welfare Officer to confirm.

**4. Is there an EMRS entrance exam preparation guide?**

The entrance exam covers Class 4–5 level Math, Language, and GK. Standard Class 5 NCERT textbooks are sufficient preparation material. Some states provide sample question papers.

**5. Can students from other states join an EMRS?**

Generally, EMRS schools admit local ST students from the same state. Inter-state admission is not typically allowed except in special circumstances.
