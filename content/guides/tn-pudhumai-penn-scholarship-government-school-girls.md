## What is Pudhumai Penn Scholarship?

**Pudhumai Penn** — meaning "Modern Woman" in Tamil — is the Tamil Nadu government's scholarship for **girls who studied in government schools** and are now pursuing undergraduate education (degree/diploma/professional courses). The scheme provides **₹1,000 per month** directly to their bank account to support higher education.

Launched in 2023, this is Tamil Nadu's signature scheme to encourage government school girl students (who are often from lower-income families) to pursue higher education.

## Scholarship Amount

| Benefit | Amount |
|---|---|
| Monthly stipend | **₹1,000/month** |
| Duration | For the entire duration of the degree/diploma course |
| Annual total | ₹12,000/year |
| Payment | Directly to student's bank account (DBT) |

## Who is Eligible?

- **Must be a girl student**
- Must have studied in a **Tamil Nadu government school** (Classes 6–12) — not private or aided school
- **Currently pursuing** UG degree, diploma, or professional course (engineering, medicine, arts, science, commerce)
- Institution must be a **government or government-aided college** in Tamil Nadu
- **No income limit** — all government school girl graduates qualify

**Key criterion:** Studying in government school **from at least Class 6 to 12** (or part thereof as specified in current rules). Check [pudhumaipen.tn.gov.in](https://pudhumaipen.tn.gov.in) for current eligibility details.

## How to Apply

1. Go to **[pudhumaipen.tn.gov.in](https://pudhumaipen.tn.gov.in)**
2. Click **"Apply"** or **"New Registration"**
3. Register with Aadhaar + mobile OTP
4. Fill application:
   - Personal details (name, DOB, Aadhaar)
   - School details (government school, class studied from)
   - Current college and course details
   - Bank account details (student's own account)
5. Upload documents
6. Submit → verified by college

**Also available through:** Direct application at your college's scholarship coordinator.

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card**
2. **Transfer Certificate (TC)** from Class 12 — showing government school
3. **Class 12 marksheet** (SSLC or HSC)
4. **College admission proof** (current year enrollment)
5. **Bank account passbook** (student's own, Aadhaar-linked)
6. Passport-size photograph

## Courses Covered

- **Arts, Science, Commerce** — BA, BSc, BCom, BBA, BCA
- **Engineering** — BE/BTech
- **Medical/Allied Health** — MBBS, BDS, Nursing, Physiotherapy
- **Polytechnic Diploma** — government polytechnics
- **ITI** courses (government institutions)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I studied in a government-aided school, not a pure government school. Am I eligible?
Pudhumai Penn requires studying in **government schools** (schools run and funded directly by Tamil Nadu state government), not government-aided private schools. If your school was a government school, you qualify. If it was aided (partially government-funded but private management), check the portal — criteria may vary.

### What if I change my college course midway? Do I still get the scholarship?
If you continue in an eligible college in Tamil Nadu, the scholarship generally continues. Report any course/college change to the scholarship authorities through the portal.

### Can I apply retroactively for missed months (if I didn't know about the scheme)?
Retroactive application for past months may not be possible. Apply immediately — you'll receive from the month of approval going forward.

### Is Pudhumai Penn different from the Tamil Nadu Government's other girl scholarships?
Yes — Pudhumai Penn is specifically for girls from government schools. Other TN scholarships (like SC/ST post-matric) have different eligibility. You may be eligible for both simultaneously if you meet criteria for each.

### I am pursuing MA (postgraduate). Am I eligible?
Pudhumai Penn is for **undergraduate (UG) courses** primarily. Check the current scheme guidelines for PG eligibility at [pudhumaipen.tn.gov.in](https://pudhumaipen.tn.gov.in).
