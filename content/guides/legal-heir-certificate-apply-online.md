## What is a Legal Heir Certificate?

A legal heir certificate is an official document issued by a state government authority (Revenue Department or Tehsildar office) that identifies all the surviving legal heirs of a deceased person. It establishes the relationship between the deceased and their heirs, confirming who is legally entitled to the deceased person's assets and benefits.

This certificate is commonly required to claim the deceased person's pending dues such as bank deposits, insurance amounts, pension arrears, provident fund, gratuity, and other government or private benefits.

The certificate is issued under the respective state revenue rules and is typically valid for claiming movable assets and government dues — not for immutable property disputes, which usually require a [succession certificate](/guide/succession-certificate-apply-online).

## Legal Heir Certificate vs Succession Certificate

Many people confuse these two documents. Here's how they differ:

| Feature | Legal Heir Certificate | Succession Certificate |
|---|---|---|
| **Issued by** | Tehsildar / Revenue authority | District Court |
| **Purpose** | Identify legal heirs, claim movable assets & dues | Establish right to inherit debts & securities |
| **Applicable law** | State revenue rules | Indian Succession Act, 1925 |
| **Processing time** | 15–30 days | 3–6 months |
| **Cost** | ₹50–₹200 (nominal fees) | Court fees (varies by claim amount) |
| **Property type** | Movable assets, government dues | Debts, securities, immovable property |
| **Complexity** | Simple administrative process | Court proceedings required |

**When to use which:** If you need to claim a deceased family member's bank balance, insurance, PF, or pension, a legal heir certificate is usually sufficient. For transferring immovable property or securities of high value, you may need a [succession certificate](/guide/succession-certificate-apply-online).

## Who Can Apply for a Legal Heir Certificate?

Any surviving legal heir of the deceased can apply. The following are recognised as legal heirs:

- **Spouse** — Husband or wife of the deceased
- **Children** — Sons and daughters (including legally adopted children)
- **Parents** — Father and mother of the deceased
- **Siblings** — Brothers and sisters (if no spouse, children, or parents survive)
- **Dependents** — Other persons who were legally dependent on the deceased

The applicant must be one of the legal heirs and must declare the complete list of all surviving heirs in the application. Concealing any legal heir is a punishable offence.

## Documents Required for Legal Heir Certificate

Keep the following documents ready before applying:

### Mandatory Documents

- **Death certificate** of the deceased — [How to apply for death certificate](/guide/death-certificate-apply-online)
- **Identity proof of applicant** — Aadhaar card, voter ID, or passport
- **Address proof of applicant** — Aadhaar, utility bill, ration card, or [domicile certificate](/guide/domicile-certificate-online)
- **Identity proof of deceased** — Aadhaar card, voter ID, or PAN card
- **Relationship proof** — Ration card, family register, marriage certificate, or birth certificates showing relationship with the deceased
- **Affidavit / Self-declaration** — Sworn affidavit listing all legal heirs of the deceased (on stamp paper)

### Additional Documents (if applicable)

- **Ration card** showing names of family members
- **Passport-size photographs** of the applicant
- **Voter ID / family details** from the local body
- **NOC from other legal heirs** (in some states)
- **Income certificate** — may be required in some states ([How to get income certificate](/guide/income-certificate-online))

> **Tip:** Requirements vary by state. Check your state's e-District portal for the exact document list before applying.

## How to Apply for Legal Heir Certificate Online — Step-by-Step Process

Most states now offer online application through their **e-District portals**. Here is the general process:

### Step 1: Visit Your State's e-District Portal

Go to your state's e-District or service portal. Some common portals:

- **Uttar Pradesh** — [edistrict.up.gov.in](https://edistrict.up.gov.in)
- **Delhi** — [edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in](https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in)
- **Maharashtra** — [aaplesarkar.maharashtra.gov.in](https://aaplesarkar.maharashtra.gov.in)
- **Madhya Pradesh** — [mpedistrict.gov.in](https://mpedistrict.gov.in)
- **Rajasthan** — [edistrict.rajasthan.gov.in](https://edistrict.rajasthan.gov.in)
- **Tamil Nadu** — [tnedistrict.tn.gov.in](https://tnedistrict.tn.gov.in)
- **Karnataka** — [nadakacheri.karnataka.gov.in](https://nadakacheri.karnataka.gov.in)

You can also access services through the **Common Service Centres (CSCs)** near you.

### Step 2: Register or Log In

- If you're a new user, click **Register** and create an account using your mobile number and Aadhaar
- Existing users can log in with their credentials
- Some states support login via **DigiLocker** or **SSO ID**

### Step 3: Select the Service

- Navigate to **Revenue Services** or **Certificate Services**
- Select **"Legal Heir Certificate"** or **"Varasudar Certificate"** (name varies by state)

### Step 4: Fill the Application Form

Enter the required details:

- **Applicant details** — Name, address, mobile number, Aadhaar number
- **Deceased person's details** — Name, date of death, place of death, death certificate number
- **Legal heir details** — Names, ages, relationship, and Aadhaar numbers of ALL legal heirs
- **Purpose** — Reason for requiring the certificate (bank claim, insurance, pension, etc.)

### Step 5: Upload Documents

Upload scanned copies of all required documents:

- Death certificate (PDF/JPG)
- Applicant's identity and address proof
- Relationship proof documents
- Affidavit on stamp paper
- Passport-size photograph

> **Note:** File size limits are usually 100KB–500KB per document. Keep scans clear and legible.

### Step 6: Pay Fees and Submit

- Pay the application fee online via **net banking, UPI, or debit card**
- Fees are typically **₹50 to ₹200** depending on the state
- After payment, submit the application and note down the **application/reference number**

### Step 7: Verification and Certificate Issuance

- The Tehsildar or Revenue Inspector may conduct a **local enquiry** to verify the claim
- Other legal heirs may be called for verification
- Once verified, the certificate is issued digitally and can be downloaded from the portal

## Legal Heir Certificate Format

A standard legal heir certificate contains the following details:

- **Certificate number** and date of issue
- **Name of the deceased** with date and place of death
- **Details of all legal heirs** — Name, age, relationship with deceased
- **Purpose** for which the certificate is issued
- **Issuing authority** — Name, designation, and seal of the Tehsildar/SDM
- **Official stamp and signature**

The certificate is typically issued on the official letterhead of the Revenue Department and carries a unique identification number for verification.

> **Digital certificates** issued through e-District portals can be verified online using the certificate number on the respective state portal.

| Detail | Information |

| **Validity** | Lifetime (unless challenged in court) |

Some states like Delhi and Karnataka have faster processing times of **7–15 days**. If applying through CSCs, an additional service charge of ₹20–₹50 may apply.

## When Do You Need a Legal Heir Certificate?

A legal heir certificate is required in many situations after a person's death:

- **Bank accounts** — To claim the deceased's bank balance, fixed deposits, or locker contents when there is no nominee
- **Insurance claims** — Life insurance, health insurance, or accidental insurance payouts
- **Pension and family pension** — To claim pending pension or start family pension from government or private employers
- **Provident Fund (PF) & Gratuity** — To claim EPF balance or gratuity dues
- **Government dues** — Salary arrears, GPF, and other pending payments
- **Property mutation** — To update land revenue records in the name of legal heirs
- **Vehicle transfer** — To transfer ownership of the deceased's vehicle
- **Share and investment transfer** — Mutual funds, shares, or other investments
- **Court proceedings** — To establish legal heir status in succession matters

## Important Tips

- **List all legal heirs** — Omitting any heir intentionally can lead to legal action and cancellation of the certificate
- **Apply from the deceased's jurisdiction** — Apply in the district/tehsil where the deceased last resided
- **Keep multiple copies** — Get certified copies as different agencies may retain originals
- **Track your application** — Use the reference number to check status on the e-District portal
- **Offline option** — If online services are unavailable, visit your Tehsildar office directly with the same documents

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is a legal heir certificate the same as a succession certificate?

No. A legal heir certificate is issued by the Revenue Department (Tehsildar) and identifies legal heirs for claiming movable assets and dues. A [succession certificate](/guide/succession-certificate-apply-online) is issued by a District Court under the Indian Succession Act and is needed for inheriting debts, securities, and immovable property.

### How long does it take to get a legal heir certificate online?

Typically **15–30 working days** from the date of application. Some states process it within 7–15 days. You can track your application status on the state e-District portal using your reference number.

### Can a legal heir certificate be used for property transfer?

A legal heir certificate is generally accepted for **mutation of land records** and transferring movable property. However, for resolving property disputes or transferring immovable property titles, a court-issued succession certificate may be required.

### What is the fee for a legal heir certificate?

The fee ranges from **₹50 to ₹200** depending on the state. If applying through a Common Service Centre (CSC), an additional service charge of ₹20–₹50 may be applicable.

### Can I apply for a legal heir certificate from a different state?

You must apply in the **district or tehsil where the deceased person last resided**. You cannot apply from a different state. However, some states allow online applications that can be initiated from anywhere.

### Who issues a legal heir certificate in India?

The **Tehsildar, Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM), or Revenue Officer** of the concerned district issues the legal heir certificate. It is a state government service available through the Revenue Department.

### Is there a time limit to apply for a legal heir certificate?

No, there is **no strict time limit**. You can apply anytime after the death of the person. However, it is advisable to apply as early as possible, especially if you need to claim time-sensitive benefits like insurance or pension.
