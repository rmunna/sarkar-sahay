# Ladli Behna Yojana Madhya Pradesh – Eligibility, Application and Benefits

Mukhyamantri Ladli Behna Yojana is a flagship scheme of the Madhya Pradesh government launched to provide financial assistance to women. Under this scheme, eligible women receive a monthly amount directly in their bank accounts via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT). The scheme aims to improve the economic independence and social status of women across the state.

## What is Ladli Behna Yojana?

Ladli Behna Yojana was launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh to provide regular monthly financial assistance to women belonging to economically weaker sections. The scheme covers married women including widows, divorced women, and deserted women who are residents of Madhya Pradesh. The benefit amount has been progressively increased since launch, with the aim of reaching ₹3,000 per month.

- **Current benefit:** ₹1,250 per month (being increased in phases)
- **Target:** ₹3,000 per month
- **Mode of payment:** Direct bank transfer (DBT)
- **Frequency:** Monthly

---

## Who is Eligible?

To apply for Ladli Behna Yojana, a woman must meet the following criteria:

- **Residency:** Must be a permanent resident of Madhya Pradesh
- **Age:** Must be between 21 and 60 years of age
- **Marital status:** Married, widowed, divorced, or deserted women are eligible
- **Family income:** Annual family income must be less than ₹2,50,000
- **Land holding:** Family must not own more than 5 acres of agricultural land
- **Tax status:** Applicant or family members should not be income tax payees
- **Government employment:** Applicant should not be a current or retired government employee (or spouse of one) drawing pension above ₹15,000/month

### Who is NOT Eligible?

- Women whose family members are income tax payees
- Women receiving benefits of ₹1,000 or more from any other government scheme (excluding certain social security pensions)
- Women with family members holding elected positions (MP, MLA, Mayor, etc.)
- Women whose families own four-wheelers (tractors excluded)

## Documents Required

To apply for the scheme, keep the following documents ready:

- **Samagra ID** (family and individual) — this is mandatory
- **[Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)** linked with bank account
- **Bank passbook** (with Aadhaar-linked account and DBT-enabled)
- **Passport-size photograph** (recent)
- **Mobile number** linked with Aadhaar

> **Note:** Your Aadhaar must be linked with your bank account for DBT. If not linked, visit your bank branch first. See our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) for details.

## How to Apply – Step-by-Step

### Online Application (via Official Portal)

1. Visit the official Ladli Behna Yojana portal at [cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in](https://cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"आवेदन करें"** (Apply) on the homepage
3. Enter your **Samagra ID** to auto-fill basic details
4. Verify your **Aadhaar number** via OTP sent to your registered mobile
5. Fill in remaining details — address, bank account, family information
6. Upload your **passport-size photograph**
7. Review all details and submit the application
8. Note down your **application number** for future reference

### Offline Application (via Gram Panchayat / Camp)

1. Visit your nearest **Gram Panchayat office**, **Ward office**, or **designated camp**
2. Carry all required documents (originals and photocopies)
3. Fill the application form with the help of the camp operator
4. Your photograph will be taken and biometric verification done on the spot
5. You will receive an **application receipt** — keep it safe
6. The application will be verified at block and district level

## How to Check Application and Payment Status

1. Visit [cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in](https://cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"आवेदन की स्थिति"** (Application Status)
3. Enter your **registration number** or **Samagra ID**
4. View your application status, approval status, and payment history

You can also check your payment status by:
- Checking your bank account passbook or statement
- Using the **DBT payment status** option on the portal

## Fees

| Item | Cost |
|------|------|
| Application fee | **Free** (no charges) |
| Form filling at camp | **Free** |

There is no fee for applying to Ladli Behna Yojana. Beware of anyone asking for money to process your application.

## Processing Time

- **Application verification:** 15–30 days after submission
- **First payment:** Within 1–2 months after approval
- **Subsequent payments:** Monthly (10th of every month, typically)

---

## Important Tips

1. **Keep Samagra ID updated** — Ensure your name, age, and family details are correct in the Samagra portal before applying
2. **Link Aadhaar with bank account** — DBT will fail if your Aadhaar is not linked with your bank account
3. **Beware of fraud** — The scheme is completely free. Do not pay anyone for application processing
4. **Check eKYC status** — Aadhaar eKYC must be completed for the application to be approved
5. **Keep mobile number active** — The mobile number linked with Aadhaar should remain active for OTP verification

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What is the monthly amount under Ladli Behna Yojana?

Currently, eligible beneficiaries receive ₹1,250 per month. The government has announced plans to increase this to ₹3,000 per month in phases.

### Can unmarried women apply for Ladli Behna Yojana?

No, the scheme is currently available only for married, widowed, divorced, or deserted women. Unmarried women are not eligible.

### Is Samagra ID mandatory for applying?

Yes, a valid Samagra ID (both family and individual) is mandatory. You can create one at [samagra.gov.in](https://samagra.gov.in/) if you don't have it.

### Can women from other states apply?

No, only women who are permanent residents of Madhya Pradesh and have a Samagra ID are eligible.

### What if my Aadhaar is not linked with my bank account?

Your payment will not be processed until Aadhaar-bank linking is complete. Visit your bank branch with your Aadhaar card to complete the linking.

### How can I update my bank account details?

You can update bank account details by visiting your Gram Panchayat office or the nearest camp with your new bank passbook and Aadhaar card.

### Is there an age limit for the scheme?

Yes, women must be between 21 and 60 years of age to be eligible.
