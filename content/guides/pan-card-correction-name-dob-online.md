# PAN Card Correction Online: Fix Name, DOB & Father's Name

If your PAN card has an incorrect name, wrong date of birth, or a misspelled father's name, you can correct it online without visiting any office. The process is available through two official portals — **Protean (formerly NSDL)** and **UTIITSL**.

This guide covers both methods, required documents, fees, and common issues.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent platform and is not affiliated with the Income Tax Department or Government of India.

## What Can Be Corrected on a PAN Card?

You can update or correct the following details:

- **Name** (spelling mistakes, name change after marriage)
- **Date of Birth**
- **Father's Name**
- **Gender**
- **Address**
- **Contact details** (phone, email)
- **Photo & signature** (updated photo/signature on card)

**Note:** Your PAN number itself **cannot be changed** — it remains the same for life.

## Documents Required

### For Name Correction

- Aadhaar Card
- Voter ID
- Passport
- Marriage certificate (for name change after marriage)
- Gazette notification (for legal name change)

### For Date of Birth Correction

- Birth certificate
- Matriculation certificate
- Passport
- Aadhaar Card (if DOB is recorded correctly)

### For Father's Name Correction

- Father's Aadhaar Card or PAN Card
- Birth certificate
- Matriculation certificate
- Passport

**Important:** Submit **any two** documents from the applicable category as proof. At least one must be a government-issued photo ID.

## Method 1: Correction via Protean (NSDL) Portal

This is the most popular method for PAN corrections.

### Step 1: Visit the Portal

Go to [onlineservices.nsdl.com](https://www.onlineservices.nsdl.com/paam/endUserRegisterContact.html)

### Step 2: Select Application Type

- Choose **"Changes or Correction in existing PAN Data"**
- Select category: **Individual**
- Fill in your existing PAN number, name, date of birth, email, and mobile number
- Enter the captcha and submit

### Step 3: Fill the Form

1. You'll receive an **OTP on your email** — enter it to proceed
2. The online form mirrors **Form 49A** (for Indian citizens)
3. **Only tick the checkboxes** next to the fields you want to correct
4. Fill in the **correct details** in those fields
5. Leave other fields unchanged

### Step 4: Upload Documents

- Upload scanned copies of supporting documents (JPEG/PDF, max 1 MB each)
- Upload a recent **passport-size photo** (JPEG, 3.5 x 2.5 cm)
- Upload your **signature** (JPEG, on white paper)

### Step 5: Select Aadhaar-Based e-KYC or Physical Documents

**Option A: e-KYC (Recommended — fastest)**
- Choose Aadhaar-based e-Sign
- No need to send physical documents
- Authenticate via Aadhaar OTP

**Option B: Physical documents**
- Print the acknowledgement form after submission
- Paste your photo and sign the form
- Send it with document copies to the NSDL address via post

### Step 6: Pay the Fee

- Pay ₹107 (including GST) online via debit/credit card, net banking, or UPI
- For dispatch outside India: ₹1,017

### Step 7: Submit and Track

- Note down the **acknowledgement number**
- Track status at [tin.tin.nsdl.com/pantan/StatusTrack.html](https://tin.tin.nsdl.com/pantan/StatusTrack.html)

## Method 2: Correction via UTIITSL Portal

### Step 1: Visit the Portal

Go to [pan.utiitsl.com](https://www.pan.utiitsl.com) and click on **"PAN Card Corrections/Update"**

### Step 2: Fill the Form

- Enter your PAN number and other details
- Select the fields to be corrected
- Fill in the correct information

### Step 3: Upload Documents & Pay

- Upload supporting documents
- Pay ₹107 (including GST) for Indian address
- For foreign address: ₹1,017

### Step 4: Submit

- Choose e-KYC via Aadhaar or physical document submission
- Note the acknowledgement/coupon number for tracking

## Online vs Offline: Which to Choose?

| Feature | Online (e-KYC) | Offline (Physical) |
|---------|----------------|---------------------|
| Speed | 15-20 days | 30-45 days |
| Document submission | Upload online | Post to NSDL/UTIITSL |
| Aadhaar required | Yes (for e-KYC) | No |
| Convenience | High | Low |
| Recommended | ✅ Yes | Only if no Aadhaar |

## Important Tips

1. **Use e-KYC** — it's faster and eliminates the need to courier documents
2. **Double-check details** — verify all entries before submission to avoid paying again
3. **Keep your PAN number handy** — you'll need it throughout the process
4. **Download e-PAN** — after correction, download your updated e-PAN from [incometax.gov.in](https://www.incometax.gov.in) for free
5. **Link with Aadhaar** — ensure your PAN is linked with Aadhaar after correction. See our [PAN-Aadhaar linking guide](/guide/pan-aadhaar-link-status-check) if needed

## Common Issues and Fixes

### "PAN details do not match" error
Ensure you're entering your name and DOB **exactly as they appear** on your current PAN card, not the corrected version.

### Application rejected
Usually happens when uploaded documents are blurry or don't match the correction requested. Reapply with clearer documents.

### Didn't receive the corrected PAN card
Check status online. If dispatched but not received, contact Speed Post or request a reprint from the NSDL/UTIITSL portal.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I correct multiple fields at once?

Yes, you can correct name, DOB, father's name, address, and other details all in a single application for one fee of ₹107.

### Will my PAN number change after correction?

No, your PAN number remains the same. Only the details printed on the card are updated.

### Can I correct PAN card details without Aadhaar?

Yes, choose the physical document submission option instead of e-KYC. You'll need to courier signed documents to the processing centre.

### How do I correct my PAN if I've changed my name after marriage?

Apply for PAN correction with your marriage certificate as supporting document. Select the name field for correction and enter your new name.

### Is there a time limit to correct PAN card details?

No, you can apply for correction at any time. However, it's advisable to correct errors promptly to avoid issues with tax filing or bank KYC.

### Can I download the corrected PAN card online?

Yes, after the correction is processed, you can download your updated **e-PAN card** from the [Income Tax portal](https://www.incometax.gov.in) at no extra cost.
