## What is Karnataka Raitha Siri Yojana?

**Raitha Siri** is Karnataka's personal accident insurance scheme for farmers that provides **₹2 lakh life insurance cover** to farmers aged 18–70 who die or are permanently disabled due to accidents. The premium is paid by the Karnataka government — farmers pay nothing.

---

## Key Benefits

| Coverage | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| Accidental death | ₹2,00,000 |
| Permanent total disability | ₹2,00,000 |
| Partial permanent disability | As per disability percentage |
| Accidental hospitalization | Up to ₹25,000 (treatment reimbursement) |

---

## Who is Covered?

- **Karnataka farmers** registered in RTC (land records)
- Age **18 to 70 years**
- Coverage includes death/disability from:
  - Road accidents
  - Agricultural accidents (tractor rollover, machinery, well fall)
  - Lightning strike
  - Snake/animal bite
  - Drowning while farming

---

## Automatic Coverage — No Application Needed

Unlike many schemes, **Raitha Siri enrollment is automatic** for farmers registered in Karnataka's land records (RTC/Pahani). The Karnataka government pays the group insurance premium to United India Insurance / empanelled insurer for all eligible farmers.

If you are a Karnataka farmer with land in your name and aged 18–70, you are **already covered**.

---

## How to Claim (After Farmer's Death or Disability)

### Step 1 — Register the Incident

For death: Obtain **death certificate** from Gram Panchayat / BBMP.
For accidents: File **FIR** at local police station.

### Step 2 — Approach District Agriculture Office

Within **90 days** of the incident, submit claim at the **District Agriculture Officer's office** (DAO office) in your district.

### Step 3 — Submit Documents

**For death claim:**
- Death certificate
- FIR / post-mortem report (for accidental death)
- RTC (land record) showing deceased was a farmer
- Aadhaar of deceased and legal heir/nominee
- Bank details of nominee (Aadhaar-linked)

**For disability claim:**
- Disability certificate from government hospital
- Medical records of treatment
- FIR (if accident was reportable)
- RTC and Aadhaar

### Step 4 — Verification and Payment

DAO verifies documents, forwards to the insurance company. After approval:
- ₹2 lakh credited to nominee's/farmer's bank account

## Raitha Bandhu vs Raitha Siri

| Scheme | What it does |
|--------|-------------|
| **Raitha Bandhu** | Financial investment support for farmers (input grants) |
| **Raitha Siri** | Personal accident insurance for farmers |

Both are Karnataka government farmer welfare schemes. Raitha Siri is the insurance component.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. My mother is a farmer and died in a road accident. How do I claim Raitha Siri?**

Go to the District Agriculture Office in your district with: mother's death certificate, FIR/post-mortem report, her land records (RTC), her Aadhaar, and your Aadhaar as legal heir. File the claim within 90 days. The DAO will process.

**2. My father was 72 and a farmer. Is he covered by Raitha Siri?**

No. Raitha Siri covers farmers aged **18 to 70**. A 72-year-old farmer has aged out of coverage.

**3. We are tenant farmers (leased land). Are we covered?**

Raitha Siri coverage is based on land ownership registration in the RTC (Record of Rights). Tenant farmers without land in their name may not be covered under this scheme. Check with the DAO.

**4. My husband was hospitalized after a tractor accident. Can I claim treatment costs?**

Yes. Hospitalization for agricultural accidents is covered up to ₹25,000. Submit hospital bills, discharge summary, FIR (if filed), and RTC to the DAO office within 30 days of discharge.

**5. How do I know if my father's Raitha Siri premium has been paid by the government?**

Raitha Siri is a group insurance scheme — you don't receive individual premium receipts. The government pays collectively for all eligible farmers. If your father was a registered farmer (land in his name, age 18–70), he is/was covered.
