# Shram Suvidha Portal: Labour Compliance & Registration

The **Shram Suvidha Portal** ([shramsuvidha.gov.in](https://shramsuvidha.gov.in)) is the unified online platform by the Ministry of Labour & Employment, Government of India. It simplifies labour law compliance for employers by providing a single window for registration, filing annual returns, and managing inspections under various central labour laws.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent informational resource and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government agency.

---

## What is the Shram Suvidha Portal?

Launched to promote ease of doing business, the Shram Suvidha Portal assigns a unique **Labour Identification Number (LIN)** to every establishment. Through this portal, employers can:

- File unified annual returns for multiple labour laws
- View and respond to inspection reports
- Track compliance status
- Apply for registrations and licences under central labour laws

---

## Who Should Use This Portal?

- **Employers** with establishments covered under central labour laws
- **Contractors** registered under the Contract Labour Act
- **Factories** registered under the Factories Act, 1948
- **Establishments** covered under the Payment of Wages Act, Minimum Wages Act, CLRA, BOCW Act, and other central labour legislations

---

## Key Features

1. **LIN (Labour Identification Number):** A unique 10-digit number assigned to every establishment for all labour law compliance
2. **Unified Annual Returns:** Single-window filing of annual returns under 9+ central labour acts
3. **Transparent Inspections:** Computerised inspection scheme with online report uploads within 72 hours
4. **Common Registration:** Single registration for multiple labour laws
5. **Grievance Redressal:** Online complaint mechanism for workers

---

## Documents Required for LIN Registration

- **PAN Card** of the establishment/proprietor
- **Establishment registration certificate** (Shops & Establishment Act or Factories Act)
- **Address proof** of the establishment
- **Details of employees** (count, categories)
- **Existing registration numbers** under various labour laws (if any)
- **Email ID and mobile number** of the authorised person

---

## How to Register on Shram Suvidha Portal (LIN Registration)

### Online Process

1. **Visit** [shramsuvidha.gov.in](https://shramsuvidha.gov.in)
2. **Click** on "New Registration" or "Sign Up"
3. **Enter** establishment details — name, type, address, PAN
4. **Provide** contact details — email, mobile number of authorised signatory
5. **Upload** required documents (PAN, registration certificates)
6. **Enter** employee details — total count, male/female/contract workers
7. **Add** existing registration numbers under various labour laws
8. **Verify** via OTP sent to registered mobile/email
9. **Submit** the application
10. **Receive LIN** — A unique Labour Identification Number is generated and sent via email

---

## How to File Annual Returns on Shram Suvidha Portal

1. **Log in** to [shramsuvidha.gov.in](https://shramsuvidha.gov.in) with your credentials
2. **Navigate** to "Annual Returns" section from the dashboard
3. **Select** the applicable labour act (e.g., Payment of Wages Act, Minimum Wages Act)
4. **Choose** the return year
5. **Fill** the return form with employee and wage details
6. **Upload** supporting documents if required
7. **Preview** and verify all details
8. **Submit** the return electronically
9. **Download** the acknowledgement receipt for your records

### Acts Covered for Unified Annual Returns

| Act | Form |
|-----|------|
| Payment of Wages Act, 1936 | Form III |
| Minimum Wages Act, 1948 | Form III |
| Maternity Benefit Act, 1961 | Form L |
| Equal Remuneration Act, 1976 | Form D |
| Contract Labour (R&A) Act, 1970 | Form XXV |
| Building & Other Construction Workers Act, 1996 | Form XXIX |
| Inter-State Migrant Workmen Act, 1979 | Form XIX |
| Child Labour (P&R) Act, 1986 | Annual Return |
| Factories Act, 1948 | Annual Return |

## Inspection Scheme on Shram Suvidha Portal

The portal implements a **Transparent Inspection Scheme**:

- Inspections are assigned through a **computerised random selection** system
- Inspectors must upload reports within **72 hours** of inspection
- Establishments can **view inspection reports** online
- **Serious matters** (accidents, complaints) can trigger mandatory inspections
- Employers can **respond** to inspection observations through the portal

### How to Check Inspection Reports

1. Log in to the portal
2. Go to "Inspection Reports" section
3. View reports by date, inspector name, or act
4. Submit responses or compliance reports if required

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. What is LIN in the Shram Suvidha Portal?
LIN stands for Labour Identification Number. It is a unique 10-digit number assigned to every establishment registered on the Shram Suvidha Portal. It serves as a unified identity for all labour law compliance.

### 2. Is registration on Shram Suvidha Portal mandatory?
Yes, all establishments covered under central labour laws are required to register on the portal and obtain a LIN. This is part of the government's initiative to digitise labour compliance.

### 3. Can I file annual returns for multiple labour laws at once?
Yes, the Shram Suvidha Portal provides a unified platform to file annual returns under multiple central labour acts through a single window, using your LIN.

### 4. How do I reset my password on the Shram Suvidha Portal?
Click on "Forgot Password" on the login page, enter your registered email ID or LIN, and follow the OTP verification process to reset your password.

### 5. What happens if I don't file annual returns on time?
Failure to file annual returns can result in penalties under the respective labour acts. The specific penalty amount varies by act but can include fines and, in some cases, prosecution.

### 6. How are inspections assigned on the portal?
Inspections are assigned through a computerised random selection system to ensure transparency. However, complaint-based and accident-related inspections are mandatory and assigned outside this random system.

### 7. Can contract labour establishments also register?
Yes, principal employers and contractors under the Contract Labour (Regulation & Abolition) Act, 1970 can register on the portal and file returns.

### 8. Is the Shram Suvidha Portal available in Hindi?
Yes, the portal supports both English and Hindi languages. You can switch the language from the top menu bar.
