## What is Manav Garima Yojana?

Manav Garima Yojana ("Human Dignity Scheme") is a self-employment scheme by the Government of Gujarat under the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment. It provides **free tool kits and equipment** to individuals from Scheduled Castes (SC), OBC, and economically weaker sections to help them start their own small businesses or trades.

### Benefits

The scheme provides equipment/tool kits worth approximately **₹4,000** for various trades including:

- Carpentry tools
- Tailoring machine and accessories
- Masonry tools
- Plumbing tools
- Vegetable/fruit vending cart
- Mobile repairing kit
- Beauty parlour equipment
- Cobbler tools
- Pottery equipment
- Flour mill
- Electrician tools
- And other trade-specific equipment

## Who is Eligible?

- Must belong to **Scheduled Caste (SC)** or **economically weaker sections**
- Must be a **resident of Gujarat**
- Family annual income must not exceed **₹47,000** (rural) or **₹68,000** (urban) — or as revised
- Age: **18 years or above**
- Must not have received similar benefit from any other government scheme
- Must be willing to undertake the selected trade/occupation

## Documents Required

- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
- Caste certificate (for SC applicants)
- Income certificate from Mamlatdar
- Domicile certificate of Gujarat
- BPL ration card or income proof
- Bank passbook
- Passport-size photographs (2)
- Self-declaration of not having received similar benefit earlier

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Online Application

1. **Visit** [e-Samaj Kalyan portal](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in) or [Digital Gujarat](https://digitalgujarat.gov.in)
2. **Register** with mobile number and Aadhaar
3. **Select** Manav Garima Yojana
4. **Choose** the trade/occupation and preferred tool kit
5. **Fill** personal details, income, and bank information
6. **Upload** documents — caste certificate, income certificate, Aadhaar, photo
7. **Submit** and note the application number
8. **Track** status on the portal

### Offline Application

1. **Visit** the District Social Justice Office or Mamlatdar office
2. **Collect** application form
3. **Fill** and attach documents
4. **Submit** to the Social Welfare Officer
5. **Collect** acknowledgement

## Processing Time

- Verification: **1-2 months**
- Tool kit distribution: **2-4 months** after approval (subject to availability)

## Fees

**No fees.** The tool kit is provided completely free.

## Important Tips

1. **Choose a trade you know** — Select a tool kit for a trade you're skilled in or willing to learn
2. **Apply early** — Tool kits are distributed on first-come-first-served basis within the budget allocation
3. **Income certificate is crucial** — Get it from the Mamlatdar; outdated certificates may be rejected
4. **Women are encouraged** — The scheme especially encourages women to apply for self-employment
5. **Combine with training** — Look for government skill training programs to complement the tool kit

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Is this scheme only for SC families?
While primarily for SC families, the scheme also covers **economically weaker sections** from other categories. Check the latest guidelines.

### Q2. Can I choose which tool kit I want?
Yes. You can select from the available list of trades when applying.

### Q3. Is this a loan or grant?
It is a **100% grant** — no repayment is required. The tool kit is given free.

### Q4. Can I apply if I already have a job?
The scheme targets **self-employment**. If you're already employed and earn above the income limit, you may not be eligible.

### Q5. What is the income limit?
Annual family income must not exceed **₹47,000** (rural) or **₹68,000** (urban), though these limits may be revised.
