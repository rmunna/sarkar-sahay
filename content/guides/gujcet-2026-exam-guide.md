# GUJCET 2026 — Gujarat Common Entrance Test

**GUJCET 2026** (Gujarat Common Entrance Test) is conducted by **GSEB (Gujarat Secondary and Higher Secondary Education Board)** for admission to engineering (B.E./B.Tech) and pharmacy (B.Pharm) courses in Gujarat government and private colleges. Admissions are handled by **ACPC (Admission Committee for Professional Courses)**.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [gujcet.gseb.org](https://gujcet.gseb.org). Gujarat domicile required for 85% state quota. **No negative marking.** Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM).

## Eligibility Criteria

| Criteria | Requirement |
|----------|-------------|
| **Qualification** | HSC (12th) from Gujarat Board or equivalent |
| **Minimum marks** | 45% in PCM / PCB (40% for SC/ST/SEBC Gujarat) |
| **Gujarat domicile** | Required for 85% state quota (10–15 years residence) |
| **Age** | No upper age limit |

### Gujarat Domicile for State Quota

For the 85% state quota in GUJCET/ACPC admissions:
- Gujarat domicile certificate (Mamlatdar-issued) required
- Typically requires **10–15 years of continuous residence** in Gujarat
- OR studied in Gujarat schools + parents' Gujarat residence proof

> [Gujarat Domicile Certificate Guide](/guide/gujarat-domicile-certificate-apply-online)

## GUJCET Application Process

1. Go to [gujcet.gseb.org](https://gujcet.gseb.org) → **"GUJCET 2026"**
2. Register with mobile number + email
3. Fill application:
   - Personal details (name, DOB, category, caste)
   - HSC details (board, school, PCM/PCB marks)
   - Stream: Engineering (PCM) or Pharmacy (PCB) or both
4. Upload: photo + signature
5. Pay fee:
   - General/SEBC: **₹300** (approximately)
   - SC/ST Gujarat: **₹150** (reduced fee)
6. Download confirmation

- **Marks**: 1 mark per correct answer

## Syllabus 2026

GUJCET follows the **GSEB (Gujarat Board) HSC syllabus**:

**Physics**: Electric Field, Current Electricity, Magnetic Field, Electromagnetic Induction, Optics, Dual Nature of Radiation, Atoms & Nuclei, Electronic Devices

**Chemistry**: Solid State, Solutions, Electrochemistry, Chemical Kinetics, Surface Chemistry, Coordination Compounds, Haloalkanes, Amines, Biomolecules

**Mathematics**: Relations & Functions, Inverse Trigonometry, Matrices & Determinants, Continuity & Differentiability, Integrals, Differential Equations, Vectors, 3D Geometry, Linear Programming, Probability

## ACPC Counselling — How It Works

After GUJCET result, engineering admissions in Gujarat go through **ACPC (Admission Committee for Professional Courses)**:

1. Register at [acpc.gujarat.gov.in](https://acpc.gujarat.gov.in)
2. Upload domicile certificate, caste certificate, HSC marks
3. Fill college-branch preferences (choice filling)
4. Seat allotment in multiple rounds
5. Report to allotted college

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is GUJCET compulsory for Gujarat engineering colleges?

**GUJCET score** is required for **state quota seats** (85%) in Gujarat government and private aided colleges. Some private self-financing colleges (outside ACPC) may directly admit based on HSC marks or JEE score — check individual college admission criteria.

### JEE Main rank — does it help in Gujarat?

**JEE Main score** is used for:
- NIT Surat (under JEE-based counselling, JoSAA)
- Some private Gujarat colleges that accept JEE Main rank
- JEE rank can substitute GUJCET in some ACPC admissions for top rankers

### GUJCET for pharmacy?

Yes — GUJCET PCB score is used for **B.Pharm** admissions via ACPC (Pharmacy Admission Committee). The exam is same format but with Biology instead of Mathematics.
