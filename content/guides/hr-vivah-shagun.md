## What is Vivah Shagun Yojana?

**Vivah Shagun Yojana** (formerly known as Kanyadan Yojana) is a marriage assistance scheme by the **Government of Haryana** that provides financial help to economically weaker families for the marriage of their daughters. The scheme is administered by the **Department of Welfare of Scheduled Castes and Backward Classes** and the **Department of Social Justice and Empowerment**.

Under this scheme, eligible families receive up to **₹71,000** as a one-time grant to support marriage-related expenses.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Category:** The bride's family must belong to one of these categories:
   - Scheduled Caste (SC)
   - Denotified Tribes
   - Backward Classes with income below ₹1,00,000/year
   - BPL families (any caste)
   - Widow/Destitute women's daughters
   - Orphan girls
2. **Age:** Bride must be **18 years or older**, groom must be **21 years or older**
3. **Residency:** Family must be a resident of Haryana (Parivar Pehchan Patra required)
4. **Income:** Annual family income up to ₹1,80,000 (varies by category)
5. **First Two Daughters:** Benefit is available for up to two daughters in a family

## Benefit Amount

| Category | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| SC/Denotified Tribes families | ₹71,000 |
| BPL / Backward Classes | ₹11,000 - ₹51,000 |
| Widow's daughters | ₹51,000 - ₹71,000 |
| Orphan girls | ₹71,000 |
| Sports women (any category) | ₹31,000 |

> **Note:** Amounts may vary based on latest government notifications. Check the official portal for current rates.

## Documents Required

### Bride's Family
- Aadhaar Card of the bride
- Parivar Pehchan Patra (Haryana Family ID)
- Age proof of bride (Birth Certificate / Aadhaar / School certificate)
- Caste Certificate (SC/BC)
- BPL Certificate (if applicable)
- Income Certificate

### Marriage Related
- Marriage Card / Invitation
- Marriage Registration Certificate (to be submitted after marriage for final payment)

### Bank Details
- Bank account of the bride or bride's parent (Aadhaar-linked)
- Passbook copy

### Groom's Details
- Aadhaar Card of the groom
- Age proof of groom (must be 21+)

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Online Application (Saral Haryana)

1. **Visit** [saralharyana.gov.in](https://saralharyana.gov.in)
2. **Login** or create a new account
3. **Search** for "Vivah Shagun" or "Marriage Assistance"
4. **Enter** Parivar Pehchan Patra number
5. **Fill** the application form with bride's details, groom's details, marriage date
6. **Upload** all required documents
7. **Submit** the application — note the reference number
8. **Apply before the marriage** or **within 6 months** of marriage (check latest rules)

### Offline Application

1. **Visit** the **District Welfare Office** or **Atal Seva Kendra**
2. **Collect** the Vivah Shagun application form
3. **Fill** and attach all documents
4. **Submit** to the concerned officer
5. **Get** acknowledgement receipt

## Processing Time

- **Verification:** 30-60 days
- **Part Payment:** Some portion may be released before marriage
- **Final Payment:** After submission of marriage registration certificate
- **Disbursement:** Via DBT to the bank account

## Important Tips

1. **Apply Before Marriage:** Submit your application well before the marriage date for timely processing
2. **Marriage Registration:** Get your marriage registered — the certificate is needed for final payment
3. **PPP is Mandatory:** Ensure Parivar Pehchan Patra is updated and verified
4. **Income Certificate:** Get it fresh from the Tehsildar if your old one has expired
5. **Don't Miss Deadline:** Apply within the stipulated time frame (usually before marriage or within 6 months after)

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. How much money do SC families get under Vivah Shagun?
SC families receive up to **₹71,000** for the marriage of their daughter.

### Q2. Can I apply after the marriage?
Yes, you can apply within **6 months of marriage** (subject to latest rules), but applying before marriage is recommended.

### Q3. Is the scheme only for SC families?
No, it covers SC, Denotified Tribes, Backward Classes, BPL families, widows' daughters, and orphan girls.

### Q4. How many daughters can avail this benefit?
Up to **two daughters** in a family can avail the Vivah Shagun benefit.

### Q5. What is the minimum age for the bride?
The bride must be at least **18 years old** and the groom must be at least **21 years old**.

### Q6. Is marriage registration mandatory?
Yes, marriage registration certificate is required for the final disbursement of the assistance.

### Q7. Where can I check my application status?
Track your application at [saralharyana.gov.in](https://saralharyana.gov.in) using your application reference number.
