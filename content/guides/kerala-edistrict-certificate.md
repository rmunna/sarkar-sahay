## What is Kerala eDistrict?

Kerala eDistrict is the official online portal of the Government of Kerala for applying and obtaining various certificates and government services digitally. Managed by the Information Kerala Mission (IKM) under the Revenue Department, it enables citizens to apply for income certificates, caste certificates, nativity certificates, residence certificates, and many other documents without visiting government offices.

Applications can be submitted online and are processed by the Village Officer, Tehsildar, or District Collector depending on the certificate type.

## Certificates Available on Kerala eDistrict

| Certificate | Issuing Authority |
|---|---|
| Income Certificate | Village Officer / Tehsildar |
| Community/Caste Certificate | Village Officer / Tehsildar |
| Nativity Certificate | Village Officer |
| Residence Certificate | Village Officer |
| Possession Certificate | Village Officer |
| Location Certificate | Village Officer |
| Legal Heir Certificate | Village Officer / Tehsildar |
| Solvency Certificate | Tehsildar |
| Inter-Caste Marriage Certificate | Tehsildar |
| No Objection Certificate (NOC) | Various authorities |

## Who Can Apply?

- Any resident of Kerala
- Indian citizens domiciled in Kerala
- Applicants must have relevant supporting documents
- For income certificate — the applicant or family member earning income
- For caste certificate — persons belonging to SC/ST/OBC communities
- For nativity — persons born in Kerala or having Kerala origin
- For residence — persons currently residing in Kerala

## Documents Required

### Income Certificate
- Aadhaar card
- Ration card
- Salary certificate / income proof (for employed)
- Self-declaration (for self-employed/unemployed)
- Village Officer verification form

### Caste Certificate
- Aadhaar card
- SSLC book or school certificate showing community
- Parent's caste certificate (if available)
- Ration card
- Declaration form

### Nativity Certificate
- Aadhaar card
- Birth certificate
- SSLC certificate
- Parent's nativity certificate (if available)
- Ration card

### Residence Certificate
- Aadhaar card
- Ration card showing current address
- Electricity/water bill
- Rental agreement (if applicable)
- Tax receipt of property (if owner)

## How to Apply for Certificates on Kerala eDistrict — Step by Step

### Step 1: Register on eDistrict Portal

1. Visit [edistrict.kerala.gov.in](https://edistrict.kerala.gov.in)
2. Click **"Citizen Login"** or **"Register"**
3. Enter your **Aadhaar number** and **mobile number**
4. Verify via OTP sent to your registered mobile
5. Create a **username** and **password**
6. Complete the registration

### Step 2: Login and Select Certificate

1. Login with your credentials at [edistrict.kerala.gov.in](https://edistrict.kerala.gov.in)
2. Go to **"Apply for Certificate"** or **"Services"**
3. Select the certificate you need:
   - Income Certificate
   - Community Certificate
   - Nativity Certificate
   - Residence Certificate
4. Click **"Apply"**

### Step 3: Fill the Application Form

1. Enter personal details — name, address, date of birth
2. For **Income Certificate**: Enter annual family income, source of income, number of family members
3. For **Caste Certificate**: Enter community name, sub-caste, supporting details
4. For **Nativity Certificate**: Enter place of birth, parents' details, duration of residence
5. For **Residence Certificate**: Enter current address, duration of stay, purpose
6. Verify all details carefully

### Step 4: Upload Documents

1. Upload scanned copies of required documents
2. File format: PDF or JPEG
3. File size: Typically under 200 KB per document
4. Ensure documents are clearly readable

### Step 5: Pay Fee and Submit

1. Review the application summary
2. Pay the application fee online (UPI, net banking, debit card)
3. Click **"Submit"**
4. Note your **Application Number** for tracking

### Step 6: Track Application Status

1. Login to [edistrict.kerala.gov.in](https://edistrict.kerala.gov.in)
2. Click **"Track Application"** or **"Application Status"**
3. Enter your **Application Number**
4. View current status — submitted, under verification, approved, or rejected

### Step 7: Download Certificate

1. Once approved, login to the portal
2. Go to **"My Applications"** or **"Download Certificate"**
3. Download the digitally signed certificate in PDF format
4. The certificate has a QR code for verification

|---|---|
| Income Certificate | 3–7 working days |
| Community/Caste Certificate | 7–15 working days |
| Nativity Certificate | 7–15 working days |
| Residence Certificate | 3–7 working days |
| Legal Heir Certificate | 15–30 working days |

> **Note:** Processing time depends on verification requirements and local office workload. Village Officer-issued certificates are typically faster.

## Applying via Akshaya Centres

If you cannot apply online:
1. Visit your nearest **Akshaya Centre** (Common Service Centre)
2. Carry original documents and photocopies
3. The Akshaya operator will fill the online application on your behalf
4. Pay the application fee plus Akshaya service charge (₹20–₹50)
5. Collect the receipt with application number

## Important Tips

1. **Keep documents ready before starting** — Upload size limits are strict; scan documents in advance at proper resolution
2. **Use Aadhaar-linked mobile number** — OTP verification requires the mobile number linked to your Aadhaar
3. **Apply at correct jurisdiction** — Apply through the Village/Tehsil where you reside; applications to wrong jurisdiction will be rejected
4. **Download within validity period** — Some certificates have validity of 6 months to 1 year; download and use them before expiry
5. **Akshaya Centres for assistance** — If you face technical difficulties, Akshaya Centres across Kerala can help with the application

## FAQs

### 1. Is Kerala eDistrict a government portal?
Yes, eDistrict (edistrict.kerala.gov.in) is the official certificate service portal of the Government of Kerala, managed by Information Kerala Mission under the Revenue Department.

### 2. Can I apply for income certificate online in Kerala?
Yes, you can apply for an income certificate online through the Kerala eDistrict portal. Register with your Aadhaar, fill the form, upload documents, pay the fee, and submit.

### 3. How long does it take to get a caste certificate in Kerala?
A caste/community certificate typically takes 7–15 working days after application submission, depending on the verification process at the Village Officer or Tehsildar level.

### 4. Is the eDistrict certificate digitally signed?
Yes, certificates issued through Kerala eDistrict are digitally signed and contain a QR code for verification. They are legally valid documents.

### 5. Can I apply for certificates for my family members?
You can apply for certificates for yourself. For minor children, parents can apply. For other family members, they should apply individually with their own Aadhaar and documents.

### 6. What if my eDistrict application is rejected?
If rejected, you can view the reason on the portal. Correct the issue (missing documents, incorrect information) and re-apply. You can also visit the concerned Village Office for clarification.

### 7. Are eDistrict certificates accepted for central government jobs?
Yes, digitally signed certificates from Kerala eDistrict are accepted for UPSC, SSC, banking exams, and other central government recruitment processes.
