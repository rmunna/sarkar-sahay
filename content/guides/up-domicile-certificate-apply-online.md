# UP Domicile Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **domicile certificate** (निवास प्रमाण पत्र — Niwas Praman Patra) in Uttar Pradesh is issued by the Revenue Department (Sub-Divisional Magistrate / Tehsildar) and is required for NEET UP state quota (85%) admissions, UP Scholarship (scholarships.up.gov.in), government job applications (UPPSC, UPSSSC), and various state scheme eligibility. Applications go through **UP e-District / eSathi** portal.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [edistrict.up.gov.in](https://edistrict.up.gov.in) or [esathi.up.gov.in](https://esathi.up.gov.in). Must have **3 years' continuous residence** in UP. Issued by SDM within **15–30 days**.

## UP Domicile Certificate — Key Facts

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Minimum residence** | 3 years continuous residence in UP |
| **Issuing authority** | Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) / Tehsildar |
| **Processing time** | 15–30 days |
| **Fee** | ₹10–30 (online/CSC) |
| **Validity** | Permanent once issued |
| **Portal** | edistrict.up.gov.in / esathi.up.gov.in |

---

## Why You Need UP Domicile Certificate

- **NEET UP state quota (85%)** — domicile certificate mandatory for DGME UP counselling
- **UP government scholarship** (scholarships.up.gov.in) — UP domicile required for all categories
- **UP government jobs (UPPSC, UPSSSC)** — some posts require UP domicile
- **BEd, LLB, other state professional courses** — state quota requires domicile
- **UP state scheme eligibility** — Kanya Sumangala, PM Awas UP, etc.
- **Admission to state colleges** — management quota sometimes requires UP domicile

---

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** — mandatory identity and address proof (with UP address)
- **Ration card** — shows UP permanent address
- **Voter ID** — UP voter enrollment shows residency
- **School certificates** — if studied in UP schools (supporting residency)
- **Property documents** — ownership/rent documents showing UP address
- **Utility bills** — electricity/water bill (last 3–6 months, UP address)
- Self-declaration of residency (online or on stamp paper)
- Passport-size photograph

> **3-year rule:** You need documents showing you have been a resident of UP for **at least 3 continuous years**. Ration card, voter ID, or utility bills covering the 3-year period are the best proof.

---

## How to Apply Online — eSathi / UP e-District

1. Go to [edistrict.up.gov.in](https://edistrict.up.gov.in) or [esathi.up.gov.in](https://esathi.up.gov.in)
2. Register / log in with mobile number
3. Click **"Revenue Court Certificate"** → **"Niwas Praman Patra"** (निवास प्रमाण पत्र)
4. Fill form:
   - Applicant name, address (district, tehsil, village)
   - Duration of residence in UP
   - Purpose of certificate
5. Upload documents (Aadhaar, ration card, voter ID, utility bills)
6. Pay fee: **₹10–30** (online)
7. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via Jan Seva Kendra / CSC

1. Visit nearest **Jan Seva Kendra** (Common Service Centre) or Lok Mitra Kendra
2. Carry Aadhaar, ration card, voter ID, utility bills
3. Operator submits on eSathi / e-District
4. Service charge: ₹30–60
5. Collect acknowledgement receipt

---

## Status Check

1. Go to [edistrict.up.gov.in](https://edistrict.up.gov.in)
2. Click **"Application Status"** → enter Application Reference Number
3. Or log in → **"My Applications"** → view status
4. Status flow: Submitted → Lekhpal Verification → SDM → Approved

---

## Download UP Domicile Certificate

Once approved:
1. Log in to eSathi / e-District UP → **"My Applications"**
2. Click approved domicile certificate
3. Download digitally signed PDF
4. Valid for NEET UP counselling (DGME), UP Scholarship portal, UPPSC applications

## UP Domicile for NEET 2026 — What You Need

For **NEET 2026 UP state quota counselling** (DGME UP):
1. UP domicile certificate — must be a UP domicile holder
2. Must have studied in UP schools for the required period (check DGME UP notification)
3. Certificate should be uploaded at DGME UP counselling portal
4. Apply for domicile certificate well in advance (takes 15–30 days)
5. Even if you have Aadhaar with UP address, you still need the formal domicile certificate

---

## UP Scholarship — Domicile Requirement

For **UP Scholarship** (scholarships.up.gov.in — pre-matric and post-matric):
- UP domicile certificate is required as address proof
- Upload digitally signed PDF from e-District UP
- Same certificate works for multiple scholarship years (no annual renewal)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My Aadhaar shows UP address — do I still need a domicile certificate?

**Yes.** Aadhaar is an identity and address document but is **not a domicile certificate**. For NEET state quota and government services requiring domicile proof, you need a formal **domicile/niwas praman patra** issued by the SDM/Tehsildar — even if your Aadhaar shows UP address.

### Student studying outside UP — can I get UP domicile?

Yes, if your **parents/family have UP residence for 3+ years** (ration card, voter ID, property at UP address). Students studying outside can get UP domicile based on their family's permanent address in UP.

### How long is UP domicile certificate valid?

UP domicile certificate is generally **permanent** — once issued it does not expire as long as you maintain your UP residence. You do not need to renew it annually. However, for some specific purposes, the issuing authority may specify a date.
