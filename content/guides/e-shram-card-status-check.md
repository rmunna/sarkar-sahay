## What is e-Shram Card?

The e-Shram card is a unique identity card for unorganised sector workers in India, issued by the Ministry of Labour and Employment. Each registered worker receives a 12-digit **Universal Account Number (UAN)** and an e-Shram card. Over 29 crore workers have already registered on the portal.

The e-Shram card helps workers access:
- **Government welfare schemes** (PM Suraksha Bima Yojana, PM Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana)
- **Accidental insurance** cover of ₹2 lakh
- **Employment opportunities** and skill development programmes
- **Social security benefits** during emergencies and disasters

## How to Check e-Shram Card Status Online

### Method 1: Check Using Mobile Number / Aadhaar on eshram.gov.in

1. Visit the official portal at [https://eshram.gov.in](https://eshram.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Already Registered"** or **"Know Your UAN"** option on the homepage
3. Enter your **registered mobile number** or **Aadhaar number**
4. Click **"Send OTP"** — an OTP will be sent to your registered mobile
5. Enter the OTP and click **"Verify"**
6. Your e-Shram card status, UAN number, and registration details will be displayed
7. If registered successfully, you can **download your e-Shram card** as a PDF

### Method 2: Check Using UAN Number

1. Go to [https://eshram.gov.in](https://eshram.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Already Registered"** on the homepage
3. Select **UAN Number** as the search option
4. Enter your 12-digit e-Shram UAN number
5. Enter the captcha code and click **"Search"**
6. Your registration status and card details will appear on screen

### Method 3: Download e-Shram Card Directly

1. Visit [https://register.eshram.gov.in](https://register.eshram.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Update/Download e-Shram Card"**
3. Enter your registered **mobile number**
4. Verify via OTP
5. Click **"Download UAN Card"** to save the PDF

## Check e-Shram Card Status via SMS

Currently, there is no official SMS-based status check service for e-Shram cards. You must use the online portal or the UMANG app.

**Via UMANG App:**
1. Download the **UMANG app** from Google Play Store or Apple App Store
2. Open the app and search for **"e-Shram"**
3. Select **"Know Your UAN"** or **"Download e-Shram Card"**
4. Enter your Aadhaar or mobile number and verify via OTP
5. View and download your e-Shram card

## Common Issues and Fixes

### 1. "Aadhaar Already Registered" Error
This means your Aadhaar is already linked to an e-Shram UAN. Use the **"Already Registered"** option to retrieve your existing UAN and download the card.

### 2. OTP Not Received
- Ensure your mobile number is linked to your **Aadhaar**
- Check for network issues and wait 2-3 minutes before retrying
- If your Aadhaar-linked mobile is different, update it at the nearest Aadhaar centre first

### 3. Website Not Loading / Server Error
The portal often faces heavy traffic. Try accessing early morning (before 9 AM) or late evening (after 8 PM). You can also use the UMANG app as an alternative.

### 4. Wrong Details on e-Shram Card
- Log in using your registered mobile number
- Click on **"Update"** option to correct name, address, or other details
- Changes sync from your Aadhaar, so update Aadhaar first if needed. See our [Aadhaar address change guide](/guide/aadhaar-address-change-online) for help.

### 5. UAN Number Lost or Forgotten
Use the **"Know Your UAN"** option on the homepage with your Aadhaar or mobile number to retrieve it.

## Important Tips

1. **Keep your UAN safe** — it is your permanent identity in the e-Shram system
2. **Ensure Aadhaar-mobile linking** — OTP verification requires this
3. **Use UMANG app** as a backup if the website is slow
4. **Registration is free** — do not pay anyone for e-Shram card registration
5. **Update details promptly** if you change your address or occupation

## FAQs

### Q1: How do I check my e-Shram card status online?
Visit [eshram.gov.in](https://eshram.gov.in), click "Already Registered," enter your mobile number or Aadhaar, verify OTP, and view your status and UAN.

### Q2: Can I check e-Shram card status without Aadhaar?
Yes, you can check using your registered mobile number or UAN number on the e-Shram portal.

### Q3: How to download the e-Shram card PDF?
Log in at [register.eshram.gov.in](https://register.eshram.gov.in) using your mobile number, verify OTP, and click "Download UAN Card."

### Q4: Is there any fee for e-Shram card registration or status check?
No. Registration and status check are completely free. Beware of anyone charging money for this service.

### Q5: My e-Shram card shows wrong name/details. How to correct it?
Update your Aadhaar details first (as e-Shram pulls data from Aadhaar), then log in to the e-Shram portal and use the "Update" option.

### Q6: Can I register for e-Shram if I already have EPFO/ESIC registration?
No. Workers already registered with EPFO or ESIC are not eligible for e-Shram registration as it is meant for unorganised sector workers only.

### Q7: What benefits do I get with the e-Shram card?
You get accidental insurance of ₹2 lakh under PM Suraksha Bima Yojana, access to various government welfare schemes, and priority during disaster relief.
