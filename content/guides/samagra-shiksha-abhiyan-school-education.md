## What is Samagra Shiksha Abhiyan?

Samagra Shiksha Abhiyan is the Government of India's **integrated school education programme** that combines three earlier schemes (SSA, RMSA, and Teacher Education) into one holistic programme for Classes 1 to 12. It aims to ensure **equitable, inclusive, and quality education** for all children in government schools.

For students, the key benefits are:
- Free **uniforms** (two sets per year)
- Free **textbooks** (Classes 1–8)
- Free **transport** for students in remote areas
- **Special education** support for children with disabilities
- **Residential schools** for tribal and girl students
- **Smart classrooms** and digital learning tools

## Who Gets What — Summary

**All students in government schools:**
- Free textbooks (Classes 1–8)
- Free mid-day meal (PM POSHAN — linked scheme)

**Girls specifically:**
- Free uniforms (2 sets/year) — Classes 1–8
- Kasturba Gandhi Balika Vidyalayas (KGBVs) — free residential schools for girls in EWS

**SC/ST/BPL/disabled students:**
- Free uniforms for boys also
- Additional scholarship support (separate Post-Matric Scholarship schemes)
- CWSN: aids, appliances, free therapy, escort allowance

---

## Who is Eligible?

**All students enrolled in government schools** from Classes 1 to 12 automatically benefit. There is no separate application for most Samagra Shiksha benefits — they are delivered through the school.

Special provisions:
- **Girls in EWS areas** — for residential schools (KGBVs)
- **Children with disabilities (CWSN)** — for special education support
- **Students in remote areas** — for transport allowance

## How to Access Samagra Shiksha Benefits

### For Uniforms and Textbooks (Automatic)

At the beginning of the academic year:
1. Enroll in any government school
2. Uniforms and textbooks are distributed by the school from government supply
3. No application needed — it is automatic

### For Transport Allowance

If you live in a remote area (>1 km from school):
1. Talk to your school headmaster
2. Request inclusion in the school's transport scheme
3. Allowance is typically paid to parents/guardians via DBT

### For CWSN Support (Children with Special Needs)

1. Contact the **Block Resource Centre Coordinator (BRCC)** or school headmaster
2. Get the child assessed by the Special Educator at the Block Resource Centre
3. Apply for aids/appliances (wheels, hearing aids, low-vision glasses) through the school
4. Receive escort allowance via DBT if required

### For KGBV (Residential Schools for Girls)

Kasturba Gandhi Balika Vidyalayas are for girls in Classes 6–12 from EWS/SC/ST/OBC/minority communities:
1. Contact the District Education Officer (DEO) for KGBV admissions in your area
2. Admission is through a selection process — based on need and academic performance

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. My child goes to a government school but hasn't received uniform or books. What do I do?**

Contact the school headmaster. If they are unable to resolve, report to the **Block Education Officer (BEO)**. Textbooks and uniforms are a state-level responsibility under Samagra Shiksha — delays happen but are a rights violation.

**2. My daughter has a visual impairment. What does Samagra Shiksha provide for her?**

Samagra Shiksha provides:
- Free low-vision aids / Braille materials
- Braille/large-print textbooks
- Special educator support
- Escort allowance (₹450/month)
Contact the Block Resource Centre for CWSN.

**3. My son dropped out in Class 9. Can he re-enroll under Samagra Shiksha?**

Yes. Samagra Shiksha includes **out-of-school children (OOSC) identification and re-enrollment** programmes. Contact the Block Education Officer for re-enrollment options.

**4. Are private school students eligible for Samagra Shiksha benefits?**

No. Samagra Shiksha benefits (uniforms, transport, CWSN support) are for students in **government and government-aided schools** only. However, private unaided school students can access RTE reservation quota (25% seats) separately.

**5. I am a teacher in a government school. Does Samagra Shiksha cover training?**

Yes. Teacher professional development, in-service training, and appointment of Cluster Resource Persons (CRPs) are funded under Samagra Shiksha.
