## What is the Gruha Jyothi Scheme?

The **Gruha Jyothi scheme** (also called Gruha Jyothi Yojana) is a flagship welfare program by the Karnataka state government that provides **up to 200 units of free electricity per month** to eligible domestic households. Launched on **18 June 2023**, it is one of the five guarantee schemes introduced by the Congress-led Karnataka government.

The scheme replaces and merges three older electricity subsidy programs — **Kuteera Jyothi**, **Amrutha Jyothi**, and **Bhagya Jyothi** — into a single, simplified benefit. Over **1.72 crore consumers** across Karnataka have registered under the scheme.

Whether you own your home or live as a tenant, the Gruha Jyothi scheme can significantly reduce or completely eliminate your monthly electricity bill. The benefit is delivered directly through your existing electricity connection — no separate payments involved.

## Who is Eligible for Gruha Jyothi Scheme?

### Eligibility Criteria

- Must be a **resident of Karnataka** (even non-Kannadigas living in the state qualify)
- Must have a **domestic/residential electricity connection** (commercial connections are not eligible)
- Only **one electricity meter per household** can be registered for the benefit
- **Tenants are eligible** even if the meter is in the landlord's name
- **Aadhaar card must be linked** to the electricity consumer ID or account ID
- Average electricity consumption for the previous financial year must be **below 200 units per month**

### Who is NOT Eligible?

- Households with **commercial or industrial** electricity connections
- Consumers whose average monthly consumption exceeds **200 units** (based on the previous financial year)
- Households that already have more than one connection registered under the scheme

As of October 2025, the Karnataka Cabinet approved important revisions:

- Consumers using **under 90 units** now get **10 additional free units** (replacing the earlier 10% increment rule)
- Around **1.95 crore consumers** use an average of 53 units/month and qualify for zero bills
- ESCOMs have simplified average consumption calculations for greater transparency

## How is the Free Electricity Calculated?

Understanding the bill calculation is key to making the most of this scheme. Here's how it works:

### Step 1: Calculate Your Average Consumption

The government calculates your **average monthly electricity consumption** based on units consumed between **April 2022 and March 2023**, plus a **10% increase**.

**Formula:** Average = (Total units from April 2022 – March 2023 ÷ 12) + 10%

### Step 2: Monthly Billing Rules

| Scenario | What You Pay |
|----------|-------------|
| Monthly usage ≤ your average | **₹0 (completely free)** |
| Monthly usage > average but ≤ 200 units | Pay only for the **difference** above your average |
| Monthly usage > 200 units | Pay the **entire bill** for that month |

### Example

Suppose your average consumption is **150 units**. With the 10% increase, your free limit becomes **165 units**.

- Use 160 units → **₹0 bill** (within 165-unit free limit)
- Use 175 units → Pay for **10 units** only (175 − 165 = 10)
- Use 210 units → Pay for the **full 210 units** (exceeds 200-unit cap)

### For New Connections

If you don't have previous consumption data (new house or new connection), the government assigns a default average of **53 units + 10% ≈ 58 free units per month**. After one year, your actual average is recalculated.

## Documents Required

You'll need the following documents to apply:

- **Aadhaar card** of the applicant
- **Latest electricity bill** showing consumer ID/account ID
- **Consumer ID or Account ID** (printed on your electricity bill)
- **Rental agreement** (required only for tenants)

## How to Apply for Gruha Jyothi Scheme Online

### Online Application (Seva Sindhu Portal)

1. Visit the official **[Seva Sindhu portal](https://sevasindhugs.karnataka.gov.in)**
2. Click on the **Gruha Jyothi scheme** icon on the homepage
3. Select your preferred **language** (Kannada or English)
4. Enter the required details:
   - **ESCOM name** (BESCOM, HESCOM, GESCOM, CESC Mysore, or MESCOM)
   - **Account ID / Connection ID** from your electricity bill
   - **Account holder name** (owner's name on the meter)
   - **Residential address**
   - **Type of occupancy** (owner or tenant)
   - **Aadhaar number**
   - **Mobile number**
5. Tick the **declaration checkbox** and enter the **CAPTCHA code**
6. Click **Submit**
7. Save the **acknowledgement number** sent via SMS/email

### Offline Application

Visit any of the following centres:

- **Bangalore One** centres
- **Karnataka One** centres
- **Grama One** centres
- **Grama Panchayat** offices
- **Nadakacheri** centres

Ask for the Gruha Jyothi application form, fill it out, attach required documents, and submit it at the counter. A small service charge may apply.

## How to Check Gruha Jyothi Application Status

1. Go to the **[Seva Sindhu Status Tracking page](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in/StatucTrack/Track_Status)**
2. Select your **ESCOM** from the dropdown
3. Enter your **Account ID** or **Connection ID**
4. Click **Track Status**

You can also reprint your acknowledgement slip if you've lost it by visiting the ESCOM-specific acknowledgement reprint portal.

## Common Issues and Solutions

### 1. Application Rejected or Not Processed
- Ensure your **Aadhaar is linked** to your electricity consumer ID
- Check that the name on Aadhaar matches the account holder name
- Visit your local ESCOM office if the issue persists

### 2. Still Getting Full Bills Despite Registration
- Verify your registration status on the Seva Sindhu portal
- Confirm your average consumption is **below 200 units**
- Contact your ESCOM's helpline for clarification

### 3. Tenant Not Getting Benefits
- The tenant must apply separately even if the meter is in the owner's name
- Submit a valid **rental agreement** as proof of residence
- Ensure previous tenant's registration has been transferred or cancelled

### 4. New Connection — No Previous Data
- Default average of **58 units** (53 + 10%) will be assigned
- After one year, your actual average consumption will be recalculated
- Keep usage under 200 units to remain eligible

### 5. Aadhaar Linking Issues
- Visit the nearest ESCOM office with your Aadhaar card and electricity bill
- Request Aadhaar-to-consumer ID seeding
- This is mandatory for receiving the Gruha Jyothi benefit

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. How many free units do I get under Gruha Jyothi?
You can get **up to 200 units free per month**, depending on your average consumption for the previous financial year.

### 2. Can tenants apply for the Gruha Jyothi scheme?
**Yes.** Tenants can apply even if the electricity meter is in the landlord's name. You'll need to submit a rental agreement.

### 3. What happens if I use more than 200 units in a month?
You will have to pay the **entire electricity bill** for that month — no partial subsidy applies when usage exceeds 200 units.

### 4. Is the scheme available for commercial connections?
**No.** Only domestic/residential electricity connections are eligible.

### 5. Do I need to apply every year?
No, you need to register only **once**. The benefit continues as long as you remain eligible. However, average consumption is recalculated periodically.

### 6. Which portal should I use to apply?
Use the **[Seva Sindhu portal](https://sevasindhugs.karnataka.gov.in)** for online applications. You can also apply offline at Bangalore One, Karnataka One, or Grama One centres.

### 7. My average consumption is below 200 units but I used more this month. Do I pay?
If your usage exceeds your calculated average but stays **under 200 units**, you pay only for the units above your average. If it **exceeds 200 units**, you pay the full bill.

### 8. Is there a deadline to apply?
As of now, **no last date has been announced**. The scheme is open for registration and applications are accepted on a rolling basis.

### 9. Can I apply if I have connections in multiple houses?
You can register only **one domestic connection** per household under this scheme.

### 10. How long does it take for the benefit to reflect in my bill?
Typically, the subsidy reflects within **1–2 billing cycles** after successful registration. Check your application status on the Seva Sindhu portal if there's a delay.
