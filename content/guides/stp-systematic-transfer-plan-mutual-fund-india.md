# STP (Systematic Transfer Plan) India 2026 — Complete Guide

STP is the solution to a common problem: you have a large lumpsum to invest, but you're worried about putting it all in equity at once (especially near market highs). STP lets you park the whole amount in a safe debt/liquid fund and auto-transfer a portion to equity every month.

---

## What Is STP?

A Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) moves a fixed amount from one mutual fund (source — usually liquid/debt) to another (target — usually equity) at regular intervals.

**Flow**:
1. Invest ₹12 lakh in liquid fund (safe, earns 7%)
2. Set STP: ₹1 lakh per month → transfers to Nifty 50 index fund
3. Every month for 12 months, ₹1 lakh moves from liquid to equity
4. After 12 months: fully invested in equity, with 12 NAV entry points (rupee cost averaging)

**Why it works**: Instead of investing ₹12 lakh in equity in one day and hoping the market doesn't crash the next week, you spread the entry — buying equity at 12 different NAV levels.

---

## STP vs SIP — What's the Difference?

| Feature | SIP | STP |
|---------|-----|-----|
| Source of money | Your bank account | Another mutual fund you own |
| Typical use | Regular monthly savings | Deploying a lumpsum gradually |
| Bank debit | Monthly debit from bank | No bank debit (fund-to-fund) |
| Best for | Salary investing | Bonus, windfall, inheritance |
| Both achieve | Rupee cost averaging in equity | Rupee cost averaging in equity |

**They're not alternatives** — SIP is for ongoing savings from salary; STP is for a lumpsum you already have.

## STP Calculator — How Much Stays in Liquid Fund?

When you set up STP from a liquid fund, the un-transferred portion continues to earn 7–7.5% per year while waiting.

**Example**: ₹12 lakh STP of ₹1L/month for 12 months:

| Month | Liquid Fund Balance | Equity Deployed |
|-------|--------------------|-----------------| 
| Start | ₹12,00,000 | ₹0 |
| After month 1 | ₹11,07,000 (earning ~7%) | ₹1,00,000 |
| After month 6 | ₹6,33,000 | ₹6,00,000 |
| After month 12 | ₹0 | ₹12,00,000+ (equity growth) |

The liquid fund interest earned during STP (approximately ₹35,000–₹45,000) partially offsets market timing risk — you don't lose this money during the deployment period.

## STP Types

### Fixed STP
Transfer a fixed amount every month — most common. ₹50,000/month from liquid to equity.

### Capital Appreciation STP
Only the *gains* from the source fund are transferred — capital stays. Useful for regular profit booking from debt to equity without reducing principal.

### Flexi STP
Variable amount transferred based on market valuations — AMC algorithm increases transfer amount when equity is cheap, decreases when expensive. More sophisticated, less common.

## Tax on STP — The Catch

Each STP transfer = a redemption from the source fund. This is a **taxable event**.

**If source fund = liquid fund**:
- Units redeemed are typically held <3 years → STCG at slab rate
- For ₹1L/month STP from liquid fund: mostly original capital, small gain
- Monthly tax: ~₹500–₹1,500 on gains per transfer (small but real)

**If source fund = arbitrage fund** (held 12+ months):
- LTCG @ 12.5% on gains
- More tax-efficient than liquid fund for STPs lasting 12+ months

**Target equity fund**: No tax event when money *arrives* in the equity fund. Tax only when you *sell* the equity fund units.

**Practical impact**: For most STP amounts (₹50K–₹1L/month from liquid fund), the monthly tax is ₹200–₹500 — negligible. Don't let the tax tail wag the investment dog.

## Best Funds for STP Source

| Source Fund Category | Return | Tax (if held <1yr) | Best For |
|---------------------|--------|-------------------|---------| 
| **Liquid Fund** | 7–7.5% | Slab rate (STCG) | Short STPs (3–6 months) |
| **Arbitrage Fund** | 7–7.5% | 20% STCG (<12m); 12.5% LTCG (>12m) | Long STPs (12+ months) — more tax-efficient |
| **Short Duration Debt** | 7–7.5% | Slab rate | Similar to liquid |
| **Money Market Fund** | 7.2–7.8% | Slab rate | Slightly better return than liquid |

**For most investors**: Start STP from a liquid fund (simplest). If you're in 30% tax bracket and the STP will run for 12+ months, park in an arbitrage fund first (for LTCG advantage on interim gains).

## How to Set Up STP — Step by Step

### On Groww

1. First, invest the lumpsum in a liquid fund (search "Liquid", select DSP Liquid or Nippon Liquid → Direct-Growth)
2. Wait for T+1 (next day) for units to be credited
3. Go to the liquid fund in your portfolio → tap "STP"
4. Select target fund (e.g., Mirae Asset Large Cap or Axis Nifty 50 Index)
5. Set amount per transfer, frequency (monthly, weekly), start date
6. Submit — STP auto-runs from chosen date

**Groww limitation**: STP only works between funds from the same AMC on Groww. To do cross-AMC STP (e.g., Nippon Liquid → HDFC Nifty 50), use MF Central.

### On Zerodha Coin

1. Buy liquid fund units first
2. Go to Portfolio → select fund → "STP"
3. Select target fund (Coin shows compatible funds)
4. Set amount, frequency, start date
5. Confirm

**Zerodha STP**: Supports same-AMC and some cross-AMC STP. Verify compatibility in the app.

### On MF Central (mfcentral.com) — Best for Cross-AMC STP

1. Login at mfcentral.com (PAN + OTP)
2. Go to Transactions → STP (Systematic Transfer Plan)
3. Select source fund and folio
4. Select target fund (any AMC)
5. Set amount, frequency (monthly/quarterly), start/end date
6. Confirm — STP registered, runs automatically

**MF Central is the most flexible option** — allows STP between any AMC's funds without restriction.

## STP Mistakes to Avoid

**Mistake 1: Setting STP too slow**
A 10-year STP of ₹1L/month on ₹1.2 crore corpus means ₹90L sits in liquid fund forever. Research shows longer STP (>24 months) doesn't significantly improve outcomes vs 12-month STP — but loses more return to liquid fund vs equity.
*Best practice*: 6–18 months for most lumpsum sizes.

**Mistake 2: Cancelling STP during market crash**
During a 20% market correction, the instinct is to stop STP ("equity is falling, stop investing!"). This is exactly backwards — market corrections are when STP buys equity cheap.
*Fix*: Set it and ignore it. Don't check NAV every day.

**Mistake 3: STP into multiple overlapping funds**
STP ₹20K each into 5 large-cap funds = owning the same 100 stocks 5 times. 
*Fix*: One or two non-overlapping target funds (index + mid-cap, not 5 large-cap active funds).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I do STP from equity to debt?

Yes — this is called a "reverse STP" and is used to gradually shift from equity to debt as you approach a goal (like retirement). Same mechanics, opposite direction.

### What happens if the source fund has insufficient balance?

STP stops automatically. Most platforms notify you via SMS/email when source fund balance is depleted.

### Is STP better than investing lumpsum directly?

Mathematically, lumpsum outperforms STP in rising markets (money invested earlier = more growth time). But psychologically and practically, most investors can't time markets. STP is the "sleep well" approach that consistently beats doing nothing.

### Can I modify STP amount after setting it up?

Yes — cancel existing STP and set new one. On most platforms, you can pause, modify amount, or change frequency from the app.
