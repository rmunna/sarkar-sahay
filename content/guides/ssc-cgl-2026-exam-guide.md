# SSC CGL 2026 — Complete Guide to India's Most Competitive Graduate Job Exam

The Staff Selection Commission Combined Graduate Level (SSC CGL) examination is India's largest recruitment exam for Group B and Group C posts in various central government ministries, departments and organisations. Over 30 lakh candidates appear for SSC CGL every year, competing for approximately 17,000–25,000 vacancies across premium government posts.

If you have a graduation degree, SSC CGL is your fastest path to a central government job.

## SSC CGL Exam Pattern 2026

### Tier 1 — Computer Based Test

| Section | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---------|-----------|-------|------|
| General Intelligence & Reasoning | 25 | 50 | Composite |
| General Awareness | 25 | 50 | Composite |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 25 | 50 | Composite |
| English Comprehension | 25 | 50 | Composite |
| **Total** | **100** | **200** | **60 min** |

- **Negative marking:** 0.5 marks per wrong answer
- **Qualifying nature:** Scores used for Tier 2 shortlisting

### Tier 2 — Computer Based Test (3 sessions)

**Session 1 (Mandatory for all):**

| Paper | Topics | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|-------|--------|-----------|-------|---------|
| Paper 1 Module 1 | Mathematical Abilities | 30 | 90 | 1 hr |
| Paper 1 Module 2 | Reasoning & GI | 30 | 90 | 1 hr |
| Paper 2 Module 1 | English Language | 45 | 135 | 1 hr |
| Paper 2 Module 2 | General Awareness | 25 | 75 | 15 min |
| Paper 3 Module 1 | Computer Knowledge | 20 | 60 | 15 min |

**Session 2 (For JSO post):** Statistics — 100 questions, 200 marks, 2 hours

**Session 3 (For AAO post):** General Studies (Finance/Economics) — 100 questions, 200 marks, 2 hours

- **Negative marking Tier 2:** 1 mark per wrong answer (Paper 1, 2); 0.50 per wrong answer (Paper 3)

## Syllabus Overview

### General Intelligence & Reasoning
Analogies, Similarities & Differences, Space Visualisation, Problem Solving, Analysis, Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations, Syllogism, Venn Diagrams, Number Series, Non-verbal Reasoning, Matrix

### Quantitative Aptitude
Number System, Percentage, Ratio & Proportion, Average, Simple & Compound Interest, Profit/Loss, Time & Work, Time & Distance, Algebra, Geometry, Trigonometry, Mensuration, Statistics & Probability, DI

### General Awareness
Current Affairs (last 6 months), History, Geography, Economy, Polity, Science & Technology, Sports, Awards, Books & Authors, Static GK

### English Language (Tier 2)
Reading Comprehension, Grammar, Fill in the blanks, Vocabulary, Error Spotting, Para Jumbles, Cloze Test, One Word Substitution, Idioms & Phrases, Active/Passive Voice

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is SSC CGL conducted every year?**
Yes, SSC CGL is an annual exam. The notification is usually released between February–April each year.

**Can a final year student apply for SSC CGL?**
Yes — candidates in their final year of graduation are eligible to apply. However, you must complete your degree before document verification.

**Which post is best in SSC CGL?**
Assistant Section Officer (CSS) and Income Tax Inspector are considered the most prestigious and highest-paying posts. Both are at Level 7 with excellent career growth.

**How many attempts are allowed in SSC CGL?**
There is no limit on attempts — you can appear as long as you are within the age limit.

**Is there an interview in SSC CGL?**
No — SSC abolished interviews for all Group B and C posts. Selection is purely based on Tier 1 + Tier 2 scores.

**What is the posting location for SSC CGL?**
Depends on the post and department. CSS officers are posted in Delhi. Income Tax Inspectors are posted across India based on cadre preference.
