# Ladli Laxmi Yojana 2026 — Apply Online, Check Status & Get ₹1 Lakh Scholarship

Ladli Laxmi Yojana is one of the most successful girl child welfare schemes in India, launched by the Madhya Pradesh government. Under this scheme, **National Savings Certificates (NSC) worth ₹1,43,000** are deposited in the name of a girl child, and she receives scholarship amounts at different stages of education — with a final payout of ₹1 lakh at the time of marriage or on turning 21.

Under **Ladli Laxmi 2.0**, the scheme has been extended to include college education support of up to ₹25,000.

## Eligibility Criteria

| Criterion | Requirement |
|-----------|------------|
| **State** | Girl child must be born to a family domiciled in Madhya Pradesh |
| **Registration** | Must be registered within 1 year of birth at Anganwadi |
| **Income** | Parents must be taxpayers or below income threshold (varies) |
| **Aadhaar** | Aadhaar registration of the girl child is mandatory |
| **Birth** | Girl born on or after 1 January 2006 (general); extended for others |
| **Schooling** | Girl must be enrolled in a government or government-aided school |
| **Second child** | If second girl child, parents must have undergone family planning |

## How to Apply Online for Ladli Laxmi Yojana

### Self Registration

1. Go to **[ladlilaxmi.mp.gov.in](https://ladlilaxmi.mp.gov.in)**
2. Click **"Application Form"** → **"Self Registration"**
3. Read eligibility conditions and click **"Continue"**
4. Select the girl's Anganwadi centre (required)
5. Fill in the form with:
   - Girl child's Aadhaar and family details
   - Parent's Aadhaar, income and domicile details
   - Bank account details (girl's or parent's)
6. Upload documents:
   - Girl child's birth certificate
   - Parents' Aadhaar cards
   - Residence/domicile proof (MP)
   - Bank passbook
   - Family planning certificate (if second girl child)
7. Submit and save the **Application Number**

> **Note:** Applications are verified by the local Anganwadi worker and CDPO. You may be called for document verification.

### Through Anganwadi / Government Office

Parents can also apply through:
- Local **Anganwadi centre** (recommended for first-time applicants)
- **Child Development Project Office (CDPO)** of your area
- **MP Online Kiosk** in your town

## How to Check Ladli Laxmi Status Online

1. Go to **[ladlilaxmi.mp.gov.in](https://ladlilaxmi.mp.gov.in)**
2. Click **"Search Application / Certificate"**
3. Enter the girl's **Ladli Laxmi registration number** OR search by name, district and date of birth
4. Application status and scholarship disbursement history will be shown

---

## How to Download Ladli Laxmi Certificate

1. Go to **[ladlilaxmi.mp.gov.in](https://ladlilaxmi.mp.gov.in)**
2. Click **"Print Certificate"**
3. Enter your **registration number**
4. Download and print the Ladli Laxmi Patra (certificate)

The certificate is required when claiming the scholarship amounts at each stage.

## Ladli Laxmi 2.0 — Additional College Support

Under the 2.0 expansion, girls registered under Ladli Laxmi who enrol in higher education receive:

| Course | Amount |
|--------|--------|
| Any graduate course (Govt. college) | ₹25,000 |
| Vocational / professional course | ₹25,000 |
| Medical / Engineering / Law | ₹25,000 |

To claim: Apply through the college/university or at MP Online, with your Ladli Laxmi certificate.

## Common Problems and Fixes

| Problem | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| **Certificate not generated** | Verification may be pending at Anganwadi/CDPO level; contact them directly |
| **Scholarship not received at Class 6/9/11 stage** | Ensure school has submitted the girl's data to the portal; contact school principal |
| **Name mismatch in certificate** | Apply for correction at CDPO office with correct documents |
| **Final ₹1 lakh not received** | Apply with Ladli Laxmi certificate + Aadhaar + bank details at District Women & Child Development Office |
| **Application rejected** | Check rejection reason in portal; common cause: registration done after 1 year of birth |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Ladli Laxmi Yojana only for Madhya Pradesh?**
Yes, the original Ladli Laxmi Yojana is an MP state scheme. Other states have similar schemes: Ladli Scheme (Delhi/Haryana), Bhagyalakshmi (Karnataka), Kanya Shree (West Bengal).

**Can twins or multiple girls be registered?**
Yes — each girl child can be registered separately under the scheme.

**What if the girl drops out of school?**
Future scholarship instalments will stop. However, the NSC deposits already made remain in her name and she will still receive the final ₹1 lakh at age 21.

**Is the ₹1 lakh given only on marriage?**
No — the ₹1 lakh is given when the girl turns 21, regardless of marriage. However, early marriage (before 21) will forfeit the amount. This is intentional — to discourage child marriage.

**Can the girl's bank account receive the final payment?**
Yes — the final ₹1 lakh is transferred directly to the girl's own bank account (not parents'), ensuring she controls the money.
