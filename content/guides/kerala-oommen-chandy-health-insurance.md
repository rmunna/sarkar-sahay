# Oommen Chandy Health Insurance Scheme — Kerala UDF Government ₹25 Lakh Health Coverage

The **Oommen Chandy Health Insurance Scheme** is a flagship welfare initiative of the Kerala United Democratic Front (UDF) government that came to power in the 2026 assembly elections, winning a historic 102-seat mandate — the alliance's best performance since 1977.

Named in memory of former Chief Minister Oommen Chandy, the scheme provides **₹25 lakh health insurance coverage per family** to eligible residents of Kerala, significantly expanding on existing health coverage programmes.

---

## Key Features

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| **Coverage** | ₹25 lakh per family per year |
| **Type** | Cashless treatment at empanelled hospitals |
| **Target** | Economically weaker families of Kerala |
| **Named after** | Former CM Oommen Chandy |

---

## What the ₹25 Lakh Covers

- Major surgeries and hospitalisation
- Cancer treatment
- Organ transplants
- Critical illnesses
- ICU / specialist care
- Accident-related treatment
- Dialysis for kidney patients

> **Note:** Exact list of covered procedures and empanelled hospitals will be published in the official scheme notification at [arogyakeralam.gov.in](https://arogyakeralam.gov.in/).

---

## Eligibility Criteria

- **Kerala resident** with valid residence proof
- Family below or at the income threshold specified in the scheme notification
- Priority for families with no existing health coverage (not already under ESIC or government employee health scheme)
- **Ration card holders** (especially priority/non-priority ration card categories) get preference

---

## Documents Required

- Ration card (to establish family unit)
- Aadhaar cards of all family members
- Kerala residence proof (voter ID, utility bill)
- Income certificate from Village Officer (if income limit applies)
- Passport-size photographs

---

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Collect the Form
- Visit your nearest Akshaya Centre, Village Office, or Taluk Office
- Or download the application form from [arogyakeralam.gov.in](https://arogyakeralam.gov.in/) when published

### Step 2: Submit Documents
- Fill the form with all family member details
- Attach self-attested photocopies of documents listed above
- Submit at the Village Office or designated enrolment centre

### Step 3: Get the Health Card
- Upon verification, a **health card** is issued to the family
- The card is used to avail cashless treatment at empanelled hospitals

### Using the Scheme for Treatment
- Show the health card at any empanelled hospital
- Hospital directly processes the claim with the insurer
- No upfront payment needed for covered treatments

## How This Compares to Existing Kerala Health Schemes

| Scheme | Coverage | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Oommen Chandy Health Insurance** | ₹25 lakh | Economically weaker families |
| Karunya Arogya Suraksha Padhathi (KASP) | ₹5 lakh | BPL + near-BPL |
| Ayushman Bharat (central) | ₹5 lakh | BPL families |
| KVBHIS (Vishwakarma) | ₹1 lakh | Artisan families |

The Oommen Chandy scheme significantly enhances coverage — families that previously had ₹5 lakh coverage may get ₹25 lakh under this new scheme.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is this separate from Ayushman Bharat?**
A: Yes. Oommen Chandy Health Insurance is a Kerala state scheme with a ₹25 lakh limit, separate from Ayushman Bharat (₹5 lakh, central government). Eligible families may use both — state scheme for higher-cost treatments.

**Q: If I already have KASP card, do I need to apply again?**
A: Likely yes — the new scheme is a separate programme with different coverage limits. Check with your village office for the transition process.

**Q: Which hospitals are empanelled?**
A: Empanelled hospital list will be published at arogyakeralam.gov.in. Government hospitals, medical colleges, and selected private hospitals are expected to be included.

**Q: When will the scheme be fully operational?**
A: The scheme was announced post-election (May 2026). Full operational rollout is expected within 3–6 months. Check arogyakeralam.gov.in for the official launch date.
