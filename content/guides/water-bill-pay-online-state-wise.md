# How to Pay Water Bill Online in India — State-Wise Guide

Paying your water bill online saves time and eliminates the hassle of visiting municipal offices. Every major Indian city and state now offers an online portal for water bill payment, bill checking, and complaint registration. This guide covers the process for all major water boards across India.

## Major Water Boards and Their Online Portals

Here is a quick reference of the major water utility providers and their official portals:

| City/State | Water Board | Official Portal |
|---|---|---|
| Delhi | Delhi Jal Board (DJB) | delhijalboard.delhi.gov.in |
| Mumbai | BMC (Water Department) | portal.mcgm.gov.in |
| Bangalore | BWSSB | bwssb.karnataka.gov.in |
| Chennai | Chennai Metro Water (CMWSSB) | chennaimetrowater.tn.gov.in |
| Hyderabad | HMWSSB | water.telangana.gov.in |
| Kolkata | KMC (Water Supply) | www.kmcgov.in |
| Uttar Pradesh | Jal Nigam / Jal Kal | payjal.up.gov.in |

> **Tip:** For smaller towns, check your state's Public Health Engineering Department (PHED) website or your local municipal corporation portal.

## Step-by-Step: Pay Delhi Jal Board (DJB) Water Bill Online

Delhi Jal Board serves over 20 lakh connections across Delhi. Here's how to pay:

1. **Visit the DJB portal** at delhijalboard.delhi.gov.in
2. Click on **"Online Bill Payment"** or **"Pay Water Bill"** on the homepage
3. Enter your **Consumer Account Number** (K-number) — this is printed on your water bill
4. Click **"Submit"** to fetch your current bill details
5. Verify the bill amount, billing period, and due date displayed on screen
6. Click **"Pay Now"** and choose your payment method — UPI, debit card, credit card, or net banking
7. Complete the payment and **save the receipt** or transaction ID for your records

### How to Check Your DJB Bill Without Paying

- On the same portal, use the **"Know Your Bill"** or **"View Bill"** option
- Enter your consumer number to see the current outstanding amount, past bills, and payment history
- You can also call the DJB helpline at **1916** for bill enquiries

### Filing a Water Complaint with DJB

- Visit the DJB portal and click on **"Complaint Registration"**
- You can also register complaints via the **Delhi 311 app** or by calling **1916**
- Common complaint types: no water supply, low pressure, contaminated water, leakage, meter issues

## Step-by-Step: Pay BWSSB Water Bill Online (Bangalore)

Bangalore Water Supply and Sewerage Board (BWSSB) manages water supply for Bangalore city.

1. **Visit** bwssb.karnataka.gov.in
2. Navigate to **"Online Payment"** or **"Pay Bill"**
3. Enter your **RR Number** (Revenue Register number) found on your water bill
4. The system displays your **account details, current charges, and arrears**
5. Select your payment method — UPI, card, or net banking
6. Confirm and pay — a digital receipt is generated instantly

### Checking Your BWSSB Meter Reading

- Use the **"Know Your Bill"** section on the BWSSB portal
- Enter your RR number to view your latest meter reading, consumption in kilolitres, and billed amount
- BWSSB bills are typically generated bimonthly

### BWSSB Complaint Registration

- Use the **"Grievance"** section on the BWSSB website
- Alternatively, call **1916** (Bangalore One) or use the **BWSSB mobile app**
- Track your complaint status online using the complaint reference number

## Step-by-Step: Pay Mumbai BMC Water Bill Online

1. Visit the **BMC citizen portal** at portal.mcgm.gov.in
2. Go to **"Water Bill"** under the services section
3. Enter your **Consumer Number** or **Property Account Number**
4. Review the displayed bill amount and due date
5. Choose your payment method and complete the transaction
6. Download or print the payment receipt

## Other Major Cities — Quick Guide

### Chennai Metro Water (CMWSSB)

- **Portal:** chennaimetrowater.tn.gov.in
- Enter your **Consumer Number** to view and pay bills
- CMWSSB also accepts payments through the **Paytm** and **PhonePe** platforms
- For complaints, use the **CMWSSB helpline 044-4567 4567** or the grievance section on the portal

### Hyderabad HMWSSB

- **Portal:** water.telangana.gov.in
- Enter your **CAN (Consumer Account Number)** to view outstanding bills
- Pay via UPI, cards, or net banking
- Complaints can be filed through **T-App Folio** or the HMWSSB website

### Kolkata KMC

- **Portal:** www.kmcgov.in
- Navigate to the water supply section for bill payment
- Enter your **Consumer ID** to fetch bill details
- KMC also offers payment through the **KMC e-Payment** portal

### Uttar Pradesh (Jal Nigam)

- **Portal:** payjal.up.gov.in
- Covers Lucknow, Kanpur, Varanasi, Agra and other UP cities
- Enter your **Connection ID** to view and pay bills online

## Paying Water Bills Through Third-Party Apps

If you prefer not to visit individual portals, you can pay through popular apps:

- **Paytm:** Go to Bill Payments → Water → Select your water board → Enter consumer number → Pay
- **PhonePe:** Bills & Recharges → Water → Choose provider → Enter details → Pay
- **Google Pay:** Bills → Water → Search for your utility → Enter consumer number → Pay
- **Amazon Pay:** Bill Payment → Water → Select board → Pay

These apps fetch your bill automatically and support UPI, wallet, and card payments.

## How to Check Your Water Bill Online

Regardless of your city, the process is similar:

1. Visit your water board's official portal
2. Look for **"View Bill"**, **"Know Your Bill"**, or **"Bill Enquiry"**
3. Enter your **consumer number / connection ID / RR number**
4. The portal displays your current bill amount, due date, consumption details, and payment history

Keep your consumer number handy — it is the key to accessing all online services.

## Registering Complaints for Water Supply Issues

All major water boards now offer online complaint registration:

- **No water supply** — Report through your city's water board portal or municipal app
- **Contaminated water** — File an urgent complaint; most boards prioritize water quality issues
- **Leakage or pipe burst** — Report online or call the emergency helpline
- **Meter malfunction** — Request a meter inspection through the grievance portal
- **Billing disputes** — File through the online grievance system with supporting documents

For unresolved complaints, you can escalate through the **[consumer complaint portal](/guide/consumer-complaint-online)** or the central **CPGRAMS** system at pgportal.gov.in.

## Tips for Managing Your Water Bill

- **Set reminders** for due dates to avoid late fees
- **Register on your water board portal** for SMS/email alerts on new bills
- **Download receipts** after every payment — useful for property-related documentation
- **Monitor consumption** through online meter readings to catch leaks early
- **Apply for a [new water connection](/guide/water-connection-apply-online)** online if you're in a new property

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q: How do I pay my water bill online?
Visit your city's water board portal (e.g., delhijalboard.delhi.gov.in for Delhi or bwssb.karnataka.gov.in for Bangalore), enter your consumer number, verify the bill amount, and pay using UPI, debit card, credit card, or net banking. You can also pay through apps like Paytm, PhonePe, or Google Pay.

### Q: Where can I find my water consumer number?
Your consumer number (also called connection ID, RR number, or CAN depending on the city) is printed on any previous water bill. If you don't have a bill, visit your local water board office with your address proof to get the number.

### Q: Is there a late fee for delayed water bill payment?
Yes, most water boards charge a surcharge or penalty of 1–2% per month on overdue bills. The exact rate varies by city, so check your bill or your water board's portal for specific late payment terms.

### Q: Can I pay water bills through Paytm or PhonePe?
Yes, most major water boards are listed on Paytm, PhonePe, Google Pay, and Amazon Pay. Go to the bill payment section, select Water, choose your water board, enter your consumer number, and the app fetches your outstanding bill automatically.

### Q: How do I file a complaint about water supply or billing issues?
Each water board has an online grievance section on their portal. You can also call city-specific helplines — 1916 for Delhi and Bangalore. For unresolved issues, escalate through the CPGRAMS portal at pgportal.gov.in.

### Q: How do I check my water bill amount without paying?
Visit your water board's portal and look for "View Bill," "Know Your Bill," or "Bill Enquiry." Enter your consumer number to see the current outstanding amount, due date, consumption details, and past payment history.
