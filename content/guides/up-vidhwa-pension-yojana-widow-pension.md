## What is UP Vidhwa Pension Yojana?

**Uttar Pradesh Vidhwa Pension Yojana** (also called Nirashrit Mahila Pension — Widow Pension) is the Uttar Pradesh government's pension scheme for **widowed women** providing **₹500 per month** as financial assistance to help them meet their basic needs.

The scheme combines:
- **Central government contribution:** ₹300/month (IGNWPS under NSAP)
- **UP state contribution:** ₹200/month
- **Total: ₹500/month** directly to the widow's bank account via DBT

## Who is Eligible?

- Must be a **widow** (husband deceased)
- Age: **18 to 60 years** (women 60+ apply for Old Age Pension — Vridha Pension)
- Must be a **resident of Uttar Pradesh**
- Annual family income: **below ₹2 lakh**
- Not employed in government service
- Not receiving any other pension from the state or central government

## How to Apply Online — Step by Step

1. Go to **[sspy-up.gov.in](https://sspy-up.gov.in)**
2. Click **"निराश्रित महिला पेंशन"** (Widow Pension) → **"आवेदन करें"** (Apply)
3. Click **"New Entry Form"**
4. Fill in:
   - Personal details (name, date of birth, address)
   - Husband's name and date of death
   - Income details
   - Bank account (for DBT)
5. Upload documents (see below)
6. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

## Apply at Gram Panchayat (Offline)

1. Visit your **Gram Panchayat office** or **Block Samaj Kalyan office**
2. Ask for the Vidhwa Pension application form
3. Fill and submit with documents
4. Panchayat Secretary helps submit online through their portal access

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card** (of the widow)
2. **Husband's death certificate** (issued by Gram Panchayat / municipality)
3. **Age proof** — Aadhaar or voter ID
4. **Income certificate** — annual family income below ₹2 lakh (from Tehsildar/SDM)
5. **Bank passbook** — in the widow's own name, Aadhaar-linked for DBT
6. **BPL card / Ration card** (if available)
7. Passport-size photograph

## Check Application Status Online

1. Go to **[sspy-up.gov.in](https://sspy-up.gov.in)**
2. Click **"निराश्रित महिला पेंशन"** → **"आवेदन की स्थिति"** (Application Status)
3. Enter your **Application Reference Number** or Aadhaar to check status

## Check Beneficiary/Payment List

1. Go to [sspy-up.gov.in](https://sspy-up.gov.in) → Vidhwa Pension → Beneficiary List
2. Select your **district → block → gram panchayat**
3. View the pension payment list — check if your name is included

## Payment Schedule

- Pension is released **quarterly** (every 3 months) by the UP government
- DBT transfer to Aadhaar-linked bank account
- Check payment dates and arrears on sspy-up.gov.in

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I applied 4 months ago but no payment has come. Why?
First, check your application status at [sspy-up.gov.in](https://sspy-up.gov.in). If "Approved," ensure your Aadhaar is linked to your bank account. If still pending, visit your Block Samaj Kalyan office (Social Welfare office).

### My income certificate shows ₹2.5 lakh. Am I eligible?
No — the income limit is **below ₹2 lakh** per year. If your actual income is below ₹2L, request a fresh income certificate from the Tehsildar.

### I am 62 years old and a widow. Which pension should I apply for?
Women 60+ can apply for **Vridha Pension (Old Age Pension)** at ₹500/month — the same amount. You can apply for Vridha Pension instead of Vidhwa Pension at [sspy-up.gov.in](https://sspy-up.gov.in).

### Can I get both Vidhwa Pension and PMJDY or PMKISAN benefits?
Yes — widow pension and PMJDY (Jan Dhan banking) or PMKISAN (if you are a farmer) are completely separate schemes with no conflict.

### My husband died recently (1 month ago). Can I apply now?
Yes — you can apply as soon as you have the **death certificate** of your husband. There is no waiting period after the husband's death.

### What if I remarry? Does pension stop?
Yes — if you remarry, you are no longer a widow and are not eligible for widow pension. The pension stops.
