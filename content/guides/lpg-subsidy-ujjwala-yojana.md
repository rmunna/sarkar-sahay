# How to Apply for LPG Subsidy & Ujjwala Yojana — Free Gas Connection

If you belong to a Below Poverty Line (BPL) family and don't have an LPG gas connection, the Indian government provides a **free LPG connection** under the **Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY)**. If you already have a connection, you can receive an **LPG subsidy** directly in your bank account through **DBTL (Direct Benefit Transfer for LPG)**.

This guide covers everything — from applying for a free connection to checking your subsidy status and booking refills.

---

## Part 1: Ujjwala Yojana — Free LPG Connection

### What is Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY)?

Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana is a government scheme launched in 2016 to provide **free LPG connections to women from poor households**. Under PMUY 2.0 (launched in 2021), the scheme was expanded to cover more categories. As of February 2026, over **10.5 crore connections** have been released under this scheme.

**Key benefits:**
- Free LPG connection (no security deposit)
- Free first refill and hot plate (stove) in many cases
- Connection issued in the name of the woman of the household

### Who is Eligible for Free LPG Connection?

You can apply if the **adult woman** in your household belongs to any of these categories:

1. **BPL families** — name in SECC-2011 (Socio-Economic Caste Census) list
2. **SC/ST households**
3. **Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (Gramin) beneficiaries**
4. **Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) beneficiaries**
5. **Forest dwellers**
6. **Most Backward Classes (MBC)**
7. **Tea and ex-tea garden tribes**
8. **People living in islands and river islands**
9. **PMUY 2.0 addition:** Migrant workers and families not covered above — **no address proof or ration card needed** (self-declaration accepted)

**Important conditions:**
- The applicant must be a woman aged 18 years or above
- No one in the household should already have an LPG connection
- Aadhaar card is mandatory

### How to Apply for Ujjwala Yojana (Online + Offline)

#### Method 1: Apply Online

1. Visit the official PMUY website: **https://www.pmuy.gov.in/**
2. Click on **"Apply for New Connection"**
3. Select your preferred Oil Marketing Company (OMC) — Indane (IOC), Bharat Gas (BPCL), or HP Gas (HPCL)
4. Fill in the application form with your details — name, address, Aadhaar number, bank account
5. Upload required documents (scanned copies)
6. Submit the application
7. You will receive an acknowledgement number — save it for tracking

You can also apply through the respective OMC portals:
- **Indane (IOC):** https://cx.indianoil.in
- **Bharat Gas (BPCL):** https://my.ebharatgas.com
- **HP Gas (HPCL):** https://myhpgas.in

#### Method 2: Apply Offline at LPG Distributor

1. Visit your nearest LPG distributor (Indane, Bharat Gas, or HP Gas)
2. Ask for the **Ujjwala Yojana application form (KYC form)**
3. Fill in the form with your details
4. Attach photocopies of required documents
5. Submit the form at the distributor
6. The distributor will verify your details and process the connection
7. You will receive your LPG connection within a few days

### Documents Required for Ujjwala Yojana

| Document | Details |
|---|---|
| **Aadhaar Card** | Mandatory for the woman applicant |
| **BPL Certificate / Ration Card** | To prove BPL status (or category proof for SC/ST/AAY etc.) |
| **Bank Account Details** | Passbook or cancelled cheque (account in applicant's name) |
| **Address Proof** | Voter ID / Aadhaar / self-declaration for migrants |
| **Passport-size Photo** | Recent photograph of the applicant |

**For PMUY 2.0 (migrants):** If you don't have address proof, a **self-declaration** about your address is accepted. No ration card is needed.

## Part 2: LPG Subsidy (DBTL) — For Existing Users

### What is LPG Subsidy (DBTL)?

DBTL stands for **Direct Benefit Transfer for LPG**, also known as **PAHAL**. Under this scheme:

1. You pay the **market price** for your LPG cylinder at the time of purchase
2. The government **subsidy amount is transferred directly** to your Aadhaar-linked bank account
3. You receive up to **12 subsidised refills per year** (for 14.2 kg domestic cylinders)

**Note:** Subsidy amounts change frequently based on international LPG prices. Sometimes the subsidy may be ₹0 when market prices are low.

### How to Check LPG Subsidy Status

#### For Indane (Indian Oil) Customers:
1. Visit **https://cx.indianoil.in**
2. Log in with your registered mobile number
3. Go to **"My Account"** → **"Subsidy Status"**
4. You can see all subsidy payments and their dates

#### For Bharat Gas (BPCL) Customers:
1. Visit **https://my.ebharatgas.com**
2. Log in with your consumer number
3. Check the **subsidy tracker** section

#### For HP Gas (HPCL) Customers:
1. Visit **https://myhpgas.in**
2. Log in with your consumer number
3. Navigate to **subsidy details**

#### Check via Bank Statement:
- Look for credits with narration containing **"DBTL"** or **"PAHAL"** in your bank account statement
- You can also check your bank passbook for these entries

### How to Link Aadhaar to LPG for Subsidy

If your Aadhaar is not linked to your LPG connection, you will **not receive the subsidy**. Here's how to link it:

1. Visit the customer portal of your OMC (links above)
2. Log in to your account
3. Go to **"Update Aadhaar"** or **"Link Aadhaar"** section
4. Enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number
5. Verify via OTP sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile number
6. Once verified, your Aadhaar will be linked

**Offline method:**
1. Visit your LPG distributor
2. Fill in the **Aadhaar seeding form**
3. Attach a photocopy of your Aadhaar card
4. Submit to the distributor

**Important:** Your Aadhaar must also be linked to your bank account. Visit your bank branch to seed Aadhaar if not already done.

### How to Surrender LPG Subsidy (GiveItUp)

If you can afford LPG at market price and want to help others benefit, you can **voluntarily give up your subsidy**:

1. Visit the customer portal of your OMC
2. Log in to your account
3. Look for the **"GiveItUp"** or **"Give Up Subsidy"** option
4. Confirm your decision
5. Your subsidy will be stopped, and you will pay market price for all future refills

**Note:** You can opt back in later if needed by contacting your distributor.

## Part 3: Managing Your LPG Connection

### How to Transfer LPG Connection to a New Address

If you are shifting to a new location:

**Within the same distributor area:**
1. Visit your distributor or their online portal
2. Submit a **transfer request** with your new address proof
3. The distributor updates your address in the system

**To a different city/area (ToD — Transfer of Distribution):**
1. Visit your current distributor and request a **No Objection Certificate (NOC)** / Termination Voucher (TV)
2. Return your regulator and surrender any pending dues
3. Take the TV to the new distributor in your new city
4. Submit the TV along with new KYC documents and address proof
5. The new distributor will issue a fresh connection book

### How to Get a Duplicate Connection Book (Passbook)

If your LPG connection book (passbook) is lost or damaged:

1. Visit your LPG distributor
2. Submit a written request for a **duplicate passbook**
3. Carry your Aadhaar card and a recent passport-size photo
4. Pay the nominal fee (usually ₹25–50)
5. The distributor will issue a duplicate book

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Can men apply for Ujjwala Yojana?

No. The LPG connection under PMUY is issued **only in the name of a woman** (adult female member of the household). The male members cannot be the primary applicant.

### 2. How many subsidised cylinders can I get per year?

You are entitled to a maximum of **12 subsidised refills per year** for a 14.2 kg domestic cylinder. Refills beyond 12 will be at full market price.

### 3. I applied for Ujjwala but haven't received the connection. What should I do?

Visit your nearest LPG distributor with your acknowledgement number and Aadhaar card. You can also call the respective OMC helpline number to check your application status.

### 4. My subsidy is not coming to my bank account. Why?

This can happen if:
- Your Aadhaar is not linked to your LPG connection
- Your Aadhaar is not seeded with your bank account
- Your bank account details are incorrect in the system
- You have exceeded 12 refills in the year

Visit your distributor or OMC portal to verify and update your details.

### 5. Can I have two LPG connections at the same address?

No. Only **one subsidised domestic LPG connection per household** is allowed. If a duplicate is found, the subsidy will be blocked.

### 6. How do I check if my name is in the Ujjwala Yojana beneficiary list?

Visit the PMUY website (https://www.pmuy.gov.in/) and look for the beneficiary list section. You can also check with your local LPG distributor.

### 7. What is the cost of an LPG cylinder under Ujjwala Yojana?

The first refill is **free**. After that, you pay the same market price as other consumers, and the subsidy (if applicable) is transferred to your bank account via DBTL.
