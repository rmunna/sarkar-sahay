# West Bengal Sabuj Sathi Scheme 2025 – Complete Guide

## What is Sabuj Sathi Scheme?

Sabuj Sathi (meaning "Green Companion") is a flagship scheme by the Government of West Bengal launched in September 2015. Under this scheme, **free bicycles** are distributed to students of **Class 9 to Class 12** studying in government and government-aided schools across the state.

The scheme addresses the major problem of school dropout in rural areas caused by long commuting distances. By providing personal transportation, Sabuj Sathi aims to improve attendance, reduce dropouts, and encourage students — especially girls — to continue their education.

Since its launch, the scheme has distributed over **1 crore bicycles** to students across West Bengal, making it one of the largest student welfare programs in India.

## Who is Eligible?

To receive a free bicycle under Sabuj Sathi, the student must meet these criteria:

- **Class:** Must be enrolled in **Class 9, 10, 11, or 12**
- **School Type:** Must be studying in a **government, government-aided, or government-sponsored school** in West Bengal
- **Domicile:** Must be a resident of West Bengal
- **No Prior Benefit:** Should not have received a bicycle under this scheme before
- **Gender:** Both **boys and girls** are eligible (girls often receive priority in distribution)

### Who is NOT Eligible?

- Students in private unaided schools
- Students below Class 9 or above Class 12
- Students who have already received a Sabuj Sathi bicycle
- Students studying outside West Bengal

## Documents Required

### Mandatory Documents
- School identity card
- Aadhaar card of the student
- Admission receipt or school enrollment proof
- Student's photograph

### Additional Documents
- Parent/guardian's Aadhaar card
- Residential proof (if needed for verification)

**Note:** For Sabuj Sathi, the documentation process is minimal because the **school handles most of the application** on behalf of students.

## How to Get Your Free Bicycle – Step-by-Step

### Step 1: Enrollment Verification
Your **school automatically prepares** the list of eligible students. There is usually no separate application needed from the student's side.

### Step 2: School Submits List
The headmaster/principal submits the list of eligible students to the **Block Education Officer (BEO)** or **District Inspector of Schools (DIS)**.

### Step 3: List Verification
The district education department verifies the student list against enrollment records.

### Step 4: Distribution Event
Bicycles are distributed at **school-level or block-level events**. Students are informed by their school about the date and location.

### Step 5: Collect Your Bicycle
Attend the distribution event with your **school ID** and **Aadhaar card**. You (or your parent/guardian) will sign an acknowledgment receipt.

### Step 6: Check Online Status
You can verify your bicycle allotment status on the [official Sabuj Sathi portal](https://wbsabujsathi.gov.in):
1. Visit the website
2. Enter your district, block, and school name
3. Check your name in the beneficiary list

## Processing Time

- **List Preparation:** Done by schools (usually at the start of the academic session)
- **Verification:** 1–2 months
- **Distribution:** Typically within the academic year, often around August–December

## Important Tips

1. **Ensure your enrollment is up to date** — if you recently transferred schools, make sure your new school has added you to the Sabuj Sathi list.
2. **Carry your Aadhaar and school ID** to the distribution event without fail.
3. **Report to your school** if you haven't received information about the bicycle — the headmaster can check your status.
4. **Take care of the bicycle** — while it's free, maintaining it is your responsibility.
5. **Girls are given priority** in many districts, so female students should ensure they are on the list.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Do I need to apply separately for the Sabuj Sathi bicycle?
**A:** No. Your **school prepares the list** of eligible students and submits it to the education department. You don't need to fill any separate application form.

### Q2: Can private school students get a free bicycle?
**A:** No. The scheme is only for students in **government, government-aided, or government-sponsored schools** in West Bengal.

### Q3: I am in Class 9. Am I eligible?
**A:** Yes. Students from **Class 9 to Class 12** are eligible for the Sabuj Sathi bicycle.

### Q4: Can I get a second bicycle if my first one was stolen or damaged?
**A:** Generally, no. The scheme provides **one bicycle per student**. If yours is stolen, file a police complaint and check with your school, but replacement is not guaranteed.

### Q5: How can I check my name in the Sabuj Sathi beneficiary list?
**A:** Visit [wbsabujsathi.gov.in](https://wbsabujsathi.gov.in), select your district, block, and school to search the beneficiary list.

### Q6: Is Sabuj Sathi available in Kolkata?
**A:** Yes, the scheme covers **all districts** of West Bengal, including Kolkata. However, distribution priority may vary by area.

### Q7: Can I also apply for the Swami Vivekananda Scholarship?
**A:** Yes! Sabuj Sathi and the [Swami Vivekananda Merit-cum-Means Scholarship](/guide/wb-swami-vivekananda-scholarship) are separate schemes. You can avail both if eligible.
