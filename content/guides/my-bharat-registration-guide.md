# MY Bharat Registration Guide 2026

**Quick answer:** MY Bharat is the official youth platform of the Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports. It helps youth register, explore opportunities, participate in events, apply for experiential learning and access certificates where available.

Some users search for NYPS 2.0 or older youth portal terms. For current youth opportunities, use the official MY Bharat portal unless a government department gives a different official link.

## What MY Bharat Offers

The official portal highlights:

- Experiential Learning Programmes
- Volunteer opportunities
- Events and participation
- Certificates for eligible activities
- CV Builder functionality
- Opportunities from government, institutions, non-profits and other organisations

MY Bharat is useful for students, first-time volunteers, job seekers, NSS/NYKS-linked youth and young citizens looking for official participation records.

## Registration Checklist

Before you register, keep:

- Active mobile number or email
- Basic personal details
- State and district
- Education or work details, if requested
- Access to your phone/email for OTP or verification

Do not use paid agents for registration. MY Bharat registration should be done through the official portal.

## How To Register on MY Bharat

1. Visit [mybharat.gov.in](https://mybharat.gov.in/).
2. Click **Register Now** or the registration option shown on the portal.
3. Use your mobile number or email.
4. Enter the requested details carefully.
5. Complete OTP or verification if prompted.
6. Save your login details.
7. Open your profile and complete missing education, skill or location details.

After registration, explore opportunities based on your location and interest.

## How To Explore Opportunities

1. Log in to MY Bharat.
2. Open **Explore Opportunities**, **Events** or **Experiential Learning**.
3. Filter by location, sector, department or opportunity type.
4. Read eligibility and dates.
5. Apply only if you can attend or complete the activity.
6. Keep screenshots or confirmation messages.

| Search Term | What To Do |

Do not rely on unofficial pages claiming guaranteed youth certificates or jobs.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can register on MY Bharat?
MY Bharat is meant for Indian youth. Specific opportunities may have their own age, location, education or organisation-level eligibility.

### Is MY Bharat registration free?
Use the official portal. Avoid paid registration agents or unofficial certificate sellers.

### Is MY Bharat the same as NYPS?
Users may search older youth-portal terms such as NYPS. Current opportunities should be verified through the official MY Bharat portal or the concerned ministry/department.

### Does MY Bharat guarantee a job?
No. It provides opportunities, experiential learning, volunteering and participation records. It does not guarantee employment.

### Can I get a certificate from MY Bharat?
Certificates depend on the specific event or opportunity and whether you complete the required activity.
