## What is UP Mahila Samarthya Yojana?

**Uttar Pradesh Mahila Samarthya Yojana** is a state-level women's empowerment scheme that focuses on developing skill-based livelihood opportunities for women across UP, especially in rural and semi-urban areas. It works through **Self Help Groups (SHGs)**, local cooperatives, and skill training centres to improve women's economic independence.

---

## Key Benefits

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Skill training | Tailoring, beauty, handicrafts, electronics assembly |
| Market linkage | Connect women to buyers, e-commerce platforms |
| SHG formation | Help women form and strengthen groups |
| Infrastructure | Common Facility Centres (CFCs) for group production |
| Loan facilitation | Micro-credit through SHG-bank linkage |
| Government support | Partial subsidy on equipment and training |

---

## Who is Eligible?

- **Women aged 18 years and above** in Uttar Pradesh
- Priority to women from rural and semi-urban areas
- Women from **BPL families, SC/ST/OBC communities, and widows**
- Women already in or willing to form SHGs

No income upper limit, but priority to economically weaker sections.

---

## Training Areas Available

Women can access training in:

**Textile and apparel:**
- Tailoring and garment making
- Embroidery (chikankari, zardozi)
- Handloom and power loom

**Beauty and wellness:**
- Mehendi, hair care, skin care
- Beauty parlour certificate course

**Food processing:**
- Pickles, papad, spices, bakery
- Food safety and packaging

**Electronics and light manufacturing:**
- LED bulb assembly
- Mobile phone repair basics

**Digital skills:**
- Basic computer, internet, UPI payments
- E-commerce selling (Flipkart, Amazon)

---

## Common Facility Centres (CFCs)

A key feature — government-funded **Common Facility Centres** are set up in blocks where women can:
- Use machinery and equipment (sewing machines, packaging equipment)
- Produce goods collectively and sell
- Receive training from skilled trainers

These are operated by women's SHG federations.

---

## How to Apply / Join

### Step 1 — Contact BDO Office

Visit your **Block Development Office (BDO)** and ask about Mahila Samarthya Yojana.

### Step 2 — Join or Form an SHG

If not already in an SHG, the BDO will help form or join one (10-12 women per group).

### Step 3 — Enrol in Training

Training enrolment is done at the Common Facility Centre or through the district Women Welfare Department.

### Step 4 — Access Market Linkage

After training, SHG is connected to buyers, MSME units, or online selling platforms.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. I am a homemaker in a UP town. Can I join Mahila Samarthya Yojana?**

Yes. The scheme covers women in urban/semi-urban areas too, not only rural. Visit your block or tehsil office for the nearest SHG or CFC.

**2. Do I need to be in an SHG to get skill training?**

For most benefits (training, market linkage, CFC access), SHG membership is preferred. Individual training may be available in some districts — check with the Women Welfare Department.

**3. Is the training free?**

Training under Mahila Samarthya Yojana is free for women from BPL families. Others may have a nominal fee. Equipment subsidy is provided for SHGs.

**4. I already do chikankari embroidery. Can this scheme help me sell more?**

Yes. The scheme's market linkage component helps traditional craft women connect to bulk buyers, government exhibitions, and online platforms like Government e-Marketplace (GeM).

**5. Which district has the most active Mahila Samarthya centres?**

Lucknow, Agra, Varanasi, Prayagraj, Kanpur, Gorakhpur, and Meerut have active CFCs. Rural districts like Bahraich, Shravasti, and Balrampur also have active SHG federations.
