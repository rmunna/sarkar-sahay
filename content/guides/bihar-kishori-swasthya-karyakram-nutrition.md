## What is Bihar Kishori Swasthya Karyakram?

**Bihar Kishori Swasthya Karyakram** (Adolescent Girls Health Programme) is a Bihar state initiative under the **Women and Child Development Department / ICDS** that provides **nutrition support, health services, and life skills education** to adolescent girls aged 11–18 years across Bihar.

It operates through **anganwadi centres** and **government schools** and addresses iron deficiency anaemia, malnutrition, and low awareness of adolescent health in Bihar's girl child population.

---

## Key Benefits

| Service | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Iron-folic acid (IFA) tablets | Weekly supplementation to prevent anaemia |
| Deworming tablets | Albendazole tablets twice a year |
| Nutrition counselling | Diet, hygiene, menstrual health awareness |
| Health check-up | Height, weight, Haemoglobin (Hb) check at anganwadi |
| Vocational training | Sewing, computer basics (in select blocks) |
| Referral services | Link to PHC/CHC for medical treatment |

---

## Who Benefits?

**All adolescent girls aged 11–18** in Bihar, particularly:
- Girls enrolled in government/aided schools
- Out-of-school girls registered at anganwadi centres
- Girls from SC/ST/OBC and BPL families (priority)

No income criteria — every adolescent girl in Bihar can access health services at the anganwadi.

---

## How the Programme Works

**At Anganwadi Centre:**
- Anganwadi Worker (AWW) registers girls in the 11–18 age group
- Weekly IFA tablets distributed every Monday
- Deworming administered in February and August each year
- Monthly group meetings on nutrition, hygiene, menstrual health

**At School (NHM coordination):**
- Weekly IFA distributed through teachers on fixed days
- Anaemia testing via rapid diagnostic kit (in select districts)

---

## Documents Needed for Registration

To register a girl at the anganwadi:
1. Age proof — birth certificate / Aadhaar / school ID
2. Aadhaar card (girl's)
3. Residence confirmation (ration card / voter ID of parent)

Registration is free and done at the local anganwadi centre.

---

## Anaemia — A Serious Problem in Bihar

Bihar has one of the highest adolescent anaemia rates in India. The programme specifically aims to:
- Bring Haemoglobin levels above 12 g/dL for girls
- Reduce school dropout due to weakness and health issues
- Improve awareness about menstrual hygiene (provides sanitary napkins at select centres)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. My daughter is 13 and out of school. Can she still get IFA tablets?**

Yes. Out-of-school girls are the primary focus. She should be registered at the nearest **Anganwadi Centre** — the AWW will include her in the programme and provide weekly tablets.

**2. Are sanitary napkins also provided?**

Under the Kishori scheme and SABLA (Rajiv Gandhi Scheme for Empowerment of Adolescent Girls), sanitary napkins are provided in select blocks. Check with your local anganwadi.

**3. What is the Hb level check, and why does it matter?**

Hb (haemoglobin) level check measures iron in blood. Below 12 g/dL means anaemia. Anganwadi workers use rapid test kits. If severely anaemic, the girl is referred to PHC for treatment and iron injections.

**4. My daughter takes the IFA tablet but it causes nausea. What should she do?**

IFA tablets are best taken after food — not on empty stomach. If nausea continues, speak to the ASHA or anganwadi worker. Sometimes a liquid syrup form is available for sensitive cases.

**5. Which other nutrition programmes does Bihar run for girls?**

**POSHAN Abhiyan** (national nutrition mission) runs in Bihar through ICDS. **Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana** helps pregnant women. **Mid-Day Meal / PM POSHAN** helps school-age girls through school meals.
