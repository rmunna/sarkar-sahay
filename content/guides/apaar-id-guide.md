# APAAR ID Guide 2026 - One Nation One Student ID

**Quick answer:** APAAR ID stands for **Automated Permanent Academic Account Registry**. It is a unique student identity under the Government of India's **One Nation, One Student ID** initiative. APAAR helps link a student's academic records, credits, certificates and achievements with DigiLocker and the Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) ecosystem.

There are two main routes:

- **School students:** APAAR is generated through the school/UDISE+ process after parent consent and student detail verification.
- **Higher education, skill and other learners:** APAAR can be created through the Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) / DigiLocker flow if the institution and student details are available.

> **Verified on 7 June 2026:** The official APAAR portal says parent consent is voluntary for school students, APAAR is generated against the student's PEN ID in UDISE+, and the APAAR ID is pushed to the student's DigiLocker account. The official ABC portal says APAAR is integrated with ABC and helps manage academic and skill-based credits.

## What Is APAAR ID?

APAAR is a permanent academic identity for students in India. The official APAAR portal describes it as a gateway to a seamless digital academic journey and a way to store, manage and access academic credits, marksheets, certificates, gradesheets, degrees, diplomas and co-curricular accomplishments.

In simple words, APAAR is meant to become the student's academic identity number, while DigiLocker stores the digital card/documents and ABC manages academic credits.

## APAAR ID Full Form

| Short Name | Full Form |
|---|---|
| APAAR | Automated Permanent Academic Account Registry |
| ABC | Academic Bank of Credits |
| PEN | Permanent Education Number / UDISE+ student identifier used in school records |

## Who Should Have APAAR ID?

APAAR is designed for students and learners in India, including:

- School students
- College and university students
- Skill education learners
- Other learners using the academic-credit ecosystem

For school students, the process is normally handled by the school after parent consent and verification. For higher-education learners, the ABC portal provides a DigiLocker-based login/registration route.

## Benefits of APAAR ID

According to the official APAAR and ABC portals, APAAR helps with:

- Student mobility between institutions
- Academic credit recognition and transfer
- Digital access to academic records
- Verification of credentials for admissions, exams, jobs and skilling
- Reducing duplicate records and paperwork
- Linking academic and skill-based achievements in one ecosystem

APAAR should not be treated as a replacement for marksheets, certificates, Aadhaar or DigiLocker. It is an academic identity that helps connect those records.

## How to Get APAAR ID - Choose Your Route

| College/university student | ABC portal / DigiLocker | DigiLocker account, institution details, roll/enrolment details |

## School Student APAAR Process

The official APAAR portal describes this flow:

1. Parents visit the school to learn about APAAR.
2. Parents submit consent voluntarily for APAAR creation.
3. The school verifies the student's demographic details.
4. The UDISE system generates APAAR ID against the student's PEN ID.
5. APAAR ID is pushed into the student's DigiLocker account.

Read the step-by-step school guide: [APAAR ID for School Students](/guide/apaar-id-school-students-parent-consent).

## Higher Education / ABC / DigiLocker Process

The official ABC portal shows a DigiLocker-based flow for higher education and other learners:

1. Create or log in to an authenticated DigiLocker account.
2. Select the education category and proceed to the APAAR ID creation service.
3. Choose your institution, admission year and roll number.
4. Submit the request.
5. Access or download the APAAR card from DigiLocker.

Read the detailed guide: [Create APAAR ID Through DigiLocker](/guide/apaar-id-create-digilocker-students).

## Prerequisites for APAAR ID

The official APAAR FAQ lists two important prerequisites for school APAAR generation:

- Student name in UDISE+ records should match the student's name as per Aadhaar.
- PEN of the student is mandatory for APAAR generation.

It also lists these required details for APAAR creation:

| Detail | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| UDISE+ / PEN | Used by school systems to identify the student |
| Student name | Must match student records and Aadhaar |
| Date of birth | Used for identity matching |
| Gender | Used for demographic verification |
| Mobile number | Used in account/contact flow |
| Mother and father name | Used in student record matching |
| Aadhaar number/name as per Aadhaar | Used for identity authentication |

If the details do not match, use this troubleshooting page: [APAAR ID Aadhaar Name Mismatch Fix](/guide/apaar-id-aadhaar-name-mismatch-fix).

## Where to Find Your APAAR ID

Once generated, APAAR should be available in DigiLocker.

Steps:

1. Log in to [DigiLocker](https://www.digilocker.gov.in/).
2. Open **Issued Documents**.
3. Search for **APAAR**, **Academic Bank of Credits**, or education-related issued documents.
4. Download or view the APAAR card if available.

If it is not visible, read: [APAAR ID Not Showing in DigiLocker - Fix](/guide/apaar-id-not-showing-digilocker-fix).

## FAQs

### What is APAAR ID?
APAAR ID is a student academic identity under the One Nation, One Student ID initiative. It helps connect academic records, credits and achievements across education platforms.

### Is APAAR ID the same as ABC ID?
The current official ABC portal refers to APAAR ID as the learner identity used within the Academic Bank of Credits. Many students still say "ABC ID", but the safer term is APAAR ID in the ABC ecosystem.

### Can a student create APAAR ID directly?
School students generally need the school/UDISE+ flow with parent consent. Higher education and skill learners can use the ABC/DigiLocker route if their institution and details are available.

### Is parent consent required for APAAR ID?
For school students, the official APAAR portal says parents submit consent voluntarily for APAAR ID creation.

### Where is APAAR ID stored?
The official APAAR portal says the generated APAAR ID is pushed to the student's DigiLocker account.

### What if my APAAR ID is not showing?
Check DigiLocker Issued Documents, confirm you are using the correct account, verify Aadhaar/name match, and ask your school or institution to check the status.
