# Union Bank of India KYC Update Online 2026 — Without Branch Visit

Union Bank of India (merged with Andhra Bank and Corporation Bank in 2020) requires periodic KYC updates. You can complete Union Bank KYC update online via the Vyom mobile app or internet banking — no branch visit needed.

---

## Why Union Bank Is Asking for KYC Update

| Reason | Union Bank Action |
|--------|-----------------|
| RBI periodic re-KYC mandate | Debit restrictions after deadline |
| Migration from Andhra/Corporation Bank accounts | KYC re-verification required |
| Address/name change | Records must be updated |
| Account dormancy | KYC mandatory for reactivation |

---

## Method 1: Vyom Mobile App (Recommended)

### Document Upload KYC

1. Open **Union Bank Vyom** app → Login
2. Navigate to **"Services"** → **"KYC Update"** or **"Profile"** → **"KYC Documents"**
3. Upload:
   - Aadhaar card (front + back photos)
   - PAN card
   - Live selfie (taken in-app)
4. Accept self-declaration
5. Submit → Note reference number
6. **Processing: 24–72 hours**

### Aadhaar OTP-based KYC (Instant)

1. Vyom app → KYC → **"Aadhaar-based e-KYC"**
2. Enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number
3. OTP received on Aadhaar-registered mobile
4. Enter OTP → UIDAI validates digitally
5. **KYC updated instantly — no document photos needed**

---

## Method 2: Union Bank Internet Banking

1. Login at **[unionbankonline.co.in](https://www.unionbankonline.co.in)**
2. **"My Account"** → **"KYC Update"** or **"Profile Services"**
3. Upload scanned documents:
   - Aadhaar (front + back — self-attested with date + signature)
   - PAN card
4. Submit
5. **Processing: 2–5 working days**

---

## Method 3: Union Bank Video KYC

For new account openings and re-KYC (select accounts):

1. Vyom app → KYC → **"Video KYC"** (if available)
2. Grant camera access
3. Union Bank executive joins for 3–5 minute call
4. Show original Aadhaar + PAN during call
5. Answer: address, occupation, purpose of account
6. **KYC verified on call — confirmation within 2 hours**

---

## Method 4: Union Bank Branch

1. Visit any Union Bank of India branch
2. Fill **"KYC Updation Form"**
3. Submit:
   - Self-attested Aadhaar copies (front + back)
   - PAN copy
   - Passport-size photo
   - Show originals for verification
4. **Same-day processing**

**Union Bank Customer Care:** **1800-22-2244** (toll-free)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Union Bank Vyom app KYC — is it available for all account types?**
KYC update via Vyom is available for most savings and current account holders. Jan Dhan, senior citizen pension accounts, and some old accounts may require branch visit.

**Union Bank KYC update pending for 10 days — no update?**
Call 1800-22-2244 with your Vyom reference number. If resolved at central level but branch not updated, ask for escalation to zonal office. Alternatively, visit home branch.

**I got a KYC notice for my Union Bank account — is there a deadline?**
Yes — Union Bank gives 30–60 days from notice date. After deadline, debits are restricted. Credit/salary deposits are not blocked — you can still receive money. Complete KYC immediately.

**My Union Bank account was dormant — will KYC update reactivate it?**
KYC update is one part of dormant reactivation. You also need to submit a written request + do a small transaction to reactivate. Visit branch with Aadhaar + PAN + application for reactivation.

**Union Bank asking for income proof for KYC update — is this normal?**
Standard periodic re-KYC only needs Aadhaar + PAN. Income proof (salary slip / ITR) is only needed for enhanced KYC for high-value accounts (typically accounts with >₹10 lakh annual transactions). If asked incorrectly, contact customer care.
