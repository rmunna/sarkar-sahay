# Property Tax Exemption: Who is Eligible & How to Claim

Property tax is levied by municipal corporations and local bodies on all properties — residential, commercial, and industrial. However, the government provides exemptions and concessions to certain categories of property owners to ease their financial burden. This guide covers all major property tax exemptions available in India, eligibility criteria, and how to claim them.

## What is Property Tax Exemption?

Property tax exemption means a full or partial waiver of the property tax that a property owner is required to pay to the municipal corporation. Exemptions are provided by state governments and municipal bodies based on:

- The **category of the owner** (senior citizen, woman, disabled person, etc.)
- The **type of property** (agricultural land, government property, religious/charitable)
- The **use of the property** (self-occupied, educational institution, hospital)

## Who is Eligible for Property Tax Exemption?

### 1. Senior Citizens

Most states offer a **10% to 30% concession** on property tax for senior citizens (aged 60 and above):

| State/City | Concession | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Delhi (MCD) | 30% rebate | Self-occupied property, owner aged 60+ |
| Mumbai (MCGM) | Up to 40% | Self-occupied, single property |
| Bengaluru (BBMP) | 50% for 65+ | Self-occupied residential property |
| Chennai (GCC) | 25% rebate | Owner aged 60+, self-occupied |
| Hyderabad (GHMC) | 10-25% | Depends on property value |

### 2. Women Property Owners

Several states offer concessions to encourage women property ownership:

- **Delhi:** 30% rebate for women-owned self-occupied residential properties
- **Rajasthan:** 25% rebate for women property owners
- **Maharashtra:** Concession varies by municipal corporation
- **Haryana:** Reduced stamp duty (indirect benefit) for women-owned properties

### 3. Ex-Servicemen and Defence Personnel

- **Delhi:** 30% rebate for ex-servicemen on self-occupied residential property
- **Most states:** Similar concessions of 20-30% for retired defence personnel
- **War widows:** Typically receive 100% exemption or the maximum available concession
- Documents needed: Ex-serviceman identity card, discharge certificate

### 4. Persons with Disabilities

- **Delhi (MCD):** 30% rebate for physically disabled persons
- **Maharashtra:** Concessions available for persons with 40%+ disability
- **Most states:** 20-30% concession with disability certificate from competent authority
- Documents needed: Disability certificate issued by the District Medical Board

### 5. Agricultural Land

- Agricultural land is generally **exempt from property tax** in most states
- Exemption applies only if the land is used for agricultural purposes
- Land converted to non-agricultural use loses exemption
- Some states exempt agricultural land below a certain size threshold

### 6. Government Properties

- Properties owned by the **Central Government or State Government** are typically exempt
- Properties used for **government offices, military installations, and public utilities** are exempt
- However, commercial use of government property may attract service charges

### 7. Religious and Charitable Properties

- Places of worship (temples, mosques, churches, gurudwaras) are generally exempt
- Properties used by **registered charitable trusts** for charitable purposes
- **Educational institutions** (government and aided) are typically exempt
- **Public hospitals and dispensaries** run by government or charitable organisations

### 8. Other Exemptions

- **Below Poverty Line (BPL) families** — full or partial exemption in some states
- **Freedom fighters** — exemption in states like Delhi, UP, Maharashtra
- **Single self-occupied residential property** — reduced rates in many municipalities
- **Green building certified properties** — some cities offer rebate for IGBC/GRIHA certified buildings

## How to Claim Property Tax Exemption

### Online Process

1. **Visit your municipal corporation's property tax portal**
2. **Login** with your property ID or registration number
3. **Navigate to "Exemption/Rebate" section** under property tax
4. **Fill the exemption application form** — select the category (senior citizen, woman, disabled, etc.)
5. **Upload supporting documents** — age proof, disability certificate, ex-serviceman card, etc.
6. **Submit the application** and note the reference number
7. **Verification** — municipal officials verify your claim
8. **Exemption applied** — the rebate is reflected in your next property tax bill

### Offline Process

1. **Visit your local municipal ward office**
2. **Submit a written application** for property tax exemption
3. **Attach supporting documents** (self-attested copies)
4. **Carry originals for verification**
5. **Collect acknowledgement receipt**
6. **Exemption reflected** in subsequent tax bills after approval

## State-Wise Property Tax Exemption Rules

### Delhi (MCD)

- 30% rebate: Senior citizens, women, ex-servicemen, physically handicapped
- Self-occupied residential property only
- Must apply at the zonal office or online via [mcdonline.nic.in](https://mcdonline.nic.in/)

### Maharashtra (Mumbai — MCGM)

- Up to 40% concession for senior citizens (self-occupied, single property)
- Exemption for properties under 500 sq ft in certain categories
- Apply via [mcgm.gov.in](https://mcgm.gov.in/) property tax section

### Karnataka (Bengaluru — BBMP)

- 50% exemption for owners aged 65 and above
- Exemption for ex-servicemen and disabled persons
- Apply online via [bbmp.gov.in](https://bbmp.gov.in/)

### Tamil Nadu (Chennai — GCC)

- 25% rebate for senior citizens
- Exemption for properties used exclusively for educational or charitable purposes
- Apply at the zonal office or through Chennai Corporation portal

### Uttar Pradesh

- Exemption for agricultural land and government properties
- Concessions for freedom fighters and war widows
- Apply through the local Nagar Nigam office

### Rajasthan

- 25% rebate for women property owners
- Senior citizen concessions available in major municipal corporations
- Ex-servicemen exemptions as per state government notification

## Important Tips

1. **Apply before the tax due date** — exemptions often apply from the next billing cycle, not retroactively
2. **Renew annually if required** — some municipalities require yearly renewal of exemption claims
3. **Only self-occupied properties qualify** — rented-out properties generally do not get owner-category exemptions
4. **Pay early for additional discount** — many municipal corporations offer 5-15% early payment rebate on top of exemptions
5. **Keep property mutation updated** — ensure your name is correctly recorded in municipal records; see related guides for [property registration](/guide/rera-complaint-file) processes

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Can I get property tax exemption on a rented property?

Generally, no. Most owner-category exemptions (senior citizen, women, disabled) apply only to **self-occupied residential properties**. Rented properties are taxed at the standard rate.

### Q2. Is there a limit on property value for exemption?

Some states cap the exemption for properties below a certain value or area. For example, some municipal corporations only grant senior citizen exemption for properties below 200 sq metres. Check your local rules.

### Q3. Can I claim exemption if the property is jointly owned?

If one co-owner qualifies for exemption (e.g., senior citizen), the exemption may apply to their share. Some municipalities require the qualifying person to be the **first or sole owner**. Check with your municipal corporation.

### Q4. What happens if I don't pay property tax?

Non-payment of property tax can result in penalties (1-2% per month), attachment and auction of the property, and denial of municipal services. Always pay on time and claim applicable exemptions.

### Q5. Is agricultural land completely exempt from property tax?

In most states, agricultural land used for farming is fully exempt from property tax. However, once land is converted for residential or commercial use, it becomes taxable. Land within municipal limits may have different rules.

### Q6. How do I check if my exemption has been applied?

Login to your municipal corporation's property tax portal and check your tax assessment. The exemption/rebate should be reflected as a deduction in the tax calculation.

### Q7. Can NRIs claim property tax exemption?

NRIs owning property in India are liable to pay property tax. Category-based exemptions (senior citizen, women) may still apply if they meet the criteria, but self-occupation requirements can be difficult to fulfil.
