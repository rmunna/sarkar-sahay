## What is Mukhyamantri Yuva Sambal Yojana?

Mukhyamantri Yuva Sambal Yojana (Chief Minister Youth Employment Scheme) is a Rajasthan government scheme that provides monthly unemployment allowance to educated youth who are unemployed and registered with the employment exchange. The scheme aims to provide financial support while youth search for jobs, and also provides skill development opportunities.

**Monthly allowance amounts:**
- **General (male):** ₹4,000 per month
- **Women / Divyang (persons with disability) / Transgender:** ₹4,500 per month

The allowance is paid for a maximum period of **2 years** (24 months).

## Key Benefits

- Monthly cash transfer of ₹4,000–₹4,500 directly to bank account
- **Skills training** — must attend training (7–15 days per month) at a government ITI or skill development center as a condition
- **Internship opportunities** — government offices may offer internships to enrolled youth
- Certificate of participation for skill training sessions
- Access to Rajasthan government's job fair and placement network

## Who is Eligible?

- **Permanent resident of Rajasthan**
- **Age:** 21 to 35 years (general); up to 30 years for some sub-categories — verify current rules at employment.rajasthan.gov.in
- **Education:** Minimum graduation (Bachelor's degree) from a recognized university
- **Employment status:** Must be unemployed — no regular private or government job
- **Income limit:** Annual family income must be below ₹2 lakh
- **Registered** with the Employment Exchange / Rozgar portal of Rajasthan
- Must not be receiving any other government scholarship or stipend

**Not eligible if:**
- Already employed (government or private sector)
- Receiving any other unemployment allowance from state or central government
- Self-employed with income above threshold

## Documents Required

1. Aadhaar card
2. Rajasthan domicile certificate (proof of permanent residence in Rajasthan)
3. Graduation degree / marksheets from recognized university
4. Income certificate of family (issued by Tehsildar/SDM)
5. Bank passbook / cancelled cheque (for IFSC and account number)
6. Passport size photograph
7. Mobile number
8. Employment Exchange registration number (if already registered)
9. Caste certificate (for reserved categories)
10. Disability certificate (if applicable)

## How to Apply Online — Step by Step

1. Go to **employment.rajasthan.gov.in** (official Rajasthan Employment portal)
2. Click **"Mukhyamantri Yuva Sambal Yojana"** or **"Unemployment Allowance"**
3. First register: **"New Applicant? Register Here"**
4. Create account using Aadhaar, mobile number, and email
5. Log in to your account
6. Click **"Apply for Unemployment Allowance"**
7. Fill the form: personal details, education qualification, family income details
8. Upload all required documents
9. Submit the application
10. Note down the **application reference number**
11. Verification is done by the Employment Exchange office — may require physical visit
12. If approved, allowance starts from the first of next month and is credited to your bank account monthly

## Conditions for Receiving Allowance

The allowance is **conditional** — you must:
- Attend skill training sessions assigned by the employment department (minimum 7–15 days per month depending on current rules)
- Report to the Employment Exchange when called
- Inform the department immediately if you get a job (allowance stops from that month)
- Not reject a government job offer without valid reason

**Failure to attend training** may lead to deduction of that month's allowance.

## How to Check Application Status

1. Visit **employment.rajasthan.gov.in**
2. Click **"Track Application Status"** or log in to your account
3. Enter application number or registered mobile number
4. Current status is displayed (under verification / approved / rejected / payment processed)

To check if payment has been credited:
- Log in to your account → "Payment History"
- Or check your bank account/passbook

## How to Check If You Are in the Beneficiary List

Visit the Rajasthan employment portal and log in with your credentials. Your payment status, training attendance, and allowance history are available in your dashboard.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a postgraduate (MA/MSc/MBA) apply?
Yes — postgraduates are eligible. The minimum education is graduation; higher degrees also qualify.

### What if I am doing part-time work?
If you have any regular employment (part-time or full-time) or self-employment earning above ₹2 lakh/year family income, you are not eligible. Casual daily wage work in agriculture may be permissible — check current rules.

### Can women get a higher allowance?
Yes — women, persons with disability, and transgender persons receive ₹4,500/month instead of ₹4,000/month.

### What happens after 2 years?
The allowance is limited to 24 months. After that, if still unemployed, you must reapply and meet the eligibility conditions again (subject to policy at that time). Check the current policy on the portal.

### Can I apply if I live in another state but was born in Rajasthan?
No — you must be a permanent resident of Rajasthan with a domicile certificate. You must currently reside in Rajasthan.

### How long does approval take?
Typically 1–3 months from application, including verification by the employment exchange. Check your application status regularly on the portal.
