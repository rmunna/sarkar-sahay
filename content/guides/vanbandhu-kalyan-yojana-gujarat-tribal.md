## What is Vanbandhu Kalyan Yojana?

**Vanbandhu Kalyan Yojana** (Forest Friends Welfare Scheme) is Gujarat government's flagship integrated development programme for **Scheduled Tribe (ST) communities** in the state's tribal belt — primarily the districts of Dahod, Panchmahal, Narmada, Valsad, Navsari, Tapi, Dang, and adjacent tribal areas.

Rather than isolated welfare measures, Vanbandhu Kalyan Yojana takes a **comprehensive, mission-mode approach** to develop tribal areas in education, health, infrastructure, livelihood, and social protection simultaneously.

## 14 Districts Covered

Vanbandhu Kalyan Yojana's intensive focus areas:
- Dahod, Panchmahal, Mahisagar (eastern)
- Narmada, Bharuch (tribal pockets)
- Valsad, Navsari, Tapi, Surat (south Gujarat tribal belt)
- Dang (100% tribal district)
- Sabarkantha, Arvalli, Banaskantha (northern tribal blocks)

## Key Schemes Under Vanbandhu

**Education:**
- **Eklavya Model Residential Schools (EMRS)** — free Class 6–12 residential education
- **Tribal pre-matric hostels** — free hostel for tribal students near good schools
- **Vanbandhu scholarships** — for higher education (engineering, medical, MBA)
- **Digital literacy** — computer labs in tribal schools

**Health:**
- **Mobile health units** — weekly visits to remote tribal villages
- **Tribal-specific disease control** — sickle cell anaemia (high prevalence in Gujarat tribals)
- **ASHA worker coverage** — every tribal habitation

**Livelihood:**
- **ATMA (Agriculture Technology Management Agency)** support for tribal farmers
- **Tribal cooperative marketing** — tribals sell forest produce, crafts at good prices
- **SHG formation** — micro-credit for tribal women

**Social Security:**
- **Tribal pension** — old age, disability, widow pensions via Social Justice Dept
- **Aadijati Vikas Nidhi** — education and medical emergency fund

## How Tribal Residents Access Benefits

**For individuals:**
- Visit your local **Tribal Development Officer (TDO)** office at taluka level
- Taluka-wise offices coordinate all Vanbandhu Kalyan Yojana benefits

**For hostel and school:**
- Apply at the nearest tribal hostel / EMRS school

**For scholarships:**
- Apply at [tribal.gujarat.gov.in](https://tribal.gujarat.gov.in) scholarship section

**For forest rights claims:**
- Apply through Gram Sabha under Forest Rights Act — TDO assists

**For sickle cell anaemia:**
- Free testing at government hospitals in tribal districts; positive cases get treatment support

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. I am an ST farmer in Dahod. What support can I get from Vanbandhu Kalyan Yojana?**

Visit the Tribal Development Office (TDO) in your taluka. For farming: drip irrigation subsidy, horticulture subsidy, crop insurance. For income: SHG formation, crop marketing support. For family: children's hostel, health van visits.

**2. My child cleared Class 10. Is there a Vanbandhu scholarship for college?**

Yes. Tribal students who pass Class 10 and want to continue education get scholarships via the tribal scholarship portal at tribal.gujarat.gov.in. Additionally, pre-matric and post-matric scholarships are available.

**3. What is sickle cell anaemia support for tribal communities?**

Sickle cell anaemia is more common in Gujarat tribal populations. The state provides: free blood testing, genetic counseling, regular blood transfusions, and hydroxyurea medication at government hospitals. Contact the District Hospital's Sickle Cell Clinic.

**4. Can I claim forest land rights under this scheme?**

The **Forest Rights Act (FRA) 2006** allows tribal communities to claim ancestral forest land rights. Vanbandhu Kalyan Yojana facilitates FRA claims through the Gram Sabha and Revenue Department. Contact the TDO for assistance.

**5. I am not from a tribal community but live in a tribal district. Do I get Vanbandhu benefits?**

Vanbandhu Kalyan Yojana primarily targets ST (Scheduled Tribe) communities. Non-tribal residents in tribal areas benefit indirectly from roads, health facilities, and schools built under the programme, but the targeted benefits are for ST community members only.
