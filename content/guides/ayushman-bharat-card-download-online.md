## What is Ayushman Bharat Health Card?

Ayushman Bharat Health Card (also called PMJAY card or Ayushman card) is issued under the **Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY)** scheme. It gives eligible families health insurance cover of **₹5 lakh per year** for secondary and tertiary hospitalisation. The card is your proof of entitlement — you show it at any empanelled hospital to get cashless treatment.

There are two types:
- **Physical Ayushman Card** — Printed PVC card distributed at Common Service Centres (CSCs) or camps
- **Ayushman e-Card** — Digital version you can download and print from the Beneficiary Portal

Both are equally valid for availing cashless treatment at empanelled hospitals.

## Who is Eligible for Ayushman Bharat Card?

Eligibility is based on the **Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC) 2011** data. The scheme covers:

- **Rural families** identified under deprivation criteria (no adult earning member, kutcha house, SC/ST households, landless labourers, etc.)
- **Urban families** in occupational categories (ragpickers, domestic workers, street vendors, construction workers, etc.)
- **Senior citizens aged 70+** — added under the expanded AB PM-JAY scheme

> **Note:** There is no income limit specified. Eligibility is determined by the SECC database, not by income declaration.

## How to Check Your Eligibility

Before downloading, confirm you are eligible:

1. Visit the official portal: **[beneficiary.nha.gov.in](https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in)**
2. Enter your **mobile number**
3. You will receive an **OTP** — enter it to verify
4. Search using your **name, Aadhaar number, ration card number, or RSBY URN**
5. If your name appears in the list, you are eligible

You can also check eligibility by calling the **Ayushman Bharat helpline: 14555** (toll-free).

## How to Download Ayushman Bharat Card Online

### Method 1: Download from Beneficiary Portal (beneficiary.nha.gov.in)

This is the primary method to download your Ayushman e-card:

1. Go to **[beneficiary.nha.gov.in](https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in)**
2. Click on **"Login"** or **"Download Ayushman Card"**
3. Enter your registered **mobile number**
4. Verify with the **OTP** sent to your phone
5. After login, your **family details** and beneficiary list will appear
6. Click on **"Download Card"** next to your name
7. Complete **Aadhaar-based eKYC** (you will receive another OTP on your Aadhaar-linked mobile)
8. Once verified, your **Ayushman e-card (PDF)** will be ready to download
9. Save the PDF and **take a printout** for hospital visits

### Method 2: Download via Common Service Centre (CSC)

If you cannot download online:

1. Visit your nearest **Common Service Centre (CSC)**
2. Carry your **Aadhaar card** and **mobile phone** (for OTP)
3. The CSC operator will verify your identity and create your Ayushman card
4. You will receive a **printed PVC card** on the spot
5. A nominal service charge may apply at the CSC

> **Important:** The Ayushman card itself is **free of cost**. The government does not charge any fee. Only CSC operators may charge a small printing/service fee.

### Method 3: Get Card at Empanelled Hospital

1. Visit any **PMJAY-empanelled hospital**
2. Go to the **Ayushman Mitra / PMJAY help desk**
3. Provide your **Aadhaar card** for eKYC verification
4. The hospital staff will generate your Ayushman card on the spot

## How to Link Ayushman Card with DigiLocker

You can store your Ayushman card digitally in DigiLocker for easy access:

1. Download the **DigiLocker app** or visit **[digilocker.gov.in](https://www.digilocker.gov.in)**
2. Log in with your **Aadhaar-linked mobile number**
3. Go to the **"Search Documents"** section
4. Search for **"National Health Authority"** or **"PMJAY"** in the issuer list
5. Select **"Ayushman Bharat Health Account Card"**
6. Enter your **PMJAY beneficiary ID** or **Aadhaar number**
7. The card will be fetched and stored in your DigiLocker account

Once linked, you can show the DigiLocker version at any empanelled hospital. Learn more in our [DigiLocker guide](/guide/digilocker-guide).

## How to Re-Download Ayushman Card If Lost

If you lose your physical card or need a fresh copy:

1. Visit **[beneficiary.nha.gov.in](https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in)**
2. Log in with your registered mobile number and OTP
3. Go to your profile and click **"Download Card"**
4. Complete Aadhaar eKYC again if prompted
5. Download and print the new copy

You can download the e-card **unlimited times** — there is no restriction. Alternatively, visit a CSC to get a new PVC card printed.

## How to Check Empanelled Hospital List

To find hospitals where your Ayushman card is accepted:

1. Visit **[hospitals.pmjay.gov.in](https://hospitals.pmjay.gov.in)**
2. Select your **state** and **district**
3. Choose **hospital type** (public/private/both)
4. Optionally filter by **speciality** (cardiology, orthopaedics, etc.)
5. The list of empanelled hospitals with addresses and contact details will appear

You can also call **14555** to ask about empanelled hospitals near you.

## How to Add Family Members to Ayushman Card

Ayushman Bharat covers the **entire family** as listed in the SECC database. To add a new family member:

1. Visit the nearest **CSC** or **empanelled hospital** with Ayushman Mitra desk
2. Carry the **existing family member's Ayushman card**
3. Provide the **new member's Aadhaar card** for eKYC
4. If the new member is listed in the SECC family data, their card will be generated
5. For newborns, carry the **birth certificate** and parent's Ayushman card

> **Note:** You cannot add members online. Family member addition requires physical verification at a CSC or hospital.

## Common Errors and How to Fix Them

| Error | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| "Beneficiary not found" | Your name may not be in SECC data. Call 14555 to verify. |
| OTP not received | Ensure your Aadhaar-linked mobile is active. Wait 2-3 minutes and retry. |
| eKYC failed | Check that your Aadhaar biometrics are updated. Visit the nearest [Aadhaar centre](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online). |
| Card shows wrong details | Visit a CSC with supporting documents for correction. |
| Website not loading | Try during non-peak hours (early morning). Clear browser cache. |
| "Already verified by another member" | Contact the helpline at 14555 for de-duplication. |

## Ayushman Card vs Ayushman e-Card

| Feature | Physical Card (PVC) | e-Card (PDF) |
|---------|---------------------|---------------|
| Issued at | CSC or hospital | Downloaded online |
| Format | PVC plastic card | PDF document |
| Validity | Same | Same |
| Accepted at hospitals | Yes | Yes |
| Cost | Free (CSC may charge for printing) | Free |
| Replacement | Visit CSC again | Download anytime |

Both versions carry the same **14-digit Ayushman Bharat Health Account (ABHA) ID** and are equally valid.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. Is Ayushman Bharat card free?

Yes, the Ayushman Bharat health card is completely **free of cost**. The government does not charge any fee. If anyone asks for payment for the card, it is likely a fraud — report it on 14555.

### 2. Can I download my Ayushman card without Aadhaar?

No. **Aadhaar-based eKYC** is mandatory to download the Ayushman card. Your Aadhaar number and Aadhaar-linked mobile number are required for verification. If you don't have Aadhaar, [apply for one first](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online).

### 3. How many times can I download my Ayushman e-card?

There is **no limit**. You can download your Ayushman e-card from the Beneficiary Portal as many times as needed. Just log in and click download.

### 4. What is the difference between ABHA card and Ayushman card?

**ABHA (Ayushman Bharat Health Account)** is a 14-digit health ID for all Indian citizens under the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission. The **Ayushman PMJAY card** is specifically for PM-JAY beneficiaries entitled to ₹5 lakh insurance cover. ABHA is a digital health identity; the PMJAY card is an insurance entitlement card.

### 5. My name is not in the beneficiary list. What should I do?

If your name is not found on the Beneficiary Portal, it means your family was not included in the SECC 2011 database. You can:
- Call **14555** to confirm
- Visit your nearest CSC for assistance
- Check if your state has an additional scheme that covers more families

### 6. Can I use Ayushman card in any hospital?

No. You can use the Ayushman card only at **PMJAY-empanelled hospitals**. Check the list at [hospitals.pmjay.gov.in](https://hospitals.pmjay.gov.in) before visiting.

### 7. Is Ayushman card valid across all states?

Yes. The Ayushman Bharat PMJAY card is **valid across India**. You can get cashless treatment at any empanelled hospital in any state, not just your home state.
