# MP Free Ration Scheme: Eligibility, Ration Card & How to Apply

The Government of Madhya Pradesh provides free and subsidised ration to eligible families under the **National Food Security Act (NFSA) 2013**. BPL and Antyodaya families can get free wheat, rice, sugar, and other essential food grains through the Public Distribution System (PDS) at their designated fair price shops.

---

## What is the MP Free Ration Scheme?

Under the NFSA, Madhya Pradesh identifies eligible households through the **Samagra portal** and provides them with subsidised or free food grains. The scheme covers:

- **Priority Households (PHH):** 5 kg food grains per person per month at subsidised rates
- **Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY):** 35 kg food grains per family per month at highly subsidised rates
- **PM Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana (PMGKAY):** Free food grains extended by the Central government

As of recent government orders, eligible families receive **free food grains** (wheat at Rs 2/kg, rice at Rs 3/kg, or fully free under PMGKAY extensions).

---

## Ration Entitlements

| Category | Entitlement | Rate |
|----------|-------------|------|
| Priority Household (PHH) | 5 kg per person/month | Rs 1-3/kg (wheat/rice) |
| Antyodaya (AAY) | 35 kg per family/month | Rs 2/kg wheat, Rs 3/kg rice |
| PMGKAY Extension | Additional free grains | Free |

---

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Residency:** Must be a permanent resident of Madhya Pradesh
2. **BPL families:** Below Poverty Line households as per state BPL survey
3. **Antyodaya families:** Poorest of the poor households identified by the government
4. **Samagra registered:** Family must be registered on Samagra portal
5. **Income criteria:** Family should fall under the defined poverty/income criteria
6. **Not excluded:** Government employees, income tax payers, and affluent families are excluded

## Documents Required for Ration Card

- **Samagra ID** (family ID — mandatory in MP)
- **Aadhaar card** of all family members (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- **Address proof** (voter ID, electricity bill, or rent agreement)
- **Income certificate** or BPL certificate
- **Passport-size photographs** of the head of family
- **Bank account details**
- **Mobile number** linked to Aadhaar

## How to Apply for Ration Card in MP

### Online Application

1. Visit the NFSA Samagra portal at [nfsa.samagra.gov.in](https://nfsa.samagra.gov.in/)
2. Check your family's eligibility by entering your **Samagra Family ID**
3. If eligible but not yet receiving ration, click on **"पात्रता पर्ची"** (Eligibility Slip)
4. For new ration card, visit [samagra.gov.in](https://samagra.gov.in/) and ensure family registration is complete
5. Apply through the local body (Gram Panchayat / Nagar Palika)

### Offline Application

1. Visit your **Gram Panchayat** or **Municipal office**
2. Request the ration card application form
3. Fill in family details with all members' information
4. Attach required documents
5. Submit the application
6. The Food & Civil Supplies department will verify and issue the ration card

### Via CSC / MP Online

1. Visit any **Common Service Centre (CSC)** or **MP Online kiosk**
2. Request ration card application assistance
3. Provide Samagra ID and Aadhaar details
4. Pay the nominal processing fee (if any)
5. Collect acknowledgment receipt

3. View your ration card details, eligible members, and entitlement
4. Check monthly distribution records

## Processing Time

- **New ration card:** 15-30 days
- **Correction/update:** 7-15 days

## Important Tips

1. **Link Aadhaar to ration card** — Aadhaar seeding is mandatory for receiving ration under NFSA
2. **Collect ration monthly** — Ration not collected in a month may not be carried over
3. **Check fair price shop** — Know your designated fair price shop (FPS) via the Samagra portal
4. **Biometric verification** — ePoS machines at FPS require Aadhaar biometric verification
5. **Report issues** — Use the CM Helpline (181) or the food department helpline for grievances

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. How much free ration do BPL families get in MP?
PHH families get 5 kg per person/month and AAY families get 35 kg per family/month.

### 2. Is Samagra ID mandatory for ration in MP?
Yes, Samagra ID is the primary identifier for ration distribution in Madhya Pradesh.

### 3. Can I check my ration card details online?
Yes, visit [nfsa.samagra.gov.in](https://nfsa.samagra.gov.in/) and enter your Samagra Family ID.

### 4. What if my name is not in the ration card?
Visit the Gram Panchayat or Municipal office to request addition of family members.

### 5. Is Aadhaar mandatory for receiving ration?
Yes, Aadhaar is mandatory and must be linked (seeded) to the ration card for biometric verification at fair price shops.

### 6. Can I get ration from any fair price shop?
Under the One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC) scheme, you can get ration from any FPS across India using Aadhaar-based portability.

### 7. What should I do if the shopkeeper denies ration?
Lodge a complaint on the CM Helpline (181) or the Food & Civil Supplies department portal.
