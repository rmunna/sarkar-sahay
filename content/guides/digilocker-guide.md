# How to Use DigiLocker — Store and Access Government Documents Online

DigiLocker is India's official platform for storing and accessing government documents digitally. No more carrying photocopies or worrying about lost certificates — everything is available on your phone or computer.

This guide covers everything: creating your account, fetching documents, sharing them, and using DigiLocker for KYC.

---

## What is DigiLocker?

DigiLocker is a free cloud-based platform launched by the **Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)**, Government of India. It works as a **digital document wallet** linked to your Aadhaar number.

**Key features:**
- Store government-issued documents (Aadhaar, PAN, driving license, etc.)
- Fetch documents directly from issuing authorities
- Upload your own scanned documents
- Share documents digitally for verification
- Documents are **legally valid** — equal to original physical copies

**Website:** [https://www.digilocker.gov.in](https://www.digilocker.gov.in)

**Storage:** Every user gets **1 GB free storage** for uploaded documents.

---

## How to Create a DigiLocker Account

You need an **Aadhaar number** linked to a **mobile number** to sign up.

### On the Website

1. Go to [https://www.digilocker.gov.in](https://www.digilocker.gov.in)
2. Click **"Sign Up"**
3. Enter your **mobile number** (must be linked to Aadhaar)
4. Enter the **OTP** received on your mobile
5. Enter your **Aadhaar number**
6. Verify with **Aadhaar OTP** (sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile)
7. Set a **6-digit security PIN**
8. Set a **username and password**
9. Your DigiLocker account is created!

### On the Mobile App

1. Download the **DigiLocker app** from [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.digilocker.android) or Apple App Store
2. Open the app and tap **"Sign Up"**
3. Follow the same steps as above (mobile OTP → Aadhaar verification → set PIN)

> **Tip:** Your Aadhaar-linked mobile number must be active. If it's changed, update it at your nearest Aadhaar centre first.

## How to Fetch Government Documents

This is DigiLocker's most powerful feature. You can **pull official documents** directly from government departments — no scanning needed.

### Steps to Fetch a Document

1. Log in to DigiLocker (website or app)
2. Go to **"Issued Documents"** section
3. Click **"Get More Issued Documents"** or the **search icon**
4. Select the **issuing department** (e.g., CBSE, Transport Department, Income Tax)
5. Select the **document type** (e.g., Driving License, PAN)
6. Enter the required details (document number, date of birth, etc.)
7. Click **"Get Document"**
8. The document will appear in your **Issued Documents** section

> **Note:** Fetched documents come with a **digital signature** from the issuing authority. They are legally equivalent to originals.

## How to Upload Your Own Documents

For documents not available through issuers, you can upload scanned copies.

1. Log in to DigiLocker
2. Go to **"Uploaded Documents"** section
3. Click **"Upload"**
4. Select the **document type** from the dropdown
5. Choose the file from your device (PDF, JPEG, PNG — max 10 MB per file)
6. Click **"Upload"**

**Storage limit:** 1 GB for uploaded documents.

> **Note:** Uploaded documents are **not** digitally signed by the government. They are like carrying a scanned copy. Fetched/issued documents carry more legal weight.

## Is DigiLocker Legally Valid?

**Yes.** DigiLocker documents are legally valid under Indian law.

### Legal Basis

- **IT Act, 2000 — Section 9:** Recognises documents issued via DigiLocker as legally equivalent to original physical documents.
- **Rule 9A of the IT Rules:** Documents accessed through DigiLocker are treated as **original documents** for all purposes.
- A **2016 government notification** declared that DigiLocker-issued documents are at par with original physical documents.

### What This Means for You

- You **don't need to carry physical copies** if you have the document in DigiLocker
- Traffic police, banks, airports, and government offices must accept DigiLocker documents
- Driving license and vehicle RC on DigiLocker are accepted during traffic checks (as per MoRTH guidelines)

## How to Use DigiLocker for KYC

Many banks, telecom companies, and financial institutions accept DigiLocker documents for KYC (Know Your Customer) verification.

### Steps

1. When a bank or service asks for KYC documents, look for the **"DigiLocker"** or **"Fetch from DigiLocker"** option
2. You'll be redirected to DigiLocker login
3. Log in and **grant consent** to share specific documents
4. The organisation fetches the document directly — no upload needed

### Where DigiLocker KYC is Accepted

- Banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, etc.)
- Mutual fund platforms (Zerodha, Groww, etc.)
- Telecom operators for SIM verification
- Government schemes and portals

> **Benefit:** KYC via DigiLocker is **faster and more secure** since documents are verified directly from the source.

## How to Share Documents

You can share your DigiLocker documents with anyone via a **link or URI**.

### Steps to Share

1. Open the document you want to share
2. Click the **"Share"** button
3. Choose to share via **link, email, or document URI**
4. The recipient can view and verify the document online

> **Note:** You control what you share. You can **revoke access** anytime from your DigiLocker dashboard under "Activity" or "Consent" settings.

## Common Issues & Solutions

### 1. OTP Not Received
- Make sure your mobile number is linked to Aadhaar
- Wait 2–3 minutes and try again
- Check if your phone has network/signal issues
- Try the **"Resend OTP"** option

### 2. Document Not Found When Fetching
- Double-check the document number and details you entered
- The issuing department may not have uploaded it yet
- Try after a few days — new documents take time to reflect

### 3. Aadhaar Not Linking
- Your Aadhaar-linked mobile number must be active
- If you changed your number, update it at an Aadhaar Seva Kendra first

### 4. App Crashing or Not Loading
- Update to the latest version of the app
- Clear app cache (Settings → Apps → DigiLocker → Clear Cache)
- Restart your phone and try again

### 5. Driving License Not Showing
- Make sure your state transport department has digitised your license
- Try fetching using your **DL number and date of birth**
- Some older licenses may not be available yet

### 6. Storage Full
- You have 1 GB for uploaded documents
- Delete old uploaded documents you no longer need
- Fetched/issued documents don't count towards storage

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Is DigiLocker free?
**Yes.** DigiLocker is completely free for all Indian citizens. There are no charges for creating an account, fetching documents, or storage.

### Q2: Can I use DigiLocker without Aadhaar?
Currently, **Aadhaar is required** to create a DigiLocker account. Your mobile number must be linked to your Aadhaar.

### Q3: Is DigiLocker driving license accepted by traffic police?
**Yes.** As per MoRTH (Ministry of Road Transport and Highways) guidelines, driving license and vehicle RC on DigiLocker are valid during traffic checks. Police must accept them.

### Q4: Can I access DigiLocker offline?
The mobile app caches previously viewed documents, so you can **show them offline**. However, you need internet to fetch new documents or log in for the first time.

### Q5: What if I lose my phone? Are my documents safe?
**Yes.** Your documents are stored on government servers, not on your phone. Simply log in from another device using your username and password.

### Q6: How is DigiLocker different from mParivahan?
**mParivahan** only shows transport documents (DL, RC). **DigiLocker** is a comprehensive platform for all types of government documents — education, health, identity, transport, and more.

### Q7: Can NRIs use DigiLocker?
**Yes**, as long as they have an Indian Aadhaar number with an active linked mobile number.
