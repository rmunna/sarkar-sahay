## What is Bihar Kushal Yuva Programme (KYP)?

**Kushal Yuva Programme (KYP)** is Bihar government's flagship youth skilling scheme under the **7 Nishchay (Seven Resolutions)** programme. It provides **free, 240-hour training** in:
- **Computer basics** (MS Office, internet, email, Tally basics)
- **Soft skills** (communication, personality development, life skills)
- **English language proficiency** (spoken and written English)

The programme is designed for youth who have passed Class 10 but may lack digital and communication skills needed for employment.

Key features:
- **Completely free** — no fees at all
- **240-hour programme** (3–4 months, part-time)
- Certificate issued by **Bihar Skill Development Mission**
- Recognised across Bihar for government and private jobs
- Centres spread across all blocks of Bihar

## Who is Eligible?

- Age: **15 to 28 years**
- Educational qualification: **Class 10 passed or above**
- Must be a **Bihar resident**
- No income limit — open to all youth

## Training Curriculum (240 Hours)

| Module | Hours | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Communication Skills | 40 hrs | Hindi/English communication, professional speaking |
| English | 40 hrs | Grammar, reading, writing, spoken English |
| Computer Basics | 80 hrs | MS Word, Excel, PowerPoint, internet, email |
| Soft Skills | 40 hrs | Personality, time management, interview skills |
| Financial Literacy | 20 hrs | Banking, digital payments, savings basics |
| Health & Hygiene | 20 hrs | Basic health awareness |

## How to Apply — Step by Step

1. Go to **[skillmissionbihar.org](https://skillmissionbihar.org)**
2. Click **"KYP Registration"** or find the KYP section
3. Enter your details: name, date of birth, mobile, educational qualification
4. Select a **KYP centre** near you (centres are at blocks, schools, and ITIs)
5. Submit registration
6. You will be contacted by the centre for batch joining

### Walk-In Registration

You can also directly visit any **KYP training centre** in your block or district:
- Bring Aadhaar card and Class 10 certificate
- Register and join the next available batch

Centres are at: Government schools, ITIs, Polytechnics, Skill Development centres, and private partner institutes empanelled by Bihar Skill Development Mission.

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card**
2. **Class 10 marksheet** (or higher — proof of Class 10 pass)
3. **Bihar residence proof** (Aadhaar with Bihar address)
4. Passport-size photograph

## KYP Certificate

After completing the course and passing the assessment:
- **Bihar Skill Development Mission** certificate
- Digitally signed and verifiable
- Accepted by government employers in Bihar for:
  - BPSC exams (some posts)
  - Bihar government job applications requiring computer knowledge
  - Private sector employment

## Placement Support

KYP centres provide **placement assistance** after certificate:
- Job fairs organised by Bihar Skill Development Mission
- Connection to employer database
- Rozgar Mela events in districts

## Bihar KYP vs PMKVY — Comparison

| Feature | KYP (Bihar) | PMKVY (Central) |
|---|---|---|
| Duration | 240 hours | 150–400 hours |
| Focus | Computer + communication | Trade-specific skills |
| Cash reward | None (free certificate) | ₹8,000 reward |
| Eligibility | Class 10 pass | Dropout/graduates |
| Geographic focus | Bihar | All India |

Both are complementary — youth can do KYP first for digital skills and then PMKVY for a trade skill.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is there a stipend or cash reward for completing KYP?
No — KYP does not come with a cash reward. The benefit is the **free training and government certificate** which improves employability.

### My area doesn't have a KYP centre. What do I do?
Check [skillmissionbihar.org](https://skillmissionbihar.org) for the nearest centre. KYP centres are at every block — the nearest centre may be 5–15 km away. New centres are added regularly.

### I am 30 years old. Am I eligible?
No — KYP is for youth aged **15 to 28 years**. If you are above 28, consider other skill training options like PMKVY (up to 35–45 years).

### How long does the training take daily?
KYP is typically conducted for **4–6 hours per day, 6 days a week**. The full 240 hours take **6–8 weeks** (approximately 1.5–2 months). Some part-time formats may extend to 3–4 months.

### Is the KYP certificate valid in other states?
The KYP certificate is issued by **Bihar Skill Development Mission** and is primarily for Bihar-based employment. For national recognition, also pursue a **NSDC/Sector Skill Council** certificate through PMKVY.
