# SBI Passbook Update Online: How to Check Transactions

Need to check your recent SBI account transactions? You no longer need to visit the branch every time. SBI provides multiple ways to view your account statement and update your passbook — online through YONO app, net banking, e-statement, and at the branch passbook printing kiosk.

This guide covers all methods to check your SBI transaction history with step-by-step instructions.

---

## Method 1: Check Transactions on YONO SBI App

The YONO SBI app is the easiest way to view your recent transactions on your phone.

### Steps:

1. Download **YONO SBI** from [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.sbi.lotusintouch) or Apple App Store
2. Log in with your **username and password** (or YONO PIN / biometric)
3. Tap on your **savings account** on the dashboard
4. Tap **"View Transactions"** or **"Account Statement"**
5. Select the **date range** (last 7 days, 1 month, 3 months, or custom range)
6. Your transaction history will be displayed on screen
7. You can **download** the statement as PDF from the options menu

**Tip:** YONO shows up to 2 years of transaction history. For older records, use net banking.

---

## Method 2: Download Statement via SBI Net Banking

SBI Internet Banking allows you to view and download detailed account statements.

### Steps:

1. Visit [https://www.onlinesbi.sbi/](https://www.onlinesbi.sbi/)
2. Log in with your **username, password, and OTP**
3. Go to **"Account Statement"** under the **"My Accounts & Profile"** menu
4. Select your **account number** from the dropdown
5. Choose the **date range** (you can select up to 2 years at a time)
6. Select the **format** — HTML (view on screen), PDF, Excel, or OFX
7. Click **"Submit"** to generate the statement
8. Click **"Download"** to save the file to your device

**Note:** You can view up to 5 years of transaction history through net banking. For statements older than 5 years, visit your home branch.

---

## Method 3: Request E-Statement by Email

SBI can send your monthly account statement directly to your registered email address.

### How to Activate E-Statement:

1. Log in to [SBI Net Banking](https://www.onlinesbi.sbi/)
2. Go to **"My Accounts & Profile"** → **"e-Statement"**
3. Select your account and choose **frequency** (monthly)
4. Confirm your **registered email address**
5. Click **"Submit"**

Once activated, SBI will send your account statement as a **password-protected PDF** to your email every month. The PDF password is usually your **account number + date of birth** (in DDMMYYYY format).

---

## Method 4: Branch Passbook Printing Kiosk

Most SBI branches have **self-service passbook printing machines** where you can update your physical passbook without waiting in queue.

### Steps:

1. Visit your nearest SBI branch
2. Locate the **passbook printing kiosk** (usually near the entrance)
3. Insert your **passbook** into the printer (open to the last printed page)
4. The machine will automatically detect your account and print pending transactions
5. Remove your passbook once printing is complete

**Tips:**
- The machine works during **branch hours** (10:00 AM to 4:00 PM on working days)
- Ensure your passbook has blank pages — if full, request a new passbook at the counter
- Some branches may require you to **swipe your ATM card** at the kiosk for verification

---

## Method 5: Request Statement at SBI Branch Counter

If you cannot access online methods, visit your SBI branch and request a printed statement.

### Steps:

1. Visit your **home branch** (or any SBI branch)
2. Fill out an **account statement request form**
3. Submit it at the counter with your **passbook** or **ID proof**
4. The bank will print your statement for the requested date range

**Note:** Branches may charge a nominal fee (₹50–₹100) for printed statements beyond a certain period, especially for older records.

## Important Tips

1. **Register for e-statement** to get monthly transaction records automatically in your email
2. **YONO app** is the fastest way to check recent transactions — no branch visit needed
3. **Keep your passbook updated** regularly to avoid missing entries (kiosks have a limit on how many pages can be printed at once)
4. **Download statements in PDF** format from net banking for tax filing and loan applications
5. **For old statements** (beyond 5 years), submit a written request at your home branch — processing may take 2-3 working days

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Can I update my SBI passbook at any branch?

Yes, you can update your passbook at any SBI branch that has a passbook printing kiosk. However, if your passbook is full and you need a new one, you must visit your **home branch**.

### Q2: How far back can I see transactions on YONO SBI?

YONO SBI allows you to view transactions for up to **2 years**. For older transactions, use SBI Net Banking (up to 5 years) or request a statement from your branch.

### Q3: Is there a charge for downloading SBI account statement online?

No, downloading your account statement through YONO app or SBI Net Banking is completely **free**. Charges may apply only for printed statements requested at the branch counter for extended periods.

### Q4: What is the password for SBI e-statement PDF?

The e-statement PDF password is typically your **account number followed by your date of birth** in DDMMYYYY format. For example, if your account number is 12345678901 and DOB is 15 Jan 1990, the password would be 1234567890115011990.

### Q5: Can I get an SBI account statement without net banking?

Yes. You can use the **YONO SBI app**, visit a **branch passbook printing kiosk**, or request a printed statement at the **branch counter**. You can also call SBI customer care at **1800-11-2211** for assistance.

### Q6: How do I download SBI bank statement for visa application?

Log in to [SBI Net Banking](https://www.onlinesbi.sbi/), go to Account Statement, select your account, choose the last **6 months** date range, and download as **PDF**. This stamped PDF is generally accepted for visa applications. For a physical stamped copy, visit your branch.
