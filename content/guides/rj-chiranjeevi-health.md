## What is Chiranjeevi Health Insurance Scheme?

Mukhyamantri Chiranjeevi Swasthya Bima Yojana (now Mukhyamantri Ayushman Arogya Yojana) is Rajasthan's universal health insurance scheme providing **cashless treatment up to ₹25 lakh per family per year** at empanelled hospitals. Launched in 2021, it is one of India's most comprehensive state health schemes.

The scheme operates in convergence with **Ayushman Bharat – PMJAY** and covers all families in Rajasthan.

### Coverage

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Coverage per family per year | ₹25,00,000 (₹25 lakh) |
| General diseases | Up to ₹25 lakh |
| Critical diseases (organ transplant, etc.) | Up to ₹25 lakh |
| Procedures covered | 1,900+ packages |
| Pre-hospitalization | 5 days |
| Post-hospitalization | 15 days |

## Who is Eligible?

### Free Coverage (No Premium)
- **All families** registered on Jan Aadhaar in Rajasthan
- NFSA beneficiaries (food security card holders)
- SECC 2011 listed families
- Small and marginal farmers
- Contractual workers (safai karamcharis, etc.)
- COVID-19 ex-gratia eligible families

### With Nominal Premium
- All other Rajasthan families can register by paying **₹850/year per family**

> Effectively, every Rajasthan family can be covered — either free or with minimal premium.

## Documents Required

- **Jan Aadhaar card** (mandatory — Rajasthan's family ID)
- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
- If paying premium: bank account/UPI for payment
- At hospital: Jan Aadhaar card for identity verification

## How to Register — Step-by-Step

### Online Registration

1. **Visit** [chiranjeevi.rajasthan.gov.in](https://chiranjeevi.rajasthan.gov.in)
2. **Login** using Jan Aadhaar number or SSO ID
3. **Verify** family details
4. **Select** category (free or premium-paying)
5. **Pay premium** if applicable (₹850/year online)
6. **Download** or note the registration confirmation
7. **Policy active** from the registration date

### Through E-Mitra Kiosk

1. **Visit** the nearest E-Mitra kiosk
2. **Carry** Jan Aadhaar card and Aadhaar
3. **E-Mitra operator** registers you on the portal
4. **Pay premium** if applicable
5. **Collect** registration receipt

### Availing Treatment

1. **Visit** any empanelled hospital (government or private)
2. **Show Jan Aadhaar card** at the reception desk
3. **Biometric verification** via Aadhaar
4. **Hospital raises** pre-authorization request
5. **Cashless treatment** — no payment for covered procedures
6. **Post-discharge** follow-up covered for 15 days

## Finding Empanelled Hospitals

- Visit [chiranjeevi.rajasthan.gov.in](https://chiranjeevi.rajasthan.gov.in) → Hospital List
- Call helpline: **14555** or **181** (toll-free)

## Processing Time

- Registration: **Instant** (online or E-Mitra)
- Policy activation: **Immediate** after registration
- Pre-authorization at hospital: **2-6 hours**

## Fees

| Category | Annual Premium |
|----------|---------------|
| NFSA/SECC/farmers/contractual workers | Free |
| All other families | ₹850/year |

## Important Tips

1. **Get Jan Aadhaar first** — Without Jan Aadhaar, you cannot register. Apply for it at any E-Mitra
2. **Register before hospitalization** — While registration is instant, ensure you're registered before needing treatment
3. **Carry Jan Aadhaar always** — This is your health card; keep it handy
4. **Use empanelled hospitals only** — Treatment at non-empanelled hospitals is not covered
5. **Report issues** — Call **14555** if any hospital refuses cashless treatment or demands payment

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Is Chiranjeevi the same as Ayushman Bharat?
Chiranjeevi is Rajasthan's state scheme that operates in convergence with AB-PMJAY. Together, they provide coverage of up to ₹25 lakh per family.

### Q2. Can I use this outside Rajasthan?
Under AB-PMJAY portability, you can get cashless treatment at empanelled hospitals **across India**.

### Q3. Are pre-existing diseases covered?
Yes. **No waiting period** for pre-existing conditions.

### Q4. What is Jan Aadhaar?
Jan Aadhaar is Rajasthan's family identity card. It is mandatory for Chiranjeevi registration. Apply at the nearest E-Mitra kiosk.

### Q5. Can I register mid-year?
Yes. Registration is open throughout the year. Coverage starts from the date of registration.

### Q6. Is dialysis covered?
Yes. Regular dialysis at empanelled centres is covered under the scheme.

### Q7. What if the hospital overcharges?
Call the helpline **14555** immediately. Hospitals overcharging for covered procedures can be de-empanelled.
