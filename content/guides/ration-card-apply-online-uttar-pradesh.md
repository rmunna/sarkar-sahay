## Overview

Uttar Pradesh has over **3.62 crore ration cards** covering more than **14.61 crore beneficiaries** across the state. The UP Food & Civil Supplies Department (खाद्य एवं रसद विभाग) manages the entire Public Distribution System through its official portal **fcs.up.gov.in**.

This guide covers the complete process to apply for a new ration card in Uttar Pradesh. For the national-level process and comparison across states, see our [national ration card guide](/guide/ration-card-apply-online).

---

## Types of Ration Cards in UP

Uttar Pradesh issues ration cards under the National Food Security Act (NFSA) in three categories:

### 1. AAY (Antyodaya Anna Yojana) — Yellow Card
- For the **poorest of the poor** families
- **35 kg food grains per month** per household
- Rice at ₹3/kg, wheat at ₹2/kg
- Widows, disabled persons, senior citizens with no income, primitive tribal groups

### 2. PHH (Priority Household) — Pink/Saffron Card
- For **Below Poverty Line** families
- **5 kg per person per month**
- Rice at ₹3/kg, wheat at ₹2/kg, coarse grains at ₹1/kg
- Annual household income below the state-defined threshold

### 3. NPHH (Non-Priority Household) — White Card
- For **Above Poverty Line** families
- Limited subsidized quota (sugar, kerosene where available)
- Useful as identity/address proof
- No income restriction

> **Note:** UP also recognises the **Eligible Household (EHH)** sub-category under NFSA for specific vulnerable groups.

## Who Can Apply?

You can apply for a new ration card in UP if:

- You are a **resident of Uttar Pradesh**
- Your household does **not already have** a ration card in any state
- You have a **valid Aadhaar card** (mandatory for all family members)
- You are the **head of the household** (or applying on behalf)

### Who Cannot Apply?
- Government employees with income above ₹10 lakh/year (for PHH/AAY)
- Families already holding a ration card in UP or any other state
- Income tax payees (for subsidized categories)

## Documents Required

Keep these ready before starting your application:

| Document | Details |
|----------|---------|
| **Aadhaar Card** | Of all family members (mandatory) |
| **Address Proof** | Electricity bill, water bill, or rent agreement |
| **Income Certificate** | From Tehsil/SDM office (for PHH/AAY) |
| **Passport-size Photos** | Of the head of household |
| **Family Details** | Name, age, gender, Aadhaar of each member |
| **Bank Account Details** | For DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer) |
| **Mobile Number** | Linked to Aadhaar |

> **Tip:** If you are a migrant worker who has moved to UP, you may need a domicile or residence certificate from your local authority.

## How to Apply Online — Step by Step

### Step 1: Visit the Official Portal

Go to **[fcs.up.gov.in](https://fcs.up.gov.in)** — this is the official website of the UP Food & Civil Supplies Department.

### Step 2: Navigate to Ration Card Services

- Look for **"नवीन राशन कार्ड के लिए आवेदन"** (Apply for New Ration Card) on the homepage
- Or go to the **"ePDS"** / **"Online Services"** section
- Click on **"New Ration Card Application"**

### Step 3: Register / Login

- If you are a first-time user, **register** with your mobile number and Aadhaar
- You will receive an **OTP** for verification
- Create your login credentials

### Step 4: Fill the Application Form

The online form asks for:

1. **District (जनपद)** — Select your district from the dropdown
2. **Tehsil (तहसील)** — Select your tehsil
3. **Town/Village** — Urban or rural area
4. **Head of Household details** — Name, father's/husband's name, Aadhaar, mobile
5. **Family members** — Add each member with name, age, gender, Aadhaar, relationship
6. **Category** — Select AAY/PHH/NPHH based on your eligibility
7. **Address details** — Full residential address
8. **Bank account** — Account number, IFSC code

### Step 5: Upload Documents

Upload scanned copies of:
- Aadhaar card (front and back)
- Passport photo of household head
- Income certificate (if applying for PHH/AAY)
- Address proof

> **File format:** JPG/PDF, usually under 200 KB per document.

### Step 6: Submit and Note Application Number

- Review all details carefully
- Click **"Submit"** (सबमिट करें)
- **Save your application number** — you will need it to track status
- You will get an SMS confirmation on your registered mobile

## Offline Application Process (CSC/Tehsil)

If you prefer offline or face issues online:

1. **Visit your nearest CSC** (Common Service Centre / Jan Seva Kendra) or **Tehsil office**
2. Collect the **ration card application form** (Form available free of cost)
3. Fill it with required details and attach documents
4. Submit at the **District Supply Office (जिला आपूर्ति कार्यालय)**
5. The Supply Inspector will verify your application with a **home visit**
6. After verification, the card is issued

### District/Tehsil Level Process

The application flows through these levels:

1. **Application submitted** → District Supply Office / online portal
2. **Verification** → Area Supply Inspector conducts field verification
3. **Approval** → District Supply Officer (DSO) approves the application
4. **Card issued** → Digital ration card generated on the portal
5. **FPS linked** → You are linked to your nearest Fair Price Shop (उचित दर दुकान)

## Fees and Processing Time

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Application Fee** | **Free** (no charge for new ration card application) |
| **CSC Service Charge** | ₹10–30 (if applying through Jan Seva Kendra) |
| **Processing Time** | **15–30 days** from submission |
| **Verification Visit** | Within 7–15 days of application |

> **Important:** There is **no government fee** for getting a ration card in UP. If anyone asks for money beyond CSC charges, it may be fraud. Report at the toll-free number **1967** or **1800-1800-150**.

## How to Check Application Status

1. Visit **[fcs.up.gov.in](https://fcs.up.gov.in)**
2. Go to **"राशन कार्ड की स्तिथि"** (Ration Card Status)
3. Enter your **application number** or **Aadhaar number**
4. Click **Search** — current status will be displayed

You can also:
- Call the **UP Food Helpline: 1967**
- Call toll-free: **1800-1800-150**
- Visit your District Supply Office in person

---

## How to Download Your UP Ration Card

Once approved:

1. Go to **fcs.up.gov.in**
2. Click on **"राशन कार्ड खोजें"** (Search Ration Card)
3. Select **District → Tehsil → Town/Village**
4. Search by **head of household name** or **ration card number**
5. View and **download/print** your e-ration card

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does it take to get a ration card in UP?
The process takes **15 to 30 days** after application submission, including field verification by the Supply Inspector.

### Can I apply for a ration card online in UP?
Yes, you can apply online through the official portal **fcs.up.gov.in**. You can also apply through CSC (Jan Seva Kendra) or your Tehsil office.

### Is there any fee for ration card in UP?
**No.** The government does not charge any fee for issuing a new ration card. CSC centres may charge a nominal service fee of ₹10–30.

### What if my ration card application is rejected?
You can:
- Check the rejection reason on the portal
- Correct the issue and **reapply**
- Visit your District Supply Office for clarification
- File a complaint on the helpline **1967**

### Can I transfer my ration card from another state to UP?
Under the **One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC)** scheme, you can use your existing ration card at any Fair Price Shop in India. For permanent transfer, you need to surrender your old card and apply fresh in UP.

### How many people can be added to one ration card?
There is no strict limit — all members of the household living together can be included. Each member needs an Aadhaar card.
