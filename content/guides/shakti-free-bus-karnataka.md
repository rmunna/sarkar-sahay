# Karnataka Shakti Scheme — Free Bus Travel for Women (2026)

The **Shakti Scheme** is a flagship initiative by the Government of Karnataka that provides **free bus travel to all women** on state-run buses. Launched on **11 June 2023** by Chief Minister Siddaramaiah as one of the five guarantee schemes, Shakti has become one of the most widely used welfare programmes in the state.

Since its launch, over **668 crore free tickets** have been issued to women passengers across Karnataka. More than **75 lakh women use the scheme daily**, with about 22–24 lakh women availing free bus travel in Bengaluru alone — saving families thousands of rupees every month on commuting costs.

> **2026 Update:** The Karnataka Cabinet has approved new Shakti Smart Cards with Namma Metro integration. Rollout is expected by mid-2026. [Read more below](#upcoming-shakti-smart-card-upgrade-2026).

---

## Who Is Eligible?

The Shakti scheme is available to **all women** — there is no income limit, age restriction, or caste-based criterion.

### Eligibility Criteria

- **Gender:** Women and transgender persons
- **Residency:** Must be a resident of Karnataka ([Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) address in Karnataka)
- **ID Proof:** Must carry Aadhaar card or Shakti Smart Card (once issued) while travelling
- **No income limit:** The scheme is universal — available to all women regardless of income
- **No age limit:** Women of all ages can avail the benefit. Minor girls can travel with a guardian's Aadhaar

> **Note:** Men, including senior citizens and students, are **not** eligible under this scheme. Check the [Gruha Lakshmi scheme](/guide/gruha-lakshmi-karnataka) for another women-centric Karnataka benefit.

---

## Which Buses Are Covered?

The Shakti scheme covers **all non-AC (ordinary) services** operated by Karnataka's four state road transport corporations:

| Corporation | Area Covered |
|---|---|
| **KSRTC** (Karnataka State Road Transport Corporation) | Bengaluru division and inter-district routes |
| **BMTC** (Bangalore Metropolitan Transport Corporation) | Bengaluru city buses |
| **NWKRTC** (North Western Karnataka Road Transport Corporation) | Hubli-Dharwad, Belgaum and surrounding districts |
| **NEKRTC** (North Eastern Karnataka Road Transport Corporation) | Kalaburagi, Bidar, Raichur and surrounding districts |

### What's NOT Covered

- ❌ **AC buses** (Airavat, Airavat Club Class, Flybus, AC Sleeper)
- ❌ **Premium/luxury services** (Ambari, Corona)
- ❌ **Reserved/booked tickets** on KSRTC online booking
- ❌ **Private buses** — only government-run state transport buses
- ❌ **Interstate services** — only routes within Karnataka
- ❌ **BMTC Vajra (AC) buses** — not covered under Shakti
- ❌ **Namma Metro** — not covered currently (metro integration coming in 2026)

## How to Get the Shakti Smart Card

### Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar Card** (with Karnataka address) — [How to update Aadhaar address](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
2. **Recent passport-size photograph**
3. **Mobile number** linked to Aadhaar

### Application Process

There are two ways to apply for the Shakti Smart Card — at a bus depot (walk-in) or online through the Seva Sindhu portal.

#### Option 1: At the Bus Depot (Walk-in) — Recommended

This is the **fastest method** and most women prefer it because the card can be issued on the spot.

1. Visit your nearest **KSRTC, BMTC, NWKRTC, or NEKRTC bus depot/station**
2. Go to the **Shakti Card counter** (dedicated counters are available at major depots)
3. Fill in the application form
4. Submit your Aadhaar card copy and photograph
5. Biometric verification (fingerprint/iris scan) is done on the spot
6. The **Shakti Smart Card is issued immediately** or within a few days

**Tip:** Major bus stations like Majestic (Kempegowda Bus Station), Shivajinagar, and district bus stands have dedicated Shakti counters with shorter wait times on weekdays.

#### Option 2: Online via Seva Sindhu Portal

1. Visit [sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in)
2. Register/login with your mobile number and OTP
3. Search for **"Shakti Smart Card"** in the services section
4. Fill in the application form with personal details (name, age, address as per Aadhaar)
5. Upload scanned copy of **Aadhaar card** and **passport-size photograph**
6. Submit the application and note down the **application reference number**
7. You will receive an SMS confirmation on your registered mobile
8. Collect the card from the **designated depot** mentioned during application

> **Cost:** The Shakti Smart Card is issued **free of charge**. No fees are required at any stage.

### Online vs Offline Application — Which Is Better?

| Factor | Walk-in (Bus Depot) | Online (Seva Sindhu) |
|---|---|---|
| **Speed** | Card issued same day or in 2–3 days | Takes 7–15 days for processing |
| **Biometric** | Done on the spot | May need depot visit for verification |
| **Ease** | Simple, staff-assisted | Requires internet, document uploads |
| **Availability** | All major depots | [sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in) |
| **Best for** | Quick access, first-time applicants | Those unable to visit a depot easily |

**Our recommendation:** If you live near a bus depot, the **walk-in method is faster and simpler**. Online is best for women in remote areas or those who find it difficult to visit a depot during working hours.

## BMTC Smart Card for Ladies — Bengaluru Women

For women in Bengaluru, the **BMTC Shakti Smart Card** works the same way as the general Shakti card but is specifically issued through BMTC depots.

### How to Apply for BMTC Shakti Card

1. Visit any **BMTC bus depot** in Bengaluru (Shantinagar, Jayanagar, Yeshwanthpur, Kengeri, etc.)
2. Carry your **Aadhaar card** with Bengaluru/Karnataka address
3. Fill in the form at the Shakti card counter
4. Complete biometric verification
5. Card is issued on the spot or within 2–3 working days

**BMTC helpline:** You can call **BMTC customer care** at the numbers listed on [mybmtc.com](https://www.mybmtc.com) for depot locations and timing.

> The BMTC Shakti card works on **all BMTC ordinary (non-AC) buses** across Bengaluru. It does **not** work on Vajra (AC) buses.

## How to Track Your Shakti Card Application Status

If you applied online through Seva Sindhu:

1. Visit [sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Track Application Status"** or **"Know Your Application Status"**
3. Enter your **application reference number** (received via SMS at the time of submission)
4. Your current application status will be displayed

If you applied at a bus depot:

- Ask at the **same counter** where you submitted the application
- Carry your **Aadhaar card** for reference
- Most depot applications are processed within **2–3 working days**

## Upcoming Shakti Smart Card Upgrade (2026)

In **February 2026**, the Karnataka Cabinet approved a major upgrade to the Shakti Smart Card system:

- **New smart cards** will be issued with electronic chip technology
- The card will integrate with **Namma Metro** as a National Common Mobility Card (NCMC)
- Women can use the **same card for free bus travel** and pay for Metro rides by recharging the card
- The new card will **replace the current Aadhaar-based verification** system — conductors will swipe the card on ETMs instead of manually checking Aadhaar
- Cards will be issued through **both online and offline modes** via citizen service facilitation centres
- Rollout is expected by **mid-2026** (within 4–5 months of Feb 2026 announcement)

Transport Minister Ramalinga Reddy confirmed that all four transport corporations now have the digital machinery required. The tender process will begin soon.

> **What this means for you:** If you already have a Shakti card, you may need to upgrade to the new smart card once it launches. Keep checking official KSRTC/BMTC websites for announcements.

## How to Use the Shakti Card

Using the Shakti card for free travel is simple:

1. **Board any non-AC government bus** (KSRTC, BMTC, NWKRTC, or NEKRTC)
2. **Show your Shakti Smart Card** (or Aadhaar card, until new smart cards are rolled out) to the conductor
3. The conductor will **swipe/tap your card** on the Electronic Ticketing Machine (ETM)
4. A **free ticket is generated** — keep it until you deboard
5. **No cash payment** is required for the journey

### Important Rules

- 🪪 You **must carry the Shakti card or Aadhaar** every time you travel — no ID, no free travel
- 🚌 The benefit is **non-transferable** — only the registered woman can use it
- 🎫 You must **collect the ticket** from the conductor even though the ride is free
- 📍 Valid only for travel **within Karnataka** on eligible bus services
- 🔄 The card can be used for **unlimited trips** — there is no daily or monthly cap

## Common Problems and Solutions

### Problem: Conductor refuses free travel even with valid ID
**Solution:** Ensure your Aadhaar clearly shows a Karnataka address. If the conductor still refuses, note the bus number, route, and time, and file a complaint at the bus depot or via the KSRTC/BMTC website.

### Problem: Aadhaar address is not from Karnataka
**Solution:** You must update your Aadhaar address to a Karnataka address first. Visit the nearest Aadhaar centre or apply online at [uidai.gov.in](https://uidai.gov.in). [See our Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) for step-by-step instructions.

### Problem: Long queue at the Shakti card counter
**Solution:** Visit on **weekdays (Tuesday–Thursday)** to avoid crowds. Avoid month-end dates. Major depots like Majestic tend to be busier — try smaller depots near your area.

### Problem: Biometric verification fails at the depot
**Solution:** This can happen if your fingerprints are worn or unclear. Request **iris scan** as an alternative. If the machine is down, ask the staff for a later appointment.

### Problem: Lost or damaged Shakti Smart Card
**Solution:** Visit the nearest bus depot with your Aadhaar card. You can apply for a **duplicate card** at the Shakti counter. Until the replacement is ready, you can continue travelling by showing your Aadhaar card.

### Problem: Seva Sindhu portal not showing Shakti Card service
**Solution:** The online option may not be available at all times due to portal updates. Try again after a few hours, or apply at a bus depot instead.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I apply for the Shakti Smart Card online?
Visit [sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in), register with your mobile number, search for "Shakti Smart Card", fill the form, upload Aadhaar and photo, and submit. Collect the card from the designated depot.

### Is the Shakti card available for BMTC buses?
**Yes.** The Shakti scheme covers all BMTC ordinary (non-AC) buses in Bengaluru. You can apply for the card at any BMTC depot.

### What are the Shakti scheme eligibility criteria?
Any woman or transgender person who is a **resident of Karnataka** (Aadhaar with Karnataka address) is eligible. There is no income limit, age limit, or caste restriction.

### Is there an age limit for the Shakti card?
No. Women of **all ages** can apply. Minor girls can also get the card with guardian details.

### Can I use the Shakti card on Volvo/AC buses?
**No.** The scheme covers only non-AC ordinary services. For AC and premium buses, you must purchase a regular ticket.

### I don't have a Karnataka Aadhaar address. Can I still apply?
No. Your Aadhaar must have a **Karnataka address**. If you've recently moved, [update your Aadhaar address](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) first.

### Can I use the card on BMTC Vajra (AC) buses?
**No.** BMTC Vajra services are AC buses and are not covered under Shakti.

### What if I lose my Shakti card?
Visit the nearest bus depot with your Aadhaar card to apply for a **duplicate card**. Until the new card is ready, you can travel using your Aadhaar.

### Can I use the Shakti card for interstate travel (e.g., Bengaluru to Chennai)?
**No.** The card is valid only for routes **within Karnataka**.

### Will the Shakti card work on Namma Metro?
**Not yet**, but the Karnataka government has approved new smart cards with **Namma Metro integration** (NCMC). Once launched (expected mid-2026), you can recharge the same card for Metro rides, though Metro travel won't be free.

### How many trips can I take per day?
There is **no limit**. You can take unlimited trips on any eligible bus service.

### Is there a helpline for Shakti scheme queries?
You can contact your nearest bus depot or reach out via the official websites: [ksrtc.in](https://www.ksrtc.in) for KSRTC and [mybmtc.com](https://www.mybmtc.com) for BMTC queries.
