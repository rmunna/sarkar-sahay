## What is the UDID Card?

The **Unique Disability ID (UDID) Card** is a government-issued identity document for persons with disabilities in India. It is issued by the **Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (DEPwD)**, Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment.

The UDID card **replaces the old disability certificates** issued by different states with a single, nationally recognised card. It contains a unique 18-digit ID number linked to your Aadhaar, making it easier to access government benefits across India.

> **Important:** The UDID card serves as both your disability certificate and your identity proof for accessing all disability-related government schemes and benefits.

## Who is Eligible?

Any person residing in India who has a **benchmark disability of 40% or more** is eligible for a UDID card. This includes:

- Indian citizens of any age (including children)
- Persons with any of the **21 types of disabilities** recognised under the **Rights of Persons with Disabilities (RPwD) Act, 2016**
- Persons who already hold a state-issued disability certificate (they should also get UDID)

### 21 Types of Disabilities Under RPwD Act 2016

The RPwD Act 2016 recognises the following disabilities:

**Physical Disabilities:**
1. Blindness
2. Low vision
3. Leprosy cured persons
4. Hearing impairment (deaf and hard of hearing)
5. Locomotor disability
6. Dwarfism
7. Muscular dystrophy

**Intellectual Disabilities:**
8. Autism spectrum disorder
9. Specific learning disabilities
10. Intellectual disability

**Mental Behaviour (Mental Illness):**
11. Mental illness

**Disability Caused by Chronic Neurological Conditions:**
12. Cerebral palsy
13. Parkinson's disease
14. Multiple sclerosis

**Blood Disorders:**
15. Haemophilia
16. Thalassemia
17. Sickle cell disease

**Multiple Disabilities:**
18. Multiple disabilities (more than one of the above)

**Speech and Language Disability:**
19. Speech and language disability

**Other:**
20. Acid attack victims
21. Chronic neurological conditions

## Documents Required

Keep these documents ready before applying:

### Mandatory Documents
- **Aadhaar Card** — linked to your mobile number
- **Passport-size photograph** — recent, white background
- **Proof of address** — Aadhaar / Voter ID / Utility bill
- **Medical records** — diagnosis reports, prescriptions, hospital records related to your disability

### Additional Documents (if available)
- **Existing disability certificate** — if you have one issued by a state government hospital
- **Birth certificate** — for children applying
- **Guardianship certificate** — if applying on behalf of a person with intellectual or mental disability

> **Tip:** Having your existing medical records and disability certificate ready will speed up the assessment process significantly.

## How to Apply for UDID Card Online — Step by Step

### Step 1: Visit the Official Portal

Go to **[swavlambancard.gov.in](https://www.swavlambancard.gov.in)** — this is the only official website for UDID registration.

### Step 2: Click on "Apply Online"

On the homepage, click on **"Apply for Disability Certificate and UDID Card"** or the **"Register"** button.

### Step 3: Fill in Personal Details

Enter your details carefully:
- Full name (as on Aadhaar)
- Date of birth
- Gender
- Aadhaar number
- Mobile number and email
- Address (state, district, pin code)

### Step 4: Select Disability Type

- Choose your **type of disability** from the dropdown (one or more of the 21 types)
- Mention if the disability is **by birth or acquired**
- Enter details of the disability

### Step 5: Upload Documents

Upload scanned copies of:
- Passport-size photograph (JPG/PNG, max 1 MB)
- Aadhaar card
- Medical records / existing disability certificate
- Any other supporting documents

### Step 6: Choose Assessment Hospital

Select a **government hospital** in your district where you want to undergo the disability assessment. The portal shows a list of empanelled hospitals.

### Step 7: Submit the Application

- Review all details carefully
- Submit the application
- Note down your **Enrollment Number / Application ID** — you will need this to track your status

### Step 8: Visit the Hospital for Assessment

- You will receive an **SMS/notification** with your appointment date
- Visit the selected government hospital on the given date
- A **medical board** (team of specialist doctors) will assess your disability
- They will determine the **type and percentage of disability**

### Step 9: Receive Your UDID Card

- After successful assessment, the medical board approves your certificate
- Your **UDID card and disability certificate** will be generated
- You can download it from the portal or receive it by post

## How to Check UDID Application Status

1. Visit **[swavlambancard.gov.in](https://www.swavlambancard.gov.in)**
2. Click on **"Track Your Application Status"**
3. Enter your **Enrollment Number** or **Aadhaar Number**
4. Click **Search** to see the current status

**Status stages:** Application Submitted → Hospital Assigned → Assessment Scheduled → Assessment Complete → Certificate Generated → UDID Card Issued

## How to Download UDID Card

1. Visit **[swavlambancard.gov.in](https://www.swavlambancard.gov.in)**
2. Click on **"Download Your UDID Card"** or **"eDisability Card"**
3. Log in using your registered mobile number or Aadhaar
4. Enter OTP received on your mobile
5. Download your UDID card in PDF format

> You can also access your UDID card through the **UDID mSeva mobile app** available on Google Play Store.

## How to Update Details on UDID Card

If you need to update your name, address, disability details, or photo:

1. Log in to **[swavlambancard.gov.in](https://www.swavlambancard.gov.in)**
2. Go to **"Update Profile"** or **"Lost UDID"** section
3. Make the required changes
4. Upload supporting documents if needed
5. Submit — changes will be updated after verification

For **re-assessment** (if your disability percentage has changed), you will need to visit the hospital again.

## Benefits of UDID Card

The UDID card unlocks access to many government benefits:

### Employment
- **3-4% reservation in government jobs** (as per RPwD Act)
- Relaxation in upper age limit for government exams
- Special recruitment drives for persons with disabilities

### Education
- **5% reservation in higher education institutions**
- Scholarships for students with disabilities (pre-matric and post-matric)
- Fee concession or waiver in many institutions
- Assistive technology and accessible study materials

### Financial Benefits
- **Disability pension** — ₹500 to ₹3,000/month (varies by state)
- **Income tax exemption** under Section 80U — ₹75,000 deduction (₹1,25,000 for severe disability of 80%+)
- Tax benefits for guardians under Section 80DD

### Travel Concessions
- **75% concession on Indian Railways** (for persons with 40%+ disability)
- Free or discounted bus travel in many states
- Concession on air travel with some airlines

### Assistive Devices
- Free or subsidised **assistive devices** — wheelchairs, hearing aids, artificial limbs, crutches, braille kits
- Under the **ADIP Scheme** (Assistance to Disabled Persons for Purchase/Fitting of Aids and Appliances)

### Other Benefits
- Free healthcare at government hospitals
- Priority in government housing schemes
- Accessible parking and public facilities
- Legal protections against discrimination

## Processing Time

- **Online application:** 10-15 minutes
- **Hospital assessment appointment:** Usually within 2-4 weeks of application
- **Certificate generation:** 1-2 weeks after successful assessment
- **Total time:** Approximately 1-2 months from application to receiving UDID card

## Fees

- **No fee** is charged for applying for UDID card
- **No fee** for the disability assessment at government hospitals
- The entire process is **completely free**

## Common Problems and Solutions

### "I applied but didn't get a hospital appointment"
Contact your **District Disability Rehabilitation Centre (DDRC)** or the Chief Medical Officer's office in your district. You can also call the national helpline.

### "My disability percentage was assessed lower than expected"
You can apply for **re-assessment** through the portal. You may also appeal to the **State Commissioner for Persons with Disabilities**.

### "I have an old disability certificate — do I still need UDID?"
Yes, the government is transitioning to UDID for all disability-related services. Apply for UDID and upload your existing certificate — it will speed up the process.

### "I cannot visit the hospital for assessment"
For persons with severe disabilities who cannot travel, request a **home visit** or **camp-based assessment** through the DDRC.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is the UDID card valid across all states?**
Yes, the UDID card is valid across India. It is a nationally recognised disability certificate.

**Q: Can I apply on behalf of someone else?**
Yes, a family member or legal guardian can apply on behalf of a person with disability, especially for children or persons with intellectual/mental disabilities.

**Q: Is the UDID card the same as a disability certificate?**
The UDID card includes your disability certificate. Once you have a UDID, you do not need a separate disability certificate.

**Q: How long is the UDID card valid?**
If the disability is permanent, the card is valid for life. For temporary or progressive conditions, it is valid for the period specified by the medical board (usually 3-5 years), after which re-assessment is needed.

**Q: Can I apply offline?**
Yes, you can also apply through your nearest **District Disability Rehabilitation Centre (DDRC)** or government hospital. They will help you fill the online form.
