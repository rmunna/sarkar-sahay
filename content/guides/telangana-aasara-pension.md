## What is Telangana Aasara Pension Scheme?

Aasara (meaning "support") is the **social security pension scheme** of the Government of Telangana that provides **monthly financial assistance** to vulnerable sections of society. Launched in 2014, it is one of the most generous state pension schemes in India.

The scheme covers:
- **Old age pension** (aged 57+)
- **Widow/single women pension**
- **Disabled persons pension**
- **Toddy tappers pension**
- **Weavers pension**
- **HIV/AIDS patients pension**
- **Beedi workers pension**
- **Filariasis patients pension**

The pension amount was significantly increased from ₹200 to **₹2,016 per month** and has been further enhanced over the years, making it one of the highest state social security pensions in India.

## Who is Eligible?

### Old Age Pension
- Age: **57 years and above** (for women) / **65 years and above** (for men, under NSAP) — state scheme covers from 57+
- Must be a **resident of Telangana**
- Must belong to **BPL category** or have annual income below the threshold
- Must not be receiving pension from any other government source

### Widow / Single Women Pension
- Women who are **widowed, divorced, or deserted**
- Age: **18 years and above**
- Must not have remarried
- Must be a resident of Telangana

### Disabled Persons Pension
- Must have **disability of 40% or more** (certified by a medical board)
- Age: **18 years and above**
- Must hold a valid **disability certificate**

### Weavers / Toddy Tappers / Beedi Workers
- Must be engaged in the respective occupation
- Must hold a valid **occupation identity card**
- Age and income criteria as specified by the department

### Common Requirements (All Categories)
- Must hold a **ration card** issued in Telangana
- Must have an **Aadhaar card**
- Must have a **bank account** linked to Aadhaar
- Must not be a **government employee** or receiving other government pension

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** of the applicant
- **Ration card** (white/pink card for BPL)
- **Age proof** — birth certificate, school certificate, or Aadhaar (for old age pension)
- **Death certificate of spouse** (for widow pension)
- **Disability certificate** from District Medical Board (for disabled pension)
- **Bank account passbook** (Aadhaar-linked)
- **Passport-size photographs** (2 copies)
- **Caste certificate** (if applicable)
- **Occupation identity card** (for weavers, toddy tappers, beedi workers)
- **Income certificate** from MRO (Mandal Revenue Officer)

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Online Application

1. Visit the **Aasara pension portal** at [aasara.telangana.gov.in](https://aasara.telangana.gov.in)
2. Click on **"New Pension Application"**
3. Select the **pension type** (old age, widow, disabled, etc.)
4. Enter **Aadhaar number** for verification
5. Fill in personal details — name, age, address, mobile number
6. Enter **family and income details**
7. Upload scanned documents (Aadhaar, ration card, age proof, etc.)
8. Submit the application and note the **reference number**
9. Your application will be verified by the **Mandal Parishad Development Officer (MPDO)** or **Municipal Commissioner**
10. After field verification, the application goes to the **District Collector** for final approval
11. Upon approval, pension is credited monthly to your bank account via DBT

### Offline Application

1. Visit the nearest **Mandal Revenue Office (MRO)** or **Gram Panchayat office**
2. Collect the **Aasara pension application form**
3. Fill in all details and attach photocopies of required documents
4. Submit the form to the **Village Revenue Assistant (VRA)** or MRO
5. A field-level officer will visit for **verification**
6. Approved applications are forwarded to the MPDO and then the District Collector
7. Pension will start within 2–3 months of approval

### Through MeeSeva Centres

1. Visit any **MeeSeva centre** in Telangana
2. Request Aasara pension application
3. Provide your Aadhaar and documents
4. The operator will fill and submit the form online
5. Collect the acknowledgement slip
6. Service charge: ₹35–50 at MeeSeva

## Processing Time

- **Application to approval** — 30 to 90 days
- **First pension credit** — Within 1 month of approval
- **Monthly disbursement** — 1st of every month (directly to bank account)

## Important Tips

1. **Apply through MeeSeva if unsure** — The centre operators are trained to fill the form correctly and avoid errors.
2. **Get disability certificate first** — For disabled pension, visit the District Hospital medical board for assessment. This can take 15–30 days.
3. **Ensure Aadhaar-bank linking** — Pension is disbursed via DBT. Without Aadhaar-bank linking, payment will fail. See our [Aadhaar linking guide](/guide/aadhaar-linking-guide).
4. **Renew annually** — Some categories require annual verification (life certificate). Visit your bank branch in November to submit the Jeevan Pramaan (life certificate).
5. **Report pension stoppage** — If your pension stops unexpectedly, visit the MRO office with your Aadhaar and pension ID. Common reasons include Aadhaar mismatch or bank account issues.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: How much pension do senior citizens get under Aasara?
Senior citizens aged 57 and above receive **₹2,016 per month** under the Aasara old age pension scheme. The amount is the same for all age groups (57-65, 65-80, and 80+).

### Q2: Can I apply for Aasara pension if I'm from another state?
No. You must be a **permanent resident of Telangana** with a Telangana ration card. If you've migrated from another state, you need to first obtain a Telangana ration card.

### Q3: How is the pension paid?
The pension is paid **directly to your bank account** via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) on the 1st of every month. Cash payments have been discontinued.

### Q4: Can a person receive two types of Aasara pensions?
No. A person can receive only **one type of pension** under Aasara. You must choose the most applicable category.

### Q5: What is the age for old age pension in Telangana?
The minimum age is **57 years** for both men and women under the state's Aasara scheme. This is lower than the central government's NSAP which starts at 60.

### Q6: How do I check my Aasara pension status?
Visit [aasara.telangana.gov.in](https://aasara.telangana.gov.in) and click on "Search Pensioner" or "Application Status." Enter your Aadhaar number or application ID.

### Q7: What if my pension application is rejected?
You can appeal to the **District Collector** within 30 days of rejection. Ensure all documents are complete and correct before reapplying.
