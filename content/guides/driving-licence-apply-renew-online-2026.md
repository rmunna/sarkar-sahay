# Driving Licence Apply & Renew Online India 2026 — Complete Guide

Indian driving licences can now be applied, renewed, and updated through the **Sarathi Parivahan** portal (sarathi.parivahan.gov.in). No more waiting in long RTO queues for most services — here's the complete guide.

---

## Types of Driving Licence Services

| Service | Online? | At RTO? | Notes |
|---------|---------|---------|-------|
| **Learner's Licence (LL)** | ✅ Online (apply + test) | For test only | LL valid for 6 months |
| **Permanent Driving Licence (DL)** | ✅ Online (apply) | Driving test at RTO | Applied after 30 days of LL |
| **DL Renewal** | ✅ Online | Optional | For expired licences |
| **DL Address Change** | ✅ Online | — | Update address on DL |
| **DL Lost / Duplicate** | ✅ Online | — | Issue duplicate |
| **International DL** | ✅ Online | Verification | For driving abroad |
| **Smart Card DL** | Online | Collected at RTO | National uniform smart card |

## Step 1: Apply for Learner's Licence (LL) Online

### Eligibility for LL:
- **Without Gear (LMV — motorcycle/car)**: 16+ years (with parent consent for 16–18), 18+ years independently
- **With Gear (MCWG/LMV)**: 18+ years
- **Heavy Vehicle (HMV)**: 20+ years

### Process:

1. Go to **[sarathi.parivahan.gov.in](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in)**
2. Select your **state**
3. **"Learner's Licence"** → **"Apply for LL"**
4. Login with Aadhaar + OTP
5. Fill application form: Personal details, vehicle class (motorcycle/car/both)
6. Upload documents (Aadhaar, DOB proof, address proof, photo)
7. Pay fee online (₹200–₹400 depending on state + vehicle class)
8. **Book LL test slot** at your nearest RTO / authorised test centre
9. Appear for test (online at some RTOs, in-person at others)
10. Pass test → **LL issued immediately** (download e-LL from portal)

### LL Test Format:
- 20 multiple-choice questions on traffic rules, signs, road safety
- Must answer 12+ correctly to pass
- Available in Hindi, English, and regional languages
- Preparation: Study Motor Vehicles Act basics + traffic signs

---

## Step 2: Apply for Permanent Driving Licence

**Eligible:** Minimum 30 days after LL issue date (before LL expires at 6 months)

### Process:

1. sarathi.parivahan.gov.in → **"Driving Licence"** → **"Apply for DL"**
2. Login → Fill DL application
3. Enter LL number
4. Upload: LL details + Aadhaar + address proof + photos
5. Pay fee: ₹500–₹1,000 (varies by state + vehicle class)
6. **Book Driving Test slot** at RTO
7. Appear for driving skill test at RTO
8. Pass → DL issued (smart card delivered by post in 7–14 days)

### DL Driving Test Tips:
- Practice basic maneuvers: Figure-of-8, H-shape course, braking
- Know traffic signs and road markings
- Carry LL (original) and appointment letter
- Bring your own vehicle (some RTOs have test vehicles)

## Step 3: Renew Expired Driving Licence Online

Driving licence is valid for 20 years (or till age 50, whichever is later) for MCWG/LMV. After that: 5-year renewals.

**If DL expired less than 1 year ago:** Online renewal only.
**If DL expired more than 1 year ago:** RTO visit may be required for medical certificate.

### Online Renewal Process:

1. sarathi.parivahan.gov.in → **"Driving Licence"** → **"Renewal of DL"**
2. Login with DL number + date of birth
3. Aadhaar verification
4. Upload: Expired DL + current address proof + photo
5. For age 40+: Medical fitness certificate from government doctor
6. Pay renewal fee: ₹500–₹800 (state varies)
7. Submit → **New DL dispatched in 7–14 working days**

## Driving Licence for Heavy Vehicle (HMV/PSV)

For trucks, buses, taxis:
- Age 20+ for HMV
- Must have valid LMV DL for 1 year before HMV application
- Badge/endorsement required for commercial (PSV/LMV-T)
- Endorsement for commercial: ₹50–₹300

## Track DL Status

1. sarathi.parivahan.gov.in → **"Know Your Application Status"**
2. Enter application number + date of birth
3. Status: Applied → Approved → Printed → Dispatched → Delivered

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I drive with LL (learner's licence)?**
Yes — with conditions: Must be accompanied by a person who has a valid DL for the vehicle class. Must display 'L' board on the vehicle. No solo driving on highways. LL valid for 6 months.

**My DL expired 3 years ago — can I still renew online?**
Yes — if less than 5 years since expiry, online renewal is possible on Sarathi portal. You may need medical certificate (Form 1A). If more than 5 years: Appear for fresh LL test + DL test (essentially start fresh).

**I moved to a new state — how to get new state DL?**
You don't need a new state DL. Indian DL is valid across all states. If you want your address updated: Apply for address change on Sarathi portal. No need to get a fresh DL from new state.

**International driving — is Indian DL valid abroad?**
Indian DL is valid in some countries for a limited period (varies by country). For long-term driving abroad: Apply for International Driving Permit (IDP) at your RTO or via Sarathi portal → IDP valid in 100+ countries for 1 year.

**Driving test failed at RTO — how many attempts?**
No limit on attempts. Book a new test slot after failing. Pay test fee again for each attempt. Practice more before the next attempt — figure-of-8 and H-route are most commonly failed.
