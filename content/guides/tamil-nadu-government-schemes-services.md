# Tamil Nadu Government Schemes and Services – Complete Guide

Tamil Nadu is a pioneer in social welfare, running one of India's most extensive networks of government schemes. From the iconic noon meal programme to modern skills development under Naan Mudhalvan, the state offers comprehensive benefits covering women, students, farmers, and senior citizens. This guide covers every major Tamil Nadu government scheme with eligibility, benefits, and application details.

## Key Government Portals in Tamil Nadu

- **TN e-Sevai Portal** – [eservices.tn.gov.in](https://eservices.tn.gov.in/) – Single window for certificates, licenses, and government services
- **TN PDS Portal** – [tnpds.gov.in](https://tnpds.gov.in/) – Ration card services and PDS management
- **TNREGINET** – [tnreginet.gov.in](https://tnreginet.gov.in/) – Property registration and encumbrance certificate
- **Patta Chitta** – Land records portal. See: [Patta Chitta Land Records Tamil Nadu](/guide/patta-chitta-land-records-tamil-nadu)

## Women Empowerment Schemes

### Kalaignar Magalir Urimai Thogai (Women's Right to Income)

Tamil Nadu's landmark scheme providing ₹1,000 per month to women heads of families. One of the most impactful direct benefit transfer schemes in the state.

**Key benefits:**
- Monthly transfer of ₹1,000 to eligible women
- Covers women aged 21-60 who are heads of families
- Benefits transferred directly to bank accounts

Read the complete guide: [Kalaignar Magalir Urimai Thogai Tamil Nadu](/guide/kalaignar-magalir-urimai-thogai-tamil-nadu)

### Tamil Nadu Marriage Assistance Scheme

Financial assistance for marriages of women from economically weaker families, including cash benefit and gold for thali (mangalsutra).

**Key features:**
- ₹25,000-₹50,000 cash assistance based on education level
- 8 grams of gold for thali
- Higher benefits for graduates and professionally qualified women

Read the complete guide: [TN Marriage Assistance Scheme](/guide/tn-marriage-assistance-scheme)

## Education and Scholarship Schemes

### Pudhumai Penn Scholarship

Monthly stipend of ₹1,000 for girl students from government schools pursuing higher education. Aims to prevent dropouts and empower first-generation graduates.

Read the complete guide: [TN Pudhumai Penn Scholarship](/guide/tn-pudhumai-penn-scholarship)

### Naan Mudhalvan – Skill Development Programme

Comprehensive skill development programme for college students covering employability skills, industry certifications, and placement support.

Read the complete guide: [TN Naan Mudhalvan Skill Development](/guide/tn-naan-mudhalvan-skill-development)

### Free Bicycle and Laptop Scheme

Tamil Nadu provides free bicycles to class 11 students and free laptops to college students in government institutions.

## Housing Schemes

### Kalaignar Kanavu Illam (Dream Home Scheme)

Affordable housing scheme providing houses at subsidized rates to urban poor and lower-income groups.

Read the complete guide: [TN Kalaignar Kanavu Illam Housing](/guide/tn-kalaignar-kanavu-illam-housing)

### TNSCB Housing

## Health Schemes

### Chief Minister's Comprehensive Health Insurance Scheme (CMCHIS)

Integrated with Ayushman Bharat, provides cashless medical treatment up to ₹5 lakh per family per year at empanelled hospitals.

**Coverage:**
- 1,027+ medical procedures and surgeries
- Cashless treatment at government and private empanelled hospitals
- All ration card holders are eligible

See: [Ayushman Bharat vs State Health Schemes](/guide/ayushman-bharat-vs-state-health-schemes)

### Amma Mini Clinics

Primary healthcare centres set up across Tamil Nadu providing free basic health services, medicines, and diagnostic tests.

## How to Apply for Tamil Nadu Government Schemes

### Online Application

1. **Visit TN e-Sevai** – [eservices.tn.gov.in](https://eservices.tn.gov.in/) for certificates and select services
2. **Register** – Create account using Aadhaar/mobile number
3. **Select service** – Choose the scheme or certificate needed
4. **Fill form and upload documents** – Aadhaar, ration card, income proof, photos
5. **Pay fees** (if applicable) and submit
6. **Track** using application number

### Offline Application

1. Visit the nearest **Taluk Office** or **e-Sevai Common Service Centre**
2. Carry original documents
3. Submit application with required documents
4. Collect acknowledgment for tracking

### Common Documents Required

- Aadhaar card
- Family ration card
- Income certificate
- Community certificate (for category-based schemes)
- Bank passbook
- Passport-size photographs

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I check Tamil Nadu scheme application status?**
Visit the e-Sevai portal or the specific scheme portal and enter your application number.

**Is family ration card mandatory for TN schemes?**
Yes, the family ration card is a primary eligibility document for most Tamil Nadu welfare schemes.

**Can NRI Tamils apply for any state schemes?**
Most welfare schemes are for residents only. However, certain property and certificate services are available to NRIs.
