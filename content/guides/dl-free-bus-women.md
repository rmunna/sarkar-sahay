## What is the Delhi Free Bus Ride for Women Scheme?

The **Delhi Free Bus Ride for Women** scheme was launched on **29 October 2019** by the **Government of NCT of Delhi**. Under this scheme, all women — regardless of age, income, or residency — can travel **free of cost** on **DTC (Delhi Transport Corporation)** and **Delhi cluster buses** by obtaining a **pink ticket** from the bus conductor.

This initiative aims to make public transport accessible and safe for women, encourage more women to use buses, and reduce their commuting costs.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Gender:** Any woman or girl
2. **Age:** No age restriction — women of all ages can avail
3. **Residency:** **No residency requirement** — all women travelling in Delhi can use it, including tourists and visitors
4. **No ID Required:** No identity card or pass is needed

## How to Avail the Free Ride

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Board** any **DTC bus** or **Delhi cluster bus** (orange buses)
2. **Request** a **pink ticket** from the bus conductor
3. **The conductor** will issue a free pink-colored ticket
4. **Keep the ticket** during the journey (for inspection if needed)
5. **Alight** at your destination — that's it!

> **No pass, no card, no registration needed.** Just board the bus and ask for a pink ticket.

## Routes Covered

- **All DTC and cluster bus routes** within Delhi NCR are covered
- No restriction on route, distance, or number of trips per day
- Available on **AC and non-AC** DTC/cluster buses

## Important Tips

1. **Ask for Pink Ticket:** Always collect the pink ticket from the conductor as proof of travel
2. **All Routes:** The scheme works on all DTC and cluster routes — no restrictions
3. **No ID Needed:** You don't need any ID card, pass, or registration
4. **Unlimited Trips:** There's no limit on the number of free trips per day
5. **Safety:** DTC buses have CCTV cameras and panic buttons for women's safety

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. Do I need any card or pass for free bus travel?
**No.** Just board the DTC or cluster bus and ask the conductor for a pink ticket. No ID or registration required.

### Q2. Is it available on Metro too?
**No**, the free ride is only on DTC and Delhi cluster buses, not on Delhi Metro.

### Q3. Can women from other states also travel free?
**Yes.** There is no residency requirement. Any woman travelling in Delhi can avail free bus rides.

### Q4. Is there a limit on daily trips?
**No.** Women can take unlimited free trips per day on DTC and cluster buses.

### Q5. Are AC buses also free for women?
**Yes**, both AC and non-AC DTC/cluster buses are covered under this scheme.

### Q6. What if the conductor refuses to give a pink ticket?
Complain at the DTC helpline **011-23467557** or via the [DTC website](https://dtc.delhi.gov.in).

### Q7. Is this scheme still active in 2026?
Yes, the scheme continues to be operational. Check [delhi.gov.in](https://delhi.gov.in) for any updates.
