# Passport Appointment Not Available: How to Fix

Booking a passport appointment at a Passport Seva Kendra (PSK) or Regional Passport Office (RPO) is often frustrating because slots get filled quickly. Many applicants see "No appointment available" for weeks or even months. This guide explains why slots are unavailable and provides proven strategies to secure an appointment.

## Common Error Messages and Symptoms

- **"No appointment available at selected PSK"** — all dates are fully booked
- **"Appointment slots are not available for selected date"** — specific date is full
- **"Service not available at this location"** — selected PSK does not offer the service you need
- Calendar shows no available dates for the entire month
- Appointment page keeps loading but never shows available slots
- Slots appear momentarily but disappear when you try to book
- **"Payment successful but appointment not confirmed"** — payment went through but slot was taken
- Tatkal appointment slots are always unavailable

## Why Passport Appointments May Not Be Available

### 1. High Demand

PSKs handle a limited number of applications per day (typically 100-200). With millions of passport applications annually, demand far exceeds capacity, especially in metro cities.

### 2. Slots Released at Specific Times

Appointment slots are released in batches, not continuously. If you check at random times, slots may already be taken.

### 3. Tatkal Slots Are Limited

Each PSK allocates only 10-20% of daily slots for Tatkal (urgent) appointments. These fill within minutes of release.

### 4. Seasonal Demand

Before summer vacations, admission seasons, and major travel periods, demand spikes significantly.

### 5. Website/Server Issues

During peak times, the Passport Seva website slows down, and slots may appear unavailable due to server load even when some exist.

## Step-by-Step Troubleshooting

### Step 1: Check Multiple PSK Locations

1. Log in to [passportindia.gov.in](https://www.passportindia.gov.in/)
2. Go to **"Schedule Appointment"**
3. Instead of your nearest PSK, check all PSKs and POPSKs (Post Office Passport Seva Kendras) in your state
4. POPSK locations often have better availability than main PSKs
5. You can apply at any PSK in India, regardless of your address

### Step 2: Check Slots at the Right Time

Passport Seva releases new slots at specific times:
1. **Normal slots:** Released around **3:00 PM to 5:00 PM** daily for dates 1-3 weeks ahead
2. **Cancelled/rescheduled slots:** Become available throughout the day
3. **Best time to check:** Between **3:00 PM and 4:00 PM** on weekdays
4. Log in a few minutes before 3 PM and keep refreshing the appointment page

### Step 3: Keep Refreshing

1. Stay logged in on the appointment scheduling page
2. Refresh every 30-60 seconds during slot release time
3. Have all your details ready so you can complete booking quickly once a slot appears
4. Keep your payment method (credit/debit card or net banking) ready

### Step 4: Try Smaller Cities/Towns

1. POPSKs in smaller towns and district headquarters have much less demand
2. Check POPSKs within 100-200 km of your city
3. The passport service is the same at all locations
4. List of POPSKs: Available on the Passport Seva website under "Locate Passport Seva Kendra"

### Step 5: Try Tatkal Appointment

For urgent travel needs:
1. Select **Tatkal** category when scheduling an appointment
2. Tatkal fees are higher (additional ₹2,000 for fresh passport, ₹1,500 for re-issue)
3. Tatkal slots are released daily — check early in the morning
4. Tatkal applications are processed within 1-3 working days

### Step 6: Use the Passport Seva App

1. Download the **mPassport Seva** app from Google Play or App Store
2. The app sometimes shows availability that the website does not (due to caching)
3. Set up your profile and application in advance for quicker booking

### Step 7: Check for Rescheduled/Cancelled Slots

1. People frequently cancel or reschedule appointments
2. These freed-up slots appear randomly throughout the day
3. Check the appointment page multiple times a day — early morning, noon, evening
4. Weekends and late evenings often have slots from cancellations

## Alternative Methods and Workarounds

### Apply at POPSK (Post Office Passport Seva Kendra)

POPSKs operate inside post offices in smaller cities and towns:
- They handle passport applications just like main PSKs
- Usually have better slot availability
- Services include fresh passport, re-issue, and police clearance certificates
- Find POPSKs at the Passport Seva portal

### Walk-in at PSK

Some PSKs allow walk-in applicants for certain services:
- This is not officially advertised but some PSKs accommodate walk-ins for rejected/incomplete applications
- Contact your nearest PSK by phone to ask about walk-in availability
- Carry all original documents and printed application

### Contact Regional Passport Office

For genuine emergencies (medical, death in family, urgent business travel):
1. Contact the RPO in writing explaining the emergency
2. Attach supporting documents (hospital letters, flight tickets, etc.)
3. The RPO can allocate out-of-turn appointments
4. Find RPO contacts at [passportindia.gov.in](https://www.passportindia.gov.in/)

### Use MP/MLA Reference (Tatkaal Political Route)

For extreme emergencies, you can approach your MP or MLA's office for a reference letter to the RPO. This is a legitimate channel for genuine urgency.

## Prevention Tips

1. **Apply well in advance** — do not wait until travel dates are near; start the passport process at least 2-3 months before you need it
2. **Book immediately when slots open** — be ready at 3 PM when new slots are released
3. **Fill the application form first** — complete the online form and save it before trying to book an appointment
4. **Keep payment method ready** — save your card details in the browser for faster checkout
5. **Check multiple locations** — do not restrict yourself to one PSK; nearby POPSKs often have better availability
6. **Set calendar reminders** — set daily reminders to check for slots at 3 PM

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What time are passport appointment slots released?

Normal appointment slots are typically released between 3:00 PM and 5:00 PM daily for dates 1-3 weeks ahead. Cancelled or rescheduled slots can appear at any time during the day. Log in a few minutes before 3 PM and keep refreshing for the best chance.

### Can I apply at any PSK in India?

Yes. You can schedule an appointment at any Passport Seva Kendra or POPSK in India, regardless of your residential address. Your application will be processed normally, and police verification will still be conducted at your residential address.

### How do I get a Tatkal passport appointment?

Select "Tatkal" when filling the online application form. Tatkal appointments are limited and fill quickly. Check early morning for availability. Tatkal passport costs ₹2,000 extra for fresh issuance and ₹1,500 extra for re-issue. Processing time is 1-3 working days after the appointment.

### What documents should I carry to the PSK?

Carry your printed application receipt (ARN), original and photocopies of address proof, date of birth proof, and identity proof (Aadhaar, voter ID, etc.). For minors, carry parents' passports and birth certificate. For re-issue, carry the old passport.

### Can I reschedule my passport appointment?

Yes. Log in to the Passport Seva portal, go to "View Saved/Submitted Applications," select your application, and click "Schedule Appointment." You can reschedule up to 3 times. The freed-up slot becomes available for other applicants.
