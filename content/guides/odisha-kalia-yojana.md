## What is KALIA Yojana?

KALIA (Krushak Assistance for Livelihood and Income Augmentation) Yojana is a flagship agricultural welfare scheme of the Odisha government. Launched in 2018, it provides financial support to small and marginal farmers, landless agricultural labourers, and vulnerable sections to help them with cultivation, livelihood, and insurance coverage.

Unlike PM-KISAN which focuses only on landholding farmers, KALIA also covers landless agricultural labourers and sharecroppers.

## Who is Eligible?

### Category 1: Small and Marginal Farmers
- Cultivators with landholding up to 5 acres
- Must be a resident of Odisha
- Engaged in agriculture as a primary occupation

### Category 2: Landless Agricultural Labourers
- Households without agricultural land
- Dependent on agriculture as a primary livelihood
- Includes sharecroppers and agricultural workers

### Category 3: Vulnerable Sections
- Old, disabled, or disease-affected cultivators and landless labourers
- Unable to actively cultivate or work

### Who is NOT Eligible
- Government employees (state/central) and pensioners
- Income tax payers
- Professionals (doctors, engineers, lawyers, CAs registered with respective bodies)
- Elected representatives (MP/MLA/Zilla Parishad members)
- Farmers with more than 5 acres of land

## Benefits

### For Small and Marginal Farmers

| Benefit | Amount | Frequency |
|---------|--------|-----------|
| Financial assistance for cultivation | ₹10,000 per family | Per Kharif & Rabi season (₹5,000 each) |
| Life insurance | ₹2,00,000 | Coverage |
| Personal accident cover | ₹2,00,000 | Coverage |
| Premium (farmer's share) | ₹330/year for life insurance | Annually |

### For Landless Agricultural Labourers

| Benefit | Amount |
|---------|--------|
| Livelihood assistance | ₹12,500 per family |
| Purpose | To pursue allied agricultural activities (goat rearing, mushroom cultivation, beekeeping, etc.) |

### For Vulnerable Sections

| Benefit | Amount |
|---------|--------|
| Monthly assistance | ₹1,000 |
| Annual total | ₹12,000 |
| Purpose | Sustenance and basic needs |

### Insurance Coverage (All Beneficiaries)
- **Life insurance:** ₹2,00,000 (premium paid by state government)
- **Accident insurance:** ₹2,00,000 (premium paid by state government)
- Insurance provided through partnership with insurance companies

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card**
- **Land records** (for landholding farmers) — ROR, Patta, etc.
- **Bank account details** linked with Aadhaar
- **Ration card** / household identification
- **Caste certificate** (if applicable)
- **Disability certificate** (for vulnerable section applicants)

## How to Apply

### Online Application
1. Visit [kalia.odisha.gov.in](https://kalia.odisha.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Online Grievance Application"** or new registration
3. Enter Aadhaar number and verify through OTP
4. Fill in personal, land, and bank details
5. Upload required documents
6. Submit the application

### Offline Application
1. Visit your **Block Development Office (BDO)** or **Gram Panchayat** office
2. Collect the KALIA application form
3. Fill in details and attach document copies
4. Submit to the designated officer
5. Collect acknowledgment receipt

### Verification Process
- Applications are verified at the Gram Panchayat level
- Land records cross-checked with revenue department
- Bank account verified for DBT
- Approved applications receive payments in the next cycle

## How to Check Your Name in the Beneficiary List

1. Visit [kalia.odisha.gov.in](https://kalia.odisha.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Beneficiary List"**
3. Select your District → Block → GP
4. Search for your name in the list
5. If not found, apply through the grievance portal

## Important Tips

- **Land records must be updated** — ensure your ROR (Record of Rights) reflects current ownership
- **Aadhaar-bank seeding** — mandatory for receiving DBT payments
- **Landless labourers are also covered** — don't assume you need land to benefit; sharecroppers and agri workers qualify
- **Check the beneficiary list regularly** — new beneficiaries are added in each cycle
- **Raise grievance if excluded** — use the online grievance portal if you believe you're eligible but not listed

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is KALIA Yojana different from PM-KISAN?
Yes, KALIA is an Odisha state scheme while PM-KISAN is a central government scheme. Eligible farmers can receive benefits from both schemes simultaneously. KALIA also covers landless labourers, which PM-KISAN does not.

### How much money will I get under KALIA?
Small and marginal farmers receive ₹10,000 per year (₹5,000 per crop season). Landless labourers get ₹12,500 as one-time livelihood support. Vulnerable sections get ₹12,000 per year (₹1,000/month).

### Can sharecroppers apply for KALIA?
Yes, sharecroppers and tenant farmers who don't own land but cultivate others' land are eligible under the landless agricultural labourer category.

### How do I check my KALIA payment status?
Visit kalia.odisha.gov.in, go to the beneficiary section, enter your Aadhaar or account number, and check the payment status and history.

### What if my name is not in the KALIA beneficiary list?
File a grievance through the online portal at kalia.odisha.gov.in or visit your BDO office. Provide your Aadhaar, land records, and other proofs.
