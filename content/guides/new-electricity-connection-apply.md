# New Electricity Connection: How to Apply Online State-Wise Guide

Getting a new electricity connection is one of the first things you need when moving into a new home or starting a business. Most state DISCOMs (Distribution Companies) now offer online application for new connections. This guide covers the complete process to apply for a new electricity connection across major Indian states.

## Types of Electricity Connections

| Type | Description | Load |
|------|-------------|------|
| Domestic (LT-1) | Residential homes, flats | 1 kW – 10 kW |
| Commercial (LT-2) | Shops, offices, small businesses | 1 kW – 100 kW |
| Industrial (LT/HT) | Factories, manufacturing | 10 kW – 1 MW+ |
| Agricultural | Farm, irrigation pumps | 3 HP – 10 HP |
| Temporary | Construction sites, events | As required |

This guide focuses primarily on **domestic (residential) connections**.

## Documents Required for New Electricity Connection

- **Identity proof** — [Aadhaar card](/guides/aadhaar-card-apply-online), voter ID, passport
- **Address proof** — registered sale deed, allotment letter, rent agreement
- **Ownership proof** — property documents, registry, allotment letter, society NOC
- **Passport-size photographs** (2–3 copies)
- **Building plan approval** (for new construction)
- **Electrical installation test report** from a licensed electrician (in some states)
- **NOC from housing society** (for apartments)

## How to Apply Online — General Process

1. **Identify your DISCOM** — based on your area/district (see state-wise list below)
2. **Visit the DISCOM website** and go to "New Connection" section
3. **Register** with your mobile number and email
4. **Select connection type** — Domestic, Commercial, Industrial, etc.
5. **Fill in the application form** — name, address, property details, load requirement
6. **Upload documents** — ownership proof, ID proof, photographs
7. **Pay the application fee and security deposit** online
8. **Submit** and note the application/request number
9. **Track status** on the portal
10. **Site inspection** by DISCOM engineer (within 3–7 days)
11. **Meter installation** after inspection and approval (7–15 days total)

## Fees and Charges

The cost of a new electricity connection varies by state, type, and load:

| Component | Approximate Cost (Domestic) |
|-----------|---------------------------|
| Application/processing fee | ₹100 – ₹500 |
| Security deposit | ₹1,000 – ₹5,000 (refundable) |
| Meter cost | ₹1,500 – ₹3,000 |
| Service line charges | ₹500 – ₹3,000 |
| **Total (typical 1-3 kW)** | **₹3,000 – ₹10,000** |

**Note:** Charges vary significantly by state and DISCOM. BPL families get subsidized or free connections under the Saubhagya scheme.

## Saubhagya Scheme (PMSBY)

The Pradhan Mantri Sahaj Bijli Har Ghar Yojana (Saubhagya) provides **free electricity connections** to economically poor households:

- Eligible: BPL families, households identified under SECC 2011
- Connection provided free including meter, wiring, and service cable
- Apply through the local DISCOM office or online portal
- Scheme extended for eligible families who still don't have connections

## Common Issues and Solutions

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Application rejected due to documents | Ensure property papers match the address; get [NOC from society](/guides/noc-certificate-apply-online) if needed |
| Delay beyond 15 days | File complaint on DISCOM portal or call the customer care helpline |
| Disputed ownership | Provide registered sale deed or court order |
| High security deposit | Request installment payment; check if BPL subsidy applies |

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Can a tenant apply for a new electricity connection?

Yes, in most states tenants can apply with a valid rent agreement, landlord's NOC, and the property owner's ID proof. The connection will be in the tenant's name.

### 2. What is the minimum load for a domestic connection?

The minimum load is typically 1 kW (single phase) for domestic connections. For loads above 5–7 kW, a three-phase connection is required.

### 3. Can I apply for electricity connection without property papers?

It's difficult. You need ownership or occupancy proof. An allotment letter, possession letter, or registered rent agreement can serve as alternatives to sale deed.

### 4. How do I check the status of my new connection application?

Login to your DISCOM's portal with your application number to check status. Most DISCOMs also send SMS updates at each stage.

### 5. Is the security deposit refundable?

Yes, the security deposit is refundable when you permanently disconnect the electricity supply. It may also be adjusted against your final bill.

### 6. Can I upgrade my electricity load later?

Yes. You can apply for load enhancement through the same DISCOM portal. Additional security deposit and wiring changes may be required.
