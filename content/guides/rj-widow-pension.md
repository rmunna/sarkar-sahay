## What is Rajasthan Widow Pension Scheme?

The Rajasthan Widow Pension (Mukhyamantri Ekal Nari Samman Pension Yojana) provides **monthly financial assistance** to widows and single women in Rajasthan. The scheme supports destitute widows, divorced women, and abandoned women who have no regular income.

### Pension Amount

| Age Group | Monthly Pension |
|-----------|----------------|
| 18-54 years | ₹750 |
| 55-59 years | ₹1,000 |
| 60-74 years | ₹1,000 |
| 75+ years | ₹1,500 |

## Who is Eligible?

- **Widows** of any age (18+) with husband's death certificate
- **Divorced women** with court decree
- **Abandoned/deserted women** with supporting documentation
- Must be a **resident of Rajasthan**
- Annual family income must not exceed **₹48,000**
- Must not be receiving any other government pension
- Must not be an income tax payer
- Must have **Jan Aadhaar** registration

## Documents Required

- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
- Jan Aadhaar card
- **Husband's death certificate** (for widows)
- Divorce decree (for divorced women) or affidavit (for deserted women)
- Age proof
- Income certificate (annual income ≤ ₹48,000)
- Bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked)
- Passport-size photograph
- Ration card

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Online Application

1. **Visit** [rajssp.rajasthan.gov.in](https://rajssp.rajasthan.gov.in)
2. **Login** with SSO ID or Jan Aadhaar
3. **Select** Ekal Nari Samman Pension / Widow Pension
4. **Fill** the application form
5. **Upload** death certificate, Aadhaar, income certificate, bank passbook
6. **Submit** and note application number
7. **Track** status online

### Through E-Mitra

1. Visit the nearest **E-Mitra kiosk**
2. Carry all original documents
3. E-Mitra fills and submits the application
4. Collect receipt

## Processing Time

- Verification: **1-2 months**
- Approval: **2-3 months**
- Monthly pension via DBT after approval

## Fees

**No fees** for application. E-Mitra may charge ₹20-50 service fee.

## Important Tips

1. **Get death certificate** — This is the most important document; apply at the municipal/gram panchayat office
2. **Jan Aadhaar is mandatory** — Register on Jan Aadhaar portal before applying
3. **Annual life certificate** — Submit via [Jeevan Pramaan](https://jeevanpramaan.gov.in)
4. **Check Jan Soochna** — Track payment status on [jansoochna.rajasthan.gov.in](https://jansoochna.rajasthan.gov.in)
5. **Also check Chiranjeevi** — Register for free [health insurance](/guide/rj-chiranjeevi-health)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Can a remarried widow apply?
No. The pension is for widows who have **not remarried**.

### Q2. Is there a minimum age?
Yes, the applicant must be at least **18 years** old.

### Q3. Can divorced women also apply?
Yes. **Divorced and deserted women** are also eligible under the Ekal Nari Samman scheme.

### Q4. Can I get both widow pension and old age pension?
No. Only **one** social security pension is allowed. See our [Rajasthan Old Age Pension guide](/guide/rj-old-age-pension).

### Q5. How do I check payment status?
Visit [rajssp.rajasthan.gov.in](https://rajssp.rajasthan.gov.in) and check with your application ID or Jan Aadhaar number.
