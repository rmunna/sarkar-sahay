## What is the UP Solar Pump Scheme?

**Mukhyamantri Solar Pump Yojana** is the Uttar Pradesh government scheme that gives **70–90% subsidy** on solar-powered irrigation pumps to UP farmers. Solar pumps allow farmers to irrigate fields without electricity bills or diesel costs.

The scheme is implemented under the state component of **PM KUSUM Yojana (Component B)** and the UP state government's own solar agriculture programme.

## Subsidy Structure

| Farmer Category | Subsidy |
|---|---|
| General / OBC farmer | **70%** of pump cost |
| SC / ST farmer | **90%** of pump cost |
| Small / marginal farmer | Additional preference |

The remaining 10–30% is paid by the farmer (can be financed through bank loan).

### Cost Example (3 HP Solar Pump)

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Total pump cost | ~₹2.50 lakh |
| 70% subsidy (general) | ₹1.75 lakh |
| Farmer's share (30%) | ₹75,000 |
| SC/ST farmer's share (10%) | ₹25,000 |

## Pump Capacities Available

- **2 HP** — for small plots
- **3 HP** — most common
- **5 HP** — for larger fields
- **7.5 HP** — for very large farms
- **10 HP** — for orchards and large acreage

## Who is Eligible?

- **Uttar Pradesh resident farmer** with agricultural land
- Must have a **bank account linked to Aadhaar**
- Land in own name or under legal tenancy
- Priority: small/marginal farmers, SC/ST farmers, tail-end farmers (far from grid electricity)
- Farmers **not already connected to grid electricity** for irrigation get higher priority

## How to Apply

1. Go to **[upagriculture.com](https://upagriculture.com)**
2. Click **"यंत्र/उपकरण खरीद पर अनुदान"** (Subsidy on equipment purchase) or **"सोलर पम्प"** section
3. Register with Aadhaar + mobile OTP
4. Fill application:
   - Land details (khasra, khatauni number)
   - Pump capacity required
   - Water source (borewell, canal, pond)
5. Pay the registration fee (₹500–₹1,000 as token advance, adjusted in final payment)
6. Submit

After online application:
- Applications are randomized / selected by lottery in oversubscribed districts
- Selected farmers receive approval letter
- Farmer pays their share; government's subsidy goes directly to the empanelled vendor
- Vendor installs the pump at the farm

## Registration and Token Money

When you register, you pay a **registration fee (₹500–₹2,500)** as token advance. This is non-refundable if selected but adjusted against your farmer contribution.

**Important:** Applications are accepted season-wise. Check the portal for the current application window.

## Benefits of Solar Pump

- **No electricity bill** for irrigation (pump runs on solar power)
- **No diesel cost** (unlike diesel pump sets)
- Works during power cuts
- Low maintenance (solar panels last 25 years)
- Excess solar energy can be fed to grid under KUSUM Component C (additional income)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I apply for solar pump if I already have a diesel pump?
Yes — having a diesel pump doesn't disqualify you. The solar pump replaces/supplements it, eliminating fuel costs.

### My land is in someone else's name but I farm it. Am I eligible?
Typically, the land must be in the applicant's name or they must have a valid tenancy/lease agreement. Check with your Agriculture Department about tenancy-based applications.

### Can I apply for a higher capacity pump than needed?
The pump capacity should match your irrigated area. Overspecification may lead to rejection. A 3 HP pump typically covers 1–2 acres depending on crop type.

### Where can I check my application status?
Log in to [upagriculture.com](https://upagriculture.com) with your registration ID and check the application dashboard.

### The vendor installed a pump but it's not working properly. Who do I contact?
Empanelled vendors are responsible for post-installation support as per the warranty (typically 5 years for the pump, 25 years for solar panels). Contact the vendor first; if unresolved, approach the **Agriculture Department helpline: 7839-000-575**.
