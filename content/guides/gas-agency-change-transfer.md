# How to Change LPG Gas Agency: Transfer Process

If you have relocated to a new city or want to switch your LPG gas distributor, you can transfer your connection from one agency to another. This process works for transfers within the same OMC (e.g., one Indane dealer to another) or across OMCs (e.g., Indane to HP Gas or Bharat Gas). This guide covers both scenarios.

## When Do You Need to Transfer?

- **Relocation** — You moved to a new city/area not served by your current distributor
- **Poor service** — Delayed deliveries, rude behaviour or other issues with current dealer
- **Convenience** — Another distributor is closer to your new address
- **Cross-OMC switch** — You want to change from Indane to HP Gas or Bharat Gas (or vice versa)

## Types of Transfer

### 1. Within Same OMC (e.g., Indane to Indane)
Called **SVB Transfer** (Subscription Voucher Book transfer). Your consumer number changes but the OMC remains the same.

### 2. Across OMCs (e.g., Indane to HP Gas)
Called **Inter-OMC Transfer** or **Portability**. You surrender your connection with one company and get a new connection with another. This is not a direct transfer but a surrender-and-reapply process.

## Documents Required

- **KYC documents** — [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online), PAN card or Voter ID
- **New address proof** — Aadhaar with updated address, electricity bill, rent agreement
- **Existing SVB (Subscription Voucher Book)** — Your current LPG passbook
- **Aadhaar-linked bank account details** — For DBT subsidy transfer
- **Passport-size photograph** — 1-2 copies
- **Surrender certificate** (for cross-OMC transfer) — Issued by the old company

## How to Transfer LPG Connection Within Same OMC

### Step 1: Inform Your Current Distributor
Visit or call your current LPG distributor and inform them about the transfer. Submit a written request for transfer.

### Step 2: Get NOC / Transfer Certificate
The current distributor will issue a No Objection Certificate (NOC) or transfer-out slip after:
- Verifying no pending dues
- Collecting any empty cylinders with regulator
- Noting down your consumer details

### Step 3: Contact the New Distributor
Visit the new distributor in your area with:
- Transfer certificate/NOC from old distributor
- KYC documents
- New address proof
- Passport photo

### Step 4: Fill the Transfer Form
Fill the SVB transfer form at the new distributor's office.

### Step 5: Get New SVB and Consumer Number
The new distributor will process the transfer and issue:
- New SVB (passbook)
- New consumer number
- New cylinder and regulator (if you surrendered old ones)

### Step 6: Link Aadhaar and Bank Account
Ensure your Aadhaar and bank account are linked with the new connection on [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in/) for subsidy.

## How to Transfer Across OMCs (Indane to HP Gas / Bharat Gas)

### Step 1: Surrender Old Connection
Visit your current distributor and request connection surrender. Submit:
- Written surrender request
- SVB/passbook
- Empty cylinder(s) with regulator and tube

### Step 2: Collect Surrender Certificate
After returning equipment, collect:
- Surrender certificate
- Security deposit refund (₹1,450-1,800 per cylinder, transferred to bank account within 15-30 days)

### Step 3: Apply for New Connection
Visit a distributor of the new OMC and apply for a fresh connection with:
- Surrender certificate from old OMC
- KYC documents and address proof
- Passport photos
- Bank account details

### Step 4: Pay New Security Deposit
Pay the security deposit for the new connection (₹1,450-1,800 per cylinder + regulator deposit).

### Step 5: Receive New Connection
You will receive your new cylinder, regulator and SVB within 7-15 days.

## Online Transfer Process via mylpg.in

For within-OMC transfers, some steps can be initiated online:

1. Visit [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in/)
2. Login with your LPG consumer number
3. Go to "Request for Transfer" or "Portability"
4. Enter new area details
5. Select a new distributor
6. Submit the request
7. Visit the new distributor with documents to complete the process

> **Note:** Online initiation is available for some OMCs. The physical visit to the new distributor is still required.

## Double Cylinder Connection

If you use LPG frequently, you can apply for a second cylinder (double bottle connection):

1. Visit your current distributor
2. Request double cylinder connection
3. Pay additional security deposit (₹1,450-1,800)
4. No additional documents needed if KYC is already done
5. Second cylinder will be delivered within 7-15 days

## Processing Time

| Transfer Type | Timeline |
|--------------|----------|
| Within same OMC (same city) | 7-15 days |
| Within same OMC (different city) | 15-30 days |
| Across OMCs | 15-30 days (surrender + new connection) |
| Double cylinder connection | 7-15 days |
| Security deposit refund | 15-30 days to bank account |

## Fees and Charges

| Item | Amount (Approx) |
|------|-----------------|
| Transfer fee (within OMC) | ₹50-100 |
| New connection deposit (14.2 kg) | ₹1,450-1,800 |
| Regulator deposit | ₹150-250 |
| Double cylinder additional deposit | ₹1,450-1,800 |

> Security deposit from the old connection is refundable.

## Important Tips

1. **Clear all dues** before initiating transfer — pending bills or advance bookings must be settled.
2. **Return equipment** — Empty cylinders, regulator and tube must be returned for surrender.
3. **Update Aadhaar address** first if you've relocated. See our [Aadhaar address change guide](/guide/aadhaar-address-change-online).
4. **Keep surrender certificate safe** — It is needed for the new connection and security deposit refund.
5. **Check dealer ratings** — Ask neighbours about service quality before choosing a new distributor.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Can I transfer my LPG connection to another person?
Yes, LPG connection can be transferred to a family member (spouse, parent, child) by submitting a transfer request with KYC documents of the new person at the distributor.

### Q2: How long does the transfer process take?
Within the same OMC, it takes 7-15 days. Cross-OMC transfer (surrender + new) takes 15-30 days.

### Q3: Will I get my security deposit back?
Yes. When you surrender your connection, the security deposit is refunded to your bank account within 15-30 days.

### Q4: Can I transfer if I have pending refills booked?
No. Cancel any pending bookings and clear all dues before initiating transfer.

### Q5: Do I need to buy a new cylinder for the transfer?
For within-OMC transfer, you may carry your existing cylinder. For cross-OMC transfer, you surrender old equipment and receive new ones.

### Q6: Is there a fee for changing my gas agency?
Within-OMC transfer costs ₹50-100. Cross-OMC transfer involves surrendering (free) and paying security deposit for the new connection.

### Q7: Can I check gas prices before choosing a new agency?
Yes. All three OMCs have similar prices, but check our [LPG price guide](/guide/cooking-gas-price-check) for current rates.
