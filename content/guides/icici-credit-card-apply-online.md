# ICICI Credit Card Apply Online: Best Cards & Eligibility

ICICI Bank offers a wide range of credit cards for shopping, travel, fuel, and lifestyle needs. This guide covers how to apply online, the most popular cards, eligibility criteria, fees, and reward programs.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent platform and is not affiliated with ICICI Bank. Fees and features may change — always verify on [ICICI Bank's official website](https://www.icicibank.com/credit-card).

## How to Apply for ICICI Credit Card Online

### Step-by-Step Process

1. Visit [ICICI Bank Credit Cards page](https://www.icicibank.com/credit-card)
2. Browse available cards and click **"Apply Now"** on your preferred card
3. Enter your **mobile number** and verify with OTP
4. Fill in personal details — name, date of birth, PAN number, address
5. Enter employment details — employer name, designation, annual income
6. Upload documents (if required) — PAN card, address proof, income proof
7. Submit the application
8. You will receive a **reference number** via SMS
9. ICICI Bank will verify your application and may call for additional details
10. If approved, the card is dispatched within **7-15 working days**

### Check Pre-Approved Offers

If you are an existing ICICI Bank customer, you may have a **pre-approved credit card** offer:

1. Log in to [ICICI NetBanking](https://infinity.icicibank.com/)
2. Go to **Credit Cards > Pre-Approved Offers**
3. Or open the **iMobile Pay** app and check the **Cards** section
4. Pre-approved cards require minimal documentation and faster approval

## Documents Required

**For Salaried Applicants:**
- PAN card (mandatory)
- Aadhaar card or passport (address proof)
- Latest 3 months salary slips
- Latest 3 months bank statement
- Passport-size photograph

**For Self-Employed Applicants:**
- PAN card (mandatory)
- Aadhaar card or passport (address proof)
- ITR for last 2 years
- Business proof (GST certificate, shop establishment)
- Latest 6 months bank statement

## Reward Points: How They Work

- Most ICICI cards earn **2 PAYBACK points per ₹100** spent
- 1 PAYBACK point ≈ ₹0.25 (varies by redemption)
- Redeem points for products, vouchers, air miles, or statement credit
- Points expire after **2-3 years** from the earning date
- Fuel transactions, EMI payments, and wallet loads may not earn points

## How to Check ICICI Credit Card Application Status

1. Visit [ICICI Bank website](https://www.icicibank.com/)
2. Go to **Credit Cards > Track Application**
3. Enter your application reference number or mobile number
4. You can also call **1800 1080** for status updates

## Important Tips

1. **Check pre-approved offers first** — these have higher approval chances and faster processing
2. **Maintain a CIBIL score of 750+** for better approval odds and higher credit limits. See our [CIBIL score guide](/guide/cibil-score-check-free) for details
3. **Don't apply for multiple cards simultaneously** — each application creates a hard inquiry on your credit report
4. **Set up auto-pay** for at least the minimum due amount to avoid late payment charges
5. **Always pay the full bill** — revolving credit on credit cards carries interest rates of 24-42% per annum

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the minimum salary required for ICICI credit card?
The minimum salary requirement varies by card — Amazon Pay ICICI card is the easiest to get with income as low as ₹3 lakh per annum. Premium cards like Sapphiro need ₹12 lakh+.

### How long does ICICI credit card approval take?
Pre-approved cards can be approved instantly. New applications typically take 7-15 working days from submission to card delivery.

### Can I get an ICICI credit card without income proof?
If you have a pre-approved offer or a fixed deposit with ICICI Bank, you can get a credit card against FD without traditional income proof.

### How do I activate my ICICI credit card?
You can activate your card via iMobile Pay app, NetBanking, or by calling 1800 1080. You'll need to set your PIN during activation.

### What is the interest rate on ICICI credit cards?
Interest rates range from 3.40% per month (approximately 40.8% per annum) on revolving credit. Always pay the full bill to avoid interest charges.

### Can I increase my ICICI credit card limit?
Yes, you can request a limit increase via NetBanking (Credit Cards > Services > Limit Enhancement) or iMobile Pay. Approval depends on your repayment history and income.

### How do I close my ICICI credit card?
Call 1800 1080 or visit a branch. Ensure all dues are cleared, redeem pending reward points, and get a no-dues certificate after closure.
