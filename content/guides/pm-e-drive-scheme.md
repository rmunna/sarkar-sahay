# PM E-DRIVE Scheme 2026 — Electric Scooter Subsidy Up to ₹5,000, Claim Before July 31

**PM E-DRIVE (Electric Drive Revolution in Innovative Vehicle Enhancement)** is a central government scheme that provides demand-side incentives (subsidies) on purchase of electric vehicles in India — primarily electric two-wheelers (e-scooters, e-motorcycles) and electric three-wheelers (e-rickshaws, e-autos).

**Quick answer:** You don't apply separately — the subsidy is deducted at point of sale. Walk into an authorised EV dealer, complete Aadhaar e-KYC, sign the e-voucher, and the subsidy (up to ₹5,000 for a 2-wheeler) is deducted from your purchase price instantly. **Deadline for 2-wheelers: July 31, 2026.**

---

## Current Subsidy Rates (2026)

| Vehicle Category | Subsidy per vehicle |
|---|---|
| Electric 2-wheeler (L2 category) | ₹2,500 per kWh, max **₹5,000** (or 15% of ex-factory price) |
| Electric 3-wheeler auto/rickshaw (L5) | ₹7,500–₹10,000 per vehicle |
| Electric buses (M2/M3 for fleet operators) | Higher slabs based on bus capacity |

> **Deadline:** Subsidies for electric **two-wheelers expire July 31, 2026**. The government is evaluating a fresh phase for FY 2026-27 but nothing has been confirmed yet. Buy before July 31 to secure the current subsidy.

## Which EVs Qualify?

The vehicle must:
- Be manufactured in India with progressive localisation requirements
- Be listed on the **NATRIP E-Vehicle Portal** (approved EV list at [evreporting.heavyindustries.gov.in](https://evreporting.heavyindustries.gov.in))
- The **OEM (manufacturer) must be registered** under PM E-DRIVE to claim subsidy on your behalf

Popular brands confirmed under PM E-DRIVE:
- Ather Energy (Ather 450X, Ather Rizta)
- TVS Motor (iQube, X)
- Ola Electric (S1 series)
- Bajaj (Chetak)
- Hero MotoCorp (Vida series)

Check with your dealer if their specific model is registered under PM E-DRIVE before purchase.

## How to Claim the PM E-DRIVE Subsidy — Step by Step

The process happens at the **dealership**, not on a government portal:

### Step 1: Choose a Registered EV Model

Buy an electric 2-wheeler or 3-wheeler from a brand registered under PM E-DRIVE. Ask the dealer: *"Is this model covered under PM E-DRIVE?"*

### Step 2: Aadhaar e-KYC at the Showroom

The dealer will initiate an **Aadhaar-based e-KYC** verification:
- Provide your Aadhaar number
- OTP will be sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile
- Verify the OTP to complete identity verification

### Step 3: Sign the E-Voucher

After KYC, a digital e-voucher is generated showing:
- Vehicle details and ex-factory price
- Subsidy amount
- Your final payable price (price minus subsidy)

Sign the e-voucher digitally on the dealer's system.

### Step 4: Subsidy Applied Instantly

The subsidy amount is **deducted from your invoice** on the spot. You pay the vehicle price minus the PM E-DRIVE subsidy. No separate disbursement or waiting.

### Step 5: Manufacturer Claims from Government

The OEM/manufacturer claims reimbursement from the Ministry of Heavy Industries within 120 days of vehicle sale. This happens in the background — you don't need to do anything more.

## Eligibility

**For individual buyers:**
- Indian citizen / resident
- Purchasing a qualifying electric vehicle from a registered dealer
- Aadhaar card with active mobile number for OTP

**For fleet operators (e-auto, e-rickshaw):**
- Registered under state transport authority
- Additional documentation may be required

**For the vehicle:**
- Must be India-manufactured with NATRIP approval
- Battery must meet BIS standards
- Advanced Chemistry Cell (ACC) localisation requirements apply

## Income Tax Benefit (Additional Saving)

Over and above the PM E-DRIVE subsidy, EV buyers also get:

- **Section 80EEB:** Interest on EV loan deductible up to ₹1.5 lakh/year (for EVs purchased up to March 31, 2026 — check latest Finance Act update)
- **GST:** 5% GST on EVs vs 28% + cess on petrol 2-wheelers — a massive structural saving on every purchase

## Why Act Before July 31, 2026?

The current PM E-DRIVE subsidy tranche for electric 2-wheelers **expires July 31, 2026**. After this:
- The government may launch a new phase with revised subsidy rates (possibly lower)
- Or there may be a gap period with no subsidy

If you were planning to buy an electric scooter this year, buying before July 31 guarantees the current ₹5,000 subsidy.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is PM E-DRIVE the same as FAME-II?**
No — PM E-DRIVE replaced FAME-II from October 2024. It has different subsidy structures and is focused on more localised, India-manufactured vehicles.

**Do I need to apply on the government portal?**
No — individual buyers don't register anywhere. The dealer handles everything through the PM E-DRIVE OEM portal.

**What if the dealer says the model isn't under PM E-DRIVE?**
Ask them to check the NATRIP EV portal. If the model is genuinely not listed, you won't get the PM E-DRIVE subsidy — but the vehicle will still have lower GST than petrol options.

**Can I claim the subsidy if I'm buying second-hand?**
No — PM E-DRIVE subsidies apply only to **new vehicle purchases** from authorised dealers.

**What about electric cars?**
PM E-DRIVE's current subsidy tranche is focused on 2-wheelers and 3-wheelers. Electric cars (4-wheelers) are not covered in the current phase for individual buyers.

**Where can I check if my vehicle model qualifies?**
Visit [pmedrive.heavyindustries.gov.in](https://pmedrive.heavyindustries.gov.in) and look for the list of registered OEMs and vehicles, or ask your dealer.
