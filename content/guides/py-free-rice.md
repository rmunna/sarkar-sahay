# Free Supply of Rice – Puducherry

The Government of Puducherry provides free rice to families under the Public Distribution System (PDS). Eligible families holding ration cards receive monthly free rice allocation from their designated fair price shops, ensuring food security for economically weaker sections.

## What is the Free Rice Scheme?

Under this scheme, the Puducherry government distributes rice free of cost to ration card holders — both BPL (Below Poverty Line) and AAY (Antyodaya Anna Yojana) families. This supplements the central government's National Food Security Act (NFSA) provisions.

### Monthly Rice Entitlement

| Ration Card Type | Rice Per Person/Month |
|-----------------|----------------------|
| AAY (Antyodaya) | 35 kg per family (free) |
| BPL / Priority Household | 5 kg per person (free) |
| Above Poverty Line | Subsidized rate (not free) |

*Quantities are as per NFSA norms. Puducherry may provide additional allocation — check current orders.*

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Ration card holders:** Must have a valid ration card issued by Puducherry UT
2. **BPL / AAY families:** Free rice is primarily for BPL and AAY card holders
3. **Residency:** Must be a resident of Puducherry UT
4. **NFSA coverage:** Families covered under the National Food Security Act

## Documents Required

- Ration card (see our [ration card guide](/guide/ration-card-apply-online)) issued by Puducherry
- Aadhaar card for biometric authentication (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- No additional documents needed for monthly collection

## How to Get Free Rice

### Monthly Collection Process

1. **Know your fair price shop:** Check your ration card for your designated fair price shop (FPS)
2. **Visit during distribution days:** Go to the FPS on the designated distribution days for your area
3. **Carry ration card:** Bring your ration card
4. **Biometric authentication:** Authenticate via Aadhaar fingerprint at the FPS PoS machine
5. **Collect rice:** Receive your monthly rice entitlement
6. **Check quantity:** Verify you receive the correct quantity as per your card type
7. **Get entry recorded:** Ensure the shopkeeper records the distribution in your ration card / digital record

### If You Don't Have a Ration Card

1. **Apply for ration card:** Visit the Department of Civil Supplies office or apply through e-District portal
2. **Submit documents:** Aadhaar, residence proof, income proof, family details
3. **Verification:** Officials verify your residence and income
4. **Card issued:** Ration card is issued within 15–30 days

## Important Tips

1. **Collect every month:** Don't skip months — uncollected rice may not carry forward
2. **Aadhaar must be linked:** Ensure all family members' Aadhaar numbers are linked to the ration card for ePoS authentication
3. **Report issues:** If the FPS denies rice or gives less quantity, complain to the Civil Supplies Department
4. **Check quality:** Inspect the rice quality at the time of collection
5. **Update ration card:** Add new family members or remove deceased members to ensure correct entitlement

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: How much free rice will I get?
AAY families get 35 kg per family, and priority/BPL families get 5 kg per person per month, free of cost.

### Q2: Do I need to pay anything?
No, the rice is completely free for BPL and AAY families in Puducherry.

### Q3: What if the ration shop is not giving the full quantity?
Complain to the Civil Supplies Department or call the food helpline. You are entitled to the full quantity.

### Q4: Can I collect rice from any ration shop?
No, you must collect from the fair price shop assigned to your ration card. Portability may be available in some cases.

### Q5: What if I miss a month's collection?
Uncollected rice for a month generally cannot be carried forward. Try to collect every month.

### Q6: Is wheat or other grains also available free?
The primary free commodity is rice. Other items like sugar and kerosene may be available at subsidized rates.

### Q7: How do I apply for a ration card in Puducherry?
Visit the Civil Supplies Department or apply online through [edistrict.py.gov.in](https://edistrict.py.gov.in/). See our [ration card guide](/guide/ration-card-apply-online) for detailed steps.
