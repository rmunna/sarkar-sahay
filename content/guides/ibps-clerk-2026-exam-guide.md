# IBPS Clerk 2026 — Complete Guide to Bank Clerk Recruitment

The IBPS Clerk examination (officially known as **CRP Clerks** — Common Recruitment Process for Clerical Cadre) is conducted annually by the Institute of Banking Personnel Selection to recruit clerks across **11 public sector banks** in India.

With 6,000–10,000 vacancies each year and no interview requirement, IBPS Clerk is one of the most accessible banking jobs for graduates. A bank clerk starts at ₹26,000–₹30,000/month gross and can be promoted to Officer cadre in 3–5 years.

## Exam Pattern

### Phase 1: Prelims — Online Objective Test

| Section | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---------|-----------|-------|------|
| English Language | 30 | 30 | 20 min |
| Numerical Ability | 35 | 35 | 20 min |
| Reasoning Ability | 35 | 35 | 20 min |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** | **60 min** |

- **Negative marking:** 0.25 marks per wrong answer
- Sectional cut-offs apply
- Shortlisting: Top candidates (typically 8–10× vacancies) proceed to Mains

### Phase 2: Mains — Online Objective Test

| Section | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---------|-----------|-------|------|
| General/Financial Awareness | 50 | 50 | 35 min |
| General English | 40 | 40 | 35 min |
| Reasoning Ability & Computer Aptitude | 50 | 60 | 45 min |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 50 | 50 | 45 min |
| **Total** | **190** | **200** | **160 min** |

- **Negative marking:** 0.25 marks per wrong answer
- **No interview** — final selection based purely on Mains score
- **Sectional cut-offs apply in Mains** — must clear each section

## Detailed Syllabus

### English Language
**Prelims:** Reading Comprehension (1 passage), Fill in the Blanks, Error Spotting, Para Jumbles, Cloze Test, Spelling Errors

**Mains:** Reading Comprehension (2–3 passages), Sentence Rearrangement, Para Completion, Error Correction, Vocabulary (synonyms, antonyms in context), Double Fillers, Word Usage

### Numerical Ability / Quantitative Aptitude
**Prelims:** Simplification/Approximation, Number Series, Quadratic Equations, Data Interpretation (bar chart, pie chart), Percentage, Ratio, Profit/Loss, SI/CI, Time-Work, Time-Speed, Averages

**Mains (Advanced):** Caselet DI, Mixed DI, Data Sufficiency, Probability, Mensuration, Number System, Sequence/Series

### Reasoning Ability
**Prelims:** Seating Arrangement (linear, circular), Puzzles, Syllogism, Inequality, Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations, Direction Sense, Alphanumeric Series

**Mains:** Complex Puzzles (3–4 variables), Input-Output, Critical Reasoning, Logical Reasoning, Data Sufficiency, Computer Aptitude (integrated)

### Computer Aptitude (Mains)
MS Office basics, Internet fundamentals, Input/Output devices, Computer hardware/software, Networks, Security, Operating systems, Keyboard shortcuts, Database basics

### General/Financial Awareness (Mains)
- **Banking & Finance:** RBI policies, CRR, SLR, Repo/Reverse Repo, PMJDY, NPAs, Basel III, DICGC
- **Government Schemes:** PM Kisan, PM Awas, PM Mudra, Ayushman Bharat, Jan Dhan
- **Current Affairs:** Last 6 months (banking, economy, government appointments)
- **Static GK:** Bank headquarters, MD/CEOs, financial regulators (SEBI, IRDAI, PFRDA)
- **Economy:** GDP, Inflation, Union Budget 2026-27 highlights, Economic Survey
- **International:** IMF, World Bank, Asian Development Bank

## How to Apply Online

1. Visit **[ibps.in](https://www.ibps.in)**
2. Click on **"CRP Clerks"** notification
3. Click **"Apply Online"**
4. Register using email + mobile number
5. Fill in: personal details, educational qualifications, category, state preference
6. Upload photo (20–50 KB, JPG) + signature (10–20 KB, JPG)
7. Select exam state preferences (up to 2)
8. Pay application fee via net banking / debit card / UPI
9. Submit → Save the application printout and confirmation email

## 3-Month Preparation Strategy

### Month 1 — Foundation

| Day | Plan |
|-----|------|
| Morning | Quant: Chapter per day (Simplification, Percentage, Ratio, SI/CI, Profit/Loss) |
| Afternoon | Reasoning: Puzzles + Seating Arrangement (30 mins each) |
| Evening | English: Grammar (SP Bakshi) + 1 RC passage |
| Night | Current Affairs: 15 min daily (The Hindu / Livemint banking section) |

### Month 2 — Speed Building

- **Prelims mock tests:** 1 full mock per day (rotate platforms — Testbook, Adda247, Oliveboard)
- **Analysis ritual:** After each mock, categorise mistakes: silly error / concept gap / time issue
- **DI sets:** 2 new DI sets daily (bar chart, caselet rotation)
- **Banking GK:** Start monthly banking capsule (Oliveboard/Testbook)

### Month 3 — Mains Preparation

- **Mains mocks:** 3 per week (Mains pattern, 160 minutes)
- **Financial Awareness:** Complete RBI annual report summary + Budget 2026 highlights
- **Computer Aptitude:** Dedicated 2 hours (basic keyboard shortcuts, MS Office, networks)
- **Weak sections:** Identify and focus 2 hours daily on weakest area

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is there any interview for IBPS Clerk 2026?**
No — IBPS Clerk has no interview or GD. Final selection is based entirely on Mains score. This makes it accessible for candidates who are strong in written exams.

**How many vacancies are expected in IBPS Clerk 2026?**
Historically 6,000–10,000. The exact number is released in the official notification. Check [ibps.in](https://www.ibps.in) when the notification is out.

**Can I choose which bank I want to join through IBPS Clerk?**
You can indicate state preferences, and IBPS allots banks based on merit, vacancies, and your preference. You cannot directly choose a specific bank — allotment is at IBPS's discretion.

**What happens if I clear Prelims but don't clear Mains sectional cut-offs?**
You will not be selected even if your total Mains score is high. All sections have minimum qualifying marks — failing any one section disqualifies you.

**Can I appear for both IBPS Clerk and IBPS PO in the same year?**
Yes — they are separate exams with different timelines. IBPS PO Prelims are usually in October, IBPS Clerk Prelims are in November–December. Apply for both.

**How is state/language requirement checked for IBPS Clerk?**
After provisional allotment, the bank may conduct a local language test (read/write/speak). If you can't pass, allotment may be revoked. Apply for states where you know the local language.
