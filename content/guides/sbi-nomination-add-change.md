## What is SBI Nomination?

Nomination in an SBI bank account allows you to designate a person (nominee) who will receive the account balance in the event of the account holder's death. Under the Banking Regulation Act, every bank account holder should register a nominee. SBI allows up to **two nominees** with customizable percentage splits.

## Why is Nomination Important?

- **Smooth claim settlement:** Nominee can claim the balance without lengthy legal proceedings
- **Mandatory for insurance:** Linked insurance benefits require a valid nominee
- **RBI advisory:** RBI encourages all bank account holders to register nominees
- **Avoids family disputes:** Clear nomination prevents confusion among legal heirs

## Who Can Be a Nominee?

- Any individual (family member, friend, or any person)
- **Minor nominees** are allowed — but a guardian must be appointed
- NRI account holders can also nominate
- For joint accounts, nomination activates after the death of all account holders

## How to Add or Change Nominee in SBI

### Method 1: Via YONO SBI App (Online)

1. Open the **YONO SBI** app and log in
2. Go to **Service Requests** or **My Accounts**
3. Select **Nomination** → **Add/Modify Nomination**
4. Select your account number
5. Enter nominee details:
   - Full name
   - Date of birth
   - Relationship with account holder
   - Address
   - Percentage share (if adding multiple nominees)
6. For **minor nominees**, enter guardian details:
   - Guardian name
   - Guardian relationship
   - Guardian address
7. Verify with OTP sent to registered mobile
8. Submit and save the acknowledgement

### Method 2: Via SBI Net Banking (OnlineSBI)

1. Log in to [www.onlinesbi.sbi](https://www.onlinesbi.sbi/)
2. Navigate to **e-Services** → **Nomination in Deposit Accounts**
3. Click **Add/Modify Nomination**
4. Select the account
5. Enter nominee details (same as YONO method)
6. Set percentage share for each nominee
7. Submit with OTP verification
8. Print the acknowledgement for records

### Method 3: Via Branch Visit Using Form DA1 (Offline)

**Form DA1** is the standard nomination form for SBI deposit accounts.

1. Visit your SBI home branch
2. Request **Form DA1** (Nomination Form) from the counter
3. Fill in the form with:
   - Account holder details and account number
   - Nominee name, date of birth, and relationship
   - Nominee address
   - For minor nominee: guardian name and address
   - Percentage share (if multiple nominees)
4. Sign the form
5. For **joint accounts**, all account holders must sign
6. Submit with a photo ID proof copy
7. Collect acknowledgement receipt

## Form DA1 — Key Details

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Account Number | Your SBI savings/current account number |
| Nominee Name | Full name as per ID proof |
| Nominee DOB | Required especially for minor nominees |
| Relationship | Relation with the account holder |
| Nominee Address | Current residential address |
| Share Percentage | Percentage of balance for this nominee (must total 100%) |
| Guardian Details | Required only if nominee is a minor (below 18) |

> **Download:** Form DA1 is available at SBI branches. It may also be downloadable from [sbi.co.in](https://sbi.co.in/) under Forms section.

## Multiple Nominees and Percentage Split

SBI allows **up to 2 nominees** per deposit account:

- Each nominee can be assigned a **percentage share** of the account balance
- The total percentage must equal **100%**
- Example: Nominee 1 — 60%, Nominee 2 — 40%
- If only one nominee is registered, they receive 100% of the balance

| Scenario | Nominee 1 Share | Nominee 2 Share |
|---|---|---|
| Single nominee | 100% | — |
| Two nominees (equal) | 50% | 50% |
| Two nominees (custom) | 70% | 30% |

## Minor Nominee — Guardian Requirement

If your nominee is below 18 years of age:

- You **must** appoint a **guardian** for the minor nominee
- The guardian will receive the funds on behalf of the minor until they turn 18
- Guardian details required: name, relationship with minor, address, and ID proof
- Once the minor turns 18, they can claim directly by updating the nomination

**Important:** The guardian for nomination purposes can be different from the legal guardian.

## Documents Required

- **Form DA1** (for branch submission)
- **Account holder's photo ID** (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport)
- **Nominee's ID proof** (Aadhaar or any valid ID — for verification)
- **Guardian's ID proof** (if nominee is a minor)
- No separate documents needed for online nomination via YONO/net banking

## Important Tips

1. **Register a nominee immediately** after opening a bank account — don't delay
2. **Update nomination** when life events occur (marriage, birth of child, death of existing nominee)
3. **Nomination is not the same as a will** — legal heirs can still contest; but nomination ensures quick initial settlement
4. **Keep acknowledgement safe** — it's proof that nomination was registered
5. **Check nomination status** periodically via YONO or net banking under account details

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. How many nominees can I add in SBI account?

SBI allows up to 2 nominees per deposit account. You can split the percentage between them (must total 100%).

### Q2. Can I add nominee in SBI account online?

Yes. You can add or change nominee via YONO SBI app or SBI net banking (OnlineSBI). The update is instant.

### Q3. What is Form DA1 in SBI?

Form DA1 is the standard nomination registration/modification form for SBI deposit accounts. It is submitted at the branch for adding or changing nominees.

### Q4. Can a minor be a nominee in SBI account?

Yes. A minor (below 18) can be a nominee, but you must appoint a guardian who will manage the funds until the nominee turns 18.

### Q5. What happens if there is no nominee in SBI account?

Without a nominee, the legal heirs must produce a succession certificate, legal heir certificate, or court order to claim the account balance, which is a lengthy process.

### Q6. Can I change my SBI nominee without visiting the branch?

Yes. Nomination can be changed online via YONO SBI app or SBI net banking. No branch visit is needed for online modification.

### Q7. Does SBI nomination override a will?

No. Nomination does not override a will. The nominee acts as a trustee. Legal heirs can claim their rightful share through legal proceedings. However, nomination ensures the bank releases funds quickly to the nominee.
