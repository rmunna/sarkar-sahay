## What is the Devnarayan Scooty Scheme?

The Devnarayan Free Scooty Distribution Scheme is a Government of Rajasthan initiative that provides **free scooties (two-wheelers)** to meritorious girls from backward classes (OBC/MBC/EBC) who have scored well in their Class 12 board exams. The scheme encourages higher education among girls from economically and socially disadvantaged communities.

The scheme is administered by the **Department of Higher and Technical Education, Rajasthan** and has been running for several years, benefiting thousands of girls across the state.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Girls/women** belonging to **Most Backward Classes (MBC)**, **Other Backward Classes (OBC)**, or **Economically Backward Classes (EBC)** of Rajasthan
- Must have passed **Class 12** from a recognised board (RBSE/CBSE/equivalent)
- Minimum **65% marks** in Class 12 (for MBC category); the cutoff may vary by year
- Must have taken **regular admission** in a graduation (UG) course in a government or recognised college in Rajasthan
- **Family annual income** should not exceed **₹2.50 lakh**
- Must have a valid **caste certificate** from a competent authority
- Must be a **resident of Rajasthan**
- The applicant should not have received a scooty under any other government scheme

## Benefits

- **Free scooty** (typically Honda Activa or equivalent) delivered to the beneficiary
- One-time benefit — no recurring payments
- The scooty is registered in the **name of the girl student**
- Insurance and registration charges covered by the government

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar Card** — [See our Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
- **Jan Aadhaar Card** (Rajasthan family ID)
- **Class 12 marksheet** (showing 65%+ marks)
- **College admission proof** (current year enrollment certificate)
- **Caste Certificate** (OBC/MBC/EBC) issued by the SDM/Tehsildar
- **Income Certificate** (family income below ₹2.50 lakh)
- **Domicile Certificate** (Rajasthan resident proof)
- **Bank account passbook** (Aadhaar-linked)
- **Passport-size photographs**
- **Affidavit** stating the applicant has not received a scooty under any other scheme

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Online Application

1. **Visit the SSO portal** — Go to [sso.rajasthan.gov.in](https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in) and login or create an account
2. **Find the scholarship portal** — Navigate to "Scholarship" or "Devnarayan Scooty Scheme" under the HTE department
3. **Fill application form** — Enter personal details, Class 12 marks, college details, caste, and income information
4. **Upload documents** — Scan and upload all required documents (marksheet, caste certificate, income certificate, etc.)
5. **Verify details** — Double-check all information before submitting
6. **Submit application** — Click submit and note down the application ID
7. **College verification** — Your college will verify your enrollment details online
8. **District verification** — The district officer verifies caste and income certificates
9. **Merit list** — After verification, a merit list is published based on Class 12 marks
10. **Scooty distribution** — Selected students are invited for scooty distribution events

### Important Deadlines

- Applications typically open in **August-September** each year (after college admissions)
- The deadline is usually **October-November**
- Check the HTE website for exact dates each year

## Processing Time

- **Application to merit list:** 2-3 months
- **Merit list to distribution:** 1-2 months
- **Total:** Approximately 4-6 months from application

## How to Check Status

1. Login to **SSO portal** at [sso.rajasthan.gov.in](https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in)
2. Navigate to the scholarship section
3. Enter your **application ID** to check status
4. Merit lists are also published on the **HTE website** — [hte.rajasthan.gov.in](https://hte.rajasthan.gov.in)

## Important Tips

1. **Apply early** — Don't wait for the deadline; the portal often faces heavy traffic in the last few days
2. **Ensure correct caste category** — MBC/OBC/EBC certificates must be recently issued and from the correct authority
3. **Maintain college attendance** — If you drop out of college before receiving the scooty, your application will be cancelled
4. **Keep original documents safe** — You may need to show originals during the scooty distribution event
5. **Check cutoff trends** — The actual cutoff marks vary each year based on the number of applications; higher marks increase your chances

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: What is the minimum percentage required?

The general guideline is **65% in Class 12** for MBC category. However, the actual cutoff may be higher depending on the number of applicants. OBC and EBC cutoffs may differ slightly.

### Q2: Can I apply if I'm studying in a private college?

Yes, you can apply if your college is **recognised by the state government** and affiliated with a university. The college must be located in Rajasthan.

### Q3: Is this scheme only for girls?

Yes, the Devnarayan Free Scooty Scheme is exclusively for **girl students**. Male students from backward classes can check other scholarship schemes.

### Q4: Can I get cash instead of a scooty?

No, the scheme provides a **physical scooty only**. There is no option for cash equivalent. The scooty is delivered directly to the beneficiary.

### Q5: What if I already have a driving licence?

A driving licence is not mandatory at the time of application. However, you will need a valid **two-wheeler driving licence** to ride the scooty. You can apply for one at your nearest RTO.

### Q6: Can SC/ST girls also apply?

SC/ST girls have a **separate scooty scheme** (Kali Bai Bheel Scooty Yojana). They should apply under that scheme instead of the Devnarayan scheme.

### Q7: Is there a similar scheme for boys?

While boys don't get scooties, backward class male students can avail of **post-matric scholarships** and other education-related financial assistance from the SJE department.
