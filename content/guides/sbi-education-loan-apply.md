# SBI Education Loan Apply: Process & Documents Needed

SBI is India's largest provider of education loans, offering multiple schemes for studies in India and abroad. Whether you're pursuing engineering, medicine, MBA, or any professional course, SBI has loan products including Scholar Loan, Student Loan, and Global Ed-Vantage.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent informational platform and is not affiliated with the State Bank of India or the Government of India.

## What is SBI Education Loan?

SBI Education Loan covers tuition fees, hostel charges, books, equipment, travel (for abroad), and other education-related expenses. The loan is available for courses in India and overseas, with a repayment moratorium period (course duration + 1 year).

## SBI Education Loan Schemes

| Scheme | Studies | Loan Limit | Collateral |
|---|---|---|---|
| SBI Student Loan | India | Up to ₹10 lakh | No collateral up to ₹7.5 lakh |
| SBI Scholar Loan | India (premier institutes) | Up to ₹40 lakh | No collateral (for listed institutes) |
| SBI Global Ed-Vantage | Abroad | Up to ₹1.5 crore | Required for loans above ₹7.5 lakh |
| SBI Skill Loan | Skill development courses | Up to ₹1.5 lakh | No collateral |

## Who is Eligible?

- **Student:** Indian citizen who has secured admission in a recognized course
- **Age:** No specific age limit (must be a student)
- **Co-applicant:** Parent/guardian is mandatory as co-borrower
- **Academic record:** Good academic track record
- **Courses covered:**
  - Graduation, post-graduation (engineering, medicine, management, law, etc.)
  - Professional courses, diplomas
  - Courses in IITs, IIMs, NITs, and other premier institutes (Scholar Loan)
  - Courses in recognized foreign universities (Global Ed-Vantage)

## Documents Required

### Student Documents
- **Admission proof:** Admission letter/offer letter from the institution
- **Academic records:** Mark sheets of 10th, 12th, and graduation (if applicable)
- **Identity proof:** Aadhaar card, PAN card
- **Age proof:** Birth certificate, 10th mark sheet, or passport
- **Passport & Visa:** For abroad studies (Global Ed-Vantage)
- **Course fee structure:** From the institution

### Co-Applicant (Parent/Guardian) Documents
- **Identity proof:** Aadhaar, PAN, voter ID
- **Address proof:** Aadhaar, utility bills
- **Income proof:**
  - Salaried: Last 6 months' salary slips, Form 16, bank statements
  - Self-employed: Last 2 years' ITR, balance sheet, bank statements
- **Property documents:** If providing collateral

### Collateral Documents (if applicable)
- Property title deed
- Encumbrance certificate
- Property valuation report
- NOC from housing society (if applicable)

## Step-by-Step: Apply for SBI Education Loan

### Online via YONO SBI
1. **Log in to YONO SBI** app
2. **Go to Loans → Education Loan**
3. **Select the loan scheme** — Student Loan, Scholar Loan, or Global Ed-Vantage
4. **Fill application form** — student details, course, institution, loan amount
5. **Upload documents** — admission letter, mark sheets, income proof
6. **Submit application** — receive reference number
7. **SBI officer contacts you** for verification and processing
8. **Sanction and disbursement** — loan is disbursed directly to the institution

### At SBI Branch
1. **Visit your nearest SBI branch** with the student and co-applicant
2. **Meet the education loan officer**
3. **Fill the application form** and submit all documents
4. **Property valuation** (if collateral-based)
5. **Loan sanctioned** — sanction letter issued
6. **Sign loan agreement** and submit post-dated cheques/ECS mandate
7. **Disbursement** — directly to the institute in instalments (semester-wise)

## Collateral Requirements

| Loan Amount | Collateral Needed |
|---|---|
| Up to ₹7.5 lakh | No collateral, no margin |
| ₹7.5 lakh to ₹20 lakh | Collateral required + third-party guarantee |
| Above ₹20 lakh | Collateral required |
| SBI Scholar Loan (premier institutes) | No collateral up to ₹40 lakh |

**Accepted collateral:** Residential property, fixed deposits, government securities, LIC policy, gold/gold ornaments.

## Moratorium Period & Repayment

- **Moratorium:** Course duration + 1 year after completion (or 6 months after getting a job, whichever is earlier)
- **During moratorium:** Only simple interest accrues (you can optionally pay interest during this period to reduce burden)
- **Repayment tenure:** Up to 15 years after moratorium
- **EMI starts:** After moratorium period ends

## Tax Benefits

- **Section 80E:** Full interest paid on education loan is deductible (no upper limit) for up to 8 consecutive years from the start of repayment
- Only the student or parent (whoever repays) can claim the deduction
- See our [income tax guide](/guide/income-tax-return-file-online) for more on claiming deductions

## Important Tips

1. **Apply early** — start the loan process 2-3 months before your course starts to avoid delays
2. **Pay interest during moratorium** if possible — this significantly reduces your total repayment burden
3. **Check if your institute is on the Scholar Loan list** — premier institute students get collateral-free loans up to ₹40 lakh at lower rates
4. **Girl students save 0.50%** — ensure the student is the primary borrower to avail the concession
5. **Keep all fee receipts** — you'll need them for claiming Section 80E tax deductions

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: What is the maximum SBI education loan amount?
Up to ₹10 lakh (Student Loan), ₹40 lakh (Scholar Loan), and ₹1.5 crore (Global Ed-Vantage for abroad studies).

### Q2: Do I need to start repaying immediately?
No. Repayment starts after the moratorium period — course duration plus 1 year after course completion.

### Q3: Can I get SBI education loan without collateral?
Yes, loans up to ₹7.5 lakh don't require collateral. Scholar Loan offers collateral-free loans up to ₹40 lakh for premier institute students.

### Q4: What is SBI Scholar Loan?
SBI Scholar Loan is a special scheme for students admitted to IITs, IIMs, NITs, and other premier institutes listed by SBI. It offers higher limits and lower interest rates without collateral.

### Q5: Can I prepay my SBI education loan?
Yes, there is no prepayment penalty on SBI education loans. You can repay partially or fully at any time.

### Q6: Is education loan interest tax deductible?
Yes, under Section 80E, the entire interest paid is deductible for up to 8 years from the start of repayment. There is no upper limit on the deduction amount.

### Q7: How is the loan disbursed?
SBI disburses the loan directly to the educational institution in instalments — usually semester-wise or as per the fee schedule.
