## What is Driving Licence Renewal?

Your driving licence (DL) in India is valid for 20 years from the date of issue or until you turn 50 — whichever is earlier. After that, it must be renewed every 5 years. You can apply for renewal up to 1 year before expiry and up to 1 year after expiry without penalty. The renewal process is handled through the **Sarathi Parivahan portal** (sarathi.parivahan.gov.in).

If your DL has expired more than 1 year ago, you may need to appear for a driving test again at your RTO.

## Common Problems During Online DL Renewal (And Fixes)

### 1. Parivahan Portal Not Loading or Showing Errors

**Problem:** The Sarathi portal shows a blank page, server error, or "Service Temporarily Unavailable."

**Fix:**
- Try during off-peak hours (early morning 6-8 AM or late night)
- Clear your browser cache and cookies
- Use Google Chrome or Mozilla Firefox (avoid mobile browsers)
- Disable any ad-blocker or VPN
- Try the direct state-specific URL from sarathi.parivahan.gov.in

### 2. Slot Not Available for DL Renewal

**Problem:** When booking an RTO appointment, no slots appear or "No slots available" message shows.

**Fix:**
- Check back daily — slots are released periodically by each RTO
- Try selecting a different nearby RTO office
- Some states allow renewal without RTO visit for age below 55 — check if your state supports fully online renewal
- Contact your RTO helpline for slot availability

### 3. Payment Failed But Amount Deducted

**Problem:** Payment was deducted from your bank but the portal shows "Payment Failed."

**Fix:**
- Wait 24-48 hours — the amount usually auto-refunds to your account
- Do NOT make a second payment immediately
- Check your application status on the portal after 24 hours
- If the amount is not refunded within 7 working days, raise a grievance at [pgportal.gov.in](https://pgportal.gov.in)
- Keep the bank transaction receipt as proof

### 4. "DL Number Not Found" Error

**Problem:** When entering your DL number, the portal says "Record not found."

**Fix:**
- Ensure you're entering the DL number exactly as printed on your licence (including state code)
- Check if your DL was issued before digitization — you may need to visit the RTO in person
- Try the format: State Code + RTO Code + Year + Number (e.g., KA01 20120001234)
- If your old DL was issued by a different state, select that state on the portal

### 5. Documents Upload Failing

**Problem:** Uploaded documents are rejected or upload fails with an error.

**Fix:**
- File size must be under 1 MB (200 KB for photos, 300 KB for documents in some states)
- Use JPEG/JPG or PDF format only
- Ensure the scan is clear and readable
- Photo must have white background, front-facing
- Reduce file size using an online compressor before uploading

## Step-by-Step DL Renewal Process (Online)

1. Visit [sarathi.parivahan.gov.in](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in)
2. Select your **state** from the dropdown
3. Click **"DL Services"** → **"Apply for DL Renewal"**
4. Enter your **DL number** and **date of birth**
5. Verify your details and update any changed information (address, mobile number)
6. Upload required **documents** (photo, signature, medical certificate if applicable)
7. **Pay the fee** online (debit card, credit card, net banking, UPI)
8. **Book an RTO slot** (if required in your state)
9. Visit the RTO on the appointment date (if required) with original documents
10. Receive the renewed DL — dispatched to your registered address via Speed Post

## Documents Required for DL Renewal

- **Existing Driving Licence** (original + photocopy)
- **Address Proof** (Aadhaar card, passport, utility bill, voter ID)
- **Age Proof** (if not available in existing records)
- **Passport-size photographs** (recent, white background)
- **Medical Certificate** (Form 1-A) — mandatory if you are above 40 years for transport vehicle, or above 50 for non-transport
- **Self-declaration in Form 1** (health declaration)

> **Tip:** If you've moved to a new address, see our [DL address change guide](/guide/driving-licence-address-change-online) to update your address during renewal.

## Processing Time

- **Online application processing:** 7-30 days (varies by state and RTO workload)
- **Smart card DL delivery:** 15-45 days via Speed Post after approval
- **Temporary receipt:** Available for download immediately after RTO approval (valid for 30 days)

## Important Tips

1. **Apply early** — start the renewal process at least 30 days before your DL expires to avoid driving with an expired licence
2. **Keep digital copies** — save scanned copies of all documents before visiting the portal
3. **Medical certificate** — get it from a registered medical practitioner (government doctor preferred) in Form 1-A
4. **Track your application** — use the [application status check](/guide/driving-licence-status-check-online) to monitor progress
5. **Carry originals** — if you need to visit the RTO, always carry original documents along with photocopies

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I renew my driving licence online without visiting the RTO?
Some states allow fully online renewal for non-transport vehicles if you are below 55 years and no changes are needed. Check your state's policy on the Sarathi portal. Many states still require an RTO visit for biometric capture.

### What happens if my driving licence expired more than 1 year ago?
You may need to appear for a driving test again. The RTO can also impose a late fee. Apply through the Sarathi portal and the RTO will inform you if a test is required.

### Can I renew my DL from a different state than where it was issued?
Yes, you can apply for renewal at any RTO in India. You'll need to provide your current address proof. The new RTO will coordinate with your original issuing RTO.

### How long is the renewed driving licence valid?
The renewed DL is valid for 5 years from the date of renewal (for those above 50 years) or until the holder turns 50, whichever applies. For commercial/transport licences, different validity periods may apply.

### What if I lost my DL and need to renew it?
You'll need to first apply for a [duplicate DL](/guide/driving-licence-duplicate-lost-apply) and then proceed with renewal. Both services can sometimes be combined — check with your RTO.

### Can I change my address during DL renewal?
Yes, you can update your address during renewal by providing new address proof documents. See our [address change guide](/guide/driving-licence-address-change-online) for details.
