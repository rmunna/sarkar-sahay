# Chhattisgarh Vridha Pension Yojana – Old Age Pension Eligibility and Application

Chhattisgarh Vridha Pension Yojana (Old Age Pension Scheme) provides monthly financial assistance to senior citizens of the state who belong to economically weaker sections. The scheme is implemented through the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Chhattisgarh, and includes both the central government's Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS) and the state's own top-up contribution.

## What is Chhattisgarh Vridha Pension?

The Chhattisgarh Old Age Pension scheme provides a monthly pension to elderly citizens who are poor and do not have a regular source of income. The scheme operates under two components:

- **Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS):** Central government provides ₹200/month (for age 60–79) and ₹500/month (for age 80+) to BPL senior citizens
- **State top-up:** Chhattisgarh government adds its own contribution, bringing the total pension to approximately **₹350–₹500 per month** for most beneficiaries

The combined pension is credited directly to the beneficiary's bank account or paid through post offices.

---

## Who is Eligible?

To receive old age pension in Chhattisgarh, an applicant must meet the following criteria:

- **Age:** Must be **60 years or above**
- **Residency:** Must be a permanent resident of Chhattisgarh
- **Economic status:** Must belong to a **Below Poverty Line (BPL)** family or be a destitute/poor person as defined under the Chhattisgarh Assistance to Dependants and Disabled Persons Act
- **Income:** Should not have a regular source of income or pension from any other government scheme
- **Not receiving other pension:** Must not be receiving pension from any other government service or PSU

### Pension Amount by Age Group

| Age Group | Central Share (IGNOAPS) | State Share | Total (approx.) |
|-----------|------------------------|-------------|-----------------|
| 60–79 years | ₹200/month | ₹100–₹300/month | ₹350–₹500/month |
| 80 years and above | ₹500/month | ₹100–₹200/month | ₹600–₹700/month |

> **Note:** The exact amount may vary based on state notifications and annual budget revisions. Check with your local office for the latest figures.

## Documents Required

- **[Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)** (mandatory)
- **Age proof** — Birth certificate, school leaving certificate, or Aadhaar card showing date of birth
- **BPL card** or **[Ration card](/guide/ration-card-apply-online)** (to prove economic status)
- **Bank account passbook** (Aadhaar-linked account preferred for DBT)
- **Domicile / residence certificate** of Chhattisgarh
- **Passport-size photographs** (2 copies)
- **Income certificate** (from Tehsildar or competent authority)
- **Voter ID** or any other government-issued identity proof

## How to Apply – Step-by-Step

### Online Application

1. Visit the Chhattisgarh Social Welfare Department portal at [sw.cg.gov.in](https://sw.cg.gov.in/)
2. Look for **"Pension Schemes"** or **"Old Age Pension"** section
3. Click on **"Apply Online"** or **"New Application"**
4. Enter your Aadhaar number for verification
5. Fill in personal details — name, age, address, bank account, BPL status
6. Upload required documents (photograph, age proof, BPL card)
7. Submit the application and note the **application reference number**

### Offline Application

1. Visit your nearest **Gram Panchayat office** (rural) or **Nagar Palika / Municipal office** (urban)
2. Collect the **pension application form** (also known as "Vridha Pension Form")
3. Fill the form with personal details, age proof, and bank information
4. Attach self-attested photocopies of all required documents
5. Submit the form to the **Gram Panchayat Secretary** or **Ward Officer**
6. The application is forwarded to the **Janpad Panchayat** (block level) or **District Social Welfare Officer** for verification
7. After verification, approved applicants are added to the pension list

### Through CSC (Jan Seva Kendra)

You can also apply through your nearest **Common Service Centre (CSC)** with all required documents.

## Processing Time

- **Application verification:** 1–3 months (depends on district workload)
- **First pension payment:** 1–2 months after approval
- **Payment cycle:** Monthly or quarterly, depending on the district

## How to Check Pension Status

1. Visit [sw.cg.gov.in](https://sw.cg.gov.in/) or the NSAP portal at [nsap.nic.in](https://nsap.nic.in/)
2. Navigate to **"Pension Status"** or **"Beneficiary Search"**
3. Enter your **application number**, **Aadhaar number**, or **name**
4. View the approval status and payment details

You can also visit your Gram Panchayat or Nagar Palika office to check the status in person.

## Important Tips

1. **Apply as soon as you turn 60** — Processing can take months, so apply early
2. **Ensure Aadhaar-bank linking** — Pension is paid via DBT; link your Aadhaar with your bank account
3. **Keep BPL card handy** — This is the primary proof for economic eligibility
4. **Verify your name** — Ensure your name matches across Aadhaar, bank account, and BPL/ration card
5. **Annual verification** — Some districts require annual life verification (Jeevan Praman). Keep it updated to avoid pension stoppage

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### How much pension do senior citizens get in Chhattisgarh?

Senior citizens aged 60–79 receive approximately ₹350–₹500 per month, and those aged 80 and above receive approximately ₹600–₹700 per month (combined central and state share).

### Is BPL card mandatory for old age pension?

Yes, the pension is primarily for BPL/destitute persons. A BPL card or equivalent proof of economic status is required.

### Can I apply if I already receive a widow pension?

No, generally you cannot receive two social security pensions simultaneously. You may need to choose one scheme.

### How is the pension paid?

The pension is credited directly to your bank account via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) or through post office in some areas.

### What is Jeevan Praman and is it required?

Jeevan Praman is a digital life certificate for pensioners. Some districts require annual submission to confirm that the beneficiary is alive. You can generate it at any CSC, bank, or via the Jeevan Praman app.

### Can I apply on behalf of an elderly family member?

Yes, a family member can submit the application on behalf of an elderly person. However, the documents and bank account must be in the name of the beneficiary.

### What if my pension is stopped?

Visit your Gram Panchayat or District Social Welfare office to inquire. Common reasons include incomplete life verification, bank account issues, or Aadhaar mismatch.
