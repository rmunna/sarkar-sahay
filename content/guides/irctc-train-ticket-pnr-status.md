# How to Check Train PNR Status, Book Ticket & Cancel — IRCTC Complete Guide

Every day, crores of Indians travel by train. Whether you want to **check your PNR status**, **book a train ticket online**, or **cancel and get a refund** — this guide covers everything in simple steps.

---

## What Is a PNR Number?

**PNR (Passenger Name Record)** is a 10-digit number printed on your train ticket. It is your unique booking reference. Using this number, you can check whether your ticket is confirmed, waitlisted, or under RAC.

You get a PNR number every time you book a ticket — whether online on IRCTC or at the railway counter.

---

## How to Check PNR Status Online

### Method 1: IRCTC Website

1. Go to [irctc.co.in](https://www.irctc.co.in)
2. Click on **"PNR Status"** on the homepage
3. Enter your **10-digit PNR number**
4. Click **Submit** — your current booking status will appear

### Method 2: Indian Railways Enquiry Portal

1. Visit [enquiry.indianrail.gov.in](https://enquiry.indianrail.gov.in)
2. Click on **"PNR Enquiry"**
3. Enter your PNR number and check the status

### Method 3: NTES Mobile App

1. Download the **NTES (National Train Enquiry System)** app from Google Play Store or Apple App Store
2. Open the app and tap **"PNR Enquiry"**
3. Enter your PNR number to see the latest status

### Method 4: SMS

Send an SMS: **PNR <your 10-digit PNR number>** to **139**

## Understanding PNR Status Codes

| Status Code | Full Form | What It Means |
|---|---|---|
| **CNF / Confirmed** | Confirmed | Your seat/berth is confirmed. You can travel. |
| **RAC** | Reservation Against Cancellation | You will get a shared berth. You can board the train. |
| **WL** | Waitlist | No seat yet. If someone cancels, your ticket may get confirmed. |
| **GNWL** | General Waitlist | Waitlist from the train's starting station. Best chance of confirmation. |
| **RLWL** | Remote Location Waitlist | Waitlist for intermediate stations. Lower chance of confirmation. |
| **PQWL** | Pooled Quota Waitlist | For shorter intermediate journeys. Limited quota. |
| **TQWL** | Tatkal Waitlist | Waitlist in tatkal quota. |
| **REGRET/WL** | Regret | Ticket not confirmed. Cannot travel. |
| **CAN** | Cancelled | Ticket has been cancelled. |

> **Tip:** GNWL tickets have the best chance of getting confirmed. RLWL and PQWL have lower chances.

## How to Create an IRCTC Account

You need an IRCTC account to book train tickets online. Registration is free.

### Steps to Register:

1. Go to [irctc.co.in](https://www.irctc.co.in)
2. Click **"Register"** (or "Sign Up") on the top-right
3. Fill in your details:
   - Full name (as per ID proof)
   - Username and password
   - Date of birth
   - Mobile number
   - Email ID
   - Address and PIN code
4. Enter the **captcha** and click **Submit**
5. You will receive an **OTP on your mobile and email** — verify both
6. Your IRCTC account is now active

> **Important:** You must use your **real name** matching your government ID (Aadhaar, PAN, etc.) because it will appear on the ticket.

## How to Book a Train Ticket Online on IRCTC

### Step-by-Step Process:

1. **Login** to [irctc.co.in](https://www.irctc.co.in) with your username and password
2. On the homepage, enter:
   - **From Station** (departure city/station)
   - **To Station** (arrival city/station)
   - **Journey Date**
   - **Class** (Sleeper, AC 3-Tier, AC 2-Tier, etc.)
   - **Quota** (General, Tatkal, Ladies, etc.)
3. Click **"Search"** — a list of available trains will appear
4. Select your preferred train and click **"Book Now"**
5. Enter **passenger details** — name, age, gender, berth preference
6. Choose your **payment method** — UPI, debit card, credit card, net banking, or wallets
7. Complete the **payment**
8. Your **e-ticket** with PNR number will be shown on screen and sent to your email/mobile

> **Keep your e-ticket** (SMS or printout) and a **valid photo ID** (Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, Passport, Driving Licence) while travelling.

## Types of Booking Quotas

Indian Railways offers different quotas for different categories of passengers:

| Quota | Who Can Use It | Key Details |
|---|---|---|
| **General (GN)** | All passengers | Default quota. Booking opens 120 days before journey. |
| **Tatkal (TQ)** | All passengers | For last-minute travel. Opens 1 day before journey at **10:00 AM** (AC classes) and **11:00 AM** (non-AC/Sleeper). |
| **Premium Tatkal (PT)** | All passengers | Dynamic pricing — fare increases with demand. No refund on cancellation. |
| **Ladies (LD)** | Women passengers | Small quota reserved for women travelling alone or with children below 12. |
| **Defence (DF)** | Defence personnel | Requires valid defence ID. |
| **Foreign Tourist (FT)** | Foreign nationals | Requires passport details. Can be booked through IRCTC or at dedicated counters. |
| **Lower Berth (LB)** | Senior citizens, women 45+, disabled | Priority for lower berths. |
| **Duty Pass (DP)** | Railway employees | For official duty travel. |

## Tatkal Ticket Booking — Tips & Rules

Tatkal tickets are in high demand. Here's how to increase your chances:

### Tatkal Booking Timings

- **AC classes (1A, 2A, 3A, 3E, EC, CC):** Booking opens at **10:00 AM**, one day before the journey
- **Non-AC classes (SL, 2S):** Booking opens at **11:00 AM**, one day before the journey

### Tatkal Rules

- **Tatkal charges** are extra, on top of the normal fare (₹100–₹500 depending on class)
- **No refund** if you cancel a confirmed tatkal ticket (only refund if train is cancelled by Railways)
- Maximum **4 passengers** per PNR in tatkal
- **No change of name** or boarding point allowed after booking
- **ID proof is mandatory** during travel — ticket checker will verify

### Tips for Faster Tatkal Booking

1. **Login early** — log in to IRCTC at least 10–15 minutes before booking opens
2. **Pre-fill passenger details** — save your master passenger list in IRCTC profile
3. **Use fast payment** — IRCTC iPay, UPI, or wallets are faster than net banking
4. **Keep your internet connection fast** and stable
5. **Be ready at exactly 10:00/11:00 AM** — even a few seconds delay matters
6. **Try the IRCTC Rail Connect app** — sometimes the app is faster than the website

## How to Cancel a Train Ticket & Get Refund

### Online Cancellation (IRCTC)

1. Login to [irctc.co.in](https://www.irctc.co.in)
2. Go to **"My Transactions"** → **"Booked Ticket History"**
3. Select the ticket you want to cancel
4. Click **"Cancel Ticket"** — you can cancel all passengers or select specific ones
5. Confirm the cancellation
6. Refund will be credited to your original payment method within **5–7 working days**

> **Note:** Counter tickets must be cancelled at the railway counter, not online.

### Online Cancellation Timings

- You can cancel online tickets **up to 4 hours before** the scheduled departure of the train
- After that window, you need to file a **TDR (Ticket Deposit Receipt)** for refund

### Refund Rules & Cancellation Charges

Cancellation charges depend on the class and how early you cancel:

| Class | Cancellation Charge (if cancelled before chart preparation) |
|---|---|
| **AC First Class (1A)** | ₹240 per passenger |
| **AC 2-Tier (2A)** | ₹200 per passenger |
| **AC 3-Tier (3A) / 3E** | ₹180 per passenger |
| **AC Chair Car (CC)** | ₹180 per passenger |
| **Sleeper (SL)** | ₹120 per passenger |
| **Second Sitting (2S)** | ₹60 per passenger |

**Additional Rules:**

- **After chart preparation but before departure:** Cancellation charge is **25% of the fare** (minimum flat charge as above)
- **Confirmed Tatkal tickets:** **No refund** (except when train is cancelled by Railways)
- **RAC / Waitlisted Tatkal tickets:** Refund available after deducting cancellation charge
- **Waitlisted tickets not confirmed after chart:** **Full refund** is given automatically (no cancellation needed)
- **E-ticket (partially confirmed):** Only unconfirmed passengers get auto-refund

> **Pro Tip:** If your waitlisted ticket doesn't get confirmed after chart preparation, you get an **automatic full refund** — you don't need to cancel it manually.

## How to Book Food on Train (E-Catering)

Indian Railways offers **e-catering** so you can order food from restaurants and get it delivered to your seat.

### How to Order:

1. Visit [ecatering.irctc.co.in](https://www.ecatering.irctc.co.in) or download the **"Food on Track"** app
2. Enter your **PNR number**
3. The system will show your train route and upcoming stations
4. Browse **available restaurants** at those stations
5. Select your food items and place the order
6. Pay online (UPI, cards, wallets)
7. Food will be **delivered to your seat/berth** at the selected station

> **Availability:** E-catering is available on most major trains and stations. Minimum order value may apply.

## How to Check Live Train Running Status

Want to know if your train is on time or delayed?

### Method 1: NTES Website

1. Go to [enquiry.indianrail.gov.in](https://enquiry.indianrail.gov.in)
2. Click **"Spot Your Train"** or **"Train Running Status"**
3. Enter your **train number or name**
4. Select the **journey date**
5. See real-time location, delay info, and expected arrival at each station

### Method 2: NTES Mobile App

Download the **NTES app** and use the "Spot Your Train" feature for live updates.

### Method 3: Call 139

Dial **139** from your phone and follow the IVR menu to check train running status.

## How to File a Railway Complaint (RailMadad)

If you face any problem during your train journey — dirty coach, no water, harassment, late running, catering issues — you can file a complaint online.

### Steps to File a Complaint:

1. Visit [railmadad.indianrailways.gov.in](https://railmadad.indianrailways.gov.in)
2. Click **"Register Grievance"**
3. Enter your **PNR number** or **train details**
4. Select the **type of complaint** (cleanliness, staff behaviour, punctuality, catering, security, etc.)
5. Describe your issue and **upload photos** if needed
6. Submit — you will receive a **reference number**
7. Track your complaint status using the reference number

You can also call **139** and select the complaint option, or use the **RailMadad app**.

> **Response time:** Railways aims to resolve complaints within **72 hours**.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does it take for IRCTC refund to come?
Refund is usually credited within **5–7 working days** to your original payment method (bank account, card, or wallet).

### Can I book train tickets without an IRCTC account?
No. You must have an IRCTC account to book tickets online. You can also book at railway counters without an account.

### What if my waitlisted ticket is not confirmed?
If your waitlisted ticket is not confirmed after chart preparation, you get an **automatic full refund**. You don't need to cancel it manually.

### Can I change the name on a booked ticket?
Name changes are **not allowed** on e-tickets. You need to cancel and rebook.

### Is Aadhaar mandatory for booking train tickets?
Aadhaar is not mandatory for booking, but you must carry a **valid photo ID** during travel.

### What is the maximum advance booking period?
You can book train tickets **120 days (4 months) in advance**, excluding the date of journey.
