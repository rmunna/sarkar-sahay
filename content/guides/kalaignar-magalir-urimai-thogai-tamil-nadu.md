## What Is Kalaignar Magalir Urimai Thogai Scheme?

**Kalaignar Magalir Urimai Thogai Thittam** (கலைஞர் மகளிர் உரிமைத் தொகை திட்டம்) is a flagship welfare scheme launched by the Government of Tamil Nadu under the DMK government led by Chief Minister M.K. Stalin. The scheme provides **₹1,000 per month** directly to eligible women who are heads of their families.

Named after former Chief Minister M. Karunanidhi (Kalaignar), the scheme aims to promote women's financial independence and support low-income households across Tamil Nadu. The amount is credited directly to the beneficiary's bank account every month.

> You'll need a [ration card](/guide/ration-card-apply-online-tamil-nadu) for this scheme.

## Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for the Kalaignar Magalir Urimai Thogai scheme, you must meet **all** of the following conditions:

| Criteria | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Gender** | Women only |
| **Age** | 21 years or above |
| **Domicile** | Must be a resident of Tamil Nadu |
| **Family status** | Must be the woman head of the family (as per ration card) |
| **Ration card** | Must hold a valid rice ration card (family card) |
| **Government employment** | Applicant or spouse must NOT be a current government employee |
| **Income tax** | Applicant or family members must NOT be income tax payers |
| **Other pensions** | Must not be receiving more than ₹1,000/month from other government pension schemes |
| **Land ownership** | Must not own more than 5 acres of wet land or 10 acres of dry land |

### Who Is NOT Eligible?

- Women whose family members are government employees (state or central)
- Women whose family members pay income tax
- Women already receiving ₹1,000 or more per month from other state/central pension schemes
- Women below 21 years of age
- Women who are not listed as head of family on the ration card

## Documents Required

Keep these documents ready before applying:

1. **Family (Rice) Ration Card** — Must list the applicant as head of family
2. **Aadhaar Card** — For identity verification
3. **Bank Account Passbook** — Account must be in the applicant's name and linked to Aadhaar
4. **Mobile Number** — Linked to Aadhaar for OTP verification
5. **Passport-size Photo** — Recent photograph

> **Tip:** Ensure your Aadhaar is linked to your bank account before applying. This is mandatory for receiving the monthly payment via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

## How to Apply

### Online — Via TN e-Sevai Portal

1. Visit the official e-Sevai portal at **[tnesevai.tn.gov.in](https://www.tnesevai.tn.gov.in)**
2. Go to your nearest **Common Service Centre (CSC)** or e-Sevai centre
3. The operator will log in and select the Magalir Urimai Thogai application
4. Provide your ration card number, Aadhaar number, and bank details
5. Complete OTP verification on your registered mobile number
6. Submit the application and collect the **acknowledgement receipt**
7. Note down your **application number** for tracking

> **Note:** Individual online self-application is not available. You must visit an e-Sevai centre or CSC for application submission.

### Offline — Via Special Camps

The Tamil Nadu government conducts **special enrollment camps** at:

- Panchayat offices
- Municipal/corporation offices
- Taluk offices
- Community halls and designated camp locations

**What to bring to the camp:**
- Original ration card
- Aadhaar card (original + photocopy)
- Bank passbook (original + photocopy)
- Passport-size photo

Camp dates are announced through local newspapers, TV channels, and district collector offices. Check with your local panchayat or ward office for upcoming camps.

## How to Check Payment Status

### Method 1: SMS/Call

- You will receive an **SMS notification** on your registered mobile number when the payment is credited to your bank account.

### Method 2: Bank Account

- Check your **bank passbook** or use your bank's mobile app/net banking to verify the monthly credit of ₹1,000.

### Method 3: e-Sevai Centre

- Visit your nearest e-Sevai centre with your application number or ration card number to check your application and payment status.

### Method 4: Helpline

- Call the Tamil Nadu government helpline **1800-425-5101** (toll-free) for scheme-related queries.

## Payment Schedule

- Payments are made **monthly** via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)
- The amount of **₹1,000** is credited directly to the beneficiary's Aadhaar-linked bank account
- Payments are typically processed between the **1st and 10th of each month**
- If your bank account is inactive or not linked to Aadhaar, the payment may be delayed

## Important Tips

- ✅ **Keep your Aadhaar linked** to your bank account — this is mandatory for DBT
- ✅ **Update your ration card** if you are not listed as head of family — visit your local taluk supply office
- ✅ **Save your acknowledgement receipt** after applying — you'll need the application number for tracking
- ✅ **Keep your bank account active** — make at least one transaction every 6 months
- ⚠️ **Beware of fraud** — the government does NOT charge any fee for this scheme. Do not pay anyone for applying
- ⚠️ **No middlemen needed** — apply only through official e-Sevai centres or government camps

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. How much money will I receive under this scheme?

You will receive **₹1,000 per month** (₹12,000 per year), credited directly to your bank account.

### 2. Can I apply online from home?

No, currently you cannot apply from home. You must visit an **e-Sevai centre** or attend a **government enrollment camp** to submit your application.

### 3. Is there any application fee?

No. The Kalaignar Magalir Urimai Thogai scheme is **completely free**. Do not pay any fee to anyone for applying. Only the standard e-Sevai service charge (if any) may apply at CSC centres.

### 4. I already receive an old-age pension. Can I still apply?

If you are receiving **less than ₹1,000/month** from another government pension scheme, you may be eligible for the difference amount. If you already receive ₹1,000 or more, you are not eligible for this scheme.

### 5. My ration card shows my husband as the head of family. What should I do?

You need to get your ration card updated to show **yourself as the head of family**. Visit your local taluk supply office or apply for the change through e-Sevai. Check our guide on [how to apply for a ration card](/guide/ration-card-apply-online-tamil-nadu).

### 6. When will I receive my first payment after applying?

After your application is verified and approved, you will typically receive your first payment within **30-60 days**. Verification includes checking your ration card details, Aadhaar, and bank account linkage.

### 7. What if my payment is not credited?

If your payment is delayed or missing:
- Check if your Aadhaar is linked to your bank account
- Ensure your bank account is active
- Visit your nearest e-Sevai centre to check status
- Call the helpline at **1800-425-5101**

### 8. Is this scheme only for BPL (Below Poverty Line) families?

The scheme is available to all women heads of families holding a **rice ration card** in Tamil Nadu, subject to the eligibility criteria mentioned above. It is not restricted to BPL card holders only.
