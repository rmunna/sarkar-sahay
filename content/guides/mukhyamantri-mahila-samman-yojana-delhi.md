## What is Mukhyamantri Mahila Samman Yojana?

**Mukhyamantri Mahila Samman Yojana** is a Delhi government scheme that provides **₹1,000 per month** to eligible women aged 18 and above who are residents of Delhi. The scheme was announced as one of the Aam Aadmi Party's key women empowerment initiatives.

The scheme aims to provide financial independence to women and support household expenses. Payment is made **directly to the woman's bank account** (DBT).

## Who is Eligible?

- **Women** aged **18 years and above**
- Must be a **Delhi resident** (voter ID or Aadhaar with Delhi address)
- Not a current or former government employee receiving pension
- Not an income taxpayer
- Income of the family should not exceed the prescribed limit

## How to Register

The registration was initially carried out through:
1. **Door-to-door registration** by AAP volunteers
2. **Registration camps** at block/ward offices
3. **Online registration** via the AAP app or Delhi government portal

**Current status:** Check the latest updates at delhi.gov.in or the AAP official channels for active registration windows and app-based registration.

**Documents required:**
- Aadhaar card
- Delhi voter ID card (Delhi address proof)
- Bank account in the woman's own name (Aadhaar-linked for DBT)

## Check Registration and Payment Status

- Check at delhi.gov.in under welfare schemes
- Contact your local ward councillor office for status
- Bank account statement will show the monthly credit

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Has the scheme started payments?
The scheme's rollout has been linked to political and administrative developments in Delhi. For the latest status, check delhi.gov.in or call the Delhi CM helpline at 1076.

### Can women from outside Delhi apply?
No — only women with permanent residence in Delhi (with Delhi voter ID or Aadhaar with Delhi address) are eligible.

### Is there an income limit?
Exact income limits are announced by the Delhi government at the time of implementation. Government employees and income taxpayers are excluded.

### Will this reduce my MGNREGS or other benefits?
No — Mahila Samman Yojana is a separate scheme and does not affect other central or state government benefits you receive.
