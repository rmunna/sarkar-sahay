## What is the KCR Kit Scheme?

**KCR Kit** (named after K. Chandrashekar Rao) is Telangana government's maternity benefit scheme that provides **16 essential items free of cost** to pregnant women who deliver at **government hospitals**. The kit supports newborns and their mothers with essential supplies for the first months of life.

**Additional cash incentive:** ₹12,000 for girl baby deliveries and ₹13,000 for boy baby deliveries (paid via cheque/DBT).

## Key Benefits

| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Baby kit (16 items) | Free — clothes, diapers, basin, soap, towels, blanket, feeding bottles, etc. |
| Cash incentive (girl child) | ₹12,000 via cheque |
| Cash incentive (boy child) | ₹13,000 via cheque |
| Who is eligible | All women who deliver at TS government hospitals |
| No income limit | Universal — any family |

---

## Who is Eligible?

**All pregnant women who:**
- Deliver at a **Telangana government hospital** (PHC, CHC, District Hospital, VIMS, NIMS, etc.)
- Telangana resident
- **No income limit — universal** (every woman who delivers at govt hospital gets the kit)

---

## How to Get the KCR Kit

1. **Register pregnancy** with your **ASHA worker or ANM** as early as possible
2. Do all **antenatal checkups at the government hospital** (minimum 4 ANC visits recommended)
3. **Deliver at the government hospital**
4. The **kit is given at the hospital** on the day of delivery — no separate application
5. The **cash incentive cheque** is given within 15–30 days of discharge — the hospital processes it automatically

---

## Kit Contents (16 Items)

The kit typically includes:
- Clothes for newborn (vests, onesies)
- Towels and blankets
- Feeding bottle
- Diaper set
- Plastic tub/basin for baby bath
- Soap and powder
- Mosquito net
- Jute bag (kit carrier)
- Additional hygiene items for mother

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. I delivered at a private hospital. Can I still get the KCR kit?**

No. KCR Kit is available only for deliveries at **government hospitals**. Private hospital deliveries are not covered.

**2. Is the ₹13,000 / ₹12,000 cash in addition to JSY (Janani Suraksha Yojana)?**

Yes. KCR Kit cash incentive is a state scheme separate from JSY (a central scheme). You may receive both — JSY incentive and KCR Kit cash — for the same delivery.

**3. My delivery was 3 months ago at a government hospital. I didn't get the kit. What can I do?**

Contact the **Medical Superintendent** of the hospital where you delivered. Inform the Hospital Manager or ASHA worker. In genuine missed cases, the hospital may provide the kit and process the cheque retrospectively.

**4. Do I have to apply for this before delivery?**

No application is needed. Register your pregnancy with the ASHA/ANM and deliver at the government hospital — the kit and cheque are automatic.
