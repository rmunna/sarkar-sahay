# HP Medha Protsahan Yojana — Himachal Pradesh Merit Scholarship

The **HP Medha Protsahan Yojana** is a flagship scholarship scheme by the **Government of Himachal Pradesh** designed to support bright students from economically weaker families. Under this scheme, meritorious students get financial assistance of **₹1,00,000 (one lakh rupees)** to prepare for national-level competitive examinations like IIT-JEE, NEET, CLAT, and other professional entrance tests.

Many talented students in Himachal Pradesh cannot afford expensive coaching for competitive exams. This scheme bridges that gap so that financial constraints do not stop deserving students from achieving their dreams.

---

## Scheme Overview

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| **Scheme Name** | Medha Protsahan Yojana |
| **State** | Himachal Pradesh |
| **Launched By** | HP Department of Higher Education |
| **Benefit** | ₹1,00,000 financial assistance |
| **Purpose** | Coaching for competitive exams (JEE, NEET, CLAT, etc.) |
| **Mode of Application** | Online and Offline |

The scholarship amount can be used to enrol in recognised coaching institutes within or outside Himachal Pradesh. The government directly transfers the funds or pays the coaching institute on the student's behalf.

---

## Who Is Eligible?

To apply for HP Medha Protsahan Yojana, you must meet all the following conditions:

### 1. Domicile Requirement
- You must be a **permanent resident of Himachal Pradesh** (bonafide Himachali).

### 2. Educational Qualification
- For **engineering/medical stream**: You must have passed Class 12 from a recognised HP board school with at least **75% marks** (65% for reserved categories like SC/ST/OBC/IRDP).
- For **CLAT/law entrance**: You must have passed Class 12 with the prescribed minimum marks.
- Students who have already appeared for or cleared competitive exams may also be eligible depending on the year's notification.

### 3. Family Income Limit
- Your **total family annual income** must not exceed **₹2,50,000 (two lakh fifty thousand rupees)** from all sources.
- Income proof from a competent authority (Tehsildar or SDM) is required.

### 4. Age Limit
- Generally, there is no strict upper age limit beyond what the respective competitive exam prescribes. However, the student should be applying within a reasonable time after passing Class 12.

### 5. Other Conditions
- You must not be availing any other scholarship or financial assistance for coaching from the state or central government for the same purpose.
- Only **one child per family** can avail this benefit at a time (check the latest notification for updates).

---

## Scholarship Amount and How It Is Used

The scheme provides a **one-time financial assistance of ₹1,00,000** per selected student.

- The amount is meant exclusively for **coaching/tuition fees** at a recognised coaching institute.
- Funds may be released in instalments — typically in two parts — to the student's bank account or directly to the coaching institute.
- The student must join coaching within the stipulated time after selection.

> **Note:** The scholarship is for coaching fees only. Hostel, travel, and personal expenses are generally not covered unless specified in that year's guidelines.

## How to Apply — Step by Step

### Online Application

1. **Visit the official portal**: Go to the HP Department of Higher Education website at [education.hp.gov.in](https://education.hp.gov.in) or the ePASS scholarship portal at [hpepass.cgg.gov.in](https://hpepass.cgg.gov.in).
2. **Find the scheme**: Look for "Medha Protsahan Yojana" under scholarships or schemes section.
3. **Register**: Create an account using your Aadhaar number, mobile number, and email.
4. **Fill the application form**: Enter personal details, educational qualifications, family income, and coaching institute details.
5. **Upload documents**: Scan and upload all required documents (listed below).
6. **Submit**: Review the form carefully and submit. Save/print the acknowledgement slip.

### Offline Application

1. **Collect the form**: Get the application form from the **Director of Higher Education, Shimla** or download it from the official website.
2. **Fill the form**: Complete all fields in block letters. Attach self-attested photocopies of required documents.
3. **Submit**: Send or hand-deliver the completed form to the **Directorate of Higher Education, Himachal Pradesh, Shimla — 171001** before the deadline.

### Important Dates
- Applications are typically invited between **January and March** each year. The exact dates vary — always check the latest official notification.

## Documents Required

Keep these documents ready before applying:

1. **Class 10 marksheet and certificate** (for age and identity proof)
2. **Class 12 marksheet** (showing required percentage)
3. **Bonafide Himachali certificate** (domicile proof)
4. **Family income certificate** (issued by Tehsildar/SDM, not older than 6 months)
5. **Caste certificate** (for SC/ST/OBC candidates, if claiming relaxation)
6. **Aadhaar card** of the student
7. **Bank passbook** (first page with account details, in student's name)
8. **Passport-size photographs** (2-3 recent)
9. **Coaching institute admission letter or fee receipt** (if already enrolled)
10. **Self-declaration** that you are not receiving any other scholarship for coaching

## Selection Process

The selection of students under HP Medha Protsahan Yojana follows a **merit-based process**:

1. **Merit list preparation**: A merit list is prepared based on Class 12 marks. Separate lists may be made for medical, engineering, and law streams.
2. **Category-wise seats**: A fixed number of seats are allocated for General, SC, ST, OBC, and other reserved categories.
3. **Verification**: Shortlisted candidates' documents are verified by the Directorate of Higher Education.
4. **Final selection**: After verification, the final list of beneficiaries is published on the official website and communicated to students.
5. **Fund release**: Selected students receive the scholarship amount after joining a recognised coaching institute.

> Typically, around **350–500 students** are selected each year, but this number can change based on the annual budget.

---

## Renewal and Continuation

- HP Medha Protsahan Yojana is a **one-time grant**, not a recurring scholarship. There is no renewal process.
- If you are selected and receive the amount, you are expected to complete the coaching programme.
- If a student drops out of coaching midway without a valid reason, the government may recover the amount.
- Students who do not get selected in one year can re-apply the next year if they still meet eligibility criteria and the scheme is open.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Can I use this scholarship for coaching outside Himachal Pradesh?
Yes, you can join a recognised coaching institute anywhere in India. The scheme does not restrict you to HP-based institutes only.

### 2. Is this scholarship available for students preparing for UPSC or state PCS exams?
The scheme primarily targets students preparing for entrance exams like JEE, NEET, and CLAT after Class 12. For UPSC/PCS coaching, Himachal Pradesh has separate schemes — check the latest state notifications.

### 3. What if my family income is slightly above ₹2.5 lakh?
Unfortunately, the income limit is strict. If your family income exceeds ₹2,50,000 per annum, you will not be eligible. Make sure the income certificate reflects accurate figures from the competent authority.

### 4. Can both siblings from the same family apply?
Generally, only one student per family can receive the benefit at a time. If one sibling has already availed the scheme, the other may need to wait or check the latest rules.

### 5. What happens if I don't get admission in any competitive exam after coaching?
The scholarship is for coaching, not for guaranteed admission. You are not required to clear the exam to keep the scholarship. However, you must complete the coaching programme.

### 6. Is there a separate quota for girl students?
The scheme encourages participation of girl students. Some years, a certain percentage of seats are reserved for female candidates. Check the official notification for the current year's reservation policy.

### 7. How long does it take to receive the money after selection?
Typically, the funds are released within **1–3 months** after the final selection list is published and document verification is complete.
