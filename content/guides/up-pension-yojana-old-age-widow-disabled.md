## What is UP Pension Yojana (SSPY)?

The Uttar Pradesh government runs an **Integrated Social Security Pension Scheme** under the **SSPY (Social Security Pension Yojana)** umbrella. This provides monthly financial assistance to three vulnerable categories:

1. **Vridha Pension (Old Age Pension)** — For senior citizens aged 60+
2. **Vidhwa Pension (Widow Pension)** — For destitute women who have lost their husbands
3. **Divyang Pension (Disability Pension)** — For persons with 40%+ disability

All pensions are managed through a single portal — **sspy-up.gov.in** — making it easy to apply and track status online.

## Pension Amounts

| Pension Type | Monthly Amount | Funding |
|-------------|---------------|---------|
| Old Age Pension (60-79 years) | ₹500/month | State government |
| Old Age Pension (80+ years) | ₹500/month (state) + ₹500/month (IGNOAPS central) = ₹1,000/month | State + Central |
| Widow Pension | ₹500/month | State government |
| Disability Pension | ₹500/month | State government |

> **Note:** The central government contributes under IGNOAPS (Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme) for BPL beneficiaries aged 80+.

## Eligibility Criteria

### Old Age Pension (Vridha Pension)
- **Age:** 60 years or above
- **Domicile:** Permanent resident of UP
- **Income:** BPL or annual income below ₹46,080 (rural) / ₹56,460 (urban) from all sources
- **Not receiving** any other government pension

### Widow Pension (Vidhwa / Nirashrit Mahila Pension)
- **Age:** 18 to 60 years (after 60, eligible for old age pension)
- **Marital status:** Husband deceased (death certificate required)
- **Domicile:** Permanent resident of UP
- **Income:** BPL or annual income below ₹2 lakh
- **Not remarried**
- **Not receiving** any other government pension

### Disability Pension (Divyang Pension)
- **Disability:** 40% or more disability (as certified by a government medical board)
- **Age:** 18 to 59 years (after 60, eligible for old age pension)
- **Domicile:** Permanent resident of UP
- **Income:** BPL or annual income below ₹46,080 (rural) / ₹56,460 (urban)
- **Not receiving** any other government pension

## Documents Required

**Common for all pension types:**
- Aadhaar Card (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- Bank account passbook (Aadhaar-linked)
- Passport-size photograph
- Income certificate
- BPL ration card (if applicable)
- Age proof (Aadhaar / birth certificate / voter ID)

**Additional for Widow Pension:**
- Husband's death certificate
- Self-declaration of not being remarried

**Additional for Disability Pension:**
- Disability certificate (40%+ disability, issued by government hospital / Chief Medical Officer)
- UDID (Unique Disability ID) card, if available

## How to Apply Online — Step-by-Step

### Online Application via SSPY Portal

1. **Visit SSPY portal:** Go to [sspy-up.gov.in](https://sspy-up.gov.in)
2. **Select pension type:** Click on the relevant pension category:
   - "वृद्धावस्था पेंशन" for Old Age
   - "निराश्रित महिला पेंशन" for Widow
   - "दिव्यांग पेंशन" for Disability
3. **Click "ऑनलाइन आवेदन करें"** (Apply Online)
4. **Select district and tehsil:** Choose your district, tehsil, and town/village
5. **Fill personal details:** Enter name, date of birth, gender, Aadhaar number, mobile number, and bank account details
6. **Upload photograph:** Upload a recent passport-size photo (JPEG, under 200 KB)
7. **Upload documents:** Upload income certificate, age proof, and category-specific documents
8. **Submit application:** Click Submit and note your **registration number**
9. **Print acknowledgement:** Download and print the application acknowledgement

### Offline Application

1. Visit your **Block Development Office (BDO)** or **Sub-District Magistrate (SDM) office**
2. Collect the pension application form
3. Fill in details and attach required documents
4. Get the form **attested by the village head (Gram Pradhan)** or ward member
5. Submit at the BDO / SDM office
6. Collect the acknowledgement receipt

### Via CSC (Jan Seva Kendra)

1. Visit your nearest **Common Service Centre (CSC)**
2. The CSC operator will help fill and submit your application online
3. Nominal service charge of ₹20-30 may apply

## How to Check Pension Status

1. Go to [sspy-up.gov.in](https://sspy-up.gov.in)
2. Click on the relevant pension category
3. Click on **"आवेदन की स्थिति"** (Application Status)
4. Enter your **registration number** or **Aadhaar number**
5. View your application status, approval date, and payment history

## How to Check Pension Beneficiary List

1. Go to [sspy-up.gov.in](https://sspy-up.gov.in)
2. Click on the relevant pension category
3. Click on **"पेंशनर सूची"** (Pensioner List)
4. Select your **district → tehsil → village/ward**
5. View the complete list of pension beneficiaries in your area

## Processing Time

| Stage | Timeline |
|-------|----------|
| Application submission | Immediate (online) |
| Village/ward level verification | 15-30 days |
| SDM/BDO approval | 30-60 days |
| First pension payment | Within 3 months of approval |
| Subsequent payments | Quarterly (every 3 months) |

## Fees

There is **no application fee** for pension under SSPY. The service is completely free.

## Payment Method

- Pension is **credited quarterly** (every 3 months) directly to the beneficiary's Aadhaar-linked bank account via DBT
- For ₹500/month pension, the quarterly payment is **₹1,500**
- Payment schedule: April, July, October, January (approximate)
- Ensure your bank account is active and linked with Aadhaar (see our [Aadhaar linking guide](/guide/aadhaar-linking-guide))

## Important Tips

1. **Link Aadhaar to bank account** — This is mandatory for receiving pension via DBT. Visit your bank if not already done.
2. **Apply early** — There is a waiting list in many districts. The sooner you apply, the sooner you get approved.
3. **Keep documents ready** — Have your income certificate and age proof ready before applying. Income certificate must be issued within the last 6 months.
4. **Update if details change** — If you change your bank account or address, update it on the SSPY portal immediately.
5. **Life certificate (Jeevan Praman)** — Pensioners need to submit an annual life certificate to continue receiving pension. This can be done online via Jeevan Pramaan portal or at the bank.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. How much pension do senior citizens get in UP?

Senior citizens aged 60-79 get **₹500 per month**. Those aged 80+ can get up to **₹1,000 per month** (₹500 state + ₹500 central IGNOAPS).

### Q2. Is pension paid monthly or quarterly?

The pension is **paid quarterly** (every 3 months). So for ₹500/month, you receive ₹1,500 every quarter directly in your bank account.

### Q3. Can I apply for pension if I have agricultural land?

Yes, having agricultural land does not disqualify you, as long as your **total annual income** is within the prescribed limit (below ₹46,080 for rural areas).

### Q4. My pension has stopped. What should I do?

Common reasons: bank account inactive, Aadhaar not linked, life certificate not submitted, or applicant turned 60 (widow/disability pensioners shift to old age pension). Check your status on SSPY portal and contact your BDO office.

### Q5. Can a widow above 60 years get both widow pension and old age pension?

No, you can get only **one pension at a time**. Widows above 60 are automatically shifted to old age pension. The pension amount remains the same.

### Q6. How do I submit the annual life certificate?

You can submit it through:
- **Online:** Jeevan Pramaan portal using Aadhaar biometric
- **Bank visit:** Submit at your bank branch with Aadhaar
- **CSC center:** Visit a Common Service Centre for biometric submission

### Q7. What if I don't have a disability certificate?

The disability certificate is **mandatory** for Divyang Pension. Get it issued from the **Chief Medical Officer (CMO)** office or a government hospital in your district. It requires a medical board examination.
