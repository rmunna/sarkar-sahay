# How to Check and Download CBSE, ICSE & State Board Exam Results Online

Every year, crores of students in India appear for Class 10th and 12th board exams conducted by CBSE, ICSE, and various state boards. Once the exams are over, the biggest question is — **how to check the result online?**

This guide covers **step-by-step instructions** for checking results of all major boards, downloading your marksheet, using DigiLocker, applying for re-evaluation, and getting a duplicate marksheet if needed.

---

## CBSE Board Result — How to Check Online

The Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) declares results for Class 10 and Class 12 on its official websites.

### Official Websites for CBSE Results

- **cbseresults.nic.in** — Primary result portal
- **results.cbse.nic.in** — Alternate result portal
- **DigiLocker** — For digital marksheet and certificates

### How to Check CBSE Result by Roll Number

1. Visit [cbseresults.nic.in](https://cbseresults.nic.in)
2. Click on **"Class 10 Result"** or **"Class 12 Result"**
3. Enter your **Roll Number**, **School Number**, and **Admit Card ID**
4. Enter the security code (captcha)
5. Click **"Submit"**
6. Your result will appear on screen — note down your marks or take a screenshot

### How to Check CBSE Result by Name

CBSE does not officially provide a "search by name" option on its main portal. However, you can try:

- **UMANG App** — Download the app, go to CBSE section, and search using your details
- **DigiLocker** — Login with your Aadhaar-linked mobile number to access your result

### How to Download CBSE Marksheet from DigiLocker

1. Visit [digilocker.gov.in](https://digilocker.gov.in) or download the DigiLocker app
2. Sign up or login using your **Aadhaar-linked mobile number**
3. Go to **"Issued Documents"** section
4. Search for **CBSE** under the education category
5. Enter your **Roll Number** and **Year of Passing**
6. Your digital marksheet and passing certificate will appear
7. Click **"Download"** to save the PDF

> **Tip:** DigiLocker marksheets are legally valid and accepted everywhere — for college admissions, government jobs, and more.

## ICSE / ISC Board Result — How to Check Online

The Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations (CISCE) conducts ICSE (Class 10) and ISC (Class 12) exams.

### Official Website

- **cisce.org** — The only official portal for ICSE and ISC results

### How to Check ICSE / ISC Result

1. Visit [cisce.org](https://cisce.org)
2. Click on **"Results"** on the homepage
3. Select your exam — **ICSE** or **ISC**
4. Enter your **Unique ID**, **Index Number**, and **captcha**
5. Click **"Show Result"**
6. Your result with subject-wise marks will be displayed

### How to Check ICSE Result by Name

CISCE does not allow checking results by name. You must have your **Unique ID** and **Index Number** from your admit card.

### Downloading ICSE/ISC Marksheet

- Digital marksheets are available through the **CISCE website** after results are declared
- Physical marksheets are distributed through the school
- Digital marksheets are also available on **DigiLocker**

## State Board Results — Top 5 Boards

### 1. UP Board Result (Uttar Pradesh)

**Official Website:** [upresults.nic.in](https://upresults.nic.in)

**Steps to check:**
1. Visit upresults.nic.in
2. Click on **"Board Result 10th"** or **"Board Result 12th"**
3. Enter your **Roll Number**
4. Click **"Submit"**
5. Your result will be displayed with marks in each subject

### 2. Maharashtra Board Result (SSC / HSC)

**Official Website:** [mahresult.nic.in](https://mahresult.nic.in)

**Steps to check:**
1. Visit mahresult.nic.in
2. Select **SSC (Class 10)** or **HSC (Class 12)** result
3. Enter your **Seat Number** and **Mother's First Name**
4. Click **"View Result"**
5. Download or print your result

### 3. Bihar Board Result (BSEB)

**Official Website:** [biharboardonline.bihar.gov.in](https://biharboardonline.bihar.gov.in)

**Steps to check:**
1. Visit biharboardonline.bihar.gov.in
2. Click on **"Result"** section
3. Select **Matric (10th)** or **Intermediate (12th)**
4. Enter your **Roll Number** and **Roll Code**
5. Click **"Search"**
6. Your result with marks and division will appear

### 4. MP Board Result (Madhya Pradesh)

**Official Website:** [mpbse.nic.in](https://mpbse.nic.in)

**Steps to check:**
1. Visit mpbse.nic.in
2. Click on **"Result"** link
3. Select Class 10 or Class 12
4. Enter your **Roll Number**
5. Click **"Get Result"**
6. View and download your result

### 5. Rajasthan Board Result (RBSE)

**Official Website:** [rajresults.nic.in](https://rajresults.nic.in)

**Steps to check:**
1. Visit rajresults.nic.in
2. Select your exam (10th or 12th)
3. Enter your **Roll Number**
4. Click **"Submit"**
5. Your result will be shown on screen

## How to Check Board Result by Name (Any Board)

Most boards **do not officially support** checking results by name for privacy reasons. However, here are some alternatives:

- **Third-party websites** like IndiaResults.com or ExamResults.net sometimes allow name-based search
- **DigiLocker** — If your board has partnered with DigiLocker, login and check
- **SMS Service** — Some boards provide SMS-based result checking (details are announced at result time)
- **UMANG App** — Supports result checking for CBSE and some state boards

> **Important:** Always verify results from the official board website. Third-party sites may show incorrect data.

## How to Download Marksheet Online

### Using the Board's Official Website
1. Check your result on the official website (steps given above)
2. Once result is displayed, look for a **"Download"** or **"Print"** button
3. Save the page as PDF or take a printout
4. Note: This is usually a provisional marksheet — the original is sent through your school

### Using DigiLocker
DigiLocker provides **legally valid digital marksheets** for the following boards:
- CBSE
- ICSE / ISC (CISCE)
- UP Board
- Maharashtra Board
- And many other state boards

**Steps:**
1. Go to [digilocker.gov.in](https://digilocker.gov.in)
2. Login or create an account
3. Go to **"Issued Documents"** → **"Education"**
4. Select your board
5. Enter roll number and year
6. Download your marksheet as PDF

## How to Apply for Re-evaluation / Rechecking

If you are not satisfied with your marks, most boards allow you to apply for **re-evaluation** (answer sheet re-checking) or **re-totalling** (recounting of marks).

### CBSE Re-evaluation Process
1. Visit [cbse.gov.in](https://cbse.gov.in)
2. Go to the **"Verification of Marks / Revaluation"** section
3. Apply online within **the deadline** (usually 15-20 days after result)
4. Pay the fee online (per subject)
5. Results of re-evaluation are communicated later

### General Steps for Other Boards
1. Visit your board's official website
2. Look for **"Re-evaluation"** or **"Rechecking"** link (available after results)
3. Fill the application form online
4. Pay the required fee (usually ₹500–₹1000 per subject)
5. Submit within the deadline
6. Revised marks (if any) will be updated on the portal

> **Note:** Re-evaluation can result in marks going up or down. Apply only if you are sure about a discrepancy.

## Compartment Exam Results

If a student fails in one or two subjects, most boards allow a **compartment exam** (also called supplementary or improvement exam).

### How to Check Compartment Result
1. Visit the same official website used for regular results
2. Look for **"Compartment Result"** or **"Supplementary Result"** link
3. Enter your roll number and other details
4. View your result

### Key Points
- CBSE compartment exams are usually held in **July-August**
- State boards have their own schedule — check the board website
- If you pass the compartment exam, your marksheet is updated accordingly
- You can apply for compartment through your school

## How to Get a Duplicate Marksheet

Lost your original marksheet? Here's how to get a duplicate copy:

### CBSE Duplicate Marksheet
1. Visit [cbse.gov.in](https://cbse.gov.in)
2. Go to **"Duplicate Documents"** section
3. Fill the online application form
4. Upload required documents — FIR copy (for lost marksheet), passport photo, ID proof
5. Pay the fee online
6. The duplicate marksheet will be dispatched to your address

### State Boards — General Process
1. Visit your board's official website
2. Look for **"Duplicate Certificate"** or **"Migration Certificate"** section
3. Submit an application with:
   - FIR or affidavit for lost document
   - Photocopy of the original (if available)
   - Passport-size photos
   - ID proof
4. Pay the applicable fee
5. Collect the duplicate from the board office or receive it by post

### Via DigiLocker
If your marksheet is available on DigiLocker, you don't need a physical duplicate — the **digital copy is legally valid** and can be used for all purposes.

## Important Tips for Students and Parents

- **Save your roll number and admit card** — You will need these to check results
- **Check results only on official websites** — Avoid fake or phishing sites
- **Download and save your marksheet** as soon as results are declared
- **Register on DigiLocker** before results — It reduces server load issues
- **Don't panic if the website is slow** — Result day sees heavy traffic. Try after some time or use the SMS/app method
- **Keep physical copies safe** — Store originals in a secure place

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Can I check my board result without a roll number?
**A:** Most boards require a roll number. However, you can try DigiLocker (using Aadhaar-linked mobile) or contact your school to get your roll number.

### Q2: Is the DigiLocker marksheet valid for college admission?
**A:** Yes. DigiLocker documents are legally valid under the IT Act, 2000. They are accepted by all colleges, universities, and government departments.

### Q3: How long does re-evaluation take?
**A:** Usually 30-45 days after applying. CBSE and most state boards update the result on their portal once re-evaluation is done.

### Q4: Can I apply for re-evaluation in all subjects?
**A:** It depends on the board. CBSE allows re-evaluation for all subjects. Some state boards have restrictions — check your board's rules.

### Q5: What if I fail in more than 2 subjects?
**A:** Most boards allow compartment exams only for 1-2 subjects. If you fail in more, you may need to repeat the year and appear as a regular or private candidate.

### Q6: How to check old board results (previous years)?
**A:** Visit the board's official website and look for **"Previous Year Results"** or **"Archive"** section. DigiLocker also stores results from past years if your board has uploaded them.

### Q7: Is there any mobile app to check board results?
**A:** Yes. You can use the **UMANG App** (for CBSE and some state boards), **DigiLocker App**, or your specific board's official app if available.
