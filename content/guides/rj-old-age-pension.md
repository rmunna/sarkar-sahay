## What is Rajasthan CM Old Age Pension Scheme?

The Rajasthan Chief Minister Old Age Pension Scheme (Mukhyamantri Vridhavastha Samman Pension Yojana) provides **monthly pension** to senior citizens who are destitute or from economically weaker families. It is managed by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department.

### Pension Amount

| Age & Category | Monthly Pension |
|----------------|----------------|
| Women 55+ / Men 58+ | ₹750 |
| Age 75 and above | ₹1,000 |

> Rajasthan has a **lower age threshold** than most states — women from 55 and men from 58 years.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Women:** 55 years or above
- **Men:** 58 years or above
- Must be a **resident of Rajasthan**
- **Annual family income** must not exceed ₹48,000
- Must not be receiving any other government pension
- Must not be an income tax payer
- Must have **Jan Aadhaar** registration

## Documents Required

- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
- Jan Aadhaar card
- Age proof (birth certificate, voter ID, or school records)
- Income certificate (from Tehsildar or Patwari)
- Bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked)
- Passport-size photograph
- BPL ration card (if available)

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Online Application

1. **Visit** [Rajasthan SSP portal](https://rajssp.rajasthan.gov.in) or [SSP portal](https://ssp.rajasthan.gov.in)
2. **Login** using Jan Aadhaar or SSO ID
3. **Select** Old Age Pension (Vridhavastha Pension)
4. **Fill** application form with personal, age, income, and bank details
5. **Upload** documents
6. **Submit** and save the application number
7. **Track** status on the portal

### Through E-Mitra

1. **Visit** the nearest E-Mitra kiosk
2. **Carry** all documents
3. **E-Mitra operator** fills and submits the application
4. **Pay** nominal E-Mitra service charge (if any)
5. **Collect** application receipt

### Offline Application

1. **Visit** the Sub-Divisional Officer (SDO) or Block Development Office
2. **Collect** pension application form
3. **Fill** and attach all documents
4. **Submit** to the SDO office
5. **Collect** acknowledgement

## Processing Time

- Verification: **1-2 months**
- Approval: **2-3 months**
- Monthly pension starts after approval via DBT

## Fees

**No fees** for the application. E-Mitra may charge a nominal service fee (₹20-50).

## Important Tips

1. **Get Jan Aadhaar** — It is mandatory for all Rajasthan government schemes
2. **Submit life certificate annually** — Via [Jeevan Pramaan](https://jeevanpramaan.gov.in)
3. **Keep bank account active** — Pension is via DBT
4. **Lower age for women** — Women can apply from age 55 itself
5. **Check for health coverage** — Register for [Chiranjeevi health insurance](/guide/rj-chiranjeevi-health) for medical coverage

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. What is the minimum age for old age pension in Rajasthan?
**55 years for women** and **58 years for men**.

### Q2. Can I get both old age and widow pension?
No. You can receive only **one** social security pension at a time.

### Q3. Is Jan Aadhaar mandatory?
Yes. Jan Aadhaar is required for all Rajasthan social security schemes.

### Q4. How much pension do I get after 75?
₹1,000 per month for beneficiaries aged **75 and above**.

### Q5. How do I check pension status?
Visit [rajssp.rajasthan.gov.in](https://rajssp.rajasthan.gov.in) and check with your application number or Jan Aadhaar ID.
