## Why Does PF Transfer Online Fail?

Transferring your PF balance online through the EPFO Unified Portal should be straightforward — but in practice, many members face rejections, errors, and claims stuck for months. Common reasons include mismatched UAN details, incomplete KYC, employer inaction, and incorrect member IDs.

This guide covers **every major PF transfer issue** and how to fix it step by step.

> 📌 Before troubleshooting, make sure you've followed the correct [EPF transfer process for job changes](/guides/epf-transfer-online-job-change).

---

## 1. UAN Mismatch Between Old and New Employer

### The Problem

Your old employer registered you under one UAN while your new employer created a different UAN. The EPFO portal won't let you transfer between two different UANs because it can't verify you're the same person.

### How to Fix

1. **Identify both UANs** — Check your old and new salary slips or log in to the [EPFO Member Portal](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in/) with each UAN
2. **Verify which UAN has approved KYC** — The UAN with Aadhaar, PAN, and bank KYC approved is your primary UAN
3. **Ask your new employer** to link your existing (old) UAN instead of creating a new one — this is the ideal fix
4. **If both UANs already exist**, you need to merge them (see [Section 7: How to Merge Multiple UANs](#7-how-to-merge-multiple-uans) below)

> ⚠️ Never try to transfer PF between two UANs without merging first — the claim will be rejected.

## 2. KYC Not Approved on EPFO Portal

### The Problem

PF transfer requires approved KYC (Aadhaar, PAN, and bank account) on your UAN. If KYC shows "Pending" or "Rejected," the transfer claim won't go through.

### How to Fix

1. **Log in** to [EPFO Member Portal](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in/) → **Manage** → **KYC**
2. Check the status of Aadhaar, PAN, and Bank Account — all three should show **"Verified"**
3. If KYC is **Pending**:
   - Your employer needs to approve it from their employer portal
   - Contact your HR/payroll team and ask them to approve pending KYC documents
   - Approval usually takes 2–5 working days after employer action
4. If KYC is **Rejected**:
   - Check the rejection reason (name mismatch, wrong document number, etc.)
   - Correct the details and resubmit
   - **Name mismatch** is the #1 reason — ensure your name on Aadhaar, PAN, and EPF records is identical (spelling, initials, order)
5. If your employer is unresponsive, you can raise a grievance on [EPFiGMS](https://epfigms.gov.in/)

> 💡 If your UAN activation itself failed, check our guide on [EPFO UAN activation issues](/guides/epfo-uan-activation-failed-fix).

## 3. Employer Not Approving PF Transfer Request

### The Problem

After you submit a transfer claim (Form 13) on the portal, **both your old and new employers** must approve it digitally. If either employer doesn't act, the claim stays pending indefinitely.

### How to Fix

1. **Check claim status**: Login → **Online Services** → **Track Claim Status** to see which employer hasn't approved
2. **Contact your current employer's HR** — they should approve within 3–5 days of submission
3. **Contact your previous employer's HR** — if the company still exists, email or call them directly
4. **If your old employer has shut down or is unresponsive**:
   - Select **"Previous employer is not available"** option while filing the claim (if available)
   - Alternatively, submit the transfer request through your **current employer only** (the portal allows this for certain EPFO regional offices)
   - File a complaint on [EPFiGMS](https://epfigms.gov.in/) mentioning the employer's non-cooperation
5. **If the company has been deregistered from EPFO**, visit the nearest EPFO regional office with:
   - Your UAN and member ID details
   - A written request for transfer
   - ID proof (Aadhaar)

> ⚡ **Pro tip**: When filing the claim, choose **"Current Employer"** as the attestation authority if your old employer is non-responsive — this routes the approval only through your current employer.

## 4. Wrong Member ID in Transfer Claim

### The Problem

Your member ID format is `STATE/OFFICE_CODE/ESTABLISHMENT_CODE/MEMBER_EXTENSION` (e.g., `MH/BAN/12345/67890`). If any part of this ID is wrong — wrong establishment code, wrong extension number, or wrong regional office code — the transfer claim gets rejected.

### How to Fix

1. **Find your correct member ID**:
   - Check your salary slip from the relevant employer
   - Log in to the EPFO portal → **View** → **Service History** to see all linked member IDs
   - Check your EPF passbook at [passbook.epfindia.gov.in](https://passbook.epfindia.gov.in/)
2. **If the member ID shown on the portal is wrong**:
   - Ask your employer (old or current) to correct it through the employer portal
   - The employer can update member ID details via the **Establishment Login**
3. **Re-submit the transfer claim** with the correct member ID after correction
4. If your old employer cannot correct it, raise a grievance on EPFiGMS with the correct details and supporting documents (salary slips as proof)

## 5. Form 13 Rejection — Common Reasons and Fixes

### The Problem

Form 13 (online transfer claim) gets rejected by EPFO with a specific rejection reason. Here are the most common ones:

| Rejection Reason | Fix |
|---|---|
| **Name mismatch** between old and new account | Get name corrected via employer joint declaration or submit name change request |
| **Date of birth mismatch** | Update DOB through employer using joint declaration form |
| **Date of joining/exit not updated** | Ask previous employer to update exit date in portal |
| **KYC not verified** | Complete and get KYC approved (see Section 2) |
| **Contribution not received** | Wait for employer to deposit all dues; check passbook for latest entry |
| **Establishment not e-enabled** | The old employer's EPFO office hasn't enabled digital transfers — file complaint on EPFiGMS |
| **Invalid member ID** | Correct member ID (see Section 4) |
| **Already transferred** | Check passbook — the amount may already be in your current account |

### General Fix Steps

1. Read the **exact rejection reason** in the claim status page
2. Address the specific issue
3. Wait 7 days after the correction
4. **Re-submit** the Form 13 transfer claim

> 📖 For detailed claim rejection reasons, see our [EPF claim rejection guide](/guides/epf-claim-rejected-reasons-fix).

## 6. PF Transfer Claim Stuck at "Under Process"

### The Problem

You submitted the transfer claim weeks (or months) ago, both employers approved it, but the status still shows **"Under Process"** with no movement.

### How to Fix

1. **Wait at least 20 working days** — EPFO officially takes 15–20 working days to process transfers after all approvals
2. **Check claim status** regularly at **Online Services** → **Track Claim Status**
3. **If stuck beyond 30 days**:
   - File a complaint on [EPFiGMS](https://epfigms.gov.in/) with your claim reference number
   - Call the **EPFO helpline**: **1800-118-005** (toll-free) or **14470**
   - Tweet to [@aboraboraborabo](https://twitter.com/socialaborabora) (EPFO's official X handle — they are sometimes responsive)
4. **If stuck beyond 60 days**:
   - Visit the EPFO regional office that handles your old employer's account
   - Carry printouts of your claim status, UAN card, and Aadhaar
   - Request to speak with the Accounts Officer handling your case
5. **If the old EPFO office is in a different city**, file a formal written complaint via registered post to that regional office

> 🕐 Transfer claims between the **same EPFO regional office** are faster (10–15 days). Cross-office transfers between different states can take 30–60+ days.

## 7. How to Merge Multiple UANs

If you have more than one UAN (which shouldn't happen but commonly does), you **must merge them** before transferring PF.

### Steps to Merge UANs

1. **Log in** to the [EPFO Member Portal](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in/) using your **primary UAN** (the one with approved KYC and current employment)
2. Go to **Online Services** → **One Member – One EPF Account (Transfer Request)**
3. Select the member ID associated with your **other UAN** — it may appear if the Aadhaar is linked
4. Submit the merge/transfer request
5. Both employers need to attest the claim

### If the Other UAN Doesn't Appear

1. **Link Aadhaar to both UANs** — the system uses Aadhaar to auto-detect duplicate UANs
2. **Contact EPFO** via EPFiGMS or helpline to request manual UAN merge
3. **Visit the EPFO office** with both UAN details, Aadhaar, and PAN — request a manual merge
4. You may need to submit a **physical joint declaration** signed by your employer

> ⚠️ After merging, one UAN gets deactivated. All PF balance and service history moves to the primary UAN.

## 8. How to File a Complaint on EPFiGMS

[EPFiGMS](https://epfigms.gov.in/) (EPF Integrated Grievance Management System) is your official channel to escalate any PF issue.

### Steps to File a Grievance

1. Go to [epfigms.gov.in](https://epfigms.gov.in/)
2. Click **"Register Grievance"**
3. Select **"EPF Account Related"** as the grievance type
4. Choose the sub-category:
   - **"Transfer of PF Account"** for stuck or rejected transfers
   - **"UAN Related"** for UAN merge issues
5. Enter your **UAN**, **PF member ID**, mobile number, and email
6. Write a clear description of your problem — include:
   - Claim reference number
   - Date of submission
   - Which employer hasn't acted (if applicable)
   - Specific error message or rejection reason
7. Upload supporting documents (claim screenshot, passbook, ID proof)
8. Submit and **note down the grievance registration number**

### Expected Timeline

- **Acknowledgment**: Within 24–48 hours
- **Resolution**: 7–30 days depending on complexity
- **If no response in 30 days**: File another grievance referencing the old one, or escalate by writing to the Regional PF Commissioner

> 💡 You can also track your grievance status on the same portal using your registration number.

## Quick Troubleshooting Checklist

Before submitting (or re-submitting) a PF transfer claim, verify:

- [ ] Both UANs are the **same** (or merged)
- [ ] Aadhaar, PAN, and bank KYC are **verified** (not just submitted)
- [ ] Name, DOB, and gender match **exactly** across Aadhaar, PAN, and EPF records
- [ ] Previous employer has updated your **date of exit**
- [ ] You have the **correct member ID** for both old and new accounts
- [ ] Your current employer is willing to **attest** the claim promptly
- [ ] You've waited at least **7 days** after any correction before re-filing

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does PF transfer take online?

Typically **15–20 working days** after both employers approve. Cross-office transfers may take 30–60 days.

### Can I transfer PF without my old employer's approval?

Yes, in some cases. Choose **"Current Employer"** as the attestation authority while filing. If that option isn't available, file a grievance on EPFiGMS.

### What if my old company has shut down?

Visit the EPFO regional office with your UAN, Aadhaar, and any old salary slips. They can process the transfer without the employer's attestation.

### Can I transfer PF from one state to another?

Yes. The process is the same, but cross-state transfers take longer (30–60 days). File on EPFiGMS if it exceeds 45 days.

### How many times can I re-submit a rejected transfer claim?

There's no limit. Fix the rejection reason and re-submit. Each submission is treated as a fresh claim.
