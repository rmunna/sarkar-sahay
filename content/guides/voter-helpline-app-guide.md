# Voter Helpline App: How to Use for All Voter Services

The **Voter Helpline App** is the official mobile application by the Election Commission of India (ECI) that brings all voter services to your smartphone. From new voter registration to downloading e-EPIC, finding your polling booth, and filing grievances — everything is available in one app.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent platform and is not affiliated with the Election Commission of India or any government body.

**App requirements:**
- Android 5.0 or above / iOS 12.0 or above
- Internet connection required
- Camera access (for photo upload)
- Location access (for polling booth finder)

## How to Register on the App

1. Open the Voter Helpline App after installation
2. Tap **"Register"** or **"Sign Up"**
3. Enter your **mobile number**
4. Enter the **OTP** sent to your mobile
5. Fill in basic details — name, date of birth, state
6. If you already have an EPIC number, link it to your account
7. Set a password for future logins
8. Registration complete — you can now access all services

**Tip:** Use the same mobile number that is linked to your voter registration for seamless access.

## Key Services Available on the App

### 1. New Voter Registration (Form 6)

Apply for a new voter ID card directly through the app:

1. Open the app and go to **"Register as New Voter"** or **"Form 6"**
2. Fill in personal details — name, date of birth, gender, address
3. Upload your **passport-size photo** (see our [photo requirements guide](/guide/voter-id-photo-size-format))
4. Upload **age proof** — birth certificate, Class 10 marksheet, or passport
5. Upload **address proof** — Aadhaar, utility bill, bank passbook, or ration card
6. Select your **constituency** and **polling station area**
7. Declare that the information is correct
8. Submit the form
9. Track your application status under **"Track Application"**

### 2. Voter ID Corrections (Form 8)

To correct details on your existing voter ID:

1. Go to **"Correction in Entries"** or **"Form 8"**
2. Select the field you want to correct (name, age, photo, address, etc.)
3. Upload supporting documents for the correction
4. Submit and track status

### 3. Address Change / Shifting (Form 6A / Form 8A)

If you've moved to a new address:

1. Go to **"Shifting / Migration"** or the appropriate form option
2. Enter your new address details
3. Upload address proof for the new location
4. Submit the form

### 4. Deletion Request (Form 7)

To request removal of a deceased person or someone who has shifted:

1. Go to **"Objection / Deletion"** or **"Form 7"**
2. Enter details of the person to be removed
3. Provide reason — death, shifting, duplicate entry
4. Upload supporting proof
5. Submit the request

### 5. Know Your BLO / ERO

Find your Booth Level Officer and Electoral Registration Officer:

1. Go to **"Know Your BLO"** or **"Know Your Officials"**
2. Enter your EPIC number or address details
3. The app will display your BLO's name and contact number
4. You can also find your ERO and District Election Officer details

### 6. Find Your Polling Booth

Locate your assigned polling station:

1. Go to **"Polling Booth"** or **"Know Your Polling Station"**
2. Enter your EPIC number or search by name and address
3. The app shows your polling station name, address, and map location
4. On election day, use this to navigate to your booth

### 7. Download e-EPIC

Get your digital voter ID card:

1. Go to **"e-EPIC Download"** section
2. Verify your identity with OTP sent to your registered mobile number
3. Download the e-EPIC as a PDF file
4. The e-EPIC includes a QR code and is valid as official ID proof

See our [PVC Smart Card guide](/guide/voter-id-pvc-smart-card-apply) for information about getting the physical card.

### 8. File a Grievance

Report election-related issues:

1. Go to **"Grievance"** section
2. Select the complaint category
3. Describe the issue in detail
4. Attach photos or documents if available
5. Submit and note your grievance tracking number
6. Track resolution status within the app

For serious MCC violations during elections, use the **cVIGIL app** for faster response. See our [Election Commission complaint guide](/guide/election-commission-complaint).

### 9. Electoral Search

Check your name in the voter list:

1. Go to **"Search in Electoral Roll"**
2. Search by **EPIC number** or by **name and address**
3. View your complete voter details including Part Number and Serial Number
4. Verify all details are correct before elections

## Important Tips

1. **Keep your app updated** — ECI regularly adds new features and fixes; update from Play Store/App Store
2. **Link your EPIC number** to your app account for faster access to all services
3. **Save your e-EPIC offline** — download the PDF and keep it on your phone for use without internet
4. **Check your details before every election** — verify your name, photo, and address are correct in the electoral roll
5. **Use the app for tracking** — all applications (Form 6, 7, 8) can be tracked in real-time through the app

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Is the Voter Helpline App free?
Yes, the app is completely free to download and use. It is an official app developed by the Election Commission of India.

### Q2. Can I register as a new voter through the app?
Yes, you can fill and submit Form 6 for new voter registration directly through the app. You'll need to upload your photo, age proof, and address proof.

### Q3. Is the e-EPIC downloaded from the app valid as ID proof?
Yes, the e-EPIC is a valid identity document issued by ECI. It is accepted at polling stations and can also be used as general ID proof.

### Q4. Can I apply for voter ID for my family members through the app?
You can fill forms for family members, but each application requires the individual's details, documents, and their own photo. Each person should ideally register with their own mobile number.

### Q5. What should I do if the app is not working?
Ensure you have the latest version installed. Clear the app cache from your phone settings. If the issue persists, try using the web portal at [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in/) as an alternative.

### Q6. Can I change my polling booth through the app?
Your polling booth is assigned based on your registered address. If you've shifted to a new address, file a shifting request (Form 6A/8A) and your polling booth will be updated accordingly.

### Q7. How long does Form 6 take to get approved?
Form 6 applications typically take 15-30 days for verification and approval. The BLO will visit your address for verification. Check our [Form 6 rejection guide](/guide/voter-id-form-6-rejection-reasons) if your application is rejected.
