## What is Ladli Behna Yojana?

Ladli Behna Yojana is a flagship welfare scheme of the Madhya Pradesh government that provides **₹1,250 per month (₹15,000 per year)** to eligible women in the state. Launched in 2023, the scheme aims to promote women's economic independence, improve their health and nutrition, and strengthen their role in family decision-making.

The scheme has been one of the most popular state welfare programs, benefiting over 1.25 crore women across Madhya Pradesh.

## Who is Eligible?

### Eligibility Criteria
- **Women aged 21 to 60 years**
- Must be a **resident of Madhya Pradesh**
- Must be **married** (including widowed, divorced, and separated women)
- Family annual income should not exceed **₹2,50,000**
- Must have a **Samagra ID** (Madhya Pradesh family identification)
- Must not be an income tax payer
- Family should not own more than **5 acres of agricultural land**

### Who is NOT Eligible
- Unmarried women below 21 or women of any age who have never been married
- Government employees (state/central) or their spouses
- Income tax-paying families
- Women receiving ₹1,000+ per month from other government pension schemes
- Families owning a four-wheeler (tractor excluded)

## Benefits

| Component | Details |

## Documents Required

- **Samagra ID** (family and individual) — mandatory
- **Aadhaar card** linked with bank account
- **Bank account passbook** (in the applicant's name)
- **Passport-size photograph**
- **Mobile number**

**Note:** Samagra ID is the most critical document. If you don't have one, apply at [samagra.gov.in](https://samagra.gov.in) first.

## How to Apply

### Online Application
1. Visit [cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in](https://cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Apply"** or **"आवेदन करें"**
3. Enter your Samagra ID
4. Verify your details (auto-populated from Samagra database)
5. Enter bank account and mobile number
6. Upload photograph
7. Submit the application
8. Note the application reference number

### Offline Application (Through Camps)
1. Attend **Ladli Behna enrollment camps** organized at ward/panchayat level
2. Carry Aadhaar card, Samagra ID, and bank passbook
3. Camp officials fill the application form and verify documents
4. Biometric/photo verification done on the spot
5. Acknowledgment slip provided

### Through Gram Panchayat / Ward Office
1. Visit your local Gram Panchayat (rural) or Ward office (urban)
2. Request the Ladli Behna application form
3. Fill in details with assistance from staff
4. Submit with document copies
5. Collect acknowledgment

## Application Status Check

1. Visit [cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in](https://cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Application Status"**
3. Enter your Samagra ID or application number
4. View status: pending, approved, rejected, or payment initiated

## Verification and Approval

1. Application verified against Samagra database
2. Income and land ownership cross-checked
3. Aadhaar-bank linking verified
4. Gram Panchayat/Ward level approval
5. District-level final approval
6. Payment initiated from the next payment cycle

## Important Tips

- **Get your Samagra ID first** — without this, you cannot apply; visit [samagra.gov.in](https://samagra.gov.in) or your Gram Panchayat
- **Aadhaar must match Samagra** — name, date of birth, and address should match across both IDs
- **Bank account in your own name** — joint accounts may cause DBT failure
- **Update Samagra if details are wrong** — any mismatch will delay or reject your application
- **Attend camps for fastest processing** — enrollment camps have on-the-spot verification and approval

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can widows and divorced women apply?
Yes, widowed, divorced, and separated women are eligible. They are classified as "married" (previously married) under the scheme guidelines.

### What is Samagra ID and how to get it?
Samagra ID is Madhya Pradesh's family and individual identification system. Apply at [samagra.gov.in](https://samagra.gov.in) or at your Gram Panchayat/Ward office. You'll get a family ID (8 digits) and individual ID (9 digits).

### Can I receive Ladli Behna and PM-KISAN both?
Yes, Ladli Behna is a state scheme and PM-KISAN is a central scheme. Eligible women can receive both.

### My application was rejected. What should I do?
Check the rejection reason on the portal. Common reasons: incomplete Samagra, Aadhaar mismatch, income exceeding limit, or family member being a government employee. Fix the issue and reapply.

### Will the monthly amount increase?
The government has already increased it from ₹1,000 to ₹1,250. Further increases may happen based on government decisions and budget allocation.

### Is there a similar scheme in Chhattisgarh?
Yes, Chhattisgarh has the Mahtari Vandan Yojana providing ₹1,000/month to married women. See our [Mahtari Vandan guide](/guide/chhattisgarh-mahtari-vandan) for details.
