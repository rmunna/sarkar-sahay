## What is Jagananna Vidya Deevena?

Jagananna Vidya Deevena is a flagship education scheme of the Andhra Pradesh government that provides **complete fee reimbursement** (tuition fees + examination fees) to students from economically weaker families. The scheme ensures that no student in AP drops out of higher education due to financial constraints.

The fee reimbursement amount is credited **directly to the mother's bank account**, making it one of the most mother-centric welfare schemes in the country.

## Who is Eligible?

To qualify for Jagananna Vidya Deevena, you must meet **all** of the following criteria:

- **Domicile:** Must be a resident of Andhra Pradesh
- **Family income:** Annual family income must be **₹2.5 lakh or below**
- **Enrollment:** Must be enrolled in a recognized institution in AP (government, aided, or private)
- **Course type:** Must be pursuing ITI, polytechnic, degree, postgraduation, or professional courses (engineering, medicine, law, MBA, MCA, etc.)
- **Attendance:** Must maintain minimum required attendance as per university norms
- **No arrears/backlogs:** Students with excessive backlogs may be ineligible (varies by institution)
- **Aadhaar-linked bank account:** Mother's bank account must be linked to Aadhaar

### Who is NOT Eligible?

- Students whose family income exceeds ₹2.5 lakh per annum
- Students enrolled in institutions outside Andhra Pradesh
- Students in unrecognized or non-affiliated institutions
- Students already receiving full fee waiver from another government scheme

## What Expenses Are Covered?

The scheme covers:

| Component | Covered? |
|-----------|----------|
| Tuition fees | ✅ Yes — 100% |
| Examination fees | ✅ Yes — 100% |
| Special fees | ✅ Yes (as per GO norms) |
| Hostel fees | ❌ No (covered under Vasathi Deevena) |
| Mess/food charges | ❌ No |

> **Note:** For hostel and mess expenses, students can apply separately under the **Jagananna Vasathi Deevena** scheme, which provides ₹10,000–₹20,000 per year depending on the course.

## How to Apply for Jagananna Vidya Deevena

### Step 1: Check Your Eligibility on Jnanabhumi

1. Visit [jnanabhumi.ap.gov.in](https://jnanabhumi.ap.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Vidya Deevena"** section
3. Enter your Aadhaar number or application ID to check eligibility status

### Step 2: Apply Through Your Institution

- Jagananna Vidya Deevena applications are routed **through the college/institution**
- At the time of admission, inform your college that you wish to apply under Vidya Deevena
- The college will initiate the application process on the Jnanabhumi portal

### Step 3: Submit Required Documents

Provide the following documents to your college:

- **Aadhaar card** (student)
- **Aadhaar card** (mother/guardian)
- **Income certificate** (issued by Tahsildar/MeeSeva, showing family income ≤₹2.5 lakh)
- **Caste certificate** (if applicable, for SC/ST/BC students)
- **Previous year marksheet** or qualifying exam certificate
- **College admission receipt/bonafide certificate**
- **Mother's bank passbook** (first page with account number and IFSC)
- **Family ration card** (White ration card holders get priority)
- **Study certificate** or TC from previous institution

### Step 4: Verification

- Your application is verified at the college level, then forwarded to the district-level authorities
- Income and caste details are verified through the AP government's integrated database
- Aadhaar-based e-KYC authentication is done for the student and mother

### Step 5: Approval and Disbursement

- Once approved, the fee reimbursement amount is credited directly to the **mother's bank account**
- The college receives a confirmation, and the student's fee dues are settled

## How to Check Vidya Deevena Status Online

1. Go to [jnanabhumi.ap.gov.in](https://jnanabhumi.ap.gov.in)
2. Navigate to **"Vidya Deevena"** → **"Know Your Status"**
3. Enter your **Aadhaar number** or **application ID**
4. Click **"Search"**
5. Your application status, sanctioned amount, and disbursement details will be displayed

You can also check via:
- **Calling the helpline:** 0866-2456789 (AP Education Department)
- **Visiting your college's scholarship cell** for offline status updates

## Disbursement Schedule

Fee reimbursement is typically disbursed in **installments aligned with the academic year:**

| Installment | Typical Timeline |
|-------------|-----------------|
| 1st installment | Within 2–3 months of admission/semester start |
| 2nd installment | Mid-academic year (if applicable) |
| Final installment | Before end of academic year |

> **Important:** Disbursement timelines may vary. The government usually announces specific release dates through official press releases and the Jnanabhumi portal.

## Benefits at a Glance

- **100% tuition + exam fee reimbursement** — no out-of-pocket cost for eligible students
- **Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)** to mother's bank account — reduces middlemen
- Covers a wide range of courses: ITI, diploma, degree, PG, and professional courses
- Available for students in government, aided, and private recognized institutions
- No separate online application needed — processed through the institution

## Common Issues and Solutions

### "My application shows pending for months"
- Ensure all documents are correctly submitted to your college
- Check if your Aadhaar is linked to your mother's bank account (verify via NPCI mapper)
- Visit the college scholarship cell and ask them to escalate

### "Amount credited is less than expected"
- The reimbursement amount is based on **government-approved fee structure**, not the actual fee charged
- If your college charges above the approved ceiling, the difference must be borne by the student

### "Mother's bank account not verified"
- Ensure the bank account is active and Aadhaar-seeded
- Visit the bank to confirm Aadhaar-bank linking
- Update Aadhaar details if there are name mismatches

### "Status shows rejected"
- Common reasons: income exceeds ₹2.5 lakh, incorrect documents, non-recognized institution
- Approach your college's scholarship section to understand the rejection reason
- You can reapply after correcting the issue in the next cycle

### "College not processing my application"
- Escalate to the District Welfare Officer or Regional Joint Director of Collegiate Education
- File a grievance on the Jnanabhumi portal or via the Spandana grievance system

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I get both Vidya Deevena and Vasathi Deevena?**
Yes. Vidya Deevena covers tuition/exam fees, while Vasathi Deevena covers hostel and mess expenses. Eligible students can avail both.

**Q: Is this scheme only for SC/ST/BC students?**
No. Jagananna Vidya Deevena is available to students of **all categories** (including OC/General) as long as the family income is within ₹2.5 lakh per annum.

**Q: What if my mother is not alive?**
In such cases, the amount is transferred to the father's or guardian's bank account. Proper documentation (death certificate) must be submitted.

**Q: Do I need to reapply every year?**
Your college typically renews the application each academic year. However, you must ensure your documents (especially income certificate) are updated annually.

**Q: Are self-financed/private college students eligible?**
Yes, if the private college is recognized and affiliated with a university approved by the AP government. The reimbursement is limited to the government-approved fee ceiling.
