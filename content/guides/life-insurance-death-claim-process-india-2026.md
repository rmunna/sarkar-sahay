# Life Insurance Death Claim India 2026 — Complete Guide for Nominees

When a policyholder dies, the nominee must file a death claim with the insurance company. The process is straightforward when you have the right documents. Here's exactly what to do.

---

## Step 1: Intimate the Insurance Company

**As soon as possible** after the policyholder's death, intimate the insurer — don't wait.

### For LIC
- Call LIC helpline: **1800-447-2288** (toll-free)
- Visit nearest LIC branch with preliminary documents
- Or online at **licindia.in** → Claims → Report a Claim

### For Private Insurers (HDFC Life, ICICI Prudential, SBI Life, Max Life, etc.)
- Most have 24/7 claim helplines
- Online claim filing via insurer's portal
- Nearest branch visit

**IRDAI mandate**: Insurance company must acknowledge the claim within **3 working days** of intimation.

---

## Documents Required for Death Claim

### Standard Documents (All Policies)

1. **Death Claim Form** (Form A) — from insurer
2. **Original Policy Bond** — the actual policy document
3. **Death Certificate** — original or certified copy from municipal corporation/registrar
4. **Nominee's Identity Proof** — Aadhaar, PAN, passport
5. **Nominee's Address Proof** — Aadhaar, voter ID, utility bill
6. **Nominee's Bank Account** details (IFSC, account number, cancelled cheque)
7. **KYC documents** of nominee

### Additional Documents for Accidental Death

- **FIR / Police Report** (if accident)
- **Post-Mortem Report** (if conducted)
- **Inquest Report** (if applicable)

### Additional Documents for Early Death (within 3 years of policy)

- **Medical records** of the deceased (hospitalization, doctor's certificates)
- **Employer records** (if death was work-related)
- Insurer investigates early deaths more thoroughly (within 3 years)

## Timeline: How Long Does Settlement Take?

| Stage | IRDAI Mandated Timeline |
|-------|------------------------|
| Claim intimation acknowledged | Within 3 working days |
| Document submission → decision | Within 30 days |
| If investigation required (early death) | Maximum 6 months total |
| Payment after settlement approval | Within 7 working days |

**Total**: For a clean claim (death after 3 years, all documents submitted) — expect **30–45 days** from document submission to money in nominee's account.

If investigation is required (death within 3 years): can take up to 6 months.

## LIC Death Claim Process — Step by Step

1. **Go to nearest LIC branch** where policy was issued (or any LIC branch — they can transfer)
2. Submit intimation in writing with policy number, date of death, cause of death
3. Collect death claim forms from branch (or download from licindia.in)
4. Submit completed forms + all documents (listed above)
5. LIC processes and issues claim settlement letter
6. Payment via cheque/NEFT to nominee's registered bank account

**LIC Online Claim** (for internet-friendly nominees):
- licindia.in → Customer Zone → Online Claims Service
- Available for some policy types; requires policy login

## Term Insurance Claim (Private Insurers)

Private insurer term plans often have faster claim processes (digital-first):

1. **Online intimation**: Go to insurer's website → Claims → New Death Claim
2. Fill claim form online, upload documents as PDF/JPEG
3. Insurer contacts nominee for verification
4. Physical document submission may be required at branch
5. Settlement via bank transfer

**Private insurers with high claim settlement ratios** (FY 2024-25, per IRDAI data):
- Max Life: 99.75%
- LIC: 98.79%
- HDFC Life: 99.41%
- ICICI Prudential: 99.17%
- SBI Life: 97.63%

Claim settlement ratio = % of death claims paid vs received. High ratio = fewer rejections.

## Why Claims Get Rejected — and How to Avoid It

| Rejection Reason | How to Avoid |
|-----------------|-------------|
| Policy lapsed (premium not paid) | Keep premium auto-debit enabled; set reminders; pay in grace period (usually 30 days) |
| Non-disclosure at time of purchase | Declare all medical conditions honestly when buying; insurer can reject if they find undisclosed pre-existing disease |
| Death during waiting period (suicide clause) | Suicide in first 2 years → no claim (IRDAI rule). After 2 years, insured against suicide |
| Cause of death excluded | Read exclusions: adventure sports, war, drug overdose may be excluded in some policies |
| Wrong nominee details (PAN/Aadhaar mismatch) | Keep nominee records updated; ensure nominee's PAN/Aadhaar is correctly registered |
| Fraudulent claim | Insurer has full right to reject and prosecute |

**Most common rejection**: Non-disclosure of pre-existing illness. Always declare honestly — penalty for non-disclosure (claim rejection) far outweighs any premium savings.

## EPF/EDLI Death Claim (Employee's Death)

If the deceased was an EPFO member, nominee also claims:
- **EPF balance** (employee + employer contributions + interest)
- **EPS pension** (if member was above 58 or member had worked 10+ years)
- **EDLI (Employee's Deposit Linked Insurance)**: Minimum ₹2.5L, maximum ₹7L life insurance — free for all active EPFO members

**How to claim EPFL/EDLI**:
- Visit EPFO regional office or file online via employer
- Submit Form 10-D (monthly pension claim), Form 5IF (EDLI insurance), Form 20 (EPF final settlement claim)
- Requires: Death certificate, employer certificate, nominee bank details

## If Claim is Rejected — Complaint Process

**Step 1: Internal grievance** with insurer. Escalate to insurer's grievance redressal officer.

**Step 2: IRDAI Integrated Grievance Management System (IGMS)**
- Visit: **bimabharosa.irdai.gov.in**
- Register complaint with claim reference number
- IRDAI directs insurer to respond within 15 days

**Step 3: Insurance Ombudsman**
- For claims up to ₹50L
- File complaint at nearest ombudsman office (IRDAI website has list by state)
- Ombudsman decision is binding on insurer (not on you — you can still go to court if unsatisfied)
- Process is free, usually completed in 3–6 months

**Step 4: Consumer Court / Civil Court**
- For claims above ₹50L or if ombudsman award is unsatisfactory
- Consumer Protection Act 2019 covers insurance claims under deficiency in service

## Tax on Life Insurance Death Claim

**Death claim proceeds are completely tax-free** under Section 10(10D) — regardless of policy type, sum assured, or premium paid.

This applies to:
- Term plan death benefit
- LIC death claim
- ULIP death benefit
- Endowment policy death claim

No deduction at source (TDS) on death claims. Money received by nominee is 100% tax-free.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does LIC death claim take?

LIC is mandated by IRDAI to settle death claims within 30 days of complete document submission. In practice, many claims are settled in 30–45 days. If investigation is required (death within 3 years of policy), maximum 6 months.

### What if the original policy bond is lost?

Apply for a duplicate policy bond from LIC branch before filing the death claim. Submit an indemnity bond (₹100 stamp paper) stating the original was lost. After duplicate is issued, proceed with claim.

### Can nominee file death claim if they're in another city?

Yes — approach any LIC branch (not necessarily where policy was issued). They can transfer the case. Alternatively, file online at licindia.in or via courier of documents.

### What happens if there's no nominee?

If no nominee was registered, the legal heir must file the claim with succession certificate or legal heir certificate from court/municipality. This process takes longer (3–6 months). This is why adding a nominee is critical.

### Is the entire EPF balance available to nominee immediately after death?

Yes — the nominee can claim the full EPF balance (not subject to any waiting period). Additionally, claim EDLI insurance separately and EPS pension if applicable.
