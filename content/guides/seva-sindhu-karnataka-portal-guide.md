# Seva Sindhu Portal Karnataka — Complete Guide to Register, Apply & Track Services Online

Seva Sindhu is the Government of Karnataka's unified online portal that provides access to **800+ government services** from over 50 departments — all in one place. From applying for caste certificates and income certificates to accessing welfare schemes, Seva Sindhu eliminates the need to visit multiple government offices.

This guide covers everything: how to register, login, apply for certificates, track your application, and download certificates from the Seva Sindhu portal.

## What is Seva Sindhu?

Seva Sindhu (meaning "Ocean of Services") is Karnataka's integrated e-governance platform launched by the Department of Electronics, IT, BT and S&T (e-Governance). It serves as the single-window access point for citizens to avail government services online, including:

- **Certificates** — Caste, income, residence/domicile, birth, death, marriage
- **Welfare schemes** — Social security pensions, scholarships, housing schemes
- **Licences & permits** — Trade licences, NOCs, permits
- **Revenue services** — Land records, mutation, RTC
- **Labour services** — Employment registration, labour welfare

The portal connects with Aadhaar-based authentication and DigiLocker for seamless document verification.

## How to Register on Seva Sindhu Portal

### Step 1 — Visit the Official Portal

Go to [https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in).

### Step 2 — Click on "New User? Register Here"

On the homepage, click the registration link.

### Step 3 — Enter Your Details

Fill in the registration form:

- **Full Name** (as per Aadhaar)
- **Mobile Number** (a valid number is mandatory for OTP)
- **Email Address**
- **Aadhaar Number** (optional but recommended for faster processing)
- **Create Password** (must include uppercase, lowercase, number, and special character)

### Step 4 — Verify OTP

An OTP will be sent to your registered mobile number. Enter the OTP to verify.

### Step 5 — Complete Registration

After OTP verification, your account is created. You will receive a confirmation SMS with your login credentials.

## How to Login to Seva Sindhu

1. Visit [https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in)
2. Click **"Registered User? Login"**
3. Enter your **mobile number/email** and **password**
4. Enter the captcha code
5. Click **Login**

> **Forgot Password?** Click "Forgot Password" on the login page. Enter your registered mobile number to receive a reset OTP.

## How to Apply for Certificates on Seva Sindhu

### Applying for Caste Certificate (Step-by-Step)

1. **Login** to your Seva Sindhu account
2. Go to **"Apply for Services"** → **"Revenue Department"**
3. Select **"Caste Certificate"**
4. Fill the application form:
   - Personal details (name, father's name, DOB, address)
   - Caste/sub-caste details
   - Purpose of certificate
5. **Upload required documents:**
   - Aadhaar card
   - Ration card (see our [Karnataka ration card guide](/guide/ration-card-apply-online-karnataka))
   - Previous caste certificate (if renewal)
   - School records showing caste
   - Passport-size photo
6. **Pay the application fee** (if applicable) via net banking/UPI/debit card
7. **Submit** the application
8. Note down your **Application Reference Number**

### Applying for Income Certificate

1. Login → **"Revenue Department"** → **"Income Certificate"**
2. Fill personal details and income source information
3. Upload documents:
   - Aadhaar card
   - Salary slip / income proof
   - Ration card
   - Self-declaration of income
4. Pay fee and submit
5. Save the application reference number

### Applying for Residence/Domicile Certificate

1. Login → **"Revenue Department"** → **"Residence Certificate"**
2. Enter address details and duration of stay
3. Upload documents:
   - Aadhaar card
   - Voter ID / ration card
   - Electricity/water bill (as address proof)
   - Rent agreement (if applicable)
4. Submit and note the reference number

## Documents Required (General List)

For most Seva Sindhu services, keep these documents ready:

- **Identity Proof** — Aadhaar card, Voter ID, PAN card
- **Address Proof** — Ration card, electricity bill, rent agreement
- **Passport-size Photograph** — Recent, white background
- **Income Proof** — Salary slip, Form 16, self-declaration
- **Caste Proof** — Previous caste certificate, school records
- **Age Proof** — Birth certificate, SSLC marks card

> **Tip:** All documents should be scanned in PDF/JPG format, under 2 MB each.

## How to Track Application Status on Seva Sindhu

### Method 1 — Through Your Account

1. Login to Seva Sindhu
2. Go to **"Track Application Status"** or **"My Applications"**
3. View the status of all your submitted applications

### Method 2 — Without Login

1. Visit [https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in)
2. Click **"Track Your Application"** on the homepage
3. Enter your **Application Reference Number**
4. Enter your **registered mobile number**
5. Click **"Track"** to see the current status

### Application Status Meanings

| Status | What It Means |
|---|---|
| Submitted | Application received, pending assignment |
| Under Process | Assigned to officer, verification in progress |
| Field Verification | Officer visiting your address for verification |
| Approved | Application approved, certificate being generated |
| Rejected | Application rejected (reason will be shown) |
| Certificate Issued | Ready to download |

## How to Download Certificates from Seva Sindhu

1. Login to your Seva Sindhu account
2. Go to **"My Applications"** or **"Download Certificate"**
3. Find the approved application
4. Click **"Download Certificate"**
5. The certificate will be downloaded as a **digitally signed PDF**

> **Note:** Digitally signed certificates from Seva Sindhu are legally valid and accepted across all government departments in Karnataka.

You can also access your certificates through **DigiLocker** if your Aadhaar is linked.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Is Seva Sindhu a government website?

Yes, Seva Sindhu is the official government portal of Karnataka, managed by the Department of Electronics, IT, BT and S&T (e-Governance). The official URL is [sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in).

### 2. How can I check my Seva Sindhu application status?

Visit the Seva Sindhu portal and click "Track Your Application." Enter your Application Reference Number and registered mobile number to see the current status. You can also check status after logging into your account under "My Applications."

### 3. How long does it take to get a caste certificate through Seva Sindhu?

A caste certificate typically takes **7 to 15 working days** from the date of application. If field verification is required, it may take up to 21 days.

### 4. Are certificates downloaded from Seva Sindhu legally valid?

Yes, certificates issued through Seva Sindhu are digitally signed and are legally valid. They are accepted by all government departments, educational institutions, and employers in Karnataka.

### 5. Can I apply on Seva Sindhu without Aadhaar?

You can register without Aadhaar, but many services require Aadhaar-based verification. Having your Aadhaar linked speeds up the process significantly. For services requiring Aadhaar, you can visit a Karnataka One centre for biometric verification.

### 6. What should I do if my Seva Sindhu application is rejected?

Check the rejection reason in "My Applications." Common reasons include incomplete documents or unclear scans. You can re-apply with corrected documents. For disputes, visit your nearest Taluk office or contact the Seva Sindhu helpline at 080-22230282.

### 7. Can I apply for multiple certificates at the same time?

Yes, you can submit multiple applications simultaneously through your Seva Sindhu account. Each application will have a separate reference number and will be processed independently.

### 8. Is there a mobile app for Seva Sindhu?

The Seva Sindhu portal is mobile-responsive and works well on smartphone browsers. The Karnataka government also provides the **Mobile One** app which integrates several Seva Sindhu services.
