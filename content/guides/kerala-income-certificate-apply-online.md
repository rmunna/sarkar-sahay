# Kerala Income Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **income certificate** in Kerala is issued by the Revenue Department and is required for state and central scholarship applications, SEBC (Socially and Educationally Backward Classes) non-creamy layer certificates, fee waivers, and government scheme eligibility. Applications are submitted through **e-Citizen Kerala** or **Akshaya Centres** — the state's CSC network.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [ecitizen.kerala.gov.in](https://ecitizen.kerala.gov.in) or nearest **Akshaya Centre** / **Village Office**. Issued by Tahsildar after Village Officer verification. Processed in **15–30 days**.

---

## Why You Need Kerala Income Certificate

- **Kerala State Scholarship** (SEBC, OBC, SC, ST categories)
- **NSP National Scholarship** — income proof mandatory
- **Merit-cum-Means scholarship** — income below ₹2.5 lakh
- **Non-Creamy Layer (NCL)** certificate for OBC/SEBC — needs income proof
- **Government college fee concession** (income-based waiver)
- **BPL ration card** (Blue Card) eligibility in Kerala
- **Kairali Housing Scheme** and other state housing benefits

---

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (all family members — mandatory)
- **Ration card** (Kerala Blue/Yellow card — shows family composition)
- **Voter ID** (Kerala address important for state schemes)
- Self-declaration of income (online affidavit or Village Officer form)
- For salaried: **salary certificate** from employer
- For farmers: **land records** / Pokkuvaravu (from e-Rekha portal)
- Passport-size photograph

---

## How to Apply Online — e-Citizen Kerala

1. Go to [ecitizen.kerala.gov.in](https://ecitizen.kerala.gov.in)
2. Register / log in with mobile number (OTP)
3. Click **"Apply for Certificate"** → **"Income Certificate"** under Revenue
4. Fill form:
   - Name, address (district, taluk, village)
   - Annual family income (all sources declared)
   - Purpose of certificate
5. Upload required documents
6. Pay fee: **₹10–25** (online payment)
7. Submit — note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via Akshaya Centre

1. Visit nearest **Akshaya Centre** (every panchayat in Kerala has one)
2. Ask for "Income Certificate" application
3. Provide Aadhaar, ration card, and income declaration
4. Operator fills and submits form on e-Citizen
5. Service charge: approximately ₹30–50
6. Collect acknowledgement

---

## Apply at Village Office

1. Visit your **Village Office** (the basic revenue unit in Kerala)
2. Meet the **Village Officer** — submit application form with documents
3. Village Officer verifies income details, visits if necessary
4. Forwards to Tahsildar (Taluk Supply Officer) for issuance
5. Collect from Village Office after approval

---

## Status Check

1. Go to [ecitizen.kerala.gov.in](https://ecitizen.kerala.gov.in)
2. Click **"Track Application"** → enter reference number
3. Or log in → **"My Applications"** → view status
