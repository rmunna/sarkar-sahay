# Digital India Programme: Transforming India Through Technology

The **Digital India Programme** is a flagship initiative of the Government of India launched on 1 July 2015 to transform India into a digitally empowered society and knowledge economy. The programme focuses on three key areas: digital infrastructure, digital services, and digital literacy — ensuring that government services are available to citizens electronically.

---

## What is Digital India?

Digital India is an umbrella programme that brings together multiple existing and new initiatives to ensure that government services are available digitally to all citizens. It aims to:

- Provide **high-speed internet** to every village
- Make all government services available **online**
- Promote **digital literacy** across the country
- Create **IT-based jobs** and encourage digital entrepreneurship

---

## Nine Pillars of Digital India

1. **Broadband Highways:** High-speed connectivity to rural and urban areas through BharatNet
2. **Universal Access to Mobile Connectivity:** Mobile coverage in all villages
3. **Public Internet Access Programme:** CSCs (Common Service Centres) in every gram panchayat
4. **e-Governance:** Reforming government through technology (e-Office, e-Sign, DigiLocker)
5. **e-Kranti:** Electronic delivery of services (health, education, agriculture, financial inclusion)
6. **Information for All:** Open data platform and MyGov citizen engagement
7. **Electronics Manufacturing:** Make in India for electronics
8. **IT for Jobs:** Training youth in IT skills for employment
9. **Early Harvest Programmes:** Quick-win initiatives like Wi-Fi in universities, email for all

## How Digital India Benefits You

### As a Citizen
- Apply for government services online (passport, driving license, certificates)
- Store documents in DigiLocker — no need for physical copies
- Make digital payments via UPI anywhere in India
- Access education through SWAYAM and other platforms
- Get healthcare services through e-Hospital and ABHA

### As a Student
- Free online courses on SWAYAM platform
- Digital scholarships through National Scholarship Portal
- Wi-Fi in universities and educational institutions
- Access to e-books and digital libraries

### As a Business Owner
- Register business online through Udyam portal
- Sell to government through GeM (Government e-Marketplace)
- Access digital payments through UPI for transactions
- Use GSTN for tax compliance

### As a Farmer
- Sell produce on e-NAM platform
- Access weather and crop information through Kisan Suvidha app
- Get soil health information through Soil Health Card portal
- Receive PM-KISAN payments directly through DBT

## How to Access Digital India Services

1. **Download UMANG app** — One app for 1,600+ government services
2. **Create DigiLocker account** — Store Aadhaar, PAN, marksheets, driving license
3. **Register on MyGov** — Participate in governance and policy discussions
4. **Visit CSC** — Common Service Centres provide assisted digital services in villages
5. **Use UPI** — Link your bank account through any UPI app for digital payments

## Important Tips

1. **Use DigiLocker** — Store your Aadhaar, PAN, marksheets, and other documents digitally
2. **UMANG app** — One stop for most government services on your phone
3. **CSCs for rural access** — If you don't have internet, visit the nearest Common Service Centre

5. **Digital literacy** — PMGDISHA scheme offers free digital literacy training for rural households

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. What is Digital India?
Digital India is the government's flagship programme to transform India into a digitally empowered society through digital infrastructure, e-governance, and digital literacy.

### 2. How can I benefit from Digital India?
Use services like DigiLocker, UMANG, UPI, and SWAYAM. Visit CSCs for assisted access if needed.

### 3. Is internet connectivity available in villages under Digital India?
BharatNet aims to connect all 6 lakh+ villages. Over 2 lakh gram panchayats are already connected.

### 4. What is DigiLocker?
DigiLocker is a cloud-based document storage platform where you can store and access official documents like Aadhaar, PAN, marksheets, and driving license.

### 5. Is UPI part of Digital India?
Yes, UPI was developed by NPCI as part of the digital payments push under Digital India.

### 6. How can I learn digital skills?
The PMGDISHA (Pradhan Mantri Gramin Digital Saksharta Abhiyan) provides free digital literacy training. Contact your nearest CSC.

### 7. Are Digital India services free?
Most Digital India platforms and services are free to use. Some services may have nominal government fees.
