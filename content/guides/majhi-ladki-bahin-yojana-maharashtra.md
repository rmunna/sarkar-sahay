# Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana Maharashtra — ₹1,500/Month for Women, Apply Online (2026)

The **Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana** (माझी लाडकी बहीण योजना) is a Maharashtra state government scheme that provides **₹1,500 per month** directly to women from economically weaker families. Announced in the **2024-25 Maharashtra state budget**, the scheme aims to support women aged 21 to 65 by transferring money straight to their bank accounts.

If you are a woman living in Maharashtra and your family income is within the limit, this guide will help you understand eligibility, required documents, and how to apply — both online and offline.

---

## What is Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana?

Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana (meaning "My Dear Sister" in Marathi) is a **Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) scheme** by the Government of Maharashtra. It was launched to improve the financial independence of women in the state.

**Key highlights:**

- **Monthly benefit:** ₹1,500 transferred directly to your bank account
- **Annual benefit:** ₹18,000 per year
- **Who can apply:** Women aged 21–65 from economically weaker families in Maharashtra
- **Payment mode:** Direct Bank Transfer (DBT) linked to [Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
- **Launched:** 2024 by the Maharashtra state government

The scheme is managed by the **Women and Child Development Department, Government of Maharashtra**.

---

## Who is Eligible for Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana?

To apply for this scheme, you must meet **all** of the following conditions:

| Requirement | Details |
|---|---|
| **Gender** | Women only |
| **Age** | 21 to 65 years |
| **Domicile** | Must be a resident of Maharashtra |
| **Family income** | Annual family income must be **₹2.5 lakh or less** |
| **Ration card** | Must hold a valid ration card (Yellow or Orange — priority/Antyodaya category) |
| **Income tax** | Applicant or family members should **not be income tax payers** |
| **Bank account** | Must have a bank account linked to Aadhaar |
| **Not a government employee** | Should not be a current government employee or pensioner |

### Who Cannot Apply?

- Women whose family income exceeds ₹2.5 lakh per year
- Income tax payers or families where any member files income tax returns
- Women already receiving ₹1,500 or more from another state government scheme
- Current or retired government employees drawing pension

## Documents Required

Keep these documents ready before applying:

1. **Aadhaar card** (mandatory — must be linked to your bank account)
2. **Domicile certificate** or proof of Maharashtra residency
3. **Ration card** (Yellow or Orange category)
4. **Age proof** (birth certificate, school leaving certificate, or Aadhaar)
5. **Income certificate** (family income ≤₹2.5 lakh, issued by Tehsildar)
6. **Bank passbook** or cancelled cheque (account must be in applicant's name and linked to Aadhaar)
7. **Passport-size photographs** (recent)
8. **Mobile number** linked to Aadhaar (for OTP verification)

> **Tip:** Make sure your name is spelled the same way on your Aadhaar card and bank account. Mismatches can delay your payment.

## How to Apply for Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana

You can apply through two methods — online or offline at your nearest government office.

### Method 1: Apply Online

1. Visit the official portal at **[nfrm.mahait.org](https://nfrm.mahait.org)**
2. Click on **"New Registration"** or **"नवीन नोंदणी"**
3. Enter your Aadhaar number and verify with OTP
4. Fill in your personal details — name, age, address, ration card number, bank account details
5. Upload scanned copies of required documents
6. Review all details carefully and submit the application
7. Note down your **application reference number** for tracking

### Method 2: Apply Offline

1. Visit your nearest **Anganwadi centre**, **Gram Panchayat office**, or **Nagar Palika/Municipal office**
2. Ask for the **Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana application form**
3. Fill the form with your details — use block letters
4. Attach photocopies of all required documents (self-attested)
5. Submit the form to the designated officer
6. Collect the **acknowledgement receipt** with your application number

> **Note:** Many women in rural areas have successfully applied through their local Anganwadi workers. If you are not comfortable with online applications, this is a perfectly good option.

## How to Check Application Status

After submitting your application, you can track its status:

1. Go to **[nfrm.mahait.org](https://nfrm.mahait.org)**
2. Click on **"Application Status"** or **"अर्जाची स्थिती"**
3. Enter your **application reference number** or **Aadhaar number**
4. Click **Search** — your current application status will be displayed

### Common Status Messages

| Status | What It Means |
|---|---|
| **Submitted** | Your application has been received |
| **Under Verification** | Documents are being verified by officials |
| **Approved** | Your application is approved — payments will start soon |
| **Rejected** | Application was rejected — check the reason and reapply |
| **Payment Initiated** | Money has been sent to your bank account |

If your application is stuck in "Under Verification" for more than 30 days, visit your local office with your acknowledgement receipt to follow up.

## Payment Schedule

- Payments are credited **monthly** directly to your Aadhaar-linked bank account
- Amount: **₹1,500 per month**
- Payments are typically made between the **1st and 10th of each month**
- You will receive an **SMS notification** when the amount is credited

> **Important:** If your bank account is not linked to Aadhaar, your payment will fail. Visit your bank branch to complete Aadhaar-bank linking before applying.

---

## Important Tips

1. **Link Aadhaar to bank account first** — This is the most common reason for payment failures. Visit your bank and get it done before applying.

2. **Keep your mobile number updated** — The mobile number linked to your Aadhaar will receive OTPs and payment alerts. If you have changed your number, update it at an Aadhaar centre first.

3. **Name matching matters** — Your name on Aadhaar, bank account, and ration card should match. Even small differences (like initials vs full name) can cause problems.

4. **Apply through trusted channels only** — Use only the official portal or government offices. Do not pay any middleman or agent. This scheme is **completely free** to apply.

5. **Keep your acknowledgement receipt safe** — You will need the application reference number to check status and resolve any issues.

6. **Check your bank account regularly** — Sometimes payments arrive without SMS notifications. Check your passbook or bank app monthly.

7. **Already have an Aadhaar card?** Our [Aadhaar linking guide](/guides/aadhaar-linking-guide) can help you connect it to your bank account.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. How much money will I get under Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana?

You will receive **₹1,500 per month** (₹18,000 per year) directly in your bank account.

### 2. What is the age limit for this scheme?

Women between **21 and 65 years** of age can apply.

### 3. Can I apply if I don't have a ration card?

No. A valid **ration card** (Yellow or Orange category) is mandatory for this scheme. If you don't have one, apply for a ration card first through your local Tehsildar office or the Maharashtra PDS portal.

### 4. My husband pays income tax. Can I still apply?

No. If **any member of your family** files income tax returns, you are not eligible for this scheme. The family income limit is ₹2.5 lakh per year.

### 5. Is there any fee to apply?

**No.** The application is completely free — both online and offline. Do not pay anyone who claims to charge a fee for this scheme.

### 6. How long does it take to get approved?

Typically **30 to 60 days** from the date of application. It depends on document verification at the local level.

### 7. Can women from other states living in Maharashtra apply?

No. You must be a **domicile of Maharashtra** (permanent resident) to be eligible. A domicile certificate or long-term residency proof is required.

### 8. I am already receiving benefits from another scheme. Can I still apply?

It depends. If you are receiving **less than ₹1,500 per month** from another state scheme, you may be eligible for the difference amount. If you already receive ₹1,500 or more, you cannot avail this scheme simultaneously. Check with your local office for clarity.
