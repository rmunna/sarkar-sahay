## What is Shravan Bal Seva Rajya Nivruttivetan Yojana?

Shravan Bal Seva Rajya Nivruttivetan Yojana is the Maharashtra state government's old age pension scheme for senior citizens who are destitute or have no regular source of income. It is implemented by the Department of Social Justice and Special Assistance, Government of Maharashtra.

Under this scheme, eligible senior citizens receive a **monthly pension** to support their basic needs. The scheme has two components:

- **Below 80 years:** ₹600 per month (₹400 from state + ₹200 from central IGNOAPS)
- **80 years and above:** ₹900 per month (₹400 from state + ₹500 from central IGNOAPS)

> The pension amount may vary based on state budget revisions. Check the official portal for the latest rates.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Age:** 65 years or above (as per Aadhaar/birth certificate)
- **Domicile:** Must be a resident of Maharashtra for at least 15 years
- **Income:** Family annual income must not exceed **₹21,000** from all sources
- **BPL:** Applicant should be from a BPL family or have an income below the poverty line
- Must **not be receiving** any other government pension (central or state)
- Must not be an income tax payer

## Documents Required

- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
- Age proof (birth certificate, school leaving certificate, or election card)
- Domicile certificate or 15-year residence proof in Maharashtra
- Income certificate (from Tehsildar)
- BPL ration card or income certificate showing below poverty line
- Bank passbook (nationalized bank with IFSC code)
- Passport-size photographs (2)
- Self-declaration of not receiving any other pension

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Online Application (via Aaple Sarkar)

1. **Visit** [Aaple Sarkar portal](https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in)
2. **Register** or log in with your mobile number
3. **Navigate** to Social Justice Department → Shravan Bal Seva Rajya Nivruttivetan Yojana
4. **Fill** the application form with personal details, age, income, and bank details
5. **Upload** scanned copies of Aadhaar, age proof, income certificate, bank passbook, and photo
6. **Submit** the form and note the application ID
7. **Track** status using the application ID on the same portal

### Offline Application

1. **Visit** the nearest Tehsil office or District Social Welfare Office
2. **Collect** the Shravan Bal pension application form
3. **Fill** the form and attach self-attested copies of all required documents
4. **Submit** to the Talathi or Social Welfare officer
5. **Collect** acknowledgement receipt
6. **Follow up** at the Tehsil office for verification and approval

## Processing Time

- Verification at Talathi/Tehsil level: **15-30 days**
- District-level approval: **30-60 days**
- First pension credit after approval: **1-3 months**

## Fees

There are **no fees** to apply for this pension. The service is completely free.

## Important Tips

1. **Keep bank account active** — Pension is credited via DBT; an inactive account will cause delays
2. **Life certificate** — Submit annual life certificate (Jeevan Pramaan) to continue receiving pension. You can do this online via [Jeevan Pramaan portal](https://jeevanpramaan.gov.in)
3. **Update Aadhaar** — Ensure your Aadhaar is linked to your bank account for seamless DBT
4. **Apply through CSC** — If you're unable to apply online, visit the nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) for assistance
5. **Report changes** — Inform the welfare office if you start receiving any other pension or your income changes

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. What is the current pension amount under Shravan Bal Yojana?
For beneficiaries aged 65-79, the pension is approximately **₹600/month**. For those 80 and above, it is approximately **₹900/month** (combined state + central contribution).

### Q2. Can I apply if I already receive Sanjay Gandhi Niradhar pension?
No. You cannot receive two state pension benefits simultaneously. See our [Sanjay Gandhi Niradhar Pension guide](/guide/mh-sanjay-gandhi-niradhar-pension) for that scheme.

### Q3. Is there an income limit?
Yes, the family's annual income must not exceed **₹21,000**. An income certificate from the Tehsildar is required.

### Q4. How do I submit my annual life certificate?
You can submit it digitally via the [Jeevan Pramaan portal](https://jeevanpramaan.gov.in) using biometrics, or visit your bank branch or Tehsil office in person.

### Q5. Can NRIs or people living outside Maharashtra apply?
No. You must be a **resident of Maharashtra for at least 15 years** and currently living in the state.

### Q6. What if my application is rejected?
You can re-apply after correcting the reason for rejection. Common reasons include incomplete documents, incorrect age proof, or income exceeding the limit.
