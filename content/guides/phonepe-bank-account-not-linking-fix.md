# PhonePe Bank Account Not Linking? How to Fix It

If you're unable to link your bank account on PhonePe, you're not alone. This is one of the most common issues faced by UPI users. The problem could be with your SIM registration, bank server, account type, or the NPCI mapper. This guide covers every cause and its fix.

## How Bank Account Linking Works on PhonePe

When you add a bank account on PhonePe:

1. PhonePe sends an **SMS from your registered mobile number** to verify your identity.
2. This SMS goes to NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India) which checks if your mobile number is linked to any bank account.
3. If a match is found, your bank account details are fetched and linked.

If any step in this process fails, you'll see a linking error.

## Common Errors and Fixes

### 1. SIM Not Linked to Bank Account

**Error:** "Mobile number is not registered with this bank" or "SMS verification failed."

This is the **most common reason** for linking failure. Your bank-registered mobile number must match the SIM card in your phone.

**How to fix:**
1. Check which mobile number is registered with your bank — look at your bank's SMS alerts or call customer care.
2. Ensure the **SIM card with that number is inserted in your phone** (Slot 1 is preferred on some phones).
3. If you have dual SIM, go to PhonePe > **Settings** > select the correct SIM for sending SMS.
4. If your mobile number is not updated with the bank, visit your bank branch with your Aadhaar and PAN to update it.
5. After updating your mobile number at the bank, wait **24–48 hours** before trying to link on PhonePe.

**Pro tip:** Send a regular SMS from the registered SIM to verify it's working before trying to link.

### 2. Bank Server Down or Busy

**Error:** "Bank server is not responding" or "Unable to fetch account details."

**How to fix:**
1. Wait 30 minutes to 1 hour and try again — bank UPI servers go down during maintenance or high traffic.
2. Check if the issue is widespread by searching on Twitter/X for your bank name + "UPI down."
3. Try during **off-peak hours** (early morning 6–8 AM or late night).
4. If the issue persists for over 24 hours, contact your bank.

### 3. Account Type Not Supported

**Error:** "This account type is not supported for UPI" or linking silently fails.

Not all bank account types work with UPI. **Supported account types:**
- Savings accounts ✅
- Current accounts ✅ (most banks)
- Salary accounts ✅

**Not supported:**
- Fixed deposit (FD) accounts ❌
- Recurring deposit (RD) accounts ❌
- PPF accounts ❌
- Loan accounts ❌
- NRE/NRO accounts ❌ (limited support by some banks)
- Basic Savings Bank Deposit (BSBD) accounts — may have restrictions

**How to fix:**
1. Ensure you're trying to link a **savings or current account**.
2. If your account is a zero-balance or Jan Dhan account, some banks may restrict UPI. Contact your bank to enable UPI.
3. Check if your bank supports UPI — see the [NPCI UPI member banks list](https://www.npci.org.in/what-we-do/upi/upi-member-banks).

### 4. NPCI Mapper Issue

**Error:** "Could not find any account associated with this number" even though your mobile is registered with the bank.

The **NPCI mapper** is a central database that maps mobile numbers to bank accounts. Sometimes this mapping is outdated or missing.

**How to fix:**
1. Visit your bank branch and ask them to **refresh your mobile number mapping with NPCI**.
2. Some banks allow you to update NPCI mapping via net banking — look for "UPI Registration" or "Mobile Number Registration for UPI."
3. Call your bank's toll-free number and request UPI re-registration.
4. After the bank updates the mapper, wait **24–48 hours** before trying again on PhonePe.
5. Try linking the same bank account on another UPI app (Google Pay, Paytm) — if it fails there too, the NPCI mapping is the issue.

### 5. PhonePe App Issues

**Error:** Various unexpected errors or app crashes during linking.

**How to fix:**
1. **Update PhonePe** to the latest version from Play Store / App Store.
2. **Clear app cache:** Settings > Apps > PhonePe > Clear Cache (don't clear data).
3. **Restart your phone** and try again.
4. **Check permissions:** Ensure PhonePe has SMS permission enabled (required for automatic SMS verification).
5. **Reinstall PhonePe** as a last resort — you'll need to log in again.

## Step-by-Step: How to Link Bank Account on PhonePe

1. Open PhonePe app.
2. Tap **My Money** > **Bank Accounts** > **Add New Bank Account**.
3. Select your bank from the list.
4. PhonePe will **auto-detect your SIM** and send a verification SMS.
5. Wait for the SMS to be sent and verified (this may take 30–60 seconds).
6. Once verified, your bank account(s) linked to that mobile number will appear.
7. Select the account and set your **UPI PIN** using your debit card details.

## Important Tips

1. Always keep your bank-registered mobile SIM in the phone where PhonePe is installed.
2. Ensure SMS permissions are granted to PhonePe.
3. If you recently changed your mobile number at the bank, wait at least 48 hours before linking.
4. Some banks require a minimum balance to enable UPI — maintain at least ₹100.
5. If nothing works, try linking via your bank's own UPI app first, then switch to PhonePe.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why does PhonePe say "no account found" for my bank?

Your mobile number may not be mapped in the NPCI system for that bank. Visit your bank branch and request them to register/refresh your mobile number for UPI with NPCI.

### Can I link multiple bank accounts on PhonePe?

Yes. PhonePe supports linking multiple bank accounts from different banks. Each account will have its own UPI PIN.

### PhonePe SMS is not being sent. What should I do?

Ensure SMS permissions are enabled for PhonePe, your SIM has balance (some carriers charge for outgoing SMS), and you're not in airplane mode. Also try selecting the correct SIM in PhonePe settings.

### Does my bank support PhonePe/UPI?

Most major Indian banks support UPI. Check the [NPCI UPI member banks list](https://www.npci.org.in/what-we-do/upi/upi-member-banks) for the complete list.

### Can I link an NRI account to PhonePe?

NRE and NRO account support for UPI is very limited. Most banks do not support UPI for NRI accounts. Check with your specific bank.

### How long does it take to link a bank account on PhonePe?

If everything is in order, linking takes less than 2 minutes. If there are NPCI mapper or server issues, you may need to wait 24–48 hours after the bank updates records.
