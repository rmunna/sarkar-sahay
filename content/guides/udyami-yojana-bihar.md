# Mukhyamantri Udyami Yojana Bihar: ₹10 Lakh Loan for Entrepreneurs

Mukhyamantri Udyami Yojana is an entrepreneurship promotion scheme by the Bihar government that provides **₹10 lakh** financial assistance to aspiring entrepreneurs. The assistance includes **₹5 lakh as subsidy (grant)** and **₹5 lakh as interest-free loan** repayable in 84 easy instalments. The scheme covers SC, ST, OBC, women, and youth from all categories.

## What is Mukhyamantri Udyami Yojana?

Launched to boost self-employment and entrepreneurship in Bihar, this scheme helps unemployed youth set up small and medium industrial units. The scheme has multiple variants:

| Variant | Target Group |
|---------|-------------|
| Mukhyamantri SC/ST Udyami Yojana | SC and ST candidates |
| Mukhyamantri OBC Udyami Yojana | OBC (Other Backward Classes) |
| Mukhyamantri Mahila Udyami Yojana | Women entrepreneurs |
| Mukhyamantri Yuva Udyami Yojana | Youth (all categories) |

### Key Benefits

- **₹10 lakh** total financial assistance
- **₹5 lakh subsidy** (50% grant — no repayment needed)
- **₹5 lakh interest-free loan** (repayable in 84 monthly instalments)
- **Training and handholding** support for entrepreneurs
- Project preparation and DPR support

## Who is Eligible?

- Must be a **permanent resident of Bihar**
- Age: **18 to 50 years**
- Education: Minimum **10+2 (Intermediate)** pass, or ITI, Polytechnic, or equivalent
- **Category criteria** as per the variant applied (SC/ST/OBC/Women)
- Must not be a **defaulter** of any bank or financial institution
- Must have a **proprietorship firm, partnership firm, LLP, or private limited company** registered or willing to register in Bihar
- The proposed project must be for **manufacturing, services, or trading** sector

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card**
- **10+2 or equivalent marksheet/certificate**
- **Caste certificate** (for SC/ST/OBC variants — issued by competent authority)
- **Residential certificate** of Bihar
- **Bank account passbook** (in applicant's name)
- **PAN card**
- **Passport-size photograph** and **signature**
- **Project report / DPR** (Detailed Project Report)
- **Firm registration certificate** (or undertaking to register within specified time)
- **Cancelled cheque** of the bank account

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Online Application

1. Visit the official portal at **[udyami.bihar.gov.in](https://udyami.bihar.gov.in/)**
2. Click on **"New Registration"** or the relevant scheme variant
3. Enter your **Aadhaar number** and verify via OTP
4. Fill in personal details — name, father's name, DOB, address, education, category
5. Upload required documents — photo, signature, Aadhaar, caste certificate, educational certificates
6. Prepare and upload your **project report (DPR)** detailing the proposed business
7. Select the **type of industry/business** you want to start
8. Enter **bank account details** for fund transfer
9. Review all details carefully and **submit the application**
10. Note the **Application Reference Number** for tracking

### After Submission

1. Application is screened by the **District Industries Centre (DIC)**
2. Shortlisted candidates are called for **interview/verification**
3. Selected candidates receive **approval letter**
4. Beneficiary registers the firm and opens a **current account**
5. **₹5 lakh subsidy** is released first
6. **₹5 lakh loan** is disbursed through the bank
7. Business operations begin with **training and mentorship** support

### Check Application Status

1. Visit [udyami.bihar.gov.in](https://udyami.bihar.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"Application Status"**
3. Enter your Registration Number or Aadhaar number
4. View current status and any remarks

## Fees

There are **no application fees**. The scheme is free to apply. However, you may incur costs for:
- DPR preparation (can be done yourself or through a consultant)
- Firm registration charges (as per government rates)

## Processing Time

- Application review: **1-3 months**
- Interview/verification: **1-2 months** after shortlisting
- Fund disbursement: **1-2 months** after selection
- Total process: Approximately **4-6 months**

## Important Tips

1. **Prepare a strong DPR** — this is the most critical document. Include market analysis, projected revenue, and clear use of funds
2. **Apply in the right category** — check which variant (SC/ST/OBC/Women/Youth) you qualify for
3. **Keep all original documents ready** — you'll need them during verification
4. **Start with firm registration** early — it can take time to get proprietorship/partnership registered
5. **Repay loan on time** — the ₹5 lakh loan must be repaid in 84 instalments. Default will affect your credit score

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Can I apply for multiple variants of Udyami Yojana?
No, you can apply under only **one variant** of the scheme. Choose the most applicable category.

### Q2: Is the ₹5 lakh subsidy returnable?
No, the subsidy component of ₹5 lakh is a **grant** and does not need to be repaid. Only the ₹5 lakh loan component needs repayment.

### Q3: What types of businesses are covered?
The scheme covers **manufacturing, services, and trading** businesses. This includes food processing, textile, IT services, retail, and many other sectors.

### Q4: Can government employees apply?
No, government employees are not eligible. The scheme is for **unemployed youth and aspiring entrepreneurs**.

### Q5: Do I need to start a new business or can I expand an existing one?
The scheme is primarily for **starting new businesses**. Expansion of existing units may be considered under certain conditions.

### Q6: What is the loan repayment schedule?
The ₹5 lakh interest-free loan must be repaid in **84 equal monthly instalments** (7 years), starting after a moratorium period.

### Q7: Can I apply if I've already taken a bank loan?
You can apply, but you must not be a **defaulter** on any existing bank loan.
