# RPSC RAS Exam Guide — Eligibility, Pattern, Syllabus & Preparation Tips

The Rajasthan Public Service Commission (RPSC) conducts the Rajasthan Administrative Service (RAS) and Rajasthan Taxation Service (RTS) combined competitive examination. Known simply as the RAS exam, it is the most prestigious state-level civil services exam in Rajasthan.

RAS is the gateway to top administrative positions in the Rajasthan state government, including posts like SDM, DSP, and Block Development Officer.

## Posts Recruited Through RPSC RAS

- Sub Divisional Magistrate (SDM)
- Deputy Superintendent of Police (DSP)
- Block Development Officer (BDO)
- Assistant Collector
- Tax Assistant Commissioner
- District Food & Supply Controller
- Tehsildar
- Employment Officer
- And various other Group A and Group B posts

## Eligibility Criteria

### Age Limit

| Category | Minimum Age | Maximum Age |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| General (Male) | 21 years | 40 years |
| General (Female) | 21 years | 45 years |
| OBC (Rajasthan) | 21 years | 43 years |
| SC/ST (Rajasthan) | 21 years | 45 years |
| PwBD | 21 years | As per rules |

*Age calculated as on 1st January of the recruitment year.*

### Educational Qualification

- **Minimum:** Bachelor's degree from a recognized university
- Any stream accepted
- Final year students can apply

### Nationality & Domicile

- Must be a citizen of India
- Rajasthan domicile required for reserved category candidates
- Female candidates from Rajasthan get age relaxation regardless of category

### Number of Attempts

No limit on attempts for any category as long as age criteria is met.

## Exam Pattern

RPSC RAS is conducted in three stages:

### Stage 1: Preliminary Examination

Single paper, objective (MCQ):

| Paper | Subject | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|-------|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| Paper 1 | General Knowledge & General Science | 150 | 200 | 3 hours |

- Qualifying in nature — marks not counted for final merit
- Negative marking: 1/3rd of marks per wrong answer
- Prelims serves as a screening test for Mains

### Stage 2: Mains Examination

Four papers (all descriptive):

| Paper | Subject | Marks | Duration |
|-------|---------|-------|----------|
| Paper 1 | General Studies I (History, Economy, Sociology) | 200 | 3 hours |
| Paper 2 | General Studies II (Governance, Constitution, Law) | 200 | 3 hours |
| Paper 3 | General Studies III (Science, Tech, Geography, Environment) | 200 | 3 hours |
| Paper 4 | General Hindi & General English | 200 | 3 hours |
| **Total** | | **800** | |

**Note:** RPSC RAS has no optional subject. All papers are compulsory.

### Stage 3: Interview / Personality Test

- **Marks:** 100
- **Total (Mains + Interview):** 900
- Final merit based on Mains + Interview marks

## Syllabus Overview

### Prelims — General Knowledge & General Science
- History of Rajasthan — with special focus on culture, heritage, arts, architecture
- Indian History — Ancient, Medieval, Modern
- Geography of Rajasthan — Desert, Aravalli, Rivers, Climate, Minerals
- Geography of India and World
- Indian Polity and Constitution
- Economy of India and Rajasthan
- General Science — Physics, Chemistry, Biology basics
- Current Affairs — National and Rajasthan-specific
- Reasoning and Mental Ability

### Mains — Key Areas
- **GS I:** History of India and Rajasthan; Economy of India and Rajasthan; Sociology; Management; Accounting & Auditing
- **GS II:** Indian Constitution; Administrative Theory; Public Administration; Law; International Relations
- **GS III:** Science & Technology; Geographical perspective of Rajasthan; Environment & Ecology
- **GS IV:** General Hindi (grammar, essay, précis, comprehension) and General English (grammar, essay, comprehension)

### Rajasthan-Specific Topics (High Weightage)
- Rajasthani culture — folk arts, music, dance, festivals
- Historical forts, palaces, and heritage sites
- Rajasthan's economy — agriculture, industries, minerals, tourism
- Rajasthan geography — Thar Desert, Aravalli Range, rivers, lakes
- Rajasthan polity — Panchayati Raj, state administration
- Rajasthan government schemes and policies

## Important Dates Pattern

RPSC RAS is generally conducted every 1–2 years:

- **Notification:** Usually between June–October
- **Application window:** 3–4 weeks
- **Prelims exam:** 3–5 months after notification
- **Mains exam:** 3–6 months after prelims results
- **Interview:** 2–3 months after mains results
- **Final result:** 12–18 months from notification

## Preparation Tips

### 1. Rajasthan GK is the Game Changer
RPSC gives massive weightage to Rajasthan-specific topics in both Prelims and Mains. Master Rajasthan's history, geography, culture, and economy. Use "Rajasthan" by L.P. Mathur or Rai Publication's Rajasthan GK.

### 2. No Optional — Cover All GS Areas
Like MPPSC, RPSC RAS has no optional subject. You need well-rounded preparation across all GS areas.

### 3. Start with NCERTs
Read NCERT textbooks (Class 6–12) for History, Geography, Polity, and Economy. This builds a solid foundation.

### 4. Language Paper is Scoring
Paper 4 (Hindi + English) carries 200 marks and is often neglected. Strong grammar and essay writing skills can boost your total significantly.

### 5. Practice Answer Writing Regularly
Mains is descriptive. Practice writing structured, concise answers with proper formatting. Write at least 2–3 answers daily during preparation.

### 6. Current Affairs with Rajasthan Focus
Read newspapers daily (Rajasthan Patrika, Dainik Bhaskar). Focus on Rajasthan government schemes, state budget, and recent developments.

### 7. Solve Previous Year RAS Papers
RPSC has a pattern of repeating topics, especially in Rajasthan GK. Solving last 10–15 years' papers is extremely valuable.

### 8. Join a Test Series
Regular mock tests for both Prelims and Mains help with time management, revision, and identifying weak areas.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. What is the salary of a RAS officer?
RAS officers start at Pay Level 10 (₹56,100–₹1,77,500) for Group A posts. With allowances, the initial gross salary is approximately ₹70,000–₹85,000 per month. RTS and lower Group B posts start at Pay Level 8.

### 2. Is there an optional subject in RAS?
No, RPSC RAS does not have an optional subject. All four Mains papers are compulsory General Studies and language papers.

### 3. Can non-Rajasthan candidates appear for RAS?
Yes, candidates from any state can apply for unreserved posts. Reserved category benefits are available only to Rajasthan domicile candidates.

### 4. How many vacancies does RAS offer?
Vacancies vary — typically 300 to 1,000+ posts across RAS, RTS, and allied services combined.

### 5. Is RAS tougher than BPSC or UPPSC?
Each state PCS has its own competition level. RAS has a strong emphasis on Rajasthan-specific content, which can be an advantage for local candidates. The overall difficulty is comparable to other top state PCS exams.

### 6. What is the medium of RAS exam?
The exam can be taken in Hindi or English. Paper 4 includes both Hindi and English sections that are compulsory for all candidates.

### 7. How long should I prepare for RAS?
Dedicated preparation of 10–14 months is recommended for fresh candidates. Those with UPSC or other PCS preparation background may need 6–8 months.
