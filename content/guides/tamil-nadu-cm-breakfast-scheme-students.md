## What is Tamil Nadu CM Breakfast Scheme?

**Chief Minister's Breakfast Scheme** is Tamil Nadu's innovative school welfare programme that provides **free nutritious breakfast** to students in **Classes 1 to 5** in government schools every school day. Launched in 2022, the scheme aims to:
- Reduce hunger and improve classroom concentration
- Improve school enrollment and attendance
- Address malnutrition among children

---

## Key Benefits

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Who gets it | All students in Classes 1–5 in government schools |
| Meal | Nutritious hot breakfast (daily) |
| Cost | Completely free — no fees, no BPL card needed |
| Days | All working school days |
| Time | Before class hours |
| Combined with | PM POSHAN (mid-day meal) — students get both breakfast and lunch |

---

## What is Served

The breakfast menu (varies by day) typically includes:
- Pongal (sweet or savoury) + sambar
- Upma + chutney
- Idli/dosa + chutney
- Rava khichdi
- Egg (on protein-days, in districts where applicable)
- Milk + banana (some days)

---

## Who is Eligible?

**All students enrolled in Classes 1 to 5 in government schools in Tamil Nadu.** No income limit, no BPL card, no registration needed. Every enrolled student automatically gets the breakfast.

---

## How to Access

1. Enroll your child in any **Tamil Nadu government school**
2. The school provides breakfast **before morning classes start**
3. No separate registration or form is needed

---

## Expansion to Higher Classes

After initial success in Classes 1–5:
- The scheme is being **expanded to Classes 6–8** in select districts
- Government-aided schools are also being included in later phases

Check with your local school for current coverage.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. My child goes to a private school. Can she get the CM Breakfast?**

No. The scheme is for **government school students only**. Private school students are not included.

**2. My child is in Class 6. Does she get breakfast?**

The scheme started with Classes 1–5. Expansion to Class 6 and above is being phased in. Check with your school if Class 6 is now included in your district.

**3. Do children also get the mid-day meal (PM POSHAN) in addition to breakfast?**

Yes! Both PM POSHAN (lunch) and CM Breakfast Scheme (breakfast) operate in government schools. Children receive two meals per day at government primary schools in Tamil Nadu.

**4. Who prepares the breakfast?**

Breakfast is prepared by **Women's Self Help Groups (SHGs)** contracted by the school management committee. This also creates income for SHG members.
