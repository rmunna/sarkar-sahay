## Why Nomination Matters

Nomination ensures your money and benefits reach the right person if something happens to you. Without a valid nominee:

- **Bank accounts:** Money gets frozen. Legal heirs must produce succession certificate or probate (takes 6–18 months and ₹5,000–₹50,000 in legal costs)
- **Insurance:** Claim settlement gets delayed significantly
- **EPF:** Family has to go through a lengthy claim process with EPFO
- **Mutual funds:** Redemption requires legal heir certificate, court order, or succession certificate

> **Important:** A nominee is a **custodian**, not the legal heir. The nominee receives the money on behalf of legal heirs. However, having a nominee makes the process dramatically faster and simpler.

## Documents Required

For most nomination updates, you need:
- **Your ID proof** — Aadhaar, PAN, or passport
- **Nominee's details** — Full name, date of birth, relationship, address
- **Nominee's Aadhaar number** (required for EPFO, recommended for others)
- **Passport-size photograph** of nominee (some banks require this)
- **Witness details** — Name, address, and signature (1-2 witnesses for some forms)

## 1. Update Nominee in Bank Account

### SBI — Online Process

1. Login to **[onlinesbi.sbi](https://www.onlinesbi.sbi)**
2. Go to **"My Accounts & Profile"** → **"Nomination"**
3. Click **"Add/Modify Nominee"**
4. Enter nominee's name, date of birth, relationship, and address
5. If nominee is a minor, provide guardian details
6. Verify with **OTP** and submit
7. Download the confirmation receipt

### HDFC Bank — Online Process

1. Login to **HDFC NetBanking**
2. Go to **"Accounts"** → **"Nomination Registration"**
3. Select the account to update
4. Enter nominee details (up to 2 nominees)
5. Submit with OTP verification

### Offline Process (Any Bank)

1. Visit your bank branch
2. Fill **Form DA-1** (for new nomination) or **Form DA-2** (for changing nominee)
3. Submit with your ID proof copy
4. Nominee appears on your passbook/statement after processing

> **Tip:** You can nominate up to **2 persons** for bank accounts. Mention the percentage share for each.

## 2. Update Nominee in LIC Policy

### Online Process

1. Visit **[licindia.in](https://licindia.in)** → Login to your account
2. Go to **"Policy Services"** → **"Change of Nominee"**
3. Enter policy number and new nominee details
4. Upload scanned ID proof
5. Submit the request — processed within 7–15 days

### Offline Process

1. Download **Form No. 3738** from LIC website
2. Fill in policy number, old nominee, new nominee details
3. Submit at your **LIC branch** with:
   - Policy bond (original)
   - ID proof of policyholder
   - Nominee's ID proof copy
4. Collect acknowledgement receipt

> **Note:** For married women, the nominee assignment under **Married Women's Property Act (Section 6)** is irrevocable and cannot be changed.

## 3. Update Nominee in EPF (EPFO Portal)

### Online Process (Recommended)

1. Login to **[EPFO Member Portal](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in)**
2. Go to **"Manage"** → **"e-Nomination"**
3. Click **"Save Family Declaration"** — add family member details:
   - Name, date of birth, relationship, address, Aadhaar, photo
4. After saving family declaration, go to **"Nomination Details"**
5. Select family members as nominees with **share percentage**
6. Enter **e-Sign** with Aadhaar OTP to submit
7. Status changes to **"e-Nomination Submitted"**

### Important Points for EPFO

- **e-Nomination is mandatory** — EPFO has been pushing all members to complete it
- Family includes: spouse, children, parents (if unmarried member)
- **If married**, only spouse and children can be nominated (parents cannot be nominees)
- **Total share** must add up to 100%
- Check nomination status under **"Manage"** → **"e-Nomination"** → **"Nomination Details"**

## 4. Update Nominee in PPF Account

### At Post Office

1. Visit the post office where your PPF account is held
2. Fill **Form F** (PPF Nomination Form)
3. Provide nominee details — name, address, relationship, and share percentage
4. Submit with your PPF passbook and ID proof
5. Get acknowledgement on the form copy

### At Bank

1. Visit the bank branch holding your PPF account
2. Fill **Form F** (same form)
3. Submit with passbook and ID proof
4. Some banks allow nomination update via NetBanking (check with your bank)

> **Note:** If PPF holder dies without nomination, the legal heir must submit:
> - Death certificate
> - Succession certificate / legal heir certificate
> - Indemnity bond
> - This process takes 3–12 months

## 5. Update Nominee in Mutual Funds

### Online Process (AMC Website/App)

1. Login to the **AMC website** (e.g., SBI MF, HDFC MF, ICICI Prudential)
2. Go to **"Profile"** or **"Account Settings"** → **"Nomination"**
3. Add up to **3 nominees** with share percentage
4. Verify with OTP and submit

### Through MFCentral

1. Visit **[mfcentral.com](https://mfcentral.com)** (AMFI's official portal)
2. Login with PAN and mobile OTP
3. Go to **"Service Request"** → **"Nomination"**
4. Update nominees for multiple AMCs in one place

### Offline Process

1. Fill **Nomination Form (SH-13)** available from the AMC or registrar (CAMS/KFintech)
2. Submit at AMC office or registrar's branch
3. Provide PAN, folio number, and nominee details

### Through Registrar (CAMS/KFintech)

1. Visit nearest **CAMS** or **KFintech** office
2. Fill the nomination form for all folios under that registrar
3. Submit with PAN copy and signature verification

> **Tip:** If you invest through platforms like Zerodha, Groww, or Kuvera, check their app for in-app nomination updates.

## What Happens Without a Nominee?

| Financial Product | Without Nominee | With Nominee |
|-------------------|----------------|--------------|
| Bank Account | Frozen → succession certificate needed (6–18 months) | Nominee claims in 7–15 days |
| LIC Policy | Delayed claim → legal heir proof needed | Claim settled in 30 days |
| EPF | Complex process → employer verification + legal heir | Nominee claims via EPFO portal |
| PPF | Legal heir certificate needed (3–12 months) | Nominee claims with death certificate |
| Mutual Funds | Transmission via legal heir certificate | Nominee redeems directly |

## Processing Time

| Product | Online Update | Offline Update |
|---------|--------------|----------------|
| Bank Account | Instant to 3 days | 3–7 days |
| LIC | 7–15 days | 15–30 days |
| EPF | Instant (with e-Sign) | Not applicable |
| PPF | Not available at most institutions | 7–15 days |
| Mutual Funds | 3–7 days | 7–15 days |

## Important Tips

1. **Update nominee after every major life event** — marriage, birth of child, death of nominee, divorce
2. **Nomination ≠ inheritance** — a nominee is a trustee, not the owner. Will and succession laws still apply
3. **Complete EPFO e-Nomination urgently** — many old EPF accounts have no nomination, causing problems for families
4. **Keep nominee details consistent** across bank, insurance, EPF, and mutual funds
5. **Inform your family** about your nominations and where your financial documents are stored

## FAQs

### Q1. Can I nominate someone who is not a family member?
**A.** In bank accounts and mutual funds — yes, anyone can be a nominee. In EPF — only family members (spouse, children, parents for unmarried members). In LIC — anyone can be nominee, but insurance benefit goes to legal heirs.

### Q2. Can I change my nominee anytime?
**A.** Yes, nomination can be changed any number of times. The latest nomination supersedes all previous ones. Exception: LIC policies under MWP Act Section 6.

### Q3. What if I have multiple bank accounts — do I need to update each?
**A.** Yes, nomination is per account, not per person. You must update each bank account, FD, locker, and savings account separately.

### Q4. Is it mandatory to have a nominee for bank accounts?
**A.** RBI strongly recommends it but it is not legally mandatory. However, banks ask for nomination at the time of account opening. Accounts without nominees cause significant hardship to families.

### Q5. Can a minor be a nominee?
**A.** Yes, a minor can be nominated, but you must also provide details of a **guardian** who will receive the money on behalf of the minor until they turn 18.

### Q6. How do I check if my EPF nomination is updated?
**A.** Login to [unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in) → "Manage" → "e-Nomination". If it shows "e-Nomination Submitted" with family details, your nomination is active.

### Q7. What documents does a nominee need to claim money after death?
**A.** Generally: death certificate of the account/policy holder, nominee's ID proof (Aadhaar/PAN), and a claim form. Some institutions may ask for a signature verification or indemnity bond for large amounts.
