# Link Aadhaar with Mobile SIM: Process for All Operators

Linking your [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) with your mobile SIM ensures proper identity verification as per Department of Telecommunications (DoT) guidelines. While the Supreme Court of India has ruled that Aadhaar-SIM linking is **voluntary and not mandatory**, telecom operators still use Aadhaar-based e-KYC as the primary method for new SIM activations and re-verification.

## Background: Supreme Court Ruling

In September 2018, the Supreme Court of India (Justice K.S. Puttaswamy vs Union of India) struck down Section 57 of the Aadhaar Act, ruling that **private companies (including telecom operators) cannot mandate Aadhaar** for their services. This means:

- Linking Aadhaar with mobile SIM is **voluntary, not mandatory**
- Telecom operators cannot deactivate SIMs for non-linking
- Alternative KYC documents (passport, voter ID, driving licence) are accepted for new SIM purchases
- However, Aadhaar-based e-KYC remains the **fastest and most convenient** method

## Why Link Aadhaar with Mobile SIM?

- **Instant SIM activation** — Aadhaar e-KYC enables same-day SIM activation
- **Re-verification compliance** — DoT periodically mandates SIM re-verification
- **Faster processes** — Aadhaar OTP-based verification is quicker than document-based KYC
- **Digital services** — Many government and banking services require Aadhaar-linked mobile for OTP

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar card (original for biometric verification, or Aadhaar number for OTP)
- Existing SIM card (for re-verification of current number)
- For non-Aadhaar KYC: Passport, Voter ID, or Driving Licence

## Method 1: OTP-Based Linking (Online/App)

This method works when your mobile number is already registered with Aadhaar.

1. Visit your operator's website or app
2. Go to **"Re-KYC"** or **"Aadhaar Verification"** section
3. Enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number
4. An OTP is sent to your Aadhaar-registered mobile number
5. Enter the OTP to complete verification
6. Confirmation SMS is sent

> **Note:** This works only if the mobile number you are verifying is the same one registered with Aadhaar.

## Method 2: Store Visit (Biometric Verification)

1. Visit the nearest operator store or authorized retailer
2. Carry your original Aadhaar card
3. The store executive will use a biometric device to verify your fingerprint/iris
4. Your SIM is linked to Aadhaar after successful biometric authentication
5. Confirmation SMS is sent within 24 hours

## Method 3: Doorstep Verification

Some operators offer doorstep verification in select cities:

1. Request doorstep verification through the operator's app or customer care
2. An executive visits your address with a biometric device
3. Complete fingerprint/iris scan with your Aadhaar
4. SIM is linked after verification

## Operator-Specific Steps

### Jio

- **MyJio App:** Open MyJio → Go to My Account → Aadhaar Re-verification → Enter Aadhaar → OTP verification
- **Store Visit:** Visit any Jio Store or Reliance Digital with Aadhaar for biometric verification
- **Customer Care:** Call 198 or 1800-889-9999 for assistance

### Airtel

- **Airtel Thanks App:** Open app → Account → Re-verification/KYC → Enter Aadhaar → Verify with OTP or biometric
- **Store Visit:** Visit any Airtel Store with original Aadhaar
- **Doorstep:** Request via app in select cities
- **Customer Care:** Call 121 or visit [airtel.in](https://www.airtel.in/)

### Vi (Vodafone Idea)

- **Vi App:** Open Vi App → My Account → SIM Verification → Enter Aadhaar → OTP
- **Store Visit:** Visit any Vi Store with Aadhaar
- **Customer Care:** Call 199 from Vi number
- **Website:** Visit [myvi.in](https://www.myvi.in/) for online re-verification

### BSNL

- **Store Visit:** Visit nearest BSNL Customer Service Centre with Aadhaar
- **Online:** Limited online options — primarily store-based verification
- **Customer Care:** Call 1800-180-1503 (toll-free)

## How to Check Aadhaar-Mobile Linking Status

To check if your mobile number is linked to Aadhaar:

1. Visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/)
2. Log in with your Aadhaar number and OTP
3. Check your registered mobile number under profile details

Alternatively, contact your telecom operator's customer care to check KYC/verification status.

## Processing Time

- **OTP-based:** Instant to a few hours
- **Biometric at store:** Instant to 24 hours
- **Doorstep verification:** 24-48 hours after executive visit

## Important Tips

1. Keep your [Aadhaar mobile number updated](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) — OTP-based linking requires it
2. Aadhaar-SIM linking is **voluntary** after the Supreme Court ruling — operators cannot force or deactivate SIMs
3. For new SIM purchases, Aadhaar e-KYC gives **instant activation** vs 24-48 hours for document-based KYC
4. If biometric verification fails, try cleaning your fingers or use iris scan as an alternative
5. You can link multiple SIM cards to a single Aadhaar — the DoT limit is **9 SIM cards per Aadhaar** (across all operators)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Aadhaar-mobile linking still mandatory?

No. After the Supreme Court's 2018 judgment, Aadhaar-mobile linking is voluntary. Telecom operators cannot mandate it or deactivate your SIM for not linking. However, it remains the fastest KYC method for new SIM activations.

### Can my SIM be deactivated if I don't link Aadhaar?

No. As per the Supreme Court ruling, telecom operators cannot deactivate existing SIM cards for non-linking of Aadhaar. If you receive such threats, you can complain to TRAI.

### How many SIM cards can be linked to one Aadhaar?

As per DoT rules, a maximum of **9 SIM connections** can be issued against one Aadhaar number across all telecom operators.

### What if my Aadhaar has a different mobile number?

If your Aadhaar-registered mobile number is different from the SIM you want to link, OTP-based verification won't work. Visit the operator store for biometric-based verification instead. Also consider [updating your mobile number in Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online).

### Can I use Aadhaar for postpaid connections too?

Yes, Aadhaar-based e-KYC works for both prepaid and postpaid SIM activations and re-verifications across all operators.

### What alternatives to Aadhaar are accepted for SIM KYC?

You can use Passport, [Voter ID](/guide/aadhaar-card-link-voter-id), [Driving Licence](/guide/aadhaar-card-link-driving-licence), or any government-issued photo ID for SIM KYC. Document-based verification takes 24-48 hours compared to instant Aadhaar e-KYC.
