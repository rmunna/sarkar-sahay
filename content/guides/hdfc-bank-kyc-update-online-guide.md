# HDFC Bank KYC Update Online 2026 — Without Branch Visit

HDFC Bank notifies customers for periodic KYC updates (usually every 5–8 years, or sooner for high-risk accounts). If you get an HDFC KYC update notice, do it online — it takes under 30 minutes and doesn't require a branch visit.

---

## Why HDFC Is Asking for KYC Update

| Reason | HDFC Action |
|--------|------------|
| Periodic re-KYC mandate (RBI) | Account debit may be restricted if not updated |
| Address/name change | KYC records need updating |
| New income threshold crossed | Higher KYC level required |
| Account dormant | KYC mandatory for reactivation |

---

## Method 1: HDFC Mobile Banking App (Easiest)

### Document Upload Method

1. Open **HDFC Mobile Banking** app → Login
2. Go to **"More"** → **"Profile"** → **"Update KYC"** or **"Documents"**
3. Select documents to upload: **Aadhaar** (front + back) + **PAN**
4. Take clear photo of each document (no blurring, all text visible)
5. Self-attest: In app, digitally accept document authenticity
6. Submit → Status shown in app
7. **Processing time: 24–48 hours**

### Video KYC Method

1. HDFC App → **"Complete Video KYC"**
2. Schedule video call with HDFC executive
3. During call: Show live Aadhaar + PAN to camera
4. Answer verification questions
5. KYC completed on the call (real-time)
6. **Confirmation via SMS/email within 2 hours**

---

## Method 2: HDFC Net Banking

1. Login at **[netbanking.hdfcbank.com](https://netbanking.hdfcbank.com)**
2. **"My Account"** → **"Update KYC Documents"**
3. Upload scanned/photo copies of:
   - Aadhaar card (front + back, self-attested)
   - PAN card
   - Passport photo (if required)
4. Submit
5. **Processing: 2–3 working days**

---

## Method 3: HDFC WhatsApp Banking

1. Save HDFC WhatsApp number: **7290077711**
2. Send "**Hi**" to activate conversation
3. Type "**KYC Update**"
4. Follow bot instructions to upload documents
5. Simpler but slower — recommend app/net banking for critical updates

---

## Method 4: HDFC Branch (If Digital Options Fail)

1. Visit any HDFC Bank branch (not necessarily home branch)
2. Fill **"KYC Update Form"** (given at counter)
3. Submit original documents for verification + self-attested copies:
   - Aadhaar
   - PAN
   - Passport-size photo
4. **Processing: Same day at branch**

## Frequently Asked Questions

**HDFC sent KYC update notice but account works fine — do I need to update?**
Update KYC as soon as asked — even if account works now, banks can restrict it after a deadline. HDFC gives 30–90 days notice before enforcement.

**HDFC KYC update shows "pending approval" for 3 days — what to do?**
Call HDFC customer care: 1800-202-6161. Provide reference number from your app/submission and ask for status. Usually resolved in 5 working days.

**Can I update KYC for HDFC joint account online?**
Both joint account holders need to complete KYC separately. Online KYC update covers the primary account holder. For joint account KYC of both: Visit branch.

**HDFC is asking for "enhanced KYC" — what does that mean?**
Enhanced KYC requires additional documents (income proof, source of funds) for accounts with high transaction values. Visit branch with: latest ITR/salary slip + bank statements (6 months) + Aadhaar + PAN.

**My HDFC KYC was rejected online — why?**
Common reasons: Document photo is blurry/cropped, name doesn't exactly match records, document is expired. Retry with clear, complete, current documents.
