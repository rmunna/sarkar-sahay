## What is NREGA / MGNREGA?

The **Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA)** guarantees **100 days of wage employment per year** to every rural household whose adult members volunteer to do unskilled manual work. The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development.

Key features:
- **100 days guaranteed employment** per household per financial year
- **Minimum wages** as per state notification (typically ₹250-350/day)
- **Job Card** issued to every registered household
- **Payment via bank/post office** account (Direct Benefit Transfer)
- **Unemployment allowance** if work is not provided within 15 days of demand

## How to Check NREGA Job Card Status Online

### Method 1: Check Job Card on nrega.nic.in

1. Visit [https://nrega.nic.in](https://nrega.nic.in)
2. Click on **"Job Cards"** under the "Reports" section (or select your state directly)
3. Select your **State → District → Block → Panchayat**
4. A list of all registered Job Card holders will appear
5. Find your name and click on your **Job Card Number**
6. Your complete Job Card details will be displayed — including:
   - Family members registered
   - Work days demanded and provided
   - Wages earned
   - Muster roll details

### Method 2: Check Payment Status

1. Go to [https://nrega.nic.in](https://nrega.nic.in)
2. Navigate to **"Reports"** → select your **State**
3. Under the state page, click on **"R6 - FTO Status"** or **"Payment to Worker"**
4. Select **Financial Year → District → Block → Panchayat**
5. Click on your name to see:
   - Payment amount per work period
   - FTO (Fund Transfer Order) reference number
   - Payment date and bank/post office details
   - Transaction status (Processed / Pending / Rejected)

### Method 3: Check via Gram Rozgar Sahayak

1. Visit your **Gram Panchayat office**
2. Ask the **Gram Rozgar Sahayak (GRS)** for your Job Card status and payment details
3. They can also help you register for a new Job Card or demand work

## Check NREGA Payment via Mobile

### Nrega Mobile App (Nrega Services)
1. Download the **"Nrega Services"** app from Google Play Store
2. Select your **State, District, Block, and Panchayat**
3. Search for your Job Card by name or Job Card number
4. View your payment history, work days, and FTO status

There is no official SMS-based status check for NREGA. Use the website or mobile app.

## Common Issues and Fixes

### 1. Name Not Found in Job Card List
- Ensure you are searching the correct **Panchayat, Block, and District**
- If you are not registered, visit the Gram Panchayat office with a passport-size photo and Aadhaar to apply for a Job Card
- It takes **15-30 days** for a new Job Card to be issued

### 2. Payment Not Received Despite Work Done
- Check the FTO status on the portal — it may show "Pending" at bank level
- Ensure your **bank account is active** and linked to Aadhaar for DBT
- If payment shows "Rejected," the bank account number may be wrong — contact GRS to correct it
- Wages must be paid within **15 days** of work completion as per MGNREGA rules

### 3. Fewer Work Days Shown Than Actually Worked
- Check the **Muster Roll** on the portal — verify your attendance is recorded correctly
- If days are missing, file a complaint with the **Gram Panchayat** or **Block Programme Officer**
- You can also file a grievance online on nrega.nic.in

### 4. Job Card Shows Wrong Details
- Visit the Gram Panchayat office with supporting documents
- The GRS can update names, photos, bank account details, and family members on the Job Card

### 5. Website Too Slow or Not Loading
- The NREGA portal can be slow due to heavy traffic
- Try accessing during off-peak hours (early morning or late night)
- Use state-specific portals which may load faster

## Important Tips

1. **Save your Job Card number** — you need it to check status online
2. **Demand work in writing** at the Gram Panchayat — this creates an official record
3. **Keep copies of muster rolls** and wage slips for your records
4. **Link Aadhaar to bank account** for timely DBT payment
5. **File grievances online** if payment is delayed beyond 15 days — this is your legal right

## FAQs

### Q1: How do I check my NREGA payment status online?
Visit [nrega.nic.in](https://nrega.nic.in), select your state, go to "Payment to Worker" report, and navigate to your Panchayat. Click your name to see payment details.

### Q2: How to find my NREGA Job Card number?
Go to [nrega.nic.in](https://nrega.nic.in) → Job Cards → select State/District/Block/Panchayat → search your name in the list. Your Job Card number will be displayed.

### Q3: Can I check NREGA status using Aadhaar number?
The NREGA portal does not have a direct Aadhaar-based search. You need to navigate through State → District → Block → Panchayat to find your Job Card.

### Q4: How many days of work am I entitled to under NREGA?
Every rural household is entitled to **100 days of unskilled manual work** per financial year under MGNREGA.

### Q5: What is the daily wage under NREGA?
Wages vary by state, typically ranging from **₹250 to ₹350 per day**. Check your state's notification for the exact rate.

### Q6: How to apply for a new NREGA Job Card?
Visit your Gram Panchayat office with a passport-size photo and Aadhaar card. The Job Card is issued free of cost within 15-30 days.

### Q7: What to do if my NREGA payment is delayed?
Check FTO status online first. If delayed beyond 15 days, file a written complaint with the Block Programme Officer or use the grievance portal on nrega.nic.in. You are entitled to unemployment allowance if work is not provided.
