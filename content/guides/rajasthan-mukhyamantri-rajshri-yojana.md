## What is Mukhyamantri Rajshri Yojana?

Mukhyamantri Rajshri Yojana is a flagship scheme of the Government of Rajasthan to promote the birth and education of girl children. Launched on **1st June 2016**, the scheme provides a total financial assistance of **₹50,000** to families on the birth of a girl child, disbursed in **6 instalments** from birth to Class 12 completion.

The scheme aims to create a positive attitude towards girls in society, improve their health and education outcomes, and reduce the gender gap. It is administered by the Women and Child Development Department, Rajasthan.

## Financial Benefits — 6 Instalments

| Instalment | Stage | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | At the time of birth (institutional delivery) | ₹2,500 |
| 2nd | On completion of 1 year (all vaccinations done) | ₹2,500 |
| 3rd | On admission to Class 1 | ₹4,000 |
| 4th | On admission to Class 6 | ₹5,000 |
| 5th | On admission to Class 10 | ₹11,000 |
| 6th | On passing Class 12 | ₹25,000 |
| **Total** | | **₹50,000** |

## Who is Eligible?

- **Girl child born on or after 1st June 2016** in Rajasthan
- Birth must be in a **government hospital** or **JSY-registered private hospital** (institutional delivery)
- The family must have a **Jan Aadhaar Card** (Rajasthan family ID)
- **First two girl children** of the family are eligible
- For the 3rd instalment onwards, the girl must be enrolled in a **government school** or government-recognised school
- No income limit for the first two instalments; subsequent instalments require continued education

## Documents Required

- **Jan Aadhaar Card** of the family
- **Aadhaar Card** of the mother/father — [See our Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
- **Birth Certificate** of the girl child
- **Hospital discharge slip** (for 1st instalment — proving institutional delivery)
- **Vaccination card** (for 2nd instalment — complete immunisation record)
- **School admission proof** (for 3rd to 6th instalments)
- **Bank account details** (Aadhaar-linked, mother's account preferred)
- **Passport-size photographs** of mother and child

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### For 1st Instalment (At Birth)

1. **Ensure institutional delivery** — Deliver at a government hospital or registered private hospital
2. **Registration at hospital** — The hospital staff will register the girl child under Rajshri Yojana at the time of birth
3. **Provide Jan Aadhaar details** — Give your Jan Aadhaar number to the hospital
4. **Receive unique ID** — A unique Rajshri ID will be generated for the girl child
5. **Amount credited** — ₹2,500 transferred to the mother's bank account

### For 2nd Instalment (After 1 Year)

1. **Complete all vaccinations** as per the Universal Immunisation Programme
2. **Visit the Anganwadi centre** or health centre with the vaccination card
3. **Anganwadi worker** or health worker will update the immunisation status online
4. **Amount credited** — ₹2,500 transferred after verification

### For 3rd to 6th Instalments (School Admissions)

1. **Enroll in a government or recognised school**
2. **School will verify** the admission through the Shala Darpan portal
3. **Apply through SSO portal** — Login to [sso.rajasthan.gov.in](https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in), find Rajshri Yojana, and apply for the relevant instalment
4. **Upload documents** — School admission letter, previous instalment receipt
5. **Verification** — District Women and Child Development office verifies
6. **Amount credited** to bank account

### Offline Application

1. Visit the nearest **Anganwadi centre** or **Women and Child Development office**
2. Fill the application form
3. Attach all required documents
4. Submit and collect receipt

## Processing Time

- **1st & 2nd instalments:** Usually processed within 15-30 days
- **3rd to 6th instalments:** 30-60 days after application
- **Payment mode:** Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to Aadhaar-linked bank account

## How to Check Status

1. Visit **Jan Soochna Portal** — [jansoochna.rajasthan.gov.in](https://jansoochna.rajasthan.gov.in)
2. Search for **Rajshri Yojana**
3. Enter the girl child's **Rajshri ID** or **Jan Aadhaar number**
4. View payment status and instalment history

## Important Tips

1. **Hospital registration is crucial** — Ensure the hospital registers the birth under Rajshri at the time of delivery itself
2. **Complete vaccinations on time** — Delayed vaccinations delay the 2nd instalment
3. **Government school preferred** — Admissions in government schools make the process smoother from 3rd instalment onwards
4. **Keep Jan Aadhaar updated** — Ensure the girl child is added to the family's Jan Aadhaar card
5. **Don't miss the window** — Apply for each instalment within the specified timeframe to avoid delays

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Is Rajshri Yojana available for a third girl child?

No, the scheme covers only the **first two girl children** of the family. If the first two are girls and both are registered, the third girl child is not eligible.

### Q2: What if the girl is born in a private hospital?

The private hospital must be **registered under JSY** (Janani Suraksha Yojana). If it is, the birth qualifies. If not, the 1st instalment may not be processed. Government hospital delivery is recommended.

### Q3: Can the father's bank account be used?

The scheme prefers the **mother's bank account**. However, if the mother doesn't have an account, the father's Aadhaar-linked account can be used.

### Q4: What if we move to another state?

Rajshri Yojana is a **Rajasthan-specific scheme**. If the family moves out of Rajasthan permanently, subsequent instalments may not be disbursed. The child must continue education in Rajasthan.

### Q5: Is this scheme available along with Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana?

Yes, Rajshri Yojana benefits can be availed **along with** central schemes like Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana and Beti Bachao Beti Padhao. There is no restriction on combining benefits.

### Q6: What documents are needed for the Class 12 instalment?

For the 6th instalment (₹25,000), you need the **Class 12 marksheet/passing certificate**, school verification, and the Rajshri ID. The girl must have passed from a Rajasthan government or recognised school.
