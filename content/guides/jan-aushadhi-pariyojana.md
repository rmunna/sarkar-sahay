# Jan Aushadhi Pariyojana – Affordable Generic Medicines Scheme

Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Pariyojana (PMBJP) is a Government of India initiative to provide **quality generic medicines at affordable prices** through dedicated retail outlets called Jan Aushadhi Kendras. These medicines cost **50-90% less** than branded equivalents while maintaining the same quality and efficacy.

## What is Jan Aushadhi Pariyojana?

Under PMBJP, the government has set up a network of **10,000+ Jan Aushadhi Kendras** (stores) across India that sell:

- **Quality-tested generic medicines** at heavily discounted prices
- **Surgical supplies and consumables**
- **Nutraceuticals** (health supplements)
- **Suvidha sanitary napkins** at ₹1 per pad

The Bureau of Pharma PSUs of India (BPPI), now renamed **Pharmaceuticals & Medical Devices Bureau of India (PMBI)**, manages the scheme under the Department of Pharmaceuticals.

## How Jan Aushadhi Works

- Generic medicines contain the **same active ingredient** as branded medicines
- All medicines sold undergo **quality testing** at NABL-accredited labs
- Prices are set by PMBI — typically **50-90% lower** than market rates
- Over **1,900 medicines** and **290 surgical/medical devices** available

## Who Can Buy from Jan Aushadhi Kendras?

- **Anyone** — no prescription needed for OTC medicines
- **Prescription medicines** require a valid doctor's prescription
- No income or eligibility criteria — open to all citizens
- No need for any card or registration

## How to Find Your Nearest Jan Aushadhi Kendra

### Using the Mobile App

1. Download **"Jan Aushadhi Sugam"** app from Google Play Store or Apple App Store
2. Open the app and allow **location access**
3. The app will show **nearest Jan Aushadhi Kendras** on a map
4. You can also **search for specific medicines** and check availability

### Online Search

1. Visit [janaushadhi.gov.in](https://janaushadhi.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"Store Locator"**
3. Select your **State and District**
4. View the list of Jan Aushadhi Kendras with addresses and contact numbers

### Helpline
- Call **1800-180-8080** (toll-free) for store locations and medicine information

## How to Open a Jan Aushadhi Kendra

If you want to open your own Jan Aushadhi store, here's how:

### Eligibility to Open a Store
- **Pharmacist** — any registered pharmacist (B.Pharm/D.Pharm)
- **Institutions** — hospitals, NGOs, charitable trusts, cooperative societies
- **Entrepreneurs** — any individual (must employ a registered pharmacist)
- **Self-Help Groups** — women's SHGs and similar groups
- **SC/ST/Divyang entrepreneurs** get additional incentives

### Application Process

1. Visit [janaushadhi.gov.in](https://janaushadhi.gov.in/) → **"Apply for Kendra"**
2. Register and fill the **online application form**
3. Upload documents — pharmacy licence, identity proof, shop details
4. Pay the **application fee** (if applicable)
5. PMBI reviews and approves the application
6. After approval, **set up the store** with PMBI-supplied medicines
7. Start operations — medicines supplied at pre-fixed rates

### Financial Support for Store Owners

| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Furniture & fixtures grant** | Up to ₹2 lakh |
| **Additional grant for SC/ST/women/Divyang/NE states** | Up to ₹2.5 lakh |
| **Retail margin** | 20% on MRP |
| **Additional incentive** | Up to 15% on monthly purchases exceeding ₹3 lakh |
| **No franchise fee** | Free to apply and operate |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are Jan Aushadhi medicines safe and effective?
Yes. All medicines are procured from WHO-GMP certified manufacturers and tested at NABL-accredited laboratories before being supplied to stores. They contain the same active ingredients as branded medicines.

### How much cheaper are Jan Aushadhi medicines?
Jan Aushadhi medicines are 50-90% cheaper than their branded equivalents. For example, a medicine costing ₹100 as a branded product may cost just ₹10-50 at a Jan Aushadhi Kendra.

### Do I need a prescription to buy from Jan Aushadhi?
OTC (over-the-counter) medicines do not require a prescription. For prescription/scheduled medicines, you need a valid doctor's prescription, just like any pharmacy.

### How can I find a Jan Aushadhi store near me?
Use the **Jan Aushadhi Sugam app** or visit [janaushadhi.gov.in](https://janaushadhi.gov.in/) and use the store locator. You can also call 1800-180-8080.

### Can anyone open a Jan Aushadhi Kendra?
Yes. Individuals, pharmacists, hospitals, NGOs, trusts, and SHGs can apply. A registered pharmacist must be employed at the store. Financial support of up to ₹2.5 lakh is provided.

### What is the Suvidha sanitary napkin?
Suvidha is a brand of affordable sanitary napkins sold at Jan Aushadhi Kendras for just ₹1 per pad. It is part of the government's initiative to promote menstrual hygiene.
