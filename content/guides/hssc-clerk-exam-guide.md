# HSSC Clerk Exam Guide — Eligibility, Pattern & Preparation Tips

The Haryana Staff Selection Commission (HSSC) conducts the recruitment exam for Clerk posts in various government departments of Haryana. This is one of the most popular entry-level government exams in the state.

HSSC Clerk offers a stable government career with decent salary, job security, and opportunities for promotion to higher clerical positions.

## Eligibility Criteria

### Age Limit

| Category | Minimum Age | Maximum Age |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| General | 18 years | 42 years |
| OBC | 18 years | 42 years |
| SC/ST | 18 years | 42 years |

*Haryana has a uniform age limit of 42 for most posts.*

### Educational Qualification

- **Minimum:** 12th pass (Senior Secondary) from a recognized board
- Hindi/Sanskrit up to Matric level
- Basic computer knowledge

## Exam Pattern

### Written Examination (CET-based)

| Component | Marks |
|-----------|-------|
| Common Eligibility Test (CET) Score | 60 |
| Experience / Qualification Weightage | 10 |
| Socio-economic Criteria | 10 |
| Sports / NCC / Other Weightage | 20 |
| **Total** | **100** |

### CET Exam Pattern

| Subject | Questions |
|---------|-----------|
| General Awareness | 25 |
| Reasoning | 25 |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 25 |
| English / Hindi | 25 |
| **Total** | **100** |

- **Duration:** 1.5 hours
- **Type:** Online (Computer-based)

## Syllabus Overview

### General Awareness
- Haryana GK, Indian History, Geography, Polity, Science, Current Affairs

### Reasoning
- Analogies, Series, Coding-decoding, Blood relations, Directions

### Quantitative Aptitude
- Arithmetic, Percentage, Ratio, Average, Time & Work, Data Interpretation

### English / Hindi
- Grammar, Comprehension, Vocabulary

## How to Apply

1. Visit HSSC at [hssc.gov.in](https://hssc.gov.in)
2. Register for CET if not already done
3. Apply for Clerk notification
4. Fill application with CET score
5. Upload documents and submit

## Application Fee

| Category | Fee |
|----------|-----|
| General (Male) | ₹150 |
| General (Female) | ₹75 |
| SC/BC (Haryana) | ₹35 / ₹75 |

## Salary & Benefits

- **Pay Scale:** ₹19,900 – ₹63,200 (Level 2)
- DA, HRA, and other allowances
- Pension (NPS), medical benefits

## Preparation Tips

1. **Haryana GK:** Essential — study Haryana history, geography, districts, and schemes
2. **CET score matters:** Focus on maximizing your CET score
3. **Basic arithmetic:** Practice speed calculations
4. **Current affairs:** National + Haryana-specific news
5. **Previous HSSC papers:** Follow the pattern from past exams

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is CET mandatory for HSSC Clerk?
Yes, Haryana CET score is used for shortlisting candidates.

### Is there an interview?
No, selection is based on CET score and other weightage criteria.

### What is the age limit for HSSC Clerk?
18-42 years for all categories.

### Is 12th pass sufficient?
Yes, 12th pass is the minimum qualification.

### How many vacancies are released?
It varies — recent cycles have had 2,000-4,000+ vacancies.

### Can other state candidates apply?
Haryana domicile is generally required.
