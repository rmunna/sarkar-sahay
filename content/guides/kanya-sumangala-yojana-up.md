## What is Kanya Sumangala Yojana?

Mukhyamantri Kanya Sumangala Yojana (MKSY) is a flagship scheme of the Uttar Pradesh government launched in 2019. It provides **₹25,000 in 6 installments** to families for the welfare of girl children — from birth up to graduation. The scheme aims to promote the education of girls and eliminate negative attitudes towards the girl child.

The scheme is managed by the Department of Women and Child Development, Government of Uttar Pradesh.

## Eligibility Criteria

To apply for Kanya Sumangala Yojana, the following conditions must be met:

- **Domicile:** The family must be a permanent resident of Uttar Pradesh
- **Family Income:** Annual family income must be **₹3 lakh or less**
- **Number of Daughters:** Maximum **2 daughters** in the family can benefit (if twins are born as the third, the third girl also gets the benefit)
- **Children Limit:** Family should have a maximum of 2 children (relaxation for twins)
- **Adopted Daughters:** If a family has adopted a girl child, she is also eligible (max 2 children including biological and adopted)

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar card of the applicant (parent/guardian)
- Aadhaar card of the girl child (if available)
- **Bank account details** (joint account of parent and daughter, or parent's account)
- Birth certificate of the girl child
- Address proof / domicile certificate of UP
- **Income certificate** (annual income ≤ ₹3 lakh)
- Family photo
- Affidavit / self-declaration
- Latest school admission certificate (for education-linked installments)

> You may also need an [income certificate](/guide/income-certificate-online) for this scheme.

## Installment Details — ₹25,000 in 6 Stages

| Installment | Stage | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | On the birth of the girl child | ₹2,000 |
| 2nd | After completing 1 year of age (vaccination) | ₹1,000 |
| 3rd | Admission in Class 1 | ₹2,000 |
| 4th | Admission in Class 6 | ₹2,000 |
| 5th | Admission in Class 9 | ₹3,000 |
| 6th | Passing 10th/12th and admission in graduation or 2-year diploma | ₹5,000 |

**Total:** ₹15,000

> **Note:** The UP government increased the total amount to ₹25,000 in recent updates. The revised installment structure distributes ₹25,000 across the same 6 stages. Check the official portal mksy.up.gov.in for the latest installment amounts.

## How to Apply Online — Step-by-Step

Follow these steps to apply on the official MKSY portal:

1. **Visit the official website** — Go to [mksy.up.gov.in](https://mksy.up.gov.in)
2. **Click on "Citizen Service Portal"** (नागरिक सेवा पोर्टल) or "Quick Links > Apply Here"
3. **Read and accept the terms** — Tick the agreement checkbox and click "Continue"
4. **Register yourself** — Fill in your details:
   - Name, mobile number, Aadhaar number
   - Set a password
   - Enter the OTP received on your mobile
5. **Login** — Use your registered mobile number and password to log in
6. **Fill the application form** — Enter details of the girl child:
   - Girl's name, date of birth, class/school
   - Bank account details
   - Upload required documents (photo, birth certificate, etc.)
7. **Submit the application** — Review all details and click "Submit"
8. **Note down your Application ID** — Save this for tracking

Your application will be verified by the Block Development Officer (BDO) / SDM and then approved at the district level.

## How to Check Application Status

1. Visit [mksy.up.gov.in](https://mksy.up.gov.in)
2. Log in with your registered mobile number and password
3. Go to **"Application Status"** section
4. Enter your Application ID
5. Your current status will be displayed (Pending / Approved / Rejected)

## Important Tips

- **Apply early** — Submit the application within the required time window for each installment stage
- **Keep documents ready** — Scanned copies of all documents should be clear and within the upload size limit
- **Bank account must be active** — Money is transferred directly via DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer)
- **One application per daughter** — You need to fill separate applications for each daughter
- **Contact the helpline** if your application is stuck — Helpline: **18001801111** (toll-free)
- **Offline option** — You can also apply through the BDO/SDM office or District Probation Officer if you cannot apply online

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Who can apply for Kanya Sumangala Yojana?
Any family permanently residing in Uttar Pradesh with an annual income of ₹3 lakh or less can apply. The scheme covers up to 2 daughters per family.

### 2. What is the total benefit amount under MKSY?
The scheme provides ₹25,000 in 6 installments from birth to graduation.

### 3. Can I apply if my daughter is already in school?
Yes. You can apply for the installment relevant to your daughter's current education stage. For example, if she is entering Class 6, you can apply for the 4th installment onward.

### 4. Is there an offline application process?
Yes. You can visit your nearest Block Development Officer (BDO) office, SDM office, or District Probation Officer to submit a physical application.

### 5. How long does approval take?
Typically 1–3 months after submission. The application is verified at the block and district levels before approval.

### 6. Can adopted daughters get the benefit?
Yes. Adopted girl children are eligible. The family must have a maximum of 2 children (biological + adopted combined) to qualify.

### 7. What if I have twins and both are girls?
Both daughters are eligible. If twins are born as the second and third child, all eligible daughters receive the benefit.
