## What is MP Mukhyamantri Udyam Kranti Yojana?

**Mukhyamantri Udyam Kranti Yojana (MUKY)** is the Madhya Pradesh government's self-employment scheme providing **loans with 3% interest subvention** and **no guarantor requirement** to youth for starting new businesses in manufacturing, service, or retail sectors.

Launched to boost entrepreneurship among educated unemployed youth, the scheme connects applicants with banks and provides government-backed interest relief.

## Loan Details

| Sector | Min Loan | Max Loan | Interest Subsidy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Manufacturing | ₹1 lakh | **₹50 lakh** | **3% per year** |
| Service sector | ₹1 lakh | **₹25 lakh** | 3% per year |
| Retail / Trading | ₹1 lakh | **₹25 lakh** | 3% per year |

**Key features:**
- No collateral / guarantor for loans up to ₹25 lakh (CGTMSE-backed)
- Interest subvention of 3% for **7 years** from the date of first disbursement
- Margin money (down payment): **10% of project cost** (to be contributed by applicant)

## Who is Eligible?

- **Age:** 18 to 45 years
- **Education:** Must have passed **Class 12** (Higher Secondary)
- **Residence:** Permanent resident of Madhya Pradesh
- Not holding a government/regular private job
- Not a defaulter on any previous government loan
- **Annual family income:** Below ₹12 lakh per year (broad income limit)

### Priority Categories
- **SC/ST applicants** — preference
- **Women** — preference
- Differently-abled

## New Business Only

MUKY is for **new ventures only** — it does not support expansion of existing businesses. If you already run a business, look at CGTMSE or MSME expansion loans.

## How to Apply

1. Go to **[samast.mponline.gov.in](https://samast.mponline.gov.in)**
2. Click **"Mukhyamantri Udyam Kranti Yojana"**
3. Register with Aadhaar + mobile OTP
4. Fill application:
   - Personal and education details
   - Business type and project description
   - Project cost estimate
   - Preferred bank
5. Upload documents
6. Submit → Application goes to district MSME office
7. After DIC verification → forwarded to selected bank
8. Bank sanctions loan → government releases 3% subsidy into loan account annually

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card**
2. **Class 12 mark sheet / certificate**
3. **MP domicile / residence certificate** (or Aadhaar with MP address)
4. **Business project report** (1-2 pages describing business idea, cost estimate, expected revenue)
5. **Affidavit** — that the applicant is unemployed and not a defaulter
6. **Caste certificate** (SC/ST for priority consideration)
7. **Bank account** details
8. Passport-size photograph

## MUKY vs Yuva Swarozgar (MYSY in UP) — for context

MUKY is MP's equivalent of UP's Yuva Swarozgar Yojana. Both offer government-backed loans for youth entrepreneurship:
- MUKY: 3% interest subvention, larger loan (up to ₹50L for manufacturing)
- UP MYSY: 25% margin money, up to ₹25L

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I apply for a shop or grocery store under MUKY?
Yes — retail/trading sector is covered up to ₹25 lakh. A grocery shop, clothing store, or general store qualifies.

### Is 10% margin money mandatory?
Yes — you need to bring 10% of the project cost as your own contribution. Example: ₹10 lakh project → you pay ₹1 lakh, bank gives ₹9 lakh.

### I am a Class 10 pass (10th). Am I eligible?
No — the minimum educational qualification is Class 12 (12th pass). Class 10 only does not qualify.

### Can women start a beauty parlour or tailoring centre under MUKY?
Yes — beauty parlours and tailoring centres fall under the service sector. Women applicants also get priority processing.

### How is the 3% interest subsidy credited?
The bank charges you the full interest rate (e.g., 10%). At the end of each year, the government pays 3% directly to the bank from the subsidy account, reducing your effective interest to ~7%.
