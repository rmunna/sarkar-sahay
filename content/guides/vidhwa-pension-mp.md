# MP Vidhwa Pension Yojana (Kalyani Pension): Eligibility, Amount & How to Apply

The Madhya Pradesh Vidhwa Pension Yojana, also known as **Mukhyamantri Kalyani Pension Sahayata Yojana**, provides monthly financial assistance to widows (Kalyani) in the state. The scheme is implemented through the Social Justice Department and aims to provide economic security to women who have lost their husbands.

---

## What is MP Vidhwa Pension Yojana?

The MP government provides pension to widows under two schemes:

1. **Mukhyamantri Kalyani Pension Sahayata Yojana** — State scheme for widows aged 18 and above
2. **Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme (IGNWPS)** — Central scheme for BPL widows aged 40-79

Under the Kalyani Pension scheme, the state refers to widows respectfully as **"Kalyani"** (meaning auspicious/welfare-bringer).

---

## Pension Amount

| Scheme | Age | Monthly Pension |
|--------|-----|----------------|
| Mukhyamantri Kalyani Pension | 18-79 years | Rs 600 per month |
| Mukhyamantri Kalyani Pension | 80+ years | Rs 800 per month |
| IGNWPS (Central) | 40-79 years | Rs 300/month (Central share) |

*Note: State and central shares are combined for eligible beneficiaries.*

---

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Gender:** Women (widows) only
2. **Age:** 18 years and above (for Kalyani Pension)
3. **Marital status:** Must be a widow (husband deceased)
4. **Residency:** Permanent resident of Madhya Pradesh
5. **BPL status:** Must belong to BPL category
6. **Samagra ID:** Must have a valid Samagra ID
7. **Not remarried:** Should not have remarried
8. **Not receiving other pension:** Should not be receiving pension from any other government scheme

---

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- **Samagra ID** (family and member)
- **Husband's death certificate**
- **Age proof** of the applicant
- **BPL ration card**
- **Bank account passbook** (in applicant's name)
- **Passport-size photograph**
- **Residence proof**

## How to Apply: Step-by-Step Process

### Online Application

1. Visit [socialsecurity.mp.gov.in](https://socialsecurity.mp.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"पेंशन योजनाओं हेतु ऑनलाइन आवेदन"**
3. Select **"मुख्यमंत्री कल्याणी पेंशन सहायता योजना"** or **Widow Pension**
4. Enter your **Samagra Member ID**
5. Verify pre-filled details from Samagra database
6. Fill in additional details and upload documents
7. Submit the application
8. Save the application reference number

### Offline Application

1. Visit your nearest **Gram Panchayat** (rural) or **Municipal office** (urban)
2. Collect the widow pension application form
3. Fill in all details accurately
4. Attach certified copies of all required documents
5. Submit to the local body official
6. Application is forwarded to the District Social Justice Officer for verification
7. After approval, pension starts from the next disbursement cycle

---

## Processing Time

- **Application verification:** 30-45 days
- **Approval:** Within 60 days
- **First pension payment:** Next quarterly cycle after approval

---

## Important Tips

1. **Death certificate is crucial** — Ensure you have the husband's death certificate; apply at the local registrar if not available
2. **Samagra ID must be updated** — Marital status should reflect "widow" in Samagra records
3. **Bank account should be single** — Joint accounts may cause issues with DBT
4. **Annual life certificate** — Some areas require annual Jeevan Pramaan verification
5. **Apply at multiple touchpoints** — You can apply through CSC (Common Service Centres), MP Online kiosks, or Gram Panchayat

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. What is the pension amount for widows in MP?
Widows aged 18-79 receive Rs 600/month and those aged 80+ receive Rs 800/month.

### 2. Can a widow receiving this pension also get Vridha Pension?
No, a person can receive pension under only one scheme at a time.

### 3. Is there any age limit?
The Kalyani Pension starts from age 18 with no upper age limit. For IGNWPS, the age range is 40-79 years.

### 4. What if I don't have my husband's death certificate?
You must obtain a death certificate from the local municipal/panchayat office. It is a mandatory document.

### 5. Will pension stop if the widow remarries?
Yes, the pension is discontinued if the widow remarries.

### 6. Can I apply at a CSC centre?
Yes, Common Service Centres (CSCs) and MP Online kiosks can help with the application process.

### 7. How is pension paid?
Pension is paid directly to the beneficiary's bank account through DBT.
