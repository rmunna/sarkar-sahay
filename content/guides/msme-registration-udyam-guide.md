## What is MSME / Udyam Registration?

MSME stands for **Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises**. The Government of India provides a unique identification called **Udyam Registration** to MSMEs through a fully online, paperless, and **free of cost** process based on **Aadhaar self-declaration**.

The Udyam Registration replaced the earlier Udyog Aadhaar Memorandum (UAM) system from **July 1, 2020**. The registration gives you a permanent **Udyam Registration Number** and an e-certificate called the **Udyam Certificate**.

## MSME Classification Criteria

The current classification is based on **investment in plant & machinery/equipment** AND **annual turnover**:

### Manufacturing & Service Enterprises

| Category | Investment | Annual Turnover |
|----------|-----------|----------------|
| **Micro** | Up to ₹1 crore | Up to ₹5 crore |
| **Small** | Up to ₹10 crore | Up to ₹50 crore |
| **Medium** | Up to ₹50 crore | Up to ₹250 crore |

> **Both criteria must be satisfied.** The enterprise is classified based on the **higher of the two** thresholds. For example, if investment is Micro but turnover is Small, the enterprise is classified as Small.

## Who Can Register?

- Any business entity involved in **manufacturing or production of goods**
- Any business entity providing **services**
- **Proprietorship, Partnership, LLP, Pvt Ltd, Hindu Undivided Family, Co-operative Society, Trust**, or any other entity
- **No minimum investment** required — even home-based businesses can register
- Both existing and new businesses can register

## Documents Required

Udyam registration is **Aadhaar-based** and requires minimal documents:

- **Aadhaar number** of the proprietor/managing partner/authorised signatory — [Apply for Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
- **PAN number** and **GST number** (if available) — [Apply for GST](/guide/gst-number-apply-online)
- Bank account details
- Business address
- NIC (National Industrial Classification) code of your business activity
- Details of investment and turnover

> **For proprietorships**, only the Aadhaar number of the proprietor is sufficient. PAN and GST are auto-validated if provided.

## Step-by-Step Online Process

### Step 1: Visit Udyam Portal
1. Go to **[udyamregistration.gov.in](https://udyamregistration.gov.in)**
2. Click on **"For New Entrepreneurs who are not Registered yet as MSME"**

### Step 2: Enter Aadhaar Details
1. Enter your **Aadhaar number**
2. Enter **name as per Aadhaar**
3. Click **"Validate & Generate OTP"**
4. Enter the OTP received on your Aadhaar-linked mobile

### Step 3: Select Type of Organisation
- Choose your entity type: Proprietorship, Partnership, Company, LLP, etc.
- Enter **PAN** of the enterprise (for non-proprietorship entities)

### Step 4: Fill Enterprise Details
1. **Enterprise name** — trade name of your business
2. **Type of enterprise** — Manufacturing or Services
3. **Office address** — complete address with PIN code
4. **Date of commencement** of business
5. **Bank details** — account number, IFSC code
6. **NIC code** — select the 2-digit NIC code matching your activity
7. **Number of employees**
8. **Investment in plant & machinery/equipment**
9. **Turnover** (previous year)

### Step 5: Submit Declaration
1. Review all details
2. Check the self-declaration box
3. Click **"Submit & Get Final OTP"**
4. Enter the OTP to complete registration

### Step 6: Download Udyam Certificate
- Upon successful submission, your **Udyam Registration Number** (format: UDYAM-XX-00-0000000) is generated immediately
- Download and print your **Udyam Certificate** from the portal
- Certificate is also sent to your registered email

## Benefits of MSME/Udyam Registration

### Financial Benefits
- **Priority sector lending** from banks at lower interest rates
- **Collateral-free loans** under CGTMSE scheme (up to ₹5 crore)
- **Subsidy on patent and trademark registration** — [Register trademark](/guide/trademark-registration-online)
- **Interest rate subsidy** under various government schemes

### Government Procurement
- **25% procurement reservation** from government departments for MSMEs
- **Exemption from earnest money deposit (EMD)** in government tenders
- **GeM (Government e-Marketplace)** priority listing

### Tax Benefits
- **Reduced corporate tax rate** options
- **MAT credit** carry forward
- **Exemption from certain direct taxes** for initial years (under specific schemes)

### Other Benefits
- **Protection against delayed payments** — buyers must pay within 45 days
- **Interest on delayed payments** at 3x bank rate
- Access to **MSME Sambandh** portal for government procurement
- Eligibility for **MSME awards** and recognitions
- Easier access to **ISO certification** reimbursement
- Benefits under **PM Vishwakarma**, **PMEGP**, and other MSME schemes

## Updating Udyam Registration

If your business details change (address, turnover, investment), update your Udyam registration:
1. Visit udyamregistration.gov.in
2. Click **"For those already having registration"** → **"Update Udyam Registration"**
3. Enter Udyam Registration Number and verify with OTP
4. Update the required details

## Important Tips

1. **It's completely free** — do not pay any intermediary; the official portal is udyamregistration.gov.in
2. **Aadhaar is mandatory** — ensure your Aadhaar is linked to an active mobile number
3. **Auto-classification** — the portal auto-classifies your enterprise based on ITR and GST data linked to your PAN
4. **One enterprise, one Udyam** — each enterprise gets only one Udyam number (but can have multiple activities)
5. **Update regularly** — if your turnover/investment changes category, update your registration

## FAQs

### Q1: Is Udyam registration free?
**Yes**, Udyam registration is 100% free. There is no government fee. The process is entirely online at udyamregistration.gov.in.

### Q2: Is Aadhaar mandatory for Udyam registration?
**Yes**, Aadhaar is mandatory for Udyam registration. For proprietorships, the proprietor's Aadhaar is used. For companies/LLPs, the authorised signatory's Aadhaar is required.

### Q3: What is the difference between Udyam and Udyog Aadhaar?
Udyam Registration replaced Udyog Aadhaar from July 1, 2020. All existing Udyog Aadhaar holders were required to re-register on the Udyam portal. Udyam uses PAN-linked auto-validation of investment and turnover.

### Q4: Can a company register as MSME?
**Yes**, any entity (proprietorship, partnership, LLP, Pvt Ltd, etc.) can register as MSME if it meets the investment and turnover criteria.

### Q5: How long does Udyam registration take?
Registration is **instant**. You get your Udyam Registration Number and certificate immediately after OTP verification and submission.

### Q6: Is Udyam registration mandatory?
It is not legally mandatory for all businesses, but it is **required to avail MSME benefits** like priority lending, government tender preferences, and scheme subsidies.

### Q7: What if my enterprise crosses the MSME limits?
If your investment or turnover exceeds Medium enterprise limits, you will be **graduated out** of MSME classification. The portal auto-updates classification based on ITR and GST data.
