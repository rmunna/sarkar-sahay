# Paytm KYC Not Working? Fix Verification Errors

KYC (Know Your Customer) is mandatory for using Paytm wallet beyond basic limits. If your Paytm KYC is failing — whether it's video KYC, Aadhaar verification, or at a KYC center — this guide will help you fix the issue.

## Minimum KYC vs Full KYC — What's the Difference?

| Feature | Minimum KYC | Full KYC |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly wallet limit | ₹10,000 | ₹1,00,000 balance |
| Wallet to bank transfer | ❌ Not allowed | ✅ Allowed |
| Verification method | OTP-based (self) | Video KYC / KYC center visit |
| Documents needed | Mobile number only | Aadhaar + PAN (or other ID) |
| Validity | Limited | Ongoing (periodic re-verification may apply) |

**Important:** After RBI guidelines, full KYC is required for most wallet features. Minimum KYC wallets have restricted functionality.

## Fix 1: Video KYC Failed

Video KYC is the easiest way to complete full KYC from home. If it keeps failing:

### Common Reasons for Video KYC Failure
- Poor lighting or blurry camera
- Face not matching Aadhaar photo
- Background noise during video call
- Unstable internet connection
- Aadhaar details don't match Paytm profile

### How to Fix
1. **Ensure good lighting** — sit facing a window or light source. Avoid backlighting.
2. **Use a stable internet connection** — prefer Wi-Fi over mobile data. Minimum 1 Mbps recommended.
3. **Keep your Aadhaar card ready** — the agent will ask you to show it on camera.
4. **Match your Paytm profile name** with your Aadhaar name exactly. If there's a mismatch, update your Paytm profile first.
5. **Clean your phone camera** for a clear image.
6. **Find a quiet place** — background noise can cause the video call to disconnect.
7. **Retry at non-peak hours** — try early morning (8-10 AM) or late evening when agents are more available.

### Video KYC Steps
1. Open Paytm → Profile → **KYC & Account Settings**.
2. Tap **"Complete KYC"** → select **Video KYC**.
3. Grant camera and microphone permissions.
4. Wait for an agent to connect (usually 2-10 minutes).
5. Follow the agent's instructions — show your face, show your Aadhaar card, answer verification questions.
6. KYC is usually approved within minutes after the call.

## Fix 2: Aadhaar Verification Error

If you see "Aadhaar verification failed" or "Unable to verify Aadhaar":

### Check These First
1. **Aadhaar number is correct** — verify the 12-digit number you entered.
2. **Mobile number linked to Aadhaar** — OTP will be sent to your Aadhaar-registered mobile. Check at [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/).
3. **Name mismatch** — your name in Paytm must match your Aadhaar name. Update if different.
4. **Aadhaar not locked** — if you've locked your Aadhaar biometrics, unlock them temporarily at [UIDAI portal](https://uidai.gov.in/).

### If OTP Is Not Received
1. Ensure your mobile number is linked to Aadhaar — if not, visit an [Aadhaar enrolment center](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) to update it.
2. Check for network issues — try after some time.
3. UIDAI's authentication service may be temporarily down — wait and retry after 1-2 hours.

## Fix 3: KYC at Paytm KYC Center Not Working

If video KYC repeatedly fails, you can visit a physical Paytm KYC center.

### How to Find a KYC Center
1. Open Paytm → Profile → **KYC & Account Settings**.
2. Tap **"Complete KYC"** → select **"Visit KYC Centre"**.
3. The app will show nearby KYC centers with addresses.

### What to Carry
- **Original Aadhaar card** (or e-Aadhaar printout)
- **Original PAN card** (if available)
- Your phone with Paytm app installed

### Common Issues at KYC Centers
| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Center closed/not found | Use the in-app locator for updated addresses. Some centers have been discontinued — try another. |
| Agent says "system down" | Visit during working hours (10 AM - 6 PM). Try a different center. |
| Biometric verification failed | Clean your fingers, try again. If fingerprints don't work, request iris-based verification. |
| KYC done but status not updated | Wait 24-48 hours. If still pending, contact Paytm support. |

## Fix 4: KYC Status Stuck at "Pending" or "In Progress"

1. Open Paytm → Profile → **KYC & Account Settings** → check status.
2. If it says **"Pending"** after video KYC — wait up to 48 hours for verification.
3. If stuck for more than 48 hours:
   - Go to Paytm → **Help & Support** → search "KYC" → raise a ticket.
   - Or call **0120-4456-456**.
4. You may need to redo the KYC process if verification failed silently.

---

## Fix 5: "KYC Expired" or Re-KYC Required

RBI mandates periodic KYC re-verification. If your KYC has expired:

1. Open Paytm → you'll see a banner saying "Complete KYC" or "KYC Expired".
2. Tap and follow the video KYC or center visit process again.
3. Your wallet functionality will be restricted until re-KYC is complete.

## Important Tips

- Keep your Aadhaar details updated — mismatched name or address is the #1 reason for KYC failure.
- Video KYC is faster and more convenient than visiting a center.
- If video KYC fails 3 times, wait 24 hours before trying again.
- Never pay anyone to "do KYC for you" — Paytm KYC is free. Avoid scams.
- Keep your Aadhaar biometrics unlocked when attempting KYC.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Is Paytm KYC free?
Yes, Paytm KYC is completely free. Do not pay any agent or third party for KYC completion.

### Q2: Can I do Paytm KYC without Aadhaar?
Aadhaar is the primary document for Paytm KYC. In some cases, other government-issued IDs (voter ID, passport) may be accepted at physical centers, but Aadhaar is preferred.

### Q3: How long does Paytm video KYC take?
The video call itself takes 3-5 minutes. Waiting for an agent can take 2-15 minutes depending on demand. KYC approval is usually instant after the call.

### Q4: My Paytm KYC was rejected. Why?
Common reasons: name mismatch between Paytm and Aadhaar, blurry document photo, face not matching Aadhaar photo, or Aadhaar details not verifiable through UIDAI.

### Q5: What happens if I don't complete Paytm KYC?
Your wallet will be restricted to Minimum KYC limits (₹10,000/month). You won't be able to transfer wallet balance to bank or use full wallet features.

### Q6: Can I complete Paytm KYC for someone else?
No, KYC must be done by the account holder themselves. The video KYC requires live face verification.
