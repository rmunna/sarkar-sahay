# HDFC Bank Net Banking / Mobile Banking Not Working — How to Fix

HDFC Bank is one of India's largest private banks, with over 8 crore customers using NetBanking and the MobileBanking app daily. Login failures, transaction errors, app crashes, and blocked debit cards are common issues. This guide covers every major problem and its step-by-step fix.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent informational platform and is not affiliated with HDFC Bank. Always verify details on the [official HDFC Bank website](https://www.hdfcbank.com/).

---

## 1. HDFC NetBanking Login Not Working

### Problem: Wrong Password / Invalid Credentials

**Symptoms:** "Invalid Customer ID or Password" error on the NetBanking login page.

**Fix:**
1. Ensure you're entering your **Customer ID** (not account number) — it's an 8-digit number found on your welcome kit, passbook, or any HDFC email
2. Check that **Caps Lock** is off — passwords are case-sensitive
3. Clear browser cache and cookies, or try in **Incognito/Private mode**
4. Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge)
5. If you've forgotten your password, reset it (see [Password Reset section below](#5-password--ipin-reset-step-by-step))

### Problem: Account Locked After Multiple Wrong Attempts

**Symptoms:** "Your NetBanking access has been locked" message.

**Fix:**
1. HDFC locks NetBanking after **5 consecutive wrong password attempts**
2. Wait **24 hours** — the account auto-unlocks
3. For immediate unlock, call HDFC PhoneBanking: **1800-202-6161** (toll-free) and request a NetBanking unlock
4. You can also visit your nearest HDFC branch with your **PAN card** and **account details** to get it unlocked

### Problem: Session Expired / Auto-Logout

**Symptoms:** "Session expired, please login again" even during active use.

**Fix:**
1. HDFC NetBanking sessions expire after **5 minutes of inactivity**
2. Do not open NetBanking in **multiple tabs** — this causes session conflicts
3. Disable browser extensions (especially ad blockers) that may interfere
4. Clear cookies for `netbanking.hdfcbank.com`
5. If the issue persists, try a different browser or device

## 2. HDFC Mobile Banking App Not Working

### Problem: App Crashes or Won't Open

**Fix:**
1. **Update the app** — go to Play Store (Android) or App Store (iOS) and install the latest version of HDFC MobileBanking
2. **Clear app cache:** Settings → Apps → HDFC MobileBanking → Clear Cache
3. **Restart your phone** and try again
4. If the app still crashes, **uninstall and reinstall** the app
5. Ensure your phone OS is up to date — HDFC app requires Android 6+ or iOS 13+

### Problem: "Service Unavailable" Error

**Fix:**
1. This usually means HDFC servers are under maintenance or overloaded
2. Check [HDFC Bank's official Twitter/X](https://twitter.com/HDFCBank_Cares) for maintenance announcements
3. Wait **15–30 minutes** and try again
4. Switch from **Wi-Fi to mobile data** (or vice versa) to rule out network issues
5. If the error persists for hours, call **1800-202-6161**

### Problem: Biometric Login (Fingerprint/Face ID) Failed

**Fix:**
1. Go to HDFC MobileBanking → **Settings → Login Preferences**
2. **Disable** biometric login, then **re-enable** it
3. Re-register your fingerprint/Face ID in your phone's **system settings**
4. Clear the HDFC app cache and try again
5. If biometric keeps failing, use **MPIN login** as an alternative
6. After a phone change, you'll need to **re-register** the device for mobile banking

## 3. Transaction Failed on HDFC (NEFT / IMPS / UPI)

### Common Error Messages and Fixes

| Error | Likely Cause | Fix |
|-------|-------------|-----|
| "Transaction declined" | Insufficient balance or daily limit exceeded | Check balance; increase limits via NetBanking → Manage Limits |
| "Beneficiary not active" | Beneficiary cooling period not over | Wait 30 minutes (IMPS) or up to 24 hours (NEFT) after adding |
| "Technical error, try later" | Server issue | Retry after 15–30 minutes |
| "OTP expired" | OTP entered after 3-minute validity | Request a new OTP and enter it within 3 minutes |
| "Account frozen/blocked" | KYC issue or bank-imposed hold | Visit the branch with KYC documents |

### NEFT/IMPS Transaction Failed — Money Debited

1. **Don't panic** — if money is debited but the transaction fails, the amount is auto-reversed within **24–48 hours** (NEFT) or **1–2 hours** (IMPS)
2. Note the **transaction reference number** from your account statement
3. If money isn't reversed within 48 hours:
   - Raise a complaint via NetBanking → **Service Requests → Transaction Dispute**
   - Or call **1800-202-6161** with the reference number
4. You can also email **netbanking@hdfcbank.com** with your Customer ID, transaction date, amount, and reference number

### UPI Transaction Failed

1. Check if your **UPI daily limit** (₹1 lakh) has been reached
2. Ensure your **UPI PIN** is correct — 3 wrong attempts will block UPI
3. If UPI is blocked, reset via: HDFC MobileBanking → UPI → **Reset UPI PIN**
4. For "beneficiary bank not responding" errors, the issue is with the receiver's bank — try after some time
5. Check your **linked bank account** in the UPI app — it should be your active HDFC account

## 4. Beneficiary Addition Not Working

**Symptoms:** Unable to add a new beneficiary for fund transfer, or beneficiary approval OTP not received.

**Fix:**
1. Ensure you're entering the correct **IFSC code** — verify at [HDFC IFSC search](https://www.hdfcbank.com/personal/resources/ifsc-code)
2. The **account number** must match exactly (no spaces, correct number of digits)
3. If OTP is not received:
   - Check that your registered **mobile number** is correct and reachable
   - Wait 2–3 minutes; if no OTP, click **Resend OTP**
   - Check SMS filters/spam on your phone
4. HDFC has a **cooling period** after adding a beneficiary:
   - **IMPS:** 30 minutes
   - **NEFT/RTGS:** Up to 24 hours
5. Maximum beneficiary limit: You can add up to **50 beneficiaries** in NetBanking
6. If the page shows "Service temporarily unavailable," try during non-peak hours (before 10 AM or after 8 PM)

## 5. Password / IPIN Reset Step-by-Step

### Reset NetBanking Password (Online)

1. Go to [HDFC NetBanking login page](https://netbanking.hdfcbank.com/)
2. Click **"Forgot Password?"** below the login button
3. Enter your **Customer ID** and registered **mobile number**
4. You'll receive an **OTP** on your registered mobile
5. Enter the OTP → Set a **new password**
6. Password rules: 8–20 characters, must include uppercase, lowercase, number, and special character
7. Login with your new password

### Reset IPIN (ATM PIN / Phone Banking PIN)

1. Visit any **HDFC Bank ATM**
2. Insert your debit card → Select **"PIN Change"** or **"Generate PIN"**
3. Enter your registered mobile number → You'll get an **OTP**
4. Enter OTP → Set your new **4-digit IPIN**
5. Alternatively, generate IPIN via **NetBanking:** Login → Cards → Debit Card → Generate ATM PIN

### Reset via PhoneBanking

1. Call **1800-202-6161** (toll-free) or **1860-267-6161** (paid)
2. Authenticate via registered mobile number + date of birth
3. Request **NetBanking password reset** or **IPIN regeneration**
4. You'll receive a temporary password/IPIN via SMS — change it on first use

## 6. Debit Card Blocked After Wrong PIN — Unblock Process

**Why it happens:** HDFC blocks your debit card after **3 consecutive wrong ATM PIN attempts** for security.

### How to Unblock

**Method 1: Via NetBanking**
1. Login to [HDFC NetBanking](https://netbanking.hdfcbank.com/)
2. Go to **Cards → Debit Cards → Manage Debit Card**
3. Select your blocked card → Click **"Unblock"** or **"Reset PIN"**
4. Complete OTP verification
5. Set a new PIN → Your card is unblocked

**Method 2: Via HDFC MobileBanking App**
1. Open the app → Go to **Cards → Debit Cards**
2. Select the blocked card → Tap **"Unblock Card"**
3. Verify via OTP → Set a new PIN

**Method 3: Visit an HDFC Branch**
1. Carry your **PAN card / Aadhaar** and **debit card**
2. Fill the debit card unblock request form
3. The branch will unblock your card and issue a new PIN

**Method 4: Call PhoneBanking**
1. Call **1800-202-6161**
2. Request debit card unblock
3. Verify identity → Receive new PIN via SMS

> **Note:** If your card is blocked due to suspected fraud, only a branch visit can unblock it. The bank may issue a replacement card.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. Why is HDFC NetBanking not working today?

HDFC Bank performs scheduled maintenance, usually between **12:00 AM – 6:00 AM** on weekends. If NetBanking is down during other times, it could be a server issue. Check [@HDFCBank_Cares](https://twitter.com/HDFCBank_Cares) on Twitter for live updates or call 1800-202-6161.

### Q2. How do I unlock my HDFC NetBanking account after it's locked?

Your account auto-unlocks after **24 hours**. For immediate unlock, call 1800-202-6161 or visit your nearest HDFC branch with your PAN card.

### Q3. HDFC Mobile Banking shows "Device not registered." What should I do?

This happens after a phone change or app reinstall. Open the app → select **"New Device Registration"** → enter your Customer ID and registered mobile number → complete OTP verification → set a new MPIN.

### Q4. My HDFC NEFT/IMPS transaction failed but money was debited. When will I get a refund?

Failed IMPS transactions are usually reversed within **1–2 hours**. NEFT reversals take up to **24–48 hours**. If money isn't returned within 48 hours, raise a dispute via NetBanking or call 1800-202-6161.

### Q5. How do I reset my HDFC NetBanking password without a registered mobile number?

You cannot reset it online without a registered mobile. Visit your **nearest HDFC branch** with PAN card and photo ID. The branch will update your mobile number and help reset the password.

### Q6. Why is HDFC Bank app showing "Service Unavailable"?

This indicates a temporary server issue or scheduled maintenance. Update the app, clear cache, switch networks, and retry after 15–30 minutes. If it persists, contact HDFC support.

### Q7. Can I unblock my HDFC debit card online?

Yes. Login to NetBanking → Cards → Debit Cards → Manage Debit Card → select your card → click Unblock. You can also do this via the MobileBanking app.

### Q8. How long does HDFC beneficiary activation take?

After adding a beneficiary, there's a cooling period: **30 minutes for IMPS** and **up to 24 hours for NEFT/RTGS**. You cannot transfer funds during this period.

### Q9. HDFC UPI is not working. How to fix it?

Ensure your UPI PIN is correct (3 wrong attempts block UPI). Reset UPI PIN via the HDFC MobileBanking app. Also check if your daily UPI limit (₹1 lakh) has been reached. See our [UPI guide](/guide/upi-payment-failed-money-debited-refund-fix) for general UPI troubleshooting.

### Q10. Is there a way to increase HDFC NetBanking transaction limits?

Yes. Login to NetBanking → **Manage Transaction Limits** → increase NEFT/IMPS/UPI limits (subject to bank-defined maximum caps). Some limit changes require OTP or secure access verification.
