## What is Aadhaar Name Correction?

Aadhaar name correction lets you fix spelling errors, typos, or update your name on Aadhaar records through the **SSUP portal** (Self-Service Update Portal) or at an **Aadhaar Enrolment Centre**. However, UIDAI frequently rejects name correction requests due to wrong documents, mismatched details, or confusion between **name correction** and **name change**.

This guide explains **why your Aadhaar name correction was rejected** and gives you a step-by-step plan to get it approved.

## Name Correction vs Name Change — The Key Difference

Understanding this distinction is critical because UIDAI treats them differently:

| Type | What It Means | Example | Process |
|------|--------------|---------|---------|
| **Name Correction** (minor) | Fixing spelling mistakes or typos in existing name | "Rajkumar" → "Raj Kumar", "Sunita" → "Sunitha" | Online via SSUP or at Aadhaar centre |
| **Name Change** (major) | Legally changing your name entirely | "Rahul Sharma" → "Arjun Verma" (after marriage, adoption, court order) | **Only at Aadhaar Enrolment Centre** with gazette/court order |

> **Important:** You cannot do a complete name change online through SSUP. Only corrections (fixing errors in the same name) are allowed online. Attempting a full name change via SSUP is a top reason for rejection.

## Document Requirements by Level of Change

UIDAI classifies name changes into levels. Each level requires different supporting documents:

### Level 1: Minor Correction (Up to 2 Letters Change)

Small spelling fixes — adding, removing, or changing up to 2 characters.

**Example:** "Amit" → "Amith", "Priya" → "Priy"

**Accepted Documents (any one):**
- Passport
- PAN card
- Voter ID (EPIC)
- Driving licence
- Ration card
- Bank passbook or statement with photo
- Government-issued photo ID card
- NREGA job card
- Pension card with photo

### Level 2: Moderate Correction (3 or More Letters Change)

Larger spelling corrections or adding middle name / surname.

**Example:** "S Kumar" → "Suresh Kumar", "Anita Devi" → "Anitha Devika"

**Accepted Documents (any one):**
- Passport
- PAN card
- Voter ID (EPIC)
- Driving licence
- SSLC / Class 10 certificate or mark sheet
- Birth certificate (government-issued)
- Bank passbook with photo

> **Tip:** For Level 2 changes, educational certificates (Class 10 mark sheet) have the highest approval rate because they are hard to forge and clearly show your full legal name.

### Level 3: Complete Name Change

Changing your name entirely — typically after marriage, divorce, adoption, or personal choice.

**Example:** "Fatima Khan" → "Fatima Siddiqui" (after marriage)

**Required Documents (must provide):**
- **Gazette notification** of name change (published in official government gazette), OR
- **Court order** for name change
- **Plus** one supporting ID proof showing the new name (passport, PAN, voter ID, or driving licence in the new name)

> **Complete name changes can only be processed at an Aadhaar Enrolment Centre**, not online. Visit [appointments.uidai.gov.in](https://appointments.uidai.gov.in) to book a slot.

## Common Rejection Reasons on SSUP Portal

### 1. Wrong Document Category
- **Error:** "Document not valid for requested change"
- **Cause:** You uploaded a document not accepted for your level of name change
- **Fix:** Match your document to the correct level above. For changes of 3+ letters, use SSLC certificate or passport — not a utility bill or rent agreement

### 2. Name on Document Doesn't Match Requested Name
- **Error:** "Name on proof does not match requested name"
- **Cause:** The name on your uploaded document is different from the corrected name you entered
- **Fix:** The corrected name you type must **exactly match** the name printed on your supporting document — letter for letter, including initials and spacing

### 3. Blurry or Unreadable Upload
- **Error:** "Document not readable" or "Poor quality scan"
- **Cause:** Image is too dark, blurry, or cropped
- **Fix:** Scan at **300 DPI minimum**, JPEG or PNG format, file size under **2 MB**. All text and photo must be clearly visible

### 4. Attempting Full Name Change Online
- **Error:** "Requested update cannot be processed online"
- **Cause:** SSUP only supports corrections, not complete name changes
- **Fix:** Visit an Aadhaar Enrolment Centre with gazette notification or court order

### 5. Multiple Pending Requests
- **Error:** "Update request already pending"
- **Cause:** A previous name update request is still being processed
- **Fix:** Check status at [ssup.uidai.gov.in](https://ssup.uidai.gov.in) → "Check Update Status". Wait for it to complete or get rejected before re-applying

### 6. Aadhaar Locked or Biometric Issues
- **Error:** "Unable to authenticate" or "Biometric verification failed"
- **Cause:** Your Aadhaar biometric is locked, or OTP authentication failed
- **Fix:** Unlock biometrics at [resident.uidai.gov.in](https://resident.uidai.gov.in) and ensure your registered mobile number is active for OTP

### 7. Name Change Limit Exceeded
- **Error:** "Maximum name updates exceeded"
- **Cause:** UIDAI allows only **2 name changes** over the lifetime of an Aadhaar number
- **Fix:** If you've exhausted your limit, write to the **UIDAI Regional Office** with supporting documents for an exception (see escalation section below)

## How to Re-Apply Online (SSUP Portal)

Follow these steps for corrections (Level 1 and Level 2 only):

1. Visit [ssup.uidai.gov.in](https://ssup.uidai.gov.in)
2. Enter your **12-digit Aadhaar number** and request OTP
3. Enter OTP received on your registered mobile
4. Select **"Name"** as the field to update
5. Type your **corrected name exactly as shown on your proof document**
6. Upload the supporting document — scan must be clear, complete, and under 2 MB
7. Submit and note the **Update Request Number (URN)**
8. Track status using URN at the same portal

**Pro Tips for Approval:**
- Double-check spelling before submitting — even one extra space can cause rejection
- Use a document where your name is printed (not handwritten)
- If your first attempt was rejected, use a **different, stronger document** for re-application
- Wait at least **48 hours** after a rejection before re-applying

## How to Apply at Aadhaar Enrolment Centre

For Level 3 (complete name change) or if online keeps getting rejected:

1. Book appointment at [appointments.uidai.gov.in](https://appointments.uidai.gov.in)
2. Visit the centre with:
   - **Original documents** (for verification) + **photocopies**
   - Gazette notification / court order (for complete name change)
   - Your Aadhaar card or Aadhaar number
3. Fill the **Aadhaar Update/Correction Form** at the centre
4. Submit biometrics (fingerprint + iris scan)
5. Collect the **acknowledgement slip** with URN
6. Update typically takes **7–15 working days**
7. Fee: ₹50 (paid at the centre)

## How to Escalate to UIDAI Regional Office

If your correction keeps getting rejected despite correct documents, escalate:

### Step 1: File a Complaint Online
- Visit [uidai.gov.in/en/contact-support.html](https://uidai.gov.in/en/contact-support.html)
- Call UIDAI toll-free: **1947**
- Email: help@uidai.gov.in

### Step 2: Write to UIDAI Regional Office
- Find your regional office at [uidai.gov.in](https://uidai.gov.in) → "About UIDAI" → "Regional Offices"
- Send a written application with:
  - Copy of Aadhaar card
  - URN of rejected request(s)
  - Supporting documents (attested photocopies)
  - A clear explanation of the correction needed and why it was rejected

### Step 3: Grievance Portal
- File a formal grievance at [pgportal.gov.in](https://pgportal.gov.in) (Centralised Public Grievance Redress and Monitoring System)
- Select "Ministry of Electronics and IT" → "UIDAI"
- Attach all documents and previous rejection details
- You'll get a tracking number and typically receive a response within **30 days**

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How many times can I change my name on Aadhaar?**
A: UIDAI allows a maximum of **2 name updates**. After that, changes require special approval from the regional office.

**Q: Can I correct my name online if my mobile number isn't linked to Aadhaar?**
A: No. SSUP requires OTP authentication via your registered mobile. First [update your mobile number](/guide/aadhar-mobile-number-update-fix) and then apply for name correction.

**Q: How long does a name correction take?**
A: Online corrections via SSUP take **5–10 working days**. At an Aadhaar centre, it takes **7–15 working days**. Escalated cases may take up to **30–60 days**.

**Q: Is there a fee for name correction?**
A: Online corrections via SSUP are **free**. At an Aadhaar centre, the fee is **₹50**.

**Q: My name was correct earlier but got changed after a biometric update. What do I do?**
A: This is a known issue. Visit an Aadhaar Enrolment Centre with your old Aadhaar letter and a valid ID proof showing the correct name. File a complaint at UIDAI helpline **1947** simultaneously.
