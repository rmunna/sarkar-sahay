# Madhya Pradesh Ration Card Apply Online 2026

The Madhya Pradesh ration card is issued by the **Department of Food, Civil Supplies & Consumer Protection, MP**. Ration cards in MP are linked to the **Samagra ID** — the state's family registry. You must have a Samagra ID before applying for an MP ration card.

> **Quick summary:** Get Samagra ID first at [samagra.gov.in](https://samagra.gov.in), then apply for ration card at [rationmitra.nic.in](https://rationmitra.nic.in). Each person in PHH family gets 5 kg grain/month free.

---

## Ration Card Types in Madhya Pradesh

| Type | Category | Entitlement |
|------|----------|-------------|
| **BPL (Below Poverty Line)** | NFSA eligible families | 5 kg wheat/rice per person/month free |
| **AAY (Antyodaya Anna Yojana)** | Poorest of the poor | 35 kg grain/family/month free |
| **Patra Grihastha (PHH)** | Priority Household | 5 kg grain/person/month @ ₹1/kg |
| **APL** | Non-BPL families | Limited subsidy or market rate |

---

## Eligibility

**BPL/PHH ration card:**
- Family income below ₹1.20 lakh/year (rural) or ₹1.50 lakh/year (urban)
- Not a regular government employee
- No income taxpayer in family
- No motorised 4-wheeler owned
- No land over 2.5 acres irrigated (rural criteria)
- Must have a valid **Samagra ID** (family and individual)

**AAY:**
- Landless labourers with no regular income
- Old age pension recipients living alone
- Widows without family support
- Tribal families in remote areas

---

## Samagra ID — Mandatory First Step

Samagra ID is Madhya Pradesh's family identification system. Every family member must be registered.

**If you don't have Samagra ID:**
1. Visit [samagra.gov.in](https://samagra.gov.in)
2. Click "Register New Member" or visit your Gram Panchayat / Ward Office
3. Provide Aadhaar, name, DOB, and family details
4. Get your 9-digit Samagra family ID and 8-digit member ID

---

## Documents Required

- **Samagra ID** (family ID — mandatory)
- Aadhaar cards of all family members
- Residence proof (electricity bill or property tax receipt)
- Income certificate from Tehsildar/SDM
- Passport-size photographs
- Caste certificate (if SC/ST/OBC)
- Bank passbook (for DBT linkage)

## How to Apply Online

**Method 1: Rationmitra Portal**

1. Go to [rationmitra.nic.in](https://rationmitra.nic.in)
2. Click **"Apply for Ration Card"** (नवीन राशन कार्ड आवेदन)
3. Enter your **Samagra Family ID**
4. Family member details will auto-populate from Samagra database
5. Select ration card category applied for
6. Upload income certificate and address proof
7. Submit — note the acknowledgement number

**Method 2: Lok Seva Kendra (LSK) / CSC**

1. Visit your nearest Lok Seva Kendra
2. Operator fills form with your Samagra ID and documents
3. Application is submitted online
4. Service charge: approximately ₹30–50

**Method 3: Gram Panchayat / Ward Office**

1. Submit application form with all documents
2. Gram Sachiv/Ward Clerk forwards to Food Inspector
3. Verification and forwarding to District Supply Officer

## Status Check

1. Go to [rationmitra.nic.in](https://rationmitra.nic.in)
2. Click **"Application Status"**
3. Enter Samagra ID or acknowledgement number
4. View status — received / under verification / approved

**SMS check:** Send `RC STATUS <Samagra ID>` to 7738299899 (NFSA helpline — may vary)

## Processing Time

- Local verification: 7–15 days
- Block / District approval: 15–30 days
- Total: **30–45 days**

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I have Samagra ID but no ration card. How do I apply?

Go to rationmitra.nic.in or visit your nearest Lok Seva Kendra with your Samagra ID and apply for new ration card.

### My name is in Samagra but not on the ration card. What do I do?

Apply for member addition at rationmitra.nic.in or Lok Seva Kendra. The member must already be in the Samagra family database.

### Can I check MP ration card status by Samagra ID?

Yes — at rationmitra.nic.in, use Samagra ID to check your ration card and application status.
