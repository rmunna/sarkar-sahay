# Jharkhand Income Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **income certificate** (आय प्रमाण पत्र — Aay Praman Patra) in Jharkhand is issued by the Revenue Department (Circle Officer / Anchal Adhikari) and is required for post-matric scholarships (SC/ST/OBC), tribal welfare scheme eligibility, EWS reservation, and government college fee waivers. Applications are processed through **Jharsewa** — Jharkhand's e-District platform.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [jharsewa.jharkhand.gov.in](https://jharsewa.jharkhand.gov.in) or at **Pragya Kendra / CSC**. Issued by Circle Officer within **30 days**.

---

## Why You Need Jharkhand Income Certificate

- **Post-matric SC/ST scholarship** (state + NSP) — income proof mandatory
- **OBC scholarship** — income-based eligibility
- **Jharkhand tribal welfare schemes** — income verification for Adivasi community benefits
- **EWS reservation** — income < ₹8 lakh/year
- **JCECEB / NEET state counselling** — income certificate for reserved category
- **PMAY / housing scheme** — income-based prioritization
- **Chief Minister's scholarship / excellence scheme** — income criterion

---

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory)
- **Ration card** (shows family composition and category)
- Self-declaration of annual income (all sources)
- For salaried: **salary certificate** from employer or Form 16
- For farmers: **land records / khatian** (from Circle Office)
- **Voter ID** (address proof)
- Passport-size photograph

> For **tribal (ST) applicants**: the Circle Officer may also verify through the Gram Sabha panchayat records. Having a Gram Pradhan letter can expedite verification.

---

## How to Apply Online — Jharsewa Portal

1. Go to [jharsewa.jharkhand.gov.in](https://jharsewa.jharkhand.gov.in)
2. Click **"Register Yourself"** → register with mobile number
3. Log in → select **"Revenue and Land Reforms"** → **"Income Certificate"**
4. Fill form:
   - Applicant name, address (district, sub-division, block, panchayat)
   - Annual family income (all sources declared)
   - Purpose of certificate
5. Upload documents (Aadhaar, income proof, photograph)
6. Pay fee: **₹25–40** (online or at kiosk)
7. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via Pragya Kendra / CSC

Jharkhand's **Pragya Kendras** (government service kiosks at block level) handle Jharsewa applications:

1. Visit nearest **Pragya Kendra** or CSC
2. Carry Aadhaar, ration card, income proof documents
3. Operator fills and submits income certificate request
4. Service charge: approximately ₹30–60
5. Collect acknowledgement receipt

---

## Status Check

1. Go to [jharsewa.jharkhand.gov.in](https://jharsewa.jharkhand.gov.in)
2. Click **"Track Application Status"** → enter Application Reference Number
3. Or log in → **"My Applications"** → view status

## Download Jharkhand Income Certificate

Once approved:
1. Log in to Jharsewa → **"My Applications"** → click approved income certificate
2. Download digitally signed PDF
3. Valid for Jharkhand state scholarships and NSP applications

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Income limit for Jharkhand SC/ST scholarship?

For **Jharkhand state SC/ST post-matric scholarship**: income < **₹2.5 lakh/year**. For **NSP central scholarship for SC**: income < ₹2.5 lakh/year. For **OBC post-matric (central NSP)**: income < ₹1.5 lakh/year (check latest NSP guidelines, may vary).

### What is the Circle Officer's role in Jharkhand?

The **Circle Officer (Anchal Adhikari)** is the primary revenue officer at the sub-division level in Jharkhand. They function as the Tehsildar equivalent — verifying and issuing certificates including income, caste, and domicile certificates. Their office is at the Anchal level (block headquarters).
