# Gujarat Ration Card Apply Online 2026

The Gujarat ration card is issued by the **Food, Civil Supplies and Consumer Affairs Department, Gujarat**. Gujarat uses the **IPDS (Integrated PDS)** system and Digital Gujarat portal for ration card management. The state has a well-digitised system with Aadhaar-linked biometric verification at FPS (Fair Price Shops).

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [digitalgujarat.gov.in](https://digitalgujarat.gov.in) or visit nearest Antyoday Seva Kendra. Gujarat priority households get 5 kg grain/person/month free under PM GKAY.

---

## Ration Card Types in Gujarat

| Card | Category | Colour | Entitlement |
|------|----------|--------|-------------|
| **Priority Household (PHH)** | NFSA BPL families | Yellow | 5 kg grain/person/month free (PM GKAY) |
| **Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY)** | Poorest families | Yellow (special) | 35 kg/family/month free |
| **Non-Priority (NPH/APL)** | Above poverty line | White | Market rate / limited subsidy |
| **DBTL / White Card** | Non-subsidised LPG linked | White | For LPG subsidy tracking |

---

## Eligibility

**PHH/BPL ration card:**
- Annual family income below ₹1.50 lakh (rural) or ₹2 lakh (urban)
- Not a government employee (central or state)
- No income taxpayer in family
- No 4-wheeler motorised vehicle
- No land above 5 acres (unirrigated) or 2.5 acres (irrigated)
- Aadhaar card mandatory for all members

**AAY:**
- Landless labourers, destitute households
- Widow/elderly living alone
- Disability pensioners with no other income
- Rural tribal families with minimal income

---

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar cards (all family members — mandatory)
- Residence proof (electricity bill, gas bill, rent agreement)
- Passport-size photographs
- Income certificate from Mamlatdar/District Collector office
- Caste certificate (if SC/ST/OBC)
- Death/divorce certificate (if applicable for family changes)
- Bank account passbook (for DBT)

## How to Apply Online

**Method 1: Digital Gujarat Portal (Recommended)**

1. Go to [digitalgujarat.gov.in](https://digitalgujarat.gov.in)
2. Search for **"Ration Card"** in the services section
3. Click **"Apply for New Ration Card"**
4. Register/log in with your Aadhaar-linked mobile number
5. Fill in family member details, upload documents
6. Submit and save the acknowledgement number
7. Download the acknowledgement receipt

**Method 2: IPDS Gujarat Portal**

1. Go to [ipds.gujarat.gov.in](https://ipds.gujarat.gov.in)
2. Select **"Citizen Services"**
3. Navigate to new ration card application
4. Fill form with Aadhaar details and submit

**Method 3: Antyoday Seva Kendra / CSC / Taluka Mamlatdar Office**

1. Visit nearest Antyoday Seva Kendra (government CSC)
2. Operator fills online application with your documents
3. Biometric verification may be done on the spot
4. Application forwarded to Taluka Mamlatdar for verification

## Status Check

1. Go to [digitalgujarat.gov.in](https://digitalgujarat.gov.in) or [ipds.gujarat.gov.in](https://ipds.gujarat.gov.in)
2. Click **"Application Status"** or **"Track Application"**
3. Enter acknowledgement number or Aadhaar number
4. View current status

**SMS:** Contact your Mamlatdar office for status updates.

## Download Gujarat Ration Card

Once approved:
1. Log in to [digitalgujarat.gov.in](https://digitalgujarat.gov.in)
2. Go to **"Ration Card"** → **"Download"**
3. Download digital ration card PDF — valid at all FPS

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between white card and yellow card in Gujarat?

Yellow card = Priority Household (BPL/AAY) — eligible for subsidised/free grain. White card = Non-priority (APL) — generally for market-rate purchases but required for identity and LPG DBTL purposes.

### Can I apply without going to an office?

Yes — through digitalgujarat.gov.in you can apply online. However, Aadhaar-based biometric verification at the Seva Kendra may be needed for final approval.

### My Aadhaar address is in a different state. Can I apply in Gujarat?

Yes — if you're residing in Gujarat, you can apply using current address proof from Gujarat even if your Aadhaar shows another state address.
