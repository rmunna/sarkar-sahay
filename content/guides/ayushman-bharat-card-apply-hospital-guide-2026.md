# Ayushman Bharat Card 2026 — Apply, Eligibility & Hospital Guide

**Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB-PMJAY)** provides **free health coverage of ₹5 lakh per family per year** at empaneled government and private hospitals. It's the world's largest government-funded health insurance scheme, covering 50+ crore Indians from low-income families.

---

## Who Is Eligible for Ayushman Bharat?

Eligibility is based on **SECC 2011** (Socio-Economic and Caste Census) data:

**Rural Families (PMJAY):**
- Scheduled caste / Scheduled tribe families
- Families with no adult member between ages 16–59
- Female-headed households with no adult male
- Families with a disabled member
- Manual scavenger families
- Bonded labour families
- Families without shelter / kachha (non-pucca) house

**Urban Families (PMJAY):**
- Ragpickers, domestic workers, construction workers
- Street vendors, cobblers, hawkers, other service workers
- Plumbers, masons, electricians, washermen, home-based workers
- Beggars and dependents

**AB-PMJAY (2024 expansion):**
- Senior citizens aged 70+ are now also eligible regardless of income (new 2024 addition)

## Benefits of Ayushman Bharat Card

| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Coverage amount** | ₹5 lakh per family per year |
| **Treatment** | Fully cashless at empaneled hospitals |
| **Diseases covered** | 1,900+ medical procedures |
| **Pre-existing conditions** | Covered from Day 1 |
| **No waiting period** | Immediate coverage upon card issuance |
| **Family size** | Covers entire family (no member cap) |
| **Government + Private hospitals** | Both empaneled under AB-PMJAY |

**Major diseases covered:**
Cancer treatment, heart surgery, orthopedic procedures, kidney dialysis, neurosurgery, maternity (complex), cataract, joint replacement — and 1,900 more.

## Check Ayushman Bharat Eligibility

**Method 1: Online**
1. Go to **[beneficiary.nha.gov.in](https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in)**
2. Enter mobile number → OTP
3. Enter Aadhaar number → OTP on Aadhaar-linked mobile
4. **Eligibility status shown**

**Method 2: Toll-free Helpline**
- Call **14555** or **1800-111-565** (PMJAY helpline)
- Provide: Name, Aadhaar, SECC details → Agent checks eligibility

**Method 3: PMJAY App**
- Download PMJAY Mitra App (or similar state-specific app)
- Enter Aadhaar → Check eligibility

## Apply for Ayushman Card — How to Get It

### At Ayushman Mitra Kendra / Common Service Centre (CSC):

1. Visit nearest **Ayushman Mitra** (at hospitals) or **CSC (Jan Seva Kendra)**
2. Carry: Aadhaar card + family ration card (or SECC data reference)
3. Ayushman Mitra verifies eligibility
4. Biometric authentication (fingerprint/OTP)
5. **Ayushman Card issued immediately** (digital format)
6. Physical card optionally printed

### At Empaneled Hospital:

1. Visit any PMJAY-empaneled hospital
2. Ask for "Ayushman Mitra" at the hospital counter
3. They verify eligibility + issue card + get you admitted

## Download Ayushman Card Online

If already registered:
1. [beneficiary.nha.gov.in](https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in) → Login with mobile OTP
2. View + download your Ayushman Card (PDF)
3. Store digitally — no physical card needed for cashless treatment

## How to Use Ayushman Bharat for Treatment

1. Visit any **PMJAY-empaneled hospital** (government or private)
2. Show Ayushman Card at hospital counter
3. Hospital verifies on PMJAY portal
4. **Cashless treatment authorized** up to ₹5 lakh
5. Hospital bills directly to PMJAY — you pay nothing

**Find empaneled hospitals:** pmjay.gov.in → "Find Hospital" → Enter location → All empaneled hospitals listed.

## Ayushman Bharat — Senior Citizens (70+)

The 2024 expansion added senior citizens (70+) regardless of income:

- **AB PM-JAY Senior Citizen scheme**: ₹5 lakh coverage
- Existing AB-PMJAY family members: ₹5 lakh (existing) + ₹5 lakh for senior = ₹10 lakh for senior citizen in PMJAY family
- How to apply: beneficiary.nha.gov.in → Senior Citizen application
- Aadhaar-based verification

## Frequently Asked Questions

**I'm eligible per SECC but not getting card — why?**
If eligible but card not generating: (1) Aadhaar linkage pending — link Aadhaar at CSC, (2) Name/DOB mismatch between Aadhaar and SECC records — visit CSC for correction, (3) Call PMJAY helpline 14555.

**Can I use Ayushman Bharat card outside my home state?**
Yes — AB-PMJAY is portable across India. Show your card at any empaneled hospital in any state. Hospital verifies online — cashless treatment works pan-India.

**I'm not in SECC 2011 — any way to get Ayushman Bharat?**
For those not in SECC: Check your state scheme (many states have broader coverage). Senior citizens (70+) are now separately eligible. BPL cardholders may be added in some states. Check pmjay.gov.in for latest inclusions.

**What if treatment costs exceed ₹5 lakh?**
For the additional amount: Private hospitals may charge you directly. For government hospitals: Treatment continues without additional cost (government bears extra). Consider super top-up private insurance for critical illnesses if you anticipate high-cost treatment.

**Ayushman Bharat coverage — does it include all medicines, tests?**
Coverage includes surgery, hospitalization, medicines for the covered condition during the hospital stay, post-hospitalization follow-up (3 days), and pre-hospitalization (3 days). OPD (outpatient / doctor consultation without hospitalization) is NOT covered by standard AB-PMJAY.
