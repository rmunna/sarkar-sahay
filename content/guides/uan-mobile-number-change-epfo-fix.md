# UAN Mobile Number Change 2026 — Fix Old or Wrong Mobile in EPF

If your **UAN (Universal Account Number)** has an old or wrong mobile number registered, you can't receive OTPs for:
- UAN portal login
- EPF withdrawal
- EPF transfer
- KYC verification

Here's how to change the mobile number in your UAN, depending on your current employment status.

## Which Method to Use?

| Situation | Best Method |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Currently employed** | Ask HR/employer to update mobile via employer portal |
| **Aadhaar mobile matches the new mobile you want** | Self-update via member portal using Aadhaar OTP |
| **Neither employed nor Aadhaar mobile matches** | File grievance with EPFO |
| **Joint declaration needed** | Submit via employer + joint declaration form |

---

## Method 1: Employer Updates Mobile (Easiest — If Currently Employed)

Your current employer has access to update your UAN contact details:

1. Email or contact your **HR / Payroll / Admin team** with:
   - Your UAN number
   - New mobile number
   - Request: "Please update my mobile number in the EPFO employer portal"
2. HR logs into the **EPFO employer portal** and updates your mobile under "Manage Employee Details"
3. Takes 2–7 working days for the update to reflect
4. You'll receive confirmation SMS on your new mobile

**This is the fastest method** — no documentation needed beyond your request to HR.

---

## Method 2: Self-Update via Aadhaar OTP (If Aadhaar Mobile Is Correct)

This works when your **current Aadhaar-registered mobile** is the same as the new mobile you want for UAN:

1. Go to **[unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in/)**
2. Login with your UAN + Password (if you can login — if old mobile OTP is needed to login, this won't work directly)
3. Go to **"Manage"** → **"Contact Details"**
4. Click **"Update Mobile Number"**
5. Enter new mobile → OTP sent to **Aadhaar-linked mobile** (NOT the old UAN mobile)
6. Enter OTP → verify → mobile updated

**Condition**: Your Aadhaar must have the mobile number you want to register. This is because EPFO uses Aadhaar OTP to authenticate the update.

---

## Method 3: Can't Login Due to Old Mobile? Use Virtual ID Method

If you **can't even login** because the old mobile OTP is required:

1. Get your **EPFO UAN** from your salary slip, Form 16, or old EPF passbook
2. Go to UAN portal → **"Forgot Password"** → try to reset using Aadhaar OTP
3. Once logged in via Aadhaar OTP: update mobile via Manage → Contact Details (same as Method 2)

---

## Method 4: Raise EPFO Grievance (No Active Employer & Aadhaar Mobile Also Mismatch)

If you've changed mobile, don't have an active employer, and Aadhaar also doesn't have the new mobile:

1. **First update Aadhaar mobile**: Visit Aadhaar Seva Kendra with current mobile to update Aadhaar (takes 30–90 days)
2. Once Aadhaar has new mobile, use Method 2 above

**Alternative — Grievance Route:**
1. Visit **[epfigms.gov.in](https://epfigms.gov.in/)** (EPFO Grievance Portal)
2. Click **"Register Grievance"**
3. Select category: **"KYC/Service Details"** → **"Updation of Contact Details"**
4. Upload supporting documents:
   - Aadhaar card (with new mobile linked)
   - Proof of new mobile number (mobile bill, Jio/Airtel ID card)
   - PAN card for identity
5. Submit grievance; EPFO will call back on the new mobile to verify and update

Grievance processing: **15–30 working days**

---

## Method 5: Joint Declaration Form for Comprehensive Correction

If you need to change mobile + correct name/DOB/date of joining simultaneously, use a **Joint Declaration Form**:

1. Download Joint Declaration Form from **[epfindia.gov.in](https://www.epfindia.gov.in/)** → Forms section
2. Fill with your details and the corrections needed
3. **Get it signed by your previous employer** (even if you're no longer working there — employers are legally required to sign)
4. Submit at your **nearest EPFO regional office**
5. EPFO processes and updates all details including mobile

---

## What If I Can't Reach My Previous Employer?

For companies that have shut down or HR is unresponsive:

1. Try grievance portal (Method 4) with maximum documentation
2. Visit your **nearest EPFO regional office** in person with:
   - Aadhaar (ideally with new mobile)
   - Old salary slips showing UAN
   - Any EPF document showing your UAN
3. EPFO regional office staff can manually update in edge cases with proper documentation

---

## After Mobile Update — Test Login

Once the mobile is updated:
1. Go to UAN portal → click **"Forgot Password"**
2. Use your UAN + new mobile + Aadhaar OTP to set a new password
3. Login with new credentials

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why can't I update mobile directly without Aadhaar OTP?

EPFO requires Aadhaar OTP for mobile update as a security measure to prevent unauthorized changes. If someone else changes your mobile, they could intercept EPF withdrawal OTPs. That's why Aadhaar verification is mandatory.

### My Aadhaar is linked to wife's mobile (old phone). Can I use her OTP?

Yes — the OTP goes to the Aadhaar-linked mobile. If your wife's phone has the old number linked to Aadhaar and you want to update Aadhaar's mobile to yours, do the Aadhaar mobile update first at an Aadhaar Seva Kendra.

### I'm self-employed/freelance and never had an employer. How do I access UAN?

If you have EPF contributions from a previous employer, you still have a UAN. Use Method 2 (Aadhaar OTP) or Method 4 (Grievance) — no employer verification needed. If you never had EPF, you don't have a UAN.

### How long does EPFO take to process the mobile update?

- Employer update: 2–7 working days
- Self-update via Aadhaar: Immediate (same day)
- Grievance route: 15–30 working days
