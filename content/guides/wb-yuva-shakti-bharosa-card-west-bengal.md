# Yuva Shakti Bharosa Card — West Bengal ₹3,000/Month Youth Allowance

The **Yuva Shakti Bharosa Card** is the BJP government of West Bengal's flagship youth employment welfare scheme, announced after Chief Minister **Suvendu Adhikari** took office on May 9, 2026. The scheme provides **₹3,000 per month** to unemployed educated youth in West Bengal until they secure employment.

> **Current Status (June 2026):** The scheme has been **announced** and is in final preparation. Formal launch and enrolment opening is expected between **June–July 2026**. Check [wb.gov.in](https://wb.gov.in) for the official launch notification.

---

## Key Benefits

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| **Monthly allowance** | ₹3,000 per month |
| **Payment method** | DBT — direct to beneficiary's bank account |
| **Beneficiary card** | Yuva Shakti Bharosa Card (physical ID card) |
| **Duration** | Until employed or scheme duration ends |
| **Target group** | Unemployed educated youth of West Bengal |

---

## Expected Eligibility Criteria

Based on the government announcement, the expected eligibility criteria are:

- **West Bengal permanent resident** (domicile certificate or voter ID required)
- **Age:** 18–35 years
- **Education:** At least passed Class 10 (Madhyamik) or equivalent
- **Currently unemployed** — not in any salaried employment
- **Not a government employee** or income tax payee
- Must have an **Aadhaar-linked bank account** for DBT
- One applicant per household

> Exact income eligibility and educational qualification requirements will be specified in the official gazette notification. The criteria listed above are based on the government's public announcement and may be updated.

---

## Documents Expected to Be Required

- Aadhaar card (mandatory)
- West Bengal voter ID or domicile certificate
- Educational certificates (Madhyamik marksheet or equivalent)
- Bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked account)
- Income certificate (family income)
- Unemployment declaration / self-certificate
- Passport-size photograph

## How to Apply (When Available)

The application is expected to open through two channels:

### 1. Duare Sarkar Camps

West Bengal's doorstep service initiative (Duare Sarkar) will likely be the primary enrolment channel:
- Watch for camp announcements via your local Panchayat or Municipality
- Bring originals + photocopies of all documents
- Registration and card issuance done at the camp

### 2. Online Portal (Expected)

A dedicated online portal or integration with the existing Duare Sarkar digital system is expected:
- Visit [wb.gov.in](https://wb.gov.in) for the portal link when launched
- Register with Aadhaar and mobile OTP
- Fill application and upload documents
- Note your reference number for tracking

### 3. Block Development Office / BDO

- Visit your nearest BDO office (rural) or Municipal office (urban)
- Collect and submit the Yuva Shakti Bharosa Card application form
- Bring all supporting documents

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is this scheme live now? Can I apply today?**
A: As of June 2026, the scheme has been announced but formal enrolment has not yet opened. Watch [wb.gov.in](https://wb.gov.in) and your local Panchayat/Municipality for the launch date.

**Q: What if I am a graduate but still unemployed?**
A: Yes, graduates are eligible. The scheme targets educated unemployed youth — completing Class 10 or above qualifies.

**Q: Can a woman apply for both Annapurna Bhandar and Yuva Shakti Bharosa Card?**
A: If she meets the eligibility criteria for both (25–60 years for Annapurna Bhandar; 18–35 and unemployed for Yuva Shakti), she may apply for both. However, the government may specify exclusions; check the official notification.

**Q: How long will the ₹3,000/month continue?**
A: Until the beneficiary secures employment or the scheme duration ends. Beneficiaries are expected to report employment status periodically.

**Q: Is this scheme only for Hindus or for all communities?**
A: The scheme is for all eligible West Bengal youth regardless of religion or caste. SC/ST youth may receive priority enrolment.
