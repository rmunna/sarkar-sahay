## What is Mahtari Vandan Yojana?

Mahtari Vandan Yojana is a scheme launched by the Government of Chhattisgarh that gives **₹1,000 every month** directly to eligible married women. The money goes straight into the woman's bank account — no middlemen, no cash.

Over a full year, that adds up to **₹12,000 per year**.

The scheme started disbursing payments from **March 2024** and is currently **LIVE** — money is being transferred to beneficiaries each month.

The main goal is to help women become financially independent and improve their health and nutrition.

## Key Benefits

| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Monthly payment | ₹1,000 per month |
| Annual total | ₹12,000 per year |
| How it's paid | Direct to your bank account (DBT) |
| Who sends the money | Government of Chhattisgarh, Women and Child Development Department |
| When payments start | After application is verified and approved |

## Who is Eligible?

To get this benefit, you must meet **all** of the following:

**You ARE eligible if:**
- You are a **married woman** living in Chhattisgarh
- You are a **permanent resident** (domicile) of Chhattisgarh
- Your **annual family income is ₹2.5 lakh or less** (as per latest notification — verify at the official website)
- You are in the **age group of 21 to 60 years** (as per latest notification — verify exact age limits at the official website)
- You have a **bank account in your own name** — not a joint account

> Widowed and divorced women may also be eligible. Verify your specific situation at the official portal or your local Anganwadi / gram panchayat office.

**You are NOT eligible if:**
- You are an **income tax payer** or your family pays income tax
- You or your husband is a **government employee** (state or central)
- Your family income exceeds ₹2.5 lakh per year
- You do not have a Chhattisgarh domicile

> **Note:** The exact exclusion list should be confirmed from the official portal or your local Women and Child Development office, as criteria can be updated by government notification.

## Documents Required

Keep these documents ready before you apply:

1. **Aadhaar Card** — the woman's own Aadhaar
2. **Bank Passbook** — account must be in the woman's name only (no joint accounts)
3. **Aadhaar linked to the bank account** — this is mandatory for DBT
4. **Mobile number** — registered with your Aadhaar and bank account
5. **Marriage Certificate** or other proof of marriage (where applicable)
6. **Domicile / Residence Proof** — proof that you are a permanent resident of Chhattisgarh
7. **Passport-size photograph**

> If your Aadhaar is not yet linked to your bank account, visit your bank branch before applying.

## How to Apply — Step by Step

New applications are typically submitted **offline** through your local government office. There is no open online self-registration portal at this time.

### Step 1 — Visit Your Nearest Office

Go to any of these places:
- **Anganwadi Centre** in your area
- **Gram Panchayat office** (in villages)
- **Ward office** (in towns and cities)
- **Common Service Centre (CSC / Jan Seva Kendra)**

### Step 2 — Collect the Application Form

Ask for the **Mahtari Vandan Yojana application form**. It is free of cost. Do not pay anyone for the form.

### Step 3 — Fill the Form

Fill in all the details carefully:
- Your full name (as on Aadhaar)
- Husband's name
- Date of birth
- Chhattisgarh address
- Bank account number and IFSC code
- Aadhaar number
- Mobile number

### Step 4 — Attach Documents

Attach self-attested photocopies of all required documents listed above. Keep the originals with you for verification.

### Step 5 — Submit and Get a Receipt

Submit the form to the officer. **Ask for an acknowledgment slip or receipt** with your application number. You will need this to check your application status later.

### Step 6 — Wait for Verification

The officer will verify your documents. After verification, your name will be added to the beneficiary list and payments will begin.

> **Tip:** If anyone asks for money to help you apply, refuse. The application is completely free.

## How to Check Application / Payment Status

This is the most searched part of this scheme. Follow these steps:

### Check Status Online

1. Go to **[mahtarivandan.cgstate.gov.in](https://mahtarivandan.cgstate.gov.in)**
2. Click on **"लाभार्थी आवेदन एवं भुगतान की स्थिति"** (Beneficiary Application and Payment Status)
3. You will see a form — enter **one** of these:
   - Your **registered mobile number**, OR
   - Your **beneficiary number** (हितग्राही क्रमांक — found on your receipt), OR
   - Your **Aadhaar card number**
4. Enter the CAPTCHA shown on screen
5. Click **"सबमिट करें"** (Submit)
6. Your application status and latest payment details will appear

### What the Status Means

| Status shown | What it means |
|-------------|--------------|
| आवेदन प्राप्त (Application received) | Your form has been submitted |
| सत्यापन प्रक्रिया में (Under verification) | Documents are being checked |
| स्वीकृत (Approved) | You are a confirmed beneficiary |
| भुगतान हो चुका है (Payment done) | Money has been sent to your bank account |
| अस्वीकृत (Rejected) | Application was not approved — check the reason |

### Check Payment in Your Bank

After the government sends the payment, check your bank account:
- Via **bank passbook update** at your bank branch
- Via **missed call or SMS banking** on your registered mobile number
- Via your bank's **mobile app** or **ATM**

Payments typically arrive in the first week of each month.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. I am a widow. Can I get this benefit?**

Widowed women may be eligible for this scheme. Visit your nearest Anganwadi centre or gram panchayat office with your documents to confirm and apply.

**2. My husband is a government employee. Am I eligible?**

No. Women whose husbands are employed in state or central government service are generally not eligible. Confirm this at the official portal or local office.

**3. I submitted my application but my status shows "under verification" for many weeks. What should I do?**

You can contact the helpline at **0771-2220006** or visit your local Women and Child Development office with your acknowledgment slip to follow up.

**4. My Aadhaar is not linked to my bank account. Can I still apply?**

You should link your Aadhaar to your bank account before or immediately after applying. Without Aadhaar-linked DBT activation, payments cannot be sent to your account. Visit your bank branch to get this done — it is free.

**5. Can I use a joint bank account?**

No. The bank account must be in the **woman's own name only**. Joint accounts are not accepted.

**6. I lost my acknowledgment slip and don't have a beneficiary number. How do I check status?**

You can check your status using your **registered mobile number** or your **Aadhaar number** on the official portal — you do not need the beneficiary number if you have either of these.

**7. I was approved but I haven't received payment for 2–3 months. What do I do?**

First, verify that your Aadhaar is correctly linked to your bank account and that DBT is active on the account. If DBT is active and money still hasn't arrived, contact: **Email:** dirwcd.cg@gov.in | **Phone:** 0771-2220006

**8. Can I apply from outside Chhattisgarh if I am originally from Chhattisgarh?**

No. You must be a **permanent resident currently living in Chhattisgarh** with a valid domicile. Women residing outside the state are not eligible.
