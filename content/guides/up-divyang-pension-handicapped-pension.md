## What is UP Divyang Pension Yojana?

**Uttar Pradesh Divyang Pension Yojana** (also called Viklang Pension / Disability Pension) provides **₹500 per month** to persons with disabilities in Uttar Pradesh who meet the income eligibility criteria.

The ₹500 is combined:
- **₹300/month** — UP state government
- **₹200/month** — Central government (IGNDPS under NSAP)
- **Total: ₹500/month** paid quarterly via DBT

## Who is Eligible?

- Must have a **disability of 40% or more** (certified by government medical officer)
- Age: **18 years and above**
- Must be a **Uttar Pradesh resident**
- Annual family income: **below ₹2 lakh**
- Not receiving any other government pension for the same disability
- Not a government employee

**Types of disabilities covered** (as per RPWD Act 2016):
- Blindness / Low vision
- Deaf / Hard of hearing
- Locomotor disability (orthopedic)
- Mental illness / Intellectual disability
- Autism spectrum disorder
- Cerebral palsy
- Specific learning disabilities
- Multiple disabilities

## How to Apply Online — Step by Step

1. Go to **[sspy-up.gov.in](https://sspy-up.gov.in)**
2. Click **"दिव्यांग पेंशन"** (Divyang Pension) → **"आवेदन करें"**
3. Click **"New Entry Form"**
4. Fill in:
   - Personal details (name, date of birth, UP address)
   - Type and percentage of disability
   - Disability certificate details
   - Income details
   - Bank account for DBT
5. Upload documents (see below)
6. Submit → save the **Application Reference Number**

## Apply Offline at Block Office

1. Visit your **Block Samaj Kalyan (Social Welfare) office** or **Gram Panchayat**
2. Collect the Divyang Pension application form
3. Submit with required documents
4. Block office uploads the application online

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card**
2. **Disability certificate** with 40%+ disability rating — issued by **Chief Medical Officer (CMO)** or government district hospital
3. **Age proof** — Aadhaar, voter ID, birth certificate
4. **Income certificate** — annual family income below ₹2 lakh (from Tehsildar/SDM)
5. **Bank passbook** — in the applicant's own name, Aadhaar-linked for DBT
6. **Ration card / BPL card** (if available)
7. Passport-size photograph

## How to Get a Disability Certificate (if you don't have one)

1. Visit your **District Hospital** or **CMO (Chief Medical Officer) office**
2. Request an assessment for **disability certificate**
3. A medical board evaluates and issues a certificate with the **disability percentage**
4. If 40%+ disability: you are eligible for the pension and also for **UDID card** (Unique Disability ID)

> To get the disability certificate, bring: Aadhaar, medical reports, hospital records. Assessment is free at government hospitals.

## UDID Card (Unique Disability ID)

Along with the pension, apply for the **UDID card** at [swavlambancard.gov.in](https://swavlambancard.gov.in):
- National digital disability identity
- Used across states for all disability benefits
- Eliminates need for multiple disability certificates

## Check Application Status

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My disability is 35%. Am I eligible?
No — the minimum disability threshold is **40%**. For a 35% assessment, visit a government CMO or hospital for re-assessment if you believe the rating is incorrect.

### My old disability certificate expired. Do I need a fresh one?
Disability certificates for **permanent disabilities** generally don't expire. For progressive/changing conditions, periodic re-evaluation may be needed. Check with the CMO office.

### Can I apply for both Divyang Pension and PMJDY or other benefits?
Yes — disability pension has no conflict with Jan Dhan account benefits, PMJAY health insurance, or other schemes. You can and should avail all applicable benefits.

### I have an UDID card. Does that replace the disability certificate for pension?
UDID card information is linked to disability records. In many cases, UDID card is accepted as the disability proof. Confirm with your Block Social Welfare office.

### My application was rejected. What to do?
Check the rejection reason on [sspy-up.gov.in](https://sspy-up.gov.in). Common reasons: income above limit, disability below 40%, missing documents. Fix the issue and reapply.
