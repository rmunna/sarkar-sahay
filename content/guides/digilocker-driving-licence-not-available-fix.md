## Why Is Your Driving Licence Not on DigiLocker?

Many Indians try to access their Driving Licence (DL) on DigiLocker for use as digital ID proof — only to find it's **not available or not showing**. This happens because DigiLocker pulls DL data from the **Parivahan/Sarathi database**, and the connection depends on your state RTO's digital integration.

This guide explains **why your DL isn't appearing** and **how to fetch it**.

## Common Reasons Your DL Is Missing

### 1. Your State RTO Hasn't Digitised Records

Not all state RTOs have fully digitised their driving licence records. Older DLs issued before digital systems may not exist in the Parivahan database.

**Affected:** DLs issued before 2010 in many states, and some smaller RTOs even for recent DLs.

### 2. DL Number Mismatch

When fetching your DL on DigiLocker, the DL number you enter must **exactly match** the Parivahan database — including format, dashes, and state code.

### 3. Name/DOB Mismatch with Aadhaar

DigiLocker cross-references your Aadhaar details. If the name or date of birth on your DL is different from Aadhaar, the system may not link the document.

### 4. DL Is in "Pending" or "Expired" Status

If your DL application is still being processed, or if the DL has expired, it may not be available for digital fetch.

### 5. Parivahan-DigiLocker Sync Issues

Sometimes there's a temporary sync issue between the Parivahan database and DigiLocker servers. This usually resolves in 24-48 hours.

## How to Fetch Your Driving Licence on DigiLocker

### Method 1: Pull from Issued Documents

1. **Log in** to [DigiLocker](https://www.digilocker.gov.in) with your Aadhaar-linked mobile
2. Go to **"Issued Documents"**
3. Click **"Get More Issued Documents"**
4. Search for **"Ministry of Road Transport and Highways"** or **"Driving Licence"**
5. Select **"Driving Licence"** as document type
6. Enter your **DL number** exactly as printed on your physical DL
7. Enter your **date of birth**
8. Click **"Get Document"**

If successful, your DL will appear in Issued Documents.

### Method 2: Try Through Parivahan Portal First

1. Visit [Parivahan](https://parivahan.gov.in)
2. Go to **"Know Your DL"** or **"DL Status"**
3. Enter your DL number and date of birth
4. If your DL appears here, it's in the database and should be fetchable on DigiLocker
5. If it **doesn't appear on Parivahan**, the issue is with your RTO's records — not DigiLocker

### Method 3: Search by State Transport Department

Some states have their own issuer name on DigiLocker:

1. In DigiLocker's Issued Documents → Get More
2. Search for your **state transport department** name (e.g., "Transport Department, Maharashtra")
3. Try fetching through the state-specific issuer

## If Your DL Is Not in Parivahan Database

If your DL doesn't appear on Parivahan either:

1. **Visit your issuing RTO office** with your physical DL
2. Request them to **update/digitise your DL record** in the Sarathi system
3. Carry: Original DL + Aadhaar card + passport-size photo
4. Some RTOs may charge a nominal fee for record digitisation
5. Once digitised (takes 7-15 days), try fetching on DigiLocker again

## If DL Number Format Is Wrong

DL numbers follow this format: **SS-RRYYYYNNNNNNN** (e.g., KA-0120190012345)
- SS = State code
- RR = RTO code
- YYYY = Year of issue
- NNNNNNN = Serial number

**Common mistakes:**
- Entering spaces instead of dashes
- Using lowercase state code
- Missing leading zeros in RTO code

Try different format variations when fetching on DigiLocker.

## Fix Name/DOB Mismatch

If details don't match between DL and Aadhaar:

1. **Option A:** Get DL details corrected at your RTO through [Sarathi portal](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in) — see our [DL correction guide](/guide/driving-licence-address-change-online)
2. **Option B:** Update Aadhaar details to match — see our [Aadhaar correction guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
3. Ensure **exact name spelling and DOB** match on both documents

## Important Tips

1. **Check Parivahan first** — if your DL isn't there, DigiLocker can't fetch it
2. **Try fetching during off-peak hours** for better server response
3. **Smart card DLs** (issued after 2015) are more likely to be available than older laminated ones
4. **Renewed DLs** should have updated records — if you recently renewed, wait 2-3 weeks before checking
5. **Keep a screenshot/PDF** of your Parivahan DL details as backup proof

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Can I use my DL on DigiLocker as valid ID proof?

Yes. A DL fetched through DigiLocker (issued document, not uploaded) is legally valid as original under IT Act, 2000. Traffic police and most government services accept it.

### Q2. My DL is showing on Parivahan but not on DigiLocker. Why?

There may be a sync delay. Wait 24-48 hours and try again. Also ensure the name/DOB on your DL exactly matches your Aadhaar details linked to DigiLocker.

### Q3. Can I upload my DL scan to DigiLocker instead?

Yes, you can upload a scanned copy under "Uploaded Documents," but it won't carry the digital verification stamp. Some services may not accept uploaded copies.

### Q4. I have a learner's licence. Will it show on DigiLocker?

Learner's licences from most states are available on Parivahan and can be fetched on DigiLocker if the RTO has digitised records.

### Q5. How do I know my RTO's DigiLocker integration status?

Search for your DL on Parivahan first. If it appears there, DigiLocker integration is likely working. If not, your RTO needs to update records.

### Q6. Will my renewed DL automatically update on DigiLocker?

Once the renewal is processed and updated in Parivahan/Sarathi, the new DL details should reflect on DigiLocker. Remove the old document and re-fetch.

### Q7. I lost my physical DL. Can I get it from DigiLocker?

If your DL is available on DigiLocker, you can download the digital copy. For a physical duplicate, apply through [Sarathi portal](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in) at your RTO. See our [DL renewal guide](/guide/driving-licence-renewal-online).
