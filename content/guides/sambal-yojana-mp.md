# Sambal Yojana MP (Jan Kalyan): Benefits, Eligibility & How to Apply

The Mukhyamantri Jan Kalyan (Sambal) Yojana is a comprehensive welfare scheme by the Government of Madhya Pradesh for unorganised sector workers. It provides a safety net covering accidental insurance, education support, funeral assistance, power bill subsidy, and more — all through a single Sambal Card.

---

## What is Sambal Yojana?

Sambal Yojana, officially known as **Mukhyamantri Jan Kalyan (Sambal) Yojana**, was launched to provide social security benefits to workers in the unorganised sector of Madhya Pradesh. The scheme consolidates multiple welfare benefits under one umbrella and is accessed through the **Sambal Card**, which serves as the beneficiary's identity for all scheme benefits.

The scheme covers workers such as street vendors, construction labourers, rickshaw pullers, domestic workers, and other daily-wage earners who are BPL card holders.

---

## Key Benefits Under Sambal Yojana

| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Accidental Death Insurance** | Rs 4,00,000 to the family |
| **Permanent Disability** | Rs 4,00,000 |
| **Partial Disability** | Rs 2,00,000 |
| **Normal Death Assistance** | Rs 2,00,000 |
| **Funeral Assistance** | Rs 5,000 |
| **Education Encouragement** | Scholarships for children of beneficiaries |
| **Electricity Bill Subsidy** | Subsidised electricity for BPL families |
| **Antyeshti (Last Rites) Assistance** | Rs 5,000 |

Additional benefits include support under various linked schemes for pregnant women, newborns, and skill development.

---

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Residency:** Must be a permanent resident of Madhya Pradesh
2. **Occupation:** Must be an unorganised sector worker (daily wage earner, construction worker, street vendor, etc.)
3. **BPL status:** Must be a BPL (Below Poverty Line) card holder
4. **Age:** Between 18 and 60 years for primary registration
5. **Income:** Should not be an income tax payer
6. **Not covered:** Should not be covered under ESIC or EPF (organised sector benefits)

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- **BPL ration card** or Samagra ID
- **Samagra ID** (MP family ID — mandatory)
- **Bank account passbook** with IFSC code
- **Passport-size photograph**
- **Mobile number** linked to Aadhaar
- **Occupation proof** or self-declaration
- **Age proof** (birth certificate or school leaving certificate)

## How to Apply: Step-by-Step Process

### Online Application

1. Visit the official Sambal portal at [sambal.mp.gov.in](https://sambal.mp.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"पंजीयन हेतु आवेदन करें"** (Apply for Registration)
3. Enter your **Samagra ID** and verify details
4. Fill in personal details, occupation, and bank account information
5. Upload required documents
6. Submit the application
7. The application will be verified by the local body (Gram Panchayat/Ward)
8. After approval, the **Sambal Card** will be issued

### Offline Application

1. Visit your **Gram Panchayat** office or **Nagar Palika/Nagar Nigam** office
2. Request the Sambal Yojana registration form
3. Fill in the form with complete details
4. Attach photocopies of all required documents
5. Submit the form — verification will be done by local officials
6. Upon approval, you will receive your Sambal Card

## Processing Time

- **Registration verification:** 15-30 days
- **Sambal Card issuance:** Within 30 days of approval
- **Claim settlement:** 30-60 days after claim submission with documents

## Important Tips

1. **Samagra ID is mandatory** — Ensure your family is registered on the Samagra portal before applying
2. **Keep bank account active** — All benefits are transferred via DBT to your linked bank account
3. **Report incidents promptly** — For insurance claims, report accidents within 30 days
4. **Update details regularly** — If your occupation or address changes, update on the Sambal portal
5. **Children's education** — Register children's school details to claim education scholarships

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. What is the Sambal Card?
The Sambal Card is a beneficiary identity card issued under the scheme that enables access to all welfare benefits under Sambal Yojana.

### 2. Can organised sector workers apply?
No, workers covered under ESIC or EPF (formal sector) are not eligible for Sambal Yojana.

### 3. Is there any registration fee?
No, registration under Sambal Yojana is completely free.

### 4. Can I apply if I don't have a Samagra ID?
No, Samagra ID is mandatory. You must first register on the [Samagra portal](https://samagra.gov.in/) to get your family ID.

### 5. What documents are needed for an insurance claim?
For accidental death/disability claims, you need the Sambal Card, FIR copy, death certificate (if applicable), disability certificate from a government hospital, and bank details of the nominee.

### 6. Can women head of household apply?
Yes, any adult member of an eligible BPL family can register, including women heads of household.

### 7. How is electricity subsidy provided?
Eligible Sambal cardholders receive subsidised electricity rates. The subsidy is adjusted in the electricity bill automatically based on Sambal registration.
