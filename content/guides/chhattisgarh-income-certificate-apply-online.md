# Chhattisgarh Income Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **income certificate** (आय प्रमाण पत्र — Aay Praman Patra) in Chhattisgarh is issued by the Revenue Department (Sub-Divisional Magistrate / Tehsildar) and is required for post-matric scholarships (SC/ST/OBC), Mukhyamantri Kanyadan Yojana, EWS reservation, government college fee waivers, and tribal welfare schemes. Applications are processed through **CG e-District** — Chhattisgarh's integrated public service portal.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [edistrict.cgstate.gov.in](https://edistrict.cgstate.gov.in) or at **Lok Seva Kendra (LSK)**. Issued by Tehsildar/SDM within **7–30 days**.

---

## Why You Need CG Income Certificate

- **Post-matric SC/ST/OBC scholarship** (state + NSP) — income proof mandatory
- **Mukhyamantri Bal Bhavishya Yojana / scholarships** — income criterion
- **NEET CG state counselling** — income certificate for reserved category
- **EWS reservation** — income < ₹8 lakh/year
- **CG tribal development scheme eligibility** — income verification for Adivasi welfare
- **Mukhyamantri Kanyadan Yojana** (marriage assistance) — income-based eligibility
- **PMAY / housing scheme** — income proof for EWS/LIG

---

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory)
- **Ration card** (if available — shows family and category)
- Self-declaration of annual income (all sources)
- For salaried: **salary certificate** from employer or Form 16
- For farmers: **Khasra / Bhu Adhikar Patra** (land records from Patwari)
- **Voter ID** (address proof)
- Passport-size photograph

---

## How to Apply Online — CG e-District Portal

1. Go to [edistrict.cgstate.gov.in](https://edistrict.cgstate.gov.in)
2. Click **"New Registration"** → register with mobile number
3. Log in → click **"Revenue Department"** → **"Income Certificate"** (आय प्रमाण पत्र)
4. Fill form:
   - Applicant name, address (district, tehsil, village)
   - Annual family income (all sources declared)
   - Purpose of certificate
5. Upload documents (Aadhaar, income proof, photograph)
6. Pay fee: **₹20–40** (online payment)
7. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via Lok Seva Kendra (LSK)

Chhattisgarh's **Lok Seva Kendras** (public service centres — at tehsil/block level) are the recommended route for first-time applicants:

1. Visit nearest **Lok Seva Kendra**
2. Carry Aadhaar, ration card, income proof documents
3. Operator fills and submits on CG e-District portal
4. Service charge: approximately ₹30–60
5. Collect acknowledgement / tracking number

---

## Apply via CG Mitaan / CSC

1. Visit **CG Mitaan** kiosk or CSC (Common Service Centre)
2. Provide documents to operator
3. Application submitted on e-District portal
4. Service charge: ₹30–70
5. Receive acknowledgement

---

## Status Check

1. Go to [edistrict.cgstate.gov.in](https://edistrict.cgstate.gov.in)
2. Click **"Track Application"** → enter Application Reference Number
3. Or log in → **"My Applications"** → view status
4. Status flow: Submitted → Patwari Verification → Tehsildar/SDM → Approved

---

## Download CG Income Certificate

Once approved:
1. Log in to CG e-District → **"My Applications"** → click approved income certificate
2. Download digitally signed PDF
3. Valid for CG scholarship portal, NSP, government job EWS claims

## Processing Time

| Stage | Duration |
|-------|----------|
| Patwari verification | 5–10 days |
| Tehsildar / SDM approval | 7–20 days |
| Total | **7–30 days** |

> CG has an **RTPS-style commitment** for Lok Seva under the Lok Sewa Guarantee Act. If delayed beyond the committed timeline, apply for compensation or escalate.

## CG Lok Sewa Guarantee Act — Your Rights

Under **Chhattisgarh Lok Sewa Guarantee Act 2011**:
- Income certificate must be issued within the **notified timeframe**
- If delayed: apply to the Designated Officer or First Appellate Authority
- Repeated delays attract penalty on the responsible officer

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Income limit for CG SC/ST post-matric scholarship?

For **CG state SC/ST post-matric scholarship**: family income below **₹2.5 lakh/year** for most schemes. For **NSP central scholarship**: SC < ₹2.5 lakh, OBC < ₹1.5 lakh (check latest NSP notifications as limits update annually).

### Tribal applicants in CG — any special provision?

Chhattisgarh has a large tribal population (ST — 32%). For tribal welfare schemes under the **Tribal Development Department**, income certificates are required with additional community/tribe identity. The Gram Sabha panchayat letter or Gram Panchayat resolution letter supporting your tribal status may also be attached.

### Can I use CG income certificate for engineering admissions in other states?

Income certificates from Chhattisgarh are valid for **NSP** (national) applications anywhere. For state-specific quota in other states, the income certificate must be from that state's authority.
