# LPG Gas Connection Transfer Failed — How to Fix

Transferring your LPG gas connection — whether you are moving to a new city, changing your distributor, or taking over a connection after the death of the original subscriber — should be straightforward. But in practice, many consumers face rejections, portal errors, and endless delays. This guide walks you through every type of LPG transfer, the exact documents needed, common errors on the MyLPG portal, and how to escalate complaints.

## Types of LPG Connection Transfer

There are three main scenarios where you need to transfer your LPG connection:

1. **Transfer due to relocation** — You are moving to a new city or area and need to shift your connection to a different distributor.
2. **Transfer after death of subscriber** — The original connection holder has passed away, and a family member needs to take over the connection.
3. **Change of distributor** — You want to switch from one distributor to another (e.g., Indane to HP Gas) in the same area.

---

## 1. Transfer LPG Connection After Moving to a New City

This is the most common transfer. When you relocate, you must transfer your connection to a distributor in your new area.

### Steps to Transfer Online (MyLPG Portal)

1. Go to [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in) and log in with your **17-digit LPG ID**
2. Navigate to **"Request"** → **"Transfer of Connection"** (or **"Connection Transfer"**)
3. Select **transfer reason** as "Relocation"
4. Enter your **new address** and choose a **new distributor** from the dropdown list
5. Upload the required documents (see below)
6. Submit the request — you will get a **reference number**
7. Your **current distributor** will process a "Transfer Out" (TV-Out)
8. The **new distributor** will process a "Transfer In" (TV-In) and contact you for verification

### Steps to Transfer Offline

1. Visit your **current distributor** and submit a written application for Transfer Out
2. Surrender your existing **regulator and empty cylinders** (or pay the deposit difference)
3. Collect the **Transfer Out (TV-Out) certificate**
4. Visit your **new distributor** in the destination city with the TV-Out certificate and required documents
5. The new distributor processes the Transfer In and issues you a new consumer number

### Documents Required for Relocation Transfer

| Document | Details |
|----------|---------|
| Transfer application form | Available at distributor or MyLPG portal |
| Proof of new address | Aadhaar card, electricity bill, rent agreement, or passport |
| Proof of identity | Aadhaar, PAN, voter ID, or passport |
| Original subscription voucher | If available (not mandatory for all OMCs) |
| Surrender certificate / TV-Out | From old distributor |
| Passport-size photograph | 1-2 copies |

### Common Issues During Relocation Transfer

- **Old distributor delays TV-Out** — If the old distributor does not process TV-Out within 7 days, escalate via MyLPG complaint or call the LPG helpline (1906).
- **Deposit refund stuck** — You are entitled to a refund for surrendered cylinders and regulator. If not refunded within 15 days, file a complaint.
- **No distributor available in new area** — Use the MyLPG portal's distributor locator to find the nearest one. In remote areas, you may need to register for a new connection instead.

## 2. Transfer LPG Connection After Death of Subscriber

When the original subscriber passes away, the connection can be transferred to a legal heir or family member.

### Steps for Transfer After Death

1. Visit the distributor with the required documents
2. Submit a written application requesting transfer of the connection to the nominee/legal heir
3. The distributor verifies documents and processes the transfer
4. A new consumer ID is issued in the name of the transferee

> **Note:** This type of transfer is usually done **offline only**. Some OMCs may allow online request initiation via MyLPG, but document verification must be done in person.

### Documents Required

| Document | Details |
|----------|---------|
| Death certificate | Of the original subscriber (original + photocopy) |
| Legal heir certificate / Succession certificate | From the Tehsildar or court (if no nomination exists) |
| Nominee declaration | If the subscriber had declared a nominee with the distributor |
| Identity proof of transferee | Aadhaar, PAN, voter ID, or passport |
| Address proof of transferee | Aadhaar, electricity bill, or ration card |
| Affidavit / NOC from other family members | Some distributors require a notarized affidavit or no-objection certificate from other legal heirs |
| Passport-size photographs | 2 copies of the transferee |

### Common Issues

- **Distributor asking for court order** — If there is a valid nominee registered, a court order is **not required**. Insist on nominee-based transfer and escalate if refused.
- **Multiple legal heirs disputing** — The distributor cannot transfer until the dispute is resolved. Obtain a succession certificate from the court.
- **Connection blocked or inactive** — If the deceased subscriber's connection was inactive for over a year, the distributor may cancel it. You may need to apply for a new connection.

## 3. Change of Distributor (Same Area)

You can switch your LPG distributor without changing your address — for example, if you are unhappy with your current distributor's service.

### Steps to Change Distributor

1. Log in to [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in)
2. Go to **"Request"** → **"Change of Distributor"**
3. Select your preferred new distributor from the list
4. Submit the request
5. The old distributor will process TV-Out, and the new distributor will process TV-In

### Important Rules

- You can change distributors only **within the same Oil Marketing Company (OMC)**. For example, you can switch from one Indane distributor to another Indane distributor.
- To switch **across OMCs** (e.g., Indane to HP Gas), you must surrender and take a new connection, or request an inter-OMC transfer (subject to availability and OMC policy).
- There is usually a **minimum period** (6 months to 1 year) before you can request another change.

## 4. Fixing MyLPG Portal Errors During Transfer

Many transfer requests fail because of portal errors. Here are the most common ones and how to fix them:

### "Transfer Request Not Submitted" / Page Error

- **Clear browser cache** and try using **Google Chrome or Edge**
- Try during **off-peak hours** (early morning or late night)
- If the portal is down, wait and retry after a few hours
- Use the **MyLPG mobile app** as an alternative

### "Consumer ID Not Found"

- Double-check your **17-digit LPG ID** — it is printed on your gas passbook or delivery receipt
- If your ID was recently changed due to a distributor migration, contact your distributor for the updated ID

### "Transfer Already Pending"

- A previous transfer request may be pending. You cannot submit a new request until the old one is resolved.
- Check the request status under **"My Requests"** on MyLPG
- If the old request is stuck, contact your distributor to cancel or complete it

### "Address Proof Mismatch"

- The address on your uploaded document must match the new address you entered in the form
- Ensure Aadhaar address is updated before starting the transfer — update at [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in)

### "Document Upload Failed"

- Keep file size under **1 MB** per document
- Use **JPEG or PDF** format only
- Ensure the document is **legible and not blurry**

## 5. Updating Aadhaar and Bank Details During Transfer

When transferring your LPG connection, it is the right time to also update your Aadhaar and bank details to avoid [subsidy payment issues](/guides/lpg-subsidy-not-received-fix).

### Update Aadhaar on LPG Connection

1. Log in to [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in)
2. Go to **"Update Aadhaar"** under the customer section
3. Enter your Aadhaar number and verify via OTP
4. The updated Aadhaar is linked to your connection within 2-3 working days

### Update Bank Account for Subsidy

1. On MyLPG, go to **"Update Bank Account"**
2. Enter your new bank account number, IFSC code, and account holder name
3. Verify via OTP
4. Subsidy will be credited to the new bank account from the next refill

> **Important:** If your Aadhaar address does not match your new city address, update Aadhaar first at [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in) or visit an Aadhaar Enrolment Centre. Mismatched addresses can cause the transfer request to be rejected.

## 6. How to Complain if Transfer is Stuck or Rejected

If your LPG connection transfer is stuck, rejected without reason, or excessively delayed, you can escalate through these channels:

### Step 1: Complain on MyLPG Portal

1. Go to [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in) → **"Complaints"**
2. Select complaint type related to **"Transfer"**
3. Describe the issue in detail and submit
4. Note the **complaint number** for follow-up

### Step 2: Call the LPG Emergency Helpline

- Dial **1906** (toll-free, available 24×7)
- Provide your LPG ID and complaint details
- Ask for a complaint reference number

### Step 3: Contact the Oil Marketing Company

| OMC | Helpline | Email |
|-----|----------|-------|
| Indian Oil (Indane) | 1800-2333-555 | [indane.co.in](https://www.indane.co.in) |
| HP Gas | 1800-233-3555 | [hindustanpetroleum.com](https://www.hindustanpetroleum.com/hpgas) |
| Bharat Gas | 1800-224-344 | [bharatgas.com](https://www.bharatgas.com) |

### Step 4: File on PG Portal (Centralized Public Grievance)

1. Go to [pgportal.gov.in](https://pgportal.gov.in)
2. Register and file a grievance under **Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas**
3. Attach all supporting documents and previous complaint references
4. The ministry must respond within **30 days**

### Step 5: Approach the Consumer Forum

If all other channels fail and you have suffered financial or service loss, you can file a complaint with the **District Consumer Disputes Redressal Forum** under the Consumer Protection Act 2019.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does LPG connection transfer take?
Online transfers typically take **7-15 working days** if all documents are in order. Offline transfers may take **15-30 days** depending on distributor workload.

### Can I transfer my LPG connection to another person?
LPG connections are **non-transferable** to unrelated persons. Transfer is allowed only to a **spouse, legal heir, or family member** in case of death or legal separation.

### Will I lose my subsidy during transfer?
Your subsidy may be paused during the transfer process. It resumes once the transfer is complete and your Aadhaar and bank details are linked with the new distributor. If subsidy doesn't resume, see our guide on [LPG subsidy not received — how to fix](/guides/lpg-subsidy-not-received-fix).

### Do I need to surrender cylinders when transferring?
For **relocation transfers**, you usually need to surrender empty cylinders and the regulator to the old distributor and collect the deposit refund. The new distributor will issue fresh equipment (with a new deposit).

### Can I transfer between different OMCs (e.g., Indane to HP Gas)?
Direct inter-OMC transfers are generally **not supported**. You would need to surrender your connection with one OMC and take a new connection with the other. Check with both distributors for current policy.

### What if I don't have the original subscription voucher?
The transfer can still proceed. Inform your distributor — they can verify your connection details from their records. You may need to sign a declaration or indemnity bond.
