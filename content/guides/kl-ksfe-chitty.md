# KSFE Pravasi Chitty Kerala – Government Chit Fund Scheme

## What is KSFE Pravasi Chitty?

KSFE Pravasi Chitty is a **government-backed chit fund scheme** operated by the **Kerala State Financial Enterprises (KSFE)**, a Kerala government undertaking. The scheme is specifically designed for **Non-Resident Keralites (NRKs/Pravasis)** and also open to Kerala residents. It allows participants to save money systematically and access lump-sum amounts through auctions — backed by government security.

KSFE is the **only government-run chit fund company** in India, making it one of the safest chit fund options.

## How Does a Chitty Work?

1. A group of people (say 50) join a chit of ₹5 lakh each
2. Each person pays a **monthly installment** (e.g., ₹10,000/month for 50 months)
3. Every month, an **auction** is held — the person willing to take the lowest amount wins
4. The **winning bidder** gets the lump sum (minus the discount) early
5. The **discount amount** is distributed among all members (reducing their monthly installment)
6. This continues for 50 months until everyone has received the chit amount

### Example

- Chit value: ₹5,00,000 | Duration: 50 months
- Monthly installment: ~₹10,000 (reduced by dividend each month)
- If auction discount is ₹1,50,000 → winner gets ₹3,50,000 and the ₹1,50,000 is shared among all members

## Who is Eligible?

1. **NRIs (Non-Resident Keralites)** living abroad
2. **Kerala residents** — anyone with a Kerala address
3. Must be **18 years or above**
4. Must have a valid **Indian bank account**
5. Must provide **KYC documents** (Aadhaar, PAN, passport)
6. **No income criteria** — open to all

## How to Join KSFE Pravasi Chitty

### Online Registration

1. **Visit** [ksfe.com](https://ksfe.com)
2. **Click** on "Pravasi Chitty" or "Online Chitty"
3. **Register** with your mobile number and email
4. **Select** the chitty value and duration
5. **Upload** KYC documents (Aadhaar, PAN, passport)
6. **Enter** bank account details for installment payments
7. **Pay** the first installment online
8. **Submit** — you'll receive confirmation and chitty number

### Through KSFE Branch

1. Visit the nearest **KSFE branch** (branches available in Kerala and in some Gulf countries)
2. Fill the chitty enrollment form
3. Submit KYC documents
4. Pay the first installment
5. Receive the chitty pass book

### For NRIs Through KSFE Gulf Offices

- KSFE has offices in **Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Muscat, Bahrain, Kuwait, and Qatar**
- NRIs can visit these offices to enroll
- Installments can be paid through the KSFE online portal or through the Gulf offices

## Benefits of KSFE Chitty

- **Government-Backed:** Unlike private chit funds, KSFE is a government company — your money is 100% safe
- **Guaranteed Returns:** Even if you don't bid, you get your full chit amount at the end
- **Emergency Access:** If you need money urgently, you can bid in the auction to get early access
- **Dividend Benefits:** Monthly dividend from auction discounts reduces your effective installment
- **NRI-Friendly:** Offices in Gulf countries and online payment options

## Important Tips

1. **Start Early:** Enroll when new chitty groups open — they fill up quickly, especially Pravasi Chitty.
2. **Pay Installments on Time:** Late payments attract penalties. Set up auto-debit for hassle-free payments.
3. **Bid Strategically:** If you don't need money urgently, wait for later months when you'll get a higher amount.
4. **Check KSFE App:** Use the KSFE mobile app for installment payments, auction details, and chitty tracking.
5. **Tax Implications:** Chitty dividends may have tax implications. Consult a tax advisor.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Is KSFE Chitty safe?
**Yes.** KSFE is a **Kerala government company**. It's the safest chit fund option in India — unlike private chit funds that can collapse.

### Q2: Can I get my money before the chitty ends?
**Yes.** You can bid in the monthly auction to get the chit amount early (minus the auction discount).

### Q3: What if I can't pay installments midway?
You can request a **temporary pause** or **transfer your chitty** to someone else. However, defaulting may lead to penalties.

### Q4: Is KSFE Chitty available only for Keralites?
Pravasi Chitty is for NRKs and Kerala residents. Regular KSFE chitties are available to anyone with a Kerala address.

### Q5: How much return will I get?
If you don't bid and wait till the end, you'll receive the **full chit value**. Plus, you earn **dividends** from auction discounts throughout the period — making effective returns competitive with FDs.

### Q6: Can I join multiple chitties?
**Yes.** You can join as many chitties as you can afford to pay the monthly installments for.
