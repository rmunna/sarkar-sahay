# Kerala Ration Card Apply Online 2026

The Kerala ration card is issued by the **Department of Civil Supplies, Government of Kerala**. Kerala manages its PDS through the Civil Supplies portal and **e-Citizen** platform. Kerala is known for having one of the best-managed PDS systems in India with high-quality grain distribution, Aadhaar-linked biometric FPS, and a strong digital infrastructure.

> **Quick summary:** Apply online at [ecitizen.kerala.gov.in](https://ecitizen.kerala.gov.in) or visit your nearest Akshaya Centre. BPL (Blue card) holders get 5 kg grain/person/month free under PM GKAY.

---

## Ration Card Types in Kerala

| Card | Category | Colour | Entitlement |
|------|----------|--------|-------------|
| **BPL / Priority Household** | NFSA BPL families | Blue | 5 kg grain/person/month free (PM GKAY) |
| **AAY (Antyodaya Anna Yojana)** | Poorest families | Yellow (AAY) | 35 kg/family/month free |
| **APL (Above Poverty Line)** | Non-subsidised | Yellow / White | Limited; mainly identity use |

> Kerala's BPL card is commonly called the **Blue Card**. The APL card is referred to as the **Yellow Card** in common usage. AAY cards are separately identified.

---

## Eligibility

**BPL / Blue Card (PHH):**
- Annual household income below ₹1 lakh (general rural/urban threshold — Kerala uses own criteria)
- Not a government employee (central or state)
- No income taxpayer in the family
- No motorised 4-wheeler
- No house above 1,000 sq ft (urban) or 5 cents of land in certain urban localities
- Aadhaar mandatory for all members

**AAY:**
- Widow living alone with no income
- Destitute elderly persons
- Persons with disabilities without family support
- Landless agricultural labourers with no regular employment

---

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar cards (all family members — mandatory)
- Residence proof (electricity bill, water bill, property tax receipt, rent agreement)
- Passport-size photographs
- Income certificate from Village Officer (VO) / Tahsildar
- Caste certificate (if SC/ST)
- Bank account passbook (for DBT)
- Voter ID (for additional address verification)

## How to Apply

**Method 1: e-Citizen Kerala Portal (Online)**

1. Go to [ecitizen.kerala.gov.in](https://ecitizen.kerala.gov.in)
2. Register / log in with your mobile number (OTP)
3. Search for **"Ration Card"** services
4. Click **"Apply for New Ration Card"**
5. Fill in family head details and add all members with Aadhaar
6. Upload documents (address proof, income certificate, photographs)
7. Submit and download acknowledgement

**Method 2: Akshaya Centre (Recommended for offline assistance)**

Kerala's **Akshaya Centres** are the state's CSC network — one of the first in India:

1. Visit your nearest **Akshaya Centre** (every panchayat has one)
2. Ask for "New Ration Card Application"
3. Operator fills form with your documents
4. Biometric verification may be done on site
5. Service fee: approximately ₹30–50
6. Collect acknowledgement slip

**Method 3: Village Office / Taluk Office**

1. Visit your **Village Office** (Kerala's local government unit)
2. Meet the Village Officer with all documents
3. Application is accepted and forwarded to Taluk Supply Office (TSO)
4. Field verification by TSO Inspector

## Status Check

1. Go to [ecitizen.kerala.gov.in](https://ecitizen.kerala.gov.in)
2. Click **"Application Status"** or **"Track Application"**
3. Enter acknowledgement number or Aadhaar number
4. View status

**Via Civil Supplies Kerala:** Visit [civilsupplieskerala.gov.in](https://civilsupplieskerala.gov.in) for online ration card search by name/Aadhaar.

## e-Ration Card Download (Kerala)

Kerala issues digital ration cards accessible online:

1. Go to [ecitizen.kerala.gov.in](https://ecitizen.kerala.gov.in)
2. Log in and go to **"My Services"** → **"Ration Card"**
3. Download e-ration card PDF
4. Print or show on mobile at FPS — Aadhaar biometric verification is done at the shop

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is an Akshaya Centre in Kerala?

Akshaya Centres are Kerala's pioneering Common Service Centres (CSC) — one of India's first e-governance CSC networks. They offer government services including ration card applications, Aadhaar updates, and income certificates. Find your nearest centre at [akshaya.kerala.gov.in](https://akshaya.kerala.gov.in).

### I live in Kerala but my Aadhaar shows another state. Can I apply?

Yes — apply with current Kerala residence proof (electricity bill, rent agreement, Village Officer certificate). You must also surrender your ration card from the home state or get a no-objection certificate from the home state civil supplies department.

### How do I add a newborn to my Kerala ration card?

Visit your Village Office or Akshaya Centre with the child's birth certificate and existing ration card. Apply for "member addition" — the process typically takes 7–15 days.

### Can I update my ration card name or address online in Kerala?

Yes — log in to ecitizen.kerala.gov.in and use the ration card correction service. Minor corrections (spelling, address) can be done online; major changes (category upgrade) require field verification.
