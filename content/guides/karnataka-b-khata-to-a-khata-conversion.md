# Karnataka B-Khata to A-Khata Conversion 2026 — Namma Khate, Namma Hakku

The Karnataka government has launched **"Namma Khate, Namma Hakku"** (My Khata, My Right) — a 100-day one-time scheme that allows B-Khata property owners in Bengaluru (BBMP jurisdiction) to convert their properties to A-Khata at a **drastically reduced fee of just 2%** (down from the regular 5%).

**Quick answer:** If you own a B-Khata property in Bengaluru, apply for A-Khata conversion at [bbmp.karnataka.gov.in/BtoAKhata](https://bbmp.karnataka.gov.in/BtoAKhata) with a ₹500 application fee + 2% conversion charges on the guidance value. **Deadline: August 23, 2026** (100 days from May 16, 2026). This window is not expected to return at this rate.

---

## What is the Difference Between A-Khata and B-Khata?

| Feature | A-Khata | B-Khata |
|---|---|---|
| Official status | Full BBMP property record | Secondary register — property may have irregularities |
| Home loan | Banks readily provide | Many banks refuse or add conditions |
| Building plan approval | Available | Difficult to get |
| BESCOM/BWSSB connections | Easy | Complicated |
| Sale/resale | Clean title | Buyers hesitant, price discounted |
| Legal disputes | Low risk | Higher risk |

B-Khata properties are those where the property was built on land with some irregularity — layout not sanctioned, deviation from approved plan, property formed on agricultural land without conversion, etc. Millions of Bengaluru residents own B-Khata properties.

## Eligibility

Your property must:
- Be located **within BBMP limits** (Bengaluru Metropolitan area)
- Currently be listed as a **B-Khata / B-Register** property
- Have **property tax paid** up to date (any arrears must be cleared before applying)
- Not be under active court dispute or government acquisition

**Who is NOT eligible:**
- Properties outside BBMP limits (Peri-urban BDA areas, rural panchayat areas)
- A-Khata properties (already regularised)
- Properties under ongoing court stay orders
- Encroached government/revenue land

---

## Documents Required

**Mandatory:**
- Sale deed / title document (registered)
- Khata extract (B-Register extract from BBMP)
- Latest property tax paid receipt (up to date)
- Encumbrance Certificate (EC) — last 15 years
- Aadhaar card of property owner
- Recent passport-size photograph

**If property was purchased (not original allottee):**
- Chain of title documents (all sale deeds from first owner)
- Previous owner's Khata documents

**For inherited property:**
- Legal heir certificate or will
- Succession certificate (if applicable)

**For joint ownership:**
- Consent letter from all co-owners
- All co-owners' Aadhaar cards

## How to Apply — Step by Step

### Option 1: Online Application (Recommended)

1. Go to **[bbmp.karnataka.gov.in/BtoAKhata](https://bbmp.karnataka.gov.in/BtoAKhata)**
2. Click **"New Application"** and login with your Aadhaar / mobile OTP
3. Enter your **SAS Property ID** (from your property tax receipt) or PID number
4. System auto-fetches existing B-Khata details and property tax status
5. Verify pre-filled details — owner name, property address, area
6. Upload documents: sale deed, EC, tax receipt, Aadhaar (PDF, max 2MB each)
7. Pay **₹500 application fee** via UPI/Debit card/Net banking
8. System calculates **2% conversion charge** on guidance value — pay this online
9. Submit application and note your **Application Reference Number**

### Option 2: Seva Kendra / BBMP Zonal Offices

If you face issues online:
1. Visit nearest BBMP Seva Kendra (ward-level office)
2. Carry all original documents and photocopies
3. Staff will assist with online submission
4. Pay application fee + 2% charge at the counter (cash/UPI)

### Option 3: Government-Authorised Document Services

BBMP-empanelled document service operators (DocuPro and similar) can assist for an additional service fee. Useful if your documents need verification or legal opinion before submission.

## How is the 2% Conversion Charge Calculated?

The charge is 2% of the **guidance value** of your property:

**Guidance Value (₹) × 2% = Conversion Charge**

Example:
- Property area: 600 sq ft flat
- Guidance value: ₹4,500 per sq ft (varies by area)
- Total guidance value: ₹27,00,000
- 2% conversion charge: **₹54,000**
- Application fee: ₹500
- **Total to pay: ₹54,500**

At the regular 5% rate (after August 23), the same property would cost ₹1,35,000 in conversion charges.

Check guidance values at [kaveri.karnataka.gov.in](https://kaveri.karnataka.gov.in).

## After Application — What Happens Next?

1. **BBMP verification:** Officers verify documents and property details (7–30 days)
2. **Site inspection:** Field officer may visit the property for physical verification
3. **Approval:** If all documents are in order, BBMP issues A-Khata
4. **A-Khata issuance:** Downloadable from the BBMP portal after approval

If documents are incomplete, BBMP sends a deficiency notice — rectify and resubmit within the deadline window.

## Common Problems & What to Do

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Property tax arrears | Clear all dues at BBMP zonal office first |
| EC shows multiple transactions / disputes | Get a legal opinion; resolve before applying |
| Property area mismatch in sale deed vs BBMP records | File a rectification request with BBMP before applying |
| Documents in different name (inheritance) | Get legal heir certificate / succession certificate from court |
| PID/SAS number not found | Contact nearest BBMP zonal office with original sale deed |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**After August 23, can I still convert B-Khata to A-Khata?**
Yes, but at the regular 5% conversion charge. The 2% benefit is only during the 100-day window. It's not clear if another such scheme will come; don't wait.

**Can I get a home loan on B-Khata property?**
Most nationalised banks and major private banks (HDFC, SBI, ICICI) are reluctant to fund B-Khata properties. After A-Khata conversion, loan eligibility improves significantly.

**My property is outside BBMP but in BDA/BMRDA area — does this apply?**
This specific scheme is for BBMP jurisdiction only. Properties in BDA layouts, peri-urban areas, or rural panchayats are not covered. Check with the respective authority.

**Is there a risk of rejection after paying the fee?**
The application fee (₹500) is non-refundable. The 2% conversion charge is typically refundable if rejected. Ensure your documents are complete before submitting to avoid rejection.

**What is the SAS Property ID?**
It's a unique property identifier in BBMP's Self-Assessment Scheme system. You can find it on your property tax receipt. If you don't have one, contact your BBMP ward office.
