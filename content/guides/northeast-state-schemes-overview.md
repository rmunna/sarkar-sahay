# Northeast India State Schemes – Complete Overview

## Introduction

The eight states of Northeast India — **Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim, and Tripura** — have unique welfare schemes tailored to the region's geography, demographics, and development needs. In addition to central government schemes, each state runs its own programs covering education, livelihood, women's empowerment, agriculture, and social security.

This guide provides a consolidated overview of major state-specific schemes across all eight NE states.

## Central Schemes for Northeast

Before diving into state-specific schemes, here are key central government programs with special provisions for the Northeast:

| Scheme | Benefit | Who Can Apply |
|--------|---------|---------------|
| North East Special Infrastructure Development Scheme (NESIDS) | Infrastructure projects | State governments |
| DoNER schemes | Various development projects | Through state govts |
| PM-DevINE | Development of NE | Infrastructure & social projects |
| NEC (North Eastern Council) Schemes | Capacity building, infrastructure | Residents via state govts |

## Arunachal Pradesh

### Key Schemes

**1. Dulari Kanya Scheme**
- **Benefit:** ₹20,000 fixed deposit for every girl child born in the state
- **Eligibility:** All girl children born in Arunachal Pradesh
- **How to Apply:** Through the local District Medical Officer or hospital where birth is registered

**2. Chief Minister's Arogya Arunachal Yojana (CMAAY)**
- **Benefit:** Free medical treatment up to ₹5 lakh per family per year
- **Eligibility:** All indigenous residents with CMAAY card
- **Portal:** [https://cmaay.com](https://cmaay.com)

**3. Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Swalamban Yojana (Arunachal)**
- **Benefit:** Subsidized loans for self-employment
- **Eligibility:** Youth aged 18–45

## Assam

### Key Schemes

**1. Orunodoi Scheme**
- **Benefit:** ₹1,250 per month to women-headed households
- **Eligibility:** Women from families with income below ₹2 lakh/year
- **Portal:** [https://orunodoi.assam.gov.in](https://orunodoi.assam.gov.in)

**2. Arundhati Gold Scheme**
- **Benefit:** 1 tola (10g) gold for brides during registered marriage
- **Eligibility:** Brides from families with income below ₹5 lakh/year, marriage must be registered

**3. Kanyadan Scheme (Assam)**
- **Benefit:** Financial aid for marriage of BPL family daughters
- **Eligibility:** BPL families in Assam

**4. Assam Darshan Scheme**
- **Benefit:** Free pilgrimage travel for senior citizens
- **Eligibility:** Senior citizens of Assam (60+ years)

## Manipur

### Key Schemes

**1. Chief Minister's Saubhagyawati Scheme**
- **Benefit:** ₹25,000 one-time financial assistance for marriage
- **Eligibility:** BPL families in Manipur

**2. Manipur Start-Up Scheme**
- **Benefit:** Financial support and mentorship for startups
- **Eligibility:** Youth entrepreneurs of Manipur

**3. Chief Minister's Hakselna Tengbang (CMHT)**
- **Benefit:** Health insurance up to ₹2 lakh per family
- **Eligibility:** Families not covered under Ayushman Bharat

## Meghalaya

### Key Schemes

**1. Chief Minister's Solar Mission**
- **Benefit:** Subsidized solar panels for households
- **Eligibility:** Rural households in Meghalaya

**2. Focus Meghalaya Scheme**
- **Benefit:** Entrepreneurship support and livelihood training
- **Eligibility:** Youth aged 18–35

**3. Meghalaya Health Insurance Scheme (MHIS)**
- **Benefit:** Cashless treatment up to ₹4 lakh per family
- **Eligibility:** Families with annual income below ₹5 lakh
- **Portal:** [https://mhis.gov.in](https://mhis.gov.in)

## Mizoram

### Key Schemes

**1. New Land Use Policy (NLUP)**
- **Benefit:** Financial and technical support for alternative livelihood (away from jhum cultivation)
- **Eligibility:** Families dependent on shifting cultivation

**2. Mizoram State Healthcare Scheme**
- **Benefit:** Free healthcare at government hospitals
- **Eligibility:** All residents of Mizoram

**3. Mizoram Scholarship Scheme**
- **Benefit:** Scholarships for students pursuing higher education outside the state
- **Eligibility:** Students from Mizoram enrolled in recognized institutions

## Nagaland

### Key Schemes

**1. Chief Minister's Health Insurance Scheme (CMHIS)**
- **Benefit:** Health insurance coverage up to ₹5 lakh
- **Eligibility:** All Nagaland residents

**2. Nagaland Beekeeping Mission**
- **Benefit:** Training and starter kits for beekeeping
- **Eligibility:** Rural families in Nagaland

**3. Chief Minister's Micro Finance Initiative**
- **Benefit:** Micro-loans for self-employment and small businesses
- **Eligibility:** Self-help groups and individual entrepreneurs

## Sikkim

### Key Schemes

**1. One Family One Job Scheme**
- **Benefit:** One government job guaranteed per family
- **Eligibility:** Sikkimese families where no member has a government job
- **Note:** One of the most unique schemes in India

**2. Chief Minister's Meritorious Scholarship**
- **Benefit:** Full scholarship for meritorious students
- **Eligibility:** Top-performing students from Sikkim

**3. Chief Minister's Self Employment Scheme**
- **Benefit:** Financial assistance for self-employment ventures
- **Eligibility:** Unemployed youth of Sikkim

**4. Sikkim Subject Free Healthcare**
- **Benefit:** Free treatment at all government hospitals
- **Eligibility:** All Sikkim Subject Certificate holders

## Tripura

### Key Schemes

**1. Tripura Lakshmi Bhandar Scheme**
- **Benefit:** Monthly financial assistance to women
- **Eligibility:** Women-headed households in BPL/APL families

**2. Chief Minister's Tripura Gramin Employment Yojana**
- **Benefit:** 100+ days of employment in rural areas
- **Eligibility:** Rural households in Tripura

**3. Mukhyamantri Yuba Yogayog Yojana**
- **Benefit:** Free bicycles and transport assistance for students
- **Eligibility:** Students in Classes 9–12

## How to Apply for NE State Schemes

### General Process

1. **Check Eligibility:** Visit the respective state government website or district office
2. **Gather Documents:** Common requirements across most schemes:
   - Aadhaar card
   - State domicile/resident certificate
   - Income certificate
   - Bank account details
   - Category-specific documents (caste, disability, etc.)
3. **Apply Online:** Many states now have online portals:
   - Assam: [https://serviceonline.gov.in](https://serviceonline.gov.in)
   - Meghalaya: [https://meghalaya.gov.in](https://meghalaya.gov.in)
   - Sikkim: [https://sikkim.gov.in](https://sikkim.gov.in)
4. **Apply Offline:** Visit the District Commissioner's office, Block Development Office, or concerned department office
5. **Track Status:** Use the application reference number on the respective portal

## Important Tips

1. **Inner Line Permits:** Some NE states (Arunachal Pradesh, Nagaland, Mizoram, Manipur) require Inner Line Permits for non-residents. Ensure you have proper documentation.
2. **Tribal Status:** Many schemes have special provisions for Scheduled Tribes — ensure you have a valid ST certificate.
3. **DoNER Ministry:** The Ministry of Development of North Eastern Region ([https://mdoner.gov.in](https://mdoner.gov.in)) is a good starting point for central schemes applicable to all NE states.
4. **District Offices:** In NE states, district administration offices are often the most effective point of contact for scheme information.
5. **Language Support:** Application forms are often available in local languages — ask at the district office if needed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Are central schemes like Ayushman Bharat available in NE states?
**A:** Yes. All central schemes including [Ayushman Bharat](/guide/ayushman-bharat-pmjay-apply), [PM Kisan](/guide/pm-kisan-samman-nidhi-apply), and others are fully operational in NE states alongside state-specific schemes.

### Q2: Do I need to be a tribal to avail NE state schemes?
**A:** Not necessarily. While some schemes are exclusively for ST communities, many are open to all residents regardless of tribal status.

### Q3: Which NE state has the best welfare schemes?
**A:** Sikkim stands out with unique schemes like "One Family One Job" and free healthcare. Assam's Orunodoi reaches the largest number of beneficiaries. Each state has schemes tailored to its specific needs.

### Q4: Can people from other states avail NE state schemes?
**A:** No. State schemes require domicile/residency certificates of that specific state. You must be a resident to avail state-specific benefits.

### Q5: How do I find the latest scheme information for my NE state?
**A:** Visit your state government's official website, contact the district administration, or check the DoNER ministry portal at [https://mdoner.gov.in](https://mdoner.gov.in).
