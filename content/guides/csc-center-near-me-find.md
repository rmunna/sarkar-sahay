# CSC Center Near Me — Find Nearest Common Service Centre

Common Service Centres (CSCs) are access points for government services across India, especially in rural and semi-urban areas. Whether you need to apply for Aadhaar, PAN card, passport, or access banking services, a CSC center near you can help. Here's how to find one.

## What is a CSC Center?

A **Common Service Centre (CSC)** is a government-approved kiosk run by a Village Level Entrepreneur (VLE). CSCs are part of the Digital India initiative under the **Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)**.

CSCs are also known as:
- **Jan Seva Kendra**
- **Digital Seva Kendra**
- **e-Seva Kendra**
- **Nagrik Suvidha Kendra** (in some states)

There are over **5 lakh CSC centers** across India, covering nearly every gram panchayat.

## How to Find CSC Center Near You — Official Locator Tool

### Method 1: CSC Locator Website

The official CSC locator is the most reliable way to find a nearby center.

**Steps:**

1. Visit the **CSC Locator**: [https://locator.csc.gov.in/](https://locator.csc.gov.in/)
2. You will see a map-based interface
3. Search by any of these:
   - **State + District + Block** — for area-wise search
   - **PIN Code** — for nearby centers
   - **Village/Town name** — for local search
4. The map will show **CSC centers as markers**
5. Click on a marker to see:
   - VLE name
   - Center address
   - Contact number
   - Services available
6. Note down the address and contact number
7. Call before visiting to confirm availability and timings

### Method 2: Google Maps Search

1. Open **Google Maps** on your phone or browser
2. Type **"CSC center near me"** or **"Jan Seva Kendra near me"**
3. Google Maps will show nearby CSC centers with:
   - Address and directions
   - User ratings and reviews
   - Phone number (if listed)
   - Opening hours (if listed)
4. This works best in urban and semi-urban areas

> **Note:** Google Maps listings may not always be updated. Always verify with the official CSC locator.

### Method 3: Call CSC Helpline

If you can't access the internet:

- Call **1800-3000-3468** (toll-free)
- Tell them your district and block/village
- They will provide the nearest CSC center details

CSC centers do not have a single uniform timing. However, typical working hours are:

| Saturday | 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM |

## Fees at CSC Centers

CSC services are **not free** — VLEs charge a nominal service fee. Typical charges:

| Service | Approximate Fee |
|---|---|
| Aadhaar Enrollment | ₹50-100 |
| Aadhaar Update | ₹30-50 |
| PAN Card Application | ₹110-150 |
| Voter ID Application | ₹50-100 |
| Birth/Death Certificate | ₹30-50 |
| Banking Services | Free to ₹20 per transaction |
| Bill Payments | ₹5-20 per transaction |

> **Note:** Government-mandated fees are fixed, but VLEs may charge a small additional service fee. Ask for a receipt for every transaction.

## How to Verify if a CSC Center is Genuine

Fraudulent centers sometimes operate under the CSC name. To verify:

1. Check on the **official CSC Locator**: [https://locator.csc.gov.in/](https://locator.csc.gov.in/)
2. A genuine CSC center will have:
   - **CSC branding/signboard** with the official logo
   - A **VLE ID** — ask the operator for their ID
   - **Digital Seva Portal** running on their computer
   - **Official receipts** with transaction IDs
3. If in doubt, call the CSC helpline: **1800-3000-3468**

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Is there a CSC center in every village?
The government aims to have at least one CSC per gram panchayat. Over 5 lakh CSCs are operational. Most villages have a CSC within 5 km, but coverage varies in very remote areas.

### Q2: Can I apply for a passport at a CSC center?
Some CSC centers are linked to Passport Seva Kendras and can help with passport applications. Not all CSCs offer this service — check with the specific center.

### Q3: Are CSC services free?
Government fees are as per official rates. VLEs may charge a small additional service fee (₹10-50) for their service. Always ask for the fee breakdown before proceeding.

### Q4: How do I become a CSC VLE?
Apply at [https://register.csc.gov.in/](https://register.csc.gov.in/). You need to be 18+, have a basic infrastructure (computer, internet, printer), and clear the VLE registration process. See the CSC official website for detailed eligibility.

### Q5: Can I get banking services at a CSC center?
Yes. Many CSCs operate as Banking Correspondents (BCs) for banks like SBI, PNB, and Bank of Baroda. You can open accounts, deposit/withdraw cash, and check balance.

### Q6: What if the nearest CSC center is too far from my village?
Contact the CSC helpline (1800-3000-3468) to report the gap. You can also check if a new CSC is being set up in your area, or apply to become a VLE yourself.

### Q7: Do CSC centers provide Aadhaar biometric update?
Yes, CSC centers with Aadhaar enrollment kits can process biometric updates (fingerprint, iris, photo). Not all CSCs have this facility — confirm before visiting.
