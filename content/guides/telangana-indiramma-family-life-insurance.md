# Telangana Indiramma Family Life Insurance 2026 — ₹5 Lakh Life Cover for 1.15 Crore Families

The Telangana government announced the **Indiramma Family Life Insurance Scheme** in the state Budget 2026-27, launching it on **June 2, 2026** (Telangana Formation Day). The scheme provides **₹5 lakh life insurance coverage** to **1.15 crore families** in Telangana — at no premium cost to the beneficiaries (government-funded).

**Quick answer:** Eligible Telangana families get ₹5 lakh life insurance cover under Indiramma scheme. Enrollment through MeeSeva centres and the Telangana welfare portal. On death of the insured member, family receives ₹5 lakh insurance payout. No premium payment required from beneficiaries.

---

## What the Scheme Covers

- **Life insurance: ₹5 lakh** paid to nominee/family on death of the insured household member (head of family or designated insured member)
- Coverage is per family, not per individual — each enrolled family gets ₹5 lakh if the primary insured member passes away
- The government pays the insurance premium on behalf of all enrolled beneficiary families

---

## Who Is Eligible?

The scheme targets economically vulnerable families in Telangana. Based on government announcements:

- **Permanent resident families of Telangana**
- Families **not already covered** by government employee insurance or similar central/state group life insurance
- BPL and low-income families are priority beneficiaries
- Families enrolled in **Telangana's welfare databases** (ration card holders, PMJAY, pensions, etc.) are expected to be covered automatically

> Specific income ceiling and exclusion criteria will be published in the official scheme notification. Check [telangana.gov.in](https://telangana.gov.in) for the gazette notification.

## How to Enroll

### Option 1: Mee Seva Centre (Offline)

1. Visit your nearest **Mee Seva centre** in Telangana
2. Request enrollment for **Indiramma Family Life Insurance Scheme**
3. Carry documents: Aadhaar card, Telangana ration card / domicile proof, nominee details (name, relation, Aadhaar)
4. Complete the enrollment form
5. Collect reference number / enrollment certificate

### Option 2: Telangana Welfare Portal (Online)

1. Visit the **Telangana welfare portal** (link to be activated post-launch)
2. Login with Aadhaar / mobile OTP
3. Search for "Indiramma Family Life Insurance" under welfare schemes
4. Fill in family details and nominee details
5. Submit and note reference number

> **Note:** As of June 2026, the scheme is in the initial enrollment phase. MeeSeva is the primary enrollment channel while the online portal is being configured.

---

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** of head of household (insured member)
- **Telangana ration card** or domicile/residence certificate
- **Nominee details:** Name, relation, Aadhaar (nominee receives the ₹5 lakh if the insured dies)
- **Bank account details** of nominee (for claim settlement)
- Passport-size photograph

---

## Claim Process (In Case of Death)

If the insured member passes away:

1. Nominee should contact the nearest **Mee Seva centre** or the insurance implementing agency
2. Submit: Death certificate, insured member's Aadhaar, policy/enrollment number, nominee's Aadhaar and bank details
3. Claim is processed and ₹5 lakh is credited to the nominee's bank account
4. Processing time: typically 30–60 days from claim submission

---

## How It Differs from PMJJBY

| Feature | Indiramma Insurance | PMJJBY (Central Scheme) |
|---|---|---|
| Coverage | ₹5 lakh | ₹2 lakh |
| Premium | Government pays | ₹436/year auto-deducted from bank account |
| Target | 1.15 crore Telangana families | Any Indian with bank account (18-50 yrs) |
| Who is covered | Telangana-specific | All-India |

Telangana families can benefit from both — Indiramma for ₹5 lakh + PMJJBY for ₹2 lakh, giving ₹7 lakh total life cover.

---

## Background

The scheme is named after **Indira Gandhi** (Indiramma — "our Indira" in Telugu), consistent with the Congress government's Indiramma-branded welfare programme series. The announcement was made by Deputy CM and Finance Minister Mallu Bhatti Vikramarka in the Telangana Budget 2026-27.

The objective is to prevent financial hardship for families that lose their primary breadwinner — ensuring no child in Telangana faces deprivation due to the sudden death of a parent.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is there any premium I need to pay?**
No — the Telangana government fully funds the premium for enrolled beneficiary families. You pay nothing.

**Can I enroll even if I already have PMJJBY or PMSBY?**
Yes — Indiramma Family Life Insurance is a separate state scheme. You can hold it alongside central insurance schemes.

**What if my family already has a government employee?**
Government employees typically have separate group insurance through their service. The eligibility for Indiramma may exclude government employee families — check the official notification for specific exclusion clauses.

**Can I nominate a minor as my nominee?**
Yes — but for claim settlement to a minor nominee, the claim will be held in trust until the nominee is 18. Designate a guardian if the nominee is a minor.

**What happens if both enrolled members die simultaneously?**
The insurance covers the primary insured member. For additional coverage, consider PMJJBY (individual enrollment, ₹2 lakh per person).

**Is there an age limit to enroll?**
Specific age limits will be in the official notification. Based on the budget announcement targeting "crore families," the scheme appears broadly accessible to working-age household heads.
