# Yes Bank UPI Blocked or Not Working? Fix (2026)

Yes Bank UPI is available via the **YES PAY** app and all major third-party UPI apps. Here's how to fix all common Yes Bank UPI issues.

---

## Common Yes Bank UPI Issues

| Issue | Fix |
|-------|-----|
| Wrong UPI PIN 5 times | Reset PIN |
| Account debit restricted | Fix account block first |
| Daily limit exceeded | Wait till midnight |
| UPI registration failed | Re-register |
| Bank server down | Try after 30 min |

---

## Reset Yes Bank UPI PIN

### Via YES PAY App
1. Open **YES PAY** → **UPI** → **Manage**
2. **"Change/Forgot UPI PIN"**
3. Debit card details → OTP → New PIN

### Via PhonePe / GPay
1. App → Bank Accounts → Yes Bank
2. **"Forgot UPI PIN"** → Card → OTP → New PIN

---

## Register Yes Bank UPI

1. YES PAY → UPI → Register
2. Or: Any UPI app → Add Bank → Yes Bank → Verify mobile → Set PIN

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Yes Bank UPI safe? I heard Yes Bank had issues earlier.**
Yes Bank underwent RBI-supervised reconstruction in 2020 and is now stable. UPI transactions are governed by NPCI — your money is safe.

**Yes Bank UPI daily limit?**
₹1,00,000 per day (NPCI standard).

**Yes Bank UPI registered on PhonePe but PIN reset fails — why?**
Your Yes Bank debit card details must be correct (card number last 6 digits + expiry). If your card expired, get a new card from Yes Bank first, then reset UPI PIN with new card.
