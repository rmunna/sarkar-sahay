## What is UP Kisan Sarvhit Bima?

**UP Kisan Sarvhit Bima Yojana** is Uttar Pradesh government's comprehensive farmer insurance scheme that provides **₹5 lakh accidental death/permanent disability cover** and additional benefits to farmers aged 18–70. The **entire premium is paid by the UP government** — farmers pay nothing.

---

## Key Benefits

| Coverage | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| Accidental death | ₹5,00,000 |
| Permanent total disability | ₹5,00,000 |
| Partial permanent disability | ₹25,000 to ₹5,00,000 (as per disability %) |
| Snake bite / animal bite | ₹1,00,000 |
| Eye/limb loss (partial) | ₹25,000 to ₹2,50,000 |
| Hospitalization (accident) | Up to ₹25,000 |
| Cycle/herd/crop damage | Up to ₹1,000 |

---

## Who is Eligible?

- **Farmers registered with Uttar Pradesh agriculture department** (verified land record)
- Age **18 to 70 years**
- Landholding up to **2 hectares** (5 acres) — small and marginal farmers
- Must have **Kisan Credit Card (KCC)** or registered on upagripardarshi.gov.in

---

## How Enrollment Works

**Enrollment is automatic** for farmers registered with the UP Agriculture Department:
1. UP government pays insurance premium to the insurer (United India Insurance / empanelled insurer)
2. Farmer is covered from the date of registration
3. Farmers receive an **insurance certificate** after enrollment

If you are not yet registered, enroll at **[upagripardarshi.gov.in](https://upagripardarshi.gov.in)** or at your district agriculture office.

---

## How to Claim Insurance

### For Accidental Death:

**Step 1 — File FIR** (for road accidents, animal attacks) at local police station

**Step 2 — Get death certificate** from municipality / gram panchayat

**Step 3 — Apply at Tehsil / Collector office**
Submit:
- Death certificate
- FIR / post-mortem report (if applicable)
- Land records of deceased farmer
- Aadhaar of deceased and nominee
- Bank details of nominee (Aadhaar-linked)
- Photograph

**Step 4 — Insurance disbursement**
After verification by the District Agriculture Officer, claim is processed. ₹5 lakh credited to nominee's bank account.

---

### For Hospitalization (Accident):

- Admit in **empanelled government hospital**
- Hospital directly processes cashless treatment up to ₹25,000
- Or submit bills at district agriculture office for reimbursement

---

## Claim Timeline

- Claim must be filed within **45 days** of the incident
- Insurance company processes within **30 days** of complete documentation
- Payment directly to nominee/farmer via DBT

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. My husband died in a tractor accident. He was a farmer with 1.5 acres. How do I claim?**

Go to your **Tehsil Agriculture Office** immediately. Bring: FIR, death certificate, his land record (khasra), Aadhaar of both of you, and your bank passbook. The office will process the claim for ₹5 lakh.

**2. I am a farmer but don't have a Kisan Credit Card. Am I covered?**

Registration at upagripardarshi.gov.in is the key requirement. You don't necessarily need a KCC — register your land details on the portal or at the district agriculture office to get covered.

**3. My father is 72 and a farmer. Is he covered?**

Maximum age under Kisan Sarvhit Bima is **70 years**. A 72-year-old farmer is not covered under this scheme. He may be covered under other state/central schemes — check with the agriculture office.

**4. Does this cover crop damage due to flood?**

This scheme primarily covers **personal accident** (death, disability, hospitalization). Crop damage due to flood/drought is covered under **PM Fasal Bima Yojana** — a separate scheme. Apply on pmfby.gov.in.

**5. Do tenant farmers (those who lease land) get this insurance?**

Kisan Sarvhit Bima primarily covers landowners registered with the agriculture department. Tenant farmers should check with the district agriculture office — in some cases, tenants registered as cultivators are eligible.
