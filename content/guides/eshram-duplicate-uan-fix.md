# e-Shram Duplicate UAN? Fix Multiple Registration Problem

Discovered you have **two UAN numbers** or getting "already registered" errors on e-Shram? Duplicate registrations are a common problem that can block your benefits. Here's how to identify and fix the issue.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent platform, not affiliated with any government body. Verify at [eshram.gov.in](https://eshram.gov.in).

## How Duplicate e-Shram Registrations Happen

| Cause | How It Happens |
|-------|---------------|
| **CSC camp registration** | Operators register workers in bulk without checking existing accounts |
| **Forgot previous registration** | Worker registers again thinking they don't have an account |
| **Technical glitch** | Portal error during registration creates partial duplicate |
| **Aadhaar misuse** | Someone else uses your Aadhaar to register |
| **Multiple CSC visits** | Different operators at different centers create separate registrations |

## Step 1: Identify Your Correct UAN

First, find out which UAN is your valid one:

### Method 1: Login to Portal
1. Visit [register.eshram.gov.in](https://register.eshram.gov.in)
2. Click **"Already Registered"**
3. Enter your **Aadhaar number** + captcha
4. Verify with OTP
5. The system shows your **active UAN** — note this down

### Method 2: Check SMS History
1. Search your phone messages for "ESHRAM", "NDUW", or "UAN"
2. You may find multiple UAN numbers if registered multiple times
3. The **most recent valid registration** is usually the active one

### Method 3: Call Helpline
1. Call **14434** (toll-free)
2. Provide your Aadhaar number
3. Ask which UAN is active and which should be deactivated

## Step 2: Check Details on Each UAN

If you have two UAN numbers:
1. Log in using Aadhaar on the portal
2. Check if the displayed profile has **correct details** (name, bank account, mobile, occupation)
3. Note which UAN has the **correct bank details** — this is important for benefits
4. The UAN with accurate details should be kept active

## Step 3: Deactivate the Duplicate UAN

**You cannot deactivate a duplicate UAN yourself online.** Here are the available methods:

### Method 1: Call Helpline 14434
1. Call **14434** during working hours (Mon-Sat, 9:30 AM - 5:30 PM)
2. Explain that you have duplicate UANs
3. Provide:
   - Both UAN numbers
   - Your Aadhaar number
   - Which UAN you want to keep
4. The helpline will raise a ticket for deactivation
5. Resolution typically takes **7-15 working days**

### Method 2: Visit District Labour Office
1. Go to your **District Labour Commissioner's office**
2. Carry:
   - Aadhaar card (original + copy)
   - Both e-Shram cards (if downloaded)
   - Mobile phone for OTP verification
3. Submit written application for duplicate UAN deactivation
4. Get acknowledgment receipt
5. Follow up after 15 days if not resolved

### Method 3: File Online Grievance
1. Visit [eshram.gov.in](https://eshram.gov.in)
2. Look for **"Grievance"** or **"Complaint"** section
3. Select issue type: "Duplicate Registration"
4. Enter both UAN numbers and your Aadhaar
5. Describe the problem
6. Submit and note the grievance number
7. Track status using the grievance number

## What Happens After Deactivation

- The **duplicate UAN is permanently deactivated**
- Your **active UAN continues** to work normally
- Scheme benefits are routed to the active UAN only
- Insurance enrollment (PMJJBY/PMSBY) continues under the active account
- Download a fresh e-Shram card after deactivation is confirmed

## If Someone Misused Your Aadhaar

If you find an e-Shram registration you didn't create:

1. **Don't panic** — this is fixable
2. **Call 14434** and report unauthorized registration
3. **Visit District Labour Office** with original Aadhaar
4. **File a police complaint** if you suspect identity fraud
5. **Check other portals** — if someone misused your Aadhaar for e-Shram, check for unauthorized accounts elsewhere too
6. **Lock your Aadhaar biometrics** at [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in) to prevent future misuse

## Prevention Tips

- **Register yourself** online instead of through unknown agents
- **Never share Aadhaar** OTP or copy with unauthorized persons
- **Lock Aadhaar biometrics** when not in use at [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in)
- **Save your UAN** — if you remember your UAN, you won't accidentally re-register
- **Verify before CSC registration** — ask the operator to check if you're already registered before creating a new account

## Important Tips

- **Keep both UAN numbers** noted until deactivation is confirmed
- **Don't try to re-register** — it will fail since Aadhaar is already linked
- **Update correct bank details** on your active UAN to ensure benefits aren't disrupted
- **Follow up** on helpline tickets if not resolved in 15 days
- **Save grievance number** for tracking

## Helpline & Support

- **e-Shram helpline:** 14434 (toll-free)
- **Email:** helpdesk-eshram@gov.in
- **District Labour Office:** Contact your state's labour department
- **Hours:** Monday to Saturday, 9:30 AM to 5:30 PM
