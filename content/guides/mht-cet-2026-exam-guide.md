# MHT-CET 2026 — Maharashtra Common Entrance Test

**MHT-CET 2026** (Maharashtra Common Entrance Test) is conducted by the **MHSSCEB (Maharashtra State Common Entrance Test Cell)** for admission to engineering (B.E./B.Tech), pharmacy (B.Pharm), and agricultural courses in Maharashtra government and private colleges. Over 4 lakh students appear in MHT-CET annually, making it one of India's largest state CETs.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [mahacet.org](https://mahacet.org). PCM exam for engineering, PCB for pharmacy. Maharashtra domicile (15-year residency) required for state quota. **No negative marking.**

## Eligibility Criteria

| Criteria | Engineering (PCM) | Pharmacy (PCB) |
|----------|-------------------|----------------|
| **Qualification** | HSC (12th) with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics | HSC with Physics, Chemistry, Biology |
| **Minimum marks** | 50% in PCM (45% SC/ST/OBC Maharashtra) | 50% in PCB (45% for reserved categories) |
| **Age limit** | No upper age limit | As per PCI norms |
| **Domicile** | Maharashtra domicile for state quota | Maharashtra domicile for state quota |

### Maharashtra Domicile — 15-Year Rule

Maharashtra has the **strictest domicile rule in India**: **15 years of continuous residence** required for the state quota.

- OR born in Maharashtra AND any parent continuously residing in Maharashtra
- OR children of central government / defence employees posted in Maharashtra (check notification)

> [Maharashtra Domicile Certificate Guide](/guide/maharashtra-domicile-certificate-apply-online)

## MHT-CET 2026 Application Process

1. Go to [mahacet.org](https://mahacet.org) → **"MHT-CET 2026"**
2. Register with mobile + email
3. Fill application:
   - Personal details (name, DOB, category)
   - HSC details (school, board, PCM/PCB marks)
   - Choose PCM or PCB or both
   - Select exam centre (district)
4. Upload: photo (white background) + signature + certificate scans
5. Pay fee:
   - General (UR): **₹800** (PCM) or ₹800 (PCB) or ₹1,500 (both)
   - OBC/EWS Maharashtra: **₹600**
   - SC/ST/SBC/NT Maharashtra: **₹600**
6. Download confirmation

## Syllabus 2026

MHT-CET follows **80% Class 12 + 20% Class 11 Maharashtra State Board syllabus**:

**Physics**: Circular Motion, Gravitation, Rotational Motion, Oscillations, Current Electricity, Magnetism, Electromagnetic Induction, Electrons & Photons, Communication Systems, etc.

**Chemistry**: Solid State, Solutions, Chemical Thermodynamics, Electrochemistry, Chemical Kinetics, Coordination Compounds, Halogen Derivatives, Organic Acids & Bases, etc.

**Mathematics**: Trigonometric Functions, Differentiation, Applications of Derivatives, Integration, Differential Equations, Probability Distributions, Matrices, Statistics, etc.

## CAP (Centralised Admission Process)

After MHT-CET result, admissions happen via **CAP** (online centralised counselling):
1. **Registration** at CET Cell website
2. **Document verification** (domicile, caste certificate, HSC marks)
3. **Choice filling** (up to 300 college-branch combinations)
4. **Seat allotment** (3 rounds — CAP Round 1, 2, 3)
5. **Reporting to allotted college**

## Frequently Asked Questions

### MHT-CET vs NEET — are both needed for pharmacy?

- **B.Pharm**: MHT-CET PCB score used (NEET not required for B.Pharm in Maharashtra)
- **MBBS/BDS**: NEET score used (not MHT-CET)
- **Pharm.D**: Check specific requirements

### Students from other states — can they appear?

Yes. Students from other states can appear and get seats in the **15% open seats** (All India Quota category in CAP). Maharashtra domicile (15 years) is required for 85% state quota.

### What is a good MHT-CET score for engineering?

- **99+ percentile** — COEP, VJTI, ICT Mumbai (government top colleges)
- **95–98 percentile** — Good private aided colleges in Pune/Mumbai
- **85–95 percentile** — Decent engineering colleges across Maharashtra
- **70–85 percentile** — Tier 3 colleges, admission possible
