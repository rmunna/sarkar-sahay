## What is Udyam Registration?

**Udyam Registration** is the official, free, government registration for **Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs)** in India. It replaced the old Udyog Aadhaar Memorandum (UAM) system from July 2020. Any business — manufacturing or service — that qualifies as micro, small, or medium can register at [udyamregistration.gov.in](https://udyamregistration.gov.in) and get a unique **Udyam Registration Number (URN)** and **Udyam Certificate**.

Registration is **completely free**, takes about 15–30 minutes, and gives your business legal MSME status — which unlocks a large set of government benefits, subsidies, and protections.

## MSME Classification (Who Qualifies?)

| Category | Investment in Plant & Machinery | Annual Turnover |
|---|---|---|
| **Micro** | Up to ₹1 crore | Up to ₹5 crore |
| **Small** | Up to ₹10 crore | Up to ₹50 crore |
| **Medium** | Up to ₹50 crore | Up to ₹250 crore |

- **Both criteria (investment AND turnover)** must be within limits for the category
- The classification is based on the PAN-linked data auto-fetched from the Income Tax and GSTN portals

**Eligible:** Manufacturing businesses, service businesses, traders, artisans, professionals, sole proprietors, partnership firms, companies, LLPs, trusts, societies — all can register.

## Key Benefits of Udyam Registration

**Financial benefits:**
- Collateral-free loans up to ₹10 crore under CGTMSE guarantee scheme
- Priority sector lending — banks must lend to MSMEs
- Lower interest rates on bank loans (1–2% lower than regular commercial rates)
- SIDBI SMILE loans and other MSME-specific credit schemes

**Government procurement:**
- **25% of all central government procurement** must come from MSMEs
- Micro and small enterprises get tender preference and price preference of 15%
- Exempted from Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) in government tenders
- Registered on GeM (Government e-Marketplace) with MSME status for easier selling

**Tax and compliance benefits:**
- Exemption from certain central excise duties
- Protection under MSMED Act — buyers must pay within 45 days (or pay interest at 3× bank rate)
- Eligible for GST composition scheme benefits

**Other benefits:**
- PM Vishwakarma, PMEGP, Stand Up India — all require Udyam registration
- Technology upgradation fund (TUFS) subsidies
- Credit-linked capital subsidy scheme (CLCSS) for machinery

## How to Register on Udyam Portal — Step by Step

1. Go to **[udyamregistration.gov.in](https://udyamregistration.gov.in)**
2. Click **"For New Entrepreneurs who are not Registered yet as MSME"**
3. Enter your **Aadhaar number** (the owner's / proprietor's Aadhaar) and validate with OTP
4. Verify your PAN:
   - The portal auto-fetches your name from Aadhaar
   - Enter PAN number — it auto-fetches IT return data and GST turnover
5. Fill business details:
   - **Business name** (trade name or company name)
   - **Type of organisation** (proprietorship / partnership / Pvt. Ltd. / LLP / others)
   - **Date of commencement** of business
   - **Bank account** (current account of the business)
   - **Main activity** — manufacturing or service
   - **NIC code** (National Industry Classification code — select the one matching your business activity)
   - **Plant and machinery investment** amount
   - **Number of employees** (male/female separately)
6. Submit → you'll receive an **OTP on registered mobile**
7. Verify OTP → Udyam Registration is complete
8. **Download your Udyam Certificate** immediately or from the portal later

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** of the proprietor / managing partner / director
- **PAN card** (business PAN for companies/LLPs; personal PAN for proprietorships)
- **GSTIN** (if registered for GST — mandatory for businesses with turnover above GST threshold)
- Bank account details (current account)

> **No documents need to be uploaded.** The portal fetches all data directly from Aadhaar, PAN (Income Tax), and GSTN databases. The process is completely self-declaration based.

## Who Should Register Under Which Category?

| Type | Registration Approach |
|---|---|
| Sole proprietor / individual | Proprietor's Aadhaar used |
| Partnership firm | Any partner's Aadhaar (managing partner preferred) |
| Pvt. Ltd. / OPC / LLP | Director's / Designated Partner's Aadhaar |
| Hindu Undivided Family (HUF) | Karta's Aadhaar |

## How to Update / Amend Udyam Registration

If your business grows and crosses into a higher category (e.g., micro to small), or if details change:

1. Go to udyamregistration.gov.in → "Update Details"
2. Log in with your Udyam Registration Number and OTP
3. Update the relevant fields — turnover, investment, address, employees
4. Submit → certificate is updated automatically

## How to Download Udyam Certificate

1. Go to **udyamregistration.gov.in**
2. Click **"Print/Verify Udyam Certificate"**
3. Enter your **Udyam Registration Number** (e.g., UDYAM-XX-00-0000000)
4. Verify with OTP on registered mobile
5. Download the certificate as PDF

The certificate contains your Udyam Number, business name, NIC code, category (micro/small/medium), and date of registration. It is valid as long as the business is within MSME limits.

## Udyam Assist Platform (for Informal / Micro Businesses)

For **very small informal businesses** (street vendors, home-based artisans, small traders) who don't have GST or IT return filings, the government has launched **Udyam Assist Platform (UAP)** at [udyamassist.gov.in](https://udyamassist.gov.in):

- No GST / ITR required
- Apply with just Aadhaar and PAN
- Get a "Udyam Assist Certificate" (similar to Udyam but for non-GST micro businesses)
- Useful for PM Vishwakarma, PMEGP, MUDRA loan applications

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Udyam registration mandatory for MSMEs?
It is not legally mandatory to run a business, but it is **required to access MSME benefits** — loans, subsidies, government tenders, and welfare schemes. Without it, you are not officially classified as an MSME.

### I already have Udyog Aadhaar (old registration). Do I need to re-register?
Yes — Udyog Aadhaar and EM (Entrepreneur Memorandum) registrations were valid till December 31, 2021. If you haven't migrated to Udyam yet, you must re-register on the Udyam portal. The old registration is no longer valid.

### Can I register multiple businesses under the same Aadhaar?
No — one Aadhaar can be used for only one Udyam registration. If you have multiple businesses, each needs a separate registration (but uses the same Aadhaar of the owner).

### Is there a fee for Udyam registration?
No — Udyam Registration is completely free on udyamregistration.gov.in. Be wary of third-party websites that charge fees for this registration.

### What NIC code should I select?
NIC (National Industry Classification) codes describe your business activity. Visit [mospi.gov.in](https://mospi.gov.in) and search for your business activity to find the right NIC code. The Udyam portal also provides a search function.

### My business turnover crossed ₹250 crore. What happens?
If your investment or turnover exceeds the medium enterprise limits, you must update your Udyam registration. The business will then be reclassified as a "large enterprise" and will lose MSME status.
