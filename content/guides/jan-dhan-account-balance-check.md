# Jan Dhan Account Balance Check — Missed Call & Online (2026)

Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY) accounts provide basic banking services to millions of Indians. Whether you want to check your balance to confirm DBT payments, pension credits, or just regular savings — there are multiple easy methods. This guide covers all ways to check your Jan Dhan account balance including missed call numbers for every major bank.

## Method 1: Missed Call Balance Check (Easiest)

Give a missed call from your **registered mobile number** to your bank's toll-free number. You'll receive an SMS with your balance within seconds.

### How Missed Call Balance Works:
1. Dial the missed call number from your registered mobile
2. The call will automatically disconnect after 1-2 rings
3. You'll receive an SMS within 30 seconds showing your account balance
4. The service is completely free — no charges apply

## Method 2: SMS Balance Check

Send an SMS from your registered mobile number:

**For SBI:**
- Send **BAL** to **09223766666**
- You'll receive balance details via SMS

**For PNB:**
- Send **BAL <space> Account Number** to **5607040**

**For Bank of Baroda:**
- Send **BAL** to **8422009988**

> SMS charges may apply as per your telecom plan.

## Method 3: Check Balance via Net Banking

### SBI Online Banking:
1. Visit [https://www.onlinesbi.sbi](https://www.onlinesbi.sbi)
2. Log in with your username and password
3. Go to **"Account Summary"**
4. Your Jan Dhan account balance will be displayed

If you don't have net banking:
1. Visit SBI branch with Aadhaar and passbook
2. Request internet banking activation
3. Or register via SBI YONO app

### PNB Online Banking:
1. Visit [https://www.pnbindia.in](https://www.pnbindia.in) → **Internet Banking**
2. Log in with credentials
3. View account balance under **Account Summary**

## Method 4: Check via Mobile Banking App

### SBI YONO App:
1. Download **YONO SBI** from Play Store / App Store
2. Register using debit card or branch visit
3. Log in → view balance on dashboard

### PNB ONE App:
1. Download **PNB ONE** app
2. Register with account number and mobile
3. View balance after login

### Bank of Baroda (bob World):
1. Download **bob World** app
2. Register and log in
3. Balance visible on home screen

## Method 5: UMANG App (Works for All Banks)

1. Download **UMANG** app from Play Store / App Store
2. Register with your mobile number
3. Search for your bank's service
4. Link your account
5. Check balance and mini statement

## Method 6: At ATM

1. Visit any ATM of your bank
2. Insert your RuPay debit card (issued with Jan Dhan account)
3. Enter your 4-digit PIN
4. Select **"Balance Inquiry"**
5. Balance will be displayed and printed on slip

> Jan Dhan accounts come with a free RuPay debit card. If you haven't received one, request it at your branch.

## Method 7: At Bank Branch / Passbook Update

1. Visit your bank branch with your passbook
2. Use the **passbook printing machine** (available in most branches)
3. Or request the counter staff to update your passbook
4. All transactions and current balance will be printed

## Common Issues and Fixes

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Missed call doesn't return SMS | Ensure mobile number is registered with bank. Visit branch to update. |
| "Number not registered" error | Your mobile isn't linked to the account. Update at branch with Aadhaar. |
| Wrong balance shown | Check if you have multiple accounts. The SMS shows primary account balance. |
| No RuPay card received | Visit branch with passbook and Aadhaar to request debit card issuance. |
| Net banking not activated | Visit branch or use the bank's mobile app to activate. |

## How to Check if DBT/Scheme Money is Credited

Many government benefits (LPG subsidy, PM Kisan, scholarships) are credited to Jan Dhan accounts via DBT:

1. Check balance using any method above
2. For detailed transactions, use **net banking** or **passbook update**
3. Check the **PFMS portal** at [https://pfms.nic.in](https://pfms.nic.in) → "Know Your Payment" for DBT payment status

## Important Tips

1. **Keep your mobile number updated** with the bank — it's essential for missed call and SMS banking
2. **Activate RuPay debit card** at any ATM for easy balance checks
3. **Jan Dhan accounts have zero balance requirement** — no minimum balance penalty
4. **Overdraft facility** of up to ₹10,000 available for eligible Jan Dhan account holders
5. **Free accident insurance** of ₹2 lakh comes with the RuPay card

## FAQs

### 1. Is there any charge for missed call balance check?
No, the missed call balance service is completely free. You won't be charged for the call or the SMS you receive.

### 2. Can I check Jan Dhan balance without registered mobile?
No, missed call and SMS services require the registered mobile number. You can check at ATM (with debit card) or by visiting the bank branch with passbook.

### 3. What is the maximum balance I can keep in a Jan Dhan account?
There's no maximum limit on balance. However, Jan Dhan accounts are basic savings accounts, and the overdraft facility is limited to ₹10,000.

### 4. How do I register my mobile number with Jan Dhan account?
Visit your bank branch with Aadhaar card and passbook. Fill the mobile registration form. It's usually activated within 24-48 hours.

### 5. Can I get a mini statement via missed call?
Some banks offer mini statement via missed call (SBI: 9223866666 for mini statement). Check your bank's specific number.

### 6. Is my Jan Dhan account still active if I haven't used it?
Accounts with no transactions for 24 months may become dormant. Visit your branch with ID proof to reactivate.

### 7. Can someone else check my Jan Dhan balance?
Only through the registered mobile number (missed call/SMS) or with your debit card (ATM) or login credentials (net banking). No one can check without these.
