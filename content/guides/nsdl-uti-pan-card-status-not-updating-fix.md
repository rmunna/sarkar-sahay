# NSDL/UTI PAN Card Status Not Updating — How to Fix & Track

Applied for a PAN card through NSDL or UTI and the status has been stuck on "under process" for weeks? You're not alone. This guide covers every common PAN status issue — from stuck applications and rejected forms to missing dispatches and e-PAN download failures — with step-by-step fixes.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent informational resource and is not affiliated with NSDL, UTI, or the Income Tax Department.

---

## How to Track PAN Card Status on NSDL

If you applied through **Protean (formerly NSDL e-Gov):**

1. Visit [https://tin.tin.nsdl.com/pantan/StatusTrack.html](https://tin.tin.nsdl.com/pantan/StatusTrack.html)
2. Select **PAN - New/Change Request** from the application type dropdown
3. Enter your **15-digit acknowledgement number** (starts with your application date, e.g., 020320261234567)
4. Enter the captcha and click **Submit**
5. The status will show one of: Under Process, Dispatched, Returned, or Rejected

**Alternative:** You can also track via the NSDL toll-free number **1800-222-990** or send an SMS: `NSDLPAN <15-digit ack number>` to 57575.

## How to Track PAN Card Status on UTI

If you applied through **UTIITSL:**

1. Visit [https://www.pan.utiitsl.com/PAN/](https://www.pan.utiitsl.com/PAN/)
2. Click on **Track PAN Card** or **Application Status**
3. Enter your **PAN application coupon number** or **PAN number**
4. Enter date of birth and captcha
5. Click **Submit** to view status

**UTI helpline:** 033-40802999 or email at uaboraclemail@utiitsl.com

## PAN Application Stuck on "Under Process" — What to Do

Normal processing time is **15–20 working days** from the date of application. If your status has been "under process" beyond this:

### Step 1: Wait the Full Processing Period
- NSDL online applications: 15–20 working days
- UTI applications: 15–20 working days
- Physical form submissions via PAN centres: may take longer (up to 30 days)

### Step 2: Check for Communication from NSDL/UTI
- Check the email ID you provided during application for any queries or rejection notices
- Also check your spam/junk folder

### Step 3: Contact the Respective Agency
- **NSDL:** Call 1800-222-990 (toll-free) or 020-27218080. Email: tininfo@nsdl.co.in
- **UTI:** Call 033-40802999. Email: uaboraclemail@utiitsl.com
- Keep your acknowledgement number ready

### Step 4: File a Grievance on TIN-NSDL
If no response after contacting:
1. Visit [https://www.tin-nsdl.com/](https://www.tin-nsdl.com/)
2. Go to **Services → Grievance** section
3. Select grievance type as **PAN**
4. Enter your acknowledgement number, PAN (if existing), and details of the issue
5. Submit and note the grievance ticket number for follow-up

### Step 5: Escalate to the Income Tax Department
If the issue persists beyond 45 days:
1. Visit [https://www.incometax.gov.in/](https://www.incometax.gov.in/)
2. Go to **Grievances** under the Help section
3. File an e-Nivaran complaint with your PAN application details

## PAN Application Rejected — Common Reasons & How to Reapply

### Common Rejection Reasons
| Reason | Explanation |
|--------|-------------|
| Mismatch in name/DOB | Name or date of birth on documents doesn't match the application form |
| Blurry or incomplete documents | Photo, signature, or identity proof not clearly visible |
| Invalid identity/address proof | Document submitted is not in the list of acceptable proofs |
| Duplicate PAN exists | You already have a PAN card — cannot get a second one |
| Incomplete form | Missing fields, incorrect AO code, or unsigned form |
| Photo not meeting specifications | Photo doesn't meet the size/format/background requirements |

### How to Reapply After Rejection
1. Note the exact rejection reason from your email or status check
2. Visit the NSDL or UTI portal and start a **fresh application**
3. Correct the issue — upload clearer documents, fix name spelling, etc.
4. Pay the application fee again (₹107 for Indian communication address, ₹1,017 for foreign address — inclusive of GST)
5. If you have a duplicate PAN, apply for **cancellation/surrender** of the extra PAN instead

## PAN Card Dispatched but Not Received

If the status shows **"Dispatched"** but you haven't received your PAN card:

### Step 1: Check Courier Details
- The status page on NSDL shows the **courier partner name and tracking number**
- Common courier partners: Speed Post (India Post), Blue Dart, DTDC
- Track on the courier partner's website using the tracking number

### Step 2: Wait a Reasonable Time
- Speed Post: 7–15 days after dispatch
- Private couriers: 3–7 days after dispatch
- Remote areas may take longer

### Step 3: Request a Reprint
If the card is lost in transit or not delivered within 30 days:
1. Visit [https://onlineservices.nsdl.com/paam/endUserRegisterContact.html](https://onlineservices.nsdl.com/paam/endUserRegisterContact.html)
2. Select **Reprint of PAN Card** (choose "Changes or Correction in existing PAN Data / Reprint of PAN Card")
3. Enter your PAN number and submit the form
4. No need to re-upload documents if only requesting reprint
5. Pay the reprint fee: ₹50 + GST (for Indian address dispatch)

### Step 4: Download e-PAN as Interim
While waiting for physical card, download your e-PAN (see next section). The e-PAN is legally valid.

## e-PAN Download Not Working — Fixes

### Download e-PAN from NSDL
1. Visit [https://onlineservices.nsdl.com/paam/endUserRegisterContact.html](https://onlineservices.nsdl.com/paam/endUserRegisterContact.html)
2. Select **Download e-PAN**
3. Enter your **PAN number** and **date of birth**
4. An OTP will be sent to your registered email/mobile
5. Enter OTP and download the PDF

### Download e-PAN from Income Tax Portal
1. Visit [https://eportal.incometax.gov.in/](https://eportal.incometax.gov.in/)
2. Go to **Instant e-PAN** under Quick Links
3. Enter your Aadhaar number (must be linked to PAN)
4. Verify via Aadhaar OTP and download

### Common Issues & Fixes
| Problem | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| OTP not received | Check registered email/mobile. If changed, update on NSDL first |
| "PAN not found" error | Double-check PAN number. Try after some time if server is busy |
| PDF won't open | Password is your date of birth in DDMMYYYY format |
| e-PAN not available yet | e-PAN is generated only after PAN is allotted. Check status first |
| Aadhaar method not working | PAN must be linked to Aadhaar for the Income Tax portal method |

## PAN-Aadhaar Linking Status Stuck

PAN-Aadhaar linking is mandatory. If your linking request is stuck:

### Check Linking Status
1. Visit [https://eportal.incometax.gov.in/iec/foservices/#/pre-login/link-aadhaar-status](https://eportal.incometax.gov.in/iec/foservices/#/pre-login/link-aadhaar-status)
2. Enter PAN and Aadhaar number
3. Status will show: Already Linked, Pending, or Failed

### Common Reasons for Stuck/Failed Linking
- **Name mismatch** between PAN and Aadhaar — update the name on either document to match
- **Date of birth mismatch** — correct on whichever document has the error
- **Payment not processed** — linking fee is ₹1,000 (paid via challan 280 on NSDL). Check if payment reflected
- **Server issues** — the Income Tax portal sometimes takes 5–7 days to process. Wait and recheck
- **Biometric Aadhaar authentication needed** — in some mismatch cases, you may need to visit a PAN centre for biometric verification

### How to Fix
1. Ensure name and DOB match exactly on both PAN and Aadhaar
2. If you paid the ₹1,000 fee, wait 5–7 working days for processing
3. If still stuck after 7 days, file a grievance on [https://www.incometax.gov.in/](https://www.incometax.gov.in/) under e-Nivaran

See our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) for help updating Aadhaar details.

## NSDL vs UTI — Which One Should You Use?

Both **Protean (NSDL)** and **UTIITSL** are authorised PAN service agencies by the Income Tax Department. There is no difference in the PAN card issued.

| Feature | NSDL (Protean) | UTI (UTIITSL) |
|---------|---------------|----------------|
| Apply online | Yes — onlineservices.nsdl.com | Yes — pan.utiitsl.com |
| Apply offline | PAN centres and TIN facilitation centres | UTI PAN centres and authorised agents |
| Fee (Indian address) | ₹107 (incl. GST) | ₹107 (incl. GST) |
| Fee (Foreign address) | ₹1,017 (incl. GST) | ₹1,017 (incl. GST) |
| Processing time | 15–20 working days | 15–20 working days |
| e-PAN | Available for download | Available for download |
| Tracking | tin.tin.nsdl.com | pan.utiitsl.com |

**Recommendation:** Both are equally reliable. NSDL's online portal is generally considered more user-friendly. Choose whichever has a physical centre nearer to you if applying offline.

## How to File a Grievance on TIN-NSDL

If your PAN issue remains unresolved:

1. Visit the TIN-NSDL website
2. Navigate to **Services → Grievance Redressal**
3. Choose complaint type: **PAN Related**
4. Fill in:
   - Your PAN or acknowledgement number
   - Contact details (email and mobile)
   - Description of the problem
5. Submit and save the **complaint reference number**
6. Expected resolution time: 7–15 working days
7. If unresolved, escalate to the Income Tax Ombudsman in your jurisdiction

## Important Tips

1. **Save your acknowledgement number** — it's the only way to track your PAN application status
2. **Keep registered mobile/email active** — NSDL and UTI send updates and OTPs to these
3. **Download e-PAN immediately** after allotment — it's legally valid and serves as backup
4. **Don't apply for a second PAN** if your first is delayed — having two PANs attracts a ₹10,000 penalty under Section 272B
5. **Check AO code carefully** while applying — incorrect AO (Assessing Officer) code is a common reason for delays

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does it take for PAN card status to update on NSDL?
PAN card status on NSDL typically updates within 24–48 hours of any change. Full processing takes 15–20 working days from application date.

### My PAN application has been "under process" for over a month. What should I do?
Contact NSDL at 1800-222-990 or UTI at 033-40802999 with your acknowledgement number. If no resolution, file a grievance on the TIN-NSDL portal or an e-Nivaran complaint on the Income Tax website.

### Can I download e-PAN if my physical PAN card is not delivered?
Yes. Once your PAN is allotted (status shows "Dispatched" or you receive a PAN number), you can download the e-PAN from the NSDL portal or the Income Tax e-filing portal. The e-PAN is legally valid.

### Why was my PAN application rejected?
Common reasons include name/DOB mismatch with documents, blurry document uploads, invalid proof of identity or address, or an existing duplicate PAN. Check your email for the specific rejection reason.

### How do I get a reprint of my PAN card?
Visit the NSDL online portal, select "Reprint of PAN Card," enter your PAN number, verify via OTP, and pay ₹50 + GST. The reprinted card will be dispatched to your registered address.

### Is PAN-Aadhaar linking still mandatory?
Yes. All PAN holders (except exempt categories like NRIs and super senior citizens) must link PAN with Aadhaar. Unlinked PANs become inoperative. The linking fee is ₹1,000.

### How can I check if my PAN is linked to Aadhaar?
Visit the Income Tax e-filing portal at eportal.incometax.gov.in, go to "Link Aadhaar Status," and enter your PAN and Aadhaar numbers.

### NSDL or UTI — does it matter which one I use for PAN?
No. Both are authorised by the Income Tax Department and issue the same PAN card. Fees and processing times are identical. Choose based on convenience.
