## Why Change Your Voter ID Photo?

Your Voter ID card (EPIC) photo may need updating if:
- The existing photo is **old, blurry, or unrecognizable**
- The photo was **wrongly assigned** to your entry
- You want to update it for **better identification** at the polling booth
- Your appearance has **significantly changed** over the years

The Election Commission allows photo corrections through **Form 8** (Application for Correction of Entries in Electoral Roll).

## Photo Requirements

Before uploading, ensure your photo meets these specifications:

| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| **File format** | JPEG / JPG |
| **File size** | 10 KB to 200 KB |
| **Dimensions** | Passport-size (recommended 150×200 pixels) |
| **Background** | White or light-coloured |
| **Recency** | Recent photograph |
| **Face coverage** | 70–80% of the frame |
| **Expression** | Neutral, mouth closed |
| **Accessories** | No sunglasses; religious head coverings allowed |

> **Tip:** Use your phone camera against a white wall, then crop to passport size. Many free apps can resize to the exact specifications.

## Documents Required

- Current Voter ID (EPIC) number
- New passport-size photograph (as per specs above)
- Aadhaar Card or other ID proof (for verification)
- Mobile number linked to your voter registration

## Step-by-Step Process — Online (Form 8)

### Step 1: Visit the Voter Portal
Go to [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in) and log in with your mobile number and OTP.

### Step 2: Select Form 8
Click on **"Correction of Entries"** or navigate to **Form 8** from the dashboard.

### Step 3: Search Your Entry
Enter your **EPIC number** to fetch your current details from the electoral roll.

### Step 4: Select "Photo" for Correction
From the list of fields available for correction, select **"Photo"** as the field you want to update.

### Step 5: Upload New Photo
- Click **"Upload Photo"**
- Select your new passport-size JPEG photo
- Ensure it meets the size and format requirements
- Preview the uploaded photo before proceeding

### Step 6: Upload Supporting Document
Upload a scanned copy of your Aadhaar card or another government-issued photo ID as supporting proof.

### Step 7: Submit and Track
- Review all details and submit the form
- Note the **Reference ID** provided
- You will receive an SMS confirmation

## Step-by-Step Process — Offline

### Step 1: Get Form 8
Download Form 8 from [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in) or collect it from the ERO office or BLO.

### Step 2: Fill the Form
Fill in your personal details and EPIC number. In the correction section, mention **"Photo correction"**.

### Step 3: Attach Photo
Paste two recent passport-size photographs on the form.

### Step 4: Submit
Submit the form with photocopies of your ID proof to:
- Your **Booth Level Officer (BLO)**, or
- The **Electoral Registration Officer (ERO)** office

### Step 5: BLO Verification
The BLO may visit to verify your identity before the photo is updated.

## Processing Time & Approval

| Stage | Timeline |
|-------|----------|
| Form submission to BLO verification | 7–15 days |
| ERO approval | 15–30 days |
| Updated EPIC card dispatch | 30–60 days |
| e-EPIC download available | After ERO approval |

> **Note:** You can download your updated **e-EPIC** (digital Voter ID) from [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in) once the correction is approved, without waiting for the physical card. See our [Voter ID apply guide](/guide/voter-id-card-apply-online) for e-EPIC details.

## How to Track Your Application

1. Visit [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in)
2. Click **"Track Application Status"**
3. Enter your **Reference ID**
4. Check the current status — Pending / Under Verification / Approved / Rejected

## Fees

There are **no fees** for changing your Voter ID photo. The entire process — online and offline — is completely free.

## Important Tips

1. **Check photo specs before uploading** — Most rejections happen due to wrong file size or format. Keep it under 200 KB in JPEG format.
2. **Use a recent photo** — The photo should match your current appearance for smooth verification at polling booths.
3. **Download e-EPIC after approval** — Don't wait for the physical card. The digital version is equally valid.
4. **Keep your mobile updated** — Ensure the mobile number linked to your voter registration is active for OTP and status updates.
5. **Contact BLO if delayed** — If your request is pending beyond 30 days, contact your [BLO](/guide/voter-id-blo-details-check) for a status update.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I change my Voter ID photo online?
Yes. Use Form 8 on [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in) to upload a new photo and submit the correction request online.

### What photo format is accepted for Voter ID?
Only **JPEG/JPG** format is accepted. The file size should be between 10 KB and 200 KB with a white or light background.

### How long does the photo change take?
The entire process takes **15–60 days** depending on BLO verification and ERO workload. The e-EPIC is available sooner than the physical card.

### Is there any fee for changing the Voter ID photo?
No. Photo correction through Form 8 is completely free of charge.

### Can I change my photo and name together?
Yes. Form 8 allows you to correct multiple fields in a single application — photo, name, date of birth, address, etc.

### What if my photo change request is rejected?
Check the rejection reason on the tracking portal. Common reasons include poor photo quality or mismatch with supporting documents. Resubmit with a better photo.

### Can I use the e-EPIC as valid ID after the photo update?
Yes. The e-EPIC downloaded from [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in) is a valid digital identity document accepted at polling booths and for other ID purposes.
