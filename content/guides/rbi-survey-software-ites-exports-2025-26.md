## What is RBI Survey on Computer Software and Information Technology Enabled Services (ITES) Exports: 2025-26
The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has launched a comprehensive survey specifically targeting Computer Software and Information Technology Enabled Services (ITES) exports for the financial year 2025-26. This initiative serves as a crucial data collection exercise, aiming to gather detailed information on India's burgeoning software and ITES export sector. The survey is designed to provide robust statistical insights into the trends and performance of this vital economic segment.

## Key Benefits
As a data collection initiative, the RBI Survey on Computer Software and ITES Exports 2025-26 primarily offers indirect benefits through its contribution to economic policy and understanding. Direct monetary benefits for participants are not applicable to a survey of this nature. The key benefits are realised at a macroeconomic level:

*   **Informed Policy Formulation:** The collected data will enable the government and RBI to formulate more effective policies supporting the growth and competitiveness of India's software and ITES export sector.
*   **Economic Analysis and Research:** Comprehensive data allows for in-depth analysis of sector-specific trends, identifying strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats.
*   **Enhanced Sector Visibility:** The survey helps to accurately assess the contribution of software and ITES exports to India's overall economy, improving the sector's national and international profile.
*   **Resource Allocation:** Accurate data can guide better allocation of resources and incentives for businesses operating in this domain.
*   **Benchmark for Future Growth:** The survey establishes a baseline for measuring future growth and performance of the software and ITES export industry.
