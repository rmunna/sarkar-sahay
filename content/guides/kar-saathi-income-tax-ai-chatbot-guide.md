# Kar Saathi AI Chatbot and Income Tax Website 2.0 Guide

Kar Saathi is the Income Tax Department's AI-enabled taxpayer assistant introduced under PRARAMBH 2026, along with Income Tax Website 2.0. The official launch was tied to the transition to the Income Tax Act, 2025, which came into effect from April 1, 2026.

Use this page as the hub for CitizenNest's taxpayer AI coverage: what Kar Saathi can help with, how to ask better questions, where it may fail, and what to do when the income tax portal itself is not working.

## Current Status

| Item | Status on June 8, 2026 |
|---|---|
| Kar Saathi | Launched as an AI-enabled chatbot for direct tax queries |
| Income Tax Website 2.0 | Launched as the upgraded information website |
| PRARAMBH 2026 | Nationwide awareness campaign for the Income Tax Act, 2025 |
| E-filing portal | Still accessed through the official e-filing portal |

Do not treat the chatbot as your tax preparer. It can explain rules, forms, notices, refunds and compliance steps, but you must still verify final figures before filing.

## What Kar Saathi Is Useful For

Kar Saathi is best for quick explanations and navigation help:

- Which ITR form may apply to your situation
- What a tax notice or compliance message generally means
- Where to find forms, FAQs and guidance material
- How to understand basic salary, refund, TDS and AIS concepts
- What changed under the newer tax law framework

For actual filing, use the official e-filing portal and cross-check with Form 16, AIS, Form 26AS and bank statements. Start with [How to File Income Tax Return Online](/guide/income-tax-return-file-online) and [AIS vs Form 26AS vs TIS](/guide/ais-vs-form26as-vs-tis-income-tax).

## Prompt Examples That Work Better

Ask narrow questions with your taxpayer type, year and problem.

| Instead of asking | Ask this |
|---|---|
| "How to file tax?" | "I am a salaried individual filing ITR for AY 2026-27. Which documents should I keep ready before starting?" |
| "Refund problem" | "My ITR refund is not credited but bank account is validated. What checks should I do on the income tax portal?" |
| "Notice received" | "I received an income tax notice for AIS mismatch. What documents should I compare before replying?" |
| "Which form?" | "I have salary income, savings interest and one house property. Which ITR form is usually used?" |

## What Not To Ask Kar Saathi

Avoid using it for final professional decisions:

- "Calculate my exact tax payable from these numbers"
- "Can I ignore this notice?"
- "Give me a guaranteed deduction strategy"
- "Draft a legal reply to the department"
- "Tell me whether this cash transaction is safe"

For notices, use [Income Tax Notice Received: How to Respond](/guide/income-tax-notice-response) and consider a tax professional if the amount is large or there is a penalty risk.

## Income Tax Website 2.0 Troubleshooting

Website 2.0 is primarily the taxpayer information site. E-filing tasks still move through official login flows. If something fails:

1. Confirm you are on an official domain: `incometax.gov.in` or `eportal.incometax.gov.in`.
2. Try another browser or private window.
3. Disable aggressive ad blockers and script blockers.
4. Check whether the issue is on the information website or the e-filing portal.
5. Save screenshots with date and time if a deadline is near.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Kar Saathi an official Income Tax Department chatbot?

Yes. It was launched as part of the Income Tax Department's PRARAMBH 2026 outreach and Website 2.0 effort.

### Can Kar Saathi file my ITR for me?

No. Treat it as a help assistant. Use the official e-filing portal to file and verify your return.

### Can I rely on Kar Saathi for final tax advice?

Use it for guidance, not final professional advice. For notices, complex capital gains, foreign assets or business income, verify with official instructions or a qualified tax professional.

### What should I do if the chatbot gives a confusing answer?

Ask a narrower question, include the assessment year and taxpayer type, and cross-check with the official portal guidance before acting.
