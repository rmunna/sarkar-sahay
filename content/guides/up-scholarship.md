# UP Scholarship 2025: Apply Online, Eligibility & Status Check

The **Uttar Pradesh Scholarship Scheme** is one of India's largest state scholarship programmes, providing financial assistance to lakhs of students from economically weaker sections. Managed by the UP Scholarship and Fee Reimbursement Online System (SAKSHAM), it covers pre-matric (Class 1-10) and post-matric (Class 11 onwards) scholarships for SC, ST, OBC, Minority, and General category students.

---

## What is UP Scholarship?

The Government of Uttar Pradesh provides scholarships to students from disadvantaged backgrounds to ensure they can continue their education without financial barriers. The scheme covers:

- **Pre-Matric Scholarship:** Class 1 to 10
- **Post-Matric Scholarship:** Class 11 and 12
- **Post-Matric (Other than Intermediate):** Graduation, Post-graduation, Professional courses
- **Fee Reimbursement:** Full tuition fee reimbursement for eligible students

---

## Key Benefits

| Scholarship Type | Coverage |
|-----------------|----------|
| Pre-Matric (Class 1-8) | Maintenance allowance + fee reimbursement |
| Pre-Matric (Class 9-10) | Up to Rs 3,500-5,000/year (varies by category) |
| Post-Matric (Class 11-12) | Tuition fee + maintenance allowance |
| Post-Matric (Higher Education) | Full fee reimbursement + maintenance |
| Minority Scholarship | Fee reimbursement + maintenance allowance |

*Exact amounts vary by category (SC/ST/OBC/Minority/General), course type, and whether the student is a day scholar or hosteller.*

## Who is Eligible?

### General Eligibility

1. **Domicile:** Must be a permanent resident of Uttar Pradesh
2. **Enrolled:** Must be enrolled in a recognised educational institution in UP
3. **Income limit:** Family annual income should be within prescribed limits:
   - **SC/ST:** Up to Rs 2,50,000/year
   - **OBC:** Up to Rs 2,00,000/year
   - **Minority:** Up to Rs 2,00,000/year
   - **General (EWS):** Up to Rs 1,00,000/year
4. **Attendance:** Minimum 75% attendance required
5. **Not failed:** Should have passed the previous examination (for renewal)

## How to Apply: Step-by-Step

### Fresh Application

1. Visit [scholarship.up.gov.in](https://scholarship.up.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"Student"** section
3. Select **"Fresh Login"** (for new applicants)
4. Choose the appropriate scholarship type:
   - Pre-Matric (Class 1-10)
   - Post-Matric Intermediate
   - Post-Matric Other Than Intermediate
5. **Register** with your details and get registration number
6. **Login** with your registration number and date of birth
7. Fill in the **application form** with personal, educational, and bank details
8. Upload **passport photo** and required documents
9. **Submit** the application
10. Take a **printout** of the filled application
11. Submit the printout to your school/college for **institutional verification**

### Renewal Application

1. Visit [scholarship.up.gov.in](https://scholarship.up.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"Renewal Login"**
3. Login with your previous year registration number
4. Update details if changed (income, marks, class)
5. Submit the renewal application
6. Get institutional verification done

## How to Check Scholarship Status

1. Visit [scholarship.up.gov.in](https://scholarship.up.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"Status"** section
3. Enter your **registration number** and **date of birth**
4. View application status, verification status, and payment details
5. For payment tracking, also check [pfms.nic.in](https://pfms.nic.in/) using your bank account or Aadhaar

## Processing Time

- **Application processing:** 2-4 months after submission
- **Scholarship disbursement:** Usually between January and March (may extend)

## Important Tips

1. **Apply early** — Don't wait for the last date; server load increases near deadlines
2. **Double-check bank details** — Incorrect bank account or IFSC code delays payment
3. **Institutional verification is mandatory** — Ensure your school/college verifies your application on time
4. **Keep login credentials safe** — You'll need them for renewal and status checking
5. **Income certificate validity** — Ensure your income certificate is recent and from the Tehsildar

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. How much scholarship amount do students get?
Amount varies by category, course, and whether day scholar or hosteller. Post-matric scholars typically get full fee reimbursement plus maintenance allowance.

### 2. Can students from other states apply?
No, only permanent residents of Uttar Pradesh studying in UP institutions are eligible.

### 3. Is there any application fee?
No, the scholarship application is completely free.

### 4. What if my application is rejected?
Check the rejection reason on the portal. Common reasons include income limit exceeded, incorrect documents, or institutional verification failure. You may apply again in the next cycle.

### 5. Can I apply if I'm studying in a private college?
Yes, students in recognised private institutions in UP can apply, subject to the institution being approved on the scholarship portal.

### 6. How is the scholarship paid?
Directly to the student's bank account via DBT. Fee components may be paid to the institution.

### 7. Do I need to apply every year?
Yes, fresh applicants apply once and then apply for **renewal** every subsequent year.
