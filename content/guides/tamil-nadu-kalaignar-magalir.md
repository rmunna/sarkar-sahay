## What is Kalaignar Magalir Urimai Thogai Scheme?

The Kalaignar Magalir Urimai Thogai (Women's Right Assistance) scheme is a welfare program by the Tamil Nadu government that provides **₹1,000 per month** to eligible women heads of families. The scheme aims to ensure financial independence for women and support low-income households across Tamil Nadu.

Named after former Chief Minister Kalaignar M. Karunanidhi, this scheme benefits women who are the primary caregivers and managers of their households.

## Who is Eligible?

### Eligibility Criteria
- Must be a **woman who is the head of the family**
- Must be a **resident of Tamil Nadu**
- Must hold a **family ration card** issued in Tamil Nadu
- Age: **21 years and above**
- The woman's name must be listed as head of family on the ration card

### Who is NOT Eligible
- Families where any member pays income tax
- Families where any member is a government employee (state/central) drawing ₹25,000+ monthly
- Families owning more than 5 acres of wetland or 10 acres of dry land
- Women already receiving ₹1,000 or more from other state government pension schemes
- Families where any member is an elected representative (MP/MLA/local body head)

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** of the applicant
- **Family ration card** showing the woman as head of family
- **Bank account details** (in the woman's name, linked with Aadhaar)
- **Mobile number** linked with Aadhaar
- **Voter ID** for address verification
- **Recent passport-size photograph**

## How to Apply

### Online Application
1. Visit the official scheme portal
2. Enter your ration card number
3. Verify your Aadhaar through OTP
4. Confirm personal and bank details
5. Upload supporting documents if required
6. Submit the application
7. Save the application reference number

### Offline Application
1. Visit your **Village Panchayat office** (rural) or **Corporation/Municipality office** (urban)
2. Collect the application form from the designated counter
3. Fill in all details and attach required documents
4. Submit the form and collect the acknowledgment receipt
5. You can also apply through **Makkal Sevai Maiyam** (Public Service Centre)

### Through Ration Shops
- In many areas, applications are accepted through **fair price shops (ration shops)**
- Carry your ration card and Aadhaar card
- The shop dealer can help with the application process

## Verification Process

After application submission:
1. **Document verification** by local authorities
2. **Field verification** may be conducted at your residence
3. **Approval** from the District Collector's office
4. **Payment initiation** after successful verification
5. Processing typically takes **30-45 days**

## Important Tips

- **Update your ration card first** — ensure your name is listed as head of family; visit your taluk supply office if needed
- **Aadhaar-bank linking is essential** — without this, DBT will fail; visit your bank branch to complete linking
- **Keep documents ready** — carry originals and photocopies when applying offline
- **Track your application** — use the reference number to check status online
- **Beware of middlemen** — do not pay any agent; the application process is free

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can working women apply for this scheme?
Yes, as long as no family member pays income tax and the family income is below the threshold, working women can apply.

### What if my ration card is in my husband's name?
You need to get the ration card transferred to your name (as head of family). Visit the taluk supply office with required documents for this change.

### Is there an age limit for this scheme?
Yes, the applicant must be at least 21 years old. There is no upper age limit, but women receiving old age pension of ₹1,000+ from the state government are excluded.

### Can widows and divorced women apply?
Yes, widows and divorced women who are heads of their families are eligible. In fact, they are given priority as vulnerable sections.

### What happens if my application is rejected?
If rejected, you will receive the reason for rejection. Common reasons include: incomplete documents, ration card not in woman's name, or family member being a government employee. Rectify the issue and reapply.

### Can I receive both this scheme and old age pension?
If your state old age pension is less than ₹1,000, you can receive the difference under this scheme. If it's ₹1,000 or more, you cannot avail both.
