# SSC JE Exam Guide — SSC Junior Engineer

The **SSC JE (Junior Engineer)** exam is one of India's most sought-after engineering recruitment exams conducted by the **Staff Selection Commission (SSC)**. It recruits Junior Engineers in **Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, and Quantity Surveying & Contracts** disciplines for various central government departments.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent informational platform and is **not affiliated** with SSC or any government body. Always verify details on the [official SSC website](https://ssc.gov.in).

---

## What is the SSC JE Exam?

SSC JE is a national-level competitive examination conducted to fill Junior Engineer posts in organizations such as:

- **Central Public Works Department (CPWD)**
- **Central Water Commission (CWC)**
- **Border Roads Organisation (BRO)**
- **Military Engineer Services (MES)**
- **Farakka Barrage Project**
- **National Technical Research Organisation (NTRO)**

Selected candidates work on infrastructure projects including roads, buildings, dams, and defence installations across India.

---

## Who is Eligible?

### Age Limit

- **Minimum:** 18 years (for most posts)
- **Maximum:** 32 years (as on the cutoff date specified in the notification)
- **Age relaxation** applies for SC/ST (5 years), OBC (3 years), PwD (up to 10 years), and ex-servicemen as per government rules

### Educational Qualification

- **Civil Engineering:** Diploma or Degree in Civil Engineering from a recognized institution
- **Mechanical Engineering:** Diploma or Degree in Mechanical Engineering
- **Electrical Engineering:** Diploma or Degree in Electrical Engineering
- **Quantity Surveying & Contracts:** Diploma or Degree in Civil Engineering

> **Note:** The exact qualification requirements may vary slightly by post and department. Always refer to the official notification for the specific recruitment cycle.

### Nationality

- Must be a citizen of India (subject to standard government eligibility rules)

## Exam Pattern

The SSC JE exam is conducted in **two stages**:

### Paper 1 (Computer-Based Examination)

| Subject | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| General Intelligence & Reasoning | 50 | 50 | — |
| General Awareness | 50 | 50 | — |
| General Engineering (Civil/Mechanical/Electrical) | 100 | 100 | — |
| **Total** | **200** | **200** | **2 hours** |

- **Mode:** Online (Computer-Based Test)
- **Question type:** Objective (Multiple Choice)
- **Negative marking:** 0.25 marks deducted for each wrong answer

### Paper 2 (Descriptive/Conventional)

| Subject | Marks | Duration |
|---------|-------|----------|
| General Engineering (Civil/Mechanical/Electrical) | 300 | 2 hours |

- **Mode:** Pen-and-paper (Descriptive)
- **Question type:** Conventional (written answers)
- Only candidates who qualify Paper 1 are called for Paper 2

### Final Merit

The final selection is based on the **combined score of Paper 1 and Paper 2**, followed by document verification.

## Syllabus Overview

### General Intelligence & Reasoning

Analogies, similarities, differences, spatial visualization, problem-solving, analysis, judgement, decision-making, visual memory, observation, relationship concepts, arithmetical reasoning, verbal and figure classification, arithmetic number series, coding and decoding.

### General Awareness

Current affairs, history, geography, polity, economy, general science, and topics relevant to everyday observation.

### General Engineering

#### Civil Engineering
- Building Materials, Estimating & Costing, Surveying
- Soil Mechanics, Hydraulics, Irrigation Engineering
- Transportation Engineering, Environmental Engineering
- Structural Engineering, Concrete Technology, RCC Design

#### Mechanical Engineering
- Theory of Machines & Machine Design
- Engineering Mechanics & Strength of Materials
- Thermal Engineering, Fluid Mechanics
- Production Engineering, Industrial Engineering

#### Electrical Engineering
- Basic Electrical Concepts, Circuit Law, AC/DC Fundamentals
- Electrical Machines (Motors & Generators)
- Measurements & Measuring Instruments
- Transmission & Distribution, Estimation & Costing
- Power Electronics, Basic Electronics

> For the complete, detailed syllabus, always refer to the [official SSC JE notification](https://ssc.gov.in).

## Application Process

1. **Visit** the official SSC website at [https://ssc.gov.in](https://ssc.gov.in)
2. **Register** on the SSC portal (one-time registration if not already done)
3. **Fill the application form** with personal, educational, and preference details
4. **Upload documents** — photograph, signature, and relevant certificates
5. **Pay the application fee** online (Net Banking, UPI, or Challan)
6. **Download** and save the confirmation page for future reference

> Applications are accepted only during the official window. Check the SSC annual calendar or notification for exact dates.

## Preparation Strategy

### 1. Know the Syllabus Thoroughly
Download the official syllabus and map every topic. Prioritize high-weightage areas in General Engineering.

### 2. Start with Paper 1
Since Paper 1 is qualifying, focus on it first. Aim for strong scores in General Engineering (100 marks) — this is the differentiator.

### 3. Practice Previous Year Papers
SSC JE has a pattern. Solving 5–10 years of previous papers helps identify repeated topics and expected difficulty.

### 4. Build a Study Schedule
Dedicate 3–4 months for preparation. Allocate time daily:
- **General Engineering:** 2–3 hours
- **Reasoning:** 1 hour
- **General Awareness:** 30 minutes (daily current affairs)

### 5. Use Standard Textbooks
- **Civil:** R.S. Khurmi, B.C. Punmia, S.K. Garg
- **Mechanical:** R.S. Khurmi, R.K. Jain
- **Electrical:** B.L. Theraja, J.B. Gupta

### 6. Take Mock Tests
Regular online mock tests simulate exam conditions and improve time management. Aim for at least 10–15 full-length mocks before the exam.

### 7. Revise Paper 2 Concepts
Paper 2 is descriptive — practice writing neat, structured answers with diagrams. Accuracy and presentation matter.

## Salary & Posting

### Pay Scale

- **Pay Level:** Level 6 (as per 7th Pay Commission)
- **Pay Band:** ₹35,400 – ₹1,12,400
- **Gross Salary (approximate):** ₹44,000 – ₹52,000 per month (varies by posting city and allowances)

### Allowances Include

- Dearness Allowance (DA)
- House Rent Allowance (HRA)
- Transport Allowance
- Other benefits as per central government rules

### Posting Locations

Postings are across India in departments like CPWD, CWC, BRO, and MES. Locations can range from metro cities to remote border areas, depending on the department.

### Career Growth

Junior Engineers can be promoted to **Assistant Engineer** and eventually to **Executive Engineer** through departmental exams and seniority.

## Important Tips

1. **Start early** — 3 to 4 months of focused preparation is ideal. Don't wait for the notification to start studying.

2. **Focus on General Engineering** — It carries the most weight in both Paper 1 (100 marks) and Paper 2 (300 marks). Master your branch-specific subjects.

3. **Don't ignore Reasoning & GA** — These 100 marks in Paper 1 are scoring with consistent practice. Use them to build a safety margin.

4. **Practice descriptive writing for Paper 2** — Neat diagrams, stepwise solutions, and clear handwriting can earn more marks than you'd expect.

5. **Stay updated on current affairs** — Read a daily newspaper or a monthly current affairs digest. Focus on national events, schemes, and science.

6. **Manage your time in the exam** — Paper 1 gives 200 questions in 120 minutes — that's 36 seconds per question. Skip and return to tough ones.

7. **Keep official documents ready** — Caste certificate, domicile, educational certificates — have everything ready well before document verification.

8. **Check the SSC calendar regularly** — SSC publishes an annual exam calendar. Bookmark [https://ssc.gov.in](https://ssc.gov.in) and check frequently.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Can diploma holders apply for SSC JE?
**Yes.** Both diploma and degree holders in the relevant engineering discipline (Civil, Mechanical, Electrical) are eligible to apply.

### Q2: Is there negative marking in SSC JE?
**Yes**, in Paper 1 only. A deduction of **0.25 marks** is made for each incorrect answer. Paper 2 (descriptive) has no negative marking.

### Q3: How many times can I attempt SSC JE?
There is **no limit** on the number of attempts, as long as you meet the age eligibility criteria for that recruitment cycle.

### Q4: What is the SSC JE selection process?
Selection is based on **Paper 1 + Paper 2 combined score**, followed by document verification. There is no interview.

### Q5: Which departments recruit through SSC JE?
Major recruiting departments include **CPWD, CWC, BRO, MES, Farakka Barrage Project**, and NTRO, among others.

### Q6: Can final-year students apply for SSC JE?
Candidates must have **completed** their diploma/degree by the cutoff date mentioned in the notification. Final-year students who will complete before the cutoff date may apply — check the specific notification for clarity.

### Q7: Is SSC JE conducted every year?
SSC generally conducts the JE exam **annually**, but schedules can shift. Refer to the SSC annual calendar for confirmed dates.
