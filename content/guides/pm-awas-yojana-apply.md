# How to Apply for Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) — Housing for All

Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) is the Government of India's flagship housing scheme that helps low and middle-income families buy or build their own pucca house. If you have been dreaming of owning a home but worried about the cost, PMAY can help you with interest subsidies on home loans and direct financial assistance.

This guide covers everything you need to know — eligibility, documents required, how to apply online and offline, subsidy amounts, and common questions.

## What is PMAY?

Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana was launched on **25th June 2015** by Prime Minister Narendra Modi under the vision of **"Housing for All."** The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA).

PMAY has two components:

- **PMAY-Urban (PMAY-U):** For people living in urban areas (cities and towns). Implemented by MoHUA.
- **PMAY-Gramin (PMAY-G):** For people living in rural areas. Implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development.

### Four Verticals of PMAY-Urban

1. **In-Situ Slum Redevelopment (ISSR):** Rehabilitation of slum dwellers with participation of private developers.
2. **Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme (CLSS):** Interest subsidy on home loans for EWS, LIG, and MIG categories.
3. **Affordable Housing in Partnership (AHP):** Central assistance of ₹1.5 lakh per house for EWS category built in partnership with public/private sectors.
4. **Beneficiary-Led Individual House Construction (BLC-IHC):** Central assistance of ₹1.5 lakh for individual house construction or enhancement for EWS category.

## Who is Eligible for PMAY?

### Income Categories

| Category | Annual Household Income |
|---|---|
| EWS (Economically Weaker Section) | Up to ₹3 lakh |
| LIG (Low Income Group) | ₹3 lakh – ₹6 lakh |
| MIG-I (Middle Income Group I) | ₹6 lakh – ₹12 lakh |
| MIG-II (Middle Income Group II) | ₹12 lakh – ₹18 lakh |

### Key Eligibility Conditions

- The beneficiary family should **not own a pucca house** anywhere in India.
- The beneficiary family includes husband, wife, and unmarried children.
- **Women ownership:** For EWS and LIG categories, the house must be in the name of a female member of the household, or in joint name. *(This condition is relaxable for MIG categories.)*
- **[Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) number** is mandatory for availing benefits.
- The beneficiary should not have availed central assistance under any other government housing scheme.

### PMAY-Gramin Eligibility

For PMAY-G, beneficiaries are selected from the **Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC) 2011** data. Priority is given to:

- Households with no house or living in kutcha/dilapidated houses
- SC/ST households
- Minorities
- Families of ex-servicemen and widows

## Documents Required

Keep these documents ready before applying:

1. **Aadhaar Card** (mandatory for all family members)
2. **[income certificate](/guide/income-certificate-online)** or salary slips
3. **Address Proof** (voter ID, utility bill, etc.)
4. **Bank Account Details** (linked with Aadhaar)
5. **Caste Certificate** (if applicable — SC/ST/OBC)
6. **Proof of Land Ownership** (for BLC component)
7. **Affidavit** stating you do not own a pucca house in India
8. **Passport-size Photographs**
9. **Property Agreement / Builder Agreement** (for CLSS component)
10. **Income Tax Returns** (for MIG categories)

## How to Apply for PMAY Online (PMAY-Urban)

### Step 1: Visit the Official Portal

Go to **[pmaymis.gov.in](https://pmaymis.gov.in)** — this is the official PMAY-Urban MIS portal.

### Step 2: Select "Citizen Assessment"

On the homepage, click on **"Citizen Assessment"** in the menu bar. You will see options:

- **Slum Dwellers** — if you live in a notified slum area
- **Benefits Under Other 3 Components** — for CLSS, AHP, or BLC

Select the option that applies to you.

### Step 3: Enter Aadhaar Details

Enter your **12-digit Aadhaar number** and name as per Aadhaar. Click **"Check"** to verify.

### Step 4: Fill the Application Form

Fill in all required details:

- Personal information (name, father's name, date of birth, gender)
- Contact details (mobile number, email)
- Residential address
- Income details and category (EWS/LIG/MIG-I/MIG-II)
- Bank account and IFSC code
- Details of current housing status
- Property details (if applying under CLSS)

### Step 5: Upload Documents

Upload scanned copies of required documents as specified. Ensure all files are clear and within the allowed file size.

### Step 6: Submit and Save

Review all details carefully. After submission, you will get an **application reference number**. **Save or print this number** — you will need it to track your application.

### Step 7: Track Your Application

Go to **pmaymis.gov.in** → **"Track Your Assessment Status"** → Enter your reference number or Aadhaar number to check the status.

## How to Apply for PMAY Offline

If you are not comfortable with the online process, you can apply offline:

1. Visit your nearest **Common Service Centre (CSC)** or **Urban Local Body (ULB) / Municipal Office**.
2. Ask for the **PMAY application form**. A nominal fee of **₹25 + GST** may be charged at CSCs.
3. Fill the form with all required details.
4. Attach photocopies of all required documents.
5. Submit the form. Collect the **acknowledgement receipt**.
6. Your application will be verified by the ULB and forwarded for approval.

## How to Apply for PMAY-Gramin

For rural areas, the process is different:

1. Beneficiaries are **identified from SECC 2011 data** by the Gram Sabha.
2. The Gram Sabha verifies and finalises the list.
3. The list is approved by the District-level and State-level committees.
4. Selected beneficiaries receive **direct benefit transfer (DBT)** of financial assistance to their Aadhaar-linked bank accounts.
5. You can check your name on the PMAY-G beneficiary list at **[pmayg.nic.in](https://pmayg.nic.in)**.

> **Note:** For PMAY-Gramin, you generally cannot apply on your own — beneficiaries are selected through government surveys. However, if you believe you were missed, approach your **Gram Panchayat** or **Block Development Office**.

## PMAY Subsidy and Assistance Amounts

### CLSS (Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme) — PMAY-Urban

| Category | Max Loan for Subsidy | Interest Subsidy | Max Subsidy Amount | Max Carpet Area |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| EWS | ₹6 lakh | 6.5% | ~₹2.67 lakh | 30 sq.m. |
| LIG | ₹6 lakh | 6.5% | ~₹2.67 lakh | 60 sq.m. |
| MIG-I | ₹9 lakh | 4.0% | ~₹2.35 lakh | 160 sq.m. |
| MIG-II | ₹12 lakh | 3.0% | ~₹2.30 lakh | 200 sq.m. |

*Subsidy is calculated on a 20-year loan tenure at NPV (Net Present Value) with a 9% discount rate.*

> **Note:** The CLSS component for MIG categories was available until March 2021. As of 2025, CLSS may not be accepting new MIG applications. Check the latest status on the official portal. *(⚠️ Verify current availability before applying.)*

### PMAY-Gramin Assistance

- **Plain areas:** ₹1.20 lakh per household
- **Hilly / difficult areas, IAP districts, Naxal-affected areas:** ₹1.30 lakh per household
- Additional ₹12,000 for toilet construction under Swachh Bharat Mission

## PMAY Application Fee

- **Online:** Free on the official PMAY portal
- **Through CSC:** ₹25 + GST (approximately ₹30)
- **No other fee** is charged by the government. Beware of middlemen demanding extra money.

## Timeline

- **Application processing:** 2–4 weeks for initial verification by ULB
- **CLSS subsidy credit:** 3–6 months after loan disbursement (credited to the lending bank)
- **BLC/AHP assistance:** Released in 3 instalments linked to construction milestones
- **PMAY-G construction:** Must be completed within 12 months of sanction

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Can I apply for PMAY if I already own a house?

No. PMAY is only for families who **do not own a pucca house** anywhere in India. If any family member already owns a pucca house, you are not eligible.

### 2. Is PMAY only for women?

No, but for EWS and LIG categories, the house must be **in the name of a female member** or jointly owned. This is to promote women empowerment. MIG categories have some relaxation in this rule.

### 3. Can I apply for PMAY through a bank?

Yes, for the **CLSS component**, you can approach any scheduled bank, housing finance company, or other lending institution. They will process your subsidy claim through the Central Nodal Agencies (NHB, HUDCO, SBI, or PFHL).

### 4. How do I check if my PMAY application is approved?

Visit **[pmaymis.gov.in](https://pmaymis.gov.in)** → Click on **"Track Your Assessment Status"** → Enter your Aadhaar number or application reference number.

### 5. What is the last date to apply for PMAY?

PMAY-Urban was originally planned until 2022, but has been extended. The government periodically updates deadlines. Check the official portal for the most current end date. *(⚠️ Verify latest extension dates on pmaymis.gov.in.)*

### 6. Is there any age limit for PMAY?

There is **no specific age limit**. However, since CLSS involves a home loan, banks may have their own age-related lending criteria (typically up to 65–70 years at loan maturity).

### 7. Can NRIs apply for PMAY?

NRIs are generally **not eligible** for PMAY. The scheme is for Indian residents. However, NRI family members may benefit if the eligible resident family member applies.

## Important Tips

- **Apply only through official channels** — pmaymis.gov.in or authorized CSCs.
- **Never pay large fees** to any agent or middleman. The application is nearly free.
- **Keep your Aadhaar linked** to your bank account for smooth DBT.
- **Track your application** regularly using the reference number.
- For CLSS, approach your bank directly — they will handle the subsidy processing.
