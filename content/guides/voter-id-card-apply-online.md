## What is a Voter ID Card?

A Voter ID Card (also called EPIC — Electors Photo Identity Card) is an identity document issued by the Election Commission of India. It serves as proof of identity and is required for voting in elections. It also works as a valid ID proof for many government services.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Indian citizen** who is a resident of the constituency
- **Minimum age:** 18 years (as on January 1 of the year of registration)
- Not disqualified under any law (e.g., unsound mind, convicted for certain offences)
- Not already registered as a voter in another constituency

> **Students/Working professionals:** You can register at your current residence even if your permanent address is different.

## Documents Required

**Proof of Age (any one):**
- Birth Certificate
- Class 10 Marksheet
- Passport
- Aadhaar Card
- PAN Card

**Proof of Address (any one):**
- Aadhaar Card
- Passport
- Utility Bill (electricity, water, gas — recent)
- Bank Passbook
- Ration Card
- Rent Agreement with latest utility bill

**Passport-size Photograph:**
- Recent colour photograph with white background

## Step-by-Step Process — Online

### Step 1: Visit NVSP Portal
Go to [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in) or [nvsp.in](https://www.nvsp.in)

### Step 2: Register / Login
- New users: Register with mobile number and OTP
- Existing users: Login with credentials

### Step 3: Select "New Voter Registration"
Click on **"Register as New Voter"** (Form 6)

### Step 4: Fill Personal Details
- Full name (as per documents)
- Father's/Mother's/Husband's name
- Date of birth
- Gender
- Mobile number and email

### Step 5: Enter Address Details
- Current residential address (where you want to vote)
- This determines your constituency and polling booth

### Step 6: Upload Documents
- Upload age proof
- Upload address proof
- Upload passport-size photo

### Step 7: Declaration and Submit
- Review all details carefully
- Submit the form
- Note the **Reference ID**

### Step 8: BLO Verification
A **Booth Level Officer (BLO)** will visit your address for physical verification. Keep your original documents ready.

### Step 9: Receive Voter ID
After verification, your name is added to the electoral roll and EPIC card is dispatched.

## Offline Process

1. Download **Form 6** from [eci.gov.in](https://eci.gov.in) or collect from local ERO office
2. Fill the form and paste photograph
3. Attach photocopies of age and address proof
4. Submit at the **Electoral Registration Officer (ERO)** office of your constituency
5. BLO visits for verification
6. EPIC card delivered after approval

## Fees

| Service | Fee |
|---------|-----|
| New Voter ID Registration | **Free** |
| Correction in Voter ID | **Free** |
| Replacement of lost EPIC | **Free** |
| Shifting to new constituency | **Free** |

> All voter ID services are completely free of cost.

## Processing Time

- **Online application:** 15-30 days (depends on BLO verification scheduling)
- **Offline:** 30-45 days
- **During special revision drives:** May be faster

## How to Track Application Status

1. Visit [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in)
2. Login with your credentials
3. Go to **"Track Application Status"**
4. Enter Reference ID
5. Check current status

## Download e-EPIC (Digital Voter ID)

1. Visit [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in)
2. Login → Click **"Download e-EPIC"**
3. Verify via OTP on registered mobile
4. Download PDF — this is your digital Voter ID

> e-EPIC is a valid identity document and can be stored on your phone via the **Voter Helpline App**.

## Important Tips

1. **Register before you turn 18** — You can apply from age 17 (added in next revision after you turn 18)
2. **Update address when you move** — File Form 6 for new constituency or Form 8A for corrections
3. **Download Voter Helpline App** — Check booth details, download e-EPIC, find BLO contact
4. **Verify your name in electoral roll** before every election at [electoralsearch.eci.gov.in](https://electoralsearch.eci.gov.in)

## FAQs

**Q: Can I apply for Voter ID card online?**
A: Yes. Visit voters.eci.gov.in, register with your mobile number, and fill Form 6 online. Upload documents and submit.

**Q: What is the minimum age for Voter ID?**
A: 18 years as on January 1 of the year of revision. You can apply at 17 and be enrolled when you turn 18.

**Q: How long does it take to get Voter ID?**
A: Typically 15-30 days after online application, depending on BLO verification. During special drives, it may be faster.

**Q: Is there any fee for Voter ID?**
A: No. All voter registration services are completely free.

**Q: Can I have Voter ID in two places?**
A: No. You can only be registered as a voter in one constituency. If you move, file Form 6 at your new address (your old registration will be cancelled).

**Q: What if I lost my Voter ID card?**
A: Download e-EPIC from voters.eci.gov.in. For physical card reprint, apply through the NVSP portal or contact your local ERO office.
