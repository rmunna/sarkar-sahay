## What is Madhu Babu Pension Yojana?

**Madhu Babu Pension Yojana (MBPY)** is the flagship social security pension scheme of the **Government of Odisha**, providing monthly pensions to elderly persons, widows, differently-abled individuals, and other vulnerable groups. Named after **Madhusudan Das** (Madhu Babu), a revered Odia leader, the scheme was launched by merging the **Revised Old Age Pension Rules** and the **Disability Pension Scheme**.

Administered by the **Social Security and Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (SSEPD) Department**, MBPY covers over **48 lakh beneficiaries** across Odisha, making it one of India's largest state pension programs.

## Who is Eligible?

### Old Age Pension
- Age: **60 years and above**
- Must be a **resident of Odisha** for at least 5 years
- Must belong to a **BPL (Below Poverty Line) family**
- Annual household income must be below **₹60,000** (for non-BPL applicants in some categories)

### Widow Pension
- **Widows of any age** belonging to BPL families
- Must not have remarried
- Must be a resident of Odisha

### Disability Pension
- Persons with **40% or more disability** (certified by medical board)
- Age: **5 years and above**
- Must be a resident of Odisha
- Must belong to a BPL family

### Other Categories
- **Leprosy patients** (any age)
- **AIDS/HIV patients** (certified)
- **Cohabiting unmarried women** aged 30+ (in some sub-categories)

### Who is NOT Eligible?
- Persons receiving pension from another government scheme (state or central)
- Government employees (serving or retired) receiving pension
- Income tax payers

## Benefits Provided

| Category | Monthly Pension |
|---|---|
| Age 60-79 years | ₹500 per month |
| Age 80 years and above | ₹700 per month |
| Widows | ₹500 per month |
| Persons with disability (40-79%) | ₹500 per month |
| Persons with disability (80%+) | ₹700 per month |
| Leprosy patients | ₹500-₹700 per month |

> **Note:** In addition to state pension, beneficiaries aged 60+ may also receive **₹200-₹500/month under IGNOAPS** (central scheme) on top of MBPY. Amounts are subject to periodic revision.

## Documents Required

### Identity & Residence
- **Aadhaar card** — see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
- **Voter ID card**
- **Ration card** (BPL) — refer to our [ration card guide](/guide/ration-card-apply-online)

### Age Proof (for old age pension)
- Birth certificate / School leaving certificate
- Voter ID showing date of birth
- Aadhaar card with date of birth

### Widow Proof
- **Death certificate** of husband
- **Marriage certificate** or affidavit

### Disability Proof
- **Disability certificate** from the District Medical Board (minimum 40% disability)
- **UDID (Unique Disability Identity Card)** if available

### Bank Details
- **Bank account passbook** (first page copy)
- Aadhaar-linked bank account required for DBT

### Additional
- **Passport-size photographs** (3 copies)
- **BPL certificate** or inclusion in BPL list

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Online Application

1. **Visit** the MBPY portal at [mbpy.odisha.gov.in](https://mbpy.odisha.gov.in) or SSEPD portal
2. **Click** "Apply Online" or "New Application"
3. **Enter** Aadhaar number for e-KYC verification
4. **Fill** the application form — personal details, category, bank details
5. **Upload** scanned documents (photograph, Aadhaar, age/disability proof)
6. **Submit** the application and save the reference number
7. **Track** application status on the portal

### Offline Application

1. **Visit** the **Block Development Office (BDO)** or **Gram Panchayat office**
2. **Collect** the MBPY application form (free of cost)
3. **Fill** the form completely in Odia or English
4. **Attach** self-attested photocopies of all documents
5. **Submit** to the **BDO** or **Panchayat Executive Officer**
6. **Collect** acknowledgement receipt
7. **Verification** will be done by the Block-level committee

### Through Mo Seva Kendra / CSC

1. Visit the nearest **Common Service Centre**
2. Provide your Aadhaar, photographs, and documents
3. Operator fills the application online
4. Collect printed acknowledgement

## Processing Time

- **Application review:** 30-60 days
- **Verification by BDO:** 15-30 days after submission
- **Approval by Sub-Collector/District Collector:** 30-45 days
- **First pension disbursement:** Within 1-2 months of approval
- **Monthly payments:** Credited by **1st week** of each month to bank account

## Important Tips

1. **Ensure BPL status** — Having your name in the BPL list significantly strengthens your application
2. **Link Aadhaar to bank** — All payments are via DBT; Aadhaar-bank linking is mandatory (see our [Aadhaar linking guide](/guide/aadhaar-linking-guide))
3. **Annual life verification** — Pensioners must complete annual life verification (Jeevan Pramaan) to continue receiving pension
4. **Report the death of a pensioner** — Family members should inform the BDO to avoid legal complications
5. **Check for arrears** — If approval is delayed, you are entitled to arrears from the date of application

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: How much pension will I get under MBPY?
**A:** Persons aged 60-79 and widows receive **₹500/month**. Persons aged 80+ and those with 80%+ disability receive **₹700/month**.

### Q2: Can I get both MBPY and PM-KISAN?
**A:** MBPY and PM-KISAN are different schemes. You can receive both if you are eligible for each separately. However, you cannot receive MBPY and another pension scheme simultaneously.

### Q3: How do I complete annual life verification?
**A:** Visit your bank branch with your Aadhaar card, or use the **Jeevan Pramaan app** for biometric verification online. This must be done once a year to continue pension.

### Q4: My application was rejected. Can I reapply?
**A:** Yes. Check the **rejection reason** on the portal, correct the issue, and **reapply**. Common reasons include incomplete documents, ineligibility, or already receiving another pension.

### Q5: Can a widow aged below 60 get the pension?
**A:** Yes. **Widows of any age** belonging to BPL families are eligible for the widow pension under MBPY. There is no minimum age requirement.

### Q6: How do I check my pension status or payment history?
**A:** Visit [mbpy.odisha.gov.in](https://mbpy.odisha.gov.in), enter your beneficiary ID or Aadhaar number, and view your payment status and history.

### Q7: Is there a pension for disabled children?
**A:** Yes. Children aged **5 years and above** with 40%+ disability, belonging to BPL families, are eligible for the disability pension under MBPY.
