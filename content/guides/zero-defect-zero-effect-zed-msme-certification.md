## What is ZED Certification?

**ZED (Zero Defect Zero Effect) Certification** is a Government of India programme under the Ministry of MSME that helps small and medium enterprises improve their **quality standards** and reduce environmental impact. Certified MSMEs get:

- **50–80% subsidy** on certification costs
- Quality rating (Bronze, Silver, Gold) recognised in government procurement
- Advantage in public procurement (GeM portal gives preference to ZED-certified MSMEs)
- Better access to export markets through quality credibility
- Free or subsidised handholding support to improve processes

| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Certification subsidy | 80% for micro, 60% for small, 50% for medium enterprises |
| Additional for SC/ST/women/NE | Extra 10% subsidy |
| ZED rating levels | Bronze → Silver → Gold (progressively higher standards) |
| GeM advantage | ZED-certified MSMEs preferred on Government e-Marketplace |
| Export credibility | ZED certificate recognized by EEPC and export councils |
| Handholding | Free consultants assigned to guide improvement |

## Who is Eligible?

**You ARE eligible if:**
- Your enterprise is registered as a **Micro, Small, or Medium Enterprise (MSME)** — with a valid **Udyam Registration Number (URN)**
- You are in manufacturing or service sector
- You are willing to undergo the ZED assessment process

**You are NOT eligible if:**
- You do not have Udyam Registration
- Your enterprise is above the MSME size threshold (turnover > ₹250 crore for medium)

---

## ZED Rating Levels

| Level | What it means |
|-------|--------------|
| Bronze | Basic quality processes in place — compliance with statutory requirements |
| Silver | Systematic quality management — ISO 9001 level practices |
| Gold | World-class quality and environmental management — export-ready |

Enterprises can upgrade their rating over time.

## Documents Required

1. **Udyam Registration Certificate** (URN) — mandatory
2. **Aadhaar Card** — of proprietor/director
3. **GST registration certificate** (if applicable)
4. **Bank account details** — for subsidy disbursement
5. **Recent financial statements** — balance sheet, P&L

## How to Apply — Step by Step

### Step 1 — Register on ZED Portal

Go to **[zed.msme.gov.in](https://zed.msme.gov.in)** and register with your:
- Udyam Registration Number
- Mobile number
- Aadhaar

### Step 2 — Self-Assessment

Complete the **online self-assessment questionnaire** covering:
- Quality practices (defect rates, return rates, customer complaints)
- Environmental practices (waste, energy, pollution)
- Production processes and workforce practices

### Step 3 — Handholding and Improvement

A **ZED consultant** is assigned (free for micro/small enterprises) to:
- Review your self-assessment
- Suggest improvements
- Help you achieve the rating you are targeting

### Step 4 — External Assessment

An **external assessor** (certified by Quality Council of India or Bureau of Indian Standards) visits your unit and assesses actual practices.

### Step 5 — Rating Certificate

Based on the assessment, you receive your **ZED rating certificate** (Bronze/Silver/Gold) — valid for 3 years.

### Step 6 — Subsidy on Certification Cost

Claim the subsidy (80% for micro, 60% for small, 50% for medium) on the assessor's fee and certification cost directly on the portal.

## ZED and GeM Marketplace

ZED-certified enterprises get:
- **Priority listing** on **Government e-Marketplace (gem.gov.in)**
- **Purchase preference** from government buyers
- Access to **Make in India** and **Atmanirbhar Bharat** procurement

This directly increases your chances of government contracts.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. I have Udyam registration but no ISO certification. Can I apply for ZED?**

Yes. ZED is a separate quality framework — you do not need ISO certification to start. ZED Bronze is for enterprises with basic quality practices in place.

**2. How much does ZED certification actually cost after subsidy?**

Assessment and certification cost is typically ₹20,000–₹80,000 before subsidy, depending on enterprise size. After 80% subsidy (for micro), net cost is ₹4,000–₹16,000.

**3. Does ZED certification expire?**

ZED certificates are valid for **3 years**. You can renew and upgrade your rating.

**4. What is the advantage of Gold vs Bronze ZED rating?**

Gold-rated enterprises have stronger credibility in domestic and export markets. For government procurement on GeM, all ZED-certified enterprises get preference, but Gold-rated ones rank higher.

**5. I export to Europe. Does ZED help with EU market access?**

ZED Gold is broadly equivalent to international quality management standards. While it is not a direct substitute for ISO or CE marking (required for EU), it demonstrates quality discipline that improves your credibility in export markets.
