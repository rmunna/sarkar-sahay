# Income Tax Refund Status Check & Delayed Refund Fix (2026)

Income tax refunds for ITR filed in 2025–26 should typically arrive within **4–5 weeks** of ITR processing. If your refund is delayed, showing "Failed", or you haven't received it in your bank account — this guide gives you the exact steps to fix it.

> **Note:** The tax department processes refunds after ITR is verified (e-verified) and assessed. Refund timeline starts from **assessment completion**, not from filing date.

---

## Check Income Tax Refund Status — Step by Step

### Method 1: Income Tax Portal (Main Method)

1. Go to **[incometax.gov.in](https://www.incometax.gov.in)**
2. Login with your **PAN + password**
3. Go to: **"e-File"** → **"Income Tax Returns"** → **"View Filed Returns"**
4. Find the relevant assessment year (e.g., AY 2025-26 for FY 2024-25 returns)
5. Click on the ITR → Look for **"Refund Status"** column
6. Or: **Dashboard** → **"Pending Actions"** → **"Refund Status"**

### Method 2: NSDL TIN Website

1. Go to **[tin.tin.nsdl.com/oltas/refundstatuslogin.html](https://tin.tin.nsdl.com)**
2. Enter: PAN Number + Assessment Year
3. Enter captcha → Submit
4. Status displays: Processed / Failed / In Progress / Credited

### Method 3: IT Portal Refund Reissue Page

1. Login at incometax.gov.in
2. **"Services"** → **"Refund Reissue"**
3. Shows current refund status + option to raise reissue request if failed

## Understanding Refund Status Messages

| Status | What It Means | Action |
|--------|--------------|--------|
| **Refund Issued** | Refund sent to bank | Check bank account / wait 5–7 days |
| **Refund Credited** | Money in bank account | Done — check account |
| **Refund Failed** | Bank account validation failed | Re-raise refund request (see below) |
| **Refund Returned** | Returned from bank | Update bank account + re-raise |
| **Processing** | ITR being processed | Wait |
| **Under Examination** | ITR selected for scrutiny | Respond to scrutiny notices |
| **No Refund** | Your computation shows no refund due | Check ITR calculation |

## Most Common Reason: Bank Account Validation Failed

The #1 reason for refund failure is **bank account not validated on the income tax portal**.

**Fix — Validate Bank Account on IT Portal:**

1. Login to **incometax.gov.in**
2. **"My Profile"** → **"My Bank Accounts"**
3. Check if your bank account is listed and shows **"Validated"** ✓
4. If not validated: Click **"Add Bank Account"**
5. Enter: Account number, IFSC, account type, bank name
6. IT portal will send a **penny drop test** (₹1 credit + debit) to verify
7. Once verified: Status changes to **"Validated"**
8. Only validated accounts receive tax refunds

**Important:** Account must be:
- Linked to your PAN
- In your name (not joint account where you're second holder)
- Active (not dormant)
- Mobile number linked to it must match ITR registered mobile

## Re-Raise Refund Request (If Refund Failed/Returned)

1. Login to **incometax.gov.in**
2. Go to: **"Services"** → **"Refund Reissue"**
3. Select the relevant ITR (Assessment Year)
4. Click **"Request for Refund Reissue"**
5. Choose bank account (must be validated)
6. Select mode: **"ECS (NEFT/RTGS)"** — standard choice
7. Submit
8. **Timeline after re-raise:** 7–21 working days

## ITR Not Processed — Refund Stuck

If your ITR status shows "Filed" but not "Processed" for more than 60 days:

**Check if e-verification is done:**
1. IT Portal → e-File → ITR → View Filed Returns
2. If status shows "Pending e-verification" → Your ITR is unverified
3. **E-verify immediately:** ITR → Actions → e-Verify Now
4. Options: Aadhaar OTP, net banking, bank account EVC, demat account

**After e-verification:** ITR enters processing queue. Typically processed in 3–6 weeks.

## If Refund Is Correct But Bank Rejected It

Banks reject IT refunds if:
- Account is **dormant** → Reactivate account first
- **Name mismatch** between PAN and bank account → Visit branch to correct name
- **Account closed** → Update new account on IT portal
- **Minimum balance issue** (rare) → Not a standard rejection reason

**Steps:**
1. Confirm account is active and in your name
2. Validate account on IT portal
3. Re-raise refund request

---

## Raise Grievance for Delayed Refund

If refund status shows "Issued" but money not received after 10 working days:

1. IT Portal → **"Grievance"** → **"Submit Grievance"**
2. Select: "Refund not received" → Fill details
3. Or: Login → **Help** → **"e-Nivaran"** → Submit complaint
4. Attach: Refund status screenshot, bank statement showing no credit
5. **Timeline:** 15–30 days for grievance resolution

**Helpdesk:** 1800-103-0025 (Income Tax Helpdesk, 8 AM – 8 PM)

## How Long Does Income Tax Refund Take?

| Scenario | Typical Timeline |
|----------|----------------|
| E-filed + e-verified, simple ITR | 15–30 days from processing |
| Filed early (April–May) | Often faster — 2–3 weeks |
| Filed after July 31 | Slower — 4–8 weeks |
| Refund failed + re-raised | Additional 7–21 days |
| Refund under scrutiny | 3–12 months |

*Timeline for AY 2025-26 (FY 2024-25). Department is getting faster — many refunds now arrive within 2–3 weeks.*

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I check income tax refund status without login?**
Yes — NSDL TIN website lets you check refund status with just PAN + Assessment Year (no password needed).

**My refund status shows "Credited" but I don't see it in my account — what do I do?**
It may take 5–7 working days for bank to reflect. If not received in 7 working days after "Credited" status: Call bank customer care with the refund reference number shown on IT portal.

**Income tax sent refund to a closed bank account — how to get it back?**
The refund bounces back to IT department within 30 days. Login to IT portal → Validate a new active account → Re-raise refund request. The amount goes to your new account.

**My refund amount is less than expected — why?**
IT department may have:
1. Disallowed certain deductions claimed
2. Made tax demand adjustment against old outstanding tax
3. Calculated tax differently
Check "Intimation under Section 143(1)" in IT portal for detailed computation.

**Can I get interest on delayed income tax refund?**
Yes — if refund is delayed by more than 3 months from the end of FY in which ITR was filed, IT department pays interest at 6% per annum on the refund amount (Section 244A). This is added automatically when refund is processed.
