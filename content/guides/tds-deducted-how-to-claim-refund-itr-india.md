# TDS Deducted — How to Claim Full Refund in ITR India 2026

TDS (Tax Deducted at Source) is not a final tax — it's an advance payment. If your total income is below the taxable limit, or if TDS was deducted at a higher rate than your actual tax, you get it all back as a refund when you file ITR.

---

## Common Situations Where TDS Refund is Due

| Situation | TDS Rate Deducted | Who Gets Refund |
|-----------|------------------|-----------------|
| Bank FD interest, income below ₹12L (new regime) | 10% | Everyone with income below taxable limit |
| Employer deducted excess TDS (salary projection changed) | Per slab | Employee |
| Freelancer/consultant TDS, but income after deductions is low | 10% | Freelancer/consultant |
| Rent received, TDS by tenant (194IB), but house property loss | 10% | Landlord |
| Foreign remittance TCS (LRS travel, education) | 5–20% | Remitter |
| NRI who became resident mid-year, extra TDS deducted | Various | NRI |
| EPFO TDS on withdrawal (less than 5 years service) | 10% or 30% | Employee (if total income is low) |

## Step 1: Check Your TDS in 26AS and AIS

Before filing ITR, verify all TDS deducted in your name:

1. Login to [incometax.gov.in](https://incometax.gov.in) → PAN/Aadhaar + OTP
2. Go to **e-File → Income Tax Returns → View Form 26AS** (or Services → Annual Information Statement)
3. Form 26AS shows: TDS by employer (Part A), TDS by banks/others (Part B), TCS (Part C), advance tax paid
4. AIS shows expanded view — all 50+ sources including dividends, MF redemptions, savings interest

**Match against what was actually deducted**: Check your salary slips, FD maturity receipts, and freelance payment proofs. Discrepancies in 26AS need to be raised with the deductor (your employer or bank) to file a TDS correction statement.

---

## Step 2: Calculate Your Actual Tax Liability

Your actual tax = Tax on total income (after all deductions) − any applicable rebate

**Common reasons TDS is more than actual tax**:
- Your income is below ₹12L (new regime) → zero tax due to 87A rebate
- You have Section 80C deductions under old regime that your employer didn't account for
- You have home loan interest (Section 24b) or HRA exemption that employer didn't apply
- You submitted Form 15G/H to bank (no FD TDS) but employer's 26AS still shows it
- Multiple employers in same year — each deducted TDS without knowing the other

---

## Step 3: File ITR with Correct Income and TDS Credit

The key: **always declare the full income** even if TDS was already deducted on it. The ITR computes your total tax, then subtracts TDS already paid. If TDS > tax due, the difference is your refund.

**ITR filing process**:

1. Go to incometax.gov.in → e-File → Income Tax Returns → File ITR
2. Select the correct ITR form:
   - **ITR-1**: Salaried, only FD interest, no capital gains (income ≤₹50L)
   - **ITR-2**: Capital gains, multiple income heads
   - **ITR-3 / ITR-4**: Freelance/business income
3. Income details pre-fill from AIS/26AS → verify and correct if needed
4. Under "Tax Details" (Schedule TDS): all TDS deducted auto-populates from 26AS
5. Deductions: Enter 80C, 80D, HRA, etc. under old regime (or accept new regime defaults)
6. Tax computation screen shows: Total tax payable − TDS already paid = **Refund due**
7. Submit and e-verify (Aadhaar OTP is fastest)

**After e-verify**: ITR is processed. Refund reaches your Aadhaar-linked bank account.

## Special Case: FD TDS Refund

Bank deducts TDS at 10% if FD interest exceeds ₹40,000/year (₹50,000 for senior citizens).

**If total income is below ₹7L** (new regime): Zero tax due. Entire TDS = refund.

**Example**: Salary ₹6L/year + FD interest ₹60,000.
- Total income: ₹6.6L
- Tax (new regime): Zero (₹12L rebate under 87A applies; income well below ₹12L)
- TDS deducted by bank: ₹6,000 (10% on ₹60,000)
- **Refund: ₹6,000**

**How to avoid FD TDS in future (if income is below taxable limit)**:
Submit **Form 15G** (below 60 years) or **Form 15H** (60+ years) to your bank at the start of every financial year. Bank doesn't deduct TDS. Forms declare that your total income is below taxable limit.

## Special Case: Employer Deducted Excess TDS

Employer projects your salary for the year and deducts TDS monthly. If you:
- Changed jobs mid-year (new employer didn't account for old employer's income)
- Had higher deductions than employer assumed (home loan taken after April)
- Received a variable pay in Q1 that employer projected as recurring

**All TDS from all employers shows in 26AS**. You file ITR combining income from both employers and all your actual deductions. If total TDS > actual tax = refund.

**Preventive fix**: Submit declaration to your employer in April about your deductions (home loan interest certificate, HRA claim, 80C investments). Most employers take this at face value and reduce TDS accordingly.

---

## Special Case: Freelancer / Consultant TDS

When you invoice a company for ₹1,00,000:
- If aggregate payments to you exceed ₹30,000/year: Client deducts **10% TDS** (₹10,000) under Section 194J (professional services) or 194C (contractor)
- If you're a foreign-client freelancer (USDINR invoices): No Indian TDS, but income is still taxable in India

**Your actual tax** depends on your Section 44ADA (50% deemed profit for eligible professionals) or full accounts. After deductions, many freelancers in the ₹5–15L range pay much less than 10% effective tax.

**File ITR-3 or ITR-4**: Show total receipts, deduct allowable expenses (or use 44ADA), compute tax. TDS credit reduces your tax. Refund = TDS paid − actual tax.

## How Long Does TDS Refund Take?

After filing and e-verifying ITR:
- **Processing**: 3–6 weeks for ITR-1, 4–8 weeks for ITR-2/3/4
- **Refund credit**: 3–8 weeks from processing date (via NEFT to your bank)
- **Total timeline**: 4–12 weeks for most cases

**Check refund status**: incometax.gov.in → e-File → Income Tax Returns → View Filed Returns → Click on the year → Refund Status shows.

Or check at: [tin.tin.nsdl.com](https://tin.tin.nsdl.com) → TAN Based View → enter your PAN.

## Why Your Refund Might Be Delayed or Zero

### Bank Account Not Pre-Validated
Bank account must be pre-validated (PAN + IFSC linked to IT portal) to receive refund. Add and validate at: incometax.gov.in → My Profile → Bank Account → Add Bank Account → Validate.

### Name Mismatch in Bank Account
PAN name must exactly match bank account name. Even small differences (initials vs full name) delay refund. Fix the mismatch with your bank.

### Outstanding Tax Demand
If you have a previous year's demand, IT dept adjusts refund against demand first. Check: e-File → Pending Actions → Response to Outstanding Demand.

### ITR Processing Delay
Complex returns (with multiple capital gains, foreign income, etc.) take longer. If 12 weeks passed: raise a grievance at incometax.gov.in → Help → Grievances.

### Refund Re-Issue Needed
If refund was returned (wrong account number, account closed): File refund re-issue request at incometax.gov.in → Services → Refund Reissue → Select your bank account → Submit.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My income is ₹4L and bank deducted ₹8,000 TDS on FD. Will I get it back?

Yes — full ₹8,000 refund. Your income of ₹4L is below ₹12L (new regime zero-tax limit). File ITR-1, declare FD interest, take TDS credit. Refund should arrive within 4–8 weeks of filing.

### Can I get TDS refund without filing ITR?

No. Filing ITR is the only way to claim TDS refund. There's no other mechanism. Even if you have zero income other than FD interest, you must file ITR to claim the refund.

### I didn't file ITR last year. Can I still claim TDS refund from that year?

Yes — file a belated ITR under Section 139(4) by December 31 of the relevant Assessment Year. For FY 2024-25 (AY 2025-26), belated ITR deadline is December 31, 2025. After that, you can file an Updated Return under Section 139(8A) for 2 years (additional tax applies on Updated Return if there's tax due).

### Employer deducted TDS but didn't deposit it. What can I do?

If TDS was deducted from your salary but not deposited to the government (shows as deducted in Form 16 but NOT in your 26AS), you can still claim TDS credit. Supreme Court ruling: taxpayer is not penalized for deductor's default. File ITR claiming credit; if demand is raised, respond explaining the situation. The department recovers from the defaulting employer.

### What if AIS shows TDS but I never received the payment?

Raise feedback in AIS portal: "Income/TDS does not pertain to me" or "Income has been incorrectly reported." Also contact the deductor to investigate. This happens when deductor's records have wrong PAN.
