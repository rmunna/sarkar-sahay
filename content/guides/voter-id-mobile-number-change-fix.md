# Voter ID Mobile Number Change Not Working: How to Fix

Your mobile number linked to your Voter ID (EPIC) is essential for many Election Commission services — downloading your e-EPIC, logging into the NVSP portal, making corrections, and receiving OTPs for verification. If your linked number is old, inactive, or incorrect, you may get locked out of critical services.

This guide covers how to update your mobile number and fix common problems when the change isn't working.

## Why Your Linked Mobile Number Matters

The mobile number registered with your Voter ID is used for:

- **OTP verification** for downloading e-EPIC (digital Voter ID)
- **NVSP portal login** — OTP is sent to registered mobile
- **Online corrections** to name, address, photo, or other details via Form 8
- **Voter Helpline app** authentication
- **Election-day SMS alerts** and polling booth information

If your registered number is no longer active or accessible, you cannot perform any of these actions online.

## How to Change Mobile Number Linked to Voter ID

There are two main ways to update your mobile number: online (NVSP/Voter Portal) and offline (through BLO/ERO).

### Method 1: Update via Voter Portal (voters.eci.gov.in)

**Note:** You need access to your currently registered mobile number to receive the OTP for login. If you don't have access to the old number, skip to [Method 3 (Offline Route)](#method-3-offline-route-through-bloero).

1. Go to [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in/)
2. Click **Login** and enter your EPIC number or registered mobile number
3. Enter the OTP received on your **currently registered mobile number**
4. After login, go to your **Profile** section
5. Look for the option to **Edit Profile** or **Update Mobile Number**
6. Enter your **new mobile number**
7. You will receive an OTP on the **new number** — enter it to verify
8. Submit the change — your mobile number will be updated

### Method 2: Update via Voter Helpline App

1. Download the **Voter Helpline** app from [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.eci.citizen) or Apple App Store
2. Open the app and tap **Login**
3. Enter your EPIC number and verify with OTP on your registered mobile
4. Go to **My Profile** or **Account Settings**
5. Tap **Edit** next to your mobile number
6. Enter the new mobile number and verify with OTP sent to the new number
7. Save changes

### Method 3: Offline Route Through BLO/ERO

If you **cannot access your old registered mobile number**, this is the most reliable method:

1. Visit your **nearest Electoral Registration Office (ERO)** or contact your **Booth Level Officer (BLO)**
2. Carry the following documents:
   - Original Voter ID card (EPIC)
   - Aadhaar card (for identity verification)
   - New mobile number's SIM (active and ready to receive OTP)
3. Request a **mobile number update** — the BLO/ERO can update your mobile number in the electoral roll system
4. You may need to fill a simple application or Form 8 (correction form)
5. The BLO will verify your identity and update the number
6. You will receive a confirmation SMS on your **new number** once updated

**How to find your BLO:** Visit [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in/), log in (or use the search feature), and look for "Know Your BLO" section. Alternatively, call the **Voter Helpline at 1950** (toll-free).

## Common Problems and Fixes

### Problem 1: OTP Not Received on Registered Mobile

**Symptoms:** You click "Send OTP" but no SMS arrives.

**Fixes:**
- Wait at least 2-3 minutes — NVSP servers can be slow
- Check if your phone has **DND (Do Not Disturb)** activated — disable it via your telecom provider (SMS "STOP" to 1909)
- Ensure your phone has network signal and SMS storage is not full
- Try during **off-peak hours** (early morning or late evening) — the portal gets heavy traffic during elections
- Clear browser cache and try again
- Try on a different browser or device
- If using the app, force close and reopen it

### Problem 2: Old Mobile Number No Longer Active

**Symptoms:** You changed your SIM or your old number is deactivated, so you can't receive the login OTP.

**Fixes:**
- **Get the old number reactivated:** Contact your telecom provider — if the number was recently deactivated (within 90 days), you may be able to get a new SIM with the same number
- **Use the offline route:** Visit your BLO/ERO with your Voter ID and Aadhaar to update the number without needing OTP (see [Method 3](#method-3-offline-route-through-bloero) above)
- **Call 1950:** Explain your situation to the Voter Helpline — they can guide you to the nearest ERO office

### Problem 3: Can't Login to NVSP Portal

**Symptoms:** Login page shows errors, keeps loading, or rejects your EPIC number.

**Fixes:**
- The old NVSP portal (nvsp.in) has been largely replaced by [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in/) — use the new portal
- Clear cookies and cache, or use an incognito/private window
- Try a different browser (Chrome or Firefox recommended)
- If the portal shows "Invalid EPIC Number," double-check your Voter ID card for the correct number
- Try again after some time — the portal may be under maintenance

### Problem 4: Mobile Number Update Option Not Visible

**Symptoms:** You logged in successfully but can't find where to change the mobile number.

**Fixes:**
- Look under **Profile** → **Edit** or **Account Settings**
- The Voter Portal interface changes periodically — try both the website and the Voter Helpline app
- If the option is genuinely missing, use the offline BLO/ERO route

### Problem 5: New OTP Not Arriving on New Number

**Symptoms:** You entered the new mobile number but the verification OTP doesn't arrive on it.

**Fixes:**
- Ensure the new number is active and can receive SMS
- Check DND status on the new number
- Wait 2-3 minutes and click "Resend OTP"
- Ensure you entered the correct 10-digit number
- Try again after 30 minutes if the system shows "Too many OTP requests"

## Important Tips

1. **Always keep your Voter ID mobile number updated** — update it whenever you change your phone number
2. **DND is the #1 reason for OTP failures** — send "STOP" to 1909 from your registered number to disable it
3. **The offline BLO/ERO route always works** — when all online methods fail, this is your fallback
4. **Carry Aadhaar along with Voter ID** when visiting the ERO office for faster verification
5. **Save the Voter Helpline number: 1950** — it's toll-free and available across India

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I change my Voter ID mobile number without OTP?

Not online. The NVSP portal and Voter Helpline app require OTP verification on the registered number for login. However, you can change it **offline** by visiting your BLO or ERO office with your Voter ID and Aadhaar card — no OTP needed.

### How long does the mobile number update take?

Online updates are usually **instant** — the new number is linked immediately after OTP verification. Offline updates through the BLO/ERO may take **3-7 working days** to reflect in the system.

### Can I update my mobile number using Form 8?

Yes. Form 8 is the correction form for Voter ID details. While it is mainly used for name, address, and photo corrections, you can include a mobile number update request. Submit Form 8 online at [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in/) or offline at the ERO office.

### What if I never registered a mobile number with my Voter ID?

Older Voter IDs may not have a mobile number linked. You can add one by:
- Visiting [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in/) and registering with your EPIC number
- Using the Voter Helpline app to create an account
- Visiting your BLO/ERO to link a mobile number to your voter record

### Is there a fee to change the mobile number on Voter ID?

No. Updating your mobile number linked to your Voter ID is **completely free** — both online and offline.

### Can I link the same mobile number to multiple Voter IDs?

Yes, the same mobile number can be linked to multiple Voter IDs (for example, family members). Each Voter ID is identified by its unique EPIC number.

### The NVSP portal shows my old mobile number. How do I know which number is linked?

When you try to login at [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in/), the system will show a **partially masked version** of your registered number (e.g., ******7890). This helps you identify which number is currently linked.
