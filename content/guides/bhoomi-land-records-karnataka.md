# Karnataka Bhoomi Land Records — Check RTC, Mutation Status Online (2026)

Karnataka's **Bhoomi** portal is one of India's oldest and most successful land-record digitisation projects. Through it, any citizen can view their **RTC (Record of Rights, Tenancy and Crops)**, check **mutation status**, download **Khatha extracts**, and more — all online, for free.

This guide walks you through every service available on the Bhoomi portal in 2026.

> Also see our [land records / Bhulekh guide](/guide/land-records-bhulekh-online) for other states.

---

## What Is the Bhoomi Portal?

**Bhoomi** (ಭೂಮಿ) is the Karnataka Revenue Department's online land records system, accessible at [landrecords.karnataka.gov.in](https://landrecords.karnataka.gov.in). Launched in 2000, it digitised over 20 million land records across 176 taluks.

**What you can do on Bhoomi:**

- View and download RTC / Pahani (ಪಹಣಿ)
- Check mutation (ಹಕ್ಕು ಬದಲಾವಣೆ) application status
- Apply for mutation online
- Download Khatha extract
- View revenue maps and survey sketches
- Apply for land conversion
- Verify RTC XML authenticity

---

## What Is RTC / Pahani?

**RTC** stands for **Record of Rights, Tenancy and Crops** — locally called **Pahani** (ಪಹಣಿ). It is the primary land ownership document in Karnataka and contains:

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Owner name(s)** | Current title holder(s) |
| **Survey number** | Unique plot identifier |
| **Land area** | Extent in acres/guntas |
| **Land type** | Agricultural, non-agricultural, etc. |
| **Crop details** | Current/last cultivated crop |
| **Tenancy info** | If any tenant is recorded |
| **Encumbrance** | Loans, liens, court orders |
| **Mutation history** | Past ownership transfers |

You need an RTC for: bank loans, property sales, government schemes, court disputes, and Aadhaar-linked land verification.

## How to Check RTC Online (Step-by-Step)

### Method 1: View RTC on Bhoomi Portal

1. Go to [landrecords.karnataka.gov.in](https://landrecords.karnataka.gov.in)
2. Click **"View RTC and MR"** under Citizen Services
3. Select your **District → Taluk → Hobli → Village**
4. Enter your **Survey Number** (or owner name / Khatha number)
5. Click **"Fetch Details"**
6. Your RTC/Pahani will be displayed — you can view or print it

### Method 2: i-RTC (Digitally Signed RTC)

For a **legally valid, digitally signed** copy:

1. Visit [rtc.karnataka.gov.in/Service78](https://rtc.karnataka.gov.in/Service78)
2. Select District → Taluk → Hobli → Village → Survey Number
3. Pay ₹15 online (fees may change)
4. Download the digitally signed i-RTC PDF
5. Verify authenticity at [RTC XML Verification](https://landrecords.karnataka.gov.in/service5)

> **Tip:** Banks and courts increasingly accept i-RTC as valid. Always verify using the XML verification link.

### Method 3: Dishaank Mobile App

The **Dishaank** app (by Karnataka Revenue Department) lets you check RTC on your phone:

1. Download **Dishaank** from [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.karnataka.dishaank)
2. Open the app and select **"RTC Information"**
3. Choose District → Taluk → Hobli → Village
4. Enter Survey Number
5. View your Pahani instantly

The app also supports GPS-based land identification — stand on your plot and the app can identify the survey number.

## How to Check Mutation Status Online

**Mutation** (ಹಕ್ಕು ಬದಲಾವಣೆ) is the process of updating the RTC when land ownership changes (sale, inheritance, gift, partition, etc.).

### Check Mutation Status

1. Go to [landrecords.karnataka.gov.in](https://landrecords.karnataka.gov.in)
2. Click **"Bhoomi RTC Mutation History"** under Report Services
3. Select District → Taluk → Hobli → Village → Survey Number
4. View the mutation register (MR) showing all recorded changes
5. Pending mutations show current stage of processing

### Understanding Mutation Status

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Approved** | Mutation complete, RTC updated |
| **Pending** | Application under review |
| **Objection raised** | Someone has objected; hearing scheduled |
| **Rejected** | Application denied (reasons will be stated) |

## How to Apply for Mutation Online

Since the introduction of **Bhoomi Online Mutation Services**, you can apply without visiting the Tahsildar office:

### Steps to Apply

1. Visit [landrecords.karnataka.gov.in/service93/citizen](https://landrecords.karnataka.gov.in/service93/citizen)
2. **Register / Login** with your mobile number and Aadhaar
3. Select **mutation type**: Sale, Inheritance, Gift, Partition, Court Order, etc.
4. Enter **property details**: District, Taluk, Village, Survey Number
5. **Upload documents**:
   - Sale deed / Gift deed / Death certificate (as applicable)
   - Aadhaar card of new owner
   - Previous RTC copy
   - Passport-size photo
6. Pay the **application fee** online (₹100–₹500 depending on type)
7. Submit and note your **application number**
8. Track status using the same portal

### Processing Time

- Simple mutations (sale deed): **15–30 days**
- Inheritance/succession: **30–60 days** (requires notice period)
- Disputed cases: Can take **several months**

> **Important:** The Tahsildar may issue a notice period of 30 days for objections. If no objections are received, mutation is approved.

## How to View Khatha Extract Online

The **Khatha extract** shows property details for urban/municipal areas:

1. Go to [landrecords.karnataka.gov.in/service53](https://landrecords.karnataka.gov.in/service53)
2. Select District → Taluk → Hobli → Village
3. Enter Survey Number or owner name
4. View/download the Khatha extract

## How to Get a Certified RTC Copy

If you need a **physically certified** copy (stamped by Tahsildar):

### Option 1: Nada Kacheri (Taluk Office)

1. Visit your Taluk's **Nada Kacheri** (ನಾಡ ಕಚೇರಿ)
2. Fill in the application form
3. Pay ₹15 per copy
4. Collect the stamped RTC — usually issued **same day**

### Option 2: Atalji Jana Snehi Kendra / CSC

1. Visit your nearest **Common Service Centre (CSC)**
2. Provide survey number and village details
3. Pay the service fee
4. Receive certified print

### Option 3: i-RTC (Digital Certified Copy)

As described above, the i-RTC from [rtc.karnataka.gov.in](https://rtc.karnataka.gov.in/Service78) is a digitally signed, legally valid alternative.

## Other Useful Services on Bhoomi

### Revenue Maps

View village-level revenue survey maps at [landrecords.karnataka.gov.in/service3](https://landrecords.karnataka.gov.in/service3).

### RTC with Sketch (Beta)

A newer feature that shows your RTC along with a survey sketch of the land parcel, available at [rdservices.karnataka.gov.in/BhoomiMaps/](https://rdservices.karnataka.gov.in/BhoomiMaps/).

Apply online to convert agricultural land to non-agricultural use via [landrecords.karnataka.gov.in/citizenportal](https://landrecords.karnataka.gov.in/citizenportal). Track status at [landconversion.karnataka.gov.in/service80](https://landconversion.karnataka.gov.in/service80).

### Dispute Cases & Pendency

Check pending dispute cases village-wise at [landrecords.karnataka.gov.in/service22](https://landrecords.karnataka.gov.in/service22).

## Common Issues and Solutions

### "Survey number not found"

- **Check spelling** of village name (try Kannada and English variants)
- Ensure you selected the **correct Hobli** — villages can have similar names across hoblis
- The survey number may have been **subdivided** (e.g., 45/1, 45/2). Try the parent number
- Contact the Village Accountant (ಗ್ರಾಮ ಲೆಕ್ಕಿಗ) for clarification

### "Website not loading / slow"

- The portal experiences heavy traffic during working hours (10 AM – 4 PM). **Try early morning or after 6 PM**
- Use the **Dishaank app** as an alternative
- Clear your browser cache and try again

### "RTC shows wrong owner name"

- File a **mutation application** with supporting documents
- If the error is a clerical mistake, file a correction request at the Tahsildar office
- For disputed ownership, consult a revenue lawyer

### "Mutation pending for months"

- Check status online — if stuck, file an **RTI application** to the Tahsildar
- Visit the Tahsildar office with your application number
- Escalate to the **Assistant Commissioner** if unresolved beyond 90 days
- Use the [Bhoomi Dashboard](https://landrecords.karnataka.gov.in/service127) to check village-wise pendency

### "i-RTC verification fails"

- Ensure you're using the latest downloaded copy (not an older version)
- Use the official verification page: [landrecords.karnataka.gov.in/service5](https://landrecords.karnataka.gov.in/service5)
- The XML file must not be modified after download

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q: What is RTC (Pahani) and why do I need it?
RTC stands for Record of Rights, Tenancy and Crops, locally called Pahani. It is the primary land ownership document in Karnataka containing owner details, survey number, land area, crop details, and encumbrance information. You need it for bank loans, property sales, government schemes, and court disputes.

### Q: How can I check my land records online in Karnataka?
Visit landrecords.karnataka.gov.in and click "View RTC and MR." Select your District, Taluk, Hobli, and Village, then enter your survey number. You can also use the Dishaank mobile app for quick access on your phone.

### Q: What is i-RTC and is it legally valid?
i-RTC is a digitally signed RTC copy available for ₹15 from rtc.karnataka.gov.in. It is legally valid and increasingly accepted by banks and courts. You can verify its authenticity using the RTC XML verification page on the Bhoomi portal.

### Q: How long does mutation take in Karnataka?
Simple mutations based on a sale deed take 15–30 days. Inheritance or succession mutations take 30–60 days because of a mandatory 30-day objection notice period. Disputed cases can take several months.

### Q: What should I do if my survey number is not found on the Bhoomi portal?
Check the spelling of your village name in both Kannada and English. Ensure you selected the correct Hobli, as villages can have similar names across hoblis. The survey number may have been subdivided — try the parent number. Contact your Village Accountant for clarification.

### Q: How do I apply for mutation online in Karnataka?
Visit landrecords.karnataka.gov.in/service93/citizen and register with your mobile number and Aadhaar. Select the mutation type, enter property details, upload documents like sale deed or death certificate, pay the application fee (₹100–₹500), and submit.

### Q: What is the Bhoomi helpline number?
The Bhoomi toll-free helpline number is 1800-425-9908. You can also email bhoomi.helpdesk@karnataka.gov.in or visit your local Taluk office for in-person help.
