# UCO Bank Account Blocked or Frozen? How to Unblock (2026)

UCO Bank, headquartered in Kolkata, is a public sector bank with over 3,000 branches. If your UCO Bank account is blocked or frozen, this guide walks you through every option to restore it.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is independent. For account-specific issues, contact UCO Bank at 1800-274-0123.

---

## Why Is Your UCO Bank Account Blocked?

| Reason | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| KYC not updated | Complete re-KYC online or at branch |
| PAN not linked | Link PAN via net banking or branch |
| Dormant account | Reactivation at branch |
| Suspected fraud/government order | Call customer care + branch visit |
| Minimum balance | Pay maintenance fee (rare for salary accounts) |

---

## Check the Reason

1. **SMS alerts** — UCO Bank sends block notification via SMS
2. **Call 1800-274-0123** — staff can confirm the exact reason
3. **UCO mBanking Plus app** — may show KYC update prompt

---

## Unblock UCO Bank Account Online

### Via UCO mBanking Plus App

1. Open **UCO mBanking Plus** app
2. Login → **Services → Account Services**
3. If KYC update prompt visible: Upload Aadhaar + PAN
4. Raise **Service Request → Account Reactivation** for other issues

### Via UCO Bank Net Banking

1. Login at **[ucobank.com](https://www.ucobank.com)** → Online Banking
2. Navigate to **Profile → KYC Update**
3. Upload documents or submit service request

---

## Call UCO Bank Customer Care

**Toll-Free:** **1800-274-0123**
**Alternate:** 1800-103-0123

When calling, say: "My UCO Bank account XXXX has debit restrictions. I need to know why and how to fix it."

## Visit UCO Bank Branch

1. Visit your home branch
2. Carry: Aadhaar, PAN, passbook, passport photos (2)
3. Fill Account Reactivation + KYC Update form
4. Get written acknowledgment
5. Processing time: **1–2 working days**

---

## UCO Bank KYC Update

**Via UCO mBanking Plus:**
1. App → Profile → Re-KYC
2. Upload Aadhaar + PAN photos (clear, self-attested)
3. Timeline: 1–2 working days

**At Branch:**
- Fill KYC form, submit documents
- Processed same day at counter

---

## UCO Bank Dormant Account Reactivation

1. Visit home branch
2. Fill Dormant Account Reactivation form
3. Submit fresh KYC documents + photo
4. Do one cash deposit (any amount)
5. Account active: 1 working day

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long to unblock UCO Bank account?**
KYC update: 1–2 days. Dormant reactivation: 1 day. Fraud-related hold: 3–7 working days.

**Can UCO Bank KYC be updated without visiting branch?**
Yes — UCO mBanking Plus and net banking support digital document upload. For dormant or complex cases, branch visit may still be needed.

**My UCO Bank debit card works but ATM says "account blocked" — why?**
The account may have a debit freeze but credit operations are fine. Call 1800-274-0123 to confirm the exact restriction type.
