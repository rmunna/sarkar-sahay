# Aadhaar eKYC Failed at Bank? How to Fix Aadhaar-Based KYC Issues

You walk into your bank or try to open an account online, and the Aadhaar eKYC process fails. The bank says they can't verify your identity. This is more common than you think — thousands of Indians face eKYC failures daily due to biometric issues, server problems, or Aadhaar data mismatches.

Here's how to diagnose and fix every type of Aadhaar eKYC failure.

## Types of Aadhaar eKYC and Where They Fail

| Type | How It Works | Common Failure Point |
|---|---|---|
| **Biometric eKYC** | Fingerprint/iris scan at bank | Worn fingerprints, dirty scanner, eye issues |
| **OTP-based eKYC** | OTP sent to Aadhaar-linked mobile | Mobile number not linked or changed |
| **Offline eKYC** | XML file download from UIDAI | File expired or bank doesn't accept it |
| **Digilocker eKYC** | Aadhaar via Digilocker app | App errors, document not fetched |

## Common Error Messages and Fixes

### Error: "Biometric Authentication Failed" / "Fingerprint Mismatch"

This is the most common eKYC failure. Your fingerprint at the bank doesn't match UIDAI records.

**Causes:**
- Worn or damaged fingerprints (manual labourers, elderly)
- Dry or wet fingers
- Dirty biometric scanner at bank
- Outdated biometric data in UIDAI system

**Fixes:**
1. **Clean your fingers** — wash hands, dry thoroughly
2. **Ask the bank to clean the scanner** — dust/oil causes false rejections
3. **Try different fingers** — all 10 fingerprints are registered
4. **Try iris scan** if the bank has an iris scanner
5. **Switch to OTP-based eKYC** (see below)
6. **Update biometrics** at nearest Aadhaar Enrolment Centre (₹100 fee, appointment on uidai.gov.in)

### Error: "OTP Not Received" / "Mobile Number Not Registered"

For OTP-based eKYC, your Aadhaar must have a **linked mobile number**.

**How to check:**
1. Visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/)
2. Login with Aadhaar number
3. Check if your current mobile number is linked

**If mobile is not linked:**
1. Visit nearest **Aadhaar Enrolment Centre**
2. Carry original Aadhaar card + mobile phone with the new SIM
3. Submit **Aadhaar Update Form** for mobile number update
4. Biometric verification will be done
5. Mobile update takes **7-30 days** to reflect
6. You'll receive SMS confirmation when updated

### Error: "Aadhaar Authentication is Locked"

If you previously locked your Aadhaar for security:

1. Go to [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/)
2. Login → **"Lock/Unlock Aadhaar"**
3. **Unlock** your Aadhaar
4. Retry eKYC at the bank
5. **Re-lock** your Aadhaar after successful eKYC (recommended for security)

### Error: "UIDAI Server Down" / "Technical Error"

UIDAI servers face downtime, especially during:
- Peak hours (11 AM – 2 PM)
- Month-end when banks process bulk verifications
- Government scheme enrollment drives

**Fix:** Simply retry after 2-3 hours, or try the next day morning.

### Error: "Name/Address Mismatch Between Aadhaar and Bank Records"

Banks sometimes reject eKYC if your name or address in Aadhaar differs from bank records.

**Fix:**
1. Check your Aadhaar details at [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/)
2. Either **update Aadhaar** to match bank records, or **update bank records** to match Aadhaar
3. Name spelling must be **exactly identical** — even "Raj Kumar" vs "Rajkumar" causes failure

## Alternative KYC Methods When eKYC Fails

If Aadhaar eKYC repeatedly fails, you can complete KYC using:

1. **Offline Aadhaar XML:**
   - Download from [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/) → "Download Aadhaar"
   - Share the XML file and share code with the bank
   - Valid for limited time — download fresh before visiting

2. **Physical KYC Documents:**
   - Passport
   - Voter ID (EPIC)
   - Driving Licence
   - NREGA Job Card
   - Any OVD (Officially Valid Document)

3. **Video KYC (V-KYC):**
   - Many banks now offer video call-based KYC
   - Show your Aadhaar + face verification on video
   - Available in bank apps like SBI YONO, ICICI iMobile

## How to Update Aadhaar Biometrics

If biometric failure is recurring, update your biometrics:

1. Book appointment at [uidai.gov.in](https://uidai.gov.in/) → "Book an Appointment"
2. Visit Aadhaar Enrolment Centre with:
   - Original Aadhaar card
   - Any other valid ID proof
3. Request **biometric update** (fingerprints + iris + photograph)
4. Fee: **₹100** (pay at centre)
5. Receive **Update Request Number (URN)**
6. Track status at [uidai.gov.in](https://uidai.gov.in/) using URN
7. Update reflects in **7-30 days**

## Filing Complaint for Repeated eKYC Failure

If you've tried everything and eKYC still fails:

1. **UIDAI Helpline:** Call **1947** (toll-free)
2. **Online complaint:** [uidai.gov.in](https://uidai.gov.in/) → Contact & Support → File Complaint
3. **Bank complaint:** File with your bank's grievance cell
4. **Banking Ombudsman:** If bank refuses alternative KYC — [rbi.org.in/Scripts/Complaints.aspx](https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/Complaints.aspx)

## Quick Summary

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Biometric failed | Clean fingers, try other fingers, try iris, update biometrics |
| OTP not received | Link mobile number at Aadhaar centre |
| Aadhaar locked | Unlock at myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in |
| Server down | Retry after 2-3 hours |
| Name mismatch | Match Aadhaar and bank records exactly |
| All eKYC fails | Use offline XML, physical documents, or video KYC |

Don't let eKYC failure stop your banking. There are always alternative verification methods available — know your options.
