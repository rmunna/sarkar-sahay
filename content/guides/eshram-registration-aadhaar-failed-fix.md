# e-Shram Registration Aadhaar Failed? Fix Linking Errors

Getting **"Aadhaar Authentication Failed"** during e-Shram registration is one of the most common problems faced by unorganized workers. This guide covers all the reasons and fixes for Aadhaar-related errors on the e-Shram portal.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent information platform and is not affiliated with any government body. Verify details on [eshram.gov.in](https://eshram.gov.in).

## Common Aadhaar Errors During e-Shram Registration

| Error Message | Cause |
|--------------|-------|
| "Aadhaar Authentication Failed" | Mobile not linked or UIDAI server issue |
| "OTP Not Received" | Wrong mobile linked to Aadhaar |
| "Name Mismatch" | Name difference between Aadhaar and entered data |
| "Aadhaar Already Registered" | Duplicate registration exists |
| "Invalid Aadhaar Number" | Typo or deactivated Aadhaar |

## Fix 1: Aadhaar OTP Not Coming

This is the most common issue. The OTP is sent to the mobile number **linked to your Aadhaar** (not the number you enter on e-Shram).

**Steps to fix:**

1. **Check which number is linked to Aadhaar:**
   - Visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in)
   - Click "Verify Aadhaar" — it will show the last 4 digits of your linked mobile
2. **If the number is wrong or old:**
   - Visit your nearest **Aadhaar Enrollment Centre**
   - Carry your Aadhaar card + new mobile phone
   - Request mobile number update (free of charge)
   - Update takes **24-48 hours** to reflect
3. **If the number is correct but OTP isn't coming:**
   - Check if your phone has network signal
   - Check SMS inbox (not just notifications)
   - Wait 2-3 minutes — OTPs can be delayed during peak hours
   - Try the **"Resend OTP"** button after 60 seconds
   - Check if your phone's SMS inbox is full

## Fix 2: Mobile Number Not Linked to Aadhaar

e-Shram requires **Aadhaar eKYC** which needs a mobile-linked Aadhaar. Without a linked mobile, registration is impossible.

**How to link mobile to Aadhaar:**

1. Find nearest Aadhaar centre: [appointments.uidai.gov.in](https://appointments.uidai.gov.in)
2. Visit with:
   - Original Aadhaar card or Aadhaar enrollment slip
   - Mobile phone with the SIM you want to link
3. Fill the Aadhaar Update form — select "Mobile Number Update"
4. Provide biometric verification (fingerprint/iris)
5. You'll get an **Update Request Number (URN)**
6. Check status after 48 hours at [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in)

**Cost:** Free (no charges for mobile update)

> **Tip:** See our [Aadhaar card guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) for detailed Aadhaar update steps.

## Fix 3: Name Mismatch Error

e-Shram fetches your name from the Aadhaar database. If there's a mismatch:

**Scenario 1: Aadhaar has the wrong name**
1. Update your name on Aadhaar first
2. Visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in) → "Update Aadhaar" → Name
3. Upload supporting document (PAN card, passport, voter ID)
4. Fee: ₹50
5. After Aadhaar update (7-15 days), retry e-Shram registration

**Scenario 2: You're entering the name differently**
1. During e-Shram registration, your name auto-fills from Aadhaar
2. **Do not try to edit** the auto-filled name
3. If the name shows incorrectly, the issue is in Aadhaar — fix Aadhaar first

## Fix 4: "Aadhaar Already Registered" Error

This means someone already used your Aadhaar to create an e-Shram account. This commonly happens when:

- A CSC operator registered you without your knowledge (during a camp or scheme enrollment)
- You registered earlier and forgot about it
- Someone misused your Aadhaar

**What to do:**

1. **Access your existing account:**
   - Go to [register.eshram.gov.in](https://register.eshram.gov.in)
   - Click **"Already Registered"**
   - Enter Aadhaar → verify OTP → access your dashboard
   - Download your existing UAN card

2. **If you didn't register and suspect misuse:**
   - Call helpline **14434** and report unauthorized registration
   - Visit your nearest **District Labour Office** with Aadhaar for investigation
   - File a complaint on the e-Shram portal under "Grievance"

3. **If details in existing registration are wrong:**
   - Log in to your existing account
   - Update details from the dashboard
   - See our [e-Shram update guide](/guide/eshram-card-update-mobile-name-fix)

## Fix 5: UIDAI Server Down

Sometimes the Aadhaar authentication server itself is down, causing all e-Shram registrations to fail.

**Signs of UIDAI server issue:**
- The error happens to everyone, not just you
- "Technical error" or "Server not responding" messages
- OTP never arrives despite correct mobile

**What to do:**
1. Wait **1-2 hours** and retry
2. Try during **off-peak hours** (early morning or late night)
3. Check [UIDAI Twitter](https://twitter.com/UIDAI) for maintenance announcements

## e-Shram Registration Requirements

Before attempting registration, ensure you have:

- ✅ Valid **Aadhaar card** with linked mobile number
- ✅ **Active bank account** (with IFSC code)
- ✅ **Mobile phone** with the Aadhaar-linked SIM
- ✅ Your **occupation details** (type of unorganized work)
- ✅ Monthly **income details** (approximate)

## Step-by-Step e-Shram Registration

1. Visit [register.eshram.gov.in](https://register.eshram.gov.in)
2. Click **"Self Registration"**
3. Enter your **Aadhaar number** and **captcha**
4. Click **"Send OTP"** → enter the OTP from your Aadhaar-linked mobile
5. Your details auto-fill from Aadhaar (name, DOB, address, photo)
6. Fill remaining fields: occupation, bank details, nominee details
7. Review all information
8. Click **"Register"**
9. Your **UAN (Universal Account Number)** is generated
10. Download your e-Shram card

## Important Tips

- **Link mobile to Aadhaar first** — this is the #1 reason registrations fail
- **Use your own Aadhaar-linked phone** during registration for OTP
- **Don't pay agents** — e-Shram registration is completely free
- **One Aadhaar = One registration** — duplicate registrations are not allowed
- **Save your UAN** immediately after registration
