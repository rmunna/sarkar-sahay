## What is Godhan Nyay Yojana?

Godhan Nyay Yojana is an innovative scheme launched by the Government of Chhattisgarh on **20 July 2020** (Hareli festival) to purchase cow dung from cattle rearers and farmers at a fixed price of **₹2 per kilogram**. The purchased cow dung is used to produce **vermicompost** (organic fertilizer) at Gauthans (cattle shelters), promoting organic farming and providing additional income to livestock owners.

This is a unique scheme in India where the government directly purchases cow dung, turning what was considered waste into a source of income for rural households. The vermicompost produced is sold to farmers at subsidized rates, supporting the organic farming movement in Chhattisgarh.

## Key Benefits

| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Purchase rate | ₹2 per kg of cow dung |
| Vermicompost production | At Gauthans across the state |
| Additional income | For cattle rearers and farmers |
| Organic fertilizer | Available at subsidized rates |
| Employment | At Gauthans for women SHGs |
| Stray cattle management | Gauthans also shelter stray cattle |

### How It Works
1. Farmers/cattle rearers bring cow dung to **Gauthans** (government cattle shelters)
2. Cow dung is weighed and purchased at **₹2/kg**
3. Payment is made through **DBT** to the seller's bank account
4. **Women SHG groups** at Gauthans convert cow dung into **vermicompost**
5. Vermicompost is sold to farmers for organic farming

## Who is Eligible?

### For Selling Cow Dung
- **Any cattle rearer or farmer** in Chhattisgarh who owns cattle
- **Landless cattle rearers** who keep cows/buffaloes
- **Dairy farmers** in the state
- Must be a **resident of Chhattisgarh**
- No income limit — any cattle owner can sell cow dung

### For Gauthan Employment (SHG Members)
- **Women SHG groups** registered in Chhattisgarh
- SHGs selected by the local administration to manage Gauthans
- Members trained in vermicompost production

### For Buying Vermicompost
- Any farmer in Chhattisgarh can purchase vermicompost produced at Gauthans
- Available at **subsidized rates** from the nearest Gauthan

## Documents Required

### For Registration as Cow Dung Seller
- **Aadhaar Card**
- **Bank passbook** (Aadhaar-linked)
- **Residence proof** — Voter ID or ration card
- **Mobile number**
- **Details of cattle owned** (number and type)
- **Passport-size photograph**

## How to Register and Sell Cow Dung

### Registration Process

1. Visit the **nearest Gauthan** in your Gram Panchayat
2. Meet the **Gauthan committee** or Gram Panchayat representative
3. Fill the **registration form** with personal and cattle details
4. Submit **Aadhaar Card** and **bank passbook** copies
5. Get registered as a **cow dung supplier**

### Online Registration

1. Visit the **Godhan Nyay Portal** at [godhannyay.cgstate.gov.in](https://godhannyay.cgstate.gov.in)
2. Click on **registration/new application**
3. Enter Aadhaar and personal details
4. Enter cattle ownership details
5. Submit bank account information
6. Complete registration

### Selling Process

1. Collect **cow dung** from your cattle shed
2. Bring it to the **nearest Gauthan** on the designated collection day
3. Cow dung is **weighed** at the Gauthan
4. **Receipt is issued** with quantity and amount
5. Payment of **₹2/kg** is credited to your **bank account via DBT**
6. Payment is typically processed within **15-30 days**

### How to Check Payment Status

1. Visit [godhannyay.cgstate.gov.in](https://godhannyay.cgstate.gov.in)
2. Login with your registered credentials
3. Check **payment history** and pending amounts
4. Or contact the Gauthan committee for payment status

## Gauthan Network

- Over **10,000 Gauthans** have been established across Chhattisgarh
- Each Gram Panchayat has at least one Gauthan
- Gauthans serve as **cattle shelters**, **cow dung collection centres**, and **vermicompost production units**
- Many Gauthans also produce **biogas** and **organic products**

## Fees

- **Registration** — Free of cost
- **No hidden charges** — Cow dung purchase is at a fixed government rate
- **Vermicompost** — Available at subsidized rates to farmers

## Processing Time

- Registration: **Same day** at Gauthan or **3-5 days** online
- Cow dung weighing: **Immediate** at collection point
- Payment: **15-30 days** via DBT after cow dung delivery

## Important Tips

1. **Register at your nearest Gauthan** — You must be registered before you can sell cow dung regularly

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. What is Godhan Nyay Yojana?
Godhan Nyay Yojana is a Chhattisgarh government scheme where the state purchases cow dung from cattle rearers at **₹2 per kg** and converts it into vermicompost at Gauthans.

### Q2. How much does the government pay for cow dung?
The government pays **₹2 per kilogram** of cow dung, credited directly to the seller's bank account via DBT.

### Q3. Where do I sell cow dung?
You can sell cow dung at the **nearest Gauthan** (government cattle shelter) in your Gram Panchayat. Over 10,000 Gauthans operate across Chhattisgarh.

### Q4. Is there a limit on how much cow dung I can sell?
There are practical limits based on your cattle ownership and the Gauthan's capacity. Check with your local Gauthan for daily/weekly collection limits.

### Q5. Can buffalo dung also be sold?
The scheme primarily focuses on cow dung. Check with your local Gauthan whether buffalo dung is accepted at your location.

### Q6. Is Godhan Nyay Yojana still active?
The scheme was launched by the previous Congress government. With the BJP government taking over in 2024, the scheme's continuation status should be verified on the official portal.

### Q7. How is the vermicompost used?
Vermicompost produced at Gauthans is sold to farmers at subsidized rates for organic farming, reducing dependence on chemical fertilizers.
