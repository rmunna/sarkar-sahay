## What is YSR Aarogyasri?

**YSR Aarogyasri** is Andhra Pradesh government's comprehensive health protection scheme that provides **free cashless treatment** for **2,500+ medical and surgical procedures** at government hospitals and over 400 empanelled private hospitals. There is **no monetary cap per family** — treatments are covered without a limit.

> This is different from PM-JAY (₹5 lakh cap) — Aarogyasri in AP covers all eligible procedures without an annual cap.

## Who is Eligible?

- **White ration card** (BPL) holders in Andhra Pradesh
- **Pink ration card** (priority) holders in AP
- **Antyodaya families**
- Any economically weaker section family with valid AP ration card

## How to Use YSR Aarogyasri

### Step 1 — Get Aarogyasri Health Card

Your **Aarogyasri Smart Health Card** is linked to your ration card. Verify yours:
1. Visit **[aarogyasri.ap.gov.in](https://aarogyasri.ap.gov.in)**
2. Or check at the nearest Village Secretariat

### Step 2 — Go to Empanelled Hospital

Visit any **Aarogyasri empanelled hospital** (govt or private). Find hospital list at the Aarogyasri portal.

### Step 3 — Show Card at Help Desk

Show your Aarogyasri card + ration card at the hospital's Aarogyasri help desk. They verify eligibility in real time.

### Step 4 — Free Treatment

All listed procedures are done **cashless**. The hospital claims from the government.

### Step 5 — Travel Reimbursement

After discharge, claim ₹750 travel reimbursement at the help desk.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. My ration card is from Telangana. Can I use AP Aarogyasri?**

No. AP Aarogyasri is for Andhra Pradesh ration card holders. Telangana residents should use the Aarogyasri scheme under Telangana government.

**2. My father needs dialysis twice a week. Is this covered?**

Yes. Kidney dialysis (per session) is covered under Aarogyasri. Ongoing treatment can be continued at an empanelled facility.

**3. Is there an annual limit like PM-JAY's ₹5 lakh?**

No annual monetary cap under YSR Aarogyasri — procedures are covered based on the approved procedure list, not a rupee limit.

**4. I need a hip replacement. Is it in the 2,500 procedures?**

Yes. Hip and knee replacement surgeries are in the Aarogyasri approved procedure list.
