# Aadhaar PVC Card Order Not Delivered — How to Fix

Ordered your Aadhaar PVC card through the myAadhaar portal but haven't received it yet? You're not alone — delayed or missing PVC card deliveries are a common issue. This guide covers everything from tracking your order to filing a complaint and getting a refund.

## How Aadhaar PVC Card Delivery Works

When you order an Aadhaar PVC card from [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in), here's what happens:

1. **Payment of ₹50** (including GST and speed post charges) is collected online
2. UIDAI processes and prints the PVC card
3. The card is dispatched via **India Post Speed Post**
4. Typical delivery time: **15–30 days** from the order date

> ⚠️ Delivery can take longer in remote or rural areas — up to 45 days in some cases.

## Step 1: Check Your Order Status on myAadhaar

Before assuming your card is lost, verify the current status:

1. Go to [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in)
2. Log in with your **Aadhaar number** and OTP
3. Navigate to **Order Aadhaar PVC Card** → **Check Order Status**
4. You'll see the current status: Processing, Dispatched, or Delivered
5. If dispatched, note the **Speed Post tracking number**

## Step 2: Track via India Post

Once you have the Speed Post consignment number:

1. Visit [India Post Tracking](https://www.indiapost.gov.in/_layouts/15/DOP.Portal.Tracking/TrackConsignment.aspx)
2. Enter the **consignment/tracking number**
3. Click **Track** to see the delivery journey

### Common Tracking Statuses

| Status | What It Means |
|--------|---------------|
| Booked | Card handed to India Post |
| In Transit | Moving through postal network |
| Out for Delivery | Will arrive today |
| Delivered | Successfully delivered |
| Returned to Sender | Delivery failed — card sent back to UIDAI |

## Step 3: What If the Address Is Wrong?

If you entered the wrong address while ordering the PVC card:

- **Before dispatch:** You **cannot** change the delivery address after placing the order. There's no edit option on the myAadhaar portal.
- **After failed delivery:** The card will be returned to UIDAI. You'll need to place a fresh order with the correct address.

### Important: Address on PVC Card vs Delivery Address

The PVC card is sent to the **address currently on your Aadhaar record** — not a custom delivery address. If your Aadhaar address itself is outdated:

1. First, **update your address** on Aadhaar via [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in) or at an Aadhaar Enrollment Centre
2. Wait for the update to be processed (3–7 days)
3. Then place a new PVC card order

## Step 4: How to Reorder Your Aadhaar PVC Card

If your card was lost in transit, returned to sender, or never arrived:

1. Go to [Order Aadhaar PVC Card](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/orderpvc)
2. Enter your **12-digit Aadhaar number**
3. Verify with OTP
4. Pay **₹50** via credit/debit card, UPI, or net banking
5. A new PVC card will be printed and dispatched

> There is **no limit** on how many times you can reorder. Each order costs ₹50.

## Step 5: Refund for Non-Delivery

UIDAI's current refund policy for PVC card orders:

- **Refunds are generally not provided** for non-delivery caused by incorrect address or recipient unavailability
- If the order was placed but **never dispatched** (stuck in processing for 30+ days), you can raise a grievance for a refund
- If payment was **deducted but order not confirmed**, the amount is usually auto-refunded within 7–10 business days

### How to Request a Refund

1. Visit the [UIDAI Grievance Portal](https://uidai.gov.in/en/contact-support/grievance-redressal.html)
2. Select grievance type related to **PVC Card Order**
3. Provide your Aadhaar number, order date, and transaction details
4. Attach a screenshot of the failed transaction or order status
5. Submit and note the **grievance number** for tracking

## Step 6: File a Complaint with UIDAI

If your PVC card is significantly delayed or lost:

### Option A: Online Grievance

- Visit [UIDAI Grievance Redressal](https://uidai.gov.in/en/contact-support/grievance-redressal.html)
- Fill in the complaint form with your Aadhaar number and order details

### Option B: UIDAI Helpline

- **Toll-free number:** 1947
- **Available:** Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM
- Have your Aadhaar number and order details ready

### Option C: Email

- Send your complaint to **help@uidai.gov.in**
- Include: Aadhaar number (masked), order date, tracking number, and issue description

## PVC Card vs e-Aadhaar: Which Do You Actually Need?

While waiting for your PVC card, consider whether you really need it:

| Feature | Aadhaar PVC Card | e-Aadhaar (PDF) |
|---------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **Cost** | ₹50 per order | Free |
| **Format** | Physical plastic card | Digital PDF |
| **Legally Valid** | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes (equally valid) |
| **Delivery Time** | 15–30 days | Instant download |
| **Has QR Code** | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes |
| **Wallet-Friendly** | ✅ Credit card size | Needs print or phone |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does Aadhaar PVC card delivery take?
Typically **15–30 days** from the date of order. In remote areas it may take up to 45 days.

### Can I track my Aadhaar PVC card delivery?
Yes. Log in to myAadhaar, check order status to get the Speed Post tracking number, and track it on the India Post website.

### What if my Aadhaar PVC card is returned to sender?
You'll need to **place a new order** (₹50). Make sure your Aadhaar address is correct before reordering.

### Is there a refund if my PVC card is not delivered?
Refunds are not automatic. You must raise a grievance through the UIDAI portal. Refunds are considered on a case-by-case basis.

### Can I order Aadhaar PVC card to a different address?
No. The card is always sent to the address registered on your Aadhaar. Update your address first if needed.

### Is e-Aadhaar valid as ID proof?
Yes. e-Aadhaar is legally valid and accepted everywhere the physical Aadhaar card is accepted.

## Summary: Quick Fix Checklist

- [ ] Check order status on [myAadhaar portal](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in)
- [ ] Track Speed Post consignment on [India Post](https://www.indiapost.gov.in/_layouts/15/DOP.Portal.Tracking/TrackConsignment.aspx)
- [ ] Wait at least 30 days before raising a complaint
- [ ] File grievance at [UIDAI Grievance Portal](https://uidai.gov.in/en/contact-support/grievance-redressal.html) or call **1947**
- [ ] Download [e-Aadhaar](https://eaadhaar.uidai.gov.in) as an immediate alternative
- [ ] Reorder from [myAadhaar](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/orderpvc) if needed (₹50)
