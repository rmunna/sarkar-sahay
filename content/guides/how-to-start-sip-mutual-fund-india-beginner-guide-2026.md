# How to Start SIP in Mutual Funds India 2026 — Beginner's Complete Guide

SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) is the simplest way to build wealth in India — invest a fixed amount monthly, let compounding work over years. In 2026, starting a SIP takes less than 10 minutes on your phone. Here's everything you need to know.

---

## What is SIP? The Core Concept

SIP = Invest a fixed amount every month into a mutual fund. The fund manager (or index) grows the money over time.

**Why SIP beats lump sum for beginners**:
- **Rupee cost averaging**: You buy more units when NAV is low, fewer when high — averages out market volatility
- **Discipline**: Auto-debit forces regular investing without you needing willpower
- **Compounding**: Returns on returns. ₹5,000/month for 20 years at 12% = ₹50 lakh (invested only ₹12 lakh)

**SIP Power of Compounding** (at 12% CAGR):

| Monthly SIP | 10 Years | 20 Years |
|------------|---------|---------|
| ₹2,000 | ₹4.6L | ₹19.8L |
| ₹5,000 | ₹11.6L | ₹49.5L |
| ₹10,000 | ₹23.2L | ₹99L |
| ₹20,000 | ₹46.4L | ₹1.98Cr |

**Direct vs Regular Plans**:
- **Direct Plan**: You invest directly (via app/platform), 0% distributor commission, higher NAV growth by 0.5–1.5% annually
- **Regular Plan**: Through a broker/advisor who earns commission from your returns
- **Always choose Direct Plan** — over 20 years, the difference is significant

## Step 2: Complete KYC (One-Time, Takes 5 Minutes)

KYC (Know Your Customer) is mandatory before investing:

**Documents needed**:
- PAN card
- Aadhaar card (linked to your mobile for OTP verification)
- Bank account details

**KYC Process on Groww**:
1. Open Groww app → Sign up with mobile number
2. Enter PAN → PAN verified automatically via Income Tax database
3. Enter Aadhaar → Enter OTP sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile
4. Take a selfie (liveness check)
5. KYC verified instantly (or within 30 minutes)

**CVL KRA**: Your KYC is registered with CVL KRA (CDSL Ventures Limited KYC Registration Agency) — it's valid across all mutual fund platforms. You don't redo KYC for each app.

## Step 5: Decide Your SIP Amount

**The "invest 20% of take-home" rule**: If take-home is ₹60,000, target ₹12,000/month SIP.

**But start small if uncertain**: Even ₹1,000/month is fine to start. Increase every year.

**Where to split** (sample for ₹10,000/month SIP):
- ₹5,000 → Nifty 50 Index Fund (core, stable)
- ₹3,000 → Nifty Next 50 Index Fund (next tier, more growth)
- ₹2,000 → Mid Cap Fund (higher risk, higher return potential)

## Step 6: Set Up the SIP

On Groww (similar on other platforms):

1. Select the fund → Click "Start SIP"
2. Choose amount (e.g., ₹5,000)
3. Choose date (1st, 5th, 10th — any)
4. Choose frequency (monthly — most common)
5. Link bank account (HDFC, SBI, ICICI, etc.)
6. Set up mandate: Banks auto-debit your account on the SIP date
7. Confirm → SIP activated

**Bank mandate**: Most platforms use e-mandate (OTP-based instant activation). If your bank doesn't support e-mandate, you may need to submit a physical mandate form.

## Common SIP Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Why It Hurts | Better Approach |
|---------|-------------|----------------|
| Stopping SIP when market falls | Misses the best buy prices during dip | Keep SIP running; buy more if possible |
| Choosing based on past 1-year returns | Past short-term returns are meaningless | Look at 5–10 year consistency |
| Too many funds | Overlapping portfolios, hard to track | 3–5 funds maximum |
| Regular plan instead of direct | Paying 1.5% extra annually (compounded) | Always choose Direct plans |
| Setting SIP and forgetting completely | Doesn't review and rebalance | Annual review — check if fund still consistent |

## Taxes on SIP Withdrawals

| Type | Tax |
|------|-----|
| LTCG on equity SIP (held >12 months per SIP installment) | 12.5% on gains above ₹1.25L |
| STCG on equity SIP (sold within 12 months of purchase) | 20% |
| Debt fund gains (regardless of holding) | As per slab rate |

**Tax note on SIP**: Each SIP installment has its own 12-month holding period. When you sell, FIFO (first-in-first-out) basis applies — earliest units sold first.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Minimum amount to start SIP?

Most funds: ₹100–500 per month minimum. Groww and Coin allow ₹100. There's no maximum.

### Can I pause SIP if I need money?

Yes — most platforms allow you to pause SIP for 1–3 months without penalty. The existing invested amount stays in the fund growing; you just don't add more during the pause.

### What happens if money isn't in account on SIP date?

SIP debit fails. After 2–3 consecutive failures, some banks charge a small fee (₹25–100). Too many failures may pause the SIP. Ensure money is in the account on the SIP date.

### Is SIP risky?

Equity SIPs have market risk — NAV can fall (especially in 1–3 years). Over 5–10 years, equity SIPs historically have not given negative returns in India. Risk reduces with longer holding period.

### Which is better — SIP or lump sum?

For salary earners (regular income): SIP is ideal — invests systematically, no need to time market. For large surplus (bonus, inheritance): Lump sum in liquid fund, then STP (Systematic Transfer Plan) monthly into equity = best of both.
