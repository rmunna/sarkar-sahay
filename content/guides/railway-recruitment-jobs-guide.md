# Railway Recruitment — Types of Jobs and How to Apply

Indian Railways is one of the largest employers in the world, with over 12 lakh employees. Every year, Railways recruits thousands of candidates through various exams conducted by Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) and Railway Recruitment Cells (RRCs).

This guide covers all major railway recruitment exams, job types, eligibility, and the application process.

## Railway Recruitment Bodies

| Body | Full Name | Recruits For |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| RRB | Railway Recruitment Board | Group C posts (NTPC, ALP, JE) |
| RRC | Railway Recruitment Cell | Group D posts (Track Maintainer, Helper) |
| RPF | Railway Protection Force | Constable and SI |
| UPSC | Union Public Service Commission | Indian Railway Service officers (Group A) |

There are 21 RRBs across India (Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, etc.). You apply through the RRB of your zone.

## Types of Railway Jobs

### Non-Technical Popular Categories (NTPC)

These are office-based and station-based posts:
- Station Master
- Commercial Apprentice
- Traffic Assistant
- Goods Guard
- Senior Clerk
- Junior Account Assistant

**Qualification:** Graduation (for some posts, 12th pass is sufficient)

### Assistant Loco Pilot (ALP) and Technicians

- ALP: Drives trains under the guidance of a Loco Pilot
- Technicians: Maintain railway equipment and infrastructure

**Qualification:** 10th + ITI or Diploma in Engineering

### Group D (Level 1 Posts)

- Track Maintainer Grade IV
- Helper/Assistant in various departments
- Porter, Gateman

**Qualification:** 10th pass + ITI (for some posts, 10th alone is sufficient)

### Railway Protection Force (RPF)

- Constable
- Sub-Inspector

**Qualification:** 10th pass (Constable), Graduation (SI)

### Group A Services (Through UPSC)

- Indian Railway Traffic Service (IRTS)
- Indian Railway Service of Engineers (IRSE)
- Indian Railway Accounts Service (IRAS)

These are recruited through [UPSC Civil Services](/guide/upsc-civil-services-preparation-guide) or UPSC Engineering Services exam.

## Eligibility Comparison

| Exam | Education | Age Limit | Medical Standard |
|------|-----------|-----------|-----------------|
| RRB NTPC | 12th/Graduation (post-wise) | 18-33 years | Varies by post |
| RRB ALP | 10th + ITI/Diploma | 18-28 years | Aye-One (distance vision) |
| RRC Group D | 10th + ITI | 18-33 years | Aye-Three |
| RPF Constable | 10th pass | 18-25 years | Physical standards apply |
| RPF SI | Graduation | 20-25 years | Physical standards apply |

**Age relaxation:** SC/ST — 5 years, OBC — 3 years, Ex-Servicemen — varies

## Exam Pattern

### RRB NTPC

**CBT 1 (Computer-Based Test — Stage 1):**
- 100 questions, 100 marks, 90 minutes
- Sections: Mathematics (30), General Intelligence & Reasoning (30), General Awareness (40)
- Negative marking: 1/3rd per wrong answer

**CBT 2 (Stage 2):**
- 120 questions, 120 marks, 90 minutes
- Same sections with higher difficulty

**Typing/CBAT:** For specific posts (Station Master needs CBAT, Clerk needs typing test)

### RRB ALP

**CBT 1:**
- 75 questions, 75 marks, 60 minutes
- Mathematics, General Intelligence, General Science, General Awareness

**CBT 2:**
- Part A: Same as CBT 1 (100 questions)
- Part B: Trade-specific questions (75 questions)

### RRC Group D

**CBT:**
- 100 questions, 100 marks, 90 minutes
- Mathematics (25), General Intelligence (25), General Science (25), General Awareness (25)

**PET (Physical Efficiency Test):**
- Male: Lift 35 kg and carry for 100m in 2 minutes; run 1000m in 4 min 15 sec
- Female: Lift 20 kg and carry for 100m in 2 minutes; run 1000m in 5 min 40 sec

## How to Apply for Railway Jobs

### Step 1: Find the Notification

1. Visit [rrbcdg.gov.in](https://rrbcdg.gov.in) or your zonal RRB website
2. Check "Latest Notifications" section
3. Download the official CEN (Centralised Employment Notice)
4. Read eligibility and vacancy details carefully

### Step 2: Register Online

1. Visit the application portal (link given in the notification)
2. Create an account with email and mobile number
3. Complete registration with personal details
4. Note down your Registration Number

### Step 3: Fill Application Form

1. Log in and fill personal, educational, and other details
2. Select your preferred RRB zone
3. Choose post preferences (in order of priority)
4. Select exam language preference

### Step 4: Upload Documents

1. Upload recent passport-size photograph (20-50 KB, JPEG)
2. Upload signature (10-20 KB, JPEG)
3. Verify the preview

### Step 5: Pay Fee and Submit

- General/OBC: ₹500 (₹400 refunded after CBT stage 1 if appeared)
- SC/ST/Ex-Servicemen/PwBD/Female/Transgender/Minorities/EBC: ₹250 (fully refunded)
- Payment: Online via debit card, credit card, net banking, UPI

For more details on online applications, see our [government job application guide](/guide/how-to-apply-government-jobs-online).

## Preparation Strategy

### Mathematics

1. Focus on basic arithmetic: Percentage, Ratio, Time & Speed, Time & Work
2. Number System, HCF/LCM, Simplification
3. Geometry basics, Mensuration
4. Practice speed calculation — railway maths is more about speed than difficulty

### General Intelligence & Reasoning

1. Analogies, Classification, Series
2. Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations
3. Syllogism, Statement & Conclusion
4. Calendar, Clock, Mirror Image

### General Awareness

1. Current affairs (last 6-8 months)
2. Indian history (freedom struggle, ancient kingdoms)
3. Geography of India
4. Indian polity and constitution basics
5. General science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology basics)
6. Railways-specific GK (history of Indian Railways, zones, headquarters)

### General Science

1. Physics: Mechanics, Heat, Light, Sound, Electricity
2. Chemistry: Elements, Compounds, Acids & Bases, Daily life chemistry
3. Biology: Human body, Diseases, Nutrition, Plants

### Recommended Books

- **Mathematics:** R.S. Aggarwal or Kiran's Railway Mathematics
- **Reasoning:** R.S. Aggarwal (Verbal & Non-Verbal Reasoning)
- **General Science:** Lucent's General Science
- **GK/GA:** Lucent's General Knowledge + monthly current affairs
- **Previous Year Papers:** Kiran's or Youth Competition Times compilations

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How often does Railway recruitment happen?

Major railway recruitments (NTPC, Group D, ALP) happen every 2-3 years. RPF recruitment is less frequent. Check the official RRB websites regularly for notifications.

### Can I apply for both NTPC and Group D simultaneously?

Yes, if both notifications are open at the same time and you meet the eligibility for both, you can apply separately for each.

### Is ITI mandatory for Group D posts?

For most Group D posts, 10th pass + ITI is required. However, some posts accept 10th pass with NAC (National Apprenticeship Certificate) or equivalent.

### What is the medical standard for railway jobs?

Railway jobs have strict medical standards, especially for safety-related posts. ALP requires Aye-One vision (distance vision 6/6 without glasses). Group D requires Aye-Three. RPF has additional physical standards.

### Can I choose my posting location after selection?

During the application, you select your preferred RRB zone. Posting is within that zone, but the exact location is decided by the railway administration based on vacancies.

### How do I check my railway exam result?

Visit your zonal RRB website and check the results section. You can also visit our [exam results guide](/guide/exam-results-check-online-guide) for step-by-step instructions.
