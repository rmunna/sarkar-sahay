# Passport Police Verification Delayed? How to Follow Up (2026)

Applied for a passport and police verification has been pending for weeks or even months? This is one of the most common delays in the Indian passport process. Here's how to track, follow up, and escalate.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent platform and is not affiliated with the Ministry of External Affairs or Police.

## How Passport Police Verification Works

After your visit to the Passport Seva Kendra (PSK):

1. **RPO sends request** to the SP/Commissioner of Police in your district
2. **SP office assigns** the case to a local police station (your area)
3. **Police officer visits** your address for physical verification
4. **Officer submits report** to SP office
5. **SP office forwards** the report to the Passport Office (RPO)
6. **RPO processes** and issues/rejects the passport

**Normal timeline:** 2-4 weeks for police verification

## Check Your Police Verification Status

### On Passport India Portal:
1. Visit [passportindia.gov.in](https://www.passportindia.gov.in)
2. Click **"Track Application Status"**
3. Select **"Passport/PCC/IC"**
4. Enter your **File Number** and **Date of Birth**
5. Check the status — it will show:
   - "Police verification pending"
   - "Police verification initiated"
   - "Verification report received — Clear/Adverse"
   - "Passport printed / Dispatched"

### Via SMS:
Send SMS: `STATUS <File Number>` to **9704100100**

## Why Police Verification Gets Delayed

| Reason | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Address mismatch | Your current address differs from documents |
| Officer not visiting | Assigned officer is busy or on leave |
| Multiple addresses | If you've lived at different addresses, all need verification |
| Criminal record check | Additional background checks if any record exists |
| Rented accommodation | Landlord verification adds time |
| SP office backlog | Too many applications, not enough staff |
| Report not uploaded | Officer verified but hasn't uploaded report |
| Festival/holiday season | Government offices have reduced working |

## Step 1: Visit Your Local Police Station

If verification is pending for more than 3 weeks:

1. **Find out which police station** your area falls under
2. **Visit the police station** during working hours (10 AM - 5 PM)
3. **Carry these documents:**
   - Passport application acknowledgment (printed from portal)
   - Address proof (Aadhaar, utility bill, rent agreement)
   - Original ID proof
4. **Ask to meet the officer** assigned for passport verification
5. **Politely inquire** about the status and provide your contact number
6. **Be available** — sometimes the officer visits shortly after you remind them

## Step 2: Contact the SP/Commissioner's Office

If the police station doesn't help:

1. **Find your SP office contact:**
   - Search "[Your District] SP office passport verification contact"
   - Visit [passportindia.gov.in](https://www.passportindia.gov.in) → **Contact Us** for regional details
2. **Call the SP office** and provide your passport file number
3. **Visit in person** if phone doesn't work — carry all documents
4. **Write a formal letter** to the SP requesting expedition of verification
5. Most districts have a **designated passport verification cell** — ask for it

## Step 3: Contact the Regional Passport Office (RPO)

If SP office doesn't resolve within a week:

1. **Call RPO helpline:** Check your RPO number at [passportindia.gov.in](https://www.passportindia.gov.in) → Contact Us
2. **National helpline:** Call **1800-258-1800** (toll-free)
3. **Visit RPO** in person during public grievance hours
4. **Email the RPO** — find email at the Contact Us page
5. **Explain:** Mention the file number, date of application, and that police verification is delayed

## Step 4: Use the Passport Seva App

1. Download the **mPassport Seva** app
2. Login with your credentials
3. Track application status in real-time
4. Use the **grievance** feature to report delays
5. You can also **reschedule appointments** if needed

## Step 5: File a Formal Grievance

### On Passport India Portal:
1. Visit [passportindia.gov.in](https://www.passportindia.gov.in)
2. Click **"Grievance Tracking"**
3. Register a new grievance
4. Select: "Delay in Police Verification"
5. Enter file number and detailed description
6. Submit and note grievance number

### On CPGRAMS:
1. Visit [pgportal.gov.in](https://pgportal.gov.in)
2. Register → Lodge Grievance
3. Select Ministry: **Ministry of External Affairs**
4. Describe the delay with file number
5. This escalates directly to MEA

### Via Social Media:
- Tweet to **@MEAIndia** and **@PassportSevaMEA** with your file number
- They often respond within 24-48 hours
- Be professional and include specific details

## Step 6: Write to the Chief Passport Officer

If all else fails:

1. Write a formal letter/email to the **Chief Passport Officer (CPO)**
2. Include:
   - Your full name and file number
   - Date of PSK appointment
   - How long verification has been pending
   - Steps you've already taken
   - Request for immediate action
3. Send to the MEA Passport Division email

## Tips to Speed Up Police Verification

1. **Keep your phone ON** — the officer may call before visiting
2. **Be present at the verification address** — if you miss the visit, it delays further
3. **Keep all original documents ready** at home
4. **Inform your landlord** (if rented) — they may need to verify
5. **Be polite with the police officer** — cooperation speeds things up
6. **Follow up every week** until verification is complete
7. **Submit clear address proof** — ambiguous addresses cause delays
8. **For Tatkal passport:** Verification happens post-issuance, so passport comes faster

## Tatkal vs Normal Passport Verification

| Aspect | Normal Passport | Tatkal Passport |
|--------|----------------|-----------------|
| Verification timing | Before passport issuance | After passport issuance |
| Passport delivery | After verification clear | Within 1-3 days of PSK visit |
| If verification adverse | Passport not issued | Passport may be impounded |
| Fee | ₹1,500 | ₹3,500 |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does passport police verification take?
Normal police verification takes 2-4 weeks. However, it can take 1-3 months in some cases due to officer unavailability, address issues, or backlog at the SP office.

### Can I get passport without police verification?
For normal passports, police verification is mandatory before issuance. For Tatkal passports, the passport is issued first and verification happens later (post-issuance verification).

### How to check if police verification is complete?
Track your application at passportindia.gov.in → Track Application Status. If status shows "Verification report received," police verification is complete.

### What if police officer doesn't visit for verification?
Visit your local police station with your passport application acknowledgment, contact the SP office, and file a grievance on the Passport India portal. Follow up weekly.

### Can I contact police station directly for passport verification?
Yes, you can visit your local police station and politely ask about your pending passport verification. Carry your application acknowledgment and address proof.

### What happens if police verification is adverse?
If the police report is adverse (negative), your passport may be denied or issued with a shorter validity. You can appeal the decision at the RPO with supporting documents.

### Does changing address delay police verification?
Yes, if your current address differs from the address on your application or if you've lived at multiple addresses recently, verification takes longer as each address may need checking.

### How to follow up on passport verification after 30 days?
Contact the SP office, call RPO helpline 1800-258-1800, file grievance on passportindia.gov.in, and tweet to @PassportSevaMEA. Persistent follow-up usually speeds up the process.
