# Passport Police Verification Delayed? Track & Speed Up

After submitting your passport application and completing the appointment at the Passport Seva Kendra (PSK), police verification is the next critical step. Unfortunately, it's also where most delays happen. If your passport police verification has been pending for weeks or months, this guide explains how to track the status, follow up effectively, and escalate when needed.

## How Passport Police Verification Works

After your application is processed at the PSK/POPSK:

1. **Your file is sent** to the Passport Office, which forwards the verification request to the **Superintendent of Police (SP)** of your district
2. The SP office assigns it to the **local police station** (thana) covering your residential address
3. A **police constable or sub-inspector** visits your address to verify your identity and residence
4. The police station submits the **verification report** back to the SP office
5. The SP office forwards it to the **Regional Passport Office (RPO)**
6. The RPO processes your passport based on the report

### Expected Timeline

| Type | Expected Duration |
|------|-------------------|
| Normal Passport | 2–4 weeks for police verification |
| Tatkal Passport | Post-issuance verification (passport issued first) |
| Reissue (same address) | 1–2 weeks |
| Reissue (new address) | 2–4 weeks |

---

## Step 1: Track Police Verification Status on Passport Seva

1. Visit the Passport Seva status tracker: [passportindia.gov.in](https://www.passportindia.gov.in/AppOnlineProject/statusTracker/trackStatusInp498498)
2. Select **Track Application Status**
3. Choose **Application Type** → Passport
4. Enter your **File Number** (format: XX-1234567890123) and **Date of Birth**
5. Click **Track Status**

### Understanding Status Messages

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Pending for Police Verification | Request sent to police, no report yet |
| Police Verification Initiated | SP office has received the request |
| Police Verification Report Received – Clear | Positive report received |
| Police Verification Report Received – Adverse | Negative report, may need clarification |
| Passport Printed | Verification done, passport being printed |

> **Tip:** If status shows "Pending for Police Verification" for more than 3 weeks, it's time to follow up.

## Step 2: Visit Your Local Police Station

The most effective first step is visiting the police station assigned to verify your application:

### How to Find Which Police Station

- Your Passport Seva status page sometimes shows the police station name
- Alternatively, visit your local thana (the one covering your residential area)

### What to Do at the Police Station

1. **Visit during working hours** (10 AM – 5 PM, weekdays)
2. **Carry these documents:**
   - Passport application acknowledgement receipt
   - Original Aadhaar card
   - Address proof (electricity bill, rent agreement)
   - Two passport-size photos
3. **Ask to meet the officer handling passport verifications** (usually a Head Constable or ASI)
4. **Politely inquire about your verification status** — share your file number
5. **If the officer hasn't received your file**, note down details and escalate to the SP office

> **Important:** Be polite but firm. Many delays happen because files pile up at the police station. A personal visit often gets things moving.

---

## Step 3: Escalate to the SP Office

If the police station visit doesn't help or if they claim they haven't received the file:

1. **Visit the SP/SSP office** in your district headquarters
2. **Meet the passport verification cell** — most SP offices have a dedicated cell for passport verifications
3. **Submit a written complaint** mentioning:
   - Your full name and file number
   - Date of PSK appointment
   - Police station assigned
   - Number of days pending
4. **Request them to expedite** and send a reminder to the police station

### Contact the SP Office by Phone

- Find your district SP office number on the state police website
- Call and ask for the passport verification cell
- Follow up every 3–4 days

---

## Step 4: Complain to the Regional Passport Office (RPO)

If police verification remains stuck even after SP office escalation:

### Passport Seva Grievance Portal

1. Visit [passportindia.gov.in](https://passportindia.gov.in/)
2. Log in to your account
3. Go to **Grievance / Feedback**
4. File a complaint mentioning the delay
5. You'll receive a grievance tracking number

### Call the Passport Seva Call Centre

- **Toll-free number:** 1800-258-1800
- **Available:** Monday to Saturday, 8 AM to 10 PM
- Share your file number and explain the delay

### Email the RPO

- Find your RPO's email on the Passport Seva website
- Send a formal email with your file number, application date, and delay details

---

## Step 5: File an RTI for Persistent Delays

If all other methods fail and your verification has been pending for **60+ days**, file an RTI (Right to Information) application:

### Online RTI Filing

1. Visit [rtionline.gov.in](https://rtionline.gov.in/)
2. Click **Submit Request**
3. **Select Ministry:** Ministry of External Affairs
4. **Select Public Authority:** Passport Office (your regional office)
5. **Write your query:**
   > "Please provide the current status of police verification for passport application File Number [XX-1234567890123] submitted on [date]. Please provide reasons for delay beyond the standard processing time, the name and designation of the officer responsible for verification, and expected date of completion."
6. Pay **₹10** online
7. Submit and save the registration number

> **RTI usually gets a response within 30 days** and often triggers immediate action on pending verifications.

### RTI to Police Department

You can also file a separate RTI to the **state Home Department** or **DGP office** asking about the delay from the police side.

---

## Important Tips

1. **Keep all documents ready** before the police visit — don't let the constable return empty-handed because you weren't home or didn't have documents
2. **Inform your neighbours and landlord** that police may visit for passport verification
3. **For rented accommodation**, keep your rent agreement and landlord's contact ready — police often verify with the landlord
4. **Track status weekly** on the Passport Seva portal after your PSK appointment
5. **Don't pay any bribe** — passport police verification is a free government service. Report any demands to the SP office or Anti-Corruption Bureau

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does passport police verification take normally?

For normal passports, police verification typically takes **2–4 weeks**. For reissue at the same address, it may take **1–2 weeks**. Tatkal passports are issued first, with verification done afterward.

### What if the police constable never visits my address?

Visit the police station in person and meet the verification officer. If that doesn't work, escalate to the SP office. You can also file a grievance on the Passport Seva portal.

### Can I get a passport without police verification?

For **Tatkal passports**, the passport is issued before police verification is completed. For normal passports, police verification must be completed before the passport is printed.

### What happens if police verification is "adverse"?

An adverse report means the police found something concerning. You'll receive a notice from the Passport Office. You can submit a clarification or additional documents. The passport may still be issued after review.

### Will filing an RTI delay my passport further?

No. Filing an RTI is your legal right and cannot be held against you. In practice, RTI applications often **speed up** the process as officers don't want to report delays.

### What if I've moved to a different city after applying?

Police verification happens at the address mentioned in your application. If you've moved, you may need to visit the original police station or contact the RPO to update the verification address.

### Is police verification done for minor passports too?

Yes, police verification is required for minor passports as well, though it's typically faster as it only verifies the parents' address and identity.
