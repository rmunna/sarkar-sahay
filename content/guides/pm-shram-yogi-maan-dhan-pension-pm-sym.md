## What is PM Shram Yogi Maan-dhan?

**Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maan-dhan (PM-SYM)** is a voluntary pension scheme for **unorganized sector workers** — such as street vendors, domestic workers, construction workers, auto-rickshaw drivers, handloom weavers, and others who work outside the formal employment system.

It provides a **guaranteed pension of ₹3,000 per month** after age 60, with the government matching every rupee the worker contributes.

Key features:
- **₹3,000/month pension** after age 60 — for life
- **Equal contribution** from worker and government
- Monthly contribution: **₹55 to ₹200** (depending on age at enrollment)
- Enroll at **CSC (Common Service Centre)** — no internet or smartphone needed
- Spouse also eligible for separate enrollment (additional ₹3,000/month)

## Who is Eligible?

- **Unorganized sector workers** — not covered by EPFO, ESIC, or NPS
- Age: **18 to 40 years** at enrollment
- Monthly income: **₹15,000 or below**
- Must have a **savings bank account** or Jan Dhan account

**Who counts as unorganized sector workers:**
- Street vendors / hawkers
- Domestic workers (maids, cooks, drivers)
- Construction and daily wage workers (mazdoors)
- Weavers, beedi rollers, handloom workers
- Rickshaw and auto-rickshaw drivers
- Waste pickers, sanitation workers
- Agricultural labourers not covered by any scheme

**NOT eligible:**
- EPFO/ESIC members (formal sector workers)
- Income taxpayers
- Those already in NPS or any other government pension scheme
- PM-KMY (farmer pension) enrollees — the two schemes are separate

## Monthly Contribution Table

| Age at Entry | Monthly Contribution (You + Govt equally) |
|---|---|
| 18 years | ₹55/month each |
| 25 years | ₹80/month each |
| 30 years | ₹110/month each |
| 35 years | ₹150/month each |
| 40 years | ₹200/month each |

**Total monthly deposit to pension fund = 2× your contribution** (you + government).

## How to Enroll — Step by Step

### Via CSC (Common Service Centre)

1. Locate your nearest **CSC / Jan Seva Kendra** (find at [locator.csccloud.in](https://locator.csccloud.in))
2. Carry your **Aadhaar card** and **savings bank passbook**
3. CSC operator will:
   - Verify your Aadhaar (OTP on mobile)
   - Register you on [maandhan.in](https://maandhan.in)
   - Set up **auto-debit** from your savings account for monthly contribution
4. You receive a **Shram Yogi Pension Card** with your unique account number

### Via Self-Enrollment at maandhan.in

1. Go to **[maandhan.in](https://maandhan.in)**
2. Click **"Self Enrollment"** → Unorganized Worker
3. Enter Aadhaar + OTP verification
4. Fill details and set up monthly contribution

> **Most workers enroll via CSC** as it is easier and the operator guides through the process.

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card** (mandatory)
2. **Savings bank passbook** — in your own name (for auto-debit and pension credit)
3. **Mobile number** linked to Aadhaar for OTP

No income proof is required — self-declaration is sufficient.

## Pension After 60

- **₹3,000/month for life** — credited to bank account on the 1st of every month
- If the worker dies **after 60**, the **spouse gets 50% family pension (₹1,500/month)** for the rest of their life
- If the worker dies **before 60**, the **spouse** can continue contributing and get the full pension at 60, OR exit and get the savings back with interest

## Exit Options Before Age 60

| Exit Scenario | What You Get |
|---|---|
| Exit before 10 years | Your contribution + savings bank interest |
| Exit after 10 years | Your contribution + accumulated interest (NPS fund returns) |
| Permanent disability | Option to exit and get corpus, or spouse continues |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I am a domestic worker. How do I prove my income is below ₹15,000?
Self-declaration is accepted — no income certificate is required. You simply state your occupation and approximate monthly income at the CSC.

### I am 42. Can I still enroll?
No — maximum entry age is **40 years**. Enroll before you turn 40 to be eligible.

### Can I miss a monthly contribution?
If you miss, the auto-debit will fail. Your subscription gets suspended. To reactivate, pay the missed months' contributions + 8% simple interest. Visit your CSC to reactivate.

### What if my bank account closes?
Your pension payments will fail. Update your bank account details at the CSC or at [maandhan.in](https://maandhan.in) immediately if you change bank accounts.

### I also work as a farmer. Can I have both PM-KMY and PM-SYM?
No — you can enroll in only **one** scheme. Choose PM-KMY if your primary occupation is farming (land up to 2 hectares), or PM-SYM if you are primarily an unorganized non-farm worker.

### Will the ₹3,000 pension increase in the future?
The scheme design provides ₹3,000/month. The government may revise amounts over time, but there is no automatic inflation indexing.
