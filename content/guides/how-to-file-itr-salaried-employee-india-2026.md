# How to File ITR for Salaried Employee India 2026 — Step-by-Step

ITR filing for most salaried employees takes 15–30 minutes using the pre-filled option on the income tax portal. Here's the complete walkthrough.

---

## Which ITR Form Should Salaried Employees Use?

| Form | Who Uses It | Condition |
|------|------------|-----------|
| **ITR-1 (Sahaj)** | Most salaried employees | Salary + 1 house property + other sources (interest, FD); total income ≤ ₹50L; NO capital gains |
| **ITR-2** | Salaried with capital gains | Mutual fund redemptions, stock selling in the year; or income > ₹50L; or 2+ properties |
| **ITR-3** | Salaried + freelance/business income | Has business income alongside salary |

**95% of salaried employees file ITR-1 (Sahaj)**. Use ITR-2 if you sold any stocks or mutual funds in the year.

---

## Before You Start — Documents to Keep Ready

| Document | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| **Form 16 Part A** | TDS deducted by employer (from TRACES) |
| **Form 16 Part B** | Salary computation and deductions |
| **Form 26AS** | All TDS credits across all deductors |
| **AIS (Annual Information Statement)** | Complete income picture: FD interest, dividends, rent |
| **PAN and Aadhaar** | For login and e-verification |
| **Bank account details** | For refund credit if any |

**Download AIS**: Login to incometax.gov.in → Services → AIS / 26AS

## Understanding Key Documents

### Form 16 — Employer-Issued Certificate

- **Part A**: TDS deducted quarter by quarter (directly from TRACES, pre-filled in ITR)
- **Part B**: Salary structure (basic, HRA, allowances), declared deductions (80C investments you submitted to employer), tax computed

**Issued by**: Your employer by May 31 of the following year. If not received by June, email HR.

### Annual Information Statement (AIS)

Comprehensive view of all financial transactions:
- Salary paid by employer
- Dividend received
- Savings account interest, FD interest
- Mutual fund SIP data
- Rent receipts (if landlord filed TDS)
- Property transactions
- Stock transactions

**Cross-check AIS vs Form 16**: If employer-reported salary in AIS doesn't match Form 16, resolve before filing.

### Form 26AS

Shows all TDS deducted:
- Employer TDS (salary)
- Bank TDS (FD interest if > ₹40K/year)
- Any other TDS

Your ITR must claim exactly what's in Form 26AS — any mismatch causes notices from ITD.

## Step-by-Step ITR Filing at incometax.gov.in

### Step 1: Log in to e-Filing Portal

1. Go to [incometax.gov.in](https://incometax.gov.in) → e-Filing Login
2. Enter PAN + password (if registered) OR Create account with PAN
3. Complete mobile/email OTP verification

### Step 2: Start Filing

1. Dashboard → **e-File** → **Income Tax Returns** → **File Income Tax Return**
2. Assessment Year: Select **AY 2026-27** (for income earned April 2025–March 2026)
3. Mode: Online (recommended)
4. Status: Individual
5. ITR Form: Select **ITR-1** (if applicable — see criteria above)
6. Submission: Taxable income or claiming refund (select appropriate)

### Step 3: Use Pre-Filled Data

The portal pre-fills data from:
- Form 16 submitted by your employer
- AIS/Form 26AS (FD interest, dividends, etc.)
- Previous year's ITR

**Carefully verify each pre-filled field** — errors here cause tax mismatches.

### Step 4: Fill/Verify Each Section

**Part A — General Information**
- Name, PAN, DOB (auto-filled)
- Filing Status: Individual
- Select tax regime: **New (recommended for most salaried without deductions)** or Old

**Part B — Gross Total Income**
- Salary: Pre-filled from Form 16
- House Property: Add rental income (if any) or interest on home loan (if any)
- Other Sources: FD interest, savings interest (check AIS for amounts)

**Part C — Deductions and Taxable Income**
If using **new regime**: No deductions to claim (only standard deduction ₹75,000 for salaried)
If using **old regime**: Claim 80C (LIC, PF, ELSS, PPF etc.), 80D (health insurance), 24(b) (home loan interest), HRA, etc.

**Part D — Tax Computation**
System calculates tax based on:
- Your income
- Standard deduction (₹75K new regime / ₹50K old regime)
- Deductions claimed
- Applicable slab rates

**New regime zero tax**: If net taxable income ≤ ₹12L (after ₹75K standard deduction = salary ≤ ₹12.75L), rebate Section 87A makes tax = zero.

**Part E — TDS and Taxes Paid**
Pre-filled from Form 26AS. Verify TDS deducted matches your Form 16 Part A.

**If tax payable**: A positive balance means you owe more tax — pay via "e-Pay Tax" before submitting.
**If refund due**: Negative balance means ITD owes you — verify bank account for refund credit.

### Step 5: Verify and Submit

- Review all details
- Click **Preview Return** → Check each section
- Click **Proceed to Validation** → system checks for errors
- Fix any validation errors flagged
- Click **Submit**

### Step 6: e-Verify (Mandatory)

Filing is INCOMPLETE until e-verified. Options:
1. **Aadhaar OTP** (fastest, instant) — preferred
2. **Net Banking** → Login to bank → EVC
3. **Bank ATM** (at SBI ATMs)
4. **Digital Signature Certificate** (DSC)
5. **Post signed ITR-V** to CPC Bangalore (takes 30 days)

**Aadhaar OTP**: Your Aadhaar must be linked to a mobile number. OTP sent to that mobile.

## New Regime vs Old Regime — Quick Comparison

**New Regime (Default from FY 2023-24)**:

| Slab | Rate |
|------|------|
| ₹0–4L | 0% |
| ₹4L–8L | 5% |
| ₹8L–12L | 10% |
| ₹12L–16L | 15% |
| ₹16L–20L | 20% |
| ₹20L–24L | 25% |
| ₹24L+ | 30% |

**Rebate at ₹12L**: Section 87A rebate = ₹60,000. Wipes out all tax if net income ≤ ₹12L.
**Salaried**: Additional ₹75K standard deduction → zero tax up to ₹12.75L.

**Old Regime**: Same structure with 80C (₹1.5L), 80D (₹25K), home loan interest, HRA exemption.

**Use old regime if**: 80C + 80D + home loan interest + HRA > ₹4.25L (more than the difference in slabs covers). Use an online calculator to compare.

## Common Mistakes in ITR Filing

| Mistake | Consequence |
|---------|------------|
| Not including FD interest income | Mismatch with AIS → notice |
| Wrong bank account number for refund | Refund returned, needs correction |
| Claiming wrong deductions in wrong regime | Notice + penalty + interest |
| Not e-verifying after filing | ITR not considered filed (invalid) |
| ITR-1 when you have capital gains | Wrong form → notice |
| Mismatch between TDS claimed and 26AS | Tax department notice |

## What Happens After Filing

- **Acknowledgment (ITR-V)**: Generated immediately. Save the PDF.
- **Processing**: Income Tax Department processes your return
- **Refund**: If refund is due, typically credited to bank in 15–45 days (for most simple returns)
- **Intimation Section 143(1)**: Email from ITD confirming your return is processed

**Check refund status**: incometax.gov.in → e-File → View Filed Returns → Refund status

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My employer deducted enough TDS. Do I still need to file ITR?

Yes — ITR filing is mandatory if: total income > ₹3L (new regime), or if you want to claim refund, or if you have foreign assets. Many salaried employees must file even if no additional tax is due.

### My Form 16 shows different income from what I received. What do I do?

Contact HR to issue a corrected Form 16. Filing with wrong Form 16 numbers causes mismatches with employer's TDS return → notice.

### I joined a new company mid-year. How do I handle two Form 16s?

File ITR-1. The portal allows you to enter income from multiple employers. Add both salaries together (check for standard deduction claimed once by each employer — standard deduction ₹75K is once per year, not per employer). Also submit Form 12B to your new employer showing previous salary.

### What's the deadline and penalty for late filing?

**Deadline**: July 31, 2026 for FY 2025-26.
**Late filing (August to December 2026)**: ₹5,000 penalty (₹1,000 if income ≤ ₹5L).
**After December 31**: Cannot file belated return; must file Updated Return (139(8A)) with 25% extra tax.
