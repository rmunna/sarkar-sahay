# NAD Account: How to Access Academic Records Online

The National Academic Depository (NAD) is a government initiative that stores academic awards — degrees, diplomas, certificates, and mark sheets — in digital form. If your university or board has deposited your records on NAD, you can access, download, and share them online anytime.

## What is NAD?

NAD is a digital repository established by the Ministry of Education under UGC regulations. It acts as a national-level online store for all academic awards issued by institutions and boards registered with NAD. The depository is managed by authorised entities and allows:

- **Students** to access and share their academic records digitally
- **Employers** to verify academic credentials online
- **Universities** to deposit and manage academic awards

NAD is integrated with [DigiLocker](https://www.digilocker.gov.in/) for seamless access.

---

## Who Can Use NAD?

- **Students/Alumni** whose institution has deposited records on NAD
- **Employers and verifiers** who need to authenticate academic credentials
- **Academic institutions** registered with NAD for record deposit
- No restriction on year of passing — older records can also be lodged on request

---

## Documents Required for Registration

- **Aadhaar number** (for identity verification)
- **Mobile number** linked to Aadhaar
- **Email address**
- **Academic details** (institution name, course, year of passing, roll number)

---

## How to Register on NAD: Step-by-Step

### Method 1: Via NAD Portal

1. **Visit** [nad.gov.in](https://nad.gov.in/)
2. **Click "Register"** or **"Student Login"**
3. **Select registration type** — Student / Verifier / Institution
4. **Enter Aadhaar number** for identity verification
5. **Verify via OTP** sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile
6. **Fill in personal details** — name, date of birth, email
7. **Add academic details:**
   - Select your university/board
   - Enter course name, year of passing
   - Provide roll number or registration number
8. **Submit the registration**
9. Your NAD account will be created and login credentials sent to your email

### Method 2: Via DigiLocker

1. Log in to [DigiLocker](https://www.digilocker.gov.in/)
2. Go to **"Issued Documents"** section
3. Search for your university or board
4. If your institution has deposited records on NAD, they will appear in DigiLocker
5. You can download or share them directly

---

## How to Search and Access Certificates

1. **Log in** to your NAD account at [nad.gov.in](https://nad.gov.in/)
2. Go to **"My Records"** or **"Search Certificates"**
3. You will see all academic awards deposited by your institution
4. **Click on any certificate** to view details
5. **Download** the certificate in PDF format
6. **Share** the certificate link with employers or other institutions for verification

---

## University Verification Process

Employers and institutions can verify academic records through NAD:

1. Register as a **Verifier** on [nad.gov.in](https://nad.gov.in/)
2. Enter the student's details (name, institution, year)
3. Or use the **verification link** shared by the student
4. NAD confirms the authenticity of the academic award
5. Verification results are displayed instantly

---

## How to Lodge a Request for Missing Records

If your academic records are not available on NAD:

1. Log in to your NAD account
2. Go to **"Lodge Request"** or **"Request Record"**
3. Select your institution and provide academic details
4. Submit the request
5. NAD forwards the request to your institution
6. The institution verifies and deposits the record
7. You receive a notification once the record is available

**Note:** Processing time depends on the institution. Follow up with your university if there is a delay.

## Important Tips

1. **Check DigiLocker first** — your NAD records may already be accessible there
2. **Use your exact details** (name, roll number) as per your institution records to avoid mismatch
3. **Contact your university** if records are not deposited — request them to upload to NAD
4. **NAD records are legally valid** and accepted by employers and government bodies
5. **Keep your Aadhaar mobile active** for OTP-based login and verification

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Is NAD registration mandatory?

NAD registration is not mandatory for students, but it gives you easy digital access to your academic records. Many institutions now deposit records on NAD by default.

### Q2: Can I access records from any university on NAD?

Only if your university is registered with NAD and has deposited your records. Check the list of registered institutions on [nad.gov.in](https://nad.gov.in/).

### Q3: What if my name is different on NAD and my certificate?

Contact your institution to correct the records. NAD displays whatever the institution has deposited.

### Q4: Can employers directly verify my degree on NAD?

Yes, employers can register as verifiers on NAD and authenticate your academic records online.

### Q5: Is NAD the same as ABC?

No. NAD stores completed academic awards (degrees, mark sheets). [ABC (Academic Bank of Credits)](/guide/abc-academic-bank-credits-guide) stores ongoing academic credits for flexible learning.

### Q6: Are old degrees (before NAD was launched) available?

Only if your institution has retrospectively uploaded them to NAD. You can lodge a request for your institution to do so.

### Q7: Is NAD accepted for government job applications?

Yes, NAD records are digitally signed and legally valid. They are accepted for government job applications, higher education admissions, and visa processing.
