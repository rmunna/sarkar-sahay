## What is Mukhyamantri Abhyudaya Yojana?

**Mukhyamantri Abhyudaya Yojana** is the Uttar Pradesh government scheme that provides **completely free coaching** to students preparing for competitive examinations including UPSC IAS/IPS, UPPSC PCS, SSC, NDA, JEE, NEET, and other national and state-level exams.

The scheme was launched on **Basant Panchami 2021** and has trained over 2 lakh+ students. Coaching is offered by experienced IAS/PCS officers and subject experts — at **no cost** to the student.

Key features:
- **Free coaching** for UPSC, UPPSC, SSC, NEET, JEE, NDA, CDS, and more
- **Online (on abhyuday.up.gov.in) and offline classes** at divisional headquarters
- Study material provided free
- Access to IAS/IPS officer-led sessions
- Mock tests and doubt sessions
- Hostel facility for outstation students (in some centres)

## Who is Eligible?

- Must be a **resident of Uttar Pradesh**
- Must have passed **graduation** (for UPSC/PCS)
- **Class 12 pass** for NDA/SSC/NEET/JEE
- No income limit — open to all UP youth
- Priority to economically weaker sections

## How to Apply — Step by Step

### Online Registration

1. Go to **[abhyuday.up.gov.in](https://abhyuday.up.gov.in)**
2. Click **"Registration"**
3. Enter name, mobile number, Aadhaar (or UP residence proof)
4. Select:
   - **Course/Exam** you are preparing for
   - **Batch type:** Online or Offline
   - For offline: Select **divisional headquarters** (Lucknow, Agra, Prayagraj, Kanpur, Varanasi, Gorakhpur, etc.)
5. Submit → receive confirmation SMS with batch schedule

### Offline Classes

Available at **divisional headquarter centres** across UP. Students from nearby districts can attend in-person classes. Some centres have hostel accommodation for outstation students — ask at the centre.

## What You Get

- **Live and recorded video lectures** by IAS/PCS officers on the portal
- **Downloadable study material** — curated notes, previous year papers
- **Mock tests** with ranking and performance feedback
- **Doubt-clearing sessions** — scheduled on the portal
- Access to **library resources** at offline centres
- Guest lectures by toppers and civil servants

## Documents Required for Registration

1. Aadhaar card or UP domicile proof
2. Educational qualification certificate (Class 12/graduation)
3. Passport-size photograph

No income certificate or category certificate is required for registration.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are the classes conducted by real IAS/IPS officers?
Yes — Abhyudaya Yojana is known for faculty that includes **serving IAS, IPS, IFS, and PCS officers** who volunteer their time. The quality is comparable to premium private coaching centres.

### Can a student from another state apply?
The scheme is **for UP residents only**. If you have UP domicile (Aadhaar/voter ID with UP address), you are eligible.

### I am already enrolled in a private coaching centre. Can I also register for Abhyudaya?
Yes — Abhyudaya is supplementary and free. Many students attend private coaching and also use Abhyudaya's free resources (especially mock tests and IAS officer lectures).

### Is there any hostel facility?
Selected divisional headquarters have hostel arrangements. Contact the specific centre (via abhyuday.up.gov.in) to check hostel availability and rules.

### Is Abhyudaya available on mobile app?
Yes — access the portal via mobile browser at [abhyuday.up.gov.in](https://abhyuday.up.gov.in). Recorded lectures and study material can be downloaded for offline viewing.

### When are new batches announced?
New batches typically start in February–March (after board exams) and September–October. Check the portal for current batch schedules.
