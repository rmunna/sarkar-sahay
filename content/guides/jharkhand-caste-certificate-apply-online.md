# Jharkhand Caste Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **caste certificate** (जाति प्रमाण पत्र — Jaati Praman Patra) in Jharkhand is issued by the Revenue Department (Circle Officer / Anchal Adhikari) and is required for OBC/SC/ST reservations, Jharkhand government scholarships, JCECEB (Jharkhand Combined Entrance Exam Board) reserved category seats, government job applications (JPSC), and tribal welfare scheme eligibility. Applications go through **Jharsewa** — Jharkhand's e-District portal.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [jharsewa.jharkhand.gov.in](https://jharsewa.jharkhand.gov.in) or **Pragya Kendra**. Issued by Circle Officer within **20–30 days**.

---

## Caste Categories in Jharkhand

| Category | Reservation % | Notes |
|----------|---------------|-------|
| **SC** | 10% | Scheduled Castes |
| **ST** | 26% | Scheduled Tribes — Santali, Mundari, Ho, Oraon, Kharia communities |
| **OBC-I** | 14% | OBC Class I (most backward communities) |
| **OBC-II** | 8% | OBC Class II (backward communities) |
| **EWS** | 10% | General category, income < ₹8 lakh/year |

> Jharkhand has one of the **highest ST reservations** (26%) due to its large Adivasi population. The Santhali, Mundari, Ho, Oraon (Kurukh), and Kharia are major tribal communities.

## Why You Need Jharkhand Caste Certificate

- **Jharkhand state SC/ST/OBC scholarship** — caste proof mandatory
- **JCECEB engineering/medical/other admissions** — reserved category seats
- **NEET Jharkhand state quota** — caste certificate required
- **Government job reservation** — JPSC, JSSC (Jharkhand Staff Selection Commission)
- **Tribal welfare scheme eligibility** — caste certificate for Adivasi schemes
- **Central OBC NCL** — for UPSC/SSC (if community in central OBC list)

---

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory)
- **Ration card** (shows family caste/community)
- **Father's school leaving certificate** (TC with caste listed)
- **Khatian / land records** (for ST applicants — showing tribal land rights)
- Self-declaration of caste (online affidavit)
- **Voter ID** (address proof)
- Passport-size photograph

---

## How to Apply Online — Jharsewa Portal

1. Go to [jharsewa.jharkhand.gov.in](https://jharsewa.jharkhand.gov.in)
2. Register / log in with mobile number
3. Click **"Revenue & Land Reforms"** → **"Caste Certificate"**
4. Fill form:
   - Applicant name, address (district, sub-division, block, village)
   - Caste/community declaration (SC / ST / OBC-I / OBC-II)
   - Father's community details
5. Upload documents (Aadhaar, father's TC, ration card, photograph)
6. Pay fee: **₹25–40** (online or at kiosk)
7. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via Pragya Kendra / CSC

Jharkhand's **Pragya Kendras** (block-level service kiosks) handle Jharsewa applications:

1. Visit nearest **Pragya Kendra** or CSC
2. Carry Aadhaar, ration card, father's caste proof
3. Operator fills and submits caste certificate request
4. Service charge: approximately ₹30–60
5. Collect acknowledgement receipt

---

## Status Check & Download

1. Go to [jharsewa.jharkhand.gov.in](https://jharsewa.jharkhand.gov.in)
2. Click **"Track Application Status"** → enter reference number
3. Once approved: download digitally signed PDF
4. Valid for Jharkhand state scholarships, JCECEB, JPSC applications

## Frequently Asked Questions

### OBC-I vs OBC-II in Jharkhand — which is my category?

**OBC-I** (more backward): Chamar, Dhobi, Nut, Bazigar, and 21 other communities with 14% reservation. **OBC-II** (backward): Yadav, Koiri, Kurmi, Teli, Kayastha (some sub-castes), and others with 8% reservation. Check the Jharkhand OBC-I and OBC-II government schedule or ask at your Circle Office.

### ST caste certificate Jharkhand — who issues?

The **Circle Officer (Anchal Adhikari)** issues all caste certificates in Jharkhand including ST. For tribal communities (Santhali, Mundari, Ho, Oraon, etc.), the Circle Officer verifies through local records, Gram Sabha, and community documentation.

### Jharkhand ST caste certificate for JPSC — how valid?

For **JPSC (Jharkhand Public Service Commission)**, the ST caste certificate is permanent once issued. However, for annual scholarships and some scheme applications, a fresh certificate may be required.
