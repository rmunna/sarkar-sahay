## What is Apna Khata Rajasthan?

Apna Khata is the official online land records portal of the Rajasthan government, maintained by the Board of Revenue. It provides free access to land records including jamabandi (record of rights), nakal (copy), khasra details, and plot maps for all 33 districts of Rajasthan.

The portal allows citizens to view and download land records without visiting the Patwari or tehsil office. It is one of the earliest and most widely used state land record portals in India.

## Key Features

- **Jamabandi Nakal** — View and download ownership records
- **Khasra details** — Plot-level land information
- **Plot map (Naksha)** — Boundary map of land plots
- **District/tehsil search** — Navigate by location hierarchy
- **Owner name search** — Find records using landowner's name
- **Free access** — No charges for online viewing

## How to Check Rajasthan Land Records Online (Step-by-Step)

### Step 1: Visit Apna Khata Portal

Go to [apnakhata.raj.nic.in](https://apnakhata.raj.nic.in).

### Step 2: Select District

Click on your **जिला (District)** from the Rajasthan map displayed on the homepage.

### Step 3: Select Tehsil

Choose your **तहसील (Tehsil)** from the list displayed for the selected district.

### Step 4: Select Village

Select your **गाँव (Village)** from the list. You can also search by village name.

### Step 5: Enter Details

Enter your details to search:
- **खाता संख्या (Khata Number)** — Account number
- **खसरा संख्या (Khasra Number)** — Plot number
- **नाम से खोजें (Search by Name)** — Owner's name

### Step 6: View Jamabandi Nakal

Click **"नकल"** (Copy) to view the jamabandi record showing:
- Owner name(s) and father's name
- Khata and khasra numbers
- Plot area (in bigha/hectare)
- Land type (irrigated, unirrigated, residential)
- Revenue details
- Mutation history and remarks

### Step 7: Download/Print

You can print the jamabandi nakal directly from the browser or save as PDF.

## How to View Plot Map (Naksha)

1. Visit [apnakhata.raj.nic.in](https://apnakhata.raj.nic.in)
2. Navigate to your district → tehsil → village
3. Click on **"नक्शा"** (Map) option
4. Enter the khasra number
5. View the plot boundary map showing adjacent plots and measurements

## How to Get Certified Copy (Nakal)

The online jamabandi is for reference. For a certified copy:

### Online Method
1. Visit the **e-Mitra portal** or an e-Mitra kiosk
2. Select "Jamabandi Nakal" service
3. Enter district, tehsil, village, and khasra number
4. Pay the fee (₹10 per nakal)
5. Download the certified/stamped nakal

### Offline Method
1. Visit the nearest **Patwari office** or **tehsil office**
2. Submit an application with khasra number details
3. Pay the prescribed fee
4. Collect the certified nakal

## How to Check Mutation (Dakhil Kharij) Status

1. Visit [apnakhata.raj.nic.in](https://apnakhata.raj.nic.in)
2. Look for **"दाखिल खारिज"** (Mutation) section
3. Select district → tehsil
4. Search by application number or party name
5. View mutation status — pending, hearing date, approved, or rejected

## Land Records Terminology in Rajasthan

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Jamabandi | Record of rights (ownership details) |
| Nakal | Copy of the land record |
| Khasra | Plot/survey number |
| Khata | Account containing one or more plots |
| Girdawari | Crop inspection record |
| Dakhil Kharij | Mutation (ownership transfer record) |
| Patta | Allotment document for government land |

## Important Tips

1. **Use khasra number** — Searching by khasra (plot) number gives the most accurate results. Keep this number handy from your previous records.
2. **Verify before purchase** — Always check the jamabandi and mutation history on Apna Khata before buying property in Rajasthan.
3. **Certified copy for legal use** — The online nakal is for reference. For bank loans, court cases, or registration, get a certified copy via e-Mitra.
4. **Check girdawari** — The girdawari (crop inspection) record helps verify if the land is being used as claimed.
5. **Track mutations** — After any property transfer, ensure the mutation is completed on record. Pending mutations can cause problems during future transactions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Apna Khata Rajasthan?
Apna Khata ([apnakhata.raj.nic.in](https://apnakhata.raj.nic.in)) is the official online portal of the Rajasthan government to check land records. You can view jamabandi nakal, khasra details, and plot maps for all 33 districts.

### How do I check my land record in Rajasthan?
Visit [apnakhata.raj.nic.in](https://apnakhata.raj.nic.in), select your district from the map, then choose tehsil and village. Search by khata number, khasra number, or owner name to view your land record.

### Is Apna Khata free to use?
Yes, viewing land records online on Apna Khata is completely free. Charges apply only for certified copies via e-Mitra (₹10 per page).

### Can I download jamabandi nakal from Apna Khata?
You can view and print the jamabandi online for reference. For a certified copy, use the e-Mitra portal or visit an e-Mitra kiosk.

### How do I find my khasra number in Rajasthan?
You can search by owner name on Apna Khata to find your khasra number. Alternatively, check your old land documents or ask your village Patwari.

### Is the online jamabandi nakal legally valid?
The online nakal from Apna Khata is for reference only. For legal purposes, you need a certified copy from e-Mitra or the Patwari office.

### Can I check land records for any district in Rajasthan?
Yes, Apna Khata covers all 33 districts of Rajasthan. You can check records for any village in any tehsil.
