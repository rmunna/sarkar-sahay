## What is Indian Navy Recruitment?

The Indian Navy offers multiple entry schemes for officers and sailors. Officers can join through UPSC exams (NDA/CDS), university entry schemes, or direct recruitment through the Indian Navy's own selection process. Sailors are recruited through periodic recruitment drives conducted by the Navy.

The Indian Navy has three main branches — **Executive**, **Engineering**, and **Education** — with further specializations within each.

## Officer Entry Schemes

### Through UPSC
- **NDA (National Defence Academy):** For 10+2 candidates (16.5-19.5 years). See our [NDA exam guide](/guide/nda-exam-guide)
- **CDS (Combined Defence Services):** For graduates with Physics and Maths at 10+2 or engineering graduates. See our [CDS exam guide](/guide/cds-exam-guide)

### Through Indian Navy (Direct Entry)

| Entry Scheme | Qualification | Age |
|-------------|--------------|-----|
| 10+2 B.Tech Cadet Entry | 10+2 with PCM (70%+) | 17-19.5 years |
| Graduate Cadet Entry (Engineering) | BE/BTech with 60% | 19.5-25 years |
| Short Service Commission (Executive/Technical) | Engineering degree with 60% | 19.5-25 years |
| SSC (Logistics) | MBA/B.Com/CA/ICWA with 60% | 19.5-25 years |
| SSC (Education) | M.Sc/MA with 50% in specified subjects | 21-25 years |
| SSC (Law) | LLB with 55% | 22-27 years |
| SSC (Pilot/Observer) | Any degree with 60%, Maths & Physics at 10+2 | 19-24 years |
| University Entry Scheme (UES) | Final year engineering students | 19-24 years |

### For Women Officers
- Women can join as SSC officers in Executive, Education, Law, Logistics, ATC, and Naval Architecture branches
- Permanent Commission is available in select branches

## Sailor Entry Schemes

### Agniveer (Navy)
- **Agniveer SSR (Senior Secondary Recruit):** 10+2 with PCM, age 17.5-21 years
- **Agniveer MR (Matric Recruit):** 10th pass, age 17.5-21 years
- **Duration:** 4 years under Agnipath scheme (25% retained for permanent service)

### Artificer Apprentice
- 10+2 with PCM (60%+)
- Age: 17.5-21 years
- Technical training at INS Chilka

## Selection Process for Officers

### Stage 1: Shortlisting
- Based on academic marks, JEE score (for 10+2 B.Tech), or written exam
- SSC entries use a separate online exam or merit-based shortlisting

### Stage 2: SSB Interview (5 Days)
- **Day 1:** Screening — Officer Intelligence Rating (OIR) and PPDT
- **Days 2-4:** Psychological tests, Group Testing Officer (GTO) tasks, Personal Interview
- **Day 5:** Conference
- SSB centres: Bangalore, Bhopal, Coimbatore, Visakhapatnam

### Stage 3: Medical Examination
- Conducted at INHS hospitals
- Vision: 6/6 without glasses for executive and pilot branches
- Strict standards for color vision and hearing

### Stage 4: Merit List
- Final list based on SSB marks and academic record
- IHQ-MoD (Navy) publishes the final selection

## Selection Process for Sailors

1. **Online Exam** — computer-based test covering Maths, Science, English, and General Awareness
2. **Physical Fitness Test (PFT)** — 1.6 km run, push-ups, squats, sit-ups
3. **Medical Examination** — strict fitness standards
4. **Merit List** — based on exam score and PFT

## Application Process

### For Officers
1. Visit [joinindiannavy.gov.in](https://joinindiannavy.gov.in)
2. Register and create a profile
3. Apply against specific entry notifications
4. Upload documents and photograph
5. No application fee for most entries

### For Sailors (Agniveer)
1. Visit [joinindiannavy.gov.in](https://joinindiannavy.gov.in)
2. Register with Aadhaar and personal details
3. Apply when Agniveer recruitment notification is live
4. Download admit card for the online exam
5. No application fee

## Important Tips

- **Physical fitness is non-negotiable** — start a fitness regimen early; Navy values fitness highly
- **SSB is the main filter** — focus on developing officer-like qualities, leadership, and communication
- **Eye sight matters** — many entries require 6/6 vision; get checked early and consider corrective measures if needed
- **Swimming is important** — Navy officers must know swimming; learn before joining if possible
- **Multiple entries available** — if you miss one entry scheme, explore others; Navy has diverse routes for different qualifications

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can women join the Indian Navy?
Yes, women can join as officers through SSC entries in Executive, Education, Law, Logistics, and other branches. Permanent Commission is available in select branches.

### What is the Agnipath scheme for Navy?
Agnipath recruits Agniveers for 4-year terms. After 4 years, 25% are retained for permanent service. Others receive a Seva Nidhi package of approximately ₹11.71 lakh.

### Is there a written exam for Navy officer entry?
Some entries require a written exam (like SSC IT/Engineering). NDA and CDS entries require UPSC written exams. 10+2 B.Tech entry uses JEE Mains score. University Entry Scheme is based on academic merit.

### What is the retirement age in the Navy?
Officers retire at 54-58 years depending on rank. Sailors retire at 42-52 years depending on rank and service.

### Can I join the Navy after engineering?
Yes, engineering graduates have multiple entry options — CDS, SSC (Engineering/Executive), and Graduate Cadet Entry.
