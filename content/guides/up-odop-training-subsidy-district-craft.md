## What is UP ODOP Scheme?

**One District One Product (ODOP)** is Uttar Pradesh's flagship scheme to promote, preserve, and scale up the **signature craft or product of each district** — Varanasi silk, Agra leather, Bijnor handloom, Mirzapur carpet, Lucknow chikankari, and 70+ more. Each of UP's 75 districts has one promoted product.

Launched in 2018, ODOP provides artisans and entrepreneurs with **training, tools subsidy, marketing support, and preferential credit**.

## Key Benefits for Artisans

| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Skill training | Free/subsidized craft-specific training |
| Margin money subsidy | 25% subsidy on project cost (up to ₹25 lakh project) |
| Interest-free loan | Up to ₹5 lakh interest-free via MSME department |
| Toolkits | Free toolkit distribution to individual artisans |
| Market access | Sale at ODOP outlets, GeM portal, Amazon, Flipkart |
| GI tag support | Help with Geographical Indication tagging of products |
| Export support | Facilitation at international trade fairs |

## Who is Eligible?

- **Artisans and craftsmen** working in the district's ODOP craft
- **Micro enterprises** (up to ₹1 crore investment) producing ODOP items
- **SHG groups and cooperatives** of artisans
- **New entrepreneurs** wanting to start production in an ODOP craft

Priority to **SC/ST artisans**, **women**, and **traditional craftsmen families**.

## How to Apply

### Step 1 — Register on ODOP Portal

Go to **[odopup.in](https://odopup.in)**:
- Register as individual artisan / enterprise
- Select your district and ODOP product
- Upload Aadhaar, bank details, craft details

### Step 2 — Apply for Training

- Training application opens periodically through DIC (District Industries Centre)
- Training is organized at craft clusters and government facilities

### Step 3 — Apply for Loan / Subsidy

After training certificate:
- Apply for ODOP margin money subsidy at DIC
- Bank loan facilitated through empanelled banks
- Subsidy credited to loan account directly

### Step 4 — Market Access

After production starts:
- List products on ODOP Bazaar (odopup.in)
- Apply for GeM seller registration (gem.gov.in)
- Participate in ODOP exhibitions held quarterly across UP

## ODOP Common Facility Centres

Government-run Common Facility Centres in craft clusters provide:
- Modern machinery (brass casting, leather stitching, carpet weaving, etc.)
- Quality testing labs
- Packaging and design support
- Shared use at nominal charge

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. I make chikankari in Lucknow. How do I apply for the ODOP scheme?**

Register at odopup.in — select Lucknow — Chikankari. Then contact the District Industries Centre (DIC) Lucknow for training enrollment and toolkit distribution.

**2. Can I sell my ODOP products online?**

Yes. ODOP portal has an e-commerce marketplace. You can also register on Amazon, Flipkart, and GeM. Training on online selling is provided at Common Facility Centres.

**3. I want to start a small pottery unit in Khurja. Can ODOP give me a loan?**

Yes. Khurja's ODOP product is pottery. You are eligible for the ₹5 lakh interest-free loan and 25% margin money subsidy. Apply at DIC Bulandshahr.

**4. My product has a GI tag. What additional support does ODOP give?**

ODOP supports GI-tagged products with marketing at national/international exhibitions, brand development, and e-commerce listing under official GI branding.

**5. Can a women's SHG apply collectively for ODOP benefits?**

Yes. Women's SHGs working in craft production can apply collectively for subsidized CFCs, group training, and bulk market linkage under ODOP.
