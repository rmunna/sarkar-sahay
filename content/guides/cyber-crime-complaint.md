# Cyber Crime Complaint – How to Report Cyber Crime in India Online

Cyber crimes — including online fraud, hacking, identity theft, phishing, and cyber bullying — are on the rise in India. The Government of India has set up a dedicated portal and helpline to report these crimes quickly. This guide covers how to file a complaint online and offline.

---

## What is Cyber Crime?

Cyber crime is any criminal activity that involves a computer, network, or digital device. Common types include:

- **Online financial fraud** — UPI fraud, credit/debit card fraud, phishing
- **Identity theft** — someone using your personal details fraudulently
- **Hacking** — unauthorized access to your accounts, devices, or data
- **Cyber stalking & harassment** — threats, bullying, or stalking online
- **Sextortion & revenge porn** — blackmail using intimate images
- **Social media fraud** — fake profiles, impersonation, scam pages
- **Online job/loan scams** — fake job offers or loan approval tricks
- **Ransomware** — locking your data and demanding payment

---

## How to Report Cyber Crime — Two Immediate Steps

### 1. Call the Cyber Crime Helpline: 1930

- **Available:** 24x7
- **Purpose:** Immediate reporting, especially for financial fraud
- **Action:** The helpline can freeze fraudulent transactions quickly
- **Call IMMEDIATELY** if money has been stolen — the faster you call, the higher the chance of recovery

### 2. File Online at cybercrime.gov.in

The National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal handles two categories:

- **Women/Child Related Crimes** — cyber stalking, child pornography, sextortion
- **Other Cyber Crimes** — financial fraud, hacking, identity theft, etc.

---

## Step-by-Step: File Cyber Crime Complaint Online

1. Visit [cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in/)
2. Click **"File a Complaint"**
3. Read the terms and click **"Accept"**
4. **Login/Register:**
   - Enter your mobile number
   - Verify with OTP
5. Select the **category:**
   - Women/Child Related Crime
   - Other Cyber Crime
6. For **financial fraud**, select the sub-category:
   - UPI fraud
   - Internet banking fraud
   - Debit/credit card fraud
   - Business email compromise
   - E-wallet fraud
   - Crypto fraud
7. Fill in the **incident details:**
   - Date and time of incident
   - Description of what happened
   - Amount lost (if any)
   - Suspect details (if known)
   - Bank/payment details involved
8. **Upload evidence:**
   - Screenshots of messages/transactions
   - Bank statements
   - Email headers
   - Any other relevant proof
9. **Submit** the complaint
10. Note down your **Complaint ID** for tracking

---

## Step-by-Step: File Cyber Crime Complaint Offline

1. Visit your **nearest police station** or the dedicated **Cyber Crime Cell**
2. Bring a **written complaint** with:
   - Description of the incident
   - Timeline of events
   - Details of financial loss
   - Suspect information (if any)
3. Bring **printed evidence:** screenshots, bank statements, messages
4. Bring your **ID proof** (Aadhaar, PAN, etc.)
5. An FIR will be registered under relevant sections of the IT Act and BNS/IPC

---

## What Evidence to Preserve

Act quickly to preserve evidence before it disappears:

- **Screenshots** of fraudulent messages, emails, or transactions
- **Bank/UPI statements** showing unauthorized transactions
- **Call logs** and phone numbers of the scammer
- **Email headers** (full headers, not just the body)
- **URLs/website links** used in the scam
- **Social media profiles** of the accused (screenshot before they delete)
- **Any OTP or verification messages** received

**Important:** Do NOT delete any messages or communications related to the crime.

## Track Your Complaint

1. Visit [cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in/)
2. Click **"Track Your Complaint"**
3. Enter your **Complaint ID** and mobile number
4. View the current status and any actions taken

## Important Tips

1. **Report financial fraud within 24 hours** — call 1930 immediately to freeze the transaction
2. **Don't share OTP** with anyone — banks/UPI apps never ask for OTP over phone
3. **Preserve all evidence** — screenshots, messages, transaction records
4. **Block your cards** immediately if you suspect unauthorized use
5. **Change passwords** of all affected accounts right away
6. **Report to your bank** separately — they have their own fraud reporting mechanism

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Q1. Is there a fee for filing a cyber crime complaint?**
No. Filing a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in or the police station is completely free.

**Q2. Will the police help if the scammer is in another state?**
Yes. Cyber crime complaints are handled regardless of jurisdiction. The National Cyber Crime portal routes complaints to the appropriate state.

**Q3. Can I get my money back after a cyber fraud?**
It depends on how quickly you report. If you call 1930 within minutes/hours, the helpline can freeze the funds in the scammer's account. Recovery rates decrease with delay.

**Q4. What laws cover cyber crime in India?**
Primarily the Information Technology Act, 2000 (and amendments), along with relevant sections of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS) / Indian Penal Code (IPC).

**Q5. Can I file a complaint anonymously?**
For women/child related crimes, you can initially report anonymously on the portal. For other crimes, you need to provide your identity.

**Q6. What is the CEIR portal for?**
The Central Equipment Identity Register (ceir.gov.in) lets you block your lost or stolen phone's IMEI number, rendering it unusable on Indian networks.

**Q7. How long does investigation take?**
It varies depending on the complexity of the case. Financial fraud cases where money is traced quickly may see faster resolution (weeks). Complex cases may take months.
