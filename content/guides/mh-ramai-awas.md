## What is Ramai Awas Yojana?

Ramai Awas Yojana is a housing scheme by the Government of Maharashtra under the Department of Social Justice and Special Assistance. It provides **financial assistance for building pucca houses** to homeless families belonging to Scheduled Castes (SC), Navbouddha, Nomadic Tribes (NT), De-Notified Tribes (DNT), and Similar Communities.

The scheme is named after **Ramai** (Ramabai Ambedkar, wife of Dr. B.R. Ambedkar) and aims to provide dignified housing to the most marginalized communities in Maharashtra.

### Financial Assistance

| Area | Assistance Amount |
|------|------------------|
| Rural areas | ₹1,20,000 – ₹1,40,000 |
| Urban areas | ₹2,00,000 – ₹2,50,000 |

> The exact amount may vary based on current government notifications and revisions. Additional amounts may be available for toilet construction under Swachh Bharat Mission.

## Who is Eligible?

- Must belong to **SC, Navbouddha, NT, DNT, or Similar Communities** (with valid caste certificate)
- Must be a **resident of Maharashtra**
- Must be **homeless** — should not own a pucca house anywhere in India
- **Annual family income** should not exceed ₹1 lakh (rural) or ₹1.50 lakh (urban) — as per current norms
- Applicant should have **own land** or land allotted by government/gram panchayat for house construction
- Applicant should **not have availed** housing benefit from any other government scheme
- Age: 18 years or above

## Documents Required

- Caste certificate (SC/NT/DNT category) issued by competent authority
- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
- Income certificate from Tehsildar
- Domicile certificate of Maharashtra
- Land ownership document (7/12 extract, property card, or allotment letter from Gram Panchayat)
- BPL ration card
- Bank passbook (nationalized bank, Aadhaar-linked)
- Passport-size photographs
- Self-declaration of not owning a pucca house
- Self-declaration of not having received housing benefit from other government schemes

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Online Application

1. **Visit** [Aaple Sarkar portal](https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in) or the SJSA department portal
2. **Register** with your mobile number and create an account
3. **Select** Ramai Awas Yojana under Social Justice Department schemes
4. **Fill** the application form — personal details, caste, income, land details, bank account
5. **Upload** scanned documents — caste certificate, Aadhaar, income certificate, land document, bank passbook
6. **Submit** and note the application reference number
7. **Track** your application status online

### Offline Application

1. **Visit** the District Social Welfare Office or Tehsil office
2. **Collect** the Ramai Awas Yojana application form
3. **Fill** the form and attach self-attested copies of all documents
4. **Submit** to the Social Welfare Officer
5. **Collect** acknowledgement receipt
6. **Verification** — officials will inspect your land and verify documents
7. **Approval** — after verification, the application is approved at district level

## Disbursement

The financial assistance is typically released in **3 instalments**:
1. **First instalment** — upon approval and commencement of construction
2. **Second instalment** — after construction reaches lintel level
3. **Third instalment** — after completion of the house

Each instalment is released after physical verification by the concerned officer.

## Processing Time

- Application verification: **1-3 months**
- Approval: **3-6 months** (subject to fund availability and beneficiary selection)
- Construction completion deadline: **12-18 months** from first instalment

## Fees

**No fees** are charged for the application. The scheme is entirely free.

## Important Tips

1. **Secure land first** — You must have land (own or allotted) before applying. If landless, approach the Gram Panchayat for government land allotment
2. **Get caste certificate early** — The caste certificate verification process can take time; apply well in advance
3. **Don't use middlemen** — Apply directly through the portal or government office
4. **Complete construction on time** — Failure to complete construction within the deadline may result in recovery of funds
5. **Combine with other benefits** — You may be eligible for additional toilet construction funds under Swachh Bharat Mission

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Can women apply for Ramai Awas Yojana?
Yes. In fact, preference is given to applications where the **house is in the woman's name** or joint name.

### Q2. Can I apply if I have a kutcha house?
Yes. If you own only a kutcha (non-permanent) house and meet other eligibility criteria, you can apply.

### Q3. Is this scheme only for rural areas?
No. Ramai Awas Yojana covers both **rural and urban** areas, with different assistance amounts.

### Q4. What if I don't have land?
You must have land to apply. Approach your **Gram Panchayat** (rural) or **Municipal Corporation** (urban) for government land allotment for housing.

### Q5. Can I get benefits from both Ramai Awas and Gharkul Yojana?
No. You can avail housing benefit from **only one** government housing scheme. See our [Gharkul Yojana guide](/guide/mh-gharkul-yojana) for comparison.

### Q6. How is the beneficiary selected?
Selection is based on a priority list prepared at the district level considering homelessness, income, family situation, and other vulnerability criteria.
