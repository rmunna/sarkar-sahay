# Nepal, Bhutan, Sri Lanka — Visa Rules for Indians 2026

Three of the most popular budget-international destinations from India — and each has very different entry rules. Nepal is free and easy; Bhutan requires permits + daily fee; Sri Lanka needs an online ETA. Here's the complete guide.

---

## Nepal — No Visa Required for Indians

### Entry Rules

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| **Visa** | NOT required — Indians enter Nepal visa-free |
| **ID document required** | One of: Passport, Aadhaar card, Voter ID, Birth Certificate (for minors) |
| **Passport not mandatory** | Any of the above Indian ID documents accepted |
| **Duration** | Unlimited — no official stay limit for Indian citizens |
| **Currency** | Indian Rupees accepted widely; bring INR 100 denomination and below (INR 500 and 2,000 notes not accepted in Nepal) |

**Important**: INR 500 and ₹2,000 notes are NOT accepted in Nepal. Carry ₹100 notes or lower, or exchange at border/Kathmandu for Nepalese Rupees.

### Entry Points

India-Nepal border open at multiple points:
- **Raxaul–Birgunj** (Bihar → Kathmandu)
- **Sunauli–Bhairahawa** (Gorakhpur → Pokhara/Kathmandu)
- **Kakarbhitta–Panitanki** (West Bengal → east Nepal)
- **Banbasa–Mahendranagar** (Uttarakhand → west Nepal)
- **Kathmandu airport**: Direct flights from Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Bangalore

### What to Carry to Nepal

| Item | Notes |
|------|-------|
| Indian ID (Aadhaar, Voter ID, or Passport) | Required at border/airport immigration |
| INR cash (100 and below denominations) | ₹500 and ₹2000 notes NOT accepted |
| Return bus/flight ticket | Border exit proof isn't mandatory, but good to have |

### Nepal Practical Info

- **Currency**: Nepalese Rupee (NPR); 1 INR = ~1.6 NPR
- **ATMs**: Available in Kathmandu, Pokhara; limited in trekking areas
- **Calling**: Indian SIM works with roaming; or buy Nepal SIM at airport/border (NTC/NCELL)
- **Trekking permits**: For Annapurna, Everest Base Camp — separate permits required (TIMS + ACAP/SAGARMATHA fee)

## Bhutan — Protected Area Permit + Daily Fee

### Entry Rules

Unlike Nepal, Bhutan requires Indian travelers to get permits:

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| **Visa** | NOT required (Indians exempt from visa) |
| **Protected Area Permit (PAP)** | Required — obtained at border/airport; FREE |
| **Sustainable Development Fee (SDF)** | USD 100 per person per night (reduced from USD 200 in 2023 temporarily) |
| **ID document** | Passport OR Voter ID for Indians |

**SDF (Sustainable Development Fee)**:
- Bhutan charges USD 100/night/person since September 2022 (previously USD 200 from 2022 change)
- Covers sustainable tourism fund, high-value low-impact policy
- A 7-night Bhutan trip: USD 700 per person in SDF alone (₹58,000+)
- Bhutan is deliberately expensive to stay exclusive and low-impact

**What SDF covers** (partially): SDF contributes to your accommodation, guided tours, and local tax — you still pay separately for accommodation and tours; SDF is an additional government levy.

### How to Get Permits

**Option 1: At the border/airport (Indian nationals)**
- Carry Indian passport or Voter ID
- Pay SDF at border/airport
- PAP issued at the entry point

**Option 2: Book through Bhutan tour operator**
- Bhutan Tourism mandates a Bhutanese licensed guide for most areas
- Tour operator can handle permits and SDF payment
- Simplest approach for first-time visitors

### Entry Points to Bhutan from India

- **Phuentsholing** (from Siliguri, West Bengal — popular road entry)
- **Samdrup Jongkhar** (from Assam/Guwahati)
- **Gelephu** (from Assam)
- **Paro Airport** (Druk Air and Bhutan Airlines flights from Delhi, Kolkata)

### Bhutan Practical Info

- Bhutan Ngultrum (BTN) is pegged 1:1 to INR — your Indian rupees are accepted everywhere
- Minimum spend: With SDF, hotels, food, guide — budget ₹1,00,000+ per person for a week
- Best time: March–May (spring flowers, clear skies) and September–November (harvest season, festivals)

## Sri Lanka — ETA Required (Online, Pre-Arrival)

### Entry Rules

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| **Visa** | ETA (Electronic Travel Authorization) required — apply online before travel |
| **Where to apply** | [eta.gov.lk](https://www.eta.gov.lk) |
| **Fee** | USD 35 (₹2,900 approx) — paid online |
| **Processing** | Instant to 24 hours (usually within minutes) |
| **Duration** | 30 days per entry (extendable) |
| **Max stay** | Up to 90 days total per year |

**Important**: Apply only at the official site **eta.gov.lk**. There are many third-party sites that charge much more for the same service. The official site costs USD 35.

### How to Apply for Sri Lanka ETA

1. Go to [eta.gov.lk](https://www.eta.gov.lk) → Apply for Tourist ETA
2. Enter passport details, contact info, arrival date
3. Pay USD 35 via credit/debit card
4. Receive ETA approval via email (ETA reference number + authorization code)
5. Show ETA reference at Sri Lanka airport immigration

**No physical document needed**: Just carry the email with your ETA reference number.

### Documents at Sri Lanka Airport

| Document | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| **Passport** | Valid 6+ months beyond stay |
| **ETA confirmation email** | Print or show on phone |
| **Return/onward ticket** | Proof of departure |
| **Hotel booking** | First accommodation at minimum |

### Sri Lanka Practical Info

- **Currency**: Sri Lankan Rupee (LKR). USD and INR accepted in tourist areas; ATMs available
- **Best time**: West coast + Colombo: December–April; East coast (Trincomalee, Arugam Bay): May–September
- **Popular spots**: Colombo, Sigiriya (rock fortress), Kandy (Temple of Tooth), Galle (colonial fort), Ella (hill station, tea estates)
- **INR acceptance**: Many tourist areas in Sri Lanka now accept INR informally — verify before relying on it

### Sri Lanka ETA Extension

30-day ETA can be extended inside Sri Lanka:
- Visit Department of Immigration, Colombo
- Extend up to 90 days total (30 days at a time)
- Fee for extension: USD 30

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I go to Nepal with only my Aadhaar card?

Yes — Aadhaar card is accepted at all India-Nepal border crossings and at Tribhuvan International Airport, Kathmandu. Carry the original Aadhaar (not laminated photocopy, though in practice it usually works).

### Is Bhutan expensive for Indians?

Yes — the SDF (USD 100/night) makes Bhutan deliberately expensive. A 7-night trip per person costs ₹58,000+ just in SDF, plus accommodation, tours, food. Budget ₹80,000–1,20,000 per person for a week.

### Can I take a train to Sri Lanka from India?

No — there is no operational train or road connection between India and Sri Lanka. You must fly (Colombo from Chennai/Mumbai/Delhi) or take a ferry (Rameswaram to Talaimannar — currently not operational as of 2026, check latest status).

### Do I need travel insurance for Nepal?

Not mandatory for entry, but essential if you're trekking — helicopter evacuation in the mountains can cost USD 3,000–8,000 without insurance. Buy adventure travel insurance with helicopter rescue coverage for trekking trips.
