## What is PM Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY)?

Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (formerly Indira Gandhi Matritva Sahyog Yojana) is a **maternity benefit programme** by the Government of India launched in **2017**. It provides **cash incentive of ₹11,000** (enhanced from ₹5,000 under PMMVY 2.0 from 2024) to pregnant women and lactating mothers to compensate for wage loss, ensure proper nutrition, and promote institutional delivery.

> **Key Change (PMMVY 2.0):** From 2024, the benefit has been enhanced to **₹11,000 for the second child** (if the child is a girl), in addition to ₹5,000 for the first child.

## Who is Eligible?

### For First Living Child — ₹5,000
- **All pregnant women and lactating mothers** for their first living child
- Must be **19 years of age or above**
- Registration must be done **within 150 days of last menstrual period (LMP)**
- **Government/PSU employees** and those availing similar benefits under other schemes are **not eligible**

### For Second Child (Girl) — ₹6,000 Additional
- Second child must be a **girl child**
- Total benefit: ₹5,000 (first child) + ₹6,000 (second girl child) = **₹11,000**

## Instalment Schedule

### First Child (₹5,000)

| Instalment | Amount | When |
|-----------|--------|------|
| **1st Instalment** | ₹3,000 | After registration of pregnancy (within 150 days of LMP) |
| **2nd Instalment** | ₹2,000 | After institutional delivery and birth registration |

### Second Child — Girl (₹6,000)

| Instalment | Amount | When |
|-----------|--------|------|
| **Single Instalment** | ₹6,000 | After birth of girl child and birth registration |

> **Note:** Under the Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY), an additional ₹1,400 is available for institutional delivery, bringing the total benefit close to ₹6,400 for the first child.

## Documents Required

### For Registration (1st Instalment)
- **Aadhaar Card** of the beneficiary (mother)
- **Aadhaar Card** of husband/family member
- **Bank account passbook** (Aadhaar-linked, in mother's name)
- **MCP Card** (Mother and Child Protection Card) from Anganwadi/health centre
- **LMP date certificate** from ANM/doctor
- **Mobile number**

### For 2nd Instalment
- **Institutional delivery proof** (hospital discharge slip)
- **Child's birth certificate**
- **Child vaccination record** (first cycle completed)

### For Second Girl Child Benefit
- **Birth certificate** of second child (girl)
- **First child's birth certificate** (to verify birth order)
- **Self-declaration** form

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Through Anganwadi Centre (Most Common)

#### Step 1: Visit Your Anganwadi
- Go to the **nearest Anganwadi centre** or **health sub-centre**
- Inform the **Anganwadi Worker (AWW)** or **ASHA worker** about your pregnancy
- They will help you register

#### Step 2: Fill Application Form
- The AWW/ASHA fills the **PMMVY form** (Form 1A for 1st instalment)
- Provide all documents (Aadhaar, bank details, MCP card)
- Sign or provide thumb impression

#### Step 3: Verification
- Block/District Programme Officer verifies the application
- Details are entered in the **PMMVY-CAS (Common Application Software)**

#### Step 4: Receive Benefit
- Amount is **directly transferred to your bank account** via DBT
- You receive an SMS notification

### Online Application

#### Step 1: Visit Portal
Go to [pmmvy.wcd.gov.in](https://pmmvy.wcd.gov.in)

#### Step 2: Register
- Click **"Beneficiary Login"**
- Register with your **mobile number**
- Verify via OTP

#### Step 3: Fill Details
- Enter personal details, pregnancy details, bank account
- Upload Aadhaar and other documents
- Submit the application

#### Step 4: Track Status
- Log in to the portal to **track your application status**
- You can also call the **PMMVY helpline**

## Processing Time

- **Application to 1st instalment:** 30-45 days after verification
- **2nd instalment:** 30-45 days after submitting delivery/birth documents
- **Second girl child benefit:** 30-45 days after birth registration submission

## Important Tips

1. **Register early** — you must register within 150 days of your last menstrual period. Don't delay.
2. **Get MCP card first** — register at your nearest Anganwadi or PHC to get the Mother and Child Protection Card.
3. **Ensure Aadhaar-linked bank account** — benefits are transferred only to Aadhaar-linked accounts in the mother's name.
4. **Complete vaccinations** — the second instalment requires proof of child's first vaccination cycle.
5. **ASHA/AWW are your helpers** — don't hesitate to ask your local ASHA or Anganwadi worker for assistance with forms and documents.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Is PMMVY available for the third child?
**No.** PMMVY is available only for the **first child** and **second child (if girl)**. Third and subsequent pregnancies are not covered.

### Q2: Can government employees apply?
**No.** Women who are **government employees, PSU employees**, or those receiving maternity benefits under any other law are not eligible for PMMVY.

### Q3: What if I had a miscarriage?
If you received the 1st instalment and subsequently had a miscarriage, you **can apply again** for a future pregnancy. The earlier benefit is not counted against your eligibility.

### Q4: Is there an income limit?
**No.** There is no income limit for PMMVY. All pregnant women (except government employees and those covered under other maternity benefit schemes) are eligible.

### Q5: Can I get the benefit if I deliver at home?
For the **1st instalment**, home delivery or institutional delivery — both are fine (it's for registration). However, the **2nd instalment** requires **institutional delivery** (hospital/health centre).

### Q6: What if I don't have an Aadhaar card?
Aadhaar is required for DBT. If you don't have Aadhaar, apply for one immediately at your nearest enrolment centre. See our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online). Some states may accept alternative ID temporarily.

### Q7: Can unmarried mothers apply?
**Yes.** PMMVY does not require marriage certificate. Unmarried mothers, single mothers, and widows are all eligible.
