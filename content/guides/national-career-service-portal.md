# National Career Service Portal: Register & Find Jobs Online

The National Career Service (NCS) portal is the official government platform by the Ministry of Labour & Employment for connecting job seekers with employers across India. Through **ncs.gov.in**, you can register for free, search for jobs, access career counselling, explore skill development courses, and connect with local employment exchanges — all in one place.

This guide covers registration, job search, and all services on the NCS portal.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent platform and is not affiliated with the Ministry of Labour & Employment or any government body.

## What is the National Career Service Portal?

NCS is a one-stop platform that brings together:

- **Job seekers** looking for government and private sector jobs
- **Employers** looking to post vacancies and recruit
- **Career counsellors** offering guidance
- **Skill providers** offering training and certification
- **Employment exchanges** now integrated digitally

The portal aggregates job listings from multiple sources including the National Job Portal, private job boards, and government vacancies.

## Who Can Use NCS?

- **Job Seekers:** Anyone looking for employment — freshers, experienced professionals, differently-abled persons
- **Employers:** Government departments, PSUs, private companies, startups
- **Career Counsellors:** Certified professionals offering career guidance
- **Skill Training Providers:** Institutes offering vocational and skill development courses

## Step-by-Step: Register as a Job Seeker

1. Visit [ncs.gov.in](https://www.ncs.gov.in/)
2. Click **"Register"** or **"Job Seeker Registration"**
3. Choose registration method: **Aadhaar, Email, or Mobile Number**
4. Enter your basic details:
   - Full name, date of birth, gender
   - Contact information (email and mobile)
   - Aadhaar number (optional but recommended)
5. Set a **password**
6. Verify via OTP sent to your mobile/email
7. After basic registration, **complete your profile**:
   - Educational qualifications
   - Skills and certifications
   - Work experience (if any)
   - Preferred job location, salary, and industry
   - Upload resume (PDF or DOC)
8. Your profile is now active and visible to employers

> **Tip:** Complete your profile 100% for better job matches and visibility to employers.

## Step-by-Step: Register as an Employer

1. Visit [ncs.gov.in](https://www.ncs.gov.in/)
2. Click **"Register"** > **"Employer"**
3. Enter organisation details:
   - Company name and type (private, government, PSU, etc.)
   - CIN/GSTIN or registration number
   - Industry and sector
   - Company address and contact details
4. Enter authorised person's details
5. Verify via email OTP
6. Complete the employer profile
7. After verification, you can **post job vacancies** for free

## How to Search and Apply for Jobs

1. Log in to [ncs.gov.in](https://www.ncs.gov.in/)
2. Use the **Job Search** bar on the homepage
3. Filter by:
   - **Keyword** (e.g., "accountant," "data entry")
   - **Location** (city, state, or "Work from Home")
   - **Industry/Sector**
   - **Experience level**
   - **Salary range**
   - **Government/Private**
4. Browse matching job listings
5. Click on a job to view full details (eligibility, salary, last date)
6. Click **"Apply"** to submit your application
7. Track application status from your dashboard

## Career Counselling on NCS

The NCS portal offers free career guidance:

1. Log in to your NCS account
2. Go to **"Career Counselling"** section
3. Browse available counsellors by specialisation and location
4. **Book a session** (online or in-person)
5. Get guidance on career paths, skill gaps, and job market trends

> Career counselling is especially useful for students, fresh graduates, and professionals looking to switch careers.

## Skill Development & Training

NCS integrates with skill development initiatives:

1. Go to **"Skill Providers"** section on NCS
2. Browse training courses by:
   - Industry (IT, Healthcare, Manufacturing, etc.)
   - Location
   - Duration
   - Certification type
3. Enrol in courses — many are free or subsidised under government schemes like PMKVY (Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana)
4. Complete training and add the certification to your NCS profile

## Local Employment Exchange Registration

NCS has digitised the traditional employment exchange system:

- If you were registered at a local employment exchange, your records may already be on NCS
- New registrations on NCS serve as digital employment exchange registration
- This is useful for government job applications that require employment exchange registration number

## Important Tips

1. **Complete your profile 100%** — incomplete profiles get fewer job matches and lower visibility
2. **Update your profile regularly** — add new skills, certifications, and experience as you gain them
3. **Enable job alerts** — get notified when new jobs matching your profile are posted
4. **Use the NCS mobile app** — apply for jobs on the go and get instant notifications
5. **Check for government job drives** — NCS regularly lists mega job fairs and government recruitment drives

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Is NCS a government website?
Yes, NCS (ncs.gov.in) is an official portal of the Ministry of Labour & Employment, Government of India. All services are free.

### Q2: Can I find private sector jobs on NCS?
Yes, NCS lists both government and private sector jobs. Many private companies and startups post vacancies on NCS.

### Q3: Is NCS registration equivalent to employment exchange registration?
Yes, NCS registration serves as digital employment exchange registration in most states. Check with your state's employment department for specific requirements.

### Q4: How do I get job alerts from NCS?
After registering, go to your profile settings and enable email/SMS job alerts. Set your preferred job criteria (location, industry, salary) to receive relevant notifications.

### Q5: Can differently-abled persons use NCS?
Yes, NCS has a dedicated section for differently-abled job seekers. Employers posting vacancies can also specify positions reserved for PwD candidates.

### Q6: Is there a mobile app for NCS?
Yes, the NCS mobile app is available on Google Play Store and Apple App Store. You can search jobs, apply, and track applications from the app.

### Q7: How many jobs are listed on NCS?
NCS aggregates lakhs of job listings from across India, including government vacancies, PSU jobs, and private sector openings. The numbers are updated regularly.
