## What is Soil Health Card Scheme?

**Soil Health Card (SHC) Scheme** is the Government of India's programme under the Ministry of Agriculture to provide every farmer a **free soil health card** — a printed report showing the nutrient status of their soil along with **specific fertilizer recommendations** for different crops.

The goal: Stop over-application or under-application of fertilizers, reduce input costs, and improve yields through precise nutrition management.

## What Information is on a Soil Health Card?

A Soil Health Card reports 12 soil parameters:

| Parameter | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **pH** | Soil acidity/alkalinity |
| **Electrical Conductivity (EC)** | Salinity level |
| **Organic Carbon (OC)** | Soil health and structure |
| **Nitrogen (N)** | Primary macronutrient |
| **Phosphorus (P)** | Secondary macronutrient |
| **Potassium (K)** | Third macronutrient |
| **Sulphur (S)** | Micronutrient |
| **Zinc (Zn)** | Micronutrient |
| **Iron (Fe)** | Micronutrient |
| **Copper (Cu)** | Micronutrient |
| **Manganese (Mn)** | Micronutrient |
| **Boron (B)** | Micronutrient |

Based on these, the card gives **crop-specific fertilizer recommendations** — which fertilizers to use, how much, and when.

## Who is Eligible?

- **All farmers** in India with agricultural land
- No income limit
- Soil testing is provided **free of cost** for all farmers

## How to Get a Soil Health Card

### Option 1: Through State Agriculture Department (Most Common)
- Soil collection is done by **soil testers** (trained VLW/agriculture staff) who come to your field
- Contact your **State Agriculture Department** or nearest **Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK)**
- Soil samples are collected from your field (100 grams from 6-inch depth)
- Tested at the nearest **Soil Testing Laboratory (STL)**
- Card issued within 2–4 weeks

### Option 2: Apply Online
1. Go to **[soilhealth.dac.gov.in](https://soilhealth.dac.gov.in)**
2. Click **"Apply for SHC"** (check if your state's online module is active)
3. Enter farmer details and land information
4. A field visit will be arranged for soil sample collection

### Option 3: At KVK / Agriculture Office
- Visit your nearest **Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK)** or Block Agriculture office
- Request soil testing
- Take your own soil sample in a plastic bag (follow the sample collection method)
- Submit for testing

## How to Read Your Soil Health Card

- **Green column** = Sufficient — no need to add this nutrient
- **Yellow column** = Medium — add moderate amounts
- **Red column** = Deficient — must add this nutrient

The card's fertilizer recommendation section tells exactly how many bags of urea/DAP/MOP/micronutrients to use per acre for your specific crop.

## Soil Health Card Validity

The Soil Health Card is valid for **3 years** — every 3 years, a fresh soil test should be done as soil nutrition changes with each crop cycle.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the soil test completely free?
Yes — the soil test and the Soil Health Card are provided **free of charge** to all farmers under the central scheme.

### My Soil Health Card shows zinc deficiency. What should I do?
Apply **zinc sulphate** as recommended on the card (typically 5–10 kg/acre for basal application). Your local agriculture officer or KVK can advise on the best product available in your area.

### How many soil samples can I get tested?
Typically one sample per 2.5 acres (1 hectare) of land. If your farm has multiple soil types (different plots), each plot ideally needs a separate sample.

### The recommendations on my card don't match what my dealer suggests. Whom to trust?
Trust the Soil Health Card — it's based on actual scientific testing of your soil. Dealer suggestions are sometimes driven by sales targets. The SHC recommendation will save you money and improve yield.
