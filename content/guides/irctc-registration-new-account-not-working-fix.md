# IRCTC New Account Registration Not Working? Fix Signup Errors

IRCTC (Indian Railway Catering and Tourism Corporation) requires a registered account to book train tickets online. Many users face errors during the registration process — from OTP failures to "mobile already registered" messages. This guide covers every common IRCTC signup error and how to fix it.

## Common IRCTC Registration Error Messages

- **"Mobile number already registered"** — your number is linked to an existing account
- **"Email ID already registered"** — your email is used in another IRCTC account
- **"OTP not received"** — verification OTP does not arrive on mobile or email
- **"Invalid Captcha"** — captcha validation keeps failing
- **"Error in saving data"** — server-side error during form submission
- **"Master list download failed"** — city/state dropdown not loading
- **"Age should be between 5 and 125"** — date of birth validation error
- **"Email verification failed"** — verification link expired or not working
- **"Service Unavailable"** — IRCTC server overloaded
- Registration page goes blank after clicking Submit

## Fix 1: Mobile Number Already Registered

If IRCTC says your mobile number is already registered:

1. **You may already have an account.** Try logging in at [irctc.co.in](https://www.irctc.co.in/) using your mobile number as the username
2. Click **"Forgot Username"** on the login page — IRCTC will send your existing username to your registered email
3. If you never created an account, someone may have used your number by mistake. Contact IRCTC Care:
   - Call **139** (IVRS) or **14646**
   - Email: **care@irctc.co.in**
   - Use the "Ask DISHA" chatbot on irctc.co.in
4. Request them to delink your mobile number from the old account
5. Once delinked, try registration again

> **Note:** Each mobile number can be linked to only one IRCTC account. This is a security requirement.

## Fix 2: Email Already Registered

1. Try password reset using the email — you may already have an account. See our [IRCTC password reset guide](/guide/irctc-forgot-password-reset-fix) for steps
2. If you don't remember creating an account, use a different email address for registration
3. Alternatively, contact care@irctc.co.in to delink the email from the old account

## Fix 3: Signup OTP Not Coming

If the verification OTP does not arrive on your mobile or email after registration:

1. **Wait at least 2-3 minutes** — OTPs can be delayed during peak hours (10 AM – 12 PM, 8 PM – 10 PM)
2. Check your **SMS inbox carefully** — the OTP comes from a short code, not a regular number
3. Check your **email spam/junk folder** for the email OTP
4. Make sure your **mobile number is correct** and has network signal
5. **DND (Do Not Disturb) may block OTPs:**
   - Send SMS **"STOP DND"** to **1909** from your mobile
   - Or call your telecom provider and ask to disable DND
   - Wait 24 hours after disabling DND, then retry
6. Click **"Resend OTP"** on the IRCTC registration page (you get 3 attempts)
7. If OTP still doesn't arrive, try again after 30 minutes or during off-peak hours

> **Tip:** IRCTC sends separate OTPs to mobile and email. You need to verify both to complete registration.

## Fix 4: Email Verification Failed

After registration, IRCTC sends a verification link to your email. If it's not working:

1. **Check spam/junk folder** — the email often lands there
2. The verification link **expires after 24 hours**. If expired, log in to IRCTC and request a new verification email from your profile settings
3. Make sure you're opening the link in the **same browser** where you registered
4. **Don't click the link on mobile** if it's not opening — try on a desktop browser
5. Copy-paste the link directly into the browser address bar instead of clicking it
6. Clear browser cache and cookies, then try the link again

## Fix 5: Age Validation Error

If you see "Age should be between 5 and 125" or date of birth errors:

1. Make sure you're entering the date in the **correct format** (DD/MM/YYYY)
2. The year must be realistic — check you haven't accidentally entered 2005 instead of 1995
3. **Minimum age for IRCTC registration is 5 years** (guardian details required for minors)
4. If the date picker is not working, try typing the date manually
5. Use Google Chrome or Mozilla Firefox — date pickers may not work properly in older browsers

## Fix 6: Master List Download Failed

This error occurs when IRCTC's city/state dropdown menus fail to load:

1. **Refresh the page** (press Ctrl + F5 for a hard refresh)
2. **Clear browser cache and cookies** completely
3. Try a **different browser** — Chrome, Firefox, or Edge
4. **Disable ad blockers and VPN** — they can interfere with IRCTC's data loading
5. Check your internet connection — master list requires a stable connection to download
6. Try during **off-peak hours** (early morning 6-8 AM or late night after 11 PM)
7. If on mobile browser, switch to the **IRCTC Rail Connect app** or try on a desktop

## Fix 7: "Error in Saving Data" or Server Errors

1. **Don't click Submit multiple times** — wait for the page to respond
2. Try during off-peak hours when IRCTC servers are less loaded
3. Clear browser cache and try in an **incognito/private window**
4. Disable browser extensions that might interfere
5. If the error persists, try the **IRCTC Rail Connect app** instead of the website
6. Check [IRCTC's Twitter handle @IRCTCofficial](https://twitter.com/IRCTCofficial) for any announced maintenance

## Fix 8: Registration Page Not Loading or Going Blank

1. **Check your internet connection** — try loading other websites
2. Clear browser cache: Press **Ctrl + Shift + Delete** → select "All time" → clear
3. Try an **incognito/private browsing window**
4. Disable all browser extensions temporarily
5. Try a **different browser** or device
6. IRCTC may be under maintenance — try again after 30 minutes

## Step-by-Step: How to Register on IRCTC Successfully

Follow these steps for a smooth registration:

1. Go to [www.irctc.co.in](https://www.irctc.co.in/) and click **"Register"** (or **"Sign Up"**)
2. Fill in your **username** (create a unique ID, 3-35 characters)
3. Enter a **strong password** (8-15 characters, must include uppercase, lowercase, number, and special character)
4. Enter your **Security Question and Answer** (remember this for password recovery)
5. Fill in **personal details**: First Name, Last Name, Gender, Date of Birth, Marital Status, Occupation
6. Enter your **residential address** with correct PIN code
7. Enter your **mobile number** and **email ID** (both must be unique/not already registered)
8. Fill in the **Captcha** correctly
9. Click **"Register"**
10. Enter the **OTP sent to your mobile** and click verify
11. Enter the **OTP sent to your email** and click verify
12. Registration is complete — you can now log in and book tickets

## Important Tips

- Use **Google Chrome** (latest version) for the smoothest experience
- Register during **off-peak hours** (6-8 AM or after 11 PM) to avoid server issues
- Keep your **mobile phone nearby** during registration for OTP verification
- **Screenshot your username** after registration — many users forget it later
- Each person should have only **one IRCTC account** — creating multiple accounts may get all of them blocked

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Can I create multiple IRCTC accounts with the same mobile number?
No. IRCTC allows only one account per mobile number. If your number is already linked to another account, you'll need to delink it first by contacting IRCTC customer care.

### Q2: How long does IRCTC email verification take?
The verification email is sent instantly, but during peak hours it may take up to 30 minutes. Check your spam folder. The verification link expires after 24 hours.

### Q3: I completed registration but can't log in. What should I do?
Make sure you've verified both your mobile OTP and email. If either is unverified, your account won't be fully active. Log in and check if it prompts you to verify again.

### Q4: What is the minimum age for IRCTC registration?
The minimum age is 5 years. For users under 18, guardian details are required during registration.

### Q5: Can I change my mobile number or email after registration?
Yes. After logging in, go to **My Profile → Update Profile** to change your registered mobile number or email. You'll need to verify the new number/email with an OTP.

### Q6: IRCTC registration works but I can't book tickets — why?
New accounts may need to complete email and mobile verification before booking is enabled. Also, make sure your profile is fully filled including address details.

### Q7: What browsers work best for IRCTC registration?
Google Chrome (latest version) and Mozilla Firefox work best. Avoid using Internet Explorer or very old browser versions.
