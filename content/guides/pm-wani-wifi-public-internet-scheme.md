## What is the PM WANI Scheme?

**PM WANI (Wi-Fi Access Network Interface)** is a Government of India scheme to create a nationwide network of **public Wi-Fi hotspots** through small shopkeepers and entrepreneurs called **Public Data Offices (PDOs)**. The scheme allows anyone to become a Wi-Fi hotspot provider without a licence and provides **affordable or free public internet access** across India.

Launched by the Department of Telecommunications (DoT) in December 2020, PM WANI is India's answer to making broadband internet access ubiquitous — especially in rural and semi-urban areas.

| Who | Benefit |

| Small entrepreneurs | Become a PDO — earn income by sharing Wi-Fi |
| Rural areas | Internet access without SIM or mobile data |

## How PM WANI Works

The scheme has three components:

| Component | Role |
|-----------|-----|
| **PDO (Public Data Office)** | Local shopkeeper/entrepreneur who sets up a Wi-Fi hotspot |
| **PDOA (PDO Aggregator)** | Company that manages PDOs, handles billing and authentication |
| **APP Provider** | Provides the app through which users connect to hotspots |
| **Central Registry** | Maintained by C-DoT — registers all PDOs and PDOAs |

**For citizens:** Connect to any PM WANI hotspot near you using a WANI-compliant app.

## How to Use PM WANI as a User

### Step 1 — Download a PM WANI App

Download any PM WANI-compliant app from Play Store. Popular options:
- **Airtel Wi-Fi App** (WANI-compliant)
- **BSNL Wi-Fi App**
- **Vi (Vodafone Idea) Wi-Fi**
- Several regional PDOA apps

### Step 2 — Register with Your Mobile Number

Register in the app with your mobile number — OTP verification (no other registration needed).

### Step 3 — Find a Nearby Hotspot

The app shows nearby PM WANI hotspots. Look for the PM WANI logo or ask at local shops.

### Step 4 — Connect and Browse

Select the hotspot, authenticate via the app, and browse the internet. Pricing is set by the PDOA — typically:
- ₹1–₹5 per hour, OR
- Prepaid data vouchers (₹5 for 100MB, etc.)
- Some hotspots offer **free access** for educational platforms (SWAYAM, DIKSHA)

## How to Become a PM WANI PDO (Earn Income)

Any shopkeeper, kirana store owner, or entrepreneur can become a PDO:

### Step 1 — Contact a PDOA

Find a registered PDOA (Public Data Office Aggregator) in your area. Examples:
- BSNL, Airtel, Quadrant, Ciena, Saankhya Labs, and many others

### Step 2 — Get Wi-Fi Equipment

The PDOA provides a router/access point. You need an existing broadband connection (BSNL/private ISP).

### Step 3 — Registration

The PDOA registers you as a PDO on the Central Registry (no separate government licence needed — it's licence-exempt).

### Step 4 — Start Earning

You earn a share of the revenue from each user who buys data at your hotspot. Earnings vary by PDOA partnership — typically ₹3,000–₹10,000/month for active hotspots in busy areas.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. Is PM WANI completely free for users?**

Some hotspots may offer free access (for certain content or limited data). Most PM WANI hotspots charge a nominal amount (₹1–₹5/hour). It is much cheaper than mobile data for heavy users.

**2. Where are PM WANI hotspots available?**

PM WANI hotspots are found at local shops, panchayat offices, CSCs, schools, and public places across India. Open any WANI-compliant app to see nearby hotspot locations.

**3. Do I need Aadhaar to use PM WANI?**

No. You only need a mobile number for OTP authentication. No Aadhaar or other ID is required by the user.

**4. I have a kirana shop and broadband. How do I become a PDO?**

Contact any registered PDOA — BSNL has a simple process for kirana stores. They provide the access point hardware and register you as a PDO within a few days.

**5. Is PM WANI different from BharatNet?**

Yes. **BharatNet** lays optical fibre from blocks to gram panchayats (rural backhaul). **PM WANI** is the last-mile distribution — it creates Wi-Fi hotspots from the existing broadband (including BharatNet connections) to reach individual users.
