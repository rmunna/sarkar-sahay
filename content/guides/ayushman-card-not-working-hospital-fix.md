# Ayushman Card Not Working at Hospital — How to Fix

You arrive at a hospital with your Ayushman Bharat (PMJAY) card and the hospital says it's "not in the system," expired, or simply refuses to accept it. This is frustrating, especially during a medical emergency. This guide covers every common problem and how to fix it quickly.

## Common Error Messages and Problems

When your Ayushman card doesn't work at a hospital, you may hear or see:

- **"Beneficiary not found in system"** — your PMJAY ID doesn't show up in the hospital's portal
- **"Card expired / inactive"** — the card status is not active
- **"Aadhaar mismatch"** — name or details on the card don't match Aadhaar
- **"Hospital not empanelled under PMJAY"** — the hospital is not part of the Ayushman scheme
- **"Treatment not covered"** — the procedure you need is not in the PMJAY package list
- **"Family ID exhausted"** — the annual ₹5 lakh limit has been used up
- **"eKYC failed"** — biometric or OTP verification failed at the hospital

---

## Why Your Ayushman Card May Not Work

### 1. Beneficiary Not Found in System

- Your name was not included in the SECC 2011 database or state-specific beneficiary list
- Data entry error during card creation — wrong name, age, or address
- Your family was removed from the eligible list during periodic data cleaning

### 2. PMJAY ID or Aadhaar Mismatch

- Name on Ayushman card doesn't match your Aadhaar exactly
- Aadhaar number linked to the card is wrong or outdated
- You updated your Aadhaar details after creating the Ayushman card

### 3. Card Expired or Inactive

- Old Ayushman cards issued before the digital transition may need renewal
- Card was deactivated due to suspected fraud or duplicate entries
- eKYC not completed — cards without Aadhaar-based eKYC may be marked inactive

### 4. Hospital Not Empanelled

- The hospital was empanelled earlier but its empanelment has been suspended or expired
- The hospital is private and was never part of PMJAY
- The specific department or treatment is not covered under the hospital's empanelment

### 5. Treatment Not Covered

PMJAY covers 1,929+ treatment packages but does not cover:
- OPD (outpatient) consultations
- Cosmetic procedures
- Fertility treatments
- Organ transplants (some exceptions apply)

## Step-by-Step Fixes

### Fix 1 — Verify Your Eligibility Online

1. Go to [beneficiary.nha.gov.in](https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in/) or [pmjay.gov.in](https://pmjay.gov.in/)
2. Click **"Am I Eligible"**
3. Enter your mobile number and complete OTP verification
4. Search by name, ration card number, or PMJAY ID
5. If your name appears with status **"Active"**, your card should work — the issue is at the hospital's end

### Fix 2 — Check if the Hospital Is Empanelled

1. Visit [pmjay.gov.in](https://pmjay.gov.in/) → **"Find Hospital"**
2. Select your state, district, and hospital type (public/private)
3. Search for the specific hospital
4. If the hospital is not listed, it is not empanelled — you need to go to a different hospital

### Fix 3 — Fix Aadhaar/Name Mismatch

1. Visit the nearest **Ayushman Arogya Mandir (AAM)** or Common Service Centre (CSC)
2. Carry your Aadhaar card and Ayushman card
3. Request a correction in your PMJAY beneficiary details
4. Complete Aadhaar-based eKYC to update your records
5. The correction typically takes 3-7 working days

### Fix 4 — Reactivate an Inactive Card

1. Visit your nearest Ayushman Arogya Mandir or district PMJAY office
2. Carry Aadhaar card, ration card, and any old Ayushman card
3. Request card reactivation and complete eKYC
4. If your name was removed from the beneficiary list, you may need to apply fresh under your state's extended scheme

### Fix 5 — What to Do in an Emergency

If your card is not working and you need immediate treatment:

1. **Ask the hospital to call the PMJAY helpline (14555)** — they can verify your details in real-time
2. **Request provisional treatment** — empanelled hospitals are required to provide emergency care and sort out verification later
3. **Pay upfront and claim reimbursement** — if the hospital refuses, pay for the treatment and file a reimbursement claim through the grievance portal

## How to File a Grievance

If a hospital refuses your valid Ayushman card or you face any issues:

1. Visit [grievance.nha.gov.in](https://grievance.nha.gov.in/)
2. Register with your mobile number
3. Select the grievance type (card not working, hospital refusal, etc.)
4. Upload supporting documents (Ayushman card, hospital bills, Aadhaar)
5. Submit and note the grievance ID
6. Track resolution status on the same portal

You can also:
- **Call 14555** (PMJAY toll-free helpline, available 24x7)
- **WhatsApp:** Send "Hi" to the NHA WhatsApp helpline (check pmjay.gov.in for current number)

---

## When to Contact the Helpline

Contact the PMJAY helpline (14555) immediately if:

- The hospital is empanelled but refuses to honour your active card
- You need emergency treatment and the card verification is failing
- Your grievance has been pending for more than 30 days
- You suspect the hospital is demanding money despite being an empanelled PMJAY hospital
- Your card was deactivated without any reason

## FAQs

### Why does the hospital say my Ayushman card is "not in the system"?

Your beneficiary data may not have been synced to the hospital's portal, or there is a name/Aadhaar mismatch. Verify your eligibility at [beneficiary.nha.gov.in](https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in/) first. If you're eligible, ask the hospital to contact the PMJAY helpline at 14555.

### Can I use my Ayushman card at any hospital?

No. You can only use it at hospitals empanelled under PMJAY. Check the list at [pmjay.gov.in](https://pmjay.gov.in/) → "Find Hospital." Both government and select private hospitals are covered.

### My Ayushman card shows "inactive." How do I reactivate it?

Visit the nearest Ayushman Arogya Mandir or Common Service Centre with your Aadhaar and ration card. Complete eKYC verification to reactivate the card. This usually takes 3-7 working days.

### What is the annual limit under Ayushman Bharat?

PMJAY provides coverage up to ₹5 lakh per family per year. This covers hospitalisation, pre and post-hospitalisation expenses, and listed surgical procedures. If the ₹5 lakh limit is exhausted, you cannot use the card until the next policy year.

### Can I get reimbursement if I paid out of pocket at an empanelled hospital?

Yes. File a grievance at [grievance.nha.gov.in](https://grievance.nha.gov.in/) with your hospital bills, discharge summary, and Ayushman card details. The NHA will investigate and process reimbursement if the claim is valid.

### Does Ayushman Bharat cover OPD consultations?

No. PMJAY only covers hospitalisation (inpatient treatment). OPD consultations, medicines purchased outside the hospital, and diagnostic tests without hospitalisation are not covered.

### How do I check if a specific treatment is covered under PMJAY?

Visit [pmjay.gov.in](https://pmjay.gov.in/) and search the Health Benefit Packages (HBP) list. You can also call 14555 and ask whether your specific procedure is covered.
