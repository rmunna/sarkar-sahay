## What is CG Bhuiya (Chhattisgarh Land Records)?

**Bhuiya** (भुइयां) is the official online land records portal of the Chhattisgarh government. It provides digital access to all land revenue records including ownership details (B1/Khatoni), plot information (P2/Khasra), and mutation records. The portal is managed by the Revenue Department, Government of Chhattisgarh.

Key records available on Bhuiya:

- **B1 (Khatoni):** Ownership register showing landowner details, area, and land classification
- **P2 (Khasra):** Plot-wise register showing crop details, cultivation, and possession
- **Digital Nakal:** Certified digital copy of land records
- **Mutation (Namantaran):** Record of ownership changes
- **Abhilekh (Record):** Historical land records and documents

## Who Needs CG Land Records?

- **Property buyers and sellers** — for ownership verification before transactions
- **Farmers** — for crop loan, PM Kisan, and government scheme registration
- **Banks** — for mortgage and agricultural loan processing
- **Legal matters** — for court cases involving land disputes
- **Government applications** — for caste certificates, income certificates, and domicile linked to land

## How to Check B1 (Khatoni) — Ownership Record Online

### Step 1: Visit the Bhuiya Portal

Go to [bhuiyan.cg.nic.in](https://bhuiyan.cg.nic.in).

### Step 2: Click on "B1/Khatoni"

From the main menu or homepage, select the **"B1/Khatoni"** option.

### Step 3: Select Location

Choose from the dropdown menus:
1. **District** (e.g., Raipur, Bilaspur, Durg)
2. **Tehsil**
3. **RI Circle** (Revenue Inspector Circle)
4. **Village**

### Step 4: Search by Name or Number

Choose your search method:
- **By Khasra Number** — enter the plot number
- **By Name** — enter the landowner's name
- **By Khata Number** — enter the khata (account) number

### Step 5: View B1 Record

The B1 record displays:
- Owner name and father's name
- Khata number
- Khasra numbers with area (hectare/acre)
- Land type (irrigated, unirrigated, residential, etc.)
- Ownership share details

## How to Check P2 (Khasra) — Plot Details Online

1. Visit [bhuiyan.cg.nic.in](https://bhuiyan.cg.nic.in)
2. Click on **"P2/Khasra"**
3. Select **District → Tehsil → RI Circle → Village**
4. Enter the **Khasra Number**
5. The P2 record shows:
   - Plot area and dimensions
   - Crop/cultivation details (Kharif, Rabi)
   - Name of cultivator/possessor
   - Land classification and soil type
   - Irrigation source

## How to Get Digital Nakal (Certified Copy)

### Online Method
1. Visit [bhuiyan.cg.nic.in](https://bhuiyan.cg.nic.in)
2. Click on **"Digital Nakal"** or **"Digitised Copy"**
3. Select your district, tehsil, and village
4. Enter khasra or khata number
5. The digitally signed nakal can be downloaded and is valid for official purposes

### Offline Method
1. Visit the **Tehsil Office** or **Janpad Panchayat** office
2. Submit an application with land details
3. Pay the prescribed fee
4. Collect the certified nakal

## How to Check Mutation (Namantaran) Status

1. Visit [bhuiyan.cg.nic.in](https://bhuiyan.cg.nic.in)
2. Click on **"Namantaran"** (mutation)
3. Select your district and tehsil
4. Search by application number or date range
5. View the mutation status: pending, approved, or rejected
6. Details include type of transfer, parties involved, and sanction date

## Understanding CG Land Record Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| B1 (Khatoni) | Ownership register — who owns the land |
| P2 (Khasra) | Plot register — details of each plot |
| Khata | Account grouping all plots of one owner |
| Khasra | Individual plot/survey number |
| Rakba | Area of land |
| Namantaran | Mutation/transfer of ownership |
| Patwari | Village-level revenue official |
| RI Circle | Revenue Inspector's jurisdiction |

## Important Tips

1. **Digital nakal from Bhuiya portal with digital signature is legally valid** — check for the QR code and signature
2. **Cross-verify khasra details** in both B1 and P2 registers before any property transaction
3. **Check mutation status regularly** after applying — delays can be reported to the SDM office
4. **Use the CG e-District portal** for related services like income and caste certificates
5. **Report data errors** to the concerned patwari or tehsildar with supporting documents

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Is the Bhuiya portal available in Hindi?

Yes, the Bhuiya portal is fully available in Hindi. All records and the interface are in Hindi, as Chhattisgarh's official language is Hindi.

### Q2: Can I download land records from Bhuiya?

Yes, you can view and download B1 and P2 records. Digital nakal (certified copies) with digital signatures are available for download and are legally valid.

### Q3: How do I apply for mutation in Chhattisgarh?

Submit a mutation application at your tehsil office along with supporting documents (sale deed, succession certificate, etc.). You can track the status online on the Bhuiya portal.

### Q4: What if my land record shows wrong ownership?

File a correction application at the tehsil office with supporting documents. You can also approach the Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) if the tehsildar does not resolve the issue.

### Q5: Are all villages of Chhattisgarh covered on Bhuiya?

Yes, the Bhuiya portal covers all districts and villages of Chhattisgarh. Records have been digitised for the entire state.

### Q6: How do I find my Khasra number?

If you don't know your khasra number, search by owner name on the Bhuiya portal. You can also check your previous land documents or ask your village patwari.

### Q7: Can I verify if a property is mortgaged?

The B1 record shows encumbrances and mortgage details. Check the "Bhar" (burden) column in the khatoni for any registered mortgages or liens.
