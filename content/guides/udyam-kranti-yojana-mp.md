# Mukhyamantri Udyam Kranti Yojana MP: Loan, Interest Subsidy & How to Apply

The Mukhyamantri Udyam Kranti Yojana is a flagship entrepreneurship promotion scheme of the Government of Madhya Pradesh. It provides bank loans of up to **Rs 50 lakh** with **interest subsidy** and **loan guarantee** to help unemployed youth start their own manufacturing or service businesses.

---

## What is Udyam Kranti Yojana?

Launched by the MP government to reduce unemployment and promote self-employment, the Mukhyamantri Udyam Kranti Yojana enables eligible youth to set up micro and small enterprises. The government provides:

- **Interest subsidy** of 3% on the bank loan for 7 years
- **Loan guarantee fee** borne by the state government
- Loans through nationalised banks and scheduled commercial banks

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME), Government of Madhya Pradesh.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Age:** 18 to 40 years
2. **Education:** Minimum Class 12 pass
3. **Residency:** Permanent resident of Madhya Pradesh
4. **Employment:** Must be unemployed (not employed in any government or private organisation)
5. **Income:** Family annual income should not exceed Rs 12,00,000
6. **Business type:** New enterprise (not for existing businesses)
7. **Not availed before:** Should not have availed benefits from similar state/central schemes
8. **No defaulter:** Should not be a defaulter of any bank or financial institution

## How to Apply: Step-by-Step Process

### Online Application

1. Visit the SAMAST portal at [samast.mponline.gov.in](https://samast.mponline.gov.in/)
2. Register with your mobile number and create an account
3. Login and select **"Mukhyamantri Udyam Kranti Yojana"**
4. Fill in personal details, educational qualifications, and business plan
5. Upload all required documents including project report
6. Select the bank branch where you wish to apply for the loan
7. Submit the application
8. Note the application number for tracking

### After Application Submission

1. The application is verified by the MSME department
2. If eligible, the application is forwarded to the selected bank
3. Bank evaluates the project and processes the loan
4. After loan sanction, the interest subsidy is directly handled by the government
5. Loan guarantee fee is paid by the state government to the guarantee agency

---

## Processing Time

- **Application verification:** 15-30 days
- **Bank processing:** 30-60 days
- **Total (application to loan disbursement):** 60-90 days

---

## Interest Subsidy Details

- The government pays **3% interest subsidy** directly to the bank on behalf of the borrower
- Subsidy is available for **7 years** from the date of loan disbursement
- The borrower pays the remaining interest rate to the bank
- Subsidy continues only if the borrower maintains regular repayment

## Important Tips

1. **Prepare a solid project report** — Banks evaluate the business viability; a detailed project report increases approval chances
2. **Choose the right sector** — Manufacturing projects get higher loan limits (Rs 50 lakh vs Rs 25 lakh for services)
3. **Maintain repayment discipline** — Default will result in loss of interest subsidy and guarantee benefits
4. **Apply early** — Funds and targets are allocated annually; early applicants have better chances
5. **Get MSME registration** — Register your enterprise on the Udyam portal after starting the business

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. What is the maximum loan amount under Udyam Kranti Yojana?
Rs 50 lakh for manufacturing units and Rs 25 lakh for service sector units.

### 2. Is there any margin money requirement?
The margin money requirement depends on the bank's lending policy. The government provides interest subsidy and guarantee, not direct margin money.

### 3. Can women entrepreneurs apply?
Yes, the scheme is open to both men and women. Women entrepreneurs are encouraged to apply.

### 4. What is the interest subsidy?
The government pays 3% of the loan interest directly to the bank for 7 years.

### 5. Can I apply for an existing business?
No, the scheme is only for setting up new enterprises. Existing businesses are not eligible.

### 6. Is there any application fee?
There may be a nominal processing fee on the SAMAST portal. The government scheme itself has no application fee.

### 7. What happens if I default on the loan?
If you default, the interest subsidy will stop and the guarantee agency may recover the amount. Your credit score will also be affected.
