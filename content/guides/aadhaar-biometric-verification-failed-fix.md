# Aadhaar Biometric Verification Failed: How to Fix

Aadhaar biometric verification is used across India for SIM card activation, bank account opening, PF withdrawals, ration distribution, and many other services. When your fingerprint or iris scan fails during authentication, it can block critical transactions. This guide covers all common causes and step-by-step fixes for Aadhaar biometric verification failure.

## Common Error Messages and Symptoms

You may encounter these errors during biometric authentication:

- **"Biometric authentication failed"** — generic failure at POS devices
- **"Fingerprint mismatch — please try again"** — fingerprint does not match UIDAI records
- **"Error code 330 — Biometric data did not match"** — returned by UIDAI API
- **"Error code 996 — Biometrics locked by resident"** — you have locked your biometrics
- **"Iris scan failed"** — iris authentication unsuccessful
- **"Technical error — please try after some time"** — server or device issue
- Authentication times out without any result
- Repeated failures across multiple attempts at the same centre

## Why Aadhaar Biometric Verification Fails

### 1. Worn or Damaged Fingerprints

Manual labourers, elderly persons, and people with skin conditions often have faded fingerprints. This is the most common cause of biometric failure.

### 2. Biometrics Are Locked

If you have enabled [Aadhaar biometric lock](/guide/aadhaar-biometric-lock-unlock) for security, all biometric authentications will be rejected until you unlock them.

### 3. Dirty or Wet Fingers

Moisture, dust, oil, or ink on your fingers can prevent the scanner from reading your prints accurately.

### 4. Poor Quality Scanner

Old or poorly maintained biometric devices at shops and centres may not capture prints properly.

### 5. Outdated Biometric Data in UIDAI Records

If your fingerprints have changed significantly since your last Aadhaar enrolment (due to ageing, injury, or skin treatment), the stored biometric data may no longer match.

### 6. Server or Network Issues

UIDAI authentication servers occasionally face downtime, especially during peak hours.

### 7. Operator Error

The person operating the biometric device may not be placing your finger correctly or may be using the wrong authentication mode.

## Step-by-Step Troubleshooting

### Step 1: Check if Your Biometrics Are Locked

Visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/) and check the biometric lock status:

1. Log in with your Aadhaar number and OTP
2. Go to **My Aadhaar** → **Aadhaar Services**
3. Check **Biometric Lock/Unlock** status
4. If locked, **unlock** your biometrics temporarily for authentication

You can also use the **mAadhaar app** to quickly toggle the biometric lock.

### Step 2: Clean and Dry Your Fingers

Before the next attempt:

- Wash your hands with soap and water
- Dry them thoroughly with a clean cloth
- Avoid applying cream, oil, or sanitizer before scanning
- Wipe the scanner surface with a dry cloth

### Step 3: Try Different Fingers

UIDAI stores all 10 fingerprints. If one finger fails:

- Ask the operator to try your **thumb** (usually has the deepest ridges)
- Try fingers from your **other hand**
- Some devices allow selecting which finger to scan

### Step 4: Request Iris Authentication

If fingerprint keeps failing, ask the operator to switch to **iris-based authentication**. Most modern Aadhaar authentication devices support iris scanning. This works well when fingerprints are worn.

### Step 5: Try at a Different Centre or Device

If the device at one location seems faulty:

- Visit a different bank branch, Aadhaar centre, or authorized agency
- Government offices and banks typically have better-maintained devices

### Step 6: Update Your Biometrics at an Aadhaar Centre

If your fingerprints have changed due to ageing or injury:

1. Visit the nearest **Aadhaar Enrolment/Update Centre** — find one at [appointments.uidai.gov.in](https://appointments.uidai.gov.in/)
2. Request **biometric update** (fingerprints + iris)
3. Carry a valid ID proof (any one: PAN, passport, voter ID, driving licence)
4. Pay the update fee of ₹100 (as per current UIDAI charges)
5. Collect the acknowledgement slip — update takes 10–30 days

### Step 7: Try During Off-Peak Hours

UIDAI servers experience high load during morning hours (10 AM – 1 PM). Try authenticating:

- Early morning (before 10 AM)
- After 4 PM
- Avoid month-end dates when PF/ration distributions peak

### Step 8: Use OTP-Based Authentication as Fallback

Many services (bank KYC, SIM activation, PF claims) also accept **Aadhaar OTP authentication** as an alternative to biometrics. Request the operator to use OTP mode — an OTP will be sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile number.

## When to Contact UIDAI Helpline

Contact UIDAI support if:

- Biometric authentication fails consistently across multiple centres and devices
- Your biometric update request has been pending for over 30 days
- You suspect someone has tampered with your Aadhaar biometric data
- You cannot unlock your biometrics through the portal

**UIDAI Helpline:**
- **Toll-free number:** 1947
- **Email:** help@uidai.gov.in
- **Online grievance:** [uidai.gov.in/en/contact-support.html](https://uidai.gov.in/en/contact-support.html)

## Prevention Tips

1. **Update biometrics every 10 years** — UIDAI recommends periodic biometric updates, especially for children and elderly
2. **Use biometric lock** — keep your [biometrics locked](/guide/aadhaar-biometric-lock-unlock) when not in use and unlock only when needed
3. **Keep your mobile number updated** in Aadhaar for OTP fallback — see [how to update mobile in Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-update-mobile-email)
4. **Moisturize your hands** regularly if you do manual labour — this preserves fingerprint ridges
5. **Download mAadhaar app** — it lets you generate Virtual ID and toggle biometric lock instantly
6. **Avoid injuring fingertips** before important transactions requiring Aadhaar authentication

| Error Code | Meaning | Quick Fix |
|---|---|---|

| 996 | Biometrics locked by resident | Unlock via myAadhaar or mAadhaar |

| 999 | Unknown/server error | Retry after some time |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why does my Aadhaar fingerprint keep failing?

The most common reasons are worn fingerprints (especially in manual workers and elderly), locked biometrics, dirty fingers, or a faulty scanner device. Try cleaning your fingers, using a different finger, or switching to iris/OTP authentication.

### Can I use OTP instead of biometric for Aadhaar verification?

Yes, many services accept Aadhaar OTP authentication. The OTP is sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile number. Ask the operator to switch to OTP mode if biometric fails.

### How much does Aadhaar biometric update cost?

UIDAI charges ₹100 for biometric update (fingerprints and iris) at any Aadhaar enrolment centre. You need to carry one valid ID proof.

### How long does biometric update take to reflect?

After submitting a biometric update at an Aadhaar centre, it typically takes 10–30 days for the new biometrics to be updated in UIDAI's system.

### What if iris scan also fails?

If both fingerprint and iris scans fail, use OTP-based authentication for the current transaction. Then visit an Aadhaar enrolment centre to get your biometrics updated with fresh captures.

### Can someone misuse my Aadhaar biometrics?

To prevent misuse, always keep your [biometrics locked](/guide/aadhaar-biometric-lock-unlock) and unlock only when you need authentication. This way, even if someone has access to a biometric device, they cannot authenticate using your Aadhaar.
