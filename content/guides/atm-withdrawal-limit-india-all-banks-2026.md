# ATM Withdrawal Limit India 2026 — Daily Cash Limit All Major Banks

Every bank sets a **daily ATM withdrawal limit** based on your debit card type and account. Knowing this limit prevents failed transactions at ATMs. Banks also restrict **free ATM transactions** per month before charging fees.

> **Note:** ATM limits can be changed by banks periodically. The figures below are indicative as of May 2026. Verify the exact limit for your specific debit card type in your bank's app, net banking, or by calling customer care.

## Free ATM Transactions Per Month — RBI Rules

The RBI mandates minimum free ATM transactions for all savings account holders:

| ATM Type | Metro Cities (6 major cities) | Other Cities/Towns |
|----------|------------------------------|-------------------|
| Own bank ATM | **5 free transactions/month** | **5 free transactions/month** |
| Other bank ATM | **3 free transactions/month** | **5 free transactions/month** |

**6 Metro cities for this rule:** Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai, Kolkata, Bengaluru, Hyderabad

> **RBI mandate:** These are the minimum free transactions. Some banks (like Kotak 811) may offer more free transactions as part of their savings account benefits.

## ATM Charges After Free Limit (Effective Jan 2022)

After exhausting free transactions, each transaction costs:

| Transaction Type | Charge per Transaction |
|-----------------|----------------------|
| Financial transaction (cash/balance) | ₹21 + GST = **₹21 + applicable GST** |
| Non-financial (mini-statement, PIN change) | ₹21 + GST (at other bank ATMs) |

> **RBI update:** From January 2022, the per-transaction charge after free limit was revised to ₹21 per transaction (up from ₹20). Banks may charge more than ₹21 (but not less) for additional transactions.

## How to Check Your Current ATM Limit

1. **Bank Mobile App** — Go to Cards → Card Details → Daily limits shown
2. **Net Banking** — Account/Card → Transaction limits
3. **SMS** — Most banks respond with limits via SMS to registered mobile
4. **Customer Care** — Call and ask for your card's daily ATM withdrawal limit

## How to Increase ATM Withdrawal Limit

Most banks allow temporary or permanent limit increases:

### SBI:
1. YONO app → Cards → Debit Card Limit
2. Or: SBI Net Banking → e-Services → ATM Card Services → Change Limit
3. Or: SMS: `LIMIT <last 4 digits of card> <new limit>` to 567676

### HDFC Bank:
1. HDFC Mobile Banking → Cards → Manage Card → ATM Daily Limit
2. Select new limit (up to card's maximum)

### ICICI Bank:
1. iMobile → Cards → Manage → Change ATM limit
2. OTP verification → Select new daily limit

### Axis Bank:
1. Axis Mobile → Cards → Card Controls → ATM Limit
2. Increase up to your card type's maximum

## ATM Limits for Specific Scenarios

**₹10,000 salary account (SBI) — what's the ATM limit?**
Typically ₹20,000/day (standard Classic card). You can withdraw more in multiple days.

**Can I withdraw ₹50,000 from ATM in one go?**
Only if your card's daily limit is ₹50,000 or above. Additionally, physical ATM notes are typically dispensed in ₹100, ₹200, ₹500, ₹2,000 denominations. Most ATMs have cassette limits — individual transactions may be capped at ₹10,000–₹20,000 per transaction (multiple transactions in a day are allowed up to daily limit).

**What happens if ATM cash runs out mid-transaction?**
Transaction fails. No debit unless the amount was dispensed (some machines partially dispense). Use another ATM. Disputed transactions are resolved within 7 working days by RBI rule.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the maximum ATM withdrawal per day in India?**
For most premium debit cards (SBI Platinum, HDFC Platinum, ICICI Platinum), the maximum is **₹1,00,000 per day**. Standard/Classic cards are usually ₹20,000–₹25,000.

**Can I withdraw cash more than once from ATM on the same day?**
Yes — you can make multiple ATM withdrawals in a day as long as your total doesn't exceed your daily limit. Free transaction limit counts each separate ATM visit.

**My bank charges ₹23 per ATM transaction — is this correct?**
Yes. Banks charge ₹21 + 18% GST = ₹24.78 (rounded to ₹23–₹25) per transaction after free limit. This is within RBI guidelines.

**Which bank gives the most free ATM transactions?**
All banks are RBI-mandated to give at least 5 free transactions at own ATMs and 3 free at other bank ATMs in metros. Some premium account types offer unlimited free transactions at own ATMs.

**My Kotak 811 debit card ATM withdrawal limit?**
Kotak 811 virtual card cannot be used at ATMs. You need a physical Kotak debit card (issued after Video KYC) for ATM withdrawals. The physical card typically has a ₹25,000 daily limit, upgradeable via the Kotak app.
