# SIM Card Lost? How to Block & Get Replacement

If your SIM card is lost or stolen, you must **block it immediately** to prevent misuse. After blocking, you can get a **replacement SIM** (duplicate SIM) with the same mobile number. This guide covers the blocking and replacement process for all major Indian operators.

## Why Block Your SIM Immediately?

- Prevent **unauthorized calls and messages** from your number
- Protect **banking apps, UPI, and OTP-based services** linked to your number
- Avoid **financial fraud** — someone could receive OTPs for your bank accounts
- Prevent **identity misuse** — your number is linked to Aadhaar, PAN, and other services

## How to Block Your SIM Card

### Method 1: Call Customer Care (Fastest)

Call from any phone (friend's, family, landline):

| Operator | Customer Care Number | Alternate Number |
|----------|---------------------|-----------------|
| Jio | 198 (from Jio) | 1800-889-9999 |
| Airtel | 198 (from Airtel) | 9876012345 |
| Vi | 199 (from Vi) | 9886098860 |
| BSNL | 198 (from BSNL) | 1800-180-1503 |
| MTNL | 1503 | 1800-11-1503 |

1. Call the customer care number from any phone
2. Verify your identity (they'll ask your name, date of birth, address, or alternate number)
3. Request **immediate SIM block**
4. Note the **complaint/reference number**

### Method 2: Via Operator App (If Logged In on Another Device)

**Jio:**
1. Open MyJio app → Menu → Help → Report Lost SIM
2. Or visit [jio.com](https://www.jio.com/) → Support

**Airtel:**
1. Open Airtel Thanks app → Help → SIM Block/Replace
2. Or visit [airtel.in](https://www.airtel.in/) → Support

**Vi:**
1. Open Vi app → Help → Block SIM
2. Or visit [myvi.in](https://www.myvi.in/) → Support

**BSNL:**
1. Call 198 (app/online SIM block may not be available)

### Method 3: Visit the Store

1. Go to the nearest **operator store** (company-owned or authorized)
2. Carry a **valid ID proof** (Aadhaar, passport, etc.)
3. Request SIM block and replacement in one visit

## Do You Need to File an FIR?

- **FIR is generally NOT mandatory** for SIM replacement
- However, filing an FIR/NCR (Non-Cognizable Report) at the police station is **recommended** if:
  - Your phone was stolen (not just lost)
  - You suspect the SIM may be misused for fraud
  - Your operator or bank specifically asks for it
- You can file an FIR online in many states via the state police portal

## How to Get a Replacement SIM

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Visit the operator's store** (company-owned preferred for faster processing)
2. **Carry these documents:**
   - Aadhaar card (original + photocopy) — See our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
   - Passport-size photo (1-2 copies)
   - FIR copy (if filed, optional)
   - Blocked SIM complaint reference number
3. **Fill the SIM Replacement Form** (Customer Application Form)
4. Complete **Aadhaar biometric verification** (fingerprint/iris)
5. **Pay the replacement fee**
6. Receive the **new SIM card** with your same mobile number
7. **Activation time:** Usually **2-4 hours**, sometimes up to 24 hours

### Replacement Fee by Operator

| Operator | Replacement SIM Fee |
|----------|-------------------|
| Jio | Free – ₹50 (varies by circle) |
| Airtel | ₹25 – ₹50 |
| Vi | ₹25 – ₹50 |
| BSNL | ₹20 – ₹50 |

> Fees vary by circle and store. Some operators waive fees during promotional periods.

## eSIM Alternative

Instead of a physical replacement SIM, you can consider an **eSIM** (embedded SIM):

### What is eSIM?
- A digital SIM embedded in your phone — no physical card needed
- Supported by newer phones (iPhone XR and later, Samsung Galaxy S20+, Pixel 3a and later, etc.)
- Same number, same operator — just digital

### How to Get eSIM (if your phone supports it)

**Jio eSIM:**
1. Visit a Jio store with Aadhaar
2. Or call 198 and request eSIM activation
3. Scan the QR code provided by Jio on your phone

**Airtel eSIM:**
1. Send SMS `eSIM <registered email>` to 121
2. Or visit an Airtel store
3. Complete verification and scan QR code

**Vi eSIM:**
1. Send SMS `eSIM <registered email>` to 199
2. Or visit a Vi store

**BSNL eSIM:**
- BSNL eSIM support may be limited — check with your local BSNL office

### eSIM Benefits
- **Cannot be physically stolen** — even if phone is lost, eSIM is device-locked
- **Faster activation** — no waiting for physical SIM
- **Dual SIM support** — use eSIM + physical SIM together

## After Getting Replacement SIM

1. **Re-login to apps** — Banking apps, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, etc.), WhatsApp
2. **Re-verify your number** in apps that use SMS verification
3. **Check your balance** — Your existing plan and balance should be intact
4. **Enable SIM lock/PIN** on your phone for extra security
5. **Update your Aadhaar-linked mobile** if it was changed — see our [Aadhaar mobile update guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)

## Important Tips

1. **Block your SIM within minutes** of losing it — every minute counts for preventing fraud
2. Also **lock your phone remotely** using Find My iPhone (Apple) or Find My Device (Google)
3. Inform your **bank immediately** if your SIM is lost — request temporary block on OTP-based transactions
4. Keep a **record of your IMEI number** (dial `*#06#`) — useful for FIR and tracking
5. Consider using **eSIM** for your primary number to avoid physical SIM theft risks

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How quickly should I block a lost SIM?
**Immediately.** Call customer care within minutes. A lost SIM can be used to receive OTPs for banking, UPI, and other sensitive accounts.

### Can someone misuse my lost SIM?
Yes. They can receive OTPs, make calls, send messages from your number, and potentially access your bank accounts if your phone is also compromised.

### Will I get the same number on replacement SIM?
Yes. The replacement SIM (duplicate SIM) retains your **same mobile number**. Your contacts and plan balance are also preserved.

### How long does SIM replacement take?
The store visit takes 15-30 minutes. SIM activation takes **2-4 hours** (up to 24 hours in some cases).

### Can I get a replacement SIM without Aadhaar?
Aadhaar is the primary document for biometric verification. Some operators may accept **passport or voter ID** as alternatives, but Aadhaar is preferred and fastest.

### Do I need to visit the same operator store where I bought the SIM?
No. You can visit **any authorized store** of your operator, anywhere in India.

### What if I'm abroad when I lose my SIM?
Call your operator's **international helpline** (check their website). Some operators allow SIM block via email. Ask a trusted person in India to visit the store for replacement.
