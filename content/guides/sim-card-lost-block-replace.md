# SIM Card Lost? How to Block & Get Replacement

If you have lost your SIM card or your phone has been stolen, the first thing you must do is block your SIM to prevent misuse. After blocking, you can get a replacement SIM with the same number. This guide covers the complete process for all major Indian operators.

## Why You Should Block Your SIM Immediately

A lost or stolen SIM can be misused for:
- **Fraudulent calls and messages** in your name
- **OTP-based transactions** on your bank accounts and UPI
- **Identity theft** — someone could reset passwords on your accounts

Block your SIM within minutes of discovering the loss.

---

## How to Block Your SIM Card

### Method 1: Call Customer Care (Fastest)

| Operator | Customer Care Number | Alternate |
|---|---|---|
| **Jio** | 198 (from Jio) / 1800-889-9999 | MyJio app |
| **Airtel** | 121 (from Airtel) / 1800-103-4444 | Airtel Thanks app |
| **Vi** | 199 (from Vi) / 1800-120-9000 | Vi app |
| **BSNL** | 1800-180-1503 | Visit BSNL office |

Call from any other phone. Provide your mobile number and verify your identity (name, date of birth, address, or last recharge details).

### Method 2: Via Operator App

- **MyJio App** → Menu → SIM Block Request
- **Airtel Thanks App** → Help → Report Lost SIM
- **Vi App** → Help → Block My Number

### Method 3: Visit the Store

Visit the nearest operator store with your ID proof. Request immediate SIM block.

### Method 4: Online (Where Available)

Some operators allow SIM blocking through their website by logging into your account.

## Should You File an FIR?

Filing an FIR (First Information Report) is **optional but recommended** if:
- Your **phone was stolen** (not just the SIM)
- You suspect **financial fraud** may occur
- You want an official record for insurance claims

You can file an FIR at your nearest police station or online through your state's citizen portal. The FIR copy may speed up the replacement process.

## How to Get a Replacement SIM (Same Number)

### Step 1: Visit the Operator Store

Go to an authorized store of your telecom operator. Carry:
- **ID proof** — [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online), PAN card, Passport, Voter ID, or Driving Licence
- **Passport-size photograph** (1-2 copies)
- **FIR copy** (if filed — optional)
- **Alternate contact number**

### Step 2: Fill Replacement Form

- Fill the SIM Replacement / Duplicate SIM Application Form
- Provide your blocked mobile number
- Submit ID proof and photograph
- Verify identity through alternate means (security questions, last recharge, etc.)

### Step 3: Verification

- The operator verifies your identity against their records
- Some operators send an OTP to your registered email for additional verification
- TRAI mandates a **waiting period** before activating the replacement SIM

### Step 4: New SIM Activation

- **Replacement SIM activation:** Usually within **2-24 hours**
- You may receive a temporary blank SIM that gets activated after verification
- Once active, all services on your number resume

## Replacement Process by Operator

### Jio
- Visit Jio Store or Reliance Digital store
- Carry Aadhaar and photo
- Aadhaar-based eKYC for instant verification
- Activation: **2-4 hours**
- Also available: **eSIM replacement** via MyJio app

### Airtel
- Visit Airtel Store
- Carry ID proof and photo
- Biometric/eKYC verification
- Activation: **2-4 hours**
- eSIM option available for supported devices

### Vi (Vodafone Idea)
- Visit Vi Store
- Carry ID proof and photo
- eKYC verification
- Activation: **4-24 hours**

### BSNL
- Visit BSNL Customer Service Centre
- Carry ID proof, photo, and written application
- Activation: **24-48 hours**

## eSIM as Replacement

If your phone supports eSIM, you may be able to get a replacement eSIM instead of a physical SIM:

- **Jio** and **Airtel** support eSIM replacement
- Request through the app or at the store
- Faster activation — often within **1-2 hours**
- See our [eSIM guide](/guide/esim-activate-guide-india) for setup steps

## Important Tips

1. **Block your SIM immediately** — don't wait, every minute counts for preventing misuse
2. **Inform your bank** to temporarily block SMS-based transactions if you suspect fraud
3. **Change passwords** of important accounts (email, banking, UPI) from another device
4. **Track your lost phone** using Find My iPhone (Apple) or Find My Device (Google)
5. **Keep a record** of your IMEI number (dial *#06#) — it helps in tracking stolen phones

- **SIM blocking:** Immediate (within minutes)
- **Replacement SIM activation:** 2-48 hours depending on operator
- **eSIM replacement:** 1-2 hours

## FAQs

### Will I lose my contacts saved on the SIM?
Yes, contacts saved on the SIM card will be lost. Contacts saved on your phone or Google/iCloud account will be safe.

### Can someone misuse my SIM before I block it?
Yes. Until you block the SIM, anyone with access can make calls, send messages, and receive OTPs. Block it immediately.

### Can I get the same number back after blocking?
Yes. The SIM replacement process gives you a new SIM card with the same mobile number.

### What happens to my balance and active plans?
Your balance and active plans are linked to your number, not the SIM card. They will be restored when the replacement SIM is activated.

### Can I get a replacement SIM in a different city?
Yes. Most operators allow replacement from any authorized store across India, though some may require you to visit a store in your registered circle.

### How do I block my SIM if I don't have another phone?
Visit the nearest operator store directly with your ID proof and request immediate blocking and replacement.

### Can I port my number to another operator while getting a replacement?
No. You must first get the replacement SIM, wait for activation, and then initiate the [MNP process](/guide/mobile-number-port-mnp-guide) separately.
