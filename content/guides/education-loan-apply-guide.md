# Education Loan Apply Online: Process, Banks & Documents

An education loan helps you fund higher education in India or abroad without financial burden during studies. Major banks like SBI, HDFC, and Bank of Baroda offer education loans with competitive interest rates, moratorium periods, and government subsidy options. This guide covers the complete application process, bank comparison, and important rules.

## What is an Education Loan?

An education loan is a financial product offered by banks and NBFCs to cover the cost of higher education. It covers:

- **Tuition fees** — college/university fees
- **Hostel and living expenses**
- **Books, equipment, and study materials**
- **Travel expenses** (for studies abroad)
- **Examination and library fees**
- **Laptop/computer** (if required by the course)

The loan is repaid after the course completion, with a moratorium (grace) period.

## Vidyalakshmi Portal — Apply to Multiple Banks

The **Vidyalakshmi Portal** ([vidyalakshmi.co.in](https://www.vidyalakshmi.co.in/)) is a government initiative that allows students to apply for education loans from multiple banks through a single application.

### How to Apply via Vidyalakshmi

1. **Visit** [vidyalakshmi.co.in](https://www.vidyalakshmi.co.in/)
2. **Register** with your email ID and mobile number
3. **Complete your profile** — personal details, academic details, course details
4. **Search for loan schemes** — filter by bank, loan amount, course type
5. **Apply to up to 3 banks** simultaneously
6. **Track application status** on the dashboard
7. **Banks will contact you** for further documentation and processing

### Benefits of Vidyalakshmi

- Apply to multiple banks in one place
- Compare loan schemes and interest rates
- Link with government scholarship/subsidy schemes
- Track application status online

## Step-by-Step: How to Apply for Education Loan

### 1. Choose Your Bank and Scheme

- Compare interest rates, loan limits, and processing fees
- Check if your course and college are covered
- Use Vidyalakshmi portal for comparison

### 2. Check Eligibility

| Criteria | Requirement |
|----------|------------|
| Nationality | Indian citizen |
| Age | 18-35 years (at the time of application) |
| Admission | Confirmed admission to a recognized institution |
| Course | Graduation, post-graduation, professional courses, studies abroad |
| Co-applicant | Parent/guardian as co-borrower (mandatory) |
| Academic record | Generally 50% or above in qualifying exam |

### 3. Gather Documents

#### Student Documents
- Admission letter from the institution
- Class 10 and 12 mark sheets
- Graduation mark sheets (for PG courses)
- Entrance exam score card (JEE/NEET/GMAT/GRE etc.) — see our [JEE Main guide](/guide/jee-main-result-check-guide) or [NEET guide](/guide/neet-result-score-card-guide)
- Course fee structure from the institution
- Passport-size photographs
- Aadhaar card — see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
- PAN card — see our [PAN card guide](/guide/pan-card-apply-online) if needed

#### Co-applicant (Parent/Guardian) Documents
- Aadhaar card and PAN card
- Income proof (salary slips / ITR for last 2 years)
- Bank statements (last 6 months)
- Address proof

#### For Collateral (if loan > ₹7.5 lakh)
- Property documents (sale deed, title deed)
- Property valuation report
- NOC from housing society (if applicable)

### 4. Submit Application

- **Online:** Through bank website or Vidyalakshmi portal
- **Offline:** Visit the nearest bank branch with all documents
- **Processing time:** 7-15 working days

### 5. Loan Sanction and Disbursement

- Bank verifies documents and credit profile
- Loan sanction letter is issued
- Loan amount is disbursed directly to the institution (in most cases)
- Disbursement happens semester-wise or year-wise

## Interest Rates Explained

### Fixed vs Floating Rate

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Floating rate** | Linked to bank's MCLR/RLLR — changes with RBI policy. Most education loans are floating rate. |
| **Fixed rate** | Rate remains same throughout tenure. Rarely offered for education loans. |

### Interest During Moratorium

- **Simple interest** accrues during the course and moratorium period
- Some banks offer the option to pay interest during the study period (reduces total cost)
- If you don't pay during moratorium, interest is added to the principal (compounding)

**Tip:** If possible, pay at least the interest during your study period. This can save you lakhs over the loan tenure.

---

## Moratorium Period

The moratorium is the **grace period during which you don't need to make EMI payments**:

- **Duration:** Course period + 6 to 12 months after completion (varies by bank)
- **Interest:** Interest accrues during moratorium but EMI is not required
- **Repayment starts:** After moratorium ends
- **Repayment tenure:** Up to 15 years after moratorium

### Example
If your B.Tech is 4 years and moratorium is course + 12 months:
- You start repaying EMIs 5 years after the loan is disbursed
- You have up to 15 years to repay after that

## Collateral Rules

| Loan Amount | Collateral Required? |
|-------------|---------------------|
| Up to ₹4 lakh | No collateral, no third-party guarantee |
| ₹4 lakh to ₹7.5 lakh | No collateral, but third-party guarantee needed |
| Above ₹7.5 lakh | Collateral required (property, FD, etc.) |

### Acceptable Collateral
- Residential/commercial property
- Fixed deposits
- Government securities
- LIC policies
- Gold/gold ornaments (some banks)

**Note:** Collateral value should typically be 1.25x to 1.5x the loan amount.

## Government Subsidy — Interest Subsidy Scheme

The **Central Sector Interest Subsidy Scheme (CSIS)** provides full interest subsidy during the moratorium period for economically weaker students:

### Eligibility for Subsidy

| Criteria | Requirement |
|----------|------------|
| Family income | Up to ₹4.5 lakh per annum |
| Course | Technical/professional courses from recognized institutions |
| Loan source | Scheduled banks under IBA model education loan scheme |
| Subsidy coverage | Full interest during moratorium period |
| Application | Through the lending bank — no separate application needed |

### How It Works

1. Apply for an education loan from a scheduled bank
2. Provide family income certificate showing income ≤ ₹4.5 lakh
3. Bank forwards your subsidy claim to the government
4. Government pays the interest directly to the bank during your moratorium period
5. You repay only the principal + interest after moratorium

**This can save ₹2-5 lakh** depending on loan amount and course duration.

## Tax Benefits on Education Loan

Under **Section 80E of the Income Tax Act**, the interest paid on education loans is fully deductible from taxable income:

- **Deduction:** Full interest amount (no upper limit)
- **Duration:** Up to 8 years from the year you start repaying, or until interest is fully paid (whichever is earlier)
- **Who can claim:** The person who repays the loan (student or parent)
- **Principal repayment:** Not eligible for deduction under 80E

## Important Tips

1. **Apply to multiple banks** through Vidyalakshmi for the best deal
2. **Start repaying interest during studies** if possible — it significantly reduces the total cost
3. **Check if your college is in the bank's approved list** — some banks have lists of recognized institutions
4. **Keep all fee receipts** — banks disburse based on actual fee demands
5. **Claim Section 80E tax deduction** every year while repaying — it can save significant tax
6. **For studies abroad**, start the loan process 3-4 months before departure

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Can I get an education loan without collateral?

Yes. Loans up to ₹7.5 lakh are available without collateral. Above ₹7.5 lakh, collateral (property/FD) is required.

### Q2: What is the interest rate for SBI education loan?

SBI education loan interest rates currently range from 8.15% to 10.50% depending on the loan amount and scheme. Check [sbi.co.in](https://sbi.co.in/) for the latest rates.

### Q3: When do I start repaying the education loan?

Repayment begins after the moratorium period ends (course duration + 6-12 months). You have up to 15 years to repay.

### Q4: Can I prepay my education loan?

Yes. There is no prepayment penalty on education loans as per RBI guidelines. You can prepay partially or fully at any time.

### Q5: What if I don't get a job after completing my course?

Banks may extend the moratorium period in genuine cases. Contact your bank and explain the situation. The loan does not get waived — you will still need to repay.

### Q6: Is education loan available for part-time or distance learning courses?

Most banks do not offer education loans for part-time or correspondence courses. The course must be full-time from a recognized institution.

### Q7: Can I get a loan for studying abroad?

Yes. SBI offers up to ₹1.50 crore for foreign education, Bank of Baroda up to ₹80 lakh, and HDFC up to ₹50 lakh. Collateral is usually required for foreign education loans.
