## Why Is Aadhaar Mobile Number Update Important?

Your Aadhaar-linked mobile number is used for **OTP verification** across government services — PM Kisan eKYC, PF withdrawal, income tax e-filing, bank KYC, and more. If your mobile number is outdated or wrong, you'll be locked out of these services.

**Important:** Aadhaar mobile number update **cannot be done online**. You must visit an Aadhaar Enrolment Centre in person. This is a security measure by UIDAI.

## Common Errors & Fixes

### 1. "Mobile Number Already Linked to Another Aadhaar"
- **Error:** "This mobile number is already registered with another Aadhaar"
- **Cause:** The same mobile number can be linked to **multiple Aadhaar cards** — this error typically occurs due to a system glitch or incorrect interpretation
- **Reality:** UIDAI **allows the same mobile number** to be linked to multiple Aadhaar numbers (e.g., family members sharing a phone). If you're getting this error at a centre:
  1. Ask the operator to **proceed anyway** — this should not block the update
  2. If the operator refuses, visit a **different Aadhaar centre**
  3. Contact UIDAI helpline at **1947** if the issue persists

### 2. OTP Not Received on Old Number
- **Error:** You're trying to verify your identity but the OTP goes to the old (inactive) number
- **Cause:** OTP-based authentication requires the currently registered number
- **Fix:**
  1. **You don't need OTP from the old number** to update mobile at an Aadhaar centre — biometric verification is used instead
  2. Visit an Aadhaar centre and authenticate via **fingerprint or iris scan**
  3. The new number will be registered without needing access to the old one

### 3. Biometric Authentication Failed at Centre
- **Error:** "Fingerprint not matched" at the enrolment centre
- **Fix:**
  1. Clean and dry your fingers before scanning
  2. Try a **different finger** (all 10 are registered)
  3. Try **iris scan** as an alternative
  4. If biometrics are degraded, you may need to **update your biometrics** first (separate process)
  5. Visit a **different centre** with better equipment

### 4. "Update Request Already Pending"
- **Error:** Centre operator says there's already a pending request
- **Fix:**
  1. Wait for the previous request to complete — typically **7-10 working days**
  2. Check status at [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in) using the URN from your previous visit
  3. If more than 15 days have passed, file a complaint at UIDAI

### 5. Appointment Booking Issues
- **Error:** No slots available on the UIDAI appointment portal
- **Fix:**
  1. Check [appointments.uidai.gov.in](https://appointments.uidai.gov.in) early morning (slots open for next 3 days)
  2. Try nearby pin codes — centres in adjacent areas may have availability
  3. **Walk-in option:** Many centres accept walk-ins, especially in smaller towns. Call the centre first
  4. Check for **bank-based Aadhaar centres** (SBI, Post Office) which are less crowded

## Step-by-Step: How to Update Aadhaar Mobile Number

### At Aadhaar Enrolment Centre (Only Method)

1. **Book appointment** at [appointments.uidai.gov.in](https://appointments.uidai.gov.in) (recommended but not always required)
2. **Visit the centre** with:
   - Your **Aadhaar card** (original or downloaded e-Aadhaar)
   - The **new mobile phone** with the SIM you want to link
3. Tell the operator you want to **update mobile number only**
4. The operator will enter your Aadhaar number and select "Mobile Update"
5. **Authenticate via biometrics** (fingerprint or iris scan) — no OTP from old number needed
6. Provide your **new mobile number**
7. You'll receive an **OTP on the new number** to verify it's active
8. Enter the OTP at the centre
9. Collect the **acknowledgement slip** with URN (Update Request Number)
10. **Fee: ₹50** (government-mandated, paid at centre)

### Processing Time
- Mobile number update takes **7-10 working days**
- You'll receive an **SMS on your new number** once the update is complete
- Verify by trying to generate OTP on any Aadhaar service

### Using mAadhaar App (For Verification Only)
The mAadhaar app **cannot change your mobile number** — it can only register a profile using your **already-linked** number. However, after updating at a centre:

1. Download **mAadhaar** from Google Play Store or Apple App Store
2. Register with your **new (updated) mobile number**
3. Verify via OTP sent to the new number
4. Your Aadhaar profile will appear in the app
5. Use mAadhaar for quick access to e-Aadhaar, OTP generation, and biometric lock

## When to Contact UIDAI Helpline

Contact UIDAI if:
- Mobile update is **not reflected after 15 working days**
- Biometric authentication **keeps failing** at multiple centres
- Someone has **changed your mobile number without your consent**
- The Aadhaar centre is **charging more than ₹50**

**UIDAI Helpline:** 1947 (toll-free, available 24/7)
**Email:** help@uidai.gov.in
**Online Complaint:** [uidai.gov.in/contact-support](https://uidai.gov.in/contact-support)

## Important Tips

1. **Carry the new SIM** to the centre — you'll need to receive an OTP on it
2. **No online method exists** — don't fall for scam websites claiming online mobile update
3. **₹50 is the only fee** — if anyone charges more, report to UIDAI
4. **Check update status** using your URN at myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in
5. **Update mobile first** before attempting any OTP-based service (eKYC, PF, tax filing)

## FAQs

### Can I update my Aadhaar mobile number online?
No. UIDAI does not allow mobile number updates online for security reasons. You **must visit an Aadhaar Enrolment Centre** in person with biometric verification.

### How much does Aadhaar mobile number update cost?
The fee is **₹50** (inclusive of GST). This is the standard government charge. If any centre charges more, you can report it to UIDAI.

### How long does Aadhaar mobile number update take?
Typically **7-10 working days** from the date of your visit to the centre. You'll receive a confirmation SMS on your new number.

### Can I link the same mobile number to multiple Aadhaar cards?
Yes. UIDAI allows the **same mobile number** to be linked to multiple Aadhaar numbers. This is common for families sharing a phone number.

### Do I need my old mobile number to update to a new one?
No. At the Aadhaar centre, authentication is done via **biometrics (fingerprint/iris)**, not via OTP on the old number. You only need the new number to be active.

### What documents do I need for mobile number update?
No additional documents are required. Just carry your **Aadhaar card** (or e-Aadhaar printout) and the **new mobile phone** with active SIM.

### Can I update mobile number through mAadhaar app?
No. The mAadhaar app only works with your **already-linked** mobile number. It cannot change or update the linked number. Visit an Aadhaar centre for that.
