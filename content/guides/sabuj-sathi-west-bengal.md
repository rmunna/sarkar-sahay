# Sabuj Sathi Scheme West Bengal: Free Bicycle for Class 9-12 Students

Sabuj Sathi (Green Companion) is a flagship education scheme of the West Bengal government that provides **free bicycles** to students of Class 9 to 12 studying in government and government-aided schools. Launched in 2015, the scheme has distributed over **1 crore bicycles** to students across the state, making it one of the largest bicycle distribution programmes in India.

## What is Sabuj Sathi?

"Sabuj Sathi" means "Green Companion" in Bengali. The scheme aims to reduce school dropout rates by providing free bicycles to students who often walk long distances to attend school. The bicycles are distributed directly to students through their schools, not as cash transfers.

### Key Features

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Benefit | Free bicycle (physical distribution) |
| Eligible Classes | Class 9, 10, 11, 12 |
| Schools Covered | Government, government-aided, and recognised schools |
| Gender | Both boys and girls |
| Bicycle Type | Standard bicycle suitable for students |

## Who is Eligible?

- Must be enrolled in **Class 9, 10, 11, or 12** in a government, government-aided, or recognised school in West Bengal
- Must be a **resident of West Bengal**
- Both **boys and girls** are eligible
- **One bicycle per student** — students who received a bicycle in Class 9 don't get another in subsequent classes
- Students from **all economic backgrounds** are eligible (no income criterion)

## Documents Required

- **School enrolment proof** / student ID card
- **Aadhaar card** of the student
- **Photograph** of the student

> Note: Most documentation is handled by the school. Students primarily need to be enrolled and verified by the school.

## Step-by-Step Process

### How Bicycles Are Distributed

The Sabuj Sathi scheme is administered **through schools**, not through individual applications:

1. **School Registration:** Schools register on the **[Sabuj Sathi portal](https://wbsabujsathi.gov.in/)** (Banglar Shiksha platform)
2. **Student Data Upload:** School uploads the list of eligible students with details
3. **Verification:** Data is verified by the District Inspector of Schools
4. **Bicycle Procurement:** The government procures bicycles from approved manufacturers
5. **Distribution:** Bicycles are distributed to students through their schools in organised ceremonies
6. **Acknowledgement:** Students sign an acknowledgement receipt upon receiving the bicycle

### What Students/Parents Should Do

1. Ensure the student is **enrolled in a recognised school**
2. Provide **Aadhaar card** and photographs to the school when requested
3. **Confirm with the school** that the student's name is in the distribution list
4. **Attend the distribution event** at the school to receive the bicycle
5. **Sign the acknowledgement** receipt

### Check Distribution Status

Visit **[wbsabujsathi.gov.in](https://wbsabujsathi.gov.in/)** to check the distribution status for your district and school.

## Fees

The scheme is completely **free**. Students receive bicycles at no cost.

## Distribution Timeline

- Schools submit data at the beginning of the academic session
- Bicycle distribution typically happens **within 3-6 months** of the academic session start
- Distribution schedules vary by district

## Important Tips

1. **The bicycle is a physical distribution**, not a cash transfer — unlike Bihar's Cycle Yojana
2. **Maintain the bicycle** — it is your personal property after distribution
3. **Only first-time recipients** get bicycles — if you received one in Class 9, you won't get another in Class 11
4. **Private school students may also be eligible** if the school is recognised and registered on the portal
5. **Report non-distribution** to the District Inspector of Schools if your school is not distributing bicycles

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Do I get money or a physical bicycle?
You receive a **physical bicycle**, not cash. The bicycle is distributed through your school.

### Q2: Can college students avail this scheme?
No, the scheme is only for students in **Class 9 to 12** in schools. College students are not eligible.

### Q3: What brand of bicycle is distributed?
The government procures bicycles from approved manufacturers. The brand may vary by year and region.

### Q4: Can a student who received a cycle in Class 9 get another in Class 11?
No, each student receives **only one bicycle** under the scheme, typically when they first become eligible (entering Class 9).

### Q5: Is the scheme available in Kolkata?
Yes, the scheme covers **all districts** of West Bengal including Kolkata.

### Q6: What if my school is not distributing bicycles?
Check with the school administration whether they have registered on the Sabuj Sathi portal. If not, approach the **District Inspector of Schools (DI)** or **Block Development Officer**.

### Q7: Can I apply individually without school involvement?
No, the scheme works only through schools. Your school must register and submit your details on the portal.
