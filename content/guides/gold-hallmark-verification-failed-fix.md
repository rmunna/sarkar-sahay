# Gold Hallmark Verification Failed — How to Fix

Bought gold jewellery and the hallmark verification isn't working? Whether the HUID number is not found on the BIS website or you suspect the hallmark is fake, this guide covers exactly what to do — from verification steps to filing complaints and legal action.

## What Is Gold Hallmarking and HUID?

Since **June 2021**, the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) made hallmarking mandatory for gold jewellery sold in India. Every hallmarked piece now carries a **6-digit alphanumeric HUID (Hallmark Unique Identification Number)** that can be verified online.

### New Hallmarking System (Post-July 2021)

Every hallmarked gold item has **three marks**:

1. **BIS Logo** — triangular logo of Bureau of Indian Standards
2. **Purity Grade** — e.g., 916 (22 carat), 750 (18 carat), 585 (14 carat)
3. **HUID Number** — 6-character alphanumeric code (e.g., A1B2C3)

### Old Hallmarking System (Before July 2021)

The older system had **four marks**:

1. BIS Logo
2. Purity grade (e.g., 916)
3. Assaying & Hallmarking Centre's logo
4. Jeweller's identification mark and year of hallmarking

> **Note:** Old hallmarks without HUID are still valid if the jewellery was purchased before July 2021. You cannot verify old-format hallmarks via HUID lookup.

## How to Verify Gold Hallmark Online

### Method 1: BIS Website

1. Go to [https://bis.gov.in/verify-huid/](https://bis.gov.in/verify-huid/)
2. Enter the **6-character HUID** engraved on your jewellery
3. Enter the CAPTCHA and click **Verify**
4. If valid, you'll see: jewellery type, purity, jeweller name, hallmarking centre, and date

### Method 2: BIS Care App

1. Download **BIS Care App** from [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bis.care) or Apple App Store
2. Open the app → tap **Verify HUID**
3. Enter the HUID or scan the QR code (if available)
4. View complete hallmark details

### Method 3: SMS Verification

- Send an SMS: **`HUID <your 6-digit code>`** to **14100**
- You'll receive verification details via reply SMS

## Why HUID Verification May Fail

If your HUID shows "not found" or fails verification, here are the possible reasons:

| Reason | What It Means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Fake HUID** | The hallmark is forged; the jewellery was never hallmarked by BIS |
| **Data entry error** | The HUID was not uploaded correctly by the assaying centre |
| **Old hallmark format** | Jewellery hallmarked before July 2021 won't have a HUID |
| **Typo in HUID** | You may be reading the engraved code incorrectly (e.g., confusing 0 with O) |
| **Server issue** | BIS website may be temporarily down — try again later |

## Signs of a Fake Hallmark

Watch for these red flags:

- **Blurry or uneven markings** — Genuine hallmarks are laser-engraved and crisp
- **Missing BIS logo** — Every genuine piece must have the BIS triangle
- **HUID with fewer or more than 6 characters**
- **No hallmark certificate** provided by the jeweller
- **Jeweller refuses to show BIS licence** — All registered jewellers have a BIS registration number
- **Price significantly below market rate** — too-good-to-be-true pricing

## What to Do If Jeweller Sold Non-Hallmarked Gold

### Step 1: Confront the Jeweller

- Visit the jeweller with your **purchase bill, jewellery, and any certificate**
- Demand a **replacement with properly hallmarked gold** or a **full refund**
- Get everything in writing

### Step 2: File a Complaint on BIS Portal

If the jeweller refuses to cooperate:

1. Go to [BIS Complaint Portal (MANAK Online)](https://www.manakonline.in/MANAK/complaint.do)
2. Select **"Hallmarking"** as the complaint category
3. Fill in details: jeweller name, address, your purchase details, HUID (if any)
4. Upload supporting documents — bill, photos of jewellery and hallmark
5. Submit and note your **complaint reference number**

**BIS helpline:** **14100** (toll-free) or email **cmd3@bis.gov.in**

### Step 3: File a Consumer Complaint

If no resolution from BIS:

1. Go to [Consumer Helpline Portal](https://consumerhelpline.gov.in/)
2. Register and file a grievance under **"Gold / Jewellery"**
3. Or call the **National Consumer Helpline: 1800-11-4000** (toll-free)

### Step 4: Approach Consumer Court

For significant losses, file a case in **Consumer Dispute Redressal Forum**:

- **District Forum** — claims up to ₹1 crore
- **State Commission** — claims ₹1 crore to ₹10 crore
- **National Commission** — claims above ₹10 crore

**How to file:**
1. Visit [https://ncdrc.nic.in/](https://ncdrc.nic.in/) or your district consumer forum
2. Submit a written complaint with purchase bill, jewellery photos, BIS complaint copy
3. Pay nominal court fees (₹100–₹5,000 depending on claim amount)
4. No lawyer required — you can represent yourself

### Step 5: File an FIR (For Fraud)

If the jewellery is proven fake or significantly impure:

- File an **FIR at your local police station** under:
  - **Section 420 IPC** (cheating) / **Section 318 BNS**
  - **Section 417 IPC** (cheating by personation) where applicable
- Attach BIS complaint copy and any lab test reports

## How to Get Gold Purity Tested Independently

If you doubt the purity even after hallmark verification:

1. Visit a **BIS-recognized Assaying & Hallmarking Centre** (find one at [hallmarking.bis.gov.in](https://hallmarking.bis.gov.in/))
2. Submit your jewellery for **XRF (X-ray fluorescence) testing** — non-destructive
3. Cost: approximately ₹200–₹500 per item
4. You'll get a **test report** with exact purity — use this as evidence if needed

## Important Things to Remember

- **Always ask for a hallmarked jewellery bill** with HUID mentioned
- **Verify HUID before leaving the shop** using BIS Care app
- **Keep purchase bills safely** — needed for complaints and resale
- **BIS registration of jeweller** can be checked at [hallmarking.bis.gov.in](https://hallmarking.bis.gov.in/)
- **Hallmarking is mandatory** for 14, 18, 20, 22, 23, and 24 carat gold jewellery
- Old jewellery (pre-2021) without HUID is still valid but cannot be HUID-verified

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is hallmarking mandatory for all gold jewellery?

Yes, since June 2021, hallmarking is mandatory for gold jewellery of 14, 18, 20, 22, 23, and 24 carat sold by registered jewellers. Items below 2 grams and certain categories like kundan, polki, and jadau are exempt.

### Can I sell old gold jewellery without HUID?

Yes. Old hallmarked jewellery (pre-July 2021) without HUID is still considered valid. Jewellers can accept it for exchange or resale.

### What if the BIS website shows wrong details for my HUID?

File a complaint on the BIS portal with your purchase bill and jewellery photos. The mismatch could indicate data error or potential fraud.

### How long does BIS take to resolve complaints?

BIS typically acknowledges complaints within **7 working days** and aims to resolve them within **30–60 days**, depending on the complexity and investigation required.

### Is there a penalty for jewellers selling non-hallmarked gold?

Yes. Under the BIS Act, 2016, selling non-hallmarked gold jewellery as hallmarked attracts **imprisonment up to 1 year**, a **fine up to ₹5 lakh**, or both.
