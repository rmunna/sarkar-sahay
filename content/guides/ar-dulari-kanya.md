# Dulari Kanya Yojana Arunachal Pradesh – Complete Guide 2026

## What is Dulari Kanya Yojana?

The **Dulari Kanya Yojana** is a girl child welfare scheme launched by the Government of Arunachal Pradesh. The scheme provides a **fixed deposit** in the name of girl children born in the state, which matures when the girl turns 18. This encourages families to value the girl child, ensure her education, and prevent child marriage.

## Benefits

| Component | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Fixed Deposit at birth | ₹20,000 (deposited by the state government) |
| Maturity | When the girl turns 18 years |
| Maturity amount | ₹20,000 + accumulated interest |
| Condition | Girl must remain unmarried until 18 and complete education milestones |

> **Note:** The exact FD amount may have been revised. Verify with the Women & Child Development Department.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Gender:** Girl child
- **Birth:** Born in Arunachal Pradesh (institutional delivery preferred)
- **Residency:** Parents must be permanent residents of Arunachal Pradesh with **APST (Arunachal Pradesh Scheduled Tribe)** status or domicile
- **Birth registration:** Birth must be officially registered
- **Family:** Benefits typically available for up to **two girl children** per family
- **Income:** Primarily targets BPL and economically weaker families

## Documents Required

- Birth certificate of the girl child
- Aadhaar Card of parent(s)
- APST certificate or residential/domicile certificate
- Hospital discharge certificate (institutional delivery proof)
- BPL card / income certificate
- Bank passbook of parent
- Passport-size photographs of the child and parent

## How to Apply – Step-by-Step

1. **Register the birth** at the hospital or nearest registrar
2. Visit the **District Women & Child Development Office** or **ICDS (Anganwadi) Centre**
3. Collect the **Dulari Kanya application form**
4. Fill in details of the girl child, parents, and residential status
5. Attach all required documents
6. Submit at the WCD office or Anganwadi Centre
7. After verification, the **state government opens a Fixed Deposit** in the girl's name
8. The FD matures when the girl turns **18 years**

### Claiming the Maturity Amount

1. When the girl turns 18, visit the **District WCD Office** with:
   - Original FD certificate
   - Girl's Aadhaar Card and bank account
   - Age proof (Class 10 certificate)
   - Proof of being unmarried (self-declaration)
2. The matured amount is transferred to the girl's bank account

## Processing Time

- **Application to FD creation:** 1-3 months
- **Maturity:** When the girl turns 18

## Important Tips

1. **Apply as soon as possible after birth** — don't delay
2. **Keep the FD certificate safe** — it will be needed at maturity
3. **Ensure institutional delivery** for easier documentation
4. **Educate the girl child** — some benefits may be linked to education milestones
5. **Don't allow child marriage** — benefits are forfeited if the girl marries before 18

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: How much money does the girl receive at 18?
**A:** The **₹20,000 FD plus accumulated interest** over 18 years. The exact maturity amount depends on the interest rate.

### Q2: Can I apply for a third girl child?
**A:** Benefits are typically for **up to two girl children** per family.

### Q3: Is the scheme only for APST families?
**A:** The scheme primarily targets **APST families and BPL households**. Non-APST permanent residents may also be eligible — check with the WCD office.

### Q4: What if the girl marries before 18?
**A:** The benefit is **forfeited** if the girl marries before the age of 18.

### Q5: Can I withdraw the FD before maturity?
**A:** No, the FD is meant to mature only when the girl turns 18. **Premature withdrawal is not allowed.**

### Q6: Is institutional delivery mandatory?
**A:** Institutional delivery is preferred and may be a requirement. Contact the WCD office for current policy.
