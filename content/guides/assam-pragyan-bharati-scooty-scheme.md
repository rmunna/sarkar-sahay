# Assam Pragyan Bharati Scooty Scheme 2025 – Complete Guide

## What is the Pragyan Bharati Scooty Scheme?

The Pragyan Bharati Scooty Scheme is an initiative by the Government of Assam to encourage higher education among girls. Under this scheme, **free scooties** are distributed to meritorious girl students who have secured **first division marks** in their Higher Secondary (Class 12) examinations and are pursuing higher education in colleges or universities within Assam.

The scheme was introduced to address the challenge of transportation that many rural and semi-urban girls face while commuting to colleges, which often leads to dropout. By providing personal mobility, the government aims to improve female enrollment and retention in higher education.

## Who is Eligible?

To qualify for a free scooty under Pragyan Bharati, the student must meet these criteria:

- **Gender:** Only **girl students** are eligible
- **Domicile:** Must be a permanent resident of Assam
- **Education:** Must have passed **Class 12 (HS) with first division** (60% and above) from a recognized board
- **Enrollment:** Must be enrolled in a **degree course** (BA, BSc, BCom, BCA, etc.) at a recognized college or university in Assam
- **Family Income:** Annual family income should not exceed **₹2 lakh** (for general category) or as specified by the government
- **Age:** No strict upper age limit, but the student should be pursuing the first undergraduate degree

### Who is NOT Eligible?

- Male students
- Students who scored below first division in Class 12
- Students pursuing education outside Assam
- Students already owning a two-wheeler
- Students who have received similar vehicle benefits under other schemes

## Documents Required

### Mandatory Documents
- Aadhaar card of the student
- Class 12 (HS) marksheet showing first division
- College/university admission receipt or enrollment certificate
- Bank account passbook (in student's name)
- Family income certificate from Circle Officer
- Domicile/residential certificate

### Additional Documents
- Passport-size photographs (3 copies)
- Caste certificate (if applicable, for reserved category applicants)
- Self-declaration that no other vehicle benefit has been availed
- Parent/guardian's identity proof

## How to Apply – Step-by-Step

### Step 1: Check Your Eligibility
Verify that you have scored first division in your HS exams and are enrolled in a degree program within Assam.

### Step 2: Obtain the Application Form
Collect the Pragyan Bharati scheme application form from:
- Your **college office**
- **Directorate of Higher Education, Assam**
- District Education Office

### Step 3: Fill the Application Form
Provide all required details including personal information, HS exam roll number and marks, college enrollment details, and bank account information.

### Step 4: Get Institutional Verification
Have your college principal or registrar **verify and stamp** your application form, confirming your enrollment and first-division status.

### Step 5: Attach Documents
Compile self-attested copies of all required documents listed above.

### Step 6: Submit the Application
Submit the completed application through your **college** or directly at the **District Education Office**. Some years, online submission portals are also made available.

### Step 7: Verification and Selection
The Directorate of Higher Education verifies all applications. Eligible students are shortlisted based on merit and other criteria.

### Step 8: Scooty Distribution
Selected beneficiaries receive their scooties at **official distribution events** organized by the district administration or the state government.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: What marks are required to get a free scooty under Pragyan Bharati?
**A:** You need to score **first division (60% and above)** in your Class 12 (HS) examination from a recognized board.

### Q2: Can students from private colleges apply?
**A:** Yes, students enrolled in **recognized** private or government colleges within Assam are eligible, provided they meet all other criteria.

### Q3: Is there an income limit?
**A:** Yes, the annual family income should generally not exceed **₹2 lakh**. However, income criteria may vary, so check the latest notification from the Directorate of Higher Education.

### Q4: What type of scooty is provided?
**A:** The government typically provides a **standard model scooty** (such as Honda Activa or similar). The exact model may vary based on government procurement.

### Q5: Can I sell the scooty after receiving it?
**A:** No. The scooty is meant for the personal use of the student for commuting to college. Selling or transferring it is not permitted and may attract penalties.

### Q6: I passed HS last year but didn't apply. Can I still apply?
**A:** The scheme usually applies to students who passed in the **current or most recent exam year**. Check the latest notification for specific eligibility windows.

### Q7: Do I need a driving license before applying?
**A:** You don't need it to apply, but you will need a **valid driving license** before taking possession of the scooty.
