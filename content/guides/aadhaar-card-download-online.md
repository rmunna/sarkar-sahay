## What is e-Aadhaar?

e-Aadhaar is the electronic/digital version of your Aadhaar card issued by UIDAI (Unique Identification Authority of India). It is a password-protected PDF document that is legally valid and equivalent to the physical Aadhaar card. You can download it anytime from the official UIDAI portal.

e-Aadhaar contains:
- Your **12-digit Aadhaar number**
- **Name, date of birth, and gender**
- **Address** as recorded in Aadhaar
- **Photograph** and **QR code** for verification
- **Digital signature** of UIDAI for authenticity

> **Important:** e-Aadhaar is equally valid as the original Aadhaar card for all purposes — bank KYC, government schemes, mobile SIM purchase, and more.

## Who Can Download e-Aadhaar?

- Any Aadhaar holder with a **registered mobile number** linked to Aadhaar
- Aadhaar holders who know their **Enrolment ID (EID)**
- Those who have generated a **Virtual ID (VID)**
- Both Indian residents and NRIs with Aadhaar

> **Note:** If your mobile number is not registered with Aadhaar, you cannot download e-Aadhaar online. Visit an [Aadhaar enrolment centre](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) to update your mobile number first. Also see our guide on [updating mobile number in Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-update-mobile-email).

## Methods to Download e-Aadhaar

There are **three ways** to download your e-Aadhaar:

### Method 1: Using Aadhaar Number

1. Visit the official portal at [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Download Aadhaar"** option
3. Select **"Aadhaar Number"** tab
4. Enter your **12-digit Aadhaar number**
5. Enter the **security code (captcha)** displayed on screen
6. Click **"Send OTP"** — an OTP will be sent to your registered mobile number
7. Enter the **6-digit OTP** received on your mobile
8. Click **"Verify & Download"**
9. The e-Aadhaar PDF will be downloaded to your device

### Method 2: Using Virtual ID (VID)

1. Visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Download Aadhaar"**
3. Select the **"Virtual ID"** tab
4. Enter your **16-digit VID**
5. Enter the **captcha code**
6. Click **"Send OTP"** and enter the OTP received
7. Click **"Verify & Download"**

### Method 3: Using Enrolment ID (EID)

1. Visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Download Aadhaar"**
3. Select the **"Enrolment ID"** tab
4. Enter your **28-digit Enrolment ID** (14-digit enrolment number + 14-digit date/time stamp)
5. Enter the **captcha code**
6. Click **"Send OTP"** and enter the OTP
7. Click **"Verify & Download"**

> **Tip:** Your Enrolment ID is printed on the acknowledgement slip given at the time of Aadhaar enrolment.

## How to Download e-Aadhaar via mAadhaar App

1. Download the **mAadhaar app** from Google Play Store or Apple App Store
2. Open the app and **register with your Aadhaar-linked mobile number**
3. Verify via OTP
4. Your Aadhaar profile will be loaded automatically
5. Tap on **"View Aadhaar"** to see your details
6. Use the **share/download** option to save the PDF

## How to Open e-Aadhaar PDF (Password)

The downloaded e-Aadhaar PDF is **password-protected**. The password is:

**First 4 letters of your name (in CAPS) + Year of birth (YYYY)**

**Examples:**
- Name: Rajesh Kumar, DOB: 15/03/1990 → Password: **RAJE1990**
- Name: Anu Sharma, DOB: 22/07/2001 → Password: **ANU 2001** (with space if name is less than 4 letters)

> **Note:** If your name has fewer than 4 letters, use the full name followed by spaces to make it 4 characters, then add the birth year.

## Important Tips

1. **Always download from official UIDAI website** — never use third-party websites claiming to provide Aadhaar downloads
2. **Use Masked Aadhaar** when sharing with private entities to protect your full Aadhaar number
3. **Keep your mobile number updated** in Aadhaar records to ensure smooth OTP-based downloads
4. **e-Aadhaar is legally valid** — no one can refuse it in place of the physical card (as per UIDAI guidelines)
5. **Generate a VID** for enhanced privacy — use it instead of sharing your actual Aadhaar number

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### OTP Not Received
- Ensure your **mobile number is registered** with Aadhaar
- Check if your phone has **network connectivity**
- Wait at least **2-3 minutes** before requesting a new OTP
- If using a dual SIM phone, ensure the correct SIM is active

### Download Fails
- Try using a different browser (Chrome recommended)
- Clear your browser cache and cookies
- Disable popup blockers
- Try during non-peak hours (early morning or late night)

### Wrong Password Error
- Use CAPITAL letters for your name
- Use the name exactly as it appears on your Aadhaar (check spelling)
- Year of birth must be 4 digits (e.g., 1990, not 90)

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Is e-Aadhaar legally valid for all purposes?
Yes, e-Aadhaar is equally valid as the original Aadhaar card. As per UIDAI, any entity accepting Aadhaar must accept e-Aadhaar as well. It contains a digital signature from UIDAI that confirms its authenticity.

### 2. How many times can I download e-Aadhaar?
There is no limit on the number of times you can download your e-Aadhaar. It is completely free and can be downloaded as many times as needed.

### 3. Can I download e-Aadhaar without a registered mobile number?
No, OTP verification on a registered mobile number is mandatory for online download. If your mobile is not registered, visit the nearest [Aadhaar enrolment centre](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) to update your mobile number or check our [mobile update guide](/guide/aadhaar-update-mobile-email).

### 4. What if I don't know my Aadhaar number?
You can retrieve your Aadhaar number using the **"Retrieve Lost or Forgotten EID/UID"** option on the UIDAI website. See our guide on [retrieving lost Aadhaar](/guide/lost-aadhaar-card-retrieval) for detailed steps.

### 5. Is e-Aadhaar accepted for passport application?
Yes, e-Aadhaar is accepted as both proof of identity and proof of address for [passport applications](/guide/tatkal-passport-apply).

### 6. Can I download someone else's e-Aadhaar?
No, you can only download e-Aadhaar using the OTP sent to the registered mobile number linked with that specific Aadhaar. Each person must download their own e-Aadhaar.

### 7. How long does it take for e-Aadhaar to be available after enrolment?
After new Aadhaar enrolment or any update, it typically takes **3-5 working days** for the e-Aadhaar to be available for download.
