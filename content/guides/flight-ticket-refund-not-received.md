## What Happens When Your Flight Ticket Refund is Not Received?

When you cancel a flight or your flight gets cancelled by the airline, you are entitled to a refund as per DGCA (Directorate General of Civil Aviation) rules. However, many passengers face delays or non-receipt of refunds. This guide explains how to track, claim, and escalate your flight ticket refund in India.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent informational platform and is not affiliated with any airline or government body. Always verify details on the [official DGCA website](https://www.dgca.gov.in/).

## Airline Refund Policy in India (DGCA Rules)

### When Airlines Cancel the Flight

If the **airline cancels** the flight, you are entitled to:

- **Full refund** of the ticket price (including taxes and fees)
- Refund must be processed within **7 days** for cash/card payments
- Refund must be processed within **7 days** for credit shell/wallet (if you agree)

### When You Cancel the Flight

If **you cancel** the ticket, the refund depends on:

| Cancellation Timing | Typical Refund |
|---|---|
| More than 24 hours before departure | Fare minus cancellation charges |
| Within 24 hours of departure | Partial refund or no refund (varies by airline) |
| No-show (missed flight) | Usually no refund (only statutory taxes may be refunded) |

### Refund Timeline by Payment Method

| Payment Method | Expected Refund Timeline |
|---|---|
| Credit card | 7–14 business days |
| Debit card | 7–14 business days |
| UPI / Net banking | 5–10 business days |
| Wallet / Credit shell | 1–7 days |
| Booked via travel agent | 15–30 days (agent processes via airline) |

## How to Track Your Flight Refund

### Step 1: Check Airline Website or App

1. Log in to the airline's website or app
2. Go to **My Bookings** or **Manage Booking**
3. Find your cancelled booking and check refund status
4. Note the **refund reference number** if available

### Step 2: Check Your Bank Statement

- Look for the refund credit in your bank or credit card statement
- Refunds may appear as a separate transaction (not always with the airline name)
- For credit cards, check the **unbilled transactions** section

### Step 3: Contact the Airline

1. Call the airline's customer care helpline
2. Provide your **PNR number** and **booking reference**
3. Ask for the **refund processing date** and **ARN (Acquirer Reference Number)**
4. Use the ARN to track with your bank

## How to File a DGCA Complaint (AirSewa Portal)

If the airline does not process your refund within the stipulated time:

### Step 1: Visit AirSewa Portal

Go to [https://AirSewa.gov.in/](https://AirSewa.gov.in/) — this is the official DGCA grievance portal.

### Step 2: Register and Log In

1. Create an account with your mobile number and email
2. Verify via OTP

### Step 3: File Your Complaint

1. Click on **Grievance** → **Create Grievance**
2. Select the **airline name**
3. Enter your **PNR number**, **flight details**, and **booking date**
4. Describe the issue — mention refund not received, amount, and days elapsed
5. Upload supporting documents (booking confirmation, cancellation email, bank statement)
6. Submit the complaint

### Step 4: Track the Complaint

- You will receive a **complaint reference number**
- Airlines are required to respond within **15 days**
- Track status on the AirSewa portal or app

## Credit Card Chargeback Option

If the airline does not refund after 30+ days, you can initiate a **chargeback** through your credit card issuer:

1. Call your **credit card bank's** customer care
2. Request a **chargeback/dispute** for the airline transaction
3. Provide: transaction date, amount, merchant name, and proof of non-refund
4. The bank will investigate and may reverse the charge within **30–90 days**
5. Keep all documentation — booking confirmation, cancellation proof, complaint copies

> **Important:** Chargeback must typically be filed within **120 days** of the transaction date.

## Escalation Steps

| Step | Action | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Contact airline customer care | Wait 7–14 days |
| 2 | File complaint on AirSewa.gov.in | Wait 15 days for response |
| 3 | Escalate on DGCA portal | Wait 30 days |
| 4 | File credit card chargeback | Within 120 days of transaction |
| 5 | File complaint on consumerhelpline.gov.in (NCH) | Anytime |
| 6 | Approach Consumer Court (District Forum) | If all else fails |

## Important Tips

1. **Save all proof** — keep screenshots of booking confirmation, cancellation, and all emails from the airline
2. **Note the ARN** — the Acquirer Reference Number helps your bank trace the refund
3. **Don't wait too long** — if refund is not received within 15 days, immediately file on AirSewa
4. **Check credit shell** — some airlines issue refunds as credit shell by default; check your airline account wallet
5. **Travel agents add delay** — if booked through a travel agent (MakeMyTrip, Goibibo etc.), the refund goes to the agent first, then to you

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. How long does a flight ticket refund take in India?

Refunds typically take **7–14 business days** for card payments and **5–10 days** for UPI/net banking. If booked via a travel agent, it may take up to **30 days**.

### Q2. Where can I complain if my flight refund is not received?

File a complaint on the **AirSewa portal** ([AirSewa.gov.in](https://AirSewa.gov.in/)) which is the official DGCA grievance redressal platform. You can also call the AirSewa helpline at **1800-11-4646**.

### Q3. Can I get a full refund if the airline cancels my flight?

**Yes.** If the airline cancels your flight, you are entitled to a **full refund** including all taxes and fees, as per DGCA regulations.

### Q4. What is a credit card chargeback for flight refund?

A chargeback is when your credit card bank reverses the transaction amount back to your account after you dispute non-receipt of refund. File it within **120 days** of the original transaction.

### Q5. Is flight refund taxable in India?

**No.** A flight refund is a return of your own money and is not considered income. It is not taxable.

### Q6. Can I file a consumer complaint for flight refund?

**Yes.** You can file a complaint on [consumerhelpline.gov.in](https://consumerhelpline.gov.in/) or approach the **District Consumer Disputes Redressal Forum** if the airline or agent does not process your refund.
