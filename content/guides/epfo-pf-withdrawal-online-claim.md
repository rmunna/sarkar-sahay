## What is EPFO and EPF?

The **Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO)** manages the Employees' Provident Fund (EPF) — a mandatory retirement savings scheme for employees in India working in establishments with 20+ employees. Every month, **12% of your basic salary + DA** is deducted and deposited into your EPF account, and your employer contributes another 12% (8.33% goes to EPS — Employee Pension Scheme; 3.67% to EPF).

At any point, this accumulated balance + interest (currently **8.25% per annum**) is your PF savings. You can withdraw it partially or fully depending on your situation.

## What Can You Claim Online?

| Claim Type | Form | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Full PF withdrawal** | Form 19 | After leaving job (unemployed 2+ months) |
| **Pension withdrawal** | Form 10C | Withdraw pension corpus (service < 10 years) |
| **Monthly pension** | Form 10D | Pension after 58 years (service 10+ years) |
| **Partial PF advance** | Form 31 | Medical, education, marriage, housing |
| **PF transfer** | Form 13 | Transfer PF to new employer |
| **Death claim** | Form 20 | Nominee claims after employee death |

## Eligibility for Full PF Withdrawal

- Retired (age 58+) — can withdraw 100%
- Unemployed for **2 months or more** — full withdrawal allowed
- Permanently emigrating outside India — full withdrawal
- Female member leaving for marriage — full withdrawal

**Partial withdrawal (Form 31) — allowed for:**
- Medical treatment — any time, up to 6 months' basic salary
- Marriage/education — after 7 years, up to 50% of employee's share
- Home loan repayment — after 10 years, up to 90%
- COVID-19 (special provision) — 3 months' basic+DA or 75% of balance

## Step-by-Step: Withdraw PF Online

### Step 1: Activate Your UAN (if not done)

1. Go to [unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in)
2. Click **"Activate UAN"** on the home page
3. Enter your UAN (from salary slip or HR), member ID, Aadhaar, PAN, date of birth, mobile number
4. Verify OTP sent to registered mobile
5. Set a password — your UAN is now active

**What is UAN?** Universal Account Number — a 12-digit permanent number assigned to every EPF member. It stays the same across jobs.

### Step 2: Link Aadhaar with UAN (mandatory for online claims)

1. Log in to the unified portal
2. Go to **Manage → KYC**
3. Add Aadhaar number → submit
4. Your employer approves the Aadhaar linking (or it auto-approves if Aadhaar is OTP-verified)
5. Status shows "Approved" — you're ready to claim online

### Step 3: Submit Online Claim

1. Log in at unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in with UAN and password
2. Go to **Online Services → Claim (Form 31/19/10C/10D)**
3. Enter your bank account number (must be linked with UAN) → verify
4. Select claim type:
   - **Form 19** for full PF withdrawal
   - **Form 10C** for pension withdrawal
   - **Form 31** for PF advance/partial withdrawal
5. Fill details — purpose of withdrawal, amount (for Form 31)
6. Upload a scanned cheque leaf (for bank verification)
7. Submit — you'll get a reference number

### Step 4: Track Claim Status

1. Go to **Online Services → Track Claim Status**
2. Enter reference number OR check under your claim history
3. Status shows: Submitted → Under Process → Settled → Payment transferred to bank

**Processing time:** 3–7 working days for most online claims.

## How to Check PF Balance

**Method 1: EPFO Passbook**
1. Go to [passbook.epfindia.gov.in](https://passbook.epfindia.gov.in)
2. Log in with UAN and password
3. View passbook for all member IDs linked to your UAN

**Method 2: UMANG App**
- Download UMANG app → search "EPFO" → check balance with UAN and OTP

**Method 3: Missed Call**
- Give a missed call from your registered mobile to **011-22901406**
- You'll receive an SMS with your PF balance

**Method 4: SMS**
- Send SMS: `EPFOHO UAN ENG` to 7738299899 from your registered mobile

## Employee Pension Scheme (EPS) — Claim Form 10C vs 10D

| Situation | Form | What You Get |
|---|---|---|
| Service < 10 years, leaving job | 10C | Lump sum pension withdrawal benefit |
| Service ≥ 10 years, age < 50 | 10D | Deferred pension (starts at 58) |
| Service ≥ 10 years, age 58+ | 10D | Monthly pension (₹1,000–₹7,500/month) |

**Pension formula:** Monthly pension = (Pensionable salary × Pensionable service) ÷ 70

## Documents Required for Online Claim

- Aadhaar card (linked with UAN)
- Bank account (linked with UAN — must match your name)
- PAN card (mandatory for withdrawal if taxable)
- Cancelled cheque / bank passbook copy

**No physical documents need to be submitted** for online claims — everything is verified digitally via Aadhaar eKYC.

## Common Problems and Fixes

### "Aadhaar seeding not approved"
→ Your employer hasn't approved your Aadhaar KYC yet. Contact HR or the EPFO field office.

### "KYC details not verified"
→ Name mismatch between Aadhaar and EPFO records. File a correction request at the EPFO office with your name change supporting documents.

### "Claim rejected — bank account mismatch"
→ Your bank account name doesn't match your EPFO name. Link the correct account or correct your name in EPFO records.

### UAN not activated / forgot UAN
→ Your UAN is on every salary slip. You can also find it by providing your PAN or Aadhaar at the unified portal's "Know Your UAN" option.

### Previous employer hasn't transferred PF
→ File Form 13 online to transfer the old PF balance to your current account.

## Tax on PF Withdrawal

- **Tax-free** if you have completed **5 continuous years** of service (even across employers with PF transfer)
- If you withdraw before 5 years of service, **TDS is deducted at 10%** (if PAN linked) or **34.608%** (if PAN not linked) on the taxable portion
- **Employer's share + interest** is taxable; your own contribution is not taxable on withdrawal

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does it take to receive PF money after claim submission?
Online claims are typically settled in **3–7 working days** from submission. The amount is directly credited to your linked bank account.

### Can I withdraw PF while still employed?
You cannot make a full withdrawal while employed. You can claim a **PF advance (Form 31)** for specific purposes like medical emergency, marriage, education, or home loan repayment.

### My employer is not depositing PF deducted from salary. What to do?
You can file a complaint at the **EPFO grievance portal** (epfigms.gov.in) or visit your local EPFO regional office. EPFO can take action against non-compliant employers.

### Can I withdraw PF if I am changing jobs?
You should **transfer** (not withdraw) PF when changing jobs using Form 13 online. This maintains continuity of service for the 5-year tax-free threshold and EPS pension calculation.

### Can the nominee claim PF after the member's death?
Yes — the nominee/legal heir can claim PF using Form 20 and pension using Form 10D by visiting the EPFO office with the member's death certificate and nominee's documents.

### How to update mobile number in EPFO?
Through your employer's digital signature certificate (DSC) — ask HR to update it. Or visit the EPFO office with Aadhaar and request mobile number update.
