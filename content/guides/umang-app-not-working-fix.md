# UMANG App Not Working: How to Fix

UMANG (Unified Mobile Application for New-age Governance) provides access to over 1,200 government services from more than 200 departments. When the app stops working, you lose access to EPF services, Aadhaar services, DigiLocker, passport tracking, and many other essential functions. This guide covers all common UMANG app problems and their solutions.

## Common Error Messages and Symptoms

- **"Something went wrong. Please try again later"** — generic app error
- **"Login failed — invalid credentials"** — cannot log into the app
- **"OTP not received"** — registration or login OTP does not arrive
- **"Service temporarily unavailable"** — specific government service not loading
- **"Network error — please check your internet connection"** — connectivity issue
- **"Session expired — please login again"** — frequent session timeouts
- App crashes immediately after opening
- App is stuck on the loading/splash screen
- Services load but show blank pages
- App works on WiFi but not on mobile data (or vice versa)
- MPIN not working after app update

## Why UMANG App May Not Work

### 1. Outdated App Version

UMANG releases frequent updates. An outdated version may have bugs or compatibility issues with newer phone operating systems.

### 2. Server-Side Issues

UMANG connects to multiple government department servers. If any backend server is down, the corresponding service will not work.

### 3. Corrupted Cache or Data

Over time, the app's cached data can become corrupted, causing crashes and loading failures.

### 4. Network Configuration Issues

Some mobile networks or WiFi configurations block the specific ports or APIs that UMANG uses.

### 5. Phone Storage Full

If your phone storage is critically low, the app cannot save temporary files needed for operation.

### 6. Incompatible Device

UMANG requires Android 5.0+ or iOS 11.0+. Very old devices may not run it properly.

## Step-by-Step Troubleshooting

### Step 1: Update the App

1. Open **Google Play Store** or **Apple App Store**
2. Search for "UMANG"
3. If an update is available, tap **Update**
4. Wait for the update to complete and reopen the app

### Step 2: Clear App Cache and Data

**On Android:**
1. Go to **Settings → Apps → UMANG**
2. Tap **Storage**
3. Tap **Clear Cache** first and test the app
4. If the problem persists, tap **Clear Data** (you will need to log in again)

**On iOS:**
1. Delete the UMANG app
2. Reinstall from the App Store
3. Log in again with your credentials

### Step 3: Check Internet Connection

1. Open a web browser and load any website to confirm internet works
2. Switch between WiFi and mobile data to test both
3. If using WiFi, restart your router
4. Disable any VPN or proxy that may be active
5. Try accessing [web.umang.gov.in](https://web.umang.gov.in/) in a browser — if it works, the issue is app-specific

### Step 4: Reset MPIN

If your MPIN is not working after an update:
1. On the login screen, tap **Forgot MPIN**
2. Enter your registered mobile number
3. Verify with OTP
4. Set a new MPIN

### Step 5: Check Phone Storage

1. Go to **Settings → Storage**
2. Ensure at least 500 MB of free space is available
3. Delete unnecessary apps, photos, or files if storage is low

### Step 6: Re-register on UMANG

If login issues persist:
1. Uninstall the UMANG app
2. Reinstall from your app store
3. Tap **Register** and enter your mobile number
4. Verify with OTP and Aadhaar (optional)
5. Set a new MPIN

### Step 7: Check for Device Compatibility

1. Ensure your device runs **Android 5.0+** or **iOS 11.0+**
2. Check if your device has at least 2 GB RAM
3. If your device is too old, use the web version at [web.umang.gov.in](https://web.umang.gov.in/)

## Alternative Methods and Workarounds

### Use UMANG Web Portal

Access UMANG services through your browser at [web.umang.gov.in](https://web.umang.gov.in/). Most services available on the app are also accessible on the web version.

### Access Services Directly

Instead of using UMANG, go directly to the service portal:
- **EPF services:** [unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in/)
- **Aadhaar services:** [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/)
- **DigiLocker:** [digilocker.gov.in](https://digilocker.gov.in/)
- **Passport:** [passportindia.gov.in](https://passportindia.gov.in/)
- **Income Tax:** [eportal.incometax.gov.in](https://eportal.incometax.gov.in/)

### Try During Off-Peak Hours

Government app servers experience heavy load during business hours (10 AM - 5 PM). Try using the app early morning or late evening for better performance.

### Use a Different Network

If the app does not work on your current network, try switching to a different WiFi network or mobile data provider. Some ISPs may have routing issues with government servers.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why does UMANG app keep crashing?

UMANG crashes are usually caused by an outdated app version, corrupted cache, or insufficient phone storage. Update the app to the latest version, clear the cache from Settings → Apps → UMANG → Storage → Clear Cache, and ensure you have at least 500 MB free storage.

### Can I use UMANG without Aadhaar?

Yes. UMANG registration requires only a mobile number and OTP. Aadhaar linking is optional but recommended as it unlocks additional services like e-KYC verification and EPF access.

### Why are specific services not available on UMANG?

Individual services on UMANG depend on the respective government department's servers. If a specific service shows "temporarily unavailable," the department's backend server may be down for maintenance. Try again after a few hours or access the service directly through the department's own portal.

### How do I reset my UMANG MPIN?

On the UMANG login screen, tap "Forgot MPIN," enter your registered mobile number, verify with OTP, and set a new 4-digit MPIN. If OTP is not received, check your DND settings and ensure your number is correct.

### Is UMANG app safe to use?

Yes. UMANG is developed by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) and the National e-Governance Division (NeGD). It uses encrypted communication and does not store sensitive data locally. Always download it from official app stores only.
