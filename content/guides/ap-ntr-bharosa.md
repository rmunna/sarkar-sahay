# NTR Bharosa Pension – Andhra Pradesh Complete Guide

NTR Bharosa Pension is the flagship social security pension scheme of the Government of Andhra Pradesh. It provides monthly financial assistance to elderly citizens, widows, persons with disabilities, weavers, toddy tappers, fishermen, and other vulnerable sections. The pensions are disbursed directly at the doorstep of beneficiaries through Village/Ward Secretariats.

## What is NTR Bharosa Pension?

Launched by the AP government, NTR Bharosa consolidates multiple pension categories under one umbrella. The pension amount varies by category and is credited directly to beneficiaries' bank accounts or disbursed through Secretariat staff at the doorstep on the 1st of every month.

### Pension Amounts

| Category | Monthly Pension |
|----------|----------------|
| Old Age Pension (60-79 years) | ₹3,000 |
| Old Age Pension (80+ years) | ₹3,500 |
| Widow Pension | ₹3,000 |
| Disability Pension (40-79%) | ₹3,000 |
| Disability Pension (80%+) | ₹3,500 |
| Weavers Pension | ₹3,000 |
| Toddy Tappers Pension | ₹3,000 |
| Fishermen Pension | ₹3,000 |
| Dhobi / Nayee Brahmin Pension | ₹3,000 |

*Note: Amounts are as per recent AP government orders. Please verify latest amounts on the official portal.*

## Who is Eligible?

### Old Age Pension
- Age: 60 years or above
- Resident of Andhra Pradesh
- Annual family income below ₹2,40,000
- Should not be receiving any other government pension

### Widow Pension
- Women whose husband is deceased
- Age: 18 years or above
- Resident of Andhra Pradesh
- Annual family income below ₹2,40,000

### Disability Pension
- Persons with 40% or more disability (certified by medical board)
- Resident of Andhra Pradesh
- Annual family income below ₹2,40,000

### Other Categories
- Weavers, toddy tappers, fishermen, etc. must belong to the respective occupation and meet income criteria

## Documents Required

### Mandatory Documents
- Aadhaar card (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- Age proof (Aadhaar / voter ID / birth certificate)
- Ration card (see our [ration card guide](/guide/ration-card-apply-online))
- Income certificate from MeeSeva / Tahsildar
- Bank passbook (account linked to Aadhaar)
- Passport-size photographs (2 copies)

### Category-Specific Documents
- **Widow pension:** Husband's death certificate
- **Disability pension:** Disability certificate from District Medical Board (40%+ disability)
- **Weavers/Toddy tappers:** Occupation certificate from concerned department
- Caste certificate (if applicable)

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Online Method (via Spandana / Village Secretariat Portal)

1. **Visit nearest Village/Ward Secretariat:** Approach the Welfare & Education Assistant at your Ward/Village Secretariat
2. **Or apply online:** Visit the AP Pensions portal or Spandana portal
3. **Fill application:** Provide personal details, Aadhaar number, bank details, and pension category
4. **Submit documents:** Provide all required documents for verification
5. **Biometric verification:** Your biometric/Aadhaar authentication will be done
6. **Field verification:** Ward/Village Secretary conducts door-to-door verification
7. **Approval:** Application is approved at the mandal/district level
8. **Pension starts:** Once approved, pension is credited monthly to your bank account

### Offline Method

1. **Visit Village/Ward Secretariat** in your area
2. **Meet the Welfare Assistant** and request the NTR Bharosa pension application form
3. **Fill the form** with all required details
4. **Attach documents** — self-attested copies of all required documents
5. **Submit** the form at the Secretariat
6. **Receive acknowledgment** with application number
7. **Track status** through the Secretariat or by calling the helpline

## Processing Time

- **Application to approval:** 30–60 days
- **First pension credit:** Within 1–2 months after approval
- Pensions are disbursed on the **1st of every month**

## Important Tips

1. **Aadhaar-bank linking is mandatory:** Ensure your Aadhaar is linked to your bank account for DBT
2. **Door-to-door disbursement:** If you're unable to visit the bank, pensions can be collected at doorstep through Secretariat volunteers
3. **Annual verification:** Beneficiaries may need to undergo annual life verification — attend when notified
4. **Report death/migration:** Family members should inform the Secretariat if a beneficiary passes away to avoid legal issues
5. **No middlemen needed:** Apply directly at the Secretariat — do not pay any agent or middleman

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: How much pension will I get under NTR Bharosa?
The standard pension is ₹3,000/month for most categories. For beneficiaries aged 80+ or with 80%+ disability, the amount is ₹3,500/month.

### Q2: Can I apply online for NTR Bharosa Pension?
While the primary channel is through Village/Ward Secretariats, you can also apply through the Spandana grievance portal or AP Pensions portal online.

### Q3: Is there an income limit?
Yes, the annual family income should generally be below ₹2,40,000. This may vary — check the latest government order.

### Q4: When is the pension disbursed?
Pensions are disbursed on the 1st of every month directly to the beneficiary's bank account.

### Q5: Can a widow receiving pension remarry and still get benefits?
If a widow remarries, she may no longer be eligible for widow pension. However, she may qualify under other categories like old age pension if eligible.

### Q6: What if my pension is stopped?
Visit your Village/Ward Secretariat with your documents. If stopped due to verification issues, complete the verification process to restart payments.

### Q7: Can NRIs or people living outside AP apply?
No, you must be a current resident of Andhra Pradesh to receive NTR Bharosa pension.
