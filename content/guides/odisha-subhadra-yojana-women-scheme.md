# Odisha Subhadra Yojana — ₹50,000 Financial Aid for Women

## What is Subhadra Yojana?

Subhadra Yojana is the flagship women empowerment scheme of the Odisha state government, launched by Prime Minister Narendra Modi on **September 17, 2024**. Named after Devi Subhadra, the sister of Lord Jagannath, this scheme provides **₹50,000 in direct financial assistance** to eligible women over a period of five years (2024–2029).

Under this scheme, women aged 21 to 60 years from economically weaker sections receive **₹10,000 per year** — transferred in two instalments of ₹5,000 each — directly into their Aadhaar-linked bank accounts. The scheme was a key election promise by the BJP government led by Chief Minister **Mohan Charan Majhi** and aims to benefit over **1 crore women** across Odisha.

The total budget allocation for Subhadra Yojana is **₹55,825 crore** for the 2024–2029 period.

## Who is Eligible?

You can apply for Subhadra Yojana if you meet **all** these criteria:

1. **Gender:** Must be a woman
2. **Age:** Between 21 and 60 years
3. **Residence:** Must be a resident of Odisha
4. **Economic status:** Must belong to an economically weaker section

### Who is NOT Eligible?

- Government employees (state or central)
- Women who pay income tax
- Women already receiving more than ₹1,500 per month under other government schemes
- Women from families with government pensioners (excluding social security pensions)

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar Card** (mandatory — must be linked to mobile number and bank account)
- **Bank account passbook** (Aadhaar-linked)
- **Mobile number** linked to Aadhaar
- **Resident/address proof** (ration card, voter ID, or other valid document)
- **Age proof** (birth certificate, school leaving certificate, or Aadhaar)
- **Passport-size photograph**

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step Process

### Online Application (via Official Portal)

1. **Visit the official portal:** Go to [subhadra.odisha.gov.in](https://subhadra.odisha.gov.in/)
2. **Register:** Click on "Apply Online" and enter your Aadhaar-linked mobile number
3. **OTP verification:** Enter the OTP received on your registered mobile
4. **Fill application form:** Enter personal details — name, age, address, bank account number, and Aadhaar number
5. **Upload documents:** Upload scanned copies of required documents
6. **Complete e-KYC:** Aadhaar-based e-KYC is mandatory for verification
7. **Submit application:** Review all details and submit
8. **Save reference number:** Note down the application reference number for status tracking

### Offline Application (via Mo Seva Kendra / Common Service Centres)

1. Visit your nearest **Mo Seva Kendra**, Anganwadi Centre, or Common Service Centre (CSC)
2. Carry all original documents listed above
3. The centre operator will fill the form and complete e-KYC on your behalf
4. Collect the application receipt with reference number

> **Tip:** Registration began on September 1, 2024. There is currently no deadline for registration — you can apply anytime.

## How the Money is Disbursed

| Instalment | Amount | Disbursement Date |
|---|---|---|
| 1st instalment | ₹5,000 | On Raksha Bandhan (August) |
| 2nd instalment | ₹5,000 | On International Women's Day (March 8) |
| **Total per year** | **₹10,000** | — |
| **Total over 5 years** | **₹50,000** | 2024–2029 |

- Funds are transferred via **Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)** into your Aadhaar-linked bank account
- Beneficiaries also receive a **Subhadra Debit Card** for seamless digital transactions

### Digital Transaction Bonus

The top 100 beneficiaries in each gram panchayat and urban area with the **highest number of digital transactions** receive an additional **₹500 bonus**.

## How to Check Application Status

1. Visit [subhadra.odisha.gov.in](https://subhadra.odisha.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"Check Status"**
3. Enter your Aadhaar number or application reference number
4. View your application status, e-KYC status, and payment details

## Fees

There are **no fees** to apply for Subhadra Yojana. The application process is completely free — both online and at Mo Seva Kendras.

> ⚠️ **Warning:** Beware of fraudsters charging money for Subhadra Yojana registration. The government does not charge any fees.

## Processing Time

- **e-KYC verification:** 7–15 days after registration
- **First instalment:** Credited on the next scheduled disbursement date (Raksha Bandhan or Women's Day) after successful verification

## Current Status (2025–26)

- The scheme is **actively running** with disbursements ongoing
- Over **50 lakh women** have been registered as beneficiaries since launch
- ₹1,250 crore was transferred to 25+ lakh women on the launch day itself (September 17, 2024)
- The 2025–26 budget includes **₹10,145 crore** allocated for the scheme

## Important Tips

1. **Complete e-KYC immediately** after registration — your application won't be processed without it
2. **Link your Aadhaar** to both your mobile number and bank account before applying
3. **Use the Subhadra Debit Card** for digital transactions to qualify for the ₹500 bonus
4. **Check your status regularly** on the official portal if your payment is delayed
5. **Do not pay anyone** for registration — the process is 100% free

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: What is the official website for Subhadra Yojana?

The official portal is [subhadra.odisha.gov.in](https://subhadra.odisha.gov.in/). All applications, status checks, and beneficiary lists are available here.

### Q2: How much money will I receive under Subhadra Yojana?

You will receive **₹50,000 over 5 years** — that's ₹10,000 per year in two instalments of ₹5,000 each (on Raksha Bandhan and International Women's Day).

### Q3: Is there a deadline to apply for Subhadra Yojana?

Currently, there is **no fixed deadline** for registration. However, to receive benefits for the current financial year, it is advisable to apply and complete e-KYC as early as possible.

### Q4: Can I apply if I already receive benefits under another government scheme?

You are **not eligible** if you receive more than ₹1,500 per month under any other government welfare scheme. If you receive less than that, you can still apply.

### Q5: Do I need to visit a government office to apply?

No. You can apply **online** at subhadra.odisha.gov.in. However, if you need assistance, you can visit any Mo Seva Kendra or Common Service Centre for free help.

### Q6: What if my e-KYC fails?

If your e-KYC fails, you can attempt **Re-eKYC** through the official portal. Ensure your Aadhaar details (name, date of birth) match your bank account details exactly.

### Q7: Is Subhadra Yojana available for women outside Odisha?

No. This scheme is **exclusively for women residents of Odisha**.

### Q8: When was the first payment disbursed?

The first disbursement of ₹5,000 was made on the launch date — **September 17, 2024** — to over 25 lakh registered beneficiaries.
