# CSC VLE ID Forgot — How to Recover Your Digital Seva ID

If you are a Village Level Entrepreneur (VLE) and have forgotten your CSC ID or Digital Seva login credentials, don't worry. This guide walks you through every method to recover your CSC VLE ID and regain access to the Digital Seva portal.

## What is a CSC VLE ID?

Your CSC VLE ID is the unique identifier assigned when you registered as a Village Level Entrepreneur under the Common Services Centre (CSC) scheme. This ID is used to:

- Log in to the **Digital Seva Portal** (digitalseva.csc.gov.in)
- Access government services for citizens
- Track your commissions and wallet balance
- Manage your CSC center operations

Your VLE ID is typically a numeric code (e.g., 8-12 digits) linked to your Aadhaar number and registered email/mobile.

---

## Method 1: Recover CSC VLE ID via Email

If your email is registered with CSC, this is the fastest recovery method.

### Steps:

1. Go to the **Digital Seva Portal**: [https://digitalseva.csc.gov.in/](https://digitalseva.csc.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"Login"** on the homepage
3. On the login page, click **"Forgot Password"** or **"Forgot Username"**
4. Select the **email recovery option**
5. Enter your **registered email address**
6. Click **"Submit"** — a recovery email will be sent
7. Open the email from CSC/Digital Seva (check spam/junk folder too)
8. The email will contain your **VLE ID** and a password reset link
9. Click the link, set a new password, and log in

### If Email Not Received:

- Check **spam/junk** folder
- Ensure you entered the **exact email** used during CSC registration
- Wait 15-30 minutes — sometimes there's a delay
- Try again after clearing browser cache

---

## Method 2: Recover CSC ID via Aadhaar Number

Your CSC VLE ID is linked to your Aadhaar number. You can use this for recovery.

### Steps:

1. Visit the **CSC Registration Portal**: [https://register.csc.gov.in/](https://register.csc.gov.in/)
2. Look for the **"Already Registered?"** or **"Check Application Status"** option
3. Enter your **12-digit Aadhaar number**
4. Complete the **OTP verification** (OTP sent to Aadhaar-linked mobile)
5. Your CSC VLE ID and registration details will be displayed
6. Note down your VLE ID and use it to log in at Digital Seva portal

### If Aadhaar OTP Not Received:

- Ensure your **mobile number is linked to Aadhaar** — check at [https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/)
- If mobile is not linked, visit the nearest **Aadhaar Seva Kendra** to update it
- See our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) for mobile linking help

## Method 3: Contact Your District Manager

If both email and Aadhaar recovery methods fail, contact your CSC District Manager (DM) directly.

### Steps:

1. Visit the **CSC Locator**: [https://locator.csc.gov.in/](https://locator.csc.gov.in/)
2. Select your **State** and **District**
3. Find the **District Manager** contact details listed for your area
4. Call or email the District Manager with the following details:
   - Your full name (as per Aadhaar)
   - Aadhaar number
   - Registered mobile number
   - CSC center address
   - Any old correspondence or registration receipt
5. The DM will verify your identity and share your VLE ID
6. They may also help reset your password

### What to Keep Ready:

- Aadhaar card (original or copy)
- CSC registration acknowledgement (if available)
- Any previous login emails from Digital Seva

## Method 4: Call CSC Helpline

You can also contact the central CSC helpline for assistance.

| Contact Method | Details |
|---|---|
| CSC Helpline | **1800-3000-3468** (toll-free) |
| Email Support | helpdesk@csc.gov.in |
| Working Hours | Monday to Saturday, 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM |

### When Calling:

- Keep your Aadhaar number ready
- Mention your state and district
- Explain that you need VLE ID recovery
- The helpdesk may ask security questions to verify your identity

---

## Method 5: Visit CSC District Office

If remote methods fail, visit your CSC district office in person.

1. Find the nearest **CSC District Office** via [https://locator.csc.gov.in/](https://locator.csc.gov.in/)
2. Carry your **Aadhaar card** and any CSC registration documents
3. The district office staff can look up your VLE ID in the system
4. They will help you reset credentials on the spot

## Common Problems and Fixes

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| "Invalid email" during recovery | You may have registered with a different email — try alternate IDs |
| OTP not coming on mobile | Check if mobile is linked to Aadhaar; wait and retry |
| VLE ID found but password not working | Use "Forgot Password" on Digital Seva login page |
| Account shows "Deactivated" | Contact District Manager — your CSC may need reactivation |
| Multiple VLE IDs linked to same Aadhaar | Contact helpline to identify the active ID |
| Digital Seva portal not loading | Try different browser, clear cache, or use incognito mode |

## Important Tips

1. **Save your VLE ID** in a secure place — write it down or save in your phone contacts
2. **Keep your registered email and mobile updated** with CSC to avoid recovery issues
3. **Never share your VLE ID and password** with anyone — it's linked to your financial transactions
4. **Check your CSC registration email** — your original VLE ID was sent when you first registered
5. **Bookmark the Digital Seva portal** to avoid phishing sites that mimic CSC login pages

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: What is my CSC VLE ID format?
Your CSC VLE ID is typically an 8-12 digit numeric code assigned during registration. It is different from your Aadhaar number or mobile number.

### Q2: Can I recover my CSC ID without Aadhaar?
It is difficult but possible. Contact the CSC helpline (1800-3000-3468) or your District Manager with your registration details and identity proof.

### Q3: I forgot both my CSC ID and password. What should I do?
First recover your VLE ID using any method above (email, Aadhaar, or District Manager). Once you have the ID, use "Forgot Password" on the Digital Seva login page to reset your password.

### Q4: How long does it take to recover a CSC VLE ID?
Email recovery is instant. Aadhaar-based recovery takes 5-10 minutes. District Manager route may take 1-3 working days depending on responsiveness.

### Q5: Is there a fee to recover my CSC VLE ID?
No. CSC VLE ID recovery is completely free. Do not pay anyone who claims to recover your ID for a fee — it is likely a scam.

### Q6: My CSC center was deactivated. Can I still recover my ID?
Yes, you can recover your VLE ID even if the center is deactivated. Contact your District Manager to also discuss reactivation if needed.

### Q7: Can I have multiple CSC VLE IDs?
No. Each VLE is assigned one unique ID linked to their Aadhaar. If you see multiple IDs, contact the helpline to consolidate.
