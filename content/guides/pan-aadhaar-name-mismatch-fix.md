# PAN Aadhaar Name Mismatch? How to Fix Name Difference for Linking

PAN-Aadhaar linking is **mandatory** for all Indian taxpayers. But millions of people can't link because their name on PAN doesn't exactly match their Aadhaar name. This mismatch also blocks ITR filing, refund processing, and bank KYC. Here's how to fix it.

## Common Error Messages

- **"Aadhaar-PAN linking failed. Name on PAN does not match with Aadhaar."**
- **"Your PAN-Aadhaar linking request cannot be processed as the details do not match."**
- **"Date of Birth in PAN and Aadhaar do not match."**
- **"Gender mismatch between PAN and Aadhaar records."**
- **"The Aadhaar number entered is already linked with some other PAN."**
- **"PAN is already linked with another Aadhaar number."**
- **"Your PAN has been made inoperative due to non-linking with Aadhaar."**
- **"Linking failed — demographic mismatch"**

## Types of Mismatches That Block Linking

### 1. Name Spelling Differences
- PAN: **RAJA KUMAR** vs Aadhaar: **RAJAKUMAR**
- PAN: **S. KRISHNAN** vs Aadhaar: **SHANKAR KRISHNAN**
- PAN: **MOHAMMAD** vs Aadhaar: **MUHAMMED**

### 2. Name Order Differences
- PAN: **SHARMA RAJESH** vs Aadhaar: **RAJESH SHARMA**
- PAN: **K RAVI** vs Aadhaar: **RAVI KUMAR**

### 3. Middle Name Present/Absent
- PAN: **ANITA SINGH** vs Aadhaar: **ANITA KUMARI SINGH**

### 4. Date of Birth Mismatch
- PAN: **15/06/1990** vs Aadhaar: **15/06/1989**

### 5. Gender Mismatch
- Rare but happens due to clerical errors

## How to Fix the Mismatch

You have two options: **correct PAN details** or **correct Aadhaar details**. Choose whichever is easier based on which document has the correct information.

### Option A: Correct Your PAN Details

If your Aadhaar has the correct name and PAN has errors:

**Online Process:**
1. Visit [onlineservices.nsdl.com](https://www.onlineservices.nsdl.com/paam/endUserRegisterContact.html) (for NSDL-issued PAN)
   OR [utiitsl.com](https://www.utiitsl.com/UTIITSL/site/pan/PanOnlineApp) (for UTIITSL-issued PAN)
2. Select **"Changes or Correction in existing PAN Data"**
3. Fill the form with your **correct name** (matching Aadhaar)
4. Upload supporting documents:
   - Aadhaar card (as identity proof)
   - Passport or voter ID (additional proof)
5. Fee: **₹107** (including GST) for Indian address
6. Submit and pay online
7. New PAN card with corrected details will be dispatched in **15-20 working days**
8. Your PAN number remains the same — only the details change

**Offline Process:**
1. Download **Form for PAN Change Request** from NSDL/UTIITSL website
2. Fill in the corrected details
3. Attach self-attested copies of identity proof
4. Submit at nearest PAN center or mail to NSDL/UTIITSL
5. Fee: ₹107

### Option B: Correct Your Aadhaar Details

If your PAN has the correct name and Aadhaar has errors:

1. Visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in)
2. Login with Aadhaar + OTP
3. Click **"Update Demographics"** (only address is available online)
4. For **name correction**, visit the nearest **Aadhaar Enrolment Centre**
5. Carry supporting documents:
   - PAN card
   - Passport
   - 10th marksheet
6. Fee: ₹50 at enrolment centre
7. Update takes **5-10 working days**

See our detailed guide: [Aadhaar Demographic Update Rejected Fix](/guides/aadhaar-demographic-update-rejected-fix)

### Option C: Try Linking Despite Minor Mismatch

For very minor differences (like spacing, initials), linking might still work:

1. Go to [incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/)
2. Click **"Link Aadhaar"** under Quick Links
3. Enter your PAN, Aadhaar number, and name **exactly as on Aadhaar**
4. Check **"I have only year of birth in Aadhaar"** if applicable
5. The system tolerates minor variations — try linking before correcting

## After Fixing: How to Link PAN with Aadhaar

Once names match:

1. Visit [incometax.gov.in](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/)
2. Click **"Link Aadhaar"**
3. Enter PAN number, Aadhaar number, and name as per Aadhaar
4. Enter mobile number for OTP
5. Pay **₹1,000** fee (if linking after the deadline — late fee applicable)
6. Verify OTP
7. Linking request submitted — takes **3-7 working days** to process
8. Check status: Go to **"Link Aadhaar Status"** on the same portal

## What Happens If You Don't Link PAN with Aadhaar?

If your PAN is not linked with Aadhaar:
- PAN becomes **inoperative** — it cannot be used for any financial transactions
- **Cannot file ITR** — income tax returns will be rejected
- **Cannot receive tax refunds** — pending refunds will be held
- **Higher TDS** — TDS will be deducted at **20% instead of regular rates**
- **Cannot open bank accounts or demat accounts** with that PAN
- **Mutual fund investments** may face issues

### How to Reactivate Inoperative PAN
1. First fix the name mismatch (Steps above)
2. Link PAN with Aadhaar successfully
3. Pay ₹1,000 late fee
4. PAN will be reactivated within **30 days** of successful linking

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I check if my PAN is already linked with Aadhaar?
Visit [incometax.gov.in](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/) and click **"Link Aadhaar Status"**. Enter your PAN and Aadhaar number. It will show whether linking is complete, pending, or failed.

### Can I link PAN with Aadhaar if names partially match?
Yes, the Income Tax system uses **fuzzy matching** and tolerates minor differences like spacing, initials, and common abbreviations. Try linking first before making corrections.

### My PAN is linked to a different Aadhaar. How to fix?
This is a serious issue. Contact the **Income Tax helpdesk at 1800-103-0025** immediately. You may need to visit your Assessing Officer with both PAN and Aadhaar originals to resolve this.

### How much does it cost to correct PAN or Aadhaar details?
PAN correction costs **₹107** (online). Aadhaar correction at an enrolment centre costs **₹50**. After linking (with late fee), it's ₹1,000 additionally.

### Can I file ITR without linking PAN and Aadhaar?
No, the **Income Tax portal will not allow ITR filing** if your PAN is inoperative due to non-linking. You must complete the linking process first.

### I changed my name after marriage. Which document should I correct?
Correct **both PAN and Aadhaar** to reflect your married name. Update Aadhaar first (using marriage certificate), then update PAN to match. Then link them.
