# PNG/CNG Connection Apply Online: Process & Documents

Piped Natural Gas (PNG) is a cleaner, safer and more economical alternative to LPG cylinders for home cooking. If your area is covered under City Gas Distribution (CGD), you can apply for a PNG connection online. This guide explains the complete process for applying through major gas distribution companies like IGL, MGL, GAIL Gas and Adani Gas.

## What is PNG Connection?

PNG (Piped Natural Gas) delivers natural gas directly to your kitchen through underground pipelines. Unlike LPG cylinders, there is no need for booking, delivery or storage. The gas supply is continuous, and you pay only for what you use based on meter readings.

**Key benefits:**
- No cylinder booking or waiting
- Continuous gas supply 24×7
- Cheaper than LPG (₹5-8 per unit savings approximately)
- Safer — lighter than air, disperses quickly
- Metered billing — pay for actual usage

## Who is Eligible?

- Any residential household in a CGD-authorized area
- The applicant must be the owner or authorized tenant of the property
- Valid address proof of the property is required
- Commercial establishments (restaurants, hotels) can also apply for commercial PNG connections

## Documents Required

### For Residential Connection
- **Identity Proof** — [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online), PAN card, Voter ID, Passport
- **Address Proof** — Aadhaar, electricity bill, property tax receipt, registered sale deed
- **Property Ownership Proof** — Sale deed, allotment letter, property tax receipt
- **For Tenants** — NOC from property owner + rent agreement
- **Passport-size photograph** — 2 copies
- **Cancelled cheque or bank passbook copy** (for auto-debit facility)

### For Commercial Connection
- All above documents
- GST registration certificate
- Business registration or trade licence
- Fire NOC (for restaurants)

## How to Apply for PNG Connection Online

### Step 1: Visit Your Local Gas Company Portal
Go to the official website of your area's gas distribution company:
- IGL: [iglonline.net](https://www.iglonline.net/)
- MGL: [mahanagargas.com](https://www.mahanagargas.com/)
- Adani Gas: [adanigas.com](https://adanigas.com/)
- GAIL Gas: [gailgas.com](https://gailgas.com/)

### Step 2: Select "New Connection" or "Apply Online"
Click on the new domestic/commercial PNG connection option on the homepage.

### Step 3: Fill the Application Form
Enter your details:
- Full name, mobile number, email
- Complete address with PIN code
- Property type (owned/rented)
- Type of connection (domestic/commercial)

### Step 4: Upload Documents
Upload scanned copies of identity proof, address proof and property documents.

### Step 5: Pay the Registration Fee
Pay the connection charges online via net banking, UPI or debit/credit card.

### Step 6: Submit Application
After payment, submit the form. You will receive an application reference number via SMS and email.

### Step 7: Site Survey
A company representative will visit your premises for a feasibility survey within 7-15 days.

### Step 8: Installation
After survey approval, the company schedules installation of the pipeline, meter and regulator.

## How to Apply Offline

1. Visit the nearest customer care centre of your gas company
2. Collect and fill the application form
3. Attach required documents
4. Pay the registration fee
5. A representative will schedule site visit and installation

## Important Tips

1. **Check coverage first** — Confirm your area and building is covered before applying. Use the PIN code checker on the company website.
2. **Keep NOC ready if tenant** — Landlord's NOC is mandatory for rented properties.
3. **EMI option** — Many companies offer 0% EMI on installation charges through credit cards.
4. **Existing LPG** — You can surrender your LPG connection after PNG installation to get the LPG security deposit refund.
5. **Safety inspection** — The company conducts periodic safety checks. Always allow access for inspection.

## CNG Connection for Vehicles

CNG connections for vehicles are available at CNG stations. No separate application is needed — simply visit any CNG station to refuel. For fleet operators needing dedicated CNG infrastructure, contact your local CGD company directly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Is PNG cheaper than LPG?
Yes. PNG costs approximately ₹30-45 per SCM (Standard Cubic Metre). An average household uses 10-15 SCM per month, costing ₹300-675 compared to ₹800-900 for an LPG cylinder.

### Q2: Can I apply for PNG if I live in a rented house?
Yes, but you need a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the property owner along with the rent agreement.

### Q3: Is the security deposit refundable?
Yes. The security deposit (₹1,500-2,000) is fully refundable when you surrender the connection.

### Q4: What if PNG pipeline is not available in my area?
You can register your interest on the company website. Once the pipeline reaches your area, you will be contacted. Check [PNGRB](https://www.pngrb.gov.in/) for CGD authorization details.

### Q5: Is PNG safe for high-rise buildings?
Yes. PNG is supplied through regulated pressure pipelines and is safe for high-rise buildings. Natural gas is lighter than air, so it disperses quickly in case of any leak.

### Q6: Can I have both PNG and LPG connections?
Yes, there is no restriction. However, PNG eliminates the need for LPG in most cases.

### Q7: How is billing done for PNG?
Billing is based on meter reading, similar to [electricity bills](/guide/electricity-bill-view-download). You receive monthly or bi-monthly bills that can be paid online.
