# BSNL Recharge Not Working — How to Fix Online Recharge Errors

BSNL recharge failures are frustrating — whether on the official portal, through UPI apps like Paytm, PhonePe, or Google Pay, or via third-party platforms. This guide covers every common BSNL recharge problem and how to fix it, including getting refunds when money is deducted but recharge doesn't go through.

## Common BSNL Recharge Error Messages

You may encounter these errors when trying to recharge your BSNL number:

- **"Payment Failed"** — transaction declined by bank or UPI app
- **"Recharge Failed — Please try again later"** — BSNL server issue
- **"Invalid Mobile Number"** — number may be deactivated or wrong circle selected
- **"Service temporarily unavailable"** — BSNL portal downtime
- **"Transaction successful but recharge pending"** — money deducted, recharge not applied
- **"This number is not eligible for recharge"** — SIM validity expired or number surrendered
- **"Payment gateway error"** — BSNL portal's payment system is down

## Fix 1: BSNL Portal (portal.bsnl.in) Recharge Failures

The BSNL official portal often faces technical issues. Try these steps:

1. **Clear browser cache and cookies** — old session data causes payment failures
2. **Use Chrome or Firefox** — BSNL portal works poorly on Safari and older browsers
3. **Disable ad blockers and VPN** — these can interfere with the payment gateway
4. **Try a different payment method** — if credit card fails, try debit card or net banking
5. **Check BSNL portal status** — visit [portal.bsnl.in](https://portal.bsnl.in/) and check if the site loads; if not, the server is down
6. **Try during off-peak hours** — BSNL portal is less loaded between 10 PM and 8 AM
7. **Use the BSNL Recharge URL directly** — go to [pgw.bsnl.co.in/bsnlportal/](https://pgw.bsnl.co.in/bsnlportal/) instead of the main portal

> **Tip:** If the BSNL portal keeps failing, use the My BSNL app or third-party apps as alternatives (see below).

## Fix 2: BSNL Recharge Failed via Paytm, PhonePe, or Google Pay

UPI app recharge failures for BSNL are common. Here's how to fix them:

### Check These First
- **Verify the mobile number** — ensure you entered the correct 10-digit BSNL number
- **Select the right operator** — some apps auto-detect the operator incorrectly; manually select "BSNL" or "BSNL Special Tariff" if available
- **Check your UPI/bank balance** — insufficient funds cause silent failures
- **Update the app** — outdated versions may have broken BSNL integration

### If Payment Goes Through But Recharge Fails
1. Check your bank statement or UPI app transaction history
2. Wait 30 minutes — some recharges are delayed during high traffic
3. If recharge doesn't apply within 1 hour, raise a complaint with the UPI app (see refund section below)

### App-Specific Tips
- **Paytm:** Go to Passbook → check transaction status → raise issue if failed
- **PhonePe:** Go to History → tap transaction → "Need Help" → select "Recharge not received"
- **Google Pay:** Go to Activity → tap transaction → "Get help with this payment"

## Fix 3: Money Deducted But Recharge Not Done — How to Get Refund

This is the most common complaint. Follow these steps:

### Step 1: Wait for Auto-Reversal
Most failed transactions are auto-reversed within **24-72 hours** (3-5 working days for some banks). Check your bank account/UPI app after this period.

### Step 2: Raise a Complaint with BSNL
If money was deducted and recharge not received after 24 hours:

1. **Call BSNL Customer Care:** Dial **1503** (from BSNL number) or **1800-180-1503** (toll-free from any number)
2. **Provide details:** Your BSNL number, transaction ID, date, amount, and payment method
3. **Get a complaint number** — note it down for follow-up

### Step 3: Raise a Complaint with Your Payment App/Bank
- For UPI payments: File a dispute through the UPI app (within 30 days of transaction)
- For card/net banking: Contact your bank's customer care with the transaction reference number

### Step 4: Escalate if No Resolution
- **BSNL PG Portal:** File a grievance at [pgportal.gov.in](https://pgportal.gov.in/) under Department of Telecommunications
- **RBI Ombudsman:** For payment-related disputes unresolved after 30 days, file at [cms.rbi.org.in](https://cms.rbi.org.in/)

## Fix 4: BSNL Number Not Accepting Recharge (Validity Expired)

If your BSNL number shows "invalid" or "not eligible for recharge," the SIM validity may have expired.

### Understanding BSNL Validity
- **Plan validity expired:** You can still receive calls for a grace period (usually 15-30 days) but cannot make calls or use data
- **Grace period expired:** Number enters "suspended" state — you can still recharge to reactivate
- **After 90 days of suspension:** Number may be permanently disconnected and cannot be recovered

### How to Reactivate an Expired BSNL Prepaid Number

1. **Visit the nearest BSNL Customer Service Centre (CSC)** — this is the most reliable method
2. Carry your **Aadhaar card** and the **SIM card** in question
3. Request reactivation — the BSNL staff will check if the number is still recoverable
4. **Pay the reactivation charges** — typically ₹20-50 plus a recharge of minimum ₹100-200
5. Your number will be reactivated within **2-4 hours**

> **Important:** If your BSNL number has been disconnected for more than 90 days, it may have been allocated to someone else. Visit the BSNL office early to check status.

You can also try:
- **Dialing 1503** from another phone and requesting reactivation status
- **Visiting [portal.bsnl.in](https://portal.bsnl.in/)** and checking if the number accepts recharge — if it does, a successful recharge will reactivate the SIM

## Fix 5: BSNL Broadband / Fibre Bill Payment Issues

BSNL landline, broadband, and Bharat Fibre bill payment failures have different causes:

### Common Issues
- **"Invalid Account Number"** — use your landline number with STD code (e.g., 04412345678) as account ID
- **Portal shows old bill** — BSNL billing system updates are delayed; check after the 5th of the month
- **Payment received but service not restored** — service restoration takes 2-4 hours after payment

### How to Pay BSNL Broadband/Fibre Bills
1. **BSNL Portal:** [portal.bsnl.in](https://portal.bsnl.in/) → Landline/Broadband → Enter number with STD code
2. **My BSNL App:** Download from Play Store/App Store → login with your number
3. **UPI Apps:** Paytm/PhonePe → Bill Payment → Broadband → BSNL
4. **BSNL CSC:** Pay at any BSNL Customer Service Centre with cash

> **Tip:** For Bharat Fibre connections, use the landline number (not a mobile number) for bill payments on any platform.

## Important Tips

1. **Always save transaction IDs** — take a screenshot of every payment attempt for dispute resolution
2. **Check BSNL server status first** — if portal.bsnl.in is down, no online recharge method will work via BSNL's system
3. **Recharge before validity expires** — once your number enters grace period, some plans may not be available
4. **Use BSNL's own app** — the My BSNL app has better success rates than the web portal
5. **Keep your BSNL number active** — recharge at least once every 90 days to prevent disconnection

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is my BSNL recharge not working?
BSNL recharge can fail due to server downtime on portal.bsnl.in, incorrect operator selection in UPI apps, expired SIM validity, or payment gateway issues. Try an alternative method like the My BSNL app or a local retailer.

### Money was deducted but BSNL recharge not received. What should I do?
Wait 24-72 hours for auto-reversal. If the money is not returned, call BSNL at 1800-180-1503 with your transaction ID. Also raise a dispute through your UPI app or bank.

### How do I reactivate my expired BSNL prepaid number?
Visit the nearest BSNL Customer Service Centre with your Aadhaar card. If the number has been suspended for less than 90 days, it can be reactivated by paying reactivation charges and doing a minimum recharge.

### Can I recharge a BSNL number from another state?
Yes, BSNL recharge works across circles. However, on some apps, you may need to manually select the correct BSNL circle (state) for the recharge to go through.

### Why does the BSNL portal show "Service Unavailable"?
The BSNL portal (portal.bsnl.in) frequently faces downtime, especially during peak hours. Try again during off-peak hours (late night or early morning) or use the My BSNL app instead.

### How long does a BSNL recharge take to activate?
Typically, BSNL prepaid recharges activate within 5-10 minutes. During server issues, it may take up to 4 hours. If not activated after 4 hours, contact 1503.

### Is there a BSNL recharge app?
Yes, the official **My BSNL** app is available on Google Play Store and Apple App Store. It supports prepaid recharge, postpaid bill payment, broadband bill payment, and plan browsing.

### How do I check my BSNL balance and validity?
Dial **\*123#** from your BSNL number or use the My BSNL app. You can also call **1503** and follow the IVR menu for balance and validity information.
