# Sikkim Government Schemes and Services – Complete Guide

Sikkim, India's first fully organic state and one of the cleanest in the Northeast, offers a remarkably progressive set of welfare schemes for its residents. Governed by the Sikkim Krantikari Morcha (SKM) under Chief Minister **Prem Singh Tamang (P.S. Golay)**, the state has pioneered policies like universal free healthcare, guaranteed government employment per family, and 100% organic agriculture — programmes that are unique even by national standards.

This comprehensive hub covers every major Sikkim government scheme, service portal, and benefit programme to help residents, students, and job seekers access what they're entitled to.

## Employment Schemes

### One Family One Job (OFOJ) Scheme

Sikkim's flagship employment guarantee — one of the most ambitious state-level job programmes in India. Under OFOJ, **one member from every family** that has no government employee is given a regular government job.

**Key details:**
- Salary of ₹20,000–₹25,000 per month for appointees
- Applicable to families with no existing government employee
- Covers posts across departments — clerks, assistants, drivers, peons, and field staff
- Appointments made through district-level screening committees
- Candidates must be Sikkim Subject Certificate (SSC) holders or Certificate of Identification (COI) holders

**How to apply:** Applications are invited through district administration notifications published in local newspapers and the state portal.

### Chief Minister's Self Employment Scheme (CMSES)

Designed to promote entrepreneurship among Sikkim's youth, CMSES provides **subsidised loans and capital support** for starting small businesses.

**Benefits:**
- Loan subsidy up to ₹15–20 lakh for eligible businesses
- Covers sectors like tourism, hospitality, agriculture, food processing, and handicrafts
- Back-ended capital subsidy (typically 25–50% of project cost)
- Training and handholding support through district industries centres

**Eligibility:** Sikkim domicile, age 18–45, valid business plan in approved sectors.

## Agriculture and Organic Farming

### Sikkim Organic Mission

Sikkim became **India's first 100% organic state** in 2016 after a phased transition that began in 2003. All farming in Sikkim is now certified organic — chemical pesticides and fertilisers are completely banned.

**Key features:**
- Complete ban on chemical fertilisers, pesticides, and GMO seeds statewide
- Over 75,000 hectares of farmland converted to organic certification
- Government provides free organic inputs — vermicompost, bio-fertilisers, and organic seeds
- Premium pricing support for organic produce in national and international markets
- Won the UN Future Policy Gold Award (2018) for best policies promoting agroecology
- Organic farming training through Sikkim State Institute of Rural Development

**Support for farmers:**
- Subsidy on organic inputs and farm equipment
- Free soil health cards
- Market linkage through organic festivals and Sikkim Organic Markets
- Cold storage and packaging infrastructure at district level

## Housing Schemes

### Sikkim Garib Awas Yojana (SGAY)

The state counterpart to central housing schemes, SGAY provides **financial assistance to BPL families** for constructing pucca houses.

**Benefits:**
- Grant of ₹3–5 lakh per household for house construction
- Priority for SC/ST, widows, disabled persons, and families affected by natural disasters
- Convergence with PMAY-G for additional central funding
- Technical support and approved house designs from the Housing Department

**Eligibility:**
- Must be a Sikkim domicile (SSC/COI holder)
- BPL family or houseless/kutcha house dweller
- Not availed similar housing benefit from any other scheme

**How to apply:** Through Block Development Officers (BDOs) or the District Collector's office. Applications are also accepted during special housing drives.

## Healthcare

### Free Healthcare for All Residents

Sikkim provides **universal free healthcare** at all government hospitals and health centres — a distinction very few Indian states can claim.

**What's covered:**
- Free OPD consultations, diagnostics, and inpatient treatment at all government facilities
- Free essential medicines through the state's drug distribution system
- Free surgeries including major operations at STNM Hospital (Gangtok) and district hospitals
- Referral support to AIIMS and other tertiary centres for complex cases — travel and treatment costs often covered
- Free ambulance service (108 helpline)
- Comprehensive immunisation and maternal health programmes

**Key facilities:**
- Sir Thutob Namgyal Memorial (STNM) Hospital, Gangtok — state's premier hospital
- District hospitals in Namchi, Mangan, Gyalshing, and Singtam
- Primary Health Centres (PHCs) across all blocks

### Sikkim State Health Insurance

Beyond free government healthcare, the state also participates in **Ayushman Bharat – PMJAY** for treatment at empanelled private hospitals with coverage up to ₹5 lakh per family per year.

## Education and Scholarships

### Chief Minister's Meritorious Scholarship Scheme

Financial support for meritorious students from Sikkim pursuing higher education.

**Benefits:**
- Scholarship for students who score above prescribed cut-offs in Class 10 and Class 12 board exams
- Covers tuition, hostel fees, and maintenance allowance for studies within and outside Sikkim
- Special provisions for students pursuing professional courses — engineering, medicine, law, and management
- Additional merit-cum-means scholarships for economically weaker sections

### Other Education Schemes

- **Free education** up to college level in government institutions
- **State post-matric scholarships** for SC/ST/OBC students (converges with central post-matric scholarship)
- **Laptop distribution scheme** for college students
- **Textbook and uniform grant** for government school students up to Class 12

## e-District Sikkim – Online Services

The **e-District Sikkim** portal is the primary digital gateway for residents to access government certificates and services without visiting offices.

**Services available online:**
- Income Certificate
- Domicile / Residential Certificate
- Caste Certificate (SC/ST/OBC)
- Character Certificate
- Inner Line Permit (ILP) for restricted areas
- Trade licences and NOCs
- Birth and Death Certificate requests
- Land and revenue services

**How to use:**
1. Visit [edistrict.sikkim.gov.in](https://edistrict.sikkim.gov.in)
2. Register with mobile number and Aadhaar
3. Select the required service and fill the application
4. Upload supporting documents
5. Track application status online

## Sikkim State Lottery

The Sikkim State Lottery is one of India's oldest and most popular legal lottery systems, operated by the Directorate of State Lotteries.

**Key details:**
- Multiple daily draws — Dear Morning, Dear Day, Dear Evening (typically at 11:55 AM, 4:00 PM, and 8:00 PM)
- Ticket price starting from ₹6
- First prize typically ₹1 crore; consolation and smaller prizes also available
- Results published on [sikkimlottery.com](https://sikkimlottery.com) and in local newspapers
- Revenue generated supports state welfare programmes

**Important:** Only purchase tickets from authorised agents. Verify results on the official website.

## Government Jobs and SPSC Exams

### Sikkim Public Service Commission (SPSC)

SPSC conducts recruitment examinations for Group A and Group B gazetted posts in the Sikkim state government.

**Major exams:**
- Sikkim State Civil Services (Combined) Examination
- Judicial Service Examination
- Medical Officer recruitment
- Engineering Services
- Various departmental exams

**How to apply:**
1. Visit [spsc.sikkim.gov.in](https://spsc.sikkim.gov.in)
2. Check current notifications under "Advertisements"
3. Apply online or offline as per notification instructions
4. Download admit cards from the portal

**Other recruitment:** Non-gazetted posts are recruited through the **Sikkim Subordinate Services Selection Board** and departmental notifications.

## Tourism and Hospitality Schemes

Sikkim's tourism sector is a major employer and the state offers several support schemes:

- **Homestay Scheme:** Financial support up to ₹5 lakh for families to set up registered homestays — covers construction, furnishing, and amenity upgrades
- **Tourism Infrastructure Development:** Grants for hotels, resorts, and tourism units in notified tourist areas
- **Adventure Tourism Subsidy:** Support for trekking, river rafting, paragliding, and mountaineering operators
- **Eco-Tourism Initiative:** Funding for community-based ecotourism projects in village clusters
- **Skill development** in hospitality through state-funded training programmes

## Tribal Welfare and Social Security

### Tribal Welfare Schemes

Sikkim has a significant tribal population (Bhutia, Lepcha, and other scheduled tribes), and the state runs dedicated programmes:

- **Tribal Sub-Plan (TSP):** Earmarked budget allocation for tribal development in education, health, and livelihoods
- **Scheduled Tribe scholarships** for pre-matric and post-matric students
- **Land protection laws** preventing alienation of tribal land
- **Cultural preservation grants** for monasteries, traditional institutions, and indigenous festivals
- **Limboo-Tamang reservation** benefits in education and government employment

### State Socio Economic Census

The **State Socio Economic Census (SSEC)** is Sikkim's own household survey — separate from the central SECC — used to identify beneficiaries for state welfare schemes.

- Updated periodically by the Department of Economics, Statistics, Monitoring and Evaluation
- Forms the basis for BPL identification and scheme targeting in the state
- Residents can request inclusion/correction through the Block Development Officer

### Other Social Security

- **Old Age Pension:** Monthly pension for senior citizens (60+) under state and national schemes
- **Widow Pension:** Financial assistance for widows with no other income source
- **Disability Pension:** Monthly support for persons with disabilities
- **State food security** through subsidised ration distribution

## How to Apply for Sikkim Government Schemes

Most Sikkim government schemes follow a common application process:

1. **Check eligibility** — Visit the relevant department page on [sikkim.gov.in](https://www.sikkim.gov.in) or contact the Block Development Officer
2. **Gather documents** — Sikkim Subject Certificate or COI, Aadhaar, income certificate, caste certificate, bank passbook, passport photos
3. **Apply online or offline** — Use [e-District Sikkim](https://edistrict.sikkim.gov.in) for certificate services; for scheme-specific applications, apply through the BDO or district office
4. **Track status** — Use the e-District portal or CM Dashboard for application tracking
5. **Collect benefit** — Funds are transferred via DBT to your linked bank account; physical benefits distributed through block offices

> **Tip:** Keep your Aadhaar linked to your bank account for faster Direct Benefit Transfer. See our guide on [Aadhaar-Bank Linking](/guide/aadhaar-bank-linking-guide).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for the One Family One Job scheme?
Any Sikkim domicile family (SSC/COI holders) where no member is currently employed in government service is eligible. Applications are screened at the district level and appointments are made based on vacancies.

### Is healthcare really free in Sikkim?
Yes. All government hospitals and health centres in Sikkim provide free consultations, diagnostics, medicines, and surgeries to residents. For treatment outside the state, referral support with financial assistance is available for serious cases.

### How do I apply for Sikkim Garib Awas Yojana?
Approach your Block Development Officer with proof of BPL status, domicile certificate, and Aadhaar. Applications are processed through the Housing Department and convergence with PMAY-G is facilitated automatically.

### Can I use chemical fertilisers for farming in Sikkim?
No. Sikkim has completely banned chemical fertilisers, pesticides, and GMO seeds. All farming must follow organic methods. The state provides free organic inputs and training to farmers.

### How do I check Sikkim State Lottery results?
Visit [sikkimlottery.com](https://sikkimlottery.com) after the scheduled draw times. Results are also published in local newspapers. Always verify from the official website before claiming prizes.

### What services are available on e-District Sikkim?
Income certificates, domicile certificates, caste certificates, character certificates, Inner Line Permits, trade licences, and various other documents can be applied for and tracked online at [edistrict.sikkim.gov.in](https://edistrict.sikkim.gov.in).

### How do I apply for SPSC exams?
Visit [spsc.sikkim.gov.in](https://spsc.sikkim.gov.in), check the current advertisements section, and follow the application instructions in the notification. Most exams now accept online applications.
