# Aadhaar Enrollment Centre Near Me — Find & Book Appointment

Whether you need a new Aadhaar card, want to update your details, or need to link your mobile number, you'll need to visit an Aadhaar Enrollment Center. Here's how to find one near you and book an appointment.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent platform and is not affiliated with UIDAI.

## How to Find Aadhaar Enrollment Centers

### Method 1: UIDAI Appointment Portal (Recommended)
1. Visit [appointments.uidai.gov.in](https://appointments.uidai.gov.in)
2. Select your **State** and **District**
3. You'll see a list of all active enrollment centers with:
   - Center name and address
   - Working hours
   - Services available
   - Option to book appointment

### Method 2: UIDAI Bhuvan Map
1. Visit [bhuvan.nrsc.gov.in/aadhaar](https://bhuvan.nrsc.gov.in/aadhaar)
2. Allow location access or search for your area
3. The map shows all Aadhaar centers near your location
4. Click on a pin to see center details

### Method 3: Google Maps
1. Open Google Maps on your phone
2. Search **"Aadhaar enrollment center near me"** or **"Aadhaar seva kendra near me"**
3. Browse results with ratings and reviews
4. Check opening hours before visiting

### Method 4: Helpline
- Call **1947** (UIDAI toll-free helpline)
- Ask for nearest enrollment center in your area

## How to Book an Appointment Online

Booking an appointment helps you avoid long queues:

1. Visit [appointments.uidai.gov.in](https://appointments.uidai.gov.in)
2. Click **"Book Appointment"**
3. Select your **City/Location**
4. Choose a **center** from the list
5. Select **appointment type:**
   - New Aadhaar Enrollment
   - Aadhaar Update (name, address, mobile, DOB, photo, etc.)
6. Pick a **date and time slot**
7. Enter your **mobile number** and **captcha**
8. You'll receive an **appointment confirmation** via SMS
9. **Save the appointment number** — you'll need it at the center

**Tip:** Book 2-3 days in advance. Same-day appointments are usually not available.

## Walk-In Process (Without Appointment)

If you can't book online or need urgent service:

1. Visit the nearest Aadhaar center during working hours
2. Check the **queue** — some centers have token machines
3. Fill the **enrollment/update form** (available at the center)
4. Submit your documents and complete biometrics
5. **Expected wait time:** 30 minutes to 3 hours depending on crowd

**Best time for walk-in:** Early morning (9-10 AM) or late afternoon (3-4 PM)

## Documents to Carry

### For New Aadhaar Enrollment:

**Proof of Identity (any one):**
- Passport
- PAN Card
- Voter ID
- Driving License
- Government photo ID
- NREGA Job Card

**Proof of Address (any one):**
- Passport
- Bank statement/passbook
- Voter ID
- Utility bill (electricity, water, gas — not older than 3 months)
- Ration card
- Rent agreement with NOC

**Proof of Date of Birth (any one):**
- Birth certificate
- Passport
- SSLC/10th marksheet
- PAN card

**For children below 5:** Parent's Aadhaar card + birth certificate

### For Aadhaar Update:
- Original Aadhaar card or Aadhaar number (mandatory)
- Supporting document for the field being updated
- For mobile update: No document needed (biometric verification only)

## What Happens at the Center

1. **Token/Queue:** Get a token number or join the queue
2. **Form filling:** Fill enrollment/update form with correct details
3. **Document verification:** Operator checks your documents
4. **Biometric capture:**
   - All 10 fingerprints
   - Iris scan (both eyes)
   - Photograph
5. **Data entry:** Operator enters your details
6. **Verification:** Review the screen and confirm all details
7. **Acknowledgment:** You get a receipt with:
   - Enrollment ID (EID) for new enrollment
   - Update Request Number (URN) for updates

## Fees

| Service | Fee |
|---------|-----|
| New Aadhaar Enrollment | **Free** |
| Any Aadhaar Update | **₹50** |
| Reprint/duplicate Aadhaar | **₹50** |
| e-Aadhaar download | **Free** (online) |

**Important:** If a center charges more than ₹50 for updates or any fee for new enrollment, report it to UIDAI at 1947.

## After Your Visit

### For New Enrollment:
- Aadhaar generation takes **15-30 days**
- Track status at [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in) using EID
- Aadhaar card is sent by post to your registered address
- Download e-Aadhaar online once generated

### For Updates:
- Updates reflect in **7-15 working days**
- Track at [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in) using URN
- Download updated e-Aadhaar after the update is complete

## Tips for a Smooth Visit

1. **Book appointment online** — saves hours of waiting
2. **Go early morning** — centers are least crowded at opening time
3. **Carry original + photocopy** of all documents
4. **Keep hands clean and dry** — helps with fingerprint capture
5. **Verify all details on screen** before the operator submits
6. **Keep the acknowledgment slip safe** — you'll need EID/URN to track
7. **Avoid month-end and Mondays** — these are the busiest days
8. **Carry ₹50 in cash** for update fees

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I find Aadhaar enrollment center near me?
Visit appointments.uidai.gov.in, select your state and district to see all centers. You can also search "Aadhaar center near me" on Google Maps or call 1947.

### Can I walk in to Aadhaar center without appointment?
Yes, most Aadhaar centers accept walk-ins. However, you may need to wait 1-3 hours. Booking an appointment online is recommended to save time.

### Is Aadhaar enrollment free?
Yes, new Aadhaar enrollment is completely free. Updates (name, address, mobile, etc.) cost ₹50 per update. If a center charges for new enrollment, report it.

### What are Aadhaar center working hours?
Most Aadhaar centers work Monday to Saturday, 9:30 AM to 5:30 PM. Some bank-based centers may have shorter hours. Check specific timings on the UIDAI portal.

### How long does Aadhaar enrollment take at the center?
The actual process takes 10-15 minutes. Waiting in queue may add 30 minutes to 3 hours. With an appointment, total time is usually under 30 minutes.

### Can I update Aadhaar at any center in India?
Yes, you can visit any Aadhaar enrollment center in India regardless of your registered address. Aadhaar is not location-specific.

### What if the nearest Aadhaar center is very far?
Check if your nearest post office offers Aadhaar services. India Post has set up Aadhaar centers in many post offices, especially in rural areas. Call 1947 for guidance.

### Do I need to carry original documents to Aadhaar center?
Yes, carry original documents for verification along with photocopies that will be retained. The operator verifies originals and keeps the copies.
