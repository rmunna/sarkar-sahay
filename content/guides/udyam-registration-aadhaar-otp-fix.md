# Udyam Registration Aadhaar OTP Not Coming? Fix It Now

The [Udyam registration portal](https://udyamregistration.gov.in/) requires Aadhaar-based OTP verification to complete MSME registration. If you're not receiving the OTP, this guide will help you identify the exact problem and fix it.

## Why Aadhaar OTP Is Required for Udyam

Udyam registration uses UIDAI's e-KYC system to verify your identity. The OTP is sent to the **mobile number linked to your Aadhaar** (not your current mobile number). This is a common source of confusion.

## Problem 1: Mobile Number Not Linked to Aadhaar

This is the **#1 reason** for OTP failure during Udyam registration.

### How to Check

1. Visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/)
2. Click **"Verify Aadhaar"**
3. Enter your Aadhaar number and captcha
4. If it shows a partially masked mobile number, note the last 4 digits
5. If no mobile number is linked, you need to update it first

### How to Link Mobile Number to Aadhaar

1. **Visit your nearest Aadhaar Enrolment Centre** — This cannot be done online
2. Carry your [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) and your mobile phone with the new SIM
3. Fill the Aadhaar Update/Correction Form
4. Provide biometric verification (fingerprint + iris)
5. Pay **₹50** update fee
6. You'll receive an acknowledgment slip
7. Mobile number will be updated in **7–15 working days**
8. Find nearest centre: [appointments.uidai.gov.in](https://appointments.uidai.gov.in/)

> **Important:** There is no online method to add or change the mobile number linked to Aadhaar. Anyone claiming to do it online is fraudulent.

## Problem 2: OTP Delayed or Not Delivered

If your mobile number is correctly linked but OTP isn't arriving:

### Quick Fixes

1. **Wait 2–3 minutes** — During peak hours (10 AM – 5 PM), OTP delivery can be delayed
2. **Check signal strength** — Move to an area with better network coverage
3. **Check SMS inbox carefully** — Search for sender "UIDAI" or any 6-digit code
4. **Check spam/blocked messages** — Some phones block OTP messages. Check your blocked messages list
5. **Phone storage full** — If your SMS inbox is full, new messages won't be delivered. Delete old messages
6. **DND (Do Not Disturb) active** — If DND is enabled, transactional SMS may be blocked
   - Send `START` to 1909 from your registered number to deactivate DND
   - Or activate only promotional DND: send `START 0` to 1909

### Resend OTP Correctly

- Wait at least **60 seconds** before clicking "Resend OTP"
- Don't click resend more than **3 times** — the system may temporarily block your number
- If blocked, wait **30 minutes** before trying again

## Problem 3: UIDAI Server Down

The Udyam portal depends on UIDAI's authentication servers for OTP. If UIDAI is down, no Aadhaar OTP will work.

### How to Check

1. Try generating OTP on [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/) directly
2. If OTP fails there too, it's a UIDAI server issue — not a Udyam problem
3. Check UIDAI's status page or social media for announcements

### What to Do

- **Wait and retry** — UIDAI maintenance usually lasts 1–4 hours
- **Try late evening or early morning** — Servers are least loaded between 6–8 AM and after 9 PM
- **Check for scheduled maintenance** — UIDAI announces maintenance on [uidai.gov.in](https://uidai.gov.in/)

## Problem 4: "Aadhaar Authentication Failed" Error

Even after entering the correct OTP, you may see "Authentication Failed."

### Causes & Fixes

| Cause | Fix |
|-------|-----|
| Entered OTP after expiry (10 minutes) | Request a new OTP and enter within 10 minutes |
| Aadhaar number entered incorrectly | Double-check all 12 digits |
| Name mismatch in Udyam form vs Aadhaar | Enter your name exactly as it appears on Aadhaar |
| Biometric lock enabled | Disable biometric/OTP lock on myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in |
| Aadhaar cancelled or suspended | Contact UIDAI regional office |

### Check Aadhaar Biometric Lock

1. Visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/) → Login
2. Go to **"Lock/Unlock Aadhaar"**
3. If OTP authentication is locked, **unlock it** before attempting Udyam registration
4. After completing Udyam registration, you can lock it again for security

## Problem 5: Virtual ID (VID) Not Working

Some users try using Aadhaar VID instead of the full Aadhaar number for Udyam registration.

- The Udyam portal currently requires the **full 12-digit Aadhaar number**
- VID may not be supported for Udyam OTP verification
- Use your actual Aadhaar number for registration

## Alternative: Assisted Registration at DIC

If OTP issues persist and you cannot resolve them:

1. Visit your nearest **District Industries Centre (DIC)**
2. Carry your Aadhaar card, PAN card, and business documents
3. DIC officials can assist with registration using **biometric Aadhaar verification** (fingerprint) instead of OTP
4. This is a free service — don't pay any agent or consultant
5. Find your nearest DIC through [msme.gov.in](https://msme.gov.in/)

## Important Tips

1. Update your Aadhaar-linked mobile number **before** attempting Udyam registration
2. Keep your phone charged and with good signal during the registration process
3. Complete the OTP step within 10 minutes — don't leave the page idle
4. Never share your Aadhaar OTP with anyone claiming to be a "Udyam registration agent"
5. If all else fails, DIC office biometric verification is the most reliable alternative

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use someone else's mobile number for Udyam OTP?
No. The OTP is sent only to the mobile number registered with UIDAI against your Aadhaar. You cannot change the destination number.

### How do I know if my mobile number is linked to Aadhaar?
Visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/) → Verify Aadhaar. It will show the last 4 digits of your linked mobile number.

### I changed my phone number. Can I still get Udyam OTP?
Only if your new number is linked to Aadhaar. If your old number is still linked, you must visit an Aadhaar centre to update it.

### How many times can I request OTP for Udyam registration?
You can request OTP up to 5–8 times in a session. After too many attempts, the system may temporarily block requests for 30 minutes.

### Is there any way to complete Udyam registration without Aadhaar?
No. Aadhaar is mandatory for Udyam registration. For proprietorship firms, the owner's Aadhaar is required. For companies/LLPs, the authorized signatory's Aadhaar plus organization PAN are needed.

### The OTP field on Udyam portal is greyed out. What do I do?
This usually means the Aadhaar verification request hasn't been sent yet. Re-enter your Aadhaar number and click "Validate Aadhaar" or "Send OTP" button first.
