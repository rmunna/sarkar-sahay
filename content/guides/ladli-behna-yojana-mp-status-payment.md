## What is Ladli Behna Yojana MP?

Ladli Behna Yojana is a flagship scheme of the Madhya Pradesh government that provides monthly financial assistance of ₹1,250 to eligible women. The amount is transferred directly to the beneficiary's bank account via DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer). The scheme aims to improve the economic independence and health of women in MP.

## How to Check Status Online (via Official Portal)

### Step 1: Visit the Official Portal

Go to: [https://ladlibahna.mp.gov.in](https://ladlibahna.mp.gov.in)

### Step 2: Click on "आवेदन की स्थिति" (Application Status)

On the homepage, find and click the **"आवेदन की स्थिति"** (Application Status) button.

### Step 3: Enter Your Samagra ID

Enter your **9-digit Samagra ID** in the input field. Your Samagra ID is your unique family/member ID issued by the MP government.

> **Don't know your Samagra ID?** Visit [https://samagra.gov.in](https://samagra.gov.in) and search using your name, mobile number, or Aadhaar number.

### Step 4: Enter Captcha

Type the captcha code shown on the screen and click **"खोजें"** (Search).

### Step 5: View Your Status

Your complete application details will be shown:
- **Application Status** — Approved (स्वीकृत), Pending (लंबित), or Rejected (अस्वीकृत)
- **Payment Status** — Month-wise payment history
- **Amount Credited** — ₹1,250 per month (or current applicable amount)
- **Bank Account** — Linked bank details (masked)
- **eKYC Status** — Whether Aadhaar eKYC is completed

## How to Check Payment Status via Samagra Portal

1. Visit [https://samagra.gov.in](https://samagra.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Samagra ID जानें"** (Know Your Samagra ID)
3. Search by name, mobile, or Aadhaar
4. Note your **Samagra Member ID** (9 digits)
5. Use this ID on the Ladli Behna portal to check payment status

## Alternative Ways to Check Status

## Common Errors and Fixes

### "Samagra ID Not Found"

- **Cause:** Incorrect Samagra ID entered, or your ID is deactivated
- **Fix:** Verify your Samagra ID at [samagra.gov.in](https://samagra.gov.in). If deactivated, visit your Gram Panchayat or Ward office to reactivate

### "eKYC Pending" or "eKYC Failed"

- **Cause:** Aadhaar eKYC was not completed or failed during biometric verification
- **Fix:** Visit the nearest MP Online Kiosk or CSC centre with your Aadhaar card to complete biometric eKYC. Ensure your fingerprints are clear

### "Payment Not Received Despite Approved Status"

- **Cause:** Bank account mismatch, inactive account, or Aadhaar-DBT linking issue
- **Fix:**
  1. Ensure your bank account is linked to your Aadhaar (check at your bank)
  2. Verify the bank account on the portal matches your active account
  3. If account was changed, update it at the Gram Panchayat office

### "Application Rejected — अपात्र (Ineligible)"

- **Cause:** You may not meet eligibility criteria (age, income, family income above ₹2.5 lakh, government employee, etc.)
- **Fix:** Check the specific rejection reason on the portal. If you believe it's an error, file a grievance at your block office or through the portal

### "Captcha Not Loading"

- **Cause:** Browser compatibility issue or slow internet
- **Fix:** Clear browser cache, try Google Chrome or Firefox, or switch to mobile data if Wi-Fi is slow

### "Website Shows 'Server Busy'"

- **Cause:** Heavy traffic, especially on payment dates (usually 1st–10th of each month)
- **Fix:** Try early morning (6–8 AM) or late night. Avoid checking on the 1st of the month

## Important Tips

1. **Complete eKYC before the deadline** — Payments are held if eKYC is pending
2. **Keep your Samagra ID safe** — It's your primary identifier for all MP government schemes
3. **Ensure Aadhaar-bank linking** — Payment goes to the Aadhaar-linked bank account via NPCI mapping
4. **Update mobile number** — Keep your registered mobile number active to receive OTPs and payment SMS
5. **Check every month** — Payment dates may vary; check status after the 10th of each month

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: How much money is given under Ladli Behna Yojana MP?

Eligible women receive ₹1,250 per month (₹15,000 per year), transferred directly to their bank account.

### Q2: What is the Samagra ID and where do I find it?

Samagra ID is a unique 9-digit family/member ID issued by the MP government. Find yours at [samagra.gov.in](https://samagra.gov.in) by searching with your name, mobile number, or Aadhaar.

### Q3: Can I check Ladli Behna status without Samagra ID?

No, the Samagra ID is mandatory for checking status on the official portal. Get your Samagra ID first from [samagra.gov.in](https://samagra.gov.in).

### Q4: When is the payment date for Ladli Behna Yojana?

Payments are typically processed between the 1st and 10th of every month. Exact dates may vary and are announced by the state government.

### Q5: What should I do if my eKYC keeps failing?

Visit an MP Online Kiosk or CSC centre. If biometric (fingerprint) verification fails repeatedly, request Iris (eye) scan-based eKYC. Ensure your Aadhaar biometrics are not locked.

### Q6: Can unmarried women apply for Ladli Behna Yojana?

Yes, both married and unmarried women aged 21–60 years who meet the income criteria are eligible.

### Q7: Is Ladli Behna Yojana available in other states?

This guide is specific to Madhya Pradesh. Some other states have similar schemes with different names and portals.
