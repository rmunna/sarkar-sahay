# Rythu Bima – ₹5 Lakh Farmer Life Insurance in Telangana

Rythu Bima is a life insurance scheme by the Government of Telangana providing ₹5,00,000 life insurance coverage to farmers. In case of a farmer's death (natural or accidental), the insurance amount is paid to the nominee. The premium is fully paid by the state government — farmers pay nothing.

## What is Rythu Bima?

Rythu Bima provides free group life insurance to all enrolled farmers in Telangana aged 18–59 years. The entire annual premium (approximately ₹2,271 per farmer) is paid by the government. Upon the death of an enrolled farmer, ₹5 lakh is paid to the nominee within 10 days through a streamlined claim process.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Farmer status:** Must be a Pattadar (land-owning farmer) in Telangana
2. **Age:** Between 18 and 59 years
3. **Land records:** Must have a valid Pattadar passbook
4. **Enrollment:** Must be enrolled under Rythu Bima (linked to Rythu Bharosa data)
5. **Premium:** ₹0 — fully paid by the government
6. **Exclusions:** Farmers above 59 years are not covered under this scheme

## Documents Required

### For Enrollment
- Aadhaar card (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- Pattadar passbook
- Bank account details
- Nominee details (name, Aadhaar, relationship)
- Passport-size photograph

### For Claim
- Death certificate of the farmer
- FIR / police report (in case of accidental death)
- Nominee's Aadhaar card
- Nominee's bank passbook
- Pattadar passbook of the deceased farmer
- Rythu Bima enrollment proof

## Step-by-Step Process

### Enrollment

1. **Automatic enrollment:** Farmers enrolled under Rythu Bharosa / Rythu Bandhu are automatically covered under Rythu Bima
2. **Verify enrollment:** Check your enrollment status at the Agriculture Extension Office or on the [Rythu Bima portal](https://rythubima.telangana.gov.in/)
3. **Update nominee:** Ensure your nominee details are correctly recorded
4. **New enrollment:** If not enrolled, visit the mandal Agriculture Extension Officer with your Pattadar passbook and Aadhaar

### Filing a Claim

1. **Report death:** The family should report the farmer's death to the Village Revenue Officer (VRO) or Agriculture Extension Officer within 30 days
2. **Submit documents:** Provide death certificate, nominee's Aadhaar, bank passbook, and Pattadar passbook
3. **AEO verification:** The Agriculture Extension Officer verifies the claim
4. **Claim forwarded:** The claim is forwarded to the insurance company (LIC)
5. **Processing:** LIC processes the claim
6. **Payment:** ₹5,00,000 is credited to the nominee's bank account — typically within 10 days of complete documentation

## Important Tips

1. **Update nominee details:** Ensure your nominee information is current — this determines who receives the ₹5 lakh
2. **Keep Pattadar passbook updated:** Land records must be current for enrollment
3. **Report promptly:** In case of farmer's death, report within 30 days for smooth claim processing
4. **No premium to pay:** The scheme is completely free for farmers — don't pay anyone
5. **Covers natural death:** Unlike many insurance schemes, Rythu Bima covers both natural and accidental death

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: How much insurance coverage does Rythu Bima provide?
₹5,00,000 (five lakh rupees) life insurance coverage.

### Q2: Do farmers need to pay any premium?
No. The entire premium is paid by the Government of Telangana. Farmers pay nothing.

### Q3: Who gets the money if a farmer dies?
The registered nominee receives the ₹5 lakh amount in their bank account.

### Q4: Is accidental death also covered?
Yes, both natural death and accidental death are covered under Rythu Bima.

### Q5: What is the age limit?
Farmers aged 18 to 59 years are covered. Farmers above 59 are not eligible.

### Q6: How quickly is the claim settled?
The government aims to settle claims within 10 days of complete documentation.

### Q7: Are tenant farmers covered?
Rythu Bima is primarily for Pattadar (land-owning) farmers. Tenant farmers may not be covered under this specific scheme.
