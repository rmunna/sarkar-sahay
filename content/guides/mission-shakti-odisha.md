## What is Mission Shakti?

Mission Shakti is a flagship women empowerment programme of the Government of Odisha, launched in **2001** to promote self-reliance among women through Self-Help Groups (SHGs). The programme has been elevated to a full-fledged department — the **Mission Shakti Department** — making Odisha one of the first states in India to have a dedicated department for women's SHGs.

Under Mission Shakti, women form groups of 10-20 members, pool their savings, access bank credit, and take up income-generating activities. The programme now covers over **70 lakh women** across **6 lakh+ SHGs** in Odisha, making it one of the largest women's collective movements in the country.

## Key Benefits

- **Interest-free loans** up to ₹5 lakh for SHG groups (interest subvention by state government)
- **Revolving fund** of ₹15,000-₹25,000 per SHG
- **Seed capital** for starting micro-enterprises
- **Skill training** in tailoring, food processing, handicrafts, digital literacy, and more
- **Market linkage support** through Mission Shakti bazaars and e-commerce platforms
- **Community investment fund** for SHG federations
- **Bank credit linkage** with priority sector lending norms
- **Insurance coverage** for SHG members
- **Digital financial inclusion** — SHG members trained in digital payments

## Who is Eligible?

### For SHG Formation
- **Women aged 18 years and above** residing in Odisha
- Must be willing to form a group of **10-20 women** from the same village/ward
- Members should preferably be from **similar socio-economic background**
- BPL families, SC/ST women, and single women get priority
- Each woman can be a member of **only one SHG**

### For Loans and Financial Support
- The SHG must be **active for at least 6 months** with regular savings and meetings
- SHG must maintain **proper books of accounts**
- Must have satisfactory **credit rating** from the bank
- No outstanding default on previous loans

### For Skill Training
- Any Mission Shakti SHG member can participate
- Priority for women from vulnerable backgrounds

## Documents Required

### For SHG Formation/Registration
- **Aadhaar Card** of all members
- **Voter ID** or other identity proof
- **Passport-size photographs** (2 per member)
- **Resolution** of group formation signed by all members
- **SHG savings register** and meeting register
- **Bank account opening form** (joint account in SHG name)

### For Loan Application
- **SHG registration certificate**
- **Minutes of meeting** approving loan application
- **Business plan/activity proposal**
- **SHG passbook** showing regular savings
- **Grading certificate** from DRDA/Block office
- **Bank account statement**

## How to Join or Form a Mission Shakti SHG

### Step 1: Form a Group
1. Gather **10-20 women** from your village or ward
2. All members should agree on **regular savings** (minimum ₹30-100 per month)
3. Elect a **President** and **Secretary** for the group
4. Choose a **group name**
5. Start holding **weekly/fortnightly meetings**

### Step 2: Register the SHG
1. Contact the **Block Mission Shakti office** or **Anganwadi Worker/ICDS Supervisor**
2. Submit the **group formation resolution** with all members' details
3. Get the SHG registered with the **Block Administration**
4. Open a **joint savings account** in a nearby bank/post office
5. Collect the **SHG registration certificate**

### Step 3: Access Financial Support
1. Maintain **regular savings and meetings** for 6 months
2. Start **inter-loaning** within the group from pooled savings
3. After 6 months, apply for **bank credit linkage** through the block office
4. SHG will be **graded** by the Block/DRDA office
5. A-grade SHGs can access **revolving fund** and **bank loans**
6. Interest subvention (interest-free loans) is provided by the state government on eligible loans

### Online Registration
1. Visit [missionshakti.odisha.gov.in](https://missionshakti.odisha.gov.in)
2. Click on **SHG registration** or relevant service
3. Enter SHG details and member information
4. Upload documents
5. Submit for approval

## Mission Shakti Federation Structure

| Level | Name | Composition |
|-------|------|-------------|
| Village | SHG | 10-20 women |
| GP Level | Panchayat Level Federation (PLF) | 15-25 SHGs |
| Block Level | Block Level Federation (BLF) | All PLFs in block |
| District Level | District Level Federation (DLF) | All BLFs in district |

## Fees

- **SHG formation** — No fee (free registration)
- **Bank account opening** — Free under priority sector norms
- **Loan processing** — Minimal bank charges (no government fee)
- **Training programmes** — Free of cost

## Processing Time

- SHG registration: **7-15 days**
- Bank account opening: **3-7 days**
- First loan (after 6 months): **15-30 days** from application
- Revolving fund disbursement: **30-45 days** after grading

## Important Tips

1. **Maintain regular meetings** — Weekly or fortnightly meetings are essential for SHG grading and loan eligibility
2. **Keep proper records** — Savings register, meeting minutes, and loan register must be maintained accurately
3. **Start small** — Begin with small inter-loans before approaching banks for larger credit
4. **Attend training** — Mission Shakti offers free skill training in various trades; participate actively
5. **Use digital tools** — Many Mission Shakti SHGs now use digital platforms for bookkeeping and payments

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. What is Mission Shakti in Odisha?
Mission Shakti is Odisha's flagship women empowerment programme that promotes Self-Help Groups (SHGs) for savings, credit access, skill development, and income generation among women.

### Q2. How many members are needed to form an SHG?
A minimum of **10 women** and maximum of **20 women** from the same village/ward can form an SHG under Mission Shakti.

### Q3. What is the loan limit for Mission Shakti SHGs?
SHGs can access bank loans up to **₹5 lakh** with interest subvention from the state government, making them effectively interest-free.

### Q4. Can men join Mission Shakti SHGs?
No. Mission Shakti is exclusively for women. However, men can benefit indirectly through family-level income generation activities.

### Q5. Is there any fee to join Mission Shakti?
No. SHG formation and registration are free. Members only contribute their agreed-upon monthly savings (minimum ₹30-100).

### Q6. What activities can Mission Shakti SHGs take up?
SHGs can engage in food processing, tailoring, handicrafts, poultry farming, dairy, mushroom cultivation, retail shops, and various micro-enterprises with government support.

### Q7. How do I find a Mission Shakti SHG near me?
Contact your nearest Anganwadi Worker, ICDS Supervisor, or Block Mission Shakti office. You can also visit missionshakti.odisha.gov.in.
