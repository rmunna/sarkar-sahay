# JoSAA Counselling 2026 — Complete IIT/NIT/IIIT Seat Allotment Guide

JoSAA (Joint Seat Allocation Authority) runs the centralized counselling for all IITs, NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs. Understanding the process — especially choice filling — can get you a significantly better seat than going in blind.

## How JoSAA Works

1. **Who participates**: Students who qualified JEE Main (for NIT/IIIT/GFTI) or JEE Advanced (for IIT)
2. **One common portal**: josaa.nic.in — single registration for all institutions
3. **6 rounds**: Seats are filled over 6 rounds; better options open as candidates don't accept or withdraw
4. **Seat acceptance fee**: Non-refundable if you don't report; partially refundable on resignation

## Home State Quota vs Other State — What's the Difference?

For NITs and IIITs:
- **50% seats** are "Home State" quota — only candidates whose state of domicile matches the NIT's state
- **50% seats** are "Other State" quota — open to all India candidates

**Which is more competitive?**
- Other state quota often has *lower opening ranks* because fewer candidates qualify as "home state" for a distant NIT
- Example: NIT Trichy CSE for Tamil Nadu home state candidates is harder (cutoff ~4,200) vs Other State cutoff (~3,400) because more Tamil Nadu students compete for home state seats

**Check your domicile carefully**: Fill Category properly during JoSAA registration — incorrect state can lead to seat cancellation.

## Choice Filling Strategy — The Most Important Step

Seat allotment is entirely based on your rank and your choices. Poor choice filling = missed opportunity.

### Rule 1: Fill At Least 50 Choices

JoSAA allows 100+ choices. Fill at least 50. More choices = more fallback options. Many candidates fill only 10–15 and lose good seats that they would have gotten with proper filling.

### Rule 2: Order by GENUINE Preference, Not by Rank

Fill choices in the order you actually want to join — most desired first, least desired last. JoSAA allots the BEST available seat from your choices based on your rank.

**Don't put a mediocre college first thinking you'll definitely get it.** If a better option exists based on your rank, you want that. Put it higher.

### Rule 3: Mix IIT + NIT + IIIT Choices

If you have both JEE Advanced and JEE Main ranks, you can add IIT choices alongside NIT/IIIT choices. JoSAA uses the relevant rank for each:
- IIT seats → JEE Advanced rank used
- NIT/IIIT → JEE Main rank used

### Rule 4: Don't Ignore Lower-Ranked Branches at Top Institutes

IIT Bombay Civil vs NIT Trichy CSE — different trade-offs. Consider: placement data, future branch change possibility, your interest.

### Rule 5: Include "Lock" Choices

Add a few choices you're certain to get (rank well below cutoff) as bottom entries. Ensures you always get something rather than going unallotted.

### What to Do If No Seat Allotted in Round 1

**CSAB counselling**: If you don't get a seat in 6 rounds of JoSAA, participate in CSAB (Central Seat Allocation Board) special rounds — opens extra NIT/IIIT seats. Registration on josaa.nic.in in August.

## Step-by-Step JoSAA 2026 Registration

### Step 1: Register

- Visit [josaa.nic.in](https://josaa.nic.in) on opening day
- Login with JEE Advanced/Main Application Number + Password
- Verify JEE rank shown is correct (for both Main and Advanced if applicable)

### Step 2: Fill Personal Details

- Name, category, date of birth, state of domicile
- PwD status (if applicable)
- **Category is critical** — affects which quota seats you're eligible for

### Step 3: Document Upload

- Photo, signature
- Category certificate (OBC NCL/SC/ST/EWS — must be valid non-creamy layer for OBC)
- State domicile certificate

### Step 4: Choice Filling

- Browse all institutes and branches
- Use filters: state, institute type, branch
- Add choices to your list
- Reorder by drag-and-drop
- Lock choices before deadline (mandatory — unverified choices = not considered)

### Step 5: Seat Allotment Check

Check result on allotment date. Three outcomes:
- **Seat Allotted**: Proceed to accept
- **Provisionally Allotted** (for later rounds): Don't pay, re-participate
- **Not Allotted**: Participate in next round or CSAB

### Step 6: Seat Acceptance

If seat allotted:
- Pay seat acceptance fee online (₹35,000 general / ₹15,000 SC/ST/PwD)
- Choose option: "Freeze" (stop here), "Float" (accept this but try for better in next round), or "Slide" (try for better in same institute)
- **Freeze**: Take this seat, no further participation
- **Float**: Keep this seat, also compete for higher choices in next round
- **Slide**: Compete for better branch at same institute

### Step 7: Report to Institute

After final round, report to allotted institute with original documents before reporting deadline. Medical examination, document verification, fee payment at institute.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I got an IIT seat in Round 1. Should I freeze or float?

If you got your 1st or 2nd choice — freeze. If you want a better branch at the same IIT or a higher-ranked IIT — float. Risk of floating: very small chance of losing current seat if you don't meet cutoff in next round. In practice, floating is safe because your current seat is held.

### Can I participate in state counselling while in JoSAA?

Yes — JOSAA is for central institutions (IITs/NITs/IIITs). State engineering counselling (Maharashtra: CAP, Karnataka: KEA, TN: TNEA) is separate. You can participate in both simultaneously. Report to whichever gives you the better seat by the deadline.

### My JEE Main rank is 50,000. Any chance of NIT seat?

Possible for lower-demand branches (Civil, Production, Metallurgy) at lower-ranked NITs, or home state quota in specific states. Fill 50+ choices including those branches to maximize allotment chance.

### What if I don't report to the allotted institute by the deadline?

Your seat is cancelled. You cannot re-participate in JoSAA. You can still try CSAB and state counsellings.
