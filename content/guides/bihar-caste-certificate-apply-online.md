# Bihar Caste Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **caste certificate** (जाति प्रमाण पत्र) in Bihar is issued by the Revenue Department and is required for reservations, scholarships (NSP, state), government job applications, and government scheme eligibility. Bihar's **RTPS (Right to Public Service) Act** guarantees issuance within **7–21 working days**. Applications go through the **RTPS/serviceonline.bihar.gov.in** portal.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [serviceonline.bihar.gov.in](https://serviceonline.bihar.gov.in) under Revenue Department → Caste Certificate. Guaranteed under RTPS within **7 to 21 days**.

---

## Caste Categories in Bihar

| Category | Reservation % | Notes |
|----------|---------------|-------|
| **SC** | 16% | Scheduled Castes (Bihar state list) |
| **ST** | 1% | Scheduled Tribes |
| **EBC** | 18% | Extremely Backward Classes (101 communities) |
| **BC** | 12% | Backward Classes (OBC — two sub-lists) |
| **EWS** | 10% | General category, income < ₹8 lakh/year |

> Bihar has a special **EBC (Extremely Backward Class)** category in addition to standard OBC. Your community may fall under BC or EBC — check the Bihar EBC/BC list.

---

## RTPS Right to Public Service — Bihar Guarantee

Bihar's **RTPS Act** guarantees:
- **SC/ST caste certificate:** 7 working days
- **OBC/EBC caste certificate:** 21 working days
- If delayed: complaint to First Appellate Authority (SDO)

---

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory)
- **Ration card** (shows family caste and composition)
- **Father's / grandfather's caste proof** (school certificate, service record, old voter ID with caste)
- Self-declaration of caste (online affidavit)
- **Voter ID** (additional address proof)
- Passport-size photograph

## How to Apply Online — RTPS Bihar Portal

1. Go to [serviceonline.bihar.gov.in](https://serviceonline.bihar.gov.in)
2. Click **"Apply for Services"** → search **"Caste Certificate"**
3. Select category: **OBC / EBC / SC / ST caste certificate**
4. Register / log in with mobile number (OTP)
5. Fill form:
   - Applicant name, address (district, block, panchayat)
   - Community/caste declaration
   - Upload caste proof documents
6. Pay fee: **₹15–30** (online)
7. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via CSC / Jan Seva Kendra

1. Visit nearest **CSC** or **Jan Seva Kendra** (Pragya Kendra in Bihar)
2. Provide Aadhaar, ration card, father's caste documents
3. Operator fills RTPS form and submits
4. Service charge: approximately ₹30–50
5. Collect acknowledgement

---

## Status Check

1. Go to [serviceonline.bihar.gov.in](https://serviceonline.bihar.gov.in)
2. Click **"Track Application Status"**
3. Enter Application Reference Number
4. Status: Submitted → Under Verification → Circle Officer → Approved

---

## Download Bihar Caste Certificate

Once approved:
1. Log in to RTPS portal → **"My Applications"**
2. Click approved caste certificate
3. Download digitally signed PDF
4. Valid for all official purposes

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is EBC category in Bihar?

**Extremely Backward Class (EBC)** is Bihar's special category for 101 communities that are socially and educationally most backward. It's different from the standard OBC (BC) list. Many lower-income rural communities fall under EBC. Check the Bihar government's EBC list at your District Magistrate office or block office.

### My caste is in OBC at central level but EBC in Bihar — which to use?

For **state government jobs** in Bihar: use Bihar's EBC certificate. For **central government jobs / UPSC**: use OBC certificate (as per central OBC list). Both can be obtained through RTPS — specify which category when applying.
