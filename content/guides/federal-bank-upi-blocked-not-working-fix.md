# Federal Bank UPI Blocked or Not Working? Fix (2026)

Federal Bank UPI works via the **FedMobile** app (formerly FedNet) and all major UPI apps. This guide covers Federal Bank UPI issues and fixes.

---

## Common Federal Bank UPI Issues

| Issue | Fix |
|-------|-----|
| Wrong UPI PIN | Reset PIN |
| Account debit freeze | Fix account block |
| Daily limit exceeded | Wait till midnight |
| UPI not registered | Register via FedMobile |
| Bank server issue | Try after 30 minutes |

---

## Reset Federal Bank UPI PIN

### Via FedMobile App
1. Open **FedMobile** → **UPI** → **Manage UPI PIN**
2. **"Change/Forgot UPI PIN"**
3. Debit card last 6 digits + expiry → OTP → New PIN

### Via PhonePe / GPay
1. App → Bank Accounts → Federal Bank
2. **"Forgot UPI PIN"** → Card details → OTP → New PIN

---

## Register Federal Bank UPI

1. FedMobile → **UPI** → **Register UPI**
2. Select account → verify mobile → set PIN
3. Or: Any UPI app → Add Bank → Federal Bank

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Federal Bank UPI daily limit?**
₹1,00,000 per day per account (standard NPCI limit).

**FedMobile UPI registered but PhonePe shows Federal Bank failed — why?**
PhonePe and FedMobile are separate registrations. Remove and re-add Federal Bank in PhonePe, reset UPI PIN in PhonePe.

**Is Federal Bank UPI good? I see fewer people using it.**
Federal Bank has strong UPI infrastructure. Less popular in North India (stronger in South/Kerala). UPI reliability is similar to major banks.
