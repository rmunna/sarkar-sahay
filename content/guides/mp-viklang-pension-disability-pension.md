## What is MP Viklang Pension Yojana?

**Viklang Pension Yojana** (also called Divyang Pension) is the Madhya Pradesh government scheme providing **₹600 per month** to persons with disabilities from BPL families in Madhya Pradesh. The pension is paid directly to the beneficiary's bank account via DBT.

The state pension integrates with the central **IGNDPS (Indira Gandhi National Disability Pension Scheme)** — eligible beneficiaries receive combined support.

## Pension Amount

| Component | Monthly Amount |
|---|---|
| State share (MP government) | ₹300/month |
| Central share (IGNDPS) | ₹300/month |
| **Total pension** | **₹600/month** |

## Who is Eligible?

- **Madhya Pradesh resident**
- **Disability: 40% or more** (as per disability certificate from government hospital)
- **Age: 6 to 79 years** (central scheme covers 18–79; state may extend to younger)
- **BPL (Below Poverty Line)** status
- Not already receiving a government salary or pension

### Disabilities Covered
- Visual impairment (blindness, low vision)
- Hearing impairment (deaf, hard of hearing)
- Locomotor disability (mobility impairment)
- Intellectual disability (mental retardation)
- Mental illness
- Multiple disabilities
- All 21 categories under RPWD Act 2016

## How to Apply

1. Go to **[socialjustice.mp.gov.in](https://socialjustice.mp.gov.in)**
2. Navigate to **"दिव्यांग पेंशन योजना"** (Divyang Pension)
3. Click **"ऑनलाइन आवेदन करें"** (Apply Online)
4. Enter Aadhaar number + OTP
5. Fill application:
   - Personal details and disability details
   - BPL/income status
   - Bank account (Aadhaar-linked)
6. Upload documents
7. Submit → application verified by district Social Justice office

**Offline route:** Apply at the **District Social Justice office** or through CSC (Jan Seva Kendra).

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card**
2. **Disability certificate** — with 40%+ disability, issued by a civil surgeon / chief medical officer / government hospital
3. **UDID card** (Unique Disability Identity card) — preferred if available
4. **BPL ration card** or below-poverty proof
5. **Bank passbook** (account in applicant's name, Aadhaar-linked)
6. **Age proof** (Aadhaar, birth certificate, Class 10 certificate)
7. **MP residence proof**
8. Passport-size photograph

## Getting a Disability Certificate (if not already held)

1. Visit the nearest **government district hospital**
2. Request a disability assessment
3. A medical board examines and issues the **disability certificate** showing type and percentage of disability
4. For UDID card (Unique Disability ID): register at [swavlambancard.gov.in](https://swavlambancard.gov.in)

## Checking Pension Status

1. Go to [socialjustice.mp.gov.in](https://socialjustice.mp.gov.in)
2. Click "पेंशन हितग्राही की स्थिति जानें" (Know pension beneficiary status)
3. Enter Aadhaar or SAMAGRA ID
4. View payment history

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I have a 35% disability. Am I eligible?
No — the minimum is 40% disability. For a borderline case, visit a government hospital for re-evaluation. Sometimes assessments under-measure disability — a re-evaluation may give a different result.

### My child is 10 years old and visually impaired. Can they receive this pension?
The central IGNDPS component is for 18+ years. However, the MP state scheme may cover children from a younger age. Confirm with the district Social Justice office — there may be a separate provision for disabled children.

### Can I get Viklang Pension and also apply for Divyangjan Scholarship?
Yes — these are different schemes. Viklang Pension is for income support; Divyangjan Scholarship is for students with disabilities. You can receive both simultaneously if eligible.

### How long does approval take?
Typically 1–3 months from complete document submission to pension activation. Follow up with your district Social Justice office if there's no update after 2 months.

### My disability certificate was issued 10 years ago. Is it still valid?
Permanent disabilities (congenital or established) on old certificates are usually accepted. However, having a recent UDID card (Unique Disability ID) from [swavlambancard.gov.in](https://swavlambancard.gov.in) simplifies verification. Get one if you don't have it.
