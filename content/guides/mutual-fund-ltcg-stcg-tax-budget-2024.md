# Mutual Fund Tax India 2026 — LTCG, STCG, Debt Fund Tax After Budget 2024

Budget 2024 (July 2024) significantly changed how mutual fund gains are taxed. If you invest in equity mutual funds or debt mutual funds, here's the updated tax picture for 2025-26.

> **Key changes**: Equity LTCG raised from 10% → 12.5%; Equity STCG raised from 15% → 20%. Both effective from July 23, 2024.

## Quick Reference: Mutual Fund Tax Rates

| MF Type | Holding Period | Tax Rate (After Budget 2024) |
|---------|---------------|------------------------------|
| **Equity MF / Hybrid (65%+ equity)** — LTCG | > 1 year | **12.5%** (no indexation); ₹1.25L annual exemption |
| **Equity MF / Hybrid (65%+ equity)** — STCG | ≤ 1 year | **20%** |
| **Debt MF (not equity-oriented)** | Any period | **Slab rate** (your income tax bracket) |
| **International / Overseas Fund of Funds** | Any period | **Slab rate** (since April 2023) |
| **Gold ETF / Gold Fund** | Any period | **Slab rate** (since April 2023) |
| **Hybrid (< 65% equity)** | Any period | **Slab rate** |
| **ELSS** — LTCG | > 1 year (3-yr lock-in applies) | **12.5%** (₹1.25L exemption) |

## Equity Mutual Fund Tax Rules

### LTCG on Equity MF (Holding > 1 Year)

- **Rate**: 12.5% (up from 10% before July 23, 2024)
- **Annual exemption**: ₹1,25,000 (₹1.25 lakh) of LTCG is tax-free each financial year
- **Gains above ₹1.25L**: Taxed at 12.5% (+ 4% cess = effective 13%)
- **No indexation benefit**: You cannot adjust the cost price for inflation

**Example**:
- Bought Equity MF units worth ₹5,00,000 in Jan 2022
- Sold in March 2026 for ₹8,00,000
- LTCG = ₹3,00,000
- Exempt: ₹1,25,000
- Taxable LTCG: ₹1,75,000
- **Tax: ₹1,75,000 × 12.5% = ₹21,875** (+ cess ₹875 = ₹22,750)

### STCG on Equity MF (Holding ≤ 1 Year)

- **Rate**: 20% (up from 15% before July 23, 2024)
- No exemption limit — entire STCG taxed at 20%
- Applies when you redeem/sell within 12 months of investment

**Example**:
- Bought MF units in July 2025 for ₹2,00,000
- Sold in February 2026 for ₹2,50,000 (held 7 months)
- STCG = ₹50,000
- **Tax: ₹50,000 × 20% = ₹10,000** (+ cess ₹400 = ₹10,400)

### Budget 2024 — What Changed vs Before

| Tax Type | Before July 23, 2024 | After July 23, 2024 |
|----------|---------------------|---------------------|
| Equity LTCG | 10% | **12.5%** |
| Equity STCG | 15% | **20%** |
| LTCG exemption | ₹1,00,000 | **₹1,25,000** |
| Holding period for LTCG | > 1 year | > 1 year (unchanged) |

**Grandfathering for gains before Budget 2024**: Gains accrued till July 22, 2024 are taxed at the old rates (10% LTCG, 15% STCG). Gains from July 23, 2024 onwards use new rates. If your investment spans this date, the gain is split proportionately.

## Debt Mutual Fund Tax Rules

### After April 1, 2023 — Slab Rate (Big Change)

Effective from April 1, 2023, all debt mutual funds (funds with < 35% equity) are taxed at your **income tax slab rate** regardless of holding period.

- **No LTCG/STCG distinction** for debt funds anymore
- **No indexation benefit** (removed)
- Tax at your slab: 5%, 10%, 15%, 20%, 25%, or 30% depending on your income bracket
- Applies to: Liquid funds, overnight funds, ultra-short, short-duration, credit risk, gilt, dynamic bond, income funds

**For investors in 30% tax bracket**: Debt funds now cost 30% tax. Bank FDs also cost 30% tax. The advantage debt funds had over FDs (indexation + LTCG rate) is gone.

### Debt Funds Bought Before April 1, 2023

If you bought debt fund units **before April 1, 2023** and still haven't sold them:
- These are **also taxed at slab rate** (no grandfathering for debt funds)
- The indexation benefit advantage those old units used to have is no longer available

## ELSS (Equity Linked Savings Scheme) Tax

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **80C deduction** | Investment up to ₹1,50,000 qualifies for deduction (old tax regime only) |
| **Lock-in period** | 3 years mandatory |
| **Tax on gains** | LTCG at 12.5% (same as other equity MFs) after 3-year lock-in |
| **Annual exemption** | Same ₹1.25L LTCG exemption applies |

> Note: 80C deduction for ELSS is only available if you're filing under the **old tax regime**. Under the new default regime, no 80C deduction is allowed, making ELSS less attractive vs regular equity MFs.

## SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) Taxation

Each SIP installment is treated as a **separate investment** with its own purchase date. When you redeem, units are sold on FIFO basis (First In, First Out):

- Oldest SIP installments (held > 12 months) → LTCG at 12.5%
- Recent SIP installments (held < 12 months) → STCG at 20%

**Example**: 36 monthly SIPs of ₹5,000. After 3 years, you redeem everything. The first 24 installments (held > 12 months) are LTCG; the last 12 installments (held < 12 months) are STCG.

## Capital Gains from Dividend Mutual Funds

- **Dividend income** from MFs: Taxed at your slab rate (not 12.5% or 20%)
- TDS on dividends: 10% TDS if dividend > ₹5,000 in a financial year
- **IDCW (Income Distribution cum Capital Withdrawal)** — rebranded term for dividend option — taxed the same way

## ITR Reporting for Mutual Fund Gains

- Your **broker / demat statement / AIS** automatically shows MF redemptions
- In ITR:
  - **LTCG from equity MF**: Schedule 112A
  - **STCG from equity MF**: Schedule CG (short-term)
  - **Debt MF / Slab rate gains**: Schedule OS (Other Sources) or Schedule BP if business income
- The AIS (Annual Information Statement) will pre-populate most MF transactions

## Tax-Efficient MF Strategies in 2026

1. **Harvest ₹1.25L LTCG each year tax-free**: Redeem equity MF gains up to ₹1.25L per FY, then reinvest. Resets your cost base without paying tax.
2. **Hold equity MF > 12 months**: Pay 12.5% LTCG instead of 20% STCG — saving 7.5% on gains.
3. **Prefer equity-oriented funds over pure debt**: Debt MFs are now at slab rate; equity MFs at 12.5% cap.
4. **For debt exposure**: Consider PPF (tax-free), NSC (80C + slab rate), or bank FD (slab rate — same as debt MF but predictable).
5. **ELSS only in old regime**: No point in ELSS if you're under the new regime (no 80C benefit).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I have equity MF I bought in 2019 and haven't sold. What tax do I pay when I sell in 2026?

The entire gain from sale in 2026 on units held > 12 months is LTCG. Gains up to ₹1.25L are tax-free. Gains above ₹1.25L are taxed at 12.5% (since your sale is after July 23, 2024, the new rate applies to gains from that date onwards).

### Does TDS get deducted on mutual fund redemption?

No TDS for **resident Indian** investors on equity MF redemption. TDS applies to NRI investors (30% on STCG, 12.5% on LTCG). For dividends, TDS of 10% applies if > ₹5,000.

### I redeemed debt mutual fund I bought in 2021 for a profit. What tax do I pay?

At your income slab rate. If you're in the 30% bracket, you pay 30% on the gain. No LTCG rate applies, no indexation — this changed in April 2023.

### Are liquid funds / overnight funds taxed the same as debt funds?

Yes — liquid funds, overnight funds, ultra-short duration funds are all debt category. Taxed at slab rate from April 2023.
