# FCI Recruitment Guide — Food Corporation of India Manager Exam

The Food Corporation of India (FCI) is one of India's largest public sector organisations, responsible for food grain procurement, storage, and distribution under the Public Distribution System. FCI regularly recruits candidates for managerial and non-managerial posts across its zonal offices.

This guide covers everything you need to know about FCI recruitment — eligibility, exam pattern, how to apply, salary, and preparation strategy.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent informational platform. We are **not** affiliated with the Food Corporation of India or any government body. Always verify details on the [official FCI website](https://fci.gov.in).

---

## What is FCI Recruitment?

FCI conducts recruitment drives to fill vacancies for various posts, primarily:

- **Category I** — Manager-level posts (General, Depot, Movement, Accounts, Technical, Civil Engineering, Electrical/Mechanical Engineering)
- **Category II** — Typist (Hindi)
- **Category III** — Assistant Grade III (General, Accounts, Technical, Depot)
- **Category IV** — Steno Grade II, Assistant Grade II (Hindi)

The most sought-after is the **Category I Manager exam**, which is conducted in multiple phases — an online exam followed by an interview or skill test depending on the post.

## Posts & Vacancies Overview

FCI announces vacancies based on organisational needs. The number of posts varies each recruitment cycle. Major posts include:

| Category | Post | Level |
|----------|------|-------|
| I | Manager (General / Depot / Movement / Accounts / Technical / Civil Engg / Electrical-Mechanical Engg) | Managerial |
| II | Typist (Hindi) | Non-Managerial |
| III | AG-III (General / Accounts / Technical / Depot) | Non-Managerial |
| IV | Steno Grade II, AG-II (Hindi) | Non-Managerial |

Vacancies are zone-wise (North, South, East, West, North-East). Candidates apply for a specific zone.

> **Note:** Check the [official FCI recruitment page](https://fci.gov.in/recruitments.php) for the latest vacancy numbers when a notification is released.

## Who is Eligible?

Eligibility varies by post. Below are the general requirements:

### Age Limit

| Category | Minimum Age | Maximum Age |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| I (Manager) | 18 years | 28 years |
| II | 18 years | 25 years |
| III | 18 years | 25 years |
| IV | 18 years | 25 years |

**Age relaxation** applies as per Government of India rules — 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC (NCL), 10 years for PwBD, and as applicable for ex-servicemen.

### Educational Qualification (Category I — Manager Posts)

| Post | Qualification |
|------|---------------|
| Manager (General) | Graduation in any discipline from a recognised university |
| Manager (Depot) | Graduation in any discipline; preference for Science graduates |
| Manager (Movement) | Graduation in any discipline |
| Manager (Accounts) | CA / ICWA / MBA (Finance) / M.Com |
| Manager (Technical) | B.Sc. in Agriculture / Food Science or B.Tech in Food Technology / Agricultural Engineering |
| Manager (Civil Engg) | B.E. / B.Tech in Civil Engineering |
| Manager (Electrical/Mechanical Engg) | B.E. / B.Tech in Electrical / Mechanical Engineering |

### Educational Qualification (Category II, III, IV)

- **AG-III (General):** Graduation in any discipline
- **AG-III (Accounts):** B.Com or equivalent
- **AG-III (Technical):** B.Sc. in Agriculture / relevant science stream
- **AG-III (Depot):** Graduation in any discipline
- **Typist (Hindi):** Graduation with Hindi typing proficiency
- **Steno Grade II:** Graduation with shorthand proficiency

> Always refer to the official notification for the exact qualifications, as FCI may update requirements.

## Exam Pattern & Syllabus

### Category I (Manager) — Exam Pattern

The selection process typically involves:

1. **Phase I — Online Exam (Objective)**
2. **Phase II — Online Exam (Objective + Descriptive for some posts)**
3. **Interview / Skill Test**

#### Phase I — Common for all Category I posts

| Section | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| English Language | 30 | 30 | Combined: 60 minutes |
| Reasoning Ability | 30 | 30 | |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 30 | 30 | |
| General Awareness | 30 | 30 | |
| **Total** | **120** | **120** | **60 minutes** |

- Negative marking: 1/4th mark deducted for wrong answers

#### Phase II — Post-specific paper

Phase II tests domain knowledge relevant to each post. For example:
- **Manager (General):** General Intelligence, Management
- **Manager (Accounts):** Accountancy, Finance, Management
- **Manager (Technical):** Food Science, Agriculture, relevant technical subjects

### Category III — Exam Pattern

| Section | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| English Language | 30 | 30 |
| Reasoning | 30 | 30 |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 30 | 30 |
| General Awareness | 30 | 30 |
| **Total** | **120** | **120** |

### Key Syllabus Topics

- **English:** Comprehension, Cloze Test, Error Spotting, Vocabulary
- **Reasoning:** Puzzles, Seating Arrangement, Syllogism, Coding-Decoding, Inequality
- **Quantitative Aptitude:** Simplification, DI, Percentage, Profit & Loss, Time & Work
- **General Awareness:** Current Affairs, Economy, Agriculture, Food Management, Government Schemes

## Step-by-Step Application Process

Follow these steps when the FCI recruitment notification is released:

1. **Visit the official FCI website** — Go to [https://fci.gov.in](https://fci.gov.in) and check the "Recruitments" section.
2. **Read the notification carefully** — Download the official PDF and review eligibility, vacancies, and important dates.
3. **Register online** — Click the application link. Create an account using your email and mobile number.
4. **Fill the application form** — Enter personal details, educational qualifications, and choose your preferred zone and post.
5. **Upload documents** — Upload your photograph, signature, and other required documents as per specifications mentioned in the notification.
6. **Pay the application fee** — Use net banking, debit/credit card, or UPI. (See fee details below.)
7. **Submit and take a printout** — Review all details, submit the form, and save/print the confirmation page for your records.

> **Tip:** Apply early. Don't wait for the last date — server load increases and technical issues are common.

## Preparation Strategy

### 1. Understand the Syllabus First

Download the official notification and note the exact syllabus for your chosen post. Don't waste time on irrelevant topics.

### 2. Build Strong Basics

For aptitude and reasoning, start from fundamentals. Use standard books like R.S. Aggarwal (Quantitative Aptitude) and Verbal & Non-Verbal Reasoning.

### 3. Focus on General Awareness

FCI exams often ask questions related to:
- Indian agriculture and food policy
- Government schemes (especially food security)
- Current affairs (last 6 months)
- Basic economics

### 4. Practice Previous Year Papers

Previous year question papers give you the best idea of difficulty level and question types. Solve at least 5-10 past papers.

### 5. Take Mock Tests

Online mock tests help you manage time. Aim to complete 120 questions in 60 minutes — that's 30 seconds per question.

### 6. Domain Preparation (Phase II)

For Category I posts, Phase II requires domain-specific knowledge. Study relevant textbooks and reference materials for your chosen specialisation.

## Important Tips

1. **Check zone-wise vacancies** — Some zones have more seats. Choose strategically based on vacancies and competition.
2. **Keep documents ready** — Caste certificate, domicile, educational certificates, and ID proof should be ready before applying.
3. **Don't ignore negative marking** — With 1/4th penalty, random guessing hurts. Attempt only questions you are reasonably confident about.
4. **Stay updated on FCI news** — Follow the [official FCI website](https://fci.gov.in) and employment news for recruitment notifications.
5. **Prepare for the interview** — Category I selection includes an interview. Practice common questions about FCI's role, food security, and your domain.
6. **Physical standards** — Some posts (Depot, Movement) may have physical fitness requirements. Check the notification.
7. **Apply for the right post** — Match your qualification precisely. Applications with mismatched qualifications get rejected.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: How often does FCI conduct recruitment?

FCI does not have a fixed annual schedule. Recruitment is conducted based on vacancies, typically every 2-3 years. Monitor the [official FCI recruitment page](https://fci.gov.in/recruitments.php) for updates.

### Q2: Can final-year students apply for FCI?

This depends on the specific notification. Some FCI recruitments allow final-year students to apply provisionally, while others require a completed degree at the time of application. Check the eligibility section of the official notification.

### Q3: Is there an interview for all FCI posts?

No. Interview is generally required for **Category I (Manager)** posts. Category II, III, and IV posts are usually filled based on the written exam and skill test (typing/stenography where applicable).

### Q4: What is the difference between FCI and SSC CGL recruitment?

FCI conducts its own recruitment independently. SSC CGL is a separate exam conducted by the Staff Selection Commission for various central government posts. Both are central government opportunities but have different selection processes and pay structures. Check our [SSC CGL Guide](/guides/ssc-cgl-guide) for more details.

### Q5: Can I apply for multiple FCI posts in the same notification?

Generally, a candidate can apply for only **one post** per notification. Choose the post that best matches your qualification and career goal.

### Q6: Is FCI a central government job?

FCI is a **Public Sector Undertaking (PSU)** under the Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution. It follows IDA pay pattern, not the Central Pay Commission structure. It is a central government body but with a PSU-style pay and benefits structure.

### Q7: Where can I find the official FCI notification?

All official notifications are published on [https://fci.gov.in](https://fci.gov.in) under the "Recruitments" section. Beware of fake notifications on unofficial websites.
