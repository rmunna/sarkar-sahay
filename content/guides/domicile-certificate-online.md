## What is a Domicile Certificate?

A Domicile Certificate (Nivas Praman Patra / Residential Certificate) is an official document issued by the state government that certifies you are a permanent resident of a particular state. It proves your domicile status for education, employment, and government benefits.

## Why Do You Need It?

- **State quota admission** in educational institutions
- **State government job applications**
- **Scholarship applications** restricted to state residents
- **Property purchase** in certain states
- **Various government schemes** limited to state residents
- **Competitive exam reservations** based on state domicile

## Who is Eligible?

- Any person who has been a **permanent resident** of the state
- Residency requirements vary by state:
  - Some states: **Born in the state** or **15+ years of continuous residence**
  - Some states: **10+ years** of residence
  - Some states: **Parent's domicile** is sufficient
- Must have proof of residence in the state

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar Card**
- **Birth Certificate** (proves place of birth)
- **Class 10 / 12 Marksheet** (if school was in the state)
- **Proof of Address** (Voter ID, ration card, utility bill, property documents)
- **Ration Card** (family details with state address)
- **Parent's domicile certificate** (if available — simplifies process)
- **Affidavit / Self-declaration** on stamp paper
- **Passport-size photographs** (2-3)

> **Born in the state?** Birth certificate + school records usually suffice. **Migrant?** Need proof of continuous residence (utility bills, rent agreements over multiple years).

## Step-by-Step Process — Online

### Step 1: Visit Your State e-District Portal
- **Uttar Pradesh:** edistrict.up.gov.in
- **Bihar:** serviceonline.bihar.gov.in
- **Delhi:** edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in
- **Maharashtra:** aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in
- **Rajasthan:** emitra.rajasthan.gov.in
- **Madhya Pradesh:** mpedistrict.gov.in
- **Other states:** Search "[your state] domicile certificate apply online"

### Step 2: Register / Login
Create account with mobile number and Aadhaar OTP.

### Step 3: Select "Domicile Certificate"
Navigate to Certificate Services → Domicile / Nivas Praman Patra / Residential Certificate

### Step 4: Fill Application Form
- Personal details
- Parent details
- **Residence history** (how long you've lived in the state)
- Current and permanent address
- Purpose of certificate

### Step 5: Upload Documents
- Aadhaar
- Birth certificate
- Educational certificates
- Address proof
- Affidavit
- Photograph

### Step 6: Pay Fee and Submit
Pay fee online. Note reference number.

### Step 7: Verification
- Tehsildar/SDM office verifies residence claims
- May involve local body certification (Gram Panchayat / Municipal Corporation)
- Police verification in some cases

### Step 8: Certificate Issued
Download from portal or collect from Tehsil office.

## Offline Process

1. Visit **Tehsil / SDM / Collector Office**
2. Collect domicile certificate application form
3. Fill and attach all documents + affidavit
4. Get **local body verification** (Gram Pradhan / Corporator letter — some states)
5. Submit at counter with fee
6. Verification by revenue officer
7. Certificate issued

## Processing Time

- **Online:** 7-21 working days
- **Offline:** 15-30 working days
- **With parent's domicile certificate:** Faster (7-10 days)

## Validity

- **Most states:** Lifetime (once issued, valid permanently)
- **Some states:** 3-5 years
- **Always verify** with the institution requesting it

## Special Cases

### Students Born Outside the State
If you were born elsewhere but have lived in the state for the required duration:
- Provide continuous residence proof (rent agreements, school transfer certificates, utility bills)
- Affidavit from two local witnesses
- Local body verification may be needed

### Government Employee's Children (Transferable Jobs)
- Parent's posting order showing residence in the state
- School transfer certificates
- Affidavit

## Important Tips

1. **Apply early** — Don't wait until exam/admission deadlines
2. **Parent's domicile certificate** speeds up the process significantly
3. **School records matter** — If you studied in the state, marksheets are strong proof
4. **Keep affidavit on proper stamp paper** — Incorrect denomination can cause rejection
5. **Digital certificates** from e-District portals are legally valid

## FAQs

**Q: Can I apply for domicile certificate online?**
A: Yes, through your state's e-District portal. Most states have online application systems.

**Q: How long does it take to get domicile certificate?**
A: 7-21 days online, 15-30 days offline. Having parent's domicile speeds it up.

**Q: Is domicile certificate valid for a lifetime?**
A: In most states, yes. Some states issue it with a specific validity period. Check with your state.

**Q: Can I get domicile certificate of a state where I was not born?**
A: Yes, if you have been a continuous resident for the required period (usually 10-15 years). You'll need extensive residence proof.

**Q: What is the difference between domicile and residential certificate?**
A: They are often the same document. Some states use "domicile" and others use "residential certificate" or "nivas praman patra." The purpose is identical — proving state residency.

**Q: Do I need domicile certificate for central government jobs?**
A: Generally no. Domicile is primarily needed for state government jobs and state-quota admissions. Some central posts in specific regions may require it.
