## How to Calculate Mudra Loan EMI

### EMI Formula

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n / [(1 + r)^n – 1]

Where:
- **P** = Loan principal amount
- **r** = Monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100)
- **n** = Number of monthly instalments (tenure in months)

### Example Calculations

#### Example 1: Shishu Loan — ₹50,000 at 11% for 3 years

- P = ₹50,000
- r = 11 ÷ 12 ÷ 100 = 0.00917
- n = 36 months

**EMI = ₹1,637 per month**
Total repayment = ₹58,932
Total interest = ₹8,932

#### Example 2: Kishore Loan — ₹3,00,000 at 12% for 5 years

- P = ₹3,00,000
- r = 12 ÷ 12 ÷ 100 = 0.01
- n = 60 months

**EMI = ₹6,674 per month**
Total repayment = ₹4,00,440
Total interest = ₹1,00,440

#### Example 3: Tarun Loan — ₹10,00,000 at 13% for 7 years

- P = ₹10,00,000
- r = 13 ÷ 12 ÷ 100 = 0.01083
- n = 84 months

**EMI = ₹17,653 per month**
Total repayment = ₹14,82,852
Total interest = ₹4,82,852

## Factors That Affect Your Interest Rate

### 1. Credit Score (CIBIL)

| CIBIL Score | Impact on Rate |
|-------------|---------------|
| 750+ | Best rates — lower end of range |
| 700–749 | Good rates — mid-range |
| 650–699 | Higher rates — upper end of range |
| Below 650 | May face rejection for Kishore/Tarun |

### 2. Loan Category

Shishu loans have the lowest rates because the loan amount is small and risk is low. Tarun loans carry higher rates due to larger amounts.

### 3. Bank Type

- **Public sector banks** (SBI, PNB, BoB) generally offer lower rates
- **Private banks** (HDFC, ICICI) may charge slightly more
- **NBFCs and MFIs** typically charge the highest rates (14%–18%)

### 4. Business Type and Vintage

Established businesses with proven revenue get better rates. New businesses or high-risk sectors may face higher rates.

### 5. Relationship with Bank

Existing customers with good account history often get preferential rates.

## Repayment Options

### EMI (Equated Monthly Instalment)

Most common. Fixed monthly payment that includes both principal and interest.

### Moratorium Period

Some banks offer a **3–6 month moratorium** (grace period) before EMI starts. This is helpful for new businesses that need time to generate revenue. Ask the bank specifically about this.

### Prepayment

You can repay the loan early without penalty for Shishu and most Kishore loans. This saves interest. For Tarun, check prepayment terms with your bank.

### Auto-Debit

Set up **ECS/NACH auto-debit** from your bank account to avoid missing EMI payments. Missed payments damage your CIBIL score and may attract penalties.

## What Happens If You Miss EMI Payments?

| Missed EMIs | Consequence |
|-------------|-------------|
| 1 EMI | Late fee charged, reminder from bank |
| 2–3 EMIs | Account flagged as SMA (Special Mention Account) |
| 3+ EMIs (90 days) | Account classified as NPA |
| NPA status | CIBIL score drops significantly, recovery action starts |

Avoid missing payments at all costs. If you're facing difficulty, contact the bank immediately to discuss restructuring options.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the lowest interest rate for Mudra loan?

The lowest rates are offered by public sector banks for Shishu loans — currently around **10%–10.5% per annum** at SBI and Bank of Baroda. Your actual rate depends on your credit profile.

### Is Mudra loan interest rate fixed or floating?

Most banks offer Mudra loans at **floating rates** linked to their MCLR or repo rate. This means your rate may change if RBI changes the repo rate. Some banks offer fixed rates for Shishu loans.

### Can I negotiate the interest rate on Mudra loan?

For Shishu loans, rates are fairly standard. For Kishore and Tarun, you can negotiate — especially if you have a good CIBIL score and existing relationship with the bank.

### Is there any interest subsidy on Mudra loans?

There is no direct interest subsidy on PMMY loans. However, if you're also eligible under schemes like **Stand Up India** or **PMEGP**, you may get a subsidy component.

### How much EMI will I pay for a ₹5 lakh Mudra loan?

At 12% interest for 5 years: **EMI ≈ ₹11,122 per month**. At 13% for 5 years: **EMI ≈ ₹11,370 per month**. Use the formula above to calculate for your specific rate.

### Can I prepay my Mudra loan early?

Yes. For Shishu and Kishore loans, there is generally no prepayment penalty. For Tarun loans, check with your bank — some charge 1%–2% on prepaid amount.

### Do NBFCs charge higher interest on Mudra loans than banks?

Yes, typically. NBFCs and MFIs charge 14%–18% per annum compared to 10%–14% at banks. Always compare rates across lenders before choosing. Check our [Mudra loan application guide](/guide/mudra-loan-apply-online) for how to compare offers.
