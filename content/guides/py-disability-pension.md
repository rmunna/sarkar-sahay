# Disability Pension – Puducherry

The Government of Puducherry provides monthly disability pension to persons with disabilities (PwD) who are unable to support themselves financially. The pension ensures basic financial security for differently-abled residents of the Union Territory.

## What is the Disability Pension Scheme?

This social security pension is paid monthly to persons with 40% or more disability as certified by a competent medical authority. The scheme is administered by the Department of Social Welfare, Puducherry.

### Pension Amount

| Disability Level | Monthly Pension |
|-----------------|----------------|
| 40%–79% disability | ₹2,000 – ₹3,000* |
| 80% and above disability | ₹2,500 – ₹3,500* |

*Amounts may vary as per latest government orders. Verify on official portals.*

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Disability:** Must have 40% or more disability (as per RPWD Act, 2016)
2. **Disability certificate:** Must have a valid disability certificate from a government medical board
3. **Residency:** Must be a resident of Puducherry UT for at least 5 years
4. **Income:** Annual income should not exceed the prescribed limit
5. **Not receiving other pension:** Should not be receiving any other government disability pension
6. **Age:** No specific age limit — persons of all ages with qualifying disability are eligible

## Types of Disabilities Covered

- Blindness and low vision
- Hearing impairment
- Locomotor disability
- Intellectual disability
- Mental illness
- Cerebral palsy
- Multiple disabilities
- Autism spectrum disorder
- Other disabilities as per RPWD Act, 2016

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar card (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- Disability certificate (40%+ disability) from District Medical Board
- Income certificate from Revenue Department
- Residence proof (5+ years in Puducherry)
- Bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked)
- Ration card
- Passport-size photographs (2 copies)
- UDID card (Unique Disability ID), if available

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### How to Apply

1. **Get disability certificate:** If you don't have one, visit the District Hospital / Government Hospital and apply for disability assessment by the Medical Board
2. **Collect application form:** Obtain the disability pension form from the Social Welfare Department or Commune Panchayat office
3. **Fill the form:** Enter personal details, disability details, income, and bank information
4. **Attach documents:** Self-attested copies of disability certificate, Aadhaar, income certificate, etc.
5. **Submit application:** Submit at the Social Welfare office or through e-District portal
6. **Verification:** Social welfare inspector conducts field verification
7. **Approval:** Application approved by competent authority
8. **Pension starts:** Monthly pension credited to bank account

### Online Application

1. Visit [edistrict.py.gov.in](https://edistrict.py.gov.in/)
2. Register/Login
3. Select Disability Pension under Social Welfare
4. Fill application and upload documents
5. Submit and track status

### Getting a Disability Certificate

1. **Visit government hospital** in Puducherry
2. **Apply for assessment** by the Medical Board
3. **Medical examination** — the board assesses disability percentage
4. **Certificate issued** — if disability is 40% or more, certificate is provided
5. **Also apply for UDID card** at [swavlambancard.gov.in](https://www.swavlambancard.gov.in/)

## Processing Time

- **Disability certificate:** 15–30 days from medical board examination
- **Pension application to approval:** 30–60 days
- **First pension credit:** Within 1–2 months of approval

## Important Tips

1. **Get UDID card:** Apply for the Unique Disability ID (UDID) card — it's increasingly required for all disability benefits
2. **Annual life certificate:** Submit a life certificate annually to continue receiving pension
3. **Reassessment:** Temporary disability certificates may need reassessment — renew before expiry
4. **Combine with other benefits:** Check eligibility for free bus pass, railway concession, and other PwD benefits
5. **No middlemen:** Apply directly — the process is free

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: What percentage of disability is required?
A minimum of 40% disability (as assessed by a government medical board) is required.

### Q2: How much pension will I receive?
Approximately ₹2,000–₹3,500 per month depending on disability level and current government orders.

### Q3: Can children with disabilities get this pension?
Yes, there is no age restriction. Parents/guardians can apply on behalf of children with qualifying disabilities.

### Q4: What is a UDID card?
The Unique Disability ID (UDID) card is a national ID for persons with disabilities, issued through [swavlambancard.gov.in](https://www.swavlambancard.gov.in/).

### Q5: Can I get disability pension and old age pension both?
Generally, only one social security pension is provided. Once you turn 60, you may transition to old age pension if the amount is higher.

### Q6: What if my disability certificate expires?
Get reassessed before expiry. An expired certificate will suspend your pension until renewed.

### Q7: Is this pension taxable?
Disability pensions from government schemes are generally not taxable.
