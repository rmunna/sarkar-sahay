# Andhra Pradesh Ration Card Apply Online 2026

The Andhra Pradesh ration card is issued by the **Civil Supplies Department, Government of Andhra Pradesh**. AP manages its PDS through the **EPDS AP** (Electronic Public Distribution System) portal and **MeeSeva** — the state's multi-service delivery platform. Andhra Pradesh has a well-digitised ration system with Aadhaar-based biometric authentication at all Fair Price Shops.

> **Quick summary:** Apply online at [epdsap.ap.gov.in](https://epdsap.ap.gov.in) or through any MeeSeva centre. AP PHH households get 5 kg grain/person/month free under PM GKAY. The state also provides rice at ₹1/kg under its own scheme for eligible families.

---

## Ration Card Types in Andhra Pradesh

| Type | Category | Entitlement |
|------|----------|-------------|
| **PHH (Priority Household)** | NFSA BPL families | 5 kg grain/person/month free (PM GKAY) |
| **AAY (Antyodaya Anna Yojana)** | Poorest households | 35 kg/family/month free |
| **AAP (Above APL / Non-Priority)** | Non-subsidised households | Identity document; limited/no food subsidy |

> AP also previously had a **white card** (APL) category. Most white card holders have been transitioned to AAP or removed from PDS rolls under NFSA rationalisation.

---

## Eligibility

**PHH (BPL ration card):**
- Annual household income below ₹1.5 lakh (rural) or ₹2 lakh (urban)
- Not a government or PSU employee
- No income taxpayer in family
- No motorised 4-wheeler
- No pucca house above 1,200 sq ft (urban) or 5 acres of agricultural land (rural)
- Aadhaar mandatory for all members

**AAY:**
- Landless agricultural labourers
- Widow/elderly person living alone with no income
- Disabled persons with no family support
- Bonded labourers, destitute migrant workers

---

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar cards (all family members — mandatory)
- Residence proof (electricity bill, house tax receipt, rent agreement)
- Passport-size photographs
- Income certificate from MRO (Mandal Revenue Officer) or Tehsildar
- Caste certificate (if SC/ST)
- Bank account passbook (for DBT linkage)
- Voter ID (as additional identity proof)

## How to Apply

**Method 1: MeeSeva Centre (Recommended)**

1. Visit nearest **MeeSeva kiosk** or **MeeSeva service centre**
2. Ask for "New Ration Card Application"
3. Provide Aadhaar, income certificate, and address proof
4. Operator fills and submits the form online
5. Service fee: approximately ₹25–40
6. Receive printed acknowledgement slip with application number

**Method 2: EPDS AP Online Portal**

1. Go to [epdsap.ap.gov.in](https://epdsap.ap.gov.in)
2. Click **"New Ration Card Application"** under Citizen Services
3. Register with your mobile number (OTP verification)
4. Fill in household head and member details with Aadhaar
5. Select district, mandal, and gram panchayat
6. Upload all required documents
7. Submit and note the acknowledgement number

**Method 3: MeeSeva Online Portal**

1. Go to [meeseva.gov.in](https://meeseva.gov.in)
2. Log in with Aadhaar-linked mobile number
3. Search for **"Ration Card"** services
4. Select **"New Ration Card Application"**
5. Fill details and upload documents
6. Submit application

**Method 4: Gram Panchayat / Ward Secretariat**

1. Visit your **Village/Ward Secretariat** (AP's doorstep governance model)
2. Ward Volunteer or Panchayat Secretary assists with the application
3. Documents submitted and forwarded to MPDO or DSO for verification

## Status Check

**Via EPDS AP:**
1. Go to [epdsap.ap.gov.in](https://epdsap.ap.gov.in)
2. Click **"Application Status"** under Citizen Services
3. Enter acknowledgement number or Aadhaar number
4. View current application status

**Via MeeSeva:**
- Log in to meeseva.gov.in → **"My Applications"** → Track status

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I add a newborn to my AP ration card?

Visit your nearest MeeSeva centre or apply at epdsap.ap.gov.in under "Member Addition." You'll need the child's Aadhaar (or application receipt if Aadhaar is pending), birth certificate, and the existing ration card number.

### Can I check AP ration card status by Aadhaar?

Yes — at [epdsap.ap.gov.in](https://epdsap.ap.gov.in), you can search by Aadhaar number to find linked ration card details and application status.

### My AP ration card shows wrong name or DOB. How to correct?

Visit your nearest MeeSeva centre and apply for "Ration Card Correction." Bring your Aadhaar, ration card, and the supporting document showing the correct information.

### I moved to AP from another state. Can I get a ration card?

Yes — surrender your ration card from the previous state (or get a no-objection certificate), then apply fresh via MeeSeva or epdsap.ap.gov.in with current AP address proof.

### What is the Ward Secretariat system in AP?

Andhra Pradesh operates **Ward/Village Secretariats** across the state — these are government offices at the ward/village level. Ward Volunteers make door-to-door visits for government scheme applications including ration cards. This is AP's doorstep delivery model.
