## What is Mukhyamantri Digital Seva Yojana?

**Mukhyamantri Digital Seva Yojana** (also known as **Indira Gandhi Smartphone Yojana**) is Rajasthan government's scheme to provide **free smartphones with 3 years of internet connectivity** to women — specifically the **female head of each Chiranjeevi-enrolled family**. The scheme aims to bridge the digital divide for women in Rajasthan.

**Benefit:**
- 1 free smartphone per eligible family (to the female head)
- 3 years of free internet data (via telecom partner)
- SIM card included

---

## Who is Eligible?

**Phase 1 — Already implemented:**
- Women who are head of their family (per Jan Aadhaar records)
- **Widowed women** from IGNOAPS / IGNWPS
- Mahatma Gandhi NREGA women with 100 days' work in 2022–23
- Women from PM Jan Arogya Yojana families
- Women from Mukhyamantri Chiranjeevi Durghatna Insurance families

**Phase 2 / Further phases:**
- Govt school Girls (Class 9–12) and college girls
- Additional Chiranjeevi beneficiary families

**The key check:**
- The **woman must be listed as family head** in the **Jan Aadhaar database**
- If the man is the family head in Jan Aadhaar, the woman may not be included in Phase 1

---

## Documents Required at Distribution Camp

1. **Jan Aadhaar card** — of the family
2. **Aadhaar card** — of the woman receiving the phone
3. **Chiranjeevi card** (if applicable)
4. **Any supporting ID** (voter card, NREGA job card)

> **No online application is needed** — phone distribution happens at government camps (shivirs).

## How to Get the Phone

### Step 1 — Check if Your Family is Eligible

Go to **[cmdigitalseva.rajasthan.gov.in](https://cmdigitalseva.rajasthan.gov.in)**:
1. Enter your **Jan Aadhaar number**
2. Check if your family is in the eligible list
3. See the scheduled distribution camp date and location

### Step 2 — Visit the Distribution Camp

Distribution happens through **government shivirs (camps)** at block/gram panchayat level. Camps are scheduled and announced in advance.

**At the camp:**
- Show Jan Aadhaar and Aadhaar of the woman
- The phone is issued in the woman's name
- SIM is activated at the camp

### Step 3 — Register the Phone

At the camp, the VLE registers the phone with your details and issues a receipt.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. The family head in Jan Aadhaar is my husband. Can I still get a phone as his wife?**

Phase 1 targets **female heads of household** per Jan Aadhaar records. If your husband is the family head, you may not be covered in Phase 1. Update the family head to a woman in Jan Aadhaar — visit E-Mitra. Or wait for Phase 2 which may cover a wider group.

**2. How do I change the family head in Jan Aadhaar to my mother/wife?**

Visit any **E-Mitra kiosk** with family Aadhaar cards and Jan Aadhaar card. Request family head correction. This process may require specific documentation — ask the E-Mitra operator.

**3. The camp was scheduled in our village last month but I missed it. What do I do?**

Check [cmdigitalseva.rajasthan.gov.in](https://cmdigitalseva.rajasthan.gov.in) for rescheduled camps. Missed beneficiaries are usually covered in subsequent rounds or special camps.

**4. The phone given is basic — not a good smartphone. Is there a minimum specification?**

The government specifies minimum specifications for phones distributed under this scheme — typically Android smartphone with 4G capability. The exact model varies by district and telecom partner.

**5. The 3 years of free internet — how much data?**

The internet plan details vary by telecom partner (Jio/Airtel/Vi). Typically it includes daily data (1–2 GB/day) for 3 years. The SIM and plan are included at no cost.
