# Haryana Ration Card Apply Online 2026

The Haryana ration card is issued by the **Food, Civil Supplies & Consumer Affairs Department, Government of Haryana**. Haryana manages ration cards through the **EPDS Haryana** system accessible at [haryanafood.gov.in](https://haryanafood.gov.in). An important feature of Haryana's system: ration card benefits are linked to **Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP)** — the state's family identity card. You must have a PPP before applying.

> **Quick summary:** Get Parivar Pehchan Patra first at [meraparivar.haryana.gov.in](https://meraparivar.haryana.gov.in), then apply for ration card at haryanafood.gov.in or Atal Sewa Kendra. BPL/PHH families get 5 kg grain/person/month free under PM GKAY.

---

## Ration Card Types in Haryana

| Type | Category | Entitlement |
|------|----------|-------------|
| **BPL / PHH (Priority Household)** | NFSA BPL families | 5 kg grain/person/month free (PM GKAY) |
| **AAY (Antyodaya Anna Yojana)** | Poorest households | 35 kg/family/month free |
| **APL (Above Poverty Line)** | Non-subsidised families | Identity document; limited/no grain subsidy |

> Haryana also distributes **sugar** and **kerosene** through PDS to BPL/AAY families in some districts.

---

## Eligibility

**BPL / PHH ration card:**
- Annual household income below ₹1.80 lakh (rural) or ₹2 lakh (urban) — based on PPP economic profile
- Not a regular government or PSU employee
- No income taxpayer in family
- No motorised 4-wheeler vehicle
- No agricultural land above 5 acres (rural criteria)
- **Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP)** mandatory — must have valid family ID
- Aadhaar mandatory for all members

**AAY:**
- Landless agricultural labourers with no stable income
- Old age pensioners living alone
- Widows and persons with disabilities without family support
- Destitute households

## Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP) — Mandatory First Step

**Parivar Pehchan Patra** is Haryana's unique family identity system. Every family must be registered. Ration card eligibility is now auto-determined based on PPP economic data.

**If you don't have PPP:**
1. Visit [meraparivar.haryana.gov.in](https://meraparivar.haryana.gov.in)
2. Or visit your nearest Atal Sewa Kendra / Common Service Centre
3. Register your family with Aadhaar, income, and asset details
4. Get your 8-digit PPP Family ID
5. All family members get individual PPP member IDs

> Once PPP is registered and income verified as BPL, the system may **automatically generate your ration card** without a separate application. Check at haryanafood.gov.in using your PPP ID.

---

## Documents Required

- **Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP) Family ID** — mandatory
- Aadhaar cards (all family members)
- Residence proof (electricity bill, property tax, rent agreement)
- Passport-size photographs
- Income certificate from Patwari / Tehsildar
- Caste certificate (if SC/ST)
- Bank account passbook (for DBT)

## How to Apply

**Method 1: Haryana Food Portal (Online)**

1. Go to [haryanafood.gov.in](https://haryanafood.gov.in)
2. Click **"Apply for Ration Card"** or Citizen Services
3. Log in with Aadhaar-linked mobile number or PPP Family ID
4. Family details auto-populate from PPP database
5. Verify details, select category applied for
6. Upload additional documents if required
7. Submit — download acknowledgement

**Method 2: Atal Sewa Kendra / CSC**

1. Visit nearest **Atal Sewa Kendra** (Haryana's CSC)
2. Bring PPP ID, Aadhaar, and income documents
3. Operator fills form and submits online
4. Service fee: approximately ₹30–50
5. Collect acknowledgement slip

**Method 3: Block / Tehsil Office**

1. Visit Block Food Supply Officer or Tehsil office
2. Submit filled application form with all documents
3. Field verification by Food Inspector
4. Forwarded to DFSO for approval

## Auto-Generated Ration Cards via PPP

Haryana has implemented **automatic ration card provisioning** — if your family is registered on PPP and the economic profile (income, assets) qualifies as BPL, the system automatically includes you in the NFSA list without a separate application. To check:

1. Go to [haryanafood.gov.in](https://haryanafood.gov.in)
2. Enter your **PPP Family ID**
3. View if ration card has been automatically generated

If not generated yet, apply manually through the portal or Atal Sewa Kendra.

## Status Check

2. Click **"Application Status"** or **"Ration Card Status"**
3. Enter PPP Family ID or acknowledgement number or Aadhaar
4. View current status

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Parivar Pehchan Patra and why is it needed for ration card in Haryana?

PPP is Haryana's family identity document with economic profiling. The government uses PPP data to automatically identify BPL families and generate ration cards. It's mandatory for any government scheme in Haryana, including ration cards.

### My PPP shows wrong income. Will it affect my ration card eligibility?

Yes — if PPP income data shows you above the BPL threshold, you may not be auto-included. Visit your nearest Atal Sewa Kendra to update PPP income data with proper income certificate from Patwari/Tehsildar.

### I have PPP but no ration card. What do I do?

First check at haryanafood.gov.in if a ration card has been auto-generated. If not, apply online at haryanafood.gov.in or visit Atal Sewa Kendra for manual application.

### Does ONORC work in Haryana?

Yes — Haryana is part of **One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC)**. Migrant workers with ration cards from other states can use Aadhaar biometric authentication at Haryana FPS shops to collect their entitled grain.
