## What is NVS Teacher Recruitment?

Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (NVS) recruits teachers and staff for 661 Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas (JNVs) spread across India. These are residential schools established by the Ministry of Education to provide quality education to talented rural children. NVS teaching positions are Central Government jobs with attractive pay, free housing, and meals.

NVS recruits for PGT (Post Graduate Teacher), TGT (Trained Graduate Teacher), Miscellaneous Category Teachers (Music, Art, PET, Librarian), and non-teaching staff.

## Who is Eligible?

### PGT (Post Graduate Teacher)
- Master's degree in the relevant subject with **50% marks**
- B.Ed from a recognized institution
- **Age:** 40 years maximum
- **Subjects:** English, Hindi, Physics, Chemistry, Math, Biology, History, Geography, Economics, Commerce, Computer Science

### TGT (Trained Graduate Teacher)
- Bachelor's degree with relevant subjects and **50% marks**
- B.Ed from a recognized institution
- **CTET Paper II** qualified
- **Age:** 35 years maximum

### PRT (Primary Teacher)
- 10+2 with 50% marks + D.El.Ed / B.El.Ed / B.Ed
- **CTET Paper I** qualified
- **Age:** 30 years maximum

### Miscellaneous Teachers
- **Music Teacher:** Bachelor's in Music or equivalent
- **Art Teacher:** Drawing/Painting qualification from recognized institution
- **PET (Physical Education):** B.P.Ed from recognized university
- **Librarian:** Bachelor's in Library Science

## NVS Exam Pattern

### Part I — Common (All Posts)

| Section | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| General Awareness | 10 | 10 |
| Reasoning Ability | 10 | 10 |
| Computer Literacy | 5 | 5 |
| Hindi | 10 | 10 |
| English | 10 | 10 |
| **Part I Total** | **45** | **45** |

### Part II — Subject Specific

| Post | Questions | Marks |
|------|-----------|-------|
| PGT / TGT / PRT | 80 | 80 |

### Part III — Perspectives on Education
- 25 questions, 25 marks

- **Total:** 150 questions, 150 marks
- **Duration:** 3 hours
- **No negative marking**

## Selection Process

1. **Written Exam (CBT)** — computer-based test at centres across India
2. **Shortlisting** — based on CBT score (3x vacancies)
3. **Demo Teaching** — assessed on content delivery and methodology
4. **Interview** — personal assessment
5. **Document Verification** — original certificates checked
6. **Medical Examination** — fitness for residential school posting

### Merit Weightage

| Component | Weightage |
|-----------|-----------|
| Written Exam | 85% |
| Demo Teaching + Interview | 15% |

### Unique NVS Benefits
- **Free accommodation** in campus quarters (residential school)
- **Free meals** in the school mess
- **Children's education** in the same JNV
- DA, HRA (if quarters not available), medical (CGHS)
- NPS pension and group insurance
- Vacations similar to other Central Government schools

## Preparation Tips

- **NCERT books (Class 6-12)** are essential for subject preparation
- Study **NCF 2005 and NEP 2020** for education perspectives section
- Practice reasoning and general awareness regularly
- Review NVS previous year papers for question patterns
- Prepare for demo teaching — focus on residential school context

## Important Tips

- **NVS is residential** — be prepared for postings in rural areas across India
- **Free meals and accommodation** offset the rural posting challenges significantly
- **Transfer policy** — NVS transfers happen every 3-5 years; you may move across states
- **CTET is mandatory for TGT and PRT** — clear it before applying
- **Subject preparation from NCERT** — the exam tests teaching-level knowledge, not research-level

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is NVS a Central Government job?
Yes, NVS teachers are Central Government employees under the Ministry of Education. They receive 7th CPC pay, pension, and all central government benefits.

### What is the difference between KVS and NVS?
KVS (Kendriya Vidyalaya) schools are mostly in urban areas for Central Government employees' children. NVS (Navodaya Vidyalaya) schools are residential schools in rural areas for talented students selected through an entrance exam. See our [KVS teacher guide](/guide/kvs-teacher-recruitment) for comparison.

### Can I choose my posting location?
Initial posting is based on vacancy and NVS requirement. You can request transfer after completing the minimum service period (usually 3 years). Remote area postings are common initially.

### Is there an interview in NVS recruitment?
Yes, shortlisted candidates appear for demo teaching and interview. However, the written exam carries 85% weightage in the final merit.

### Do NVS teachers get summer vacation?
Yes, NVS teachers get summer and winter vacations. However, being a residential school, some duties during vacations (like admission, maintenance) may be assigned on rotation.

### What is the career growth for NVS teachers?
NVS teachers can be promoted to Vice Principal and Principal roles. TGTs can be promoted to PGT level based on qualifications and experience. The career path is well-structured.
