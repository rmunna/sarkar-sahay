## What is Uttarakhand Old Age Pension?

The **Uttarakhand Old Age Pension** scheme provides monthly financial assistance to senior citizens of the state who are **60 years and above** and belong to economically weaker sections. The scheme combines the central NSAP (Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme) and the state government's top-up.

Current pension: **₹1,200 per month** (amount includes state + central components).

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Age:** Must be **60 years or older**
2. **Residency:** Permanent resident of Uttarakhand
3. **Income:** Must be from BPL family or annual family income below prescribed limit
4. **Other Pension:** Must not be receiving any other government pension
5. **Aadhaar:** Required for DBT

## Pension Amount

| Age Group | Monthly Amount |
|-----------|---------------|
| 60-79 years | ₹1,200 |
| 80+ years | ₹1,200 + additional NSAP (₹500) |

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar Card** (linked to bank account)
- **Age Proof** (Birth Certificate / Aadhaar / School records)
- **Domicile Certificate** of Uttarakhand
- **Income Certificate** / BPL Card
- **Bank Passbook** (Aadhaar-linked)
- **Passport-size photographs**
- **Voter ID** (optional, for additional verification)

## How to Apply

### Online (e-District Portal)

1. **Visit** [edistrict.uk.gov.in](https://edistrict.uk.gov.in)
2. **Register** / Login
3. **Navigate** to Social Security Pension → Old Age Pension
4. **Fill** application with personal, age, and bank details
5. **Upload** documents
6. **Submit** and save application ID
7. **Track** status online

### Offline

1. **Visit** **Block Development Office** or **Tehsil Office**
2. **Collect** and fill the pension application form
3. **Attach** all documents
4. **Submit** to BDO or Tehsildar
5. **Get** acknowledgement receipt

## Processing Time

- **Verification:** 15-30 days
- **Approval:** By district authority
- **First Payment:** 1-2 months after approval
- **Ongoing:** Monthly via DBT

## Important Tips

1. **Aadhaar-Bank Link:** Must have for DBT payments
2. **Life Certificate:** Submit annually (Jeevan Pramaan) to continue pension
3. **One Pension Only:** Cannot combine with widow or disability pension
4. **Apply at Tehsil:** For rural areas, the Tehsil office is the most accessible
5. **Helpline:** Contact the District Social Welfare Officer for queries

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. What is the pension amount?
**₹1,200 per month** for ages 60-79. Additional ₹500 NSAP for 80+ age group.

### Q2. What is the income limit?
Must be BPL or below the prescribed income limit set by the state.

### Q3. Can I apply online?
Yes, through [edistrict.uk.gov.in](https://edistrict.uk.gov.in).

### Q4. Is Aadhaar mandatory?
Yes, for DBT disbursement.

### Q5. How often is pension paid?
Monthly, directly to bank account.

### Q6. Can I get Old Age Pension and Atal Ayushman together?
Yes — Atal Ayushman is a health scheme and doesn't conflict with pension.

### Q7. What if my application is rejected?
Re-apply after fixing deficiencies. Contact the District Social Welfare Officer.
