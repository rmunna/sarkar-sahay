## What is Mission Shakti Odisha?

**Mission Shakti** is Odisha government's flagship women's empowerment programme that covers over **70 lakh women** across 6.5 lakh Self Help Groups (SHGs) in Odisha. It is one of the largest women's SHG networks in India, providing:

- **SHG formation and training** — how to form groups, maintain accounts, conduct meetings
- **Low-interest microfinance loans** through bank linkages
- **Skill development** — tailoring, food processing, handicrafts, agriculture
- **Market linkage** — Mission Shakti branded products sold at Mahila Bazaar outlets
- **Social security** — convergence with pensions, health insurance, and government schemes

## Who Can Join?

**Any woman in Odisha can join a Mission Shakti SHG if:**
- She is an adult woman (18+ years)
- She is a resident of the village/ward where the SHG operates
- She is willing to save a small amount monthly (minimum ₹50/month) and attend meetings

> There is no income limit. BPL, APL, all categories are welcome. The SHG must have 10–15 members.

---

## How to Join a Mission Shakti SHG

### Step 1 — Find a Local SHG

Ask the **ASHA worker, Anganwadi worker, or Block Mission Shakti Coordinator** about existing SHGs in your area. Every village in Odisha should have at least one SHG.

### Step 2 — Join an Existing SHG or Form a New One

- **Join existing:** If a local SHG has a vacancy, fill in the membership form
- **Form new:** If no SHG exists, gather 10–15 women and register with the Block Mission Shakti Coordinator

### Step 3 — Regular Savings and Meetings

- Attend **weekly or fortnightly meetings**
- Save the agreed monthly amount (minimum ₹50/month)
- The SHG opens a **joint bank account**

### Step 4 — Access Benefits

After 3–6 months of regular savings and meetings:
- **First SHG loan** from the Revolving Fund (up to ₹10,000 interest-free from government)
- **Bank linkage** — bank provides larger loans based on savings record
- Skill training enrollment through Block Resource Centre

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. My village has no SHG. How can I start one?**

Contact the **Block Mission Shakti Coordinator** (BMSC) at your Block Development Office. They guide you through forming a group of 10–15 women, registering it, and getting the initial Revolving Fund support.

**2. I am from an upper-caste wealthy family. Can I still join an SHG?**

Yes. Mission Shakti is open to all women regardless of caste or income. SHGs in Odisha are inclusive. However, BPL women get priority for government convergence benefits like BSKY health insurance.

**3. How much loan can I get from my SHG?**

Initial SHG loan (from internal savings + Revolving Fund): ₹5,000–₹10,000. Bank-linked loans (after 6 months of good record): ₹25,000–₹2 lakh per member. Larger enterprise loans available to mature SHGs.

**4. Do I have to be married to join?**

No. Single women, widows, and divorced women are actively encouraged to join Mission Shakti SHGs.

**5. Are there any SHGs specifically for disabled women?**

Mission Shakti works with all women including those with disabilities. Some blocks have specifically formed SHGs for disabled women — contact the BMSC.
