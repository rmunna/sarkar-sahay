# Uttarakhand Gaura Devi Kanya Dhan Yojana – Complete Guide

## What is Gaura Devi Kanya Dhan Yojana?

Gaura Devi Kanya Dhan Yojana is a flagship scheme of the Uttarakhand state government aimed at empowering girls through financial assistance for education. Named after the legendary environmentalist Gaura Devi, this scheme provides **₹51,000** as a fixed deposit in the name of girls from economically weaker families upon passing Class 12 (Intermediate).

The scheme encourages families to educate their daughters and discourages child marriage by providing substantial financial support at a critical stage of a girl's education.

## Who is Eligible?

To qualify for the Gaura Devi Kanya Dhan Yojana, the applicant must meet these criteria:

- **Domicile:** The girl must be a permanent resident of Uttarakhand
- **Education:** Must have passed Class 12 from a recognized school in Uttarakhand
- **Age:** Must be unmarried and below 25 years of age at the time of application
- **Family Income:** Annual family income must not exceed ₹72,000 (rural) or ₹1,00,000 (urban) — BPL families are automatically eligible
- **Family Size:** Maximum two daughters per family can benefit
- **School Type:** Must have studied in a government, government-aided, or recognized private school in Uttarakhand

## Documents Required

### Identity & Residence
- Aadhaar card of the applicant
- Domicile certificate of Uttarakhand
- Family photograph

### Education
- Class 12 marksheet and passing certificate
- School leaving certificate
- School verification certificate (from the principal)

### Financial
- BPL card or income certificate (annual income below the threshold)
- Bank account passbook (in the girl's name, linked to Aadhaar)

### Other
- Caste certificate (if applicable, for SC/ST/OBC)
- Affidavit stating the girl is unmarried
- Passport-size photographs (2-3)

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Online Process

1. **Visit the official portal:** Go to [https://escholarship.uk.gov.in](https://escholarship.uk.gov.in)
2. **Register:** Click on "New Registration" and enter your details (name, Aadhaar, mobile number)
3. **Login:** Use the credentials received via SMS to log in
4. **Select Scheme:** Choose "Gaura Devi Kanya Dhan Yojana" from the list
5. **Fill Application Form:** Enter personal details, educational qualifications, family income, and bank details
6. **Upload Documents:** Upload scanned copies of all required documents (each file should be under 200 KB, in JPG/PDF format)
7. **Submit:** Review all details carefully and submit the application
8. **Print Acknowledgment:** Download and print the application receipt with reference number

### Offline Process

1. **Collect Form:** Get the application form from the District Social Welfare Office or the Block Development Office
2. **Fill Details:** Complete the form with accurate information in block letters
3. **Attach Documents:** Attach self-attested photocopies of all required documents
4. **School Verification:** Get the form verified by your school principal
5. **Submit:** Submit the completed form at the District Social Welfare Office or through your school
6. **Collect Receipt:** Get an acknowledgment receipt with your application number

> **Note:** Applications are typically submitted through schools in bulk. Check with your school administration for the submission timeline.

## Benefits

| Benefit | Amount |
|---------|--------|
| Fixed Deposit in girl's name | ₹51,000 |
| Maturity after 5 years | Approximately ₹75,000+ (with interest) |

The ₹51,000 is deposited as a Fixed Deposit (FD) in a nationalized bank in the girl's name. The amount can be withdrawn after the FD matures (typically 5 years) or when the girl turns 25, whichever is earlier.

## Processing Time

- **Application Window:** Usually opens after Class 12 results are declared (June–September)
- **Verification:** 30–60 days after submission
- **Disbursement:** 2–4 months after verification is complete
- **FD Maturity:** 5 years from the date of deposit

## Important Tips

1. **Apply Early:** Submit your application as soon as the window opens after Class 12 results. Late applications may be rejected.
2. **Bank Account:** Ensure you have a bank account in your own name (not joint) linked to your Aadhaar before applying.
3. **School Coordination:** Many schools coordinate bulk applications — check with your school first before applying individually.
4. **Income Certificate:** Get a fresh income certificate (not older than 6 months) from the Tehsildar/SDM office.
5. **Track Application:** Use the reference number on the e-Scholarship portal to track your application status regularly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Can girls from private schools apply for Gaura Devi Kanya Dhan Yojana?
**A:** Yes, girls from recognized private schools in Uttarakhand can apply, provided they meet all other eligibility criteria including the family income limit.

### Q2: Is this scheme only for BPL families?
**A:** No. While BPL families are automatically eligible, APL families can also apply if their annual income is below ₹72,000 (rural) or ₹1,00,000 (urban).

### Q3: Can both daughters in a family apply?
**A:** Yes, up to two daughters per family can benefit from this scheme.

### Q4: When can the FD amount be withdrawn?
**A:** The Fixed Deposit matures after 5 years. The beneficiary can withdraw the full matured amount (principal + interest) after maturity or upon turning 25 years old.

### Q5: What if I got married after receiving the benefit?
**A:** If you received the FD before marriage, it remains valid. However, you must be unmarried at the time of application.

### Q6: Can I apply if I studied in another state but am a domicile of Uttarakhand?
**A:** No. You must have studied Class 12 in a recognized school within Uttarakhand to be eligible.

### Q7: Is there any reservation for SC/ST candidates?
**A:** The scheme does not have a separate quota, but SC/ST candidates from BPL families are given priority during processing.
