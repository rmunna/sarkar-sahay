## What is Gaura Devi Kanya Dhan Yojana?

The **Gaura Devi Kanya Dhan Yojana** (also linked with the **Nanda Gaura** scheme) is a girl child welfare scheme by the **Government of Uttarakhand**. Under this scheme, girls from BPL families who pass Class 12 (Intermediate) receive **₹50,000** as a fixed deposit to support their higher education or future needs.

The scheme aims to encourage girls' education and reduce the dropout rate at the higher secondary level in Uttarakhand.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Gender:** Must be a girl student
2. **Education:** Must have passed or be studying in **Class 12** from an Uttarakhand school
3. **Residency:** Must be a resident of Uttarakhand
4. **Income:** Family must be **BPL** or annual income below **₹15,976** (rural) / **₹21,206** (urban) — as per BPL criteria
5. **Age:** Must be **unmarried** and below **25 years** at the time of application
6. **Limit:** Maximum **two girls** per family can avail the benefit

## Benefits

| Benefit | Amount |
|---------|--------|
| Fixed Deposit on passing Class 12 | ₹50,000 |
| Maturity | After completing higher education or on turning a prescribed age |

> The amount is deposited as FD in the girl's name, ensuring long-term financial security.

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar Card** of the girl student
- **BPL Certificate** / Income Certificate (from Tehsildar)
- **Class 12 Marksheet / Admit Card** (from Uttarakhand board/school)
- **Family Ration Card**
- **Domicile Certificate** of Uttarakhand
- **Bank Account** (in the girl's name, Aadhaar-linked)
- **School Certificate** verifying enrollment/passing
- **Passport-size photographs**
- **Caste Certificate** (if applicable for SC/ST/OBC)

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Online Application

1. **Visit** [escholarship.uk.gov.in](https://escholarship.uk.gov.in) or the WCD Uttarakhand portal
2. **Register** with basic details and mobile number
3. **Select** "Gaura Devi Kanya Dhan Yojana"
4. **Fill** the application: student details, class 12 information, family income
5. **Upload** documents: marksheet, BPL certificate, Aadhaar, bank passbook
6. **Submit** and note the application ID
7. **Track** your application status online

### Through School

1. **Collect** the form from your school principal or office
2. **Fill** the form and attach all documents
3. **Submit** through the school — they forward it to the Block Education Officer
4. **School verification** is done before forwarding

### Offline (District Office)

1. **Visit** the **District Education Office** or **Women & Child Development Office**
2. **Submit** the filled form with documents
3. **Get** acknowledgement receipt

## Processing Time

- **Application Review:** 30-60 days
- **Verification:** By school and district education authorities
- **FD Creation:** After approval, ₹50,000 FD is created in the girl's name
- **Maturity:** As per FD terms

## Important Tips

1. **Apply Through School:** The easiest way is to apply through your school — they assist with the process
2. **BPL Certificate:** Ensure your family has a valid BPL card or income certificate
3. **Unmarried Condition:** Applicant must be unmarried at the time of application
4. **Keep Marksheet Safe:** Class 12 marksheet is essential for the application
5. **Two Girls Per Family:** Only two daughters per family can avail this benefit

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. How much money is given under this scheme?
**₹50,000** as a Fixed Deposit in the girl's name after passing Class 12.

### Q2. What is the income limit?
Family must be BPL or have rural income below ₹15,976 and urban income below ₹21,206 per year.

### Q3. Can married girls apply?
No, the applicant must be **unmarried** at the time of application.

### Q4. Can both sisters apply?
Yes, up to **two girls per family** can avail the benefit.

### Q5. When can the FD be withdrawn?
After the prescribed maturity period or as per the scheme guidelines for higher education expenses.

### Q6. Do I need to study in a government school?
Both government and recognized private school students from Uttarakhand are eligible.

### Q7. How do I apply?
Apply online at [escholarship.uk.gov.in](https://escholarship.uk.gov.in) or through your school.
