## What is Biju Swasthya Kalyan Yojana (BSKY)?

**Biju Swasthya Kalyan Yojana (BSKY)** is Odisha government's universal health insurance scheme that provides cashless treatment to all households that hold a state/central government ration card. Key features:

- **₹5 lakh/year** per family for all ration card holders
- **₹10 lakh/year** for families where the female member is the head

The scheme covers treatment at 200+ government and 900+ empanelled private hospitals across India (not just Odisha).

## Who is Eligible?

**All households with a valid ration card** under NFSA (National Food Security Act) in Odisha — both SFAS and NFSA cards. This covers:
- Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) card holders
- Priority Household (PHH) card holders
- State Food Security Scheme card holders

> No separate income verification required beyond the ration card. If you have a valid Odisha ration card under NFSA, you are eligible.

---

## BSKY Smart Health Card

Every eligible beneficiary receives a **BSKY Smart Health Card**:
- Contains your photo, name, family details
- Used for cashless hospital admission
- Linked to your ration card number

How to get your BSKY card:
1. Visit [bsky.odisha.gov.in](https://bsky.odisha.gov.in) → Download e-Card
2. Or visit the nearest **Common Service Centre (CSC)** or Mo Seva Kendra

---

## How to Use BSKY at a Hospital

1. Visit any **BSKY empanelled hospital** (show the BSKY card or ration card)
2. Go to the **BSKY help desk / Ayushman Bharat desk** (scheme is integrated)
3. Present your BSKY card or ration card + Aadhaar
4. Hospital verifies eligibility in real-time
5. **Cashless admission and treatment** proceeds
6. Hospital bills BSKY directly

---

## Diseases and Procedures Covered

BSKY covers nearly all conditions including:
- Cancer (chemotherapy, radiotherapy, surgery)
- Heart diseases and surgery
- Kidney/liver transplants
- Brain and spine surgery
- Neonatal ICU
- Burn treatment
- Orthopaedic surgeries
- Maternal health complications

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. I am from Odisha but my treatment is at a hospital in Bhubaneswar. Does BSKY work there?**

Yes. BSKY works at any empanelled hospital anywhere in India — not limited to your home district or even Odisha. Check the hospital list at bsky.odisha.gov.in.

**2. My family doesn't have a ration card. Can we apply for BSKY?**

BSKY eligibility is linked to the ration card. If your family is not in the NFSA database, you need to first apply for a ration card through your local Civil Supplies office. Once you have the ration card, BSKY enrollment follows automatically.

**3. Our family head is a woman. Do we automatically get ₹10 lakh coverage?**

Yes. If the female member is the household head in the ration card database, the family is entitled to ₹10 lakh coverage. Verify at bsky.odisha.gov.in.

**4. My husband needed a heart bypass. Does BSKY cover it?**

Yes. Open heart surgery including bypass is covered under BSKY. The hospital must be empanelled — check the hospital list at bsky.odisha.gov.in.

**5. How do I check if my ration card is in the BSKY database?**

Go to [bsky.odisha.gov.in](https://bsky.odisha.gov.in) → Beneficiary Status → Enter your ration card number or Aadhaar to check.
