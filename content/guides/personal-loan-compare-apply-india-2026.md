# Personal Loan in India 2026 — Compare, Apply & Get Approved

A personal loan is an unsecured loan (no collateral needed) used for any purpose — medical emergency, home renovation, wedding, travel, or debt consolidation. With banks competing aggressively in 2026, rates have dropped to **10.5%–11%** for good credit profiles.

This guide compares the best personal loan offers in India and shows you how to get approved at the lowest rate.

> **Note:** Interest rates are as of May 2026. Verify current rates before applying. Personal loan rates depend heavily on your credit score and income profile.

## Bank vs App Loans — Key Differences

### Bank Personal Loans (SBI, HDFC, ICICI)
✅ Lower interest rates (10.5%–16%)  
✅ Higher loan amounts (up to ₹50 lakh)  
✅ Longer tenure (up to 6 years)  
✅ Regulated, safe  
❌ Stricter eligibility (CIBIL 700+, stable income)  
❌ Takes 2–5 working days for disbursal

### App Loans (KreditBee, MoneyView, Navi)
✅ Instant disbursal (minutes to hours)  
✅ Accessible with lower CIBIL or informal income  
✅ Smaller amounts available (₹5,000 onwards)  
❌ Very high interest (17%–45% per year)  
❌ High processing fees  
❌ Short tenure (1–2 years)

**Bottom line:** If you qualify for a bank loan, always prefer it. App loans are only for genuine emergency when banks reject you — and even then, use sparingly.

## How to Get the Lowest Rate

1. **Check CIBIL Score first** — [freecibil.com](https://freecibil.com) or through your bank app. 750+ gets best rates. Improve if below 700
2. **Apply to your existing bank first** — Pre-approved offers for existing account/credit card holders often have 0.5–1% lower rates and faster approval
3. **Compare using aggregator** — [BankBazaar](https://www.bankbazaar.com/personal-loan.html) or [PaisaBazaar](https://www.paisabazaar.com/personal-loan/) show live offers from 30+ lenders. Soft inquiry — doesn't affect credit score
4. **Negotiate Processing Fee** — 100% negotiable. Ask for waiver directly with the bank representative
5. **Avoid multiple simultaneous applications** — Each hard inquiry reduces your CIBIL score by 3–8 points. Compare first, then apply to one lender

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the minimum salary for a personal loan in India?**
Most banks require ₹20,000–₹25,000/month minimum take-home salary. Some NBFCs approve loans for ₹15,000/month income.

**Can I get a personal loan without salary slips?**
Self-employed individuals can get loans with ITR and bank statements instead of salary slips. Some app-based lenders use bank statement analysis and don't need salary slips.

**How fast can I get a personal loan in India?**
HDFC, ICICI, Axis Bank offer disbursal in 4–24 hours for pre-approved customers. For new applicants, 2–5 working days. App loans (Navi, MoneyView) disburse in minutes to hours.

**Does applying for a personal loan affect my credit score?**
Yes — each application triggers a hard inquiry, reducing CIBIL score by 3–8 points. This is why you should compare first (using aggregators which do soft inquiries) and then apply to 1–2 lenders maximum.

**Can I close a personal loan before the tenure ends?**
Yes — prepayment/foreclosure is allowed. Most banks charge 2%–4% of outstanding amount as foreclosure fee. Check this fee before taking a loan if you expect to prepay.
