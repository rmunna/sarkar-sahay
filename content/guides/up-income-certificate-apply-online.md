# UP Income Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **UP income certificate** (आय प्रमाण पत्र) is issued by the Revenue Department, Government of Uttar Pradesh. It is a mandatory document for UP scholarship applications, BPL ration card eligibility, government scheme benefits, and various government job reservations. Applications are processed through the **eSathi portal** at [edistrict.up.gov.in](https://edistrict.up.gov.in).

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [edistrict.up.gov.in](https://edistrict.up.gov.in) → "आय प्रमाण पत्र". Issued within **10–30 days** by the Tehsildar. Certificate is valid for **1–3 years** depending on purpose.

---

## Who Needs UP Income Certificate?

- **UP Scholarship (NSP / state portal)** — mandatory for OBC, SC, ST, General scholarship
- **BPL/Ration card** application — to prove below poverty line income
- **Government scheme benefits** — PM Awas Yojana, Kisan credit, etc.
- **Income-based fee waiver** — colleges, hospitals
- **Court affidavits** and legal proceedings
- **Caste-based reservation** applications (income limit criteria)

---

## Income Certificate vs Aay Praman Patra in UP

In UP, "income certificate" and "aay praman patra" (आय प्रमाण पत्र) are the same document. It certifies the annual family income and is issued by:
- **Rural:** Lekhpal + Tehsildar verification
- **Urban:** SDM office (Sub-Divisional Magistrate)

---

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory)
- **Ration card** (if BPL/APL — shows family members)
- **Voter ID** (additional address proof)
- Self-declaration of income (affidavit on ₹10 stamp paper or online declaration)
- For salaried: **salary slip** or **employer certificate**
- For farmers: **Khasra/Khatauni** (land records from Bhulekh UP)
- Passport-size photograph

## How to Apply Online — eSathi Portal

1. Go to [edistrict.up.gov.in](https://edistrict.up.gov.in) or [esathi.up.gov.in](https://esathi.up.gov.in)
2. Click **"नागरिक लॉगिन"** (Citizen Login) → Register if new user
3. Register with mobile number (OTP verification)
4. After login, go to **"सेवाएं"** (Services) → **"राजस्व विभाग"** (Revenue Department)
5. Click **"आय प्रमाण पत्र"** (Income Certificate)
6. Fill the form:
   - Applicant name (as per Aadhaar)
   - Address (district, tehsil, gram panchayat)
   - Annual family income (self-declaration)
   - Purpose of income certificate
7. Upload documents (Aadhaar, supporting income proof)
8. Pay the fee: **₹10–30** (varies by tehsil)
9. Submit — note the application reference number

---

## How to Apply via Jan Seva Kendra / CSC

1. Visit nearest **Jan Seva Kendra** (JSK) or **Common Service Centre** (CSC)
2. Provide Aadhaar, documents, and declare income
3. Operator fills and submits form on eSathi
4. Service charge: approximately ₹30–60 (CSC fee)
5. Collect acknowledgement

---

## Status Check

1. Go to [edistrict.up.gov.in](https://edistrict.up.gov.in)
2. Click **"आवेदन की स्थिति"** (Application Status) or log in → "Track Application"
3. Enter application number
4. View status: submitted → verification pending → approved / rejected

---

## Download Income Certificate UP

Once approved:
1. Log in to eSathi portal
2. Go to **"मेरे प्रमाण पत्र"** (My Certificates)
3. Click **"डाउनलोड"** next to your income certificate
4. PDF is digitally signed — valid for official use
5. Verify at [edistrict.up.gov.in](https://edistrict.up.gov.in) → "प्रमाण पत्र सत्यापन" (Certificate Verification)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What income should I declare for UP income certificate?

Declare your **total annual family income** from all sources: farming, salary, business, pension, rent. For families with no formal income, declare based on approximate agricultural/daily wage earnings. The Lekhpal verifies during field visit.

### Is eSathi and edistrict UP the same portal?

Yes — **eSathi (esathi.up.gov.in)** and **edistrict.up.gov.in** are the same portal under the UP government's citizen services platform. Both redirect to the same login and services.

### Income certificate rejected in UP — why?

Common reasons: documents not clear/uploaded, Aadhaar mismatch with name, income declaration seems too high/low for the area, or Lekhpal couldn't verify at given address. Check the rejection reason in eSathi portal and re-apply with corrections.

### Can I get UP income certificate offline?

Yes — visit your Tehsil office (Tehsildar / SDM office for urban). Submit the application form with documents. The process is the same but online is faster.
