## What is Namo Saraswati Vigyan Sadhana Yojana?

Namo Saraswati Vigyan Sadhana Yojana is a scholarship scheme by the Government of Gujarat to encourage students to pursue **science stream** (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics/Biology) in Class 11 and 12. Announced in the Gujarat Budget 2024-25, it aims to increase the number of students choosing science and build a strong STEM workforce.

### Benefits

| Class | Annual Scholarship |
|-------|-------------------|
| Class 11 (Science) | ₹10,000 |
| Class 12 (Science) | ₹15,000 |
| **Total** | **₹25,000** |

## Who is Eligible?

- Students studying **Science stream** (PCM/PCB) in Class 11 or 12
- Must be enrolled in a **government or grant-in-aid school** in Gujarat
- Must be a **resident of Gujarat**
- Both **boys and girls** are eligible
- Must have scored required minimum marks in Class 10 board examination (typically 50%+)
- Must maintain satisfactory attendance

## Documents Required

- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
- Class 10 marksheet (showing eligibility for science stream)
- School bonafide certificate (Class 11/12 science stream)
- Bank account in student's name
- Bank passbook copy
- Passport-size photograph

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

1. **Schools compile** eligible student data and upload to the education department portal
2. **Students submit** Aadhaar, bank details, and marksheet to school
3. **School verifies** and forwards to the education department
4. **Department approves** eligible applications
5. **Scholarship credited** to student's bank account via DBT

Students may also check [Digital Gujarat](https://digitalgujarat.gov.in) for direct application options.

## Processing Time

- Data compilation by schools: Start of academic year
- Verification and approval: **2-4 months**
- Disbursement: Within the academic year

## Fees

**No fees** for applying.

## Important Tips

1. **Choose science early** — Decide on science stream before Class 11 admissions
2. **Maintain marks** — Satisfactory academic performance is typically required for continuation
3. **Girls can combine** — Girl students may be eligible for both Namo Saraswati and [Namo Lakshmi Yojana](/guide/gj-namo-lakshmi)
4. **Open bank account** — Have a personal bank account ready before the academic year
5. **Inform your school** — Ensure your school has included you in the scholarship list

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Is this scholarship only for government school students?
Yes, it is for students in **government and grant-in-aid schools**. Private school students are not eligible.

### Q2. Can arts or commerce students apply?
No. Only **science stream** students (PCM or PCB) are eligible.

### Q3. Is there an income limit?
No specific income limit has been announced for this scheme.

### Q4. Can I get both Namo Saraswati and Namo Lakshmi?
Girl students in science stream may be eligible for both. Check with the education department for combinability rules.

### Q5. What minimum marks are required in Class 10?
Typically, students need a minimum of **50% in Class 10 board exams**, but check the latest notification for exact criteria.
