# UKPSC Exam Guide — Uttarakhand PSC Eligibility, Syllabus & Tips

The Uttarakhand Public Service Commission (UKPSC) conducts the PCS (Provincial Civil Services) exam for recruitment to various administrative positions in Uttarakhand. Known as UKPCS or Uttarakhand PCS, it is the premier state-level civil services exam. This guide covers the full process from eligibility to final selection.

## Overview

| Detail | Information |
|--------|-------------|
| Conducting Body | Uttarakhand Public Service Commission (UKPSC) |
| Exam Name | UKPCS (Combined State Civil/Upper Subordinate Services) |
| Posts | SDM, Dy.SP, Nayab Tehsildar, District Supply Officer, and more |
| Level | State Level |
| Selection Process | Prelims → Mains → Interview |
| Job Location | Uttarakhand |

## Posts Under UKPCS

- Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM)
- Deputy Superintendent of Police (Dy.SP)
- Nayab Tehsildar
- Block Development Officer (BDO)
- District Supply Officer
- District Panchayat Raj Officer
- District Welfare Officer
- Assistant Registrar (Cooperative Societies)
- District Information Officer

## Eligibility Criteria

### Age Limit

| Category | Minimum Age | Maximum Age |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| General | 21 years | 42 years |
| OBC (Uttarakhand) | 21 years | 47 years |
| SC / ST (Uttarakhand) | 21 years | 47 years |
| PwD | 21 years | 52 years |

**Note:** Age as on 1st July of the year of exam.

### Educational Qualification

- Bachelor's Degree from a recognised university
- For certain posts, professional qualifications may be needed

### Nationality

- Must be an Indian citizen
- Domicile of Uttarakhand required for state-reserved categories
- Knowledge of Hindi in Devanagari script is mandatory

## Exam Pattern

### Prelims

| Paper | Subject | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|-------|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| Paper I | General Studies | 150 | 150 | 2 hours |
| Paper II | CSAT (Aptitude) | 100 | 100 | 2 hours |

- Paper II is qualifying (33% minimum)
- Only Paper I marks count for prelims merit
- **Negative marking:** 1/3rd mark per wrong answer
- Objective type (MCQ)

### Mains

| Paper | Subject | Marks | Duration |
|-------|---------|-------|----------|
| Paper I | General Hindi | 150 | 3 hours |
| Paper II | Essay | 150 | 3 hours |
| Paper III | General Studies I | 200 | 3 hours |
| Paper IV | General Studies II | 200 | 3 hours |
| Paper V | General Studies III | 200 | 3 hours |
| Paper VI | General Studies IV | 200 | 3 hours |
| **Total** | | **1100** | |

- Hindi paper is qualifying
- Merit based on Essay (150) + GS Papers (800) = **950 marks**

### Interview

- **Marks:** 150
- **Total for ranking:** 950 + 150 = **1100**

## Syllabus Overview

### Prelims — General Studies
- Current national and international events
- Indian History — Ancient, Medieval, Modern
- Uttarakhand History — Gorkha rule, Tehri state, freedom movement, state formation
- Geography of India and Uttarakhand
- Indian Polity and Governance
- Indian and Uttarakhand Economy
- General Science (up to 10th level)
- Environment and Ecology — biodiversity, Uttarakhand's ecological significance
- Uttarakhand culture, art, tourism

### Prelims — CSAT
- Comprehension, Logical reasoning, Analytical ability
- Decision-making, Data interpretation
- Numerical ability, Mental ability

### Mains

**GS I:** Indian History, Culture, Heritage, Geography, Society (with Uttarakhand specifics)
**GS II:** Governance, Constitution, Polity, Social Justice, International Relations
**GS III:** Economic Development, Technology, Environment, Biodiversity, Disaster Management
**GS IV:** Ethics, Integrity, Aptitude — case studies and theoretical concepts

**Essay:** Two essays from a choice of topics (current affairs, social, philosophical, abstract)

## How to Apply

1. Visit [ukpsc.gov.in](https://ukpsc.gov.in) or [ukpsc.net.in](https://ukpsc.net.in)
2. Register with valid email and mobile number
3. Apply when PCS notification is released
4. Fill in all required details — personal, educational, domicile
5. Upload photograph, signature, and certificates
6. Pay application fee online
7. Submit and download confirmation

## Important Dates Pattern

- **Notification:** No fixed schedule; roughly every 1–2 years
- **Application Window:** 21–30 days
- **Prelims:** 3–6 months after notification
- **Mains:** 4–8 months after prelims result
- **Interview:** 2–3 months after mains result
- **Final Result:** 15–24 months from notification

## Preparation Tips

### 1. Uttarakhand-Specific Topics
UKPSC increasingly focuses on Uttarakhand's history, geography, culture, ecology, and governance. Study *Uttarakhand Samanya Gyan* and state government publications.

### 2. Disaster Management is Important
Uttarakhand is a disaster-prone state (floods, landslides, earthquakes). This topic carries extra weight in GS III.

### 3. NCERT + Standard References
- NCERT Class 6–12 for foundation
- Polity: Laxmikanth, Economy: Ramesh Singh
- Ethics: Lexicon Ethics for GS IV

### 4. Essay Writing Practice
The Essay paper (150 marks) is significant. Practice writing essays weekly — one philosophical, one current affairs-based. Aim for structured, well-argued pieces.

### 5. Previous Year Papers
UKPSC papers are available on the official website. Study last 5–7 years' trends carefully.

### 6. Hindi Paper — Don't Ignore
The qualifying Hindi paper tests grammar, translation, and essay. Even native Hindi speakers should practice formal writing.

### 7. Current Affairs — Uttarakhand Focus
Follow Dainik Jagran (Dehradun edition), Amar Ujala. Track Uttarakhand Budget, Char Dham project, eco-sensitive zone policies, and state schemes.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Is there negative marking in UKPCS Prelims?
Yes. 1/3rd of the mark allotted per question is deducted for wrong answers.

### Is Uttarakhand domicile mandatory?
For reserved category candidates (SC/ST/OBC), Uttarakhand domicile is generally required. Unreserved candidates from any state can apply.

### How many posts are filled through UKPCS?
Typically 80–200 posts across various services. Numbers vary each recruitment cycle.

### Can I write the exam in English?
Yes, the exam is bilingual (Hindi and English). You can answer in either language (except the Hindi qualifying paper).

### Is UKPCS easier than UPSC?
UKPCS is less competitive in terms of applicant numbers, but the standard is solid. Uttarakhand-specific topics require focused preparation.

### What is the hill area allowance?
Officers posted in hill areas of Uttarakhand receive an additional hill area/difficult area allowance ranging from ₹1,000–₹5,300 depending on the area classification.

### How important is the interview?
The interview carries 150 marks out of 1100 total — about 14%. It can make a significant difference in final ranking. Prepare for questions on Uttarakhand affairs, your background, and administrative scenarios.
