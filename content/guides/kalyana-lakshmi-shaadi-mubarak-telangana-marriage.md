## What are Kalyana Lakshmi and Shaadi Mubarak?

Telangana government runs two parallel marriage grant schemes:
- **Kalyana Lakshmi** — for brides from **SC, ST, BC, and Economically Backward** communities
- **Shaadi Mubarak** — for brides from **minority communities** (Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain, Zoroastrian)

**Both schemes give the same amount: ₹1,00,116** (one lakh one hundred and sixteen rupees) per marriage — one of the highest marriage grants in India.

---

## Key Benefits

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Grant amount | ₹1,00,116 per marriage |
| Payment | DBT to bride's Aadhaar-linked bank account |
| Who applies | Bride (not groom or parents — bride applies directly) |
| Application timing | Before marriage (14 days before) or within 6 months after |
| Target | SC/ST/BC (Kalyana Lakshmi) and minorities (Shaadi Mubarak) |

---

## Who is Eligible?

**For Kalyana Lakshmi (bride's requirements):**
- She is from **SC, ST, BC, or EBC** community
- Her family annual income is **below ₹2 lakh** (SC/ST) or **below ₹2 lakh** (BC/EBC)
- She is a **Telangana resident** with valid domicile
- She is **18 years or older** on the date of marriage
- It is her **first marriage**

**For Shaadi Mubarak:**
- She is from a **minority community** (Muslim, Christian, Sikh, etc.)
- Same income and age criteria as above
- First marriage

---

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar Card** — bride's (mandatory)
2. **Caste/community certificate** — SC/ST/BC/minority
3. **Age proof** — confirming bride is 18+ (Aadhaar with DoB)
4. **Income certificate** — family income < ₹2 lakh from MRO/Tahsildar
5. **Domicile/residence certificate** — Telangana residency
6. **Bank passbook** — bride's own account (Aadhaar-linked)
7. **Marriage invitation card** — for advance application OR marriage certificate for post-marriage application
8. **Groom's Aadhaar** — for record

## How to Apply — Step by Step

Applications can be submitted **before the marriage (14 days in advance)** or **within 6 months after marriage**.

### Step 1 — Apply Online

Go to **[telanganaepass.cgg.gov.in](https://telanganaepass.cgg.gov.in)**:
1. Click on **Kalyana Lakshmi / Shaadi Mubarak**
2. Register with bride's Aadhaar
3. Fill personal, family, and marriage details
4. Upload all documents
5. Submit and note Application ID

### Step 2 — Meeseva / CSC Application (Alternative)

Visit your nearest **Meeseva centre** for assisted application.

### Step 3 — Revenue Officer Verification

The Mandal Revenue Officer (MRO) or Tehsildar verifies:
- Caste/community certificate
- Income certificate
- Age (bride ≥ 18 years)
- Residency

### Step 4 — Approval and Payment

After approval, ₹1,00,116 is directly credited to the **bride's bank account** via DBT.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. My daughter belongs to BC-A community. Is she eligible for Kalyana Lakshmi?**

Yes. BC-A (and all BC categories) are covered under Kalyana Lakshmi.

**2. Can my father apply for the grant on my behalf?**

The application must be in the **bride's name** with her Aadhaar — but parents/relatives can assist in filling the online form. The bank account and Aadhaar must be the bride's.

**3. The grant was approved but money hasn't come 2 months after marriage. What do I do?**

Check [telanganaepass.cgg.gov.in](https://telanganaepass.cgg.gov.in) with your Application ID. If status shows "Approved" but money not received, check if your Aadhaar is linked to your bank account. Contact your nearest Meeseva for follow-up.

**4. My daughter had a civil marriage (registered). Does it count?**

Yes. Both religious and civil/registered marriages are eligible. Submit the marriage registration certificate as proof.

**5. I applied before marriage (14 days advance). When will I receive money?**

After the marriage date, the MRO verifies the marriage took place. The grant is typically released within 30–45 days of marriage verification.
