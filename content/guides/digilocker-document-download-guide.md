## What is DigiLocker?

DigiLocker is a flagship initiative of the Government of India under the Ministry of Electronics & IT (MeitY). It provides every Indian citizen a secure cloud-based platform to store, access, and share official documents digitally. Documents fetched via DigiLocker are legally valid and equivalent to original documents as per the IT Act, 2000.

**Key benefits:**
- **Free** for all Indian citizens
- **Legally valid** — accepted by all government agencies, banks, and airports
- **Secure** — Aadhaar-authenticated access
- **1,000+ issuing organisations** — Central and state government departments

## How to Register on DigiLocker

### Step 1: Visit DigiLocker

- **Website:** [https://digilocker.gov.in](https://digilocker.gov.in)
- **Android App:** Download "DigiLocker" from Google Play Store
- **iOS App:** Download from Apple App Store

### Step 2: Click "Sign Up"

Click on **"Sign Up"** and enter your **mobile number**.

### Step 3: Verify OTP

Enter the OTP sent to your mobile number.

### Step 4: Set Username and Password

Create a **username** (or use your Aadhaar number) and set a **6-digit security PIN**.

### Step 5: Link Aadhaar

Enter your **12-digit Aadhaar number** and verify with Aadhaar OTP. This is required to fetch government-issued documents.

Your DigiLocker account is now ready.

## How to Download Documents on DigiLocker

### Aadhaar Card (e-Aadhaar)

1. Log in to DigiLocker
2. Go to **"Issued Documents"** section
3. Search for **"UIDAI"** (issuing organisation)
4. Select **"Aadhaar"**
5. Your Aadhaar will be auto-fetched (since your account is Aadhaar-linked)
6. Click **"Download"** to save the PDF

### PAN Card (e-PAN)

1. Go to **"Issued Documents"** → Search for **"Income Tax Department"** or **"CBDT"**
2. Select **"PAN Verification Record"** or **"e-PAN"**
3. Enter your **PAN number** (e.g., ABCDE1234F)
4. The document will be fetched and added to your locker
5. Click **"Download"** to save

### Driving Licence

1. Go to **"Issued Documents"** → Search for your **state transport department** (e.g., "Transport Department, Karnataka")
2. Select **"Driving Licence"**
3. Enter your **DL number**
4. The licence will be fetched from the Parivahan database
5. Download and save

### Vehicle Registration Certificate (RC)

1. Go to **"Issued Documents"** → Search for **"Ministry of Road Transport"** or your state transport department
2. Select **"Vehicle Registration Certificate"**
3. Enter your **vehicle registration number** (e.g., KA01AB1234)
4. The RC will be fetched from the Vahan database
5. Download and save

### Class 10 / Class 12 Marksheets

1. Go to **"Issued Documents"** → Search for your **board** (e.g., "CBSE", "ICSE", or your state board)
2. Select **"Class X Marksheet"** or **"Class XII Marksheet"**
3. Enter your **roll number** and **year of passing**
4. The marksheet will be fetched if available in the digital database
5. Download and save

> **Note:** Not all state boards have integrated with DigiLocker. CBSE, ICSE, and many state boards are available. Check the "Issuers" section for your board.

### Other Available Documents

DigiLocker supports 1,000+ issuers. Other popular documents include:
- **Voter ID (EPIC)** — from Election Commission
- **CBSE/ICSE Certificates** — Migration, passing certificates
- **Insurance Policies** — LIC, health insurance
- **University Degrees** — Select universities
- **Caste/Income/Domicile Certificates** — Select state governments
- **COVID Vaccination Certificate** — from CoWIN

## How to Upload Your Own Documents

1. Go to **"Uploaded Documents"** section
2. Click **"Upload"**
3. Select the document type and upload a scanned PDF or image
4. Uploaded documents are stored in your cloud locker but are **not** verified by the issuer

> **Important:** Only "Issued Documents" (fetched from government databases) are legally equivalent to originals. Uploaded documents are for your convenience.

## Common Errors and Fixes

### "Aadhaar Authentication Failed"

- **Cause:** Aadhaar number mismatch, biometrics locked, or UIDAI server issue
- **Fix:** Ensure your Aadhaar number is correct. If biometrics are locked, unlock at [https://uidai.gov.in](https://uidai.gov.in). Try again after some time if it's a server issue

### "Document Not Found" or "No Records Found"

- **Cause:** The issuing organisation hasn't digitised your document, or the details entered don't match their records
- **Fix:** Verify the exact details (DL number, PAN, roll number) match your original document. If the issuer hasn't integrated with DigiLocker, the document won't be available

### "OTP Not Received"

- **Cause:** Network issue or Aadhaar-linked mobile number is different
- **Fix:** Ensure your mobile number is linked to Aadhaar. Wait 2–3 minutes and retry. If the number has changed, update it at an Aadhaar Seva Kendra

### "Unable to Fetch Document — Issuer Service Unavailable"

- **Cause:** The issuing organisation's server is down or under maintenance
- **Fix:** Try again after a few hours. This is a temporary issue on the issuer's end

### "Account Locked After Multiple Failed Attempts"

- **Cause:** Too many wrong PIN/OTP attempts
- **Fix:** Wait 30 minutes and try again, or use the **"Forgot PIN"** option to reset your security PIN

## Important Tips

1. **DigiLocker documents are legally valid** — You can show them on your phone at airports, banks, and government offices instead of carrying originals
2. **Link Aadhaar for maximum benefit** — Most documents require Aadhaar authentication to fetch
3. **Use the mobile app** — Much more convenient for quick document access on the go
4. **Check issuer availability** — Not all organisations have integrated yet; check the "Issuers" list on the portal
5. **Keep your PIN safe** — Your 6-digit security PIN protects all your documents

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Is DigiLocker free?

Yes, DigiLocker is completely free for all Indian citizens. There are no charges for account creation, document fetching, or storage.

### Q2: Are DigiLocker documents accepted at airports?

Yes, DigiLocker documents (issued documents section) are accepted as valid ID proof at all Indian airports as per government notification.

### Q3: Can I download my driving licence from DigiLocker?

Yes, if your state transport department has integrated with DigiLocker. Search for your state's transport department under "Issued Documents" and enter your DL number.

### Q4: Is DigiLocker safe and secure?

Yes, DigiLocker uses Aadhaar-based authentication, 256-bit encryption, and is hosted on government cloud infrastructure. It complies with ISO 27001 security standards.

### Q5: What if my document is not available on DigiLocker?

Not all organisations have digitised their records. You can upload a scanned copy for your personal storage, but it won't have the legal validity of an issued document. Contact the issuing organisation to request DigiLocker integration.

### Q6: Can I share DigiLocker documents with others?

Yes, DigiLocker allows you to share documents via a secure URI (link) that the recipient can verify. This is useful for verification by banks, employers, and government offices.

### Q7: How many documents can I store in DigiLocker?

There is a storage limit of **1 GB** for uploaded documents. However, issued documents (fetched from government databases) don't count toward this limit.
