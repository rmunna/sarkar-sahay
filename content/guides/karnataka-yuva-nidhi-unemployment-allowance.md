## What is the Karnataka Yuva Nidhi Scheme?

The Yuva Nidhi Scheme is one of the five guarantee schemes launched by the Karnataka state government to provide **monthly financial assistance to unemployed youth** who have completed their degree or diploma courses. The scheme helps young graduates stay financially independent while they search for employment or prepare for competitive exams.

Under the scheme, eligible beneficiaries receive a monthly allowance directly into their bank accounts via **Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)** for a maximum of **two years** or until they secure employment — whichever comes first.

### How Much Money Do You Get?

| Category | Monthly Allowance | Annual Total |
|----------|-------------------|--------------|
| Degree holders (UG/PG) | ₹3,000 | ₹36,000 |
| Diploma holders | ₹1,500 | ₹18,000 |
| Maximum duration | 2 years | — |
| Payment mode | Direct Bank Transfer (DBT) | — |

The scheme also provides access to **skill training programmes** during the benefit period, helping youth become more employable or self-employed.

## Yuva Nidhi Eligibility Criteria

To qualify for the Yuva Nidhi scheme, you must meet all of the following conditions:

### Who Can Apply

- **Permanent resident of Karnataka** with at least 6 years of education in the state
- Must have completed a **degree (UG/PG) or diploma** in the academic year 2022-23 or later
- Must have been **unemployed for at least 6 months** after passing out
- Aadhaar must be **linked to your bank account**

### Who is NOT Eligible

- Youth already employed in government or private sector (full-time or part-time)
- Those pursuing **higher education** after their degree or diploma
- Beneficiaries of **ESI, PF, or NPS** schemes
- Self-employed individuals or those who have taken loans under state/central self-employment schemes
- Those receiving a **stipend from apprenticeship** programmes
- Beneficiaries of similar schemes from state or central government

## Required Documents

Keep these documents ready before starting your application:

1. **Aadhaar Card** — linked to your bank account
2. **USN (University Seat Number)** or Diploma Register Number
3. **Degree or Diploma certificate** (UG/PG/Diploma)
4. **Domicile proof** — CET number, Ration Card, Caste Certificate, Income Certificate, or Residence Certificate
5. **Bank account details** — Aadhaar-linked bank account
6. **Mobile number** — for OTP verification
7. **Email ID**
8. **Unemployment self-declaration**

## How to Apply for Yuva Nidhi Online (Seva Sindhu Portal)

### Step 1: Check Your Academic Details on NAD Portal

Before applying, verify that your academic records are available on the Karnataka NAD (National Academic Depository) portal:

1. Visit [nad.karnataka.gov.in](https://nad.karnataka.gov.in)
2. Click on **Yuva Nidhi Eligibility Check**
3. Select your qualification (degree or diploma)
4. Enter your university name, registration number, year of passing, and name as per Aadhaar
5. Click **Send** to check if your records are available

If your records are not found, contact your university or board to update your academic information on the NAD portal.

### Step 2: Apply on Seva Sindhu

1. Visit the **Seva Sindhu Guarantee Schemes Portal** at [sevasindhugs.karnataka.gov.in](https://sevasindhugs.karnataka.gov.in)
2. Click on **Yuva Nidhi Yojana**
3. **Log in** with your Seva Sindhu credentials or **register** as a new user
4. Read and accept the **self-declaration consent**
5. Fill in the application form — personal details, contact information, educational qualifications, and bank account details
6. Upload scanned copies of required documents if prompted
7. Click **Save Annexure**, preview the application, and click **Submit**
8. **Note down your application reference number** for tracking

### Offline Application

You can also apply in person at:

- **Grama One** centres
- **Bangalore One** or **Karnataka One** offices
- **Bapuji Seva Kendras**
- **Nada Kacheris**

Collect the application form, fill it out, attach the required documents, and submit it at any of these centres.

## How to Check Yuva Nidhi Payment Status

1. Visit the Seva Sindhu Services portal at [sevasindhuservices.karnataka.gov.in](https://sevasindhuservices.karnataka.gov.in)
2. **Log in** with your registered credentials
3. Under **Apply for Services**, click **View All Available Services**
4. Search for **Yuva Nidhi Application Status Check**
5. Enter your **Application Reference Number**
6. Select the **month** for which you want to check DBT status
7. Click **Get Payment Details**

The screen will display your application status, current DBT payment status, and self-declaration status.

## Self-Declaration: Important Requirement

Beneficiaries must submit a **periodic self-declaration** confirming that they are still unemployed to continue receiving payments. If you miss the self-declaration deadline, your payments may be paused.

In March 2025, the Karnataka government extended the self-declaration period by three months to provide additional flexibility to beneficiaries. Check the Seva Sindhu portal regularly for updated deadlines.

## Common Problems and Solutions

### Academic Details Not Found on NAD Portal
**Problem:** Your degree or diploma details are not showing on the NAD portal.
**Solution:** Contact your university or college examination section and request them to upload your academic records to NAD. This is a prerequisite for applying.

### Application Stuck in Pending Status
**Problem:** Your application has been pending for weeks without any update.
**Solution:** Call the Yuva Nidhi helpline at **1800-599-9918** (toll-free) or **1902**. You can also visit your nearest Seva Sindhu centre for assistance.

### Payment Not Received Despite Approval
**Problem:** Application is approved but DBT payment is not credited.
**Solution:** Verify that your Aadhaar is linked to your bank account. Check your bank's NPCI mapping to ensure Aadhaar is mapped to the correct bank. Contact the helpline if the issue persists.

### Aadhaar-Bank Account Linking Issues
**Problem:** Unable to link Aadhaar to bank account.
**Solution:** Visit your bank branch with Aadhaar card and request Aadhaar seeding. You can also check linking status at [npci.org.in](https://www.npci.org.in).

### Self-Declaration Missed
**Problem:** Missed the self-declaration deadline and payments stopped.
**Solution:** Log in to Seva Sindhu and submit the self-declaration immediately. Payments typically resume after successful declaration. Contact the helpline if the option is not available.

### Seva Sindhu Portal Not Working
**Problem:** Website is slow, unresponsive, or showing errors.
**Solution:** Try during off-peak hours (early morning or late evening). Clear your browser cache and try a different browser. If the issue persists, apply offline at a Bangalore One or Karnataka One centre.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. What is the Yuva Nidhi scheme amount for graduates?

Graduates (both UG and PG degree holders) receive **₹3,000 per month** under the Yuva Nidhi scheme. Diploma holders receive **₹1,500 per month**.

### 2. How long can I receive Yuva Nidhi benefits?

You can receive the monthly allowance for a **maximum of 2 years** from the date of approval, or until you get a job — whichever comes first.

### 3. Can I apply for Yuva Nidhi if I am pursuing higher education?

**No.** If you enrol in higher education (such as a PG course after graduation), your Yuva Nidhi benefits will be discontinued. The scheme is only for those who are not pursuing further studies.

### 4. What happens if I get a job while receiving Yuva Nidhi?

Your benefits will stop immediately once you secure employment. Beneficiaries are required to declare their employment status through the periodic self-declaration on the Seva Sindhu portal.

### 5. Can I apply offline for the Yuva Nidhi scheme?

**Yes.** You can apply offline by visiting Grama One, Bangalore One, Karnataka One centres, Bapuji Seva Kendras, or Nada Kacheris with the required documents.

### 6. Is there an age limit for the Yuva Nidhi scheme?

There is no specific age limit mentioned, but applicants must have completed their degree or diploma in the academic year 2022-23 or later and must have been unemployed for at least 6 months after passing out.

### 7. My university is not listed on the NAD portal. What should I do?

Contact your university's examination department and ask them to upload your academic records to the National Academic Depository. Without NAD verification, your application cannot be processed.

### 8. Can both a husband and wife apply for Yuva Nidhi?

Yes, both can apply independently if they individually meet all the eligibility criteria — each must be an unemployed graduate or diploma holder from Karnataka.

### 9. Will Yuva Nidhi benefits affect my eligibility for other Karnataka guarantee schemes?

The Yuva Nidhi scheme is independent of other guarantee schemes like Gruha Lakshmi, Gruha Jyoti, or Shakti. However, you cannot receive benefits from similar unemployment assistance schemes simultaneously.

### 10. How do I update my bank account details for Yuva Nidhi?

Log in to the Seva Sindhu portal, navigate to your Yuva Nidhi application, and update your bank account details. Ensure the new bank account is also linked to your Aadhaar card.
