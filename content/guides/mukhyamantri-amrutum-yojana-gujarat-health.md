## What is Mukhyamantri Amrutum (MA) Yojana?

**Mukhyamantri Amrutum (MA) Yojana** is Gujarat government's flagship health insurance scheme that provides **₹5 lakh per year cashless medical treatment** to BPL and lower-income families. The scheme covers:
- Tertiary care hospitalization (surgeries, ICU, cancer treatment)
- Day care procedures
- Pre and post-hospitalization expenses (3 days before, 15 days after)

There is also **MA Vatsalya Yojana** — an extended version for families earning ₹4–6 lakh, with the same ₹5 lakh cover but beneficiaries pay a small annual premium.

## Who is Eligible for MA Yojana (Free)?

**You ARE eligible if:**
- You are a Gujarat resident
- Your family holds a **BPL ration card** (Below Poverty Line) OR an **APL ration card** with income ≤ ₹4 lakh/year
- Your family name appears in the **MA Yojana beneficiary database** (automatically enrolled if BPL card holders)

---

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar Card** — all family members
2. **Ration card** — BPL/APL
3. **MA Card** — if already issued (check if your family has one)
4. **Bank account details** (for any direct transfers)

---

## How to Check if You Are Enrolled

Many BPL families in Gujarat are **automatically enrolled** in MA Yojana. Check your status:
1. Go to **[ma.gujarat.gov.in](https://ma.gujarat.gov.in)**
2. Enter your ration card number or Aadhaar
3. Check if your family is in the beneficiary list

If not enrolled, apply through:
- **District Health Office** in your district
- **Common Service Centre (CSC)**

---

## How to Use MA Yojana at a Hospital

1. Visit any **MA Yojana empanelled hospital** (900+ hospitals in Gujarat, including government and private)
2. Go to the **MA Yojana help desk / Arogya Mitra**
3. Show your **Aadhaar + ration card** (or MA card if you have one)
4. The hospital verifies your eligibility in real-time
5. **Cashless admission** — no upfront payment for covered procedures
6. Hospital claims directly from the government

---

## Diseases and Procedures Covered

MA Yojana covers a comprehensive list including:
- Cancer treatment (chemotherapy, radiotherapy, surgery)
- Heart surgery (bypass, valve replacement)
- Kidney transplant / dialysis
- Neurosurgery (brain, spine)
- Orthopaedic surgery (joint replacement, fractures)
- Diabetes complications
- Burns
- Maternal health emergencies

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. We have a BPL card. Are we automatically enrolled in MA Yojana?**

Yes. Gujarat BPL ration card holders are typically auto-enrolled. Verify at [ma.gujarat.gov.in](https://ma.gujarat.gov.in). If your family is not showing, apply through the District Health Office.

**2. My mother needs dialysis. Is it covered under MA Yojana?**

Yes. Kidney dialysis and related renal treatments are covered under MA Yojana. Ongoing treatment requires approval from the MA Yojana empanelled hospital.

**3. The empanelled hospital near me is in a different district. Can I go there?**

Yes. MA Yojana is valid across Gujarat — you can use any empanelled hospital in any Gujarat district.

**4. What is the difference between MA Yojana and PM-JAY?**

PM-JAY covers ₹5 lakh nationally for SECC-listed families. MA Yojana covers ₹5 lakh for Gujarat-specific BPL families. Both can work together — your family may be eligible under one or both. In Gujarat, these are often integrated.

**5. I belong to an APL family but my income is ₹3 lakh. Can I apply?**

Yes. MA Yojana covers APL families with income ≤ ₹4 lakh. Apply through your District Health Office with your APL ration card and income certificate.
