# Haryana Domicile Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **domicile certificate** (निवास प्रमाण पत्र) in Haryana is issued by the Revenue Department (SDM / Tehsildar) and is required for NEET Haryana state quota (85%) admissions, HSSC/HCS government jobs, Haryana state scholarships, and scheme eligibility. Applications go through **Antyodaya Saral** — Haryana's integrated services portal. **Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP)** is mandatory.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [saralharyana.gov.in](https://saralharyana.gov.in). **PPP (Parivar Pehchan Patra) required** before applying. Issued by SDM within **7–21 days**.

## Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP) — First Step

**PPP is mandatory for all Haryana government services.** Without PPP, you cannot apply for a domicile certificate.

- PPP is Haryana's family identity card (8-digit Family ID)
- Apply at nearest **CSC centre / Atal Seva Kendra** — free of cost
- Or update online at [meraparivar.haryana.gov.in](https://meraparivar.haryana.gov.in)
- PPP auto-fills family details (income, members, address) from the state database
- Once you have PPP, domicile application is faster (data pre-filled)

## Why You Need Haryana Domicile Certificate

- **NEET Haryana state quota (85%)** — domicile mandatory for UHSR (University of Health Sciences Rohtak) counselling
- **HSSC (Haryana Staff Selection Commission)** — most posts require Haryana domicile
- **HCS (Haryana Civil Services) / HPSC** — state government jobs
- **Haryana state scholarship** — Post-Matric Scholarship for Haryana residents
- **Mukhyamantri Antyodaya Parivar Utthan Yojana** — scheme eligibility
- **Mahila Samridhi / Kisan Credit** — state scheme beneficiary verification

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory — with Haryana address)
- **PPP (Parivar Pehchan Patra)** — Family ID (8-digit) — **mandatory**
- **Ration card** (Haryana ration card)
- **Voter ID** (Haryana voter enrollment)
- **School Transfer Certificate (TC)** — from Haryana schools
- **Utility bills** — electricity/water bills at Haryana address
- **Property documents / Jamabandi** — land records (for rural applicants)
- Self-declaration of Haryana residency period
- Passport-size photograph

## How to Apply Online — Antyodaya Saral Portal

1. Go to [saralharyana.gov.in](https://saralharyana.gov.in)
2. Register / log in with mobile number (OTP) or PPP Family ID
3. Navigate to **"Revenue Department"** → **"Domicile Certificate"**
4. System auto-fills details from PPP database
5. Fill remaining fields:
   - Duration of residence in Haryana
   - Purpose of certificate
6. Upload documents (Aadhaar, voter ID, school TC, utility bills)
7. Pay fee: **₹10–₹30** (online)
8. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via Antyodaya Kendra / CSC

1. Visit nearest **Atal Seva Kendra** or **CSC (Common Service Centre)**
2. Carry Aadhaar, PPP card, voter ID, school TCs
3. Operator submits on Antyodaya Saral
4. Service charge: approximately ₹30–60
5. Collect acknowledgement

---

## Status Check & Download

1. Go to [saralharyana.gov.in](https://saralharyana.gov.in) → **"Track Application"**
2. Enter application reference number
3. Once approved: download digitally signed PDF
4. Valid for UHSR NEET counselling, HSSC, HPSC applications

## NEET Haryana State Quota 2026

For **NEET 2026 Haryana state quota** (UHSR Rohtak / government medical college counselling):
1. Haryana domicile certificate required
2. Applicant or parent must be Haryana domicile holder
3. Students studying in Haryana schools for the prescribed period also qualify (check UHSR notification)
4. Certificate must be SDM/Tehsildar issued — Antyodaya Saral digital certificate accepted
5. Apply well before NEET results — 7–21 day processing

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I don't have PPP — can I still apply?

**No — PPP is mandatory** for all Antyodaya Saral services including domicile certificate. Get your PPP first (free at any CSC/Atal Seva Kendra with Aadhaar + family details). Takes 1–3 days.

### HSSC — is Haryana domicile mandatory for all posts?

**Yes, for most Haryana state government posts** (HSSC Group C/D), Haryana domicile is mandatory. Some Group A posts (HCS) may have different provisions — always check the specific HSSC/HPSC advertisement.

### How many years of residence for Haryana domicile?

Haryana typically requires **5 continuous years** of residence OR being born in Haryana. If you have PPP (which captures long-term residence), the Antyodaya Saral system can verify this automatically.
