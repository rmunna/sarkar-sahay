## Why Is DigiLocker OTP Not Coming?

DigiLocker uses OTP-based authentication linked to your **Aadhaar-registered mobile number** for login. If you're not receiving the OTP, you won't be able to access your digital documents. This is one of the most common DigiLocker complaints.

This guide covers **all reasons why DigiLocker OTP fails** and **how to fix each one**.

## Common Reasons for OTP Failure

### 1. Wrong Mobile Number Linked to Aadhaar

DigiLocker sends OTP to the mobile number registered with your **Aadhaar**. If your current number is different from what's on Aadhaar, you won't receive the OTP.

**Fix:**
- Check your Aadhaar-linked mobile at [UIDAI](https://uidai.gov.in) → "Verify Mobile/Email"
- If the number is old/wrong, update it by visiting an **Aadhaar Enrolment Centre** (mobile update cannot be done online)
- Carry your Aadhaar card and the new SIM for biometric verification

### 2. Network or Signal Issues

Poor mobile network can delay or block SMS delivery.

**Fix:**
- Ensure you have **good network signal**
- Try switching between **4G/5G and 2G** mode — SMS sometimes works better on 2G
- Restart your phone and try again
- Check if your phone's **SMS inbox is full** — delete old messages

### 3. DND (Do Not Disturb) Is Active

If DND is enabled on your mobile number, transactional SMS may get blocked by some carriers.

**Fix:**
- Dial **1909** from your phone and disable DND
- Or send SMS: **STOP** to **1909**
- Wait 24 hours for DND deactivation, then retry

### 4. DigiLocker Server Overload

During result seasons (CBSE, university results), DigiLocker servers face heavy traffic, causing OTP delays or failures.

**Fix:**
- Wait and try after **30 minutes to 1 hour**
- Try during **off-peak hours** (early morning or late night)
- Use the **DigiLocker mobile app** instead of the website — it sometimes works better during high traffic

### 5. Aadhaar Not Linked to DigiLocker Account

If you're a new user trying to sign up, or your Aadhaar isn't properly linked, OTP generation itself may fail.

**Fix:**
- Go to [DigiLocker Sign Up](https://www.digilocker.gov.in) and enter your **Aadhaar number**
- Ensure you enter the **12-digit Aadhaar number correctly**
- The system will send OTP to your Aadhaar-linked mobile — not any other number

### 6. OTP Expired

DigiLocker OTPs are valid for **3-5 minutes**. If you enter the OTP after it expires, login will fail.

**Fix:**
- Click **"Resend OTP"** to get a fresh code
- Enter the OTP as soon as you receive it
- Don't request multiple OTPs rapidly — wait for one to arrive first

## Step-by-Step Fix Process

### Step 1: Verify Your Aadhaar Mobile Number

1. Visit [UIDAI portal](https://uidai.gov.in)
2. Click **"My Aadhaar"** → **"Verify Mobile/Email"**
3. Enter your Aadhaar number and the mobile number you think is linked
4. It will confirm if the number matches or not

### Step 2: Check SMS and Network

1. Ask someone to send you a regular SMS — confirm SMS is working
2. Check if your **inbox is full** (some phones block new SMS when storage is full)
3. Disable **DND** by calling 1909
4. Restart your phone

### Step 3: Try Alternative Login Methods

1. **Use the DigiLocker app** — download from Google Play Store or Apple App Store
2. Try **"Sign in with Aadhaar"** option instead of mobile number
3. If you had set a **username/password** previously, use that to log in

### Step 4: Clear Browser Cache (If Using Website)

1. Open your browser settings
2. Clear **cache and cookies**
3. Try in **Incognito/Private mode**
4. Use a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge)

### Step 5: Wait and Retry

If it's a server-side issue:
1. Wait **30-60 minutes**
2. Check [DigiLocker Twitter](https://twitter.com/diaborin) for any downtime announcements
3. Try during **non-peak hours** — early morning (6-8 AM) works best

### Step 6: Contact DigiLocker Support

If nothing works:
1. Email: **support@digilocker.gov.in**
2. Call DigiLocker helpline (check the official website for current number)
3. Raise a ticket on the DigiLocker portal under "Help" section
4. Include your Aadhaar number (masked) and describe the exact error message

## If You've Changed Your Mobile Number

If you no longer have access to your Aadhaar-linked number:

1. **Visit the nearest Aadhaar Enrolment Centre**
2. Carry: Aadhaar card + new SIM card (active) + any ID proof
3. Request **mobile number update** — requires biometric verification
4. Processing time: **5-10 working days**
5. Once updated, DigiLocker OTP will come to your new number

Book an appointment at [appointments.uidai.gov.in](https://appointments.uidai.gov.in) to avoid long queues.

## Important Tips

1. **Always keep your Aadhaar mobile number active** — don't let the SIM expire
2. **Don't click Resend OTP repeatedly** — wait at least 60 seconds between attempts
3. **During CBSE result season**, expect delays — try early morning
4. **The DigiLocker app** is generally more reliable than the website for OTP delivery
5. **Save your DigiLocker username and password** as a backup login method

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. DigiLocker OTP goes to my old number. How do I change it?

DigiLocker OTP is sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile number. To change it, update your mobile number in Aadhaar by visiting an Aadhaar Enrolment Centre with biometric verification.

### Q2. How many times can I request OTP resend?

You can request OTP resend multiple times, but there may be a cooldown period. If you exceed the limit, you'll be temporarily blocked — wait 30 minutes and try again.

### Q3. Can I log into DigiLocker without OTP?

Yes, if you've set up a username and password during registration. Use the "Sign in with Username" option. You can also use the Aadhaar-based sign-in.

### Q4. DigiLocker shows "Technical Error" during login. What do I do?

This usually means the server is overloaded. Clear browser cache, try incognito mode, or switch to the mobile app. If it persists, try after a few hours.

### Q5. Is DigiLocker OTP different from Aadhaar OTP?

They use the same system. DigiLocker authentication goes through UIDAI's OTP service, so the OTP is sent by UIDAI to your Aadhaar-linked mobile.

### Q6. OTP is coming but DigiLocker says "Invalid OTP". Why?

The OTP may have expired (valid for 3-5 minutes), or you may be entering an old OTP. Click Resend, wait for the new OTP, and enter it immediately.

### Q7. Can I use DigiLocker with a non-Aadhaar mobile number?

No. DigiLocker requires Aadhaar-based authentication. Your login mobile number must be the one linked to your Aadhaar. There's no way to use a different number.
