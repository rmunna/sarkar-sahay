# Ration Card eKYC: How to Complete Aadhaar Verification Online

Aadhaar-based eKYC (electronic Know Your Customer) is mandatory for ration card holders to receive subsidised grains under the National Food Security Act (NFSA). If your Aadhaar is not linked to your ration card, you may face issues while collecting rations. This guide explains how to complete Aadhaar seeding and eKYC verification for your ration card.

## What is Ration Card eKYC?

Ration card eKYC is the process of linking and verifying your Aadhaar number with your ration card. It involves two parts:

1. **Aadhaar Seeding** — linking your Aadhaar number to your ration card in the PDS database
2. **eKYC Verification** — verifying your identity through biometric (fingerprint/iris) or OTP authentication

Once eKYC is complete, you can:
- Collect rations using biometric authentication at any ePoS-enabled FPS
- Use **One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC)** portability across India
- Avoid ration card fraud and duplicate beneficiary issues

## Who Needs to Complete eKYC?

- **All NFSA beneficiaries** — every member listed on the ration card should have Aadhaar linked
- **Children under 5** — Aadhaar is not mandatory but recommended; guardian's Aadhaar is used instead
- **New ration card applicants** — Aadhaar seeding is done during application itself
- **Existing card holders** — must complete eKYC if not already done

## Methods to Complete Aadhaar eKYC

### Method 1: Online Through State PDS Portal

1. **Visit your state's PDS portal** (e.g., nfsa.gov.in → state links, or directly visit state portals)
2. **Log in** using ration card number or registered mobile number
3. **Navigate to "Aadhaar Seeding"** or "eKYC" section
4. **Enter Aadhaar number** for each family member
5. **Verify via OTP** — an OTP is sent to the mobile number linked with your Aadhaar
6. **Enter the OTP** and submit
7. **Confirmation** — you'll receive a success message once Aadhaar is linked
8. **Repeat** for all family members on the ration card

### Method 2: Biometric Verification at FPS (Fair Price Shop)

1. **Visit your allotted FPS** that has an **ePoS (electronic Point of Sale)** machine
2. **Carry your ration card and Aadhaar card**
3. **Request the FPS dealer** to complete your eKYC
4. **Place your fingerprint** on the biometric device or scan your iris
5. **Verification happens in real-time** against the UIDAI database
6. **Confirmation** — the dealer will confirm successful authentication
7. **Get it done for all family members** in one visit

### Method 3: At Common Service Centre (CSC)

1. **Visit your nearest CSC** or e-Seva Kendra
2. **Carry Aadhaar card and ration card** (original)
3. **The CSC operator** will enter your details and perform biometric/OTP verification
4. **Service charge** — ₹15-30 per member (varies by state)
5. **Collect the confirmation slip**

### Method 4: At Taluk/Block Supply Office

1. **Visit the local Food & Civil Supplies office** or Taluk Supply Office
2. **Carry Aadhaar and ration card**
3. **Staff will perform biometric verification** using the office's biometric device
4. **Free of charge**
5. **Useful when FPS doesn't have a working ePoS device**

## Linking All Family Members' Aadhaar

All members on the ration card should have their Aadhaar linked individually. Here's how:

1. **List all members** on your ration card
2. **Ensure each member has an Aadhaar card** — if not, apply at the nearest Aadhaar Enrolment Centre. See our [Aadhaar Card guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
3. **Complete eKYC for each member** using any of the methods above
4. **For children under 5** — use the parent/guardian's Aadhaar for verification
5. **Check status** on the state PDS portal — it should show "Aadhaar Seeded" against each member's name

## Common Issues and Solutions

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| OTP not received | Ensure Aadhaar-linked mobile is active; try after some time; use biometric method instead |
| Biometric authentication failed | Clean fingers and try again; use iris scan; visit UIDAI centre to update biometrics. See our [Biometric Failed Fix guide](/guide/ration-card-biometric-failed-fix) |
| Aadhaar number mismatch | Verify correct Aadhaar number; check for typos; visit Food office for correction |
| Server error on portal | Try during off-peak hours (early morning or late evening) |
| "Already seeded" message | Your Aadhaar may be linked to another ration card — contact Food office to resolve |
| Child doesn't have Aadhaar | Use parent's Aadhaar for children under 5; for older children, get Aadhaar first |

## Deadline for eKYC

- The central government has made Aadhaar seeding **mandatory for all NFSA beneficiaries**
- States periodically set deadlines; missing the deadline may result in **temporary suspension of ration entitlement**
- Even without a strict deadline, complete eKYC as soon as possible to avoid distribution issues
- Check your state's Food Department website for current deadlines

## Important Tips

1. **Keep Aadhaar mobile number active** — OTP verification requires the mobile number linked to your Aadhaar to be working
2. **Biometric is more reliable** — if OTP method fails, visit FPS or CSC for biometric verification
3. **Check seeding status regularly** — visit the state portal to confirm all family members are "Aadhaar Seeded"
4. **Update Aadhaar if details mismatch** — if your name on Aadhaar doesn't match the ration card, update either document first to avoid eKYC failure
5. **Don't share OTP with anyone** — government officials will never ask for your OTP over phone; verify only at official centres

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Is Aadhaar mandatory for ration card?
**A:** Yes, Aadhaar seeding is mandatory for all NFSA beneficiaries for biometric authentication at FPS. Without it, you may not be able to collect subsidised grains.

### Q2: Can I complete ration card eKYC from my phone?
**A:** Yes, if your state portal has a mobile-friendly website or app (like Mera Ration app), you can complete OTP-based eKYC from your phone.

### Q3: What if biometric verification keeps failing?
**A:** Try cleaning your fingers, use a different finger, or try iris scan. If the issue persists, visit a UIDAI centre to update your biometrics. See our [Biometric Failed Fix guide](/guide/ration-card-biometric-failed-fix).

### Q4: My Aadhaar is linked to a different ration card. What do I do?
**A:** Contact your local Food & Civil Supplies office. They can de-link your Aadhaar from the wrong ration card and link it to the correct one. Carry both Aadhaar and your ration card.

### Q5: Is eKYC needed for children?
**A:** For children under 5, Aadhaar is not strictly mandatory — the parent/guardian's Aadhaar is used. For children above 5, it is recommended to get their own Aadhaar linked.

### Q6: What is the Mera Ration app?
**A:** Mera Ration is a mobile app by the Department of Food & Public Distribution. It helps NFSA beneficiaries check entitlement, find nearby FPS, view recent transactions, and verify Aadhaar seeding status. Available on Google Play Store.

### Q7: Will I lose my rations if eKYC is not done?
**A:** You may face temporary suspension of ration delivery if eKYC is not completed by the state's deadline. Complete it at the earliest to avoid disruption.
