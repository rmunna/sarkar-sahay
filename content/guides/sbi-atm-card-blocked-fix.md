# SBI ATM Card Blocked — How to Unblock Your Debit Card

If your SBI ATM/debit card is blocked — whether from entering the wrong PIN multiple times, suspicious activity, or if you blocked it yourself — this guide explains exactly how to unblock it or get a replacement.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent informational platform and is not affiliated with SBI or any government body.

---

## Why Was Your SBI ATM Card Blocked?

| Reason | What Happens |
|--------|-------------|
| Wrong PIN entered 3 times | Card is temporarily blocked for 24 hours |
| You hotlisted the card (lost/stolen) | Card is permanently blocked — new card needed |
| Suspicious transaction detected | SBI may block the card as a security measure |
| Card expired | Old card stops working; new card issued automatically |
| KYC not updated | Account restrictions may affect card usage |

## How to Unblock SBI Debit Card

### Method 1: Wait 24 Hours (Auto-Unblock)

If your card was blocked due to **3 wrong PIN attempts**, it automatically unblocks after **24 hours**. Simply wait and try again with the correct PIN.

### Method 2: Unblock via YONO App

1. Open the **YONO SBI** app and log in
2. Go to **Service Requests** → **ATM Card Services**
3. Select **Unblock ATM Card**
4. Choose your account and card
5. Verify with OTP
6. Your card will be unblocked immediately

### Method 3: Unblock via SBI Internet Banking

1. Log in to [onlinesbi.sbi](https://www.onlinesbi.sbi/)
2. Go to **e-Services** → **ATM Card Services**
3. Select **Unblock ATM Card**
4. Select your account number and card
5. Confirm with transaction password/OTP
6. Card gets unblocked instantly

### Method 4: Unblock via SMS

Send an SMS from your **registered mobile number**:

```
UNBLOCK <last 4 digits of card> to 567676
```

Example: `UNBLOCK 4589` to **567676**

You will receive a confirmation SMS once the card is unblocked.

### Method 5: Visit SBI Branch

1. Visit your **home branch** with:
   - Aadhaar card or other valid ID
   - Passbook or account details
   - Your blocked debit card
2. Fill the card unblock request form
3. The branch will process the request (usually same day)

## How to Generate a New SBI ATM PIN

If you've forgotten your PIN and that's why the card got blocked, generate a new one:

### Via SBI ATM:
1. Visit any SBI ATM
2. Insert your debit card
3. Select **PIN Generation** → **Green PIN**
4. Enter your **registered mobile number** and **account number**
5. An OTP will be sent to your mobile
6. Enter the OTP at the ATM
7. Set your new 4-digit PIN
8. Confirm the PIN and collect your card

### Via YONO App:
1. Open YONO → **Service Requests** → **ATM Card Services**
2. Select **ATM PIN Generation**
3. Verify via OTP
4. Set a new PIN

### Via Internet Banking:
1. Log in to [onlinesbi.sbi](https://www.onlinesbi.sbi/)
2. Go to **e-Services** → **ATM Card Services** → **ATM PIN Generation**
3. Select your card and set a new PIN via OTP verification

## What to Do If Your SBI Card Is Lost or Stolen

If your card is lost or stolen, **block it immediately** to prevent misuse:

### Block (Hotlist) via These Methods:

1. **Call SBI helpline:** 1800-425-3800 or 1800-11-2211 (24/7, toll-free)
2. **SMS method:** Send `BLOCK <last 4 digits>` to **567676**
3. **YONO app:** Service Requests → ATM Card Services → Block/Hotlist Card
4. **Internet Banking:** e-Services → ATM Card Services → Hotlist
5. **Visit branch:** Carry ID proof and request card hotlisting

> **Important:** Once hotlisted, the card is **permanently blocked** and cannot be unblocked. You must apply for a new card.

### Apply for a Replacement Card:

1. Visit your home branch with Aadhaar and passbook
2. Fill the debit card replacement form
3. New card will be delivered to your registered address within **7-10 working days**
4. You can also request a new card via YONO app or Internet Banking

**Replacement card charges:**

| Card Type | Replacement Fee |
|-----------|----------------|
| Classic Debit Card | ₹200 + GST |
| Global Debit Card | ₹300 + GST |
| Platinum Debit Card | ₹350 + GST |

*Fees may vary. Check with your branch for current charges.*

## Important Tips

1. **Never write your PIN** on the card or keep it in your wallet
2. **Cover the keypad** when entering your PIN at an ATM
3. **Enable SMS alerts** for all debit card transactions
4. If you suspect **skimming** (unauthorized charges), block the card immediately and file a complaint
5. **Report unauthorized transactions** within 3 days to limit your liability to zero (as per RBI guidelines)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does SBI ATM card stay blocked after wrong PIN?
The card is automatically unblocked after 24 hours. You can also unblock it immediately via YONO, Internet Banking, or SMS.

### Can I unblock my SBI card without visiting the branch?
Yes. Use the YONO app, SBI Internet Banking, or SMS to unblock your card from home.

### Will I get the same card number after unblocking?
Yes. Unblocking restores your existing card. Your card number, expiry date, and CVV remain the same. Only hotlisted cards require a new card with a new number.

### Can I use my SBI debit card for online transactions if it's blocked at ATM?
No. If the card is blocked, it won't work at ATMs, POS machines, or for online transactions.

### How do I check if my SBI debit card is active?
Log in to YONO or Internet Banking → ATM Card Services → View Card Status. You can also check by attempting a balance inquiry at an SBI ATM.

### What is the difference between blocking and hotlisting?
Blocking is temporary and reversible. Hotlisting is permanent — used for lost or stolen cards. A hotlisted card must be replaced with a new one.

### Can I generate a new ATM PIN online?
Yes. Use SBI Internet Banking or the YONO app to generate a new Green PIN. You can also do it at any SBI ATM using OTP verification.
