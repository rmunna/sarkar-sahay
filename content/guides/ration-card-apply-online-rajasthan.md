# Rajasthan Ration Card Apply Online 2026

The Rajasthan ration card is issued by the **Department of Food and Civil Supplies, Rajasthan**. It is linked to the **Jan Aadhaar card** — Rajasthan's state family ID — and is the primary document for accessing subsidised foodgrains under NFSA (National Food Security Act) and state schemes.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [food.raj.nic.in](https://food.raj.nic.in) or your nearest E-Mitra kiosk using your Jan Aadhaar ID. Free ration entitlement: 5 kg grain per person/month for eligible families.

---

## Ration Card Types in Rajasthan

| Type | Category | Entitlement |
|------|----------|-------------|
| **Priority Household (PHH/BPL)** | Families below poverty line | 5 kg wheat per person/month @ ₹2/kg |
| **Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY)** | Poorest of the poor | 35 kg grain/family/month @ ₹2/kg |
| **State BPL** | State-identified poor | Subsidised grain at state rates |
| **APL (Above Poverty Line)** | Non-BPL families | Market rate or limited subsidy |
| **BPL Fold** | Migrant/floating households | Portability across Rajasthan |

---

## Who Is Eligible?

**For PHH / BPL ration card:**
- Family income below the state-defined BPL threshold
- Not a government employee or income taxpayer
- Not already holding a ration card in Rajasthan
- Must have a valid **Jan Aadhaar family ID**

**For AAY (Antyodaya):**
- Landless agricultural labourers
- Marginal farmers (less than 1 hectare)
- Rural artisans with very low income
- Persons with disabilities, widows, elderly living alone

**Who is NOT eligible:**
- Government employees (central or state)
- Income taxpayers
- Families owning large agricultural land (more than 1 acre irrigated or 2 acres unirrigated in some cases — check state norms)
- Families with a ration card in another state (surrender the old one first)

---

## Documents Required

- **Jan Aadhaar card** (mandatory — head of family)
- Aadhaar cards of all family members to be included
- Passport-size photographs of all members
- Proof of residence (electricity bill, water bill, rent agreement)
- Income certificate (for BPL classification)
- Caste certificate (if SC/ST — affects priority)
- Bank account details (for DBT linkage)

> **Jan Aadhaar is mandatory.** If you don't have one, register at [janaadhaar.rajasthan.gov.in](https://janaadhaar.rajasthan.gov.in) first. Your ration card will be linked to your Jan Aadhaar family ID.

## How to Apply Online

**Method 1: E-Mitra Portal (Recommended)**

1. Visit any **E-Mitra kiosk** in your area (government-authorised CSC)
2. The E-Mitra operator will fill the application on [emitra.rajasthan.gov.in](https://emitra.rajasthan.gov.in)
3. Provide your **Jan Aadhaar ID** and Aadhaar for all members
4. Submit supporting documents (scanned copies)
5. Receive an **acknowledgement slip** with tracking number
6. The Food Department verifies and issues the ration card within 30–45 days

**Method 2: Food Department Portal (Self-Service)**

1. Go to [food.raj.nic.in](https://food.raj.nic.in)
2. Click on **"Ration Card"** → **"Apply New Ration Card"**
3. Log in with your Jan Aadhaar ID or mobile number
4. Fill in household details, member details, income information
5. Upload documents
6. Submit and save the acknowledgement number

**Method 3: Gram Panchayat / Ward Office**

1. Visit your nearest Gram Panchayat (rural) or Ward Office (urban)
2. Submit a written application with documents
3. The Gram Sachiv / Ward Officer forwards it to the Food Inspector
4. Verification and issue within 45 days

## Status Check

**Online:**
1. Go to [food.raj.nic.in](https://food.raj.nic.in)
2. Click **"Ration Card"** → **"Application Status"**
3. Enter your acknowledgement number or Jan Aadhaar ID
4. View current status (received / under verification / approved / rejected)

**Via E-Mitra:**
Visit any E-Mitra kiosk with your acknowledgement number.

**Via SMS:**
Send SMS: `RCARD <Application Number>` to **5616200**

## Download Ration Card Online

Once approved, download your digital ration card:
1. Go to [food.raj.nic.in](https://food.raj.nic.in) → **"Ration Card"** → **"Download/Print"**
2. Enter your ration card number or Jan Aadhaar ID
3. Download the PDF — valid for use at PDS shops

## Common Issues and Fixes

**Name not matching with Aadhaar:**
Get the name corrected in Aadhaar first (at nearest Aadhaar Seva Kendra), then reapply.

**Jan Aadhaar not linked:**
Visit E-Mitra kiosk to link Aadhaar with your Jan Aadhaar family ID.

**Application rejected:**
Check rejection reason in the portal. Most common reasons: income too high, duplicate application, incorrect Jan Aadhaar details. Correct and reapply.

**Ration dealer not giving ration:**
Call toll-free: **1800-180-6127** or file complaint at the food department portal.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I apply for Rajasthan ration card without Jan Aadhaar?

No. Jan Aadhaar card is mandatory for ration card in Rajasthan. Register for Jan Aadhaar at janaadhaar.rajasthan.gov.in first.

### Can I apply online from home without E-Mitra?

Yes — through food.raj.nic.in with your Jan Aadhaar login. However, E-Mitra is more reliable as the operator handles the technical steps.

### How many members can be on one ration card?

All family members living in the same household can be included. Each member gets 5 kg grain/month under NFSA.

### Can I include a newborn baby?

Yes — apply for member addition at the food department portal or E-Mitra after the birth certificate is issued.

### Is the Rajasthan ration card valid across India?

Under the One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC) scheme, you can use your Rajasthan ration card at any PDS shop in India. Use the Mera Ration app.
