# Aadhaar Operator Near Me: How to Find Certified Aadhaar Operators

Need to enrol for a new [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online), update your details, or get biometric updates done? You will need to visit a certified Aadhaar enrolment or update centre. UIDAI provides an official locator tool to find operators near your location. This guide explains how to find one, what types of centres exist, and the applicable charges.

## How to Find Aadhaar Centres Using UIDAI Locator

### Online Method (Recommended)

1. Visit [https://appointments.uidai.gov.in/easearch.aspx](https://appointments.uidai.gov.in/easearch.aspx)
2. Select your **State** and **District**
3. Optionally select **Sub-District**, **Village/Town/City**, and **Pin Code**
4. Enter the security code (captcha)
5. Click **"Search"**
6. The page displays a list of active Aadhaar centres with:
   - Centre name and address
   - Contact details
   - Working hours
   - Type of centre (permanent or camp)

### Using myAadhaar Portal

1. Visit [https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"Locate an Enrolment Centre"** from the services menu
3. Enter your state, district, and pin code
4. View the list of nearby centres

### By Calling UIDAI Helpline

- Call **1947** (toll-free) from any phone
- Provide your state and city
- The executive will share details of nearby Aadhaar centres

## Types of Aadhaar Operators

| Operator Type | Description | Where to Find |
|---|---|---|
| **Bank branches** | Scheduled banks authorized by UIDAI | SBI, PNB, Bank of India, and other public/private banks |
| **Post offices** | India Post branches with Aadhaar facility | Head post offices and select sub-post offices |
| **CSC (Common Service Centre)** | Government-run digital service centres | Found in most villages and towns under Digital India |
| **Government offices** | Registrar offices, municipal bodies | District collectorate, tehsil offices |
| **Permanent enrolment centres** | Dedicated Aadhaar centres | Usually in urban areas with high footfall |
| **Camp-based centres** | Temporary camps organized by UIDAI/registrars | Schools, community halls (announced locally) |

## Services Available at Aadhaar Centres

- **New Aadhaar enrolment** — for first-time applicants including children
- **Demographic update** — name, address, date of birth, gender, mobile, email
- **Biometric update** — fingerprints, iris, photo
- **[Baal Aadhaar](/guide/baal-aadhaar-card-apply)** — Aadhaar for children under 5 years
- **Mandatory biometric update** — UIDAI recommends updating biometrics every 10 years

## Charges at Aadhaar Centres

| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| New Aadhaar enrolment (first time) | **Free** |
| Mandatory biometric update (every 10 years) | **Free** |
| Demographic update (name, address, DOB, gender) | ₹50 per update |
| Biometric update (optional, within 10 years) | ₹100 |
| Re-print of Aadhaar letter (PVC card) | ₹50 (online order) |
| New enrolment for children (Baal Aadhaar) | **Free** |

**Important:** The first-ever Aadhaar enrolment is always free. No operator can charge any fee for new enrolment. If someone demands money for new enrolment, report it to UIDAI.

## How to Book an Appointment

Walk-in visits are accepted at most centres, but booking an appointment saves waiting time.

1. Visit [https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/)
2. Log in with your Aadhaar number and OTP
3. Select **"Book an Appointment"**
4. Choose your city and preferred centre
5. Select date and time slot
6. Confirm the booking — you will receive an SMS with the appointment details

**Note:** Appointment booking is available only for updates. New enrolments are generally walk-in.

## Documents to Carry

When visiting an Aadhaar centre, carry:

- **For new enrolment:** One proof of identity (PAN, voter ID, passport) + one proof of address (utility bill, bank statement, ration card)
- **For updates:** Original supporting document for the field being updated + current Aadhaar card or enrolment slip
- **For children under 5:** Parent's Aadhaar card + birth certificate

See the [complete list of Aadhaar documents](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) for details.

## Important Tips

1. **Check centre timings before visiting** — many centres operate only on weekdays from 9:30 AM to 5:30 PM
2. **Carry original documents** — operators need to verify originals before scanning copies
3. **Take the acknowledgement slip** — it contains your Update Request Number (URN) to track status
4. **Do not pay extra** — official charges are fixed by UIDAI; report overcharging on 1947
5. **Book appointments for updates** — walk-in queues can be very long, especially in urban areas

## How to Report a Fraudulent Operator

If an operator demands extra charges, refuses service, or behaves improperly:

1. Note the centre name, operator name, and enrolment/update ID
2. Call UIDAI helpline at **1947**
3. Email complaint to **help@uidai.gov.in**
4. File a complaint on [https://uidai.gov.in/](https://uidai.gov.in/) under Grievance Redressal

## FAQs

### Is Aadhaar enrolment free?

Yes. First-time Aadhaar enrolment is completely free for all residents. No operator can charge any fee for new enrolment.

### Can I update Aadhaar at any bank branch?

No. Only bank branches authorized by UIDAI as Aadhaar enrolment/update centres can provide this service. Use the UIDAI locator to check if your bank branch is listed.

### Do I need an appointment to visit an Aadhaar centre?

For new enrolments, most centres accept walk-ins. For updates, booking an appointment through myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in is recommended to avoid long queues.

### Can I update my mobile number at an Aadhaar centre?

Yes. Mobile number and email updates require biometric verification, so you must visit an Aadhaar centre in person. This cannot be done online.

### How long does an Aadhaar update take at the centre?

The update process at the centre takes about 10-15 minutes. However, the actual Aadhaar update in UIDAI's system takes 30-90 days. You can track the status using your URN.

### Are CSC centres authorized for Aadhaar services?

Yes. Many Common Service Centres (CSCs) are authorized Aadhaar enrolment and update agencies. Verify using the UIDAI locator before visiting.
