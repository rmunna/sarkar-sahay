# TNPSC Group 2 Exam Guide — Eligibility, Syllabus & Preparation Tips

The TNPSC Group 2 exam (Combined Civil Services Examination — Group II) is a popular state-level recruitment exam conducted by the Tamil Nadu Public Service Commission. It offers posts like Sub-Registrar, Municipal Commissioner, Probation Officer, and other administrative roles. This guide provides a complete roadmap for your preparation.

## Overview

| Detail | Information |
|--------|-------------|
| Conducting Body | Tamil Nadu Public Service Commission (TNPSC) |
| Exam Name | CCSE — Group II / Group IIA |
| Posts | Sub-Registrar, Municipal Commissioner, Probation Officer, and more |
| Level | State Level |
| Selection Process | Prelims → Mains (Group 2) or Single Exam (Group 2A) |
| Job Location | Tamil Nadu |

## Posts Under TNPSC Group 2

- Sub-Registrar Grade II
- Municipal Commissioner Grade II
- Probation Officer Grade II
- Assistant Inspector of Labour
- Revenue Officer (Firka)
- Senior Inspector of Cooperative Societies
- And other Group II services

**TNPSC Group 2A** covers interview-free posts like Assistant Section Officer, Personal Clerk, and others.

## Eligibility Criteria

### Age Limit

| Category | Minimum Age | Maximum Age |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| General (Others) | 21 years | 32 years |
| BC / BCM / MBC / DNC | 21 years | 34 years |
| SC / SCA / ST | 21 years | 37 years |

### Educational Qualification

- Bachelor's Degree from a recognised university
- Some posts may require specific qualifications (e.g., Law degree for Sub-Registrar)

### Nationality

- Must be an Indian citizen
- Knowledge of Tamil is mandatory

## Exam Pattern

### Group 2: Prelims + Mains

**Prelims (Objective):**

| Paper | Subject | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|-------|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| Paper I | General Studies + Aptitude | 200 | 300 | 3 hours |

- No negative marking (generally)
- Qualifying in nature

**Mains (Descriptive):**

| Paper | Subject | Marks | Duration |
|-------|---------|-------|----------|
| Paper I | Tamil (Qualifying) | 300 | 3 hours |
| Paper II | General Studies | 300 | 3 hours |
| Paper III | Aptitude & Mental Ability | 300 | 3 hours |
| **Total** | | **600** (counted) | |

**No interview** for Group 2 (as per recent changes; verify with latest notification).

### Group 2A: Single Paper

| Paper | Subject | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|-------|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| Single Paper | General Studies + Aptitude | 200 | 300 | 3 hours |

- Objective type (MCQ)
- No interview

## Syllabus Overview

### General Studies
- **General Science:** Physics, Chemistry, Biology (10th standard level)
- **Indian History:** Ancient, Medieval, Modern India
- **Tamil Nadu History:** Pallava, Chola, Pandya, Nayak, freedom movement
- **Geography:** India and Tamil Nadu physical, economic geography
- **Indian Polity:** Constitution, fundamental rights, governance
- **Indian Economy:** Five-year plans, banking, fiscal policy, state economy
- **Current Affairs:** National, international, and state-level events

### Aptitude & Mental Ability
- Number series, simplification, percentage, ratio and proportion
- Profit & loss, time and work, time and distance
- Logical reasoning, coding-decoding, blood relations
- Data interpretation, Venn diagrams, puzzles
- Calendar, clock, dice problems

### Tamil (Qualifying — Mains Only)
- Tamil literature and grammar
- Comprehension, essay, letter writing, translation
- Thirukkural, Sangam literature basics

## How to Apply

1. Go to [tnpsc.gov.in](https://www.tnpsc.gov.in)
2. Complete One-Time Registration (OTR) with your details
3. When notification is published, click "Apply Online" for Group 2 / Group 2A
4. Verify your profile details and upload photo/signature
5. Pay the fee and submit
6. Download and save the application confirmation

### Application Fees

| Category | Fee |
|----------|-----|
| General | ₹100 – ₹150 |
| SC / ST / PwD / Destitute Widow | Nil |

*Fees vary by notification. Check the official notification for exact amounts.*

## Important Dates Pattern

- **Notification:** Published on TNPSC website (no fixed month)
- **Application Window:** 30–45 days
- **Prelims:** 3–4 months after notification closing
- **Mains:** 3–5 months after prelims result
- **Final Result:** 8–14 months from notification

**Tip:** Enable notifications on the TNPSC portal for real-time updates.

## Preparation Tips

### 1. Know the Syllabus Inside Out
Download the official syllabus from TNPSC and map every topic. Don't waste time on topics outside the syllabus.

### 2. Use Standard Books
- *Indian Polity* by M. Laxmikanth (Tamil edition available)
- *Tamil Nadu History* by TNPSC-specific publishers
- NCERT books for Science, Geography, History (Class 6–10)
- *Quantitative Aptitude* by RS Aggarwal

### 3. Focus on Tamil Nadu Specifics
TNPSC heavily tests Tamil Nadu history, geography, and state schemes. Dedicate 30–40% of your study time to state-specific content.

### 4. Practice Previous Year Papers
TNPSC often repeats or rephrases questions. Solve last 10 years' papers for Group 2 and Group 2A.

### 5. Daily Current Affairs
Spend 30–45 minutes daily on current affairs. Focus on government schemes, awards, sports, and Tamil Nadu state news.

### 6. Answer Writing Practice (For Mains)
Practice writing answers in Tamil (if choosing Tamil medium). Focus on clarity, structure, and time management — 3 hours for 300 marks is tight.

### 7. Mock Tests
Take weekly mock tests. Aim for 200+ marks in prelims for a comfortable clearing margin.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What is the difference between Group 2 and Group 2A?
Group 2 has prelims + mains and offers higher-level posts (Sub-Registrar, Municipal Commissioner). Group 2A is a single-paper exam for lower-level posts (Assistant Section Officer, Personal Clerk).

### Is there negative marking?
Generally, TNPSC does not apply negative marking. However, always confirm from the official notification.

### Can I write the exam in English?
Yes, the exam is available in both Tamil and English. You can choose your medium.

### How many vacancies are typically released?
Varies by notification — usually 50–200 posts for Group 2, and 500–2000 posts for Group 2A.

### Is there an interview for Group 2?
As per recent TNPSC changes, interview has been removed for many Group 2 posts. Check the specific notification for confirmation.

### Can final-year students apply?
Candidates must have completed their degree by the date specified in the notification. Final-year students can typically apply if results are expected before the cutoff date.
