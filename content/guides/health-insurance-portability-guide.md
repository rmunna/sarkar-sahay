# Health Insurance Portability: How to Switch Your Insurer Without Losing Benefits

Unhappy with your current health insurance company? You have the right to switch. The Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI) allows policyholders to port their health insurance from one insurer to another **without losing waiting period credits or accumulated benefits**. This guide covers the complete portability process, rules, and what to watch out for.

## What Is Health Insurance Portability?

Health insurance portability means transferring your existing health insurance policy from one insurance company to another. Under IRDAI guidelines, the new insurer must give you credit for the waiting periods you have already served with your previous insurer. This means you don't start from scratch when you switch.

**Key point:** Portability was introduced by IRDAI in 2011 and applies to all individual health insurance and family floater policies.

## IRDAI Portability Guidelines (Key Rules)

1. **Waiting period credit is mandatory** — The new insurer must give credit for waiting periods already completed (for pre-existing diseases, specific disease waiting periods, and initial waiting period)
2. **No break in coverage** — You must port before your existing policy expires. There should be no gap between policies
3. **Apply 45 days before renewal** — Submit your portability request at least 45 days before your current policy's renewal date
4. **Sum insured can be equal or different** — You can port with the same sum insured or opt for a different amount. Waiting period credit applies up to the previous sum insured amount
5. **Individual to individual only** — You can port between similar types of policies (individual/family floater). Group policies are not eligible
6. **New insurer decides within 15 days** — The new insurer must accept or reject your portability application within 15 days of receiving your data from the previous insurer

## Who Is Eligible for Portability?

- Any individual with an active health insurance policy (individual or family floater)
- The policy must be up for renewal (portability is exercised at renewal time)
- Continuous coverage without any break is required
- **Not eligible:** Group health insurance policies, top-up plans (portability is insurer-discretionary for top-ups)

## Documents Required

### From You (Policyholder)
- Portability application form (from the new insurer)
- Copy of existing health insurance policy
- Health ID card of existing insurer
- Last 3-4 years' claim history
- Medical records if any claims were made
- KYC documents (Aadhaar, PAN)
- Proposal form of the new insurer

### From Previous Insurer (Shared Automatically)
- Policy details and tenure
- Claims history and amounts
- Waiting period status
- Pre-existing disease declarations
- Premium payment history

## Step-by-Step Process to Port Health Insurance

### Step 1: Choose Your New Insurer
Compare plans from different insurers. Check coverage, network hospitals, claim settlement ratio, and premium. Ensure the new plan covers your needs.

### Step 2: Apply 45 Days Before Renewal
Contact the new insurer at least **45 days before** your current policy's renewal date. Fill the portability application form along with the proposal form.

### Step 3: New Insurer Requests Data
The new insurer will request your policy data from the old insurer through IRDAI's portability portal. The old insurer must share this data within **7 working days**.

### Step 4: Underwriting by New Insurer
The new insurer reviews your medical history, claims record, and policy details. They may:
- Accept your application as-is
- Accept with modified terms (higher premium or exclusions)
- Reject (must provide written reasons)

**Timeline:** The new insurer must decide within **15 days** of receiving your data.

### Step 5: Pay Premium and Get New Policy
If accepted, pay the premium to the new insurer before your old policy expires. Your new policy starts from the renewal date of your old policy — ensuring no gap.

### Step 6: Confirm Old Policy Is Not Renewed
Do **not** pay the renewal premium to your old insurer after your new policy is active. Let the old policy lapse.

## Waiting Period Credit: How It Works

| Waiting Period Type | Rule |
|---|---|
| Initial waiting period (30 days) | Full credit if already served |
| Pre-existing disease (2-4 years) | Credit for years already completed |
| Specific disease waiting period (1-2 years) | Credit for years already completed |
| Time-bound exclusions | Credit proportional to time served |

**Example:** If you have completed 2 years with your old insurer and the new insurer has a 4-year pre-existing disease waiting period, you only need to wait 2 more years — not start over.

**Important:** Waiting period credit applies only up to the sum insured of your previous policy. If you increase your sum insured, the additional amount may have fresh waiting periods.

## Pre-Existing Disease Coverage During Portability

- The new insurer **cannot reset** your pre-existing disease waiting period to zero
- Credit must be given for the time already served
- However, the new insurer can apply their own underwriting — they may charge a higher premium or add exclusions
- If your pre-existing condition waiting period was already fully served with the old insurer, the new insurer must cover it from day one

## Timeline Summary

| Step | Timeline |
|---|---|
| Submit portability application | At least 45 days before renewal |
| Old insurer shares data | Within 7 working days |
| New insurer decision | Within 15 days of receiving data |
| Policy issuance | Before old policy renewal date |
| Total process | 3-6 weeks |

## Fees and Costs

- **No portability charges** — IRDAI does not allow any fee for porting
- You only pay the **premium of the new policy** (which may be higher or lower depending on the plan)
- No cancellation charges from the old insurer for portability

## Important Tips

1. **Start early** — Apply at least 45 days before renewal. Late applications may not be processed in time
2. **Don't let the old policy lapse first** — If there's a gap in coverage, portability rights are lost and you start fresh
3. **Compare thoroughly** — Don't just port for lower premium. Check network hospitals, sub-limits, room rent caps, and claim settlement ratio
4. **Keep all medical records** — The new insurer will review your claims history. Complete records speed up the process
5. **Read the new policy terms** — The new insurer may have different exclusions, co-pay clauses, or sub-limits even if they accept portability

## What If Portability Is Rejected?

If the new insurer rejects your portability request:
- They must provide written reasons
- You can renew with your existing insurer (you haven't lost that policy yet since the 45-day advance window protects you)
- You can file a complaint with IRDAI via the IGMS portal at [igms.irda.gov.in](https://igms.irda.gov.in/)
- The IRDAI Insurance Ombudsman can also help resolve disputes

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I port my health insurance at any time?
No. Portability can only be exercised at the time of renewal. You must apply at least 45 days before your policy renewal date.

### Will I lose my No Claim Bonus (NCB) when porting?
The new insurer is not obligated to carry forward your NCB. However, many insurers offer equivalent benefits to attract customers. Check with the new insurer before porting.

### Can I port a family floater policy?
Yes. You can port a family floater policy to another family floater plan. You can also split it into individual policies during portability, though this depends on the new insurer's terms.

### Is there any charge for porting health insurance?
No. IRDAI does not allow any portability fee. The old insurer cannot charge for transferring your data, and the new insurer cannot charge a porting fee.

### Can I increase my sum insured while porting?
Yes. You can increase your sum insured with the new insurer. However, the waiting period credit applies only up to your previous sum insured. The increased portion may have fresh waiting periods.

### What happens if I have an ongoing claim during portability?
Complete your ongoing claim with your current insurer first. The portability process is separate from claims settlement. The new insurer will honour waiting period credits regardless of past claims.

### Can I port from a public sector insurer to a private one?
Yes. Portability applies across all IRDAI-registered health insurers — public or private.
