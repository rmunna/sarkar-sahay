## What is Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY)?

Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) is the Government of India's flagship housing scheme launched in 2015 to provide affordable housing to economically weaker sections of society. The scheme aims to ensure "Housing for All" by providing financial assistance for building or buying homes.

PMAY has two components:

- **PMAY-Urban (PMAY-U):** For urban areas — covers cities and towns
- **PMAY-Gramin (PMAY-G):** For rural areas — covers villages and small settlements

The scheme provides interest subsidies on home loans, direct financial assistance, and support for construction of pucca houses. See our [PMAY Gramin vs Urban comparison guide](/guide/pmay-gramin-vs-urban-difference) for detailed differences.

## Who is Eligible for PMAY?

### PMAY-Urban Eligibility

| Category | Annual Household Income | Carpet Area Limit | Subsidy Rate |
|----------|----------------------|-------------------|-------------|
| EWS (Economically Weaker Section) | Up to ₹3 lakh | 30 sq.m. | 6.5% |
| LIG (Low Income Group) | ₹3–6 lakh | 60 sq.m. | 6.5% |
| MIG-I (Middle Income Group I) | ₹6–12 lakh | 120 sq.m. | 4% |
| MIG-II (Middle Income Group II) | ₹12–18 lakh | 150 sq.m. | 3% |

### PMAY-Gramin Eligibility

- Beneficiary must be from BPL (Below Poverty Line) household
- Must not own a pucca house anywhere in India
- Selected from Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC) 2011 data
- Priority given to SC/ST, minorities, and women-headed households
- The house must be built within the stipulated timeline

### General Conditions (Both)

- Beneficiary or family member should not own a pucca house in any part of India
- Family should not have availed benefits under any other government housing scheme
- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) is mandatory for application
- Women ownership or co-ownership is preferred under PMAY-U

## How to Apply for PMAY-Urban Online

### Step 1: Visit Official PMAY Portal

Go to the official PMAY-Urban portal at [pmaymis.gov.in](https://pmaymis.gov.in).

### Step 2: Click "Citizen Assessment"

On the homepage, click **"Citizen Assessment"** in the top menu. Select the appropriate option:
- **"For Slum Dwellers"** — if you live in a notified slum area
- **"Benefit Under Other 3 Components"** — for EWS, LIG, MIG categories

### Step 3: Enter Aadhaar Number

Enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number and name as per Aadhaar. Click **"Check"** to verify.

### Step 4: Fill Application Form

Complete the online form with:
- Personal details (name, date of birth, gender, religion, category)
- Income details and income category (EWS/LIG/MIG-I/MIG-II)
- Current residential address
- Bank account details
- Property details (if already identified)

### Step 5: Upload Documents

Upload scanned copies of required documents in the specified format (usually PDF/JPEG).

### Step 6: Submit and Save Application ID

Review all details carefully and click **"Submit"**. Note down your **Application Reference Number** for [tracking your application status](/guide/pmay-status-check-online).

### Step 7: Visit Common Service Centre (CSC) if Needed

If you face difficulties applying online, visit the nearest **Common Service Centre (CSC)** or approach the Urban Local Body (ULB) office. A fee of ₹25 + GST is applicable at CSCs.

## How to Apply for PMAY-Gramin

PMAY-Gramin follows a different process — beneficiaries are selected from government data, not through direct online applications.

### Step 1: Check SECC Data

Beneficiaries are identified through **Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC) 2011** data. Visit [pmayg.nic.in](https://pmayg.nic.in) to check if your name is in the list.

### Step 2: Gram Sabha Verification

The Gram Sabha verifies and prioritizes the list of eligible beneficiaries. Attend your village's Gram Sabha meetings to stay informed.

### Step 3: Gram Panchayat Registration

If eligible, register with your **Gram Panchayat** office. Submit all required documents.

### Step 4: Verification by Block/District Office

Block Development Officer (BDO) and district officials verify your eligibility and approve the application.

### Step 5: Sanction Order

Once approved, you receive a sanction order. The financial assistance of **₹1.20 lakh** (plain areas) or **₹1.30 lakh** (hilly/difficult areas) is released in installments linked to construction milestones.

### Step 6: Track Progress

Check your [beneficiary status online](/guide/pmay-beneficiary-list-check) at the official PMAY-G portal.

## PMAY Subsidy Details

Under PMAY-Urban CLSS (Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme):

| Category | Max Loan for Subsidy | Subsidy Rate | Max Subsidy Amount |
|----------|---------------------|-------------|-------------------|
| EWS | ₹6 lakh | 6.5% | ~₹2.67 lakh |
| LIG | ₹6 lakh | 6.5% | ~₹2.67 lakh |
| MIG-I | ₹9 lakh | 4% | ~₹2.35 lakh |
| MIG-II | ₹12 lakh | 3% | ~₹2.30 lakh |

The subsidy is credited upfront to the loan account, reducing your EMI. Use our [PMAY EMI calculator](/guide/pmay-loan-emi-calculator) to estimate your savings.

## Processing Time

- **Online application submission:** 15–30 minutes
- **Verification and approval:** 2–6 months (varies by state and ULB)
- **Subsidy credit to loan account:** 3–6 months after approval
- **PMAY-G house construction:** 12 months from sanction

## Important Tips

1. **Apply through official portal only** — avoid third-party websites that charge extra fees
2. **Keep Aadhaar linked to bank account** — subsidy is transferred via DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer)
3. **Women ownership is preferred** — property should be in the name of female family member or jointly owned
4. **Don't delay construction** — PMAY-G requires completion within the stipulated timeline or the benefit may be cancelled
5. **Save your application ID** — you'll need it to [check status](/guide/pmay-status-check-online) and track progress

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Can I apply for PMAY if I already own a house?

No. PMAY is for families who do not own a pucca house anywhere in India. If any family member owns a pucca house, the family is not eligible.

### 2. Is there any fee to apply for PMAY online?

Applying directly on the PMAY portal is free. If you apply through a Common Service Centre (CSC), a nominal fee of ₹25 + GST is charged.

### 3. Can I apply for both PMAY-Urban and PMAY-Gramin?

No. You can apply under only one component based on your residential location — Urban or Gramin. You cannot apply under both.

### 4. What if my PMAY application is rejected?

Check the rejection reason on the portal and rectify the issue. You can reapply after fixing the problem. See our [PMAY rejection fix guide](/guide/pmay-application-rejected-fix) for detailed steps.

### 5. How do I check my PMAY application status?

Visit the official PMAY portal and enter your Application Reference Number or Aadhaar number. Read our detailed [PMAY status check guide](/guide/pmay-status-check-online).

### 6. Is PMAY subsidy available for home renovation?

No. PMAY subsidy is available only for new construction or purchase of a new house, not for renovation or repair of an existing house.

### 7. Can a single woman apply for PMAY?

Yes. Single women, widows, and women-headed households are given priority under PMAY. The property should preferably be in the woman's name.
