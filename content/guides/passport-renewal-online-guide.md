## What is Passport Renewal?

Passport renewal is the process of getting a new passport when your existing passport is about to expire, has expired, or when all pages are used up. In India, passport renewal is handled by the **Ministry of External Affairs (MEA)** through the Passport Seva Portal at [passportindia.gov.in](https://www.passportindia.gov.in/).

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent informational platform and is not affiliated with the Ministry of External Affairs or Passport Seva. Always verify details on the [official Passport Seva website](https://www.passportindia.gov.in/).

## Who is Eligible for Passport Renewal?

You can apply for passport renewal (re-issue) if:

- Your passport has **expired** or is **about to expire** (within 1 year of expiry for adults)
- All **pages are exhausted** (no blank pages left)
- Your passport is **damaged or lost**
- You need to change details like **name, address, or appearance**
- You want to add/delete **ECR (Emigration Check Required)** status

## Documents Required for Passport Renewal

### Mandatory Documents

- **Old/expiring passport** (original + self-attested photocopy of first and last 2 pages)
- **Aadhaar card** (original + photocopy)
- **Recent passport-size photographs** (2 copies, 3.5cm x 3.5cm, white background)

### Additional Documents (if applicable)

- **Address change:** New address proof (Aadhaar, utility bill, bank statement)
- **Name change:** Marriage certificate / Gazette notification / Deed poll
- **Damaged passport:** Damaged passport + FIR (if applicable)
- **Lost passport:** FIR copy + Passport Seva NOC

## Passport Renewal Fees

| Type | 36 Pages | 60 Pages |
|---|---|---|
| Normal (Re-issue) | ₹1,500 | ₹2,000 |
| Tatkal (Re-issue) | ₹3,500 | ₹4,000 |

> **Note:** An additional **Tatkal fee of ₹2,000** is included in the tatkal amounts above. Fees are non-refundable.

## How to Renew Passport Online: Step-by-Step

### Step 1: Visit Passport Seva Portal

Go to [https://www.passportindia.gov.in/](https://www.passportindia.gov.in/) and click on **"Register Now"** if you don't have an account, or **"Existing User Login"** if you already have one.

### Step 2: Register / Log In

1. Register with your **email ID** — this will be your login ID
2. Set a **password** and complete registration
3. Verify your email via the activation link
4. Log in with your credentials

### Step 3: Apply Online — Click "Apply for Re-issue of Passport"

1. After login, click **"Apply for Re-issue of Passport"**
2. Fill in the application form with your details:
   - Personal details (name, DOB, gender)
   - Family details (father/mother/spouse name)
   - Present and permanent address
   - Old passport details (number, issue date, expiry, place of issue)
   - Emergency contact
3. Select **reason for re-issue** (validity expired, pages exhausted, change in details, etc.)

### Step 4: Upload Documents

- Upload **self-attested copies** of required documents
- Supported formats: **PDF, JPEG** (max 1 MB per file)
- Ensure documents are clear and legible

### Step 5: Pay the Fee

1. Click **"Pay and Schedule Appointment"**
2. Pay **₹1,500** (36 pages) or **₹2,000** (60 pages) via:
   - **Online:** Credit card, debit card, net banking, UPI
   - **Challan:** SBI bank challan (pay at SBI branch within 24 hours)

### Step 6: Book Appointment at PSK/POPSK

1. Select **Passport Seva Kendra (PSK)** or **Post Office Passport Seva Kendra (POPSK)** near you
2. Choose an available **date and time slot**
3. Confirm the appointment
4. Download and print the **appointment confirmation slip**

### Step 7: Visit PSK/POPSK on Appointment Date

Carry the following:

1. **Printed application receipt** (ARN)
2. **Appointment confirmation slip**
3. **Original documents** + self-attested photocopies
4. **Old passport** (original)

At the PSK:
- **Counter A:** Document verification
- **Counter B:** Biometrics (photo, fingerprint, signature)
- **Counter C:** Final verification (Granting Officer)

### Step 8: Police Verification

- For passport renewal at the **same address**, police verification may be done **post-issuance**
- For **address change** or other changes, police verification is done **before issuance**
- A police officer will visit your address for verification

### Step 9: Receive Your New Passport

- Your new passport will be delivered via **Speed Post** to your registered address
- Track status on the Passport Seva portal using your **File Number**

## Tatkal Passport Renewal

For urgent renewal:

1. Select **"Tatkal"** while filling the application form
2. Pay the additional tatkal fee (total ₹3,500 for 36 pages)
3. Provide proof of urgency (medical emergency, job requirement, etc.)
4. Tatkal passports are typically issued within **1–3 working days** after the PSK visit (subject to police verification)

> **Note:** Tatkal facility is available only for certain categories and may require additional verification.

## Processing Time

| Type | Timeline |
|---|---|
| Normal renewal (same address) | 7–15 working days |
| Normal renewal (address change) | 15–30 working days (police verification required) |
| Tatkal renewal | 1–3 working days (after PSK visit) |

## Important Tips

1. **Apply before expiry** — apply for renewal at least 6 months before your passport expires, especially if you have upcoming travel
2. **Aadhaar linking** — having your Aadhaar linked can speed up police verification through the e-verification process
3. **Correct details** — double-check all details before submission; errors may cause delays
4. **Reschedule if needed** — you can reschedule your PSK appointment up to 3 times through the portal
5. **Track online** — track your application status on [passportindia.gov.in](https://www.passportindia.gov.in/) using your File Number and Date of Birth

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. Can I renew my passport online in India?

**Yes.** You can apply for passport renewal online through the **Passport Seva Portal** at [passportindia.gov.in](https://www.passportindia.gov.in/). You fill the form online, pay fees, and book a PSK appointment — then visit the PSK for document verification and biometrics.

### Q2. How much does passport renewal cost?

Passport renewal costs **₹1,500** for a 36-page booklet and **₹2,000** for a 60-page booklet under normal processing. Tatkal renewal costs **₹3,500** and **₹4,000** respectively.

### Q3. What documents are needed for passport renewal?

You need your **old passport**, **Aadhaar card**, and **recent passport-size photographs**. Additional documents are required for address change, name change, or if the passport is lost/damaged.

### Q4. How long does passport renewal take?

Normal renewal takes **7–15 working days** if there is no address change. With address change, it takes **15–30 working days** due to police verification. Tatkal processing takes **1–3 working days**.

### Q5. Can I renew my passport if it has already expired?

**Yes.** You can apply for re-issue even if your passport has expired. There is no penalty for applying after expiry.

### Q6. Do I need to submit my old passport for renewal?

**Yes.** You must carry your **original old passport** to the PSK on your appointment date. It will be cancelled and returned to you along with the new passport.
