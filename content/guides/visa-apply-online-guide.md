# How to Apply for Visa Online: Country-Wise Process

Planning to travel abroad from India? Most countries now offer **online visa applications** or **e-visa** facilities. This guide covers the visa application process for popular destinations — US, UK, Canada, Schengen, and e-visa countries — with documents, fees, and practical tips.

## What is a Visa?

A visa is an official endorsement on your passport that allows you to enter, stay in, or transit through a foreign country for a specific purpose and duration. Types include:

- **Tourist Visa** — for leisure travel
- **Business Visa** — for meetings, conferences
- **Student Visa** — for education
- **Work Visa** — for employment
- **Transit Visa** — for passing through a country

> Make sure your [passport](/guide/passport-tatkal-apply-guide) is valid for at least 6 months beyond your travel dates.

## US Visa Application from India

### Process Overview

1. **Determine visa type** — B1/B2 (tourist/business), F1 (student), H1B (work), etc.
2. **Complete DS-160 form** online at [ceac.state.gov](https://ceac.state.gov/genniv/)
3. **Pay the MRV fee** — $185 (₹15,400 approx.) for B1/B2
4. **Schedule appointment** — Biometrics (VAC) + Interview (US Embassy/Consulate)
5. **Attend biometrics appointment** — fingerprints and photo
6. **Attend visa interview** at the US Embassy/Consulate
7. **Receive passport** with visa via courier (5–7 days after approval)

### Documents Required
- Valid passport (current + old passports)
- DS-160 confirmation page
- Appointment confirmation letter
- Passport-size photograph (US specifications: 2x2 inch, white background)
- Proof of financial stability (bank statements, ITR, salary slips)
- Travel itinerary and hotel bookings
- Employment/business proof (offer letter, business registration)
- For students: I-20 form, university admission letter, SEVIS fee receipt

### Fees
| Visa Type | Fee |
|-----------|-----|
| B1/B2 (Tourist/Business) | $185 |
| F1/M1 (Student) | $185 |
| H1B/L1 (Work) | $205 |
| K (Fiancé) | $265 |

### Interview Tips
- Answer **honestly and concisely**
- Show strong **ties to India** (job, property, family)
- Carry **organized documents** but only show when asked
- Be confident and polite
- Know your travel purpose, dates, and funding source

## UK Visa Application from India

### Process Overview

1. **Visit** [gov.uk/apply-to-come-to-the-uk](https://www.gov.uk/apply-to-come-to-the-uk)
2. **Choose visa type** — Standard Visitor, Student, Skilled Worker, etc.
3. **Complete online form** and pay the fee
4. **Book appointment** at VFS Global centre in India
5. **Visit VFS centre** — submit biometrics and documents
6. **Receive decision** — typically 3–4 weeks for visitor visa

### Documents Required
- Valid passport
- Completed online application
- Passport-size photographs
- Financial documents (bank statements for 6 months, ITR)
- Travel itinerary, flight bookings, hotel reservations
- Cover letter explaining purpose of visit
- Sponsorship letter (if someone in UK is sponsoring)
- Employment proof or business documents

### Fees
| Visa Type | Fee |
|-----------|-----|
| Standard Visitor (6 months) | £115 (~₹12,000) |
| Student Visa | £490 |
| Skilled Worker | £719+ |

## Canada Visa Application from India

### Process Overview

1. **Visit** [canada.ca/immigration](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship.html)
2. **Check eligibility** using the online tool
3. **Create account** on IRCC portal
4. **Fill application** — Visitor visa (TRV), Study permit, or Work permit
5. **Upload documents** and pay fees online
6. **Submit biometrics** at VFS Global centre (within 30 days)
7. **Wait for processing** — visitor visa typically 30–60 days
8. **Receive passport** with visa stamp

### Documents Required
- Valid passport
- Digital photograph (as per IRCC specifications)
- Proof of funds (bank statements, FDR, property documents)
- Travel history (previous visas and travel stamps)
- Purpose of visit documentation
- Family information form (IMM 5645)
- Employment proof or business registration

### Fees
| Visa Type | Fee |
|-----------|-----|
| Visitor Visa (TRV) | CAD $100 (~₹6,200) |
| Biometrics | CAD $85 |
| Study Permit | CAD $150 |
| Work Permit | CAD $155 |

## Schengen Visa Application from India

A **Schengen visa** allows travel to **29 European countries** with a single visa, including France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Netherlands, and others.

### Process Overview

1. **Identify main destination** — apply at the embassy/consulate of the country where you'll spend the most time
2. **Visit the VFS/embassy website** of that country
3. **Fill the Schengen visa application form**
4. **Gather documents** (see below)
5. **Book appointment** at VFS Global or embassy
6. **Submit application** with biometrics
7. **Processing time** — 15 calendar days (can take up to 45 days)

### Documents Required
- Valid passport (issued within last 10 years, valid 3+ months beyond stay)
- Two passport-size photos (35x45mm, white background)
- **Travel insurance** — minimum €30,000 coverage (mandatory!) — see our [travel insurance guide](/guide/travel-insurance-buy-guide)
- Flight reservation (round trip)
- Hotel bookings for entire stay
- Bank statements (last 3–6 months)
- Cover letter with travel itinerary
- Leave approval from employer
- Income tax returns (last 2–3 years)

### Fees
| Category | Fee |
|----------|-----|
| Adult | €80 (~₹7,200) |
| Child (6-12 years) | €40 |
| Child (below 6) | Free |

> **Important:** Schengen visa requires **mandatory travel insurance** with minimum €30,000 medical coverage.

## General Visa Application Tips

1. **Apply early** — Start the process at least 2–3 months before travel, especially for US, UK, and Canada
2. **Keep documents organized** — Use a checklist and arrange documents in order
3. **Financial proof is key** — Show stable income and sufficient bank balance; avoid sudden large deposits
4. **Travel history helps** — Previous international travel strengthens your application
5. **Be truthful** — Never submit false documents or provide incorrect information; it can result in permanent ban

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Which countries offer e-visa for Indian passport holders?
Over 60 countries offer e-visa or visa on arrival for Indian passport holders, including Thailand, UAE, Turkey, Sri Lanka, Singapore, Kenya, and others. Check the destination country's official website.

### 2. How long does a US visa interview take?
The interview itself takes **3–5 minutes**. However, you may spend 2–4 hours at the embassy including waiting time, security, and biometrics.

### 3. Can I apply for Schengen visa without travel insurance?
No. **Travel insurance is mandatory** for Schengen visa applications. You need minimum €30,000 medical coverage valid for all Schengen countries.

### 4. How much bank balance is needed for a tourist visa?
It varies by country. General guideline: **₹5–10 lakh** in savings for US/UK/Canada, **₹3–5 lakh** for Schengen. Show consistent balance, not sudden deposits.

### 5. What is the most common reason for visa rejection?
Insufficient financial documents, weak ties to home country, incomplete application, and inconsistent information are the top reasons for visa rejection.

### 6. Can I apply for multiple visas at the same time?
Yes, you can apply for visas to different countries simultaneously, as long as you have your passport available for each appointment.

### 7. Is travel insurance required for all countries?
Travel insurance is **mandatory** for Schengen countries. For US, UK, and Canada, it is not mandatory but highly recommended.
