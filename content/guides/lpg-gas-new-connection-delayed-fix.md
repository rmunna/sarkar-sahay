# Indane/HP/Bharat Gas New Connection Not Coming — How to Fix Delayed LPG Connection

You applied for a new LPG gas connection weeks ago, but haven't received it yet. The distributor isn't giving a clear answer. You don't know if your application was rejected or just stuck. This is a common problem — and this guide covers exactly how to fix it for Indane (IOC), HP Gas (HPCL), and Bharat Gas (BPCL).

## Common Reasons Why New LPG Connection Gets Delayed

1. **Document verification pending** — Your address proof, ID proof, or photograph may not have been accepted or verified yet by the distributor.
2. **Duplicate connection detected** — If your Aadhaar is already linked to an existing LPG connection (even a surrendered one), the system may block the new application.
3. **Distributor backlog** — Some distributors in high-demand areas have a waiting list, especially in semi-urban and rural areas.
4. **Incomplete application** — Missing documents, blurry photographs, or unsigned forms can stall your application without notification.
5. **Wrong area distributor** — You applied at a distributor outside your delivery area, and the application was not forwarded.
6. **KYC mismatch** — Name or address mismatch between your Aadhaar, ration card, and other documents.
7. **Cylinder/equipment shortage** — Temporary stock issues at the distributor can delay connection delivery.
8. **Application rejected silently** — Some applications get rejected without proper intimation to the consumer.

## How to Apply for New LPG Connection — Quick Overview

### Indane (Indian Oil Corporation)

1. Visit [indane.co.in](https://www.indane.co.in) → **New Connection** → **Apply Online**
2. Or visit your nearest Indane distributor with documents
3. Fill the **Subscription Voucher (SV)** form
4. Documents: Aadhaar card, address proof, passport-size photograph, ration card (if available)
5. Pay the **security deposit** for cylinder and regulator (refundable)
6. **Expected timeline:** 7–15 working days after document verification

### HP Gas (Hindustan Petroleum)

1. Visit [hindustanpetroleum.com/hpgas](https://www.hindustanpetroleum.com/hpgas) → **New Connection**
2. Or visit the nearest HP Gas distributor
3. Fill the application form (Form 16/17)
4. Documents: Aadhaar card, address proof, photograph, PAN (optional)
5. Pay security deposit
6. **Expected timeline:** 7–15 working days

### Bharat Gas (Bharat Petroleum)

1. Visit [bharatgas.com](https://www.bharatgas.com) → **New Connection** → **Apply Online**
2. Or visit the nearest Bharat Gas distributor
3. Submit application with required documents
4. Pay security deposit for cylinder and regulator
5. **Expected timeline:** 7–15 working days

### Under Ujjwala Yojana (Free Connection)

If you are eligible under **PM Ujjwala Yojana 2.0**:
1. Visit [pmuy.gov.in](https://www.pmuy.gov.in) or your nearest LPG distributor
2. Eligibility: Women from BPL households, SC/ST, PMAY beneficiaries, and other priority groups
3. Documents: Aadhaar card, BPL/ration card, bank passbook
4. The connection is **free** (no security deposit)
5. Timeline may be **longer** (15–30 days) due to additional verification

## How to Check Application Status Online

### Indane

1. Go to [indane.co.in](https://www.indane.co.in)
2. Click **"Track Request"** or **"Customer Services"**
3. Enter your **application reference number** or **registered mobile number**
4. The status will show: Submitted → Under Verification → Approved → Dispatched

### HP Gas

1. Go to [hindustanpetroleum.com/hpgas](https://www.hindustanpetroleum.com/hpgas)
2. Look for **"Track New Connection"** under customer services
3. Enter your reference number or registered mobile
4. Check whether verification is complete

### Bharat Gas

1. Go to [bharatgas.com](https://www.bharatgas.com)
2. Navigate to **"Track Application"** or **"New Connection Status"**
3. Enter your application ID or registered mobile number

### Via MyLPG Portal

1. Visit [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in)
2. If your application was processed, you can check connection details here
3. MyLPG is useful after the connection is assigned — for pre-connection tracking, use the company-specific portals above

> **Tip:** If you didn't receive a reference number when you applied, visit the distributor and ask for the application tracking number.

## Document Verification Stuck — How to Follow Up

If your application is stuck at the verification stage:

1. **Visit the distributor in person** — Ask specifically what document is pending or causing the issue
2. **Carry original documents** — Aadhaar, address proof (electricity bill/passport/voter ID), and passport photograph
3. **Check for name/address mismatch** — If your name is slightly different on Aadhaar vs other documents, this causes delays. Get it corrected first (see our [Aadhaar correction guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
4. **Ask for the area manager's contact** — If the distributor is not helpful, ask for the area office or territory manager's contact
5. **Re-submit clearer documents** — Sometimes a blurry scan or unclear photograph causes rejection. Provide fresh copies.

## Application Rejected — Reasons and How to Reapply

### Common Rejection Reasons

| Reason | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Duplicate Aadhaar found on another connection | Surrender the old connection or get de-duplication done |
| Address proof not matching delivery area | Provide address proof of current residence within the distributor's delivery area |
| KYC documents expired or unclear | Submit fresh, valid documents |
| Already have connection in same household | Only one subsidised connection per household is allowed — can apply for non-subsidised |
| Ujjwala eligibility not met | Check eligibility at [pmuy.gov.in](https://www.pmuy.gov.in) |

### How to Reapply

1. Fix the specific issue that caused rejection
2. Visit the **same distributor** with corrected documents
3. Submit a **fresh application** — there is no penalty for reapplying
4. If rejected unfairly, escalate (see escalation section below)

## Distributor Not Responding — Escalation Path

If your distributor is ignoring your application or not giving proper updates:

### Step 1: Contact the Oil Company Directly

| Company | Customer Helpline | Email/Website |
|---------|------------------|---------------|
| **Indane (IOC)** | 1800-2333-555 (toll-free) | [indane.co.in](https://www.indane.co.in) |
| **HP Gas (HPCL)** | 1800-2333-666 (toll-free) | [hindustanpetroleum.com](https://www.hindustanpetroleum.com/hpgas) |
| **Bharat Gas (BPCL)** | 1800-22-4344 (toll-free) | [bharatgas.com](https://www.bharatgas.com) |
| **LPG Emergency** | 1906 | All three companies |

Call the helpline, give your **application reference number** and **registered mobile number**, and ask for the status. They can directly contact the distributor.

### Step 2: Complain on MyLPG Portal

1. Go to [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in) → **Lodge Complaint/Feedback**
2. Select **"New Connection"** as the complaint category
3. Describe the issue with dates and distributor details
4. Save the **complaint reference number**

### Step 3: Escalate to PG Portal (Government Grievance)

If no response within **15–30 days**:
1. Go to [pgportal.gov.in](https://pgportal.gov.in)
2. Register and file a grievance under **Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas**
3. Mention your complaint number from Step 2
4. Government mandates a response within **30 days**

### Step 4: Contact the Nodal Officer

Each oil company has a **State Level Coordinator (SLC)** or **Nodal Officer** for consumer grievances. Ask for their details when you call the helpline, or find them on the company's website under "Grievance Redressal."

## Transfer Connection When Moving to a New City

If you are relocating and want to transfer your existing LPG connection:

1. **Visit your current distributor** and submit a **Transfer Connection request** (TV — Transfer Voucher)
2. Provide: **new address proof**, **Aadhaar** (updated address preferred), and **LPG passbook**
3. The distributor will issue a **Transfer Release Order**
4. **Visit a distributor in your new city** and submit the Transfer Release Order along with your documents
5. The new distributor will allot you a new consumer number and deliver a cylinder
6. **Deposit adjustment** — Your old security deposit is adjusted. You may need to pay a small differential if rates differ.

### Online Transfer

1. Go to [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in) → **Request Transfer of Connection**
2. Enter your LPG ID and select the new area/distributor
3. Upload address proof for the new location
4. The transfer is processed within **7–15 working days**

> **Note:** You can transfer between different companies (e.g., Indane to HP Gas). This is called **inter-company portability**, but it takes longer — usually 15–30 days.

## Important Tips

- **Keep your application receipt safe** — The reference number is essential for tracking and complaints.
- **Apply online if possible** — Online applications create a digital trail that is easier to track than walk-in applications.
- **One subsidised connection per household** — If another family member already has an LPG connection, you may face rejection. Check before applying.
- **Don't pay extra to agents/touts** — The only legitimate payment is the **security deposit** (₹1,600–₹2,200 approximately, varies by company). Demand a proper receipt.
- **Ujjwala connections take longer** — Due to additional BPL/eligibility verification, expect 15–30 days instead of the usual 7–15.
- **Update Aadhaar address first** — If you recently moved, update your Aadhaar address before applying for a new connection to avoid verification delays.

## FAQs

### How long does it take to get a new LPG gas connection?
Under normal circumstances, a new LPG connection from Indane, HP Gas, or Bharat Gas takes **7–15 working days** after submitting all documents and paying the security deposit. If there's a backlog, it may take up to 30 days.

### What is the cost of a new LPG connection?
You need to pay a **refundable security deposit** for the cylinder and pressure regulator. This is approximately **₹1,600–₹2,200** depending on the company and cylinder type (14.2 kg or 5 kg). The exact amount is listed on the company's website.

### Can I apply for a new LPG connection online?
Yes. All three companies — Indane, HP Gas, and Bharat Gas — allow online applications through their respective websites. You can also apply through [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in) for a unified process.

### What documents are needed for a new LPG connection?
You need: **Aadhaar card** (mandatory), **address proof** (electricity bill, passport, voter ID, or rent agreement), **passport-size photograph**, and **ration card** (if available). For Ujjwala scheme, you also need a BPL card or SECC list inclusion.

### My application was rejected. Can I apply again?
Yes. There is no restriction on reapplying. First, find out the **specific reason for rejection** from the distributor, fix the issue (e.g., correct documents, surrender duplicate connection), and submit a fresh application.

### Can I choose which company (Indane/HP/Bharat) I want?
Yes. You can apply to any LPG company's distributor that serves your area. Check which distributors operate in your locality before applying.

### How do I transfer my gas connection to a new city?
Submit a **Transfer Request** at your current distributor or online through [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in). You'll receive a Transfer Release Order. Take this to a distributor in your new city to complete the transfer. The process takes **7–15 working days** for same-company transfers.

### The distributor is asking for extra money. What should I do?
This is illegal. The only legitimate charge is the **security deposit** (with official receipt). Report any demand for extra payment to the oil company's helpline or on [pgportal.gov.in](https://pgportal.gov.in).
