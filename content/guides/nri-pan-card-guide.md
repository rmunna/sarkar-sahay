## Do NRIs Need a PAN Card?

Yes, NRIs (Non-Resident Indians) need a PAN (Permanent Account Number) card if they:
- Earn **income in India** (rental income, capital gains, interest, etc.)
- Want to make **investments in India** (mutual funds, stocks, property)
- Need to **sell property** in India
- Have to **file income tax returns** in India
- Want to open an **NRO/NRE bank account**
- Receive income exceeding ₹2,50,000 in India in a financial year

PAN is mandatory for all financial transactions above specified limits in India, regardless of residential status.

## How to Apply for PAN Card as an NRI

### Option 1: Apply Online Through NSDL

1. Visit [onlineservices.nsdl.com](https://www.onlineservices.nsdl.com/paam/endUserRegisterContact.html)
2. Select **"Application Type: New PAN — Indian Citizen (Form 49A)"** if you hold Indian citizenship, or **"Form 49AA"** for foreign citizens
3. Select category: **Individual**
4. Fill in personal details — name, date of birth, father's name
5. Enter your **foreign address** as the communication address
6. Enter your **Indian address** (if any) for correspondence
7. Select **AO Code** (Assessing Officer code based on your income source location in India)
8. Upload documents:
   - Photograph
   - Signature
   - Identity proof
   - Address proof
9. Pay the fee (higher for dispatch to foreign address)
10. Submit and note the **acknowledgment number**

### Option 2: Apply Through UTIITSL

1. Visit [utiitsl.com](https://www.utiitsl.com)
2. Navigate to PAN card services
3. Fill Form 49A or 49AA as applicable
4. Upload documents and pay fee
5. Submit online and track using the coupon number

### Option 3: Apply Through Indian Embassy

- Some Indian embassies and consulates facilitate PAN card applications
- Contact your nearest Indian mission for assistance

## Documents Required

### Identity Proof (Any One)
- **Indian passport** (valid or expired) — most preferred
- **OCI card**
- Passport of the foreign country
- PIO card (if still held)
- Bank account statement from an Indian bank attested by the bank

### Address Proof (Any One)
**For Indian Address:**
- Aadhaar card
- Voter ID
- Bank statement
- Utility bill

**For Foreign Address:**
- Foreign passport
- Bank account statement from the foreign country
- NRE/NRO bank statement
- Utility bill from abroad
- Certificate of residence from Indian embassy

### Date of Birth Proof
- Passport
- Birth certificate
- School leaving certificate
- Aadhaar card

**Note:** For NRIs, documents issued abroad must be **attested by the Indian Embassy/Consulate** or **apostilled** as per Hague Convention requirements.

## PAN Card for OCI/Foreign Nationals

### Form 49AA
- Foreign nationals and OCI holders use **Form 49AA** (not 49A)
- Acceptable ID: Foreign passport, OCI card, PIO card
- AO Code: Must select the correct AO code for NRI/foreign citizens
- Tax Representative: May need to appoint a representative in India

## NRI Tax Obligations with PAN

### When NRIs Must File Returns
- Total Indian income exceeds **₹2,50,000** in a financial year
- Want to claim **TDS refund** on income where excess tax was deducted
- Capital gains from **sale of Indian property** or investments
- Rental income from Indian property

### TDS for NRIs
- NRI income in India is subject to TDS at higher rates:
  - Interest income: 30% (unless DTAA benefit claimed)
  - Rental income: 30%
  - Capital gains (long-term): 20%
  - Capital gains (short-term): 30%
- With PAN, you can claim **DTAA (Double Tax Avoidance Agreement)** benefits with many countries

## NRI PAN and Aadhaar Linking

### Is Aadhaar-PAN Linking Required for NRIs?
- **NRIs are exempt** from mandatory Aadhaar-PAN linking
- This is because Aadhaar is issued only to residents of India
- If an NRI has an Aadhaar from when they were a resident, linking is optional but recommended
- See our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) for more details on Aadhaar for NRIs

## Important Tips

- **Apply before transacting** — get PAN before buying property, investing, or opening bank accounts in India
- **Use correct form** — Form 49A for Indian citizens (including NRIs), Form 49AA for foreign nationals/OCI holders
- **AO Code matters** — select the AO code based on where your income originates in India; consult a CA if unsure
- **Keep PAN active** — even if you become an NRI later, your existing PAN remains valid; don't apply for a new one
- **File returns on time** — NRIs must file returns by July 31 for non-audit cases; penalties apply for late filing

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I already have a PAN from when I was in India. Is it still valid?
Yes, your existing PAN card remains valid forever regardless of your residential status. Do not apply for a new one.

### Can NRIs apply for instant e-PAN?
The instant e-PAN facility (using Aadhaar) is primarily for residents. NRIs without Aadhaar should use the regular application process through NSDL or UTIITSL.

### Is PAN mandatory for selling property in India?
Yes, PAN is mandatory for both buyer and seller in property transactions. Without PAN, TDS is deducted at a higher rate of 20% (instead of the applicable rate).

### Can I use a digital/e-PAN for transactions?
Yes, e-PAN (downloaded from the income tax portal) is legally valid for all purposes. You don't need a physical card.

### How do I update my NRI status in PAN records?
File a PAN correction/change request through NSDL or UTIITSL. Update your address to your foreign address and residential status to NRI.
