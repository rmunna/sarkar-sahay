# Rajasthan Income Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **income certificate** (आय प्रमाण पत्र / Aay Praman Patra) in Rajasthan is issued by the Revenue Department and is required for scholarship applications, BPL ration card eligibility, OBC non-creamy layer verification, and various government scheme benefits. Applications go through **E-Mitra** — Rajasthan's digital service delivery platform.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [emitra.rajasthan.gov.in](https://emitra.rajasthan.gov.in) or visit any E-Mitra kiosk. Processed by SDM/Tehsildar within **15–30 days**. Jan Aadhaar may be needed.

---

## Why You Need Rajasthan Income Certificate

- **Rajasthan State Scholarship** — for OBC, SC, ST, EBC (Economically Backward Classes)
- **NSP National Scholarship** — income certificate required
- **BPL ration card** application eligibility
- **Government scheme benefits** — CM Free Bijli, PM Awas Yojana, etc.
- **Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) certificate** for OBC — needs income certificate first
- **Scholarship for girl child** — Kalibai Bheel, Devnarayan, Pratibha Puraskar

---

## Jan Aadhaar — Rajasthan's Requirement

Rajasthan's E-Mitra system increasingly uses **Jan Aadhaar** (Rajasthan's family identity card) for service delivery. If you have Jan Aadhaar, the income certificate application is streamlined. If not, Aadhaar alone works.

**Get Jan Aadhaar:** Apply at [janaadhaar.rajasthan.gov.in](https://janaadhaar.rajasthan.gov.in) or nearest E-Mitra centre.

---

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory)
- **Jan Aadhaar** (if available — Rajasthan family ID)
- **Ration card** (shows family members)
- Self-declaration of annual income (affidavit or online declaration)
- For salaried: **salary slip** / employer certificate
- For farmers: **Khasra / Jamabandi** (from Apna Khata Rajasthan)
- Passport-size photograph

## How to Apply Online — E-Mitra Portal (via SSO)

1. Go to [sso.rajasthan.gov.in](https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in) (Rajasthan Single Sign-On)
2. Log in or register with Aadhaar / Jan Aadhaar / Gmail
3. After login, search **"Income Certificate"** or **"आय प्रमाण पत्र"**
4. Select **"Revenue Department — Income Certificate"**
5. Fill form: name, address, district, tehsil, income sources, annual income amount
6. Upload documents (Aadhaar, ration card, income proof)
7. Pay fee: **₹20–40** (online via SSO portal)
8. Submit — note the **Application Reference Number**

---

## How to Apply via E-Mitra Kiosk

1. Visit nearest **E-Mitra kiosk** (every panchayat and ward has one)
2. Tell the operator: "Income Certificate chahiye" (आय प्रमाण पत्र)
3. Provide Aadhaar / Jan Aadhaar + income documents
4. Operator fills and submits online form
5. E-Mitra service fee: approximately ₹30–50
6. Collect acknowledgement receipt

---

## Status Check

1. Go to [emitra.rajasthan.gov.in](https://emitra.rajasthan.gov.in) or log in at SSO
2. Click **"Track Application"** → enter reference number
3. Or: SMS **RJSTATUS <Application Number>** to **56070** (may vary)
4. Status: Submitted → Under Review → Tehsildar Verification → Approved

---

## Download Income Certificate Rajasthan

Once approved:
1. Log in to [sso.rajasthan.gov.in](https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in)
2. Go to **"My Applications"** → Click approved income certificate
3. Click **"Download"** — digitally signed PDF
4. Can also be downloaded from E-Mitra kiosk counter

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What income to declare for Rajasthan income certificate?

Total **annual family income** of all earning members (head + spouse + earning adult children). Include: salary, agricultural income, business, pension, rent. Student family members with no income are excluded.

### Can I apply for Rajasthan income certificate without Jan Aadhaar?

Yes — Aadhaar alone is sufficient. Jan Aadhaar is not mandatory but speeds up the process as family details are pre-filled.

### Rajasthan income certificate for scholarship — which income limit?

For **OBC scholarship**: income below ₹2.5 lakh/year. For **SC/ST**: income criterion varies by scheme — check Rajasthan Scholarship Portal at [hte.rajasthan.gov.in](https://hte.rajasthan.gov.in).

### Income certificate rejected in Rajasthan — how to fix?

Check rejection reason at SSO portal. Common reasons: Patwari couldn't verify at address, income declared seems unreasonable, documents missing. Fix the issue and re-apply with correct documents.
