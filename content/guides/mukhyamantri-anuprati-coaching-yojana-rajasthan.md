## What is Mukhyamantri Anuprati Coaching Yojana?

**Mukhyamantri Anuprati Coaching Yojana** is the Rajasthan government scheme providing **free coaching at top private coaching institutes** and a **maintenance stipend** to SC, ST, OBC (non-creamy layer), MBC, and EWS students preparing for competitive exams.

The scheme covers preparation for: UPSC (IAS/IPS), RPSC (RAS), SSC, banking exams, engineering (IIT-JEE), medical (NEET), law (CLAT), and CA exams.

## Exam Categories and Seats

| Exam Category | Approximate Seats |
|---|---|
| UPSC Civil Services | 200 |
| RPSC (Grade-1 & RAS) | 500 |
| Sub-Inspector (RPSC Grade-2) | 800 |
| Technical exams (RSMSSB, Rajasthan Police) | 800 |
| Engineering (IIT-JEE) | 100 |
| Medical (NEET) | 500 |
| CLAT (Law) | 50 |
| CA (Chartered Accountancy) | 100 |

Total of approximately **3,000–10,000 seats per year** (varies by budget and year).

## Annual Stipend (Maintenance Allowance)

| Residential Status | Amount |
|---|---|
| Hostel / out-of-home students | **₹40,000/year** |
| Day scholars | **₹20,000/year** |

For UPSC and engineering/medical (top institutes), the stipend may be higher — up to ₹1,00,000/year for some categories. Check current notifications.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Categories:** SC, ST, OBC (non-creamy layer), MBC, EWS
- **Residency:** Rajasthan domicile
- **Age:** As per exam eligibility (varies by exam)
- **Annual family income:**
  - SC/ST: Below ₹8 lakh/year
  - OBC/MBC/EWS: Below ₹8 lakh/year
- **Education:** Class 12 or graduation as per exam requirement
- Must be **not currently in government service**

## How to Apply

1. Go to **[sje.rajasthan.gov.in](https://sje.rajasthan.gov.in)** or the SMS portal **[sjms.rajasthan.gov.in](https://sjms.rajasthan.gov.in)**
2. Click **"Anuprati Coaching Yojana"**
3. Register with Jan Aadhaar card + mobile OTP
4. Fill application:
   - Category (SC/ST/OBC/EWS)
   - Exam you are preparing for
   - Choice of coaching institute (from approved list)
   - Educational qualifications
5. Upload documents
6. Submit → applications are assessed by the department
7. Merit-based selection (past exam marks or written test results)

**Jan Aadhaar card** (Rajasthan's family ID) is mandatory — apply for Jan Aadhaar at [janaadhaar.rajasthan.gov.in](https://janaadhaar.rajasthan.gov.in) if you don't have one.

## Empanelled Coaching Institutes

The government empanels top coaching institutes for each exam:
- For UPSC: Major coaching centres in Jaipur and Delhi
- For IIT-JEE: Kota coaching institutes (Resonance, Allen, Vibrant, etc.)
- For NEET: Kota biology coaching centres

The student selects from the empanelled list. Coaching institute fees are paid directly by the government to the institute.

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card**
2. **Jan Aadhaar card** (Rajasthan family ID)
3. **Category certificate** — caste certificate (SC/ST/OBC/MBC) or EWS certificate
4. **Income certificate** — annual family income below ₹8 lakh
5. **Domicile certificate** (Rajasthan)
6. **Class 10/12 marksheet** or qualifying exam certificate
7. **Bank account** details (applicant's own account)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I am OBC (creamy layer). Am I eligible?
No — OBC eligibility is limited to non-creamy layer (annual income below ₹8 lakh). If your family income exceeds this, you do not qualify for OBC category.

### Can I choose any coaching institute, or only government-empanelled ones?
Only **empanelled institutes** are covered under the scheme. The government selects reputed institutes and pays fees directly. Private arrangements outside this list are not reimbursed.

### When does the application window open?
Applications typically open once or twice a year. Check [sje.rajasthan.gov.in](https://sje.rajasthan.gov.in) for current year announcements.

### I am already enrolled in a coaching class and paid fees myself. Can I get reimbursement?
No — the scheme works prospectively. You must apply before joining the coaching, and the government pays the institute directly. Prior self-paid coaching is not reimbursed.

### Is Anuprati Coaching different from the older Anuprati Incentive Scheme?
Yes — the old **Anuprati Protsahan Yojana** gave cash incentives after clearing IAS/RAS prelims. **Mukhyamantri Anuprati Coaching Yojana** (launched 2021) covers actual coaching costs + stipend. The older incentive scheme continues separately.
