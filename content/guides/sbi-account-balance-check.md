# SBI Account Balance Check: 5 Easy Ways to Check

Checking your SBI account balance is quick and free. Whether you have a smartphone or a basic feature phone, SBI offers multiple ways to check your balance without visiting the branch. Here are all the methods with step-by-step instructions.

---

## Method 1: Missed Call — 09223766666

The simplest way to check your SBI balance. No internet needed — works on any phone.

### Steps:

1. Open the **dialer** on your phone
2. Call **09223766666** from your **registered mobile number**
3. The call will **disconnect automatically** after 1-2 rings
4. You will receive an **SMS** with your account balance within a few seconds

**Example SMS:** "Your a/c XXXX1234 has a balance of Rs. 25,000.00 on 06-Mar-2026"

**Requirements:**
- Your mobile number must be **registered** with your SBI account
- If you have multiple accounts, the SMS will show the balance of your **primary account**

---

## Method 2: SMS Balance Check

Send an SMS from your registered mobile number to check your balance.

### Steps:

1. Open the **SMS/Messages** app on your phone
2. Type **BAL** and send it to **09223766666**
3. You will receive a reply SMS with your **account balance**

**For specific account:** If you have multiple SBI accounts, type **BAL XXXX** (last 4 digits of account number) and send to 09223766666.

**Note:** Standard SMS charges may apply as per your telecom plan.

---

## Method 3: YONO SBI App

The YONO app shows your balance instantly on the dashboard.

### Steps:

1. Open the **YONO SBI** app on your smartphone
2. Log in with your **credentials** (username + password, YONO PIN, or biometric)
3. Your **account balance** is displayed on the **home screen dashboard**
4. Tap on the account to see **available balance** and **total balance** separately

**Tip:** Enable **Quick Balance** in YONO settings to check balance without full login — just enter your MPIN.

---

## Method 4: SBI Net Banking (OnlineSBI)

Check your balance through the SBI internet banking portal.

### Steps:

1. Visit [https://www.onlinesbi.sbi/](https://www.onlinesbi.sbi/)
2. Log in with your **username, password, and OTP**
3. Click on **"Account Summary"** under **"My Accounts & Profile"**
4. Your **balance** for all linked accounts will be displayed

You can also view your [account statement and transaction history](/guide/sbi-passbook-update-online) from the same section.

---

## Method 5: USSD Code *99#

Works on any phone (smartphone or feature phone) — no internet or app required. This is part of the **National Unified USSD Platform (NUUP)**.

### Steps:

1. Dial **\*99#** from your **registered mobile number**
2. A menu will appear on screen
3. Select option **1** — "Account Balance"
4. Enter your **bank short code**: type **SBI** or **SBIN** or **SBINO000XXX** (your branch IFSC)
5. Enter your **registered mobile number** or account number when prompted
6. Your balance will be displayed on screen

**Note:**
- USSD works without internet but requires **mobile network**
- There is a charge of **₹0.50 per session** (charged by your telecom provider)
- Maximum transaction limit via USSD is ₹5,000 (for transfers, not balance check)

---

## Method 6: ATM Balance Enquiry

Check your balance at any SBI or other bank ATM.

### Steps:

1. Insert your **SBI ATM/debit card** into the machine
2. Select your preferred **language**
3. Enter your **4-digit ATM PIN**
4. Select **"Balance Enquiry"**
5. Choose **Savings Account** or **Current Account**
6. Your balance will be displayed on screen (with option to print a mini slip)

**Note:** Balance enquiry at SBI ATMs is **free**. At other bank ATMs, free transactions are limited (currently **5 free transactions per month** at other bank ATMs for savings accounts, including financial and non-financial transactions).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: What is the SBI balance check number?

The SBI balance check number is **09223766666**. Give a missed call from your registered mobile number and you will receive an SMS with your balance.

### Q2: Can I check SBI balance without registered mobile number?

No, the missed call and SMS methods require a **registered mobile number**. Without it, you can check your balance at an **ATM** using your debit card, or through **net banking** if activated.

### Q3: Why am I not getting SMS after missed call to 09223766666?

This can happen if: (a) your mobile number is **not registered** with SBI, (b) there is a **network delay**, or (c) your **DND settings** are blocking banking SMS. Contact SBI customer care at **1800-11-2211** if the issue persists.

### Q4: Is SBI missed call balance check free?

Yes, the missed call balance check is completely **free**. SBI does not charge anything for this service. The call disconnects automatically and you receive a free SMS.

### Q5: How do I register my mobile number with SBI for balance check?

Visit your nearest SBI branch with your **passbook** and **Aadhaar card** (or any valid ID). Fill out the mobile number registration form. Your number will be activated within **1-2 working days**. See our guide on [SBI mobile number change](/guide/sbi-mobile-number-change) for details.

### Q6: Can I check balance for multiple SBI accounts via missed call?

The missed call shows the balance of your **primary account** only. For multiple accounts, use the **SMS method** — send "BAL XXXX" (last 4 digits of account number) to 09223766666, or use **YONO app / net banking** to view all accounts.
