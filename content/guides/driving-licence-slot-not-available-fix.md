# Driving Licence Slot Not Available: How to Fix

Booking a driving licence (DL) appointment slot on the Parivahan/Sarathi portal is one of the most common frustrations for Indian citizens. The slots for learner's licence, permanent DL test, and DL renewal fill up almost instantly in many cities. This guide provides practical solutions to secure your slot.

## Common Symptoms

- **"No slots available"** message when trying to book on Sarathi portal
- All dates show **greyed out** with no clickable slots
- Slots appear briefly and disappear within seconds
- **"Technical error"** when clicking on an available date
- Portal loads slowly or times out during slot booking
- Only dates weeks or months away are available
- **"Maximum booking limit reached"** for the selected RTO
- Payment completed but slot not confirmed

---

## Why DL Slots Are Hard to Get

### 1. High Demand, Limited Capacity

RTOs (Regional Transport Offices) have fixed daily capacity for tests and services. In metros and tier-1 cities, demand far exceeds available slots.

### 2. Slots Released at Specific Times

Most RTOs release new slots at fixed times (usually early morning or midnight). If you don't know the release time, you miss the window.

### 3. Portal Performance Issues

The Sarathi/Parivahan portal can be slow during peak hours, causing timeouts when trying to book slots.

### 4. Agents/Touts Bulk-Booking

In some areas, unauthorized agents bulk-book slots and resell them at a premium, reducing availability for genuine applicants.

### 5. Specific RTO Selected

Some RTOs are more popular or conveniently located. Less-known or suburban RTOs may have better availability.

## Step-by-Step Solutions

### Step 1: Know When Slots Are Released

Slots are typically released:
- **Midnight (12 AM)** — many RTOs release the next batch of slots at midnight
- **Early morning (6–8 AM)** — some RTOs release slots early morning
- **Start of the week (Monday)** — weekly slot batches in some states

**How to find your RTO's release time:**
1. Visit [sarathi.parivahan.gov.in](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/) and select your state
2. Try booking at different times over a few days to identify the pattern
3. Check local RTO Facebook pages or Google reviews — other applicants often share the slot release schedule
4. Call your RTO directly and ask when new slots become available

### Step 2: Be Ready Before Slot Release

Preparation is key:

1. **Complete your application** first — fill out all forms, upload documents, and reach the slot booking page before the release time
2. **Stay on the slot booking page** and refresh at the exact release time
3. Have your **payment method ready** (debit card, net banking, or UPI) — don't waste time searching for credentials
4. Use a **fast, stable internet connection** — wired broadband is better than mobile data
5. Use a **desktop/laptop browser** — it's faster than the mobile site or app

### Step 3: Try Different RTOs

You're not limited to the nearest RTO:

1. On the Sarathi portal, change the **RTO selection** to nearby RTOs
2. Suburban or rural RTOs often have significantly more availability
3. For permanent DL test, you can choose any RTO in your state
4. Check 3–4 nearby RTOs for slot availability
5. The commute to a farther RTO may be worth avoiding weeks of waiting

### Step 4: Try Alternate Browsers and Devices

Portal issues can be browser-specific:

1. Try **Google Chrome** (incognito mode), **Firefox**, or **Edge**
2. Clear browser cache and cookies before attempting
3. Disable browser extensions that might interfere
4. If the website is slow, try during **off-peak hours** (late night, early morning)
5. Try from a different device — sometimes mobile works when desktop doesn't (or vice versa)

### Step 5: Use the Parivahan Mobile App

The **mParivahan** app sometimes has different slot availability:

1. Download **mParivahan** from Play Store or App Store
2. Log in with your credentials
3. Navigate to the DL services section
4. Check slot availability — some slots may appear on the app but not the website

### Step 6: Handle Payment-Related Slot Issues

If payment was deducted but slot not confirmed:

1. **Don't pay again immediately** — check your application status first
2. Go to **Application Status** on the Sarathi portal
3. If status shows "Payment Successful" but no slot, the slot should be auto-assigned or you'll get a retry option
4. If the payment was deducted but failed on the portal, the amount is typically **refunded within 7–10 working days**
5. Contact the RTO with your transaction receipt if the issue persists

### Step 7: Visit the RTO in Person

If online booking consistently fails:

1. Visit your RTO during working hours (usually 10 AM – 5 PM)
2. Go to the **Help Desk/Enquiry Counter**
3. Explain that online slots are unavailable
4. Many RTOs have a **walk-in quota** or can book slots from their internal system
5. Carry all required documents (application printout, payment receipt if paid, original ID/address proof)

### Step 8: Check for Special Drives

Some RTOs conduct:
- **Weekend DL test drives** with extra slots
- **Camp-based licensing** in rural areas
- **Special drives** during festival seasons or government campaigns

Check your RTO's notice board or website for announcements.

## When to Contact Helpline

Contact if:
- You've been unable to book a slot for over 30 days despite trying at release times
- Payment was deducted but slot not confirmed and refund not received after 10 days
- The portal consistently shows technical errors
- You suspect agents are bulk-booking slots at your RTO

**Contact Options:**
- **Parivahan Helpdesk:** 0120-2459169
- **Email:** helpdesk-vahan@gov.in
- **State Transport Department** — contact details vary by state; check your state transport website
- **RTO direct contact** — visit in person or call the local RTO
- **Grievance:** File on [pgportal.gov.in](https://pgportal.gov.in/) under Ministry of Road Transport

## Prevention Tips

1. **Apply early** — don't wait until you urgently need the DL; start the process months in advance
2. **Set a reminder** for slot release time and book immediately when slots open
3. **Keep all documents ready** before starting the online application — incomplete applications waste time
4. **Bookmark the slot booking page** for quick access during release windows
5. **Avoid unauthorized agents** — they inflate demand and charge illegal premiums. All DL services are available directly through the Parivahan portal
6. **Renew before expiry** — [DL renewal](/guide/driving-licence-renewal-online) slots are easier to get than fresh DL test slots; renew well before your DL expires
7. **Learn from forums** — local subreddits, Facebook groups, and Google reviews for your RTO often have tips on the best booking times

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What time do DL slots open on the Parivahan portal?

It varies by RTO. Most RTOs release slots at **midnight (12 AM)** or **early morning (6–8 AM)**. Some release weekly batches on Mondays. Check by attempting booking at different times or calling your RTO.

### Can I book a DL test slot at any RTO in my state?

Yes, for most DL services, you can choose any RTO within your state. Some states may have restrictions for learner's licence (jurisdictional RTO only) but allow flexibility for permanent DL tests.

### What if slots are available but the page shows a technical error?

Clear your browser cache, try incognito mode, or switch browsers. The Parivahan portal can be unstable during peak hours. Try during off-peak times (late night or early morning).

### How far in advance can I book a DL slot?

Most RTOs allow booking **1–4 weeks in advance**. The booking window varies by state and RTO. Some RTOs release a month's worth of slots at once, while others release daily or weekly batches.

### I paid but my slot was not confirmed. Will I get a refund?

Yes. Failed transactions are typically refunded to your original payment method within **7–10 working days**. If the refund doesn't arrive, contact the RTO with your transaction receipt and application number.

### Can I change my DL slot after booking?

Yes, most RTOs allow **one rescheduling** through the Sarathi portal. Log in, go to your application, and look for the reschedule option. Some RTOs may not allow rescheduling — in that case, you may need to book a fresh slot.
