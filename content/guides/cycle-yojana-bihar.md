# Mukhyamantri Balak Balika Cycle Yojana Bihar: Free Cycle for Class 9 Students

The Mukhyamantri Balak Balika Cycle Yojana is a popular education scheme by the Bihar government that provides **₹3,000** to students of Class 9 studying in government schools to purchase a bicycle. The scheme aims to reduce school dropout rates by making it easier for students in rural areas to commute to school.

## What is the Bihar Cycle Yojana?

Launched by the Bihar Education Department, this scheme provides financial assistance to both boys and girls enrolled in Class 9 at government and government-aided schools. The ₹3,000 is transferred directly to the student's bank account via DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer), enabling them to buy a bicycle for their daily school commute.

The scheme has significantly improved school attendance, especially in rural Bihar where schools may be several kilometres from students' homes.

## Who is Eligible?

- Student must be enrolled in **Class 9** of a **government or government-aided school** in Bihar
- Both **boys and girls** are eligible
- The student must be a **resident of Bihar**
- The student must have a **bank account** in their own name (or joint account with parent)
- The student must have an **Aadhaar card**
- The student should have **passed Class 8** from a recognised school

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** of the student
- **Bank account passbook** (in student's name)
- **Class 8 marksheet** / passing certificate
- **School enrolment proof** for Class 9
- **Passport-size photograph**
- **Parent's Aadhaar card** (for verification)

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### How to Apply (Through School)

The application for Bihar Cycle Yojana is typically processed **through the school**. Individual students do not apply directly.

1. **School Registration:** The school headmaster registers the school and students on the **[Medhasoft portal](https://medhasoft.bihar.gov.in/)**
2. **Student Data Entry:** The school enters details of all eligible Class 9 students including Aadhaar number, bank account details, and photograph
3. **Verification:** The data is verified by the Block Education Officer (BEO)
4. **Approval:** After verification, the list is approved at the district level
5. **DBT Transfer:** ₹3,000 is directly transferred to the student's bank account
6. **Acknowledgement:** Students can check their payment status on the Medhasoft portal

### What Students/Parents Should Do

1. Ensure the student has an **Aadhaar card** linked to their bank account
2. Open a **bank account** in the student's name if not already available
3. Submit all required documents to the **school office**
4. Confirm with the school that your name is included in the **Medhasoft portal list**
5. Check the payment status on Medhasoft once the school confirms submission

## Fees

There are **no fees** for this scheme. The entire process is free of cost.

## Processing Time

- Schools upload data within the first few months of the academic session
- Verification takes **2-4 weeks** at block/district level
- DBT transfer usually happens within **1-3 months** after approval
- Check status at [medhasoft.bihar.gov.in](https://medhasoft.bihar.gov.in/)

## Important Tips

1. **Open a bank account early** — if your child is entering Class 9, ensure they have a bank account with Aadhaar linked before the session starts
2. **Follow up with the school** — ensure the headmaster has included your child's details in the Medhasoft portal
3. **Keep Aadhaar updated** — make sure the student's name and details match between Aadhaar and school records
4. **Private school students are not eligible** — only government and government-aided school students qualify
5. **Don't pay middlemen** — this is a free government scheme. No fees or bribes are required

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Is the cycle given directly or is money transferred?
The Bihar government transfers **₹3,000 directly to the student's bank account** via DBT. Students can then purchase a bicycle of their choice.

### Q2: Are private school students eligible?
No, only students studying in **government or government-aided schools** in Bihar are eligible for this scheme.

### Q3: Can students from other states studying in Bihar avail this scheme?
No, the student must be a **resident of Bihar** studying in a Bihar government school.

### Q4: What if the school has not uploaded my details on Medhasoft?
Contact your school headmaster and request them to upload your details. You can also approach the **Block Education Officer (BEO)** if the school is not cooperating.

### Q5: Is this scheme available every year?
Yes, the Bihar Cycle Yojana is an ongoing scheme available every academic year for fresh Class 9 students.

### Q6: Can a student who already received the cycle money in a previous year get it again?
No, the benefit is a **one-time assistance** per student when they enter Class 9.

### Q7: What is the Medhasoft portal?
Medhasoft ([medhasoft.bihar.gov.in](https://medhasoft.bihar.gov.in/)) is the official Bihar Education Department portal used for managing various student welfare schemes including Cycle Yojana, Poshak Yojana, and scholarship disbursements.
