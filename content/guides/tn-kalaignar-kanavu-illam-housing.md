## What is Kalaignar Kanavu Illam Housing Scheme?

Kalaignar Kanavu Illam (Kalaignar's Dream Home) is a **housing scheme** launched by the Government of Tamil Nadu to provide **free or affordable houses** to economically weaker sections and homeless families. Named after former Chief Minister M. Karunanidhi, the scheme aims to ensure that every family in Tamil Nadu has a pucca (permanent) house.

The scheme works alongside the central **Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY)** to provide housing to urban and rural poor. Under this scheme, beneficiaries receive financial assistance to construct a house or are allotted ready-built houses in government housing complexes.

Key features:
- **Free or highly subsidised housing** for BPL families
- **Financial assistance** of ₹2.5 lakh to ₹4 lakh per unit (combined state and central funding)
- **Both urban and rural** components
- Houses built with modern amenities — toilet, kitchen, electricity, water connection

## Who is Eligible?

### Basic Eligibility
- Must be a **resident of Tamil Nadu**
- Must be **homeless** or living in a kutcha (temporary) house
- Must belong to **economically weaker section (EWS)** or **low-income group (LIG)**
- Must not own any pucca house anywhere in India
- At least one family member must hold a valid **Aadhaar card**

### Income Criteria
- **EWS (Economically Weaker Section):** Annual household income up to ₹3,00,000
- **LIG (Low Income Group):** Annual household income between ₹3,00,001 and ₹6,00,000
- BPL families get priority

### Priority Categories
- **Women-headed households** (widows, single mothers)
- **SC/ST families**
- **Differently-abled persons**
- **Families displaced by natural disasters**
- **Transgender persons**
- **Senior citizens living alone**

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** of all adult family members
- **Ration card** (priority/BPL card preferred)
- **Income certificate** from Tahsildar
- **Proof of landlessness** or declaration of not owning a pucca house
- **Voter ID** or other residence proof
- **Community certificate** (SC/ST/OBC if applicable)
- **Bank account passbook** (Aadhaar-linked, preferably in woman's name)
- **Passport-size photographs** (4 copies)
- **Land patta** (if applying for construction assistance on own land)
- **Disability certificate** (if applicable)

## Financial Assistance

| Component | Amount |
|-----------|--------|
| Central share (PMAY-G) | ₹1,20,000 |
| State share | ₹1,30,000 – ₹2,80,000 |
| Total per unit (rural) | ₹2,50,000 – ₹4,00,000 |
| Total per unit (urban) | Up to ₹4,50,000 |
| Toilet construction (SBM) | ₹12,000 (additional) |
| MGNREGA labour | 90 days of wages |

*Note: Amounts vary based on location (rural/urban/hilly areas) and may be revised.*

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Online Application (Urban Areas)

1. Visit the **TNHB portal** at [tnhb.tn.gov.in](https://tnhb.tn.gov.in) or the PMAY portal
2. Click on **"Housing Scheme Application"**
3. Register with your mobile number and Aadhaar
4. Fill in the application form — personal details, income, current housing status
5. Upload required documents
6. Select the **housing project/location** if applying for ready-built houses
7. Submit and note the **application number**
8. Verification will be conducted by local municipal authorities
9. Approved beneficiaries will be allotted houses through a **transparent draw** or priority list

### Offline Application (Rural Areas)

1. Visit your **Panchayat office** or **Block Development Office (BDO)**
2. Inform the Village Panchayat president about your housing need
3. Your name should be included in the **Awaas+ list** (SECC/PMAY-G beneficiary list)
4. Collect and fill the application form
5. Attach photocopies of all required documents
6. Submit to the BDO office
7. A field verification will be conducted by the Block-level officer
8. Approved beneficiaries receive funds in **instalments** linked to construction milestones:
   - 1st instalment: At foundation level
   - 2nd instalment: At lintel level
   - 3rd instalment: At roof completion

### Through Amma Makkal Munnetra Kazhagam / District Collectorate

1. Visit the **District Collectorate** during grievance redressal day
2. Submit a petition for housing allotment
3. The Collector's office will forward your request to the TNHB or DRDA

## Processing Time

- **Application to approval** — 2 to 6 months
- **House allotment (ready-built)** — 6 to 12 months after approval
- **Self-construction** — Funds released in 3 instalments over 12–18 months
- **Total timeline** — 1 to 2 years from application to house possession

## Important Tips

1. **Register on Awaas+ portal** — For rural housing, your name must be on the SECC/PMAY-G beneficiary list. Visit [pmayg.nic.in](https://pmayg.nic.in) to check.
2. **Women's name on title** — The house will preferably be registered in the **woman's name** or jointly. Ensure the bank account is also in her name.
3. **Don't pay middlemen** — Housing allotment is free. Report anyone demanding money to the District Collector.
4. **Photograph construction stages** — For self-construction, take geotagged photos at each stage as the department requires them for instalment release.
5. **Link Aadhaar to bank account** — All payments are via DBT. See our [Aadhaar linking guide](/guide/aadhaar-linking-guide) for help.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Is Kalaignar Kanavu Illam completely free?
For EWS/BPL families, the house is effectively **free** — the government covers the full construction cost. Some beneficiaries may need to contribute labour through MGNREGA.

### Q2: Can I apply if I already own land but not a house?
Yes. If you own land, you can apply for **construction assistance** on your own plot. You'll receive the financial assistance to build a pucca house.

### Q3: How is this different from PM Awas Yojana?
Kalaignar Kanavu Illam is the **Tamil Nadu state component** that works alongside PMAY. The financial assistance combines central (PMAY) and state funds. See our [PM Awas Yojana guide](/guide/pm-awas-yojana-apply) for the central scheme details.

### Q4: Can I choose the location of my house?
For ready-built houses, you can indicate **preferred locations** but final allotment depends on availability. For self-construction, you build on your own land.

### Q5: What is the size of houses provided?
Houses are typically **300–400 sq ft** carpet area with 1–2 rooms, kitchen, toilet, and basic amenities. Urban apartments may vary in size.

### Q6: How do I check if my name is on the beneficiary list?
Visit [pmayg.nic.in](https://pmayg.nic.in) for rural areas and search by your Aadhaar number or name. For urban areas, check with your local municipal office.

### Q7: Can I sell the house after receiving it?
No. There is a **lock-in period** (usually 5–10 years) during which the house cannot be sold, transferred, or mortgaged. This ensures the house is used by the intended beneficiary.
