# e-Shram Card Update Mobile or Name? Step-by-Step Fix

Need to update your **mobile number, name, address, or bank details** on your e-Shram card? This guide covers every type of update with step-by-step instructions for both online and offline methods.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent platform, not affiliated with any government body. Verify at [eshram.gov.in](https://eshram.gov.in).

## What Can You Update on e-Shram?

| Field | Update Method |
|-------|--------------|
| Mobile number | Directly on e-Shram portal |
| Bank account/IFSC | Directly on e-Shram portal |
| Occupation | Directly on e-Shram portal |
| Nominee details | Directly on e-Shram portal |
| Name | Update Aadhaar first → reflects on e-Shram |
| Address | Update Aadhaar first → reflects on e-Shram |
| Date of Birth | Update Aadhaar first → reflects on e-Shram |
| Photo | Update Aadhaar first → reflects on e-Shram |

## Update 1: Change Mobile Number on e-Shram

### Online Method

1. Visit [register.eshram.gov.in](https://register.eshram.gov.in)
2. Click **"Already Registered"**
3. Enter your **Aadhaar number** and captcha
4. Click **"Send OTP"** — OTP goes to your **Aadhaar-linked mobile** (not old e-Shram mobile)
5. Enter OTP and log in
6. Click **"Update Profile"** on your dashboard
7. Change your mobile number in the mobile field
8. An OTP will be sent to your **new mobile number** for verification
9. Enter the new OTP and submit
10. Download updated e-Shram card

> **Important:** You need access to your Aadhaar-linked mobile to log in. If you've lost that number too, first update mobile on Aadhaar at an enrollment centre.

### At CSC Centre

1. Visit nearest CSC with Aadhaar card and new mobile phone
2. Request e-Shram mobile number update
3. Verify with Aadhaar OTP + new mobile OTP
4. Fee: ₹20-30

## Update 2: Correct Name on e-Shram

Your name on e-Shram is **pulled directly from Aadhaar**. You cannot edit it independently on the e-Shram portal.

**Steps to fix:**

1. **Update name on Aadhaar first:**
   - Visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in)
   - Click "Update Aadhaar" → select "Name"
   - Upload supporting document (PAN, passport, voter ID, etc.)
   - Pay ₹50 fee
   - Takes 7-15 working days

2. **After Aadhaar name is updated:**
   - Log in to [register.eshram.gov.in](https://register.eshram.gov.in)
   - Your updated name from Aadhaar will reflect automatically
   - Download fresh e-Shram card with corrected name

## Update 3: Change Address on e-Shram

Like name, your address comes from Aadhaar.

**Steps:**

1. Update address on Aadhaar:
   - **Online:** [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in) → Update Aadhaar → Address (₹50 fee, needs address proof document)
   - **Offline:** Visit Aadhaar centre with address proof document
   - See our [Aadhaar address change guide](/guide/aadhaar-address-change-online) for detailed steps

2. After Aadhaar update reflects (7-15 days):
   - Log in to e-Shram portal
   - Download updated card — new address will show

## Update 4: Change Bank Account Details

Bank details can be updated directly on the e-Shram portal. This is important for receiving scheme benefits via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

**Steps:**

1. Visit [register.eshram.gov.in](https://register.eshram.gov.in)
2. Click **"Already Registered"** → log in with Aadhaar + OTP
3. Go to **"Update Profile"**
4. Edit **Bank Account Number** and **IFSC Code**
5. Ensure the account is:
   - In **your name** (matching Aadhaar name)
   - A **savings account**
   - **Active** and linked to Aadhaar (for DBT)
6. Submit and verify
7. Download updated e-Shram card

> **Tip:** Link your bank account to Aadhaar via your bank for seamless DBT. Most banks offer this through net banking or by visiting a branch.

## Update 5: Change Occupation or Nominee

These can also be updated directly:

1. Log in at [register.eshram.gov.in](https://register.eshram.gov.in)
2. Go to **"Update Profile"**
3. Edit occupation category, sub-category, or nominee details
4. Submit changes
5. Download updated card

## Troubleshooting Update Errors

### "Unable to Update" Error
- Clear browser cache and try again
- Use Chrome or Firefox
- Try during off-peak hours

### OTP Not Received for Login
- The login OTP goes to your **Aadhaar-linked mobile**, not the e-Shram mobile
- If you don't have access to Aadhaar-linked mobile, update it at an Aadhaar centre first

### Changes Not Reflecting on Card
- Wait 24 hours after updating
- Log out and log in again
- Download a fresh copy of the card

## Important Tips

- **Always download a new card** after making any updates
- **Bank account must match Aadhaar name** for DBT benefits
- **Keep your Aadhaar updated** — e-Shram depends on Aadhaar data for name, address, and photo
- **Free service** — don't pay anyone for online updates (CSC may charge ₹20-30)
- **Save your UAN** — you'll need it for all future interactions
