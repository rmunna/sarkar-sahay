# Arunachal Pradesh Old Age Pension – Complete Guide 2026

## What is the Arunachal Pradesh Old Age Pension?

The **Arunachal Pradesh Old Age Pension** provides monthly financial assistance to senior citizens from BPL families under the **IGNOAPS** with state top-up from the Arunachal Pradesh government.

## Pension Amount

| Age Group | Approximate Monthly Pension |
|-----------|---------------------------|
| 60-79 years | ₹400-800 |
| 80+ years | ₹700-1,200 |

> Includes central and state components. Verify with the District Social Welfare Office.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Age:** 60 years or above
- **Residency:** Permanent resident of Arunachal Pradesh (APST certificate or domicile)
- **Income:** BPL or below prescribed income limit
- **Not receiving** any other government pension or retirement benefits

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar Card
- APST certificate or permanent resident certificate
- Age proof (birth certificate / voter ID / school certificate)
- BPL card or income certificate
- Bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked)
- Residential proof
- Passport-size photographs (2)
- Gaon Burah / Village Authority certificate

## How to Apply – Step-by-Step

1. Visit the **Gaon Burah (Village Head) office** or **Circle Office**
2. Collect the **Old Age Pension application form**
3. Fill in personal, age, income, and bank details
4. Attach self-attested document copies
5. Get verified by the **Gaon Burah / Village Authority**
6. Submit at the **Circle Office** or **District Social Welfare Office**
7. After verification and district-level approval, pension is credited via DBT

## Processing Time

- **Verification to first payment:** 2-4 months

## Important Tips

1. **Gaon Burah certification** is important in Arunachal Pradesh
2. **APST certificate** serves as primary identity/residency proof
3. **Link Aadhaar to bank account** before applying
4. **Annual life verification** is mandatory
5. **Contact the DSWO** for any pension-related grievances

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: What is the pension amount?
**A:** Approximately **₹400-800/month** for 60-79 and **₹700-1,200** for 80+.

### Q2: Can I apply online?
**A:** Check [nsap.nic.in](https://nsap.nic.in). Primary method is offline through the Circle Office.

### Q3: Is APST certificate mandatory?
**A:** APST certificate or permanent resident certificate is needed as residency proof.

### Q4: What role does the Gaon Burah play?
**A:** The **Gaon Burah (village head)** verifies the applicant's identity, residency, and eligibility at the village level.

### Q5: How is the pension paid?
**A:** Through **DBT** to the Aadhaar-linked bank account.

### Q6: Can I avail this along with Dulari Kanya for my daughter?
**A:** Yes, these are **separate schemes** — old age pension for the senior citizen and Dulari Kanya for the girl child. See our [Dulari Kanya guide](/guide/ar-dulari-kanya).
