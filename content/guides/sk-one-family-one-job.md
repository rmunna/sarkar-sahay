# One Family One Job Scheme Sikkim – Complete Guide 2026

## What is the One Family One Job (OFOJ) Scheme?

The **One Family One Job (OFOJ) Scheme** is a landmark employment programme launched by the Government of Sikkim in 2019. Under this scheme, one member from each family in Sikkim that has **no government employee** is guaranteed a job in the state government or public sector undertaking. This is one of the most ambitious employment guarantees by any Indian state.

The scheme was introduced by Chief Minister P.S. Tamang (Golay) to address unemployment and ensure that every family in Sikkim has a source of stable income through government employment.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Residency:** Must be a permanent resident of Sikkim with a valid **Sikkim Subject Certificate (SSC)** or **Certificate of Identification (COI)**
- **Family condition:** No member of the family should be currently employed in any government department, PSU, or government-aided organisation
- **Age:** The applicant must be between **18-40 years** of age
- **Education:** Minimum qualification varies by post (Class 8 to graduation depending on the position)
- **One per family:** Only **one member per family** can avail the benefit

### Who is NOT Eligible?

- Families where any member is already a government employee
- Applicants outside the 18-40 age bracket
- Those not holding a valid Sikkim Subject Certificate or COI
- Families who have already availed the benefit for one member

## Documents Required

### Mandatory Documents
- Sikkim Subject Certificate (SSC) or Certificate of Identification (COI)
- Aadhaar Card
- Educational qualification certificates (marksheets, degree)
- Age proof (birth certificate / Class 10 certificate)
- Bank passbook (in applicant's name)
- Passport-size photographs (3)
- Family member certificate from the concerned Panchayat/Municipal authority

### Supporting Documents
- Employment exchange registration (if available)
- Unemployment certificate
- Self-declaration that no family member is in government service
- Residential proof

## How to Apply – Step-by-Step

### Online Application

1. Visit the OFOJ portal at [ofoj.sikkim.gov.in](https://ofoj.sikkim.gov.in) (when active)
2. Click on **"New Registration"**
3. Enter your Sikkim Subject Certificate/COI number and mobile number
4. Verify OTP and create your account
5. Fill in personal details, educational qualifications, and family information
6. Upload scanned copies of all required documents
7. Submit the application and **note your registration number**
8. Wait for verification by the concerned department

### Offline Application

1. Visit the **District Collector's Office** or **Block Administrative Centre (BAC)**
2. Collect the OFOJ application form
3. Fill in all details completely
4. Attach self-attested copies of all documents
5. Submit at the designated counter
6. Collect the acknowledgment receipt

### Selection Process

1. **Document verification** by the district administration
2. **Family verification** to confirm no government employee exists in the family
3. **Posting** based on educational qualification and available vacancies
4. Selected candidates receive **appointment letters** from the concerned department

## Salary / Remuneration

| Category | Approximate Monthly Salary |
|----------|---------------------------|
| Class IV / Group D posts | ₹10,000-15,000 |
| Skilled / Technical posts | ₹12,000-20,000 |
| Graduate-level posts | ₹15,000-25,000 |

> **Note:** Salaries are fixed by the state government and may be on a consolidated basis initially. Exact amounts depend on the specific post.

## Processing Time

- **Application review:** 1-3 months
- **Verification:** 1-2 months
- **Posting/appointment:** Varies based on vacancies (may take several months)

## Important Tips

1. **Keep your SSC/COI updated** — this is the primary identity document for eligibility
2. **File a complete application** — incomplete forms are rejected
3. **Regularly check the OFOJ portal** for updates on your application status
4. **Ensure no family member is in government service** — false declarations lead to disqualification and legal action
5. **Register at the Employment Exchange** for additional job opportunities

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: What type of jobs are offered under OFOJ?
**A:** Jobs range from **Group D (peon, driver, helper)** to **skilled posts (computer operator, clerk)** depending on qualifications and available vacancies.

### Q2: Is the job permanent?
**A:** Initially, many appointments are on a **temporary/consolidated basis**. Regularisation depends on government policy and performance.

### Q3: Can I choose my preferred department?
**A:** You can indicate preference, but **posting is subject to available vacancies**. The government allocates posts based on need.

### Q4: What if I have a family member in a PSU?
**A:** If any family member is employed in a **government department, PSU, or government-aided organisation**, the family is **not eligible**.

### Q5: How do I check my application status?
**A:** Visit [ofoj.sikkim.gov.in](https://ofoj.sikkim.gov.in) and log in with your registration number, or contact the **District Collector's Office**.

### Q6: Is there any application fee?
**A:** **No fee** is charged for applying under the OFOJ scheme.
