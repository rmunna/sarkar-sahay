# Goa Government Schemes and Services – Complete Guide

Goa, India's smallest state by area, is known for its high per-capita income, progressive social welfare policies, and strong human development indicators. Governed by the BJP under Chief Minister **Pramod Sawant**, the state runs an extensive network of schemes covering social security, women's empowerment, healthcare, education, IT entrepreneurship, and welfare for fishermen, persons with disabilities, and mining-affected communities.

This comprehensive hub covers every major Goa government scheme, eligibility criteria, benefits, and how to apply — with direct links to official portals.

## Women and Family Welfare Schemes

### Griha Aadhar Scheme (Housewife Allowance)

Goa's flagship **Griha Aadhar** scheme provides a monthly financial allowance to homemakers — one of the few such programs in India recognizing the contribution of housewives.

- **Benefit:** ₹1,500 per month to the woman head of household
- **Eligibility:** Female homemaker, Goa resident for 15+ years, family income below specified limit
- **How to apply:** Through Goa Online portal or local Mamlatdar office

> 📖 **Detailed guide:** [Goa Griha Aadhar Scheme – Eligibility, Documents & Application](/guide/goa-griha-aadhar-scheme)

### Laadli Laxmi Scheme

Aimed at improving the status of the girl child and ensuring her education:

- **Benefit:** Financial assistance deposited in stages from birth to age 18, totalling up to ₹1 lakh
- **Eligibility:** Girl child born to Goa-domiciled family, registered within one year of birth
- **Conditions:** Child must remain enrolled in school; family income criteria apply
- **How to apply:** Department of Women and Child Development, or Goa Online portal

### Chief Minister's Rojgar Yojana (Women Component)

Provides subsidised loans and skill training to women entrepreneurs looking to start small businesses in Goa.

## Social Security and Pension Schemes

### Dayanand Social Security Scheme (DSSS)

Goa's comprehensive social security pension scheme covers senior citizens, persons with disabilities, and other vulnerable groups.

- **Benefit:** Monthly pension ranging from ₹2,500 to ₹3,500 depending on category
- **Categories covered:** Old age (60+), disability, single women, widows
- **Eligibility:** Goa resident for 15+ years, income below prescribed limit
- **How to apply:** Mamlatdar office or Goa Online

> 📖 **Detailed guide:** [Goa Dayanand Social Security Scheme – Complete Details](/guide/goa-dayanand-social-security)

### Persons with Disability (PwD) Welfare Schemes

Goa provides multiple benefits for persons with disabilities:

- **Disability pension** under DSSS (₹3,000–₹3,500/month)
- **Free bus travel** on Kadamba Transport buses
- **Assistive devices** — wheelchairs, hearing aids, prosthetics provided free
- **Scholarship schemes** for students with disabilities
- **Reservation** in government jobs (4% as per RPwD Act)
- **How to apply:** Directorate of Social Welfare or Goa Online portal with disability certificate (40%+ disability)

## Healthcare Schemes

### Deen Dayal Swasthya Seva Yojana (DDSSY)

Goa's universal health insurance scheme provides cashless treatment to all residents — one of the most generous state health programs in India.

- **Benefit:** Cashless hospitalisation up to ₹4 lakh per family per year
- **Coverage:** 1,400+ medical procedures including surgeries, cancer treatment, and organ transplants
- **Eligibility:** All Goa residents with Aadhaar and ration card; no income cap for basic coverage
- **Empanelled hospitals:** Government and private hospitals across Goa and select hospitals in Mumbai, Pune, and Bengaluru
- **How to apply:** Register at [ddssy.goa.gov.in](https://www.ddssy.goa.gov.in) or any empanelled hospital

### Other Health Initiatives

- **Free generic medicines** at all government health centres
- **108 Emergency Ambulance Service** — free across Goa
- **Goa Medical College** subsidised treatment for BPL families

## Education and Youth Schemes

### Cyber Age Scheme (Free Smartphones)

The Goa government's **Cyber Age** scheme provides free smartphones to students and youth to bridge the digital divide.

- **Benefit:** Free smartphone with data connectivity
- **Eligibility:** Students from government-aided schools and colleges; economically weaker sections prioritised
- **Purpose:** Digital literacy, e-learning access, and exposure to online government services
- **How to apply:** Through educational institutions or Directorate of Education

### Other Education Schemes

- **Merit-based scholarships** — For Class 10 and 12 toppers in state board exams
- **Post-matric scholarships** for SC/ST/OBC students
- **Dr. T.M.A. Pai Scholarship** — For higher education in professional courses
- **Free education** in government schools up to higher secondary level
- **Book grant and uniform allowance** for economically weaker students

## IT, Startup, and Entrepreneurship Schemes

### Goa IT Policy

Goa's IT Policy aims to establish the state as an IT and electronics hub with incentives for technology companies:

- **Capital subsidy** up to 25% on fixed assets for IT/ITES units
- **Stamp duty exemption** on property purchase for IT companies
- **Employment incentive** — reimbursement of EPF contributions for new hires
- **Internet bandwidth subsidy** for startups and IT firms

### Goa Startup Policy

Designed to foster innovation and entrepreneurship:

- **Seed funding** up to ₹10 lakh for early-stage startups
- **Incubation support** through Goa IT Innovation Centre
- **Mentorship programs** with industry experts
- **Patent filing cost reimbursement** up to ₹10 lakh
- **How to apply:** Through Goa IT Department or the state's startup portal

### Chief Minister's Rojgar Yojana

Subsidised loans for educated unemployed youth to start self-employment ventures:

- **Loan amount:** Up to ₹15 lakh (₹25 lakh for manufacturing)
- **Subsidy:** 15–30% capital subsidy on sanctioned loan
- **Eligibility:** Goa resident, age 18–45, relevant qualification

## Fishermen Welfare Schemes

Goa's coastline-dependent fishing community benefits from targeted welfare measures:

- **Fuel subsidy** for mechanised and traditional fishing boats
- **Fishing ban period assistance** — ₹3,500/month during monsoon fishing ban
- **Housing assistance** under the Fishermen Housing Scheme
- **Insurance coverage** — Accidental death and disability insurance for fishermen
- **Boat and net subsidy** — Up to 50% subsidy on fishing equipment
- **Save the Fish Campaign** — Conservation and sustainable fishing initiatives
- **How to apply:** Directorate of Fisheries, Panaji, or through local Panchayat

## Tourism Industry Schemes

As India's premier tourist destination, Goa offers incentives to tourism stakeholders:

- **Tourism Trade Incentive Scheme** — Subsidies for hotels, resorts, and tourism service providers
- **Heritage tourism grants** — Financial assistance for restoration of heritage properties for tourism use
- **Shack allotment policy** — Regulated beach shack licensing for local entrepreneurs
- **Skill development** in hospitality through Goa Institute of Management and IITTM
- **Eco-tourism initiatives** — Support for sustainable tourism ventures in hinterland areas
- **How to apply:** Department of Tourism, Government of Goa

## Mining-Affected Area Welfare Schemes

Following the Supreme Court-imposed mining ban and its aftermath, Goa provides specific relief:

- **Direct financial assistance** to mining-dependent families and workers
- **Skill retraining programs** for displaced mining workers
- **Goa Mining Permanent Fund** — Portion of mining revenue earmarked for affected communities
- **Alternative livelihood support** — Subsidies for shifting to agriculture, fisheries, or small business
- **Infrastructure development** in mining belt talukas (Bicholim, Sattari, Quepem, Sanguem)
- **How to apply:** District Collector's office in the respective taluka

## How to Apply for Goa Government Schemes

Most Goa government schemes follow a standardised application process:

1. **Visit Goa Online Portal** — Go to [goaonline.gov.in](https://goaonline.gov.in) and create an account using your Aadhaar number and mobile
2. **Select the scheme** — Browse by department or search for the specific scheme
3. **Fill the application form** — Enter personal details, income information, and upload required documents
4. **Common documents needed:**
   - Aadhaar card
   - Goa domicile certificate (15-year residence proof)
   - Income certificate from Mamlatdar
   - Ration card
   - Bank passbook (for DBT)
   - Passport-size photographs
5. **Submit and track** — Note the application reference number and track status online
6. **Offline option** — Visit your nearest Mamlatdar office, BDO office, or concerned department with physical copies

**Pro tip:** Many schemes require a **15-year domicile certificate** — apply for this first if you don't have one. It can be obtained through the Mamlatdar office in your taluka.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for Goa's Griha Aadhar scheme?
Any female homemaker who is a Goa resident for 15+ years and whose family income is below the prescribed limit. The woman must be the head of the household. [Read our detailed Griha Aadhar guide](/guide/goa-griha-aadhar-scheme).

### Is DDSSY available for all Goa residents regardless of income?
Yes, the Deen Dayal Swasthya Seva Yojana covers all Goa residents. Basic coverage of up to ₹4 lakh per family per year is available regardless of income, though enhanced benefits may have income criteria.

### How do I apply for the Dayanand Social Security pension?
You can apply through the Goa Online portal or visit your local Mamlatdar office with Aadhaar, income certificate, age proof, and bank details. [See our complete DSSS guide](/guide/goa-dayanand-social-security).

### Can non-Goans benefit from state government schemes?
Most Goa schemes require 15 years of continuous residence (domicile). Central schemes implemented in Goa (like PMAY, Ayushman Bharat) may have different eligibility criteria based on ration card or Aadhaar address.

### What is the Cyber Age scheme and who gets free smartphones?
The Cyber Age scheme provides free smartphones to students from government and government-aided institutions, prioritising economically weaker sections. Applications are routed through educational institutions.

### How can I check the status of my scheme application?
Log in to [goaonline.gov.in](https://goaonline.gov.in) with your credentials, go to "My Applications," and enter your reference number to track real-time status.

### Are there special schemes for the fishing community in Goa?
Yes, Goa offers fuel subsidies, monsoon ban period assistance, housing schemes, insurance, and equipment subsidies specifically for fishermen through the Directorate of Fisheries.
