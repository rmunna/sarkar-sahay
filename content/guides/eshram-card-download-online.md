# E-Shram Card Download Online: Print Your UAN Card Easily

The **E-Shram card** is a digital identity card for India's unorganised sector workers issued by the Ministry of Labour & Employment. If you've already registered on the E-Shram portal, you can **download your UAN card online** as a PDF and print it. This guide covers the complete download process, benefits, and how to update your details.

## What is E-Shram Card?

E-Shram is the national database for **unorganised workers** in India — including street vendors, domestic workers, construction labourers, gig workers, agricultural labourers, and others not covered under EPFO or ESIC. Each registered worker receives a **UAN (Universal Account Number)** — a unique 12-digit identification number.

## How to Download E-Shram Card Online

### Step 1: Visit the E-Shram Portal
1. Go to [register.eshram.gov.in](https://register.eshram.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Already Registered"** on the homepage

### Step 2: Enter Mobile Number
1. Enter your **registered mobile number**
2. Enter the **captcha code** shown on screen
3. Click **"Send OTP"**

### Step 3: Verify OTP
1. Enter the **OTP** received on your mobile
2. Click **"Verify"**

### Step 4: Download UAN Card
1. Your E-Shram profile page will open
2. Click on **"Download UAN Card"** button
3. Your card downloads as a **PDF file**
4. Open the PDF and **print** it

### Step 5: Save and Print
- The PDF contains your **photo, name, UAN number, and QR code**
- Print on **A4 paper** for best quality
- Laminate the card for durability
- Keep a digital copy on your phone

## How to Find Your UAN Number

If you've forgotten your UAN number:

1. Visit [register.eshram.gov.in](https://register.eshram.gov.in)
2. Click **"Already Registered"**
3. Enter your registered mobile number
4. Verify with OTP
5. Your **UAN number** will be displayed on the dashboard
6. You can also find it on any previous SMS from E-Shram

## Benefits of E-Shram Card

### Insurance Benefits
- **₹2 lakh accidental death insurance** under PM Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY)
- **₹1 lakh partial disability** coverage
- Premium paid by Government of India

### Scheme Access
- **PM-SVANidhi:** Micro-credit for street vendors
- **PMJJBY:** Life insurance at ₹436/year
- **BOCW:** Building and construction worker benefits
- **MGNREGA:** Priority in employment guarantee scheme
- **PM Kisan:** For eligible agricultural workers
- **Ration card benefits:** Priority for PDS

### Other Benefits
- Acts as **identity proof** for unorganised workers
- **Portable** — valid across all states
- Helps government **plan welfare schemes** better
- May be required for future **social security benefits**

## How to Update E-Shram Card Details

If your details have changed (address, mobile number, occupation):

### Online Update
1. Visit [register.eshram.gov.in](https://register.eshram.gov.in)
2. Click **"Already Registered"** and log in with OTP
3. Click **"Update Profile"**
4. Edit the details you want to change
5. Submit and verify with OTP
6. Download the **updated UAN card**

### Update via CSC Centre
1. Visit your nearest **Common Service Centre (CSC)**
2. Carry your **Aadhaar card** and **registered mobile**
3. The CSC operator will update your details
4. Fee: **₹20** (CSC service charge)
5. Collect the updated printout

**Note:** Some details like name and date of birth are linked to Aadhaar and cannot be changed on E-Shram directly. Update them on Aadhaar first.

## Download E-Shram Card via CSC

If you don't have internet access or face technical issues:

1. Visit your nearest **CSC centre** (find at [locator.csccloud.in](https://locator.csccloud.in))
2. Provide your **Aadhaar number** or **registered mobile number**
3. The CSC operator will download and print your card
4. Fee: **₹20** approximately
5. Carry your **Aadhaar card** as identity proof

## Common Issues and Fixes

### "Mobile Number Not Registered"
- You may have registered with a different number
- Visit a CSC centre with Aadhaar to check your registered number

### "OTP Not Received"
- Wait 2-3 minutes before requesting again
- Check if your mobile has network signal
- Try during off-peak hours (early morning or late evening)

### "Card Download Failed"
- Try using **Google Chrome** browser
- Clear browser cache and cookies
- Disable pop-up blockers
- Try on a **desktop/laptop** instead of mobile

### "Details Wrong on Card"
- Details are pulled from **Aadhaar** — update Aadhaar first
- For occupation change, update on the E-Shram portal directly

## Important Tips

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### How do I download my E-Shram card online?
Visit register.eshram.gov.in, click "Already Registered", enter your mobile number, verify OTP, and click "Download UAN Card" to get your PDF card.

### What is the UAN number on E-Shram card?
UAN (Universal Account Number) is a unique 12-digit number assigned to every registered unorganised worker on the E-Shram portal. It serves as your identity for accessing government welfare schemes.

### Can I download E-Shram card without mobile number?
No, you need the registered mobile number for OTP verification. If your number has changed, visit a CSC (Common Service Centre) to update it first.

### Is E-Shram card download free?
Yes, downloading your E-Shram card from eshram.gov.in is completely free. CSC centres may charge ₹20 for assistance.

### What benefits do I get with E-Shram card?
E-Shram card holders get ₹2 lakh accidental insurance under PMSBY, access to PMJJBY, PM-SVANidhi, BOCW schemes, ration benefits, and priority in government welfare programmes.

### How do I update my mobile number on E-Shram?
Visit a CSC centre with your Aadhaar card. The CSC operator can update your mobile number after Aadhaar-based verification. This cannot be done online without the old registered number.
