## Lost Your Aadhaar? Don't Worry

If you've lost your physical Aadhaar card or forgotten your 12-digit Aadhaar number, you can easily retrieve it online through UIDAI's official portal. There are multiple recovery methods available depending on what information you have.

## Methods to Retrieve Lost Aadhaar Number

### Method 1: Using Registered Mobile Number or Email

This is the **fastest and most common** method.

1. Visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Retrieve Lost or Forgotten EID/UID"**
3. Select **"Aadhaar Number (UID)"**
4. Enter your **full name** (as in Aadhaar records)
5. Enter your **registered mobile number** or **email address**
6. Enter the **security code (captcha)**
7. Click **"Send OTP"**
8. Enter the OTP received on your registered mobile/email
9. Your **Aadhaar number** will be sent to your registered mobile/email

### Method 2: Retrieve Enrolment ID (EID)

If you need your Enrolment ID instead:

1. Visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Retrieve Lost or Forgotten EID/UID"**
3. Select **"Enrolment ID (EID)"**
4. Enter your **full name** and registered **mobile number/email**
5. Complete OTP verification
6. Your EID will be sent to your registered contact

### Method 3: Using Aadhaar Enrolment Slip

If you still have the **acknowledgement slip** from your original Aadhaar enrolment:
- Your **Enrolment ID (EID)** is printed on this slip
- Use this EID to [download your e-Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-card-download-online) from the UIDAI website

### Method 4: Visit an Aadhaar Enrolment Centre

If your mobile number is not registered or no longer active:

1. Visit the nearest **Aadhaar enrolment/update centre**
2. Carry a valid **photo ID proof** (Voter ID, PAN, Driving Licence, etc.)
3. Request Aadhaar retrieval through **biometric verification** (fingerprint/iris)
4. The operator will look up your Aadhaar using your biometrics
5. You can then [update your mobile number](/guide/aadhaar-update-mobile-email) and download e-Aadhaar

### Method 5: Using mAadhaar App

1. If you had previously registered on the **mAadhaar app**, your Aadhaar details are stored
2. Open the app and view your stored Aadhaar profile
3. If not registered, you'll need your Aadhaar number to set up the app

## How to Get a Replacement Aadhaar Card

Once you've retrieved your Aadhaar number, you have several options:

| Option | How | Cost |
|--------|-----|------|
| **Download e-Aadhaar** | From [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](/guide/aadhaar-card-download-online) | Free |
| **Order PVC Aadhaar card** | From myaadhaar portal → "Order Aadhaar PVC Card" | ₹50 |
| **Order Aadhaar letter** | From myaadhaar portal → "Order Aadhaar Reprint" | ₹50 |
| **Print at Aadhaar centre** | Visit an enrolment centre | ₹50 |

## Important Tips

1. **e-Aadhaar is legally equivalent** to the physical card — you don't necessarily need a replacement physical card
2. **Keep a digital copy** of your Aadhaar saved on your phone (mAadhaar app) and in DigiLocker
3. **Lock your Aadhaar biometrics** if you suspect misuse — use the [biometric lock feature](/guide/aadhaar-biometric-lock-unlock)
4. **Register your mobile number** with Aadhaar to make future retrieval easy
5. **Never share your Aadhaar number** publicly — use Masked Aadhaar or VID for privacy

## What to Do If You Suspect Aadhaar Misuse

If you've lost your Aadhaar and suspect it may be misused:
1. **Lock your biometrics** immediately at [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in)
2. **Generate a VID** (Virtual ID) and use it instead of your Aadhaar number
3. **Check Aadhaar authentication history** on the UIDAI portal to see recent usage
4. **File a complaint** at UIDAI helpline **1947** if you notice unauthorized use
5. **File a police complaint** if you suspect identity theft

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Can I retrieve my Aadhaar number without a registered mobile number?
Not online. You must visit the nearest Aadhaar enrolment centre with a valid photo ID. The operator will use biometric verification to locate your Aadhaar record.

### 2. Is there any charge to retrieve a lost Aadhaar number?
No, retrieving your Aadhaar number online is **completely free**. You only pay if you order a reprint (PVC card or letter) — ₹50.

### 3. How long does it take to retrieve lost Aadhaar?
Online retrieval is **instant** — you receive your Aadhaar number via SMS/email within seconds of OTP verification.

### 4. Can someone misuse my lost Aadhaar card?
Aadhaar alone cannot be used for financial transactions without biometric or OTP verification. However, it's good practice to lock your biometrics and generate a VID. See our [biometric lock guide](/guide/aadhaar-biometric-lock-unlock).

### 5. I lost both my Aadhaar and my phone. What should I do?
Visit the nearest Aadhaar enrolment centre with any valid government photo ID. Biometric verification (fingerprint/iris) will be used to retrieve your Aadhaar. Then update your mobile number.

### 6. Can I retrieve my child's Aadhaar (Baal Aadhaar)?
Yes, a parent or guardian can retrieve a child's Aadhaar using the same methods. For children under 5 (without biometrics), the parent's Aadhaar-linked mobile number is used.
