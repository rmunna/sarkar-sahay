## What is an Indian Visa?

An Indian visa is an official travel document that allows foreign nationals to enter, stay, and travel within India for a specific purpose and duration. The visa is issued by the Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) through Indian missions and consulates abroad, or as an electronic visa (e-Visa) through the online portal.

India offers multiple visa categories depending on the purpose of visit — tourism, business, employment, medical treatment, education, and more. With the introduction of the e-Visa system, the process has become significantly faster and more convenient.

## Types of Indian Visas

### 1. e-Visa (Electronic Visa)

The e-Visa is the most popular and convenient option for short visits. It is available in the following sub-categories:

- **e-Tourist Visa** — For sightseeing, casual visits, meeting friends/relatives
- **e-Business Visa** — For business meetings, trade, setting up ventures
- **e-Medical Visa** — For medical treatment at recognized hospitals
- **e-Medical Attendant Visa** — For accompanying a patient holding e-Medical Visa
- **e-Conference Visa** — For attending conferences hosted by Government or PSUs

e-Visa is available to citizens of **160+ countries** and allows entry through **28 designated airports** and **5 seaports**.

### 2. Tourist Visa

For longer tourism stays, a regular tourist visa is issued for **up to 10 years** (for US citizens) or 1-5 years depending on nationality. It allows multiple entries and each stay can be up to 180 days (90 days for some nationalities).

### 3. Business Visa

Issued to foreign nationals who wish to:
- Attend business meetings or establish a business
- Engage in trade or commerce
- Recruit workers or attend technical meetings
- Valid for up to **5 years** with multiple entries

### 4. Employment Visa

For foreigners who have a job offer from an Indian company. Requirements:
- Annual salary must be **above USD 25,000** (some exemptions apply)
- Valid employment contract from an Indian entity
- Duration: **Up to 5 years**, extendable
- Requires registration with FRRO within 14 days of arrival

### 5. Student Visa

For foreign students admitted to recognized Indian institutions. Valid for the duration of the academic course or 5 years, whichever is less.

### 6. Research Visa

For foreign nationals undertaking research in India. Requires prior approval from MEA and the relevant ministry.

### 7. Medical Visa

For foreigners seeking medical treatment in India. Requires a letter from the recognized hospital. Issued for up to 1 year or the treatment period.

### 8. Other Categories

- **Transit Visa** — For layovers (up to 72 hours)
- **Journalist Visa** — For media professionals
- **Diplomatic/Official Visa** — For government officials
- **Intern Visa** — For internship at Indian organizations

## How to Apply for e-Visa Online

### Step 1: Visit the Official Portal

Go to [indianvisaonline.gov.in](https://indianvisaonline.gov.in) and click on **"e-Visa Application"**.

### Step 2: Fill the Application Form

- Select visa type (e-Tourist, e-Business, etc.)
- Enter personal details — name, date of birth, nationality
- Provide passport details — number, issue date, expiry date
- Upload **passport-size photo** (dimensions: 2x2 inches, white background)
- Upload **passport bio page** scan (clear and legible)

### Step 3: Pay the Visa Fee

- Fee varies by nationality (USD 10 to USD 100)
- Payment via credit/debit card, UPI, or PayPal
- US, UK, and Canadian citizens typically pay USD 25-100

### Step 4: Receive ETA (Electronic Travel Authorization)

- Processing time: **72 hours** (usually within 24-48 hours)
- ETA is sent to your registered email
- Print the ETA and carry it while travelling

### Step 5: Arrive in India

- Present the ETA printout at the immigration counter
- Biometric data (fingerprint and photo) will be captured
- Immigration officer stamps the visa on your passport

## How to Apply for Regular Visa

### Step 1: Fill Online Application

Visit [indianvisaonline.gov.in](https://indianvisaonline.gov.in) and fill the **Regular Visa Application** form.

### Step 2: Print and Sign

Print the completed application form and sign it.

### Step 3: Visit Indian Embassy/Consulate

Submit the application with required documents at your nearest **Indian Mission or Visa Application Centre (IVAC/VFS)**.

### Step 4: Pay Fee and Collect

Pay the visa fee, submit biometrics, and collect your passport with visa within **5-10 working days**.

## Important Rules and Conditions

- **Registration:** Foreign nationals staying beyond **180 days** must register with the Foreigners Regional Registration Office (FRRO)
- **Employment visa holders** must register within **14 days** of arrival
- **No visa-on-arrival** for most nationalities (except e-Visa which is processed online before travel)
- **Yellow Fever certificate** required if travelling from endemic countries
- **Pakistan and Bangladesh nationals** have separate processes and restrictions

## FRRO Registration

If your visa requires FRRO registration:
1. Visit [indianfrro.gov.in](https://indianfrro.gov.in)
2. Fill Form C and upload documents
3. Book an appointment at the nearest FRRO/FRO office
4. Carry passport, visa, and address proof
5. Registration is free of cost

## Visa Extension

To extend your visa while in India:
- Apply online through the FRRO portal
- Extension is granted only for valid reasons (medical emergency, special circumstances)
- Tourist visas are generally **not extendable** except in emergencies
- Employment and student visas can be extended based on contract/admission validity

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I get a visa on arrival in India?
India does not offer visa on arrival for most countries. However, the e-Visa system works like a pre-approved electronic authorization. Citizens of Japan, South Korea, and UAE can get visa on arrival at select airports.

### How long does e-Visa processing take?
The standard processing time is **72 hours**, but most applications are processed within **24-48 hours**. Apply at least 4 days before travel.

### Can I work on a tourist visa in India?
No. Working on a tourist visa is strictly prohibited. You need an **Employment Visa** or **Business Visa** depending on the nature of work.

### What if my e-Visa application is rejected?
You can re-apply after correcting the issues. Common rejection reasons include unclear photos, passport nearing expiry, or incomplete details. No refund is given for rejected applications.

### Is travel insurance mandatory for Indian visa?
Travel insurance is not mandatory but strongly recommended. Some visa categories may require proof of adequate financial means.
