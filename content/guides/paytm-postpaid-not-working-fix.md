# Paytm Postpaid Not Working? Fix Credit Issues

Paytm Postpaid (now integrated with Paytm's lending partners) is a "buy now, pay later" service that gives you a monthly credit limit. If it's not working, showing errors, or has been deactivated, this guide will help you fix it.

## What is Paytm Postpaid?

Paytm Postpaid gives you a credit limit (typically ₹500 to ₹60,000) to make purchases, pay bills, and recharge. You use the credit during the month and pay it back by the due date (usually 7th of the next month). It's offered in partnership with lending NBFCs.

---

## Fix 1: Paytm Postpaid Not Showing / Not Eligible

If you can't see Paytm Postpaid or get a "Not Eligible" message:

### Eligibility Requirements
- **Age:** 18 years or older
- **KYC:** Full KYC must be completed on Paytm
- **Credit profile:** Based on your spending history, repayment behavior, and credit score
- **Active Paytm account:** Regular usage of Paytm helps

### How to Fix
1. **Complete Full KYC** — Postpaid requires full KYC. See our [KYC fix guide](/guide/paytm-kyc-not-working-fix).
2. **Use Paytm regularly** — make payments, recharges, and bill payments through Paytm to build a usage history.
3. **Check your credit score** — a very low CIBIL score (below 600) may affect eligibility.
4. **Wait and retry** — eligibility is assessed periodically. Check back after a few weeks of regular Paytm usage.
5. **Update the app** — the Postpaid option may not appear on older app versions.

**Note:** Paytm Postpaid eligibility is decided by lending partners and is not guaranteed. There's no way to force-activate it.

## Fix 2: Credit Limit Too Low or Reduced

If your Postpaid credit limit is too low or has been reduced:

### Why Credit Limit Decreases
- Missed or late payments on Postpaid dues
- Reduced Paytm usage
- Changes in your credit profile
- Lending partner's risk assessment

### How to Increase Credit Limit
1. **Pay dues on time** — consistently pay before the due date for 3-6 months.
2. **Use Paytm Postpaid regularly** — frequent usage signals responsible credit behavior.
3. **Maintain a good credit score** — pay all loans and credit card EMIs on time.
4. **Don't exhaust the full limit** — using 100% of your limit every month can be seen negatively.
5. Credit limit increases are **automatic** — you cannot manually request one.

## Fix 3: Payment Overdue — Paytm Postpaid Blocked

If you missed the payment due date:

### Consequences of Late Payment
| Days Overdue | Impact |
|---|---|
| 1-7 days | Late fee charged. Reminder notifications. |
| 7-30 days | Postpaid may be temporarily blocked. |
| 30+ days | Account may be deactivated. Negative impact on credit score. |
| 60+ days | Sent to collection. Reported to credit bureaus. |

### How to Fix
1. Open Paytm → go to **Paytm Postpaid** section.
2. Tap **"Pay Now"** or **"Pay Dues"**.
3. Pay the **full outstanding amount** including any late fees.
4. Payment options: UPI, debit card, net banking, or Paytm wallet.
5. After payment, your Postpaid access is usually **restored within 24-48 hours**.
6. If it's still blocked after payment, contact Paytm support.

**Important:** Late payments are reported to credit bureaus (CIBIL, Experian, etc.) and can affect your credit score for future loans and credit cards.

## Fix 4: Paytm Postpaid Deactivated

If Postpaid has been deactivated completely:

### Common Reasons
- Repeated late payments
- Fraudulent activity detected
- You requested deactivation
- Lending partner discontinued your account
- KYC expired or revoked

### Reactivation Steps
1. Open Paytm → check if there's a **"Reactivate Postpaid"** option.
2. If available, follow the on-screen steps — you may need to verify your identity again.
3. If not available:
   - Clear all pending dues first.
   - Complete/re-verify KYC.
   - Wait 30-90 days for re-assessment.
   - Contact Paytm support: **Help & Support** → search "Postpaid" → raise a ticket.
4. **If you deactivated it yourself**, you can reactivate from: Paytm → **Paytm Postpaid** → **Activate**.

**Note:** Reactivation is not guaranteed. It depends on the lending partner's reassessment.

## Fix 5: Paytm Postpaid Payment Failed at Merchant

If Postpaid isn't working at a specific merchant:

1. **Not all merchants accept Postpaid** — it works at select online and offline merchants.
2. **Check your available credit** — if you've exhausted your limit, the payment will fail.
3. **Check if Postpaid is selected** as the payment method during checkout.
4. **Some categories are restricted** — certain products/services (like jewellery, forex) may not be payable via Postpaid.
5. **Try a smaller amount** — the merchant may have a minimum/maximum limit for Postpaid payments.

## Important Tips

- Always pay Postpaid dues by the 7th of the month to avoid late fees and credit score impact.
- Don't overspend on Postpaid — treat it like a credit card with responsibility.
- Late Postpaid payments affect your CIBIL score — this impacts future loan and credit card applications.
- If you're not using Postpaid, deactivate it to avoid accidental charges.
- Paytm Postpaid interest rates and fees are set by the lending partner, not Paytm.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Is Paytm Postpaid a loan?
Technically, yes. It's a short-term credit facility offered by Paytm's lending partners (NBFCs). Usage is reported to credit bureaus.

### Q2: Does Paytm Postpaid affect my CIBIL score?
Yes. Timely payments can improve your credit score, while late payments will negatively impact it.

### Q3: What is the late fee for Paytm Postpaid?
Late fees vary but typically range from ₹50 to ₹500 depending on the overdue amount. Check your Postpaid section for exact charges.

### Q4: Can I pay Paytm Postpaid dues in EMI?
Some users may see an EMI option for larger dues. Check the payment screen for available options.

### Q5: How do I deactivate Paytm Postpaid?
Open Paytm → Paytm Postpaid → Settings → Deactivate. Clear all pending dues before deactivating.

### Q6: I never activated Postpaid but see charges. What happened?
You may have activated it accidentally during a payment. Check your Paytm Postpaid section, pay any dues, and deactivate if not needed.
