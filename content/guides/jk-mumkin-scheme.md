# J&K Mumkin Scheme Livelihood Scheme for Youth

## What is the J&K Mumkin Scheme?

The **Mumkin – Livelihood Generation Scheme** is a government initiative by the **Jammu & Kashmir administration** under the **Mission Youth** programme. It helps unemployed youth purchase small commercial vehicles at subsidized rates so they can earn a livelihood in the transport sector.

Under this scheme, eligible youth receive a **subsidy of up to ₹80,000** (or 10% of the vehicle's on-road price, whichever is less) from Mission Youth. Additionally, the **vehicle manufacturer provides a matching subsidy** of the same amount. This means beneficiaries can get a total benefit of up to ₹1.60 lakh on the purchase of a new small commercial vehicle.

The remaining amount is financed through a **bank loan**, making vehicle ownership accessible even for youth with limited savings.

## Who is Eligible for the Mumkin Scheme?

To apply for the Mumkin scheme, you must meet **all** of the following criteria:

1. **Domicile:** You must be a permanent resident (domicile) of Jammu & Kashmir.
2. **Age:** You must be between **18 and 35 years** old.
3. **Employment Status:** You must be **unemployed** at the time of application.
4. **Driving Licence:** You must hold a valid **commercial driving licence**. If you plan to hire a driver to operate the vehicle, you must submit an affidavit along with the driver's commercial driving licence.
5. **No Previous Benefits:** You must not have received financial assistance or subsidies from any other government self-employment scheme. Benefits under this scheme can be availed **only once**.

## Documents Required

Keep these documents ready before applying:

1. **Passport-size Photograph** — Recent, clear photograph of the applicant
2. **Domicile Certificate** — Proof of J&K residency (or Ration Card as alternative proof)
3. **Date of Birth Certificate** — For age verification
4. **Commercial Driving Licence** — Of the applicant
5. **Driver's Driving Licence** — Required only if hiring another driver to operate the vehicle
6. **Affidavit** — Declaration regarding vehicle usage and compliance with scheme rules
7. **Unemployment Certificate** — Proof that you are currently unemployed
8. **Aadhaar Card** — For identity verification
9. **Bank Account Details** — For loan processing and subsidy credit

> **Tip:** All documents should be scanned clearly in PDF or JPEG format for online upload.

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Online Process

1. **Visit the Official Portal:** Go to [https://www.jk.gov.in/jkeservices/home](https://www.jk.gov.in/jkeservices/home).
2. **Register as a New Citizen:** Click on "Citizen Registration." Fill in your personal details, address, and create login credentials. Submit the form to create your account.
3. **Log In:** Use your registered username and password to log in.
4. **Navigate to E-Services:** Go to the **E-Services** section and select **Finance / Mission Youth**.
5. **Select Mumkin Scheme:** Choose the Mumkin – Livelihood Generation Scheme from the available options.
6. **Fill the Application Form:** Enter your personal details, vehicle preference, and other required information.
7. **Upload Documents:** Attach scanned copies of all required documents listed above.
8. **Review and Submit:** Double-check all details and submit your application.
9. **Note Your Application Number:** Save the application reference number for tracking.

### Offline Process

1. **Visit the District Mission Youth Office:** Go to the Mission Youth office in your district.
2. **Collect the Application Form:** Ask for the Mumkin scheme application form.
3. **Fill the Form:** Complete all sections with accurate details.
4. **Attach Documents:** Attach photocopies of all required documents (carry originals for verification).
5. **Submit:** Hand over the completed form with documents to the office.
6. **Collect Acknowledgement:** Get a receipt or acknowledgement slip for your records.

### After Submission

- Your application will be **verified** by the district-level committee.
- If approved, you will be connected with a **bank for loan processing**.
- After the bank approves the loan, the **subsidy amount** is applied to the vehicle purchase.
- You can then **purchase the vehicle** from an authorized dealer.

## Benefits and Subsidy Details

| Component | Amount |
|-----------|--------|
| Mission Youth Subsidy | Up to ₹80,000 or 10% of on-road price (whichever is less) |
| Manufacturer Subsidy | Matching amount (equal to Mission Youth's share) |
| **Total Benefit** | **Up to ₹1,60,000** |
| Remaining Cost | Covered through bank loan |

## Processing Time

- **Application Review:** Typically 2–4 weeks after submission *(may vary by district)*
- **Bank Loan Processing:** Additional 2–4 weeks after approval
- **Total Time:** Expect approximately **1–2 months** from application to vehicle delivery

> ⚠️ Processing times are approximate and may vary depending on document verification and bank procedures.

## Fees

There is **no application fee** to apply for the Mumkin scheme. However, standard bank loan processing charges may apply as per the financing bank's policies.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. What is the J&K Mumkin Scheme?

The Mumkin scheme is a livelihood generation programme by the J&K government under Mission Youth. It provides subsidies to unemployed youth for purchasing small commercial vehicles to start a transport business.

### 2. How much subsidy is given under the Mumkin Scheme?

Beneficiaries receive up to **₹80,000 from Mission Youth** (or 10% of the vehicle's on-road price, whichever is less). The vehicle manufacturer provides an **equal matching subsidy**, bringing the total benefit to up to ₹1,60,000.

### 3. Who can apply for the Mumkin Scheme?

Unemployed youth aged **18–35 years** who are **domiciles of Jammu & Kashmir** and hold a **commercial driving licence** can apply. You must not have availed benefits from any other government self-employment scheme.

### 4. Can I apply if I don't have a commercial driving licence?

No. A commercial driving licence is mandatory. However, if you plan to hire a driver, you can submit the driver's commercial licence along with an affidavit.

### 5. Can I apply for the Mumkin Scheme more than once?

No. The benefits under this scheme can be availed **only once** per person.

### 6. Is there any application fee?

No. There is no fee to apply for the Mumkin scheme.

### 7. What is the official website to apply?

You can apply through the J&K e-Services portal at [https://www.jk.gov.in/jkeservices/home](https://www.jk.gov.in/jkeservices/home). Scheme details are also available at [https://missionyouth.jk.gov.in/Schemes](https://missionyouth.jk.gov.in/Schemes).

### 8. Can someone from outside J&K apply?

No. Only permanent residents (domiciles) of Jammu & Kashmir are eligible.
