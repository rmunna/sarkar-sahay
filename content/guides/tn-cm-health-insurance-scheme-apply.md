## What is Tamil Nadu CM Health Insurance Scheme?

The **Chief Minister's Comprehensive Health Insurance Scheme (CMCHIS)** is Tamil Nadu government's flagship health insurance programme that provides **₹5 lakh cashless treatment per year** to families with an annual income of ₹72,000 or less. The scheme covers tertiary and secondary care at over 1,000 government and empanelled private hospitals across Tamil Nadu.

---

## Key Benefits

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Annual coverage | ₹5,00,000 per family per year |
| Premium | Free (government pays 100%) |
| Cashless | Yes — no advance payment at empanelled hospitals |
| Pre-existing diseases | Covered from Day 1 |
| Family unit | Entire family (husband, wife, dependent children, dependent parents) |
| Hospital network | 1,100+ government and private hospitals in TN |

---

## Who is Eligible?

**You ARE eligible if:**
- You have an **annual family income ≤ ₹72,000** (check current limit at cmchistn.com)
- You have a **Tamil Nadu government-issued Smart Family Card** (ration card) — the family card must show income below the threshold

> The scheme auto-enrolls families based on the Family Card database. The eligibility check is done through your **Smart Family Card (ration card)** number.

---

## Documents Required

1. **Smart Family Card** (Tamil Nadu ration card) — mandatory proof
2. **Aadhaar Card** — for identity verification
3. **Hospital admission documents** (at the time of hospitalization)

---

## How to Use the Scheme at a Hospital

### Step 1 — Visit Empanelled Hospital

Go to any **CMCHIS-empanelled hospital** (government or private). Look for the "CM Health Insurance Scheme" board.

### Step 2 — Go to the CMCHIS Help Desk

In the hospital, find the **CMCHIS Help Desk** (all empanelled hospitals must have one). Show:
- Your Tamil Nadu Smart Family Card
- Aadhaar card

### Step 3 — Eligibility Verification

The help desk verifies your family's eligibility in real-time using the Family Card number.

### Step 4 — Cashless Admission

If eligible, you are admitted **cashlessly** for covered procedures. No deposit required. The hospital claims from the government directly.

---

## Procedures Covered

CMCHIS covers 1,027+ medical and surgical procedures including:
- Open heart surgery, angioplasty
- Cancer treatment (chemo, radio, surgery)
- Kidney transplant, dialysis
- Brain surgery, spine surgery
- Bone marrow transplant
- Burn treatment
- Hip/knee replacement
- Cataract surgery
- Neonatal ICU (newborn intensive care)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. I have a TN ration card but my income is ₹1 lakh/year. Am I eligible?**

Current income threshold is ₹72,000/year for CMCHIS. If your income exceeds this, you may not be eligible. However, TN is expanding the scheme in phases — check cmchistn.com for current limits.

**2. I need dialysis 3 times a week. Is it covered?**

Yes. Kidney dialysis is covered under CMCHIS. Ongoing treatments may require periodic renewal authorizations at the empanelled hospital's help desk.

**3. Can I use this scheme in another state (e.g., Apollo Chennai vs Apollo Delhi)?**

CMCHIS is valid only at hospitals in **Tamil Nadu**. Hospitals outside TN are not in the network.

**4. My family card has 6 members. Is there a per-person limit?**

The ₹5 lakh limit is **per family per year** — shared across all members. If one member uses ₹3 lakh, the remaining family has ₹2 lakh for the year.

**5. My husband died last year. Am I still covered under the family card?**

The widow and dependent children continue to be eligible under the same family card. Contact your nearest CMCHIS facilitation centre if any updates are needed in the system.
