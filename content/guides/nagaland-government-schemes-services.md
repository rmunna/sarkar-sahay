# Nagaland Government Schemes and Services – Complete Guide

Nagaland, one of India's northeastern hill states, is home to 17 major tribes and over two million people. Governed by the NDPP-BJP alliance under Chief Minister Neiphiu Rio, the state has introduced several progressive schemes focused on community-driven development, healthcare, startup support, and tribal welfare. This comprehensive guide covers every major Nagaland government scheme and service you need to know about.

> **Quick Tip:** Most Nagaland government services are now accessible through the [e-District portal](https://edistrict.nagaland.gov.in). Keep your Aadhaar, tribal certificates, and residential proof ready before applying.

## Health Schemes

### Chief Minister's Health Insurance Scheme (CMHIS)

CMHIS is Nagaland's flagship health insurance programme providing cashless treatment to families across the state. The scheme covers hospitalisation expenses up to ₹5 lakh per family per year at empanelled hospitals across Nagaland and referral hospitals outside the state.

**Key Features:**
- Cashless treatment at government and empanelled private hospitals
- Coverage of up to ₹5 lakh per family annually
- Covers pre-existing diseases from day one
- No age limit for enrolment
- Integrated with Ayushman Bharat – Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB-PMJAY)

**Eligibility:** All families holding a valid ration card or listed under the SECC database in Nagaland.

**How to Enrol:** Visit the nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) or District Hospital with Aadhaar, ration card, and family details. You can also check eligibility on the [CMHIS portal](https://cmhis.nagaland.gov.in).

> Related: [Ayushman Bharat Guide](/guide/ayushman-bharat-pmjay-guide) for understanding the central health insurance component.

## Startup and Entrepreneurship

### Nagaland Beehive Initiative

The Beehive Initiative is Nagaland's startup and innovation programme designed to nurture entrepreneurship among the state's youth. Operating under the Department of Industries & Commerce, it provides mentorship, funding support, co-working spaces, and market access to aspiring entrepreneurs.

**Key Features:**
- Incubation support for early-stage startups
- Seed funding and linkages to angel investors
- Co-working spaces in Kohima and Dimapur
- Business mentorship and skills workshops
- Annual startup challenges and pitch competitions
- Sector focus on agriculture, tourism, handicrafts, and technology

**Eligibility:** Nagaland residents aged 18–45 with a viable business idea or early-stage venture.

**How to Apply:** Register on the [Beehive portal](https://beehive.nagaland.gov.in) and submit your business plan. Shortlisted candidates are invited for pitching rounds.

## Community-Driven Governance

### Communitisation of Public Institutions

Nagaland pioneered the **Communitisation Act, 2002** – a unique model of governance where local communities take ownership and management responsibility of public institutions like schools, health centres, water supply systems, and electricity infrastructure.

**How It Works:**
- Village Councils and community bodies form management committees
- Government provides funding; communities manage day-to-day operations
- Covers education, health, water supply, power, and rural roads
- Improves accountability and service delivery at the grassroots level

**Impact:** Over 5,000 public institutions across Nagaland operate under communitisation, significantly improving attendance in schools and functionality of rural health centres.

This model has been studied and appreciated nationally as an example of participatory governance.

## Digital Services and Certificates

### e-District Nagaland

The e-District portal enables citizens to apply for essential government certificates and services online, reducing the need for physical visits to government offices.

**Services Available:**
- Income Certificate
- Scheduled Tribe (ST) Certificate
- Residential/Domicile Certificate
- Character Certificate
- Birth and Death Certificates
- Permits and NOCs
- Land-related services

**How to Apply:**
1. Visit [edistrict.nagaland.gov.in](https://edistrict.nagaland.gov.in)
2. Register with your mobile number and Aadhaar
3. Select the required certificate/service
4. Upload supporting documents
5. Pay applicable fees online
6. Track application status via the portal

## Government Jobs and Examinations

### Nagaland Public Service Commission (NPSC)

NPSC conducts recruitment examinations for state civil services and other government positions in Nagaland.

**Major Exams:**
- Nagaland Civil Services (NCS) Combined Examination
- Nagaland Police Service (NPS) Examination
- Technical and Non-Technical posts in various departments
- Departmental Examinations

**How to Apply:**
1. Visit [npsc.nagaland.gov.in](https://npsc.nagaland.gov.in)
2. Check current notifications and vacancies
3. Register and fill the online application form
4. Upload photograph, signature, and certificates
5. Pay the application fee online
6. Download admit card when released

**Tip:** Candidates from Scheduled Tribes are eligible for age relaxation and fee concessions as per government norms.

## Youth Development

### Nagaland Youth Mission

The Youth Mission addresses unemployment and skill gaps among Nagaland's young population through training programmes, career counselling, and placement support.

**Key Programmes:**
- Vocational training in IT, hospitality, healthcare, and construction trades
- Career guidance and counselling centres in every district
- Placement linkages with private sector employers
- Leadership and life-skills development workshops
- Support for youth-led community development projects

**Eligibility:** Nagaland youth aged 15–35 years. Priority given to school dropouts and unemployed graduates.

## Rural Livelihoods

### Nagaland State Rural Livelihood Mission (NSRLM)

NSRLM, operating under the National Rural Livelihoods Mission (NRLM) framework, aims to reduce rural poverty by building strong community institutions and enabling sustainable livelihoods.

**Key Activities:**
- Formation of Self-Help Groups (SHGs) across all districts
- Revolving fund and community investment fund for SHGs
- Farm and non-farm livelihood promotion
- Financial literacy and bank linkage programmes
- Market access support for rural producers
- Special focus on women-led enterprises

**Coverage:** All 12 districts of Nagaland with special focus on remote tribal villages.

## Agriculture and Horticulture

### Organic Farming and Horticulture Promotion

Nagaland's agriculture is predominantly organic by default, and the state government actively promotes this through dedicated schemes.

**Key Initiatives:**
- **Mission Organic Value Chain Development for North Eastern Region (MOVCDNER):** Supports organic certification and market linkages for Naga farmers
- **Horticulture Mission:** Promotes cultivation of Naga King Chilli (Bhut Jolokia), kiwi, passion fruit, oranges, and large cardamom
- **Technology Mission on Horticulture:** Provides subsidies on seeds, tools, drip irrigation, and cold storage
- **Organic Certification Support:** Financial assistance for PGS and third-party organic certification

**Benefits:**
- Subsidies up to 50-90% on cultivation inputs
- Free training on organic farming techniques
- Support for farmer producer organisations (FPOs)
- Market access through organic bazaars and e-commerce platforms

### Nagaland Bamboo Mission

Nagaland has vast bamboo resources, and the Bamboo Mission promotes bamboo cultivation, processing, and value-added product development.

**Key Features:**
- Bamboo plantation support on degraded and community lands
- Training in bamboo handicraft, furniture, and construction
- Support for setting up bamboo processing units
- Market linkage for bamboo products domestically and internationally
- Aligned with the National Bamboo Mission

**Eligibility:** Farmers, SHGs, cooperatives, and entrepreneurs in Nagaland.

## Tribal Welfare

Nagaland's population is over 85% Scheduled Tribe, making tribal welfare central to state governance.

**Key Schemes and Benefits:**
- **Tribal Welfare Scholarships:** Pre-matric and post-matric scholarships for ST students funded by the Ministry of Tribal Affairs
- **ST Certificate Services:** Available through e-District for accessing reservations in education and employment
- **Tribal Land Protection:** Customary land laws protect tribal land rights through Village Councils
- **Cultural Preservation Grants:** Funding for preservation of tribal languages, festivals (like Hornbill Festival), and traditional arts
- **Hostel and Residential School Support:** Government-funded hostels for tribal students in urban centres

## Skill Development

### Skill Development Schemes

Multiple skill-building programmes operate in Nagaland to improve employability:

- **Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY) in Nagaland:** Short-term skill training across 30+ sectors through registered training centres
- **NIELIT Kohima:** IT and electronics skill training with government certification
- **State Institute of Capacity Building:** Training for government employees and village council leaders
- **Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Grameen Kaushalya Yojana (DDU-GKY):** Placement-linked skill training for rural youth

## How to Apply for Nagaland Government Schemes

Most Nagaland government schemes follow a similar application process:

1. **Check Eligibility:** Visit the relevant department website or portal listed above
2. **Gather Documents:** Aadhaar card, tribal/ST certificate, ration card, income certificate, residential proof, passport-size photographs
3. **Apply Online:** Use the e-District portal or scheme-specific website
4. **Offline Option:** Visit the District Commissioner's office, Block Development Office, or designated department office
5. **Track Status:** Use the application reference number on the respective portal
6. **Grievance Redressal:** Contact the CM's Grievance Cell or use the [CPGRAMS portal](https://pgportal.gov.in) for unresolved issues

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for CMHIS in Nagaland?
All families listed in the SECC database or holding a valid ration card in Nagaland are eligible. The scheme provides cashless treatment up to ₹5 lakh per year per family at empanelled hospitals.

### How do I get an ST Certificate in Nagaland?
Apply through the [e-District Nagaland portal](https://edistrict.nagaland.gov.in) with your Aadhaar, family records, and Village Council verification. The certificate is typically issued within 15-30 days.

### What is communitisation in Nagaland?
Communitisation is Nagaland's unique governance model where local communities manage public institutions like schools and health centres. It was established through the Communitisation Act of 2002 and covers over 5,000 institutions.

### How can I register my startup under the Beehive Initiative?
Visit [beehive.nagaland.gov.in](https://beehive.nagaland.gov.in), create an account, and submit your business idea or plan. Selected applicants receive incubation support, mentorship, and funding assistance.

### What exams does NPSC conduct?
NPSC conducts the Nagaland Civil Services (NCS) Combined Examination, Nagaland Police Service exam, and recruitment tests for various technical and non-technical government posts. Check [npsc.nagaland.gov.in](https://npsc.nagaland.gov.in) for current notifications.

### Are there organic farming subsidies in Nagaland?
Yes. Under MOVCDNER and the Horticulture Mission, Nagaland farmers can receive 50-90% subsidies on organic farming inputs, certification costs, and market linkage support.

### How do I apply for skill development training in Nagaland?
Register at your nearest PMKVY training centre or NIELIT Kohima for IT-related courses. DDU-GKY centres offer placement-linked training for rural youth. Contact the District Skill Development Committee for local options.
