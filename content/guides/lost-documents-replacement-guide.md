# How to Get Duplicate Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID — Lost Document Replacement Guide

**Lost your important documents? Take a deep breath.** Almost every government document in India can be replaced online — often for free and within minutes. This guide covers step-by-step replacement for Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, Driving License, Passport, Ration Card, and Birth/Death Certificate.

> 💡 **Quick Tip:** Before anything else, check [DigiLocker](/guides/digilocker-guide) — your lost document might already be available there as a legally valid digital copy!

---

## 1. Lost Aadhaar Card — How to Get a Replacement

**Good news:** Your Aadhaar data is safe with UIDAI. You can get a digital or physical copy anytime.

### Option A: Download e-Aadhaar Online (Free)

This is the fastest option — takes just 2 minutes.

1. Visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in)
2. Log in with your Aadhaar number + OTP on registered mobile
3. Click **"Download Aadhaar"**
4. Choose masked or full Aadhaar
5. Download the PDF (password: first 4 letters of name in CAPS + birth year, e.g., RAJA1990)

**Cost:** ₹0 (Free)
**Time:** Instant

👉 **Detailed steps:** [How to Download e-Aadhaar Card Online](/guides/download-e-aadhaar-card-online)

### Option B: Order Aadhaar PVC Card (₹50)

Want a physical card? Order the official PVC (plastic) Aadhaar card:

1. Go to [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in)
2. Log in and select **"Order Aadhaar PVC Card"**
3. Pay ₹50 online
4. Card delivered to your registered address in 15–20 days

**Cost:** ₹50
**Time:** 15–20 days delivery

👉 **Full guide:** [How to Order Aadhaar PVC Card Online](/guides/aadhaar-pvc-card-order-online)

### Option C: Use mAadhaar App

Download the **mAadhaar** app (official UIDAI app) on your phone. It stores a digital copy of your Aadhaar that you can show anywhere.

- Available on Android and iOS
- Set up using Aadhaar number + OTP
- Works as valid ID proof

### What If Your Mobile Number is Not Linked?

If OTP doesn't come (mobile not linked to Aadhaar):
- Visit the nearest **Aadhaar Enrolment Centre** with any other ID proof
- Request Aadhaar reprint there

👉 **Related:** [How to Change Mobile Number in Aadhaar](/guides/change-mobile-number-in-aadhaar-card)

## 2. Lost PAN Card — How to Get a Duplicate

Your PAN number never changes. You just need a new copy of the card.

### Option A: Download e-PAN (Free)

1. Visit [incometax.gov.in](https://www.incometax.gov.in)
2. Click **"Instant e-PAN"** → **"Check Status / Download e-PAN"**
3. Enter your Aadhaar number → Verify with OTP
4. Download e-PAN PDF instantly

**Cost:** ₹0 (Free)
**Time:** Instant

👉 **Step-by-step:** [How to Download e-PAN Card Online](/guides/download-e-pan-card-online)

### Option B: Reprint Physical PAN Card

**Through Protean (NSDL):**
1. Go to [onlineservices.nsdl.com](https://www.onlineservices.nsdl.com)
2. Select **"Reprint of PAN Card"**
3. Enter PAN number and Aadhaar
4. Pay **₹50** (e-PAN) or **₹50 + delivery** for physical card
5. Physical card dispatched within 15–20 days

**Through UTIITSL:**
1. Visit [pan.utiitsl.com](https://pan.utiitsl.com)
2. Apply for duplicate/reprint
3. Fee: approximately **₹50 + GST** for domestic delivery

**Cost:** ~₹50–107 depending on card type and delivery
**Time:** 15–20 working days

👉 **Also useful:** [PAN Card Status Check](/guides/pan-card-status-check) | [PAN Card Correction](/guides/pan-card-correction-online)

### Don't Know Your PAN Number?

- Go to [incometax.gov.in](https://www.incometax.gov.in) → **"Know Your PAN"**
- Enter name, date of birth, and mobile number
- Your PAN will be displayed

## 3. Lost Voter ID (EPIC) — How to Get a Duplicate

### Option A: Download e-EPIC (Instant & Free)

1. Visit [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in) (National Voters Service Portal)
2. Log in or register with your mobile number
3. Go to **"Download e-EPIC"**
4. Your digital Voter ID will be downloaded as PDF

**Cost:** ₹0 (Free)
**Time:** Instant

### Option B: Apply for Duplicate Voter ID

If you need a physical replacement:

1. Visit [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in)
2. Go to **"Forms"** → Select **Form 001** (formerly Form 6)
3. Select reason: **"Replacement of lost/damaged EPIC"**
4. Upload passport photo and supporting documents
5. Submit and note the reference number

**Documents needed:**
- Passport-size photo
- Any address proof
- FIR copy (if stolen, recommended but not always mandatory)

**Cost:** Free
**Time:** 15–30 days

👉 **Detailed guide:** [How to Apply for Voter ID Card Online](/guides/voter-id-card-apply-online) | [Voter ID Correction](/guides/voter-id-correction-online)

## 4. Lost Driving License — How to Get a Duplicate

### Option A: Download from DigiLocker (Instant)

If your DL was issued after digitisation:
1. Log in to [digilocker.gov.in](https://www.digilocker.gov.in)
2. Search for **"Driving License"** under Ministry of Road Transport
3. Enter your DL number → Fetch document
4. Download the digitally signed copy

This is legally valid as original under IT Act.

👉 **Full guide:** [DigiLocker Guide](/guides/digilocker-guide)

### Option B: Apply for Duplicate DL via Parivahan

1. Visit [parivahan.gov.in](https://parivahan.gov.in) → **Sarathi** portal
2. Select your state → Click **"Duplicate DL"**
3. Fill in your DL number and details
4. Upload documents:
   - Passport photo
   - Address proof
   - FIR copy (for lost DL) or affidavit
5. Pay fee online (~₹200–600, varies by state)
6. Book appointment if required at RTO
7. New DL issued within 7–30 days

**Cost:** ₹200–600 (varies by state)
**Time:** 7–30 days

👉 **Related:** [How to Apply for Driving License Online](/guides/driving-license-apply-online) | [Driving License Renewal](/guides/driving-license-renewal-online)

## 5. Lost Passport — How to Get a Replacement

Losing a passport is more serious — it requires an FIR and a fresh application.

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **File an FIR** at your nearest police station reporting the loss. Get a copy of the FIR.

2. **Apply fresh on Passport Seva:**
   - Visit [passportindia.gov.in](https://www.passportindia.gov.in)
   - Register/login → Select **"Fresh Passport"** (not re-issue)
   - In the form, mention that previous passport was lost
   - Upload FIR copy, old passport details (if you remember the number)

3. **Book appointment** at the nearest Passport Seva Kendra (PSK)

4. **Visit PSK** with:
   - Printed application form
   - FIR copy (original)
   - Address & identity proof
   - Old passport photocopy (if available)

5. **Police verification** will be conducted (mandatory for lost passports)

6. **New passport issued** within 30–45 days after police verification

**Cost:** ₹1,500 (36 pages) / ₹2,000 (60 pages) — same as fresh passport
**Time:** 30–45 days (longer due to police verification)

> ⚠️ **Important:** Keep a photocopy of your passport's first and last page separately. Also save it in DigiLocker.

👉 **Detailed guide:** [How to Apply for Passport Online](/guides/passport-apply-online) | [Track Passport Status](/guides/passport-application-status-track) | [Passport Renewal](/guides/passport-renewal-online-india)

## 6. Lost Ration Card — How to Get a Duplicate

Ration card replacement is handled at the **state level**, so the process varies.

### General Process

1. Visit your state's food & civil supplies portal (or [nfsa.gov.in](https://nfsa.gov.in) for links)
2. Look for **"Duplicate Ration Card"** or **"Issue of Duplicate"** option
3. Fill in the application with:
   - Old ration card number (if known)
   - Head of family details
   - FIR copy or affidavit for lost card
4. Upload documents and submit
5. New card issued within 15–30 days

**Some states allow online application, others require visiting the local Food & Supply office (Tehsildar/Block office).**

**Cost:** ₹0–50 (varies by state)
**Time:** 15–30 days

## 7. Lost Birth or Death Certificate — How to Get a Duplicate

### Online (Available in Many States)

1. Visit [crsorgi.gov.in](https://crsorgi.gov.in) (Civil Registration System)
2. Select your state and search for your record by name and date
3. If found, apply for a duplicate certificate online
4. Pay the fee and download or receive by post

### Offline Process

1. Visit the **Municipal Corporation / Nagar Palika / Gram Panchayat** where the birth/death was registered
2. Submit an application for duplicate certificate
3. Provide:
   - Approximate date of birth/death
   - Hospital name (if applicable)
   - Parent/family details
   - Affidavit stating the certificate was lost
4. Fee: ₹10–100 depending on municipality
5. Certificate issued within 7–15 days

**Cost:** ₹10–100
**Time:** 7–15 days

👉 **Detailed guides:** [Birth Certificate Online](/guides/birth-certificate-apply-online) | [Death Certificate Online](/guides/death-certificate-apply-online)

## 8. General Tips — Protect Yourself from Future Loss

### ✅ Use DigiLocker as Your Digital Backup

[DigiLocker](https://www.digilocker.gov.in) stores legally valid digital copies of your documents. Set it up **today**:

- Aadhaar, PAN, Driving License, Vehicle RC, Marksheets — all available
- Documents fetched directly from issuing authorities
- Accepted everywhere as original under IT Act 2000

👉 **Complete setup guide:** [How to Use DigiLocker](/guides/digilocker-guide)

### ✅ Keep Photocopies & Scans

- Take photos of all important documents and save them in a secure cloud folder (Google Drive, iCloud)
- Keep one set of photocopies at home and one with a trusted family member
- Note down important numbers: Aadhaar, PAN, DL, Passport number

### ✅ File FIR for Identity Theft Protection

If your documents were **stolen** (not just lost):

1. File an **FIR** at the nearest police station immediately
2. This protects you from misuse of your identity
3. You can also file an **online FIR** in many states through the state police website
4. Keep the FIR copy — you'll need it for all replacement applications

### ✅ Link Your Mobile Number

Make sure your **current mobile number** is linked to:
- Aadhaar (for OTP-based services)
- PAN (via Income Tax portal)
- Voter ID (via NVSP)
- Bank accounts

This ensures you can access replacements online without visiting offices.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is e-Aadhaar valid as original Aadhaar?
**Yes.** e-Aadhaar downloaded from UIDAI is equally valid as the physical Aadhaar card as per UIDAI guidelines.

### Can I get a duplicate PAN card with a different number?
**No.** Your PAN number stays the same for life. A duplicate card will have the same PAN number with a new physical/digital copy.

### Do I need an FIR to get duplicate documents?
For **Aadhaar, PAN, and Voter ID** — generally no FIR is needed. For **Passport and Driving License** — an FIR or affidavit is usually required. It's always a good idea to file an FIR if documents were stolen.

### What if I don't remember my document numbers?
- **Aadhaar:** Use the "Retrieve Aadhaar" feature on UIDAI using your registered mobile
- **PAN:** Use "Know Your PAN" on the Income Tax website
- **Voter ID:** Search by name on voters.eci.gov.in
- **DL:** Check DigiLocker or contact your RTO

### Can someone misuse my lost documents?
This is why filing an **FIR** is important if documents were stolen. Also:
- Lock your Aadhaar biometrics on myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in
- Monitor your bank accounts and credit report ([Check CIBIL Score](/guides/cibil-score-check-improve))
