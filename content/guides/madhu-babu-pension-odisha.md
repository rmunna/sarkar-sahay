## Check MBPY Status by Aadhaar — SSEPD Portal

The most searched question about this scheme is how to check pension status by Aadhaar number online.

**Steps to check Madhu Babu Pension / SSEPD application status by Aadhaar:**

1. Go to **[ssepd.odisha.gov.in](https://ssepd.odisha.gov.in)**
2. On the homepage, look for **"Application Status"** or **"Beneficiary Status"** link
3. Select **"Search by Aadhaar"** from the dropdown
4. Enter your **12-digit Aadhaar number**
5. Click **Search / Submit**
6. Your pension application status, beneficiary ID, and payment history will appear

**What the status screen shows:**
- Application number and date
- Approval status (Pending / Approved / Rejected)
- Current pension amount (₹3,000 or ₹3,500/month)
- Last payment date and amount
- Bank account details linked

**If status is not found:** Your application may still be in verification. Contact your Block Development Office (BDO) with your application acknowledgement slip.

**SSEPD helpline:** 155335 (Odisha government toll-free)

## What is Madhu Babu Pension Yojana (MBPY)?

Madhu Babu Pension Yojana (MBPY) is a social security pension scheme run by the Government of Odisha under the Department of Social Security and Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (SSEPD). Named after the legendary Odia leader Madhusudan Das (fondly called Madhu Babu), this scheme provides monthly financial assistance to elderly citizens, widows, persons with disabilities, and leprosy-affected individuals in the state.

The scheme was formed by merging the earlier **Revised Old Age Pension Rules** and **Disability Pension Rules** into a single comprehensive pension programme. It now benefits lakhs of vulnerable citizens across Odisha.

> **Note:** With the new BJP government in Odisha (2024), the pension amounts have been significantly enhanced under the **Mukhyamantri Sahayata Yojana**, which builds upon MBPY. Current pension amounts are ₹3,000-₹3,500 per month.

## Key Benefits

| Category | Monthly Pension | Age/Criteria |
|----------|----------------|--------------|
| Old Age (60-79 years) | ₹3,000 | Age 60+ years |
| Old Age (80+ years) | ₹3,500 | Age 80+ years |
| Widow | ₹3,000 | All eligible widows |
| Persons with Disability (40-79%) | ₹3,000 | Certified disability |
| Persons with Disability (80%+) | ₹3,500 | Severe disability |
| Leprosy affected | ₹3,000 | Certified leprosy patients |

> Pension amounts were enhanced from ₹500-700 under old MBPY to ₹3,000-3,500 under the current government's Mukhyamantri Sahayata Yojana.

## Who is Eligible?

### Old Age Pension
- Must be a **resident of Odisha**
- Age **60 years or above**
- Family income should not exceed **₹40,000 per year** (BPL or near-BPL)
- Should not be receiving pension from any other government scheme (except NSAP overlap)

### Widow Pension
- Must be a **widow** residing in Odisha
- Age **18 years or above** (no upper age limit)
- Family income should not exceed the prescribed BPL limit
- Should not have remarried

### Disability Pension
- Must be a **resident of Odisha**
- Disability of **40% or more** (certified by a government medical board)
- Age **5 years or above**
- Family income within the prescribed limit

### Leprosy Pension
- Must be a certified leprosy patient residing in Odisha
- No specific income restriction for leprosy-affected persons

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar Card** (mandatory)
- **Age proof** — Birth certificate, school certificate, or medical age certificate
- **Income Certificate** from Tehsildar/BDO
- **BPL Ration Card** (if available)
- **Residence proof** — Voter ID, ration card, or electricity bill
- **Disability Certificate** (for disability pension — from District Medical Board)
- **Death Certificate of husband** (for widow pension)
- **Bank passbook** (Aadhaar-linked account)
- **Passport-size photographs** (2 copies)

## How to Apply

### Online Process

1. Visit the **SSEPD Portal** at [ssepd.odisha.gov.in](https://ssepd.odisha.gov.in)
2. Navigate to the **pension application** section
3. Enter your **Aadhaar number** for e-KYC verification
4. Fill in personal details — name, age, address, category (old age/widow/disability)
5. Upload required documents
6. Enter **bank account details** (must be Aadhaar-linked)
7. Submit the application
8. Note the application ID for tracking

### Offline Process

1. Visit the **Block Development Office (BDO)** or **Urban Local Body (ULB)** office
2. Collect the **MBPY application form**
3. Fill the form in capital letters with accurate details
4. Attach self-attested copies of all required documents
5. Submit to the BDO office (rural) or Municipal office (urban)
6. Collect the acknowledgement receipt
7. Application will be verified by the **Block/ULB Committee**
8. Approved applications are forwarded to the **District Social Security Officer (DSSO)**

### How to Check Pension Status

1. Visit [ssepd.odisha.gov.in](https://ssepd.odisha.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Beneficiary Status"** or **"Track Application"**
3. Enter your **application ID** or **Aadhaar number**
4. View the current status of your pension application

## Payment Method

- Pension is credited **monthly** to the beneficiary's **Aadhaar-linked bank account** via DBT
- Some beneficiaries may also receive payment through **post office savings accounts**
- Payment is typically credited between the **1st and 10th of each month**

## Processing Time

- Application verification: **30-45 days**
- Approval and first payment: **60-90 days** from submission
- Annual verification is conducted to confirm continued eligibility

## Important Tips

1. **Link Aadhaar to bank account** — This is mandatory for receiving pension through DBT. See our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
2. **Get disability certificate early** — If applying for disability pension, the District Medical Board certificate can take 2-4 weeks
3. **Annual life certificate** — Pensioners must submit a life certificate (Jeevan Pramaan) annually to continue receiving pension
4. **Report changes immediately** — If the pensioner passes away or circumstances change, inform the BDO office to avoid recovery proceedings
5. **Apply through CSC** — Common Service Centres (Jan Seva Kendras) can help with online applications

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q0. How to check SSEPD application status by Aadhaar?
Go to [ssepd.odisha.gov.in](https://ssepd.odisha.gov.in) → click "Application Status" → select "Search by Aadhaar" → enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number → click Search. The portal shows your pension application status, approval stage, and last payment details.

### Q0b. How to check Madhu Babu Pension Yojana status by Aadhaar card?
The process is the same: visit ssepd.odisha.gov.in, use the "Beneficiary Status" or "Application Status" link, enter your Aadhaar number. If the portal is down, call the SSEPD helpline 155335 or visit your nearest BDO office with your application ID.

### Q1. What is the current pension amount under MBPY?
As of 2025-26, the pension amount is **₹3,000 per month** for most categories and **₹3,500 per month** for persons aged 80+ or with 80%+ disability, under the enhanced Mukhyamantri Sahayata Yojana.

### Q2. Can I apply for MBPY online?
Yes. You can apply through the SSEPD portal at ssepd.odisha.gov.in or visit your nearest Block Development Office for offline application.

### Q3. What is the income limit for MBPY eligibility?
The family income should not exceed ₹40,000 per annum. BPL card holders get priority.

### Q4. Can a widow above 60 years get both old age and widow pension?
No. A beneficiary can receive pension under only one category. However, the higher applicable amount will be provided.

### Q5. How is the pension paid?
Pension is paid monthly through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to the beneficiary's Aadhaar-linked bank account.

### Q6. What happens if the pensioner dies?
The family must inform the BDO/ULB office. Pension will be stopped from the next month. Any excess payment may be recovered.

### Q7. Is there any application fee?
No. There is no fee for applying to MBPY. Beware of anyone asking for money to process your application.
