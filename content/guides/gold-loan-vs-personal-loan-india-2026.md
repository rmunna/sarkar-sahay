# Gold Loan vs Personal Loan India 2026 — Complete Comparison

When you need money urgently, both gold loans and personal loans can serve the purpose. Choosing the right one depends on your situation, collateral availability, and how important rate vs speed is to you.

---

## Gold Loan — How It Works

**How LTV (Loan-to-Value) is calculated**:
- RBI cap: Maximum 75% LTV for gold loans
- Gold value is calculated per gram at today's market rate (discounted by lender)
- **Example**: If gold is ₹7,000/gram and you have 20 grams
  - Market value: ₹1,40,000
  - Max loan: ₹1,05,000 (75% LTV)

## Gold Loan vs Personal Loan — When Each Wins

### Choose Gold Loan When:

**1. Your CIBIL score is low or you have no credit history**
- Gold loan doesn't depend on CIBIL
- First-time borrowers, people with past defaults, no formal income proof → gold loan only viable option

**2. You need money VERY fast**
- Gold loan in 30 minutes. Personal loan takes 1–5 days minimum (even pre-approved instant loans take hours)

**3. Self-employed with difficulty proving income**
- Gold loan only needs the gold — no income documents required
- Personal loan requires 2–3 years ITR for self-employed

**4. Lowest interest rate is the priority**
- SBI gold loan at 8.5–9.5% is cheaper than any personal loan

**5. Short-term need (3–12 months)**
- Gold loan bullet repayment: Pay only interest monthly, return principal when cash comes
- Perfect for bridging loan, tax payment gap, business cash flow

### Choose Personal Loan When:

**1. You don't have idle gold**
- Most families keep gold in use (jewelry worn regularly) — pledging it means not wearing it

**2. You don't want to risk losing the gold**
- If you can't repay gold loan, lender auctions the gold to recover. Personal loan default has CIBIL impact but doesn't touch physical assets

**3. Higher loan amount than gold value allows**
- ₹10L personal loan easy if income supports; ₹10L gold loan requires 14+ kg gold at ₹7,000/gram

**4. Longer tenure at fixed EMI**
- 5-year EMI personal loan for planned expenses (home renovation, wedding)
- Gold loans are typically 1–3 year maximum

**5. No gold available but good credit score**
- If CIBIL is 750+, personal loan rates can be competitive (11–13%) — not far from gold loan rates

## Special Consideration: Gold Loan vs Personal Loan Tax Angle

- **Neither is tax-deductible** for personal use
- Gold loan for business purposes: Interest is a business expense (deductible)
- Personal loan for business: Interest also deductible (maintain documentation)
- Home renovation loan (personal): No deduction in new tax regime; old regime has no specific deduction either for renovation (only home purchase)

## Gold Loan Process — Step by Step

1. **Visit lender**: Muthoot/Manappuram branch or bank branch
2. **Carry gold**: Jewelry, coins, bullion (gold bars accepted by banks)
3. **Gold purity test**: Tested on the spot (fire assay or acid test)
4. **Valuation**: Gold graded and weighed; loan amount offered at 75% LTV
5. **Documents**: PAN + Aadhaar only
6. **Disbursement**: Cash or bank transfer within 30 minutes

**At loan end**: Repay principal (+ any pending interest) → gold returned to you.

## Risks of Gold Loan — What to Know

| Risk | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Auction risk** | If you can't repay, lender can auction your gold after notice |
| **LTV fluctuation** | If gold price falls after taking loan, your margin may require additional gold or partial repayment |
| **Storage risk** | Gold is at lender's premises — ensure reputed lender with proper vault insurance |
| **Interest pileup** | Bullet repayment with no payment = interest compounding; have a clear repayment plan |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I get a gold loan on gold coins bought from bank or jewellery shops?

Bank-issued gold coins: Yes (both banks and NBFCs accept). Jewellery: Yes. Foreign coins or antique gold: Generally not accepted. Gold bars with hallmark: Yes.

### I have gold loan from Muthoot. Can I transfer it to SBI for lower rate?

Yes — called gold loan balance transfer. Pay off Muthoot → take gold to SBI → get new lower-rate loan against same gold. Similar to home loan balance transfer. Feasible if rate difference is significant (2%+ savings).

### If I default on a gold loan, will it appear in my CIBIL?

Yes — gold loan default is reported to credit bureaus. It will appear as a negative entry in your CIBIL report and impact your credit score. The lender will also auction the gold, but CIBIL impact remains.
