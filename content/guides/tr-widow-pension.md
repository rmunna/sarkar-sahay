# Tripura Widow Pension – Complete Guide 2026

## What is the Tripura Widow Pension?

The **Tripura Widow Pension** provides monthly financial assistance to widowed women from economically weaker sections. Operating under the **Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme (IGNWPS)** with Tripura state top-up, the scheme helps widows sustain themselves financially.

## Pension Amount

| Category | Approximate Monthly Pension |
|----------|---------------------------|
| Widows aged 40-79 | ₹400-700 |
| Widows aged 80+ | ₹700-1,000 |

> Includes central (₹300) and state top-up. Verify with District Social Welfare Office.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Status:** Widow (husband deceased), not remarried
- **Age:** 40-79 years (IGNWPS); state scheme may cover younger widows
- **Residency:** Permanent resident of Tripura
- **Income:** BPL category or below prescribed income limit
- **Not receiving** any other government pension

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar Card
- Husband's death certificate
- Age proof
- Income certificate / BPL card
- Bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked)
- Residential proof
- Passport-size photographs (2)
- Self-declaration of not being remarried

## How to Apply – Step-by-Step

1. Visit the **Gram Panchayat office** (rural) or **Municipal office** (urban)
2. Collect the **Widow Pension application form**
3. Fill in all personal, bank, and income details
4. Attach self-attested copies of all documents
5. Get verification from the **Gram Pradhan / Ward Member**
6. Submit at the **Block Development Office** or **SDM office**
7. After verification and approval, pension is credited via DBT monthly

## Processing Time

- **Verification to first payment:** 2-4 months typically

## Important Tips

1. **Obtain death certificate first** — mandatory for application
2. **Pension stops on remarriage** — inform authorities promptly
3. **Annual life verification** is required
4. **Keep bank account active** to avoid payment issues
5. **Apply promptly** — don't delay after becoming eligible

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Can I receive both old age and widow pension?
**A:** No, only **one NSAP pension** at a time. See our [Tripura Old Age Pension guide](/guide/tr-old-age-pension).

### Q2: What if I don't have a death certificate?
**A:** Obtain one from the **Municipal/Panchayat office** where the death was registered.

### Q3: Is there an online application?
**A:** Check [nsap.nic.in](https://nsap.nic.in). Primary method is offline through Panchayat/Municipal office.

### Q4: What is the age limit?
**A:** IGNWPS covers widows aged **40-79**. Those 80+ should apply for old age pension for higher benefits.

### Q5: How long does approval take?
**A:** Approximately **2-4 months** from application to first payment.
