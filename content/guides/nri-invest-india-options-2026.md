# NRI Investment in India 2026 — Complete Guide

India offers NRIs strong investment opportunities — tax-free NRE FDs, equity market access via PIS, and mutual funds. But there are rules about which account to use, FATCA compliance, and repatriation limits. Here's the complete picture.

---

## NRI Investment Rules — The Foundation

NRIs fall under **FEMA (Foreign Exchange Management Act)** for investments in India. All investments must flow through designated NRI bank accounts:

| Account | Purpose | Tax on Interest |
|---------|---------|----------------|
| **NRE (Non-Resident External)** | Foreign earnings invested in India | Tax-free in India |
| **NRO (Non-Resident Ordinary)** | Indian income (rent, dividends from Indian stocks) | Taxable (30% TDS) |
| **FCNR (Foreign Currency Non-Resident)** | FD in USD, GBP, EUR | Tax-free in India; no currency risk |

**Investment principle**: Use NRE or FCNR for investments you want to freely repatriate. NRO for Indian-source income investments with limited repatriation.

---

## Investment Option 1: NRE and NRO Fixed Deposits

### NRE Fixed Deposit

- **Interest rate**: 6.5–7.5% (varies by bank and tenure; private banks offer higher rates)
- **Tax**: Fully exempt from Indian income tax (interest not taxable)
- **Repatriation**: Fully repatriable at any time (principal + interest can be sent abroad)
- **Currency**: INR (but funded from foreign currency)
- **Insurance**: DICGC covers ₹5L per bank (same as resident FD)

**Best NRE FD rates (2026)**:
- SBI: 6.5–7.1%
- HDFC Bank: 6.6–7.25%
- Kotak Mahindra: 7.0–7.25%
- IDFC First: 7.25–7.5%
- Small finance banks (AU, ESAF): 8–9% (higher rate, higher risk)

### FCNR Fixed Deposit

- **Currency**: USD, GBP, EUR, JPY, AUD, CAD
- **No currency risk**: Principal + interest returned in the same foreign currency
- **Tax**: Exempt in India
- **Rate**: Typically 4–5.5% in USD (lower than INR FD but no rupee depreciation risk)
- **Best for**: NRIs who want to keep money in USD without forex risk

### NRO Fixed Deposit

- Same as regular FD; interest taxed at 30% TDS
- Only viable if you have existing NRO balance (rental income, Indian dividends) that can't go to NRE
- Not recommended for fresh investment of foreign earnings

## Investment Option 2: Indian Stock Market (via PIS Account)

### What is PIS?

**PIS (Portfolio Investment Scheme)**: RBI-mandated account structure for NRIs to buy/sell listed Indian equities (NSE/BSE).

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| PIS account | Open with a designated PIS bank (HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis etc.) |
| Demat + trading account | Link to PIS account; many brokers (HDFC Securities, ICICI Direct) offer integrated NRI account |
| Permission | PIS bank ensures RBI reporting for each transaction |

### How to Open NRI Demat/PIS Account

1. Choose broker: HDFC Securities, ICICI Direct, Kotak Securities, or some Zerodha (limited)
2. Open NRE/NRO linked PIS account at the bank
3. Open demat account linked to PIS account
4. Fund the demat from NRE (for repatriable investments) or NRO (non-repatriable)
5. Trade on NSE/BSE

### Tax on Stock Market Gains (NRI)

| Gain Type | Tax Rate | TDS by Broker |
|-----------|---------|--------------|
| STCG (equity, <12 months) | 20% | Yes — TDS deducted |
| LTCG (equity, >12 months) | 12.5% above ₹1.25L exemption | Yes |
| STCG (debt/others) | Slab rate | Yes |

**NRIs cannot claim the ₹12L zero-tax rebate (Section 87A)** — taxes start from first rupee.

Broker deducts TDS on each transaction. File ITR-2 to reconcile and claim refund if excess TDS.

## Investment Option 3: Mutual Funds for NRIs

### Can NRIs Invest in Indian Mutual Funds?

**Yes** — but with some restrictions:

**AMCs that accept NRI investments** (both NRI from USA/Canada AND other countries):
- Most AMCs accept NRIs from countries except USA/Canada OR
- Specific AMCs that have completed FATCA compliance: Nippon, UTI, HDFC, ICICI Prudential, Mirae Asset, Axis, Kotak

**FATCA issue for USA/Canada-based NRIs**: Some AMCs don't accept NRIs from USA/Canada due to complex FATCA (US tax law) reporting requirements. Check with each AMC or use platforms that specifically support US-NRI investors.

### How NRIs Invest in Mutual Funds

1. **KYC (CKYC)**: Complete KYC with Aadhaar + PAN + NRI status proof
2. **Platform**: Use platforms like NRIInvestIndia, Kuvera (for some), or AMC direct websites
3. **Bank source**: NRE bank account (for repatriable folio) or NRO (non-repatriable)
4. **Funds**: Access to same funds (direct plans) as resident Indians

### Tax on NRI Mutual Fund Returns

Same as stocks (equity funds taxed as equity), applied from first rupee without Section 87A rebate.

TDS is deducted by the AMC at source when you redeem.

## Investment Option 4: Real Estate

### Buying Property as NRI

- NRIs can buy **residential and commercial property** in India without RBI permission
- Cannot buy: Agricultural land, plantation property, farmhouse

**Payment route**: Must be paid from NRE/NRO account or home loan from Indian bank.

### Home Loan for NRI

Banks offer home loans to NRIs:
- Rate: 8.5–9.5% (similar to resident Indian rates)
- Documentation: Foreign income proof, NRE/NRO bank statements, employment proof
- Repaid through NRE/NRO account EMI

### Rental Income

Rental income goes to NRO account, taxed at 30% TDS by tenant, declared in Indian ITR-2. See the NRI property rent management guide for full details.

## Investment Option 6: INvITs and REITs

NRIs can invest in listed InvITs (Infrastructure Investment Trusts) and REITs via the PIS account on NSE/BSE:
- Same tax treatment as stocks
- Quarterly distributions (partly taxable)
- Embassy REIT, Mindspace REIT, Nexus REIT accessible

## Repatriation Rules — Moving Money Back Abroad

| Source | Limit | Process |
|--------|-------|---------|
| NRE FD proceeds | No limit | Direct transfer from NRE account |
| FCNR FD | No limit | Return in original currency |
| Sale of Indian stocks (PIS) | No limit (if from NRE-linked) | Via PIS bank |
| NRO balance (rent, dividends, NRO proceeds) | USD 1 million/year | Form 15CA + 15CB from CA |

**NRE account is freely repatriable** — no limits, no forms. This is the key advantage of investing via NRE.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I invest in SGB (Sovereign Gold Bond) as NRI?

NRIs cannot purchase SGB as primary investors. SGBs held by resident Indians remain valid even if the investor later becomes NRI — they can hold until maturity but cannot buy new ones after becoming NRI.

### Is PPF available to NRIs?

NRIs cannot open new PPF accounts. If you opened a PPF before becoming NRI, the account can continue for its 15-year maturity — you can contribute from NRO account (non-repatriable). After maturity, you can close and repatriate under USD 1M limit.

### Which broker supports NRI accounts in India?

HDFC Securities, ICICI Direct, Kotak Securities, SBICAP Securities, Axis Direct — all support NRI PIS accounts. Zerodha supports NRIs from some countries but not USA/Canada. Groww does not currently support NRI accounts (as of 2026 — verify current status).

### Do I need to file ITR in India as NRI?

File ITR if you have Indian income (NRO interest, rental income, capital gains from Indian stocks/property) exceeding ₹2.5L. Also file if you want to claim refund of excess TDS.
