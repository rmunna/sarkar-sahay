## What is Ambedkar DBT Voucher Yojana?

**Ambedkar DBT Voucher Yojana** is a Rajasthan state scheme that provides **monthly rent assistance** to SC/ST/OBC/EWS students who leave their villages to study in **divisional headquarters cities** (Jaipur, Jodhpur, Kota, Udaipur, Bharatpur, Bikaner, Ajmer). Since the cost of living is high in these cities, this DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer) voucher helps cover accommodation costs.

---

## Key Benefits

| Category | Monthly Voucher Amount |
|----------|----------------------|
| SC/ST students | ₹7,000/month |
| OBC/MBC students | ₹5,000/month |
| EWS students | ₹5,000/month |
| Duration | 10 months per academic year |
| Payment | Directly to student's bank account (DBT) |

---

## Who is Eligible?

**Student criteria:**
- Belongs to **SC, ST, OBC, MBC, or EWS** category
- Studying in a **government college, university, or recognized institution** in one of the 7 divisional HQ cities
- Studies: Graduation (BA, BSc, BCom, BTech), Post-graduation, Diploma
- Does **not reside in the same city** as the institution (migrated from outside)
- **Not staying in a government hostel** (if already in govt hostel, not eligible)

**Family income criteria:**
- SC/ST: No income limit
- OBC/MBC: Family income below ₹1.5 lakh per year
- EWS: Family income below ₹8 lakh per year

**Age:** Up to 35 years

---

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card**
2. **Caste certificate** (SC/ST/OBC as applicable) from competent authority
3. **Income certificate** (for OBC/EWS categories) from Tehsildar
4. **Bonafide certificate** from college/university (current enrolment)
5. **Rent agreement / accommodation proof** (with landlord name and address)
6. **Bank passbook** (student's account — Aadhaar linked)
7. **Photograph**

## How to Apply

### Step 1 — Online Application

Go to **[sje.rajasthan.gov.in](https://sje.rajasthan.gov.in)** or **SSO Rajasthan portal (sso.rajasthan.gov.in)**:
- Login with SSO ID
- Navigate to: Social Justice → Ambedkar DBT Voucher Yojana
- Fill application with personal, caste, education, and accommodation details
- Upload all documents

### Step 2 — College Verification

The applying student's college/university verifies the bonafide status through the online portal.

### Step 3 — District Social Justice Office Verification

The District Social Justice and Empowerment Officer (DSJEO) verifies caste certificate and income documents.

### Step 4 — Voucher Disbursement

After verification and approval, monthly amount is credited directly to the student's Aadhaar-linked bank account each month.

---

## When to Apply

Applications are accepted at the **start of each academic session** (typically July–September). Late applications may also be accepted — check sje.rajasthan.gov.in for the current year's deadline.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. I am an OBC student studying BTech in Jaipur but I come from Sikar. Am I eligible?**

Yes. You are migrated to Jaipur (a divisional HQ) from outside, studying in a recognized institution, and belong to OBC — you are eligible. Check that your family income is below ₹1.5 lakh/year and you are not staying in a government hostel.

**2. I am already in a government hostel. Can I also claim the voucher?**

No. If you are provided government hostel accommodation, you are not eligible for the DBT voucher — you are already receiving housing support.

**3. I study in Kota coaching. Do I qualify?**

The scheme is for students enrolled in **recognized colleges/universities**, not just coaching institutes. If you are also enrolled in a college in Kota, and the coaching is concurrent, discuss with the college. Pure coaching students without college enrollment are not eligible.

**4. Can I change my accommodation address after applying?**

Yes, but you need to update the portal with the new rent agreement. The voucher is linked to your accommodation status.

**5. Is this the same as the post-matric scholarship?**

No. This is a **separate rent/accommodation voucher** scheme on top of the post-matric scholarship. You can receive both if eligible.
