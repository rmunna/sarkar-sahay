## Why You May Need a Duplicate Electricity Bill

There are many situations where you need a copy of your electricity bill:

- **Original bill lost or damaged** — common during monsoon or shifting
- **Address proof** — electricity bill is accepted as valid address proof for Aadhaar, bank accounts, passport, and other services
- **Property documents** — needed for property registration, loan applications
- **Bill payment** — if you misplaced the physical bill and need to pay
- **Record keeping** — for tax purposes or expense tracking
- **Tenant verification** — landlords may ask for bill copy

## How to Download Electricity Bill Online

### Step 1: Know Your DISCOM

Identify which electricity distribution company (DISCOM) serves your area. Your consumer number and DISCOM name are printed on your existing bills or meter card.

### Step 2: Visit DISCOM Website or App

Go to your DISCOM's official website. Most DISCOMs now have mobile apps too.

**Major DISCOM Portals:**

- **Delhi:** BSES (bfresco.bfresco.com), TPDDL (tatapower-ddl.com)
- **Maharashtra:** MSEDCL (mahadiscom.in)
- **Uttar Pradesh:** UPPCL (uppcl.org)
- **Tamil Nadu:** TANGEDCO (tnebnet.org)
- **Karnataka:** BESCOM (bescom.karnataka.gov.in)
- **Rajasthan:** JVVNL (energy.rajasthan.gov.in)
- **Madhya Pradesh:** MPEB (portal.mpcz.in)
- **West Bengal:** WBSEDCL (wbsedcl.in)
- **Gujarat:** UGVCL, PGVCL, MGVCL, DGVCL

### Step 3: Enter Consumer Number

- On the homepage, look for "View/Download Bill" or "Bill Payment" section
- Enter your consumer number (also called account number, CA number, or service number)
- Some portals also allow search by registered mobile number

### Step 4: View and Download Bill

- Your latest bill will be displayed with all details
- Click "Download" or "Print" to save as PDF
- Most portals show bill history for the last 6-12 months
- You can download any previous month's bill

### Step 5: Print the Bill

Open the downloaded PDF and print it. This duplicate bill is valid as address proof and for all official purposes.

## Download Bill via Mobile App

Most DISCOMs have official mobile apps:

1. Download the app from Google Play Store or Apple App Store
2. Register with your consumer number and mobile number
3. OTP verification
4. View current and past bills
5. Download or share bill as PDF

Popular apps: BSES Rajdhani App, MSEDCL App, UPPCL Urban App, TANGEDCO App, BESCOM App.

## Download Bill via Third-Party Apps

You can also view and download bills through:

- **Paytm** — Go to Electricity > Enter consumer number > View bill
- **PhonePe** — Recharge & Pay Bills > Electricity
- **Google Pay** — Bill Payments > Electricity
- **UMANG App** — Government services app with DISCOM integration

*Note: Third-party apps show bill amount but may not provide a downloadable PDF suitable as address proof.*

## Get Duplicate Bill Offline

If you cannot access the bill online:

1. **Visit DISCOM office** — Go to your nearest subdivision office with your consumer number or old bill copy. Request a duplicate bill at the counter.
2. **Call customer care** — Many DISCOMs send bill copies via email or SMS on request.
3. **SMS service** — Some DISCOMs allow you to get bill details by sending an SMS with your consumer number to a specific number.

## Understanding Your Electricity Bill

Your electricity bill contains:

- **Consumer number** — your unique account identifier
- **Billing period** — month and dates covered
- **Units consumed** — kWh reading (current minus previous)
- **Tariff details** — rate per unit slab-wise
- **Fixed charges** — monthly fixed component
- **Energy charges** — based on consumption
- **Fuel surcharge** — additional charge for fuel cost
- **Taxes** — electricity duty, cess
- **Total amount due** — sum of all charges
- **Due date** — last date for payment without penalty
- **Subsidy** (if applicable) — government subsidy credited

## Tips

- **Save consumer number** in your phone contacts or notes for quick access
- **Register mobile number** with DISCOM to receive bill alerts via SMS
- **Enable e-bill** — opt for paperless billing to receive bills on email/WhatsApp
- **Download regularly** — keep a digital archive of bills for address proof needs
- **Check meter reading** — verify the units on your bill match your meter

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is a downloaded electricity bill valid as address proof?

Yes, a downloaded/printed electricity bill is accepted as valid address proof for Aadhaar, bank accounts, passport, and most government services. It should be recent (within 3 months).

### I don't know my consumer number. How to find it?

Check your electricity meter — the consumer number is usually printed on a sticker on the meter. You can also find it on any old bill, receipt, or by visiting the DISCOM office with your address details.

### Can I get bills from previous years?

Most DISCOM portals show bill history for the last 6-12 months. For older bills, you may need to visit the DISCOM office and request archived copies.

### How often are electricity bills generated?

Most DISCOMs generate bills monthly. Some rural areas may have bimonthly billing cycles. The billing date varies by area and DISCOM.

### Can I get someone else's electricity bill?

You can view and download bills using any consumer number. However, for official purposes (address proof), the bill should be in your name or your family member's name at the same address.

### What if my bill shows wrong reading?

Contact your DISCOM immediately through customer care or visit the office. Request a meter reading correction. You can also file a complaint on the DISCOM portal or through the state electricity regulatory commission.
