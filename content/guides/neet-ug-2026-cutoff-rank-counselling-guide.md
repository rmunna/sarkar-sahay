## NEET Counselling — MCC vs State Counselling

**Two types of counselling for MBBS/BDS**:

1. **MCC Counselling** (mcc.nic.in) — for **AIQ 15% seats** at all government colleges + all seats at central institutions (AIIMS, JIPMER, etc.)
2. **State Counselling** — for **State Quota 85% seats** at state government and private colleges (each state has its own process)

**You can participate in BOTH simultaneously.**

---

## MCC AIQ Counselling — Step by Step

### Step 1: Registration

- Visit [mcc.nic.in](https://mcc.nic.in)
- Register with NEET Roll Number + Date of Birth
- Create login credentials
- Fill personal details, category, PWD status

### Step 2: Document Verification (if required)

For reserved category candidates: upload caste certificate, state domicile, etc. during registration.

### Step 3: Choice Filling

The most critical step:
- Browse all participating colleges and courses
- Add colleges to your choice list in order of preference
- Higher the preference, the better your chances
- Fill as many choices as possible (no limit) — more choices = more security

**Strategy**: Fill in order of your genuine preference, not reverse-ranking by difficulty. If you want a specific college most, put it first.

### Step 4: Lock Choices

After the choice-filling window closes, lock your choices. Locked choices are final — cannot be changed.

### Step 5: Seat Allotment

Based on rank, category, and choices, MCC allocates a seat. Result: "Seat Allotted" (which college, which course).

### Step 6: Reporting

If a seat is allotted:
- Pay the seat acceptance fee (online) within 24 hours
- Report to the allotted college within the reporting date with original documents
- Failure to report = seat cancelled, no upgrade in next round

### Step 7: Round 2 / Mop-Up

If you don't get your preferred college in Round 1, participate in Round 2. Upgraded allotments happen if seats fall vacant.

## State Counselling — How It Works

Each state runs its own 85% quota counselling. Broadly similar to MCC process but:
- Only state domicile candidates compete
- Separate registration on state's authority website
- Parallel to MCC counselling (don't wait for MCC to finish)
- State-specific documents (domicile, income certificate, caste) needed

**Key states' counselling authorities**:
- Delhi: UGC/DGHS Delhi (dghs.delhi.gov.in)
- Maharashtra: DMER Maharashtra (dmer.org)
- Tamil Nadu: TNMGRMU / MCC-TN
- Karnataka: KEA (kea.kar.nic.in)
- UP: DGME UP (upneet.gov.in)

## Important NEET Counselling Tips

**Don't skip the mop-up round**: Even if round 1 and 2 didn't give a good seat, mop-up round and stray vacancy round (some states) have seats that fall vacant when candidates don't report.

**Prefer government college over private** at equal rank: Government medical college cost is ₹10,000–₹50,000/year vs private college ₹10–₹25 lakh/year for MBBS.

**Know your state's reservation policy**: OBC/SC/ST/EWS quotas vary by state. Your effective cutoff in your category may be 50–150 marks lower than general cutoff.

**Register on MCC portal on Day 1**: Registration windows open and close fast. Late registration = missed round.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I get MBBS with 500 marks in a government college?

Unlikely in major states (Maharashtra, Delhi, Karnataka, TN) via AIQ. Possible in state quota in less-competitive states (Northeast, UP/Bihar lower-ranked colleges). With 500 marks in general category, most candidates target BDS government, BAMS government, or private MBBS.

### Do I need to participate in MCC counselling to get a state quota seat?

No — state quota counselling is separate from MCC. You can participate in state counselling even if you don't register for MCC. However, registering for both maximizes your chances.

### What if I get a seat in Round 1 but want a better seat in Round 2?

Participate in Round 2 by selecting "Upgrade" option. If a better seat is allotted, you must move to it (you cannot hold two seats). Your Round 1 seat is released when you upgrade.

### Can NRI candidates get seats through NEET?

Yes — NRI quota seats exist in private colleges (15% NRI quota). NRI candidates participate in management quota counselling through respective college/state authority. NRI quota fees are typically higher (₹20–₹40 lakh/year).
