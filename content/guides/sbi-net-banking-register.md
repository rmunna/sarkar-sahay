# SBI Net Banking Register: How to Activate Online

State Bank of India (SBI) Internet Banking lets you manage your account, transfer funds, pay bills, and access various banking services from anywhere. You can register for SBI Net Banking online through the official onlinesbi.sbi portal or activate it via the YONO SBI app.

## What is SBI Net Banking?

SBI Net Banking (Internet Banking) is a digital banking service that allows SBI account holders to perform banking transactions online — including fund transfers (NEFT/RTGS/IMPS), bill payments, fixed deposit booking, account statements, and more — without visiting a branch.

## Who is Eligible?

- Any SBI savings or current account holder
- Must have a registered mobile number linked to the account
- Must have an active ATM/debit card (for online self-registration)

## Documents Required

### For Online Self-Registration
- SBI account number
- Registered mobile number (for OTP)
- ATM/debit card details (card number, PIN, expiry date)
- CIF number (available on passbook first page)

### For Branch Registration
- Filled Internet Banking registration form
- Valid photo ID proof (Aadhaar, PAN, passport, voter ID)
- Passbook or account statement
- Registered mobile number

## Step-by-Step Process: Online Registration via onlinesbi.sbi

1. **Visit the official SBI portal:** Go to [https://www.onlinesbi.sbi/](https://www.onlinesbi.sbi/)
2. **Click on "New User Registration"** on the login page
3. **Choose "New User"** and click "OK"
4. **Fill the registration form:**
   - Enter your account number
   - Enter your CIF number
   - Select your branch
   - Select the country code and enter registered mobile number
   - Choose the facility — "Full Transactions Rights" is recommended
5. **Submit and verify OTP:** An OTP will be sent to your registered mobile number. Enter it to proceed
6. **Enter ATM card details:** Provide your debit card number, ATM PIN, and expiry date for verification
7. **Set your username:** Choose a unique username for login
8. **Set temporary password:** The system generates a temporary password
9. **First login:** Log in using your new username and temporary password
10. **Set profile password:** You'll be prompted to create a new login password and transaction password
11. **Activation complete:** Your SBI Net Banking is now active

## Step-by-Step Process: Registration via YONO SBI App

1. **Download YONO SBI** from Google Play Store or Apple App Store
2. **Open the app** and tap "New User? Register Here"
3. **Enter your account number** and registered mobile number
4. **Verify OTP** sent to your registered mobile number
5. **Set MPIN and login credentials** as prompted
6. **Complete profile setup** — your YONO and Net Banking access is activated simultaneously

## Step-by-Step Process: Registration via Branch

1. **Visit your home branch** with photo ID and passbook
2. **Fill the Internet Banking registration form** (available at the branch)
3. **Submit the form** at the customer service counter
4. **Receive the Internet Banking Kit** — contains your username and temporary password in a sealed envelope
5. **Log in at onlinesbi.sbi** using the credentials from the kit
6. **Change temporary password** to your own login and transaction passwords

## Understanding Your Passwords

| Password Type | Purpose | Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| Login Password | To log into Net Banking | 8-20 characters, mix of letters, numbers, special characters |
| Transaction Password | To authorise financial transactions | Different from login password, same complexity rules |
| YONO MPIN | Quick login to YONO app | 6-digit numeric PIN |

## Important Tips

1. **Always use the official URL** — [onlinesbi.sbi](https://www.onlinesbi.sbi/) — never click links from emails or SMS claiming to be from SBI
2. **Keep your CIF number handy** — it's printed on the first page of your SBI passbook
3. **Register your mobile number first** — if your mobile number is not updated, visit the branch to link it before registering for Net Banking
4. **Set strong passwords** — use a unique combination of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters
5. **Enable high-security OTP** — after activation, enable the OTP-based transaction authentication for safer banking

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: What is the CIF number and where do I find it?
CIF (Customer Information File) number is an 11-digit unique number assigned to every SBI customer. You can find it on the first page of your SBI passbook or by calling SBI customer care.

### Q2: Can I register for SBI Net Banking without an ATM card?
No, for online self-registration you need an active ATM/debit card. Without it, you must visit your home branch to get the Internet Banking Kit.

### Q3: What should I do if I forget my Net Banking password?
Visit onlinesbi.sbi and click "Forgot Login Password." You can reset it using your ATM card details and registered mobile OTP. Alternatively, visit your branch.

### Q4: Is YONO registration the same as Net Banking registration?
Registering on YONO SBI automatically activates your Net Banking access as well. You can use the same credentials on both platforms.

### Q5: Can NRI account holders register for SBI Net Banking?
Yes, NRI customers with SBI NRE/NRO accounts can register for Net Banking. The process is similar, but some features may differ based on account type.

### Q6: What is the daily transaction limit for SBI Net Banking?
The default NEFT/RTGS limit is ₹10 lakh per day for retail users. You can modify limits within the allowed range through Net Banking settings or by visiting your branch.

### Q7: Is SBI Net Banking safe to use?
Yes, SBI uses multi-layer security including login password, transaction password, OTP verification, and virtual keyboard. Always ensure you're on the official website.
