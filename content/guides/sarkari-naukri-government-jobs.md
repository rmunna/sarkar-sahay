# Sarkari Naukri – How to Find and Apply for Government Jobs in India 2026

Government jobs (sarkari naukri) are among the most sought-after career options in India. They offer job security, good salary, pension benefits, housing allowances, and social respect. Every year, lakhs of vacancies are released by central and state governments through various recruiting bodies.

This guide covers everything you need to know — types of government jobs, major recruiting bodies, how to find vacancies, eligibility criteria, documents needed, and the step-by-step application process.

> **Disclaimer:** This guide is for informational purposes only. Always verify details on the official recruitment website before applying. Fees, eligibility, and dates may change. CitizenNest is not affiliated with any government body.

---

## Types of Government Jobs in India

Government jobs in India can be broadly classified into the following categories:

### 1. Central Government Jobs
Jobs under ministries, departments, and offices of the Government of India. Examples include IAS, IPS, IFS, Income Tax Officer, Customs Officer, and Central Secretariat Service posts.

### 2. State Government Jobs
Each state conducts its own recruitment for posts like State Civil Services, police, teachers, clerks, and other administrative positions through State Public Service Commissions (PSCs).

### 3. Public Sector Undertaking (PSU) Jobs
Government-owned companies like BHEL, ONGC, NTPC, SAIL, and Indian Oil recruit engineers, managers, and other professionals through GATE scores or their own exams.

### 4. Defence Jobs
The Indian Army, Navy, and Air Force recruit through NDA, CDS, AFCAT, and Agniveer schemes. Defence civilian posts are also available.

### 5. Banking Jobs
Public sector banks like SBI, PNB, Bank of Baroda, and others recruit through IBPS and SBI's own exams for PO, Clerk, and Specialist Officer positions.

### 6. Railway Jobs
Indian Railways, one of the largest employers in the world, recruits through Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) for posts like ALP, Technician, NTPC, and Group D.

## How to Find Government Jobs (Official Portals)

Never rely on unofficial websites for job notifications. Use these official sources:

1. **National Career Service (NCS) Portal** – [ncs.gov.in](https://www.ncs.gov.in) — The Government of India's official job portal listing vacancies from central and state governments.
2. **India.gov.in** – [india.gov.in](https://www.india.gov.in) — The national portal of India with links to all government departments and recruitment pages.
3. **Employment News** – [employmentnews.gov.in](https://www.employmentnews.gov.in) — Weekly publication by the Government of India listing all government vacancies.
4. **Individual Recruiting Body Websites** – Check UPSC, SSC, IBPS, RRB websites directly for notifications.
5. **State Government Portals** – Each state has its own recruitment portal (e.g., uppsc.up.nic.in, tnpsc.gov.in, mpsc.gov.in).

## General Eligibility for Government Jobs

Eligibility varies by post, but here are the common requirements:

- **Nationality:** Indian citizen (some posts open to NRIs or OCI holders)
- **Age:** Typically 18–27 years for Group C/D posts, 21–32 years for Group A/B posts
  - Age relaxation: SC/ST (5 years), OBC (3 years), PwD (10 years), Ex-servicemen (as per rules)
- **Education:** Ranges from 10th pass (Group D) to graduation/post-graduation (Group A/B)
- **Physical Standards:** Required for defence, police, and paramilitary posts
- **Language:** Knowledge of Hindi and/or English (varies by post)

> **Note:** Always check the specific notification for exact eligibility criteria, as they differ for every recruitment.

## Documents Required

Keep these documents ready before applying:

1. **Aadhaar Card** — for identity verification
2. **10th and 12th Marksheets** — for age proof and educational qualification
3. **Graduation/Post-Graduation Degree or Marksheets** — as required by the post
4. **Caste/Category Certificate** — SC/ST/OBC/EWS certificate from competent authority
5. **Domicile Certificate** — for state-level recruitments
6. **Passport-size Photographs** — recent, with white background (usually 3.5 cm × 4.5 cm)
7. **Signature Scan** — digital scan as per specified dimensions
8. **PwD Certificate** — if applicable, from government medical board
9. **Experience Certificate** — for posts requiring prior experience
10. **NOC (No Objection Certificate)** — if currently employed in government service

> **Tip:** Scan all documents in advance and keep them in JPEG/PDF format with file sizes under the specified limits (usually 50–300 KB).

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Online Application (Most Common)

1. **Find the Official Notification**
   Visit the recruiting body's website (e.g., ssc.nic.in, upsc.gov.in). Download and read the full notification PDF carefully.

2. **Check Eligibility**
   Verify your age, education, category, and other requirements match the post you want to apply for.

3. **Register on the Portal**
   Create an account using your email ID, mobile number, and basic details. You will receive a registration number — save it.

4. **Fill the Application Form**
   Log in and fill in personal details, educational qualifications, work experience, and preferences. Double-check every field before proceeding.

5. **Upload Documents**
   Upload your photograph, signature, and other required documents in the specified format and size.

6. **Pay the Application Fee**
   Pay online via debit card, credit card, UPI, or net banking. Fee varies by post and category:
   - General/OBC: ₹100 to ₹600 (varies by exam)
   - SC/ST/PwD/Female: Often exempted or reduced
   - Keep the payment receipt/transaction ID

7. **Submit the Application**
   Review all details one final time. Click Submit. Download and print the confirmation page.

8. **Track Your Application**
   Use your registration number to check application status on the portal. Save admit card download dates.

### Offline Application (Less Common Now)

Some state-level and local body recruitments still accept offline applications:

1. Download the application form from the official website or collect it from the designated office.
2. Fill it in block letters with black ink.
3. Attach self-attested photocopies of all required documents.
4. Paste a passport-size photograph.
5. Send by registered post or submit in person at the address mentioned in the notification before the deadline.

## Important Tips for Government Job Aspirants

1. **Start early** — Begin preparation at least 6–12 months before the exam. Consistency matters more than last-minute cramming.

2. **Read the notification thoroughly** — Every detail matters. Missing a small eligibility clause can lead to rejection.

3. **Apply before the deadline** — Don't wait for the last day. Server crashes are common on final dates.

4. **Use only official websites** — Never pay money to agents or third-party websites promising government jobs. All applications are direct.

5. **Keep multiple printouts** — Save confirmation pages, payment receipts, and admit cards in both digital and printed formats.

6. **Prepare for multiple exams simultaneously** — SSC CGL, IBPS PO, and State PSC exams have overlapping syllabi. Preparing for one helps with others.

7. **Stay updated** — Check official websites regularly or subscribe to notifications via NCS portal.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: What is the best website to find sarkari naukri?
The National Career Service portal ([ncs.gov.in](https://www.ncs.gov.in)) is the Government of India's official platform for government job listings. You should also check individual recruiting body websites like UPSC, SSC, IBPS, and RRBs.

### Q2: Is there any fee to apply for government jobs?
Yes, most exams charge an application fee ranging from ₹100 to ₹600 for General/OBC candidates. SC/ST, PwD, and female candidates are often exempted. The exact fee is mentioned in each notification.

### Q3: What is the age limit for government jobs?
It varies by post. Generally, 18–27 years for Group C/D and 21–32 years for Group A/B. Age relaxation is available for reserved categories as per government rules.

### Q4: Can I apply for government jobs after 12th?
Yes. Many posts like SSC CHSL (LDC, DEO), SSC MTS, Railway Group D, and state-level clerk posts require only 12th pass qualification.

### Q5: How do I know if a government job notification is genuine?
Always verify from the official website of the recruiting body. Genuine notifications are published on .gov.in or .nic.in domains. Never trust WhatsApp forwards or private websites blindly.

### Q6: Can I apply for multiple government exams at the same time?
Yes, there is no restriction on applying for multiple exams. Many aspirants apply for SSC, IBPS, UPSC, and state-level exams simultaneously.

### Q7: What happens after I clear the written exam?
Most recruitments have multiple stages — written exam, physical test (if applicable), skill test, document verification, and medical examination. The exact process is mentioned in the notification.

### Q8: Is computer knowledge required for government jobs?
For many clerical and officer-level posts, basic computer knowledge (typing speed, MS Office) is required. Some exams include a computer proficiency test.

### Q9: Where can I download my admit card?
Admit cards are available on the official website of the conducting body. Log in with your registration number and date of birth to download.
