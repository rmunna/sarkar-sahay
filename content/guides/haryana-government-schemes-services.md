# Haryana Government Schemes and Services – Complete Guide

Haryana, governed by the BJP under Chief Minister Nayab Singh Saini, offers a wide range of welfare schemes and digital services for its residents. From the unique Parivar Pehchan Patra (Family ID) system to the Saral Haryana single-window portal, the state has built a robust framework for delivering government benefits directly to citizens.

This comprehensive hub covers all major Haryana government schemes across welfare, education, health, housing, agriculture, employment, and more — with official links and step-by-step guidance.

## Parivar Pehchan Patra (Family ID)

The **Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP)** is Haryana's flagship initiative — a unique 8-digit Family ID assigned to every family in the state. It serves as the backbone for delivering government benefits and has become mandatory for most welfare schemes.

**Key Benefits:**
- Single identity for the entire family
- Auto-verification of eligibility for government schemes
- Links Aadhaar, ration card, and other documents
- Required for pension, scholarship, and subsidy applications

## Women & Social Welfare Schemes

### Ladli Scheme (Ladli Yojana)

Financial assistance for families with two daughters to promote the girl child. The state deposits ₹5,000 per year for 5 years into a Kisan Vikas Patra in the girl's name, redeemable at age 18.

**Key Benefits:**
- ₹5,000/year for 5 years (total ₹25,000 + interest)
- Applicable for second daughter born on or after 20 August 2005
- Maturity amount available when the girl turns 18

👉 **Complete details:** [Haryana Ladli Scheme](/guide/haryana-ladli-scheme)

### Vivah Shagun Yojana (Kanyadan Yojana)

Financial assistance of **₹51,000 to ₹71,000** for marriages of daughters from economically weaker families, SC/ST families, and widows/destitute women.

**Key Benefits:**
- ₹51,000 for BPL/SC/ST families
- ₹11,000 for sports-women
- Applied through Saral Haryana or Atal Seva Kendra
- Family ID (PPP) mandatory

### Mukhyamantri Vivah Shagun Yojana for Widows/Divorcees

Additional financial support for widows and divorcees remarrying, with grants up to ₹51,000.

### Old Age Samman Allowance

Monthly pension for senior citizens aged 60 and above. The amount varies by age group and is directly transferred via DBT.

👉 **Full guide:** [Haryana Old Age Samman Allowance](/guide/haryana-old-age-samman-allowance)

### Widow Pension Scheme

Monthly pension of ₹2,750+ for widowed women in Haryana. Applied through Saral Haryana with Family ID verification.

### Disability Pension

Monthly financial assistance for persons with 60%+ disability, delivered through Direct Benefit Transfer.

## Mukhyamantri Antyodaya Parivar Utthan Yojana

This flagship scheme targets the **poorest families** in Haryana (annual income below ₹1,00,000) to uplift them through skill development, employment, and convergence of existing welfare benefits.

**Key Benefits:**
- Identification of poorest families through Parivar Pehchan Patra data
- Skill training and employment linkage
- Convergence of all eligible scheme benefits automatically
- Goal to raise family income above ₹1,00,000/year

The scheme uses the Family ID database to automatically identify and connect eligible families with relevant government programmes.

## Health Schemes

### Chirayu Haryana (Ayushman Bharat – Chirayu Haryana)

The state's health insurance scheme providing **free treatment up to ₹5 lakh per family per year** at empanelled hospitals. It extends the Ayushman Bharat scheme to cover families with annual income up to ₹1,80,000.

**Key Benefits:**
- Cashless treatment at 600+ empanelled hospitals
- Covers hospitalization, surgery, and day-care procedures
- Family ID (PPP) used for automatic eligibility verification
- Covers pre and post-hospitalization expenses

**How to check eligibility:** Visit [Chirayu Haryana portal](https://chirayuharyana.gov.in) or use your Parivar Pehchan Patra number.

Also read: [Ayushman Bharat Health Card Guide](/guide/ayushman-bharat-health-card)

## Housing Schemes

### Deen Dayal Jan Awas Yojana

Affordable housing scheme providing plots and built-up houses to economically weaker sections at subsidized rates in urban areas of Haryana.

**Key Benefits:**
- Affordable plots (ranging from 50-150 sq. yards) at below-market rates
- Developed colonies with basic infrastructure
- Priority for BPL families, SC/ST, and senior citizens
- EMI-based payment options available
- Applied through the Town & Country Planning Department

### Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) – Haryana

Central housing scheme implemented in Haryana for both urban and rural beneficiaries. Read more: [Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana Guide](/guide/pm-awas-yojana-apply)

## Agriculture Schemes

### Meri Fasal Mera Byora

An essential portal for Haryana farmers to **register their crops** for MSP procurement, insurance, and subsidy benefits. Registration is mandatory for selling crops at government mandis at MSP.

**Key Benefits:**
- Mandatory for MSP procurement at government mandis
- Links to crop insurance and subsidy schemes
- Real-time information on mandi rates
- Portal: [fasal.haryana.gov.in](https://fasal.haryana.gov.in)

### Bhavantar Bharpayee Yojana

A price deficiency payment scheme for **horticultural crops**. If market prices fall below the protected price, the government compensates farmers for the difference.

**Key Benefits:**
- Covers vegetables and fruits (tomato, onion, potato, etc.)
- Direct compensation for price difference via DBT
- Registration through Meri Fasal Mera Byora portal
- Encourages diversification from wheat-paddy cycle

### Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) in Haryana

₹6,000/year in 3 instalments for all farmer families. Applied through Parivar Pehchan Patra integration.

Also read: [PM Kisan Samman Nidhi Guide](/guide/pm-kisan-samman-nidhi)

## Food & Ration Services

### Ration Card Services through PPP

Haryana has integrated ration card services with the Parivar Pehchan Patra system. Eligible families receive subsidised food grains under the National Food Security Act.

**Key Benefits:**
- Automatic identification of eligible families through Family ID
- Subsidized wheat, rice, sugar, and kerosene
- Apply/update ration card through Saral Haryana portal
- eKYC verification through Aadhaar-linked PPP

## Jobs & Employment Schemes

### Haryana Kaushal Rozgar Nigam (HKRN)

A major reform replacing the outsourcing/contract worker system. All contractual hiring by the Haryana government is now done transparently through the **HKRN portal** on a merit basis.

**Key Benefits:**
- Transparent, merit-based contractual recruitment
- Online application for government contractual posts
- EPF and ESI benefits for contractual workers
- Portal: [hkrnl.itiharyana.gov.in](https://hkrnl.itiharyana.gov.in)

### Saksham Yuva Yojana

Unemployment allowance of ₹3,000/month (graduates) or ₹1,500/month (10+2 pass) for youth registered on the employment exchange, along with job matching support.

### Haryana Police Constable Recruitment

Regular recruitment drives for police constable and SI positions through HSSC.

## How to Apply for Haryana Government Schemes

Most Haryana government schemes follow a unified application process:

1. **Get your Parivar Pehchan Patra (Family ID):** This is the first step — almost all schemes require it. [Apply here](/guide/haryana-family-id-parivar-pehchan-patra).

2. **Visit Saral Haryana portal:** Go to [saralharyana.gov.in](https://saralharyana.gov.in) and register/login using your Family ID.

3. **Search for the scheme:** Use the search function or browse by department to find the relevant scheme.

4. **Fill the application:** Most details auto-populate from your Family ID. Upload required documents (Aadhaar, income proof, etc.).

5. **Track your application:** Use the Saral Haryana tracking feature or visit your nearest **Atal Seva Kendra** for assistance.

6. **Grievance redressal:** If your application is delayed or rejected, file a complaint on [CM Window](https://cmwindow.haryana.gov.in).

💡 **Tip:** If you're not comfortable with online applications, visit your nearest **Atal Seva Kendra** (Common Service Centre) where operators can help you apply for any scheme for a nominal fee.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is Parivar Pehchan Patra and is it mandatory?

Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP) is Haryana's unique Family ID — an 8-digit number assigned to every family. Yes, it is now mandatory for applying to almost all government schemes, pensions, scholarships, and ration card services in Haryana. [Learn more](/guide/haryana-family-id-parivar-pehchan-patra).

### How can I check my eligibility for Haryana government schemes?

Login to [Saral Haryana](https://saralharyana.gov.in) with your Family ID. The portal shows schemes you're eligible for based on your family data. You can also visit an Atal Seva Kendra for in-person assistance.

### What is CM Window and how do I file a grievance?

CM Window ([cmwindow.haryana.gov.in](https://cmwindow.haryana.gov.in)) is the Chief Minister's grievance redressal portal. You can file complaints about delayed services, corruption, or any government-related issue. Complaints are monitored at the CM level with mandated resolution timelines.

### How do I apply for Haryana pension schemes (old age, widow, disability)?

All pension applications are submitted through Saral Haryana using your Parivar Pehchan Patra. Eligibility is auto-verified. Visit [Old Age Samman Allowance guide](/guide/haryana-old-age-samman-allowance) for detailed steps.

### What is the difference between Saral Haryana and e-Disha?

**Saral Haryana** is the comprehensive online portal for 500+ government services and scheme applications. **e-Disha** centres are physical offices specifically for issuing certificates (caste, income, domicile, etc.). Many e-Disha services are now also available on Saral Haryana.

### How do farmers register for MSP procurement in Haryana?

Farmers must register their crops on the **Meri Fasal Mera Byora** portal ([fasal.haryana.gov.in](https://fasal.haryana.gov.in)) before the procurement season. Registration requires Parivar Pehchan Patra and land ownership details (Jamabandi).
