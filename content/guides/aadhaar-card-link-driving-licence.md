# Link Aadhaar with Driving Licence: How to Link Online

Linking your [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) with your driving licence (DL) helps create a unified identity database and enables easier verification during traffic checks. The process is **voluntary** and can be done online through the Parivahan portal or mParivahan app, or by visiting your RTO office.

## Why Link Aadhaar with Driving Licence?

- **Digital verification** — Traffic police can verify your DL instantly via Aadhaar
- **DigiLocker integration** — Access your DL digitally linked to Aadhaar
- **Address update** — Easier to update DL address when Aadhaar is linked
- **Reduce fraud** — Prevents duplicate or fake driving licences
- **Unified database** — Helps government maintain accurate transport records
- **Online services** — Easier access to DL renewal and other services

## Documents Required

- Driving Licence (DL number)
- Aadhaar card (12-digit number)
- Mobile number registered with Aadhaar (for OTP)
- Vehicle registration details (optional, for linking RC too)

## Method 1: Link via Parivahan Portal (Online)

1. Visit [parivahan.gov.in](https://parivahan.gov.in/) or [sarathi.parivahan.gov.in](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/)
2. Select your **state** from the dropdown
3. Click on **"DL Services"** → **"Link Aadhaar with DL"** or **"Update Aadhaar in DL"**
4. Enter your **Driving Licence number** and **date of birth**
5. Enter your **12-digit Aadhaar number**
6. An OTP is sent to your Aadhaar-registered mobile number
7. Enter the OTP and submit
8. Your Aadhaar is linked with DL — confirmation is displayed

> **Note:** The exact menu options may vary by state as Sarathi portal has state-specific interfaces.

## Method 2: Link via mParivahan App

1. Download the **mParivahan app** from Google Play Store or Apple App Store
2. Open the app and tap **"Create Profile"** or **"Link Aadhaar"**
3. Enter your **Driving Licence number**
4. Enter your **Aadhaar number**
5. Verify via OTP sent to your Aadhaar-registered mobile
6. Your DL is linked to Aadhaar within the app
7. You can now access your **virtual DL** linked to Aadhaar

The mParivahan app also allows you to keep a digital copy of your DL, which is accepted by traffic police in most states.

## Method 3: Link at RTO Office (Offline)

1. Visit your nearest **Regional Transport Office (RTO)**
2. Carry your original Aadhaar card and Driving Licence
3. Fill the **Aadhaar linking form** (available at RTO or download from Parivahan portal)
4. Submit the form with self-attested photocopies of Aadhaar and DL
5. Biometric verification may be done at select RTOs
6. The RTO updates the record — linking is completed within 7-15 days

## Method 4: Link via DigiLocker

1. Log in to [digilocker.gov.in](https://www.digilocker.gov.in/) using your Aadhaar
2. Go to **"Issued Documents"** → Search for **"Driving Licence"**
3. Select **Ministry of Road Transport** as the issuer
4. Enter your DL number and other details
5. Your DL is fetched and linked to your Aadhaar-based DigiLocker account

> DigiLocker linking creates a verified digital copy of your DL associated with your Aadhaar. This digital DL is legally valid under IT Act 2000.

## Is Aadhaar-DL Linking Mandatory?

**No, it is currently voluntary.** The government has encouraged linking for better database management and digital governance, but it is not mandatory. You will not face penalties for not linking.

However, linking provides several practical benefits including digital DL access and faster verification during traffic stops.

- **Parivahan Portal:** Instant to 48 hours
- **mParivahan App:** Instant
- **RTO Office:** 7-15 working days
- **DigiLocker:** Instant

## Important Tips

1. Ensure your **name and date of birth match** in both Aadhaar and DL — mismatches will cause linking failure
2. The mParivahan app digital DL is **accepted by traffic police** in most states — you don't need to carry the physical DL
3. If the Parivahan portal shows an error, try during off-peak hours (early morning or late evening)
4. Link your [vehicle RC (Registration Certificate)](/guide/aadhaar-card-link-driving-licence) as well for complete digital transport records
5. Keep your Aadhaar [address updated](/guide/aadhaar-address-change-online) — it helps if you need to transfer DL to another state

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it compulsory to link Aadhaar with driving licence?

No, linking Aadhaar with driving licence is voluntary as of now. The government encourages it for better record management but there is no penalty for not linking.

### Can I link Aadhaar with a learner's licence?

Yes, you can link Aadhaar with both learner's licence and permanent driving licence through the Parivahan portal or mParivahan app.

### What if my name is different in Aadhaar and DL?

If there is a name mismatch, the linking may fail. Update either your [Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) or driving licence to ensure names match before attempting to link.

### Is the digital DL on mParivahan legally valid?

Yes, the digital driving licence on mParivahan app is legally valid as per the Motor Vehicles Act and IT Act 2000. Traffic police across most states accept the digital DL during checks.

### Can I link Aadhaar with DL from any state?

Yes, the Parivahan portal and mParivahan app support DL linking across all states and UTs in India, as driving licence data is centralized under the Sarathi platform.

### How do I check if my Aadhaar is linked with DL?

Log in to the Parivahan portal or mParivahan app and view your DL details. If Aadhaar is linked, it will show the masked Aadhaar number in your DL record.
