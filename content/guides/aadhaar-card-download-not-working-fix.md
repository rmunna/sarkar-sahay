## Why Is Your Aadhaar Card Download Not Working?

Downloading e-Aadhaar from the UIDAI portal should be simple, but many users face errors — from OTP failures to PDF password issues. This troubleshooting guide covers every common problem and its fix so you can download your Aadhaar successfully.

> **Quick Tip:** The official portal for Aadhaar download is now **myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in** (not the older resident.uidai.gov.in). Always use the new portal.

---

## Common Errors When Downloading Aadhaar

| # | Error / Problem | Likely Cause |
|---|----------------|--------------|
| 1 | "Invalid Aadhaar Number" | Typo or entering VID in Aadhaar field |
| 2 | OTP not received on mobile | Unregistered mobile, network issue, or DND |
| 3 | "Invalid VID" or VID expired | VID was regenerated or never created |
| 4 | TOTP not working | Time sync issue or expired TOTP |
| 5 | Website shows error / won't load | Server downtime or browser cache |
| 6 | PDF downloaded but won't open | Wrong password entered |
| 7 | "Technical Error, Please try again" | UIDAI server overload |
| 8 | mAadhaar app download fails | App version outdated or device issue |

## Fix for Each Error

### 1. "Invalid Aadhaar Number" Error

- **Double-check** your 12-digit Aadhaar number — no spaces, no extra digits.
- Do **not** enter your VID (16 digits) in the Aadhaar number field. Use the VID option separately.
- If your Aadhaar was recently enrolled, wait **72 hours** before attempting download.
- Verify your Aadhaar number at [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in) → "Verify Aadhaar."

### 2. OTP Not Received on Mobile

This is the most common issue. Quick fixes:

1. Ensure your **mobile number is registered** with Aadhaar (check via "Verify Aadhaar").
2. Wait at least **2-3 minutes** — OTP delivery can be delayed.
3. Check if **DND (Do Not Disturb)** is active on your number — disable it via your telecom provider.
4. Ensure you have proper **network signal** — try switching between Wi-Fi and mobile data.
5. Don't request OTP more than **3 times in 24 hours** — you may get temporarily blocked.
6. If your mobile number is not linked, visit your nearest [Aadhaar Enrolment Centre](/guide/find-nearest-aadhaar-centre-google-maps) to update it.

> 📖 **Detailed guide:** [Aadhaar OTP Not Coming? 8 Quick Fixes](/guide/aadhaar-otp-not-received-fix)

### 3. "Invalid VID" or VID Expired

- VID (Virtual ID) is a **temporary 16-digit number** that maps to your Aadhaar.
- If you regenerated your VID, the **old VID becomes invalid** immediately.
- Generate a fresh VID: Go to [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in) → Login → "VID Generator."
- Alternatively, **use your Aadhaar number** directly instead of VID for download.

### 4. TOTP Not Working

TOTP (Time-based OTP) is generated via the **mAadhaar app** and is valid for only **30 seconds**.

- Ensure your phone's **date & time is set to automatic** (time sync is critical).
- Open mAadhaar → Generate TOTP → enter it on the website **within 30 seconds**.
- If it keeps failing, switch to **regular OTP** instead.

### 5. UIDAI Website Not Loading / Technical Error

- **Try after some time** — UIDAI servers face heavy load during peak hours (10 AM – 2 PM).
- **Clear browser cache** and cookies, or try in **incognito/private mode**.
- Use **Google Chrome or Firefox** (latest version).
- Disable browser extensions like ad blockers that might interfere.
- Check if the site is down for everyone at [downdetector.in](https://downdetector.in).

### 6. Password-Protected Aadhaar PDF Won't Open

The e-Aadhaar PDF is password-protected. The password is:

> **First 4 letters of your name (UPPERCASE) + Year of birth (YYYY)**

**Example:** If your name is "Rajesh Kumar" and birth year is 1990, the password is: `RAJE1990`

**Rules for the password:**
- Name must be in **UPPERCASE** as printed on Aadhaar
- Use the **first 4 letters** of your first name
- If your name has fewer than 4 letters, use all available letters
- Birth year must be in **4-digit format** (e.g., 1990, not 90)
- **No spaces** between name and year

**Still not working?**
- Check the exact spelling of your name on Aadhaar — it may differ from what you expect.
- Try with and without special characters if your name has them.
- Use **Adobe Acrobat Reader** — some PDF viewers don't handle password-protected PDFs well.

### 7. "Technical Error, Please Try Again" Message

- This is usually a **server-side issue**. Wait 15-30 minutes and retry.
- Don't refresh repeatedly — it may lock your session.
- Try during **off-peak hours** (early morning or late evening).

### 8. mAadhaar App Errors

- Update to the **latest version** from Google Play Store or Apple App Store.
- The app requires your **mobile number to be registered** with Aadhaar.
- Clear app cache: Settings → Apps → mAadhaar → Clear Cache.
- If the app crashes on login, **uninstall and reinstall**.
- mAadhaar supports only **one Aadhaar profile per device**.

## How to Download Aadhaar Card Online (Step-by-Step)

Follow these steps on the official portal:

1. Go to **[myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in)**
2. Click **"Login"** and enter your **12-digit Aadhaar number**
3. Enter the **captcha** and click **"Send OTP"**
4. Enter the **OTP** received on your registered mobile number
5. After login, click **"Download Aadhaar"**
6. Choose between **Aadhaar number** or **VID** as identifier
7. Click **"Download"** — the PDF will be saved to your device
8. Open the PDF with the **password** (first 4 letters of name in caps + birth year)

## Alternative: Download Aadhaar via mAadhaar App

If the website isn't working, use the official **mAadhaar app**:

1. Download **mAadhaar** from [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=in.gov.uidai.mAadhaarPlus) or Apple App Store
2. Set a **4-digit PIN** on first launch
3. Enter your **Aadhaar number** and verify with **OTP**
4. Your Aadhaar profile loads — tap **"Download Aadhaar"** or **"View Aadhaar"**
5. You can also **share the PDF** directly from the app

**mAadhaar Advantages:**
- Works when the website is down
- Supports **TOTP generation** (no need to wait for SMS OTP)
- Biometric lock/unlock from your phone
- Offline Aadhaar access once downloaded

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### How many times can I download e-Aadhaar in a day?

There is no strict limit on downloads, but UIDAI may temporarily restrict if you make **too many OTP requests** (usually more than 3-5 in a short period). Wait a few hours and try again.

### Can I download Aadhaar without a registered mobile number?

**No.** You need a registered mobile number to receive the OTP. If your number isn't linked, visit your nearest [Aadhaar Enrolment Centre](/guide/find-nearest-aadhaar-centre-google-maps) to update your mobile number. You can also use the **Aadhaar Enrolment ID (EID)** method if you have your enrolment slip.

### Is e-Aadhaar valid as ID proof?

**Yes.** e-Aadhaar (the digitally signed PDF) is legally valid and accepted for all purposes — [bank KYC](/guide/aadhaar-linking-guide), government schemes, SIM activation, passport application, and more.

### What if my name or details are wrong on the downloaded Aadhaar?

You need to update your Aadhaar details first. Follow our guide on [Aadhaar Name Correction Online](/guide/aadhaar-name-correction-online) or [Aadhaar Address Change Online](/guide/aadhaar-address-change-online), then download again after the update is processed.

### Can I download someone else's Aadhaar card?

**No.** You can only download Aadhaar for a number that has **your registered mobile number** linked to it. The OTP is sent to the registered mobile, so you must have access to that phone. For children's Aadhaar, the parent's linked mobile receives the OTP — see our [Baal Aadhaar guide](/guide/baal-aadhaar-child-aadhaar-card).

### How do I check if my mobile number is linked to Aadhaar?

Go to [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in) → "Verify Aadhaar" → Enter your Aadhaar number. It will show a **masked mobile number** if one is linked. You can also check via [Aadhaar Update Mobile & Email guide](/guide/aadhaar-update-mobile-email).

### Why does the Aadhaar download page keep showing captcha errors?

Captcha issues usually happen due to **browser cache or slow internet**. Try clearing cache, using incognito mode, or switching browsers. If the captcha image doesn't load, refresh the page — don't try to guess a blank captcha.
