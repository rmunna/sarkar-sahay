# SIP Pause, Cancel, and Amount Change India 2026 — Platform-by-Platform Guide

SIP modifications are straightforward but platform-specific. Here's exactly what to do on each major platform.

---

## Pause vs Cancel vs Stop — What's the Difference?

| Action | What Happens | Units You Hold | Duration |
|--------|-------------|---------------|---------|
| **Pause** | New installments stop temporarily | Continue to grow | 1–6 months (platform dependent) |
| **Cancel** | New installments stop permanently | Continue to grow until you redeem | Permanent |
| **Redeem** | Sell existing units for cash | Units converted to cash | Immediate |

**Key point**: Pausing or cancelling a SIP does NOT sell your existing units. Your accumulated units continue to grow at market rate. You're just stopping new investments.

---

## How to Pause SIP

### Groww

1. Open Groww app → Portfolio → SIPs
2. Select the SIP you want to pause
3. Tap "Pause SIP"
4. Choose pause duration: 1, 2, 3, 4, or 6 months
5. Confirm

The SIP automatically resumes after the selected pause period.

**Groww pause limit**: Maximum 6 months. After that, you'll need to cancel and create a new SIP.

### Zerodha (Coin)

Zerodha Coin doesn't have a "pause" option as of current version. To effectively pause:
1. Cancel the existing SIP (instructions below)
2. Create a new SIP when you're ready to resume

### MF Central / AMC Direct

1. Login at mfcentral.com or the AMC's direct portal
2. Go to SIP section → select your SIP
3. Look for "Modify SIP" → some AMCs support pause (typically 1–3 months)
4. Process varies by AMC — not all support pause

---

## How to Cancel SIP

### Groww

1. Groww app → Portfolio → SIPs tab
2. Select the SIP
3. Tap "Cancel SIP"
4. Confirm cancellation

Cancellation takes **2–3 business days** to process. Installments already in the queue (within 5–7 days) may still debit.

### Zerodha (Coin)

1. Coin app → SIPs → Select SIP
2. Tap "Stop SIP" or "Cancel SIP"
3. Confirm

### Angel One / Paytm Money / ET Money

Similar flow — go to SIP details page → Stop/Cancel SIP → Confirm.

### MF Central (For Direct Plan SIPs)

1. mfcentral.com → Login → SIP dashboard
2. Select SIP → Modify → Cancel
3. OTP verification required

### Via AMC Website Directly

If you invested directly through an AMC (HDFC MF, Nippon, etc.):
1. Login to AMC's website
2. Go to SIP Management or Transactions
3. Select SIP → Terminate/Cancel
4. Physical letter method (old policies): Submit form to AMC branch — takes 3–5 working days

## Important: Cancel Before the Debit Date

If your SIP date is on the 5th and you cancel on the 3rd, the installment may still debit (bank mandate takes 2–5 days to cancel). To avoid an extra installment, **cancel at least 7 days before the next SIP date**.

## How to Increase or Change SIP Amount

You **cannot directly edit** an existing SIP amount on most platforms. Options:

### Option 1: Cancel + New SIP (Most Common)

1. Cancel the existing SIP
2. Create a new SIP for the higher amount in the same fund
3. New mandate request via UPI

**Limitation**: A new SIP starts a fresh mandate, and the existing units' purchase dates remain unchanged (for LTCG tracking).

### Option 2: Add a Second SIP in Same Fund

Instead of cancelling, you can simply start an **additional SIP** in the same fund.

- Existing SIP: ₹5,000/month
- New SIP (same fund): ₹2,000/month
- Total: ₹7,000/month (two installments — two separate SIP IDs)

This preserves the purchase dates of older units. Slightly messier to track, but functionally identical.

### Option 3: Step-Up SIP (Pre-Set Annual Increase)

When creating a new SIP, set a step-up percentage (10%, 15%, 20%) to automatically increase yearly. Groww and Coin support this during SIP creation. [See our step-up SIP guide](/guide/step-up-sip-calculator-how-it-works-india).

## How to Change SIP Date

Most platforms don't allow direct date change. Process:

1. Cancel existing SIP
2. Create new SIP with the date you want

Wait for existing SIP cancellation to reflect (2–3 days) before creating the new one, to avoid confusion.

**Common SIP dates and their implications**:
- 1st, 3rd, 5th: Beginning of month — captures interest well; no bank balance risk if salary is delayed
- 10th, 15th, 20th: Mid-month — most popular; salary reliably received by then
- 25th, 28th: Month-end — risk if salary delayed; also gets slightly different NAV

No SIP date is significantly better or worse for long-term returns — pick what works with your salary credit date.

## How to Change Bank Account for SIP

If you're changing your bank account:

1. **Add new bank** to broker account (Settings → Bank Accounts → Add)
2. **Set new bank as primary** (if platform allows)
3. **Cancel existing SIP** (tied to old bank mandate)
4. **Create new SIP** with the new bank selected for mandate

The mandate (auto-debit permission) is bank-specific — it cannot be transferred. A new mandate needs to be created for the new bank.

**NPCI mandate portability**: As of 2026, NPCI has introduced mandate portability for UPI AutoPay — you can migrate an existing UPI mandate to a new bank without recreating it. Check if your broker supports this.

## Exit Load After Cancelling SIP — Will I be Charged?

**Cancelling SIP**: No charge. Zero exit load for the cancellation action itself.

**When you redeem units** (sell):
- Units held more than 1 year → No exit load (for most equity funds)
- Units held less than 1 year → Exit load typically 1% (for most equity funds)

Exit load is on the units you sell, not on the SIP itself. Your existing units accumulate without any exit load being charged until you redeem.

---

## What Happens to Units After SIP is Cancelled?

Nothing changes with existing units:
- They continue to be invested in the fund
- Market movements affect their value
- You can redeem them anytime
- They generate capital gains based on their individual purchase dates

Cancelling SIP = stopping new investments. Existing investments continue untouched.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I pause SIP without penalty?

Yes — pausing or cancelling SIP has zero penalty. The existing units you hold remain in the fund. No fees are charged for pausing or cancelling.

### My SIP debited even after cancellation. What happened?

Cancellation takes 2–5 days to process through the mandate system. If your cancellation and next debit date were close together, the debit may have gone through. Contact your broker — the installment will be invested (not lost). You can redeem it if you don't want the investment.

### Can I have multiple SIPs in the same mutual fund?

Yes — you can have multiple SIP registrations in the same fund (from same or different platforms). Each SIP tracks its own installments separately for LTCG purposes.

### If I cancel SIP, do I lose my returns?

No — cancelling SIP only stops future investments. All existing units remain in the fund, earning returns. To "lose" returns, you'd have to actually redeem (sell) the units.

### How to change HDFC Bank SIP to SBI if I changed my salary account?

1. Add SBI account to your broker (Zerodha/Groww) via Settings → Bank
2. Cancel the existing HDFC mandate-linked SIP
3. Create a new SIP, selecting SBI as the debit bank
4. New UPI mandate request will go to your SBI UPI ID
