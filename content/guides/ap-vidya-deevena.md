# Vidya Deevena – Fee Reimbursement for Students in Andhra Pradesh

Jagananna Vidya Deevena is a flagship education scheme of the Government of Andhra Pradesh that provides complete fee reimbursement for eligible students pursuing higher education. The scheme covers tuition fees, hostel fees, and examination fees for students from economically weaker families.

## What is Vidya Deevena?

Under Vidya Deevena, the AP government reimburses the entire college/university fees for students whose families earn below the income threshold. The fee amount is paid directly to the educational institution on behalf of the student, ensuring that no deserving student drops out due to financial constraints.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Residency:** Must be a domicile of Andhra Pradesh
2. **Income limit:** Annual family income should not exceed ₹2,50,000
3. **Courses covered:** Degree, engineering, medical, MBA, MCA, pharmacy, and other professional courses in government, aided, and recognized private colleges
4. **Fresh admissions:** Students admitted through EAMCET, ICET, ECET, PGECET, or university-level counseling
5. **Attendance:** Must maintain minimum 75% attendance
6. **Exclusions:**
   - Students in deemed/autonomous universities charging capitation fees
   - Students who have failed and are repeating the same year
   - Government employee families (certain categories)

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar card of student and parent (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- Income certificate (annual income below ₹2,50,000)
- Caste/community certificate
- College admission letter / bonafide certificate
- Fee structure from college
- Previous academic mark sheets
- Bank passbook of mother (fees are linked to mother's account)
- Ration card
- SSC / Intermediate certificates

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Online Application (via Jnanabhumi Portal)

1. **Visit Jnanabhumi portal:** Go to [jnanabhumi.ap.gov.in](https://jnanabhumi.ap.gov.in/)
2. **Student registration:** Register as a new student using Aadhaar and mobile number
3. **Login:** Log in with your credentials
4. **Select Vidya Deevena:** Choose the Vidya Deevena scheme
5. **Fill application:** Enter personal details, educational details, family income, and course information
6. **Upload documents:** Upload scanned copies of all required documents
7. **Submit:** Review and submit the application
8. **College verification:** Your college will verify the application
9. **District verification:** District education officials verify eligibility
10. **Fee reimbursement:** Approved fee amount is paid directly to the college

### Through College

1. **Inform college:** At the time of admission, inform the college about Vidya Deevena eligibility
2. **Submit documents:** Provide all required documents to the college office
3. **College uploads data:** The college uploads your details to the Jnanabhumi portal
4. **Verification:** Online and offline verification is completed
5. **Fees paid to college:** Government pays the fees directly to the institution

## Processing Time

- **Application submission:** At the start of the academic year
- **Verification:** 30–60 days
- **Fee disbursement to college:** Typically in 2–3 installments during the academic year

## Important Tips

1. **Apply immediately after admission:** Don't delay — apply as soon as you get admission
2. **Mother's bank account:** The scheme is linked to the mother's Aadhaar and bank account
3. **Maintain attendance:** Below 75% attendance can lead to disqualification
4. **Check Jnanabhumi regularly:** Track your application status on the portal
5. **No middlemen:** Apply directly through the portal or college — no fees for application

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: What is the income limit for Vidya Deevena?
Annual family income should not exceed ₹2,50,000 for eligibility.

### Q2: Are private college students eligible?
Yes, students in recognized private colleges admitted through government counseling (EAMCET, ICET, etc.) are eligible.

### Q3: Is the fee paid to the student or the college?
The fee is paid directly to the college/institution by the government. Students don't receive cash.

### Q4: Can I avail Vidya Deevena and Vasathi Deevena together?
Yes, eligible students can avail both Vidya Deevena (fee reimbursement) and Vasathi Deevena (maintenance/hostel allowance).

### Q5: What happens if I fail an exam?
If you fail and repeat a year, fee reimbursement may not be available for the repeated year. Pass all subjects to continue receiving benefits.

### Q6: Is this scheme available for PG courses?
Yes, postgraduate courses like MBA, MCA, M.Tech, and other PG programs are covered.

### Q7: How do I check my application status?
Login to [jnanabhumi.ap.gov.in](https://jnanabhumi.ap.gov.in/) and check under the Vidya Deevena section.
