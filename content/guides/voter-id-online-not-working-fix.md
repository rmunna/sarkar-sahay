# Voters.eci.gov.in Not Working? How to Fix Portal Issues

The [Election Commission of India](https://www.eci.gov.in/) portal at voters.eci.gov.in is the primary website for voter registration, corrections, and electoral roll searches. If the portal is not working for you, this guide covers all common issues and their fixes, plus alternative methods to complete your voter ID tasks.

## Common Issues with Voters.eci.gov.in

Users frequently face these problems when accessing the ECI voter portal:

1. **Server errors** (502 Bad Gateway, 503 Service Unavailable)
2. **Page not loading** or blank white screen
3. **OTP not received** on registered mobile number
4. **Login failures** after entering correct credentials
5. **Form submission errors** or timeout during application
6. **Captcha not loading** or not accepting input
7. **PDF download failures** for e-EPIC

---

## How to Fix Voters.eci.gov.in Not Working

### Fix 1: Check if the Server is Down

The ECI portal experiences heavy traffic during election seasons, voter list revision periods, and registration deadlines. Server downtime is common during these peaks.

**What to do:**
- Wait 30-60 minutes and try again
- Check during off-peak hours (early morning 6-8 AM or late night after 10 PM)
- During election season, the portal may be slow — this is expected

### Fix 2: Clear Browser Cache and Cookies

Old cached data can cause loading issues with the updated portal.

**Steps:**
1. Open browser settings (Chrome: Ctrl+Shift+Delete)
2. Select "Cached images and files" and "Cookies"
3. Set time range to "All time"
4. Click "Clear data"
5. Restart browser and visit [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in/) again

### Fix 3: Use a Compatible Browser

The ECI portal works best with updated browsers.

**Recommended browsers:**
- Google Chrome (latest version)
- Mozilla Firefox (latest version)
- Microsoft Edge (latest version)

**Avoid:**
- Internet Explorer (not supported)
- Very old browser versions
- Mini/Lite browser versions on mobile

### Fix 4: Disable VPN or Proxy

VPN and proxy connections can block the portal or cause geo-restriction issues.

1. Disable any active VPN connection
2. Turn off proxy settings in your browser
3. Use a direct internet connection
4. Try switching between Wi-Fi and mobile data

### Fix 5: Fix OTP Not Received Issue

If you are not receiving OTP from the voter portal:

1. **Check mobile number** — ensure it matches the one registered with your voter ID
2. **Wait 2-3 minutes** — OTPs may be delayed during high traffic
3. **Check SMS filters** — look in spam/blocked messages folder
4. **Ensure network signal** — poor signal can delay SMS delivery
5. **Try "Resend OTP"** — use the resend option after the timer expires (usually 60 seconds)
6. **Try after some time** — the SMS gateway may be temporarily overloaded

### Fix 6: Try HTTPS and Direct URL

Sometimes typing the URL incorrectly or using HTTP causes issues.

- Use the exact URL: **https://voters.eci.gov.in/**
- Do not use http:// (without 's')
- Clear any bookmarks to old NVSP URLs and use the new portal

## Alternative Methods When Portal is Down

If voters.eci.gov.in is not working, you can complete most voter ID tasks through these alternatives:

### 1. Voter Helpline App

Download the **Voter Helpline** app from [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.eci.citizen) or Apple App Store. The app allows you to:
- Register as a new voter (Form 6)
- Apply for corrections (Form 8)
- Search your name in the voter list
- Track application status

### 2. Call Helpline 1950

The **Election Commission toll-free helpline 1950** is available for:
- Voter registration queries
- Application status checks
- Complaint registration
- General voter ID information

**Timing:** Available during office hours (Monday to Saturday, 10 AM to 5 PM)

### 3. Visit the ERO/BLO Office

You can apply offline by visiting your local:
- **Electoral Registration Officer (ERO)** office
- **Booth Level Officer (BLO)** — your local area BLO can help with registration and corrections
- **District Election Office**

Carry the required documents and passport-size photographs.

### 4. Common Service Centres (CSC)

Visit your nearest **Common Service Centre (CSC)** or Jan Seva Kendra. They can submit voter ID applications on your behalf for a small service fee.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. Why is voters.eci.gov.in not opening?

The portal may be down due to server maintenance, heavy traffic during election season, or internet connectivity issues on your end. Try clearing cache, using a different browser, or accessing during off-peak hours.

### Q2. What is the old NVSP portal? Is it still working?

The National Voter Service Portal (NVSP) at nvsp.in has been merged into voters.eci.gov.in. If you try to access nvsp.in, it should redirect to the new portal. Use voters.eci.gov.in for all voter services.

### Q3. I am not receiving OTP on voters.eci.gov.in. What should I do?

Check if your mobile number is correctly registered with your voter ID. Wait 2-3 minutes for delayed OTPs, check spam/blocked messages, and ensure good network signal. If OTP still fails, try the Voter Helpline App or call 1950.

### Q4. Can I apply for voter ID without the online portal?

Yes. You can apply offline by visiting your local ERO/BLO office or a Common Service Centre (CSC). Carry required documents like age proof, address proof, and passport-size photographs.

### Q5. What is the helpline number for voter ID issues?

Call **1950** (toll-free) for all voter ID related queries including registration, corrections, and portal issues. Available during office hours, Monday to Saturday.

### Q6. Is the Voter Helpline App a reliable alternative?

Yes. The Voter Helpline App is the official ECI app and supports most services available on the web portal including new registration, corrections, voter list search, and complaint filing.

### Q7. Does voters.eci.gov.in work on mobile browsers?

Yes, the portal is mobile-responsive. Use Chrome or Firefox on your mobile with a stable internet connection. If it doesn't load, try the Voter Helpline App instead.
