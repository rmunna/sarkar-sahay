## What is PM Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY)?

Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana is a **government-backed life insurance scheme** launched on **9th May 2015** that provides a **₹2 lakh life insurance cover** for a premium of just **₹436 per year** (revised from ₹330 w.e.f. June 2022). The premium is auto-debited from your bank account annually.

In case of the policyholder's death (due to any cause), the nominee receives **₹2,00,000** as a lump sum.

> **Why it matters:** This is the cheapest life insurance available in India — ideal for low and middle-income families who cannot afford regular insurance premiums.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Age:** 18 to 50 years (policy coverage continues till age 55)
- **Bank account holder** in any participating bank
- **Aadhaar linked** to the bank account
- **One policy per person** (even if you have multiple bank accounts)
- **No medical examination** required
- Available to all Indian citizens regardless of income

## Coverage Details

| Parameter | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Sum Assured** | ₹2,00,000 |
| **Premium** | ₹436/year (auto-debit from bank account) |
| **Coverage Period** | 1st June to 31st May each year |
| **Risk Coverage** | Death due to **any cause** (natural or accidental) |
| **Entry Age** | 18–50 years |
| **Exit Age** | 55 years |
| **Renewal** | Annual (auto-renewed unless opted out) |

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar Card** (must be linked to bank account)
- **Bank account passbook**
- **Nomination form** (provided by bank)
- **Consent-cum-declaration form** (self-declaration of good health)
- **Mobile number** linked to bank account

## How to Enrol — Step-by-Step

### Through Your Bank (Recommended)

#### Step 1: Visit Your Bank Branch
- Go to your **savings account bank branch**
- Ask for the **PMJJBY enrolment form**

#### Step 2: Fill the Form
- Fill in your **personal details** (name, DOB, Aadhaar, nominee details)
- Sign the **consent-cum-declaration form** (declaring good health)
- Provide **nominee details** (name, relationship, age)

#### Step 3: Auto-Debit Consent
- Give written consent for **annual auto-debit** of ₹436 from your account
- Ensure your account has **sufficient balance** (at least ₹436) before 31st May each year

#### Step 4: Confirmation
- The bank processes your enrolment
- You receive an **SMS confirmation** and certificate of insurance
- Coverage starts from **1st June** of that year

### Through Net Banking / Mobile Banking

1. Log in to your **bank's net banking or mobile app**
2. Navigate to **"Insurance"** or **"Social Security Schemes"** section
3. Select **"PMJJBY"**
4. Verify your details and **add nominee information**
5. Confirm **auto-debit consent**
6. Coverage is activated from the next coverage period

### Through CSC
- Visit your nearest **Common Service Centre**
- The CSC operator can help enrol you if your bank supports CSC enrolment

## Premium Payment

| Detail | Information |
|--------|------------|
| **Annual Premium** | ₹436 |
| **Debit Date** | Auto-debited on or before 31st May each year |
| **Grace Period** | If auto-debit fails, you can renrol by paying premium with a self-declaration of good health |
| **GST** | GST is included in the ₹436 premium |

> **Tip:** Ensure your bank account has at least ₹500 balance by end of May to avoid lapse of coverage.

## How to File a Claim

### Step 1: Inform the Bank
- The **nominee** must visit the bank branch where the deceased held the PMJJBY-linked account
- Inform them about the policyholder's death

### Step 2: Submit Documents
- **Death certificate** (from municipal authority)
- **Claim form** (provided by bank)
- **Nominee's Aadhaar and bank account details**
- **Cancelled cheque** of nominee's account (for electronic transfer)

### Step 3: Claim Processing
- The bank forwards the claim to the **insurance company** (LIC or other participating insurer)
- Claim is verified and processed
- **₹2,00,000 is transferred** to the nominee's bank account

### Processing Time
- **Claim settlement:** Typically within **30 days** of submitting all documents

## Important Tips

1. **Link Aadhaar to bank account** — this is mandatory for enrolment. Visit your bank if not done yet.
2. **Keep nominee details updated** — if your family situation changes (marriage, etc.), update the nominee at your bank.
3. **Maintain balance in May** — the premium is auto-debited around 31st May. If it fails, your coverage lapses.
4. **Combine with PMSBY** — for comprehensive coverage, also enrol in [PM Suraksha Bima Yojana](/guide/pm-suraksha-bima-yojana) (accident insurance at ₹20/year).
5. **Inform your family** — make sure your nominee knows about this policy so they can file a claim if needed.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Is PMJJBY same as PMSBY?
**No.** PMJJBY is a **life insurance** scheme (covers death due to any cause) costing ₹436/year. PMSBY (PM Suraksha Bima Yojana) is an **accident insurance** scheme (covers accidental death/disability) costing ₹20/year. You can enrol in both.

### Q2: What if I miss the premium payment?
If the auto-debit fails, your coverage **lapses**. You can **re-enrol** by paying the full annual premium and submitting a self-declaration of good health. There is no penalty for late enrolment within the year.

### Q3: Can I enrol after age 50?
**No.** The maximum entry age is 50 years. However, if you enrolled before 50, coverage continues until age **55** with annual premium payments.

### Q4: Is there a waiting period?
There is a **30-day waiting period** from the date of enrolment. Death within 30 days of first enrolment (except accidental death) is not covered.

### Q5: Can NRIs enrol in PMJJBY?
**Yes**, if they have a valid Indian bank account linked with Aadhaar. However, claim settlement for NRI nominees may involve additional documentation.

### Q6: Which insurance company provides the cover?
**LIC (Life Insurance Corporation of India)** is the primary insurer for most banks. Some banks may partner with other life insurance companies. The terms remain the same regardless.

### Q7: Can I have PMJJBY from multiple bank accounts?
**No.** Only one PMJJBY enrolment per person is allowed, regardless of how many bank accounts you hold.
