# Haryana Caste Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **caste certificate** in Haryana is issued by the Revenue Department (Tehsildar / Naib Tehsildar) and is required for BC/OBC/SC/ST reservations, Haryana government scholarships, government job applications (HPSC, HSSC), and welfare scheme eligibility. Applications go through the **Antyodaya Saral** portal — Haryana's integrated service delivery platform — with **Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP)** integration.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [saralharyana.gov.in](https://saralharyana.gov.in) with your **PPP family ID**. Issued by Tehsildar within **7–21 days**.

---

## Caste Categories in Haryana

| Category | Reservation % | Notes |
|----------|---------------|-------|
| **SC** | 20% | Scheduled Castes (Haryana state list) |
| **BC-A** | 16% | Backward Classes A (most backward communities) |
| **BC-B** | 11% | Backward Classes B (general backward communities) |
| **SBC** | 2% | Special Backward Classes (Jat community under Haryana Act) |
| **EWS** | 10% | General category, income < ₹8 lakh/year |

> Haryana has **separate BC-A and BC-B** sub-lists. Identify your community's correct sub-list before applying. The **Jat community** has a politically contentious SBC status — check current legal/judicial status before claiming SBC.

## Why You Need Haryana Caste Certificate

- **Haryana SC/BC post-matric scholarship** — income + caste proof required
- **NSP National Scholarship** — all categories
- **Government job reservation** — HPSC (Haryana PSC), HSSC (staff selection)
- **Haryana police / constable recruitment** — SC/BC reservation in recruitment
- **Central OBC NCL** — for UPSC/SSC/railways (if in central OBC list)
- **Antyodaya scheme eligibility** — welfare benefits

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory)
- **Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP) family ID** (mandatory for all Haryana services)
- **Ration card** (if available)
- **Father's / grandfather's caste proof** (school TC with caste, old ration card/voter ID)
- Self-declaration of caste
- **Voter ID** (address proof)
- Passport-size photograph

---

## How to Apply Online — Antyodaya Saral Portal

1. Go to [saralharyana.gov.in](https://saralharyana.gov.in)
2. Register / log in with mobile and Aadhaar
3. Click **"Services"** → search **"Caste Certificate"**
4. Select **"Revenue Department — Caste Certificate"**
5. Enter your **PPP family ID** — data auto-fills from PPP
6. Select category: **SC / BC-A / BC-B / SBC**
7. Upload documents (Aadhaar, father's TC, photograph)
8. Pay fee: **₹30** (online)
9. Submit → note **Service Request Number (SRN)**

---

## Apply via Antyodaya Saral Kendra / CSC

1. Visit nearest **Antyodaya Saral Kendra** (at every block/tehsil) or CSC
2. Carry Aadhaar, PPP card, father's caste proof
3. Operator logs in and submits caste certificate request
4. Service charge: approximately ₹30–60
5. Collect acknowledgement / SRN

---

## Status Check

1. Go to [saralharyana.gov.in](https://saralharyana.gov.in)
2. Click **"Track Application"** → enter SRN
3. Or log in → **"My Applications"** → view status
4. Status: Applied → Under Verification → Tehsildar Approved

## Download Haryana Caste Certificate

Once approved:
1. Log in to Saral portal → **"My Certificates"**
2. Download digitally signed PDF
3. Valid for all Haryana government purposes, NSP, and HPSC applications

## Frequently Asked Questions

### BC-A vs BC-B in Haryana — which is my community?

Haryana has two sub-lists. **BC-A** (more backward): Aheri, Nut, Bazigar, Majhabi Sikh, Rai Sikh, and other very backward communities. **BC-B** (backward): Ahir (Yadav), Mochi, Jogi, Dhobi, Nai (Barber), Bhambhi, and others. Check the **Haryana BC-A and BC-B lists** at your Tehsil office — applying to the wrong sub-category leads to rejection.

### Haryana BC certificate for UPSC — is it valid?

The **Haryana BC certificate** is for state-level benefits. For UPSC, SSC, railways, you need an **OBC NCL certificate** (issued at Haryana Saral portal). Your community must also be in the **central OBC list** (check [ncbc.nic.in](https://ncbc.nic.in)). Many Haryana BC communities (like Jats) are **not on the central OBC list**.

### Jat community — BC or SBC in Haryana?

Jats are categorized under **SBC (Special Backward Class)** in Haryana. However, this has been legally contested. Check the **current Supreme Court / Punjab & Haryana High Court** orders before applying as SBC. For central OBC, Jats are **not on the central OBC list** for Haryana.
