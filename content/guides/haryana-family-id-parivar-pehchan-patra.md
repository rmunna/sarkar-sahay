## What is Parivar Pehchan Patra (Family ID)?

Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP) is a unique 8-digit Family ID issued by the Government of Haryana to every family in the state. Launched in 2021, it serves as a single-point identity for the entire family, linking all members to a verified database. The scheme aims to ensure transparent and targeted delivery of government benefits, subsidies, and services.

Every family residing in Haryana — regardless of income, caste, or religion — is eligible for a Parivar Pehchan Patra. The Family ID has become mandatory for availing most Haryana government schemes, including old age pension, scholarships, ration cards, and employment services.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Any family residing in Haryana** can apply for Parivar Pehchan Patra
- No income, caste, or religion restriction
- Both rural and urban families are eligible
- Families must have at least one member with a valid Aadhaar card
- Single individuals living independently can also register as a single-member family
- Migrant workers living in Haryana for an extended period may also apply

## Documents Required

### Mandatory Documents
- **Aadhaar Card** of all family members (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) for more details)
- **Mobile number** linked to Aadhaar (for OTP verification)
- **Address proof** — Ration card, electricity bill, rent agreement, or voter ID

### Additional Documents (if applicable)
- **Marriage certificate** — to link spouse
- **Birth certificates** — for children
- **Death certificate** — to remove deceased member
- **Income certificate** — for scheme-specific benefits
- **Caste certificate** — if applying for reservation-based schemes

## Step-by-Step Registration Process

### Online Process

1. **Visit the official portal** at [meraparivar.haryana.gov.in](https://meraparivar.haryana.gov.in)
2. **Click on "New Family Registration"** or "Parivar Pehchan Patra" option
3. **Enter your Aadhaar number** and verify via OTP sent to your registered mobile
4. **Fill in family details** — head of family name, address, income, and other required fields
5. **Add family members** — enter Aadhaar numbers of each family member and verify
6. **Upload documents** — address proof, marriage certificate, etc., as applicable
7. **Review all information** carefully and submit the application
8. **Note your application reference number** for tracking purposes
9. **Family ID will be generated** after verification — typically within 15-30 days

### Offline Process (via CSC / Atal Seva Kendra)

1. **Visit your nearest CSC** (Common Service Centre) or Atal Seva Kendra
2. **Carry original documents** — Aadhaar cards of all family members, address proof
3. **The operator will fill your application** in the PPP portal
4. **Verify details** on screen before the operator submits
5. **Get acknowledgement receipt** with reference number
6. **Family ID will be issued** after data verification by the concerned authority

### Through SARAL Portal

1. Visit the **Saral Haryana portal** at [saralharyana.gov.in](https://saralharyana.gov.in)
2. Login or register on the portal
3. Search for "Parivar Pehchan Patra" service
4. Fill the application form and submit with required documents
5. Track your application using the service request ID

## Processing Time

- **Online applications:** 15–30 days for verification and ID generation
- **Offline applications via CSC:** 15–30 days after submission
- **Updates/corrections:** 7–15 days

## Benefits of Parivar Pehchan Patra

- **Single identity** for the entire family to access all Haryana government schemes
- **Automatic eligibility check** — the system identifies which schemes your family qualifies for
- **Transparent benefit delivery** — reduces duplication and fraud in government subsidies
- **Required for:** Old age pension (see our [Haryana Old Age Samman Allowance guide](/guide/haryana-old-age-samman-allowance)), scholarships, ration card services, employment schemes, and more
- **Digital verification** eliminates need for multiple documents at different offices

## Important Tips

1. **Keep your Aadhaar details updated** — PPP relies on Aadhaar verification, so ensure your Aadhaar has the correct address and mobile number
2. **Update family changes promptly** — births, deaths, marriages, or member moving out should be updated in the PPP portal to avoid issues with benefits
3. **Save your Family ID number** — you'll need it for almost every Haryana government scheme application
4. **Check scheme eligibility** on the meraparivar portal — it shows which schemes your family can avail based on your verified data
5. **Don't pay extra at CSCs** — registration is free; only nominal operator charges may apply

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Is Parivar Pehchan Patra mandatory in Haryana?
Yes, the Family ID has been made mandatory for availing most government schemes and services in Haryana, including pensions, scholarships, ration cards, and employment benefits.

### Q2. Can a single person living alone get a Parivar Pehchan Patra?
Yes. A single individual living independently can register as a single-member family and receive their own Family ID.

### Q3. How do I check if my Parivar Pehchan Patra has been issued?
Visit [meraparivar.haryana.gov.in](https://meraparivar.haryana.gov.in) and use the "Check Status" or "Search Family ID" option. You can search using your Aadhaar number or application reference number.

### Q4. What if my family member's Aadhaar details don't match?
The system verifies data against Aadhaar. If there's a mismatch (name spelling, date of birth), first update your Aadhaar card through UIDAI and then retry the PPP registration. See our [Aadhaar common problems guide](/guide/aadhaar-common-problems-solutions) for help.

### Q5. Can I add a newborn baby to my existing Family ID?
Yes. Once the baby's birth certificate is issued, you can add the child to your Family ID through the meraparivar portal or at a CSC. Aadhaar for the child is recommended but not immediately mandatory.

### Q6. How do I remove a deceased family member from the Family ID?
You can apply for removal through the meraparivar portal or at a CSC by submitting the death certificate. The change will be updated after verification.

### Q7. Is the Parivar Pehchan Patra the same as a ration card?
No. PPP is a family identity document, while a ration card is specifically for food subsidy under PDS. However, your Family ID is now linked to ration card services in Haryana.
