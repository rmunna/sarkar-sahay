# LPG Subsidy Not Received in Bank Account — How to Fix

If your LPG gas subsidy has stopped coming into your bank account, you are not alone. Thousands of consumers face this issue due to Aadhaar-bank linking problems, incorrect bank details on MyLPG, or DBTL (Direct Benefit Transfer for LPG) not being activated. This guide covers every reason and the exact steps to fix it.

## Why LPG Subsidy Stops

Your LPG subsidy may stop for any of these reasons:

1. **Aadhaar not linked to bank account** — The most common reason. Your Aadhaar must be seeded (linked) with the bank account registered for LPG subsidy.
2. **Wrong bank account in MyLPG portal** — If you changed your bank but didn't update it on MyLPG, the subsidy goes to the old account or fails.
3. **DBTL (PAHAL) not activated** — Direct Benefit Transfer for LPG must be active on your connection. If you never joined or it got deactivated, subsidy won't come.
4. **Subsidy stopped after Ujjwala scheme** — Ujjwala beneficiaries sometimes face subsidy discontinuation after a certain number of free refills end or due to incomplete KYC.
5. **Duplicate Aadhaar or connection** — If Aadhaar is linked to multiple LPG connections, subsidy may be blocked for one.
6. **Bank account is inactive or closed** — If your linked bank account is dormant or closed, subsidy transfer fails.
7. **Income above ₹10 lakh** — Consumers with annual income above ₹10 lakh are not eligible for LPG subsidy under the GiveItUp scheme norms.
8. **KYC not complete** — Incomplete Know Your Customer documents with the distributor can block subsidy.

## How to Check LPG Subsidy Status

### On MyLPG Portal

1. Go to [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in)
2. Click **"Customer"** → **"DBTL Status"** (or go directly to the DBTL status page)
3. Enter your **17-digit LPG ID** (printed on your gas passbook or delivery receipt)
4. The portal shows:
   - Whether DBTL/PAHAL is **active or inactive**
   - The **bank account** linked for subsidy
   - **Aadhaar linking status**
   - Recent subsidy transaction history

### Via UMANG App

1. Open the **UMANG app** (available on Android/iOS)
2. Search for **"MyLPG"** or **"LPG Subsidy"**
3. Enter your LPG ID to check DBTL status and subsidy history

### Via SMS (Indane, HP Gas, Bharat Gas)

- **Indane:** Send SMS `DBTL <LPG ID>` to **7738299899**
- **HP Gas:** Send SMS `DBTL <LPG ID>` to **9222201122**
- **Bharat Gas:** Send SMS `DBTL <LPG ID>` to **9222201122**

## Fix for Each Reason

### 1. Aadhaar Not Linked to Bank Account

**Steps to link Aadhaar with your bank:**

1. Visit your **bank branch** with your Aadhaar card and passbook
2. Fill the **Aadhaar seeding form** — request that your Aadhaar be linked to your savings account
3. The bank will seed your Aadhaar within **1–7 working days**
4. You can verify the linking on the [NPCI Aadhaar Mapper](https://www.npci.org.in/what-we-do/aadhaar-payment-bridge/aadhaar-mapper) — check which bank is receiving Aadhaar-based payments

> **Important:** Aadhaar must be linked to the **same bank account** that is registered on MyLPG for LPG subsidy. If they don't match, subsidy will fail.

### 2. Wrong Bank Account on MyLPG

**Steps to update bank account:**

1. Go to [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in) → **Customer Login**
2. Navigate to **"Update Bank Account"**
3. Enter your new **bank account number**, **IFSC code**, and **account holder name**
4. Submit the request — it takes **5–10 working days** for verification
5. Alternatively, visit your **LPG distributor** with a cancelled cheque or passbook copy and request bank account update

### 3. DBTL (PAHAL) Not Activated

**Steps to activate DBTL:**

1. Visit [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in) → **Customer** → **Join DBTL (PAHAL)**
2. Enter your **LPG ID** and **Aadhaar number**
3. Verify with OTP sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile
4. Once joined, subsidy will be credited from your **next refill onwards**

You can also activate DBTL by:
- Visiting your **LPG distributor** with Aadhaar and passbook
- Calling the **LPG helpline** for your company

### 4. Subsidy Stopped After Ujjwala Scheme

Ujjwala beneficiaries may face subsidy issues because:
- The **initial free refills** (usually 3–8 cylinders) have been exhausted, and DBTL was not properly activated afterward
- **KYC documents** are incomplete

**Fix:**
1. Visit your **distributor** and confirm your Ujjwala connection status
2. Ensure **Aadhaar, bank account, and KYC** are all up to date
3. Activate DBTL on MyLPG if not already done
4. If refills were paid at full price (non-subsidised), check if subsidy was credited separately to your bank — sometimes it arrives with a delay

### 5. Duplicate Connection

If you have **two LPG connections on the same Aadhaar**, one will be blocked:
1. **Surrender the extra connection** at the respective distributor
2. Keep only one active connection per Aadhaar
3. Subsidy will resume on the active connection after verification

### 6. Bank Account Inactive or Closed

1. Visit the bank and **reactivate** your account (make a small deposit or transaction)
2. Or update a **new active bank account** on MyLPG (see steps above)
3. Once the account is active and linked, subsidy transfers will resume

## How to Update Bank Account for LPG Subsidy

### Online (MyLPG Portal)

1. Visit [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in) and log in
2. Go to **My Account** → **Update Bank Account**
3. Enter the **new bank account number**, **IFSC code**, and **account holder name**
4. The name on the bank account should match the LPG connection holder's name
5. Submit and wait **5–10 working days** for verification

### Offline (At Distributor)

1. Visit your **LPG distributor/agency**
2. Carry: **Aadhaar card**, **new bank passbook** (or cancelled cheque), **LPG passbook**
3. Fill the bank account change request form
4. The distributor forwards it to the oil company for processing

## How to File a Complaint

### 1. Through MyLPG Portal

1. Go to [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in) → **Customer** → **Lodge Complaint/Feedback**
2. Select complaint category: **"Subsidy Related"**
3. Enter your **LPG ID**, describe the issue, and submit
4. You will get a **complaint reference number** — save it for tracking

### 2. Contact Your LPG Distributor (Vitarak)

- Visit or call your local distributor directly
- Provide your **LPG consumer number** and explain the subsidy issue
- The distributor can escalate to the oil company on your behalf

### 3. Oil Company Helplines

If no response from the oil company:
1. Go to [pgportal.gov.in](https://pgportal.gov.in)
2. Register and lodge a grievance under **Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas**
3. Government mandates a response within **30 days**

## Important Tips

- **Always verify Aadhaar-bank linking** on the NPCI mapper before expecting subsidy. This is the #1 reason subsidy fails.
- **Keep only one LPG connection per Aadhaar** — duplicate connections block subsidy for both.
- **Subsidy is credited after you pay full price** — under DBTL, you pay market price for the cylinder and the subsidy amount is deposited directly into your bank account afterward.
- **Check your bank statement** after each refill — subsidy usually arrives within **3–7 working days** of delivery.
- **Don't ignore SMS alerts** — oil companies send SMS about subsidy credit. If you stop receiving them, investigate immediately.
- **Ujjwala beneficiaries** should complete KYC and ensure DBTL is active even after free refills are exhausted.
- **Subsidised cylinders are limited to 12 per year** per household — beyond that, you pay market price with no subsidy.

## FAQs

### How long does it take for LPG subsidy to reach my bank account?
LPG subsidy is usually credited within **3–7 working days** after the cylinder is delivered. If it doesn't arrive within 15 days, check your DBTL status on MyLPG and contact your distributor.

### How do I know if my Aadhaar is linked to my LPG connection?
Check on [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in) → **DBTL Status**. Enter your LPG ID and it will show whether Aadhaar is linked. You can also verify Aadhaar-bank linking on the NPCI Aadhaar mapper.

### Can I receive LPG subsidy without Aadhaar?
No. Under the DBTL/PAHAL scheme, **Aadhaar is mandatory** for receiving LPG subsidy. You must link your Aadhaar to both your LPG connection and your bank account.

### What is the current LPG subsidy amount?
The subsidy amount varies and is decided by the government based on international LPG prices. It changes every month. You can check the current subsidy amount on the MyLPG portal or your delivery receipt.

### I gave up subsidy under GiveItUp. Can I get it back?
Yes. If you voluntarily gave up your subsidy, you can **reclaim it** by visiting [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in) and selecting **"I want to reclaim subsidy"** or by contacting your LPG distributor.

### My subsidy is going to an old bank account. How do I change it?
Update your bank account on [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in) → **Update Bank Account**, or visit your distributor with a new passbook/cancelled cheque. Also ensure your Aadhaar is seeded with the new bank account.

### What should I do if my complaint is not resolved?
If your LPG distributor or oil company doesn't resolve the issue within 15–30 days, escalate to the **PG Portal** ([pgportal.gov.in](https://pgportal.gov.in)) under the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas. You can also contact the **LPG Emergency Helpline 1906**.
