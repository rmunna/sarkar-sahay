# Rent Agreement Format — How to Make Rental Agreement Online and Offline

A rent agreement (also called a rental agreement or lease deed) is the most important document in any landlord-tenant relationship. Whether you are a landlord renting out property or a tenant moving into a new home, having a properly drafted rent agreement protects both parties legally.

This guide covers the standard rent agreement format in India, essential clauses you must include, and a complete walkthrough of how to make a rental agreement both online and offline.

## What Is a Rent Agreement?

A rent agreement is a legal contract between a landlord (lessor) and a tenant (lessee) that defines the terms and conditions of renting a residential or commercial property. It specifies the rent amount, duration, security deposit, maintenance responsibilities, and other important conditions.

In India, rent agreements are governed by state-level Rent Control Acts and the Registration Act, 1908. Most residential agreements are executed for **11 months** to avoid mandatory registration under Section 17 of the Registration Act.

### Types of Rent Agreements

- **Leave and Licence Agreement** — Most common in Maharashtra; grants a licence to occupy, not a lease
- **Lease Agreement** — For longer durations (typically 12 months or more); must be registered
- **Rent Agreement (11-month)** — Standard format used across India for short-term residential rentals

## Essential Clauses to Include in a Rent Agreement

A well-drafted rent agreement must contain these clauses:

1. **Names and Details of Parties** — Full names, addresses, and identification details of landlord and tenant
2. **Property Description** — Complete address, floor, area (sq. ft.), and type of property (residential/commercial)
3. **Monthly Rent Amount** — Exact rent with due date and mode of payment (bank transfer, cheque, UPI)
4. **Security Deposit** — Amount deposited (usually 2–10 months' rent depending on city), refund conditions
5. **Tenure / Duration** — Start date, end date, and whether it is an 11-month or longer-term agreement
6. **Rent Escalation Clause** — Annual increase percentage (typically 5–10%)
7. **Maintenance and Utility Charges** — Who pays electricity, water, society maintenance, and repairs
8. **Lock-in Period** — Minimum stay period before either party can terminate
9. **Notice Period** — Usually 1–2 months' notice required before vacating
10. **Termination Conditions** — Grounds for early termination by either party
11. **Subletting Clause** — Whether the tenant can sublet the property
12. **Usage Restrictions** — Property to be used only for residential/commercial purpose as agreed
13. **Furnishing Details** — Inventory of furniture, appliances, and fixtures provided
14. **Dispute Resolution** — Jurisdiction and mechanism (arbitration/court) for resolving disputes
15. **Signatures and Witnesses** — Both parties must sign; two witnesses are recommended

## How to Make a Rent Agreement Online

Making a rent agreement online is now possible in most Indian states through e-stamping portals. Here is the step-by-step process:

### Step 1: Draft the Agreement

Prepare the agreement content with all essential clauses. You can use online templates or hire a legal professional.

### Step 2: Purchase an e-Stamp Certificate

Visit the **SHCIL e-Stamping portal** ([shcilestamp.com](https://www.shcilestamp.com)) or your state-specific portal:

- **Maharashtra** — [igrmaharashtra.gov.in](https://igrmaharashtra.gov.in) (e-SBTR system)
- **Karnataka** — [kaveri.karnataka.gov.in](https://kaveri.karnataka.gov.in) (Kaveri Online)
- **Uttar Pradesh** — [igrsup.gov.in](https://igrsup.gov.in) (IGRSUP portal)
- **Delhi NCR** — Through SHCIL authorised centres or online
- **Tamil Nadu, Gujarat, Assam, Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand** — SHCIL e-stamping

Select the stamp duty amount based on your state's rates and the rent/tenure of your agreement.

### Step 3: Print Agreement on e-Stamp Paper

Print or type the drafted agreement on the e-stamp certificate. Ensure the Unique Identification Number (UIN) is visible and unaltered.

### Step 4: Sign and Add Witnesses

Both landlord and tenant must sign the agreement. Two witnesses should also sign with their details.

### Step 5: Register (If Required)

For agreements exceeding 11 months, registration at the local Sub-Registrar Office is mandatory. Some states allow online registration as well.

## How to Make a Rent Agreement Offline

The traditional offline process is still widely used across India:

### Step 1: Buy Non-Judicial Stamp Paper

Purchase stamp paper of the appropriate denomination from a licensed stamp vendor. The value depends on your state and the rent amount.

### Step 2: Draft or Print the Agreement

Get the agreement drafted by a lawyer or use a standard template. Print it on the stamp paper or write it by hand.

### Step 3: Sign the Agreement

Both parties and two witnesses must sign on all pages. Affix photographs of landlord and tenant if required by your state.

### Step 4: Notarize the Agreement

Get the agreement notarized by a licensed Notary Public. The notary verifies identities and stamps the document. Notarization fee is typically ₹100–₹500.

### Step 5: Register (If Applicable)

For long-term leases, visit the Sub-Registrar Office with the original agreement, ID proofs, and passport-size photographs of both parties.

## Stamp Duty for Rent Agreements by Major States

Stamp duty varies significantly across states. Here are the approximate rates:

| State | Stamp Duty (11-Month Agreement) | Stamp Duty (Longer Term) |
|-------|-------------------------------|--------------------------|
| **Maharashtra** | 0.25% of total rent + deposit | Higher rates for 1–5 years; registration mandatory |
| **Delhi** | ₹50–₹100 for under 1 year | 2% of average annual rent for up to 5 years |
| **Karnataka** | 1% of rent + deposit (min ₹500) | 1% with registration for leases over 12 months |
| **Tamil Nadu** | 1% of annual rent | 1% with registration for longer leases |
| **Uttar Pradesh** | ₹200 minimum for short-term | 2% for agreements over 1 year |
| **West Bengal** | 0.25% or ₹25 minimum | Higher for registered leases |
| **Rajasthan** | ₹20–₹50 for short-term | Scaled rates based on tenure |
| **Telangana** | 0.25% of total consideration | Registration needed above 11 months |

> **Note:** Rates are approximate and may change. Always verify the current rates on your state's official stamp duty portal.

## Registration Requirements

Under the Registration Act, 1908:

- **Agreements up to 11 months** — Registration is **optional** (but recommended for legal protection)
- **Agreements of 12 months or more** — Registration is **mandatory**
- **Registration fee** — Typically 1% of annual rent or a flat fee (varies by state)
- **Documents required** — Original agreement on stamp paper, ID proofs (Aadhaar, PAN), passport-size photos, property documents
- **Biometric verification** — Many states now require biometric verification at the Sub-Registrar Office

### Police Verification

In most Indian cities, landlords are required to submit tenant details to the local police station within 24–48 hours of a new tenant moving in. Many states now offer **online police verification portals**. This is mandatory in cities like Bengaluru, Pune, Mumbai, and Delhi.

## 11-Month Rent Agreement vs Long-Term Lease

| Feature | 11-Month Agreement | Long-Term Lease (12+ Months) |
|---------|-------------------|------------------------------|
| **Registration** | Optional | Mandatory |
| **Stamp Duty** | Lower | Higher |
| **Legal Protection** | Moderate (enforceable but less weight in court) | Strong (registered document is stronger evidence) |
| **Renewal** | Needs fresh agreement | Can include auto-renewal clause |
| **Common Use** | Residential rentals | Commercial leases, premium properties |

### Why 11 Months?

The 11-month convention exists because agreements under 12 months do not require compulsory registration, saving both parties time and money on registration fees and stamp duty. However, an unregistered agreement may carry less weight in legal disputes.

## Sample Rent Agreement Format — Overview

A standard rent agreement follows this structure:

```
RENT AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on [Date] at [City]

BETWEEN
[Landlord Name], S/o [Father's Name], residing at [Address]
(hereinafter called the "LANDLORD/OWNER")

AND
[Tenant Name], S/o [Father's Name], residing at [Address]
(hereinafter called the "TENANT")

WHEREAS the Landlord is the owner of [Property Address]
and agrees to let it to the Tenant on the following terms:

1. TENURE: [Start Date] to [End Date] (11 months)
2. MONTHLY RENT: ₹[Amount], payable by [Date] of each month
3. SECURITY DEPOSIT: ₹[Amount], refundable on termination
4. MAINTENANCE: [Terms]
5. LOCK-IN PERIOD: [X months]
6. NOTICE PERIOD: [X months]
7. RENT ESCALATION: [X]% annually on renewal
...

LANDLORD SIGNATURE          TENANT SIGNATURE

WITNESS 1:                  WITNESS 2:
Name:                       Name:
Address:                    Address:
```

> This is an overview only. Consult a legal professional for a complete, binding agreement tailored to your state laws.

## Important Tips for Making a Rent Agreement

1. **Always use stamp paper** — An agreement without proper stamp duty has no legal validity
2. **Keep multiple copies** — Both landlord and tenant should keep signed originals
3. **Verify property ownership** — Tenant should check that the landlord actually owns the property
4. **Document property condition** — Take photos/video of the property at move-in to avoid disputes later
5. **Include an inventory list** — List all furniture, appliances, and fittings with their condition
6. **Aadhaar and PAN details** — Include these for both parties for legal compliance
7. **Register for long-term leases** — Unregistered agreements above 11 months are not admissible as evidence under Section 49 of the Registration Act
8. **Complete police verification** — This is mandatory in most cities and protects both parties
9. **Use e-stamping where available** — Faster, tamper-proof, and verifiable online via the UIN
10. **Renew on time** — Don't let agreements lapse; execute a fresh agreement or renewal deed before expiry

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Is a rent agreement valid without registration?

Yes, an 11-month rent agreement is valid even without registration. However, it carries less evidentiary value in court compared to a registered agreement. Agreements exceeding 11 months **must** be registered to be legally enforceable.

### What is the cost of making a rent agreement?

The cost includes stamp duty (₹50–₹500+ depending on state and rent), notarization (₹100–₹500), and lawyer drafting fees (₹500–₹2,000). Registration adds 1% of annual rent in most states.

### Can I make a rent agreement online without visiting any office?

In states with e-stamping (Maharashtra, Karnataka, Delhi, UP, Tamil Nadu, etc.), you can purchase e-stamp paper online, draft and print the agreement, and get it notarized. Full online registration is available in some states like Maharashtra.

### What happens if there is no rent agreement?

Without a rent agreement, neither party has legal proof of the arrangement. Disputes become difficult to resolve, tenants may claim tenancy rights under Rent Control Acts, and landlords may struggle to evict tenants.

### How many witnesses are needed for a rent agreement?

A minimum of **two witnesses** is recommended. Each witness should provide their name, address, and signature on the agreement. Witnesses strengthen the document's legal standing.

### Can NRIs enter into a rent agreement in India?

Yes, NRIs can execute rent agreements for their Indian properties. They can authorize someone via a **Power of Attorney (PoA)** to sign on their behalf. The PoA should be notarized or apostilled in the NRI's country of residence.

### Is notarization mandatory for a rent agreement?

Notarization is not legally mandatory but is **highly recommended**. A notarized agreement carries more credibility. In some cities, notarization is required for police verification and address proof purposes.
