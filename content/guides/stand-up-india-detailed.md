# Stand Up India Scheme — Loans for SC/ST and Women Entrepreneurs

Starting a business requires capital, and for many SC, ST, and women entrepreneurs in India, getting a bank loan has traditionally been difficult. The **Stand Up India scheme** was launched by the Government of India on **5 April 2016** to bridge this gap. It provides bank loans between ₹10 lakh and ₹1 crore to at least one SC/ST borrower and one woman borrower per bank branch for setting up a greenfield (new) enterprise.

This guide explains everything you need to know — eligibility, loan amount, how to apply, documents required, and repayment terms.

---

## What is Stand Up India?

Stand Up India is a flagship scheme of the Government of India under the Department of Financial Services (DFS), Ministry of Finance. It aims to promote entrepreneurship among **Scheduled Castes (SC)**, **Scheduled Tribes (ST)**, and **women** by facilitating bank loans for greenfield enterprises.

**Key highlights:**

- Each bank branch must give at least **one loan to an SC/ST borrower** and **one loan to a woman borrower**
- The enterprise must be a **greenfield** (first-time) venture in manufacturing, services, trading, or agriculture-allied activities
- The scheme is facilitated through the **Stand Up India portal (standupmitra.in)** managed by SIDBI
- The scheme has been **extended up to 2025** and continues to operate

> **Note:** Stand Up India is different from [Startup India](https://www.startupindia.gov.in), which focuses on registered startups. Stand Up India is specifically for SC/ST and women entrepreneurs starting new businesses.

## Loan Amount

| Detail | Amount / Terms |
|---|---|
| **Minimum loan** | ₹10 lakh |
| **Maximum loan** | ₹1 crore |
| **Covers** | Up to 75% of the project cost |
| **Borrower's margin** | Minimum 25% of the project cost |
| **Nature of loan** | Composite loan (term loan + working capital) |

The loan is a **composite loan** — meaning it covers both the cost of setting up the business (machinery, equipment, infrastructure) and working capital needs in a single sanction.

**Margin money:** You need to bring at least 25% of the project cost from your own funds. This can be met through:
- Own contribution
- Convergence with eligible Central or State government subsidy schemes

---

## Who is Eligible?

To apply for Stand Up India loan, you must meet **all** of the following criteria:

1. **Category:** You must be an **SC, ST, or woman** entrepreneur (of any category)
2. **Age:** Must be **18 years or above**
3. **First-time entrepreneur:** The enterprise must be a **greenfield** (new) project — meaning you should not have taken a loan for a similar enterprise before
4. **Non-defaulter:** You should **not be a defaulter** to any bank or financial institution
5. **Business type:** The enterprise can be in **manufacturing, services, trading, or agriculture-allied** sector

**For joint ventures or partnerships:**
- At least **51% of the shareholding and controlling stake** must be held by an SC/ST or woman entrepreneur

**Not eligible:**
- Existing businesses seeking expansion loans (this is for new enterprises only)
- Individuals who are existing defaulters

## Documents Required

Keep the following documents ready before applying:

### Identity & Address Proof
- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
- [PAN card](/guide/pan-card-apply-online) (mandatory for loans above ₹5 lakh)
- Voter ID / Passport / Driving Licence

### Category Certificate
- **SC/ST certificate** issued by the competent authority (Tehsildar / SDM) — required for SC/ST applicants
- Women applicants do not need a category certificate but must provide identity proof

### Business Documents
- **Detailed project report (DPR)** — a written plan of your proposed business
- Business address proof (rent agreement / ownership document)
- Quotations for machinery and equipment (if applicable)
- Any licences or permissions required for the business (FSSAI, Shop Act, etc.)

### Financial Documents
- Bank statements of the last 6 months
- [Income Tax returns](/guide/income-tax-return-file-online) (if applicable)
- Proof of margin money / own contribution

### Photographs
- 2 recent passport-size photographs

> **Tip:** Banks may ask for additional documents depending on the nature of business. Always check with your branch before submitting.

## How to Apply

You can apply for Stand Up India loan through **two channels**:

### Option 1: Apply Online via StandUpMitra Portal

1. Visit [standupmitra.in](https://www.standupmitra.in)
2. Click on **"Loans"** or **"Register"**
3. Select your category — **SC**, **ST**, or **Woman**
4. Fill in your details — name, Aadhaar, mobile number, business details
5. Select your preferred bank branch
6. Upload required documents
7. Submit the application
8. You will receive a **reference number** — save it for tracking
9. The portal connects you with the nearest bank branch for processing

### Option 2: Visit Your Bank Branch Directly

1. Visit any **Scheduled Commercial Bank** branch near you
2. Ask for the **Stand Up India loan application form**
3. Submit the filled form along with all required documents
4. The bank will review your project report and assess the proposal
5. If approved, the loan is sanctioned and disbursed

### After Applying

- Banks are required to process applications in a **time-bound manner**
- If a branch delays or rejects your application without valid reason, you can escalate to the **Lead District Manager (LDM)** or contact the **SIDBI Connect Centre** at standupmitra.in
- You can also call the **Stand Up India helpline** for support

## Repayment Terms

| Detail | Terms |
|---|---|
| **Repayment period** | Up to **7 years** |
| **Moratorium** | Maximum **18 months** (you don't pay EMI during this period) |
| **Interest rate** | Lowest applicable rate of the bank for that category (typically linked to MCLR/EBLR) |
| **Security** | The loan can be secured by **CGTMSE (Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises)** coverage — so collateral requirement is minimal |

**How repayment works:**
- After the moratorium period (up to 18 months), you start paying monthly instalments (EMI)
- Total repayment period including moratorium is **7 years**
- Interest rate varies by bank but must be the **lowest applicable rate** for the category

## Important Tips

1. **Prepare a strong project report** — Banks assess your loan mainly on the viability of your business plan. Get professional help if needed.

2. **Use government subsidies for margin money** — Many State governments offer subsidies for SC/ST and women entrepreneurs. You can use these to meet the 25% margin requirement.

3. **Apply to multiple branches if rejected** — If one branch rejects your application, you can approach another branch or escalate through the standupmitra portal.

4. **Don't pay any middlemen** — The application is **free**. No agent or consultant fee is required. Beware of touts who promise guaranteed loan approval.

5. **Keep all documents updated** — Ensure your Aadhaar, PAN, and caste certificate are current and have matching details.

6. **Compare with [Mudra Loan](/guide/pm-mudra-yojana-loan)** — If your loan requirement is below ₹10 lakh, the [PM Mudra Yojana](/guide/pm-mudra-yojana-loan) may be a better fit. Also read our [Mudra vs Stand Up India comparison](/guide/mudra-vs-standup-india-loan).

7. **Track your application** — Use the reference number from standupmitra.in to check your application status online.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Can a general category woman apply for Stand Up India loan?

**Yes.** Any woman entrepreneur aged 18+ can apply, regardless of caste category. The scheme covers all women, plus SC and ST applicants of any gender.

### 2. What if my project cost is less than ₹10 lakh?

The minimum loan under Stand Up India is ₹10 lakh. If your requirement is smaller, consider the [PM Mudra Yojana](/guide/pm-mudra-yojana-loan) which offers loans up to ₹10 lakh without collateral.

### 3. Can I apply if I already have an existing business?

**No.** Stand Up India is only for **greenfield (new) enterprises**. If you already have an established business and need expansion funding, explore other schemes like [PMEGP](/guide/pm-mudra-yojana-loan) or MUDRA.

### 4. Is collateral required for Stand Up India loan?

The scheme provides coverage under **CGTMSE (Credit Guarantee Fund Trust)**, which significantly reduces the need for collateral. However, the bank may ask for a charge on the assets created from the loan.

### 5. What is the interest rate for Stand Up India loans?

The interest rate is not fixed across banks. Each bank offers the **lowest applicable rate** for that category, typically linked to MCLR or External Benchmark Lending Rate (EBLR). You should compare rates across banks before applying.

### 6. How long does it take to get the loan sanctioned?

There is no official timeline, but banks are expected to process applications promptly. Typical processing takes **2 to 6 weeks** depending on the completeness of documents and the bank's internal processes.

### 7. Can I get Stand Up India loan from a private bank?

**Yes.** Stand Up India applies to all **Scheduled Commercial Banks** — including SBI, PNB, Bank of Baroda, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, and others.
