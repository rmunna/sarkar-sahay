# Haryana Income Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **income certificate** in Haryana is issued by the Revenue Department and is required for EWS reservation (income < ₹8 lakh/year), SC/BC/OBC scholarships, government scheme eligibility, and PMAY housing. Haryana uses the **Antyodaya Saral** portal — a unified service delivery platform — for all citizen certificates including income certificates. The **Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP)** family ID is linked to all Haryana services.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [saralharyana.gov.in](https://saralharyana.gov.in) with your **Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP) family ID**. Issued by Tehsildar within **7–21 days**.

---

## Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP) — Mandatory for Haryana Income Certificate

Haryana's **PPP (family ID)** is linked to income data automatically. Many citizens don't need a separate income certificate — their **PPP-verified annual family income** is already on record and accepted by most Haryana departments.

However, for NSP national scholarships, EWS reservation, central government schemes, and external institutions, a **printed and signed income certificate from Tehsildar** may still be required.

---

## Why You Need Haryana Income Certificate

- **SC/BC/OBC post-matric scholarships** — income-based eligibility
- **NSP National Scholarship** — all categories
- **EWS reservation** — income < ₹8 lakh/year for general category
- **Haryana government scheme eligibility** — Mukhyamantri Parivar Samridhi Yojana
- **PMAY housing scheme** — income proof for EWS/LIG/MIG categories
- **Government college fee concession** — income-based scholarships

---

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory)
- **Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP) family ID** (mandatory — 8-digit family ID)
- Self-declaration of annual income (all sources)
- For salaried: **salary certificate** from employer or Form 16
- For farmers: **Jamabandi / Khasra** (land records from Patwari)
- **Voter ID** (additional address proof)
- Passport-size photograph

## How to Apply Online — Antyodaya Saral Portal

1. Go to [saralharyana.gov.in](https://saralharyana.gov.in)
2. Click **"New User? Register"** → register with mobile number and Aadhaar
3. Log in → click **"Services"** → search **"Income Certificate"**
4. Select **"Revenue Department — Income Certificate"**
5. Enter your **PPP family ID** — family data auto-fills from PPP database
6. Verify income details and edit if required
7. Upload supporting income documents
8. Pay fee: **₹30** (online)
9. Submit → note **Service Request Number (SRN)**

---

## Apply via Antyodaya Saral Kendra / CSC

1. Visit nearest **Antyodaya Saral Kendra** (every block/tehsil has one) or CSC
2. Carry Aadhaar, PPP card, income proof documents
3. Operator logs in and submits the income certificate request
4. Service charge: approximately ₹30–60
5. Collect acknowledgement / SRN

---

## Status Check

1. Go to [saralharyana.gov.in](https://saralharyana.gov.in)
2. Click **"Track Application"** → enter Service Request Number (SRN)
3. Or log in → **"My Applications"** → view income certificate status
4. Status flow: Applied → Under Verification → Tehsildar Approved

---

## Download Haryana Income Certificate

Once approved:
1. Log in to Saral portal → **"My Certificates"**
2. Click income certificate → Download digitally signed PDF
3. Can also be downloaded via PPP portal
4. Valid for all official purposes

## PPP Auto-Verification vs Income Certificate — Which to Use?

| Situation | What to Use |
|-----------|------------|
| **Haryana state scholarship** | PPP-verified income (often accepted directly) |
| **Central NSP scholarship** | Printed income certificate from Tehsildar |
| **EWS reservation (state job)** | Income certificate from Tehsildar |
| **Central government job EWS** | Income certificate specifying < ₹8 lakh/year |
| **PMAY / housing** | Income certificate or PPP income data |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My PPP shows income as ₹1.5 lakh but actual income is different — what to do?

Update your PPP family income at the nearest **PPP enrollment centre** or Antyodaya Saral Kendra. Bring income proof documents (salary certificate, Jamabandi). The updated PPP income flows to the income certificate system.

### Income limit for Haryana BC/OBC scholarship?

For **Haryana BC Post-Matric Scholarship**: family income below **₹1.8 lakh/year** (two sub-categories — BC-A and BC-B). For **EWS**: income < ₹8 lakh/year. Check specific scheme notifications as limits may vary.
