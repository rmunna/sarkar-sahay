# Andhra Pradesh Income Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **income certificate** in Andhra Pradesh is issued by the Revenue Department and is essential for BC/OBC Non-Creamy Layer certificates, SC/ST scholarship fee reimbursement, Jagananna Vidya Deevena scheme, and other government benefits. Applications are processed through **MeeSeva** — AP's multi-service delivery platform.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at any MeeSeva centre or [meeseva.gov.in](https://meeseva.gov.in). Verified by VRO/MRI and approved by MRO within **7–21 days**. Fee: approximately ₹35.

---

## Why You Need AP Income Certificate

- **BC/OBC Non-Creamy Layer (NCL)** certificate — income proof mandatory
- **SC/ST Scholarship (Pre/Post-Matric)** — income below ₹2.5 lakh for PMS
- **AP Fee Reimbursement (BC, SC, ST)** — government engineering/medical colleges
- **Jagananna Vidya Deevena** scheme eligibility
- **BPL ration card** and government scheme enrollment
- **EWS (Economically Weaker Section)** reservation (income < ₹8 lakh)

---

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory for all members)
- **Ration card** (if available)
- **Voter ID** (additional ID proof)
- Self-declaration of income (form provided at MeeSeva)
- For salaried: **salary certificate** from employer
- For farmers: **Pahani** (land records — from Meebhoomi portal)
- Passport-size photograph

---

## How to Apply via MeeSeva

**Method 1: MeeSeva Kiosk (Recommended)**

1. Visit nearest **MeeSeva centre** or **kiosk**
2. Ask for **"Income Certificate"** (BC/SC/ST as applicable)
3. Provide Aadhaar, ration card, and income declaration
4. Operator submits the application
5. Fee: approximately **₹35**
6. Receive acknowledgement with application number

**Method 2: MeeSeva Online Portal**

1. Go to [meeseva.gov.in](https://meeseva.gov.in)
2. Log in / register with mobile OTP
3. Search **"Income Certificate"**
4. Fill form, upload documents, pay fee, submit

**Method 3: Ward Secretariat (AP's Doorstep Service)**

AP's **Ward/Village Secretariat** system means Ward Volunteers assist with income certificate applications door-to-door. Contact your Ward Volunteer or Panchayat Secretary.

---

## Status Check

1. Log in to [meeseva.gov.in](https://meeseva.gov.in)
2. Click **"My Applications"** → view income certificate status
3. Or track at MeeSeva kiosk with application number
