# Home Loan Documents Checklist India 2026 — Complete Guide

Submitting the right documents upfront speeds up home loan approval from weeks to days. Missing documents = delays, multiple bank visits, and frustration. Here's the complete checklist organized by applicant type and loan stage.

---

## Part 1: KYC / Identity Documents (All Applicants)

| Document | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| **PAN card** | Mandatory; used for CIBIL check and TDS |
| **Aadhaar card** | Identity + address |
| **Passport** (if applicable) | For NRIs and additional identity |
| **Voter ID** (alternative ID) | Alternative to Aadhaar |
| **Recent photographs** | 2–3 passport-size photos |

---

## Part 2: Income Documents — Salaried Employees

| Document | Why Needed |
|----------|-----------|
| **Salary slips (last 6 months)** | Income verification; EMI eligibility calculation |
| **Form 16 (last 2 years)** | Annual income confirmation from employer |
| **Bank statements (last 6 months)** | Shows salary credits, EMI history, financial behavior |
| **Appointment/Offer letter** | Confirms current employment and salary |
| **Employment letter** | On company letterhead confirming permanent employment |
| **Income Tax Return (ITR)** | Last 2 years (for some banks); validates income |
| **Office ID card** | Additional employment proof |

**Job stability matters**: Banks prefer 2+ years at current employer and 3+ years total work experience. If you changed jobs recently, provide both employers' documents.

---

## Part 3: Income Documents — Self-Employed / Business Owners

| Document | Why Needed |
|----------|-----------|
| **ITR (last 3 years)** | Shows consistent income; banks average 3-year ITR income |
| **CA-certified Balance Sheet (last 3 years)** | Business assets and liabilities |
| **CA-certified Profit & Loss Statement (last 3 years)** | Business income/profit |
| **Bank statements (all business accounts, last 12 months)** | Business cash flow |
| **GST registration certificate** | Business legitimacy |
| **Business registration** | Partnership deed / LLP agreement / company incorporation certificate |
| **Current business vintage certificate** | Bank/CA certificate showing business has been running 3+ years |

**Income calculation for self-employed**: Banks take average of last 3 years ITR-declared income. If income is fluctuating, the lowest year pulls the average down. Stable or growing income = higher eligibility.

**Common issue**: Self-employed with low ITR income (to save taxes) but high actual income struggle to get loans. Banks lend based on declared income.

---

## Part 4: Address Proof

| Document | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| **Aadhaar with current address** | Most accepted |
| **Utility bills (electricity/gas/water)** | Not older than 3 months |
| **Rental agreement** | If renting; helps explain no property in own name |
| **Passport with current address** | If Aadhaar shows old address |
| **Bank statement with current address** | Printed on statement header |

---

## Part 5: Property Documents (Provided After Loan Sanction, Before Disbursement)

### For Under-Construction Property (Builder/Developer)

| Document | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| **Sale Agreement / Allotment Letter** | Confirms you're buying; price agreed |
| **Builder's RERA Registration** | Mandatory — verify at rera.gov.in for your state |
| **Land/Plot ownership documents of builder** | Confirms builder owns the land |
| **Approved Building Plan** | Municipal/authority-approved floor plan |
| **NOC from Society / Competent Authority** | No objection from relevant authorities |
| **Builder's track record** | Completed project list (banks verify) |
| **Previous chain of title deeds** | For land on which project is built |

### For Resale Property (Flat/House from Existing Owner)

| Document | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| **Title deed / Sale deed** | Confirms current owner's title |
| **Encumbrance Certificate (EC)** | Shows no existing loan or lien on property; get from Sub-Registrar for last 15–30 years |
| **Previous chain of title deeds** | Previous ownership history (minimum 30 years) |
| **Approved building plan** | Municipality-approved plan for the building |
| **Occupancy Certificate (OC)** | Confirms building was legally completed |
| **Completion Certificate (CC)** | Issued by authority on completing construction |
| **Property tax receipts** | Shows taxes paid up to date |
| **Society NOC** | Society has no objection to transfer |
| **Share certificate** | In cooperative housing societies |

### For Plot + Self-Construction Loan

| Document | Additional Requirement |
|----------|----------------------|
| Plot title deed | Clear ownership in your name |
| Cost estimate | CA-certified or engineer-certified construction cost estimate |
| Approved building plan | Municipal/panchayat approved |

## Part 6: The Bank's Process — Sanction vs Disbursement

Understanding the two stages:

### Stage 1: Loan Sanction (Financial Approval)

Based on income documents + credit check. Bank issues **sanction letter** stating:
- Loan amount approved
- Interest rate
- EMI
- Tenure
- Conditions (property valuation, legal verification)

**What happens**: Bank evaluates your income, CIBIL score, existing obligations, and decides maximum loan eligibility.

### Stage 2: Disbursement (Property-Based)

After sanction, bank does:
1. **Legal verification**: Bank's empaneled lawyer verifies property title, encumbrances
2. **Technical valuation**: Bank's technical expert visits property and estimates value (bank loans 75–90% of lower of sale price or valuation)
3. **Disbursement**: Loan released to seller/builder after all property documents clear

**Timeline**: Sanction in 7–14 days. Disbursement after legal/technical: additional 7–21 days.

## CIBIL Score and Loan Eligibility

| CIBIL Score | Impact |
|-------------|--------|
| 750+ | Best rates (0.25–0.5% lower), easy approval |
| 700–749 | Standard approval, may have slightly higher rate |
| 650–699 | Possible approval at higher rate or with co-borrower |
| Below 650 | Difficult; most banks reject or approve at very high rate |

**Check your CIBIL**: At [cibil.com](https://www.cibil.com) (paid) or free at Paytm, CRED, or BankBazaar.

**If CIBIL is low**: Don't apply broadly (multiple rejections lower score further). Improve over 6–12 months.

## Co-Borrower Documents

If applying with spouse or family member:
- All income documents of co-borrower (separate set)
- KYC of co-borrower
- Co-borrower's bank statements
- Joint declaration of financial relationship

**Benefit of co-borrower**: Higher eligibility (both incomes added), better rates possible. Tax benefit: both can claim Section 24(b) interest deduction (up to ₹2L each) and 80C principal deduction (₹1.5L each).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What if I am self-employed but my business is 2 years old, not 3?

Most banks require 3 years of business vintage. Some NBFCs and private banks may lend after 2 years with compensating factors (high income growth, strong CIBIL, significant savings). Approach multiple lenders.

### Do I need to get the EC from Sub-Registrar myself?

Your lawyer or bank's empaneled lawyer typically gets the EC as part of legal verification. For your own due diligence before applying, you can request it from the Sub-Registrar's office in the property's jurisdiction.

### The builder doesn't have OC yet. Can I get a home loan?

Most banks won't disburse without OC for resale. For under-construction, OC comes after completion — loan is disbursed in installments during construction. Final disbursement typically on OC receipt.

### I'm buying through a builder. Is RERA registration mandatory?

Yes — RERA registration is mandatory for all residential projects with 8 or more units or 500+ sqm plot. Banks typically won't finance unregistered projects. Verify at your state's RERA portal.
