# Section 87A Tax Rebate India 2026 — Zero Tax for Incomes Below ₹7L (New Regime)

**Section 87A** provides a tax rebate that makes your **net income tax payable zero** if your total taxable income is within a threshold. This effectively means no income tax — not zero slab rate, but an actual rebate that eliminates your tax liability.

## The Current Limits

| Tax Regime | Taxable Income Threshold | Maximum Rebate |
|------------|--------------------------|---------------|
| **New tax regime** (Section 115BAC) | Up to **₹7,00,000** | ₹25,000 |
| **Old tax regime** | Up to **₹5,00,000** | ₹12,500 |

**How it works**: If your income is ₹7L under new regime, you'd normally compute tax on ₹7L and get tax of ₹25,000 (at the applicable slabs). Section 87A gives you a rebate of ₹25,000 — exactly wiping out the tax. Net tax = ₹0.

## Section 87A Under New Tax Regime (₹7L threshold)

Under the new tax regime with Standard Deduction of ₹75,000:
- You earn ₹7,75,000 gross salary
- Minus ₹75,000 standard deduction
- **Net taxable income: ₹7,00,000**
- Tax on ₹7L: ₹25,000 (at new regime slabs)
- 87A rebate: ₹25,000
- **Net tax payable: ₹0**

So effectively, in the new regime, you pay zero tax if your gross salary (after standard deduction) is up to ₹7.75L — making the **effective threshold ₹7.75L** for salaried employees.

## What Happens at ₹7,00,001 — Cliff Effect

If your income is ₹1 above the threshold, the rebate disappears:
- Income: ₹7,00,001
- Tax on ₹7,00,001: ~₹25,003
- 87A rebate: ₹0 (income exceeds ₹7L)
- **Net tax: ₹25,003** on ₹1 extra income

This "cliff effect" means making ₹1 more can cost you ₹25,000+ in tax. Tax planning to stay within the threshold is important.

**How to avoid the cliff**: Increase deductions (under new regime, you can use employer NPS contributions under 80CCD(2) to reduce taxable income).

## Section 87A Under Old Tax Regime (₹5L threshold)

- Taxable income ≤ ₹5,00,000 → Rebate up to ₹12,500 → Net tax = ₹0
- Since 80C can reduce income by ₹1.5L, a person earning ₹6.5L with full 80C deduction gets to ₹5L taxable income → zero tax under old regime
- Similarly, senior citizens (₹3L exemption) with additional deductions can benefit

## What "Total Taxable Income" Includes for 87A Purposes

**87A rebate is calculated on TOTAL income** — not just salaried income. This includes:
- Salary/business income
- Capital gains (STCG, LTCG)
- Interest income
- Rental income

**The key question**: If your income includes capital gains, does 87A still apply?

## 87A Rebate on Capital Gains — The Controversy

This is one of the most searched tax questions in India.

**Section 112A capital gains (LTCG on equity)**: The rebate under Section 87A does NOT apply to LTCG under 112A. If your ₹7L income includes LTCG, the LTCG portion is not covered by 87A.

**Section 111A capital gains (STCG on equity)**: As per a **controversial IT portal interpretation**, Section 87A rebate may not be available on STCG under 111A either. The CBDT/IT portal blocks 87A offset against 111A tax in the ITR filing utility (from FY 2024-25 onwards).

**Practical position (current)**:
- If income is only salary/interest/other sources (no capital gains) → 87A applies normally within the threshold
- If income includes LTCG (112A) → 87A does NOT reduce LTCG tax
- If income includes STCG (111A) → IT portal does NOT allow 87A against STCG (though legally contested)

**Example**:
- Salary: ₹5L; STCG from equity: ₹2L; Total income: ₹7L
- IT portal calculation: Tax on salary ₹5L = ₹12,500; 87A rebate = ₹12,500; Net salary tax = ₹0
- STCG tax: ₹2L × 20% = ₹40,000 — NOT offset by 87A per current IT portal
- Total tax: ₹40,000

This is contested and some taxpayers file returns asserting 87A applies to STCG — but the portal may reject. Consult a CA if this applies to your situation.

## 87A Is NOT Available For

| Situation | 87A Available? |
|-----------|---------------|
| NRI taxpayers (non-residents) | ❌ No — only resident individuals |
| Companies, HUF, firms | ❌ No — only individuals |
| Income above ₹7L (new regime) | ❌ No |
| Income above ₹5L (old regime) | ❌ No |
| LTCG on equity (Section 112A) | ❌ No |
| STCG on equity (Section 111A) | ❌ Effectively no (IT portal blocks it) |

## How to Check If 87A Applies in Your ITR

In the ITR filing portal, when you compute tax:
1. Tax is computed on total income
2. If total income ≤ ₹7L (new) or ₹5L (old): System shows "87A rebate" = ₹25,000 or ₹12,500
3. Net tax after rebate = ₹0

The rebate is automatic — you don't need to apply for it separately. Just ensure your total taxable income is within the threshold.

## Health and Education Cess on 87A

87A rebate is applied BEFORE cess:
- Tax before 87A: ₹25,000
- 87A rebate: ₹25,000
- Net tax: ₹0
- Cess on ₹0: ₹0
- **Total tax payable: ₹0**

If there's any tax remaining after 87A: 4% cess is applied on that remaining tax.

## Marginal Relief — When Income Is Just Above ₹50L/₹1Cr

87A is separate from **marginal relief** on surcharge. Marginal relief applies when income crosses ₹50L or ₹1Cr (surcharge thresholds). If your income is ₹51L, the additional tax from surcharge can't exceed ₹1L (the increment above ₹50L). That's a different provision from 87A.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My income is ₹7.5L in the new regime. I lose 87A. How do I get it?

Reduce taxable income to ₹7L. Under new regime, options are limited:
- **Employer NPS contribution (80CCD(2))**: If your employer contributes to NPS, that amount is deductible even in new regime. Ask your employer to restructure salary to include NPS contribution
- **Standard deduction**: Already included (₹75K for salaried)
- **Home loan interest on let-out property**: Available even in new regime (only self-occupied is blocked)

### I'm self-employed with ₹8L gross income. Under old regime after 80C I'm at ₹6.5L. Am I below ₹5L for 87A?

Old regime threshold for 87A is ₹5L. ₹6.5L taxable income exceeds it. 87A doesn't apply. But your tax is still relatively low (slab rate on ₹6.5L). You can't claim 87A at ₹6.5L.

### Senior citizen with ₹5.5L income (old regime). With ₹3L senior citizen exemption, taxable = ₹2.5L. 87A rebate?

Taxable income ₹2.5L is below ₹5L threshold — 87A applies. But the tax on ₹2.5L for a senior citizen is actually already ₹0 (₹3L exemption means no tax below ₹3L). So 87A isn't even needed in this case — the senior citizen exemption already covers it.
