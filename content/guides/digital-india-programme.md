## What is Digital India Programme?

Digital India is a flagship programme launched in 2015 by the Government of India to transform India into a digitally empowered society and knowledge economy. The programme focuses on three key areas:

1. **Digital infrastructure** as a core utility for every citizen
2. **Governance and services on demand** — making government services digitally accessible
3. **Digital empowerment** of citizens through digital literacy

With a vision to ensure that government services are made available to citizens electronically, Digital India has launched numerous platforms and initiatives that touch every aspect of citizen-government interaction.

## Nine Pillars of Digital India

### 1. Broadband Highways
- **BharatNet** — Connecting 2.5 lakh+ gram panchayats with optical fibre
- **NOFN** (National Optical Fibre Network) — Backbone infrastructure
- Target: Broadband connectivity to all villages

### 2. Universal Access to Mobile Connectivity
- Coverage of uncovered villages with mobile connectivity
- 55,000+ villages connected through telecom towers
- Subsidy scheme for mobile towers in remote areas

### 3. Public Internet Access Programme
- **Common Service Centres (CSCs)** — 4 lakh+ centres across India
- Post offices as multi-service centres
- CSCs deliver government and private services at village level

### 4. e-Governance — Reforming Government Through Technology
- Process re-engineering for online delivery of services
- Integration of services across departments
- **e-District** project for district-level services online

### 5. e-Kranti — Electronic Delivery of Services
- **e-Education** — Digital classrooms, MOOCs, virtual labs
- **e-Healthcare** — Telemedicine, online health records (ABHA)
- **e-Courts** — Case status, filing, and hearing online
- **e-Agriculture** — Soil health cards, mandi prices, PM-KISAN

### 6. Information for All
- **Open Government Data (data.gov.in)** — Public datasets
- **MyGov** — Citizen engagement platform
- Social media presence of all government departments

### 7. Electronics Manufacturing
- Promotion of domestic manufacturing through PLI scheme
- Make in India for electronics
- Target: Net-zero imports in electronics

### 8. IT for Jobs
- Training for IT/ITES sector jobs
- BPO promotion scheme for smaller cities
- Skill development through PMKVY and NASSCOM partnership

### 9. Early Harvest Programmes
- Wi-Fi in universities and public places
- Email for all government employees
- Biometric attendance system
- Digital textbooks

## Key Digital India Platforms You Can Use

### DigiLocker
A cloud-based document storage platform linked to your Aadhaar:
- **Store documents** — Aadhaar, PAN, driving licence, mark sheets, etc.
- **Access issued documents** directly from issuing agencies (CBSE, universities, transport departments)
- **Legally valid** — DigiLocker documents are accepted as originals
- **Free to use** — 1 GB storage for every citizen
- Website: [digilocker.gov.in](https://www.digilocker.gov.in)

### UMANG App
Unified Mobile Application for New-age Governance:
- **1,000+ government services** in one app
- PF balance check, passport status, scholarship tracking
- Utility bill payments, tax filing links
- Available on Android and iOS
- Website: [web.umang.gov.in](https://web.umang.gov.in)

### Common Service Centres (CSC)
- **4 lakh+ centres** across India — find one at [locator.csccloud.in](https://locator.csccloud.in)
- Services: Aadhaar, PAN, passport, banking, insurance, certificates
- Operated by Village Level Entrepreneurs (VLEs)
- Brings government services to rural areas

### e-Hospital / Online Registration System (ORS)
- Online appointment booking at government hospitals
- View lab reports and discharge summaries
- Track blood availability
- Website: [ers.nha.gov.in](https://ers.nha.gov.in)

### Government e-Marketplace (GeM)
- Online procurement platform for government departments
- Open to MSME sellers — register and sell to government
- Transparent and paperless procurement

### Aarogya Setu
- Health and wellness app
- COVID vaccination certificate
- Telemedicine services
- Health ID (ABHA) integration

### BHIM UPI
- Digital payment app by NPCI
- Send/receive money using UPI
- Scan and pay at shops
- Bill payments and recharges

## How to Access Digital India Services

### Step 1: Get an Aadhaar Card
Aadhaar is the gateway to most digital services. See our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online).

### Step 2: Create a DigiLocker Account
1. Visit [digilocker.gov.in](https://www.digilocker.gov.in)
2. Sign up with Aadhaar and mobile number
3. Verify via OTP
4. Start pulling documents from issuers

### Step 3: Download UMANG App
1. Install from Google Play or App Store
2. Register with mobile number
3. Browse available government services
4. Access services with Aadhaar verification

### Step 4: Find Your Nearest CSC
Visit [locator.csccloud.in](https://locator.csccloud.in) to find a Common Service Centre near you for assisted access to digital services.

## Important Tips

1. **DigiLocker documents are legally valid** — Stop carrying photocopies; pull documents on your phone
2. **UMANG app is underrated** — One app for PF, pension, scholarships, utility bills, and much more
3. **CSCs help the non-tech-savvy** — If you're not comfortable online, visit a CSC for assisted service
4. **Keep Aadhaar updated** — Most Digital India services are Aadhaar-linked; ensure your details are current
5. **Use official platforms only** — Avoid third-party apps/websites claiming to offer government services

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is DigiLocker safe and secure?
Yes, DigiLocker uses Aadhaar-based authentication and 256-bit SSL encryption. Documents are stored on government servers and are accessible only by the account holder. It's managed by MeitY.

### Can I use UMANG app for all government services?
UMANG covers 1,000+ services but not everything. For specialised services (like ITR filing or passport application), you may need to use the respective department's portal. UMANG provides links to these portals.

### Are Digital India services free?
Most Digital India platforms are free to use. Specific services (like certificate applications) may have government fees, but the digital platforms themselves don't charge extra.

### How do I complain about poor digital service delivery?
Use the **CPGRAMS** portal (pgportal.gov.in) for grievances related to government digital services. You can also use the MyGov platform for feedback.

### Is internet connectivity required for all Digital India services?
Yes, most services require internet access. However, USSD-based services (like *99# for banking) work on basic phones without internet. CSCs provide internet access in rural areas.

### What is the role of Common Service Centres?
CSCs are the last-mile delivery points for digital services. They bridge the digital divide by providing assisted access to over 400 government and private services in rural and semi-urban areas.
