## When Do You Need to Transfer Your Voter ID?

When you **move to a new address**, you need to update your voter registration so you can vote at your new location. The process depends on whether you've moved:

- **Within the same assembly constituency** → Use **Form 8A** (address change within constituency)
- **To a different assembly constituency** (same or different state) → Use **Form 6** (new registration in the new constituency)

Your old registration will be **automatically deleted** from the previous electoral roll once the new one is processed.

## Form 6 vs Form 8A — Which One to Use?

| Scenario | Form to Use | What Happens |
|---|---|---|
| Moved to a **different constituency** (same city/district) | Form 6 | New registration; old entry deleted |
| Moved to a **different state** | Form 6 | New registration in new state; old entry deleted |
| Moved **within the same constituency** (new house/area) | Form 8A | Address updated; same EPIC number retained |

> **Tip:** Not sure about your constituency? Search your address on [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in) to find which assembly constituency it falls under.

## Documents Required

### For Form 6 (New Constituency)

- **Address proof at new location** (any one):
  - Aadhaar card with updated address
  - Utility bill (electricity/water/gas — not older than 3 months)
  - Bank passbook with new address
  - Rent agreement with landlord's ID proof
  - Passport with new address
  - Ration card

- **Identity proof** (any one):
  - Aadhaar card
  - Passport
  - Driving licence
  - PAN card

- **Passport-size photograph** (JPEG, 10–50 KB, white background)

- **Previous EPIC number** (optional but recommended for faster deletion of old entry)

### For Form 8A (Same Constituency)

- **Address proof at new location** (same list as above)
- **EPIC number** (your existing Voter ID number)

## How to Transfer Voter ID Online — Different Constituency (Form 6)

### Step-by-Step Process

1. Visit [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in)
2. **Login** or create a new account with your mobile number
3. Select **"New Voter Registration (Form 6)"**
4. In the form, select the reason: **"Shifted from another constituency"**
5. Enter your **personal details** (name, DOB, gender, relative's name)
6. Enter your **new residential address** with:
   - State, district, assembly constituency
   - Full address with pin code
7. In the **"Previous Registration"** section:
   - Enter your **previous EPIC number**
   - Select the **state and constituency** where you were previously registered
   - This ensures your old entry is deleted
8. Upload required documents:
   - New address proof
   - Identity proof
   - Passport-size photograph
9. Fill in the **declaration**
10. **Preview and submit**
11. Note the **Reference ID** for tracking

> **Important:** Always provide your previous EPIC number and old constituency details. This prevents duplicate entries in the electoral roll across constituencies.

## How to Transfer Voter ID Online — Same Constituency (Form 8A)

### Step-by-Step Process

1. Visit [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in)
2. **Login** with your registered credentials
3. Select **"Correction of Entries / Form 8"** (Form 8A is filed through the same section)
4. Enter your **EPIC number** to fetch your existing details
5. Select **"Address Change"** as the correction type
6. Enter your **new address** within the same constituency
7. Upload **address proof** for the new address
8. Fill in the **declaration**
9. **Preview and submit**
10. Note the **Reference ID**

## Offline Transfer Process

### For Different Constituency (Form 6)
1. Download **Form 6** from [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in)
2. Fill in your details and mention previous registration details
3. Attach address proof, ID proof, and photograph copies
4. Submit to the **ERO** of your **new constituency**

### For Same Constituency (Form 8A)
1. Download **Form 8A** from the portal
2. Fill in your EPIC number and new address
3. Attach new address proof
4. Submit to the **ERO** of your constituency

## BLO Verification After Transfer

After you file the transfer application:

1. **BLO at your new address** will visit to verify your residence
2. **BLO at your old address** (for Form 6) may verify that you have moved out
3. Both reports are submitted to the respective EROs
4. Once verified, your name is **added to the new electoral roll** and **deleted from the old one**

## Processing Timeline

| Transfer Type | Typical Timeline |
|---|---|
| Same constituency (Form 8A) | 15–30 days |
| Different constituency, same state (Form 6) | 15–45 days |
| Different state (Form 6) | 30–60 days |
| During special revision drives | 7–15 days |

> **Note:** Cross-state transfers may take longer as coordination between two state election commissions is required.

## Important Tips

1. **Don't register in the new place without mentioning old registration** — this creates duplicate entries, which is illegal under electoral law
2. **Apply as soon as you move** — don't wait for election season when processing may be paused
3. **Keep address proof ready** at the new location — rent agreement works if Aadhaar isn't updated yet
4. **Update your Aadhaar address first** — it makes the Voter ID transfer smoother since Aadhaar is the most accepted address proof. See our [Aadhaar address update guide](/guide/aadhaar-update-mobile-email)
5. **Verify after approval** — search your name on [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in) to confirm you're listed in the new constituency

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Will my EPIC number change if I move to a new constituency?
Yes, when you register via Form 6 in a new constituency, you will be issued a **new EPIC number**. The old number becomes invalid.

### Will my old voter registration be deleted automatically?
When you provide your previous EPIC number in Form 6, the Election Commission initiates deletion of your old entry. However, it's good practice to verify after a few weeks.

### Can I vote at my old address while the transfer is pending?
Until your new registration is approved and old one deleted, your name remains on the old electoral roll. Technically you can vote there, but it's best to complete the transfer before election season.

### I've moved to a different state. Is the process different?
The process is the same — file Form 6 at [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in) with your new state and constituency details. Cross-state transfers may take slightly longer (30–60 days).

### What if I don't know my previous EPIC number?
Search for your name on [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in) using the "Search in Electoral Roll" feature. You can search by name, state, and constituency to find your EPIC number.

### Can I transfer my Voter ID for a temporary address?
You should register at the address where you **ordinarily reside**. Temporary stays (e.g., short work assignments) generally don't qualify. If you've permanently moved, proceed with the transfer.

### What if I'm a student staying in a hostel in another state?
Students can register at their hostel address if it's their **ordinary place of residence**. The hostel allotment letter or institution's address certificate can serve as address proof.
