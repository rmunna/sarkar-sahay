# WBJEE 2026 — West Bengal Joint Entrance Examination

**WBJEE 2026** (West Bengal Joint Entrance Examination) is conducted by the **WBJEEB (West Bengal Joint Entrance Examinations Board)** for admission to engineering, architecture, and pharmacy courses in West Bengal government and private colleges. WBJEE is also accepted by some other state universities. Around 1.5 lakh students appear annually.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [wbjeeb.nic.in](https://wbjeeb.nic.in). Exam in **April/May 2026**. West Bengal domicile for state quota. Syllabus based on **WBCHSE (West Bengal Board) Class 11 & 12**.

## Eligibility Criteria

| Criteria | Requirement |
|----------|-------------|
| **Qualification** | Class 12 (10+2) with PCM from any recognised board |
| **Minimum marks** | 45% in PCM (40% for SC/ST/OBC-A/OBC-B WB) |
| **Age** | Must be 17+ as of 31 December of admission year |
| **Domicile** | WB residence/domicile for state quota seats |

### West Bengal Domicile for State Quota

For the **85% state quota** in WBJEE:
- Must be a West Bengal resident (permanent resident or born in WB)
- Studied in WB schools for prescribed period, OR
- Parent is WB government employee

## WBJEE 2026 Application Process

1. Go to [wbjeeb.nic.in](https://wbjeeb.nic.in) → **"WBJEE 2026"**
2. Register with mobile number + email
3. Fill application:
   - Personal details (name, DOB, category)
   - Class 12 board and marks
   - Exam centre preference (WB districts)
4. Upload: photo (white/light background) + signature
5. Pay fee:
   - General/OBC: **₹500**
   - SC/ST West Bengal: **₹350**
6. Download admit card closer to exam date

## Exam Pattern

| Paper | Subjects | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|-------|----------|-----------|-------|---------|
| Paper 1 | Mathematics | 75 | 100 | 2 hours |
| Paper 2 | Physics + Chemistry | 40+40 = 80 | 100 | 2 hours |

- **Mode**: Offline (OMR-based)
- **Marking**: Category 1 (1 mark each), Category 2 (2 marks each), Category 3 (2 marks, partial)
- **Negative marking**: -½ for Category 1 MCQs

---

## Syllabus 2026

WBJEE follows the **WBCHSE (West Bengal Board) Class 11 & 12 syllabus**:

**Mathematics**: Algebra (Complex Numbers, Permutations, Matrices), Trigonometry, Coordinate Geometry, Calculus (Differential + Integral), Vector Algebra, Statistics & Probability

**Physics**: Mechanics, Wave & Optics, Current Electricity, Electromagnetism, Modern Physics, Electronics

**Chemistry**: Physical Chemistry (Atomic Structure, Chemical Bonding, Thermodynamics, Equilibrium, Electrochemistry), Organic Chemistry, Inorganic Chemistry

## WBJEE vs JEE Main — Key Differences

| Aspect | WBJEE | JEE Main |
|--------|-------|---------|
| Difficulty | **Moderate** | Higher |
| Marks | 200 (both papers) | 300 (Paper 1) |
| Negative marking | Yes (−½) | Yes (−1 for MCQ) |
| Syllabus | WBCHSE Board | NCERT + beyond |
| Mode | Offline (OMR) | Online (CBT) |
| Used for | WB state colleges | NITs, IIITs |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can JEE Main score be used for WBJEE counselling?

**No** — WBJEE counselling uses only WBJEE rank. However, some central institutions in West Bengal (like IIT Kharagpur) use JEE Advanced/Main. For Jadavpur and state colleges, WBJEE rank is required.

### Students from other states — are there seats?

Yes — some state colleges reserve seats for **outside-West Bengal students**. However, the bulk of seats (85%) go to WB domicile holders. Check specific college seat matrix during counselling.

### Is WBJEE score accepted outside West Bengal?

Some universities outside West Bengal (like Tripura, Assam for specific seats) may accept WBJEE. Verify each institution's admission criteria directly.
