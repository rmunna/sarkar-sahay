# Google Pay Money Sent But Not Received — How to Get a Refund

One of the most stressful UPI issues is when money gets debited from your account but the recipient says they have not received it. This can happen due to a stuck transaction, bank server delays, or a failed settlement between banks. This guide explains what to do step by step.

## Why Does This Happen?

When you make a UPI payment on Google Pay, the money flows through multiple systems:

1. Your bank debits your account
2. NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India) routes the transaction
3. The recipient's bank credits their account

If any step fails midway, the money may be debited from your account but not credited to the recipient. Common reasons include:

- **Bank server timeout** — the sender's bank debited but the receiver's bank did not respond in time
- **NPCI routing delay** — the transaction is stuck in NPCI's system
- **Transaction pending** — the transaction has not fully completed yet
- **Incorrect beneficiary details** — rare with UPI, but possible if the UPI ID is linked to a different account than expected

## Step 1: Check the Transaction Status

Before panicking, check the actual status:

1. Open Google Pay → tap on the transaction in your history
2. Check if it shows **"Completed,"** **"Pending,"** or **"Failed"**
3. If **Pending:** Wait. The transaction is still being processed. Most pending transactions resolve within 30 minutes to 2 hours
4. If **Completed:** The money has left your bank. Ask the recipient to check their bank statement (not just the UPI app balance)
5. If **Failed:** The money should be auto-reversed. Check your bank statement

## Step 2: Find Your UTR (UPI Transaction Reference) Number

The UTR is the unique tracking ID for your transaction. You will need it for any complaint:

1. Open Google Pay → tap on the transaction
2. Look for **"UPI Transaction ID"** or **"UTR Number"** — it is a 12-digit alphanumeric code
3. Note it down or take a screenshot
4. You can also find the UTR in your bank's SMS alert or net banking transaction details

## Step 3: Wait for Auto-Reversal

If the transaction failed midway, NPCI's system automatically reverses the amount:

- **Standard timeline:** 3-5 business days as per RBI guidelines
- **In practice:** Most auto-reversals happen within 24-48 hours
- **Maximum:** Up to 7 working days in rare cases
- Check your bank statement daily — the reversal will appear as a credit entry
- The reversal amount will match the original transaction amount

**Do NOT initiate a new payment assuming the first one failed** — the recipient may receive both payments.

## Step 4: Ask the Recipient to Check Their Bank

Sometimes the money is credited but does not show in the recipient's UPI app:

1. Ask the recipient to check their **bank statement** or **passbook** (not just the UPI app)
2. UPI app balance may not refresh instantly — they should check net banking or call their bank
3. If the recipient's bank confirms the credit, the issue is resolved — the UPI app will eventually sync

## Step 5: Raise a Complaint in Google Pay

If auto-reversal does not happen within 3 business days:

1. Open Google Pay → go to transaction history
2. Tap on the problematic transaction
3. Select **"Report a problem"** or **"Get help"**
4. Choose the appropriate issue (money debited but not received by recipient)
5. Submit the complaint — Google Pay will investigate with the banks
6. You will receive updates in the app and via email
7. Note the complaint reference number for follow-up

## Step 6: Contact Your Bank Directly

If the Google Pay complaint does not resolve the issue:

1. Call your bank's customer care number (found on the back of your debit card or bank website)
2. Provide the UTR number, transaction date, amount, and recipient details
3. Request them to trace the transaction and confirm if it was settled
4. Ask for a written complaint reference number
5. Banks are required to resolve UPI complaints within 30 days as per RBI guidelines

## Step 7: File an NPCI Dispute

If neither Google Pay nor your bank resolves the issue within 30 days:

1. Visit the NPCI UPI dispute redressal page
2. You can also write to NPCI at their official email mentioned on [npci.org.in](https://www.npci.org.in)
3. Provide: UTR number, transaction date, amount, sender and receiver bank details, and details of complaints already filed
4. NPCI will coordinate between both banks to resolve the dispute

## Step 8: File a Banking Ombudsman Complaint (Last Resort)

If the issue remains unresolved after 30 days:

1. Visit the RBI Complaint Management System at [https://cms.rbi.org.in](https://cms.rbi.org.in)
2. File a complaint against your bank under the Banking Ombudsman Scheme
3. Provide all documentation: UTR number, bank complaint reference, Google Pay complaint reference
4. The Ombudsman will investigate and issue a binding decision
5. You can claim compensation for the delay as per RBI guidelines

## Transaction Status Meanings

| Status | What It Means | Action |
|--------|--------------|--------|
| **Completed** | Money debited and credited successfully | Ask recipient to check bank statement |
| **Pending** | Transaction is being processed | Wait 2-4 hours; usually auto-resolves |
| **Failed** | Transaction did not go through | Auto-reversal within 3-5 business days |
| **Deemed** | Timed out; treated as failed | Auto-reversal will be initiated by NPCI |

## Important Tips

- Always save your UTR number for every UPI transaction — it is your proof
- Do not make a second payment until the first one is confirmed as failed and reversed
- Check your bank statement, not just the Google Pay app, for the most accurate transaction status
- If the recipient claims they did not receive the money, ask them to check with their bank directly
- Keep screenshots of all transaction details and complaint references

## FAQs

**Q: How long does a Google Pay refund take for a failed transaction?**
A: Auto-reversals typically happen within 3-5 business days. In most cases, the refund is credited within 24-48 hours. If it takes more than 7 working days, raise a complaint.

**Q: The transaction shows "Completed" but the recipient did not get the money. What do I do?**
A: Ask the recipient to check their bank statement (not just UPI app). If the bank confirms no credit, raise a complaint in Google Pay with the UTR number. The issue is likely between the two banks.

**Q: Can I cancel a completed Google Pay UPI transaction?**
A: No. Once a UPI transaction is completed, it cannot be reversed or cancelled by the sender. You must request the recipient to send the money back, or raise a dispute if it was unauthorized.

**Q: What is a UTR number and where do I find it?**
A: UTR (Unique Transaction Reference) is a 12-digit code assigned to every UPI transaction. Find it in Google Pay by tapping on the transaction, or in your bank's SMS or net banking transaction details.

**Q: My money was debited twice for the same transaction. What should I do?**
A: One of the transactions will likely be auto-reversed. Check your bank statement after 3-5 business days. If both amounts remain debited, raise a complaint with Google Pay and your bank with both UTR numbers.

**Q: Can I file a police complaint for UPI transaction issues?**
A: Transaction failures are banking issues, not criminal matters. Use the complaint process: Google Pay → Bank → NPCI → Banking Ombudsman. However, if you suspect fraud or unauthorized transactions, file a cyber crime complaint at [https://cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in).
