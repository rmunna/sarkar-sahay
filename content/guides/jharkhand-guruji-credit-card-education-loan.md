## What is the Jharkhand Guruji Credit Card?

**Guruji Credit Card** is an education loan scheme by the **Jharkhand Shiksha Evam Protsahan Vittiya Corporation (JSEFC)** that gives **loans up to ₹15 lakh at 4% interest** to students in Jharkhand for all levels of education from Class 10 to post-graduation. The scheme aims to make higher education accessible to students from economically weaker backgrounds.

---

## Key Benefits

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Loan amount | Up to ₹15 lakh per student |
| Interest rate | 4% per annum (subsidised) |
| Target | Students from Class 10 to post-graduation |
| Courses covered | All accredited courses — school, graduation, PG, professional |
| Repayment | Starts after course completion + 6 months |
| Moratorium | Course duration + 6 months |
| Collateral | Not required for loans up to ₹7.5 lakh |

---

## Who is Eligible?

**You ARE eligible if:**
- You are a **domicile of Jharkhand** (Aadhaar with Jharkhand address)
- You are admitted to or appearing for admission in an **accredited educational institution**
- Your family's **annual income is ₹3 lakh or less** (check current income limit at jsefc.jharkhand.gov.in)
- Your age is 18–35 years (check current limit)

**Courses covered:**
- Madhyamik/Matric (Class 10)
- Class 11–12 (Intermediate)
- Graduation (BA, BSc, BCom, BBA, BCA, B.Tech, MBBS, etc.)
- Post-graduation (MA, MSc, MBA, M.Tech, MD, MCA, etc.)
- Professional/vocational courses at accredited institutions

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar Card** — student and parent/guardian
2. **Domicile certificate** — Jharkhand residency
3. **Admission letter** — from the educational institution
4. **Previous year's marksheet** — to show academic progress
5. **Income certificate** — family income from SDM/BDO
6. **Bank account** — in student's name (Aadhaar-linked)
7. **Photograph**
8. **Fee structure** — from the institution

## How to Apply — Step by Step

### Step 1 — Register on JSEFC Portal

Go to **[jsefc.jharkhand.gov.in](https://jsefc.jharkhand.gov.in)**:
1. Click **"Student Registration"**
2. Enter your Aadhaar and mobile number
3. Complete profile creation

### Step 2 — Apply for Guruji Credit Card

After registration:
1. Click **"Apply for Guruji Credit Card"**
2. Fill in educational details, institution name, course, fee structure
3. Enter family income and upload income certificate
4. Upload all required documents

### Step 3 — Bank Processing

Your application is forwarded to the lending bank. Bank may call for an interview/document verification.

### Step 4 — Loan Sanction and Disbursement

After sanction, the loan amount is disbursed:
- Directly to the institution for tuition fees
- To student's account for living expenses (hostels, books, etc.)

### Step 5 — Repayment

Repayment begins 6 months after course completion:
- 4% interest rate applies throughout
- Monthly EMIs from Jharkhand domicile-linked bank account

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. I got admission in a private engineering college in Ranchi. Does Guruji Credit Card cover it?**

Yes, provided the college is an **accredited institution** (AICTE-approved). Confirm accreditation status with JSEFC before applying.

**2. Do I need to provide a guarantor for the loan?**

For loans up to ₹7.5 lakh, no collateral or guarantor is typically required. Above ₹7.5 lakh, the institution may ask for a co-applicant/guarantor.

**3. My family income is ₹4 lakh but we are very poor. Can I still apply?**

The income limit may vary — check current norms at jsefc.jharkhand.gov.in. If slightly above the limit, some flexibility may be available for very poor families on case-by-case basis.

**4. I am studying outside Jharkhand (in Delhi). Am I eligible?**

Yes. The scheme covers students studying at accredited institutions anywhere in India — not just within Jharkhand. You must be a Jharkhand domicile.

**5. I completed my course 1 year ago and haven't started repayment. What happens?**

Interest accrues during the moratorium period (course + 6 months). After the moratorium ends, EMI begins automatically. If you haven't started repayment, contact JSEFC to regularize your account and avoid penalty.
