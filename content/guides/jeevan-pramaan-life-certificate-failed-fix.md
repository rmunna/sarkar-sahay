# Jeevan Pramaan Digital Life Certificate Failed — How to Fix

Every year, **lakhs of pensioners** across India must submit a Digital Life Certificate (DLC) through Jeevan Pramaan to continue receiving their pension. But the process doesn't always go smoothly — biometric failures, Aadhaar authentication errors, face recognition glitches, and PPO mismatches can block your submission entirely.

If your Jeevan Pramaan attempt **failed**, this guide walks you through every known issue and its fix — so your pension doesn't get disrupted.

> **Related:** If your pension has already stopped or wasn't credited, see our guide on [Pension Payment Not Credited to Bank — How to Fix](/guides/pension-payment-not-credited-fix).

---

## What Is Jeevan Pramaan?

Jeevan Pramaan is a **biometric-based Digital Life Certificate** system by the Government of India. It lets pensioners prove they are alive — a requirement to keep receiving pension — without physically visiting their bank or pension office.

You can submit your life certificate through:
- **Jeevan Pramaan mobile app** (Android/iOS)
- **Jeevan Pramaan desktop app** (with USB biometric device)
- **CSC (Common Service Centre)**
- **Bank branch** or **post office** (for postal pensioners)
- **Doorstep service** by postman (India Post pensioners)

## 1. Biometric Device Not Working

This is the **most common failure** when using the desktop version of Jeevan Pramaan with a USB fingerprint scanner.

### Symptoms
- "Unable to capture biometric" or "Device not detected"
- Fingerprint scanner light doesn't turn on
- App freezes during biometric capture

### How to Fix

**Step 1: Check device compatibility**
Only RD (Registered Device) compliant biometric devices work with Jeevan Pramaan. Common supported devices:
- Mantra MFS100 / MFS110
- Morpho MSO 1300 E2 / E3
- Startek FM220
- Cogent CSD200 / SecuGen Hamster Pro

> ⚠️ Old devices without RD service will NOT work. You need the latest RD service driver installed.

**Step 2: Install/update the RD service**
1. Go to the biometric device manufacturer's website
2. Download the latest **RD Service** driver for your OS
3. Install and restart your computer
4. Check if the RD service is running (look for the device management utility in the system tray)

**Step 3: Fix fingerprint capture issues**
- **Clean your fingers** and the scanner surface with a dry cloth
- If fingers are dry/worn (common in elderly), **moisturize slightly** before scanning
- Try **different fingers** — index finger or thumb works best
- Press firmly but don't slide across the sensor

**Step 4: Test the device separately**
Visit the device manufacturer's test portal to confirm your fingerprint scanner is working before attempting Jeevan Pramaan again.

**Step 5: Use face authentication instead**
If biometric keeps failing, switch to the **mobile app** and use face authentication (see [Section 3](#3-face-authentication-errors-on-app)).

## 2. Aadhaar Authentication Failed

The Jeevan Pramaan system verifies your identity through UIDAI's Aadhaar database. If Aadhaar authentication fails, the certificate cannot be generated.

### Symptoms
- "Aadhaar authentication failed — please try again"
- "Technical error from UIDAI"
- OTP not received on registered mobile

### How to Fix

**Step 1: Verify your Aadhaar mobile link**
Jeevan Pramaan sends an OTP to your Aadhaar-linked mobile number. Check if your number is linked:
1. Visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in)
2. Enter your Aadhaar number → Request OTP
3. If you don't receive the OTP, your mobile number is **not linked or outdated**

**How to update mobile in Aadhaar:**
- Visit your nearest **Aadhaar Enrolment Centre**
- Carry original Aadhaar card + the new mobile phone
- This is a **free service** — no charges

**Step 2: Check for demographic mismatch**
Your name, date of birth, and gender in Aadhaar must match what's in the pension records. Even a small spelling difference can cause failure.
- Download your Aadhaar from [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in)
- Compare name/DOB with your PPO or pension passbook
- If there's a mismatch, update whichever record is incorrect

**Step 3: Wait and retry**
UIDAI servers occasionally go down during peak hours. If you get a "technical error":
- Wait 30 minutes and try again
- Avoid peak hours (10 AM–1 PM on deadline days)
- Try late evening or early morning

**Step 4: Check if Aadhaar is locked**
If you locked your Aadhaar biometrics for security:
1. Visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in)
2. Go to **Lock/Unlock Biometrics**
3. Unlock temporarily, complete Jeevan Pramaan, then re-lock

## 3. Face Authentication Errors on App

Since 2021, the Jeevan Pramaan mobile app supports **face authentication** — a blessing for elderly pensioners whose fingerprints don't scan well. But it comes with its own issues.

### Symptoms
- "Face not detected" or "Liveness check failed"
- "Unable to match face with Aadhaar photo"
- App camera shows black screen
- "Face authentication is not enabled for your Aadhaar"

### How to Fix

**Step 1: Update the app**
Download the latest version of **Jeevan Pramaan Face App** from:
- [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=in.gov.jeevanpramaan.faceverification)
- Apple App Store (search "Jeevan Pramaan")

**Step 2: Ensure face data exists in Aadhaar**
Face authentication works only if your photo is available in the UIDAI database. If you enrolled in Aadhaar a long time ago, your photo quality may be poor.
- Visit an Aadhaar centre to **update your photo** (free of cost if updating along with biometrics)

**Step 3: Fix camera and lighting**
- Use a well-lit room — **natural daylight** works best
- Face the camera directly, keep your face centered
- Remove glasses, cap, or anything covering your face
- Hold the phone steady at **arm's length**
- Ensure the phone's front camera is clean

**Step 4: Pass the liveness check**
The app checks you are a real person (not a photo). Tips:
- **Blink naturally** when prompted
- Follow on-screen instructions (turn head, nod)
- Don't move too fast or too slow
- Avoid backlighting (don't stand in front of a window)

**Step 5: Grant app permissions**
Go to your phone's Settings → Apps → Jeevan Pramaan → Permissions:
- ✅ Camera — must be allowed
- ✅ Location — may be required
- ✅ Storage — for saving the certificate

**Step 6: If face auth still fails**
- Try using the **fingerprint option** on the app (needs OTG-compatible phone + USB biometric device)
- Visit a **CSC or bank** as a fallback (see [Section 6](#6-submit-via-csc-bank-or-post-office))

## 4. PPO Number Not Found

The PPO (Pension Payment Order) number uniquely identifies your pension account. If Jeevan Pramaan says "PPO not found," your certificate submission will fail.

### Symptoms
- "PPO number not found in records"
- "Invalid PPO number"
- "No pension details found for this Aadhaar"

### How to Fix

**Step 1: Verify your PPO number**
Check these documents for the correct PPO number:
- **Pension Payment Order** (original document from your department)
- **Pension passbook** issued by your bank
- **Previous life certificate** (if you have a copy)
- **SMS/email from CPAO** when pension was sanctioned

> 💡 PPO numbers follow a specific format depending on your pension type (e.g., central government, state, defence). Make sure you're entering the complete number including any prefix.

**Step 2: Check on Bhavishya portal (for central govt.)**
1. Visit [bhavishya.nic.in](https://bhavishya.nic.in)
2. Log in with your credentials
3. Your PPO details should be visible under your profile

**Step 3: Check with CPAO (for central civil pensioners)**
1. Visit [cpao.nic.in](https://cpao.nic.in)
2. Click **"Pensioner's Corner"** → **"Know Your PPO Status"**
3. Enter your details to find your PPO number

**Step 4: Register on Jeevan Pramaan portal first**
Before generating a certificate on the app, register your details at:
1. Visit [jeevanpramaan.gov.in](https://jeevanpramaan.gov.in)
2. Click **"Pensioner Registration"**
3. Enter your Aadhaar number, PPO number, bank details, and pension type
4. Verify via OTP
5. Now try generating the DLC again

**Step 5: Contact your pension disbursing authority**
If the PPO is still not found:
- **Central govt:** Contact CPAO at cpao-delhi@nic.in
- **State govt:** Contact your state treasury/AG office
- **Defence:** Contact PCDA (Pensions) Allahabad
- **Bank pensioners:** Visit your pension-disbursing bank branch

## 5. Pension Details Mismatch

Even if your PPO number is correct, a mismatch in your personal details between Aadhaar and pension records will block the life certificate.

### Symptoms
- "Name mismatch with pension records"
- "Bank details do not match"
- "Pension scheme type mismatch"
- Certificate generates but pension authority rejects it

### How to Fix

**Step 1: Identify what's mismatched**
Compare these fields across your Aadhaar card, PPO, and bank records:
- **Full name** (spelling must match exactly)
- **Date of birth**
- **Bank account number**
- **IFSC code**
- **Pension scheme/type** (e.g., superannuation, family pension, disability)

**Step 2: Update Aadhaar if it's wrong there**
- Visit an Aadhaar Enrolment Centre with supporting documents
- Or update online at [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in) (limited fields only)

**Step 3: Update pension records if Aadhaar is correct**
- **Central govt pensioners:** Submit a correction request through [cpengrams.gov.in](https://cpengrams.gov.in)
- **State pensioners:** Contact your state treasury office
- **Bank update:** Visit your pension-disbursing branch with PPO and Aadhaar copy

**Step 4: Ensure bank account is in your name**
Joint accounts may cause issues. The pension account must be either:
- In **your name only**, or
- A joint account where **you are the primary holder**

## 6. Submit via CSC, Bank, or Post Office

If digital methods keep failing, you have **several offline alternatives** to submit your life certificate. Your pension won't be affected as long as you submit through any valid channel before the deadline.

### Option A: Common Service Centre (CSC)

CSCs are available across India and have trained operators with biometric devices.

1. Find your nearest CSC at [locator.csccloud.in](https://locator.csccloud.in)
2. Carry: **Aadhaar card + PPO copy + pension passbook**
3. The operator will capture your biometrics and generate the DLC
4. **Fee:** ₹0 (free for pensioners in most states; some CSCs may charge ₹20–50 — this is not official)
5. Collect the **Pramaan ID** receipt for your records

### Option B: Bank Branch

Most nationalized banks accept life certificates at the branch.

1. Visit your **pension-disbursing bank branch**
2. Carry: Aadhaar card + PPO + pension passbook
3. The bank will either:
   - Generate a digital life certificate using their biometric device, or
   - Accept a **physical life certificate** (Form-9 for central govt.)
4. Get an acknowledgment receipt

### Option C: Post Office (for postal pensioners)

India Post offers **doorstep life certificate** services for pensioners above 80 years.

1. Visit your post office or request a postman visit
2. The postman carries a biometric device to your home
3. Your DLC is generated on the spot
4. Applicable for pensioners under **Department of Posts pension scheme**

### Option D: Video-Based Life Certificate

Some pension authorities now accept life certificates via video call. Check with your specific pension disbursing authority if this option is available.

## Jeevan Pramaan Deadline and Validity

| Detail | Information |
|--------|-------------|
| **Submission period** | 1st November to 30th November every year |
| **Extended deadline** | Usually extended to 31st December or 28th February (varies by year) |
| **Validity** | One year from the date of submission |
| **Super senior citizens (80+)** | Can submit from **1st October** (one month early) |
| **Who needs it** | All pensioners — central, state, defence, family pension holders |

> ⚠️ **Don't miss the deadline.** If you don't submit a life certificate, your pension will be **stopped** from the following month. If that has already happened, follow our guide on [Pension Payment Not Credited — How to Fix](/guides/pension-payment-not-credited-fix).

## Checklist Before You Start

Use this checklist to avoid common failures:

- [ ] Aadhaar card with **updated mobile number** linked
- [ ] Aadhaar biometrics **unlocked** (if you had locked them)
- [ ] Correct **PPO number** verified from pension documents
- [ ] **Bank details** matching across Aadhaar, PPO, and bank records
- [ ] **Jeevan Pramaan app** updated to the latest version
- [ ] **RD service** installed and running (for desktop with biometric device)
- [ ] Phone camera working + permissions granted (for face authentication)
- [ ] Good lighting and clean camera lens (for face authentication)

## How to Check If Your Life Certificate Was Accepted

After successful submission:

1. You receive a **Pramaan ID** (a unique certificate number)
2. Visit [jeevanpramaan.gov.in](https://jeevanpramaan.gov.in) → **"Search Certificate"**
3. Enter your Pramaan ID or Aadhaar number
4. The certificate should show as **"Active"**
5. Your pension disbursing authority (bank/treasury) automatically receives it

Also check:
- **DigiLocker:** Your DLC is also available in your [DigiLocker](https://digilocker.gov.in) account
- **SMS:** You'll receive a confirmation SMS on your Aadhaar-linked mobile

## Still Stuck? File a Grievance

If nothing works and your pension is at risk:

1. **CPENGRAMS** (for central govt. pension grievances): [cpengrams.gov.in](https://cpengrams.gov.in)
2. **IGMS** (banking complaints): [cms.rbi.org.in](https://cms.rbi.org.in)
3. **Centralized Pension Grievance:** [pgportal.gov.in](https://pgportal.gov.in)
4. **Jeevan Pramaan helpline:** 1800-111-555 (toll-free)
5. **Email:** support-jeevanpramaan@gov.in

When filing a grievance, include:
- Your PPO number
- Aadhaar number (masked)
- Screenshot of the error
- Date and time of failed attempt
- Pramaan ID (if partially generated)

---

## Key Takeaways

1. **Biometric failures** → Clean fingers, update RD service, or switch to face authentication
2. **Aadhaar errors** → Ensure mobile is linked, biometrics unlocked, details match
3. **Face auth issues** → Good lighting, updated app, clean camera, follow liveness prompts
4. **PPO not found** → Verify number, register on portal, contact pension authority
5. **Details mismatch** → Align name/DOB/bank across Aadhaar, PPO, and bank records
6. **Can't do it digitally** → Visit CSC, bank, or post office — they're equally valid
7. **Don't miss the deadline** — submit before November 30 (or extended date) every year
