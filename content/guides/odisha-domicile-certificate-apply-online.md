# Odisha Domicile Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **domicile certificate** (ଅଧିବାସ ପ୍ରମାଣ ପତ୍ର — Adhibasa Praman Patra) in Odisha is issued by the Revenue Department (Tahasil office) and is required for NEET/OJEE Odisha state quota (85%) admissions, OPSC government job applications, Odisha state scholarships, and scheme eligibility. Applications go through **Service Online Odisha**.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [serviceonline.odisha.gov.in](https://serviceonline.odisha.gov.in) or **Mo Seva Kendra**. Issued by Tahasildar within **15–30 days**.

## Why You Need Odisha Domicile Certificate

- **NEET Odisha state quota (85%)** — domicile mandatory for OJEE (Odisha Joint Entrance Exam) / Odisha medical counselling
- **OPSC (Odisha PSC)** — Odisha government jobs
- **OSSC (Odisha Staff Selection Commission)** — state recruitment
- **PRERANA / Post-Matric Scholarship** — Odisha SC/ST scholarships (ST < ₹2.5L income)
- **NMMS / State Merit Scholarship** — Odisha state scholarships
- **PM Awas Yojana / Biju Pakka Ghara** — Odisha government housing schemes

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory — with Odisha address)
- **Ration card** (Odisha ration card)
- **Voter ID** (Odisha voter enrollment)
- **Birth certificate** — showing birth in Odisha
- **School Transfer Certificate (TC)** — from Odisha schools
- **ROR (Record of Rights)** — land records (Pahani/Khatiyan for rural applicants)
- Utility bills (electricity/water) at Odisha address
- Self-declaration of Odisha residency
- Passport-size photograph

---

## How to Apply Online — Service Online Odisha

1. Go to [serviceonline.odisha.gov.in](https://serviceonline.odisha.gov.in)
2. Register / log in with mobile number (OTP)
3. Navigate to **"Revenue & Disaster Management"** → **"Domicile Certificate"**
4. Fill form:
   - Applicant name, father's name, date of birth
   - District, Sub-Division, Tahasil, Village/Ward
   - Duration of residence in Odisha
   - Purpose of certificate
5. Upload documents (Aadhaar, ration card, birth certificate, school TC)
6. Pay fee: **₹10–₹30** (online)
7. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via Mo Seva Kendra

1. Visit nearest **Mo Seva Kendra** (Odisha government digital services kiosk)
2. Carry Aadhaar, ration card, voter ID, school TC
3. Operator fills and submits on Service Online Odisha
4. Service charge: approximately ₹30–50
5. Collect acknowledgement

---

## Status Check & Download

1. Go to [serviceonline.odisha.gov.in](https://serviceonline.odisha.gov.in) → **"Track Application"**
2. Enter application reference number
3. Once approved: download digitally signed PDF

## NEET / OJEE Odisha State Quota 2026

For **NEET 2026 Odisha state quota** (Odisha government medical/dental counselling) and **OJEE** (engineering):
1. Odisha domicile certificate — proof of Odisha residency
2. OR studied in Odisha schools for prescribed period (check State Counselling Authority notification)
3. Certificate must be Tahasildar-issued — Service Online Odisha digital certificate accepted
4. Apply well before NEET/OJEE results (15–30 day processing)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### ROR (Record of Rights) — is it needed for students?

**ROR is needed for rural/agricultural families** applying through land-based residency proof. Students can use school TC from Odisha schools + ration card instead. Mo Seva Kendra operators will guide on which documents are needed.

### OPSC — is Odisha domicile mandatory?

**Yes, for most Odisha state government posts**, OPSC requires Odisha domicile. Check the specific OPSC advertisement — some central government cadre posts don't require state domicile.

### ST reservation in Odisha — does domicile help?

Domicile proves Odisha residency, but for **Odisha ST/SC reservation benefits** you need the **caste certificate** separately. For scholarship purposes (PRERANA), you need both domicile + caste certificate.
