# Chhattisgarh Caste Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **caste certificate** (जाति प्रमाण पत्र — Jaati Praman Patra) in Chhattisgarh is issued by the Revenue Department (Sub-Divisional Magistrate / Tehsildar) and is required for OBC/SC/ST reservations, Chhattisgarh state scholarships, CGPET/NEET state counselling, government job applications (CGPSC), and tribal development scheme eligibility. Applications are processed through **CG e-District** portal.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [edistrict.cgstate.gov.in](https://edistrict.cgstate.gov.in) or at **Lok Seva Kendra (LSK)**. Issued by Tehsildar/SDM within **15–30 days**.

---

## Caste Categories in Chhattisgarh

| Category | Reservation % | Notes |
|----------|---------------|-------|
| **SC** | 13% | Scheduled Castes |
| **ST** | 32% | Scheduled Tribes — one of highest ST % in India |
| **OBC** | 14% | Other Backward Classes |
| **EWS** | 10% | General category, income < ₹8 lakh/year |

> Chhattisgarh has the **third highest ST reservation (32%)** in India. Major tribal communities include: Gond, Baiga, Kamar, Halba, Abujhmadia, Kol, Saura. The Gond community is the largest tribal group.

## Why You Need CG Caste Certificate

- **Chhattisgarh state scholarship** — OBC/SC/ST post-matric scholarship
- **CGPET / NEET CG state counselling** — caste certificate for reserved seats
- **Government job reservation** — CGPSC, CG Vyapam (CGPEB), district recruitment
- **Tribal Development Department schemes** — eligibility for Adivasi welfare
- **Central OBC NCL** — for UPSC/SSC/railways (if community is in central OBC list)
- **Mukhyamantri Kanyadan Yojana** — caste certificate for scheme benefits

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory)
- **Ration card** (shows family caste/community)
- **Father's school leaving certificate** (TC with caste)
- **Khasra / Bhu Adhikar Patra** (for ST applicants — land records showing tribal holding)
- Self-declaration of caste
- **Voter ID** (address proof)
- Passport-size photograph

---

## How to Apply Online — CG e-District Portal

1. Go to [edistrict.cgstate.gov.in](https://edistrict.cgstate.gov.in)
2. Register / log in with mobile number
3. Click **"Revenue Department"** → **"Caste Certificate"** (जाति प्रमाण पत्र)
4. Fill form:
   - Applicant name, address (district, tehsil, village)
   - Caste/community declaration (SC / ST / OBC)
   - Father's community details
5. Upload documents (Aadhaar, father's TC, ration card, photograph)
6. Pay fee: **₹20–40** (online)
7. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via Lok Seva Kendra (LSK)

CG's **Lok Seva Kendras** (at tehsil/block level) are the recommended route:
1. Visit nearest **Lok Seva Kendra**
2. Carry Aadhaar, ration card, father's caste proof
3. Operator submits application on CG e-District
4. Service charge: approximately ₹30–60
5. Collect acknowledgement

---

## Status Check & Download

1. Go to [edistrict.cgstate.gov.in](https://edistrict.cgstate.gov.in)
2. Click **"Track Application"** → enter Application Reference Number
3. Once approved: download digitally signed PDF

## CG Lok Sewa Guarantee Act — Your Rights

Under **Chhattisgarh Lok Sewa Guarantee Act 2011**, caste certificate must be issued within the notified timeframe. If delayed, apply to the Designated Officer for compensation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Gond community — which category in CG?

**Gond** is classified as **Scheduled Tribe (ST)** in Chhattisgarh. Gonds are the largest tribal group in CG. The Gond community is also in the central ST list — so CG ST certificate is valid for both state and central ST reservations.

### CG OBC certificate vs central OBC — same?

Not necessarily. The **CG OBC list** and **central OBC list** are different. For **CG state government jobs (CGPSC)**: CG OBC certificate applies. For **UPSC/SSC/railways**: verify if your community is in the central OBC list at [ncbc.nic.in](https://ncbc.nic.in). Apply for OBC NCL certificate separately if needed.
