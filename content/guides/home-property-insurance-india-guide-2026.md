# Home / Property Insurance India 2026 — Complete Guide

Most Indian homeowners protect their car (mandatory) but leave their home — often worth ₹50L–3Cr+ — uninsured. A fire, earthquake, or flood can destroy what took a lifetime to build. Home insurance is surprisingly cheap and often overlooked.

---

## What Does Home Insurance Cover?

### Standard Perils (All Home Insurance Plans)

| Risk Covered | What It Includes |
|-------------|-----------------|
| **Fire** | House fire (kitchen fire, electrical short circuit, accidental fire) |
| **Lightning** | Direct lightning strike damage |
| **Earthquake** | Structure damage from tremors |
| **Storm, cyclone, typhoon** | Wind and rain damage from named storms |
| **Flood** | Inundation damage to structure and contents |
| **Landslide/rockslide** | In hilly areas |
| **Explosion** | Gas cylinder explosion, boiler explosion |
| **Aircraft damage** | Damage from falling aircraft debris |
| **Riot and strike** | If officially declared |

### Optional Add-Ons

| Add-On | Coverage |
|--------|---------|
| **Burglary + theft** | Stolen household items, break-in |
| **Terrorism** | If area is conflict-prone |
| **Accidental damage** | Accidental breakage of goods |
| **Electronic items** | Specific cover for AC, refrigerator, TV, laptop |
| **Jewelry and valuables** | Specific items up to declared value |
| **Alternative accommodation** | Hotel costs while house is under repair |
| **Public liability** | If someone is injured in your house |

## Structure vs Contents — Two Coverage Layers

### Structure Insurance (Building)

Covers the physical structure:
- Walls, roof, floors, fitted cupboards, electrical wiring, plumbing
- **Rebuilding cost** (not market value of property)

**Important**: Home insurance does NOT compensate market value — it pays the cost to rebuild the structure from scratch. For a ₹1Cr Mumbai flat, the land (which is 70–80% of value) is not insured; only the construction cost (₹30–40L) is.

**How to calculate**: Carpet area × reconstruction cost per sq ft. For a 1,000 sq ft apartment at ₹3,000/sq ft construction cost = ₹30L structure sum insured.

### Contents Insurance

Covers items inside the house:
- Furniture, electronics, appliances, clothing
- **Declared value** of specific items
- Excludes wear-and-tear; covers sudden damage/theft

## Bank Loan and Home Insurance

If you have a home loan, the bank requires **fire insurance** on the property (at minimum):

**Bank's approach**: Banks often sell bundled home insurance alongside the home loan (through their partner insurer). This is:
- Sometimes the cheapest option (bank negotiates volume discounts)
- Sometimes the most expensive (captive sale with no comparison)

**Compare before buying**: Get quotes from 3 insurers before accepting the bank's insurance. RBI allows you to choose your own insurer — the bank cannot compel you to buy from their partner.

## How to File a Home Insurance Claim

### Immediate Steps After Incident

1. **Take photographs/video** of all damage immediately
2. **File FIR** if theft or vandalism involved (required for claim)
3. **Call insurer's claims helpline** — get claim reference number
4. **Don't repair or dispose of damaged items** until surveyor visits

### Claims Process

1. Register claim online or at branch (with reference number)
2. Insurer assigns surveyor — visits within 3–7 days
3. Surveyor assesses damage and submits report
4. Insurer issues settlement or requests additional documents
5. Settlement credited to your account

**Timeline**: Simple claims settle in 2–4 weeks. Large or disputed claims may take 2–3 months.

## What Is NOT Covered (Common Exclusions)

| Exclusion | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Normal wear and tear** | Old paint peeling, natural depreciation |
| **Land value** | Only structure; land is not insured |
| **Electrical/mechanical breakdown** | Appliance breakdown (use warranty/AMC instead) |
| **War and nuclear risk** | Standard exclusion |
| **Pre-existing damage** | Damage existing before policy start |
| **Unoccupied property** | House unoccupied >30–60 days (varies by insurer) |
| **Construction defects** | Poor-quality construction leading to damage |

## Is Home Insurance Tax Deductible?

**No** — home insurance premium cannot be claimed as deduction under Section 80C, 80D, or any other section for a self-occupied home.

**Business premises**: Home insurance for commercial property (office, shop) IS a business expense deductible under Section 37.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I rent an apartment. Do I need home insurance?

As a tenant: You don't need structure insurance (that's the landlord's responsibility). But you can buy **contents insurance** to protect your electronics, jewelry, and furniture from fire/theft/flood. Very affordable (₹1,000–2,000/year for ₹3–5L contents).

### My building society has insurance. Is my flat covered?

Society/association insurance typically covers common areas (parking, lobby, society office) — NOT individual flats' interiors. Buy individual home insurance for your flat.

### What is the claim-to-premium ratio for home insurance?

Home insurance claims are rare (most years: ₹0 in claims), which is why premiums are low. The occasional disaster claim (flood, fire) is where it earns its keep. Think of it as protection against low-probability, high-impact events.
