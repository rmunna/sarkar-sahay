# MP Patwari Exam Guide — Eligibility, Pattern & Preparation Tips

The MP Professional Examination Board (MPPEB/Vyapam) conducts the recruitment exam for Patwari posts under the Revenue Department of Madhya Pradesh. The Patwari is a village-level revenue officer responsible for maintaining land records.

MP Patwari is one of the most applied-for government jobs in Madhya Pradesh, offering a stable career in revenue administration.

## Eligibility Criteria

### Age Limit

| Category | Minimum Age | Maximum Age |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| General | 18 years | 40 years |
| OBC (MP) | 18 years | 43 years |
| SC/ST (MP) | 18 years | 45 years |

### Educational Qualification

- **Minimum:** Higher Secondary (12th) or equivalent from a recognized board
- Computer proficiency (Diploma/Certificate in Computer)
- Knowledge of Hindi (Devanagari script)

## Exam Pattern

| Subject | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| General Knowledge | 25 | 25 |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 25 | 25 |
| Hindi | 25 | 25 |
| Computer Knowledge | 25 | 25 |
| Village Economy / Panchayat | 20 | 20 |
| **Total** | **120** | **120** |

- **Duration:** 2 hours
- **Negative marking:** 0.25 per wrong answer
- **Type:** Online (Computer-based)

## Syllabus Overview

### General Knowledge
- MP-specific GK, Indian History, Geography, Polity, Science, Current Affairs

### Quantitative Aptitude
- Number system, Percentage, Profit & Loss, SI/CI, Ratio, Average, Time & Work

### Hindi
- Grammar, Comprehension, Sandhi, Samas, Vilom, Paryayvachi, Muhavare

### Computer Knowledge
- MS Office, Operating Systems, Internet basics, Computer fundamentals

### Village Economy / Panchayat
- Revenue administration, land records, Panchayati Raj system, government welfare schemes

## How to Apply

1. Visit MPPEB at [peb.mp.gov.in](https://peb.mp.gov.in)
2. Find the Patwari recruitment notification
3. Register and fill the application form
4. Upload photo and signature
5. Pay fee and submit

## Application Fee

| Category | Fee |
|----------|-----|
| General / OBC | ₹500 |
| SC / ST (MP) | ₹250 |

## Salary & Benefits

- **Pay Scale:** ₹19,500 – ₹62,000 (approx.)
- DA, HRA, and allowances as per MP Government
- Pension (NPS), medical benefits
- Promotion to Naib Tehsildar

## Preparation Tips

1. **Computer knowledge:** Don't neglect this section — it's easy scoring
2. **MP GK:** Study MP history, geography, districts, and schemes
3. **Village economy:** Understand Panchayati Raj and revenue systems
4. **Hindi grammar:** Practice Muhavare, Lokoktiyan, and comprehension
5. **Previous papers:** Solve last 5 years' MP Patwari papers

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is 12th pass sufficient for MP Patwari?
Yes, 12th pass with computer knowledge is the minimum requirement.

### Is there an interview for MP Patwari?
No, selection is purely based on the written exam merit.

### What is the role of a Patwari?
Patwari maintains village land records, assists in revenue collection, and helps in crop surveys.

### Is computer certificate mandatory?
Yes, a computer diploma/certificate is required.

### Can candidates from other states apply?
MP domicile is generally required for Patwari posts.

### How many vacancies are released?
It varies — recent cycles have had 4,000-9,000+ vacancies.
