## What is Indira Gandhi Pyari Behna Sukh Samman Nidhi?

The **Indira Gandhi Pyari Behna Sukh Samman Nidhi** is a flagship scheme of the **Government of Himachal Pradesh** that provides **₹1,500 per month** to eligible women in the state. Launched by the HP government to empower women financially, this scheme targets women aged 18 years and above who are not receiving any other government pension or salary.

The scheme was announced in the HP Budget 2024-25 and aims to provide financial independence and social security to women across Himachal Pradesh.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Gender:** Must be a woman
2. **Age:** Must be **18 years or older**
3. **Residency:** Must be a permanent resident of Himachal Pradesh
4. **Income:** No strict income ceiling, but government employees and pensioners are excluded
5. **Exclusions:**
   - Women receiving any other government pension
   - Women who are government employees (state/central)
   - Women whose family income exceeds the prescribed limit (if applicable)
   - Women who are income tax payees

## Monthly Benefit

| Benefit | Amount |
|---------|--------|
| Monthly allowance | ₹1,500 |
| Annual total | ₹18,000 |
| Payment mode | Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) |

## Documents Required

### Identity & Address
- Aadhaar Card (mandatory, linked to mobile)
- Himachali Bonafide Certificate / Domicile Certificate
- Voter ID Card

### Age Proof
- Aadhaar Card / Birth Certificate / School certificate

### Bank Details
- Bank passbook (account in applicant's own name)
- Account must be Aadhaar-linked for DBT

### Other
- Passport-size photographs
- Self-declaration that applicant is not receiving other pension/government salary

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Online Application

1. **Visit** the HP e-District portal at [edistrict.hp.gov.in](https://edistrict.hp.gov.in) or the dedicated scheme portal
2. **Register** or login with your credentials
3. **Navigate** to "Pyari Behna Sukh Samman Nidhi" under Social Security / Women Welfare
4. **Fill** the application form with personal details, Aadhaar number, and bank account
5. **Upload** required documents (Aadhaar, bonafide, bank passbook)
6. **Submit** the application and save the reference number
7. **Track** application status on the same portal

### Offline Application

1. **Visit** the nearest **Lok Mitra Kendra (LMK)** or **Block Development Office**
2. **Collect** the application form
3. **Fill** with accurate details and attach all documents
4. **Submit** to the designated officer
5. **Get** acknowledgement receipt with application number
6. **Gram Panchayat / Ward** level verification will follow

## Processing Time

- **Verification:** 15-30 days (local level verification)
- **Approval:** After verification, benefit is sanctioned
- **First Payment:** Within 1-2 months of approval
- **Ongoing:** Monthly payment to bank account via DBT

## Important Tips

1. **Aadhaar Mandatory:** Aadhaar must be linked to your bank account for receiving payments
2. **Self-Declaration:** Honestly declare your employment and pension status — false claims may lead to recovery
3. **Bank Account:** Open a bank account in your own name if you don't have one
4. **Lok Mitra Kendra:** For any difficulty, visit the nearest LMK for free assistance
5. **Keep Mobile Updated:** OTP verification requires an active mobile number linked to Aadhaar

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. How much money do women get under Pyari Behna scheme?
**₹1,500 per month** is directly transferred to the beneficiary's bank account.

### Q2. Is there any age limit?
Women must be **18 years or older**. There is no upper age limit (unless receiving old age pension).

### Q3. Can government employees apply?
No, women who are government employees or pensioners are not eligible.

### Q4. Do I need Aadhaar?
Yes, **Aadhaar Card linked to bank account** is mandatory for DBT payments.

### Q5. Can I get this along with HP Old Age Pension?
No, you cannot receive this scheme if you are already getting another government pension. You may need to choose one.

### Q6. How do I check my application status?
Visit [edistrict.hp.gov.in](https://edistrict.hp.gov.in) and check using your application reference number.

### Q7. Is there an income limit?
Women who are income tax payees or government employees are excluded. For others, there is generally no strict income ceiling.
