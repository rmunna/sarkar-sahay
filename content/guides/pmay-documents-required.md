## PMAY Documents Overview

Having the right documents ready is crucial for a smooth PMAY application. Missing or incorrect documents are one of the top reasons for [application rejection](/guide/pmay-application-rejected-fix) and [subsidy delays](/guide/pmay-subsidy-not-received-fix).

This guide lists all documents required for both PMAY-Urban and PMAY-Gramin applications, categorized for easy reference.

## Identity Documents

| Document | PMAY-Urban | PMAY-Gramin | Notes |
|----------|-----------|-------------|-------|
| **Aadhaar Card** | ✅ Mandatory | ✅ Mandatory | Must be linked to bank account for DBT |
| **PAN Card** | ✅ Required (MIG) | ❌ Not required | Mandatory for MIG-I and MIG-II categories |
| **Voter ID Card** | ✅ Optional (address proof) | ✅ Helpful | Can serve as additional identity proof |
| **Passport** | ✅ Optional | ❌ Not needed | Accepted as identity proof if available |

Don't have an Aadhaar card? See our [Aadhaar application guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online).

## Income Documents

### For PMAY-Urban (All Categories)

- **Income Certificate** from competent authority (Tehsildar/SDM) — [How to get income certificate](/guide/income-certificate-online-apply)
- **Salary slips** (last 6 months) — for salaried applicants
- **Form 16 / IT Returns** (last 2–3 years) — for MIG categories
- **Business income proof** — for self-employed (profit & loss statement, bank statements)
- **CA certificate** — some banks accept chartered accountant certification of income

### Income Category Classification

| Category | Annual Household Income | Key Income Document |
|----------|----------------------|-------------------|
| EWS | Up to ₹3 lakh | Income certificate / self-declaration |
| LIG | ₹3–6 lakh | Income certificate / salary slips |
| MIG-I | ₹6–12 lakh | IT returns + Form 16 + PAN |
| MIG-II | ₹12–18 lakh | IT returns + Form 16 + PAN |

### For PMAY-Gramin

- Income documents are generally **not required** — beneficiary selection is based on SECC 2011 data
- **BPL card** (if available) — supports eligibility
- **MGNREGA Job Card** — needed for convergence benefits

## Property Documents (PMAY-Urban)

For CLSS (Credit Linked Subsidy) applications:

- **Sale agreement / Allotment letter** — from builder or housing authority
- **Builder's RERA registration** — mandatory for under-construction properties
- **NOC from housing society** — if applicable
- **Property title deed** — for BLC (Beneficiary-Led Construction)
- **Building plan approval** — from municipal authority
- **Land ownership document** — for self-construction on own land
- **Carpet area certificate** — from builder confirming area within PMAY limits

### Carpet Area Limits by Category

| Category | Maximum Carpet Area |
|----------|-------------------|
| EWS | 30 sq.m. (323 sq.ft.) |
| LIG | 60 sq.m. (645 sq.ft.) |
| MIG-I | 120 sq.m. (1,292 sq.ft.) |
| MIG-II | 150 sq.m. (1,614 sq.ft.) |

## Bank and Loan Documents

- **Bank account passbook** (first page with account number, IFSC)
- **Bank statement** (last 6 months)
- **Home loan sanction letter** — from the lending bank
- **Home loan agreement** — signed copy
- **Loan account number** — for CLSS subsidy crediting
- **Cancelled cheque** — with printed name and account details

**Important:** Your [Aadhaar must be linked to your bank account](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) for DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer) of subsidy.

## Address Proof Documents

Any one of the following:

- Aadhaar card (preferred — also serves as identity proof)
- Voter ID card
- Utility bills (electricity, water, gas — recent, within 3 months)
- Ration card
- Rent agreement (if applicable)
- Passport

## Category/Caste Documents

- **Caste certificate** — for SC/ST/OBC applicants (issued by Tehsildar/SDM)
- **Minority community certificate** — for minority category applicants
- **Disability certificate** — for persons with disabilities (issued by government hospital)
- **Widow certificate** — for widows applying under priority category

## Affidavits and Declarations

- **Affidavit of no pucca house ownership** — self-declaration (notarized) that no family member owns a pucca house anywhere in India
- **Affidavit of not availing other housing scheme** — declaration that family hasn't benefited from any other government housing scheme
- **Consent for Aadhaar verification** — authorization to verify Aadhaar details

These affidavits are typically on stamp paper (₹10–100 depending on state) and must be notarized.

## PMAY-Gramin Specific Documents

- **SECC reference** — your household's SECC 2011 registration details
- **Gram Sabha resolution** — verification of beneficiary by Gram Sabha
- **Land ownership proof** — for the plot where house will be built
- **MGNREGA Job Card** — for convergence of labour wages
- **Photographs** — passport-size photos of all adult family members
- **Bank account details** — Aadhaar-linked bank account mandatory

## Document Format Requirements (Online Application)

When uploading documents on the PMAY portal:

| Requirement | Specification |
|-------------|--------------|
| **File format** | PDF or JPEG/JPG |
| **File size** | Usually under 1 MB per document |
| **Scan quality** | Clear, readable, all corners visible |
| **Photographs** | Passport-size, white background, recent |
| **Affidavits** | Scanned with notary seal visible |

## Common Document Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Name mismatch** — ensure your name is identical across Aadhaar, PAN, bank account, and application form
2. **Expired income certificate** — some states require income certificates issued within the last 6 months
3. **Wrong carpet area** — get the exact carpet area (not built-up area) from the builder
4. **Missing Aadhaar-bank linking** — verify DBT readiness before applying
5. **Outdated address** — if you've moved, [update your Aadhaar address](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) first
6. **Blurry scans** — illegible documents cause rejection; use a proper scanner or high-quality phone camera

## Important Tips

1. **Prepare documents before starting application** — have all scans ready to avoid incomplete submissions
2. **Keep originals safe** — you may need to show originals during physical verification
3. **Get income certificate early** — it takes 7–15 days in most states via [online application](/guide/income-certificate-online-apply)
4. **Verify Aadhaar details** — name, address, and date of birth should be accurate
5. **Make multiple copies** — keep 2–3 photocopies of each document for bank and ULB submissions

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Is Aadhaar mandatory for PMAY application?

Yes. Aadhaar is mandatory for both PMAY-Urban and PMAY-Gramin. It is used for identity verification and for transferring subsidies through DBT.

### 2. Do I need an income certificate for EWS category?

For EWS, a self-declaration of income is accepted in some states. However, having a formal [income certificate](/guide/income-certificate-online-apply) from the Tehsildar strengthens your application and avoids delays.

### 3. What if my name in Aadhaar doesn't match my PAN card?

Get the name corrected in either document before applying. Name mismatches are a common reason for [application rejection](/guide/pmay-application-rejected-fix). Update [Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) or PAN as needed.

### 4. Are documents different for PMAY-Urban and PMAY-Gramin?

Yes. PMAY-Urban requires more financial documents (income proof, loan papers, property documents) since it involves home loans. PMAY-Gramin is simpler as beneficiaries are selected from SECC data.

### 5. Can I apply with an expired income certificate?

Most ULBs and banks require income certificates issued within the last 6–12 months. Apply for a fresh income certificate before submitting your PMAY application.

### 6. Do I need property documents at the time of PMAY application?

For PMAY-Urban CLSS, yes — you need the sale agreement or allotment letter at the time of application. For BLC component, you need land ownership proof. For PMAY-Gramin, property documents are needed for the plot where the house will be built.
