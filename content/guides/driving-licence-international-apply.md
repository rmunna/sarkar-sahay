## What is an International Driving Permit (IDP)?

An International Driving Permit (IDP) is an official document that allows you to drive in foreign countries. It is essentially a translation of your Indian driving licence into multiple international languages and is recognized in over 150 countries that are signatories to the **Geneva Convention on Road Traffic (1949)** and the **Vienna Convention on Road Traffic (1968)**.

The IDP is issued by the Regional Transport Office (RTO) in India and is **valid for 1 year** from the date of issue. It is **not a standalone licence** — you must always carry your original Indian DL along with the IDP while driving abroad.

## Who is Eligible for an IDP?

- Must hold a **valid Indian driving licence** (not expired, not suspended)
- Must be **18 years or older**
- DL must be valid for the **vehicle class** you intend to drive abroad
- Your Indian DL must have a **remaining validity of at least 1 year** at the time of IDP application (recommended)
- Indian citizens and residents with a valid Indian DL

> **Note:** If your DL is expiring soon, [renew it first](/guide/driving-licence-renewal-online-fix) before applying for an IDP.

## Countries Where Indian IDP is Accepted

The Indian IDP is accepted in countries that are part of the Geneva or Vienna conventions, including:

- **Europe:** UK, France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Switzerland, Netherlands, and most EU countries
- **Americas:** USA, Canada, Brazil, Mexico, Argentina
- **Asia-Pacific:** Australia, New Zealand, Japan, South Korea, Thailand, Malaysia, Singapore
- **Middle East:** UAE, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Qatar, Bahrain, Kuwait
- **Africa:** South Africa, Kenya, Nigeria, Egypt

> **Important:** Some countries may require additional local permits or have specific rules. Always check the driving laws of your destination country before travelling. Countries like **China** and some others do not accept the IDP — you'll need a local licence.

## Documents Required for IDP Application

- **Valid Indian Driving Licence** (original + photocopy)
- **Passport** (original + photocopy — first and last page)
- **Valid Visa** or travel documents for the destination country (if available)
- **Passport-size photographs** (4-6 copies, white background, as per passport specifications)
- **Address proof** (Aadhaar card, passport, utility bill)
- **Medical Certificate in Form 1-A** (from a registered medical practitioner)
- **Self-declaration in Form 1**
- **Application in Form 4-A** (available on the Sarathi portal)

## Step-by-Step Process: Apply for IDP Online

1. Visit [sarathi.parivahan.gov.in](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in)
2. Select your **state** from the dropdown
3. Click **"DL Services"** → **"Issue of International Driving Permit"**
4. Enter your **DL number** and **date of birth**
5. Your DL details will be displayed — verify them
6. Fill in **Form 4-A** (IDP application form) with your travel details
7. Enter the **countries you plan to visit** (as required by some states)
8. Upload **documents** (passport copy, photos, medical certificate)
9. **Pay the fee** online (₹1,000 approximately)
10. **Book an RTO appointment**
11. Visit the RTO on the appointment date with all **original documents**
12. The IDP will be issued — some RTOs issue it on the **same day**, others dispatch it via Speed Post

## IDP Validity and Renewal

- **Validity:** 1 year from the date of issue
- **Renewal:** You cannot renew an IDP — you must apply for a **new IDP** each time
- **Extension:** Not possible; apply afresh if your IDP expires while abroad
- The IDP is **invalid if your Indian DL expires** — ensure your DL remains valid throughout your travel period

## Processing Time

| Stage | Timeline |
|---|---|
| Online application | 15-20 minutes |
| RTO appointment | 3-10 days after applying |
| IDP issuance | Same day to 7 days (varies by RTO) |
| If dispatched via post | 7-15 days |

> **Tip:** Apply at least **2-3 weeks before your travel date** to account for processing time and any unexpected delays.

## Important Tips

1. **Apply early** — don't wait until the last minute; some RTOs take up to 2 weeks to issue the IDP
2. **Carry both documents** — always carry your original Indian DL along with the IDP when driving abroad
3. **Check destination rules** — some countries accept IDP for only 3-6 months; you may need a local licence for longer stays
4. **IDP is not a visa** — it does not grant you entry into any country; you still need a valid visa and passport
5. **Vehicle class matters** — your IDP will be valid only for the vehicle classes mentioned in your Indian DL

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I apply for an IDP online without visiting the RTO?
The application is submitted online through the Sarathi portal, but you typically need to visit the RTO for document verification and IDP collection. Some RTOs may dispatch it via post.

### How long is the International Driving Permit valid?
The IDP is valid for **1 year** from the date of issue. It cannot be renewed — you must apply for a new one each time.

### Can I drive in the USA with an Indian IDP?
Yes, the USA accepts the Indian IDP. However, rules vary by state — most US states accept an IDP for up to 1 year. For longer stays, you may need to obtain a local US driving licence.

### Is the IDP valid without my Indian driving licence?
No, the IDP is **not valid on its own**. You must always carry your original Indian driving licence along with the IDP when driving in a foreign country.

### Can I get an IDP for a two-wheeler?
Yes, if your Indian DL includes the motorcycle/two-wheeler class (MC 50CC, MC EX50CC, or LMV with MCWG), your IDP will cover two-wheelers as well.

### What if I need the IDP urgently for travel next week?
Visit your RTO in person and explain the urgency. Some RTOs offer expedited processing — they may issue the IDP on the same day or within 1-2 days. Carry all original documents and the printed online application.
