# Karnataka Free Bus Pass for Students 2026 — All Students, Including Outside-State Colleges

The Karnataka government has expanded its **free bus pass scheme** to cover **all school and college students** across the state — and now also includes students from Karnataka who are studying in institutions **outside Karnataka**.

**Quick answer:** Karnataka students get free travel on all KSRTC, BMTC, NWKRTC, and NEKRTC non-AC buses. Apply at [sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in) or through your school/college. Students studying outside Karnataka can also apply and get a route-based bus pass for travel to/from their institution.

---

## Who is Eligible?

- Students enrolled in **any school (Class 1–12)** in Karnataka
- Students enrolled in **any college / university** in Karnataka
- Students from Karnataka enrolled in institutions **outside Karnataka** (newly expanded)
- Both **boys and girls** — no gender restriction
- Applies to **government and private institution** students

**Not eligible:**
- Students already receiving scholarship-linked transport allowance
- Students in distance/online education (no physical commute)

---

## What's Covered?

| Bus operator | Coverage |
|---|---|
| KSRTC | All Karnataka state routes |
| BMTC | Bengaluru city routes |
| NWKRTC | North-West Karnataka routes |
| NEKRTC | North-East Karnataka routes |

- **Free on all non-AC ordinary/express services**
- AC Volvo and other premium services require normal fare
- Valid on routes between home and educational institution
- No cap on number of trips per day

## How to Apply — School/College Students in Karnataka

### Option 1: Through Your School/College (Easiest)

Most schools and colleges handle bus pass applications centrally:
1. Contact your school or college office and ask about the "Student Bus Pass" application
2. Submit a filled form with school ID, address proof, and photo
3. School/college forwards to the transport department
4. Pass is issued at school/college or sent home

### Option 2: Seva Sindhu Portal (Online)

1. Go to **[sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in)**
2. Click **"Services"** → search for **"Student Bus Pass"**
3. Login with your Aadhaar / mobile OTP
4. Select operator: KSRTC / BMTC / NWKRTC / NEKRTC
5. Fill in:
   - Student name, date of birth, Aadhaar
   - School/college name, class/year
   - Home address and institution address
   - Route needed (from/to)
6. Upload: school/college ID card, address proof, photo
7. Submit — pass is issued within 7–15 days

### Option 3: KSRTC / BMTC Divisional Office

For students with route-specific requirements or issues:
1. Visit your nearest KSRTC/BMTC divisional office
2. Collect the student pass application form
3. Attach attested copies: institution ID, address proof, photograph
4. Collect the pass from the office (typically 3–7 days after submission)

## For Students Studying Outside Karnataka

This is the newly expanded provision as of June 2026:

**Who qualifies:**
- Student must be a **Karnataka domicile resident**
- Must be enrolled in a recognised institution **outside Karnataka**
- Pass covers travel **from Karnataka home to the institution** (long-route trips)

**How to apply:**
1. Collect a **bonafide certificate** from your institution stating your course, year, and address
2. Apply via the nearest KSRTC divisional office in Karnataka (your home district)
3. Or apply via the Seva Sindhu portal selecting "Outside State Study" category
4. Submit along with Karnataka domicile proof (Aadhaar, ration card) and institution enrollment proof
5. Pass covers route from your Karnataka home address to the nearest Karnataka border/depot for connections

**Refund for existing pass holders:**
Students who paid for bus passes before the free scheme was extended will receive a **refund** of the amount paid. Refunds are processed automatically to your bank account linked to the bus pass application. Contact your school/college or KSRTC if the refund is not received.

---

## Renewal

Bus passes are valid for **one academic year** and must be renewed:
- At the start of each new academic year
- At the same channel where you originally applied (school/Seva Sindhu/KSRTC office)
- Submit updated institution ID/fee receipt for the new year

---

## Common Problems & Fixes

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Pass expired mid-year | Visit nearest KSRTC office or Seva Sindhu — passes can be renewed anytime |
| Route not matching | Apply for a route correction at KSRTC divisional office with updated address proof |
| Pass not issued after 15 days | Check application status on Seva Sindhu with application reference number |
| Refund not received | Contact school/college or visit KSRTC office with pass details |
| Studying outside state — pass rejected | Ensure Karnataka domicile proof is submitted; contact KSRTC divisional office directly |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does this free bus pass work on Namma Metro too?**
No — this pass is for KSRTC/BMTC/NWKRTC/NEKRTC bus services only. Metro (Namma Metro/BMRCL) has a separate student concession card available directly from Namma Metro counters.

**Can a private college student apply?**
Yes — the scheme covers both government and private school/college students.

**Is there an income limit to apply?**
No income limit — the free bus pass is available to all Karnataka students regardless of family income.

**My college is in another district of Karnataka — do I get a pass?**
Yes — the pass is issued based on your home-to-institution route. It covers inter-district travel within Karnataka.

**Can I use the pass during weekends and holidays?**
The pass is valid all days of the week but is intended for commuting to/from educational institutions. Buses are available on weekends but this is for student use.

**What happens if I lose my bus pass?**
Visit the issuing office (school/KSRTC/Seva Sindhu) with a police complaint copy and request a duplicate pass. A nominal fee may be charged for duplicates.
