# FASTag KYC Update Failed: How to Fix

NHAI mandates KYC verification for all FASTag accounts. If your KYC is incomplete or failed, your FASTag may get blacklisted, deactivated, or attract double toll charges at highway plazas. This guide covers every common KYC update failure and how to resolve it.

## FASTag KYC Deadline — What You Need to Know

- NHAI has made **minimum KYC mandatory** for all FASTags. Tags without valid KYC face blacklisting.
- Banks periodically send SMS/email reminders for KYC completion.
- If your KYC deadline has passed and you haven't updated, your tag may already be **deactivated or blacklisted** — but it can usually be reactivated after completing KYC.

> **Important:** Even if your FASTag was purchased years ago, KYC re-verification may be required due to updated RBI/NHAI norms.

## Common Error Messages and Symptoms

- **"KYC verification failed"** — Aadhaar or document validation did not succeed
- **"OTP not received"** or **"OTP expired"** — Aadhaar-linked mobile OTP issue
- **"Vehicle details mismatch"** — RC number or vehicle class doesn't match tag records
- **"FASTag is blacklisted"** — tag deactivated due to incomplete KYC or other violations
- **"Unable to process KYC request"** — bank server or NHAI portal error
- FASTag balance intact but toll plaza shows "invalid tag"
- KYC shows "pending" for days after submission
- Double toll charged despite having balance

## How to Update FASTag KYC — Two Methods

### Method 1: Through Your Bank's App or Website

Most FASTag issuers (Paytm, ICICI, HDFC, Airtel Payments Bank, Axis, SBI, etc.) allow KYC updates through their app:

1. Open the bank/wallet app linked to your FASTag.
2. Go to **FASTag** → **My FASTag** → **Update KYC** or **Complete KYC**.
3. Enter your **Aadhaar number** and verify via OTP sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile.
4. Upload or auto-fetch **vehicle RC** details.
5. Confirm and submit. KYC status updates within **24–72 hours**.

### Method 2: Through NHAI / IHMCL Portal

1. Visit [https://fastagpartner.ihmcl.co.in/](https://fastagpartner.ihmcl.co.in/).
2. Log in with your registered mobile number.
3. Select your FASTag and click **Update KYC**.
4. Complete Aadhaar-based eKYC (OTP verification).
5. Verify vehicle RC details and submit.

> **Tip:** If neither method works online, visit your FASTag issuer bank branch with Aadhaar, RC, and vehicle photos for in-person KYC.

## Fix: Aadhaar OTP Verification Failed

This is the most common KYC failure. Causes and fixes:

| Cause | Fix |
|-------|-----|
| Mobile number not linked to Aadhaar | Update mobile in Aadhaar at your nearest enrolment centre — this cannot be done online |
| OTP expired (entered too late) | Request a new OTP and enter within 3 minutes |
| UIDAI server down | Try after 1–2 hours; avoid peak hours (10 AM – 1 PM) |
| Multiple OTP requests blocked | Wait 30 minutes before retrying; UIDAI blocks after ~10 failed attempts |
| Network issues on mobile | Switch to Wi-Fi or ensure strong mobile signal |
| Aadhaar biometric locked | Unlock biometric via mAadhaar app or UIDAI portal if biometric-based KYC is used |

> **Check:** Verify your Aadhaar-linked mobile at [https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/) → Verify Email/Mobile.

## Fix: Vehicle RC Mismatch

Your FASTag KYC will fail if RC details don't match the tag registration. Common scenarios:

- **Wrong vehicle number entered during FASTag purchase** — Contact issuer bank to correct vehicle number.
- **Vehicle ownership transferred** — You need a new FASTag in the new owner's name. The old tag must be closed.
- **RC details changed (address, hypothecation removed)** — Upload the updated RC during KYC.
- **Vehicle class mismatch** (car vs LCV) — Contact issuer to update vehicle class; wrong class causes toll overcharges.

**To fix:**
1. Download your latest RC from [https://parivahan.gov.in/](https://parivahan.gov.in/) → **mParivahan** or check the mParivahan app.
2. Ensure the **vehicle registration number, owner name, and vehicle class** on the RC match what's on your FASTag account.
3. If mismatched, contact your FASTag issuer bank's customer care to update records before retrying KYC.

## Fix: FASTag Blacklisted or Deactivated

A FASTag gets blacklisted when:
- KYC not completed within the deadline
- Duplicate tags detected for the same vehicle
- Negative wallet balance for an extended period
- Bank-initiated deactivation due to KYC non-compliance

**How to check if your FASTag is blacklisted:**
1. Try making a payment at a toll plaza — if it shows "blacklisted" or "invalid tag," confirm via the issuer app.
2. Call your FASTag issuer bank's helpline.
3. Check the **My FASTag** section in your bank app — it will show tag status.

**How to reactivate:**
1. **Complete KYC first** — use the bank app or portal methods above.
2. **Contact issuer bank** customer care and request reactivation after KYC is approved.
3. **Ensure minimum balance** — some banks require a minimum wallet balance (₹100–₹200) to reactivate.
4. If the tag was blacklisted due to duplicates, close the extra tag and keep only one active tag per vehicle.

> **Note:** Reactivation typically takes **24–48 hours** after KYC completion. In some cases, the bank may issue a new tag number while retaining your balance.

## Fix: Toll Deduction Issues After KYC Update

Even after successful KYC, you may face toll problems:

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
|-------|-------|-----|
| Double toll charged | Tag status not yet updated at plaza | Wait 24–48 hours; if persists, raise complaint on NHAI helpline **1033** |
| Toll deducted but tag shows inactive | Backend sync delay between bank and NHAI | Contact issuer bank; they can push a status update to NPCI |
| Excess amount deducted | Vehicle class mismatch in records | Get vehicle class corrected with issuer bank |
| Balance deducted but barrier didn't open | RFID reader issue or tag placement problem | Ensure tag is pasted on windshield as per guidelines; don't keep it in dashboard |
| No deduction, had to pay cash | Tag not yet whitelisted after reactivation | Call issuer bank and confirm your tag is active on the NETC network |

**For refund of excess/wrong toll deductions:**
1. Note the **toll plaza name, date, time, and transaction ID**.
2. Raise a complaint on **NHAI helpline 1033** or via the **My FASTag** app.
3. You can also file a complaint at [https://complaints.ihmcl.co.in/](https://complaints.ihmcl.co.in/).

## Step-by-Step: If Nothing Works

1. **Call your FASTag issuer bank helpline** — explain the KYC failure reason. They can manually verify documents.
2. **Visit the nearest bank branch** with original Aadhaar, RC book, vehicle photos (front + back with number plate), and your FASTag sticker/card.
3. **Call NHAI helpline 1033** — for blacklisted tag issues or toll plaza disputes.
4. **Close and get a new FASTag** — if the tag is permanently deactivated or linked to a wrong vehicle, close it and buy a new one from any issuer bank. Your old balance can be refunded.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is FASTag KYC free?**
A: Yes, eKYC via Aadhaar OTP is completely free. Banks cannot charge for KYC updates.

**Q: Can I update KYC for someone else's FASTag?**
A: No. KYC must be done using the vehicle owner's Aadhaar and RC. The Aadhaar-linked mobile must be accessible for OTP.

**Q: My FASTag was bought from a petrol pump. How do I complete KYC?**
A: Check which bank issued the FASTag (printed on the tag or receipt). Download that bank's app or visit their branch for KYC.

**Q: Will I lose my FASTag balance if the tag is blacklisted?**
A: No. Your balance remains in the wallet. Once the tag is reactivated after KYC, the balance becomes usable again. If you close the tag, the balance is refunded to your linked bank account.

**Q: How long does FASTag KYC verification take?**
A: Online eKYC is usually instant to 72 hours. In-person KYC at a bank branch may take 3–5 working days.
