# Voter ID Download Not Working: How to Fix

The Election Commission of India allows citizens to download their digital Voter ID (e-EPIC) from the Voter Portal and the Voter Helpline app. However, the download process frequently fails due to server issues, OTP problems, and technical errors. This guide covers all common download failures and how to resolve them.

## Common Error Messages and Symptoms

- **"Unable to download e-EPIC"** — generic download failure
- **"OTP not received"** — verification OTP does not arrive
- **"e-EPIC is not available for your EPIC number"** — card not in digital format
- **"Invalid EPIC number"** — voter ID number not recognized
- **"Service temporarily unavailable"** — portal is down
- Download starts but PDF is corrupted or blank
- Portal login works but the download button is greyed out
- Voter Helpline app crashes during download
- "Face authentication failed" during e-EPIC download
- KYC verification step fails repeatedly

## Why Voter ID Download May Not Work

### 1. e-EPIC Not Generated for Your ID

Not all Voter IDs have a digital version. If your voter card was issued before 2021 or through certain manual processes, the e-EPIC may not be available yet.

### 2. Mobile Number Not Registered with ECI

The download process requires OTP verification. If your mobile number is not linked to your voter registration, you cannot receive the OTP.

### 3. Portal Server Issues

The NVSP and Voter Portal frequently face downtime, especially during election periods when traffic spikes.

### 4. Browser Compatibility

The Voter Portal has known issues with certain browsers and mobile browsers.

### 5. Face Authentication Failure

Some e-EPIC downloads require face authentication, which can fail due to lighting, camera quality, or photo mismatch with records.

## Step-by-Step Troubleshooting

### Step 1: Use the Correct Portal

The Election Commission has multiple portals. Use the right one:
1. **Voter Portal (recommended):** [voterportal.eci.gov.in](https://voterportal.eci.gov.in/)
2. **NVSP:** [nvsp.in](https://nvsp.in/) — older portal, being phased out
3. **Voter Helpline App:** Download from Google Play or App Store
4. Try the Voter Portal first — it is the most updated platform

### Step 2: Log In and Check e-EPIC Availability

1. Visit [voterportal.eci.gov.in](https://voterportal.eci.gov.in/)
2. Log in with your registered mobile number and OTP
3. Go to **"Download e-EPIC"** section
4. Enter your **EPIC number** (printed on your physical voter ID, format: ABC1234567)
5. If the system says e-EPIC is not available, your card has not been digitized yet (see alternatives below)

### Step 3: Fix OTP Issues

1. Ensure your mobile number is registered with ECI — check at the Voter Portal under your profile
2. If your number is not registered, update it:
   - Visit [voterportal.eci.gov.in](https://voterportal.eci.gov.in/) → **Corrections in EPIC**
   - Or submit **Form 8** at your local ERO (Electoral Registration Officer) office
3. Check DND settings and disable if active (SMS `STOP` to 1909)
4. Try TOTP if available in the app

### Step 4: Fix Face Authentication Failure

If face authentication is required:
1. Ensure good lighting — face should be clearly visible
2. Remove glasses, caps, or masks
3. Hold the phone at arm's length, face centred
4. Ensure the background is plain and well-lit
5. Match the pose in your voter ID photo if possible
6. If it keeps failing, try at different times or with a different device camera

### Step 5: Try a Different Browser

1. Use **Google Chrome** on a desktop/laptop for best results
2. Clear browser cache and cookies
3. Disable extensions especially ad blockers
4. Try in Incognito mode
5. If using mobile, switch to the desktop version of the website

### Step 6: Use the Voter Helpline App

1. Download **Voter Helpline** app from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.eci.citizen) or App Store
2. Register/login with your mobile number
3. Go to **e-EPIC Download** section
4. The app sometimes works when the web portal does not
5. Clear app cache if you face errors: Settings → Apps → Voter Helpline → Clear Cache

### Step 7: Register for e-EPIC Generation

If your e-EPIC has not been generated yet:
1. Log in to the Voter Portal
2. Submit a request for e-EPIC generation
3. You may need to upload a recent passport-sized photo
4. Once approved (7-15 days), you can download the e-EPIC

## Alternative Methods and Workarounds

### Download Voter Slip Instead

If e-EPIC is not available, download your voter information slip:
1. Visit [electoralsearch.eci.gov.in](https://electoralsearch.eci.gov.in/)
2. Search by your name, EPIC number, or other details
3. Download the voter slip — this shows your name, constituency, and polling booth
4. While not a full ID, it can be used for verification purposes

### Get Voter ID Through DigiLocker

1. Log in to [DigiLocker](https://www.digilocker.gov.in/)
2. Go to **Browse Documents → Election Commission of India**
3. Fetch your Voter ID / EPIC card
4. This provides a legally valid digital copy

### Visit BLO or ERO Office

For offline help:
1. Contact your **Booth Level Officer (BLO)** — find details on the Voter Portal
2. Visit your **Electoral Registration Officer (ERO)** office
3. They can help with e-EPIC registration and issue a physical voter ID
4. Carry Aadhaar and a passport-sized photo

### Use Physical Voter ID

If digital download is not urgent, use your physical Voter ID card for all purposes. It remains valid for voting and identity verification.

## Prevention Tips

1. **Register your mobile number** — ensure your current mobile number is linked to your voter registration for OTP access
2. **Request e-EPIC early** — do not wait until election time to download; servers are overloaded during elections
3. **Keep a copy in DigiLocker** — fetch your voter ID into DigiLocker as a backup
4. **Update your photo** — if your voter ID has an old or unclear photo, update it for smoother face authentication
5. **Save the downloaded PDF** — store it securely on your phone and cloud storage
6. **Keep the Voter Helpline app installed** — useful for e-EPIC access and election-day polling booth lookup

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is my e-EPIC not available for download?

e-EPIC (digital Voter ID) is available only for voter IDs issued or updated after January 2021, and for older IDs where the Election Commission has completed digitization. If your e-EPIC is not available, submit a request for generation on the Voter Portal or fetch your voter ID through DigiLocker.

### Can I use e-EPIC as valid ID proof?

Yes. The e-EPIC downloaded from the Voter Portal or Voter Helpline app is a legally valid identity document. It is accepted for voting, bank account opening, and other KYC purposes. The PDF contains a secure QR code for verification.

### How do I link my mobile number with Voter ID?

Log in to the Voter Portal at [voterportal.eci.gov.in](https://voterportal.eci.gov.in/) and update your mobile number under profile settings. Alternatively, submit Form 8 at your local ERO office requesting mobile number addition. You can also request the update through the Voter Helpline app.

### Why does face authentication keep failing for e-EPIC?

Face authentication compares your live photo with the photo on your voter ID record. It fails when there is poor lighting, the camera quality is low, you are wearing accessories (glasses, mask), or your appearance has changed significantly from the ID photo. Try in natural daylight with a clean camera lens.

### How long does it take to generate e-EPIC?

If your e-EPIC is not yet available and you submit a generation request, it typically takes 7-15 working days for approval and generation. Once generated, you can download it instantly from the Voter Portal or Voter Helpline app.
