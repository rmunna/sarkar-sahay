# E-Shram Card Download Online — UAN Card Print (2026)

The E-Shram card is a digital identity card for unorganized sector workers in India, issued by the Ministry of Labour & Employment. It contains a 12-digit Universal Account Number (UAN) that links you to various government welfare schemes. This guide shows you how to download, print, and check the status of your E-Shram card online.

## What is the E-Shram Card?

The E-Shram card is issued under the e-Shram portal to workers in the unorganized sector — including construction workers, street vendors, domestic workers, agricultural labourers, gig workers, and others. The card:

- Provides a unique 12-digit UAN (Universal Account Number)
- Links you to welfare schemes like PMJJBY, PMSBY, and Atal Pension Yojana
- Serves as identity proof for availing government benefits
- Includes accidental insurance cover of ₹2 lakh under PMSBY

## How to Download E-Shram Card Online

### Method 1: Download from E-Shram Portal

1. Visit [https://eshram.gov.in](https://eshram.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Already Registered"** or **"Download UAN Card"**
3. Enter your registered **mobile number**
4. Enter the **OTP** received on your mobile
5. Your E-Shram UAN card will be displayed on screen
6. Click **"Download"** or **"Print"** to save as PDF
7. Take a printout for your records

### Method 2: Download via UMANG App

1. Download the **UMANG app** from Google Play Store or Apple App Store
2. Open the app and search for **"E-Shram"**
3. Select **"Download UAN Card"**
4. Log in with your registered mobile number and OTP
5. Your E-Shram card will be displayed — download or share

### Method 3: Download Using DigiLocker

1. Open the **DigiLocker app** or visit [https://digilocker.gov.in](https://digilocker.gov.in)
2. Log in with your Aadhaar-linked mobile number
3. Go to **"Issued Documents"** or search for **"Ministry of Labour"**
4. Select **E-Shram Card**
5. Download the digitally signed document

### Method 4: Via CSC Centre

If you're unable to download online:
1. Visit your nearest CSC (Common Service Centre)
2. Provide your Aadhaar number and mobile number
3. The VLE will download and print your E-Shram card
4. A nominal printing fee may apply

## How to Check E-Shram Registration Status

1. Visit [https://eshram.gov.in](https://eshram.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Already Registered"**
3. Enter your mobile number and verify via OTP
4. Your registration status and UAN will be displayed

**Alternatively:**
- Check via SMS: Send **EPFO UAN STATUS** to the designated number
- Call the **E-Shram helpline: 14434**

## What Details Are on the E-Shram Card?

Your E-Shram card contains:
- 12-digit UAN (Universal Account Number)
- Name and photograph
- Date of birth
- Aadhaar number (masked)
- Occupation/skill type
- QR code for verification
- Address and mobile number

## E-Shram Card Not Downloading? Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| OTP not received | Check if mobile number matches registration. Wait 2 minutes and retry. |
| "No record found" | You may not be registered. Register at eshram.gov.in first. |
| Blank page after OTP | Clear browser cache, try Chrome or Firefox |
| Download button not working | Disable popup blocker, try incognito mode |
| Card shows wrong details | Visit CSC to get details corrected with supporting documents |
| Mobile number changed | Visit CSC centre to update mobile number with Aadhaar verification |

## How to Update E-Shram Card Details

If your details are incorrect or need updating:

1. Visit [https://eshram.gov.in](https://eshram.gov.in) → **"Update Profile"**
2. Verify with OTP on registered mobile
3. Update details — name, address, occupation, nominee, bank account
4. Submit and download the updated card

> **Note:** Name and date of birth changes require matching Aadhaar details. Update your Aadhaar first if needed — see our [Aadhaar update guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online).

## FAQs

### 1. Is E-Shram card download free?
Yes, downloading the E-Shram UAN card is completely free. You can download it from the portal, UMANG app, or DigiLocker at no cost.

### 2. Can I download E-Shram card without mobile number?
No, OTP verification on your registered mobile number is required. If your number has changed, visit a CSC centre to update it.

### 3. What is the UAN on the E-Shram card?
UAN stands for Universal Account Number — a unique 12-digit number assigned to each registered unorganized worker. It's different from the EPFO UAN used by organized sector employees.

### 4. Can I use the E-Shram card as ID proof?
The E-Shram card serves as proof of registration as an unorganized worker. While it contains identity details, it is primarily meant for linking to welfare schemes rather than as a standalone ID proof.

### 5. I registered at CSC but can't download my card. Why?
Wait 24–48 hours after registration for the card to become available for download. If the issue persists, contact the E-Shram helpline at 14434.

### 6. Is E-Shram card linked to Aadhaar?
Yes, E-Shram registration is Aadhaar-based. Your UAN is linked to your Aadhaar number for identity verification and deduplication.

### 7. Can I download E-Shram card for a family member?
You can download it using their registered mobile number and OTP. Each registered worker has their own individual E-Shram card.

### 8. How do I know if my E-Shram registration is complete?
Visit eshram.gov.in → Already Registered → enter mobile and OTP. If your UAN and card are displayed, registration is complete.
