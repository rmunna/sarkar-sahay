# Annual Information Statement (AIS) India 2026 — Complete Guide

The **Annual Information Statement (AIS)** is the IT Department's comprehensive view of your financial life — all transactions across banks, mutual funds, stock exchanges, property registrars, forex dealers, and more are aggregated here. Before filing your ITR, always check your AIS.

## AIS vs Form 26AS — What's the Difference?

| | Form 26AS | AIS |
|-|-----------|-----|
| **Content** | TDS/TCS credits, advance tax, refund | Everything Form 26AS has + investments, property, mutual funds, dividends, foreign remittance, etc. |
| **Who reports** | Employers, banks, deductors | 50+ sources: banks, MF houses, brokers, property registrars, EPFO, SEBI, etc. |
| **Introduced** | Old (years old) | November 2021 |
| **Use for ITR** | TDS credit verification | Comprehensive income verification + ITR pre-fill |

**AIS replaced/enhanced Form 26AS** as the primary document for ITR verification. Always check AIS first; Form 26AS is now a subset.

## What AIS Shows

AIS has two parts:
1. **Part A**: General information (PAN, name, DOB, mobile, email, Aadhaar status)
2. **Part B**: Transaction details — the main content

**Part B categories include**:

| Category | What It Captures |
|----------|-----------------|
| **Salary** | Salary reported by employers (matches Form 16) |
| **Rent received** | Rent payments made to you (>₹50K/month) reported by tenant |
| **Dividends** | Dividends paid by companies/MF directly |
| **Interest income** | Interest from banks (savings, FD), bonds |
| **Capital gains** | MF/stock buy-sell transactions (from SEBI/exchanges) |
| **SFT** (Statement of Financial Transactions) | High-value transactions: FD > ₹10L, credit card > ₹1L/month, MF investments > ₹10L, property >₹30L |
| **Foreign remittances** | Remittances out (LRS) and in (received from abroad) |
| **EPF withdrawals** | EPFO withdrawal data |
| **Business receipts** | Payment made to you by companies (from TDS returns) |
| **GST data** | Turnover from GST filings |
| **Property purchase/sale** | From sub-registrar's data when you buy/sell property |

## How to Download AIS

1. Go to **incometax.gov.in** → Login
2. Under "Services" → **"Annual Information Statement (AIS)"**
3. Click on the AIS tile
4. You'll see **AIS** (full detail) and **TIS** (Taxpayer Information Summary — condensed version)
5. For ITR purposes, the portal also pre-fills using AIS/TIS data

**Download formats**:
- PDF: Human-readable report
- JSON: Machine-readable (for developers)

## How to Read AIS for ITR Filing

### Step 1: Cross-check salary
Compare AIS salary figures with your Form 16 Part A. They should match.

### Step 2: Check interest income
Find all FD interest, savings account interest from multiple banks. Ensure you declare all of these in ITR Schedule OS.

### Step 3: Verify mutual fund transactions
AIS shows all MF buy and sell transactions. Compare to your capital gains statement from CAMS/KFintech. If there's a mismatch, investigate before filing ITR.

### Step 4: Check dividends
Dividends received are taxable. AIS shows all dividend credits from companies and MFs. Include in Schedule OS.

### Step 5: Look for SFT entries
High-value transactions trigger SFT reporting. If AIS shows a ₹15L property purchase or ₹12L FD deposit, you may need to explain the source in ITR or through IT Department's online compliance portal.

## How to Correct Wrong AIS Entries

AIS sometimes has errors — transactions may be misclassified, duplicated, or show wrong amounts. The IT Department collects data from multiple sources and errors do occur.

**Process to flag wrong entries**:

1. In AIS portal, next to each transaction, there's a **"Feedback"** button
2. Click feedback and select:
   - "Information is correct"
   - "Information is not fully correct" (amount wrong)
   - "Information relates to other PAN/Year"
   - "Information is duplicate"
   - "Information is denied"
3. Add remarks explaining the discrepancy
4. Submit

**What happens**: Your feedback goes to the information source (e.g., the bank that reported wrong FD interest). The source may confirm or update the data. AIS is updated in 15–30 days.

**Meanwhile**: Don't skip it in your ITR. If AIS shows ₹50,000 in FD interest but actual is ₹35,000, flag the error in AIS and in your ITR, declare the actual correct amount. Include a note in ITR if your return is scrutinized — your AIS feedback record supports your position.

## TIS — Taxpayer Information Summary

**TIS** is a simplified version of AIS — it aggregates transactions by category:
- Total salary reported
- Total interest income
- Total dividend
- Total capital gains from MF/stocks
- etc.

TIS is what the IT portal uses for **pre-filling** your ITR. When you start filing ITR, it pulls from TIS. Review TIS first — if it looks correct, the pre-fill will be close to accurate.

## AIS Compliance Portal — AI-100 and Similar Notices

The IT Department uses AIS data to send compliance notices:
- **SFT mismatch notice**: If you have large SFT transactions but low income declared
- **Non-filer notice**: If AIS shows significant income but no ITR filed
- **High-value transaction response** via Compliance Portal

All responses to these are handled at **incometax.gov.in → Compliance → E-Campaign → High-Value Transactions**. Respond online with explanation of the source of funds.

## AIS Data and Revised ITR

If you already filed your ITR but later notice something in AIS that you missed (e.g., dividend income you forgot), file a **Revised Return** before December 31 of the AY. AIS is available year-round — don't wait to check it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### AIS shows ₹2L in dividends I never received. What do I do?

Some companies may have reported dividend as paid to your PAN but the actual credit went somewhere else (old bank account, unclaimed dividend). Flag the entry in AIS feedback as "Amount incorrect" or "Not received." Also check with your registrar/share transfer agent for unclaimed dividend. If you can't reconcile, declare it in ITR and pay tax — then check with the company separately. Alternatively, raise a grievance on the IT portal if you have evidence the dividend was not received.

### AIS shows a property purchase I made but the value is different from actual registry. Which to use in ITR?

AIS gets property data from the sub-registrar — it shows the registered sale consideration (stamp duty value). Use the actual registered consideration in your ITR (same as what's in your sale deed). If there's a genuine difference (e.g., AIS shows circle rate vs actual), declare actual and provide documentation if asked.

### My AIS shows FD interest from Bank X that I closed 3 years ago. Is this an error?

Could be interest accrued in the final year before closure. Contact Bank X for a statement. If truly incorrect, flag in AIS feedback. Keep bank closure documents as proof.
