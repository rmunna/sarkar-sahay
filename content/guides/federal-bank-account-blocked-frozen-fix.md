# Federal Bank Account Blocked or Frozen — How to Fix It (2026)

If your Federal Bank account shows "Account Blocked", "Debit Freeze" or you can't access FedMobile, FedNet (net banking) or make transactions — this guide explains every step to get it resolved.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is independent and not affiliated with Federal Bank. Contact Federal Bank directly for account-specific help.

---

## Why Is Your Federal Bank Account Blocked?

| Reason | Explanation |
|--------|-------------|
| **KYC Not Updated** | RBI mandates periodic KYC — Federal Bank strictly enforces this |
| **Aadhaar / PAN Not Linked** | Mandatory compliance |
| **Wrong Password Attempts** | 3–5 failed login attempts block FedMobile / FedNet |
| **Suspicious Transactions** | Fraud detection |
| **Income Tax Department Order** | Tax freeze |
| **Dormant Account** | No transactions for 2+ years |

---

## Fix 1: KYC Freeze

**Via FedMobile App:**
1. Open **FedMobile** app
2. Look for KYC Update prompt on home screen
3. Complete online KYC — upload Aadhaar + PAN + selfie

**Via FedNet (Net Banking):**
1. Login at **[federalbank.co.in](https://www.federalbank.co.in)** → FedNet
2. Navigate to **Services → Update KYC**
3. Upload documents and submit
4. Verification: 1–3 working days

**At Branch:**
1. Visit Federal Bank branch with Aadhaar + PAN (originals)
2. Fill KYC form
3. Account unblocked in 1–3 working days

---

## Fix 2: FedMobile / FedNet Blocked (Wrong Password)

**FedNet Reset:**
1. Go to federalbank.co.in → FedNet Login
2. Click **"Forgot Password"**
3. Enter Customer ID + registered mobile number
4. OTP verification → Set new password

**FedMobile App Reset:**
1. Open FedMobile → **"Forgot MPIN"**
2. Verify via OTP + debit card
3. Set new MPIN

---

## Fix 3: Suspicious Activity / Other Block

1. Call **Federal Bank Customer Care: 1800-420-1199** (toll-free, 24×7)
2. Explain issue and complete identity verification
3. For permanent unblock: visit branch with Aadhaar + PAN

---

## Fix 4: Dormant Account

1. Visit Federal Bank branch with Aadhaar + PAN (originals) + photos
2. Fill Reactivation form
3. Make a minimum deposit
4. Account reactivated in 1–2 working days

## FAQ

**Is my money safe if Federal Bank freezes my account?**
Yes — DICGC-insured up to ₹5 lakh. Freeze restricts transactions only.

**Will auto-debits fail during a freeze?**
Yes — EMI, SIP, insurance auto-debits will fail. Inform providers immediately.
