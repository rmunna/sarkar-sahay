## What is Udyam Registration?

Udyam Registration is the official process for registering Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) in India. Launched on 1st July 2020, it replaced the earlier Udyog Aadhaar Memorandum (UAM) system. The registration is **completely free, paperless, and done on self-declaration basis** through the Udyam portal.

Every business that qualifies as an MSME should register on the Udyam portal to access numerous government benefits, subsidies, and priority sector lending advantages.

## MSME Classification

MSMEs are classified based on **investment in plant and machinery** AND **annual turnover**:

### Manufacturing and Services (Unified)
| Category | Investment | Turnover |
|----------|-----------|----------|
| **Micro** | Up to ₹1 crore | Up to ₹5 crore |
| **Small** | Up to ₹10 crore | Up to ₹50 crore |
| **Medium** | Up to ₹50 crore | Up to ₹250 crore |

**Both criteria must be satisfied.** The higher of the two classification criteria determines the category.

**Note:** Export turnover is excluded from the turnover calculation, encouraging export-oriented MSMEs.

## Benefits of Udyam Registration

### Financial Benefits
- **Priority sector lending** — Banks must allocate specific credit to MSMEs
- **Collateral-free loans** under CGTMSE up to ₹5 crore
- **Lower interest rates** from banks for registered MSMEs
- **Credit guarantee** schemes
- **MUDRA loans** eligibility — See our [Stand Up India guide](/guide/standup-india-scheme) for additional options

### Government Subsidies and Schemes
- **CLCSS** — Credit Linked Capital Subsidy for technology upgradation (15% subsidy)
- **ZED Certification** — Quality certification with government subsidy
- **Design Clinic** — Product design support
- **Marketing assistance** and trade fair support
- **Incubation support** for innovative businesses

### Tax Benefits
- **Lower corporate tax rate** for manufacturing companies
- **MAT credit** carry forward
- **Excise exemption** for specified goods
- **Stamp duty and registration** concessions in some states

### Procurement Benefits
- **25% government procurement** reserved for MSMEs
- **GeM portal** priority for government supplies
- **No EMD** (Earnest Money Deposit) for government tenders
- **Tender set cost** waivers

### Protection Against Delayed Payments
- Buyers must pay MSME suppliers within **45 days** (Section 15 of MSMED Act)
- **Interest at 3x bank rate** on delayed payments
- **MSME SAMADHAAN** portal for delayed payment complaints
- No minimum claim amount

### Other Benefits
- **Patent and trademark** registration at subsidised rates (50% rebate)
- **ISO certification** reimbursement
- **Barcode registration** subsidy
- **Industrial promotion** subsidy (state-specific)

## How to Register on Udyam Portal

### Prerequisites
- **Aadhaar number** of the proprietor/partner/director — see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
- **PAN and GST** number of the enterprise (if applicable)
- **Business details** — Type, activity, investment, turnover

### Step-by-Step Process

#### Step 1: Visit Udyam Portal
Go to [udyamregistration.gov.in](https://udyamregistration.gov.in)

#### Step 2: Select Registration Type
- **For New Entrepreneurs** — "For those who are not registered yet as MSME"
- **For Existing UAM Holders** — "For those already having UAM"

#### Step 3: Enter Aadhaar Details
- Enter Aadhaar number of the proprietor/managing partner/karta
- Enter name as on Aadhaar
- Validate with OTP sent to Aadhaar-linked mobile

#### Step 4: Validate PAN
- For partnership firms, companies, LLPs — Enter enterprise PAN
- For proprietorships — Personal PAN is used
- PAN is validated with Income Tax database
- **GST details and ITR data are auto-fetched** from government databases

#### Step 5: Fill Enterprise Details
- **Enterprise name** — Your business name
- **Type of organization** — Proprietorship, partnership, HUF, company, LLP, cooperative, trust
- **Major activity** — Manufacturing or Services
- **NIC Code** — Select the National Industrial Classification code for your activity
- **District, state** of the enterprise
- **Date of commencement**
- **Number of employees** (male/female/others)
- **Investment in plant and machinery** (auto-calculated from ITR if available)
- **Turnover** (auto-calculated from GST returns if available)

#### Step 6: Submit Declaration
- Self-declare that information is correct
- Submit the form

#### Step 7: Receive Udyam Registration Number
- **Udyam Registration Number** is generated instantly
- Format: UDYAM-XX-00-0000000 (state code-district code-serial number)
- **E-certificate is emailed** to your registered email
- No need to visit any office or upload any document

## Udyam Registration for Different Business Types

### Proprietorship
- Only proprietor's Aadhaar is needed
- PAN is optional (recommended)
- GST is optional for turnover under ₹40 lakh

### Partnership Firm
- Managing partner's Aadhaar
- Firm's PAN (mandatory)
- Partnership deed details

### Private Limited Company / LLP
- Authorized person's Aadhaar
- Company/LLP PAN (mandatory)
- CIN/LLPIN details

### One Person Company (OPC)
- Director's Aadhaar
- Company PAN

## How to Update or Amend Udyam Registration

1. Visit [udyamregistration.gov.in](https://udyamregistration.gov.in)
2. Click **"Update Udyam Registration"**
3. Enter your Udyam Registration Number
4. Verify with Aadhaar OTP
5. Update the required fields — address, activity, investment, turnover, employees
6. Submit

**Important:** Udyam registration must be updated whenever there's a significant change in investment or turnover that could change your MSME classification.

## How to Print/Download Udyam Certificate

1. Visit [udyamregistration.gov.in](https://udyamregistration.gov.in)
2. Click **"Print/Verify"**
3. Select **"Print Udyam Registration Certificate"**
4. Enter Udyam Registration Number or Aadhaar number
5. Verify with OTP
6. Download and print the certificate

## Udyam Assist Platform (For Informal Enterprises)

For businesses without PAN or GST:
- Visit [udyamassist.gov.in](https://udyamassist.gov.in)
- Register through CSC or facilitation centres
- Get **Udyam Assist Certificate** for informal/micro enterprises
- Access to schemes like PM Vishwakarma, PMEGP

## Important Tips

1. **Registration is free** — Don't pay any agent or website; the official portal charges nothing
2. **No documents to upload** — It's entirely self-declaration based with Aadhaar verification
3. **Update annually** — Review and update your registration when turnover or investment changes
4. **Use Udyam number** on invoices, tenders, and bank applications for MSME benefits
5. **Register on GeM** after getting Udyam certificate — to sell products/services to government

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Udyam registration mandatory?
It's not legally mandatory to operate a business, but it's required to avail any MSME benefits, subsidies, priority lending, and government procurement preferences. It's highly recommended.

### Can service businesses register as MSME?
Yes, both manufacturing and service enterprises can register. The classification criteria (investment and turnover) are the same for both since the 2020 revision.

### What is the validity of Udyam registration?
Udyam registration has **no expiry date**. It remains valid as long as the enterprise exists. However, the MSME classification may change based on updated investment/turnover data pulled from ITR and GST returns.

### Can I have multiple Udyam registrations?
No, an enterprise can have only **one Udyam registration**. If you operate multiple business activities, they should all be listed under one registration. However, different entities (separate PAN) can have separate registrations.

### My UAM is expiring — what should I do?
All existing UAM registrations should be migrated to Udyam. Visit the portal, select "For those already having registration as MSME," and re-register. Your UAM data will be migrated.

### Does Udyam registration help in getting bank loans?
Yes, banks are mandated to provide priority sector lending to registered MSMEs. Having a Udyam certificate makes loan applications stronger and may get you lower interest rates and collateral-free loans under CGTMSE.
