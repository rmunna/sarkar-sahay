# 2BHK Dignity Housing / Indiramma Illu – Telangana

The 2BHK Dignity Housing scheme (also known as Indiramma Illu under the current government) is Telangana's flagship housing program providing free 2BHK houses to economically weaker sections. Each house is approximately 560 sq. ft. with two bedrooms, a hall, kitchen, and attached bathrooms.

## What is the 2BHK Dignity Housing Scheme?

The Government of Telangana constructs 2BHK houses and allots them free of cost to eligible families who do not own a pucca house. The houses are built in multi-storied complexes in urban areas and individual/row houses in rural areas. The scheme is one of the largest state-level housing programs in India.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **No own house:** The applicant should not own a pucca house anywhere in Telangana
2. **Residency:** Must be a resident of Telangana for at least 10 years
3. **Age:** 18 years or above
4. **Income:** Must belong to EWS/LIG (Economically Weaker Section / Low Income Group) — annual income below ₹3,00,000
5. **Priority categories:**
   - SC/ST families
   - Widows and single women
   - Physically handicapped persons
   - Victims of natural calamities
   - Families living in slums or kutcha houses
6. **One house per family:** Only one house per family unit

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### How to Apply

1. **Check for notifications:** The government periodically announces application windows for 2BHK housing — watch for notifications in local newspapers and government portals
2. **Visit Gram Panchayat / Municipal office:** Go to your local Gram Panchayat (rural) or Municipal Corporation office (urban)
3. **Collect application form:** Obtain the 2BHK housing application form
4. **Fill the form:** Enter personal details, family details, current housing status, income, and caste information
5. **Attach documents:** Self-attested copies of all required documents
6. **Submit application:** Submit the completed form with documents
7. **Verification:** Revenue officials conduct field inspection and verify:
   - Current housing status (kutcha/no house)
   - Income and family details
   - Residency in Telangana
8. **Selection:** Beneficiaries are selected based on priority criteria and availability
9. **Allotment:** Selected beneficiaries receive allotment letters
10. **House handover:** Keys are handed over after construction is completed

### Online Application (When Available)

1. Visit the Telangana Housing portal
2. Register with Aadhaar and mobile number
3. Fill the online application form
4. Upload required documents
5. Submit and note the application reference number

## Processing Time

- **Application to selection:** 3–12 months (depends on availability)
- **Construction to handover:** 12–24 months after selection
- **Total timeline:** May take 1–3 years from application to possession

## Important Tips

1. **Apply during open windows:** Watch for government notifications about application periods
2. **Registered in woman's name:** Houses are registered in the name of the woman head of the household
3. **Cannot sell for 10 years:** Beneficiaries cannot sell or transfer the house for 10 years
4. **No cost to beneficiary:** The house is entirely free — do not pay any middleman or broker
5. **Keep in touch with local officials:** Follow up periodically with your Gram Panchayat or Municipal office

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Is the 2BHK house completely free?
Yes, the house is allotted free of cost. Beneficiaries pay nothing for the house itself.

### Q2: In whose name is the house registered?
The house is registered in the name of the woman of the household.

### Q3: Can I sell the house after getting it?
No, beneficiaries cannot sell or transfer the house for a minimum of 10 years from allotment.

### Q4: What if I already own land but no house?
Owning a plot without a pucca house may still make you eligible. Check with local authorities.

### Q5: How are beneficiaries selected?
Selection is based on priority criteria — SC/ST, widows, disabled persons, and those in worst housing conditions get priority.

### Q6: Is this available in all districts?
Yes, the scheme covers all districts in Telangana, both urban and rural areas.

### Q7: Can I apply if I live in a rented house?
Yes, families living in rented accommodation and not owning a pucca house are eligible.
