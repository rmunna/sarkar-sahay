# PM Kisan Samman Nidhi Payment Not Received? Check Status & Fix (2026)

PM Kisan Samman Nidhi gives **₹6,000/year (₹2,000 per installment × 3 installments)** to eligible farmer families. If you're a registered beneficiary but haven't received the latest installment, this guide helps you check status and fix the issue.

> **Latest:** PM Kisan 17th installment (₹2,000) is expected in 2026. Check [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in) for exact disbursement date.

---

## Check PM Kisan Payment Status Online

### Method 1: PM Kisan Portal (Beneficiary Status)

1. Go to **[pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in)**
2. Click **"Beneficiary Status"**
3. Enter: Aadhaar Number OR Mobile Number OR Registration Number
4. Click **"Get Data"**
5. Status shows: ✅ All installments credited OR ❌ Reason for non-payment

### Method 2: PM Kisan Mobile App

1. Download **PM Kisan** app (Google Play / App Store)
2. Login with mobile number → View payment status
3. Shows installment-wise payment status

### Method 3: Common Service Centre (CSC)

- Visit nearest CSC or Jan Seva Kendra
- Operator can check your beneficiary status with Aadhaar

## Reasons PM Kisan Installment Is Stuck

| Reason Shown | Fix |
|-------------|-----|
| **Aadhaar not linked/verified** | Link Aadhaar to PM Kisan account |
| **Bank account invalid** | Update correct bank account |
| **Land record mismatch** | Verify with local Patwari/Agriculture office |
| **Application under verification** | Wait or visit Agriculture office |
| **eKYC not done** | Complete eKYC on portal |
| **Mobile number not updated** | Update mobile in PM Kisan portal |
| **Multiple accounts** | Only one account per family is eligible |

---

## Fix 1: Complete PM Kisan eKYC

eKYC is mandatory for receiving installments. If not done:

1. Go to **pmkisan.gov.in** → **"eKYC"**
2. Enter Aadhaar number
3. OTP on Aadhaar-registered mobile → Verify
4. eKYC confirmed → installments will resume from next cycle

**OR: Via Face Authentication (biometric)**
1. PM Kisan app → **eKYC** → **Face Auth** option
2. Take selfie → System verifies against Aadhaar database
3. Confirmation received instantly

---

## Fix 2: Update Bank Account in PM Kisan

If you changed bank account or the old account is closed:

1. **pmkisan.gov.in** → **"Updation of Self Registered Farmer"**
2. Enter Registration number / Aadhaar
3. OTP on registered mobile
4. Update: Bank account number + IFSC
5. Submit — changes reflect in next installment cycle

**Also update with your district Agriculture office** for faster processing.

---

## Fix 3: Link Aadhaar to PM Kisan Account

If application shows "Aadhaar not seeded":

1. Visit **Common Service Centre (CSC)** or district Agriculture office
2. Or: pmkisan.gov.in → "Aadhaar Link"
3. Enter registration number + new Aadhaar + OTP
4. Link confirmed

---

## Fix 4: Application Rejected — How to Re-apply

If status shows "Rejected" with reason:

| Common Rejection Reason | Fix |
|------------------------|-----|
| Land record not found | Verify with local Patwari |
| Aadhaar name mismatch with land records | Correct Aadhaar or land record |
| Ineligible (government employee, tax payer) | Check eligibility criteria |
| Multiple applications | Only one per family allowed |

**Re-application:**
1. pmkisan.gov.in → "New Farmer Registration"
2. Or visit nearest Agriculture office/CSC with corrected documents

## Frequently Asked Questions

**PM Kisan 17th installment — when will it come?**
PM Kisan releases 3 installments per year (April, August, December approximately). The 17th installment timing will be announced on pmkisan.gov.in — check directly.

**My Aadhaar and bank account are linked but installment still not received — why?**
Could be: eKYC not completed (must be done separately), land record verification pending, or state-level verification queue. Call PM Kisan helpline 155261 with your registration number.

**I am a farmer but registered under wrong Aadhaar — how to fix?**
Visit your nearest Agriculture office with correct Aadhaar and land documents. They can update the registration. Cannot be done on the portal alone.

**Can I check PM Kisan status without registration number?**
Yes — use Aadhaar number or registered mobile number on pmkisan.gov.in → Beneficiary Status.

**My PM Kisan shows ₹2,000 sent but not in my bank account — why?**
Transaction may take 2–3 working days after disbursement. If not received in 5 working days after the PM Kisan portal shows "transferred": Call your bank with the PFMS (Public Financial Management System) transaction reference number shown on the portal.
