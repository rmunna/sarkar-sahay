# PM Awas Yojana Beneficiary List — Check Name, Status & Gramin/Urban List

Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) provides financial assistance to eligible families to build or buy their own pucca house. Once applications are processed, the government releases **beneficiary lists** at regular intervals. If you have applied under PMAY-Gramin or PMAY-Urban, checking whether your name appears in the beneficiary list is the most important step to know if you will receive the housing benefit.

This guide explains **exactly how to check your name** in the PMAY beneficiary list — for both rural (Gramin) and urban areas — using Aadhaar number, registration number, or name search.

## How to Check Name in PMAY Gramin (Rural) Beneficiary List

PMAY-Gramin beneficiary lists are published on the official portal **pmayg.nic.in**. You can check your name using the AwaasSoft reporting system.

### Method 1: Check by Registration Number

1. Visit the official PMAY-G portal: [https://pmayg.nic.in](https://pmayg.nic.in)
2. Click on **"Stakeholders"** in the top menu
3. Select **"IAY / PMAYG Beneficiary"** from the dropdown
4. Enter your **Registration Number** in the search box
5. Click **Submit**
6. Your beneficiary details — including name, sanction amount, installments received, and house construction status — will appear on screen

### Method 2: Search by Name in the Beneficiary List (Advanced Search)

If you do not have your registration number, you can search the full list area-wise:

1. Go to [https://rhreporting.nic.in/netiay/beneficiarylist.aspx](https://rhreporting.nic.in/netiay/beneficiarylist.aspx)
2. Select your **State**, **District**, **Block**, **Panchayat**, and **Village** from the dropdown menus
3. Select the scheme as **Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Gramin** under the scheme/benefit section
4. Enter the **captcha code** and click **Submit**
5. A complete beneficiary list for your village will appear — search for your name in the list

> **Tip:** Use Ctrl+F (or Command+F on Mac) to quickly search your name on the page.

### Method 3: Using the Awaas+ Mobile App

1. Download the **Awaas+** app from the PMAY-G official website or Google Play Store
2. Log in using your Aadhaar number and face authentication
3. You can check your beneficiary status and house construction progress directly from the app

## How to Check Name in PMAY Urban Beneficiary List

For PMAY-Urban, the beneficiary list and application status are available on the **pmaymis.gov.in** portal.

### Method 1: Search by Name, Father's Name & Mobile Number

1. Visit [https://pmaymis.gov.in](https://pmaymis.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Search Beneficiary"** on the homepage
3. Enter the **first 3 letters of your name** in the search box
4. Click **Show**
5. A list of matching beneficiaries will appear — find your name and check your status

### Method 2: Track Application Status by Application ID

1. Go to [https://pmaymis.gov.in/track_application_status.aspx](https://pmaymis.gov.in/track_application_status.aspx)
2. You can search by:
   - **Father's Name and Mobile Number**
   - **Application ID**
   - **Assessment ID**
3. Enter the required details and click **Track**
4. Your application status with all details will be displayed

### Method 3: Check via UMANG App

1. Download the **UMANG** app from Google Play Store or Apple App Store
2. Search for **PMAY** in the app
3. Select **PMAY-Urban** and choose **Track Application**
4. Enter your Application ID or Aadhaar number
5. Your status will be shown on screen

## How to Search PMAY Beneficiary List by Aadhaar Number

For **PMAY-Gramin**, you can search by Aadhaar:

1. Visit [https://pmayg.nic.in](https://pmayg.nic.in)
2. Click on **"Stakeholders"** → **"IAY / PMAYG Beneficiary"**
3. Instead of the registration number, enter your **Aadhaar Number**
4. Click **Submit** to view your beneficiary details

For **PMAY-Urban**, Aadhaar-based search is available through:

1. The **CLSS Awas Portal (CLAP)** at [https://pmay-urban.gov.in](https://pmay-urban.gov.in)
2. Click on **"For Beneficiary"** and enter your Aadhaar number
3. Your subsidy and application status will be displayed

> **Note:** Your Aadhaar must be linked to your PMAY application for this search to work.

## How to Check PMAY Subsidy Status (CLSS Tracker)

If you applied under the **Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme (CLSS)** of PMAY-Urban, you can track your subsidy status:

### Online Method

1. Visit the **CLAP portal**: [https://pmay-urban.gov.in](https://pmay-urban.gov.in)
2. Click on **"CLSS Tracker"** tab
3. Enter your **Aadhaar number**, **Application ID**, or **bank loan account number**
4. Click **Submit**
5. Your subsidy disbursement status — including amount sanctioned, amount credited, and pending status — will be displayed

### Offline Method

1. Visit your **nearest bank branch** where you applied for the home loan
2. Ask the loan officer for an update on your CLSS subsidy status
3. You can also visit your **local Municipal Corporation** or **Urban Local Body (ULB)** office for assistance

### Through UMANG Platform

1. Open the **UMANG app** or visit [https://web.umang.gov.in](https://web.umang.gov.in)
2. Search for **PMAY** → Select **CLSS Tracker**
3. Enter your details to check subsidy status

## What to Do If Your Name Is Not in the PMAY List

If you applied for PMAY but cannot find your name in the beneficiary list, follow these steps:

### Step 1: Verify Your Application Was Submitted

- Check if you received an **acknowledgment receipt** or **Application ID** when you applied
- If you applied through a Common Service Centre (CSC), contact them with your receipt

### Step 2: Check Application Status

- Use the tracking methods described above to check if your application is still **under review** or has been **rejected**
- Applications may take several months to process

### Step 3: Contact Your Local Authority

- **For PMAY-Gramin:** Visit your **Gram Panchayat** office or **Block Development Office (BDO)**
- **For PMAY-Urban:** Visit your **Municipal Corporation** or **Urban Local Body (ULB)** office
- Carry your Application ID, Aadhaar card, and original application receipt

### Step 4: File a Grievance Online

- **PMAY-G Grievance:** Visit [https://pmayg.nic.in](https://pmayg.nic.in) → Grievance section
- **PMAY-U Grievance:** Visit [https://pmaymis.gov.in](https://pmaymis.gov.in) → Grievance Redressal
- You can also file complaints through the **CPGRAMS portal** at [https://pgportal.gov.in](https://pgportal.gov.in)

### Step 5: Contact the Helpline

- **PMAY-Urban Helpline:** 1800-11-6163 (toll-free)
- **PMAY-Gramin Helpline:** 1800-11-6446 (toll-free)

## How to Apply If You Are Eligible but Not Listed

If you meet the eligibility criteria for PMAY but have not yet applied, you can still submit your application:

- **For PMAY-Gramin:** Applications are identified through the **Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC)** and verified by Gram Sabha. Visit your Gram Panchayat or use the **Awaas+** app for self-survey registration.
- **For PMAY-Urban 2.0:** Apply online through [https://pmaymis.gov.in](https://pmaymis.gov.in) or visit a Common Service Centre (CSC).

For a complete step-by-step application guide, read our detailed article: [How to Apply for PM Awas Yojana (PMAY)](/guide/pm-awas-yojana-apply).

## Common Problems and Solutions

### Problem: Website Not Loading or Showing Error

**Solution:** The PMAY portals experience heavy traffic. Try accessing during off-peak hours (early morning or late evening). Clear your browser cache and try a different browser. The mobile app may work better during high-traffic periods.

### Problem: Registration Number Not Found

**Solution:** Double-check the registration number for typos. If the number is correct, your application may still be under processing. Contact your Gram Panchayat (Gramin) or ULB office (Urban) for verification.

### Problem: Aadhaar Number Not Linked

**Solution:** If your Aadhaar-based search shows no results, your Aadhaar may not be seeded with your application. Visit your local PMAY office with your Aadhaar card and application receipt to get it linked.

### Problem: Name Spelling Mismatch

**Solution:** If your name is spelled differently in the PMAY list and your Aadhaar, the search may not match. Try searching with both spellings. Get the name corrected at your local authority office.

### Problem: Subsidy Not Credited to Bank Account

**Solution:** Subsidy disbursal can take 3-6 months after approval. Check subsidy status on the CLAP portal. If delayed beyond 6 months, contact your bank and the Central Nodal Agency (NHB, HUDCO, or SBI).

### Problem: Application Shows "Rejected"

**Solution:** Common rejection reasons include incomplete documents, already owning a pucca house, or income exceeding the limit. You can file a fresh application after correcting the issue or file a grievance if you believe the rejection was wrong.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. How can I check my PM Awas Yojana beneficiary list online?

For PMAY-Gramin, visit pmayg.nic.in and search under "Stakeholders" → "IAY/PMAYG Beneficiary." For PMAY-Urban, visit pmaymis.gov.in and click "Search Beneficiary." You can search by name, Aadhaar, or application ID.

### 2. Can I check the PMAY list by Aadhaar number?

Yes. On the PMAY-G portal, enter your Aadhaar number in the beneficiary search. For PMAY-U, use the CLAP portal or UMANG app to search by Aadhaar.

### 3. How do I check PMAY Gramin list by village name?

Visit rhreporting.nic.in, select your State, District, Block, Panchayat, and Village, choose PMAY-G as the scheme, and submit to see the full village-wise list.

### 4. What is the PMAY-Urban 2.0 scheme?

PMAY-Urban 2.0 was launched in September 2024 to continue the Housing for All mission. It targets building 1 crore additional urban houses with enhanced financial assistance and interest subsidies.

### 5. How much money do PMAY-Gramin beneficiaries get?

PMAY-G beneficiaries receive **₹1.20 lakh in plain areas** and **₹1.30 lakh in hilly/difficult areas** as financial assistance. Additionally, 90/95 person-days of unskilled labour wages are provided under MGNREGA.

### 6. How many installments are paid under PMAY-Gramin?

The financial assistance is released in **3 installments** linked to the stages of house construction — foundation, lintel level, and roof completion. Each stage is verified through geo-tagged photographs.

### 7. Can I apply for PMAY online?

Yes. PMAY-Urban applications can be submitted online at pmaymis.gov.in. PMAY-Gramin applications are typically identified through SECC data, but you can register through the Awaas+ app or visit your Gram Panchayat. See our [complete application guide](/guide/pm-awas-yojana-apply).

### 8. What should I do if my name is missing from the PMAY list?

First verify your application status online. Then contact your Gram Panchayat (rural) or Municipal office (urban). If needed, file a grievance online through the respective PMAY portal or call the toll-free helpline.

### 9. How do I check my PMAY CLSS subsidy status?

Visit the CLAP portal at pmay-urban.gov.in, click on "CLSS Tracker," and enter your Aadhaar number or loan account number. You can also check through the UMANG app.

### 10. Is PMAY available for middle-income families?

Under PMAY-Urban CLSS, **MIG-I** (income ₹6-12 lakh) and **MIG-II** (income ₹12-18 lakh) categories were eligible for interest subsidies on home loans. Under PMAY 2.0, check the latest eligibility criteria on the official portal.

### 11. Can I check the PMAY list using my mobile phone?

Yes. All PMAY portals are mobile-friendly. You can also use the **UMANG app** or **Awaas+ app** to check your beneficiary status on your smartphone.

### 12. How often is the PMAY beneficiary list updated?

The beneficiary list is updated periodically as new applications are processed and approved. For PMAY-Gramin, lists are typically updated after Gram Sabha verification. For Urban, updates happen as ULBs process applications.

### 13. What documents are needed to verify PMAY beneficiary status?

You need your **Registration Number or Application ID**, **Aadhaar number**, and the **name as submitted in the application**. Keep your original application receipt handy for reference.
