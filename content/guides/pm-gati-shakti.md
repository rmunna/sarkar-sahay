# PM Gati Shakti: National Master Plan for Multi-Modal Connectivity

**PM Gati Shakti — National Master Plan for Multi-Modal Connectivity** was launched on 13 October 2021 as a transformative approach to integrated infrastructure planning. Backed by an investment of **Rs 100 lakh crore**, this digital platform brings together 16 ministries to ensure coordinated planning of roads, railways, airports, ports, waterways, and logistics infrastructure across India.

---

## What is PM Gati Shakti?

PM Gati Shakti is a **GIS-based digital master plan** that provides a unified platform for infrastructure planning. Before this scheme, different ministries planned roads, railways, and other infrastructure independently, leading to duplication, delays, and poor connectivity. Gati Shakti solves this by:

- Providing a **single digital platform** with 1,600+ data layers
- Enabling **integrated planning** across 16 central ministries
- Reducing **logistics costs** from 14% to 8% of GDP (target)
- Eliminating **siloed infrastructure development**
- Ensuring **last-mile connectivity** to economic zones

---

## Key Objectives

1. **Reduce logistics costs** by creating efficient multi-modal transport
2. **Improve connectivity** to industrial clusters, SEZs, and agricultural zones
3. **Coordinate infrastructure projects** across ministries to avoid duplication
4. **Speed up project approvals** through digital planning tools
5. **Boost manufacturing and exports** through better infrastructure
6. **Create employment** through massive infrastructure development

## How Does It Work?

### The Gati Shakti Platform

1. **Data integration:** 1,600+ GIS layers from various ministries on one platform
2. **Project planning:** New infrastructure projects are planned using the platform to ensure connectivity with existing networks
3. **Gap identification:** The platform identifies connectivity gaps and priority corridors
4. **Inter-ministerial coordination:** Network Planning Group reviews projects for alignment
5. **Monitoring:** Real-time tracking of project progress

### The 7 Engines of Growth

PM Gati Shakti focuses on 7 key areas:
1. Roads and Highways
2. Railways
3. Airports
4. Ports
5. Mass Transport (Metro, Bus)
6. Waterways
7. Logistics Infrastructure

## For Businesses: How to Leverage Gati Shakti

1. **Access the portal** at [pmgatishakti.gov.in](https://pmgatishakti.gov.in/) to view planned infrastructure
2. **Plan factory locations** based on upcoming connectivity projects

5. **Provide feedback** through state-level Gati Shakti coordination committees

## For States and Local Bodies

States have been asked to create their own **State Gati Shakti Master Plans** aligned with the national plan. This ensures:
- State infrastructure projects complement central projects
- No duplication of effort between state and central schemes
- Unified land acquisition and clearance processes

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. What is PM Gati Shakti?
It is a digital master plan for integrated multi-modal infrastructure planning across India, bringing together 16+ ministries on one platform.

### 2. What is the total investment planned?
Rs 100 lakh crore (Rs 100 trillion) in infrastructure development.

### 3. Can common people access the Gati Shakti portal?
The main planning portal is for government use, but project information and logistics data are progressively being made public.

### 4. How does it reduce logistics costs?
By ensuring coordinated development of roads, railways, ports, and warehouses, goods move faster and cheaper across the country.

### 5. Which ministries are involved?
16 ministries including Road Transport, Railways, Shipping, Aviation, Petroleum, Power, Telecom, and more.

### 6. Does this create jobs?
Yes, massive infrastructure development creates direct and indirect employment in construction, logistics, and related sectors.

### 7. Is this different from Sagarmala and Bharatmala?
Sagarmala (ports) and Bharatmala (roads) are individual schemes. Gati Shakti integrates all such schemes on one planning platform for coordination.
