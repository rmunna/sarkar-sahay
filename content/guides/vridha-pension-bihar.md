# Mukhyamantri Vridha Pension Yojana Bihar: Apply Online on SSPMIS Portal

Mukhyamantri Vridhjan Pension Yojana (MVPY) is a social security scheme by the Bihar government that provides a monthly pension to senior citizens aged 60 years and above. The pension amount ranges from **₹400 to ₹500 per month** depending on the age group. The scheme is managed through the **SSPMIS (Social Security Pension Management Information System)** portal.

## What is Mukhyamantri Vridha Pension Yojana?

This scheme was launched to provide financial security to elderly citizens of Bihar who have no regular source of income. Unlike the central government's IGNOAPS (Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme) which has stricter BPL criteria, Bihar's MVPY covers a wider population with more relaxed eligibility requirements.

### Pension Amount

| Age Group | Monthly Pension |
|-----------|----------------|
| 60-79 years | ₹400 per month |
| 80 years and above | ₹500 per month |

The pension is transferred directly to the beneficiary's bank account every month via DBT.

## Who is Eligible?

- Must be a **permanent resident of Bihar**
- Age must be **60 years or above**
- Must not be receiving pension from any **other government scheme** (state or central government retirement pension)
- Must not be a **government retiree**
- Applicant should belong to the **general category or any other category** — the scheme is universal for Bihar residents
- Must have a **bank account** linked with Aadhaar

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** of the applicant
- **Age proof** — voter ID, birth certificate, or school leaving certificate
- **Bank account passbook** (with IFSC code)
- **Passport-size photograph**
- **Residential proof** — voter ID or ration card
- **Mobile number** linked to Aadhaar
- **Aadhaar consent form** (available on SSPMIS portal)

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Online Application via SSPMIS

1. Visit the official SSPMIS portal at **[sspmis.bihar.gov.in](https://www.sspmis.bihar.gov.in/)**
2. Click on **"Register New Beneficiary"**
3. Select your **district, block, panchayat/ward**
4. Enter your **Aadhaar number** for verification
5. Complete the **Aadhaar authentication** (biometric or OTP)
6. Fill in personal details — name, father's name, date of birth, address
7. Enter **bank account details** — account number, IFSC code, bank name
8. Upload **passport-size photograph**
9. Review all information and **submit the application**
10. Note down your **Beneficiary ID** for tracking

### Offline Application

1. Visit your nearest **Block/Circle office** or **RTPS counter**
2. Obtain the **Vridha Pension application form**
3. Fill in all details and attach required documents
4. Submit the form to the concerned officer
5. Collect the **acknowledgement receipt**
6. The application is processed and verified at the block/district level

### Check Application Status

1. Visit [sspmis.bihar.gov.in](https://www.sspmis.bihar.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"Beneficiary Status"** or **"Search Application Status"**
3. Enter your **Beneficiary ID** or **Aadhaar number**
4. View your application status and payment history

## Fees

There are **no fees** to apply for Vridha Pension. The application is completely free both online and offline.

## Processing Time

- Online applications: **1-3 months** for verification and approval
- Offline applications: **2-4 months**
- First pension credit after approval: within **1-2 months**

## Important Tips

1. **Download Aadhaar Consent Form** from the SSPMIS portal and keep it ready before applying
2. **Ensure Aadhaar biometric is active** — if it's locked, unlock it before applying as biometric authentication may be required
3. **Check pension status regularly** on SSPMIS to ensure timely credits
4. **Update bank details** immediately if you change your bank account — contact the block office
5. **Report non-receipt of pension** to the Block Development Officer (BDO) or call the helpline

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Is there any income limit for this pension?
There is no strict income ceiling for MVPY. However, the applicant should not be receiving any other government pension (retirement pension, central pension scheme, etc.).

### Q2: Can BPL and APL both apply?
Yes, both BPL and APL category senior citizens of Bihar can apply for MVPY. It is a universal scheme for all senior citizens.

### Q3: How is the pension amount credited?
The pension is transferred directly to the beneficiary's **Aadhaar-linked bank account** via DBT every month.

### Q4: What if the pensioner passes away?
The family should inform the block office immediately. The pension will be stopped from the next month. Any excess payment may need to be refunded.

### Q5: Can I apply for both central and state pension?
No, if you are already receiving pension under IGNOAPS or any other central scheme, you cannot apply for MVPY. You can choose one.

### Q6: What is SSPMIS?
SSPMIS stands for Social Security Pension Management Information System. It is the official Bihar government portal ([sspmis.bihar.gov.in](https://www.sspmis.bihar.gov.in/)) for managing all pension-related schemes.

### Q7: Can NRIs from Bihar apply?
No, the applicant must be a **current permanent resident of Bihar** living in the state.
