# NPS Vatsalya — NPS Account for Minor Children (Budget 2024)

**NPS Vatsalya** is a new category of National Pension System account announced in the **Union Budget 2024-25** (July 2024) and operationalized by PFRDA. It allows **parents or legal guardians to open an NPS account for a minor child** — starting investment for the child's future retirement from birth.

> Unlike PPF (matures in 15 years) or Sukanya Samriddhi (only for girls, matures at 21), NPS Vatsalya is designed as a **long-term retirement corpus** that the child inherits as a regular NPS account when they turn 18.

## Key Features of NPS Vatsalya

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Who can open** | Parents or legal guardians of minors |
| **Age of child** | 0 to 17 years (any age, including newborns) |
| **Minimum contribution** | ₹1,000 per year |
| **Maximum contribution** | No limit |
| **Account holder** | The minor child (guardian manages until age 18) |
| **Conversion at 18** | Automatically converts to regular NPS account |
| **Lock-in** | Until age 18; then regular NPS lock-in (until 60) |
| **Partial withdrawal** | Up to 3 times, max 25% of corpus each, for education/medical |
| **PRAN** | Minor gets a Permanent Retirement Account Number (PRAN) |

## How to Open NPS Vatsalya Account

### Online via enps.nsdl.com

1. Visit **[enps.nsdl.com](https://enps.nsdl.com/)** or **[npscra.nsdl.co.in](https://npscra.nsdl.co.in/)**
2. Click **"Open NPS Account"** → Select **"NPS Vatsalya"** account type
3. Enter **guardian's details**: Aadhaar, PAN, mobile
4. Enter **minor child's details**: name, date of birth, Aadhaar (or birth certificate if no Aadhaar)
5. Upload documents: child's birth certificate, guardian's Aadhaar, guardian's PAN
6. Select **fund manager** (LIC Pension Fund, SBI Pension Funds, HDFC, ICICI, Kotak, etc.)
7. Choose **investment pattern**: Auto (default) or Active (manual allocation)
8. Pay initial contribution (minimum ₹1,000)
9. Receive **PRAN (Permanent Retirement Account Number)** via email/SMS

### Offline via Bank Branch (PoP — Point of Presence)

1. Visit any bank branch that is an **NPS Point of Presence (PoP)**
   - SBI, HDFC, ICICI, PNB, BOB, Axis, and most major banks are PoPs
2. Ask for **NPS Vatsalya registration form**
3. Fill guardian + minor child details
4. Submit with documents
5. Pay initial contribution
6. PRAN generated and sent to registered email

### Via Post Office

All post offices in India are NPS Points of Presence:
1. Visit your nearest post office with documents
2. Ask for **NPS Vatsalya** form
3. Fill and submit; contribution can be paid in cash

## Investment Options Under NPS Vatsalya

Same as regular NPS — you choose:

**Asset Classes:**
- **Equity (E)**: Up to 75% in stocks (higher risk, higher potential return)
- **Corporate Bonds (C)**: Up to 100% in corporate debt
- **Government Securities (G)**: Up to 100% in government bonds
- **Alternative (A)**: REITs, InvITs (max 5%)

**Investment Approaches:**
- **Auto Choice (Default)**: Allocation shifts from aggressive to conservative as child approaches 18
- **Active Choice**: Guardian manually sets the equity-bond allocation

For young children (0–10 years), higher equity allocation is generally recommended given the long investment horizon.

## Partial Withdrawal Rules

Unlike regular NPS which restricts withdrawal until 60, NPS Vatsalya allows **limited partial withdrawal**:

| Condition | Rules |
|-----------|-------|
| **Number of withdrawals** | Maximum 3 during the minor period |
| **Amount per withdrawal** | Up to 25% of the child's own contributions |
| **Allowed purposes** | Education, serious illness (hospital treatment), disability |
| **Not allowed for** | General expenses, vehicle, property, marriage |

After age 18: The regular NPS rules apply (partial withdrawal allowed after 3 years of NPS for education, home, critical illness).

## What Happens at Age 18?

When the minor turns 18:
1. The NPS Vatsalya account **automatically converts to a regular NPS Tier-1 account**
2. The child (now adult) becomes the account holder directly
3. They can either:
   - **Continue** contributing to the NPS until retirement (60)
   - **Exit partially**: If corpus < ₹2.5 lakh, withdraw everything; if ≥ ₹2.5L, withdraw 20% and use 80% for annuity
   
The conversion is smooth — no new paperwork needed unless the child wants to change fund manager or investment pattern.

## Tax Benefits of NPS Vatsalya

**For the guardian (parent):**
- Contributions to NPS Vatsalya may be claimed under **Section 80CCD(1)** (up to ₹1.5L combined with other 80C investments) — verify with current PFRDA/IT guidelines, as tax benefit rules for Vatsalya were being finalized
- Check at pfrda.org.in or with your CA for AY-specific guidance

**For the child:**
- No immediate tax liability for the minor
- When they withdraw at 60: tax rules same as regular NPS (40% of corpus tax-free as annuity purchase; 60% can be withdrawn with tax on partial withdrawals)

## NPS Vatsalya vs Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana vs PPF

| Feature | NPS Vatsalya | Sukanya Samriddhi | PPF |
|---------|-------------|------------------|-----|
| **Who can open** | Any child (boy/girl) | Only girls (up to 10 years) | Anyone |
| **Purpose** | Long-term retirement | Marriage/education fund | Long-term savings |
| **Maturity** | At 60 (child's age) | At 21 years of daughter | 15 years |
| **Returns** | Market-linked | 8.2% (government fixed) | 7.1% (government fixed) |
| **Withdrawal before maturity** | 3 times for specific purposes | At 18 (50% for education) | After 5 years (partial) |
| **Tax benefit** | 80CCD | 80C | 80C |
| **Risk** | Market risk (equity option) | Risk-free | Risk-free |

**Choose NPS Vatsalya if**: You want higher growth potential over decades and are okay with market-linked returns and NPS lock-in until 60.

**Choose Sukanya Samriddhi if**: You have a daughter and want a risk-free, government-backed corpus for marriage/education by her 21st birthday.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I open NPS Vatsalya for my newborn?

Yes. NPS Vatsalya can be opened from birth onwards. For infants without Aadhaar, birth certificate is the primary document.

### What is the minimum annual contribution?

₹1,000 per year. No maximum limit.

### Can I make monthly contributions instead of annual?

Yes. SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) through NPS is available — set up monthly auto-debit via D-Remit or online portal.

### My daughter is 14. Is it too late to open NPS Vatsalya?

Not too late — you can open up to when she's 17. She'll have 4 years of contributions as Vatsalya, then it converts to regular NPS at 18. The corpus continues to grow for another 42 years (until she's 60), so even a late start benefits from compounding.

### Is NPS Vatsalya part of the ₹50,000 80CCD(1B) additional deduction?

The 80CCD(1B) additional ₹50,000 deduction is for the subscriber's own NPS contributions. Since NPS Vatsalya is for a minor, the tax benefit treatment may differ — as of 2025-26, verify with your CA or at pfrda.org.in for the specific section applicable.
