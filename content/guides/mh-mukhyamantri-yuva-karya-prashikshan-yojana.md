# Mukhyamantri Yuva Karya Prashikshan Yojana – Maharashtra 6-Month Paid Internship Scheme

Mukhyamantri Yuva Karya Prashikshan Yojana is a flagship employment scheme launched by the Maharashtra Mahayuti government under Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis. It provides 6-month paid internships to unemployed youth in Maharashtra, helping them gain hands-on work experience in government and private sector organisations.

The scheme is managed by the **Department of Skills, Employment, Entrepreneurship & Innovation, Maharashtra** and implemented through the official portal at **rojgar.mahaswayam.gov.in**.

---

## What is Mukhyamantri Yuva Karya Prashikshan Yojana?

This scheme connects unemployed educated youth with employers — both government departments and private companies — for structured 6-month internships. During the internship:

- Candidates gain real-world work experience in their sector of interest
- A **monthly stipend is paid directly to the candidate's bank account** via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)
- Employers get access to young, trained talent at zero hiring cost
- Candidates who perform well often get preference in regular hiring by the same organisation

---

## Eligibility Criteria

| Criteria | Details |
|---|---|
| **Age** | 18 to 35 years |
| **Education** | Minimum 12th pass (HSC); also open to ITI holders, diploma holders, graduates, and postgraduates |
| **Residence** | Maharashtra resident |
| **Employment status** | Currently unemployed or not in regular employment |

---

## Stipend Details

The monthly stipend is paid via **Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)** directly into the candidate's bank account. The exact stipend amount varies by educational qualification — candidates with higher qualifications receive a higher stipend.

> Specific stipend slabs by qualification level are notified on the official portal at [rojgar.mahaswayam.gov.in](https://rojgar.mahaswayam.gov.in/) at the time of registration opening.

---

## Duration and Structure

- **Duration:** 6 months
- **Mode:** In-office or hybrid (depending on the employer)
- **Sectors:** Both government departments and registered private companies
- **Location:** Within Maharashtra (interns are typically placed near their home district)

---

## Employer Eligibility (Who Can Offer Internships)

Private companies wishing to participate must meet these criteria:

- **Operational in Maharashtra** for at least **3 years**
- Registered with **EPF, ESIC, and GST**
- Registered as an employer on the Mahaswayam portal

Government departments participate directly through the scheme.

---

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar card
- Maharashtra domicile/residence proof
- Educational qualification certificates (12th marksheet / ITI / diploma / degree certificate)
- Bank account passbook (for DBT)
- Passport-size photograph
- Mobile number linked to Aadhaar (for OTP verification)

---

## How to Apply

1. Visit the official portal: [rojgar.mahaswayam.gov.in](https://rojgar.mahaswayam.gov.in/)
2. Click on **Mukhyamantri Yuva Karya Prashikshan Yojana** or the youth internship section
3. Register with your Aadhaar-linked mobile number (OTP verification required)
4. Fill in personal details, educational qualifications, and preferred sector/district
5. Upload required documents
6. Submit the application and note your registration ID
7. Await employer matching — you will be notified via SMS/email when an internship is assigned

For offline help, visit your nearest **District Skill Development Centre (DSDC)**.

**Helpline:** 1800 120 8040 (toll-free, Monday–Saturday)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can engineering graduates apply?**
A: Yes. Both diploma and degree holders in any field can apply, as long as they are between 18–35 and Maharashtra residents.

**Q: Is the 6-month internship mandatory to complete?**
A: Yes. Withdrawal before completion may disqualify from re-applying. Full completion is required to receive the full stipend.

**Q: Can someone domiciled in another state but studying in Maharashtra apply?**
A: No. Maharashtra domicile/residence is required.

**Q: When are new registration batches opened?**
A: The portal opens for fresh registrations periodically. Check [rojgar.mahaswayam.gov.in](https://rojgar.mahaswayam.gov.in/) or call 1800 120 8040 for current windows.

**Q: Is this the same as Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana?**
A: No. Ladki Bahin is a monthly cash assistance for women. Yuva Karya Prashikshan is an internship scheme for unemployed youth (men and women) aged 18–35.
