# GeM Portal Login OTP Not Coming — How to Fix Authentication

Unable to log into the GeM portal because the OTP isn't arriving? Login and authentication issues are among the most reported problems on the Government e-Marketplace. This guide covers fixes for OTP delays, registered mobile issues, password reset problems, and account deactivation.

## Common Login & OTP Errors

- **OTP not received on mobile** — SMS not delivered after clicking "Send OTP"
- **OTP not received on email** — email OTP missing from inbox and spam
- **"Invalid OTP"** — OTP entered but not accepted
- **"Account is deactivated"** — login blocked with deactivation message
- **"User ID not found"** — entered credentials not recognized
- **"Too many OTP attempts"** — temporarily blocked due to multiple requests
- **Password reset link not working** — reset email not received or link expired

## Fix 1: OTP Not Received on Registered Mobile

The OTP is sent to the mobile number registered on your GeM profile:

1. **Verify your registered mobile** — Make sure the mobile number on your GeM profile is current and active
2. **Check network signal** — Weak signal or no service area means SMS won't be delivered
3. **Wait 2-3 minutes** — OTP delivery can take time during peak hours (10 AM - 1 PM)
4. **Don't request multiple OTPs rapidly** — Wait at least 2 minutes between requests; too many attempts trigger a temporary block
5. **Check DND/spam filter** — If you have DND activated, transactional SMS should still come through, but some carriers block them; contact your mobile operator
6. **Phone storage full** — If your phone's SMS inbox is full, new messages won't be received; delete old messages
7. **Restart your phone** — Sometimes a simple restart fixes SMS delivery issues
8. **Try at off-peak hours** — GeM OTP servers are less loaded early morning (7-9 AM) or after 7 PM

## Fix 2: Email OTP Not Received

If you selected email OTP option:

1. **Check spam/junk folder** — GeM emails often land in spam; search for emails from @gem.gov.in
2. **Whitelist GeM emails** — Add noreply@gem.gov.in and support@gem.gov.in to your contacts
3. **Check registered email** — The OTP goes to the email on your GeM profile, not any other email
4. **Gmail Promotions tab** — If using Gmail, check the Promotions or Updates tab
5. **Corporate email filters** — Government email servers (@gov.in, @nic.in) may have strict spam filters; check with your IT team
6. **Request OTP again** — Wait 2 minutes, then request a fresh OTP

## Fix 3: "Invalid OTP" Error

If the OTP arrives but the system rejects it:

1. **OTP expiry** — GeM OTPs typically expire in 3-5 minutes; enter immediately after receiving
2. **Copy-paste the OTP** — Avoid manual typing errors; copy from SMS and paste
3. **Latest OTP only** — If you requested multiple OTPs, only the most recent one is valid
4. **No spaces** — Ensure there are no leading/trailing spaces when pasting the OTP
5. **Browser auto-fill interference** — Disable browser auto-fill for OTP fields
6. **Clock sync** — Ensure your device time is set to automatic (IST); time mismatch can cause OTP validation failures

## Fix 4: Password Reset Not Working

If you've forgotten your password:

1. Go to [gem.gov.in](https://gem.gov.in/) → Click **"Login"** → **"Forgot Password"**
2. Enter your **registered email ID** or **User ID**
3. Complete the **CAPTCHA**
4. Check your email for the **reset link** (also check spam folder)
5. The reset link expires in **30 minutes** — click it promptly
6. Set a new password following GeM requirements:
   - Minimum 8 characters
   - At least one uppercase letter, one lowercase, one number, one special character
7. After reset, try logging in with the new password

**If reset email doesn't arrive:**
- Ensure you're using the email registered on GeM (not a different one)
- Try again after 15 minutes
- Contact GeM helpline at 1800-419-3436

## Fix 5: Account Deactivated

If you see "Account is deactivated":

1. **Inactivity** — GeM may deactivate accounts inactive for 12+ months
2. **Compliance issues** — Expired GST, suspended PAN, or document issues can trigger deactivation
3. **Policy violation** — Accounts may be deactivated for bid manipulation or fake listings
4. **Organization removed** — If your department/organization was removed from GeM, associated accounts get deactivated

**How to reactivate:**
- **Contact GeM helpline:** 1800-419-3436
- **Email:** helpdesk-gem@gov.in with your User ID, organization name, and PAN
- **Raise a ticket** on the GeM portal (if accessible) under "Account Related"
- **Submit documents** — You may need to resubmit PAN, GST, and organization proof
- Reactivation typically takes **5-10 working days**

## Fix 6: "User ID Not Found"

If the system doesn't recognize your login:

1. **Check exact User ID** — GeM User ID may be different from your email; check your original registration email
2. **Case sensitivity** — User IDs may be case-sensitive; try exact case
3. **Buyer vs Seller portal** — Ensure you're logging into the correct portal (buyer or seller)
4. **Registration incomplete** — If your registration was never approved, you won't have active login credentials
5. **Multiple accounts** — You may have registered with a different email; check all email accounts for GeM registration confirmation

## Fix 7: "Too Many OTP Attempts"

If blocked due to excessive OTP requests:

1. **Wait 30 minutes** — The system automatically unblocks after the cooldown period
2. **Don't keep retrying** — Each failed attempt extends the lockout
3. **Try email OTP** — If mobile OTP is blocked, switch to email-based OTP (if available)
4. **Clear browser cookies** — Sometimes clearing cookies and using a fresh browser session helps
5. **Try after 1 hour** — If 30 minutes doesn't work, wait a full hour

## Important Tips

1. **Keep your registered mobile active** — Don't let your SIM expire or port your number without updating GeM
2. **Bookmark the correct URL** — Always access GeM via [gem.gov.in](https://gem.gov.in/); beware of phishing sites
3. **Update contact details proactively** — If you change your mobile or email, update on GeM immediately before you need to log in
4. **Use password manager** — Store your GeM credentials securely; don't rely on memory
5. **Browser matters** — Use the latest Chrome or Firefox; avoid outdated browsers

## How to Update Registered Mobile/Email on GeM

If you can still log in:
1. Login → Go to **Profile** → **Account Settings**
2. Click **Edit** next to mobile number or email
3. Verify via OTP sent to your **current** registered number/email
4. Enter the new number/email and verify again

If you cannot log in:
- Contact GeM helpline: **1800-419-3436**
- Email **helpdesk-gem@gov.in** with your User ID, PAN copy, and new contact details
- Visit your organization's GeM admin for buyer accounts

## FAQs

### Why is GeM OTP taking so long?
GeM OTP delays usually happen during peak hours (10 AM - 1 PM) when server load is high. Wait 2-3 minutes and try again during off-peak hours.

### Can I login to GeM without OTP?
No, OTP-based two-factor authentication is mandatory for GeM login. You cannot skip this step for security reasons.

### I changed my phone number — how do I get GeM OTP?
You need to update your registered mobile on GeM. If you can't log in, contact GeM helpline at 1800-419-3436 with your User ID and identity proof to update your number.

### How long does GeM account reactivation take?
Account reactivation typically takes 5-10 working days after submitting the required documents and contacting GeM support.

### Is there a GeM mobile app for login?
GeM has a mobile app available on Google Play Store, but for critical operations like bidding and payment tracking, the desktop portal is recommended for reliability.

### Can my organization admin reset my GeM password?
For buyer accounts, your organization's GeM nodal officer can help reset your credentials. For seller accounts, you must use the self-service password reset or contact GeM helpline directly.
