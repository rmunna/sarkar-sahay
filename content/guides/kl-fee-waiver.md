# Kerala Fee Concession for Higher Education – Complete Guide

The Government of Kerala provides fee concession (fee waiver) for students pursuing higher education in government and aided colleges. This scheme ensures that financial constraints do not prevent deserving students from accessing quality education. Students from economically weaker sections, SC/ST communities, and other eligible categories can avail partial or full fee concession.

## What is the Fee Concession Scheme?

Kerala's Fee Concession for Higher Education covers tuition fees, special fees, and examination fees for eligible students enrolled in government and government-aided colleges and universities in Kerala. The scheme is administered by the Directorate of Collegiate Education and covers undergraduate, postgraduate, and professional courses.

## Who is Eligible?

To qualify for the fee concession, you must meet the following criteria:

1. **Domicile:** Must be a permanent resident of Kerala
2. **Institution:** Must be enrolled in a government or government-aided college/university in Kerala
3. **Income Limit:** Annual family income should be below ₹1,00,000 (for general category) or as notified by the government
4. **Category-wise eligibility:**
   - **SC/ST students:** Full fee concession irrespective of income (tuition fee exemption)
   - **OBC/OEC students:** Fee concession subject to income criteria
   - **BPL families:** Full fee concession with valid BPL certificate
   - **Differently-abled students:** Fee concession as per government orders
5. **Academic:** Must maintain satisfactory academic progress (no repeated failures in consecutive years)

## Documents Required

### Mandatory Documents
- Filled application form (available at college office or online portal)
- Recent passport-size photographs (2 copies)
- Aadhaar card (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- Income certificate issued by Village Officer / Tahsildar
- Community certificate (for SC/ST/OBC/OEC candidates)
- College admission receipt / bonafide certificate

### Additional Documents
- BPL ration card (if claiming BPL concession)
- Disability certificate from competent medical authority (if applicable)
- Previous year mark sheets / certificates
- Bank account details (for direct benefit transfer, if applicable)
- Nativity certificate

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Online Method (via e-Grantz Portal)

1. **Visit the e-Grantz portal:** Go to [egrantz.kerala.gov.in](https://egrantz.kerala.gov.in/)
2. **Register / Login:** Create an account using your Aadhaar number and mobile number, or log in if already registered
3. **Select scheme:** Choose the appropriate fee concession / scholarship scheme
4. **Fill application:** Enter personal details, educational details, family income, and bank account information
5. **Upload documents:** Scan and upload all required documents (income certificate, community certificate, etc.)
6. **Submit application:** Review all details and submit the application
7. **College verification:** Your college will verify the application and forward it to the Directorate
8. **Track status:** Use the application ID to track status on the portal

### Offline Method (Through College)

1. **Collect form:** Obtain the fee concession application form from your college office
2. **Fill the form:** Complete all fields accurately
3. **Attach documents:** Attach self-attested copies of all required documents
4. **Submit to college:** Submit the form to your college principal / office
5. **College forwards:** The college verifies and forwards applications to the Directorate of Collegiate Education
6. **Approval:** Once approved, the fee concession is applied to your account

| Category | Concession Type | Coverage |

| SC/ST students | Full tuition fee exemption | Tuition + special fees |
| BPL families | Full fee concession | Tuition + exam fees |
| OBC (income < ₹1 lakh) | Partial fee concession | Tuition fees |
| OEC students | Fee concession as per norms | Tuition fees |

## Processing Time

- **Online applications:** 30–60 days from submission
- **Offline applications:** 45–90 days depending on verification
- The fee concession is typically processed at the beginning of each academic year

## Important Tips

1. **Apply early:** Submit your application at the start of the academic year to avoid delays
2. **Keep originals ready:** You may need to show original documents during verification
3. **Renew annually:** Fee concession must be renewed each academic year — don't assume automatic continuation
4. **Check e-Grantz regularly:** Track your application status online to catch any issues early
5. **Contact college office:** If your application is pending for more than 60 days, follow up with your college administration

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Is the fee concession available for self-financing colleges?
No, the fee concession scheme generally applies only to government and government-aided colleges in Kerala. Students in self-financing colleges may check for other scholarship schemes.

### Q2: Can students from other states apply?
No, this scheme is exclusively for permanent residents of Kerala studying in Kerala's government/aided institutions.

### Q3: What if my income certificate is from last year?
Income certificates must be current (issued within the last 6 months). Get a fresh income certificate from your Village Officer or Tahsildar.

### Q4: Is the fee concession available for professional courses like engineering and medicine?
Yes, fee concession is available for professional courses in government colleges. For self-financing professional colleges, separate scholarship schemes may apply.

### Q5: What happens if I fail in exams?
If you fail in consecutive years, your fee concession may be discontinued. You must maintain satisfactory academic progress.

### Q6: Can I avail both fee concession and other scholarships?
You cannot avail fee concession and another government scholarship for the same fee component simultaneously. However, you may receive maintenance allowances from other schemes.

### Q7: How do I check the status of my application?
Log in to the [e-Grantz portal](https://egrantz.kerala.gov.in/) with your credentials and check application status under your dashboard.
