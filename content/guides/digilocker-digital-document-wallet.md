## What is DigiLocker?

**DigiLocker** is a Government of India platform (under Digital India initiative) that provides every Indian citizen a **free, secure digital document wallet** to store and share government-issued documents in digital format.

Documents stored in DigiLocker are issued directly by government agencies and are legally equivalent to originals — accepted by courts, traffic police, banks, and other institutions under the **IT Act 2000**.

Key features:
- **Free account** — 1 GB storage
- Documents directly issued by government agencies (not uploaded by you — pulled from official databases)
- **Legally valid** — accepted everywhere original documents are accepted
- Aadhaar-linked — secure and verified identity
- Share documents digitally via link (no physical copies needed)

## How to Create a DigiLocker Account

### Via App (Recommended)

1. Download **DigiLocker app** from Google Play or App Store
2. Click **"Sign Up"**
3. Enter your **mobile number** → OTP verification
4. Enter your **Aadhaar number** → OTP (to link Aadhaar)
5. Set a **6-digit PIN** for your DigiLocker
6. Your account is ready

### Via Website

1. Go to **[digilocker.gov.in](https://digilocker.gov.in)**
2. Click **"Sign Up"**
3. Follow the same steps — mobile OTP → Aadhaar OTP → set PIN

## How to Download Your Driving Licence / Vehicle RC

1. Open DigiLocker app / website
2. Go to **"Issued Documents"** → **"Search Documents"**
3. Type **"Driving Licence"** or **"Vehicle Registration"**
4. Select the issuing authority (your state — e.g., "Transport Dept, Maharashtra")
5. Enter your **DL number** or **vehicle registration number**
6. Your document appears in DigiLocker — it is now digitally issued and legally valid

## Can I Use DigiLocker Documents for Traffic Police?

Yes — **DigiLocker documents are legally accepted by traffic police** under Rule 139A of the Central Motor Vehicle Rules, as amended in 2020. Show the DigiLocker-stored RC and DL on your phone if asked.

**Note:** Traffic police apps (like mParivahan, DigiLocker) are also accepted for documents.

## How to Share Documents from DigiLocker

1. Open the document in DigiLocker
2. Click **"Share"**
3. Choose:
   - **Share via link** (send a link that expires in a set time)
   - **Download PDF** (with QR code for verification)
   - **Push to email**
4. The recipient can verify the document's authenticity by scanning the QR code

## Documents You Can Upload to DigiLocker (Self-Upload)

Beyond government-issued documents, you can also upload your own scanned documents (caste certificates, property papers, etc.) in DigiLocker for personal storage (not government-verified — marked as "Uploaded by user").

## DigiLocker for Students — CBSE Marksheets

CBSE integrates directly with DigiLocker:
1. Open DigiLocker → "Issued Documents" → "Search" → CBSE
2. Enter your **roll number and year of passing**
3. Class 10/12 marksheets and certificates appear instantly
4. These are digitally signed by CBSE — accepted everywhere

Most **state boards** (Maharashtra, UP, TN, Karnataka, etc.) are also integrated.

## DigiLocker Security

- All documents are fetched directly from government databases — **not stored locally**
- 6-digit PIN + OTP authentication for access
- Aadhaar-linked identity — only you can access your documents
- Share links expire — no permanent access given to others

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is DigiLocker mandatory?
No — it is voluntary. However, it makes life significantly easier for document sharing (job applications, banking, vehicle checks, etc.)

### My state DL is not showing in DigiLocker. Why?
Not all state transport authorities are integrated yet. Check if your state's transport department is listed. Alternatively, download from mParivahan app.

### Can I use DigiLocker documents for passport application?
Yes — DigiLocker documents (Aadhaar, birth certificate, educational certificates) are accepted for Passport Seva Kendra appointments.

### Is there an age restriction for DigiLocker?
Anyone with an Aadhaar card (including minors with parents' assistance) can use DigiLocker.

### What if I lose my phone? Is my data safe?
DigiLocker doesn't store documents on your phone — it fetches them from government databases. As long as your Aadhaar + mobile OTP is secure, your documents are safe. Install DigiLocker on a new phone and log in.

### Can I add a nominee or family member's documents?
DigiLocker is individual — each person has their own account linked to their Aadhaar. You cannot store another person's government-issued documents in your account.
