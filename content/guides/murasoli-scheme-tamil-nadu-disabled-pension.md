## What is Tamil Nadu Disability Pension?

**Tamil Nadu's Disability Pension** (part of the social security scheme under the Social Welfare and Women Empowerment Department) provides a **monthly pension of ₹1,500** to persons with **40% or more disability** who have no regular income. The scheme is supported by both state and central government (IGNDPS — Indira Gandhi National Disability Pension Scheme).

## Who is Eligible?

**You ARE eligible if:**
- You are a **Tamil Nadu resident** (minimum 5 years)
- You have **40% or more disability** (certified by competent government authority — CMO, District Medical Board)
- You are **18 years or older**
- You have **no regular income** and your family is BPL or low income
- You are **not an income tax payer**
- You are **not receiving another government pension**

**Disabilities covered:**
- Physical (locomotor) disability
- Visual impairment
- Hearing impairment
- Intellectual disability
- Mental illness (psychiatric disability)
- Multiple disabilities

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar Card**
2. **Disability certificate** — from CMO / District Medical Board, with percentage stated
3. **UDID card** (if available — apply at [swavlambancard.gov.in](https://swavlambancard.gov.in))
4. **Residence proof** — ration card, voter ID
5. **Income certificate** — from Village Administrative Officer or Revenue Inspector
6. **Bank passbook** — Aadhaar-linked
7. **Photograph**

---

## How to Apply — Step by Step

### Step 1 — Get Disability Certificate

Visit your nearest **Government Hospital** or **District Medical Board**:
- Request a disability assessment
- Certificate is issued with disability type and percentage

### Step 2 — Apply at Block Development Office

Visit the **Block Development Officer (BDO)** or **Tahsildar office** in your area.

Alternatively, for **urban areas**: visit the **Corporation / Municipality Social Welfare Officer**.

### Step 3 — Fill Application

Fill the disability pension application form. Attach:
- Disability certificate (40%+ certified)
- Aadhaar, residence proof, income certificate, bank details

### Step 4 — District Social Welfare Office Verification

The District Social Welfare Officer verifies documents and inclusion in BPL list.

### Step 5 — Pension Approved

After verification, pension of ₹1,500/month starts via DBT.

## Tamil Nadu Welfare Fund for Disabled (TNSCB)

The **Tamil Nadu Corporation for Development of Women (TNCDW)** and other bodies also run additional benefits for disabled persons including:
- Scholarship for disabled students
- Aids and appliances (wheelchairs, crutches, hearing aids) — free from ALIMCO camps
- Travel concession (50% on buses)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. My disability certificate was issued 10 years ago. Is it still valid for pension?**

For permanent disabilities (limb loss, congenital), old certificates are generally accepted. For conditions that may change, a fresh assessment may be needed. Check with the District Medical Board.

**2. I have 40% disability but my family income is ₹1.5 lakh/year. Am I eligible?**

Tamil Nadu disability pension is for BPL/low-income families. ₹1.5 lakh/year may be above the BPL threshold in some categories. Check with the BDO — income criteria can vary.

**3. Can a child with 60% disability get the pension?**

Minimum age is 18. A disabled child cannot get this pension, but may qualify for **scholarships and aids/appliances** through the Social Welfare department.

**4. How do I check if my pension is being credited?**

Check your bank passbook regularly. You can also visit the Social Welfare Office with your Aadhaar to verify.
