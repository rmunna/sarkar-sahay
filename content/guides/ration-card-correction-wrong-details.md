# Ration Card Details Wrong? How to Correct Name, DOB & Other Details

If your ration card has incorrect details — wrong spelling of name, wrong date of birth, incorrect address, or any other error — you must get it corrected to avoid issues at the Fair Price Shop (FPS). Mismatched details between your Aadhaar and ration card can also block biometric authentication under the e-POS system. This guide covers how to fix errors through both online and offline methods.

## What Details Can Be Corrected?

You can apply for correction of the following details on your ration card:

- **Name** (spelling errors, wrong name)
- **Date of birth** (incorrect DOB)
- **Gender**
- **Relationship** with head of family
- **Address** (within the same jurisdiction)
- **Aadhaar number** (linking/correction)
- **Mobile number** (see our [mobile number update guide](/guide/ration-card-mobile-number-update))
- **Photo** update

## Documents Required for Correction

The documents you need depend on the type of correction:

### For Name Correction

| Document | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) | Primary identity proof with correct name |
| Voter ID / PAN card | Supporting identity proof |
| School certificate / marksheet | Name verification |
| Gazette notification (if name change) | Legal name change proof |
| Affidavit on stamp paper | Declaration of correct name |

### For Date of Birth Correction

| Document | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Birth certificate | Primary DOB proof |
| Aadhaar card (with correct DOB) | Supporting proof |
| School leaving certificate | DOB verification |
| Passport | Alternative DOB proof |
| Voter ID | Supporting document |

### For Address Correction

| Document | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Updated Aadhaar card | Address proof |
| Voter ID with new address | Supporting proof |
| Electricity / water bill | Address verification |
| Rent agreement | For rented premises |

## Step-by-Step Correction Process

### Online Process

Most states now allow ration card corrections through their food & civil supplies portal:

1. **Visit your state portal** (see state portal list below)
2. **Login** with your ration card number and registered mobile number
3. Navigate to **"Correction/Modification"** or **"Edit Ration Card"** section
4. Select the **type of correction** — name, DOB, address, etc.
5. Enter the **correct details** in the form
6. **Upload supporting documents** (scanned copies in JPG/PDF, under 200KB)
7. **Submit** the application
8. Note the **application/reference number** for tracking
9. Track status through the same portal under "Track Application"

### Offline Process

1. **Obtain the correction form** from your Tehsil/Taluk office or Food & Supply office
2. **Fill in the form** specifying which details need correction and the correct information
3. **Attach photocopies** of supporting documents (self-attested)
4. **Carry originals** for verification at the counter
5. **Submit** at the Tehsil/Food & Supply office
6. Collect the **acknowledgment receipt** with reference number
7. The office may conduct **field verification** if the correction involves address changes
8. **Collect the corrected card** or check online for the updated digital card

## Processing Time

| Correction Type | Approximate Timeline |
|----------------|---------------------|
| Name correction | 7-15 days |
| DOB correction | 7-15 days |
| Address correction | 15-30 days (may need field verification) |
| Photo update | 7-15 days |
| Aadhaar linking | 3-7 days |

> **Note:** Online corrections are generally processed faster. Offline applications may take longer depending on the state and verification requirements.

## Fees

Ration card corrections are generally **free of cost** in most states. Some states may charge a nominal fee of ₹2-5 for form/processing.

## Important Tips

1. **Match your Aadhaar details first** — ensure your name and DOB on Aadhaar are correct before applying for ration card correction, as e-POS uses Aadhaar for authentication
2. **Keep copies of all documents** you submit — both online uploads and offline submissions
3. **Follow up** at the Food & Supply office if the correction is not reflected within the expected timeline
4. **Check the digital ration card** on the state portal after correction — physical cards may take longer to update
5. For **name changes after marriage**, carry the marriage certificate along with the Gazette notification if applicable

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. How long does ration card name correction take?
Name corrections typically take 7-15 days when applied online. Offline applications may take up to 30 days depending on the state.

### Q2. Can I correct my ration card details online?
Yes, most states offer online correction through their food & civil supplies portals. Visit your state's portal (listed above) to apply.

### Q3. Is there any fee for ration card correction?
In most states, correction is free. Some states charge a nominal fee of ₹2-5.

### Q4. My name on ration card and Aadhaar don't match. Will I face issues?
Yes. Mismatched names can cause biometric authentication failures at FPS. Correct whichever document has the error — update [Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) or ration card.

### Q5. Can I correct someone else's details on my family ration card?
Yes. The head of household can apply for correction of any family member's details on the ration card.

### Q6. What if my ration card correction request is rejected?
Check the reason for rejection on the portal or at the office. Common reasons include insufficient documents or mismatched information. Reapply with the correct documents.

### Q7. Do I need to visit the office for online corrections?
For most corrections (name, DOB), no physical visit is needed after online submission. Address corrections may require field verification.
