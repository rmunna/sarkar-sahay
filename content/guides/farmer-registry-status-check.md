# Farmer Registry Status Check

**Quick answer:** Farmer Registry status check is state-specific. Some states use official `agristack.gov.in` subdomains with options such as Farmer login, CSC login or enrollment status. Use only official state Farmer Registry or agriculture department portals.

Do not enter Aadhaar, OTP or bank details on unofficial "Farmer ID status" websites.

## State Portal Examples

| State | Official portal pattern | What to look for |
|---|---|---|
| Odisha | `odfr.agristack.gov.in` | Farmer login, CSC login, enrollment status |
| Madhya Pradesh | `mpfr.agristack.gov.in` | Farmer Registry login/status options |
| Chhattisgarh | `cgfr.agristack.gov.in` | Farmer Registry login/status options |
| Other states | State agriculture/revenue department or Agri Stack-linked portal | Official announcement or department link |

Availability changes by state. If your state is not listed, check the agriculture department website first.

## How To Check Status Safely

1. Open the official state Farmer Registry portal or agriculture department link.
2. Check whether the domain is a government domain or `agristack.gov.in` subdomain.
3. Choose the status or enrollment option if available.
4. Enter only the details requested on the official page.
5. If the portal routes through CSC, visit an authorised CSC centre.
6. Save the acknowledgement or status screenshot for your records.

## What Status Messages May Mean

| Status / Situation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Enrollment pending | Your application or data mapping may still be under verification |
| Land record mismatch | Name, survey number or ownership data may need correction |
| Farmer not found | Registration may not be completed, or wrong mobile/ID was entered |
| CSC login required | State may require assisted enrollment through CSC |
| Portal not live | State rollout may still be pending |

Status labels can differ by state, so follow the exact portal wording.

## Details You May Need

- Mobile number
- Farmer ID or acknowledgement number, if generated
- Aadhaar-linked mobile for OTP, if official portal asks
- Land record / khata / survey details
- Village, district and state details

Never share OTPs over phone calls. If a CSC operator is helping, confirm you are at an authorised centre.

## If Farmer Registry Status Is Not Found

Try this checklist:

- Confirm the portal is for your state.
- Recheck mobile number and spelling.
- Ask whether registration was completed or only started.
- Check if land records are updated in revenue records.
- Contact local agriculture office, revenue office or CSC.
- Wait if the state has announced phased rollout.

## Scam Warning

Avoid websites or agents claiming:

- "Farmer ID in 10 minutes for a fee"
- "Guaranteed PM-KISAN payment after Farmer ID"
- "Send OTP to complete Agri Stack registration"
- "Bank subsidy unlocked by Farmer Registry payment"

Farmer Registry status does not guarantee scheme approval.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where can I check Farmer ID status?
Use your state Farmer Registry or agriculture department portal. Some official portals are hosted as `statecodefr.agristack.gov.in` subdomains.

### Can I check Farmer Registry status with Aadhaar?
Some official flows may use Aadhaar-linked OTP or mobile verification, but this depends on the state. Do not enter Aadhaar on unofficial sites.

### Is CSC required for Farmer Registry?
Some states may support CSC-assisted registration or login. Check the official state portal.

### Why is my Farmer ID pending?
Common reasons include land record mismatch, incomplete enrollment, mobile mismatch or state-level verification delay.

### Does Farmer Registry status affect PM-KISAN?
It may support future verification in some states, but PM-KISAN has its own official status and eKYC process. Check both separately.
