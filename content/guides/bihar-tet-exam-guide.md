# Bihar TET (BTET/STET) Exam Guide — Eligibility, Pattern & Preparation Tips

The Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB) conducts the Bihar Teacher Eligibility Test (BTET) for primary teachers and the State Teacher Eligibility Test (STET) for secondary teachers. Clearing Bihar TET is mandatory for government teaching positions in Bihar.

Bihar has a massive demand for teachers, making BTET/STET a crucial exam for aspiring educators in the state.

## Eligibility Criteria

### Age Limit

| Category | Minimum Age | Maximum Age |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| All Categories | 18 years | No upper limit |

*There is no upper age limit for Bihar TET.*

### Educational Qualification

**BTET Paper 1 (Classes 1-5):**
- Senior Secondary (12th) with at least 50% marks AND D.El.Ed/BTC
- OR Graduation with B.Ed from NCTE recognized institution

**BTET Paper 2 (Classes 6-8):**
- Graduation with at least 50% marks AND B.Ed

**STET (Classes 9-12):**
- Post-graduation with B.Ed in the relevant subject

## Exam Pattern

### BTET Paper 1 (Primary: Classes 1-5)

| Subject | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| Child Development & Pedagogy | 30 | 30 |
| Language I (Hindi/Urdu/Bangla) | 30 | 30 |
| Language II (English/Arabic) | 30 | 30 |
| Mathematics | 30 | 30 |
| Environmental Studies | 30 | 30 |
| **Total** | **150** | **150** |

### BTET Paper 2 (Upper Primary: Classes 6-8)

| Subject | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| Child Development & Pedagogy | 30 | 30 |
| Language I | 30 | 30 |
| Language II | 30 | 30 |
| Maths & Science OR Social Studies | 60 | 60 |
| **Total** | **150** | **150** |

- **Duration:** 2 hours 30 minutes
- **No negative marking**
- **Qualifying:** General 60%, OBC/BC 55%, SC/ST 55%

## Syllabus Overview

### Child Development & Pedagogy
- Theories of child development (Piaget, Vygotsky), learning theories, inclusive education, assessment, NCF 2005

### Languages
- Comprehension, grammar, pedagogy of language teaching

### Mathematics & EVS
- Number systems, geometry, measurement, environmental concepts, pedagogy

## How to Apply

1. Visit the BSEB website at [bsebonline.com](https://bsebonline.com)
2. Click on BTET/STET notification
3. Register with valid email and phone number
4. Fill in details and select paper
5. Upload photo and signature
6. Pay fee and submit

## Application Fee

| Category | Fee |
|----------|-----|
| General | ₹500 |
| OBC/BC | ₹250 |
| SC/ST | ₹125 |

## Salary & Benefits

After clearing BTET and teacher recruitment:
- **Primary Teacher:** ₹25,000 – ₹40,000 per month (approx.)
- **Upper Primary Teacher:** ₹30,000 – ₹50,000 per month (approx.)
- Government benefits including DA, HRA, pension (NPS)

## Preparation Tips

1. **NCERT books:** Study NCERT for Classes 1-8 thoroughly
2. **Child Development:** Focus on key theories — this section is scoring
3. **Language sections:** Practice comprehension and grammar daily
4. **Previous papers:** Solve past Bihar TET papers for pattern familiarity
5. **Time management:** 150 questions in 150 minutes — practice speed

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between BTET and STET?
BTET is for primary/upper primary teachers (Classes 1-8), while STET is for secondary teachers (Classes 9-12).

### Is there negative marking in Bihar TET?
No, there is no negative marking.

### What is the validity of Bihar TET certificate?
Bihar TET certificate is valid for 7 years from the date of passing.

### Can candidates from other states apply?
Yes, Bihar TET is open to candidates from all states.

### Can I appear for both Paper 1 and Paper 2?
Yes, you can appear for both papers.

### What is the qualifying percentage?
General: 60% (90/150), OBC/BC/SC/ST: 55% (82.5/150).
