# Property Tax Online Payment Failed — How to Fix

Paying property tax online should be simple, but municipal portals across India are notorious for errors — failed transactions, money deducted without receipts, wrong property IDs, and server timeouts. This guide covers **exactly what to do** when your property tax online payment fails, with city-specific steps for BBMP Bangalore, GHMC Hyderabad, BMC Mumbai, MCD Delhi, and Chennai Corporation.

---

## Common Reasons Property Tax Payment Fails

Before troubleshooting, understand why payments fail on municipal portals:

| Reason | What Happens |
|---|---|
| **Server timeout** | Portal crashes mid-payment; bank debits money but portal doesn't confirm |
| **Wrong Property ID / UPIC** | Payment goes to wrong account or gets rejected |
| **Assessment mismatch** | Portal shows different tax amount than expected due to revised rates |
| **Browser incompatibility** | Many civic portals only work properly on Chrome or specific browsers |
| **Payment gateway error** | Bank OTP timeout, session expiry, or gateway downtime |
| **Duplicate payment** | Retrying after timeout causes double deduction |
| **Outdated property records** | Mutation not updated, ownership mismatch blocks payment |

---

## Step 1: Check if Money Was Actually Deducted

**Do this first before anything else.**

1. Log in to your **bank account or UPI app** and check transaction history
2. Look for the exact amount — note the **transaction reference number (UTR/RRN)**
3. Check your **email and SMS** for bank debit alerts
4. If using a credit card, check your **card statement** (may take 24 hours to reflect)

### If money was NOT deducted:
- Simply **retry the payment** after 30 minutes
- Use a different browser (Chrome recommended) or clear cache
- Try during **off-peak hours** (early morning or late night)

### If money WAS deducted but no receipt:
- **Do NOT retry immediately** — this causes double payment
- Wait **48–72 hours** — many portals auto-reconcile failed transactions
- Proceed to Step 2

## Step 2: Payment Deducted but Receipt Not Generated

This is the most common issue. Here's how to resolve it:

### Option A: Check Payment Status on the Portal

Most municipal portals have a payment status or transaction history section:

- **BBMP Bangalore:** Go to bbmptax.karnataka.gov.in → "Payment Status" → Enter Property ID (PID) or SAS Application Number
- **GHMC Hyderabad:** Visit onlinepayment.ghmc.gov.in → "Reprint Receipt" → Enter PTIN number
- **BMC Mumbai:** Go to ptax.mcgm.gov.in → "View Payment History" → Enter Account Number
- **MCD Delhi:** Visit mcdonline.nic.in → "Check Payment Status" → Enter UPIC number
- **Chennai Corp:** Go to chennaicorporation.gov.in → Online Services → Property Tax → "Payment Receipt"

### Option B: File a Grievance with the Portal

If the status shows "pending" or "failed" but money is deducted:

1. Take a **screenshot** of the failed transaction page
2. Get your **bank statement** showing the debit (PDF from net banking)
3. Note your **Property ID, transaction date, amount, and UTR number**
4. File a complaint on the respective portal's grievance section (details in city-specific sections below)

### Option C: Contact Your Bank

If the portal shows no record of your payment:

1. Call your bank's **customer care** and quote the UTR/RRN
2. Ask for a **transaction status check** with the payment gateway
3. If the transaction is in "pending" state, the bank can initiate a **reversal** (takes 5–7 working days)
4. Get a **written confirmation** (email/letter) from the bank for your records

## Step 3: Wrong Property ID or Assessment Mismatch

### Wrong Property ID Entered

If you paid tax using an incorrect Property ID:

1. **Do not panic** — the payment can be transferred or refunded
2. Visit your **local municipal ward office** with:
   - Payment receipt or bank statement showing deduction
   - Correct and incorrect Property IDs
   - ID proof and property ownership documents
3. Submit a written application for **payment transfer** to the correct Property ID
4. Timeline: Usually resolved in **15–30 working days**

### Assessment Amount Mismatch

If the portal shows a different tax amount than you expected:

- **Self-Assessment Scheme (SAS):** Check if your property's zone, usage type, or built-up area was revised
- **ARV-based cities:** Annual Rental Value may have been reassessed
- Check for **retrospective revisions** — some cities revise rates and apply them to previous years
- Visit the **Assessment section** of your ward office to get a breakdown
- You can file an **objection** within 30 days of receiving the revised assessment notice

## City-Specific Troubleshooting

### BBMP Bangalore (Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike)

- **Portal:** [bbmptax.karnataka.gov.in](https://bbmptax.karnataka.gov.in)
- **Property ID format:** PID (e.g., AS-EWS2-A001-0004C) or SAS Application Number
- **Grievance:** Use the "Complaint" section on BBMP portal or visit Sahakarnagar/Head Office
- **Helpline:** 080-22660000 (BBMP Control Room)
- **Common issue:** SAS portal frequently times out during March (deadline rush). Pay before the last week.
- **Penalty:** 2% per month on unpaid tax after due date (typically March 31)

### GHMC Hyderabad (Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation)

- **Portal:** [onlinepayment.ghmc.gov.in](https://onlinepayment.ghmc.gov.in)
- **Property ID format:** PTIN (Property Tax Identification Number)
- **Grievance:** Dial 040-21111111 or visit the Citizen Service Centre at your circle office
- **Receipt reprint:** Available directly on the portal under "Reprint Receipt"
- **Common issue:** PTIN mismatch after property mutation — ensure transfer is updated before paying
- **Penalty:** 2% per month for delayed payment

### BMC Mumbai (Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation)

- **Portal:** [ptax.mcgm.gov.in](https://ptax.mcgm.gov.in)
- **Property ID format:** Account Number or Property Number from assessment bill
- **Grievance:** Email propertytax@mcgm.gov.in or visit the Ward Office Assessment & Collection Department
- **Helpline:** 1800-103-8999 (toll-free)
- **Common issue:** Capital value-based assessment disputes — request a detailed calculation from the ward
- **Penalty:** 2% per month after due date

### MCD Delhi (Municipal Corporation of Delhi)

- **Portal:** [mcdonline.nic.in](https://mcdonline.nic.in)
- **Property ID format:** UPIC (Unique Property Identification Code)
- **Grievance:** File on Delhi government's PGMS portal or visit zonal office
- **Helpline:** 155304 (MCD helpline)
- **Common issue:** UPIC not found — if property is new or recently mutated, apply for UPIC first at the zonal office
- **Penalty:** Late payment penalty varies; typically 1% per month. Check current year's notification.
- **Note:** After MCD unification (2022), use the unified portal for all three former corporations (NDMC properties remain separate)

### Chennai Corporation (Greater Chennai Corporation)

- **Portal:** [chennaicorporation.gov.in](https://chennaicorporation.gov.in/online-civic-services/property-tax-online-payment.html)
- **Property ID format:** Zone-Division-Bill Number
- **Grievance:** Visit the zonal office or use the GCC grievance portal
- **Helpline:** 044-25619206
- **Common issue:** Half-year payment option not reflecting correctly — ensure you select the right half
- **Penalty:** 2% per month for late payment after due date (April 1 for first half, October 1 for second half)

## How to Get a Duplicate Property Tax Receipt

If your payment went through but you lost the receipt or it wasn't generated:

### Online Method
1. Visit your city's property tax portal
2. Look for **"Reprint Receipt"**, **"Payment History"**, or **"Download Receipt"**
3. Enter your Property ID and financial year
4. Download the PDF receipt

### Offline Method
1. Visit your **municipal ward office**
2. Carry: Property ID, payment proof (bank statement/UTR), ID proof
3. Request a **duplicate receipt** — usually issued same day or within 3 working days
4. Some cities charge a nominal fee (₹10–₹50) for duplicate receipts

## How to File a Complaint

If your issue isn't resolved through the portal or helpline:

### Level 1: Municipal Portal Grievance
- Use the **online grievance/complaint section** on your city's portal
- Attach: Bank statement, transaction screenshot, Property ID details
- Note the **complaint/ticket number** for tracking

### Level 2: Ward Office Visit
- Visit your **jurisdictional ward office** in person
- Submit a written complaint with all documents
- Get an **acknowledgment receipt** with date and reference number

### Level 3: Centralized Public Grievance (PG Portal)
- Visit [pgportal.gov.in](https://pgportal.gov.in)
- Register and file a grievance under the relevant municipal body
- Complaints filed here are tracked and must be resolved within **30 days**

### Level 4: Consumer Forum / Ombudsman
- If money is not refunded after repeated complaints, approach:
  - **Banking Ombudsman** (for bank-side issues): [rbi.org.in](https://rbi.org.in)
  - **District Consumer Forum** (for persistent non-resolution)
- Keep all complaint numbers, emails, and acknowledgments as evidence

## Tips to Avoid Property Tax Payment Failures

1. **Pay early** — avoid last-week-of-deadline rush when servers crash
2. **Use Chrome browser** — most municipal portals are optimized for it
3. **Disable pop-up blockers** — some payment gateways open in pop-ups
4. **Keep screenshots** at every step of the payment process
5. **Note your UTR/RRN** immediately after bank debit
6. **Use net banking over UPI** for large amounts — fewer timeout issues on civic portals
7. **Check your Property ID** twice before initiating payment
8. **Save the payment receipt PDF** immediately after generation — don't just close the browser
9. **Pay during off-peak hours** — early morning (6–8 AM) works best
10. **Ensure property mutation is updated** before paying if ownership recently changed

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My payment was deducted twice. What do I do?
Contact your bank immediately with both UTR numbers. Simultaneously file a complaint on the municipal portal. The duplicate amount is typically refunded within 15–30 working days.

### Can I pay property tax for previous years online?
Yes, most portals allow payment of arrears with applicable penalty. Enter your Property ID and the portal will show all outstanding dues.

### The portal says "Property ID not found." What should I do?
Visit your ward office to verify your Property ID. If it's a new property, you may need to apply for assessment first. For recently mutated properties, ensure the transfer is recorded.

### Is the online payment receipt valid for legal purposes?
Yes. The digitally generated receipt from the official municipal portal is legally valid. Keep a printed copy for property transactions.

### Can I get a refund if I paid to the wrong Property ID?
Yes, but only through the ward office. Submit an application with proof of wrong payment and correct Property ID. Refunds or transfers take 15–30 working days.
