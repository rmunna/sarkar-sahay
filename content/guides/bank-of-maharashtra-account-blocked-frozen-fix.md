# Bank of Maharashtra Account Blocked or Frozen? How to Unblock (2026)

Bank of Maharashtra (Mahabank), a public sector bank headquartered in Pune, serves over 2 crore customers with 2,000+ branches. If your Mahabank account is blocked or frozen, here's how to resolve it quickly.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is independent. Contact Bank of Maharashtra directly at 1800-233-4526 for account-specific assistance.

---

## Why Is Your Bank of Maharashtra Account Blocked?

| Reason | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| KYC not updated | Re-KYC via app or branch |
| PAN not linked | Link PAN at branch or net banking |
| Dormant account | Reactivate at branch |
| Suspicious transactions | Call customer care + branch visit |
| Government / court order | Legal process required |

---

## Check the Reason

1. **Check SMS alerts** from Bank of Maharashtra
2. **Call 1800-233-4526** — ask for exact restriction reason
3. **Maha Mobile app** — may show KYC notification

---

## Unblock Bank of Maharashtra Account Online

### Via Maha Mobile App

1. Open **Maha Mobile** (Bank of Maharashtra's official app)
2. Login → **Services → My Account**
3. If KYC update is prompted: Upload Aadhaar + PAN
4. For other issues: **Raise Service Request → Account Activation**

### Via Bank of Maharashtra Net Banking

1. Login at **[mahabank.co.in](https://www.bankofmaharashtra.in)**
2. Navigate to **Profile → KYC Update**
3. Upload required documents or submit request

## Visit Branch for Unblock

1. Visit any Bank of Maharashtra branch (home branch preferred)
2. Carry: Aadhaar (original + copy), PAN, passbook, 2 passport photos
3. Fill: **Account Unfreeze / KYC Update** form
4. Submit documents + get acknowledgment
5. Processing time: **1–2 working days**

---

## KYC Update for Bank of Maharashtra

**Via Maha Mobile App:**
1. Maha Mobile → Profile → KYC Update
2. Upload Aadhaar + PAN clearly
3. Timeline: 1–2 working days

**At Branch:**
- Fill KYC form + submit self-attested documents
- Processed same day

---

## Dormant Account Reactivation

1. Visit home branch
2. Submit: Dormant reactivation form + KYC documents
3. Do one cash deposit transaction
4. Account active: 1 working day

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does Bank of Maharashtra account unblocking take?**
KYC update: 1–2 working days. Dormant reactivation: 1 day. Regulatory holds: 3–7 working days.

**Can I update Bank of Maharashtra KYC online?**
Yes — Maha Mobile app supports digital KYC upload. Net banking also accepts document submissions. Branch visit required for dormant reactivation.

**Bank of Maharashtra UPI is not working but account seems active — why?**
UPI may be separately disabled. Check in Maha Mobile app → UPI → Manage. Also call customer care to check if there's a partial transaction restriction.

**Is Bank of Maharashtra safe? My funds are protected?**
Yes — Bank of Maharashtra is a government of India undertaking and deposits are protected by DICGC up to ₹5 lakh per depositor.
