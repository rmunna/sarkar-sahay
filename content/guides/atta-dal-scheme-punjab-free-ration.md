## What is Punjab Atta Dal Scheme?

**Punjab Atta Dal Scheme** is the Government of Punjab's state ration scheme providing **free wheat flour (atta) and lentils (dal)** to eligible BPL families every month through the Public Distribution System (PDS). This supplements the central **PM Garib Kalyan Ann Yojana (PMGKAY)** which provides rice/wheat.

Punjab's scheme specifically provides **processed atta (flour)** instead of raw wheat — more practical for direct consumption.

## Monthly Entitlement

| Commodity | Quantity Per Person |
|---|---|
| Wheat flour (Atta) | **5 kg/person/month** |
| Lentils (Dal) | **1 kg/person/month** |

A family of 4 would receive 20 kg atta + 4 kg dal every month — completely free.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Punjab resident** with a valid **BPL ration card**
- Priority Household (PHH) category under NFSA
- Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) card holders
- **Women-headed households** get additional preference

The ration card is issued by the **Punjab Food and Civil Supplies Department**.

## How to Collect Your Ration

1. Visit your designated **Fair Price Shop (FPS)** — the one listed on your ration card
2. Use the **EPOS (Electronic Point of Sale)** machine at the shop
3. Authentication:
   - **Aadhaar fingerprint/OTP** on the EPOS machine
   - Or family member (if the card holder is unable)
4. Collect atta and dal as per your family entitlement
5. Get a receipt from the FPS dealer

**Monthly frequency:** Ration is distributed each month — ideally in the first two weeks.

## How to Get a BPL Ration Card in Punjab

If you don't have a ration card:
1. Go to your nearest **Tehsildar office** or **DSO (District Supply Officer)** office
2. Apply for a new ration card with documents
3. OR apply online at [epos.punjab.gov.in](https://epos.punjab.gov.in) if the portal accepts new applications

For existing ration card holders, modifications (adding/removing family members) can be done at the DSO office.

## Checking Your Ration Status

1. Go to **[epos.punjab.gov.in](https://epos.punjab.gov.in)**
2. Enter your ration card number or Aadhaar
3. View monthly distribution status — whether ration collected or pending

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My FPS dealer says atta is not available this month. What do I do?
Non-availability at FPS is a distribution lapse. Contact the District Supply Officer (DSO) or call the **Punjab Food helpline: 1967**. You are entitled to claim the missed ration in the next month.

### Can I get ration from a different FPS (not my designated one)?
Typically no — each family is assigned to a specific FPS. However, Punjab has implemented **portability** in some districts, allowing collection from any FPS. Check with your DSO.

### My Aadhaar is not seeded to the ration card. Can I still collect?
Aadhaar seeding is mandatory for EPOS-based collection. Visit your DSO office to get Aadhaar linked to your ration card. Until linked, you may face difficulty in biometric authentication.

### We are a family of 6. How much atta and dal do we get?
6 persons × 5 kg atta = **30 kg atta** and 6 × 1 kg dal = **6 kg dal** per month, all free.

### Is the atta pre-packaged or loose?
The atta is usually provided in **5 kg or 10 kg pre-packaged bags** at the FPS. Quality is checked by Punjab Food Corporation.
