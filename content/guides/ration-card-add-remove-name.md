# Ration Card — How to Add or Remove Family Members

Your [ration card](/guide/ration-card-apply-online) should always reflect the current members of your household. Whether a new baby is born, someone gets married, or a family member passes away, you need to update your ration card accordingly. This guide explains the complete process to add or remove names from your ration card — both online and offline.

## When Should You Update Your Ration Card?

You should add or remove names in these situations:

**Add a name when:**
- A baby is born in the family
- Marriage — spouse joins the household
- An elderly parent or dependent moves in
- Adoption of a child

**Remove a name when:**
- A family member passes away
- Daughter or son gets married and moves to another household
- A family member gets their own separate ration card
- A member permanently migrates and joins another ration card
- Divorce or separation

## Documents Required to Add a Name

1. Existing ration card (original and photocopy)
2. [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) of the person being added
3. **For newborn:** Birth certificate
4. **For marriage:** Marriage certificate
5. Address proof showing same household
6. Passport-size photograph of the new member
7. Application form (available at the office or online)
8. Self-declaration by the head of the family

## Documents Required to Remove a Name

1. Existing ration card (original and photocopy)
2. **For death:** Death certificate
3. **For marriage (member leaving):** Marriage certificate + new household's ration card copy
4. **For separation:** Written application explaining the reason
5. Self-declaration by the head of the family
6. Aadhaar of the person being removed (if available)

## Online Process (Step-by-Step)

Most states now offer online ration card modification. Here's the general process:

### Step 1: Visit State Portal

Go to your state's Food & Civil Supplies department website. Some popular portals:
- UP: fcs.up.gov.in
- Bihar: epds.bihar.gov.in
- Maharashtra: mahafood.gov.in
- Tamil Nadu: tnpds.gov.in
- Rajasthan: food.rajasthan.gov.in

### Step 2: Login or Register

- If you already have an account, log in with your credentials
- If not, register using your ration card number and mobile number

### Step 3: Select Modification Type

Look for options like:
- "Add Member" / "New Member Addition"
- "Delete Member" / "Remove Member"
- "Ration Card Modification" or "Correction"

### Step 4: Fill in Details

- Enter the new member's name, date of birth, gender, Aadhaar number, and relationship with head of family
- For removal, select the member's name from the list and provide the reason

### Step 5: Upload Documents

Upload scanned copies of required documents (birth certificate, death certificate, marriage certificate, Aadhaar, etc.)

### Step 6: Submit and Track

- Submit the application
- Note down the application/reference number
- Track status online using this number

## Offline Process

### Step 1: Get the Form

Visit your nearest:
- Tehsil office
- Block Development Office
- Food & Civil Supplies Department office
- Common Service Centre (CSC)

Collect the ration card modification form.

### Step 2: Fill and Attach Documents

Fill the form with required details and attach photocopies of supporting documents.

### Step 3: Submit

Submit the form along with your original ration card for endorsement. Get an acknowledgement receipt.

### Step 4: Verification

A food inspector or designated officer may visit your home for verification, especially for name additions.

### Step 5: Updated Card

After verification and approval, your ration card will be updated with the changes. This typically takes **15–30 working days**.

## Important Points to Remember

- **Aadhaar linking is mandatory** — the person being added must have an [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online). For newborns, the Aadhaar enrolment can be done later, but should be linked within a few months.
- **One person, one ration card** — a person's name cannot appear on two ration cards simultaneously. If you're adding someone, ensure their name is removed from their previous card first.
- **Update within 30 days** — for births, deaths, or marriages, update the ration card within 30 days to avoid complications.
- If you've recently moved states, look into the [ration card transfer process](/guide/ration-card-transfer-another-state) or use the [ONORC scheme](/guide/one-nation-one-ration-card-onorc) for portability.

## Fees

- Name addition or removal is generally **free of cost** in most states
- Some states may charge a nominal fee of ₹5–₹25
- CSC centres may charge a small service fee for online applications

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. How long does it take to add a name to a ration card?

It typically takes 15 to 30 working days after submitting the application and completing verification. Online applications are usually faster.

### 2. Can I add my spouse's name after marriage?

Yes. Submit a marriage certificate along with your spouse's Aadhaar card and a photograph. If your spouse had a separate ration card, their name must first be removed from that card.

### 3. What if a family member has passed away but their name is still on the ration card?

Submit a death certificate to your local Food & Civil Supplies office along with your ration card. The name will be removed after processing. Continue to do this promptly to avoid legal issues.

### 4. Can I add a newborn baby without an Aadhaar card?

Yes, a newborn can be added using the birth certificate. However, you should enrol the baby for Aadhaar at the earliest and link it to the ration card for biometric-based PDS distribution.

### 5. Is there any fee for adding or removing names?

In most states, it is free. Some states or Common Service Centres may charge a nominal fee of ₹5 to ₹25.

### 6. Can I do this process through a Common Service Centre (CSC)?

Yes, CSCs provide ration card services including name addition and deletion. They may charge a small service fee for processing.

### 7. What happens if I don't remove a deceased member's name?

You may continue to receive extra rations, which is considered fraud. If caught, your ration card could be cancelled and legal action may be taken.
