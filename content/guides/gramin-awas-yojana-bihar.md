# Gramin Awas Yojana Bihar (PMAY-G): Apply Online for Rural Housing Scheme

Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana – Gramin (PMAY-G), also known as Gramin Awas Yojana, provides financial assistance to rural families in Bihar for building pucca houses. Under this scheme, beneficiaries in plain areas receive **₹1.20 lakh** and those in hilly/difficult areas receive **₹1.30 lakh** for house construction. Additionally, 90/95 person-days of unskilled labour are provided under MGNREGA.

## What is Gramin Awas Yojana?

PMAY-G is a centrally sponsored scheme with cost sharing between the central and state government (60:40 ratio in plain areas). It aims to provide "Housing for All" by constructing pucca houses with basic amenities for rural families who are either houseless or living in kutcha/dilapidated houses. Bihar is one of the largest beneficiary states under this scheme.

### Financial Assistance

| Component | Plain Areas | Hilly Areas |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| House construction | ₹1,20,000 | ₹1,30,000 |
| Toilet (under SBM) | ₹12,000 | ₹12,000 |
| MGNREGA wages | 90 person-days | 95 person-days |

The total assistance including MGNREGA wages and toilet can amount to approximately **₹1.50-1.60 lakh**.

## Who is Eligible?

Beneficiaries are selected from the **Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC) 2011** data. The selection criteria include:

- **Houseless families** — those without any house
- **Families living in kutcha houses** — houses with kutcha walls and roof
- **Families with dilapidated houses** — houses in poor structural condition
- Must be a **rural resident** (rural areas of Bihar)
- Must not have a **pucca house** in any family member's name
- Must not be a **government employee** or income tax payer
- Must not own a **motorised vehicle** (four-wheeler)

### Priority Groups
- SC/ST households
- Minorities
- Families headed by women
- Disabled persons
- Ex-servicemen

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** of all family members
- **SECC data** — name should appear in the SECC 2011 list
- **Bank account passbook** linked with Aadhaar
- **Photograph** of the applicant
- **Land documents** (if available) — even a small piece of land is sufficient
- **MGNREGA job card** (if available)
- **BPL card / ration card**

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### How Beneficiaries Are Selected

Unlike many schemes, PMAY-G beneficiaries are **not selected through individual applications**. The process is:

1. **SECC 2011 Data:** Government identifies eligible families from the SECC census data
2. **Gram Sabha Verification:** The list is verified and prioritised in the Gram Sabha (village meeting)
3. **Permanent Wait List:** Approved names are placed on the Permanent Wait List (PWL)
4. **Annual Selection:** Each year, beneficiaries are selected from the PWL based on priority
5. **Sanction Order:** Selected beneficiaries receive a sanction order from the Block Development Officer

### After Selection

1. Beneficiary receives **sanction letter** with details of assistance
2. **First instalment** is released for foundation and plinth work
3. Use the **AwasSoft app** to upload geotagged photos at each construction stage
4. **Second instalment** is released after verification of construction progress
5. **Third instalment** is released upon roof completion
6. **Final inspection** is done by the concerned authority

### How to Check Your Name in the List

1. Visit **[pmayg.nic.in](https://pmayg.nic.in/)**
2. Click on **"Stakeholders"** → **"IAY/PMAYG Beneficiary"**
3. Search by your **Registration Number** or go to **"Advanced Search"**
4. Select **State: Bihar**, then District, Block, and Panchayat
5. Check if your name appears in the beneficiary list

### How to Register a Grievance

If you believe you are eligible but your name is not in the list:

1. Visit **[pmayg.nic.in](https://pmayg.nic.in/)** and click on **"Grievance"**
2. Or approach the **Block Development Officer (BDO)**
3. Or contact the **District Magistrate's office**

## Fees

There are **no fees** for PMAY-G. The scheme is fully government-funded.

## Processing Time

- Selection from PWL: Depends on government allocation each year
- First instalment: Within **1 month** of sanction
- House completion deadline: **12 months** from sanction date
- Total construction with all instalments: **12-18 months**

## Important Tips

1. **Link Aadhaar to bank account** — all payments are through DBT via Aadhaar-linked accounts
2. **Download AwasSoft app** from Play Store — you'll need it to upload construction progress photos
3. **Complete construction within 12 months** — delays may lead to cancellation
4. **Don't pay middlemen** — the scheme is free. Report corruption to the District Magistrate
5. **Attend Gram Sabha meetings** — this is where beneficiary lists are verified and approved

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Can I apply directly for PMAY-G?
No, beneficiaries are selected from the SECC 2011 data verified by the Gram Sabha. You cannot submit an individual application. If eligible, your name should appear in the Permanent Wait List.

### Q2: How much money do I get in total?
In Bihar's plain areas, you receive ₹1,20,000 for house construction plus ₹12,000 for toilet (under Swachh Bharat Mission) and 90 days of MGNREGA wages.

### Q3: Can I use the money for renovation?
No, PMAY-G is for **new house construction only**, not for renovation or repair of existing houses.

### Q4: What is the minimum house size?
The minimum house size under PMAY-G is **25 square metres** (approximately 269 sq ft) with a dedicated cooking space.

### Q5: What if my name is in SECC but not in the PMAY-G list?
Raise the issue in the **Gram Sabha** meeting. You can also file a grievance on [pmayg.nic.in](https://pmayg.nic.in/) or approach the BDO.

### Q6: Is land ownership mandatory?
You need a piece of land to build the house. If you don't own land, the government may provide a homestead plot under the scheme.

### Q7: Can urban residents apply?
No, PMAY-G is only for **rural areas**. For urban housing, see the Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana – Urban (PMAY-U).
