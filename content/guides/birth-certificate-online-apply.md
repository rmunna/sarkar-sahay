## What Is a Birth Certificate?

A birth certificate is an **official government document** that records a person's birth. It's issued by the local municipal body or gram panchayat and is essential for:

- School/college admission
- Passport application
- Aadhaar enrolment
- Government scheme benefits
- Age proof for competitive exams
- Inheritance and legal matters

In India, birth registration is **mandatory** under the Registration of Births and Deaths Act, 1969. The **CRSORGI (Civil Registration System)** portal allows online registration in many states.

## Who Can Apply?

- **Parents** of the child (for newborns)
- **Head of the household** or family member
- **Head of the institution** (hospital/nursing home) where the birth occurred
- **The individual** (for adults applying for delayed registration)

## Documents Required

### For Newborn (Within 21 Days of Birth)

- **Hospital discharge certificate** or birth report from the hospital
- **Aadhaar card** of both parents
- **Marriage certificate** of parents (recommended, not always mandatory)
- **Address proof** of parents
- **Mobile number** linked to Aadhaar

### For Delayed Registration (After 21 Days)

| Delay Period | Additional Requirements |
|-------------|----------------------|
| 21 days to 30 days | Declaration by the parent + late fee |
| 30 days to 1 year | Magistrate/SDM order may be required |
| Beyond 1 year | Court order + affidavit + newspaper publication (in some states) |

### For Adult Birth Certificate (If Never Registered)

- **Affidavit** on stamp paper declaring birth details
- **School records** (Class 1 admission register, transfer certificate)
- **Aadhaar card** or any ID proof
- **Court order** from First Class Magistrate (in most states)
- **Newspaper publication** of the birth registration application

## Online Application Process via CRSORGI

### Step 1: Visit CRSORGI Portal

1. Go to [crsorgi.gov.in](https://crsorgi.gov.in)
2. Click **"Register"** to create an account (if new user)
3. Enter your name, mobile number, email, and create a password
4. Verify with OTP

### Step 2: Log In and Start Application

1. Log in with your credentials
2. Click **"Birth Registration"** or **"Apply for Birth Certificate"**
3. Select your **state, district, and registration unit** (municipal body/panchayat)

### Step 3: Fill Birth Details

1. **Child's name** (can be added later if not yet decided)
2. **Date of birth** and **time of birth**
3. **Place of birth** — hospital name and address, or home address
4. **Gender** of the child
5. **Father's details** — name, Aadhaar, education, occupation
6. **Mother's details** — name, Aadhaar, education, occupation, mother's age at birth
7. **Permanent address** and **present address** of parents
8. **Informant details** — person reporting the birth

### Step 4: Upload Documents

1. Upload **hospital birth report/discharge summary** (PDF/JPEG, max 2 MB)
2. Upload **Aadhaar of parents** (PDF/JPEG)
3. Upload **address proof** if different from Aadhaar address
4. For delayed registration: upload **affidavit and court order**

### Step 5: Submit and Pay

- **Fee:** Varies by state — typically ₹0-50 for timely registration, ₹50-500 for delayed
- Pay online via debit card, net banking, or UPI (where available)
- Download the **acknowledgement receipt**

### Step 6: Verification and Certificate Issue

1. Your application is reviewed by the **local registrar**
2. For timely registrations: processed in **7-15 days**
3. For delayed registrations: may take **30-60 days** (depends on required approvals)
4. Once approved, download the **birth certificate** from the portal
5. You may also collect the physical copy from the registration office

## Offline Application Process

1. Visit your local **Municipal Corporation, Municipality, or Gram Panchayat** office
2. Collect the **birth registration form**
3. Fill in all details and attach required documents
4. Submit to the **Birth & Death Registrar**
5. Pay the fee (₹0-50 for timely, more for delayed)
6. Collect the birth certificate after **7-21 days**

## Delayed Birth Registration — Special Process

If the birth was **not registered within 21 days**:

### Within 30 Days
- Apply normally with a **late fee** (₹5-50 depending on state)
- Declaration by the informant is sufficient

### 30 Days to 1 Year
- Requires **written permission from the Registrar** or **SDM/Magistrate**
- Submit an affidavit explaining the delay
- May require verification by health authorities

### After 1 Year
- Requires **court order** from First Class Magistrate
- Steps:
  1. Publish a notice in a local newspaper
  2. File an application in the Magistrate's court
  3. Submit supporting evidence (school records, hospital records, affidavit)
  4. Court issues an order directing the Registrar to register the birth
  5. Take the court order to the Registrar's office
  6. Birth is registered and certificate is issued

## Important Tips

1. **Register the birth within 21 days** to avoid delays and extra paperwork
2. **Hospital births are easier** — the hospital usually initiates registration
3. **Keep the hospital discharge summary safe** — it's the primary proof
4. **Name can be added later** — you can register the birth first and add the child's name within 1 year
5. **Check your state's specific portal** — some states don't use CRSORGI

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. How many days do I have to register a birth?

Birth must be registered within **21 days** for free/simple registration. After 21 days, you'll need additional documentation and approvals.

### Q2. Can I apply for birth certificate online?

Yes, through the [CRSORGI portal](https://crsorgi.gov.in) or your state's e-District portal. Not all states have online registration — check your state portal.

### Q3. How much does a birth certificate cost?

₹0-50 for timely registration. Delayed registration may cost ₹50-500+ depending on the delay period and state.

### Q4. How long does it take to get a birth certificate?

7-15 days for timely registration, 30-60 days for delayed registration, and potentially months if a court order is required.

### Q5. Can I get a birth certificate without hospital records?

Yes, but you'll need alternative proof — an affidavit, school admission records, or a court order for delayed registration.

### Q6. Is birth certificate mandatory for school admission?

Yes, most schools require a birth certificate for admission. It serves as the official age proof.

### Q7. Can I apply for someone else's birth certificate?

Yes, parents or legal guardians can apply for a child's birth certificate. For adults, the individual or a legal representative can apply.

### Q8. How do I get a duplicate birth certificate if the original is lost?

Apply for a duplicate/copy at the same registration office where the birth was originally registered. You'll need to provide the registration number or approximate date and place of birth.
