## What is Karnataka Vidyasiri Scholarship?

The Vidyasiri Scholarship is a financial assistance programme by the **Karnataka Backward Classes Welfare Department** for students from backward classes pursuing higher education. The scheme provides **hostel fees, food allowance, and monthly stipends** to students from OBC, SC, and ST categories who are studying away from home.

The goal is to ensure that financial constraints do not prevent meritorious students from disadvantaged backgrounds from accessing quality higher education. The scholarship covers students from **PUC (Pre-University) to postgraduate and professional courses**.

## Who is Eligible?

### Student Eligibility
- Must belong to **Category-I, Category-II A, II B, III A, or III B** of Karnataka backward classes, or **SC/ST** category
- Must be a **resident of Karnataka**
- Must be enrolled in a **recognised college or university** in Karnataka
- Must be studying in **PUC, degree, postgraduate, or professional courses** (engineering, medical, law, etc.)
- Must be a **day scholar or hosteller** (different rates apply)
- **No minimum percentage** required for initial application (renewal may require passing marks)

### Income Criteria
- **Annual family income** must not exceed:
  - ₹2,50,000 for Category-I
  - ₹1,50,000 for Category-II A, II B, III A, III B
  - Income criteria may vary for SC/ST students under separate department schemes
- Income certificate must be issued by the **Tahsildar**

### Who is NOT Eligible?
- Students already receiving scholarships from other state or central schemes (no double benefit)
- Students in private unaided institutions (some exceptions for professional courses)
- Students who have failed in the previous academic year (for renewal)
- Students employed full-time

## Documents Required

- **Caste/Category certificate** issued by the Tahsildar
- **Income certificate** (annual family income) from the Tahsildar
- **Aadhaar card** of the student
- **College admission receipt** or bonafide certificate
- **Previous year marksheet** (for renewal applications)
- **Bank account details** (in student's name, linked to Aadhaar)
- **Passport-size photographs**
- **Ration card** (for address and family proof)
- **SSLC marksheet** (for PUC students)

## Scholarship Amounts

| Course Level | Hostellers (per month) | Day Scholars (per month) |
|-------------|----------------------|------------------------|
| PUC (I & II) | ₹1,500 | ₹750 |
| Degree (BA, BSc, BCom) | ₹2,000 | ₹1,000 |
| PG (MA, MSc, MCom) | ₹2,500 | ₹1,250 |
| Professional (Engineering, Medical) | ₹3,000 | ₹1,500 |
| Research (PhD) | ₹3,500 | ₹1,750 |

*Note: Amounts are approximate and may be revised by the department. Additionally, hostel fee reimbursement and book allowance may be provided separately.*

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Online Application

1. Visit the **Backward Classes Welfare Department portal** at [sw.kar.nic.in](https://sw.kar.nic.in)
2. Click on **"Vidyasiri Scholarship"** or search on **Seva Sindhu** portal
3. **Register** using your mobile number and email
4. Fill in personal details — name, Aadhaar number, caste category, parent details
5. Enter **educational details** — college name, course, year of study
6. Enter **bank account details** (must be in the student's name with IFSC code)
7. **Upload documents** — caste certificate, income certificate, Aadhaar, admission receipt, marksheet
8. **Review and submit** the application
9. Note the **application ID** for tracking
10. The college will **verify your application** through the institution login
11. After college and department verification, scholarship amount will be **credited to your bank account**

### Offline Application

1. Visit the **District Backward Classes Welfare Office** in your district
2. Collect the Vidyasiri scholarship application form
3. Get the form **attested by your college principal**
4. Attach photocopies of all required documents
5. Submit the form to the welfare office
6. Track your application using the acknowledgement receipt

## Processing Time

- **Online application processing** — 30 to 60 days after college verification
- **First disbursement** — Usually within 2–3 months of the academic year starting
- **Subsequent disbursements** — Quarterly or semester-wise
- **Renewal** — Must apply fresh each academic year

## Important Tips

1. **Apply early** — The application window usually opens in July–August. Don't wait until the deadline.
2. **Get your caste and income certificates in advance** — These can take 15–30 days from the Tahsildar office. See our [income certificate guide](/guide/income-certificate-online-apply) if available.
3. **Ensure Aadhaar is linked to bank account** — DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer) requires Aadhaar-linked bank accounts. Check our [Aadhaar linking guide](/guide/aadhaar-linking-guide) for help.
4. **No double benefits** — If you're receiving any other scholarship, you cannot claim Vidyasiri. Choose the one with higher value.
5. **Keep passing grades** — Renewal requires passing marks in previous year exams. Focus on academics.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Can I apply for Vidyasiri if I'm studying outside Karnataka?
No, the scholarship is only for students studying in **recognised institutions within Karnataka**. For studies outside Karnataka, check central government scholarships.

### Q2: What if my income certificate shows income above the limit?
Your application will be rejected. Ensure the income certificate accurately reflects your family's annual income. If your family income has decreased, get a fresh income certificate.

### Q3: Can I get Vidyasiri along with a central government scholarship?
No. The government does not allow **double benefit** from multiple scholarship schemes. You must choose one. If you're receiving a Post Matric Scholarship from the central government, you cannot also claim Vidyasiri.

### Q4: When is the application deadline?
The deadline varies each year, typically between **September and November**. Check the department website or your college notice board for exact dates. Late applications are usually not accepted.

### Q5: How do I track my Vidyasiri scholarship status?
Visit [sw.kar.nic.in](https://sw.kar.nic.in), log in with your application ID, and check the status. You can also enquire at your college's scholarship cell.

### Q6: Is Vidyasiri available for diploma courses?
Yes, students pursuing **diploma courses** from government or government-aided polytechnics are also eligible, subject to the same income and category criteria.

### Q7: What if the scholarship amount is not credited?
Check if your bank account is properly linked to Aadhaar. Visit the District Backward Classes Welfare Office with your application ID for follow-up. You can also raise a grievance on the Seva Sindhu portal.
