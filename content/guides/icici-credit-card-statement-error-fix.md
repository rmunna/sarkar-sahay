# ICICI Credit Card Wrong Charge? Dispute & Fix Transaction

Spotted an unfamiliar charge on your ICICI credit card statement? Whether it's a duplicate billing, an unrecognized merchant transaction, or a refund that never arrived — this guide walks you through identifying the issue and raising a dispute with ICICI Bank.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent informational resource and is not affiliated with ICICI Bank. Always verify details on [ICICI Bank's official website](https://www.icicibank.com/).

---

## Types of Credit Card Statement Errors

| Error Type | Description |
|---|---|
| Unrecognized charge | Transaction you don't recall making |
| Duplicate billing | Same amount charged twice for one purchase |
| Wrong amount | Charged more than the actual purchase amount |
| Refund not received | Merchant confirmed refund but it's not in your statement |
| Subscription charge | Recurring charge from a cancelled subscription |
| Foreign currency error | Incorrect conversion rate or unexpected forex charges |

---

## Step 1: Verify the Transaction First

Before raising a dispute, check these common explanations:

1. **Check the merchant name** — Merchants often bill under a different company name. For example, "GOOGLE *SERVICE" could be a Google Play purchase. Search the merchant name online.
2. **Check with family members** — If your card has add-on/supplementary cards, a family member may have made the transaction.
3. **Check pending subscriptions** — Services like Netflix, Spotify, Amazon Prime, or app subscriptions auto-renew. Check your active subscriptions.
4. **Check the date** — Sometimes a transaction takes 2-3 days to appear on the statement, making it seem unfamiliar.
5. **Review SMS alerts** — Check your SMS history for transaction alerts matching the amount and date.

## Step 2: Raise a Dispute with ICICI Bank

If the charge is genuinely wrong, raise a dispute through any of these channels:

### Method 1: Via iMobile Pay App (Fastest)

1. Open [ICICI iMobile Pay](/guide/icici-imobile-app-not-working-fix).
2. Go to **Credit Cards** → Select your card.
3. Tap **"View Statement"** or **"Unbilled Transactions."**
4. Find the disputed transaction and tap on it.
5. Select **"Raise a Dispute"** or **"Report an Issue."**
6. Choose the reason (unrecognized, duplicate, wrong amount, etc.).
7. Add details and submit. You'll receive a reference number.

### Method 2: Via Net Banking

1. Log in to [ICICI Net Banking](https://infinity.icicibank.com/).
2. Go to **Card Services** → **Credit Card** → **Statement.**
3. Select the transaction and click **"Dispute Transaction."**
4. Fill in the dispute form with the reason and details.
5. Submit and note the reference number.

### Method 3: Via Customer Care

1. Call **1800 1080** (toll-free) or **022-33667777**.
2. Select the credit card option from the IVR menu.
3. Explain the disputed transaction to the executive with the date, amount, and merchant name.
4. The executive will raise a dispute and provide a reference number.

### Method 4: Via Email

1. Send an email to **customer.care@icicibank.com**.
2. Subject: "Credit Card Dispute — [Last 4 digits of card] — [Transaction Date]"
3. Include: Your name, registered mobile number, last 4 digits of card, transaction date, amount, merchant name, and reason for dispute.
4. **Never share your full card number, CVV, or OTP in the email.**

## Step 3: What Happens After You Raise a Dispute

1. **Acknowledgment** — ICICI Bank acknowledges your dispute within 24-48 hours.
2. **Temporary credit** — For eligible disputes, the bank may issue a temporary/provisional credit to your card while investigating.
3. **Investigation** — The bank investigates with the merchant's acquiring bank. This typically takes **30-90 days** depending on the case complexity.
4. **Resolution** — You'll be notified of the outcome:
   - **In your favour:** The disputed amount is permanently reversed.
   - **Against you:** The temporary credit (if given) is reversed, and you must pay the amount. You can escalate further.

## Chargeback Process

A **chargeback** is a formal reversal of a credit card transaction, governed by Visa/Mastercard rules. ICICI Bank initiates a chargeback on your behalf when you raise a dispute.

**Key points about chargebacks:**

- **Time limit:** You must raise a dispute within **90 days** of the transaction date (60 days for some card types). Don't delay.
- **Documentation:** Keep receipts, cancellation confirmations, refund emails, or any proof to strengthen your case.
- **Multiple chargebacks:** If you have several disputed transactions, raise each one separately.
- **International transactions:** Forex markup (up to 3.5%) may not be reversed even if the base amount is refunded.

## Duplicate Billing — Specific Fix

If charged twice for the same purchase:

1. **Check if both entries are settled** — Sometimes a "pending" charge drops off automatically in 3-5 working days.
2. **Contact the merchant first** — Many merchants can reverse duplicate charges faster than the bank dispute process.
3. **If merchant doesn't help** — Raise a dispute with ICICI Bank (see Step 2) and select "Duplicate Transaction" as the reason. Provide the merchant receipt showing single payment.

---

## Refund Not Received — Specific Fix

If a merchant confirmed a refund but it's not in your statement:

1. **Check the timeline** — Credit card refunds take **5-10 working days** from the merchant's processing date.
2. **Get the ARN** — Ask the merchant for the **Acquirer Reference Number (ARN)** of the refund. This is the tracking number.
3. **Share ARN with ICICI** — Call **1800 1080** and share the ARN. The bank can trace the refund.
4. **Check unbilled section** — Refunds sometimes appear in the "Unbilled Transactions" section, not the current statement.
5. **If 10+ working days passed** — Raise a formal dispute with ICICI Bank.

## Important Tips

1. Always check your credit card statement within the first week of the billing cycle to catch errors early.
2. Set up **SMS and email alerts** for all transactions so you're notified in real time.
3. Keep a record of all online purchases, cancellation confirmations, and refund emails.
4. If you suspect card fraud, **block your card immediately** and request a replacement.
5. RBI mandates that for unauthorized transactions reported within 3 working days, your liability is zero (subject to bank's investigation).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. How long does ICICI take to resolve a credit card dispute?
Most disputes are resolved within 30-90 days. Simple duplicate charges may be resolved in 7-10 days. Complex international disputes can take up to 120 days.

### Q2. Will I have to pay the disputed amount during investigation?
You should pay the undisputed portion of your bill to avoid late fees and interest. For the disputed amount, ICICI may issue a temporary credit. If not, you may still be charged interest — this is reversed if the dispute is resolved in your favour.

### Q3. Can I dispute a charge older than 90 days?
It's very difficult. Card network rules (Visa/Mastercard) generally have a 90-120 day window for chargebacks. Beyond that, the bank may not be able to process the dispute.

### Q4. What if the dispute is resolved against me?
You can escalate to ICICI's Grievance Redressal team. If still unresolved, file a complaint with the [RBI Banking Ombudsman](https://cms.rbi.org.in/).

### Q5. Is there a charge for raising a dispute?
No. ICICI Bank does not charge any fee for raising a transaction dispute.

### Q6. Can I dispute a transaction made on my add-on card?
Yes. The primary cardholder can raise disputes for add-on card transactions through the same process.

### Q7. How do I block my ICICI credit card if I see fraud?
Call **1800 1080** immediately or open iMobile Pay → Credit Cards → Block Card. You can also SMS **BLOCK** to **9215676766** from your registered mobile number.
