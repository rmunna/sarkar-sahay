# UAN Activation & EPF Login Guide 2026 — EPFO Unified Portal

**UAN (Universal Account Number)** is your unique 12-digit number for EPF. It stays constant across employers — even when you change jobs, your PF accounts are linked to the same UAN. Activating UAN is the first step to manage your EPF online.

---

## How to Get Your UAN

**Your UAN is provided by your employer.** Sources:

| Source | How to Find |
|--------|------------|
| **Salary slip** | UAN usually printed on monthly payslip |
| **Employer HR** | Ask HR department for your UAN |
| **EPF passbook** | If you have physical PF passbook |
| **EPFO portal** | Know your UAN via mobile number |

### Find UAN via Mobile Number:
1. Go to **[unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in)**
2. Click **"Know Your UAN"**
3. Enter registered mobile number + OTP
4. Enter PAN or Aadhaar + DOB
5. **UAN displayed**

---

## Step-by-Step: UAN Activation

1. Go to **[unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in)**
2. Click **"Activate UAN"**
3. Enter:
   - UAN number
   - Aadhaar number (or PAN or Member ID)
   - Name, date of birth
   - Mobile number (registered with Aadhaar or employer)
4. OTP verification on mobile
5. Set password (8+ characters, mix of letters/numbers/symbols)
6. **UAN activated → Login with UAN + password**

## Link Aadhaar to UAN (Mandatory for Withdrawals)

Aadhaar must be linked for online PF withdrawal:

1. Login to EPFO portal → **"Manage"** → **"KYC"**
2. Select **"Aadhaar"** from document list
3. Enter Aadhaar number + name (exactly as on Aadhaar)
4. Submit → Employer approves → UIDAI verifies
5. **Status: "Approved" shown** after employer verification

*Some employers auto-approve. Others may take 3–7 days.*

---

## Link PAN to UAN

Required for withdrawals + for TDS calculation:

1. EPFO portal → **"Manage"** → **"KYC"**
2. Select **"PAN"**
3. Enter PAN + name → Submit
4. Auto-verified within 24–48 hours
5. **Status: "Digitally Approved"**

---

## Link Bank Account to UAN

Required for PF withdrawal (money goes here):

1. EPFO portal → **"Manage"** → **"KYC"**
2. Select **"Bank"**
3. Enter: Bank name + IFSC code + Account number
4. Submit → Employer approves
5. **Status: "Approved" after employer verification**

---

## Check EPF Balance via UAN Portal

After UAN activation:

1. Login to EPFO portal
2. **"View"** → **"Passbook"**
3. Member passbook shows:
   - Employee share (your PF)
   - Employer share (employer PF contribution)
   - Monthly contributions
   - Interest credited
   - Total balance

**Alternative — Quick Balance Check (No Login):**
- **SMS:** EPFOHO UAN ENG to 7738299899
- **Missed call:** 011-22901406 (registered mobile only)
- **UMANG App:** EPFO section → Member Passbook

---

## Forgot EPFO Password — Reset

1. EPFO portal → **"Forgot Password"**
2. Enter UAN
3. Verification: OTP on registered mobile OR Aadhaar OTP
4. Set new password
5. **Login with new password**

## Common UAN Activation Issues

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|---------|
| "Mobile number mismatch" | Your EPF-registered mobile differs from Aadhaar mobile. Enter EPF-linked mobile (check with employer) |
| "Name mismatch" | Name in EPFO records ≠ Aadhaar. Update name via employer or EPFO portal |
| "UAN already activated" | Someone else may have activated. Call EPFO: 1800-118-005 |
| "Member ID not found" | Employer hasn't registered you in EPFO. Contact HR |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**I changed jobs — does my UAN change?**
No — UAN remains constant throughout your career. Your new employer adds a new Member ID under the same UAN. Previous PF balance links to the new employer's PF. UAN is truly universal.

**My employer deducts PF but I don't have UAN — what to do?**
Employer must provide UAN when registering you on EPFO. Ask HR for your UAN. If they haven't generated it: Employer is violating EPFO rules. Raise complaint at EPFO portal or on Shram Suvidha portal.

**Can I have two UAN numbers?**
No — one person should have only one UAN. If two UANs exist (can happen due to employer error): Contact EPFO at 1800-118-005 to merge. Multiple UANs can cause issues with PF withdrawal and transfer.

**I activated UAN but passbook shows ₹0 — why?**
Passbook takes 24–48 hours to populate after activation. Also: If employer hasn't deposited PF for the current month, current month won't show. Allow 2–3 days after end of month for contributions to appear.

**UAN linked to old mobile number — how to update?**
Mobile update requires physical visit to EPFO office (or through employer's portal). Online mobile update is not available to prevent fraud. Visit regional EPFO office with Aadhaar + UAN + new mobile SIM.
