# PM Vishwakarma Yojana: How to Apply Online for Artisan Scheme Benefits

PM Vishwakarma Yojana is a central government scheme launched on September 17, 2023, to support traditional artisans and craftsmen across India. With a budget of ₹13,000 crore, the scheme provides recognition, skill training, toolkit incentives, collateral-free credit, and market support to artisans working with their hands and tools.

## What is PM Vishwakarma Yojana?

PM Vishwakarma is a comprehensive scheme by the Ministry of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises (MSME) to empower artisans and craftsmen (Vishwakarmas) who work in 18 traditional trades. The scheme aims to strengthen the guru-shishya tradition and help artisans integrate into domestic and global value chains.

### Key Benefits

| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Recognition** | PM Vishwakarma Certificate and ID Card |
| **Skill Training** | 5–15 days basic training + 15 days advanced training |
| **Training Stipend** | ₹500 per day during training |
| **Toolkit Incentive** | Up to ₹15,000 (grant, not loan) |
| **Collateral-free Credit** | Up to ₹1 lakh (first tranche) + up to ₹2 lakh (second tranche) at 5% interest |
| **Digital Incentive** | ₹1 per transaction for digital payments (max ₹100/month) |
| **Marketing Support** | Quality certification, branding, e-commerce (GeM, ONDC) |

### 18 Eligible Trades

1. Carpenter (Suthar)
2. Boat Maker
3. Armourer (Blacksmith)
4. Hammer and Tool Kit Maker
5. Locksmith
6. Goldsmith (Sonar)
7. Potter (Kumhar)
8. Sculptor (Moortikar)
9. Stone Carver
10. Cobbler (Charmakar) / Shoe Maker
11. Mason (Rajmistri)
12. Basket/Mat/Broom Maker
13. Doll & Toy Maker
14. Barber (Nai)
15. Garland Maker (Malakaar)
16. Washerman (Dhobi)
17. Tailor (Darzi)
18. Fishing Net Maker

## Eligibility Criteria

- Must be an **artisan or craftsman** working in one of the 18 listed trades
- Must work **with hands and tools** as a self-employed individual
- Age: **18 years or above**
- Must **not be employed** in government/PSU or receiving benefits under similar central/state schemes (like PM SVANidhi, PMEGP for the same purpose)
- **One member per family** can register (family = husband, wife, and unmarried children)
- Must not have taken MUDRA loan or similar in last 5 years for the same trade
- Registration through **Common Service Centre (CSC)** is mandatory for biometric verification

## How to Apply for PM Vishwakarma Yojana Online

### Step 1: Register on the Portal

1. Visit [pmvishwakarma.gov.in](https://pmvishwakarma.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"How to Register"** or **"Apply Now"**
3. You'll be redirected to the registration page
4. Enter your **mobile number** linked to Aadhaar
5. Verify with OTP
6. Enter Aadhaar number for e-KYC verification

### Step 2: Visit Common Service Centre (CSC)

- After initial registration, you must visit a **CSC (Jan Seva Kendra)** for:
  - Biometric verification using Aadhaar
  - Trade verification by the Gram Panchayat / Urban Local Body
  - Document upload assistance
- Find nearest CSC at [locator.csccloud.in](https://locator.csccloud.in/)

### Step 3: Complete Application Form

6. Fill in personal details — name, address, family details
7. Select your **trade** from the 18 listed trades
8. Upload documents:
   - Aadhaar card
   - Passport-size photograph
   - Bank account details (for DBT)
   - Trade-related proof (if available — tools, products, etc.)
9. Gram Panchayat / Municipal body verifies your trade

### Step 4: Verification and Approval

10. Application is verified at **Block/District level**
11. Gram Panchayat / ULB head recommends the application
12. District Implementation Committee (DIC) approves
13. You receive **PM Vishwakarma Certificate and ID Card**

### Step 5: Avail Benefits

14. Enrol for **skill training** at the designated centre
15. Receive **₹500/day stipend** during training
16. Get **toolkit incentive** of ₹15,000 credited to bank account
17. Apply for **collateral-free loan** (₹1 lakh first tranche) at the designated bank
18. After repaying first tranche, apply for **₹2 lakh second tranche**

## Loan Details Under PM Vishwakarma

| Parameter | First Tranche | Second Tranche |
|-----------|--------------|----------------|
| Amount | Up to ₹1,00,000 | Up to ₹2,00,000 |
| Interest Rate | 5% (government subsidized) | 5% (government subsidized) |
| Actual bank rate | 8%+ (MoMSME pays the difference) | 8%+ (MoMSME pays the difference) |
| Collateral | Not required | Not required |
| Repayment | 18 months | 30 months |
| Eligibility | After training completion | After repaying first tranche |

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar card (mandatory — linked to mobile)
- Bank account passbook (for DBT)
- Passport-size photograph
- Mobile number linked to Aadhaar
- Trade-related proof (optional — photographs of work, tools, products)
- Caste certificate (if applicable — not mandatory)
- [Ration card](/guides/ration-card-new-apply-online) (helpful for family verification)

## How to Check Application Status

1. Visit [pmvishwakarma.gov.in](https://pmvishwakarma.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"Know Your Status"** or **"Track Application"**
3. Login with your registered mobile number
4. View your application status, certificate, and benefit disbursement details

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Can women artisans apply for PM Vishwakarma Yojana?

Yes, absolutely. The scheme is open to all artisans regardless of gender. Women artisans are especially encouraged to apply.

### 2. Is PM Vishwakarma available in all states?

Yes. It's a centrally sponsored scheme available across all states and Union Territories of India.

### 3. Can I apply if I already have a MUDRA loan?

If you have an existing MUDRA loan for a different purpose, you may still be eligible. However, if you've received PMEGP or MUDRA benefits for the same trade in the last 5 years, you may not qualify.

### 4. Do I need a caste certificate to apply?

No. PM Vishwakarma is **not caste-based**. It is trade-based. Any artisan working in the 18 listed trades can apply regardless of caste.

### 5. Can I apply online without visiting CSC?

Initial registration can be done online, but **biometric verification at CSC is mandatory**. You must visit a CSC for Aadhaar-based biometric authentication.

### 6. What if my trade is not in the 18 listed trades?

Currently, only the 18 specified trades are eligible. The government may expand the list in future. Check the portal for updates.

### 7. Is the ₹15,000 toolkit incentive a loan or grant?

It is a **grant** (free money), not a loan. You do not need to repay it. It is directly credited to your bank account after skill training.
