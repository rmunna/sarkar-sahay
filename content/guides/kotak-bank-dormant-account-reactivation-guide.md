# Kotak Mahindra Bank Dormant Account Reactivation 2026 — Complete Guide

Kotak Mahindra Bank (including Kotak 811 zero-balance accounts) marks accounts dormant after **24 months** of no customer-initiated transactions. For Kotak 811 accounts, dormancy can additionally occur if minimum KYC wasn't upgraded to full KYC within 12 months.

---

## Kotak 811 vs Regular Savings — Dormancy Differences

| Account Type | Dormancy Rule | Reactivation |
|-------------|---------------|-------------|
| Kotak 811 (minimum KYC expired) | After 12 months without full KYC | Complete Video KYC immediately |
| Kotak 811 (full KYC) | 24 months no transaction | Branch / app reactivation |
| Kotak Regular Savings | 24 months no transaction | Branch / app reactivation |

---

## Kotak Dormant Account — Restrictions

| Feature | Active | Dormant |
|---------|--------|---------|
| ATM withdrawals | ✅ | ❌ |
| Debit card / UPI | ✅ | ❌ |
| Net banking debits | ✅ | ❌ |
| Incoming credits | ✅ | ✅ Continues |
| Balance enquiry | ✅ | ✅ |

---

## Method 1: Kotak 811 — Video KYC Reactivation

If your **Kotak 811 account was blocked due to minimum KYC expiry** (not 24-month dormancy):

1. Open **Kotak Mobile Banking** app → Login
2. Go to **"Profile"** → **"KYC Status"** → **"Complete Video KYC"**
3. Video KYC with Kotak executive (5 minutes)
4. After KYC: Account upgraded to full KYC → Reactivated automatically
5. **No branch visit needed for KYC-expiry dormancy**

---

## Method 2: Kotak Mobile App — Dormant Account Reactivation

For 24-month dormancy:

1. Kotak Mobile Banking → Login
2. **"Accounts"** → Dormant account → **"Account Services"**
3. Look for **"Reactivate Account"** option
4. Update KYC via Aadhaar OTP if prompted
5. Submit
6. **Processing: 24–72 hours**

---

## Method 3: Kotak Net Banking

1. Login at **[netbanking.kotak.com](https://netbanking.kotak.com)**
2. **"My Accounts"** → Dormant account → **"Account Services"** → **"Reactivate"**
3. KYC update if required
4. **Processing: 2–3 working days**

---

## Method 4: Kotak Bank Branch

1. Visit any Kotak Mahindra Bank branch
2. Carry:
   - Aadhaar card (original + copy)
   - PAN card (original + copy)
3. Request: **"Dormant Account Reactivation Form"**
4. Fill form → Deposit or withdraw ₹500 at counter
5. **Same-day reactivation**

**Kotak Customer Care:** **1860-266-0811**

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Kotak 811 account blocked after 12 months — is this the KYC issue or dormancy?**
If blocked at 12 months: It's the minimum KYC expiry (not the 24-month dormancy rule). Fix: Complete Video KYC immediately via Kotak app. This is the most common Kotak 811 issue.

**Kotak dormant account — I can still see balance but can't transact — why?**
Dormant accounts allow balance enquiry and receive credits but block all debits. This is normal and by RBI rule (to protect unclaimed deposits). Reactivate via branch + one cash transaction.

**Can Kotak 811 be reactivated fully online without branch?**
Yes — if the block is due to minimum KYC expiry: Video KYC in the app solves it. If due to 24-month dormancy: Try app reactivation; if that fails, branch visit needed.

**Kotak charged me ₹500 demat AMC even though account is dormant — is this valid?**
Kotak demat charges are separate from savings account. Savings account dormancy doesn't affect demat charges. For demat charge waiver: Contact Kotak Securities.

**I've been abroad for 3 years — my Kotak account is dormant. What to do?**
Option 1: Authorize someone with Kotak POA to visit branch. Option 2: On your next India visit, reactivate at any Kotak branch. Option 3: Contact Kotak NRI services (for NRE/NRO accounts).
