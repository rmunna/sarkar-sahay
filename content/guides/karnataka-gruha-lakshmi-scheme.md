## What is the Gruha Lakshmi Scheme?

The Gruha Lakshmi scheme is a flagship welfare program launched by the Karnataka state government to provide financial assistance of **₹2,000 per month** to women heads of households. The scheme aims to empower women economically and support low-income families by providing a guaranteed monthly income directly to the woman of the household.

This is one of five guarantee schemes launched by the Karnataka government and has benefited over 1 crore women across the state.

## Who is Eligible?

### Basic Criteria
- The applicant must be a **woman who is the head of the household**
- Must be a **resident of Karnataka**
- Must hold a valid **ration card** (BPL, APL, or Antyodaya)
- Only **one woman per household** can avail the benefit
- The woman's name must appear as the head of family on the ration card

### Who is NOT Eligible
- Families where any member is a government employee (state or central) or a pension holder
- Families where any member is a current or former MLA, MLC, MP, or Zilla Panchayat/Taluk Panchayat member
- Families paying income tax
- Women already receiving ₹2,000 or more per month from other state government schemes

## Benefits

| Benefit | Amount |
|---------|--------|
| Monthly financial assistance | ₹2,000 |
| Annual total | ₹24,000 |
| Payment mode | Direct Bank Transfer (DBT) |
| Payment frequency | Monthly |

The amount is credited directly to the woman's bank account linked with Aadhaar.

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** of the applicant
- **Ration card** (BPL/APL/Antyodaya) showing the woman as head of household
- **Bank account details** — account in the woman's name, linked with Aadhaar
- **Mobile number** linked with Aadhaar
- **Voter ID** or other address proof (if needed)

## How to Apply

### Online Application
1. Visit [sevasindhuservices.karnataka.gov.in](https://sevasindhuservices.karnataka.gov.in) or the Gruha Lakshmi portal
2. Click on **"Gruha Lakshmi"** scheme
3. Enter your ration card number and Aadhaar number
4. Verify details through OTP sent to Aadhaar-linked mobile
5. Fill in bank account details
6. Upload required documents
7. Submit the application
8. Note the application reference number

### Offline Application
1. Visit your nearest **Bangalore One / Karnataka One** centre or **Gram Panchayat** office
2. Carry original ration card, Aadhaar card, and bank passbook
3. Fill the application form with assistance from the centre staff
4. Submit the form and collect the acknowledgment receipt
5. Track application status online using the reference number

### Through Seva Sindhu App
- Download the Seva Sindhu app from Google Play Store
- Register and login
- Apply for Gruha Lakshmi through the app interface

## Application Status Check

1. Visit the Gruha Lakshmi portal or Seva Sindhu
2. Enter your ration card number or application reference number
3. Check the current status — pending, approved, or rejected
4. If rejected, check the reason and reapply with correct information

## Processing Time
- Applications are typically processed within **15-30 days**
- First payment is credited within 30-45 days of approval
- Subsequent payments are monthly

## Important Tips

- **Ensure your ration card is updated** — your name must appear as the head of household; if not, get it updated first
- **Aadhaar-bank linking is mandatory** — ensure your Aadhaar is seeded with your bank account for DBT
- **One per household** — only the woman listed as head of household on the ration card can apply
- **Keep your mobile number active** — OTP verification uses the Aadhaar-linked mobile number
- **Apply through official channels only** — beware of fraud websites; use only government portals

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can unmarried women apply for Gruha Lakshmi?
Yes, if the unmarried woman is listed as the head of household on the ration card, she can apply.

### What if my ration card shows my husband as head of family?
You need to get the ration card updated to show the woman as the head of household. Visit your taluk office or AHARA office to make this change.

### Can I apply if I already receive a widow pension?
If you receive less than ₹2,000 per month from a state pension, you can receive the difference under Gruha Lakshmi. If you already receive ₹2,000 or more, you are not eligible.

### Is Gruha Lakshmi available for APL ration card holders?
Yes, both BPL and APL ration card holders are eligible, subject to other eligibility conditions (no government employee in family, no income tax payer).

### How will I receive the money?
The ₹2,000 is transferred directly to your Aadhaar-linked bank account through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) every month.

### Can I apply if I live in a rented house?
Yes, house ownership is not a criteria. You need a valid Karnataka ration card with your name as head of household.
