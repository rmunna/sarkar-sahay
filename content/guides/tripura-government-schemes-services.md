# Tripura Government Schemes and Services – Complete Guide

Tripura, the third-smallest state in India nestled in the northeast, is governed by the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) under Chief Minister Dr. Manik Saha. With a population of approximately 40 lakh and a significant tribal population (~31%), the state government has rolled out a range of welfare schemes covering education, women empowerment, tribal development, agriculture, healthcare, and digital governance.

This comprehensive hub covers all major Tripura government schemes and services — from smartphone distribution and girl child welfare to plantation subsidies and online citizen portals — so residents can find the right scheme and apply with confidence.

## Youth and Education Schemes

### Mukhyamantri Yuba Yogayog Yojana (Free Smartphones)

The flagship Mukhyamantri Yuba Yogayog Yojana provides **₹5,000 financial assistance** to eligible students for purchasing smartphones, bridging the digital divide for youth in the state.

- **Eligibility:** Students who are permanent residents of Tripura and enrolled in recognised educational institutions
- **Benefit:** One-time financial assistance of ₹5,000 credited via DBT
- **How to apply:** Through the official portal or designated educational institutions
- **Official notification:** [tripura.gov.in](https://tripura.gov.in/launching-mukhyamantri-yuba-yogayog-yojana)

> **Tip:** Ensure your [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) is linked to your bank account for DBT transfer.

### Education Scholarships and Stipends

The Directorate of Higher Education runs multiple scholarship and stipend programmes for Tripura students:

- **Post-Matric Scholarship** for SC/ST/OBC students pursuing higher education
- **Pre-Matric Scholarship (Class IX–X)** for ST students with Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts
- **Merit-cum-Means Scholarship** for meritorious students from economically weaker sections
- **Boarding House Stipend** for tribal students from remote areas staying in government hostels
- **Ishan Uday Scholarship** (UGC) for NE region students in professional courses

Apply through the [National Scholarship Portal](/guide/national-scholarship-portal-guide) or directly through the Higher Education Directorate at [highereducation.tripura.gov.in](https://highereducation.tripura.gov.in/DHE/scholarship-scheme).

### TPSC – Tripura Public Service Commission

The TPSC conducts recruitment examinations for Group A and Group B government posts in Tripura:

- **Tripura Civil Service (TCS)** and **Tripura Police Service (TPS)** exams
- **Junior Medical Officer**, **Assistant Professor**, and other professional posts
- **Apply online:** [tpsc.tripura.gov.in](https://tpsc.tripura.gov.in) or [tpsconline.in](https://tpsconline.in)
- Tentative exam schedules are published on the TPSC website

> Check our [admit card download guide](/guide/admit-card-download-online-guide) for tips on downloading your TPSC hall ticket.

## Women and Girl Child Welfare Schemes

### Mukhyamantri Balika Samriddhi Yojana

Launched by the BJP government, this scheme invests in the future of girl children born in BPL families:

- **Benefit:** A **₹50,000 bond** deposited in the name of every girl child born in a BPL family
- **Maturity:** The bond matures to approximately **₹10 lakh** when the girl turns 18
- **Objective:** Discourage child marriage, promote girl child education, and provide financial security

### Mukhyamantri Kanya Atmanirbhar Yojana

This scheme rewards academic excellence among girls:

- **Benefit:** Free scooters to the **top 140 girls** who score the highest marks across Tripura Board, CBSE, and ISC examinations
- **Objective:** Encourage higher education and mobility for meritorious girls
- **Eligibility:** Must be a resident of Tripura and have appeared in Class XII board exams

### Other Women Welfare Programmes

- **Swadhar Greh** – Shelter and rehabilitation for women in difficult circumstances
- **One Stop Centre (Sakhi)** – Support for women affected by violence
- **Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana** – ₹5,000 maternity benefit for first live birth (centrally sponsored, implemented in Tripura)

## Tribal Welfare Schemes

With approximately 31% of Tripura's population belonging to Scheduled Tribes, the Tribal Welfare Department runs extensive programmes:

### Educational Support

- **Boarding houses and hostels** across all districts for ST students from remote areas
- **Pre-Matric and Post-Matric Scholarships** with DBT-linked Aadhaar payments
- **Coaching and training centres** for competitive examination preparation
- **Book grants and uniform allowances** for tribal students

### Livelihood and Economic Development

- **Tribal Sub Plan (TSP)** allocations for infrastructure, roads, and connectivity in tribal areas
- **Self-Help Group (SHG) support** for tribal women through the National Rural Livelihoods Mission
- **Skill development training** under various central and state programmes
- **Rubber, tea, and coffee plantation assistance** for tribal farmers (see Agriculture section below)

### Cultural Preservation

- Support for tribal languages, art forms, and festivals
- Tribal Research and Cultural Institute programmes
- Grants for tribal cultural organisations

For details, visit [twd.tripura.gov.in](https://twd.tripura.gov.in/programs-schemes).

## Agriculture and Plantation Schemes

### Rubber Plantation Schemes

Tripura is India's **second-largest rubber-producing state** after Kerala. The Rubber Board and state government jointly support:

- **New planting and replanting subsidies** for rubber cultivation
- **Rain guarding and tapping training** for rubber tappers
- **Group processing centres** for small and marginal rubber growers
- **Financial assistance** through the Rubber Board's schemes accessible via [UMANG portal](https://web.umang.gov.in/landing/department/rubber-board.html)

### Tea Plantation Support

Tripura produces quality orthodox and CTC tea. Government support includes:

- **Replanting and rejuvenation subsidies** through the Tea Board
- **Small grower development programmes** with technical assistance
- **Market linkage support** and quality certification assistance

### Other Agriculture Schemes

- **PM-KISAN** – ₹6,000 annual income support for farmer families ([PM-KISAN guide](/guide/pm-kisan-status-check))
- **Kisan Credit Card** – Subsidised crop loans for farmers
- **Soil Health Card** distribution for optimised farming
- **Crop insurance** under Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana

## Healthcare Schemes

### Ayushman Bharat – Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB-PMJAY)

Tripura actively implements the Ayushman Bharat scheme providing **₹5 lakh annual health cover** per family for secondary and tertiary hospitalisation:

- **Eligibility:** Based on SECC 2011 deprivation criteria — identified BPL families
- **Coverage:** Free treatment at empanelled government and private hospitals
- **How to check eligibility:** Visit [pmjay.gov.in](https://pmjay.gov.in) or call **14555**
- **Download your Ayushman card:** Follow our [Ayushman Bharat card guide](/guide/ayushman-bharat-health-card)

> Tripura has empanelled multiple hospitals across districts for cashless treatment under AB-PMJAY. Check the [hospital list guide](/guide/ayushman-bharat-hospital-list-check) to find one near you.

### Other Health Programmes

- **Janani Suraksha Yojana** – Cash incentive for institutional delivery
- **National Health Mission (NHM)** programmes across primary health centres
- **Free drug and diagnostic services** at government health facilities

## e-Governance and Citizen Services

### e-District Tripura

The e-District portal offers online application and tracking for key certificates and services:

- **Income Certificate**
- **Caste Certificate (SC/ST/OBC)**
- **Residence/Domicile Certificate**
- **Character Certificate**
- **Land-related services**
- **Permit applications**

Apply at [edistrict.tripura.gov.in](https://edistrict.tripura.gov.in). Most services require Aadhaar authentication.

### Jan Seva Kendra (Common Service Centres)

Jan Seva Kendras across Tripura provide assisted access to government services for citizens who lack internet access:

- Aadhaar enrolment and updates
- Digital Life Certificate (Jeevan Pramaan) for pensioners
- PAN card application
- Passport services
- Banking and insurance services
- e-District certificate applications

> If you need to [download your Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-card-download-online) or [link Aadhaar with PAN](/guide/aadhaar-pan-link), Jan Seva Kendras can assist.

### Other Digital Services

- **Tripura State Data Centre** – Backend infrastructure for all e-governance services
- **BharatNet connectivity** – High-speed internet reaching gram panchayats
- **DigiLocker** – Access government-issued documents digitally

## Food Security and Ration

Under the **National Food Security Act (NFSA)**, eligible families in Tripura receive subsidised foodgrains:

- **Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY)** – 35 kg per family per month at ₹2/kg (wheat) and ₹3/kg (rice)
- **Priority Household (PHH)** – 5 kg per person per month
- **One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC)** – Portability for migrant families

Check your ration card status at [nfsa.tripura.gov.in](https://nfsa.tripura.gov.in). For general guidance, see our [ration card guide](/guide/ration-card-apply-online).

## How to Apply for Tripura Government Schemes

Most Tripura government schemes follow a standard application process:

1. **Check eligibility** – Visit the relevant department website or [myscheme.gov.in](https://www.myscheme.gov.in) for scheme-wise eligibility criteria
2. **Gather documents** – Typically required: Aadhaar card, income certificate, caste certificate, bank passbook, passport-size photos, domicile certificate
3. **Apply online** – Use e-District Tripura portal or the specific scheme portal
4. **Apply offline** – Visit the nearest Jan Seva Kendra, BDO office, or district collectorate
5. **Track status** – Use the application reference number on the respective portal
6. **Receive benefit** – Most cash benefits are transferred via DBT to your Aadhaar-linked bank account

> **Important:** Keep your [Aadhaar details updated](/guide/aadhaar-update-mobile-email) and bank account linked for smooth DBT transfers.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How can I check if I'm eligible for Tripura government schemes?

Visit [myscheme.gov.in](https://www.myscheme.gov.in), select Tripura as your state, and use the eligibility checker. You can also visit the specific department website or your nearest Jan Seva Kendra.

### How do I apply for a caste or income certificate in Tripura?

Apply online through the e-District Tripura portal at [edistrict.tripura.gov.in](https://edistrict.tripura.gov.in). You'll need Aadhaar, address proof, and supporting documents. Processing typically takes 7–15 working days.

### What is the Mukhyamantri Yuba Yogayog Yojana eligibility?

You must be a permanent resident of Tripura, enrolled in a recognised educational institution, and meet the income criteria set by the government. The scheme provides ₹5,000 for purchasing a smartphone.

### How do I get an Ayushman Bharat card in Tripura?

Check your eligibility at [pmjay.gov.in](https://pmjay.gov.in) or call 14555. If eligible, visit your nearest Ayushman Bharat Kendra or empanelled hospital with your Aadhaar and ration card to get your e-card generated. See our [detailed guide](/guide/ayushman-bharat-health-card).

### How can I apply for TPSC exams?

Visit [tpsc.tripura.gov.in](https://tpsc.tripura.gov.in) or [tpsconline.in](https://tpsconline.in) for the latest notifications. Create an account, fill in your details, upload documents, pay the fee online, and download your admit card when released.

### Are there special schemes for tribal communities in Tripura?

Yes, the Tribal Welfare Department runs extensive programmes including scholarships, boarding house facilities, livelihood support, plantation assistance, and cultural preservation grants. Visit [twd.tripura.gov.in](https://twd.tripura.gov.in) for the full list.

### How do I check my ration card status in Tripura?

Visit [nfsa.tripura.gov.in](https://nfsa.tripura.gov.in) and search using your ration card number or family member name. You can also use the One Nation One Ration Card facility if you've migrated to another state.
