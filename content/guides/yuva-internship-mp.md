# MP Yuva Internship Yojana: Stipend, Eligibility & How to Apply

The Madhya Pradesh Yuva Internship Yojana (also known as Mukhyamantri Yuva Internship Yojana) provides work experience opportunities to unemployed graduates of the state. Interns receive a monthly stipend and gain professional experience in government departments and offices, improving their employability.

---

## What is MP Yuva Internship Yojana?

The Yuva Internship Yojana is a state government initiative to bridge the gap between education and employment for young graduates in Madhya Pradesh. Under this scheme, eligible graduates are placed as interns in various government departments, district offices, and public sector organisations for a period of one year. Interns receive a monthly stipend to support themselves during the internship period.

---

## Key Benefits

- **Monthly stipend:** Rs 8,000 per month
- **Duration:** 12 months (1 year)
- **Work experience:** Hands-on experience in government departments
- **Certificate:** Internship completion certificate issued
- **Skill development:** Practical exposure to governance and administration

---

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Age:** 18 to 29 years
2. **Education:** Must be a graduate (minimum) from a recognised university
3. **Residency:** Must be a permanent resident of Madhya Pradesh
4. **Employment status:** Must be unemployed (not employed in any government or private job)
5. **Income:** Family annual income should not exceed Rs 6,00,000
6. **Not availed before:** Should not have previously completed this internship

---

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- **Graduation marksheet and degree certificate**
- **Domicile certificate** of Madhya Pradesh
- **Income certificate** (family annual income)
- **Bank account passbook** with IFSC code
- **Passport-size photograph**
- **Samagra ID**
- **Mobile number** linked to Aadhaar

---

## How to Apply: Step-by-Step Process

### Online Application

1. Visit the MP Yuva Portal at [yuvaportal.mp.gov.in](https://yuvaportal.mp.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"Yuva Internship"** or **"युवा इंटर्नशिप"**
3. Register with your mobile number and create an account
4. Login and fill in personal details, educational qualifications, and preferences
5. Upload all required documents
6. Select preferred department/district for internship
7. Submit the application
8. Note the application reference number

### Selection Process

1. Applications are screened based on eligibility criteria
2. Merit-based selection considering educational qualifications
3. Department-wise allocation based on vacancies and preferences
4. Selected candidates are notified via SMS and portal
5. Reporting date and department details are communicated

---

## Stipend Payment

- Stipend of Rs 8,000/month is paid via **DBT** to the intern's bank account
- Payment is made after monthly attendance verification by the supervising officer
- Stipend may be withheld for unauthorized absence

---

## Processing Time

- **Application review:** 15-30 days after last date of application
- **Selection notification:** Within 30 days of review
- **Internship start:** As per the notification schedule

---

## Important Tips

1. **Apply during application window** — The scheme opens applications periodically; watch for notifications
2. **Choose department wisely** — Select departments aligned with your career goals
3. **Maintain attendance** — Regular attendance is mandatory for stipend payment
4. **Get your certificate** — Ensure you collect the completion certificate; it adds value to your resume
5. **Prepare for the future** — Use the internship to prepare for competitive exams or develop job skills

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. What is the stipend amount under MP Yuva Internship?
The monthly stipend is Rs 8,000.

### 2. Can postgraduates apply?
Yes, graduates and postgraduates both can apply as the minimum qualification is graduation.

### 3. Is there any application fee?
No, the application process is free.

### 4. Does this internship guarantee a government job?
No, this is an internship for work experience only. It does not guarantee any permanent employment.

### 5. Can I apply if I am doing a private job?
No, the scheme is specifically for unemployed graduates. You must be currently unemployed.

### 6. What departments can I be placed in?
Interns are placed in various government departments including district administration, revenue, health, education, agriculture, and other state departments.

### 7. Can I reapply if not selected?
Yes, you can reapply in the next application cycle if you remain eligible.
