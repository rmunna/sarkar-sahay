## What is Telangana Dalit Bandhu Scheme?

Dalit Bandhu is a **landmark welfare scheme** launched by the Government of Telangana to provide **₹10,00,000 (₹10 lakh) as a direct grant** to every Scheduled Caste (SC) family in the state. Unlike loans, this is a **non-refundable grant** meant to help SC families set up their own business or enterprise.

Launched in 2021, this is one of the **largest direct benefit schemes** for Dalit empowerment in India. The scheme aims to break the cycle of poverty by providing capital for entrepreneurship rather than traditional welfare benefits.

Key features:
- **₹10 lakh per SC family** — direct grant, not a loan
- **No repayment required** — the amount is a gift from the state
- **Business-oriented** — beneficiaries must set up an enterprise or livelihood unit
- **Saturation model** — aims to cover all eligible SC families in each constituency

## Who is Eligible?

### Basic Eligibility
- Must belong to a **Scheduled Caste (SC)** family
- Must be a **resident of Telangana**
- Must hold a valid **caste certificate** issued by the Telangana government
- One grant per family — only **one member per household** can apply
- Must be **18 years or older**

### Additional Criteria
- Applicant must have a **viable business plan** or enterprise idea
- Must have an **Aadhaar card** linked to the bank account
- Must be willing to undergo **basic training** on business management (provided by the government)
- Priority is given based on **constituency-wise selection** by the local MLA and district committee

### Who is NOT Eligible?
- Non-SC families (OBC, ST, and general category are not covered)
- Families where a member is a **government employee** (in some district guidelines)
- Those who have already received the grant
- Families not residing in Telangana

## Documents Required

- **Caste certificate** (SC) issued by the Tahsildar/MRO
- **Aadhaar card** of the applicant
- **Ration card** (for family and residence proof)
- **Income certificate** (from MRO)
- **Bank account passbook** (Aadhaar-linked, in applicant's name)
- **Passport-size photographs** (4 copies)
- **Business proposal** — description of the enterprise to be set up
- **Voter ID** or other residence proof
- **Mobile number** linked to Aadhaar

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Application Process

1. **Check if your constituency is selected** — Dalit Bandhu is rolled out constituency by constituency. Check with your local MLA office or the district SC welfare office
2. **Attend the Gram Sabha / Ward Sabha** — Your name must be recommended in the local meeting. The MLA and constituency-level committee identify beneficiaries
3. **Submit your application** — Visit the **District SC Development Office** or use the online portal at [dalitbandhu.telangana.gov.in](https://dalitbandhu.telangana.gov.in)
4. **Fill in personal details** — Name, Aadhaar, caste certificate, family details
5. **Submit your business proposal** — Describe the enterprise you want to start (dairy, shop, auto, salon, etc.)
6. **Document verification** — Officials will verify your caste certificate, income, and residence
7. **Committee approval** — The District Dalit Bandhu Committee reviews and approves applications
8. **Receive training** — Attend the mandatory business orientation/training programme
9. **Grant disbursement** — ₹10 lakh is transferred directly to your **bank account** via DBT
10. **Set up your enterprise** — Use the funds to purchase equipment, stock, or assets as per your proposal

### What Kind of Businesses Can You Start?

Beneficiaries have set up businesses including:
- **Dairy units** (buffalo/cow purchase)
- **Auto-rickshaws and taxis**
- **Small shops** (grocery, mobile, hardware)
- **Beauty parlours and salons**
- **Tractors** for agricultural services
- **Cloth and garment shops**
- **Poultry and fishery units**

## Processing Time

- **Constituency selection** — Varies (government announces batches)
- **Application to approval** — 30 to 90 days depending on verification workload
- **Grant disbursement** — Within 15–30 days of approval
- **Total timeline** — 2 to 4 months from application to receiving funds

## Important Tips

1. **Prepare your business plan in advance** — The stronger your plan, the faster the approval. Think about what business suits your skills and local demand.
2. **Ensure all certificates are current** — Get fresh caste and income certificates if your existing ones are older than 6 months.
3. **Keep your bank account active** — The ₹10 lakh will come via DBT. Ensure your account is active and Aadhaar-linked. See our [Aadhaar linking guide](/guide/aadhaar-linking-guide).
4. **Don't fall for middlemen** — The scheme is free. Do not pay anyone for "guaranteed selection." Report fraud to the district collector.
5. **Use funds only for the stated purpose** — Misuse of funds can lead to action. Invest in the business you proposed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Is Dalit Bandhu a loan or a grant?
It is a **100% grant (free gift)** from the government. There is no repayment required. It is not a loan.

### Q2: Can more than one person from a family apply?
No. Only **one member per household** is eligible. The scheme is designed per family unit as defined by the ration card.

### Q3: How are beneficiaries selected?
Selection is done at the **constituency level** through a committee headed by the local MLA, along with district officials. Eligible SC families are identified through Gram Sabhas and ward meetings.

### Q4: What if my constituency hasn't been selected yet?
The government is rolling out the scheme in phases. Wait for your constituency to be announced. Check with your local MLA office or the district SC Development Officer for updates.

### Q5: Can I use the ₹10 lakh to buy land or build a house?
No. The grant is specifically for **setting up a business or enterprise**. It cannot be used for personal consumption, land purchase, or house construction. For housing, check separate schemes like PMAY. See our [PM Awas Yojana guide](/guide/pm-awas-yojana-apply-online) for details.

### Q6: What happens if the business fails?
The grant does not need to be repaid even if the business doesn't succeed. However, the government monitors utilisation, and beneficiaries are encouraged to seek guidance and training support.

### Q7: Is there an age limit?
The applicant must be **at least 18 years old**. There is no upper age limit, though the enterprise should be viable for the applicant to manage.
