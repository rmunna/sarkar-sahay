# Unemployment Allowance – Puducherry

The Government of Puducherry provides a monthly unemployment allowance to educated unemployed youth who are registered with the Employment Exchange. This financial support helps job seekers meet basic expenses while they search for suitable employment.

## What is the Unemployment Allowance?

The scheme provides a monthly stipend to unemployed youth from Puducherry who have completed their education but have not found employment. The allowance amount varies based on the educational qualification of the applicant.

### Allowance Amounts

| Education Level | Monthly Allowance |
|----------------|-------------------|
| SSLC / Class 10 pass | ₹500 – ₹1,000* |
| HSC / Class 12 pass | ₹750 – ₹1,200* |
| Degree holders | ₹1,000 – ₹1,500* |
| Professional degree (Engineering, Medical, etc.) | ₹1,200 – ₹1,500* |
| ITI / Diploma holders | ₹750 – ₹1,000* |

*Amounts are approximate and may vary as per current government orders. Verify latest rates on the official portal.*

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Residency:** Must be a resident of Puducherry UT for at least 5 years
2. **Education:** Must have completed at least SSLC / Class 10
3. **Age:** Generally between 18 and 35 years (may vary)
4. **Employment Exchange registration:** Must be registered with the Puducherry Employment Exchange
5. **Unemployed:** Must not be employed or self-employed
6. **Income:** Annual family income below the prescribed limit
7. **Not availing other benefits:** Should not be receiving any other government employment-linked stipend

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar card (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- Employment Exchange registration card
- Educational certificates (SSLC, HSC, Degree, etc.)
- Income certificate from Revenue Department
- Residence proof (5+ years in Puducherry)
- Bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked)
- Passport-size photographs
- Ration card
- Self-declaration of unemployment

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### How to Apply

1. **Register at Employment Exchange:** Visit the Puducherry Employment Exchange office and register as a job seeker
2. **Obtain registration number:** Get your Employment Exchange registration card/number
3. **Collect application form:** Get the unemployment allowance form from the Employment Exchange or District Employment Office
4. **Fill the form:** Enter personal details, educational qualifications, Employment Exchange number, and bank details
5. **Attach documents:** Self-attested copies of all required documents
6. **Submit application:** Submit at the Employment Exchange or District Employment Office
7. **Verification:** Officials verify your educational qualifications, unemployment status, and income
8. **Approval:** Application approved by competent authority
9. **Monthly payment:** Allowance credited to bank account monthly

### Renewal

- The allowance must be renewed periodically (usually annually)
- You must renew your Employment Exchange registration regularly
- If you get employed, inform the Exchange and the allowance will stop

## Processing Time

- **Registration to approval:** 30–60 days
- **Monthly disbursement:** After approval, amount is credited monthly

## Important Tips

1. **Keep Employment Exchange registration active:** Renew it before expiry — lapsed registration disqualifies you
2. **Update if you get a job:** Report employment immediately — receiving allowance while employed is punishable
3. **Attend interviews:** If the Employment Exchange refers you for job interviews, attend them — skipping may affect your allowance
4. **Apply for skill training:** Puducherry offers skill development programs — enrolling can improve your job prospects
5. **Check for enhanced benefits:** SC/ST and disabled applicants may be eligible for higher allowance amounts

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: How much unemployment allowance will I get?
The amount depends on your educational qualification — from ₹500 to ₹1,500 per month approximately.

### Q2: How long can I receive the allowance?
The allowance is provided until you find employment or reach the maximum age limit, subject to annual renewal.

### Q3: Do I need to visit the Employment Exchange regularly?
Yes, you must renew your registration periodically and attend any job referrals or interviews arranged by the Exchange.

### Q4: Can I do part-time work and still receive the allowance?
Generally, the allowance is for fully unemployed persons. Part-time or casual employment may disqualify you — check with the Exchange.

### Q5: Is this scheme available for school dropouts?
No, you must have completed at least SSLC (Class 10) to be eligible.

### Q6: Can applicants from other states living in Puducherry apply?
You must be a resident of Puducherry UT for at least 5 years to be eligible.

### Q7: Will the allowance stop if I join a skill training program?
Not necessarily — some training programs are compatible with the allowance. Check with the Employment Exchange.
