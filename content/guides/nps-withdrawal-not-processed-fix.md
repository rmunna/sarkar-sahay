# NPS Withdrawal Not Processed? How to Fix National Pension Exit Issues

You've worked for decades, contributed regularly to NPS, and now at retirement — the withdrawal is stuck. Your NPS exit request shows "pending," money hasn't arrived, or the claim got rejected. For retirees depending on this money, every day of delay is stressful.

Here's how to diagnose and fix NPS withdrawal issues.

## Understanding NPS Exit Types

| Exit Type | When | Lump Sum Allowed | Annuity Required |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Normal exit** (age 60+) | At superannuation | Up to 60% | Minimum 40% |
| **Premature exit** (before 60) | After 5 years in NPS | Up to 20% | Minimum 80% |
| **Small corpus exit** | Corpus ≤ ₹5 lakh | 100% | Not required |
| **Death of subscriber** | Nominee claims | 100% | Not required |

## Common Reasons NPS Withdrawal Gets Stuck

### 1. Incomplete or Mismatched KYC

The most common reason for delays:

- **Name mismatch** between PRAN card and bank account
- **Aadhaar not linked** to PRAN
- **Bank details incorrect** — wrong account number or IFSC code
- **PAN not linked** — required for tax processing

**Fix:**
1. Login to [cra-nsdl.com](https://cra-nsdl.com/CRA/)
2. Go to **"Update Details"** → Verify all personal details
3. Ensure **name, DOB, bank account, PAN, Aadhaar** all match exactly
4. If details need updating, submit a **S2 form** (change request) to your nodal office or POP (Point of Presence)

### 2. Annuity Service Provider (ASP) Not Selected

At exit, you must select an annuity provider and annuity type. If you haven't done this, your withdrawal will be stuck.

**Empanelled ASPs:**
- LIC of India
- SBI Life Insurance
- ICICI Prudential Life
- HDFC Life
- Star Union Dai-ichi Life
- Tata AIA Life
- Max Life Insurance

**How to select:**
1. During exit process on CRA portal, select your ASP
2. Choose annuity type (single life, joint life, with/without return of purchase price)
3. Submit the selection — this unblocks the withdrawal

### 3. Government Employee DDO Delay

For government sector NPS subscribers, the process involves your **Drawing & Disbursing Officer (DDO)**:

1. You submit exit request
2. DDO verifies and forwards to PAO (Pay & Accounts Office)
3. PAO verifies and forwards to CRA
4. CRA processes and releases funds

**If stuck at DDO level:**
- Contact your DDO directly and ask for status
- Provide your **PRAN number** and retirement date
- Ask them to process on the **NPSCAN portal** (govt processing system)
- If DDO is unresponsive, escalate to your department head

### 4. Technical Errors on CRA Portal

**Error: "Request already in process"**
- A previous request may be pending. Check **"Transaction Statement"** for existing requests.
- If an old request was rejected, it may not have been cleared. Call CRA helpline.

**Error: "PRAN is frozen/inactive"**
- PRAN gets frozen for non-contribution. Government employees: ask DDO to remit pending contributions.
- Private sector: make minimum contribution (₹1,000) to reactivate.

**Error: "eSign failed / Aadhaar authentication failed"**
- Ensure Aadhaar biometrics are unlocked at [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/)
- Try OTP-based authentication instead of biometric
- If persistent, submit physical exit form to your POP/nodal office

## Step-by-Step: Track NPS Withdrawal Status

1. Visit [cra-nsdl.com](https://cra-nsdl.com/CRA/)
2. Login with **PRAN** and **Password/IPIN**
3. Go to **"Transaction Statement"**
4. Check withdrawal/exit request status
5. Status meanings:
   - **"Initiated"** — Request submitted, pending verification
   - **"Pending with Nodal Office"** — DDO/employer hasn't approved yet
   - **"Pending with CRA"** — Under processing at NSDL
   - **"Pending with ASP"** — Annuity provider processing your annuity
   - **"Processed"** — Money should arrive in 3-5 working days
   - **"Rejected"** — See rejection reason and resubmit

## How to File NPS Withdrawal Offline

If the online process isn't working:

1. Download **Exit/Withdrawal Form (UoS-S12A)** from [npscra.nsdl.co.in](https://www.npscra.nsdl.co.in/)
2. Fill in PRAN, personal details, bank details, annuity selection
3. Attach:
   - PRAN card photocopy
   - Cancelled cheque / bank passbook copy
   - Aadhaar photocopy
   - PAN card photocopy
4. Submit to your **POP (Point of Presence)** — usually your bank branch
5. POP forwards to CRA for processing

## Escalation Path for Delayed NPS Withdrawal

If your withdrawal is stuck beyond 30 days:

1. **CRA Helpline:** 1800-222-080 (toll-free) or 022-2499-3499
2. **Email:** npslite@nsdl.co.in
3. **PFRDA Grievance:** File complaint at [pgportal.gov.in](https://pgportal.gov.in/) → Select "PFRDA"
4. **PFRDA Direct:** Visit [pfrda.org.in](https://pfrda.org.in/) → Grievance Redressal
5. **Ombudsman:** PFRDA has appointed ombudsmen for NPS grievances — details at pfrda.org.in

## Tax on NPS Withdrawal

Understanding tax helps avoid surprises:

| Component | Tax Treatment |
|---|---|
| 60% lump sum (at 60) | **Tax-free** |
| 40% annuity | **Taxable** as per income slab yearly |
| Premature withdrawal (before 60) | 20% lump sum tax-free, annuity taxable |
| Complete withdrawal (corpus ≤ ₹5 lakh) | **Tax-free** |

## Quick Summary

| Issue | Fix |
|---|---|
| KYC mismatch | Update name/bank/PAN on CRA portal |
| Annuity not selected | Choose ASP and annuity type during exit process |
| DDO not processing | Contact DDO directly, escalate to department head |
| PRAN frozen | Make minimum ₹1,000 contribution to reactivate |
| Online exit failing | Submit physical form (UoS-S12A) to your POP |
| Stuck beyond 30 days | Call 1800-222-080 and file PFRDA grievance |

Don't wait silently. The NPS system has defined timelines, and if those are breached, you have the right to escalate through PFRDA's grievance mechanism.
