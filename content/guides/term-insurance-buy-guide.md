# Term Insurance: How to Choose & Buy the Best Plan in India

Term insurance is the **purest and most affordable form of life insurance**. It pays a lump sum to your family if you pass away during the policy term — no maturity benefit, no investment component, just protection. This guide helps you choose and buy the right term plan.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent informational platform and is not affiliated with IRDAI or any insurance company. Always read the policy document before purchasing.

---

## What Is Term Insurance?

Term insurance provides a **death benefit** (sum assured) to your nominee if you die during the policy term. Key features:

- **Pure protection** — no savings/investment component
- **Cheapest life insurance** — a 30-year-old can get ₹1 crore cover for ₹600–800/month
- **Fixed premium** for the entire term (if you choose regular pay)
- **Tax benefit** — premiums qualify under **Section 80C** (up to ₹1.5 lakh/year); death benefit is **tax-free under Section 10(10D)**

## How Much Coverage Do You Need?

Use the **Human Life Value (HLV)** method:

| Factor | Calculation |
|---|---|
| **Annual income** | Your current annual income |
| **Multiplier** | 10–15x your annual income |
| **Add:** Outstanding loans | Home loan, car loan, education loan |
| **Add:** Future goals | Children's education, marriage |
| **Subtract:** Existing coverage | Employer group cover, other policies |

**Example:** If you earn ₹10 lakh/year with a ₹30 lakh home loan:
- Base cover: ₹10L × 15 = ₹1.5 crore
- Add home loan: ₹30 lakh
- **Total recommended cover: ₹1.8 crore** (round up to ₹2 crore)

## Who Should Buy Term Insurance?

- **Anyone with financial dependents** — spouse, children, parents
- **Ideal age to buy:** 25–35 years (premiums are lowest)
- **Working professionals and self-employed** individuals
- **Home loan borrowers** — cover at least the outstanding loan amount

## Key Factors When Choosing a Plan

### 1. Claim Settlement Ratio (CSR)
This is the **percentage of claims an insurer pays** out of total claims received. Check the IRDAI annual report for latest data.

| CSR | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| **95%+** | Excellent — prefer these insurers |
| **90–95%** | Good |
| **Below 90%** | Proceed with caution |

### 2. Riders (Add-ons)
| Rider | What It Covers |
|---|---|
| **Accidental Death** | Extra payout if death is due to an accident |
| **Critical Illness** | Lump sum on diagnosis of specified illnesses (cancer, heart attack, etc.) |
| **Waiver of Premium** | Premiums waived if you become permanently disabled |
| **Terminal Illness** | Early payout if diagnosed with a terminal illness |

### 3. Payout Options
- **Lump sum** — entire sum assured paid at once (most common)
- **Monthly income** — nominee receives monthly payments
- **Lump sum + monthly income** — combination of both
- **Increasing cover** — sum assured increases annually to account for inflation

### 4. Policy Term
- Cover should last until your **youngest dependent becomes financially independent**
- Common terms: **until age 60 or 65**
- Longer terms have marginally higher premiums but better protection

## Online vs Offline Term Insurance

| Feature | Online | Offline (Agent) |
|---|---|---|
| **Premium** | 30–40% cheaper | Higher (includes agent commission) |
| **Process** | Self-serve, 15–20 minutes | Agent-assisted |
| **Documentation** | Upload online | Physical submission |
| **Claim process** | Same | Same |
| **Policy servicing** | Digital portal | Agent + branch |

**Recommendation:** Buy online for significant premium savings. The coverage and claim process are identical.

## How to Buy Term Insurance Online: Step-by-Step

### Step 1: Calculate Your Coverage Need
Use the HLV method above. Round up to the nearest ₹25 lakh or ₹50 lakh.

### Step 2: Compare Plans
- Visit insurer websites or IRDAI-regulated comparison platforms
- Compare: premium, CSR, riders, payout options, and exclusions

### Step 3: Fill the Application
1. Enter **personal details** — name, date of birth, gender, contact
2. Enter **income details** — annual income, occupation
3. Declare **health history** — existing conditions, surgeries, family medical history
4. Choose **sum assured, term, and riders**
5. Add **nominee details**

### Step 4: Medical Tests (If Required)
- Most insurers require medical tests for **sum assured above ₹50 lakh** or applicants **above 35–40 years**
- Tests typically include: **blood tests (CBC, sugar, cholesterol, HIV, Hepatitis B), urine test, ECG, chest X-ray**
- The insurer arranges tests at your home/office **at no cost** through empanelled diagnostic labs
- Results go directly to the insurer

### Step 5: Pay the Premium
- Pay via **net banking, UPI, debit/credit card**
- First premium payment initiates the cover (subject to underwriting approval)

### Step 6: Policy Issuance
- If medical tests are clear and application is approved, policy is issued within **7–15 days**
- You get a **30-day free-look period** — if unhappy, you can cancel and get a refund (minus medical test costs)

## Important Tips

1. **Buy early** — a 25-year-old pays almost half the premium compared to a 35-year-old for the same cover
2. **Never hide medical history** — non-disclosure is the #1 reason for claim rejection
3. **Don't replace term insurance with investment plans** — ULIPs and endowment plans offer much lower coverage per rupee
4. **Review coverage every 3–5 years** — increase cover when you take a home loan, have children, or get a salary hike
5. **Inform your nominee** about the policy — keep details accessible so they can file a claim when needed

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: What happens if I survive the policy term?
In a **pure term plan**, there is no maturity benefit — the policy simply expires. Some insurers offer "Return of Premium" (ROP) variants, but they are significantly more expensive.

### Q2: Can I buy term insurance without a medical test?
**Yes**, many insurers offer no-medical-test policies for **young applicants (under 35–40)** with **sum assured up to ₹50 lakh–₹1 crore**. However, health declarations are mandatory.

### Q3: Is term insurance premium tax-deductible?
**Yes.** Premium paid is deductible under **Section 80C** (up to ₹1.5 lakh/year). The death benefit received by the nominee is **tax-free under Section 10(10D)**.

### Q4: Can I increase my sum assured later?
Some policies offer an **increase option** at specific milestones (marriage, child birth, home purchase). Otherwise, you can buy an additional term policy.

### Q5: What are the common exclusions in term insurance?
Most term plans exclude: **suicide within the first year**, death due to participation in hazardous activities (if not declared), and death due to substance abuse. Always read the exclusion list in the policy document.

### Q6: Should I buy term insurance from LIC or a private insurer?
Both are regulated by IRDAI. Compare **CSR, premium, and features**. Private insurers often offer cheaper online plans, while LIC has the highest trust factor and asset base.

### Q7: What is the free-look period?
You have **30 days from receiving the policy** to review it. If you're not satisfied, you can cancel and get a full refund (minus medical test and stamp duty costs).
