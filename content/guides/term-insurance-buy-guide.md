# Term Insurance: How to Choose & Buy the Best Plan

Term insurance is the simplest and most affordable form of life insurance. It provides a large death benefit to your family at a low premium. If you die during the policy term, your nominees receive the sum assured. If you survive, there is no maturity benefit — which is why it's so affordable. This guide helps you choose the right plan, calculate cover amount, understand riders, and buy online.

## What is Term Insurance?

Term insurance is a pure life cover — no investment, no savings component. You pay a fixed premium for a chosen term (e.g., 20, 30, or 40 years). If you die during this period, your family gets the sum assured (₹50 lakh, ₹1 crore, or more). If you survive the term, nothing is paid out (in basic plans).

**Why buy term insurance?**
- Provides financial security to your family
- Very affordable — ₹500-700/month can get ₹1 crore cover for a 30-year-old
- Tax benefit under Section 80C (premium) and Section 10(10D) (claim amount)

## How Much Cover Do You Need? (Cover Amount Formula)

A commonly used formula:

**Ideal Cover = (Annual Income × Years Left to Retire) + Total Liabilities − Existing Savings/Investments**

### Simplified Rule of Thumb

| Age Group | Recommended Cover |
|-----------|------------------|
| 25-30 years | 15-20× annual income |
| 30-35 years | 12-15× annual income |
| 35-40 years | 10-12× annual income |
| 40-50 years | 8-10× annual income |

**Example:** If you're 30 years old earning ₹10 lakh/year → Cover should be ₹1.2 to ₹1.5 crore.

Also account for:
- Home loan outstanding
- Children's education costs
- Spouse's financial independence
- Ageing parents' medical needs

## Key Factors to Compare Before Buying

### 1. Claim Settlement Ratio (CSR)

CSR is the percentage of claims an insurer paid out of total claims received. Published annually by IRDAI.

| Insurer | CSR (2024-25 approx.) |
|---------|----------------------|
| LIC of India | 98%+ |
| HDFC Life | 99%+ |
| ICICI Prudential | 98%+ |
| Max Life | 99%+ |
| Tata AIA | 99%+ |
| SBI Life | 97%+ |

**Tip:** Choose an insurer with CSR above 95%. But also check the total number of claims settled — a high CSR on very few claims is less meaningful.

### 2. Premium Amount

- Online plans are 30-40% cheaper than offline (no agent commission)
- Smokers pay 50-100% higher premium
- Women get lower premiums than men (statistically lower mortality)
- Premium increases significantly with age — buy early

### 3. Policy Term

- Choose a term that covers you until retirement (age 60-65)
- Longer terms cost slightly more but provide extended protection

### 4. Payout Options

- **Lump sum** — Full amount paid at once
- **Monthly income** — Family gets regular monthly payments
- **Lump sum + monthly income** — Combination
- **Increasing cover** — Sum assured increases each year to beat inflation

## Riders (Add-On Covers)

| Rider | What It Covers | Should You Add? |
|-------|---------------|-----------------|
| Accidental Death Benefit | Extra payout if death is due to accident | Recommended |
| Critical Illness | Lump sum on diagnosis of serious illness (cancer, heart attack) | Highly recommended |
| Waiver of Premium | Premiums waived if you become permanently disabled | Recommended |
| Accidental Disability | Payout for total/partial permanent disability | Optional |
| Terminal Illness | Early payout if diagnosed with terminal illness | Often included free |

**Tip:** Don't overload with riders. Critical illness and waiver of premium are the most valuable.

## Online vs Offline Term Insurance

| Factor | Online | Offline (Agent) |
|--------|--------|-----------------|
| Premium | 30-40% lower | Higher (includes commission) |
| Buying process | Self-service, 15-30 min | Agent-assisted |
| Policy document | Digital (email/app) | Physical + digital |
| Claim process | Same | Same |
| Advice | Self-research needed | Agent guides you |

**Verdict:** Online is almost always better value. If you can read and compare, buy online.

## How to Buy Term Insurance Online — Step by Step

### Step 1: Calculate Your Cover Amount
Use the formula above. Aim for at least 10× your annual income.

### Step 2: Compare Plans
- Visit insurer websites or IRDAI-licensed aggregators
- Compare: premium, CSR, riders, payout options, policy term
- Check customer reviews for claim experience

### Step 3: Apply Online
1. Visit the insurer's website
2. Enter your age, income, cover amount, and policy term
3. Select payout option and riders
4. Fill personal and health details honestly (any misrepresentation can void the claim)
5. Upload documents: PAN, Aadhaar, income proof
6. Pay first premium via UPI/net banking/card

### Step 4: Complete Medical Test (if required)
- For cover above ₹50 lakh–₹1 crore, most insurers require a medical test
- The insurer arranges the test at your home or a designated lab — **free of cost**
- Tests typically include: blood tests, ECG, urine test, BMI check
- Some insurers offer "no medical test" plans for younger applicants with lower cover amounts

### Step 5: Policy Issuance
- After medical test clearance and document verification, the policy is issued (usually 5-15 days)
- You receive the policy document via email
- You get a **30-day free-look period** — if not satisfied, you can cancel and get a refund

## Important Tips

1. **Buy early** — A 25-year-old pays nearly half the premium of a 35-year-old for the same cover
2. **Never hide health conditions** — Non-disclosure is the #1 reason for claim rejection
3. **Nominate correctly** — Keep nominee details updated; inform your family about the policy
4. **Don't buy term insurance as investment** — It's pure protection; invest separately in mutual funds or PPF
5. **Review annually** — Increase cover if your income or liabilities grow significantly

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. What happens if I survive the policy term?
In a basic term plan, nothing is paid. Some insurers offer "Return of Premium" (TROP) plans where premiums are refunded on survival — but these cost 2-3× more and are generally not recommended.

### Q2. Can my claim be rejected?
Yes, if you provided incorrect or incomplete health/lifestyle information at the time of buying. Always disclose pre-existing conditions, smoking/drinking habits, and hazardous occupations honestly.

### Q3. Is a medical test always required?
Not always. For younger applicants (under 35-40) with cover up to ₹50 lakh–₹1 crore, some insurers offer no-medical-test plans. However, a medical test can sometimes get you a lower premium.

### Q4. Can I buy multiple term insurance policies?
Yes. There is no restriction. You can hold policies from different insurers. All valid policies will pay out independently on a claim.

### Q5. What is the free-look period?
IRDAI mandates a 30-day free-look period for online policies (15 days for offline). If you're not satisfied, you can cancel and get a refund minus proportionate risk charges and medical test costs.

### Q6. Should I buy term insurance from LIC or a private insurer?
Both are regulated by IRDAI. Private insurers often offer cheaper online plans with similar or higher CSRs. LIC has strong trust but slightly higher premiums. Compare and decide based on CSR, premium, and features.

### Q7. How much term insurance cover does a salaried person need?
At minimum, 10× your annual CTC. If you have a home loan, add the outstanding amount. If children are young, add their education costs. A cover of ₹1-2 crore is common for urban salaried professionals.
