# Family Pension After Death: How to Apply, Documents & Process

Family pension is a monthly payment made to the family of a deceased government employee or pensioner. If your spouse, parent, or family member was a central or state government employee (or was already receiving pension), you are entitled to family pension. This guide covers the complete process, documents, forms, and timeline.

## What is Family Pension?

Family pension is paid to eligible family members after the death of:
- A **government employee** (who died while in service), or
- A **government pensioner** (who was already receiving pension after retirement)

The pension is paid from the government treasury and managed by the **Central Pension Accounting Office (CPAO)** at [cpao.nic.in](https://cpao.nic.in/) for central government employees, or by the respective state AG (Accountant General) office for state employees.

### Family Pension Rates (Central Government)

| Period | Pension Rate |
|--------|-------------|
| First 7 years (or until pensioner would have turned 67, whichever is earlier) | **50% of last pay** |
| After the enhanced period | **30% of last pay** (minimum ₹9,000/month) |

**Note:** The minimum family pension is ₹9,000/month and maximum is ₹1,25,000/month (as per 7th Pay Commission). State government rates vary.

---

## Who is Eligible for Family Pension?

Family pension is paid in the following priority order:

1. **Spouse** (widow/widower) — first priority
2. **Children** — unmarried son below 25 years, unmarried/divorced/widowed daughter
3. **Disabled children** — if suffering from disorder/disability, pension continues for life
4. **Dependent parents** — if no eligible spouse or children

### Conditions
- Spouse must not have remarried (for continued eligibility — rule relaxed for central government w.e.f. 2024)
- The deceased must have completed at least **1 year of continuous service** (for death in service)
- For pensioners — family pension is automatic if the pensioner was receiving pension

## Documents Required

### If the Employee/Pensioner Was Already Retired

- **Death certificate** of the pensioner
- **Pension Payment Order (PPO)** — original copy
- **Application in Form 14** (for central govt) or prescribed state form
- **Bank details** of the claimant (passbook first page)
- **[Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)** of the claimant
- **Photographs** (passport size, 2–4 copies)
- **Marriage certificate** (to establish relationship)
- **Non-remarriage certificate** / self-declaration
- **Life certificate** of the claimant

### If the Employee Died While in Service

- **Death certificate**
- **Nomination form** (submitted by employee during service)
- **Service records** from the department
- **Last pay certificate (LPC)** from the office
- **Bank details** of the claimant
- **Aadhaar card** of the claimant
- **Marriage certificate** / proof of relationship
- **No objection from other family members** (if applicable)

## How to Apply for Family Pension

### Step 1: Collect the Death Certificate

Obtain the death certificate from the municipal corporation or Gram Panchayat. Apply within 21 days of death for free registration; late registration requires additional process.

### Step 2: Inform the Department/Bank

- **If pensioner (retired):** Inform the **pension-disbursing bank** immediately. Submit the death certificate to the bank
- **If employee (in service):** Inform the **Head of Office** of the employee's department

### Step 3: Submit Form 14 (Central Government)

For central government employees/pensioners, submit **Form 14** to:
- The **Head of Office** (if death in service), or
- The **Pension Disbursing Bank** (if pensioner was already retired)

Form 14 can be downloaded from [doppw.gov.in](https://doppw.gov.in/).

### Step 4: Submit Supporting Documents

Along with Form 14, submit:
- Death certificate (original + photocopy)
- PPO (original, if available)
- Bank passbook of the claimant
- Aadhaar card copy
- Photographs
- Non-remarriage declaration

### Step 5: Bank Processes the Claim

- The bank forwards the claim to **CPAO** (central govt) or **AG office** (state govt)
- CPAO/AG office verifies and issues a **Family Pension PPO** in the claimant's name
- Pension starts getting credited to the claimant's bank account

### Step 6: Track Status

- **Central government:** Track at [cpao.nic.in](https://cpao.nic.in/) → "Pensioner's Corner" → "Status of Pension"
- **EPFO pension:** Track at [epfindia.gov.in](https://epfindia.gov.in/)

## For EPFO (Private Sector) Family Pension

If the deceased was a private sector employee contributing to EPF:

1. **File Form 10-D** with the regional EPFO office or online at [unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in/)
2. Required: Death certificate, relationship proof, bank details, Aadhaar
3. **EPS pension** (Employees' Pension Scheme) provides family pension to the spouse
4. Minimum EPS family pension: ₹1,000/month

## Processing Time

| Stage | Timeline |
|-------|----------|
| Bank acknowledgement | 1–3 days |
| Document verification | 15–30 days |
| CPAO/AG office processing | 30–60 days |
| First family pension credit | 1–3 months from submission |
| PPO issuance | 2–4 months |

**Tip:** Arrears from the date of death are paid along with the first pension credit.

---

## Important Tips

1. **Inform the bank immediately** — The pensioner's bank account will be frozen after death. Inform the branch and submit documents quickly to avoid delays
2. **Keep the PPO safe** — The Pension Payment Order is the most important document. If lost, apply for a duplicate at CPAO/AG office
3. **Update nominee in bank** — Ensure the bank account nominee is updated to avoid complications
4. **Submit life certificate annually** — Family pensioners must also submit [Jeevan Pramaan](https://jeevanpramaan.gov.in/) every year
5. **Check for arrears** — Family pension is payable from the day after the pensioner's death. Ensure you receive arrears for any gap period

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. What is Form 14 for family pension?
Form 14 is the prescribed application form for claiming family pension after the death of a central government employee or pensioner. It must be submitted to the bank or Head of Office.

### Q2. How much is the family pension amount?
For central government, it is **50% of last pay** for the first 7 years, then **30% of last pay** (minimum ₹9,000/month). State government and EPFO rates differ.

### Q3. Can a son get family pension?
Yes, an unmarried son below 25 years can receive family pension if there is no eligible spouse. A disabled son can receive it for life regardless of age.

### Q4. Does family pension stop on remarriage?
For central government, the rules have been relaxed — family pension continues even after remarriage (w.e.f. 2024). State rules may vary.

### Q5. What is a PPO number?
PPO stands for **Pension Payment Order**. It is a unique identification number for your pension account. You need it to claim family pension and for all pension-related correspondence.

### Q6. How to apply for family pension for a private sector employee?
File **Form 10-D** with the EPFO regional office or online. The spouse receives EPS (Employees' Pension Scheme) family pension.

### Q7. What if the pensioner did not nominate anyone?
Family pension is paid in the order of priority: spouse first, then children, then dependent parents. Nomination is not mandatory for family pension — it is a statutory right.
