# Family Pension After Death: How to Apply & Documents Required

When a government employee or EPF member passes away, their family (spouse, children, or dependent parents) is entitled to **family pension**. This guide covers both **government employee family pension** (under CCS Pension Rules) and **EPF/EPS family pension** (under the Employees' Pension Scheme). Learn the complete application process, documents needed, and timelines.

## What is Family Pension?

Family pension is a monthly payment made to the family of a deceased government employee or pensioner. There are two main types:

### 1. Government Employee Family Pension (Central/State)
- Paid to the family of deceased central or state government employees
- Governed by **CCS (Pension) Rules, 2021** for central government
- Amount: 50% of last drawn pay (for first 10 years or up to 67 years of age, whichever is earlier), then 30% of last drawn pay

### 2. EPF/EPS Family Pension
- Paid to the family of deceased employees registered under **Employees' Pension Scheme (EPS-95)**
- Managed by **EPFO** (Employees' Provident Fund Organisation)
- Amount depends on years of service and average salary
- Minimum pension: ₹1,000/month

## Who is Eligible for Family Pension?

### Priority of Beneficiaries

1. **Spouse** (widow/widower) — first priority
2. **Children** (until age 25 or marriage, whichever is earlier; disabled children get lifelong pension)
3. **Dependent parents** (if no spouse or eligible children)

### Eligibility Conditions

- **Government pension:** Employee must have completed at least 1 year of continuous service (or died due to service-related cause)
- **EPF/EPS pension:** Employee must have completed at least 10 years of eligible service for pension benefit. If less than 10 years, withdrawal benefit is available instead
- Spouse must not have remarried (for government pension — remarriage stops family pension)
- Nomination made by the deceased is considered for determining beneficiary

## Family Pension Amount

### Government Employee Family Pension

| Period | Rate |
|--------|------|
| First 10 years or until deceased would have been 67 (whichever is earlier) | **50% of last drawn pay** (enhanced rate) |
| After the above period | **30% of last drawn pay** (normal rate) |
| Minimum family pension | ₹9,000/month + DA (as per 7th Pay Commission) |
| Maximum family pension | 30% of last drawn pay + DA |

### EPF/EPS Family Pension

| Scenario | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| If employee had 10+ years of service | Pension based on EPS formula |
| Minimum pension | ₹1,000/month |
| Widow pension | Monthly pension for lifetime (until remarriage) |
| Children pension | 25% of widow pension per child (max 2 children) |

**EPS pension formula:**
> Monthly pension = (Pensionable salary × Pensionable service) / 70

## Documents Required

### For Government Employee Family Pension
- **Death certificate** of the employee/pensioner
- **PPO (Pension Payment Order)** of deceased (if already a pensioner)
- **Form 14** (Application for family pension — available from the office/department)
- **Nomination form** (if filed by deceased)
- **Service book** / service records
- **Last pay certificate** (from the department)
- **Identity proof** of claimant: [Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online), [PAN card](/guide/pan-card-apply-online)
- **Bank account details** of claimant (passbook first page)
- **Marriage certificate** (to prove relationship)
- **Photographs** of claimant (passport size)
- **Undertaking/declaration** that claimant has not remarried

### For EPF/EPS Family Pension
- **Death certificate** of the member
- **Form 20** (EPF — for PF withdrawal by nominee/family)
- **Form 10-D** (EPS — for family pension claim)
- **Succession certificate** or **legal heir certificate** (if no nomination exists)
- **Identity proof** of claimant: Aadhaar, PAN
- **Bank account details** of claimant
- **Marriage certificate** / relationship proof
- **Member's UAN and EPF account details**
- **Photographs** of claimant

## How to Apply for Government Employee Family Pension

### Step 1: Obtain Death Certificate
Get the death certificate from the municipal corporation or gram panchayat. This is the first and most essential document.

### Step 2: Inform the Department
Notify the deceased employee's office/department immediately. The Head of Office (HOO) initiates the pension process.

### Step 3: Submit Form 14
1. Obtain **Form 14** from the department or download from [doppw.gov.in](https://doppw.gov.in/)
2. Fill in all details: deceased's service details, claimant's details, bank account
3. Attach all required documents
4. Submit to the **Head of Office**

### Step 4: Verification by Department
1. The Head of Office verifies service records, nomination, and documents
2. Issues **No Demand Certificate** (no outstanding dues)
3. Prepares pension papers and forwards to **Pay & Accounts Office** or **AG Office**

### Step 5: PPO Issuance
1. The Accounts office processes and issues a new **Pension Payment Order (PPO)** for family pension
2. PPO is sent to the nominated bank
3. Family pension starts from the month following the death

**Timeline:** 1-3 months if all documents are in order

## How to Apply for EPF/EPS Family Pension

### Step 1: Gather Documents
Collect death certificate, member's UAN details, and EPF account information.

### Step 2: Fill Required Forms
1. **Form 10-D** — for EPS family pension (monthly pension)
2. **Form 20** — for EPF accumulation withdrawal (lump sum)
3. Download from [epfindia.gov.in](https://epfindia.gov.in/) or the EPFO unified portal

### Step 3: Submit to EPFO
1. Submit filled forms with documents to the **regional EPFO office** where the member's PF account is maintained
2. If the member had a UAN and Aadhaar linked, the nominee can also submit a claim online through the [EPFO unified portal](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in/)

### Step 4: Online Claim (if UAN is available)
1. Login to EPFO member portal with the deceased's UAN
2. Go to **Online Services → Claim (Form 10-D / Form 20)**
3. Fill nominee/family details
4. Upload documents
5. Submit claim with Aadhaar OTP verification

### Step 5: Processing and Payment
1. EPFO verifies the claim
2. EPF accumulation (Form 20) is credited as lump sum
3. EPS pension (Form 10-D) is sanctioned as monthly pension

**Timeline:** 30-60 days for online claims; 2-4 months for offline

## Important Tips

1. **File nomination early** — Every employee should file nomination for PF and pension during service. This avoids legal heir certificate hassles later
2. **Apply immediately after death** — Family pension is payable from the next day of death. Delays in applying don't reduce entitlement, but cause payment delays
3. **Keep PPO safe** — The Pension Payment Order is a critical document. Keep the original safely and maintain a photocopy
4. **Jeevan Pramaan** — Family pensioners must submit annual life certificate. Use the [Jeevan Pramaan app](https://jeevanpramaan.gov.in/) or visit a CSC/bank
5. **Check for additional benefits** — Government employees' families may be eligible for CGEGIS, leave encashment, gratuity, and CGHS continuation

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Who gets family pension after death of a government employee?
**A:** The spouse gets first priority. If no surviving spouse, eligible children receive it. If no eligible children, dependent parents can claim it.

### Q2: How much family pension is paid?
**A:** For government employees, 50% of last drawn pay for the first 10 years (enhanced rate), then 30% (normal rate). For EPS, it depends on the pension formula with a minimum of ₹1,000/month.

### Q3: What is Form 14 in family pension?
**A:** Form 14 is the application form for claiming family pension of a deceased central government employee. It is submitted by the eligible family member to the Head of Office.

### Q4: Can family pension continue after remarriage?
**A:** For government employee family pension, remarriage of the spouse generally stops family pension. The pension then passes to eligible children. For EPS, similar rules apply.

### Q5: Is family pension taxable?
**A:** Yes, family pension is taxable as "Income from Other Sources" under the Income Tax Act. However, a standard deduction of ₹15,000 or 1/3rd of the pension (whichever is less) is available.

### Q6: What if no nomination was filed by the deceased?
**A:** If no nomination exists, the claimant must obtain a **legal heir certificate** or **succession certificate** from the court or tehsil. This can take 1-3 months additional time.

### Q7: Can children receive family pension?
**A:** Yes. Children can receive family pension until age 25 or marriage, whichever is earlier. Disabled children (with 40%+ disability) can receive lifelong family pension.

### Q8: How long does it take to start receiving family pension?
**A:** If all documents are in order: 1-3 months for government pension, 1-2 months for EPF online claims. Delays usually happen due to missing documents or lack of nomination.
