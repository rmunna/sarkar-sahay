# Electricity Bill Download Online: State-Wise Portal Links

You can view, download and print your electricity bill online through your state's DISCOM (Distribution Company) portal. This guide provides direct links to electricity bill portals for all Indian states and union territories, along with steps to check your bill using your consumer number.

## How to Download Your Electricity Bill Online

The general process is the same across most DISCOMs:

1. Visit your DISCOM's official website (links below)
2. Go to "View/Pay Bill" or "Bill Download" section
3. Enter your **Consumer Number** (printed on your physical bill)
4. View your current and past bills
5. Download as PDF or print directly

> **Consumer Number** is a unique identifier assigned by your DISCOM. It is printed on the top of your electricity bill. It may be called CA Number, Account Number, K Number or Service Number depending on your state.

## How to Find Your Consumer Number

Your consumer number is available on:
- **Physical electricity bill** — Printed at the top
- **Previous online bill** — Login to your DISCOM portal
- **Electricity meter** — Sometimes displayed on the meter
- **DISCOM customer care** — Call with your registered details

## How to Pay Electricity Bill Online

1. **DISCOM website** — Direct payment via net banking, UPI, cards
2. **UPI apps** — Pay via Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm using consumer number
3. **BBPS (Bharat Bill Payment System)** — Through any bank's net banking or app
4. **Mobile wallets** — Paytm, Amazon Pay, Freecharge

## Understanding Your Electricity Bill

Key components on your bill:
- **Consumer Number** — Your unique identifier
- **Billing Period** — Month/dates covered
- **Units Consumed** — kWh used (current reading - previous reading)
- **Tariff Slab** — Rate per unit based on consumption
- **Fixed Charges** — Monthly fixed charge based on connection load
- **Fuel Surcharge** — Additional charge for fuel cost variation
- **Electricity Duty** — State government levy
- **Total Amount Due** — Sum of all charges
- **Due Date** — Last date for payment without penalty

## Important Tips

1. **Save your consumer number** — You need it for every bill check and payment.
2. **Set up auto-pay** — Most DISCOMs support auto-debit via bank or UPI.
3. **Compare meter reading** — Cross-check the bill reading with your physical meter to catch errors.
4. **Apply for subsidy** — Many states offer subsidies for farmers, BPL families and SC/ST households.
5. **Register complaints online** — For wrong billing, use the DISCOM portal's grievance section rather than visiting the office.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: How do I download my electricity bill?
Visit your DISCOM's website, enter your consumer number, and click "View Bill" or "Download Bill." The bill is available as a downloadable PDF.

### Q2: Can I view old electricity bills online?
Yes. Most DISCOM portals allow you to view bill history for the last 6-12 months after logging in.

### Q3: What if I don't know my consumer number?
Check your old physical bill, call DISCOM customer care with your registered mobile number, or visit the nearest DISCOM office with your ID proof.

### Q4: Is there a penalty for late payment?
Yes. Most DISCOMs charge a late payment surcharge of 1-2% per month on the outstanding amount after the due date.

### Q5: How do I apply for a new electricity connection?
Visit your DISCOM's website and look for "New Connection" section. You will need identity proof, address proof and property ownership documents.

### Q6: Can I pay my electricity bill through UPI?
Yes. You can pay through any UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) by searching for your DISCOM and entering your consumer number.

### Q7: How do I register a complaint about wrong billing?
Use your DISCOM's online grievance portal or call the toll-free customer care number printed on your bill. You can also visit the nearest subdivision office.

### Q8: How can I check my gas bill online?
For piped natural gas bills, visit your gas company portal. See our [PNG connection guide](/guide/png-cng-connection-apply) for details.
