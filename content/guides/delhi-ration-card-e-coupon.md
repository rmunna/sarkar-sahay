# Delhi Ration Card & e-Coupon – Complete Guide

## What is the Delhi Ration Card & e-Coupon System?

The Delhi ration card is an essential document issued by the Department of Food, Supply and Consumer Affairs, Government of NCT of Delhi. It entitles eligible families to subsidized food grains (wheat, rice, sugar) from Fair Price Shops (FPS) under the National Food Security Act (NFSA).

The **e-Coupon system** is Delhi's digital initiative where beneficiaries receive electronic coupons instead of physical ration cards for collecting their monthly quota. This system ensures transparency and reduces fraud.

## Who is Eligible?

### For New Ration Card
- **Residency:** Must be a resident of Delhi
- **Income:** Family income should not exceed ₹1,00,000 per annum (for NFSA category)
- **No Duplication:** Should not hold a ration card in any other state
- **Family Unit:** One ration card per household

### Ration Card Categories in Delhi
| Category | Eligibility | Entitlement |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| Priority Household (PHH) | Families meeting NFSA criteria | 5 kg per person/month at ₹1-3/kg |
| Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) | Poorest of the poor | 35 kg per family/month at ₹1-3/kg |

## Documents Required

### For New Ration Card Application
- Aadhaar card of all family members
- Voter ID card
- Proof of residence in Delhi (electricity bill, water bill, rent agreement)
- Income certificate or self-declaration
- Passport-size photographs of the head of family
- Surrender certificate (if holding a ration card from another state)

### For e-Coupon
- Aadhaar card (mandatory for e-coupon authentication)
- Mobile number linked to Aadhaar
- Existing ration card number (if applicable)

## Step-by-Step: Apply for New Delhi Ration Card

### Online Process

1. **Visit the Portal:** Go to [https://nfs.delhi.gov.in](https://nfs.delhi.gov.in) or [https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in](https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in)
2. **Register:** Create a citizen account with your mobile number and Aadhaar
3. **Login:** Sign in with your credentials
4. **Select Service:** Choose "Apply for New Ration Card" under Food & Supply Department
5. **Fill Application:** Enter details of all family members, address, income details
6. **Upload Documents:** Upload scanned copies of required documents
7. **Submit:** Review and submit the application
8. **Note Application Number:** Save the application reference number
9. **Verification:** A food supply inspector will visit your address for verification within 15–30 days

### Offline Process

1. **Visit Circle Office:** Go to your local Food & Supply circle office
2. **Get Form:** Collect Form-N (application for new ration card)
3. **Fill Form:** Complete all details of family members
4. **Attach Documents:** Attach photocopies of all required documents
5. **Submit:** Submit at the circle office and get acknowledgment receipt
6. **Inspection:** Wait for the food inspector's verification visit

## How to Use the e-Coupon System

### Getting Your e-Coupon

1. **Check Eligibility:** Visit [https://nfs.delhi.gov.in](https://nfs.delhi.gov.in) and check if your ration card is mapped to the e-coupon system
2. **Link Aadhaar:** Ensure all family members' Aadhaar numbers are linked to the ration card
3. **SMS Notification:** You will receive an SMS with e-coupon details each month when your quota is available
4. **Visit FPS:** Go to your designated Fair Price Shop with your Aadhaar card

### Collecting Ration with e-Coupon

1. **Visit FPS** during operating hours (usually 8 AM – 1 PM)
2. **Authenticate** using Aadhaar biometric (fingerprint/iris) on the ePoS machine
3. **Verify Quantity** displayed on the ePoS machine
4. **Collect Ration** — wheat, rice, sugar as per your entitlement
5. **Collect Receipt** — get the transaction receipt from the FPS dealer

## Check Ration Card / e-Coupon Status

### Online
1. Visit [https://nfs.delhi.gov.in](https://nfs.delhi.gov.in)
2. Click on "Ration Card Details" or "e-Coupon Status"
3. Enter your ration card number or Aadhaar number
4. View your status, entitlement, and transaction history

### Via SMS
- Send your ration card number to the department's SMS service (check the NFS portal for the latest number)

## Processing Time

- **New Ration Card:** 30–45 days from application
- **Corrections/Updates:** 15–30 days
- **e-Coupon Activation:** 7–15 days after Aadhaar linking

## Important Tips

1. **Aadhaar Linking is Mandatory:** Without Aadhaar-ration card linking, you cannot use the e-coupon system or collect ration from FPS. Visit [our Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) if you need to get an Aadhaar card.
2. **Monthly Window:** Collect your ration within the designated monthly window. Uncollected ration does not carry over to the next month.
3. **FPS Complaints:** If the FPS dealer overcharges, gives less quantity, or is closed during operating hours, file a complaint on the NFS portal or call the Delhi food helpline.
4. **One Nation One Ration Card:** Delhi residents can also collect ration in other states under the [One Nation One Ration Card](/guide/one-nation-one-ration-card) scheme.
5. **Update Details:** If a family member is added or removed, update your ration card promptly to get the correct entitlement.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: How do I check if my name is on the Delhi ration card list?
**A:** Visit [https://nfs.delhi.gov.in](https://nfs.delhi.gov.in), click on "FPS wise Ration Card Details," select your area, and search for your name.

### Q2: Can I apply for a Delhi ration card if I have one from another state?
**A:** You must first surrender your ration card from the other state and get a surrender certificate before applying in Delhi.

### Q3: What if biometric authentication fails at the FPS?
**A:** If your fingerprint doesn't work, try iris authentication. If both fail, ask the FPS dealer to use the exception handling process. You can also visit the circle office to update your biometrics.

### Q4: Is the e-coupon different from the physical ration card?
**A:** The e-coupon is a digital layer on top of the ration card system. You still have a ration card number, but authentication and distribution are done digitally through Aadhaar and ePoS machines.

### Q5: Can tenants in Delhi get a ration card?
**A:** Yes. Tenants can apply with a rent agreement as address proof. The food inspector will verify during the home visit.

### Q6: What grains are available under Delhi's ration scheme?
**A:** Typically wheat (atta) at ₹2/kg, rice at ₹3/kg, and sugar at ₹13.50/kg for NFSA beneficiaries. Quantities depend on family size.

### Q7: How do I transfer my ration card to a different FPS in Delhi?
**A:** Visit [https://nfs.delhi.gov.in](https://nfs.delhi.gov.in) or your circle office to request an FPS transfer. You'll need to provide your new address proof.
