# Aadhaar Biometric Update Failed — How to Fix

Aadhaar biometric update — updating your fingerprints, iris scan, or photograph — can only be done at an Aadhaar Enrolment/Update Centre. If your update was rejected, failed at the centre, or the status shows "rejected" after submission, this guide explains every reason and fix.

---

## Why Is Biometric Update Important?

Your Aadhaar biometrics (fingerprints, iris, photo) are used for:
- **eKYC authentication** at banks, telecom companies, and government services
- **Aadhaar-based attendance** (government offices)
- **PDS ration distribution** via biometric verification
- **EPF, pension, and scheme withdrawals** requiring biometric authentication

If your biometrics are outdated (especially for children who enrolled before age 5 or 15), or worn/damaged fingerprints cause authentication failures, you need a biometric update.

> **UIDAI recommends** updating biometrics every 10 years and mandates it for children at ages 5, 10, and 15.

---

## Common Reasons for Biometric Update Failure

| Reason | Details |
|---|---|
| **Operator error at centre** | Enrolment operator captured poor-quality fingerprints or iris |
| **Document mismatch** | Supporting document name doesn't match existing Aadhaar record |
| **Duplicate biometric detected** | UIDAI system found your biometrics already linked to another Aadhaar |
| **Poor fingerprint quality** | Worn, damaged, or very dry/wet fingerprints couldn't be captured |
| **Centre equipment issues** | Biometric scanner malfunction, camera issues |
| **Incorrect form filled** | Wrong update type selected by operator |
| **Aadhaar locked** | You locked your biometrics and didn't unlock before visiting |
| **Network error at centre** | Centre's internet connection failed during upload |
| **Child mandatory update missed** | Update after age milestone rejected due to wrong process |

## Step 1: Check Your Update Request Status

1. Go to [Aadhaar Update Status](https://ssup.uidai.gov.in/CheckSSUPStatus/checkupdatestatus)
2. Enter your **Aadhaar number** or **Enrolment ID (URN)**
3. Enter the captcha and click **Check Status**

Status meanings:
- **Under Process** — Still being verified by UIDAI (takes 5-90 days)
- **Completed/Accepted** — Update successful
- **Rejected** — Update failed with a reason
- **Sent to Adjudicator** — Under manual review (biometric conflict detected)

If rejected, note the **reason code** — it determines your next steps.

---

## Step 2: Fix Rejected Updates by Reason

### Reason: "Poor Quality Biometrics"
Your fingerprints or iris scan didn't meet UIDAI quality standards.

**Fix:**
1. Visit the centre again (or a different, better-equipped centre)
2. **Prepare your hands** — wash with soap, dry thoroughly, apply light moisturizer if hands are very dry
3. Ask the operator to capture fingerprints slowly, pressing firmly and evenly
4. Ensure proper lighting for photo capture
5. Try a **government-run Aadhaar Seva Kendra** instead of private centres — they typically have better equipment

### Reason: "Biometric Duplicate Found"
UIDAI detected your biometrics match another Aadhaar number. This is a serious issue.

**Fix:**
1. This means either you have a duplicate Aadhaar or someone else's biometrics are similar
2. File a complaint at [UIDAI](https://uidai.gov.in/) → **Contact & Support** → **File a Complaint**
3. Visit your nearest **Aadhaar Seva Kendra** with original documents
4. UIDAI will investigate and may require you to appear for adjudication
5. The case goes to the **Adjudicator** who verifies your identity manually

### Reason: "Document Mismatch"
The name/details on your supporting document don't match your existing Aadhaar.

**Fix:**
1. If you're only updating biometrics (not demographics), you typically don't need supporting documents
2. If asked for documents, ensure the name matches exactly — use the same document that was used for original Aadhaar enrolment
3. If your name changed, first update your name/demographics online or at a centre, then do biometric update separately

### Reason: "Aadhaar Biometrics Locked"
You previously locked your biometrics for security.

**Fix:**
1. Go to [myAadhaar Portal](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/)
2. Login with Aadhaar + OTP
3. Go to **Lock/Unlock Biometrics**
4. **Unlock** your biometrics temporarily
5. Visit the enrolment centre for update
6. Re-lock your biometrics after the update is complete

See our [Aadhaar biometric lock/unlock guide](/guide/aadhaar-biometric-lock-unlock) for details.

## Step 3: Book an Appointment Properly

Don't just walk into a centre. Book an appointment to avoid long waits and ensure the centre is operational:

1. Go to [UIDAI Appointment Booking](https://appointments.uidai.gov.in/bookappointment.aspx)
2. Select your **city/location**
3. Choose **Biometric Update** as the service type
4. Select an available date and time slot
5. Enter your Aadhaar number and mobile number
6. Confirm the booking — you'll receive an SMS with appointment details

### Tips for Centre Visit
- Carry your **Aadhaar card** (original or printed e-Aadhaar)
- Bring a **valid supporting document** (any POI document from UIDAI's list)
- Reach on time — slots are limited
- **Fee: ₹100** for biometric update at centres (as per current UIDAI rates)

---

## Step 4: Choose the Right Type of Centre

| Centre Type | Quality | Wait Time | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Aadhaar Seva Kendra (ASK)** | Best equipment, trained operators | Moderate (appointment-based) | **Best choice** |
| **Government-run centres** (banks, post offices) | Good | Can be long | Good alternative |
| **Private enrolment centres** | Variable quality | Short | Avoid if previous update failed |

Find your nearest centre: [UIDAI Centre Locator](https://appointments.uidai.gov.in/easearch.aspx)

## Step 5: Mandatory Biometric Update for Children

UIDAI mandates biometric updates for children at specific ages:

| Age | What's Updated | Fee |
|---|---|---|
| **5 years** | All biometrics (fingerprint, iris, photo) | Free |
| **10 years** | All biometrics | Free |
| **15 years** | All biometrics (final mandatory update) | Free |

**If the child's update fails:**
1. Carry the child's **birth certificate** and parent's Aadhaar
2. Visit an Aadhaar Seva Kendra (better equipped for children)
3. Small children's fingerprints are often hard to capture — iris scan is the primary biometric for children under 5

## Step 6: File a Grievance If Update Keeps Failing

If your biometric update has been rejected multiple times:

### UIDAI Grievance
1. Call **1947** (UIDAI toll-free helpline)
2. Or email: **help@uidai.gov.in**
3. Or visit [UIDAI Contact Support](https://uidai.gov.in/) → Contact & Support

### File Online Complaint
1. Go to [UIDAI website](https://uidai.gov.in/)
2. Navigate to **Contact & Support → File a Complaint**
3. Select complaint type related to biometric update
4. Provide your Aadhaar number, URN (Update Request Number), and details
5. Upload rejection proof if available

### Regional UIDAI Office
For persistent issues, visit your **Regional UIDAI office**. Find the address on the UIDAI website under Regional Offices.

## Important Tips

1. **Moisturize your hands before visiting** — Dry, cracked, or calloused fingers are the #1 reason for fingerprint capture failure
2. **Go to Aadhaar Seva Kendra for retries** — They have the best equipment and trained operators
3. **Book an appointment** — Walk-ins often face rushed operators and poor-quality captures
4. **Unlock biometrics before visiting** — If you've enabled biometric lock, unlock it first via myAadhaar portal
5. **Keep the URN safe** — The Update Request Number on your receipt is needed to track status

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does Aadhaar biometric update cost?
**₹100** at Aadhaar Enrolment/Update Centres. Mandatory child updates at ages 5, 10, and 15 are **free**.

### How long does biometric update take to reflect?
Typically **5-30 days** after submission. Complex cases (adjudication) can take up to 90 days.

### Can I update Aadhaar biometrics online?
No. Biometric updates (fingerprint, iris, photo) **must be done in person** at an Aadhaar Enrolment/Update Centre. Only demographic details (name, address, DOB) can be updated online.

### What if my fingerprints don't get captured at all?
If fingerprints cannot be captured (due to age, medical condition, or manual labour), the operator should capture **iris scan** as the primary biometric. Inform the operator beforehand.

### Do I need to update biometrics every 10 years?
UIDAI recommends it for better authentication accuracy but hasn't made it mandatory for adults yet. It **is mandatory** for children at ages 5, 10, and 15.

### What documents are needed for biometric update?
For biometric-only update, you typically need just your **Aadhaar card** and the **₹100 fee**. If doing demographic + biometric update together, carry supporting documents (POI/POA from UIDAI's approved list).

### Can I update biometrics at any centre in India?
Yes. Aadhaar updates are not location-restricted. You can visit any Aadhaar Enrolment/Update Centre anywhere in India.
