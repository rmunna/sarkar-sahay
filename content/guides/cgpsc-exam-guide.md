# CGPSC Exam Guide — Chhattisgarh Public Service Commission

The Chhattisgarh Public Service Commission (CGPSC) conducts the State Services Examination for recruiting officers to various administrative posts in Chhattisgarh. Established in 2000 after the creation of Chhattisgarh, CGPSC has become an important recruitment body for state government positions.

## About CGPSC

CGPSC conducts exams for:

- **State Civil Services** — Deputy Collector, DSP, Naib Tehsildar
- **State Police Services** — Police administration posts
- **State Forest Services** — Forest officers
- **Other Posts** — Block Development Officer, Excise Inspector, and others

## Eligibility Criteria

### Age Limit

| Category | Minimum Age | Maximum Age |
|----------|------------|------------|
| General | 21 years | 40 years |
| OBC (CG) | 21 years | 43 years |
| SC/ST (CG) | 21 years | 45 years |
| PwD | 21 years | 45 years |
| Women (General, CG) | 21 years | 45 years |

Age is calculated as on 1st January of the year of notification.

**Note:** Chhattisgarh provides generous age relaxation, especially for women.

### Educational Qualification

- Bachelor's degree from a recognised university for State Services
- Technical posts require relevant professional qualifications

### Nationality

- Must be a citizen of India
- Chhattisgarh domicile required for reserved category posts

## CGPSC Exam Pattern

### Stage 1: Preliminary Examination

| Paper | Subject | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|-------|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| Paper 1 | General Studies | 100 | 200 | 2 hours |
| Paper 2 | Aptitude Test (CSAT) | 100 | 200 | 2 hours |

- Objective type (MCQ)
- **Negative marking:** 1/3 for wrong answers
- Paper 2 qualifying (33%) — only Paper 1 for cutoff

### Stage 2: Mains Examination

| Paper | Subject | Marks | Duration |
|-------|---------|-------|----------|
| Paper 1 | Hindi Language | 200 | 3 hours |
| Paper 2 | Constitution & Public Administration | 200 | 3 hours |
| Paper 3 | History, Geography & Sociology of Chhattisgarh | 200 | 3 hours |
| Paper 4 | Economy of Chhattisgarh & India | 200 | 3 hours |
| Paper 5 | Science & Technology | 200 | 3 hours |
| Paper 6 | Philosophy & Sociology | 200 | 3 hours |
| Paper 7 | General Studies (Essay) | 200 | 3 hours |

- Descriptive type
- Paper 1 (Hindi) is qualifying
- **Merit Papers Total:** 1200 marks (Papers 2–7)

### Stage 3: Interview

- **Marks:** 150
- **Total for final ranking:** 1350 (Mains 1200 + Interview 150)

## Syllabus Overview

### Prelims — General Studies

- Current affairs — national and Chhattisgarh
- Indian and Chhattisgarh history
- Geography — India and Chhattisgarh
- Indian Polity, governance, Constitution
- Economy — Indian and Chhattisgarh
- General science and technology
- Logical reasoning and mental ability

### Chhattisgarh-Specific Topics (Very Important)

CGPSC heavily tests Chhattisgarh-specific knowledge:

- **History:** Chhattisgarh from ancient to modern times, tribal history
- **Geography:** Chhattisgarh rivers (Mahanadi, Indravati), minerals, forests
- **Economy:** Mining, industrial development, state schemes
- **Tribal Culture:** Gond, Baiga, Oraon tribes and their traditions
- **Art & Culture:** Chhattisgarhi folk art, music, festivals (Bastar Dussehra, Madai)
- **State Schemes:** Government welfare and development programmes

## How to Apply

1. Visit [www.psc.cg.gov.in](https://www.psc.cg.gov.in)
2. Click on "Online Application" when notification is active
3. Register with email and mobile number
4. Fill in the application form with personal and educational details
5. Upload photograph and signature
6. Pay application fee online
7. Submit and save the confirmation

### Application Fee

| Category | Fee |
|----------|-----|
| General/OBC | ₹400 |
| SC/ST (CG) | ₹300 |
| PwD | Exempted |

**Payment Mode:** Online via Net Banking, Debit/Credit Card

## Important Dates Pattern

- **Notification:** Released roughly annually
- **Prelims:** 2–3 months after application deadline
- **Mains:** 3–6 months after prelims result
- **Interview:** 2–3 months after mains result
- **Final Result:** 1–2 months after interview

## Preparation Tips

### 1. Prioritise Chhattisgarh-Specific Content

Questions on Chhattisgarh history, geography, tribal culture, and economy carry significant marks. Prepare dedicated notes.

### 2. Hindi Language Preparation

Mains Hindi paper is qualifying — don't neglect it. Practice essay writing and comprehension in Hindi.

### 3. Cover Tribal Culture Thoroughly

Chhattisgarh has rich tribal heritage. Study major tribes, their customs, art forms, and festivals.

### 4. Standard Books + CG-Specific Material

Start with NCERTs and standard GS books, then add Chhattisgarh-specific publications.

### 5. Solve CGPSC Previous Papers

Past papers reveal the pattern and important recurring topics.

### 6. Essay Writing Practice

The essay paper carries 200 marks. Practice writing structured essays on social, economic, and current topics.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is CGPSC exam conducted every year?

CGPSC generally conducts the state services exam annually, though there can be delays. Check the official website for the latest notification.

### What is the medium of CGPSC exam?

Prelims is bilingual (Hindi and English). Mains can be written in Hindi or English.

### Is there negative marking in CGPSC prelims?

Yes, 1/3 of marks are deducted for each wrong answer in the preliminary examination.

### How many posts does CGPSC typically advertise?

State services exam typically has 100–200 posts covering various Group A and B positions.

### Can non-CG domicile candidates apply?

Yes, unreserved posts are open to all Indian citizens. Reserved category benefits require Chhattisgarh domicile.

### What is the starting salary for CGPSC officers?

State civil services officers start at approximately ₹56,100 basic pay (Level 10–13 depending on post) plus DA, HRA, and allowances.
