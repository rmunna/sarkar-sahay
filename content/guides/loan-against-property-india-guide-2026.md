# Loan Against Property (LAP) India 2026 — Complete Guide

If you own property and need a large loan (₹10L to several crores) for business or personal needs, a Loan Against Property offers significantly lower interest rates than personal loans, with tenure up to 15 years. The catch: your property is pledged as collateral — defaulting risks your home.

---

## What Is a Loan Against Property?

A Loan Against Property (LAP) is a **secured loan** where you:
- Pledge your residential or commercial property as collateral to the lender
- Get a loan of 50–70% of the property's market value
- Pay a lower interest rate than unsecured loans because the bank has collateral
- The property remains in your possession and use throughout the loan tenure

**Mortgage vs LAP**: LAP is technically a "mortgage loan" or "secured term loan." The term "mortgage" in India typically refers to any loan where property is pledged.

## LAP vs Other Loan Types

| Feature | LAP | Home Loan | Personal Loan |
|---------|-----|-----------|--------------|
| **Collateral** | Existing property (already owned) | Property being purchased | None |
| **Interest rate** | 9–13% | 8.35–10.5% | 10.5–24% |
| **LTV** | 50–70% of market value | 75–90% of property value | NA (income-based) |
| **Loan tenure** | Up to 15 years | Up to 30 years | Up to 7 years |
| **Loan amount** | ₹10L to ₹10Cr+ | Depends on purchase price | ₹50K–₹40L (typical) |
| **Fund use restriction** | None | Only for property purchase | None |
| **Processing time** | 2–4 weeks | 3–6 weeks | 1–7 days |
| **Best for** | Large amount, low rate, existing property owner | Buying a home | Quick, small, no property |

## Loan-to-Value (LTV) — How Much Can You Borrow?

LTV cap by property type:

| Property Type | Maximum LTV | Example |
|-------------|------------|---------|
| Residential property | 60–70% | ₹1Cr property → ₹60–70L loan |
| Commercial property | 50–60% | ₹1Cr office → ₹50–60L loan |
| Industrial property | 40–55% | Less preferred by lenders |
| Plot/land (vacant) | 40–50% | Only if clear title, urban/peri-urban |

**Market value vs registration value**: Lender sends their technical appraiser to assess market value. This may differ from the stamp duty registration value. Lender uses **their appraised value** (conservative) — not what you paid for the property.

## LAP Eligibility

### Salaried Employee

| Criteria | Typical Requirement |
|---------|-------------------|
| Age | 21–65 years (loan must close by 70) |
| Income | ₹30,000–₹50,000/month minimum (varies by city) |
| Employment | Minimum 2 years at current employer; 3+ total |
| CIBIL Score | 700+ (750+ for best rates) |
| Property ownership | Must own the property (sole or joint) |

### Self-Employed / Business Owner

| Criteria | Typical Requirement |
|---------|-------------------|
| Business vintage | 3+ years |
| ITR-declared income | Consistent for 3 years (banks average last 3 years) |
| Business stability | Positive P&L for 2+ years |
| CIBIL | 700+ |

## Documents Required for LAP

### Personal / Income Documents

**Salaried**:
- Last 6 months salary slips
- Form 16 (last 2 years)
- 6-month bank statements
- PAN + Aadhaar

**Self-Employed**:
- ITR 3 years (with CA computation sheet)
- Balance sheet and P&L (CA certified, 3 years)
- Bank statements all accounts (12 months)
- GST registration and returns

### Property Documents

- Original title deed / sale deed
- Encumbrance certificate (last 15 years minimum)
- Property tax receipts (latest)
- Approved building plan
- Occupancy certificate / completion certificate
- Society NOC (if apartment)
- Chain of title documents

## How LAP Is Processed

### Step-by-Step Timeline

1. **Application**: Submit online or at branch with income documents (Day 1)
2. **Processing / credit check**: Bank verifies income, CIBIL, existing obligations (Days 1–5)
3. **Property inspection**: Bank sends technical appraiser to assess market value (Days 3–7)
4. **Legal verification**: Bank's lawyer verifies title, encumbrances, building plan (Days 5–15)
5. **Sanction letter**: Bank issues sanction with approved amount, rate, tenure (Day 10–20)
6. **Documentation**: Loan agreement signed, mortgage registered at Sub-Registrar (Day 15–25)
7. **Disbursement**: Loan credited to your account (Day 20–30)

**Total time**: 2–4 weeks (faster if property title is clear and documents ready).

## Uses of LAP — No Restriction

Unlike home loans (property purchase only), LAP funds can be used for:

| Use Case | LAP Suitability |
|---------|----------------|
| **Business expansion** | Excellent — working capital, equipment, expansion |
| **Medical emergency** | Good — large amounts at lower rate than personal loan |
| **Children's education** | Alternative to education loan (no moratorium needed) |
| **Debt consolidation** | Replace high-rate debt with lower-rate LAP |
| **Wedding expenses** | Large one-time expense over years |
| **Home renovation** | With existing property as collateral |
| **Property purchase** | Less common (home loan better for this) |

**Note**: Some lenders may ask about the purpose but there is no legal restriction on use.

## LAP vs Loan Against Securities (LAS)

| Feature | LAP | LAS (Shares/MF) |
|---------|-----|----------------|
| Collateral | Physical property | Shares/mutual funds/bonds |
| LTV | 50–70% | 50–70% of portfolio |
| Rate | 9–13% | 9–11% |
| Liquidation risk | Property auction if default | Margin call / liquidation of securities |
| Processing | 2–4 weeks | 1–3 days |
| Amount | ₹10L–₹10Cr+ | Limited by portfolio size |

## Tax on LAP Interest

### For Business Borrowers

If LAP is used for business purposes, the **interest is a business expense** deductible under Section 37 (reduces taxable income).

### For Salaried/Personal Use

No tax deduction on LAP interest for personal use (unlike home loan where Section 24(b) allows ₹2L deduction — that's only for property purchase, not LAP).

**Exception**: If LAP proceeds are used for constructing/buying a residential property and LAP is secured against that or another property, interest may qualify under Section 24(b) — consult a CA.

## Risks of Loan Against Property

| Risk | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Property loss on default** | Non-payment → bank can attach and auction your property under SARFAESI Act without court order |
| **Long commitment** | 10–15 year tenure ties up your property as collateral for years |
| **Undervaluation by bank** | Bank's technical appraisal is conservative — you may get 50% of what you expected |
| **EMI burden on business loan** | Using LAP for business: business downturn + fixed EMI obligation = high stress |
| **Impact on sale** | Property cannot be freely sold while LAP is outstanding (requires NOC from lender) |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I take LAP on a property that already has a home loan?

If you have an existing home loan, the property is already mortgaged to the home loan lender. A second LAP (second charge) is possible only:
- From the same lender (they extend additional loan)
- From a second lender with the first lender's NOC

Second-charge LAP is uncommon and typically at higher rates.

### Can I foreclose a LAP early?

Yes. RBI guidelines on foreclosure charges:
- **Floating-rate LAP**: No prepayment penalty for individuals (same as home loan)
- **Fixed-rate LAP**: Lender may charge 1–2% prepayment penalty

### Can NRIs take LAP on Indian property?

Yes — NRIs can take LAP on Indian residential property. Income documentation involves foreign income proof, employment contract, NRE/NRO bank statements. More documentation, but feasible with major banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI).

### What happens if property value falls after LAP is taken?

The existing loan is unaffected — your EMI and terms don't change. The LTV issue matters only if you want to take a top-up loan later; if property value fell, bank won't sanction top-up until value recovers.
