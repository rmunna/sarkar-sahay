# UP Shadi Anudan Yojana 2026 — ₹20,000 Marriage Grant for Daughters

The **Uttar Pradesh Shadi Anudan Yojana** (विवाह अनुदान योजना) provides **₹20,000 financial assistance** to economically weaker families for the marriage of their daughters. The scheme covers families from **SC, ST, OBC, and General (EWS)** categories.

Applications are managed by three departments based on caste category:
- **OBC** — Other Backward Class Welfare Department
- **SC & General** — Social Welfare Department
- **ST** — Scheduled Tribe Development Department

## Grant Amount

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| **Grant per daughter** | ₹20,000 |
| **Maximum daughters per family** | 2 |
| **Maximum benefit per family** | ₹40,000 |
| **Payment mode** | Direct Bank Transfer (DBT) |

## Eligibility Criteria

To apply for UP Shadi Anudan, you must meet **all** of the following conditions:

### Applicant Requirements
- Must be a **permanent resident of Uttar Pradesh**
- Must be the **father or mother** of the bride (or legal guardian)
- Family must fall under **SC / ST / OBC / General EWS** category

### Income Limits

| Area | SC / ST / General | OBC |
|---|---|---|
| **Rural** | ≤ ₹46,460/year | ≤ ₹1,00,000/year |
| **Urban** | ≤ ₹56,460/year | ≤ ₹1,00,000/year |

### Bride & Groom Requirements
- **Daughter's age**: 18 years or above on the date of marriage
- **Groom's age**: 21 years or above on the date of marriage

### Application Window
- You must apply **within 90 days before or 90 days after** the marriage date

## Documents Required

Keep these documents ready before starting your application:

1. **Aadhaar Card** — of applicant and daughter
2. **Income Certificate** — issued by the competent authority
3. **Caste Certificate** — for SC/ST/OBC applicants
4. **Marriage Certificate / Invitation Card** — as proof of marriage
5. **Bank Passbook** — front page with account number and IFSC
6. **Daughter's Age Proof** — birth certificate or school certificate
7. **Passport-size Photographs** — of applicant and daughter
8. **Domicile Certificate** — proving UP residency

> 💡 You'll need an [income certificate](/guide/income-certificate-online) and [caste certificate](/guide/caste-certificate-online) — apply for these first if you don't have them.

## How to Apply Online — Step by Step

### Step 1: Visit the Official Portal
Go to **[shadianudan.upsdc.gov.in](https://shadianudan.upsdc.gov.in)**.

### Step 2: Select Your Category
On the homepage, click the appropriate link based on your caste:
- **सामान्य, अनुसूचित जाति, अनुसूचित जनजाति वर्ग** — for General / SC / ST
- **अन्य पिछड़ा वर्ग** — for OBC

### Step 3: Fill the Application Form
Enter the required details:
- Applicant's name, address, and contact info
- Daughter's name, date of birth, and marriage date
- Groom's details (name, age)
- Bank account details (must be in applicant's name)
- Income and caste details

### Step 4: Upload Documents
Upload scanned copies of all required documents. Ensure:
- File size is within the specified limits
- Documents are clearly legible

### Step 5: Submit and Save
- Review all details carefully
- Click **Submit**
- **Save/print the application number** — you'll need this to check status

## How to Check Application Status

1. Visit [shadianudan.upsdc.gov.in](https://shadianudan.upsdc.gov.in)
2. Click on **"आवेदन पत्र की स्थिति"** (Application Status)
3. Enter your **application number**
4. Click **Search** to view your current status

## Processing Time

| Stage | Estimated Time |
|---|---|
| Application verification | 15–30 days |
| District-level approval | 30–60 days |
| Fund disbursement | 60–90 days after approval |

> ⚠️ Processing times vary by district. Follow up with your District Social Welfare Office if there are delays.

## Important Points to Remember

- **Apply on time** — the 90-day window (before/after marriage) is strictly enforced
- **Bank account must be in applicant's name** and linked to Aadhaar
- **Maximum 2 daughters** per family can receive this benefit
- The scheme is **not applicable** for inter-family (same gotra) or child marriages
- **No duplicate applications** — applying more than once may lead to rejection

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I apply after the marriage?
Yes, you can apply **up to 90 days after** the marriage date.

### Is this scheme only for BPL families?
It's for economically weaker families within the income limits — ₹46,460 (rural) or ₹56,460 (urban) for SC/ST/General, and ₹1,00,000 for OBC.

### Can the daughter herself apply?
No, the application must be filed by the **parent or legal guardian**.

### What if my application is rejected?
Check the rejection reason on the portal. You can re-apply after correcting the issues, provided you're still within the 90-day window.

### Is the amount ₹20,000 or ₹51,000?
As per the official UP Shadi Anudan portal, the sanctioned amount is **₹20,000 per daughter**. Some third-party sources cite higher figures, but the official portal confirms ₹20,000.
