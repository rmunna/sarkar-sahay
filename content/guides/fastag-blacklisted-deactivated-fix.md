# FASTag Blacklisted or Deactivated? How to Reactivate

If your FASTag is not working at toll plazas and the barrier won't open, your tag may have been blacklisted or deactivated. This is a common issue faced by lakhs of vehicle owners across India. This guide explains why FASTags get blacklisted, how to check your tag status, and the exact steps to reactivate it.

## What Does "FASTag Blacklisted" Mean?

When a FASTag is blacklisted, it is flagged in the NETC (National Electronic Toll Collection) system as invalid. The toll plaza reader will detect the tag but reject the transaction. You'll be asked to pay cash and may face double toll charges.

A deactivated FASTag is similar — the tag exists in the system but has been disabled by the issuing bank or NHAI.

## Why Does a FASTag Get Blacklisted or Deactivated?

There are several reasons your FASTag may stop working:

### 1. Low or Zero Balance
- If your FASTag prepaid wallet balance drops below the minimum required amount, the bank may blacklist it
- Some banks blacklist after a single failed transaction due to low balance

### 2. KYC Not Completed or Expired
- NHAI mandates KYC verification for all FASTags
- If your KYC documents have expired or were never submitted properly, the tag gets deactivated
- This commonly happens with tags purchased at toll plazas with minimal documentation

### 3. Duplicate FASTag on Same Vehicle
- As per NHAI rules, only one active FASTag is allowed per vehicle registration number
- If a second FASTag is issued for the same vehicle, the older one is automatically blacklisted
- Sometimes both tags get blacklisted if the system detects a conflict

### 4. Bank Account Closure or Change
- If the bank account linked to your FASTag is closed or frozen, the FASTag gets deactivated

### 5. Vehicle Sold Without FASTag Deactivation
- When a vehicle is sold and the new owner gets a new FASTag, the old tag is blacklisted against that registration number

### 6. Issuer Bank Action
- Banks may deactivate tags for compliance or regulatory reasons
- Tags from certain banks that have lost their NETC licence get mass-blacklisted

## How to Check If Your FASTag Is Blacklisted

### Method 1: MyFASTag App
1. Download the **MyFASTag** app from Google Play Store or Apple App Store
2. Log in with your registered mobile number
3. Go to **My FASTags** section
4. Check the tag status — it will show as "Active", "Blacklisted", or "Deactivated"

### Method 2: NHAI Website
1. Visit the IHMCL/NHAI FASTag portal
2. Enter your vehicle registration number
3. The system will show the tag status and issuing bank details

### Method 3: Contact Your Issuing Bank
- Call the customer care number of the bank that issued your FASTag
- Provide your vehicle number and tag ID to check current status

## How to Reactivate a Blacklisted FASTag

### Step 1: Identify the Reason
Before attempting reactivation, find out why the tag was blacklisted. Call your issuing bank's FASTag helpline or check the MyFASTag app for details.

### Step 2: Fix the Root Cause

**If blacklisted due to low balance:**
1. Recharge your FASTag wallet to a minimum of ₹200-500 (varies by vehicle class)
2. The tag should automatically move from blacklist to active within 1-2 hours
3. If it doesn't, contact your bank to manually whitelist it

**If blacklisted due to KYC issues:**
1. Visit the nearest branch of your FASTag-issuing bank
2. Carry: Aadhaar card, vehicle RC (Registration Certificate), PAN card, passport-size photo
3. Submit the KYC form and updated documents
4. The bank will update KYC and reactivate the tag within 2-4 working days

**If blacklisted due to duplicate tag:**
1. Check how many FASTags are registered against your vehicle number
2. Decide which tag you want to keep active
3. Contact the issuing bank of the tag you want to deactivate and request closure
4. Ask the other bank to whitelist/reactivate the tag you want to keep
5. This process takes 3-7 working days

**If blacklisted due to bank account issues:**
1. Ensure your linked bank account is active and has sufficient balance
2. Visit the bank branch with your account details and FASTag information
3. Request relinking or reactivation

### Step 3: Request Reactivation
1. Call your FASTag issuing bank's dedicated helpline
2. You can also raise a request through the bank's internet banking or mobile app (if FASTag management is available)
3. Some banks allow reactivation through email — send your vehicle RC copy, tag ID, and request to the bank's FASTag support email

### Step 4: Verify Reactivation
- After the bank confirms reactivation, wait 2-4 hours for the NETC system to update
- Check status on the MyFASTag app
- Test at the next toll plaza you pass through

## Reactivation Timeline

| Reason | Expected Time |
|---|---|
| Low balance (after recharge) | 1-4 hours |
| KYC update | 2-4 working days |
| Duplicate tag resolution | 3-7 working days |
| Bank account relinking | 2-5 working days |
| Bank-initiated deactivation | 5-10 working days |

## What If Reactivation Is Not Possible?

In some cases, the old FASTag cannot be reactivated:
- If the issuing bank has stopped FASTag services
- If the tag is physically damaged
- If there are multiple unresolved disputes

In such cases, you need to **get a new FASTag**:
1. Close/deactivate the old blacklisted tag through the issuing bank
2. Get any remaining wallet balance refunded
3. Purchase a new FASTag from any authorised bank or Point of Sale
4. Link it to your vehicle RC and complete KYC

## Important Tips

1. **Maintain minimum balance** — Keep at least ₹200-500 in your FASTag wallet to avoid auto-blacklisting
2. **Complete KYC promptly** — If you bought a FASTag at a toll plaza, complete full KYC at the bank within 90 days
3. **One vehicle, one FASTag** — Never get a second FASTag for the same vehicle without deactivating the first
4. **Save your tag details** — Keep a record of your FASTag ID, issuing bank, and linked vehicle number
5. **Don't remove the sticker** — A damaged or improperly placed FASTag sticker won't scan at toll plazas

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Can I use my vehicle at toll plazas while my FASTag is blacklisted?
**A:** Yes, but you'll have to pay cash. Under NHAI rules, vehicles without a valid FASTag must pay double the toll fee at National Highway toll plazas.

### Q: How long does it take to reactivate a blacklisted FASTag?
**A:** It depends on the reason. Low balance issues are resolved in 1-4 hours after recharge. KYC and duplicate tag issues take 2-7 working days.

### Q: Can I reactivate my FASTag online without visiting the bank?
**A:** If the issue is low balance, yes — just recharge online. For KYC updates, you may need to visit the bank branch. Some banks allow document upload through their app.

### Q: My FASTag was working yesterday but suddenly stopped. What happened?
**A:** This usually happens due to a failed transaction that drained your balance below the minimum, or a system-level blacklisting due to duplicate tag detection. Check your balance and tag status on MyFASTag app.

### Q: I sold my car. Will the FASTag automatically get deactivated?
**A:** No. You should manually deactivate your FASTag and get the wallet balance refunded. The new owner should get a fresh FASTag linked to their name and the vehicle's RC.

### Q: Can I transfer my blacklisted FASTag to another vehicle?
**A:** No. FASTags cannot be transferred between vehicles. You need a new FASTag for each vehicle. See our [FASTag transfer guide](/guide/fastag-transfer-new-vehicle-owner) for details.

### Q: Is there a penalty for having a blacklisted FASTag on my windshield?
**A:** There is no specific penalty for having a blacklisted tag, but you'll be charged double toll as your tag won't be read as valid.
