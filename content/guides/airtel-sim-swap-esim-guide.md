# Airtel SIM Swap or eSIM: How to Activate

Lost your Airtel SIM card? Want to switch to an eSIM on your iPhone or Android phone? Airtel allows you to replace a damaged or lost physical SIM (SIM swap) or activate an eSIM on compatible devices. This guide covers both processes with documents, steps, and timelines.

## What is SIM Swap vs eSIM?

| Feature | SIM Swap | eSIM |
|---|---|---|
| **What** | Replace physical SIM (same number) | Digital SIM embedded in phone |
| **When** | Lost, stolen, damaged, or upgrading SIM size | Want to go SIM-less or use dual SIM |
| **Physical card** | Yes (new SIM issued) | No (activated digitally) |
| **Process** | Store visit or online request | Online via app or store |

## Airtel SIM Swap: Replace Lost or Damaged SIM

### Documents Required

- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) (for identity verification)
- Any other photo ID as backup (PAN card, voter ID, passport)
- FIR copy (recommended for stolen SIM, though not mandatory)

### Method 1: Visit an Airtel Store

1. Visit the nearest **Airtel Store** (not a retailer — must be an official Airtel store)
2. Carry your **Aadhaar card** and a valid photo ID
3. Fill the **SIM swap form**
4. Complete **biometric verification** (Aadhaar-based)
5. Pay the SIM swap fee (if applicable — usually **free to ₹25**)
6. You'll receive a new SIM card immediately
7. The new SIM will activate within **2-4 hours**

### Method 2: Request via Airtel Thanks App

1. Open **Airtel Thanks** app
2. Go to **Help → SIM Related → SIM Swap / Replacement**
3. Follow the on-screen instructions
4. You may need to visit a store for biometric verification
5. Alternatively, a delivery agent may bring the SIM to your address (available in select cities)

### Method 3: Call Customer Care

1. Call **121** from another phone (use a family member's Airtel number)
2. Or call **1800-103-4444** from any number
3. Request SIM swap — they'll guide you to the nearest store or arrange delivery

### After SIM Swap

- Your old SIM will be **deactivated immediately** once the new SIM is activated
- All your contacts stored on the old SIM will be lost — keep a cloud backup
- Your number, plan, and balance will remain the same
- If you had VoLTE or WiFi Calling enabled, you may need to re-enable them

## Airtel eSIM Activation

### Compatible Devices

**iPhone:** iPhone XS and later models (including all iPhone 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16 series)

**Android:** Select models from Samsung (Galaxy S20 and later, Z Flip/Fold series), Google Pixel (3 and later), and other flagships. Check [airtel.in/esim](https://www.airtel.in/esim) for the full list.

### Documents Required

- Registered Airtel mobile number (active)
- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) linked to the Airtel number
- eSIM-compatible device

### Method 1: Activate eSIM via Airtel Thanks App

1. Open **Airtel Thanks** app on your eSIM-compatible device
2. Go to **My Account → SIM Management → Convert to eSIM**
3. Verify your identity (OTP sent to registered number)
4. You'll receive a **QR code** or activation prompt
5. Go to your phone's **Settings → Mobile Network → Add eSIM / Add Cellular Plan**
6. Scan the QR code or follow the automatic activation
7. Your physical SIM will be deactivated once eSIM activates

### Method 2: Activate eSIM via SMS

1. From your registered Airtel number, send SMS:
   - **eSIM \<registered email ID\>** to **121**
2. You'll receive an email with a **QR code**
3. Scan the QR code from your device's eSIM settings
4. Follow on-screen instructions to complete activation

### Method 3: Visit Airtel Store

1. Visit an Airtel store with your device and Aadhaar card
2. Request eSIM activation
3. Complete biometric verification
4. Staff will help you activate the eSIM on the spot

### eSIM Activation on iPhone (Step-by-Step)

1. Go to **Settings → Cellular → Add Cellular Plan**
2. Scan the **QR code** provided by Airtel
3. Wait for the plan to download and activate
4. Label the line (e.g., "Airtel") if you have dual SIM
5. Set it as default for calls, data, or both

### eSIM Activation on Android (Step-by-Step)

1. Go to **Settings → Network & Internet → Mobile Network → Add Carrier**
2. Select **Scan QR Code**
3. Scan the Airtel eSIM QR code
4. Wait for activation (may take a few minutes)
5. Enable the new eSIM profile

## Important Tips

1. **Block your SIM immediately if lost** — Call **121** or use Airtel Thanks app to block the SIM before requesting a swap
2. **Backup contacts** — Contacts stored on SIM will be lost during swap. Use Google/iCloud backup
3. **eSIM can be transferred** — If you change phones, you'll need to re-activate the eSIM on the new device
4. **Only one active profile** — You can't use physical SIM and eSIM for the same Airtel number simultaneously
5. **Re-link banking apps** — After SIM swap, re-verify your number on UPI, banking, and OTP-dependent apps

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does Airtel SIM swap take to activate?

A SIM swap done at an Airtel store typically activates within **2-4 hours**. During this time, your old SIM stops working and the new SIM begins receiving network.

### Is Airtel SIM swap free?

SIM swap at Airtel stores is usually free or costs up to ₹25 depending on the store and SIM type (nano/micro). There is no charge for eSIM conversion in most cases.

### Can I convert my Airtel physical SIM to eSIM?

Yes. Use the Airtel Thanks app → My Account → SIM Management → Convert to eSIM. Your phone must be eSIM-compatible. The physical SIM will be deactivated after eSIM activation.

### Do I lose my number during SIM swap?

No. Your mobile number, active plan, and balance are retained. Only the physical SIM card changes. You'll have the same number on the new SIM.

### What if I don't have Aadhaar for SIM swap?

Aadhaar is the preferred document for biometric verification. If you don't have Aadhaar, carry a passport or voter ID. However, Aadhaar-based verification is the fastest and most widely accepted method.

### Can I use eSIM and physical SIM together?

Yes, if your phone supports dual SIM (eSIM + physical SIM). You can use one Airtel eSIM and one physical SIM from another operator, or vice versa. You cannot have two profiles for the same Airtel number active simultaneously.

### How to transfer Airtel eSIM to a new phone?

You'll need to deactivate the eSIM on the old phone and re-activate it on the new phone. Use the Airtel Thanks app or visit a store. You'll receive a new QR code for the new device.
