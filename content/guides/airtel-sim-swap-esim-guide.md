# Airtel SIM Swap or eSIM: How to Activate

Need to replace a damaged Airtel SIM, activate an eSIM, or upgrade to a 5G SIM? This guide covers all SIM swap and eSIM activation methods with complete steps for both iPhone and Android users.

## When Do You Need a SIM Swap?

- **Damaged or broken SIM** — SIM card is physically damaged
- **Lost or stolen SIM** — need a replacement on the same number
- **SIM size change** — switching from standard to nano SIM (or vice versa)
- **4G to 5G upgrade** — older SIM needs replacement for 5G support
- **Moving to eSIM** — switching from physical SIM to embedded SIM

---

## Method 1: Physical SIM Replacement (SIM Swap)

### Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (original for verification)
- **Old SIM card** (if available — not mandatory for lost SIM)
- **SIM swap form** (available at the store)

### Steps

1. **Visit the nearest Airtel store** — find one at [airtel.in/store](https://www.airtel.in/)
2. **Carry your Aadhaar card** — original is required for biometric verification
3. **Fill the SIM swap form** at the store
4. **Complete Aadhaar-based biometric verification** (fingerprint)
5. **Receive the new SIM** — it will have the same mobile number
6. **Wait for activation** — the new SIM activates within **2-4 hours**
7. You will receive an SMS confirming the SIM swap

### Fees

| Type | Fee |
|------|-----|
| SIM replacement (damaged/lost) | Free to ₹25 (varies by store) |
| SIM size change (nano/micro) | Free at most stores |
| 4G to 5G SIM upgrade | Free |

**Note:** During the swap process, your old SIM will stop working. The new SIM will activate within 2-4 hours. During this time, you won't be able to make or receive calls.

## Method 2: eSIM Activation

An eSIM is a digital SIM embedded in your phone — no physical card needed. Airtel supports eSIM on compatible devices.

### Compatible Devices (Partial List)

**iPhone:** iPhone XR, XS, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16 series and newer
**Samsung:** Galaxy S20 and above, Galaxy Z Fold/Flip series
**Google Pixel:** Pixel 3 and above
**Other:** OnePlus, Motorola (select models with eSIM support)

### Method 2A: New eSIM Activation (Converting Physical SIM to eSIM)

#### For iPhone

1. Ensure your iPhone is connected to WiFi
2. Go to **Settings** → **Cellular** → **Add eSIM** (or **Add Cellular Plan**)
3. Select **Transfer from Nearby iPhone** or **Use QR Code**
4. If using QR code method, you'll need the QR from Airtel (see below)

#### For Android

1. Go to **Settings** → **Network & Internet** → **SIMs** → **Add eSIM**
2. Select **Scan QR code**
3. Scan the Airtel eSIM QR code

### How to Get the Airtel eSIM QR Code

**Method A: Airtel Thanks App**
1. Open the Airtel Thanks app
2. Go to **Account** → **SIM & Network Settings** → **Convert to eSIM**
3. Follow the on-screen instructions
4. A QR code will be displayed or sent to your registered email
5. Scan the QR code on your device

**Method B: SMS Method**
1. From your Airtel number, SMS: **eSIM <registered email ID>** to **121**
   - Example: `eSIM user@email.com`
2. You will receive a confirmation SMS
3. Reply with **1** to confirm
4. The eSIM QR code will be sent to your email within a few minutes
5. Open the email on another device and scan the QR code on your phone

**Method C: Visit Airtel Store**
1. Visit any Airtel store with your Aadhaar card
2. Request eSIM activation
3. The store executive will process the conversion and provide the QR code

### eSIM Activation Fee

| eSIM Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| Physical SIM to eSIM conversion | Free |
| New eSIM activation | Free |
| eSIM to physical SIM (reversal) | Free (at Airtel store) |

## Method 3: 4G to 5G SIM Upgrade

If you have an older Airtel 4G SIM and want to use 5G:

1. **Check eligibility:** Open Airtel Thanks app → search "5G" or check if your area has 5G coverage
2. **Visit Airtel store** with your Aadhaar card
3. Request a **free 5G SIM upgrade**
4. Complete biometric verification
5. The new 5G SIM will activate within 2-4 hours
6. Ensure your phone supports 5G and select 5G in network settings

**Note:** You can also convert directly to eSIM which supports 5G automatically on compatible devices.

## Important Tips

1. **Backup OTP-linked services** — during SIM swap, you won't receive OTPs for 2-4 hours. Plan accordingly.
2. **eSIM + Physical SIM** — on dual-SIM phones, you can use eSIM for Airtel and keep the physical SIM slot for another operator.
3. **eSIM transfer between phones** — when switching phones, you need to re-do the eSIM activation process. The eSIM doesn't transfer automatically.
4. **Keep your Aadhaar linked** — SIM swap requires Aadhaar biometric verification. Ensure your mobile number is linked to Aadhaar.
5. **Report lost SIM immediately** — call 198 to block your SIM before requesting a replacement to prevent misuse.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does Airtel SIM swap take?
The new SIM activates within 2-4 hours after the swap is processed at the store. During this time, both old and new SIMs will not work.

### Can I activate Airtel eSIM at home without visiting a store?
Yes. You can activate eSIM through the Airtel Thanks app or by sending an SMS to 121. You'll receive a QR code on your registered email that you can scan from home.

### Is there a charge for Airtel SIM swap or eSIM?
SIM replacement and eSIM activation are generally free. Some retail stores may charge up to ₹25 for a physical SIM card.

### Can I convert back from eSIM to physical SIM?
Yes. Visit an Airtel store with your Aadhaar card and request a physical SIM. The conversion is free and takes 2-4 hours.

### Will I lose my number during SIM swap?
No. Your mobile number remains the same after a SIM swap or eSIM activation. Only the SIM card changes.

### How do I check if my phone supports eSIM?
On iPhone, go to Settings → Cellular — if you see "Add eSIM" or "Add Cellular Plan," your phone supports eSIM. On Android, go to Settings → Network & Internet → SIMs and look for an eSIM option.
