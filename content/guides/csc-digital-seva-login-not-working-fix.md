# CSC Digital Seva Portal Login Not Working? How to Fix Common Errors

CSC (Common Service Centre) operators — Village Level Entrepreneurs (VLEs) — depend on the Digital Seva portal daily to deliver government services to citizens. When login fails, it directly affects their livelihood. Here's how to fix every common login issue.

## Common Error Messages

- **"Invalid Username or Password. Please try again."**
- **"Your account has been temporarily locked."**
- **"OTP not received for login verification."**
- **"Your CSC ID is not activated yet."**
- **"Access Denied — Your session has expired."**
- **"Biometric authentication failed."**
- **"The page cannot be displayed — server error."**
- **"Your VLE application is under process."**
- **"Certificate error — This site is not secure."**
- **"Your wallet balance is insufficient."**
- **"Digital Seva portal is under maintenance."**

## Fix 1: Invalid Username or Password

**Your CSC login ID** is usually your **CSC ID number** (e.g., 110123456789) — not your email or name.

**Steps:**
1. Go to [digitalseva.csc.gov.in](https://digitalseva.csc.gov.in)
2. Enter your **CSC ID** (12-digit number) as username
3. Enter your password (case-sensitive — check Caps Lock)
4. If forgotten, click **"Forgot Password"**
5. Enter your CSC ID and registered mobile/email
6. Receive OTP and reset password
7. New password requirements: 8+ characters, uppercase, lowercase, number, special character

## Fix 2: Account Locked After Multiple Attempts

1. **Wait 30 minutes** — the lock auto-releases
2. After 30 minutes, use **"Forgot Password"** to reset
3. Don't keep trying wrong passwords — each failed attempt extends the lock
4. If still locked after 1 hour, contact **CSC helpdesk: 1800-121-3468**

## Fix 3: OTP Not Received

1. Ensure you're using the **mobile number registered with CSC** (not your personal number if different)
2. Check if **DND is enabled** — disable it by calling your operator or sending SMS "STOP DND" to 1909
3. Wait at least **5 minutes** — CSC OTPs can be delayed during peak hours
4. Check network signal — CSC centres in rural areas may have weak connectivity
5. Try **email OTP** if mobile OTP fails
6. If OTP consistently fails, contact CSC district manager to update your mobile number

## Fix 4: CSC ID Not Activated

If you recently applied to become a VLE:
1. Check application status at [register.csc.gov.in](https://register.csc.gov.in)
2. Login with your **application reference number**
3. If status shows **"Under Process"**, wait — it takes **30-60 days** for approval
4. If status shows **"Approved"** but you can't login, your ID needs to be activated by the **District Manager**
5. Contact your state CSC team — find contact details at [csc.gov.in](https://csc.gov.in)

## Fix 5: Biometric Authentication Failed

Many CSC services require biometric authentication via Aadhaar:

1. Ensure your **biometric device (IRIS scanner / fingerprint device)** is properly connected
2. Install the latest **RD (Registered Device) Service** for your device:
   - Mantra: [mantra.com.in](https://www.mantra.com.in)
   - Morpho/IDEMIA: [idemia.com](https://www.idemia.com)
   - Startek: [startek.co.in](https://www.startek.co.in)
3. Check if the device shows **"Ready"** status in the RD Service
4. Clean the fingerprint scanner surface
5. Try **different fingers** — index finger and thumb have highest success rates
6. If fingerprints don't work, try **IRIS scanner** if available
7. Ensure your internet speed is at least **1 Mbps** for biometric authentication

## Fix 6: Portal Not Loading / Server Error

1. **Check portal status** — visit [csc.gov.in](https://csc.gov.in) for maintenance announcements
2. **Clear browser cache:** `Ctrl + Shift + Delete` > Clear all browsing data
3. **Use Google Chrome** — the CSC portal works best on Chrome
4. **Disable pop-up blockers** for digitalseva.csc.gov.in
5. **Try incognito mode:** `Ctrl + Shift + N`
6. **Check internet connection** — run a speed test; need minimum 2 Mbps
7. **Try during off-peak hours** — early morning (7-9 AM) or late evening (after 8 PM)
8. If using a **proxy or VPN**, disable it

## Fix 7: Certificate / Security Errors

If you see "This site is not secure" or certificate warnings:

1. Check your **computer's date and time** — incorrect date causes certificate errors
2. Clear **SSL cache:** Chrome > Settings > Privacy > Security > Clear SSL state
3. Update your browser to the latest version
4. Try a different browser
5. If your antivirus is blocking the site, add digitalseva.csc.gov.in to the **whitelist**

## Fix 8: Wallet Balance Issues

CSC services require wallet balance. If facing wallet issues:

1. Login and check balance: **My Account > Wallet Balance**
2. To recharge: **My Account > Recharge Wallet**
3. Minimum recharge: ₹500 via net banking or debit card
4. If payment deducted but wallet not updated, wait **24 hours**
5. For stuck payments, raise a ticket under **Support > Raise Complaint**
6. Keep transaction reference number for all wallet recharges

## Tips for CSC Operators

- **Bookmark the login page** — don't search for it on Google (phishing sites exist)
- **Save your CSC ID** in a secure place — you need it for everything
- **Update your password every 90 days** — CSC may force a reset
- **Use a UPS/inverter** — power cuts during biometric auth cause failed transactions
- **Maintain minimum ₹2,000 wallet balance** — avoid service interruptions
- **Keep RD Service updated** — outdated biometric drivers cause 80% of authentication failures

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I forgot my CSC ID number. How do I find it?
Check your **CSC registration email** — your CSC ID was sent when your application was approved. You can also contact your **District Manager** or call CSC helpdesk at 1800-121-3468 with your registered mobile number.

### Can I use the CSC portal on my mobile phone?
The CSC Digital Seva portal is **optimized for desktop/laptop**. While it opens on mobile, many services (especially biometric authentication) require a computer. Use the **CSC DigiPay app** for basic Aadhaar-enabled payment services on mobile.

### My CSC centre was deactivated. How do I reactivate it?
Contact your **District Manager** with your CSC ID and explain the reason for deactivation. If it was due to inactivity, you may need to reapply. If due to compliance issues, resolve them first and request reactivation.

### Why does biometric authentication fail repeatedly?
Common causes: outdated RD Service driver (most common), dirty fingerprint scanner, dry/damaged fingertips, slow internet, or Aadhaar biometrics locked. Update your device driver and try IRIS authentication if fingerprints fail.

### Is there a CSC app for Android?
Yes, CSC has multiple apps including **CSC DigiPay**, **CSC Grameen eStore**, and **CSC BhIM Aadhaar**. Download from Google Play Store. However, the full Digital Seva portal services are only available via the web portal.

### Can two VLEs share one CSC ID?
No, each **CSC ID is unique** to one VLE. Sharing CSC IDs violates the VLE agreement and can result in deactivation. Each operator must have their own registered CSC ID.
