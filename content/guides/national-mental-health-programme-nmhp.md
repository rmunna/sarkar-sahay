## What is the National Mental Health Programme (NMHP)?

The National Mental Health Programme (NMHP) is a Government of India initiative to provide **free, accessible mental health care** to all citizens — especially those in rural and underserved areas. The programme's key component is the **District Mental Health Programme (DMHP)** which integrates mental health services into every district hospital.

Under DMHP and NMHP:
- Free **psychiatry outpatient (OPD)** services at district hospitals
- Free **medicines** for mental illness (antidepressants, antipsychotics)
- **Counselling and psychotherapy** (in some hospitals)
- **Tele-mental health services** (TELE-MANAS helpline: 14416)
- Mental health emergency services

## Who is Eligible?

**All Indian citizens.** There is no income limit, no registration, no BPL requirement. Mental health services at government facilities are free for everyone.

---

## How to Access Free Mental Health Services

### Option 1 — Visit District Hospital / Government Hospital

1. Go to your **District Hospital** or **Government Medical College**
2. Register at the **OPD reception** (free registration)
3. Ask for the **Psychiatry OPD** or **DMHP centre**
4. See the psychiatrist or DMHP-trained medical officer
5. Collect prescribed **free medicines** from the government pharmacy

### Option 2 — TELE-MANAS (National Helpline)

**Call 14416** — India's National Tele Mental Health Helpline (operational 24×7, available in multiple languages)

What TELE-MANAS provides:
- Immediate counselling for distress, anxiety, depression
- Referral to nearby DMHP facility
- Follow-up calls from mental health professionals

### Option 3 — NIMHANS and Government Psychiatric Hospitals

For severe mental illness:
- **NIMHANS, Bengaluru** — [nimhans.ac.in](https://nimhans.ac.in)
- **IHBAS Delhi** — Institute of Human Behaviour and Allied Sciences
- **RINPAS, Ranchi** — Ranchi Institute of Neuro-Psychiatry
- State-run psychiatric hospitals in each state capital

## Conditions Treated Under NMHP

| Condition | Available treatment |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Depression | Medicines + counselling |
| Anxiety disorders | Medicines + CBT |
| Schizophrenia | Long-term antipsychotics |
| Bipolar disorder | Mood stabilizers + follow-up |
| Substance use disorder | De-addiction services (separate DST programme) |
| Childhood/adolescent mental health | Referral to child psychiatry OPD |
| Dementia (elderly) | Referral to geriatric psychiatry (available at large hospitals) |

## TELE-MANAS — National Tele Mental Health Programme

TELE-MANAS was launched in 2022 specifically to scale mental health access:
- **Helpline number:** 14416
- **Languages:** 20+ Indian languages
- **Available:** 24 hours, 7 days a week
- **Services:** First-level counselling, referrals, e-prescriptions in some states

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. Is the psychiatrist consultation really free at government hospitals?**

Yes. At government district hospitals and medical college hospitals under DMHP, consultation is free. Medicines from the government pharmacy are also free (from the NMHP medicine kit, which includes common psychiatric medications).

**2. I am depressed but afraid of social stigma. Can I call the helpline anonymously?**

Yes. TELE-MANAS counsellors maintain confidentiality. You do not have to give your name. You can call 14416 and speak in your regional language.

**3. I have schizophrenia and need regular medicines. Can I get them free?**

Yes. Under DMHP, antipsychotic medicines including risperidone, olanzapine, and haloperidol are in the government essential medicines list and dispensed free at district hospitals.

**4. My village has no psychiatrist. How do I get help?**

Call **14416** (TELE-MANAS) first. You will be connected to a counsellor and then referred to the nearest DMHP facility. Some states have mobile mental health units that visit remote areas periodically.

**5. I need therapy (CBT/talk therapy), not just medicines. Is that available free?**

Clinical Psychologists and counsellors are part of DMHP teams at district hospitals. Availability varies by state and district. Ask the DMHP centre head for psychotherapy appointments.
