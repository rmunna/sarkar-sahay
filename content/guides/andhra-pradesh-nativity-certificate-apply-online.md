# Andhra Pradesh Nativity Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **nativity certificate** in Andhra Pradesh proves your nativity/domicile in AP and is required for EAMCET AP state quota engineering/medical admissions, NEET AP state quota counselling, AP government job applications (APPSC), and state scheme eligibility. Like Telangana, AP uses a **10-year residence OR school study-based** eligibility for state quota. Applications go through **MeeSeva** or **Ward/Village Secretariat**.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at any **MeeSeva centre** or [meeseva.gov.in](https://meeseva.gov.in). Issued by MRO/Tahsildar within **7–21 days**.

---

## AP State Quota Eligibility — EAMCET/NEET

For **EAMCET AP state quota** (85% engineering/medical seats):

| Route | Requirement |
|-------|------------|
| **Residence-based** | 10 years continuous residence in AP |
| **Study-based** | Studied in AP schools for **7 years** (any combination from Class 1–12) |
| **Local area** | Special local area quotas within AP based on district study |
| **Parents** | If parents are AP locals (10-year residents) |

> AP EAMCET has district-level **local area quotas** (70% local, 15% non-local within AP, 15% all-India). Verify the current year's APSCHE EAMCET notification for specific local area definitions.

## Why You Need AP Nativity Certificate

- **EAMCET AP state quota** — 85% engineering, pharmacy, agriculture seats
- **NEET AP state quota** — 85% government medical college seats (KNRUHS, NTR Health University)
- **APPSC (AP PSC)** — government job reservation for AP residents
- **AP scholarships** — BC/SC/ST welfare scholarships for AP residents
- **AP government schemes** — Jagananna, YSR schemes eligibility
- **AP Jagananna Vidya Deevena** — fee reimbursement for AP domicile students

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory)
- **Ration card** (White/NFSA card showing AP address)
- **Voter ID** (AP voter enrollment)
- **School Transfer Certificates (TCs)** from AP schools
- **Pattadar Passbook / land records** (if claiming local through land in AP)
- **Utility bills** (electricity/water showing AP address)
- Self-declaration of AP nativity/residence
- Passport-size photograph

## How to Apply via MeeSeva

**Method 1: MeeSeva Kiosk**

1. Visit nearest **MeeSeva centre**
2. Ask for **"Nativity Certificate"** or **"Domicile Certificate"**
3. Provide Aadhaar, ration card, school TCs, voter ID
4. Operator submits application
5. Fee: approximately **₹35–60**
6. Receive acknowledgement

**Method 2: MeeSeva Online**

1. Go to [meeseva.gov.in](https://meeseva.gov.in)
2. Log in / register
3. Search **"Nativity Certificate"**
4. Fill form, upload documents, submit

---

## Apply via Ward/Village Secretariat (Free)

AP's **Ward Secretariat** (urban) and **Village Secretariat** (rural) provide free assistance:
1. Visit your **Ward/Village Secretariat**
2. Submit nativity certificate application with documents
3. No charge at the secretariat
4. Application forwarded to MRO for approval

## Status Check & Download

1. Log in to [meeseva.gov.in](https://meeseva.gov.in) → **"My Applications"**
2. Once approved: download digitally signed PDF
3. Valid for EAMCET, NEET AP counselling, APPSC applications

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I studied in Andhra Pradesh schools for 8 years — am I AP local?

If you studied in **AP schools for 7+ years** (in any combination of Classes 1–12), you qualify for AP state quota through the study-based route. Collect Transfer Certificates (TCs) from your AP schools as proof.

### AP nativity for Jagananna Vidya Deevena fee reimbursement?

For **Jagananna Vidya Deevena** (fee reimbursement for BC/SC/ST/EBC students), you need: AP nativity/domicile + caste certificate + income certificate. Apply at the APOSS scholarship portal or through your college.

### Andhra Pradesh students in Telangana — EAMCET AP state quota?

If you are an AP native studying in Telangana for your intermediate (Class 11–12), you may still qualify for AP EAMCET state quota if you can prove AP nativity/domicile through your earlier schooling in AP or parent's AP domicile. Verify with APSCHE notification.
