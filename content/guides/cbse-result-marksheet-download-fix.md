# CBSE Result Not Showing? Fix Marksheet Download Issues

Every year, millions of students check their CBSE Class 10 and Class 12 board exam results online. Due to heavy traffic, the result portal often crashes or shows errors. This guide covers all the ways to check your CBSE result, download your marksheet, and fix common issues.

## How to Check CBSE Result Online

### Method 1: CBSE Official Result Portal

1. Visit [results.cbse.nic.in](https://results.cbse.nic.in/) or [cbseresults.nic.in](https://cbseresults.nic.in/)
2. Select your exam — **Class 10** or **Class 12**
3. Enter your **Roll Number** (7 digits), **School Number** (5-7 digits), and **Date of Birth**
4. Enter the security code (captcha)
5. Click **Submit** to view your result
6. Take a screenshot or print the result page

### Method 2: DigiLocker

DigiLocker provides the official digital marksheet issued by CBSE:

1. Visit [digilocker.gov.in](https://www.digilocker.gov.in/) or download the DigiLocker app
2. Log in with your Aadhaar-linked mobile number
3. Go to **Issued Documents** section
4. Search for **CBSE** under the education category
5. Enter your roll number and year of passing
6. Your digital marksheet will appear — download as PDF

**Tip:** DigiLocker marksheets are legally valid and equivalent to original marksheets under the IT Act.

### Method 3: Check Result via SMS

If the website is not loading, you can check your result via SMS:

- **Class 10:** Send SMS `CBSE10 <ROLLNUMBER> <DOB>` to **7738299899**
- **Class 12:** Send SMS `CBSE12 <ROLLNUMBER> <DOB>` to **7738299899**

Date of birth format: DDMMYYYY (e.g., 15031995)

### Method 4: UMANG App

1. Download the UMANG app from Google Play or App Store
2. Search for **CBSE Results**
3. Enter your roll number and other details
4. View and download your result

## Common CBSE Result Errors and Fixes

### Error 1: "Server Busy" or Page Not Loading

**Why it happens:** Millions of students access the portal simultaneously on result day.

**Fix:**
- Wait 15-20 minutes and try again
- Try during off-peak hours (early morning 6-7 AM or late night)
- Use DigiLocker or SMS method instead
- Clear your browser cache and cookies
- Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge)
- Use mobile data instead of Wi-Fi (or vice versa)

### Error 2: "Roll Number Not Found"

**Fix:**
- Double-check your roll number — it should be exactly **7 digits**
- Ensure you selected the correct exam (Class 10 vs Class 12)
- Check the correct year — results from previous years are on the archive portal
- Verify the school number is correct
- Contact your school if the issue persists

### Error 3: Marksheet PDF Not Downloading

**Fix:**
- Disable pop-up blocker in your browser
- Try right-clicking the download link and selecting "Save link as"
- Use DigiLocker for a clean PDF download
- Try on a desktop/laptop instead of mobile

### Error 4: Result Shows Wrong Details

**Fix:**
- If name, date of birth, or subject is incorrect, contact your school
- School will raise a correction request with CBSE
- For marksheet corrections, apply through the CBSE regional office

## How to Download CBSE Marksheet for Previous Years

For results from previous years (archive results):

1. Visit [cbse.gov.in](https://cbse.gov.in/)
2. Go to **Results** → **Archive** section
3. Select the year and class
4. Enter your roll number
5. Download or print the result

**For marksheet copies:**
- DigiLocker stores CBSE marksheets from 2015 onwards
- For older marksheets, apply to CBSE for a duplicate marksheet (fee applies)

## CBSE Roll Number Format

Understanding the CBSE roll number helps avoid errors:

| Component | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Total digits | 7 digits |
| First 2 digits | Region code |
| Next 3 digits | School code (last 3 digits) |
| Last 2 digits | Student serial number |

**Example:** 8601234 — Region 86, School 012, Student 34

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Is the DigiLocker marksheet valid for college admission?

Yes. DigiLocker marksheets are legally valid under the IT Act, 2000. Most colleges and universities accept them. However, some institutions may still ask for the original marksheet — collect it from your school.

### Q2: How long does CBSE keep results on the portal?

CBSE results are available on the main portal for the current year. For previous years, use the archive section on [cbse.gov.in](https://cbse.gov.in/) or check DigiLocker.

### Q3: Can I check CBSE result without roll number?

No, the roll number is mandatory to check results. If you have lost your roll number, contact your school — they maintain records of all students' roll numbers.

### Q4: What if my CBSE result shows "FAIL" or "Compartment"?

If you fail in one or two subjects, CBSE grants a compartment exam. See our [CBSE Compartment Exam guide](/guide/cbse-compartment-exam-apply) for how to apply.

### Q5: How to get a duplicate CBSE marksheet?

Apply online through [cbse.gov.in](https://cbse.gov.in/) under **Document Services**. You will need your roll number, year of passing, and need to pay the applicable fee. Processing takes 15-30 days.

### Q6: Why does CBSE result show different marks than expected?

CBSE uses a moderation/standardization process. If you believe there is an error, apply for [Verification of Marks](/guide/cbse-verification-of-marks-apply) within 15 days of result declaration.
