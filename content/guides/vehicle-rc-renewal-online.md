# Vehicle RC Renewal Online: Step-by-Step Process in India

The Registration Certificate (RC) of a vehicle in India is valid for 15 years from the date of registration. After that, it must be renewed to legally operate the vehicle on public roads. This guide explains the complete RC renewal process — online via the Parivahan portal and offline at the RTO — including fees, documents, fitness certificate requirements, and green tax.

## What is RC Renewal?

RC renewal is the process of extending the validity of your vehicle's Registration Certificate beyond the initial 15-year period. Under Section 41 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, the RC must be renewed every 5 years after the initial 15-year validity expires. Driving with an expired RC is a punishable offence with fines up to ₹10,000.

## When Should You Renew Your RC?

- **Before expiry:** You can apply for renewal up to 60 days before the RC expiry date
- **After expiry:** Renewal can be done after expiry, but a penalty may apply
- **Grace period:** No official grace period — driving with expired RC is illegal
- **Re-registration:** If RC has been expired for a long time, re-registration may be required

## Who Needs RC Renewal?

- Owners of vehicles that are 15+ years old (from date of first registration)
- After the first renewal, subsequent renewals are needed every 5 years
- Applicable to all vehicle categories — two-wheelers, cars, commercial vehicles, etc.

## Documents Required

- Original Registration Certificate (RC)
- Valid [Insurance Certificate](/guide/vehicle-insurance-expired-renew)
- Valid [PUC (Pollution Under Control) Certificate](/guide/vehicle-pollution-certificate-puc-apply)
- Fitness Certificate (for transport/commercial vehicles — mandatory; for private vehicles — required by some states)
- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) or address proof
- Identity proof (PAN card, voter ID)
- Form 25 (Application for Renewal of Certificate of Registration)
- Green tax payment receipt (applicable in many states)
- Passport-size photographs

## Step-by-Step Online Process (Parivahan Portal)

### Step 1: Visit Parivahan Portal

Go to [parivahan.gov.in](https://parivahan.gov.in/) → **Online Services** → **Vehicle Related Services**.

### Step 2: Select State and RTO

Choose your state and the RTO where the vehicle is registered.

### Step 3: Select "Renewal of RC"

From the available services, select **"Renewal of Registration"** or **"RC Renewal"**.

### Step 4: Enter Vehicle Details

Provide registration number, chassis number, and engine number for verification.

### Step 5: Fill Application Form (Form 25)

Complete the online Form 25 with vehicle and owner details.

### Step 6: Upload Documents

Upload scanned copies of RC, insurance, PUC certificate, address proof, and fitness certificate.

### Step 7: Pay Fee and Green Tax

Pay the renewal fee and green tax (if applicable) via net banking, debit card, credit card, or UPI.

### Step 8: Book Appointment for Vehicle Inspection

Schedule a date for vehicle fitness inspection at the RTO. The vehicle must pass the inspection to qualify for renewal.

### Step 9: Vehicle Inspection at RTO

Take the vehicle to the RTO on the appointed date. The inspector checks:
- Brakes and steering
- Headlights, indicators, and tail lights
- Emission levels
- Chassis and engine number verification
- Overall roadworthiness

### Step 10: Receive Renewed RC

After successful inspection, the renewed RC is issued within 7-15 working days. It will be sent to your registered address or can be collected from the RTO.

## Offline Process at RTO

1. Obtain Form 25 from the RTO or download from the Parivahan website
2. Fill the form with vehicle and owner details
3. Attach all required documents
4. Pay the green tax at the RTO (if applicable)
5. Submit the form at the RTO counter
6. Get the vehicle inspected at the RTO
7. Pay the renewal fee
8. Collect the acknowledgment receipt
9. New RC will be issued in 7-15 working days

## Fees for RC Renewal

| Component | Approximate Fee |
|-----------|----------------|
| Renewal fee (two-wheeler) | ₹300 – ₹600 |
| Renewal fee (four-wheeler, private) | ₹600 – ₹1,500 |
| Renewal fee (commercial vehicle) | ₹1,000 – ₹2,500 |
| Fitness certificate fee | ₹200 – ₹500 |
| Smart card fee | ₹200 – ₹300 |
| Late renewal penalty | ₹300 – ₹500 per month |

> **Note:** Fees vary significantly by state. Check your state transport department website for exact amounts.

## Green Tax

Many states levy a **green tax** on older vehicles at the time of RC renewal:

| Vehicle Type | Green Tax (Approximate) |
|-------------|----------------------|
| Two-wheelers (15+ years) | ₹500 – ₹2,000 |
| Private cars (15+ years) | ₹2,000 – ₹5,000 |
| Commercial vehicles (8+ years) | ₹3,000 – ₹15,000 |
| Diesel vehicles (additional) | 10-25% extra in some states |

> Green tax rates vary by state. States like Delhi, Maharashtra, Karnataka, and UP have different slabs.

## Processing Time

- **Online application processing:** 7-15 working days after inspection
- **Vehicle inspection:** Usually within 3-7 days of appointment booking
- **Total time:** 15-30 days from application to renewed RC

## Fitness Certificate

A fitness certificate is mandatory for commercial/transport vehicles and increasingly required for private vehicles over 15 years old:

- Issued after vehicle passes roadworthiness inspection
- Valid for 2 years for transport vehicles, 5 years for private vehicles (upon renewal)
- Automated fitness centres are being set up across India under the Vehicle Scrappage Policy

## What Happens If You Don't Renew?

- Fine of up to ₹10,000 for driving with expired RC
- Vehicle can be impounded by traffic police
- Insurance claims may be rejected
- Vehicle cannot be sold or transferred without valid RC
- Prolonged non-renewal may require re-registration with higher fees

## Important Tips

1. **Apply before expiry** — Start the renewal process at least 30-60 days before RC expiry to avoid penalties and gaps in legality.
2. **Get fitness check done** — Ensure your vehicle is in good mechanical condition before the RTO inspection. Service brakes, lights, and emission systems beforehand.
3. **Clear pending challans** — Check and pay any [pending traffic challans](/guide/vehicle-challan-check-pay-online) before applying for renewal.
4. **Keep insurance valid** — Ensure your [vehicle insurance](/guide/vehicle-insurance-expired-renew) is active before applying for RC renewal.
5. **Consider scrappage** — For very old vehicles that may not pass fitness tests, consider the Vehicle Scrappage Policy for benefits on new vehicle purchase.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. Can I renew my RC after it has expired?

Yes, you can renew an expired RC by paying a late fee penalty. However, you cannot legally drive the vehicle until the renewal is complete.

### 2. Is vehicle inspection mandatory for RC renewal?

Yes, for vehicles over 15 years old, a fitness inspection at the RTO or authorized inspection centre is mandatory before RC renewal.

### 3. What is the validity of renewed RC?

After the initial 15-year period, the renewed RC is valid for 5 years. Subsequent renewals are also for 5-year periods.

### 4. Can I renew RC of a vehicle registered in another state?

No, RC renewal must be done at the RTO where the vehicle is registered. If you have relocated, first apply for [RC transfer](/guide/vehicle-registration-certificate-transfer) to the new state.

### 5. Will my vehicle be scrapped if I don't renew the RC?

Not automatically. However, under the Vehicle Scrappage Policy, vehicles failing fitness tests may not get renewal. You can still keep the vehicle but cannot drive it on public roads.

### 6. What is the green tax and why do I have to pay it?

Green tax is levied on older vehicles to compensate for higher pollution levels. It is charged at the time of RC renewal and varies by state and vehicle type.

### 7. Can I apply for RC renewal online without visiting the RTO?

You can submit the application and pay fees online, but vehicle inspection at the RTO or authorized centre is mandatory and requires a physical visit.
