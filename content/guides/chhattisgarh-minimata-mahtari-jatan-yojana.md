# Chhattisgarh Minimata Mahtari Jatan Yojana 2026 — ₹20,000 for Pregnant Women

The **Minimata Mahtari Jatan Yojana** is a Chhattisgarh government scheme that provides **₹20,000 financial assistance** to pregnant women in the state to support maternal health, nutrition, and safe delivery.

The scheme is named after **Minimata** (Meenakshi Devi), a respected social reformer and MP from Chhattisgarh. The "Mahtari Jatan" means "mother's care" in the local dialect.

**Quick answer:** Register at your nearest **Anganwadi centre** or **approved government hospital** after pregnancy confirmation. Show Aadhaar, BPL/domicile certificate, and medical certificate. After verification, ₹20,000 is directly credited to your bank account via DBT.

---

## Benefit Details

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| **Financial assistance** | ₹20,000 |
| **Payment mode** | Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to bank account |
| **Beneficiary** | Pregnant women of Chhattisgarh |
| **Category** | Maternal health and welfare |

> **Special provision:** If the beneficiary (mother) dies during childbirth, the full ₹20,000 is payable to her **husband**.

---

## Eligibility Criteria

- **Permanent resident of Chhattisgarh** — domicile/residence certificate required
- **Pregnant woman** with a confirmed pregnancy (registered at government hospital or Anganwadi)
- Priority to **BPL (Below Poverty Line)** families and SC/ST women
- Must register at a **government-approved hospital or Anganwadi centre** for antenatal care
- Aadhaar card required for DBT

---

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (applicant's own)
- **Bank passbook** (Aadhaar-linked bank account)
- **Domicile/residence certificate** of Chhattisgarh
- **BPL ration card** or income certificate (for priority eligibility)
- **Pregnancy/medical certificate** from government hospital (confirming pregnancy and estimated delivery date)
- **Caste certificate** (SC/ST, if applicable)
- **Passport-size photograph**

---

## How to Apply — Step by Step

### Step 1: Register Your Pregnancy

As soon as you confirm pregnancy:
- Visit your nearest **Anganwadi centre** (village or ward level) OR
- Visit your nearest **government hospital / PHC (Primary Health Centre)**
- Register for antenatal care (ANC) — this is mandatory for the scheme

### Step 2: Inform the Anganwadi Worker / ANM

- Tell the Anganwadi worker (Sahayika / Anganwadi Karyakarta) that you want to apply for Mahtari Jatan Yojana
- Or tell the **ANM (Auxiliary Nurse Midwife)** at the Primary Health Centre

### Step 3: Fill the Application Form

- The Anganwadi worker or ANM will provide the application form
- Fill in: name, address, Aadhaar number, bank account details, pregnancy details

### Step 4: Submit Documents

Submit self-attested copies of all required documents to the Anganwadi centre or PHC

### Step 5: Verification

- Local health department officer verifies the application (typically within 15 days)
- Anganwadi Supervisor or Lady Supervisor may visit for physical verification

### Step 6: DBT Transfer

After verification, **₹20,000 is credited to your Aadhaar-linked bank account** within 15 days of approval.

---

## When to Apply

Apply **as early as possible** after confirming pregnancy — ideally in the **first trimester (first 3 months)**. Early registration ensures:
- Better antenatal care tracking
- Faster document verification
- Funds available well before delivery

---

## How This Is Different from Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY)

Both schemes support pregnant women, but they are separate:

| Scheme | Amount | Eligibility |
|---|---|---|
| Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (Central) | ₹5,000 (in 3 installments) | First live birth only |
| **Minimata Mahtari Jatan Yojana (CG State)** | **₹20,000** | CG residents, all pregnancies |

Eligible women in Chhattisgarh can potentially receive both schemes' benefits simultaneously. Confirm with your Anganwadi worker.

---

## Role of Anganwadi in This Scheme

Anganwadi centres are the frontline delivery point for this scheme:
- Anganwadi Karyakarta (AWW) registers beneficiaries
- Monitors antenatal visits, iron tablets, and nutrition
- Fills application forms and forwards to block/district
- Assists with document submission

If you don't know your nearest Anganwadi centre, ask at the village Panchayat or nearest PHC.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I apply if I'm already in the third trimester?**
Apply as early as possible — registration during first trimester is preferred. However, applications during second or third trimester may be accepted. Confirm with your Anganwadi worker.

**Can a working woman apply?**
Yes — there is no employment restriction. The scheme targets pregnant women of Chhattisgarh regardless of employment status, with priority for BPL families.

**What if my bank account is not linked to Aadhaar?**
DBT cannot be processed to an account not seeded with Aadhaar. Visit your bank branch to link Aadhaar before applying.

**Is this available for the second child too?**
Unlike some central maternity schemes that restrict to first birth, confirm with your Anganwadi or district health office whether Minimata Mahtari Jatan Yojana applies to subsequent pregnancies.

**What happens if the delivery is at a private hospital?**
The scheme is linked to registration for **government antenatal care** at Anganwadi or PHC. Even if you deliver at a private hospital, you should register for ANC at a government facility to be eligible.

**If the mother dies during delivery, how does the husband claim?**
The husband should present the death certificate and all original documents at the Anganwadi centre or district Women and Child Development (WCD) office. ₹20,000 will be processed to the bank account on record (or updated to husband's account with proper documentation).
