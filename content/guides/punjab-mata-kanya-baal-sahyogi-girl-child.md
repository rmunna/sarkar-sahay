## What is Mata Kanya Baal Sahyogi Yojana?

**Mata Kanya Baal Sahyogi Yojana** is Punjab government's girl child welfare scheme that provides **₹21,000 financial assistance** to BPL (Below Poverty Line) families when a girl child is born. The scheme is designed to promote positive attitudes towards girl children and address female foeticide in Punjab.

---

## Key Benefits

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Grant amount | ₹21,000 per girl child |
| Eligible families | BPL (as per BPL list) |
| Application window | Within 6 months of birth |
| Payment | DBT to mother's Aadhaar-linked bank account |
| Department | Social Security, Women & Child Development Punjab |

---

## Who is Eligible?

- **Punjab resident** family
- Family listed in the **BPL (Below Poverty Line)** database
- The **girl child is the first or second child** in the family (most scheme versions cover upto 2nd girl)
- Birth registered in Punjab
- Parents must not be government employees (in most versions)

---

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card** of both parents
2. **Birth certificate** of the girl child (from Gram Panchayat / Municipal Office)
3. **BPL ration card** (proof of BPL status)
4. **Bank passbook** of the mother (Aadhaar-linked account)
5. **Residence proof** (voter ID, ration card with Punjab address)
6. **Photograph** of mother with child

---

## How to Apply

### Step 1 — Register Birth

Register the girl child's birth at the **Gram Panchayat** (rural) or **Municipal office** (urban). Get the birth certificate — this is the primary document.

### Step 2 — Apply at Anganwadi / CDPO

Within 6 months of birth, visit:
- Your local **Anganwadi Centre** (rural) — the AWW will fill the form
- **Child Development Project Officer (CDPO)** office (urban / if no anganwadi)

Submit all documents along with the application.

### Step 3 — BPL Verification

The CDPO verifies BPL status through the ration card / BPL list and residence.

### Step 4 — Disbursement

After approval, ₹21,000 is credited to the mother's Aadhaar-linked bank account via DBT.

## Combined Benefits for Girl Child in Punjab

Punjab has multiple schemes for girl child welfare:
- **Mata Kanya Baal Sahyogi** — ₹21,000 at birth (BPL families)
- **Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana** — savings account with 8%+ interest (any family)
- **Beti Bachao Beti Padhao** — district-level campaigns and scholarship support
- **Post-matric scholarship** — for SC/BC girl students continuing higher education

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. My first child was a girl. The second child is also a girl. Can I claim for both?**

Check the current year's scheme notification — most versions cover up to the second girl child. The first girl is typically covered when born in a BPL family. For the second girl, apply again. Third girl child may not be covered.

**2. I recently moved to Punjab from Haryana. Am I eligible?**

Punjab residence is required. If you have been residing in Punjab and your name is in the Punjab BPL list, you may be eligible. New migrants may need 1–3 years of residence proof.

**3. I don't have a BPL card but my family is poor. Can I still apply?**

The scheme is specifically for BPL families. Without BPL card, you are not eligible for this scheme. However, check if your name appears in the state BPL database at the panchayat level.

**4. The birth certificate is taking time. Can I apply without it?**

No. Birth certificate is mandatory. Apply for the birth certificate immediately at the Gram Panchayat or CDPO office — they will issue it promptly. Then apply for the scheme benefit.

**5. Is the ₹21,000 given all at once or in installments?**

Generally, the amount is disbursed as a lump sum to the mother's bank account. Some scheme versions may have conditions or phased disbursement — confirm with the CDPO office.
