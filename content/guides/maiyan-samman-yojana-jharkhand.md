# Maiyan Samman Yojana Jharkhand: ₹1,000/Month for Women (Age 18-50)

Maiyan Samman Yojana is a women-centric financial assistance scheme launched by the Jharkhand government that provides **₹1,000 per month (₹12,000 per year)** to women aged 18 to 50 years. "Maiyan" means "mother/woman" in local dialect, and the scheme aims to empower women financially and improve their socio-economic status.

## What is Maiyan Samman Yojana?

Launched in 2024, this scheme provides monthly direct benefit transfers to eligible women in Jharkhand. It is one of the largest women's financial empowerment schemes in eastern India, targeting approximately **50 lakh women** across the state.

### Key Details

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Monthly Amount | ₹1,000 |
| Annual Amount | ₹12,000 |
| Age Group | 18 to 50 years |
| Payment Mode | DBT to bank account |
| Target Beneficiaries | ~50 lakh women |

## Who is Eligible?

- Must be a **woman aged 18 to 50 years**
- Must be a **permanent resident of Jharkhand**
- Must have an **Aadhaar card**
- Must have a **bank account** in her own name linked with Aadhaar
- Family must hold a **ration card** (National Food Security Act beneficiary)
- Must **not be a government employee** or receiving regular government pension
- Must **not be an income tax payer**
- Women receiving benefits under **other similar state/central monthly schemes** may be excluded (check latest rules)

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card**
- **Bank account passbook** (in the woman's name, linked with Aadhaar)
- **Ration card** (NFSA beneficiary card)
- **Voter ID** or other age proof
- **Passport-size photograph**
- **Mobile number** linked with Aadhaar
- **Self-declaration form** (available at application centres)

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Online Application

1. Visit the official portal at **[maiyansamman.jharkhand.gov.in](https://maiyansamman.jharkhand.gov.in/)**
2. Click on **"Apply Now"** or **"New Registration"**
3. Enter your **Aadhaar number** and verify via OTP
4. Fill in personal details — name, date of birth, address, marital status
5. Enter ration card number for family verification
6. Upload **photograph** and **documents**
7. Enter **bank account details** (account number, IFSC code)
8. Review all details and **submit the application**
9. Note the **Application Reference Number**

### Offline Application

1. Visit the nearest **Block Development Office, Pragya Kendra, or Common Service Centre**
2. Obtain the **Maiyan Samman Yojana application form**
3. Fill in all details and attach required documents
4. Submit the form with **biometric verification** (Aadhaar fingerprint)
5. Collect the **acknowledgement receipt**

### Check Application Status

1. Visit [maiyansamman.jharkhand.gov.in](https://maiyansamman.jharkhand.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"Check Status"**
3. Enter your Aadhaar number or Application Reference Number
4. View application status and payment history

## Fees

There are **no fees** for applying. The application is completely free. Do not pay anyone for registration.

## Processing Time

- Application verification: **2-4 weeks**
- Approval: **1-2 months**
- First payment: Within **1 month** of approval
- Subsequent payments: Monthly via DBT

## Important Tips

1. **Ensure Aadhaar-bank linking** — payments are made only to Aadhaar-linked accounts
2. **Use your own bank account** — the account must be in the woman's name, not a family member's
3. **Update Aadhaar** if your address or name has changed — mismatches cause payment failures
4. **Apply through official channels only** — don't trust agents who charge fees
5. **Keep ration card updated** — ration card is a key eligibility document

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Can women above 50 years apply?
No, the scheme is for women aged **18 to 50 years**. Women above 50 can check eligibility for the [Universal Pension Scheme](/guide/universal-pension-jharkhand).

### Q2: Can government employees' wives apply?
The woman herself should not be a government employee. If she is a homemaker but her husband is a government employee, she may still be eligible if the family meets the ration card criteria.

### Q3: Is this ₹1,000 monthly or annually?
The benefit is **₹1,000 per month** (₹12,000 per year), credited directly to the bank account.

### Q4: Can widows also apply?
Yes, widows aged 18-50 can apply. They may also check eligibility for the [Jharkhand Widow Pension](/guide/universal-pension-jharkhand) for additional benefits.

### Q5: Do I need to renew my application every year?
Once approved, the benefit continues automatically. However, periodic verification (Aadhaar-based) may be required.

### Q6: What if I turn 51 during the year?
The benefit stops when you turn 51. You may then become eligible for pension schemes for older women.

### Q7: Can I get both Maiyan Samman and Savitribai Phule Kishori Samridhi?
These are different schemes for different age groups. Maiyan Samman is for women 18-50, while [Savitribai Phule Kishori Samridhi](/guide/savitribai-phule-kishori-samridhi-jharkhand) is for school-going girls (Class 8 to 18 years).
