# Bank of Baroda Dormant Account Reactivation 2026 — Complete Guide

Bank of Baroda (which merged with Vijaya Bank and Dena Bank in 2019) marks accounts dormant after **24 consecutive months** without a customer-initiated debit. Here's how to reactivate your BOB dormant account — including legacy Vijaya Bank and Dena Bank accounts.

---

## BOB Dormant Account — Restrictions

| Feature | Active | Dormant |
|---------|--------|---------|
| ATM withdrawals | ✅ | ❌ |
| BOB World app debits | ✅ | ❌ |
| UPI payments | ✅ | ❌ |
| Salary/credit received | ✅ | ✅ Continues |
| Balance enquiry | ✅ | ✅ |

---

## Method 1: BOB World Mobile App

1. Open **BOB World** app → Login
2. **"Services"** → **"Account Maintenance"** → **"Activate Dormant Account"** (if available)
3. Complete Aadhaar OTP KYC
4. Submit request
5. **Processing: 24–72 hours**

---

## Method 2: BOB Net Banking

1. Login at **[baroda connect](https://barodaconnect.com)**
2. **"Services"** → **"Dormant Account Activation"**
3. Update KYC documents online
4. Submit
5. **Processing: 2–5 working days**

---

## Method 3: BOB Branch (Standard)

1. Visit any Bank of Baroda branch
2. Carry: Aadhaar (original + copy) + PAN (original + copy) + account passbook
3. Request: **"Dormant Account Activation Form"**
4. Fill form → Deposit ₹100 at counter
5. **Same-day processing**

**BOB Customer Care:** **1800-5700** (toll-free)

---

## Legacy Vijaya Bank / Dena Bank Dormant Accounts

- BOB merged with Vijaya Bank and Dena Bank in April 2019
- Old passbook from Vijaya/Dena is valid for branch identification
- Same reactivation process applies
- Get new BOB account number confirmed (if account was migrated)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Bank of Baroda dormant account for 6 years — still reactivable?**
Yes — up to 10 years. Visit BOB branch with Aadhaar + PAN. Balance is still at BOB. After 10 years: DEAF transfer applies but funds are claimable.

**My BOB dormant account has salary pending for 2 years — can I access it?**
Yes — all credited salary is still in your account (dormant accounts receive credits). Reactivate via branch to withdraw accumulated salary.

**Can BOB World app reactivate dormant accounts for all customers?**
BOB World dormant reactivation is available for select accounts with up-to-date KYC. Branch visit is the guaranteed method for all cases.

**BOB charged ₹700/year maintenance on dormant account — can I dispute?**
Charges are valid per BOB's schedule of charges. For waiver: Raise request at branch or BOB customer care 1800-5700. Waivers are given on a case-by-case basis.

**DEAF transfer — when does BOB transfer dormant account funds?**
After 10 years of inactivity, BOB transfers balance to RBI DEAF Fund. Claim process: BOB branch → DEAF Claim Form → Takes 5–7 working days for recovery.
