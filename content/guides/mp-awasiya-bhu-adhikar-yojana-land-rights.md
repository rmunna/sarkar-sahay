## What is MP Awasiya Bhu Adhikar Yojana?

**MP Awasiya Bhu Adhikar Yojana** is Madhya Pradesh government's programme to provide **residential land pattas (ownership rights)** to landless families living in rural areas of MP. Under this scheme, eligible families receive a free plot of approximately **60 square metres** to build their own home.

The scheme addresses the situation where many rural families live on government or gram sabha land without any legal ownership document.

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Plot size | 60 sq metres (approx.) |

| Portal | clr.mp.gov.in |

## Who is Eligible?

**You are eligible if:**
- Your family is **permanently residing in a rural area** of Madhya Pradesh
- Your family **does not own residential land** in any village/town (landless)
- You are living on **government, gram sabha, or common land** without a title
- Your family is **not registered under PM Awas Yojana** as a pucca house beneficiary already owning land

**Priority to:**
- SC/ST families
- BPL families
- Women-headed households

---

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card** (all family members)
2. **Samgra ID** (Madhya Pradesh family ID — mandatory for MP state schemes)
3. **Residence proof** — any document showing the house location in that village
4. **Ration card**
5. **Photograph** of the family at the current residence

---

## How to Apply

### Step 1 — Contact Gram Panchayat

Visit your **Gram Panchayat Secretary** and inform them you want to apply under Awasiya Bhu Adhikar Yojana. They will:
- Verify your residence and landless status
- Add your name to the pending list

### Step 2 — Gram Sabha Approval

The Gram Sabha (village assembly) reviews and approves the list of eligible landless families. Attend the Gram Sabha if called.

### Step 3 — Verification by Patwari / Revenue Department

The Revenue Department (Patwari + Tehsildar) verifies land availability and the family's landless status.

### Step 4 — Patta Allotment

After verification and approval:
- A plot is identified in the village (gram sabha land or government land)
- A **residential land patta** is issued in the name of the woman of the household (or joint name of husband-wife)
- The patta is registered in land records (CLR portal)

### Step 5 — PM Awas Yojana (Housing)

Once a patta is received, the family automatically becomes eligible to apply for **PM Awas Yojana (Gramin) — ₹1.2–1.5 lakh** housing grant to build a pucca house on the plot.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. I have been living in a hut on the gram sabha land for 20 years. Can I get a patta?**

Yes. Long-term residents on gram sabha land are the primary target. Contact your Gram Panchayat Secretary with Aadhaar and Samgra ID to apply.

**2. Will I be relocated to a new plot or can I get a patta for my current location?**

If your current location is legally available (gram sabha land, not encroached on reserved/forest land), you will get the patta for your existing plot. If not available, a nearby alternative plot will be identified.

**3. I am a woman living alone (widow). Will the patta be in my name?**

Yes. Priority is given to women-headed households. The patta should ideally be in the woman's name or jointly with the husband.

**4. Can the land under this scheme be sold?**

Residential land pattas under this scheme may have conditions restricting sale for a period (usually 5–10 years) to prevent misuse. Check the patta conditions at the time of issue.

**5. After getting the patta, how do I apply for PM Awas Yojana house?**

After patta issuance, go to your Gram Panchayat and ask to be added to the PM Awas Yojana Gramin waiting list. The patta serves as proof of land ownership required for the housing scheme.
