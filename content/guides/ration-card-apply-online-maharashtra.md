# How to Apply for Ration Card Online in Maharashtra (2026)

> **Also read:** [National Guide — How to Apply for Ration Card Online](/guide/ration-card-apply-online) for a general overview of the ration card process across India.

A ration card in Maharashtra lets you buy subsidised wheat, rice, sugar, and kerosene from your nearest Fair Price Shop (FPS) under the Public Distribution System (PDS). The **Maharashtra Food, Civil Supplies & Consumer Protection Department** handles all ration card services through its official portal — **mahafood.gov.in**.

This guide walks you through the complete online application process specific to Maharashtra.

---

## Who Can Apply?

You can apply for a new ration card in Maharashtra if:

- You are a **resident of Maharashtra**
- You **do not already hold** a ration card in any state
- You have a **valid Aadhaar card** (mandatory for all family members above 5 years)
- You are the **head of the family** or an authorised adult member

### Types of Ration Cards in Maharashtra

| Type | Colour | Eligibility | Monthly Entitlement |
|------|--------|-------------|---------------------|
| **Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY)** | Yellow | Poorest of the poor families | 35 kg foodgrains at ₹2-3/kg |
| **Priority Household (PHH)** | Saffron/Orange | BPL families under NFSA | 5 kg per person at ₹2-3/kg |
| **Non-Priority (APL)** | White | Above poverty line families | No subsidised grains (identity proof only) |

The type of card you receive depends on your family income and eligibility as assessed by the department.

---

## Documents Required

Keep these documents ready before starting your application:

### Mandatory Documents

1. **Aadhaar card** of the applicant (head of family)
2. **Aadhaar cards** of all family members to be included
3. **Address proof** — any one of the following:
   - Electricity bill or gas bill (recent)
   - Rent agreement or property tax receipt
   - Passport or driving licence
   - Voter ID card
4. **Income certificate** (from Tehsildar or online through Aaple Sarkar)
5. **Passport-size photographs** of the head of the family (2 copies)

### Additional Documents (if applicable)

- **Marriage certificate** — if adding spouse
- **Birth certificates** — for children
- **Surrender certificate** — if you held a ration card in another state or district and cancelled it
- **Gas connection details** — LPG consumer number (if available)
- **Bank account details** — passbook front page or cancelled cheque

> **Tip:** All documents should be scanned in PDF or JPEG format, under 1 MB each, for online upload.

## Step-by-Step Online Application Process

### Step 1: Visit the Official Portal

Go to **[https://rcms.mahafood.gov.in](https://rcms.mahafood.gov.in)** — this is the Ration Card Management System (RCMS) portal of Maharashtra.

You can also reach it through the main department website **[https://mahafood.gov.in](https://mahafood.gov.in)** → click on "New Ration Card Application" or "Online Services".

### Step 2: Register or Log In

- If you are a **new user**, click on **"New Registration"**
- Enter your **mobile number** and **Aadhaar number**
- You will receive an **OTP** on your registered mobile
- Verify the OTP and create your login password
- If already registered, simply log in with your credentials

### Step 3: Fill the Application Form

After logging in, select **"Apply for New Ration Card"** and fill in the following details:

- **Personal details** — Name, date of birth, gender, caste category, mobile number
- **Address details** — District, Taluka, Village/City, Ward, Pin Code
- **Family details** — Add each family member with their name, age, Aadhaar number, and relationship to head
- **Income details** — Annual family income
- **Existing ration card** — Declare if you or any family member holds a ration card elsewhere
- **Bank and gas connection details**

> **Important:** Your name must match your Aadhaar card exactly. Any mismatch will delay the application.

### Step 4: Upload Documents

Upload scanned copies of all required documents:

- Aadhaar card (head + all members)
- Address proof
- Income certificate
- Photograph
- Any additional supporting documents

Make sure files are clear, readable, and within the size limit.

### Step 5: Review and Submit

- Review all entered information carefully
- Check the declaration checkbox
- Click **"Submit Application"**
- You will receive an **Application Reference Number** — save this! You need it to track your status

### Step 6: Application Verification

After submission:

1. **Taluka Supply Officer (TSO)** reviews your application online
2. A **field inspection** may be conducted at your home by the local Fair Price Shop dealer or ward officer
3. You may receive a call or SMS to visit the Tehsil office with original documents for verification
4. The officer verifies your details against Aadhaar and other government databases

## Fees

- **Online application fee:** **Free** (No charge for applying online)
- **Ration card printing/issuance charge:** ₹5 to ₹15 (nominal, varies by office)
- **No middlemen or agents needed** — the process is completely free and direct

> **Warning:** Do not pay any agent or tout for ration card services. The entire process is free. Report corruption to the department's helpline.

---

## Processing Time

| Stage | Expected Time |
|-------|--------------|
| Application submission to initial review | 3-7 days |
| Field verification | 7-15 days |
| Final approval and card issuance | 15-30 days |
| **Total estimated time** | **30-45 days** |

In urban areas like Mumbai, Pune, and Nagpur, processing may take slightly longer due to higher volume. Rural areas often get faster approvals.

## How to Track Your Application Status

1. Visit **[https://rcms.mahafood.gov.in](https://rcms.mahafood.gov.in)**
2. Click on **"Track Application Status"** or **"Application Status"**
3. Enter your **Application Reference Number** or **Aadhaar Number**
4. View the current status — Submitted, Under Review, Field Verification, Approved, or Rejected

You will also receive **SMS updates** on your registered mobile number at each stage.

---

## What to Do If Your Application Is Rejected

If your application is rejected:

- Check the **reason for rejection** on the portal (common reasons: duplicate Aadhaar, incomplete documents, income mismatch)
- **Correct the issue** and re-apply online
- You can also visit your **Tehsil Office** or **Taluka Supply Office** for help
- File a **grievance** on the portal or through the **Aaple Sarkar** grievance system

---

## Offline Alternative

If you are not comfortable applying online, you can:

1. Visit your nearest **Taluka Supply Office** or **Tehsil Office**
2. Collect **Form No. 10** (application for new ration card)
3. Fill the form, attach documents, and submit it at the counter
4. You will receive an acknowledgement slip with a reference number

Many **Common Service Centres (CSCs)** and **Setu Suvidha Kendras** across Maharashtra also help with online applications for a nominal service charge (₹20-50).

## Quick Tips

- **Link Aadhaar first** — Make sure all family members' Aadhaar cards have the correct address and are linked to mobile numbers
- **Use Aaple Sarkar** — You can also apply through the Aaple Sarkar portal for various government services including ration card
- **eKYC verification** — Some offices now use biometric eKYC; carry your Aadhaar and be prepared for fingerprint verification
- **One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC)** — If you move to Maharashtra from another state, you can use your existing ration card at any FPS in Maharashtra through portability. No need to apply for a new one immediately
- **Keep your mobile updated** — All OTPs and status updates go to your registered mobile number

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I apply for a ration card online from my phone?**
Yes, the RCMS portal works on mobile browsers. You can complete the entire application from your smartphone.

**Q: How many members can I add to one ration card?**
There is no fixed limit, but all members must be part of the same family and living at the same address.

**Q: I moved within Maharashtra. Do I need a new ration card?**
No. You can apply for an **address change or transfer** through the same portal instead of a new card.

**Q: Can I check my ration card details online?**
Yes. Visit mahafood.gov.in → "Know Your Ration Card" and enter your ration card number or Aadhaar number.
