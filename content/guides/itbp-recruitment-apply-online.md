# ITBP Recruitment — How to Apply for ITBP Constable, Head Constable, SI and Other Posts

## What is ITBP Recruitment?

The Indo-Tibetan Border Police Force (ITBP) is a Central Armed Police Force under the Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India. Raised on 24th October 1962, ITBP specialises in high-altitude border guarding along the 3,488 km India-China border — from Karakoram Pass in Ladakh to Jachep La in Arunachal Pradesh.

ITBP regularly recruits candidates for posts such as **Constable (General Duty, Telecom, Tradesman)**, **Head Constable (Telecom, Ministerial)**, **Sub Inspector (Telecom, Overseer)**, **ASI (Stenographer)**, and **Assistant Commandant** through direct recruitment. All applications are accepted online through the official ITBP recruitment portal at [recruitment.itbpolice.nic.in](https://recruitment.itbpolice.nic.in/).

## Who is Eligible?

Eligibility varies by post. Below are the key requirements for the most commonly recruited posts:

### Constable (General Duty / Telecom / Tradesman)

- **Nationality:** Indian citizen
- **Education:** Matriculation (10th pass) from a recognised board. For Tradesman posts, an ITI certificate or work experience in the relevant trade is also required.
- **Age:** 18 to 23 years (relaxation: 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC-NCL)

### Head Constable (Telecom / Ministerial)

- **Education:**
  - **Telecom:** 12th pass with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics (45% aggregate in PCM), OR 10th + 2-year ITI in Electronics/Electrical/Computer, OR 10th + 3-year Diploma in relevant engineering field.
  - **Ministerial:** 12th pass (10+2) with typing speed of 35 wpm in English or 30 wpm in Hindi on computer.
- **Age:** 18 to 25 years (relaxation: 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC-NCL)

### Sub Inspector (Telecom / Overseer)

- **Education:**
  - **Telecom:** B.Sc. with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics, or B.Tech/BCA/BE in Electronics, Computer Science, IT or related field.
  - **Overseer:** Matriculation with Diploma in Civil Engineering from a recognised institute.
- **Age:** 20 to 25 years (relaxation: 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC-NCL)

### Assistant Commandant (Engineer / General Duty)

- **Education:** Bachelor's Degree in relevant field (Civil Engineering for Engineer post) from a recognised university.
- **Age:** 18 to 30 years (relaxation: 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC-NCL)

> **Note:** Exact eligibility conditions are specified in each recruitment notification. Always refer to the official advertisement for the specific post you are applying for.

## Physical Standards

ITBP has strict physical standards that vary by post and candidate category. Below are standards for the most common posts (Constable, Head Constable, SI):

### For Male Candidates

| Category | Height | Chest (Unexpanded – Expanded) |
|---|---|---|
| General (all states except below) | 170 cm | 80 – 85 cm |
| Garhwalis, Kumaonis, Gorkhas, Dogras, Marathas and candidates from Sikkim, NE states, HP, J&K | 165 cm | 78 – 83 cm |
| Scheduled Tribes | 162.5 cm | 76 – 81 cm |

### For Female Candidates

| Category | Height |
|---|---|
| General (all states except below) | 157 cm |
| Hill area / NE state candidates | 155 cm |
| Scheduled Tribes | 150 cm |

- **Weight:** Must be proportionate to height and age as per medical standards.
- **Eyesight:** Visual acuity standards as per CAPF medical guidelines (generally 6/6 and 6/9 without glasses for combatant posts).

> Physical standards for officer-level posts (Assistant Commandant) differ — male: 165 cm height, 81 cm chest with 5 cm expansion; female: 157 cm height.

## Selection Process

The selection process for ITBP recruitment typically involves these stages:

### 1. Physical Efficiency Test (PET)

Candidates must clear physical tests which generally include:

- **For Male:** 1.6 km race in 7 minutes 30 seconds, 11 feet long jump, 3.5 feet high jump
- **For Female:** 800 metres race in 4 minutes 30 seconds, 9 feet long jump, 3 feet high jump

### 2. Physical Standard Test (PST)

Measurement of height, chest and weight against prescribed standards (see table above).

### 3. Written Examination

- **Constable:** Objective type questions on General Awareness, General Science, Quantitative Aptitude, and relevant subjects.
- **Head Constable / SI:** Higher-level questions on relevant technical subjects along with General Knowledge and reasoning.
- The exam is OMR-based or computer-based depending on the notification.

### 4. Skill / Trade Test (where applicable)

- Typing test for Ministerial posts
- Stenography test for ASI/Stenographer
- Trade test for Tradesman posts

### 5. Document Verification

Original documents and certificates are verified against the details provided in the application.

### 6. Detailed Medical Examination (DME)

Comprehensive medical test as per CAPF medical standards. Candidates must meet vision, hearing and overall fitness requirements.

### 7. Review Medical Examination (RME)

Candidates declared unfit in DME can request a Review Medical Examination within 15 days.

## How to Apply Online — Step-by-Step

Follow these steps to apply for ITBP recruitment:

### Step 1: Visit the Official Portal

Go to [recruitment.itbpolice.nic.in](https://recruitment.itbpolice.nic.in/) and look for the active recruitment notification.

### Step 2: Read the Notification

Download and read the official notification carefully. Check eligibility, age limits, and required documents before proceeding.

### Step 3: Click "Apply Online"

Click the "Apply Online" link for the relevant post. You will be redirected to the application form.

### Step 4: Register / Create Account

- Enter your basic details — name, email, mobile number, date of birth.
- Create a login ID and password.
- An OTP will be sent to your registered mobile/email for verification.

### Step 5: Fill the Application Form

- Enter personal details, educational qualifications, and category information.
- Fill in your communication address and permanent address.
- Select your preferred exam centre (if applicable).

### Step 6: Upload Documents

- **Photograph:** Recent passport-size colour photo (as per size/format specified in the notification, typically JPEG, 20–50 KB).
- **Signature:** Scanned signature (typically JPEG, 10–20 KB).
- **Certificates:** Some recruitments may require uploading category/qualification certificates.

### Step 7: Pay the Application Fee

| Category | Fee |
|---|---|
| General / OBC / EWS | ₹100 |
| SC / ST / Ex-Servicemen | Nil (Exempted) |
| Female candidates | Nil (Exempted) |

Payment can be made online via Net Banking, UPI, Debit Card or Credit Card. Some notifications also allow fee payment through SBI Challan.

> **Note:** Application fee amount may vary across different recruitments. Always check the specific notification.

### Step 8: Submit and Print

- Review all details carefully before final submission.
- Click "Submit" to complete the application.
- Download and print the confirmation page for your records.

## Documents Required

Keep these documents ready for document verification stage:

- **Identity proof:** Aadhaar Card, Voter ID or PAN Card
- **Educational certificates:** Marksheets and passing certificates (10th, 12th, Graduation as applicable)
- **Date of birth proof:** 10th class certificate or birth certificate
- **Category certificate:** SC/ST/OBC-NCL/EWS certificate (if applicable), issued by competent authority
- **Domicile certificate:** If required for state-specific relaxation
- **Passport-size photographs:** Recent colour photos (4–6 copies)
- **Character certificate:** From the head of the educational institution last attended
- **NOC:** From current employer (if already employed in government)
- **Discharge certificate:** For ex-servicemen candidates
- **Sports certificate:** If claiming sports quota (where applicable)

## Important Tips

1. **Apply early** — Do not wait until the last date. Server traffic increases close to the deadline and the portal may slow down.
2. **Check physical standards** — Start physical preparation well in advance. ITBP deployments are at high altitudes, so fitness standards are strict.
3. **Keep documents ready** — Ensure all certificates (especially caste/category certificates) are issued before the application deadline.
4. **Save your application ID** — Note down your registration number and keep a printout of the submitted application.
5. **Check the official portal regularly** — Admit cards, results and updates are posted on [recruitment.itbpolice.nic.in](https://recruitment.itbpolice.nic.in/). ITBP does not send individual intimation letters.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. How can I apply for ITBP recruitment?

Visit the official ITBP recruitment portal at [recruitment.itbpolice.nic.in](https://recruitment.itbpolice.nic.in/), find the active notification for your desired post, and click "Apply Online". Complete the registration, fill the form, upload documents, pay the fee and submit.

### Q2. What is the age limit for ITBP Constable?

The age limit for ITBP Constable (GD/Telecom/Tradesman) is 18 to 23 years. SC/ST candidates get 5 years relaxation and OBC (NCL) candidates get 3 years relaxation.

### Q3. What is the minimum height required for ITBP?

For male candidates in the General category applying for Constable/HC/SI posts, the minimum height is 170 cm. For hill area and NE state candidates it is 165 cm, and for Scheduled Tribe candidates it is 162.5 cm. Female candidates need a minimum of 157 cm (General), 155 cm (hill areas) or 150 cm (ST).

### Q4. Is there any application fee for ITBP recruitment?

Yes, the application fee is generally ₹100 for General/OBC/EWS candidates. SC/ST, ex-servicemen and female candidates are exempted from paying the fee.

### Q5. What is the selection process for ITBP?

The selection process typically includes Physical Efficiency Test (PET), Physical Standard Test (PST), Written Examination, Document Verification and Detailed Medical Examination. Some posts also include skill/trade tests.

### Q6. Can female candidates apply for ITBP?

Yes, female candidates can apply for ITBP recruitment. Several posts are open to both male and female Indian citizens. Female candidates are also exempted from the application fee.

### Q7. What is the salary of an ITBP Constable?

ITBP Constable (GD) is appointed in Pay Level 3 (₹21,700 – ₹69,100) under the 7th Pay Commission, plus allowances like Dearness Allowance, HRA and Ration Money. The in-hand salary is approximately ₹30,000 – ₹35,000 per month for a fresh recruit.

### Q8. How do I check my ITBP application status or result?

Visit [recruitment.itbpolice.nic.in](https://recruitment.itbpolice.nic.in/) and check the "Results" or "Notice Board" section. You can also download admit cards from the same portal.
