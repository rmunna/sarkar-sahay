# Personal Loan Rejected? Common Reasons & How to Fix Them

Getting your personal loan application rejected is frustrating, especially when you need funds urgently. Banks and NBFCs in India reject personal loan applications for specific reasons — and most of them can be fixed. Here's a detailed guide on why personal loans get rejected and what you can do about it.

## Top Reasons for Personal Loan Rejection

### 1. Low CIBIL / Credit Score

**The Problem:** Most banks require a minimum CIBIL score of **700–750** for personal loans. A score below 700 almost certainly leads to rejection.

**Common causes of low score:**
- Late or missed EMI/credit card payments
- High credit card utilization (using more than 30% of limit)
- Too many loan applications in a short period
- Errors in your credit report

**How to Fix:**
1. Check your [CIBIL report for errors](/guide/credit-score-wrong-fix) and dispute any mistakes
2. Pay all pending EMIs and credit card bills on time for 6 months
3. Reduce credit card utilization below 30%
4. Don't apply for any new credit for 3–6 months
5. After improving your score, reapply

**Timeline to improve:** 3–6 months of consistent good behavior can improve your score by 50–100 points.

### 2. High FOIR (Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio)

**The Problem:** FOIR measures what percentage of your monthly income goes towards existing EMIs and obligations. Banks prefer FOIR below **40–50%**.

**Example:**
- Monthly income: ₹50,000
- Existing EMIs: ₹22,000 (car loan + credit card EMI)
- FOIR: 44% — **Too high for most banks**

**How to Fix:**
1. **Close existing loans** — Foreclose small loans or credit card EMIs
2. **Increase income proof** — Include all income sources (rental, freelance, bonus)
3. **Apply for a smaller amount** — Request only what you absolutely need
4. **Add a co-applicant** — A spouse or family member with income can bring FOIR down
5. **Wait for existing loans to reduce** — As EMIs reduce, FOIR improves

### 3. Insufficient or Unstable Income

**The Problem:** Banks have minimum income requirements for personal loans:

| Bank Type | Minimum Monthly Income (Approx.) |
|-----------|--------------------------------|
| Major banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI) | ₹15,000–₹25,000 |
| NBFCs (Bajaj, Tata Capital) | ₹15,000–₹20,000 |
| Fintech lenders | ₹10,000–₹15,000 |

**How to Fix:**
1. **Include all income sources** — Salary, rental income, freelance earnings
2. **Show ITR** — File Income Tax Returns for at least 2 years
3. **Bank statements** — Show consistent income credits for 6+ months
4. **Apply at NBFCs** — They may have lower income thresholds than banks
5. **Wait for salary increment** — Reapply after your next raise

### 4. Employment Type or Job Instability

**The Problem:** Banks assess your employment stability:

- **Salaried:** Minimum 1–2 years total experience, 6 months in current job
- **Self-employed:** Minimum 2–3 years of business with ITR proof
- **Contract/gig workers:** Many banks don't lend to contractual employees
- **Probation period:** Most banks reject applications during probation

**How to Fix:**
1. **Complete probation** before applying
2. **Stay in current job** for at least 6 months
3. **Self-employed:** Ensure 2+ years of ITR filing with consistent income
4. **Apply at NBFCs/fintechs** — They are more flexible with employment types
5. **Get employer verification ready** — Salary slip, appointment letter, company ID

### 5. Too Many Existing Loans / Credit Cards

**The Problem:** Having multiple active loans signals "credit hungry" behavior. Each loan application also creates a hard enquiry on your CIBIL report.

**How to Fix:**
1. **Close unnecessary credit cards** — Keep only 1–2 active cards
2. **Foreclose small loans** — Pay off any loan under ₹1 lakh
3. **Don't apply to multiple banks** — Each rejection adds a hard enquiry
4. **Wait 6 months** between applications
5. **Use a loan aggregator** — They do a soft check first before applying

### 6. Incomplete or Incorrect Documentation

**The Problem:** Missing or incorrect documents lead to automatic rejection:

**Required documents typically include:**
- PAN card
- Aadhaar card
- Last 3–6 months salary slips
- Last 6 months bank statements
- Form 16 / ITR for last 2 years
- Address proof
- Employment verification (offer letter, company ID)

**How to Fix:**
1. Prepare all documents before applying
2. Ensure name and details match across all documents
3. Bank statements should be from the salary account
4. ITR should match the income claimed in the application

### 7. Age Criteria Not Met

**The Problem:** Most banks require:
- **Minimum age:** 21–23 years
- **Maximum age:** 55–60 years (at loan maturity)

**How to Fix:** If you're too young, build a credit history first with a secured credit card. If nearing the upper age limit, apply for shorter tenure loans.

## What to Do After Loan Rejection

### Immediate Steps

1. **Don't panic and reapply immediately** — Multiple applications lower your CIBIL score
2. **Ask the bank for the reason** — Banks are required to provide rejection reasons
3. **Check your CIBIL report** — Download and review for errors
4. **Calculate your FOIR** — Understand your debt-to-income ratio
5. **Wait at least 3–6 months** before reapplying

### Alternative Options If You Need Funds Urgently

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|--------|------|------|
| Loan against FD | Low interest, quick approval | Need existing FD |
| Gold loan | No CIBIL check, instant | Need gold collateral |
| Loan against insurance | Low interest | Reduces insurance cover |
| Peer-to-peer lending | Flexible criteria | Higher interest rates |
| Credit card cash limit | Instant access | Very high interest (24–36%) |
| Employer advance | No interest/low interest | Limited amount |

## How to Improve Your Chances Before Reapplying

1. **Fix your CIBIL score** — Pay all dues, reduce utilization, dispute errors
2. **Reduce existing debt** — Close small loans, pay off credit card balances
3. **Increase income proof** — File ITR, include all income sources
4. **Apply at the right lender** — NBFCs and fintechs have more relaxed criteria than banks
5. **Use pre-approved offers** — Check your bank app for pre-approved personal loan offers (these have higher approval rates)

## Important Tips

- Never apply at more than 2 banks within a 6-month period
- Pre-approved loan offers from your existing bank have the highest approval rates
- Keep your CIBIL score above 750 for the best loan terms
- FOIR below 40% significantly improves your chances
- Self-employed applicants should file ITR regularly — it's your strongest income proof

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long should I wait before reapplying after rejection?
Wait at least 3–6 months. Use this time to fix the issue that caused rejection — improve your CIBIL score, reduce existing debt, or gather better documentation.

### Can I get a personal loan with a CIBIL score of 600?
It's difficult with mainstream banks. Some NBFCs and fintech lenders may approve loans at scores of 600–650, but at significantly higher interest rates (18–30% per annum).

### Does personal loan rejection affect my CIBIL score?
The rejection itself doesn't affect your score, but the hard enquiry made during the application process reduces it by 5–10 points. Multiple rejections within a short period compound this effect.

### Can I get a personal loan without income proof?
Banks require income proof for personal loans. However, you may consider alternatives like gold loans or loans against FD, which focus on collateral rather than income.

### What is the ideal FOIR for personal loan approval?
Banks prefer FOIR below 40%. Some NBFCs may accept up to 50%, but the lower your FOIR, the better your chances of approval and the more favorable interest rate you'll get.

### Can a co-applicant help get my personal loan approved?
Yes. A co-applicant with good income and CIBIL score can significantly improve your chances. Their income is added to yours, reducing the FOIR.
