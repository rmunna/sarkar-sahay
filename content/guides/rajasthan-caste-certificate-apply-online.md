# Rajasthan Caste Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **caste certificate** (जाति प्रमाण पत्र / Jaati Praman Patra) in Rajasthan is issued by the Revenue Department and certifies your community/caste for reservations, scholarships, government scheme eligibility, and government job applications. Rajasthan's caste categories include SC, ST, OBC, MBC (Most Backward Class), and EWS. Applications go through **E-Mitra** — the state's digital service platform.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [emitra.rajasthan.gov.in](https://emitra.rajasthan.gov.in) / SSO portal or E-Mitra kiosk. Approved by SDM/Tehsildar within **15–30 days**.

## Caste Categories in Rajasthan

| Category | Reservation % | Key Communities |
|----------|---------------|-----------------|
| **SC** | 16% | Scheduled Castes (state list) |
| **ST** | 12% | Scheduled Tribes (Meena, Bhil, Garasia, etc.) |
| **OBC** | 21% | Other Backward Classes |
| **MBC** | 5% | Most Backward Classes (Gujjar, etc.) |
| **EWS** | 10% | General category, income < ₹8 lakh |

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory for applicant)
- **Jan Aadhaar** (Rajasthan family ID — if available)
- **Ration card** (shows family caste and details)
- **Father's / grandfather's caste proof** (school certificate, old government document showing caste, gazette notification)
- **Voter ID** (address proof)
- Affidavit declaring caste (on ₹50 stamp paper or online declaration)
- Passport-size photograph

---

## How to Apply Online — E-Mitra / SSO Portal

1. Go to [sso.rajasthan.gov.in](https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in) (Rajasthan Single Sign-On)
2. Log in / register with Aadhaar / Jan Aadhaar / Gmail
3. After login, search **"Caste Certificate"** or **"जाति प्रमाण पत्र"**
4. Select **"Revenue Department — Caste Certificate"**
5. Fill form:
   - Applicant name, address (district, tehsil, gram panchayat)
   - Caste/category declaration
   - Upload father's caste documents
6. Pay fee: **₹25–50** (online via SSO)
7. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via E-Mitra Kiosk

1. Visit nearest **E-Mitra kiosk** (every panchayat and ward)
2. Provide Aadhaar / Jan Aadhaar + father's caste proof documents
3. Operator fills and submits form
4. Service charge: approximately ₹30–60
5. Collect acknowledgement receipt

---

## Status Check & Download

1. Log in at [sso.rajasthan.gov.in](https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in)
2. Go to **"My Applications"** → view caste certificate status
3. Or: SMS **RJSTATUS <Application Number>** to **56070**
4. Once approved: download digitally signed certificate PDF

## MBC Certificate (Rajasthan — Gujjar & Others)

For **MBC (Most Backward Class)** category (includes Gujjar, Rebari, Gadaria, Banjara communities):
- Same process as OBC caste certificate on E-Mitra
- Select "MBC" when filling category
- Community must be in the Rajasthan MBC list (as per state government order)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What document proves my caste in Rajasthan?

The strongest proof is your **father's school leaving certificate** showing his caste, or any **old government document** (voter ID, service record) with caste mentioned. A **Caste Sarpanch/Gazetted Certificate** from your village is also accepted.

### Rajasthan caste certificate for scholarship — which income limit?

Caste certificate itself has no income limit. But for OBC/MBC scholarships, you also need a **Non-Creamy Layer** income certificate (income < ₹8 lakh/year). For SC/ST scholarships, no income limit applies.
