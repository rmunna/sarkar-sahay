## What is Digital India?

**Digital India** is a flagship programme of the Government of India launched on **1 July 2015** to transform India into a digitally empowered society and knowledge economy. It encompasses multiple government apps, portals, and services that make governance accessible to every citizen through technology.

This guide provides a **complete list of major Digital India apps and portals** with their features and official URLs.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent informational platform and is not affiliated with any government body. Verify all details on official portals.

## Top Digital India Apps & Portals

### 1. DigiLocker

| Detail | Information |
|--------|-------------|
| **Purpose** | Digital document storage and verification |
| **Official URL** | [https://www.digilocker.gov.in/](https://www.digilocker.gov.in/) |
| **Available on** | Web, Android, iOS |
| **Key features** | Store Aadhaar, PAN, driving licence, marksheets, vehicle RC digitally |

DigiLocker provides every citizen with a **cloud-based digital locker** to store and share verified government documents. Over 600 crore documents have been issued through DigiLocker.

**What you can do:**
- Store and access documents anytime, anywhere
- Share verified documents with government agencies
- Fetch documents directly from issuing departments
- Use digital documents as legally valid equivalents of originals (as per IT Act)

### 2. UMANG (Unified Mobile Application for New-age Governance)

| Detail | Information |
|--------|-------------|
| **Purpose** | Single app for multiple government services |
| **Official URL** | [https://web.umang.gov.in/](https://web.umang.gov.in/) |
| **Available on** | Web, Android, iOS |
| **Key features** | 1,700+ services from 300+ departments |

UMANG provides access to central and state government services in one place.

**Key services available:**
- EPFO (PF balance, UAN activation, passbook)
- Aadhaar services
- PAN verification
- Passport application status
- CBSE/UGC results
- Soil health card
- PM-KISAN status
- NPS account management

### 4. eShram Portal

| Detail | Information |
|--------|-------------|
| **Purpose** | Unorganised workers registration |
| **Official URL** | [https://eshram.gov.in/](https://eshram.gov.in/) |
| **Available on** | Web, App |
| **Key features** | UAN card, ₹2L accident insurance, scheme linkage |

**Benefits:**
- Unique eShram card (UAN)

- Future social security benefits

## How to Get Started with Digital India Services

1. **Get an Aadhaar card** — Most services require Aadhaar authentication
2. **Link your mobile number** to Aadhaar — Required for OTP-based login
3. **Download DigiLocker** — Start storing your documents digitally
4. **Download UMANG** — One app to access 1,700+ services
5. **Register on relevant portals** — Based on your needs

## Important Tips

1. **Always download apps from official sources** — Use Google Play Store, Apple App Store, or links from .gov.in websites only
2. **Never share OTPs** — Government portals will never call and ask for your OTP or Aadhaar
3. **Keep your Aadhaar mobile number updated** — Most services rely on Aadhaar-linked OTP
4. **Use DigiLocker documents** — They are legally valid and accepted everywhere (IT Act 2000, amended)
5. **Check UMANG first** — Before downloading separate apps, check if the service is available on UMANG

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Are all Digital India services free?
Most Digital India apps and portals are free to use. Some services (like passport application or property registration) have their own government-mandated fees, but the digital platform itself is free.

### 2. Do I need Aadhaar for all government apps?
Most government services use Aadhaar for authentication, but not all require it. Some accept other identity proofs. However, having Aadhaar makes the process significantly easier.

### 3. Is DigiLocker safe for storing documents?
Yes. DigiLocker uses the same security infrastructure as Aadhaar (UIDAI). Documents are stored in government cloud servers and are encrypted. DigiLocker documents are legally valid under the IT Act.

### 4. Can I access these services without a smartphone?
Many services are available on the web and can be accessed from any computer. Some services like UMANG also work via SMS and IVR (call-based). AePS allows banking without any device — see our [AePS guide](/guide/aadhar-enabled-payment-aeps-guide).

### 5. What should I do if a government website is not working?
Government portals sometimes face high traffic. Try during off-peak hours (early morning or late night). Clear your browser cache, try a different browser, or use the mobile app alternative.

### 6. How do I report a fake government app?
Report fake apps to the Cyber Crime portal at [https://cybercrime.gov.in/](https://cybercrime.gov.in/) or call the helpline 1930. Only download apps linked from official .gov.in websites.
