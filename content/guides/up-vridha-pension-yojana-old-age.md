## What is UP Vridha Pension Yojana?

**Uttar Pradesh Vridha Pension Yojana** (Old Age Pension / Mukhyamantri Vridha Samman Pension) provides **₹500 per month** to elderly citizens aged 60 years and above who are economically disadvantaged.

The ₹500 monthly pension combines:
- **₹200/month** — Central government (IGNOAPS under NSAP)
- **₹300/month** — UP state government
- **Total: ₹500/month** paid quarterly via DBT to Aadhaar-linked bank account

With over **55 lakh beneficiaries**, UP Vridha Pension is one of the largest old age pension programmes in India.

## Who is Eligible?

- Age: **60 years and above**
- Must be a **Uttar Pradesh resident**
- Annual family income: **below ₹2 lakh**
- Not receiving any other state or central government pension
- Not an income taxpayer
- Not a government employee or pensioner

## How to Apply Online — Step by Step

1. Go to **[sspy-up.gov.in](https://sspy-up.gov.in)**
2. Click **"वृद्धावस्था पेंशन"** (Vridha / Old Age Pension) → **"आवेदन करें"**
3. Click **"New Entry Form"**
4. Fill in:
   - Applicant's name, date of birth, address (in UP)
   - Income details (self-declaration or certificate)
   - Bank account details for DBT
5. Upload documents (Aadhaar, age proof, income certificate, bank passbook)
6. Submit → save the **Application Reference Number**

## Apply via Gram Panchayat (Offline)

1. Visit your **Gram Panchayat office** or **Block Samaj Kalyan / Social Welfare office**
2. Collect the Vridha Pension form
3. Fill and submit with required documents
4. Panchayat/block office uploads your application online

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card** (mandatory — for identity and DBT)
2. **Age proof** — Aadhaar with DOB, voter ID, birth certificate, or school certificate
3. **Income certificate** — annual income below ₹2 lakh (from Tehsildar/SDM)
4. **Bank passbook** — in the applicant's own name, Aadhaar-linked for DBT
5. **Ration card / BPL card** (if available)
6. Passport-size photograph

## Check Application Status

1. Go to **[sspy-up.gov.in](https://sspy-up.gov.in)**
2. Click **"वृद्धावस्था पेंशन"** → **"आवेदन की स्थिति"**
3. Enter your Application Reference Number or Aadhaar number
4. View current status: Submitted → Verified → Approved → Payment

## Check Beneficiary and Payment List

1. Go to [sspy-up.gov.in](https://sspy-up.gov.in) → Vridha Pension → Pensioner List
2. Select **District → Block/Urban → Gram Panchayat**
3. View the list for your area — check if your name appears

## When is Pension Paid?

- Pension is released **quarterly** (every 3 months)
- Credited to your Aadhaar-linked bank account via DBT
- Check payment dates and arrear status on sspy-up.gov.in

## All Three UP Social Pensions at sspy-up.gov.in

| Pension | For | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Vridha Pension | Senior citizens 60+ | ₹500/month |
| Vidhwa Pension | Widows 18–60 | ₹500/month |
| Divyang Pension | Disabled persons | ₹500/month |

All three are applied through **[sspy-up.gov.in](https://sspy-up.gov.in)** with the same process.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My father is 65 and has no income. How quickly will he get the pension?
Applications take **2–3 months** for approval if all documents are in order. Apply as soon as possible. Follow up at the Block Samaj Kalyan office if approval takes more than 3 months.

### My father doesn't have a bank account. How to open one?
Open a **Jan Dhan Account** at any nationalised bank — zero balance, free, and Aadhaar can be used for KYC. Get it linked to Aadhaar for DBT before applying for pension.

### Can a person receive both PMKISAN and Vridha Pension?
Yes — PMKISAN (farm income support) and Vridha Pension are completely separate schemes. Both can be received simultaneously.

### My income certificate expired 2 years ago. Do I need a fresh one?
Yes — most states require income certificates issued within the **last 1 year**. Get a fresh certificate from the Tehsildar office.

### I live in a UP city (not village). Where do I apply?
Urban residents apply at the **Nagar Panchayat / Nagar Palika / Municipal Corporation** Social Welfare office or through the same [sspy-up.gov.in](https://sspy-up.gov.in) online portal.

### The Gram Pradhan says my application was submitted but there is no status online. What to do?
Ask the Gram Panchayat secretary for the **online submission receipt** or application ID. If they cannot provide it, apply directly online at [sspy-up.gov.in](https://sspy-up.gov.in).
