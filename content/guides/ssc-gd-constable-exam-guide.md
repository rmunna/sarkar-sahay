# SSC GD Constable Exam Guide — Eligibility, Pattern, Syllabus & Preparation Tips

The SSC GD (General Duty) Constable exam recruits constables for India's Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs) and other organisations like BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP, SSB, NIA, SSF, and Assam Rifles. It is one of the largest recruitment drives in the country with tens of thousands of vacancies.

This guide covers the complete SSC GD Constable exam — eligibility, pattern, physical standards, and how to prepare.

## What Is SSC GD Constable?

SSC GD Constable is a recruitment exam for General Duty posts in:

- **BSF** — Border Security Force
- **CRPF** — Central Reserve Police Force
- **CISF** — Central Industrial Security Force
- **ITBP** — Indo-Tibetan Border Police
- **SSB** — Sashastra Seema Bal
- **NIA** — National Investigation Agency
- **SSF** — Secretariat Security Force
- **Assam Rifles**

Constables (GD) perform general duty tasks including border patrolling, security of vital installations, anti-insurgency operations, and law enforcement support.

## Eligibility Criteria

### Nationality

- Citizen of India

### Age Limit

- **18 to 23 years** (as on the cutoff date)
- **Age relaxation:**
  - SC/ST — 5 years
  - OBC — 3 years
  - Ex-servicemen — 3 years after deduction of military service

### Educational Qualification

- **Class 10 pass** (Matriculation) from a recognised board

### Physical Standards

Physical fitness is critical for GD Constable. Minimum requirements:

#### Height

| Category | Male | Female |
|----------|------|--------|
| General/SC/OBC | 170 cm | 157 cm |
| ST | 162.5 cm | 150 cm |
| Categories from hill areas/NE states | Relaxation of 5 cm | Relaxation of 5 cm |

#### Chest (Male only)

- Unexpanded: 80 cm
- Expanded: 85 cm (minimum 5 cm expansion)

#### Weight

- Proportionate to height and age as per medical standards

### Physical Efficiency Test (PET)

| Test | Male | Female |
|------|------|--------|
| Race | 5 km in 24 minutes | 1.6 km in 8.5 minutes |

**Note:** PET standards may vary slightly. Check the official notification.

## Exam Pattern

### Computer-Based Examination (CBE)

| Section | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| General Intelligence & Reasoning | 20 | 40 | — |
| General Knowledge & General Awareness | 20 | 40 | — |
| Elementary Mathematics | 20 | 40 | — |
| English/Hindi | 20 | 40 | — |
| **Total** | **80** | **160** | **60 minutes** |

- Each question carries 2 marks
- **Negative marking:** 0.50 marks deducted per wrong answer
- Questions are bilingual (English and Hindi) except the language section

### Selection Process

1. Computer-Based Examination (CBE)
2. Physical Efficiency Test (PET)
3. Physical Standard Test (PST)
4. Medical Examination
5. Document Verification

## Syllabus Overview

### General Intelligence & Reasoning

- Analogies, similarities, differences
- Spatial visualisation
- Problem solving and analysis
- Coding-decoding
- Number series and letter series
- Blood relations
- Direction sense
- Non-verbal reasoning (figures, patterns)

### General Knowledge & General Awareness

- India and its neighbours — history, geography, culture
- Indian polity and constitution
- Current affairs — national and international
- Sports, awards, important events
- General science basics
- Indian economy basics

### Elementary Mathematics

- Number system, BODMAS
- Percentages, ratio, proportion
- Profit and loss
- Simple and compound interest
- Time and work, time and distance
- Averages
- Geometry basics
- Data interpretation (tables, charts)

### English/Hindi

**English:** Comprehension, fill in the blanks, error spotting, synonyms/antonyms, one-word substitution, idioms

**Hindi:** समझ (comprehension), रिक्त स्थान, त्रुटि पहचान, पर्यायवाची/विलोम, मुहावरे

## How to Apply

1. **Check notification:** SSC publishes GD Constable notification on [ssc.gov.in](https://ssc.gov.in)
2. **Register:** Complete one-time registration on SSC portal
3. **Fill application:** Personal details, educational qualifications, force preferences, exam centre choices
4. **Upload documents:** Photo and signature
5. **Pay fee and submit**

### Application Fee

- **General/OBC/EWS:** ₹100
- **SC/ST/Women/Ex-servicemen:** Exempted

## Salary and Benefits

- **Pay Level 3** (7th Pay Commission): Basic pay ₹21,700–69,100
- **Gross salary:** ₹28,000–35,000 per month (varies by posting and force)
- **Allowances:** DA, HRA, ration allowance, uniform allowance, risk/hardship allowance
- **Other benefits:** Free medical, canteen facilities, accommodation, pension (NPS)

## Preparation Tips

### 1. Focus on Physical Fitness First

Unlike other exams, GD Constable requires you to pass physical tests. Start running daily — build up to 5 km. If you fail PET, your written score doesn't matter.

### 2. Keep Maths Simple

Questions are Class 10 level. Practice basic arithmetic, percentages, and time-speed-distance problems. Speed matters more than complexity.

### 3. Read GK Daily

Follow a Hindi/English newspaper for current affairs. Cover basic static GK — Indian history, geography, polity. Focus on defence and security-related news.

### 4. Practice Reasoning Puzzles

Reasoning is highly scoring with practice. Focus on patterns, series, analogies, and coding. Solve 20–30 questions daily.

### 5. Choose Your Language Wisely

Pick English or Hindi based on your strength. Don't switch — practice consistently in one language.

### 6. Previous Papers Are Your Best Resource

SSC GD papers from the last 3–5 cycles cover almost all question types you'll face.

### 7. Time Management in Exam

80 questions in 60 minutes = 45 seconds per question. Don't spend too long on any single question. Mark and move on.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. What is the qualification for SSC GD Constable?

Class 10 pass (Matriculation) from a recognised board.

### 2. Is there negative marking in SSC GD?

Yes, 0.50 marks are deducted for each wrong answer (each question is worth 2 marks).

### 3. Can women apply for SSC GD Constable?

Yes, women can apply. Physical standards differ for female candidates.

### 4. How many vacancies are there in SSC GD?

Vacancies are typically large — ranging from 25,000 to 75,000+ per cycle across all forces.

### 5. What is the height requirement?

170 cm for general male candidates, 157 cm for general female candidates. Relaxations apply for ST and hill area candidates.

### 6. Can I choose which force I join?

You can indicate preferences in the application, but final allocation depends on your rank and available vacancies.

### 7. Is SSC GD Constable a permanent job?

Yes, it is a permanent government position in CAPFs with full benefits and career progression opportunities.
