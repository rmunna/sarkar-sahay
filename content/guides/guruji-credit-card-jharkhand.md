# Guruji Student Credit Card Yojana Jharkhand: ₹15 Lakh Education Loan

The Guruji Student Credit Card Yojana is a flagship education financing scheme by the Jharkhand government that provides **interest-free education loans up to ₹15 lakh** to students from economically weaker families. Named after Shibu Soren (affectionately called "Guruji"), this scheme enables students to pursue higher education without financial burden.

## What is Guruji Student Credit Card?

Launched by the Jharkhand government, this scheme provides collateral-free, interest-free education loans to students who have been admitted to recognised institutions for higher education. The loan covers tuition fees, hostel charges, books, and other educational expenses. The interest on the loan is borne entirely by the state government.

### Loan Details

| Component | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Maximum Loan Amount | ₹15,00,000 |
| Interest Rate | 0% (interest paid by state government) |
| Collateral | Not required |
| Repayment Period | Starts 1 year after course completion |
| Repayment Duration | Up to 15 years |

## Who is Eligible?

- Must be a **permanent resident of Jharkhand**
- Must have been **admitted to a recognised institution** for higher education (graduation, post-graduation, professional courses)
- Family annual income should be **less than ₹4 lakh**
- Age: Generally **up to 25 years** at the time of admission
- Must have passed **10+2 or equivalent** from a recognised board
- Must not have availed of any other education loan from a government scheme
- Course must be from a **NAAC/NBA accredited or government-recognised institution**

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** of the student and parent/guardian
- **10th and 12th marksheets**
- **Admission letter** from the recognised institution
- **Fee structure** of the course
- **Income certificate** of parents (annual income less than ₹4 lakh)
- **Residential certificate** of Jharkhand
- **Bank account passbook** (student's)
- **Passport-size photographs**
- **Caste certificate** (if applicable)
- **College/university ID card**

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Online Application

1. Visit the official Guruji Student Credit Card portal at **[jscc.jharkhand.gov.in](https://jscc.jharkhand.gov.in/)**
2. Click on **"New Registration"** or **"Apply Now"**
3. Register with your **mobile number and Aadhaar**
4. Verify via **OTP**
5. Fill in personal details — name, father's name, DOB, address, category
6. Enter **educational details** — 10th marks, 12th marks, course enrolled
7. Enter **institution details** — college name, course name, duration, fee structure
8. Upload required documents — marksheets, admission letter, income certificate, Aadhaar
9. Enter **bank account details**
10. Review and **submit the application**
11. Note the **Application Reference Number**

### After Submission

1. Application is reviewed by the **District Welfare Officer**
2. Document verification at the district level
3. Eligible applications are forwarded to the **designated bank**
4. Bank processes the loan and **disburses funds** directly to the institution
5. Student receives confirmation of loan sanction

### Check Status

Visit [jscc.jharkhand.gov.in](https://jscc.jharkhand.gov.in/) and enter your Application Reference Number to track status.

## Fees

There are **no processing fees or charges** for the student. The scheme is entirely free to apply.

## Processing Time

- Application review: **2-4 weeks**
- Document verification: **2-4 weeks**
- Bank processing: **2-4 weeks**
- Total: **1-3 months** from application to disbursement

## Important Tips

1. **Apply early after getting admission** — don't wait until fees are due
2. **Keep admission letter and fee receipt ready** — these are mandatory documents
3. **Ensure income certificate is current** — get a fresh income certificate if the existing one is old
4. **Loan is disbursed to the institution** — the money goes directly to the college/university, not your bank account
5. **Plan for repayment** — though interest-free, the principal must be repaid within 15 years after course completion

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Can I use this for studying outside Jharkhand?
Yes, you can use the Guruji Student Credit Card for studying at **recognised institutions anywhere in India**.

### Q2: Is there any collateral or guarantee required?
No, the loan is **collateral-free**. No property or guarantor is required.

### Q3: What courses are covered?
The scheme covers graduation, post-graduation, professional courses (engineering, medical, law, MBA), and diploma courses from recognised institutions.

### Q4: What if I drop out mid-course?
If you drop out, the loan becomes repayable from the date of discontinuation. The interest subsidy from the government may also be stopped.

### Q5: Can working professionals apply?
The scheme is meant for **students pursuing full-time courses**. Part-time or distance learning may not be eligible — check the latest guidelines.

### Q6: How is the interest-free benefit provided?
The state government pays the interest on the loan directly to the bank. You only need to repay the **principal amount** in EMIs after the moratorium period.

### Q7: Can I apply if my family income is above ₹4 lakh?
No, the scheme is specifically for families with **annual income below ₹4 lakh**. Consider other education loan options for higher income brackets.
