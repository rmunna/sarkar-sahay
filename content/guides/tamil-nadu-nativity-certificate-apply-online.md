# Tamil Nadu Nativity Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **nativity certificate** (தாய்நாட்டு சான்றிதழ் — Thaynadu Saanrithal) in Tamil Nadu proves that you are a native/resident of Tamil Nadu. It is required for the **85% state quota** in NEET TN counselling (MBBS/BDS), TN government engineering college admissions (TNEA), and state-specific scheme eligibility. Applications go through the **TN edistrict** portal.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [tnedistrict.tn.gov.in](https://tnedistrict.tn.gov.in). Issued by Tahsildar. For NEET TN state quota, having **studied in TN schools** (Classes 6–12 or as per NMC/MCC notification) is often more important than the nativity certificate itself.

---

## Nativity Certificate vs Community Certificate in Tamil Nadu

| Certificate | Purpose |
|-------------|---------|
| **Nativity Certificate** | Proves you are a native/resident of Tamil Nadu |
| **Community Certificate** | Proves your caste/community (SC/BC/MBC etc.) |
| **Income Certificate** | Proves annual family income |

> For **government college admissions in TN**: you need **all three** — nativity, community, and income certificates. For **general admissions** (OC category): nativity + income are needed. For **NEET TN counselling**: nativity is required for state quota eligibility.

---

## NEET TN State Quota 2026 — Nativity Rules

Tamil Nadu has strict eligibility rules for the 85% state quota in medical admissions:

**Category 1 (Higher priority):** Studied in TN government/aided/matriculation schools from **Class 6 to Class 12** (or as specified in the current year's notification)

**Category 2:** Born in Tamil Nadu (nativity based on birth)

**Category 3:** Parents are Tamil Nadu government employees permanently posted in TN

> The Tamil Nadu government periodically revises state quota eligibility rules. Always check the **TN health department's NEET counselling notification** for the specific year's requirements.

- **NEET TN state quota (85%)** — nativity/domicile proof mandatory
- **TNEA (Tamil Nadu Engineering Admissions)** — state quota eligibility
- **TN government scholarship** — for residents of TN
- **TNPSC (Tamil Nadu Public Service Commission)** — some posts require TN nativity
- **EWS / general scheme eligibility** restricted to TN residents
- **TN government schemes** — Kalaignar, CM schemes for TN residents

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (with Tamil Nadu address)
- **Ration card** (Tamil Nadu ration card showing address)
- **Voter ID** (Tamil Nadu voter enrollment)
- **School certificates** — Transfer Certificate (TC) and marks sheets from TN schools (Classes 6–12)
- **Birth certificate** — if claiming nativity by birth in TN
- Self-declaration of TN residence
- **Voter ID of parent** — if claiming through parental TN residence
- Passport-size photograph

## How to Apply Online — TN edistrict Portal

1. Go to [tnedistrict.tn.gov.in](https://tnedistrict.tn.gov.in)
2. Register / log in with mobile number (OTP)
3. Click **"Revenue Department"** → **"Nativity Certificate"**
4. Fill form:
   - Applicant name, address (district, taluk, revenue village)
   - Duration of stay in TN / school study period
   - Purpose of certificate
5. Upload documents (Aadhaar, ration card, school TCs, birth certificate)
6. Pay fee: **₹20–30** (online)
7. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via Arasu Kalavai / CSC

1. Visit nearest **Arasu Kalavai** (government kiosk) or CSC
2. Carry Aadhaar, ration card, school TCs
3. Operator fills and submits application
4. Service charge: approximately ₹30–60
5. Collect acknowledgement

---

## Apply at Village Office / Taluk Office

1. Visit your **Village Administrative Officer (VAO)** or Taluk office
2. Submit nativity certificate application with all documents
3. VAO verifies at village level
4. Tahsildar issues certificate after approval

---

## Status Check & Download

1. Go to [tnedistrict.tn.gov.in](https://tnedistrict.tn.gov.in)
2. Click **"Application Status"** → enter reference number
3. Once approved: log in → **"My Certificates"** → download digitally signed PDF

## Frequently Asked Questions

### TN nativity certificate vs community certificate — different documents?

**Yes — completely different certificates.** Nativity = proves you are a Tamil Nadu resident. Community = proves your caste (BC/MBC/SC/ST). For NEET and scholarship applications, you need both separately from tnedistrict.tn.gov.in.

### I studied in TN schools but my family is from another state — am I eligible for TN state quota?

If you studied in **TN schools (Classes 6–12)** as specified in the TN NEET counselling notification, you are generally eligible for the TN state quota — regardless of where your family is originally from. The school study period in TN is the primary eligibility criterion. Obtain nativity certificate from the Tahsildar of your TN address.

### NEET TN state quota — nativity enough or do I need school study proof?

For NEET TN state quota, the **school study proof** (Transfer Certificates from TN schools showing study from Class 6 to 12) is the primary document — nativity certificate alone may not be sufficient. Check the specific NEET TN counselling notification each year for document requirements.
