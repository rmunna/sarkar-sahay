# E-Shram Card Benefits — How to Claim Insurance & Pension

The E-Shram card offers several valuable benefits to unorganized sector workers, including accidental insurance of ₹2 lakh, pension schemes, and linkage to various government welfare programmes. However, many registered workers don't know how to actually claim these benefits. This guide explains every benefit and the step-by-step process to claim them.

## Key Benefits of E-Shram Card

### 1. Accidental Insurance Cover (PMSBY)

The primary benefit linked to E-Shram is the **Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY)**:

| Coverage | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| Accidental death | ₹2,00,000 |
| Permanent total disability | ₹2,00,000 |
| Permanent partial disability | ₹1,00,000 |
| Annual premium | ₹12 (auto-debited from bank account) |

> **Important:** The ₹2 lakh insurance is not automatic. You must have an active PMSBY enrollment linked to your bank account with annual premium deduction.

### 2. Pension Benefits (PM-SYM / Atal Pension Yojana)

E-Shram registered workers can enroll in:

**Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maan-Dhan (PM-SYM):**
- Monthly pension of ₹3,000 after age 60
- Government contributes matching amount
- Monthly contribution: ₹55–200 (depending on age at entry)
- Entry age: 18–40 years
- Enrollment at [https://maandhan.in](https://maandhan.in) or CSC centres

**Atal Pension Yojana (APY):**
- Guaranteed monthly pension of ₹1,000–5,000 after age 60
- Government co-contribution for eligible workers
- Enrollment through banks

### 3. Linkage to Government Welfare Schemes

E-Shram registration provides access to:

- **PMJJBY** (life insurance of ₹2 lakh at ₹436/year premium)
- **PM Kisan** (₹6,000/year for eligible farmer workers)
- **MGNREGA** (guaranteed rural employment)
- **PM Awas Yojana** (housing assistance)
- **Ayushman Bharat** (health insurance of ₹5 lakh)
- **National Food Security** (ration card benefits)
- **PDS** (subsidized food grains)
- State-specific welfare schemes

### 4. Emergency & Disaster Relief

During COVID-19 and natural disasters, E-Shram registered workers received:
- Direct cash transfers (₹500–1,000 per month during lockdowns in some states)
- Priority access to relief materials
- State-specific emergency benefits

## How to Claim ₹2 Lakh Accidental Insurance

### Eligibility for Insurance Claim
- Worker must have active PMSBY enrollment
- Annual premium of ₹12 must be paid (auto-debited from linked bank account)
- Claim must be for accidental death or disability (not natural death or illness)

### Step-by-Step Claim Process

**In case of accidental death:**

1. **Nominee contacts the bank** where the worker's account is linked
2. **Collect claim form** from the bank branch
3. **Submit required documents:**
   - Death certificate
   - FIR/police report (for accident)
   - Nominee's Aadhaar and bank details
   - E-Shram card / UAN number
   - Claimant's relationship proof with deceased
4. **Bank forwards claim** to the insurance company
5. **Insurance company processes** the claim within 30 days
6. **₹2 lakh credited** to nominee's bank account

**In case of disability:**

1. **Worker visits the bank** with disability certificate from a government hospital
2. **Submit claim form** with:
   - Medical/disability certificate
   - FIR/police report
   - Aadhaar card
   - E-Shram card / UAN number
3. **Claim processed** and amount credited to worker's account

### Important Documents for Insurance Claim

- E-Shram UAN card
- Bank passbook/statement showing PMSBY premium deduction
- FIR or accident report
- Death certificate (for death claims) / Disability certificate (for disability claims)
- Nominee's Aadhaar and bank details
- Photograph of claimant

## How to Enroll for PM-SYM Pension

1. Visit [https://maandhan.in](https://maandhan.in) or nearest CSC centre
2. Click on **"Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maan-Dhan"**
3. Enter your **mobile number** and verify via OTP
4. Fill in details — name, Aadhaar, date of birth, occupation
5. Enter **bank account details** (savings account for auto-debit)
6. Select **monthly contribution amount** (based on your age)
7. Set up **auto-debit** from your bank account
8. Submit and download the enrollment acknowledgment

### PM-SYM Contribution Chart

| Entry Age | Monthly Contribution | Government Match |
|-----------|---------------------|-----------------|
| 18 years | ₹55 | ₹55 |
| 25 years | ₹80 | ₹80 |
| 30 years | ₹105 | ₹105 |
| 35 years | ₹150 | ₹150 |
| 40 years | ₹200 | ₹200 |

After contributing until age 60, you receive ₹3,000 per month as pension.

## How to Link E-Shram to Other Schemes

### Link to PM Kisan
If you're an eligible farmer:
1. Visit [https://pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in)
2. Register with Aadhaar (same as E-Shram)
3. Benefits automatically linked through Aadhaar

### Link to Ayushman Bharat (PMJAY)
1. Check eligibility at [https://pmjay.gov.in](https://pmjay.gov.in)
2. Visit nearest Ayushman Mitra or CSC centre
3. Provide Aadhaar and E-Shram UAN
4. Get your Ayushman Bharat card issued

### Link to Ration Card / PDS
1. Apply for ration card through your state's food department portal
2. Provide E-Shram UAN as supporting documentation
3. E-Shram registration strengthens your application for NFSA benefits

## Important Tips

1. **Ensure PMSBY premium is paid** — check with your bank that ₹12 annual premium is being deducted; without it, insurance cover is inactive
2. **Keep nominee details updated** — visit eshram.gov.in or your bank to update nominee information
3. **Link your bank account** properly — benefits are transferred through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)
4. **Start PM-SYM early** — the younger you join, the lower your monthly contribution for the same ₹3,000 pension
5. **Don't fall for scams** — no one can guarantee additional benefits for a fee; all E-Shram benefits are free

## FAQs

### 1. Do I automatically get ₹2 lakh insurance with E-Shram card?
Not automatically. You must have active PMSBY enrollment through your linked bank account with the ₹12 annual premium paid. E-Shram registration makes you eligible, but enrollment and premium payment are separate.

### 2. How do I check if my PMSBY insurance is active?
Check with your linked bank branch or through net banking. The ₹12 premium deduction should appear in your annual bank statement (usually debited in May–June).

### 3. Can I get pension through E-Shram?
E-Shram itself doesn't provide pension directly, but it makes you eligible for PM-SYM (₹3,000/month after 60) and Atal Pension Yojana. You must separately enroll for these schemes.

### 4. What happens to pension if I stop paying contributions?
If you stop PM-SYM contributions, you can either resume payments with interest or withdraw your contribution with accumulated interest. The pension benefit is only available if you contribute until age 60.

### 5. Is E-Shram insurance for natural death too?
No, PMSBY covers only accidental death and disability. For natural death coverage, you need PMJJBY (Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana) at ₹436/year premium — available through your bank.

### 6. Who is the nominee for E-Shram insurance?
The nominee you specified during E-Shram registration or PMSBY enrollment. You can update nominee details at your bank branch or through the E-Shram portal.

### 7. How long does the insurance claim process take?
Typically 30–60 days from submission of complete documents. Contact your bank branch for status updates.
