# Money Order Online: How to Send via India Post

## What is a Money Order?

A Money Order (MO) is a service by India Post that lets you **send money to anyone across India** — the recipient collects cash at their doorstep through the postman. India Post also offers **electronic Money Order (eMO)**, which can be sent online for faster processing. No bank account is needed by the receiver.

## Key Details

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Maximum per MO | ₹5,000 |
| Commission | 5% of the amount (minimum ₹25) |
| Delivery | Postman delivers cash at receiver's address |
| Online Option | eMO via India Post portal |
| Tracking | Available for eMO |
| Payment Modes | Cash (offline), online payment (eMO) |

## Money Order Commission Charges

| Amount | Commission (5%) |
|---|---|
| ₹500 | ₹25 (minimum) |
| ₹1,000 | ₹50 |
| ₹2,000 | ₹100 |
| ₹3,000 | ₹150 |
| ₹5,000 | ₹250 |

**For amounts above ₹5,000**, you need to send **multiple Money Orders** of ₹5,000 each.

## How to Send Money Order Online (eMO) — Step-by-Step

### Method 1: eMO via India Post Portal

1. Visit [India Post eMO Service](https://www.epostoffice.gov.in/)
2. **Register** or **log in** with your credentials
3. Click on **"Send eMO"** (electronic Money Order)
4. Enter **sender details** — name, address, phone number
5. Enter **receiver details** — name, full address with PIN code, phone number
6. Enter the **amount** (up to ₹5,000 per eMO)
7. Add an optional **message** (up to 30 words)
8. Pay the amount + commission online (net banking/debit card)
9. Note the **eMO number** for tracking
10. The postman delivers cash to the receiver's address

### Method 2: At the Post Office (Offline)

1. Visit your nearest post office
2. Fill the **Money Order form** (MO form available at the counter)
3. Provide receiver's **full name and address with PIN code**
4. Pay the MO amount + 5% commission in cash
5. Get the **MO receipt** with tracking number
6. Money is delivered by the postman at the receiver's address

## How to Track Money Order

### Track eMO Online

1. Visit [India Post Tracking](https://www.indiapost.gov.in/_layouts/15/dop.portal.tracking/trackconsignment.aspx)
2. Enter your **eMO number** from the booking receipt
3. View delivery status — booked, in transit, delivered

### Track via SMS

- Send **POST TRACK <eMO number>** to **166**
- Receive status via SMS reply

## Delivery Process

1. **Sender books MO** at post office or online
2. MO is transmitted to the **destination post office** (based on receiver's PIN code)
3. The **postman visits the receiver's address** with cash
4. Receiver **signs the MO receipt** and collects the money
5. If receiver is absent, postman leaves a **notice** to visit the post office

**Typical delivery time:** 1-3 days for eMO, 3-5 days for traditional MO.

## Who Can Send and Receive?

- **Any person in India** can send a Money Order — no ID required for amounts below ₹50,000 (cumulative)
- **Receiver does not need a bank account** — cash is delivered at their door
- Useful for sending money to **rural areas** where banking access is limited
- **Government departments** also use MO for disbursing payments

## Important Tips

1. **Double-check the address and PIN code** — incorrect details cause delivery failure
2. **Use eMO for speed** — electronic MO is faster than traditional MO
3. **Keep the receipt** — it has the tracking number for checking delivery status
4. **₹5,000 limit per MO** — for larger amounts, send multiple MOs
5. **Inform the receiver** — let them know a Money Order is coming so they are available

## FAQs

### What is the maximum amount for a Money Order?

The maximum amount per Money Order is **₹5,000**. For larger amounts, you need to send multiple Money Orders.

### What are the Money Order charges?

India Post charges a **5% commission** on the Money Order amount, with a minimum charge of ₹25. So for ₹5,000, the commission is ₹250.

### How long does a Money Order take to deliver?

**eMO (electronic):** 1-3 days. **Traditional MO:** 3-5 days. Delivery time depends on the distance and location.

### Can I send a Money Order online?

Yes, you can send an **electronic Money Order (eMO)** through [epostoffice.gov.in](https://www.epostoffice.gov.in/). Register, enter receiver details, pay online, and the postman delivers cash.

### Does the receiver need a bank account?

No, the receiver does **not need a bank account**. The postman delivers the cash directly at the receiver's address.

### What if the receiver is not at home?

The postman will leave a **notice** asking the receiver to collect the Money Order from the local post office. The MO is held for a period before being returned to sender.

### Can I cancel a Money Order?

Yes, you can request cancellation at the **booking post office** before the MO is dispatched. After dispatch, cancellation is not possible — you must wait for it to be returned if undelivered.
