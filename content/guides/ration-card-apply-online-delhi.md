# Delhi Ration Card Apply Online 2026

The Delhi ration card is issued by the **Department of Food, Supplies and Consumer Affairs, Government of NCT of Delhi**. Delhi manages rations through the **NFS Delhi** (National Food Security) portal. Delhi has one of the most digitised PDS systems, with e-ration cards and Aadhaar-based biometric verification at all fair price shops.

> **Quick summary:** Apply online at [nfs.delhi.gov.in](https://nfs.delhi.gov.in) or through e-District Delhi. Delhi priority households get 5 kg grain/person/month free under PM GKAY.

---

## Ration Card Types in Delhi

| Type | Category | Colour | Entitlement |
|------|----------|--------|-------------|
| **Priority Household (PHH)** | BPL/NFSA eligible | Yellow | 5 kg grain/person/month free |
| **Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY)** | Poorest families | Yellow (AAY) | 35 kg/family/month free |
| **Others (Non-Priority)** | Non-subsidised | Pink | For identity purposes; state may provide limited subsidy |

> Delhi has eliminated the APL category. Families not eligible for NFSA get "Others" category cards, which are primarily used as identity documents.

---

## Eligibility

**Priority Household (PHH):**
- Annual family income below ₹1 lakh (Delhi-specific threshold)
- Not a government employee in central/state service
- No income taxpayer in household
- Family must not own a 4-wheeler vehicle
- Must be a Delhi resident (with proof)
- Aadhaar mandatory for all members

**AAY:**
- Widow/elderly living alone with no income
- Disabled persons without support
- Landless agricultural households (rare in Delhi)
- Destitute migrant workers

**Others:**
- Families above the income threshold
- Government employees
- Useful as identity document and for LPG subsidy tracking

---

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar cards (all family members — mandatory)
- Proof of residence in Delhi (electricity bill, rent agreement, voter ID with Delhi address)
- Passport-size photographs of all members
- Income certificate from SDM or Tehsildar
- Voter ID (for address verification)
- Relationship proof (birth certificate, marriage certificate for newly married)

## How to Apply Online

**Method 1: NFS Delhi Portal (Recommended)**

1. Go to [nfs.delhi.gov.in](https://nfs.delhi.gov.in)
2. Click **"Apply for Ration Card"**
3. Log in with Aadhaar-linked mobile OTP
4. Select **"New Ration Card"** application
5. Fill in family head details and add all members with Aadhaar
6. Upload documents (residence proof, photographs, income certificate)
7. Submit — download acknowledgement

**Method 2: e-District Delhi**

1. Go to [edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in](https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in)
2. Register as a citizen (one-time)
3. Search for **"Ration Card"** services
4. Select new application and fill in details
5. Attach documents and submit

**Method 3: Circle Office / Food Inspector Office**

1. Visit your area's Circle Office (Food Inspector)
2. Collect and fill Form RC-1 for new ration card
3. Submit with documents — get acknowledgement
4. Inspector verifies and forwards to Assistant Commissioner (Food)

## e-Ration Card Download (Delhi)

Delhi issues **e-Ration Cards** — digital copies valid at all FPS:

1. Go to [nfs.delhi.gov.in](https://nfs.delhi.gov.in)
2. Click **"Download e-Ration Card"**
3. Enter ration card number or Aadhaar number
4. Download the e-ration card PDF
5. Show on mobile at your Fair Price Shop — biometric verification is done there

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My Aadhaar address shows a different state. Can I apply in Delhi?

Yes — you can apply if you have proof of Delhi residence (electricity bill, rent agreement, etc.), even if your Aadhaar shows another state address. You'll need to surrender your ration card from the home state first.

### Is one card per household or per family?

One ration card per household (family living together). All members at the same address should be on the same ration card.

### Can I add my child to my existing Delhi ration card?

Yes — visit the Circle Office or apply online at nfs.delhi.gov.in for member addition with the child's Aadhaar and birth certificate.

### What is the pink ration card in Delhi?

Pink card = "Others" category — non-priority households. It doesn't give subsidised grain but serves as an official identity/residence proof and is used for LPG subsidy tracking.
