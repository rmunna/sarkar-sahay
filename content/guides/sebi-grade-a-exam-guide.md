## What is the SEBI Grade A Exam?

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) conducts the Grade A Officer exam to recruit Assistant Managers in various streams — General, Legal, IT, Engineering, and Official Language. SEBI is India's capital market regulator, and Grade A officers play a crucial role in market regulation, investor protection, and securities law enforcement.

SEBI Grade A is among the top regulatory body exams in India, offering compensation comparable to RBI Grade B.

## Who is Eligible?

### Educational Qualification
- **General Stream:** Master's degree or equivalent in any discipline, OR Bachelor's degree with 60% marks and relevant professional qualification (CA/CS/CMA/CFA)
- **Legal Stream:** Bachelor's degree in Law (LLB) with 55% marks
- **IT Stream:** B.Tech/BE in Computer Science/IT or MCA with 60% marks
- **Engineering Stream:** B.Tech/BE in Civil/Electrical/Electronics with 60% marks
- **Official Language:** Master's in Hindi with English as a subject at graduation

### Age Limit
- **Maximum 30 years** (relaxation: 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC, 10 years for PwD)

## SEBI Grade A Exam Pattern

### Phase I — Screening (Online)

**Paper 1 — General (Common)**

| Section | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| General Awareness | 30 | 30 |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 30 | 30 |
| English Language | 30 | 30 |
| Reasoning | 30 | 30 |
| **Total** | **120** | **120** |

**Paper 2 — Stream-Specific (Objective)**
- 40 questions, 40 marks, 40 minutes
- Covers commerce, economics, finance for General stream

- **Total Phase I:** 160 marks, 160 minutes
- Negative marking: 0.25 per wrong answer

### Phase II — Mains

**Paper 1 — Stream-Specific (Objective)**
- 40 questions, 100 marks, 60 minutes

**Paper 2 — Stream-Specific (Descriptive)**
- 3 questions (Essay + Case study + Comprehension), 100 marks, 60 minutes

- **Total Phase II:** 200 marks

### Phase III — Interview
- 30 marks
- **Final Merit:** Phase II (200) + Interview (30) = **230 marks**

## Syllabus

### General Awareness (Phase I)
- Current affairs — economic, financial, capital markets
- SEBI regulations and recent circulars
- Indian economy and fiscal/monetary policy
- Banking and financial institutions

### Stream-Specific (General Stream)
- Securities market — primary and secondary markets
- Mutual funds and portfolio management
- Corporate governance and company law
- Financial management and accounting
- Derivatives — options, futures, swaps
- SEBI Act, regulations, and guidelines
- Economic theory — micro and macro
- International finance and trade

### English (Descriptive)
- Essay on capital market, economic policy, or social issues
- Case study analysis — securities market scenarios
- Comprehension with analytical answers

## Preparation Strategy

### Phase I (2 months)
- General awareness — focus on capital markets, SEBI news, and economic affairs
- Standard bank exam preparation for reasoning and quantitative aptitude
- Stream-specific paper — basic commerce and finance concepts

### Phase II (3 months)
- Deep study of securities market — SEBI modules on NISM website
- Corporate governance, company law, and financial management
- Practice descriptive writing — essays on capital market topics
- Read SEBI annual report and recent discussion papers

### Key Resources
- **NISM certification modules** — excellent for securities market concepts
- **SEBI Annual Report** — must-read for exam and interview
- **Sanjiv Verma's Indian Economy** for economic concepts
- **Financial Express and Mint** for daily capital market news

## Important Tips

- **SEBI-specific knowledge is critical** — understand SEBI's role, powers, major regulations (LODR, ICDR, Insider Trading)
- **Capital market awareness** — study IPO process, stock exchanges, mutual funds, derivatives; this is the core domain
- **Descriptive paper is decisive** — practice writing analytical essays on market regulation and investor protection
- **Interview will test domain knowledge** — expect questions on recent SEBI actions, market developments, and your views on regulation
- **NISM study material is free** — use it extensively for Phase II preparation

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is SEBI Grade A better than RBI Grade B?
Both are top regulatory body positions. RBI Grade B offers slightly higher compensation and broader economic policy role. SEBI Grade A focuses on capital market regulation. Many candidates prepare for both simultaneously. See our [RBI Grade B guide](/guide/rbi-grade-b-exam-guide) for comparison.

### Can commerce graduates apply for SEBI Grade A?
Yes, commerce graduates with a Master's degree or CA/CS/CMA qualification can apply for the General stream.

### Where are SEBI offices located?
SEBI's head office is in Mumbai. Regional offices are in Delhi, Chennai, Kolkata, and Ahmedabad. Most postings are in Mumbai.

### Is there a service bond?
SEBI may have a service bond of 2-3 years. Check the specific notification for details.

### What is the career growth at SEBI?
Grade A → Grade B → Grade C → Executive Director → Whole Time Member → Chairman. Promotions are based on performance and seniority.
