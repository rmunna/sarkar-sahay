# West Bengal Domicile Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **residence certificate** (বাসস্থান শংসাপত্র — Basasthan Shansapatra) in West Bengal — the state's equivalent of a domicile certificate — is issued by the Revenue Department (BDO / SDO) and is required for WBJEE/NEET West Bengal state quota (85%) admissions, WBPSC government job applications, West Bengal state scholarships, and scheme eligibility. Applications go through the **West Bengal e-District portal**.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [edistrict.wb.gov.in](https://edistrict.wb.gov.in) or **Duare Sarkar** camp. Issued by BDO / SDO within **15–21 days**.

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|

| **Eligibility** | Born in WB OR permanent resident |
| **Issuing authority** | BDO (Block Development Officer) / SDO |
| **Processing time** | 15–21 days |
| **Fee** | ₹20–₹30 |
| **Validity** | Generally valid for 1–3 years for admissions use |
| **Portal** | edistrict.wb.gov.in |

## Why You Need WB Residence Certificate

- **WBJEE state quota** — engineering admissions (WBJEEB counselling)
- **NEET West Bengal state quota (85%)** — WBMCC (WB Medical Counselling Committee) counselling
- **WBPSC (West Bengal PSC)** — government job preference for WB residents
- **WBSSC (West Bengal Staff Selection Commission)** — state recruitment
- **Swami Vivekananda Merit-cum-Means Scholarship** — WB state scholarship (requires WB resident proof)
- **Aikyashree (minority scholarship)** — WB residents
- **Kanyashree / Rupashree** — WB girl schemes requiring domicile proof

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory — with West Bengal address)
- **Ration card** (West Bengal ration card — preferably WBPDS card)
- **Voter ID** (West Bengal voter enrollment)
- **Birth certificate** — showing birth in West Bengal (if claiming by birth)
- **School Transfer Certificate (TC)** / Madhyamik certificate from WB school
- **Utility bills** — electricity/water bills at WB address
- **Land records / Deed** (for property-based proof)
- Self-declaration of WB residency
- Passport-size photograph

## How to Apply Online — WB e-District Portal

1. Go to [edistrict.wb.gov.in](https://edistrict.wb.gov.in)
2. Register / log in with mobile number (OTP)
3. Click **"Revenue"** → **"Residence Certificate"**
4. Fill form:
   - Applicant name, father's name, date of birth
   - District, Sub-Division, Block/Municipality, Gram Panchayat / Ward
   - Duration of residence in West Bengal
   - Purpose of certificate
5. Upload documents (Aadhaar, ration card, voter ID, birth certificate or school TC)
6. Pay fee: **₹20–₹30** (online)
7. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via Duare Sarkar / Tathya Mitra Kendra

1. Visit nearest **Duare Sarkar** camp (held periodically, check district schedule) or **Tathya Mitra Kendra**
2. Carry Aadhaar, ration card, voter ID, school TC
3. Operator fills and submits application on WB e-District
4. No service charge at Duare Sarkar — government-provided free service
5. Collect acknowledgement

---

## Apply at BDO Office

1. Visit your **Block Development Office (BDO)** for rural applicants or **SDO** for urban
2. Submit residence certificate application form with self-attested documents
3. BDO's office verifies residency from records
4. Certificate issued after SDO approval

---

## Status Check & Download

1. Go to [edistrict.wb.gov.in](https://edistrict.wb.gov.in) → **"Track Application"**
2. Enter application reference number
3. Once approved: download digitally signed PDF
4. Valid for WBJEE, NEET WB counselling, WBPSC applications

## WBJEE / NEET WB State Quota 2026

For **WBJEE 2026** (West Bengal engineering admissions) and **NEET 2026 WB state quota** (WBMCC counselling):
1. WB residence certificate — proof of WB residency
2. OR studied in West Bengal schools for the prescribed period (check WBMCC/WBJEEB notification)
3. Certificate must be BDO/SDO issued — WB e-District digital certificate accepted
4. Apply at least 4–6 weeks before counselling
5. WBMCC/WBJEEB verifies during document verification round

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many years of residence in WB for domicile?

West Bengal does not have a rigid fixed-year mandate like Maharashtra (15 years) or Rajasthan (10 years). **Permanent residence** with supporting documents (ration card, voter ID, school TC) is the requirement. Students who studied in WB schools can use school documents as residency proof.

### Duare Sarkar vs e-District — which is faster?

Both use the same backend (WB e-District portal), so processing time is identical (15–21 days) after submission. **Duare Sarkar is free** — no service charges — whereas private CSC kiosks may charge ₹30–60. Use Duare Sarkar if a camp is near you.

### Swami Vivekananda Scholarship — does it need a fresh certificate?

SVMCM (Swami Vivekananda Merit-cum-Means Scholarship) requires proof of WB residence. A recent residence certificate (issued within 6 months for most portals) is recommended. Check the SVMCM portal for exact requirements each year.
