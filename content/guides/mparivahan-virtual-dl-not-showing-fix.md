# mParivahan Virtual DL Not Showing? Fix It Now

Your **virtual Driving Licence (DL)** on the mParivahan app is a legally valid digital document you can show during traffic checks. But many users face the frustrating problem of their DL not appearing in the app even after successful login.

This guide explains why your virtual DL is not showing and how to fix it.

## Why Is Your Virtual DL Not Showing?

The most common reasons are:

- **Parivahan database sync delay** — Your RTO hasn't synced data with the central server
- **RTO records not updated** — DL issued recently or renewed but not yet in the system
- **Name mismatch** — Your name in DL records doesn't match your Aadhaar name
- **Old DL not digitized** — DLs issued before 2010 may not be in the digital database
- **Wrong DL number** — Incorrect DL number entered in the app
- **Server issues** — Parivahan servers temporarily down

## Fix 1: Check Your DL on the Sarathi Portal

Before troubleshooting the app, verify your DL exists in the central database:

1. Visit [sarathi.parivahan.gov.in](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in)
2. Select your **State** and **RTO**
3. Click **"Know Your Driving Licence Status"** or **"DL Search"**
4. Enter your **DL number** and **date of birth**
5. Complete the captcha and click **Search**

**If your DL appears here** — The issue is with the mParivahan app. Follow the fixes below.

**If your DL does NOT appear** — Your DL data has not been uploaded to the central Parivahan database. You need to visit your RTO office.

## Fix 2: Parivahan Data Sync Delay

The mParivahan app fetches data from the central Parivahan/Sarathi database. Data sync between your local RTO and the central server can take time:

| Scenario | Expected Sync Time |
|---|---|
| New DL issued | 7-15 days |
| DL renewed | 7-15 days |
| Address change | 10-20 days |
| Name correction | 10-20 days |
| Duplicate DL issued | 5-10 days |

**What to do:**
1. Wait for the expected sync period to pass
2. Check the Sarathi portal periodically
3. If data doesn't appear after 20 days, visit your RTO with the original DL

## Fix 3: RTO Records Not Updated

Some RTOs, especially smaller ones, have delays in uploading data:

1. **Visit your RTO office** with your original DL and a photocopy
2. Go to the **Data Entry / Record Room** counter
3. Request them to **update your DL record in the Sarathi system**
4. Carry your **Aadhaar card** as identity proof
5. Data should reflect in 3-7 days after the RTO updates it

> **Tip:** Take a printout of the Sarathi portal showing your DL is "not found" — this helps the RTO staff understand the issue quickly.

## Fix 4: Name Mismatch Between DL and Aadhaar

mParivahan uses Aadhaar-based OTP login. If your name on the DL doesn't match your Aadhaar name, the app may not link your DL:

**How to check:**
1. Compare your name on the **physical DL** with your **Aadhaar card**
2. Common mismatches: spelling differences, middle name present/absent, initials vs full name

**How to fix:**
- **If Aadhaar name is wrong** — Update it at [uidai.gov.in](https://uidai.gov.in) or visit an Aadhaar centre
- **If DL name is wrong** — Apply for name correction at [sarathi.parivahan.gov.in](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in) → DL Services → Change of Name
- Ensure both documents have the **exact same name spelling**

## Fix 5: Old DL Not Digitized

If your Driving Licence was issued before 2005-2010 (varies by state), it may not be in the digital database:

1. Visit your **RTO office** with the original DL
2. Request **data entry of your DL** into the Sarathi system
3. You may need to provide:
   - Original DL
   - Aadhaar card
   - Passport-size photos
   - Address proof
4. Some RTOs may ask you to apply for a **duplicate/smart card DL** which automatically enters your data into the system
5. The smart card DL costs approximately **₹200-₹400** depending on your state

## Fix 6: Use DigiLocker as an Alternative

If mParivahan is consistently not showing your DL, **DigiLocker** is an excellent alternative:

1. Download **DigiLocker** from [Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.digilocker.android) or App Store
2. Sign in using your **Aadhaar number** and OTP
3. Go to **"Issued Documents"**
4. Search for **"Ministry of Road Transport and Highways"**
5. Select **"Driving Licence"**
6. Enter your **DL number** and **date of birth**
7. Your DL will be pulled from the Parivahan database

> **Important:** DigiLocker documents are legally valid under the IT Act, 2000. Traffic police must accept them.

See our [DigiLocker guide](/guide/digilocker-account-setup) for detailed instructions.

## Fix 7: Clear mParivahan App Data and Retry

Sometimes cached data causes display issues:

1. Go to **Settings → Apps → mParivahan**
2. Tap **Storage → Clear Cache**
3. Tap **Clear Data**
4. Open mParivahan and **log in again**
5. Go to **My DL** section and enter your DL number
6. Wait for the data to load (may take 30-60 seconds)

## Important Tips

1. **Always verify on Sarathi first** — If your DL doesn't show on the Sarathi portal, it won't show on mParivahan either
2. **Keep your physical DL handy** — Until your virtual DL works, carry the physical copy
3. **DigiLocker is equally valid** — Use it as a reliable backup for your digital DL
4. **Morning hours work best** — Try accessing mParivahan between 6-8 AM for fewer server issues
5. **Save a screenshot** — Once your virtual DL loads, take a screenshot for offline use

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the virtual DL on mParivahan legally valid?

Yes, the virtual DL displayed on mParivahan is legally valid as per MoRTH notification. It can be presented during traffic checks, and police officers are instructed to accept it.

### My DL shows on Sarathi but not on mParivahan. Why?

This is usually a temporary sync issue between the Sarathi database and the mParivahan app. Clear the app cache, update to the latest version, and try again. If it persists for more than a week, reinstall the app.

### Can I get a virtual DL without Aadhaar?

No, mParivahan requires Aadhaar-based OTP verification to display your virtual DL. If your DL is not linked to your Aadhaar, you need to update your mobile number at the RTO first.

### How long does it take for a renewed DL to show on mParivahan?

After DL renewal, it typically takes **7-15 days** for the updated data to sync with the central Parivahan database and appear on mParivahan.

### My name is different on DL and Aadhaar. Will the virtual DL still work?

Minor spelling differences may not cause issues, but significant name mismatches can prevent your DL from appearing. It's best to ensure both documents have matching names.

### Can traffic police fine me if mParivahan is not loading my DL?

If you have a valid DL but the app is not loading it due to technical issues, carry your physical DL as backup. You can also show your DL via DigiLocker. If neither works, the officer may verify your DL online using the Vahan/Sarathi system.
