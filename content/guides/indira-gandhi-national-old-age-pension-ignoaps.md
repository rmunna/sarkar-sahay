## What is IGNOAPS?

**Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS)** is a centrally sponsored social assistance scheme under **NSAP (National Social Assistance Programme)** that provides a monthly cash pension to **elderly BPL citizens** aged 60 years and above.

The scheme provides basic financial security to senior citizens who have no stable source of income or support.

Key features:
- **₹200/month** for persons aged **60–79 years**
- **₹500/month** for persons aged **80 years and above**
- Paid directly to beneficiary's bank/post office account via DBT
- State governments often **add their own top-up** — combined pension can be ₹500–₹2,000+/month depending on state

> **State top-ups are significant:** UP adds ₹300 (total ₹500), Bihar adds ₹200 (total ₹400), Rajasthan adds ₹500–750 (total ₹700–1,000), Tamil Nadu pays ₹1,000 total. Check your state's amount.

## Who is Eligible?

- Age: **60 years or above**
- Must be from a **BPL (Below Poverty Line) family**
- Should have **no stable income or financial support** from family or other sources
- Must be a **resident of India**
- Not already receiving any other government pension of equivalent nature

## How to Apply

### Via Gram Panchayat / Ward Office (Most Common Method)

1. Visit your **Gram Panchayat office** (rural) or **Ward office / Municipal Corporation** (urban)
2. Ask for the **IGNOAPS application form**
3. Fill the form with your personal details, age, BPL card number, bank details
4. Submit with documents (see below)
5. Gram Panchayat forwards to Block/Tehsil for verification
6. If approved, your name is added to the pension list
7. Pension starts in the next quarter after approval

### Via State Online Portal

Many states have online application systems:
- **UP:** [sspy-up.gov.in](https://sspy-up.gov.in)
- **Bihar:** [sspmis.bihar.gov.in](https://sspmis.bihar.gov.in)
- **MP:** [socialjustice.mp.gov.in](https://socialjustice.mp.gov.in)
- **Rajasthan:** [ssp.rajasthan.gov.in](https://ssp.rajasthan.gov.in)
- **Maharashtra:** [aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in](https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in)

Search "[state name] old age pension apply online" for your state-specific portal.

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card** (identity and Aadhaar-linked DBT)
2. **Age proof** — Aadhaar (if DOB is there), voter ID, birth certificate, or school certificate
3. **BPL card / ration card** showing BPL status
4. **Bank account passbook** — in the applicant's name (Aadhaar-linked)
5. **Income certificate** or self-declaration of no stable income
6. Passport-size photograph

## IGNOAPS vs Other NSAP Pensions

NSAP has multiple pension components — IGNOAPS is for the elderly. Other NSAP pensions:

| Scheme | For | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| **IGNOAPS** | BPL elderly (60+) | ₹200–500/month (central) |
| **IGNWPS** | BPL widows (40–79 years) | ₹300/month (central) |
| **IGNDPS** | BPL disabled (18–79 years) | ₹300/month (central) |
| **NFBS** | BPL families on breadwinner death | ₹20,000 one-time |

A person can only receive one NSAP pension — IGNOAPS is the most widely availed.

## Check Payment Status

1. Go to **[nsap.nic.in](https://nsap.nic.in)**
2. Click "Reports" → select state → beneficiary search
3. Or check your **state social welfare pension portal** for individual status

Payment comes on the **1st of each month** or as per state schedule.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I am 62 and BPL but haven't received pension despite applying 6 months ago. What to do?
Visit your Block Development Office (BDO) or District Social Welfare Office. Ask for your application status in their register. Delays are common — persistent follow-up at the block level helps.

### My mother doesn't have a bank account. Can she still get the pension?
She needs a bank account for DBT. Help her open a Jan Dhan / savings account with her Aadhaar. Post office savings accounts also work.

### Can someone get both widow pension and old age pension?
No — only one NSAP pension per person. A widow over 60 typically gets IGNOAPS (old age pension) as it may be a higher amount than widow pension in some states.

### I live in a city but am BPL. Do I apply at the ward office?
Yes — for urban areas, apply at the **Municipal Ward Office** or Urban Local Body (ULB). The process is similar but run by the city's urban local body.

### Is the pension taxable?
NSAP pension amounts are very small and fall well below the taxable income threshold. In practice, they are not taxed.
