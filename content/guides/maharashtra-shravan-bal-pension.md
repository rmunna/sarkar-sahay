## What is Shravan Bal Seva Rajya Nivruttivetan Yojana?

Shravan Bal Seva Rajya Nivruttivetan Yojana (commonly called Shravan Bal Pension) is a **state old age pension scheme** by the Government of Maharashtra that provides monthly financial assistance to elderly citizens who are **65 years or older** and belong to economically weaker sections.

The scheme is administered by the **Social Justice and Special Assistance Department, Maharashtra** and aims to provide financial dignity and security to senior citizens who have no regular source of income.

## Pension Amount

| Category | Monthly Pension |
|---|---|
| Beneficiaries aged 65-79 years | ₹600 per month |
| Beneficiaries aged 80 years and above | ₹900 per month |

**Note:** The central government's Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS) provides ₹200-₹500, and the state top-up under Shravan Bal brings the total pension amount. The amounts above reflect the combined benefit.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Age:** Must be **65 years or older**
- **Domicile:** Must be a **permanent resident of Maharashtra** (minimum 15 years)
- **Income:** Annual family income should not exceed **₹21,000** (BPL category)
- Must hold a **BPL ration card** (Yellow/Orange/Antyodaya)
- Must **not be receiving pension** from any other government source (central/state/PSU pension)
- Must **not be an income tax payer**
- Not residing in a government-funded old age home

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar Card** — [See our Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
- **Age proof** — Birth certificate, school leaving certificate, or Aadhaar (with DOB)
- **Ration Card** (BPL/Antyodaya) — [Check our ration card guide](/guide/ration-card-apply-online)
- **Income Certificate** (annual income below ₹21,000)
- **Domicile Certificate** (15+ years Maharashtra residence)
- **Bank account passbook** (Aadhaar-linked for DBT)
- **Passport-size photographs** (2 copies)
- **Self-declaration** — Not receiving any other government pension

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Online Application (Aaple Sarkar / MahaDBT)

1. **Register on Aaple Sarkar** — Visit [aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in](https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in) or [mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in](https://mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in)
2. **Create account** using Aadhaar number and mobile number
3. **Login** and navigate to Social Justice department schemes
4. **Select Shravan Bal Pension** from the list
5. **Fill application form** — Enter personal details, age, income, bank details
6. **Upload documents** — Aadhaar, age proof, ration card, income certificate, photo
7. **Submit** and note the application number
8. **Track status** through the same portal

### Offline Application

1. Visit the nearest **Tehsil office** or **District Social Welfare office**
2. Collect the **Shravan Bal pension application form**
3. Fill the form in Marathi or English
4. Attach self-attested photocopies of all documents
5. Submit at the Tehsil office or through the **Setu Suvidha Centre**
6. Collect the acknowledgement receipt

### Application Through CSC (Common Service Centre)

1. Visit any **CSC centre** or **Maha e-Seva Kendra**
2. Provide your Aadhaar and documents
3. The operator will fill and submit the form online on your behalf
4. Nominal processing fee may apply

## Processing Time

- **Application to verification:** 30-60 days
- **Verification to approval:** 15-30 days
- **First pension payment:** Within 1-3 months of approval
- **Subsequent payments:** Monthly via DBT to bank account

## How to Check Status

1. Login to **Aaple Sarkar portal**
2. Go to **"Track Application"**
3. Enter your **application number**
4. View current status, verification stage, and payment history

## Important Tips

1. **Apply as soon as eligible** — There is often a backlog; early application means earlier pension start
2. **Keep bank account active** — Dormant accounts may cause payment failures; make at least one transaction every 6 months
3. **Annual life certificate** — Some districts require an annual "Jeevan Praman" (life certificate) to continue receiving pension. You can submit it digitally via [jeevanpramaan.gov.in](https://jeevanpramaan.gov.in)
4. **Inform on address change** — If you move within Maharashtra, update your address at the new Tehsil office
5. **Combine with other benefits** — You can avail senior citizen rail/bus concessions, free health checkups, and other welfare benefits alongside this pension

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Can I apply if I'm 60 years old?

No, the minimum age for Shravan Bal Pension is **65 years**. If you are between 60-64, you may check the **Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS)** which has a lower age threshold in some states.

### Q2: What if I have a government job pension?

If you are already receiving any **government pension** (central, state, or PSU), you are **not eligible** for Shravan Bal Pension. This scheme is for those with no pension income.

### Q3: Can both husband and wife apply?

Yes, **both spouses** can apply individually if they both meet the eligibility criteria (age 65+, BPL, no other pension). Each will receive the pension separately.

### Q4: What happens if the beneficiary passes away?

The pension **stops immediately** upon the death of the beneficiary. The family should inform the Tehsil office to avoid recovery issues. There is no survivor benefit under this scheme.

### Q5: Is the income limit per person or per family?

The income limit of ₹21,000 is the **annual family income** (combined income of all family members), not individual income.

### Q6: Can NRIs apply?

No, the applicant must be a **permanent resident of Maharashtra** and physically living in the state. NRIs and persons living outside Maharashtra are not eligible.
