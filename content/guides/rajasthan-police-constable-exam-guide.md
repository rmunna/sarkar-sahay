# Rajasthan Police Constable Exam Guide — Eligibility, Syllabus & Tips

The Rajasthan Police Constable recruitment is one of the largest police hiring drives in India. Conducted by the Rajasthan Police Subordinate Board, it attracts lakhs of applicants for the post of Constable (General Duty) and Constable (Driver). This guide covers everything you need to know about the exam — from eligibility to preparation strategy.

## Overview

| Detail | Information |
|--------|-------------|
| Conducting Body | Rajasthan Police Subordinate Board |
| Post | Constable (GD), Constable (Driver) |
| Level | State Level |
| Selection Process | Written Exam → Physical Test → Document Verification |
| Job Location | Rajasthan |
| Salary | ₹21,700 – ₹69,100 (Pay Matrix Level 10) |

## Eligibility Criteria

### Age Limit

- **Minimum Age:** 18 years
- **Maximum Age:** 26 years (as on 1st January of recruitment year)
- **Age Relaxation:** OBC – 3 years, SC/ST – 5 years, EWS – 10 years (as per Rajasthan government norms)

### Educational Qualification

- Passed Class 10th (Matriculation) or equivalent from a recognised board
- For Constable (Driver): Must hold a valid driving licence (LMV/HMV as applicable)

### Nationality

- Must be an Indian citizen
- Must be a domicile of Rajasthan (for certain reserved categories)

### Physical Standards

**For Male Candidates:**
- Height: 168 cm (General/OBC/EWS), 160 cm (SC/ST)
- Chest: 81 cm (unexpanded), 86 cm (expanded)

**For Female Candidates:**
- Height: 152 cm (General/OBC/EWS), 145 cm (SC/ST)

## Exam Pattern

The selection process has two main stages:

### Stage 1: Written Examination

| Subject | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| Reasoning & Logic | 30 | 30 | — |
| General Knowledge & GS | 40 | 40 | — |
| Rajasthan GK | 45 | 45 | — |
| Computer Knowledge | 10 | 10 | — |
| Hindi | 25 | 25 | — |
| **Total** | **150** | **150** | **2 hours** |

- **Marking:** 1 mark for each correct answer
- **Negative Marking:** 0.25 marks deducted for each wrong answer
- **Medium:** Hindi and English (bilingual)

### Stage 2: Physical Efficiency Test (PET)

**For Male Candidates:**
- 5 km running in 25 minutes (flat ground)

**For Female Candidates:**
- 5 km running in 35 minutes (flat ground)

**Note:** PET is qualifying in nature. No marks are awarded.

## Syllabus Overview

### Reasoning & Logic
- Analogy, Series, Coding-Decoding, Direction Test, Blood Relations, Venn Diagram, Syllogism, Non-verbal reasoning, Mirror/Water Image

### General Knowledge & General Science
- Indian History, Geography, Polity, Economics
- Physics, Chemistry, Biology (up to Class 10 level)
- Current Affairs (national and international)

### Rajasthan GK
- Rajasthan History — Rajput dynasties, freedom movement, major historical events
- Rajasthan Geography — climate, rivers, wildlife, lakes, minerals
- Rajasthan Art & Culture — folk dances, music, festivals, paintings, handicrafts
- Rajasthan Polity — governance, Panchayati Raj, state schemes
- Current Affairs of Rajasthan

### Computer Knowledge
- Computer basics, MS Office, Internet, Networking basics, Input/Output devices

### Hindi
- Grammar — Sandhi, Samas, Upsarg, Pratyay, Vilom Shabd, Paryayvachi
- Comprehension passage, Error correction, Muhavare & Lokoktiyan

## Important Dates Pattern

Rajasthan Police Constable recruitment does not follow a fixed annual cycle. Vacancies are released as needed. Typically:

- **Notification:** Released on official website and newspapers
- **Application Window:** 30–45 days from notification date
- **Admit Card:** 15–20 days before exam
- **Written Exam:** Usually within 2–3 months of notification closing
- **PET:** 1–2 months after written exam results
- **Final Result:** 1–2 months after PET completion

**Tip:** Subscribe to official notifications at [police.rajasthan.gov.in](https://police.rajasthan.gov.in) and check regularly.

## Preparation Tips

### 1. Master Rajasthan GK
This is the highest-weighted section (45 marks). Use books like *Rajasthan Adhyayan* by Rai Publication and current affairs compilations specific to Rajasthan.

### 2. Build a Daily Routine
- 2 hours — Rajasthan GK
- 1.5 hours — General Knowledge & Science
- 1 hour — Reasoning
- 30 minutes — Hindi
- 30 minutes — Computer Knowledge

### 3. Practice Previous Year Papers
Rajasthan Police papers follow a pattern. Solving past 5–7 years' papers will help you understand question types and difficulty level.

### 4. Focus on Current Affairs
Read a Hindi daily newspaper (Rajasthan Patrika or Dainik Bhaskar) and maintain monthly notes of state and national current affairs.

### 5. Start Physical Preparation Early
Practice 5 km running daily. Gradually improve your timing. PET eliminates a large number of candidates — don't neglect it.

### 6. Take Mock Tests Weekly
Attempt at least one full-length mock test per week in exam-like conditions. Review mistakes thoroughly.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Is there negative marking in the Rajasthan Police Constable exam?
Yes. 0.25 marks are deducted for each wrong answer. Avoid blind guessing.

### Can candidates from other states apply?
Yes, unreserved category candidates from any state can apply. However, reserved category benefits are generally for Rajasthan domicile holders.

### What is the minimum qualifying percentage in the written exam?
There is no fixed qualifying percentage. A merit-based cutoff is prepared based on the number of vacancies and candidates' performance.

### Is the PET conducted before or after the written exam?
The written exam is conducted first. Only candidates who qualify the written test are called for the Physical Efficiency Test.

### How many attempts are allowed?
There is no limit on the number of attempts as long as you meet the age criteria.

### Is computer knowledge important for the exam?
Computer Knowledge carries 10 marks. It covers basics, so you can score well with minimal preparation.

### What documents are needed for the physical test?
Admit card, original ID proof (Aadhaar/Voter ID), and the original application confirmation. Wear sports clothing and shoes.
