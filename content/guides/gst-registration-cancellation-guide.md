# GST Registration Cancellation 2026 — Step-by-Step Guide

If your business has closed, your turnover has fallen below the GST threshold, or you registered voluntarily and want to exit, you can apply to cancel your GST registration. This guide explains when you can cancel, how to file the application, and what to do after cancellation.

## When Can You Cancel GST Registration?

### You Can Voluntarily Apply If:

| Reason | Eligible? |
|--------|----------|
| Business discontinued / wound up | ✅ Yes |
| Change in business constitution (merger, sale) | ✅ Yes |
| Turnover falls below threshold (₹40L for goods, ₹20L for services) | ✅ Yes |
| Voluntary registrant who no longer needs GST | ✅ Yes |
| Business transferred as going concern | ✅ Yes |

### You Cannot Cancel If:

- You have **pending GST returns** (all returns must be filed first)
- Your registration is already **suspended by the department** (different process)
- You are registered under **composition scheme** — use a different procedure

## Prerequisites Before Cancellation

1. **File all pending GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B returns** (including the period up to the date of cancellation)
2. **Pay all pending GST dues** — outstanding tax, interest, and late fees
3. **Account for input tax credit (ITC)**: You must reverse ITC on remaining stock/assets when cancelling

## Step-by-Step: How to Cancel GST Online

### Step 1: Login to GST Portal

1. Go to **[gst.gov.in](https://gst.gov.in/)**
2. Login with your credentials (username + password)
3. Go to **Services** → **Registration** → **Application for Cancellation**

### Step 2: Fill Form GST REG-16

Fill in:
1. **Reason for cancellation** (select from dropdown):
   - Business discontinued
   - Change in constitution
   - Ceased to be liable to pay tax (turnover below threshold)
   - Others
2. **Date from which cancellation is sought**: The date you want cancellation effective
3. **Value of stock on cancellation date**: Goods in stock + capital goods on the cancellation date
4. **ITC to be reversed**: Calculated on the stock value mentioned

### Step 3: Submit the Application

1. Verify with DSC (Digital Signature Certificate) or EVC (Electronic Verification Code)
2. Submit the application
3. Application Reference Number (ARN) generated

### Step 4: Reply to Any Department Queries

The GST officer may ask for clarifications or additional documents. Respond within the time specified in the notice.

### Step 5: Receive Cancellation Order

If satisfied, the officer issues:
- **Form GST REG-19**: Cancellation Order — your GSTIN is cancelled effective from the mentioned date

## ITC Reversal at Cancellation

When you cancel GST registration, you must reverse Input Tax Credit on:
- Stock in hand (raw material, semi-finished, finished goods)
- Capital goods (adjusted for useful life remaining)

**ITC to reverse** = ITC taken on current stock × (remaining life for capital goods)

This reversed ITC is paid as tax in the final return (GSTR-10).

## After Cancellation: File GSTR-10 (Final Return)

After cancellation, you must file **GSTR-10 (Final Return)**:
- **Deadline**: Within **3 months** of cancellation date (or date of order, whichever is later)
- **What to report**: Details of stock on hand, ITC to be reversed, tax payable
- **Platform**: gst.gov.in → Returns → File Final Return

> If you fail to file GSTR-10 within 3 months, you'll receive a notice (GSTR-3A) and may be liable for late fees.

## How Long Does GST Cancellation Take?

| Stage | Timeline |
|-------|---------|
| Application submission | Immediate |
| Officer review | 30–90 days typically |
| If no deficiency notice | Cancelled within 30 working days |
| If officer raises query | 90+ days (after query response) |

The cancellation is effective from the date you specify in the application (or a date approved by the officer).

## Difference: Cancellation vs Suspension

| Aspect | Voluntary Cancellation | Suspension |
|--------|----------------------|-----------|
| **Who initiates** | Taxpayer | GST Department |
| **Reason** | Business decision | Non-compliance |
| **Returns required** | File up to cancellation date | Returns still must be filed |
| **Restoration** | Can re-register afresh | Can be revoked if compliance done |

## Revocation of Cancellation (If Department Cancelled Your GST)

If the GST department cancelled your registration (not you), you can apply for **revocation of cancellation**:
- File Form GST REG-21 within **90 days** of cancellation order
- File all pending returns and pay dues first
- If officer rejects, you have 30 days to appeal

## FAQs

### I closed my business but didn't file GST returns for the last 6 months. Can I cancel?

You must first file all pending returns (GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B for each unpaid month) and pay all outstanding tax + late fees. Only then can you apply for cancellation.

### After cancellation, can I use my GSTIN for existing invoices?

No — once cancelled, the GSTIN is no longer valid. Any invoices dated after the effective cancellation date cannot have this GSTIN. For pending transactions, close them before the cancellation date.

### My turnover is ₹18 lakh but I'm registered for GST. Can I cancel?

Yes — if your aggregate turnover is below ₹40 lakh (goods) or ₹20 lakh (services), you're not required to be GST registered. You can cancel as a "voluntary cancellation — ceased to be liable." However, check if you have inter-state supplies, as that requires GST registration regardless of turnover.

### Do I need CA help for GST cancellation?

For simple cancellations (business closed, no complex ITC reversals), you can do it yourself on gst.gov.in. For businesses with large ITC balances or disputed reversals, a CA helps calculate the reversal correctly and avoid demands.
