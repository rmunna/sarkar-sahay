# NIOS Open Schooling Admission Guide 2026 – Eligibility, Process, Results & More

The **National Institute of Open Schooling (NIOS)** is India's largest open schooling system, offering flexible education to millions of students who cannot attend regular schools. Whether you dropped out, failed board exams, or want to study while working, NIOS provides a recognised pathway to complete your **Class 10th (Secondary)** and **Class 12th (Senior Secondary)** education.

This guide covers everything you need to know about NIOS — from admission and eligibility to exam pattern, results, and study material.

---

## What is NIOS?

NIOS (National Institute of Open Schooling), formerly known as the National Open School (NOS), was established in **1989** by the Ministry of Education, Government of India. It is an autonomous organisation that provides education through **open and distance learning (ODL)** mode.

### Key Facts About NIOS

- **Recognition:** NIOS certificates are recognised by all Indian universities, government jobs, and competitive exams — equivalent to CBSE and state boards.
- **Courses offered:** Secondary (Class 10), Senior Secondary (Class 12), Vocational Education, and Open Basic Education (equivalent to Class 1–8).
- **Medium of instruction:** Hindi, English, Urdu, Marathi, Telugu, Gujarati, Malayalam, Odia, Bengali, Tamil, Kannada, and Punjabi.
- **Flexibility:** Students can choose subjects, exam timing, and study at their own pace.
- **Toll-free helpline:** 1800-180-9393

## Who is Eligible for NIOS Admission?

### Secondary Course (Class 10th)
- **Minimum age:** 14 years as on the date of admission
- **Qualification:** No minimum educational qualification required
- **Who can apply:** Anyone — school dropouts, those who failed Class 9 or 10, working adults

### Senior Secondary Course (Class 12th)
- **Minimum age:** 15 years as on the date of admission
- **Qualification:** Must have passed Class 10 from any recognised board (CBSE, ICSE, State Board, or NIOS)
- **Who can apply:** Class 10 pass students, those who failed Class 11 or 12, professionals wanting higher qualification

### Special Provisions
- No upper age limit for any course
- Differently-abled students get fee concession and extra exam time
- Ex-servicemen, SC/ST candidates, and women get fee concessions
- Transfer of Credit (TOC) available for students who passed some subjects from other boards

## How to Get Admission in NIOS – Step-by-Step

NIOS admission is done entirely **online** through the SDMIS (Student Data Management Information System) portal. Follow these steps:

### Step 1: Visit the Official Portal
Go to [sdmis.nios.ac.in](https://sdmis.nios.ac.in) and click on **"Online Admission"** for the relevant stream (Stream 1 for block admission or Stream 2 for on-demand).

### Step 2: Register and Fill the Form
- Create an account with your mobile number and email
- Fill in personal details, course selection, and subject choices
- Choose a minimum of **5 subjects** (you can pick up to 7)
- Select your nearest **Study Centre (AI/AVI)**

### Step 3: Upload Documents
Upload scanned copies of:
- Passport-size photograph
- Signature
- Date of birth proof (Aadhaar, birth certificate)
- Educational qualification certificate (for Class 12 admission)
- Category certificate (if applicable)

### Step 4: Pay Fees Online
- **Secondary (Class 10):** Approximately ₹1,800 (General) / ₹1,200 (SC/ST/Ex-Servicemen)
- **Senior Secondary (Class 12):** Approximately ₹2,000 (General) / ₹1,350 (SC/ST/Ex-Servicemen)
- Payment via net banking, UPI, debit/credit card
- *Fee amounts are approximate and may change. Check the official site for current fees.*

### Step 5: Download Confirmation
After payment, download and save your admission confirmation letter and reference number for future use.

### Admission Streams
| Stream | Description | Admission Window |
|--------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Stream 1 (Block 1)** | Regular admission | Usually March–September |
| **Stream 1 (Block 2)** | Regular admission | Usually September–January |
| **Stream 2** | On-Demand Examination | Open throughout the year |

> **Tip:** Stream 2 (On-Demand) lets you give exams any month — ideal if you need quick results for college admission or job applications.

## NIOS Exam Pattern

NIOS follows a **flexible examination system** unlike traditional boards:

- **Theory exams:** Held twice a year — **April/May** and **October/November**
- **Practical exams:** Conducted at study centres before theory exams
- **Tutor Marked Assignments (TMA):** 20% weightage — must be submitted to your study centre
- **Theory paper:** 80% weightage
- **Pass marks:** 33% in each subject individually
- **Validity:** Students get up to **5 years** (9 cumulative chances) to clear all subjects
- **On-Demand Exam (ODE):** Available for select subjects — appear when ready

### Grading System
NIOS awards grades from A to E based on marks obtained, with the overall percentage mentioned on the marksheet.

## How to Check NIOS Results

NIOS results are declared on the official results portal after each examination cycle. The October/November 2025 results were declared in **January 2026**.

### Steps to Check Results:
1. Visit [results.nios.ac.in](https://results.nios.ac.in)
2. Select your examination (Secondary or Senior Secondary)
3. Enter your **enrolment number**
4. Click **"Submit"** to view your result
5. Download and print your marksheet

### How to Download Hall Ticket:
1. Visit [sdmis.nios.ac.in](https://sdmis.nios.ac.in)
2. Log in with your enrolment number and date of birth
3. Click on **"Hall Ticket"** section
4. Download and take a printout for the exam

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Is the NIOS certificate valid for government jobs?
**Yes.** NIOS certificates are fully recognised by the Government of India, UGC, AICTE, and all state governments. You can apply for any government job, competitive exam (UPSC, SSC, banking), or university admission with an NIOS certificate.

### 2. Can I appear for NIOS exams without attending classes?
**Yes.** NIOS is an open schooling system — there is no mandatory attendance. You study at home using NIOS study material (available free online) and appear for exams at your assigned centre.

### 3. How many times can I attempt the NIOS exam?
You get **9 chances spread over 5 years** from the date of admission. You only need to re-appear in subjects you haven't cleared yet.

### 4. Can I take admission in NIOS if I failed in CBSE or a state board?
**Yes.** You can take fresh admission in NIOS. Additionally, through the **Transfer of Credit (TOC)** facility, subjects you already passed in another board can be transferred to NIOS — you only need to clear the remaining subjects.

### 5. What is the difference between Stream 1 and Stream 2 in NIOS?
**Stream 1** follows a regular exam schedule (April/May and October/November). **Stream 2** is On-Demand Examination — you can choose your own exam date and get results within 45 days. Stream 2 is faster but available for limited subjects.

### 6. Can I change my subjects after admission?
**Yes.** NIOS allows subject change within a specified period after admission by paying a nominal fee. Check the SDMIS portal for the change window.

### 7. Is there an age limit for NIOS admission?
There is a **minimum age** requirement (14 years for Class 10, 15 years for Class 12) but **no maximum age limit**. Anyone can study through NIOS regardless of age.
