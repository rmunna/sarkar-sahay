## What is Mukhyamantri Jan Kalyan Sambal Yojana?

**Mukhyamantri Jan Kalyan Sambal Yojana** (also known as Sambal Yojana) is a comprehensive welfare scheme by the Madhya Pradesh government for **unorganized sector workers**. The scheme provides a safety net covering accidental insurance, education support, funeral assistance, maternity benefits, and more — all under one umbrella.

The scheme was originally launched as **Mukhyamantri Jan Kalyan Yojana** and later restructured. It is managed through the **Sambal portal** where eligible workers can register and claim benefits using a **Sambal Card / Shramik Card**.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Worker type:** Must be an unorganized sector worker (daily wage laborer, construction worker, street vendor, rickshaw puller, domestic worker, agricultural laborer, etc.)
- **Domicile:** Must be a permanent resident of Madhya Pradesh
- **BPL/Low income:** Must be from Below Poverty Line (BPL) family or have income below prescribed limit
- **Age:** 18 to 60 years for registration
- **Not in organized sector:** Should not be employed in any government or organized private sector job
- **Samagra ID:** Must have a **Samagra ID** (MP's family/individual identity system)

## Benefits Under the Scheme

| Benefit | Amount / Coverage |
|---------|-------------------|
| Accidental death insurance | ₹4 lakh |
| Permanent disability (accident) | ₹4 lakh |
| Partial disability (accident) | ₹2 lakh |
| Normal death benefit | ₹2 lakh |
| Funeral assistance | ₹5,000 |
| Education support (Class 12 pass) | Varies by merit |
| Maternity benefit (for women workers) | ₹16,000 (₹4,000 x 4 installments) |
| Skill development training | Free |
| Subsidized power bill | For BPL families |

### Education Benefits (Meritorious Students)

Children of registered Sambal cardholders who pass Class 12 with good marks receive scholarships:

| Board Exam Result | Amount |
|-------------------|--------|
| 85%+ marks in Class 12 | ₹30,000 |
| 75-85% marks in Class 12 | ₹25,000 |

Students admitted to government colleges/ITIs also get additional support for higher education.

## Documents Required

**For Sambal Card Registration:**
- **Samagra ID** (family and individual) — this is mandatory
- Aadhaar Card (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- BPL card / Income certificate
- Bank account passbook (Aadhaar-linked)
- Passport-size photographs
- Proof of being an unorganized worker (self-declaration or employer certificate)
- Mobile number

**For claiming benefits:**
- Sambal Card / Registration number
- Relevant proof (death certificate, disability certificate, hospital records, marksheet, etc.)
- FIR copy (for accident claims)

## How to Register — Step-by-Step

### Online Registration

1. **Visit Sambal portal:** Go to [sambal.mp.gov.in](https://sambal.mp.gov.in)
2. **Click on "पंजीयन हेतु आवेदन करें"** (Apply for Registration)
3. **Enter Samagra ID:** Enter your Samagra family ID and individual member ID
4. **Verify details:** Your name, address, and family details will auto-populate from the Samagra database
5. **Fill worker details:** Select your occupation type (construction worker, street vendor, domestic worker, etc.)
6. **Upload documents:** Upload Aadhaar, bank passbook, and photograph
7. **Submit application:** Review and submit
8. **Gram Panchayat / Ward verification:** Your application is verified at the local body level
9. **Sambal Card issued:** After verification, your Sambal Card / registration number is generated

### Offline Registration

1. Visit your nearest **Gram Panchayat** (rural) or **Nagar Palika / Ward office** (urban)
2. Collect the Sambal Yojana registration form
3. Fill in details and attach required documents
4. Submit at the Gram Panchayat / Ward office
5. The Sarpanch / Ward member verifies your worker status
6. Your application is forwarded to the district level for approval

### Via Jan Seva Kendra / MP Online

1. Visit your nearest **Jan Seva Kendra** or **MP Online Kiosk**
2. The operator will assist with online registration
3. Nominal service fee of ₹20-50 may apply

## How to Claim Benefits

### For Accidental Death / Disability:

1. Log in to [sambal.mp.gov.in](https://sambal.mp.gov.in)
2. Click on "दुर्घटना दावा" (Accident Claim)
3. Enter Sambal Card number and Samagra ID
4. Upload FIR, death certificate / disability certificate, hospital records
5. Submit claim — it will be verified by the district collector's office
6. Amount disbursed to nominee's bank account within 30-60 days

### For Education Scholarship:

1. After Class 12 results are declared, eligible students are auto-identified through the Sambal database
2. Students can also apply through the portal with their marksheet and Sambal Card details
3. Scholarship amount is transferred to student's bank account

## Processing Time

| Claim Type | Timeline |
|------------|----------|
| Registration | 15-30 days |
| Accidental death/disability claim | 30-60 days |
| Education scholarship | 60-90 days (after results) |
| Maternity benefit | 30-45 days per installment |

## Fees

Registration and all claims under Sambal Yojana are **completely free**. There is no premium or fee to be paid by the worker.

## Important Tips

1. **Get Samagra ID first** — Samagra ID is mandatory. Apply at [samagra.gov.in](https://samagra.gov.in) if you don't have one.
2. **Keep Sambal Card safe** — Note down your Sambal Card registration number. You'll need it for all claims.
3. **Report accidents immediately** — For accidental death/disability claims, file FIR and hospital records promptly. Delays can complicate the claim process.
4. **Update bank details** — Ensure your bank account is active and linked with Aadhaar for smooth DBT payments (see our [Aadhaar linking guide](/guide/aadhaar-linking-guide)).
5. **Check scholarship eligibility** — If your child is in Class 12, ensure your Sambal registration is active before the board exam results.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. What is a Sambal Card?

A Sambal Card is a registration document/number issued to unorganized sector workers in MP under the Jan Kalyan Sambal Yojana. It is linked to your Samagra ID and enables you to claim all scheme benefits.

### Q2. Is there any premium or fee for the insurance benefit?

No, all benefits including the **₹4 lakh accidental insurance** are completely free. The MP government funds the scheme entirely.

### Q3. Who is considered an "unorganized worker"?

Workers not covered under ESI or EPFO — such as daily wage laborers, construction workers, street vendors, rickshaw pullers, agricultural laborers, domestic workers, and similar occupations.

### Q4. Can women workers get maternity benefits?

Yes, registered women workers can get **₹16,000 in maternity benefit** paid in 4 installments of ₹4,000 each during pregnancy and after delivery.

### Q5. My Samagra ID shows wrong details. What should I do?

Visit your Gram Panchayat or Nagar Palika office to get your Samagra ID details updated. You can also update some details online at [samagra.gov.in](https://samagra.gov.in).

### Q6. Can I register if I already have a labour card from the Labour Department?

Yes, having a construction worker labour card does not prevent you from registering under Sambal Yojana. However, for some specific benefits, only one scheme's benefit can be claimed at a time.

### Q7. Is the scheme available for workers from other states living in MP?

No, you must be a **permanent resident of Madhya Pradesh** with a valid Samagra ID to register under this scheme.
