# UP Police Constable 2026 — Complete Recruitment Guide

Uttar Pradesh Police conducts one of India's largest state police recruitments. The last major recruitment (2023–24) had **60,244 vacancies** — and UP Police 2026 is expected to have a significant vacancy count again. This is one of the most sought-after state police jobs.

**Conducting Body:** UP Police Recruitment and Promotion Board (UPPRPB)  
**Official website:** [uppbpb.gov.in](https://uppbpb.gov.in)

---

## UP Police Constable 2026 — Eligibility

| Criteria | Requirement |
|----------|------------|
| **Age** | 18–22 years (General); 18–25 years (SC/ST/OBC UP domicile) |
| **Education** | 10+2 (Intermediate) from recognized board |
| **Gender** | Male + Female (separate vacancy count) |
| **Domicile** | UP domicile mandatory |
| **Height (Male)** | General/OBC: 168 cm; SC/ST: 160 cm |
| **Height (Female)** | General/OBC: 152 cm; SC/ST/ST: 147 cm |
| **Chest (Male only)** | Minimum 79 cm (unexpanded); 84 cm expanded |
| **Weight (Female)** | Proportionate to height |

## Selection Process — 3 Stages

### Stage 1: Written Exam (300 Marks)
### Stage 2: Physical Efficiency Test (PET) + Physical Standard Test (PST)
### Stage 3: Medical Examination

## Syllabus — Topic-wise

### General Knowledge:
Current affairs, Indian history, Indian geography, Indian polity (Constitution basics), important government schemes, UP-specific GK (UP history, culture, geography), Sports, important dates

### General Hindi:
Grammar (Sandhi, Samas, Vilom, Paryayvachi), Reading comprehension, Sentence correction, Fill in the blanks, Hindi literature (basic)

### Numerical & Mental Ability:
Number systems, HCF-LCM, Percentage, Ratio and proportion, Average, Profit & Loss, Simple and Compound Interest, Time & Work, Time & Distance, Simple Data Interpretation

### Mental Aptitude / IQ / Reasoning:
Logical reasoning, Series completion, Analogy, Coding-decoding, Classification, Blood relations, Direction & Distance, Clock and Calendar, Mirror images

## Physical Efficiency Test (PET)

| Test | Male | Female |
|------|------|--------|
| 4.8 km Run | ≤ 25 minutes | Not applicable |
| 2.4 km Run | Not applicable | ≤ 14 minutes |
| Long Jump | ≥ 4 meters (3 chances) | ≥ 2.5 meters |
| High Jump | ≥ 1.1 meters | ≥ 1.0 meter |
| Shot Put (7.26 kg / 4 kg) | ≥ 4.5 meters | ≥ 2.5 meters (3 kg) |

**PET is qualifying** — must pass all events. No extra marks for better performance.

## Preparation Strategy

### 3-Month Plan:

**Month 1: Foundation**
- General Hindi: Grammar rules (Sandhi, Samas) — UP Police gives heavy Hindi weightage
- UP GK: History (1857 revolt UP connections, Mughal era, modern UP), districts, rivers, culture
- Maths: Basic arithmetic, percentage, ratio — solve 20 questions daily

**Month 2: Practice**
- Previous year papers (2023 pattern) — available on Safalta, Testbook
- Reasoning: Daily 30 questions (series, coding, direction)
- Current Affairs: Last 6 months — focus on UP government schemes

**Month 3: Revision + Physical**
- Mock tests: 2 full mocks per week
- Physical training: Run 4.8 km daily (build to 23-min completion)
- Practice Long Jump + Shot Put (3 attempts each event in PET)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**UP Police Constable — can I apply from other states?**
No — UP domicile is mandatory. You need a UP domicile certificate. Children of UP government employees posted outside UP may also qualify — check the notification for exact domicile rules.

**UP Police Constable after 12th — is graduation needed?**
No — 12th pass (Intermediate) is sufficient. Graduation is not required. This makes it accessible to a large candidate pool.

**What is the physical standard test (PST) vs PET?**
PST is physical measurement (height, chest). Done first — if you don't meet the standard, you're eliminated. PET is the running/jumping performance test — done after PST clearing.

**Can a female candidate apply for UP Police Constable 2026?**
Yes — females can apply. Separate vacancy count for female candidates (typically 20% reservation). Female PET is different (shorter run, lower jump standards). There is no chest measurement for females.

**UP Police Constable — how many attempts allowed?**
No attempt limit. Age is the limiting factor. If you're within 18–22 years (General) or 18–25 years (SC/ST/OBC), you can apply every time a notification comes.
