# Bihar Bhumi Jankari — Check [land records](/guide/land-records-bhulekh-online), Khata & Khesra Online (2026)

Bihar's land record system has been digitised under the **Bhumi Jankari** portal. Citizens can now view Khata (खाता), Khesra (खेसरा), Jamabandi (जमाबंदी), land maps, and mutation status online — without visiting the Circle Office.

This guide covers every step, from basic search to getting a certified copy.

---

## What Is Bhumi Jankari?

**Bhumi Jankari** (भूमि जानकारी) is the official Bihar government portal for digitised land records. It is managed by the **Revenue & Land Reforms Department, Government of Bihar**.

The system consolidates:

- **Khata (खाता)** — land account/ownership record
- **Khesra (खेसरा)** — individual plot number within a Khata
- **Jamabandi (जमाबंदी)** — register of land rights showing ownership, area, rent, and crop details
- **Land maps (भू-नक्शा)** — cadastral plot maps
- **Mutation records (दाखिल-खारिज)** — ownership transfer entries

### Key Portals

| Portal | URL | Purpose |
|--------|-----|---------|
| Bhumi Jankari | bhumijankari.bihar.gov.in | Land records (Khata, Khesra, Jamabandi) |
| Apna Khata / LRC | lrc.bih.nic.in | Record of Rights (RoR), old records |
| Bihar Bhunaksha | bhunaksha.bihar.gov.in | Cadastral maps |
| e-Mutation | emutation.bihar.gov.in | Mutation application & status |
| Revenue Court | biharrevenuecourt.gov.in | Land dispute case status |

## How to Check Land Records Online (Step-by-Step)

### Method 1: Search via Bhumi Jankari Portal

1. Visit **http://bhumijankari.bihar.gov.in**
2. Click **"अपना खाता देखें"** (View Your Account)
3. Select your **जिला (District)** from the map or dropdown
4. Select your **अनुमंडल (Sub-division)**
5. Select your **अंचल (Circle/Block)**
6. Select your **मौजा (Village)** — you can search alphabetically
7. Choose a search method:
   - **खाता संख्या (Khata Number)** — if you know your account number
   - **खेसरा संख्या (Khesra/Plot Number)** — if you know the plot number
   - **खाताधारी का नाम (Owner Name)** — search by name
8. Click **"खाता खोजें"** (Search Account)
9. Your land record will display: owner name, father's name, Khata number, Khesra numbers, area (in acres/decimals), and land type

> **Tip:** If searching by name, type at least the first few characters in Hindi (Devanagari script) for accurate results.

### Method 2: Search via LRC Portal (Apna Khata)

1. Visit **http://lrc.bih.nic.in/ror.aspx**
2. Follow the same District → Sub-division → Circle → Village hierarchy
3. Enter Khata or Khesra number
4. View the digitised Record of Rights (RoR)

## How to View Jamabandi (जमाबंदी)

Jamabandi is the detailed register of rights for each plot. To view it:

1. Go to **bhumijankari.bihar.gov.in**
2. Click **"जमाबंदी पंजी देखें"** (View Jamabandi Register)
3. Select District → Sub-division → Circle → Halka → Village → Khata
4. The Jamabandi will show:
   - Owner name and share
   - Plot-wise area
   - Land classification (agricultural, homestead, etc.)
   - Rent/revenue amount
   - Remarks on encumbrances or disputes

## How to View Land Map (भू-नक्शा / Bhunaksha)

1. Visit **http://bhunaksha.bihar.gov.in**
2. Select **District → Sub-division → Circle → Mauza**
3. The cadastral map will load showing all plots
4. Click on any plot to see:
   - Khesra number
   - Owner name
   - Area
   - Land use type
5. Use **Print** to save or download the map

> **Note:** Map data may not be available for all villages. Digitisation is ongoing.

## How to Get a Certified Copy of Land Records

A certified copy (प्रमाणित प्रति) is legally valid and needed for bank loans, property sales, and court matters.

### Online Method

1. Visit **bhumijankari.bihar.gov.in**
2. Click **"दाखिल खारिज / एल.पी.सी. आवेदन"**
3. Register or log in
4. Select the record type (Jamabandi, Khata, etc.)
5. Fill in land details (District, Circle, Village, Khata/Khesra)
6. Pay the fee online (₹50–₹100 depending on record type)
7. Download the digitally signed copy, or collect from the Circle Office

### Offline Method

1. Visit your local **अंचल कार्यालय (Circle Office)**
2. Submit an application with Khata/Khesra details
3. Pay the prescribed fee
4. Collect the certified copy (usually 3–7 working days)

## Mutation Process in Bihar (दाखिल-खारिज)

Mutation (Dakhil Kharij) updates the land record when ownership changes — through sale, inheritance, gift, or partition.

### How to Apply for Mutation Online

1. Visit **http://emutation.bihar.gov.in**
2. Click **"आवेदन करें"** (Apply)
3. Select District → Circle → Village
4. Enter Khata and Khesra number
5. Upload supporting documents:
   - Sale deed / succession certificate / gift deed
   - Aadhaar card of applicant
   - LPC (Land Possession Certificate) if applicable
6. Pay the application fee online
7. Submit — you will get an **application number**

### How to Check Mutation Status

1. Visit **emutation.bihar.gov.in**
2. Click **"दाखिल खारिज स्थिति"** (Mutation Status)
3. Enter your application number or search by District/Circle
4. Status will show: Pending / Under Process / Approved / Rejected

> **Timeline:** Mutation should be completed within **60 days** as per government guidelines. In practice, it may take 2–4 months.

## Revenue Court Case Status

For land dispute cases in Bihar Revenue Courts:

1. Visit **http://biharrevenuecourt.gov.in**
2. Select your **District** and **Court**
3. Search by:
   - Case number
   - Party name
   - Advocate name
4. View case status, hearing dates, and orders

## Understanding Bihar Land Record Terms

| Term (Hindi) | English | Meaning |
|--------------|---------|---------|
| खाता (Khata) | Account | Group of plots held by one owner/family |
| खेसरा (Khesra) | Plot Number | Unique survey number of a land parcel |
| जमाबंदी (Jamabandi) | Register of Rights | Detailed ownership and land-use record |
| दाखिल-खारिज (Dakhil Kharij) | Mutation | Transfer of ownership in revenue records |
| रैयत (Raiyat) | Tenant/Owner | Person who holds land rights |
| मौजा (Mauza) | Village | Revenue village unit |
| अंचल (Anchal) | Circle | Revenue administrative circle |
| हल्का (Halka) | Halka | Sub-unit within a Circle |
| भू-नक्शा (Bhu-Naksha) | Land Map | Cadastral/plot map |
| एल.पी.सी. (LPC) | Land Possession Certificate | Proof of current possession |

## Common Issues & Troubleshooting

### "My name is not showing in the records"

- Records may use an older name spelling (in Hindi). Try variations.
- If land was inherited but mutation not done, the previous owner's name will appear. Apply for mutation first.

### "The website is not loading"

- Bihar government portals experience heavy traffic. Try during off-peak hours (early morning or late evening).
- Use **Google Chrome** or **Mozilla Firefox** for best compatibility.
- Clear browser cache if the page loads partially.

### "Map is not available for my village"

- Bhunaksha digitisation is still in progress for some districts. Visit the Circle Office for a physical copy of the map.

### "Records show wrong area or wrong owner"

- File a correction application at your Circle Office with supporting documents (old records, sale deed, etc.).
- For disputes, approach the Revenue Court.

### "I need records for land before 2006"

- Older records may not be digitised. Visit the District Collectorate or Sub-Registrar office for manual records.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the online land record legally valid?

Online records from Bhumi Jankari are for **informational purposes**. For legal use, you need a **certified copy** (प्रमाणित प्रति) from the Circle Office or a digitally signed copy from the portal.

### Can I download the land record as PDF?

Yes. After viewing your Khata/Khesra details, use the **Print** option in your browser (Ctrl+P) and select "Save as PDF".

### Is there any fee to view records online?

No. Viewing records online is **free**. Fees apply only for certified copies and mutation applications.

### How do I check if my land has any encumbrance?

Check the Jamabandi register for remarks. For a full encumbrance search, visit the **Sub-Registrar Office** of your district.

### Can NRIs check Bihar land records?

Yes. The portal is accessible from anywhere. For mutation or certified copies, an NRI can appoint a power-of-attorney holder to act on their behalf.
