## What is Him Care Health Scheme?

**Him Care** is a health insurance scheme by the **Government of Himachal Pradesh** that provides cashless treatment of up to **₹5 lakh per family per year** at empanelled hospitals. It was launched to cover families who are **not eligible** under the central Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY scheme.

The scheme is managed by the **HP Swasthya Bima Yojana Society (HPSBYS)** and covers hospitalization expenses including surgeries, diagnostics, medicines, and pre/post-hospitalization care.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Residency:** Must be a permanent resident of Himachal Pradesh
2. **Not Covered Under PM-JAY:** Families already covered under Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY cannot enroll under Him Care (they are already covered)
3. **Registration:** Family must register and pay the nominal annual premium
4. **All Categories:** Open to all HP residents irrespective of income — anyone not covered under PM-JAY can enroll

## Premium & Coverage

| Category | Annual Premium | Coverage |
|----------|---------------|----------|
| BPL families | Free (state pays) | ₹5,00,000/year |
| Registered Street Vendors | Free | ₹5,00,000/year |
| Single Women / Elderly (70+) | Free / Subsidized | ₹5,00,000/year |
| General families (APL) | ₹365 - ₹1,000/year (nominal) | ₹5,00,000/year |

> Premium rates may be updated. Check [hpsbys.in](https://www.hpsbys.in) for the latest rates.

## Benefits

- Cashless hospitalization at empanelled hospitals (government + private)
- 1,500+ medical/surgical procedures covered
- Pre-existing diseases covered
- Pre-hospitalization (3 days) and post-hospitalization (15 days) expenses
- Transport allowance
- No cap on family size — all members covered

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar Card** of all family members
- **Ration Card** or family details
- **HP Bonafide Certificate** / Domicile proof
- **Bank account details** (for premium payment if applicable)
- **Passport-size photographs**
- **BPL Card** (for free enrollment)

## How to Register for Him Care Card

### Online Registration

1. **Visit** [himcare.hp.gov.in](https://himcare.hp.gov.in) or [hpsbys.in](https://www.hpsbys.in)
2. **Click** on "Him Care Registration" or "Apply Now"
3. **Enter** Aadhaar number and family details
4. **Upload** required documents
5. **Pay** the annual premium online (if applicable — BPL families are free)
6. **Download** the Him Care e-card after successful registration
7. **Print** or save the card on your phone

### Offline Registration

1. **Visit** the nearest **Lok Mitra Kendra (LMK)** or **Common Service Centre (CSC)**
2. **Carry** Aadhaar Card and Ration Card for all family members
3. **Pay** the premium (if applicable)
4. **Biometric verification** will be done
5. **Him Care card** will be generated

### At Hospital (Emergency Registration)

1. **Visit** any empanelled hospital
2. **Go to** the Ayushman/Him Care help desk
3. **Emergency registration** can be done at the hospital itself
4. **Carry** Aadhaar Card for biometric verification

## How to Use Him Care Card

1. **Visit** any Him Care-empanelled hospital
2. **Show** your Him Care card and Aadhaar at the reception/Ayushman desk
3. **Biometric verification** will confirm your identity
4. **Get admitted** for cashless treatment
5. **No payment** needed for covered procedures — the hospital bills the government directly

## Important Tips

1. **Renew Annually:** Him Care registration must be renewed every year with premium payment
2. **Carry Aadhaar:** Always carry your Aadhaar for biometric verification at hospitals
3. **Check Hospital List:** Verify the hospital is empanelled before going — check at [hpsbys.in](https://www.hpsbys.in)
4. **Helpline:** Call **104** (HP health helpline) for Him Care queries
5. **Don't Pay Cash:** For covered treatments, you should not need to pay anything out of pocket

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. What is the coverage under Him Care?
**₹5 lakh per family per year** for hospitalization and surgical procedures.

### Q2. Who needs to pay premium?
BPL families, single women, and elderly (70+) get free enrollment. APL families pay a nominal annual premium of ₹365-₹1,000.

### Q3. Can I use Him Care at private hospitals?
Yes, at any empanelled private hospital in HP or outside HP (for referral cases).

### Q4. Is Him Care the same as Ayushman Bharat?
No. Him Care is the state scheme for families **not covered** under Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY. Together, they cover almost all HP residents.

### Q5. Are pre-existing diseases covered?
Yes, pre-existing conditions are covered from the date of enrollment.

### Q6. How do I renew my Him Care card?
Visit the HPSBYS portal or nearest LMK and pay the annual premium before your card expires.

### Q7. What if the hospital refuses cashless treatment?
Contact the Him Care helpline or HPSBYS office. You can also call **104** for assistance.
