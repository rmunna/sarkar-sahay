# TSPSC Exam Guide — Telangana State Public Service Commission

The Telangana State Public Service Commission (TSPSC) conducts recruitment examinations for Group 1, Group 2, Group 3, and Group 4 posts in the Telangana state government. Established after the formation of Telangana in 2014, TSPSC has become one of the most sought-after state PSC exams in South India.

## What is TSPSC?

TSPSC is the constitutional body responsible for recruiting candidates to various civil services and government posts in Telangana. The commission conducts exams for:

- **Group 1 Services** — Deputy Collector, DSP, District Registrar, and other gazetted posts
- **Group 2 Services** — Municipal Commissioner, Mandal Parishad Development Officer, Excise Superintendent
- **Group 3 Services** — Junior Assistant, Typist, Stenographer
- **Group 4 Services** — Junior Steno, Junior Accountant, attender-level posts

## Eligibility Criteria

### Age Limit

| Category | Minimum Age | Maximum Age |
|----------|------------|------------|
| General | 18 years | 44 years |
| OBC | 18 years | 47 years |
| SC/ST | 18 years | 49 years |
| PwD | 18 years | 54 years |

Age is calculated as on 1st July of the notification year.

### Educational Qualification

- **Group 1:** Bachelor's degree from a recognised university
- **Group 2:** Bachelor's degree from a recognised university
- **Group 3:** Intermediate (10+2) / Degree depending on post
- **Group 4:** SSC (10th class) / Intermediate depending on post

### Nationality

- Must be a citizen of India
- Must satisfy local status requirements as per Telangana state rules

## TSPSC Group 1 Exam Pattern

### Stage 1: Preliminary Examination

| Subject | Questions | Marks | Duration |

- Objective type (MCQ)

- Qualifying in nature only

| Paper | Subject | Marks | Duration |
|-------|---------|-------|----------|

| Paper 2 | History, Culture & Geography of India and Telangana | 150 | 3 hours |

| Paper 4 | Economy & Development of India and Telangana | 150 | 3 hours |
| Paper 5 | Science & Technology, Environment | 150 | 3 hours |

- Descriptive type
- Total: 900 marks

### Stage 3: Interview

- **Marks:** 75
- **Total for final ranking:** 975

## TSPSC Group 2 Exam Pattern

## Syllabus Overview

### General Studies

- **History:** Indian history, Telangana history, cultural heritage
- **Polity:** Indian Constitution, governance, local self-government
- **Economy:** Indian economy, Telangana state economy
- **Geography:** India and Telangana geography
- **Science & Technology:** General science, current tech developments
- **Current Affairs:** National and Telangana state events

### Telangana-Specific Topics (Very Important)

TSPSC heavily tests knowledge about Telangana:
- Telangana movement history (from 1948 to 2014)
- Telangana State Formation Act 2014
- Telangana state schemes — Rythu Bandhu, Kaleshwaram, Mission Bhagiratha
- Telangana geography, districts, rivers, and demographics
- Telangana budget and economic indicators

## How to Apply

1. Visit [www.tspsc.gov.in](https://www.tspsc.gov.in)
2. Complete One Time Registration (OTR) if new user
3. Fill in personal, educational, and address details
4. When notification is live, log in and apply for the exam
5. Upload photograph, signature, and certificates
6. Pay application fee online
7. Submit and download confirmation

## Important Dates Pattern

TSPSC does not have a fixed annual calendar. Exams are notified based on vacancies.

- **Notification:** Varies (check website regularly)
- **Prelims:** 2–3 months after notification closing date
- **Mains:** 3–5 months after prelims
- **Interview (Group 1):** 2–3 months after mains results
- **Final Results:** 1–2 months after interview

## Preparation Tips

### 1. Master Telangana-Specific Content

Telangana movement history and state-specific topics carry significant weightage. Study dedicated Telangana GK material thoroughly.

### 2. Use NCERT Books as Foundation

Class 6–12 NCERTs for History, Geography, Polity, and Economics provide the essential base knowledge.

### 3. Follow Current Affairs Regularly

Read Eenadu/Sakshi (Telugu) or The Hindu (English) daily. Pay special attention to Telangana government announcements and schemes.

### 4. Practise Previous Year Papers

Solve TSPSC previous year question papers to understand the pattern and difficulty level.

### 5. Answer Writing Practice (Group 1)

For mains, practice writing structured descriptive answers. Time management is crucial — practice writing within 3-hour limits.

### 6. Take Mock Tests

Regular mock tests help identify weak areas and improve time management for the screening test.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is there negative marking in TSPSC exams?

No, TSPSC generally does not apply negative marking in screening tests. Always verify with the latest notification.

### Can I write TSPSC exams in Telugu?

Yes, candidates can choose Telugu or English as the medium for both prelims and mains examinations.

### What is the difference between TSPSC Group 1 and Group 2?

Group 1 leads to gazetted officer posts (Deputy Collector, DSP) with higher pay and has a descriptive mains + interview. Group 2 leads to mid-level posts with objective mains and no interview.

### How many vacancies does TSPSC usually announce?

Vacancy numbers vary each notification. Group 1 typically has 50–150 posts, Group 2 has 500–1500 posts, and Group 4 can have several thousand posts.

### Is there any educational requirement for TSPSC Group 4?

Yes, most Group 4 posts require SSC (10th class) or Intermediate (12th class) pass as minimum qualification.

### What is the starting salary for TSPSC Group 1 officers?

Group 1 officers start at approximately ₹56,100 per month (Level 13) plus dearness allowance, HRA, and other benefits. Total take-home can be ₹70,000–₹85,000 initially.
