# PM Surya Ghar Subsidy Not Received? Fix Guide

**PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana** subsidizes rooftop solar panels with ₹30,000 for 1-2 kW systems and up to ₹78,000 for 3 kW+ systems. If your solar panels are installed but the subsidy hasn't arrived in your bank account, here's why and how to fix it.

> **Typical waiting time**: Subsidy is credited **90–180 days after successful net meter commissioning**. Delays up to 6-8 months are common in high-demand states like UP, Rajasthan, Maharashtra.

## How the Subsidy Process Works

Understanding the flow helps you identify where yours is stuck:

1. **Registration** → Apply at consumer.pmsuryaghar.gov.in
2. **DISCOM approval** → Your electricity board approves the installation
3. **Solar panel installation** → Vendor installs panels (1-5 working days)
4. **DISCOM inspection** → DISCOM inspects the installation
5. **Net meter installation** → DISCOM installs the net energy meter
6. **Commissioning** → DISCOM uploads commissioning certificate
7. **Bank subsidy release** → Subsidy credited to your bank account

**Your subsidy is calculated and released ONLY after Step 6 — net meter commissioning certificate upload.**

## Fix 1: Net Meter Not Yet Installed

**Most common reason for delay.**

Subsidy cannot be released until the DISCOM installs a net meter and uploads the commissioning certificate. Net meter installation:
- Is **DISCOM's responsibility** (you cannot install it yourself)
- Takes **30–90 days after installation** in many states (backlog is common)
- **What to do**:
  1. Log into [consumer.pmsuryaghar.gov.in](https://consumer.pmsuryaghar.gov.in/) → Dashboard → Check application status
  2. If status shows "Installation Verified" or "Awaiting Net Metering": Contact your DISCOM's rooftop solar helpdesk
  3. Note the date of vendor installation; DISCOM is required to install net meter within 30-60 days per state rules
  4. If DISCOM is taking too long: file at [consumer.pmsuryaghar.gov.in](https://consumer.pmsuryaghar.gov.in/) → Raise Ticket

## Fix 2: Commissioning Certificate Not Uploaded by DISCOM

Even after net meter installation, DISCOM staff may not have uploaded the **commissioning certificate** on the portal. This delays subsidy release.

**What to do:**
1. Visit your DISCOM's solar section / sub-division office
2. Ask: "Has the commissioning certificate been uploaded for my connection?"
3. If not uploaded: Request the DISCOM JE (Junior Engineer) to upload it
4. Alternatively, raise a ticket at consumer.pmsuryaghar.gov.in mentioning commissioning was done but certificate not uploaded

## Fix 3: Bank Account Not Aadhaar-Linked (NPCI Mapper)

Subsidy is paid via DBT — it goes to the bank account whose Aadhaar is registered in NPCI Mapper. If your Aadhaar isn't seeded in the bank where you want the subsidy:

1. **Visit your bank branch** with Aadhaar card + bank passbook
2. Request: "Aadhaar seeding for DBT payments"
3. Bank updates NPCI mapper within 24-48 hours
4. Check which bank account is registered in your PM Surya Ghar application → must match your NPCI-seeded bank

## Fix 4: Application Status Shows "Subsidy Disbursed" But Not in Bank

If the portal shows your subsidy as disbursed/released but you haven't received it in 7+ working days:

1. Check PFMS → Know Your Payment at [pfms.nic.in](https://pfms.nic.in/) using your bank account number
2. If PFMS shows payment made: check with your bank — it may be in transit (1-3 working days)
3. If PFMS shows no payment: status on PM Surya Ghar portal may be premature — subsidy may not have been transferred yet. Wait 15 more days.
4. If PFMS shows "return" or "failed": Likely NPCI mapper issue — fix Fix 3 above

## Fix 5: Check Application/Subsidy Status Online

### consumer.pmsuryaghar.gov.in — Status Tracking

1. Visit **[consumer.pmsuryaghar.gov.in](https://consumer.pmsuryaghar.gov.in/)**
2. Login with your registered mobile number
3. Go to **Dashboard** → Your Application
4. Status progression:
   - "Application Submitted" → "DISCOM Approved" → "Installation Verified" → "Net Meter Installed" → "Subsidy Released"
5. If you see "Subsidy Released": check your bank + PFMS
6. If stuck at any earlier stage: raise a ticket

### Raise Ticket on Portal

1. Login to consumer.pmsuryaghar.gov.in
2. Go to **"Raise Ticket"** or **"Grievance"**
3. Describe the specific stage you're stuck at
4. Include: Application Number, system size (kW), installation date, DISCOM name
5. DISCOM gets 7 days to respond via the portal

## Subsidy Amounts — What You Should Receive

| System Size | Subsidy Amount |
|-------------|---------------|
| Up to 2 kW | **₹30,000** |
| 2 kW to 3 kW | **₹60,000** (₹30K for first 2 kW + ₹18K for next 1 kW) |
| Above 3 kW / residential | **₹78,000** (capped at 3 kW equivalent) |

> Subsidy is calculated based on the **installed capacity** approved by DISCOM, not what you applied for.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does PM Surya Ghar subsidy take after installation?

The process timeline:
- DISCOM approval: 7–15 days
- Net meter installation by DISCOM: 30–90 days (varies by state)
- Subsidy release after commissioning: 30–60 days

**Realistic total**: 3–6 months from installation to subsidy credit. In states with high demand (UP, Rajasthan), 6–9 months have been reported.

### My vendor installed the panels 3 months ago but net meter hasn't come. What to do?

This is the most common complaint. First check with your DISCOM. If no action in 30 days after installation, you can:
1. File at consumer.pmsuryaghar.gov.in → Raise Ticket
2. Escalate to state DISCOM's solar grievance cell
3. If still unresolved, contact Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE) at mnre.gov.in → Grievance

### Can I use the solar system before net meter installation?

Technically yes — your panels generate power and your electricity bill reduces. But the **subsidy** and the ability to **export excess power** to the grid start only after the net meter is installed.

### The solar vendor (installer) is saying the subsidy will come via them. Is that correct?

**No.** PM Surya Ghar subsidy comes **directly to your bank account via DBT** — never through the vendor. If a vendor asks you to pay extra "until the subsidy comes" or claims to receive the subsidy on your behalf, that is incorrect. The subsidy is a government-to-consumer payment.
