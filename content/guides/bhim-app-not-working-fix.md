# BHIM App Not Working — How to Fix Login & Payment Errors

BHIM (Bharat Interface for Money) is India's official UPI payment app developed by NPCI. While it's widely used for instant bank transfers, many users face issues like app crashes, login failures, server errors, and payment declines. This guide covers every common BHIM app problem and how to fix it.

## Common Error Messages

You may encounter these errors when using the BHIM app:

- **"Login Failed. Please try again"** — authentication or server issue
- **"Server is temporarily unavailable"** — NPCI/bank server downtime
- **"Transaction Failed"** — bank-side decline or UPI timeout
- **"Update Required"** — outdated app version blocking access
- **"Unable to connect to server"** — network or firewall issue
- App crashes immediately after opening
- Blank screen or infinite loading after login
- OTP not received during registration
- UPI PIN entry screen freezes

---

## Fix 1: Check If BHIM/UPI Servers Are Down

Before troubleshooting your device, confirm whether the issue is server-side:

1. Visit [NPCI's official website](https://www.npci.org.in/) for any UPI downtime announcements
2. Check Twitter/X for **#BHIMDown** or **#UPIDown** trending reports
3. Try making a small transaction using another UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe) — if those also fail, UPI servers are likely down
4. Wait 30-60 minutes and retry

**Note:** UPI scheduled maintenance usually happens between 12:00 AM – 2:00 AM IST.

---

## Fix 2: Update the BHIM App

An outdated app is one of the most common causes of crashes and login failures:

1. Open **Google Play Store** (Android) or **App Store** (iOS)
2. Search for **BHIM - MAKING INDIA CASHLESS**
3. If an **Update** button appears, tap it
4. Wait for the update to install completely
5. Open the app and try again

**Important:** Always download BHIM only from official app stores. The developer should show as **National Payments Corporation of India**.

---

## Fix 3: Clear Cache and App Data (Android)

Corrupted cache often causes crashes and login loops:

1. Go to **Settings → Apps → BHIM**
2. Tap **Storage & Cache**
3. Tap **Clear Cache** first and try the app
4. If the issue persists, tap **Clear Data** (this will log you out — you'll need to re-register)
5. Restart the app

**Warning:** Clearing data removes your BHIM profile from the device. You'll need your registered mobile number, debit card, and bank account to set up again.

---

## Fix 4: Check Your Internet Connection

BHIM requires a stable data connection for all operations:

1. Switch between **Wi-Fi and Mobile Data** to test both
2. Disable VPN if active — some VPNs block UPI traffic
3. Enable **Airplane Mode** for 10 seconds, then disable it to refresh your connection
4. Try loading any website in your browser to confirm internet is working
5. If on mobile data, ensure you have sufficient balance/data pack

---

## Fix 5: Verify Your Mobile Number and SIM

BHIM is linked to your mobile number registered with your bank:

1. Ensure the **SIM with your bank-registered mobile number** is active and inserted
2. For dual-SIM phones, place the bank-linked SIM in **Slot 1** (primary slot)
3. Check that your mobile number can send and receive SMS
4. If you recently changed your number or ported, update it with your bank first

See our [BHIM UPI registration guide](/guide/bhim-upi-registration-failed-fix) for SIM-related fixes.

---

## Fix 6: Reinstall the BHIM App

If clearing cache doesn't help, a fresh install often resolves persistent issues:

1. Uninstall the BHIM app from your device
2. Restart your phone
3. Download BHIM again from [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=in.org.npci.upiapp) or App Store
4. Open the app and register with your bank-linked mobile number
5. Set a new UPI PIN using your debit card details

---

## Fix 7: Check Device Compatibility

BHIM may not work properly on all devices:

- **Android:** Requires Android 5.0 (Lollipop) or above
- **iOS:** Requires iOS 12.0 or above
- Rooted/jailbroken devices may be blocked for security reasons
- Ensure your phone has at least 100 MB free storage
- Very old devices with less than 1 GB RAM may experience crashes

---

## Fix 8: Fix Payment Transaction Failures

If the app works but payments keep failing:

1. Check your **bank account balance** — insufficient funds is the most common cause
2. Verify the **recipient's UPI ID** is correct
3. Ensure you haven't exceeded the [daily transaction limit](/guide/bhim-app-transaction-limit-exceeded-fix)
4. Try a smaller amount first (₹1) to test
5. If the amount was debited but not credited, wait 24-48 hours for auto-reversal
6. Contact your bank if the reversal doesn't happen within 3 working days

---

## Fix 9: Reset Your UPI PIN

If you've forgotten your UPI PIN or it's not being accepted:

1. Open BHIM app → Go to **Bank Accounts**
2. Select your bank account
3. Tap **Reset UPI PIN**
4. Enter your **debit card number** and **expiry date**
5. Enter the **OTP** sent to your registered mobile number
6. Set a new **6-digit UPI PIN**

## Important Tips

1. **Always keep the BHIM app updated** to the latest version to avoid compatibility issues
2. **Don't use third-party app cleaners** that aggressively clear cache — they can corrupt BHIM data
3. **Never share your UPI PIN** with anyone, including people claiming to be from NPCI or your bank
4. **Check your bank's UPI status** — some banks temporarily disable UPI during maintenance
5. **Keep your phone's date and time set to automatic** — incorrect time causes server authentication failures

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Why does BHIM app keep crashing on my phone?
BHIM crashes are usually caused by outdated app versions, insufficient storage, or corrupted cache. Update the app, clear cache from Settings → Apps → BHIM → Clear Cache, and ensure at least 100 MB free storage. If it persists, uninstall and reinstall.

### Q2: BHIM shows "Server Temporarily Unavailable" — what should I do?
This means NPCI or your bank's UPI server is down. Wait 30-60 minutes and try again. If the issue lasts more than a few hours, check NPCI's website or social media for maintenance announcements.

### Q3: My BHIM payment was debited but not received by the recipient. What now?
Don't panic. UPI transactions usually auto-reverse within 24-48 hours if they fail mid-way. Check your bank statement after 48 hours. If the amount isn't reversed, file a complaint through the BHIM app or contact your bank directly.

### Q4: Can I use BHIM on a rooted or jailbroken phone?
BHIM may block rooted Android or jailbroken iOS devices for security reasons. If your device is rooted, you may need to unroot it or use a different device for BHIM.

### Q5: BHIM app shows "Update Required" but no update is available on Play Store. What do I do?
This sometimes happens due to regional rollout delays. Wait a few hours and check again. If the update still doesn't appear, clear Play Store cache (Settings → Apps → Google Play Store → Clear Cache) and retry. You can also download the APK from BHIM's official website as a last resort.

### Q6: Is BHIM app safe to use?
Yes. BHIM is developed by NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India) and uses the same UPI infrastructure as other apps. It uses device binding, UPI PIN authentication, and doesn't store your bank credentials on the device.
