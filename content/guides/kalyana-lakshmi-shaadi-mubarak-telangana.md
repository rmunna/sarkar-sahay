# Telangana Kalyana Lakshmi & Shaadi Mubarak — Marriage Assistance Scheme (2026)

The Telangana government provides **₹1,00,116** as one-time financial assistance for the marriage of girls from economically weaker families. The scheme operates under two names based on the beneficiary's community:

- **Kalyana Lakshmi** — for Hindu families (SC, ST, BC, and EBC categories)
- **Shaadi Mubarak** — for Muslim (Minority) families

Both schemes offer the **same benefit amount** and follow the same application process through the Telangana ePASS portal.

## Who Is Eligible?

To qualify for Kalyana Lakshmi or Shaadi Mubarak, the following conditions must be met:

| Criteria | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Bride's age** | 18 years or above at the time of marriage |
| **Domicile** | Bride must be a resident of Telangana |
| **Family income** | Annual household income must not exceed **₹2,00,000** |
| **Caste/Community** | Must belong to SC, ST, BC, EBC, or Minority community |
| **Mother's voter ID** | Mother of the bride must be a registered voter in Telangana |
| **Application timing** | Apply within **1 year** of the marriage date |

> **Note:** The benefit is available for up to **two daughters** per family.

## How Much Money Do You Get?

The scheme provides a **one-time grant of ₹1,00,116** deposited directly into the bride's bank account. The amount is the same for both Kalyana Lakshmi and Shaadi Mubarak.

## Documents Required

Keep these documents ready before applying:

1. **Aadhaar card** of the bride
2. **Age proof** of the bride (SSC certificate, birth certificate, or Aadhaar)
3. **Caste certificate** issued by the Tahsildar/MRO
4. **Income certificate** (family income ≤ ₹2 lakh per annum)
5. **Ration card** (white ration card preferred)
6. **Mother's voter ID card** (Telangana)
7. **Marriage certificate** or wedding invitation card
8. **Bank passbook** (bride's account, linked with Aadhaar)
9. **Passport-size photographs** of the bride

You may need a [caste certificate](/guide/caste-certificate-online) and [income certificate](/guide/income-certificate-online) — check our guides if you don't have them yet.

## How to Apply Online

### Step 1: Visit the ePASS Portal

Go to [telanganaepass.cgg.gov.in](https://telanganaepass.cgg.gov.in) and select **Kalyana Lakshmi** or **Shaadi Mubarak** from the scheme list.

### Step 2: Register / Login

- New users: Click **"New Registration"** and create an account using your Aadhaar number and mobile number.
- Existing users: Log in with your credentials.

### Step 3: Fill the Application Form

Enter the required details:

- Bride's personal details (name, age, Aadhaar, caste)
- Family income details
- Marriage details (date, place)
- Bank account details (bride's account)

### Step 4: Upload Documents

Upload scanned copies of all required documents listed above. Ensure files are clear and within the specified file size limits.

### Step 5: Submit the Application

Review all details carefully and click **Submit**. Note down the **application reference number** for tracking.

### Offline Alternative

You can also apply through your nearest **MeeSeva centre** or the **District Welfare Office**. Carry original documents along with photocopies.

## How to Check Application Status

1. Visit [telanganaepass.cgg.gov.in](https://telanganaepass.cgg.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Track Your Application"** or **"Check Status"**
3. Enter your **application reference number** or **Aadhaar number**
4. Click **Search** to view the current status

Status stages typically include: *Submitted → Under Verification → Approved → Payment Processed*.

## Key Things to Remember

- **Apply within 1 year** of the marriage — late applications are not accepted
- The bride's **bank account must be Aadhaar-linked** for DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer)
- Both schemes are **mutually exclusive** — apply under the one matching your community
- **No middlemen needed** — the application is free and can be done online or at MeeSeva
- If your application is rejected, check the reason on the portal and re-apply with corrected documents

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I apply after the marriage?
Yes. You can apply **after the marriage** but within **1 year** of the marriage date.

### What if the family income is slightly above ₹2 lakh?
The income limit is strictly ₹2,00,000 per annum. Applications with income above this limit will be rejected. The income certificate from the Tahsildar/MRO is the accepted proof.

### Is the scheme available for second marriages?
The scheme is intended for first marriages. Remarriages or second marriages are generally not eligible.

### Can the groom's family apply?
No. The application must be made by the **bride or bride's family**.

### How long does it take to receive the money?
After approval, the amount is typically credited within **30–60 days** via Direct Benefit Transfer.
