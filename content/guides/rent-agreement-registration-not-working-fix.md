# Rent Agreement Online Registration Not Working — How to Fix

Registering a rent agreement online should be quick, but state government portals are notorious for crashing, payment failures, and verification errors. Whether you're using **Maharashtra IGR**, **Karnataka Kaveri**, **UP IGRS**, or **Delhi's e-Stamp portal**, this guide covers every common error and how to fix it — plus offline alternatives when portals won't cooperate.

## Quick Checklist Before You Start

Before troubleshooting, make sure you have:

- **Aadhaar numbers** of both landlord and tenant
- **PAN card** of the landlord (required in most states)
- **Property details** — address, area, survey/plot number
- **Passport-size photos** of both parties (JPEG, under 200 KB)
- **Rent amount, deposit, and agreement duration** decided
- **Two witnesses** with Aadhaar (required in many states)

## 1. State Portal Not Loading or Showing Errors

### Maharashtra IGR (igrmaharashtra.gov.in)

**Symptoms:**
- Portal shows "Service Unavailable" or loads a blank page
- Login page loops or OTP screen freezes
- "Session expired" immediately after login

**Fixes:**
- **Use Chrome or Edge** — IGR portal has compatibility issues with Firefox and Safari.
- **Clear cache and cookies** completely, then retry.
- **Try between 7–10 AM or after 9 PM** — the portal is overloaded during business hours.
- **Disable VPN and ad blockers** — IGR blocks certain IP ranges.
- If you get "Session expired" repeatedly, **open the portal in incognito mode**.
- Check [@IGaborMah on Twitter/X](https://twitter.com/IGaborMah) for downtime announcements.

### Karnataka Kaveri (kaveri2.karnataka.gov.in)

**Symptoms:**
- "Server is busy, try again later" message
- Kaveri 2 dashboard loads but rent agreement module is missing
- Payment page redirects to error

**Fixes:**
- Kaveri 2 is the current portal — do **not** use old kaveri.karnataka.gov.in links.
- Ensure you've **selected the correct district and sub-registrar office** before proceeding.
- If the rent agreement option is missing, log out, clear cookies, and log in again — module visibility can glitch.
- **Try early morning (before 9 AM)** for best performance.

### UP IGRS (igrsup.gov.in)

**Symptoms:**
- "Website under maintenance" page
- Form submission hangs or shows "Transaction failed"
- Hindi/English language toggle breaks the page

**Fixes:**
- **Stick to one language** throughout the session — switching mid-form causes errors.
- If the form hangs on submission, **do not press back** — wait 2–3 minutes, then check "My Applications" to see if it saved.
- **Use the mobile app** (IGRSUP) as an alternative — it's often more stable.
- For persistent issues, call the UP IGRS helpline: **0522-2304706**.

### Delhi (estamp.delhi.gov.in / dfrregistration.delhi.gov.in)

**Symptoms:**
- Delhi e-Stamp portal not generating stamp paper
- Registration appointment booking fails
- "Slot not available" error

**Fixes:**
- Delhi uses a **two-step process**: first buy e-Stamp paper, then book a Sub-Registrar appointment for registration.
- e-Stamp paper is generated via [shcilestamp.com](https://shcilestamp.com) — if delhi.gov.in isn't working, buy the e-Stamp directly from SHCIL.
- For appointment booking, **new slots open at midnight** — try exactly at 12:00 AM.
- If you cannot book a slot online, **walk in to the Sub-Registrar office** — they accept walk-ins for rent agreements in most Delhi offices.

## 2. Stamp Duty Payment Failed

**Symptoms:**
- Payment deducted from bank but portal shows "Payment failed"
- "Transaction timeout" during payment
- Double deduction

**Fixes:**

1. **Wait 24–48 hours** — most failed payments auto-refund to your bank account.
2. **Check payment status** on the portal under "Payment History" or "Transaction Status" section.
3. **Do not make a second payment** until the first one is confirmed as failed — this avoids double charges.
4. If money was deducted but the portal didn't update:
   - Take a **screenshot of your bank statement** showing the deduction.
   - Contact the portal's **grievance section** with your transaction ID and application number.
   - For Maharashtra: use the [IGR Grievance portal](https://igrmaharashtra.gov.in).
   - For Karnataka: email **kaveri.helpdesk@karnataka.gov.in**.
   - For UP: call **0522-2304706** with your transaction reference.
5. **Use net banking instead of UPI** if UPI keeps failing — net banking has longer timeout windows.
6. **SBI and some PSU bank gateways** are more compatible with government portals than private bank UPI.

### Stamp Duty Rates (Quick Reference)

| State | Stamp Duty on Rent Agreement | Registration Fee |
|-------|------------------------------|------------------|
| Maharashtra | 0.25% of total rent (Leave & Licence) | ₹1,000 |
| Karnataka | 1% of annual rent + deposit | 1% (min ₹500) |
| Uttar Pradesh | 2% of annual rent | 2% (min ₹100) |
| Delhi | 2% of annual rent (average) | ₹1,100 |

*Rates are indicative — check your state portal for current rates.*

## 3. Aadhaar / Biometric Verification Failed

**Symptoms:**
- "Aadhaar authentication failed" during e-sign
- Biometric device not detected
- OTP not received for Aadhaar verification
- "Aadhaar number not linked with mobile" error

**Fixes:**

1. **Ensure your mobile number is linked to Aadhaar** — this is required for OTP-based e-sign. Check at [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in).
2. If OTP is not received:
   - Wait 2–3 minutes — UIDAI OTPs can be delayed.
   - Check if your phone has **DND (Do Not Disturb) enabled** — disable it temporarily.
   - Try the **"Resend OTP"** option (usually available after 60 seconds).
3. **Biometric device issues:**
   - Make sure your **fingerprint scanner driver is installed** and updated.
   - Clean the scanner surface and your fingertips (dry, clean fingers work best).
   - If fingerprint fails repeatedly, use **iris scanner** if available.
   - **RD Service app** must be installed and running — check in your system tray (Windows) or applications.
4. If Aadhaar authentication keeps failing:
   - Your Aadhaar biometrics may be **locked** — unlock them at [resident.uidai.gov.in](https://resident.uidai.gov.in).
   - If your Aadhaar details (name/DOB) don't match the rent agreement details, **update Aadhaar first**.
5. **Both landlord and tenant** need to complete Aadhaar verification — coordinate timing so both are available.

## 4. Document Upload Errors

**Symptoms:**
- "File size exceeds limit" error
- "Invalid file format" error
- Upload stuck at 0% or hangs mid-upload
- Uploaded document appears blank or corrupted

**Fixes:**

1. **Check file size and format requirements:**
   - Most portals accept **PDF or JPEG** only.
   - Maximum file size: typically **1–2 MB** per document.
   - Photos: **JPEG only**, under **200 KB**.
2. **Compress large PDFs** using [ilovepdf.com](https://www.ilovepdf.com/compress_pdf) or [smallpdf.com](https://smallpdf.com).
3. **Convert images to the correct format** — use any image converter to switch between PNG and JPEG.
4. If upload hangs:
   - Switch to a **wired internet connection** or a stable Wi-Fi.
   - **Disable antivirus temporarily** — some antivirus software blocks file uploads to government sites.
   - Try a **different browser**.
5. **Do not upload password-protected PDFs** — the portal cannot read them.
6. Ensure scanned documents are **legible** — blurry or tilted scans get rejected during verification.

### Commonly Required Documents

| Document | Format | Max Size |
|----------|--------|----------|
| Aadhaar card (both parties) | PDF / JPEG | 1 MB |
| PAN card (landlord) | PDF / JPEG | 1 MB |
| Passport photos | JPEG | 200 KB |
| Property ownership proof | PDF | 2 MB |
| Draft rent agreement | PDF | 5 MB |

*Exact limits vary by state — check your portal's upload page.*

## 5. E-Stamp vs Physical Stamp Paper

Not sure which one to use? Here's the difference:

| Feature | E-Stamp | Physical Stamp Paper |
|---------|---------|---------------------|
| **Issued by** | SHCIL (Stock Holding Corporation of India) | Authorised stamp vendors |
| **Verification** | Can be verified online at [shcilestamp.com](https://shcilestamp.com) | Difficult to verify; fraud risk |
| **Availability** | 24/7 online | Depends on vendor availability |
| **Accepted for registration** | Yes, in all states | Yes, but increasingly discouraged |
| **Recommended** | ✅ Yes — more secure and verifiable | Use only if e-Stamp is unavailable |

### How to Get E-Stamp Paper

1. Visit [shcilestamp.com](https://shcilestamp.com) or your state's e-Stamp portal.
2. Select your **state** and **stamp duty amount**.
3. Enter details of **first party (landlord)** and **second party (tenant)**.
4. Pay online via net banking, debit card, or UPI.
5. Download and **print the e-Stamp certificate** — this is your stamp paper.

**Tip:** Always **verify your e-Stamp** after purchase using the certificate number on the SHCIL website to ensure it's genuine.

## 6. How to Register Offline at Sub-Registrar Office

If the online portal is not working or you prefer offline registration, follow these steps:

### Step 1: Get Stamp Paper

- Buy **e-Stamp paper** online (recommended) or **physical stamp paper** from an authorised vendor.
- Stamp duty value must match your state's requirement based on rent amount and duration.

### Step 2: Draft the Agreement

Get the rent agreement **typed or printed on the stamp paper**. Include:

- Full names and Aadhaar numbers of landlord, tenant, and two witnesses
- Property address and description
- Monthly rent, security deposit, maintenance charges
- Agreement duration (start date and end date)
- Lock-in period and notice period for termination
- Terms for rent escalation (if any)

### Step 3: Visit the Sub-Registrar Office

- Go to the **Sub-Registrar office** that has jurisdiction over the property's location.
- **All parties must be present** — landlord, tenant, and two witnesses.
- Carry **original Aadhaar cards** of all parties, plus one photocopy each.
- Carry **passport-size photos** (2 each for landlord and tenant).

### Step 4: Pay Registration Fee

- Pay the registration fee at the office counter (cash, DD, or sometimes UPI).
- Collect the **receipt**.

### Step 5: Biometric and Signature

- The Sub-Registrar will take **photographs, fingerprints, and signatures** of all parties.
- The agreement is then **entered into the system** and registered.

### Step 6: Collect Registered Document

- You'll receive the **registered copy** within **3–7 working days** (varies by state).
- Some states offer **same-day registration** for rent agreements.

## 7. Leave and Licence vs Rent Agreement — What's the Difference?

Many people confuse these two. Here's a clear comparison:

| Feature | Leave and Licence | Rent Agreement (Lease) |
|---------|------------------|----------------------|
| **Legal basis** | Indian Easements Act, 1882 | Transfer of Property Act, 1882 |
| **Tenant's right** | Licensee — no ownership interest | Lessee — has an interest in the property |
| **Eviction** | Easier for landlord | More difficult; tenant has stronger rights |
| **Duration** | Typically 11 months (renewable) | Can be for any period |
| **Registration required** | Mandatory if > 12 months (mandatory in Maharashtra for any period) | Mandatory if > 11 months |
| **Commonly used in** | Maharashtra, Karnataka | North India (Delhi, UP, Haryana) |
| **Stamp duty** | Generally lower | Generally higher |
| **Recommended for landlords** | ✅ Yes — better protection | Use for long-term commercial leases |

### Which One Should You Use?

- **If you're in Maharashtra:** You must use **Leave and Licence** — it's legally required for residential rentals and must be registered on the IGR portal.
- **If you're in other states:** A standard **rent agreement** (11-month tenure, renewable) is most common.
- **For landlords:** Leave and Licence is safer — the licensee has fewer rights than a tenant/lessee, making eviction simpler.
- **For commercial properties:** Long-term lease agreements are more common and should always be registered.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is online rent agreement registration legally valid?
Yes. An online registered rent agreement has the same legal validity as one registered at a Sub-Registrar office. The digital signature and e-Stamp make it legally enforceable.

### Can I register a rent agreement without Aadhaar?
In most states, Aadhaar is mandatory for online registration (used for e-Sign). For offline registration, you can use a passport or voter ID as identity proof in some states, but Aadhaar is strongly preferred.

### What happens if I don't register my rent agreement?
An unregistered rent agreement for a period exceeding 11 months is **not admissible as evidence** in court. In Maharashtra, even agreements under 12 months must be registered. You also can't claim HRA tax benefits without a registered agreement.

### How long does online registration take?
If the portal works smoothly, the entire process takes **15–30 minutes**. Document verification and final registration confirmation may take **1–3 working days**.

### Can NRIs register rent agreements online?
Yes, NRIs can register as landlords. They need to authenticate via Aadhaar e-Sign (if Aadhaar is active) or appoint a **Power of Attorney holder** who can register on their behalf at the Sub-Registrar office.

### My rent agreement is for 11 months — do I still need to register it?
In most states, registration is **not mandatory** for agreements under 12 months but is **strongly recommended**. In **Maharashtra**, registration is mandatory regardless of duration.
