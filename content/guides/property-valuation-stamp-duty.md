# Property Valuation & Stamp Duty Calculator: State-Wise

When buying or selling property in India, you must pay **stamp duty** and **registration charges** to the state government. The amount depends on the property's value and the state where it is located. Understanding how property is valued and how stamp duty is calculated can save you significant money — especially if you are eligible for concessions.

This guide covers circle rates, guideline values, state-wise stamp duty rates, women concessions, and how to calculate the total cost.

---

## What is Property Valuation for Stamp Duty?

The government does not accept the transaction price at face value for stamp duty calculation. Instead, stamp duty is charged on the **higher of**:

1. **Actual sale consideration** — the price agreed between buyer and seller
2. **Government guideline value** — the minimum value fixed by the government for the property's location

### Different Names for Guideline Value

| State | Term Used |
|-------|-----------|
| Delhi, UP, Haryana | Circle Rate |
| Maharashtra | Ready Reckoner Rate |
| Karnataka | Guidance Value |
| Tamil Nadu | Guideline Value |
| Rajasthan | DLC Rate (District Level Committee) |
| West Bengal | Market Value |
| Gujarat | Jantri Rate |

### Maharashtra
- Visit [igr.maharashtra.gov.in](https://igr.maharashtra.gov.in/) → Ready Reckoner → Select district, taluka, village

### Karnataka
- Visit [kaveri2.karnataka.gov.in](https://kaveri2.karnataka.gov.in/) → Guidance Value → Select district, taluka, village

### Uttar Pradesh
- Visit [igrs.up.gov.in](https://igrs.up.gov.in/) → Circle Rate → Select district, area

### Rajasthan
- Visit [epanjiyan.rajasthan.gov.in](https://epanjiyan.rajasthan.gov.in/) → DLC Rate

### Tamil Nadu
- Visit [tnreginet.gov.in](https://tnreginet.gov.in/) → Guideline Value

### Gujarat
- Visit [garvi.gujarat.gov.in](https://garvi.gujarat.gov.in/) → Jantri Rate

## Stamp Duty Concession for Women

Many states offer reduced stamp duty for women buyers to encourage women's property ownership:

| State | Concession |
|-------|-----------|
| Delhi | 2% lower stamp duty for women |
| Uttar Pradesh | 2% lower (5% vs 7%) for sole woman buyer |
| Rajasthan | 1% lower for women |
| Haryana | 2% lower for women |
| Karnataka | 2%–3% lower for women |
| West Bengal | 2% lower for women |
| Punjab | 2% lower for women |

**Tip:** If husband and wife are buying jointly, register in the wife's name to save on stamp duty (where applicable). Some states only give the concession when the woman is the **sole buyer**.

## How to Calculate Stamp Duty — Example

**Scenario:** You are buying a flat in Noida (UP) for ₹60 lakh. The circle rate valuation is ₹55 lakh. You (male buyer) are registering it.

| Component | Calculation | Amount |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| Property value for stamp duty | Higher of ₹60L and ₹55L = **₹60,00,000** | — |
| Stamp duty (UP male: 7%) | 7% × ₹60,00,000 | ₹4,20,000 |
| Registration fee (1%) | 1% × ₹60,00,000 | ₹60,000 |
| **Total** | | **₹4,80,000** |

If a **woman** buyer registered the same property:

| Component | Calculation | Amount |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| Stamp duty (UP woman: 5%) | 5% × ₹60,00,000 | ₹3,00,000 |
| Registration fee (1%) | 1% × ₹60,00,000 | ₹60,000 |
| **Total** | | **₹3,60,000** |

**Savings:** ₹1,20,000 by registering in the woman's name.

## Stamp Duty Exemptions and Deductions

### Income Tax Benefit

- Stamp duty and registration charges paid for a **residential property** can be claimed as deduction under **Section 80C** of the Income Tax Act (up to ₹1.5 lakh limit)
- This deduction is available only in the **year of payment**

### Common Exemptions

- **Government-to-government** transfers are exempt
- **Agricultural land** transactions may attract lower stamp duty in some states
- **EWS/LIG housing** under PMAY may get stamp duty waiver in some states
- **Gift deeds** between close family members attract reduced stamp duty in many states

## Important Tips

1. **Never undervalue** the property below circle rate — the registrar will reject it or charge you on the circle rate
2. **Register in a woman's name** where possible to save 1%–2% stamp duty
3. **Claim Section 80C** deduction for stamp duty paid in the same financial year
4. **Use e-stamping** — it's safer than physical stamp paper (no risk of fake stamps)
5. **Check for ongoing concessions** — states occasionally announce temporary stamp duty reductions

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between stamp duty and registration charges?

Stamp duty is a tax on the property transaction document (sale deed), charged as a percentage of the property value. Registration charges are a separate fee paid to the sub-registrar for officially recording the document. Both are mandatory.

### Can I pay stamp duty online?

Yes. Most states offer e-stamping through [shcilestamp.com](https://shcilestamp.com/) or their state registration portals. You can pay online and download the e-stamp certificate.

### What happens if I don't pay the correct stamp duty?

The registrar will not register the document if stamp duty is insufficient. If an under-stamped document is discovered later, the government can levy a penalty of up to 10 times the deficit amount.

### Is stamp duty refundable?

Stamp duty is generally **non-refundable** once paid. However, if the transaction does not go through, some states allow refund within a specified period (usually 6 months) with a deduction of 10%.

### How often do circle rates change?

Circle rates are revised periodically — typically once a year or once every few years, depending on the state. Major cities may see more frequent revisions.

### Is stamp duty different for resale and new property?

No. Stamp duty is calculated the same way for both resale and new properties — based on the higher of the sale consideration or the guideline value. However, for under-construction property, some states charge stamp duty only on the land component until possession.

### Can NRIs buy property in India? What stamp duty applies?

Yes, NRIs can buy residential and commercial property in India. The same stamp duty rates apply as for resident Indians. NRIs cannot buy agricultural land, farmhouse, or plantation property.
