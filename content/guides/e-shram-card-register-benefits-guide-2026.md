# e-Shram Card 2026 — Register, Benefits & Insurance Guide

**e-Shram** is India's national database of unorganized workers. By registering on the e-Shram portal, workers get a **Universal Account Number (UAN)** and access to multiple government welfare schemes including **₹2 lakh accident insurance** under PMSBY.

---

## Who Should Register for e-Shram?

**Eligible workers:** Any worker aged 16–59 years in the **unorganized sector** who is NOT a member of EPFO (Employee Provident Fund) or ESIC (Employee State Insurance Corporation).

**Includes:**
- Construction workers (masons, carpenters, laborers)
- Agricultural workers (farm laborers, tenants, sharecroppers)
- Domestic workers (house help, maids, cooks)
- Street vendors, small traders, hawkers
- Transport workers (auto drivers, taxi drivers, loaders)
- Garment/textile workers
- Beedi workers, brick kiln workers
- Fishermen, salt workers
- Gig workers (delivery workers, freelancers)

**NOT eligible:**
- EPFO members (PF account holders — organized sector employees)
- ESIC members
- Income tax payers
- MNREGA workers (already separately registered)

## Benefits of e-Shram Card

| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **PMSBY Insurance (₹2 lakh)** | ₹2 lakh accidental death / ₹1 lakh partial disability — first year free |
| **UAN (Universal Account Number)** | Unique ID linking all government benefits |

| **PM Awas Yojana** | Priority access for housing scheme |

## e-Shram Registration — Step by Step Online

1. Go to **[eshram.gov.in](https://eshram.gov.in)**
2. Click **"Register on e-SHRAM"**
3. Enter **Aadhaar-linked mobile number** → OTP
4. Enter **Aadhaar number** → OTP on Aadhaar-registered mobile
5. Aadhaar data auto-filled (name, DOB, gender, address)
6. Fill additional details:
   - Type of work / occupation
   - Monthly income range
   - Migrant worker status (yes/no)
   - Bank account details (for future scheme payments)
7. Preview and submit
8. **e-Shram Card generated instantly** → Download (UAN on card)

**Entire process takes 5–10 minutes — no documents to upload!**

## Register via CSC / Mobile (If No Internet)

If no personal internet:
1. Visit nearest **CSC (Common Service Centre / Jan Seva Kendra)**
2. Operator registers on your behalf
3. Bring: Aadhaar card + mobile number (Aadhaar-linked)
4. ₹20 nominal fee at CSC (government has also run free camps)

---

## Download e-Shram Card

If registered, download UAN card:
1. eshram.gov.in → **"Already Registered? Login"**
2. Enter registered mobile + OTP
3. Download e-Shram card (PDF with UAN)
4. Or: Download via **UMANG app** → e-Shram section

## PMSBY Insurance — How to Claim

**PMSBY (Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana):** ₹2 lakh accidental death insurance, ₹1 lakh partial disability — linked to e-Shram registration.

**To claim (in case of accident):**
1. File FIR (for road accident, etc.)
2. Notify bank where e-Shram UAN is linked
3. Submit: Death certificate / medical certificate + hospital bills
4. Insurance company processes claim through the bank
5. **Settlement: 30–45 days**

**Year 2 onwards:** Auto-debit of ₹20/year from bank account to renew PMSBY.

## PM Shram Yogi Maan-Dhan (PMSYM) — Pension Scheme

Separate pension scheme (not automatic with e-Shram — need separate enrollment):
- Workers aged 18–40 with income < ₹15,000/month
- Monthly contribution: ₹55–₹200/month (based on age)
- Government matches contribution
- Pension: **₹3,000/month from age 60**
- Enroll at CSC with Aadhaar + bank account

## e-Shram Card — Frequently Asked Questions

**I'm a construction worker with daily wages — do I need e-Shram card?**
Yes — construction workers are one of the primary target groups. Register immediately to get ₹2 lakh free insurance and access to future welfare payments. Your UAN will be used for all government cash transfers.

**My Aadhaar mobile number is different from my current number — how to register?**
You need Aadhaar OTP (on Aadhaar-linked mobile) for e-Shram registration. First update your mobile number in Aadhaar at nearest Aadhaar Seva Kendra. Then register for e-Shram.

**e-Shram registered but I'm not getting any money — why?**
e-Shram is a database/registration platform, not a cash transfer scheme by itself. Schemes like PM-Kisan, PMAY, SVANidhi are separate — e-Shram registration helps you link to them. Ask at your local Block Development Office which schemes you're eligible for based on your e-Shram profile.

**Can gig workers (Swiggy/Zomato delivery) register for e-Shram?**
Yes — gig/platform workers are explicitly included. Register under "Transport" or "Service" category. Access PMSBY insurance and future gig worker welfare fund benefits.

**e-Shram card expired — does it need renewal?**
e-Shram UAN is a permanent number. No expiry. However, update your details if you change occupation, bank account, or address. Login at eshram.gov.in to update.
