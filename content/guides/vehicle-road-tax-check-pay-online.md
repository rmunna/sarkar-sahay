# Road Tax Check & Pay Online: State-Wise Guide

Road tax (motor vehicle tax) is a mandatory tax levied by state governments on all vehicles registered in India. The tax amount depends on the vehicle type, engine capacity, fuel type, age, and the state of registration. This guide covers how to check and pay road tax online, one-time vs annual tax, state-wise portals, and exemptions.

## What is Road Tax?

Road tax is a state-level tax collected under the Motor Vehicles Taxation Act of each state. It funds road construction, maintenance, and transport infrastructure. Every vehicle owner must pay road tax at the time of registration and/or periodically, depending on the state and vehicle type.

### Types of Road Tax

| Tax Type | Description | Applicable To |
|---------|-------------|---------------|
| **One-time tax** | Paid once at the time of registration, valid for the vehicle's lifetime (or 15 years) | Private vehicles in most states |
| **Annual/periodic tax** | Paid every year or quarter | Commercial vehicles, some states for private vehicles |
| **Lifetime tax** | Paid once, covers the entire registration period | Private vehicles in states like Maharashtra, Karnataka |
| **Green tax** | Additional tax for older vehicles (>8 years for transport, >15 years for private) | Older vehicles as per state rules |

## How to Check Road Tax Online

### Method 1: Parivahan / Vahan Portal

1. Visit [vahan.parivahan.gov.in](https://vahan.parivahan.gov.in/vahan4dashboard/)
2. Go to **Know Your Vehicle Details** or **Tax Details**
3. Enter your vehicle registration number
4. View tax paid status, amount, and validity

### Method 2: State Transport Department Portal

Each state has its own transport portal where you can check and pay road tax. See the state-wise list below.

### Method 3: mParivahan App

1. Download the **mParivahan** app from Google Play or App Store
2. Enter your vehicle registration number
3. View RC details including tax status

## How to Pay Road Tax Online

### Step 1: Visit Your State Portal

Go to your state's transport department website or the Parivahan portal.

### Step 2: Enter Vehicle Details

- Vehicle registration number
- Chassis number (last 5 digits)
- Owner details

### Step 3: Calculate Tax

The portal will show the tax amount based on:
- Vehicle type and category
- Engine capacity / cubic capacity (CC)
- Fuel type (petrol, diesel, CNG, electric)
- Vehicle age
- Seating capacity (for transport vehicles)
- Purchase price / ex-showroom price (in some states)

### Step 4: Pay Online

- Payment modes: Net banking, debit card, credit card, UPI
- Download the tax payment receipt
- Keep it with your vehicle documents

## Road Tax Calculation (General Guidelines)

Road tax rates vary significantly by state. Here are general ranges:

### Private Vehicles (Cars)

| Vehicle Price / CC | One-Time Tax (Approx.) |
|-------------------|----------------------|
| Up to 1000cc | 3%–6% of vehicle price |
| 1000cc–1500cc | 5%–8% of vehicle price |
| 1500cc–2000cc | 7%–10% of vehicle price |
| Above 2000cc | 8%–15% of vehicle price |
| Electric vehicles | 0%–2% (exempted in many states) |

### Two-Wheelers

| Engine Capacity | One-Time Tax (Approx.) |
|----------------|----------------------|
| Up to 100cc | ₹1,500–₹4,000 |
| 100cc–200cc | ₹3,000–₹8,000 |
| Above 200cc | ₹5,000–₹15,000 |
| Electric two-wheelers | ₹0 (exempted in most states) |

> These are approximate ranges. Actual amounts depend on your state's tax schedule.

## Exemptions

- **Electric vehicles (EVs)** — Many states offer full or partial road tax exemption to promote EV adoption
- **Government vehicles** — Exempt from road tax
- **Defence and military vehicles** — Exempt
- **Vehicles for differently-abled persons** — Some states offer concessions
- **Emergency vehicles** — Ambulances and fire tenders are often exempt
- **Agricultural vehicles** — Tractors used purely for agriculture may be exempt in some states
- **Hybrid vehicles** — Some states offer reduced rates

---

## Penalty for Non-Payment

- **Late fee:** Most states charge a penalty of 1%–2% per month on unpaid road tax
- **Compounding fee:** Some states allow payment of a lump-sum penalty to regularize
- **Vehicle seizure:** Persistent non-payment can lead to vehicle impounding by the transport department
- **Registration suspension:** Your vehicle's registration may be suspended for tax default
- **Cannot renew insurance:** Some insurers require proof of road tax payment

> Pay road tax on time to avoid steep penalties and legal complications.

---

## Important Tips

1. **Pay at registration** — For one-time tax states, road tax is typically collected at the time of vehicle registration itself
2. **Keep receipts** — Always keep your road tax payment receipt with vehicle documents. It may be needed during insurance claims or vehicle transfer
3. **Interstate transfer** — When transferring your vehicle to another state, you may need to pay road tax in the new state. Some states offer refunds for unused tax from the previous state
4. **Check EV benefits** — If buying an electric vehicle, check your state's EV road tax exemption policy to save significantly
5. **Commercial vehicle owners** — Set reminders for quarterly or annual tax payments to avoid penalties and vehicle impounding

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Is road tax the same across all states?

No, road tax rates vary from state to state. Each state has its own Motor Vehicles Taxation Act with different rates based on vehicle type, price, engine capacity, and fuel type.

### Q2: Do I need to pay road tax again if I move to a new state?

Yes, if you re-register your vehicle in a new state, you must pay road tax in that state. Some states offer a partial refund of tax paid in the original state if claimed within a specified period.

### Q3: Are electric vehicles exempt from road tax?

Many states offer full or partial road tax exemption for electric vehicles. States like Delhi, Maharashtra, Gujarat, and Karnataka have announced EV-friendly policies. Check your state's specific rules.

### Q4: How do I get a road tax refund?

Road tax refunds are available in specific cases — vehicle scrapped, exported, or re-registered in another state. Apply to the RTO with relevant documents (RC surrender, NOC, scrap certificate).

### Q5: What is green tax?

Green tax is an additional tax levied on older, more polluting vehicles. Transport vehicles older than 8 years and private vehicles older than 15 years may be subject to green tax in states that have implemented it.

### Q6: Can I pay road tax for another state's vehicle online?

Generally, you pay road tax in the state where your vehicle is registered. For interstate tax, visit the destination state's transport portal or RTO.

### Q7: Is road tax included in the vehicle's on-road price?

Yes, the on-road price quoted by dealers typically includes road tax (registration charges, road tax, insurance, and other fees). However, verify the breakup with your dealer.
