# Vidiyal Payanam – Free Bus Travel for Women in Tamil Nadu

## What is Vidiyal Payanam?

Vidiyal Payanam (Dawn Journey) is a Tamil Nadu government scheme providing **free bus travel for all women** on Tamil Nadu State Transport Corporation (TNSTC) and Metropolitan Transport Corporation (MTC) government buses. All women, regardless of age or income, can travel for free on ordinary government buses across Tamil Nadu.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **All women** — no age restriction
2. **No income criteria** — available to all women
3. **No registration required** — just board the bus and travel free
4. **Transgender women** are also eligible

### Buses Covered

- **TNSTC** ordinary buses (all divisions)
- **MTC** (Metropolitan Transport Corporation) ordinary buses in Chennai
- **SETC** ordinary services
- **NOT covered:** AC buses, deluxe buses, ultra-deluxe, sleeper services

## How to Avail Free Bus Travel

### No Registration Needed

Unlike some other states, Tamil Nadu's free bus scheme for women **does not require any card or registration**:

1. **Board** any ordinary TNSTC/MTC bus
2. **Inform** the conductor that you are availing the free travel scheme
3. **Collect** a free ticket from the conductor
4. **Travel** to your destination

That's it — no smart card, no Aadhaar, no ration card needed.

## Important Tips

1. **Ordinary Buses Only:** The scheme applies only to ordinary (non-AC, non-deluxe) government buses.
2. **Collect the Ticket:** Even though travel is free, always collect the ticket from the conductor as proof of travel.
3. **No ID Needed:** You do not need to carry any ID specifically for this scheme, though carrying basic ID is always recommended.
4. **Peak Hours:** Buses may be crowded during peak hours. Plan accordingly.
5. **Report Issues:** If any conductor denies free travel, note the bus number and complaint at the nearest TNSTC depot or call the helpline.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Do I need a pass or smart card?
**No.** No pass, card, or registration is needed. Just board the bus and travel free.

### Q2: Can I travel on AC or deluxe buses for free?
**No.** Only ordinary government buses are covered under this scheme.

### Q3: Is there a limit on how many trips I can take in a day?
**No.** There is no daily or monthly limit. Women can take unlimited free trips.

### Q4: Can school girls also travel free?
**Yes.** There is no minimum age requirement. Girls of all ages can travel free.

### Q5: Does this scheme work on private buses?
**No.** Only government-operated TNSTC, MTC, and SETC buses are covered.

### Q6: Is this scheme available across all of Tamil Nadu?
**Yes.** It covers all TNSTC divisions across Tamil Nadu and MTC in Chennai.
