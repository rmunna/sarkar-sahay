## What is Maharashtra Swadhar Yojana?

**Swadhar Yojana** is the Maharashtra government scheme providing **₹51,000 per year** to Scheduled Caste (SC) and Nav-Buddha students who pursue education in **Class 11 and above** — and live in non-government hostels or their own rented accommodation (outside their home district). The scheme covers hostel/board, food, stationery, and other educational expenses.

It is also called **"Babasaheb Ambedkar Swadhar Yojana"** in some government communications.

## Annual Amount Breakdown

| Component | Annual Amount |
|---|---|
| Board and lodging | ₹28,000 |
| Stationery | ₹6,000 |
| Other miscellaneous expenses | ₹2,000 |
| Professional/non-professional courses (medicine, engineering) | Additional ₹15,000 |
| **Total (general)** | **₹36,000/year** |
| **Total (professional courses)** | **₹51,000/year** |

*Note: Amounts may be revised by the government. Check [sjsa.maharashtra.gov.in](https://sjsa.maharashtra.gov.in) for latest figures.*

## Who is Eligible?

- **Category:** Scheduled Caste (SC) or Nav-Buddha community in Maharashtra
- **Course level:** Class 11 and above (junior college, graduation, PG, professional courses like MBBS, engineering, law)
- **Annual family income:** Below **₹2.50 lakh**
- **Residence:** The student must live **outside their home district** or in a place where there is no government hostel available
- **Age:** For Class 11–12: up to 25 years; for graduation: up to 30 years; PG: 35 years; professional: as per course
- Must maintain **minimum 50% marks** in previous year

## How to Apply

1. Go to **[sjsa.maharashtra.gov.in](https://sjsa.maharashtra.gov.in)**
2. Navigate to **"Swadhar Yojana"**
3. Register with Aadhaar + mobile number
4. Fill application:
   - Personal details, caste category
   - Current course and institution details
   - Accommodation details (private hostel or rented room)
   - Bank account details
5. Upload documents (see below)
6. Submit → application reviewed by District Social Justice office
7. Amount credited to bank account after approval

**Annual renewal:** Apply each year with fresh income certificate and previous year's marksheet.

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card**
2. **Caste certificate** (SC/Nav-Buddha)
3. **Income certificate** — annual family income below ₹2.50 lakh
4. **Previous year marksheet** (minimum 50% marks)
5. **College/institution admission letter** and fee receipt
6. **Hostel or rent proof** — hostel receipt OR landlord's receipt/rent agreement
7. **Bank account** (own name, Aadhaar-linked)
8. **Domicile** (Maharashtra resident)

## What if Government Hostel is Available?

Swadhar Yojana is specifically for students where **government accommodation is not available** in their college location. If a government hostels exists and you refuse to use it, you may not qualify for Swadhar.

**Check:** Whether a government hostel (run by state's Backward Class Welfare department) exists in your college city. If not, you are eligible for Swadhar.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My family income is ₹3 lakh. Can I still apply?
The income limit is ₹2.5 lakh per annum. At ₹3 lakh, you do not qualify for Swadhar. However, check other Maharashtra scholarship schemes — the Post-Matric Scholarship for SC (NSP portal) has a higher income limit.

### I live in a private PG (paying guest). Does it count as private hostel?
Yes — a paid PG arrangement qualifies as private accommodation for Swadhar purposes. Submit the receipt from your PG owner.

### I'm studying in the same district as my home. Am I eligible?
You must be studying **outside your home district** (or in a location with no government hostel). If studying in the same district, Swadhar does not apply.

### Can I get Swadhar along with Post-Matric Scholarship?
You generally cannot get two housing-related scholarships simultaneously. Swadhar replaces the hostel/boarding component of Post-Matric Scholarship. If you get Swadhar's housing support, the boarding component of Post-Matric Scholarship is adjusted accordingly. Consult your district Social Justice officer.

### I failed in one subject. Am I still eligible for renewal?
50% marks requirement is for the passing aggregate. Check current norms — some grace is given for supplementary exams. Consult the district Social Justice office for your specific situation.
