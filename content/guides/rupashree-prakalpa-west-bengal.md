# Rupashree Prakalpa West Bengal: ₹25,000 Marriage Grant for Girls

Rupashree Prakalpa is a social welfare scheme by the West Bengal government that provides a **one-time grant of ₹25,000** to girls from economically weaker families at the time of their marriage. The scheme aims to reduce the financial burden of marriage on poor families and discourage the practice of dowry.

## What is Rupashree Prakalpa?

Launched in 2018, Rupashree Prakalpa is managed by the Department of Women Development and Social Welfare, West Bengal. The scheme provides financial assistance to adult girls (18+ years) belonging to families with annual income below the prescribed limit. The ₹25,000 is transferred directly to the bride's bank account.

### Key Details

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Grant Amount | ₹25,000 (one-time) |
| Payment Mode | DBT to bride's bank account |
| Managed By | Dept. of Women Development & Social Welfare |
| Coverage | All districts of West Bengal |

## Who is Eligible?

- The bride must be **18 years or above** at the time of marriage
- Must be a **permanent resident of West Bengal**
- Family annual income must be **₹1.5 lakh or less**
- The marriage must be **legally registered**
- Must be the **first marriage** of the bride
- The bride must have an **Aadhaar card**
- Must have a **bank account** in the bride's name
- **Maximum two daughters** per family can avail this benefit

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** of the bride
- **Age proof** — birth certificate, school certificate, or voter ID
- **Income certificate** of the family (annual income ≤ ₹1.5 lakh)
- **Residential certificate** of West Bengal
- **Bank account passbook** in the bride's name
- **Passport-size photograph** of the bride
- **Marriage registration certificate** or marriage invitation with date
- **Self-declaration** that it is the first marriage
- **Voter ID** (if available)

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Online Application

1. Visit the official portal **[jaaboroithak.wb.gov.in](https://jaaboroithak.wb.gov.in/)** or **[wbrupashree.gov.in](https://wbrupashree.gov.in/)**
2. Click on **"Apply for Rupashree"** or **"New Application"**
3. Enter the bride's **Aadhaar number** and verify via OTP
4. Fill in personal details — name, father's name, date of birth, address
5. Enter **marriage details** — date of marriage, groom's details
6. Upload required documents — Aadhaar, age proof, income certificate, photo
7. Enter **bank account details** (bride's account)
8. Review all details and **submit the application**
9. Note the **Application Reference Number**

### Offline Application

1. Visit the nearest **Block Development Office** (rural) or **Municipality Office** (urban)
2. Obtain the **Rupashree application form**
3. Fill in all details and attach documents
4. Submit the form to the concerned officer
5. Collect the **acknowledgement receipt**

### Application Timeline

- Apply **before the marriage date** (typically 30-60 days before)
- Some applications are also accepted within a specified period after marriage
- Check the latest notification for exact timelines

## Fees

There are **no fees** for applying. The process is free.

## Processing Time

- Application verification: **1-2 months**
- Approval and payment: **1-3 months** after verification
- Total: **2-4 months** from application to payment

## Important Tips

1. **Apply well before the marriage date** — last-minute applications may not be processed in time
2. **Ensure income certificate is current** — obtain it from the BDO or SDO office
3. **Register the marriage** — a legally registered marriage is required. Apply for marriage registration at the local registrar
4. **Bride must be 18+** — underage marriage is illegal and disqualifies the application
5. **Only the bride receives the money** — the amount is credited to the bride's bank account, not the family's

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Can I apply after the marriage?
Yes, applications can be submitted after marriage within the prescribed time period. Check the latest notification for the deadline.

### Q2: Is this available for inter-caste or inter-religion marriages?
Yes, Rupashree Prakalpa is available for **all communities** in West Bengal regardless of caste or religion.

### Q3: What if my family income exceeds ₹1.5 lakh?
If the family income exceeds ₹1.5 lakh per year, you are **not eligible** for Rupashree. Consider other financial assistance options.

### Q4: Can a divorcee or widow apply for their second marriage?
The scheme is primarily for **first marriages**. Divorcees and widows should check the latest guidelines for eligibility.

### Q5: Do I need to submit the marriage certificate before the wedding?
You can apply with the marriage invitation/date initially. The **marriage registration certificate** may be required for final verification and payment disbursement.

### Q6: Is there a difference between Rupashree and Kanyashree?
Yes, **Kanyashree** is an education scholarship scheme for unmarried girls (Class 8 to graduation level), while **Rupashree** is a one-time marriage grant. They are different schemes.

### Q7: Can both my daughters avail this benefit?
Yes, up to **two daughters per family** can receive Rupashree benefits.
