## What is the National Livestock Mission (NLM)?

The National Livestock Mission (NLM) is a Government of India scheme to promote entrepreneurship and livelihood development in the animal husbandry sector. It provides **50% subsidy (up to ₹50 lakh per project)** for setting up poultry units, goat/sheep farms, piggeries, fodder production, and feed processing units.

NLM is implemented by the **Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying (DAHD)** through banks and state animal husbandry departments.

---

## Key Benefits

| Sub-mission / Activity | Subsidy |
|------------------------|---------|
| Poultry development (layer/broiler/duck) | 50% subsidy, max ₹25 lakh |
| Small ruminant (goat/sheep farming) | 50% subsidy, max ₹25 lakh |
| Piggery development | 50% subsidy, max ₹30 lakh |
| Fodder and feed development | 50% subsidy, max ₹50 lakh |
| Breed multiplication farms | 50% subsidy, max ₹50 lakh |
| Enumeration & information systems | Linked to state implementation |

Subsidy is **credit-linked and back-ended** — you first take a bank loan, implement the project, then the subsidy is credited.

---

## Who is Eligible?

**You ARE eligible if:**
- You are an **individual entrepreneur, SHG, JLG, FPO** or cooperative
- You have land available for the proposed livestock/fodder unit
- You can arrange a **bank loan** (minimum 50% of project cost from bank)
- You are 18+ years of age

**Priority groups:**
- SC/ST farmers
- Women entrepreneurs
- Progressive farmers with existing animal husbandry experience

**You are NOT eligible if:**
- You are a government employee
- You already availed a subsidy for the same unit under another scheme in the same year

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar Card** — individual or firm representative
2. **Land documents** — ownership or lease deed
3. **Bank loan sanction letter** — from scheduled commercial bank, cooperative bank, or RRB
4. **Detailed Project Report (DPR)** — describing the livestock unit, costs, and projections
5. **Caste certificate** (for SC/ST priority)
6. **Partnership deed / firm registration** (for firms)
7. **Photograph**

## How to Apply — Step by Step

NLM applications go through banks. The process is:

### Step 1 — Prepare a Project Report

Prepare a **Detailed Project Report (DPR)** specifying:
- Type of livestock unit (broiler poultry / layer / goat farm / piggery)
- Number of animals / birds
- Infrastructure required (shed, equipment)
- Total project cost
- Loan amount and margin money you can contribute

### Step 2 — Apply on NLM Portal

Register and submit at: **[nlm.udyamimitra.in](https://nlm.udyamimitra.in)**

Alternatively, submit directly to:
- Your **District Animal Husbandry Officer**, OR
- Your bank (many banks accept directly)

### Step 3 — Bank Sanction

The bank evaluates your DPR, verifies land documents, and sanctions the loan (50% of project cost). You contribute the remaining 50% as **margin money** — and the subsidy covers the margin money.

### Step 4 — Implementation

Set up the unit — purchase animals, construct sheds, install equipment.

### Step 5 — Inspection and Subsidy Release

After inspection by the District Animal Husbandry Officer and bank officials:
- Subsidy (50%) is deposited into a **TDR (Term Deposit Receipt)** in your bank for 3 years
- After 3 years of successful operation, the TDR is converted to your account

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. How much money do I need to start? I don't have funds.**

NLM is designed so that the **subsidy replaces your margin money**. You do not need to put in your own money upfront — the bank funds the project and the subsidy covers 50% of the cost. However, you will need to service the remaining 50% as a loan.

**2. Can a woman SHG apply for goat farming subsidy?**

Yes. Women's Self-Help Groups (SHGs) are a priority beneficiary category. The SHG can apply as a group and receive the 50% subsidy on a collective goat farming unit.

**3. What is the minimum scale for a NLM poultry unit?**

Typical minimum: **5,000 broiler birds** per batch (layer farms typically start at 1,000 birds). Smaller backyard poultry is covered under separate state schemes.

**4. I already have a small goat farm. Can I expand under NLM?**

Yes, you can apply for expansion/modernisation of existing units, as long as the total project cost is at least the minimum threshold for the component.

**5. How long does it take to get the subsidy after application?**

Typically 3–6 months from loan sanction to inspection to subsidy TDR creation. The full subsidy becomes accessible after 3 years (from TDR).
