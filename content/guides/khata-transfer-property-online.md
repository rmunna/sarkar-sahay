# Khata Transfer Online: How to Apply for Property Khata Transfer

When you buy, inherit, or receive a property as a gift, you need to transfer the **Khata** (property revenue record) to your name in the municipal corporation records. This guide explains the complete Khata transfer process, with a focus on online application methods.

## What Is Khata?

Khata is an account-like document maintained by the municipal corporation (such as BBMP in Bengaluru) that records:

- **Property owner's name**
- **Property location and area**
- **Property tax assessment details**
- **Property identification number**

Khata is essential for:
- Paying property tax in your name
- Applying for building plan approval
- Getting bank loans against the property
- Selling the property in the future
- Applying for utility connections (water, electricity)

## A Khata vs B Khata (Karnataka)

In Karnataka, particularly Bengaluru:

| Feature | A Khata | B Khata |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Property status | Approved layout, legal construction | Unauthorized layout or pending approval |
| Building license | Can apply | Cannot apply |
| Bank loan | Eligible | Usually not eligible |
| Property tax | Full tax assessment | Tax assessment possible |
| Transferable | Yes | Limited |

**Goal:** Always aim for A Khata. If you have B Khata, check if your property is eligible for conversion under periodic government schemes.

## When Is Khata Transfer Required?

- **After purchasing a property** — Transfer from seller to buyer
- **After inheriting property** — Transfer to legal heir
- **After receiving property as gift** — Transfer to donee
- **After property partition** — Transfer to respective owners
- **After court decree** — As per court order

## Documents Required for Khata Transfer

### For Purchase/Sale
1. Registered sale deed (certified copy)
2. Previous Khata certificate and extract
3. Up-to-date property tax paid receipts
4. Encumbrance certificate (last 13 years)
5. Possession certificate
6. Betterment charges receipt (if applicable)
7. Aadhaar card and PAN card of new owner
8. Passport-size photographs
9. Building plan approval (if constructed property)

### For Inheritance
All of the above, plus:
- Death certificate of the previous owner
- Legal heir certificate
- Will and probate (if applicable)
- No-objection from other legal heirs
- Succession certificate (if no will)

### For Gift
All standard documents, plus:
- Registered gift deed
- Relationship proof

## How to Apply for Khata Transfer Online (Karnataka/BBMP)

### Step 1: Register on the Portal
- Visit [sakala.karnataka.gov.in](https://sakala.karnataka.gov.in) or [bbmpgov.in](https://www.bbmpgov.in)
- Create an account with your mobile number and email
- Verify your account via OTP

### Step 2: Select Service
- Navigate to **BBMP Services** → **Revenue** → **Khata Transfer**
- Select the type of transfer (sale, inheritance, gift, partition)

### Step 3: Fill the Application Form
- Enter the **existing Khata number** and **Property ID**
- Fill in the **new owner's details** — name, address, Aadhaar, PAN
- Enter the **property details** — area, location, zone, ward
- Mention the **sale deed registration number and date**

### Step 4: Upload Documents
- Upload scanned copies of all required documents (PDF format)
- Ensure documents are clear and legible
- File size limits typically 2-5 MB per document

### Step 5: Pay Fees
- Pay the Khata transfer fee online
- Fee is typically 2% of the property value (BBMP)
- Additional processing charges may apply
- Payment via net banking, UPI, or card

### Step 6: Track Application
- Note your **application/reference number**
- Track status on the Sakala portal
- Under the Sakala Act, the service should be delivered within **30 days**

## Khata Transfer Process in Other States

### Maharashtra (Mumbai, Pune)
- Called **Property Mutation** or **Name Transfer**
- Apply through the municipal corporation portal
- Submit at the ward office with sale deed and tax receipts
- Fee: 1-2% of property value

### Tamil Nadu (Chennai)
- Called **Title Transfer** in municipal records
- Apply at the zonal office of Chennai Corporation
- Submit sale deed, previous tax receipt, and EC
- Processing time: 30-45 days

### Delhi (MCD)
- Called **Mutation of Property**
- Apply online through MCD portal
- Submit UPIC, sale deed, and identity proof
- Fee: Nominal charges

### Telangana (GHMC)
- Called **Transfer of Title** or **Mutation**
- Apply at GHMC Citizen Service Centre
- Online application available for some categories
- Processing time: 15-30 days

## Fees for Khata Transfer

| City | Fee Structure |

## Common Issues and Solutions

### Khata Transfer Rejected
**Reasons:** Incomplete documents, unpaid property tax arrears, encumbrance on property, mismatch in property details.
**Solution:** Clear all dues, get fresh encumbrance certificate, ensure sale deed details match property records.

### Delay Beyond 30 Days
**Solution:** File a complaint on the Sakala portal (Karnataka). For other states, approach the municipal commissioner's office or file on CPGRAMS.

### B Khata Property — Cannot Transfer
**Solution:** Check if the property is eligible for A Khata conversion. Apply for regularization if a government scheme is active. Consult a property lawyer.

### Previous Owner Not Cooperating
**Solution:** If you have the registered sale deed, you can apply for Khata transfer independently. The sale deed is sufficient proof of ownership.

## Tips for Smooth Khata Transfer

- **Clear all property tax arrears** before applying
- **Get the encumbrance certificate** well in advance (takes 5-10 days)
- **Keep multiple copies** of all documents
- **Apply within 3-6 months** of property registration
- **Follow up regularly** using the application tracking number
- **Hire a document writer** if you're unfamiliar with the process — available near municipal offices
