# Internet Speed Slow? How to Test & Fix WiFi/Broadband Speed in India

Slow internet is one of the most common frustrations in India. Whether you are on broadband, WiFi, or mobile data, this guide helps you test your actual speed, diagnose the issue, and fix it. If your ISP is not delivering promised speeds, you can also file a formal complaint.

## Step 1: Test Your Internet Speed

Before fixing anything, measure your current speed. Compare it with the speed promised in your plan.

### Recommended Speed Test Tools

| Tool | Type | Link |
|------|------|------|
| TRAI MySpeed | Official TRAI app | [myspeed.trai.gov.in](https://myspeed.trai.gov.in/) |
| Speedtest by Ookla | Web/App | speedtest.net |
| Fast.com | Web (by Netflix) | fast.com |
| Google Speed Test | Search | Google "speed test" |

### How to Test Properly

1. **Connect directly** — use LAN cable instead of WiFi for accurate broadband speed
2. **Close other apps** — stop downloads, streaming, and cloud sync
3. **Test multiple times** — run 3 tests at different times of day
4. **Test on different devices** — rule out device-specific issues
5. **Note download, upload, and ping** — all three matter

### Understanding Your Results

| Metric | Good | Acceptable | Poor |
|--------|------|-----------|------|
| Download speed | Above 80% of plan | 50-80% of plan | Below 50% of plan |
| Upload speed | Above 50% of plan | 30-50% of plan | Below 30% of plan |
| Ping (latency) | Below 20ms | 20-50ms | Above 50ms |

## Step 2: Fix Slow WiFi Speed

### Router Placement

- Place the router in a **central location** in your home
- Keep it **elevated** — on a shelf or mounted on a wall
- Avoid placing near **metal objects, microwaves, or thick walls**
- Keep away from **other electronic devices** that cause interference
- **Do not keep in a closed cabinet** — routers need ventilation and open space

### WiFi Channel Optimization

If many neighbors use the same WiFi channel, your speed drops due to interference.

1. Download a WiFi analyzer app (e.g., WiFi Analyzer on Android)
2. Check which channels are congested
3. Log into your router (usually 192.168.1.1 or 192.168.0.1)
4. Go to **Wireless Settings** → **Channel**
5. Switch to the **least congested channel**
6. For 2.4 GHz: channels 1, 6, or 11 are best (non-overlapping)
7. For 5 GHz: use any available channel with low usage

### Switch to 5 GHz Band

- If your router supports **dual-band** (2.4 GHz + 5 GHz), connect to the 5 GHz network
- 5 GHz offers **faster speeds** but shorter range
- 2.4 GHz has **better range** but slower speeds and more interference

### DNS Optimization

Slow DNS resolution can make websites feel sluggish even with good speed.

1. Open your device's **network settings**
2. Change DNS servers to:
   - **Google DNS:** 8.8.8.8 and 8.8.4.4
   - **Cloudflare DNS:** 1.1.1.1 and 1.0.0.1
3. On your router: go to **WAN/Internet settings** → change DNS

**On Android:** Settings → Network → Private DNS → enter `dns.google` or `one.one.one.one`

**On iPhone:** Settings → WiFi → tap your network → Configure DNS → Manual → add 8.8.8.8

## Step 3: Fix Slow Broadband Speed

### Quick Fixes

1. **Restart your router** — unplug for 30 seconds, then reconnect
2. **Check for firmware updates** — log into router admin panel and update
3. **Check connected devices** — too many devices slow down the network; disconnect unused ones
4. **Check for background downloads** — Windows updates, cloud backups, app updates
5. **Use a wired connection** — Ethernet cable always gives better speed than WiFi

### Check Your Plan

- Log into your ISP account and verify your plan speed
- Check if you have exceeded your **FUP (Fair Usage Policy)** data limit
- After FUP, most ISPs reduce speed to 1-2 Mbps
- Consider upgrading your plan if you regularly exceed FUP

## Step 4: Use TRAI MySpeed App

TRAI's official MySpeed app lets you test and record your internet speed. The data is sent to TRAI and helps them monitor ISP performance.

1. Download **TRAI MySpeed** from Play Store or App Store
2. Open the app and tap **Start Test**
3. View your download/upload speed and latency
4. Results are automatically shared with TRAI (anonymously)
5. Use this data as evidence if you file an ISP complaint

## Step 5: File a Complaint Against Your ISP

If your ISP consistently delivers speeds below what is promised in your plan, you can escalate:

### Level 1: ISP Customer Care

1. Call your ISP's customer care and register a complaint
2. Note down the **complaint/ticket number**
3. Give them **7 days** to resolve

### Level 2: ISP Nodal Officer

1. If unresolved, escalate to the ISP's **Nodal Officer**
2. Find nodal officer details on your ISP's website or bill
3. Send a written complaint via email with speed test screenshots

### Level 3: TRAI / PG Portal

1. Visit the [TRAI website](https://www.trai.gov.in/) for consumer complaint information
2. File a complaint on the Department of Telecom's **PG Portal** — see our [telecom complaint guide](/guide/telecom-complaint-trai-portal) for detailed steps
3. Include speed test results from TRAI MySpeed as evidence

## Important Tips

1. **Test speed via LAN cable** for accurate broadband results — WiFi always shows lower speed
2. **FUP limits** are the most common cause of sudden speed drops — check your data usage
3. **Old routers** (5+ years) may not support modern speeds — consider upgrading
4. **Peak hours** (8-11 PM) will have slower speeds due to network congestion — this is normal
5. **WiFi range extenders** or mesh systems help in large homes with dead zones

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. What speed should I be getting?

You should get at least 80% of your plan's advertised speed when tested via LAN cable. WiFi speeds are typically 50-70% of the plan speed.

### 2. Why is my WiFi slow but wired connection fast?

This indicates a WiFi issue, not a broadband issue. Check router placement, WiFi channel congestion, and switch to 5 GHz band.

### 3. What is FUP and how does it affect speed?

Fair Usage Policy (FUP) is a data limit set by ISPs. Once you exceed it, your speed is reduced (usually to 1-2 Mbps) for the rest of the billing cycle.

### 4. Is TRAI MySpeed app reliable?

Yes. It is the official TRAI tool and results can be used as evidence for complaints. However, test via LAN cable for most accurate results.

### 5. Can I get a refund for slow internet?

If your ISP consistently fails to deliver promised speeds and you have documented evidence, you can demand compensation through the consumer forum or TDSAT.

### 6. How often should I restart my router?

Restarting once a week can help maintain optimal performance. If you face frequent issues, restart whenever speed drops.

### 7. Does changing DNS really improve speed?

DNS change improves website loading time (how fast URLs resolve), not your raw download speed. It can make browsing feel noticeably faster.
