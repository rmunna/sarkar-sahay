# Internet Speed Slow? Test & Fix WiFi/Broadband Speed in India

Slow internet is one of the most common frustrations in India. Whether you use broadband, fibre, or mobile data, there are simple steps to test your actual speed and fix common issues. If the problem is from your ISP's side, you can also file a formal complaint.

## How to Test Your Internet Speed

### 1. TRAI MySpeed App (Official)

TRAI's official speed test app records your actual internet speed and sends data to TRAI for quality monitoring.

1. Download **TRAI MySpeed** app from Google Play Store or Apple App Store
2. Open the app and tap **"Start Test"**
3. It measures download speed, upload speed, and latency
4. Results are automatically shared with TRAI (helps them monitor ISP quality)
5. You can view history of all your past tests

### 2. Speedtest by Ookla

1. Visit [speedtest.net](https://www.speedtest.net/) or download the Speedtest app
2. Click **"Go"** to start the test
3. Note your download speed, upload speed, and ping
4. Compare with your ISP plan speed

### 3. Fast.com (by Netflix)

1. Visit [fast.com](https://fast.com/) in any browser
2. It automatically starts measuring download speed
3. Click **"Show more info"** for upload speed and latency

### 4. Google Speed Test

1. Search **"internet speed test"** on Google
2. Click **"Run Speed Test"** in the search results
3. Get instant results without installing anything

## How to Fix Slow Internet Speed

### Fix 1: Restart Your Router

1. Turn off your WiFi router
2. Wait **30 seconds**
3. Turn it back on
4. Wait 2-3 minutes for it to fully boot
5. Test your speed again — this fixes most temporary issues

### Fix 2: Improve Router Placement

- Place router in the **centre of your house**, not in a corner
- Keep it **elevated** (on a shelf or table), not on the floor
- Avoid placing near **metal objects, microwaves, or thick walls**
- Keep away from other electronic devices that cause interference
- If your house is large, consider a **WiFi range extender or mesh system**

### Fix 3: Change DNS Settings

Slow DNS can make websites load slowly even when your speed is fine.

**Change to Google DNS:**
- Primary DNS: `8.8.8.8`
- Secondary DNS: `8.8.4.4`

**Change to Cloudflare DNS:**
- Primary DNS: `1.1.1.1`
- Secondary DNS: `1.0.0.1`

**How to change DNS on Windows:**
1. Open **Settings > Network & Internet > WiFi > Hardware Properties**
2. Click **Edit** next to DNS server assignment
3. Select **Manual** and enter the DNS addresses above
4. Save

**How to change DNS on Android:**
1. Go to **Settings > Network & Internet > Private DNS**
2. Select **Private DNS provider hostname**
3. Enter `dns.google` or `one.one.one.one`
4. Save

### Fix 4: Switch WiFi Band

- If your router supports **dual-band** (2.4 GHz and 5 GHz):
  - Use **5 GHz** for faster speed when you are close to the router
  - Use **2.4 GHz** for better range when you are far from the router
- Check your router settings page (usually at `192.168.1.1` or `192.168.0.1`)

### Fix 5: Reduce Connected Devices

- Too many devices on the same WiFi slow down speed for everyone
- Disconnect devices you are not using
- Check for unknown devices on your network (someone may be using your WiFi)
- Change your WiFi password if needed

### Fix 6: Update Router Firmware

1. Log in to your router admin page (`192.168.1.1`)
2. Go to **System or Administration** section
3. Check for **firmware updates**
4. Update if available — this can fix bugs and improve performance

### Fix 7: Use Wired Connection

- For important work or video calls, connect your laptop directly to the router using an **Ethernet cable**
- Wired connections are always faster and more stable than WiFi

## How to File Complaint Against Your ISP

If your internet speed is consistently lower than what your plan promises, you can escalate:

### Step 1: Complain to Your ISP

1. Call your ISP's customer care number
2. Register a formal complaint and get a **ticket number**
3. Keep screenshots of speed test results as evidence
4. ISPs usually have **7 days** to resolve the issue

### Step 2: Escalate to ISP's Nodal Officer

1. If unresolved, find the **Appellate Authority/Nodal Officer** details on your ISP's website
2. Email your complaint with ticket number and speed test evidence
3. They must respond within **7 days**

### Step 3: Complain on TRAI/PG Portal

1. If still unresolved after 30 days, file on [pgportal.gov.in](https://pgportal.gov.in/)
2. File under **Department of Telecommunications**
3. Attach speed test screenshots and complaint history

### Step 4: Consumer Forum

1. If no resolution, file a consumer complaint on [consumerhelpline.gov.in](https://consumerhelpline.gov.in/)
2. You can also file in **District Consumer Forum** for compensation
3. See our [telecom complaint guide](/guide/telecom-complaint-trai-portal) for detailed steps

## Important Tips

1. **Test at different times** — speed varies during peak hours (8-11 PM). Test multiple times for accurate results
2. **Use TRAI MySpeed app** — your test data helps TRAI monitor ISP quality across India
3. **Check your plan first** — make sure your data limit is not exhausted (speed drops after FUP limit)
4. **Factory reset your router** as a last resort if nothing else works
5. **Document everything** — save speed test screenshots before filing any complaint

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is my internet slow even with a high-speed plan?

Common reasons: WiFi interference, too many connected devices, router placed in a bad location, ISP network congestion during peak hours, or your FUP data limit is exhausted.

### What is a good internet speed for video calls?

For HD video calls (Zoom, Google Meet), you need at least 5 Mbps download and 3 Mbps upload. For 4K streaming, you need 25+ Mbps.

### Does changing DNS really improve speed?

Changing DNS improves how fast website names are resolved, which makes pages load faster. It does not increase your raw download speed but improves browsing experience.

### Can I get a refund if my ISP gives slow speed?

Yes. If your ISP consistently delivers speed below the promised plan, you can demand a refund or plan downgrade through the consumer forum. Keep speed test evidence.

### How do I check if someone is stealing my WiFi?

Log in to your router admin page (192.168.1.1) and check the list of connected devices. If you see unknown devices, change your WiFi password immediately.

### What is the TRAI MySpeed app?

It is TRAI's official app to test internet speed on mobile networks and broadband. Test results are shared with TRAI to help them monitor and enforce ISP quality standards.

### Should I use 2.4 GHz or 5 GHz WiFi?

Use 5 GHz when you are close to the router (faster speed). Use 2.4 GHz when you are far from the router (better range through walls).
