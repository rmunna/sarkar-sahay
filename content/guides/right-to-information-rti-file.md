## What is RTI (Right to Information)?

The **Right to Information Act, 2005** gives every Indian citizen the right to request information from any public authority (government body). The government body must reply within **30 days**. RTI is one of the most powerful tools for transparency and accountability in India.

Key facts about RTI:

- **Fee:** Just ₹10 for filing an RTI application
- **Response time:** 30 days (48 hours in matters involving life and liberty)
- **Applicable to:** All Central and State Government bodies, PSUs, and bodies funded by government
- **Who can file:** Any Indian citizen (companies, organizations, and foreigners cannot file)

## How to File RTI Online (Step-by-Step)

### Step 1 — Visit the RTI Online Portal

1. Go to [rtionline.gov.in](https://rtionline.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Submit Request"**

> **Note:** The RTI Online portal covers **Central Government** ministries and departments only. For **State Government** bodies, you need to use the respective state RTI portal or file offline.

### Step 2 — Fill the RTI Application Form

1. Select the **Ministry/Department** you want information from
2. Select the **Public Authority** (specific office/department)
3. Fill your details:
   - Name
   - Gender
   - Address
   - State, district, pincode
   - Email ID (mandatory for online filing)
   - Mobile number
   - Citizenship: Indian
4. Select **BPL category** if applicable (fee is waived for BPL applicants — attach BPL card copy)

### Step 3 — Write Your RTI Query

- Write your question(s) clearly and specifically in the **"Text of Application"** field
- Maximum **3,000 characters** allowed
- You can upload a **supporting document** (PDF, up to 1 MB)

**Tips for writing a good RTI query:**

- Be specific — ask for exact information, documents, or records
- Mention the time period you're asking about
- Number your questions (Question 1, Question 2, etc.)
- Don't ask for opinions or explanations — ask for facts, data, and documents
- Example: *"Please provide copies of all expenditure records for road construction project on NH-44 between km 120-150 during April 2024 to March 2025."*

### Step 4 — Pay the Fee

- **Fee:** ₹10 (pay via Internet Banking, credit/debit card, or UPI)
- **BPL applicants:** Fee is waived (upload BPL card)
- After payment, you'll receive a **Registration Number** — save this for tracking

### Step 5 — Track Your Application

1. Go to [rtionline.gov.in](https://rtionline.gov.in)
2. Click **"View Status"**
3. Enter your Registration Number
4. Check the status of your application and any response from the PIO

## How to File RTI Offline

1. Write your RTI application on **plain paper** (no special format required)
2. Address it to the **Public Information Officer (PIO)** of the concerned department
3. Include your name, address, and contact details
4. Write your specific questions
5. Attach a **₹10 Indian Postal Order (IPO)** or demand draft (payable to the Accounts Officer of the department), or pay ₹10 in cash at the counter
6. Send by **registered post** or submit in person
7. Keep the postal receipt or acknowledgment

## Who is the PIO (Public Information Officer)?

Every government department designates a **PIO** who is responsible for receiving and responding to RTI requests. You don't need to know the PIO's name — address your application to "The Public Information Officer, [Department Name]" and it will be routed correctly.

If you send the RTI to the wrong department, they are **legally required to transfer it** to the correct department within **5 days**.

## Response Timeline

| Stage | Time Limit |
|-------|-----------|
| Normal response | **30 days** from receipt |
| Life and liberty matters | **48 hours** |
| Transfer to correct department | **5 days** (+ 30 days from transfer date) |
| Third-party information | **40 days** (extra 10 days for third-party objection) |
| First Appeal response | **30 days** (extendable to 45 days) |

## What If You Don't Get a Response? — Appeal Process

### First Appeal

If you don't receive a reply within 30 days, or the reply is incomplete/unsatisfactory:

1. File a **First Appeal** to the **First Appellate Authority (FAA)** of the same department
2. Deadline: Within **30 days** of receiving the reply (or 30 days after the response was due)
3. **No fee** for first appeal
4. The FAA must decide within **30 days** (extendable to 45 days)
5. On rtionline.gov.in, click **"Submit First Appeal"** and enter your registration number

### Second Appeal — Central Information Commission (CIC)

If the first appeal is also unsatisfactory:

1. File a **Second Appeal** with the **Central Information Commission (CIC)** at [cic.gov.in](https://cic.gov.in)
2. Deadline: Within **90 days** of FAA's decision
3. **No fee** for second appeal
4. CIC can impose a **penalty of ₹250 per day** (up to ₹25,000) on the PIO for unjustified delay or refusal
5. CIC's decision is binding

> For **State Government** RTIs, the second appeal goes to the respective **State Information Commission (SIC)**.

## Exemptions — What Cannot Be Asked Under RTI?

The RTI Act lists exemptions under **Section 8** where information can be denied:

1. **National security** — Information that affects sovereignty, security, or strategic interests
2. **Court-prohibited** — Information forbidden by any court or tribunal
3. **Parliamentary privilege** — Information that would breach privilege of Parliament or State Legislature
4. **Trade secrets** — Commercial confidence, trade secrets, intellectual property
5. **Fiduciary relationship** — Information available in fiduciary capacity
6. **Foreign government** — Information received from foreign governments in confidence
7. **Safety of person** — Information that would endanger life or safety
8. **Investigation records** — Information that would impede investigation or prosecution
9. **Cabinet papers** — Cabinet deliberations (but decisions and reasons must be disclosed after the decision is made)
10. **Personal privacy** — Personal information with no public interest justification

> **Important:** Even exempt information can be disclosed if the **public interest outweighs the harm** of disclosure. Also, information related to **corruption and human rights violations** cannot be denied.

## Important Tips

1. **Be specific** — Vague questions get vague answers. Ask for specific documents, data, or records with clear time periods
2. **File to the right department** — While wrong-department RTIs get transferred, it adds 5+ days delay. Identify the correct ministry/department first
3. **Keep copies** — Save your registration number, payment receipt, and a copy of your application
4. **Don't hesitate to appeal** — Many PIOs delay or give incomplete answers. First appeal is free and usually more effective
5. **RTI is powerful** — RTI has been used to expose corruption, get pending government work done, and hold officials accountable. Use it responsibly

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I file RTI for state government departments online?
The rtionline.gov.in portal is only for **Central Government** departments. For state government bodies, check if your state has an online RTI portal (several states like Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Delhi have them). Otherwise, file offline via postal order.

### Is there a limit on how many RTI applications I can file?
No. There is no limit. You can file as many RTI applications as you want. Each application requires a ₹10 fee.

### Can I file RTI anonymously?
No. You must provide your name and address. However, the PIO cannot ask **why** you want the information or reject your application because they disagree with the purpose.

### What if the PIO refuses to accept my RTI?
If a PIO refuses to accept your RTI, you can file a complaint with the **Central/State Information Commission**. Refusal to accept an RTI application is a violation of the Act and can result in penalties.

### Can I file RTI about private companies?
RTI applies only to **public authorities** — government departments, PSUs, and government-funded bodies. Private companies are not covered. However, if a private body receives substantial government funding, it may be covered.

### How long does RTI information remain valid?
There is no validity period. The information provided is a snapshot of records at the time of your request. You can file a new RTI for updated information at any time.

### Can BPL families file RTI for free?
Yes. Applicants below the poverty line (BPL) are **exempt from all fees** — application fee, photocopy charges, etc. They must attach a copy of their **BPL card** or certificate.
