## What is Biju Swasthya Kalyan Yojana (BSKY)?

Biju Swasthya Kalyan Yojana (BSKY) is a comprehensive health assurance scheme launched by the Government of Odisha to provide cashless and paperless healthcare to the people of the state. The scheme covers both government and empanelled private hospitals across Odisha and India.

Under BSKY, eligible families receive a **Smart Health Card** that entitles them to free treatment with an annual coverage of **₹5 lakh per family** and an additional **₹5 lakh for women members** (total ₹10 lakh for women). The scheme covers approximately **3.5 crore people** across Odisha, making it one of the largest state health assurance programmes in India.

BSKY works alongside the central **Ayushman Bharat - Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB-PMJAY)** to ensure comprehensive healthcare coverage.

## Who is Eligible?

### Eligible Beneficiaries
- All families included in the **State Food Security Scheme (SFSS)** list
- All families covered under **National Food Security Act (NFSA)** in Odisha
- **Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY)** card holders
- **Priority Household (PHH)** ration card holders
- Families identified through the **Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC)** data

### Who is NOT Eligible
- Families not included in SFSS/NFSA beneficiary lists
- Government employees covered under CGHS or state health schemes
- Families with annual income above the prescribed limit (non-ration card holders)

> **Approximately 96 lakh families** (about 3.5 crore individuals) in Odisha are covered under BSKY.

## Documents Required

### For Getting BSKY Smart Health Card
- **Ration Card** (NFSA/SFSS)
- **Aadhaar Card** of all family members
- **Mobile number** (for OTP verification)
- **Passport-size photographs** of family members

### For Availing Treatment
- **BSKY Smart Health Card** (mandatory)
- **Aadhaar Card** for identity verification
- **Doctor's referral** (for some procedures)

## How to Get the BSKY Smart Health Card

### Online Process

1. Visit the **BSKY Portal** at [bsky.odisha.gov.in](https://bsky.odisha.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Check Eligibility"** or **"Search by Ration Card"**
3. Enter your **Ration Card number** to verify eligibility
4. If eligible, you will see your family details
5. Smart Health Cards are issued at **designated camps** organised by the district administration
6. Cards are also available at **government hospitals** and **Mo Seva Kendras**

### At Hospitals/Camps

1. Visit the **BSKY Mitra counter** at any empanelled hospital
2. Carry your **Ration Card** and **Aadhaar Card**
3. The BSKY Mitra will check your eligibility in the system
4. **Biometric verification** (fingerprint) will be done
5. Smart Health Card will be issued on the spot (at designated camps)
6. For treatment, the card is used for cashless claim processing

### How to Check BSKY Eligibility Online

1. Go to [bsky.odisha.gov.in](https://bsky.odisha.gov.in)
2. Click **"Am I Eligible?"**
3. Enter your **Ration Card number** or **Aadhaar number**
4. The system will show whether your family is covered under BSKY

## How to Avail Free Treatment

1. Visit any **BSKY-empanelled hospital** (government or private)
2. Show your **BSKY Smart Health Card** at the BSKY Mitra desk
3. Complete **Aadhaar-based biometric verification**
4. The hospital will check your remaining coverage balance
5. Treatment will be provided **cashless** — no payment required
6. Hospital sends the claim to BSKY for reimbursement
7. You receive **₹500 transport allowance** per hospitalization

## List of Covered Treatments

BSKY covers **1,900+ medical/surgical packages** including:

- **Heart surgeries** — bypass, valve replacement, angioplasty
- **Cancer treatment** — chemotherapy, radiation, surgery
- **Kidney treatment** — dialysis, transplant
- **Joint replacement** — knee, hip
- **Eye surgeries** — cataract, retinal procedures
- **Maternity care** — normal and caesarean delivery
- **General surgeries** — appendix, hernia, gallbladder
- **Neurosurgery** — brain and spine procedures
- **Burns treatment**
- **Accident and trauma care**

> **OPD consultations** at government hospitals are also covered under BSKY.

## Fees

- **Smart Health Card** — Free of cost
- **Treatment** — Completely cashless (no out-of-pocket expense)
- **Registration** — No fee required
- **No premium** — The state government bears the full cost

## Processing Time

- Eligibility check: **Instant** (online)
- Smart Health Card: Issued at camps/hospitals within **same day**
- Treatment approval: **Immediate** for emergency; **24-48 hours** for planned procedures
- Transport allowance: Credited after discharge

## Important Tips

1. **Always carry your BSKY card and Aadhaar** when visiting a hospital — biometric verification is mandatory
2. **Check hospital empanelment** — Not all private hospitals accept BSKY. Check the list on bsky.odisha.gov.in
3. **Women get extra coverage** — Female members are entitled to ₹10 lakh total (₹5 lakh general + ₹5 lakh additional)
4. **No need to pay first** — Treatment is fully cashless. If a hospital asks for payment, complain to the BSKY helpline
5. **Call the helpline** — For queries, call **104** (Odisha Health Helpline) or visit the BSKY portal

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. What is the BSKY Smart Health Card?
The BSKY Smart Health Card is a health assurance card issued by the Odisha government that provides cashless treatment up to ₹5 lakh per family (₹10 lakh for women) at empanelled hospitals.

### Q2. How much coverage does BSKY provide?
₹5 lakh per family per year. Women members get an additional ₹5 lakh, totalling ₹10 lakh in annual coverage.

### Q3. Can I use BSKY outside Odisha?
Yes. BSKY can be used at empanelled hospitals across India, not just in Odisha. This is useful for specialized treatments not available locally.

### Q4. Is BSKY the same as Ayushman Bharat?
BSKY is Odisha's own health scheme that works alongside Ayushman Bharat (PMJAY). BSKY provides higher coverage (₹5-10 lakh vs ₹5 lakh under PMJAY) and covers more families in Odisha.

### Q5. What if a hospital refuses BSKY treatment?
Call the BSKY helpline at **104** or file a complaint on the BSKY portal. Empanelled hospitals are obligated to provide cashless treatment under the scheme.

### Q6. Is pre-existing disease covered?
Yes. All pre-existing diseases are covered from Day 1 under BSKY. There is no waiting period.

### Q7. How can I find BSKY-empanelled hospitals?
Visit bsky.odisha.gov.in and click on "Hospital List" to find empanelled hospitals by district.
