## What is MahaBhulekh?

MahaBhulekh is the official online land records portal of the Maharashtra government, maintained by the Department of Revenue. It provides digital access to crucial land documents — primarily the **7/12 extract (Satbara Utara)** and **8A extract** — for all districts and talukas in Maharashtra.

The 7/12 extract is arguably the most important land document in Maharashtra, combining information from two revenue records:
- **Form 7** — Ownership details (who owns the land)
- **Form 12** — Cultivation/crop details (how the land is used)

## Key Documents on MahaBhulekh

| Document | Marathi Name | Purpose |
|----------|-------------|---------|
| 7/12 Extract | सातबारा उतारा | Combined ownership + cultivation record |
| 8A Extract | ८अ उतारा | Khata (account) extract showing all plots of an owner |
| Property Card | मालमत्ता पत्रक | For urban properties (city survey areas) |
| Digital Signed Copy | डिजिटल स्वाक्षरित | Legally valid signed digital copy |

## How to Check 7/12 Utara Online (Step-by-Step)

### Step 1: Visit MahaBhulekh Portal

Go to [mahabhulekh.maharashtra.gov.in](https://mahabhulekh.maharashtra.gov.in).

### Step 2: Select Region

Maharashtra is divided into 6 revenue divisions. Select your region:
- **Pune** (पुणे)
- **Nashik** (नाशिक)
- **Aurangabad** (औरंगाबाद)
- **Nagpur** (नागपूर)
- **Amravati** (अमरावती)
- **Konkan** (कोकण)

### Step 3: Select District, Taluka & Village

1. Select your **जिल्हा (District)**
2. Select your **तालुका (Taluka)**
3. Select your **गाव (Village)**

### Step 4: Choose Search Method

Search the 7/12 extract by:
- **सर्वे नंबर / गट नंबर** (Survey Number / Gat Number)
- **पहिले नाव / अंतिम नाव** (First Name / Last Name of owner)

### Step 5: View 7/12 Extract

Click **"7/12 पहा"** (View 7/12). The extract shows:
- **Owner name(s)** and share details
- **Survey/Gat number** and sub-division
- **Total area** (in hectare-are)
- **Land type** — Jirayat (dry), Bagayat (irrigated), etc.
- **Crop details** and season
- **Encumbrances** — loans, disputes, court orders
- **Mutation entries** — recent changes

### Step 6: Print or Download

You can print the 7/12 extract directly. For a **digitally signed copy** (legally valid), follow the process below.

## How to Get Digital Signed 7/12 Copy

A digitally signed 7/12 copy is a legally valid document that can be used for bank loans, property registration, and legal proceedings.

1. Visit [bhulekh.mahabhumi.gov.in](https://bhulekh.mahabhumi.gov.in) or the **Digital Signed Copy** section on MahaBhulekh
2. Register/login with your mobile number
3. Select district → taluka → village → survey number
4. Pay the prescribed fee online (₹15 per copy)
5. Download the digitally signed PDF

## How to Check 8A Extract

The 8A extract shows all plots owned by a particular person in a village.

1. Visit [mahabhulekh.maharashtra.gov.in](https://mahabhulekh.maharashtra.gov.in)
2. Select your region, district, taluka, and village
3. Click on **"8अ"** (8A) tab
4. Enter the khata number or search by owner name
5. View all survey numbers linked to the owner with area details

## How to Check Property Card (City Survey)

For urban areas, property details are maintained in Property Cards instead of 7/12 extracts.

1. Visit [mahabhulekh.maharashtra.gov.in](https://mahabhulekh.maharashtra.gov.in)
2. Select **"प्रॉपर्टी कार्ड"** option
3. Select district, taluka, and city/village
4. Enter the CTS (City Survey) number or search by owner name
5. View property card details

## Important Tips

1. **7/12 is essential** — Always check the 7/12 extract before buying any property in Maharashtra. It reveals ownership, encumbrances, and disputes.
2. **Check encumbrances** — The "इतर हक्क" (Other Rights) column in the 7/12 shows bank loans, court orders, and government claims on the land.
3. **Digital copy is valid** — The digitally signed 7/12 from the official portal is legally valid and accepted by banks and registration offices.
4. **Verify mutation** — After buying property, ensure mutation (फेरफार) is completed and reflected in the 7/12.
5. **Urban vs rural** — For city properties, check the Property Card (मालमत्ता पत्रक) instead of 7/12.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a 7/12 extract (Satbara Utara)?
The 7/12 extract is the most important land document in Maharashtra. It combines ownership details (Form 7) and cultivation details (Form 12) into a single record. It shows who owns the land, its area, type, and any encumbrances.

### How can I check my 7/12 online?
Visit [mahabhulekh.maharashtra.gov.in](https://mahabhulekh.maharashtra.gov.in), select your region, district, taluka, and village, then search by survey number or owner name to view your 7/12 extract.

### Is the online 7/12 legally valid?
The plain online view is for reference only. To get a legally valid copy, you need to download a **digitally signed copy** (₹15) from the official portal, which is accepted by banks and government offices.

### What is an 8A extract?
The 8A extract is an account-level document that lists all survey numbers (plots) owned by a person in a particular village. It's useful to see the complete landholding of an owner.

### How much does a digital signed 7/12 cost?
A digitally signed 7/12 copy costs ₹15 per copy. It can be downloaded online after payment.

### What is the difference between 7/12 and Property Card?
The 7/12 extract is for rural agricultural land, while the Property Card (मालमत्ता पत्रक) is for urban properties in city survey areas. Both serve as ownership records.

### Can I check 7/12 for any village in Maharashtra?
Yes, MahaBhulekh covers all villages across all 36 districts and 6 revenue divisions of Maharashtra.
