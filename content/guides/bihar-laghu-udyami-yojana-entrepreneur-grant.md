## What is Bihar Laghu Udyami Yojana?

**Mukhyamantri Laghu Udyami Yojana** is the Bihar government's scheme to provide **₹2 lakh as a grant (not a loan — no repayment)** to BPL families to start a small business or micro-enterprise. It specifically targets families identified in the **Jati Aadharit Ganana (Caste-Based Survey) 2022** conducted by the Bihar government.

Key features:
- **₹2 lakh grant per family** — does not need to be repaid
- For families with monthly income below **₹6,000 (₹72,000/year)**
- **94 business categories** to choose from (food, handicraft, services, etc.)
- **SC, ST, OBC, EBC families** and **women** are prioritized
- Applied online at [udyami.bihar.gov.in](https://udyami.bihar.gov.in)

## Who is Eligible?

- Must be identified in the **Bihar Jati Aadharit Ganana (Caste-Based Survey)** as a poor family
- Monthly family income **below ₹6,000**
- Age of applicant: **18 to 50 years**
- Must be a **Bihar resident**
- Priority categories: **SC, ST, EBC (Extremely Backward Class), OBC, women**
- **One grant per family** — if someone in the family has already received it, others are not eligible

## Grant Amount and Payment Structure

The ₹2 lakh grant is paid in **3 installments:**

| Installment | Amount | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | ₹25,000 | After application approval + training |
| 2nd | ₹50,000 | After 1st installment utilised (with proof) |
| 3rd | ₹1,25,000 | After business setup (with inspection) |

**Total: ₹2,00,000 — zero repayment required.**

## 94 Business Categories Available

The scheme covers a wide range of micro-businesses:

| Sector | Examples |
|---|---|
| Food processing | Papad making, pickle making, flour mill, spice unit |
| Garments/textiles | Tailoring, embroidery, bag making |

Full list available on [udyami.bihar.gov.in](https://udyami.bihar.gov.in).

## How to Apply — Step by Step

1. Go to **[udyami.bihar.gov.in](https://udyami.bihar.gov.in)**
2. Click **"पंजीकरण करें"** (Register) or **"आवेदन करें"** (Apply)
3. Enter your **Aadhaar number** → OTP verification
4. Fill in personal details — your survey ID from Jati Ganana (if available)
5. Select your preferred **business category** from the 94 options
6. Fill in business plan details
7. Enter **bank account** for DBT
8. Upload documents
9. Submit → note Application ID

> **Important:** You must be in the Jati Aadharit Ganana database to apply. If your family was surveyed and found poor (income < ₹6,000/month), you are eligible.

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card**
2. **Caste certificate** — SC/ST/OBC/EBC (as applicable)
3. **Income proof** — from Jati Ganana survey data (or self-declaration + village survey confirmation)
4. **Bank account passbook** — Aadhaar-linked
5. **Age proof** (Aadhaar with DOB)
6. Business plan (brief description of chosen business)
7. Passport-size photograph

## After Approval — Training

After approval, selected beneficiaries receive:
- **Free training** in their chosen business (2–15 days depending on category)
- Training provided by Bihar Skill Development Mission or partner institutes
- After training, 1st installment (₹25,000) is released

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is this a loan or a grant? Do I need to repay?
This is a **grant — no repayment is needed.** Unlike MUDRA loans, this ₹2 lakh does not create any debt for you.

### My family was surveyed but I couldn't apply. What to do?
Application windows open periodically. Check [udyami.bihar.gov.in](https://udyami.bihar.gov.in) for open registration dates. Applications are accepted in batches.

### My family income is ₹7,000/month. Am I eligible?
No — the income limit is ₹6,000/month (₹72,000/year). If your income is ₹7,000, you are above the threshold.

### Can I choose any business? Are there restrictions?
You can choose from the **94 approved business categories**. You cannot choose businesses outside this list. However, the list is comprehensive — most micro-enterprise types are included.

### After getting the grant, do I need to show business proof?
Yes — the 2nd and 3rd installments are released after **utilisation proof** and **business setup verification** by an inspector. Keep receipts for all business-related purchases.

### My neighbour got this in 2023 batch. Can my wife also apply in 2024?
**One grant per family** is the rule. If your neighbour is the family head and received the grant, other family members in the same household cannot apply again.
