# LPG Gas Subsidy Not Received — How to Check & Fix

Under the PAHAL (Pratyaksh Hanstantrit Labh) scheme, also called DBTL (Direct Benefit Transfer for LPG), the government transfers LPG subsidy directly to your bank account when you buy a subsidized cylinder. If you bought a cylinder but didn't receive the subsidy in your bank account, this guide will help you check the status and fix the problem.

## How PAHAL/DBTL Subsidy Works

1. You buy an LPG cylinder at **market price** (non-subsidized rate)
2. The subsidy amount is **transferred directly** to your Aadhaar-linked bank account within 2-7 working days
3. The subsidy amount varies — it is the difference between the market price and the subsidized price set by the government

**Note:** As of 2024-25, subsidized cylinders are available to Ujjwala Yojana beneficiaries at ₹300 per cylinder. For other consumers, the government periodically announces subsidy amounts.

## How to Check LPG Subsidy Status

### Method 1: Check on Your LPG Provider's Website

**For Indane (Indian Oil - IOCL):**
1. Visit [https://www.iocl.com/indane-for-you](https://www.iocl.com/indane-for-you)
2. Click on **"Check Subsidy Status"** or **"PAHAL Status"**
3. Enter your **17-digit LPG consumer number**
4. View your subsidy credit history

**For HP Gas (HPCL):**
1. Visit [https://www.hindustanpetroleum.com/hpgas](https://www.hindustanpetroleum.com/hpgas)
2. Log in or use the **"Check DBTL Status"** option
3. Enter your LPG ID and registered mobile number

**For Bharat Gas (BPCL):**
1. Visit [https://www.bharatpetroleum.in/our-businesses/bharat-gas.aspx](https://www.bharatpetroleum.in/our-businesses/bharat-gas.aspx)
2. Click **"My LPG"** and check subsidy status
3. Enter your consumer number

### Method 2: Check on mylpg.in

1. Visit [https://mylpg.in/](https://mylpg.in/)
2. Select your LPG distributor
3. Enter your **17-digit LPG ID**
4. Check your subsidy transfer history

### Method 3: Check via Bank Statement

- Log in to your net banking or mobile banking app
- Look for credits from **"DBTL"** or **"PAHAL"** or your oil company name
- The credit usually appears 2-7 working days after cylinder delivery

### Method 4: UMANG App

1. Download the **UMANG app** from Play Store / App Store
2. Search for **"LPG Subsidy"**
3. Enter your consumer number to check status

## Why LPG Subsidy Is Not Credited — Common Reasons

### 1. Aadhaar Not Linked to LPG Connection

This is the **most common reason**. Your Aadhaar must be linked to your LPG consumer account for DBTL subsidy transfer.

**Fix:**
- Visit your LPG distributor with your Aadhaar card and LPG consumer card
- Fill the Aadhaar seeding form
- Or link online via your provider's website/app

### 2. Aadhaar Not Linked to Bank Account

Even if Aadhaar is linked to your LPG account, it must also be linked to your bank account for the subsidy to reach you.

**Fix:**
- Visit your bank branch with Aadhaar card and passbook
- Request **Aadhaar-bank account linking (seeding)**
- Ensure your bank account is the one set as the **default account** for Aadhaar-based payments (check via NPCI mapper)

### 3. Wrong Bank Account Receiving the Subsidy

If you have multiple bank accounts linked to Aadhaar, the subsidy goes to the one mapped as default in the **NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India) Aadhaar Payment Bridge**.

**Fix:**
1. Visit the bank where you want to receive the subsidy
2. Request them to **re-seed your Aadhaar** and set it as the default account for Aadhaar-based payments
3. Wait 2-3 days for the update to reflect

### 4. Bank Account Closed or Inactive

If your linked bank account is closed, frozen, or has KYC issues, the subsidy transfer will fail.

**Fix:**
- Reactivate your bank account by visiting the branch
- Or link a different active bank account to your Aadhaar and LPG connection

### 5. LPG Connection in "Non-Subsidized" Category

Some consumers are in the **Give It Up** (non-subsidized) category — either voluntarily or due to income tax filing above ₹10 lakh.

**Fix:**
- Check your subsidy status on mylpg.in
- If you were wrongly categorized, contact your LPG distributor with supporting documents

### 6. Duplicate LPG Connection

If you have more than one LPG connection (in your name or at the same address), subsidy may be blocked.

**Fix:**
- Surrender the duplicate connection at your distributor
- Retain only one connection per household

### 7. Processing Delay

Subsidy can take **up to 7 working days** after cylinder delivery. During holidays or system maintenance, it may take longer.

**Fix:**
- Wait for 10 working days before raising a complaint
- Check your bank statement after 10 days

## How to File a Complaint for Missing Subsidy

### Step 1: Contact Your LPG Distributor
- Call your local distributor's number (printed on your LPG consumer card)
- Provide your consumer number and delivery date

### Step 2: Call Oil Company Customer Care

| Company | Helpline Number |
|---|---|
| Indane (IOCL) | 1800-233-3555 |
| HP Gas (HPCL) | 1800-233-3555 |
| Bharat Gas (BPCL) | 1800-224-344 |

### Step 3: File Online Grievance

- **Indane:** [https://www.iocl.com/](https://www.iocl.com/) → Customer Grievance
- **HP Gas:** [https://www.hindustanpetroleum.com/](https://www.hindustanpetroleum.com/) → Grievance
- **Bharat Gas:** [https://www.bharatpetroleum.in/](https://www.bharatpetroleum.in/) → Customer Complaints

### Step 4: Escalate to MoPNG (Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas)

If your complaint is not resolved within 30 days:
- File a grievance on **PG Portal**: [https://pgportal.gov.in/](https://pgportal.gov.in/)
- Select Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas
- Attach your consumer number, bank statement, and previous complaint reference

## Important Tips

1. **Keep your Aadhaar linked** to both your LPG connection and your bank account — this is mandatory for DBTL subsidy
2. **Check NPCI mapper** — Your bank should be the default for Aadhaar-based payments
3. **Don't have duplicate connections** — Only one subsidized LPG connection is allowed per household
4. **Save delivery receipts** — Keep cylinder delivery receipts for at least 6 months as proof
5. **Update your mobile number** — Keep your registered mobile number active with your distributor for subsidy notifications

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does it take for LPG subsidy to be credited?
The subsidy is usually credited within 2-7 working days after cylinder delivery. During peak periods or system maintenance, it may take up to 10-15 days.

### How much is the LPG subsidy per cylinder?
The subsidy amount changes periodically based on the difference between market price and government-set subsidized price. Ujjwala Yojana beneficiaries receive cylinders at ₹300. Check your provider's website for the current subsidy amount.

### I gave up my LPG subsidy. Can I get it back?
Yes. Contact your LPG distributor and request to be moved back to the subsidized category. You can also update this on mylpg.in.

### My Aadhaar is linked but I still don't get subsidy. Why?
Your Aadhaar may be linked to a different bank account. Check with your bank if your account is set as the default for Aadhaar Payment Bridge (APB) transactions.

### Can I change the bank account for receiving LPG subsidy?
Yes. Visit the bank where you want to receive the subsidy and request Aadhaar re-seeding. The new bank will become the default for all Aadhaar-based benefit transfers.

### What is the PAHAL scheme?
PAHAL (Pratyaksh Hanstantrit Labh), also known as DBTL (Direct Benefit Transfer for LPG), is a government scheme where LPG subsidy is transferred directly to consumers' bank accounts linked with Aadhaar.
