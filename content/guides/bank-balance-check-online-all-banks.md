## Net Banking

Net banking lets you check your balance, view statements, and do much more from any browser.

**How to check balance via net banking:**

1. Visit your bank's official website (e.g., [onlinesbi.sbi](https://www.onlinesbi.sbi/) for SBI)
2. Log in with your **User ID** and **Password**
3. Navigate to **Account Summary** or **Balance Enquiry**
4. Your current balance and available balance will be displayed

If you don't have a net banking account, most banks allow online registration using your debit card and registered mobile number. See our guide on [how to open a bank account online](/guide/open-bank-account-online) for more details.

## UPI Apps — Check Balance Instantly

UPI (Unified Payments Interface) apps are the most popular way to check balance on smartphones. You can check your balance without making any transaction.

**Popular UPI apps:**

- **Google Pay** — Tap on bank account → Check Balance
- **PhonePe** — Go to bank accounts → Check Balance
- **Paytm** — Passbook → Bank Balance
- **BHIM** — Bank Account → Check Balance
- **iMobile Pay** (ICICI) — Dashboard → Account Balance
- **YONO** (SBI) — View Accounts

**Steps to check balance via any UPI app:**

1. Open the UPI app
2. Go to your linked **bank account**
3. Tap **"Check Balance"** or **"View Balance"**
4. Enter your **UPI PIN**
5. Your balance appears on screen

New to UPI? Check our [UPI payment setup guide](/guide/upi-payment-setup-guide) to get started.

## USSD *99# Method (No Internet Needed)

The **USSD banking** method works on any phone — even basic feature phones — without internet. Dial `*99#` and follow the menu.

**How to use:**

1. Dial **\*99#** from your registered mobile number
2. Enter your **bank's 3-letter short code** or first 4 letters of IFSC
3. Select **"Balance Enquiry"** from the menu
4. Enter your **UPI PIN** (set it first via your bank)

**Common bank short codes for *99#:**

> **Note:** USSD sessions cost ₹0.50 per session (charged by your telecom operator). Service is managed by NPCI.

## Passbook & ATM Balance Check

### Passbook Update

Visit your bank branch and use the **passbook printing machine** (available in most branches). Insert your passbook, and all recent transactions along with the current balance get printed automatically.

### ATM Balance Enquiry

1. Insert your **debit/ATM card** into any ATM
2. Enter your **4-digit PIN**
3. Select **"Balance Enquiry"**
4. Your balance appears on screen (you can also print a mini statement)

> Using another bank's ATM? You get **3-5 free transactions per month** (as per RBI guidelines). After that, a small fee (₹20-21 + GST) may apply.

## Important Tips

- **Always use your registered mobile number** — Missed call and SMS services only work from the number linked to your bank account.
- **Keep your mobile number updated** — Visit your branch or use net banking to update your number if it has changed.
- **UPI PIN is not your ATM PIN** — Your UPI PIN is a separate 4 or 6-digit code set through your UPI app.
- **Beware of fraud** — Never share your OTP, UPI PIN, or banking passwords with anyone. Banks never ask for these details.
- **Free service** — Missed call balance check is free. No call charges apply as the call disconnects automatically.
- **Multiple accounts?** — If you have multiple accounts with the same bank, the balance of your primary account is usually sent via missed call. Use SMS or net banking for specific accounts.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Is missed call balance check free?

Yes, missed call balance enquiry is completely free. The call disconnects automatically, so no call charges apply. You receive your balance via SMS within a few seconds.

### 2. Can I check my balance from a different phone number?

No. Missed call and SMS balance services only work from the **registered mobile number** linked to your bank account. This is a security feature to protect your account information.

### 3. What if I don't receive an SMS after the missed call?

This can happen if your mobile number is not registered with the bank, or if there's a network delay. Try again after a few minutes. If the issue persists, visit your branch to verify your registered number.

### 4. Does the *99# USSD service work on all phones?

Yes, USSD works on all phones including basic feature phones. You don't need a smartphone or internet connection. However, you need to have a UPI PIN set up, which requires initial registration through a UPI app or bank.

### 5. How many times can I check my balance in a day?

There is no fixed limit for most banks, but excessive use may be flagged. For practical purposes, checking 3-5 times a day via missed call is perfectly fine.

### 6. Can I check my bank balance using WhatsApp?

Some banks like HDFC, ICICI, and Kotak offer WhatsApp banking. You can send a message to the bank's official WhatsApp number to check your balance. Check your bank's website for availability.

### 7. Which method is best for checking bank balance?

For quick checks without internet, **missed call** is the easiest. For detailed information including transaction history, use **net banking** or **UPI apps**. Choose based on your convenience and available resources.
