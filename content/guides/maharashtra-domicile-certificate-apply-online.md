# Maharashtra Domicile Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **domicile certificate** in Maharashtra is issued by the Revenue Department (Sub-Divisional Officer / Tehsildar) and is required for NEET Maharashtra state quota (85%) medical admissions, MHT-CET reserved category, state government jobs (MPSC), and Maharashtra government scheme eligibility. Maharashtra's **15-year continuous residence requirement** is one of the strictest in India. Applications go through **Aaple Sarkar** — Maharashtra's e-services portal.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in](https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in). Must have **15 years continuous residence** in Maharashtra. Issued by SDO/Tehsildar within **15–30 days**.

---

## Maharashtra's 15-Year Domicile Rule — Explained

Maharashtra requires **15 years of continuous residence** for domicile certificate. This is far stricter than most other states (UP: 3 years, Delhi: 3 years). The key aspects:

| Rule | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Minimum residence** | **15 years continuous** in Maharashtra |
| **Gap allowed** | Minimal — continuous residence is strict |
| **School study** | Can substitute for part of the 15-year requirement in some cases |
| **Parents' domicile** | If parent is Maharashtra domicile holder, you may qualify |
| **Birth in Maharashtra** | Does not automatically confer domicile — still need 15 years |

> For **NEET Maharashtra state quota**: The Directorate of Medical Education (DMER Maharashtra) may have specific residency rules that differ from the general domicile certificate rules. Always check the NEET MH counselling notification for the specific year.

## NEET Maharashtra State Quota 2026 — Domicile Rules

For **NEET 2026 Maharashtra state quota** (85% seats in government medical colleges):

Maharashtra's state quota eligibility is based on one of these criteria (DMER notification — check each year):
1. **Studied in Maharashtra schools for Class 11–12** (last 2 years of schooling in MH)
2. **Parents are Maharashtra domicile holders** (minimum 15-year residence)
3. **Born in Maharashtra** + continuous residence

> Maharashtra has been known to change state quota rules. The 2022 Supreme Court judgment on OBC reservation in NEET and various Maharashtra government GRs affect state quota rules. Always verify at [dmer.maharashtra.gov.in](https://dmer.maharashtra.gov.in) for the current year.

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (with Maharashtra address)
- **Ration card** — Maharashtra ration card showing 15-year address
- **Voter ID** — Maharashtra voter enrollment showing address history
- **School/college certificates** — Transfer Certificates (TC) from Maharashtra schools
- **Property documents** — ownership/rent documents at Maharashtra address
- **Utility bills** — electricity/gas bills (covering 15 years or significant portion)
- **Parent's domicile certificate** (if applying through parent's domicile)
- Self-declaration of 15-year continuous residency

## How to Apply Online — Aaple Sarkar Portal

1. Go to [aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in](https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in)
2. Register / log in with mobile number and Aadhaar
3. Click **"Revenue Department"** → **"Domicile Certificate"**
4. Fill form:
   - Applicant name, address (district, tehsil, village/ward)
   - Duration of residence in Maharashtra (must be 15+ years)
   - Purpose of certificate
5. Upload documents (Aadhaar, ration card, voter ID, school TCs, utility bills)
6. Pay fee: **₹20–30** (online)
7. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via Setu Suvidha Kendra / CSC

1. Visit **Aaple Sarkar Setu** (government service centre) or CSC
2. Carry Aadhaar, ration card, school TCs, utility bills
3. Operator submits application
4. Service charge: approximately ₹30–60
5. Collect acknowledgement

## Maharashtra Domicile — Special Situations

### Born in Maharashtra but lived outside for 10 years?

If you've lived outside Maharashtra for a significant period, you may NOT qualify for domicile unless you can prove **15 continuous years** of recent residence. Gaps in residence (even for study) may disqualify you. Consult your SDO office.

### Maharashtra children of government employees posted outside?

Children of **Maharashtra state government employees** posted outside Maharashtra as part of their service may still be eligible for Maharashtra domicile. Submit the parent's service posting order and Maharashtra domicile.

### NEET Maharashtra quota for children of Maharashtra government employees?

Children of Maharashtra state government employees posted outside Maharashtra may claim Maharashtra state quota — this is a specific provision in the DMER counselling rules. Check the current year's DMER NEET notification.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 15-year rule for domicile — can I count school years?

Yes, years spent in Maharashtra schools count towards the 15-year residence requirement. A student who studied in Maharashtra from age 5 to 18 (13 years in school) and then returned for college can count both periods.

### My parents have Maharashtra domicile — do I automatically qualify?

**Not automatically.** If both parents are Maharashtra domicile holders and you were born and raised in Maharashtra, you likely qualify. But if you were born/raised outside Maharashtra, you need your own 15-year residence proof or claim through special provisions.

### Maharashtra domicile for MPSC — mandatory?

For **MPSC (Maharashtra PSC)** recruitment: domicile is required for Maharashtra-reserved posts. However, for all-India open competition posts at the state level, domicile may not be mandatory. Check the specific MPSC advertisement.
