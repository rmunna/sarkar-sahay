## What is Labanyavati Yojana?

Labanyavati Yojana is a women welfare scheme announced by the Government of Odisha aimed at providing financial assistance and empowerment support to women in the state. Named after "Labanyavati" — a symbol of grace and beauty in Odia literature — the scheme is part of the Odisha government's broader push to improve the economic condition and social status of women across the state.

The scheme is designed to complement other women-centric programmes like [Subhadra Yojana](/guide/subhadra-yojana-odisha) and [Mission Shakti](/guide/mission-shakti-odisha), creating a comprehensive support ecosystem for women in Odisha.

> **Note:** This scheme is relatively new and some operational details are still being finalized by the government. We recommend checking the official Odisha government portal for the latest updates and notifications.

## Key Benefits

- **Direct financial assistance** to eligible women through DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer)
- **Monthly/periodic payments** credited to Aadhaar-linked bank accounts
- **Women's empowerment** — Supporting economic independence of women
- **Nutrition and health support** — Enabling better nutrition and healthcare access
- **Complementary to other schemes** — Works alongside Subhadra Yojana, KALIA, and Mission Shakti

## Who is Eligible?

### General Eligibility Criteria
- Must be a **woman resident of Odisha**
- Must be within the **prescribed age group** (as notified by the government)
- Must have a **valid Aadhaar Card**
- Must have a **bank account in her own name** (Aadhaar-linked)
- Must not be an **income tax payer**
- Must not be a **government employee** (Central/State/PSU)

### Priority Categories
- **BPL (Below Poverty Line) women**
- **Widows and destitute women**
- **SC/ST women**
- **Single mothers**
- Women from **economically weaker sections (EWS)**

### Who May NOT be Eligible
- Government employees and their spouses (if income exceeds limit)
- Income tax paying families
- Women already receiving benefits from overlapping schemes (subject to government notification)

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar Card** (mandatory for e-KYC and DBT)
- **Bank passbook** (Aadhaar-linked account in applicant's name)
- **Voter ID Card** / Residence proof
- **Ration Card**
- **Income Certificate** (from Tehsildar if required)
- **Caste Certificate** (for SC/ST applicants)
- **Passport-size photographs** (2 copies)
- **Mobile number** linked to Aadhaar

## How to Apply

### Online Application
1. Visit the **official Odisha government portal** at [odisha.gov.in](https://odisha.gov.in)
2. Look for the **Labanyavati Yojana** section or relevant WCD Department link
3. Click on **"Apply Online"** or **"New Registration"**
4. Complete **Aadhaar-based e-KYC** verification
5. Fill in personal details — name, age, address, district, block, GP
6. Enter **bank account details**
7. Upload required documents
8. Submit the application and note the reference number

### Offline Application
1. Visit the **nearest Anganwadi Centre**, **Block Development Office**, or **Women & Child Development office**
2. Collect the application form
3. Fill in all details accurately
4. Attach self-attested copies of required documents
5. Submit the form and collect acknowledgement receipt
6. Application will be verified at block and district level

### Through Mo Seva Kendra
1. Visit the nearest **Mo Seva Kendra** or **Common Service Centre**
2. Carry original documents (Aadhaar, bank passbook, etc.)
3. The operator will assist with online registration
4. Complete biometric/OTP verification
5. Collect the application receipt

## Fees

- **Application** — Free of cost
- **Registration** — No charges
- **CSC assistance** — Nominal service fee may apply

## Processing Time

- Application verification: **15-30 days**
- Approval and first payment: **30-60 days** (subject to government disbursement schedule)
- Subsequent payments: As per notified schedule
