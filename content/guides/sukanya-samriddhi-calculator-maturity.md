# Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana Calculator: How to Calculate Maturity Amount

Planning how much your Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY) account will grow? This guide explains the calculation formula, provides year-wise breakdowns for different deposit amounts, and helps you estimate the maturity value of your investment.

## SSY Interest Calculation Formula

The maturity amount is calculated using the compound interest formula:

**A = P × (1 + r/n)^(n×t)**

Where:
- **A** = Maturity amount
- **P** = Principal (annual deposit)
- **r** = Annual interest rate (currently 8.2%)
- **n** = Compounding frequency (1, since SSY compounds annually)
- **t** = Time in years

Since SSY involves **annual deposits for 15 years** with interest compounding for **21 years**, the calculation works like an annuity for the first 15 years, then pure compound interest for the remaining 6 years.

## How SSY Interest Works

- Interest is calculated on the **lowest balance between the 5th and last day** of each month
- Interest is **compounded annually** and credited at the end of the financial year
- Deposits made **before the 5th of the month** earn interest for that full month
- Deposits after the 5th earn interest only from the next month

**Pro tip:** Always deposit before the 5th of the month to maximize interest earnings.

## Step-by-Step Manual Calculation

Let's calculate for ₹50,000 annual deposit at 8.2%:

### Year 1
- Deposit: ₹50,000
- Interest: ₹50,000 × 8.2% = ₹4,100
- Balance: ₹54,100

### Year 2
- New deposit: ₹50,000
- Balance before interest: ₹1,04,100
- Interest: ₹1,04,100 × 8.2% = ₹8,536
- Balance: ₹1,12,636

### Years 3-15
Continue the same pattern — add ₹50,000 deposit, calculate 8.2% interest on the total balance.

### Years 16-21
No new deposits. Interest compounds on the existing balance for 6 more years.

**Final maturity amount: ≈ ₹23,09,000**

## Tips to Maximize Your SSY Returns

1. **Start early** — Open the account as soon as your daughter is born to get the full 21-year compounding benefit
2. **Deposit early in the year** — Make lump sum deposits before April 5th for maximum interest
3. **Deposit the maximum** — ₹1,50,000 per year gives the highest maturity amount
4. **Don't miss deposits** — Missing the minimum ₹250 attracts a penalty and disrupts compounding
5. **Combine with 80C** — SSY deposits qualify for [Section 80C tax deduction](/guide/income-tax-saving-tips-80c-investments)

## Tax Savings on SSY Deposits

SSY deposits up to ₹1,50,000 qualify for deduction under Section 80C. If you're in the 30% tax bracket, depositing the maximum saves you **₹46,800 in taxes annually** (including cess).
