# KEAM 2026 — Kerala Engineering Architecture Medical Entrance Exam

**KEAM 2026** (Kerala Engineering Architecture Medical) is conducted by the **CEE Kerala (Commissioner for Entrance Examinations)** for admission to engineering, architecture, and pharmacy courses in Kerala government and private self-financing colleges. KEAM is the gateway to Kerala's professional colleges for the 85% state quota.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [cee.kerala.gov.in](https://cee.kerala.gov.in). **Kerala nativity certificate** required for state quota. PCM (engineering/architecture) exam. NEET used for MBBS/BDS.

## Eligibility Criteria

| Criteria | Engineering | Pharmacy |
|----------|-------------|----------|
| **Qualification** | Plus Two (+2) / Class 12 with PCM | Plus Two with Physics, Chemistry, Biology |
| **Minimum marks** | 45% in Physics + Chemistry + Mathematics (40% for SC/ST) | 45% in Physics, Chemistry, Biology |
| **Age** | No upper age limit (AICTE guidelines followed) | Per PCI norms |
| **Kerala nativity** | Required for 85% state quota | Required for state quota |

### Kerala Nativity — State Quota Eligibility

For the **85% state quota** in Kerala engineering colleges:

| Route | Requirement |
|-------|-------------|
| **Born in Kerala** | Kerala nativity certificate (birth in Kerala) |
| **Studied in Kerala** | Attended Kerala schools for minimum CEE-prescribed period |
| **Parent is Kerala native** | Parent's nativity + family's Kerala residence |

## KEAM 2026 Application Process

1. Go to [cee.kerala.gov.in](https://cee.kerala.gov.in) → **"KEAM 2026 Online Application"**
2. Register with mobile number + email
3. Fill application:
   - Personal details (name, DOB, category, nativity)
   - Plus Two details (board, school, marks)
   - Stream: Engineering (PCM) / Pharmacy (PCB)
4. Upload: photo + signature + relevant certificates
5. Pay fee:
   - General: **₹700**
   - SC/ST Kerala: **₹350**
6. Download and preserve acknowledgement

## Exam Pattern

**KEAM Paper (Engineering / Pharmacy):**

| Subject | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| Mathematics | 72 | 288 |
| Physics | 54 | 216 |
| Chemistry | 54 | 216 |
| **Total** | **180** | **720** |

- **Mode**: Offline (OMR-based paper)
- **Marking**: +4 for correct, **-1 for wrong** (negative marking applies)
- **Duration**: 3 hours (engineering exam)

## KEAM Syllabus 2026

KEAM follows **Kerala Plus Two (DHSE/VHSE) syllabus**:

**Mathematics**: Mathematical Reasoning, Algebra (Matrices, Determinants), Coordinate Geometry, Calculus, Vectors, Statistics & Probability

**Physics**: Physical World, Motion, Laws of Motion, Work-Energy, Rotational Motion, Gravitation, Thermodynamics, Electrostatics, Current Electricity, Electromagnetism, Optics, Dual Nature, Atoms & Nuclei

**Chemistry**: Atomic Structure, Chemical Bonding, States of Matter, Thermodynamics, Equilibrium, Solutions, Electrochemistry, Organic Chemistry, Polymers, Biomolecules

## NEET and KEAM — Medical Admissions in Kerala

- **MBBS/BDS** in Kerala: Uses **NEET score** only (not KEAM)
- **Engineering/Architecture**: KEAM score
- **B.Pharm**: KEAM PCB or NEET score (check CEE Kerala notification)

CEE Kerala conducts both KEAM allotment (engineering) and NEET Kerala counselling (medical) separately.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### KEAM rank vs NEET score — which for Kerala medical?

For **MBBS/BDS in Kerala**: NEET score (not KEAM). CEE Kerala uses NEET score for the 85% state medical quota. KEAM score is used for engineering, architecture, and pharmacy only.

### How is KEAM rank calculated?

KEAM rank = weighted score of KEAM paper marks + Plus Two (Class 12) marks:
- **50% weight** from KEAM exam score
- **50% weight** from Plus Two marks (normalized)

### Can students who didn't study in Kerala get a Kerala state quota seat?

If you were **born in Kerala** (even if you studied elsewhere), you can get Kerala nativity certificate and claim state quota. Verify with the current year's CEE notification for exact eligibility routes.
