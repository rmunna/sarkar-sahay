# Banglar Yuva Sathi Scheme — ₹1,500 Monthly for West Bengal Youth

The **Banglar Yuva Sathi Scheme** (বাংলার যুব সাথী প্রকল্প) is a new financial assistance programme launched by Chief Minister Mamata Banerjee on **5 February 2026**. Administered by the **Sports and Youth Services Department**, the scheme provides **₹1,500 per month** to unemployed youth in West Bengal aged 21–40 years. Payments begin **1 April 2026** through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to Aadhaar-linked bank accounts.

Over a maximum duration of five years, an eligible beneficiary can receive up to **₹90,000** in total support — or until they secure employment or self-employment, whichever comes first.

## Who Is Eligible for Banglar Yuva Sathi?

To qualify for the Banglar Yuva Sathi Scheme, applicants must meet all of the following criteria:

### Age Requirement
- Must be between **21 and 40 years** of age on the date of application.

### Residency
- Must be a **permanent resident of West Bengal** with valid proof such as a ration card or domicile certificate.

### Education
- Must have passed **Class 10 (Madhyamik or equivalent)** from a recognised board — WBBSE, CBSE, ICSE, or equivalent.
- Higher education qualifications are welcome but not required.

### Employment Status
- Must be **currently unemployed** — no full-time or part-time employment.
- Self-declaration of unemployment is mandatory.

### Who Cannot Apply?
- Beneficiaries already receiving **Yuvashree Arpan** or **Yuva Utsaha** benefits.
- Persons residing outside West Bengal.
- Those who have not cleared Class 10.
- Note: Students or scholarship recipients who are unemployed **can** still apply.
- No family income ceiling has been specified — the scheme relies on self-declared unemployment status.

## How to Apply for Banglar Yuva Sathi Scheme

There are two application channels: offline through Duare Sarkar camps, and online through the official portal.

### Option 1: Offline Application (Duare Sarkar Camps)

The offline application window ran from **15 February to 26 February 2026** at designated camps across West Bengal.

**Steps:**
1. Visit your nearest **block office, municipal office, or Duare Sarkar camp**.
2. Collect and fill out **Form-A**, the **Self-Declaration Form**, and the **bank mandate form**.
3. Submit all required documents (originals for verification, photocopies to attach).
4. Collect your **acknowledgement slip** — keep it safe for tracking.
5. Late applications after 26 February will not be accepted at camps.

### Option 2: Online Application (Portal-Based)

Online applications open from **15 August 2026** onwards on the official portal.

**Steps:**
1. Visit **[banglaryuvasathi.gov.in](https://banglaryuvasathi.gov.in/)**.
2. Register using your **mobile number and OTP**.
3. Fill in your personal details, educational qualifications, and bank account information (Aadhaar-seeded account mandatory).
4. Upload scanned copies of all required documents.
5. Preview the form, confirm details, and **submit**.
6. Note down your **reference ID** for future tracking.

> **Tip:** Ensure your Aadhaar is linked to your bank account before applying, as payments are disbursed via Aadhaar-based DBT only.

## Documents Required for Application

Keep these documents ready before visiting a camp or applying online:

- **Passport-size photograph** (recent)
- **Aadhaar Card** — for identity, address proof, and DBT linkage
- **Voter ID** — additional identity proof (if available)
- **Ration Card or Domicile Certificate** — proof of West Bengal residency
- **Class 10 Marksheet and Admit Card** — proof of minimum education
- **Highest Qualification Certificate** — if education is above Class 10
- **Income Certificate** — issued by local authority (self or family)
- **Caste Certificate** — SC/ST/OBC, if applicable
- **Bank Passbook or Cancelled Cheque** — showing Aadhaar-linked account details

## How Payments Work

- **Amount:** ₹1,500 credited monthly to your Aadhaar-linked bank account.
- **Mode:** Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
- **Duration:** Up to 5 years from the date of approval.
- **Termination:** Payments stop automatically once you secure employment or self-employment, or when the 5-year period ends.
- **First disbursement:** Expected from **1 April 2026**.

Processing is handled by **Block Development Officers (BDOs)** in rural areas and **municipal authorities** in urban areas.

## How to Check Your Application Status

1. Visit **[banglaryuvasathi.gov.in](https://banglaryuvasathi.gov.in/)** or the **[West Bengal Government portal](https://wb.gov.in/)**.
2. Click on **"Check Application Status"** or **"Track Status"**.
3. Enter your registered **mobile number**, verify via **OTP**, and enter your **reference ID or application number**.
4. Your status will show as one of: **Submitted → Verified → Approved → Payment Initiated**.

## How Banglar Yuva Sathi Differs from Existing West Bengal Youth Schemes

West Bengal already runs youth-focused programmes like **Yuvashree Arpan** and **Yuva Utsaha**. Here is how the new scheme compares:

| Feature | Banglar Yuva Sathi | Yuvashree Arpan |
|---|---|---|
| **Monthly Amount** | ₹1,500 | ₹1,500 |
| **Age Group** | 21–40 years | 18–45 years |
| **Duration** | Up to 5 years | 1 year (renewable) |
| **Education** | Class 10 pass | Varies by category |
| **Can overlap?** | No — exclusive | No — exclusive |

The key difference is the **5-year duration** and structured DBT mechanism of Banglar Yuva Sathi, making it a longer-term support system for unemployed youth compared to the annual renewal model of Yuvashree.

> **Important:** You cannot receive benefits from both Banglar Yuva Sathi and Yuvashree Arpan/Yuva Utsaha simultaneously. If you are already enrolled in one of those schemes, you are not eligible for this scheme.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is there any income limit for applicants?
No specific family income ceiling has been announced. Eligibility is based on self-declared unemployment status.

### Can college students apply?
Yes, students who are unemployed can apply, even if they receive a scholarship — as long as they are not beneficiaries of Yuvashree or Yuva Utsaha.

### What happens if I get a job during the 5-year period?
Your monthly allowance will stop once you secure employment or self-employment. You are expected to update your status.

### Can I apply if I passed Class 10 from outside West Bengal?
Yes, as long as you are a **permanent resident of West Bengal** and passed Class 10 from a recognised board (WBBSE, CBSE, ICSE, or equivalent).

### When does online application open?
Online applications through the portal are expected to open from **15 August 2026**. The initial application window (February 2026) was offline only through Duare Sarkar camps.
