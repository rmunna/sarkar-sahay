## What is Domicile Certificate Status Check?

A domicile certificate (also called a residence certificate in some states) is an official document that proves you are a permanent resident of a particular state. After applying through your state's e-District or service portal, you can check the application status online.

Domicile certificates are required for state government job applications, educational admissions under state quota, scholarships, and various state-level benefits.

> **Karnataka residents:** See our detailed guide on [how to apply for a domicile/residence certificate in Karnataka](/guide/karnataka-domicile-residence-certificate-online).

## State-Wise Domicile Certificate Status Check

### 1. Karnataka — Nadakacheri Portal

**Portal:** [nadakacheri.karnataka.gov.in](https://nadakacheri.karnataka.gov.in)

#### Step-by-Step Process

1. Visit [https://nadakacheri.karnataka.gov.in](https://nadakacheri.karnataka.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Check Application Status"**
3. Enter your **Application Number** received during application
4. Click **"Get Status"**
5. View the current status — Applied, Under Verification, Approved, or Rejected

**Helpline:** 080-22230282

### 2. Uttar Pradesh — e-District UP

**Portal:** [edistrict.up.gov.in](https://edistrict.up.gov.in)

#### Step-by-Step Process

1. Visit [https://edistrict.up.gov.in](https://edistrict.up.gov.in)
2. Click on **"आवेदन की स्थिति"** (Application Status)
3. Enter your **Application Number**
4. Enter the **captcha** and click **"Search"**
5. Your domicile certificate application status will be displayed

**Helpline:** 0522-2304706 / 1800-1800-150

### 3. Maharashtra — Aaple Sarkar Portal

**Portal:** [aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in](https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in)

## Check Domicile Certificate Status by Mobile Number

Most state portals send SMS updates to the registered mobile number:

- **Uttar Pradesh:** Automatic SMS at each processing stage
- **Bihar (RTPS):** SMS notification on approval/rejection
- **Maharashtra:** SMS alerts via Aaple Sarkar
- **Other states:** Check if your mobile was registered during the application

## Check Domicile Certificate Status by Aadhaar

Some states support Aadhaar-based tracking:

1. Visit your state's e-District portal
2. Select **"Search by Aadhaar"** (if available)
3. Enter your Aadhaar number and verify via OTP
4. View all linked applications

> Available in select states including Karnataka, Delhi, and Bihar.

## Check Domicile Certificate Status by SMS

There is **no universal SMS code** for domicile certificate tracking. Status updates are typically sent automatically by the portal if your mobile number was registered during application.

## Common Issues and Fixes

### 1. Application Stuck at "Under Verification"
- **Reason:** The verifying officer (Tehsildar/SDM) may need additional documents or have a backlog
- **Fix:** Visit the Tehsil or SDM office with your acknowledgement slip. Ask for the specific reason

### 2. Application Rejected
- **Reason:** Insufficient proof of residence, missing documents, or residency period not met
- **Fix:** Check the rejection reason on the portal. Most states require 3–15 years of continuous residence. Gather stronger proof and re-apply

### 3. Wrong Address or Name on Certificate
- **Reason:** Data entry error during processing
- **Fix:** Apply for correction at the issuing authority. Submit supporting documents (Aadhaar, utility bills, school records)

### 4. Cannot Find Application on Portal
- **Reason:** Applied at CSC/Jan Seva Kendra or offline
- **Fix:** Visit the CSC or Tehsil office with your receipt to check status

### 5. Certificate Not Accepted by Another State's Institution
- **Reason:** Some institutions require a domicile certificate from their own state
- **Fix:** Confirm the requirement. If you need a domicile certificate from another state, you must be a permanent resident there

## Important Tips

1. **Apply through the official e-District portal** for easy online tracking and faster processing
2. **Keep your application number safe** — it is essential for status tracking
3. **Ensure your address proof matches** across all documents (Aadhaar, voter ID, utility bills)
4. **Follow up in person** if status doesn't change for more than 15 days
5. **Some states issue a combined domicile + residence certificate** — check your state's specific requirements

## FAQs

### How long does it take to get a domicile certificate?
Typically 7–30 working days depending on the state. Bihar and Karnataka are usually faster (7–15 days).

### Can I check domicile certificate status without an application number?
Some portals allow searching by name or Aadhaar. However, the application number is the most reliable method.

### Is domicile certificate status check free?
Yes, checking status online is completely free on all state portals.

### What is the difference between domicile certificate and residence certificate?
In many states, they are the same document with different names. A domicile certificate proves permanent residence, while a residence certificate may prove temporary or current residence. Check your state's terminology.

### What documents are needed for a domicile certificate?
Typically: [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online), voter ID, ration card, utility bills (electricity/water in your name), school/college certificates, and a self-declaration affidavit.

### How long must I reside in a state to get a domicile certificate?
Residency requirements vary by state — typically 3 to 15 years of continuous residence. Some states require birth in the state or parents' domicile.

### Can I hold domicile certificates from two states?
No, you can be a domicile of only one state at a time. If you change your permanent residence, you need to surrender the old certificate and apply for a new one.
