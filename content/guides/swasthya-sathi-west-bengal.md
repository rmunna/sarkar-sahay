# Swasthya Sathi (স্বাস্থ্য সাথী) — West Bengal's Free Health Insurance Scheme

**Swasthya Sathi** is West Bengal's flagship universal health insurance scheme providing **₹5 lakh per family per year** of completely free, cashless hospitalisation. Launched in December 2016, it now covers over **2.42 crore families** across the state with **2,800+ empanelled hospitals**.

> Similar to [Ayushman Bharat](/guide/ayushman-bharat-health-card) at the national level, but Swasthya Sathi covers **all** West Bengal families — not just BPL households.

---

## Who Is Eligible?

Swasthya Sathi is a **universal scheme** — almost every family in West Bengal qualifies:

- **All families with a West Bengal ration card** (digital or physical)
- No income limit — covers APL and BPL families alike
- **No cap on family size** — parents of both spouses are included
- Dependent physically challenged family members are also covered
- **100% government-funded** — zero contribution from the beneficiary

### You are eligible if:

1. You are a resident of West Bengal
2. Your family holds a valid ration card issued by the West Bengal government

That's it. No income proof, no BPL certificate needed.

## What Does Swasthya Sathi Cover?

### Coverage Amount

| Category | Annual Limit per Family |
|---|---|
| **General hospitalisation** (secondary care) | Up to ₹1.5 lakh |
| **Critical illness** (tertiary/specialised care) | Up to ₹5 lakh |
| **Overall cap** | **₹5 lakh per family per year** |

### What's Included

- ✅ All **pre-existing diseases** — covered from day one
- ✅ **Hospitalisation** (IPD) — room, nursing, ICU, medicines
- ✅ **Surgeries** — general, orthopaedic, cardiac, cancer, neuro, etc.
- ✅ **Pre-hospitalisation** expenses (up to 15 days before admission)
- ✅ **Post-hospitalisation** follow-up (up to 15 days after discharge)
- ✅ **Medicines, diagnostics, and consumables** during treatment
- ✅ **Maternity and newborn care**
- ✅ **Dialysis, chemotherapy, radiotherapy**

### What's NOT Covered

- ❌ OPD (outpatient) consultations (unless converted to IPD)
- ❌ Cosmetic surgery
- ❌ Substance abuse treatment
- ❌ Dental treatment (unless hospitalisation is required)

## How to Get Your Swasthya Sathi Smart Card

The Swasthya Sathi card is a **biometric smart card** issued in the **name of the woman head of the family**.

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Attend a Duare Sarkar (দুয়ারে সরকার) camp** — these are held regularly at the block/ward level across West Bengal
2. **Carry these documents:**
   - Ration card (mandatory)
   - Aadhaar card of all family members
   - Mobile number
3. **Biometric enrolment** — fingerprints and photographs of all family members are captured on-site
4. **Instant card issuance** — the smart card is printed and handed over on the same day
5. **Card is issued in the woman head's name** — empowering women as the family's health guardian

### Can't find a camp?

- Call the **toll-free helpline: 1800-345-5384** (24×7)
- Visit your nearest **Block Development Office** or **Municipal office**
- Check the [official website](https://swasthyasathi.gov.in) for camp schedules

## How to Use Swasthya Sathi at a Hospital

### At the Time of Admission

1. **Go to any empanelled hospital** (government or private)
2. **Show your Swasthya Sathi smart card** at the Swasthya Sathi help desk
3. **Deposit the card** at the hospital within **24 hours** of admission (mandatory)
4. The hospital will verify your identity via biometric/photo and **block the required amount** on your card
5. **No payment needed** — treatment is completely cashless

### During Treatment

- All medicines, diagnostics, and procedures are covered under the package rate
- You will receive **SMS alerts** on your registered mobile for every transaction
- If you need to check your remaining balance, use the **Swasthya Sathi mobile app** (available on Google Play)

### At Discharge

- The hospital settles the claim directly with the government
- You receive a **discharge slip** showing the amount deducted
- Your smart card is returned to you
- An **e-health record** is uploaded online in real-time

## Finding Empanelled Hospitals

With **2,800+ hospitals** empanelled (both government and private), finding one near you is easy:

- **Online:** Visit [swasthyasathi.gov.in → Hospital Search](https://swasthyasathi.gov.in/HospitalSearch/HospitalSearch)
- **Mobile App:** Download "Swasthya Sathi" from Google Play Store → tap "Active Hospitals"
- **Helpline:** Call **1800-345-5384** (toll-free, 24×7)

### Hospital Grades

Hospitals are graded based on facilities and infrastructure:

| Grade | Type |
|---|---|
| **Grade A** | Full tertiary care (cardiac surgery, neuro, cancer, etc.) |
| **Grade B** | Advanced secondary care |
| **Grade C** | Basic secondary care |
| **Grade R** | Referral hospitals |

You are free to choose any empanelled hospital regardless of grade.

## Claim Process

**You don't need to file any claim yourself.** The process is fully cashless and paperless:

1. Hospital submits a **pre-authorisation request** online
2. The request is approved within **24 hours**
3. After discharge, the hospital submits the final claim
4. Government reimburses the hospital within **30 days** (interest charged on delays)
5. **98.45% of claims** have been settled successfully (as of December 2025)

### If You Face Issues

- Call the **24×7 helpline: 1800-345-5384**
- File a **grievance online** at [swasthyasathi.gov.in](https://swasthyasathi.gov.in)
- The scheme has an **online grievance monitoring mechanism** with escalation matrix

## Renewal and Validity

- The Swasthya Sathi card **renews automatically every year** — no separate renewal process
- Coverage resets to ₹5 lakh at the start of each policy year
- If you add a new family member, visit the next Duare Sarkar camp to update your card
- Lost card? Call the helpline or visit a camp for a replacement

## Swasthya Sathi vs Ayushman Bharat — Key Differences

| Feature | Swasthya Sathi (WB) | Ayushman Bharat (Central) |
|---|---|---|
| **Coverage** | ₹5 lakh/family/year | ₹5 lakh/family/year |
| **Eligibility** | All WB families with ration card | SECC-based BPL families only |
| **Card issued to** | Woman head of family | Any family member |
| **Funded by** | 100% State Government | Centre + State |
| **Pre-existing diseases** | Covered from day one | Covered from day one |
| **Hospitals** | 2,800+ in WB | Pan-India network |

> **Note:** West Bengal has **opted out** of the central Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY) scheme. Swasthya Sathi is the state's own alternative with broader coverage.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Swasthya Sathi really free?
Yes. The entire cost is borne by the West Bengal state government. You pay nothing — no premium, no co-pay, no deductible.

### Can I use it at private hospitals?
Yes. Both government and private hospitals that are empanelled under the scheme accept Swasthya Sathi cards.

### Is OPD covered?
No. Only hospitalisation (IPD) is covered. However, if an OPD visit leads to hospitalisation, the entire treatment cost is covered.

### What if my card balance runs out?
The ₹5 lakh limit resets every year. If you exhaust it within a year, you'll need to pay out-of-pocket or wait for the next policy year.

### Can I use Swasthya Sathi outside West Bengal?
The scheme primarily covers treatment at empanelled hospitals within West Bengal.

### Do I need Aadhaar for Swasthya Sathi?
Aadhaar is required for enrolment and biometric verification at hospitals.
