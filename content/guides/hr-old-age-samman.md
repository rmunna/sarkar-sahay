## What is Old Age Samman Allowance?

The **Old Age Samman Allowance** is a social security pension scheme by the **Government of Haryana** under the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment. It provides a monthly pension of **₹2,750** to senior citizens of Haryana who are aged 60 years and above.

This scheme ensures that elderly residents who do not have adequate financial support can live with dignity. The pension is transferred directly to the beneficiary's bank account through DBT.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Age:** Must be **60 years or older**
2. **Residency:** Must be a permanent resident of Haryana (verified via Parivar Pehchan Patra)
3. **Income:** Annual income from all sources should not exceed **₹2,00,000**
4. **Other Pension:** Should not be receiving any other government pension (central or state)
5. **Family ID:** Must have a verified Parivar Pehchan Patra (Haryana Family ID)

## Pension Amount

| Age Group | Monthly Pension |
|-----------|----------------|
| 60-79 years | ₹2,750 |
| 80 years and above | ₹2,750 (additional NSAP component may apply) |

## Documents Required

### Identity & Address
- Aadhaar Card
- Parivar Pehchan Patra (Haryana Family ID) — **mandatory**
- Voter ID Card

### Age Proof
- Aadhaar Card / Birth Certificate / School Leaving Certificate
- Any government document showing date of birth

### Income Proof
- Self-declaration or Income Certificate (annual income ≤ ₹2,00,000)

### Bank Details
- Bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked account)
- IFSC code

### Photographs
- Recent passport-size photograph

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Online Application (Saral Haryana)

1. **Visit** [saralharyana.gov.in](https://saralharyana.gov.in)
2. **Login** using your credentials or register as a new user
3. **Search** for "Old Age Samman Allowance" in the services section
4. **Enter** Parivar Pehchan Patra (Family ID) number — details will auto-populate
5. **Fill** remaining details: age proof, bank account, income declaration
6. **Upload** required documents
7. **Submit** the application and note the reference number
8. **Track** status online via Saral Haryana

### Offline Application (Atal Seva Kendra / DSJO)

1. **Visit** the nearest **Atal Seva Kendra** or **District Social Justice Office**
2. **Provide** your Parivar Pehchan Patra number
3. **Fill** the application form with assistance from the centre staff
4. **Attach** photocopies of documents
5. **Submit** and collect the acknowledgement receipt
6. **Verification** will be done at block level

## Processing Time

- **Verification:** 15-30 days (block-level verification)
- **Approval:** After verification, pension is sanctioned
- **First Payment:** Typically within 1-2 months of approval
- **Ongoing:** Monthly, directly to bank account via DBT

## Important Tips

1. **Parivar Pehchan Patra is Mandatory:** Without Family ID, you cannot apply for this scheme. Get it from any Atal Seva Kendra
2. **Aadhaar-Bank Link:** Ensure your bank account is linked with Aadhaar for DBT
3. **Annual Life Certificate:** Submit Jeevan Pramaan (life certificate) annually to continue receiving pension
4. **No Duplicate Pension:** If you receive any other government pension, you are not eligible
5. **Update Contact:** Keep your mobile number updated for OTP-based verifications

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. What is the current pension amount under Old Age Samman Allowance?
The monthly pension is **₹2,750** for senior citizens aged 60 and above.

### Q2. What is the income limit for eligibility?
Annual income from all sources should not exceed **₹2,00,000**.

### Q3. Is Parivar Pehchan Patra mandatory?
Yes, the Haryana Family ID is mandatory for applying to this scheme.

### Q4. How can I apply?
Apply online at [saralharyana.gov.in](https://saralharyana.gov.in) or visit your nearest Atal Seva Kendra.

### Q5. Can I get both Old Age Pension and Widow Pension?
No, only one social security pension can be availed at a time.

### Q6. How is the pension paid?
Directly to your Aadhaar-linked bank account via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) every month.

### Q7. What if my application is rejected?
You can re-apply after correcting the issues. Contact the District Social Justice Office for clarification.
