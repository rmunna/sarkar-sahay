# GeM Catalogue Upload Failed — How to Fix Product Listing Errors

Uploading your product catalogue on GeM is essential to start selling to government buyers. But sellers frequently face errors — image rejections, specification mismatches, wrong category selection, and brand approval blocks. This guide covers every common catalogue upload error and its fix.

## Common Catalogue Upload Errors

- **"Image upload failed"** — image rejected due to format, size, or quality
- **"Specification mismatch"** — product specs don't match the selected category template
- **"Category not applicable"** — wrong product category selected
- **"Brand not approved"** — brand requires OEM authorization
- **"OEM panel authorization required"** — resellers need manufacturer approval
- **"Catalogue rejected"** — manual review rejected the listing
- **"Duplicate catalogue"** — similar product already listed by you

## Fix 1: Image Format and Size Errors

GeM has strict image requirements:

1. **Accepted formats** — JPG, JPEG, and PNG only; no BMP, GIF, TIFF, or WebP
2. **File size limit** — Each image must be under 2 MB; compress large files
3. **Minimum resolution** — At least 500 x 500 pixels; blurry or low-resolution images are rejected
4. **White background** — Product images should have a clean white background for catalogue display
5. **No watermarks or text** — Don't add company logos, watermarks, or promotional text on the product image
6. **Multiple images** — Upload 2-3 images showing different angles of the product
7. **Actual product photo** — Use real product photos, not 3D renders or stock images

**How to fix:**
- Use free tools like [tinypng.com](https://tinypng.com/) to compress images below 2 MB
- Resize to at least 800 x 800 pixels for best display
- Use a white bedsheet or poster board as background for product photography
- Save as JPG at 80% quality for optimal file size

## Fix 2: Specification Mismatch

Each GeM category has a predefined specification template:

1. **Fill all mandatory fields** — Fields marked with * are required; leaving them blank causes rejection
2. **Match exact specifications** — Enter specifications exactly as per your product; don't exaggerate or generalize
3. **Unit of measurement** — Use the exact unit the template asks for (mm, cm, kg, litres); don't enter "5 centimeters" in a "mm" field
4. **Dropdown values only** — Some fields have fixed dropdown options; you must select from available choices
5. **Model number** — Enter the exact manufacturer model number, not your internal SKU
6. **BIS/ISI certification** — If the category requires BIS certification, you must have it and enter the license number
7. **Country of origin** — Mandatory field; select accurately (Made in India products get preference)

**Common mistakes:**
- Entering "NA" or "N/A" in numeric fields — enter 0 if not applicable
- Mixing up "Brand Name" and "Model Name" fields
- Entering dimensions in wrong order (L x W x H)

## Fix 3: Wrong Category Selected

Selecting the wrong product category causes specification template mismatch:

1. **Search before browsing** — Use the category search box and enter your product name or type
2. **Check category hierarchy** — GeM categories have multiple levels; drill down to the most specific sub-category
3. **Similar but different categories** — "Office Furniture" and "Educational Furniture" are different categories with different specs
4. **Product vs Service** — Don't list products under service categories or vice versa
5. **Category mapping guide** — Check GeM's product category list for the exact match

**How to fix wrong category:**
- You cannot change the category of an existing listing
- Delete the rejected listing and create a new one with the correct category
- Search for similar products on GeM to see which category other sellers use

## Fix 4: Brand Approval Pending

Many product categories require brand authorization:

1. **Brand registration** — If you are the brand owner, register your brand on GeM through the seller dashboard
2. **Brand authorization document** — Upload the brand registration certificate (trademark registration)
3. **Unregistered brands** — If your brand is not trademarked, GeM may allow listing under "Unbranded" or "Generic" categories
4. **Brand spelling** — Ensure the brand name matches exactly with GeM's database (including capitalization and spacing)
5. **Brand approval timeline** — Brand registration review takes 5-7 working days on GeM

**For resellers:**
- You need the brand owner's authorization letter to list their products
- The brand owner must be registered on GeM and approve you through the OEM panel

## Fix 5: OEM Panel Authorization

If you're a reseller, Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) authorization is mandatory for many categories:

1. **What is OEM panel?** — The manufacturer (OEM) must add you as an authorized reseller on their GeM OEM panel
2. **Contact the manufacturer** — Ask your supplier/manufacturer to log into their GeM seller account and add you to their OEM panel
3. **OEM not on GeM** — If the manufacturer isn't registered on GeM, they must register first before they can authorize resellers
4. **Authorization per category** — OEM authorization is category-specific; you need separate authorization for each product category
5. **Authorization expiry** — OEM authorizations have validity periods; check if yours has expired

**Steps for OEM to authorize a reseller:**
1. OEM logs into GeM seller dashboard
2. Goes to **OEM Panel** → **Add Reseller**
3. Enters the reseller's GeM seller ID
4. Selects the product categories for authorization
5. Approves the reseller

**Steps for reseller after OEM authorization:**
1. Log into your GeM seller dashboard
2. Check **OEM Authorizations** section — you should see the approved categories
3. Now upload your catalogue under the authorized categories

## Fix 6: Catalogue Rejected After Review

If your catalogue passes initial upload but is rejected during review:

1. **Check rejection reason** — Go to Dashboard → My Catalogues → Rejected → View reason
2. **Common rejection reasons:**
   - Product image doesn't match specifications
   - Misleading product title or description
   - Price significantly below market rate (suspected fake listing)
   - Missing mandatory certifications (BIS, FSSAI for food items)
   - Duplicate of existing listing
3. **Fix and resubmit** — Correct the identified issues and upload again
4. **Appeal** — If you believe the rejection is incorrect, raise a ticket with supporting documents

## Catalogue Upload Step-by-Step

1. Log into [gem.gov.in](https://gem.gov.in/) → Seller Dashboard
2. Click **"Add Catalogue"** or **"Upload Product"**
3. **Select category** — Search and select the correct product category
4. **Fill specifications** — Complete all mandatory fields in the specification template
5. **Upload images** — Add 2-3 clear product images (JPG/PNG, under 2 MB each)
6. **Set pricing** — Enter your offered price (inclusive or exclusive of GST as specified)
7. **Add description** — Write a clear, accurate product description
8. **Review** — Preview your listing and verify all details
9. **Submit** — Your catalogue goes for review (1-3 working days)
10. **Check status** — Monitor approval status in "My Catalogues"

## Important Tips

1. **Study existing listings** — Search for similar products on GeM to understand the correct category, specs format, and pricing
2. **Keep certificates ready** — BIS, ISO, FSSAI, and other certifications in PDF format before starting upload
3. **Competitive pricing** — GeM shows price comparisons; price your products competitively but realistically
4. **Bulk upload** — For large catalogues, use GeM's bulk upload template (Excel format) instead of adding one product at a time
5. **Update regularly** — Keep your catalogue updated with current prices and stock availability

## FAQs

### How long does GeM catalogue approval take?
Catalogue review typically takes 1-3 working days. Complex products requiring certification verification may take up to 5 working days.

### Can I list products without GST registration?
No, GST registration is mandatory for all GeM sellers. Your GSTIN must be active and linked to your GeM seller profile.

### How many products can I list on GeM?
There is no limit on the number of products you can list. You can upload catalogues across multiple categories that you're registered for.

### Can I change the price after listing?
Yes, you can update your catalogue price at any time from the seller dashboard. Go to My Catalogues → Edit → Update Price.

### What if my product category doesn't exist on GeM?
You can suggest a new category through the GeM portal. Go to Help → Suggest New Category and provide product details. GeM periodically adds new categories based on demand.

### Do I need separate catalogue entries for different sizes/variants?
Yes, each variant (size, color, configuration) typically needs a separate catalogue entry with its own specifications and pricing.

### Why was my catalogue marked as duplicate?
GeM flags listings that have identical specifications to your existing active catalogues. Each listing must be for a distinct product variant.
