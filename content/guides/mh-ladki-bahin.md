# Mukhyamantri Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana – Maharashtra

Mukhyamantri Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana is a flagship welfare scheme of the Government of Maharashtra providing ₹1,500 per month to eligible women. The scheme aims to empower women financially and improve their economic independence across the state.

## What is Ladki Bahin Yojana?

Launched in 2024, the Ladki Bahin Yojana provides direct monthly financial assistance of ₹1,500 to women aged 21 to 65 years from economically weaker families. The amount is transferred directly to the woman's bank account via DBT. The scheme covers women across all communities and religions.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Gender:** Women only
2. **Age:** 21 to 65 years
3. **Residency:** Must be a domicile of Maharashtra
4. **Income:** Annual family income should not exceed ₹2,50,000
5. **Marital status:** Married, widowed, divorced, deserted, and single women are all eligible
6. **Aadhaar and bank account:** Must have Aadhaar-linked bank account
7. **Exclusions:**
   - Women already receiving ₹1,500 or more from other government schemes
   - Government employees (state/central)
   - Income tax payers
   - Women whose family members are income tax payers
   - Women owning 4-wheelers (except those used for livelihood)

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar card (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- Domicile certificate of Maharashtra
- Age proof (birth certificate / school leaving certificate)
- Income certificate (family income below ₹2,50,000)
- Ration card (yellow/orange)
- Bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked, in the woman's name)
- Passport-size photograph
- Mobile number linked to Aadhaar
- Voter ID (optional but helpful)

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Online Application

1. **Visit the official portal:** Go to the Ladki Bahin Yojana portal (accessible through [womenchild.maharashtra.gov.in](https://womenchild.maharashtra.gov.in/))
2. **Register:** Create an account with Aadhaar and mobile number
3. **Fill application:** Enter personal details, age, income, marital status, and bank account information
4. **Upload documents:** Upload scanned copies of Aadhaar, income certificate, domicile certificate, etc.
5. **Submit:** Review and submit the application
6. **Track status:** Note the application ID and track status on the portal

### Offline Application (Through Gram Panchayat / Nagar Palika)

1. **Visit Gram Panchayat / Municipal office:** Go to your local Gram Panchayat (rural) or Nagar Palika / Municipal Corporation (urban)
2. **Collect form:** Get the Ladki Bahin Yojana application form
3. **Fill the form:** Complete all fields with accurate information
4. **Attach documents:** Self-attested copies of all required documents
5. **Submit:** Submit the form at the office
6. **Acknowledgment:** Receive acknowledgment with reference number
7. **Verification:** Field officers verify income, address, and eligibility
8. **Approval:** Application approved at taluka/district level
9. **Monthly DBT:** ₹1,500 credited to bank account monthly

### Through Anganwadi Worker

- In some areas, Anganwadi workers help women fill and submit applications
- Contact your local Anganwadi center for assistance

## Benefit Details

| Detail | Information |
|--------|------------|
| Monthly amount | ₹1,500 |
| Annual total | ₹18,000 |
| Payment mode | Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) |
| Payment to | Woman's Aadhaar-linked bank account |
| Frequency | Monthly |

## Processing Time

- **Application to verification:** 15–30 days
- **Approval:** 30–60 days from submission
- **First payment:** Within 1–2 months of approval

## Important Tips

1. **Aadhaar-bank link is mandatory:** Without this, payment cannot be processed
2. **Keep mobile number updated:** OTPs and updates are sent to the registered mobile
3. **Income certificate must be current:** Get a fresh income certificate if yours is older than 6 months
4. **No need for middlemen:** Apply directly — the process is free
5. **Women from all communities eligible:** There is no caste or religion restriction — all women meeting income criteria qualify

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: How much money will I get under Ladki Bahin Yojana?
₹1,500 per month (₹18,000 per year), credited to your bank account.

### Q2: Is there a caste or religion restriction?
No. Women from all castes, communities, and religions are eligible, provided they meet income and age criteria.

### Q3: Can unmarried women apply?
Yes, all women aged 21–65 are eligible regardless of marital status — unmarried, married, widowed, divorced, or deserted.

### Q4: Can I get Ladki Bahin and another pension simultaneously?
If you're already receiving ₹1,500 or more from another government scheme, you may not be eligible. If receiving less than ₹1,500, you may get the difference.

### Q5: Is there an online portal to check application status?
Yes, check status on the official Ladki Bahin portal or through the Maharashtra government's benefit tracking system.

### Q6: When was this scheme launched?
The scheme was launched in 2024 by the Government of Maharashtra.

### Q7: What if my application is rejected?
Check the rejection reason on the portal. Correct any issues with documents or information and reapply.
