## What is NREGA / MGNREGA?

The **Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA)** is a landmark social security scheme that guarantees **100 days of wage employment per year** to every rural household in India. Enacted in 2005, it is the world's largest public works programme.

Key features:
- **100 days guaranteed work** per household per financial year
- **Minimum wages:** ₹267-₹333 per day (varies by state, as of 2025-26)
- **Work within 5 km** of the applicant's village
- **Unemployment allowance** if work is not provided within 15 days of application
- **Women must get at least 33%** of total employment
- **Wages paid directly** to bank/post office account within 15 days

> MGNREGA covers **all rural districts** across India. As of 2025-26, the scheme has issued over 16 crore job cards.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Adult members** (18+ years) of any rural household
- **Must be willing** to do unskilled manual work
- **Residence:** Must reside in the rural area of the Gram Panchayat where applying
- **No income limit** — any rural household can apply
- **No educational qualification** required

> Every eligible household gets **one job card** listing all adult members who want work. Each member can individually demand employment.

## Documents Required

- **Application form** (available at Gram Panchayat office or online)
- **Passport-size photographs** of all adult household members seeking work
- **Proof of residence** — Aadhaar card, voter ID, or ration card
- **Aadhaar card** (for wage payment linkage)
- **Bank/post office account details** (for direct wage transfer)

## How to Apply for NREGA Job Card

### Online Application

1. **Visit** [nrega.nic.in](https://nrega.nic.in) or your state's NREGA portal
2. **Select your state** from the homepage
3. **Click** on "Data Entry" → "Registration of Household"
4. **Fill in household details** — head of household name, address, village, Gram Panchayat
5. **Add adult members** who want to work under NREGA (with photographs)
6. **Enter Aadhaar and bank details** for each member
7. **Submit the application** and note the registration number
8. **Gram Panchayat verification** — officials will verify your details within 15 days
9. **Job card issued** — collect from the Gram Panchayat office or download online

### Offline Application (Recommended for Most)

1. **Visit your Gram Panchayat office**
2. **Collect the application form** (Form-1) for job card registration
3. **Fill in details** of all adult household members seeking work
4. **Attach photographs** of each applicant
5. **Submit the form** to the Gram Panchayat Secretary/Rozgar Sahayak
6. **Get an acknowledgment receipt** with the date of submission
7. **Job card issued within 15 days** of verification
8. **Job card is free** — no fee can be charged

### How to Demand Work

Once you have a job card:

1. **Submit a written application** to the Gram Panchayat demanding work
2. **Specify the number of days** you want work (minimum 14 days at a time)
3. **Get a dated receipt** of your application
4. **Work must be provided within 15 days** of application
5. If work is not provided within 15 days, you are entitled to **unemployment allowance**

## How to Check NREGA Job Card Status Online

1. **Visit** [nrega.nic.in](https://nrega.nic.in)
2. **Select your state** → District → Block → Panchayat
3. **Click** "Job Card / Employment Register"
4. **Find your name** in the list or search by job card number
5. **View details** — employment history, days worked, wages received

### Check Payment Status

1. Go to [nrega.nic.in](https://nrega.nic.in) → Select your state
2. Click on **"R1. Job Card/Employment Register"**
3. Select District → Block → Panchayat
4. Click on your **job card number**
5. View **work allocation, muster roll, and payment details**

### Check Muster Roll

1. Visit [nrega.nic.in](https://nrega.nic.in) → Select state
2. Navigate to **Reports** → **R6. Muster Roll**
3. Select the work/project name
4. View **attendance, days worked, and wage calculation**

## Important Rights Under NREGA

- **Right to work within 15 days** of demand
- **Right to unemployment allowance** if work not provided
- **Right to work within 5 km** of village (travel allowance if beyond)
- **Right to worksite facilities** — drinking water, shade, crèche, first aid
- **Right to wages within 15 days** of work completion
- **Right to compensation** for delayed wages (0.05% per day)
- **Right to social audit** — gram sabha can audit NREGA works

## Important Tips

1. **Always get a dated receipt** when you apply for work — this starts the 15-day clock for unemployment allowance
2. **Ensure your Aadhaar is linked** to your bank account for timely wage payments
3. **Check muster rolls regularly** on nrega.nic.in to verify your attendance and wages are recorded correctly
4. **Report irregularities** to the District Programme Coordinator or call the helpline
5. **Job card belongs to you** — never hand it over to anyone permanently; the Gram Panchayat cannot keep it

## FAQs

### How many days of work does NREGA guarantee?
NREGA guarantees 100 days of unskilled manual work per household per financial year (April-March). Some states provide additional days from their own funds.

### What is the current NREGA wage rate?
Wages vary by state, ranging from ₹237 to ₹333 per day (2025-26 rates). Check your state's rate on nrega.nic.in. Wages are revised annually by the central government.

### Can I apply for NREGA job card online?
While some states allow online registration, the recommended method is to apply at your Gram Panchayat office. The job card must be issued within 15 days of a valid application.

### What happens if work is not provided within 15 days?
If the Gram Panchayat fails to provide work within 15 days of your written demand, you are entitled to unemployment allowance. The rate is one-fourth of the wage rate for the first 30 days and half the wage rate thereafter.

### Can women work under NREGA?
Yes. In fact, NREGA mandates that at least 33% of beneficiaries must be women. Women receive equal wages as men. Worksites must have crèche facilities if 5+ children under 6 are present.

### How are NREGA wages paid?
Wages are paid directly to the worker's bank or post office account through electronic fund transfer. Cash payments are not permitted except in areas with no banking access. Wages must be paid within 15 days of work completion.

### Can I check my NREGA payment status online?
Yes. Visit nrega.nic.in, select your state, navigate to Job Card/Employment Register, and click on your job card number to view all payment details including FTO (Fund Transfer Order) status.
