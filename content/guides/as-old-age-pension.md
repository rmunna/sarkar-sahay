# Assam Old Age Pension – Complete Guide 2026

## What is the Assam Old Age Pension?

The **Assam Old Age Pension** is a social security scheme providing monthly financial assistance to senior citizens from economically weaker sections. The scheme operates under the **National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP)** — specifically the **Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS)** — with additional top-up from the Assam state government.

Senior citizens who are below the poverty line (BPL) or meet income criteria receive a monthly pension to support their basic living expenses.

## Pension Amount

| Age Group | Central Share | State Share | Total Monthly Pension |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|----------------------|
| 60-79 years | ₹200 | ₹200-400 | ₹400-600 |
| 80 years and above | ₹500 | ₹200-400 | ₹700-900 |

> **Note:** The exact state top-up amount may vary. Check with the District Social Welfare Office for current rates.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Age:** 60 years or above
- **Residency:** Permanent resident of Assam
- **Income:** Belongs to BPL category or annual family income below ₹1 lakh
- **Not receiving** any other government pension
- Must not be a government retiree drawing pension

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar Card
- Age proof (birth certificate / school certificate / voter ID)
- BPL card or income certificate from Circle Officer
- Bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked)
- Passport-size photographs (2)
- Residential proof (voter ID / ration card)
- Self-declaration form

## How to Apply – Step-by-Step

### Offline Application

1. **Visit the nearest Gaon Panchayat office** (rural) or **Municipal Office** (urban)
2. Collect the **Old Age Pension application form**
3. Fill in personal details, age, income, and bank information
4. Attach self-attested copies of all required documents
5. Submit the form to the **Gaon Panchayat Secretary** or **Municipal Ward Councillor**
6. The application is verified and forwarded to the **Block Development Officer (BDO)**
7. After approval at the block/district level, the pension is sanctioned
8. Pension is credited monthly to the beneficiary's bank account via DBT

### Online Application

1. Visit [nsap.nic.in](https://nsap.nic.in) for the central portal
2. Some districts may also accept applications through state portals
3. Contact the District Social Welfare Officer for online submission guidance

## Processing Time

- **Verification:** 2-4 weeks
- **Approval:** 1-3 months
- **First pension credit:** 1-2 months after approval

## Important Tips

1. **Apply through your Gaon Panchayat** for the fastest processing
2. **Ensure accurate age proof** — the pension amount depends on your age bracket
3. **Link Aadhaar to your bank account** for uninterrupted pension payments
4. **Renew annually** — some districts require annual life verification
5. **Report changes** in address or bank account promptly to the BDO office

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: What is the pension amount for those above 80?
**A:** Senior citizens aged **80 years and above** receive a higher pension (₹500 from the Centre plus state top-up).

### Q2: Can I apply online?
**A:** The primary method is **offline through Gaon Panchayat/Municipal Office**. Some districts accept online applications via [nsap.nic.in](https://nsap.nic.in).

### Q3: What if I don't have a BPL card?
**A:** You can submit an **income certificate** from the Circle Officer as an alternative to the BPL card.

### Q4: Is annual renewal required?
**A:** Yes. **Life verification** is required annually or as directed by the district office to continue receiving the pension.

### Q5: Can I receive old age pension along with Orunodoi?
**A:** Generally, **both cannot be availed simultaneously**. Check with the District Social Welfare Office for the latest policy. See our [Orunodoi guide](/guide/as-orunodoi) for details.

### Q6: How do I report non-receipt of pension?
**A:** Contact the **Block Development Officer (BDO)** or **District Social Welfare Officer** with your beneficiary ID.
