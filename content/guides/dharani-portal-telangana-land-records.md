## What is the Dharani Portal?

Dharani (dharani.telangana.gov.in) is Telangana's integrated land records and registration portal launched by the Chief Commissioner of Land Administration (CCLA). It digitises all land-related services — ownership verification, Pattadar passbook issuance, property registration, mutation/transfer, encumbrance certificates, and market value search — into a single online platform.

Before Dharani, citizens had to visit multiple offices (MRO, Tahsildar, SRO) for basic land transactions. Now most services are available online or through slot-booked appointments at Sub-Registrar Offices (SROs).

### Key Services on Dharani

- **Land records search** — by survey number, Pattadar name, or Aadhaar
- **Pattadar Passbook** download and verification
- **Property registration** — sale deeds, gift deeds, mortgage, partition
- **Mutation / Transfer** of land ownership
- **Encumbrance Certificate (EC)** — check property transaction history
- **Prohibited Properties List** — verify if land is under litigation or government-owned
- **Market Value Search** — check circle rates / government valuation
- **Slot Booking** — book appointment at SRO for registration

## How to Check Land Records on Dharani

### Search by Survey Number

1. Go to [dharani.telangana.gov.in](https://dharani.telangana.gov.in)
2. Click **"Know Your Land Status"** or **"Search Land Records"**
3. Select **District → Mandal → Village**
4. Enter the **Survey Number** (and sub-division if applicable)
5. Click **Search** — the portal displays ownership details, extent, land type, and Pattadar information

### Search by Pattadar Name

1. On the Dharani homepage, choose **"Search by Pattadar"**
2. Select District, Mandal, and Village
3. Enter the **Pattadar name** (in English or Telugu)
4. The portal lists all land parcels registered under that name

### Search by Aadhaar Number

If the Pattadar's Aadhaar is linked to land records, you can search using the Aadhaar number directly. This is the most reliable method as names may have spelling variations.

> **Tip:** If your land doesn't appear, it may not yet be digitised or there could be a data-entry mismatch. Visit the Tahsildar office with original documents for correction.

## Pattadar Passbook — Download & Verification

The **Pattadar Passbook (పట్టాదారు పాస్‌బుక్)** is the primary document proving agricultural land ownership in Telangana. Dharani allows you to view and download it online.

### How to Download Pattadar Passbook

1. Visit [dharani.telangana.gov.in](https://dharani.telangana.gov.in)
2. Navigate to **"Pattadar Passbook"** section
3. Enter **District, Mandal, Village, and Survey Number**
4. The passbook details appear — including owner name, extent (acres/guntas), land classification (wet/dry), and survey boundaries
5. Click **Download / Print** for your records

### What the Passbook Contains

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Pattadar Name | Legal owner of the land |
| Survey Number | Unique identification of the land parcel |
| Extent | Area in acres and guntas |
| Classification | Wet (irrigated), Dry, or Converted |
| Khata Number | Revenue account number |
| Enjoyment Type | Self, Joint, or Government |

> **Note:** The downloaded passbook is for reference. For legal purposes (bank loans, land sale), obtain a certified copy from the Tahsildar's office.

## How to Apply for Mutation / Transfer of Land

Mutation (మ్యుటేషన్) updates the land records when ownership changes — through sale, inheritance, gift, or partition. On Dharani, mutation happens **automatically** after property registration at the SRO.

### Automatic Mutation (Post-Registration)

For transactions registered through Dharani (sale deed, gift deed, etc.), mutation is processed automatically. The new owner's name reflects in land records within **1–3 working days** after registration.

### Manual Mutation (Inheritance / Succession)

For inherited property where the original owner is deceased:

1. Log in to Dharani portal with your credentials
2. Go to **"Succession / Mutation"** section
3. Upload required documents:
   - Death certificate of the deceased Pattadar
   - Legal heir certificate / Succession certificate
   - Aadhaar of all legal heirs
   - Copy of the existing Pattadar passbook
4. Select the land parcel (survey number) for mutation
5. Submit the application — you'll receive an acknowledgement number
6. The Tahsildar verifies and processes the mutation (typically **15–30 days**)
7. Track status online using the acknowledgement number

### Documents Required for Mutation

- Death certificate (mandatory for succession)
- Legal heir certificate from Tahsildar / Succession certificate from court
- Aadhaar card of applicant(s)
- Existing Pattadar passbook or land record printout
- Sale deed / Gift deed / Partition deed (if applicable)
- Court order (if disputed property)

## Encumbrance Certificate (EC) — How to Get It

An Encumbrance Certificate (EC / భారం ధృవీకరణ పత్రం) shows all registered transactions on a property for a given period. Banks require it before sanctioning home loans.

### How to Apply for EC Online

1. Visit [registration.telangana.gov.in](https://registration.telangana.gov.in) or the Dharani portal
2. Click on **"Encumbrance Certificate"** or **"EC Search"**
3. Enter:
   - **District and SRO** jurisdiction
   - **Document number** or **property details** (survey number / house number)
   - **Period** — start date and end date for the search
4. Pay the fee online (₹200 for 1–10 years, additional charges for longer periods)
5. The EC is generated and available for download

### Understanding Your EC

- **Nil Encumbrance** — No transactions recorded in the period; property is free of registered liabilities
- **Encumbrance entries** — Lists each transaction (sale, mortgage, release, court attachment) with document details and dates

> **Important:** EC only covers *registered* transactions. Unregistered agreements, oral sales, or pending litigation won't appear.

## Prohibited Properties List

Before buying any property, check if it's on the **Prohibited Properties List (నిషేధిత ఆస్తుల జాబితా)**. These are properties where registration is blocked due to:

- Government land (poramboke, assigned land)
- Endowment / Wakf property
- Court-stayed or litigated property
- Ceiling surplus land
- Forest land

### How to Check

1. On Dharani portal, go to **"Prohibited Properties"**
2. Select District, Mandal, and Village
3. Enter the Survey Number
4. The system shows whether the property is prohibited or free for registration

> **Warning:** Never purchase property without checking this list. Transactions on prohibited properties are void and non-recoverable.

## Slot Booking for SRO (Property Registration)

All property registrations in Telangana require a **slot booking** at the concerned Sub-Registrar Office (SRO).

### How to Book a Slot

1. Log in to [dharani.telangana.gov.in](https://dharani.telangana.gov.in) with registered mobile number
2. Navigate to **"Slot Booking"**
3. Select the **SRO** based on property location
4. Choose the **type of transaction** (sale, gift, mortgage, partition, etc.)
5. Enter property details — survey number or municipal property details
6. Enter buyer and seller details along with Aadhaar numbers
7. Select an available **date and time slot**
8. Upload scanned copies of required documents
9. Pay the **registration fee and stamp duty** online
10. Download the slot confirmation — carry it to the SRO on the appointed date

### What to Carry to SRO

- Slot booking confirmation printout
- Original Aadhaar cards of buyer, seller, and 2 witnesses
- Existing title deed / sale deed of the property
- Pattadar passbook (for agricultural land)
- Passport-size photographs
- PAN card (if transaction value exceeds ₹10 lakh)

## Property Registration Process

### Step-by-Step

1. **Slot Booking** — Book online as described above and pay stamp duty + registration fee
2. **Visit SRO** — Buyer, seller, and 2 witnesses must appear at the SRO on the booked date
3. **Biometric Verification** — Aadhaar-based fingerprint/iris authentication of all parties
4. **Document Verification** — SRO staff verifies the deed and uploaded documents
5. **Signing** — All parties sign the deed digitally or physically
6. **Registration Complete** — Document number is generated; registered deed is available for download on Dharani within 24 hours
7. **Auto-Mutation** — Land records update automatically in 1–3 days

### Stamp Duty & Registration Fee in Telangana

| Transaction Type | Stamp Duty | Registration Fee |
|-----------------|------------|-----------------|
| Sale Deed | 4% of market value | 0.5% |
| Gift Deed (non-family) | 4% | 0.5% |
| Gift Deed (family) | 1% | 0.5% |
| Mortgage | 0.5% | 0.1% |
| Partition Deed | Varies | 0.5% |

> **Note:** Rates are indicative. Check the latest Government Order (GO) for current rates. Additional surcharges (transfer duty, cess) may apply.

## Market Value Search

The government fixes **market value (సర్కిల్ రేటు)** for every area, which is the minimum value for stamp duty calculation.

### How to Check Market Value

1. On Dharani portal, go to **"Market Value"** or **"Know Your Market Value"**
2. Select **District → Mandal → Village** (for rural) or **District → ULB → Locality** (for urban)
3. Choose the **land classification** (agricultural, residential, commercial)
4. The portal displays the **per-unit market value** (per sq. yard or per acre)

This value determines the **minimum stamp duty** payable. If your actual transaction value is higher, stamp duty is calculated on the higher amount.

## Common Issues & Solutions

### "My land is not showing on Dharani"
- Check for spelling variations in Pattadar name (English/Telugu)
- Try searching by survey number instead of name
- Land may not be digitised yet — visit the Tahsildar office with original documents
- If the land was part of a recent subdivision, updated records may be pending

### "Pattadar passbook shows wrong details"
- Apply for correction at the Tahsildar office with supporting documents
- For name corrections, carry Aadhaar, old passbook, and an affidavit
- Corrections typically take 15–30 days after verification

### "Slot booking is not available / all slots taken"
- Slots are released a few days in advance — check early morning (after midnight)
- High-demand SROs fill quickly; try alternate dates
- Ensure your Aadhaar biometric is active (not locked)

### "Registration rejected at SRO"
- Common reasons: mismatch in Aadhaar details, property in prohibited list, pending revenue dues
- Verify all party details match Aadhaar exactly before booking
- Clear any pending property tax or revenue dues beforehand

### "EC not showing all transactions"
- Ensure you've selected the correct SRO jurisdiction
- Extend the date range — old transactions may predate digitisation
- For very old records, request a manual search at the SRO

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q: What is the Dharani portal and what can I do on it?
Dharani is Telangana's integrated land records and registration portal managed by the CCLA. You can search land records, download Pattadar passbooks, register properties, apply for mutation, get encumbrance certificates, check prohibited properties, and book SRO slots — all in one place.

### Q: How do I check if a property is prohibited on Dharani?
Go to dharani.telangana.gov.in and click "Prohibited Properties." Select District, Mandal, and Village, then enter the survey number. The system will show whether the property is blocked from registration due to government ownership, litigation, or other reasons.

### Q: Does mutation happen automatically after property registration in Telangana?
Yes, for transactions registered through Dharani (sale deed, gift deed, etc.), mutation is processed automatically. The new owner's name reflects in land records within 1–3 working days after registration.

### Q: How do I book a slot at the Sub-Registrar Office for property registration?
Log in to dharani.telangana.gov.in, go to "Slot Booking," select your SRO, choose the transaction type, enter property and party details, upload documents, pay stamp duty and registration fee online, and select an available date and time slot.

### Q: What is the stamp duty for property registration in Telangana?
Stamp duty for a sale deed is 4% of the market value, with a registration fee of 0.5%. Gift deeds within the family attract 1% stamp duty. Rates may change, so check the latest Government Order for current rates.

### Q: How can I get an Encumbrance Certificate (EC) online in Telangana?
Visit registration.telangana.gov.in or the Dharani portal and click "Encumbrance Certificate." Enter the district, SRO jurisdiction, property details, and time period. Pay the fee (₹200 for 1–10 years) online and download the EC.

### Q: What should I do if my land is not showing on Dharani?
Try searching by survey number instead of name, and check for spelling variations in both English and Telugu. Your land may not be digitised yet — visit the Tahsildar office with original documents for correction or data entry.
