# Ration Card Mobile Number Update: How to Change Online & Offline

Your mobile number linked to the ration card is used for OTP verification at Fair Price Shops (FPS), receiving SMS alerts about grain allocation, and logging into state food portals. If you have changed your phone number or never registered one, you must update it to continue receiving benefits smoothly. This guide explains how to update your mobile number through online portals, eKYC, and offline methods.

## Why Is Mobile Number Important on Ration Card?

- **OTP-based authentication** at e-POS enabled Fair Price Shops
- **SMS alerts** for grain availability and distribution dates
- **Online portal login** for checking entitlement, downloading e-ration card
- **Aadhaar-ration card linking** verification
- **One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC)** portability requires active mobile number

## Methods to Update Mobile Number

There are three main ways to update your ration card mobile number:

### Method 1: Online via State Portal

This is the fastest method, available in most states:

1. **Visit your state's food & civil supplies portal** (see portal list below)
2. **Login** using your ration card number and existing registered mobile (or Aadhaar number)
3. Navigate to **"Update Mobile Number"** or **"Edit Profile"** section
4. Enter your **new mobile number**
5. An **OTP will be sent** to your new number — enter it to verify
6. **Upload Aadhaar card** copy if prompted (for identity verification)
7. **Submit** the request
8. The new number is usually updated within **24-72 hours**
9. You will receive a confirmation SMS on the new number

> **Note:** If you don't have access to the old registered mobile, you may need to visit the Tehsil/FPS for offline update.

### Method 2: At Fair Price Shop (FPS) via eKYC

Many states allow mobile number updates through the e-POS device at your designated FPS:

1. **Visit your assigned FPS** during working hours
2. Carry your **ration card and Aadhaar card** (original)
3. Request the FPS dealer to **update your mobile number**
4. The dealer will authenticate you via **biometric (fingerprint) on the e-POS machine**
5. Enter your **new mobile number** when prompted
6. The number is updated in the system, usually **within 24 hours**

### Method 3: At Tehsil/Food & Supply Office (Offline)

1. **Visit the Tehsil office** or Food & Civil Supplies office in your area
2. **Fill the mobile number update form** (available at the counter)
3. **Attach photocopies** of:
   - Ration card
   - [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) of the head of family
   - New SIM card proof (if required by state)
4. **Submit the form** and collect the acknowledgment receipt
5. The update is processed within **7-15 days**

## Fees

Mobile number update is **completely free** across all states and methods. No charges apply.

## Important Tips

1. **Use the head of family's mobile number** — the registered number should belong to the ration card head (eldest woman of household under NFSA)
2. **Keep your Aadhaar mobile updated too** — if your Aadhaar has an old number, update it at an [Aadhaar centre](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) as well for seamless eKYC
3. **One mobile number per ration card** — you cannot link multiple ration cards to the same mobile number in most states
4. **Check after update** — visit the state portal or FPS to verify the new number is reflected in the system
5. **Don't share OTPs** — government officials will never call you asking for OTPs. Report such calls as fraud

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Can I update my ration card mobile number online?
Yes, most states allow mobile number updates through their food & civil supplies portal. Login with your ration card number and follow the update process.

### Q2. What if I don't have access to my old registered mobile number?
Visit your nearest FPS for biometric (eKYC) update, or go to the Tehsil/Food & Supply office with your Aadhaar card and ration card for offline update.

### Q3. Is there any charge for updating the mobile number?
No. Mobile number update on ration card is completely free across all states.

### Q4. Can I link someone else's mobile number to my ration card?
The mobile number should ideally belong to the head of household. However, in practice, any active number accessible to the family can be linked.

### Q5. How do I know if my mobile number is updated?
Login to your state's food portal and check your ration card details. You can also verify at the FPS during your next ration collection.

### Q6. Will changing my mobile number affect my ration entitlement?
No. Changing your mobile number does not affect your ration card category, entitlement, or family details. Only the contact number is updated.

### Q7. Can I update the mobile number at any FPS?
No. You must visit your **assigned/designated FPS** where your ration card is registered. The FPS dealer can only update details for cards assigned to their shop.
