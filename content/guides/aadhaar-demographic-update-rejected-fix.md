# Aadhaar Name/DOB Update Rejected? How to Fix Demographic Update Request

Getting your Aadhaar demographic update rejected is frustrating — especially when you need corrected details for bank KYC, passport, or government schemes. This guide covers the exact error messages you'll see, why UIDAI rejects updates, and how to fix and resubmit successfully.

## Common Error Messages You'll See

When your Aadhaar demographic update is rejected, UIDAI typically shows one of these messages:

- **"Your request has been rejected. Please visit the nearest Aadhaar Enrolment Centre."**
- **"Update request rejected due to insufficient/invalid supporting document."**
- **"Demographic update failed. Document not as per UIDAI guidelines."**
- **"Request rejected: Name change not supported through online mode."**
- **"Your update request (URN: XXXX) has been rejected by the adjudicator."**
- **"Biometric mismatch – update cannot be processed."**

You can check your update request status at [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in) using your URN (Update Request Number).

## Why Does UIDAI Reject Aadhaar Updates?

### 1. Invalid or Unclear Supporting Documents

The most common reason. UIDAI has a strict [list of valid documents](https://uidai.gov.in/en-ecosystem/authentication-devices-documents/documents.html). Your document must:

- Be clearly legible (no blurry scans)
- Show the **exact** updated name/DOB you requested
- Be from the approved document list
- Not be expired

### 2. Name Change Exceeds Online Limits

UIDAI's online portal (SSUP) allows name updates only **once** and only for minor corrections. Major name changes (e.g., after marriage, legal name change) must be done at an **Aadhaar Enrolment Centre** with original documents.

### 3. Date of Birth Change Restrictions

DOB can be updated only **once** in a lifetime on Aadhaar. If you've already changed it before, further requests will be rejected. Additionally, changing DOB by more than 3 years requires visiting an enrolment centre.

### 4. Biometric Mismatch

If your fingerprints or iris don't match during operator-assisted updates, the request gets rejected automatically.

### 5. Document-Data Mismatch

The name/DOB on your supporting document must **exactly match** what you're requesting. Even minor spelling differences cause rejection.

## Step-by-Step Fix: How to Resubmit Successfully

### Step 1: Check Your Rejection Reason

1. Visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in)
2. Log in with your Aadhaar number and OTP
3. Go to **"Check Aadhaar Update Status"**
4. Enter your **URN** (sent via SMS when you submitted the update)
5. Note the exact rejection reason displayed

### Step 2: Prepare the Correct Documents

Based on the rejection reason, gather the right documents:

**For Name Update:**
- Passport (strongest document for name proof)
- PAN Card
- Voter ID (EPIC)
- Driving License
- Marriage Certificate (for name change after marriage)
- Gazette Notification (for legal name change)

**For Date of Birth Update:**
- Birth Certificate (most accepted)
- SSLC/10th Marksheet
- Passport
- PAN Card

**For Address Update:**
- Passport
- Bank Statement/Passbook
- Utility Bill (electricity, water, gas — not older than 3 months)
- Ration Card
- Rent Agreement with notarization

### Step 3: Resubmit Online (Minor Corrections)

1. Go to [ssup.uidai.gov.in](https://ssup.uidai.gov.in)
2. Enter Aadhaar number and captcha
3. Click **"Send OTP"** and verify
4. Select the field you want to update
5. Upload a **clear, high-resolution scan** (JPG/PNG, max 2MB)
6. Ensure the document shows the **exact spelling** you're requesting
7. Submit and save your new URN

### Step 4: Visit Aadhaar Enrolment Centre (If Online Rejected Again)

If online resubmission fails:

1. Find your nearest centre at [appointments.uidai.gov.in](https://appointments.uidai.gov.in)
2. Book an appointment (walk-ins accepted but expect long queues)
3. Carry **original documents** plus one photocopy
4. Operator will capture your biometrics and submit the update
5. Fee: ₹50 for demographic update (₹100 with biometric update)
6. Collect your acknowledgement slip with URN

### Step 5: Escalate If Still Rejected

If your update is rejected even after visiting a centre:

1. **Call UIDAI helpline:** 1947
2. **Email:** help@uidai.gov.in with your URN and rejection details
3. **File grievance** at [grievance.uidai.gov.in](https://grievance.uidai.gov.in)
4. **Regional UIDAI office:** Visit in person with all original documents

## Tips to Avoid Rejection

- **Scan documents at 300 DPI** minimum — blurry uploads are the #1 rejection cause
- **Use passport or birth certificate** as proof — they have the highest acceptance rate
- **Match spelling exactly** — if your passport says "RAJA KUMAR" don't request "Rajakumar"
- **Don't attempt multiple changes at once** — update one field at a time
- **Wait 48 hours** after rejection before resubmitting

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does an Aadhaar demographic update take after resubmission?
Online updates typically take **5-10 working days**. Enrolment centre updates may take **10-30 working days** depending on the type of change and verification required.

### Can I update my name on Aadhaar more than once?
You can update your name on Aadhaar a maximum of **2 times**. After that, further name changes require visiting a regional UIDAI office with strong supporting documents like a Gazette Notification.

### Is there a fee for online Aadhaar demographic update?
Online address updates are **free**. However, name, DOB, and gender updates require a visit to an enrolment centre and cost **₹50**. If biometric update is also needed, the fee is **₹100**.

### What if my Aadhaar update is rejected due to biometric mismatch?
Visit the nearest Aadhaar Enrolment Centre and request a **biometric update** first. Once your biometrics are refreshed in the system, resubmit your demographic update request.

### Can I update my date of birth on Aadhaar online?
DOB updates are **not available online** through SSUP. You must visit an Aadhaar Enrolment Centre with your birth certificate or 10th marksheet. DOB can only be changed **once** in a lifetime.

### How do I check if my Aadhaar update was successful?
Visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in), log in with OTP, and check the update status using your URN. You'll also receive an SMS on your registered mobile number once the update is processed.
