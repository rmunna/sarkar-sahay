# Snehapoorvam Scheme Kerala – Stipend for Orphan Children

## What is Snehapoorvam?

Snehapoorvam (With Affection) is a Kerala government scheme providing a **monthly educational stipend** to orphan children and children from families with a single parent (one parent deceased). The scheme ensures that the death of a parent does not prevent a child from continuing education.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Children who have lost one or both parents**
2. Must be a **student** currently enrolled in a recognized school or college
3. **Age:** Must be studying (typically up to undergraduate level)
4. **Residency:** Must be a resident of Kerala
5. **Income:** Guardian/family income must be below **₹1 lakh per annum**

## Stipend Amount

| Education Level | Monthly Stipend |
|----------------|-----------------|
| Class 1 to 5 | ₹300/month |
| Class 6 to 10 | ₹500/month |
| Class 11 to 12 (Plus One/Two) | ₹750/month |
| Degree / Professional Course | ₹1,000/month |

*Note: Amounts may be revised. Check the latest government order.*

## Documents Required

- **Death Certificate** of the deceased parent(s)
- **Birth Certificate** of the child
- **Aadhaar Card** of the child and guardian
- **School/College Bonafide Certificate**
- **Income Certificate** of the guardian (below ₹1 lakh)
- **Ration Card**
- **Bank Account** in child's name (or guardian's for younger children)
- **Passport Size Photo**

## How to Apply

### Online Application

1. **Visit** [welfarepension.lsgkerala.gov.in](https://welfarepension.lsgkerala.gov.in)
2. **Click** on "New Application" → Select "Snehapoorvam"
3. **Enter** child's details — name, age, school/college, class
4. **Enter** guardian's details and income
5. **Upload** death certificate, school bonafide, income certificate
6. **Enter** bank account details
7. **Submit** and note the application number

### Offline Application

1. **Visit** the local **Grama Panchayat, Municipality, or Corporation** office
2. **Collect** the Snehapoorvam application form
3. **Fill** in all details
4. **Attach** copies of required documents
5. **Submit** to the ICDS/Social Welfare officer or Panchayat Secretary
6. **Collect** acknowledgment

## Verification & Disbursement

1. The local body verifies the child's status, school enrollment, and guardian's income
2. Ward-level committee recommends the application
3. Panchayat/Municipality approves
4. Stipend is credited monthly to the bank account

## Processing Time

- **Verification:** 30-60 days
- **Approval:** 1-3 months
- **First Payment:** Within 3 months of approval

## Important Tips

1. **Apply as Soon as Possible:** The stipend is calculated from the date of approval, not retroactively.
2. **Renewal Every Year:** The stipend must be renewed each academic year with an updated bonafide certificate.
3. **Continue Education:** Dropping out of school will stop the stipend.
4. **Bank Account for Child:** Open a bank account in the child's name for direct credit.
5. **Report Grade Changes:** When the child moves to a higher class/level, update the Panchayat to get the revised (higher) stipend amount.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Can children who lost both parents apply?
**Yes.** Children who lost both parents are eligible and should apply through their legal guardian.

### Q2: Is the scheme only for school students?
**No.** It covers students up to the undergraduate level in recognized institutions.

### Q3: Can children in private schools apply?
**Yes**, if the school is recognized by the government.

### Q4: What if the guardian's income crosses ₹1 lakh during the year?
The stipend may be stopped from the next renewal. Income is verified annually.

### Q5: Can a child get both Snehapoorvam and another scholarship?
Generally, only one government benefit at a time is allowed. Check with the local office.

### Q6: Is this scheme available for children in orphanages?
Children in government/recognized orphanages may be eligible through the institution's application.
