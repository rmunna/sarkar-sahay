# IRCTC Ticket Cancellation Refund Not Received — How to Fix

Cancelled your train ticket on IRCTC but still haven't received the refund? This is one of the most common complaints among Indian Railways passengers. Refund delays can happen due to payment gateway processing, TDR requirements, or simply not knowing the correct refund timeline for your payment method.

This guide explains exactly how IRCTC refunds work, the timelines you should expect, how to check your refund status, and what to do if the money never arrives.

## How IRCTC Refunds Work

When you cancel a ticket on IRCTC, the refund process depends on three factors:

1. **How you booked** — online (IRCTC website/app) vs. counter (railway station)
2. **How you paid** — net banking, UPI, debit card, credit card, or IRCTC eWallet
3. **Type of cancellation** — normal cancellation vs. TDR (Ticket Deposit Receipt)

The refund is processed after deducting applicable cancellation charges as per Indian Railways rules.

## Refund Timeline by Payment Method

| Payment Method | Refund Timeline | Where It Appears |
|---|---|---|
| IRCTC eWallet | Instant to 24 hours | IRCTC eWallet balance |
| UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, BHIM) | 5–7 working days | Linked bank account |
| Net Banking | 5–7 working days | Bank account |
| Debit Card | 5–7 working days | Bank account linked to card |
| Credit Card | 7–21 working days | Credit card statement |
| International Cards | 21–45 working days | Card statement |

> **Note:** Working days exclude Sundays and bank holidays. If you cancelled on a Friday evening, counting starts from Monday.

## Normal Cancellation vs. TDR — Key Difference

### Normal Cancellation (Online)

You cancel the ticket yourself through the IRCTC website or app **before chart preparation**. The refund (minus cancellation charges) is processed automatically.

- Go to **My Transactions → Booked Ticket History**
- Select the ticket → Click **Cancel Ticket**
- Refund is initiated immediately

### TDR (Ticket Deposit Receipt)

A TDR must be filed when:

- **Train is cancelled** by Indian Railways
- **Train is late by more than 3 hours** at the boarding station
- **AC not working** or downgraded coach allotted
- **Ticket not cancelled before chart preparation** but you didn't travel
- **Partially confirmed ticket** (some passengers confirmed, others not)
- **Passenger falls ill** and cannot travel (with medical certificate)

With TDR, refund is **not automatic** — Indian Railways reviews the claim and processes it, which takes **60–90 days**.

## Claimable vs. Non-Claimable Cancellations

### Full Refund Claimable (No Cancellation Charges)

- Train cancelled by Railways
- Train delayed by 3+ hours at boarding point
- Boarding station or destination removed from route
- RAC/Waitlisted ticket that didn't get confirmed (auto-refund)

### Partial Refund (After Deducting Cancellation Charges)

- Confirmed ticket cancelled before chart preparation
- Cancellation charges apply based on class and timing:

| Class | Cancellation Charge (Flat) |
|---|---|
| AC First / Executive | ₹240 |
| AC 2 Tier / First Class | ₹200 |
| AC 3 Tier / AC Chair Car | ₹180 |
| Sleeper | ₹120 |
| Second Sitting | ₹60 |

> Additional **25% deduction** applies if you cancel **less than 4 hours but more than 12 hours before departure** (after chart preparation).

### No Refund

- Confirmed ticket cancelled **after chart preparation** without filing TDR
- Ticket cancelled less than 4 hours before departure (for some categories)
- Tatkal tickets cancelled after booking (only partial refund if train is cancelled by Railways)
- No-show with no TDR filed

## How to Check IRCTC Refund Status

### Method 1: IRCTC Website

1. Go to [irctc.co.in](https://www.irctc.co.in/)
2. Log in to your account
3. Navigate to **My Transactions → Booked Ticket History**
4. Find the cancelled ticket
5. Check the **Refund Status** column — it will show:
   - **Refund Credited** with date and amount
   - **Refund Initiated** (payment gateway is processing)
   - **Under Process** (still being reviewed)

### Method 2: IRCTC App

1. Open the IRCTC Rail Connect app
2. Go to **Menu → My Bookings**
3. Tap the cancelled ticket
4. View refund status and transaction reference number

### Method 3: Check Your Bank Statement

Search your bank statement or UPI app transaction history for a credit matching the expected refund amount. Sometimes the refund arrives but under a different merchant name (like "IRCTC" or "PGI Refund").

> **Tip:** If IRCTC shows "Refund Credited" but money hasn't appeared in your account, the issue is with your bank or payment gateway — not IRCTC.

## How to File a TDR on IRCTC

### Step-by-Step

1. Log in at [irctc.co.in](https://www.irctc.co.in/)
2. Go to **My Transactions → Booked Ticket History**
3. Find the ticket and click **File TDR**
4. Select the **reason** from the dropdown:
   - Train cancelled
   - Train late by 3+ hours
   - AC failure
   - Did not travel
   - Partially confirmed
5. Select **passengers** for whom you're claiming
6. Upload supporting documents if needed (e.g., medical certificate)
7. Click **Submit**

### TDR Filing Deadline

- TDR must be filed **within 72 hours** of the train's scheduled departure
- For train cancellations: within **72 hours** of the original schedule
- Late filing → claim rejected

### TDR Refund Timeline

- TDR cases are reviewed by the concerned Zonal Railway
- Decision takes **60–90 days**
- If approved, refund is credited to the original payment method
- Track TDR status: **My Transactions → TDR History**

## Counter Ticket vs. Online Ticket Refund

| Factor | Counter Ticket | Online Ticket (IRCTC) |
|---|---|---|
| Cancellation | At PRS counter only | IRCTC website/app |
| Refund method | Cash at counter or to bank | To original payment method |
| TDR filing | At PRS counter | Online via IRCTC |
| Timeline | Immediate (cash) | 5–21 days depending on payment |

> **Important:** Counter tickets **cannot** be cancelled online. You must visit a PRS (Passenger Reservation System) counter at any railway station.

## What to Do If Refund Is Not Received

Follow this escalation path in order:

### Step 1: Wait for the Standard Timeline

Don't panic before the standard timeline (see table above) has passed. Most UPI/net banking refunds arrive within 5–7 working days.

### Step 2: Check IRCTC Refund Status

Verify whether IRCTC has marked the refund as "Credited." If yes, the issue is with your bank.

### Step 3: Contact Your Bank

If IRCTC shows "Refund Credited" but your account doesn't reflect it:
- Call your bank's customer care
- Share the IRCTC transaction ID and refund reference number
- Ask them to trace the credit

### Step 4: Contact IRCTC Customer Care

If IRCTC still shows "Under Process" beyond the expected timeline:

- **Phone:** 14646 or 011-23340000
- **Email:** care@irctc.co.in
- **IRCTC Customer Care Portal:** [irctc.co.in → Contact Us](https://www.irctc.co.in/)
- Include your **PNR number**, **Transaction ID**, and **cancellation date**

### Step 5: File a Complaint on RailMadad

If IRCTC customer care doesn't resolve it within 7 days:

1. Go to [railmadad.indianrailways.gov.in](https://railmadad.indianrailways.gov.in/)
2. Register/Login
3. Select **Refund of Tickets** as complaint category
4. Enter PNR, transaction details, and describe the issue
5. Submit — you'll get a complaint reference number
6. Track status online

### Step 6: Escalate to Railway Ombudsman

If RailMadad complaint is not resolved in **30 days**, escalate to the Railway Claims Tribunal / Railway Ombudsman:

1. Visit [rbo.indianrailways.gov.in](https://rbo.indianrailways.gov.in/)
2. File an appeal against the unresolved complaint
3. Attach the RailMadad complaint reference and all supporting documents
4. The Ombudsman must dispose of the complaint within **30 days** of receipt

### Step 7: Consumer Forum

As a last resort, file a complaint on the **National Consumer Helpline** (1800-11-4000) or through the [Consumer Grievance Portal](https://consumerhelpline.gov.in/).

## Common Reasons for Refund Delay or Rejection

| Reason | Solution |
|---|---|
| Payment gateway timeout during cancellation | Retry cancellation; check if ticket was actually cancelled |
| TDR not filed within 72 hours | Cannot be recovered — deadline is strict |
| Tatkal ticket cancelled | Only refundable if train cancelled by Railways |
| Bank account closed or changed | Contact IRCTC care with new bank details |
| Duplicate cancellation request | Check if amount was deducted twice; file complaint |
| Wrong UPI ID or bank mapping | Contact payment app support |
| "Refund Credited" but not in account | Bank processing delay — contact bank |

## Cancellation Charges Quick Reference

### Timing-Based Charges (Confirmed Tickets)

| When You Cancel | Deduction |
|---|---|
| More than 48 hours before departure | Flat cancellation charge only |
| 48–12 hours before departure | Flat charge + 25% of fare |
| 12–4 hours before departure | Flat charge + 50% of fare |
| Less than 4 hours before departure | No refund (file TDR if train cancelled) |

### Tatkal Ticket Cancellation

- **No refund** on tatkal tickets if cancelled by passenger
- **Full refund** if train is cancelled by Railways
- **Difference refund** if lower class allotted

## Tips to Avoid Refund Issues

- **Cancel early** — the sooner you cancel, the lower the deduction
- **Screenshot everything** — save cancellation confirmation, transaction ID, and PNR
- **Use IRCTC eWallet** — fastest refund (instant to 24 hours)
- **Don't close browser** mid-cancellation — may cause gateway errors
- **File TDR promptly** — within 72 hours, no exceptions
- **Check email** — IRCTC sends refund confirmation via email and SMS

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does IRCTC refund take to credit?

5–7 working days for UPI/net banking/debit card. Up to 21 days for credit cards. eWallet refunds are near-instant.

### Can I get a refund for a waitlisted ticket that wasn't cancelled?

Yes. If a waitlisted ticket is not confirmed after chart preparation, it's automatically cancelled and a full refund (minus ₹60 service charge for e-tickets) is processed.

### I forgot to cancel my ticket. Can I still get a refund?

You can file a TDR within 72 hours of the train's departure if you didn't travel. However, refund is not guaranteed — it depends on Railways' review.

### Is there a refund for Tatkal tickets?

No refund for passenger-initiated cancellation of Tatkal tickets. Refund is only given if the train is cancelled by Indian Railways.

### My IRCTC account login is not working — how do I cancel?

If you can't log in to cancel your ticket, first fix the login issue. See our guide: [IRCTC Account Login Problems — How to Fix](/guide/irctc-account-login-problems-fix).

### Can I cancel an e-ticket at the railway counter?

No. E-tickets booked on IRCTC can only be cancelled online through the IRCTC website or app. Counter tickets can only be cancelled at PRS counters.
