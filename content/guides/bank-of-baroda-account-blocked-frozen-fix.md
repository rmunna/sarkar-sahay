# Bank of Baroda Account Blocked or Frozen — How to Fix It (2026)

If your Bank of Baroda account shows "Account Blocked", "Debit Freeze", "Transactions Suspended" or you cannot withdraw money, use net banking (BOB World) or make UPI payments — this guide has the step-by-step fix.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is independent and not affiliated with Bank of Baroda. For account-specific help, contact Bank of Baroda directly.

---

## Why Is Your Bank of Baroda Account Blocked?

| Reason | Explanation |
|--------|-------------|
| **KYC Not Updated** | RBI mandates periodic KYC. Accounts with expired or incomplete KYC are frozen |
| **Aadhaar / PAN Not Linked** | Mandatory RBI requirement — accounts without linked Aadhaar/PAN are restricted |
| **Too Many Wrong Password Attempts** | 3–5 failed login attempts block BOB World / net banking |
| **Suspicious Transactions** | Unusual or large transactions trigger Bank of Baroda's fraud detection |
| **Income Tax Department Order** | Tax freeze for pending demands or evasion suspicion |
| **Dormant Account** | No transactions for 2+ years → dormant → restricted access |
| **Court Order / Loan Recovery** | Legal block for debt recovery, court proceedings |

## Fix 1: KYC Freeze

**Via BOB World App:**
1. Open **BOB World** (Bank of Baroda mobile banking app)
2. Look for a **KYC Update** notification on the home screen
3. Tap the notification and complete KYC online
4. Upload Aadhaar (front + back), PAN, and a selfie

**Via Bank of Baroda Net Banking:**
1. Go to **[bankofbaroda.in](https://www.bankofbaroda.in)** → Login → Net Banking
2. Navigate to **Services → KYC Update**
3. Upload required documents: Aadhaar, PAN, address proof
4. Submit — verification takes 1–3 working days

**At Branch (For Full Freeze):**
1. Visit your **home branch** with original Aadhaar + PAN
2. Fill the KYC Update Form
3. Submit documents — account typically unblocked in 1–3 working days

---

## Fix 2: BOB World / Net Banking Blocked (Wrong Password)

**Net Banking Password Reset:**
1. Go to **[bankofbaroda.in](https://www.bankofbaroda.in)** → Internet Banking Login
2. Click **"Forgot Login/Transaction Password"**
3. Enter your User ID and registered mobile number
4. Verify via OTP
5. Set a new password — access restored immediately

**BOB World App MPIN/Password Reset:**
1. Open **BOB World** app → Tap **"Forgot MPIN"**
2. Enter your registered mobile number + OTP
3. Verify with debit card details
4. Set a new MPIN

If app is locked and reinstalling is needed:
1. Uninstall BOB World app
2. Reinstall from Play Store / App Store
3. Re-register with mobile number + Debit Card Number + expiry + CVV

## Fix 3: Suspicious Activity Block

1. Call **Bank of Baroda Customer Care: 1800-5700** (24×7, toll-free)
2. Explain that your account is blocked and you want to know the reason
3. Complete identity verification (OTP + security questions)
4. Request temporary unblock if urgent
5. For permanent unblock, visit **home branch** with:
   - Aadhaar + PAN (originals)
   - Written explanation of flagged transactions
   - Supporting documents (salary slip, business invoice, etc. if applicable)

---

## Fix 4: Income Tax Department Freeze

1. Log into **[incometax.gov.in](https://www.incometax.gov.in)** → e-Filing → Pending Actions → Outstanding Demand
2. Check for any pending notices or demands
3. Pay the demand or file a rectification online
4. Obtain the defreeze order or NIL outstanding certificate from the IT department
5. Submit the order to your Bank of Baroda home branch
6. Branch processes the defreeze in 3–7 working days

---

## Fix 5: Dormant Account Reactivation

1. Visit your **home branch** with:
   - Original Aadhaar card
   - Original PAN card
   - 2 passport-size photos
2. Fill the **Account Reactivation Request Form**
3. Make a cash deposit or sign a debit mandate to activate
4. Account is reactivated within 1–2 working days

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is my money safe if Bank of Baroda freezes my account?**
Yes — your money is completely safe. A freeze only restricts transactions; your balance is not touched and is insured by DICGC up to ₹5 lakh.

**Can I receive salary / transfers in a frozen BOB account?**
During a debit freeze, incoming credits (salary, transfers) usually still work. A full credit + debit freeze blocks everything.

**Will my EMI and insurance auto-debits fail during a BOB account freeze?**
Yes — auto-debits for EMI, SIP, insurance premiums will fail during debit freeze. Immediately inform your lender/insurer to avoid penalties.

**My Bank of Baroda account shows "KYC Pending" — how serious is this?**
If KYC is pending, your account will be progressively restricted. First debit transactions may be blocked, then net banking access. Complete KYC immediately via BOB World app or branch visit.

**How do I raise a grievance if BOB is not resolving my freeze?**
1. Escalate to **Bank of Baroda Nodal Officer** via their grievance portal at [bankofbaroda.in/grievance-redressal](https://www.bankofbaroda.in)
2. If unresolved in 30 days, file a complaint with the **Banking Ombudsman** at [bankingombudsman.rbi.org.in](https://bankingombudsman.rbi.org.in)

**Bank of Baroda merged with Vijaya Bank and Dena Bank — is my old account affected?**
Yes — Vijaya Bank and Dena Bank merged into Bank of Baroda in April 2019. If you had an account in these banks, it is now a Bank of Baroda account. Contact BOB if you face issues with old account numbers or IFSC codes.
