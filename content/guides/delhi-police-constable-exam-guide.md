# Delhi Police Constable Exam Guide 2025

The **Delhi Police Constable** recruitment is conducted by the **Staff Selection Commission (SSC)** on behalf of the Delhi Police. It is one of the most popular government job exams in India, attracting lakhs of candidates for positions in India's largest metropolitan police force.

## About Delhi Police Constable Recruitment

Delhi Police falls under the Union Home Ministry (not Delhi state government), and its constable recruitment is handled by SSC. The exam is conducted in computer-based test (CBT) format followed by physical endurance and measurement tests.

## Eligibility Criteria

### Age Limit
- **Minimum Age:** 18 years
- **Maximum Age:** 25 years
- **Age Relaxation:**
  - OBC: 3 years (max 28 years)
  - SC/ST: 5 years (max 30 years)
  - PwBD: 10 years
  - Ex-Servicemen: 3 years after deducting military service

### Educational Qualification
- **12th Pass** (Senior Secondary) from a recognized board
- No minimum percentage required

### Physical Standards

#### For Male Candidates
| Parameter | General/OBC/SC | ST |
|-----------|---------------|-----|
| Height | 170 cm | 165 cm |
| Chest (unexpanded) | 81 cm | 76 cm |
| Chest (expanded) | 85 cm | 80 cm |

#### For Female Candidates
| Parameter | General/OBC/SC | ST |
|-----------|---------------|-----|
| Height | 157 cm | 152 cm |

### Physical Endurance Test (PET)

#### Male
- 1.6 km race in 6 minutes 30 seconds

#### Female
- 800 meters race in 4 minutes

### Nationality
- Indian citizen

## Exam Pattern

### Computer Based Test (CBT)
| Section | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| General Knowledge / Current Affairs | 50 | 50 |
| Reasoning | 25 | 25 |
| Numerical Ability | 15 | 15 |
| Computer Fundamentals | 10 | 10 |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** |

- **Duration:** 90 minutes
- **Negative Marking:** 0.25 marks for each wrong answer
- **Language:** English and Hindi (bilingual)

## Syllabus

### General Knowledge / Current Affairs (50 marks)
- Current events — national and international
- History of India and Delhi
- Geography, polity, economy
- General science
- Sports, awards, and important days
- Delhi-specific knowledge

### Reasoning (25 marks)
- Analogies, similarities, differences
- Space visualization, problem-solving
- Judgment, decision making
- Visual memory, discrimination
- Relationship concepts, arithmetic reasoning
- Verbal and figure classification
- Coding-decoding, series

### Numerical Ability (15 marks)
- Number systems, simplification
- Decimals, fractions, percentage
- Ratio & proportion, average
- Profit & loss, discount
- Time & distance, time & work
- Data interpretation (tables, graphs)

### Computer Fundamentals (10 marks)
- Computer basics, hardware, software
- MS Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint)
- Internet, email basics
- Operating systems
- Computer abbreviations and terminology

## Selection Process

1. **Computer Based Test (CBT)** — Written exam
2. **Physical Endurance Test (PET)** — Running
3. **Physical Measurement Test (PMT)** — Height, chest
4. **Document Verification**
5. **Medical Examination**

## Preparation Strategy

### 1. Master GK & Current Affairs
This section carries 50% weightage. Read a daily newspaper, focus on last 6 months' current affairs, and cover static GK thoroughly.

### 2. Reasoning — Practice Daily
Solve 20-30 reasoning questions daily. Focus on speed and accuracy. This section is scoring if you practice well.

### 3. Numerical Ability
Focus on basic arithmetic. Practice mental calculations to save time. This section has only 15 questions — don't spend too much time here.

### 4. Computer Fundamentals
Easy scoring section. Cover basics of computer hardware, software, MS Office, and internet. 10 easy marks if prepared.

### 5. Previous Year Papers
SSC Delhi Police papers from previous years are available online. Solve at least 10 papers.

### 6. Physical Preparation
Start running practice 3-4 months before PET. Build stamina gradually:
- Male: Target 1.6 km in under 6 minutes
- Female: Target 800m in under 3:30

### 7. Mock Tests
Take weekly mock tests in exam-like conditions (90 minutes, 100 questions).

## How to Apply

1. Visit [ssc.nic.in](https://ssc.nic.in) when notification is released
2. Register on the SSC portal
3. Fill the application form with personal, educational, and preference details
4. Upload photo, signature
5. Pay fee: ₹100 (free for women/SC/ST/Ex-SM)
6. Submit and save confirmation

## Career Growth

Delhi Police Constable → Head Constable → ASI → SI → Inspector. Promotions are based on seniority, departmental exams, and service record. A constable can reach the Inspector rank during their career with dedicated service.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Delhi Police Constable exam conducted by SSC or Delhi Police?
The exam is conducted by the **Staff Selection Commission (SSC)**, not by Delhi Police directly. SSC handles the written exam, while Delhi Police conducts the physical tests.

### Can candidates from other states apply for Delhi Police?
Yes, Delhi Police Constable recruitment is open to all Indian citizens regardless of state domicile. There is no Delhi residency requirement.

### What is the minimum height for Delhi Police Constable?
For General/OBC/SC males: 170 cm, ST males: 165 cm. For General/OBC/SC females: 157 cm, ST females: 152 cm.

### Is there negative marking in Delhi Police Constable exam?
Yes, 0.25 marks are deducted for each wrong answer. With 100 questions worth 100 marks, careful attempt strategy is important.

### How many vacancies are usually released?
Delhi Police Constable recruitment typically has 5,000 to 15,000+ vacancies, making it one of the largest single-exam recruitments in India.

### What is the qualifying marks for Delhi Police Constable?
General: 35%, OBC: 30%, SC/ST: 25% in the written exam. However, actual cutoff depends on competition and varies each year (usually 55-70 marks).

### Can I prepare for Delhi Police and SSC GD simultaneously?
Yes! The syllabus overlaps significantly. Both exams are conducted by SSC and have similar patterns. Preparing for one helps with the other.
