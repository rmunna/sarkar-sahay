## What is Griha Aadhar Scheme?

The **Griha Aadhar Scheme** is a unique social welfare initiative by the **Government of Goa** under the **Directorate of Women and Child Development**. It provides **₹2,500 per month** to women homemakers in Goa who are not employed or earning from any other source.

The scheme recognizes the contribution of women who manage households and aims to provide them with financial independence and dignity.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Gender:** Must be a woman
2. **Role:** Must be a homemaker (not employed in government/private sector)
3. **Residency:** Must be a resident of Goa for at least **15 years**
4. **Age:** Must be **18 years or older**
5. **Income:** Annual family income should not exceed **₹3,00,000**
6. **One Per Family:** Only one woman per household can avail this benefit
7. **Exclusions:** Women receiving other government pensions are not eligible

## Monthly Benefit

| Benefit | Amount |
|---------|--------|
| Monthly payment | ₹2,500 |
| Annual total | ₹30,000 |
| Mode | Bank transfer |

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar Card**
- **Goa Residence Certificate** (15 years) from Mamlatdar
- **Income Certificate** (family income ≤ ₹3,00,000)
- **Bank Passbook** (account in own name)
- **Ration Card** (Goa)
- **Passport-size photographs**
- **Self-declaration** that applicant is a homemaker and not employed
- **Marriage Certificate** (if married)
- **Voter ID** (Goa)

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Online Application

1. **Visit** [goaonline.gov.in](https://goaonline.gov.in)
2. **Register** / Login
3. **Navigate** to Women & Child Development → Griha Aadhar Scheme
4. **Fill** application: personal details, family income, homemaker declaration
5. **Upload** documents (Aadhaar, residence certificate, income certificate, bank passbook)
6. **Submit** and note application number
7. **Track** status on Goa Online

### Offline Application

1. **Visit** the **Directorate of Women & Child Development** or **Mamlatdar Office**
2. **Collect** the Griha Aadhar application form
3. **Fill** and attach all documents
4. **Submit** to the designated officer
5. **Get** acknowledgement receipt

## Processing Time

- **Verification:** 30-60 days
- **Approval:** After field verification
- **First Payment:** 1-2 months after approval
- **Ongoing:** Monthly to bank account

## Important Tips

1. **15-Year Residency:** Strictly required — get the certificate from Mamlatdar's office well in advance
2. **One Per Household:** Only one woman per family can apply
3. **Self-Declaration:** You must declare you are a homemaker and not receiving any salary/pension
4. **Goa Online:** The easiest way to apply and track is through [goaonline.gov.in](https://goaonline.gov.in)
5. **Income Certificate:** Must be recent (within 6 months)

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. How much do homemakers get under Griha Aadhar?
**₹2,500 per month** directly to the bank account.

### Q2. Can working women apply?
No, the scheme is specifically for **homemakers** who are not employed.

### Q3. What is the residency requirement?
Must have lived in Goa for at least **15 years**.

### Q4. Is there an income limit?
Yes, annual family income should not exceed **₹3,00,000**.

### Q5. Can both mother and daughter-in-law apply?
No, only **one woman per household** can avail the benefit.

### Q6. Can I apply online?
Yes, through [goaonline.gov.in](https://goaonline.gov.in).

### Q7. Can I get Griha Aadhar and Dayanand Social Security pension together?
Generally no — you should not be receiving any other government pension.
