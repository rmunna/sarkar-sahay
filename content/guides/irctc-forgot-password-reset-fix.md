# IRCTC Forgot Password — How to Reset Step by Step

Forgot your IRCTC password? You're not alone — this is one of the most common issues Indian train travellers face. Whether you haven't logged in for months or simply can't remember your credentials, IRCTC provides multiple ways to reset your password.

This guide walks you through every method — on the website and the IRCTC Rail Connect app — including what to do if your registered mobile number or email is no longer accessible.

> **Change vs Reset:** If you **know** your current password and just want to update it, see [How to Change Password](#how-to-change-password-when-you-know-current-one) below. If you've **forgotten** it entirely, follow the reset steps in this guide.

## Method 1: Reset Password via IRCTC Website (irctc.co.in)

This is the most reliable method and works on any browser.

### Steps to Reset

1. Go to [https://www.irctc.co.in](https://www.irctc.co.in)
2. Click **"Login"** in the top-right corner
3. On the login popup, click **"Forgot Password?"** (below the password field)
4. Enter your **User ID** (your IRCTC username) and the **Captcha** shown on screen
5. Click **"Submit"**
6. Choose your verification method:
   - **OTP on registered mobile number**
   - **OTP on registered email ID**
   - **Security question** (if you set one during registration)
7. Enter the OTP or answer the security question
8. You'll be taken to the **"Set New Password"** page
9. Enter your new password (following the [password requirements](#irctc-password-requirements) below)
10. Confirm the new password and click **"Submit"**
11. You'll see a success message — log in with your new password

> **Tip:** If the page doesn't load or shows errors, try during off-peak hours (before 8 AM or after 11 PM). IRCTC servers are busiest between 10 AM–12 PM when Tatkal booking opens.

## Method 2: Reset Password via IRCTC Rail Connect App

The IRCTC Rail Connect mobile app also lets you reset your password.

### Steps to Reset on the App

1. Open the **IRCTC Rail Connect** app on your phone
2. Tap **"Login"**
3. Tap **"Forgot Password?"** below the password field
4. Enter your **User ID**
5. Complete the captcha verification
6. Select your preferred OTP method — **mobile** or **email**
7. Enter the OTP received
8. Set your new password and confirm it
9. Tap **"Submit"**

> **Note:** Make sure you're using the latest version of the app. Older versions may show errors during password reset. Update from [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=cris.org.in.prs.ima) or Apple App Store.

## Reset Using Mobile OTP vs Email OTP vs Security Question

IRCTC offers three verification methods. Here's when to use each:

| Method | Best When | Time to Receive |
|--------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Mobile OTP** | You have access to your registered mobile number | 10–60 seconds |
| **Email OTP** | Mobile OTP not arriving, but you have access to registered email | 1–5 minutes (check spam folder) |
| **Security Question** | Neither mobile nor email OTP is working | Instant (if you remember the answer) |

### Tips for Each Method

- **Mobile OTP:** Ensure your registered SIM is active and has network signal. OTP comes from IRCTC's bulk SMS service — it won't work if you've blocked promotional messages via DND. To unblock, send `START` to `1909`.
- **Email OTP:** Check your **Spam/Junk** folder. IRCTC emails sometimes get filtered. Add `irctc@irctc.co.in` to your contacts to prevent this.
- **Security Question:** The answer is case-sensitive and must match exactly what you entered during registration. Common questions include "What is your favourite colour?" or "What is your pet's name?"

## What to Do If Registered Email or Phone Is No Longer Accessible

This is the trickiest situation. If you've changed your phone number or lost access to your registered email, you cannot receive the OTP.

### Option 1: Try Security Question

If you set a security question during registration, use that method instead — it doesn't require OTP.

### Option 2: Contact IRCTC Customer Care

If no verification method works:

1. **Email IRCTC:** Send an email to `care@irctc.co.in` from any email address
   - Subject: "Password Reset — Cannot Access Registered Mobile/Email"
   - Include: Your User ID, full name as registered, date of birth, and a copy of your Aadhaar or PAN for identity verification
2. **Call IRCTC Helpline:** `14646` or `011-23340000` (available 24/7)
3. **Use the IRCTC Feedback Form:** Visit [https://www.irctc.co.in](https://www.irctc.co.in) → scroll to bottom → click "Contact Us" or "Feedback"

> **Important:** IRCTC may take 3–7 working days to manually verify your identity and reset your account. Keep your ID proof ready.

### Option 3: Create a New Account

If the old account is not linked to any upcoming bookings, it may be faster to create a new IRCTC account with your current mobile number and email. Note: you'll lose access to old booking history and any wallet balance.

## IRCTC Password Requirements

When setting a new password, IRCTC requires:

- **Minimum 8 characters, maximum 15 characters**
- At least **one uppercase letter** (A–Z)
- At least **one lowercase letter** (a–z)
- At least **one number** (0–9)
- At least **one special character** (`@`, `#`, `$`, `%`, `&`, `*`, `!`, etc.)
- **Cannot be the same** as your last 3 passwords
- **Cannot contain** your User ID or common sequences like `123456`

**Example of a valid password:** `Train@2026safe`

> **Tip:** Avoid using your name, date of birth, or phone number in the password. Use a password manager to store it securely.

## How to Change Password (When You Know Current One)

If you're already logged in or know your current password and simply want to update it:

1. Log in to [https://www.irctc.co.in](https://www.irctc.co.in) with your current credentials
2. Click on **"My Profile"** (or your username in the top-right corner)
3. Select **"Change Password"**
4. Enter your **current password**
5. Enter your **new password** and confirm it
6. Click **"Submit"**

On the **IRCTC Rail Connect app:**

1. Log in → tap the **menu icon** (☰) → **My Profile**
2. Tap **"Change Password"**
3. Enter current and new passwords
4. Tap **"Submit"**

## Common Errors During Password Reset and Fixes

### "Invalid User ID"

- Double-check your username — it's case-sensitive
- If you don't remember your User ID, click **"Forgot User ID"** on the login page and recover it using your registered email or mobile

### "OTP Not Received"

- Wait at least 2 minutes before clicking "Resend OTP"
- Check if your phone has network signal
- Disable DND (Do Not Disturb) — send `START` to `1909`
- For email OTP, check Spam/Junk folder
- Try during off-peak hours if IRCTC servers are overloaded

### "OTP Expired"

- OTPs are valid for **10 minutes** only
- Don't request multiple OTPs — use the latest one received
- Click "Resend OTP" and enter the new OTP immediately

### "Password Does Not Meet Requirements"

- Ensure your password has 8–15 characters with uppercase, lowercase, number, and special character
- Don't reuse your last 3 passwords
- Avoid spaces in the password

### "Captcha Error" or Captcha Not Loading

- Refresh the page and try a new captcha
- Clear browser cache and cookies
- Try a different browser (Chrome recommended)
- Disable browser extensions that may block images

### "Service Temporarily Unavailable"

- IRCTC servers face heavy load during Tatkal hours (10 AM–12 PM)
- Try again after 30 minutes or during off-peak hours (early morning or late night)
- Use a wired internet connection for better stability

### "Account Locked After Multiple Failed Attempts"

- If you entered the wrong password or OTP too many times, your account may be temporarily locked
- Wait **30 minutes** and try again
- See our detailed guide: [IRCTC Account Locked — How to Unlock](/guide/irctc-account-locked-unlock-fix)

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### How do I reset my IRCTC password if I forgot it?

Go to [irctc.co.in](https://www.irctc.co.in), click "Login" → "Forgot Password", enter your User ID, verify via OTP (mobile/email) or security question, then set a new password.

### Can I reset my IRCTC password from the mobile app?

Yes. Open the IRCTC Rail Connect app → tap "Login" → tap "Forgot Password" → follow the on-screen steps to reset via OTP.

### What if I don't receive the OTP for password reset?

Wait 2 minutes, then click "Resend OTP". Check your phone signal and ensure DND is disabled (send `START` to `1909`). For email OTP, check the Spam folder. If nothing works, try the security question method.

### What are IRCTC password requirements?

Your password must be 8–15 characters long and include at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number, and one special character. It cannot be the same as your last 3 passwords.

### How do I change my IRCTC password without forgetting it?

Log in to irctc.co.in → click "My Profile" → "Change Password" → enter your current and new password → submit.

### What should I do if my registered mobile number is changed?

Try using the email OTP or security question method. If none work, contact IRCTC at `care@irctc.co.in` or call `14646` with your ID proof for manual verification.

### How long does IRCTC OTP take to arrive?

Mobile OTPs usually arrive within 10–60 seconds. Email OTPs may take 1–5 minutes. During peak hours, there may be delays.

### Can I use the same password again on IRCTC?

No. IRCTC does not allow reusing your last 3 passwords. You must choose a new one each time.
