## What is Rajasthan Vishwakarma Pension Yojana?

**Rajasthan Vishwakarma Pension Yojana** provides a **monthly pension of ₹2,000** to traditional artisans and craftsmen aged 60 years and above who have been engaged in specific skilled trades (Vishwakarma professions). The scheme recognises the lifelong contribution of artisans in traditional trades and provides retirement-like social security.

## Who is Eligible?

**You ARE eligible if:**
- You are a **Rajasthan resident**
- You are **60 years or above**
- You practice one of the **18 listed Vishwakarma trades**
- Your family's annual income is **₹50,000 or less**
- You are **not receiving any other government pension**

---

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card**
2. **Age proof** (Aadhaar with DOB, birth certificate)
3. **Residence proof** (Rajasthan — voter ID, ration card)
4. **Income certificate** from Patwari/Tehsildar (income ≤₹50,000/year)
5. **Proof of trade/craft** — any document showing you work in the trade (registration, work record, caste-linked craft certification)
6. **Bank passbook** (Aadhaar-linked)

## How to Apply

### Step 1 — Online Application

Visit **[rajssp.raj.nic.in](https://rajssp.raj.nic.in)** — Rajasthan Social Security Pension portal:
- Select Vishwakarma Pension Yojana
- Login via SSO or Aadhaar OTP
- Fill personal details and trade details
- Upload documents
- Submit

### Step 2 — Offline at Gram Panchayat / Ward

Submit a filled application form at:
- **Gram Panchayat** (rural)
- **Urban Local Body / Ward office** (urban)

### Step 3 — Verification

The local revenue official (Patwari / RI) verifies income. The Social Security Officer verifies trade and age.

### Step 4 — Pension Starts

After approval, ₹2,000/month is credited to the Aadhaar-linked bank account via DBT.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. My father is a 65-year-old blacksmith (lohar) in a village. Can he get this pension?**

Yes. Lohar (blacksmith) is listed. He should apply at the Gram Panchayat with Aadhaar, income certificate, and age proof. Income must be ₹50,000/year or less.

**2. If I already get the state old age pension, can I also get Vishwakarma pension?**

No. You cannot receive two state social security pensions simultaneously. If already getting old age pension, you are not eligible for Vishwakarma pension.

**3. I am a tailor by trade but moved to daily wage work. Am I still eligible?**

The scheme requires you to be in the artisan trade. If you have permanently left the trade and are no longer a practicing tailor, eligibility may be questioned. Consult the Social Security Office.

**4. Is the Rajasthan Vishwakarma Pension the same as PM Vishwakarma scheme?**

No. **PM Vishwakarma** (central scheme) provides training, toolkits, and credit for artisans of working age. **Rajasthan Vishwakarma Pension** is the state's pension for retired/older artisans 60+. Both can be explored — they serve different purposes.

**5. How do I check if my application is approved?**

Track at rajssp.raj.nic.in using your application number or Aadhaar. You will also receive an SMS on your registered mobile.
