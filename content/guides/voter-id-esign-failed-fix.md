# Voter ID eSign Failed: How to Fix "Something Went Wrong" Error

When applying for a new Voter ID or making corrections through the NVSP portal or Voter Helpline app, many applicants face the dreaded **"eSign failed — Something went wrong"** error at the final stage. After filling in all details and uploading documents, the Aadhaar-based eSign step fails, and the entire application gets stuck.

This guide covers every known reason for eSign failure during voter ID applications and gives you working fixes.

---

## Why Does eSign Fail During Voter ID Application?

The eSign step uses **Aadhaar-based electronic signature** to verify your identity and digitally sign the application form (Form 6, Form 6A, Form 8, etc.). The process involves:

1. You click "eSign" on the NVSP portal or Voter Helpline app
2. You're redirected to the eSign gateway (NSDL or CDAC)
3. An OTP is sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile number
4. After OTP verification, your application is digitally signed

Failure can happen at **any of these steps**. Here are the most common causes:

---

## Common Error Messages

| Error | Meaning |
|---|---|
| "Something went wrong" | Generic server or browser error |
| "eSign failed" | OTP verification or certificate issue |
| "Session expired" | Took too long; session timed out |
| "Aadhaar authentication failed" | Mobile number not linked to Aadhaar |
| "Technical error, try again later" | NVSP or eSign gateway server down |
| "Form rejected by ERO" | Application issue (not eSign-related) |

---

## Fix 1: Check Your Aadhaar-Mobile Link

The most common reason for eSign failure is that your **mobile number is not linked to Aadhaar** or has been recently changed.

**How to verify:**
1. Visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/)
2. Log in and check your linked mobile number
3. If the number is different or not linked, update it by visiting the nearest Aadhaar Enrolment Centre

> **Important:** After updating your mobile number in Aadhaar, wait **24-48 hours** before attempting eSign again. The UIDAI database takes time to propagate changes.

See our [Aadhaar mobile update guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) for step-by-step instructions.

---

## Fix 2: Use the Right Browser

The NVSP eSign gateway has known compatibility issues with certain browsers.

**Recommended browsers:**
- ✅ Google Chrome (latest version) — best compatibility
- ✅ Microsoft Edge (Chromium-based)
- ❌ Mozilla Firefox — frequently causes eSign popup issues
- ❌ Safari — not fully supported
- ❌ Brave / Opera — may block eSign popups

**Browser settings to check:**
- **Disable popup blocker** for voters.eci.gov.in and nvsp.in
- **Allow third-party cookies** (required for eSign gateway redirect)
- **Disable ad blockers** temporarily
- **Clear browser cache and cookies** before retrying

---

## Fix 3: Disable VPN and Proxy

If you're using a VPN, proxy server, or corporate network, the eSign gateway may reject the connection. The Aadhaar eSign service verifies the connection origin, and VPNs trigger security blocks.

**Fix:** Disconnect VPN, switch to a regular mobile data or home Wi-Fi connection, and retry.

---

## Fix 4: Try During Off-Peak Hours

The NVSP portal and eSign gateway experience heavy load during:
- Last few days before electoral roll revision deadlines
- Morning 10 AM – 2 PM on weekdays

**Best times to try:**
- Early morning (6 AM – 9 AM)
- Late evening (8 PM – 11 PM)
- Weekends (lower traffic)

---

## Fix 5: Switch Between NVSP Portal and Voter Helpline App

If eSign fails on the website, try the **Voter Helpline app** (available on Android and iOS), or vice versa. The app uses a slightly different eSign flow and may work when the website doesn't.

- **Voter Helpline App:** [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.eci.citizen) | [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/voter-helpline/id1456535429)
- **NVSP Portal:** [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in/)

---

## Fix 6: Use Incognito/Private Mode

Browser extensions, cached data, and saved cookies can interfere with the eSign process.

1. Open Chrome → Press **Ctrl + Shift + N** (or Cmd + Shift + N on Mac)
2. Go to [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in/)
3. Log in and retry the eSign process

---

## Fix 7: Check if eSign Gateway is Down

Sometimes the issue isn't on your end — the eSign service provider (NSDL eGov or CDAC) may be down.

**How to check:**
- Try a test eSign on [DigiLocker](https://www.digilocker.gov.in/) — if that also fails, the eSign gateway is likely down
- Check Twitter/X for "NVSP down" or "eSign down" reports
- Wait a few hours and retry

---

## Alternative: Submit Without eSign (Offline Method)

If eSign repeatedly fails, you can submit your voter ID application **offline**:

1. **Download Form 6** (new registration) or the relevant form from [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in/)
2. Fill it out manually and sign it physically
3. Attach photocopies of required documents (ID proof, address proof, passport photo)
4. Submit at your nearest **Electoral Registration Office (ERO)** or **BLO (Booth Level Officer)**

You can find your ERO/BLO details on the Voter Helpline app or by calling **1950** (toll-free voter helpline).

---

## What If Your Form Was Rejected by ERO?

Form rejection is different from eSign failure. Common rejection reasons:

| Reason | Fix |
|---|---|
| Blurry or unclear photo | Upload a clear passport-size photo with white background |
| Address proof doesn't match constituency | Use a document showing your current address |
| Duplicate application | Check if you already have a voter ID in another constituency |
| Incomplete information | Re-fill all mandatory fields carefully |
| Age proof missing | Upload birth certificate, school leaving certificate, or passport |

After fixing the issue, resubmit through the portal or app.

---

## Important Tips

1. **Keep your Aadhaar mobile number active** — the eSign OTP goes to this number only
2. **Complete the eSign within 3 minutes** — the session expires quickly
3. **Don't click the back button** during eSign — it breaks the session
4. **Take a screenshot** of your application reference number before attempting eSign
5. **Try mobile data** if Wi-Fi doesn't work — some ISPs block eSign gateway ports

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is eSign mandatory for Voter ID application?
No. You can submit offline with a physical signature. eSign is required only for online submission through the NVSP portal or Voter Helpline app.

### Can I use someone else's Aadhaar for eSign?
No. The eSign must be done using **your own Aadhaar number** linked to your active mobile number.

### eSign OTP is not coming to my phone. What do I do?
Check if your mobile number is linked to Aadhaar. If it is, ensure you have network signal. OTPs can take up to 3 minutes during peak hours. If it still doesn't arrive, your telecom operator may be blocking bulk SMS — try after some time.

### I get "Session Expired" immediately after clicking eSign. Why?
This usually happens due to browser cache issues or slow internet. Clear cookies, use incognito mode, and ensure stable internet before retrying.

### The eSign popup doesn't open at all. What should I do?
Your browser is blocking the popup. Allow popups for voters.eci.gov.in in your browser settings. In Chrome: Settings → Privacy → Site Settings → Pop-ups → Allow for this site.

### Can I track my Voter ID application status?
Yes. Visit [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in/) → Track Application Status, and enter your reference number.

### How long does it take to get a Voter ID after successful eSign?
Typically 15-30 days. The BLO will visit your address for verification, after which the card is processed and dispatched or made available for download as an e-EPIC.
