# New LPG Gas Connection Apply Online: Indane, HP, Bharat Gas

Getting a new LPG gas connection in India is now a simple online process. You can apply through any of the three Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs) — Indian Oil (Indane), Hindustan Petroleum (HP Gas), or Bharat Petroleum (Bharat Gas). This guide covers the complete process, documents, fees, and Ujjwala Yojana eligibility.

## What is an LPG Gas Connection?

An LPG (Liquefied Petroleum Gas) connection allows you to receive subsidised or non-subsidised cooking gas cylinders from a registered distributor. Each household is entitled to one subsidised LPG connection. The connection includes a cylinder, regulator, and gas stove (optional, at extra cost).

---

## Who is Eligible?

- Any Indian citizen aged 18 years or above
- Must have a valid address proof for the area where connection is needed
- **One subsidised connection per household** (based on Aadhaar/ration card address)
- If another family member already has a subsidised connection at the same address, you can only get a **non-subsidised (market price) connection**
- BPL families, SC/ST, and Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana beneficiaries are eligible for free connections under **Ujjwala Yojana**

---

## Documents Required

### Mandatory Documents
- **Identity Proof** — [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online), Voter ID, Passport, or Driving Licence
- **Address Proof** — Aadhaar card, Passport, Voter ID, Ration Card, Electricity Bill, or Telephone Bill
- **Passport-size photographs** — 2 copies

### For Ujjwala Yojana (Free Connection)
- BPL certificate or ration card
- Aadhaar card (mandatory)
- Bank account passbook (for subsidy transfer)
- Caste certificate (for SC/ST applicants)

### Additional Documents (if applicable)
- **Tenant** — NOC from landlord + landlord's address proof
- **Self-declaration** — that no other subsidised LPG connection exists in the household

## How to Apply Online — Step-by-Step

### Option 1: Indane Gas (Indian Oil — IOCL)

1. Visit [www.iocl.com/indane-for-you](https://www.iocl.com/indane-for-you)
2. Click **"New Connection"** or **"Request New Connection"**
3. Enter your mobile number and verify with OTP
4. Fill in personal details — name, address, Aadhaar number
5. Upload scanned copies of identity proof, address proof, and photograph
6. Select your preferred distributor from the list
7. Submit the application and note down the reference number
8. Pay the security deposit online (₹1600–₹2000 depending on cylinder type)
9. Wait for the distributor to verify documents and schedule delivery

### Option 2: HP Gas (Hindustan Petroleum — HPCL)

1. Visit [www.hindustanpetroleum.com/hpgas](https://www.hindustanpetroleum.com/hpgas)
2. Click **"New Connection"** under HP Gas section
3. Register with your mobile number
4. Fill the online application form with personal and address details
5. Upload required documents
6. Select the nearest HP Gas distributor
7. Submit and pay the security deposit online
8. The distributor will verify and deliver within 7–15 days

### Option 3: Bharat Gas (Bharat Petroleum — BPCL)

1. Visit [www.bharatgas.com](https://www.bharatgas.com/)
2. Click **"New Connection"** on the homepage
3. Enter your details — name, Aadhaar, address, mobile number
4. Upload identity proof, address proof, and photograph
5. Choose your preferred distributor
6. Pay the refundable security deposit
7. Application is forwarded to the distributor for verification and delivery

---

## How to Apply Offline

1. Visit the nearest LPG distributor (Indane, HP Gas, or Bharat Gas)
2. Collect the **Subscription Voucher (SV)** form
3. Fill in the form with your personal and address details
4. Attach self-attested copies of identity proof, address proof, and photographs
5. Submit the form along with the security deposit
6. The distributor will verify your documents and install the connection at your address

## Fees and Charges

| Item | Approximate Cost |
|------|-----------------|
| Security deposit (14.2 kg cylinder) | ₹1,450–₹1,600 |
| Security deposit (regulator) | ₹150–₹200 |
| First cylinder refill (at subsidised rate) | ₹800–₹900 |
| **Total approximate cost** | **₹1,600–₹2,000** |
| Security deposit (5 kg cylinder) | ₹800–₹1,000 |

> **Note:** Security deposits are **refundable** when you surrender the connection. Prices vary slightly by company and region.

## Ujjwala Yojana — Free LPG Connection

Under the **Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY)**, eligible families can get a free LPG connection. The government covers the security deposit.

### Eligibility for Ujjwala 2.0
- Women from BPL households
- SC/ST households
- Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (Gramin) beneficiaries
- Antyodaya Anna Yojana beneficiaries
- Forest dwellers
- Most Backward Classes (MBC)
- Tea and ex-tea garden tribes
- People residing in islands and river islands
- **No existing LPG connection** in the household

### How to Apply
1. Visit [www.pmuy.gov.in](https://www.pmuy.gov.in/) or your nearest LPG distributor
2. Fill in the Ujjwala application form
3. Submit Aadhaar card, BPL/ration card, and bank passbook
4. Self-declaration that no other LPG connection exists in the family
5. The connection is provided free of cost; the first refill may also be free

## Important Tips

1. **Link your Aadhaar to bank account** before applying — this ensures you receive the LPG subsidy directly via DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer). See our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) for details.
2. **Choose the nearest distributor** for faster delivery and easier refill booking.
3. **Keep your mobile number updated** — all booking confirmations, delivery updates, and subsidy notifications come via SMS.
4. **Opt for a composite cylinder** (fibre-wrapped) if available — it is lighter and safer than the traditional metal cylinder.
5. **Surrender duplicate connections** — if you have more than one subsidised connection in the household, surrender the extra one to avoid issues.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Can I apply for LPG connection online without Aadhaar?
Yes, you can use Voter ID or Passport as identity proof. However, Aadhaar is mandatory for receiving the LPG subsidy under the PAHAL (DBT) scheme.

### 2. How much does a new LPG connection cost?
The total cost is approximately ₹1,600–₹2,000, which includes a refundable security deposit for the cylinder and regulator, plus the cost of the first refill.

### 3. Can I get a second LPG connection at the same address?
You can get a second connection, but it will be at **non-subsidised (market price)** rates. Only one subsidised connection is allowed per household.

### 4. What is the difference between subsidised and non-subsidised LPG?
Subsidised LPG is available at a government-controlled rate, and the subsidy amount is credited to your bank account. Non-subsidised cylinders are sold at market price without any subsidy.

### 5. How do I check the status of my new LPG connection application?
- **Indane:** Call 1800-233-3555 or visit the IOCL website
- **HP Gas:** Call 1800-233-3555 or visit the HPCL website
- **Bharat Gas:** Call 1800-22-4344 or visit the BPCL website

### 6. Can tenants apply for a new LPG connection?
Yes, tenants can apply by providing a **NOC (No Objection Certificate)** from the landlord along with the landlord's address proof.

### 7. Is the security deposit refundable?
Yes, the security deposit for the cylinder and regulator is fully refundable when you surrender the connection.

### 8. How long does it take to get a new LPG connection?
Typically 15–30 days from the date of application, depending on document verification and distributor availability.
