## What is Subhadra Yojana?

Subhadra Yojana is a women empowerment scheme launched by the BJP-led Government of Odisha in **September 2024**. The scheme provides **₹50,000** to eligible women over a period of **5 years** (₹10,000 per year), credited directly to their bank accounts. The scheme was a key election promise and is named after Subhadra, symbolizing women's strength and dignity.

The first installment of ₹5,000 was disbursed on **17 September 2024** (the birth anniversary of PM Narendra Modi), with subsequent payments of ₹5,000 every 6 months (in two installments of ₹5,000 each per year). Over **1 crore women** in Odisha are expected to benefit from this scheme.

| Duration | 5 years (2024-2029) |
| Payment mode | Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) |

## Who is Eligible?

### Eligible Women
- **Women aged 21 to 60 years** (as on the date of application)
- Must be a **permanent resident of Odisha**
- Must have an **Aadhaar-linked bank account** in her own name
- Must not be an **income tax payer**
- Must not be a **government employee** (Central/State/PSU)

### Who is NOT Eligible
- Women below 21 years or above 60 years of age
- Women who are income tax payees or whose spouse is an income tax payee
- Serving or retired government employees
- Women receiving benefits under similar cash transfer schemes (some exclusions may apply)
- Elected representatives (MPs, MLAs, Zilla Parishad members, etc.)
- Women with family income exceeding the prescribed limit
- Professionals (doctors, engineers, lawyers, CAs) registered with professional bodies

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar Card** (mandatory — for e-KYC and DBT)
- **Bank passbook** (Aadhaar-linked account in applicant's name)
- **Voter ID Card** (for residence and age proof)
- **Ration Card** (for family verification)
- **Mobile number** linked to Aadhaar (for OTP)
- **Self-declaration form** (declaring eligibility)

## How to Apply for Subhadra Yojana

### Online Application

1. Visit the **Subhadra Portal** at [subhadra.odisha.gov.in](https://subhadra.odisha.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Apply Now"** or **"New Registration"**
3. Enter your **Aadhaar number** for e-KYC verification
4. Complete **OTP verification** on your registered mobile number
5. Fill in personal details — name, age, address, district, block
6. Enter **bank account details** (must be Aadhaar-linked)
7. Upload or verify documents through e-KYC
8. Submit **self-declaration** of eligibility
9. Review all details and **submit the application**
10. Note the **application reference number** for tracking

### Through Mo Seva Kendra / Common Service Centre

1. Visit the **nearest Mo Seva Kendra** or **CSC centre**
2. Carry original **Aadhaar Card** and **bank passbook**
3. The operator will help you fill the application online
4. Complete **biometric/OTP verification**
5. Collect the **application receipt**

### At Special Camps

The government organizes **Subhadra registration camps** at GP and block level:
1. Visit the camp with your documents
2. Officials will verify your eligibility
3. Complete Aadhaar-based e-KYC
4. Application will be submitted on your behalf
5. You'll receive SMS confirmation

### Check Application Status

1. Visit [subhadra.odisha.gov.in](https://subhadra.odisha.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Check Status"**
3. Enter your **Aadhaar number** or **application reference number**
4. View your application status and payment details

## Important Tips

1. **Link Aadhaar to bank account** — Without Aadhaar-bank linking, payment cannot be processed. Visit your bank if not yet linked. See our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
2. **Use your own bank account** — The bank account must be in the applicant's (woman's) name, not a joint or family account
3. **Apply early** — Early applicants receive priority processing. Don't wait for deadlines
4. **Beware of fraud** — Subhadra Yojana registration is free. Don't pay anyone for registration or to "speed up" payments
5. **Keep mobile number updated** — OTP and payment notifications are sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile number

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. How much money will women get under Subhadra Yojana?
Eligible women will receive **₹50,000** over 5 years — ₹10,000 per year in two installments of ₹5,000 each.

### Q2. What is the age limit for Subhadra Yojana?
Women aged **21 to 60 years** who are permanent residents of Odisha can apply.

### Q3. Can government employees apply?
No. Current or retired government employees (Central/State/PSU) are not eligible for Subhadra Yojana.

### Q4. How do I check my Subhadra Yojana payment status?
Visit [subhadra.odisha.gov.in](https://subhadra.odisha.gov.in), click on "Check Status", and enter your Aadhaar or reference number to see payment details.

### Q5. Is Subhadra Yojana available for unmarried women?
Yes. Marital status is not a criterion. All women aged 21-60 meeting the eligibility criteria can apply, regardless of whether they are married, unmarried, widowed, or divorced.

### Q6. Can I get Subhadra Yojana if I already receive KALIA benefits?
The government has clarified eligibility exclusions. Check the official portal or contact the helpline to confirm whether your specific case qualifies.

### Q7. What if my Aadhaar is not linked to my bank account?
You must link your Aadhaar to your bank account before applying. Visit your bank branch with Aadhaar Card and bank passbook to complete the linking process.
