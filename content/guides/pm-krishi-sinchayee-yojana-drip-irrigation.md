## What is PM Krishi Sinchayee Yojana?

**Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY)** is the Government of India's comprehensive irrigation scheme operating under the motto **"Har Khet Ko Pani, More Crop Per Drop"** (Water to Every Field, More Crop Per Drop).

The scheme has three components:
- **Accelerated Irrigation Benefits Programme (AIBP)** — completing pending irrigation projects
- **Har Khet Ko Pani (HKKP)** — extending water to every farmland
- **Per Drop More Crop (PDMC)** — **micro-irrigation (drip and sprinkler systems)** at farm level

> **Most farmers apply under PDMC** for the drip/sprinkler subsidy.

## PDMC — Micro-Irrigation Subsidy Details

| Farmer Category | Drip/Sprinkler Subsidy |
|---|---|
| Small and marginal farmers (≤2 ha) | **55% subsidy** (Central 45% + State 10%) |
| Other farmers (>2 ha) | **45% subsidy** (Central 35% + State 10%) |
| States may add additional subsidy | Some states up to 75–90% |

States like **Maharashtra, Gujarat, Rajasthan, and Karnataka** offer higher subsidies — in some cases up to **90% for small farmers** including state's own contribution.

## Benefits of Drip/Sprinkler Irrigation

| Benefit | Impact |
|---|---|
| Water saving | 40–50% less water used |
| Yield increase | 20–40% higher crop yields |
| Fertiliser efficiency | Fertigation (applying fertiliser via drip) reduces wastage |
| Labour saving | Less labour for irrigation |
| Electricity saving | Less pump hours needed |

## Who is Eligible?

- **All farmers** with cultivable land — no income limit
- Both small (≤2 ha) and large farmers (small farmers get higher subsidy)
- Farmers with **assured water source** (borewell, canal, pond, river)
- Scheme works on a **first-come, first-served** basis — apply early as annual budgets are limited

## How to Apply — Step by Step

The application process is **state-wise** through the state agriculture department:

### General Process:

1. Visit your **state agriculture department** portal or office:
   - **Maharashtra:** [mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in](https://mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in) (Mahadbt — agriculture)
   - **Rajasthan:** [rajkisan.rajasthan.gov.in](https://rajkisan.rajasthan.gov.in)
   - **UP:** [upagriculture.com](https://upagriculture.com)
   - **MP:** [mpagric.nic.in](https://mpagric.nic.in) → Micro Irrigation
   - **Karnataka:** [kisanmitra.karnataka.gov.in](https://kisanmitra.karnataka.gov.in)
   - **Gujarat:** [ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in)

2. Register and apply for **Micro Irrigation Subsidy** (PMKSY-PDMC)
3. Enter land details, water source type, crop details
4. Select an **empanelled vendor** from the state's approved list
5. Approval is granted based on budget availability
6. **Vendor installs the drip/sprinkler system**
7. Inspection by agriculture department officer
8. **Subsidy credited to your bank account** via DBT (after installation verification)

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card**
2. **Land records** — Khasra-Khatauni / 7/12 extract (land ownership proof)
3. **Water source proof** — borewell completion certificate, canal allotment letter, or any proof of water availability
4. **Bank account passbook** (Aadhaar-linked for DBT)
5. **Farmer registration** (many states require pre-registration on PM-KISAN or Kisan ID)

## System Types Covered

| System Type | Crops | Water Saving |
|---|---|---|
| Drip irrigation | Vegetables, fruits, sugarcane, cotton, pulses | 50–70% |
| Mini sprinkler | Vegetables, groundnut, onion, spices | 30–50% |
| Micro sprinkler | Orchards, plantations | 40–60% |
| Rain gun / Sprinkler | Paddy, wheat, maize | 30–40% |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the subsidy for the purchase of drip equipment or for installation?
The subsidy covers **both equipment and installation costs** — the total system cost including pipes, drippers, connectors, filter, pump connection, and labour. Only approved vendors are eligible.

### Can I buy the drip system first and then claim subsidy?
In some states, you need **prior approval** before installation. Buying without approval and then claiming may not be eligible. Always get approval first.

### My land is leased (rented). Can I apply?
Most states require you to be the **land owner** for subsidy. Some states allow long-term lease holders (5+ years) with a no-objection from the landowner. Check your state's rules.

### What is the minimum land area required?
There is usually a **minimum area** requirement — commonly 0.2 hectares (2000 sq m / 20 guntha). Smaller plots may not be eligible. Check your state's minimum threshold.

### Is PMKSY applicable for greenhouse/polyhouse?
Yes — protected cultivation (greenhouse, polyhouse, net house) is covered under PMKSY, often with higher subsidy rates.
