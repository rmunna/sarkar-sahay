# Forgot IRCTC User ID? How to Recover Your IRCTC Login Username

Can't remember your IRCTC username? You're not alone — millions of users face this issue, especially when they created accounts years ago or used a random user ID. Here's how to recover it quickly.

## Common Error Messages

- **"Invalid User ID or Password"**
- **"User ID does not exist"**
- **"No account found with the given email/mobile"**
- **"Your account has been locked. Please try after 30 minutes."**
- **"OTP not received for user ID recovery"**
- **"The email ID is already registered with another user"**

## How to Recover Your IRCTC User ID

### Method 1: Recover via Registered Email

This is the fastest method if you remember which email you used:

1. Go to [irctc.co.in](https://www.irctc.co.in)
2. Click **"Login"** then click **"Forgot User ID?"**
3. Select **"Forgot User ID"** option
4. Enter your **registered email address**
5. Enter the captcha
6. Click **"Submit"**
7. Your User ID will be sent to your email — check Inbox and Spam folders
8. The email comes from `care@irctc.co.in` with subject "Your IRCTC User ID"

### Method 2: Recover via Registered Mobile Number

1. Go to [irctc.co.in/nget/profile/user-forgot](https://www.irctc.co.in/nget/profile/user-forgot)
2. Select **"Get User ID via SMS"**
3. Enter your **10-digit mobile number** registered with IRCTC
4. Enter captcha
5. Click **"Submit"**
6. You'll receive an SMS with your User ID within 1-2 minutes

### Method 3: Using a Previous Booking PNR

If you have a PNR number from any previous booking:

1. Check your email for past IRCTC booking confirmations
2. The booking email contains your **User ID** in the header
3. Search your email for "IRCTC" or "e-ticket" to find old booking mails
4. The User ID is mentioned as "Booked by: [YOUR_USER_ID]"

### Method 4: Check Your Browser's Saved Passwords

1. **Chrome:** Go to `chrome://settings/passwords` → Search "irctc"
2. **Firefox:** Settings → Privacy & Security → Saved Logins → Search "irctc"
3. **Safari:** Preferences → Passwords → Search "irctc"
4. Your saved User ID and password will appear

### Method 5: Contact IRCTC Support

If none of the above methods work:

1. **Call:** 14646 or 011-23340000
2. **Email:** care@irctc.co.in with:
   - Your full name
   - Registered email and mobile
   - Date of birth
   - Any previous PNR number
3. **Twitter/X:** Tweet to [@IRCTCofficial](https://twitter.com/IRCTCofficial) (they respond fast)
4. **Chat:** Use the live chat option on [care.irctc.co.in](https://care.irctc.co.in)

## Common Problems and Fixes

### "No Account Found with This Email/Mobile"

This means either:
- You used a **different email/mobile** when creating the account
- Your account was **deleted due to inactivity** (IRCTC deactivates accounts inactive for 12+ months)

**Fix:** Try all your email addresses and phone numbers. If the account was deleted, create a new account.

### "OTP Not Received"

- Wait at least **5 minutes** — IRCTC OTPs are often delayed during peak hours
- Check if your phone has **DND (Do Not Disturb)** enabled — disable it
- Ensure you have **network connectivity** and aren't in a low-signal area
- Try again during **off-peak hours** (avoid 8 AM - 10 AM and 10 PM - 12 AM)

### "Account Locked After Multiple Attempts"

- Wait **30 minutes** and try again
- Don't keep refreshing or retrying — it extends the lock
- If still locked after 30 minutes, clear browser cookies and try

### Can't Access Old Email or Mobile Number

If you no longer have access to your registered email or mobile:

1. You'll need to **create a new IRCTC account**
2. IRCTC doesn't allow recovery without the registered email/mobile
3. Use your current email and mobile for the new account
4. Note: Each Aadhaar can only be linked to **one IRCTC account**
5. If Aadhaar conflict arises, contact care@irctc.co.in to delink the old account

## Tips to Avoid Losing Your IRCTC User ID Again

- **Save your User ID** in your phone contacts or password manager
- **Use a memorable username** — IRCTC allows you to choose custom IDs
- **Enable browser password saving** for irctc.co.in
- **Link your Aadhaar** — makes future recovery easier
- **Keep your email and mobile updated** on your IRCTC profile

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does it take to receive the User ID via email?
Usually **instant to 5 minutes**. During peak booking hours (8-10 AM, Tatkal time), it may take up to **15-20 minutes**. Check your spam/junk folder if you don't see it in your inbox.

### Can I recover IRCTC User ID without email and mobile number?
Unfortunately, no. IRCTC requires at least one of these for verification. If you've lost access to both, you must create a new account. Contact care@irctc.co.in if your Aadhaar is linked to the old account.

### Is IRCTC User ID the same as my username?
Yes, your **User ID is your username** that you created during registration. It's different from your name or email. It's the alphanumeric ID you use to log in.

### Can I change my IRCTC User ID after recovery?
No, IRCTC **does not allow changing the User ID** once created. You can only change your password, email, mobile number, and other profile details. If you want a different User ID, create a new account.

### My IRCTC account was deactivated. Can I reactivate it?
IRCTC deactivates accounts after prolonged inactivity. To reactivate, try logging in — some accounts auto-reactivate. If not, contact IRCTC support at 14646 or email care@irctc.co.in with your account details.

### Can two people have the same IRCTC User ID?
No, each User ID is **unique**. If your preferred ID is taken, IRCTC will suggest alternatives. You can use a combination of your name and numbers.
