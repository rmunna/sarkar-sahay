# MPSC (Maharashtra Public Service Commission) Exam Guide — Eligibility, Pattern & Tips

The Maharashtra Public Service Commission (MPSC) conducts the State Services Examination for recruitment to various Group A and Group B posts in Maharashtra government. This includes the prestigious Maharashtra Civil Service (Deputy Collector, DSP, etc.) and other allied services.

MPSC is considered one of the toughest state-level competitive exams in India, offering prestigious administrative and police positions.

## Eligibility Criteria

### Age Limit

| Category | Minimum Age | Maximum Age |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| General (Open) | 19 years | 38 years |
| OBC | 19 years | 41 years |
| SC/ST | 19 years | 43 years |

### Educational Qualification

- **Minimum:** Bachelor's degree from a recognized university
- Any discipline accepted

### Nationality

- Must be a citizen of India
- Maharashtra domicile required for certain posts

## Exam Pattern

### Stage 1: Preliminary Exam (Screening)

| Paper | Subject | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|-------|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| Paper 1 | General Studies | 100 | 200 | 2 hours |
| Paper 2 | CSAT | 80 | 200 | 2 hours |

- Paper 2 is qualifying (33% minimum)
- Only Paper 1 marks count for cutoff

### Stage 2: Main Exam

| Paper | Subject | Marks | Duration |
|-------|---------|-------|----------|
| Paper 1 | Marathi & English (Qualifying) | 100 | 1 hour |
| Paper 2 | Marathi & English (Merit) | 200 | 2 hours |
| Paper 3 | General Studies I | 200 | 2 hours |
| Paper 4 | General Studies II | 200 | 2 hours |
| Paper 5 | General Studies III | 200 | 2 hours |
| Paper 6 | General Studies IV | 200 | 2 hours |

### Stage 3: Interview/Personality Test

- 100 marks
- Tests personality, communication, and knowledge

## Syllabus Overview

### General Studies I
- History of Modern India, Maharashtra History, Geography of India & Maharashtra

### General Studies II
- Indian Constitution, Governance, Social Justice, International Relations

### General Studies III
- Economy, Agriculture, Science & Technology, Environment

### General Studies IV
- Ethics, Integrity, Aptitude, Emotional Intelligence

### CSAT (Paper 2)
- Comprehension, Logical reasoning, Decision making, Data interpretation, Maths

## How to Apply

1. Visit MPSC at [mpsc.gov.in](https://mpsc.gov.in)
2. Register on the MPSC portal
3. Apply for the State Services Exam notification
4. Fill in personal, educational, and preference details
5. Upload photo and signature
6. Pay fee and submit

## Salary & Benefits

- **Deputy Collector:** ₹56,100 – ₹1,77,500 (Level 9)
- **DSP:** ₹56,100 – ₹1,77,500
- **Other Group A/B posts:** ₹41,800 – ₹1,32,300
- DA, HRA, vehicle, medical benefits
- Government bungalow, pension, prestigious career

## Preparation Tips

1. **Maharashtra focus:** MPSC heavily emphasizes Maharashtra history, geography, and governance
2. **Marathi proficiency:** Strong Marathi is essential for merit paper
3. **NCERT foundation:** Build basics from NCERT then move to state-specific books
4. **Answer writing:** Practice descriptive answer writing for Mains
5. **Current affairs:** Maharashtra + India focus — read Loksatta/Maharashtra Times daily
6. **Previous papers:** Analyze last 10 years' MPSC papers

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is MPSC only for Maharashtra residents?
No, but Maharashtra domicile is required for certain reserved posts. Open category is available to all.

### What is the selection process?
Prelims → Mains → Interview → Document Verification.

### How many attempts are allowed?
General: 6 attempts, OBC: 9, SC/ST: No limit (within age).

### Is Marathi mandatory?
Yes, knowledge of Marathi is essential and tested in the exam.

### What posts are available through MPSC?
Deputy Collector, DSP, Tahsildar, BDO, and various Group A/B administrative posts.

### How long does the entire process take?
Typically 12-18 months from Prelims to final appointment.
