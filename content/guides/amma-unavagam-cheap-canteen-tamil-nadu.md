## What is Amma Unavagam?

**Amma Unavagam** (Mother's Canteen) is Tamil Nadu government's subsidized canteen scheme that provides **affordable meals to anyone** — especially the urban poor, daily wage workers, and homeless persons. 

Key prices:
- **Idli:** ₹1 per plate (2 idlis + sambar + chutney)
- **Full Meal (Thali):** ₹5 per plate (rice + 3 curries + papad + pickle)
- **Upma/Pongal:** ₹5 per plate

## Who Can Use Amma Unavagam?

**Anyone.** There is no income limit or eligibility criteria. Any person — rich or poor — can eat at Amma Unavagam. The price is the same for everyone.

---

## Where are Amma Unavagam Canteens Located?

Canteens are at:
- **Bus stands** — major and minor bus terminals
- **Railway stations**
- **Hospitals** — government hospital premises
- **Markets** and **commercial areas**
- **Residential areas** in major cities

Tamil Nadu has 400+ Amma Unavagam canteens across the state.

---

## When are Canteens Open?

Most canteens are open:
- **Morning:** 6 AM – 11 AM (breakfast — idli/upma/pongal)
- **Afternoon:** 12 PM – 3 PM (full meal)
- Some canteens: 6 AM – 3 PM continuously

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. Do I need to show any ID to eat at Amma Unavagam?**

No. Simply walk in and buy food. No ID required.

**2. Why is the food so cheap? Is it subsidized?**

Yes. The Tamil Nadu government subsidizes the difference between the actual cost and the price charged. The government pays the rest to the operating agency.

**3. Are Amma Unavagam canteens clean and safe to eat?**

Government inspections are conducted periodically. Quality and cleanliness vary by location. Many canteens are well-maintained; some may have limited facilities. Standards have improved significantly over the years.

**4. Is Amma Unavagam still operating? I heard it was closed.**

The scheme has seen operational fluctuations over the years. As of 2024-25, Amma Unavagam continues to operate across Tamil Nadu. Some closures happened due to location-specific issues but the scheme is active overall. Check locally for the nearest operational canteen.

**5. Can I order food to take home from Amma Unavagam?**

Most Amma Unavagam locations are dine-in. Take-away may be allowed at specific canteens — ask the canteen operator.
