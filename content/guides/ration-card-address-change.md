# Ration Card Address Change: How to Update Address Online

When you move to a new residence — whether within the same city or to a different district — you must update the address on your ration card. Without this update, you may face issues collecting rations from your Fair Price Shop (FPS). This guide covers the complete process to change your ration card address online and offline across Indian states.

## Why Update Your Ration Card Address?

- Your ration card is linked to a specific FPS near your registered address
- If you move, your old FPS may be too far to collect rations
- Address mismatch can cause issues during verification at the FPS
- Under **One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC)**, you can temporarily buy rations anywhere, but your permanent address should still be updated

## Who Can Apply for Address Change?

- The head of the family (ration card holder) whose name appears on the card
- Any adult family member with authorization from the card holder
- The applicant must have a valid address proof for the new residence

## Documents Required

### Mandatory Documents
- Existing ration card (original and photocopy)
- Aadhaar card of the head of the family
- New address proof (any one):
  - Electricity or water bill (recent, within 3 months)
  - Rent agreement or lease deed
  - Property tax receipt
  - Voter ID with new address
  - Bank passbook with updated address
  - Gas connection bill

### Additional Documents (if applicable)
- Self-declaration or affidavit for address change
- NOC from the landlord (for rented accommodation)
- Passport-size photograph of the card holder

## Step-by-Step Online Process

The exact process varies by state, but the general flow is similar across most state portals.

### Step 1: Visit Your State's PDS Portal

Go to your state's food and civil supplies portal. Some major state portals:

| State | Portal |
|-------|--------|
| Maharashtra | https://rcms.mahafood.gov.in |
| Delhi | https://nfs.delhi.gov.in |
| Rajasthan | https://ration.raj.nic.in |
| Uttar Pradesh | https://fcs.up.gov.in |
| Tamil Nadu | https://www.tnpds.gov.in |
| Karnataka | https://ahara.kar.nic.in |

### Step 2: Log In or Register

- Log in using your ration card number, Aadhaar number, or registered mobile number
- If you don't have an account, register first using your ration card details

### Step 3: Select "Address Change" or "Correction"

- Navigate to the "Services" or "Modifications" section
- Select "Address Change" or "Update Address" option

### Step 4: Enter New Address Details

- Fill in the new residential address carefully
- Enter the correct district, taluk/tehsil, and PIN code
- Select the new FPS (Fair Price Shop) you want to be assigned to, if prompted

### Step 5: Upload Documents

- Upload scanned copies of your new address proof
- Upload Aadhaar card copy
- Upload the existing ration card copy
- File size is usually limited to 200 KB–1 MB per document (PDF or JPEG)

### Step 6: Submit and Note Application Number

- Review all details before submitting
- Save the application/reference number for tracking

### Step 7: Track Your Application

- Use the "Track Application" feature on the same portal
- You will also receive SMS updates on your registered mobile number

## Offline Process

If online facility is not available in your state or you prefer offline:

1. **Visit your nearest Food & Civil Supplies Office** (Taluk/Tehsildar office or District Supply Officer)
2. **Collect the application form** for address change (or download from the state portal)
3. **Fill in the form** with your existing ration card details and new address
4. **Attach all required documents** (photocopies, address proof, photographs)
5. **Submit the form** at the counter and collect the acknowledgement receipt
6. **Verification visit**: A food inspector may visit your new address for verification
7. **Collect the updated ration card** once the change is approved

## Fees

| Item | Fee |
|------|-----|
| Online address change | Free in most states |
| Offline application fee | ₹2 to ₹25 (varies by state) |
| Document attestation (if required) | Free to ₹50 |

> **Note:** Most states do not charge any fee for address corrections on ration cards.

## Processing Time

- **Online applications:** 7 to 30 days
- **Offline applications:** 15 to 45 days
- Delays can occur if the food inspector's verification is pending

## What Happens to Your FPS (Fair Price Shop)?

When you change your address:

- If you move **within the same area**, your FPS may remain the same
- If you move to a **different area or district**, you will be assigned a new FPS
- Under **ONORC**, you can buy rations from any FPS in India using Aadhaar-based authentication while your transfer is being processed
- Request FPS change along with address change to avoid disruption — see our [ration shop change guide](/guide/ration-shop-change-transfer) for details

## Important Tips

1. **Update Aadhaar first** — Ensure your Aadhaar card has the new address before applying for ration card address change, as many states verify against Aadhaar
2. **Apply within 30 days** of moving to avoid missing ration distribution cycles
3. **Keep photocopies** of all submitted documents and the acknowledgement receipt
4. **Use ONORC** — While your address change is being processed, you can still buy rations from any FPS using Aadhaar biometric authentication
5. **Interstate transfer** — If you move to a different state, you may need to surrender your old ration card and apply for a new one in the new state

## FAQs

### Can I change my ration card address online?

Yes, most states now offer online address change through their food and civil supplies portal. Visit your state's PDS website to check availability.

### What documents are needed to change address on ration card?

You need your existing ration card, Aadhaar card, and a new address proof such as electricity bill, rent agreement, or voter ID with the new address.

### How long does ration card address change take?

Online applications are usually processed within 7 to 30 days. Offline applications may take 15 to 45 days depending on verification.

### Is there any fee for ration card address change?

Most states offer free online address change. Offline applications may have a nominal fee of ₹2 to ₹25.

### Will my Fair Price Shop change if I update my address?

If you move to a different area, you will be assigned a new FPS near your updated address. Within the same area, your FPS may remain the same.

### Can I buy rations while my address change is being processed?

Yes, under the One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC) scheme, you can buy rations from any FPS in India using Aadhaar-based biometric authentication.

### What if I move to a different state?

For interstate transfers, you typically need to surrender your ration card in the old state and apply for a new one in the new state. Some states have started online interstate transfer under ONORC.
