## What is Haryana Saksham Yuva?

**Saksham Yuva** is the Haryana government scheme providing **monthly financial assistance to unemployed educated youth** of Haryana. The scheme provides both an unemployment allowance and work opportunity (to reduce idleness and build experience).

Key feature: Registered Saksham Yuva are assigned 100 hours of work per month at government offices and receive both the allowance and the work honorarium.

## Benefits

| Component | Monthly Amount |
|---|---|
| **Unemployment allowance** | ₹3,000/month |
| **Work honorarium** (for 100 hours work at govt office) | ₹6,000/month |
| **Total monthly income** | **₹9,000/month** |

The allowance alone (without work) is ₹3,000/month.

## Eligibility

| Criteria | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Age** | 21 to 35 years |
| **Education** | Minimum **graduation** (postgraduate preferred) |
| **Domicile** | Haryana resident (must have Aadhaar/voter ID of Haryana) |
| **Employment** | Unemployed — must not hold any job (government or private) |
| **Income** | Annual family income below ₹3 lakh |
| **Registration** | Must be registered on Haryana Employment Exchange |

## How to Apply

1. Register on **Haryana Employment Exchange** at [hreyahs.gov.in](https://hreyahs.gov.in)
   - This is the employment exchange registration (needed first)
2. Once registered, find **"Saksham Yuva"** on the same portal
3. Apply for Saksham Yuva with:
   - Employment exchange registration number
   - Educational qualification certificates
   - Income certificate (family income < ₹3 lakh)
   - Aadhaar + Haryana domicile proof
4. After approval, you will be assigned to a government office for 100 hours/month
5. Monthly allowance (₹3,000) and honorarium (₹6,000 after work completion) are directly credited to bank account

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card** (with Haryana address)
2. **Graduation degree/marksheet**
3. **Income certificate** (annual family income < ₹3 lakh)
4. **Employment Exchange registration** (from hreyahs.gov.in)
5. **Haryana domicile certificate**
6. **Bank account** (own name, Aadhaar-linked)
7. **Affidavit of unemployment** (that you are not currently employed)

## Work Assignment Under Saksham Yuva

After enrollment, you are assigned to a government department for **100 hours of work per month** (roughly 4-5 hours per working day). Work typically involves:
- Data entry and record management
- Office assistance
- Document scanning/filing
- Municipal/panchayat work

You must physically mark attendance and complete the 100 hours to receive the ₹6,000 honorarium. The allowance of ₹3,000 is paid even without work completion (for those not assigned yet or pending assignment).

## Scheme Duration

Saksham Yuva allowance is typically paid for a **maximum of 3 years** per person or until the person finds a job (whichever is earlier). If you get a job, you must report it and exit the scheme.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I am a postgraduate (MA/MSc). Do I get more than a graduate?
Postgraduate candidates have preferential eligibility and may be assigned to higher-level work. The allowance amount is the same (₹3,000 + ₹6,000 work).

### I am working part-time (freelance). Can I still apply?
Part-time freelance work technically makes you "self-employed" — verify with the Haryana Employment department. Regular part-time jobs likely disqualify you.

### What if I don't want to work 100 hours at a government office?
You can still receive the unemployment allowance (₹3,000/month). The ₹6,000 honorarium is specifically for completing the 100-hour work component. You can opt out of the work component.

### I applied 6 months ago but no work assignment given. Am I still getting the allowance?
Check your bank account — the ₹3,000 allowance should be credited even without a work assignment. Contact the Employment Department at [hreyahs.gov.in](https://hreyahs.gov.in) for work assignment status.

### How long will the scheme run?
The Saksham Yuva scheme has been running since 2016. Check the current year's notification for any changes in amount or eligibility at [hreyahs.gov.in](https://hreyahs.gov.in).
