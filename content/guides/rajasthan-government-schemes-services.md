# Rajasthan Government Schemes and Services – Complete Guide

Rajasthan operates a robust welfare system with innovative schemes like Mukhyamantri Ayushman Arogya (universal health insurance), Shri Annapurna Rasoi (subsidized meals), and the Jan Aadhaar family identity system. After the BJP government took office in December 2023 under CM Bhajan Lal Sharma, several schemes were renamed or revamped. This guide covers every major Rajasthan government scheme across health, education, women's welfare, agriculture, and social security with eligibility, benefits, and application details.

## Key Government Portals in Rajasthan

- **Rajasthan SSO** – [sso.rajasthan.gov.in](https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in/) – Single Sign-On for all government services
- **Jan Aadhaar Portal** – [janaadhaar.rajasthan.gov.in](https://janaadhaar.rajasthan.gov.in/) – Family ID system. See: [Jan Aadhaar Card Rajasthan](/guide/jan-aadhaar-card-rajasthan)
- **e-Mitra** – Online and kiosk-based citizen services
- **Raj Kisan Sathi** – Agriculture information portal
- **Apna Khata** – [apnakhata.raj.nic.in](https://apnakhata.raj.nic.in/) – Land records (Jamabandi, Nakal)

## Health Schemes

### Mukhyamantri Ayushman Arogya Yojana (formerly Chiranjeevi Swasthya Bima Yojana)

Rajasthan's universal health insurance provides cashless treatment up to ₹25 lakh per family per year – one of the highest coverages among Indian states. The scheme was renamed from Chiranjeevi to Mukhyamantri Ayushman Arogya Yojana by the BJP government in 2024.

**Key features:**
- ₹25 lakh annual coverage per family
- Covers 1,576+ medical packages and procedures
- Cashless treatment at 900+ empanelled hospitals
- Free for government employees, BPL, NFSA beneficiaries; nominal premium for others

Read the complete guide: [Chiranjeevi Yojana Rajasthan](/guide/chiranjeevi-yojana-rajasthan)

### Shri Annapurna Rasoi Yojana (formerly Indira Rasoi Yojana)

Subsidized meal scheme providing nutritious food at just ₹8 per meal across Rajasthan's cities and towns. Renamed from Indira Rasoi to Shri Annapurna Rasoi Yojana by the BJP government in January 2024.

**Key features:**
- Lunch and dinner at ₹8 per plate
- 1,000+ Annapurna Rasoi centres across Rajasthan
- Run through NGOs with government subsidy

Read the complete guide: [Indira Rasoi Yojana Rajasthan](/guide/indira-rasoi-yojana-rajasthan)

## Women and Girl Child Schemes

### Lado Protsahan Yojana (formerly Mukhyamantri Rajshri Yojana)

Financial assistance of up to ₹1,50,000 in seven instalments for girl children born in government hospitals, from birth to graduation. The BJP government revamped and renamed the earlier Rajshri Yojana (₹50,000) to Lado Protsahan Yojana with significantly increased benefits.

Read the complete guide: [Rajasthan Mukhyamantri Rajshri Yojana](/guide/rajasthan-mukhyamantri-rajshri-yojana)

### Devnarayan Scooty Scheme

Free scooty for meritorious girl students from backward classes who score 75%+ in class 12.

Read the complete guide: [Rajasthan Devnarayan Scooty Scheme](/guide/rajasthan-devnarayan-scooty-scheme)

## Child Welfare Schemes

### Palanhar Yojana

Monthly financial assistance for orphans and children in vulnerable situations, provided to their guardians (Palanhars).

**Benefits:**
- ₹500/month for children aged 0-5
- ₹1,000/month for school-going children (6-18)
- ₹2,000/year for clothing, shoes, and supplies

Read the complete guide: [Rajasthan Palanhar Yojana](/guide/rajasthan-palanhar-yojana)

## Jan Aadhaar – Family Identity

Jan Aadhaar is Rajasthan's unique family identity system, replacing individual scheme-specific IDs with a single family card linked to all government benefits.

**Uses:**
- Required for applying to most Rajasthan government schemes
- Links to health insurance, ration, pension, and other benefits
- Digital and physical card formats available

Read the complete guide: [Jan Aadhaar Card Rajasthan](/guide/jan-aadhaar-card-rajasthan)

## Social Security and Pension

### Old Age Pension (Rajasthan Vriddhajan Samman)

Monthly pension for senior citizens:
- ₹750/month for ages 55-74 (women) and 58-74 (men)
- ₹1,000/month for ages 75+

### Widow Pension (Ekal Nari Samman)

₹500-1,500/month for widows and single women depending on age.

### Disability Pension

Monthly financial assistance for persons with 40%+ disability.

See: [Old Age Pension Guide](/guide/old-age-pension-vridha-pension-apply) | [Widow Pension Guide](/guide/widow-pension-vidhwa-pension-apply)

## How to Apply for Rajasthan Government Schemes

### Online Application

1. **Register on SSO** – [sso.rajasthan.gov.in](https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in/) using Jan Aadhaar, Aadhaar, or mobile
2. **Select the scheme portal** from the SSO dashboard
3. **Fill application form** with personal and eligibility details
4. **Upload documents** – Jan Aadhaar, Aadhaar, income proof, bank passbook
5. **Submit** and note the application number for tracking

### Offline Application

1. Visit the nearest **e-Mitra centre** or **Block Development Office**
2. Carry Jan Aadhaar card, Aadhaar, and required documents
3. Submit application through the centre operator
4. Collect receipt for tracking

### Common Documents Required

- Jan Aadhaar card (mandatory for most schemes)
- Aadhaar card
- Income certificate
- Caste certificate (for category-based schemes)
- Bank passbook
- Passport-size photographs

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Jan Aadhaar mandatory for Rajasthan schemes?**
Yes, Jan Aadhaar has replaced individual IDs for most Rajasthan government schemes. Apply for it first.

**How do I check Rajasthan scheme application status?**
Log in to SSO portal or the specific scheme website and check using your application number.

**Can I apply for Chiranjeevi health insurance online?**
Yes, through SSO portal or at any e-Mitra centre. Jan Aadhaar is required.
