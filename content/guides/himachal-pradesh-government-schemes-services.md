# Himachal Pradesh Government Schemes and Services – Complete Guide

Himachal Pradesh, known as "Dev Bhoomi" (Land of the Gods), is a hill state in northern India with a population of approximately 73 lakh. Governed by the Indian National Congress under Chief Minister Sukhvinder Singh Sukhu, the state has launched several progressive welfare schemes focusing on women's empowerment, orphan welfare, healthcare, education, and rural development.

This comprehensive guide covers all major Himachal Pradesh government schemes, services, and how to access them online through portals like e-District HP.

## Women and Social Welfare Schemes

### Indira Gandhi Pyari Behna Sukh-Samman Nidhi Yojana

The flagship women's welfare scheme of the Sukhu government, this initiative provides **₹1,500 per month** to eligible women of Himachal Pradesh.

**Key Benefits:**
- Monthly financial assistance of ₹1,500 directly to bank accounts
- Covers women aged 18–59 years
- Recognises women's contribution to household and family economy
- No income tax payee in the family

**How to Apply:** Apply through Lok Mitra Kendra, Atal Seva Kendra, or designated camps organised by the district administration.

### Mukhyamantri Sukh Aashray Yojana

A compassionate scheme for orphans, destitute children, and vulnerable women. The state government acts as the guardian ("Mukhyamantri Parivar") for children who have lost both parents.

**Key Benefits:**
- Complete care, education, and skill development for orphaned children
- Monthly financial support and hostel facilities
- Free coaching for competitive exams
- ₹2 lakh assistance on marriage for girls under the scheme
- Ownership of government-built houses on reaching adulthood
- Job reservation in government vacancies

**Official Portal:** [hpsukhaashray.hp.gov.in](https://hpsukhaashray.hp.gov.in)

### Grihini Suvidha Yojana

Launched to provide **free LPG connections** to women from BPL (Below Poverty Line) families who were left out of the Ujjwala Yojana.

**Key Benefits:**
- Free LPG gas connection to BPL women
- Reduces indoor air pollution from wood/coal burning
- Empowers rural women by saving time on fuel collection

## Education and Scholarship Schemes

### Mukhyamantri Vidyarthi Protsahan Yojana

An education incentive scheme providing financial support to meritorious students from economically weaker sections of Himachal Pradesh.

**Key Benefits:**
- Financial assistance for students at various academic levels
- Encourages higher education among rural and underprivileged students
- Covers tuition fees and related expenses

### Rajiv Gandhi Free Laptop Yojana

Aimed at promoting digital literacy, this scheme provides **free laptops** to meritorious students who pass Class 10 and Class 12 board exams with distinction.

**Key Benefits:**
- Free laptops for high-scoring students in HP Board exams
- Bridges the digital divide in rural Himachal
- Encourages academic excellence

**Apply through:** Directorate of Higher Education, Shimla — [education.hp.gov.in](https://education.hp.gov.in)

### Mukhyamantri Sukh Shiksha Yojana

Linked to the Sukh Aashray initiative, this scheme covers complete education expenses for orphaned and vulnerable children adopted under the Mukhyamantri Parivar.

**Key Benefits:**
- Free education from primary to postgraduate level
- Books, uniforms, and hostel fees covered
- Coaching support for professional entrance exams

### Medha Protsahan Yojana

A coaching assistance scheme for students preparing for national-level competitive exams like UPSC, JEE, NEET, and CLAT.

**Key Benefits:**
- Up to ₹1 lakh financial support for coaching
- Available for students from families with annual income below ₹2.50 lakh
- Covers coaching at reputed institutes across India

## Health Schemes

### Himcare Health Scheme

Himachal Pradesh's own health insurance scheme providing **cashless treatment up to ₹5 lakh per family per year** in empanelled hospitals.

**Key Benefits:**
- Coverage of ₹5 lakh per family per annum
- Covers hospitalisation, surgeries, and critical illnesses
- Available to all HP residents not covered under Ayushman Bharat
- Nominal annual premium (₹365–₹1,000 based on category)
- Cashless treatment in 200+ empanelled hospitals

**Enrolment:** [himcare.hp.gov.in](https://himcare.hp.gov.in) or Common Service Centres

### Sahara Yojana

A unique healthcare scheme providing **monthly financial assistance to patients suffering from serious diseases** who require long-term treatment.

**Key Benefits:**
- ₹3,000 per month to patients with serious illnesses (cancer, paralysis, Parkinson's, muscular dystrophy, thalassemia, haemophilia, renal failure)
- Financial support during extended treatment and recovery
- Available to BPL and low-income families

**Apply through:** Chief Medical Officer (CMO) of the concerned district.

## Housing and Urban Development

### Mukhyamantri Awas Yojana (HP)

State housing scheme providing financial assistance for construction of pucca houses for homeless and below poverty line families.

**Key Benefits:**
- Financial assistance for new house construction
- Priority to SC/ST, widows, and differently-abled families
- Convergence with Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) for enhanced benefits

### Him Pragati

A flagship scheme to promote **start-ups and entrepreneurship** among the youth of Himachal Pradesh by providing subsidised loans and capital support.

**Key Benefits:**
- Loan subsidy for setting up new businesses
- Special focus on youth, women, and SC/ST entrepreneurs
- Covers manufacturing, services, and tourism-related businesses
- Mentoring and hand-holding support

## Agriculture and Horticulture

### Apple and Horticulture Development Schemes

Himachal Pradesh is India's leading apple-producing state, and the government runs several schemes to support horticulture:

**Key Schemes:**
- **Prakritik Kheti Khushhal Kisan Yojana** — Promotes natural/zero-budget farming with subsidies and training
- **HP Horticulture Development Project** — Infrastructure support for apple, cherry, kiwi, and other fruit growers
- **Anti-Hail Net Subsidy** — Up to 80% subsidy on anti-hail nets to protect apple orchards
- **Cold Chain and Pack House** — Subsidies for cold storage and pack house infrastructure

**Key Benefits:**
- Subsidised planting material and drip irrigation
- Training in modern horticulture techniques
- Market linkage and brand support for Himachali apples
- Insurance coverage for horticulture crops

### MNREGA Plus (Mukhyamantri MNREGA Plus Yojana)

An enhancement over the central MGNREGA scheme, providing **additional wage days and kitchen garden support** to rural families.

**Key Benefits:**
- Extra employment days beyond the central 100-day guarantee
- Women with land up to 1 bigha can grow backyard kitchen gardens
- Convergence with horticulture and agriculture departments
- Focuses on asset creation in rural areas

## How to Apply for HP Government Schemes

Most Himachal Pradesh government schemes can be accessed through these channels:

1. **Online Portals** — e-District HP, Himcare portal, and scheme-specific websites
2. **Lok Mitra Kendra / Atal Seva Kendra** — Government facilitation centres in every block
3. **Common Service Centres (CSC)** — For digital-first applications
4. **District Offices** — DC office, BDO office, and tehsil offices
5. **Camp Mode** — The government organises periodic camps for scheme enrolment (especially for women's schemes and Sukh Aashray)

### Documents Commonly Required:
- Aadhaar Card
- Himachal Pradesh Domicile / Bonafide Certificate
- BPL Ration Card (for BPL schemes)
- Bank Passbook (for DBT-linked schemes)
- Income Certificate
- Caste Certificate (for SC/ST/OBC category schemes)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for Indira Gandhi Pyari Behna Sukh-Samman Nidhi?
Women aged 18–59 years who are residents of Himachal Pradesh and whose family does not pay income tax are eligible. The scheme provides ₹1,500 per month directly to the beneficiary's bank account.

### How can I enrol in Himcare health scheme?
Visit [himcare.hp.gov.in](https://himcare.hp.gov.in) or any Common Service Centre in HP. You need Aadhaar card and a family photo. The annual premium ranges from ₹365 to ₹1,000 depending on your category.

### What benefits does Mukhyamantri Sukh Aashray Yojana provide?
Orphans and destitute children get complete care including education, hostel, skill development, ₹2 lakh marriage assistance for girls, government-built house ownership, and priority in government jobs.

### How do I apply for certificates on e-District HP?
Visit [edistrict.hp.gov.in](https://edistrict.hp.gov.in), register with your mobile number, select the certificate type, fill the form, upload documents, and submit. You can track the application status online.

### Is Sahara Yojana available for all diseases?
No, Sahara Yojana covers specific serious diseases including cancer, paralysis, Parkinson's disease, muscular dystrophy, thalassemia, haemophilia, and renal failure. Patients must apply through the Chief Medical Officer of their district.

### Can I get both Himcare and Ayushman Bharat benefits?
Himcare is designed for residents not covered under Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY). If you are already an Ayushman Bharat beneficiary, you are covered under that scheme. Others can enrol in Himcare for similar cashless treatment benefits.

### What is Him Pragati scheme?
Him Pragati is an entrepreneurship and start-up promotion scheme providing subsidised loans to youth, women, and SC/ST entrepreneurs for setting up businesses in Himachal Pradesh across manufacturing, services, and tourism sectors.
