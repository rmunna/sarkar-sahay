# Ration Card Name Not Updated: How to Fix

Having an incorrect name on your ration card can prevent you from receiving subsidized food grains under the National Food Security Act (NFSA) and other government benefits. Many families face issues where name correction requests remain pending for months or get rejected. This guide covers all reasons for name update failures and provides practical solutions.

## Common Symptoms

- Name correction application submitted but status shows **"Pending"** for months
- Name update **rejected** without clear reason
- Online portal shows **"Application under process"** indefinitely
- Name updated online but **physical ration card** still shows old name
- **eKYC/Aadhaar seeding failed** due to name mismatch between ration card and Aadhaar
- Incorrect name causing **biometric authentication failure** at ration shop
- Family member's name **not appearing** on the ration card
- Name shows as **"Unknown" or garbled text** on the ration card

---

## Why Ration Card Name Updates Fail

### 1. Name Mismatch with Aadhaar

Since ration cards are now linked with Aadhaar under NFSA, the name on your ration card must match your Aadhaar. If there's a discrepancy, the system may reject the update or fail during eKYC verification.

### 2. Incomplete Application or Missing Documents

Name correction applications require specific supporting documents. Missing or incorrect documents lead to rejection.

### 3. State Portal Technical Issues

Ration card management is handled by individual states. Many state portals have technical issues, downtime, or slow processing.

### 4. Pending Verification by Supply Inspector

Name changes require verification by the local Supply Inspector or Food Inspector. If they haven't visited or processed the request, it remains pending.

### 5. Duplicate or Ghost Entries

Your name may be linked to duplicate entries in the NFSA database, preventing updates until the duplicates are resolved.

### 6. Administrative Backlog

In many states, there's a significant backlog of ration card update requests, especially after Aadhaar seeding drives.

## Step-by-Step Troubleshooting

### Step 1: Check Application Status

1. Visit your **state food department portal** — each state has a different website:
   - **UP:** fcs.up.gov.in
   - **Bihar:** epds.bihar.gov.in
   - **Maharashtra:** mahafood.gov.in
   - **Rajasthan:** food.rajasthan.gov.in
   - **Tamil Nadu:** www.tnpds.gov.in
   - **Others:** Search "[your state] ration card portal"
2. Log in with your ration card number or Aadhaar
3. Check the application status for your name correction request
4. Note the **reason for rejection** if applicable

### Step 2: Ensure Aadhaar Name Matches

Before applying for ration card name correction:

1. Verify your name in Aadhaar at [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/)
2. If Aadhaar name is incorrect, fix it first — see [Aadhaar name correction guide](/guide/aadhaar-name-correction-online)
3. The name you want on the ration card should **exactly match** your Aadhaar name
4. This ensures eKYC/Aadhaar seeding works smoothly after the update

### Step 3: Gather Required Documents

For ration card name correction, you typically need:

- **Application form** (state-specific — download from your state portal)
- **Aadhaar card** of the person whose name needs correction
- **Existing ration card** (photocopy)
- **Supporting document** for correct name: marriage certificate, school certificate, passport, or court affidavit for name change
- **Passport-size photograph**
- **Self-declaration** or affidavit (some states require this)

### Step 4: Apply Online (Where Available)

Many states now allow online name corrections:

1. Visit your state's ration card portal
2. Log in or register
3. Select **"Ration Card Correction/Modification"** or **"Member Details Correction"**
4. Enter the corrected name and upload supporting documents
5. Submit and note the **application/reference number**
6. Track status regularly

### Step 5: Apply Offline at Taluk/Block Supply Office

If online application isn't available or has been rejected:

1. Visit your **Taluk Supply Officer (TSO)** or **Block Supply Officer's** office
2. Submit the application form with all required documents
3. Request an **acknowledgement receipt** with date stamp
4. Ask about the expected processing time (typically 15–30 days)
5. Follow up after the expected period

### Step 6: Contact the Supply Inspector

If your application is pending verification:

1. Find your **designated Supply Inspector** — your ration shop dealer can provide this information
2. Call or visit the Supply Inspector's office
3. Request them to complete the verification and forward the application
4. If the inspector is unresponsive, escalate to the **District Supply Officer (DSO)**

### Step 7: Resolve eKYC/Aadhaar Seeding Issues

If the name mismatch is causing Aadhaar seeding failure at the ration shop:

1. Visit your ration shop during distribution hours
2. Ask the dealer to check your **eKYC status** on the ePoS device
3. If eKYC fails due to name mismatch, the dealer can report it to the supply office
4. You may need to complete eKYC at the **Taluk/Block office** directly using biometric authentication
5. Ensure your [Aadhaar biometrics are unlocked](/guide/aadhaar-biometric-lock-unlock) during this process

## When to Contact Helpline

Contact if:
- Application pending for over 60 days with no update
- Name correction rejected without valid reason
- You're being denied ration due to name mismatch despite applying for correction
- Aadhaar seeding fails repeatedly at the ration shop

**Contact Options:**
- **National Food Security Helpline:** 1967 (toll-free) or 1800-233-0071
- **State food department** — contact numbers available on state portals
- **District Supply Officer (DSO)** — visit the district collectorate
- **CM Helpline** — most states have a CM helpline for grievances
- **Online grievance:** [pgportal.gov.in](https://pgportal.gov.in/) under Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution
- **NFSA portal:** [nfsa.gov.in](https://nfsa.gov.in/) for checking entitlements

## Prevention Tips

1. **Keep names consistent** across Aadhaar and ration card from the start
2. **Complete Aadhaar seeding** promptly when your state conducts seeding drives
3. **Update ration card immediately** after marriage, name change, or when adding/removing family members
4. **Keep copies** of all submitted applications and acknowledgement receipts
5. **Verify your ration card details** online periodically — check for data entry errors
6. **Link Aadhaar of all family members** to the ration card to avoid future distribution issues
7. **Report to the DSO** if your ration shop dealer refuses to provide rations due to minor mismatches — the dealer should report the issue, not deny rations

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does ration card name correction take?

Processing time varies by state — typically **15–45 days** for online applications and **30–60 days** for offline applications. During Aadhaar seeding drives, processing may be faster.

### Can I get ration if my name is wrong on the ration card?

You should still receive ration if your Aadhaar biometric authentication succeeds at the ration shop, even with a minor name mismatch. However, if eKYC fails due to the mismatch, the dealer may not be able to issue rations. Apply for correction immediately and escalate if rations are denied.

### Is there a fee for ration card name correction?

In most states, ration card corrections are **free of charge**. Some states may charge a nominal fee of ₹5–₹25 for processing. No unofficial charges should be accepted.

### How do I add a new family member to my ration card?

Visit your state's ration card portal or the Taluk/Block Supply Office. Submit an application for **member addition** with the new member's Aadhaar, age proof, and relationship proof. The process is similar to name correction.

### My ration card shows "Aadhaar seeding failed." What does this mean?

Aadhaar seeding failure means the system couldn't link your Aadhaar with your ration card entry, usually due to name mismatch, incorrect Aadhaar number, or biometric issues. Correct the name mismatch first, then complete seeding at your Taluk/Block Supply Office.

### Can I correct my ration card name online from any state?

The online process varies by state. Most major states (UP, Bihar, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, etc.) offer online correction. For states without online facilities, you must visit the supply office in person.
