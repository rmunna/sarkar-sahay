# Ration Card Duplicate Apply: Lost or Damaged Card Fix

If your ration card is lost, stolen, or damaged beyond use, you can apply for a duplicate ration card. The process involves filing a police complaint (FIR) for lost cards and submitting an application through your state's Food & Civil Supplies Department. This guide covers both online and offline methods.

## When Can You Apply for a Duplicate Ration Card?

You can apply for a duplicate ration card in these situations:

- **Lost ration card** — misplaced, stolen, or destroyed in a natural disaster
- **Damaged ration card** — torn, faded, water-damaged, or illegible
- **Mutilated card** — partially destroyed where key details are unreadable

> **Note:** If you need to update details on your existing card (name, address, members), use the [correction process](/guide/ration-card-correction-online) instead of applying for a duplicate.

## Step 1: File an FIR (For Lost Cards Only)

If your ration card is **lost or stolen**, you must first file a police report:

### How to File FIR

1. Visit the nearest **Police Station** and file a **First Information Report (FIR)** or **Non-Cognizable Report (NCR)** stating that your ration card is lost.
2. Alternatively, file an **online FIR** through your state's police portal (available in most states).
3. Get a **copy of the FIR/NCR** — you will need this for the duplicate application.

### What to Mention in FIR

- Your full name and address
- Ration card number (if you remember)
- Approximate date and place of loss
- Category of ration card (AAY/PHH/NPHH)

> For **damaged cards**, an FIR is not required. You need to submit the damaged card along with your application.

## Step 2: Apply for Duplicate Ration Card

### Online Process (Available in Select States)

1. Visit your state's Food & Civil Supplies portal.
2. Log in or register using your Aadhaar/mobile number.
3. Select **"Apply for Duplicate Ration Card"** or **"Duplicate RC"** option.
4. Fill in the application form with your ration card details.
5. Upload required documents (FIR copy, Aadhaar, affidavit).
6. Pay the application fee online (if applicable).
7. Submit and note down the **application/acknowledgment number**.

### Offline Process

1. Visit the **Taluk Supply Office** or **Block Development Office** (BDO) in your area.
2. Collect the **duplicate ration card application form** (or download from state portal).
3. Fill in the form with your details — name, address, ration card number, reason for duplicate.
4. Attach all required documents.
5. Submit the form at the office counter.
6. Collect the **acknowledgment receipt** with application number.
7. Track status online or by visiting the office.

## Documents Required

| Document | For Lost Card | For Damaged Card |
|----------|:------------:|:---------------:|
| FIR/NCR copy from police | ✅ Required | ❌ Not needed |
| Damaged ration card (original) | ❌ Not applicable | ✅ Required |
| Aadhaar card of head of family | ✅ Required | ✅ Required |
| Address proof (utility bill, voter ID) | ✅ Required | ✅ Required |
| Affidavit/self-declaration on stamp paper | ✅ Required | ✅ Required |
| Passport-size photographs (2-3) | ✅ Required | ✅ Required |
| Ration card number (if available) | ✅ Recommended | ✅ Available on card |
| Income certificate (in some states) | Varies by state | Varies by state |

## Fee for Duplicate Ration Card

| State | Fee (Approximate) |
|-------|-------------------|
| Most states | ₹5 to ₹45 |
| Some states (e.g., Kerala) | Free for BPL/AAY families |
| Natural disaster victims | Generally free |

> Fees vary by state and ration card category. BPL and AAY families may get fee waivers in most states.

## Processing Time

| Stage | Duration |
|-------|----------|
| FIR filing | Same day |
| Application submission | Same day |
| Verification and processing | 15-30 days |
| Duplicate card issuance | 30-45 days |

> In case of natural disasters, states often issue duplicate cards on a fast-track basis within 7-15 days.

## Important Tips

1. **File the FIR immediately** after discovering the loss — it serves as legal proof and prevents misuse of your ration card.
2. Keep a **photocopy or photo** of your ration card on your phone for emergencies.
3. Download your [e-Ration Card](/guide/ration-card-download-online) as backup — you can continue using it at Fair Price Shops even while the duplicate is being processed.
4. The duplicate ration card will have the **same number and details** as your original card.
5. If you remember your ration card number, your records are safe in the government database — only the physical card needs replacement.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I get rations while my duplicate card is being processed?
Yes. If your Aadhaar is linked to your ration card, you can collect rations using Aadhaar-based biometric authentication at Fair Price Shops even without the physical card.

### Is FIR mandatory for a duplicate ration card?
FIR is required only for **lost or stolen** cards. For damaged cards, you submit the damaged card instead.

### Can I apply for a duplicate ration card online?
Many states now offer online application. Check your state's Food & Civil Supplies portal. If not available online, visit the Taluk/Block Supply Office.

### What if I don't remember my ration card number?
Visit the Taluk Supply Office with your Aadhaar card. They can look up your ration card number from the database using your Aadhaar or family details.

### Will the duplicate card have a new number?
No. The duplicate ration card will retain your **original ration card number** and all existing details.

### How much does a duplicate ration card cost?
The fee ranges from ₹5 to ₹45 in most states. BPL/AAY families and disaster-affected families may get it free.
