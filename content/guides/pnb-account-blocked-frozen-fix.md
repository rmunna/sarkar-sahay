# PNB (Punjab National Bank) Account Blocked or Frozen — How to Fix It (2026)

If your Punjab National Bank account shows "Account Blocked", "Debit Freeze", "Account Restricted" or you cannot withdraw money, use PNB ONE app or access net banking — this guide explains exactly how to get it unblocked.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is independent and not affiliated with PNB. For account-specific help, contact Punjab National Bank directly.

---

## Why Is Your PNB Account Blocked?

| Reason | Explanation |
|--------|-------------|
| **KYC Not Updated** | RBI mandates periodic KYC. Non-updated accounts face partial or full freeze |
| **Aadhaar / PAN Not Linked** | Mandatory RBI requirement |
| **Too Many Failed Login Attempts** | 3–5 wrong password attempts block PNB ONE / internet banking |
| **Suspicious Transactions** | Large/unusual transactions trigger fraud detection |
| **Income Tax Department Order** | Tax freeze for pending demands |
| **Dormant Account** | No transactions for 2+ years → dormant status |
| **OBC / Oriental Bank Migration** | Incomplete KYC from the 2020 merger (OBC, Oriental Bank → PNB) |

---

## Fix 1: KYC Freeze

**Via PNB ONE App:**
1. Open **PNB ONE** app
2. Look for a KYC Update notification on home screen
3. Complete the KYC update process (upload Aadhaar + PAN)
4. Submit — takes 1–3 working days

**Via PNB Internet Banking:**
1. Go to **[pnbindia.in](https://www.pnbindia.in)** → Internet Banking
2. Navigate to **Services → KYC Update**
3. Upload Aadhaar, PAN and address proof
4. Submit for verification

**At Branch:**
1. Visit your **home branch** with original Aadhaar + PAN
2. Fill KYC Update form
3. Submit — account unblocked in 1–3 working days

---

## Fix 2: PNB ONE App / Net Banking Blocked (Wrong Password)

**Net Banking Password Reset:**
1. Go to **[pnbindia.in](https://www.pnbindia.in)** → Internet Banking Login
2. Click **"Forgot Login Password"**
3. Enter Customer ID and registered mobile number
4. Verify via OTP
5. Set new password — access restored immediately

**PNB ONE App MPIN Reset:**
1. Open PNB ONE app → **"Forgot MPIN"**
2. Verify using OTP + debit card details
3. Set new MPIN

If reinstall needed:
1. Uninstall → Reinstall PNB ONE
2. Re-register with account number + registered mobile + debit card details

---

## Fix 3: Suspicious Activity Block

1. Call **PNB Customer Care: 1800-180-2222** (toll-free, 24×7) or **1800-103-2222**
2. Explain your account is blocked
3. Complete phone identity verification (OTP + security questions)
4. Request temporary unblock for urgent needs
5. For permanent unblock, visit home branch with Aadhaar + PAN + written explanation

---

## Fix 4: Income Tax Department Freeze

1. Log into **[incometax.gov.in](https://www.incometax.gov.in)** → Pending Actions → Outstanding Demand
2. Pay demand or file rectification
3. Obtain defreeze confirmation from IT Assessing Officer
4. Submit to PNB home branch
5. Branch processes in 3–7 working days

---

## Fix 5: Dormant Account Reactivation

1. Visit PNB home branch with Aadhaar + PAN (originals) + 2 photos
2. Fill **Account Reactivation Form**
3. Make a small cash deposit
4. Reactivated in 1–3 working days

## Frequently Asked Questions

**My PNB account was originally an OBC or Oriental Bank account — why is it frozen?**
PNB merged with Oriental Bank of Commerce and United Bank of India in April 2020. Some legacy accounts had incomplete KYC migration. Visit your nearest PNB branch (your former OBC/Oriental branch) with original Aadhaar + PAN to complete KYC update.

**Is my money safe when PNB freezes my account?**
Yes — your balance is completely safe. Freeze restricts transactions only. DICGC insurance covers up to ₹5 lakh.

**Will EMI auto-debits fail during a PNB account freeze?**
Yes — EMI, SIP, insurance auto-debits will fail during debit freeze. Inform lenders/insurers immediately.

**How to escalate if PNB doesn't resolve?**
File a complaint at [pnbindia.in/grievance](https://www.pnbindia.in) or contact the Banking Ombudsman at [bankingombudsman.rbi.org.in](https://bankingombudsman.rbi.org.in).
