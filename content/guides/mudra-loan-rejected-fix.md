# Mudra Loan Rejected? How to Fix and Reapply for Mudra Yojana

Getting your [Mudra loan application](/guide/mudra-loan-apply-online) rejected is frustrating — but it's not the end. Most rejections happen due to fixable issues like incomplete documents, low CIBIL score, or unclear business plans. This guide explains the top reasons for rejection and exactly how to fix each one before reapplying.

---

## Top Reasons Why Mudra Loans Get Rejected

### 1. Low CIBIL Score

**The problem:** Banks check your credit history before approving any loan. A CIBIL score below 600 often leads to automatic rejection, especially for [Kishore and Tarun](/guide/mudra-loan-types-shishu-kishore-tarun) categories.

**How to fix:**
- Check your CIBIL report at [cibil.com](https://www.cibil.com/) — it's free once a year
- Dispute any errors in your report
- Pay off outstanding credit card dues and EMIs
- Wait 3–6 months for your score to improve, then reapply
- For Shishu loans (up to ₹50,000), CIBIL requirements are relaxed — try applying for a smaller amount

### 2. Incomplete or Incorrect Documents

**The problem:** Missing documents is the most common reason for rejection. Banks cannot process applications without proper identity, address, and business proof.

**How to fix:**
- Review the complete [documents checklist](/guide/mudra-loan-documents-required)
- Ensure all documents are valid and not expired
- Get documents attested/self-attested as required
- Submit clear photocopies — blurred or damaged copies cause issues

### 3. No Business Registration or Proof

**The problem:** Banks need to verify that your business actually exists. Without any registration, they may reject the application.

**How to fix:**
- Get [Udyam Registration](/guide/msme-udyam-registration) — it's free and online
- Obtain GST registration if your turnover requires it
- Get a Shop and Establishment Act licence from your local municipality
- Have a trade licence or Gumasta licence ready

### 4. Weak or Missing Business Plan

**The problem:** For Kishore (₹50,001–₹5 lakh) and Tarun (₹5–₹10 lakh) loans, banks require a business plan. Vague or unrealistic plans lead to rejection.

**How to fix:**
- Write a clear 2–3 page business plan covering:
  - What your business does
  - Target market and customers
  - How you will use the loan funds
  - Expected revenue and profit
  - Repayment plan
- Include realistic financial projections
- Show existing revenue if your business is already running

### 5. Existing Loan Defaults or NPAs

**The problem:** If you have unpaid loans or are flagged as a Non-Performing Asset (NPA) in any bank, your Mudra loan will be rejected.

**How to fix:**
- Clear all outstanding dues
- Get a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the previous lender
- Wait for your CIBIL report to update (takes 30–45 days after clearance)
- Approach a different bank with proof of settlement

### 6. Business Not in Eligible Category

**The problem:** Mudra loans are only for non-farm, non-corporate micro/small enterprises. Agricultural crop loans, large corporates, and certain restricted activities are excluded.

**How to fix:**
- Verify your business falls under manufacturing, trading, services, or allied agricultural activities (like food processing, agro-related services)
- If you're in agriculture, look at Kisan Credit Card or NABARD schemes instead

### 7. Insufficient Bank Transaction History

**The problem:** Banks want to see regular transactions in your account — it shows business activity. Empty or newly opened accounts raise red flags.

**How to fix:**
- Maintain a current/savings account with regular deposits and withdrawals for at least 6 months
- Route all business transactions through your bank account
- Avoid large unexplained cash deposits

### 8. Bank-Level Issues

**The problem:** Sometimes the rejection is not your fault. The branch may have exhausted its Mudra loan allocation, or the officer may not be cooperative.

**How to fix:**
- Apply at a different branch of the same bank
- Apply at a different bank altogether
- Use online portals like [Udyami Mitra](https://udyamimitra.in/) where multiple lenders compete for your application

## How to Reapply for Mudra Loan

### Step 1: Get the Rejection Reason in Writing

Ask the bank to provide a written reason for rejection. Banks are required to give this under RBI guidelines. If they refuse, escalate (see below).

### Step 2: Fix the Issue

Address the specific reason for rejection using the fixes listed above. Don't reapply with the same application — it will get rejected again.

### Step 3: Wait the Right Amount of Time

- **Document issues:** Reapply as soon as documents are ready
- **CIBIL score:** Wait 3–6 months after improving your score
- **NPA/default:** Wait until settlement reflects in CIBIL (30–45 days)

### Step 4: Apply at a Different Bank or Portal

If one bank rejected you, try:
- A different public sector bank
- A Small Finance Bank (they are more micro-enterprise friendly)
- An NBFC or MFI
- Online via [Udyami Mitra](https://udyamimitra.in/) for multiple lender options

### Step 5: Consider Starting with Shishu

If you were rejected for a Kishore or Tarun loan, apply for a [Shishu loan](/guide/mudra-loan-types-shishu-kishore-tarun) (up to ₹50,000) first. Build a repayment track record, then apply for a larger amount.

## How to Complain About Unfair Mudra Loan Rejection

If you believe your Mudra loan was unfairly rejected:

### Level 1: Branch Manager

Write a formal complaint to the Branch Manager of the bank where you applied. Request a review of your application.

### Level 2: Bank's Grievance Cell

- Visit the bank's website and find the **"Grievances"** or **"Complaints"** section
- File a written complaint with your application details
- Banks must respond within 30 days

### Level 3: MUDRA Helpline

- Email: **help@mudra.org.in**
- Phone: Check [mudra.org.in](https://www.mudra.org.in/) for current helpline numbers
- Describe your issue with application reference number

### Level 4: RBI Banking Ombudsman

- File a complaint at [cms.rbi.org.in](https://cms.rbi.org.in/)
- This is effective when banks are unresponsive or unfairly rejecting valid applications
- The Ombudsman can direct the bank to reconsider

### Level 5: CPGRAMS

- For government bank complaints, use [pgportal.gov.in](https://pgportal.gov.in/)
- Complaints are tracked and forwarded to the relevant ministry

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I reapply for Mudra loan after rejection?

Yes. There is no restriction on reapplying. However, fix the reason for rejection first — otherwise you'll get rejected again and it will create multiple hard inquiries on your CIBIL report.

### How many times can I apply for Mudra loan?

There is no limit on the number of applications. You can apply at multiple banks simultaneously through the Udyami Mitra portal.

### Will Mudra loan rejection affect my CIBIL score?

The rejection itself does not affect your score. However, each loan application creates a "hard inquiry" on your CIBIL report. Too many inquiries in a short period can lower your score slightly.

### Can I get a Mudra loan with a CIBIL score of 500?

It's difficult for Kishore and Tarun categories. For **Shishu loans** (up to ₹50,000), some banks and MFIs are more flexible with credit scores. Try Small Finance Banks or MFIs for better chances.

### The bank is asking for collateral for Mudra loan. Is this correct?

No. Mudra loans up to ₹10 lakh are **collateral-free** under PMMY guidelines. If a bank demands collateral, refuse and file a complaint with the [RBI Banking Ombudsman](https://cms.rbi.org.in/).

### Can I apply for Mudra loan from a bank where I don't have an account?

Yes, but having an existing account with the bank improves your chances. The bank can see your transaction history and is more likely to approve.

### My Mudra loan was rejected without giving a reason. What should I do?

Under RBI guidelines, banks must provide reasons for loan rejection. Write to the Branch Manager requesting the rejection reason in writing. If they don't respond, escalate to the RBI Banking Ombudsman.
