## What is a Common Service Centre (CSC)?

A **Common Service Centre (CSC)** — also called **Jan Seva Kendra** — is a government-authorized digital centre that provides over **300 government and private services** to citizens, especially in rural and semi-urban areas.

Run by a **Village Level Entrepreneur (VLE)**, CSCs are the government's front-line delivery point for e-governance services in areas where citizens may not have easy internet or computer access.

There are over **5.5 lakh CSCs** across India — one in almost every panchayat.

## Services Available at CSC

### Government Certificates & Documents
- **Aadhaar** — enrolment, update, correction, SSUP
- **PAN card** — new application, correction
- **Passport** — Seva Kendra application assistance
- **Voter ID** — enrolment, correction (EPIC)
- **Birth/death certificate** — state portal applications
- **Caste certificate, income certificate, domicile** — state service portals
- **Land records** — RoR/khasra/nakal from state portals

### Banking & Financial Services
- **Banking Correspondent** — cash deposits, withdrawals (for rural banking)
- **Jan Dhan account** opening assistance
- **PMJJBY, PMSBY, APY** insurance premium payment
- **PM Kisan** registration, payment status
- **DigiLocker** assistance

### Welfare Scheme Applications
- **PM Awas Yojana** application assistance
- **Pension schemes** — NSAP, state pensions — application support
- **Scholarship applications** — NSP portal
- **Kisan Credit Card** — application assistance

### Education & Skill Development
- **CSC Academy** — online courses and certifications
- **PMeVIDYA** — school education content
- **Skill India** courses — assisted enrollment

### Health Services
- **ABHA Health ID** creation
- **Ayushman Bharat** card printing and checking
- **Telemedicine** — doctor consultation at some CSCs

### Other Services
- **GST Suvidha** — GST filing for small businesses
- **e-Stamping** — stamp paper
- **Insurance** — LIC, health, vehicle insurance premium collection
- **Income tax return** filing (assisted)
- **Electricity bill** payment

## How to Find the Nearest CSC

1. Go to **[locator.csccloud.in](https://locator.csccloud.in)** (official CSC locator)
2. Enter your **state, district, block**
3. See a map with nearby CSC locations
4. Call the VLE or visit during operating hours

OR Google **"CSC near me"** or **"Jan Seva Kendra near me"**

## Service Charges at CSC

CSC services are **not free** — VLEs charge a service fee. Charges are regulated:
- Government services: ₹10–₹100 typically
- Complex services (passport, driving license): ₹100–₹500
- Banking correspondent: ₹10–₹25 per transaction

The government fixes rates for most services — excessive charging should be reported.

## How to Become a CSC VLE (Operator)

If you want to open a CSC in your area:

1. Go to **[register.csc.gov.in](https://register.csc.gov.in)**
2. Apply as a **Village Level Entrepreneur (VLE)**
3. Eligibility: 18 years+, Class 10 pass, own computer/laptop + internet connection
4. After verification, you receive a CSC ID and access to the service portal

CSC VLEs earn commissions on each service transaction — a potential income of ₹10,000–₹30,000/month depending on location and services offered.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can any service be done at CSC for a fee, or are there official fixed charges?
Most services have government-fixed rates published on the CSC portal. For services like Aadhaar update, passport assistance, or PAN application, there are standard fees. Ask for a receipt — it's your proof of payment.

### My CSC is closed or the VLE is not cooperative. What to do?
You can find another CSC nearby via the locator. To report issues, call the **CSC helpdesk: 1800-121-3468** (toll-free) or email **helpdesk@csc.gov.in**.

### Can I apply for PM Kisan status correction or eKYC at CSC?
Yes — PM Kisan eKYC (using Aadhaar OTP or biometric) can be done at CSC. They can also assist with beneficiary status correction if you provide the correct documents.

### Are CSC and Jan Seva Kendra the same thing?
Yes — Common Service Centre (CSC) is the official name; Jan Seva Kendra, Pragya Kendra, or Meeseva (in AP) are state-specific names for the same type of facility.

### I live in a city. Is CSC still useful?
Urban areas may have fewer CSCs since residents have direct internet access. However, CSCs in semi-urban areas still offer useful assisted services. Alternatively, all services offered at CSC can be done directly online via respective government portals.
