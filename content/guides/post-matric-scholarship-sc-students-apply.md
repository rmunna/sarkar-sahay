## What is Post Matric Scholarship for SC Students?

**Post Matric Scholarship for Scheduled Caste (SC) Students** is a centrally sponsored scheme by the **Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment** (Government of India) that provides financial assistance to SC students pursuing education **after Class 10 (post-matriculation)**. This includes Class 11, 12, undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, and professional courses.

The scheme covers:
- **Full tuition fee** reimbursement
- **Maintenance allowance** for monthly living expenses
- **Additional allowances** for books, study materials

## Who is Eligible?

- Must belong to **Scheduled Caste (SC)**
- Annual family income must be **below ₹2.5 lakh**
- Must be **post-matriculation level** (Class 11 and above) — government or approved private institution
- Not availing any other government scholarship for the same course
- Should not have failed or have a gap year in studies (unless with valid reason)

## Scholarship Amount

**Maintenance Allowance (per month):**

| Course Level | Hosteller | Day Scholar |
|---|---|---|
| Class 11 and 12 (Group III) | ₹380 | ₹230 |
| Undergraduate non-professional (Group III) | ₹380 | ₹230 |
| Undergraduate professional (Group II) — B.Tech, MBBS, etc. | ₹570 | ₹300 |
| Postgraduate (Group I) | ₹1,200 | ₹550 |

**Plus:**
- **Full tuition fee** — paid directly to institution
- **Books/stationery allowance:** ₹1,000 (Group I/II) to ₹500 (Group III)
- **Study tour, thesis, equipment allowances** — for eligible courses

## How to Apply — Step by Step

### New Application

1. Go to **[scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in)** (National Scholarship Portal)
2. Click **"New Registration"**
3. Select **"State → Scholarship"** — choose your state and then **"Post Matric Scholarship for SC"**
4. Fill in personal details, category (SC), income details, institution details
5. Upload documents (see below)
6. Submit → note Application ID

> **Important:** Apply early — most states have October–November deadlines for the current academic year.

### Renewal (Continuing Students)

1. Log in to [scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in) with existing credentials
2. Click **"Renewal Application"**
3. Verify / update details (new year marksheet, institution details)
4. Submit renewal before deadline

## Documents Required

1. **Caste certificate** — SC certificate from competent state authority (Tehsildar/SDM)
2. **Income certificate** — from competent authority (annual family income below ₹2.5L)
3. **Aadhaar card** of student
4. **Previous year marksheet** (for renewal) / Class 10 marksheet (for fresh applicants)
5. **Admission proof / bonafide certificate** from institution
6. **Bank passbook** (student's own account — Aadhaar-linked for DBT)
7. **Fee receipt** from institution (current year)
8. Passport-size photograph

## State-Specific Implementation

The scheme is centrally funded but **implemented by state governments**. This means:
- Each state has its **own portal/window** within NSP
- Deadline and processing time varies by state
- Payment comes from your **state government's social welfare department**

States like UP, MP, Rajasthan, Bihar, Maharashtra have large SC beneficiary counts. Check your state government's scholarship portal for state-specific forms or portals running parallel to NSP.

## Check Payment Status

1. Log in to [scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in)
2. Go to **"Track Status"** → enter application ID
3. Or check your bank account — DBT transfers show as "SC PMS [state]" or similar

## Common Issues and Solutions

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Application rejected — income limit | Ensure income certificate is fresh (current year) and below ₹2.5L |
| Institution not listed in NSP | Contact your state education/social welfare dept to get institution registered |
| Payment not received despite approval | Check NPCI Aadhaar mapper — Aadhaar must be seeded to bank account |
| Tuition fee not reimbursed fully | Institution must submit fee structure on NSP — follow up with college accounts dept |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does the scholarship cover hostels outside the institution?
Private hostel accommodation is generally not reimbursed. The scheme covers accommodation at institution-recognized hostels. Day scholars receive a lower maintenance allowance.

### I changed college mid-year. What do I do?
Update your institution details on the scholarship portal immediately. Contact your district social welfare office for guidance on continuation of benefits.

### Can I apply if my family income is exactly ₹2.5 lakh?
The limit is **below ₹2.5 lakh** — if the certificate shows ₹2.5L or above, you are not eligible. Some states have a slightly different threshold — check your state-specific scholarship guidelines.

### My father is deceased. Whose income counts?
In case of the father's death, the mother's income (or the main earner's income) is considered. Submit the death certificate of the father along with the income certificate of the actual earner.

### I am doing a 3-year LLB after BA. Does the scholarship continue?
Yes — post-graduation and professional courses like LLB, MBA, MCA are covered. Apply for PMS for each new course enrollment.

### Is there an age limit?
No official age limit for SC Post Matric Scholarship — the focus is on the course level and income, not age.
