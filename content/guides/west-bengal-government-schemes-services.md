# West Bengal Government Schemes and Services – Complete Guide

West Bengal runs several high-impact welfare schemes aimed at women's financial inclusion, universal health coverage, and education support. From Lakshmir Bhandar monthly income support to Swasthya Sathi health insurance, the state offers a comprehensive welfare network. This guide covers every major West Bengal government scheme with eligibility, benefits, and application details.

## Key Government Portals in West Bengal

- **West Bengal Government Portal** – [wb.gov.in](https://wb.gov.in/) – Central hub for all departments
- **Banglar Bhumi** – [banglarbhumi.gov.in](https://banglarbhumi.gov.in/) – Land records, mutation, and plot information
- **Swasthya Sathi Portal** – [swasthyasathi.gov.in](https://swasthyasathi.gov.in/) – Health insurance
- **e-District West Bengal** – [edistrict.wb.gov.in](https://edistrict.wb.gov.in/) – Certificates and services
- **Duare Sarkar** – Doorstep government service delivery camps

## Women Empowerment Schemes

### Lakshmir Bhandar (Lakshmi's Treasure)

West Bengal's flagship income support scheme for women, providing monthly financial assistance directly to bank accounts.

**Key benefits:**
- ₹1,000/month for general category women
- ₹1,200/month for SC/ST women
- Women aged 25-60 eligible
- Must be resident of West Bengal

Read the complete guide: [Lakshmir Bhandar West Bengal](/guide/lakshmir-bhandar-west-bengal) | [West Bengal Lakshmir Bhandar](/guide/west-bengal-lakshmir-bhandar)

### Kanyashree Prakalpa

Internationally awarded scheme providing financial support to girls aged 13-18 for continuing education and preventing child marriage.

**Key benefits:**
- **K1:** Annual scholarship of ₹750 for girls aged 13-18 (enrolled in school)
- **K2:** One-time grant of ₹25,000 at age 18 (if unmarried and pursuing education)
- **K3:** Financial support for higher education (UG/PG)
- Open to all girls – income bar has been removed by the state government

Read the complete guide: [Kanyashree Prakalpa West Bengal](/guide/kanyashree-prakalpa-west-bengal)

### Rupashree Prakalpa

One-time marriage grant of ₹25,000 for girls from economically weaker families at the time of their marriage (after age 18).

## Health Schemes

### Swasthya Sathi

Universal health insurance for all West Bengal families providing cashless treatment at empanelled hospitals.

**Key features:**
- ₹5 lakh per family per year
- Covers all families in West Bengal (universal coverage)
- Cashless treatment at government and private hospitals
- Smart card-based system linked to family

Read the complete guide: [Swasthya Sathi West Bengal](/guide/swasthya-sathi-west-bengal)

## Education and Scholarship Schemes

### Sabuj Sathi (Green Companion)

Free bicycles for students of classes 9-12 in government and government-aided schools to reduce dropout rates.

Read the complete guide: [WB Sabuj Sathi Bicycle Scheme](/guide/wb-sabuj-sathi-bicycle-scheme)

### Swami Vivekananda Merit-cum-Means Scholarship

Monthly scholarship for students from economically weaker families pursuing higher education.

**Benefits:**
- ₹1,000/month for UG students
- ₹1,500/month for PG students
- ₹5,000/month for M.Phil/PhD students

Read the complete guide: [WB Swami Vivekananda Scholarship](/guide/wb-swami-vivekananda-scholarship)

### West Bengal Student Credit Card (WBSCC)

Soft loan scheme enabling students to pursue higher education without financial constraints. Covers Class X and above students for higher secondary, UG, PG, professional, diploma, and doctoral courses – within West Bengal, outside the state, or even abroad.

**Key features:**
- Available to West Bengal residents up to age 40
- Covers tuition, hostel, books, and living expenses
- Also covers coaching for competitive exams (IAS, WBCS, etc.)
- Apply at [wbscc.wb.gov.in](https://wbscc.wb.gov.in/)

### Other Education Schemes

- **Aikyashree** – Scholarship for minority students
- **Oasis** – Scholarship for SC/ST students
- **Free textbooks and uniforms** in government schools
- **Mid-Day Meal Programme** in schools

## Housing Schemes

### Bangla Awas Yojana

State housing scheme for rural BPL families providing ₹1.20–1.30 lakh financial assistance for pucca house construction.

### Nijo Griha Nijo Bhumi

Land and housing scheme for landless families providing homestead land and house construction assistance.

## Agriculture Schemes

### Krishak Bandhu (Farmer's Friend)

Comprehensive farmer welfare scheme:
- ₹10,000/year (₹5,000 per crop season) for farmers with 1+ acre
- ₹4,000/year for farmers with less than 1 acre
- ₹2 lakh life insurance for farmer families (death benefit)

### Kisan Credit Card in West Bengal

Subsidized crop loans for farmers through KCC at 4% interest.

See also: [eNAM](/guide/enam-national-agriculture-market) | [PMFBY Crop Insurance](/guide/pmfby-claim-status-crop-loss)

## How to Apply for West Bengal Government Schemes

### Online Application

1. **Visit e-District WB** – [edistrict.wb.gov.in](https://edistrict.wb.gov.in/) or specific scheme portal
2. **Register** with mobile number and Aadhaar
3. **Select service** – Choose scheme or certificate
4. **Fill form** and upload documents
5. **Submit** and track with application number

### Duare Sarkar Camps

West Bengal regularly organizes **Duare Sarkar** (Government at Doorstep) camps where citizens can:
- Apply for multiple schemes at one location
- Get Swasthya Sathi cards
- Apply for Lakshmir Bhandar, Kanyashree, and other benefits
- Enroll in pension schemes

### Common Documents Required

- Aadhaar card
- Voter ID / Ration card
- Income certificate
- Bank passbook
- Passport-size photographs
- Caste certificate (for category-based schemes)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I apply for Lakshmir Bhandar?**
Apply online through the scheme portal or at Duare Sarkar camps. Aadhaar-linked bank account is mandatory.

**Is Swasthya Sathi available for all families?**
Yes, Swasthya Sathi provides universal health coverage to all West Bengal families with a smart card.

**How to check West Bengal scheme application status?**
Visit the e-District portal or specific scheme website and enter your application number.
