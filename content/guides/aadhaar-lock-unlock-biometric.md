# Aadhaar Biometric Lock/Unlock: How to Secure Your Data

Your Aadhaar biometric data — fingerprints and iris scans — is used for identity authentication across India. But when you're not actively using Aadhaar services, your biometrics don't need to be accessible. Locking them prevents unauthorized use.

UIDAI provides a free **Biometric Lock/Unlock** feature that lets you control when your biometrics can be used for authentication. Here's how to use it.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent platform and is not affiliated with UIDAI or any government body.

## What is Aadhaar Biometric Lock?

When you **lock** your biometrics:
- No one can authenticate using your fingerprints or iris scan
- OTP-based and demographic authentication still works normally
- Your Aadhaar number remains valid for all other purposes
- You can **unlock temporarily** whenever you need biometric authentication

When you **unlock** your biometrics:
- Biometric authentication is enabled again
- After use, you should lock them back for security

**Think of it as a safety switch** — keep it off when not needed.

## Method 1: Lock/Unlock via myAadhaar Portal

### To Lock Biometrics

1. Visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/)
2. Log in with your **Aadhaar number** and **OTP**
3. Go to **My Aadhaar** → **Lock/Unlock Biometrics**
4. Click **Lock** to enable biometric lock
5. You'll see a confirmation message — your biometrics are now locked

### To Unlock Biometrics (Temporary)

1. Log in to [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/)
2. Go to **Lock/Unlock Biometrics**
3. Click **Unlock**
4. Your biometrics will be temporarily unlocked
5. **Important:** Biometrics auto-lock again after a single successful authentication or after a set time period (usually 10 minutes if unused)

## Method 2: Lock/Unlock via mAadhaar App

The mAadhaar app provides the most convenient way to manage biometric locks:

1. Open the **mAadhaar** app on your phone ([download guide](/guide/aadhaar-maadhaar-app-guide))
2. Tap on your **Aadhaar profile**
3. Go to **Biometric Lock** option
4. Toggle the lock **ON** to lock biometrics
5. Toggle **OFF** to temporarily unlock when needed
6. Authenticate with your **registered mobile OTP** or app PIN

**Advantage of mAadhaar:** You can quickly unlock biometrics right before a bank visit or SIM activation and lock them back immediately after.

## Method 3: Using Virtual ID (VID) for Privacy

A **Virtual ID (VID)** is a temporary 16-digit number that maps to your Aadhaar but doesn't reveal your actual Aadhaar number. Here's how it helps with biometric security:

### How to Generate VID

1. Visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/) → **VID Generator**
2. Or use the **mAadhaar app** → **Generate VID**
3. Enter your Aadhaar number and verify with OTP
4. A 16-digit VID is generated and sent to your registered mobile
5. Use this VID instead of your Aadhaar number wherever accepted

### VID + Biometric Lock Strategy

- Keep biometrics **locked** by default
- When you need biometric authentication:
  1. **Unlock** biometrics temporarily via mAadhaar
  2. Use your **VID** instead of Aadhaar number for the authentication
  3. **Lock** biometrics again immediately after
- This way, neither your biometrics nor your Aadhaar number is exposed

## When Should You Unlock Biometrics?

You'll need to temporarily unlock biometrics for:

- **Opening a bank account** (biometric eKYC)
- **Getting a new SIM card** (telecom eKYC)
- **Government scheme enrollment** (Aadhaar-based verification)
- **Aadhaar update at enrollment centre** (biometric verification required)
- **PDS/ration shop** (fingerprint-based ration collection)

For all other Aadhaar uses (online eKYC, DigiLocker, tax filing), OTP authentication works fine with biometrics locked.

## What Happens When Biometrics Are Locked?

| Scenario | Works? |
|---|---|
| OTP-based authentication | ✅ Yes |
| Demographic authentication | ✅ Yes |
| Fingerprint authentication | ❌ No (blocked) |
| Iris authentication | ❌ No (blocked) |
| Aadhaar download/eAadhaar | ✅ Yes (uses OTP) |
| mAadhaar app features | ✅ Yes |
| Aadhaar update online | ✅ Yes (uses OTP) |
| Ration via fingerprint at PDS | ❌ No (need to unlock first) |

## Important Tips

1. **Lock biometrics today** — If you haven't already, do it now. It takes 2 minutes and prevents misuse
2. **Unlock only when needed** — Don't leave biometrics unlocked; lock them back after every use
3. **Use mAadhaar for quick toggles** — Much faster than the web portal for urgent unlocks
4. **Generate a VID** — Use it instead of your Aadhaar number for added privacy
5. **Check authentication history regularly** — See our [Aadhaar Authentication History guide](/guide/aadhaar-history-check-online) to monitor usage

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Will locking biometrics affect my Aadhaar card validity?
No. Your Aadhaar remains completely valid. Locking only prevents biometric (fingerprint/iris) authentication. All OTP-based services continue to work.

### Can I lock biometrics permanently?
The lock stays active until you manually unlock. There's no expiry. You control it completely through the myAadhaar portal or mAadhaar app.

### What if I need biometric authentication urgently and don't have phone access?
You'll need your registered mobile phone to unlock biometrics (OTP is required). Keep your Aadhaar-registered mobile number active and accessible. If you've lost access, update your mobile at an [Aadhaar enrollment centre](/guide/aadhaar-enrollment-center-locator) first.

### Does biometric lock affect Aadhaar-linked ration (PDS)?
Yes. If your PDS shop uses fingerprint authentication, you'll need to unlock biometrics before collecting rations. Unlock via mAadhaar before visiting the shop, then lock again after.

### How is VID different from biometric lock?
They serve different purposes. **Biometric lock** prevents fingerprint/iris authentication. **VID** hides your actual Aadhaar number. Using both together provides maximum security.

### Can someone else unlock my biometrics?
No, unless they have access to your Aadhaar-registered mobile number (for OTP). Keep your registered mobile secure and never share OTPs with anyone.

### Is the biometric lock/unlock service free?
Yes, completely free on both the myAadhaar portal and the mAadhaar app.
