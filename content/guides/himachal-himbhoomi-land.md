## What is HimBhoomi?

HimBhoomi is the official online land records portal of Himachal Pradesh, maintained by the Revenue Department, Government of Himachal Pradesh. Through this portal, citizens can view and download land records including jamabandi (Record of Rights), nakal (certified copy), mutation details, and more — all free of cost.

The portal is accessible at **himbhoomi.nic.in** and covers land records for all 12 districts and their tehsils across Himachal Pradesh.

### Key Services on HimBhoomi

- **Jamabandi (Record of Rights)** — View ownership details, area, and land type
- **Nakal (Copy of Land Record)** — Download or print certified copies
- **Mutation Status** — Track mutation (intaqal) applications
- **Tehsil-wise Search** — Search by district, tehsil, village, and khata/khasra number
- **Khasra/Khatauni Details** — Plot-level land information
- **Tatima (Field Map)** — View cadastral maps where available

## Who Can Use HimBhoomi?

- Any citizen of India can search and view Himachal Pradesh land records
- No registration or login is required for basic search
- Property owners, buyers, legal heirs, and advocates
- Banks and financial institutions for loan verification
- Government officials for revenue administration

## How to Check Land Records on HimBhoomi — Step-by-Step

### Method 1: Search by Khasra Number

1. Visit **[himbhoomi.nic.in](https://himbhoomi.nic.in)**
2. Click on **"Jamabandi"** or **"Land Records"** from the main menu
3. Select your **District** from the dropdown
4. Select your **Tehsil**
5. Select your **Village**
6. Enter the **Khasra Number** (plot number)
7. Click **"Search"** or **"View"**
8. The jamabandi record will display — showing owner name, area, land type, and rights
9. Click **"Print"** or **"Download"** to save a copy

### Method 2: Search by Owner Name

1. Go to **[himbhoomi.nic.in](https://himbhoomi.nic.in)**
2. Select the **"Search by Name"** option
3. Select **District → Tehsil → Village**
4. Enter the **owner's name** (in Hindi or English)
5. Click **Search**
6. Matching records will appear — click on the relevant entry to view full jamabandi

### Method 3: Search by Khata Number

1. Visit the HimBhoomi portal
2. Select **District → Tehsil → Village**
3. Enter the **Khata Number**
4. Click **Search** to view all khasra entries under that khata

## How to Check Mutation (Intaqal) Status

Mutation is the process of updating land ownership records after a sale, inheritance, or gift.

1. Visit **[himbhoomi.nic.in](https://himbhoomi.nic.in)**
2. Go to **"Mutation Status"** or **"Intaqal"** section
3. Select your **District** and **Tehsil**
4. Enter the **mutation case number** or search by name
5. The current status (pending/approved/rejected) will be displayed
6. You can also view mutation history for any land parcel

## How to Download Nakal (Certified Copy)

1. Search for your land record using any method above
2. Once the jamabandi is displayed, click **"Nakal"** or **"Get Copy"**
3. The nakal will be generated as a PDF
4. Click **Download** to save it to your device
5. This online nakal is available **free of cost**

> **Note:** For legally certified copies with official stamp, you may need to visit the Tehsil office.

## Documents Required for Mutation

If you need to apply for mutation (ownership transfer), the following documents are typically required:

- **Sale Deed / Registry** — If land was purchased
- **Death Certificate** — If inherited
- **Succession Certificate / Will** — For inheritance cases
- **Gift Deed** — If land was gifted
- **Court Order** — If ownership was decided by court
- **Identity Proof** — Aadhaar card, voter ID, or other valid ID

## Important Tips

1. **Use correct Khasra/Khata numbers** — Even a small error will return no results. Cross-check with your existing land documents.
2. **Records are in Hindi** — Most HimBhoomi records are in Hindi/Devnagari script. Use Google Translate if needed.
3. **Online nakal is for reference only** — For legal purposes like bank loans or court cases, get a certified copy from the Tehsil office.
4. **Check mutation status regularly** — Mutation applications can take time. Follow up at the Tehsil if there's no progress beyond 30 days.
5. **Portal may be slow during peak hours** — Try early morning or late evening for faster access.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Is HimBhoomi free to use?
Yes, viewing and downloading land records on HimBhoomi is completely free. No registration or payment is required for online search.

### Q2. Can I use HimBhoomi nakal as legal proof?
The online nakal from HimBhoomi is useful for reference and verification purposes. However, for legal proceedings or bank loans, you may need a certified copy from the Tehsil office with an official seal.

### Q3. What if I cannot find my land record on HimBhoomi?
First, verify the khasra number, khata number, and village name. If records are still not found, the land may not yet be digitized. Visit your nearest Tehsil office for assistance.

### Q4. How do I apply for mutation in Himachal Pradesh?
You can apply for mutation at the Tehsil office by submitting the sale deed, identity proof, and other relevant documents. Some tehsils also accept online applications through the revenue department portal.

### Q5. Does HimBhoomi show property maps?
HimBhoomi provides tatima (field maps) for some areas. However, map availability depends on whether the cadastral survey data for your village has been digitized.

### Q6. Can I check someone else's land record on HimBhoomi?
Yes, land records are public records. Anyone can search and view land records on HimBhoomi by entering the correct district, tehsil, village, and khasra/khata details.

### Q7. Is HimBhoomi available as a mobile app?
As of now, HimBhoomi is primarily a web portal. Access it through any mobile or desktop browser at himbhoomi.nic.in.
