# Canara Bank Dormant Account Reactivation 2026 — Complete Guide

Canara Bank (merged with Syndicate Bank in 2020) marks savings accounts dormant after **24 months** of no customer-initiated debit transactions. Here's how to reactivate your Canara Bank dormant account — including legacy Syndicate Bank accounts.

---

## Canara Bank Dormant Account — What's Restricted

| Feature | Active | Dormant |
|---------|--------|---------|
| ATM withdrawals | ✅ | ❌ |
| UPI / Debit card | ✅ | ❌ |
| Net banking debits | ✅ | ❌ |
| Salary/NEFT received | ✅ | ✅ Continues |
| Balance check | ✅ | ✅ Available |
| FD interest credited | ✅ | ✅ Continues |

---

## Method 1: Canara ai1 App

1. Open **Canara ai1** app → Login
2. **"Services"** → **"Account Services"** → **"Activate Dormant Account"** (if available)
3. Complete Aadhaar OTP e-KYC if prompted
4. Submit reactivation request
5. **Processing: 24–72 hours**

---

## Method 2: Canara Bank Net Banking

1. Login at Canara Bank internet banking portal
2. **"Other Services"** → **"Dormant Account Activation"**
3. Upload KYC documents if required
4. Submit
5. **Processing: 2–5 working days**

---

## Method 3: Canara Bank Branch (Reliable for All Cases)

1. Visit any Canara Bank branch
2. Carry:
   - Aadhaar card (original + self-attested copy)
   - PAN card (original + copy)
   - Passbook (if available)
3. Request: **"Dormant Account Reactivation"** form
4. Fill + submit KYC documents
5. Make a small cash deposit at counter (₹100–₹500)
6. **Same-day processing**

**Canara Bank Customer Care:** **1800-425-0018** (toll-free)

## Syndicate Bank Legacy Account Reactivation

Syndicate Bank merged with Canara Bank in April 2020:
- Your old Syndicate Bank account is now a Canara Bank account
- Old passbook + Aadhaar + PAN → Visit any Canara branch
- Branch will trace merger records and confirm new account number
- Dormancy rules same: 24 months of inactivity = dormant
- Balance remains with Canara Bank (or DEAF if 10+ years)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Canara Bank dormant account — can Canara ai1 reactivate all dormant accounts?**
Canara ai1 reactivation works for select account types with updated KYC. For old accounts or KYC-pending accounts, branch visit is mandatory.

**My Syndicate Bank account (now Canara) has been dormant since merger — how do I reactivate?**
Take your old Syndicate Bank passbook + Aadhaar + PAN to any Canara Bank branch. The branch will look up merger records. If your account is not yet traced to Canara, the branch will escalate to the zonal migration team.

**Canara Bank dormant account reactivation — how long does it take at branch?**
Same-day processing at branch (2–4 hours) when you do a fresh cash transaction. Test ATM/UPI by end of business day.

**What is the minimum deposit needed to reactivate Canara Bank dormant account?**
No minimum specified by Canara, but ₹100–₹500 is typically sufficient. The transaction itself matters (proves customer intent) — the amount is secondary.

**DEAF transfer at Canara Bank — when?**
After 10 years of unclaimed/dormant balance: Transferred to RBI DEAF Fund. Canara Bank must return the claim amount upon request. Visit branch → DEAF Claim Form → 5–7 working days processing.
