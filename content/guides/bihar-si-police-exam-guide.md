# Bihar SI (Sub-Inspector) Exam Guide — Eligibility, Pattern & Preparation Tips

The Bihar Police Sub-ordinate Services Commission (BPSSC) conducts the recruitment exam for Sub-Inspector posts in Bihar Police. The Bihar SI exam is a gateway to a respectable law enforcement career in the state.

Bihar SI offers a Grade Pay of ₹4,200 with opportunities for promotion to Inspector and higher ranks through departmental exams.

## Eligibility Criteria

### Age Limit

| Category | Minimum Age | Maximum Age |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| General (Male) | 20 years | 25 years |
| General (Female) | 20 years | 25 years |
| OBC (Bihar) | 20 years | 27 years |
| SC/ST (Bihar) | 20 years | 30 years |

*Age relaxation as per Bihar Government norms.*

### Educational Qualification

- **Minimum:** Bachelor's degree from a recognized university
- Any discipline accepted

### Physical Standards

**For Male Candidates:**

| Parameter | General/OBC/SC | ST |
|-----------|---------------|-----|
| Height | 165 cm | 160 cm |
| Chest (unexpanded) | 79 cm | 77 cm |
| Chest (expanded) | 84 cm | 82 cm |

**For Female Candidates:**

| Parameter | General | SC/ST |
|-----------|---------|-------|
| Height | 155 cm | 150 cm |

## Exam Pattern

### Stage 1: Preliminary Exam (Written)

| Subject | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| General Knowledge | 100 | 200 |

- Duration: 2 hours
- Objective type (MCQ)
- Negative marking: 0.2 marks per wrong answer

### Stage 2: Main Exam (Written)

| Paper | Subject | Marks | Duration |
|-------|---------|-------|----------|
| Paper 1 | General Hindi | 200 | 2 hours |
| Paper 2 | General Studies | 200 | 2 hours |

### Stage 3: Physical Efficiency Test (PET)

**Male:** 1.6 km run in 6 minutes, Long Jump 12 feet, High Jump 4 feet, Shot Put (16 lbs) 16 feet
**Female:** 1 km run in 5 minutes, Long Jump 9 feet, High Jump 3 feet

### Stage 4: Document Verification & Medical

## Syllabus Overview

### General Knowledge
- Indian History, Geography, Polity, Economy, Science, Current Affairs, Bihar-specific GK

### General Hindi
- Grammar, Comprehension, Essay writing, Translation, Letter writing

### General Studies (Main)
- Indian Constitution, Law, Current Affairs, Bihar History & Culture, Science & Technology

## How to Apply

1. Visit the BPSSC website at [bpssc.bih.nic.in](https://bpssc.bih.nic.in)
2. Click on the active SI recruitment notification
3. Register with email and mobile number
4. Fill in personal, educational, and category details
5. Upload photo and signature as per specifications
6. Pay application fee online
7. Submit and download confirmation

## Salary & Benefits

- **Pay Scale:** ₹35,400 – ₹1,12,400 (Level 6)
- Grade Pay: ₹4,200
- DA, HRA, and other allowances as per Bihar Government
- Medical benefits, pension (NPS)

## Preparation Tips

1. **Bihar GK is important:** Study Bihar history, geography, and current state schemes
2. **Hindi essay writing:** Practice descriptive Hindi writing for Main exam
3. **Physical fitness:** Start running and athletics practice well in advance
4. **Mock tests:** Regular practice with previous year papers
5. **Current affairs:** Follow national and Bihar-specific news daily

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is graduation compulsory for Bihar SI?
Yes, a Bachelor's degree from a recognized university is mandatory.

### What is the selection process for Bihar SI?
Selection involves Prelims, Main Exam, PET, and Document Verification.

### Is there an interview for Bihar SI?
No, there is no interview. Selection is based on written exam and PET.

### Can candidates from other states apply?
Yes, but domicile of Bihar is required for reserved category benefits.

### How often is Bihar SI recruitment conducted?
Recruitment depends on vacancies and is typically conducted every 2-3 years.

### What is the physical test for Bihar SI female candidates?
Female candidates must complete 1 km run in 5 minutes, Long Jump 9 feet, and High Jump 3 feet.
