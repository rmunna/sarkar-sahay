# Cheyutha Pension – Financial Support for Women Aged 45-60 in Telangana

Cheyutha is a social welfare pension scheme by the Government of Telangana that provides annual financial assistance to women aged 45 to 60 years belonging to SC, ST, BC, and minority communities. The scheme aims to empower women in their middle age who may not yet qualify for old age pensions.

## What is Cheyutha?

Cheyutha (meaning "support" in Telugu) provides ₹10,000 per year to eligible women who fall in the age gap between working age and old age pension eligibility (60+). This financial assistance helps women from weaker sections maintain economic independence and meet daily expenses.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Gender:** Women only
2. **Age:** Between 45 and 60 years
3. **Community:** Must belong to SC, ST, BC (Backward Classes), or Minority communities
4. **Residency:** Must be a resident of Telangana
5. **Income:** From economically weaker families (BPL/low income)
6. **Exclusions:**
   - Women already receiving other government pensions (e.g., Aasara pension)
   - Government employees or pensioners
   - Income tax payers

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar card (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- Age proof (Aadhaar / voter ID / birth certificate)
- Caste/community certificate (SC/ST/BC/Minority)
- Bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked)
- Ration card
- Passport-size photographs
- Residence proof

## Step-by-Step Application Process

1. **Visit nearest MeeSeva center or Gram Panchayat office**
2. **Collect application form:** Request the Cheyutha/social pension application form
3. **Fill the form:** Provide personal details, age, caste, bank account, and Aadhaar information
4. **Attach documents:** Self-attested copies of all required documents
5. **Submit application:** Submit at the MeeSeva center, Gram Panchayat, or mandal office
6. **Verification:** Revenue officials conduct field verification of age, income, and residence
7. **Approval:** Approved at the mandal/district level
8. **Annual payment:** ₹10,000 credited to bank account annually (or in installments)

## Important Tips

1. **Transition to Aasara:** Once you turn 60, you become eligible for Aasara old age pension — apply for transition
2. **Keep documents updated:** Ensure your age proof and caste certificate are current
3. **Bank account must be active:** Inactive/dormant accounts will delay payments
4. **No dual benefits:** You cannot receive Cheyutha and another social pension simultaneously
5. **Apply through official channels:** Do not pay agents — the application is free

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: How much money do I get under Cheyutha?
₹10,000 per year, credited to your bank account.

### Q2: Can general category women apply?
No, Cheyutha is specifically for women from SC, ST, BC, and Minority communities.

### Q3: What happens after I turn 60?
You transition from Cheyutha to Aasara old age pension scheme. Apply for the transition at your local office.

### Q4: Can I get Cheyutha and Aasara pension both?
No, you can receive only one social pension at a time.

### Q5: Is this scheme only for BPL families?
While targeting weaker sections, the exact income criteria may vary. Check with your mandal office for current eligibility.

### Q6: How do I check my application status?
Enquire at the MeeSeva center or mandal revenue office where you submitted the application.

### Q7: Is the payment monthly or annual?
The ₹10,000 is typically disbursed annually or in 2 semi-annual installments.
