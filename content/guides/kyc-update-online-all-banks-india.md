# KYC Update Online for Bank Accounts — All Major Banks 2026

Banks periodically ask customers to update KYC (Know Your Customer) — usually every 2 years for high-risk accounts, every 5–8 years for low-risk. If KYC is not updated, the bank can restrict debit/credit operations or freeze the account entirely.

This guide covers how to update KYC **without visiting a branch** for every major Indian bank.

> **RBI Rule:** Banks cannot close an account solely for non-updation of KYC. They can freeze debit operations. Once you submit KYC documents, they must restore account operations within 3 working days.

---

## Why Is KYC Update Required?

| Reason | What Happens If You Don't Update |
|--------|----------------------------------|
| Periodic re-KYC (regulatory) | Account debit restricted |
| Change of address | KYC mismatch → account frozen |
| Mobile number change | OTP fails → banking stops |
| PAN not linked | Account becomes non-functional |
| New income threshold crossed | Higher KYC level required |

## SBI KYC Update Online

### Method 1: SBI YONO App (Aadhaar OTP-based)
1. Open **YONO** → **More** → **Service Requests**
2. Select **"Re-KYC Update"**
3. Choose **Aadhaar OTP Verification**
4. Enter Aadhaar number → OTP received on Aadhaar-linked mobile
5. Submit — KYC updated digitally
6. **Timeline:** 1–2 working days

### Method 2: SBI Net Banking
1. Login → **My Accounts** → **Profile** → **KYC Update**
2. Upload Aadhaar + PAN (scan/photo, clearly readable)
3. Submit
4. **Timeline:** 2–3 working days

### Method 3: Email to Branch (If App Not Working)
- Email your home branch with scanned KYC documents
- Attach: Self-attested Aadhaar, PAN, recent photo
- Subject: "Re-KYC Update Request — Account XXXX"

### Branch Visit (Last Resort):
- Home branch → Fill re-KYC form → Submit documents
- Same-day processing

**SBI Customer Care:** 1800-11-2211

## What Happens After You Submit KYC?

1. Bank reviews documents: **1–3 working days**
2. If approved: Account restrictions lifted, debit operations restored
3. If documents rejected: Bank sends SMS/email with reason → resubmit correct documents
4. RBI mandates: Restrictions must be lifted **within 3 working days** of receiving complete documents

---

## If Bank Still Blocks Account After KYC Submitted

1. **Get acknowledgment** — Branch/app should give reference number for KYC submission
2. **Wait 3 working days** — RBI mandates restoration within this time
3. **Escalate:** Customer Care → Ask for "KYC Processing Status"
4. **Banking Ombudsman:** If bank does not restore within 7 days after complete KYC submission, file complaint at [cms.rbi.org.in](https://cms.rbi.org.in)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**My Aadhaar mobile number is different from bank mobile number — can I still do online KYC?**
For Aadhaar OTP-based KYC, the OTP goes to your Aadhaar-registered mobile, not your bank-registered mobile. You need both numbers to be active. If not, visit branch for offline KYC.

**My bank says KYC is "pending" but I submitted documents 2 weeks ago — what do I do?**
Escalate via customer care with your submission reference number. If no resolution in 7 working days after submission, file complaint at RBI CMS portal: [cms.rbi.org.in](https://cms.rbi.org.in).

**Can KYC be done for a joint account online?**
Typically, both account holders need to complete KYC separately. Online KYC works if both are digitally verified. For complex joint accounts, branch visit is safer.

**Is Video KYC as valid as in-person KYC?**
Yes — Video KYC (V-CIP: Video based Customer Identification Process) is fully RBI-approved and legally equivalent to in-person KYC since 2021.

**Does KYC update change my bank account number?**
No — your account number, IFSC, and all details remain the same. Only your identity verification records are updated.

**PAN not linked to bank account — is that the same as KYC issue?**
Related but different. PAN linking is mandatory for accounts with transactions above certain limits. Your bank may mark the account restricted if PAN is not linked. Link via net banking (Profile → Link PAN) or branch.
