# Andhra Pradesh Caste Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **caste certificate** in Andhra Pradesh is issued by the Revenue Department (Mandal Revenue Officer / RDO) and is required for fee reimbursement (BC/SC/ST engineering/medical students), EAMCET/NEET counselling state quota, AP government scholarship applications, and government job reservations. Applications go through **MeeSeva** — AP's multi-service portal — or through **Ward/Village Secretariats**.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at any **MeeSeva centre** or [meeseva.gov.in](https://meeseva.gov.in), or through your **Ward/Village Secretariat**. Issued by MRO/Tahsildar within **7–21 days**.

## Caste Categories in Andhra Pradesh

| Category | Reservation % | Key Communities |
|----------|---------------|-----------------|
| **SC** | 15% | Scheduled Castes — AP state list |
| **ST** | 6% | Scheduled Tribes (Koya, Kondareddy, etc.) |
| **BC-A** | 7% | Backward Classes — Valmiki, Boya, Yerukula, etc. |
| **BC-B** | 10% | Backward Classes — Yadava, Golla, Kuruba, etc. |
| **BC-C** | 1% | Scheduled Caste converts — Christian SCs |
| **BC-D** | 7% | Muslim Backward Classes |
| **BC-E** | 4% | Ediga, Goud and allied communities |
| **EWS** | 10% | General category, income < ₹8 lakh/year |

## Why You Need AP Caste Certificate

- **Fee Reimbursement (Jagananna Vidya Deevena)** — BC/SC/ST engineering, medical, pharmacy students
- **EAMCET/NEET AP state quota counselling** — community certificate mandatory
- **AP BC/SC/ST post-matric scholarship** applications
- **Government job reservation** — APPSC, district recruitment
- **Central OBC NCL certificate** — for central government jobs/UPSC
- **Jagananna scheme eligibility** — Amma Vodi, NAVARATNALU schemes

---

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory)
- **Ration card** (shows family caste/community)
- **Father's school certificate** (TC/SSLC with caste)
- **Pattadar Passbook / land records** (for rural farming families)
- Self-declaration of caste (on ₹20 stamp paper or online)
- **Voter ID** (address proof)
- Passport-size photograph

## How to Apply via MeeSeva

**Method 1: MeeSeva Kiosk (Most Common)**

1. Visit nearest **MeeSeva centre**
2. Ask for **"Caste/Community Certificate"** (BC-A / BC-B / SC / ST as applicable)
3. Provide Aadhaar, ration card, father's TC/caste proof
4. Operator submits online application
5. Fee: approximately **₹35–60**
6. Receive acknowledgement with Application Number

**Method 2: MeeSeva Online Portal**

1. Go to [meeseva.gov.in](https://meeseva.gov.in)
2. Log in / register
3. Search **"Caste Certificate"**
4. Fill form, upload documents, submit

---

## Apply via Ward/Village Secretariat

AP's **Ward Secretariat** (urban) and **Village Secretariat** (rural) are the frontline delivery points:

1. Visit your **Ward Secretariat** or **Village Secretariat**
2. Meet the **Ward Welfare and Development Secretary** or **Village Revenue Assistant**
3. Submit caste certificate application with documents
4. They assist with data entry and submission to MRO
5. No extra charge at the secretariat
6. Much faster than standalone MeeSeva for first-time applicants

---

## Status Check & Download

1. Log in to [meeseva.gov.in](https://meeseva.gov.in) → **"My Applications"**
2. Or visit MeeSeva kiosk with acknowledgement
3. Once approved: download digitally signed PDF from MeeSeva portal
4. Valid for all AP and central government purposes

## BC Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) Certificate — AP

For **central government OBC reservation** (UPSC, SSC, railways, IITs):
- Apply for "BC Non-Creamy Layer Certificate" at MeeSeva
- Income < ₹8 lakh/year (non-agricultural income)
- Issued by Tahsildar/RDO
- Valid for **1 year** (or as specified)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### BC certificate vs OBC certificate — which for central government jobs?

For **AP state government jobs**: AP BC certificate (MeeSeva) is used. For **central government jobs (UPSC, SSC, railways)**: you need the **OBC NCL certificate** — which can also be obtained from MeeSeva but is a different service. The AP BC certificate alone is **not accepted** for central OBC reservation.

### Caste certificate for EAMCET counselling — what validity?

For **EAMCET/NEET AP state quota counselling**, the caste certificate must be **current and issued within the academic year** (or as specified in the counselling notification). Check the AP EAMCET counselling guidelines each year.

### Can I get caste certificate through Spandana/Meebadi Nestam?

These are grievance redressal portals, not the issuance route. Apply through MeeSeva or Ward Secretariat for the actual certificate. If there's a problem with your application, you can raise a grievance via Spandana (spandana.ap.gov.in).
