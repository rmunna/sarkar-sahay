# Bihar Domicile Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **residential certificate** (आवासीय प्रमाण पत्र — Awasiya Praman Patra) in Bihar — commonly called domicile certificate — is issued by the Revenue Department (Circle Officer / Tehsildar) and is required for NEET Bihar state quota (85%) admissions, BPSC government job applications, Bihar state scholarships, and state scheme eligibility. Applications go through **Service Plus Bihar** (Bihar's integrated e-services platform).

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [serviceonline.bihar.gov.in](https://serviceonline.bihar.gov.in). Issued by Circle Officer / Anchal Adhikari within **15–21 days**. RTPS guarantees this deadline.

## Bihar Residential Certificate — Key Facts

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Local name** | Awasiya Praman Patra (आवासीय प्रमाण पत्र) |
| **Eligibility** | Born in Bihar OR permanent resident |
| **Issuing authority** | Circle Officer (Anchal Adhikari) / Tehsildar |
| **Processing time** | 15–21 days (RTPS guaranteed) |
| **Fee** | ₹0–₹10 (nearly free) |
| **Validity** | Usually valid 1–3 years (reissue for admissions) |
| **Portal** | serviceonline.bihar.gov.in |

## Why You Need Bihar Domicile Certificate

- **NEET Bihar state quota (85%)** — domicile mandatory for BCECEB (Bihar Combined Entrance Competitive Examination Board) counselling
- **BPSC (Bihar PSC)** — Bihar government jobs require Bihar domicile/residential proof
- **BSSC (Bihar Staff Selection Commission)** — state recruitment
- **Bihar state scholarship** — NSP/state scholarships for Bihar residents
- **PMAY / PM Awas Yojana** — Bihar beneficiary proof
- **Bihar e-Kalyan scholarship** — SC/ST/OBC post-matric scholarship

## RTPS — Right to Public Service

Bihar's **RTPS (Right to Public Service)** Act guarantees delivery within 15–21 days. If not delivered:
- You can file an appeal to the Sub-Divisional Officer (SDO)
- Penalty can be levied on the defaulting officer
- RTPS tracking: [rtps.bihar.gov.in](https://rtps.bihar.gov.in)

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory — with Bihar address)
- **Ration card** (Bihar ration card at local address)
- **Voter ID** (Bihar voter enrollment)
- **Birth certificate** — showing birth in Bihar (if claiming by birth)
- **School Transfer Certificate (TC)** — from Bihar schools
- **Land records / LPC** (Land Possession Certificate) — for rural applicants
- Self-declaration of Bihar residency
- Passport-size photograph

## How to Apply Online — Service Plus Bihar

1. Go to [serviceonline.bihar.gov.in](https://serviceonline.bihar.gov.in)
2. Register / log in with mobile number (OTP)
3. Navigate to **"Revenue & Land Reform"** → **"Certificate Services"** → **"Residential Certificate"**
4. Fill form:
   - Applicant name, father's name, date of birth
   - District, Sub-Division, Anchal (Circle), Village/Ward
   - Duration of residence in Bihar
   - Purpose of certificate
5. Upload documents (Aadhaar, ration card, voter ID, birth certificate or TC)
6. Pay fee: **₹10** (online/cash) — most cases are free
7. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via RTPS Counter / Loka Seva Kendra

1. Visit the **RTPS counter** at your Block / Anchal office, or **Loka Seva Kendra**
2. Carry Aadhaar, ration card, voter ID
3. Submit form with self-attested documents
4. Get acknowledgement receipt with RTPS reference number
5. Certificate delivered within 21 days (RTPS guarantee)

---

## Status Check & Download

1. Go to [serviceonline.bihar.gov.in](https://serviceonline.bihar.gov.in) → **"Track Application"**
2. Enter reference number
3. Or visit [rtps.bihar.gov.in](https://rtps.bihar.gov.in) → **"RTPS Status"**
4. Once approved: download digitally signed PDF

## NEET Bihar State Quota 2026

For **NEET 2026 Bihar state quota** (BCECEB medical/dental counselling):
1. Bihar residential certificate (Awasiya Praman Patra) — permanent resident proof
2. OR born in Bihar (birth certificate from Bihar)
3. Studied in Bihar schools for prescribed period (check BCECEB notification)
4. Certificate must be Circle Officer / Anchal Adhikari issued
5. Apply well before NEET results — 15–21 day processing
6. BCECEB verifies during counselling document check

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Bihar domicile vs residential certificate — same thing?

Yes. In Bihar, the certificate is called **Awasiya Praman Patra** (Residential Certificate). It serves as the domicile proof for all purposes — BPSC, BCECEB, scholarships. "Domicile certificate" is an informal term for the same document.

### How long must I live in Bihar for the residential certificate?

Bihar does not mandate a strict fixed-year threshold like Rajasthan (10 years) or Maharashtra (15 years). **Birth in Bihar** or **permanent residence with supporting documents** is sufficient. Students studying in Bihar schools for years can use school TCs as proof.

### BPSC — is Bihar residential certificate mandatory?

**Yes, for most Bihar state government posts**, BPSC requires proof of Bihar domicile. Verify in the specific BPSC advertisement — some All-India posts (IAS/IPS cadre exams) don't require state domicile.
