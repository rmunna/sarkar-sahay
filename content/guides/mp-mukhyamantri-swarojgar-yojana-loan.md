## What is MP Mukhyamantri Swarojgar Yojana?

**Mukhyamantri Swarojgar Yojana (MSY)** is a Madhya Pradesh government scheme that provides **bank loans with margin money subsidy** to unemployed youth and those wanting to start their own business. The scheme aims to promote entrepreneurship and self-employment across Madhya Pradesh.

---

## Key Benefits

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Loan amount | Manufacturing: ₹50,000–₹10 lakh; Service/Trade: ₹50,000–₹2 lakh |
| Margin money (subsidy) | 15% (general), 30% (BPL/SC/ST/women/ex-servicemen) |
| Interest rate | Bank rate — subsidised through interest subvention in some cases |
| Age limit | 18–45 years |
| Education | Minimum Class 5 pass |
| Beneficiaries | Youth, SC/ST, women, ex-servicemen |

---

## Who is Eligible?

**You ARE eligible if:**
- You are between **18 to 45 years** of age
- You are a **permanent resident of Madhya Pradesh**
- You have passed at least **Class 5**
- You are **unemployed or self-employed** (not a government employee)
- You do not have a default on any previous bank loan

**Priority groups (30% margin money):**
- BPL families
- SC/ST candidates
- Women entrepreneurs
- Ex-servicemen and their dependents
- Persons with disabilities

---

## Sectors Covered

The scheme covers almost all business types:
- **Manufacturing** — food processing, tailoring, handicrafts, small workshops
- **Service** — beauty parlour, repair shops, computer institutes, laundry
- **Trade/Retail** — grocery, clothing, mobile/electronics shops
- **Agriculture-based enterprises** — agri-processing, honey, mushroom cultivation

---

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar Card** — identity proof
2. **Domicile certificate** — MP resident proof
3. **Age proof** (Aadhaar with DoB, school certificate)
4. **Educational certificate** — Class 5 or higher pass certificate
5. **Income/caste certificate** (for priority groups)
6. **Business plan / project report** — what business you will start
7. **Bank account details** — Aadhaar-linked
8. **Passport-size photograph**
9. **No-dues certificate** — from any bank (proof you have no default)

## How to Apply — Step by Step

### Step 1 — Apply Online

Go to **[msme.mponline.gov.in](https://msme.mponline.gov.in)**:
1. Register with your Aadhaar and mobile number
2. Login and select **"Mukhyamantri Swarojgar Yojana"**
3. Fill the application form with business details
4. Upload all documents
5. Submit and note the Application Reference Number

### Step 2 — Application to Bank

After online submission, your application goes to the selected bank for processing. The bank:
- Reviews your project report
- Assesses your creditworthiness
- Conducts interview/field visit

### Step 3 — Loan Sanction

Bank sanctions the loan. The margin money (subsidy component) is earmarked in your loan account.

### Step 4 — Business Setup

Use the loan to set up the business — purchase equipment, stock, or establish premises.

### Step 5 — Margin Money Credit

After verifying that the loan has been used for the stated purpose:
- The margin money (15% or 30% subsidy) is credited to your loan account by the MP government
- This reduces your outstanding principal

---

## How Margin Money Works

Example: Loan of ₹2,00,000

| Component | Amount |
|-----------|--------|
| Total loan | ₹2,00,000 |
| Your margin money contribution | ₹0 (margin money replaces your contribution) |
| Government subsidy (15%) | ₹30,000 (credited to bank) |
| Net loan you repay | ₹1,70,000 |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. Can a woman start a beauty parlour under this scheme?**

Yes. Beauty parlours are eligible under the service category. Women get the enhanced 30% margin money subsidy.

**2. I am 50 years old. Am I eligible?**

No. The maximum age is 45 years. If you are above 45, you are not eligible for Mukhyamantri Swarojgar Yojana. Look at PMEGP (national scheme with 35 years limit) or other MP schemes.

**3. Can I apply for a dairy farm under this scheme?**

Dairy/animal husbandry based enterprises may qualify under the agri-processing category. Confirm with the District Industries Centre (DIC) before applying.

**4. I was rejected once. Can I apply again?**

Yes, after 6 months. Address the reason for rejection (credit history, incomplete documents, weak project plan) before reapplying.

**5. Which bank should I go to?**

After online application, you choose a bank. Nationalized banks, cooperative banks, and RRBs all participate. SBI, Central Bank of India, Bank of India, and UCO Bank are common in MP.
