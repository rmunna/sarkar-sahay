# Post-Matric Scholarship for SC/ST Students – Puducherry

The Post-Matric Scholarship for SC/ST students in Puducherry provides financial support to Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe students pursuing education beyond Class 10. The scholarship covers tuition fees, maintenance allowance, and other expenses, funded by both the central and Puducherry governments.

## What is the Post-Matric Scholarship?

This scholarship supports SC/ST students from Class 11 onwards through degree, postgraduate, and professional courses. It covers compulsory non-refundable fees, maintenance allowance for hostelers and day scholars, and other charges. The scheme is implemented through the Department of Social Welfare, Puducherry.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Community:** Must belong to Scheduled Caste (SC) or Scheduled Tribe (ST)
2. **Education:** Studying in Class 11 or above (post-matric level) in a recognized institution
3. **Residency:** Must be a resident of Puducherry UT
4. **Income:** Annual parental/guardian income should not exceed ₹2,50,000
5. **Attendance:** Must maintain minimum required attendance
6. **Not availing similar scholarship:** Should not be receiving any other scholarship for the same purpose

## Scholarship Components

| Component | Day Scholar | Hosteler |
|-----------|------------|---------|
| Maintenance allowance | ₹550 – ₹1,200/month* | ₹820 – ₹1,500/month* |
| Tuition fees | As per actuals | As per actuals |
| Exam fees | Covered | Covered |
| Study tour charges | Covered (limited) | Covered (limited) |

*Rates vary by course level (Class 11-12, UG, PG, Professional). Check latest NSP rates.*

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar card (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- Caste certificate (SC/ST) from competent authority
- Income certificate (parental income below ₹2,50,000)
- Previous year mark sheet / passing certificate
- College/school admission receipt and bonafide certificate
- Bank passbook of student (Aadhaar-linked)
- Passport-size photograph
- Fee receipt from institution
- Residence proof

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Online Application (via National Scholarship Portal)

1. **Visit NSP:** Go to [scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in/)
2. **Register:** Create a new account (for fresh applicants) using Aadhaar and mobile number
3. **Login:** Log in with your credentials
4. **Select scholarship:** Choose "Post-Matric Scholarship for SC/ST — Puducherry"
5. **Fill application:** Enter personal, educational, income, and bank details
6. **Upload documents:** Upload scanned copies of caste certificate, income certificate, mark sheets, fee receipts
7. **Submit:** Review and submit the application
8. **Institute verification:** Your institution verifies and forwards the application
9. **District verification:** District welfare officer verifies caste and income details
10. **Scholarship sanctioned:** Approved scholarship amount credited to your bank account

### Renewal

- Existing beneficiaries must apply for renewal each academic year on NSP
- Update mark sheets and fee receipts for the new year

## Processing Time

- **Application window:** As per NSP schedule (typically August–November)
- **Verification:** 30–60 days
- **Disbursement:** Within 2–3 months of approval

## Important Tips

1. **Apply on NSP within deadline:** The National Scholarship Portal has strict deadlines — don't miss them
2. **Keep caste and income certificates current:** Expired certificates will delay processing
3. **Student's own bank account:** The scholarship is credited to the student's bank account, not parent's
4. **Maintain academic performance:** Poor academic performance or failing may affect renewal
5. **Check NSP regularly:** Track your application status and respond to any queries promptly

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: What is the income limit for eligibility?
Annual parental/guardian income should not exceed ₹2,50,000.

### Q2: Can students studying outside Puducherry apply?
Students domiciled in Puducherry studying in recognized institutions anywhere in India can apply.

### Q3: Is this scholarship available for professional courses?
Yes, medical, engineering, law, MBA, and other professional courses are covered.

### Q4: How is the scholarship amount credited?
Directly to the student's Aadhaar-linked bank account via DBT.

### Q5: Can I avail this scholarship along with other scholarships?
Generally, you cannot receive two scholarships for the same purpose. However, you may receive merit-based awards alongside.

### Q6: What if I fail an exam?
Scholarship for the repeated year may not be provided. You must pass to continue receiving benefits.

### Q7: Where do I apply — NSP or local office?
Apply on the National Scholarship Portal ([scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in/)). Physical documents may also be submitted to your institution.
