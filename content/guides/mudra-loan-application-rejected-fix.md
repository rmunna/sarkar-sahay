# Mudra Loan Application Rejected — Reasons & What to Do Next

If your Mudra loan (PMMY) application has been rejected by a bank, you're not alone. Banks reject a significant number of Mudra loan applications, but most rejections are fixable. This guide explains every common reason for rejection and gives you a clear plan to get your loan approved.

## What Is a Mudra Loan?

Under the Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY), banks and NBFCs provide loans up to ₹10 lakh to small businesses and entrepreneurs. The three categories are:

- **Shishu** — loans up to ₹50,000
- **Kishore** — loans from ₹50,001 to ₹5 lakh
- **Tarun** — loans from ₹5,00,001 to ₹10 lakh

No collateral is required for Mudra loans, and there is no processing fee charged by the government (banks may charge nominal fees).

## Common Rejection Reasons

### 1. Low CIBIL / Credit Score

This is the most common reason. Most banks require a CIBIL score of **650 or above** for Mudra loans. If your score is below this:

- You have unpaid credit card bills or old loan defaults
- You were a guarantor for someone who defaulted
- Too many recent loan inquiries lowered your score

### 2. Incomplete or Wrong Documents

- Business plan or project report not submitted
- Identity or address proof documents missing
- Bank statements for the last 6-12 months not provided
- ITR or GST returns not filed (for Kishore and Tarun categories)
- Udyam registration certificate missing

### 3. Weak Business Plan

- No clear revenue model or profit projections
- Business is in a high-risk sector (banks' internal classification)
- Business has been operating for less than 6 months (some banks prefer 1+ years)
- Projected income doesn't justify the loan amount requested

### 4. Existing Loan Defaults

- You have an active NPA (Non-Performing Asset) with any bank
- Previous Mudra loan was not repaid on time
- Outstanding dues on other loans or credit cards

### 5. Bank-Specific Issues

- The particular branch has exhausted its Mudra loan budget for the quarter
- The bank officer is not processing Mudra loans proactively (unfortunately common)
- Internal risk assessment by the bank flags your application

### 6. Ineligible Business Type

Mudra loans are for non-farm, non-corporate micro and small enterprises. You are not eligible if:
- You are applying for personal/consumption use
- Your business is a large corporation
- The activity is agricultural (farm loans have separate schemes)

## Step-by-Step: What to Do After Rejection

### Step 1 — Get the Rejection Reason in Writing

- Ask the bank for a **written rejection letter** with specific reasons
- Banks are required to provide this under RBI guidelines
- If the bank refuses, file a complaint on the RBI CMS portal at [cms.rbi.org.in](https://cms.rbi.org.in/)

### Step 2 — Fix Your Credit Score

If CIBIL score is the issue:

1. Check your CIBIL score for free at [cibil.com](https://www.cibil.com/) (one free report per year)
2. Pay off all outstanding credit card dues and EMIs
3. Dispute any errors in your CIBIL report — incorrect defaults or wrong entries can be corrected
4. Wait 2-3 months for the score to improve after clearing dues
5. Avoid applying for multiple loans simultaneously (each inquiry lowers your score)

See our [CIBIL Score Check & Improvement Guide](/guide/credit-score-cibil-check-improve) for detailed steps.

### Step 3 — Strengthen Your Documents

Prepare a complete document package:

- **Identity proof:** Aadhaar, PAN card
- **Address proof:** Aadhaar, utility bill, or rent agreement
- **Business proof:** Udyam registration, shop licence, GST certificate
- **Financial documents:** Bank statements (6-12 months), ITR (last 2 years), GST returns
- **Business plan:** Clear project report with cost breakdown, expected revenue, and repayment plan
- **Quotations:** If the loan is for equipment or machinery, include vendor quotations

### Step 4 — Apply at a Different Bank

- You are not limited to one bank. Apply at any bank, NBFC, or MFI that offers Mudra loans
- Public sector banks (SBI, PNB, BOB) and small finance banks often have higher approval rates for Mudra loans
- Try [psbloansin59minutes.com](https://psbloansin59minutes.com/) for a faster digital application process
- Microfinance institutions (MFIs) may approve Shishu loans with lower documentation requirements

### Step 5 — Escalate if Needed

If you believe the rejection is unfair:

1. **Complain to the bank's grievance cell** — write to the branch manager or nodal officer
2. **File a complaint on the PMMY portal** — visit [mudra.org.in](https://mudra.org.in/) → Grievance section
3. **RBI Banking Ombudsman** — file a complaint at [cms.rbi.org.in](https://cms.rbi.org.in/) if the bank doesn't respond within 30 days
4. **Contact DFS (Department of Financial Services)** — use the CPGRAMS portal at [pgportal.gov.in](https://pgportal.gov.in/)

## When to Contact the Helpline

Contact the Mudra/PMMY helpline if:

- The bank refuses to give a written rejection reason
- Your application has been pending for more than 30 days with no response
- You suspect the bank is deliberately not processing Mudra loans
- You've been asked to pay a bribe or unofficial fee for loan processing

**Helpline contacts:**
- **PMMY Helpline:** 1800-180-1111 (toll-free)
- **Mudra Portal:** [mudra.org.in](https://mudra.org.in/)
- **RBI CMS:** [cms.rbi.org.in](https://cms.rbi.org.in/)

## FAQs

### Can I re-apply for a Mudra loan after rejection?

Yes. There is no restriction on re-applying. Fix the issues mentioned in the rejection letter, improve your credit score if needed, and apply again — either at the same bank or a different one.

### What is the minimum CIBIL score required for a Mudra loan?

Most banks require a CIBIL score of 650 or above. For Shishu loans (up to ₹50,000), some banks and MFIs may be more flexible. There is no official minimum set by the government, but individual banks have their own thresholds.

### Can a bank reject a Mudra loan without giving a reason?

Banks are required by RBI guidelines to provide a written reason for loan rejection. If a bank refuses, you can file a complaint on the RBI CMS portal at [cms.rbi.org.in](https://cms.rbi.org.in/).

### Is there any collateral required for Mudra loans?

No. Mudra loans up to ₹10 lakh do not require any collateral or guarantee. If a bank demands collateral, it is a violation of PMMY guidelines — report it on [mudra.org.in](https://mudra.org.in/).

### How long should I wait before re-applying after rejection?

If the rejection was due to low CIBIL score, wait 2-3 months after clearing outstanding dues. For document issues, you can re-apply immediately after preparing the correct documents. For bank-specific issues, try a different bank right away.

### Can I apply for a Mudra loan at multiple banks simultaneously?

Yes, but this is not recommended. Multiple loan inquiries within a short period can lower your CIBIL score. Apply at one bank, wait for the result, and then try another if rejected.

### Do I need a business plan for a Shishu loan?

For Shishu loans (up to ₹50,000), a detailed business plan is usually not required. A simple description of your business activity is sufficient. For Kishore and Tarun categories, a proper project report is necessary.
