## What is Property Registration Status Check?

After you submit your property or land registration documents at the Sub-Registrar Office (SRO), the registration process may take a few days to complete. During this time, you can track the status of your property registration online through your state's official registration portal.

Each state in India has its own registration department and online portal. This guide covers the **top 5 states** — Maharashtra, Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, and Telangana — with step-by-step instructions for checking your property registration status.

## Why Check Property Registration Status?

- Confirm whether your property deed has been registered successfully
- Track pending registrations and know the expected completion date
- Download the registered document or encumbrance certificate
- Verify registration details (property value, stamp duty paid, parties involved)
- Detect any discrepancies early and raise corrections

## State-Wise Property Registration Status Check

### 1. Maharashtra — IGR (Inspector General of Registration)

**Portal:** [igrmaharashtra.gov.in](https://igrmaharashtra.gov.in)

#### How to Check Status Online

1. Visit [https://igrmaharashtra.gov.in](https://igrmaharashtra.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Document Search"** or **"Index-II Search"** on the homepage
3. Select your **District**, **SRO (Sub-Registrar Office)**, and **Year**
4. Enter the **Document Number** or **Token Number** received at the time of registration
5. Fill in the captcha and click **"Search"**
6. Your registration status and document details will appear on screen

#### Check by Document Number

- Use the **document number** printed on your receipt from the SRO
- Alternatively, search by **party name** (buyer or seller) and year of registration

#### Download Registered Document

- Go to **"E-Search"** section on the IGR portal
- Search using document number, year, and SRO details
- Download the certified copy (charges apply — ₹15 per page)

**Helpline:** 020-26050600 / 1800-1234-8783 (Toll-Free)

---

### 2. Karnataka — Kaveri Online Portal

**Portal:** [kaveri2.karnataka.gov.in](https://kaveri2.karnataka.gov.in)

#### How to Check Status Online

1. Visit [https://kaveri2.karnataka.gov.in](https://kaveri2.karnataka.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Document Search"** or **"Know Your Document Status"**
3. Log in with your registered mobile number or create an account
4. Enter the **Application Number** or **Document Number**
5. Select the **SRO** and **Year of Registration**
6. Click **"Search"** to view the current status

#### Check by Application Number

- Use the application number from your Kaveri online booking receipt
- Status shows: Pending, Approved, Registered, or Rejected

#### Encumbrance Certificate (EC) Check

- Go to **"EC Search"** on the Kaveri portal
- Enter property survey number and village details
- Download EC to verify property has no legal liabilities

**Helpline:** 080-22384901 / Kaveri Helpdesk: 080-22384902

---

### 3. Uttar Pradesh — IGRS UP

**Portal:** [igrsup.gov.in](https://igrsup.gov.in)

#### How to Check Status Online

1. Visit [https://igrsup.gov.in](https://igrsup.gov.in)
2. Click on **"सम्पत्ति पंजीकरण"** (Property Registration) section
3. Select **"आवेदन की स्थिति"** (Application Status)
4. Enter your **Application Number** received during property registration
5. Enter the **captcha code** and click **"Submit"**
6. The registration status with current stage will be displayed

#### Check by Party Name

- Go to **"सम्पत्ति खोजें"** (Property Search) section
- Enter the buyer or seller's name, district, and year
- View matching property registration records

**Helpline:** 0522-2620477 / Toll-Free: 1800-1800-150

---

### 4. Tamil Nadu — TNREGINET

**Portal:** [tnreginet.gov.in](https://tnreginet.gov.in)

#### How to Check Status Online

1. Visit [https://tnreginet.gov.in](https://tnreginet.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Document Status"** or **"Search Documents"** from the menu
3. Select your **District** and **Sub-Registrar Office**
4. Enter the **Document Number** and **Year of Registration**
5. Click **"Search"** to view your property registration status

#### Encumbrance Certificate Search

- Select **"EC Search"** from the TNREGINET portal
- Enter property details — survey number, village, taluk
- Check for any encumbrances or legal claims on the property
- Download EC online (₹100 per copy)

**Helpline:** 044-25346060 / Email: tnreginet@tn.gov.in

---

### 5. Telangana — IGRS Telangana

**Portal:** [registration.telangana.gov.in](https://registration.telangana.gov.in)

#### How to Check Status Online

1. Visit [https://registration.telangana.gov.in](https://registration.telangana.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Document Search"** from the top menu
3. Select search type: by **Document Number**, **Party Name**, or **Survey Number**
4. Enter the required details — district, SRO, year, and document number
5. Click **"Search"** to view the registration status and details

#### Check Encumbrance Certificate

- Go to **"EC Search"** option on the portal
- Enter the property survey number and registration district
- View and download the encumbrance certificate

#### DHARANI Portal for Land Records

- For agricultural land, use [dharani.telangana.gov.in](https://dharani.telangana.gov.in)
- Enter your Aadhaar number or Pattadar Passbook number
- View land records, mutation status, and registration details

**Helpline:** 040-24600199 / Toll-Free: 1800-599-9899

## Check Property Registration Status by SMS

Most states do not offer SMS-based property registration status checks. However:

- **Telangana:** Send SMS to 56677 with your document number (format varies — check the IGRS portal for latest SMS codes)
- **Other states:** Use the official portal or visit your local SRO office

## Common Issues and Fixes

### 1. Status Shows "Pending" for Too Long
- **Reason:** Document may be pending signature from the Sub-Registrar or awaiting stamp duty verification
- **Fix:** Visit your SRO with the receipt and enquire. Normal processing takes 3–7 working days

### 2. Cannot Find Document in Online Search
- **Reason:** Recently registered documents may take 7–15 days to reflect online
- **Fix:** Wait for 2 weeks after registration. If still not visible, contact the SRO

### 3. Wrong Details in Registered Document
- **Reason:** Data entry error during registration
- **Fix:** File a correction request at the SRO. You may need to re-submit supporting documents

### 4. Portal Not Loading or Showing Error
- **Reason:** Heavy traffic or server maintenance
- **Fix:** Try during off-peak hours (early morning or late evening). Clear browser cache and try again

### 5. Stamp Duty Mismatch / Payment Issue
- **Reason:** Incorrect stamp duty calculation or payment failure
- **Fix:** Contact the SRO with payment receipt. Check the state's stamp duty calculator on the portal

## Important Tips

1. **Keep your registration receipt safe** — the token/document number is essential for tracking
2. **Check status after 3–5 working days** — most registrations complete within this period
3. **Download and save the registered document** as soon as it becomes available online
4. **Verify all details** in the registered document (names, property details, stamp duty) immediately
5. **Get the Encumbrance Certificate** after registration to confirm clean title

## FAQs

### How long does property registration take in India?
Property registration typically takes 1–7 working days after document submission at the SRO. Online slot booking and prior stamp duty payment can speed up the process.

### Can I check property registration status without a document number?
Yes, most state portals allow you to search by party name (buyer/seller), survey number, or property address. However, document number gives the most accurate result.

### Is property registration status check free?
Yes, checking the status online is completely free on all state portals. You only pay for downloading certified copies or encumbrance certificates.

### What should I do if my property registration is rejected?
Contact your SRO to understand the reason for rejection. Common reasons include stamp duty shortfall, document discrepancy, or incomplete paperwork. Rectify the issue and re-apply.

### Can I download my registered property document online?
Yes, most state portals allow you to download a digital copy of the registered document. Maharashtra (IGR), Karnataka (Kaveri), Tamil Nadu (TNREGINET), and Telangana (IGRS) all support online document download.

### What is an Encumbrance Certificate and why do I need it?
An Encumbrance Certificate (EC) is a document that proves a property is free from any legal or monetary liability. It is essential for home loans, property resale, and legal verification.

### How do I check property registration status in states not covered here?
Visit your state's official registration department website. Most states follow a similar process — search for "IGRS [State Name]" or "[State Name] registration department" to find the official portal.
