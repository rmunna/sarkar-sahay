# CMST Scheme Manipur – Complete Guide 2026

## What is the CMST Scheme?

The **Chief Ministergi Shotharabasingi Tengbang (CMST)** — meaning "Chief Minister's Support for the People" — is a flagship financial assistance scheme of the Government of Manipur. The scheme provides monthly cash transfers to economically vulnerable families in the state, targeting women as the primary beneficiaries to ensure the money reaches the household.

CMST is Manipur's equivalent of direct benefit transfer programmes in other states, designed to address poverty and provide a financial safety net.

## Benefits

| Component | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Monthly cash transfer | ₹1,000-1,500 per family |
| Payment mode | Direct Bank Transfer (DBT) |
| Primary beneficiary | Woman of the household |
| Payment frequency | Monthly |

> Exact benefit amounts may vary based on current government notifications.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Residency:** Permanent resident of Manipur
- **Gender:** One woman per household (as the primary beneficiary)
- **Income:** Annual family income below ₹2-2.5 lakh
- **No government employee** in the family
- **Not an income tax payer**
- **Not owning** a four-wheeler vehicle
- Must have an **Aadhaar-linked bank account**

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar Card of the beneficiary (woman)
- Voter ID / residential proof
- Income certificate (from Deputy Commissioner / SDM)
- Bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked, in beneficiary's name)
- Ration card / family card
- Passport-size photographs (2)
- Self-declaration form
- Mobile number linked with Aadhaar

## How to Apply – Step-by-Step

### Offline Application (Primary)

1. Visit the **nearest Panchayat office** (rural) or **Municipal Ward office** (urban)
2. Collect the **CMST application form**
3. Fill in personal details, family information, income, and bank details
4. Attach self-attested copies of all documents
5. Get the form verified by the **Village Authority / Ward Member**
6. Submit at the designated collection centre
7. The application is verified at **block and district levels**
8. Approved beneficiaries receive monthly DBT

### Online / Camp-Based

- The government may conduct **enrolment camps** — watch for announcements
- Some applications may be accepted through the **Manipur e-services portal** at [manipur.gov.in](https://manipur.gov.in)

## Processing Time

- **Verification:** 2-4 weeks
- **Approval:** 1-3 months
- **First payment:** Within 1-2 months of approval

## Important Tips

1. **Link Aadhaar to bank account** before applying — mandatory for DBT
2. **Only one beneficiary per family** — typically the woman of the house
3. **Attend enrolment camps** when announced by the district administration
4. **Keep mobile number active** — OTP verification may be required
5. **Report discrepancies** to the Block Development Office immediately

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: What does CMST stand for?
**A:** **Chief Ministergi Shotharabasingi Tengbang**, which means "Chief Minister's Support for the People" in Manipuri.

### Q2: How much money do I get per month?
**A:** Approximately **₹1,000-1,500 per month**, depending on the current government notification.

### Q3: Can a male member apply?
**A:** The primary beneficiary is typically a **woman of the household**. However, in specific cases (no eligible woman), check with local authorities.

### Q4: Can I receive CMST along with pension schemes?
**A:** This depends on state policy. Check with the **District Social Welfare Office** for compatibility rules.

### Q5: How do I check if I'm enrolled?
**A:** Visit the **Panchayat/Municipal office** or check through the state government portal.

### Q6: Is there an application fee?
**A:** **No fee** is charged for CMST applications.
