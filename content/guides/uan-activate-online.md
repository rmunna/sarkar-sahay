# UAN Activate Online: Step-by-Step EPF Registration

Your **UAN (Universal Account Number)** is the key to accessing all EPF services online. Before you can check your PF passbook, file claims, or transfer PF, you must activate your UAN on the EPFO member portal. This guide walks you through the complete activation process.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent informational resource and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government agency.

---

## What is UAN?

The **Universal Account Number (UAN)** is a 12-digit unique number assigned to every EPF member by EPFO. It acts as an umbrella ID that links all your PF Member IDs (from different employers) under one account. Your UAN remains the same throughout your career, even when you change jobs.

---

## Who Gets a UAN?

- **All employees** covered under the EPF & MP Act, 1952
- Employees in establishments with **20 or more workers**
- Your **employer assigns** the UAN when they register you with EPFO
- You can find your UAN on your **salary slip** or by asking your **HR department**

---

## How to Find Your UAN

If you don't know your UAN:

1. **Check your salary slip** — UAN is usually printed on it
2. **Ask your HR department** — They have your UAN from the EPFO employer portal
3. **Use EPFO portal** — Visit [unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in) → Click "Know Your UAN" → Enter your PF Member ID, name, date of birth, and mobile number

---

## How to Activate UAN Online — Step-by-Step

### Prerequisites
- Your **UAN number** (from employer/salary slip)
- **Aadhaar number** or **PAN** or **Member ID**
- **Mobile number** registered with EPFO (the one your employer provided)
- Your **name and date of birth** as per EPFO records

### Activation Steps

1. **Visit** [unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in)
2. **Click** on "Activate UAN" link (below the login box)
3. **Enter** your **UAN number**
4. **Select** verification type — **Aadhaar**, **PAN**, or **Member ID**
5. **Enter** the selected document number
6. **Enter** your **name** and **date of birth** as per EPFO records
7. **Enter** your **mobile number** (must match EPFO records)
8. **Enter** the captcha code
9. **Click** "Get Authorization PIN"
10. **Enter** the **OTP** received on your registered mobile number
11. **Click** "Validate OTP and Activate UAN"
12. Your **password** will be sent via SMS to your registered mobile number

### After Activation

1. **Log in** at [unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in) using UAN and the SMS password
2. **Change your password** immediately (mandatory on first login)
3. **Set security questions** for password recovery
4. You can now access all EPF services

## How to Link Aadhaar with UAN

Aadhaar-UAN linking is mandatory for online PF claims:

1. **Log in** to the EPFO Unified Portal
2. **Go to** "Manage" → "KYC"
3. **Select** "Aadhaar" from the document type dropdown
4. **Enter** your **Aadhaar number** and **name as per Aadhaar**
5. **Click** "Save"
6. Your **employer** must approve the KYC request
7. After employer approval, **EPFO verifies** your Aadhaar with UIDAI
8. Status changes to **"Verified"** once validation is successful

> **Note:** Your name and date of birth must match between Aadhaar and EPFO records. See our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) for correction steps.

---

## How to Link Mobile Number with UAN

If your mobile number has changed:

1. **Ask your employer** to update your mobile number on the EPFO employer portal
2. Alternatively, visit the nearest **EPFO office** with your Aadhaar and UAN
3. Once updated, you can re-activate with the new number

> **Important:** Only the mobile number registered by your employer can be used for UAN activation. You cannot change it directly on the member portal.

---

## How to Reset UAN Password

### Using "Forgot Password"

1. **Visit** the Unified Portal login page
2. **Click** "Forgot Password"
3. **Enter** your UAN number
4. **Verify** via OTP on registered mobile or Aadhaar-linked mobile
5. **Set** a new password
6. **Log in** with the new password

### Password Requirements
- Minimum 8 characters
- At least one uppercase letter
- At least one lowercase letter
- At least one number
- At least one special character (@, #, $, etc.)

## Important Tips

1. **Activate your UAN** as soon as your employer provides it — Don't wait until you need to withdraw PF
2. **Link Aadhaar immediately** after activation — It enables hassle-free online claims
3. **Save your password** securely — You'll need it for passbook checks and claims
4. **Never share your UAN password** or OTP with anyone, including your employer
5. **Keep one mobile number** consistent across Aadhaar and EPFO for smooth OTP verification

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Can I activate UAN without my employer?
No, your employer must first register you on the EPFO employer portal and assign a UAN. However, once the UAN is assigned, you can activate it yourself on the member portal.

### 2. What if I have multiple UANs?
You should have only one UAN. If you have multiple UANs due to different employers, contact your current employer or visit the nearest EPFO office to merge them.

### 3. Can I activate UAN using Aadhaar instead of Member ID?
Yes, during activation you can choose Aadhaar, PAN, or Member ID as the verification document. Any one is sufficient.

### 4. My employer hasn't given me my UAN. What should I do?
Ask your HR department. If they are unresponsive, you can use the "Know Your UAN" feature on the EPFO portal with your Member ID and personal details.

### 5. How long does UAN activation take?
UAN activation is instant if your details (name, DOB, mobile number) match the EPFO records. The OTP is received within 1-2 minutes.

### 6. Can I use UAN portal on mobile?
Yes, the EPFO Unified Portal works on mobile browsers. You can also use the **UMANG app** for basic EPF services like passbook viewing and claim tracking.

### 7. Is UAN mandatory for PF withdrawal?
Yes, UAN activation with Aadhaar linking is now mandatory for online PF withdrawal claims. Without it, you would need to submit physical forms.
