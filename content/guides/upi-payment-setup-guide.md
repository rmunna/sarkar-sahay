## What Is UPI?

**Unified Payments Interface (UPI)** is India's real-time, instant payment system developed by the **National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI)** and regulated by the **Reserve Bank of India (RBI)**. UPI lets you send and receive money directly between bank accounts using your smartphone — 24/7, 365 days, including holidays.

With over **14 billion transactions per month** (as of late 2024), UPI is India's most-used digital payment method. It powers payments at shops, online orders, bill payments, and peer-to-peer transfers — all for free.

> **Good to know:** You need an [active bank account](/guide/open-bank-account-online) to use UPI. If you don't have one, open one first.

## Who Can Use UPI?

- Any Indian resident with a **bank account** linked to a **mobile number**
- The mobile number must be **registered with the bank** (for OTP verification)
- Age: **18+ years** (some banks allow minor accounts with guardian's UPI)
- Smartphone with internet connection (Android or iOS)
- Valid **debit card** (needed for first-time registration on most apps)

## Documents & Requirements

To set up UPI, you need:

- **Active bank account** — savings or current account at any Indian bank
- **Registered mobile number** — the number linked to your bank account (for OTP/SMS verification)
- **Smartphone** — Android or iOS with internet connection
- **Debit card** — last 6 digits and expiry date needed for initial UPI PIN setup
- **Valid ID (optional)** — some banks may require Aadhaar-based authentication if you don't have a debit card

## How to Set Up UPI — Step-by-Step

The setup process is similar across all apps. Here's the general flow:

### Step 1: Download a UPI App

Download any UPI app from **Google Play Store** or **Apple App Store**:
- Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM

### Step 2: Register with Your Mobile Number

1. Open the app and enter your **mobile number** (must be the same number registered with your bank)
2. The app will send an **SMS** for verification — allow SMS permissions
3. Verify the OTP sent to your number

### Step 3: Link Your Bank Account

1. Tap **"Add Bank Account"** or **"Link Bank"**
2. Select your bank from the list
3. The app will automatically detect accounts linked to your mobile number
4. Select the account you want to link
5. Your account will be verified via SMS

### Step 4: Create Your UPI ID (VPA)

Your **UPI ID** (also called Virtual Payment Address / VPA) is your unique payment address. It looks like:

- `yourname@okaxis` (Google Pay)
- `yourname@ybl` (PhonePe)
- `yourname@paytm` (Paytm)
- `yourname@upi` (BHIM)

Most apps auto-generate a UPI ID. You can usually customise it in Settings.

### Step 5: Set Your UPI PIN

1. The app will ask you to **set a UPI PIN** (4 or 6 digits, depending on your bank)
2. Enter the **last 6 digits of your debit card number** and the **expiry date**
3. Enter an OTP sent by your bank
4. Create your UPI PIN — **remember this PIN**, you'll need it for every transaction

> ⚠️ **Never share your UPI PIN with anyone** — not even bank officials.

### Step 6: Start Transacting

You're ready! You can now send money, receive payments, pay bills, and shop online using UPI.

## How to Send Money via UPI

### Option 1: Using UPI ID
1. Open your UPI app → Tap **"Send Money"** or **"Pay"**
2. Enter the receiver's **UPI ID** (e.g., `name@upi`)
3. Enter the **amount**
4. Add a note (optional)
5. Enter your **UPI PIN** to confirm

### Option 2: Using Mobile Number
1. Tap **"Send"** → Select the contact from your phone
2. If they have UPI linked to that number, you can send money directly

### Option 3: Scan QR Code
1. Tap **"Scan"** → Point camera at the receiver's QR code
2. Enter amount → Confirm with UPI PIN

### Option 4: Using Account Number & IFSC
1. Select **"Bank Transfer"**
2. Enter the receiver's **account number** and **IFSC code**
3. Enter amount → Confirm with UPI PIN

## How to Receive Money

- Share your **UPI ID** with the sender
- Show your **QR code** (available in all UPI apps)
- Request money using the **"Request"** or **"Collect"** option — the sender gets a notification to approve

## What Is UPI Lite?

**UPI Lite** is a feature launched by NPCI that allows **small-value transactions without entering your UPI PIN**. Think of it as a small digital wallet loaded from your bank account.

### How UPI Lite Works:
1. Enable UPI Lite from your UPI app's settings
2. Load money into UPI Lite wallet (max ₹2,000 balance)
3. Make payments up to **₹500 per transaction** — no PIN needed
4. Works even with **limited or no internet** (UPI Lite X)
5. Transactions are faster since they skip bank server processing

**Best for:** Quick chai, auto-rickshaw, small shop payments.

## UPI Transaction Failed? How to Fix

UPI payments can fail for several reasons. Here are common issues and fixes:

### 1. "Transaction Declined by Bank"
- **Cause:** Insufficient balance, daily limit exceeded, or bank server issue
- **Fix:** Check balance, wait 30 minutes, try again

### 2. "Money Debited but Not Received"
- **Cause:** Bank processed the debit but receiver's bank didn't respond
- **Fix:** Money is **automatically refunded within 3-5 business days**. If not, file a complaint in the app under transaction history

### 3. "UPI PIN Incorrect"
- **Cause:** Wrong PIN entered
- **Fix:** Try again carefully. After 3 wrong attempts, your UPI may be **blocked for 24 hours**

### 4. "Beneficiary Bank Not Available"
- **Cause:** Receiver's bank server is down
- **Fix:** Wait and retry after some time

### 5. "Mobile Number Not Registered"
- **Cause:** Your mobile number isn't linked to your bank account
- **Fix:** Visit your bank branch or use net banking to update your mobile number

### 6. "Transaction Timeout"
- **Cause:** Slow internet or server overload
- **Fix:** Check transaction status in app history before retrying (to avoid double payment)

### How to Raise a UPI Complaint:
1. Open your UPI app → Go to **Transaction History**
2. Select the failed transaction → Tap **"Raise Dispute"** or **"Report Issue"**
3. You can also complain on the **NPCI UPI helpline**: **npci.org.in** → "UPI" → "Dispute Redressal"
4. If unresolved in 30 days, approach the **Banking Ombudsman** via RBI's portal

## UPI Safety Tips

1. **Never share your UPI PIN** — not with friends, family, or anyone calling as "bank staff"
2. **Don't scan unknown QR codes** — scammers send QR codes for "receiving money" that actually debit your account
3. **Verify collect requests** — only approve requests from people you know
4. **Use app lock** — enable fingerprint/PIN lock on your UPI app
5. **Check the UPI ID** before sending — one wrong character and money goes elsewhere
6. **Don't install screen-sharing apps** if someone asks you to — this is a common scam
7. **Set transaction limits** — most apps let you set per-day spending limits
8. **Keep your app updated** — updates include security patches

> 🚨 **Remember:** You do NOT need to enter your UPI PIN to **receive** money. If someone asks you to enter PIN or scan a QR to receive money, it's a **scam**.

## How to Reset or Change UPI PIN

1. Open your UPI app → Go to **"Manage Bank Account"** or **"Bank Settings"**
2. Select your linked bank → Tap **"Change UPI PIN"** or **"Forgot UPI PIN"**
3. Enter last 6 digits of your debit card + expiry date
4. Verify with OTP
5. Set a new UPI PIN

## How to Deregister or Remove UPI

If you want to stop using UPI on a particular app:
1. Open the app → **Settings** → **Linked Accounts**
2. Select the bank account → Tap **"Delink"** or **"Remove"**
3. Uninstall the app if needed

Your bank account remains active — only the UPI link is removed.

## FAQs

### Is UPI free to use?
Yes. UPI transactions are **completely free** for individuals — no charges for sending or receiving money. RBI has mandated zero charges on UPI for P2P and P2M transactions until further notice.

### Can I use UPI without a debit card?
You need a debit card only for the **initial setup** (to set UPI PIN). After that, all transactions work without a card. If you don't have a debit card, some banks allow Aadhaar-based authentication through BHIM app.

### Can I link multiple bank accounts to one UPI app?
Yes. Most UPI apps allow you to link **multiple bank accounts** from different banks and switch between them while making payments.

### What happens if I send money to the wrong UPI ID?
UPI transactions are **instant and irreversible**. If you send money to the wrong person, you'll need to contact the recipient directly. If you don't know them, file a complaint through your UPI app and your bank.

### Can I use UPI on two phones?
Your UPI registration is linked to your **SIM card**. You can only use UPI on the phone that has the registered SIM. If you switch phones, you'll need to re-register.

### Is there a UPI transaction charge from banks?
As of 2025, banks do not charge for UPI P2P or P2M transactions. However, some banks may charge for transactions exceeding certain limits on specific categories. Check your bank's fee schedule.

### How do I check UPI transaction status?
Open your UPI app → Go to **Transaction History** → Tap on the transaction. Status will show as **Success**, **Pending**, or **Failed**. Pending transactions usually resolve within 48 hours.

### Can NRIs use UPI?
Yes, NRIs with an **NRE/NRO account** linked to an Indian mobile number can use UPI. NPCI has enabled UPI for NRIs from select countries using international mobile numbers as well.
