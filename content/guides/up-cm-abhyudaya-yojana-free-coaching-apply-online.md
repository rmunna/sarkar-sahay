# UP CM Abhyudaya Yojana Apply Online 2026 — Free IAS/PCS/NEET/JEE Coaching

The **Mukhyamantri Abhyudaya Yojana** is the Uttar Pradesh government's free coaching scheme for competitive exam aspirants — covering **IAS/PCS (civil services), JEE (engineering), NEET (medical), NDA, CDS, and other professional entrance exams**.

> **Quick answer:** Register at [abhyuday.up.gov.in](https://abhyuday.up.gov.in). Admissions for 2026 session began May 18, 2026. Selection is via an entrance exam or interview. SC/ST/OBC students get priority. Coaching is free — including study material.

---

## What You Get

| Benefit | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Coaching** | Free offline + online coaching |
| **Exams covered** | IAS/PCS, JEE, NEET, NDA, CDS, CLAT, other competitive exams |
| **Medium** | Hindi and English |
| **Study material** | Free |
| **Mentorship** | IAS/PCS officers as mentors |
| **Mode** | Hybrid — offline centres + online classes |

Coaching centres are set up at **divisional headquarters** across UP. Online classes run on the Abhyudaya UP portal for students in remote areas.

---

## Eligibility

| Criteria | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Residence** | Permanent resident of Uttar Pradesh |
| **Category** | SC, ST, OBC — priority; General/EWS also eligible |
| **Income** | Economically weaker students preferred (BPL/low income) |
| **Qualification** | Varies by exam — 12th pass for JEE/NEET/NDA; Graduate for IAS/PCS |
| **Age** | Per the competitive exam you're targeting |

All UP domicile students from weaker economic backgrounds can benefit — SC/ST/OBC students get preferential selection.

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Online Registration at abhyuday.up.gov.in

**Step 1:** Go to [abhyuday.up.gov.in](https://abhyuday.up.gov.in)

**Step 2:** Click **"Register"** / **"New Registration"** on the homepage.

**Step 3:** Enter your name, mobile number, Aadhaar number — verify with OTP.

**Step 4:** Fill in your details:
- Personal information (name, date of birth, address)
- Category (SC/ST/OBC/General/EWS)
- Exam you want coaching for (IAS/JEE/NEET/NDA etc.)
- Current qualification
- Preferred coaching centre (choose the division nearest to you)

**Step 5:** Upload documents (Aadhaar, domicile, caste certificate, qualification proof, photo).

**Step 6:** Submit the form. Note your registration number.

---

## Selection Process

1. **Entrance exam or interview** — conducted division-wise after registration
2. **Merit list** prepared based on exam/interview marks
3. **Selection priority:** SC/ST → OBC → EWS → General (lower income preferred within each)
4. **Seat allotment** — coaching centre + batch confirmed by SMS/portal
5. **Reporting** — attend offline orientation at the allotted centre

## Coaching Centres — Where

Abhyudaya coaching centres are at **18 divisional headquarters** across UP:
Lucknow, Agra, Varanasi, Prayagraj, Kanpur, Gorakhpur, Meerut, Bareilly, Moradabad, Aligarh, Jhansi, Chitrakoot, Azamgarh, Devipatan, Ayodhya, Basti, Mirzapur, Saharanpur.

Online classes are available for all registered students via the portal — useful if you cannot attend offline.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is this scheme only for SC/ST students?**
No — all UP domicile students from economically weaker backgrounds can apply. SC/ST/OBC get priority in selection, but General/EWS students are also considered.

**Is the coaching completely free?**
Yes — coaching, study material, and access to online classes are all free of cost.

**Can I apply for more than one exam's coaching?**
You apply for one exam/category per registration. Contact your divisional centre to clarify if multi-exam coaching is possible.

**How long is the coaching programme?**
Typically 1 year for IAS/PCS; shorter for JEE/NEET based on the batch cycle.

**What if I miss the entrance exam?**
Check the portal for the next batch schedule. The government runs multiple batches per year.
