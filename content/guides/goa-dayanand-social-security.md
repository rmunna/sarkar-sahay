# Dayanand Social Security Scheme (DSSS) Goa – Complete Guide 2026

The **Dayanand Social Security Scheme (DSSS)** is Goa's flagship social security pension program. It provides **₹2,500 to ₹3,500 per month** to senior citizens, persons with disabilities, widows, single women, and HIV/AIDS patients. If you're looking to apply, check your application status, or understand the payment schedule, this guide covers everything you need.

> **Looking for all Goa government schemes?** Check our complete [Goa Government Schemes & Services Guide](/guide/goa-government-schemes-services).

## Pension Amount Under DSSS

| Category | Monthly Pension |
|----------|----------------|
| Senior Citizens (60–79 years) | ₹2,500 |
| Senior Citizens (80+ years) | ₹3,500 |
| Persons with Disability (40%+) | ₹2,500 |
| Widows / Single Women | ₹2,500 |
| HIV/AIDS Patients | ₹2,500 |

> **Note:** If you are a widow receiving benefits under the [Griha Aadhar Scheme](/guide/goa-griha-aadhar-scheme), you must cancel that benefit before applying for DSSS (as per the February 2025 amendment). You cannot receive both simultaneously.

## Who is Eligible for DSSS?

### Senior Citizen Pension
- **Age:** 60 years or above
- **Residency:** Resident of Goa for at least **15 continuous years**
- **Income:** Annual family income below ₹3,00,000

### Disability Pension
- **Disability:** 40% or more (certified by government medical board)
- **Age:** No minimum age requirement
- **Residency:** 15 years in Goa
- **Income:** Annual family income below ₹3,00,000

### Widow / Single Woman Pension
- **Status:** Widowed, divorced, deserted, judicially separated, or unmarried woman above 50 years
- **Residency:** 15 years in Goa
- **Income:** Annual family income below ₹3,00,000
- **Age crossover:** If a widow crosses 60 years, she can choose either widow or senior citizen category — but **not both**

### HIV/AIDS Patient Pension
- **Condition:** Certified HIV/AIDS positive status from a government hospital
- **Residency:** 15 years in Goa
- **Income:** Annual family income below ₹3,00,000

## Documents Required

### For All Applicants
- Aadhaar card
- 15-year Residence Certificate from Mamlatdar (or certificate from a Gazetted Officer)
- Income Certificate from Mamlatdar (or self-declaration on ₹20 stamp paper attested by Gazetted Officer)
- Bank account passbook (Aadhaar-linked, Goa-based branch)
- Passport-size photographs (2–3 copies)

### Category-Specific Documents
- **Senior Citizens:** Birth certificate, school leaving certificate, or Aadhaar showing date of birth
- **Disabled Persons:** Disability certificate from government medical board (40%+ disability)
- **Widows:** Death certificate of spouse + marriage certificate
- **Divorced/Separated:** Divorce decree or legal separation order
- **Single Women (50+):** Self-declaration affidavit of unmarried status
- **HIV/AIDS:** Medical certificate from government hospital
- **Griha Aadhar beneficiaries:** Cancellation undertaking for Griha Aadhar (Annexure-K as per 2025 amendment)

### Life Certificate
All beneficiaries must submit an annual **Life Certificate** in the prescribed format (Annexure-D, updated in 2025) to continue receiving pension.

## How to Apply Online for DSSS

### Online Application (goaonline.gov.in)

1. **Visit** [goaonline.gov.in](https://goaonline.gov.in)
2. **Register** a new account or log in with your existing credentials
3. **Search** for "Dayanand Social Security Scheme" under Department of Social Welfare
4. **Fill** the application form — personal details, pension category, income, and bank information
5. **Upload** scanned copies of all required documents
6. **Submit** the application (no fee required)
7. **Save** the application acknowledgment number — you'll need this to track status

### Offline Application

1. Visit your local **Mamlatdar office** or **Directorate of Social Welfare**
2. Collect the DSSS application form (also available at village Panchayat offices)
3. Fill the form completely and attach self-attested photocopies of all documents
4. Submit at the Mamlatdar office and collect the **acknowledgment receipt**
5. A field inquiry may be conducted to verify your details

> **Tip:** Village Panchayat secretaries can help rural applicants with form filling and document collection.

## How to Check DSSS Application Status

Tracking your application status is one of the most common concerns. Here's how:

### Method 1: Online via goaonline.gov.in
1. Log in to [goaonline.gov.in](https://goaonline.gov.in) with your registered credentials
2. Go to **"My Applications"** or **"Track Application"** section
3. Enter your application acknowledgment number
4. View the current status — pending, under verification, approved, or rejected

### Method 2: Visit Mamlatdar Office
If you applied offline or cannot access the portal, visit your taluka **Mamlatdar office** with your acknowledgment receipt. The staff can check your application status in the system.

### Method 3: Contact Directorate of Social Welfare
- **Phone:** Contact the Directorate of Social Welfare, Panaji
- **Website:** [socialwelfare.goa.gov.in](https://socialwelfare.goa.gov.in/dayanand-social-security-scheme-dsss/)
- **In Person:** Visit the Directorate office in Panaji with your receipt

### Application Status Meanings
- **Submitted:** Application received, awaiting processing
- **Under Verification:** Field inquiry or document verification in progress
- **Approved:** Pension sanctioned — payment will begin shortly
- **Rejected:** Application not accepted — check reason and reapply with corrections

## Payment Schedule – When is Pension Credited?

Understanding the payment cycle helps you plan:

- **Payment Mode:** Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to your Aadhaar-linked bank account
- **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Credit Date:** Typically credited between the **1st and 10th of each month** for the previous month
- **First Payment:** Usually starts **1–3 months after approval**, depending on processing time
- **Payment Method:** Transferred electronically — no need to visit any office to collect

### What If Your Payment is Delayed?
1. Check if your bank account is still active and linked to Aadhaar
2. Verify that your Life Certificate is up to date
3. Visit the Mamlatdar office to confirm your beneficiary status is active
4. Contact the Directorate of Social Welfare if the delay exceeds 2 months

## Processing Timeline

| Stage | Expected Duration |
|-------|-------------------|
| Application to Verification | 30–45 days |
| Verification to Approval | 15–30 days |
| Approval to First Payment | 1–3 months |
| **Total (Application to Payment)** | **2–5 months** |

## Common Problems and Solutions

### Problem: Application Rejected Due to Residency Proof
**Solution:** The 15-year residency requirement is strict. If your residence certificate was rejected, gather additional supporting documents like old voter ID cards, ration cards, electricity bills, or property tax receipts showing 15+ years of Goa residence. Apply for a fresh residence certificate from the Mamlatdar.

### Problem: Income Certificate Expired
**Solution:** The income certificate must be less than 1 year old. If yours has expired, get a fresh one from the Mamlatdar. Alternatively, submit a self-declaration on ₹20 stamp paper attested by a Gazetted Officer.

### Problem: Bank Account Not Linked to Aadhaar
**Solution:** Visit your bank branch with your Aadhaar card and request Aadhaar seeding/linking. This is mandatory for DBT payments. The process takes 2–3 working days.

### Problem: Already Receiving Griha Aadhar Benefits
**Solution:** As per the 2025 amendment, you must cancel your [Griha Aadhar Scheme](/guide/goa-griha-aadhar-scheme) benefits first. Obtain a cancellation order from the Directorate of Women & Child Development and submit it along with your DSSS application (Annexure-K).

### Problem: Pension Stopped Without Notice
**Solution:** This usually happens when the annual Life Certificate is not submitted on time. Submit the Life Certificate (Annexure-D) at the Mamlatdar office immediately and request reactivation.

### Problem: Application Stuck in "Under Verification" for Months
**Solution:** Visit the Mamlatdar office and ask about the field inquiry status. Sometimes applications get delayed due to staff shortages. A polite follow-up can help move things along. You can also write to the Directorate of Social Welfare.

## Important Tips

1. **Keep copies of everything** — application form, acknowledgment receipt, and all documents submitted
2. **Aadhaar linking is critical** — ensure your bank account is linked before applying
3. **Annual Life Certificate** — submit on time every year to avoid pension stoppage
4. **One scheme only** — you cannot receive DSSS and [Griha Aadhar](/guide/goa-griha-aadhar-scheme) simultaneously
5. **Check eligibility for central schemes** — you may also qualify for [Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension](/guide/indira-gandhi-pension-scheme) (though dual benefit rules may apply)
6. **Apply early** — processing takes 2–5 months, so don't wait until you urgently need the pension

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: How much pension do senior citizens get under DSSS in Goa?
**A:** Senior citizens aged 60–79 receive **₹2,500 per month**, and those aged 80 and above receive **₹3,500 per month** through Direct Benefit Transfer.

### Q2: How can I check my DSSS application status online?
**A:** Log in to [goaonline.gov.in](https://goaonline.gov.in), go to "My Applications" or "Track Application," and enter your acknowledgment number to see the current status.

### Q3: Can I receive both DSSS pension and Griha Aadhar benefits?
**A:** No. As per the February 2025 amendment, you must cancel your Griha Aadhar benefits before applying for DSSS. You need to submit a cancellation order from the Directorate of Women & Child Development.

### Q4: What is the minimum residency period required?
**A:** You must have been a resident of Goa for at least **15 continuous years**. This is strictly enforced and requires a certificate from the Mamlatdar.

### Q5: Is there an age limit for disability pension under DSSS?
**A:** No. Persons with 40% or more disability of **any age** can apply, provided they meet the residency and income criteria.

### Q6: When does the pension get credited to my bank account?
**A:** The pension is typically credited between the **1st and 10th of each month** via DBT. The first payment may take 1–3 months after approval.

### Q7: Can a working person apply for DSSS?
**A:** Yes, as long as their annual family income does not exceed **₹3,00,000**. Self-employed and informally employed persons are eligible.

### Q8: What happens if the beneficiary passes away?
**A:** The pension stops from the month following the beneficiary's death. The family must inform the Mamlatdar office promptly to avoid recovery of excess payments.

### Q9: Can NRIs originally from Goa apply for DSSS?
**A:** No. The applicant must be **currently residing in Goa** with 15 years of completed residency. NRIs living abroad are not eligible.

### Q10: What if my application is rejected?
**A:** Check the rejection reason (available on the portal or from the Mamlatdar office), correct the issue (missing documents, expired certificates, etc.), and **reapply**. There is no limit on reapplications.
