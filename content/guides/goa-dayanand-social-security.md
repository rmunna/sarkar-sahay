# Goa Dayanand Social Security Scheme – Complete Guide

## What is the Dayanand Social Security Scheme?

The Dayanand Social Security Scheme (DSSS) is the primary social security pension program of the Government of Goa. It provides **monthly financial assistance** to vulnerable sections of society including senior citizens, persons with disabilities, widows, and single women.

This is one of the most generous state pension schemes in India, with monthly pension amounts significantly higher than central pension schemes.

## Who is Eligible?

### For Old Age Pension
- **Age:** 60 years or above
- **Residency:** Must be a resident of Goa for at least 15 years
- **Income:** Annual income should not exceed ₹3,00,000

### For Disability Pension
- **Disability:** 40% or more disability (certified by a government medical board)
- **Age:** No minimum age requirement
- **Residency:** Must be a resident of Goa for at least 15 years

### For Widow/Single Woman Pension
- **Status:** Widowed, divorced, separated, or unmarried woman above 25 years
- **Residency:** Must be a resident of Goa for at least 15 years
- **Income:** Annual income should not exceed ₹3,00,000

## Documents Required

### Identity & Residence
- Aadhaar card
- Resident certificate (15 years residency proof)
- Voter ID or election card showing Goa address

### Age Proof (for Senior Citizen Pension)
- Birth certificate, school leaving certificate, or Aadhaar card showing date of birth

### Category-Specific
- **Disability:** Disability certificate from government medical board (40%+ disability)
- **Widow:** Death certificate of spouse
- **Divorced/Separated:** Divorce decree or legal separation document
- **Single Woman:** Self-declaration affidavit of unmarried status

### Financial
- Income certificate from Mamlatdar (not older than 1 year)
- Bank account passbook (account in Goa, linked to Aadhaar)

### Other
- Passport-size photographs (2-3)
- Self-declaration form (available at the office)

## Benefits

| Category | Monthly Pension |
|----------|----------------|
| Senior Citizens (60-79 years) | ₹2,500 |
| Senior Citizens (80+ years) | ₹3,500 |
| Persons with Disability | ₹2,500 |
| Widows / Single Women | ₹2,500 |

> **Note:** Pension amounts are subject to revision by the Goa government. These figures reflect the latest known rates.

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Online Process

1. **Visit the Portal:** Go to [https://goaonline.gov.in](https://goaonline.gov.in)
2. **Register/Login:** Create an account or log in with existing credentials
3. **Search for Scheme:** Find "Dayanand Social Security Scheme" under the Department of Women & Child Development
4. **Fill Application:** Enter personal details, category of pension, income details, and bank information
5. **Upload Documents:** Upload scanned copies of all required documents
6. **Pay Fee:** There is no application fee
7. **Submit:** Review and submit the application
8. **Download Receipt:** Save the application acknowledgment for future reference

### Offline Process

1. **Visit Mamlatdar Office:** Go to your local Mamlatdar office or the Directorate of Women & Child Development
2. **Get Application Form:** Collect the DSSS application form (also available at Panchayat offices)
3. **Fill the Form:** Complete all sections clearly
4. **Attach Documents:** Attach self-attested photocopies of all documents
5. **Submit:** Hand over the completed application at the Mamlatdar office
6. **Get Receipt:** Collect the acknowledgment receipt
7. **Verification:** A field inquiry may be conducted to verify details

## Processing Time

- **Application to Verification:** 30–45 days
- **Verification to Approval:** 15–30 days
- **First Payment:** 1–3 months from approval
- **Payment Cycle:** Monthly, directly to bank account

## Important Tips

1. **Residency Proof:** The 15-year residency requirement is strictly enforced — ensure you have proper documentation such as old voter ID cards, ration cards, or electricity bills.
2. **Bank Account:** Open a basic savings account in any Goa-based bank branch if you don't have one. Many banks offer zero-balance accounts for pension beneficiaries.
3. **Annual Renewal:** Some categories may require annual income certificate renewal — check with the Mamlatdar office.
4. **Panchayat Help:** Village Panchayat secretaries can assist with form filling and document collection for rural applicants.
5. **Combine Benefits:** Check if you are also eligible for central pension schemes like [Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension](/guide/indira-gandhi-pension-scheme) — in some cases, both can be availed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Can a person receiving a central government pension also apply for DSSS?
**A:** Generally, beneficiaries of other pension schemes (like IGNOAPS) may not be eligible for DSSS simultaneously. However, rules may vary — check with the local Mamlatdar office.

### Q2: What is the minimum residency period required?
**A:** You must have been a resident of Goa for at least 15 continuous years to be eligible.

### Q3: Is there an age limit for disability pension?
**A:** No. Persons with 40% or more disability of any age (meeting residency and income criteria) can apply for the disability pension.

### Q4: Can a working person apply for this pension?
**A:** Yes, as long as their annual income does not exceed ₹3,00,000. Self-employed and informally employed persons are eligible.

### Q5: How is the pension amount credited?
**A:** The pension is credited directly to the beneficiary's Aadhaar-linked bank account every month through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

### Q6: What happens if the beneficiary passes away?
**A:** The pension stops from the month following the beneficiary's death. The family should inform the Mamlatdar office to avoid any recovery of excess payments.

### Q7: Can NRIs who are originally from Goa apply?
**A:** No. The applicant must be currently residing in Goa and must have completed 15 years of residency. NRIs living abroad are not eligible.
