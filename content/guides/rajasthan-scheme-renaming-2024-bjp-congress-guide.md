# Rajasthan Scheme Renaming 2024 — Congress to BJP Changes

When **BJP** (under CM Bhajan Lal Sharma) returned to power in Rajasthan in **December 2023** (defeating the Congress government of Ashok Gehlot), several flagship welfare schemes were renamed. The most significant change affects the **Chiranjeevi health scheme** and food schemes named after "Indira Gandhi."

## Key Rajasthan Scheme Renamings (2024 Onwards)

| Old Name (Congress Era) | New Name / Status (BJP 2024) | Notes |
|------------------------|------------------------------|-------|
| **Mukhyamantri Chiranjeevi Swasthya Bima Yojana** | **Mukhyamantri Ayushman Arogya Yojana** | ₹25L health cover; same benefits, renamed |
| **Indira Rasoi Yojana** | **Annpurna Rasoi Yojana** (re-branded) | ₹8/meal food scheme; operational status modified |
| **Indira Gandhi Shahri Rozgar Guarantee Yojana** | Modified / partly discontinued | Urban MGNREGA equivalent |
| **Indira Gandhi Matritva Poshan Yojana** | Continuing under PMMVY + state component | Maternity nutrition scheme |
| **Mukhyamantri Nishulk Smartphone Yojana** | Discontinued (new beneficiary tablets under review) | Free device for women scheme |
| **Palanhar Yojana** | Continuing — renamed partially | Foster care scheme for orphans |
| **Social Security Pensions** | Continuing — amounts revised | Old age, widow, disability pensions |

## Chiranjeevi → Mukhyamantri Ayushman Arogya Yojana

This is the most important change for Rajasthan residents. The Chiranjeevi health card (₹25 lakh coverage, one of India's largest state health insurance schemes) was renamed:

**Old name**: Mukhyamantri Chiranjeevi Swasthya Bima Yojana
**New name**: **Mukhyamantri Ayushman Arogya Yojana (MAAY)**

**What stayed the same**:
- ₹25 lakh annual health cover per family
- RGHS (Rajasthan Government Health Scheme) tie-up continues
- Same empanelled hospitals
- Same treatment packages

**What changed**:
- Portal: [chiranjeevi.rajasthan.gov.in](https://chiranjeevi.rajasthan.gov.in/) is still operational
- New SSO ID login at [sso.rajasthan.gov.in](https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in/) for claims and status
- New beneficiaries enroll under "MAAY" name; old card holders continue without re-registration

> **If your Chiranjeevi card still works at hospitals**, it will continue working. The rebrand is administrative — existing beneficiaries don't need to get a new card.

## Indira Rasoi → Annpurna Rasoi

- **Old**: Indira Rasoi — meals at ₹8 across Rajasthan; 1 crore+ beneficiaries daily
- **New**: **Annpurna Rasoi** (renamed under BJP)
- **Price**: Remains ₹8/meal (subsidy continued by BJP government)
- **Operations**: Most centers continue; some locations merged or closed as part of streamlining

If your local Indira Rasoi is now showing as "Annpurna Rasoi" on the signboard — same scheme, different branding.

## Indira Gandhi Shahri Rozgar Guarantee — Status

The Congress government's urban employment guarantee (urban equivalent of MGNREGA) was launched in 2022:
- BJP significantly scaled back the scheme post December 2023
- **Urban employment guarantee work**: reduced budgets; many districts have paused new work issuance
- **If you were a registered worker**: Check with your local ward office or ULB for current availability
- The scheme still exists but isn't actively being promoted under the new government

## Schemes Continuing Unchanged

The following schemes continued largely without renaming:
- **Old Age Pension, Widow Pension, Disability Pension** — continuing and amount revised upward
- **Palanhar Yojana** (orphan welfare) — continuing
- **Indira Gandhi Matritva Poshan Yojana** — partially continuing alongside central PMMVY

## Social Security Pension — Amount Changes

Rajasthan's BJP government revised pension amounts (positive change):
- Old Age Pension: Increased to ₹1,000/month (was ₹750 under Congress in some categories)
- Widow Pension: Similar revisions
- Portal: **rajssp.raj.nic.in** — check your current pension amount and bank linkage

## How to Continue Benefits Under New Names

1. **Chiranjeevi/MAAY card**: Use your existing card at empanelled hospitals as before; login at sso.rajasthan.gov.in if you need to verify enrollment or update bank details
2. **Pension**: Check current status at rajssp.raj.nic.in; if payment stopped, visit nearest Emitra centre
3. **Annpurna Rasoi**: No action needed; just visit the nearest Rasoi centre (now rebranded)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My Chiranjeevi card was made in 2022. Is it still valid?

Yes. Chiranjeevi cards from the Congress era are still valid under the renamed MAAY scheme. Existing beneficiaries don't need to re-register. The card is accepted at the same empanelled hospitals.

### Indira Rasoi near my house has closed. Where is the nearest Annpurna Rasoi?

Some Rasoi centres were consolidated or relocated under BJP. Visit your ward office or emitra.rajasthan.gov.in to find the nearest operating Annpurna Rasoi location.

### I was getting free smartphone under Nishulk Smartphone Yojana. What happened?

The smartphone scheme was discontinued. Pending smartphones from the Congress era were distributed to already-approved beneficiaries. New applications are not being accepted. BJP's device support is under a different planning stage.

### Are the "Indira" name schemes completely gone?

"Indira Gandhi Shahri Rozgar Guarantee" name has been dropped. "Indira Gandhi Matritva Poshan Yojana" still operates in modified form. "Indira Rasoi" was renamed to "Annpurna Rasoi." Most benefits continue — just under different names or reduced in scope.
