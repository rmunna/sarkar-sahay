# PM Awas Yojana Urban (PMAY-U 2.0) – Urban Housing Scheme

Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana – Urban (PMAY-U) is the Government of India's flagship urban housing scheme to provide **affordable housing to all eligible urban families**. PMAY-U 2.0, launched in September 2024, continues the mission with enhanced benefits, interest subsidy on home loans, and direct assistance for house construction and purchase in urban areas.

## What is PMAY Urban 2.0?

PMAY-U 2.0 aims to address the housing needs of urban poor and middle-class families through multiple verticals:

### Verticals under PMAY-U 2.0

1. **Beneficiary-Led Construction (BLC)** — Central assistance for individual house construction or enhancement
2. **Affordable Housing in Partnership (AHP)** — Government partners with developers to build affordable housing projects
3. **Interest Subsidy Scheme (ISS)** — Subsidy on home loan interest for EWS, LIG, and MIG families
4. **In-situ Slum Redevelopment (ISSR)** — Rehabilitation of slum dwellers with private sector participation

## Who is Eligible?

### Income Categories

| Category | Annual Household Income | Carpet Area (Max) |
|----------|----------------------|-------------------|
| **EWS** (Economically Weaker Section) | Up to ₹3 lakh | 30 sq. m. |
| **LIG** (Lower Income Group) | ₹3 – ₹6 lakh | 60 sq. m. |
| **MIG** (Middle Income Group) | ₹6 – ₹9 lakh | 60 sq. m. |

### Eligibility Conditions
- Beneficiary family should **not own a pucca house** anywhere in India
- The house must be in the **beneficiary's name** (women ownership/co-ownership mandatory for EWS and LIG)
- The family should reside in an **urban area** (statutory town, census town, or notified planning area)
- **Indian citizen**
- Only **one PMAY benefit per family** — cannot avail under both urban and rural schemes

## Documents Required

- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) of all family members
- **Income proof** — salary slip, income certificate, or ITR
- **Address proof** — utility bill, rent agreement
- [PAN card](/guide/pan-card-apply-online)
- Bank account details
- **Self-declaration** of not owning a pucca house
- Land/property documents (for BLC vertical)
- Passport-size photographs
- Caste certificate (for EWS/SC/ST applicants)

## How to Apply Online

### Through PMAY-U Portal

1. Visit [pmaymis.gov.in](https://pmaymis.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"Citizen Assessment"** → **"Apply Online"**
3. Select the **vertical** you want to apply under (BLC, AHP, ISS, or ISSR)
4. Enter your **Aadhaar number** — verify via OTP
5. Fill in personal details — name, address, income, family members
6. Enter **property/house details** — location, area, cost
7. Upload required documents
8. Review and **submit the application**
9. Note down the **Application Reference Number** for tracking

### Through Common Service Centres (CSCs)

1. Visit your nearest **CSC/Jan Seva Kendra**
2. Pay the **application fee of ₹25** (plus GST)
3. CSC operator will fill the application online on your behalf
4. Submit documents for upload
5. Collect the printed application receipt

### Through Urban Local Body (ULB)

1. Visit your **Municipal Corporation / Municipal Council / Nagar Panchayat** office
2. Collect the PMAY-U application form
3. Fill in details and attach documents
4. Submit at the designated counter
5. ULB forwards the application to the state for processing

## Interest Subsidy Details (ISS Vertical)

| Category | Loan Amount (Max) | Interest Subsidy | Subsidy (NPV) Approx. |
|----------|------------------|-----------------|----------------------|
| **EWS** | ₹6 lakh | 6.5% | Up to ₹2.67 lakh |
| **LIG** | ₹6 lakh | 6.5% | Up to ₹2.67 lakh |
| **MIG** | ₹9 lakh | 4.0% | Up to ₹1.80 lakh |

*Under PMAY-U 2.0, subsidy is available on home loans sanctioned on or after 1 September 2024.*

## BLC (Beneficiary-Led Construction) Assistance

| Detail | Amount |
|--------|--------|
| **Central assistance** | Up to ₹2.5 lakh per house |
| **State/ULB contribution** | Additional (varies by state) |
| **Beneficiary contribution** | Remaining cost |
| **House size** | Minimum 30 sq. m. carpet area |

## How to Check Application Status

1. Visit [pmaymis.gov.in](https://pmaymis.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"Track Your Assessment Status"**
3. Enter your **Application Reference Number** or **Aadhaar Number**
4. View the current status of your application

## Key Benefits

- **Subsidy on home loans** — up to ₹2.67 lakh for EWS/LIG families
- **Direct financial assistance** — up to ₹2.5 lakh for self-construction (BLC)
- **Women empowerment** — mandatory women ownership/co-ownership for EWS/LIG
- **Technology-driven construction** — promotes innovative, sustainable building technologies
- **No middlemen** — subsidy and assistance transferred directly via DBT
- **Available in all urban areas** — 4,000+ statutory towns across India

## Important Tips

1. **Ensure women ownership** — for EWS and LIG categories, the house must be in the woman's name or jointly owned
2. **Apply through the official portal only** — beware of fraudulent websites charging fees
3. **Keep your Aadhaar updated** — your Aadhaar must be linked to your bank account for subsidy transfer
4. **Self-declaration is crucial** — falsely declaring non-ownership of a pucca house can lead to legal action and recovery of benefits
5. **Check state-specific guidelines** — some states provide additional top-up assistance beyond the central subsidy

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the income limit for PMAY Urban?
Under PMAY-U 2.0: EWS — up to ₹3 lakh annual household income, LIG — ₹3 to ₹6 lakh, MIG — ₹6 to ₹9 lakh.

### Can I apply if I already own a house?
No. PMAY-U is for families that do not own a pucca house anywhere in India. You must submit a self-declaration confirming this.

### Is it mandatory for the house to be in a woman's name?
For EWS and LIG categories, the house must be in the name of a female member of the household or jointly owned. This is a mandatory requirement.

### How much subsidy will I get on my home loan?
EWS and LIG beneficiaries can get an interest subsidy of 6.5% on loans up to ₹6 lakh, amounting to approximately ₹2.67 lakh. MIG beneficiaries get 4% on loans up to ₹9 lakh.

### Can I apply for PMAY Urban if I live in a rented house?
Yes. If you are living in a rented house and do not own a pucca house anywhere in India, you are eligible to apply.

### How long does it take to get the subsidy?
The interest subsidy is typically credited to your loan account within **3-6 months** after loan disbursement, depending on the processing by the Central Nodal Agency.
