# RRB Group D 2026 — Complete Railway Recruitment Guide

The Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) conduct Group D recruitment for Level 1 posts across Indian Railways. With **32,000–40,000+ vacancies** expected in 2026, RRB Group D is one of India's largest government recruitment drives.

**10th pass is sufficient** for most Group D posts, making it accessible to millions of candidates who couldn't pursue higher education.

## Exam Pattern — Computer Based Test (CBT)

| Section | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---------|-----------|-------|------|
| Mathematics | 25 | 25 | Composite |
| General Intelligence & Reasoning | 30 | 30 | Composite |
| General Science | 25 | 25 | Composite |
| General Awareness & Current Affairs | 20 | 20 | Composite |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** | **90 min** |

- **Negative marking:** 1/3 mark per wrong answer
- Medium: English + Hindi (other regional languages available)
- Normalisation: If CBT held across multiple sessions, scores are normalised

### Physical Efficiency Test (PET) — After CBT

**Male candidates:**
- Lift and carry 35 kg weight for 100 metres in 2 minutes
- Run 1,000 metres in 4 minutes 15 seconds

**Female candidates:**
- Lift and carry 20 kg weight for 100 metres in 2 minutes
- Run 1,000 metres in 5 minutes 40 seconds

*PET is qualifying — must pass to proceed. No marks awarded.*

## Detailed Syllabus

### Mathematics (Class 10 Level)
Number System, BODMAS, Decimals, Fractions, LCM/HCF, Ratio & Proportion, Percentage, Mensuration, Time & Work, Time & Distance, Simple & Compound Interest, Profit & Loss, Geometry, Trigonometry basics, Statistics (mean, median, mode)

### General Intelligence & Reasoning
Analogies, Alphabetical Series, Mathematical Operations, Relationships, Syllogism, Jumbling, Venn Diagram, Data Interpretation, Conclusions, Statement-Action, Similarities/Differences, Coding-Decoding, Direction Sense, Blood Relations

### General Science (Class 10 Level)
**Physics:** Motion, Force, Energy, Electricity, Magnetism, Sound, Light  
**Chemistry:** Matter, Acids/Bases/Salts, Metals/Non-metals, Carbon compounds, Chemical reactions  
**Biology:** Life Processes, Reproduction, Heredity, Control & Coordination, Natural Resources

### General Awareness & Current Affairs
Events of last 6 months (national + international), Science & Technology, Sports, Culture, Indian History, Indian Geography, Indian Economy, Indian Polity, Rail Budget highlights, Awards & Honours

## How to Apply

1. Go to the **RRB website for your region** (e.g., [rrbcdg.gov.in](https://www.rrbcdg.gov.in) for Delhi)
2. Find the current Group D notification
3. Register on [rrbapply.gov.in](https://www.rrbapply.gov.in) (centralised portal)
4. Fill in personal, educational, category details
5. Upload photo (JPG, 20–50 KB) + signature (JPG, 10–20 KB)
6. Select RRB preference
7. Pay fee:

| Category | Fee |
|----------|-----|
| General / OBC / EWS | ₹500 (₹400 refunded after CBT) |
| SC / ST / PwBD / Women / Minorities | ₹250 (₹250 refunded after CBT) |

8. Submit and save confirmation

- Most candidates fail PET due to lack of preparation — start physical training now

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Class 10 pass sufficient for RRB Group D?**
Yes — Class 10 or ITI pass is the minimum eligibility for most Group D posts. No higher qualification is needed.

**How many vacancies are expected in RRB Group D 2026?**
Based on past cycles, 30,000–40,000 vacancies are expected. The 2022 cycle had 103,769 vacancies. The 2026 notification will confirm exact numbers.

**Is there any interview for RRB Group D?**
No — RRB Group D has no interview. Selection is based on CBT (written test) + PET + Document Verification + Medical Examination.

**Can I apply in multiple RRB zones?**
No — you can apply to only one RRB per notification cycle. Choose the zone based on preferred posting location.

**What happens if I fail the PET?**
You are disqualified even if your CBT score is high. Physical preparation is essential — don't neglect it.

**Is there a difference between RRB and RRC recruitment?**
RRB (Railway Recruitment Board) conducts exams for NTPC and Group D. RRC (Railway Recruitment Cell) sometimes conducts separate recruitment for specific zones. Both are under Indian Railways — same job, different administrative processes.
