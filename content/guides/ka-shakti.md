# Shakti Scheme Karnataka – Free Bus Travel for Women

## What is Shakti Scheme?

Shakti is a Karnataka government scheme providing **free bus travel for all women** on Karnataka State Road Transport Corporation (KSRTC) and its subsidiary buses (BMTC, NWKRTC, NEKRTC). Any woman residing in Karnataka can travel for free on non-premium government buses across the state.

The scheme was launched to improve women's mobility, economic participation, and independence.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **All women** residing in Karnataka
2. **No age limit** — girls and women of all ages can avail
3. **No income criteria** — available to all women regardless of economic status
4. Must obtain the **Shakti Smart Card** for free travel

### Buses Covered

- **KSRTC** ordinary and express buses
- **BMTC** (Bangalore Metropolitan Transport Corporation) ordinary buses
- **NWKRTC** and **NEKRTC** buses
- **NOT covered:** Volvo, AC, Airavat, Flybus, and other premium/luxury services

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar Card**
- **Recent Passport Size Photo**
- **Mobile Number**

## How to Get the Shakti Smart Card

### Online Registration

1. **Visit** the KSRTC official website or Seva Sindhu portal
2. **Click** on "Shakti Scheme Registration"
3. **Enter** your Aadhaar number and verify with OTP
4. **Upload** a passport-size photo
5. **Submit** the registration
6. **Collect** the Shakti Smart Card from the designated bus depot or receive it by post

### Offline Registration

1. **Visit** any major **KSRTC / BMTC bus depot** or **Karnataka One centre**
2. **Carry** your Aadhaar card and a passport photo
3. **Fill** the Shakti registration form
4. **Submit** and collect the smart card (may be issued on the spot or within a few days)

### Temporary Pass

- Women can also travel free by showing their **Aadhaar card** to the conductor even without the smart card (during the initial rollout phase)
- However, getting the smart card is recommended for hassle-free travel

## How to Use the Shakti Card

1. **Board** any eligible KSRTC/BMTC ordinary bus
2. **Show or tap** your Shakti Smart Card to the conductor
3. **Collect** a free ticket from the conductor
4. **Travel** to your destination — no payment required

## Important Tips

1. **Premium Buses Excluded:** Shakti does not cover Volvo, AC, Airavat, or Flybus services. Only ordinary and express buses.
2. **Get the Card Early:** While Aadhaar can be used temporarily, the smart card ensures faster boarding.
3. **Card is Non-Transferable:** The Shakti card has your photo and details — it cannot be used by someone else.
4. **Inter-District Travel Covered:** You can travel anywhere within Karnataka on eligible buses for free.
5. **Report Misconduct:** If any conductor refuses free travel or misbehaves, complain at the bus depot or call the KSRTC helpline.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Is the Shakti card mandatory for free travel?
During the initial phase, women could show Aadhaar. However, now the Shakti smart card is recommended for all free travel.

### Q2: Can I travel on BMTC Volvo/AC buses for free?
**No.** Only ordinary BMTC buses are covered. Volvo, AC, and premium services require regular tickets.

### Q3: Is there a limit on how many trips I can take?
**No.** There is no limit on the number of trips. Women can travel unlimited times on eligible buses.

### Q4: Can men avail this scheme?
**No.** Shakti is exclusively for women.

### Q5: Can I use Shakti for interstate travel?
**No.** The scheme covers only intra-state travel within Karnataka on KSRTC and subsidiary buses.

### Q6: What if I lose my Shakti card?
Visit the nearest KSRTC depot with your Aadhaar card to get a replacement smart card issued.
