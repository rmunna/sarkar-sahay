# PM Ujjwala Yojana: Apply for Free LPG Connection

Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY) is a flagship scheme launched in 2016 to provide free LPG connections to women from Below Poverty Line (BPL) families. The scheme aims to replace unhealthy cooking fuels like firewood, coal and dung cakes with clean LPG. Under Ujjwala 2.0 (launched August 2021), the scheme was expanded with simplified documentation and free first refill.

## What is PM Ujjwala Yojana?

| Detail | Information |
|--------|-------------|
| **Scheme Name** | Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY) |
| **Launched** | 1st May 2016 |
| **Ministry** | Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas |
| **Objective** | Free LPG connections to BPL households |
| **Phase 1 Target** | 8 crore connections (achieved) |
| **Ujjwala 2.0** | Launched August 2021 — 1 crore additional connections |
| **Official Website** | [pmuy.gov.in](https://www.pmuy.gov.in/) |

## Who is Eligible?

### Ujjwala 1.0 Eligibility
- Adult woman from a BPL household listed in **SECC-2011** (Socio Economic Caste Census)
- No existing LPG connection in any family member's name
- Age: 18 years or above

### Ujjwala 2.0 (Expanded Eligibility)
In addition to SECC beneficiaries, the following categories are now eligible:
- **SC/ST households**
- **Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) beneficiaries** — See our [PMAY guide](/guide/pm-awas-yojana-apply) if applicable
- **Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) beneficiaries**
- **Forest dwellers**
- **Most Backward Classes (MBC)**
- **Tea garden and ex-tea garden tribes**
- **People living in river islands**
- **Migrants** — No address proof needed under Ujjwala 2.0; self-declaration accepted

## Benefits Under the Scheme

| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Free LPG connection | Security deposit waived (₹1,600) |
| Free first refill | 14.2 kg cylinder provided free |
| Free regulator and hose | Equipment provided at no cost |
| Subsidy on refills | ₹200 per cylinder for up to 12 refills/year |
| Total benefit value | Approximately ₹3,200+ per beneficiary |

## Documents Required

### For Ujjwala 1.0
- **BPL card** or name in SECC-2011 list
- **Aadhaar card** of the applicant (adult woman) — [Apply for Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) if needed
- **BPL ration card**
- **Bank account passbook** (Aadhaar-linked)
- **Passport-size photograph**

### For Ujjwala 2.0 (Simplified)
- **Aadhaar card** of the applicant
- **Self-declaration** for address (if migrant — no address proof needed)
- **Bank account** linked to Aadhaar
- **Ration card** or any one of: SC/ST certificate, AAY card, PMAY beneficiary letter
- **Passport-size photograph**

> **Key simplification in Ujjwala 2.0:** Migrants do not need address proof — a self-declaration of address is accepted.

## How to Apply for Ujjwala Yojana

### Online Application

#### Step 1: Visit the Official Website
Go to [pmuy.gov.in](https://www.pmuy.gov.in/) and click "Apply for New Connection."

#### Step 2: Select Your OMC
Choose your preferred LPG company — Indane (IOCL), HP Gas (HPCL) or Bharat Gas (BPCL).

#### Step 3: Fill the Application Form
Enter details:
- Full name (as per Aadhaar)
- Aadhaar number
- Date of birth
- Address
- Bank account number and IFSC
- Mobile number

#### Step 4: Upload Documents
Upload scanned copies of Aadhaar, ration card and photograph.

#### Step 5: Submit Application
Submit the form. You will receive an application reference number.

#### Step 6: Verification
The LPG distributor will verify your details against SECC data and Aadhaar.

#### Step 7: Connection Delivery
After verification, a cylinder, regulator and hose will be delivered to your home within 15-30 days.

### Offline Application

1. Visit your nearest LPG distributor (Indane, HP Gas or Bharat Gas)
2. Ask for the **Ujjwala Yojana application form** (Form available free)
3. Fill the form and attach documents
4. Submit at the distributor's office
5. Distributor will process and deliver the connection

## How to Check Ujjwala Yojana Application Status

### Method 1: Online on pmuy.gov.in
1. Visit [pmuy.gov.in](https://www.pmuy.gov.in/)
2. Click on "Track Application" or "Check Status"
3. Enter your application reference number or Aadhaar number
4. View current status

### Method 2: Contact Your Distributor
Call or visit your LPG distributor with your application number to check status.

## Free Refill Subsidy Under Ujjwala

- Ujjwala beneficiaries receive **₹200 subsidy per cylinder** via DBT
- Subsidy is available for **up to 12 refills per year**
- The subsidy amount is credited to the Aadhaar-linked bank account within 3-5 days of refill delivery
- Check subsidy credit on [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in/) or your bank passbook

## Processing Time

| Stage | Timeline |
|-------|----------|
| Application submission to verification | 7-15 days |
| Verification to connection delivery | 15-30 days |
| Total | 3-6 weeks |

## Important Tips

1. **Only women can apply** — The connection is issued in the name of an adult woman of the household.
2. **One connection per household** — Only one Ujjwala connection per family. Duplicate applications are rejected.
3. **Link Aadhaar to bank** — Subsidy is transferred via DBT. Ensure Aadhaar-bank linkage.
4. **Free first refill** — Under Ujjwala 2.0, the first refill cylinder is free. Book it immediately after installation.
5. **Don't pay agents** — The scheme is completely free. Do not pay any middleman or agent.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Is Ujjwala Yojana still open for applications?
Yes, Ujjwala 2.0 is currently active. Eligible families can apply at any LPG distributor or online.

### Q2: Can men apply under Ujjwala Yojana?
No. The connection must be in the name of an adult woman (18+) of the household.

### Q3: I don't have a BPL card. Can I still apply?
Under Ujjwala 2.0, you can apply if you belong to SC/ST, AAY, PMAY beneficiary, forest dweller or MBC categories. Migrants can apply with self-declaration.

### Q4: Is the first refill really free?
Yes. Under Ujjwala 2.0, the first 14.2 kg LPG refill is provided free of cost.

### Q5: How many subsidised refills do I get per year?
Ujjwala beneficiaries get ₹200 subsidy per refill for up to 12 refills per year.

### Q6: What if my name is not in the SECC list?
Under Ujjwala 2.0, the eligibility criteria have been expanded. Check if you fall under any of the expanded categories (SC/ST, AAY, PMAY, etc.).

### Q7: Can I choose my LPG company under Ujjwala?
Yes. You can choose any of the three OMCs — Indane, HP Gas or Bharat Gas — based on distributor availability in your area.
