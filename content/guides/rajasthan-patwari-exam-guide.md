# Rajasthan Patwari Exam Guide — Eligibility, Pattern & Preparation Tips

The Rajasthan Subordinate and Ministerial Services Selection Board (RSMSSB) conducts the recruitment exam for Patwari posts in the Revenue Department of Rajasthan. The Patwari is a village-level land revenue officer responsible for maintaining land records and revenue collection.

Rajasthan Patwari is one of the most sought-after government jobs in the state, offering job security and a role in grassroots governance.

## Eligibility Criteria

### Age Limit

| Category | Minimum Age | Maximum Age |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| General | 18 years | 40 years |
| OBC | 18 years | 43 years |
| SC/ST | 18 years | 45 years |

### Educational Qualification

- **Minimum:** Senior Secondary (12th) from a recognized board
- Computer knowledge (RS-CIT or equivalent certificate)

## Exam Pattern

| Subject | Marks |
|---------|-------|
| General Knowledge (Rajasthan GK) | 55 |
| Hindi | 35 |
| Mathematics | 40 |
| Computer Knowledge | 20 |
| **Total** | **150** |

- **Duration:** 3 hours
- **Negative marking:** 1/3 of marks per wrong answer
- **Type:** Online (Computer-based)

## Syllabus Overview

### Rajasthan GK
- Rajasthan History, Geography, Art & Culture, Polity, Economy, Current Affairs, Rajasthan-specific schemes

### Hindi
- Grammar, Comprehension, Sandhi, Samas, Muhavare, Lokoktiyan, Vilom, Paryayvachi

### Mathematics
- Number system, Percentage, Ratio, Average, SI/CI, Time & Work, Geometry, Mensuration, Data Interpretation

### Computer Knowledge
- Computer fundamentals, MS Office, Internet, Operating Systems

## How to Apply

1. Visit RSMSSB at [rsmssb.rajasthan.gov.in](https://rsmssb.rajasthan.gov.in)
2. Find Patwari recruitment notification
3. Register and fill the application
4. Upload photo and signature
5. Pay fee and submit

## Application Fee

| Category | Fee |
|----------|-----|
| General / OBC | ₹450 |
| SC / ST | ₹250 |

## Salary & Benefits

- **Pay Scale:** ₹20,800 – ₹66,000 (Level 5)
- DA, HRA, and other allowances
- Pension (NPS), medical benefits

## Preparation Tips

1. **Rajasthan GK:** This carries the highest weight — master it
2. **RS-CIT level computer:** Cover basics of MS Office and Internet
3. **Math fundamentals:** Practice arithmetic and basic geometry
4. **Previous papers:** Study last 5 years' Patwari papers
5. **Hindi grammar:** Focus on Muhavare and common grammar topics

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is 12th pass enough for Rajasthan Patwari?
Yes, with a computer knowledge certificate (RS-CIT or equivalent).

### Is there an interview?
No, selection is based on written exam merit only.

### What is the role of Patwari in Rajasthan?
Maintaining land records (Jamabandi), revenue collection, crop surveys, and assisting in revenue administration.

### Is RS-CIT certificate mandatory?
Yes, or an equivalent recognized computer certificate.

### How many vacancies are typically released?
Recent cycles have had 4,000-5,000+ vacancies.

### Can other state candidates apply?
Rajasthan domicile is typically required.
