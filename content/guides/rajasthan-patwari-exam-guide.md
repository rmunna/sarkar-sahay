# Rajasthan Patwari Exam Guide — Eligibility, Pattern & Preparation Tips

The Rajasthan Subordinate and Ministerial Services Selection Board (RSMSSB) conducts the recruitment exam for Patwari posts in the Revenue Department of Rajasthan. The Patwari is a village-level land revenue officer responsible for maintaining land records (Jamabandi), collecting revenue, conducting crop surveys, and assisting in revenue administration at the tehsil level.

Rajasthan Patwari is one of the most sought-after government jobs in the state. The combination of a 12th-pass eligibility requirement, a decent pay scale, and the security of a government posting makes it extremely competitive. Each recruitment cycle attracts lakhs of candidates from across Rajasthan.

## What Does a Patwari Do?

- **Land Records (Jamabandi):** Maintaining and updating ownership records, issuing land documents
- **Revenue Collection:** Collecting land revenue from landowners in the assigned Patwar circle
- **Crop Survey (Girdawari):** Seasonal crop inspections and damage assessment
- **Mutation:** Processing ownership change applications and boundary disputes
- **Scheme Support:** Land verification for PM-Kisan, crop insurance, and welfare schemes
- **Election/Census Duty:** Voter list preparation and census operations

## Eligibility Criteria

### Age Limit

| Category | Minimum Age | Maximum Age |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| General (Male) | 18 years | 40 years |
| General (Female) | 18 years | 45 years |
| OBC (Male) | 18 years | 43 years |
| OBC (Female) | 18 years | 48 years |
| SC/ST (Male) | 18 years | 45 years |
| SC/ST (Female) | 18 years | 50 years |
| PwD | 18 years | 55 years |

Age is calculated as on January 1 of the recruitment year (verify from notification). Rajasthan provides generous age relaxation, especially for female and reserved category candidates.

### Educational Qualification

- **Minimum:** Senior Secondary (12th pass) from a recognized board (RBSE, CBSE, or equivalent)
- **Computer Certificate:** RS-CIT (Rajasthan State Certificate in Information Technology) from a VMOU-recognized centre, or an equivalent higher qualification (PGDCA, O-Level, BCA, etc.)

### Domicile

- Rajasthan domicile is required. Non-domicile candidates are generally not eligible.
- Domicile certificate must be available at the time of document verification.

## Exam Pattern

The Patwari exam is conducted as a single computer-based test:

| Subject | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| General Knowledge (Rajasthan GK focus) | 55 | 55 |
| Hindi | 35 | 35 |
| Mathematics | 40 | 40 |
| Computer Knowledge | 20 | 20 |
| **Total** | **150** | **150** |

- **Duration:** 3 hours
- **Negative Marking:** 1/3 of the marks allotted to the question for each wrong answer
- **Mode:** Online computer-based test (CBT)
- **Question Type:** Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)

With 150 questions in 180 minutes, you get about 72 seconds per question — use this to be thorough.

## Detailed Syllabus

### Rajasthan GK (55 marks) — Highest Weightage
- History (Rajput kingdoms, Praja Mandal movement), Geography (Aravalli, Thar, rivers, wildlife sanctuaries)
- Art & Culture (paintings, folk dances, fairs), Polity (Panchayati Raj, state legislature)
- Economy (minerals, agriculture, irrigation), Current Affairs, state schemes (Chiranjeevi, Indira Rasoi)
- State symbols, districts, divisions, census data

### Hindi (35 marks)
- Sandhi, Samas, Upsarg, Pratyay
- Muhavare and Lokoktiyan (idioms and proverbs)
- Vilom Shabd (antonyms), Paryayvachi Shabd (synonyms)
- Comprehension passage
- Sentence correction and error detection
- Letter and application writing basics
### Mathematics (40 marks)
- Number system, HCF/LCM, Simplification, Percentage, Ratio & Proportion, Average
- SI/CI, Profit & Loss, Discount, Time & Work, Time & Distance
- Geometry, Mensuration, Data Interpretation, Trigonometry basics

### Computer Knowledge (20 marks)
- Computer fundamentals: hardware, software, memory, input/output devices
- Operating Systems: Windows basics, file management
- MS Office: Word (formatting, mail merge), Excel (formulas, charts), PowerPoint basics
- Internet: Browsers, email, search engines, basic networking concepts
## How to Apply

1. Visit RSMSSB at https://rsmssb.rajasthan.gov.in
2. Find the Patwari recruitment notification
3. Register and fill in personal, educational, and category details
4. Upload photograph and signature in the prescribed format
5. Select exam centre preference
6. Pay fee online or via e-Mitra kiosk
7. Submit and download the confirmation page

## Selection Process

1. **Written Exam (CBT):** Single-stage exam. Merit list is prepared based on marks scored.
2. **Document Verification:** Candidates above the cutoff are called for document verification at the district level.
3. **Final Allocation:** Based on merit, category, and district preference, candidates are allocated Patwar circles.

There is **no interview** for Patwari recruitment. Performance in the written exam is the sole criterion.

## Salary & Benefits

- **Pay Scale:** ₹20,800 – ₹66,000 per month (Pay Matrix Level 5)
- **Gross Salary (approximate):** ₹30,000 – ₹36,000 initially (including DA, HRA)
- Dearness Allowance, House Rent Allowance, and other state government allowances
- Pension under NPS (National Pension System)
- Medical benefits and leave travel concession
- Leave entitlements: Earned Leave, Casual Leave, Medical Leave
- **Promotion Path:** Patwari → Kanungo → Naib Tehsildar → Tehsildar (through departmental exams and seniority)

## Preparation Tips

1. **Rajasthan GK is king (55/150):** This section makes or breaks your attempt. Study history, geography, art & culture, and state schemes thoroughly.
2. **RS-CIT computer knowledge:** Revise MS Office, internet basics, and terminology. These 20 marks are easy scoring.
3. **Math fundamentals:** Practice arithmetic and geometry/mensuration daily. Speed comes from repetition.
4. **Previous year papers:** Solve 5-7 past Patwari papers — RSMSSB often repeats patterns.
5. **Hindi grammar:** Focus on Muhavare, Lokoktiyan, Sandhi, and Samas.
6. **Daily current affairs:** Follow Rajasthan Patrika or Dainik Bhaskar for state-specific news.
7. **Mock tests:** Take full-length timed tests weekly to find weak areas.
8. **Negative marking:** With 1/3 penalty, avoid random guessing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is 12th pass enough for Rajasthan Patwari?
Yes, Senior Secondary (12th) from a recognized board is the minimum educational qualification. A computer certificate (RS-CIT or equivalent) is also mandatory.

### Is there an interview in the Patwari selection process?
No, selection is based entirely on written exam merit. There is no interview or physical fitness test.

### What is the role of Patwari in Rajasthan?
The Patwari maintains land records (Jamabandi), collects land revenue, conducts crop surveys (Girdawari), processes mutations, and assists in welfare scheme implementation at the village level.

### Is RS-CIT certificate mandatory?
Yes, RS-CIT (Rajasthan State Certificate in IT) or an equivalent/higher computer certificate is required. It must be obtained before document verification.

### How many vacancies are typically released?
Recent recruitment cycles have announced 4,000 to 5,500+ vacancies. The number depends on state requirements and pending vacancies.

### Can candidates from other states apply?
No, Rajasthan domicile is typically required. A valid domicile certificate must be produced during document verification.

### What is the expected cutoff for Rajasthan Patwari?
Cutoffs vary by category. For the General category, the cutoff has typically been in the 85-110 range out of 150, depending on the difficulty level and number of vacancies.

### Is there a physical fitness test?
No. A basic medical fitness certificate may be needed at the time of joining.
