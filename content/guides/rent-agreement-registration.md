# Rent Agreement Registration in India – Process, Format & Stamp Duty

A rent agreement (also called a rental agreement or leave and licence agreement) is a legal document between a landlord and tenant that defines the terms of renting a property. Registering this agreement makes it legally enforceable and protects both parties. This guide covers the process, documents, stamp duty, and format for rent agreements across India.

---

## Why Register a Rent Agreement?

- **Legal validity:** Only registered agreements are admissible as evidence in court
- **Protection:** Protects both landlord and tenant from disputes
- **Mandatory:** Many states (Maharashtra, Karnataka, etc.) mandate registration for agreements of 12+ months
- **Address proof:** Registered rent agreements are accepted as valid address proof for Aadhaar, bank accounts, etc.
- **Police verification:** Required in many cities for tenant verification

---

## Types of Rental Agreements

| Type | Duration | Registration |
|------|----------|-------------|
| **Leave and Licence** | 11 months (renewable) | Optional but recommended |
| **Lease Agreement** | 12+ months | Mandatory (Registration Act) |
| **Rent Agreement** | 11 months (common) | Optional but recommended |
| **Commercial Lease** | Varies | Typically registered |

> **Common practice:** Most landlords opt for 11-month agreements to avoid mandatory registration. However, even 11-month agreements should ideally be registered or notarized.

---

## Documents Required

### From Landlord
- **Identity proof:** Aadhaar card, PAN card
- **Address proof:** Aadhaar or utility bill
- **Property ownership proof:** Sale deed, tax receipt, or property card
- **Passport-size photographs** (2)
- **Property tax receipt** (recent)

### From Tenant
- **Identity proof:** Aadhaar card, PAN card, Passport
- **Address proof:** Aadhaar (permanent address)
- **Passport-size photographs** (2)

### Witnesses
- **Two witnesses** with their Aadhaar/ID proof and photographs

## Stamp Duty & Registration Charges (State-wise)

| State | Stamp Duty | Registration Fee |
|-------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Maharashtra** | 0.25% of total rent + deposit (min ₹100) | ₹1,000 |
| **Karnataka** | 1% of total rent + deposit | 1% (min ₹500) |
| **Delhi** | 2% of average annual rent | ₹1,100 |
| **Tamil Nadu** | 1% of rent + advance | 1% (min ₹1,000) |
| **Uttar Pradesh** | 2% of annual rent | ₹1,000 – ₹5,000 |
| **Telangana** | 0.5% of total rent | ₹1,000 |
| **Kerala** | 2% of annual rent | ₹1,000 |
| **West Bengal** | 0.25% of security deposit + rent | ₹1,000 |

> **Note:** Rates vary by state and may change. Verify with your state's registration or stamps department.

## How to Register – Step-by-Step

### Online Registration (Maharashtra – Example)

1. Visit [registration.maharashtra.gov.in](https://registration.maharashtra.gov.in/)
2. Register/login on the portal
3. Select **"Leave and Licence Agreement"**
4. Fill in details: landlord, tenant, property, rent, deposit, duration
5. Upload documents (Aadhaar, photos, property proof)
6. **Pay stamp duty and registration fee** online
7. **Book appointment** at the nearest Sub-Registrar Office (SRO)
8. **Visit SRO** on the appointment date with landlord, tenant, and 2 witnesses
9. **Biometric verification** (fingerprints) of all parties
10. **Agreement registered** — download the registered document online

### Offline Registration (All States)

1. **Draft the rent agreement** (use a lawyer or standard format)
2. **Purchase stamp paper** of the required value (e-stamp or physical)
3. **Print agreement** on stamp paper
4. **All parties sign** — landlord, tenant, and 2 witnesses
5. **Visit Sub-Registrar Office** with documents and photographs
6. **Pay registration fee** at the counter
7. **Biometric/photo verification** done at SRO
8. **Collect registered copy** (usually same day or next day)

## Rent Agreement Format – Key Clauses

Every rent agreement should include:

1. **Names and addresses** of landlord and tenant
2. **Property address** and description (flat no., floor, area)
3. **Monthly rent** and payment date
4. **Security deposit** amount and refund terms
5. **Agreement duration** (typically 11 months) and renewal terms
6. **Lock-in period** (if any — usually 3–6 months)
7. **Maintenance charges** — who pays what
8. **Allowed use** — residential/commercial
9. **Notice period** for vacating (usually 1–2 months)
10. **Restrictions** — subletting, pets, modifications
11. **Furnishing details** — inventory of provided items
12. **Termination clause** — conditions for early exit
13. **Dispute resolution** — jurisdiction for legal matters

## E-Stamp Paper

Most states now accept e-stamp paper instead of physical stamp paper:
- Purchase from authorized e-stamping centres or Stock Holding Corporation of India (SHCIL)
- Visit [shcilestamp.com](https://www.shcilestamp.com/) to find centres
- Can also be generated through some state portals
- E-stamps are tamper-proof and verifiable online

## Notarized vs Registered Agreement

| Feature | Notarized | Registered |
|---------|-----------|-----------|
| **Legal validity** | Limited (not admissible in some courts) | Full (admissible in all courts) |
| **Cost** | ₹100 – ₹500 | Stamp duty + registration fee |
| **Process** | Quick (notary stamps it) | Requires SRO visit |
| **Address proof** | Not always accepted | Universally accepted |
| **Recommended** | Only for very short durations | Always recommended |

## Important Tips

1. **Always register** agreements of 12+ months — it's legally required
2. **Even 11-month agreements** should be at least notarized; registration is better
3. **Keep multiple copies** — one for each party and one for records
4. **Photograph the property** before moving in to document existing condition
5. **Clearly mention the security deposit refund process** to avoid disputes later

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is rent agreement registration mandatory?
For agreements of 12 months or more, registration is mandatory under the Registration Act, 1908. For 11-month agreements, it's not mandatory in most states but highly recommended.

### Who pays the stamp duty — landlord or tenant?
By convention, the tenant usually pays the stamp duty and registration charges. However, this is negotiable and should be agreed upon between both parties.

### Can I register a rent agreement online?
Yes, several states (Maharashtra, Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh, etc.) allow online registration. You still need to visit the Sub-Registrar Office for biometric verification.

### What is the penalty for not registering?
An unregistered agreement (when registration is mandatory) is not admissible as evidence in court. There may also be penalties for stamp duty evasion — typically 2–10 times the deficient stamp duty amount.

### Can I use a rent agreement as address proof?
A registered rent agreement is widely accepted as address proof for Aadhaar updates, bank account opening, passport applications, and more. Notarized agreements may not be accepted everywhere.

### How do I renew a rent agreement?
You can either execute a fresh agreement or add a renewal clause/addendum to the existing one. For registered agreements, the renewal should also be registered.
