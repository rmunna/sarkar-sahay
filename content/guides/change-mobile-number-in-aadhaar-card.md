## What is Aadhaar Mobile Number Update?

Aadhaar Mobile Number Update lets you link a new mobile number to your Aadhaar card or change an existing one. This is important because many government services and banks send OTPs to your Aadhaar-registered mobile number. Please note — this update can only be done offline by visiting an Aadhaar Enrolment/Update Centre in person.

## Who is Eligible?

- Any person who holds a valid Aadhaar card and wants to update or register a mobile number
- You must visit the centre yourself — biometric verification (fingerprint/iris scan) is mandatory
- No age restriction — for children, a guardian can accompany them to the centre

## Documents Required

### Identity Proof

- Aadhaar card (original copy or just your 12-digit Aadhaar number)
- Any valid Proof of Identity (POI) from the UIDAI-approved list:
  - Passport
  - PAN Card
  - Voter ID
  - Driving Licence

### Address Proof (only if updating address too)

- Any valid Proof of Address (POA) as per the UIDAI-approved list
- Not required if you are only updating your mobile number

### Other Requirements

- Your new mobile number — it must be active and able to receive OTPs at the centre
- Aadhaar Update/Correction Form — available free of cost at the centre itself

## Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1: Find the Nearest Aadhaar Centre

Go to the UIDAI Bhuvan portal at [https://bhuvan-app3.nrsc.gov.in/aadhaar/](https://bhuvan-app3.nrsc.gov.in/aadhaar/) and search for a centre near you.

### Step 2: Visit the Centre in Person

Carry your original Aadhaar card along with a valid identity proof.

### Step 3: Fill the Aadhaar Update/Correction Form

Tick "Mobile Number" as the field you want to update.

### Step 4: Submit the Form

Hand over the filled form along with your Aadhaar number to the centre operator.

### Step 5: Complete Biometric Authentication

Fingerprint and/or iris scan. This is mandatory.

### Step 6: Provide Your New Mobile Number

Give your new active mobile number to the operator for linking.

### Step 7: Pay the Fee

₹50 (inclusive of GST) at the counter.

### Step 8: Collect the Acknowledgement Slip

Contains your Update Request Number (URN). Keep it safe.

### Step 9: Track Your Update Status

Visit [https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/) and enter your URN.

## Processing Time

Typically 10–30 days, up to 90 days maximum. Call UIDAI helpline **1947** if delayed.

## How to Track Your Aadhaar Update Status

1. Visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/)
2. Click "Check Aadhaar Update Status"
3. Enter your URN and Aadhaar number
4. View status

## Important Tips

1. **Do not fall for online scams** — There is no official way to update your Aadhaar mobile number online. Any website claiming to do so is fake.
2. **Keep your new SIM active** — The number must be working at the time of visit.
3. **Go early to avoid crowds** — Aadhaar centres get busy after 11 AM.
4. **Keep your acknowledgement slip safe** — The URN is the only way to track your update.
5. **Clean and dry fingers** — For smooth biometric scan.

## FAQs

**Q: Can I update my Aadhaar mobile number online?**
A: No. UIDAI does not allow mobile number updates online. You must visit an Aadhaar centre.

**Q: How much does it cost?**
A: ₹50 inclusive of GST.

**Q: Can someone else go on my behalf?**
A: No. Biometric authentication is mandatory — you must go in person.

**Q: How long does it take?**
A: 10 to 30 days, up to 90 days maximum.

**Q: What if fingerprint fails?**
A: Operator will try iris scan instead.

**Q: Do I need documents?**
A: Aadhaar card + valid ID proof. Address proof only if updating address too.

**Q: How to find nearest centre?**
A: Use UIDAI Bhuvan portal at [https://bhuvan-app3.nrsc.gov.in/aadhaar/](https://bhuvan-app3.nrsc.gov.in/aadhaar/)
