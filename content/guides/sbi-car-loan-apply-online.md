# SBI Car Loan Apply Online: Interest Rate & EMI Calculator

State Bank of India (SBI) offers car loans at competitive interest rates for purchasing new and pre-owned cars. You can apply for an SBI car loan online through the YONO app or SBI's official website. This guide covers the complete process, interest rates, EMI calculation, documents required, and prepayment rules.

## What is SBI Car Loan?

SBI Car Loan is a secured loan offered by State Bank of India to help individuals purchase new or used cars. The loan covers up to 90% of the on-road price of the vehicle (ex-showroom price + registration + insurance + accessories). The car itself serves as collateral for the loan.

**Key highlights:**

- Loan amount: Up to ₹50 lakh for new cars
- Tenure: Up to 7 years (84 months)
- Funding: Up to 90% of on-road price for new cars
- No guarantor required for salaried individuals

## SBI Car Loan EMI Calculator

Use this formula to estimate your monthly EMI:

**EMI = [P × R × (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N – 1]**

Where P = Loan amount, R = Monthly interest rate, N = Number of months

**Example EMI for ₹8 lakh loan at 8.65%:**

| Tenure | EMI (approx.) | Total Interest |
|---|---|---|
| 5 years (60 months) | ₹16,390 | ₹1,83,400 |
| 7 years (84 months) | ₹12,750 | ₹2,71,000 |

Use the [official SBI EMI calculator](https://sbi.co.in/web/calculator/emi-calculator) for exact figures.

## Who is Eligible?

**For salaried individuals:**
- Age: 21–65 years (at loan maturity)
- Minimum net monthly income: ₹25,000
- Minimum 1 year of employment

**For self-employed:**
- Age: 21–65 years (at loan maturity)
- Minimum 2 years in business
- ITR for last 2 years required

**For pre-owned cars:**
- Car age should not exceed 5 years at the time of loan application
- Maximum loan tenure: 5 years for used cars

## How to Apply for SBI Car Loan Online via YONO

### Method 1: YONO App

1. Download and install the **YONO SBI** app from Google Play Store or Apple App Store
2. Log in with your SBI internet banking credentials
3. Go to **"Shop and Order"** → **"Auto Loan"**
4. Enter the car details — make, model, variant, dealer, and on-road price
5. Choose loan amount and preferred tenure
6. Upload required documents (Aadhaar, PAN, salary slips)
7. Submit the application
8. You will receive an **application reference number** via SMS
9. SBI will process your application and contact you for verification
10. Upon approval, the loan amount is disbursed directly to the car dealer

### Method 2: SBI Official Website

1. Visit [SBI Auto Loan page](https://sbi.co.in/web/personal-banking/loans/auto-loans/car-loan)
2. Click **"Apply Now"**
3. Fill in personal details, employment information, and loan requirements
4. Upload scanned copies of documents
5. Submit and note the reference number
6. Visit your nearest SBI branch with original documents for verification

### Method 3: Branch Visit

1. Visit your nearest SBI branch
2. Request the car loan application form
3. Fill in the form and attach required documents
4. Submit the form to the loan officer
5. SBI will process and verify your application within 7 working days

## Prepayment and Foreclosure Rules

As per **RBI guidelines**, SBI does not charge any prepayment or foreclosure penalty on floating rate car loans taken by individual borrowers. Key points:

- You can prepay any amount at any time without penalty
- Part-prepayment reduces your outstanding principal and either reduces EMI or tenure
- Full foreclosure can be done by paying the outstanding principal
- Request for NOC (No Objection Certificate) after full repayment — SBI issues it within 30 days
- Get the **hypothecation removed** from the RC after receiving NOC

## Important Tips

1. **Check YONO for pre-approved offers** — SBI often provides pre-approved car loans to existing customers with faster processing and better rates
2. **Maintain a good CIBIL score** — A credit score of 750+ helps get the best interest rate
3. **Compare on-road price** — Negotiate with the dealer before applying, as loan amount is based on on-road price
4. **Choose the right tenure** — Shorter tenure means higher EMI but less total interest paid
5. **Set up auto-debit for EMI** — Avoid late payment charges by setting up standing instructions from your SBI account

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. What is the minimum and maximum SBI car loan amount?
The minimum car loan amount is ₹1 lakh and the maximum is up to ₹50 lakh for new cars, subject to eligibility and vehicle value.

### Q2. Can I apply for SBI car loan without being an SBI account holder?
Yes, non-SBI customers can also apply for an SBI car loan. However, having an SBI salary account may give you a slightly better interest rate.

### Q3. Does SBI provide 100% financing for car purchase?
No, SBI provides up to 90% of the on-road price. You need to pay at least 10% as down payment from your own funds.

### Q4. Can I prepay my SBI car loan early?
Yes, SBI allows prepayment of floating rate car loans without any penalty for individual borrowers, as per RBI guidelines.

### Q5. How do I check my SBI car loan status?
You can check your loan application status through the YONO app, [SBI net banking](/guide/sbi-net-banking-registration-login), or by calling SBI customer care at 1800-11-2211 (toll-free).

### Q6. What happens if I miss an EMI payment?
SBI charges a penal interest of 2% per annum on the overdue EMI amount. Repeated defaults may affect your CIBIL score and lead to recovery proceedings.

### Q7. Is car insurance mandatory for SBI car loan?
Yes, comprehensive car insurance is mandatory for the entire loan tenure. You must renew it annually and submit proof to SBI.
