## What is PM Rozgar Mela?

**PM Rozgar Mela** is a government initiative where the Prime Minister (and government officials) personally distributes **appointment letters** to newly selected government employees at large employment fairs across the country. These are **actual government job appointment letters** — not promises, but formal joining letters for already-selected candidates.

Additionally, **Rozgar Melas** in the broader sense include:
1. **PM Rozgar Mela** — distributing appointment letters to government recruits
2. **Skill India Rozgar Melas** — job fairs connecting trained youth with private employers
3. **State Rozgar Melas** — organized by state employment departments

## PM Rozgar Mela — Government Appointment Letters

- First PM Rozgar Mela: October 22, 2022
- Multiple rounds have since been held across states
- **Candidates already selected** through SSC, UPSC, Railways, etc. receive their appointment letters at the mela
- Conducted simultaneously at multiple locations across India via video conferencing

**Who receives the letter:** Central government recruits who have completed selection process — medical, verification, document checking — for posts like:
- Group B/C posts (MTS, LDC, Data Entry Operators)
- Railways staff
- Defence civilian posts
- Central armed police forces

**If you are a government exam qualifier:** Watch for announcements from your recruiting agency (SSC, UPSC, Railways, etc.) about joining via PM Rozgar Mela.

## Skill India Job Fairs (Private Sector)

**Skill India Rozgar Melas** connect PMKVY-trained and other skilled youth with private employers:

### How to Register as a Job Seeker:

1. Go to **[rozgarmela.pmkvy.in](https://rozgarmela.pmkvy.in)**
2. Click **"Job Seeker Registration"**
3. Enter: name, Aadhaar, skills, education, preferred location
4. View **upcoming Rozgar Melas** in your area
5. Register for a specific mela → receive confirmation
6. Attend the mela with your documents and CV

### At the Mela:

- Multiple employers set up booths
- You can walk up, submit CV, and be interviewed on the spot
- Some employers give **offer letters on the same day**
- Free — no registration fee

## State Rozgar Melas

Many states organize their own Rozgar Melas:
- **UP Rozgar Mela:** Employment department districts organize quarterly fairs
- **Bihar Rozgar Mela:** State skill mission organizes regularly
- **Rajasthan Rozgar Mela:** via RSRSDC (Rajasthan Skill and Livelihoods Development Corporation)

Search "[state name] rozgar mela" for your state's employment department portal.

## Documents to Carry to a Rozgar Mela

1. **Aadhaar card** (identity)
2. **Educational certificates** (10th, 12th, graduation, skill certificates)
3. **Updated CV / Resume** (at least 10 copies)
4. **Passport-size photographs**
5. **Caste certificate** (if applicable — some employers have SC/ST hiring drives)
6. **PMKVY / Skill India certificate** (if applicable)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I received a PM Rozgar Mela appointment letter. When do I join?
The appointment letter includes the **date of joining** and the office where you join. Follow the instructions on the letter. If any documents are missing, contact the recruiting agency (SSC/RRB/etc.) immediately.

### I am not PMKVY-trained. Can I still attend a Rozgar Mela as a job seeker?
Yes — Skill India Rozgar Melas are open to all job seekers, not just PMKVY trainees. However, having a skill certificate improves your chances.

### How do I know when a Rozgar Mela is happening near me?
Register at [rozgarmela.pmkvy.in](https://rozgarmela.pmkvy.in) and opt for SMS/email notifications. Also check your **district employment office** — they often display mela dates on notice boards.

### Is there a registration fee for job seekers?
No — **completely free** for job seekers. Do not pay anyone claiming to register you for a Rozgar Mela.
