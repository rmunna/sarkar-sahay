## What is Vahali Dikri Yojana?

Vahali Dikri Yojana ("Beloved Daughter Scheme") was launched by the Government of Gujarat in 2019 to promote the welfare and education of girl children and to discourage child marriage. The scheme provides **financial assistance at three key stages** of a girl's life.

### Benefits

| Stage | Amount |
|-------|--------|
| Class 1 enrollment | ₹4,000 |
| Class 9 enrollment | ₹6,000 |
| On turning 18 (if unmarried) | ₹1,00,000 |
| **Total** | **₹1,10,000** |

The scheme applies to the **first two daughters** of a family.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Girl child** born in Gujarat
- Family must be **domiciled in Gujarat**
- Family annual income must not exceed **₹2 lakh**
- Applicable for **first two daughters** only
- Girl must be enrolled in school at the relevant stages
- For the final ₹1 lakh instalment, the girl must be **unmarried and at least 18 years old**
- Must have a bank account in the girl's name

## Documents Required

- Birth certificate of the girl child
- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) of parents and girl
- Income certificate (annual income ≤ ₹2 lakh)
- Domicile certificate of Gujarat
- BPL/ration card
- School enrollment certificate (for Class 1 and Class 9 instalments)
- Bank passbook in the girl's name or joint with mother
- Passport-size photographs
- Caste certificate (if applicable)

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Online Application

1. **Visit** [Digital Gujarat portal](https://digitalgujarat.gov.in) or the WCD department portal
2. **Register** with mobile number
3. **Select** Vahali Dikri Yojana
4. **Fill** the application form with girl child and family details
5. **Upload** required documents
6. **Submit** and save the application number
7. **Track** status online

### Offline Application

1. **Visit** the nearest Anganwadi centre or District WCD office
2. **Collect** the Vahali Dikri application form
3. **Fill** and attach all documents
4. **Submit** to the Anganwadi worker or WCD officer
5. **Collect** acknowledgement receipt

## Processing Time

- Verification: **1-3 months**
- Instalment disbursement: After verification of each milestone
- Amount credited via DBT

## Fees

**No fees** for application.

## Important Tips

1. **Register early** — Apply soon after the girl's birth to ensure you don't miss the first milestone
2. **Keep documents updated** — Ensure Aadhaar and bank details are current
3. **Only first two daughters** — Third and subsequent daughters are not covered
4. **Marriage condition** — The ₹1 lakh is given only if the girl is unmarried at 18
5. **Combine with other schemes** — Check eligibility for [Namo Lakshmi Yojana](/guide/gj-namo-lakshmi) for additional education support

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. What if I have three daughters?
Only the **first two** daughters are eligible under this scheme.

### Q2. Can families above ₹2 lakh income apply?
No. The annual family income limit is **₹2 lakh**. An income certificate is required.

### Q3. Is the ₹1 lakh given in lump sum?
Yes. The final instalment of ₹1,00,000 is disbursed as a **lump sum** when the girl turns 18 (if unmarried).

### Q4. Can NRI Gujarati families apply?
No. The family must be a **resident of Gujarat** with valid domicile proof.

### Q5. Is this scheme available for adopted daughters?
Yes, provided the adoption is legally documented and the family meets all other eligibility criteria.
