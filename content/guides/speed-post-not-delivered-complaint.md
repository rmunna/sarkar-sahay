# Speed Post Not Delivered? File Complaint & Track

## When Should You File a Complaint?

File a complaint if your Speed Post:
- Has **not been delivered within the guaranteed timeframe** (typically 3-5 days for domestic)
- Shows **"Item Returned"** without a valid delivery attempt
- Tracking is **stuck for more than 48 hours** with no update
- Was delivered to the **wrong person** or is **damaged**
- Is **lost** with no tracking updates for several days

## Speed Post Delivery Guarantees

| Route | Guaranteed Delivery |
|---|---|
| Within same city | 1-2 days |
| Metro to Metro | 2-3 days |
| State capitals | 3-4 days |
| Other locations | 4-5 days |

If Speed Post is **not delivered within the guaranteed time**, you may be eligible for a **refund of the Speed Post fee**.

## How to File a Complaint — Step-by-Step

### Method 1: Online Complaint (Recommended)

1. Visit [India Post Grievance Portal](https://www.indiapost.gov.in/vas/Pages/IndiaPostHome.aspx)
2. Click on **"Register Your Complaint"**
3. Select complaint type — **"Speed Post"** or **"Accountable Mail"**
4. Enter your **13-digit consignment number**
5. Fill in details: sender name, receiver name, booking date, source and destination post offices
6. Describe the issue clearly (delay, non-delivery, damage, etc.)
7. Provide your **contact number and email** for updates
8. Submit the complaint — note down the **complaint/docket number**
9. Track your complaint status on the same portal

### Method 2: CPGRAMS (Central Government Grievance Portal)

1. Visit [CPGRAMS Portal](https://pgportal.gov.in/)
2. Register or log in with your credentials
3. Select **Ministry: Communications** → **Department of Posts**
4. File your grievance with full details and consignment number
5. Get a **unique grievance ID** for tracking
6. Response is typically received within **30-60 days**

### Method 3: Call India Post Helpline

- **Toll-free number:** 1800-11-2011
- **Available:** Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 5 PM
- Provide your consignment number and complaint details
- Note the complaint reference number given by the agent

### Method 4: Visit the Post Office

1. Visit the **booking post office** or your **local delivery post office**
2. Meet the **Postmaster** with your Speed Post receipt
3. File a written complaint with consignment number and details
4. Get acknowledgement in writing

## How to Claim Refund for Non-Delivery

If Speed Post was **not delivered within the guaranteed timeframe**:

1. File a complaint using any method above
2. Mention specifically that you are requesting a **refund under the Speed Post guarantee scheme**
3. Provide the **booking receipt** showing the Speed Post fee paid
4. India Post will investigate and process the **refund of Speed Post charges** (not the value of contents)
5. Refund is typically credited to source post office — collect it there

**Note:** Refund applies to the **postal fee only**, not the value of the article inside.

## Escalation Steps

If your complaint is not resolved:

| Step | Action | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Complaint to local post office / online portal | Wait 7 days |
| 2 | Escalate to **Divisional Superintendent** of the postal division | Wait 15 days |
| 3 | Escalate to **Chief Postmaster General** of your postal circle | Wait 30 days |
| 4 | File on **CPGRAMS** (pgportal.gov.in) | Wait 30-60 days |
| 5 | Approach **Consumer Forum** for compensation (if significant loss) | As needed |

## Compensation for Lost Articles

| Type | Maximum Compensation |
|---|---|
| Speed Post (domestic) | ₹1,000 (or as per current rules) |
| Insured Speed Post | Up to insured value |
| Registered Post | ₹500 |

**For valuable items**, always opt for **insured Speed Post** at the time of booking.

## Important Tips

1. **Always keep the booking receipt** — it is proof of your Speed Post transaction
2. **File complaints early** — don't wait too long after the guaranteed delivery period
3. **Use online portal** — faster response than visiting the post office
4. **Track before complaining** — check tracking status first; the item may be out for delivery
5. **Insure valuable items** — standard Speed Post compensation is limited

## FAQs

### How do I file a complaint for Speed Post not delivered?

Visit [India Post grievance portal](https://www.indiapost.gov.in/vas/Pages/IndiaPostHome.aspx), click "Register Your Complaint," enter your consignment number and details, and submit. Note the complaint/docket number.

### Can I get a refund if Speed Post is delayed?

Yes, if Speed Post is not delivered within the guaranteed timeframe, you can claim a **refund of the Speed Post fee** by filing a complaint with your booking receipt.

### How long does India Post take to resolve complaints?

Online complaints are typically acknowledged within **3-7 days** and resolved within **15-30 days**. CPGRAMS complaints may take **30-60 days**.

### What if Speed Post shows delivered but I didn't receive it?

File a complaint immediately on the India Post grievance portal. Mention that tracking shows "delivered" but you did not receive the item. India Post will investigate with the delivery postman.

### Can I approach Consumer Forum for lost Speed Post?

Yes, if India Post does not resolve your complaint satisfactorily, you can file a case in the **District Consumer Forum** under the Consumer Protection Act for compensation.

### What is the helpline number for India Post complaints?

Call **1800-11-2011** (toll-free), available Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 5 PM.

### How do I track my complaint status?

Visit the same portal where you filed the complaint and enter your **complaint/docket number** to check the current status and any responses.
