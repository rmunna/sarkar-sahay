# How to Apply for Ration Card Online in Bihar (2026)

If you live in Bihar and want to get a new ration card, you can now apply online. The Bihar government provides ration cards through the **Food & Consumer Protection Department**. With a ration card, your family can buy wheat, rice, sugar, and kerosene at very low prices from your nearest ration shop (Fair Price Shop).

This guide covers the complete Bihar-specific process. For a general overview of the ration card system across India, see our [national guide on how to apply for a ration card online](/guide/ration-card-apply-online).

---

## Types of Ration Cards in Bihar

Bihar issues three types of ration cards under the **National Food Security Act (NFSA)**:

| Type | Color | Who Is Eligible | Monthly Entitlement |
|------|-------|----------------|---------------------|
| **AAY (Antyodaya Anna Yojana)** | Yellow | Poorest of the poor families | 35 kg food grains per family |
| **PHH (Priority Household)** | Pink/Red | Families below poverty line | 5 kg per person per month |
| **Non-Priority** | Blue/White | Families above poverty line | Can be used as identity proof |

Most families in Bihar apply for **PHH** or **AAY** category cards.

---

## Who Can Apply?

You are eligible to apply for a ration card in Bihar if:

- You are a **permanent resident of Bihar**
- You are the **head of your family** (or an adult family member applying on behalf)
- You **do not already have a ration card** in Bihar or any other state
- Your family's **annual income** is within the limits set by the state government
- You have a valid **Aadhaar card**

> **Important:** Under the One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC) scheme, you cannot hold ration cards in two different states. If you have moved to Bihar from another state, surrender your old ration card first.

---

## Documents Required

Keep these documents ready before starting your application:

1. **Aadhaar card** of the head of family and all family members
2. **Address proof** — voter ID, electricity bill, or rent agreement showing Bihar address
3. **Income certificate** — issued by the Block Development Officer (BDO) or Circle Officer
4. **Passport-size photographs** — of the head of the family
5. **Bank account details** — passbook copy or cancelled cheque
6. **Family details** — name, age, gender, Aadhaar number, and relationship of each member
7. **Mobile number** linked to Aadhaar (for OTP verification)
8. **Surrender certificate** — if you had a ration card in another state (if applicable)

> **Tip:** Get your income certificate made first. It usually takes 7–10 days from the RTPS counter or serviceonline.bihar.gov.in itself.

## How to Apply Online — Step-by-Step

Bihar uses the **Service Online Bihar** portal for ration card applications. Here is the complete process:

### Step 1: Visit the Service Online Portal

Go to **[serviceonline.bihar.gov.in](https://serviceonline.bihar.gov.in)**

If you don't have an account, click on **"Register"** and create one using your mobile number, email, and Aadhaar details.

### Step 2: Log In to Your Account

Use your username and password to log in. You will receive an OTP on your registered mobile number.

### Step 3: Find the Ration Card Service

After logging in:
- Go to **"Food & Consumer Protection Department"** from the department list
- Click on **"Issue of New Ration Card"** or **"नया राशन कार्ड के लिए आवेदन"**

### Step 4: Fill the Application Form

The form will ask for:
- **District, Block, Panchayat/Ward** — select your location
- **Head of family details** — name, father/husband name, Aadhaar number, mobile number
- **Family member details** — add each member with their name, age, gender, Aadhaar, and relationship
- **Category** — select AAY, PHH, or Non-Priority based on your eligibility
- **Bank account details** — for any future DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer)
- **Current address** — full address in Bihar

> **Note:** Fill the form carefully. Wrong information will delay your application or lead to rejection.

### Step 5: Upload Documents

Upload scanned copies of:
- Aadhaar card (front and back)
- Passport-size photo
- Income certificate
- Address proof
- Bank passbook first page

**File format:** PDF or JPG | **File size:** Usually under 500 KB per document

### Step 6: Submit and Note Your Application Number

After reviewing all details:
- Click **"Submit"**
- You will get an **acknowledgement receipt** with an **application number**
- **Save or print this receipt** — you will need the application number to track your status

## Application Fee

| Category | Fee |
|----------|-----|
| AAY / PHH (Below Poverty Line) | **Free (No fee)** |
| Non-Priority (Above Poverty Line) | **₹5 to ₹10** (nominal processing fee) |

Most ration card applications in Bihar are **free of charge**. If applying through a CSC (Common Service Centre) or Vasudha Kendra, they may charge **₹20–₹50** as a service fee.

---

## Processing Time

| Stage | Expected Time |
|-------|--------------|
| Application submission to verification | 7–15 days |
| Field verification by supply inspector | 15–30 days |
| Ration card issue after approval | 7–15 days |
| **Total estimated time** | **30–60 days** |

In practice, it can take **1 to 2 months** from application to receiving your ration card. Urban areas (Patna, Gaya, Muzaffarpur) may be faster. Rural areas may take slightly longer depending on the block office workload.

## How to Track Your Application Status

You can check where your application stands:

1. Visit **[serviceonline.bihar.gov.in](https://serviceonline.bihar.gov.in)**
2. Click on **"Track Application Status"** (आवेदन की स्थिति देखें)
3. Enter your **application number** or **Aadhaar number**
4. Click **"Search"** to see the current status

You can also check your ration card details on the **ePDS Bihar portal**:
- Visit **[epds.bihar.gov.in](https://epds.bihar.gov.in)**
- Search by district, block, and FPS (Fair Price Shop) to find your card details
- This portal also shows your monthly ration distribution history

---

## Offline Application Option

If you are not comfortable applying online, you can also apply offline:

1. Visit your **Block Supply Office** (प्रखंड आपूर्ति कार्यालय) or nearest **Vasudha Kendra / CSC Centre**
2. Collect the ration card application form
3. Fill it with all family member details
4. Attach photocopies of required documents
5. Submit the form and collect the acknowledgement slip

The Vasudha Kendra operator can also submit the online application on your behalf for a small service fee.

---

## Common Reasons for Rejection

Your application may be rejected if:

- **Aadhaar details don't match** — name or date of birth mismatch between Aadhaar and application
- **Duplicate application** — you or a family member already has an active ration card
- **Incomplete documents** — missing income certificate or address proof
- **Wrong category selected** — income does not match the category you applied for
- **Out-of-state ration card** — you still hold a ration card in another state

> **What to do if rejected?** Correct the errors and reapply. You can also visit your block supply office to understand the exact reason.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q: How do I apply for a ration card online in Bihar?
Visit serviceonline.bihar.gov.in, register or login, go to Food & Consumer Protection Department, and click "Issue of New Ration Card." Fill in your family details, upload Aadhaar, income certificate, and address proof, then submit. You will receive an application number for tracking.

### Q: What documents are needed for a Bihar ration card application?
You need Aadhaar cards of all family members, address proof (voter ID or electricity bill), income certificate from the BDO or Circle Officer, passport-size photo, bank account details, and mobile number linked to Aadhaar.

### Q: Is there any fee for applying for a ration card in Bihar?
Ration card applications for AAY and PHH (Below Poverty Line) categories are completely free. Non-priority (APL) cards have a nominal fee of ₹5 to ₹10. If applying through a Vasudha Kendra, they may charge ₹20–₹50 as service fee.

### Q: How long does it take to get a ration card in Bihar?
The total processing time is typically 30 to 60 days, covering application submission, field verification by the supply inspector, and final card issuance. Urban areas like Patna may be faster than rural areas.

### Q: How can I track my Bihar ration card application status?
Visit serviceonline.bihar.gov.in and click "Track Application Status." Enter your application number or Aadhaar number and click Search. You can also check your ration card details on the ePDS Bihar portal at epds.bihar.gov.in.

### Q: What are the common reasons for ration card rejection in Bihar?
Common reasons include Aadhaar details mismatch, duplicate application (you or a family member already has a ration card), incomplete documents, wrong category selection, or holding a ration card in another state.
