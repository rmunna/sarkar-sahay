# UP Police SI (Sub Inspector) 2026 — Complete Guide

The Uttar Pradesh Police Sub Inspector (SI) / Daroga recruitment is one of the most competitive state police examinations in India. With **9,000–12,000 vacancies** (one of the largest SI recruitments), it attracts 15–20 lakh applications from across UP.

A UP Police SI earns ₹35,000–₹45,000/month gross and carries significant authority — responsible for investigation, law and order, and managing constables.

## Exam Pattern

| Paper | Subject | Questions | Marks | Time |
|-------|---------|-----------|-------|------|
| **Paper 1** | General Hindi | 40 | 100 | 2 hrs |
| **Paper 2** | GK + Current Affairs | 40 | 100 | 2 hrs |

| | Mental Aptitude, Intelligence, Reasoning | 40 | 100 | |

- **Total marks:** 400 (Paper 1: 100 + Paper 2: 300)
- **Negative marking:** Not specified in most cycles (check notification)
- Language: Hindi (General Hindi paper is 100% in Hindi)

Qualifying — height and chest measurement as per eligibility table

| Category | Distance | Time |
|----------|----------|------|

Standard UP Police medical — eyesight, colour blindness, fitness parameters

## Detailed Syllabus

### General Hindi (Paper 1) — 100 marks
- Hindi Grammar: संज्ञा, सर्वनाम, विशेषण, क्रिया, समास, संधि, विलोम, पर्यायवाची
- Tatsam-Tadbhav, Idioms and Phrases (मुहावरे)
- Fill in the blanks, Spelling correction
- Sentence formation and correction
- Reading Comprehension (Hindi passages)
- Letter writing and formal language

### General Knowledge + Current Affairs (Paper 2)
- Current affairs (last 6 months) — national and international
- UP-specific GK: History of UP, geography, culture, economy, UP government schemes
- Indian History (ancient, medieval, modern), Freedom struggle
- Indian Geography, World Geography
- Indian Polity, Indian Economy
- Science and Technology
- Important dates, awards, sports

### Numerical & Mental Ability
Number System, Simplification, Fractions, LCM/HCF, Percentages, Ratio & Proportion, Profit/Loss, SI/CI, Time-Work, Time-Distance, Mensuration, Averages, Statistics basics

### Mental Aptitude, Intelligence, Reasoning
Analogies, Similarities/Differences, Coding-Decoding, Number Series, Odd one out, Syllogism, Blood Relations, Direction Sense, Venn Diagrams, Non-verbal Reasoning, Arrangement problems

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is the UP Police SI exam conducted in Hindi or English?**
The exam is primarily in Hindi. Paper 1 (General Hindi) is compulsory in Hindi. Paper 2 can be attempted in Hindi or English depending on the specific cycle's notification.

**What is the age limit for UP Police SI 2026?**
21–28 years for general category. Age relaxation: OBC +3, SC/ST +5 years.

**Is there negative marking in UP Police SI written exam?**
The negative marking policy varies by recruitment cycle. Typically no negative marking, but confirm in the official notification.

**What is the selection ratio like for UP Police SI?**
Very competitive — roughly 1 in 20–30 applicants makes the final cut. Written exam + physical test both require serious preparation.

**Can OBC candidates from other states apply for UP Police SI?**
UP Police SI is a state recruitment — for maximum OBC benefit (lower cut-offs, age relaxation), you typically need UP domicile/OBC certificate from UP. Check notification for specific residency requirements.
