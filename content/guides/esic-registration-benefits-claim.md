# ESIC Registration & Benefits — How to Claim ESI Benefits

The **Employees' State Insurance Corporation (ESIC)** is a statutory body under the Ministry of Labour & Employment that provides comprehensive social security to Indian workers. The **ESI Scheme** covers medical care, maternity benefits, disability benefits, dependent benefits, and unemployment allowance for employees earning up to ₹21,000 per month.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent informational platform and is not affiliated with ESIC or the Government of India.

## What is ESIC?

The ESI (Employees' State Insurance) Scheme is a self-financing social security scheme that provides:

- **Medical benefits** — Free medical care for insured persons and their dependents
- **Sickness benefit** — Cash compensation during certified illness
- **Maternity benefit** — Paid maternity leave
- **Disablement benefit** — Compensation for employment injury/occupational disease
- **Dependent's benefit** — Pension for dependents if insured person dies
- **Unemployment allowance** — Under Rajiv Gandhi Shramik Kalyan Yojana (RGSKY)
- **Funeral expenses** — ₹15,000 for funeral of insured person

## Who is Eligible?

### For Employees (Insured Persons)
- Employees earning **gross wages up to ₹21,000 per month** (₹25,000 for PwD)
- Working in factories/establishments with **10 or more employees** (some states: 20+)
- Covers all types of employees — permanent, temporary, contract, casual
- Coverage is **mandatory** — not optional for eligible employees

### For Employers
- All factories with 10+ workers using power (or 20+ without power)
- Shops, hotels, restaurants, cinemas, road transport, newspaper establishments with 10+ employees
- Establishments notified by the government

### Dependents Covered
- Spouse
- Children (below 25 years)
- Dependent parents

## Contribution Rates

| Contributor | Rate |
|-------------|------|
| Employer | 3.25% of employee's wages |
| Employee | 0.75% of wages |
| Employees earning up to ₹137/day | Exempt from employee contribution |

*Example: If your monthly salary is ₹15,000, employer pays ₹487.50 and you pay ₹112.50 per month.*

## How to Register on ESIC

### For Employers

1. **Visit** [esic.gov.in](https://www.esic.gov.in)
2. Click **"Employer Login"** → **"Sign Up"**
3. Fill the **Employer Registration Form (Form 1)**
4. Enter establishment details — name, address, type, employee count
5. Upload documents — PAN, certificate of incorporation, address proof
6. Submit and receive **17-digit ESIC Code Number**
7. Register all eligible employees within 10 days of joining

### For Employees

1. Employer registers you on the ESIC portal
2. You receive an **Insurance Number (10-digit)**
3. **Download the ESIC Pehchan Card** from the portal or app
4. Choose your **ESIC dispensary/hospital** for primary medical care
5. Register family members (spouse, children, parents) for dependent benefits

### Self-Registration (Employee)

1. Visit [esic.gov.in](https://www.esic.gov.in) → **"Insured Person Login"**
2. Enter your Insurance Number
3. Complete your profile with personal and family details
4. Upload photograph and family member details
5. Download your **ESI Pehchan Card**

## Documents Required

### For Employer Registration
- PAN card of the business
- Certificate of Registration / Incorporation
- Address proof of the establishment
- Bank account details
- List of employees with wages

### For Employee Registration
- Aadhaar card
- Bank account details
- Passport-size photographs (employee + family members)
- Family member details (name, relation, date of birth, Aadhaar)
- Appointment letter or salary slip

## ESI Benefits — Detailed Breakdown

### 1. Medical Benefit
- **Full medical care** for insured person and family at ESIC hospitals and dispensaries
- Includes OPD, hospitalization, specialist treatment, medicines, diagnostics
- **Super specialty treatment** at empanelled hospitals
- **No upper limit** on medical expenditure
- Available from **day one** of employment

### 2. Sickness Benefit
- **70% of average daily wages** during certified sickness
- Maximum **91 days** in two consecutive benefit periods
- Extended sickness benefit: 80% of wages for up to 2 years for specified long-term diseases (TB, cancer, mental illness, etc.)

### 3. Maternity Benefit
- **100% of average daily wages** for 26 weeks (approximately 6 months)
- Additional 6 weeks for complications/miscarriage
- Covers medical expenses for delivery
- Available for up to **2 surviving children**

### 4. Disablement Benefit
- **Temporary disablement:** 90% of average daily wages during treatment
- **Permanent disablement:** Monthly pension based on percentage of disability
- Covers employment injury and occupational diseases
- Available from **day one** — no waiting period

### 5. Dependent's Benefit
- **90% of average daily wages** as monthly pension to dependents
- If insured person dies due to employment injury/occupational disease
- Widow/widower gets pension for life (or until remarriage)
- Children get pension until age 25

### 6. Unemployment Allowance (RGSKY)
- **50% of average daily wages** for up to 2 years
- Available if retrenched, involuntarily terminated, or factory closed
- Must have contributed for at least 3 years
- Free vocational training during unemployment period

### 7. Funeral Expenses
- **₹15,000** lump sum to the family for funeral expenses

## How to Claim ESI Benefits

### Medical Treatment
1. Visit your registered **ESIC dispensary/hospital** with your Pehchan Card
2. For super specialty treatment, get a **referral** from the ESIC hospital
3. Treatment at empanelled private hospitals if referred by ESIC

### Cash Benefits (Sickness/Maternity/Disability)
1. Login to [esic.gov.in](https://www.esic.gov.in) → **Insured Person Login**
2. Go to **"Claim Submission"**
3. Select the type of benefit
4. Upload supporting documents (medical certificate, hospital records)
5. Submit the claim
6. Claim is verified by the employer and ESIC Branch Office
7. **Amount credited to your bank account via DBT**

## Important Tips

1. **Register Family Members** — Ensure all dependents are registered to avail medical benefits for the whole family.
2. **Choose Dispensary Wisely** — Select an ESIC dispensary/hospital close to your residence for convenience.
3. **Maintain Contribution Record** — Check your contribution history regularly on the ESIC portal.
4. **Claim Promptly** — File sickness and maternity claims within the prescribed time to avoid delays.
5. **Know Your Rights** — Employers cannot deduct more than 0.75% or deny ESI registration to eligible employees.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Can I use ESIC benefits at any hospital?
No, you must visit your registered ESIC dispensary/hospital for primary care. Super specialty treatment at empanelled private hospitals requires a referral from the ESIC hospital.

### Q2: What happens to my ESIC benefits if I change jobs?
Your ESIC Insurance Number remains the same across employers. When you join a new company, your new employer should register you under the same Insurance Number. Benefits continue if contribution conditions are met.

### Q3: Can I get ESIC benefits during the notice period?
Yes, as long as your employer continues paying ESIC contributions during the notice period, you remain eligible for all benefits.

### Q4: Is there a waiting period for medical benefits under ESIC?
Medical benefits are available from day one of insurable employment. However, sickness and maternity cash benefits require a minimum contribution period (usually 78 days in the relevant contribution period).

### Q5: Can retired employees continue to receive ESIC medical benefits?
Yes, insured persons who have been insured for 5+ years can continue to receive medical benefits after retirement by paying a nominal annual contribution.

### Q6: How do I check my ESIC contribution details?
Login to [esic.gov.in](https://www.esic.gov.in) with your Insurance Number and check the "Contribution Details" section. You can also download your contribution statement.
