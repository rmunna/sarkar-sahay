# Meghalaya Government Schemes and Services – Complete Guide

Meghalaya, the "Abode of Clouds" in Northeast India, is governed by the National People's Party (NPP) under Chief Minister Conrad K. Sangma. The state government has introduced several innovative schemes covering healthcare, housing, energy, education, agriculture, and citizen services — designed to uplift communities across the Khasi, Jaintia, and Garo Hills.

This comprehensive hub covers all major Meghalaya government schemes and services so you can find the right programme, check eligibility, and apply with confidence.

## Healthcare Schemes

### Meghalaya Health Insurance Scheme (MHIS)

MHIS is the state's flagship health insurance programme providing cashless treatment to families, especially those not covered under central schemes. It works alongside [Ayushman Bharat – Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB-PMJAY)](/guide/ayushman-bharat-card-download-eligibility) to extend coverage to a wider population.

**Key Details:**
- **Coverage:** Up to ₹5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary hospitalisation
- **Eligibility:** Families identified through SECC data and state beneficiary lists
- **Cashless Treatment:** Available at empanelled government and private hospitals
- **Documents Required:** MHIS card, [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online), ration card

> **Tip:** If your family is already covered under AB-PMJAY, MHIS provides top-up and additional coverage for procedures not listed under the central scheme.

## Energy and Housing

### Chief Minister's Solar Mission

The Chief Minister's Solar Mission aims to promote solar energy adoption across Meghalaya's rural and semi-urban areas. The programme provides subsidised solar panels and systems to households, institutions, and community centres.

**Key Details:**
- **Benefit:** Subsidised solar rooftop panels and off-grid solar systems
- **Target:** Rural households, schools, community halls, and health centres
- **Subsidy:** Up to 40–60% subsidy on installation costs (varies by category)
- **How to Apply:** Through the district administration or the Meghalaya Energy Development Agency (MEDA)

This aligns with the central [PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana](/guide/pm-surya-ghar-muft-bijli) for rooftop solar subsidies.

### Meghalaya State Housing Financing Co-operative Society

This co-operative provides affordable housing loans to residents of Meghalaya, especially those in lower and middle-income groups. It serves as the state-level implementing agency for housing finance.

**Key Details:**
- **Purpose:** Affordable housing loans for construction, purchase, and renovation
- **Eligibility:** Residents of Meghalaya with stable income; priority for EWS/LIG categories
- **Interest Rate:** Concessional rates compared to commercial banks
- **Linkage:** Works alongside [Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY)](/guide/pm-awas-yojana-apply) for additional subsidy benefits

## Citizen Services

### FOCUS – Facilitation of Citizen's Unique Services

FOCUS is Meghalaya's single-window citizen service delivery platform. It enables residents to apply for certificates, permits, and government services online or through Common Service Centres (CSCs).

**Services Available:**
- Income certificate, domicile certificate, caste certificate
- Character certificate, backward area certificate
- Trade licence, NOCs
- Birth and death certificate corrections

**How It Works:**
1. Visit [focus.meghalaya.gov.in](https://focus.meghalaya.gov.in)
2. Register with your mobile number and Aadhaar
3. Select the required service and fill in the application form
4. Upload supporting documents and submit
5. Track application status using the reference number

> **Related:** Learn about [caste certificate processes across states](/guide/caste-certificate-state-wise-process) and [income certificate application](/guide/income-certificate-online).

### e-District Meghalaya

The e-District portal digitises the delivery of district-level services, making it easier for citizens to obtain certificates and avail government services without visiting offices.

**Key Services:**
- Scheduled Tribe (ST) certificate
- Residential certificate
- Solvency certificate
- Arms licence-related services
- Revenue court case status

**Portal:** [megedistrict.gov.in](https://megedistrict.gov.in)

## Education and Scholarships

### MeghEd Scholarships

The Meghalaya government offers multiple scholarship programmes through the Directorate of Education and allied departments to support students from economically weaker sections and meritorious backgrounds.

**Major Scholarships:**
- **State Merit Scholarship:** For top-performing students in Class 10 and 12 board exams
- **Umbrella Scheme for Education of ST Students:** Central scheme implemented by the state for tribal students
- **Post-Matric Scholarship for SC/ST/OBC:** Covers tuition, maintenance, and book allowance
- **Chief Minister's Special Scholarship:** For students pursuing higher education outside the state

**How to Apply:**
- Apply through the National Scholarship Portal ([scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in)) or the state education portal
- Required documents: marksheets, [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online), income certificate, bank passbook, caste certificate

## Entrepreneurship and Livelihoods

### Chief Minister's Entrepreneurship Promotion Scheme

This scheme encourages entrepreneurship among Meghalaya's youth by providing financial assistance, mentoring, and skill development support for setting up micro and small enterprises.

**Key Details:**
- **Benefit:** Seed capital, interest subsidy on loans, and handholding support
- **Eligibility:** Youth aged 18–45 years; residents of Meghalaya
- **Focus Sectors:** Food processing, tourism, handicrafts, IT services, agriculture-based enterprises
- **How to Apply:** Through the Directorate of Commerce & Industries or MBDA offices

### Meghalaya Basin Development Authority (MBDA)

MBDA is a key implementation body for livelihood and enterprise development programmes in the state. It manages several flagship projects including the Meghalaya Livelihoods and Access to Markets Project (LAMP) and the Community-Led Landscape Management Project (CLLMP).

**Key Programmes Under MBDA:**
- **LAMP:** Market linkages and value chain development for farmers and SHGs
- **CLLMP:** Sustainable natural resource management and landscape restoration
- **Enterprise Facilitation Centres:** Handholding support for rural entrepreneurs
- **Skill development and capacity building** for community organisations

**Website:** [mbda.gov.in](https://mbda.gov.in)

### Community-Led Landscape Management Project (CLLMP)

The CLLMP is a World Bank-supported project implemented through MBDA that focuses on sustainable land and water management, forest restoration, and climate-resilient agriculture.

**Key Features:**
- Community-driven planning for natural resource management
- Restoration of degraded landscapes and community forests
- Support for climate-resilient farming practices
- Livelihood diversification for forest-dependent communities

## Agriculture and Allied Sectors

### Meghalaya Livestock Mission

The Meghalaya Livestock Mission aims to boost animal husbandry and make the state self-sufficient in meat, egg, and milk production. It supports farmers with subsidised inputs, training, and modern infrastructure.

**Key Components:**
- **Piggery Development:** Distribution of improved pig breeds, feed, and veterinary support
- **Poultry Mission:** Support for backyard and commercial poultry farming
- **Dairy Development:** Assistance for dairy cooperatives and milk processing units
- **Fisheries:** Support for fish farming in community ponds and reservoirs

**Eligibility:** Farmers, SHGs, and rural entrepreneurs in Meghalaya

### Integrated Village Cooperative Societies (IVCS)

IVCS is a unique Meghalaya model for community-based cooperatives at the village level. These societies serve as the primary channel for delivering government inputs and services to rural areas.

**Functions:**
- Distribution of agricultural inputs (seeds, fertilisers, tools)
- Credit and savings services for members
- Procurement and marketing of agricultural produce
- Distribution of essential commodities through the Public Distribution System (PDS)
- Last-mile delivery of government scheme benefits

## How to Apply for Meghalaya Government Schemes

Most Meghalaya government schemes follow a similar application process:

1. **Check Eligibility:** Visit the scheme's official portal or FOCUS website to verify eligibility criteria
2. **Gather Documents:** Keep these ready —
   - [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
   - Domicile / residential certificate
   - Income certificate
   - Caste / tribe certificate
   - Bank passbook (for DBT)
   - Passport-size photographs
3. **Apply Online:** Use the FOCUS portal, e-District portal, or the specific scheme website
4. **Offline Option:** Visit the nearest Deputy Commissioner's office, Block Development Office, or Common Service Centre (CSC)
5. **Track Status:** Use the reference number from your application to track progress on the respective portal

> **Tip:** Many central schemes like [PMAY](/guide/pm-awas-yojana-apply), [Ayushman Bharat](/guide/ayushman-bharat-card-download-eligibility), and [PM Kisan](/guide/pm-kisan-samman-nidhi) are available in Meghalaya alongside state schemes. Check eligibility for both.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the FOCUS portal in Meghalaya?
FOCUS (Facilitation of Citizen's Unique Services) is Meghalaya's single-window online platform for applying for certificates, permits, and various government services. Visit [focus.meghalaya.gov.in](https://focus.meghalaya.gov.in) to register and apply.

### How do I get an MHIS health card in Meghalaya?
Eligible families identified through SECC data can obtain an MHIS card from the nearest empanelled hospital, Common Service Centre, or the District Medical & Health Office. Carry your Aadhaar card and ration card for verification.

### Can I apply for Meghalaya scholarships online?
Yes. Most state and central scholarships are available through the National Scholarship Portal ([scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in)) or the state education department website. Apply before the deadline with required documents.

### Who is eligible for the Chief Minister's Solar Mission?
Rural households, community institutions, schools, and health centres in Meghalaya can apply for subsidised solar installations through the district administration or MEDA. Priority is given to off-grid and remote areas.

### How do I apply for a caste or tribe certificate in Meghalaya?
Apply through the e-District portal ([megedistrict.gov.in](https://megedistrict.gov.in)) or the FOCUS portal with supporting documents including residential proof and community verification from the headman/nokma.

### What is MBDA and how does it help citizens?
The Meghalaya Basin Development Authority (MBDA) implements livelihood, enterprise, and natural resource management projects. Citizens can access skill training, enterprise support, and market linkages through MBDA's Enterprise Facilitation Centres across the state.
