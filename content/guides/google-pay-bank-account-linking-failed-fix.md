# Google Pay Bank Account Not Linking — How to Fix

Adding a bank account is the first step to using Google Pay for UPI payments. But many users face errors during the linking process — SIM not registered, SMS verification failed, or bank not supported. This guide covers all common bank account linking failures and how to fix them.

## Common Bank Linking Error Messages

- **"Mobile number not registered with this bank"** — your phone number is not linked to the bank account
- **"SMS verification failed"** — the verification SMS could not be sent or received
- **"SIM not registered for UPI"** — the SIM used for sending SMS does not match the bank records
- **"Bank not supported"** — the bank is not yet available on Google Pay's UPI platform
- **"Unable to fetch bank accounts"** — bank server not responding
- **"Registration failed. Try again later"** — generic UPI registration error

## Fix 1: Mobile Number Not Registered with Bank

**Why it happens:** UPI requires that your phone number registered with Google Pay matches the mobile number linked to your bank account.

**Steps to fix:**

1. Confirm which mobile number is registered with your bank — check your passbook, net banking, or call customer care
2. The SIM with that number must be in the phone you are using for Google Pay
3. If your bank has a different number, visit your bank branch and update your mobile number
4. After updating, wait 24-48 hours for bank systems to sync
5. Then retry linking the bank account in Google Pay

## Fix 2: SMS Verification Failed

**Why it happens:** Google Pay sends a verification SMS from your phone to validate your SIM. This can fail due to low balance, SMS block, or DND settings.

**Steps to fix:**

1. Ensure your SIM has at least ₹1 balance for outgoing SMS charges
2. Check if outgoing SMS is blocked — try sending a normal SMS to a friend
3. Disable DND (Do Not Disturb) — SMS from Google Pay may be filtered
   - Call your operator or SMS `STOP DND` to 1909
4. If using a dual-SIM phone, set the correct SIM as default for SMS:
   - Go to **Settings → SIM cards → Default SMS SIM** and select the bank-linked number
5. Disable any third-party SMS-blocking apps
6. Restart your phone and try again
7. If the issue persists, try linking the account using mobile data instead of Wi-Fi

## Fix 3: SIM Not Registered for UPI

**Why it happens:** The SIM card must be active, properly registered with the telecom operator, and the number must match your bank records.

**Steps to fix:**

1. Ensure the SIM is active — make a test call or send an SMS
2. Check that the SIM is not in "only data" mode — voice and SMS must be active
3. If you recently got a new SIM or ported your number, wait 24-48 hours after activation
4. Try removing the SIM, reinserting it, and restarting the phone
5. If using eSIM, check that it supports SMS sending (some eSIM configurations block outgoing SMS)
6. Contact your telecom provider to confirm the SIM is fully activated

## Fix 4: Bank Not Supported

**Why it happens:** While Google Pay supports most major banks, some smaller banks, cooperative banks, or regional rural banks may not be available.

**Steps to fix:**

1. Check the full list of supported banks in Google Pay: **Profile → Bank account → Add bank account** — browse the list
2. If your bank is not listed, it is not yet supported on Google Pay's UPI platform
3. Try using a different UPI app (PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM) — some apps support banks that Google Pay does not
4. If your bank recently merged with another bank (e.g., many banks merged with SBI or Bank of Baroda), search for the new bank name
5. Contact your bank to ask if they have enabled UPI and which apps support them

## Fix 5: Unable to Fetch Bank Accounts

**Why it happens:** Google Pay queries your bank's server to fetch linked accounts. If the bank server is down or slow, this fails.

**Steps to fix:**

1. Wait 15-30 minutes and try again — bank servers may be temporarily down
2. Ensure you have a stable internet connection
3. Try at a different time — avoid late night hours when banks run maintenance
4. Clear Google Pay app cache: **Settings → Apps → Google Pay → Storage → Clear Cache**
5. If the issue persists for more than a day, contact your bank to check if their UPI service is active

## Fix 6: Clear Cache and Reinstall Google Pay

If none of the above fixes work, try a fresh start:

1. **Clear cache:** Settings → Apps → Google Pay → Storage → Clear Cache
2. **Clear data (last resort):** Settings → Apps → Google Pay → Storage → Clear Data
   - ⚠️ This will remove all linked accounts — you will need to set them up again
3. **Update the app:** Go to Play Store/App Store and update to the latest version
4. **Reinstall:** Uninstall Google Pay, restart your phone, and reinstall from Play Store
5. After reinstalling, use the same Google account and phone number
6. Try linking your bank account again

## Step-by-Step: How to Link a Bank Account on Google Pay

For reference, here is the correct process:

1. Open Google Pay and tap your **profile icon** (top right)
2. Tap **"Bank account"** → **"Add bank account"**
3. Select your bank from the list
4. Google Pay will automatically detect accounts linked to your mobile number
5. An SMS will be sent from your phone for verification (ensure SMS permission is granted)
6. Once verified, your bank account(s) will appear
7. Select the account you want to link
8. Set your UPI PIN using your debit card details (card number, expiry date)
9. Your bank account is now linked and ready for transactions

## Important Tips

- Always ensure the mobile number in Google Pay matches the number registered with your bank
- Keep your SIM active with minimum balance for SMS verification
- After changing your mobile number at the bank, wait 48 hours before trying to link on Google Pay
- Google Pay requires SMS permission on your phone — grant it when prompted
- If you have multiple bank accounts, you can link all of them and set one as primary

## FAQs

**Q: Can I use Google Pay if my mobile number is different from my bank-registered number?**
A: No. UPI mandates that the mobile number on your phone (SIM) must match the number registered with your bank. Update your number at the bank first.

**Q: Google Pay SMS verification keeps failing on my dual-SIM phone. What should I do?**
A: Set the SIM linked to your bank account as the default SMS SIM. Go to Settings → SIM cards → Default SMS SIM and select the correct one. Then retry.

**Q: My bank recently merged with another bank. How do I link my account?**
A: Search for the new bank name in Google Pay's bank list. For example, if your bank merged with Bank of Baroda, search for "Bank of Baroda." Your old account details should still work.

**Q: I cleared Google Pay data and lost my linked accounts. How do I recover them?**
A: Your bank accounts are not deleted — they are just unlinked from the app. Simply add the bank account again using the same process and set your UPI PIN. Your transaction history in the bank remains unaffected.

**Q: Does Google Pay support all Indian banks?**
A: Google Pay supports most major nationalized banks, private banks, and many regional banks. However, some cooperative banks and small finance banks may not be supported yet. Check the bank list in the app.

**Q: I get "Registration failed" even though everything is correct. What do I do?**
A: Try at a different time (bank server may be down), clear Google Pay cache, and ensure your app is updated. If it still fails, try registering on BHIM app first — once UPI is active on your number via BHIM, Google Pay linking usually works.
