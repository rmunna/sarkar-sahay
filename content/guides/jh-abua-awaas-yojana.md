# Jharkhand Abua Awaas Yojana 2026 — Free Pucca House for Poor Families

The **Jharkhand Abua Awaas Yojana** (अबुआ आवास योजना) is a state government housing scheme that provides **₹2 lakh in financial assistance** to poor families who do not have a pucca (permanent) house. "Abua" means "our own" in Santali — this is Jharkhand's way of ensuring every family has a home of their own.

The scheme specifically targets families who were **left out of PM Awas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G)** due to technical exclusions or criteria gaps.

## Key Benefits

| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Financial Assistance** | ₹2 lakh per house |
| **Payment Mode** | Direct Bank Transfer (DBT) in installments |
| **Target** | 20 lakh pucca houses across Jharkhand |
| **Additional Support** | ₹18,000 for MGNREGA labour (where applicable) |
| **Toilet Facility** | Linked with Swachh Bharat Mission for toilet construction |

## Who is Eligible?

- **SC/ST families** without a pucca house
- **BPL (Below Poverty Line)** cardholders
- **Widows** and **persons with disability** with no pucca house
- **Small and marginal farmers** not covered under PMAY-G
- **Excluded under PMAY-G** due to Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC) data gaps
- Must be a **Jharkhand resident** with proof of local residence
- Family should **not already own a pucca house** anywhere in India

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar card (all adult family members)
- Bank account passbook (linked with Aadhaar)
- Income certificate / BPL ration card
- Land ownership document or patta (for land-owning families)
- Voter ID / domicile certificate (as Jharkhand residency proof)
- Passport-size photograph
- Caste certificate (for SC/ST applicants)
- Disability certificate (if applicable)

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Online Application at aay.jharkhand.gov.in

1. Visit **[aay.jharkhand.gov.in](https://aay.jharkhand.gov.in/)**
2. Click **"Apply Online"** or **"नया आवेदन"**
3. Register using your **Aadhaar number + mobile OTP**
4. Fill in family details, income, existing housing status
5. Upload all required documents (PDF/JPG under 2 MB each)
6. Submit and note your **Application Reference Number**
7. Track status using your reference number or Aadhaar on the same portal

### Offline Application at Block Development Office

1. Visit your nearest **Block Development Office (BDO)**
2. Ask for the Abua Awaas Yojana application form
3. Fill the form with all family and housing details
4. Submit with photocopies of all documents (keep originals for verification)
5. BDO staff will conduct a **field survey** of your current housing condition
6. Get a receipt with your application acknowledgement number

### Application Through Gram Panchayat

1. Contact your **Gram Panchayat Sevak** or **Panch/Sarpanch**
2. They will assist in filling and submitting the application
3. Panchayat-level verification happens before BDO approval

## How Payments Are Released

The ₹2 lakh assistance is released in **installments** after verification at each stage:

1. **First installment**: After application approval and foundation verification
2. **Second installment**: After lintel level (walls up to window) construction
3. **Third installment**: After roof completion

All payments go directly to your **Aadhaar-linked bank account** via DBT.

## Check Beneficiary List

To check if your name is in the Abua Awaas Yojana beneficiary list:

1. Visit **[aay.jharkhand.gov.in](https://aay.jharkhand.gov.in/)**
2. Click **"Beneficiary List"** or **"लाभार्थी सूची"**
3. Select your **District → Block → Panchayat → Village**
4. Your name, application ID and status will appear if approved

## Processing Timeline

| Stage | Duration |
|-------|----------|
| Application submission | Same day |
| Field verification by BDO | 15–30 days |
| Approval and first installment | 30–45 days from application |
| Full construction and final payment | 6–12 months |

## Important Tips

1. **Apply directly** — the process is free. Do not pay agents or middlemen claiming to get you the scheme faster
2. **Aadhaar-bank linkage is mandatory** — ensure your bank account is linked to your Aadhaar before applying
3. **Land ownership matters** — families on government land may need a patta certificate from the panchayat
4. **Scheme is separate from PMAY-G** — if you already received PMAY-G assistance, you are not eligible
5. **Keep all construction photos** — you will need to submit geo-tagged photos at each installment stage via the portal or BDO
6. **Jharkhand Tribal families** — SC/ST applicants get priority in selection. Attach caste certificate

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between Abua Awaas Yojana and PM Awas Yojana?

PM Awas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G) is a central government scheme based on SECC 2011 data — many poor families were excluded due to data errors. Abua Awaas Yojana is Jharkhand's **state scheme to cover this gap**, providing ₹2 lakh to families who were eligible but not included in PMAY-G.

### Can a widow apply without land in her name?

Yes. Widows are a priority group. If the widow occupies land that belongs to the family but isn't formally in her name, she can apply with a declaration letter from other family members and Panchayat verification.

### What happens if construction is not completed within 12 months?

The third and final installment is released only after completion. If construction is delayed, you can request an extension at the BDO office. Extensions are granted in genuine cases with supporting reasons.

### My name is not in the beneficiary list even though I applied. What to do?

Visit your BDO office with your acknowledgement receipt. If your application was rejected, the BDO will tell you the reason. Common reasons: land ownership disputes, already has a pucca house, income above limit, or already received PMAY-G. You can file a grievance at the District Collector's office if rejected unfairly.

### Is Abua Awaas Yojana available for urban Jharkhand residents?

The Abua Awaas Yojana primarily targets rural (Gramin) areas. For urban housing, Jharkhand implements PM Awas Yojana - Urban (PMAY-U). Contact your nearest Urban Local Body (Municipal Corporation/Town Council) for urban housing assistance.

### Where can I get more help?

- **BDO (Block Development Officer)**: your first point of contact
- **District Collector's Office**: for escalations and grievances
- **State Helpline**: contact Jharkhand government portal at jharkhand.gov.in
- **Grievance Portal**: jharsewa.jharkhand.gov.in
