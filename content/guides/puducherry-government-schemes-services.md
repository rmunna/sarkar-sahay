# Puducherry Government Schemes & Services — N. Rangasamy AINRC Government

Puducherry (Pondicherry) is a Union Territory governed by Chief Minister N. Rangasamy's AINRC-BJP NDA alliance, which retained power in the 2026 assembly elections — marking Rangasamy's fifth term as Chief Minister. The government runs a range of targeted welfare schemes focused on housing, education, women's welfare, fishing community support, and health.

## 1. Perunthalaivar Kamarajar Housing Scheme

Named after former CM K. Kamaraj, this is Puducherry's flagship housing programme that provides financial assistance for construction or renovation of homes for economically weaker families.

**Eligibility:**
- BPL (Below Poverty Line) family
- Puducherry resident (domicile required)
- Should not own a pucca house
- Land ownership or government-allotted plot required

**Benefit:**
- Subsidy for construction of new house or renovation of kutcha house
- Subsidy released in instalments linked to construction progress

**How to Apply:**
- Visit the District Collectorate or Social Security Department office
- Submit application with ration card, Aadhaar, land documents, and income certificate
- Official portal: [socialsecurity.py.gov.in](https://socialsecurity.py.gov.in/)

---

## 2. Rajiv Gandhi Breakfast Scheme

One of the most popular welfare initiatives in Puducherry, this scheme provides free, nutritious breakfast to students in government schools every morning before classes begin.

**Coverage:**
- All students enrolled in government-run schools in Puducherry
- Covers primary, middle, and high school levels

**Benefit:**
- Free cooked breakfast (idli, pongal, upma, or equivalent)
- Served on all school working days
- Complementary to the existing mid-day meal scheme

**No application required** — benefit is automatic for all government school students.

---

## 3. Fishing Ban Period Assistance

The Puducherry government provides financial support to fishing families during the annual fishing ban (typically June–July in the Bay of Bengal), when fishermen are prohibited from going to sea.

**Eligibility:**
- Active fishermen registered with the Fisheries Department, Puducherry
- Must possess a valid fishing licence
- Must be a Puducherry domicile

**Benefit:**
- ₹5,000 per fisherman during the fishing ban period
- Paid directly into the beneficiary's bank account

**How to Apply:**
- Register with the Fisheries Department, Puducherry
- Submit application before the fishing ban begins
- Required documents: fishing licence, Aadhaar, bank account details

## 4. Women Property Stamp Duty Rebate

To promote women's property ownership, the Puducherry government offers a 50% rebate on stamp duty when properties are registered in a woman's name.

**Applicable transactions:**
- Sale deed
- Exchange
- Gift deed
- Properties registered solely in a woman's name or jointly with a woman as primary holder

**Benefit:**
- 50% reduction in stamp duty payable at the time of property registration

**How to avail:**
- Applicable automatically at the Sub-Registrar's Office when the property is being registered in a woman's name
- No separate application — declare at the time of registration
- Bring Aadhaar and supporting documents proving the woman's identity

## 5. Differently-Abled Monthly Assistance

The Social Security Department provides monthly financial assistance to persons with disabilities in Puducherry.

**Eligibility:**
- Puducherry domicile
- Certified disability of 40% or above (disability certificate from government medical board)
- Below income threshold specified by the department

**Benefit:**
- ₹3,000 per month
- Paid via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to the beneficiary's bank account

**How to Apply:**
- Visit the Social Security Department, Puducherry
- Submit disability certificate, Aadhaar, ration card, and bank details
- Official portal: [socialsecurity.py.gov.in](https://socialsecurity.py.gov.in/)

---

## 6. Professional Course Fee Reimbursement

Students from SC/ST communities and underprivileged families enrolled in professional colleges in Puducherry can get their tuition fees reimbursed by the government.

**Eligibility:**
- SC/ST students or students from economically weaker sections
- Enrolled in government-recognised professional courses: engineering, medicine, law, nursing, pharmacy, etc.
- Domicile of Puducherry

**Benefit:**
- Full or partial tuition fee reimbursement as notified
- Processed through the Education Department

**How to Apply:**
- Apply through your college's scholarship/fee reimbursement cell
- Required documents: caste certificate, income certificate, college admission letter, fee receipt
- Official portal: [edudept.py.gov.in](https://edudept.py.gov.in/)

---

## 7. Education Cash Assistance for SC/ST Students

The Puducherry government provides direct cash assistance to Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe students at various milestones in their education.

**Benefits include:**
- Cash grants on passing 10th, 12th, and graduation
- Laptop assistance for higher education students
- Free textbooks at school level

## How to Access Puducherry Government Services

**Official Portal:** [py.gov.in](https://py.gov.in/) — the main Puducherry government gateway

**Key department offices:**
- **Social Security Department** — Pensions, disability assistance, housing
- **Education Department (edudept.py.gov.in)** — Fee reimbursement, scholarships
- **Fisheries Department** — Fishing community assistance
- **Revenue Department** — Certificates, property registration

**For all scheme applications:**
1. Collect the required documents listed above
2. Visit the relevant department office in Puducherry
3. Submit the application form with attested copies
4. Follow up using your acknowledgement receipt number

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can residents of the French quarters of Puducherry avail these schemes?**
A: Yes. All residents of the Puducherry Union Territory — including the French quarters — who hold a Puducherry domicile certificate are eligible.

**Q: Is Puducherry domicile required for all schemes?**
A: Yes. Most welfare schemes require a Puducherry domicile certificate (nativity certificate). This can be obtained from the Revenue Department.

**Q: Are these schemes separate from central government schemes?**
A: Yes. These are Puducherry UT-specific schemes, separate from central schemes like PM Kisan, Ujjwala, or Ayushman Bharat. Puducherry residents may be eligible for both.
