# Central Bank of India Account Blocked or Frozen? How to Unblock (2026)

Central Bank of India, one of the oldest public sector banks established in 1911, has 4,600+ branches nationwide. If your Central Bank of India account is blocked or frozen, here's a complete guide to unblock it.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is independent. Contact Central Bank of India directly at 1800-22-1911 for account-specific assistance.

---

## Common Reasons for Central Bank Account Block

| Reason | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| KYC not updated | Re-KYC via app or branch |
| PAN not linked to account | Link via net banking or branch |
| Dormant account (24 months no transaction) | Branch reactivation |
| Suspicious activity / regulatory hold | Call customer care + branch |
| Government order / court freeze | Legal resolution |

---

## Identify the Reason First

1. **Check SMS** from Central Bank — alert messages specify the reason
2. **Call 1800-22-1911** — ask customer care for account restriction details
3. **Login to Cent Mobile** — may show KYC update notification

---

## Unblock Central Bank Account Online

### Via Cent Mobile App

1. Open **Cent Mobile** (Central Bank of India's official app)
2. Login → **Services → My Account**
3. KYC Update prompt visible: Upload Aadhaar + PAN
4. For other restrictions: **Raise Request → Account Activation**

### Via Central Bank Net Banking

1. Login at **[centralbankofindia.co.in](https://www.centralbankofindia.co.in)**
2. My Account → Profile → KYC Documents
3. Upload Aadhaar + PAN or submit service request

---

## Call Central Bank Customer Care

**Toll-Free:** **1800-22-1911**
**Alternate:** 1800-200-1911

Say: "My Central Bank of India account ending XXXX has debit restrictions. Please tell me the reason and how to resolve it."

---

## Visit Central Bank Branch

1. Visit home branch (preferred) or any nearby branch
2. Carry: Aadhaar (original + copy), PAN, passbook, 2 passport photos
3. Fill: **Account Reactivation / Defreeze Request** form
4. Submit KYC documents + get acknowledgment slip
5. Processing time: **1–3 working days**

---

## Central Bank KYC Update

**Via Cent Mobile App:**
1. Cent Mobile → Profile → Re-KYC Update
2. Upload Aadhaar (front + back) + PAN
3. Timeline: 1–2 working days

**At Branch:**
- Fill KYC Update form
- Submit self-attested Aadhaar + PAN + photograph
- Processed same day

---

## Dormant Account Reactivation (Central Bank)

1. Visit home branch
2. Fill Dormant Account Reactivation form
3. Submit KYC documents: Aadhaar + PAN + photo
4. Do one cash deposit at counter
5. Active: 1–2 working days

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long to unblock Central Bank of India account?**
KYC-related freeze: 1–2 working days. Dormant reactivation: same day to 2 days. Regulatory holds vary.

**Can Central Bank KYC be done online without visiting branch?**
Cent Mobile app and net banking support digital KYC uploads. However, for dormant reactivation, a branch visit is typically required.

**My Central Bank ATM card stopped working — is it related to account block?**
Could be. An account debit freeze will stop ATM transactions. Call 1800-22-1911 to check whether the block is at account level or card level.

**PAN not linked to Central Bank account — how to fix?**
Visit branch with PAN card and account details. PAN linking can also be done via net banking: Profile → PAN Linking. RBI mandates PAN linking for full KYC status.
