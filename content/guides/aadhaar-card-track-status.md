# Aadhaar Card Track Status: Check Update Request Online

After submitting an Aadhaar update request — whether online or at an enrolment centre — you receive a reference number to track its progress. This guide explains how to check your Aadhaar update status using URN, SRN, or EID on the official UIDAI portal.

## Understanding Reference Numbers

When you submit an Aadhaar update or enrolment request, UIDAI provides a reference number. The type depends on how you made the request:

| Number | Full Form | When You Get It |
|--------|-----------|-----------------|
| **URN** | Update Request Number | Online update via myAadhaar portal |
| **SRN** | Service Request Number | PVC card order or other service requests |
| **EID** | Enrolment ID | New Aadhaar enrolment or centre-based update |

Keep this number safe — it is the only way to track your request.

---

## How to Check Aadhaar Update Status Online (URN)

If you submitted an update request online through the myAadhaar portal:

1. Visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/)
2. Click **Login** and enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number
3. Enter the OTP received on your registered mobile
4. After login, click **Check Update Status** from the dashboard
5. Your recent update request with URN will be displayed
6. The status will show one of:
   - **Submitted** — Request received, under processing
   - **Under Review** — Documents being verified
   - **Approved** — Update applied to your Aadhaar
   - **Rejected** — Update denied (reason will be shown)

---

## How to Check Aadhaar Status Using EID (Enrolment Centre Updates)

If you visited an Aadhaar enrolment centre for update or new enrolment:

1. Visit [uidai.gov.in](https://uidai.gov.in/)
2. Go to **My Aadhaar** → **Check Aadhaar Status**
3. Or directly visit [resident.uidai.gov.in/check-aadhaar](https://resident.uidai.gov.in/check-aadhaar)
4. Enter your 14-digit **Enrolment ID (EID)** and the date-time printed on your acknowledgement slip
5. Enter the captcha and click **Check Status**
6. The page will show whether your Aadhaar has been generated or if the update is still being processed

---

## How to Track PVC Aadhaar Card Order (SRN)

If you ordered a PVC Aadhaar card:

1. Visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/)
2. Login with Aadhaar number and OTP
3. Click **Check Aadhaar PVC Card Status**
4. Enter your **SRN** to see the current status
5. Once dispatched, you can also track using India Post tracking number

## Aadhaar Update Timeline

| Update Type | Expected Timeline |
|-------------|-------------------|
| Online demographic update (name, address, DOB) | 7–10 working days |
| Online document update | 7–10 working days |
| Centre-based biometric update | 10–15 working days |
| Centre-based demographic update | 10–15 working days |
| New Aadhaar enrolment | 15–30 working days |
| PVC card delivery | 10–15 working days after order |

**Note:** Timelines may vary depending on verification workload. During peak periods, processing may take longer.

## Common Reasons for Aadhaar Update Rejection

If your update request is rejected, it may be due to:

1. **Poor document quality** — Uploaded documents are blurry, cropped, or unreadable
2. **Document mismatch** — Name or details in the document don't match what you entered
3. **Invalid document** — The document is not in the accepted list of [UIDAI supporting documents](https://uidai.gov.in/images/commdoc/List_of_Supporting_Document_for_Aadhaar_Enrolment_and_Update.pdf)
4. **Expired document** — Some documents like passports or driving licences must be valid (not expired)
5. **Photo not clear** — For photo updates, the uploaded photo doesn't meet quality standards
6. **Duplicate request** — Another update request is already in progress for the same field
7. **Invalid proof for field** — For example, using an address proof to update name

### What to Do If Rejected:

- Read the rejection reason carefully on the status page
- Resubmit the request with better documents or corrected information
- If online update fails repeatedly, visit an [Aadhaar enrolment centre](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) for assisted update

## Important Tips

1. **Save your reference number** — Without URN/SRN/EID, you cannot track your request. Take a screenshot of the acknowledgement page.
2. **Check after 3-5 days** — Don't check immediately after submitting. Allow at least 3-5 working days for processing to begin.
3. **Download updated e-Aadhaar** — Once your update is approved, [download a fresh e-Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-card-reprint-online) to get the updated version.
4. **One update at a time** — UIDAI processes one update request per field at a time. Wait for one to complete before submitting another.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. How long does an Aadhaar update take to reflect?
Online updates typically take 7–10 working days. Centre-based updates may take 10–15 working days. New enrolments can take up to 30 days.

### Q2. Where can I find my URN or SRN?
The URN is displayed on screen and sent via SMS after submitting an online update on myAadhaar. The SRN is provided after ordering a PVC card. Check your SMS history if you've lost it.

### Q3. Can I track Aadhaar status without a mobile number?
You need the registered mobile number to login to myAadhaar. However, you can check enrolment status using EID at [resident.uidai.gov.in/check-aadhaar](https://resident.uidai.gov.in/check-aadhaar) without OTP.

### Q4. What if my Aadhaar update is stuck for more than 30 days?
Raise a grievance at [uidai.gov.in](https://uidai.gov.in/) or call the UIDAI helpline at 1947. You can also visit an Aadhaar enrolment centre for assistance.

### Q5. Can I cancel an Aadhaar update request after submission?
No. Once submitted, you cannot cancel an Aadhaar update request. You must wait for it to be processed (approved or rejected).

### Q6. Will I get an SMS when my Aadhaar update is approved?
Yes. UIDAI sends an SMS to your registered mobile number when the update is processed (whether approved or rejected).

### Q7. My update was rejected. Do I need to pay again?
For online updates, there is no fee, so resubmission is free. For centre-based updates, you may need to pay the update fee again (typically ₹50).
