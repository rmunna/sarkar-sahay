# Paytm Wallet to Bank Transfer Failed? Fix It

Transferring money from your Paytm wallet to a bank account is a common feature, but it can fail due to KYC issues, incorrect bank details, or transfer limits. This guide covers every reason why your wallet-to-bank transfer might fail and how to fix it.

## Why Does Paytm Wallet to Bank Transfer Fail?

| Reason | Quick Fix |
|---|---|
| KYC not complete | Complete Full KYC |
| Wrong bank details | Verify account number and IFSC |
| Daily/monthly limit exceeded | Wait or reduce transfer amount |
| Bank server issues | Retry after some time |
| Paytm app outdated | Update the app |

---

## Fix 1: Full KYC Not Completed

**This is the most common reason.** Wallet-to-bank transfers are only available for Full KYC users.

### How to Check Your KYC Status
1. Open Paytm → tap your **Profile** icon.
2. Go to **KYC & Account Settings**.
3. Check if it shows **"Full KYC"** or **"Minimum KYC"**.

### How to Fix
- If you have Minimum KYC, you **cannot** transfer wallet money to a bank account.
- Complete Full KYC via **Video KYC** or by visiting a **KYC center**.
- See our detailed [Paytm KYC troubleshooting guide](/guide/paytm-kyc-not-working-fix) for help.

---

## Fix 2: Wrong Bank Account Details

If you see "Transfer failed — invalid bank details" or "Beneficiary verification failed":

1. Open Paytm → **Passbook** → **Send Money to Bank**.
2. Check the **account number** and **IFSC code** entered.
3. Common mistakes:
   - Extra spaces or characters in the account number
   - Old IFSC code (banks merge branches and IFSC codes change)
   - Savings account number entered instead of current, or vice versa
4. **Verify your IFSC code** on your bank's website or passbook.
5. Delete the saved beneficiary and re-add with correct details.
6. Try a small ₹1 test transfer first to verify.

---

## Fix 3: Daily or Monthly Transfer Limit Exceeded

Paytm has limits on wallet-to-bank transfers:

| KYC Status | Per Transaction | Monthly Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum KYC | Not allowed | Not allowed |
| Full KYC | ₹25,000 | ₹25,000 |

**Note:** Limits may vary and Paytm updates them periodically. Check the app for current limits.

### How to Fix
1. Check how much you've already transferred this month: Paytm → **Passbook** → filter by "Wallet to Bank".
2. If you've hit the limit, wait for the next month.
3. For larger transfers, use UPI (bank-to-bank) directly instead of wallet.

---

## Fix 4: Transfer Processing Delay

Sometimes the transfer succeeds on Paytm's end but takes time to reach your bank:

### Normal Processing Times
| Transfer Method | Time |
|---|---|
| IMPS (most transfers) | Instant to 30 minutes |
| NEFT | 30 minutes to 2 hours (during banking hours) |
| Weekends/holidays | May take until next business day |

### What to Do
1. Check Paytm **Passbook** → find the transaction → check status.
2. If it shows **"Completed"** but money hasn't arrived, wait up to 4 hours.
3. If it shows **"Processing"**, wait up to 24 hours.
4. If money doesn't arrive after 24 hours:
   - Check your bank account statement (sometimes the credit doesn't show in the app immediately).
   - Raise a ticket: Paytm → **Help & Support** → select the transaction.
   - Call Paytm: **0120-4456-456**.

## Fix 5: Wallet to Bank Transfer Charges

Paytm may charge a fee for wallet-to-bank transfers:

| Scenario | Charges |
|---|---|
| Wallet to own bank account | Up to 5% (varies by amount and offers) |
| Cashback/promotional balance | May not be transferable to bank |

**Note:** Charges change frequently. Check the transfer screen for applicable charges before confirming.

### How to Avoid Charges
- Use wallet balance for direct payments (bills, recharges, shopping) instead of transferring to bank.
- Cashback earned on Paytm is best used within the Paytm ecosystem.
- Some promotional offers waive transfer charges — check the Paytm Offers section.

## General Troubleshooting Steps

1. ✅ **Update Paytm app** to the latest version.
2. ✅ **Clear app cache** — Phone Settings → Apps → Paytm → Clear Cache.
3. ✅ **Check internet connection** — ensure stable Wi-Fi or mobile data.
4. ✅ **Retry after some time** — bank servers or Paytm may be under maintenance.
5. ✅ **Check bank account status** — ensure the receiving account is active and not frozen.

## Important Tips

- Always double-check bank account number and IFSC before transferring.
- Complete Full KYC to unlock wallet-to-bank transfers — see our [KYC fix guide](/guide/paytm-kyc-not-working-fix).
- Keep transfer receipts and screenshots for dispute resolution.
- Paytm wallet balance is not the same as Paytm Payments Bank balance — ensure you're transferring from the right source.
- For large amounts, prefer direct UPI bank-to-bank transfers instead of wallet.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Can I transfer Paytm wallet money to any bank account?
Yes, with Full KYC you can transfer to any bank account in India using account number and IFSC code.

### Q2: Why can't I transfer money from Paytm wallet to bank without KYC?
RBI regulations require Full KYC for wallet-to-bank transfers to prevent money laundering. Complete your KYC to enable this feature.

### Q3: How much can I transfer from Paytm wallet to bank per day?
Full KYC users can transfer up to ₹25,000 per month. Limits may vary — check the Paytm app for current limits.

### Q4: Is there a charge for Paytm wallet to bank transfer?
Yes, Paytm may charge up to 5% depending on the amount. Check the transfer screen for applicable charges before confirming.

### Q5: Money was deducted from wallet but not received in bank. What to do?
Wait up to 24 hours. If still not received, raise a ticket through Paytm Help & Support or call 0120-4456-456.

### Q6: Can I transfer Paytm cashback to my bank account?
Some cashback is wallet balance and can be transferred (with KYC). Promotional or locked cashback may not be transferable — check the cashback terms.
