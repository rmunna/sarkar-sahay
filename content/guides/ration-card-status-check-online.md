# Ration Card Application Status Check: Track Online

After applying for a new ration card or requesting changes (name addition, address update, correction), you can track the application status online through your state's Food & Civil Supplies portal. This guide explains how to check your ration card status by application number, SMS, and helpline.

## How to Check Ration Card Application Status Online

### General Steps (Most States)

1. Visit your state's Food & Civil Supplies Department website.
2. Look for **"Application Status"**, **"Track Application"**, or **"Status Check"** option.
3. Enter your **application number** or **acknowledgment number** received at the time of applying.
4. You may also search by **Aadhaar number**, **mobile number**, or **ration card number** (for modification requests).
5. Click **"Search"** or **"Track"**.
6. The current status will be displayed — Submitted, Under Verification, Approved, or Rejected.

## Check Status via SMS

Some states allow status checks via SMS:

- **Uttar Pradesh:** SMS `STATUS <Application Number>` to the state helpline.
- **Bihar:** SMS `RC <Application Number>` to the designated number.
- **Other states:** Check your state portal for SMS-based tracking options.

> SMS-based tracking availability varies by state. Not all states support this feature.

## Check Status via Helpline

If the online portal is not working or you cannot track your application, call these helplines:

| Helpline | Number |
|----------|--------|
| National Food Security Helpline | **14445** |
| State-level helplines | Check your state portal for the toll-free number |
| Common Service Centre (CSC) | Visit nearest CSC for in-person assistance |

You can also file a grievance on [pgportal.gov.in](https://pgportal.gov.in) if your application has been pending beyond the expected timeline.

## Ration Card Application Stages

Understanding the typical approval flow helps you interpret your status:

| Stage | Description | Typical Duration |
|-------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Submitted** | Application received by the department | Day 0 |
| **Under Verification** | Field officer verifies documents and address | 7-15 days |
| **Field Inspection** | Physical verification at your address | 15-30 days |
| **Approved by AO** | Area Officer approves the application | 30-45 days |
| **Card Issued** | Ration card generated and dispatched | 45-60 days |
| **Rejected** | Application rejected (reason will be mentioned) | Varies |

> Processing times vary significantly by state. Some states process applications within 15-30 days, while others may take up to 60-90 days.

## What to Do if Application is Rejected

1. Check the **reason for rejection** on the status page.
2. Common reasons: duplicate application, incomplete documents, ineligibility, already existing ration card.
3. **Correct the issue** and re-apply with proper documents.
4. If you believe the rejection is wrong, file a **grievance** through the state portal or visit the Taluk/Block Supply Office.
5. You can also approach the [District Grievance Redressal Officer (DGRO)](https://nfsa.gov.in) for escalation.

## What to Do if Application is Stuck

If your application has been pending for more than 60 days:

1. Visit the **Taluk/Block Supply Office** with your application receipt.
2. Call the **state food helpline** or national helpline **14445**.
3. File a grievance on [pgportal.gov.in](https://pgportal.gov.in) or through the CM Helpline of your state.
4. In some states, you can escalate through [UMANG app](https://web.umang.gov.in).

## Important Tips

1. **Save your application number** — you will need it for all status checks and follow-ups.
2. Keep the **SMS acknowledgment** safe as proof of application submission.
3. Check status weekly — don't visit the office unless the application has been pending beyond 30 days.
4. Ensure your [Aadhaar details](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) match exactly with the ration card application to avoid verification failures.
5. If you applied through a CSC, the centre can also help you track the status.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does it take for a ration card to be approved?
Typically 30-60 days depending on the state. Some states like Delhi and Tamil Nadu process applications faster (15-30 days).

### Can I check status without an application number?
Some states allow tracking via Aadhaar number or mobile number. Check your state portal for available search options.

### What does "Under Verification" mean?
It means the food inspector or field officer is verifying your documents and may conduct a physical visit to your address.

### Is there a way to speed up the process?
You cannot speed up the process, but ensuring all documents are complete and correct at the time of application prevents delays due to re-verification.

### Can I apply again if my application is rejected?
Yes. Review the rejection reason, correct the issue, and submit a fresh application with complete documents.

### What is the national food helpline number?
The national toll-free number for food security grievances is **14445**. It operates across all states.
