# Odisha Ration Card Apply Online 2026

The Odisha ration card is issued by the **Department of Food Supplies and Consumer Welfare, Odisha**. The state manages the **AHARA** (Annapurna Hariyali Abhiyana Rural Area) portal at [ahara.odisha.gov.in](https://ahara.odisha.gov.in) for ration card services. Odisha provides **10 kg rice per Ration Unit (RU)** monthly under the state scheme.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [ahara.odisha.gov.in](https://ahara.odisha.gov.in) or nearest Common Service Centre (CSC). Odisha provides 10 kg rice/RU per month to PHH card holders.

---

## Ration Card Types in Odisha

| Type | Category | Entitlement |
|------|----------|-------------|
| **Priority Household (PHH)** | BPL families under NFSA | 5 kg rice/person/month @ ₹1/kg (NFSA) + 5 kg @ ₹1 (state top-up) = 10 kg total |
| **Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY)** | Destitute/poorest families | 35 kg rice/family/month @ ₹1/kg |
| **SFSS (State Food Security Scheme)** | State-identified eligible families | Subsidised rice at state rates |

> Odisha's 10 kg/RU scheme (5 kg NFSA + 5 kg state) makes it one of the best ration entitlements in India.

---

## Eligibility

**PHH/BPL card:**
- Annual family income below ₹2 lakh (rural) or ₹2.5 lakh (urban)
- Not a government employee receiving HRA or government accommodation
- Not an income taxpayer
- Not a vehicle owner (4-wheeler)
- Aadhaar seeded in the family database

**AAY card:**
- Landless agricultural labourers
- Marginal farmers with very low income
- Widow, disabled, or elderly living alone with no income
- Bonded labourers

**SFSS:**
- Families above the NFSA threshold but identified by the state as needing food support

---

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar cards of all family members (mandatory)
- Residence proof (electricity bill, water bill, rent agreement)
- Passport-size photographs (head of family + members)
- Income certificate from Tehsildar (for BPL classification)
- Caste certificate (if SC/ST)
- Birth certificates for children below 5
- Death certificate (if applying after member deletion)

## How to Apply Online

**Method 1: AHARA Portal**

1. Go to [ahara.odisha.gov.in](https://ahara.odisha.gov.in)
2. Click **"Citizen Services"** → **"Apply for Ration Card"**
3. Select your district, block/ULB, GP/Ward
4. Enter all family member details and Aadhaar numbers
5. Upload supporting documents
6. Submit — save the acknowledgement number

**Method 2: e-District Portal**

1. Go to [edistrict.odisha.gov.in](https://edistrict.odisha.gov.in)
2. Log in or register as a citizen
3. Search for "Ration Card" services
4. Fill and submit the application form

**Method 3: Block Office / Tahasil / CSC**

1. Visit Block Supply Office (BSO) for rural areas, or Urban Local Body (ULB) office for urban areas
2. Submit filled application form with documents
3. Block Supply Officer verifies and forwards to District Supply Officer (DSO)
4. DSO approves and ration card is issued

## Status Check

1. Go to [ahara.odisha.gov.in](https://ahara.odisha.gov.in)
2. Click **"Citizen Services"** → **"Application Status"**
3. Enter your acknowledgement number or Aadhaar number
4. View current status

**Offline:** Contact Block Supply Office or District Supply Office.

---

## Processing Time

- Field verification by Block Supply Officer: 7–15 days
- District level approval: 15–30 days
- Total: approximately **30–45 days** from application

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many ration units (RU) does a family get?

Each household member counts as 1 Ration Unit (RU). A family of 4 gets 40 kg rice/month under PHH.

### Can I check Odisha ration card status by Aadhaar?

Yes — at ahara.odisha.gov.in, you can search ration card by Aadhaar number.

### My ration shop is not giving ration. What do I do?

Call the Odisha Food helpline: **1967** (toll-free). You can also complain at the BSO office.

### Is One Nation One Ration Card available in Odisha?

Yes — Odisha is part of ONORC. You can use your Odisha ration card at any FPS in India via the Mera Ration app.

### Helpline

**1967** — Odisha Food Department toll-free. Also accessible via IVRS.
