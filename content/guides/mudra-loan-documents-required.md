# Mudra Loan Documents Required: Complete Checklist for PMMY Application

One of the most common reasons for [Mudra loan rejection](/guide/mudra-loan-rejected-fix) is incomplete documentation. This guide provides the complete documents checklist for all three [Mudra loan categories](/guide/mudra-loan-types-shishu-kishore-tarun) — Shishu, Kishore, and Tarun — so you can apply with confidence.

---

## Documents Common to All Mudra Loan Types

These documents are required regardless of which category you're applying for:

### 1. Identity Proof (Any One)

- ✅ **Aadhaar card** (most preferred)
- Voter ID card
- PAN card
- Driving licence
- Passport

### 2. Address Proof (Any One)

- ✅ **Aadhaar card** (most preferred — serves as both ID and address proof)
- Voter ID card
- Utility bill (electricity/water — not older than 3 months)
- Passport
- Rent agreement with utility bill

### 3. PAN Card

- **Mandatory** for all Mudra loans
- If you don't have PAN, apply at [incometax.gov.in](https://www.incometax.gov.in/)

### 4. Passport-Size Photographs

- 2 recent passport-size photographs
- White background preferred

### 5. Filled Application Form

- Available at the bank branch or downloadable from the bank's website
- Online applications through [Udyami Mitra](https://udyamimitra.in/) or [Jan Samarth](https://www.jansamarth.in/) don't require a physical form

## Documents by Mudra Loan Category

### Shishu Loan Documents (Up to ₹50,000)

Shishu has the **lightest documentation** requirement:

| Document | Required? |
|----------|-----------|
| Aadhaar card | ✅ Yes |
| PAN card | ✅ Yes |
| Passport photos (2) | ✅ Yes |
| Business proof (any one) | ✅ Yes |
| Bank statements | ❌ Not always required |
| Business plan | ❌ Not required |
| ITR | ❌ Not required |
| Quotations | ❌ Not required |

**Business proof for Shishu** — any one of these:
- Shop and Establishment licence
- Municipal trade licence
- [Udyam Registration](/guide/msme-udyam-registration) certificate
- GST registration certificate
- A self-declaration letter about your business activity (accepted by some banks for very small loans)

---

### Kishore Loan Documents (₹50,001 to ₹5,00,000)

| Document | Required? |
|----------|-----------|
| Aadhaar card | ✅ Yes |
| PAN card | ✅ Yes |
| Passport photos (2) | ✅ Yes |
| Business registration proof | ✅ Yes |
| Bank statements (6 months) | ✅ Yes |
| Business plan | ✅ Yes |
| Quotations for assets | ✅ If buying equipment |
| ITR (last 1 year) | Recommended |
| Balance sheet & P&L | Recommended |

**Additional documents that strengthen your application:**
- GST returns (if registered)
- [Udyam Registration](/guide/msme-udyam-registration) certificate
- Existing business contracts or purchase orders
- Proof of business premises (rent agreement or ownership)

### Tarun Loan Documents (₹5,00,001 to ₹10,00,000)

| Document | Required? |
|----------|-----------|
| Aadhaar card | ✅ Yes |
| PAN card | ✅ Yes |
| Passport photos (2) | ✅ Yes |
| Business registration proof | ✅ Yes |
| Bank statements (12 months) | ✅ Yes |
| Detailed business plan | ✅ Yes |
| ITR (last 2 years) | ✅ Yes |
| Balance sheet & P&L (2 years) | ✅ Yes |
| Quotations for assets | ✅ Yes (if buying) |
| Proof of business premises | ✅ Yes |

**Additional documents often required:**
- CA-certified financial statements
- GST registration and returns
- [Udyam Registration](/guide/msme-udyam-registration) certificate
- Any existing loan sanction letters or NOCs
- Partnership deed (if partnership firm)
- MOA/AOA (if private limited company)
- [Startup India Registration](/guide/startup-india-registration) certificate (if applicable)

## Business Registration Documents Accepted

Banks accept any of the following as proof of business:

| Document | Where to Get It |
|----------|----------------|
| **Udyam Registration** | [udyam.gov.in](/guide/msme-udyam-registration) — free, online |
| **GST Registration** | gst.gov.in |
| **Shop and Establishment Licence** | Local municipal corporation |
| **Trade Licence / Gumasta** | Local municipality |
| **FSSAI Licence** | fssai.gov.in (food businesses) |
| **IEC Certificate** | dgft.gov.in (import/export) |
| **Professional Licence** | Relevant professional body |

> 💡 **Tip:** [Udyam Registration](/guide/msme-udyam-registration) is the easiest to get — it's completely free and done online in minutes. Every Mudra loan applicant should have it.

## How to Prepare a Business Plan

For Kishore and Tarun loans, your business plan should include:

### Basic Structure (2–3 Pages)

1. **Business Overview** — What does your business do? When did it start?
2. **Products/Services** — What do you sell or offer?
3. **Market** — Who are your customers? What is the demand?
4. **Use of Funds** — Exactly how will the loan amount be used? (Be specific — e.g., "₹2 lakh for sewing machines, ₹1 lakh for raw materials")
5. **Revenue Projections** — Expected monthly/annual revenue for next 1–2 years
6. **Repayment Plan** — How will you repay the EMI from business income?

### Tips for a Strong Business Plan

- Be **specific and realistic** — don't overestimate revenue
- Show **existing revenue** if your business is running
- Include **quotations** for any equipment you plan to buy
- Mention any **existing customers or contracts**
- Keep it **simple** — banks don't expect MBA-level plans

## Document Submission Tips

1. **Self-attest all photocopies** — sign and write "self-attested" on each copy
2. **Carry originals** for verification — banks will check and return them
3. **Organise documents** in a file folder — creates a professional impression
4. **Keep extra copies** — banks may ask for multiple sets
5. **Ensure documents are current** — expired licences or old utility bills will be rejected
6. **Bank statements should be stamped** — get them from the bank with a seal, not just printed from net banking

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need Udyam Registration for Mudra loan?

It's not mandatory, but **highly recommended**. Udyam Registration is free and strengthens your application significantly. Many banks prefer applicants with Udyam certification. Get it at [udyam.gov.in](/guide/msme-udyam-registration).

### Can I apply for Mudra loan without GST registration?

Yes. GST registration is required only if your turnover exceeds the GST threshold (currently ₹40 lakh for goods, ₹20 lakh for services). For Shishu loans, GST is rarely required.

### What if I don't have a business plan for Kishore loan?

Some banks may still process Kishore loans without a formal business plan, especially for well-known business types (shops, small manufacturing). However, having a plan significantly improves your chances. Even a one-page summary helps.

### Can I use Aadhaar as both identity and address proof?

Yes. Aadhaar is accepted as both identity proof and address proof by all banks for Mudra loans.

### Do I need ITR to apply for Shishu loan?

No. ITR is not required for Shishu loans (up to ₹50,000). For Kishore it's recommended, and for Tarun it's usually mandatory.

### What documents are needed if I'm applying as a partnership firm?

In addition to the standard documents, you'll need: Partnership deed, PAN of the firm, all partners' KYC documents, and a resolution letter authorising the applicant partner.

### Can I submit documents online or do I need to visit the bank?

If you apply through Udyami Mitra or Jan Samarth, you can upload documents online. However, the bank may still ask you to visit the branch with originals for verification before final approval.
