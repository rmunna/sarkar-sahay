## What is Odisha Bhulekh?

Bhulekh Odisha is the official online land records portal of the Government of Odisha. Managed by the Revenue & Disaster Management Department, it allows citizens to access Record of Rights (RoR), plot details, khatiyan records, mouza maps, and tenant information — all digitally without visiting the Tehsildar office.

The word "Bhulekh" means "land record" (Bhu = land, Lekh = record). The portal covers all 30 districts of Odisha.

## Key Services on Bhulekh Odisha

- **RoR (Record of Rights)** — View ownership details, plot area, and classification
- **Plot Details** — Search by plot/khata number for specific land information
- **Khatiyan Search** — View land records by khatiyan number
- **Mouza Map View** — Digital cadastral maps showing plot boundaries
- **Tenant Details** — View tenant and occupancy information
- **Distribution Details** — Check how land has been distributed

## Who Can Use Bhulekh Odisha?

- Any citizen of India
- Land owners verifying their property details
- Prospective buyers for due diligence
- Farmers checking agricultural land records
- Banks for loan processing and verification
- Revenue officials and legal professionals

No registration is required for basic searches on Bhulekh Odisha.

## How to Check Land Records on Bhulekh Odisha — Step by Step

### Method 1: View RoR (Record of Rights)

1. Visit the official portal at [bhulekh.ori.nic.in](https://bhulekh.ori.nic.in)
2. Click on **"Know Your Land"** or **"RoR"** from the home page
3. Select your **District** from the dropdown
4. Select your **Tehsil/Tahasil**
5. Select your **RI Circle** (Revenue Inspector Circle)
6. Select the **Village/Mouza**
7. Choose search type:
   - **By Khatiyan** — Enter khatiyan number
   - **By Plot** — Enter plot number
   - **By Tenant Name** — Enter owner/tenant name
8. Enter the **CAPTCHA** code
9. Click **"RoR"** or **"Show"** to view the record

The RoR will display:
- Khatiyan number and plot number
- Owner/tenant name(s)
- Plot area (in acres and decimals)
- Land classification (Kissam) — agricultural, homestead, etc.
- Encumbrance and mutation details

### Method 2: Search by Plot Number

1. Go to [bhulekh.ori.nic.in](https://bhulekh.ori.nic.in)
2. Navigate to **"Know Your Land"**
3. Select **District → Tehsil → RI Circle → Village**
4. Select **"Plot"** as the search type
5. Enter the **Plot Number**
6. Enter CAPTCHA and click **"Show"**

### Method 3: View Mouza Map

1. Visit [bhulekh.ori.nic.in](https://bhulekh.ori.nic.in)
2. Click on **"Map View"** from the navigation menu
3. Select **District → Tehsil → RI Circle → Village**
4. The digital mouza map will be displayed with plot boundaries
5. Click on any plot to view its details including owner name and area
6. Use zoom controls to navigate the map

### Method 4: Khatiyan Search

1. Go to [bhulekh.ori.nic.in](https://bhulekh.ori.nic.in)
2. Navigate to **"Know Your Land"**
3. Select the location hierarchy (District → Tehsil → RI Circle → Village)
4. Choose **"Khatiyan"** as search type
5. Enter the **Khatiyan Number**
6. View all plots and tenants under that khatiyan

## Understanding Odisha Land Record Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Khatiyan | Record book containing ownership details of one or more plots |
| Kissam | Land classification (e.g., Sarad = irrigated, Aal = homestead) |
| RoR | Record of Rights — the primary land ownership document |
| Mouza | Revenue village — smallest administrative unit for land records |
| Hal Patta | Current possession record |
| Sabik Patta | Previous possession record |
| Tehsil/Tahasil | Sub-district revenue unit |

## Important Tips

1. **Know your RI Circle** — Odisha uses RI Circle in the search hierarchy, which is different from other states; check with your local revenue office if unsure
2. **Use khatiyan number for complete records** — Searching by khatiyan gives all plots under one ownership, while plot search shows individual plot details
3. **Verify kissam before purchase** — Land classification determines what the land can be used for; agricultural land has restrictions on non-agricultural use
4. **Map view helps verify boundaries** — Use the mouza map to confirm plot boundaries before any land transaction
5. **Check both sabik and hal patta** — Compare previous and current records to understand ownership history

## FAQs

### 1. Is Bhulekh Odisha a government website?
Yes, Bhulekh (bhulekh.ori.nic.in) is the official land records portal of the Government of Odisha, maintained by the Revenue & Disaster Management Department under NIC.

### 2. Can I download RoR from Bhulekh Odisha?
You can view and print the RoR from the portal for informational purposes. For legally valid certified copies, visit your Tehsil or RI office.

### 3. What is the difference between khatiyan and RoR?
Khatiyan is the record book number under which one or more plots are grouped. RoR (Record of Rights) is the actual document showing ownership and plot details. Khatiyan is used to organize RoR entries.

### 4. How do I apply for mutation in Odisha?
Submit a mutation application at your Tehsil office with the registered sale deed, previous RoR, identity proof, and application form. Some services are also available through the Odisha eSeva portal.

### 5. Does Bhulekh show all districts of Odisha?
Yes, Bhulekh Odisha covers all 30 districts of the state. Select your district from the dropdown to start searching.

### 6. What is Kissam in Odisha land records?
Kissam refers to the classification of land — such as Sarad (wet/irrigated), Aat (dry), Aal (homestead), Jungle, Abada Jogya (cultivable waste), etc. It determines how the land can be used.

### 7. Can I check land records using owner name on Bhulekh?
Yes, Bhulekh allows searching by tenant/owner name. However, searching by plot or khatiyan number gives more accurate results.
