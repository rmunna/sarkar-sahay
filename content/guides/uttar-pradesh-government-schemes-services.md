# Uttar Pradesh Government Schemes and Services – Complete Guide

Uttar Pradesh, India's most populous state, runs a massive network of welfare schemes benefiting crores of families. From Kanya Sumangala Yojana for girl children to Shadi Anudan for marriage assistance, the state provides comprehensive support in education, health, housing, agriculture, and social security. This guide covers all major UP government schemes with eligibility, benefits, and how to apply.

## Key Government Portals in UP

- **e-District UP** – [edistrict.up.gov.in](https://edistrict.up.gov.in/) – Certificates, pension, and government services
- **UP Bhulekh** – [upbhulekh.gov.in](https://upbhulekh.gov.in/) – Land records, khatauni, khasra. See: [UP Bhulekh Land Records](/guide/up-bhulekh-land-records)
- **e-Sathi UP** – [esathi.up.gov.in](https://esathi.up.gov.in/) – Caste, income, domicile certificates online
- **Nivesh Mitra** – Single window for business-related approvals
- **UP Scholarship Portal** – [scholarship.up.gov.in](https://scholarship.up.gov.in/) – All scholarship applications
- **UP Family ID** – [familyid.up.gov.in](https://familyid.up.gov.in/) – Family identity registration
- **SSPY Pension Portal** – [sspy-up.gov.in](https://sspy-up.gov.in/) – Old age, widow, and disability pension
- **Abhyudaya Portal** – [abhyuday.up.gov.in](https://abhyuday.up.gov.in/) – Free competitive exam coaching

## Women and Girl Child Schemes

### Kanya Sumangala Yojana

Flagship scheme providing ₹25,000 in six instalments from birth to graduation for girl children from families with income below ₹3 lakh per year. The amount was increased from ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 effective from 2024-25.

**Instalment structure (revised 2024-25):**
- ₹5,000 at birth
- ₹2,000 at age 1 (vaccination completion)
- ₹3,000 on admission to class 1
- ₹3,000 on admission to class 6
- ₹5,000 on admission to class 9
- ₹7,000 on graduation/diploma admission

Read the complete guide: [Kanya Sumangala Yojana UP](/guide/kanya-sumangala-yojana-up)

### Shadi Anudan Yojana (Marriage Grant)

Financial assistance of ₹20,000 for marriage of daughters from SC, ST, OBC, and minority families with annual income below ₹46,080 (rural) or ₹56,460 (urban).

Read the complete guide: [Shadi Anudan Yojana UP](/guide/shadi-anudan-yojana-up)

## UP Family ID (Ek Parivar Ek Pahchan)

The UP Family ID scheme assigns a unique 12-digit identity number to every family in the state. Families with ration cards are automatically covered, while others can register at [familyid.up.gov.in](https://familyid.up.gov.in/). As of 2025, approximately 3.6 crore families are covered. The Family ID is being linked to all government welfare schemes for targeted benefit delivery.

## Education Schemes

### UP Scholarship (Pre-Matric and Post-Matric)

Comprehensive scholarship programme for students from SC, ST, OBC, minority, and general (EWS) categories covering tuition fees, maintenance allowance, and hostel charges.

### Mukhyamantri Abhyudaya Yojana (Free Coaching)

Free coaching for competitive exams including UPSC, NEET, IIT-JEE, NDA, and CDS for students from economically weaker sections. Coaching is provided at divisional headquarters and district levels. Registration is available at [abhyuday.up.gov.in](https://abhyuday.up.gov.in/).

### Free Laptop/Tablet Yojana

Distribution of free tablets and smartphones to students who have passed class 12 from UP Board or equivalent.

Read the complete guide: [UP Free Laptop Tablet Yojana](/guide/up-free-laptop-tablet-yojana)

## Social Security and Pension

### UP Pension Yojana

UP runs three major pension schemes through the SSPY portal:

- **Vridhavastha Pension** (Old Age) – ₹500-1,000/month for seniors 60+
- **Nirasrit Mahila Pension** (Widow) – ₹500-1,000/month for widows
- **Divyang Pension** (Disability) – ₹500-1,000/month for persons with 40%+ disability

Read the complete guide: [UP Pension Yojana – Old Age, Widow, Disabled](/guide/up-pension-yojana-old-age-widow-disabled)

## Housing Schemes

### Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana in UP

Both urban and rural components active in UP:
- **PMAY-Gramin** – ₹1.20 lakh for plain areas, ₹1.30 lakh for hilly areas
- **PMAY-Urban** – Interest subsidy of 3-6.5% on home loans

### Mukhyamantri Awas Yojana

State-level housing scheme for urban and rural poor in UP.

## How to Apply for UP Government Schemes

### Online Application

1. **Visit e-District UP** – [edistrict.up.gov.in](https://edistrict.up.gov.in/) or respective scheme portal
2. **Register** – Create account with Aadhaar/mobile
3. **Select service** – Choose the scheme or certificate
4. **Fill application** – Enter personal and eligibility details
5. **Upload documents** – Aadhaar, income proof, caste certificate, bank details
6. **Submit and track** – Use application number for status

### Offline Application

1. Visit **Tehsil office**, **Block Development Office**, or **CSC centre**
2. Carry original documents and photocopies
3. Submit application form with documents
4. Collect acknowledgment receipt

### Common Documents Required

- Aadhaar card
- Ration card
- Income certificate
- Caste certificate (for reserved category schemes)
- Bank passbook
- Domicile certificate
- Passport-size photographs

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I check UP scheme status online?**
Visit e-District or the specific scheme portal and enter your application/registration number.

**What is the income limit for UP welfare schemes?**
Varies by scheme – Kanya Sumangala requires below ₹3 lakh, pension schemes have different BPL criteria.

**Can I apply for UP schemes from another state?**
No, you need a UP domicile certificate and residence proof to apply for state-specific schemes.
