## What is AICTE Pragati Scholarship?

**AICTE Pragati Scholarship** is a scholarship scheme by the **All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE)** to encourage **girl students** to pursue higher technical education. It provides **₹50,000 per year** (₹30,000 academic expenses + ₹20,000 incidental charges) to eligible girl students studying in AICTE-approved institutions.

The scheme is open to **5,000 girl students per year** — 4,000 for degree-level (B.Tech, B.Arch, B.Pharm, MBA, MCA) and 1,000 for diploma-level programmes.

Key features:
- **₹50,000 per year** (tuition + incidentals)
- For **girls studying in AICTE-approved technical institutions**
- Income limit: **₹8 lakh/year** (relatively high — covers middle-income families)
- No restriction by caste/category — open to all girls
- Renewable annually based on performance

## Who is Eligible?

- Must be a **girl student**
- Must be admitted to **1st year of AICTE-approved Degree or Diploma technical programme**
- Annual family income: **below ₹8 lakh**
- Maximum **2 girls per family** can avail this scholarship
- Must not be availing any other government scholarship (single scholarship rule applies)

**Courses covered:**
- Degree: B.Tech, B.E., B.Arch, B.Pharm, MBA, MCA, and other AICTE-approved degree programmes
- Diploma: Diploma in Engineering/Technology at AICTE-approved polytechnics

## Scholarship Amount

| Component | Amount per Year |
|---|---|
| Academic expenses (tuition, lab, etc.) | ₹30,000 |
| Incidentals (books, stationery, laptop, etc.) | ₹20,000 |
| **Total** | **₹50,000** |

Paid directly to student's bank account via DBT.

## How to Apply — Step by Step

1. Go to **[scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in)** (National Scholarship Portal)
2. Click **"New Registration"**
3. After registration, log in → click **"Apply for Scholarship"**
4. Select **"Central Schemes"** → **"AICTE — Pragati Scholarship for Girl Students (Degree/Diploma)"**
5. Fill the application form:
   - Personal details
   - Institution and course details (AICTE institution code)
   - Family income details
   - Bank account details
6. Upload required documents
7. Submit → note Application ID

> **Apply immediately after admission** — the application window is typically August–October each year.

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card** (mandatory)
2. **Income certificate** — annual family income below ₹8 lakh (from competent authority)
3. **Admission proof / bonafide certificate** from AICTE-approved institution
4. **Previous year marksheet** — Class 12 (for degree) or Class 10 (for diploma) for fresh applicants
5. **Current year fee receipt** from institution
6. **Bank account passbook** (girl student's own account — Aadhaar-linked)
7. Passport-size photograph

## Renewal Process

Pragati Scholarship must be renewed each year:
1. Log in to [scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in)
2. Submit current year marksheet showing you have passed (no backlog)
3. Updated fee receipt and income certificate
4. Renewal application submitted before deadline (typically October–November)

**Performance requirement:** No more than one backlog subject in the previous year.

## AICTE Saksham Scholarship (For Differently-Abled Students)

AICTE also runs **Saksham Scholarship** for **differently-abled students** (male and female) in AICTE-approved institutions — same ₹50,000/year. If you are a girl with disability, you can apply for Pragati (check if both are allowed simultaneously in the current year's notification).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is this scholarship for all states or specific states?
AICTE Pragati is a **central scheme** — available to girl students across all states studying in any AICTE-approved institution in India.

### I am in 2nd year. Can I apply fresh?
Pragati is primarily for **1st year** students (fresh admission). Continuing students apply via renewal. If you missed applying in 1st year, check the current year's notification — sometimes 2nd year fresh applications are accepted if seats are unfilled.

### What if my institution is not AICTE-approved?
You are not eligible. Verify your institution's AICTE approval status at [aicte-india.org](https://www.aicte-india.org) → Institution section.

### Can a girl from a general/open category apply?
Yes — Pragati has **no caste restriction**. It is solely based on being female and income below ₹8 lakh.

### I already have a state government scholarship. Can I also get Pragati?
You cannot receive **two scholarships for the same purpose from different sources**. If your state scholarship covers tuition, you may not be eligible for Pragati. Check the "single scholarship" rule in the current year's notification.

### My income is ₹8.5 lakh. Am I disqualified?
Yes — income above ₹8 lakh/year (family annual income) disqualifies you. Pragati's ₹8L ceiling is generous and covers most middle-class families.
