# Karnataka Caste Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **caste certificate** (ಜಾತಿ ಪ್ರಮಾಣ ಪತ್ರ — Jaati Praman Patra) in Karnataka is issued by the Revenue Department (Tahsildar) and is required for Karnataka-specific OBC categories (2A, 3A, 3B), SC/ST reservations, KCET reserved category counselling, government job applications (KPSC), and post-matric scholarships. Applications go through **Seva Sindhu** — Karnataka's unified e-services platform — or the dedicated **Nadakacheri** portal.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in) or [nadakacheri.karnataka.gov.in](https://nadakacheri.karnataka.gov.in). Issued by Tahsildar within **15–30 days**.

---

## Karnataka's Unique OBC Classification System

Karnataka uses a **four-category OBC system** that is different from the central OBC classification:

| Category | Reservation % | Key Communities |
|----------|---------------|-----------------|
| **Category I** | 4% | Scheduled Tribes converted, Hakkipikki, Koraga, etc. |
| **Category IIA (2A)** | 15% | Vokkaliga, Gowda, Lingayat, Edigas, Curumbas, etc. |
| **Category IIB (2B)** | 3% | Muslim OBC communities |
| **Category IIIA (3A)** | 4% | Veerashaiva/Lingayat sub-castes (specific communities) |
| **Category IIIB (3B)** | 5% | Brahmin sub-castes (specific communities) |
| **SC** | 15% | Scheduled Castes |
| **ST** | 3% | Scheduled Tribes |

> Karnataka's OBC system is unique — **Vokkaligas and Lingayats** are politically dominant OBC communities. They fall under 2A and 3A respectively. These categories provide state reservation but are **not covered** by the central OBC list for central government benefits.

## Why You Need Karnataka Caste Certificate

- **KCET (Karnataka CET)** — reserved category admissions in engineering/pharmacy/medical
- **COMEDK / Karnataka NEET state counselling** — community certificate required
- **Karnataka government scholarships** — SC/ST/OBC post-matric
- **Government job reservation** — KPSC, KSSB, district recruitment
- **Rajiv Gandhi Scholarship** (SC/ST) — caste proof required
- **Central OBC NCL** — for UPSC, SSC, railways (if your community is in central OBC list)

---

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory)
- **Ration card** (shows family caste/community)
- **Father's school leaving certificate (TC)** — caste listed in school records
- **Father's / grandfather's caste proof** (old voter ID with caste, service record)
- Self-declaration of caste
- **Voter ID** (address proof)
- Passport-size photograph

## How to Apply Online — Seva Sindhu Portal

1. Go to [sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in)
2. Click **"Services Available"** → search **"Caste Certificate"**
3. Register / log in with mobile number
4. Select **"Revenue Department — Caste Certificate"**
5. Fill form:
   - Applicant name, address (district, taluk, hobli, village)
   - Community/category declaration (Category I / 2A / 2B / 3A / 3B / SC / ST)
   - Father's community details
6. Upload documents (Aadhaar, father's TC, ration card)
7. Pay fee: **₹25–50** (online)
8. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via Nadakacheri / Atalji Jana Snehi Kendra

**Nadakacheri** is Karnataka's dedicated revenue services portal:
1. Go to [nadakacheri.karnataka.gov.in](https://nadakacheri.karnataka.gov.in) for online application
2. Or visit the nearest **Atalji Jana Snehi Kendra** (citizen service kiosk — at hobli and taluk level)
3. Operator fills and submits the application
4. Service charge at kiosk: approximately ₹30–60

---

## Apply at Taluk Office / Nadakacheri Counter

1. Visit your **Taluk office** (Tahsildar's office)
2. Apply at the **Nadakacheri counter** within the taluk office
3. Submit form with documents to Revenue Inspector
4. Revenue Inspector verifies at village level
5. Tahsildar issues caste certificate after approval

---

## Status Check & Download

1. Log in to [sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in) → **"My Applications"**
2. Or go to [nadakacheri.karnataka.gov.in](https://nadakacheri.karnataka.gov.in) → **"Application Status"**
3. Enter Application Reference Number
4. Once approved: download digitally signed PDF

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 2A category in Karnataka — am I OBC at the central level too?

**Not automatically.** Karnataka's 2A (Vokkaliga, Gowda, etc.) and 3A (Lingayat communities) have state reservation. However, most of these communities are **not in the central OBC list**. For UPSC/SSC/railways, check your community at [ncbc.nic.in](https://ncbc.nic.in). If not listed, you do not get central OBC reservation even with a Karnataka OBC certificate.

### Vokkaliga caste certificate — which category in Karnataka?

**Vokkaliga** and allied communities (Gowda, Morasu Vokkaliga, etc.) are categorized under **Category IIA (2A)** in Karnataka. Apply as "Category IIA" in the caste certificate form. The Karnataka government has been pursuing inclusion of Vokkaligas in central OBC list — check current status before applying for central OBC NCL.

### Lingayat caste certificate — which category?

**Lingayat / Veerashaiva Lingayat** communities are in **Category IIIA (3A)** in Karnataka. Like 2A, most Lingayat sub-communities are not in the central OBC list. Apply for Karnataka 3A certificate for state purposes.

### KCET caste certificate — same as income certificate?

No — **caste certificate** proves community (OBC/SC/ST category). **Income certificate** proves annual family income. For KCET reserved category: you need **caste certificate + income certificate** (income < ₹2 lakh/year for most OBC scholarship schemes). Both applied separately on Seva Sindhu.
