# HSRP Number Plate Apply Online: Book High Security Plate

High Security Registration Plates (HSRP) are mandatory for all vehicles in India as per a Supreme Court directive. HSRP plates are tamper-proof, have a unique identification number, and use a hot-stamped chromium hologram for security. This guide explains how to book your HSRP online, the fees, fitment process, and color coding rules.

## What is HSRP?

HSRP (High Security Registration Plate) is a standardized, tamper-proof vehicle number plate mandated by the Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989 (Rule 50). Key features include:

- **Chromium-based hologram** — hot-stamped, cannot be removed without damage
- **Unique laser-etched PIN** — a 10-digit permanent identification number
- **Snap-lock fitting** — tamper-evident, non-reusable locking mechanism
- **Retro-reflective sheeting** — for better visibility at night
- **IND inscription** — with the Ashoka Chakra emblem

### Who Needs HSRP?

- **All vehicles** registered in India — both old and new
- New vehicles are fitted with HSRP at the time of registration by the dealer
- **Old vehicles** (registered before HSRP became mandatory) must retrofit HSRP plates
- The Supreme Court has made HSRP mandatory for all vehicles; deadlines vary by state

## How to Apply for HSRP Online

### Step 1: Visit the Booking Portal

1. Go to your state's HSRP booking portal. Common portals:
   - [bookmyhsrp.com](https://bookmyhsrp.com/) — for states covered by this vendor
   - Some states have their own portals (check your state transport website)
2. Select your state and vehicle type

### Step 2: Enter Vehicle Details

1. Enter your **vehicle registration number**
2. Enter your **chassis number** (last 5 digits)
3. Enter your **engine number** (last 5 digits)
4. Verify details against your RC

### Step 3: Verify and Confirm

1. The system will fetch your vehicle details from the Vahan database
2. Verify that all details are correct — owner name, vehicle model, registration date
3. Enter your mobile number and email for communication

### Step 4: Pay the Fee

| Vehicle Type | Approximate Fee |
|-------------|----------------|
| Two-wheeler (single plate) | ₹400–₹450 |
| Two-wheeler (both plates) | ₹600–₹700 |
| Four-wheeler / car (both plates) | ₹900–₹1,100 |
| Commercial vehicles | ₹900–₹1,100 |
| Third number plate (for tractors, trailers) | ₹200–₹300 |

> Fees include the plate cost and fitment charges. Exact fee depends on your state and vendor.

Payment modes: Debit card, credit card, net banking, UPI

### Step 5: Book Fitment Appointment

1. After payment, select a **fitment centre** near you
2. Choose a **date and time slot** for plate installation
3. Download the appointment confirmation
4. You will receive an SMS/email with details

### Step 6: Visit the Fitment Centre

1. Go to the selected centre on the appointed date and time
2. Carry:
   - Original RC (Registration Certificate)
   - Booking confirmation/receipt
   - Old number plates (will be replaced)
   - Valid ID proof
3. The technician will:
   - Remove old plates
   - Fix the new HSRP plates with snap-lock fittings
   - Affix the hologram
   - Record the HSRP unique number
4. The process takes about **15–30 minutes**

## Documents Required

- **Registration Certificate (RC)** — original
- **HSRP booking receipt** — printed or digital
- **Identity proof** — [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online), driving licence, or voter ID
- **Old number plates** — to be surrendered at the fitment centre
- **Insurance certificate** (some centres may ask)

## Processing Time

- **Online booking to fitment:** Depends on slot availability — typically 3–15 days
- **Fitment process:** 15–30 minutes at the centre
- **Plate manufacturing:** If plates need to be made, allow 3–7 days after booking

## Important Tips

1. **Don't use fancy plates** — Using non-standard, stylized, or modified number plates is illegal. Only HSRP plates are valid
2. **Check deadlines** — States issue periodic deadlines with fines for non-compliance. Get your HSRP before the cutoff
3. **Verify details carefully** — Ensure your vehicle details on the HSRP match your RC exactly. Errors are difficult to fix later
4. **Keep the receipt** — The HSRP booking receipt and unique PIN number should be kept safe for future reference
5. **Penalty for non-compliance** — Driving without HSRP can attract a fine of up to ₹5,000–₹10,000 depending on the state

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Is HSRP mandatory for all vehicles?

Yes, the Supreme Court of India has mandated HSRP for all vehicles — old and new. New vehicles are fitted with HSRP at the time of registration. Old vehicles must retrofit HSRP plates.

### Q2: Can I install HSRP myself?

No. HSRP plates must be installed only at authorized fitment centres using snap-lock fittings. Self-installation is not permitted.

### Q3: What happens if my HSRP plate is damaged?

You must apply for a replacement through the same HSRP booking portal. You cannot use the vehicle with a damaged or illegible number plate.

### Q4: Is the HSRP fee one-time?

Yes, you pay only once. However, if the plate is damaged or you need a replacement, you will need to pay again.

### Q5: Can I keep my old number plate after HSRP fitment?

Policies vary by state. Some fitment centres retain old plates, while others return them to the owner. Check with your local centre.

### Q6: How do I find the nearest HSRP fitment centre?

The booking portal shows available fitment centres based on your location and RTO. You can also check your state transport department's website.

### Q7: What is the penalty for not having HSRP?

Under the Motor Vehicles Act, driving without a valid number plate can attract a fine of ₹5,000 for the first offence and ₹10,000 for subsequent offences.
