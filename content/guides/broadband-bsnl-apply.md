# BSNL Broadband Connection — Apply Online, Plans & Process

BSNL (Bharat Sanchar Nigam Limited) is India's government-owned telecom company that offers broadband internet across the country. Whether you want a basic DSL connection or high-speed **Bharat Fiber (FTTH)**, BSNL provides affordable internet plans especially in areas where private ISPs don't reach.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent informational website. We are not affiliated with BSNL or any government body. Always verify plans and pricing on the official BSNL website.

---

## What is BSNL Broadband?

BSNL offers two types of broadband connections:

1. **BSNL DSL Broadband** — Uses your existing telephone line. Speeds up to 24 Mbps. Being phased out in many areas.
2. **BSNL Bharat Fiber (FTTH)** — Fiber-to-the-home connection with speeds from 30 Mbps to 300 Mbps. This is BSNL's flagship broadband product.

Bharat Fiber is available in most cities and towns where BSNL has laid fiber infrastructure. It offers unlimited data with many plans.

---

## Eligibility

- Any Indian resident with a valid **address proof** can apply.
- **BSNL Bharat Fiber** requires fiber availability at your location — check on the BSNL portal.
- **Existing BSNL landline customers** can upgrade to broadband easily.
- No age restriction, but the applicant must be **18 years or older**.
- Both **residential and commercial** connections are available.

---

## Documents Needed

- **Identity proof:** Aadhaar card, PAN card, Voter ID, or Passport
- **Address proof:** Aadhaar, electricity bill, ration card, or rent agreement
- **Passport-size photograph** (1–2 copies)
- **Cancelled cheque or bank details** (for auto-debit plans)

For Aadhaar-based eKYC, you may not need physical documents — just your Aadhaar number and OTP.

## Step-by-Step: Apply for BSNL Broadband Online

### Online Application (Bharat Fiber / FTTH)

1. Visit [booking.bsnl.co.in](https://booking.bsnl.co.in) or [selfcare.bsnl.co.in](https://selfcare.bsnl.co.in).
2. Click **"Book New Connection"** or **"Apply for Bharat Fiber"**.
3. Enter your **area / pincode** to check fiber availability.
4. If available, fill the application form:
   - Name, address, contact details
   - Select plan (see plans below)
   - Upload ID proof and address proof
5. Choose **payment method** for the installation fee.
6. Pay the **installation / security deposit** online.
7. You will receive a **booking reference number** via SMS and email.
8. A BSNL engineer will contact you within **3–7 days** for installation.

### Online Application via BSNL Portal

1. Visit [bsnl.co.in](https://www.bsnl.co.in).
2. Navigate to **Broadband** → **Apply Online**.
3. Follow the same steps as above.

---

## Offline Application

1. Visit your nearest **BSNL Customer Service Centre (CSC)** or exchange office.
2. Carry your **documents** (ID proof, address proof, photos).
3. Fill the application form available at the counter.
4. Pay the installation charges.
5. Collect the acknowledgment slip.
6. Installation will be scheduled within **5–15 days**.

## Processing / Installation Time

- **Urban areas:** **3–7 working days** from application.
- **Semi-urban / rural areas:** **7–15 working days** depending on fiber availability and manpower.
- **Existing landline customers:** Faster installation, usually **2–5 days**.

You can track your application status at [selfcare.bsnl.co.in](https://selfcare.bsnl.co.in) using your booking reference number.

## Tips and Best Practices

1. **Check fiber availability first** before applying — enter your pincode on the BSNL portal.
2. **Choose FTTH over DSL** — Bharat Fiber is faster, more reliable, and similarly priced.
3. **Use your own router** if the BSNL-provided one is basic — any Wi-Fi 6 router works.
4. **Pay bills on time** to avoid disconnection. Set up auto-debit for convenience.
5. **Monitor your usage** via the BSNL Selfcare portal — check remaining data and speed.
6. **Escalate delays** — if installation takes more than 15 days, file a complaint at [pgportal.gov.in](https://pgportal.gov.in) or call BSNL customer care at **1800-345-1500**.
7. **Compare with private ISPs** before deciding — see our guide on [Jio Fiber vs Airtel Xstream](/guide/jio-fiber-airtel-broadband).
8. **BSNL bill payment** can be done online via the selfcare portal, UPI apps, or at CSCs. See our [electricity bill payment guide](/guide/electricity-bill-payment-online) for similar online payment tips.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Is BSNL Bharat Fiber available in my area?

Check on [booking.bsnl.co.in](https://booking.bsnl.co.in) by entering your pincode. If fiber is not yet available, you can register your interest and BSNL will notify you when it's ready.

### 2. Do I need a BSNL landline for broadband?

For **Bharat Fiber (FTTH)**, a landline comes free with the plan — you don't need an existing one. For older **DSL broadband**, yes, a landline is mandatory.

### 3. Can I use my own Wi-Fi router with BSNL?

Yes. BSNL provides an ONT (Optical Network Terminal) for FTTH. You can connect your own router to the ONT for better Wi-Fi coverage.

### 4. How do I pay my BSNL broadband bill online?

Visit [selfcare.bsnl.co.in](https://selfcare.bsnl.co.in), log in with your account, and pay via UPI, card, or net banking. You can also pay through Paytm, PhonePe, or Google Pay.

### 5. Can I change my BSNL broadband plan?

Yes. Log in to the Selfcare portal and go to **"Change Plan"**. The new plan takes effect from the next billing cycle. No charges for plan migration.

### 6. What if BSNL broadband speed is slow?

First, test your speed at [speedtest.net](https://www.speedtest.net). If consistently below your plan speed, call BSNL customer care (1800-345-1500) or lodge a complaint on the portal. Check your router placement and cables too.

### 7. Can I transfer my BSNL connection to a new address?

Yes. Apply for a **shift request** at your BSNL office or via the Selfcare portal. A nominal shifting charge (₹250–₹500) applies, and fiber must be available at the new location.
