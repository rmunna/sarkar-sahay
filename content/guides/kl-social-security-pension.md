# Kerala Social Security Pension – ₹1,600/Month

## What is the Kerala Social Security Pension?

The Kerala Social Security Pension scheme provides **₹1,600 per month** to eligible citizens including **senior citizens (old age), widows, differently-abled persons, and unmarried women above 50 years**. It is one of India's most generous state pension schemes, managed by the Local Self Government Department (LSGD) through Panchayats and Municipalities.

## Types of Pensions Under This Scheme

| Pension Type | Beneficiary |
|-------------|-------------|
| Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension | Persons aged 60+ from BPL families |
| Widow Pension | Widows aged 18+ |
| Disability Pension | Persons with 40%+ disability |
| Unmarried Women Pension | Unmarried women aged 50+ |
| Agricultural Workers Pension | Agricultural labourers aged 60+ |

**All categories receive ₹1,600/month.**

## Who is Eligible?

### Old Age Pension
1. Age **60 years or above**
2. Annual family income below **₹1 lakh**
3. Resident of Kerala

### Widow Pension
1. Must be a **widow** (husband deceased)
2. Age **18 years or above**
3. Annual income below **₹1 lakh**
4. Must not have remarried

### Disability Pension
1. Must have **40% or more disability** (certified by Medical Board)
2. Annual income below **₹1 lakh**
3. Any age

### Unmarried Women Pension
1. **Unmarried women** aged **50 years or above**
2. Annual income below **₹1 lakh**

## Documents Required

- **Age Proof** (Aadhaar, SSLC certificate, birth certificate)
- **Aadhaar Card**
- **Ration Card**
- **Income Certificate** (from Village Officer)
- **Bank Account Passbook** (linked to Aadhaar)
- **Death Certificate of Husband** (for widow pension)
- **Disability Certificate** from Medical Board (for disability pension)
- **Passport Size Photo**
- **Residence Certificate** (from Village Officer, if required)

## How to Apply

### Online Application

1. **Visit** [welfarepension.lsgkerala.gov.in](https://welfarepension.lsgkerala.gov.in)
2. **Click** on "New Application" or "Apply Online"
3. **Select** the pension type (old age, widow, disability, etc.)
4. **Enter** Aadhaar number and verify with OTP
5. **Fill** personal details — name, age, address, income
6. **Upload** required documents
7. **Enter** bank account details
8. **Submit** the application
9. **Note** the application number

### Offline Application

1. **Visit** your local **Grama Panchayat, Municipality, or Corporation** office
2. **Collect** the pension application form
3. **Fill** the form with required details
4. **Attach** copies of all documents
5. **Submit** to the Secretary of the Local Self Government body
6. **Collect** acknowledgment receipt

## Verification & Approval

1. The LSG body verifies the application and documents
2. A verification report is prepared by the local ward member/committee
3. The Panchayat/Municipality approves the pension
4. Pension is sanctioned and payment starts

## Processing Time

- **Verification:** 30-60 days
- **Approval:** 1-3 months
- **First Payment:** Within 3 months of approval
- **Ongoing:** Monthly credit to bank account

## Important Tips

1. **Apply at Local Body:** Your Panchayat/Municipality is the primary application point. Start there.
2. **Life Certificate Annually:** Submit Jeevan Pramaan/life certificate every year (usually in November).
3. **Update Bank Details:** If you change your bank account, immediately update at the Panchayat office.
4. **Check Payment Status:** Visit [welfarepension.lsgkerala.gov.in](https://welfarepension.lsgkerala.gov.in) to check payment status online.
5. **Disability Certificate Renewal:** If your disability certificate has an expiry date, renew it before it lapses.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Can I get the pension if I already receive a retirement pension?
If you receive a government retirement pension, you are generally **not eligible** for the social security pension.

### Q2: Is the ₹1,600 amount the same for all categories?
**Yes.** All pension categories (old age, widow, disability, unmarried women) receive ₹1,600/month.

### Q3: How do I submit the annual life certificate?
Visit your Panchayat office in person, or use the **Jeevan Pramaan app** for digital submission.

### Q4: Can NRIs apply for Kerala pension?
**No.** You must be a **resident of Kerala** living in the state to be eligible.

### Q5: What if my application is rejected?
You can appeal to the higher authority (Block Panchayat or District Collector) within the specified timeframe.

### Q6: Is there a deadline to apply?
Applications are accepted **year-round**. There is no specific deadline, but processing may take longer during peak periods.
