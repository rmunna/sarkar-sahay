# UP Caste Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **caste certificate** (जाति प्रमाण पत्र — Jaati Praman Patra) in Uttar Pradesh is issued by the Revenue Department (Sub-Divisional Magistrate / Tehsildar) and is required for OBC/SC/ST reservations, UP scholarship (scholarships.up.gov.in), NEET/CPAT state counselling, government job applications (UPPSC, UPSSSC), and scheme eligibility. Applications go through **UP e-District / eSathi portal**.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [edistrict.up.gov.in](https://edistrict.up.gov.in) or [esathi.up.gov.in](https://esathi.up.gov.in). Issued by SDM/Tehsildar within **15–30 days**.

---

## Caste Categories in Uttar Pradesh

| Category | Reservation % | Notes |
|----------|---------------|-------|
| **SC** | 21% | Scheduled Castes — UP state list |
| **ST** | 2% | Scheduled Tribes |
| **OBC** | 27% | Other Backward Classes (UP OBC list — 79 communities) |
| **EWS** | 10% | General category, income < ₹8 lakh/year |

> UP has a substantial OBC population. Notable OBC communities include Yadav, Kurmi, Lodhi, Saini, Prajapati (Kumhar), Nishad, Mallah, and many others. Check the UP OBC list for your community.

## Why You Need UP Caste Certificate

- **UP Scholarship (scholarships.up.gov.in)** — OBC/SC/ST pre-matric and post-matric
- **NEET UP state quota counselling** — caste certificate mandatory
- **UPPSC / UPSSSC / UP Police** — government job reservation
- **Central OBC NCL certificate** — for UPSC, SSC, central railways
- **PMAY / housing scheme eligibility** — caste-based prioritization
- **Kanya Sumangala Yojana** eligibility verification

---

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory)
- **Ration card** (shows family composition and caste)
- **Father's / grandfather's caste proof** (school TC with caste, old ration card, voter ID with caste)
- Self-declaration of caste (online or on ₹10 stamp paper)
- **Voter ID** (address proof)
- Passport-size photograph

## How to Apply Online — eSathi / UP e-District

1. Go to [edistrict.up.gov.in](https://edistrict.up.gov.in) or [esathi.up.gov.in](https://esathi.up.gov.in)
2. Click **"New User? Register"** → register with mobile number
3. Log in → click **"Revenue Court Certificate"** → **"Caste Certificate"** (जाति प्रमाण पत्र)
4. Fill form:
   - Applicant name, address (district, tehsil, village)
   - Caste/community declaration
   - Father's details and community
5. Upload documents (Aadhaar, ration card, father's caste proof)
6. Pay fee: **₹15–30** (online)
7. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via Jan Seva Kendra / CSC

1. Visit nearest **Jan Seva Kendra** (Common Service Centre — CSC) or Lok Mitra Kendra
2. Carry Aadhaar, ration card, father's caste proof
3. Operator submits on eSathi / e-District portal
4. Service charge: approximately ₹30–60
5. Collect acknowledgement receipt

---

## Apply at Tehsil (Offline)

1. Visit your **Tehsil** office
2. Obtain caste certificate application form
3. Submit with documents to Lekhpal (revenue inspector)
4. Lekhpal verifies at village level → forwards to Naib Tehsildar → SDM
5. Collect certificate from Tehsil / SDM office after approval

---

## Status Check

1. Go to [edistrict.up.gov.in](https://edistrict.up.gov.in)
2. Click **"Application Status"** → enter Application Reference Number
3. Or log in → **"My Applications"** → view status
4. Status flow: Submitted → Lekhpal Verification → Naib Tehsildar → SDM → Approved

---

## Download UP Caste Certificate

Once approved:
1. Log in to eSathi / e-District UP → **"My Applications"**
2. Click approved caste certificate
3. Download digitally signed PDF
4. Valid for UP scholarship portal (scholarships.up.gov.in), UPPSC, and NSP

## Processing Time

| Stage | Duration |
|-------|----------|
| Lekhpal / Revenue Inspector verification | 7–15 days |
| Naib Tehsildar / SDM approval | 10–20 days |
| Total | **15–30 days** |

> **UP RTPS:** UP's Right to Public Service Act mandates caste certificate delivery within **30 days**. If delayed, file a complaint at the first appellate authority.

## OBC NCL (Non-Creamy Layer) Certificate — UP

For **central government OBC reservation** (UPSC, SSC, IIT, NIT admissions):
- Apply for **OBC Non-Creamy Layer Certificate** at UP e-District (separate service)
- Income < ₹8 lakh/year (all non-agricultural sources)
- Issued by SDM / Collector
- Valid for **1 year**

> UP OBC certificate alone is **not sufficient** for central government OBC reservation — you need the OBC NCL certificate issued specifically for central purposes.

---

## UP Scholarship — Caste Certificate Requirements

For **UP Scholarship (scholarships.up.gov.in)**:
- Upload caste certificate directly on the scholarship portal
- Must be **digitally signed** from e-District / eSathi
- Renewed every year for annual scholarship renewal
- OBC: income < ₹2 lakh/year (post-matric class 9-10); income < specified limit for other levels

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which communities are OBC in UP?

UP has 79 communities in its OBC list (Schedule 2 of UP OBC Act). Major communities include: Yadav, Kurmi, Lodhi (Lodha), Saini, Prajapati, Nishad/Mallah, Bind, Teli, Kewat, Rajbhar, Baghel, and many more. Check the UP government OBC list or ask at your Tehsil office.

### UP caste certificate for NEET — is it enough for state quota?

For **NEET UP state quota** (DGME UP counselling), you need the UP caste certificate. Additionally, for OBC category, an **OBC NCL certificate** (income < ₹8 lakh) is required. Submit both to the DGME counselling portal.

### My community is in central OBC list but not in UP OBC list — what happens?

For **UP state government jobs**: only UP OBC list applies — central OBC listing alone does not give state OBC benefits. For **central government jobs**: central OBC NCL applies regardless of state OBC status. Apply for the relevant certificate based on the job type.
