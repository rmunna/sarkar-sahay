# Passport Application Rejected? How to Resubmit Correctly

Your passport application was rejected at the Passport Seva Kendra (PSK) or after processing. Don't worry — you can fix the issues and reapply. This guide covers the most common rejection reasons and how to resubmit correctly.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent platform and is not affiliated with the Ministry of External Affairs.

## Common Passport Rejection Reasons

### Document-Related Rejections

| Reason | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Name mismatch | Name on application differs from supporting documents |
| Address proof insufficient | Expired utility bill, missing address details |
| DOB proof not accepted | Document type not in approved list |
| Blurry/illegible documents | Photocopies too dark or unclear |
| Self-attested missing | Documents not self-signed |
| Old photographs | Photos not meeting current specifications |
| Missing documents | Required document not submitted |

### Application-Related Rejections

| Reason | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Form filled incorrectly | Wrong details, spelling errors, incomplete fields |
| Wrong application type | Applied for fresh instead of re-issue or vice versa |
| Fee payment issue | Wrong fee amount or payment failed |
| Duplicate application | Multiple applications for the same person |
| ECR/ECNR category wrong | Emigration category selected incorrectly |
| Annexure missing | Required annexure (E, F, H, etc.) not attached |

### Verification-Related Rejections

| Reason | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Adverse police verification | Police report flagged issues |
| Criminal record | Pending cases or convictions |
| Address not verified | Couldn't verify your residential address |
| Impersonation suspected | Identity could not be confirmed |

## How to Check Rejection Reason

1. Visit [passportindia.gov.in](https://www.passportindia.gov.in)
2. **Track Application Status** → Enter file number and DOB
3. The status page shows the **reason for rejection/return**
4. You may also receive an **SMS and email** with the rejection reason
5. If reason is unclear, **call RPO helpline:** 1800-258-1800

## How to Reapply After Rejection

### Step 1: Fix the Issue

Based on the rejection reason, prepare the correct documents:

**For name mismatch:**
- Ensure name is identical across all documents
- If names differ, get an affidavit or gazette notification for name change
- Use the name exactly as on your Aadhaar/birth certificate

**For address proof issues:**
- Use a recent utility bill (less than 3 months old)
- Aadhaar card with current address
- Bank statement with current address
- If rented: rent agreement + Annexure F from landlord

**For DOB proof:**
- Birth certificate (preferred)
- 10th/SSLC marksheet
- PAN card
- Aadhaar card

**For photo issues:**
- Get fresh passport-size photos (2x2 inches)
- White background, 80% face coverage
- No glasses, no smile, ears visible

### Step 2: Fill a New Application

1. Visit [passportindia.gov.in](https://www.passportindia.gov.in)
2. **Register/Login** to your account
3. Click **"Apply for Fresh Passport/Re-issue"**
4. Select the correct application type:
   - **Fresh:** First-time applicant or previous passport expired 3+ years ago
   - **Re-issue:** Renewal, name change, address change, damaged passport
5. Fill all details carefully — **double-check spelling and dates**
6. Upload documents if applying online
7. **Pay the fee** online

### Step 3: Book PSK Appointment

1. After submitting the application, book an appointment at your nearest PSK
2. Choose a date and time slot
3. Print the **appointment confirmation**
4. **Arrive 15 minutes early** with all original documents

### Step 4: Visit PSK

**Documents to carry:**
- Printed application form (signed)
- Appointment confirmation
- Original documents + self-attested photocopies
- Previous passport (if re-issue)
- Photographs (if required)
- Any additional documents that caused the earlier rejection

## Specific Rejection Fixes

### Rejected Due to ECR/ECNR Error

ECR (Emigration Check Required) vs ECNR (Emigration Check Not Required):

**You qualify for ECNR if you have:**
- 10th pass certificate or higher education
- Income tax assessed
- Professional degree
- Government servant
- Age 50+ or under 18

If you selected wrong category, reapply with the correct one and attach the qualifying document.

### Rejected Due to Missing Annexure

Common annexures required:

| Annexure | When Required |
|----------|--------------|
| Annexure A | Minor's passport — signed by both parents |
| Annexure C | Minor with single parent/guardian |
| Annexure D | Government employee — NOC from employer |
| Annexure E | Urgent/Tatkal — verification certificate |
| Annexure F | Proof of present address — landlord's declaration |
| Annexure H | Prior intimation letter (duplicate passport) |
| Annexure I | Identity certificate from employer |

Download annexures from [passportindia.gov.in](https://www.passportindia.gov.in) → Downloads.

### Rejected Due to Adverse Police Verification

If police verification came back adverse:

1. **Find out the specific reason** — contact RPO
2. **If address couldn't be verified:** Provide additional address proof and request re-verification
3. **If criminal case exists:** Provide court documents showing case status. Passports can be issued even with pending cases (except certain crimes)
4. **If impersonation suspected:** Visit RPO in person with original documents for personal identification
5. You can **appeal** to the Chief Passport Officer

## Fee for Reapplication

You need to **pay the full fee again** for a new application:

| Application Type | Normal | Tatkal |
|-----------------|--------|--------|
| Fresh (36 pages) | ₹1,500 | ₹3,500 |
| Fresh (60 pages) | ₹2,000 | ₹4,000 |
| Re-issue (36 pages) | ₹1,500 | ₹3,500 |
| Re-issue (60 pages) | ₹2,000 | ₹4,000 |
| Minor (under 18) | ₹1,000 | ₹3,000 |

**Note:** The fee from the rejected application is generally **not refunded**.

## Tips to Avoid Rejection

1. **Double-check all details** before submitting — name spelling, DOB, address
2. **Use same name** across all documents
3. **Self-attest all photocopies** — sign "Self-Attested" on each page
4. **Carry extra photographs** to the PSK
5. **Bring originals + photocopies** of every document
6. **Address proof must be recent** — utility bills less than 3 months old
7. **Fill the form in CAPITAL LETTERS** — reduces confusion
8. **Read the document checklist** on the passport portal before your appointment
9. **Arrive early** at PSK — rushing leads to mistakes

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I reapply after passport rejection?
Yes, you can reapply immediately after fixing the issue that caused rejection. You need to submit a fresh application and pay the fee again.

### Will I get refund for rejected passport application?
Generally no. The fee paid for the rejected application is not refunded. You'll need to pay the full fee again when reapplying.

### How many times can I apply for passport after rejection?
There's no limit on the number of times you can reapply. Fix the rejection reason and submit a new application each time.

### Why was my passport rejected at PSK?
Common reasons include document mismatch (name/DOB), insufficient address proof, wrong application type, missing annexures, or photo specification issues.

### Can I go to a different PSK for reapplication?
Yes, you can book an appointment at any PSK in India for your new application. You're not restricted to the same PSK.

### How long does reapplication take?
The reapplication process takes the same time as a fresh application — typically 15-30 days for normal and 1-3 days for Tatkal, plus police verification time.

### What if my passport is rejected due to criminal case?
Having a pending criminal case doesn't automatically disqualify you. Provide court documents and case status to the RPO. Passports can be issued with limited validity or after review.

### Can I appeal passport rejection?
Yes, you can appeal to the Chief Passport Officer (CPO) at your Regional Passport Office. Submit a written appeal with supporting documents explaining why the rejection should be reconsidered.
