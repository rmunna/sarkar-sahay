## What is Ghar Ghar Rozgar Yojana?

**Ghar Ghar Rozgar (Rozgar Aur Karobar Mission) — PGRKAM** is Punjab government's employment and livelihoods portal that connects **unemployed youth** to private sector jobs, government scheme benefits, and skill training opportunities. The portal aggregates private job openings in Punjab and facilitates job fairs.

---

## Key Features

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Job registration | Free registration for job seekers |
| Private jobs | Companies post vacancies; job seekers apply directly |
| Job fairs | Mega job melas organized district-wise (free entry) |
| Skill training | Links to government-funded skill programmes |
| Rojgar guarantee | Self-employment scheme access |
| Career guidance | Online counselling and aptitude assessment |

---

## Who Can Register?

- **Punjab resident** youth aged 18–35 years
- Unemployed or under-employed
- Any education level — 8th pass to post-graduate
- Both men and women

No income criteria. Registration is free.

---

## How to Register on PGRKAM Portal

### Step 1 — Visit Portal

Go to **[pgrkam.com](https://www.pgrkam.com)**

### Step 2 — Create Job Seeker Account

- Click "Register as Job Seeker"
- Enter Aadhaar number, mobile, email
- OTP verification
- Fill education and skill details
- Upload photo and resume (optional)

### Step 3 — Browse and Apply for Jobs

After registration:
- Browse current job openings (filter by district, qualification, salary)
- Apply directly on portal
- Employer contacts shortlisted candidates

### Step 4 — Attend Job Fairs

The portal announces district-level **Mega Job Fairs**:
- Register on portal to get entry pass
- Carry documents (Aadhaar, education certificates, resume copies)
- Multiple companies interview candidates on-site

## Self-Employment — Rozgar Loan Schemes

PGRKAM also facilitates access to:
- **Punjab Scheduled Caste Land Development & Finance Corp** — loans for SC individuals
- **Punjab State Women Development Corp** — loans for women entrepreneurs
- **MSME loan schemes** — business loans through DIC Punjab

These are linked from the PGRKAM portal under "Self Employment" section.

## Skill Training Opportunities

Through PGRKAM, you can access:
- **Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVYA)** — free short-term skill training
- **Punjab Skill Development Mission** — state-funded training
- Training in: electrician, beauty, tailoring, computer, mobile repair, and more

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. I am a Class 12 pass 22-year-old from Ludhiana. What jobs can I find on PGRKAM?**

Many manufacturing, retail, and service sector jobs are available for Class 12 candidates. Register on pgrkam.com and filter by "Ludhiana + minimum qualification 12th." Also check upcoming job fairs in Ludhiana district.

**2. Is registration on PGRKAM really free?**

Yes. Job seeker registration and job application through PGRKAM is completely free. Do not pay any agent who charges you for registration.

**3. How often are job fairs held?**

Job fairs are organized approximately once a month in different districts. Check the "Events" section on pgrkam.com or follow their social media for announcements.

**4. I applied for jobs on PGRKAM 6 months ago but got no calls. What should I do?**

Update your profile with latest skills and ensure your mobile number is active. Also attend a job fair in person — on-the-spot interviews are more effective than online applications. Consider skill training to improve employability.

**5. I want to start a business, not a job. Can PGRKAM help?**

Yes. The "Self Employment" section links to loan schemes. You can also contact the District Industries Centre (DIC) in your district directly for MSME and startup loan guidance.
