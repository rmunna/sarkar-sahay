## What is Tatkal Aadhaar Enrollment?

While there is no officially branded "Tatkal" Aadhaar service like passport tatkal, UIDAI provides several expedited enrollment options for individuals who urgently need an Aadhaar card. The standard Aadhaar enrollment process takes 15-90 days, but through the methods described in this guide, you can get your Aadhaar number much faster.

UIDAI has introduced measures to ensure that Aadhaar enrollment is accessible and timely, especially for those who need it urgently for government benefits, bank accounts, or SIM cards.

## Fastest Ways to Get Aadhaar

### Method 1: Aadhaar Seva Kendra (ASK)

UIDAI has set up permanent **Aadhaar Seva Kendras** in major cities that offer walk-in and appointment-based enrollment.

**Process:**
1. Visit [appointments.uidai.gov.in](https://appointments.uidai.gov.in)
2. Book an appointment at the nearest **Aadhaar Seva Kendra**
3. Select "New Enrollment" and choose a date/time
4. Visit the ASK with original documents
5. Complete biometric capture (fingerprints, iris, photograph)
6. Receive acknowledgment slip with **Enrollment ID (EID)**
7. Aadhaar number typically generated within **5-15 days**

**Advantages:**
- Government-run centres with better infrastructure
- Faster processing compared to private enrollment centres
- Walk-in slots available in many centres
- Open 6 days a week

### Method 2: Head Post Office Enrollment

India Post has partnered with UIDAI to provide Aadhaar enrollment at **head post offices** across India.

**Process:**
1. Visit your nearest **head post office** that offers Aadhaar services
2. Carry your documents (identity proof, address proof, date of birth proof)
3. Fill in the enrollment form
4. Biometric capture done at the post office
5. Collect the acknowledgment slip

**Advantages:**
- Available in smaller towns where ASKs may not exist
- Trusted government infrastructure
- Can also update existing Aadhaar here

### Method 3: Bank Branch Enrollment

Several banks have set up Aadhaar enrollment centres at their branches.

**Participating banks include:**
- State Bank of India (SBI)
- Bank of Baroda
- Punjab National Bank
- Various regional rural banks

**Process:**
1. Visit a bank branch with Aadhaar enrollment facility
2. Carry documents and complete enrollment
3. Useful if you're opening a bank account simultaneously

### Method 4: CSC (Common Service Centre)

CSC centres across India offer Aadhaar enrollment services.

1. Find nearest CSC at [locator.csccloud.in](https://locator.csccloud.in)
2. Visit with documents for enrollment
3. See our [CSC guide](/guide/common-service-centre-guide) for details

## Documents Required

### For New Enrollment

You need **one document each** for:

**Proof of Identity (POI) — Any one:**
- Passport
- PAN card
- Voter ID (EPIC)
- Driving License
- Government photo ID card
- NREGS Job Card

**Proof of Address (POA) — Any one:**
- Passport
- Voter ID
- Driving License
- Utility bill (electricity, water, gas — not older than 3 months)
- Bank statement / Passbook
- Ration card
- Property tax receipt

**Proof of Date of Birth (POB) — Any one:**
- Birth certificate
- Passport
- 10th class marksheet
- PAN card
- Driving License

**For children below 5 years:**
- Birth certificate
- Parent's Aadhaar card (mandatory)
- No biometrics captured for children under 5

## How to Get Aadhaar Number Before Physical Card

### e-Aadhaar (Digital Aadhaar)
Once your Aadhaar is generated, you can download it immediately:

1. Visit [eaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://eaadhaar.uidai.gov.in)
2. Enter your **Enrollment ID (EID)** or **Aadhaar number**
3. Verify through OTP sent to your registered mobile
4. Download e-Aadhaar (PDF)
5. **e-Aadhaar is legally equivalent** to the physical card

### Aadhaar Number via SMS/Email
- Once generated, UIDAI sends your 12-digit Aadhaar number via SMS and email
- This typically happens **5-15 days** after enrollment at an ASK

### mAadhaar App
- Download the **mAadhaar** app from Google Play Store or Apple App Store
- Add your Aadhaar profile using OTP
- Access your Aadhaar card digitally anytime

## Check Enrollment Status

1. Visit [uidai.gov.in](https://uidai.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Check Aadhaar Status"**
3. Enter your 14-digit **Enrollment ID** and enrollment date
4. View current status: processing, generated, or rejected

## Tips for Faster Processing

### Before You Visit
- **Book appointment online** — walk-ins may have long queues
- **Check centre availability** — use [appointments.uidai.gov.in](https://appointments.uidai.gov.in) to find active centres
- **Carry original documents** — photocopies alone are not sufficient
- **Ensure mobile number is active** — you'll need it for OTP-based verification and receiving your Aadhaar number

### At the Centre
- **Arrive on time** — centres process on first-come basis for walk-ins
- **Clean fingers** — ensure fingers are clean and dry for clear biometric capture
- **Remove glasses and head coverings** — for photograph (unless religious)
- **Verify details on screen** — check your name, address, and date of birth before biometric capture

### After Enrollment
- **Save your EID slip** — this is your only proof of enrollment until Aadhaar is generated
- **Check status after 7 days** — visit UIDAI website to track progress
- **Download e-Aadhaar** — as soon as the number is generated, download the digital version

## Important Tips

- **There is no official "Tatkal" Aadhaar** — be wary of agents promising instant Aadhaar for extra fees; it's a scam
- **ASKs are the fastest** — UIDAI's own Aadhaar Seva Kendras process enrollments faster than third-party centres
- **e-Aadhaar is valid everywhere** — you don't need to wait for the physical card; the digital version is legally accepted
- **New enrollment is free** — never pay for new Aadhaar enrollment; report such centres to UIDAI
- **Children need mandatory updates** — Aadhaar for children must be updated with biometrics at ages 5, 10, and 15

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How fast can I get an Aadhaar number?
At Aadhaar Seva Kendras, the number is typically generated within 5-15 days. You can download e-Aadhaar immediately after generation.

### Is there a way to get Aadhaar in one day?
There is no guaranteed same-day Aadhaar. However, in some cases, ASKs generate Aadhaar within 48-72 hours. The fastest way to use it is through e-Aadhaar download.

### Can I use the enrollment slip as Aadhaar?
The enrollment slip (EID) is not a substitute for Aadhaar. However, some banks and agencies accept the EID temporarily while your Aadhaar is being processed.

### What if my Aadhaar enrollment is rejected?
Visit the enrollment centre again with correct documents. Common rejection reasons: blurred biometrics, incomplete documents, or duplicate enrollment (you may already have an Aadhaar).

### Can I apply for Aadhaar online without visiting a centre?
No, Aadhaar requires in-person biometric capture (fingerprints, iris, photograph). Online application is not available for new enrollment.
