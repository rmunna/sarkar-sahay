# How to Register on e-Shram Portal — Unorganized Worker Card & Benefits

If you are a daily wage worker, street vendor, domestic helper, construction worker, or any unorganized worker in India, the **e-Shram portal** is made for you. This guide explains everything — what is e-Shram, who can register, what benefits you get, and how to apply step by step.

---

## What is e-Shram?

**e-Shram** is a national portal launched by the **Ministry of Labour & Employment, Government of India**. The official website is [eshram.gov.in](https://eshram.gov.in).

It is a **national database for unorganized workers**. The goal is to register all unorganized workers in India so that the government can:

- Know how many unorganized workers are in the country
- Deliver welfare schemes and benefits directly to workers
- Provide social security and insurance cover

When you register on e-Shram, you get a **UAN (Universal Account Number)** — a unique 12-digit number. This is your e-Shram card.

---

## Who is an Unorganized Worker?

An **unorganized worker** is any person who works but does not have a formal employment contract, regular salary, or employer-provided social security. Examples include:

- **Construction workers** (mistry, labour, painter, plumber)
- **Street vendors** and hawkers
- **Domestic workers** (maid, cook, driver)
- **Agricultural labourers** and farmers
- **Gig workers** (delivery persons, app-based drivers)
- **Auto-rickshaw and taxi drivers**
- **Fishermen and fish workers**
- **Beedi and brick kiln workers**
- **Washer men and women (dhobi)**
- **Rag pickers and waste collectors**
- **ASHA and Anganwadi workers**
- **Mid-day meal workers**
- **Any self-employed or daily wage worker**

If you do not get PF (Provident Fund) or ESI from your employer, you are most likely an unorganized worker.

## Benefits of e-Shram Card

### 1. Accidental Insurance (₹2 Lakh)

Every registered e-Shram worker gets **accidental insurance** under the **Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY)**:

| Situation | Amount |
|-----------|--------|
| Death due to accident | ₹2,00,000 |
| Full permanent disability | ₹2,00,000 |
| Partial permanent disability | ₹1,00,000 |

### 2. Access to Government Schemes

Your e-Shram card helps you get benefits from many central and state government schemes, including ration, housing, pension, and loan schemes.

### 3. Portability Across States

If you move from one state to another for work, your e-Shram card and UAN number remain the same. Your benefits follow you.

### 4. Emergency Relief

During COVID-19, the government used the e-Shram database to send direct cash transfers to unorganized workers. Future relief can also reach you through this registration.

## Eligibility for e-Shram Card

You can register on e-Shram if you meet **all** these conditions:

1. **Age:** You are between **16 to 59 years** old
2. **Unorganized worker:** You are not in formal/organized employment
3. **Not a member of:** EPFO (Employees' Provident Fund Organisation), ESIC (Employees' State Insurance Corporation), or NPS (National Pension System — government version)
4. **Indian citizen** with a valid Aadhaar number

---

## Documents Required

You need only a few things to register:

1. **Aadhaar Card** — mandatory
2. **Mobile number linked to Aadhaar** — for OTP verification
3. **Bank account details** — account number and **IFSC code**
4. **Aadhaar-linked mobile number** — the OTP will come on this number only

> **Important:** Your mobile number must be linked to your Aadhaar. If it is not linked, visit your nearest Aadhaar centre first.

**Optional documents** (not required but helpful):
- Ration card
- Income certificate
- Disability certificate (if applicable)

## How to Register on e-Shram Portal Online (Step-by-Step)

You can register yourself from your mobile phone or computer. Follow these steps:

### Step 1: Visit the Official Website

Go to **[register.eshram.gov.in](https://register.eshram.gov.in)** or **[eshram.gov.in](https://eshram.gov.in)** and click on **"Register on e-Shram"**.

### Step 2: Enter Your Aadhaar Number

- Type your **12-digit Aadhaar number**
- Enter the **captcha code** shown on screen
- Tick the declaration checkbox
- Click **"Send OTP"**

### Step 3: Verify OTP

- You will receive an OTP on your **Aadhaar-linked mobile number**
- Enter the OTP and click **"Verify"**

### Step 4: Fill Personal Details

After OTP verification, your basic details (name, date of birth, gender) will auto-fill from Aadhaar. You need to fill:

- **Father's/Husband's name**
- **Social category** (SC/ST/OBC/General)
- **Education qualification**
- **Marital status**
- **Address details** (state, district, pin code)

### Step 5: Fill Occupation Details

- Select your **primary occupation** (e.g., construction worker, street vendor, domestic worker)
- Select **monthly income range**
- Add **skill details** if any

### Step 6: Enter Bank Account Details

- Enter your **bank account number**
- Enter **IFSC code**
- Confirm the bank name

### Step 7: Add Nominee Details

- Enter **nominee name** (family member who will get insurance money)
- Select **relationship** with nominee

### Step 8: Review and Submit

- Check all your details carefully
- Click **"Submit"**
- Your **UAN (Universal Account Number)** will be generated

### Step 9: Download Your e-Shram Card

- After successful registration, you can download your **e-Shram card** immediately
- Save it on your phone or take a printout

## How to Register via CSC (Common Service Centre)

If you cannot register online by yourself, you can visit your nearest **CSC (Common Service Centre)** or **Jan Seva Kendra**:

1. Go to your nearest **CSC centre** (find one at [locator.csccloud.in](https://locator.csccloud.in))
2. Carry your **Aadhaar card** and **bank passbook**
3. The CSC operator will fill the form for you
4. You will need to verify using **OTP** or **biometric (fingerprint)**
5. The operator will give you a printed **e-Shram card**

> **Note:** CSC may charge a small service fee (usually ₹20). The government registration itself is **free**.

---

## How to Download e-Shram Card

If you have already registered and want to download your card again:

1. Visit **[eshram.gov.in](https://eshram.gov.in)**
2. Click on **"Already Registered"** or **"Know Your UAN"**
3. Enter your **Aadhaar number** or **UAN number**
4. Verify with **OTP**
5. Your e-Shram card will appear on screen
6. Click **"Download UAN Card"** to save as PDF

Your e-Shram card will have:
- Your **photo** (from Aadhaar)
- **Name** and **date of birth**
- **12-digit UAN number**
- **QR code** for verification
- **Occupation** details

---

## How to Update Details on e-Shram Card

If you want to change your mobile number, bank account, address, or occupation:

1. Visit **[eshram.gov.in](https://eshram.gov.in)**
2. Click on **"Update/Modify Profile"**
3. Enter your **UAN number** or **Aadhaar number**
4. Verify with **OTP**
5. Edit the details you want to change
6. Click **"Submit"**

You can update:
- Mobile number
- Bank account and IFSC
- Address
- Occupation
- Nominee details

## Linked Government Schemes

Your e-Shram registration connects you to many government welfare schemes:

### 1. Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY)
- Accidental insurance of ₹2 lakh
- Premium of just ₹12/year (auto-deducted from bank)

### 2. Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY)
- Life insurance of ₹2 lakh
- Premium of ₹436/year
- Covers death due to any reason

### 3. PM-SVANidhi (Street Vendor Scheme)
- Loan up to ₹50,000 for street vendors
- No collateral required
- Website: [pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in](https://pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in)

### 4. Building & Other Construction Workers (BOCW)
- Welfare benefits for registered construction workers
- Education assistance, medical aid, pension

### 5. Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY)
- Affordable housing for economically weaker sections
- Subsidy on home loans
- Website: [pmaymis.gov.in](https://pmaymis.gov.in)

### 6. Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maan-Dhan (PM-SYM)
- Monthly pension of ₹3,000 after age 60
- For unorganized workers with income below ₹15,000/month

### 7. Atal Pension Yojana (APY)
- Guaranteed pension of ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 per month
- Contribution based on age of joining

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Is e-Shram registration free?

**Yes.** Registration on the e-Shram portal is completely **free**. If you register through a CSC centre, a small service charge (around ₹20) may apply, but the government does not charge anything.

### Q2: I don't have a smartphone. Can I still register?

**Yes.** You can visit your nearest **CSC (Common Service Centre)** or **Jan Seva Kendra**. The operator will help you register using biometric or OTP verification. You do not need a smartphone.

### Q3: My mobile number is not linked with Aadhaar. What should I do?

Visit your nearest **Aadhaar Enrolment Centre** with your Aadhaar card and the mobile number you want to link. It takes about 5-10 minutes. After linking, you can register on e-Shram.

### Q4: Can I register if I am already getting benefits from a state government scheme?

**Yes.** e-Shram registration is separate from state schemes. However, you should **not** be a member of EPFO, ESIC, or NPS (government) to be eligible.

### Q5: How do I check my e-Shram registration status?

Visit **[eshram.gov.in](https://eshram.gov.in)** and click on **"Know Your UAN"** or **"Already Registered"**. Enter your Aadhaar number and verify with OTP. Your registration status and UAN will be shown.

### Q6: What if I lose my e-Shram card?

No problem. You can download it again anytime from the e-Shram portal using your Aadhaar number or UAN. There is no need to re-register.

### Q7: Can I have more than one e-Shram card?

**No.** One person can have only **one UAN number**. Since it is linked to your Aadhaar, duplicate registrations are not possible.
