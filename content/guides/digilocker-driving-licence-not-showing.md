# DigiLocker Driving Licence Not Showing? Fix Missing DL

Many users face issues when trying to fetch their driving licence (DL) on [DigiLocker](https://digilocker.gov.in/). The DL may show "Document Not Found", fail to load, or simply not appear. This guide explains why your driving licence is not showing in DigiLocker and how to fix it.

## Why Is Your Driving Licence Not Showing in DigiLocker?

Driving licences on DigiLocker are fetched from the **Parivahan (Sarathi)** database maintained by the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways. For your DL to appear, the following must be true:

1. Your DL data must exist in the **Parivahan/Sarathi** database
2. Your **name and date of birth** must match between Aadhaar and Parivahan records
3. Your state's RTO must have **digitized and synced** the DL data
4. You must enter the **correct DL number** in the exact format

## Common Issues and Fixes

### 1. Parivahan Database Not Synced

Many older driving licences (issued before 2010-2015) may not be in the Parivahan digital database.

**Fix:**
1. Check if your DL exists in Parivahan: visit [parivahan.gov.in](https://parivahan.gov.in/) → "Know Your DL" → Enter DL number and DOB
2. If your DL is found on Parivahan but not on DigiLocker, the sync may be delayed — try after 24-48 hours
3. If your DL is **not found on Parivahan** either:
   - Visit your **RTO (Regional Transport Office)** with the original DL
   - Request them to **update/digitize your DL** in the Sarathi system
   - This process may take 7-15 working days

### 2. RTO Data Mismatch

Your name or date of birth on the DL may not match your [Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online):

**Common mismatches:**
- Name: "Suresh Kumar" on DL vs "Suresh K" on Aadhaar
- Date of birth differs between DL and Aadhaar
- Father's name format differs

**Fix:**
1. First, verify your details on Parivahan's "Know Your DL" service
2. If DL details are wrong, apply for **DL correction at your RTO** through [sarathi.parivahan.gov.in](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/)
3. If Aadhaar details are wrong, update your [Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)
4. After correction, wait **48-72 hours** for the data to sync with DigiLocker
5. Then try fetching the DL again

### 3. DL Number Format Error

DigiLocker requires the DL number in a specific format. Entering it incorrectly will show "Document Not Found".

**Correct DL number format:**
- Format: `SS-RRRRYYYNNNNNNN` (e.g., `KA-0120190001234`)
- **SS** = State code (KA, MH, DL, UP, TN, etc.)
- **RRRR** = RTO code (01, 02, etc.)
- **YYY** = Year of issue (2019 → 2019 or 019)
- **NNNNNNN** = Serial number

**Fix:**
1. Check the exact DL number on your physical driving licence or Parivahan portal
2. Enter it **without spaces** but **with hyphens** where shown on the card
3. Some older DLs have different formats — enter exactly as printed
4. Try both formats: with and without leading zeros

### 4. State RTO Not Supported or Delayed

Not all state RTOs have fully integrated with DigiLocker. Some states have limited or no support:

**Well-integrated states (DL usually works):**
- Karnataka, Maharashtra, Delhi, Tamil Nadu, Uttar Pradesh, Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Telangana, Kerala

**States with known issues (DL may not fetch):**
- Some North-Eastern states
- Newly created districts where RTO data migration is pending

**Fix:**
1. Check the DigiLocker partner list for your state's transport department
2. If your state is not listed, DL fetching may not be available yet
3. Use the **mParivahan app** as an alternative — it directly accesses the Parivahan database

### 5. Manual Fetch Method

If automatic DL fetch does not work, try the manual method:

**On DigiLocker App:**
1. Open DigiLocker app → tap **"Get Document"**
2. Search for **"Driving Licence"**
3. Select **"Ministry of Road Transport & Highways"** as the issuer
4. Enter your **DL number** and **date of birth**
5. Tap **"Get Document"**

**On DigiLocker Website:**
1. Log in to [digilocker.gov.in](https://digilocker.gov.in/)
2. Go to **"Issued Documents"** → **"Get from Partner"**
3. Select **"Ministry of Road Transport & Highways"**
4. Choose **"Driving Licence"**
5. Enter your DL number and date of birth
6. Click **"Get Document"**

### 6. DL Shows "Expired" or Old Details

If your DL fetches successfully but shows expired or old details:

1. Renew your DL through [sarathi.parivahan.gov.in](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/) if it has expired
2. After renewal, the updated DL will automatically reflect in DigiLocker within **7-15 days**
3. If it does not update, remove the old DL from DigiLocker and re-fetch

## Alternative: Use mParivahan App

If DigiLocker is not showing your DL, the **mParivahan** app is an excellent alternative:

1. Download **mParivahan** from Google Play or App Store
2. Enter your **DL number** and **date of birth**
3. Your virtual DL will be displayed
4. mParivahan virtual DL is also **legally accepted** by traffic police

2. The mParivahan app is a reliable backup for digital DL access

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is my driving licence not showing in DigiLocker?

Your DL may not be in the Parivahan digital database, your name/DOB may not match Aadhaar records, or your state's RTO may not have fully integrated with DigiLocker.

### How do I check if my DL is in the Parivahan database?

Visit [parivahan.gov.in](https://parivahan.gov.in/), go to "Know Your DL", and enter your DL number and date of birth. If found, it should be fetchable on DigiLocker.

### Is the digital driving licence on DigiLocker legally valid?

Yes, the digital DL on DigiLocker is legally valid and accepted by traffic police across India as per the IT Act 2000 and Motor Vehicles Act amendments.

### What is the correct format for entering DL number in DigiLocker?

Enter the DL number exactly as printed on your physical licence, typically in the format SS-RRRRYYYNNNNNNN (e.g., KA-0120190001234). Include hyphens but no spaces.

### Can I use mParivahan instead of DigiLocker for my DL?

Yes, the mParivahan app directly accesses the Parivahan database and displays your virtual DL. It is legally accepted and works even when DigiLocker has issues.

### How long does it take for a renewed DL to appear on DigiLocker?

After DL renewal through Sarathi, it typically takes 7-15 days for the updated DL to appear on DigiLocker. Remove the old document and re-fetch after this period.
