## What is SEHAT Scheme?

**SEHAT (Social, Endeavour for Health And Telemedicine)** is a health insurance scheme for the Union Territory of **Jammu & Kashmir** that provides **₹5 lakh per family per year** for cashless hospitalization. It extends the **Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY** scheme to cover **all residents of J&K** — making it a universal health coverage scheme.

Launched on **26 December 2020**, SEHAT covers approximately 21 lakh families (the entire population of J&K). It is managed by the **State Health Agency (SHA), J&K**.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Residency:** Must be a resident of Jammu & Kashmir
2. **Universal Coverage:** **All families** in J&K are eligible — no income restriction
3. **Ration Card:** Must have a J&K ration card or be in the family database
4. **Exclusions:** Government employees covered under CGHS/ECHS are excluded

## Coverage

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Sum Insured | ₹5,00,000 per family per year |
| Procedures | 1,500+ medical/surgical packages |
| Pre-existing Diseases | Covered from day one |
| Hospitals | Government + empanelled private |
| Portability | Can be used across India at PM-JAY hospitals |

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar Card** of family members
- **Ration Card** (J&K)
- **Family ID / household data** as per J&K records
- **Mobile number**

## How to Get Your SEHAT Card

### Online

1. **Visit** [sha.jk.gov.in](https://sha.jk.gov.in)
2. **Check eligibility** by entering ration card number or Aadhaar
3. **If eligible**, proceed to card generation
4. **Visit** nearest CSC or hospital for biometric verification and card printing

### At Common Service Centre (CSC)

1. **Visit** the nearest CSC in J&K
2. **Carry** Aadhaar Card and Ration Card
3. **Biometric verification** of family members
4. **SEHAT Golden Card** is generated
5. **Nominal fee** of ₹30

### At Hospital

1. **Visit** any SEHAT-empanelled hospital
2. **Ayushman Mitra desk** will verify your details
3. **Card can be generated** at the hospital
4. **Cashless treatment** starts immediately

## How to Use SEHAT Card

1. **Visit** any empanelled hospital (government or private)
2. **Show** SEHAT card and Aadhaar at the Ayushman desk
3. **Biometric authentication** confirms identity
4. **Get cashless treatment** — no out-of-pocket payment
5. **Pre and post hospitalization** expenses are also covered

## Important Tips

1. **Universal Coverage:** Almost everyone in J&K is covered — check your eligibility on the portal
2. **Get Card Early:** Don't wait until emergency — get your Golden Card made in advance
3. **Carry Aadhaar:** Required for biometric authentication at hospitals
4. **Use Across India:** SEHAT card works at PM-JAY empanelled hospitals across India
5. **Helpline:** Call **14555** for Ayushman/SEHAT queries

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. What is the coverage under SEHAT?
**₹5 lakh per family per year** for cashless hospitalization.

### Q2. Is everyone in J&K eligible?
Yes, SEHAT provides **universal coverage** to all J&K residents (except CGHS/ECHS beneficiaries).

### Q3. Do I need to pay any premium?
**No**, the J&K government bears the full cost.

### Q4. Can I use SEHAT outside J&K?
**Yes**, at any PM-JAY empanelled hospital across India.

### Q5. Are pre-existing diseases covered?
**Yes**, from day one.

### Q6. How do I get my SEHAT card?
Visit the nearest CSC or empanelled hospital with Aadhaar and Ration Card.

### Q7. What if a hospital refuses treatment?
Call the Ayushman helpline **14555** or contact SHA J&K.
