# Orunodoi (Arunodoi) Scheme Assam – Complete Guide 2026

## What is the Orunodoi Scheme?

The **Orunodoi (Arunodoi) Scheme** is a flagship social welfare programme of the Government of Assam launched in October 2020. Under this scheme, eligible women from economically weaker families receive **₹1,250 per month** directly into their bank accounts via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT). The scheme aims to empower women and provide financial assistance for household expenses including healthcare, education, and nutrition.

The name "Orunodoi" means "sunrise" in Assamese, symbolising a new dawn of empowerment for women in the state.

## Who is Eligible?

To qualify for the Orunodoi scheme, applicants must meet **all** the following criteria:

- **Gender:** Only women (one woman per family)
- **Residency:** Permanent resident of Assam
- **Age:** 18 years or above
- **Family Income:** Total annual family income must not exceed ₹2 lakh
- **Housing:** Family should not own a pucca house (more than 3 rooms)
- **Government Job:** No family member should be a permanent government employee (state or central)
- **Vehicles:** Family should not own a four-wheeler vehicle
- **Land:** Family should not own more than 5 bighas of agricultural land (in Brahmaputra Valley) or equivalent
- **Tax:** No family member should be an income tax payer

### Who is NOT Eligible?

- Families already receiving benefits under other pension schemes (NSAP)
- Families with any member drawing a government pension
- Applicants without an Aadhaar-linked bank account

## Documents Required

### Mandatory Documents
- Aadhaar Card of the applicant
- Bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked, in the applicant's name)
- Passport-size photograph
- Proof of residence in Assam (voter ID / ration card / domicile certificate)

### Supporting Documents
- BPL certificate or income certificate (issued by Circle Officer)
- Family declaration form
- Self-declaration regarding eligibility criteria
- Mobile number linked with Aadhaar

## How to Apply – Step-by-Step

### Online Application

As of 2026, the Orunodoi scheme applications are primarily collected through **camp-based drives** organised by the district administration. However, you can check your status online:

1. Visit the official portal at [orunodoi.assam.gov.in](https://orunodoi.assam.gov.in)
2. Click on "Check Beneficiary Status" or "New Application" (when open)
3. Enter your Aadhaar number and mobile number
4. Fill in family details, income details, and upload documents
5. Submit the application and note the reference number

### Offline Application (Primary Method)

1. **Visit your local Gaon Panchayat office** or the nearest Circle Office
2. Collect the **Orunodoi application form**
3. Fill in all details — personal, family, income, and bank information
4. Attach self-attested copies of all required documents
5. Submit the form at the designated collection centre during the application drive
6. The **Gaon Panchayat President / Ward Member** verifies the application
7. After verification, the application is forwarded to the **Circle Officer** for approval
8. Approved beneficiaries receive the amount directly in their bank account

### Application Through Camps

The Assam government periodically conducts **Orunodoi enrolment camps** at:
- Gaon Panchayat offices
- Community halls
- Schools and public buildings

Watch for announcements from your local MLA office or district administration.

## Benefit Amount

| Component | Amount |
|-----------|--------|
| Monthly benefit | ₹1,250 |
| Annual benefit | ₹15,000 |
| Payment mode | Direct Bank Transfer (DBT) |
| Payment frequency | Monthly (usually 1st-10th of each month) |

> **Note:** The benefit amount has been revised from the original ₹830 to ₹1,250 per month.

## Processing Time

- **Verification:** 2-4 weeks after submission
- **Approval:** 1-2 months after verification
- **First payment:** Within 1-2 months of approval
- Total timeline: approximately **2-4 months** from application to first payment

## How to Check Orunodoi Beneficiary Status

1. Visit [orunodoi.assam.gov.in](https://orunodoi.assam.gov.in)
2. Click on "Beneficiary List" or "Check Status"
3. Select your **District**, **Block/Circle**, and **Gaon Panchayat**
4. Search by name or Aadhaar number
5. Your application status will be displayed

## Important Tips

1. **Ensure your Aadhaar is linked to your bank account** — payments are made only through Aadhaar-enabled DBT
2. **Keep your mobile number updated** — OTP verification is required
3. **Apply during official drives** — applications outside the enrolment window may not be accepted
4. **Only one woman per household** can receive the benefit
5. **Report any issues** to the District Social Welfare Office or call the helpline

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: What is the current monthly amount under Orunodoi?
**A:** The current monthly benefit is **₹1,250 per month**, credited directly to the beneficiary's bank account.

### Q2: Can more than one woman from a family apply?
**A:** No. Only **one woman per family** is eligible under the Orunodoi scheme.

### Q3: Is there an online application portal?
**A:** Applications are primarily collected through **camp-based drives** conducted by the district administration. The online portal ([orunodoi.assam.gov.in](https://orunodoi.assam.gov.in)) is used mainly for status checks and beneficiary lists.

### Q4: What if my Aadhaar is not linked to my bank account?
**A:** You must link your Aadhaar to your bank account before applying. Visit your bank branch with your Aadhaar card to complete the linking process. See our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) for help.

### Q5: Can widows and divorced women apply?
**A:** Yes, widows and divorced women can apply provided they meet all other eligibility criteria.

### Q6: How do I report non-receipt of payment?
**A:** Contact your **Block Development Officer (BDO)** or the **District Social Welfare Officer**. You can also raise a complaint at the Circle Office.

### Q7: Is this scheme only for BPL families?
**A:** While it primarily targets economically weaker sections, the eligibility is based on the specific criteria (income below ₹2 lakh, housing, land ownership, etc.) rather than strictly BPL status.
