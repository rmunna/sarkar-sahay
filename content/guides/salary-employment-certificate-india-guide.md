# Salary Certificate & Employment Letter India — Your Rights and How to Get Them

Salary certificates and employment letters are needed for visas, bank loans, rental agreements, school admissions, and dozens of other purposes. Employers have a legal obligation to provide these. Here's what each document is, when you need it, and what to do if HR delays or refuses.

---

## Types of Employment Documents

| Document | What It Contains | When Needed |
|----------|-----------------|-------------|
| **Salary Certificate** | Letterhead + breakdown: basic + HRA + allowances + PF deduction + net salary | Visa, home loan, rental agreement |
| **Salary Slip** | Monthly payroll document — detailed deductions and gross/net | Bank loan, ITR verification |
| **Employment Letter** | Confirms you're employed + role + salary + date of joining | Visa, home loan, rental, school admission |
| **Experience Letter** | Confirms total tenure + role; issued on resignation/termination | New employer, background check |
| **NOC (No Objection Certificate)** | Company has no objection to you taking up another job/activity | Part-time work, directorship, visa |

---

## Salary Certificate — Standard Format

A salary certificate should be on company letterhead and contain:

```
[Company Name]
[Address]
[CIN/Registration No.]

Date: __/__/____

TO WHOM IT MAY CONCERN

This is to certify that Mr./Ms. [Name], bearing PAN [XXXXXXXXX], is employed 
with [Company Name] as [Designation] since [Date of Joining].

His/Her current monthly gross salary is as follows:

Basic Salary:          ₹ __________
House Rent Allowance:  ₹ __________
Special Allowance:     ₹ __________
Other Allowances:      ₹ __________
Gross Monthly Salary:  ₹ __________

Deductions:
Provident Fund (12%):  ₹ __________
Professional Tax:      ₹ __________
TDS (per month):       ₹ __________
Net Take-Home:         ₹ __________

Annual CTC:            ₹ __________

This certificate is issued for bonafide purposes only.

[Authorized Signatory]
[Name and Designation]
[Company Seal]
```

## When You Need Each Document

### Visa Applications
- Most embassies want an **employment letter** (not just salary certificate)
- The letter should include: your designation, salary, leave approved, and that you'll return to employment
- Some embassies also want salary certificate showing breakup

### Bank Loans (Home Loan / Personal Loan)
- **Salary slips**: Last 3–6 months (most commonly requested)
- **Salary certificate**: Sometimes requested as alternative
- **Form 16**: Required for annual income verification
- **Bank statements**: 6 months showing salary credits

### Rental Agreement / Lease
- Landlords request employment proof to verify your ability to pay rent
- Salary certificate or employment letter with salary details is sufficient
- Salary slips are also widely accepted

### School/College Admissions (for children of employees)
- Many management quota / reserved seats require parent's salary proof
- Employment letter on letterhead with salary breakdown

## Your Legal Right to Get These Documents

**You have the right to receive**:
1. **Salary slips**: Every month after receiving salary — this is an employer's statutory obligation under various state Shop & Establishment Acts
2. **Experience letter on resignation**: Employer must provide within 30 days of last working day (varies by state)
3. **Form 16**: Must be issued by May 31 after every financial year by employer (mandatory under Income Tax Act)

**Experience/Relieving letter**: You have the right to receive a relieving/experience letter on resignation. An employer cannot permanently withhold it — courts have held this to be a form of coercion.

## What to Do If HR is Delaying or Refusing

### Step 1: Submit Written Request

Email HR with your specific request:
> "I request a salary certificate / employment letter / experience letter for [purpose]. Could you please issue this at the earliest? I need it by [date] for [specific reason]."

Keep the email thread — document your request with timestamps.

### Step 2: Escalate to HR Manager / Manager

If no response in 3–5 business days:
- Forward the email to HR manager and your direct manager
- CC the document in the same chain

### Step 3: Labour Commissioner (For Experience Letter Disputes)

If employer refuses to issue experience/relieving letter after resignation:
- File complaint at the **Labour Commissioner** in your city/district
- Grounds: Employer holding experience certificate = restraint of trade, unlawful under Contract Labour Regulation Act
- Labour Commissioner can direct employer to issue the letter

### Step 4: Courts (For Salary Slip Non-Issuance)

Monthly salary slips are a statutory requirement. Non-issuance is a violation:
- File complaint at District Labour Office
- In extreme cases, civil court injunction forcing employer to issue documents

## Salary Slip vs Salary Certificate — Difference

| Aspect | Salary Slip | Salary Certificate |
|--------|------------|-------------------|
| Frequency | Monthly | On-demand |
| Contains | Gross, deductions, net for one month | Breakup + confirmation for any period |
| Format | Payroll-generated (often auto-generated) | HR-issued letterhead document |
| Most useful for | Bank statements, ITR, quick income proof | Visa, formal applications |
| Employer's obligation | Monthly — must provide automatically | On request |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My employer doesn't have a company letterhead. What do I do?

For very small businesses, a letter with company name, address, proprietor/partner signature, and mobile number is usually accepted for rental purposes. For banks and visa applications, they may require a more formal letter. In such cases, some banks accept IT returns, GST registration, and bank statements as alternative proof.

### I left my company 3 years ago. Can I still get an experience letter?

Yes — employers can issue backdated experience letters. Contact HR/email the ex-employer requesting the letter. Most companies comply; legal obligation persists even after employment ends. If they refuse, you can show offer letters, salary slips, Form 16, and bank statements as proof of tenure.

### My employer is giving a salary certificate that shows less than actual salary. What should I do?

If it's a structured CTC with different components, ensure ALL components are included. If the employer is deliberately understating salary: request the exact CTC structure in writing, compare with offer letter. If the certificate misrepresents your actual salary, request a correction in writing and document the request.

### Can a private company refuse to issue a salary slip?

Technically no — under most state Shop & Establishment Acts, employers must issue salary slips. File a complaint at the district Labour Commissioner if refused.
