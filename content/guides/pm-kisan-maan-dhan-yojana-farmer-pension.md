## What is PM Kisan Maan Dhan Yojana?

**PM Kisan Maan Dhan Yojana (PM-KMY)** is a government pension scheme for **small and marginal farmers**, providing a **guaranteed pension of ₹3,000 per month** after the age of 60. It is a voluntary, contributory scheme where both the farmer and the government contribute equally to the pension corpus.

Key features:
- **₹3,000/month pension** after age 60
- **Equal contribution** by farmer and government (government matches your deposit)
- Monthly contribution: **₹55 to ₹200** depending on your age at enrollment
- Enrollment at **CSC (Common Service Centre)** or at [maandhan.in](https://maandhan.in)
- Spouse can also join separately and get another ₹3,000/month

## Who is Eligible?

- **Small and marginal farmers** — those with cultivable land up to **2 hectares**
- Age: **18 to 40 years** at the time of enrollment
- Must be enrolled in the **PM-KISAN scheme** (or have land records confirming small/marginal farmer status)

**Not eligible:**
- Farmers with more than 2 hectares of land
- Income taxpayers
- Government employees
- Those already enrolled in EPFO, NPS, or any other pension scheme

## Monthly Contribution

The contribution is based on your **age at the time of enrollment**:

| Entry Age | Monthly Contribution (Farmer) | Govt. Contribution (Equal) |
|---|---|---|
| 18 years | ₹55/month | ₹55/month |
| 25 years | ₹80/month | ₹80/month |
| 30 years | ₹110/month | ₹110/month |
| 35 years | ₹150/month | ₹150/month |
| 40 years | ₹200/month | ₹200/month |

> **The government matches every rupee you put in** — so your effective return is doubled at the source.

## How to Enroll — Step by Step

### Via CSC (Common Service Centre)

1. Visit your **nearest CSC (Jan Seva Kendra / Common Service Centre)**
2. Carry documents (see below)
3. CSC operator will:
   - Register you on the **maandhan.in** portal
   - Collect your first monthly contribution (cash)
   - Set up auto-debit from your bank account for future months
4. You receive a **Kisan Pension Number**

### Online via maandhan.in

1. Go to **[maandhan.in](https://maandhan.in)**
2. Click **"Self Enrollment"**
3. Enter Aadhaar and mobile OTP
4. Fill in land details, bank account
5. Pay first contribution online
6. Download and keep the **Kisan Pension Card**

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card** (mandatory)
2. **Land records / Khasra-Khatauni** showing agricultural land (up to 2 hectares)
3. **Bank savings account** (linked to Aadhaar for auto-debit and pension credit)
4. **Mobile number** linked to Aadhaar

## Pension After 60

- **₹3,000/month** credited to the farmer's Aadhaar-linked bank account on the 1st of every month
- **For life** — no end date for the pension
- If the farmer dies, the **spouse gets 50% of the pension** (₹1,500/month) as family pension
- If the farmer exits before 60, the contribution amount with bank savings rate interest is returned

## If Farmer Exits Before 60

| Scenario | What Farmer Gets |
|---|---|
| Exit before 10 years | Contribution back + bank savings interest |
| Exit after 10 years but before 60 | Contribution back + accumulated interest (NPS pension fund rate) |
| Death before 60 | Spouse can continue contributing OR exit and get the accumulated amount |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I am in PM-KISAN scheme. Do I automatically get this pension?
No — PM-KISAN (income support ₹6,000/year) and PM-KMY (pension scheme) are separate. You need to **enroll separately** in PM-KMY.

### My land is in my father's name. Can I still enroll?
The eligibility is based on the **cultivating farmer** — if you are the one who cultivates the land even if it's in your father's name, you may be eligible. Check at your nearest CSC with land records showing your family as cultivators.

### What if I miss my monthly contribution?
Your subscription may be suspended. You can regularize by paying the missed months' amount + simple interest at the rate of 8% per annum. Visit CSC or the maandhan.in portal to reactivate.

### I am 42 years old. Can I join?
No — the maximum entry age is **40 years**. If you are above 40, you are not eligible for PM-KMY. Consider PPF or NPS as alternatives.

### Will the ₹3,000/month pension increase with inflation?
The pension amount is fixed at ₹3,000/month as per the scheme design. The government may revise it periodically, but there is no guaranteed automatic inflation indexing.

### Can my wife also get a separate pension?
Yes — your spouse can **also enroll separately** and contribute separately to get an additional ₹3,000/month pension after 60. Each person's subscription is independent.
