# EPF Withdrawal Online: How to Withdraw PF Money

The Employees' Provident Fund (EPF) is a retirement savings scheme managed by the Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO). You can withdraw your PF balance — fully or partially — through the EPFO unified portal online. This guide covers the complete process using Form 19 (full withdrawal), Form 31 (partial/advance), and Form 10C (pension withdrawal).

## Types of EPF Withdrawal

| Type | Form | When to Use |
|------|------|-------------|
| Full withdrawal | Form 19 | After leaving job (unemployed for 2+ months) or retirement |
| Partial/Advance withdrawal | Form 31 | Medical, housing, education, marriage, or other specified needs |
| Pension withdrawal | Form 10C | EPS pension fund withdrawal (less than 10 years of service) |

## Who is Eligible?

### Full Withdrawal (Form 19)
- Employee who has left the job
- Unemployed for **2 months or more** (or 1 month for certain cases)
- At retirement (age 58)
- Women leaving employment for marriage (with conditions)

### Partial Withdrawal (Form 31)
- Medical treatment (self/family) — no minimum service
- Housing loan repayment — 3 years of service
- Home purchase/construction — 5 years of service
- Home renovation — 5 years after house purchase
- Marriage (self/children/siblings) — 7 years of service
- Education of children (Class 10+) — 7 years of service
- Pre-retirement (1 year before retirement at age 54)

### Pension Withdrawal (Form 10C)
- Less than 10 years of total EPF service
- Unemployed and want to withdraw pension corpus
- If 10+ years of service, you receive monthly pension instead

## Documents Required

- **UAN (Universal Account Number)** — must be activated
- **Aadhaar card** linked with UAN
- **Bank account** linked with UAN (IFSC verified)
- **PAN card** (mandatory if withdrawal exceeds ₹50,000)
- **Cancelled cheque or bank passbook** (for bank verification)
- **Form 15G/15H** (to avoid TDS if applicable — less than 5 years of service)

> **Important:** Your Aadhaar, PAN, and bank account must all be verified (KYC approved) on the EPFO unified portal before you can submit an online claim.

## Step-by-Step: EPF Full Withdrawal Online (Form 19)

1. Visit the **EPFO Unified Portal**: [unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in/memberinterface/)
2. **Log in** with your UAN and password
3. Go to **Online Services** → **Claim (Form-31, 19, 10C & 10D)**
4. Verify your details — name, date of birth, and bank account will be displayed
5. Enter the **last 4 digits** of your bank account for verification
6. Click **Proceed for Online Claim**
7. Select **PF Withdrawal (Form 19)** from the "I Want to Apply for" dropdown
8. Enter your full address and upload a **cancelled cheque** if prompted
9. Check the **Terms and Conditions** checkbox
10. Click **Get Aadhaar OTP** — an OTP will be sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile
11. Enter the OTP and **submit** your claim

## Step-by-Step: EPF Partial Withdrawal Online (Form 31)

1. Log in to the **EPFO Unified Portal** with UAN and password
2. Go to **Online Services** → **Claim (Form-31, 19, 10C & 10D)**
3. Verify bank details by entering last 4 digits of your account
4. Click **Proceed for Online Claim**
5. Select **PF Advance (Form 31)** from the dropdown
6. Choose the **Purpose of Advance** (medical, housing, education, etc.)
7. Enter the **amount** you wish to withdraw (within eligible limits)
8. Upload supporting documents if required for the selected purpose
9. Check the declaration checkbox
10. Authenticate with **Aadhaar OTP** and submit

## Step-by-Step: Pension Withdrawal Online (Form 10C)

1. Log in to the **EPFO Unified Portal**
2. Go to **Online Services** → **Claim (Form-31, 19, 10C & 10D)**
3. Verify bank account details
4. Select **Pension Withdrawal (Form 10C)** from the dropdown
5. Fill in the required details
6. Authenticate with **Aadhaar OTP** and submit

> **Note:** You can submit Form 19 and Form 10C together to withdraw both PF and pension amounts simultaneously.

## Fees

There are **no fees** charged by EPFO for withdrawal claims. However:

| Scenario | TDS Deduction |
|----------|---------------|
| Service ≥ 5 years | No TDS |
| Service < 5 years, PAN provided | 10% TDS |
| Service < 5 years, no PAN | 20% TDS |
| Withdrawal < ₹50,000 | No TDS |
| Form 15G/15H submitted | No TDS |

## Processing Time

| Claim Type | Expected Timeline |
|------------|-------------------|
| Full withdrawal (Form 19) | 10–20 working days |
| Partial withdrawal (Form 31) | 15–20 working days |
| Pension withdrawal (Form 10C) | 10–20 working days |
| Employer-approved online claims | 3–10 working days |

Processing is faster when:
- All KYC documents are verified
- Employer has digitally approved the claim
- Bank account and IFSC are correct

## How to Track Your EPF Claim Status

1. Log in to the **EPFO Unified Portal**
2. Go to **Online Services** → **Track Claim Status**
3. Your claim status will show: Submitted → Under Process → Settled

You can also check via **UMANG app** or by calling the **EPFO helpline: 1800-118-005** (toll-free).

## Important Tips

1. **Complete KYC first** — Ensure Aadhaar, PAN, and bank account are verified on the portal before filing a claim
2. **Link correct bank account** — The account must be in your name and active; joint accounts may cause rejection
3. **Submit Form 15G** — If you have less than 5 years of service but withdrawal is below taxable limit, submit Form 15G to avoid TDS
4. **Transfer instead of withdraw** — If changing jobs, transfer your PF using Form 13 instead of withdrawing (preserves tax benefits and continuity)
5. **Keep employer informed** — Some claims require employer digital approval; follow up with your previous employer if the claim is pending

## FAQs

### Can I withdraw my EPF while still employed?
You cannot do a full withdrawal while employed. However, you can apply for partial withdrawal (Form 31) for specific purposes like medical emergency, housing, or education while still working.

### How long does EPF withdrawal take after submission?
Online claims typically take 10–20 working days. If your employer has approved the claim digitally and all KYC is verified, it can be processed in as few as 3–10 days.

### Is EPF withdrawal taxable?
If you have completed 5 years of continuous service, EPF withdrawal is **tax-free**. If less than 5 years, TDS is deducted at 10% (with PAN) or 20% (without PAN). You can submit Form 15G to claim exemption if your total income is below the taxable limit.

### Can I withdraw EPF without employer approval?
Yes, if you have an Aadhaar-verified UAN and your KYC is complete, you can file an online claim directly. For some establishments, employer e-approval is still required within a time window.

### What if my UAN is not activated?
You must activate your UAN first at the unified portal using your UAN, Aadhaar, and mobile number. See our [UAN activation guide](/guide/epf-passbook-not-updated-fix) for help.

### Can I withdraw the full PF amount immediately after leaving a job?
You must be unemployed for at least 2 months to claim full PF withdrawal. However, you can withdraw up to 75% of your PF balance after 1 month of unemployment.

### What is the maximum amount I can withdraw under Form 31?
The maximum depends on the purpose. For example, for housing you can withdraw up to 36 months' basic wages + DA or the total cost (whichever is lower). For medical emergencies, up to 6 months' basic wages or the total cost.
