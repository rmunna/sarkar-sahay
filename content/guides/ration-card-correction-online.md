# How to Correct Ration Card Details Online — Name, Address, Members

Your ration card is one of the most important government documents in India. It helps you get subsidised food grains under the Public Distribution System (PDS). But what happens when there is a spelling mistake in your name, your address has changed, or you need to add or remove a family member? You need to apply for a ration card correction.

The good news is that most Indian states now allow you to correct ration card details online through their respective food and civil supplies portals. This guide will walk you through the complete process.

---

## What is Ration Card Correction?

Ration card correction refers to updating or fixing incorrect information on your existing ration card. Common corrections include:

- **Name correction** — fixing spelling mistakes in the cardholder or member names
- **Address change** — updating your residential address after shifting
- **Adding members** — including new family members (newborn child, spouse after marriage)
- **Removing members** — deleting members who have passed away, married out, or separated
- **Age or gender correction** — fixing wrong date of birth or gender details
- **Aadhaar linking** — updating or correcting the Aadhaar number linked to the card

---

## Eligibility

Anyone who already holds a valid ration card can apply for corrections. You must be:

- The **head of family** listed on the ration card, or an authorised family member
- A resident of the state where the ration card was issued
- Able to provide **supporting documents** for the requested change

There is no income or caste requirement for corrections — it is available to all card types (AAY, PHH, APL, BPL).

---

## Documents Required

The documents you need depend on the type of correction:

### For Name Correction
- Aadhaar card (with correct name)
- Voter ID, PAN card, or passport
- School certificate or birth certificate

### For Address Change
- New address proof (electricity bill, rent agreement, Aadhaar with updated address)
- Self-declaration of address change

### For Adding Members
- Birth certificate (for newborn)
- Marriage certificate (for spouse)
- Aadhaar card of the new member

### For Removing Members
- Death certificate (if member passed away)
- Marriage certificate (if member married and moved out)
- Written declaration from the head of family

### General Documents
- Existing ration card (original and photocopy)
- Aadhaar card of the applicant
- Passport-size photographs

## Step-by-Step Process: Online

The online process varies slightly by state, but the general steps are similar across most portals.

### Step 1: Visit Your State's Food Portal

Go to the official food and civil supplies website of your state. Some common ones:

- **Uttar Pradesh:** fcs.up.gov.in
- **Maharashtra:** mahafood.gov.in
- **Tamil Nadu:** tnpds.gov.in
- **Rajasthan:** food.rajasthan.gov.in
- **Bihar:** epds.bihar.gov.in

You can also start from the national portal at [nfsa.gov.in](https://nfsa.gov.in) and navigate to your state.

### Step 2: Log In or Register

If you already have an account, log in with your credentials. If not, register using your ration card number, Aadhaar number, and mobile number.

### Step 3: Select "Ration Card Correction" or "Edit Details"

Look for options like "Correction in Ration Card," "Update Details," or "Modify Card" in the menu. Click on it.

### Step 4: Choose the Type of Correction

Select what you want to change — name, address, add member, remove member, etc.

### Step 5: Enter Correct Details

Fill in the updated information carefully. Double-check spellings and numbers before submitting.

### Step 6: Upload Supporting Documents

Upload scanned copies of the required documents in the specified format (usually PDF or JPG, under 1 MB each).

### Step 7: Submit and Note the Reference Number

After submission, you will receive an application reference number. Save this for tracking your application status.

### Step 8: Track Your Application

Use the reference number to check the status on the same portal. Most states also send SMS updates to your registered mobile number.

## Step-by-Step Process: Offline

If you prefer the offline method or your state does not support online corrections:

### Step 1: Visit Your Local Ration Office (FPS or Taluk Office)

Go to the nearest Fair Price Shop (FPS) or the Food and Civil Supplies office in your district or taluk.

### Step 2: Collect the Application Form

Ask for the ration card correction form. Some offices also have forms available for download on their websites.

### Step 3: Fill the Form

Fill in your ration card number, existing details, and the corrections you want. Attach the required documents.

### Step 4: Submit the Form

Submit the completed form along with photocopies of supporting documents. Keep the acknowledgement receipt safe.

### Step 5: Verification

A food inspector may visit your home for physical verification, especially for address changes or adding members.

### Step 6: Collect Updated Card

Once approved, you will be informed to collect your updated ration card from the same office.

## Fees

- **Online corrections:** Usually **free of charge** in most states
- **Offline corrections:** Some states may charge a nominal fee of ₹5 to ₹25 for processing
- **No stamp duty** or legal charges apply

## Processing Time

| Type of Correction | Estimated Time |
|---|---|
| Name correction | 7–15 days |
| Address change | 15–30 days |
| Adding a member | 15–30 days |
| Removing a member | 7–15 days |
| Aadhaar linking | 3–7 days |

Processing times vary by state and the workload at your local office. Urban areas tend to be faster than rural regions.

## Tips for a Smooth Process

1. **Ensure your Aadhaar is updated first** — many corrections require Aadhaar as the primary proof. If your Aadhaar itself has errors, fix that first. See our guide on [Aadhaar card update](/guide/aadhaar-card-update).

2. **Keep document sizes small** — when uploading online, compress your PDFs and images to avoid upload failures.

3. **Apply through the correct state portal** — ration cards are state-issued, so always use your state's official website.

4. **Track regularly** — if your application is pending for more than 30 days, follow up at your local ration office.

5. **Use CSC centres** — if you are not comfortable with online applications, visit your nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) for assistance. They can help you file the application for a small fee.

6. **Keep old documents** — do not discard your old ration card until the new one is issued.

7. **Check NFSA portals** — verify that your corrected details reflect on the National Food Security Act portal as well.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Can I correct my ration card details online from any state?

Most states now support online corrections, but the exact features vary. States like Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Rajasthan, and Bihar have well-developed online portals. For other states, check the official food department website or visit your local ration office.

### 2. How long does a ration card name correction take?

A name correction typically takes 7 to 15 working days after submission. If physical verification is needed, it may take up to 30 days.

### 3. Can I add my newborn baby to the ration card online?

Yes, in most states you can add a newborn by submitting the birth certificate and Aadhaar enrolment slip online. The child will be added as a new member under the family head.

### 4. Is there any fee for ration card correction?

Online corrections are generally free. Offline corrections may involve a small processing fee of ₹5 to ₹25 depending on the state.

### 5. What if my ration card correction application is rejected?

If rejected, you will usually receive a reason (such as incomplete documents). You can re-apply after fixing the issue. If you believe the rejection is unfair, you can file a grievance on the state food portal or approach the District Supply Officer.

### 6. Can I change the head of family on my ration card?

Yes, you can request a change of head of family in cases of death, divorce, or family separation. You will need to submit supporting documents like a death certificate or legal separation order.

### 7. Do I need to visit the ration office even if I apply online?

In most cases, no. However, for certain changes like address modification, a food inspector may visit your home for verification. You may also need to visit the office to collect the updated card in some states.
