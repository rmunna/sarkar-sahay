# HDFC Account Balance Check: 6 Easy Ways

Checking your HDFC Bank account balance is quick and free. Whether you have a smartphone or a basic phone, there's a method for everyone. Here are 6 ways to check your HDFC Bank balance — from a simple missed call to NetBanking.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent guide and not affiliated with HDFC Bank. Verify details on the [official HDFC Bank website](https://www.hdfcbank.com/).

---

## Method 1: Missed Call (Fastest — No Internet Needed)

This is the simplest method. Works on any phone.

1. From your **registered mobile number**, give a missed call to:
   - **1800-270-3355** (toll-free) — for account balance
   - **1800-270-3355** — same number works for balance
2. The call will auto-disconnect after 1–2 rings
3. You'll receive an **SMS with your account balance** within seconds

**For Mini Statement (last 5 transactions):**
- Give a missed call to **1800-270-3355** from your registered number

> **Note:** This only works from the mobile number registered with your HDFC Bank account.

---

## Method 2: SMS Banking (No Internet Needed)

Send an SMS from your registered mobile number to **5676712**:

| SMS Format | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| `BAL` | Account balance |
| `MINI` | Mini statement (last 5 transactions) |
| `BAL <last 4 digits of account>` | Balance for specific account (if you have multiple) |

**Example:** Send `BAL` to `5676712`

You'll receive a reply SMS with your current balance within minutes. Standard SMS charges may apply.

---

## Method 3: HDFC Mobile Banking App (Most Detailed)

1. Open the **HDFC Bank Mobile Banking** app on your smartphone
2. Login with MPIN, fingerprint, or Face ID
3. Your **account balance is displayed on the dashboard** immediately after login
4. Tap on the account to see:
   - Available balance
   - Current balance
   - Recent transactions
   - Mini statement
   - Detailed statement (downloadable)

**Bonus:** The app also shows FD, RD, loan, and credit card details in one place.

If the app isn't working, see our [HDFC Mobile Banking fix guide](/guide/hdfc-mobile-banking-not-working-fix).

## Method 4: NetBanking (Full Statement Access)

1. Visit [HDFC NetBanking](https://netbanking.hdfcbank.com/)
2. Login with your Customer ID and password
3. Go to **Accounts** → **Savings Account** → **Account Summary**
4. Your balance is shown along with recent transactions
5. Download full bank statement in PDF/Excel for any date range

**First time?** Register for NetBanking by visiting an HDFC branch or using the mobile app to generate NetBanking credentials.

---

## Method 5: ATM Balance Enquiry

1. Visit any **HDFC Bank ATM** (or any bank ATM via National Financial Switch)
2. Insert your [HDFC debit card](/guide/hdfc-debit-card-not-working-fix)
3. Enter your **4-digit ATM PIN**
4. Select **"Balance Enquiry"**
5. Choose **Savings Account** or **Current Account**
6. Your balance is displayed on screen and a printed slip is optional

**Charges:**
- Free at HDFC Bank ATMs (5 transactions/month in metro, 3 in non-metro after March 2022 RBI guidelines)
- ₹21 + GST after free limit at non-HDFC ATMs

---

## Method 6: USSD Banking (*99#) — Works Without Internet

This works on any phone (even basic/feature phones) without internet:

1. Dial **\*99#** from your registered mobile number
2. Select your bank — enter **HDFC** or bank code **016**
3. Select **"Balance Enquiry"** from the menu
4. Enter your **UPI PIN** (set this up first if you haven't)
5. Your balance is displayed on screen

**To set up USSD banking:**
- You need a UPI PIN linked to your HDFC account
- Set it up via any UPI app (BHIM, Google Pay, PhonePe) first

> **Note:** USSD charges may apply (₹0.50 per session as per NPCI guidelines). Works even without smartphone or internet.

## How to Get HDFC Bank Statement

Beyond just balance, if you need a detailed statement:

1. **Mobile App:** Accounts → Statement → Select date range → Download PDF
2. **NetBanking:** Accounts → Statement → Choose format (PDF/Excel/CSV)
3. **Email Request:** Some accounts have auto-email statement facility
4. **Branch:** Visit with ID proof for stamped physical statement

---

## Important Tips

1. **Register your mobile number** — All non-internet methods (missed call, SMS, USSD) only work from the mobile number registered with your HDFC Bank account
2. **Check available balance, not current balance** — Available balance excludes any holds or cheques under clearing
3. **Use missed call for quick checks** — It's the fastest method and completely free
4. **Download statements before financial year end** — Keep yearly statements saved for ITR filing
5. **Set up balance alerts** — Via NetBanking, enable SMS alerts for every debit/credit transaction for real-time tracking

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. What is the HDFC Bank balance check missed call number?
Give a missed call to **1800-270-3355** from your registered mobile number. You'll receive an SMS with your account balance within seconds.

### Q2. How to check HDFC Bank balance without ATM card?
Use missed call (1800-270-3355), SMS (send BAL to 5676712), mobile app, NetBanking, or USSD (*99#). Only ATM requires a physical card.

### Q3. Can I check HDFC balance from a non-registered mobile number?
No, missed call and SMS methods only work from the mobile number registered with your HDFC Bank account. Use NetBanking or visit an ATM/branch instead.

### Q4. How to register mobile number for HDFC SMS banking?
Visit any HDFC Bank branch with your Aadhaar and [PAN card](/guide/pan-card-apply-online) to register or update your mobile number. This cannot be done online for security.

### Q5. Is there a charge for HDFC balance enquiry via SMS?
HDFC Bank doesn't charge for the service, but your telecom operator may charge standard SMS rates (typically ₹1–₹1.50 per SMS).

### Q6. How to check HDFC Bank mini statement?
Send `MINI` to 5676712 from your registered number, or use the mobile app/NetBanking for the last 5–10 transactions.

### Q7. Can I check HDFC FD balance via missed call?
Missed call gives only savings/current account balance. For FD balance, use the HDFC Mobile Banking app or NetBanking where all deposits are shown.
