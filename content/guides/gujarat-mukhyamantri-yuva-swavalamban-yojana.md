## What is Mukhyamantri Yuva Swavalamban Yojana (MYSY)?

Mukhyamantri Yuva Swavalamban Yojana (MYSY) is a flagship **education scholarship scheme** by the Government of Gujarat that provides financial assistance to meritorious students from economically weaker families for pursuing **higher education** (diploma, graduation, and post-graduation courses).

Launched in **2015**, MYSY covers **tuition fees, hostel expenses, and examination fees** for students who have passed Class 10 or Class 12 with good marks and whose family income is below the prescribed limit. It is one of the largest state-level scholarship programs in India.

## Scholarship Benefits

### For Diploma Courses (After Class 10)

| Component | Amount (per year) |
|---|---|
| Tuition fees | Up to ₹50,000 |
| Hostel expenses | Up to ₹10,000 |
| Books & instruments | Up to ₹5,000 |

### For Degree Courses (After Class 12)

| Component | Amount (per year) |
|---|---|
| Tuition fees | Up to ₹2,00,000 (for medical/engineering) |
| Tuition fees | Up to ₹50,000 (for other courses) |
| Hostel expenses | Up to ₹10,000 |
| Books & instruments | Up to ₹5,000 |

**Note:** The actual scholarship amount depends on the course type and college fees. Medical and engineering courses receive higher assistance.

## Who is Eligible?

### For Diploma (After Class 10)

- Must have passed **Class 10** from a Gujarat board (GSEB) or equivalent
- Minimum **80 percentile** in Class 10
- Family annual income must not exceed **₹6,00,000**
- Must be admitted to a **government or grant-in-aid diploma college** in Gujarat

### For Degree (After Class 12)

- Must have passed **Class 12** from Gujarat board (GSEB) or equivalent
- Minimum **80 percentile** in Class 12 (Science/Commerce/Arts)
- Family annual income must not exceed **₹6,00,000**
- Must be admitted to a **recognised college/university** in Gujarat
- Courses covered: Engineering, Medical, Pharmacy, Nursing, Agriculture, Science, Commerce, Arts, Law, and more

### General Conditions

- Must be a **resident of Gujarat**
- Must be studying in a **Gujarat-based institution**
- Should not be receiving scholarship from any other state government scheme
- Must maintain minimum attendance and academic performance

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Online Application (Fresh)

1. **Visit MYSY portal** — Go to [mysy.guj.nic.in](https://mysy.guj.nic.in)
2. **Register** — Click on "New Registration" and enter your Class 10/12 seat number, date of birth, and mobile number
3. **Create login** — Set up username and password
4. **Fill application form** — Enter personal details, family income, college details, course information, and bank details
5. **Upload documents** — Scan and upload marksheet, income certificate, college admission letter, Aadhaar, photo
6. **Verify details** — Review all information carefully
7. **Submit application** — Click submit; note your application ID
8. **College verification** — Your college will verify your enrollment online
9. **Income verification** — Mamlatdar office verifies your income certificate
10. **Approval** — After all verifications, the scholarship is approved

### Renewal (Existing Beneficiaries)

1. Login to **MYSY portal** with existing credentials
2. Click on **"Renewal Application"**
3. Upload **current year** college fee receipt and marksheet
4. Submit for reverification
5. Scholarship continues if academic performance is maintained

### Important Deadlines

- Fresh applications: Usually **September-November** each year
- Renewal applications: **October-December**
- Check the MYSY portal for exact dates

## Processing Time

- **Application to verification:** 1-2 months
- **Verification to disbursement:** 1-3 months
- **Total:** 3-6 months from application
- **Payment mode:** Direct transfer to student's bank account

## How to Check Status

1. Visit [mysy.guj.nic.in](https://mysy.guj.nic.in)
2. Login with your credentials
3. Click on **"Application Status"**
4. View verification status, approval status, and payment details

## Important Tips

1. **Apply in the first year itself** — Don't wait; the scholarship is available from the first year of your course
2. **Maintain academic performance** — Poor grades or backlogs may lead to scholarship discontinuation at renewal
3. **Keep attendance above minimum** — Colleges report attendance; low attendance may affect your scholarship
4. **Open a bank account early** — You need a bank account in your own name (not parents') for receiving the scholarship
5. **Apply for renewal every year** — The scholarship is not automatic for subsequent years; you must apply for renewal annually

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Can I get MYSY if I'm studying in a private college?

Yes, MYSY covers students in **government, grant-in-aid, and self-financed (private) colleges** in Gujarat, as long as the college is recognised and the course is approved.

### Q2: Is there a caste-based reservation in MYSY?

No, MYSY is **not caste-based**. It is a merit-cum-means scholarship open to all categories. However, SC/ST/OBC students can also avail separate post-matric scholarships alongside MYSY.

### Q3: What if my family income exceeds ₹6 lakh?

If your family income exceeds the limit, you are **not eligible** for MYSY. However, you may check central government scholarships or institution-specific fee waivers.

### Q4: Can students studying outside Gujarat apply?

No, you must be studying in a **Gujarat-based institution**. Students from Gujarat studying in other states cannot avail MYSY.

### Q5: Does MYSY cover MBA and MTech courses?

Yes, MYSY covers **post-graduation courses** including MBA, MTech, MSc, MCom, and others, subject to the income and merit criteria.

### Q6: What is the percentile requirement for Science stream?

For Class 12 Science stream, you typically need a minimum of **80 percentile** in the GSEB board exam. The cutoff may vary slightly each year.
