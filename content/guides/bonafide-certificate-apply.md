## What is a Bonafide Certificate?

A Bonafide Certificate is an official document certifying that a person is a **genuine (bona fide) member** of an organization — typically a student of a school/college or an employee of a company. The word "bonafide" comes from Latin meaning "in good faith."

The certificate confirms:
- The person is **currently enrolled/employed** at the institution
- Their **registration/admission details** are genuine
- They belong to the institution for the stated period

Bonafide certificates are commonly required for:
- **Student identity verification** — Bank account opening, scholarship applications
- **Travel concessions** — Railway and bus pass for students
- **Visa applications** — Proof of student/employment status
- **Competitive exam registration** — Some exams require it
- **Loan applications** — Education loans
- **Address proof** — When other proofs are unavailable
- **Internship applications**
- **Passport application** — As supporting document

## Types of Bonafide Certificates

### 1. Student Bonafide Certificate
- Issued by school, college, or university
- Confirms student is enrolled in a specific course/class
- Most commonly requested type

### 2. Employee Bonafide Certificate
- Issued by employer
- Confirms employment status, designation, and tenure
- Used for loans, visa, and official purposes

### 3. Resident Bonafide Certificate
- Issued by local authorities (tehsildar, panchayat)
- Confirms domicile/residence in a particular area
- Used for admissions, government schemes — similar to [domicile certificate](/guide/domicile-certificate-apply-online)

## How to Get a Student Bonafide Certificate

### From School
1. Write an **application to the Principal** requesting a bonafide certificate
2. State the purpose (bank account, travel pass, scholarship, etc.)
3. Submit through class teacher
4. School office issues the certificate within **1-3 days**
5. Usually free or nominal fee (₹10-₹50)

### From College/University
1. Visit the **college administration/office**
2. Fill the bonafide certificate request form
3. Pay the fee (if applicable) — ₹20-₹100
4. Certificate issued within **1-7 days**
5. Some colleges offer **online requests** through their student portal

### Online (Where Available)
Some universities and colleges allow online bonafide requests:
1. Log in to the student portal/ERP
2. Navigate to **Certificate Requests** → **Bonafide Certificate**
3. Select purpose and submit
4. Download after approval or collect from office

## How to Get an Employee Bonafide Certificate

1. Submit a written/email request to **HR department**
2. Mention the purpose and details needed
3. HR verifies your employment records
4. Certificate issued on **company letterhead** within 1-5 days
5. Usually free of charge

## Documents Required

### For Student Certificate
- **Student ID card** or admission receipt
- **Application letter** stating purpose
- **Previous mark sheets** (sometimes)
- **Parent/guardian ID** (for minors)

### For Employee Certificate
- **Employee ID** or joining letter
- **Request letter** to HR
- **Purpose document** (if applying for visa, loan, etc.)

## Bonafide Certificate Format

### Student Bonafide Certificate
```
[Institution Letterhead]

BONAFIDE CERTIFICATE

This is to certify that [Student Name], S/o or D/o [Father's/Mother's Name],
bearing Roll No. [Number] / Registration No. [Number], is a bonafide student
of this institution.

He/She is currently studying in Class/Year [X] of [Course Name] during
the academic year [Year].

This certificate is issued at his/her request for the purpose of [Purpose].

Date: [Date]
Place: [Place]

[Signature]
[Name and Designation]
Principal/Registrar
[Institution Name]
[Official Seal]
```

### Employee Bonafide Certificate
```
[Company Letterhead]

BONAFIDE CERTIFICATE

This is to certify that [Employee Name], Employee ID [Number],
is a bonafide employee of [Company Name] since [Date of Joining].

He/She is currently designated as [Designation] in the [Department] department.

His/Her current monthly gross salary is ₹[Amount] (if required to mention).

This certificate is issued at his/her request for the purpose of [Purpose].

Date: [Date]

[Signature]
[Name and Designation]
HR Manager/Authorized Signatory
[Company Name]
[Official Seal]
```

## Common Uses in Detail

### For Bank Account Opening
- Students need bonafide to open a bank account in the college city
- Banks accept it as identity and address verification
- Some banks accept the student ID card instead

### For Railway/Bus Concession Pass
- Students get discounted travel on trains and buses
- Bonafide from the institution is mandatory
- Valid for the academic year
- Significant savings on daily commute

### For Scholarships
- Scholarship portals like [NSP](/guide/national-scholarship-portal) require bonafide as part of the application
- Confirms genuine student status
- Must be for the current academic year

### For Education Loan
- Banks require bonafide for education loan processing
- Confirms admission and course details
- See our guide on education-related financial planning

### For Passport Application
- Bonafide can be used as supporting document for passport — see our [passport guide](/guide/passport-apply-online)
- Especially useful for students studying away from home
- Confirms current address and student status

### For Visa Application
- Foreign embassies may require bonafide for student visa
- Confirms enrollment status and expected duration
- Must be recent (within 3 months)

## Important Tips

1. **Specify the purpose clearly** in your application — Some institutions customize the certificate based on the use
2. **Request in advance** — Don't wait till the last day; allow 3-7 days for processing
3. **Keep copies** — Multiple organizations may need the bonafide simultaneously
4. **Check if original or copy is needed** — Some organizations accept photocopies, others need the original
5. **For travel concessions**, get a fresh bonafide each academic year — Railway authorities check validity

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is a bonafide certificate the same as a character certificate?
No. A bonafide certificate confirms your **genuine association** with an institution. A [character certificate](/guide/character-certificate-apply) certifies your **moral conduct and criminal record status** from the police.

### Can I get a bonafide certificate after passing out?
After completing your course, you typically can't get a current bonafide. However, you can request a certificate stating you **were a bonafide student** during the specified period. Contact your institution's alumni or administrative office.

### Is a bonafide certificate valid as address proof?
Some organizations accept it as address proof, especially for students living in hostels or college cities. However, it's not universally accepted — Aadhaar, voter ID, and utility bills are stronger address proofs.

### Can the institution refuse to issue a bonafide certificate?
If you're a genuine student/employee with no pending dues, the institution cannot refuse. If your fees are unpaid, they may withhold it until dues are cleared.

### How many bonafide certificates can I get?
There's no limit. You can request as many as needed. Each request may incur a small fee at some institutions.

### Is an online bonafide certificate valid?
If it's generated through the institution's official portal with digital signatures or QR code verification, it's valid. Some organizations may still require a physically signed copy.
