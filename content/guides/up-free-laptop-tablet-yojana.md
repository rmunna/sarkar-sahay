## What is UP Free Laptop/Tablet Yojana?

The Uttar Pradesh government launched the **Free Laptop and Tablet Distribution Scheme** (also known as DigiShakti or UP Free Laptop/Tablet Yojana) to promote digital literacy among students and youth. Under this scheme, the UP government distributes **free tablets and smartphones** to students who have passed their graduation, post-graduation, diploma, or equivalent courses from UP-recognized institutions.

The scheme was initially announced by CM Yogi Adityanath with a budget of ₹3,000 crore to distribute devices to over **1 crore youth** across the state. The devices come pre-loaded with educational content and government service apps.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Education:** Must have passed Class 12, graduation, post-graduation, diploma, ITI, or equivalent from a UP-recognized institution
- **Domicile:** Must be a resident of Uttar Pradesh
- **Age:** No strict age limit, but must be a current student or recent graduate
- **Income:** Annual family income should not exceed ₹2 lakh (priority given to economically weaker sections)
- **Institution:** Must be enrolled in or recently passed from a DIOS/AICTE/UGC recognized institution in UP

**Priority categories:**
- Students from SC/ST/OBC/EWS categories
- Students from rural areas
- Female students (given preference)
- Students with disabilities

## What Do You Get?

| Device | Specifications (Approximate) |
|--------|------------------------------|
| Tablet | 10.1-inch display, 2GB RAM, 32GB storage, 4G SIM support |
| Smartphone | Basic Android smartphone with 4G connectivity |

**Pre-loaded content:**
- DIKSHA educational app
- UP government service apps (eDistrict, UMANG)
- Digital literacy content
- Free internet data for initial period

> **Note:** The government has distributed tablets/smartphones in various phases. The exact device specifications may vary by distribution batch.

## How to Apply / Register — Step-by-Step

### Online Registration

1. **Visit DigiShakti portal:** Go to [digishakti.up.gov.in](https://digishakti.up.gov.in)
2. **Check eligibility:** The portal may display eligible student lists uploaded by universities/institutions
3. **Institution-based registration:** In most phases, institutions submit the list of eligible students directly. Students do not need to apply individually.
4. **Verify your name:** Check the beneficiary list on the portal using your enrollment number or Aadhaar number
5. **Distribution event:** If your name is in the list, you'll receive SMS notification about the distribution date and venue
6. **Collect device:** Attend the distribution event with original documents for verification

### How to Check Your Name in the Beneficiary List

1. Go to [digishakti.up.gov.in](https://digishakti.up.gov.in)
2. Click on "विद्यार्थी अनुभाग" (Student Section)
3. Enter your registration/enrollment number
4. Check your eligibility and distribution status

> **Important:** In most phases, the selection is done by the university/institution, not by individual application. Contact your college/university administration if your name is not in the list.

## Distribution Process

| Phase | Target Group | Status |
|-------|-------------|--------|
| Phase 1 | Post-graduate and graduate students | Completed |
| Phase 2 | Intermediate (Class 12) pass students | Ongoing in select districts |
| Phase 3 | Diploma / ITI / Skill course students | Announced |

The distribution happens in **district-wise camps** organized by the District Magistrate's office in coordination with educational institutions.

## Fees

The scheme is **completely free**. There is no registration fee, processing fee, or any hidden charges.

> **⚠️ Beware of fraud:** If anyone asks you for money to get a free laptop/tablet under this scheme, it is a scam. Report such cases to the nearest police station or call the UP helpline 1076.

## Important Tips

1. **Keep your Aadhaar updated** — Your mobile number must be linked with Aadhaar for OTP verification during device collection.
2. **Contact your institution** — Most selections are institution-based. Ensure your college has submitted your details to the government portal.
3. **Check the portal regularly** — New phases and lists are updated on the DigiShakti portal periodically.
4. **Don't fall for scams** — The government does NOT ask for any fee or payment. Avoid fake websites and WhatsApp messages.
5. **Carry original documents** — You'll need to show originals at the distribution event for identity verification.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. How can I apply for the UP Free Laptop/Tablet Yojana?

In most cases, you **don't need to apply individually**. Your educational institution submits the list of eligible students. Check your name on the DigiShakti portal or contact your college administration.

### Q2. What is the income limit for this scheme?

Priority is given to students with **annual family income below ₹2 lakh**, but the exact cutoff may vary by distribution phase. SC/ST/OBC students may have relaxed criteria.

### Q3. Will I get a laptop or a tablet?

The government primarily distributes **tablets and smartphones** (not laptops) under the current phases of the scheme. The name "laptop yojana" is popularly used, but the actual devices are tablets.

### Q4. I passed Class 12 this year. Am I eligible?

Yes, Class 12 pass students from UP Board, CBSE, and ICSE (studying in UP) are eligible. Your school/institution should submit your name for the scheme.

### Q5. Can private college students apply?

Yes, students from **AICTE/UGC recognized private institutions** in UP are also eligible, provided their institution has submitted the student data on the government portal.

### Q6. When will the next distribution happen?

The distribution happens in phases, district by district. Check the DigiShakti portal or follow UP government announcements for the latest schedule.

### Q7. My name is not in the list. What should I do?

Contact your college/university administration and ask them to verify your data submission on the government portal. You can also visit the District Magistrate's office for help.
