# PM Kisan 21st Installment 2026 — When Will It Come & How to Check

The **PM Kisan Samman Nidhi** scheme gives ₹6,000/year to eligible farmers in 3 installments of ₹2,000 each. The **21st installment** is expected in **July–August 2026** (the 20th installment was released in February 2026).

> **Quick answer:** Check your status at [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in) → "Know Your Status" → enter Aadhaar or mobile. If it shows "Payment Success" you'll receive it within 2–3 days of release.

## How to Check PM Kisan 21st Installment Status

**Step 1:** Go to [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in)

**Step 2:** Click **"Farmers Corner"** → **"Beneficiary Status"**

**Step 3:** Enter your **Aadhaar number** or **registered mobile number** or **account number**

**Step 4:** Click **"Get Data"**

**What the status means:**

| Status shown | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Payment Success | Money sent — check your bank in 2–3 days |
| FTO is Generated | Payment order raised, bank processing |
| Rft Signed | Funds approved, awaiting bank credit |
| PFMS Transferred | Money transferred to bank, check statement |
| Pending for Approval | Application under review |
| Rejected | Check reason — fix and re-apply |

## 21st Installment Not Received — Fix by Reason

### 1. e-KYC Not Done / Expired
e-KYC is mandatory. If not done, installments are blocked.

- **OTP-based e-KYC:** pmkisan.gov.in → e-KYC → enter Aadhaar → OTP verify
- **Biometric e-KYC:** Nearest CSC center (if OTP fails due to mobile mismatch)
- After e-KYC, the next installment releases automatically — you won't get skipped ones

### 2. Land Seeding / Aadhar Seeding Pending
Your land records must be linked to your Aadhaar in your state's land database.

- Contact your **Patwari / Lekhpal / Village Revenue Officer**
- Or apply at the nearest CSC or Jan Seva Kendra

### 3. Bank Account Not Linked to Aadhaar (DBT Issue)
The ₹2,000 transfers via DBT — your bank account must be Aadhaar-linked.

- Visit your bank branch with Aadhaar → request Aadhaar seeding
- Check: pmkisan.gov.in → "Beneficiary Status" → look for "Account Validation Failed"

### 4. Name/Details Mismatch
If your name in PM Kisan records doesn't match Aadhaar, payment blocks.

- Visit your nearest PM Kisan help desk (Agriculture Department office)
- Or call PM Kisan helpline: **155261 / 011-24300606**

### 5. Application Rejected
Check the rejection reason in "Beneficiary Status". Common reasons:
- Not a farmer (ineligible occupation)
- Land not in your name
- Income tax filer
- Government employee

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Who is NOT eligible for PM Kisan?**
Income tax payers, government employees (central/state), retired pensioners earning ₹10,000+/month, constitutional post holders, and families of former CM/PM are excluded.

**Can I get missed installments credited later?**
No — once an installment window closes and your status was "pending", that installment is lost. Only future ones can be received after fixing the issue.

**Will I get the 21st installment if I just did e-KYC now?**
Yes, if e-KYC is done before the installment release date, you should receive it. After release, it typically takes 3–5 working days to credit.

**How to check which installments I've received?**
pmkisan.gov.in → "Beneficiary Status" → scroll down to payment history — shows all installments credited with dates.
