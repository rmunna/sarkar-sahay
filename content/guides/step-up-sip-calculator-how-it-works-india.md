# Step-Up SIP India 2026 — Complete Guide with Calculator Examples

A step-up SIP (also called top-up SIP) automatically increases your SIP amount by a fixed percentage or amount each year. Most mutual fund investors stick to the same SIP amount for years — step-up fixes this by letting your investment grow alongside your income.

## Why Step-Up SIP Makes a Massive Difference

The math is stark. Starting with ₹5,000/month:

| Strategy | 20 Years @ 12% CAGR | Corpus |
|----------|---------------------|--------|
| Flat SIP: ₹5,000/month | ₹5,000 every month for 20 years | **₹49.9 lakh** |
| 10% step-up SIP | Starts ₹5,000, increases 10% each year | **₹1.10 crore** |
| 15% step-up SIP | Starts ₹5,000, increases 15% each year | **₹1.69 crore** |

The corpus more than doubles just by adding a 10% annual increase — from ₹49.9L to ₹1.10 crore. The extra investment from the step-ups is roughly ₹25–30L total, but the compounding on that extra generates ₹60L+.

## How to Start a Step-Up SIP

### On Groww

1. Open Groww app → Explore → Mutual Funds
2. Select the fund you want
3. Tap **Start SIP**
4. On the SIP setup screen, look for **"Step-up SIP"** or **"Top-up SIP"** toggle
5. Select percentage (5%, 10%, 15%, 20%, 25%, 50%) or custom amount
6. Set frequency: **Annual** (most common)
7. Complete the mandate via UPI or net banking

**Groww shows step-up in percentage increments only** (not fixed amount). 10% is the most popular option.

### On Zerodha (Coin)

1. Open Coin app (Zerodha's MF platform) → Mutual Funds
2. Select fund → Buy
3. Choose **SIP**
4. Look for **"Step-up amount"** field
5. Enter the fixed amount to increase each year (e.g., ₹500/month increase per year)
6. Zerodha uses **fixed amount step-up** (not percentage) — note this difference
7. Set date and complete mandate

### On Angel One / Paytm Money / ET Money

Similar to Groww — step-up percentage is configurable during SIP setup. Usually 10%, 20%, or 25% annual options.

### On MF Central (Direct Plans)

1. Visit [mfcentral.com](https://www.mfcentral.com)
2. Register / login with PAN + OTP
3. New Purchase → SIP
4. Enable **"Top-up SIP"** option
5. Set percentage and frequency

MF Central is the unified platform run by SEBI where you can manage all mutual fund folios across AMCs from one place.

### Directly on AMC Websites

Most AMCs (Axis, Mirae, Nippon, UTI, HDFC, SBI, Kotak) support step-up SIP on their own portals. Look for "SIP Top-up" or "Booster SIP" during the SIP setup screen.

## How Step-Up SIP Works Mechanically

1. You set up a standard SIP mandate (say ₹5,000/month)
2. One year after the first installment date, the SIP amount automatically increases to ₹5,500 (10% step-up)
3. The following year, it becomes ₹6,050 (10% of ₹5,500)
4. Your bank mandate is auto-upgraded — some platforms require a new mandate for the higher amount (check with your broker)

**Compounding of step-ups**: With 10% annual step-up, the amount compounds:
- Year 1: ₹5,000/month
- Year 2: ₹5,500/month
- Year 3: ₹6,050/month
- Year 4: ₹6,655/month
- Year 10: ₹11,789/month

## Step-Up % — Which to Choose?

| Your Situation | Recommended Step-Up |
|---------------|---------------------|
| Salaried with 8–12% annual increment | 10% |
| High-growth career (early in career, tech) | 15–20% |
| Stable income, limited growth | 5–7% |
| Business income (variable) | Fixed amount increase (e.g., ₹500/year) |

**Rule of thumb**: Set step-up to roughly match your expected annual salary increment. If you get 10% raise, step up SIP by 10% — net investment burden stays constant as a % of income.

## Step-Up SIP vs Lumpsum Investment vs Regular SIP

| Strategy | Suited For | Return Potential | Discipline Required |
|----------|-----------|-----------------|---------------------|
| Regular SIP | All investors | Good | Low |
| Step-up SIP | Growing income earners | Better | Medium |
| Lumpsum | Windfall (bonus, inheritance) | Best (timing-dependent) | High |
| SIP + occasional lumpsum | Optimal | Highest | Medium |

Step-up SIP is the most practical for salaried professionals — it auto-adjusts investment as income grows without requiring any annual decision.

## Tax on Step-Up SIP Returns

Step-up SIP units are subject to the same tax rules as regular SIP:

- **Equity funds**: Each SIP installment has its own purchase date. Units held >12 months → LTCG 12.5% above ₹1.25L annual exemption. Units sold within 12 months → STCG 20%.
- **Debt funds**: Taxed at slab rate regardless of holding period (Budget 2023 removed indexation).
- **ELSS (tax-saving fund)**: 3-year lock-in per installment; LTCG 12.5% above ₹1.25L. But 80C deduction of ₹1.5L/year on old regime.

Step-up SIP increases the total amount invested — your 80C limit (₹1.5L) stays the same for ELSS regardless of how much you invest via step-up.

For detailed SIP taxation, see our [mutual fund tax guide](/guide/mutual-fund-taxation-complete-guide-india-2026).

## Can You Pause or Modify Step-Up SIP?

Yes, most platforms allow:
- **Pause step-up**: Keep the current amount without further annual increases
- **Modify step-up %**: Change the increment percentage
- **Cancel step-up**: Revert to fixed SIP (your current amount continues as-is)

To modify, go to your broker's SIP management section → select the SIP → edit step-up settings.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a step-up SIP?

A step-up SIP (top-up SIP) automatically increases your monthly SIP amount by a set percentage (e.g., 10%) every year. A ₹5,000/month SIP with 10% annual step-up becomes ₹5,500 in year 2, ₹6,050 in year 3, and so on.

### Is step-up SIP available on all mutual funds?

Step-up SIP is a feature of the platform (broker or AMC), not the mutual fund itself. Any mutual fund that supports regular SIP can have a step-up feature — provided your platform (Groww, Zerodha, etc.) supports it. Direct plans via MF Central or AMC websites also support it.

### Does step-up SIP need a new mandate every year?

It depends on the platform. Groww and most major platforms set up an enhanced mandate upfront (with maximum expected amount). Zerodha may require a new mandate when the fixed step-up amount pushes you beyond the original mandate limit. Check with your specific platform.

### What's the difference between step-up and top-up SIP?

They're the same thing — different AMCs/platforms use different terminology. "Step-up SIP" and "Top-up SIP" and "Booster SIP" all refer to the same mechanism of annual automatic increase.

### Should I do step-up SIP in ELSS or index funds?

Both. Use step-up SIP in ELSS up to ₹1.5L/year (for 80C benefit on old regime). Beyond that, step-up SIP in a Nifty 50 or Nifty 500 index fund is the most straightforward long-term wealth builder. See our [index fund guide](/guide/index-fund-investing-guide-india).

### Can step-up SIP be done in lumpsum intervals too?

Step-up SIP is specifically for systematic/monthly investments. For lumpsum investing at year-end bonus time, use a regular purchase (non-SIP) in your mutual fund folio.

### ₹5,000 SIP step-up 10% — what's the corpus in 20 years?

At 12% CAGR with 10% annual step-up starting at ₹5,000/month: approximately **₹1.10 crore** in 20 years. Compare to flat ₹5,000/month SIP which gives ₹49.9 lakh — the step-up more than doubles the outcome.
