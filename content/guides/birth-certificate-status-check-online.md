## What is Birth Certificate Status Check?

After applying for a birth certificate — either online through the CRSORGI (Civil Registration System) portal or offline at your local municipal office — you can track the status of your application online. The CRSORGI portal ([crsorgi.gov.in](https://crsorgi.gov.in)) is India's central system for birth and death registration, used by most states and UTs.

A birth certificate is a vital document required for school admissions, [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online), passport, and various government services.

## How to Check Birth Certificate Status Online (CRSORGI Portal)

### Step-by-Step Process

1. Visit the official portal: [https://crsorgi.gov.in](https://crsorgi.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Track Application Status"** or **"Status Check"** on the homepage
3. Enter your **Registration Number** or **Application ID** (received at the time of application)
4. Enter your **Date of Birth** (of the person whose certificate is being tracked)
5. Fill in the **captcha code**
6. Click **"Search"** or **"Track"**
7. The current status of your application will be displayed — Pending, Under Review, Approved, or Ready for Collection

### Check by Registration Number

- The registration number is printed on the acknowledgement slip received from the hospital, municipal corporation, or online portal
- Format varies by state (e.g., MH/2024/123456 for Maharashtra)

## Check Birth Certificate Status by Mobile Number

1. Visit [https://crsorgi.gov.in](https://crsorgi.gov.in)
2. If your mobile number was linked during registration, select **"Search by Mobile Number"**
3. Enter your **registered mobile number**
4. You will receive an **OTP** for verification
5. After OTP verification, all applications linked to your number will be shown with their current status

## Check Birth Certificate Status by Aadhaar Number

Some state portals allow tracking by Aadhaar:

1. Visit your state's birth registration portal (see state-wise portals below)
2. Select **"Search by Aadhaar"** option (if available)
3. Enter the **Aadhaar number** of the parent or applicant
4. Verify via OTP sent to Aadhaar-linked mobile
5. View the application status

> **Note:** Aadhaar-based search is available only in states that have integrated Aadhaar with birth registration.

## Check Birth Certificate Status by SMS

Currently, there is **no universal SMS service** for birth certificate status checks in India. However:

- Some municipal corporations send **automatic SMS updates** to the registered mobile number when the certificate is ready
- You can also call the helpline number for your area to check the status

## Common Issues and Fixes

### 1. Application Status Shows "Pending" for Weeks
- **Reason:** The hospital or registrar may not have forwarded the registration to the municipal office
- **Fix:** Contact the hospital where the birth was registered and ask them to confirm submission. Also visit or call your local municipal office

### 2. Cannot Find Application on CRSORGI Portal
- **Reason:** Some states or municipalities haven't fully migrated to CRSORGI
- **Fix:** Try your state-specific portal (see table above). If not found, visit the municipal office in person with the acknowledgement slip

### 3. Birth Certificate Shows Wrong Details (Name, Date, Parent Names)
- **Reason:** Data entry error at the time of registration
- **Fix:** Apply for correction/amendment at the municipal office. Submit an affidavit and supporting documents (hospital discharge summary, [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))

### 4. Delayed Registration (Birth Not Registered Within 21 Days)
- **Reason:** Late registration requires additional approvals — from the local registrar (21 days–1 year) or a magistrate (after 1 year)
- **Fix:** Submit a late registration application with an affidavit, hospital records, and school certificate (if applicable). Fees may apply

### 5. Unable to Download Certificate from Portal
- **Reason:** Certificate may not be digitally signed yet or the portal may have technical issues
- **Fix:** Wait 2–3 days and try again. If still unavailable, collect the physical copy from your municipal office

## Important Tips

1. **Register the birth within 21 days** to avoid late registration fees and additional paperwork
2. **Keep the hospital discharge summary** — it is the primary proof for birth registration
3. **Save the acknowledgement slip** with registration/application number for tracking
4. **Link Aadhaar** during registration for easier tracking and future verification
5. **Download and save the digital certificate** as soon as it becomes available on the portal

## FAQs

### How long does it take to get a birth certificate in India?
If the birth is registered within 21 days, the certificate is usually issued within 7–15 working days. Late registrations take longer due to additional verification.

### Can I check birth certificate status without a registration number?
On some portals, you can search by the child's name, date of birth, parent's name, or mobile number. However, the registration number gives the most accurate result.

### Is birth certificate status check free?
Yes, checking the status online is completely free on CRSORGI and all state portals. You only pay for downloading or collecting certified copies.

### Can I get a birth certificate online without visiting any office?
In many states, you can apply and receive the birth certificate entirely online through CRSORGI or the state portal. However, some states still require you to collect the physical copy.

### What if my birth was not registered at all?
You can apply for late registration at your local municipal office. You'll need an affidavit, hospital records, school records, and identity proof. A magistrate's order may be required for births registered after 1 year.

### Can NRIs get a birth certificate from India online?
Yes, NRIs can apply through the CRSORGI portal or contact the Indian embassy/consulate. The process may require a local representative to visit the municipal office if physical verification is needed.

### How do I get a birth certificate corrected?
Apply for an amendment at the issuing municipal office. Submit supporting documents like hospital records, [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online), passport, or school certificate along with an affidavit.
