## What is Punjab Aashirwad Scheme?

The Punjab Aashirwad scheme (also known as the Shagun scheme) provides **one-time financial assistance** to economically weaker families for the marriage of their daughters. The scheme aims to reduce the financial burden on poor families during their daughter's wedding and promote simple marriages.

The amount of assistance varies based on the applicant's category:

| Category | Assistance Amount |
|----------|------------------|
| SC/ST families | ₹51,000 |
| BPL families (General/OBC) | ₹31,000 |
| Widows/Destitute women's daughters | ₹51,000 |
| Divorced women (remarriage) | ₹31,000 |

## Who is Eligible?

### Eligibility Criteria
- The family must be a **resident of Punjab**
- Family annual income should not exceed **₹1,00,000** for general, or as per BPL criteria
- For SC/ST benefits, valid caste certificate is required
- The bride must be at least **18 years** of age
- The groom must be at least **21 years** of age
- The marriage must be the **first marriage** of the daughter (remarriage eligible only for widows/divorcees)
- Only **two daughters per family** can avail this benefit

### Who is NOT Eligible
- Families with annual income exceeding the limit
- Second/subsequent marriages (except for widows/divorcees)
- Third daughter onwards from the same family
- Non-residents of Punjab

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** of the bride and her parents
- **Residence proof** — voter ID, ration card, or electricity bill
- **Income certificate** from the Tehsildar/SDM office
- **Caste certificate** (for SC/ST applicants)
- **BPL certificate** (for BPL category applicants)
- **Age proof of bride** (10th marksheet, birth certificate)
- **Marriage invitation card** (for pre-marriage application) or **Marriage certificate** (for post-marriage application)
- **Bank account details** of the bride or her parent
- **Photograph of the bride**
- **Widow certificate / death certificate** of husband (for widow category)

## How to Apply

### Online Application
1. Visit [edistrict.punjabgovt.gov.in](https://edistrict.punjabgovt.gov.in)
2. Register and log in to the e-District portal
3. Navigate to Social Security → Aashirwad/Shagun scheme
4. Fill in the application form with bride's and family details
5. Upload all required documents
6. Submit the application
7. Note the application reference number

### Offline Application
1. Visit the **District Social Welfare Office** or **Tehsil Welfare Office**
2. Collect the Aashirwad scheme application form
3. Fill in all details and attach document photocopies
4. Submit at least **15 days before the marriage** (pre-marriage) or **within 6 months after marriage** (post-marriage)
5. Collect the acknowledgment receipt

### Application Timeline
- **Pre-marriage application:** Submit at least 15 days before the scheduled marriage date
- **Post-marriage application:** Submit within 6 months of the marriage date
- Applications submitted after 6 months of marriage may be rejected

## Verification and Disbursement

1. Application received at District Social Welfare Office
2. Field verification by designated officer
3. Documents verified with issuing authorities
4. Approval by District Social Welfare Officer
5. Payment disbursed through cheque or DBT within 30-60 days

## Important Tips

- **Apply early** — submit your application at least a month before the wedding for pre-marriage disbursement
- **Income certificate must be current** — get a fresh income certificate from the Tehsildar, not more than 6 months old
- **Keep marriage proof ready** — for post-marriage applications, get the marriage certificate registered
- **Two daughters only** — plan accordingly as only two daughters per family can avail this benefit
- **SC/ST families get higher amount** — ensure you have a valid caste certificate to claim ₹51,000

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I apply after the marriage has taken place?
Yes, you can apply within 6 months after the marriage date. However, applying before the marriage is recommended for timely disbursement.

### What if my income certificate shows more than ₹1,00,000?
You will not be eligible for the scheme. Ensure that the income certificate accurately reflects your family income.

### Can the groom's family apply for this scheme?
No, only the bride's family can apply. The assistance is meant to support the bride's family with marriage expenses.

### Is this scheme available for inter-caste marriages?
Yes, inter-caste marriages are eligible. In fact, the Punjab government may provide additional assistance for inter-caste marriages under a separate scheme.

### Can NRI families avail this scheme?
The family must be a resident of Punjab. If the bride's family is based in Punjab and meets all criteria, they can apply regardless of where the groom is located.

### What documents are needed for a widow's daughter?
In addition to standard documents, you need the death certificate of the deceased husband and a widow certificate from the competent authority.
