# KPSC FDA SDA Syllabus & Exam Pattern 2026 — Complete Topic-Wise Guide

The **KPSC FDA/SDA exam** is one of the most competitive Karnataka state government recruitment exams. Understanding the syllabus and exam pattern is the first step to cracking it.

> **Quick answer:** KPSC FDA/SDA has 2 written papers — Paper 1 is Kannada language (50 marks, 1 hour) and Paper 2 is General Studies (100 marks, 2 hours). Both are objective type. FDA requires a degree, SDA requires PUC pass.

---

## FDA vs SDA — Key Difference

| Feature | FDA (First Division Assistant) | SDA (Second Division Assistant) |
|---|---|---|
| **Qualification** | Bachelor's degree in any subject | PUC (10+2) pass |
| **Starting salary** | ₹27,650/month (pay level 8) | ₹21,400/month (pay level 4) |
| **Work** | Clerical + supervisory duties | Clerical/data entry duties |
| **Exam papers** | Same pattern as SDA | Same pattern as FDA |

## KPSC FDA SDA Exam Pattern 2026

### Paper 1 — Kannada Language (Mandatory for all)

| Component | Marks | Time |
|---|---|---|
| General Kannada | 50 | 1 hour |
| **Total** | **50** | **1 hour** |

- Objective type (MCQ)
- Questions on grammar, comprehension, vocabulary, proverbs
- Minimum qualifying marks: Usually 17–20 (varies by year)

### Paper 2 — General Studies

| Component | Marks | Time |
|---|---|---|
| General Studies & Current Affairs | 100 | 2 hours |
| **Total** | **100** | **2 hours** |

- Objective type (MCQ)
- No negative marking (confirm in official notification)
- Combined score (Paper 1 + Paper 2) determines merit list

## KPSC FDA SDA Syllabus — Topic-Wise

### Paper 1: Kannada Language (50 Marks)

| Topic | Expected Questions |
|---|---|
| Grammar (Vyakarana) | 10–12 |
| Comprehension passage | 8–10 |
| Vocabulary / synonyms / antonyms | 8–10 |
| Fill in the blanks | 5–8 |
| Proverbs and idioms | 5–6 |
| Letter / official writing basics | 3–5 |

### Paper 2: General Studies (100 Marks)

**History (10–15 marks)**
- Indian national movement (1857–1947)
- Karnataka history — Hyder Ali, Tipu Sultan, Mysore Kingdom
- Post-independence events

**Geography (10–15 marks)**
- Indian geography — rivers, mountains, climate
- Karnataka geography — districts, rivers (Cauvery, Krishna, Tungabhadra)
- Economic geography

**Indian Constitution & Polity (15–20 marks)**
- Fundamental rights and duties
- Directive principles
- Parliament, President, Governor
- Panchayati Raj & local bodies
- Karnataka state administration

**Economy (10–12 marks)**
- Indian economy basics
- Budget, GDP, inflation
- Five-year plans (historical)
- Karnataka economy

**Science & Technology (10–12 marks)**
- Basic Physics, Chemistry, Biology
- Inventions and discoveries
- Space (ISRO) and nuclear programs
- IT / Computer basics

**Current Affairs (15–20 marks)**
- National events (last 12 months)
- Karnataka government schemes and programs
- Sports achievements
- Awards and honours

**Environment & Ecology (5–8 marks)**
- National parks in Karnataka
- Environmental issues
- Climate change basics

**Miscellaneous (5–10 marks)**
- Karnataka culture, festivals, folk arts
- Important personalities from Karnataka

## Preparation Strategy

### Kannada Paper (Paper 1)
- Study 10th and PUC Kannada grammar books
- Read **Prajavani or Vijaya Karnataka** daily for language exposure
- Practice previous year question papers — pattern repeats

### General Studies (Paper 2)
- **History:** NCERT Class 6–10, Karnataka History by Dr. Suryanath Kamath
- **Polity:** NCERT Class 11 (Indian Constitution at Work) + M. Laxmikanth basics
- **Current Affairs:** Last 12 months — focus on Karnataka events
- **Geography:** NCERT Class 9–10 Geography

### Best Books for KPSC FDA SDA 2026

| Subject | Book |
|---|---|
| Kannada | KPSC Kannada Grammar by Chandrashekhar |
| General Studies | KPSC FDA SDA Guide (any Karnataka publisher) |
| Current Affairs | Monthly magazines + Prajavani clippings |
| Previous Papers | KPSC question paper bank (available at book shops in Bengaluru) |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is there a negative marking in KPSC FDA SDA exam?**
No negative marking as per recent notifications. Confirm in the official notification before the exam.

**How many times can I appear for KPSC FDA SDA?**
There is no attempt limit — you can appear until you exceed the age limit (usually 18–35 years; 18–40 for OBC; 18–43 for SC/ST).

**Is Kannada medium mandatory?**
Paper 1 is Kannada language. Paper 2 can be answered in English or Kannada — your choice.
