## What is the UMANG App?

UMANG (Unified Mobile Application for New-age Governance) is the Government of India's super-app that provides access to **1,700+ government services** from **200+ departments** through a single mobile application. Developed by MeitY and NeGD, UMANG eliminates the need to visit multiple government websites or offices.

From checking your PF balance to filing income tax, from paying utility bills to tracking your passport — UMANG brings India's major government services to your smartphone.

## How to Register

### Step 1: Download the App
- **Android:** Download from Google Play Store (search "UMANG")
- **iOS:** Download from Apple App Store
- **Web:** Visit [web.umang.gov.in](https://web.umang.gov.in)

### Step 2: Register
1. Open the app and click **"Register"**
2. Enter your **mobile number**
3. Verify through **OTP**
4. Set a **4-digit MPIN** for security
5. Optionally link your **Aadhaar** for enhanced services
6. Registration is complete — you can now access services

- **Check PF balance** — view your EPF passbook

- **Raise grievance** — file complaints about PF
- **KYC status** — check Aadhaar/bank seeding status

## How to Use Key Services

### Checking PF Balance on UMANG

1. Open UMANG app → Search **"EPFO"**
2. Select **"View Passbook"**
3. Enter your **UAN number**
4. Verify through OTP sent to your UAN-registered mobile
5. View your EPF balance and transaction history

### Tracking Passport Application

1. Open UMANG → Search **"Passport Seva"**
2. Select **"Track Application Status"**
3. Enter your **file number** and **date of birth**
4. View current status with timeline

### Paying Utility Bills

1. Open UMANG → Go to **"Pay Bills"** section
2. Select the utility type (electricity, water, gas)
3. Choose your **service provider** and enter consumer number
4. View bill amount
5. Pay through UPI, debit card, or net banking

### Checking PM-KISAN Status

1. Open UMANG → Search **"PM-KISAN"**
2. Select **"Beneficiary Status"**
3. Enter your **Aadhaar number** or **mobile number**
4. View payment status and installment history

## Important Tips

- **Link your Aadhaar** — many services require Aadhaar verification; linking it upfront saves time
- **EPFO services are the most used** — PF balance check and claim tracking are killer features; set up your UAN
- **Bill payment through UMANG** — consolidate all utility bill payments; it supports most providers through Bharat Bill Payment
- **Check before visiting offices** — most status checks (passport, PF, pension) can be done on UMANG without office visits
- **Keep the app updated** — new services are added frequently; update to access the latest features

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is UMANG app free?
Yes, UMANG is completely free to download and use. Standard data charges from your mobile operator apply.

### Is UMANG safe for financial transactions?
Yes, UMANG uses government-grade encryption, OTP verification, and MPIN authentication. It's developed by the National e-Governance Division under MeitY.

### Can I withdraw PF through UMANG?
You can check PF balance and track claims on UMANG. For actual PF withdrawal, you need to use the EPFO member portal or submit a claim through your employer.

### Does UMANG work on all phones?
UMANG works on Android (5.0+) and iOS (11+). It also has a web version at [web.umang.gov.in](https://web.umang.gov.in) accessible from any browser.

### How is UMANG different from individual government apps?
UMANG is a unified platform — instead of downloading separate apps for EPFO, passport, PAN, etc., you get everything in one app. Individual apps may offer deeper functionality for specific services.

### Can I use UMANG without Aadhaar?
You can register and use basic services without Aadhaar. However, many government services require Aadhaar-based verification, so linking it is recommended.
