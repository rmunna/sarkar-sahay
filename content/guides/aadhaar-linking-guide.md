# How to Link Aadhaar with PAN, Bank Account, Mobile & Other Services

Aadhaar is India's universal identity document. The government requires you to link Aadhaar with many services — some mandatory, some optional but useful. This guide covers **every major Aadhaar linking scenario** with step-by-step instructions.

If you don't have an Aadhaar card yet, see our guide on [how to apply for Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online).

---

## Why Link Aadhaar?

Linking Aadhaar serves several purposes:

- **Get government subsidies** directly in your bank account (DBT)
- **File income tax returns** — PAN-Aadhaar link is mandatory
- **Avoid service disruption** — unlinked accounts may be frozen or deactivated
- **KYC compliance** — Aadhaar is accepted as valid KYC across banks, telecom and mutual funds
- **Eliminate duplicates** — ensures benefits reach the right person

---

## 1. Link Aadhaar with PAN Card (Mandatory)

**Why:** Without this link, your PAN becomes inoperative. You cannot file ITR, process refunds, or do high-value financial transactions.

**Deadline:** The government has set multiple deadlines in the past. Check the latest deadline on the Income Tax portal. A late linking fee of ₹1,000 applies.

**Is it mandatory?** Yes, for all PAN holders (with a few exemptions like NRIs and senior citizens above 80).

We have a detailed step-by-step guide here: **[How to Link Aadhaar with PAN Card](/guide/aadhaar-pan-link)**

### Quick Steps

1. Visit [incometax.gov.in](https://www.incometax.gov.in)
2. Click **"Link Aadhaar"** on the homepage
3. Enter your PAN number, Aadhaar number and name as per Aadhaar
4. Click **"Link Aadhaar"**
5. Pay ₹1,000 fee if the free deadline has passed (via e-Pay Tax → Minor Head 500)

### Other Methods
- **SMS:** Send `UIDPAN <12-digit Aadhaar> <10-digit PAN>` to **567678** or **56161**

**Official portal:** [incometax.gov.in](https://www.incometax.gov.in)

## 2. Link Aadhaar with Bank Account

**Why:** Required to receive government subsidies (LPG, PM-Kisan, scholarships) directly via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT). Also needed for KYC compliance under RBI guidelines.

**Is it mandatory?** Yes, for receiving government benefits. RBI also mandates Aadhaar-based KYC for bank accounts.

### How to Link — Online (Net Banking)

1. Log in to your bank's **net banking** or **mobile banking app**
2. Go to **Profile / Settings / KYC / Aadhaar Linking**
3. Enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number
4. Verify via OTP sent to your Aadhaar-registered mobile number
5. Done — linking is confirmed instantly or within 24 hours

### How to Link — Offline (Branch Visit)

1. Visit your nearest bank branch
2. Fill the **Aadhaar seeding form** (available at the branch)
3. Attach a photocopy of your Aadhaar card
4. Submit to the bank officer
5. Linking is done within 3–5 working days

### How to Link — ATM

Some banks (SBI, PNB, BOB) allow Aadhaar linking via ATM:
1. Insert your debit card and enter PIN
2. Select **"Aadhaar Registration"** or **"Link Aadhaar"**
3. Enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number
4. Confirm

**Tip:** Make sure the name and date of birth in your bank account match your Aadhaar. If there's a mismatch, update your bank records first.

## 3. Link Aadhaar with Mobile Number

**Why:** An Aadhaar-linked mobile number is essential for receiving OTPs during Aadhaar authentication, e-KYC, and accessing most government services online.

**Is it mandatory?** Not mandatory by law (Supreme Court struck down mandatory mobile-Aadhaar linking in 2018). However, having a mobile number linked to Aadhaar is practically necessary for OTP-based services.

**Important:** You **cannot** link your mobile number with Aadhaar online. This must be done in person.

### How to Link

1. Visit your nearest **Aadhaar Enrolment/Update Centre**
   - Find centres at [uidai.gov.in](https://uidai.gov.in) → "Locate Enrolment Centre"
2. Fill the **Aadhaar Update/Correction Form**
3. Select **"Mobile Number Update"**
4. Provide your mobile number and biometric verification (fingerprint/iris)
5. You'll receive an acknowledgement slip with a URN (Update Request Number)
6. The new mobile number is linked within **7–10 working days**

**Fee:** ₹75 (for any Aadhaar update at the centre)

For a detailed guide, see: **[How to Change Mobile Number in Aadhaar](/guide/change-mobile-number-in-aadhaar-card)**

**Official portal:** [uidai.gov.in](https://uidai.gov.in)

## 4. Link Aadhaar with EPF / UAN (Provident Fund)

**Why:** EPFO requires Aadhaar-UAN linking for online PF withdrawal, transfer, and to receive employer contributions. Without it, you cannot submit online claims.

**Is it mandatory?** Yes, as per government notification. Employers must also ensure Aadhaar verification of all EPF members.

### How to Link — Online

1. Visit [EPFO Unified Portal](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in)
2. Log in with your **UAN** and password
3. Go to **Manage → KYC**
4. Select **"Aadhaar"** from the document type dropdown
5. Enter your **Aadhaar number** and **name as per Aadhaar**
6. Click **Save** — your employer will approve the KYC
7. Once approved, status changes to **"Verified"**

### How to Link — Via Umang App

1. Download the **UMANG app** from Play Store / App Store
2. Go to **EPFO → Employee Centric Services → e-KYC**
3. Enter UAN and verify via OTP
4. Link Aadhaar through the app

**Tip:** If your name in Aadhaar doesn't match your UAN records, the linking will fail. Correct the mismatch first — either update Aadhaar or ask your employer to update UAN records.

For more on PF: **[EPF/PF Withdrawal Guide](/guide/epf-pf-withdrawal-online)**

**Official portal:** [unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in)

## 5. Link Aadhaar with LPG Connection

**Why:** To receive LPG subsidy directly in your bank account via PAHAL (DBT) scheme. Without Aadhaar linking, your subsidy may not be credited.

**Is it mandatory?** Yes, for receiving LPG subsidy.

### How to Link — Online

1. Visit [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in)
2. Select your distributor (Indian Oil / Bharat Petroleum / HP Gas)
3. Go to **"My Account"** → **"Update Aadhaar"**
4. Enter your 17-digit LPG consumer ID and Aadhaar number
5. Verify via OTP
6. Submit

### How to Link — Offline

1. Visit your LPG distributor's office
2. Fill the Aadhaar seeding form
3. Attach Aadhaar copy
4. Submit

For more on LPG subsidies: **[LPG Subsidy & Ujjwala Yojana Guide](/guide/lpg-subsidy-ujjwala-yojana)**

**Official portal:** [mylpg.in](https://mylpg.in)

## 6. Link Aadhaar with Ration Card

**Why:** Mandatory for receiving subsidised food grains under the National Food Security Act (NFSA) and state PDS schemes. Unlinked ration cards may be deactivated.

**Is it mandatory?** Yes, the government has made Aadhaar-ration card linking mandatory for PDS benefits.

### How to Link — Online

The process varies by state. Generally:

1. Visit your **state's food & civil supplies portal** (e.g., nfsa.gov.in links to state portals)
2. Log in or search using your ration card number
3. Select **"Aadhaar Seeding"** or **"Link Aadhaar"**
4. Enter Aadhaar numbers for **each family member** listed on the ration card
5. Verify via OTP
6. Submit

### How to Link — Offline

1. Visit your nearest **Fair Price Shop (ration shop)** or **Tehsildar / Food Department office**
2. Fill the Aadhaar seeding form
3. Provide Aadhaar copies of all family members on the ration card
4. Submit

For more on ration cards: **[How to Apply for Ration Card](/guide/ration-card-apply-online)**

**Official portal:** [nfsa.gov.in](https://nfsa.gov.in)

## 7. Link Aadhaar with Voter ID (EPIC)

**Why:** The Election Commission allows voluntary Aadhaar-Voter ID linking to clean up duplicate entries in the electoral roll. This is **not mandatory** — you can refuse without losing your voting rights.

**Is it mandatory?** No. Completely voluntary as per the Election Commission.

### How to Link — Online

1. Visit [NVSP (National Voter Service Portal)](https://www.nvsp.in)
2. Log in or register
3. Go to **Form 6B** — "Linking of Aadhaar with EPIC"
4. Enter your **EPIC number** (Voter ID number) and **Aadhaar number**
5. Verify via OTP
6. Submit

### How to Link — Offline

1. Visit your nearest **Electoral Registration Office (ERO)** or **BLO (Booth Level Officer)**
2. Fill **Form 6B**
3. Submit with Aadhaar copy

**Official portal:** [nvsp.in](https://www.nvsp.in)

## 8. Link Aadhaar with Driving Licence

**Why:** Helps in creating a unified transport database. Some states require it for online DL services. Also useful for DigiLocker integration.

**Is it mandatory?** Not mandatory at the central level, but some states have started requiring it for DL renewal and other services.

### How to Link — Online

1. Visit [Parivahan Sewa](https://parivahan.gov.in)
2. Select your **state** from the dropdown
3. Log in to your account
4. Go to **"Driving Licence Related Services"** → **"Link Aadhaar"** (option varies by state)
5. Enter Aadhaar number and verify via OTP
6. Submit

### How to Link — Offline

1. Visit your nearest **RTO (Regional Transport Office)**
2. Fill the Aadhaar linking form
3. Submit with Aadhaar and DL copies
4. Biometric verification may be done on the spot

**Official portal:** [parivahan.gov.in](https://parivahan.gov.in)

---

## 9. Link Aadhaar with PPF / Post Office Savings

**Why:** Mandatory for PPF accounts and other small savings schemes (NSC, KVP, Sukanya Samriddhi) held at post offices or banks. Without Aadhaar linking, your account may be frozen.

**Is it mandatory?** Yes, as per Department of Economic Affairs notification.

### How to Link

**For Post Office accounts:**
1. Visit your nearest **Post Office** where your account is held
2. Fill the **Aadhaar seeding form** (available at the counter)
3. Submit Aadhaar copy along with your passbook
4. The postmaster will update your records

**For Bank-held PPF accounts:**
1. Use **net banking** or visit the bank branch
2. Link Aadhaar via the same process as bank account linking (see Section 2 above)

**Tip:** If you have a **Sukanya Samriddhi Account** or **Senior Citizens Savings Scheme**, the same process applies. Visit the branch/post office with Aadhaar.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. What happens if I don't link Aadhaar with PAN?

Your PAN card becomes **inoperative**. You won't be able to file income tax returns, receive refunds, or carry out financial transactions that require PAN. To reactivate, you need to link Aadhaar and pay a ₹1,000 fee. See our [Aadhaar-PAN linking guide](/guide/aadhaar-pan-link) for details.

### Q2. Can I link my mobile number with Aadhaar online?

**No.** After the Supreme Court's 2018 ruling, telecom companies cannot do Aadhaar-based e-KYC for SIM cards. To update your mobile number in Aadhaar, you must visit an [Aadhaar Enrolment Centre](/guide/change-mobile-number-in-aadhaar-card) in person.

### Q3. My name in Aadhaar doesn't match my bank/PAN records. What should I do?

You need to fix the mismatch first. Either update your name in Aadhaar (via [uidai.gov.in](https://uidai.gov.in)) or update your name in the other document. Aadhaar linking requires an exact name match in most systems.

### Q4. Is Aadhaar-Voter ID linking mandatory? Will I lose my vote if I don't link?

**No.** The Election Commission has clarified that Aadhaar-Voter ID linking is **completely voluntary**. Your name will NOT be removed from the voter list if you don't link. You can refuse without giving any reason.

### Q5. How do I check if my Aadhaar is already linked with my bank account?

- Check via **net banking** or **mobile banking app** under profile/KYC section
- Call your bank's **customer care** and ask
- Visit the branch with your Aadhaar and passbook

### Q6. Can NRIs link Aadhaar with their Indian bank accounts?

Yes, if you have a valid Aadhaar number. NRIs can link Aadhaar with their NRE/NRO bank accounts by visiting the bank branch in India or through net banking (if the bank supports it).

### Q7. I linked Aadhaar with my LPG but still not getting subsidy. What should I do?

Make sure your **Aadhaar is also linked with your bank account** (the same bank account registered with your LPG distributor). The subsidy flows through: LPG → Aadhaar → Bank Account. If any link is missing, the subsidy won't come.
