## What is the Ishan Uday Scholarship?

Ishan Uday is a **special scholarship scheme** by the Government of India for students from the **8 North-Eastern states** — Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim, and Tripura — who are pursuing higher education outside or within the Northeast region. It provides:

- **₹5,400/month** for general degree programmes (arts, science, commerce)
- **₹7,800/month** for technical and professional courses (engineering, medicine, agriculture, etc.)

The scheme is implemented by the **University Grants Commission (UGC)** in collaboration with the **North East Council (NEC)**.

## Who is Eligible?

**You ARE eligible if:**
- You are a **permanent resident of one of the 8 North-Eastern states**
- You have passed **Class 12 from a school in the North-Eastern region**
- You are pursuing a **full-time undergraduate or postgraduate degree** at a UGC-recognized university/institution
- Your family's **annual income does not exceed ₹4.5 lakh**
- You have secured **admission in first year** of a course (fresh applications) or are renewing for continuation

**Courses covered:**
- BA, BSc, BCom (general degrees)
- B.Tech, B.E., MBBS, BDS, B.Arch, B.Pharma, B.Sc Agriculture (professional)
- MBA, M.Tech, MD, MS (postgraduate professional)

**You are NOT eligible if:**
- You are already receiving another central government scholarship
- Your family income exceeds ₹4.5 lakh per year
- You are studying through distance/correspondence mode

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar Card** — student's own
2. **Domicile certificate** — proving permanent residence in a Northeast state
3. **Income certificate** — issued by competent authority (family income ≤ ₹4.5 lakh)
4. **Class 12 marksheet and pass certificate**
5. **Admission letter / enrollment certificate** from current institution
6. **Bonafide student certificate** from the college/university
7. **Bank passbook** — Aadhaar-linked account in student's name
8. **Passport-size photograph**
9. **Caste certificate** (if applicable — SC/ST)

## How to Apply — Step by Step

### Step 1 — Register on NSP

Go to **[scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in)** and click **"New Registration"**. Fill in your name, mobile number, email, and state.

### Step 2 — Login and Apply

After registration, log in and:
- Go to **Apply for Scholarship**
- Select **"Ishan Uday — Special Scholarship Scheme for NE Region"**
- Fill in personal, academic, and bank details
- Upload all required documents

### Step 3 — Institute Verification

Your college/university verifies your application on the NSP portal. This usually takes 1–2 weeks. Follow up with your college's scholarship coordinator if pending.

### Step 4 — State Nodal Agency Verification

After institute verification, the State Nodal Agency (your home state's scholarship authority) verifies your domicile and income details.

### Step 5 — UGC Approval and Disbursement

After final verification by UGC, the scholarship amount is disbursed directly to your bank account via DBT. Payment is usually quarterly.

## Application Dates

Ishan Uday applications open on the **National Scholarship Portal** typically in **August–October** for the academic year. Check [scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in) for exact dates.

**Important:** Don't miss the deadline — late applications are not accepted. The window is usually open for 60–90 days.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. I am from Assam studying in Delhi. Am I eligible?**

Yes. Ishan Uday is specifically designed for Northeast students studying **anywhere in India** — within or outside the Northeast. Studying in Delhi makes you equally eligible.

**2. I am already receiving a state scholarship. Can I also get Ishan Uday?**

You cannot hold two central government scholarships simultaneously. However, if your state scholarship is a **state government scheme** (not a central scheme), check with the NSP helpline for compatibility.

**3. My income certificate shows ₹4.8 lakh. Am I ineligible?**

Yes. The income limit is strictly ₹4.5 lakh. If your family income exceeds this, you are not eligible for Ishan Uday.

**4. Can I apply if I'm already in second year without having applied in first year?**

No. Ishan Uday accepts fresh applications only in the **first year of the course**. Second and subsequent year students must be renewing a previously approved application.

**5. The payment hasn't arrived for 3 months. What should I do?**

Check your application status on the NSP portal. Common reasons for delay: Aadhaar not seeded to bank, PFMS validation pending. Contact your college's scholarship coordinator and the NSP helpline: **0120-6619540**.
