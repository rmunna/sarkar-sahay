## What is UP Mathrubhumi Yojana?

**Uttar Pradesh Mathrubhumi Yojana** is a state government initiative that allows **Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) and other donors** to co-fund village infrastructure projects in their ancestral or chosen villages in Uttar Pradesh — and get naming rights on the completed facility in memory of family members.

The Government of UP contributes **40% of the project cost**, while the donor contributes **60%**.

---

## Key Benefits

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Government co-funding | 40% of total project cost |
| Donor contribution | 60% of project cost |
| Naming rights | Project named after donor's choice (family name, parent, etc.) |
| Eligible for | NRIs, Indian citizens, corporate donors |
| Projects | Roads, health centres, anganwadis, crematorium, more |
| Portal | mathrubhumi.up.gov.in |

---

## Eligible Projects for Funding

Donors can fund the following infrastructure in UP villages:

| Category | Projects |
|----------|---------|
| Health | Health sub-centre, health post, community health centre |
| Education | Anganwadi centre, primary school building |
| Sanitation | Crematorium, community toilet complex |
| Connectivity | Internal village roads (CC road) |
| Water | Hand pumps, water purification units |
| Community | Community hall, panchayat bhavan |

---

## Who Can Apply as a Donor?

- **NRIs** with roots in UP
- **Indian citizens** residing in India or abroad
- **Corporations** (under CSR — Corporate Social Responsibility)
- **Trusts and societies**

There is no upper limit on donation amount. A donor can fund multiple projects across different villages.

---

## How to Apply as a Donor

### Step 1 — Register on Portal

Go to **[mathrubhumi.up.gov.in](https://mathrubhumi.up.gov.in)**:
- Register as individual / NRI / corporate
- Provide Aadhaar / PAN / passport details

### Step 2 — Select Village and Project

- Choose the village (by district, block, gram panchayat)
- Select the type of project you want to fund
- System shows estimated cost (40% GOI + 60% donor)

### Step 3 — Payment and Agreement

- Pay your 60% contribution via online banking
- Sign the MoU (agreement) with the government
- Get naming rights confirmation

### Step 4 — Project Execution

- The state government (through district administration) executes the project
- Donor can track progress on the portal
- Naming plaque is installed upon completion

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. I am an NRI in the USA. Can I fund a road in my ancestral village in UP?**

Yes. This scheme is specifically designed for NRIs. You can register on mathrubhumi.up.gov.in, select your village, and co-fund a project of your choice. Foreign currency payments are processed via authorized banking channels.

**2. Can I name the project after my late father?**

Yes. Naming rights are a core feature of this scheme. The completed infrastructure (road, health centre, anganwadi, etc.) is named as per your choice — usually on a permanent plaque at the site.

**3. Is there a minimum donation amount?**

The minimum amount depends on the project selected. A basic hand pump or anganwadi renovation may be available at lower costs. Road projects are typically higher. Check the portal for project-wise costs.

**4. Can an Indian company fund projects under this scheme as CSR?**

Yes. Corporate contributions under UP Mathrubhumi Yojana qualify for CSR expenditure under Companies Act Section 135.

**5. How do I verify the project was actually completed?**

The portal at mathrubhumi.up.gov.in provides project-wise photo and status updates. District administration submits completion certificates with geo-tagged photos.
