# NSP Scholarship Institute Not Verified? Fix It

Your NSP scholarship application is submitted, but it's stuck at "Pending at Institute" or "Institute Not Verified." This is one of the most common issues on the National Scholarship Portal. Here's how to fix it.

## Why Institute Verification Is Required

Every NSP scholarship application goes through a multi-level verification process:

1. **Student submits** the application on [scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in/)
2. **Institute Nodal Officer** verifies student details, enrolment, and documents
3. **District/State Nodal Officer** gives final approval
4. **Scholarship is sanctioned** and amount is disbursed via DBT

If the institute does not verify your application, it cannot move forward — regardless of how complete your application is.

## Common Institute Verification Issues and Fixes

### 1. Nodal Officer Has Not Taken Action

**Problem:** Your application shows "Pending at Institute" for weeks.

**Fix:**
1. Visit your college/university's scholarship section or accounts office
2. Ask for the **Institute Nodal Officer** (INO) assigned for NSP
3. Request them to log in to the NSP Institute Login portal and verify your application
4. The INO must check your details, mark the application as "Verified," and forward it to the district level
5. If the INO is unresponsive, escalate to the college principal or registrar

> **Important:** Institutes have a deadline to complete verification. If the INO misses the deadline, your application may lapse.

### 2. AISHE Code Not Found or Incorrect

**Problem:** Your institute's AISHE (All India Survey on Higher Education) code is not appearing on NSP, or you selected the wrong code.

**Fix:**
1. Verify your institute's AISHE code at [aishe.gov.in](https://aishe.gov.in/)
2. Search by institute name, district, and state
3. If your institute is not listed, the institute must first register on the AISHE portal and obtain a code
4. If you selected the wrong AISHE code in your application, contact the NSP helpline (0120-6619540) to request a correction before verification
5. New institutes (established in the last 1–2 years) may not have an AISHE code yet — the institute must apply through the AISHE portal

### 3. How to Track Verification Status

1. Log in to [scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in/) with your Application ID
2. Go to **"Check Your Status"** or **"Application Status"**
3. The status will show one of:
   - **Pending at Institute** — INO has not yet verified
   - **Verified by Institute** — forwarded to district/state level
   - **Returned by Institute** — INO found an issue; check defect reason
   - **Pending at District/State** — institute verification complete
4. If returned, log in, fix the defect, and resubmit

### 4. How to Contact Your Institute's Nodal Officer

- Check your college notice board or scholarship section for the INO's name and contact
- Ask the accounts or administrative office
- On NSP, go to **"Know Your INO"** feature (if available) to find the assigned nodal officer
- Email or call the INO directly and share your Application ID

### 5. Escalation Steps If Institute Does Not Verify

If your institute's nodal officer is not responding:

**Step 1:** Write a formal letter to the Principal or Registrar requesting immediate verification

**Step 2:** File a grievance on the NSP Grievance Portal:
1. Go to [scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"Grievance"** or **"Student Helpdesk"**
3. Fill in your Application ID, describe the issue, and submit
4. Note the grievance tracking number

**Step 3:** Contact the state nodal agency for your scholarship scheme — details available on the NSP portal under scheme guidelines

**Step 4:** Call the NSP helpline at **0120-6619540** or email **helpdesk@nsp.gov.in**

**Step 5:** As a last resort, approach the District Education Officer (DEO) or District Welfare Officer

## Verification Timelines

| Stage | Typical Timeline |
|---|---|
| Institute verification | 2–4 weeks after application deadline |
| District/State verification | 2–4 weeks after institute verification |
| Sanction and disbursement | 1–3 months after final verification |

> **Note:** Timelines vary by state and scheme. Check the specific scheme's notification for exact dates.

## Important Tips

1. **Apply early** — late applications give the INO less time to verify before the deadline
2. **Keep all original documents ready** — the INO may ask to physically verify originals
3. **Follow up in person** if possible; emails and calls are often ignored
4. **Check status weekly** after submission to catch returns early
5. **Multiple scholarships:** If you applied for more than one scheme, each requires separate institute verification

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. My institute is not registered on NSP. Can I still apply?
No. Your institute must be registered on NSP with a valid AISHE code. Ask the institute administration to complete registration first.

### Q2. Can I verify my own application on NSP?
No. Only the Institute Nodal Officer can verify student applications. Students can only submit and track.

### Q3. The INO verified my application but it still shows "Pending at Institute."
This may be a system delay. Wait 48 hours. If it persists, the INO may have saved but not forwarded. Ask them to check their dashboard.

### Q4. My application was returned by the institute. What now?
Log in, check the defect reason under your application status, correct the issue (re-upload document, fix details), and resubmit before the correction deadline.

### Q5. Can I change my institute after submitting the application?
No. If you have changed your institute, you must cancel this application (if possible) and submit a fresh one with the new institute's details.

### Q6. The INO says they cannot see my application on their dashboard.
This happens if the AISHE code you selected doesn't match the INO's login. Verify you selected the correct institute and AISHE code. If wrong, contact NSP helpline for correction.
