## What is YSR Rythu Bharosa?

YSR Rythu Bharosa is a **Government of Andhra Pradesh** scheme that provides **₹13,500 per year** to eligible farmers. It combines the state's own Rythu Bharosa component (₹7,500) with the central PM-KISAN benefit (₹6,000), delivering the total amount directly to farmers' bank accounts.

A key advantage over PM-KISAN alone is that **tenant farmers and landless cultivators** are also covered under the state component, making it one of the most inclusive farmer support schemes in India.

Also see our [PM Kisan guide](/guide/pm-kisan-samman-nidhi) for the central component.

## Benefit Breakdown

| Component | Amount | Paid When | Source |
|-----------|--------|-----------|--------|
| AP Rythu Bharosa (Kharif) | ₹7,500 | May (before Kharif season) | State Government |
| PM-KISAN Installment 1 | ₹2,000 | April–July | Central Government |
| PM-KISAN Installment 2 | ₹2,000 | August–November | Central Government |
| PM-KISAN Installment 3 | ₹2,000 | December–March | Central Government |
| **Total per year** | **₹13,500** | | **State + Centre** |

> The ₹7,500 state component is paid as a **single lump sum in May** each year to help farmers with Kharif season investment costs (seeds, fertilisers, labour).

## Who is Eligible?

**Eligible:**
- Farmers residing in Andhra Pradesh
- Land-owning farmers (pattadar) with cultivable land
- **Tenant farmers and sharecroppers** (enrolled through village/ward secretariats)
- Farmers registered in the **e-Crop booking system**
- Must have an Aadhaar-linked bank account

**NOT Eligible:**
- Government employees or pensioners (state/central)
- Income tax payers
- Professionals registered with professional bodies (doctors, lawyers, CAs, engineers)
- Farmers owning land in urban/municipal areas (unless actively cultivating)
- Those already excluded under PM-KISAN guidelines

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar Card** (mandatory, linked to bank account)
- **Land records** (pattadar passbook / 1-B extract) — for land-owning farmers
- **Tenant farmer certificate** — issued via village/ward secretariat for landless cultivators
- **Bank passbook** (Aadhaar-seeded account)
- **e-Crop registration** proof
- **Mobile number** linked to Aadhaar

## How to Apply / Register

### For Land-Owning Farmers
1. Visit your nearest **Village Secretariat** or **Ward Secretariat**
2. Submit Aadhaar, land records (pattadar passbook), and bank details
3. Ensure your land is registered in the **e-Crop system** (crop booking done by VRO/agriculture assistant)
4. Verification is done at the village level by the Village Revenue Officer (VRO)
5. Once verified, your name appears in the beneficiary list

### For Tenant Farmers
1. Visit the **Village Secretariat** in your area
2. Request a **tenant farmer certificate** — the secretariat verifies your cultivation status with the landowner's consent
3. Submit Aadhaar, bank details, and the tenant certificate
4. After verification, you are added to the beneficiary list for the state component (₹7,500)

> **Note:** Tenant farmers receive only the state Rythu Bharosa component (₹7,500). PM-KISAN (₹6,000) requires land ownership in the farmer's name.

## How to Check Payment Status

### Online (YSR Rythu Bharosa Portal)
1. Visit [ysrrythubharosa.ap.gov.in](https://ysrrythubharosa.ap.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Payment Status"** or **"Beneficiary Search"**
3. Enter your **Aadhaar number** or **mobile number**
4. View payment history and upcoming installment details

### PM-KISAN Status (Central Component)
1. Visit [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in)
2. Go to **Beneficiary Status**
3. Enter Aadhaar number or mobile number
4. Check installment-wise payment details

### Via Village Secretariat
- Visit your local secretariat and ask the **Digital Assistant** to check your payment status
- They can also help resolve bank account or Aadhaar mismatch issues

## Common Issues & Solutions

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Payment not received | Check Aadhaar-bank linking at village secretariat; ensure e-Crop registration is active |
| Name mismatch | Update name in Aadhaar to match bank account; visit secretariat for correction |
| Tenant farmer not listed | Apply for tenant certificate at village secretariat with landowner consent |
| e-Crop not registered | Contact your Village Agriculture Assistant or VRO for crop booking |
| Bank account rejected | Ensure Aadhaar is seeded/linked in your bank account (visit bank branch) |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can tenant farmers get the full ₹13,500?**
No. Tenant farmers receive ₹7,500 from the state Rythu Bharosa component. The PM-KISAN ₹6,000 requires land ownership. However, ₹7,500 is still a significant benefit unavailable in most other states.

**Q: Is there an online application form?**
Registration is primarily done **offline through village/ward secretariats**. The online portal is for status checking and information only.

**Q: When is the next payment expected?**
The Rythu Bharosa state component (₹7,500) is typically credited in **May** each year, before the Kharif season begins.

**Q: Do I need to re-apply every year?**
No. Once registered and verified, payments continue automatically each year as long as you remain eligible and your e-Crop registration is active.

**Q: What if I own land in another state?**
You must be a resident farmer of Andhra Pradesh. Land in other states does not qualify under AP Rythu Bharosa.
