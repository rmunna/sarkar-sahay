# RRB NTPC Exam Guide — Eligibility, Pattern, Syllabus & Preparation Tips

The RRB NTPC (Non-Technical Popular Categories) exam is one of India's largest recruitment drives, conducted by the Railway Recruitment Boards to fill various non-technical posts in Indian Railways. With lakhs of vacancies and candidates from across the country, NTPC is a gateway to stable railway jobs.

This guide covers the complete RRB NTPC exam — eligibility, pattern, syllabus, and how to prepare effectively.

RRB NTPC recruits candidates for non-technical positions in Indian Railways across multiple graduate and undergraduate levels:

## Eligibility Criteria

### Nationality

- Citizen of India

### Age Limit

Age varies by post:

| Posts | Age Range |
|-------|-----------|
| Undergraduate level | 18–30 years |
| Graduate level (most) | 18–33 years |
| Goods Guard, Commercial Apprentice, Station Master | 18–33 years |

**Age relaxation:** SC/ST — 5 years, OBC — 3 years, PwBD — 15 years, Ex-servicemen — as per rules

### Educational Qualification

| Level | Qualification |
|-------|--------------|
| Undergraduate posts | Class 12 pass (10+2) from a recognised board |
| Graduate posts | Bachelor's degree from a recognised university |

**Some posts may have additional requirements like typing proficiency.**

## Exam Pattern

RRB NTPC has a multi-stage selection process:

### Stage 1: CBT-1 (First Stage Computer-Based Test)

| Section | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| Mathematics | 30 | 30 | — |
| General Intelligence & Reasoning | 30 | 30 | — |
| General Awareness | 40 | 40 | — |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** | **90 minutes** |

- Negative marking: 1/3 of marks per wrong answer
- Qualifying stage — score determines CBT-2 eligibility
- CBT-1 is common for all NTPC posts

### Stage 2: CBT-2 (Second Stage Computer-Based Test)

| Section | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| Mathematics | 35 | 35 | — |
| General Intelligence & Reasoning | 35 | 35 | — |
| General Awareness | 50 | 50 | — |
| **Total** | **120** | **120** | **90 minutes** |

- Negative marking: 1/3 of marks per wrong answer
- Separate CBT-2 for graduate and undergraduate levels
- Final merit primarily based on CBT-2 score

### Stage 3: Typing Skill Test / Computer-Based Aptitude Test

- **Typing test** (for posts requiring typing): 30 wpm in English or 25 wpm in Hindi on computer
- **CBAT (Aptitude Test):** For Station Master and Traffic Assistant posts only
- Both are qualifying in nature

### Stage 4: Document Verification and Medical Examination

## Syllabus Overview

### Mathematics

- Number system, BODMAS, fractions, decimals
- LCM, HCF
- Ratio, proportion, percentages
- Simple and compound interest
- Profit and loss
- Time and work
- Time, speed, and distance
- Mensuration — area, volume
- Algebra — basic equations
- Geometry — triangles, circles
- Trigonometry — basic identities
- Data interpretation — tables, charts, graphs
- Statistics — mean, median, mode

### General Intelligence & Reasoning

- Analogies
- Coding-decoding
- Number and letter series
- Mathematical operations
- Syllogism
- Venn diagrams
- Blood relations
- Direction sense
- Statement and conclusion
- Mirror/water image
- Paper folding
- Classification
- Puzzles and arrangements

### General Awareness

- Current affairs — national and international (last 6–12 months)
- Indian history — ancient, medieval, modern, freedom struggle
- Indian polity — constitution, governance, judiciary
- Indian geography — physical, rivers, climate
- Indian economy — planning, budget, banking
- General science — Physics, Chemistry, Biology basics
- Railway-specific GK — Indian Railway facts, zones, recent developments
- Sports, awards, important days
- Books and authors
- Science and technology

## How to Apply

1. **Check notification:** RRB publishes NTPC notifications on regional RRB websites (e.g., [rrbcdg.gov.in](https://www.rrbcdg.gov.in))
2. **Register:** Create an account on the respective RRB portal
3. **Fill application:** Choose posts, enter personal and educational details, select exam city preferences
4. **Upload documents:** Photo, signature, and certificates
5. **Pay fee and submit**

### Application Fee

- **General/OBC/EWS:** ₹500 (₹400 refunded after CBT-1 appearance)
- **SC/ST/Ex-servicemen/PwBD/Female/Transgender/Minorities/EBC:** ₹250 (₹250 refunded after CBT-1 appearance)

## Preparation Tips

### 1. General Awareness Is Key

GK carries the highest marks in both CBT-1 (40) and CBT-2 (50). This is where most selection happens. Strategy:
- Read a newspaper daily
- Maintain monthly current affairs notes
- Cover railway-specific facts
- Revise static GK — history, geography, polity

### 2. Maths Should Be Consistent

Practice maths daily — even 30 minutes. Focus on:
- Shortcuts for calculations
- Data interpretation (DI) — this carries multiple questions
- Previous years' paper patterns

### 3. Build Reasoning Gradually

Reasoning is scoring with practice. Start with basic topics (series, analogies) and move to puzzles and arrangements.

### 4. Previous Years' Papers Are Essential

RRB NTPC papers from the last 2 cycles (2016 and 2021) cover thousands of questions. Solve them all.

### 5. Take Full-Length Mocks

Mock tests are the best way to build exam temperament. Take at least 20–30 full mocks before CBT-1.

### 6. Prepare for Typing Test Early

If applying for posts requiring typing (clerk positions), start practicing:
- English: 30 wpm
- Hindi: 25 wpm
- Use free online typing tools

### 7. Choose Posts Wisely

Apply for posts matching your qualification. Graduate-level posts (Level 5–6) offer significantly better salary and career growth.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. What is the qualification for RRB NTPC?

Class 12 pass for undergraduate-level posts and Bachelor's degree for graduate-level posts.

### 2. Is there negative marking in RRB NTPC?

Yes, 1/3 of the marks allotted to a question are deducted for each wrong answer.

### 3. How many vacancies are there in RRB NTPC?

NTPC recruitment is massive — the 2019 cycle had over 35,000 vacancies. Numbers vary per cycle.

### 4. Can I apply to multiple RRBs?

No, you can apply to only one RRB per recruitment cycle. Choose based on your preferred posting zone.

### 5. Is RRB NTPC exam conducted every year?

No, it is conducted based on vacancy requirements. Recent cycles were in 2016, 2019, and 2024.

### 6. What is the salary of Station Master?

Station Master (Pay Level 6) earns approximately ₹45,000–55,000 per month gross salary, plus railway benefits like free passes and housing.

### 7. Can I choose my posting?

You indicate RRB zone preference while applying. Posting within that zone depends on vacancy and merit rank.
