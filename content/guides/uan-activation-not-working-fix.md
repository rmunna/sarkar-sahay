# UAN Activation Failed? Fix EPFO Registration Errors (2026)

Can't activate your UAN (Universal Account Number) on the EPFO portal? Getting errors like "Aadhaar verification failed," "Member details not found," or "OTP not received"? This guide covers every UAN activation error and how to fix it.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent platform and is not affiliated with EPFO.

## What Is UAN Activation?

UAN activation is the process of creating your login credentials on the EPFO Member Portal. Until you activate your UAN, you cannot:
- Check your EPF balance online
- Download EPF passbook
- File online PF withdrawal/transfer claims
- Update KYC details

Your employer generates your UAN, but **you must activate it yourself.**

## How to Activate UAN (Standard Process)

1. Visit [unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in)
2. Click **"Activate UAN"** under "Important Links"
3. Enter your **UAN number**, **Aadhaar number**, and **name** (as in Aadhaar)
4. Enter your **date of birth** and **mobile number**
5. Click **"Get Authorization Pin"** — OTP sent to your mobile
6. Enter OTP and submit
7. Your password is sent via SMS to your registered mobile

## Common UAN Activation Errors & Fixes

### Error 1: "Member Details Not Found"

**Cause:** Your UAN is not yet registered in the EPFO system, or employer hasn't uploaded your details.

**Fix:**
1. Confirm your UAN number with your employer/HR department
2. Ask employer if they've submitted your UAN registration with EPFO
3. Wait 2-3 days after joining — employer ECR (Electronic Challan Return) filing takes time
4. Check if you have a **different UAN** from a previous employer — search at the activation page

### Error 2: "Aadhaar Verification Failed"

**Cause:** Your name or DOB on Aadhaar doesn't match EPFO records.

**Fix:**
1. Check your name on Aadhaar: visit [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in)
2. Check your name in EPFO: ask employer for the name they registered
3. If names don't match:
   - Ask employer to correct your name in EPFO records, OR
   - Update your Aadhaar name to match (see our [Aadhaar update guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
4. Ensure DOB matches exactly on both
5. Try entering name exactly as on Aadhaar — including middle name, initials, etc.

### Error 3: "OTP Not Received"

**Cause:** Mobile number mismatch or network issues.

**Fix:**
1. The OTP goes to the mobile number **registered with your Aadhaar** — not your personal number
2. Ensure your Aadhaar has the correct mobile linked (see our [Aadhaar mobile guide](/guide/aadhaar-mobile-number-not-linked-fix))
3. Wait 5 minutes before requesting resend
4. Check if your phone has network coverage
5. If using dual SIM, check both SIMs for the OTP

### Error 4: "UAN Already Activated"

**Cause:** You or someone already activated this UAN previously.

**Fix:**
1. Try logging in with the UAN — you may have forgotten
2. Click **"Forgot Password"** on the login page
3. Reset password using Aadhaar or mobile OTP
4. If you never activated it, contact EPFO — someone may have misused your UAN

### Error 5: "Name/DOB Mismatch with Employer Records"

**Cause:** The name you entered doesn't match what your employer registered.

**Fix:**
1. Enter your name **exactly** as your employer submitted it (ask HR)
2. Common issues: middle name missing, father's name included, initials vs full name
3. Try different combinations: "RAJESH KUMAR" vs "RAJESH K" vs "R KUMAR"
4. If nothing works, ask employer to correct the name in EPFO records

### Error 6: "Service Temporarily Unavailable"

**Cause:** EPFO servers are down or overloaded.

**Fix:**
1. Try during **off-peak hours** — early morning (6-8 AM) or late night
2. Clear browser cache and try again
3. Try a different browser (Firefox, Edge)
4. EPFO portal has maintenance windows — try the next day

## What If Employer Hasn't Registered Your UAN?

If your employer hasn't created your UAN:

1. **Ask HR directly** — they need to register you via the employer portal
2. Employer must file ECR (Electronic Challan Return) — UAN is generated during this process
3. If employer refuses or delays, **file a complaint**:
   - EPFO helpline: **1800-118-005**
   - EPFiGMS: [epfigms.gov.in](https://epfigms.gov.in)
   - Labour Commissioner of your state

## How to Find Your UAN Number

If you don't know your UAN:

1. **Check salary slip** — UAN is often printed on it
2. **Ask employer HR** — they have your UAN on record
3. **EPFO portal:** Visit the activation page → Use your Aadhaar/PAN to search for your UAN
4. **SMS:** Send `EPFOHO UAN` to **7738299899** from your registered mobile
5. **UMANG App:** EPFO section → Know Your UAN
6. **Previous employer records** — if you had PF in an earlier job, your UAN is the same

## After Successful UAN Activation

Once activated:
1. **Login** at [unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in)
2. **Update KYC:** Add Aadhaar, PAN, and bank account under Manage → KYC
3. **Check passbook:** View your EPF balance at [passbook.epfindia.gov.in](https://passbook.epfindia.gov.in)
4. **Link previous PF accounts:** If you had PF with previous employers, transfer them under Online Services → Transfer Request

## Tips for Smooth UAN Activation

1. **Use Chrome or Firefox** — EPFO portal works best on these
2. **Enter name exactly** as on Aadhaar — same spelling, same case
3. **Ensure Aadhaar mobile is active** — OTP goes to Aadhaar-linked number
4. **Try during off-peak hours** if the portal is slow
5. **Keep your UAN, Aadhaar, and PAN** numbers ready before starting
6. **Don't create multiple UANs** — one UAN follows you for life across all employers

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is my UAN not activating?
Common reasons: Aadhaar name/DOB doesn't match EPFO records, employer hasn't registered you yet, wrong mobile number linked to Aadhaar, or EPFO server issues. Verify details and try again.

### How to activate UAN without employer?
You cannot create a UAN without an employer. However, you can activate an existing UAN (created by a current or previous employer) independently on the EPFO portal.

### How long does UAN activation take?
UAN activation is instant if all details match. You receive your password via SMS immediately after successful activation. If there are mismatches, fixing them may take days.

### Can I activate UAN with old mobile number?
The OTP for UAN activation is sent to the mobile number linked to your Aadhaar, not the one registered with your employer. Ensure your Aadhaar has your current mobile number.

### What if I have two UAN numbers?
Having multiple UANs is common when changing jobs. You should merge them by filing a transfer request on the EPFO portal. One UAN becomes primary and the other is merged into it.

### How to reset UAN password?
Visit the EPFO member portal → Forgot Password → Verify using UAN + Aadhaar OTP or mobile OTP → Set new password.

### Can I activate UAN from previous employer?
Yes. Your UAN remains the same across all employers. If it was created by a previous employer, you can activate it now and your current employer will link to the same UAN.

### Is UAN activation mandatory?
UAN activation is not legally mandatory, but without it, you cannot access any EPFO online services — including checking balance, downloading passbook, or filing online claims.
