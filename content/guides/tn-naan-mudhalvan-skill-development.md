## What is Naan Mudhalvan Scheme?

**Naan Mudhalvan (I Am the First)** is a flagship skill development and employment programme launched by the Government of Tamil Nadu in 2022. The scheme aims to bridge the gap between education and employment by providing **free industry-relevant training, certifications, and placement support** to college students and graduates across Tamil Nadu.

The programme partners with **leading technology companies** like Google, Microsoft, IBM, Infosys, TCS, Coursera, and others to offer world-class courses at **zero cost** to students.

Key features:
- **Free online and offline courses** in technology, management, and vocational skills
- **Industry-recognised certifications** from top companies
- **Placement drives** and job fairs organised through the platform
- **Internship opportunities** with partner companies
- **Career guidance** and mentorship programmes
- Available to students in **all colleges** across Tamil Nadu (government, aided, and self-financing)

## Who is Eligible?

### Student Eligibility
- Must be a **student enrolled in any college** in Tamil Nadu (degree, diploma, or professional course)
- Open to students of **all streams** — arts, science, commerce, engineering, medicine, law, etc.
- No income or caste restrictions — scheme is **universal**
- Must have a valid **college ID**
- Must be a **full-time student** (some courses also open to recent graduates)

### Graduate Eligibility
- **Recent graduates** (within 1–2 years of passing out) from Tamil Nadu institutions
- **Job seekers** registered on the Tamil Nadu Employment Portal
- **ITI and diploma holders** looking to upskill

### Who Can Benefit Most?
- Students seeking **placements** in IT and tech companies
- Arts and commerce students wanting to learn **coding and digital skills**
- Rural students who lack access to expensive private training
- First-generation graduates looking for career guidance

## How to Register — Step-by-Step

### Online Registration

1. Visit **[naanmudhalvan.tn.gov.in](https://naanmudhalvan.tn.gov.in)**
2. Click on **"Student Registration"**
3. Enter your **college name** and select your institution from the dropdown
4. Enter **personal details** — name, date of birth, mobile number, email
5. Enter **educational details** — course, department, year of study, register number
6. Create a **username and password**
7. Verify your mobile number via **OTP**
8. Complete your **profile** — add skills, interests, and career preferences
9. Browse the **course catalogue** and enroll in courses of your choice
10. Start learning immediately — most courses are **self-paced online**

### Through College

1. Many colleges conduct **Naan Mudhalvan orientation sessions** at the start of each academic year
2. The placement cell or department coordinator will **guide batch registration**
3. Your college will assign a **Naan Mudhalvan coordinator** to track progress
4. Offline training sessions may be conducted at your college campus

## How the Programme Works

### Learning Path
1. **Register** on the portal and complete your profile
2. **Choose courses** aligned with your career interest (recommended: 2–3 per semester)
3. **Complete online modules** — video lectures, quizzes, assignments
4. **Take the certification exam** — most are free online exams
5. **Get certified** — download your industry-recognised certificate
6. **Update your profile** with certifications earned

### Placement Support
1. **Companies post jobs** on the Naan Mudhalvan portal
2. **Eligible students** (with matching certifications) are shortlisted
3. **Placement drives** are conducted at district and state level
4. **Virtual interviews** through the portal for remote opportunities
5. **Internship opportunities** with partner companies during semester breaks

## Benefits

- **100% free** — no registration fee, no course fee, no certification fee
- **Industry certifications** that are valued by employers (Google, Microsoft, IBM)
- **Direct placement linkage** — companies recruit directly from the platform
- **Skill gap analysis** — the platform suggests courses based on your profile and market demand
- **Accessible from anywhere** — courses are online, learn at your own pace
- **Government-backed** — adds credibility to your certification

## Important Tips

1. **Start early** — Don't wait until final year. Begin taking courses from 1st or 2nd year to build a strong certification portfolio.
2. **Focus on in-demand skills** — Data Science, Cloud Computing, and Full Stack Development have the highest placement rates.
3. **Complete certifications fully** — Partial completion doesn't count. Finish the course and take the exam.
4. **Keep your profile updated** — Recruiters search profiles on the portal. Add all certifications, projects, and skills.
5. **Attend placement drives** — Register for every relevant drive. Check the portal weekly for new opportunities.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Is Naan Mudhalvan completely free?
Yes. All courses, certifications, and placement services are **100% free** for registered students. The government pays for the course licences.

### Q2: Can arts and commerce students join?
Absolutely. The programme is open to **all streams** — not just engineering. There are specific courses for digital marketing, data entry, office automation, and soft skills designed for non-technical students.

### Q3: Do I get a certificate after completing a course?
Yes. You receive an **industry-recognised certificate** (e.g., Google Data Analytics Certificate, IBM Cybersecurity Certificate). These are the same certifications that professionals pay for on platforms like Coursera.

### Q4: Can I do Naan Mudhalvan courses if I'm already working?
The primary focus is on **current students and recent graduates**. However, some courses may be open to job seekers. Check the portal for eligibility.

### Q5: How does placement work?
Companies post jobs on the Naan Mudhalvan portal. Students with matching skills and certifications are **shortlisted automatically**. Placement drives are conducted at college, district, and state levels.

### Q6: Is this the same as TNSDC (Tamil Nadu Skill Development Corporation)?
Naan Mudhalvan is a **separate initiative** by the Higher Education Department, though it works in coordination with TNSDC. TNSDC focuses on vocational training for school dropouts and informal sector workers.

### Q7: Can students from private self-financing colleges apply?
Yes. The scheme is open to students from **all types of colleges** in Tamil Nadu — government, government-aided, and self-financing institutions.
