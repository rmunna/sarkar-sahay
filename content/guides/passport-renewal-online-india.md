## What is Passport Renewal (Re-issue)?

Passport renewal in India is officially called **"Re-issue of Passport"**. You can apply for it online through the Passport Seva portal (passportindia.gov.in) and visit a Passport Seva Kendra (PSK) or Post Office Passport Seva Kendra (POPSK) for document verification.

The entire process — from filling the form to booking an appointment — can be done online.

## When Should You Renew Your Passport?

You should apply for passport renewal in any of these situations:

1. **Expiring soon** — Your passport is expiring within the next 1 year (or has less than 6 months validity, which many countries require for entry)
2. **Already expired** — Your passport has already expired
3. **Damaged passport** — Pages are torn, cover is damaged, or details are not readable
4. **All pages used** — No blank pages left for visa stamps
5. **Change in personal details** — Name change (after marriage, etc.), address change, or correction of details
6. **Lost or stolen** — Passport has been lost or stolen (requires FIR/police report)

> **Tip:** Many countries require at least 6 months validity on your passport for entry. Plan your renewal well in advance if you have upcoming travel.

## Documents Required for Passport Renewal

### Mandatory Documents

| Document | Details |
|----------|---------|
| **Old Passport** (original + photocopy) | First and last page, and all stamped pages |
| **Aadhaar Card** | For address proof and identity |
| **Self-declaration** | Annexure D (if name/address changed) |

### Additional Documents (if applicable)

- **Marriage Certificate** — If name changed after marriage
- **Deed Poll / Gazette Notification** — For other name changes
- **FIR Copy** — If passport was lost or stolen
- **Court Order** — If name changed by court order

### For Minor Applicants

- Birth Certificate
- Parents' passports (photocopies)
- School ID or Bonafide Certificate

> **Note:** If your address has changed from the old passport, you'll need fresh address proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID, utility bill, etc.)

## Step-by-Step Online Renewal Process

### Step 1: Register on Passport Seva Portal

1. Go to [passportindia.gov.in](https://www.passportindia.gov.in)
2. Click on **"New User? Register Now"**
3. Enter your details — name, email, date of birth, login ID and password
4. Verify your email by clicking the link sent to your inbox
5. If you already have an account, simply log in

### Step 2: Apply Online — Fill the Application Form

1. After logging in, click **"Apply for Re-issue of Passport"**
2. Select the type of application — choose **"Re-issue"**
3. Fill in your personal details, family details, and address
4. Upload a recent passport-size photo (as per specifications)
5. Review all details carefully before submitting

### Step 3: Pay the Fee Online

1. After submitting the form, you'll be redirected to the payment page
2. Pay via **credit card, debit card, net banking, UPI, or SBI challan**
3. Save the payment receipt for your records

### Step 4: Book an Appointment

1. After payment, click **"Schedule Appointment"**
2. Choose your preferred **Passport Seva Kendra (PSK)** or **Post Office Passport Seva Kendra (POPSK)**
3. Select a date and time slot
4. Download and print the appointment confirmation

### Step 5: Visit the PSK/POPSK

1. Reach the PSK/POPSK on the appointed date and time
2. Carry all **original documents** along with **photocopies**
3. Carry the **printed appointment confirmation** and **application receipt**
4. Your documents will be verified, biometrics (photo, fingerprints) will be captured
5. You'll receive an acknowledgment receipt

### Step 6: Police Verification (if required)

1. Police verification may or may not be needed (see section below)
2. If required, a police officer will visit your address
3. Keep your documents ready at home

### Step 7: Receive Your New Passport

1. Once everything is verified, your new passport will be dispatched via **Speed Post**
2. Track using the file number on the Passport Seva portal

## Passport Renewal Fees

### Normal (Tatkal Nahi) Application

| Booklet Size | Fee |
|--------------|-----|
| 36 pages | ₹1,500 |
| 60 pages | ₹2,000 |

### Tatkal Application

| Booklet Size | Fee |
|--------------|-----|
| 36 pages | ₹3,500 |
| 60 pages | ₹4,000 |

> **Note:** Tatkal fee = Normal fee + ₹2,000 tatkal surcharge. Fees are the same for adults and minors.

### Additional Charges

- If applying at a **POPSK** (Post Office Passport Seva Kendra), there is no extra charge — fees remain the same
- **SMS tracking charges**: Included in the fee
- Payment can be made online or via SBI bank challan

## PSK vs POPSK — Where to Go?

| Feature | Passport Seva Kendra (PSK) | Post Office Passport Seva Kendra (POPSK) |
|---------|---------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| **Location** | Major cities (37 PSKs across India) | District-level post offices (400+ locations) |
| **Services** | All passport services | Re-issue, fresh passport (normal) |
| **Tatkal available?** | Yes | No (only normal processing) |
| **Processing speed** | Faster, dedicated staff | Slightly longer |
| **Best for** | Urgent/tatkal renewals | Applicants in smaller cities |

> **Tip:** If you need tatkal renewal, you must book at a PSK, not a POPSK.

## Police Verification — When Is It Required?

Police verification is **NOT always required** for passport renewal. Here's when it is needed:

### Police Verification Required:
- Change of address to a different police station jurisdiction
- Change in personal details (name, spouse name, etc.)
- Lost or damaged passport (replacement)
- If there's any adverse police report on record

### Police Verification NOT Required:
- Simple renewal with same address and no changes in details
- Re-issue due to expiry (with same details and address)
- Additional booklet pages needed

> When police verification is not needed, your passport may be issued within 1–3 days of your PSK visit.

## Processing Time

| Type | Normal Processing | Tatkal Processing |
|------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Without police verification | 1–7 working days | 1–3 working days |
| With police verification | 2–4 weeks | 1–2 weeks |

> **Note:** Processing time starts after your PSK/POPSK appointment. Tatkal applications are processed on priority but still depend on police verification timelines.

## How to Track Passport Renewal Status

### Method 1: Online via Passport Seva Portal

1. Go to [passportindia.gov.in](https://www.passportindia.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Track Application Status"**
3. Select **"Passport/PCC/IC/GEP"**
4. Enter your **File Number** (from your application receipt)
5. Enter your **Date of Birth**
6. Click **Submit** to see the current status

### Method 2: Via mPassport Seva App

1. Download **mPassport Seva** app from Google Play Store or Apple App Store
2. Open the app and go to **"Track Application Status"**
3. Enter your File Number and Date of Birth
4. View your status instantly

### Method 3: Via SMS

- SMS **STATUS space FILE NUMBER** to **9704100100**
- Example: `STATUS AB1234567890123`

### Method 4: Call the Helpline

- Call **1800-258-1800** (toll-free) for passport-related queries

## Important Tips for Passport Renewal

- **Apply early** — Don't wait till your passport expires. You can apply up to 1 year before expiry
- **Keep photocopies** of all documents (old passport pages, Aadhaar, etc.)
- **Reach PSK on time** — Late arrival may lead to your appointment being cancelled
- **Dress code** — Avoid wearing white clothes for the photo (background is white)
- **ECR/ECNR** — If you're a graduate (10th pass or above), you can apply for ECNR (Emigration Check Not Required) stamp during renewal
- **Address mismatch?** Update your Aadhaar address first, then apply for renewal

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Can I renew my passport online without visiting a PSK?

**No.** You can fill the form and pay fees online, but you must visit a PSK or POPSK in person for document verification and biometric capture. There is no fully online process.

### Q2: My passport expired 5 years ago. Can I still renew it?

**Yes.** Even if your passport has been expired for a long time, you can apply for re-issue. You'll need to submit the old expired passport along with your application.

### Q3: Do I need to submit my old passport during renewal?

**Yes.** You must carry the original old passport to the PSK. It will be returned to you after cancellation (punched/stamped as cancelled).

### Q4: How long is the renewed passport valid for?

A renewed passport is valid for **10 years** from the date of issue (for adults). For minors under 18, it is valid for **5 years** or until they turn 18, whichever is earlier.

### Q5: Can I change my address during passport renewal?

**Yes.** You can update your address during the re-issue process. Just provide valid address proof for the new address. Police verification will be required for the new address.

### Q6: What is the difference between renewal and re-issue?

There is **no difference** — "renewal" is the common term, but officially the process is called **"Re-issue of Passport"** on the Passport Seva portal.

### Q7: Can I apply for tatkal passport renewal at a POPSK?

**No.** Tatkal services are only available at Passport Seva Kendras (PSKs), not at Post Office Passport Seva Kendras (POPSKs).

### Q8: What if I lost my passport? Can I still renew?

**Yes**, but you'll need to file an **FIR** at your local police station first. Submit the FIR copy along with your re-issue application. Police verification will be mandatory.
