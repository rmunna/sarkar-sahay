# HDFC Bank Dormant Account Reactivation 2026 — Complete Guide

HDFC Bank marks an account dormant after **24 consecutive months** with no customer-initiated debit transaction. Credit entries (salary received, interest credited) don't count toward keeping the account active. Here's how to reactivate your HDFC dormant account.

---

## HDFC Dormant Account — What Gets Restricted

| Feature | Active Account | Dormant Account |
|---------|---------------|-----------------|
| ATM cash withdrawals | ✅ Available | ❌ Blocked |
| Mobile/internet banking debits | ✅ Available | ❌ Blocked |
| UPI / PhonePe / GPay | ✅ Available | ❌ Blocked |
| Credit/salary received | ✅ Available | ✅ Continues |
| Balance enquiry | ✅ Available | ✅ Available |
| Cheque clearing (inward) | ✅ Available | ✅ Continues |

---

## Method 1: HDFC Mobile App (Online Reactivation — Where Available)

HDFC Mobile Banking may allow reactivation for some dormant accounts:

1. Open **HDFC Mobile Banking** app → Login
2. Go to **"Accounts"** → Your dormant account → **"Account Services"**
3. If **"Reactivate Account"** option appears:
   - Update KYC (Aadhaar OTP or document upload)
   - Submit reactivation request
4. **Processing: 24–48 hours**
5. Test with small transfer to confirm

If option not available: Branch visit required.

---

## Method 2: HDFC Net Banking

1. Login at **[netbanking.hdfcbank.com](https://netbanking.hdfcbank.com)**
2. **"Accounts"** → Dormant account → **"Account Summary"**
3. Look for **"Reactivate Account"** or **"Request"** option
4. Submit KYC update if prompted
5. **Processing: 2–3 working days**

---

## Method 3: HDFC Branch (Reliable for All Cases)

1. Visit any **HDFC Bank branch** (home branch preferred — faster processing)
2. Carry:
   - **Aadhaar card** (original + self-attested copy)
   - **PAN card** (original + copy)
   - **HDFC account passbook or welcome letter**
3. At counter: Request **"Dormant Account Reactivation"**
4. Fill reactivation form
5. Perform a **fresh debit transaction**: Withdraw ₹500 from counter (cash withdrawal triggers reactivation)
6. **Same-day reactivation**: Account active by end of business day

**HDFC Customer Care:** **1800-202-6161** (can guide nearest branch + check status)

---

## HDFC Dormant Account — KYC Requirement

HDFC requires valid KYC before reactivation:
- Aadhaar + PAN must be linked and verified
- If KYC is pending: Complete KYC update simultaneously during reactivation
- HDFC Video KYC can be used to complete KYC before branch reactivation attempt

---

## HDFC Dormant Account — 10 Year DEAF Transfer

- After 10 years of dormancy: Balance transferred to **RBI's DEAF Fund**
- Account still exists at HDFC — not closed
- To claim: Visit HDFC home branch → Fill claim form → Takes 3–7 working days for DEAF amount recovery

## Frequently Asked Questions

**HDFC account dormant for 3 years — is it too late to reactivate?**
No — HDFC accounts are reactivable up to 10 years. After 10 years, DEAF transfer applies but is still claimable. Go to HDFC home branch immediately.

**HDFC dormant account — I'm in a different city. Must I go to home branch?**
Any HDFC branch can process reactivation, but home branch is faster. Non-home branches may need to send a request to home branch — adds 1–2 extra days.

**HDFC reactivation via call centre — is it possible?**
HDFC call centre (1800-202-6161) cannot reactivate dormant accounts remotely. They can guide you and check your account status. Physical verification (branch or video KYC) is required.

**My HDFC dormant account has pending EMI deductions — will they fail?**
Standing instructions (EMI, SIP) fail on dormant accounts. After reactivation, you must resume all standing instructions manually. Contact HDFC loan/SIP team separately for any pending dues.

**HDFC charged me annual fees during dormancy — can I get a refund?**
HDFC may waive maintenance charges for the dormancy period upon request. Raise a complaint at the branch or call 1800-202-6161. HDFC has waived charges in many cases when the account was genuinely dormant due to customer circumstances.
