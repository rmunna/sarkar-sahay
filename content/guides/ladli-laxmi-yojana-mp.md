# Ladli Laxmi Yojana 2.0 MP: Eligibility, Benefits & How to Apply Online

Madhya Pradesh's Ladli Laxmi Yojana is one of India's most successful girl child welfare schemes. Launched on 1 April 2007 and upgraded to version 2.0, this scheme provides financial assistance of **Rs 1,43,000** to families of girl children to promote their education, health, and overall development. The scheme aims to change societal attitudes towards girl children and improve the sex ratio in the state.

---

## What is Ladli Laxmi Yojana 2.0?

Ladli Laxmi Yojana 2.0 is a flagship scheme of the Government of Madhya Pradesh that provides financial support to girl children from birth through education and up to age 21. The government issues an **Assurance Certificate (Aashwasan Patra)** of Rs 1,43,000 in the girl's name, which is disbursed in installments linked to her educational milestones.

Under version 2.0, the state government also bears the tuition fees for higher education (graduation level) of registered Ladli girls.

---

## Who is Eligible?

To benefit from Ladli Laxmi Yojana 2.0, the following conditions must be met:

1. **Birth date:** The girl child must be born on or after 1 January 2006
2. **Registration:** The girl must be registered at a local Anganwadi centre
3. **Domicile:** Parents must be permanent residents of Madhya Pradesh
4. **Income tax:** Parents should not be income tax payers
5. **Family planning:** For the second girl child, parents must have adopted family planning after two children
6. **First child:** If the first child is a girl and parents have not adopted family planning, only the first two girl children are eligible
7. **Orphaned/adopted girls:** Girls adopted by families can also be registered if legal adoption documents are provided

---

## Benefits and Financial Assistance

| Milestone | Amount |
|-----------|--------|
| Admission in Class 6 | Rs 2,000 |
| Admission in Class 9 | Rs 4,000 |
| Admission in Class 11 | Rs 6,000 |
| Admission in Class 12 | Rs 6,000 |
| Graduation/Professional course (min 2 years) | Rs 25,000 (in 2 installments) |
| Final payment at age 21 (after Class 12 exam) | Rs 1,00,000 |
| **Total** | **Rs 1,43,000** |

**Additional benefit under 2.0:** The state government pays tuition fees for higher education (graduation) for registered Ladli girls.

## Documents Required

- **Birth certificate** of the girl child
- **Aadhaar card** of the girl and parents (see our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online))
- **Domicile certificate** of parents (MP resident proof)
- **Ration card** or family ID
- **Passport-size photographs** of the girl child
- **Bank account details** (mother's or girl's account)
- **Anganwadi registration** proof
- **Family planning certificate** (for second girl child)
- **Adoption certificate** (if applicable)

## How to Apply: Step-by-Step Process

### Online Application

1. Visit the official portal at [ladlilaxmi.mp.gov.in](https://ladlilaxmi.mp.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"आवेदन करें"** (Apply Now)
3. Select **Normal** or **Special** category as applicable
4. Enter the required details — mother's/father's Aadhaar, mobile number
5. Verify via OTP sent to the registered mobile number
6. Fill in the complete application form with girl child's details
7. Upload required documents (birth certificate, photographs, etc.)
8. Submit the application
9. Note down the **Ladli Registration Number** for future reference
10. The application will be verified by the local Anganwadi worker and project officer

### Offline Application

1. Visit your nearest **Anganwadi centre** or **Nagar Nigam/Nagar Palika** office
2. Collect the Ladli Laxmi Yojana application form
3. Fill in the form with accurate details
4. Attach all required documents
5. Submit the form to the Anganwadi worker
6. The application will be forwarded to the Women and Child Development Department
7. After verification, the Assurance Certificate will be issued

---

## Scholarship Disbursement Process

Once registered, scholarship amounts are automatically credited through **DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer)** to the linked bank account when the girl reaches each educational milestone. The school/college needs to verify the enrollment through the Shiksha Portal.

---

## Processing Time

- **Application verification:** 30-60 days
- **Assurance certificate issuance:** Within 60 days of successful verification
- **Scholarship credit:** Within 30 days of milestone verification by the school

---

## Important Tips

1. **Register early** — Apply within one year of the girl's birth for smooth processing
2. **Keep Aadhaar updated** — Ensure the girl's Aadhaar is linked for DBT payments
3. **Track your application** — Use the Ladli Registration Number on the official portal to check status
4. **School verification is mandatory** — Scholarships are released only after the school verifies enrollment on Shiksha Portal
5. **Marriage age compliance** — The final payment of Rs 1,00,000 is released only if the girl is not married before the legal age (18 years)

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Can a family register more than one girl under Ladli Laxmi Yojana?
Yes, up to two girl children can be registered, provided parents have adopted family planning after the second child.

### 2. What if parents are not income tax payers but have good income?
The key condition is that parents should not be income tax payers. There is no specific income limit mentioned.

### 3. Can girls born before 2006 apply?
No, only girls born on or after 1 January 2006 are eligible for this scheme.

### 4. Is there any fee for applying?
No, the application process is completely free both online and offline.

### 5. What happens if the girl drops out of school?
Scholarship installments are linked to educational milestones. If the girl drops out, subsequent installments will not be released, but the final amount may still be available at age 21 subject to conditions.

### 6. Can NRI families from MP apply?
Parents must be permanent residents of Madhya Pradesh. NRI families are not eligible.

### 7. How can I check the application status?
Visit [ladlilaxmi.mp.gov.in](https://ladlilaxmi.mp.gov.in/) and enter your Ladli Registration Number to check the current status.
