**Mukhyamantri Noni Sashaktikaran Sahayata Yojana** is a Chhattisgarh government scheme that provides **₹20,000 financial assistance** to unmarried daughters (aged 18–21 years) of registered unorganised sector workers. The scheme aims to empower girls from economically weaker families by supporting their education, skill development, self-employment or marriage expenses.

## What is Noni Sashaktikaran Yojana?

"Noni" means daughter in Chhattisgarhi. This scheme specifically targets girls from families where the father/mother is a registered construction worker or unorganised sector labourer under the Chhattisgarh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. The ₹20,000 assistance is provided as a one-time grant to the first two daughters of the family.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Unmarried girls** aged **18 to 21 years**
- Father or mother must be a **registered worker** under the Chhattisgarh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (or state labour welfare board)
- The worker must have been **registered for at least 1 year** and contributions must be up to date
- **Permanent resident** of Chhattisgarh
- Available for a **maximum of 2 daughters** per family
- The girl must be **unmarried** at the time of application
- Family must belong to the **unorganised/construction worker category**
- Must not have received similar assistance from any other state scheme

## Benefits

| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Financial assistance | ₹20,000 per eligible daughter |
| Maximum per family | 2 daughters (total ₹40,000) |
| Purpose | Education, skill training, self-employment or marriage |
| Transfer mode | Direct Benefit Transfer to girl's bank account |

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar card of the girl
- Aadhaar card of the registered worker (parent)
- Labour registration card (Shramik Panjikaran Patra) of the parent
- Age proof of the girl (birth certificate, Class 10 marksheet)
- Bank account passbook of the girl (in her name, Aadhaar-linked)
- Passport-size photographs
- Residence/domicile certificate of Chhattisgarh
- Self-declaration of being unmarried
- Family identity documents (ration card)

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Online Application

1. **Ensure** the parent's labour registration is active and contribution is up to date
2. **Visit** the Chhattisgarh Labour Department portal at [cglabour.nic.in](https://cglabour.nic.in/)
3. **Login** using the parent's Shramik registration number
4. **Navigate** to "Noni Sashaktikaran Sahayata Yojana" under schemes
5. **Fill** the application form — girl's details, age, Aadhaar, bank account
6. **Upload** required documents (Aadhaar, age proof, bank passbook, photographs)
7. **Submit** the application and note the reference number
8. **Application is verified** by the district labour office
9. **Once approved**, ₹20,000 is transferred to the girl's bank account via DBT

### Offline Application

1. **Visit** the District Labour Office or Lok Seva Kendra
2. **Collect** the Noni Sashaktikaran application form
3. **Fill** the form and attach photocopies of all documents
4. **Submit** to the labour office for processing
5. **Verification** is conducted by the district authority
6. **Amount credited** to the girl's bank account after approval

## Fees

There is **no application fee** for this scheme.

## Processing Time

- **Application processing:** 30–45 days
- **Verification:** 15–30 days
- **DBT disbursement:** Within 15 days of approval
- **Total timeline:** 2–3 months from application

## Important Tips

1. **Register as a labourer first** — the parent must have a valid Shramik Panjikaran before the girl can apply
2. **Keep labour registration renewed** — contributions must be current or the application will be rejected
3. **Open the bank account in the girl's name** — the amount is transferred only to the beneficiary girl's account
4. **Apply before turning 21** — the upper age limit is strict
5. **Both daughters can apply** — if you have two eligible daughters, submit separate applications for each

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a girl from a non-labour family apply?

No. The parent must be a **registered worker** under the Chhattisgarh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board or equivalent state labour welfare board.

### Is this scheme only for construction workers' daughters?

Primarily yes, but it covers daughters of all **unorganised sector workers** registered under the state welfare board, including construction workers.

### Can married girls apply?

No. The girl must be **unmarried** at the time of application. Marriage after receiving the benefit does not require repayment.

### How do I register as a construction worker in Chhattisgarh?

Visit the District Labour Office with your Aadhaar, address proof, photographs and evidence of working in the construction/unorganised sector for at least 90 days. Registration can also be done through [cglabour.nic.in](https://cglabour.nic.in/).

### Can the third daughter in a family also get the benefit?

No. The scheme is limited to a **maximum of 2 daughters per family**.

### What can the ₹20,000 be used for?

The amount can be used for **education, skill development training, self-employment or marriage expenses**. There is no restriction on the specific use after the money is credited.
