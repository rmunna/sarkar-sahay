## What is UP Saur Urja Sahayata Yojana?

**Saur Urja Sahayata Yojana** is a scheme by the **Uttar Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (UPBOCW)** that provides **free 2 KW solar rooftop panels** to registered construction workers (shramiks) in Uttar Pradesh. The scheme eliminates electricity bills for eligible workers.

---

## Key Benefits

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Solar system size | 2 KW rooftop solar panels |
| Cost to beneficiary | Free — 100% funded by UPBOCW |
| Monthly electricity generation | ~200–240 units/month (enough for basic household needs) |
| Electricity bill savings | Eliminates monthly bill for most households |
| Additional benefit | Net metering — sell excess power back to DISCOM |

---

## Who is Eligible?

**You ARE eligible if:**
- You are a **registered construction worker** (shramik) under **UPBOCW**
- Your UPBOCW registration is **active** (renewed annually by paying ₹20/year)
- You have been registered for **at least 1 year**
- You own a house where solar panels can be installed (roof must be suitable)

**You are NOT eligible if:**
- You are not registered under UPBOCW
- Your registration has lapsed
- You are already covered under PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana for the same property

---

## How to Register as a Construction Worker (UPBOCW)

If you work in construction (mason, carpenter, plumber, painter, electrician, helper, welding, etc.) but are not yet registered:

1. Go to **[upbocw.in](https://upbocw.in)**
2. Click on **"Shramik Registration"**
3. Enter your Aadhaar number and register
4. Upload employment certificate from contractor or self-declaration
5. Pay ₹20/year registration fee
6. Get your **UPBOCW Registration Number**

After 1 year of registration, you become eligible for all UPBOCW benefits including solar panels.

## How to Apply for Solar Panels — Step by Step

### Step 1 — Login to UPBOCW Portal

Go to **[upbocw.in](https://upbocw.in)** and log in with your UPBOCW registration number.

### Step 2 — Apply for Saur Urja Sahayata

Navigate to:
**Schemes → Saur Urja Sahayata Yojana → Apply**

### Step 3 — Fill Application

Provide:
- Your registration number
- House address (where solar panels will be installed)
- Type of roof (RCC/flat — suitable for panels)
- Electricity connection number (from DISCOM)

### Step 4 — Upload Documents

- Aadhaar card
- UPBOCW registration card
- House ownership proof
- Electricity bill (latest)
- Photograph of roof

### Step 5 — Verification and Installation

After application approval:
- UPBOCW/DISCOM verifies house roof suitability
- Empanelled solar vendor installs the 2KW solar system on your roof
- Net meter is installed to measure export to grid

### Step 6 — Start Saving on Bills

Solar system generates ~200 units/month. Your electricity bill drops to near zero for most household needs.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. I am a construction worker but my house is a rented property. Am I eligible?**

The scheme requires **house ownership** — the solar panels are installed on your own house roof. Rented/leased property is typically not eligible as panels are a permanent fixture.

**2. How long does it take from application to installation?**

Typically 2–4 months from application to installation, depending on queue and DISCOM approval.

**3. Can I sell extra electricity back to UPPCL?**

Yes. With **net metering**, if your solar panels generate more than you consume, the extra units are credited to your UPPCL account, reducing your future bills. You effectively earn back from the grid.

**4. Is UPBOCW registration free?**

The initial registration is processed online at [upbocw.in](https://upbocw.in). There is a nominal annual contribution (₹20/year), but registration assistance is free.

**5. I applied 6 months ago but no update. What do I do?**

Log in to [upbocw.in](https://upbocw.in) and check application status. Also contact the **District Labour Welfare Officer** (DLO) in your district for follow-up.
