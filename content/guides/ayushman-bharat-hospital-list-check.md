# Ayushman Bharat Hospital List: How to Find Empanelled Hospitals Near You

Under the Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY scheme, you can get free cashless treatment up to ₹5 lakh per year — but only at empanelled hospitals. This guide shows you how to find PM-JAY empanelled hospitals in your area, check package rates, and verify a hospital's empanelment status.

## What Are Empanelled Hospitals?

Empanelled hospitals are healthcare facilities (government and private) that have been approved by the National Health Authority (NHA) to provide cashless treatment under PM-JAY. These hospitals have agreed to treat PM-JAY beneficiaries at pre-defined package rates set by the government.

There are over **29,000 empanelled hospitals** across India, including both public and private facilities.

---

## How to Search for PM-JAY Hospitals Online

### Method 1: Search on pmjay.gov.in

1. Visit the official PM-JAY hospital finder at [hospitals.pmjay.gov.in](https://hospitals.pmjay.gov.in/) or [pmjay.gov.in/hospital](https://pmjay.gov.in/hospital)
2. Select your **State**
3. Select your **District**
4. Choose **Hospital Type** (Public/Private/All)
5. Choose **Speciality** (if looking for specific treatment)
6. Click **Search**
7. The results will show hospital names, addresses, contact numbers, and empanelment status

### Method 2: Call the Helpline

- Call **14555** (toll-free, 24x7)
- Provide your district and the type of treatment you need
- The helpline will give you the nearest empanelled hospitals

### Method 3: Ayushman Bharat App

- Download the **Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY)** app from Google Play Store
- Search for hospitals by location and speciality
- The app also shows real-time empanelment status

### Method 4: Visit a Common Service Centre (CSC)

- Go to the nearest CSC and ask the operator to search for empanelled hospitals in your area
- The CSC operator can also help generate your Ayushman card

## State-Wise Hospital List

PM-JAY is implemented across most states and union territories. Some key points:
- **Most states** use the PM-JAY framework directly
- **Some states** have merged PM-JAY with their own health schemes (e.g., Ayushman Bharat-MJPJAY in Maharashtra, Ayushman Bharat-Mahatma Jyotiba Phule Jan Arogya Yojana)
- **Delhi, West Bengal, and Odisha** had initially opted out but may have their own state health schemes

To check state-specific hospital lists:
1. Visit [pmjay.gov.in/hospital](https://pmjay.gov.in/hospital)
2. Select your state from the dropdown
3. Browse the complete list of empanelled hospitals in your state

## PM-JAY Package Rates: What's Covered?

PM-JAY covers over **1,900 treatment packages** across 27 specialities. Package rates are pre-defined and include:
- Consultation fees
- Surgery/procedure charges
- Medicine and consumables
- Diagnostic tests
- Room charges (up to 5 days post-surgery)
- Food for the patient
- Follow-up care (up to 15 days post-discharge)

### Key Specialities Covered

| Speciality | Example Treatments |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Cardiology | Bypass surgery, angioplasty, pacemaker |
| Oncology | Chemotherapy, radiation, tumour removal |
| Orthopaedics | Knee replacement, hip replacement, fracture surgery |
| Neurosurgery | Brain tumour surgery, spinal surgery |
| Nephrology | Dialysis, kidney transplant |
| Ophthalmology | Cataract surgery, glaucoma treatment |
| Obstetrics | C-section, normal delivery (complicated) |
| General Surgery | Hernia, appendectomy, gallbladder removal |

**Note:** Outpatient (OPD) treatment is generally NOT covered under PM-JAY. Only hospitalisation (minimum 24-hour admission) is covered.

## How to Check If a Hospital Is Empanelled

If a hospital claims to accept Ayushman Bharat cards, verify:

1. **Online:** Search the hospital name at [hospitals.pmjay.gov.in](https://hospitals.pmjay.gov.in/)
2. **Helpline:** Call 14555 and ask about the specific hospital
3. **At the hospital:** Look for the PM-JAY empanelment board/poster at the entrance
4. **Ayushman Mitra:** Every empanelled hospital should have an Ayushman Mitra (help desk) to assist PM-JAY patients

### Red Flags
- Hospital asks you to pay upfront for covered treatments — **report this**
- Hospital says "PM-JAY not available today" — **call 14555**
- Hospital charges extra beyond the package rate — **this is a violation**

## How Hospitals Get Empanelled

For reference, hospitals must meet these criteria to join PM-JAY:
- Minimum 10 beds (relaxed for speciality hospitals)
- Registered under Clinical Establishments Act or equivalent
- Valid fire safety and biomedical waste certificates
- Adequate medical staff and infrastructure
- Apply through the NHA empanelment portal

## Important Tips

1. **Search before you go** — Always check empanelment status online before visiting a hospital
2. **Check speciality coverage** — Not all empanelled hospitals offer all specialities; verify the specific treatment is available
3. **Carry your documents** — Take your Ayushman card, [Aadhaar](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online), and ration card to the hospital
4. **Don't pay for covered treatments** — PM-JAY treatment is cashless; if asked to pay, report on 14555
5. **Check your eligibility first** — Make sure you're a PM-JAY beneficiary before visiting; see our [eligibility guide](/guide/ayushman-bharat-eligibility-check)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. How many hospitals are empanelled under Ayushman Bharat?
Over 29,000 hospitals (both government and private) are empanelled under PM-JAY across India. The number is regularly updated as new hospitals join.

### Q2. Can I get treatment at a government hospital using Ayushman card?
Yes. Most government district hospitals and medical college hospitals are empanelled under PM-JAY. Treatment is cashless.

### Q3. What if no empanelled hospital is available in my district?
You can visit an empanelled hospital in any other district or state. PM-JAY is portable — you can get treatment anywhere in India at any empanelled hospital.

### Q4. Can the hospital refuse treatment if I have an Ayushman card?
No. An empanelled hospital cannot refuse treatment to a verified PM-JAY beneficiary for covered procedures. If refused, call 14555 immediately.

### Q5. Are private hospitals included in the Ayushman Bharat list?
Yes. Both government and private hospitals can get empanelled. Many private hospitals across India accept PM-JAY cards.

### Q6. How do I know if a specific treatment is covered under PM-JAY?
Check the package list on [pmjay.gov.in](https://pmjay.gov.in/) or call 14555. The Ayushman Mitra at the hospital can also confirm if your treatment is covered.

### Q7. Is emergency treatment covered at non-empanelled hospitals?
PM-JAY cashless benefit is only at empanelled hospitals. In an emergency, seek treatment at the nearest hospital and then contact 14555 for guidance on reimbursement or transfer to an empanelled facility.
