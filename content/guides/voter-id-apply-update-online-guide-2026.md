# Voter ID (EPIC) Apply & Update Online India 2026 — Complete Guide

Voter ID (officially EPIC — Electoral Photo Identity Card) is your right as an Indian citizen aged 18+. Since 2022, voter registration and updates are fully digitized through the **Voter Service Portal** (voters.eci.gov.in). Here's how to apply, update, and download your Voter ID.

---

## Types of Voter ID Services

| Service | Form | Online? |
|---------|------|---------|
| **New Voter Registration** | Form 6 | ✅ Fully online |
| **Correction in Voter Card** (name/DOB/photo) | Form 8 | ✅ Fully online |
| **Address Change** (same constituency) | Form 8A | ✅ Fully online |
| **Transfer to new constituency** | Form 6 (as new registration) | ✅ Online |
| **Deletion of entry** (deceased / migrated) | Form 7 | ✅ Online |
| **Download e-EPIC** | — | ✅ Free online |

---

## Who Can Apply for Voter ID?

- Indian citizen aged **18+ years** (as of January 1 of the registration year)
- Residing at the declared address in India
- Not already a voter in another constituency
- NRI Indians: Can register in their constituency of origin (Form 6A)

---

## Apply for New Voter ID Online

1. Go to **[voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in)** (National Voter Service Portal)
2. Click **"Register as New Voter"** → **"Form 6"**
3. Login with mobile number → OTP
4. Fill Form 6:
   - Personal details (name, DOB, gender)
   - Current address details (must match proof)
   - Assembly constituency (auto-detected from address)
   - Upload documents
5. Upload:
   - **Age proof** (any one): Aadhaar, PAN, birth certificate, school certificate
   - **Address proof** (any one): Aadhaar, utility bill, bank passbook, rent agreement
   - **Recent passport-size photograph** (JPEG, < 2 MB)
6. Submit → **Reference number generated**
7. **BLO (Booth Level Officer) verifies** and adds to electoral roll
8. **Processing: 15–30 days** (before election dates: expedited)

---

## Download e-EPIC (Digital Voter ID) — Free

1. voters.eci.gov.in → **"Download e-EPIC"**
2. Enter EPIC number (on old Voter ID card) OR registered mobile number
3. OTP verification
4. e-EPIC (PDF) downloaded → Store on phone
5. **e-EPIC is legally valid for all purposes** (elections, KYC, identity proof)

*e-EPIC introduced in 2021 — digitally signed, tamper-proof PDF.*

---

## Update/Correct Voter ID — Form 8

For name correction, DOB correction, or photo update:

1. voters.eci.gov.in → **"Correction in Voter ID"** → **"Form 8"**
2. Enter EPIC number + OTP
3. Select what to correct: Name / DOB / Photo
4. Upload supporting document (Aadhaar with correct details, for name/DOB)
5. Upload new photo (for photo update)
6. Submit → Reference number
7. BLO verifies → Correction made
8. **Processing: 15–30 days**

---

## Change Address on Voter ID — Form 8A / Form 6

**If you moved within the same constituency (assembly): Form 8A**
1. voters.eci.gov.in → Form 8A
2. Enter new address + upload new address proof
3. Constituency remains same — only address updated

**If you moved to a different constituency: Delete + New Registration**
1. Delete entry at old constituency: Form 7 (or it auto-deletes when new registration is verified)
2. Register at new constituency: Form 6
3. Your new Voter ID card is issued for the new constituency
4. **You can only vote at the constituency where you're registered**

---

## Check Voter ID / Registration Status

1. voters.eci.gov.in → **"Search in Electoral Roll"**
2. Enter: Name + state + district + constituency → Search
3. OR enter EPIC number directly

## NRI Voter Registration — Special Process

NRI Indian citizens can register as voters in their home constituency:

1. voters.eci.gov.in → **"NRI Voter"** → Form 6A
2. Upload: Indian passport copy + current overseas address proof
3. BLO verifies → NRI voter registered
4. NRI voters must be physically present in India to vote (postal ballot for NRI not yet implemented for Lok Sabha)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**My Voter ID has wrong name spelling — how to fix?**
Use Form 8 online (voters.eci.gov.in → Correction → Form 8). Select "Name" correction. Upload Aadhaar or other document with correct spelling. BLO verifies and corrects. New Voter ID card dispatched or e-EPIC updated.

**I lost my Voter ID — how to get a duplicate?**
Download e-EPIC (digital voter ID) — free and instant from voters.eci.gov.in → Download e-EPIC. For physical card replacement: voters.eci.gov.in → Replacement → Submit application → New card delivered in 15–30 days.

**Can I vote from anywhere in India with my Voter ID?**
No — Voter ID is constituency-specific. You can only vote at your registered polling station. If you've moved: Update address/transfer constituency via Form 8A / Form 6. Otherwise your vote is useless.

**Is there a last date to register for voting?**
Continuous enrollment: You can register any time. However, enrollment is "frozen" 30 days before elections in that constituency. Register early — don't wait for election season (massive queues and delays).

**Aadhaar-Voter ID linking — is it mandatory?**
Linking Aadhaar to Voter ID is voluntary (as per Supreme Court ruling). Government encourages it to remove duplicate entries. Link via voters.eci.gov.in → "Aadhaar-EPIC Link" → Enter EPIC number + Aadhaar → OTP. Does NOT make either document mandatory for voting.
