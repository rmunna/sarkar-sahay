# Anna Bhagya Scheme Karnataka – 10 kg Free Rice Per Month

## What is Anna Bhagya Scheme?

Anna Bhagya is Karnataka's food security scheme providing **10 kg of free rice per month** to every member of BPL (Below Poverty Line) and Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) ration card households. This is in addition to the rice provided under the National Food Security Act (NFSA). The scheme ensures that the poorest families in Karnataka have access to sufficient food grains.

Under NFSA, eligible families already receive 5 kg of rice per person per month at ₹1/kg. Anna Bhagya provides an **additional 5 kg free**, making it a total of **10 kg per person per month** (5 kg at ₹1 + 5 kg free).

## Who is Eligible?

1. **BPL ration card holders** in Karnataka
2. **Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY)** card holders
3. **Priority Household (PHH)** card holders under NFSA
4. Must be a **resident of Karnataka** with a valid ration card

### Who is NOT Eligible?

- APL (Above Poverty Line) ration card holders
- Families not covered under NFSA
- Households without a valid Karnataka ration card

## Documents Required

- **BPL / AAY / PHH Ration Card** (Karnataka-issued)
- **Aadhaar Card** of the ration card holder (for biometric verification at fair price shops)

## How to Avail Anna Bhagya Rice

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Ensure you have a valid BPL/AAY ration card** issued by the Karnataka Food & Civil Supplies Department
2. **Visit your designated Fair Price Shop (FPS)** — the shop assigned to your ration card
3. **Carry your ration card and Aadhaar card**
4. **Authenticate via biometric (fingerprint)** at the electronic Point of Sale (ePoS) machine at the shop
5. **Collect your monthly rice quota** — the shopkeeper will provide the entitled quantity
6. **Check the quantity** before leaving — ensure you receive the full 10 kg per member

### If You Don't Have a Ration Card

1. Apply for a new ration card at [ahara.kar.nic.in](https://ahara.kar.nic.in)
2. Visit your taluk's Food & Civil Supplies office with income proof, address proof, and Aadhaar
3. See our [ration card guide](/guide/ration-card-apply-online) for detailed steps

## Entitlement Details

| Category | NFSA Quota | Anna Bhagya Extra | Total Per Person/Month |
|----------|-----------|-------------------|----------------------|
| BPL/PHH | 5 kg at ₹1/kg | 5 kg free | **10 kg** |
| AAY | 35 kg/household at ₹1/kg | Additional free rice | **35 kg + extra** |

## Processing Time

- **No separate application needed** — benefit is automatic for eligible ration card holders
- Just visit your FPS monthly with your ration card and Aadhaar

## Important Tips

1. **Biometric Authentication:** Your fingerprint must match at the ePoS machine. Keep your fingers clean and dry.
2. **Visit Early in the Month:** Rice stocks at FPS can run out. Visit in the first two weeks.
3. **Check Entitlement Online:** Visit [ahara.kar.nic.in](https://ahara.kar.nic.in) to check your entitlement and FPS allocation.
4. **Report Issues:** If your FPS denies rice or provides less quantity, call the food helpline **1967** or complain at the taluk office.
5. **Update Aadhaar on Ration Card:** Ensure all family members' Aadhaar numbers are linked to the ration card for smooth biometric verification.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Do I need to apply separately for Anna Bhagya?
**No.** If you hold a valid BPL/AAY/PHH ration card in Karnataka, you are automatically eligible. Just visit your FPS.

### Q2: Can I get rice from any Fair Price Shop?
**No.** You must collect rice only from the FPS assigned to your ration card. Check your assigned shop at [ahara.kar.nic.in](https://ahara.kar.nic.in).

### Q3: What if the FPS says no stock is available?
Report to the taluk Food Inspector or call the helpline **1967**. FPS shops are mandated to maintain stock.

### Q4: Is the rice really free?
Under Anna Bhagya, the additional 5 kg is completely free. The first 5 kg under NFSA costs ₹1/kg. So you pay ₹5 total for 10 kg of rice per person.

### Q5: Can I get wheat instead of rice?
The scheme primarily provides rice. Wheat availability depends on your region and FPS stock.

### Q6: How many kg does a family of 4 get?
A PHH family of 4 would get **40 kg of rice per month** (10 kg × 4 members).
