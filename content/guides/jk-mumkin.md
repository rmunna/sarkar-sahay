## What is Mumkin Scheme?

The **Mumkin Scheme** is a livelihood generation initiative by the **Government of Jammu & Kashmir** aimed at providing self-employment opportunities to unemployed youth. Under this scheme, the government provides **subsidized commercial vehicles** to eligible youth so they can earn a livelihood through transport services.

The scheme operates on a **subsidy + loan model** where the government provides a subsidy on the vehicle cost and the remaining is financed through bank loans.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Age:** Must be between **18 to 35 years**
2. **Residency:** Must be a permanent resident of Jammu & Kashmir
3. **Education:** Minimum 8th pass (varies by vehicle category)
4. **Driving License:** Must hold a valid commercial driving license
5. **Employment:** Must be unemployed and not availing any other government self-employment scheme
6. **Income:** Family income should not exceed the prescribed limit

## Benefits

| Component | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Vehicle Subsidy | Government subsidy on vehicle cost (amount varies by vehicle type) |
| Bank Loan | Remaining cost financed through partner banks |
| Vehicle Types | Small commercial vehicles, passenger vehicles, goods carriers |
| Training | Skill development training provided |

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar Card**
- **Domicile Certificate** (J&K permanent resident)
- **Age Proof** (Birth Certificate / Aadhaar / 10th marksheet)
- **Educational Certificates** (minimum 8th pass)
- **Commercial Driving License** (valid and current)
- **Income Certificate** of family
- **Bank Account Details** (for loan processing)
- **Passport-size photographs**
- **Unemployment Certificate** (if available)

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Online Application

1. **Visit** the J&K EDI portal [jkedi.org](https://jkedi.org) or the designated scheme portal
2. **Register** with personal details and mobile number
3. **Fill** the application form: personal details, education, driving license, vehicle preference
4. **Upload** all required documents
5. **Submit** and note the application ID
6. **Wait** for shortlisting and interview call

### Offline Application

1. **Visit** the **District Employment & Counselling Centre** or **J&K EDI office**
2. **Collect** the Mumkin scheme application form
3. **Fill** and attach all documents
4. **Submit** to the designated officer
5. **Get** acknowledgement receipt

## Selection Process

1. **Application screening** by district-level committee
2. **Shortlisted candidates** called for counselling/interview
3. **Vehicle allocation** based on merit and availability
4. **Bank loan processing** initiated
5. **Subsidy sanctioned** by government
6. **Vehicle delivered** to the beneficiary

## Processing Time

- **Application Review:** 30-60 days
- **Selection & Counselling:** Depends on batch processing
- **Loan Approval:** 15-30 days (bank processing)
- **Vehicle Delivery:** After loan and subsidy sanction

## Important Tips

1. **Get Commercial License First:** A valid commercial driving license is mandatory — obtain it before applying
2. **Bank Account:** Have an active bank account for loan processing
3. **Training:** Attend all mandatory training sessions — this is part of the scheme
4. **Repay Loan On Time:** The bank loan portion must be repaid as per EMI schedule
5. **Vehicle Maintenance:** Maintain the vehicle properly — it's your livelihood asset

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. What type of vehicles are provided?
Small commercial vehicles like passenger vehicles, goods carriers, and light commercial vehicles.

### Q2. Is the vehicle free?
No, the government provides a **subsidy** and the rest is financed through a **bank loan** which must be repaid.

### Q3. What is the age limit?
Applicants must be between **18 to 35 years** of age.

### Q4. Do I need a driving license?
Yes, a **valid commercial driving license** is mandatory.

### Q5. Can women also apply?
Yes, women can also apply for the Mumkin scheme.

### Q6. What if I already have a job?
The scheme is specifically for **unemployed youth**. Employed persons are not eligible.

### Q7. Where can I apply?
Apply online at [jkedi.org](https://jkedi.org) or visit the District Employment Centre.
