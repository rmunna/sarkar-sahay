# Driving Licence Renewal Rejected — How to Fix

Getting your driving licence (DL) renewal application rejected by the RTO is frustrating, especially when you've already paid fees and submitted documents. This guide covers every common rejection reason, how to fix each one, and how to successfully re-apply through the Parivahan/Sarathi portal.

## Common Rejection Reasons and How to Fix Them

### 1. DL Expired for More Than 5 Years

Under the Motor Vehicles Act, if your driving licence has been expired for **more than 5 years**, a simple renewal may not be accepted. The RTO may require you to:

- Re-apply as a **fresh DL applicant** (obtain LL first, then DL)
- Appear for a **driving test** again at the RTO
- Some states allow renewal up to **10 years after expiry** with additional documentation

**How to fix:**
- If expired **less than 1 year**: Apply normally; no penalty in most states
- If expired **1–5 years**: Apply with a late fee (varies by state, typically ₹200–₹1,000)
- If expired **more than 5 years**: Contact your RTO directly — some states accept it with a driving test, others require a fresh application

> **Tip:** Don't wait. You can apply for renewal **up to 1 year before** the expiry date with no issues.

---

### 2. Medical Certificate Issues

A **Form 1A medical certificate** is mandatory for renewal if:

- You are **aged 40 or above** (for non-transport vehicles)
- You are renewing a **transport/commercial licence** (any age)
- The RTO specifically requests it

**Common medical certificate rejections:**

| Issue | Fix |
|-------|-----|
| Certificate not signed by registered medical practitioner | Get it signed by a doctor registered with the state medical council |
| Certificate older than 30 days | Obtain a fresh medical certificate |
| Missing stamp/registration number of the doctor | Ask the doctor to add their registration number and stamp |
| Wrong form used (not Form 1A) | Download and use the official **Form 1A** from the Sarathi portal |
| Medical condition flagged (eyesight, epilepsy) | Get a specialist certificate clearing you to drive |

---

### 3. Wrong Application Form

Using the wrong form is a common mistake:

- **Form 9**: For DL renewal (permanent driving licence)
- **Form 2**: For learner's licence (LL) — this is NOT for renewal
- **Form 1A**: Medical certificate (submitted along with Form 9)

**How to fix:** Log in to the [Sarathi portal](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/), select your state, and choose **"DL Renewal"** under Services. The portal auto-generates the correct form.

---

### 4. Address Mismatch

If your current address doesn't match the address on your existing DL, the application may be rejected.

**How to fix:**
- Apply for **"Change of Address + Renewal"** simultaneously on the Sarathi portal (combined service available)
- Submit valid address proof: Aadhaar card, passport, utility bill, bank statement, or rent agreement
- If you've moved to a **different state**, you may need to apply for a **transfer of DL** to the new state's RTO before or along with renewal

---

### 5. Photo and Signature Issues

**Common photo rejections:**

- Photo is blurry, low resolution, or not in passport size format
- Background is not white/light-coloured
- Face not clearly visible (sunglasses, cap, heavy filters)
- File size exceeds the portal's upload limit (usually **20 KB–200 KB**)
- Signature image is unclear or cropped

**How to fix:**
- Use a **recent passport-size photo** with white background
- Ensure dimensions are **200×230 pixels** (JPEG format)
- Signature should be on **white paper, black ink**, scanned clearly
- Compress the image if it exceeds the file size limit

---

### 6. Incomplete or Mismatched Documents

Other document-related rejection reasons:

- **Name mismatch** between DL and Aadhaar/ID proof — get it corrected on either document first
- **Missing DL number** or wrong DL number entered during application
- **Aadhaar not linked** to mobile number (OTP verification fails)
- **Old DL is damaged/illegible** — carry the original to the RTO for verification

## How to Re-Apply on Parivahan/Sarathi Portal

After fixing the rejection reason, follow these steps:

### Step-by-Step Process

1. Visit [https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/)
2. Select your **state** from the dropdown
3. Click **"DL Renewal"** under the Services menu
4. Enter your **DL number** and **date of birth**
5. Verify via **Aadhaar OTP** or upload documents manually
6. Fill in the application form — double-check every field
7. Upload **photo**, **signature**, and **Form 1A** (if applicable)
8. Pay the renewal fee online (₹200–₹500 depending on state and vehicle class)
9. **Book an appointment slot** if your state requires an RTO visit
10. Download and save the **application receipt**

> **Important:** If your previous application was rejected, you can re-apply immediately after fixing the issue. No waiting period.

For slot booking issues, see our guide: [Driving Licence Slot Not Available — How to Fix](/guide/driving-licence-slot-not-available-fix)

## Learner's Licence (LL) vs Driving Licence (DL) Renewal

| Aspect | Learner's Licence (LL) | Driving Licence (DL) |
|--------|----------------------|---------------------|
| **Validity** | 6 months | 20 years or until age 50 (whichever is earlier); 5 years after age 50 |
| **Renewal possible?** | No — you must re-apply for a fresh LL if expired | Yes — renewal is available |
| **Form** | Form 2 (fresh LL application) | Form 9 (DL renewal) |
| **Test required?** | Online test at RTO | Usually no test for timely renewal |
| **Medical certificate** | Not required for non-transport | Required if age 40+ or transport vehicle |
| **Fee** | ₹150–₹300 | ₹200–₹500 |

**Key point:** An expired LL cannot be "renewed." You must apply afresh. An expired DL can be renewed (with conditions based on how long it's been expired).

## State RTO Differences

DL renewal rules vary across Indian states:

| State | Online renewal available? | RTO visit required? | Notes |
|-------|--------------------------|--------------------|----|
| **Delhi** | Yes | No (for timely renewals) | Fully online; DL delivered by post |
| **Maharashtra** | Yes | Sometimes | Biometric verification may be required |
| **Karnataka** | Yes | No (most cases) | Online with Aadhaar eKYC |
| **Tamil Nadu** | Yes | Yes (usually) | RTO visit for photo/biometric capture |
| **Uttar Pradesh** | Yes | Yes | Appointment slot required |
| **Kerala** | Yes | Sometimes | Depends on RTO workload |
| **West Bengal** | Partial | Yes | Some services still offline |
| **Gujarat** | Yes | No (most cases) | Smooth online process |

> **Tip:** Even if your state supports fully online renewal, keep the original DL handy. Some RTOs may call you for physical verification.

## Penalty for Driving with an Expired DL

Under the **Motor Vehicles (Amendment) Act, 2019**:

| Offence | Penalty |
|---------|---------|
| Driving without a valid licence | **₹5,000** fine |
| Repeat offence | **₹10,000** fine and/or community service |
| Driving with expired DL (grace period over) | Treated as driving without a licence |
| Accident while driving with expired DL | Insurance claim may be **rejected**; criminal liability possible |

**Grace period:** Most states allow a **30-day grace period** after expiry. After that, you're legally driving without a licence.

> **Warning:** If you're in an accident with an expired DL, your motor insurance company can deny your claim entirely. Renew on time.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I renew my DL online without visiting the RTO?
Yes, in many states. Delhi, Karnataka, and Gujarat support fully online renewal for timely applications. Check the Sarathi portal for your state's process.

### My DL expired 3 years ago. Can I still renew it?
Yes. Apply through the Sarathi portal with a late fee. You generally won't need to retake a driving test if it's within 5 years.

### I moved to a different state. How do I renew?
Apply for a **transfer + renewal** on the Sarathi portal of your current state. You'll need an NOC from your old RTO (some states handle this online).

### How long does DL renewal take?
- **Online (no RTO visit):** 3–7 working days for dispatch
- **With RTO visit:** 7–30 days depending on state and slot availability

### How do I track my renewal application status?
Use the Sarathi portal's **"Application Status"** feature with your application number. For a detailed walkthrough, see: [How to Check Driving Licence Status](/guide/driving-licence-status-check)

### Is there a fee for re-applying after rejection?
In most states, you'll need to pay the application fee again. Some states allow re-submission without additional payment if the rejection was due to an RTO-side error.

## Quick Checklist Before Applying

- [ ] DL number and date of birth ready
- [ ] Aadhaar linked to active mobile number (for OTP)
- [ ] Recent passport-size photo (JPEG, white background, under 200 KB)
- [ ] Clear signature scan (black ink on white paper)
- [ ] Form 1A medical certificate (if age 40+ or transport DL)
- [ ] Address proof (if address has changed)
- [ ] Original DL (physical copy, for RTO visit)
- [ ] Payment method ready (debit card/UPI/net banking)
