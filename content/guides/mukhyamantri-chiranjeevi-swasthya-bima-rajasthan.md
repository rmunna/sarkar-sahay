## What is Chiranjeevi Swasthya Bima Yojana?

**Mukhyamantri Chiranjeevi Swasthya Bima Yojana (MMCSBY)** is Rajasthan's flagship health insurance scheme that provides **₹25 lakh cashless health insurance per family per year** to all families residing in Rajasthan — regardless of their economic status. It is one of India's most comprehensive state health insurance schemes.

Launched in May 2021, the scheme covers:
- All **BPL, SECC, and government-identified economically weak families** — absolutely FREE
- Other Rajasthan resident families — at **₹850/year premium** (very nominal amount for ₹25 lakh cover)
- Cashless treatment at **2,000+ empanelled government and private hospitals**

## Key Benefits

- **₹25 lakh cover per family per year** — for a wide range of medical treatments
- **Cashless hospitalisation** — no upfront payment needed at empanelled hospitals
- **1,800+ medical packages** covered including surgeries, dialysis, chemotherapy, ICU
- **COVID-19 treatment** included
- **OPD cover**: ₹2,500/year for OPD consultations and medicines (for card holders)
- **Critical illness:** Cancer, heart surgery, kidney transplant, organ transplant — all covered
- Pre-existing diseases are covered from Day 1 (no waiting period for most conditions)
- No restriction on family size — the entire family is covered

## Who is Eligible?

**Free (no premium) — all NFSA beneficiaries:**
- Families with **NFSA/food security ration cards** (PHH/AAY cardholders)
- SECC (Socio-Economic Caste Census) listed families
- Small/marginal farmers registered under PM Kisan
- Contractual employees of state government
- Construction workers registered under BOCW Board

**Paid registration (₹850/year per family):**
- Any **other family resident in Rajasthan** not covered in the above categories
- Government employees who are NOT covered under RGHS (Rajasthan Government Health Scheme)

## How to Register — Step by Step

### For BPL/NFSA Families (Free Registration)

If you are an NFSA ration card holder, you are **automatically registered**. You only need to activate your card:
1. Visit any e-Mitra kiosk or government hospital
2. Present your **Jan Aadhaar card** (Rajasthan family ID) or Aadhaar
3. Get your Chiranjeevi card activated

### For Other Families (₹850/year)

1. Go to **[chiranjeevi.rajasthan.gov.in](https://chiranjeevi.rajasthan.gov.in)** or **SSO portal** at [sso.rajasthan.gov.in](https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in)
2. Log in with your SSO ID (create one at sso.rajasthan.gov.in if not done)
3. Search for **"Chiranjeevi Swasthya Bima Yojana"**
4. Enter your **Jan Aadhaar number** (Rajasthan family ID)
5. Your family members auto-populate from Jan Aadhaar data
6. Pay ₹850 online (Debit card/UPI/Net banking)
7. Registration complete — download your policy

**At e-Mitra kiosk:**
1. Visit your nearest e-Mitra kiosk (available at most gram panchayat offices and urban areas)
2. Operator registers your family with Jan Aadhaar
3. Pay ₹850 at the kiosk

## Documents Required

- **Jan Aadhaar card** (Rajasthan family ID) — most important
- **Aadhaar card** (for Aadhaar-based verification if Jan Aadhaar not available)
- For free category: **NFSA ration card** / SECC certificate / PM Kisan registration

> **Jan Aadhaar is mandatory.** If you don't have one, first create it at janaadhaar.rajasthan.gov.in.

## How to Use the Insurance — At the Hospital

1. Go to any **Chiranjeevi-empanelled hospital** (government or private)
2. At the admission counter, say you want cashless treatment under Chiranjeevi Yojana
3. Present your **Jan Aadhaar card / Aadhaar** for biometric verification
4. Hospital staff will check your policy and pre-authorise the treatment
5. Get treated — **pay nothing** (the insurance pays directly to the hospital)
6. For planned surgeries, the hospital sends pre-authorisation request to the TPA (Third Party Administrator)
7. Emergency treatment starts first, pre-authorisation happens simultaneously

## Hospital List — Find Empanelled Hospitals

1. Go to **chiranjeevi.rajasthan.gov.in**
2. Click **"Hospital List"**
3. Filter by district, city, or type (government/private)
4. 2,000+ hospitals across Rajasthan are empanelled

Major private hospital chains empanelled: Fortis, Manipal, Narayana Health, Apollo (select centres), and hundreds of district-level private hospitals.

## Check Chiranjeevi Registration Status

1. Go to chiranjeevi.rajasthan.gov.in
2. Click **"Check Registration Status"**
3. Enter your Jan Aadhaar number
4. View: registration date, validity, and family members covered

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the ₹25 lakh per person or per family?
Per family per year — the entire family shares ₹25 lakh. If one family member uses ₹5 lakh for surgery, the remaining ₹20 lakh is available for other members in the same year.

### My family is not in Jan Aadhaar. Can I register?
First get your family registered in Jan Aadhaar at janaadhaar.rajasthan.gov.in or the nearest e-Mitra kiosk. Jan Aadhaar is Rajasthan's family identity and is required for most state schemes.

### Can I use Chiranjeevi in hospitals outside Rajasthan?
Currently, only empanelled hospitals within Rajasthan are covered. For treatment outside Rajasthan, check if the hospital has a special empanelment — some national hospital chains have tie-ups.

### Does Chiranjeevi cover maternity and newborn expenses?
Yes — maternity packages including normal delivery, C-section, and newborn complications are covered under the 1,800+ medical packages.

### I am a government employee. Am I eligible?
Government employees covered under RGHS (Rajasthan Government Health Scheme) are not eligible for Chiranjeevi. However, contract/daily wage employees of the government can register.

### What happens if the hospital charges me despite being under Chiranjeevi?
First, contact the hospital's Chiranjeevi desk. If they still charge, file a complaint at the Chiranjeevi helpline (18001806127) or at chiranjeevi.rajasthan.gov.in → "Grievance."
