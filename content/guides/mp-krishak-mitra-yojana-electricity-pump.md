## What is Krishak Mitra Yojana (MP)?

**Krishak Mitra Yojana** is a Madhya Pradesh government scheme that provides farmers with a **new agricultural pump electricity connection** — including transformer installation and extension of power line up to 200 metres — at **no cost** to the farmer. The entire infrastructure cost is borne by the MP government and electricity distribution companies.

The scheme is specifically for farmers who need a new pump connection for irrigation.

---

## Key Benefits

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Connection capacity | Up to 5 HP (3-phase) agricultural pump |
| Cost to farmer | Free — zero cost for connection and transformer |
| Line extension | Up to 200 metres from existing line |
| Transformer | Installed by the electricity company |
| Electricity tariff | Subsidised agriculture tariff (approximately ₹3–4/unit) |
| Target farmers | Small and marginal farmers without pump connection |

---

## Who is Eligible?

**You ARE eligible if:**
- You are a **farmer in Madhya Pradesh** with agricultural land that needs irrigation
- You do not currently have an **agriculture pump electricity connection** on the land
- The nearest electricity line is **within 200 metres** of your land (for free connection)
- Your pump capacity is **5 HP or below**

> If the line extension is **more than 200 metres**, you will need to contribute proportionally for the extra distance.

---

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar Card** — farmer's identity
2. **Land records** (khasra/patwari certificate) — proof of agricultural land ownership
3. **Bank passbook** — Aadhaar-linked
4. **Mobile number** registered with Aadhaar
5. **Photograph**
6. Application form (from MP electricity company or online)

## How to Apply — Step by Step

### Step 1 — Apply Online

Go to the **MP Energy Portal** or **MP Online**:
- [mpwz.co.in](https://mpwz.co.in) (for MPWZ area — West Discom)
- [mpmkvvcl.org](https://mpmkvvcl.org) (for Central Discom)
- [mpez.co.in](https://mpez.co.in) (for East Discom)

Register and apply for a new agricultural pump connection under **Krishak Mitra Yojana**.

### Step 2 — Offline Application (Alternative)

Visit your nearest **Sub-Divisional Officer (SDO) of MP Electricity** or Discom sub-division office. Collect and fill the Krishak Mitra Yojana application form.

### Step 3 — Site Survey

After application, an electricity lineman/engineer visits the field to:
- Verify land records
- Measure distance from existing line to the pump location
- Determine transformer requirement

### Step 4 — Approval and Infrastructure Work

After approval:
- Transformer is installed
- Line is extended up to 200 metres
- Meter is installed at the pump location

### Step 5 — Pump Connection Active

Once the connection is energized, your agriculture pump is ready to run on the subsidised agriculture tariff.

## Subsidised Agriculture Tariff in MP

Madhya pradesh farmers receive a heavily subsidised electricity tariff for agriculture:
- Flat rate or metered rate (varies by scheme)
- **Free electricity up to certain hours** for small farmers in some tariff categories
- Separate agriculture feeder timing (to ensure reliable power during farming hours)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. My pump is 7.5 HP. Is it covered under Krishak Mitra?**

No. The scheme covers pumps up to **5 HP**. For higher-capacity pumps, you need to apply for a regular (paid) agriculture pump connection.

**2. The nearest power line is 400 metres from my farm. What do I pay?**

The first 200 metres is free. For the additional 200 metres, you pay the standard infrastructure cost (approximately ₹70,000–₹1,00,000 per km — prorated). Confirm exact amount with the Discom SDO.

**3. How long does it take to get the connection after application?**

Typically 30–60 days from application to energization, subject to approval, material availability, and survey scheduling.

**4. I already have a pump connection. Can I upgrade under Krishak Mitra?**

No. Krishak Mitra Yojana is specifically for **new connections** on land where no agriculture pump connection exists. Existing connection upgrades are handled through standard Discom procedures.

**5. Can a tenant farmer apply for Krishak Mitra?**

The connection is typically in the name of the **land owner**. Tenant farmers can request the landowner to apply. Some cases allow tenant applications with landowner's NOC and lease agreement — check with the Discom SDO.
