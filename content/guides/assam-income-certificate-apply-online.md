# Assam Income Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **income certificate** (আয়ৰ প্ৰমাণপত্ৰ — Aayor Praman Patra) in Assam is issued by the Revenue Department (Circle Officer) and is required for NSP/state scholarships, OBC/SC/ST/ST(Plains) welfare scheme eligibility, EWS reservation, and government college fee waivers. Applications are processed through **Assam e-District** portal or **Seva Setu** camps.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at [edistrict.assam.gov.in](https://edistrict.assam.gov.in) or at **Assam Seva Setu camp / CSC**. Issued by Circle Officer within **15–30 days**.

---

## Why You Need Assam Income Certificate

- **Pre-matric and post-matric SC/ST scholarships** (Assam state + NSP) — income proof mandatory
- **OBC scholarships** — income-based eligibility
- **Tea garden worker community benefits** — income proof required for garden worker families
- **EWS reservation** — income < ₹8 lakh/year
- **SEBA / AHSEC scholarship** — income certificate for state board scholarship
- **JNVS / Navodaya vidyalaya** lateral entry — income proof
- **PMJAY / health scheme eligibility** — income verification

---

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory)
- **Ration card** (if available — shows family composition)
- Self-declaration of annual income (all sources)
- For salaried: **salary certificate** from employer or Form 16
- For farmers: **Land Records / Patta** (from Circle Office or Revenue Circle)
- For tea garden workers: **garden employer certificate** showing annual wages
- **Voter ID** (address proof)
- Passport-size photograph

> **Tea garden workers**: A certificate from the tea garden management showing annual wages is accepted as income proof. This is unique to Assam's large tea-growing population.

## How to Apply Online — Assam e-District Portal

1. Go to [edistrict.assam.gov.in](https://edistrict.assam.gov.in)
2. Register / log in with mobile number (OTP)
3. Click **"Revenue Department"** → **"Income Certificate"**
4. Fill form:
   - Applicant name, address (district, sub-division, circle, village)
   - Annual family income (all sources declared)
   - Purpose of certificate
5. Upload documents (Aadhaar, income proof, photograph)
6. Pay fee: **₹20–30** (online payment)
7. Submit → note **Application Reference Number**

---

## Apply via Seva Setu / CSC

Assam's **Seva Setu** (government at your doorstep) camps and CSCs:

1. Visit nearest **Seva Setu camp** (organized regularly at GP/ward level) or CSC
2. Carry Aadhaar, ration card, income proof documents
3. Government staff assists with form filling and submission
4. Service charge at CSC: approximately ₹30–50; Seva Setu camps are free
5. Collect acknowledgement receipt

---

## Apply at Revenue Circle Office

1. Visit your **Revenue Circle Office** (Circle Officer's office)
2. Obtain income certificate application form
3. Submit with supporting documents to Revenue Inspector
4. Revenue Inspector verifies income at village/gaon panchayat level
5. Circle Officer approves and issues certificate
6. Collect certificate from Circle Office after approval

---

## Status Check

1. Go to [edistrict.assam.gov.in](https://edistrict.assam.gov.in)
2. Click **"Track Application Status"** → enter Application Reference Number
3. Or log in → **"My Applications"** → view status

---

## Download Assam Income Certificate

Once approved:
1. Log in to Assam e-District → **"My Applications"**
2. Click approved income certificate
3. Download digitally signed PDF
4. Valid for Assam state schemes, NSP, and central government applications

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Income limit for Assam post-matric scholarship?

For **Assam state SC/ST post-matric scholarship**: family income below **₹2 lakh/year**. For **NSP ST scholarship (central)**: income < ₹2.5 lakh/year. For **OBC post-matric (NSP)**: income < ₹1.5 lakh/year (check latest NSP guidelines).

### Tea garden worker — how to prove income?

The **tea garden management salary certificate** or monthly wage slip for the last 12 months is the accepted income proof. If the worker earns daily wages, the Circle Officer may accept a declaration countersigned by the garden's HR/welfare officer.
