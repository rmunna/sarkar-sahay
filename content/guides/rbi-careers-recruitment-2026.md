## RBI Grade B Officer — The Most Sought-After Post

### Eligibility

| Criterion | Requirement |
|-----------|------------|
| **Education** | Bachelor's degree with minimum 60% marks (50% for SC/ST/PwBD) |
| **Age** | 21–30 years (relaxation: OBC +3 yrs, SC/ST +5 yrs, PwBD +10 yrs) |
| **Nationality** | Indian citizen |

### Salary & Perks

| Component | Amount |
|-----------|--------|
| **Basic Pay** | ₹35,150 per month |
| **DA + HRA + Other Allowances** | Additional ~₹25,000–₹35,000 |
| **Gross Salary (approx.)** | ₹1.2 lakh – ₹1.5 lakh per month |
| **Annual CTC** | ~₹14–17 lakhs |

**Additional perks:** Housing accommodation or HRA, subsidised loans, medical benefits for self and family, pension, children's education allowance, LFC.

### Exam Pattern

**Phase 1 (Prelims) — Online**
| Section | Marks | Duration |
|---------|-------|----------|
| General Awareness | 80 | 25 min |
| English Language | 30 | 25 min |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 30 | 25 min |
| Reasoning | 60 | 45 min |
| **Total** | **200** | **120 min** |

**Phase 2 (Mains) — Online**
| Paper | Marks | Duration |
|-------|-------|----------|
| Paper 1: English Writing | 100 | 90 min |
| Paper 2: Economic & Social Issues | 100 | 90 min |
| Paper 3: Finance & Management | 100 | 90 min |

**Phase 3: Interview** — 50 marks

## RBI Grade B DEPR & DSIM (Specialist Posts)

RBI also recruits specialists in two departments:

- **DEPR** (Department of Economic and Policy Research) — Economists
  - Eligibility: Master's degree in Economics/Econometrics with 55%+ marks
- **DSIM** (Department of Statistics and Information Management) — Statisticians
  - Eligibility: Master's in Statistics/Mathematical Statistics with 55%+ marks

Exam pattern: Objective + Descriptive papers relevant to specialisation + Interview.

## RBI Assistant

## RBI Office Attendant

The most accessible RBI post — suitable for candidates with limited educational qualifications.

### Eligibility

| Criterion | Requirement |
|-----------|------------|
| **Education** | 10th pass (Matriculation) from a recognised board |
| **Age** | 18–25 years (relaxation applicable) |
| **Domicile** | Must be from the state/UT of the notified vacancy |

### Salary

- **Basic Pay:** ₹10,940 per month
- **With Allowances:** ₹25,000 – ₹30,000 per month

### Exam Pattern

- **Online Test:** Reasoning, Numerical Ability, English/Hindi, General Awareness — 120 marks, 2 hours
- **Language Proficiency Test:** Must pass

## How to Apply for Any RBI Post

1. Go to **[opportunities.rbi.org.in](https://opportunities.rbi.org.in)**
2. Click on the active recruitment notification
3. Click **"Apply Online"**
4. Register with your email ID and mobile number
5. Fill in personal details, educational qualifications, work experience
6. Upload photo (20–50 KB), signature (10–20 KB) as per specifications
7. Pay the application fee:
   - General/OBC/EWS: ₹850 (Grade B), ₹450 (Assistant), ₹100 (Office Attendant)
   - SC/ST/PwBD: Fees waived for most posts
8. Submit and save the application form PDF

## Tips to Crack RBI Grade B

1. **Start with NCERT Economics** (Class 11–12) for ESI paper foundation
2. **RBI Annual Report** is must-read — many direct questions come from it
3. **The Hindu Business Line / Mint** for current economic affairs daily
4. **Previous year papers** — pattern is consistent, practice is essential
5. **Phase 2 writing skills** — practice 250-word answers in 15 minutes for ESI & FM papers
6. **Interview prep** — know your state's economy, RBI's recent policy decisions, Monetary Policy Reports

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How often does RBI conduct Grade B recruitment?**
Typically once a year, usually between February–June. The notification comes on the RBI website and IBPS website.

**Is RBI Grade B tougher than UPSC?**
Different in nature. RBI Grade B requires deep knowledge of economics, finance and banking. Considered one of the toughest banking exams due to its analytical and descriptive component.

**Can I apply for RBI jobs if I'm from a non-economics background?**
Yes — for Grade B General post, any graduate with 60% marks can apply. You don't need an economics degree. The DEPR/DSIM posts are specialist roles requiring economics/statistics degrees.

**What is the bond period for RBI employees?**
There is no bond/service agreement. You can resign at any time with proper notice.

**Does RBI recruit through IBPS?**
No. All RBI recruitment is done directly by RBI at [opportunities.rbi.org.in](https://opportunities.rbi.org.in). IBPS conducts recruitment for public sector banks, not RBI.
