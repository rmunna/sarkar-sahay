# Yuva Sathi Yojana 2026 — Check Status by Mobile Number & Apply Online

Yuva Sathi is an Odisha government scheme that provides a monthly stipend to educated unemployed youth while they receive skill training or search for employment. The scheme aims to provide financial support to job-seeking youth during their transition into the workforce.

This guide covers how to check your Yuva Sathi application status by mobile number or Aadhaar, eligibility criteria, stipend amount, and how to apply.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is independent and not affiliated with the Odisha government. Verify details at the official portal before applying.

## Key Details at a Glance

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Scheme Name** | Yuva Sathi Yojana |
| **State** | Odisha |
| **Nodal Department** | Skill Development and Technical Education Department |
| **Stipend Amount** | ₹1,500 per month |
| **Duration** | Up to 2 years |
| **Target Group** | Educated unemployed youth aged 18–35 |

---

## How to Check Yuva Sathi Status by Mobile Number

1. Go to **[yuvashakthi.odisha.gov.in](https://yuvashakthi.odisha.gov.in)** or the Skill Odisha portal
2. Click on **"Track Application"** or **"Check Status"**
3. Enter your **registered mobile number** or **application reference number**
4. Enter the OTP sent to your mobile
5. Your application status (Pending / Under Review / Approved / Rejected) will be displayed
6. If approved, the stipend payment schedule and bank account details will also be shown

### Status Meanings

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Submitted** | Application received, under initial review |
| **Under Review** | Verification of documents in progress |
| **Approved** | Application accepted, stipend will start |
| **Disbursed** | Payment sent to your bank account |
| **Rejected** | Application not accepted (reason shown) |

## Eligibility Criteria

| Criterion | Requirement |
|-----------|------------|
| **State** | Must be a permanent resident of Odisha |
| **Age** | 18–35 years |
| **Education** | Minimum 10th pass; higher the education, higher priority |
| **Employment Status** | Unemployed at the time of application |
| **Income** | Household income below the state threshold |
| **Bank Account** | Must have an active bank account (preferably Aadhaar-linked) |

---

## How to Apply for Yuva Sathi Online

1. Go to **[yuvashakthi.odisha.gov.in](https://yuvashakthi.odisha.gov.in)**
2. Click **"New Registration"** or **"Apply Now"**
3. Enter your **Aadhaar number** and mobile number for OTP verification
4. Fill in your personal details, address, and educational qualifications
5. Upload required documents:
   - Aadhaar card
   - Residence/domicile certificate (Odisha)
   - Educational mark sheets / certificates
   - Bank passbook (first page)
   - Passport-size photo
6. Submit the application and save the **Application Reference Number**

---

## Stipend Payment Details

- Stipend: **₹1,500 per month** credited directly to the beneficiary's bank account
- Payment mode: Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) — Aadhaar-linked bank account
- Frequency: Monthly, typically transferred on a fixed date each month

If payment is not received:
1. Check application status on the portal
2. Verify your bank account is Aadhaar-linked
3. Contact the nearest **Employment Exchange Office** in Odisha
4. Call the Skill Odisha helpline: **1800-345-6770** (toll-free)

## Common Issues

| Problem | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| **Status stuck on "Submitted" for weeks** | Contact district employment exchange; verification may be pending |
| **Status shows "Rejected"** | Login to portal and check rejection reason; common reasons: domicile proof missing, income above threshold |
| **Payment not received after "Approved"** | Ensure Aadhaar is linked to bank account; update bank details on the portal if account changed |
| **Can't check status — mobile number not registered** | Try with Aadhaar number or application number instead |
| **OTP not received** | Wait 5 minutes and retry; check if mobile number is active |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Yuva Sathi the same as Mamata Scheme?**
No. Mamata Scheme is for pregnant and lactating mothers in Odisha. Yuva Sathi is for unemployed youth seeking skill development or employment.

**Can I apply if I'm studying?**
No — Yuva Sathi is for unemployed youth who have completed their education and are not currently enrolled in any regular course.

**How long does it take to get approved?**
Typically 4–8 weeks after submission, depending on verification at the district level.

**Can I reapply if rejected?**
Yes, after rectifying the reason for rejection. Upload the required missing documents and reapply.

**Is the stipend taxable?**
No — government welfare stipends under schemes like Yuva Sathi are generally not taxable.
