# Annapurna Bhandar Scheme — West Bengal BJP Government ₹3,000/Month Women's Allowance

The **Annapurna Bhandar Scheme** is the flagship women's welfare programme of the West Bengal BJP government formed in May 2026. The scheme provides **₹3,000 per month** directly into eligible women's bank accounts via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

The scheme was announced as the BJP came to power on **May 9, 2026**, ending the Trinamool Congress's 15-year rule in West Bengal. It effectively expands and rebrands the earlier Lakshmir Bhandar scheme with a higher benefit amount.

---

## Key Benefits

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| **Monthly amount** | ₹3,000 per month |
| **Payment method** | DBT — direct to beneficiary's bank account |
| **Target** | Adult women of West Bengal |
| **Category** | Women's welfare / financial inclusion |

---

## Eligibility Criteria

- **Resident** of West Bengal (with valid domicile)
- **Female** aged 18 years and above
- Should be from a family below or at a specified income threshold
- Must have an **Aadhaar-linked bank account** for DBT
- SC/ST women may receive priority or enhanced benefit as notified

> **Note:** Exact income eligibility criteria and SC/ST enhanced benefit slab will be published in the official government gazette. Check wb.gov.in for current notification.

---

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar card
- West Bengal voter ID or domicile certificate
- Bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked account)
- Ration card or family income certificate
- Passport-size photograph

---

## How to Apply

The application process is being rolled out through two channels:

### 1. Duare Sarkar Camps
West Bengal's doorstep service programme (Duare Sarkar) is the primary channel for Annapurna Bhandar enrolment. Government officials set up camps in each ward/village to register eligible women directly.

- Watch for Duare Sarkar camp dates in your area (announced via local panchayat/municipality)
- Bring original documents and photocopies
- Registration is done at the camp itself — no need to visit a separate office

### 2. Block Development Office / Municipal Office
- Visit your nearest BDO office (rural) or Municipality (urban)
- Collect and fill the Annapurna Bhandar application form
- Submit with self-attested copies of all documents
- Note your acknowledgement number for follow-up

---

## Relationship to Lakshmir Bhandar

The previous TMC government's **Lakshmir Bhandar** scheme provided ₹500–₹1,000/month to women. The BJP government has launched **Annapurna Bhandar** with a significantly higher benefit of **₹3,000/month**, effectively replacing and upgrading the earlier programme.

| Scheme | Government | Amount | Coverage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lakshmir Bhandar | TMC (Mamata Banerjee) | ₹500–₹1,000/month | General / SC/ST women |
| **Annapurna Bhandar** | **BJP (Suvendu Adhikari)** | **₹3,000/month** | Women 18+ |

Women already enrolled in Lakshmir Bhandar are expected to be transitioned to Annapurna Bhandar automatically; however, **fresh enrolment is required** if you were not previously enrolled.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: I was receiving Lakshmir Bhandar — will I automatically get Annapurna Bhandar?**
A: Existing Lakshmir Bhandar beneficiaries are expected to be migrated. However, re-verification may be required. Visit your BDO or Duare Sarkar camp to confirm your enrolment status.

**Q: Can working women apply?**
A: Yes, unless the government specifies employment-based exclusions in its official notification. Check the latest wb.gov.in gazette notification for current rules.

**Q: Is there a separate card or physical document issued?**
A: The scheme is expected to issue a **Matrishakti Bharosa Card** as a beneficiary ID. Carry this at Duare Sarkar camps to check your status.

**Q: When will the first payment come?**
A: The scheme was launched in June 2026. First payments are expected within 1–2 months of enrolment verification. Check wb.gov.in for the official disbursement timeline.

**Q: What if my bank account is not Aadhaar-linked?**
A: Link your Aadhaar to your bank account before applying. Visit your bank branch with your Aadhaar and passbook. DBT cannot be processed to an account that is not Aadhaar-seeded.
