## What is Income Certificate Status Check?

An income certificate is an official document issued by the state government that certifies your family's annual income. After applying through your state's e-District or service portal, you can check the application status online using your application number.

Income certificates are required for scholarships, fee concessions, government scheme benefits, subsidized housing, and reservation under EWS (Economically Weaker Section) category.

> **Karnataka residents:** See our detailed guide on [how to apply for an income certificate in Karnataka](/guide/karnataka-income-certificate-online-apply).

## State-Wise Income Certificate Status Check

### 1. Karnataka — Nadakacheri Portal

**Portal:** [nadakacheri.karnataka.gov.in](https://nadakacheri.karnataka.gov.in)

#### Step-by-Step Process

1. Visit [https://nadakacheri.karnataka.gov.in](https://nadakacheri.karnataka.gov.in)
2. Click on **"Check Application Status"**
3. Enter your **Application Number**
4. Click **"Get Status"**
5. View the current status — Applied, Under Verification, Approved, or Rejected

**Helpline:** 080-22230282

---

### 2. Uttar Pradesh — e-District UP

**Portal:** [edistrict.up.gov.in](https://edistrict.up.gov.in)

#### Step-by-Step Process

1. Visit [https://edistrict.up.gov.in](https://edistrict.up.gov.in)
2. Click on **"आवेदन की स्थिति"** (Application Status)
3. Enter your **Application Number**
4. Enter the **captcha** and click **"Search"**
5. Your income certificate status will be displayed

**Helpline:** 0522-2304706 / 1800-1800-150

---

### 3. Maharashtra — Aaple Sarkar Portal

**Portal:** [aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in](https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in)

#### Step-by-Step Process

1. Visit [https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in](https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in)
2. Log in with your credentials
3. Go to **"Track Your Application"** or **"My Applications"**
4. Locate your income certificate application
5. Click to view the current status

**Helpline:** 1800-120-8040 (Toll-Free)

## Check Income Certificate Status by Mobile Number

Most state portals send automatic SMS updates:

- **Uttar Pradesh:** SMS at each processing stage
- **Bihar (RTPS):** SMS on approval/rejection
- **Maharashtra:** SMS alerts via Aaple Sarkar
- **Karnataka:** SMS to registered mobile on status change

## Check Income Certificate Status by Aadhaar

Some states support Aadhaar-based tracking:

1. Visit your state's e-District portal
2. Select **"Search by Aadhaar"** (if available)
3. Enter your Aadhaar number and verify via OTP
4. View all linked applications

## Check by SMS

There is **no universal SMS code** for income certificate status. Updates are sent automatically if your mobile number was registered. For manual checks, use the online portal or call the helpline.

## Common Issues and Fixes

### 1. Status Shows "Under Verification" for Too Long
- **Reason:** The Tehsildar or revenue officer may need field verification or has a backlog
- **Fix:** Visit the Tehsil office in person with your acknowledgement slip. Ask about the specific reason for delay

### 2. Application Rejected
- **Reason:** Common reasons — income declared doesn't match records, missing documents, or incorrect details
- **Fix:** Check the rejection reason on the portal. Correct the issues and re-apply with proper documentation

### 3. Income Amount on Certificate is Wrong
- **Reason:** Data entry error or incorrect income assessment by the verifying officer
- **Fix:** Apply for correction at the Tehsil office. Submit salary slips, ITR, or other income proof

### 4. Certificate Expired — Need Renewal
- **Reason:** Income certificates are typically valid for 1 year (some states: 6 months or 3 years)
- **Fix:** Apply for a fresh income certificate. The process is the same as a new application

### 5. Portal Not Loading
- **Reason:** Server maintenance or heavy traffic
- **Fix:** Try during off-peak hours (early morning). Clear browser cache or try a different browser

## Important Tips

1. **Apply through the official e-District portal** for easy tracking and faster processing
2. **Keep your application number safe** — essential for status checking
3. **Declare accurate income** — misrepresentation can lead to rejection or legal issues
4. **Renew before expiry** — most income certificates are valid for only 1 year
5. **Keep supporting documents ready** — salary slips, [ITR](/guide/itr-filing-online-guide), bank statements may be needed for verification

## FAQs

### How long does it take to get an income certificate?
Typically 7–30 working days depending on the state. Bihar and Karnataka process faster (7–15 days).

### Can I check income certificate status without an application number?
Some portals allow searching by name or Aadhaar, but the application number gives the most reliable result.

### Is income certificate status check free?
Yes, checking status online is completely free on all state portals.

### How long is an income certificate valid?
Validity varies by state — typically 1 year. Some states issue certificates valid for 6 months or 3 years. Check with your state's revenue department.

### What is the difference between income certificate and EWS certificate?
An income certificate states your family's annual income. An EWS (Economically Weaker Section) certificate specifically certifies that your family income is below ₹8 lakh per annum and you don't belong to SC/ST/OBC categories. EWS is used for the 10% reservation.

### What documents are needed for an income certificate?
Typically: [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online), ration card, salary slips or self-employment proof, ITR (if filed), self-declaration affidavit, and passport-size photos.

### Can I use an income certificate from one state in another?
Income certificates are generally accepted across India for central government schemes. However, for state-specific benefits, the certificate must usually be from that state.
