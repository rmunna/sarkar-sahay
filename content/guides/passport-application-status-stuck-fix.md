# Passport Application Status Stuck — How to Fix

Applied for a passport and the status hasn't moved in weeks? You're not alone. Passport applications can get stuck at multiple stages — from processing at the Passport Office to police verification, printing, and dispatch.

This guide covers **every stuck stage**, how to check your real-time status, what each status message means, and exactly how to escalate when things are delayed.

---

## How to Check Your Passport Application Status

Before troubleshooting, confirm your current status using one of these methods:

### Method 1: Passport India Website

1. Go to [passportindia.gov.in](https://www.passportindia.gov.in/AppOnlineProject/statusTracker/trackStatusIn498)
2. Select your **Application Type** (Normal or Tatkal)
3. Enter your **File Number** (format: `AA-1234567890123`) and **Date of Birth**
4. Click **Track Status**

### Method 2: mPassport Seva App

1. Download **mPassport Seva** from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ceaborit.mpassport) or Apple App Store
2. Open the app → **Track Application Status**
3. Enter your file number and date of birth
4. The app sends push notifications on status changes — enable them

### Method 3: SMS

Send an SMS in this format to **9704100100**:

```
STATUS <File Number>
```

Example: `STATUS AA-1234567890123`

## Understanding Passport Status Messages

Here's what each common status means and whether you need to worry:

| Status Message | What It Means | Normal Wait Time |
|---|---|---|
| **Application Received at Passport Office** | Your documents reached the RPO after PSK visit | 1–3 days |
| **Under Process at Passport Office** | Officer is reviewing your application | 3–7 days |
| **Police Verification Initiated** | Request sent to local police station | 1–3 days to initiate |
| **SP Report Awaited / Police Verification Pending** | Waiting for police to submit their report | 7–21 days |
| **Police Report Received — Clear** | Police verification completed successfully | — |
| **Police Report Received — Adverse** | Police found discrepancies | Varies (may need re-verification) |
| **Passport Under Print** | Approved and sent to printing queue | 2–5 days |
| **Passport Printed** | Physical passport printed | 1–2 days |
| **Passport Dispatched** | Handed over to Speed Post/courier | 3–7 days for delivery |
| **Passport Delivered** | Delivered to your address | — |

> **Key insight:** If your status hasn't changed for longer than the "Normal Wait Time" listed above, it's likely stuck and you should take action.

## Stage 1: Stuck at "Processing at Passport Office"

### Why It Happens
- High volume of applications at the RPO
- Incomplete or unclear documents flagged by the officer
- Application queued behind Tatkal and reissue cases

### What to Do

**Wait at least 7 working days** after your PSK appointment. If the status still shows "Under Process":

1. **Call the RPO directly** — Find your RPO's contact number on the [RPO Contact Directory](https://www.passportindia.gov.in/AppOnlineProject/online/rpoContactUsAction)
2. **Call the Passport Seva helpline**: **1800-258-1800** (toll-free) — available Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 10 PM
3. **Visit your PSK/RPO** in person with your acknowledgement receipt if phone calls don't help

---

## Stage 2: Stuck at "Police Verification Pending"

This is the **most common** stage where passport applications get delayed.

### Why It Happens
- Police station hasn't received the request yet
- Assigned officer is unavailable or overburdened
- Address mismatch between your application and current proof
- You weren't home during the police visit
- Landlord/neighbour didn't cooperate with verification

### What to Do

**Wait 21 days** from the date police verification was initiated. Then:

1. **Visit your local police station** — Ask for the passport verification desk. Carry:
   - Passport application acknowledgement
   - Original address proof
   - Photo ID
2. **Contact the SP/DSP office** — If the local station is unresponsive, escalate to the Superintendent of Police office in your district
3. **Call RPO** and inform them that police verification is delayed — they can send a reminder to the police station
4. **File a grievance** on the [CPV Division portal](https://cpgrams.gov.in/) (see escalation section below)

> **Pro tip:** Police verification for the **same address as Aadhaar** is often faster. If your address documents match across Aadhaar, Voter ID, and passport application, verification is smoother.

For a detailed walkthrough, see our guide on **[Police Verification Stuck — How to Fix](/guide/passport-police-verification-stuck-fix)**.

## Stage 3: Stuck at "Passport Under Print"

### Why It Happens
- Printing facility has a backlog
- Technical issue with the print queue
- Data correction pending before printing

### What to Do

**Wait 5 working days** from when the status changed to "Under Print." Then:

1. **Call Passport Seva helpline**: 1800-258-1800
2. **Email your RPO** — Include your file number, name, and date of birth in the subject line
3. If it stays stuck beyond **10 working days**, file a grievance (see below)

---

## Stage 4: Stuck at "Dispatched" but Not Delivered

### Why It Happens
- Incorrect or incomplete address
- You weren't available for delivery and the courier returned
- Speed Post / India Post delays
- Passport returned to RPO after failed delivery attempts

### What to Do

1. **Track the Speed Post consignment** — Your dispatch SMS/email includes a tracking number. Track it at [indiapost.gov.in](https://www.indiapost.gov.in/_layouts/15/DOP.Portal.Tracking/TrackConsignment.aspx)
2. **Contact your local post office** — If the tracking shows "Out for Delivery" or "Item returned," visit your nearest post office with your ID
3. **Call Passport Seva helpline** if the passport was returned to RPO — they'll re-dispatch it
4. **Update your address** if incorrect — You may need to request re-dispatch to the correct address through the RPO

## How to Escalate: Grievance Portals

If standard channels fail, use these escalation methods in order:

### Level 1: Passport Seva Grievance

1. Go to [passportindia.gov.in](https://www.passportindia.gov.in)
2. Log in → **Grievance / Feedback** section
3. File a detailed complaint with your file number
4. You'll receive a grievance tracking number — save it

### Level 2: CPV Division (Consular, Passport & Visa)

1. Visit [cpgrams.gov.in](https://cpgrams.gov.in/)
2. Register/login → Select **Ministry of External Affairs**
3. Category: **Passport Related**
4. Write a clear description including:
   - File number
   - Application date
   - PSK visited
   - Current stuck status
   - Days elapsed
5. Upload supporting documents if any

**Expected response:** 7–15 working days

### Level 3: MEA Grievance Portal

1. Visit the [MEA Grievance page](https://www.mea.gov.in/grievance.htm)
2. Submit a formal grievance addressed to the Ministry of External Affairs
3. This is the highest level of escalation within the passport system

### Level 4: Write to the Regional Passport Officer

Send a formal letter or email to your RPO. Include:
- Subject: "Delay in Passport Processing — File No. [Your File Number]"
- Your complete details: name, DOB, file number, application date
- Current status and how long it's been stuck
- Request for expedited processing

## Timeline Expectations: How Long Should Each Stage Take?

| Application Type | Police Verification | Total Processing | Dispatch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Normal (Fresh)** | 2–4 weeks | 30–45 days | 3–7 days after printing |
| **Normal (Reissue)** | 1–3 weeks | 15–30 days | 3–7 days after printing |
| **Tatkal (Fresh)** | 1–7 days | 7–14 days | 1–3 days after printing |
| **Tatkal (Reissue)** | May be skipped | 3–7 days | 1–3 days after printing |

> **Note:** These are typical timelines. Actual processing times vary by RPO workload and your state's police verification speed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My passport has been "Under Process" for over a month. Is this normal?
No. For normal applications, processing at the passport office should take 3–7 working days. If it's been over 2 weeks, call the helpline or visit the RPO.

### Can I speed up police verification?
Yes — visiting your local police station in person with all original documents often accelerates the process. Be polite and cooperative.

### What if my passport is dispatched to the wrong address?
Contact the Passport Seva helpline immediately at 1800-258-1800. They can coordinate with India Post to redirect or re-dispatch.

### Is there a way to check police verification status separately?
Some state police portals show passport verification status. Check your state police website. Otherwise, the Passport India status tracker is your best option.

### I missed the police officer's visit. What now?
Contact your local police station and request a re-visit. Provide your phone number and a convenient time when you'll be available.
