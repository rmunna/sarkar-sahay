# MP Income Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **income certificate** (आय प्रमाण पत्र) in Madhya Pradesh is issued by the Revenue Department and is required for scholarships, OBC/SC/ST reservations, BPL ration card eligibility, and various government scheme benefits. In MP, the income certificate process is linked to **Samagra ID** — the state's family identity system. Applications go through **Lok Seva Kendra (LSK)** or the **mpedistrict.gov.in** portal.

> **Quick summary:** Get Samagra ID first at [samagra.gov.in](https://samagra.gov.in), then apply for income certificate at Lok Seva Kendra or mpedistrict.gov.in. Issued by Tehsildar within **30 days**.

---

## Samagra ID — Mandatory First Step in MP

Like the ration card, MP's income certificate is linked to the **Samagra** family registry:

**If you don't have Samagra ID:**
1. Visit [samagra.gov.in](https://samagra.gov.in)
2. Register your family with Aadhaar, name, DOB, and address
3. Get your **9-digit Samagra Family ID** and **8-digit individual member ID**
4. Then proceed with income certificate application

---

## Why You Need MP Income Certificate

- **State scholarship portal** — for OBC, EWS, SC, ST categories
- **National Scholarship Portal (NSP)** — mandatory for all scholarship categories
- **CM Scholarship and Medhavi Scholarship** — for meritorious students
- **BPL ration card** eligibility in MP
- **OBC Non-Creamy Layer** certificate (income limit < ₹8 lakh/year)
- **EWS (Economically Weaker Section)** reservation (income < ₹8 lakh/year)

---

## Documents Required

- **Samagra Family ID** (mandatory in MP)
- **Aadhaar card** of applicant and family members
- Self-declaration of income (affidavit on stamp paper or online declaration)
- For salaried: **salary slip** or employer certificate
- For farmers: **Khasra/Khatauni** (from MP Bhulekh portal)
- Ration card (for BPL claim)
- Passport-size photograph

---

## How to Apply via Lok Seva Kendra (Recommended)

1. Visit your nearest **Lok Seva Kendra (LSK)** in your tehsil / block
2. Tell the operator you need **"Aay Praman Patra"** (income certificate)
3. Provide your **Samagra Family ID** — family details auto-populate
4. Declare income and provide supporting documents
5. Operator submits application online
6. Service charge: approximately ₹30–50
7. Collect acknowledgement slip with reference number

---

## How to Apply Online — mpedistrict.gov.in

1. Go to [mpedistrict.gov.in](https://mpedistrict.gov.in)
2. Log in / register with Samagra ID and mobile number
3. Go to **"Revenue Department"** → **"Income Certificate"**
4. Family member details auto-fill from Samagra database
5. Select member, declare income, upload supporting documents
6. Pay fee (₹20–40) online
7. Submit — note reference number

---

## Status Check

1. Go to [mpedistrict.gov.in](https://mpedistrict.gov.in)
2. Click **"Track Application"** → enter reference number or Samagra ID
3. Or check at Lok Seva Kendra with your acknowledgement slip

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I get MP income certificate without Samagra ID?

Samagra ID is mandatory for most MP government services including income certificates. Visit [samagra.gov.in](https://samagra.gov.in) or your Gram Panchayat/Ward Office to get registered first.

### What income limit for MP scholarship?

For **Mukhyamantri Medhavi Vidyarthi Yojana**: no income limit. For **OBC/SC/ST**: income below ₹8 lakh/year (central NFSA). For state-specific scholarships, check [scholarshipportal.mp.nic.in](https://scholarshipportal.mp.nic.in).
