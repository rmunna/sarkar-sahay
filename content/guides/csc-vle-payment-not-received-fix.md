# CSC VLE Payment Not Received — Fix Commission Issues

As a CSC Village Level Entrepreneur (VLE), your commissions and service charges are your livelihood. If your CSC payment is not received, wallet balance is incorrect, or commissions are pending for weeks, this guide will help you diagnose and fix the issue.

## How CSC VLE Payments Work

Before troubleshooting, understand the CSC payment system:

### Payment Flow:

1. You deliver a government service (e.g., Aadhaar enrollment, PAN card, banking) through the Digital Seva portal
2. The service fee/commission is credited to your **CSC Digital Seva Wallet**
3. From the wallet, you can **transfer funds to your bank account**

### Key Points:

| Aspect | Details |
|---|---|
| Commission Credit | Varies by service — typically 1-7 working days after transaction |
| Wallet Type | Digital Seva e-Wallet (prepaid balance system) |
| Bank Transfer | Manual withdrawal from wallet to linked bank account |
| Payment Cycle | No fixed cycle — commissions credited per transaction |
| Minimum Withdrawal | Varies; typically ₹100 or more |

---

## Step 1: Check Your Digital Seva Wallet Balance

First, verify whether the payment was actually credited to your wallet.

1. Log in to **Digital Seva Portal**: [https://digitalseva.csc.gov.in/](https://digitalseva.csc.gov.in/)
2. Go to **"My Account"** or **"Wallet"** section
3. Check your **current wallet balance**
4. Click on **"Transaction History"** or **"Statement"**
5. Look for the specific transaction — check if commission is showing as:
   - **Credited** — money is in wallet, ready to withdraw
   - **Pending** — still being processed
   - **Failed** — transaction did not complete

### If Commission Shows as Credited but Balance Seems Low:

- Check if there are any **deductions** (TDS, service charges)
- Verify the **commission rate** for the specific service — rates vary
- Check if previous withdrawals reduced your balance

---

## Step 2: Verify Transaction Was Successfully Completed

Sometimes payments fail because the original service transaction was incomplete.

1. Go to **"My Transactions"** on the Digital Seva portal
2. Find the specific transaction
3. Check the **status**: Completed, Pending, or Failed
4. If **Pending or Failed** — the commission won't be credited until the transaction is resolved
5. For failed transactions, you may need to **re-initiate** the service

---

## Step 3: Check Bank Account Transfer Status

If the wallet shows correct balance but money hasn't reached your bank:

1. Go to **Wallet** → **"Withdraw to Bank"** or **"Fund Transfer"**
2. Check **withdrawal history** — look for the transfer status
3. Common statuses:
   - **Processed** — money sent to bank (allow 1-3 working days)
   - **Pending** — still being processed
   - **Failed** — transfer failed (check bank details)

### If Bank Transfer Failed:

- Verify your **bank account number** and **IFSC code** in the portal
- Ensure the bank account is **active** and not frozen
- Check if the **account name matches** your CSC registration name
- Update bank details if incorrect: go to **Profile** → **Bank Details**

---

## Step 4: Common Reasons for Payment Delays

| Reason | Solution |
|---|---|
| Service commission not yet processed | Wait 3-7 working days after transaction |
| Transaction marked as "Pending" | Check with the service provider (e.g., UIDAI for Aadhaar) |
| Incorrect bank details in profile | Update bank account and IFSC in Digital Seva profile |
| TDS deducted on commissions | Check TDS section — this is normal for high earnings |
| Wallet withdrawal failed | Re-initiate bank transfer with correct details |
| Portal technical glitch | Try again after 24 hours; clear cache and retry |
| CSC account under review/suspended | Contact District Manager immediately |
| Duplicate transaction reversal | Check if refund was processed for a cancelled service |

## Step 5: Raise a Grievance on Digital Seva Portal

If your payment is genuinely stuck, raise a formal complaint.

### Steps:

1. Log in to **Digital Seva Portal**: [https://digitalseva.csc.gov.in/](https://digitalseva.csc.gov.in/)
2. Go to **"Support"** or **"Grievance"** section
3. Click **"Raise New Ticket"**
4. Select category: **"Payment / Commission Issue"**
5. Enter details:
   - Transaction ID / Reference Number
   - Date of transaction
   - Service name
   - Expected commission amount
   - Brief description of the issue
6. Attach **screenshots** of transaction history if possible
7. Submit the ticket and **note the ticket number**
8. Expected resolution: **7-15 working days**

---

## Step 6: Contact CSC Helpline

If the grievance portal doesn't resolve your issue:

| Contact Method | Details |
|---|---|
| CSC Helpline | **1800-3000-3468** (toll-free) |
| Email | helpdesk@csc.gov.in |
| Working Hours | Monday to Saturday, 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM |

When calling, keep ready:
- Your **VLE ID**
- **Transaction ID** of the pending payment
- **Grievance ticket number** (if already raised)
- Bank account details for verification

---

## Step 7: Escalate to District Manager

If helpline support is slow, escalate to your CSC District Manager.

1. Find your DM's contact via **CSC Locator**: [https://register.csc.gov.in/](https://register.csc.gov.in/)
2. Email or call with:
   - VLE ID and transaction details
   - Grievance ticket number
   - Duration of pending payment
3. DMs have backend access and can expedite payment processing

See our [CSC VLE ID recovery guide](/guide/csc-vle-id-forgot-recover-fix) if you need help accessing your account.

## Important Tips

1. **Check wallet daily** — don't wait weeks to notice missing payments
2. **Keep transaction receipts** — screenshot every transaction for your records
3. **Ensure bank details are correct** — wrong IFSC or account number causes failed transfers
4. **File grievance early** — don't delay if payment is pending beyond 7 days
5. **Track TDS deductions** — CSC deducts TDS on commissions; file ITR to claim refund if applicable

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: How long does CSC commission take to credit?
Most commissions are credited to your Digital Seva wallet within 3-7 working days after the transaction is marked complete. Some services may take longer.

### Q2: Why is my CSC wallet balance showing zero?
Check your transaction history. If commissions were credited but balance is zero, check for recent withdrawals or deductions. If no transactions show, the commission may not have been processed yet.

### Q3: Can I withdraw CSC wallet balance to any bank account?
You can only withdraw to the bank account linked in your CSC VLE profile. To change the bank account, update your details in the Digital Seva portal under Profile settings.

### Q4: Is TDS deducted on CSC commissions?
Yes. CSC deducts TDS (Tax Deducted at Source) on commissions as per Income Tax rules. You can see TDS details in your wallet statement and claim credit while filing your ITR.

### Q5: What if my CSC payment grievance is not resolved?
If your grievance isn't resolved within 15 working days, escalate to your District Manager and also email the CSC SPV head office. Keep all ticket numbers and correspondence as evidence.

### Q6: Do CSC VLEs get paid monthly?
There is no fixed monthly salary. VLEs earn commissions per transaction. The more services you deliver, the more you earn. Payments are per-transaction, not monthly.

### Q7: My CSC account is suspended. Will I still get pending payments?
Pending commissions should still be processed even if your account is suspended. Contact your District Manager to resolve the suspension and release any held payments.
