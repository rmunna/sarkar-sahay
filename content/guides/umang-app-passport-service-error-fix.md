# UMANG Passport Service Error? Fix Application Tracking Issues

The UMANG app integrates with the **Passport Seva** portal to let you track passport applications, check appointment status, and access passport-related services. If you're facing errors, this guide covers all common fixes.

## Common UMANG Passport Service Errors

1. **Appointment not showing** or booking fails
2. **Application tracking failed** or "No records found"
3. **Fee payment error** during passport application
4. **Service unavailable** or timeout errors
5. **Wrong application status** displayed

---

## Fix 1: Passport Appointment Not Showing on UMANG

If your booked appointment is not visible on UMANG:

1. **Check on the official portal** — Log in to [passportindia.gov.in](https://www.passportindia.gov.in/) to verify your appointment status
2. **Verify your application number** — Ensure you're entering the correct file number (format: AB-1234567-8901234)
3. **Check appointment confirmation email** — Your appointment details are sent to your registered email
4. **Appointment may have expired** — If you missed your appointment window, you'll need to reschedule on the Passport Seva portal
5. **UMANG sync delay** — UMANG fetches data from the Passport Seva servers. There may be a delay of a few hours after booking

> **Note:** Passport appointment booking must be done through the [Passport Seva portal](https://www.passportindia.gov.in/) or mPassport Seva app. UMANG only provides tracking and status services.

---

## Fix 2: Application Tracking Failed

If you cannot track your passport application on UMANG:

1. **Verify the file number** — Your passport application file number is on your acknowledgement receipt
2. **Enter Date of Birth correctly** — Tracking requires your file number and DOB in the correct format
3. **Check application age** — Very old applications (filed years ago) may not be available for tracking
4. **Try the official portal** — Visit [passportindia.gov.in](https://www.passportindia.gov.in/) → "Track Application Status"
5. **Check via SMS** — Send "STATUS file_number" to the Passport Seva SMS service
6. **Clear UMANG cache** — Settings → Apps → UMANG → Storage → Clear Cache

---

## Fix 3: Fee Payment Error on UMANG

If you encounter payment issues for passport services:

1. **Payment is done on Passport Seva portal** — Fee payment for passport applications happens on the official portal, not through UMANG
2. **Check your payment status** — Log in to [passportindia.gov.in](https://www.passportindia.gov.in/) and check under "Payment and Scheduling"
3. **Payment failed but amount deducted:**
   - Wait 24-48 hours for auto-reversal
   - Check with your bank if the amount is on hold
   - If not refunded within 7 working days, raise a grievance on the Passport Seva portal
4. **Use a different payment method** — Try net banking, debit card, or credit card as alternatives
5. **Payment pending status** — Do not make a duplicate payment. Wait for the status to update

> **Important:** Passport fees are ₹1,500 for normal (fresh/re-issue) and ₹3,500 for tatkal processing (36-page booklet). See our [passport application guide](/guide/passport-apply-online) for full fee details.

---

## Fix 4: Service Unavailable or Timeout Errors

If UMANG shows "Service Unavailable" for passport services:

1. **Passport Seva servers may be down** — Check [passportindia.gov.in](https://www.passportindia.gov.in/) to verify
2. **Try during off-peak hours** — Government servers experience heavy load between 10 AM and 5 PM
3. **Update UMANG app** — Install the latest version from your app store
4. **Use the web version** — Visit [web.umang.gov.in](https://web.umang.gov.in/) as an alternative
5. **Check your internet connection** — Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data
6. **Disable VPN** — VPN connections can block government service APIs

## How to Track Passport Status on UMANG (Step-by-Step)

1. Open the **UMANG** app and log in
2. Search for **"Passport Seva"** in the search bar
3. Select **"Track Application Status"**
4. Enter your **File Number** (from acknowledgement receipt)
5. Enter your **Date of Birth**
6. Tap **"Track Status"**
7. Your current passport application status will be displayed

## Important Tips

1. UMANG provides passport tracking and status services — actual applications must be filed on [passportindia.gov.in](https://www.passportindia.gov.in/)
2. Keep your passport application file number and acknowledgement receipt safe for tracking
3. Passport processing typically takes 15-30 working days for normal and 1-3 working days for tatkal
4. For urgent passport issues, visit your nearest Passport Seva Kendra (PSK) with your acknowledgement receipt
5. Contact Passport Seva helpline at 1800-258-1800 for application-specific queries

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Can I apply for a passport through the UMANG app?
No, passport applications must be submitted through the [Passport Seva portal](https://www.passportindia.gov.in/) or the mPassport Seva app. UMANG only provides tracking and status services.

### Q2: Why does UMANG show "Application Not Found" for my passport?
Ensure you're entering the correct file number and date of birth. If your application was recently submitted, it may take a few hours to appear in the system.

### Q3: How do I check passport application status without UMANG?
You can check on the [Passport Seva portal](https://www.passportindia.gov.in/), call the helpline at 1800-258-1800, or use the mPassport Seva app.

### Q4: My passport fee was deducted but the payment shows failed. What should I do?
Wait 24-48 hours for auto-reversal. If the amount is not refunded within 7 working days, raise a grievance on the Passport Seva portal with your transaction details.

### Q5: Can I reschedule my passport appointment through UMANG?
No, appointment scheduling and rescheduling must be done through the [Passport Seva portal](https://www.passportindia.gov.in/). Log in to your account and select "Reschedule Appointment."

### Q6: What is the Passport Seva helpline number?
The toll-free helpline is 1800-258-1800. You can also email at passport@mea.gov.in.

### Q7: Does UMANG show police verification status for passport?
Yes, you can check the police verification status through the passport tracking feature on UMANG by entering your file number.
