# Parivahan Slot Booking Not Available? Fix DL/RC Slots

Booking an appointment slot on the **Parivahan/Sarathi portal** for DL tests, RC services, or other RTO work is often frustrating. Users frequently see "No slots available" or all dates greyed out. This is one of the most common problems with the Parivahan system.

This guide explains why slots are unavailable and proven strategies to book successfully.

## Why Are Slots Not Available?

Understanding the causes helps you plan better:

1. **RTO capacity is full** — Each RTO has a daily limit for DL tests, vehicle inspections, etc.
2. **High demand period** — Month-end and quarter-end see higher applications
3. **Holidays and weekends** — RTOs don't operate on Sundays, gazetted holidays, and some 2nd Saturdays
4. **Slots released in batches** — Many RTOs release new slots early in the morning or on specific weekdays
5. **Technical glitch** — The Sarathi/Parivahan portal sometimes shows no slots due to server issues
6. **State-specific restrictions** — Some states have temporarily paused online slot booking

## Fix 1: Try Early Morning (Best Strategy)

Most RTOs release new slots between **12 AM - 7 AM**. Here's the proven approach:

1. **Log in to the Sarathi portal at 6:00 AM** — [sarathi.parivahan.gov.in](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in)
2. Navigate to your application → **Slot Booking**
3. Check available dates for the **next 7-15 days**
4. **Book immediately** when you see an available slot — don't compare dates, just book
5. If no slots at 6 AM, try again at **12:00 AM midnight** when some RTOs refresh

> **Pro tip:** Set an alarm for 5:55 AM, keep the portal open, and refresh at 6:00 AM sharp. Many users report success with this approach.

## Fix 2: Try Different Dates

If your preferred date is full:

1. **Check dates 10-15 days ahead** — Near dates are usually full; farther dates may have openings
2. **Avoid Mondays** — Most popular booking day; try Tuesday through Thursday
3. **Avoid month-end** — Slots fill up faster from 25th to 31st
4. **Check the entire month** — Scroll through all available dates before giving up
5. **Avoid dates near festivals/holidays** — Slots before and after holidays get booked quickly

### Holiday Calendar Tips

RTOs are closed on:
- All Sundays
- National holidays (26 Jan, 15 Aug, 2 Oct)
- Gazetted holidays (varies by state)
- Some 2nd Saturdays

Slots on dates immediately after holidays fill up first because of backlog demand.

## Fix 3: Try a Different RTO

If your local RTO has no slots:

1. **Check nearby RTOs** — You can often book at a neighbouring RTO within your state
2. On the Sarathi portal, change the **RTO selection** to nearby offices
3. **For DL services:**
   - Learner's Licence (LL) can be from any RTO in your state
   - DL test should ideally be at the same RTO where you applied for LL
   - Some states allow cross-RTO DL tests
4. **For RC services:**
   - Most RC services must be done at the registering RTO
   - Address-related changes can sometimes be done at the new RTO

> **Note:** Rules for cross-RTO services vary by state. Check with the specific RTO.

## Fix 4: Check for Technical Issues

Sometimes "no slots" is actually a portal bug:

1. **Clear browser cache and cookies** — The portal caches old data
2. **Try a different browser** — Chrome works best with Parivahan portals
3. **Disable browser extensions** — Ad blockers can interfere
4. **Try incognito mode** — Open the portal in a private/incognito window
5. **Check on mobile** — Sometimes the mobile browser shows slots that desktop doesn't
6. **Try after 1 hour** — If the server is overloaded, it may not load slot data properly

## Fix 5: Use the Parivahan Sewa App

Instead of the website, try the **Parivahan Sewa** mobile app:

1. Download from Play Store — search "Parivahan Sewa"
2. Select your **State** and **RTO**
3. Navigate to your service (DL/RC)
4. Check slot availability
5. The app sometimes shows slots that the website doesn't due to different caching

## Fix 6: Visit the RTO Directly

If online slot booking is not working at all:

1. **Visit the RTO** early morning (before 10 AM)
2. Go to the **enquiry counter** and ask about slot availability
3. Some RTOs keep **walk-in slots** (5-10 per day) for people who couldn't book online
4. **Carry all your documents** — If a walk-in slot is available, you can complete the process the same day
5. **Ask about manual booking** — Some RTO counters can book a slot for you in the system

## Fix 7: Keep Checking Throughout the Day

Slots can open up due to cancellations:

1. **Check multiple times a day** — Morning (6 AM), afternoon (1 PM), and evening (7 PM)
2. **Cancelled slots reappear** — When someone cancels their appointment, the slot becomes available again
3. **Set a reminder** — Check the portal at fixed times daily until you get a slot
4. **Don't wait for a specific date** — Book whatever is available first

## Service-Specific Slot Tips

### DL Test Slot

- DL test slots are the most in-demand
- Apply for LL first; DL test slot can only be booked **30 days after LL issuance**
- Book the test slot immediately after the 30-day waiting period

### RC-Related Slots

- RC address change, transfer of ownership, and HP termination need slots
- These are less competitive than DL test slots
- Usually available within 7-10 days

### International Driving Permit (IDP)

- IDP slots are usually readily available
- Book through the Sarathi portal under DL services

## Important Tips

1. **6 AM is golden hour** — Most successful bookings happen between 6-7 AM
2. **Be flexible with dates** — Accepting any available date is better than waiting for a perfect one
3. **Check nearby RTOs** — A slightly farther RTO with available slots saves time overall
4. **Don't use agents/touts** — They often charge ₹500-₹2000 for what you can do free online
5. **Bookmark the portal** — Keep [sarathi.parivahan.gov.in](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in) bookmarked for quick access

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How far in advance can I book a Parivahan slot?

Most RTOs allow booking **7 to 30 days in advance**. The exact window depends on your state and RTO. Check the calendar on the Sarathi portal for available date ranges.

### Can I cancel and rebook a Parivahan slot?

Yes, you can cancel your booked slot on the Sarathi portal and book a new one. However, some RTOs allow only **one cancellation**. The cancelled slot becomes available for others.

### Why do slots disappear within seconds?

High-demand RTOs (especially in metro cities) have thousands of users trying to book simultaneously. Slots get booked within seconds of release. This is why early morning booking is essential.

### Is there any fee for slot booking on Parivahan?

Slot booking itself is **free**. You only pay the applicable service fee (DL test fee, RC processing fee, etc.) at the time of booking or at the RTO.

### Can I book a slot for someone else?

Yes, you can book a slot using another person's application number on the Sarathi portal. You just need their application number and date of birth.

### What happens if I miss my booked slot?

If you miss your appointment, you'll need to book a new slot. Some RTOs may require you to submit a fresh application. Try not to miss booked slots as they're difficult to get.
