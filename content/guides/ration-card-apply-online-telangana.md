# Telangana Ration Card Apply Online 2026

The Telangana ration card is issued by the **Civil Supplies Department, Telangana**. In Telangana, ration cards are called **Rice Cards** for the main subsidised category. Applications are processed through **MeeSeva** — Telangana's multi-service delivery platform — or the EPDS (Electronic Public Distribution System) portal.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at any MeeSeva centre or online at [ts.meeseva.gov.in](https://ts.meeseva.gov.in). PHH/Rice card holders get 6 kg rice per unit per month @ ₹1/kg under Telangana state scheme.

---

## Ration Card Types in Telangana

| Type | Category | Entitlement |
|------|----------|-------------|
| **Rice Card (PHH)** | Priority Household BPL | 6 kg rice/unit/month @ ₹1/kg |
| **AAY Card** | Antyodaya / Poorest households | 35 kg rice/family/month @ ₹1/kg |
| **Non-Priority (NPH)** | Non-BPL families | Limited / no food subsidy |

> Telangana provides **6 kg rice per Ration Unit (RU) per month** at ₹1/kg — one of the best ration rates in India for the PHH category.

---

## Eligibility

**Rice Card (PHH) / BPL:**
- Annual household income below ₹1.5 lakh (rural) or ₹2 lakh (urban)
- Not a government employee
- No income taxpayer in household
- No motorised 4-wheeler
- No pucca house above 1,000 sq ft (urban) or 5 acres land (rural) — norms may vary
- Aadhaar mandatory for all members

**AAY:**
- No regular income
- Landless agricultural labourer
- Old age / widow / disabled person living alone
- Bonded labourers

---

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar cards** (all members — mandatory)
- Residence proof (electricity bill, house tax receipt, rent agreement)
- Passport-size photographs
- Income certificate from MRO (Mandal Revenue Officer)
- Caste certificate (if SC/ST)
- Bank account details (for DBT)
- Voter ID (as additional identity proof)

## How to Apply

**Method 1: MeeSeva Centre (Recommended)**

1. Visit your nearest **MeeSeva kiosk** or **MeeSeva centre**
2. Ask for "New Ration Card Application" (Rice Card)
3. Provide Aadhaar, income certificate, and address proof
4. Operator fills and submits the form
5. Service fee: approximately ₹20–35
6. Receive acknowledgement number

**Method 2: MeeSeva Online Portal**

1. Go to [ts.meeseva.gov.in](https://ts.meeseva.gov.in)
2. Log in (register if first time — requires mobile OTP)
3. Search **"Ration Card"** or **"Rice Card"**
4. Click **"Apply for New Ration Card"**
5. Fill household details with Aadhaar
6. Upload documents and submit

**Method 3: EPDS Telangana Portal**

1. Go to [epds.telangana.gov.in](https://epds.telangana.gov.in)
2. Navigate to Citizen Services → New Card Application
3. Fill in details and upload documents

**Method 4: Gram Panchayat / Municipal Office**

Submit application at local Gram Panchayat (rural) or Municipal Corporation office (urban). Panchayat Secretary or supply officer verifies and processes.

## Status Check

1. Go to [epds.telangana.gov.in](https://epds.telangana.gov.in)
2. Click **"Application Status"**
3. Enter acknowledgement number or Aadhaar number
4. View status

**Via MeeSeva:** Log in to ts.meeseva.gov.in → "Track Application"

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a Ration Unit (RU) in Telangana?

Each family member counts as 1 Ration Unit (RU). A family of 4 gets 24 kg rice/month (6 kg × 4 RUs).

### Can I check Telangana ration card status by Aadhaar?

Yes — at epds.telangana.gov.in, you can search by Aadhaar number to find ration card details and application status.

### What is the rice price at Telangana ration shops?

₹1 per kg under the Telangana state scheme. This is significantly subsidised compared to the market price.

### I moved to Hyderabad from another state. How do I get a ration card?

Surrender your ration card from your home state (or get a non-objection certificate). Then apply fresh via MeeSeva in Telangana with current address proof.
