## What is Lek Ladki Yojana?

Lek Ladki Yojana is a flagship scheme launched by the Government of Maharashtra to provide financial assistance for the welfare and education of girl children born in economically weaker families. The scheme was announced in the Maharashtra State Budget 2023-24.

Under this scheme, the government provides financial assistance at various stages of a girl child's life — from birth to higher education — with a total benefit of up to **₹1,01,000** (₹1 lakh+). The scheme aims to reduce the gender gap, promote girl child education, and discourage practices like child marriage.

The scheme applies to girls born in families holding **Yellow or Orange ration cards** (BPL and APL families below a certain income threshold).

## Who is Eligible?

- **Girl child** born on or after the scheme's launch date in Maharashtra
- Family must hold a **Yellow (BPL) or Orange ration card**
- Family's annual income must be **₹1 lakh or less** (Yellow card) or up to **₹1.5 lakh** (Orange card) — as per ration card category
- The girl must be a **domicile of Maharashtra**
- Benefits available for up to **two girl children** per family
- The girl must be enrolled in school/college at the relevant stages to receive instalments

## Benefits — Stage-wise Financial Assistance

| Stage | Amount |
|-------|--------|
| At birth | ₹5,000 |
| Class 1 admission | ₹6,000 |
| Class 6 admission | ₹7,000 |
| Class 11 admission | ₹8,000 |
| On turning 18 years | ₹75,000 |
| **Total** | **₹1,01,000** |

The final instalment of ₹75,000 is given when the girl turns 18, provided she is unmarried and has completed at least Class 12 or equivalent.

## Documents Required

### For Birth Registration Instalment
- Birth certificate of the girl child
- Mother's/father's Aadhaar card
- Yellow or Orange ration card
- Bank account details (mother's or girl's joint account)
- Hospital discharge certificate
- Family income certificate

### For Education Stage Instalments
- School/college admission certificate or bonafide certificate
- Previous instalment receipt
- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) of the girl child
- Ration card (updated)
- Bank passbook copy

### For Final Instalment (Age 18)
- Age proof (birth certificate or Class 10 marksheet)
- Proof of being unmarried (self-declaration affidavit)
- Class 12 or equivalent certificate
- Bank account in the girl's name

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Online Application

1. **Visit** the Maharashtra Women and Child Development Department portal or the designated scheme portal
2. **Register** using mother's/father's mobile number and Aadhaar details
3. **Fill the application form** with details of the girl child, family ration card, income, and bank account
4. **Upload documents** — birth certificate, ration card, Aadhaar, bank passbook
5. **Submit** the application and note down the application reference number
6. **Track status** using the reference number on the same portal

### Offline Application

1. **Visit** the nearest Anganwadi centre, Gram Panchayat office, or Municipal Corporation office
2. **Collect** the Lek Ladki Yojana application form
3. **Fill in** all required details and attach self-attested copies of documents
4. **Submit** the form to the concerned officer
5. **Collect** the acknowledgement receipt
6. **Follow up** at the same office for status updates

> **Note:** For subsequent stage-wise instalments, the family needs to apply separately at each education milestone with updated documents.

## Processing Time

- Initial application verification: **30-60 days**
- Disbursement of each instalment: After verification of milestone (school admission, age proof)
- Amount is transferred via **Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)** to the linked bank account

## Fees

There are **no fees** for applying under this scheme. All services are free.

## Important Tips

1. **Apply early** — Register the girl child under the scheme as soon as possible after birth to avoid missing the first instalment
2. **Keep ration card updated** — Ensure your Yellow/Orange ration card is valid and the girl child's name is added
3. **Maintain bank account** — Keep the linked bank account active; prefer a joint account with the mother
4. **Collect all receipts** — Keep acknowledgement receipts and instalment records safe for subsequent claims
5. **Stay enrolled in school** — Education continuity is mandatory for stage-wise benefits

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Is Lek Ladki Yojana only for BPL families?
The scheme covers families with **Yellow ration cards (BPL)** and **Orange ration cards** (economically weaker APL families). Families with White or Saffron ration cards are not eligible.

### Q2. Can I get benefits for more than two daughters?
No. The scheme provides benefits for a maximum of **two girl children** per family.

### Q3. What happens if the girl gets married before 18?
The final instalment of ₹75,000 is only given if the girl is **unmarried at age 18** and has completed at least Class 12. Early marriage disqualifies the final benefit.

### Q4. Can families from other states apply?
No. Only **Maharashtra domicile** families with valid Maharashtra ration cards are eligible.

### Q5. Is this scheme linked to Majhi Kanya Bhagyashree?
Both are separate schemes for girl children in Maharashtra. Families may be eligible for both, subject to individual scheme conditions. See our [Majhi Kanya Bhagyashree guide](/guide/mh-manjhi-kanya-bhagyashree) for details.

### Q6. How is the money disbursed?
All amounts are transferred directly to the **bank account** linked with the application through DBT.

### Q7. What if I lose my acknowledgement receipt?
You can track your application using your Aadhaar number or registered mobile number on the scheme portal.
