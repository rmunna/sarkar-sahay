# Dharani Telangana: Land Record & Registration Online

**Dharani** is the integrated land records and registration portal of the Telangana government. Through [dharani.telangana.gov.in](https://dharani.telangana.gov.in/), you can check Pahani (land record), register property, obtain encumbrance certificates, and book registration slots. This guide covers the complete process.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent informational platform and is not affiliated with any government department. Always verify details on official government portals.

## Key Terms in Telangana Land Records

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Pahani** | Land record showing ownership, extent, survey number, and land classification |
| **Dharani** | Integrated portal for land records + property registration |
| **Encumbrance Certificate (EC)** | Document showing whether a property has any loans or legal liabilities |
| **Survey Number** | Unique identification for each land parcel |
| **Pattadar** | Landowner whose name is recorded in revenue records |
| **CCLA** | Chief Commissioner of Land Administration — manages Telangana land records |
| **SRO** | Sub-Registrar Office — handles property registration |

## How to Check Pahani (Land Record) Online

### Step-by-Step Process

1. Visit [dharani.telangana.gov.in](https://dharani.telangana.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"Known Your Land Status"** or **"Pahani"**
3. Select your **District (జిల్లా)**
4. Select **Mandal (మండలం)**
5. Select **Village (గ్రామం)**
6. Choose search method:
   - **Survey Number** — Enter your survey number
   - **Pattadar Name** — Search by landowner name
   - **Aadhaar Number** — Search by linked Aadhaar
   - **Khata Number** — By account number
7. Enter the details
8. Click **"Search"** or **"వెతకండి"**
9. The Pahani will display with: pattadar name, extent, classification, survey number, and encumbrance status

## How to Register Property on Dharani

Telangana has moved property registration to the Dharani portal:

### Step-by-Step Process

1. Visit [dharani.telangana.gov.in](https://dharani.telangana.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"Property Registration"**
3. **Create an account** or log in with your credentials
4. Select **registration type**:
   - Sale Deed
   - Gift Deed
   - Mortgage
   - Partition
   - Settlement
5. Enter **property details**: survey number, extent, location
6. Enter **buyer and seller details** with Aadhaar numbers
7. Upload required documents (sale agreement, Aadhaar, etc.)
8. Pay **stamp duty and registration fee** online
9. **Book a slot** at the SRO (Sub-Registrar Office)
10. Visit the SRO on the booked date with both parties and witnesses
11. Complete biometric verification
12. The registered deed will be available for download on Dharani

### Stamp Duty and Registration Fees

| Transaction Type | Stamp Duty | Registration Fee |
|-----------------|------------|-----------------|
| Sale Deed | 4% of property value | 0.5% of property value |
| Gift Deed (family) | 1% of property value | 0.5% |
| Gift Deed (others) | 4% of property value | 0.5% |
| Mortgage | 0.5% | 0.1% |
| Partition Deed | 1% | 0.5% |

> **Note:** Rates are indicative and may be revised by the Telangana government. Check the Dharani portal for current rates.

## How to Get Encumbrance Certificate (EC)

1. Visit [dharani.telangana.gov.in](https://dharani.telangana.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"Encumbrance Certificate"** or go to [registration.telangana.gov.in](https://registration.telangana.gov.in/)
3. Select **SRO jurisdiction**
4. Enter **property details**: document number, year, or survey number
5. Select the **period** for which EC is needed (e.g., last 15 years)
6. Pay the **fee online** (approximately ₹200 for first year + ₹100 per additional year)
7. The EC will be generated and available for download

## How to Book Registration Slot

1. Log in to [dharani.telangana.gov.in](https://dharani.telangana.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"Slot Booking"** under Property Registration
3. Select **SRO** (Sub-Registrar Office) based on property location
4. Choose an **available date and time**
5. Enter the **application reference number** from your registration application
6. Confirm the slot
7. You'll receive an SMS with slot confirmation details
8. Visit the SRO at the appointed time with all parties and documents

## Documents Required for Property Registration

- **Sale agreement** (signed by both parties)
- **Aadhaar card** of buyer, seller, and 2 witnesses
- **PAN card** (if property value exceeds ₹10 lakh)
- **Encumbrance certificate** (for the last 13-30 years)
- **Latest property tax receipt**
- **Pahani/land record** showing current ownership
- **Passport-size photographs**
- **Power of Attorney** (if applicable)

## Important Tips

1. **Complete Aadhaar linking** — Both buyer and seller must have Aadhaar linked to their Dharani profile for registration.
2. **Book slots early** — Popular SROs have limited slots. Book as soon as your application is approved.
3. **Check EC thoroughly** — Get an Encumbrance Certificate for at least the last 15 years before buying property.
4. **Pahani shows real-time status** — Dharani's Pahani is updated after registration, unlike other states where mutation is separate.
5. **Save your login credentials** — You'll need your Dharani account for future transactions and document downloads.

## Common Issues and Solutions

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Slot not available | Try different dates or nearby SROs with same jurisdiction |
| Pahani shows old owner after registration | Allow 7-15 days for automatic mutation on Dharani |
| EC application rejected | Verify property details and SRO jurisdiction are correct |
| Document upload failing | Ensure files are in PDF format under 5MB |
| Payment failed but amount deducted | Contact Dharani helpline or raise a grievance on the portal |

## FAQs

### What is Dharani portal in Telangana?

Dharani (dharani.telangana.gov.in) is the integrated land records and property registration portal of the Telangana government. It combines land records (Pahani), property registration, encumbrance certificates, and mutation into a single online platform.

### How can I check my land record in Telangana online?

Visit dharani.telangana.gov.in, click on "Know Your Land Status" or "Pahani", select your District, Mandal, and Village, then search by survey number, pattadar name, or Aadhaar number.

### How do I register property in Telangana through Dharani?

Create an account on Dharani, submit a property registration application with buyer/seller details, upload documents, pay stamp duty online, book a slot at the SRO, and visit on the appointed date for biometric verification and completion.

### What is the stamp duty for property registration in Telangana?

Stamp duty for sale deeds in Telangana is 4% of the property value, plus 0.5% registration fee. For gift deeds within family, it is 1%. These rates are subject to government revision.

### How do I book a registration slot on Dharani?

Log in to dharani.telangana.gov.in, go to Slot Booking, select your SRO, choose an available date and time, enter your application reference number, and confirm. You'll receive an SMS confirmation.

### What is an Encumbrance Certificate and how to get it in Telangana?

An Encumbrance Certificate (EC) shows whether a property has any pending loans, mortgages, or legal claims. Apply on the Dharani portal or registration.telangana.gov.in by entering property details and paying the fee online.

### Is mutation automatic after registration on Dharani?

Yes, in Telangana, mutation is automatically processed after property registration through the Dharani portal. The Pahani is updated within 7-15 working days without a separate application.
