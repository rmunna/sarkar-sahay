# Train Running Status Check Live: Track Any Train

Indian Railways runs over 13,000 trains daily. Trains often get delayed due to fog, maintenance, or congestion. Checking the live running status helps you plan when to reach the station. This guide covers all official methods to track any train in real-time.

## Method 1: Official Railway Enquiry Website

The most reliable method to check train running status:

1. Go to [enquiry.indianrail.gov.in](https://enquiry.indianrail.gov.in/)
2. Click on **Train Running Status** (or Spot Your Train)
3. Enter the **train number** or **train name**
4. Select the **journey date**
5. Click **Get Status**
6. The system shows the train's last reported station, arrival/departure time, and delay in minutes

**Information displayed:**
- Last station where the train was spotted
- Scheduled vs actual arrival/departure at each station
- Delay in minutes (e.g., "Running 45 minutes late")
- Expected arrival time at upcoming stations

---

## Method 2: NTES App (National Train Enquiry System)

NTES is the official Indian Railways app for train enquiry:

1. Download **NTES** from Google Play Store or Apple App Store
2. Open the app and tap **Spot Your Train** or **Running Status**
3. Enter train number or name
4. Select the date
5. View real-time running status with station-by-station details

**NTES app features:**
- Train running status with live updates
- [PNR status](/guide/train-pnr-status-check) enquiry
- Seat availability check
- Train schedule and route information
- Station-wise arrival/departure times
- Platform number (when available)

---

## Method 3: Where is My Train App

**Where is My Train** (by Sigmoid Labs / Google) is a popular app that works even without internet using GPS crowd-sourcing:

1. Download **Where is My Train** from Play Store
2. Enter the train number or name
3. View the live location on a map
4. Get estimated arrival time at your station

**Key features:**
- Works **offline** — uses GPS data from other passengers on the train
- Live train location on map
- Alarm when train approaches your station
- Platform number updates
- Supports Indian Railways and metro trains

**Note:** This is a third-party app, not official Indian Railways. Data accuracy depends on GPS crowd-sourcing.

---

## Method 4: SMS (No Internet Required)

Send an SMS to **139** in this format:

**SPOT \<train number\>** → Send to **139**

Example: `SPOT 12301` → Send to 139

You will receive the current running status via SMS. Standard SMS charges apply.

---

## Method 5: IVRS (Phone Call)

Call **139** from any phone and follow the voice prompts:
1. Select language
2. Choose option for train running status
3. Enter the 5-digit train number
4. The system reads out the current status

---

## Method 6: Station Enquiry (Live Station Info)

To check which trains are arriving/departing from a specific station:

1. Go to [enquiry.indianrail.gov.in](https://enquiry.indianrail.gov.in/)
2. Click on **Station Board** or **Live Station**
3. Enter the station name or code
4. View all arriving and departing trains with real-time status
5. See platform numbers, expected times, and delays

This is useful when you're at the station and want to know which platform your train will arrive on.

## Understanding Delay Information

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| On Time / Right Time | Train is running as per schedule |
| Late by X min | Train is delayed by the specified minutes |
| Rescheduled | Departure time has been changed by Railways |
| Cancelled | Train has been cancelled for the day |
| Diverted | Train is running on an alternate route |
| Not Yet Started | Train hasn't departed from its origin yet |

### Common Reasons for Train Delays

- **Fog** (common in North India during winter — December to February)
- **Signal failure** or track maintenance
- **Congestion** at major junctions
- **Weather** — heavy rain, cyclone, floods
- **Accidents or derailments** on the route
- **Late arrival of connecting rake**

## Important Tips

1. **Check status 2-3 hours before** heading to the station — especially during winter fog season
2. **Use the official enquiry site** ([enquiry.indianrail.gov.in](https://enquiry.indianrail.gov.in/)) for the most reliable data
3. **Platform numbers** may change — verify at the station display board before boarding
4. **For cancelled trains,** you can claim a full refund by [filing a TDR](/guide/train-ticket-cancellation-refund) or cancelling normally
5. **Winter travel** (December-February) in North India: expect delays of 1-8 hours due to fog; check running status before leaving home

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. How accurate is the train running status?
The official NTES/indianrail.gov.in data is updated when the train passes tracking points (stations and intermediate signals). There may be a 10-30 minute lag between actual position and reported position.

### Q2. Can I check running status of any train?
Yes, you can check the running status of any mail, express, superfast, or Rajdhani/Shatabdi train. Local/suburban train status is generally not available on these platforms.

### Q3. Does the Where is My Train app work offline?
Yes, it uses GPS crowd-sourcing — if other users on the train have the app, it can show live location even without your internet. However, internet improves accuracy.

### Q4. How do I find the platform number of my train?
Check the **Station Board** feature on enquiry.indianrail.gov.in or the NTES app. Platform numbers are usually available 1-2 hours before the train's arrival. Always verify on station display boards.

### Q5. What should I do if my train is cancelled?
If your train is cancelled by Indian Railways, you are entitled to a full refund. [Cancel your ticket](/guide/train-ticket-cancellation-refund) online or file a TDR on IRCTC.

### Q6. Can I track a train on Google Maps?
Not directly through Google Maps. Use the Where is My Train app for map-based live tracking, or use the official NTES app for station-by-station updates.

### Q7. What is the 139 helpline number?
139 is the official Indian Railways enquiry number. You can check train running status, [PNR status](/guide/train-pnr-status-check), and other train information through SMS or voice call.
