# Indian Army Agniveer Recruitment Guide — Eligibility, Process, Salary

The Agnipath scheme, launched by the Government of India in June 2022, is the new recruitment pathway for soldiers in the Indian Army. Under this scheme, young Indians are recruited as **Agniveers** for a four-year tenure. After four years, up to 25% of Agniveers are selected for permanent enrolment in the Armed Forces.

This guide covers everything about Indian Army Agniveer recruitment — eligibility, selection process, physical standards, and career prospects.

## What is the Agnipath Scheme?

Agnipath is a transformative defence recruitment scheme that replaces the earlier recruitment process for soldiers (jawans) in the Indian Army. Key features:

- **Tenure:** 4 years (including training)
- **Retention:** Up to 25% retained for permanent service (15 years)
- **Seva Nidhi:** Non-retained Agniveers receive a lump sum of approximately ₹11.71 lakh (tax-free) at the end of 4 years
- **Age:** 17.5 to 23 years (with relaxations for initial batches)

## Categories of Agniveer (Army)

| Category | Educational Qualification |
|----------|--------------------------|
| Agniveer (General Duty) | 10th Pass with 45% aggregate and 33% in each subject |
| Agniveer (Technical) | 12th Pass with Physics, Chemistry, Maths, English with 50% aggregate and 40% in each subject |
| Agniveer (Clerk / Store Keeper Technical) | 12th Pass with 60% aggregate and 50% in each subject |
| Agniveer (Tradesman) 10th | 10th Pass |
| Agniveer (Tradesman) 8th | 8th Pass |

## Eligibility Criteria

### Age Limit

- **Minimum:** 17.5 years
- **Maximum:** 23 years (as on the date specified in notification)

For initial batches, upper age was relaxed to 23 years (from the original 21 years).

### Educational Qualification

Varies by category as shown above. All qualifications must be from recognised boards/institutions.

### Physical Standards

| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|------------|
| Height | Minimum 170 cm (General), relaxations for hill areas, NE states, and tribal areas |
| Weight | Proportionate to height and age as per Army standards |
| Chest | Minimum 77 cm (unexpanded), minimum 5 cm expansion |
| Eyesight | 6/6 distance vision (correctable) |

**Physical Fitness Test (PFT):**

| Test | Requirement | Marks |
|------|------------|-------|
| 1.6 km Run | Group I: ≤5 min 30 sec (60 marks), Group II: ≤5 min 45 sec (48 marks) | Max 60 |
| Pull-ups | 10 = 40 marks, 9 = 33, 8 = 27, 7 = 21, 6 = 16 | Max 40 |
| Balance | Qualifying — on a balance beam | Pass/Fail |
| 9 Feet Ditch | Qualifying — jump across | Pass/Fail |

### Nationality

Must be a citizen of India. Subjects of Nepal and Bhutan are also eligible as per government rules.

### Marital Status

Must be **unmarried** at the time of recruitment.

## Selection Process

The Agniveer Army selection follows these stages:

### Stage 1: Online Registration
Register on the official website joinindianarmy.nic.in and apply for the relevant rally/recruitment.

### Stage 2: Rally Reporting / Admit Card
Candidates report to the designated rally venue with admit card and original documents.

### Stage 3: Physical Fitness Test (PFT)
Running, pull-ups, balance, and ditch jump as detailed above.

### Stage 4: Physical Measurement
Height, weight, and chest measurements checked against standards.

### Stage 5: Medical Examination
Comprehensive medical examination at the rally venue. Candidates found temporarily unfit may be given time to rectify deficiencies.

### Stage 6: Common Entrance Exam (CEE)
Online exam for candidates who clear physical and medical stages.

**CEE Pattern (General Duty):**

| Subject | Marks |
|---------|-------|
| General Knowledge | 30 |
| General Science | 40 |
| Mathematics | 30 |
| **Total** | **100** |

**CEE Pattern (Technical):**

| Subject | Marks |
|---------|-------|
| General Knowledge | 20 |
| Physics | 30 |
| Mathematics | 30 |
| Chemistry | 20 |
| **Total** | **100** |

**CEE Pattern (Clerk/SKT):**

| Subject | Marks |
|---------|-------|
| General Knowledge | 25 |
| General Science | 15 |
| Mathematics | 25 |
| English | 25 |
| Computer Science | 10 |
| **Total** | **100** |

### Stage 7: Merit List and Document Verification
Final merit list prepared based on CEE marks. Selected candidates undergo document verification and final medical.

## How to Apply

1. **Visit** joinindianarmy.nic.in
2. **Register** with email, mobile, and Aadhaar details
3. **Complete** your profile with education, physical details, NCC/sports certificates
4. **Apply** for the relevant recruitment rally in your state/region
5. **Download** the admit card when released
6. **Report** to the rally venue on the designated date

### Application Fee

**No application fee** is charged for Agniveer Army recruitment.

## Important Dates Pattern

- **Notifications:** Released state-wise and region-wise throughout the year
- **Rally schedule:** Varies by state — typically scheduled 1-2 months after notification
- **CEE:** Conducted within 1-2 months after rally completion
- **Results:** 2-4 weeks after CEE

Check joinindianarmy.nic.in regularly for state-wise rally schedules.

## Salary and Benefits

### Agniveer Pay Package

| Year | Monthly Package | In-Hand (approx.) | Agniveer Corpus Fund (30% contribution) |
|------|----------------|------|-----|
| 1st Year | ₹30,000 | ₹21,000 | ₹9,000 |
| 2nd Year | ₹33,000 | ₹23,100 | ₹9,900 |
| 3rd Year | ₹36,500 | ₹25,580 | ₹10,950 |
| 4th Year | ₹40,000 | ₹28,000 | ₹12,000 |

- Government contributes an equal amount to the Agniveer Corpus Fund
- **Seva Nidhi (after 4 years):** ₹11.71 lakh (approximate, tax-free)
- **Risk and hardship allowances** as applicable
- **Insurance:** ₹48 lakh non-contributory life insurance cover
- **Ration, clothing, accommodation** provided free during service

### For Retained Agniveers (25%)
Those selected for permanent enrolment serve for 15 more years (total 19 years) with regular Army pay, benefits, and pension.

## Preparation Tips

### 1. Start Physical Training Early
Begin running, pull-ups, and endurance training at least 3-4 months before the rally. Consistency is key.

### 2. Focus on Running
The 1.6 km run carries the most marks (60 out of 100 in PFT). Aim to complete it under 5 minutes 30 seconds.

### 3. Study NCERT Books for CEE
For the written exam, NCERT textbooks of Class 10 (GD) or Class 12 (Technical/Clerk) are sufficient.

### 4. Practice General Knowledge
Current affairs, Indian history, geography, and defence-related knowledge are important for CEE.

### 5. Keep Documents Ready
Aadhaar card, education certificates, domicile certificate, caste certificate (if applicable), NCC certificate, sports certificates — keep originals and photocopies ready.

### 6. Medical Preparation
Get a pre-medical checkup done. Common rejection reasons include flat feet, dental issues, colour blindness, and vision problems. Address fixable issues beforehand.

## Career After Agniveer

- **25% retained** in Indian Army for permanent service
- **Seva Nidhi** for non-retained Agniveers to start business or education
- **Preference in recruitment:** Central Armed Police Forces (CAPF) and state police give preference to Agniveer veterans
- **Education support:** 12th pass certificate equivalent for Agniveers, skill certificates
- **Bank loans:** Priority for entrepreneurship

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What happens after 4 years as an Agniveer?
Up to 25% of Agniveers are retained for permanent service. The remaining 75% are released with the Seva Nidhi amount and skill certificates.

### Is there any reservation in Agniveer recruitment?
Yes, reservation as per government norms applies — SC, ST, OBC, and EWS categories get reservation in recruitment.

### Can women apply for Agniveer Army?
As of now, Agniveer recruitment in the Indian Army is for male candidates only. Women can join through other entry schemes like NDA (since 2022) and officer entries.

### Is NCC certificate beneficial for Agniveer recruitment?
Yes, NCC 'A', 'B', and 'C' certificate holders get bonus marks and preference in recruitment. NCC 'C' holders with specific grades may get direct entry.

### What is the Seva Nidhi amount?
The Seva Nidhi is approximately ₹11.71 lakh, which includes the Agniveer's contribution and the government's matching contribution over 4 years, plus accrued interest. This amount is tax-free.

### Can Agniveers apply for officer entries later?
Yes, Agniveers can apply for officer entries like CDS, NDA (if age permits), and other commissioned officer schemes during or after their Agniveer tenure.
