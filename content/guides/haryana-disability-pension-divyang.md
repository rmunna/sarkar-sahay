## What is Haryana Disability Pension (Divyang Samman Allowance)?

**Haryana Divyang Samman Allowance** (Disability Pension) is a monthly pension for persons with **60% or more disability** who are permanent residents of Haryana. The current monthly pension is:

- **₹2,250/month** (revised periodically by the government)

The scheme is implemented by the **Department of Social Justice and Empowerment, Haryana** and payments are made via DBT.

---

## Key Benefits

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Monthly pension | ₹2,250/month |
| Minimum disability | 60% (assessed by a government medical board) |
| Income limit | None (any income level) |
| Minimum age | 18 years |
| Payment | DBT to Aadhaar-linked bank account |

> Note: Unlike many other states, Haryana disability pension has **no income limit** — even persons with higher incomes are eligible if they have 60%+ disability and no other government pension.

---

## Who is Eligible?

**You ARE eligible if:**
- You are a **permanent resident of Haryana** (minimum 15 years)
- You have **60% or more disability** (certified by a government medical authority)
- You are **18 years or older**
- You are **not receiving any other government pension** (government employee pension, old age pension)
- You have a **Parivar Pehchaan Patra (PPP)** — Haryana family ID

**Types of disability covered:**
- Physical disability (locomotor)
- Visual impairment (blindness, low vision)
- Hearing impairment
- Intellectual disability
- Mental illness
- Multiple disabilities

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar Card** — identity
2. **Parivar Pehchaan Patra (PPP)** — mandatory for Haryana schemes
3. **Disability certificate** — from CMO / Civil Surgeon / Government hospital certifying ≥60% disability
4. **UDID card** — if available (strongly recommended to get one at swavlambancard.gov.in)
5. **Domicile certificate** — Haryana resident for 15+ years
6. **Bank passbook** — Aadhaar-linked savings account
7. **Self-declaration** — no other government pension being received
8. **Photograph**

## How to Apply — Step by Step

### Step 1 — Get Disability Certificate

If you don't have a disability certificate:
1. Visit the nearest **Government Hospital / District Hospital**
2. Meet the **Medical Superintendent / CMO**
3. Request disability assessment
4. Medical board assesses and issues certificate with disability percentage

### Step 2 — Apply for UDID Card

Apply for your **Unique Disability ID (UDID) card** at **[swavlambancard.gov.in](https://swavlambancard.gov.in)** — it is the national disability ID and simplifies future applications.

### Step 3 — Ensure PPP is Made

Get your **Parivar Pehchaan Patra** at any Atal Sewa Kendra/CSC — mandatory for Haryana scheme applications.

### Step 4 — Apply on Saral Haryana

1. Go to **[saralharyana.gov.in](https://saralharyana.gov.in)**
2. Register / login
3. Search for **"Divyang Samman Allowance"** or **"Disability Pension"**
4. Fill in the application with PPP ID, Aadhaar, disability certificate details
5. Upload documents and submit

### Step 5 — Apply at Atal Sewa Kendra (Alternative)

Visit the nearest Atal Sewa Kendra/CSC for assisted application. The VLE completes the online application on your behalf.

### Step 6 — Verification and Approval

District Social Welfare Officer verifies documents. If the disability certificate is from a government facility and meets the threshold, approval is usually straightforward.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. My disability is 40%. Am I eligible?**

No. Haryana Divyang Pension requires **60% or more disability**. With 40%, you are not eligible for this pension. Check if you qualify for NHFDC loans or other disability welfare benefits for lower disability percentages.

**2. I am 15 years old with 80% disability. Can I apply?**

No. Minimum age is 18 years. You can apply once you turn 18.

**3. I have a disability certificate from 5 years ago. Is it still valid?**

Disability certificates for permanent conditions (congenital, limb loss) are generally valid permanently. For progressive or fluctuating conditions, fresh assessment may be needed. Check with the CMO.

**4. My pension was stopped. The portal says "suspended." Why?**

Common reasons: PPP data mismatch, Aadhaar-bank link broken, periodic re-verification requirement. Visit the Atal Sewa Kendra to diagnose and resolve.

**5. The UDID card shows 55% disability but the hospital certificate says 60%. Which is used?**

The **certified government hospital disability certificate** is the primary document. If there is a discrepancy, get a fresh certificate from the CMO / Medical Board.
