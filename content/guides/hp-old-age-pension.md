## What is Himachal Pradesh Old Age Pension?

The **Himachal Pradesh Old Age Pension** is a social security scheme under the **Department of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of HP**. It provides a monthly pension of **₹1,500** (combining state and NSAP components) to senior citizens aged **60 years and above** who belong to economically weaker sections.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Age:** Must be **60 years or older**
2. **Residency:** Must be a permanent resident of Himachal Pradesh
3. **Income:** Annual income should not exceed **₹35,000** (BPL or economically weaker) — varies by category
4. **Other Pension:** Should not be receiving any other government pension
5. **Aadhaar:** Must have Aadhaar Card linked to bank account

## Pension Amount

| Age Group | Monthly Pension |
|-----------|----------------|
| 60-69 years | ₹1,500 |
| 70-79 years | ₹1,500 |
| 80+ years | ₹1,500+ (additional NSAP component of ₹500) |

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar Card** (linked to bank account)
- **Age Proof** (Birth Certificate / Aadhaar / School Certificate)
- **HP Bonafide / Domicile Certificate**
- **Income Certificate** from Tehsildar
- **BPL Card** (if applicable)
- **Bank Passbook** (Aadhaar-linked account)
- **Passport-size photographs** (2 copies)

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Online Application (e-District HP)

1. **Visit** [edistrict.hp.gov.in](https://edistrict.hp.gov.in)
2. **Register** or login
3. **Navigate** to Social Security Pension → Old Age Pension
4. **Fill** the application form with personal and bank details
5. **Upload** Aadhaar, age proof, income certificate, and bank passbook
6. **Submit** and note the application ID
7. **Track** status on the same portal

### Offline Application

1. **Visit** the nearest **Lok Mitra Kendra** or **Block Development Office**
2. **Collect** the pension application form
3. **Fill** and attach all documents
4. **Submit** to the BDO or Social Welfare Officer
5. **Verification** at Gram Panchayat / Ward level

## Processing Time

- **Verification:** 15-30 days
- **Approval:** After field verification
- **First Payment:** Within 1-2 months of approval
- **Ongoing:** Monthly via DBT

## Important Tips

1. **Aadhaar-Bank Linking:** Essential for receiving DBT payments
2. **Life Certificate:** Submit Jeevan Pramaan annually to continue receiving pension
3. **Lok Mitra Kendra:** Free assistance available for online applications
4. **Update Details:** Inform the office immediately if you change bank account or address
5. **No Duplicate Pension:** You cannot receive this along with widow pension or disability pension

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. What is the monthly pension amount?
**₹1,500 per month** for senior citizens aged 60 and above.

### Q2. What is the income limit?
Annual income should generally not exceed **₹35,000** (check latest notification for updated limits).

### Q3. Can I apply online?
Yes, apply through [edistrict.hp.gov.in](https://edistrict.hp.gov.in).

### Q4. Is Aadhaar mandatory?
Yes, Aadhaar linked to bank account is required for DBT.

### Q5. Can I get both Old Age Pension and Pyari Behna scheme?
Generally, you can receive only one social security pension. Check with the local office.

### Q6. What if I move to another district?
You can request a transfer of your pension to the new district's office.

### Q7. How often is the pension paid?
Monthly, directly to your Aadhaar-linked bank account.
