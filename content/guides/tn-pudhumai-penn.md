# Pudhumai Penn Scheme Tamil Nadu – ₹1,000/Month for Girl Students

## What is Pudhumai Penn Scheme?

Pudhumai Penn (Modern Woman) is a Tamil Nadu government scheme providing **₹1,000 per month** to girl students who completed their schooling (Class 6 to 12) in **government schools** and are pursuing **undergraduate degree, diploma, or ITI courses**. The scheme encourages higher education among girls from government school backgrounds.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Gender:** Must be a **girl/woman student**
2. **Schooling:** Must have studied **Class 6 to 12 in a Tamil Nadu government school**
3. **Higher Education:** Must be currently pursuing an **undergraduate degree, diploma, or ITI** in a recognized institution
4. **No income limit** — available to all government school alumni regardless of family income
5. **Age:** Must be within the standard age for undergraduate study

### Who is NOT Eligible?

- Students who attended private schools for any class between 6-12
- Students who have already completed a degree
- Students in postgraduate or professional courses beyond UG level (varies)

## Documents Required

- **School Transfer Certificate (TC)** — showing government school attendance from Class 6-12
- **Class 12 Marks Sheet**
- **College/Institution Admission Proof** (bonafide certificate)
- **Aadhaar Card**
- **Bank Account Passbook** (in student's name, linked to Aadhaar)
- **Community Certificate** (if applicable)
- **Passport Size Photo**

## How to Apply

### Online Application

1. **Visit** the official Pudhumai Penn portal or the higher education department website
2. **Register** with Aadhaar and mobile number
3. **Enter** school details — government school name, EMIS number
4. **Enter** college/course details
5. **Upload** TC, marks sheet, bonafide certificate, photo
6. **Enter** bank account details
7. **Submit** and note the reference number

### Through College

1. Many colleges facilitate applications during the **admission process**
2. Submit your government school TC and other documents to the scholarship cell
3. The college forwards your application to the department

## Scholarship Amount

| Detail | Amount |
|--------|--------|
| Monthly Stipend | ₹1,000 |
| Annual Amount | ₹12,000 |
| Duration | Until completion of UG course (3-4 years) |
| Mode | DBT to student's bank account |

## Processing Time

- **Verification:** 1-2 months
- **First Payment:** Within 2-3 months of application
- **Ongoing:** Monthly/quarterly disbursement

## Important Tips

1. **Preserve Your School TC:** This is the primary proof of government school education. Keep the original safe.
2. **Apply Early:** Submit the application as soon as you join college to avoid delays.
3. **Bank Account in Student's Name:** Parent's accounts are not accepted. Open an account in your name.
4. **Continue Studies:** Dropping out or failing will stop the scholarship. Maintain enrollment.
5. **Cannot Combine:** Check if you can combine this with other state scholarships — typically only one government scholarship is allowed.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: I studied Class 1-5 in a private school and 6-12 in a government school. Am I eligible?
**Yes.** The requirement is Class 6-12 in a government school. Where you studied Class 1-5 does not matter.

### Q2: Does this cover professional courses like engineering and medicine?
**Yes**, undergraduate professional courses are covered if you meet the government school criteria.

### Q3: Is there a minimum marks requirement?
**No** minimum marks required. You just need to pass Class 12 and be admitted to a recognized course.

### Q4: Can boys apply for Pudhumai Penn?
**No.** This scheme is exclusively for girl students.

### Q5: What if I'm studying in a college outside Tamil Nadu?
Generally, the student must be studying in a **Tamil Nadu institution**. Check the latest notification for out-of-state provisions.

### Q6: Will I get ₹1,000 every month or once a year?
The ₹1,000 is a **monthly benefit**, though disbursement may sometimes be quarterly (₹3,000 every 3 months).
