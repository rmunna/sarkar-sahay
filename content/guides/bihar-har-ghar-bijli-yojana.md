## What is Bihar Har Ghar Bijli Yojana?

**Har Ghar Bijli Yojana** is the Government of Bihar's scheme to electrify every household in Bihar — providing **free electricity connections** to unelectrified and BPL (Below Poverty Line) households. The scheme is implemented by **BSPHCL (Bihar State Power Holding Company Limited)**.

Bihar launched this alongside the central **SAUBHAGYA scheme** to ensure complete rural electrification at the village and household level.

## What is Free Under This Scheme?

- **Free electricity connection** — the connection charges (meter, wiring from pole to house) are waived for eligible BPL families
- **LED bulb kit** — some beneficiaries receive LED bulbs
- No monthly minimum consumption charge for BPL connections in some tariff categories

**Note:** Electricity units consumed are NOT free — you pay for units used as per normal tariff. The scheme only covers the cost of getting the connection installed.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Household in Bihar** without electricity connection
- **BPL (Below Poverty Line)** families — as per the BPL/Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC) 2011 list
- Rural households in unelectrified or partially electrified villages
- APL (Above Poverty Line) families may also apply — but will pay normal connection charges

## How to Apply

### Online Application
1. Go to **[hargharbijli.bsphcl.co.in](https://hargharbijli.bsphcl.co.in)**
2. Click **"New Application"**
3. Enter your mobile number for OTP verification
4. Fill in:
   - Name, address, district, block
   - BPL/Ration card number
   - Aadhaar number
   - Details of house (type: kacha/pakka, number of rooms)
5. Upload documents
6. Submit → note application number

### Offline Application
Visit your nearest **BSPHCL office** or **electricity sub-division office** with the documents. A lineman will be assigned to inspect and install the connection.

### Via Door-to-Door Registration
BSPHCL has conducted camp-mode registrations in many villages — check if a camp is scheduled in your area through your Gram Panchayat.

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card** (all family members' photos, one to be owner of connection)
2. **BPL ration card** or SECC list inclusion proof
3. **Address proof** — Aadhaar with current address
4. **Photograph** (passport size)
5. **Property proof** (optional — voter ID, land tax receipt confirming residence)

## After Connection: Electricity Bill Payment

Once connected, pay electricity bills via:
- BSPHCL website: [bsphcl.co.in](https://bsphcl.co.in)
- BSPHCL mobile app
- CSC / Jan Seva Kendra
- Nearest electricity office

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My village has electricity poles but my house is not connected. Is this scheme for me?
Yes — even if the grid has reached your village/street, if your house is not individually connected, you qualify. Apply for a service connection.

### We already have electricity but want to shift to a larger load. Is this for us?
No — Har Ghar Bijli is for new connections only. For load enhancement on existing connections, apply directly with BSPHCL for a load upgrade.

### Will my electricity bill be zero if I get a BPL connection?
No — the scheme only provides the connection free of cost. Units consumed are billed. However, Bihar has low consumption tariffs for BPL/domestic connections. If you consume very little electricity (< 30 units/month), your bill may be ₹100–₹200.

### My name is not in the SECC/BPL list. Can I still apply?
You can apply as a non-BPL household — but you'll pay the normal connection charges (₹3,000–₹5,000 for single-phase connection). Only BPL families get the connection free of cost.

### I applied months ago but no lineman has come. What should I do?
Call the BSPHCL helpline: **1912** (electricity complaint helpline, Bihar). Alternatively, visit your nearest electricity sub-division office with your application number.
