# ABHA Health ID Not Working? How to Fix Registration Errors

Having trouble creating or accessing your ABHA Health ID? You're not alone. Common issues include OTP not being received, Aadhaar linking failures, duplicate accounts, and app errors. This guide covers all known ABHA errors and their fixes.

## Common ABHA Errors and Solutions

### 1. OTP Not Received During Registration

**Symptoms:** You entered your Aadhaar number but the OTP never arrives on your mobile.

**Fixes:**
- **Check your Aadhaar-linked mobile** — The OTP is sent to the mobile number registered with Aadhaar, not the number you're currently using. Verify at [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/)
- **Wait 2-3 minutes** — OTP delivery can be delayed during peak hours
- **Check network/signal** — Ensure your phone has proper mobile network coverage
- **Don't request OTP repeatedly** — Wait at least 60 seconds between attempts; too many requests may temporarily block OTP generation
- **Try again after 30 minutes** — If OTP fails multiple times, the system may impose a cooldown
- **Update Aadhaar mobile** — If your Aadhaar-linked mobile is old/inactive, update it first. See our [Aadhaar guide](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online)

### 2. Aadhaar Linking / e-KYC Failed

**Symptoms:** Error messages like "Aadhaar authentication failed" or "e-KYC error" during ABHA creation.

**Fixes:**
- **Verify Aadhaar number** — Re-check you entered the correct 12-digit Aadhaar number
- **Aadhaar biometric lock** — If you've enabled biometric lock on Aadhaar, OTP-based e-KYC should still work. But if the portal uses biometric, unlock at [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/)
- **UIDAI server downtime** — Aadhaar servers may be temporarily down. Try again after a few hours
- **Name mismatch** — If your name in the form doesn't match Aadhaar exactly (including spelling), authentication may fail
- **Try a different browser** — Use Chrome or Firefox in incognito mode; clear cookies first

### 3. Duplicate ABHA Account Error

**Symptoms:** "ABHA number already exists for this Aadhaar" or "Health ID already created."

**Fixes:**
- **You may already have an ABHA** — Try logging in at [abha.abdm.gov.in](https://abha.abdm.gov.in/) using your Aadhaar number with "Forgot ABHA" option
- **Recover your existing ABHA** — Click **"Already have ABHA? Login"** → **"Forgot ABHA address"** → Verify with Aadhaar OTP to recover your existing account
- **Facility-created ABHA** — Some hospitals create ABHA during registration without informing you. Your existing ABHA is still valid
- **Contact helpline** — If you need to merge or delete a duplicate, call **1800-114-477**

### 4. ABHA App Crashing or Not Loading

**Symptoms:** The ABHA mobile app freezes, crashes on launch, or shows a blank screen.

**Fixes:**
- **Update the app** — Install the latest version from Google Play Store or Apple App Store
- **Clear app cache** — Go to Phone Settings → Apps → ABHA → Clear Cache → Clear Data → Reopen
- **Check internet connection** — The app needs a stable internet connection to function
- **Restart your phone** — A simple restart often resolves app freezes
- **Use the web portal instead** — If the app keeps failing, use [abha.abdm.gov.in](https://abha.abdm.gov.in/) in a mobile or desktop browser

### 5. ABHA Login Not Working

**Symptoms:** Unable to log in with ABHA number, address, or password.

**Fixes:**
- **Reset password** — Click **"Forgot Password"** on the login page, verify with Aadhaar OTP, and set a new password
- **Use ABHA number instead of address** — Try logging in with your 14-digit ABHA number instead of the @abdm address
- **Check for typos** — ABHA addresses are case-sensitive
- **Browser issues** — Try incognito mode or a different browser
- **Account may be inactive** — If unused for a long period, try logging in via Aadhaar OTP method

### 6. Health Records Not Showing

**Symptoms:** You've visited a hospital but records don't appear in your ABHA account.

**Fixes:**
- **Hospital must be ABDM-registered** — Only facilities registered with ABDM can share records digitally. Ask the hospital if they support ABDM
- **Link the facility** — Go to your ABHA dashboard → **Link Health Records** → Search for the hospital → Approve the linking request
- **Records take time** — After linking, records may take 24-48 hours to reflect
- **Check consent** — Ensure you haven't accidentally revoked consent for that facility

## Important Tips

1. **Always use official portals** — Only create or manage ABHA at [abha.abdm.gov.in](https://abha.abdm.gov.in/). Avoid third-party websites
2. **Keep Aadhaar mobile updated** — Most ABHA issues stem from an outdated Aadhaar-linked mobile number
3. **Screenshot your ABHA details** — Save your 14-digit ABHA number and ABHA address somewhere safe
4. **One ABHA per person** — Don't try to create multiple accounts; it will cause duplicate errors
5. **Be patient during peak hours** — Government portals experience heavy traffic during mornings and scheme deadlines

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why am I not receiving OTP for ABHA registration?
The OTP is sent to your **Aadhaar-linked mobile number**. If that number is inactive or changed, you won't receive it. Update your Aadhaar mobile first at a nearby enrolment centre or via [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/).

### How do I delete a duplicate ABHA account?
You cannot delete ABHA yourself from the portal. Call the **ABDM helpline at 1800-114-477** and request account deletion or merger. You'll need to verify your identity via Aadhaar.

### Can I change my ABHA address (@abdm username)?
Yes. Log in to your ABHA account, go to **Profile Settings**, and you can update your ABHA address. The old address will be released after the change.

### ABHA portal shows "Service Unavailable" — what to do?
This usually means the server is under maintenance or experiencing high traffic. Wait **30 minutes to 1 hour** and try again. Check [abdm.gov.in](https://abdm.gov.in/) for any announced downtime.

### Is my health data safe with ABHA?
Yes. ABHA follows the **consent-based data sharing** model. No hospital or facility can access your records without your explicit approval. You can revoke access anytime from your dashboard.

### Can I create ABHA at a hospital or health centre?
Yes. Many government hospitals and Ayushman Bharat Health & Wellness Centres can create ABHA for you at the registration counter. Carry your Aadhaar card. See our [ABHA creation guide](/guide/digital-health-id-abha-create) for the full process.
