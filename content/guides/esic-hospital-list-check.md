# ESIC Hospital List: Find Nearest Dispensary & Hospital

The **Employees' State Insurance Corporation (ESIC)** operates a vast network of hospitals, dispensaries, and empanelled private hospitals across India. If you hold an ESIC e-Pehchan card, you and your family can get free medical treatment at these facilities. This guide helps you find the nearest ESIC facility and understand the services available.

## Types of ESIC Healthcare Facilities

### 1. ESIC Hospitals
- Full-fledged hospitals with OPD, IPD, surgery, ICU, and diagnostics
- Located in major industrial cities
- Provide specialist and super-specialist services
- Free treatment for insured persons and dependents

### 2. ESIC Dispensaries
- Primary healthcare centres for outpatient treatment
- Available in smaller towns and industrial areas
- Provide general consultations, medicines, and basic diagnostics
- Refer patients to ESIC hospitals for specialist treatment

### 3. Empanelled Private Hospitals
- Private hospitals tied up with ESIC for secondary and tertiary care
- Cashless treatment with ESIC referral
- Used when ESIC hospital doesn't have the required speciality
- Requires referral letter from ESIC dispensary/hospital

### 4. ESIC Branch Offices
- Administrative offices for ESIC services
- Handle registration, contribution queries, and benefit claims
- Not medical facilities but can guide you to nearest hospitals

## How to Find Nearest ESIC Hospital Online

### Method 1: ESIC Official Website

1. **Visit** [esic.gov.in](https://www.esic.gov.in)
2. **Click** on "Hospitals" or "Find ESIC Facilities" from the menu
3. **Select** your **state** from the dropdown
4. **Select** your **city/district**
5. **Choose** facility type — Hospital, Dispensary, or Empanelled Hospital
6. **View** the list with address, phone number, and services available
7. **Note** the timings and contact details for your visit

### Method 2: ESIC Branch Office Locator

1. **Visit** [esic.gov.in](https://www.esic.gov.in)
2. **Go to** "Branch Office" or "Regional Office" section
3. **Select** your state and region
4. **Get** the branch office address and contact number
5. **Call** or visit for information about nearest hospitals and dispensaries

### Method 3: UMANG App

1. **Download** the UMANG app from Play Store or App Store
2. **Search** for "ESIC" in the app
3. **Select** "Hospital/Dispensary Search"
4. **Enter** your location or allow GPS access
5. **View** nearest ESIC facilities with directions

## How to Get Treatment at ESIC Hospital

### For Direct Visit (ESIC Hospital/Dispensary)

1. **Carry** your ESIC e-Pehchan card (see our [ESIC Card guide](/guide/esic-card-apply-download))
2. **Visit** the nearest ESIC dispensary or hospital
3. **Register** at the OPD counter with your IP number
4. **Consult** the doctor — All services and medicines are free
5. **Collect medicines** from the hospital pharmacy

### For Empanelled Hospital (Referral Required)

1. **Visit** your designated ESIC dispensary first
2. **Get examined** by the ESIC doctor
3. **Request referral** if specialist treatment is needed
4. **Receive** a referral letter specifying the empanelled hospital
5. **Visit** the empanelled hospital with referral letter and e-Pehchan card
6. **Treatment is cashless** — Hospital bills ESIC directly

## Emergency Services

- ESIC hospitals provide **24x7 emergency services**
- No referral needed for emergencies
- Carry your e-Pehchan card or IP number
- In genuine emergencies, treatment is provided first, documentation later
- Post-stabilisation, you may be referred to an empanelled hospital if needed

## Important Tips

1. **Always visit your designated dispensary first** — Direct visits to empanelled hospitals without referral may not be cashless
2. **Carry e-Pehchan card** on every visit — It speeds up registration and avoids complications
3. **Check OPD timings** before visiting — Most ESIC dispensaries operate from 8 AM to 4 PM on working days
4. **Register family members** with ESIC to ensure they can also avail free treatment
5. **For emergencies**, go to the nearest ESIC hospital directly — No referral needed

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Can I visit any ESIC hospital in India?
Yes, the e-Pehchan card provides portability. You can visit any ESIC hospital or dispensary across India, not just in your registered city.

### 2. Do I need an appointment for ESIC hospital?
No appointment is needed for OPD at most ESIC hospitals and dispensaries. Simply walk in during OPD hours with your e-Pehchan card.

### 3. Can I choose which empanelled hospital to go to?
The referral letter from your ESIC dispensary will specify the empanelled hospital based on the treatment needed and availability. You generally cannot choose independently.

### 4. What are the OPD timings for ESIC dispensaries?
Most ESIC dispensaries operate from **8:00 AM to 4:00 PM** on working days (Monday to Saturday). Timings may vary by location. ESIC hospitals have 24x7 emergency services.

### 5. Is maternity treatment covered at ESIC hospitals?
Yes, full maternity care including prenatal check-ups, delivery (normal and C-section), and postnatal care are covered at ESIC hospitals. The insured woman also receives maternity cash benefit.

### 6. Can retired employees use ESIC hospitals?
ESIC medical coverage continues for 78 days after the last ESI contribution. After that, retired employees are not covered unless they have a permanent disability from an employment injury.

### 7. How do I file a complaint about ESIC hospital services?
You can file a grievance on the ESIC portal at [esic.gov.in](https://www.esic.gov.in) under the "Grievance" section, or contact the branch office in your area. You can also write to the Regional Director of ESIC.

### 8. Are ESIC hospitals only for factory workers?
No, ESIC covers employees in all types of establishments (factories, shops, hotels, restaurants, cinemas, etc.) with 10+ employees where the employee earns up to ₹21,000/month.
