## What is Uttarakhand Mukhyamantri Swarojgar Yojana?

**Mukhyamantri Swarojgar Yojana (MSY)** is Uttarakhand government's self-employment scheme that provides **subsidized loans** to unemployed youth, including migrants who returned to Uttarakhand, to start their own businesses in manufacturing, services, or trade.

---

## Key Benefits

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Loan amount — General | Up to ₹5 lakh (manufacturing/trade) |
| Loan amount — Special | Up to ₹10 lakh (for specific sectors) |
| Subsidy | 25% of loan (up to ₹2.5 lakh) |
| Interest rate | Subsidized rate via bank |
| Portal | msy.uk.gov.in |
| Department | MSME / District Industries Centre |

---

## Who is Eligible?

- **Uttarakhand domicile** — 5-year residence in the state
- Age **18 to 45 years** (relaxed to 50 for widows and disabled)
- **Educated unemployed / skill-trained youth**
- **Return migrants** who came back to Uttarakhand during COVID or other reasons
- **SC/ST/OBC applicants** get preference
- Minimum educational qualification: **Class 8 pass** (varies by loan amount)
- **Not already self-employed** in a business with turnover above threshold

## Documents Required

1. **Aadhaar card**
2. **Uttarakhand domicile certificate** (Mul Niwas Praman Patra)
3. **Age proof** (Aadhaar, birth certificate)
4. **Educational certificate** (minimum Class 8 mark sheet)
5. **Caste certificate** (if SC/ST/OBC)
6. **Project report / business plan** (prepared with DIC support)
7. **Bank account details** (Aadhaar-linked)
8. **Rent agreement / shop ownership proof** (if premises available)

## How to Apply

### Step 1 — Online Application

Visit **[msy.uk.gov.in](https://msy.uk.gov.in)**:
- Register with Aadhaar
- Fill personal, education, and business details
- Upload documents
- Submit application

### Step 2 — DIC Verification

The **District Industries Centre (DIC)** in your district reviews the application:
- Verifies eligibility (domicile, age, education)
- Assists in preparing the project report if needed
- Forwards eligible applications to empanelled banks

### Step 3 — Bank Loan Sanction

Bank appraises the project:
- Loan sanctioned (usually within 30 days of DIC recommendation)
- Subsidy amount calculated (25% of sanctioned loan)

### Step 4 — Subsidy Disbursement

After loan disbursement and start of business:
- 25% subsidy credited to the loan account directly
- Balance loan repaid to bank over the agreed tenure

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. I returned from Delhi to my village in Uttarakhand during COVID. Am I eligible?**

Yes. Return migrants are a priority category under MSY. Provide Aadhaar with Uttarakhand address, domicile certificate, and proof of return. DIC will process your application on priority.

**2. Can I use the loan to open a dhaba (food stall) on a highway?**

Yes. A food service business (dhaba, restaurant, snacks stall) qualifies under the services sector. You will need the business location detail (rent agreement or owned premises) in the project report.

**3. I am 48 years old and a widow. Am I eligible?**

Yes. The age limit is relaxed to 50 years for widows. Apply with your Aadhaar, domicile, and widow certificate. You may also get additional relaxation in collateral requirements.

**4. What if the bank rejects my loan application?**

If a bank rejects, approach the DIC — they can recommend another empanelled bank or help re-present the case. You can also appeal to the District Magistrate.

**5. Is the 25% subsidy a grant or is it also repaid?**

The 25% subsidy is a **government grant** — it is credited to your loan account and reduces your loan outstanding. You only repay the remaining 75%.
