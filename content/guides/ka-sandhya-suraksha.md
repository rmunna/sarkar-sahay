# Sandhya Suraksha Pension Karnataka – ₹600/Month for Senior Citizens

## What is Sandhya Suraksha Scheme?

Sandhya Suraksha is the Karnataka government's **old age pension scheme** providing **₹600 per month** to senior citizens belonging to BPL families. The pension is credited directly to the beneficiary's bank account or paid through the post office. This is part of the state's social security program managed by the Department of Social Welfare.

## Who is Eligible?

1. **Age:** Must be **65 years or above**
2. **Income:** Must belong to a **BPL family** or have annual income below the prescribed limit
3. **Residency:** Must be a **resident of Karnataka** for at least 5 years
4. **Not receiving other pension:** Must not be receiving any other government pension (family pension, retirement pension, etc.)
5. **Ration Card:** Must have a Karnataka ration card

## Pension Amount

| Category | Monthly Pension |
|----------|----------------|
| Age 65-79 | ₹600/month |
| Age 80+ | ₹800/month (enhanced under some provisions) |

*Note: The state may supplement the central government's IGNOAPS (Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme) amount.*

## Documents Required

- **Age Proof** (Aadhaar card, voter ID, birth certificate, or school certificate)
- **Aadhaar Card**
- **BPL / Ration Card**
- **Income Certificate** (from Tahsildar)
- **Bank Account Passbook** (linked to Aadhaar)
- **Residence Proof** (voter ID, utility bill)
- **Passport Size Photo**
- **Caste Certificate** (if applicable for priority)

## How to Apply

### Online Application

1. **Visit** [sevasindhuservices.karnataka.gov.in](https://sevasindhuservices.karnataka.gov.in)
2. **Register/Login** with mobile number
3. **Search** for "Sandhya Suraksha" or "Old Age Pension"
4. **Fill** the application form with personal details
5. **Upload** age proof, Aadhaar, income certificate, photo
6. **Enter** bank account details
7. **Submit** and note the application number

### Offline Application

1. Visit the **Taluk Social Welfare Office** or **Gram Panchayat** office
2. Collect the pension application form
3. Fill in details and attach document copies
4. Submit to the Taluk Social Welfare Officer
5. Collect acknowledgment receipt

## Verification Process

- Taluk-level social welfare officer verifies age, income, and residency
- Physical verification may be conducted at the applicant's residence
- After verification, the pension is sanctioned and payment starts

## Processing Time

- **Verification:** 30-60 days
- **First Payment:** Within 2-3 months of approval
- **Ongoing:** Monthly credit to bank account

## Important Tips

1. **Age Proof is Critical:** If you don't have a birth certificate, a school leaving certificate or voter ID mentioning age is accepted.
2. **Aadhaar-Bank Linkage:** Ensure Aadhaar is linked to your bank account for uninterrupted DBT payments.
3. **Life Certificate:** Pensioners must submit a **life certificate (Jeevan Pramaan)** annually to continue receiving the pension. See our [Jeevan Pramaan guide](/guide/jeevan-pramaan-life-certificate-failed-fix) for help.
4. **Nominee Details:** Provide a nominee at the time of application in case of the pensioner's demise.
5. **Report Death:** Family members must report the pensioner's death to stop pension credits and avoid legal issues.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Can I get both central and state old age pension?
Sandhya Suraksha may include the central IGNOAPS component. You won't get two separate pensions, but the state may top up the central amount.

### Q2: What if I'm 60 but not yet 65?
Some provisions may cover **destitute persons aged 60-64** under different categories. Check with your taluk social welfare office.

### Q3: Can I apply if I have a government family pension?
**No.** If you receive any government pension (including family pension), you are not eligible for Sandhya Suraksha.

### Q4: How do I submit the annual life certificate?
Visit your bank or use the **Jeevan Pramaan app** with biometric authentication to submit digitally.

### Q5: What happens if the pension is not credited for 2 months?
Visit the Taluk Social Welfare Office with your acknowledgment receipt and bank passbook to report the issue.
