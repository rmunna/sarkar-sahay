## What is Palanhar Yojana?

Palanhar Yojana is a unique scheme by the Government of Rajasthan that provides **monthly financial assistance to caregivers (Palanhars)** who look after orphan or vulnerable children. Instead of placing children in institutional care (orphanages), the scheme encourages community-based care by supporting relatives or willing families who take responsibility for such children.

### Benefits

| Age Group | Monthly Assistance |
|-----------|-------------------|
| 0-5 years (pre-school) | ₹750 per child |
| 6-18 years (school-going) | ₹1,500 per child |
| Annual clothing allowance | ₹2,000 per child per year |

## Who is Eligible?

### Eligible Children Categories
- **Orphans** (both parents deceased)
- Children of **parents with life imprisonment**
- Children of **widows** with up to 3 children
- Children of **divorced/abandoned women**
- Children of **disabled parents** (40%+ disability)
- Children of **leprosy-affected** parents
- Children of parents with **AIDS**
- Children of **destitute women**
- Children surrendered by parents to the state

### Caregiver (Palanhar) Conditions
- Must be a **relative or willing family** that takes care of the child
- Must be a **resident of Rajasthan**
- Caregiver's annual income must not exceed **₹1.20 lakh**
- Child aged 2-5 must be enrolled in **Anganwadi**
- Child aged 6+ must be enrolled in **school**

## Documents Required

- [Aadhaar card](/guide/aadhaar-card-apply-online) of caregiver and child
- Jan Aadhaar card
- Death certificates of parents (for orphans)
- Court order (for children of parents with life imprisonment)
- Disability certificate of parent (if applicable)
- Widow certificate / divorce decree (if applicable)
- Income certificate of caregiver (annual income ≤ ₹1.20 lakh)
- Anganwadi enrollment proof (for children 2-5 years)
- School enrollment certificate (for children 6+ years)
- Bank passbook of caregiver (Aadhaar-linked)
- Passport-size photographs

## How to Apply — Step-by-Step

### Online Application

1. **Visit** [Rajasthan SSP portal](https://rajssp.rajasthan.gov.in) or [SJE portal](https://sje.rajasthan.gov.in)
2. **Login** using SSO ID or Jan Aadhaar
3. **Select** Palanhar Yojana
4. **Fill** the application with details of child, caregiver, and applicable category
5. **Upload** all required documents
6. **Submit** and note the application number
7. **Track** on the portal

### Through E-Mitra

1. **Visit** nearest E-Mitra kiosk
2. **Carry** all original documents and copies
3. **E-Mitra operator** fills the application
4. **Submit** and collect receipt

### Offline Application

1. **Visit** the District Social Justice Office or Block Development Office
2. **Collect** Palanhar application form
3. **Fill** and attach all documents
4. **Submit** to the Social Welfare Officer
5. **Collect** acknowledgement

## Processing Time

- Verification: **1-2 months**
- Approval: **2-3 months**
- Monthly assistance starts after approval via DBT

## Fees

**No fees** for application.

## Important Tips

1. **Enroll child in Anganwadi/school** — This is mandatory for continued assistance
2. **Keep attendance records** — The child must maintain school attendance for benefits to continue
3. **Renew annually** — Submit annual renewal with updated school enrollment proof
4. **Multiple children eligible** — If caring for multiple orphans, each child is eligible separately
5. **Don't wait** — Apply as soon as the child comes under your care

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Can an orphan child living with grandparents apply?
Yes. Grandparents can be the **Palanhar** (caregiver) and apply for the scheme.

### Q2. Is there a limit on number of children?
There is no strict limit per caregiver, but each child must individually qualify under the eligible categories.

### Q3. What if the child drops out of school?
For children aged 6+, **school enrollment is mandatory**. Dropping out will stop the monthly assistance.

### Q4. Can the child get benefits if parents are alive but disabled?
Yes. Children of parents with **40% or more disability** are eligible under the scheme.

### Q5. Does the scheme cover children up to what age?
The scheme provides support until the child turns **18 years** of age.

### Q6. How can I check payment status?
Visit [jansoochna.rajasthan.gov.in](https://jansoochna.rajasthan.gov.in) or [rajssp.rajasthan.gov.in](https://rajssp.rajasthan.gov.in) and search with your application ID.
