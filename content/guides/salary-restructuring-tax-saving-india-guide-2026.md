# Salary Restructuring to Save Tax India 2026 — Complete Guide

Most employees receive basic + HRA + special allowance. With a few changes to your salary structure (negotiated with HR), you can legally save ₹50,000 to ₹2,00,000+ in tax annually — without spending more money.

---

## Why Salary Structuring Matters

Two employees with the same CTC can have very different tax bills:

| Component | Employee A (Unoptimized) | Employee B (Optimized) |
|-----------|--------------------------|------------------------|
| CTC | ₹20,00,000 | ₹20,00,000 |
| Basic | ₹10,00,000 | ₹6,00,000 |
| HRA | ₹5,00,000 | ₹3,00,000 |
| Special Allowance | ₹5,00,000 | ₹1,00,000 |
| NPS (employer 14%) | — | ₹84,000 |
| Car Lease | — | ₹1,50,000 |
| Food Coupons | — | ₹26,400 |
| Phone/Internet | — | ₹24,000 |
| Fuel/Driver | — | ₹72,000 |
| **Tax (old regime)** | ~₹3,85,000 | ~₹2,90,000 |
| **Saving** | — | **₹95,000/year** |

The CTC is identical. Employee B pays ₹95K less in tax by restructuring allowances.

## Component 1: NPS Employer Contribution — Biggest Single Lever

**Section 80CCD(2)**: Employer's contribution to NPS is tax-free up to 14% of basic salary (for central government employees and private sector employees both, effective Budget 2024).

**This is exempt from tax** — it doesn't go into your 80C limit either. It's completely separate.

**How to request**: Ask HR to convert part of your special allowance into "Employer NPS contribution". The company pays to your NPS Tier 1 account instead of paying you as taxable salary.

**Example**: Basic salary ₹6,00,000/year → 14% = **₹84,000** tax-free employer NPS.
- If you're in 30% bracket: ₹84,000 × 30% = **₹25,200 saved per year**
- You also build a retirement corpus — money is invested in equity/bond mix in NPS

**Catch**: Employer NPS is locked in your NPS account. 60% tax-free at 60 years, 40% mandatory annuity. But for someone building retirement savings, this is a pure win.

## Component 2: Car Lease / Company Car — ₹1–2L Annual Saving

If your company offers a car lease policy (or can be asked to), your vehicle becomes a business expense — reducing taxable salary significantly.

**How it works**:
1. Company leases a car (e.g., ₹15,000/month EMI) on your behalf
2. This ₹1,80,000/year is NOT part of your taxable salary (it's company's expense)
3. IT perquisite rules apply: taxable value is only **1.8%–2.4% of car's actual cost** (for company-owned cars) per year — much less than the lease amount

**Perquisite calculation for company-provided car (with driver)**:
- Car ≤1600cc: ₹3,600/month perquisite
- Car >1600cc: ₹5,400/month perquisite
- Add ₹900/month if driver provided

**Real saving**: On a ₹15K/month lease, you pay perquisite tax on ~₹6K/month instead of ₹15K/month. At 30% bracket, saving: (15,000 − 6,300) × 30% × 12 = **₹31,320/year** in perquisite tax + you're not paying for the lease from your post-tax salary.

**Car lease CTC restructuring saving**: If the ₹1,80,000 lease came from special allowance (fully taxable), restructuring saves: ₹1,80,000 − perquisite = net effective untaxed benefit.

Many large companies (Infosys, TCS, Wipro, MNCs) have flexi benefit plans that include car lease. Ask HR about "Flexible Benefit Plan" or "Car Lease Policy".

## Component 3: Meal Coupons (Sodexo / Edenred) — ₹26,400 Tax-Free

Under Rule 3(7)(iii) of Income Tax Rules, **meal vouchers** given by employers are exempt up to **₹50 per meal for 2 meals per working day**.

**Calculation**: ₹50 × 2 meals × 22 working days × 12 months = **₹26,400 per year** tax-free.

These are physical or digital meal coupons (Sodexo Multi Pass, Edenred Ticket Restaurant) accepted at restaurants, supermarkets, and food delivery apps.

**At 30% bracket**: ₹26,400 × 30% = **₹7,920/year saved**.

**How to request**: Ask HR to add "Food Allowance / Meal Coupons" under your CTC. They deduct equivalent amount from special allowance and give you Sodexo/Edenred coupons. Zero cost to company.

## Component 4: Phone and Internet Reimbursement

Monthly phone and internet bills reimbursed by employer are **exempt from tax** (no specific limit, but typically ₹500–₹1,200/month for phone and ₹500–₹1,000/month for internet based on actuals).

**Standard claim**: ₹2,000/month total = **₹24,000/year** tax-free.

**How it works**: Submit phone/internet bills monthly to HR. Company reimburses. This amount is excluded from taxable salary perquisites.

**At 30% bracket**: ₹24,000 × 30% = **₹7,200/year saved**.

## Component 5: Leave Travel Allowance (LTA)

LTA is exempt for actual travel costs (domestic transport only — no hotel/food) for 2 trips in a 4-year block.

**Current block**: 2022–2025. Two claims allowed per block.

**Standard LTA amount**: Varies — whatever your CTC has as LTA component. Common range: ₹30,000–₹1,00,000/year.

**At 30% bracket on ₹60,000 LTA**: ₹60,000 × 30% = **₹18,000/year saved** (average over 4-year block).

**Documents needed**: Travel tickets (flight/train — own car doesn't count). Book journeys through official channels to have documentary proof.

**LTA cannot be claimed in new tax regime** — old regime only.

## Component 6: Fuel and Driver Allowance

If you own a car and use it for business/work commuting:

**Tax-free reimbursements** (with maintained logbook):
- Petrol/diesel bills submitted monthly
- Typically ₹3,000–₹6,000/month reimbursed tax-free

Some companies offer **fuel card allowances** — ₹2,500–₹5,000/month in CTC as fuel reimbursement. Submit petrol bills; company reimburses tax-free.

---

## Component 7: Books and Periodicals

Reimbursement for professional books, journals, subscriptions — exempt under Rule 3(7)(ix). Typically ₹1,000–₹2,000/month.

## New Tax Regime vs Old Regime for These Benefits

Most allowances (HRA, LTA, meal coupons, phone/internet) are **NOT available in the new tax regime**.

**Exceptions that work in new regime**:
- NPS employer contribution (80CCD(2)) — **available in new regime** ✓
- Standard deduction of ₹75,000 — available in new regime ✓

If your total allowance exemptions (HRA + LTA + meal coupons + etc.) exceed ₹4–5L/year, old regime is likely better. Run both calculations.

**Rule**: New regime is default. If your allowances + deductions under old regime save you more tax than the new regime's lower rates, explicitly opt for old regime in your ITR.

## Step-by-Step: How to Request Salary Restructuring

1. **Write to HR** (email is best — creates a paper trail): "Please help me restructure my CTC to optimize tax under flexi benefit plan"
2. **Mention specific components**: "I'd like to add Employer NPS contribution (up to 14% of basic), meal coupons, and phone/internet reimbursement"
3. **Confirm no change in CTC**: The restructuring reduces taxable cash payout and adds non-taxable benefits; total CTC remains same
4. **Get written confirmation** of new salary structure with revised offer letter or increment letter
5. **Submit bills monthly** for reimbursable components (phone, internet, fuel)

**When to do this**: Best done during appraisal/increment cycle. Can also be done mid-year at many companies.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does salary restructuring affect my take-home negatively?

Not if done correctly. Restructuring converts taxable special allowance into non-taxable components — your take-home either stays the same or increases (because you pay less tax). The net cash in hand goes up.

### Does my company have to offer salary restructuring?

Many do, especially mid-size and large companies. If your company has a "Flexi Benefit Plan" or "Cafeteria Plan", restructuring is built-in. Smaller companies may not have formal policies — you can still negotiate some components informally.

### Can I restructure salary in the middle of the financial year?

Yes, but it only applies from the date of change. The tax benefit is prorated for the remaining months of the financial year. April is the best time to restructure.

### Does NPS employer contribution reduce my monthly take-home?

Yes, by the employer NPS amount. But you also pay less TDS — so the net take-home impact depends on your tax bracket. At 30% bracket: ₹7,000 employer NPS reduces take-home by ₹7,000 but reduces tax by ₹2,100 monthly. Net reduction in take-home is ₹4,900 — but you've invested ₹7,000 in NPS retirement corpus.

### Meal coupons — can I use them for groceries and e-commerce?

Yes — Sodexo Multi Pass and Edenred Ticket Restaurant are accepted at supermarkets (BigBazaar, Reliance Fresh, etc.) and online at Swiggy, Zomato, BigBasket in some cities. This makes them practically equivalent to cash for food purchases.
