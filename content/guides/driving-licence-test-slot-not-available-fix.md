## What Is the DL Test Slot Booking Issue?

When applying for a driving licence (DL) in India, you must book a test slot on the **Sarathi Parivahan portal** for your driving test at the RTO. Many applicants face the frustrating "**No slots available**" message when trying to book their DL test appointment. This happens due to high demand, limited daily slots, and server issues on the Sarathi portal.

This guide covers proven tips to fix DL test slot booking issues and secure your appointment faster.

## Why Is the DL Test Slot Not Available?

There are several reasons why you may not see available slots:

1. **High demand at your RTO** — Popular RTOs in metro cities have thousands of applicants competing for limited daily slots
2. **Slots released in batches** — RTOs release slots periodically, not all at once
3. **Holiday or weekend closure** — RTOs don't conduct tests on gazetted holidays and some Saturdays
4. **Technical issues on Sarathi portal** — Server overload, especially during peak hours
5. **Your application status** — Incomplete applications or pending fees can block slot booking
6. **RTO-specific scheduling** — Some RTOs only conduct DL tests on specific days of the week

## How to Fix DL Test Slot Not Available — Step by Step

### Method 1: Try Early Morning (Best Tip)

1. Open the Sarathi portal at **sarathi.parivahan.gov.in** between **6:00 AM and 8:00 AM**
2. Log in with your application number and date of birth
3. Navigate to **Appointment Booking** → **DL Test Slot**
4. Select your RTO and preferred date
5. Slots are most likely to be available early morning when the system refreshes

> **Pro tip:** Many RTOs release new slots around **midnight (12:00 AM)** or **early morning (6:00 AM)**. Set an alarm and try immediately.

### Method 2: Check Different Dates

1. Don't check just one date — browse through the **next 15-30 days**
2. Avoid Mondays (usually busiest) and try mid-week dates (Tuesday–Thursday)
3. Check if your RTO has specific DL test days (some RTOs only conduct tests 2-3 days a week)

### Method 3: Try a Different RTO

1. On the Sarathi portal, you can select a **different RTO** within your state
2. Smaller or suburban RTOs often have more available slots
3. Go to **Appointment Booking** → change the RTO location
4. You can take the driving test at any RTO in your state — it doesn't have to be where you applied for the learner's licence

### Method 4: Check the Holiday Calendar

1. Visit your state transport department website for the **RTO holiday calendar**
2. RTOs follow central government gazetted holidays plus state-specific holidays
3. Slots won't be available on holidays — plan around them
4. Check if your RTO closes on 2nd/4th Saturdays

### Method 5: Understand the Slot Release Pattern

1. Most RTOs release slots **1-2 weeks in advance**
2. Some RTOs release the next week's slots on **Friday evening or Saturday**
3. Check the portal consistently at the same time each day to identify the release pattern for your specific RTO
4. If you see a slot, **book immediately** — don't wait, as slots fill within minutes

### Method 6: Clear Browser Cache and Retry

1. Clear your browser cache and cookies
2. Use **Google Chrome** or **Mozilla Firefox** (avoid older browsers)
3. Disable any ad blockers or VPN
4. Try in **incognito/private browsing** mode
5. If the portal shows errors, wait 10-15 minutes and retry

## What to Do If You Still Can't Get a Slot

1. **Visit the RTO in person** — Go to your RTO office and ask about DL test slot availability. Some RTOs allow walk-in test bookings
2. **Contact the RTO helpdesk** — Call your RTO's contact number (available on the state transport website)
3. **Use the Parivahan Helpline** — Call **1800-120-4199** (toll-free) for assistance
4. **File a grievance** — Submit a complaint on [pgportal.gov.in](https://pgportal.gov.in/) if the issue persists for weeks

## Important Tips

1. **Book early morning** — 6 AM to 8 AM gives the best chance of finding available slots
2. **Be flexible with RTO** — Suburban RTOs usually have shorter waiting times
3. **Don't use third-party agents** — Book directly on sarathi.parivahan.gov.in to avoid fraud
4. **Keep your learner's licence valid** — You must book the DL test before your LL expires (typically 6 months)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. How often are DL test slots released on the Sarathi portal?
Most RTOs release new slots weekly, typically on Friday evenings or early Monday mornings. The exact pattern varies by RTO. Check consistently at the same time each day to identify your RTO's release schedule.

### Q2. Can I book a DL test slot at a different RTO?
Yes, you can book your driving test at any RTO within your state. You are not restricted to the RTO where you obtained your learner's licence. Smaller RTOs often have more availability.

### Q3. What is the best time to check for DL test slots?
Try between 6:00 AM and 8:00 AM or around midnight (12:00 AM). These are the times when the portal is least congested and new slots are most likely to appear.

### Q4. My DL test slot was booked but I missed it. Can I rebook?
Yes, you can rebook a missed slot on the Sarathi portal. Log in, go to your application, and book a new appointment. There may be a rescheduling fee depending on your state.

### Q5. How far in advance can I book a DL test slot?
Most RTOs allow booking 7-15 days in advance. Some RTOs may allow up to 30 days advance booking. The range depends on your specific RTO's configuration.

### Q6. Is there a fee for booking a DL test slot?
The DL test fee is typically ₹200-₹300 (varies by state) and is usually paid during the initial DL application. No separate slot booking fee is charged on the Sarathi portal.

### Q7. What if the Sarathi portal is not working at all?
If the portal is completely down, wait a few hours and retry. For persistent issues, contact the Parivahan helpline at 1800-120-4199 or visit your RTO in person to book a slot offline.
