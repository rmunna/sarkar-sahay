# Telangana Caste Certificate Apply Online 2026

The **caste certificate** in Telangana is issued by the Revenue Department and is required for fee reimbursement for BC/SC/ST students, BC/OBC Non-Creamy Layer certification, scholarship applications, and government job reservation benefits. Applications go through **MeeSeva** — Telangana's multi-service platform.

> **Quick summary:** Apply at any MeeSeva centre or [ts.meeseva.gov.in](https://ts.meeseva.gov.in). Issued by MRO (Mandal Revenue Officer) within **7–21 days**. Fee: approximately ₹35.

---

## Caste Categories in Telangana

| Category | Details |
|----------|---------|
| **SC** | Scheduled Castes — 15% reservation |
| **ST** | Scheduled Tribes — 6% reservation |
| **BC-A** | Backward Classes A (Aboriginal/Tribal) — 7% |
| **BC-B** | Backward Classes B (Other Hindu communities) — 10% |
| **BC-C** | Backward Classes C (Scheduled Castes converts) — 1% |
| **BC-D** | Backward Classes D (Muslim OBC communities) — 4% |
| **BC-E** | Backward Classes E (Economically Backward Brahmin families) — 1% |
| **EWS** | Economically Weaker Section — 10% (General category) |

## Why You Need Telangana Caste Certificate

- **BC/SC/ST Fee Reimbursement** — Telangana government engineering/medical/PG colleges
- **Post-Matric Scholarship** (BC/SC/ST) — mandatory caste proof
- **BC Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) Certificate** — for central OBC reservations
- **Government job reservation** — state and central level
- **Kalyanalakshmi / Shaadi Mubarak** scheme — caste eligibility check

---

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory)
- **Ration card** (if available — shows caste category)
- **Father's school certificate** or old caste document showing community
- **Voter ID** (additional address proof)
- Self-declaration of caste (MeeSeva form or affidavit)
- Passport-size photograph

---

## How to Apply via MeeSeva

**Method 1: MeeSeva Kiosk (Recommended)**

1. Visit nearest **MeeSeva centre** or kiosk
2. Ask for **"Caste/Community Certificate"** (BC-A / BC-B / SC / ST as applicable)
3. Provide Aadhaar, ration card, father's caste document
4. Operator submits online application
5. Fee: approximately **₹35**
6. Receive acknowledgement

**Method 2: MeeSeva Online Portal**

1. Go to [ts.meeseva.gov.in](https://ts.meeseva.gov.in)
2. Log in / register
3. Search **"Caste Certificate"**
4. Fill form, upload documents, submit

**Method 3: Ward/Village Secretariat**

AP's Ward Volunteers / Village Secretariat assistants can help with caste certificate applications — visit your Ward Secretariat in Hyderabad/GHMC or Gram Panchayat in rural areas.

## BC Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) Certificate — Separate Process

For **central government OBC reservation**, you need a **BC NCL Certificate** (not just caste certificate):
1. Apply separately at MeeSeva for "OBC Non-Creamy Layer Certificate"
2. Requires **income certificate** (income < ₹8 lakh/year)
3. Issued by Tahsildar
4. Valid for **3 years**

## Status Check & Download

1. Log in to [ts.meeseva.gov.in](https://ts.meeseva.gov.in)
2. Click **"My Applications"** → view caste certificate status
3. Once approved: download digitally signed PDF certificate

## Frequently Asked Questions

### BC Certificate vs OBC Certificate in Telangana — same?

In Telangana, **BC (Backward Class)** is the state-specific term. For state government purposes, use the TS BC certificate. For **central government jobs** (UPSC, SSC, railways), you need the central OBC NCL certificate — which also requires applying at MeeSeva but under "OBC NCL."

### I am from BC-B category. Do I need NCL for Telangana state jobs?

For **Telangana state government jobs**, the Non-Creamy Layer condition applies to BC categories — income limit is ₹8 lakh/year. Check the specific job advertisement for exact requirements.
