# VAHAN Parivahan: Vehicle Registration & RC Services Online

VAHAN is the official national portal by the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH) for all vehicle registration and related services in India. Through **vahan.parivahan.gov.in**, you can register a new vehicle, check RC details, pay road tax, apply for fitness certificates, transfer vehicle ownership, and much more.

This guide covers all services on the VAHAN portal with step-by-step processes.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent platform and is not affiliated with MoRTH, NIC, or any government body.

## What is VAHAN Parivahan?

VAHAN is the computerised vehicle registration system used by RTOs across India. It maintains the national register of all motor vehicles and provides online services including:

- New vehicle registration
- RC (Registration Certificate) status check
- Vehicle owner details lookup
- Road tax payment
- Fitness certificate application
- Transfer of ownership
- Hypothecation addition/termination
- NOC for interstate transfer
- Fancy/choice number booking

## How to Check Vehicle RC Details Online

1. Visit [vahan.parivahan.gov.in](https://vahan.parivahan.gov.in/)
2. Click **"Know Your Vehicle Details"** (or visit parivahan.gov.in > RC Status)
3. Enter the **vehicle registration number** (e.g., DL01AB1234)
4. Enter the captcha code
5. Click **"Search Vehicle"**
6. View details: owner name, registration date, vehicle class, fuel type, insurance validity, fitness validity, etc.

> **Note:** Some personal details may be masked for privacy. Full details are available to the registered owner through login.

## Documents Required for Vehicle Registration

### New Vehicle Registration
- **Invoice/Sale Certificate** from the dealer
- **Insurance Certificate** (valid third-party or comprehensive)
- **Form 20** (Application for registration)
- **Form 21** (Sale Certificate from manufacturer)
- **Form 22** (Roadworthiness Certificate from manufacturer)
- **Identity Proof:** Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, or Voter ID
- **Address Proof:** Aadhaar, Utility bill, Passport
- **PUC (Pollution Under Control) Certificate**
- **Road Tax Payment Receipt** (if applicable)

### For Transfer of Ownership
- **Form 29** (Notice of Transfer) — signed by both buyer and seller
- **Form 30** (Report of Transfer) — signed by both parties
- Valid insurance in buyer's name
- Identity and address proof of buyer
- Original RC
- NOC from financer (if vehicle is under loan)

## Step-by-Step: New Vehicle Registration

1. The dealer typically handles Form 20, 21, and 22
2. Visit [vahan.parivahan.gov.in](https://vahan.parivahan.gov.in/)
3. Select your **State** and **RTO**
4. Click **"New Registration"**
5. Enter vehicle details (chassis number, engine number, etc.)
6. Upload documents
7. Pay **road tax and registration fee** online
8. Book an RTO appointment for inspection (if required)
9. Visit the RTO with original documents and vehicle
10. RC is generated and dispatched (or collect from RTO)

## Step-by-Step: Pay Road Tax Online

1. Visit [vahan.parivahan.gov.in](https://vahan.parivahan.gov.in/)
2. Click **"Pay Your Tax"** or **"Tax Payment"**
3. Enter your vehicle registration number
4. The system shows pending tax amount
5. Verify vehicle details
6. Pay via net banking, debit/credit card, or UPI
7. Download the tax payment receipt

## Step-by-Step: Apply for Fitness Certificate

1. Visit the VAHAN portal
2. Select State and RTO
3. Click **"Fitness Certificate"**
4. Enter your vehicle registration number
5. Fill in the application form
6. Upload required documents (insurance, PUC)
7. Pay the fitness test fee online
8. Book an appointment for vehicle inspection at RTO
9. Get your vehicle inspected
10. Fitness certificate is issued upon passing inspection

## Step-by-Step: Transfer of Ownership

1. Visit [vahan.parivahan.gov.in](https://vahan.parivahan.gov.in/)
2. Click **"Transfer of Ownership"**
3. Enter the vehicle registration number
4. Fill in buyer and seller details
5. Upload Form 29, Form 30, insurance, and ID proofs
6. Pay the transfer fee online
7. Both parties may need to visit the RTO for verification
8. New RC in buyer's name is issued after processing

## Other VAHAN Services

- **Hypothecation Addition/Termination:** Add or remove financer details from RC when loan is taken or cleared
- **NOC (No Objection Certificate):** Required when transferring vehicle registration to another state
- **Duplicate RC:** Apply if your RC is lost, damaged, or stolen
- **Fancy/Choice Number:** Book a preferred registration number during new registration
- **RC Cancellation:** Cancel registration for scrapped or exported vehicles
- **Address Change on RC:** Update address when you move within the same RTO jurisdiction

## Important Tips

1. **Always check RC status before buying a used vehicle** — verify ownership, insurance, and fitness validity
2. **Keep your road tax paid on time** — late payment attracts penalties
3. **Renew fitness certificate before expiry** — especially for commercial vehicles, as expired fitness means the vehicle cannot ply legally
4. **Complete ownership transfer within 14 days** of sale — it's a legal requirement
5. **Use the mParivahan app** to carry a digital copy of your RC on your phone

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: How can I check vehicle owner details by registration number?
Visit [vahan.parivahan.gov.in](https://vahan.parivahan.gov.in/) and use the "Know Your Vehicle Details" option. Enter the registration number to see owner name and vehicle details. Some details may be masked for privacy.

### Q2: How do I pay road tax online?
Go to the VAHAN portal, click "Pay Your Tax," enter your registration number, verify the amount, and pay via net banking, card, or UPI.

### Q3: Can I transfer vehicle ownership online without visiting the RTO?
The application and fee payment can be done online, but both buyer and seller typically need to visit the RTO for identity verification and document submission.

### Q4: What is a fitness certificate and who needs it?
A fitness certificate certifies that a vehicle is roadworthy. It is mandatory for all transport/commercial vehicles and for private vehicles older than 15 years.

### Q5: How do I get a duplicate RC if mine is lost?
Apply for a duplicate RC on the VAHAN portal by selecting "Duplicate RC," filing an FIR (if stolen), uploading documents, and paying the fee. You'll need to visit the RTO for verification.

### Q6: Can I book a fancy registration number online?
Yes, during new vehicle registration on the VAHAN portal, you can select a fancy/choice number from available options. Charges vary by state and number.

### Q7: What is the validity of a vehicle registration in India?
For private vehicles, registration is valid for 15 years. For commercial vehicles, it is valid for 5 years (renewable). After expiry, re-registration or green tax may apply.
