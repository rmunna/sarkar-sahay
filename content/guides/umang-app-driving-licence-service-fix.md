# UMANG Driving Licence Service Not Working? Fix DL & RC Errors

The UMANG app integrates with **Parivahan Sewa** to provide driving licence (DL) and vehicle registration certificate (RC) services. If you're facing errors fetching DL details, RC information, or linking with DigiLocker, this guide will help.

## Common UMANG DL & RC Service Errors

1. **DL not found** or "No records available"
2. **RC details showing wrong information**
3. **Parivahan sync error** or service timeout
4. **DigiLocker DL linking** not working
5. **Application status not updating**

---

## Fix 1: Driving Licence Not Found on UMANG

If UMANG shows "DL not found" or no records:

1. **Verify your DL number** — Enter the complete DL number including state code (e.g., KA01-20200012345)
2. **Enter Date of Birth correctly** — DL search requires your DOB as registered with the RTO
3. **Check on Parivahan portal directly** — Visit [parivahan.gov.in](https://parivahan.gov.in/) → "Know Your DL" to verify your DL exists in the system
4. **Old DL format issue** — Very old driving licences issued before digitization may not be available online. Visit your RTO to get it updated
5. **State RTO delay** — Some states take longer to update their DL records in the central Parivahan database
6. **DL may have expired** — Expired DLs may show differently. Check your DL validity dates

> **Tip:** If your DL was recently issued or renewed, allow 7-15 days for it to appear in the Parivahan database.

---

## Fix 2: RC Details Showing Wrong Information

If vehicle RC details on UMANG are incorrect:

1. **Verify on Parivahan** — Check [parivahan.gov.in](https://parivahan.gov.in/) → "Know Your Vehicle Details" to see what's in the official database
2. **Data entry error at RTO** — If details were entered incorrectly at the RTO, you'll need to visit the RTO with supporting documents to get them corrected
3. **Ownership transfer not updated** — If you recently purchased the vehicle, the transfer may not be reflected yet. Allow 15-30 days after transfer
4. **Insurance or fitness details outdated** — These are updated by the insurance company and RTO respectively
5. **File correction application** — Visit [sarathi.parivahan.gov.in](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/) to file a correction request online (available in some states)

---

## Fix 3: Parivahan Sync Error or Timeout

If UMANG shows sync errors or timeout for Parivahan services:

1. **Parivahan servers may be down** — Visit [parivahan.gov.in](https://parivahan.gov.in/) to check if the portal is working
2. **Try during off-peak hours** — RTO-related services are busiest between 10 AM and 3 PM
3. **Update the UMANG app** — Ensure you have the latest version installed
4. **Clear app cache** — Settings → Apps → UMANG → Storage → Clear Cache
5. **Use UMANG web version** — Try [web.umang.gov.in](https://web.umang.gov.in/) as an alternative
6. **State-specific outages** — Some state RTOs have independent server infrastructure that may be down independently

---

## Fix 4: DigiLocker DL Linking Not Working

If you cannot link your driving licence to DigiLocker through UMANG:

1. **Link directly on DigiLocker** — Visit [digilocker.gov.in](https://www.digilocker.gov.in/) or use the DigiLocker app
2. **Ensure Aadhaar is linked to DigiLocker** — DigiLocker requires Aadhaar verification
3. **Search for "Driving Licence" issuer** — In DigiLocker, go to "Issued Documents" and search for your state's transport department
4. **Enter correct DL number and DOB** — Details must match exactly what's in the Parivahan database
5. **DL must exist in Parivahan database** — If your DL is not on Parivahan, it won't be available on DigiLocker either
6. **Try after DL database update** — Some states update DigiLocker data periodically (weekly or monthly)

> See our [DigiLocker guide](/guide/digilocker-nac-certificate-download) for detailed instructions on setting up and using DigiLocker.

## How to Check DL Details on UMANG (Step-by-Step)

1. Open the **UMANG** app and log in
2. Search for **"Parivahan"** in the search bar
3. Select **"Know Your DL"** or **"Know Your RC"**
4. Enter your **DL number** or **vehicle registration number**
5. Enter your **Date of Birth** (for DL) or other required details
6. Tap **"Search"**
7. Your DL or RC details will be displayed

---

## Important Tips

1. Keep your DL and RC documents updated at your RTO — outdated records cause most UMANG errors
2. Use DigiLocker to carry a digital copy of your DL — it is legally valid under IT Act 2000
3. For DL renewal, apply online at [sarathi.parivahan.gov.in](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/) at least 30 days before expiry
4. If your DL details are wrong, file a correction at the RTO with original supporting documents
5. Contact your state RTO helpline for state-specific DL and RC issues

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Can I apply for a driving licence through UMANG?
No, DL applications must be submitted through the [Sarathi Parivahan portal](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/). UMANG only provides DL and RC search and status services.

### Q2: Why does UMANG show my DL as expired?
If your DL validity period has ended, it will show as expired. You can renew it online through [sarathi.parivahan.gov.in](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/) or visit your RTO.

### Q3: Is the DL shown on UMANG legally valid as a document?
The DL information displayed on UMANG is for reference only. For a legally valid digital DL, use DigiLocker or mParivahan app.

### Q4: How do I check vehicle RC details on UMANG?
Search for "Parivahan" on UMANG, select "Know Your Vehicle Details," and enter your vehicle registration number to see RC details.

### Q5: My DL was issued recently but doesn't show on UMANG. Why?
Newly issued DLs may take 7-15 days to appear in the central Parivahan database. Some states may take longer.

### Q6: Can I download my DL from UMANG?
UMANG displays DL details but doesn't provide a downloadable document. Use [DigiLocker](https://www.digilocker.gov.in/) to get a digital copy of your DL.

### Q7: What is the helpline for Parivahan/DL related issues?
Contact the Parivahan helpdesk at 0120-2459169 or email at helpdesk-vahan@gov.in. For UMANG issues, call 1800-115-565.
