# Ladli Bahna Yojana MP — How to Apply, Eligibility & Status Check (2026)

**Mukhyamantri Ladli Bahna Yojana (मुख्यमंत्री लाड़ली बहना योजना)** is Madhya Pradesh government's flagship women empowerment scheme that provides **₹1,250 per month (₹15,000 per year)** directly into the bank accounts of eligible women. Launched on **5 March 2023** by then CM Shivraj Singh Chouhan, it is one of India's largest direct benefit transfer schemes for women.

The scheme aims to improve the financial independence of women from lower and middle-income families across Madhya Pradesh.

---

## What is Ladli Bahna Yojana?

**Key highlights:**

- **Monthly benefit:** ₹1,250 transferred via DBT to the beneficiary's bank account
- **Annual benefit:** ₹15,000 per year
- **Payment date:** 10th of every month
- **Target:** Women aged 21–60 from economically weaker families in MP
- **Beneficiaries:** Over 1.29 crore women enrolled since launch
- **Funding:** Fully funded by the Madhya Pradesh state government

> The amount was initially ₹1,000/month at launch and was increased to ₹1,250/month from October 2023.

---

## Eligibility Criteria (कौन आवेदन कर सकती हैं?)

| Requirement | Details |
|---|---|
| **Domicile** | Must be a resident of Madhya Pradesh |
| **Gender** | Women only (married, widowed, divorced, or deserted) |
| **Age** | 21 to 60 years (as on 1 January of the application year) |
| **Family income** | Annual family income must be ≤ ₹2.5 lakh |
| **Land holding** | Family must not own more than 5 acres of agricultural land |
| **Tax status** | Applicant or spouse must not be an income tax payer |
| **Government job** | Applicant or spouse must not be a current/retired government employee |
| **Existing pension** | Must not be receiving pension of ₹1,000/month or more from any other scheme |

### Who is NOT Eligible?

- Women whose family income exceeds ₹2.5 lakh per year
- Women who are income tax payers or whose spouse pays income tax
- Women who hold government jobs or whose spouse holds a government job
- Women who are already receiving pension of ₹1,000+ per month from another government scheme
- Women who own more than 5 acres of farmland

## Documents Required (ज़रूरी दस्तावेज़)

Keep these documents ready before applying:

1. **Aadhaar Card** — linked to your mobile number
2. **Samagra ID** (समग्र आईडी) — this is mandatory; get it from [samagra.gov.in](https://samagra.gov.in)
3. **Bank account passbook** — account must be linked to Aadhaar (DBT-enabled)
4. **Passport-size photograph** — recent
5. **Mobile number** — for OTP verification
6. **Ration card** (if available)
7. **Age proof** — birth certificate, school leaving certificate, or Aadhaar

> **Important:** Your Samagra ID must have **eKYC completed** and your Aadhaar must be linked to your bank account for DBT transfers.

## How to Apply for Ladli Bahna Yojana

There are two ways to apply:

### Method 1: Apply at Government Camp (शिविर)

This is the most common method. The MP government regularly organises camps at gram panchayat and ward levels.

1. **Visit the nearest camp** — check with your local gram panchayat, ward office, or Anganwadi centre for camp dates
2. **Carry all documents** — Aadhaar, Samagra ID, bank passbook, photo
3. **Fill the application form** — camp officials will help you fill the form
4. **Biometric verification** — your fingerprints will be scanned
5. **Get acknowledgement receipt** — keep this safe for future reference
6. **Application is submitted** — you will receive an SMS confirmation

### Method 2: Apply Online via Portal

1. Go to the official portal: **[cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in](https://cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in)**
2. Click on **"आवेदन करें"** (Apply Now)
3. Enter your **Samagra ID** and verify with OTP
4. Fill in your personal details — name, age, address, bank account
5. Upload your **photograph**
6. Verify via **Aadhaar eKYC** (OTP or biometric)
7. Submit the form and note down your **application number**

> **Tip:** If you face issues with the online portal, visiting a camp is the easiest and most reliable option. Camp volunteers assist with the entire process.

## How to Check Ladli Bahna Yojana Status (स्टेटस कैसे चेक करें?)

### Check Application Status

1. Visit **[cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in](https://cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in)**
2. Click on **"आवेदन की स्थिति"** (Application Status)
3. Enter your **Samagra ID** or **Application Number**
4. Enter the captcha and click **Search**
5. Your application status will be displayed — Approved, Pending, or Rejected

### Check Payment Status

1. Visit **[cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in](https://cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in)**
2. Click on **"भुगतान की स्थिति"** (Payment Status)
3. Enter your **Samagra ID**
4. View the month-wise payment history and transaction details

### Check via Samagra Portal

1. Visit **[shfrms.mp.gov.in](https://shfrms.mp.gov.in)**
2. Enter your Samagra ID
3. Check your scheme enrollment and payment status

## Payment Schedule (भुगतान कब आता है?)

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| **Payment date** | 10th of every month |
| **Amount** | ₹1,250 per month |
| **Mode** | Direct Bank Transfer (DBT) via Aadhaar-linked bank account |
| **Annual total** | ₹15,000 |

**Payment history (amount per month):**

- **March–September 2023:** ₹1,000/month
- **October 2023 onwards:** ₹1,250/month

> If the 10th falls on a holiday, the payment is usually credited on the next working day.

## Important Tips (ध्यान रखने योग्य बातें)

1. **Complete eKYC** — without Aadhaar eKYC linked to your Samagra ID, your application will be rejected
2. **Link Aadhaar to bank account** — DBT transfers require Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts
3. **Keep Samagra ID updated** — ensure your name, age, and family details are correct on Samagra portal
4. **One application per woman** — duplicate applications will be rejected
5. **Update bank details** — if you change your bank account, update it on the Samagra portal immediately
6. **Check payment regularly** — if a month's payment is missed, report to your gram panchayat or ward office
7. **Beware of fraud** — the government does NOT charge any fee for this scheme; do not pay anyone

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. How much money do I get under Ladli Bahna Yojana?

You receive **₹1,250 per month (₹15,000 per year)** directly in your bank account.

### 2. Can unmarried women apply for Ladli Bahna Yojana?

No. The scheme is for **married, widowed, divorced, or deserted women** only. Unmarried women are not eligible.

### 3. What is the age limit for Ladli Bahna Yojana?

Women aged **21 to 60 years** can apply. Age is calculated as on 1 January of the application year.

### 4. Is Samagra ID mandatory?

Yes. **Samagra ID is compulsory** for applying. If you don't have one, register at [samagra.gov.in](https://samagra.gov.in) first.

### 5. What if my payment is not received on the 10th?

Wait 2-3 working days. If still not received, check your status on the portal. If the issue persists, contact your **gram panchayat** or call the helpline at **0755-2700800**.

### 6. Is there any fee to apply?

**No.** The application is completely free. Do not pay any money to agents or middlemen.

### 7. Can women from other states apply?

No. Only **Madhya Pradesh domicile** women are eligible.

### 8. What happens if I turn 60 while receiving benefits?

Your benefits will **stop** once you cross the age of 60. You may then be eligible for other pension schemes like [Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension](/guides/old-age-pension-scheme).
