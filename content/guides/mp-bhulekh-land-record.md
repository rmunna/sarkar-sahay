# MP Bhulekh: Check Land Record Khasra Khatauni Online

**MP Bhulekh** is the official land records portal of the Madhya Pradesh government. Through [mpbhulekh.gov.in](https://mpbhulekh.gov.in/), you can check Khasra details, view Khatauni (B1) records, download free nakal (certified copy), and access plot maps (Bhu Naksha). This guide covers the complete process.

> **Disclaimer:** CitizenNest is an independent informational platform and is not affiliated with any government department. Always verify details on official government portals.

## Key Terms in MP Land Records

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Khasra** | Plot-level record with area, crop, soil type, and ownership details |
| **Khatauni (B1)** | Ownership summary linking Khasra plots to an owner |
| **Nakal** | Certified copy of land record |
| **Bhu Naksha** | Plot map showing boundaries and survey numbers |
| **Fasli Year** | Revenue year used in MP land records |
| **ROR** | Record of Rights — comprehensive ownership document |

## How to Check Khasra Khatauni Online on MP Bhulekh

### Step-by-Step Process

1. Visit [mpbhulekh.gov.in](https://mpbhulekh.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"Free Services"** or **"भू-अभिलेख"** (Land Records)
3. Click **"खसरा / B1 / नक्शा"** (Khasra / B1 / Map)
4. Select your **जिला (District)** from the dropdown
5. Select **तहसील (Tehsil)**
6. Select **गांव (Village)**
7. Choose your search method:
   - **खसरा संख्या** — By Khasra (Plot) Number
   - **भूस्वामी** — By Landowner Name
   - **खाता संख्या** — By Khata Number
8. Enter the relevant details
9. Enter the **captcha code**
10. Click **"विवरण देखें"** (View Details)
11. The Khasra and B1 record will display on screen

## How to Download Free Nakal (Certified Copy)

1. Go to [mpbhulekh.gov.in](https://mpbhulekh.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"Free Services"** → **"भू-अभिलेख प्रतिलिपि"** (Land Record Copy)
3. You can also visit [mpbhulekh.gov.in/viewFreeROR.do](https://mpbhulekh.gov.in/viewFreeROR.do) directly
4. Select District → Tehsil → Village
5. Enter Khasra number or landowner name
6. Complete the captcha
7. Click **"देखें"** (View) to see the record
8. Click **"प्रिंट"** or use your browser's print option to save as PDF

> **Note:** The free nakal from MP Bhulekh is for informational purposes. For a legally certified copy, visit your local Tehsil office.

## How to View Plot Map (Bhu Naksha)

1. Visit [mpbhulekh.gov.in](https://mpbhulekh.gov.in/)
2. Click on **"भू-नक्शा"** (Bhu Naksha) or go to [mpbhulekh.gov.in/viewBhunaksha.do](https://mpbhulekh.gov.in/viewBhunaksha.do)
3. Select **District → Tehsil → RI Halka → Village**
4. The village map will load
5. Click on any plot to see its Khasra number, area, and owner name
6. Use **"प्रिंट"** to save or print the map

## How to Search by District

MP Bhulekh covers all **52 districts** of Madhya Pradesh. Popular district searches include:

- **Bhopal** — Capital city, high demand for urban land records
- **Indore** — Largest city, frequent property transactions
- **Jabalpur** — Major city in Mahakoshal region
- **Gwalior** — Important for Gwalior-Chambal region land
- **Ujjain** — Historical city with significant agricultural land

To search any district, simply select it from the dropdown on the MP Bhulekh portal.

## Who Can Access MP Bhulekh Records

- Any citizen can view land records online — no login required
- Records are public information under the Right to Information Act
- For certified/legal copies, you may need to prove ownership or interest

## Important Tips

1. **Use the correct Fasli year** — MP Bhulekh shows records by Fasli year. Select the latest available year for current records.
2. **Try multiple search methods** — If Khasra number search doesn't work, try searching by owner name or Khata number.
3. **Check mutation status** — After any property transfer, verify that mutation (naam dakhil kharij) is updated in the records.
4. **Free nakal is informational only** — For legal purposes like bank loans or court cases, get a certified copy from the Tehsil office.
5. **Use Bhu Naksha for boundary disputes** — The plot map helps identify exact boundaries and neighbouring plots.

## FAQs

### What is MP Bhulekh?

MP Bhulekh (mpbhulekh.gov.in) is the official online portal of the Madhya Pradesh government for viewing land records including Khasra, Khatauni B1, and plot maps. It is maintained by the Commissioner Land Records, Madhya Pradesh.

### How can I check my land record in Madhya Pradesh online?

Visit mpbhulekh.gov.in, click on Free Services, select your District, Tehsil, and Village, then search by Khasra number, owner name, or Khata number. The record will display immediately.

### Is MP Bhulekh nakal (copy) free?

Yes, viewing and downloading land records from MP Bhulekh is completely free. However, the online copy is for informational purposes. For a legally certified nakal, you need to apply at the Tehsil office or through MP e-District portal (fee: approximately ₹30).

### How can I see my plot map in MP Bhulekh?

Click on "Bhu Naksha" on the MP Bhulekh portal, select your District, Tehsil, RI Halka, and Village. The village map will load and you can click on any plot to see owner details and boundaries.

### What is the difference between Khasra and Khatauni B1?

Khasra is a plot-level record showing details of a single plot (area, crop, soil type). Khatauni B1 is an ownership record that lists all plots owned by a person along with their total holding.

### Can I check MP land records by owner name?

Yes, MP Bhulekh allows search by landowner name (भूस्वामी). Select your District, Tehsil, and Village, then choose the "भूस्वामी" search option and type the owner's name.

### What should I do if my name is wrong in MP land records?

You need to apply for a correction (naam sudhar) at the Tehsil office with supporting documents like Aadhaar card, property deed, or court order. The Tehsildar will verify and update the record after due process.
