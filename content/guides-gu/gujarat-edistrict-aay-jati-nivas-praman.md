## Online Arji — Digital Gujarat

1. [digitalgujarat.gov.in](https://digitalgujarat.gov.in) kholo
2. **"Citizen Services"** → **"Revenue Certificates"** choose karo
3. Aadhaar / Mobile OTP thi register / login karo
4. Form bharo — naam, address (jilla, taluka, gam), income / caste details
5. Documents upload karo
6. ₹20–30 fee online bharyo
7. **Application Number** note karo

---

## Jaruri Dastavej (Required Documents)

### Income Certificate
- Aadhaar Card
- Ration Card
- Income self-declaration form
- Salary slip / Bank statement (employed)

### Caste Certificate
- Aadhaar Card
- Ration Card
- **Father's school TC** (caste mentioned)
- Caste self-declaration

### Domicile / Residence Certificate
- Aadhaar Card (Gujarat address)
- Voter ID / Ration Card
- Electricity bill / Bank passbook
