What is Caste Certificate?

• Official document proving SC/ST/OBC status

• Required for reservation benefits

• Issued by Revenue/Tehsildar office

• Valid for education, jobs, and schemes

• State government issued document

Who Needs It?

• SC/ST/OBC candidates for govt job reservation

• College admissions under reserved quota

• Government scheme benefits

• Scholarship applications

• Political reservation (for elections)

How to Apply Online

• Visit your state's e-District portal

• Register with mobile number and Aadhaar

• Fill caste certificate application form

• Upload required documents

• Pay processing fee (₹20–100)

Documents Required

• Aadhaar card

• Ration card with caste mentioned

• Father's/family member's caste certificate

• School records mentioning caste

• Residence proof

• Passport size photographs

Verification & Delivery

• Application reviewed by Tehsildar office

• Field inquiry may be conducted

• Processing time: 15–30 days

• Download from e-District portal

• Physical copy available at Tehsildar office

State Portals

• UP: edistrict.up.gov.in

• MP: mpedistrict.gov.in

• Bihar: serviceonline.bihar.gov.in

• Maharashtra: aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in

• Check your state portal for specific process
