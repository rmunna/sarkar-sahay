PM Kisan Samman Nidhi Yojana

Complete guide to PM Kisan scheme - eligibility, registration, and how to get ₹6,000 per year directly in your bank acco

What is PM Kisan?

• ₹6,000 per year to farmer families

• Paid in 3 installments of ₹2,000 each

• Direct bank transfer (DBT)

• Launched in February 2019

Who is Eligible?

• All landholding farmer families

• Must have cultivable land in records

• Not for income tax payers

• Not for government employees

Documents Required

• Aadhaar card (mandatory)

• Bank account with IFSC

• Land ownership records

• Mobile number linked to Aadhaar

How to Register Online

• Visit pmkisan.gov.in

• Click "New Farmer Registration"

• Enter Aadhaar number & state

• Fill form and submit documents

Check Payment Status

• Go to pmkisan.gov.in

• Click "Beneficiary Status"

• Enter Aadhaar or account number

• View installment history
