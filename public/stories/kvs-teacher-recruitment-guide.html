KVS Teacher Recruitment Guide

KVS recruitment for PGT, TGT, PRT, and Librarian posts in Kendriya Vidyalaya schools across India.

What is KVS Recruitment?

• Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan hiring

• Teaching posts in 1,200+ KV schools

• Posts: PRT, TGT, PGT, Librarian

• Central government teaching job

Post-wise Eligibility

• PRT: Graduate + B.Ed + CTET

• TGT: Subject graduate + B.Ed + CTET

• PGT: Post-graduate + B.Ed

• Age limit: 30-40 years (varies)

Exam Pattern

• Part I: General Hindi/English (20 marks)

• Part II: GK, Reasoning, Computer (40 marks)

• Part III: Subject + Pedagogy (100 marks)

• Part IV: Demo teaching & Interview

Salary & Benefits

• PRT: Level 6 (₹35,400-1,12,400)

• TGT: Level 7 (₹44,900-1,42,400)

• PGT: Level 8 (₹47,600-1,51,100)

• HRA, DA, medical, pension benefits

How to Apply

• Visit kvsangathan.nic.in

• Register and fill online application

• Upload photo, signature, documents

• Pay fee and download admit card
