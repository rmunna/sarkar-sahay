Marriage Certificate - How to Apply Online

Apply for marriage certificate online under Hindu/Special Marriage Act. Documents, process, and state-wise guide.

Why You Need It

• Legal proof of marriage

• Required for spouse visa/passport

• Name change after marriage

• Joint property & insurance claims

Which Act Applies?

• Hindu Marriage Act: Hindu, Sikh, Jain, Buddhist

• Special Marriage Act: Inter-faith/civil marriages

• Registration process differs by act

• 30-day notice period for Special Marriage Act

Documents Required

• Aadhaar of both spouses

• Age proof (birth certificate/10th marksheet)

• Wedding photos & invitation card

• Witness Aadhaar (2-3 witnesses)

Online Application

• Visit state SDM/municipal portal

• Fill marriage registration form

• Upload all documents

• Book appointment date

Appointment Day

• Both spouses must be present

• Bring witnesses with ID proof

• Fees: ₹100-500 (varies by state)

• Certificate issued same day or within 7 days
