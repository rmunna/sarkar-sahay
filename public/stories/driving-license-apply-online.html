Types of Driving License

• Learner's License (LL): Valid for 6 months

• Permanent DL: Valid for 20 years or till age 50

• Commercial License: For transport vehicles

• International Driving Permit (IDP)

• Two-wheeler, Four-wheeler, or Both

Eligibility

• Two-wheeler: 16 years (up to 50cc), 18 years (geared)

• Four-wheeler: 18 years

• Commercial vehicles: 20 years

• Must know traffic rules and signs

• No pending traffic violations

Step 1: Learner's License

• Visit parivahan.gov.in → Sarathi portal

• Fill Form 1 (medical) and Form 2 (LL application)

• Book slot and pay fee (₹200)

• Take online/offline test at RTO

• 30 questions — need 18 correct

Step 2: Permanent DL

• Apply after 30 days of getting LL

• Fill Form 4 on Sarathi portal

• Book driving test slot at RTO

• Pass the driving test

• DL delivered via post within 30 days

Documents Required

• Learner's License (for permanent DL)

• Aadhaar card

• Address proof

• Medical certificate (Form 1)

• Passport size photographs

Fees

• Learner's License: ₹200

• Permanent DL: ₹200 + smart card fee ₹200

• Commercial DL: Additional ₹300

• Renewal: ₹200

• International DL: ₹1,000
