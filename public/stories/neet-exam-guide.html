What is NEET?

• National Eligibility cum Entrance Test

• Single entrance exam for MBBS, BDS, AYUSH

• Conducted by NTA (National Testing Agency)

• Over 20 lakh candidates appear annually

• Gateway to all medical colleges in India

Eligibility

• Age: 17+ years at time of admission

• Education: Class 12 with PCB (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)

• Minimum marks: 50% for General, 40% for OBC/SC/ST

• No upper age limit (as per Supreme Court)

• Indian citizens and NRIs eligible

Exam Pattern

• Total: 200 MCQs (attempt 180)

• Physics: 45+15 questions

• Chemistry: 45+15 questions

• Biology: 90+30 questions

• Duration: 3 hours 20 minutes

• Marking: +4 correct, -1 wrong

Subject-wise Strategy

• Physics: Focus on Mechanics, Optics, Electrostatics

• Chemistry: NCERT is Bible — read line by line

• Biology: Diagrams, flowcharts, and NCERT

• Solve 10 years of previous papers

• Take mock tests weekly

Important Tips

• NCERT textbooks are non-negotiable

• Start early — ideally from Class 11

• Biology carries most marks (360/720)

• Revision > new topics in last 2 months

• Stay healthy — physical and mental fitness matters
