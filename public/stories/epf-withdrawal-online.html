When Can You Withdraw?

• Full withdrawal: After 2 months of unemployment

• Retirement at 58 years

• Partial: Medical emergency, home purchase, education

• Marriage of self/children/siblings

• 1 month before retirement (90% advance)

Online Withdrawal Process

• Login to unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in

• Go to Online Services → Claim (Form-31, 19, 10C)

• Select type of claim

• Enter bank account and verify

• Submit with Aadhaar OTP

Requirements

• UAN activated and linked to Aadhaar

• Bank account linked to UAN

• KYC verified (Aadhaar + Bank + PAN)

• Mobile number linked to Aadhaar for OTP

• Employer approval not needed if KYC complete

Processing Time

• Online claims: 10–20 working days

• Amount credited to linked bank account

• Track status on EPFO portal or UMANG app

• SMS notification on processing

• Contact EPFO helpline 1800-118-005 for delays

Tax on EPF Withdrawal

• Tax-free if service > 5 years

• TDS at 10% if PF > ₹50,000 and service < 5 years

• Submit Form 15G/15H to avoid TDS (if no tax liability)

• Transfer PF instead of withdrawal when changing jobs
