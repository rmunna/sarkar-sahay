What is Agniveer?

• New recruitment scheme for Indian Armed Forces

• 4-year service tenure

• Launched in 2022 replacing earlier system

• For Army, Navy, and Air Force

• Age: 17.5 to 21 years

Salary & Benefits

• Year 1: ₹30,000/month

• Year 2: ₹33,000/month

• Year 3: ₹36,500/month

• Year 4: ₹40,000/month

• Seva Nidhi: ₹11.71 lakh on exit (tax-free)

• ₹48 lakh life insurance during service

Eligibility — Army

• Age: 17.5–21 years

• 10th/12th pass (depending on trade)

• Physical: Height 160cm+, Weight proportional

• Medical fitness as per Army standards

• Indian citizen only

Selection Process

• Online written exam (CBT)

• Physical Fitness Test (1.6 km run, pull-ups, etc.)

• Medical examination

• Document verification

• Merit-based final selection

How to Apply

• Visit joinindianarmy.nic.in

• Register and create profile

• Fill application during rally notification

• Choose trade and center

• Download admit card before exam

After 4 Years

• 25% retained for 15-year service

• 75% exit with Seva Nidhi package

• Preference in CAPFs and state police

• Skill certificate for civilian jobs

• Higher education support schemes
