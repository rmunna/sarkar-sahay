How to Update Aadhaar Card Online

Step-by-step guide to update your Aadhaar details online - name, address, mobile, photo, and biometrics.

What Can You Update?

• Name, date of birth, gender

• Address, mobile number, email

• Photo and biometrics (at center only)

• Document updates online or at center

Online Update Process

• Visit myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in

• Login with Aadhaar + OTP

• Select "Update Aadhaar" option

• Choose fields to update

Documents Accepted

• Passport, voter ID, DL for identity

• Bank statement, utility bill for address

• Birth certificate for DOB

• Upload clear scanned copies

Fees & Timeline

• Online update: ₹50 per request

• At center: ₹50 (biometric: ₹100)

• Processing time: 10-30 days

• Track at uidai.gov.in with URN

Important Tips

• Keep URN safe for tracking

• Only 1 online update per field allowed

• Biometric update requires center visit

• Free biometric update once every 10 years
