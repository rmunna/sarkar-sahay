What is Jan Dhan Yojana?

• Financial inclusion scheme launched in 2014

• Zero balance savings account

• RuPay debit card with ₹2 lakh accident cover

• Over 50 crore accounts opened

Key Benefits

• Zero minimum balance requirement

• Free RuPay debit card

• ₹2 lakh accidental insurance cover

• ₹30,000 life insurance cover

• Overdraft facility up to ₹10,000

Who Can Open?

• Any Indian citizen above 10 years

• No minimum balance needed

• One account per household

• Existing account holders can convert

• Small accounts for those without documents

How to Open Account

• Visit any bank branch or Bank Mitra

• Fill account opening form

• Submit KYC documents (Aadhaar recommended)

• Get passbook and RuPay card instantly

• Can also apply online via bank website

Documents Needed

• Aadhaar card (primary KYC)

• Voter ID or driving license (alternative)

• Passport size photograph

• NREGA job card (if applicable)
