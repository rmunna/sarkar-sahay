What is IBPS PO?

• Probationary Officer recruitment for public banks

• Conducted by IBPS annually

• Posts in 11 participating public sector banks

• Starting salary: ₹52,000+ (including allowances)

• 3 stages: Prelims, Mains, Interview

Eligibility

• Age: 20–30 years

• Education: Any graduation

• Computer literacy required

• Valid score in Prelims to sit for Mains

• Age relaxation as per govt norms

Prelims Pattern

• English Language: 30 questions, 30 marks

• Quantitative Aptitude: 35 questions, 35 marks

• Reasoning Ability: 35 questions, 35 marks

• Duration: 60 minutes

• Sectional timing: 20 min each

Mains Pattern

• Reasoning & Computer: 45 questions, 60 marks

• English Language: 35 questions, 40 marks

• Data Analysis: 35 questions, 60 marks

• General/Economy/Banking: 40 questions, 40 marks

• Duration: 3 hours

Preparation Tips

• Practice speed maths daily

• Read financial newspapers for Banking GK

• Take sectional and full mock tests

• Focus on Data Interpretation and Puzzles

• Revise banking terms and RBI policies
