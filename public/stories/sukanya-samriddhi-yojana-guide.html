Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)

Open SSY account for your daughter - earn 8.2% interest, get tax benefits under 80C, and secure her future.

What is Sukanya Samriddhi?

• Savings scheme for girl child

• Current interest rate: 8.2% per annum

• Tax-free returns (EEE status)

• Government-backed - zero risk

Eligibility & Rules

• Girl child must be under 10 years

• Max 2 accounts (one per daughter)

• Minimum deposit: ₹250/year

• Maximum deposit: ₹1.5 lakh/year

Account Duration

• Deposit for 15 years from opening

• Account matures after 21 years

• Partial withdrawal at 18 (50% for education)

• Full withdrawal on maturity or marriage after 18

Tax Benefits

• Deposit deductible under Section 80C

• Interest earned is tax-free

• Maturity amount fully tax-free

• Triple tax benefit (EEE)

How to Open Account

• Visit any post office or authorized bank

• Fill SSY account opening form

• Carry birth certificate of girl child

• Parent's Aadhaar and PAN required
