Capital Gains Tax in India Explained

Complete guide to capital gains tax on property, stocks, mutual funds, and gold. STCG, LTCG rates and exemptions.

What is Capital Gains Tax?

• Tax on profit from selling assets

• Short-term (STCG) vs Long-term (LTCG)

• Holding period determines type

• Different rates for different assets

LTCG Rates (New Rules from July 2024)

• Listed equity/funds: 12.5% above ₹1.25 lakh

• Property/gold/debt funds: 12.5%

• No indexation benefit (new rule)

• Holding: 12 months (equity), 24 months (others)

STCG Rates

• Listed equity/funds: 20%

• Other assets: As per income tax slab

• Equity holding < 12 months = STCG

• Property holding < 24 months = STCG

Exemptions Available

• Section 54: Reinvest in new house (property)

• Section 54EC: Invest in specified bonds

• Section 54F: Non-house asset → house

• LTCG up to ₹1.25 lakh exempt (equity)

How to Report

• Report in ITR-2 or ITR-3

• Broker provides capital gains statement

• Include all asset sales in the year

• Set off losses against gains within rules
