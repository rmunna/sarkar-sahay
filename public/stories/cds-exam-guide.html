What is CDS Exam?

• Combined Defence Services Examination

• For graduates joining defence forces

• Entry to IMA, INA, AFA, OTA

• Conducted by UPSC twice a year

Eligibility

• IMA/OTA: Any degree

• INA: Engineering degree

• AFA: Degree with Physics & Math

• Age: 19-25 years (varies by academy)

Exam Pattern

• English: 100 marks

• General Knowledge: 100 marks

• Elementary Math: 100 marks (not for OTA)

• Duration: 2 hours per paper

SSB Interview

• 5-day selection process

• Tests: Psychological, Group, Interview

• Assesses Officer Like Qualities (OLQ)

• Medical examination follows

Training & Career

• IMA: 18 months (Dehradun)

• INA: Naval Academy (Ezhimala)

• AFA: Air Force Academy (Dundigal)

• Permanent/Short Service Commission
