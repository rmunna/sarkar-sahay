What is JEE Main?

• Joint Entrance Examination for engineering

• Gateway to NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs

• Also qualifying exam for JEE Advanced (IITs)

• Conducted by NTA twice a year

• Over 12 lakh candidates appear

Eligibility

• Class 12 passed or appearing with PCM

• No age limit

• Maximum 3 consecutive attempts

• Minimum 75% in Class 12 (or top 20 percentile)

• JEE Advanced: top 2.5 lakh qualifiers from Main

Exam Pattern

• Physics: 30 questions (20 MCQ + 10 numerical)

• Chemistry: 30 questions (same split)

• Maths: 30 questions (same split)

• Attempt 75 out of 90

• Duration: 3 hours

• Marking: +4, -1 for MCQ

Preparation Strategy

• Master NCERT for basics

• HC Verma (Physics), MS Chouhan (Chemistry)

• RD Sharma and Cengage for Maths

• Focus on problem-solving speed

• Solve previous 10 years papers

Tips for High Score

• Attempt easy questions first

• Don't waste time on 1 tough question

• Numerical questions have no negative marking

• Take full-length mocks every week

• Analyze mistakes after each test
