PM SVANidhi - Loans for Street Vendors

Get working capital loans up to ₹50,000 for street vendors under PM SVANidhi scheme with interest subsidy.

What is PM SVANidhi?

• Micro-credit for street vendors

• Working capital loan facility

• Digital payments incentive

• Launched during COVID-19 pandemic

Loan Amounts

• First loan: ₹10,000

• Second loan: ₹20,000 (on repayment)

• Third loan: ₹50,000

• 7% interest subsidy by government

Who is Eligible?

• Street vendors with vending certificate

• Or identified in urban survey

• Letter of recommendation from ULB

• Must vend in urban areas

Digital Payment Reward

• Cashback on digital transactions

• ₹50-100/month for using UPI/QR

• Encourages cashless payments

• Track via PM SVANidhi app

How to Apply

• Visit pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in

• Register with mobile number

• Upload photo & vending certificate

• Apply to nearest lending institution
