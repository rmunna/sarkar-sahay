How to Apply for Birth Certificate Online

Register birth and get birth certificate online. Step-by-step guide for new registration and delayed registration.

Why Birth Certificate Matters

• Essential for school admission

• Required for Aadhaar, passport

• Age proof for all purposes

• Free if registered within 21 days

Online Registration

• Visit crsorgi.gov.in

• Or state-specific portal

• Fill birth registration form

• Hospital births auto-registered usually

Documents Required

• Hospital discharge summary

• Parents' Aadhaar cards

• Marriage certificate of parents

• Proof of address

Delayed Registration

• After 21 days: registrar permission needed

• After 30 days: magistrate order required

• Affidavit may be needed

• Late fee applicable

Download Certificate

• Login to state portal after registration

• Certificate available in 7-15 days

• Download digitally signed copy

• Also available on DigiLocker
