PM Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY)

Accidental insurance cover of ₹2 lakh at just ₹20/year. Complete guide to PMSBY benefits and enrollment.

What is PMSBY?

• Accidental death & disability insurance

• Annual premium: Only ₹20

• Coverage: ₹2 lakh (death/full disability)

• ₹1 lakh for partial disability

Who Can Enroll?

• Age: 18 to 70 years

• Must have savings bank account

• Aadhaar linked to bank

• One policy per person

What is Covered?

• Accidental death: ₹2 lakh

• Total permanent disability: ₹2 lakh

• Partial permanent disability: ₹1 lakh

• Covers road, rail & other accidents

How to Enroll

• SMS your bank or visit branch

• Enroll via net banking/UPI app

• Premium debited once on 1 June yearly

• No medical examination needed

Claim Process

• Nominee informs the bank

• Submit FIR + disability/death certificate

• Hospital records for disability claims

• Claim settled within 30 days
