Section 80C - Save Tax up to ₹46,800

Complete guide to Section 80C deductions. Best investments to save up to ₹1.5 lakh in taxable income.

What is Section 80C?

• Tax deduction up to ₹1.5 lakh per year

• Reduces taxable income

• Save up to ₹46,800 in tax (30% slab)

• Available under Old Tax Regime only

Popular 80C Investments

• PPF: 7.1% tax-free (15 yr lock-in)

• ELSS Mutual Funds: Market returns (3 yr lock-in)

• NSC: 7.7% (5 yr lock-in)

• Tax-saving FD: ~7% (5 yr lock-in)

Other 80C Eligible Items

• EPF employee contribution

• Life insurance premium

• Children's tuition fees (max 2 children)

• Home loan principal repayment

Best Strategy

• EPF contribution already counts

• Top up with ELSS for higher returns

• PPF for guaranteed tax-free returns

• Don't invest just for tax saving

Beyond 80C

• 80CCD(1B): ₹50,000 extra for NPS

• 80D: Health insurance premium

• 80E: Education loan interest (no limit)

• 80TTA: ₹10,000 savings interest exemption
