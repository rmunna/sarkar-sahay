What is SIP?

• Systematic Investment Plan

• Invest fixed amount monthly in mutual funds

• Start with as low as ₹500/month

• Automates your investment discipline

How SIP Works

• Fixed amount debited monthly

• Buys units at current NAV

• Rupee cost averaging reduces risk

• Power of compounding grows wealth

SIP Returns Examples

• ₹5,000/month for 10 years at 12% = ₹11.6 lakh

• ₹10,000/month for 20 years at 12% = ₹1 crore

• ₹15,000/month for 25 years at 12% = ₹2.6 crore

• Early start = massive difference

SIP Calculation Formula

• FV = P × [(1+r)^n - 1] / r × (1+r)

• P = Monthly SIP amount

• r = Expected monthly return rate

• n = Total number of months

SIP Tips

• Start early - time is your biggest ally

• Step-up SIP: Increase amount yearly

• Don't stop during market crashes
