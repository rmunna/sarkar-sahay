PM Awas Yojana (PMAY) Housing Scheme

Apply for PMAY - get subsidy up to ₹2.67 lakh on home loans for affordable housing under Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana.

What is PM Awas Yojana?

• Affordable housing for all Indians

• Interest subsidy on home loans (CLSS)

• Up to ₹2.67 lakh subsidy benefit

• Covers urban and rural areas

Income Categories & Subsidy

• EWS: Income up to ₹3 lakh - 6.5% subsidy

• LIG: ₹3-6 lakh - 6.5% subsidy

• MIG-I: ₹6-12 lakh - 4% subsidy

• MIG-II: ₹12-18 lakh - 3% subsidy

Eligibility Requirements

• No pucca house in family name

• Not availed any housing scheme before

• Aadhaar mandatory for all members

• Property must be in woman's name (EWS/LIG)

How to Apply

• Visit pmaymis.gov.in

• Click "Citizen Assessment" > "Under CLSS"

• Fill form with Aadhaar & income details

• Apply through bank for loan + subsidy

Track Application Status

• Visit pmaymis.gov.in

• Use "Track Your Assessment" option

• Enter assessment ID or mobile/name

• Check approval and subsidy status
