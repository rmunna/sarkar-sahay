DigiLocker - Digital Document Wallet

How to use DigiLocker for storing and accessing official documents. Link Aadhaar, DL, marksheets, and more digitally.

What is DigiLocker?

• Government digital document platform

• Store and access documents online

• Legally valid digital copies

• Free for all Indian citizens

Key Features

• Issued Documents: Directly from govt departments

• Uploaded Documents: Scan and store your own

• Available as app and website

• Linked to Aadhaar for authentication

Documents You Can Access

• Aadhaar, PAN, Driving License

• Vehicle RC, Insurance policies

• Class 10/12 marksheets (CBSE)

• Birth certificate, Caste certificate

How to Register

• Download DigiLocker app or visit digilocker.gov.in

• Sign up with Aadhaar or mobile number

• Verify with OTP

• Create 6-digit security PIN

Why Use DigiLocker?

• No need to carry physical documents

• Accepted for government verification

• Self-attested = deemed verified

• Reduce use of paper documents
