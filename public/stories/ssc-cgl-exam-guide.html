What is SSC CGL?

• Combined Graduate Level Examination

• Conducted by Staff Selection Commission

• For Group B and C posts in central govt

• Posts: Tax Assistant, Auditor, Inspector, etc.

• Salary range: ₹25,000–₹1,51,000

Eligibility Criteria

• Age: 18–32 years (varies by post)

• Education: Bachelor's degree from recognized university

• Age relaxation for SC/ST/OBC/PwD

• No minimum percentage required

• Multiple attempts allowed

Exam Pattern

• Tier 1: 100 MCQs, 60 minutes (online)

• Tier 2: Paper I (Maths+Reasoning+English+GK), 60 min each

• Tier 2: Paper II (Statistics/Finance — for specific posts)

• Negative marking: 0.50 for Tier 1, 0.25 for Tier 2

Syllabus Overview

• Quantitative Aptitude: Arithmetic, Algebra, Geometry

• English: Comprehension, Grammar, Vocabulary

• General Intelligence: Analogies, Series, Puzzles

• General Awareness: Current Affairs, History, Polity

Preparation Tips

• Start 6–8 months before exam

• Focus on Maths and English (high scoring)

• Solve previous year papers daily

• Take mock tests every week

• Follow current affairs regularly

Important Resources

• Official website: ssc.nic.in

• Previous year papers on ssc.nic.in

• NCERT books for basics

• Kiran, Lucent, and Wren & Martin for practice
