Atal Pension Yojana (APY)

Get guaranteed pension of ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 per month after 60. Complete guide to APY enrollment and benefits.

What is Atal Pension Yojana?

• Guaranteed monthly pension after age 60

• Pension of ₹1,000 to ₹5,000/month

• Government co-contribution for eligible

• Available for all Indian citizens

Who Can Join?

• Age between 18-40 years

• Must have a savings bank account

• Not an income tax payer

• Must have mobile number & Aadhaar

Pension Options

• ₹1,000/month: Pay ₹42-291/month

• ₹2,000/month: Pay ₹84-582/month

• ₹3,000/month: Pay ₹126-873/month

• ₹5,000/month: Pay ₹210-1,454/month

Key Benefits

• Guaranteed pension for life

• Spouse gets same pension after death

• Nominee gets accumulated corpus

• Auto-debit from bank - hassle free

How to Enroll

• Visit your bank branch

• Or enroll via net banking/mobile app

• Fill APY registration form

• Set up auto-debit for contributions
