Driving License Renewal Online

How to renew your driving license online through Parivahan portal - step by step with documents and fees.

When to Renew DL?

• DL valid for 20 years or till age 50

• Renew 1 year before to 1 year after expiry

• Late renewal attracts penalty

• Commercial DL: renew every 3 years

Online Application

• Visit parivahan.gov.in/sarathi

• Select state and "DL Renewal"

• Fill Form 9 with DL number

• Upload photo, signature, documents

Documents Required

• Existing driving license

• Address proof (Aadhaar/passport)

• Medical certificate (Form 1A) for 50+

• Passport size photographs

Fees

• DL renewal: ₹200

• Smart card fee: ₹200

• Late fee: ₹1,000 (after 1 year)

• Medical test fee: ₹50-200

After Application

• Book slot at RTO if required

• Download receipt and track status

• DL dispatched by speed post

• Takes 7-30 days depending on state
