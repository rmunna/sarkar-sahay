Who Must File ITR?

• Gross income above ₹2.5 lakh (below 60)

• Above ₹3 lakh (senior citizens 60-80)

• Above ₹5 lakh (super senior 80+)

• TDS deducted and want refund

• Foreign assets or income

Choose the Right ITR Form

• ITR-1 (Sahaj): Salary up to ₹50L, 1 house

• ITR-2: Capital gains, multiple properties, NRI

• ITR-3: Business/profession income

• ITR-4 (Sugam): Presumptive income scheme

• Most salaried employees use ITR-1

How to File Online

• Visit incometax.gov.in

• Login with PAN and password

• Go to e-File → Income Tax Returns

• Select assessment year and ITR form

• Pre-filled data available — verify and edit

• Compute tax and submit

Documents Needed

• Form 16 from employer

• Form 26AS (tax credit statement)

• AIS (Annual Information Statement)

• Bank statements and interest certificates

• Investment proofs for deductions

E-Verification (Mandatory)

• Aadhaar OTP (most common method)

• Net banking

• Bank account EVC

• Demat account EVC

• Must verify within 30 days of filing

Due Dates

• Salaried/individuals: July 31

• Audit cases: October 31

• Belated return: December 31

• Revised return: December 31

• Late fee: ₹1,000–₹5,000
