PPF Account - How to Open & Invest

Open a Public Provident Fund account for tax-free returns at 7.1%. Complete guide to PPF rules, benefits, and process.

What is PPF?

• Government savings scheme

• Current interest rate: 7.1% p.a.

• Tax-free returns (EEE status)

• Lock-in period: 15 years

Key Features

• Minimum deposit: ₹500/year

• Maximum deposit: ₹1.5 lakh/year

• Interest compounded annually

• Can extend in blocks of 5 years

Tax Benefits

• Deposit deductible under Section 80C

• Interest earned is fully tax-free

• Maturity amount is tax-free

• Best risk-free tax saving instrument

How to Open

• Open at post office or bank (SBI, etc.)

• Online opening via net banking available

• Need PAN, Aadhaar, and photographs

• Minor account can be opened by guardian

Withdrawal & Loan

• Partial withdrawal from 7th year

• Loan against PPF from 3rd to 6th year

• Premature closure after 5 years (conditions apply)

• Nomination facility available
