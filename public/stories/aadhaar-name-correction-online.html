When Do You Need Aadhaar Name Correction?

• Spelling mistake: — Your name was entered incorrectly during enrolment (e.g., "Raaj" instead of "Raj")

• Name change after marriage: — You want to update your surname

• Name change by gazette notification: — Legal name change

• Mismatch with other documents: — Your Aadhaar name doesn't match PAN, passport, or bank records

Requirements Before You Start

• Your mobile number must be registered with your Aadhaar

• A scanned copy of a valid name proof document (clear, coloured scan of original)

• File size: max 2 MB, format: JPEG/PNG/PDF

Document Upload Tips

• Upload a coloured scan of the original document (not a photocopy)

• Ensure the document is clearly readable — no blur, no shadows

• The name on the document must match the name you are requesting

• Keep file size under 2 MB

How Many Times Can You Change Your Name in Aadhaar?

• First update:: Allowed freely with valid documents

• Second update:: Allowed with valid documents

• Third update onwards:: Not permitted through self-service; you may need to approach UIDAI's regional office with a valid reason

Status Meanings

• Submitted: — Your request has been received

• Under Review: — UIDAI is verifying your documents

• Accepted: — Name has been updated successfully

• Rejected: — Document was not accepted (see reasons below)
