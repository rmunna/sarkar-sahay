Home Loan Basics

• Loan for purchasing, constructing, or renovating

• Tenure: Up to 30 years

• Interest rates: 8.25%–9.5% (varies by bank)

• Loan amount: Up to 80-90% of property value

• Both fixed and floating rate options

Eligibility Factors

• Age: 21–65 years

• Stable income (salaried or self-employed)

• Good CIBIL score (750+ preferred)

• Property should be legally clear

• Co-applicant can increase eligibility

Documents Required

• Identity and address proof

• Income proof: Salary slips, ITR, Form 16

• Bank statements (6 months)

• Property documents

• Passport size photographs

Tax Benefits

• Section 24: Up to ₹2 lakh on interest (self-occupied)

• Section 80C: Up to ₹1.5 lakh on principal

• Section 80EEA: Additional ₹1.5 lakh (first-time buyers)

• Joint loan: Both can claim separately

• Pre-construction interest also deductible

Tips for Best Deal

• Compare rates across banks online

• Negotiate processing fee (often waived)

• Choose floating rate in falling rate scenario

• Prepay when possible to save interest

• Check for hidden charges in loan agreement
