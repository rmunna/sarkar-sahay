How to Renew Indian Passport Online

Complete guide to renew your Indian passport - online application, documents, fees, and appointment process.

When to Renew?

• Passport expired or expiring within 1 year

• All pages used up

• Damaged passport

• Name/address change needed

Online Application Steps

• Visit passportindia.gov.in

• Register/login and fill application

• Choose "Re-issue of Passport"

• Pay fee and book appointment

Documents Required

• Old passport (original)

• Aadhaar card

• Address proof if changed

• Self-attested photocopies

Fees

• 36 pages: ₹1,500 (normal), ₹3,500 (tatkal)

• 60 pages: ₹2,000 (normal), ₹4,000 (tatkal)

• Normal processing: 30 days

• Tatkal processing: 1-3 days

Appointment Day Tips

• Reach PSK 15 min before slot

• Carry all original documents

• No mobile phones inside PSK

• Police verification may follow
