How to File ITR Online - Complete Guide

Step-by-step guide to file Income Tax Return online on the new e-filing portal. Choose the right ITR form and file easil

Who Must File ITR?

• Income above ₹2.5 lakh (below 60)

• Senior citizens: above ₹3 lakh

• Super seniors: above ₹5 lakh

• Mandatory if TDS deducted

Choose the Right ITR Form

• ITR-1 (Sahaj): Salary up to ₹50 lakh

• ITR-2: Capital gains, foreign income

• ITR-3: Business/profession income

• ITR-4 (Sugam): Presumptive income

Documents Required

• Form 16 from employer

• Bank interest certificates

• Investment proofs (80C, 80D)

• AIS/TIS from income tax portal

Filing Steps

• Login at efilingindia.gov.in

• Select AY and ITR form

• Pre-fill data from AIS

• Verify deductions and submit

Verify Your ITR

• e-Verify within 30 days of filing

• Use Aadhaar OTP (fastest)

• Or net banking, DSC, bank ATM

• Unverified ITR = not filed!
