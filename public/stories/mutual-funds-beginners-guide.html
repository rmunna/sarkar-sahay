What are Mutual Funds?

• Pool of money from multiple investors

• Managed by professional fund managers

• Invest in stocks, bonds, or both

• SEBI regulated and transparent

• Start with as low as ₹500/month via SIP

Types of Mutual Funds

• Equity Funds: High risk, high returns (stocks)

• Debt Funds: Low risk, stable returns (bonds)

• Hybrid Funds: Mix of equity and debt

• Index Funds: Track Nifty 50 or Sensex

• ELSS: Tax-saving funds (Section 80C)

What is SIP?

• Systematic Investment Plan

• Fixed amount invested monthly

• Rupee cost averaging reduces risk

• Power of compounding over time

• ₹5,000/month for 20 years at 12% = ₹49+ lakh

How to Start Investing

• Complete KYC on kra.ndml.in or AMC website

• Choose Direct plan (lower expense ratio)

• Invest via AMC website, Kuvera, or Groww

• Select fund based on goals and risk appetite

• Start SIP and stay invested long-term

Common Mistakes to Avoid

• Don't chase past returns

• Don't exit during market dips

• Avoid too many funds (3-5 is enough)

• Regular plans have higher costs than Direct

• Review portfolio once a year, not daily
