PM Vishwakarma Yojana - For Artisans

PM Vishwakarma scheme for traditional artisans and craftworkers. Get training, toolkit, loans, and digital support.

What is PM Vishwakarma?

• Support scheme for traditional artisans

• Covers 18 traditional trades

• Training, toolkit & loan support

• Launched on 17 September 2023

Covered Trades

• Carpenter, blacksmith, goldsmith, potter

• Sculptor, cobbler, tailor, weaver

• Boat maker, armourer, locksmith

• Basket/mat/broom maker, toy maker

Benefits Package

• Free 5-15 day skill training

• Toolkit incentive of ₹15,000

• Collateral-free loans up to ₹3 lakh

• Digital & marketing support

Loan Details

• First tranche: ₹1 lakh at 5% interest

• Second tranche: ₹2 lakh at 5% interest

• Interest subvention by government

• Repayment period: 18 months per tranche

How to Apply

• Visit pmvishwakarma.gov.in

• Register with Aadhaar and mobile

• Verify at nearest CSC or GP office

• Get PM Vishwakarma certificate & ID
