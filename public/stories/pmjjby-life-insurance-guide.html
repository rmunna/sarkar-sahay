PM Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY)

Get ₹2 lakh life insurance cover at just ₹436/year under PMJJBY. Know eligibility, claim process, and enrollment.

What is PMJJBY?

• Life insurance cover of ₹2 lakh

• Annual premium: Just ₹436

• Covers death due to any reason

• Available through bank accounts

Eligibility

• Age: 18 to 50 years

• Must have savings bank account

• Aadhaar linked to bank account

• Auto-debit consent required

Coverage Details

• ₹2 lakh on death due to any cause

• Cover period: 1 June to 31 May

• Annual renewal by auto-debit

• Can be renewed up to age 55

How to Enroll

• Visit your bank branch

• Or enroll via net banking app

• Fill consent-cum-declaration form

• Premium auto-debited on 31 May each year

How to Claim

• Nominee contacts the bank

• Submit death certificate

• Fill claim form with bank details

• Amount credited within 30 days
