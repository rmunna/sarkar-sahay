Types of Water Connections

• Domestic (residential use)

• Commercial (business premises)

• Industrial (factory use)

• Temporary (construction)

• Fire fighting connection

How to Apply

• Visit local municipal corporation website

• Or apply at ward office in person

• Fill application form with property details

• Specify connection size needed

• Pay application and connection fees

Documents Required

• Property ownership proof (sale deed/tax receipt)

• Identity proof (Aadhaar card)

• Address proof

• Building plan approval

• NOC from society (for apartments)

Fees (Approximate)

• Application fee: ₹100–500

• Connection charge: ₹2,000–10,000

• Security deposit: ₹1,000–5,000

• Meter charges: ₹500–2,000

• Varies significantly by city and state

Tips

• Apply during non-peak season for faster processing

• Ensure no outstanding property tax dues

• Keep all receipts for future reference

• Monthly bill based on meter reading

• Report leakage to save water and money
