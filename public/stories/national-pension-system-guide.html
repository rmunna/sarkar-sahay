National Pension System (NPS)

Invest in NPS for retirement - get extra ₹50,000 tax deduction under 80CCD(1B) plus market-linked returns.

What is NPS?

• Market-linked retirement savings scheme

• Regulated by PFRDA (Government body)

• Low-cost investment with tax benefits

• Available for all citizens aged 18-70

Two Account Types

• Tier I: Retirement account (lock-in till 60)

• Tier II: Voluntary savings (no lock-in)

• Tier I minimum: ₹500/year

• Choose from Equity, Corporate Bond, Govt Securities

Tax Benefits

• Up to ₹1.5 lakh under Section 80CCD(1)

• Extra ₹50,000 under Section 80CCD(1B)

• Employer contribution: 80CCD(2) - no limit for central govt

• 60% corpus tax-free on withdrawal

Investment Choices

• Active Choice: Pick your own allocation

• Auto Choice: Age-based auto allocation

• Choose from 7+ pension fund managers

• Switch fund managers once a year

How to Open NPS Account

• Visit enps.nsdl.com for online opening

• eKYC via Aadhaar OTP or PAN

• Minimum ₹500 initial contribution

• Get 12-digit PRAN (Permanent Account Number)
