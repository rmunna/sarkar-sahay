GST Registration Online - Step by Step

Complete guide to register for GST on the GST portal. Who needs GST, documents required, and registration process.

Who Needs GST Registration?

• Turnover above ₹40 lakh (goods)

• Turnover above ₹20 lakh (services)

• Interstate suppliers (any turnover)

• E-commerce sellers

Documents Required

• PAN card of business/proprietor

• Aadhaar card

• Business address proof

• Bank account statement/cancelled cheque

Registration Process

• Visit gst.gov.in

• Fill Part A: PAN, mobile, email

• Verify with OTP → get TRN

• Fill Part B: Business details, documents

After Registration

• GSTIN allotted within 7 working days

• 15-digit GST number issued

• Start filing GST returns monthly/quarterly

• Display GSTIN at business place

Composition Scheme

• For turnover up to ₹1.5 crore

• Pay flat 1-6% tax rate

• Quarterly return instead of monthly

• Cannot claim input tax credit
