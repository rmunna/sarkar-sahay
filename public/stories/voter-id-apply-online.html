What is Voter ID?

• Official identity card for voting

• Issued by Election Commission of India

• Also known as EPIC (Electors Photo ID Card)

• Valid identity proof across India

• Essential for participating in elections

Eligibility

• Indian citizen

• Age: 18 years or above on qualifying date

• Resident of the constituency

• Not disqualified under any law

• One voter ID per person

How to Apply Online

• Visit voters.eci.gov.in or Voter Helpline app

• Click 'New Voter Registration' (Form 6)

• Fill personal details and upload photo

• Upload supporting documents

• Submit and note reference number

Documents Required

• Age proof: Birth certificate, Class 10 marksheet

• Address proof: Aadhaar, utility bill, bank statement

• Passport size photograph

• Aadhaar number (optional but recommended)

After Submission

• Application verified by BLO (Booth Level Officer)

• BLO may visit your address for verification

• Approved applications processed within 30 days

• Track status online using reference number

• Download e-EPIC from voters.eci.gov.in
