Advance Tax - Who Should Pay & How

Understand advance tax liability, due dates, calculation, and how to pay online through the income tax portal.

What is Advance Tax?

• Pay-as-you-earn tax system

• Pay tax in installments during the year

• Required if tax liability > ₹10,000

• Salaried (with other income), freelancers, businesses

Due Dates

• 15 June: 15% of total tax

• 15 September: 45% cumulative

• 15 December: 75% cumulative

• 15 March: 100% of total tax

How to Calculate

• Estimate total annual income

• Apply tax slab rates

• Subtract TDS already deducted

• Balance = advance tax payable

How to Pay Online

• Visit incometax.gov.in > e-Pay Tax

• Select Challan 280 (Income Tax)

• Enter PAN, AY, and tax amount

• Pay via net banking/UPI/debit card

Interest on Non-Payment

• Section 234B: Non/short payment of advance tax

• Section 234C: Deferment of installments

• Interest: 1% per month on shortfall

• Seniors (no business income) exempt
