EPF Withdrawal Online - Complete Process

How to withdraw EPF/PF money online. Full withdrawal, partial withdrawal, and advance claims explained.

Types of EPF Withdrawal

• Full withdrawal: On retirement/2 months unemployment

• Partial: For home, medical, education, marriage

• Advance: COVID, natural disaster

• Pension withdrawal: After 10 years service

Online Withdrawal Steps

• Login at unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in

• Go to Online Services > Claim (Form-31/19/10C)

• Select claim type and enter details

• Verify with Aadhaar OTP

Requirements for Online Claim

• UAN must be activated

• Aadhaar and bank linked to UAN

• Mobile number linked to Aadhaar

• KYC approved by employer

Partial Withdrawal Rules

• Home purchase: Up to 36 months salary (5 yrs)

• Medical: 6 months salary (no minimum)

• Marriage: 50% of employee share (7 yrs)

• Education: 50% of employee share (7 yrs)

Processing Time

• Online claims: 10-20 days

• Amount credited to linked bank

• Track at passbook.epfindia.gov.in

• Contact EPFO if delayed beyond 20 days
