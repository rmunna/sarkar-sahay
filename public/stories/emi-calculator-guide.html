What is EMI?

• Equated Monthly Installment

• Fixed monthly payment on loans

• Includes principal + interest

• Stays same throughout loan tenure

EMI Formula

• EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n - 1)

• P = Principal loan amount

• r = Monthly interest rate

• n = Number of monthly installments

Factors Affecting EMI

• Loan amount: Higher amount = higher EMI

• Interest rate: Higher rate = higher EMI

• Tenure: Longer tenure = lower EMI

• Prepayment reduces total interest

Typical EMI Examples

• Home loan ₹50L at 8.5%, 20 yrs = ₹43,391

• Car loan ₹8L at 9%, 5 yrs = ₹16,607

• Personal loan ₹5L at 12%, 3 yrs = ₹16,607

• Education loan ₹10L at 8%, 7 yrs = ₹15,586

Tips to Reduce EMI

• Negotiate for lower interest rate

• Make prepayments when possible

• Choose longer tenure (but pay more interest)

• Compare across banks before borrowing
