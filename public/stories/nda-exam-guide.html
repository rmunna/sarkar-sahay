NDA Exam - National Defence Academy Guide

NDA exam guide for joining Indian Army, Navy, or Air Force. Eligibility, exam pattern, SSB interview preparation.

What is NDA Exam?

• Entry to National Defence Academy

• Join Army, Navy, or Air Force

• Conducted by UPSC twice a year

• After 12th - youngest officer entry

Eligibility

• Age: 16.5 to 19.5 years

• Education: 12th pass (or appearing)

• Physics/Math compulsory for Air Force/Navy

• Unmarried males (females now eligible)

Written Exam Pattern

• Math: 300 marks (2.5 hours)

• GAT: 600 marks (2.5 hours)

• GAT includes English, GK, Science

• Negative marking: 1/3 of marks

SSB Interview (5 Days)

• Day 1: Screening (OIR + PPDT)

• Day 2-3: Psychology tests

• Day 4: Group tasks (GTO)

• Day 5: Conference and results

Training & Career

• 3 years training at NDA, Khadakwasla

• 1 year at respective academy (IMA/INA/AFA)

• Commissioned as Lieutenant

• Prestigious career serving the nation
