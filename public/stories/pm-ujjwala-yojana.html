What is Ujjwala Yojana?

• Free LPG connection for BPL households

• Launched in 2016 by PM Modi

• Ujjwala 2.0 launched in 2021

• Over 9 crore connections provided so far

Who is Eligible?

• Women from BPL households

• Age: 18 years or above

• No existing LPG connection in the household

• SC/ST, Pradhan Mantri Awas beneficiaries

• Most backward classes and tea garden workers

Benefits Provided

• Free LPG connection (no security deposit)

• Free first refill and hotplate

• ₹1,600 subsidy per connection

• EMI option for stove and first refill

How to Apply

• Visit nearest LPG distributor

• Fill KYC form with Aadhaar & bank details

• Submit BPL certificate or ration card

• Connection issued within 7–15 days

Documents Required

• BPL ration card or certificate

• Aadhaar card

• Bank account passbook

• Passport size photograph

• Address proof
