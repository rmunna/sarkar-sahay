Ayushman Bharat Yojana (PMJAY)

How to get Ayushman Bharat health card, check eligibility, and avail free treatment up to ₹5 lakh per family.

What is Ayushman Bharat?

• Free health insurance up to ₹5 lakh/year

• Covers 1,500+ medical procedures

• For economically weaker families

• Cashless treatment at empanelled hospitals

Eligibility Criteria

• Based on SECC 2011 data

• Rural: deprivation criteria families

• Urban: listed occupational categories

• Check at mera.pmjay.gov.in

How to Get Ayushman Card

• Visit nearest CSC or empanelled hospital

• Carry Aadhaar + ration card

• eKYC verification done on spot

• Card generated digitally

How to Use for Treatment

• Visit any PMJAY empanelled hospital

• Show Ayushman card at reception

• Treatment is 100% cashless

• No pre-existing disease exclusion

Check Your Eligibility Online

• Visit mera.pmjay.gov.in

• Enter mobile number for OTP

• Search by name, ration card, or RSBY ID

• Download e-card if eligible
