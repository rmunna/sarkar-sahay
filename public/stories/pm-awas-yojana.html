What is PM Awas Yojana?

• Government scheme for affordable housing

• Launched in 2015 for urban and rural areas

• Goal: Housing for All by 2024

• Interest subsidy on home loans up to ₹2.67 lakh

Who is Eligible?

• EWS: Annual income up to ₹3 lakh

• LIG: Annual income ₹3–6 lakh

• MIG-I: Annual income ₹6–12 lakh

• MIG-II: Annual income ₹12–18 lakh

• Applicant should not own a pucca house

Benefits & Subsidy

• EWS/LIG: 6.5% interest subsidy for 20 years

• MIG-I: 4% interest subsidy on loans up to ₹9 lakh

• MIG-II: 3% interest subsidy on loans up to ₹12 lakh

• Subsidy credited directly to loan account

How to Apply Online

• Visit pmaymis.gov.in

• Click 'Citizen Assessment' → select category

• Enter Aadhaar number for verification

• Fill application form with income & family details

Documents Required

• Aadhaar card of all family members

• Income certificate

• Property documents (if applicable)

• Bank account details

• Passport size photographs

Track Your Application

• Visit pmaymis.gov.in → Track Assessment

• Enter application ID or Aadhaar number

• Check status: Submitted, Under Review, or Approved

• Contact local municipal office for queries
