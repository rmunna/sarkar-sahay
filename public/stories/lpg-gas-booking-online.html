Booking Methods

• Online through distributor portal

• Mobile app (Indane, BharatGas, MyHPGas)

• SMS booking

• IVRS (missed call)

• WhatsApp booking (select distributors)

Indane (Indian Oil) Booking

• Online: indane.co.in → Book Refill

• SMS: IVRS to 7718955555

• App: IndianOil ONE app

• Missed call: 8454955555

• WhatsApp: Save and send 'Book' to distributor

Bharat Gas Booking

• Online: ebharatgas.com

• SMS: RSLConsumer No to 7738299899

• App: BharatGas app on Play Store

• Missed call: 7738299899

• Track delivery on portal

HP Gas Booking

• Online: myhpgas.in

• SMS: HPConsumer No to 9222201122

• App: HP Pay app

• Missed call: 9222201122

• Online payment for faster delivery

Subsidy & Pricing

• Current price: ₹803–903 (varies by city)

• Subsidy via DBT to bank account

• Check subsidy status on MyLPG.in

• Pahal (DBTL) scheme links Aadhaar to LPG

• Give-it-up option for voluntary subsidy surrender
