What is UPSC CSE?

• India's toughest competitive exam

• Recruits for IAS, IPS, IFS, and 20+ services

• Conducted by Union Public Service Commission

• 3 stages: Prelims, Mains, Interview

• Over 10 lakh aspirants every year

Eligibility

• Age: 21–32 years (relaxation for reserved)

• Education: Any bachelor's degree

• Attempts: 6 for General, 9 for OBC, unlimited for SC/ST

• Indian citizenship required

Exam Pattern — Prelims

• Paper 1 (GS): 100 MCQs, 200 marks

• Paper 2 (CSAT): 80 MCQs, 200 marks (qualifying)

• Duration: 2 hours each

• Negative marking: 1/3rd of marks

Exam Pattern — Mains

• 9 papers (7 counted for merit)

• Essay: 2 essays, 250 marks

• 4 GS papers: 250 marks each

• Optional subject: 2 papers, 250 marks each

• Total: 1,750 marks

Preparation Strategy

• Start with NCERT books (Class 6–12)

• Read The Hindu or Indian Express daily

• Choose optional subject wisely

• Answer writing practice is key

• Join test series for Prelims & Mains

Interview & Final Selection

• Interview: 275 marks

• Total: 2,025 marks (Mains + Interview)

• Personality, communication, and knowledge tested

• Results in 12–15 months from notification
