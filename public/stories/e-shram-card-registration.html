e-Shram Card Registration Guide

Register on e-Shram portal for unorganized workers. Get UAN, insurance cover, and access to government benefits.

What is e-Shram Card?

• National database of unorganized workers

• Free registration - no charges

• Get Universal Account Number (UAN)

• Access to social security schemes

Who Should Register?

• Construction workers, street vendors

• Domestic workers, agricultural laborers

• Gig workers, platform workers

• Any worker without EPFO/ESIC coverage

Benefits of e-Shram Card

• ₹2 lakh accidental insurance (PMSBY)

• ₹1 lakh for partial disability

• Access to PM schemes & benefits

• Future social security benefits

How to Register

• Visit eshram.gov.in

• Enter Aadhaar-linked mobile number

• Verify with OTP

• Fill occupation & bank details

Download e-Shram Card

• Login at eshram.gov.in

• Card available instantly after registration

• Download PDF or save to DigiLocker

• Also register at nearest CSC center
