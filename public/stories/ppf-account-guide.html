What is PPF?

• Government-backed savings scheme

• 15-year lock-in period

• Current interest rate: 7.1% (compounded yearly)

• Minimum deposit: ₹500/year

• Maximum deposit: ₹1.5 lakh/year

• Completely tax-free (EEE status)

Tax Benefits

• Section 80C deduction up to ₹1.5 lakh

• Interest earned is tax-free

• Maturity amount is tax-free

• EEE: Exempt-Exempt-Exempt

• Best for conservative, long-term savings

How to Open PPF Account

• Visit any post office or bank (SBI, BOB, etc.)

• Fill account opening form

• Submit KYC documents (Aadhaar + PAN)

• Minimum ₹500 initial deposit

• Online opening via net banking available

Withdrawal Rules

• Partial withdrawal from 7th year

• Up to 50% of balance at end of 4th year

• Loan facility from 3rd to 6th year

• Premature closure after 5 years (special cases)

• Extension in blocks of 5 years after maturity

Tips to Maximize Returns

• Deposit before 5th of every month

• Invest full ₹1.5 lakh for maximum benefit

• Lump sum early in the year earns more interest

• Don't miss yearly deposit — account becomes inactive

• Great for retirement corpus building
