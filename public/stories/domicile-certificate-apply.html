What is Domicile Certificate?

• Proof of permanent residence in a state

• Required for state quota admissions

• Needed for state government jobs

• Also called residence or bonafide certificate

• Issued by District Magistrate/Tehsildar

Who Needs It?

• Students for state quota college seats

• Job applicants for state govt positions

• Scholarship applications

• State-specific scheme benefits

• Property registration in some states

How to Apply

• Visit state e-District portal

• Login or register new account

• Select 'Domicile Certificate' application

• Fill residence and personal details

• Upload supporting documents

• Pay fee (₹10–100)

Documents Required

• Aadhaar card

• Birth certificate

• Ration card

• School/college certificate showing state

• Voter ID with current address

• Affidavit of residence (if needed)

Processing & Validity

• Verification by local revenue officer

• Processing time: 7–21 days

• Download digitally signed certificate

• Valid for lifetime in most states

• Some states require renewal every few years
