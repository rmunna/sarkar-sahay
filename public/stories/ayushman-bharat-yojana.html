What is Ayushman Bharat?

• World's largest health insurance scheme

• ₹5 lakh coverage per family per year

• Covers 1,500+ medical procedures

• Cashless treatment at empanelled hospitals

• Over 50 crore beneficiaries

Who is Eligible?

• Automatically selected from SECC database

• Rural: Kutcha house, no adult earner, SC/ST households

• Urban: Rag pickers, domestic workers, street vendors

• No age or family size limit

• Check eligibility at mera.pmjay.gov.in

How to Get Your Card

• Visit mera.pmjay.gov.in or call 14555

• Enter mobile number and verify OTP

• Check if your family is in the list

• Visit nearest CSC or empanelled hospital

• Get Ayushman card with photo & details

What's Covered?

• Hospitalization expenses (pre & post)

• Surgery, daycare procedures

• ICU charges and diagnostics

• Medicine and medical consumables

• Transport allowance up to ₹500

Find Empanelled Hospitals

• Visit pmjay.gov.in → Hospital Finder

• Search by state, district, or specialty

• Both government and private hospitals

• Over 25,000 hospitals across India
