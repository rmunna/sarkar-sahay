TDS - Tax Deducted at Source Explained

Understand TDS rates, how to check TDS in Form 26AS, and how to claim TDS refund while filing ITR.

What is TDS?

• Tax collected at source of income

• Employer/bank/client deducts before paying

• Deposited to government on your behalf

• Adjusted against total tax liability

Common TDS Rates

• Salary: As per income tax slab

• Bank FD interest: 10% (above ₹40,000)

• Rent: 10% (above ₹2.4 lakh/year)

• Professional fees: 10%

How to Check TDS

• Login at incometax.gov.in

• View Form 26AS (Tax Credit Statement)

• Check AIS for complete TDS details

• Match with Form 16/16A received

Claim TDS Refund

• File ITR if TDS exceeds actual tax

• Refund processed within 20-45 days

• Credited to bank linked with PAN

• Track refund at incometax.gov.in

Avoid Excess TDS

• Submit Form 15G/15H if no tax liability

• 15G for below 60 years, 15H for seniors

• Submit to bank at start of financial year

• Apply for lower TDS certificate (13)
