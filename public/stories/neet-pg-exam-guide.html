What is NEET PG?

• National entrance for MD, MS, and PG Diploma

• Conducted by NBEMS (formerly NBE)

• Single exam for all medical PG seats

• Computer-based test (CBT)

• Over 2 lakh doctors appear annually

Eligibility

• MBBS degree from recognized institution

• Completion of internship

• Permanent/provisional registration with MCI/NMC

• Indian citizens and OCI cardholders

• No age limit

Exam Pattern

• 200 MCQs in 3.5 hours

• Single best answer type

• All 19 subjects of MBBS covered

• +4 for correct, -1 for wrong

• Conducted in single session

Preparation Strategy

• Start preparation during internship

• Focus on high-yield subjects: Medicine, Surgery, OBG

• Use question banks (Marrow, PrepLadder)

• Revise First Aid and standard textbooks

• Grand tests weekly in last 3 months

Counselling Process

• AIQ (50% seats): Through MCC at mcc.nic.in

• State quota (50%): Through state counselling

• Deemed/central: Separate counselling

• 3 rounds + mop-up + stray vacancy

• Choose college and branch wisely
