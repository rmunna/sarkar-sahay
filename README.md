# 🏠 CitizenNest — Government Services Made Simple

**[www.citizennest.com](https://www.citizennest.com)**

Clear, accurate, step-by-step guides for every Indian government service — Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, Ration Card, Government Schemes, Jobs & Exams, and more.

## ✨ Features

- 📚 **500+ Guides** — Comprehensive step-by-step guides for government services
- 🇮🇳 **Hindi Translations** — 125+ guides available in Hindi
- 🧮 **13 Calculators** — EMI, SIP, FD, PPF, Salary, Age Eligibility & more
- 📢 **Job & Exam Updates** — Latest notifications for SSC, UPSC, Banking, Railway
- 📍 **State-wise Coverage** — 17+ states with local scheme guides
- 📖 **49 Web Stories** — Visual, swipeable AMP stories
- 🔍 **SEO Optimized** — Structured data, sitemaps, fast loading (99 Lighthouse score)

## 📂 Categories

- 🪪 Identity Documents (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, Voter ID, Driving License)
- 🏦 Government Schemes (PM Kisan, Ayushman Bharat, Mudra Loan, etc.)
- 💰 Tax & Finance (ITR, GST, TDS, EPF, PPF)
- 📝 Jobs & Exams (SSC, UPSC, Banking, Railway, State PSC)
- 📜 Certificates (Caste, Income, Domicile, Birth, Death, Marriage)
- 💡 Utilities (Electricity, Water, Gas, UPI, DigiLocker)
- 🏠 Property & Legal (Land Records, Registration, Legal Rights)
- 🍚 Food & Ration (Ration Card, Fair Price Shop)
- 🏛️ State Schemes (State-specific welfare programs)
